    Manually curated alternative records

    Keep the preferred Swiss-Prot accession in a review's top-level id. Use alternative_records to document a manually assessed relationship to another UniProt accession. It is a list because one preferred record may have more than one relevant counterpart. This is distinct from functional_isoforms, which describes products and activities rather than database records.

    The relationship REFERENCE_PROTEOME_EQUIVALENT means the curator considers the alternative an equivalent representation in the named reference proteome. A matching protein name or family is insufficient. Evaluate sequence, locus, taxonomy, and cultivar/strain differences; identical proteins can occur at distinct loci. A curator can record qualifications in reason and in each evidence item's description.

    Use REFERENCE_PROTEOME_CANDIDATE when a plausible counterpart has supporting sequence/locus and membership evidence but equivalence remains unresolved. Explain the competing interpretations and what would resolve them in reason. WIN6 is a worked example: the best matching reference sequence is documented without asserting that it is the same locus. Neither relationship automatically transfers GO annotations. Equivalence need not mean literal sequence identity: document all differences and the basis for distinguishing a counterpart from a paralog.

    Use REFERENCE_PROTEOME_LOCUS_COUNTERPART for a supported same-locus or reference-cultivar counterpart with different function or protein integrity. FRI and AOP2 are explicit active-versus-inactive allele examples; the rice RGA4 pair documents cultivar-dependent resistance-system differences. RGA5 remains a candidate: target-only locus identifiers do not independently establish cross-cultivar mapping. This relationship must not be interpreted as functional equivalence.

    If a completed search identifies no sufficiently supported counterpart, use reference_proteome_assessments instead of inventing an alternative accession:

    reference_proteome_assessments:
- reference_proteomes: [UP000000625]
  status: NO_SUPPORTED_COUNTERPART
  reviewed_on: '2026-10-18'
  reason: No supported counterpart was identified in the documented search.
  evidence: # Required cited PROTEOME_SEARCH and PROTEOME_MEMBERSHIP items
    # See the complete ECOLI/traD worked example.

    The abbreviated example above omits the required evidence entries; see traD for a complete validated record. A negative search is not proof of biological absence. Record the search settings, competing hits, limits, and relevant plasmid/strain context. For taxa with multiple reference proteomes, document all searched IDs. The report-wide generated assessment report reads the project frontmatter gene list and these curated YAML blocks. Regenerate its Markdown and CSV with just project-counterparts; use just project-counterparts --check to detect stale outputs. The project page keeps a few illustrative cases rather than a manually maintained inventory.

    For complete worked examples, see CCOAOMT1 and CCOAOMT2. Their primary IDs remain O65862 and O65922; the alternatives are A0A2K1Z5Q2 and A0A2K2C664.

    Each alternative requires:

    Field Content
    id Alternative primary UniProt accession, without a prefix
    relationship REFERENCE_PROTEOME_EQUIVALENT, REFERENCE_PROTEOME_LOCUS_COUNTERPART, or REFERENCE_PROTEOME_CANDIDATE
    reference_proteome The alternative's reference proteome, e.g. UP000006729
    reviewed_on Date of the manual assessment
    reason Curator's rationale and qualifications
    evidence Typed claims with description and supported_by citations

    Evidence types distinguish the parts of the argument:

    Type What it establishes
    PROTEOME_SEARCH Search scope, returned hits and limitations, including a negative search
    SEQUENCE_COMPARISON Sequence relationship; report identity and coverage, including differences
    LOCUS_MAPPING Shared genomic locus or transcript identifiers
    PROTEOME_MEMBERSHIP Current membership of the alternative in a reference proteome
    RECORD_HISTORY Dated observations from archived records
    CURATION_CONTEXT Qualitative context, such as cultivar, assembly, or curator correspondence

    For all alternative-record relationships, just validate SPECIES GENE requires membership evidence and at least one of sequence comparison or locus mapping. History and context alone cannot establish equivalence. It also rejects self-links, duplicate alternatives, absent citations, and citations not declared in references. Standard quote validation checks supporting_text against the cited source. These checks validate the documented argument; they do not independently adjudicate its biology. No-counterpart assessments require cited PROTEOME_SEARCH and PROTEOME_MEMBERSHIP evidence and the searched proteome IDs.

    No GO annotation is transferred automatically, and neither the review's identity nor its annotations are reassigned. The orphan-curated report still includes an eligible primary accession even when a counterpart is documented: its absence from the proteome remains a useful question for UniProt.

    Evidence snapshots and UniSave

    Use current UniProtKB records and proteome metadata for membership and locus cross-references. UniParc identifies protein sequences: a shared UniParc ID supports sequence identity, but does not establish a unique gene or current proteome membership. UniSave is the archive of previous UniProt entry versions. It can show that a proteome association was present or absent at a particular release; it is not a log of curators' reasons. Checking three old versions does not prove uninterrupted absence between those versions.

    The uniprot-orphan-analysis repository captures full API source records, URLs, retrieval timestamps, release headers, selected UniSave versions, and computed sequence/membership observations. It also owns the FASTAs, search tables, alignments, and reproduction workflows. Run the following commands in that repository; they do not create a manual equivalence judgment:

    uv run python projects/ORPHAN_CURATED/fetch_record_evidence.py O65862 A0A2K1Z5Q2 \
  --proteome UP000006729 --history-versions 94 122 132 \
  --output genes/POPTR/CCOAOMT1/CCOAOMT1-record-evidence.yaml

uv run python projects/ORPHAN_CURATED/fetch_record_evidence.py O65922 A0A2K2C664 \
  --proteome UP000006729 --history-versions 90 119 130 \
  --output genes/POPTR/CCOAOMT2/CCOAOMT2-record-evidence.yaml

    Add --ena ACCESSION to include the source nucleotide record and its organism, cultivar, or strain metadata. That repository's per-gene RESULTS.md files preserve historical interpretations; comparison.yaml and alignment files contain computed observations, and justfile recipes reproduce the work.

    AI Gene Review retains one compact GENE-record-evidence.yaml per gene. Declare this extract in references with a file: identifier and cite it from each evidence item. The extract retains original source line ranges with context, source release and retrieval metadata, and relevant structured observations. Each archived source has a SHA-256 digest and a URL pinned to its Git commit. Full candidate tables and protein sequences stay in the analysis repository.

    After committing an analysis, run these commands from its checkout:

    just export-evidence /path/to/ai-gene-review COMMIT
just check-evidence /path/to/ai-gene-review COMMIT

    The exporter reads the specified Git revision and selects original source lines; it fails if a review's quotation is absent. check-evidence verifies that the local extracts are reproducible from that revision. Normal review validation checks the local quotations offline without downloading the archive.

    Keep source observations separate from interpretation. When refreshing evidence, reassess the relationship, date, rationale, and quotations in the same change. Add the usual curation history record.

    The gene HTML renderer displays these relationships and links to their evidence extracts while continuing to present the preferred accession in the header.

    Embedded UniSave record_text fields in *-record-evidence.yaml are checked for label-only or unvetted-prediction quotations, just like *-uniprot.txt sources. Compact extracts preserve complete matching fields, including their evidence tags, for these checks. JSON metadata and computed observations are not interpreted as flat-file statements.
