# Crystal structure of a bacteriophage T7 DNA replication complex at 2.2 A resolution.
**Authors:** Doublié S, Tabor S, Long AM, Richardson CC, Ellenberger T
**Journal:** Nature (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1038/34593](https://doi.org/10.1038/34593)

## Abstract

1. Nature. 1998 Jan 15;391(6664):251-8. doi: 10.1038/34593.

Crystal structure of a bacteriophage T7 DNA replication complex at 2.2 A

Comment in
    Nature. 1998 Jan 15;391(6664):231-2. doi: 10.1038/34542.

DNA polymerases change their specificity for nucleotide substrates with each
catalytic cycle, while achieving error frequencies in the range of 10(-5) to
10(-6). Here we present a 2.2 A crystal structure of the replicative DNA
polymerase from bacteriophage T7 complexed with a primer-template and a
nucleoside triphosphate in the polymerase active site. The structure illustrates
how nucleotides are selected in a template-directed manner, and provides a
structural basis for a metal-assisted mechanism of phosphoryl transfer by a
large group of related polymerases.
