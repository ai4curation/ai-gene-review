## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1985 Nov;164(2):882-6. doi: 10.1128/jb.164.2.882-886.1985.

Occurrence of succinyl derivatives in the catabolism of arginine in Pseudomonas
cepacia.

Vander Wauven C, Stalon V.

Pseudomonas cepacia NCTC 10743 utilizes arginine as the sole source of carbon
and nitrogen for growth. Arginine is degraded to glutamate via succinyl
derivatives. The catabolic sequence in this pathway is
L-arginine----N2-succinylarginine----N2-succinylornithine---
-N2-succinylglutamate semialdehyde----N2-succinylglutamate----glutamate +
succinate. The formation of the enzymes responsible for arginine degradation is
regulated not only by induction but also by both carbon and nitrogen catabolite
repression.
