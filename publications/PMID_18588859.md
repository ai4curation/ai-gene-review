# TGF-beta activates ERK5 in human renal epithelial cells.
**Authors:** Browne JA, Pearson AL, Zahr RA, Niculescu-Duvaz I, Baines DL, Dockrell ME
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bbrc.2008.06.058](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2008.06.058)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2008 Aug 29;373(3):440-4. doi:
10.1016/j.bbrc.2008.06.058. Epub 2008 Jun 25.

TGF-beta activates ERK5 in human renal epithelial cells.

Browne JA(1), Pearson AL, Zahr RA, Niculescu-Duvaz I, Baines DL, Dockrell ME.

Author information:
(1)South West Thames Institute for Renal Research, St. Helier Hospital,
Carshalton, Surrey SM5 1AA, UK. james.browne@epsom-sthelier.nhs.uk

The role of the MAP kinase, extracellular signal-regulated kinase 5 (ERK5)
remains unknown, however it is involved in cell differentiation and survival as
highlighted by the embryonic lethality of the ERK5 knockout. ERK5 can be
activated by growth factors and other extracellular signals. TGF-beta, a
powerful controller of epithelial cell phenotype, is known to activate the MAP
kinase, ERK1/2 however its effect on ERK5 remains unknown. This study
demonstrates, fort the first time, ERK5 activation by TGF-beta, observed in both
transformed and primary adult human PTEC; activation required ALK-5 receptor
activity. In addition this work demonstrates expression of myocyte enhancer
factor-2 (MEF2C) by PTEC and that TGF-beta increased the association of MEK5
with phospho-ERK5 and MEF2C. ERK5 activation by either TGF-beta or epidermal
growth factor (EGF) was also inhibited by the p38 MAP kinase inhibitor,
SB-202190.
