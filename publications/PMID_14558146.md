# Expression in Escherichia coli of a recombinant adenosine kinase from Saccharomyces cerevisiae: purification, kinetics and substrate analyses.
**Authors:** Barrado P, Rodríguez MJ, Jiménez A, Fernández Lobato M
**Journal:** Yeast (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1002/yea.1039](https://doi.org/10.1002/yea.1039)

## Abstract

1. Yeast. 2003 Oct 15;20(13):1145-50. doi: 10.1002/yea.1039.

Expression in Escherichia coli of a recombinant adenosine kinase from
Saccharomyces cerevisiae: purification, kinetics and substrate analyses.

Barrado P(1), Rodríguez MJ, Jiménez A, Fernández Lobato M.

Author information:
(1)Centro de Biología Molecular Severo Ochoa, Departamento de Biología Molecular
(CSIC/UAM), Universidad Autónoma Madrid, Cantoblanco 28049 Madrid, Spain.

The Saccharomyces cerevisiae ADO1 gene is known to encode a homologue of
eukaryotic adenosine kinases. This gene was expressed in Escherichia coli as a
recombinant protein fused to a polyhistidine tag by using the rhamnose-inducible
bacterial promoter rhaB. The recombinant protein was purified to apparent
homogeneity and its ability to phosphorylate different substrates was evaluated.
Adenosine (Km 3 microM) is its primary substrate. In addition, it also
phosphorylates, albeit less efficiently, 3'-deoxyadenosine (cordycepin; Km 1.84
mM) and 3'-amino-3'-deoxyadenosine (Km 0.26 mM). Other kinetic properties of the
recombinant enzyme have also been determined.
