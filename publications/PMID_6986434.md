# An immunofluorescence procedure for the tissue localization of glucosephosphate isomerase.
**Authors:** Gearhart J, Oster-Granite ML
**Journal:** J Histochem Cytochem (1980)
**DOI:** [10.1177/28.3.6986434](https://doi.org/10.1177/28.3.6986434)

## Abstract

1. J Histochem Cytochem. 1980 Mar;28(3):245-9. doi: 10.1177/28.3.6986434.

An immunofluorescence procedure for the tissue localization of glucosephosphate
isomerase.

Gearhart J, Oster-Granite ML.

Anti-glucosephosphate isomerase antibodies have been used to reveal the presence
of the glycolytic and gluconeogenic enzyme, glucosephosphate isomerase (GPI) in
mouse tissues by an indirect immunofluorescence technique. The enzyme was
localized in 6-micron sections from tissues embedded in paraffin. GPI was
observed in the sarcoplasm of skeletal muscle, all hepatocytes of the liver, and
in the granulosa cells and oocytes of growing ovarian follicles. The
immunofluorescence procedure circumvents the problems associated with the
conventional localization of GPI by histochemistry and appears to be a more
sensitive procedure for the detection of low concentrations of GPI, as
demonstrated by the staining of the ovarian follicles.
