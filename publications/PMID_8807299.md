# A genetic analysis of pannier, a gene necessary for viability of dorsal tissues and bristle positioning in Drosophila.
**Authors:** Heitzler P, Haenlin M, Ramain P, Calleja M, Simpson P

**DOI:** [10.1093/genetics/143.3.1271](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/143.3.1271)

## Abstract

1. Genetics. 1996 Jul;143(3):1271-86. doi: 10.1093/genetics/143.3.1271.

A genetic analysis of pannier, a gene necessary for viability of dorsal tissues
and bristle positioning in Drosophila.

Heitzler P(1), Haenlin M, Ramain P, Calleja M, Simpson P.

Author information:
(1)Institut de Genetique et de Biologie Moleculaire et Cellulaire, Illkirch,
France. pascal@igbmc.u-strasbg.fr

A genetic and phenotypic analysis of the gene pannier is described. Animals
mutant for strong alleles die as embryos in which the cells of the amnioserosa
are prematurely lost. This leads to a dorsal cuticular hole. The dorsal-most
cells of the imagos are also affected: viable mutants exhibit a cleft along the
dorsal midline. pannier mRNA accumulates specifically in the dorsal-most regions
of the embryo and the imaginal discs. Viable mutants and mutant combinations
also affect the thoracic and head bristle patterns in a complex fashion. Only
those bristles within the area of expression of pannier are affected. A large
number of alleles have been studied and reveal that pannier may have opposing
effects on the expression of achaete and scute leading to a loss or a gain of
bristles.
