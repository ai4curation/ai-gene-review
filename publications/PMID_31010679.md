The GPCR-like transmembrane protein Smoothened (Smo) is an indispensable
transducer in Hedgehog (Hh) pathway, its hyperactivation leads to several human
cancers, including non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The mechanism governing
Smo stability still remains elusive. Here, we perform a modifier screening in
Drosophila, and find that the E3 ligase dHerc4 degrades dSmo. Depletion of
dherc4 increases dSmo protein and activates Hh pathway. In addition, we reveal
that HERC4 is downregulated in NSCLC samples, negative correlating with Smo.
HERC4 interacts with Smo reciprocally in NSCLC cells. Finally, we show that
knockdown of herc4 activates Hh pathway and promotes NSCLC cell proliferation.
Taken together, our studies have demonstrated that HERC4 acts as a tumor
suppressor via destabilizing the oncoprotein Smo, and provided HERC4 as a
promising therapeutic target for NSCLC treatment.
