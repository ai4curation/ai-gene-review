ROCKs, or Rho kinases, are serine/threonine kinases that are involved in many
aspects of cell motility, from smooth-muscle contraction to cell migration and
neurite outgrowth. Recent experiments have defined new functions of ROCKs in
cells, including centrosome positioning and cell-size regulation, which might
contribute to various physiological and pathological states.
