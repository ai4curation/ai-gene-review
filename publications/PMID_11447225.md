# Reactive oxygen species-induced phosphorylation of p53 on serine 20 is mediated in part by polo-like kinase-3.
**Authors:** Xie S, Wang Q, Wu H, Cogswell J, Lu L, Jhanwar-Uniyal M, Dai W
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M104157200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M104157200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2001 Sep 28;276(39):36194-9. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M104157200. Epub
2001  Jul 10.

Reactive oxygen species-induced phosphorylation of p53 on serine 20 is mediated
in part by polo-like kinase-3.

Xie S(1), Wang Q, Wu H, Cogswell J, Lu L, Jhanwar-Uniyal M, Dai W.

Author information:
(1)American Health Foundation, Valhalla, New York 10595, USA.

Upon exposure of cells to hydrogen peroxide (H(2)O(2)) phosphorylation of p53
was rapidly induced in human fibroblast GM00637, and this phosphorylation
occurred on serine 9, serine 15, serine 20, but not on serine 392. In addition,
H(2)O(2)-induced phosphorylation of p53 was followed by induction of p21,
suggesting functional activation of p53. Induction of phosphorylation of p53 on
multiple serine residues by H(2)O(2) was caffeine-sensitive and blocked in
ATM(-/-) cells. Polo-like kinase-3 (Plk3) activity was also activated upon
H(2)O(2) treatment, and this activation was ATM-dependent. Recombinant
His(6)-Plk3 phosphorylated glutathione S-transferase (GST)-p53 fusion protein
but not GST alone. When phoshorylated in vitro by His(6)-Plk3, but not by the
kinase-defective mutant His6-Plk3(K52R), GST-p53 was recognized by an antibody
specifically to serine 20-phosphorylated p53, indicating that serine 20 is an in
vitro target of Plk3. Also serine 20-phosphorylated p53 was coimmunoprecipitated
with Plk3 in cells treated with H(2)O(2). Furthermore, although H(2)O(2)
strongly induced serine 15 phosphorylation of p53, it failed to induce serine 20
phosphorylation in Plk3-dificient Daudi cells. Ectopic expression of a Plk3
dominant negative mutant, Plk3(K52R), in GM00637 cells suppressed
H(2)O(2)-induced serine 20 phosphorylation. Taken together, our studies strongly
suggest that the oxidative stress-induced activation of p53 is at least in part
mediated by Plk3.
