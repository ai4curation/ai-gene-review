# Mutations of calcium channel beta subunit genes in mice.
**Authors:** Freise D, Himmerkus N, Schroth G, Trost C, Weissgerber P, Freichel M, Flockerzi V
**Journal:** Biol Chem (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1515/BC.1999.110](https://doi.org/10.1515/BC.1999.110)

## Abstract

1. Biol Chem. 1999 Jul-Aug;380(7-8):897-902. doi: 10.1515/BC.1999.110.

Mutations of calcium channel beta subunit genes in mice.

Freise D(1), Himmerkus N, Schroth G, Trost C, Weissgerber P, Freichel M,
Flockerzi V.

Author information:
(1)Institut für Pharmakologie und Toxikologie, Universität des Saarlandes,

Ca2+ influx through high voltage activated Ca2+ channels initiates a number of
physiological processes including e.g. excitation-contraction coupling in
cardiac myocytes and excitation-transcription coupling in neurones. The Ca2+
channels involved are complexes of a pore-forming alpha1 subunit, a
transmembrane delta subunit disulfide-linked to an extracellular alpha2 subunit,
a intracellular beta subunit and, at least in some tissues, a gamma subunit.
Experimental analysis of beta subunit function comprises functional coexpression
of its cDNA together with the cDNAs of the other subunits. This experimental
approach can be supplemented by investigating functional alterations that result
from the genetic elimination of Ca2+ channel beta genes in mice. Here we
summarize the phenotype of mice deficient in the beta1 subunit, the beta3
subunit or the beta4 subunit, respectively.
