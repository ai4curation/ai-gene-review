# Composite signalling from Serrate and Delta establishes leg segments in Drosophila through Notch.
**Authors:** Bishop SA, Klein T, Arias AM, Couso JP
**Journal:** Development (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1242/dev.126.13.2993](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.126.13.2993)

## Abstract

1. Development. 1999 Jul;126(13):2993-3003. doi: 10.1242/dev.126.13.2993.

Composite signalling from Serrate and Delta establishes leg segments in
Drosophila through Notch.

Bishop SA(1), Klein T, Arias AM, Couso JP.

Author information:
(1)School of Biological Sciences, Royal Holloway College, University of London,
Egham, Surrey TW20 0EX, UK.

The receptor protein NOTCH and its ligands SERRATE and DELTA are involved in
many developmental processes in invertebrates and vertebrates alike. Here we
show that the expression of the Serrate and Delta genes patterns the segments of
the leg in Drosophila by a combination of their signalling activities.
Coincident stripes of Serrate and Delta expressing cells activate Enhancer of
split expression in adjacent cells through Notch signalling. These cells form a
patterning boundary from which a putative secondary signal leads to the
development of leg joints. Elsewhere in the tarsal segments, signalling by DELTA
and NOTCH is necessary for the development of non-joint parts of the leg. We
propose that these two effects result from different thresholds of NOTCH
activation, which are translated into different downstream gene expression
effects. We propose a general mechanism for creation of boundaries by Notch
signalling.
