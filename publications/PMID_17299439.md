CHARGE syndrome is a rare, usually sporadic autosomal dominant disorder due in
2/3 of cases to mutations within the CHD7 gene. The clinical definition has
evolved with time. The 3C triad (Coloboma-Choanal atresia-abnormal semicircular
Canals), arhinencephaly and rhombencephalic dysfunctions are now considered the
most important and constant clues to the diagnosis. We will discuss here recent
aspects of the phenotypic delineation of CHARGE syndrome and highlight the role
of CHD7 in its pathogeny. We review available data on its molecular pathology as
well as cytogenetic and molecular evidences for genetic heterogeneity within
CHARGE syndrome.
