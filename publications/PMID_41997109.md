Phosphoinositides regulate cellular signaling. Tan et al.1 and Li et al.2
identify PtdIns(3,5)P₂ and cholesterol as key lipids that stabilize STING
oligomers and enable TBK1 activation, explaining why STING must traffic from the
ER to the Golgi to initiate immune signaling.
