# Benzoxazole benzenesulfonamides are novel allosteric inhibitors of fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase with a distinct binding mode.
**Authors:** von Geldern TW, Lai C, Gum RJ, Daly M, Sun C, Fry EH, Abad-Zapatero C
**Journal:** Bioorg Med Chem Lett (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bmcl.2006.01.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmcl.2006.01.015)

## Abstract

1. Bioorg Med Chem Lett. 2006 Apr 1;16(7):1811-5. doi:
10.1016/j.bmcl.2006.01.015.  Epub 2006 Jan 25.

Benzoxazole benzenesulfonamides are novel allosteric inhibitors of
fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase with a distinct binding mode.

von Geldern TW(1), Lai C, Gum RJ, Daly M, Sun C, Fry EH, Abad-Zapatero C.

Author information:
(1)Metabolic Disease Research, GPRD, Abbott Laboratories, Abbott Park, IL 60064,
USA. Thomas.vongeldern@abbott.com

We have identified benzoxazole benzenesulfonamide 1 as a novel allosteric
inhibitor of fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase (FBPase-1). X-ray crystallographic and
biological studies of 1 indicate a distinct binding mode that recapitulates
features of several previously reported FBPase-1 inhibitor classes.
