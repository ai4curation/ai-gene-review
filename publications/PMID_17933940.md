Identification of a malate chemoreceptor in Pseudomonas aeruginosa by screening
for chemotaxis defects in an energy taxis-deficient mutant.

Alvarez-Ortega C(1), Harwood CS.

Author information:
(1)Department of Microbiology, Box 357242, 1705 N.E. Pacific Street, University
of Washington, Seattle, WA 98195-7242, USA.

We found that a robust energy taxis response mediated by the Aer receptor can
sometimes mask chemotaxis mediated by other methyl-accepting chemotaxis proteins
(MCPs) in Pseudomonas aeruginosa. We identified PA2652 as a chemoreceptor for
malate by screening aer mcp double mutants by using swarm plate assays.
