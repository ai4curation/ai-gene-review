# MADS-domain transcription factors and the floral quartet model of flower development: linking plant development and evolution.
**Authors:** Theißen G, Melzer R, Rümpler F

**DOI:** [10.1242/dev.134080](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.134080)

## Abstract

1. Development. 2016 Sep 15;143(18):3259-71. doi: 10.1242/dev.134080.

MADS-domain transcription factors and the floral quartet model of flower
development: linking plant development and evolution.

Theißen G(1), Melzer R(2), Rümpler F(3).

The floral quartet model of floral organ specification poses that different
tetramers of MIKC-type MADS-domain transcription factors control gene expression
and hence the identity of floral organs during development. Here, we provide a
brief history of the floral quartet model and review several lines of recent
evidence that support the model. We also describe how the model has been used in
contemporary developmental and evolutionary biology to shed light on enigmatic
topics such as the origin of land and flowering plants. Finally, we suggest a
novel hypothesis describing how floral quartet-like complexes may interact with
chromatin during target gene activation and repression.
