During development of the Drosophila retina, the R8 photoreceptor neuron induces
a neighbouring cell to assume an R7 cell fate. Genetic data suggest that the
induction is mediated by two transmembrane proteins encoded by bride of
sevenless and sevenless. A direct interaction between these two proteins was
demonstrated by the heterotypic aggregation of cell lines expressing them. In
the developing eye the sevenless-dependent internalization of bride of sevenless
by the R7 precursor cell provides evidence for a direct interaction between
these two proteins in vivo.
