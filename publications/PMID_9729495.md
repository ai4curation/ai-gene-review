# Dissecting the roles of the Drosophila EGF receptor in eye development and MAP kinase activation.
**Authors:** Kumar JP, Tio M, Hsiung F, Akopyan S, Gabay L, Seger R, Shilo BZ, Moses K
**Journal:** Development (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1242/dev.125.19.3875](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.125.19.3875)

## Abstract

1. Development. 1998 Oct;125(19):3875-85. doi: 10.1242/dev.125.19.3875.

Dissecting the roles of the Drosophila EGF receptor in eye development and MAP
kinase activation.

Kumar JP(1), Tio M, Hsiung F, Akopyan S, Gabay L, Seger R, Shilo BZ, Moses K.

Author information:
(1)Department of Cell Biology, Emory University School of Medicine, Atlanta, GA
30322-3030, USA.

A new conditional Egfr allele was used to dissect the roles of the receptor in
eye development and to test two published models. EGFR function is necessary for
morphogenetic furrow initiation, is not required for establishment of the
founder R8 cell in each ommatidium, but is necessary to maintain its
differentiated state. EGFR is required subsequently for recruitment of all other
neuronal cells. The initial EGFR-dependent MAP kinase activation occurs in the
furrow, but the active kinase (dp-ERK) is observed only in the cytoplasm for
over 2 hours. Similarly, SEVENLESS-dependent activation results in cytoplasmic
appearance of dp-ERK for 6 hours. These results suggest an additional regulated
step in this pathway and we discuss models for this.
