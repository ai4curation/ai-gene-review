# Involvement of Ypt7p, a small GTPase, in traffic from late endosome to the vacuole in yeast.
**Authors:** Schimmöller F, Riezman H
**Journal:** J Cell Sci (1993)
**DOI:** [10.1242/jcs.106.3.823](https://doi.org/10.1242/jcs.106.3.823)

## Abstract

1. J Cell Sci. 1993 Nov;106 ( Pt 3):823-30. doi: 10.1242/jcs.106.3.823.

Involvement of Ypt7p, a small GTPase, in traffic from late endosome to the
vacuole in yeast.

Schimmöller F(1), Riezman H.

Author information:
(1)Biocenter, University of Basel, Switzerland.

The YPT7 gene encodes the Saccharomyces cerevisiae homolog of mammalian rab7
protein. Data obtained from studies on a delta ypt7 mutant suggested that Ypt7p
is involved in the endocytic pathway in yeast (Wichmann et al., Cell 71,
1131-1142, 1992). We report here that endocytosed pheromone alpha-factor
accumulates in late endosomes in delta ypt7 cells, indicating that Ypt7p is
involved in the regulation of transport steps from late endosomes to the
vacuole. We also show that alpha-factor can be degraded in a PEP4-dependent
manner in a prevacuolar/endosomal compartment in delta ypt7 cells, providing
independent evidence that the pathways of vacuole biogenesis and endocytosis in
yeast may intersect in the endosomal membrane system.
