# Ku80: product of the XRCC5 gene and its role in DNA repair and V(D)J recombination.
**Authors:** Taccioli GE, Gottlieb TM, Blunt T, Priestley A, Demengeot J, Mizuta R, Lehmann AR, Alt FW, Jackson SP, Jeggo PA
**Journal:** Science (1994)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.8073286](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.8073286)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1994 Sep 2;265(5177):1442-5. doi: 10.1126/science.8073286.

Ku80: product of the XRCC5 gene and its role in DNA repair and V(D)J
recombination.

Taccioli GE(1), Gottlieb TM, Blunt T, Priestley A, Demengeot J, Mizuta R,
Lehmann AR, Alt FW, Jackson SP, Jeggo PA.

Author information:
(1)Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Children's Hospital, Boston, MA.

The radiosensitive mutant xrs-6, derived from Chinese hamster ovary cells, is
defective in DNA double-strand break repair and in ability to undergo V(D)J
recombination. The human XRCC5 DNA repair gene, which complements this mutant,
is shown here through genetic and biochemical evidence to be the 80-kilodalton
subunit of the Ku protein. Ku binds to free double-stranded DNA ends and is the
DNA-binding component of the DNA-dependent protein kinase. Thus, the Ku protein
is involved in DNA repair and in V(D)J recombination, and these results may also
indicate a role for the Ku-DNA-dependent protein kinase complex in those same
processes.
