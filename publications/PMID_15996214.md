# Functional analysis of R75Q mutation in the gene coding for Connexin 26 identified in a family with nonsyndromic hearing loss.
**Authors:** Piazza V, Beltramello M, Menniti M, Colao E, Malatesta P, Argento R, Chiarella G, Gallo LV, Catalano M, Perrotti N, Mammano F, Cassandro E
**Journal:** Clin Genet (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1399-0004.2005.00468.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1399-0004.2005.00468.x)

## Abstract

1. Clin Genet. 2005 Aug;68(2):161-6. doi: 10.1111/j.1399-0004.2005.00468.x.

Functional analysis of R75Q mutation in the gene coding for Connexin 26
identified in a family with nonsyndromic hearing loss.

Piazza V(1), Beltramello M, Menniti M, Colao E, Malatesta P, Argento R,
Chiarella G, Gallo LV, Catalano M, Perrotti N, Mammano F, Cassandro E.

Author information:
(1)Venetian Institute of Molecular Medicine, Padua, Italy.

Mutations in the gene (GJB2) coding for Connexin 26 (Cx26) are responsible for
genetic forms of sensorineural hearing loss. This article describes a family
characterized by congenital profound hearing loss, inherited in an autosomal
dominant fashion and associated to a R75Q substitution in Cx26. Cell
transfection and fluorescence imaging, dye transfer experiments and dual patch
clamp recording showed that the mutant completely prevents the formation of
functional channels despite assembling into junctional plaques, in communication
incompetent HeLa cells. The disease is not associated with palmar and plantar
keratosis in any of the family members, suggesting that R75Q substitution is not
sufficient for the development of the complete syndromic phenotype. The
association of palmar and plantar keratosis with profound hearing loss may be
dependent on genetic background, requiring a functional interaction between the
mutated Cx26 and other epidermally expressed connexins.
