# Mutations in the glucocerebrosidase gene and Parkinson disease: phenotype-genotype correlation.

## Abstract

1. Neurology. 2005 Nov 8;65(9):1460-1. doi: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000176987.47875.28.
Epub 2005 Sep 7.

Mutations in the glucocerebrosidase gene and Parkinson disease:
phenotype-genotype correlation.

Aharon-Peretz J(1), Badarny S, Rosenbaum H, Gershoni-Baruch R.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology, Rambam Medical Center, Haifa, Israel.
jaharon@rambam.health.gov.il

Mutations in the glucocerebrosidase (GBA) gene have been recently identified as
contributory to Parkinson disease (PD) in Ashkenazi Jews. In the present study,
the clinical characteristics of Ashkenazi patients with PD with GBA mutations (n
= 40) were compared to those of Ashkenazi patients with PD without any known GBA
mutation (n = 108). The overall clinical manifestations and age at disease onset
did not differ in patients with GBA mutations compared to patients without
mutations.
