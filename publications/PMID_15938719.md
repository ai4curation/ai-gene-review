# Aurora C is directly associated with Survivin and required for cytokinesis.
**Authors:** Yan X, Cao L, Li Q, Wu Y, Zhang H, Saiyin H, Liu X, Zhang X, Shi Q, Yu L
**Journal:** Genes Cells (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1365-2443.2005.00863.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2443.2005.00863.x)

## Abstract

1. Genes Cells. 2005 Jun;10(6):617-26. doi: 10.1111/j.1365-2443.2005.00863.x.

Aurora C is directly associated with Survivin and required for cytokinesis.

Yan X(1), Cao L, Li Q, Wu Y, Zhang H, Saiyin H, Liu X, Zhang X, Shi Q, Yu L.

Author information:
(1)State Key Laboratory of Genetic Engineering, Institute of Genetics, School of
Life Sciences, Fudan University, Shanghai 200433, P. R. China.

Much recent attention has been focused on Aurora C, the third member of the
mammalian Aurora kinases family that plays significant roles in mitosis. We
report here that using sensitive RT-PCR to amplify the C-terminal, we found that
Aurora C is not only expressed highly in testis, but also among 16 other human
tissues in a broad-spectrum way. Aurora C, as a chromosomal passenger protein,
is co-localized with Aurora B and Survivin in mitotic cells. Aurora C can also
be associated with Aurora B and Survivin in vivo and directly binds to Survivin
but not Aurora B in vitro. Over-expression of a catalytically inactive mutant of
Aurora C impaired the localization of Aurora C to the spindle midzone and
severely disturbed the cytokinesis, resulting in multinucleation, all of which
are consistent with the results induced by the mutant of Aurora B. Furthermore,
we provide evidence that Aurora C could rescue the multinucleate phenotype
produced by Aurora B mutant, and vice versa. Overall, these findings demonstrate
that Aurora C, a member of the chromosomal passenger complex, is required for
cytokinesis.
