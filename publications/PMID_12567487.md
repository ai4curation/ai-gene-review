# [Overexpression of Bcl-2 inhibited T lymphocyte apoptosis mediated by CD3 epsilon molecule].
**Authors:** Yang X, Liu Y, Zheng D
**Journal:** Zhongguo Yi Xue Ke Xue Yuan Xue Bao (1999)

## Abstract

1. Zhongguo Yi Xue Ke Xue Yuan Xue Bao. 1999 Dec;21(6):421-4.

[Overexpression of Bcl-2 inhibited T lymphocyte apoptosis mediated by CD3
epsilon molecule].

[Article in Chinese]

Yang X(1), Liu Y, Zheng D.

Author information:
(1)National Laboratory of Medical Molecular Biology, Institute of Basic Medical
Sciences, CAMS and PUMC, Beijing 100005.

OBJECTIVE: To study the role of bcl-2 gene overexpression in T lymphocyte
apoptosis mediated by CD8 epsilon chimera molecule.
METHODS: The cells expressed CD8 epsilon chimera molecule were transfected with
a bcl-2 expression vector. The cells with Bcl-2 overexperssion were selected by
G418 and limited dilution, and then treated with anti-CD8 antibody. The cell
death was evaluated with flow cytometry.
RESULTS: It was shown that there were only 2.6% of cells with Bcl-2
overexpression died by apoptosis. Comparing with Jurkat and TJK cells without
Bcl-2 expression, in which there were 6.1% and 33.7% of cell death respectively,
suggesting that Bcl-2 overexpression remarkbly inhibited apoptosis of the T
lymphocytes mediated by CD8 epsilon chimera molecule.
CONCLUSIONS: Bcl-2 overexpression could block T lymphocyte apoptosis mediated by
CD3 epsilon molecule and the mechanisms remained to be clarified in detail.
