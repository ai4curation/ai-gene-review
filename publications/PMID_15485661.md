# WGEF is a novel RhoGEF expressed in intestine, liver, heart, and kidney.
**Authors:** Wang Y, Suzuki H, Yokoo T, Tada-Iida K, Kihara R, Miura M, Watanabe K, Sone H, Shimano H, Toyoshima H, Yamada N
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2004)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2004 Nov 19;324(3):1053-8. doi:
10.1016/j.bbrc.2004.09.153.

WGEF is a novel RhoGEF expressed in intestine, liver, heart, and kidney.

Wang Y(1), Suzuki H, Yokoo T, Tada-Iida K, Kihara R, Miura M, Watanabe K, Sone
H, Shimano H, Toyoshima H, Yamada N.

Author information:

Rho family GTPases regulate multiple cellular processes through their downstream
effectors, where their activities are stimulated by the guanine nucleotide
exchange factors. Here, we report a new member of RhoGEF, WGEF, which has the
classical structure of DH-PH domain and a C-terminal SH3 domain. WGEF was shown
to activate RhoA, Cdc42, and Rac1 by pulldown assay, and forced expression of
WGEF resulted in marked rearrangement of the actin cytoskeleton, which is
typically seen by the activation of RhoA, Cdc42, and Rac1. WGEF was highly
expressed in intestine and also in liver, heart and kidney, which may suggest
the involvement of WGEF in the development and functions of these organs. The
expression pattern may also suggest the possible importance of WGEF in the
understanding of diseases based on metabolic disorder.
