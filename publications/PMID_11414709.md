# FMRFamide-related neuropeptides are agonists of the orphan G-protein-coupled receptor GPR54.
**Authors:** Clements MK, McDonald TP, Wang R, Xie G, O'Dowd BF, George SR, Austin CP, Liu Q
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.2001.5098](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.2001.5098)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2001 Jun 29;284(5):1189-93. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.2001.5098.

FMRFamide-related neuropeptides are agonists of the orphan G-protein-coupled
receptor GPR54.

Clements MK(1), McDonald TP, Wang R, Xie G, O'Dowd BF, George SR, Austin CP, Liu
Q.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmacology, WP26A-3000, Merck Research Laboratories, West
Point, Pennsylvania 19486, USA.

We have isolated and determined the coding sequences of human and mouse
orthologs of the rat orphan G-protein-coupled receptor GPR54. Mouse and rat
GPR54 are nearly 95% identical to each other, and both are approximately 85%
identical to human GPR54 at the amino acid level. Screening of agonists for
GPR54 identified several invertebrate neuropeptides of the RFamide and RWamide
family that were able to activate GPR54 at microM range through the G(alpha)q
pathway. Substitution analysis showed that the C-terminal optimal sequence of
GPR54-activating peptides is Gly-Leu-Arg-Trp-NH2. Northern analysis of human
GPR54 detected expression in several peripheral tissues and many regions of the
central nervous system.
