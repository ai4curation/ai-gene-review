# Involvement of PIAS1 in the sumoylation of tumor suppressor p53.
**Authors:** Kahyo T, Nishida T, Yasuda H
**Journal:** Mol Cell (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1097-2765(01)00349-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1097-2765(01)00349-5)

## Abstract

1. Mol Cell. 2001 Sep;8(3):713-8. doi: 10.1016/s1097-2765(01)00349-5.

Involvement of PIAS1 in the sumoylation of tumor suppressor p53.

Kahyo T(1), Nishida T, Yasuda H.

Author information:
(1)Tokyo University of Pharmacy and Life Science, School of Life Science, 1432-1
Horinouchi, Hachioji, Tokyo 192-0392, Japan.

Sumoylation of p53 by the ubiquitin-like protein, SUMO-1/sentrin/PIC1, has been
shown to stimulate its transcriptional activation activity. The SUMO E3 ligase,
a key enzyme in the recognition of substrates to be sumoylated, has not yet been
identified. We isolated PIAS1 (protein inhibitor of activated STAT1) as a SUMO-1
binding protein by yeast two-hybrid screening. In addition, PIAS1 bound p53 and
Ubc9, the E2 for SUMO. PIAS1 that was mutated in the RING finger-like domain
bound p53 and SUMO-1, but not Ubc9. PIAS1 catalyzed the sumoylation of p53 both
in U2OS cells and in vitro in a domain-dependent manner. These data suggest that
PIAS1 functions as a SUMO ligase, or possibly as a tightly bound regulator of
it, toward p53.
