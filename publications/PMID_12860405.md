# Characterization of Pellino2, a substrate of IRAK1 and IRAK4.
**Authors:** Strelow A, Kollewe C, Wesche H
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(03)00697-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(03)00697-5)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2003 Jul 17;547(1-3):157-61. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(03)00697-5.

Characterization of Pellino2, a substrate of IRAK1 and IRAK4.

Strelow A(1), Kollewe C, Wesche H.

Author information:
(1)Tularik Inc., 1120 Veterans Boulevard, South San Francisco, CA 94080, USA.

Interleukin-1 (IL-1) receptor-associated kinases (IRAKs) are central components
of Toll/IL-1 receptor (TIR) signaling pathways. In an attempt to discover novel
signal transducers in TIR signaling, we identified human Pellino2 as an
interaction partner of IRAK4. Pellino2 interacts with kinase-active as well as
kinase-inactive IRAK1 and IRAK4. Furthermore, Pellino2 is one of the first
substrates identified for IRAK1 and IRAK4. Functional studies using
overexpression or RNAi knock-down of Pellino2 suggest a role of Pellino2 as a
scaffolding protein similar to Pellino1. However, unlike Pellino1, Pellino2 does
not seem to activate a specific transcription factor, but links TIR signaling to
basic cellular processes.
