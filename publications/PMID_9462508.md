# LIGHT, a new member of the TNF superfamily, and lymphotoxin alpha are ligands for herpesvirus entry mediator.
**Authors:** Mauri DN, Ebner R, Montgomery RI, Kochel KD, Cheung TC, Yu GL, Ruben S, Murphy M, Eisenberg RJ, Cohen GH, Spear PG, Ware CF
**Journal:** Immunity (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1074-7613(00)80455-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1074-7613(00)80455-0)

## Abstract

1. Immunity. 1998 Jan;8(1):21-30. doi: 10.1016/s1074-7613(00)80455-0.

LIGHT, a new member of the TNF superfamily, and lymphotoxin alpha are ligands
for herpesvirus entry mediator.

Mauri DN(1), Ebner R, Montgomery RI, Kochel KD, Cheung TC, Yu GL, Ruben S,
Murphy M, Eisenberg RJ, Cohen GH, Spear PG, Ware CF.

Author information:
(1)Division of Molecular Immunology, La Jolla Institute for Allergy and
Immunology, San Diego, California 92121, USA.

Herpes simplex virus (HSV) 1 and 2 infect activated T lymphocytes by attachment
of the HSV envelope glycoprotein D (gD) to the cellular herpesvirus entry
mediator (HVEM), an orphan member of the tumor necrosis factor receptor
superfamily. Here, we demonstrate that HVEM binds two cellular ligands, secreted
lymphotoxin alpha (LTalpha) and LIGHT, a new member of the TNF superfamily.
LIGHT is a 29 kDa type II transmembrane protein produced by activated T cells
that also engages the receptor for the LTalphabeta heterotrimer but does not
form complexes with either LTalpha or LTbeta. HSV1 gD inhibits the interaction
of HVEM with LIGHT, and LIGHT and gD interfere with HVEM-dependent cell entry by
HSV1. This characterizes herpesvirus gD as a membrane-bound viokine and
establishes LIGHT-HVEM as integral components of the lymphotoxin
cytokine-receptor system.
