# Identification of the yeast cytidine deaminase CDD1 as an orphan C-->U RNA editase.
**Authors:** Dance GS, Beemiller P, Yang Y, Mater DV, Mian IS, Smith HC
**Journal:** Nucleic Acids Res (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1093/nar/29.8.1772](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/29.8.1772)
**PMC:** [PMC31303](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC31303/)

## Abstract

1. Nucleic Acids Res. 2001 Apr 15;29(8):1772-80. doi: 10.1093/nar/29.8.1772.

Identification of the yeast cytidine deaminase CDD1 as an orphan C-->U RNA
editase.

Dance GS(1), Beemiller P, Yang Y, Mater DV, Mian IS, Smith HC.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry and Biophysics, University of Rochester, 601
Elmwood Avenue, Rochester, NY 14642, USA.

Yeast co-expressing rat APOBEC-1 and a fragment of human apolipoprotein B (apoB)
mRNA assembled functional editosomes and deaminated C6666 to U in a mooring
sequence-dependent fashion. The occurrence of APOBEC-1-complementing proteins
suggested a naturally occurring mRNA editing mechanism in yeast. Previously, a
hidden Markov model identified seven yeast genes encoding proteins possessing
putative zinc-dependent deaminase motifs. Here, only CDD1, a cytidine deaminase,
is shown to have the capacity to carry out C-->U editing on a reporter mRNA.
This is only the second report of a cytidine deaminase that can use mRNA as a
substrate. CDD1-dependent editing was growth phase regulated and demonstrated
mooring sequence-dependent editing activity. Candidate yeast mRNA substrates
were identified based on their homology with the mooring sequence-containing
tripartite motif at the editing site of apoB mRNA and their ability to be edited
by ectopically expressed APOBEC-1. Naturally occurring yeast mRNAs edited to a
significant extent by CDD1 were, however, not detected. We propose that CDD1 be
designated an orphan C-->U editase until its native RNA substrate, if any, can
be identified and that it be added to the CDAR (cytidine deaminase acting on
RNA) family of editing enzymes.
