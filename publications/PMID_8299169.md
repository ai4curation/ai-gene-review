# Cloning and manipulation of the Schizosaccharomyces pombe his7+ gene as a new selectable marker for molecular genetic studies.
**Authors:** Apolinario E, Nocero M, Jin M, Hoffman CS
**Journal:** Curr Genet (1993)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00351711](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00351711)
**PMC:** [PMC4417482](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4417482/)

## Abstract

1. Curr Genet. 1993 Dec;24(6):491-5. doi: 10.1007/BF00351711.

Cloning and manipulation of the Schizosaccharomyces pombe his7+ gene as a new
selectable marker for molecular genetic studies.

Apolinario E(1), Nocero M, Jin M, Hoffman CS.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, Boston College, Chestnut Hill, MA 02167.

We have cloned the his7+ gene of the fission yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe by
complementation of the recessive mutant allele his7-366. The his7+ gene is able
to complement a mutation of the Escherichia coli hisI gene, suggesting that
his7+ encodes a phosphoribosyl-AMP cyclohydrase. Subcloning experiments localize
the gene to a 1.9-kb XbaI-BglII fragment. We describe the construction of
plasmids to facilitate the use of his7+ as a selectable marker in S. pombe
studies. Plasmid pEA2 carries his7+ cloned into the pUC18 polylinker. From
either pEA2 or the original his7+ clone, pMN1, fragments carrying his7+ can be
isolated using a variety of restriction enzymes for the construction of gene
disruptions. Plasmid pEA500 is a cloning vector that carries his7+ and ars1, yet
retains the ability to use the blue/white color screen to identify recombinants.
