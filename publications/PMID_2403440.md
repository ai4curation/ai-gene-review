## Abstract

Nonessential region of bacteriophage P4: DNA sequence, transcription, gene
products, and functions.

Ghisotti D(1), Finkel S, Halling C, Dehò G, Sironi G, Calendar R.

Author information:

Erratum in
    J Virol 1990 Apr;64(4):1878.

We sequenced the leftmost 2,640 base pairs of bacteriophage P4 DNA, thus
completing the sequence of the 11,627-base-pair P4 genome. The newly sequenced
region encodes three nonessential genes, which are called gop, beta, and cII (in
order, from left to right). The gop gene product kills Escherichia coli when the
beta protein is absent; the gop and beta genes are transcribed rightward from
the same promoter. The cII gene is transcribed leftward to a rho-independent
terminator. Mutation of this terminator creates a temperature-sensitive
phenotype, presumably owing to a defect in expression of the beta gene.
