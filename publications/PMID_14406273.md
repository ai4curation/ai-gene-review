## Abstract

1. Calif Med. 1959 Sep;91(3):149-50.

Recurrent carcinoma of the rectum, surgical treatment.

JACKSON BR, DANIEL WH.

Following operations on the rectum for carcinoma, approximately half of the
patients have recurrence in the perineum, pelvis, abdomen or at the suture line
of anastomosis. The prognosis is almost uniformly poor and although the problems
of management are complicated, dealing with them may give the patient worthwhile
physical, emotional and economic benefits. Surgical procedures used in the
treatment of the common types of recurrence are discussed.
