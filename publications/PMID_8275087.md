Comment in
    Nat Genet. 1993 Nov;5(3):209. doi: 10.1038/ng1193-209.

Mutations in the factor VIII gene have been discovered for barely more than half
of the examined cases of severe haemophilia A. To account for the unidentified
mutations, we propose a model based on the possibility of recombination between
homologous sequences located in intron 22 and upstream of the factor VIII gene.
Such a recombination would lead to an inversion of all intervening DNA and a
disruption of the gene. We present evidence to support this model and describe a
Southern blot assay that detects the inversion. These findings should be
valuable for genetic prediction of haemophilia A in approximately 45% of
families with severe disease.
