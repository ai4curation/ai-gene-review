A dynamic cycle exists in which haemoglobin is S-nitrosylated in the lung when
red blood cells are oxygenated, and the NO group is released during
arterial-venous transit. The vasoactivity of S-nitrosohaemoglobin is promoted by
the erythrocytic export of S-nitrosothiols. These findings highlight newly
discovered allosteric and electronic properties of haemoglobin that appear to be
involved in the control of blood pressure and which may facilitate efficient
delivery of oxygen to tissues. The role of S-nitrosohaemoglobin in the
transduction of NO-related activities may have therapeutic applications.
