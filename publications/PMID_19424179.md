# Novel crosstalk to BMP signalling: cGMP-dependent kinase I modulates BMP receptor and Smad activity.
**Authors:** Schwappacher R, Weiske J, Heining E, Ezerski V, Marom B, Henis YI, Huber O, Knaus P
**Journal:** EMBO J (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1038/emboj.2009.103](https://doi.org/10.1038/emboj.2009.103)

1. EMBO J. 2009 Jun 3;28(11):1537-50. doi: 10.1038/emboj.2009.103.

Novel crosstalk to BMP signalling: cGMP-dependent kinase I modulates BMP
receptor and Smad activity.

Schwappacher R(1), Weiske J, Heining E, Ezerski V, Marom B, Henis YI, Huber O,
Knaus P.

Author information:
(1)Institute for Chemistry/Biochemistry, FU Berlin, Berlin, Germany.

Integration of multiple signals into the canonical BMP/Smad pathway poses a big
challenge during the course of embryogenesis and tissue homeostasis. Here, we
show that cyclic guanosine 3',5'-monophosphate (cGMP)-dependent kinase I (cGKI)
modulates BMP receptors and Smads, providing a novel mechanism enhancing BMP
signalling. cGKI, a key mediator of vasodilation and hypertension diseases,
interacts with and phosphorylates the BMP type II receptor (BMPRII). In response
to BMP-2, cGKI then dissociates from the receptors, associates with activated
Smads, and undergoes nuclear translocation. In the nucleus, cGKI binds with
Smad1 and the general transcription factor TFII-I to promoters of BMP target
genes such as Id1 to enhance transcriptional activation. Accordingly, cGKI has a
dual function in BMP signalling: (1) it modulates BMP receptor/Smad activity at
the plasma membrane and (2) after redistribution to the nucleus, it further
regulates transcription as a nuclear co-factor for Smads. Consequently, cellular
defects caused by mutations in BMPRII, found in pulmonary arterial hypertension
patients, were compensated through cGKI, supporting the positive action of cGKI
on BMP-induced Smad signalling downstream of the receptors.
