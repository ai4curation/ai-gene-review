# The primary structure of the leu1+ gene of Schizosaccharomyces pombe.
**Authors:** Kikuchi Y, Kitazawa Y, Shimatake H, Yamamoto M
**Journal:** Curr Genet (1988)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00419995](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00419995)

## Abstract

1. Curr Genet. 1988 Oct;14(4):375-9. doi: 10.1007/BF00419995.

The primary structure of the leu1+ gene of Schizosaccharomyces pombe.

Kikuchi Y(1), Kitazawa Y, Shimatake H, Yamamoto M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Biology, Toho University, School of Medicine, Tokyo,
Japan.

A DNA fragment which carries the leu1 gene encoding beta-isopropylmalate
dehydrogenase in Schizosaccharomyces pombe has been isolated by complementation
of an E. coli leuB mutation. This 1.5 kb DNA fragment complements not only the
S. pombe leu1 mutation, but also the S. cerevisiae leu2 mutation. The nucleotide
sequence of the essential part of the leu1 gene and its flanking regions was
determined. This sequence contains an open reading frame of 371 codons, from
which a protein having a Mr = 39,732 can be predicted. The deduced amino acid
sequence and its codon usage were compared with those of the S. cerevisiae LEU2
protein. The cloned DNA will be a useful marker when transforming S. pombe.
