# MicroRNA-100 regulates osteogenic differentiation of human adipose-derived mesenchymal stem cells by targeting BMPR2.
**Authors:** Zeng Y, Qu X, Li H, Huang S, Wang S, Xu Q, Lin R, Han Q, Li J, Zhao RC
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2012)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2012.05.049](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2012.05.049)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2012 Jul 30;586(16):2375-81. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2012.05.049.
Epub  2012 Jun 7.

MicroRNA-100 regulates osteogenic differentiation of human adipose-derived
mesenchymal stem cells by targeting BMPR2.

Zeng Y(1), Qu X, Li H, Huang S, Wang S, Xu Q, Lin R, Han Q, Li J, Zhao RC.

Author information:
(1)Center of Excellence in Tissue Engineering, Chinese Academy of Medical
Sciences and Peking Union Medical College, Institute of Basic Medical Sciences
and School of Basic Medicine, Beijing, People's Republic of China.

Elucidation of the molecular mechanisms governing human adipose-derived
mesenchymal stem cells (hASCs) osteogenic differentiation is of great importance
for improving the treatment of bone-related diseases. In this study, we examined
the role of microRNA (miR)-100 on the osteogenesis of hASCs. Overexpression of
miR-100 inhibited osteogenic differentiation of hASCs in vitro, whereas
downregulation of miR-100 enhanced the process. Target prediction analysis and
dual luciferase report assay confirmed that bone morphogenetic protein receptor
type II (BMPR2) was a direct target of miR-100. Furthermore, knockdown of BMPR2
by RNA interference inhibited osteogenic differentiation of hASCs, similar as
the effect of upregulation miR-100. Taken together, our findings imply that
miR-100 plays a negative role in osteogenic differentiation and might act
through targeting BMPR2.
