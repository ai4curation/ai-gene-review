## Abstract

1. Science. 1992 Apr 3;256(5053):97-100. doi: 10.1126/science.1373520.

Molecular cloning of the interleukin-1 beta converting enzyme.

Cerretti DP(1), Kozlosky CJ, Mosley B, Nelson N, Van Ness K, Greenstreet TA,

Author information:
(1)Immunex Corporation, Seattle, WA 98101.

Interleukin-1 beta (IL-1 beta) mediates a wide range of immune and inflammatory
responses. The active cytokine is generated by proteolytic cleavage of an
inactive precursor. A complementary DNA encoding a protease that carries out
this cleavage has been cloned. Recombinant expression in COS-7 cells enabled the
cells to process precursor IL-1 beta to the mature form. Sequence analysis
indicated that the enzyme itself may undergo proteolytic processing. The gene
encoding the protease was mapped to chromosomal band 11q23, a site frequently
involved in rearrangement in human cancers.
