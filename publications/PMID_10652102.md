# Fus3 controls Ty1 transpositional dormancy through the invasive growth MAPK pathway.
**Authors:** Conte D Jr, Curcio MJ
**Journal:** Mol Microbiol (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1046/j.1365-2958.2000.01710.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-2958.2000.01710.x)

## Abstract

1. Mol Microbiol. 2000 Jan;35(2):415-27. doi: 10.1046/j.1365-2958.2000.01710.x.

Fus3 controls Ty1 transpositional dormancy through the invasive growth MAPK
pathway.

Conte D Jr(1), Curcio MJ.

Author information:
(1)Molecular Genetics Program, Wadsworth Center and School of Public Health,

Fus3, the mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) of the mating pheromone
response pathway, inhibits a post-translational step of Ty1 retrotransposition.
Fus3 also inhibits haploid invasive growth by blocking cross-activation of
invasive growth gene expression by the pheromone response signal cascade. Here,
we show that Fus3 kinase activity and dosage co-ordinately regulate Ty1
transposition and invasive growth. A chromosomal copy of the kinase-defective
fus3-K42R allele fails to inhibit either Ty1 transposition or invasive growth.
When overexpressed, kinase-defective Fus3 weakly inhibits both Ty1 transposition
and invasive growth, but is much less inhibitory than wild-type Fus3 expressed
at the same level. Moreover, increasing the dosage of wild-type Fus3 intensifies
the inhibition of both Ty1 transposition and invasive growth. To demonstrate
that Fus3 regulates Ty1 transposition via its negative regulation of the
invasive growth pathway, we show by epistatic analysis that the invasive growth
pathway transcription factors Ste12 and Tec1 are both required for Fus3-mediated
inhibition of Ty1 transposition. When haploid invasive growth is stimulated by
high-copy expression of TEC1, by expression of the dominant hypermorphic allele
STE11-4 or by deletion of HOG1, Ty1 transposition is concomitantly activated. In
summary, these results demonstrate that the haploid invasive growth pathway
activates Ty1 transposition at both transcriptional and post-transcriptional
levels and that Fus3 inhibits Ty1 transposition by inhibiting the invasive
growth pathway.
