# Prostaglandin F2alpha synthase activities of aldo-keto reductase 1B1, 1B3 and 1B7.
**Authors:** Kabututu Z, Manin M, Pointud JC, Maruyama T, Nagata N, Lambert S, Lefrançois-Martinez AM, Martinez A, Urade Y
**Journal:** J Biochem (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1093/jb/mvn152](https://doi.org/10.1093/jb/mvn152)

## Abstract

1. J Biochem. 2009 Feb;145(2):161-8. doi: 10.1093/jb/mvn152. Epub 2008 Nov 14.

Prostaglandin F2alpha synthase activities of aldo-keto reductase 1B1, 1B3 and
1B7.

Kabututu Z(1), Manin M, Pointud JC, Maruyama T, Nagata N, Lambert S,
Lefrançois-Martinez AM, Martinez A, Urade Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Behavioral Biology, Osaka Bioscience Institute, 6-2-4
Furuedai, Suita, Osaka 565-0874, Japan.

Here, we show that three enzymes belonging to the 1B group of the aldo-keto
reductase (AKR) superfamily, i.e., human placental aldose reductase (AKR1B1),
mouse kidney aldose reductase (AKR1B3) and mouse vas deferens protein (AKR1B7),
catalyse the reduction of prostaglandin (PG) H(2), a common intermediate of
various prostanoids, to form PGF(2alpha) in the presence of NADPH. AKR1B1,
AKR1B3 and AKR1B7 displayed higher affinities for PGH(2) (K(m) = 1.9, 9.3 and
3.8 microM, respectively) and V(max) values (26, 53 and 44 nmol/min/mg protein,
respectively) than did the human lung PGF(2alpha) synthase (AKR1C3; 18 microM
and 4 nmol/min/mg protein, respectively). The PGF(2alpha) synthase activity of
AKR1B1 and AKR1B3 was efficiently inhibited by two AKR inhibitors, tolrestat
(K(i) = 3.6 and 0.26 microM, respectively) and sorbinil (K(i) = 21.7 and 0.89
microM, respectively), in a non-competitive or mixed-type manner, whereas that
of AKR1B7 was not sensitive to these inhibitors (K(i) = 9.2 and 18 mM,
respectively). These data provide a molecular basis for investigating novel
functional roles for AKR1B members and PGF(2alpha) as mediators of physiological
and pathological processes in mammalian organisms.
