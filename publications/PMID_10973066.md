Author information:

The genes that drive heart-cell differentiation in vertebrates and Drosophila 
are similar, even though the Drosophila 'heart' is a simple tube and the 
vertebrate heart is a multichambered physiologically complex organ. Mutational 
analysis in mice and, as particular focus of this review, in zebrafish, reveals 
the additional genes brought into play to fashion these evolutionarily 'new' 
organotypic components.
