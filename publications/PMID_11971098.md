Giant axonal neuropathy (GAN) is an autosomal recessive neurologic disorder
clinically characterized by a severe polyneuropathy, CNS abnormalities, and
characteristic tightly curled hair. Recently, mutations in the gigaxonin gene
have been identified as the underlying genetic defect. The authors report two
novel mutations confirming that GAN is caused by mutations in the gigaxonin gene
and raise the question whether some mutations may cause a mild subclinical
neuropathy.
