Caenorhabditis elegans SAND-1 is essential for RAB-7 function in endosomal
traffic.

Poteryaev D(1), Fares H, Bowerman B, Spang A.

Author information:
(1)Biozentrum, University of Basel, Klingelbergstrasse, Basel, Switzerland.

The small rab-GTPase RAB-7 acts in endosome and endosome to lysosome traffic. We
identified SAND-1 as a protein required for RAB-7 function based on similarities
between SAND-1 and RAB-7 RNAi phenotypes. Although the initial uptake of yolk
protein in oocytes, or of soluble secreted (ss) GFP in coelomocytes, appeared
normal, further transport along the endocytic traffic route was delayed in the
absence of SAND-1 function, and yolk proteins failed to reach yolk granules
efficiently. Moreover, in coelomocytes, ssGFP and BSA-Texas-Red were endocytosed
but not transported to lysosomes. We show that SAND-1 is essential for RAB-7
function at the transition from early to late endosomes, but not for RAB-7
function at lysosomes.
