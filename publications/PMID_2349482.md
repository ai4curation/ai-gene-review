# Identification of mutations in the COL4A5 collagen gene in Alport syndrome.
**Authors:** Barker DF, Hostikka SL, Zhou J, Chow LT, Oliphant AR, Gerken SC, Gregory MC, Skolnick MH, Atkin CL, Tryggvason K
**Journal:** Science (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.2349482](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.2349482)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1990 Jun 8;248(4960):1224-7. doi: 10.1126/science.2349482.

Identification of mutations in the COL4A5 collagen gene in Alport syndrome.

Barker DF(1), Hostikka SL, Zhou J, Chow LT, Oliphant AR, Gerken SC, Gregory MC,
Skolnick MH, Atkin CL, Tryggvason K.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medical Informatics, University of Utah School of Medicine,

X-linked Alport syndrome is a hereditary glomerulonephritis in which progressive
loss of kidney function is often accompanied by progressive loss of hearing.
Ultrastructural defects in glomerular basement membranes (GBM) of Alport
syndrome patients implicate an altered structural protein as the cause of
nephritis. The product of COL4A5, the alpha 5(IV) collagen chain, is a specific
component of GBM within the kidney, and the gene maps to the same X chromosomal
region as does Alport syndrome. Three structural aberrations were found in
COL4A5, in intragenic deletion, a Pst I site variant, and an uncharacterized
abnormality, which appear to cause nephritis and deafness, with allele-specific
severity, in three Alport syndrome kindreds in Utah.
