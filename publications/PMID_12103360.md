# The identification and subcellular localization of human MRK.
**Authors:** Yang T, Jiang Y, Chen J
**Journal:** Biomol Eng (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1389-0344(02)00002-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1389-0344(02)00002-3)

## Abstract

1. Biomol Eng. 2002 Jun;19(1):1-4. doi: 10.1016/s1389-0344(02)00002-3.

The identification and subcellular localization of human MRK.

Yang T(1), Jiang Y, Chen J.

Author information:
(1)State Key Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Institute of Biochemistry and Cell
Biology, Shanghai Institutes for Biological Sciences, Chinese Academy of
Sciences, China.

A novel human kinase gene, human MRK (hMRK), was cloned by using degenerate
RT-PCR. The hMRK encoded a putative 632 amino acids protein and was highly
homologous to rat MRK (rMRK) in the entire coding region. The hMRK was located
at chromosome 6p12.3 by RH-PCR analysis. The hMRK was generally expressed a
single approximately 6.3 kb transcript at a low level in a variety of tissues
except at a high level in testis. The full-length hMRK protein was fused to
C-terminal of GFP and expressed in Hela cells. The fluorescence microscopy
results identified its nuclear localization.
