# The roles of HPr and FPr in the utilization of fructose by Escherichia coli.
**Authors:** Kornberg H
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1986)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0014-5793(86)80042-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(86)80042-4)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1986 Jan 1;194(1):12-5. doi: 10.1016/0014-5793(86)80042-4.

The roles of HPr and FPr in the utilization of fructose by Escherichia coli.

Kornberg H.

A mutant impaired in FPr activity was isolated. The altered gene (fpr), which
was located near min. 44 on the E. coli genome, was transferred by
phage-mediated transduction to appropriate recipients that lack HPr (ptsH), or
Enzyme IIman (ptsM), or neither. The rates of growth on fructose of such
transductants indicate that phosphate from PEP is transferred predominantly via
FPr to fructose that enters the cells by Enzyme IIfru, but that HPr can play a
role in transferring phosphate to fructose taken up via Enzyme IIman.
