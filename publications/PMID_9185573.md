# Characterization and crystallization of the helicase domain of bacteriophage T7 gene 4 protein.
**Authors:** Bird LE, Hâkansson K, Pan H, Wigley DB
**Journal:** Nucleic Acids Res (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1093/nar/25.13.2620](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/25.13.2620)

## Abstract

1. Nucleic Acids Res. 1997 Jul 1;25(13):2620-6. doi: 10.1093/nar/25.13.2620.

Characterization and crystallization of the helicase domain of bacteriophage T7
gene 4 protein.

Bird LE(1), Hâkansson K, Pan H, Wigley DB.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Molecular Biophysics, Rex Richards Building, University of
Oxford, South Parks Road, Oxford OX1 3QU, UK.

Limited proteolysis of bacteriophage T7 primase/helicase with endoproteinase
Glu-C produces several proteolytic fragments. One of these fragments, which is
derived from the C-terminal region of the protein, was prepared and shown to
retain helicase activity. This result supports a model in which the gene 4
proteins consist of functionally separable domains. Crystals of this C-terminal
fragment of the protein have been obtained that are suitable for X-ray
diffraction studies.
