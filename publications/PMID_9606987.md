Author information:
(1)Wyeth-Ayerst Research, Princeton, NJ 08543, USA. jonesp1@war.wyeth.com

Heterotrimeric G proteins integrate signals between receptors and effector
proteins. We have cloned the human beta 5 subunit from a human brain cDNA
library. The clone has a 1059 bp open reading frame and is highly homologous to
the murine clone. In contrast to the brain specific mouse beta 5, northern
analysis showed it to be expressed in multiple tissues.
