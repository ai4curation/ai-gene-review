# Evidence for the association of the human regulatory protein Ki-1/57 with the translational machinery.
**Authors:** Gonçalves Kde A, Bressan GC, Saito A, Morello LG, Zanchin NI, Kobarg J
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2011.07.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2011.07.010)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2011 Aug 19;585(16):2556-60. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2011.07.010.
Epub  2011 Jul 19.

Evidence for the association of the human regulatory protein Ki-1/57 with the
translational machinery.

Gonçalves Kde A(1), Bressan GC, Saito A, Morello LG, Zanchin NI, Kobarg J.

Author information:
(1)Laboratório Nacional de Biociências (LNBio), Centro Nacional de Pesquisa em
Energia e Materiais (CNPEM), Campinas, São Paulo, Brazil.

Ki-1/57 is a cytoplasmic and nuclear protein of 57 kDa first identified in
malignant cells from Hodgkin's lymphoma. Based on yeast-two hybrid protein
interaction we found out that Ki-1/57 interacts with adaptor protein RACK1
(receptor of activated kinase 1), CIRP (cold-inducible RNA-binding protein),
RPL38 (ribosomal protein L38) and FXR1 (fragile X mental retardation-related
protein 1). Since these proteins are involved in the regulation of translation
we suspected that Ki-1/57 may have a role in it. We show by immunoprecipitation
the association of Ki-1/57 with FMRP. Confocal microscopy revealed that Ki-1/57
colocalizes with FMRP/FXR1/2 to stress granules. Furthermore Ki-1/57 cosediments
with free ribosomal particles and enhances translation, when tethered to a
reporter mRNA, suggesting that Ki-1/57 may be involved in translational
regulation.
