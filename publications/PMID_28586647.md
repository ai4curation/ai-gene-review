Low inorganic phosphate (Pi) availability causes terminal differentiation of the
root apical meristem (RAM), a phenomenon known as root meristem exhaustion or
determined growth. Here, we report that the CLE14 peptide acts as a key player
in this process. Low Pi stress induces iron mobilization in the RAM through the
action of LPR1/LPR2, causing expression of CLE14 in the proximal meristem
region. CLV2 and PEPR2 receptors perceive CLE14 and trigger RAM differentiation,
with concomitant downregulation of SHR/SCR and PIN/AUXIN pathway. Our results
reveal multiple steps of the molecular mechanism of one of the most
physiologically important root nutrient responses.
