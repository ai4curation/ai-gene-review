# The Escherichia coli mismatch repair protein MutL recruits the Vsr and MutH endonucleases in response to DNA damage.

**DOI:** [10.1128/JB.00066-09](https://doi.org/10.1128/JB.00066-09)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 2009 Jun;191(12):4041-3. doi: 10.1128/JB.00066-09. Epub 2009 Apr
17.

The Escherichia coli mismatch repair protein MutL recruits the Vsr and MutH
endonucleases in response to DNA damage.

Polosina YY(1), Mui J, Pitsikas P, Cupples CG.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry and Microbiology, University of Victoria,
Victoria, BC, Canada.

The activities of the Vsr and MutH endonucleases of Escherichia coli are
stimulated by MutL. The interaction of MutL with each enzyme is enhanced in vivo
by 2-aminopurine treatment and by inactivation of the mutY gene. We hypothesize
that MutL recruits the endonucleases to sites of DNA damage.
