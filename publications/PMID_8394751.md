# Human FLT3/FLK2 gene: cDNA cloning and expression in hematopoietic cells.
**Authors:** Rosnet O, Schiff C, Pébusque MJ, Marchetto S, Tonnelle C, Toiron Y, Birg F, Birnbaum D
**Journal:** Blood (1993)

## Abstract

1. Blood. 1993 Aug 15;82(4):1110-9.

Human FLT3/FLK2 gene: cDNA cloning and expression in hematopoietic cells.

Rosnet O(1), Schiff C, Pébusque MJ, Marchetto S, Tonnelle C, Toiron Y, Birg F,
Birnbaum D.

Author information:
(1)Centre d'Immunologie de Marseille-Luminy, France.

The human FLT3 cDNA was cloned from a pre-B cell line and characterized. The
deduced amino acid sequence shows that FLT3 codes for a receptor-type tyrosine
kinase of 993 residues, presenting a strong similarity with the corresponding
mouse FLT3/FLK2 protein as well as with the receptors for colony-stimulating
factor 1 (CSF1R/FMS) and steel locus factor (SLFR/KIT). An analysis of the
expression of the gene using amplification of reverse transcribed FLT3 mRNA by
polymerase chain reaction shows that FLT3 is expressed in various
lymphohematopoietic cells and tissues, including a series of immature cell lines
and leukemias of lymphocytic origin.
