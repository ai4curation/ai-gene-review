The B.1.1.7 variant of SARS-CoV-2 first detected in the UK harbors amino-acid
substitutions and deletions in the spike protein that potentially enhance host
angiotensin conversion enzyme 2 (ACE2) receptor binding and viral immune
evasion. Here we report cryo-EM structures of the spike protein of B.1.1.7 in
the apo and ACE2-bound forms. The apo form showed one or two receptor-binding
domains (RBDs) in the open conformation, without populating the fully closed
state. All three RBDs were engaged in ACE2 binding. The B.1.1.7-specific A570D
mutation introduces a molecular switch that could modulate the opening and
closing of the RBD. The N501Y mutation introduces a π-π interaction that
enhances RBD binding to ACE2 and abolishes binding of a potent neutralizing
antibody (nAb). Cryo-EM also revealed how a cocktail of two nAbs simultaneously
bind to all three RBDs, and demonstrated the potency of the nAb cocktail to
neutralize different SARS-CoV-2 pseudovirus strains, including B.1.1.7.
