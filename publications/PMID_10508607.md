Author information:
(1)Wellcome/CRC Institute Department of Genetics Tennis Court Road, Cambridge,

In many cells, centrosomes are required to position nuclei at specific locations
in the cytoplasm. The nature of the link between centrosomes and nuclei is
mysterious, but the recently characterised UNC84 protein appears to be involved.
