## Abstract

1. Neurology. 2005 Aug 23;65(4):603-5. doi: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000178224.81169.c2.

Neuroferritinopathy: missense mutation in FTL causing early-onset bilateral

Author information:
(1)Life and Health Sciences Research Institute, School of Health Sciences,

pmaciel@ecsaude.uminho.pt

The authors identified a missense mutation in the FTL gene (474G>A; A96T) in a
19-year-old man with parkinsonism, ataxia, corticospinal signs, mild
nonprogressive cognitive deficit, and episodic psychosis. This mutation was also
present in his asymptomatic mother and younger brother, who had abnormally low
levels of ferritin in the serum. The patient and his mother displayed bilateral
involvement of the pallidum.
