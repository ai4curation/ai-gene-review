# A connexin 26 mutation causes a syndrome of sensorineural hearing loss and palmoplantar hyperkeratosis (MIM 148350).
**Authors:** Heathcote K, Syrris P, Carter ND, Patton MA
**Journal:** J Med Genet (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1136/jmg.37.1.50](https://doi.org/10.1136/jmg.37.1.50)
**PMC:** [PMC1734451](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1734451/)

## Abstract

1. J Med Genet. 2000 Jan;37(1):50-1. doi: 10.1136/jmg.37.1.50.

A connexin 26 mutation causes a syndrome of sensorineural hearing loss and
palmoplantar hyperkeratosis (MIM 148350).

Heathcote K(1), Syrris P, Carter ND, Patton MA.

Author information:
(1)Medical Genetics Unit, St George's Hospital Medical School, Tooting, London
SW17 0RE, UK.

We report a missense mutation in the connexin 26 gene (GJB2) in a family with an
autosomal dominant syndrome of hearing loss and hyperkeratosis. The affected
family members have high frequency, slowly progressive, bilateral, sensorineural
hearing loss and palmoplantar hyperkeratosis. The mutation causes an amino acid
substitution (G59A), which may disrupt a reverse turn in the first extracellular
loop of connexin 26. Connexin 26 mutations have been reported in syndromes of
deafness and palmoplantar keratoderma. These data provide additional evidence
for the role of connexin 26 in syndromes of this type.
