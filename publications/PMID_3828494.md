# Nucleotide sequence of a cDNA coding for mitochondrial fumarase from human liver.
**Authors:** Kinsella BT, Doonan S
**Journal:** Biosci Rep (1986)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF01116247](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01116247)

## Abstract

1. Biosci Rep. 1986 Oct;6(10):921-9. doi: 10.1007/BF01116247.

Nucleotide sequence of a cDNA coding for mitochondrial fumarase from human
liver.

Kinsella BT, Doonan S.

The nucleotide sequence of a 1.46 kb cDNA, selected from a human liver library
by the expression of fumarase antigenic determinants, was determined using the
dideoxy chain termination method. The cDNA contained an open reading frame
extending from the extreme 5'-base and coding for a protein with 468 amino
acids. This protein, with the exception of an N-terminal methionine, was
identified as mitochondrial fumarase. The protein showed a high degree of
identity of structure with the fumarase from Bacillus subtilis (56.6%) and a
fumarase from Escherichia coli (product of the fumC gene, 59.3%), and a lower
degree of identity with the aspartase of E. coli (37.2%).
