# Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors. Interaction of indapamide and related diuretics with 12 mammalian isozymes and X-ray crystallographic studies for the indapamide-isozyme II adduct.
**Authors:** Temperini C, Cecchi A, Scozzafava A, Supuran CT
**Journal:** Bioorg Med Chem Lett (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bmcl.2008.03.051](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmcl.2008.03.051)

## Abstract

1. Bioorg Med Chem Lett. 2008 Apr 15;18(8):2567-73. doi:
10.1016/j.bmcl.2008.03.051. Epub 2008 Mar 20.

Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors. Interaction of indapamide and related diuretics
with 12 mammalian isozymes and X-ray crystallographic studies for the
indapamide-isozyme II adduct.

Temperini C(1), Cecchi A, Scozzafava A, Supuran CT.

Author information:
(1)Università degli Studi di Firenze, Laboratorio di Chimica Bioinorganica, Room
188, Via della Lastruccia 3, I-50019 Sesto Fiorentino (Firenze), Italy.

Diuretics such as hydrochlorothiazide, hydroflumethiazide, quinethazone,
metolazone, chlorthalidone, indapamide, furosemide, and bumetanide containing
primary sulfamoyl moieties were reevaluated as inhibitors of 12 human carbonic
anhydrases (hCAs, EC 4.2.1.1). These drugs considerably inhibit (low nanomolar
range) some CA isozymes involved in critical physiologic processes, among the 16
present in vertebrates, for example, metolazone against CA VII, XII, and XIII,
chlorthalidone against CA VB, VII, IX, XII, and XIII, indapamide against CA VII,
IX, XII, and XIII, furosemide against CA I, II, and XIV, and bumetanide against
CA IX and XII. The X-ray crystal structure of the hCA II-indapamide adduct was
also resolved at high resolution.
