Author information:
(1)University of Utah Health Sciences Center, Salt Lake City, UT 84132, USA.

The twin-Cx(9)C motif protein Pet191 is essential for cytochrome c oxidase
maturation. The motif Cys residues are functionally important and appear to be
present in disulfide linkages within a large oligomeric complex associated with
the mitochondrial inner membrane. The import of Pet191 differs from that of
other twin-Cx(9)C motif class of proteins in being independent of the Mia40
pathway.
