# Location of three key enzymes of gluconeogenesis in baker's yeast.
**Authors:** Haarasilta S, Taskinen L
**Journal:** Arch Microbiol (1977)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00428597](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00428597)

## Abstract

1. Arch Microbiol. 1977 May 13;113(1-2):159-61. doi: 10.1007/BF00428597.

Location of three key enzymes of gluconeogenesis in baker's yeast.

Haarasilta S, Taskinen L.

The subcellular location of hexose diphosphatase, phosphoenolpyruvate
carboxykinase and pyruvate carboxylase in baker's yeast (Saccharomyces
cerevisiae) was investigated by density gradient centrifugation of spheroplast
lysates obtained by osmotic shock treatment of spheroplasts and centrifugation
for 10000 g x min. On the evidence obtained from zonal separations these three
enzymes of gluconeogenesis are most probably located in the soluble cytosol.
