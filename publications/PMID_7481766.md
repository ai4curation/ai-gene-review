# Orphanin FQ: a neuropeptide that activates an opioidlike G protein-coupled receptor.
**Authors:** Reinscheid RK, Nothacker HP, Bourson A, Ardati A, Henningsen RA, Bunzow JR, Grandy DK, Langen H, Monsma FJ Jr, Civelli O
**Journal:** Science (1995)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1995 Nov 3;270(5237):792-4. doi: 10.1126/science.270.5237.792.

Orphanin FQ: a neuropeptide that activates an opioidlike G protein-coupled
receptor.

Reinscheid RK(1), Nothacker HP, Bourson A, Ardati A, Henningsen RA, Bunzow JR,
Grandy DK, Langen H, Monsma FJ Jr, Civelli O.

Author information:
(1)Pharma Division, Hoffmann-La Roche AG, Basel, Switzerland.

A heptadecapeptide was identified and purified from porcine brain tissue as a
ligand for an orphan heterotrimeric GTP-binding protein (G protein)-coupled
receptor (LC132) that is similar in sequence to opioid receptors. This peptide,
orphanin FQ, has a primary structure reminiscent of that of opioid peptides.
Nanomolar concentrations of orphanin FQ inhibited forskolin-stimulated adenylyl
cyclase activity in cells transfected with LC132. This inhibitory activity was
not affected by the addition of opioid ligands, nor did the peptide activate
opioid receptors. Orphanin FQ bound to its receptor in a saturable manner and
with high affinity. When injected intracerebroventricularly into mice, orphanin
FQ caused a decrease in locomotor activity but did not induce analgesia in the
hot-plate test. However, the peptide produced hyperalgesia in the tail-flick
assay. Thus, orphanin FQ may act as a transmitter in the brain by modulating
nociceptive and locomotor behavior.
