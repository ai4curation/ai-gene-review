# Calcium tips the balance: a microtubule plus end to lattice binding switch operates in the carboxyl terminus of BPAG1n4.

Microtubules (MTs) are integral to numerous cellular functions, such as cell
adhesion, differentiation and intracellular transport. Their dynamics are
largely controlled by diverse MT-interacting proteins, but the signalling
mechanisms that regulate these interactions remain elusive. In this report, we
identify a rapid, calcium-regulated switch between MT plus end interaction and
lattice binding within the carboxyl terminus of BPAG1n4. This switch is EF-hand
dependent, and mutations of the EF-hands abolish this dynamic behaviour. Our
study thus uncovers a new, calcium-dependent regulatory mechanism for a
spectraplakin, BPAG1n4, at the MT plus end.
