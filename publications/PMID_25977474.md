F-BAR proteins are known to participate in cytokinesis, but their mechanisms are
not well understood. Here we investigated Rga7p, an Schizosaccharomyces pombe
F-BAR protein with a RhoGAP domain. Localization of Rga7p to the cytokinetic
cleavage furrow depends on its F-BAR domain, actin filaments, the formins Cdc12p
and For3p, and the presence of a contractile ring. Rga7p is not required for the
constriction of the contractile ring but does participate in the transport of a
β-glucan synthetase (Bgs4p) from the late Golgi compartments to plasma membrane
that is adjacent to the contractile ring. Cells without Rga7p moved Bgs4p
normally from the poles to the Golgi complex near to the cell center, but Bgs4p
then moved slowly from the late Golgi compartments to the cleavage site. The
late arrival and lower than normal numbers of Bgs4p result in septal defects
late in cytokinesis, and in the lysis of separating cells, similar to that in
cells with mutations in the cwg1(+) gene (which encodes Bgs4p).
