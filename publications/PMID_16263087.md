# The protein phosphatase-1 targeting subunit TIMAP regulates LAMR1 phosphorylation.
**Authors:** Kim K, Li L, Kozlowski K, Suh HS, Cao W, Ballermann BJ
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bbrc.2005.10.089](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2005.10.089)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2005 Dec 23;338(3):1327-34. doi:
10.1016/j.bbrc.2005.10.089. Epub 2005 Oct 25.

The protein phosphatase-1 targeting subunit TIMAP regulates LAMR1
phosphorylation.

Kim K(1), Li L, Kozlowski K, Suh HS, Cao W, Ballermann BJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medicine, The Albert Einstein College of Medicine, Bronx, NY,
USA.

TIMAP is a prenylated endothelial cell protein with a domain structure that
predicts it to be a protein phosphatase-1 (PP-1) regulatory subunit. We found
that TIMAP interacts with the 37/67 kDa laminin receptor (LAMR1) in yeast
two-hybrid assays. In endothelial cells, endogenous TIMAP and LAMR1
co-immunoprecipitated and co-localized at the plasma membrane. TIMAP amino acids
261-290, representing the fourth ankyrin repeat of TIMAP, are necessary and
sufficient for the interaction. In MDCK cells, lacking endogenous TIMAP,
overexpression of full-length TIMAP, but not TIMAP deleted in the fourth ankyrin
domain, allowed co-immunoprecipitation with LAMR1. PP-1 co-precipitated with
overexpressed and endogenous TIMAP in MDCK and endothelial cells, respectively.
In MDCK cells, PP-1 associated with LAMR1 in the presence, but not in the
absence, of TIMAP. LAMR1 was a substrate for PP-1 in vitro, and in MDCK cells
its phosphorylation was abrogated by expression of full-length TIMAP but not by
TIMAP deficient in the fourth ankyrin domain. Hence, TIMAP targets PP-1 to
LAMR1, and LAMR1 is a TIMAP-dependent PP-1 substrate.
