## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1990 Nov;172(11):6308-15. doi: 10.1128/jb.172.11.6308-6315.1990.

Cloning, mapping, and sequencing of the gene encoding Escherichia coli
quinoprotein glucose dehydrogenase.

Cleton-Jansen AM(1), Goosen N, Fayet O, van de Putte P.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Molecular Genetics, University of Leiden, The Netherlands.

Escherichia coli contains pyrroloquinoline quinone-dependent glucose
dehydrogenase. We cloned and sequenced the gene (gcd) encoding this enzyme and
showed that the derived amino acid sequence is highly homologous to that of the
gdhA gene product of Acinetobacter calcoaceticus. Stretches of homology also
exist between the amino acid sequence of E. coli glucose dehydrogenase and other
pyrroloquinoline quinone-dependent dehydrogenases from several bacterial
species. The position of gcd on the chromosomal map of E. coli was determined to
be at 3.1 min.
