Erratum in
    J Biol Chem. 2011 Aug 19;286(33):29442.

The Ccm cytochrome c maturation System I catalyzes covalent attachment of heme
to apocytochromes c in many bacterial species and some mitochondria. A covalent,
but transient, bond between heme and a conserved histidine in CcmE along with an
interaction between CcmH and the apocytochrome have been previously indicated as
core aspects of the Ccm system. Here, we show that in the Ccm system from
Desulfovibrio desulfuricans, no CcmH is required, and the holo-CcmE covalent
bond occurs via a cysteine residue. These observations call for reconsideration
of the accepted models of System I-mediated c-type cytochrome biogenesis.
