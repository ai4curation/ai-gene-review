# Calmodulin bridging of IQ motifs in myosin-V.
**Authors:** Martin SR, Bayley PM
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2004.04.053](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2004.04.053)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2004 Jun 4;567(2-3):166-70. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2004.04.053.

Calmodulin bridging of IQ motifs in myosin-V.

Martin SR(1), Bayley PM.

Author information:
(1)Division of Physical Biochemistry, National Institute for Medical Research,
Mill Hill, London NW7 1AA, UK.

Ca(2+)-saturated calmodulin binds to double-length IQ lever-arm sequences from
murine myosin-V, forming a 1:1 "bridging" complex with very high affinity,
(K9d)<10 pM for double motifs, IQ34, IQ45 and IQ56). Such a 1:1 complex involves
interaction of one calmodulin (CaM) molecule with two adjacent IQ-motifs,
providing a molecular mechanism for the observed Ca(2+)-dependent CaM
dissociation from the IQ-region. Structural considerations suggest that
formation of the 1:1 complex requires a severe distortion of the lever-arm,
potentially regulating functional motility. This would be consistent with a
recent report of diverse, irregular shapes of the lever arm of myosin-V induced
by the presence of Ca(2+).
