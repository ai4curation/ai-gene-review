# Wss1 is a SUMO-dependent isopeptidase that interacts genetically with the Slx5-Slx8 SUMO-targeted ubiquitin ligase.

Protein sumoylation plays an important but poorly understood role in controlling
genome integrity. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the Slx5-Slx8 SUMO-targeted Ub
ligase appears to be needed to ubiquitinate sumoylated proteins that arise in
the absence of the Sgs1 DNA helicase. WSS1, a high-copy-number suppressor of a
mutant SUMO, was implicated in this pathway because it shares phenotypes with
SLX5-SLX8 mutants, including a wss1Delta sgs1Delta synthetic-fitness defect.
Here we show that Wss1, a putative metalloprotease, physically binds SUMO and
displays in vitro isopeptidase activity on poly-SUMO chains. Like that of SLX5,
overexpression of WSS1 suppresses sgs1Delta slx5Delta lethality and the ulp1ts
growth defect. Interestingly, although Wss1 is relatively inactive on
ubiquitinated substrates and poly-Ub chains, it efficiently deubiquitinates a
Ub-SUMO isopeptide conjugate and a Ub-SUMO fusion protein. Wss1 was further
implicated in Ub metabolism on the basis of its physical association with
proteasomal subunits. The results suggest that Wss1 is a SUMO-dependent
isopeptidase that acts on sumoylated substrates as they undergo proteasomal
degradation.
