The basis of science is the hypothetico-deductive method and the recording of
experiments in sufficient detail to enable reproducibility. We report the
development of Robot Scientist "Adam," which advances the automation of both.
Adam has autonomously generated functional genomics hypotheses about the yeast
Saccharomyces cerevisiae and experimentally tested these hypotheses by using
laboratory automation. We have confirmed Adam's conclusions through manual
experiments. To describe Adam's research, we have developed an ontology and
logical language. The resulting formalization involves over 10,000 different
research units in a nested treelike structure, 10 levels deep, that relates the
6.6 million biomass measurements to their logical description. This
formalization describes how a machine contributed to scientific knowledge.
