# Identification of an HptB-mediated multi-step phosphorelay in Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1.

**DOI:** [10.1016/j.resmic.2005.06.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.resmic.2005.06.012)

## Abstract

1. Res Microbiol. 2006 Mar;157(2):169-75. doi: 10.1016/j.resmic.2005.06.012. Epub
 2005 Sep 7.

Identification of an HptB-mediated multi-step phosphorelay in Pseudomonas
aeruginosa PAO1.

Lin CT(1), Huang YJ, Chu PH, Hsu JL, Huang CH, Peng HL.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biological Science and Technology, National Chiao Tung
University, 75 Po Ai Street, Hsin Chu 30050, Taiwan, Republic of China.

We herein demonstrate that the hybrid sensor PA1611 carries out specific signal
transduction, through HptB (PA3345), to the response regulator PA3346 in
Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. As assessed by phenotypic changes in the hptB
deletion mutant, the pathway is likely to be involved in the regulation of
flagellar activity, the chemotaxis response, twitching motility, and biofilm
formation in the bacteria.
