# Bcl-2 is located predominantly in the inner membrane and crista of mitochondria in rat liver.
**Authors:** Motoyama S, Kitamura M, Saito S, Minamiya Y, Suzuki H, Saito R, Terada K, Ogawa J, Inaba H
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.1998.9205](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.1998.9205)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1998 Aug 28;249(3):628-36. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.1998.9205.

Bcl-2 is located predominantly in the inner membrane and crista of mitochondria
in rat liver.

Motoyama S(1), Kitamura M, Saito S, Minamiya Y, Suzuki H, Saito R, Terada K,
Ogawa J, Inaba H.

Author information:
(1)Second Department of Surgery, Akita University School of Medicine, Japan.

Bcl-2 is now recognized as a potent inhibitor of apoptotic cell death. It has
been reported that Bcl-2 is located in the mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum,
and nuclear membrane in some cell lines, and it is not expressed in normal human
and rat liver. An earlier study showed that Bcl-2 is an inner mitochondrial
membrane protein. On the contrary, the following investigations using
immunoelectron microscopy demonstrated that Bcl-2 resides predominantly in the
mitochondrial outer membrane. In this study, using a cryo-sectioning immunogold
labeling technique and immunoblotting, we carefully determined the subcellular
localization of Bcl-2. Here we report that Bcl-2 is expressed in normal rat
liver, and it is located predominantly in the inner membrane and crista rather
than in the outer membrane of mitochondria.
