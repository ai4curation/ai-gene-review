# The Escherichia coli biotin biosynthetic enzyme sequences predicted from the nucleotide sequence of the bio operon.
**Authors:** Otsuka AJ, Buoncristiani MR, Howard PK, Flamm J, Johnson C, Yamamoto R, Uchida K, Cook C, Ruppert J, Matsuzaki J
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1988)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1988 Dec 25;263(36):19577-85.

The Escherichia coli biotin biosynthetic enzyme sequences predicted from the
nucleotide sequence of the bio operon.

Otsuka AJ(1), Buoncristiani MR, Howard PK, Flamm J, Johnson C, Yamamoto R,
Uchida K, Cook C, Ruppert J, Matsuzaki J.

Author information:
(1)Department of Genetics, University of California, Berkeley 94720.

The nucleotide sequence of the biotin (bio) biosynthetic operon of Escherichia
coli has been determined. The 5.8-kilobase region contains the five biotin
operon genes, bioA, B, F, C, and D. and an open reading frame of unknown
function. The operon is negatively regulated and divergently transcribed from a
control region between the bioA and bioB genes. The product of the bioA gene,
7,8-diaminopelargonic acid aminotransferase, was discovered to be related to
ornithine aminotransferase. The product of the bioF gene,
7-keto-8-aminopelargonic acid synthetase, was found to be similar to
5-aminolevulinic acid synthetase.
