# TUT4 in concert with Lin28 suppresses microRNA biogenesis through pre-microRNA uridylation.
**Authors:** Heo I, Joo C, Kim YK, Ha M, Yoon MJ, Cho J, Yeom KH, Han J, Kim VN
**Journal:** Cell (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.cell.2009.08.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2009.08.002)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 2009 Aug 21;138(4):696-708. doi: 10.1016/j.cell.2009.08.002.

TUT4 in concert with Lin28 suppresses microRNA biogenesis through pre-microRNA
uridylation.

Heo I(1), Joo C, Kim YK, Ha M, Yoon MJ, Cho J, Yeom KH, Han J, Kim VN.

Author information:
(1)Creative Research Center and School of Biological Sciences, Seoul National
University, Seoul, Korea.

As key regulators in cellular functions, microRNAs (miRNAs) themselves need to
be tightly controlled. Lin28, a pluripotency factor, was reported to
downregulate let-7 miRNA by inducing uridylation of let-7 precursor (pre-let-7).
But the enzyme responsible for the uridylation remained unknown. Here we
identify a noncanonical poly (A) polymerase, TUTase4 (TUT4), as the uridylyl
transferase for pre-let-7. Lin28 recruits TUT4 to pre-let-7 by recognizing a
tetra-nucleotide sequence motif (GGAG) in the terminal loop. TUT4 in turn adds
an oligouridine tail to the pre-let-7, which blocks Dicer processing. Other
miRNAs with the same sequence motif (miR-107, -143, and -200c) are regulated
through the same mechanism. Knockdown of TUT4 and Lin28 reduces the level of
stem cell markers, suggesting that they are required for stem cell maintenance.
This study uncovers the role of TUT4 and Lin28 as specific suppressors of miRNA
biogenesis, which has implications for stem cell research and cancer biology.
