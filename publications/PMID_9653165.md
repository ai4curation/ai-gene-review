# The CC chemokine 6Ckine binds the CXC chemokine receptor CXCR3.

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1998 Jul 7;95(14):8205-10. doi:

The CC chemokine 6Ckine binds the CXC chemokine receptor CXCR3.

Soto H(1), Wang W, Strieter RM, Copeland NG, Gilbert DJ, Jenkins NA, Hedrick J,
Zlotnik A.

Author information:
(1)DNAX Research Institute for Cellular and Molecular Biology, Palo Alto, CA
94304, USA.

We cloned the mouse homologue of the chemokine receptor CXCR3, which is located
in mouse chromosome X. We screened a large panel of chemokines for their ability
to induce a calcium flux in mouse CXCR3-transfected cells and identified a new
ligand for this receptor, the recently reported CC chemokine 6Ckine. This
represents an example of a CC chemokine, which binds to a CXC chemokine
receptor. Like other ligands of this receptor, 6Ckine has angiostatic
properties. 6Ckine is known to chemoattract T cells. In line with this, CXCR3 is
expressed preferentially in Th1 cells and in lymphoid organs of the IL-10(-/-)
mouse that develops chronic colitis. Its ability to attract T cells as well as
its angiostatic properties suggest that 6Ckine may be an effective anti-tumor
agent.
