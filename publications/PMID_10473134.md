# Expression of the mouse Delta1 gene during organogenesis and fetal development.
**Authors:** Beckers J, Clark A, Wünsch K, Hrabé De Angelis M, Gossler A
**Journal:** Mech Dev (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0925-4773(99)00065-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0925-4773(99)00065-9)

## Abstract

1. Mech Dev. 1999 Jun;84(1-2):165-8. doi: 10.1016/s0925-4773(99)00065-9.

Expression of the mouse Delta1 gene during organogenesis and fetal development.

Beckers J(1), Clark A, Wünsch K, Hrabé De Angelis M, Gossler A.

Author information:
(1)The Jackson Laboratory, Bar Harbor, Maine 04609, USA.

Cell-to-cell communication mediated by the evolutionary conserved Notch
signalling pathway regulates cell fate decisions and patterning in various
tissues in diverse organisms (Artavanis-Tsakonas et al., 1995, Science 268,
225-232). Signalling between neighboring cells is transduced by binding of DSL
and Notch proteins which interact as ligand (DSL) and receptor (Notch). Mouse
Delta1 (delta-like 1; Dll1) encodes one of the four known mammalian DSL proteins
and is essential for normal somitogenesis and neuronal differentiation. Here, we
describe Delta1 expression during organogenesis and fetal development using the
highly sensitive histochemical detection of the lacZ gene product expressed from
a targeted Delta1:lacZ knock-in allele (Dll1(lacZ)). We find that Delta1 is
expressed in epithelial ducts of several organs, skeletal and smooth muscles,
the central nervous system, as well as some sensory epithelia.
