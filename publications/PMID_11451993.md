# Gamma subunit of the AP-1 adaptor complex binds clathrin: implications for cooperative binding in coated vesicle assembly.
**Authors:** Doray B, Kornfeld S
**Journal:** Mol Biol Cell (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1091/mbc.12.7.1925](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.12.7.1925)
**PMC:** [PMC55640](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC55640/)

## Abstract

1. Mol Biol Cell. 2001 Jul;12(7):1925-35. doi: 10.1091/mbc.12.7.1925.

Gamma subunit of the AP-1 adaptor complex binds clathrin: implications for
cooperative binding in coated vesicle assembly.

Doray B(1), Kornfeld S.

Author information:
(1)Washington University School of Medicine, Department of Internal Medicine,
St. Louis, Missouri 63110, USA.

The heterotetrameric AP-1 adaptor complex is involved in the assembly of
clathrin-coated vesicles originating from the trans-Golgi network (TGN). The
beta 1 subunit of AP-1 is known to contain a consensus clathrin binding
sequence, LLNLD (the so-called clathrin box motif), in its hinge segment through
which the beta chain interacts with the N-terminal domains of clathrin trimers.
Here, we report that the hinge region of the gamma subunit of human and mouse
AP-1 contains two copies of a new variant, LLDLL, of the clathrin box motif that
also bind to the terminal domain of the clathrin heavy chain. High-affinity
binding of the gamma hinge to clathrin trimers requires both LLDLL sequences to
be present and the spacing between them to be maintained. We also identify an
independent clathrin-binding site within the appendage domain of the gamma
subunit that interacts with a region of clathrin other than the N-terminal
domain. Clathrin polymerization is promoted by glutathione S-transferase
(GST)-gamma hinge, but not by GST-gamma appendage. However, the hinge and
appendage domains of gamma function in a cooperative manner to recruit and
polymerize clathrin, suggesting that clathrin lattice assembly at the TGN
involves multivalent binding of clathrin by the gamma and beta1 subunits of
AP-1.
