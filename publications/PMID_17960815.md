## Abstract

1. Mov Disord. 2008 Jan;23(1):118-22. doi: 10.1002/mds.21774.

Adult-onset Alexander disease with progressive ataxia and palatal tremor.

Howard KL(1), Hall DA, Moon M, Agarwal P, Newman E, Brenner M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology, University of Colorado at Denver Health Sciences
Center, Denver, Colorado 80262, USA. Katherine.Howard@UCHSC.edu

A novel glial fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP) mutation, Y257C, is reported in a
patient with adult-onset Alexander disease. This is the oldest reported case
with confirmation of a GFAP mutation. Onset was late in the sixth decade.
Genetic analysis of the GFAP gene is recommended in cases of progressive ataxia
and palatal tremor.
