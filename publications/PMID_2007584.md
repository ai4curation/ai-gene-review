# Selenocysteine synthase from Escherichia coli. Nucleotide sequence of the gene (selA) and purification of the protein.
**Authors:** Forchhammer K, Leinfelder W, Boesmiller K, Veprek B, Böck A
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1991)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1991 Apr 5;266(10):6318-23.

Selenocysteine synthase from Escherichia coli. Nucleotide sequence of the gene
(selA) and purification of the protein.

Forchhammer K(1), Leinfelder W, Boesmiller K, Veprek B, Böck A.

Author information:
(1)Lehrstuhl für Mikrobiologie, Universität München, Federal Republic of
Germany.

The nucleotide sequence of the selA gene from Escherichia coli whose product is
involved in the conversion of seryl-tRNA(Sec UCA) into selenocysteyl-tRNA(Sec
UCA) was determined. selA codes for a polypeptide of a calculated Mr of 50,667;
a protein of appropriate size was synthesized in vivo in a T7
promoter/polymerase system. An assay for SELA activity was devised which is
based on the seryl-tRNA(Sec UCA)-dependent incorporation of [75Se] selenium into
acid-insoluble material. It was used to follow SELA purification from cells that
overproduced the protein from a phage T7 promoter plasmid. Purified native SELA
protein migrates in gel filtration experiments with a native Mr of about
600,000. SELA contains 1 mol of bound pyridoxal 5-phosphate/mol of 50-kDa
subunit. Evidence is presented that the overall conversion of seryl-tRNA(Sec
UCA) to selenocysteyl-tRNA(Sec UCA) occurs at the SELA protein. SELA, therefore,
has the function of a selenocysteine synthase.
