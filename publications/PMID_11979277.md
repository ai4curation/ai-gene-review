# TREX is a conserved complex coupling transcription with messenger RNA export.
**Authors:** Strässer K, Masuda S, Mason P, Pfannstiel J, Oppizzi M, Rodriguez-Navarro S, Rondón AG, Aguilera A, Struhl K, Reed R, Hurt E
**Journal:** Nature (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1038/nature746](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature746)

## Abstract

1. Nature. 2002 May 16;417(6886):304-8. doi: 10.1038/nature746. Epub 2002 Apr 28.

TREX is a conserved complex coupling transcription with messenger RNA export.

Strässer K(1), Masuda S, Mason P, Pfannstiel J, Oppizzi M, Rodriguez-Navarro S,
Rondón AG, Aguilera A, Struhl K, Reed R, Hurt E.

Author information:
(1)BZH, University of Heidelberg, Im Neuenheimer Feld 328, D-69120 Heidelberg,
Germany.

The essential yeast proteins Yra1 and Sub2 are messenger RNA export factors that
have conserved counterparts in metazoans, designated Aly and UAP56,
respectively. These factors couple the machineries that function in splicing and
export of mRNA. Here we show that both Yra1 and Sub2 are stoichiometrically
associated with the heterotetrameric THO complex, which functions in
transcription in yeast. We also show that Sub2 and Yra1 interact genetically
with all four components of the THO complex (Tho2, Hpr1, Mft1 and Thp2).
Moreover, these components operate in the export of bulk poly(A)(+) RNA as well
as of mRNA derived from intronless genes. Both Aly and UAP56 associate with
human counterparts of the THO complex. Together, these data define a conserved
complex, designated the TREX ('transcription/export') complex. The TREX complex
is specifically recruited to activated genes during transcription and travels
the entire length of the gene with RNA polymerase II. Our data indicate that the
TREX complex has a conserved role in coupling transcription to mRNA export.
