# Regulation of G1 progression in fission yeast by the rum1+ gene product.
**Authors:** Martín-Castellanos C, Moreno S
**Journal:** Prog Cell Cycle Res (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1007/978-1-4615-5873-6_3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4615-5873-6_3)

## Abstract

1. Prog Cell Cycle Res. 1996;2:29-35. doi: 10.1007/978-1-4615-5873-6_3.

Regulation of G1 progression in fission yeast by the rum1+ gene product.

Martín-Castellanos C(1), Moreno S.

Author information:
(1)Departamento de Microbiología y Genética, CSIC/Universidad de Salamanca,
Spain.

Recently it has been found that B-type cyclins in fission yeast regulate the
activation of the cdc2 kinase to promote the onset of both DNA replication and
mitosis. cig2 is the major G1 cyclin while cdc13 is the principal mitotic
cyclin. cdc13 also has an additional function in G2 phase, preventing more than
one round of DNA replication per cell cycle. In opposition to these cyclins the
rum1 inhibitor, a protein present exclusively in G1, prevents premature
activation of the cdc2/cig2 and the cdc2/cdc13 complexes until cells have
reached the critical cell size required to pass Start and initiate a new cell
cycle.
