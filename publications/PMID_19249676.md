# MDM2-dependent downregulation of p21 and hnRNP K provides a switch between apoptosis and growth arrest induced by pharmacologically activated p53.

Erratum in

Comment in
    Cancer Cell. 2009 Mar 3;15(3):161-2. doi: 10.1016/j.ccr.2009.02.004.

We have previously identified the p53-reactivating compound RITA in a cell-based
screen. Here, using microarray analysis, we show that the global transcriptional
response of tumor cells to RITA is p53 dependent. Pathway analysis revealed
induction of the p53 apoptosis pathway, consistent with apoptosis being the
major response to RITA in cancer cells. We uncovered that MDM2 released from p53
by RITA promotes degradation of p21 and the p53 cofactor hnRNP K, required for
p21 transcription. Functional studies revealed MDM2-dependent inhibition of p21
as a key switch regulating cell fate decisions upon p53 reactivation. Our
results emphasize the utility of targeting wild-type p53 protein itself as a
promising approach for anticancer therapy.
