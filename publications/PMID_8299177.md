# Isolation and structure of an acetolactate synthase gene from Schizosaccharomyces pombe and complementation of the ilv2 mutation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
**Authors:** Bekkaoui F, Nadin-Davis SA, Crosby WL
**Journal:** Curr Genet (1993)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00351720](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00351720)

## Abstract

1. Curr Genet. 1993 Dec;24(6):544-7. doi: 10.1007/BF00351720.

Isolation and structure of an acetolactate synthase gene from
Schizosaccharomyces pombe and complementation of the ilv2 mutation in
Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Bekkaoui F(1), Nadin-Davis SA, Crosby WL.

Author information:
(1)Plant Biotechnology Institute, National Research Council of Canada,
Saskatoon.

A gene encoding a functional acetolactate synthase (ALS) subunit has been
isolated from the fission yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe, and has been
structurally and genetically characterized. The approximate 5-kbp cloned DNA
segment was found to contain a 2007-bp open reading frame capable of encoding a
669 aminoacid polypeptide which exhibited 57.1% similarity to the corresponding
ALS subunit from Saccharomyces cerevisiae. The putative ilv1 isolated from S.
pombe was shown to encode a functional subunit of acetolactate synthase by
complementation of an S. cerevisiae strain deleted for the ILV2 locus.
