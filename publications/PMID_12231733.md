mRNA encoding alcohol dehydrogenase (ADH) increases in etiolated seedlings and
leaves of Arabidopsis thaliana (L.) Heynh. upon exposure to low temperature. The
analysis of this response after water stress and abscisic acid (ABA) treatments
in Arabidopsis wild type and ABA-deficient and -insensitive mutants indicates
that cold accumulation of ADH mRNA could be induced by both anaerobic metabolism
and increase of ABA concentration resulting from low temperature exposure. By
using one Arabidopsis ADH null mutant, we show that ADH activity is not required
for successful development of freezing tolerance in this species.
