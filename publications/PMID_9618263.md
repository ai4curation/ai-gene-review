# Cloning and characterization of human Jak-2 kinase: high mRNA expression in immune cells and muscle tissue.
**Authors:** Saltzman A, Stone M, Franks C, Searfoss G, Munro R, Jaye M, Ivashchenko Y
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.1998.8685](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.1998.8685)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1998 May 29;246(3):627-33. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.1998.8685.

Cloning and characterization of human Jak-2 kinase: high mRNA expression in
immune cells and muscle tissue.

Saltzman A(1), Stone M, Franks C, Searfoss G, Munro R, Jaye M, Ivashchenko Y.

Author information:
(1)Gene Medicine Department, Rhône-Poulenc Rorer Central Research, Collegeville,
Pennsylvania 19426, USA.

We report on the cloning and sequence analysis of the mRNA coding for
full-length human Janus kinase 2 (Jak2). The human form of Jak2 is 1132 amino
acids in length with a M(r) of 131 KDa. It has 95% sequence similarity to pig
and rat Jak2. The highest level of mRNA expression was found in the spleen,
peripheral blood leukocytes, and testis. Also a significantly high level of Jak2
mRNA was found in heart and skeletal muscle. Northern blot analysis showed three
mRNA species in all tissues tested, except heart and skeletal muscle, of 7.6,
5.9, and 4.8 Kb. In skeletal muscle and heart, three mRNA species of 7.6, 4.8,
and 3.9 Kb were identified. The catalytic domain of the human Jak2 was expressed
and its specificity for phosphorylating peptide substrates derived from the
gp130, STAT, and Jak3 molecules was determined and compared to that for human
Jak1 and Jak3.
