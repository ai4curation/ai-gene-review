# Structure of Sindbis virus core protein reveals a chymotrypsin-like serine proteinase and the organization of the virion.

1. Nature. 1991 Nov 7;354(6348):37-43. doi: 10.1038/354037a0.

proteinase and the organization of the virion.

Comment in
    Nature. 1991 Nov 7;354(6348):22-3. doi: 10.1038/354022d0.

Sindbis virus consists of a nucleocapsid core surrounded by a lipid membrane
through which penetrate 80 glycoprotein trimers. The structure of the core
protein comprising the coat surrounding the genomic RNA has been determined. The
polypeptide fold from residue 114 to residue 264 is homologous to that of
chymotrypsin-like serine proteinases with catalytic residues His 141, Asp 163
and Ser 215 of the core protein positioned as in other serine proteinases. The
C-terminal tryptophan remains in the P1 substrate site subsequent to the
autocatalytic cis cleavage of the capsid protein, thus rendering the proteinase
inactive. Model building of the Sindbis core protein dimer shows that the
nucleocapsid is likely to have T = 4 quasisymmetry.
