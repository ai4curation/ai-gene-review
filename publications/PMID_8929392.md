# Cloning and characterization of the first two genes of the non-oxidative part of the Saccharomyces cerevisiae pentose-phosphate pathway.
**Authors:** Miosga T, Zimmermann FK
**Journal:** Curr Genet (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s002940050149](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002940050149)

## Abstract

1. Curr Genet. 1996 Nov;30(5):404-9. doi: 10.1007/s002940050149.

Cloning and characterization of the first two genes of the non-oxidative part of
the Saccharomyces cerevisiae pentose-phosphate pathway.

Miosga T(1), Zimmermann FK.

Author information:
(1)Institut für Mikrobiologie und Genetik, Technische Hochschule Darmstadt,
Schnittspahnstrasse 10, D-64287 Darmstadt, Germany.

We have cloned and characterized the two remaining unknown genes of the
non-oxidative part of the pentose-phosphate pathway of Saccharomyces cerevisiae
encoding the enzymes D-ribulose-5-phosphate 3-epimerase (Rpe1p) and
D-ribose-5-phosphate ketol-isomerase (Rki1p). Rpe1p has an unexpected high
specific activity of 2148 mU x (mg protein)-1 in crude extracts. Deletion
mutants of RPE1 show no enzyme activity and are unable to grow on D-xylulose.
Unexpectedly, haploid rki1 deletion mutants are not viable. Functional
expression of RKI1 was demonstrated following an increase of gene dosage in the
haploid rki1 deletion mutant, which restored viability and specific
D-ribose-5-phosphate ketol-isomerase activity. Both enzymes show high similarity
to the deduced protein sequences of various open reading frames, expressed
sequence tags or cDNAs from different organisms.
