# Cloning and sequencing of the gene encoding the large subunit of glutathione synthetase of Schizosaccharomyces pombe.
**Authors:** Mutoh N, Nakagawa CW, Ando S, Tanabe K, Hayashi Y
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0006-291x(05)81437-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0006-291x(05)81437-8)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1991 Nov 27;181(1):430-6. doi:
10.1016/s0006-291x(05)81437-8.

Cloning and sequencing of the gene encoding the large subunit of glutathione
synthetase of Schizosaccharomyces pombe.

Mutoh N(1), Nakagawa CW, Ando S, Tanabe K, Hayashi Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Genetics, Aichi Prefectural Colony, Japan.

The gene for the large subunit of glutathione synthetase (EC 6.3.2.3) of
Schizosaccharomyces pombe was cloned from a S. pombe genomic DNA library by
complementation of cadmium hypersensitivity of a glutathione synthetase
deficient mutant of S. pombe. A long open reading frame was found in the cloned
DNA sequence. Amino acid sequence predicted from the long open reading frame
coincided with amino acid sequences of peptides obtained by V8 protease
digestion of the large subunit of the purified glutathione synthetase. The
glutathione synthetase deficient mutant which harbored plasmids containing the
glutathione synthetase large subunit gene exhibited glutathione synthetase
activity higher than the activity in the wild type strain, though the plasmid
did not contain the gene for the small subunit of the enzyme.
