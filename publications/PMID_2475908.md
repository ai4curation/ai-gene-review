# Human KGF is FGF-related with properties of a paracrine effector of epithelial cell growth.
**Authors:** Finch PW, Rubin JS, Miki T, Ron D, Aaronson SA
**Journal:** Science (1989)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.2475908](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.2475908)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1989 Aug 18;245(4919):752-5. doi: 10.1126/science.2475908.

Human KGF is FGF-related with properties of a paracrine effector of epithelial
cell growth.

Finch PW(1), Rubin JS, Miki T, Ron D, Aaronson SA.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Cellular and Molecular Biology, National Cancer Institute,
Bethesda, MD 20892.

Keratinocyte growth factor (KGF) is a human mitogen that is specific for
epithelial cells. The complementary DNA sequence of KGF demonstrates that it is
a member of the fibroblast growth factor family. The KGF transcript was present
in stromal cells derived from epithelial tissues. By comparison with the
expression of other epithelial cell mitogens, only KGF, among known human growth
factors, has the properties of a stromal mediator of epithelial cell
proliferation.
