# MEMO associated with an ErbB2 receptor phosphopeptide reveals a new phosphotyrosine motif.
**Authors:** Feracci M, Pimentel C, Bornet O, Roche P, Salaun D, Badache A, Guerlesquin F
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2011.07.048](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2011.07.048)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2011 Sep 2;585(17):2688-92. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2011.07.048.
Epub  2011 Aug 10.

MEMO associated with an ErbB2 receptor phosphopeptide reveals a new
phosphotyrosine motif.

Feracci M(1), Pimentel C, Bornet O, Roche P, Salaun D, Badache A, Guerlesquin F.

Author information:
(1)UPR3243-CNRS, Marseille, France.

Tyrosine phosphorylations are essential in signal transduction. Recently, a new
type of phosphotyrosine binding protein, MEMO (Mediator of ErbB2-driven cell
motility), has been reported to bind specifically to an ErbB2-derived
phosphorylated peptide encompassing Tyr-1227 (PYD). Structural and functional
analyses of variants of this peptide revealed the minimum sequence required for
MEMO recognition. Using a docking approach we have generated a structural model
for MEMO/PYD complex and compare this new phosphotyrosine motif to SH2 and PTB
phosphotyrosine motives.
