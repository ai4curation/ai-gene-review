Autosomal dominant retinal vasculopathy with cerebral leukodystrophy is a
microvascular endotheliopathy with middle-age onset. In nine families, we
identified heterozygous C-terminal frameshift mutations in TREX1, which encodes
a 3'-5' exonuclease. These truncated proteins retain exonuclease activity but
lose normal perinuclear localization. These data have implications for the
maintenance of vascular integrity in the degenerative cerebral microangiopathies
leading to stroke and dementias.
