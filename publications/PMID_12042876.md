# Structural basis for the accessory protein recruitment by the gamma-adaptin ear domain.
**Authors:** Nogi T, Shiba Y, Kawasaki M, Shiba T, Matsugaki N, Igarashi N, Suzuki M, Kato R, Takatsu H, Nakayama K, Wakatsuki S
**Journal:** Nat Struct Biol (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1038/nsb808](https://doi.org/10.1038/nsb808)

## Abstract

1. Nat Struct Biol. 2002 Jul;9(7):527-31. doi: 10.1038/nsb808.

Structural basis for the accessory protein recruitment by the gamma-adaptin ear
domain.

Nogi T(1), Shiba Y, Kawasaki M, Shiba T, Matsugaki N, Igarashi N, Suzuki M, Kato
R, Takatsu H, Nakayama K, Wakatsuki S.

Author information:
(1)Photon Factory, Institute of Materials Structure Science, High Energy
Accelerator Research Organization (KEK), Tsukuba, Ibaraki 305-0801, Japan.

The adaptor proteins AP-1 and GGA regulate membrane traffic between the
trans-Golgi network (TGN) and endosomes/lysosomes through ARF-regulated membrane
association, recognition of sorting signals, and recruitment of clathrin and
accessory proteins. The gamma 1-adaptin subunits of AP-1 and GGA possess
homologous ear domains involved in the recruitment of accessory proteins,
gamma-synergin and Rabaptin-5. The crystal structure of the human gamma
1-adaptin ear domain consists solely of an immunoglobulin-like fold, unlike the
alpha-adaptin ear domain. Structure-based mutational analyses reveal a binding
site for the accessory proteins that is composed of conserved basic residues,
indicating that the recruitment mechanism in gamma 1-adaptin and GGA is distinct
from that in alpha-adaptin.
