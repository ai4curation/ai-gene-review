Fig. 1: The AcrIII-1 homologues SIRV2 gp37 and SIRV1 gp29 cannot inhibit subtype III-B CRISPR immunity when expressed from the viral genome, but exhibit anti-CRISPR activity when expressed from a plasmid.
From: Natively expressed AcrIII-1 does not function as an anti-CRISPR protein
a,c,e, Cell growth of CRISPR-III-B-positive cultures infected with the indicated viruses (multiplicity of infection (MOI) of 0.01). e,f, Plasmid-borne expression of AcrIII-1 homologues (p.gp37, p.gp29) exhibits type III-B anti-CRISPR activity. The control strain contains an empty plasmid. b,d,f, Extracellular virus titres in the cultures from a, c and e, respectively. Data are mean ± s.d. values from three biological replicates. h.p.i., hours post-infection; PFU, plaque-forming units.
