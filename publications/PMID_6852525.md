# Electrophoretic variation for X chromosome-linked hypoxanthine phosphoribosyl transferase (HPRT) in wild-derived mice.
**Authors:** Chapman VM, Kratzer PG, Quarantillo BA

**DOI:** [10.1093/genetics/103.4.785](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/103.4.785)

## Abstract

1. Genetics. 1983 Apr;103(4):785-95. doi: 10.1093/genetics/103.4.785.

Electrophoretic variation for X chromosome-linked hypoxanthine phosphoribosyl
transferase (HPRT) in wild-derived mice.

Chapman VM, Kratzer PG, Quarantillo BA.

An electrophoretic variation for hypoxanthine phosphoribosyltransferase, HPRT,
has been identified in samples of Mus spretus, a field mouse from southern
Europe and in M. m. castaneus, a house mouse from southeast Asia. These mice
will interbreed with laboratory mice to produce viable, fertile F1 progeny. The
variation for HPRT segregates as an X chromosome gene in F1 and backcross
progeny. Linkage analysis involving the markers Pgk-1 and Ags indicated a gene
order of centromere--Hprt--Pgk-1--Ags in crosses involving both stocks of wild
mice.
