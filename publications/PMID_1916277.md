# Sequences of two adjacent genes, one (DAL2) encoding allantoicase and another (DCG1) sensitive to nitrogen-catabolite repression in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
**Authors:** Yoo HS, Cooper TG
**Journal:** Gene (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0378-1119(91)90464-m](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-1119(91)90464-m)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 1991 Jul 31;104(1):55-62. doi: 10.1016/0378-1119(91)90464-m.

Sequences of two adjacent genes, one (DAL2) encoding allantoicase and another
(DCG1) sensitive to nitrogen-catabolite repression in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Yoo HS(1), Cooper TG.

Author information:
(1)Department of Microbiology and Immunology, University of Tennessee, Memphis
38163.

Reported are the nucleotide sequences of the yeast allantoicase-encoding gene
(DAL2) and that of an unknown gene adjacent to it. Expression of the
unidentified gene is sensitive to nitrogen catabolite repression (NCR) and
regulated by the DAL80 product, a previously documented control element
regulating allantoin pathway gene expression. Both genes possess multiple
upstream activation sequences (UAS) homologous to the UASNTR element shown to be
required for sensitivity to NCR. Also present upstream from DAL2 is a mutant
form of the upstream induction sequence required for response of DAL7 to
induction. Its occurrence in mutant form is consistent with the poor induction
of DAL2 expression observed in vivo.
