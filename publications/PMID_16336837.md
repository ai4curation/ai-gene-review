# GATA-3 expression in the development of anterior chamber associated immune deviation.
**Authors:** Fu T, Yang PZ, Huang XK, Huang Q, Zhou HY, Li B, Zhong HH, Chen X
**Journal:** Chin Med J (Engl) (2005)

## Abstract

1. Chin Med J (Engl). 2005 Dec 5;118(23):2000-4.

GATA-3 expression in the development of anterior chamber associated immune
deviation.

Fu T(1), Yang PZ, Huang XK, Huang Q, Zhou HY, Li B, Zhong HH, Chen X.

Author information:
(1)Uveitis Study Center, Sun Yat-sen University, Guangzhou 510060, China.

BACKGROUND: Anterior chamber associated immune deviation (ACAID) is
characterized by a Th2 cell response. GATA-3 has been shown to be necessary for
the activation of Th2 cells. This study was designed to examine the expression
of GATA-3 in the development of ACAID.
METHODS: ACAID was induced by injection of 50 microg interphotoreceptor retinoid
binding protein (IRBP) into the anterior chamber (AC) of Wistar rats.
Delayed-type hypersensitivity (DTH) was evaluated on day 3, 7, 14, 21, 28 after
IRBP inoculation. GATA-3 expression was detected using immunohistochemical
staining. The expression of GATA-3 mRNA at different time points after AC
injection of IRBP was assayed by reverse transcriptase polymerase chain reaction
(RT-PCR).
RESULTS: A significant DTH reaction was observed in Wistar rats on day 3 and 5
after IRBP inoculation. The DTH reaction was decreased 7 days after IRBP
inoculation. GATA-3 expression was weak at both mRNA and protein levels in the
normal spleen, but was significantly increased on day 5, 7, 14, and 21 after AC
injection of IRBP.
CONCLUSION: The expression of GATA-3 is increased during ACAID, suggesting that
GATA-3 may be involved in the development of ACAID.
