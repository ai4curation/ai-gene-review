## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1999 May;181(9):2984-6. doi: 10.1128/JB.181.9.2984-2986.1999.

Isolation and characterization of the Saccharomyces cerevisiae XPT1 gene
encoding xanthine phosphoribosyl transferase.

Guetsova ML(1), Crother TR, Taylor MW, Daignan-Fornier B.

Author information:
(1)Institut de Biochimie et Génétique Cellulaires, CNRS UPR9026, F-33077
Bordeaux Cedex, France.

A new Saccharomyces cerevisiae gene, XPT1, was isolated as a multicopy
suppressor of a hypoxanthine phosphoribosyl transferase (HPRT) defect.
Disruption of XPT1 affects xanthine utilization in vivo and results in a severe
reduction of xanthine phosphoribosyl transferase (XPRT) activity while HPRT is
unaffected. We conclude that XPT1 encodes XPRT in yeast.
