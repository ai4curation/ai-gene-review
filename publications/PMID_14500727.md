# Subdomain VIII is a specificity-determining region in MEKK1.

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2003 Nov 28;278(48):48498-505. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M304234200. Epub
2003 Sep 18.

Subdomain VIII is a specificity-determining region in MEKK1.

Tu Z(1), Lee FS.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine, University of Pennsylvania
School of Medicine, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19104, USA.

MAPK/ERK kinase kinase 1 (MEKK1) is a mitogenactivated protein kinase kinase
kinase (MAP3K) of the stress-induced JNK pathway. Once activated, MEKK1
phosphorylates the MAP2K MKK4, which in turn phosphorylates JNK. MEKK1 also has
the capacity to activate IKK, the central protein kinase of the NF-kappa B
pathway. The molecular determinants responsible for the ability of MEKK1 to
recognize specific substrates are poorly understood. We report here that select
point mutations in subdomain VIII of the protein kinase domain of MEKK1 (MEKK1
Delta) differentially affect its ability to activate MKK4 and IKK, and
consequently AP1 and NF-kappa B reporter genes. Moreover, binding of MKK4 to
MEKK1 Delta protects the latter from cleavage at an engineered protease target
site in subdomain VIII. Collectively these results provide evidence that
subdomain VIII of MEKK1 is involved not only in binding to, but also in
discrimination of, protein substrates.
