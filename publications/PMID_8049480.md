# puckered, a gene involved in position-specific cell differentiation in the dorsal epidermis of the Drosophila larva.
**Authors:** Ring JM, Martinez Arias A
**Journal:** Dev Suppl (1993)

## Abstract

1. Dev Suppl. 1993:251-9.

puckered, a gene involved in position-specific cell differentiation in the
dorsal epidermis of the Drosophila larva.

Ring JM(1), Martinez Arias A.

Author information:
(1)Department of Zoology, University of Cambridge, UK.

The final pattern of the cuticle of the Drosophila larva depends on the
position-specific behaviour of the epidermal cells during their differentiation.
This behaviour is dictated, in part, by the relative position of the cells
during embryogenesis which allows them to receive and integrate signals from
their neighbours. The translation of this 'positional information' into pattern
might depend on the activity of genes that are able to integrate the outcome of
cell interactions and transfer it to the genes responsible for cell
differentiation. Mutations in the gene puckered cause spatially restricted
defects during the differentiation of the larval epidermal cells. We present
data that suggests puckered may be involved in linking positional information to
cell differentiation.
