# Human pancreatic triglyceride lipase expressed in yeast cells: purification and characterization.
**Authors:** Yang Y, Lowe ME
**Journal:** Protein Expr Purif (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1006/prep.1998.0874](https://doi.org/10.1006/prep.1998.0874)

## Abstract

1. Protein Expr Purif. 1998 Jun;13(1):36-40. doi: 10.1006/prep.1998.0874.

Human pancreatic triglyceride lipase expressed in yeast cells: purification and
characterization.

Yang Y(1), Lowe ME.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics, Washington University School of Medicine, St.
Louis, Missouri 63110, USA.

A cDNA clone encoding human pancreatic triglyceride lipase was cloned into a
yeast expression vector so that the yeast PHO1 signal peptide replaced the
native signal peptide. Pichia pastoris cells were transfected with the vector,
and clones expressing human pancreatic triglyceride lipase were isolated.
Recombinant human pancreatic lipase was expressed in broth cultures and was
purified from the medium by DEAE blue Sepharose and hydroxyapatite
chromatography. The highly purified lipase had specific activities for various
triglyceride substrates identical to those of tissue-purified human pancreatic
triglyceride lipase; it was inhibited by bile salts, required colipase for
activity, and demonstrated interfacial activation. This expression system is
suitable for the rapid, efficient production of human pancreatic triglyceride
lipase in amounts adequate for biophysical studies.
