# Impaired complex I assembly in a Leigh syndrome patient with a novel missense mutation in the ND6 gene.
**Authors:** Ugalde C, Triepels RH, Coenen MJ, van den Heuvel LP, Smeets R, Uusimaa J, Briones P, Campistol J, Majamaa K, Smeitink JA, Nijtmans LG
**Journal:** Ann Neurol (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1002/ana.10734](https://doi.org/10.1002/ana.10734)

## Abstract

1. Ann Neurol. 2003 Nov;54(5):665-9. doi: 10.1002/ana.10734.

Impaired complex I assembly in a Leigh syndrome patient with a novel missense
mutation in the ND6 gene.

Ugalde C(1), Triepels RH, Coenen MJ, van den Heuvel LP, Smeets R, Uusimaa J,
Briones P, Campistol J, Majamaa K, Smeitink JA, Nijtmans LG.

Author information:
(1)Nijmegen Center for Mitochondrial Disorders, Department of Pediatrics,
University Medical Center Nijmegen, PO Box 9101, 6500 HB Nijmegen, The
Netherlands. c.ugalde@cukz.umcn.nl

We describe a novel mutation in the ND6 gene (T14487C) in a patient with Leigh
syndrome. Biochemical analyses indicated a low complex I activity in the
patient's fibroblasts but normal values in muscle and liver. Cybrid clones
showed a specific complex I defect that correlates with the mutant heteroplasmy
levels. Additionally, we demonstrate an altered mobility and a decrease in the
levels of fully assembled complex I in the patient's fibroblasts and cybrids,
suggesting that the mutation has a profound effect on complex I assembly and/or
stability.
