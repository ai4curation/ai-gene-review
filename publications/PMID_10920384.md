Ecdysteroids trigger major developmental transitions in Drosophila, including
larval molts and metamorphosis. Recent genetic studies strongly support a role
for the Ecdysteroid receptor (EcR)/Ultraspiracle (USP) heterodimer as an
ecdysteroid receptor at the onset of metamorphosis, functioning as both a
transcriptional activator and repressor in vivo. Genetic analysis also indicates
that USP, like its vertebrate homolog retinoid X receptor (RXR), might be
involved in regulatory pathways independently of EcR. The ecdysteroid hierarchy
was also shown recently to regulate Drosophila oogenesis and reproduction.
