# Toll-like receptor 8-mediated reversal of CD4+ regulatory T cell function.
**Authors:** Peng G, Guo Z, Kiniwa Y, Voo KS, Peng W, Fu T, Wang DY, Li Y, Wang HY, Wang RF
**Journal:** Science (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.1113401](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1113401)

## Abstract

1. Science. 2005 Aug 26;309(5739):1380-4. doi: 10.1126/science.1113401.

Toll-like receptor 8-mediated reversal of CD4+ regulatory T cell function.

Peng G(1), Guo Z, Kiniwa Y, Voo KS, Peng W, Fu T, Wang DY, Li Y, Wang HY, Wang
RF.

Author information:
(1)Center for Cell and Gene Therapy and Department of Immunology, Baylor College
of Medicine, Houston, TX 77030, USA.

CD4+ regulatory T (Treg) cells have a profound ability to suppress host immune
responses, yet little is understood about how these cells are regulated. We
describe a mechanism linking Toll-like receptor (TLR) 8 signaling to the control
of Treg cell function, in which synthetic and natural ligands for human TLR8 can
reverse Treg cell function. This effect was independent of dendritic cells but
required functional TLR8-MyD88-IRAK4 signaling in Treg cells. Adoptive transfer
of TLR8 ligand-stimulated Treg cells into tumor-bearing mice enhanced anti-tumor
immunity. These results suggest that TLR8 signaling could play a critical role
in controlling immune responses to cancer and other diseases.
