# Neural stem cell differentiation is dependent upon endogenous caspase 3 activity.
**Authors:** Fernando P, Brunette S, Megeney LA
**Journal:** FASEB J (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1096/fj.04-2981fje](https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.04-2981fje)

## Abstract

1. FASEB J. 2005 Oct;19(12):1671-3. doi: 10.1096/fj.04-2981fje. Epub 2005 Aug 15.

Neural stem cell differentiation is dependent upon endogenous caspase 3
activity.

Fernando P(1), Brunette S, Megeney LA.

Author information:
(1)Ottawa Health Research Institute, Molecular Medicine Program, Ottawa,
Ontario, Canada.

Caspase proteases have become the focal point for the development and
application of anti-apoptotic therapies in a variety of central nervous system
diseases. However, this approach is based on the premise that caspase function
is limited to invoking cell death signals. Here, we show that caspase-3 activity
is elevated in nonapoptotic differentiating neuronal cell populations. Moreover,
peptide inhibition of protease activity effectively inhibits the differentiation
process in a cultured neurosphere model. These results implicate caspase-3
activation as a conserved feature of neuronal differentiation and suggest that
targeted inhibition of this protease in neural cell populations may have
unintended consequences.
