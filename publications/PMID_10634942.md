# A second Escherichia coli protein with CL synthase activity.

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 2000 Jan 17;1483(2):263-74. doi:

A second Escherichia coli protein with CL synthase activity.

Guo D(1), Tropp BE.

Author information:
(1)Queens College CUNY, Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry, 65-30 Kissena
Boulevard, Flushing, NY 11367, USA.

The Escherichia coli open reading frame f413, which has the potential to code
for a polypeptide homologous to cardiolipin (CL) synthase, has been cloned. Its
polypeptide product has a molecular mass of 48 kDa, is membrane-bound, and
catalyzes CL formation but does not hydrolyze CL. A comparison of the sequences
predicted for the polypeptides encoded by f413 and cls indicates that the
N-terminal residues specified by cls may be unnecessary for CL synthase
activity. Construction of a truncated cls gene and characterization of its
polypeptide product have confirmed this conclusion.
