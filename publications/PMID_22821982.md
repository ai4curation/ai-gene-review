Comment in

Host protection from infection relies on the recognition of pathogens by innate
pattern-recognition receptors such as Toll-like receptors (TLRs). Here, we show
that the orphan receptor TLR13 in mice recognizes a conserved 23S ribosomal RNA
(rRNA) sequence that is the binding site of macrolide, lincosamide, and
streptogramin group (MLS) antibiotics (including erythromycin) in bacteria.
Notably, 23S rRNA from clinical isolates of erythromycin-resistant
Staphylococcus aureus and synthetic oligoribonucleotides carrying methylated
adenosine or a guanosine mimicking a MLS resistance-causing modification failed
to stimulate TLR13. Thus, our results reveal both a natural TLR13 ligand and
specific mechanisms of antibiotic resistance as potent bacterial immune evasion
strategy, avoiding recognition via TLR13.
