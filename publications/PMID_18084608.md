# RNA interference of interferon regulatory factor-1 gene expression in THP-1 cell line leads to Toll-like receptor-4 overexpression/activation as well as up-modulation of annexin-II.
**Authors:** Maratheftis CI, Giannouli S, Spachidou MP, Panayotou G, Voulgarelis M
**Journal:** Neoplasia (2007)
**DOI:** [10.1593/neo.07640](https://doi.org/10.1593/neo.07640)
**PMC:** [PMC2134898](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2134898/)

## Abstract

1. Neoplasia. 2007 Dec;9(12):1012-20. doi: 10.1593/neo.07640.

RNA interference of interferon regulatory factor-1 gene expression in THP-1 cell
line leads to Toll-like receptor-4 overexpression/activation as well as
up-modulation of annexin-II.

Maratheftis CI(1), Giannouli S, Spachidou MP, Panayotou G, Voulgarelis M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathophysiology, School of Medicine, National University of
Athens, Athens, Greece.

Interferon regulatory factor-1 (IRF-1) is a candidate transcription factor for
the regulation of the Toll-like receptor-4 (TLR-4) gene. Using a small
interfering RNA-based (siRNA) process to silence IRF-1 gene expression in the
leukemic monocytic cell line THP-1, we investigated whether such a modulation
would alter TLR-4 expression and activation status in these cells. The siIRF-1
cells expressed elevated levels of TLR-4 mRNA and protein compared to controls
by 90% and 77%, respectively. ICAM.1 protein expression and apoptosis levels
were increased by 8.35- and 4.25-fold, respectively. The siIRF-1 cells
overexpressed Bax mRNA compared to controls. Proteomic analysis revealed
upmodulation of the Annexin-II protein in siIRF-1 THP-1 cells. Myelodysplastic
syndrome (MDS) patients with an absence of full-length IRF-1 mRNA also
overexpressed Annexin-II. It is plausible that this overexpression may lead to
the activation of TLR-4 contributing to the increased apoptosis characterizing
MDS.
