# Immunolocalization of humanin in human sperm and testis.
**Authors:** Moretti E, Giannerini V, Rossini L, Matsuoka M, Trabalzini L, Collodel G
**Journal:** Fertil Steril (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.fertnstert.2010.04.075](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fertnstert.2010.04.075)

## Abstract

1. Fertil Steril. 2010 Dec;94(7):2888-90. doi: 10.1016/j.fertnstert.2010.04.075.
Epub 2010 Jun 9.

Immunolocalization of humanin in human sperm and testis.

Moretti E(1), Giannerini V, Rossini L, Matsuoka M, Trabalzini L, Collodel G.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biomedical Sciences, University of Siena, Siena, Italy.
moretti@unisi.it

We have discovered, by immunocytochemistry and immunoelectronmicroscopy, that
humanin (HN) is expressed in human ejaculated sperm and testis. In sperm, the HN
immunolabeling pattern depends on sperm morphology; in particular, HN is mainly
localized in the midpiece of sperm in semen samples with normal morphology and
in cytoplasmic residues and entire tail in those with abnormal morphology. We
also found HN in the cytoplasm and nucleus of spermatocytes and spermatids and
in experimentally uncoiled chromatin of mature ejaculated sperm. Because it has
been established that HN has antiapoptotic properties, it is reasonably
hypothesized that HN may play an important role in preventing apoptosis in human
sperm and testis. Thus, the examination of the HN localization in normal and
abnormal sperm could be proposed as an auxiliary test to better define sperm
quality.
