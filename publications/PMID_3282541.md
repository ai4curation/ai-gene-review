# Overexpression, purification and properties of alcohol dehydrogenase IV from Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
**Authors:** Drewke C, Ciriacy M
**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (1988)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0167-4781(88)90072-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-4781(88)90072-3)

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 1988 May 6;950(1):54-60. doi:
10.1016/0167-4781(88)90072-3.

Overexpression, purification and properties of alcohol dehydrogenase IV from
Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Drewke C(1), Ciriacy M.

Author information:
(1)Institut für Mikrobiologie, Universität Düsseldorf, F.R.G.

We have purified ADHIV, a novel alcohol dehydrogenase (ADH) isozyme in the yeast
Saccharomyces cerevisiae, after increasing the normally low amount of ADHIV
protein in laboratory strains. This was done by overexpression of the structural
gene (ADH4) on a 2micro-based multicopy vector. Characterization of the purified
enzyme revealed a dimeric structure as well as a different substrate specificity
and pH profile as compared to other alcohol dehydrogenase isozymes. On the other
hand, we could demonstrate that ADHIV is activated by zinc ions, like the other
yeast alcohol dehydrogenase isozymes, and not by ferrous ions, like a
structurally similar alcohol dehydrogenase from the bacterium Zymomonas mobilis.
