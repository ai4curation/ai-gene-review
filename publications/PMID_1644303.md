# Cloning, sequencing and heterologous expression of a Klebsiella pneumoniae gene encoding an FAD-independent acetolactate synthase.
**Authors:** Peng HL, Wang PY, Wu CM, Hwang DC, Chang HY
**Journal:** Gene (1992)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0378-1119(92)90500-o](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-1119(92)90500-o)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 1992 Aug 1;117(1):125-30. doi: 10.1016/0378-1119(92)90500-o.

Cloning, sequencing and heterologous expression of a Klebsiella pneumoniae gene
encoding an FAD-independent acetolactate synthase.

Peng HL(1), Wang PY, Wu CM, Hwang DC, Chang HY.

Author information:
(1)Department of Microbiology and Immunology, Chang-Gung Medical College,
Taiwan, ROC.

The gene encoding the valine-resistant and FAD-independent acetolactate synthase
of Klebsiella pneumoniae was isolated and expressed in Escherichia coli. The
nucleotide sequence of this gene was determined and it exhibited an open reading
frame of 1680 bp in length. In vivo expression of the acetolactate
synthase-encoding gene in E. coli revealed a single 60-kDa protein which is
consistent with the molecular weight calculated from the deduced amino acid
sequence of the gene product. The gene product shares about 20-30% homology with
the acetolactate synthases of E. coli, yeast and higher plants.
