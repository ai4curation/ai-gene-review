# Serine 62 is a phosphorylation site in folliculin, the Birt-Hogg-Dubé gene product.
**Authors:** Wang L, Kobayashi T, Piao X, Shiono M, Takagi Y, Mineki R, Taka H, Zhang D, Abe M, Sun G, Hagiwara Y, Okimoto K, Matsumoto I, Kouchi M, Hino O
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2009.11.033](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2009.11.033)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2010 Jan 4;584(1):39-43. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2009.11.033.

Serine 62 is a phosphorylation site in folliculin, the Birt-Hogg-Dubé gene
product.

Wang L(1), Kobayashi T, Piao X, Shiono M, Takagi Y, Mineki R, Taka H, Zhang D,
Abe M, Sun G, Hagiwara Y, Okimoto K, Matsumoto I, Kouchi M, Hino O.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology and Oncology, Juntendo University School of Medicine,
Tokyo, Japan.

Recently, it was reported that the product of Birt-Hogg-Dubé syndrome gene
(folliculin, FLCN) is directly phosphorylated by 5'-AMP-activated protein kinase
(AMPK). In this study, we identified serine 62 (Ser62) as a phosphorylation site
in FLCN and generated an anti-phospho-Ser62-FLCN antibody. Our analysis suggests
that Ser62 phosphorylation is indirectly up-regulated by AMPK and that another
residue is directly phosphorylated by AMPK. By binding with FLCN-interacting
proteins (FNIP1 and FNIP2/FNIPL), Ser62 phosphorylation is increased. A
phospho-mimic mutation at Ser62 enhanced the formation of the FLCN-AMPK complex.
These results suggest that function(s) of FLCN-AMPK-FNIP complex is regulated by
Ser62 phosphorylation.
