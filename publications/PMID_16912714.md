Since the discovery of SNARE proteins in the late 1980s, SNAREs have been
recognized as key components of protein complexes that drive membrane fusion.
Despite considerable sequence divergence among SNARE proteins, their mechanism
seems to be conserved and is adaptable for fusion reactions as diverse as those
involved in cell growth, membrane repair, cytokinesis and synaptic transmission.
A fascinating picture of these robust nanomachines is emerging.
