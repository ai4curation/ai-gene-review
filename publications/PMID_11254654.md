# BMK1 mediates growth factor-induced cell proliferation through direct cellular activation of serum and glucocorticoid-inducible kinase.
**Authors:** Hayashi M, Tapping RI, Chao TH, Lo JF, King CC, Yang Y, Lee JD
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.C000838200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.C000838200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2001 Mar 23;276(12):8631-4. doi: 10.1074/jbc.C000838200. Epub
2001  Jan 31.

BMK1 mediates growth factor-induced cell proliferation through direct cellular
activation of serum and glucocorticoid-inducible kinase.

Hayashi M(1), Tapping RI, Chao TH, Lo JF, King CC, Yang Y, Lee JD.

Author information:
(1)Department of Immunology, The Scripps Research Institute, La Jolla,
California 92037, USA.

Activation of the mammalian mitogen-activated protein kinase known as BMK1 is
required for growth factor-induced cell proliferation. To understand the
mechanism by which BMK1 mediates this cellular response, this kinase was used as
bait in a yeast two-hybrid-based library screening. Here, we report the
identification of serum and glucocorticoid-inducible kinase (SGK) as a cellular
protein that physically interacts with BMK1. During growth factor-induced cell
stimulation, BMK1 activates SGK by phosphorylation at serine 78. This
BMK1-mediated phosphorylation event is necessary for the activation of SGK and,
more importantly, for cell proliferation induced by growth factors.
