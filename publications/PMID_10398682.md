# Crystal structure of the conserved core of the herpes simplex virus transcriptional regulatory protein VP16.
**Authors:** Liu Y, Gong W, Huang CC, Herr W, Cheng X
**Journal:** Genes Dev (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1101/gad.13.13.1692](https://doi.org/10.1101/gad.13.13.1692)
**PMC:** [PMC316849](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC316849/)

## Abstract

1. Genes Dev. 1999 Jul 1;13(13):1692-703. doi: 10.1101/gad.13.13.1692.

Crystal structure of the conserved core of the herpes simplex virus
transcriptional regulatory protein VP16.

Liu Y(1), Gong W, Huang CC, Herr W, Cheng X.

Author information:
(1)Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, Cold Spring Harbor, New York 11724, USA.

On infection, the herpes simplex virus (HSV) virion protein VP16 (Vmw65;
alphaTIF) forms a transcriptional regulatory complex-the VP16-induced
complex-with two cellular proteins, HCF and Oct-1, on VP16-responsive
cis-regulatory elements in HSV immediate-early promoters called TAATGARAT.
Comparison of different HSV VP16 sequences reveals a conserved core region that
is sufficient for VP16-induced complex formation. The crystal structure of the
VP16 core has been determined at 2.1 A resolution. The results reveal a novel,
seat-like protein structure. Together with the activity of mutant VP16 proteins,
the structure of free VP16 suggests that it contains (1) a disordered
carboxy-terminal region that associates with HCF, Oct-1, and DNA in the
VP16-induced complex, and (2) a structured region involved in virion assembly
and possessing a novel DNA-binding surface that differentiates among TAATGARAT
VP16-response elements.
