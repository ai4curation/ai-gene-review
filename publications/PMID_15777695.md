# Characterization of a novel Eph receptor tyrosine kinase, EphA10, expressed in testis.
**Authors:** Aasheim HC, Patzke S, Hjorthaug HS, Finne EF
**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bbagen.2005.01.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbagen.2005.01.011)

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 2005 May 25;1723(1-3):1-7. doi:
10.1016/j.bbagen.2005.01.011. Epub 2005 Feb 16.

Characterization of a novel Eph receptor tyrosine kinase, EphA10, expressed in
testis.

Aasheim HC(1), Patzke S, Hjorthaug HS, Finne EF.

Author information:
(1)Department of Immunology, The Norwegian Radium Hospital, Montebello, 0310
Oslo, Norway. h.c.asheim@labmed.uio.no

In mammals, 14 members of the Eph receptor tyrosine kinase family have been
described so far. Here we present a not yet described member of this family
denoted EphA10. We report the identification of three putative EphA10 isoforms:
one soluble and two transmembrane isoforms. One of the latter isoforms lacked
the sterile alpha motif commonly found in Eph receptors. The gene encoding
EphA10 is located on chromosome 1p34 and expression studies show that EphA10
mRNA is mainly expressed in testis. Binding studies to ephrin ligands suggests
that this receptor belongs to the EphA subclass of Eph receptors binding mainly
to ephrin-A ligands.
