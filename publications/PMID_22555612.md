# SUMOylation of Blimp-1 is critical for plasma cell differentiation.
**Authors:** Ying HY, Su ST, Hsu PH, Chang CC, Lin IY, Tseng YH, Tsai MD, Shih HM, Lin KI
**Journal:** EMBO Rep (2012)
**DOI:** [10.1038/embor.2012.60](https://doi.org/10.1038/embor.2012.60)
**PMC:** [PMC3388782](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3388782/)

## Abstract

1. EMBO Rep. 2012 Jun 29;13(7):631-7. doi: 10.1038/embor.2012.60.

SUMOylation of Blimp-1 is critical for plasma cell differentiation.

Ying HY(1), Su ST, Hsu PH, Chang CC, Lin IY, Tseng YH, Tsai MD, Shih HM, Lin KI.

Author information:
(1)Genomics Research Center, Academia Sinica, Taipei 115, Taiwan.

Transcriptional repressor B lymphocyte-induced maturation protein-1 (Blimp-1) is
a master regulator of plasma cell differentiation. Here we show that Blimp-1 is
covalently modified by SUMO1 at lysine 816, a modification mediated by SUMO E3
ligase PIAS1. Mutation of Blimp-1 lysine 816 reduces transcriptional
repression--correlating with a reduced interaction with a histone deacetylase,
HDAC2--and impairs differentiation of antibody-secreting cells. Thus, the SUMO
pathway critically regulates Blimp-1 function during plasma cell
differentiation.
