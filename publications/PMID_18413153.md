# The effect of simvastatin on the serum monocyte chemoattractant protein-1 and intracellular adhesion molecule-1 levels in diabetic rats.
**Authors:** Lin Y, Ye S, Chen Y, Li X, Yang GW, Fan A, Wang Y
**Journal:** J Diabetes Complications (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.jdiacomp.2007.09.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdiacomp.2007.09.003)

## Abstract

1. J Diabetes Complications. 2009 May-Jun;23(3):214-8. doi:
10.1016/j.jdiacomp.2007.09.003. Epub 2008 Apr 16.

The effect of simvastatin on the serum monocyte chemoattractant protein-1 and
intracellular adhesion molecule-1 levels in diabetic rats.

Lin Y(1), Ye S, Chen Y, Li X, Yang GW, Fan A, Wang Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Endocrinology, Anhui Provincial Hospital affiliated to Anhui
Medical University, Hefei, 230001, China.

OBJECTIVE: This study aimed to observe the effect of simvastatin on the serum
monocyte chemoattractant protein-1 (MCP-1) and intracellular adhesion molecule-1
(ICAM-1) levels and to probe its protective mechanisms on macroangiopathy in
diabetic rats.
METHODS: Twenty-four Wistar rats were randomly assigned to a normal control
group (Group A, n=8), and STZ-induced diabetic group (Group B, n=8), or a
simvastatin-treated diabetic group (Group C, n=8). Rats in Group C were treated
with simvastatin (20 mg kg(-1) day(-1)) 1 week after the establishment of the
diabetic model. Groups A and B were treated with corresponding sodium chloride.
Peripheral blood glucose was tested weekly; serum MCP-1, ICAM-1, and HbA1c
levels were tested at the eighth week.
RESULTS: At the second, fourth, and eighth week, peripheral blood glucose levels
in Group B were similar to those of Group C, which were much higher than those
of Group A. Serum MCP-1 and ICAM-1 levels in Groups B and C were higher than
those of Group A (P<.01), and serum MCP-1 and ICAM-1 levels in Group C were
lower than those of Group B (P<.01); HbA1c was not significantly different
between Group C and Group B.
CONCLUSION: Simvastatin has the effect of anti-inflammation, which may play some
protection against the progress of atherosclerosis in diabetic rats.
