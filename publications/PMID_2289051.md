# Coproporphyrinogen oxidase, protoporphyrinogen oxidase and ferrochelatase activities in iron-overloaded and ethanol-treated rats.
**Authors:** Li F, Lim CK, Peters TJ
**Journal:** Biomed Chromatogr (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1002/bmc.1130040610](https://doi.org/10.1002/bmc.1130040610)

## Abstract

1. Biomed Chromatogr. 1990 Nov;4(6):253-6. doi: 10.1002/bmc.1130040610.

Coproporphyrinogen oxidase, protoporphyrinogen oxidase and ferrochelatase
activities in iron-overloaded and ethanol-treated rats.

Li F(1), Lim CK, Peters TJ.

Author information:
(1)Division of Clinical Cell Biology, MRC Clinical Research Centre, Harrow,
Middlesex, UK.

The activities of coproporphyrinogen oxidase, protoporphyrinogen oxidase and
ferrochelatase in liver homogenates of iron-overloaded and acutely or
chronically ethanol-treated rats were assayed by high performance liquid
chromatographic methods. Decreased coproporphyrinogen oxidase activity was found
in chronically carbonyliron-fed rats while the activities of protoporphyrinogen
oxidase and ferrochelatase remained unchanged. Coproporphyrinogen oxidase and
protoporphyrinogen oxidase activities in both chronically and acutely
ethanol-treated animals were not significantly different from controls. In
chronically ethanol-fed rats normal ferrochelatase activity was found.
