# The human intestinal H+/oligopeptide cotransporter hPEPT1 transports differently-charged dipeptides with identical electrogenic properties.
**Authors:** Mackenzie B, Fei YJ, Ganapathy V, Leibach FH
**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0005-2736(96)00170-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0005-2736(96)00170-8)

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 1996 Oct 23;1284(2):125-8. doi:
10.1016/s0005-2736(96)00170-8.

The human intestinal H+/oligopeptide cotransporter hPEPT1 transports
differently-charged dipeptides with identical electrogenic properties.

Mackenzie B(1), Fei YJ, Ganapathy V, Leibach FH.

Author information:
(1)Department of Physiology, UCLA School of Medicine 90095-1751, USA.

The human intestinal H+/oligopeptide cotransporter hPEPT1, expressed in Xenopus
oocytes, transported neutral, anionic and cationic dipeptides with identical
electrogenic properties and maximal evoked currents. Currents were activated by
1 H+ regardless of the net charge on the driven substrate, and were independent
of Na+o, K+i and Clo-, calling into question the familiar concept of the origin
of the transporter-mediated current.
