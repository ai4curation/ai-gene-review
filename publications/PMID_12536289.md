# X-linked Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease caused by a novel point mutation in the connexin-32 gene.
**Authors:** Ma W, Farrukh Nizam M, Grewal RP
**Journal:** Neurol Sci (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s100720200061](https://doi.org/10.1007/s100720200061)

## Abstract

1. Neurol Sci. 2002 Oct;23(4):195-7. doi: 10.1007/s100720200061.

X-linked Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease caused by a novel point mutation in the
connexin-32 gene.

Ma W(1), Farrukh Nizam M, Grewal RP.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Neurogenetics, New Jersey Neuroscience Institute, Seton Hall
University, 65 James Street, Edison, New Jersey 08818, USA.

We report the clinical and electrophysiological findings of a patient with
X-linked Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease and a novel point mutation in the
connexin-32 gene. A 31-year-old man presented with a 5 year history of
progressive imbalance and distal weakness in his legs. Electrophysiological
studies confirmed an asymmetric, predominantly axonal sensorimotor neuropathy
with some demyelinating features. Genetic testing revealed a G/A transition
(Ala40Thr) in a conserved transmembrane region of the connexin-32 gene.
