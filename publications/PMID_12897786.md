# Lmx1b is essential for the development of serotonergic neurons.
**Authors:** Ding YQ, Marklund U, Yuan W, Yin J, Wegman L, Ericson J, Deneris E, Johnson RL, Chen ZF
**Journal:** Nat Neurosci (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1038/nn1104](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn1104)

## Abstract

1. Nat Neurosci. 2003 Sep;6(9):933-8. doi: 10.1038/nn1104.

Lmx1b is essential for the development of serotonergic neurons.

Ding YQ(1), Marklund U, Yuan W, Yin J, Wegman L, Ericson J, Deneris E, Johnson
RL, Chen ZF.

Author information:
(1)Department of Anesthesiology, School of Medicine Pain Center, Washington
University, St. Louis, Missouri 63110, USA.

The specification and differentiation of serotonergic (5-HT) neurons require
both extrinsic signaling molecules and intrinsic transcription factors to work
in concert or in cascade. Here we identify the genetic cascades that control the
specification and differentiation of 5-HT neurons in mice. A major determinant
in the cascades is an LIM homeodomain-containing gene, Lmx1b, which is required
for the development of all 5-HT neurons in the central nervous system. Our
results suggest that, during development of 5-HT neurons, Lmx1b is a critical
intermediate factor that couples Nkx2-2-mediated early specification with
Pet1-mediated terminal differentiation. Moreover, our data indicate that genetic
cascades controlling the caudal and rostral 5-HT neurons are distinct, despite
their shared components.
