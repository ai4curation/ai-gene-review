# The Drosophila SDE3 homolog armitage is required for oskar mRNA silencing and embryonic axis specification.
**Authors:** Cook HA, Koppetsch BS, Wu J, Theurkauf WE
**Journal:** Cell (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0092-8674(04)00250-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(04)00250-8)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 2004 Mar 19;116(6):817-29. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(04)00250-8.

The Drosophila SDE3 homolog armitage is required for oskar mRNA silencing and
embryonic axis specification.

Cook HA(1), Koppetsch BS, Wu J, Theurkauf WE.

Author information:
(1)Program in Molecular Medicine and the Program in Cell Dynamics, University of
Massachusetts Medical School, 373 Plantation Street, Worcester, MA 01605, USA.

Polarization of the microtubule cytoskeleton during early oogenesis is required
to specify the posterior of the Drosophila oocyte, which is essential for
asymmetric mRNA localization during mid-oogenesis and for embryonic axis
specification. The posterior determinant oskar mRNA is translationally silent
until mid-oogenesis. We show that mutations in armitage and three components of
the RNAi pathway disrupt oskar mRNA translational silencing, polarization of the
microtubule cytoskeleton, and posterior localization of oskar mRNA. armitage
encodes a homolog of SDE3, a presumptive RNA helicase involved in
posttranscriptional gene silencing (RNAi) in Arabidopsis, and is required for
RNAi in Drosophila ovaries. Armitage forms an asymmetric network associated with
the polarized microtubule cytoskeleton and is concentrated with translationally
silent oskar mRNA in the oocyte. We conclude that RNA silencing is essential for
establishment of the cytoskeletal polarity that initiates embryonic axis
specification and for translational control of oskar mRNA.
