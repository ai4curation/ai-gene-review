# Expression of Hand2 is sufficient for neurogenesis and cell type-specific gene expression in the enteric nervous system.
**Authors:** Hendershot TJ, Liu H, Sarkar AA, Giovannucci DR, Clouthier DE, Abe M, Howard MJ
**Journal:** Dev Dyn (2007)
**DOI:** [10.1002/dvdy.20989](https://doi.org/10.1002/dvdy.20989)

## Abstract

1. Dev Dyn. 2007 Jan;236(1):93-105. doi: 10.1002/dvdy.20989.

Expression of Hand2 is sufficient for neurogenesis and cell type-specific gene
expression in the enteric nervous system.

Hendershot TJ(1), Liu H, Sarkar AA, Giovannucci DR, Clouthier DE, Abe M, Howard
MJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurosciences and Program in Cellular and Molecular
Neurobiology, Medical University of Ohio, Toledo, Ohio 43614, USA.

The basic helix-loop-helix DNA binding protein Hand2 is expressed in neural
crest-derived precursors of enteric neurons and has been shown to affect both
neurogenesis and neurotransmitter specification of noradrenergic sympathetic
ganglion neurons. In the current study, our goal was to determine whether Hand2
affects neurogenesis and/or expression of vasoactive intestinal polypeptide and
choline acetyltransferase in developing enteric neurons. Gain-of-function of
Hand2 in HNK-1(+) immmunoselected precursor cells resulted in increased
neurogenesis. The number of neurons expressing vasoactive intestinal polypeptide
increased in response to Hand2 overexpression although choline acetyltransferase
was not affected. Targeted deletion of Hand2 in neural crest cells resulted in
loss of all neurons expressing vasoactive intestinal polypeptide along the
length of the gastrointestinal tract, patterning defects in the myenteric plexus
of the stomach, and altered number and morphology of neurons expressing TH. Our
data demonstrate that expression of Hand2 is sufficient and necessary for
neurogenesis and expression of a subset of cell type-specific markers in the
developing enteric nervous system.
