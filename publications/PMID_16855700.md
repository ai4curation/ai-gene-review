**Authors:** Wright CM, Christman GD, Snellinger AM, Johnston MV, Mueller EG

## Abstract

1. Chem Commun (Camb). 2006 Aug 7;(29):3104-6. doi: 10.1039/b604040c. Epub 2006
Jun  14.

Direct evidence for enzyme persulfide and disulfide intermediates during
4-thiouridine biosynthesis.

Wright CM(1), Christman GD, Snellinger AM, Johnston MV, Mueller EG.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry, University of Delaware, Newark, DE
19716, USA.

Two proposed mechanisms for 4-thiouridine generation share key cysteine
persulfide and disulfide intermediates, and indirect evidence of their existence
has been previously reported; chemical trapping and mass spectrometry have now
provided direct and definitive evidence of these key intermediates.
