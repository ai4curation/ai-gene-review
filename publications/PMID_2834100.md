# Cloning and expression of the OMP decarboxylase gene URA4 from Schizosaccharomyces pombe.
**Authors:** Bach ML
**Journal:** Curr Genet (1987)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00419562](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00419562)

## Abstract

1. Curr Genet. 1987;12(7):527-34. doi: 10.1007/BF00419562.

Cloning and expression of the OMP decarboxylase gene URA4 from
Schizosaccharomyces pombe.

Bach ML(1).

Author information:
(1)Laboratoire de Génétique Physiologique, I.B.M.C. du C.N.R.S., Strasbourg,
France.

URA4, the gene coding for orotidine monophosphate decarboxylase (OMPdecase), has
been cloned from the fission yeast by homologous complementation and restricted
in an Escherichia coli-Schizosaccharomyces pombe (E. coli-S. pombe) replicative
plasmid to a 1.76 kb HindIII fragment. This plasmid is maintained at a high copy
number in S. pombe and allows OMPdecase expression in Saccharomyces cerevisiae
(S. cerevisiae) as well as in E. coli. After characterisation by restriction
mapping and Southern hybridisation, the cloned gene was used as a probe to
measure URA4 transcription and to examine its regulation. Messenger RNA levels
were measured by DNA/RNA filter-hybridisation with pulse labelled RNAs during
6-azauridine (6-AUR) inhibited growth in wild type and 6-AUR sensitive strains.
We found that in S. pombe the OMP analogue 6-AUR does not regulate the level of
OMPdecase formation as it does in S. cerevisiae but rather modifies the ratio of
total polyA+ to polyA- RNAs in the cell. Based on these results and on
corresponding enzyme activities this study demonstrates divergent pyrimidine
pathway regulation in the two yeasts S. cerevisiae and S. pombe. Finally, we
propose the use of the URA4 gene as a convenient selective marker for genetic
engineering in S. pombe.
