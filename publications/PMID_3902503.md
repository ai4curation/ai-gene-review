# Identification of proline carrier in Escherichia coli K-12.
**Authors:** Hanada K, Yamato I, Anraku Y
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1985)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0014-5793(85)80024-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(85)80024-7)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1985 Oct 28;191(2):278-82. doi: 10.1016/0014-5793(85)80024-7.

Identification of proline carrier in Escherichia coli K-12.

Hanada K, Yamato I, Anraku Y.

Proline carrier, a product of the putP gene of Escherichia coli, was identified
as a 35 kDa cytoplasmic membrane protein by SDS-polyacrylamide gel
electrophoresis (SDS-PAGE). Its identification was based on the following
evidence: First, the density of the band corresponding to a 35 kDa protein
correlated with the proline-binding activity of cytoplasmic membranes from
putP-deficient and putP-amplified strains. Second, by the differential labeling
method, the 35 kDa protein was specifically labeled with radioactive
N-ethyl-maleimide. The 35 kDa protein was found to aggregate on heat treatment
and to show abnormal mobility on SDS-PAGE.
