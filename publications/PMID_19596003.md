# Adiponectin promotes migration activities of endothelial progenitor cells via Cdc42/Rac1.
**Authors:** Nakamura N, Naruse K, Matsuki T, Hamada Y, Nakashima E, Kamiya H, Matsubara T, Enomoto A, Takahashi M, Oiso Y, Nakamura J
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.011)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2009 Aug 6;583(15):2457-63. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.011.
Epub  2009 Jul 14.

Adiponectin promotes migration activities of endothelial progenitor cells via
Cdc42/Rac1.

Nakamura N(1), Naruse K, Matsuki T, Hamada Y, Nakashima E, Kamiya H, Matsubara
T, Enomoto A, Takahashi M, Oiso Y, Nakamura J.

Author information:
(1)Department of Endocrinology and Diabetes, Nagoya University Graduate School

Adiponectin has anti-atherosclerotic effects through its direct actions on
vascular cells. The present study investigates the molecular mechanisms of
adiponectin in the migration of endothelial progenitor cells (EPCs) which play
an important role in neovascularization and re-endothelization. The
phosphorylation of Akt and the activations of Cdc42 and Rac1 were significantly
increased by adiponectin. Adiponectin increased the migration activity of EPCs,
which was completely inhibited by a PI3-kinase inhibitor. siRNA of Cdc42 or Rac1
completely inhibited the adiponectin-induced migration, but siRNA of Akt had no
effects, indicating that adiponectin promotes the migration activities of EPCs
mainly through PI3-kinase/Cdc42/Rac1.
