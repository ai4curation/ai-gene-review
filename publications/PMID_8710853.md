# Mammalian Sug1 and c-Fos in the nuclear 26S proteasome.
**Authors:** Wang W, Chevray PM, Nathans D
**Journal:** Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.93.16.8236](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.93.16.8236)

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1996 Aug 6;93(16):8236-40. doi:
10.1073/pnas.93.16.8236.

Mammalian Sug1 and c-Fos in the nuclear 26S proteasome.

Wang W(1), Chevray PM, Nathans D.

Author information:
(1)Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine,
Baltimore, MD 21205, USA.

In a search for regulatory proteins that interact with the leucine zipper motif
of c-Fos in the yeast two-hybrid screen, we have identified a protein (FZA-B)
that has extensive sequence similarity to SUG1 of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Here
we show that FZA-B can functionally substitute for SUG1 in yeast and that FZA-B
interacts with Fos proteins in vitro through their leucine zippers. In rat liver
and in HeLa cells, FZA-B is present in the 26S proteasome complex, as is c-Fos.
Immobilized antibody raised against an FZA-B-specific peptide depleted peptidase
activity, proteasomal proteins, FZA-B, and c-Fos from a 26S proteasome
preparation. FZA-B is found predominantly in the nuclear fraction of COS cells
expressing an FZA-B transgene and in the nuclear 26S proteasome of HeLa cells.
We conclude that FZA-B is the mammalian homolog of SUG1 (mSug1) and that it is
present in the nuclear 26S proteasome of cells. Our results suggest that mSug1
may be involved in the degradation of c-Fos and other transcription factors.
