# A model of the three-dimensional structure of ice nucleation proteins.
**Authors:** Kajava AV, Lindow SE
**Journal:** J Mol Biol (1993)
**DOI:** [10.1006/jmbi.1993.1424](https://doi.org/10.1006/jmbi.1993.1424)

## Abstract

1. J Mol Biol. 1993 Aug 5;232(3):709-17. doi: 10.1006/jmbi.1993.1424.

A model of the three-dimensional structure of ice nucleation proteins.

Kajava AV(1), Lindow SE.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Protein Research, Russian Academy of Sciences, Pushchino, Moscow
Region.

Bacterial ice-nucleation proteins are among the most active natural ice
nucleants and can reduce the supercooling point of water in plants, thereby
reducing the ability of sensitive plants to avoid damaging ice formation. We
describe a structural model for bacterial ice-nucleation proteins based on
molecular modelling. This model predicts a largely planar extended molecule,
with one side serving as a template for orienting water into an ice lattice and
the other side interacting with the membrane. The model also predicts that
single molecules can form aggregates of unlimited size by interdigitation.
