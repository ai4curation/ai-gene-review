# An adjacent arginine, and the phosphorylated tyrosine in the c-Met receptor target sequence, dictates the orientation of c-Cbl binding.
**Authors:** Sun Q, Ng C, Guy GR, Sivaraman J
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2010.11.060](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2010.11.060)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2011 Jan 21;585(2):281-5. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2010.11.060. Epub
2010 Dec 15.

An adjacent arginine, and the phosphorylated tyrosine in the c-Met receptor
target sequence, dictates the orientation of c-Cbl binding.

Sun Q(1), Ng C, Guy GR, Sivaraman J.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biological Sciences, National University of Singapore,
Singapore, Singapore.

Previously, we have demonstrated that the tyrosine phosphorylated hepatocyte
growth factor receptor (Met) binds to the c-Cbl phosphotyrosine-recognition,
tyrosine kinase binding (TKB) domain in a reverse orientation compared to other
c-Cbl binding partners. A Met peptide with the DpYR motif changed to RpYD
(MetRD) retains a similar TKB binding affinity as the native Met peptide.
However, the TKB: MetRD complex crystal structure reveals a complete reversal of
the binding orientation. Collated data indicates that both binding and
orientation is dictated by the phosphorylated tyrosine and an adjacent arginine
forming intra-peptide hydrogen bonds and aligning unidirectionally with
complementary charges in the phosphotyrosine binding pocket of c-Cbl.
