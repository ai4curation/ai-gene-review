# Fibroblast growth factor-2 interacts with free ribosomal protein S19.
**Authors:** Soulet F, Al Saati T, Roga S, Amalric F, Bouche G
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.2001.5960](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.2001.5960)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2001 Nov 30;289(2):591-6. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.2001.5960.

Fibroblast growth factor-2 interacts with free ribosomal protein S19.

Soulet F(1), Al Saati T, Roga S, Amalric F, Bouche G.

Author information:
(1)Laboratoire de Biologie Vasculaire, IPBS/CNRS, 205 Rte de Narbonne, 31077
Toulouse Cedex, France.

Exogenous FGF-2 added to cells is internalized and part of it translocates to
the nucleus of the cells. To get a better understanding of the FGF-2-induced
signaling pathway, we looked for proteins associated with FGF-2 in the cytoplasm
of the target cells. We first used the GST-FGF-2 to isolate cytoplasmic proteins
complexes containing FGF-2 from S100 extract (supernatant 100,000g). Among the
retrieved proteins, we focused our studies on RPS19, a protein of the 40S small
ribosomal subunit. We showed that FGF-2 interacts directly with RPS19 in vitro.
Second, we coimmunoprecipitated RPS19 and FGF-2 from a S240 extract (240,000g
supernatant) prepared from FGF-2-stimulated cells and devoid of 40S ribosomal
subunit. The result of these experiments suggest that a pool of free RPS19
exists in cells and that FGF-2 interacts in vivo with free RPS19.
