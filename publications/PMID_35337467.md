Choanoflagellates, the closest living relatives of animals, have the potential
to reveal the genetic and cell biological foundations of complex multicellular
development in animals. Here we describe the history of research on the
choanoflagellate Salpingoeca rosetta. From its original isolation in 2000 to the
establishment of CRISPR-mediated genome editing in 2020, S. rosetta provides an
instructive case study in the establishment of a new model organism.
