Miinalainen IJ(1), Chen ZJ, Torkko JM, Pirilä PL, Sormunen RT, Bergmann U, Qin
YM, Hiltunen JK.

Author information:
(1)Biocenter Oulu, Department of Biochemistry, University of Oulu, Finland.

A data base search with YBR026c/MRF1', which encodes trans-2-enoyl thioester
reductase of the intramitochondrial fatty acid synthesis (FAS) type II in yeast
(Torkko, J. M., Koivuranta, K. T., Miinalainen, I. J., Yagi, A. I., Schmitz, W.,
Kastaniotis, A. J., Airenne, T. T., Gurvitz, A., and Hiltunen, K. J. (2001) Mol.
Cell. Biol. 21, 6243-6253), revealed the clone AA393871 (HsNrbf-1, nuclear
receptor binding factor 1) in human EST data bank. Expression of HsNrbf-1,
tagged C-terminally with green fluorescent protein, in HeLa cells, resulted in a
punctated fluorescence signal, superimposable with the MitoTracker Red dye.
Wild-type polypeptide was immunoisolated from the extract of bovine heart
mitochondria. Recombinant HsNrbf-1p reduces trans-2-enoyl-CoA to acyl-CoA with
chain length from C6 to C16 in an NADPH-dependent manner with preference to
medium chain length substrate. Furthermore, expression of HsNRBF-1 in the
ybr026cDelta yeast strain restored mitochondrial respiratory function allowing
growth on glycerol. These findings provide evidence that Nrbf-1ps act as a
mitochondrial 2-enoyl thioester reductase, and mammalian cells may possess
bacterial type fatty acid synthetase (FAS type II) in mitochondria, in addition
to FAS type I in the cytoplasm.
