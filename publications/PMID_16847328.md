# hnRNP A1 relocalization to the stress granules reflects a role in the stress response.
**Authors:** Guil S, Long JC, Cáceres JF
**Journal:** Mol Cell Biol (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1128/MCB.00224-06](https://doi.org/10.1128/MCB.00224-06)
**PMC:** [PMC1592774](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1592774/)

## Abstract

1. Mol Cell Biol. 2006 Aug;26(15):5744-58. doi: 10.1128/MCB.00224-06.

hnRNP A1 relocalization to the stress granules reflects a role in the stress
response.

Guil S(1), Long JC, Cáceres JF.

Author information:
(1)Medical Research Council Human Genetics Unit, Western General Hospital,
Edinburgh, Scotland, United Kingdom.

hnRNP A1 is a nucleocytoplasmic shuttling protein that is involved in many
aspects of mRNA metabolism. We have previously shown that activation of the p38
stress-signaling pathway in mammalian cells results in both hyperphosphorylation
and cytoplasmic accumulation of hnRNP A1, affecting alternative splicing
regulation in vivo. Here we show that the stress-induced cytoplasmic
accumulation of hnRNP A1 occurs in discrete phase-dense particles, the
cytoplasmic stress granules (SGs). Interestingly, mRNA-binding activity is
required for both phosphorylation of hnRNP A1 and localization to SGs. We also
show that these effects are mediated by the Mnk1/2 protein kinases that act
downstream of p38. Finally, depletion of hnRNP A1 affects the recovery of cells
from stress, suggesting a physiologically significant role for hnRNP A1 in the
stress response. Our data are consistent with a model whereby hnRNP A1
recruitment to SGs involves Mnk1/2-dependent phosphorylation of mRNA-bound hnRNP
A1.
