Comment in
    Neurology. 2002 May 28;58(10):1451-3. doi: 10.1212/wnl.58.10.1451.

Early-onset (infantile) Alexander disease is associated with mutations in the
glial fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP) gene and two hot spots correlate to the
severe phenotype. No molecular mechanisms have been elucidated in late-onset
(juvenile) Alexander disease. The authors report a novel GFAP mutation in a
patient with juvenile Alexander disease. The authors discuss similar molecular
mechanisms in another intermediate filament disease and propose a possible
molecular pathogenesis in juvenile Alexander disease.
