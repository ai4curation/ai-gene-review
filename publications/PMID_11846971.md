Interferon (IFN) regulatory factor-1 (IRF-1) was isolated by virtue of its
affinity to specific DNA sequences in the IFN-beta promoter that mediate virus
responsiveness. IRF-1 was the first factor identified of the IRF family and was
most extensively characterized at the molecular level. Also, its physiologic
role in host defense against pathogens, tumor prevention, and development of the
immune system was investigated in detail. Even though some of the functions
first associated with IRF-1 were later found to be mediated in part or
predominantly by other activators of the IRF family of transcription factors,
IRF-1 has remained a central paradigm in the transcriptional regulation of the
IFN response.
