# Interactions between chip and the achaete/scute-daughterless heterodimers are required for pannier-driven proneural patterning.
**Authors:** Ramain P, Khechumian R, Khechumian K, Arbogast N, Ackermann C, Heitzler P
**Journal:** Mol Cell (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1097-2765(05)00079-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1097-2765(05)00079-1)

## Abstract

1. Mol Cell. 2000 Oct;6(4):781-90. doi: 10.1016/s1097-2765(05)00079-1.

Interactions between chip and the achaete/scute-daughterless heterodimers are
required for pannier-driven proneural patterning.

Ramain P(1), Khechumian R, Khechumian K, Arbogast N, Ackermann C, Heitzler P.

Author information:
(1)Institut de Génétique et de Biologie Moléculaire et Cellulaire,
CNRS/INSERM/ULP, Illkirch, Strasbourg, France. phr@igbmc.u-strasbg.fr

The GATA factor Pannier activates the achaete-scute (ASC) proneural complex
through enhancer binding and provides positional information for sensory bristle
patterning in Drosophila. Chip was previously identified as a cofactor of the
dorsal selector Apterous, and we show here that both Apterous and Chip also
regulate ASC expression. Chip cooperates with Pannier in bridging the GATA
factor with the HLH Ac/Sc and Daughterless proteins to allow enhancer-promoter
interactions, leading to activation of the proneural genes, whereas Apterous
antagonizes Pannier function. Within the Pannier domain of expression, Pannier
and Apterous may compete for binding to their common Chip cofactor, and the
accurate stoichiometry between these three proteins is essential for both
proneural prepattern and compartmentalization of the thorax.
