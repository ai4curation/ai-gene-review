# Calmodulin binding to the polybasic C-termini of STIM proteins involved in store-operated calcium entry.
**Authors:** Bauer MC, O'Connell D, Cahill DJ, Linse S
**Journal:** Biochemistry (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1021/bi800496a](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi800496a)

## Abstract

1. Biochemistry. 2008 Jun 10;47(23):6089-91. doi: 10.1021/bi800496a. Epub 2008
May  17.

Calmodulin binding to the polybasic C-termini of STIM proteins involved in
store-operated calcium entry.

Bauer MC(1), O'Connell D, Cahill DJ, Linse S.

Author information:
(1)Lund University, Biophysical Chemistry, Chemical Centre, P O Box 124, SE221
00 Lund, Sweden.

Translocation of STIM1 and STIM2 from the endoplasmic reticulum to the plasma
membrane is a key step in store-operated calcium entry in the cell. We show by
isothermal titration calorimetry that calmodulin binds in a calcium-dependent
manner to the polybasic C-termini of STIM1 and STIM2, a region critical for
their translocation to the plasma membrane ( K D < or = 1 microM in calcium).
HSQC NMR spectroscopy shows this interaction is in the fast exchange regime. By
binding STIM1 and STIM2, calmodulin may regulate store refilling, thereby
ensuring the maintenance of its own action in intracellular signaling.
