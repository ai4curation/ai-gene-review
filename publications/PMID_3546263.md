## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1987 Mar;169(3):1024-8. doi: 10.1128/jb.169.3.1024-1028.1987.

Pyruvate decarboxylase of Zymomonas mobilis: isolation, properties, and genetic
expression in Escherichia coli.

Neale AD, Scopes RK, Wettenhall RE, Hoogenraad NJ.

Pyruvate decarboxylase (EC 4.1.1.1) from Zymomonas mobilis purified to
homogeneity by using dye-ligand and ion-exchange chromatography. Antibodies
produced against the enzyme and the amino-terminal sequence obtained for the
pure enzyme were used to select and confirm the identity of a genomic clone
encoding the enzyme selected from a genomic library of Z. mobilis DNA cloned
into pUC9. The genomic fragment encoding the enzyme expressed high levels of
pyruvate decarboxylase in Escherichia coli. Possible RNA polymerase and
ribosome-binding sites have been identified in the 5'-untranslated region of the
pyruvate decarboxylase gene.
