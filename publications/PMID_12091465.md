# Glycine transporter isoforms show differential subcellular localization in PC12 cells.
**Authors:** Geerlings A, Núñez E, Rodenstein L, López-Corcuera B, Aragón C
**Journal:** J Neurochem (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1046/j.1471-4159.2002.00930.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1471-4159.2002.00930.x)

## Abstract

1. J Neurochem. 2002 Jul;82(1):58-65. doi: 10.1046/j.1471-4159.2002.00930.x.

Glycine transporter isoforms show differential subcellular localization in PC12
cells.

Geerlings A(1), Núñez E, Rodenstein L, López-Corcuera B, Aragón C.

Author information:
(1)Centro de Biología Molecular Severo Ochoa, Facultad de Ciencias, Universidad
Autónoma de Madrid, Consejo Superior de Investigaciones Científicas, 28049
Madrid, Spain.

The subcellular localization of glycine transporters one (GLYT1) and two (GLYT2)
stably expressed in PC12 cells has been studied. To facilitate visualization,
enhanced green fluorescent protein (GFP) was fused to the amino terminus of both
glycine transporters. Functional analysis of the GFP-GLYT1 and GFP-GLYT2 stable
cell lines demonstrated that they exhibited high affinity for glycine and the
characteristic properties of both glycine transporter subtypes. The GFP-coupled
transporters were differently distributed throughout the cell. GFP-GLYT1 was
mainly localized on the plasma membrane, whereas most of GFP-GLYT2 was present
on large dense-core vesicles and endosomes. Both transporters were absent from
the synaptic vesicle population in PC12 cells.
