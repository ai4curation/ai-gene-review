# GUD1 (YDL238c) encodes Saccharomyces cerevisiae guanine deaminase, an enzyme expressed during post-diauxic growth.
**Authors:** Saint-Marc C, Daignan-Fornier B
**Journal:** Yeast (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1002/yea.1186](https://doi.org/10.1002/yea.1186)

## Abstract

1. Yeast. 2004 Dec;21(16):1359-63. doi: 10.1002/yea.1186.

GUD1 (YDL238c) encodes Saccharomyces cerevisiae guanine deaminase, an enzyme
expressed during post-diauxic growth.

Saint-Marc C(1), Daignan-Fornier B.

Author information:
(1)Institut de Biochimie et Génétique Cellulaires, Université Bordeaux 2 CNRS
UMR 5095, 33077 Bordeaux Cedex, France.

Purine salvage is a complex pathway allowing a correct balance between adenylic
and guanylic derivatives. In this paper, we show that GUD1 (YDL238c) encodes
guanine deaminase, a catabolic enzyme producing xanthine and ammonia from
guanine. Importantly, Gud1p activity was higher during post-diauxic growth,
suggesting that a decrease of the guanylic nucleotide pool could be required
when cells shift from proliferation to quiescence.
