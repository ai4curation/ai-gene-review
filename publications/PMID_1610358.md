## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1992 Jun 15;185(2):663-9. doi:
10.1016/0006-291x(92)91676-h.

Molecular cloning and chromosomal assignment of human calbindin-D9k.

Howard A(1), Legon S, Spurr NK, Walters JR.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medicine, Royal Postgraduate Medical School, Hammersmith
Hospital, London, UK.

Human calbindin-D9k, the vitamin D-dependent calcium binding protein, has been
cloned and sequenced following initial amplification of intestinal cDNA
sequences by the polymerase chain reaction using mixed oligonucleotide primers.
The derived amino acid sequence of 79 residues has a calculated molecular weight
of 9015 and is 89% homologous with the bovine and porcine sequences. Probing DNA
from human-rodent somatic cell hybrids mapped human calbindin-D9k to chromosome
Xp. A single abundant mRNA transcript was detectable in proximal small intestine
but not in kidney, uterus or placenta.
