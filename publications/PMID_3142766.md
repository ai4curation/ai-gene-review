## Abstract

1. EMBO J. 1988 Aug;7(8):2601-9. doi: 10.1002/j.1460-2075.1988.tb03110.x.

Sequential order of target-recognizing domains in multispecific
DNA-methyltransferases.

Wilke K(1), Rauhut E, Noyer-Weidner M, Lauster R, Pawlek B, Behrens B, Trautner
TA.

Author information:
(1)Max-Planck-Institut für Molekulare Genetik, Berlin, FRG.

In the multispecific DNA(cytosine-5)-methyltransferases (Mtases) of Bacillus
subtilis phages SPR and phi 3T the domains responsible for recognition of DNA
methylation targets CCA/TGG, CCGG, GGCC (SPR) and GCNGC, GGCC (phi 3T) represent
contiguous sequences of approximately 50 amino acids each. These domains are
tandemly arranged and do not overlap. They are part of a 'variable' segment
within the enzymes which is flanked by 'conserved' amino acids, which are very
similar amongst bacterial monospecific and the multispecific Mtases studied
here. These results follow from a mutational analysis of the SPR and phi 3T
Mtase genes. They further support our concept of a modular enzyme organization,
according to which variability of type II Mtases with respect to target
recognition is achieved by a combination of the same enzyme core with a variety
of target-recognizing domains.
