## Abstract

1. Mol Gen Genet. 1982;185(2):367-8. doi: 10.1007/BF00330815.

Pentose phosphate pathway mutants of yeast.

Lobo Z, Maitra PK.

A glucose-negative mutant of Saccharomyces cerevisiae lacking 6-phosphogluconate
dehydrogenase, the second enzyme of the pentose phosphate pathway, has been
obtained by inositol starvation. Suppression of this mutant for growth on
glucose takes place by the loss of glucose 6-phosphate dehydrogenase. A lesion
in the latter enzyme alone leaves growth practically unaffected. The mutations
define the respective structural genes.
