# mTORC2 phosphorylates protein kinase Cζ to regulate its stability and activity.
**Authors:** Li X, Gao T

**DOI:** [10.1002/embr.201338119](https://doi.org/10.1002/embr.201338119)

## Abstract

1. EMBO Rep. 2014 Feb;15(2):191-8. doi: 10.1002/embr.201338119. Epub 2013 Dec 27.

mTORC2 phosphorylates protein kinase Cζ to regulate its stability and activity.

Li X(1), Gao T.

Author information:
(1)Markey Cancer Center, University of Kentucky, Lexington, KY, USA.

Protein kinase Cζ (PKCζ) is phosphorylated at the activation loop and the turn
motif (TM). However, the TM kinase and functional relevance of TM
phosphorylation remain largely unknown. We demonstrate that PKCζ TM is
phosphorylated directly by the mTORC2 complex, and this phosphorylation is
required for maintaining PKCζ kinase activity and stability. Functionally,
mTORC2 regulates the activity of Rho family of GTPases, and therefore the
organization of the actin cytoskeleton, through the control of PKCζ activity.
Taken together, our findings identify PKCζ as a novel substrate and downstream
effector of mTORC2 signaling.
