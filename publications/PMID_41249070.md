The canonical mechanism by which the phytohormone auxin regulates transcription
has been one of the cornerstones of plant signaling. The recent unexpected
discovery of cyclic AMP (cAMP) as a second messenger in this pathway has revised
its foundations while leaving many open questions and gaps in our understanding;
these will be discussed in this forum article.
