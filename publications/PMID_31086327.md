Comment in
    Nat Chem Biol. 2019 Jun;15(6):552-553. doi: 10.1038/s41589-019-0292-8.

Inhibition of the NLRP3 inflammasome is a promising strategy for the development
of new treatments for inflammatory diseases. MCC950 is a potent and specific
small-molecule inhibitor of the NLRP3 pathway, but its molecular target is not
defined. Here, we show that MCC950 directly interacts with the Walker B motif
within the NLRP3 NACHT domain, thereby blocking ATP hydrolysis and inhibiting
NLRP3 activation and inflammasome formation.
