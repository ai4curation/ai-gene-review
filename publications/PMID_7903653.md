Author information:
(1)Department of Genetics, University of Cambridge, UK.

Among the glutamate-requiring strains of Schizosaccharomyces pombe previously
described [1], glu2 and glu3 strains were both shown to lack NAD-specific
isocitrate dehydrogenase. glu4 strains were shown to lack
glutamine:2-oxoglutarate aminotransferase (GOGAT), and to be defective in
ammonia assimilation. The regulation of GOGAT activity in wild-type cells was
investigated and was consistent with GOGAT and glutamine synthetase being
involved in ammonium assimilation, particularly under conditions of nitrogen
limitation.
