# Cloning and sequencing of human complementary DNA for the phosphoribosylpyrophosphate synthetase-associated protein 39.
**Authors:** Ishizuka T, Kita K, Sonoda T, Ishijima S, Sawa K, Suzuki N, Tatibana M
**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0167-4781(96)00030-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-4781(96)00030-9)

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 1996 Apr 10;1306(1):27-30. doi:
10.1016/0167-4781(96)00030-9.

Cloning and sequencing of human complementary DNA for the
phosphoribosylpyrophosphate synthetase-associated protein 39.

Ishizuka T(1), Kita K, Sonoda T, Ishijima S, Sawa K, Suzuki N, Tatibana M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Chiba University, School of Medicine, Japan.

A human cDNA encoding a human homologue of the rat phosphoribosylpyrophosphate
synthetase-associated protein of 39 kDa was isolated. The deduced protein
contains 356 amino acids and has calculated molecular mass of 38561. The amino
acid sequence is 98% identical to that of the rat. The corresponding mRNA is
present in all human tissues examined.
