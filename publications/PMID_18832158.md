The voltage-dependent anion channel (VDAC), also known as mitochondrial porin,
is the most abundant protein in the mitochondrial outer membrane (MOM). VDAC is
the channel known to guide the metabolic flux across the MOM and plays a key
role in mitochondrially induced apoptosis. Here, we present the 3D structure of
human VDAC1, which was solved conjointly by NMR spectroscopy and x-ray
crystallography. Human VDAC1 (hVDAC1) adopts a beta-barrel architecture composed
of 19 beta-strands with an alpha-helix located horizontally midway within the
pore. Bioinformatic analysis indicates that this channel architecture is common
to all VDAC proteins and is adopted by the general import pore TOM40 of mammals,
which is also located in the MOM.
