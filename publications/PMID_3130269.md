# Synergistic activation of human T cells by interleukin 1 and interleukin 6.
**Authors:** Houssiau FA, Coulie PG, Olive D, Van Snick J
**Journal:** Eur J Immunol (1988)
**DOI:** [10.1002/eji.1830180427](https://doi.org/10.1002/eji.1830180427)

## Abstract

1. Eur J Immunol. 1988 Apr;18(4):653-6. doi: 10.1002/eji.1830180427.

Synergistic activation of human T cells by interleukin 1 and interleukin 6.

Houssiau FA(1), Coulie PG, Olive D, Van Snick J.

Author information:
(1)Unit of Experimental Medicine, Université Catholique de Louvain, Brussels,
Belgium.

Purified human interleukin 6 (IL 6) was found to stimulate the proliferation of
human tonsillar and peripheral rosetting T cells subliminally activated with
phytohemagglutinin (PHA). This response seemed independent of IL 2 but highly
dependent on the presence of accessory cells. Indeed, when accessory
cell-depleted tonsillar T cells were activated with PHA and exposed to IL 6,
only minimal proliferations were observed. A similar result was obtained with IL
1. However, a combination of these two cytokines induced strong proliferations,
indicating that IL 1 and IL 6 plays a synergistic role in the interactions
between accessory cells and T lymphocytes.
