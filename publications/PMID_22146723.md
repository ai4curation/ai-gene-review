# Systematic localization study on novel proteins encoded by meiotically up-regulated ORFs in fission yeast.
**Authors:** Ikebe C, Konishi M, Hirata D, Matsusaka T, Toda T
**Journal:** Biosci Biotechnol Biochem (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1271/bbb.110558](https://doi.org/10.1271/bbb.110558)

## Abstract

1. Biosci Biotechnol Biochem. 2011;75(12):2364-70. doi: 10.1271/bbb.110558. Epub
2011 Dec 7.

Systematic localization study on novel proteins encoded by meiotically
up-regulated ORFs in fission yeast.

Ikebe C(1), Konishi M, Hirata D, Matsusaka T, Toda T.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Cell Regulation, London Research Institute, Cancer Research UK,
London, UK.

We conducted a mitotic localization study on gene products encoded by 56
uncharacterized fission yeast ORFs that were transcriptionally up-regulated
during meiotic division. Despite meiotic gene induction, these genes were
expressed during mitosis as well. Seven gene products were localized in the
nucleus and/or chromatin; another one was a mitosis-specific spindle pole body
component and, intriguingly, its human homologue was also localized in the
centrosome of cultured HeLa cells. Two products appeared to be localized in
cytoplasmic microtubules, whereas four were mitochondrial proteins. Three other
proteins were found in the medial ring upon cytokinesis and another was
localized on the entire cell periphery. The remaining 38 proteins were detected
in the cytoplasm and showed varied spatial patterns. This systematic study helps
our integrated understanding of all the protein functions in the fission yeast
as a eukaryotic model.
