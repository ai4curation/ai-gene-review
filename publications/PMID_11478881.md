# Dynamics of the Mrf-2 DNA-binding domain free and in complex with DNA.
**Authors:** Zhu L, Hu J, Lin D, Whitson R, Itakura K, Chen Y
**Journal:** Biochemistry (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1021/bi010476a](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi010476a)

## Abstract

1. Biochemistry. 2001 Aug 7;40(31):9142-50. doi: 10.1021/bi010476a.

Dynamics of the Mrf-2 DNA-binding domain free and in complex with DNA.

Zhu L(1), Hu J, Lin D, Whitson R, Itakura K, Chen Y.

Author information:
(1)Division of Immunology, Beckman Institute of the City of Hope, Duarte,
California 91010, USA.

Mrf-2 is a member of a new class of DNA-binding proteins known as the AT-rich
interaction domain family or ARID. Chemical shift indices and characteristic NOE
values indicate that the three-dimensional structure of the Mrf-2 ARID in
complex with DNA is nearly identical to that of the free protein. The backbone
dynamics of the Mrf-2 domain free and in complex with DNA have been
characterized by (15)N NMR relaxation measurements and model-free analysis.
Chemical shift perturbations and dynamic studies suggest that two flexible
interhelical loops, the flexible C-terminal tail, and one alpha-helix are
involved in DNA recognition, indicating the importance of protein dynamics in
DNA binding. Some well-structured regions, in particular the putative
DNA-contacting helix, in Mrf-2 show a decrease in the order parameters (S(2))
upon complex formation. The less well-structured loops and the unstructured
C-terminus show reduced flexibility upon DNA binding. In addition, the
model-free analysis indicates motions on the picosecond to nanosecond and micro-
to millisecond time scales at the DNA-binding surface of the bound Mrf-2 ARID,
suggesting a model where interactions between the protein and DNA are highly
dynamic.
