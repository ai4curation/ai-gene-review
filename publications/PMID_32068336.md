A recent paper by Kidokoro et al. (2020) in The Plant Cell reported a
transgene-dependent transcriptional silencing phenomenon in the dominant ice1-1
Arabidopsis mutant containing the CBF3-LUC reporter, and questioned whether ICE1
may regulate CBF genes and may be involved in plant cold response. Here, we
evaluate available evidence supporting the involvement of ICE1 in plant cold
response, and provide ChIP-seq data showing ICE1 binding to the promoters of CBF
genes and other regulatory genes known to be critical for cold response as well
as to the promoters of some COR genes.
