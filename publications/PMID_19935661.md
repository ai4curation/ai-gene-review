Ureide catabolism in Arabidopsis thaliana and Escherichia coli.

Werner AK(1), Romeis T, Witte CP.

Author information:
(1)Freie Universität Berlin, Institut für Biologie, Abteilung Biochemie der
Pflanzen, Berlin, Germany.

The availability of whole genome sequences boosts the identification of
biochemical pathways conserved across species using tools of comparative
genomics. A cross-organism protein association analysis allowed us to identify
two enzymes, ureidoglycine aminohydrolase and ureidoglycolate amidohydrolase,
that catalyze the final reactions of purine degradation in the model plant
Arabidopsis thaliana. A similar pathway was found in Escherichia coli, while an
alternative metabolic route via ureidoglycine transaminase can be predicted for
other organisms.
