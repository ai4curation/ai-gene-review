# Purification and properties of NADP-dependent glutamate dehydrogenase from yeast nuclear fractions.
**Authors:** Camardella L, Di Prisco G, Garofano F, Guerrini AM
**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (1976)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0005-2744(76)90280-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-2744(76)90280-1)

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 1976 Apr 8;429(2):324-30. doi:
10.1016/0005-2744(76)90280-1.

Purification and properties of NADP-dependent glutamate dehydrogenase from yeast
nuclear fractions.

Camardella L, Di Prisco G, Garofano F, Guerrini AM.

1. NADP-dependent glutamate dehydrogenase (EC 1.4.1.4) extracted from nuclear
fractions of Saccharomyces cerevisiae was partially purified. The final
purification achieved was over 100-fold over the initial extract. 2. Cellulose
acetate electrophoresis shows that the preparation is close to homogeneity and
that the enzyme is slightly more anionic than cytoplasmic glutamate
dehydrogenase. 3. The response of the nuclear activity to variation of pH, of
inorganic phosphate and other electrolyte concentration and of the concentration
of the reaction substrates has been investigated. Several differences were
detected in comparison with cytoplasmic glutamate dehydrogenase.
