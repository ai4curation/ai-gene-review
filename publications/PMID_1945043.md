# Additional genes which result in an elevation of acetylcholine levels by mutations in Caenorhabditis elegans.
**Authors:** Hosono R, Kamiya Y
**Journal:** Neurosci Lett (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0304-3940(91)90270-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3940(91)90270-4)

## Abstract

1. Neurosci Lett. 1991 Jul 22;128(2):243-4. doi: 10.1016/0304-3940(91)90270-4.

Additional genes which result in an elevation of acetylcholine levels by
mutations in Caenorhabditis elegans.

Hosono R(1), Kamiya Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, School of Medicine, Kanazawa University, Japan.

Four mutant genes (unc-17, unc-18, unc-41 and unc-13) have been identified that
result in abnormal accumulation of acetylcholine (ACh). We have now identified 3
more such genes (unc-63, unc-11 and unc-64). In addition to the abnormal
accumulation of ACh, mutants in these 7 genes possess common phenotypes in
locomotion, resistance to inhibitors of acetylcholinesterase (AChE) and in
post-embryonic development. These results suggest that the 7 genes are involved
in some related functions.
