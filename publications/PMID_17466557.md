# Successful treatment of a guanidinoacetate methyltransferase deficient patient: findings with relevance to treatment strategy and pathophysiology.

## Abstract

1. Mol Genet Metab. 2007 Jul;91(3):294-6. doi: 10.1016/j.ymgme.2007.03.006. Epub

Successful treatment of a guanidinoacetate methyltransferase deficient patient:
findings with relevance to treatment strategy and pathophysiology.

Verbruggen KT(1), Sijens PE, Schulze A, Lunsing RJ, Jakobs C, Salomons GS, van
Spronsen FJ.

Author information:
(1)Beatrix Children's Hospital, University Medical Centre Groningen, University
of Groningen, The Netherlands. k.t.verbruggen@bkk.umcg.nl

Biochemical and developmental results of treatment of a guanidinoacetate
methyltransferase (GAMT) deficient patient with a mild clinical presentation and
remarkable developmental improvement after treatment are presented. Treatment
with creatine (Cr) supplementation resulted in partial normalization of cerebral
(measured with magnetic resonance proton spectroscopy) and plasma levels of Cr
and guanidinoacetate (GAA). Addition of high dose ornithine to the treatment led
to further normalization of plasma GAA, while cerebral Cr and GAA did not
improve further.
