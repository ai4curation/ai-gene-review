# Lipid rafts clustering and signalling by listeriolysin O.
**Authors:** Gekara NO, Weiss S
**Journal:** Biochem Soc Trans (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1042/BST0320712](https://doi.org/10.1042/BST0320712)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Soc Trans. 2004 Nov;32(Pt 5):712-4. doi: 10.1042/BST0320712.

Lipid rafts clustering and signalling by listeriolysin O.

Gekara NO(1), Weiss S.

Author information:
(1)Molecular Immunology, GBF, German Research Centre for Biotechnology,
Mascheroder Weg 1, D-38124 Braunschweig, Germany. nge@gbf.de

Listeriolysin O, the major virulent determinant of Listeria monocytogenes, is
known for forming pores on cholesterol-rich membranes. In the present study, we
reveal its other facet, rafts clustering. By immunofluorescence microscopy, we
show that the glycosylphosphatidylinositol-anchored proteins CD14 and CD24,
which normally exhibit uniform distribution on J774 cells, undergo clustering
upon treatment with LLO. The non-raft marker transferrin receptor is unaffected
by such treatment. Rafts clustering might explain the induction of tyrosine
phosphorylation observed on LLO-treated cells.
