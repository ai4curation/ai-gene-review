Lipid modifications of trimeric G proteins.

Wedegaertner PB(1), Wilson PT, Bourne HR.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmacology, University of California, San Francisco 94143.

G protein alpha subunits and beta gamma dimers are covalently modified by
lipids. The emerging picture is one in which attached lipids provide more than
just a nonspecific "glue" for sticking G proteins to membranes. We are only
beginning to understand how different lipid modifications of different G protein
subunits affect specific protein-protein interactions and localization to
specific cellular sites. In addition, regulation of these modifications,
particularly palmitoylation, can provide new ways to regulate signals
transmitted by G proteins.
