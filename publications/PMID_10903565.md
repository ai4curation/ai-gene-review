# A time-less function for mouse timeless.
**Authors:** Gotter AL, Manganaro T, Weaver DR, Kolakowski LF Jr, Possidente B, Sriram S, MacLaughlin DT, Reppert SM
**Journal:** Nat Neurosci (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1038/77653](https://doi.org/10.1038/77653)

## Abstract

1. Nat Neurosci. 2000 Aug;3(8):755-6. doi: 10.1038/77653.

A time-less function for mouse timeless.

Gotter AL(1), Manganaro T, Weaver DR, Kolakowski LF Jr, Possidente B, Sriram S,
MacLaughlin DT, Reppert SM.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Developmental Chronobiology, Massachusetts General Hospital,
Boston, Massachusetts 02114, USA.

The timeless (tim) gene is essential for circadian clock function in Drosophila
melanogaster. A putative mouse homolog, mTimeless (mTim), has been difficult to
place in the circadian clock of mammals. Here we show that mTim is essential for
embryonic development, but does not have substantiated circadian function.
