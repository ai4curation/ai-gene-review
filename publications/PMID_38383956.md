# Knockdown of LRCH4 Remodels Tumor Microenvironment Through Inhibiting YAP and TGF-β/Smad Signaling Pathway in Colorectal Cancer.
**Authors:** Li Z, Cui Z, Wang X, Lv Y
**Journal:** Comb Chem High Throughput Screen (2024)
**DOI:** [10.2174/0113862073267943231101065948](https://doi.org/10.2174/0113862073267943231101065948)

## Abstract

1. Comb Chem High Throughput Screen. 2024;27(12):1823-1829. doi:
10.2174/0113862073267943231101065948.

Knockdown of LRCH4 Remodels Tumor Microenvironment Through Inhibiting YAP and
TGF-β/Smad Signaling Pathway in Colorectal Cancer.

Li Z(1), Cui Z(1), Wang X(1), Lv Y(1).

Author information:
(1)The Second Hospital, Cheeloo College of Medicine, Shandong University, Jinan,
China.

BACKGROUND: Colorectal cancer is one of the most common gastrointestinal
malignancies worldwide. LRCH4 is the top 1 gene associated with an unfavorable
prognosis in colorectal cancer.
METHODS: Here, we reported that the knockdown of LRCH4 inhibited the
proliferation, migration and invasion in HT29 cells.
RESULTS: The activity of Yes-Associated Protein (YAP), a transcription factor in
the Hppo-YAP signaling pathway, was significantly inhibited by LRCH4-siRNA.
LRCH4 knockdown also reversed the EMT and regulated the expression of
extracellular matrix (ECM) protein, Fibronectin and Collagen IV in HT29 cells.
In addition, the TGF-β/Smad signaling pathway, as the downstream pathway of Yap,
was also inhibited by LRCH4 knockdown.
CONCLUSION: Knockdown of LRCH4 involved in the regulation of ECM and EMT and
inhibited YAP and the TGF-β/Smad signaling pathway in colorectal cancer cells.
Our study provided a mechanism of LRCH4 on colorectal cancer cells, and a new
potential target for clinical tumor treatment.
