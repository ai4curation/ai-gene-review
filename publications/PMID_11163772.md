# Identification of two proteins, S14 and UIP1, that interact with UCH37.
**Authors:** Li T, Duan W, Yang H, Lee MK, Bte Mustafa F, Lee BH, Teo TS
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(00)02436-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(00)02436-4)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2001 Jan 19;488(3):201-5. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(00)02436-4.

Identification of two proteins, S14 and UIP1, that interact with UCH37.

Li T(1), Duan W, Yang H, Lee MK, Bte Mustafa F, Lee BH, Teo TS.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Faculty of Medicine, National University of
Singapore, 10 Kent Ridge Crescent, 119260, Singapore, Singapore.

By the use of the yeast two-hybrid screen we have identified two proteins that
interacted with UCH37: S14, which is a subunit of PA700 and a novel protein,
UIP1 (UCH37 interacting protein 1). The interaction of UCH37 with S14 or UIP1
was confirmed by in vitro binding assay and in vivo co-immunoprecipitation
analysis. The C-terminal extension of UCH37 is essential for interaction with
S14 or UIP1 as shown by the yeast two-hybrid assay and the in vitro binding
assay. Furthermore, UIP1 blocked the interaction between UCH37 and S14 in vitro.
