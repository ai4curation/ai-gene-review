# Immunolocalization of two hydrogenosomal enzymes of Trichomonas vaginalis.
**Authors:** Brugerolle G, Bricheux G, Coffe G
**Journal:** Parasitol Res (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1007/pl00008503](https://doi.org/10.1007/pl00008503)

## Abstract

1. Parasitol Res. 2000 Jan;86(1):30-5. doi: 10.1007/pl00008503.

Immunolocalization of two hydrogenosomal enzymes of Trichomonas vaginalis.

Brugerolle G(1), Bricheux G, Coffe G.

Author information:
(1)Biologie des Protistes, UPRESA 6023, Université Blaise Pascal de
Clermont-Ferrand, Aubiere, France. brugerolle@cicsun.univ-bpclermont.fr

Three monoclonal antibodies specific for malic enzyme and for the alpha- and
beta-subunits, respectively, of the succinyl-coenzyme A (CoA) synthetase of
Trichomonas vaginalis were used to immunolocalize these proteins in the cell.
All antibodies labeled the hydrogenosome matrix as determined both by
immunofluorescence and by immunogold staining. There was no labeling on the cell
surface or in any other cell compartment. These results support the idea that
these proteins are restricted to a hydrogenosomal function and do not play a
role as adhesins at the plasma membrane surface.
