# Growth phase-dependent transcription of emrKY, a homolog of multidrug efflux emrAB genes of Escherichia coli, is induced by tetracycline.
**Authors:** Tanabe H, Yamasak K, Furue M, Yamamoto K, Katoh A, Yamamoto M, Yoshioka S, Tagami H, Aiba HA, Utsumi R
**Journal:** J Gen Appl Microbiol (1997)
**DOI:** [10.2323/jgam.43.257](https://doi.org/10.2323/jgam.43.257)

## Abstract

1. J Gen Appl Microbiol. 1997 Oct;43(5):257-263. doi: 10.2323/jgam.43.257.

Growth phase-dependent transcription of emrKY, a homolog of multidrug efflux
emrAB genes of Escherichia coli, is induced by tetracycline.

Tanabe H(1), Yamasak K, Furue M, Yamamoto K, Katoh A, Yamamoto M, Yoshioka S,
Tagami H, Aiba HA, Utsumi R.

Author information:
(1)Department of Agricultural Chemistry, Faculty of Agriculture, Kinki
University, 3327-204, Nakamachi, Nara 631, Japan.

The genes emrK and emrY were found between genes dsdA and evgA at 51 min on the
Escherichia coli chromosome and form an operon. EmrK and EmrY are 50.4 and 63.3%
identical in amino acid sequences to EmrA and EmrB, respectively, which together
make up a multidrug resistant pump. To show that the emrKY operon can be
expressed, we cloned the promoter with pMC1403 and constructed an emrK-lacZ'
protein fusion plasmid, pMKD1. In E. coli MC4100 containing pMKD1, its
expression was increased in the presence of a subinhibitory concentration of
tetracycline, chloramphenicol or salicylate, but not by carbonylcyanide
m-chlorophenylhydrazone, nalidixic acid or kanamycin. Furthermore, we have shown
that emrKY transcription dependent on the growth phase is actually induced by
tetracycline using a S1 nuclease protection assay.
