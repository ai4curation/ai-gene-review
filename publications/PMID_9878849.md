Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics and Communicable Diseases, University of Michigan,
Ann Arbor, MI 48109, USA.mruss@umich.edu

We have cloned and characterized the coding sequence of the human HAND2 basic
helix-loop-helix transcription factor. The amino acid sequence includes an
amino-terminal polyalanine repeat which is precisely conserved in the rat HAND2
gene. Northern analysis indicates that the HAND2 transcript is 2.3 kb in length
and strongly expressed in the human heart.
