# The secretory pathway Ca2+/Mn2+-ATPase 2 is a Golgi-localized pump with high affinity for Ca2+ ions.
**Authors:** Vanoevelen J, Dode L, Van Baelen K, Fairclough RJ, Missiaen L, Raeymaekers L, Wuytack F
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M501026200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M501026200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2005 Jun 17;280(24):22800-8. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M501026200. Epub
2005  Apr 14.

The secretory pathway Ca2+/Mn2+-ATPase 2 is a Golgi-localized pump with high
affinity for Ca2+ ions.

Vanoevelen J(1), Dode L, Van Baelen K, Fairclough RJ, Missiaen L, Raeymaekers L,
Wuytack F.

Author information:
(1)Laboratorium voor Fysiologie, Katholieke Universiteit Leuven, Campus
Gasthuisberg O/N, Belgium.

Accumulation of Ca(2+) into the Golgi apparatus is mediated by
sarco(endo)plasmic reticulum Ca(2+)-ATPases (SERCAs) and by secretory pathway
Ca(2+)-ATPases (SPCAs). Mammals and birds express in addition to the
housekeeping SPCA1 (human gene name ATP2C1, cytogenetic position 3q22.1) a
homologous SPCA2 isoform (human gene name ATP2C2, cytogenetic position 16q24.1).
We show here that both genes present an identical exon/intron layout. We
confirmed that hSPCA2 has the ability to transport Ca(2+), demonstrated its
Mn(2+)-transporting activity, showed its Ca(2+)- and Mn(2+)-dependent
phosphoprotein intermediate formation, and documented the insensitivity of these
functional activities to thapsigargin inhibition. The mRNA encoding hSPCA2
showed a limited tissue expression pattern mainly confined to the
gastrointestinal and respiratory tract, prostate, thyroid, salivary, and mammary
glands. Immunocytochemical localization in human colon sections presented a
typical apical juxtanuclear Golgi-like staining. The expression in COS-1 cells
allowed the direct demonstration of (45)Ca(2+) (K(0.5) = 0.27 microm) or
(54)Mn(2+) transport into an A23187-releasable compartment.
