## Abstract

1. Science. 1993 Feb 12;259(5097):971-4. doi: 10.1126/science.8438157.

Anti-oncogenic and oncogenic potentials of interferon regulatory factors-1 and
-2.

Harada H(1), Kitagawa M, Tanaka N, Yamamoto H, Harada K, Ishihara M, Taniguchi
T.

Author information:
(1)Institute for Molecular and Cellular Biology, Osaka University, Japan.

Interferon regulatory factor-1 (IRF-1), a transcriptional activator, and IRF-2,
its antagonistic repressor, have been identified as regulators of type I
interferon and interferon-inducible genes. The IRF-1 gene is itself
interferon-inducible and hence may be one of the target genes critical for
interferon action. When the IRF-2 gene was overexpressed in NIH 3T3 cells, the
cells became transformed and displayed enhanced tumorigenicity in nude mice.
This transformed phenotype was reversed by concomitant overexpression of the
IRF-1 gene. Thus, restrained cell growth depends on a balance between these two
mutually antagonistic transcription factors.
