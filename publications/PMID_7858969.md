# Enzymatic synthesis of isotopically labeled isoprenoid diphosphates.

## Abstract

1. Bioorg Med Chem. 1994 Jul;2(7):631-7. doi: 10.1016/0968-0896(94)85011-9.

Enzymatic synthesis of isotopically labeled isoprenoid diphosphates.

Christensen DJ(1), Poulter CD.

Author information:
(1)University of Utah, Department of Chemistry, Salt Lake City 84112.

Recombinant yeast isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP) isomerase and avian farnesyl
diphosphate (FPP) synthase from overproducing strains of Escherichia coli were
used to synthesize FPP from IPP and dimethylallyl diphosphate (DMAPP).
[2,4,5-13C3]IPP and [2,4,5-13C3]DMAPP were synthesized from ethyl
[2-13C]bromoacetate and [1,3-13C2]acetone. Thes compounds were used as
substrates for enzymatic synthesis of FPP selectivity labeled at the first or
third isoprene residue or at all three.
