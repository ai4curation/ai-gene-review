# Purification of the major histocompatibility complex class I transcription factor H2TF1. The full-length product of the nfkb2 gene.
**Authors:** Potter DA, Larson CJ, Eckes P, Schmid RM, Nabel GJ, Verdine GL, Sharp PA
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1993)
**PMC:** [PMC2758046](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2758046/)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1993 Sep 5;268(25):18882-90.

Purification of the major histocompatibility complex class I transcription
factor H2TF1. The full-length product of the nfkb2 gene.

Potter DA(1), Larson CJ, Eckes P, Schmid RM, Nabel GJ, Verdine GL, Sharp PA.

Author information:
(1)Center for Cancer Research, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge
02139.

H2TF1 is a ubiquitous major histocompatibility complex (MHC) class I-specific
transcription factor, which binds to the palindromic kappa B enhancer site
upstream of MHC class I genes. Here we report that H2TF1 consists of a
polypeptide with relative molecular mass 110,000, that corresponds to the
predicted 100-kDa product (NF-kappa B2 p100) encoded by the candidate
proto-oncogene nfkb2 (lyt-10). H2TF1 was purified by a novel affinity
chromatography method and identified as the NF-kappa B2 p100 polypeptide by
peptide sequencing as well as by reactivity with a specific antiserum. Purified
H2TF1 binds the MHC kappa B site with high affinity (KD = 3 x 10(-11) M), in
contrast with previous reports that NF-kappa B2 p100 did not bind DNA.
