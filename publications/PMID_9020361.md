# MAP3K-related kinase involved in NF-kappaB induction by TNF, CD95 and IL-1.
**Authors:** Malinin NL, Boldin MP, Kovalenko AV, Wallach D
**Journal:** Nature (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1038/385540a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/385540a0)

## Abstract

1. Nature. 1997 Feb 6;385(6616):540-4. doi: 10.1038/385540a0.

MAP3K-related kinase involved in NF-kappaB induction by TNF, CD95 and IL-1.

Malinin NL(1), Boldin MP, Kovalenko AV, Wallach D.

Author information:
(1)Department of Membrane Research and Biophysics, The Weizmann Institute of
Science, Rehovot, Israel.

Several members of the tumour-necrosis/nerve-growth factor (TNF/NGF) receptor
family activate the transcription factor NF-kappaB through a common adaptor
protein, Traf2 (refs 1-5), whereas the interleukin 1 type-I receptor activates
NF-kappaB independently of Traf2 (ref. 4). We have now cloned a new protein
kinase, NIK, which binds to Traf2 and stimulates NF-kappaB activity. This kinase
shares sequence similarity with several MAPKK kinases. Expression in cells of
kinase-deficient NIK mutants fails to stimulate NF-kappaB and blocks its
induction by TNF, by either of the two TNF receptors or by the receptor CD95
(Fas/Apo-1), and by TRADD, RIP and MORT1/FADD, which are adaptor proteins that
bind to these receptors. It also blocked NF-kappaB induction by interleukin-1.
Our findings indicate that NIK participates in an NF-kappaB-inducing signalling
cascade common to receptors of the TNF/NGF family and to the interleukin-1
type-I receptor.
