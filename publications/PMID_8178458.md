# Bacteriophage P2 and P4 morphogenesis: identification and characterization of the portal protein.
**Authors:** Rishovd S, Marvik OJ, Jacobsen E, Lindqvist BH
**Journal:** Virology (1994)
**DOI:** [10.1006/viro.1994.1238](https://doi.org/10.1006/viro.1994.1238)

## Abstract

1. Virology. 1994 May 1;200(2):744-51. doi: 10.1006/viro.1994.1238.

Bacteriophage P2 and P4 morphogenesis: identification and characterization of
the portal protein.

Rishovd S(1), Marvik OJ, Jacobsen E, Lindqvist BH.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Biology, University of Oslo, Norway.

The portal structure has been implicated in several aspects of the bacteriophage
life cycle, including capsid assembly initiation and DNA packaging. Here we
present evidence that P2 gene Q codes for the P2 and P4 portal protein. First,
microsequencing shows that capsid protein h6 is derived from gpQ, most probably
by proteolytic cleavage. Second, antibodies against gpQ bind to the portal
structure in disrupted P2 phage virions, as observed by electron microscopy.
Third, gpQ partially purified from an overexpressing plasmid assembles into
portal-like structures. We also show by microsequencing that capsid protein h7
is encoded by the P2 scaffold gene, O, and is probably derived from gpO by
proteolytic cleavage. Previous work has demonstrated processing of the major
capsid protein. Thus, all essential capsid proteins of P2 and P4 are
proteolytically cleaved during the morphogenetic process.
