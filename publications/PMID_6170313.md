# Purification and characterization of hypoxanthine-guanine phosphoribosyltransferase from Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
**Authors:** Nussbaum RL, Caskey CT
**Journal:** Biochemistry (1981)
**DOI:** [10.1021/bi00519a011](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi00519a011)

## Abstract

1. Biochemistry. 1981 Aug 4;20(16):4584-90. doi: 10.1021/bi00519a011.

Purification and characterization of hypoxanthine-guanine
phosphoribosyltransferase from Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Nussbaum RL, Caskey CT.

Hypoxanthine-guanine phosphoribosyltransferase (HPRT) was purified 12 000-fold
to homogeneity from yeast by a three-step procedure including acid
precipitation, anion-exchange chromatography, and guanosine 5' -monophosphate
affinity chromatography. The enzyme is a dimer consisting of two, probably
identical, subunits of Mr 29 500. The enzyme recognized hypoxanthine and
guanine, but not adenine or xanthine, as substrates. An antiserum against both
native and denatured enzyme has been raised and shown to be specific for the
enzyme. The antiserum has no affinity for Chinese hamster or human HPRT but does
recognize subunits of yeast HPRT as well as some cyanogen bromide fragments of
the enzyme.
