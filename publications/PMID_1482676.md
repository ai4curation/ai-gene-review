# Species and tissue specificity of mammalian GTP cyclohydrolase I messenger RNA.
**Authors:** Gütlich M, Schott K, Werner T, Bacher A, Ziegler I
**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (1992)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0167-4781(92)90112-d](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-4781(92)90112-d)

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 1992 Dec 29;1171(2):133-40. doi:
10.1016/0167-4781(92)90112-d.

Species and tissue specificity of mammalian GTP cyclohydrolase I messenger RNA.

Gütlich M(1), Schott K, Werner T, Bacher A, Ziegler I.

Author information:
(1)GSF-Forschungszentrum für Umwelt und Gesundheit, Institut für Experimentelle
Hämatologie, München, Germany.

Northern blot analysis of rat RNA from cell lines and isolated organs with a
specific rat cDNA probe detected two GTP cyclohydrolase I mRNA species of
approx. 1.4 and 3.6 kb. The ratio between these two species varies between 0.6
and 2.4 in different rat organs. Using primers derived from highly conserved
regions in the rat and Escherichia coli cDNA sequences a human GTP
cyclohydrolase I probe was obtained by means of reverse transcription and PCR
(polymerase chain reaction). The human PCR product consisting of 555 bp was
cloned and sequenced. It shows a 92% identity with the published sequence of the
rat gene. The analysis of various human cell lines with this specific probe
shows only one species of GTP cyclohydrolase I mRNA with an approximate size of
3.6 kb.
