The 1.3-megadalton transcription factor IID (TFIID) is required for
preinitiation complex (PIC) assembly and RNA polymerase II (Pol II)-mediated
transcription initiation on almost all genes. The 26-subunit Mediator stimulates
transcription and cyclin-dependent kinase 7 (CDK7)-mediated phosphorylation of
the Pol II C-terminal domain (CTD). We determined the structures of human
Mediator in the Tail module-extended (at near-atomic resolution) and Tail-bent
conformations and structures of TFIID-based PIC-Mediator (76 polypeptides, ~4.1
megadaltons) in four distinct conformations. PIC-Mediator assembly induces
concerted reorganization (Head-tilting and Middle-down) of Mediator and creates
a Head-Middle sandwich, which stabilizes two CTD segments and brings CTD to CDK7
for phosphorylation; this suggests a CTD-gating mechanism favorable for
phosphorylation. The TFIID-based PIC architecture modulates Mediator
organization and TFIIH stabilization, underscoring the importance of TFIID in
orchestrating PIC-Mediator assembly.
