# Borg proteins control septin organization and are negatively regulated by Cdc42.
**Authors:** Joberty G, Perlungher RR, Sheffield PJ, Kinoshita M, Noda M, Haystead T, Macara IG
**Journal:** Nat Cell Biol (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1038/ncb1001-861](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb1001-861)

## Abstract

1. Nat Cell Biol. 2001 Oct;3(10):861-6. doi: 10.1038/ncb1001-861.

Borg proteins control septin organization and are negatively regulated by Cdc42.

Joberty G(1), Perlungher RR, Sheffield PJ, Kinoshita M, Noda M, Haystead T,
Macara IG.

Author information:
(1)Markey Center for Cell Signaling and Department of Pharmacology, University
of Virginia, Charlottesville, Virginia 22908, USA. gmj4h@virginia.edu

The Cdc42 GTPase binds to numerous effector proteins that control cell polarity,
cytoskeletal remodelling and vesicle transport. In many cases the signalling
pathways downstream of these effectors are not known. Here we show that the
Cdc42 effectors Borg1 to Borg3 bind to septin GTPases. Endogenous septin Cdc10
and Borg3 proteins can be immunoprecipitated together by an anti-Borg3 antibody.
The ectopic expression of Borgs disrupts normal septin organization. Cdc42
negatively regulates this effect and inhibits the binding of Borg3 to septins.
Borgs are therefore the first known regulators of mammalian septin organization
and provide an unexpected link between the septin and Cdc42 GTPases.
