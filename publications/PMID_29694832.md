Cdc14 protein phosphatase is critical for late mitosis progression in budding
yeast, although its orthologs in other organisms, including mammalian cells,
function as stress-responsive phosphatases. We found herein unexpected roles of
Cdc14 in autophagy induction after nutrient starvation and target of rapamycin
complex 1 (TORC1) kinase inactivation. TORC1 kinase phosphorylates Atg13 to
repress autophagy under nutrient-rich conditions, but if TORC1 becomes inactive
upon nutrient starvation or rapamycin treatment, Atg13 is rapidly
dephosphorylated and autophagy is induced. Cdc14 phosphatase was required for
optimal Atg13 dephosphorylation, pre-autophagosomal structure formation, and
autophagy induction after TORC1 inactivation. In addition, Cdc14 was required
for sufficient induction of ATG8 and ATG13 expression. Moreover, Cdc14
activation provoked autophagy even under normal conditions. This study
identified a novel role of Cdc14 as the stress-responsive phosphatase for
autophagy induction in budding yeast.
