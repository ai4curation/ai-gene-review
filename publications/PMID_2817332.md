# Assay of L-3-hydroxyacyl-coenzyme A dehydrogenase with substrates of different chain lengths.
**Authors:** He XY, Yang SY, Schulz H
**Journal:** Anal Biochem (1989)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0003-2697(89)90095-x](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-2697(89)90095-x)

## Abstract

1. Anal Biochem. 1989 Jul;180(1):105-9. doi: 10.1016/0003-2697(89)90095-x.

Assay of L-3-hydroxyacyl-coenzyme A dehydrogenase with substrates of different
chain lengths.

He XY(1), Yang SY, Schulz H.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemistry, City College of The City University of New York, New
York 10031.

A method for assaying L-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase (EC 1.1.1.35) which
permits rate measurements with L-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA substrates of various chain
lengths at physiological pH is described. The method is based on a coupled assay
system in which 3-ketoacyl-CoA compounds formed by the dehydrogenase are cleaved
by 3-ketoacyl-CoA thiolase (EC 2.3.1.16) in the presence of CoASH. The
advantages of this assay method are its irreversibility and elimination of
product inhibition. The assay procedure was used to determine the kinetic
parameters (Km, Vmax) of pig heart L-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase with
several substrates of various chain lengths. The data obtained show the enzyme
to be most active with medium-chain substrates whereas Km values for
medium-chain and long-chain substrates are almost equal but much lower than
those previously reported.
