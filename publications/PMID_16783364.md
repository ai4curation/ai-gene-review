# Activation of the DExD/H-box protein Dbp5 by the nuclear-pore protein Gle1 and its coactivator InsP6 is required for mRNA export.
**Authors:** Weirich CS, Erzberger JP, Flick JS, Berger JM, Thorner J, Weis K

Comment in
    Nat Cell Biol. 2006 Jul;8(7):645-7. doi: 10.1038/ncb0706-645.

The DExD/H-box ATPase Dbp5 is essential for nuclear mRNA export, although its
precise role in this process remains poorly understood. Here, we identify the
nuclear pore protein Gle1 as a cellular activator of Dbp5. Dbp5 alone is unable
to stably bind RNA or effectively hydrolyse ATP under physiological conditions,
but addition of Gle1 dramatically stimulates these activities. A gle1 point
mutant deficient for Dbp5 stimulation in vitro displays an mRNA export defect in
vivo, indicating that activation of Dbp5 is an essential function of Gle1.
Interestingly, Gle1 binds directly to inositol hexakisphosphate (InsP6) and
InsP6 potentiates the Gle1-mediated stimulation of Dbp5. Dominant mutations in
DBP5 and GLE1 that rescue mRNA export phenotypes associated with the lack of
InsP6 mimic the InsP6 effects in vitro. Our results define specific functions
for Gle1 and InsP6 in mRNA export and suggest that local activation of Dbp5 at
the nuclear pore is critical for mRNA export.
