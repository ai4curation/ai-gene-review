## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1974 Aug;119(2):431-42. doi: 10.1128/jb.119.2.431-442.1974.

Glycerol metabolism in Bacillus subtilis: gene-enzyme relationships.

Lindgren V, Rutberg L.

Bacillus subtilis mutants unable to catabolize glycerol (Glp mutants) were
isolated and mapped. The location of the mutations on the chromosome was
determined by a density transfer technique and confirmed by PBS1 transduction
and transformation. The different mutations were ordered relative to each other.
Mutations rendering the cells glycerol auxotrophic were also mapped and found
not to be linked to the Glp mutations.
