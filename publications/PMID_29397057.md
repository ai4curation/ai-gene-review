# Role of A-kinase anchoring protein 95 in the regulation of cytochrome P450 family 19 subfamily A member 1 (CYP19A1) in human ovarian granulosa cells.
**Authors:** Gu Y, Xu W, Zhuang B, Fu W
**Journal:** Reprod Fertil Dev (2018)
**DOI:** [10.1071/RD17313](https://doi.org/10.1071/RD17313)

## Abstract

1. Reprod Fertil Dev. 2018 Jul;30(8):1128-1136. doi: 10.1071/RD17313.

Role of A-kinase anchoring protein 95 in the regulation of cytochrome P450
family 19 subfamily A member 1 (CYP19A1) in human ovarian granulosa cells.

Gu Y(1), Xu W(1), Zhuang B(1), Fu W(1).

Author information:
(1)Obstetrics and Gynecology Hospital of Fudan University, No. 128 Shenyang

Irregular expression of cytochrome P450 family 19 subfamily A member 1 (CYP19A1)
is involved in the development of polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS). Activation
of the cAMP/protein kinase A (PKA)/cAMP response element-binding protein (CREB)
pathway plays a crucial role in FSH regulation of CYP19A1 in human ovarian
granulosa cells. A-Kinase anchor protein 95 (AKAP95) is known to confine PKA to
the nucleus. However, it is unclear whether anchoring PKA to the nucleus is
essential for the induction of CYP19A1 by FSH in human ovarian granulosa cells.
Using the human granulosa cell line KGN and primary cultured human luteinised
granulosa cells (hLGCs), we found that knockdown of AKAP8, the gene encoding
AKAP95, or inhibition of AKAP95 reduced the amount of PKA anchored in the
nucleus and attenuated the phosphorylation of CREB by either FSH or activation
of the cAMP/PKA pathway. Moreover, knockdown of AKAP8 or inhibition of AKAP95
also significantly attenuated FSH-induced CYP19A1 expression and oestrogen
synthesis. Furthermore, significant decreases in AKAP95 and CYP19A1 were
observed in hLGCs obtained from PCOS patients. The results of the present study
demonstrate a crucial role for AKAP95 in CYP19A1 expression and oestrogen
synthesis in hLGCs, which implies that AKAP95 may be involved in the
pathogenesis of PCOS.
