# Claudin-like protein 24 interacts with the VEGFR-2 and VEGFR-3 pathways and regulates lymphatic vessel development.
**Authors:** Saharinen P, Helotera H, Miettinen J, Norrmen C, D'Amico G, Jeltsch M, Langenberg T, Vandevelde W, Ny A, Dewerchin M, Carmeliet P, Alitalo K

## Abstract

1. Genes Dev. 2010 May;24(9):875-80. doi: 10.1101/gad.565010.

Claudin-like protein 24 interacts with the VEGFR-2 and VEGFR-3 pathways and
regulates lymphatic vessel development.

Saharinen P(1), Helotera H, Miettinen J, Norrmen C, D'Amico G, Jeltsch M,
Langenberg T, Vandevelde W, Ny A, Dewerchin M, Carmeliet P, Alitalo K.

Author information:
(1)Molecular/Cancer Biology Program, Biomedicum Helsinki, University of
Helsinki, Helsinki FIN-00014, Finland.

The Claudin-like protein of 24 kDa (CLP24) is a hypoxia-regulated transmembrane
protein of unknown function. We show here that clp24 knockdown in Danio rerio
and Xenopus laevis results in defective lymphatic development. Targeted
disruption of Clp24 in mice led to enlarged lymphatic vessels having an abnormal
smooth muscle cell coating. We also show that the Clp24(-/-) phenotype was
further aggravated in the Vegfr2(+/LacZ) or Vegfr3(+/LacZ) backgrounds and that
CLP24 interacts with vascular endothelial growth factor receptor-2 (VEGFR-2) and
VEGFR-3 and attenuates the transcription factor CREB phosphorylation via these
receptors. Our results indicate that CLP24 is a novel regulator of VEGFR-2 and
VEGFR-3 signaling pathways and of normal lymphatic vessel structure.
