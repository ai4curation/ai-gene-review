# Regulated expression of cytochrome P-450scc (cholesterol-side-chain cleavage enzyme) in cultured cell lines detected by antibody against bacterially expressed human protein.
**Authors:** Hu MC, Guo IC, Lin JH, Chung BC
**Journal:** Biochem J (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1042/bj2740813](https://doi.org/10.1042/bj2740813)
**PMC:** [PMC1149983](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1149983/)

## Abstract

1. Biochem J. 1991 Mar 15;274 ( Pt 3)(Pt 3):813-7. doi: 10.1042/bj2740813.

Regulated expression of cytochrome P-450scc (cholesterol-side-chain cleavage
enzyme) in cultured cell lines detected by antibody against bacterially
expressed human protein.

Hu MC(1), Guo IC, Lin JH, Chung BC.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Molecular Biology, Academia Sinica, Taipei, Taiwan, Republic of
China.

The first step in the synthesis of steroids is catalysed by cytochrome P-450ssc
(cholesterol-side-chain cleavage enzyme). We have investigated the synthesis of
this enzyme in three cultured cell lines at the protein and hormone secretion
levels. Hormone levels were measured by an enzyme immunoassay using a monoclonal
antibody against progesterone. The protein level was detected using polyclonal
antibodies directed against a P-450scc fusion protein overproduced in
Escherichia coli. Utilizing a bacteriophage T7 promoter expression system, a
large amount of human P-450scc fusion protein was produced and easily purified.
P-450scc was synthesized in the mouse adrenal tumour cell line Y1 and human
choriocarcinoma cell line JEG-3, but not in monkey kidney cell line COS-1. The
production of P-450scc in Y1 and JEG-3 cells was stimulated by 8-bromo cyclic
AMP, the effect of which was not observed until 6 h after induction and was more
pronounced at 24 h. Y1 and JEG-3 cells exhibited a difference in progesterone
secretion after induction.
