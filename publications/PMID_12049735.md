Comment in
    Mol Cell. 2002 May;9(5):928-9. doi: 10.1016/s1097-2765(02)00536-1.

Terminase enzymes mediate genome "packaging" during the reproduction of DNA
viruses. In lambda, the gpNu1 subunit guides site-specific assembly of terminase
onto DNA. The structure of the dimeric DNA binding domain of gpNu1 was solved
using nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy. Its fold contains a unique winged
helix-turn-helix (wHTH) motif within a novel scaffold. Surprisingly, a predicted
P loop ATP binding motif is in fact the wing of the DNA binding motif.
Structural and genetic analysis has identified determinants of DNA recognition
specificity within the wHTH motif and the DNA recognition sequence. The
structure reveals an unexpected DNA binding mode and provides a mechanistic
basis for the concerted action of gpNu1 and Escherichia coli integration host
factor during assembly of the packaging machinery.
