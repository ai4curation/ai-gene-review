# Breast cancer metastasis suppressor 1 (BRMS1) forms complexes with retinoblastoma-binding protein 1 (RBP1) and the mSin3 histone deacetylase complex and represses transcription.
**Authors:** Meehan WJ, Samant RS, Hopper JE, Carrozza MJ, Shevde LA, Workman JL, Eckert KA, Verderame MF, Welch DR
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M307969200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M307969200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2004 Jan 9;279(2):1562-9. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M307969200. Epub 2003

retinoblastoma-binding protein 1 (RBP1) and the mSin3 histone deacetylase
complex and represses transcription.

Meehan WJ(1), Samant RS, Hopper JE, Carrozza MJ, Shevde LA, Workman JL, Eckert

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology, Jake Gittlen Cancer Research Institute, The

17033-0850, USA.

Breast cancer metastasis suppressor 1 (BRMS1) suppresses metastasis of multiple
human and murine cancer cells without inhibiting tumorigenicity. By yeast
two-hybrid and co-immunoprecipitation, BRMS1 interacts with retinoblastoma
binding protein 1 and at least seven members of the mSin3 histone deacetylase
(HDAC) complex in human breast and melanoma cell lines. BRMS1
co-immunoprecipitates enzymatically active HDAC proteins and represses
transcription when recruited to a Gal4 promoter in vivo. BRMS1 exists in large
mSin3 complex(es) of approximately 1.4-1.9 MDa, but also forms smaller complexes
with HDAC1. Deletion analyses show that the carboxyl-terminal 42 amino acids of
BRMS1 are not critical for interaction with much of the mSin3 complex and that
BRMS1 appears to have more than one binding point to the complex. These results
further show that BRMS1 may participate in transcriptional regulation via
interaction with the mSin3.HDAC complex and suggest a novel mechanism by which
BRMS1 might suppress cancer metastasis.
