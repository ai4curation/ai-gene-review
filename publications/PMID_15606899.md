# Beta-amyloid protein converting enzyme 1 and brain-specific type II membrane protein BRI3: binding partners processed by furin.
**Authors:** Wickham L, Benjannet S, Marcinkiewicz E, Chretien M, Seidah NG
**Journal:** J Neurochem (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1471-4159.2004.02840.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1471-4159.2004.02840.x)

## Abstract

1. J Neurochem. 2005 Jan;92(1):93-102. doi: 10.1111/j.1471-4159.2004.02840.x.

Beta-amyloid protein converting enzyme 1 and brain-specific type II membrane
protein BRI3: binding partners processed by furin.

Wickham L(1), Benjannet S, Marcinkiewicz E, Chretien M, Seidah NG.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Biochemical Neuroendocrinology, Clinical Research Institute of
Montreal, Montreal, Quebec, Canada.

Using a yeast two-hybrid system, we screened a human brain cDNA library for
possible interacting proteins with the C-terminal cytosolic tail of the
beta-secretase beta-amyloid protein converting enzyme (BACE)1. This identified
seven potential candidates, including the brain-specific type II membrane
protein BRI3. Co-localization and co-immunoprecipitation experiments confirmed
that BACE1 and BRI3 co-localize and interact with each other via the cytosolic
tail of BACE1. Furthermore, pulse and pulse-chase analyses revealed that the
pro-protein convertases furin, and to a lesser extent PC7 and PC5A, process BRI3
into a C-terminal secreted approximately 4-kDa product. Thus, furin efficiently
processes both pro-BACE1 and its novel interacting protein pro-BRI3.
