# Drosophila nicastrin is essential for the intramembranous cleavage of notch.
**Authors:** López-Schier H, St Johnston D
**Journal:** Dev Cell (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1534-5807(01)00109-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1534-5807(01)00109-5)

## Abstract

1. Dev Cell. 2002 Jan;2(1):79-89. doi: 10.1016/s1534-5807(01)00109-5.

Drosophila nicastrin is essential for the intramembranous cleavage of notch.

López-Schier H(1), St Johnston D.

Author information:
(1)Wellcome/CRC Institute and Department of Genetics, University of Cambridge,
Tennis Court Road, CB2 1QR, Cambridge, United Kingdom.

The catalytic subunit of gamma-secretase is thought to be Presenilin, which is
required for both the cleavage of APP and in the processing of Notch. Presenilin
is found in a multisubunit complex that also contains Nicastrin. Nicastrin has
been implicated in APP processing, but its role in Notch signaling remains
unclear. Here we show that Drosophila Nicastrin is required for Notch signaling,
and acts specifically at the S3 cleavage step. Partially processed Notch
accumulates apically in nicastrin and presenilin mutant follicle cells.
nicastrin and presenilin mutations also disrupt the spectrin cytoskeleton,
suggesting that the gamma-secretase complex has another function in Drosophila
in addition to its role in processing Notch and APP.
