# Isolation of the ERG12 gene of Saccharomyces cerevisiae encoding mevalonate kinase.
**Authors:** Oulmouden A, Karst F
**Journal:** Gene (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0378-1119(90)90039-t](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-1119(90)90039-t)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 1990 Apr 16;88(2):253-7. doi: 10.1016/0378-1119(90)90039-t.

Isolation of the ERG12 gene of Saccharomyces cerevisiae encoding mevalonate
kinase.

Oulmouden A(1), Karst F.

Author information:
(1)Laboratoire de Biochimie et Génétique des Microorganismes, Université de
Poitiers, France.

The ERG12 gene of Saccharomyces cerevisiae has been cloned by complementation of
an erg12-1 mutation affecting mevalonate kinase. From the 2.8-kb insert
isolated, the functional gene has been localized on a DNA fragment of 2.1 kb.
The mRNA is 1.45 kb long. Gene disruption shows that the ERG12 gene is essential
in yeast, both for spore germination and vegetative growth.
