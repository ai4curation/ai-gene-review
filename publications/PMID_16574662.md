# Nuclear Rho kinase, ROCK2, targets p300 acetyltransferase.

Rho-associated coiled-coil protein kinase (ROCK) is an effector for the small
GTPase Rho and plays a pivotal role in diverse cellular activities, including
cell adhesion, cytokinesis, and gene expression, primarily through an alteration
of actin cytoskeleton dynamics. Here, we show that ROCK2 is localized in the
nucleus and associates with p300 acetyltransferase both in vitro and in cells.
Nuclear ROCK2 is present in a large protein complex and partially cofractionates
with p300 by gel filtration analysis. By immunofluorescence, ROCK2 partially
colocalizes with p300 in distinct insoluble nuclear structures. ROCK2
phosphorylates p300 in vitro, and nuclear-restricted expression of
constitutively active ROCK2 induces p300 phosphorylation in cells. p300
acetyltransferase activity is dependent on its phosphorylation status in cells,
and p300 phosphorylation by ROCK2 results in an increase in its
acetyltransferase activity in vitro. These observations suggest that
nucleus-localized ROCK2 targets p300 for phosphorylation to regulate its
acetyltransferase activity.
