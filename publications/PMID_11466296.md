## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 2001 Aug;183(16):4910-3. doi: 10.1128/JB.183.16.4910-4913.2001.

YLR209c encodes Saccharomyces cerevisiae purine nucleoside phosphorylase.

Lecoq K(1), Belloc I, Desgranges C, Konrad M, Daignan-Fornier B.

Author information:
(1)Institut de Biochimie et Génétique Cellulaires, CNRS UMR5095, F-33077
Bordeaux Cedex, France.

The yeast YLR209c (PNP1) gene encodes a protein highly similar to purine
nucleoside phosphorylases. This protein specifically metabolized inosine and
guanosine. Disruption of PNP1 led to inosine and guanosine excretion in the
medium, thus showing that PNP1 plays an important role in the metabolism of
these purine nucleosides in vivo.
