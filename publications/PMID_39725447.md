# Anaerobic plasmalogen production in recombinant Escherichia coli carrying plasmalogen synthase gene from Selenomonas ruminantium.
**Authors:** Yoshii N, Higuchi K, Onodera T, Abe N, Kaneko J
**Journal:** Biosci Biotechnol Biochem (2025)
**DOI:** [10.1093/bbb/zbae208](https://doi.org/10.1093/bbb/zbae208)

## Abstract

1. Biosci Biotechnol Biochem. 2025 Mar 24;89(4):594-598. doi:
10.1093/bbb/zbae208.

Anaerobic plasmalogen production in recombinant Escherichia coli carrying
plasmalogen synthase gene from Selenomonas ruminantium.

Yoshii N(1), Higuchi K(1), Onodera T(2), Abe N(1), Kaneko J(1).

Author information:
(1)Department of Agricultural Chemistry, Graduate School of Agricultural
Science, Tohoku University, Sendai, Miyagi, Japan.
(2)Department of Nutritional Sciences, Faculty of Health and Welfare, Nayoro
City University, Nayoro, Hokkaido, Japan.

Escherichia coli expressing SrPlsAR from Selenomonas ruminantium produces
plasmalogen, comprising almost 60% of the total phospholipid content under
anaerobic conditions. Both plasmenylethanolamine and plasmenylglycerol were
detected, and the major acyl aldehyde derived from sn-1 vinyl ether was C16:1.
Plasmalogen synthesis is affected by mutations in ATP-binding sites and Cys is
expected to be involved in the formation of the [4Fe-4S] cluster.
