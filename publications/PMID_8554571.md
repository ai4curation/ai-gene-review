# A novel mutation substituting tryptophan with arginine in the carboxyl-terminal, non-collagenous domain of collagen X in a case of Schmid metaphyseal chondrodysplasia.
**Authors:** Pokharel RK, Alimsardjono H, Uno K, Fujii S, Shiba R, Matsuo M
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.1995.2890](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.1995.2890)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1995 Dec 26;217(3):1157-62. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.1995.2890.

A novel mutation substituting tryptophan with arginine in the carboxyl-terminal,
non-collagenous domain of collagen X in a case of Schmid metaphyseal
chondrodysplasia.

Pokharel RK(1), Alimsardjono H, Uno K, Fujii S, Shiba R, Matsuo M.

Author information:
(1)Division of Genetics, International Center for Medical Research, Kobe, Japan.

A novel nucleotide change in the collagen X gene was identified in a Japanese
family with Schmid metaphyseal chondrodysplasia (SMCD). The T to C change at
nucleotide 1951 resulted in replacement of tryptophan by arginine at residue 651
(W651R). This missense mutation is considered to be responsible for SMCD because
1, the same mutation was not be identified in the collagen X gene from normal
individuals; 2, the mutation segregated with the SMCD phenotype in the index
family; 3, the substituted amino acid is highly conserved in type X collagens,
and 4, the mutation causes a marked change in the hydrophobicity profile of the
surrounding region in the NC1 domain. This novel mutation (W651) seems to have
the same impact on bone development as W651X mutation.
