Comment in
    Nat Cell Biol. 2001 Dec;3(12):E270-2. doi: 10.1038/ncb1201-e270.

We have generated an epidermis-specific desmoplakin (DP) mouse knockout, and
show that epidermal integrity requires DP; mechanical stresses to DP-null skin
cause intercellular separations. The number of epidermal desmosomes in DP-null
skin is similar to wild type (WT), but they lack keratin filaments, which
compromise their function. DP-null keratinocytes have few desmosomes in vitro,
and are unable to undergo actin reorganization and membrane sealing during
epithelial sheet formation. Adherens junctions are also reduced. In vitro, DP
transgene expression rescues these defects. DP is therefore required for
assembly of functional desmosomes, maintaining cytoskeletal architecture and
reinforcing membrane attachments essential for stable intercellular adhesion.
