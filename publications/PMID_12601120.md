# Juvenile-onset glycogen storage disease type II with novel mutations in acid alpha-glucosidase gene.

## Abstract

1. Neurology. 2003 Feb 25;60(4):715-7. doi: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000048661.95327.bf.

Juvenile-onset glycogen storage disease type II with novel mutations in acid
alpha-glucosidase gene.

Lam CW(1), Yuen YP, Chan KY, Tong SF, Lai CK, Chow TC, Lee KC, Chan YW,
Martiniuk F.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemical Pathology, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Prince
of Wales Hospital, China. ching-wanlam@cuhk.edu.hk

The authors describe two novel mutations of the acid alpha-glucosidase gene,
P361L and R437C, which define the juvenile-onset glycogen storage disease type
II (GSDII) in a 16-year-old Chinese patient. The asymptomatic 13-year-old
brother of the proband is also a compound heterozygote of the two mutations.
These results confirm that intrafamilial phenotypic variation of juvenile-onset
GSDII is ethnically diverse and suggest the contribution of other genes to the
phenotypic variability of GSDII.
