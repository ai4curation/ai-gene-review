# Purification and preliminary characterization of phosphoglycerate mutase from Schizosaccharomyces pombe.
**Authors:** Price NC, Duncan D, Ogg DJ
**Journal:** Int J Biochem (1985)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0020-711x(85)90275-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-711x(85)90275-7)

## Abstract

1. Int J Biochem. 1985;17(7):843-6. doi: 10.1016/0020-711x(85)90275-7.

Purification and preliminary characterization of phosphoglycerate mutase from
Schizosaccharomyces pombe.

Price NC, Duncan D, Ogg DJ.

Phosphoglycerate mutase could be purified to over 95% homogeneity by a single
step procedure involving elution from Cibacron Blue-Sepharose by a pulse of
cofactor 2,3-bisphosphoglycerate. Although the enzyme has been isolated in only
small quantities (c. 100 micrograms), gel filtration and sodium dodecylsulphate
polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis indicated that it is monomeric with Mr
approximately 23,000, an extremely low value for this enzyme. Preliminary
investigations of the kinetic characteristics and the nature of important amino
acid side chains have been undertaken.
