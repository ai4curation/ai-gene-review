# Structure of the DNA binding domain of E. coli SSB bound to ssDNA.
**Authors:** Raghunathan S, Kozlov AG, Lohman TM, Waksman G
**Journal:** Nat Struct Biol (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1038/77943](https://doi.org/10.1038/77943)

## Abstract

1. Nat Struct Biol. 2000 Aug;7(8):648-52. doi: 10.1038/77943.

Structure of the DNA binding domain of E. coli SSB bound to ssDNA.

Raghunathan S(1), Kozlov AG, Lohman TM, Waksman G.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biophysics, Washington University
School of Medicine, Campus Box 8231, 660 S. Euclid Ave., Saint Louis, Missouri
63110, USA.

The structure of the homotetrameric DNA binding domain of the single stranded
DNA binding protein from Escherichia coli (Eco SSB) bound to two 35-mer single
stranded DNAs was determined to a resolution of 2.8 A. This structure describes
the vast network of interactions that results in the extensive wrapping of
single stranded DNA around the SSB tetramer and suggests a structural basis for
its various binding modes.
