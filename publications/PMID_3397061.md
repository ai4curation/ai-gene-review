# Molecular cloning of the mouse angiotensinogen gene.
**Authors:** Clouston WM, Evans BA, Haralambidis J, Richards RI
**Journal:** Genomics (1988)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0888-7543(88)90008-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0888-7543(88)90008-0)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 1988 Apr;2(3):240-8. doi: 10.1016/0888-7543(88)90008-0.

Molecular cloning of the mouse angiotensinogen gene.

Clouston WM(1), Evans BA, Haralambidis J, Richards RI.

Author information:
(1)Howard Florey Institute of Experimental Physiology and Medicine, University
of Melbourne, Parkville, Victoria, Australia.

We describe here the cloning, restriction mapping, and sequencing of the mouse
angiotensinogen gene. The 5' flanking region contains consensus sequences for
several hormone-responsive elements and viral-like enhancers within 750 bp of
the cap site. The deduced amino acid sequence shows 87% identity with rat
angiotensinogen, but there is a discrepancy in the number of cysteine residues
in the mature protein among rat (n = 3), human (n = 4), and mouse (n = 4).
Because angiotensinogen is homologous to other members of the serine protease
inhibitor family, we aligned the putative reactive center of angiotensinogens
from various species. This alignment shows that the inhibitor site in human
angiotensinogen is different from its rodent counterpart, but the role of this
sequence divergence in the pathogenesis of human disease remains to be
established.
