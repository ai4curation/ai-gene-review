The nuclear receptor Nurr1 can be activated by RXR via heterodimerization
(RXR-Nurr1) and is a promising target for treating neurodegenerative diseases.
We herein report the enantioselective synthesis and SAR of sterically
constricted benzofurans at RXR. The established SAR, using whole cell functional
assays, lead to the full agonist 9a at RXR (pEC50 of 8.2) and RXR-Nurr1. The
X-ray structure shows enantiomeric discrimination where 9a optimally addresses
the ligand binding pocket of RXR.
