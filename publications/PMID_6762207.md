# The determination by radiochemical assay of argininosuccinase produced in an Escherichia coli system in vitro.

## Abstract

1. Biochem J. 1982 Dec 1;207(3):529-33. doi: 10.1042/bj2070529.

The determination by radiochemical assay of argininosuccinase produced in an
Escherichia coli system in vitro.

Zidwick MJ, Rogers P.

A highly sensitive radiochemical assay used to measure the synthesis and
regulation of the product of the argH gene, argininosuccinase, in an Escherichia
coli system in vitro is described. With L-[guanidino-14C]argininosuccinic acid
as a substrate, and in the presence of excess arginase and urease, 14CO2 is
collected in a simply designed micro-vessel. With this method less than 1 nmol
of product can be measured in the presence of various concentrations of
L-arginine.
