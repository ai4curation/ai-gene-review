# tyk2, prototype of a novel class of non-receptor tyrosine kinase genes.
**Authors:** Firmbach-Kraft I, Byers M, Shows T, Dalla-Favera R, Krolewski JJ
**Journal:** Oncogene (1990)

## Abstract

1. Oncogene. 1990 Sep;5(9):1329-36.

tyk2, prototype of a novel class of non-receptor tyrosine kinase genes.

Firmbach-Kraft I(1), Byers M, Shows T, Dalla-Favera R, Krolewski JJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology, Columbia University, College of Physicians and
Surgeons, New York, New York 10032.

We previously identified a novel protein tyrosine kinase gene, tyk2, by
screening a human lymphoid cDNA library with a tyrosine kinase domain specific
c-fms restriction fragment under low stringency hybridization conditions. We
have now isolated and sequenced a full length tyk2 cDNA clone; demonstrated that
this gene is widely expressed in hematopoietic and non-hematopoietic cell lines;
and mapped it to chromosome 19p13.2. The cDNA clone is 4176 nucleotides long and
codes for a putative protein with a molecular weight of 134 kilodaltons.
Hydrophobicity analysis of our sequence does not identify a transmembrane
domain, which is found in all members of the receptor class of protein tyrosine
kinases; nor can we detect an SH2 domain, found in all previously identified
non-receptor protein kinases. We therefore propose that tyk2 is the prototype of
a new class of non-receptor protein tyrosine kinases.
