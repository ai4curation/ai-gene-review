# Cloning of cDNAs encoding isopropylmalate dehydrogenase from Arabidopsis thaliana and accumulation patterns of their transcripts.
**Authors:** Nozawa A, Takano J, Miwa K, Nakagawa Y, Fujiwara T
**Journal:** Biosci Biotechnol Biochem (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1271/bbb.69.806](https://doi.org/10.1271/bbb.69.806)

## Abstract

1. Biosci Biotechnol Biochem. 2005 Apr;69(4):806-10. doi: 10.1271/bbb.69.806.

Cloning of cDNAs encoding isopropylmalate dehydrogenase from Arabidopsis
thaliana and accumulation patterns of their transcripts.

Nozawa A(1), Takano J, Miwa K, Nakagawa Y, Fujiwara T.

Author information:
(1)Precursory Research for Embryonic Science and Technology, Saitama 332-0012,
Japan.

Isopropylmalate dehydrogenase (IPMDH) is an enzyme in the leucine biosynthetic
pathway. We isolated three IPMDH ORF sequences from Arabidopsis thaliana, and
genes corresponding to these ORF sequences were designated AtIMD1, AtIMD2, and
AtIMD3. Deduced amino acid sequences of the three genes contain a putative
transit-peptide for plastidic localization. AtIMD1, AtIMD2, and AtIMD3 were able
to complement a leu2 mutant of yeast, suggesting that these genes encode
functional IPMDH. RT-PCR analysis revealed different tissue specificity of
transcript accumulation for the three genes.
