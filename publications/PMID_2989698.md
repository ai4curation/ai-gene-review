# Cloning, sequence and expression of two distinct human interleukin-1 complementary DNAs.

## Abstract

1. Nature. 1985 Jun 20-26;315(6021):641-7. doi: 10.1038/315641a0.

Cloning, sequence and expression of two distinct human interleukin-1
complementary DNAs.

March CJ, Mosley B, Larsen A, Cerretti DP, Braedt G, Price V, Gillis S, Henney
CS, Kronheim SR, Grabstein K, et al.

Two distinct but distantly related complementary DNAs encoding proteins sharing
human interleukin-1 (IL-1) activity (termed IL-1 alpha and IL-1 beta), were
isolated from a macrophage cDNA library. The primary translation products of the
genes are 271 and 269 amino acids long, although expression in Escherichia coli
of the carboxy-terminal 159 and 153 amino acids produces IL-1 biological
activity.
