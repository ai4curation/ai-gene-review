# SNAT2 silencing prevents the osmotic induction of transport system A and hinders cell recovery from hypertonic stress.
**Authors:** Bevilacqua E, Bussolati O, Dall'Asta V, Gaccioli F, Sala R, Gazzola GC, Franchi-Gazzola R
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2005.05.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2005.05.002)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2005 Jun 20;579(16):3376-80. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2005.05.002.

SNAT2 silencing prevents the osmotic induction of transport system A and hinders
cell recovery from hypertonic stress.

Bevilacqua E(1), Bussolati O, Dall'Asta V, Gaccioli F, Sala R, Gazzola GC,
Franchi-Gazzola R.

Author information:
(1)Sezione di Patologia Generale e Clinica, Dipartimento di Medicina,
Sperimentale, Università degli Studi di Parma, Via Volturno 39, 43100 Parma,
Italy.

Under hypertonic conditions the induction of SLC38A2/SNAT2 leads to the
stimulation of transport system A and to the increase in the cell content of
amino acids. In hypertonically stressed human fibroblasts transfection with two
siRNAs for SNAT2 suppressed the increase in SNAT2 mRNA and the stimulation of
system A transport activity. Under the same condition, the expansion of the
intracellular amino acid pool was significantly lowered and cell volume recovery
markedly delayed. It is concluded that the up-regulation of SNAT2 is essential
for the rapid restoration of cell volume after hypertonic stress.
