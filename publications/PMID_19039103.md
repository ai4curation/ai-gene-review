# AMPylation of Rho GTPases by Vibrio VopS disrupts effector binding and downstream signaling.
**Authors:** Yarbrough ML, Li Y, Kinch LN, Grishin NV, Ball HL, Orth K
**Journal:** Science (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.1166382](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1166382)

## Abstract

1. Science. 2009 Jan 9;323(5911):269-72. doi: 10.1126/science.1166382. Epub 2008
Nov 27.

AMPylation of Rho GTPases by Vibrio VopS disrupts effector binding and
downstream signaling.

Yarbrough ML(1), Li Y, Kinch LN, Grishin NV, Ball HL, Orth K.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Biology, University of Texas (UT) Southwestern
Medical Center, Dallas, TX 75390, USA.

The Vibrio parahaemolyticus type III effector VopS is implicated in cell
rounding and the collapse of the actin cytoskeleton by inhibiting Rho guanosine
triphosphatases (GTPases). We found that VopS could act to covalently modify a
conserved threonine residue on Rho, Rac, and Cdc42 with adenosine
5'-monophosphate (AMP). The resulting AMPylation prevented the interaction of
Rho GTPases with downstream effectors, thereby inhibiting actin assembly in the
infected cell. Eukaryotic proteins were also directly modified with AMP,
potentially expanding the repertoire of posttranslational modifications for
molecular signaling.
