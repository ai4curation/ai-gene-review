Biochemical and immunological studies on larval serum protein 1, the major
haemolymph protein of Drosophila melanogaster third-instar larvae.

Wolfe J, Akam ME, Roberts DB.

Larval serum protein 1 is the major haemolymph protein just before puparium
formation in Drosophila melanogaster. It has been purified and characterised as
a family of hexamers (molecular weight 450000-480000) of three immunologically
related polypeptides (molecular weight 75000-81000). Homologous proteins,
present in other dipterans, are similar in structure, amino acid composition and
developmental profile.
