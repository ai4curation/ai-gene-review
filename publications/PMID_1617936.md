## Abstract

1. Comp Biochem Physiol B. 1992 Jun;102(2):255-60. doi:

Wide distribution of the skin type I collagen alpha 3 chain in bony fish.

Kimura S(1).

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Food Biochemistry, Tokyo University of Fisheries, Japan.

1. Skin Type I collagens were isolated from the five species of non-teleostean
fish and examined with respect to their subunit chains. 2. In addition to alpha
1(I) and alpha 2(I) chains, a fish-specific chain, alpha 3(I), was previously
found only in teleostean fish. The present study, however, has revealed the
occurrence of alpha 3(I) in a chondrostean fish, white sturgeon. 3. The alpha
3(I) gene appears to have arisen from a duplication of the alpha 1(I) gene near
the time of the adaptive radiation of bony fish.
