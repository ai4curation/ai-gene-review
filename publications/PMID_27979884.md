A hitherto unidentified N-ethyl-N-nitrosourea (ENU)-induced mutation affects 
dorsal root ganglia (DRG) formation in ouchless mutant zebrafish larvae. In 
contrast to previous findings assigning the ouchless phenotypes to downregulated 
sorbs3 transcript levels, this work re-attributes the phenotypes to an essential 
splice site mutation affecting adgra2 (gpr124) splicing and function. 
Accordingly, ouchless mutants fail to complement previously characterized adgra2 
mutants and exhibit highly penetrant cerebrovascular defects. The aberrantly 
spliced adgra2 transcript found in ouchless mutants encodes a receptor lacking a 
single leucine-rich repeat (LRR) within its N-terminus.
