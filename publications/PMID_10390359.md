Most isolates of hepatitis C virus (HCV) infections are resistant to interferon,
the only available therapy, but the mechanism underlying this resistance has not
been defined. Here it is shown that the HCV envelope protein E2 contains a
sequence identical with phosphorylation sites of the interferon-inducible
protein kinase PKR and the translation initiation factor eIF2alpha, a target of
PKR. E2 inhibited the kinase activity of PKR and blocked its inhibitory effect
on protein synthesis and cell growth. This interaction of E2 and PKR may be one
mechanism by which HCV circumvents the antiviral effect of interferon.
