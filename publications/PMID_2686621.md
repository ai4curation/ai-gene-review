# Cloning and expression of human salivary-gland kallikrein in Escherichia coli.
**Authors:** Angermann A, Bergmann C, Appelhans H
**Journal:** Biochem J (1989)
**DOI:** [10.1042/bj2620787](https://doi.org/10.1042/bj2620787)
**PMC:** [PMC1133342](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1133342/)

## Abstract

1. Biochem J. 1989 Sep 15;262(3):787-93. doi: 10.1042/bj2620787.

Cloning and expression of human salivary-gland kallikrein in Escherichia coli.

Angermann A(1), Bergmann C, Appelhans H.

Author information:
(1)Institit für Biochemie, Technische Hochschule Darmstadt, Federal Republic of
Germany.

CDNA clones for human kallikrein have been identified in a cDNA library
constructed from mRNA of human salivary gland. The entire coding sequence for
preprokallikrein and for the 5'- and 3'-untranslated regions were isolated by
using a mixture of oligonucleotides corresponding to amino acids 51-56 of human
urinary kallikrein and one oligonucleotide corresponding to amino acids 233-238
of human pancreatic kallikrein. The DNA sequence proved that, with the exception
of two amino acid exchanges, kallikrein of the human salivary gland is identical
with pancreatic kallikrein. Salivary gland and renal kallikrein was expressed in
Escherichia coli from plasmid pKK223-3 under the control of the tac promoter.
The protein was identified by Western-blot analysis and by demonstration of its
specific proteolytic activity.
