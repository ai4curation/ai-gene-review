# Branchio-oto-renal syndrome associated mutations in Eyes Absent 1 result in loss of phosphatase activity.
**Authors:** Rayapureddi JP, Hegde RS

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2006 Jul 10;580(16):3853-9. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2006.06.009.
Epub  2006 Jun 15.

Branchio-oto-renal syndrome associated mutations in Eyes Absent 1 result in loss

Rayapureddi JP(1), Hegde RS.

Author information:
(1)Cincinnati Children's Hospital Medical Center, Division of Developmental
Biology, University of Cincinnati School of Medicine, Department of Pediatrics,
3333 Burnet Avenue, Cincinnati, OH 45229, United States.

The Eyes Absent (Eya) proteins are tyrosine phosphatases and transcriptional
activators involved in cell-fate determination and organ development. Mutations
in the gene encoding Eya homologue 1 have been implicated in the multi-organ
developmental disorder branchio-oto-renal syndrome (BOR) and in ocular defects.
Here we report that BOR-associated mutations lead to a loss of phosphatase
activity in Eya1 proteins, while mutations associated with ocular defects yield
Eya1 proteins with near normal levels of phosphatase activity. Furthermore we
demonstrate that the N-terminal domain attenuates the catalytic activity of Eya
suggesting a mechanism of regulation.
