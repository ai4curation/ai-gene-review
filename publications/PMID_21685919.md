## Abstract

1. Nat Neurosci. 2011 Jun 19;14(7):821-3. doi: 10.1038/nn.2833.

Glia instruct developmental neuronal remodeling through TGF-β signaling.

Awasaki T(1), Huang Y, O'Connor MB, Lee T.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurobiology, University of Massachusetts, Worcester,
Massachusetts, USA. awasakit@janelia.hhmi.org

We found that glia secrete myoglianin, a TGF-β ligand, to instruct developmental
neural remodeling in Drosophila. Glial myoglianin upregulated neuronal
expression of an ecdysone nuclear receptor that triggered neurite remodeling
following the late-larval ecdysone peak. Thus glia orchestrate developmental
neural remodeling not only by engulfment of unwanted neurites but also by
enabling neuron remodeling.
