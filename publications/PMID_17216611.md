# Complex interference in the eye developmental pathway by Drosophila NF-YA.
**Authors:** Yoshioka Y, Suyari O, Yamada M, Ohno K, Hayashi Y, Yamaguchi M
**Journal:** Genesis (2007)
**DOI:** [10.1002/dvg.20260](https://doi.org/10.1002/dvg.20260)

## Abstract

1. Genesis. 2007 Jan;45(1):21-31. doi: 10.1002/dvg.20260.

Complex interference in the eye developmental pathway by Drosophila NF-YA.

Yoshioka Y(1), Suyari O, Yamada M, Ohno K, Hayashi Y, Yamaguchi M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Applied Biology, Kyoto Institute of Technology, Matsugasaki,
Sakyo-ku, Kyoto, Japan.

The CCAAT motif-binding factor NF-Y consists of three different subunits, NF-YA,
NF-YB, and NF-YC, all of which are required for formation of the NF-Y complex
and DNA-binding. NF-YA contains a DNA binding domain in its C-terminal region.
We established transgenic fly lines carrying the UAS-HA-dNF-YA or UAS-dNF-YAIR
and showed over-expression or knockdown with various GAL4 drivers to be lethal
at various developmental stages, suggesting that dNF-YA participate in various
gene regulatory pathways during Drosophila development. Expression of dNF-YA
with eyeless-GAL4 mainly resulted in lethality with a headless phenotype in
pharate-adults. Reduction of the eyeless gene dose enhanced the dNF-YA-induced
phenotype, while reduction of the Distal-less gene dose suppressed the
phenotype. On the other hand, crossing the dNF-YA over-expressing flies with
Notch mutant resulted in no apparent effect on the phenotype. These results
suggest that dNF-YA can disturb eye disc specification, but not eye disc growth.
