# Repression of enzymes of the pentose phosphate pathway by glucose in fission yeast.
**Authors:** Mehta S, Velmurugan S, Lobo Z
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(98)01420-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(98)01420-3)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1998 Dec 4;440(3):430-3. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(98)01420-3.

Repression of enzymes of the pentose phosphate pathway by glucose in fission
yeast.

Mehta S(1), Velmurugan S, Lobo Z.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biological Sciences, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research,
Mumbai, India.

We examine here the effect of carbon sources on the synthesis of the shunt
pathway enzymes in the fission yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe growing on a
mixture of ethanol and glycerol. Delta-gluconolactone induces practically every
one of these enzymes. Glucose in contrast tends to attenuate the synthesis of
the majority of them. RNA analysis confirms that their induction and repression
reflect changes in the levels of their transcripts.
