# Structural basis of Cas3 inhibition by the bacteriophage protein AcrF3.
**Authors:** Wang X, Yao D, Xu JG, Li AR, Xu J, Fu P, Zhou Y, Zhu Y

## Abstract

1. Nat Struct Mol Biol. 2016 Sep;23(9):868-70. doi: 10.1038/nsmb.3269. Epub 2016
Jul 25.

Structural basis of Cas3 inhibition by the bacteriophage protein AcrF3.

Wang X(1), Yao D(2), Xu JG(1), Li AR(1), Xu J(1), Fu P(1), Zhou Y(1), Zhu Y(1).

Author information:

Bacteriophages express proteins that inactivate the CRISPR-Cas bacterial immune
system. Here we report the crystal structure of the anti-CRISPR protein AcrF3 in
complex with Pseudomonas aeruginosa Cas3 (PaCas3). AcrF3 forms a homodimer that
locks PaCas3 in an ADP-bound form, blocks the entrance of the DNA-binding tunnel
in the helicase domain, and masks the linker region and C-terminal domain of
PaCas3, thereby preventing recruitment by Cascade and inhibiting the type I-F
CRISPR-Cas system.
