# Id1 regulates angiogenesis through transcriptional repression of thrombospondin-1.
**Authors:** Volpert OV, Pili R, Sikder HA, Nelius T, Zaichuk T, Morris C, Shiflett CB, Devlin MK, Conant K, Alani RM
**Journal:** Cancer Cell (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1535-6108(02)00209-x](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1535-6108(02)00209-x)

## Abstract

1. Cancer Cell. 2002 Dec;2(6):473-83. doi: 10.1016/s1535-6108(02)00209-x.

Id1 regulates angiogenesis through transcriptional repression of
thrombospondin-1.

Volpert OV(1), Pili R, Sikder HA, Nelius T, Zaichuk T, Morris C, Shiflett CB,
Devlin MK, Conant K, Alani RM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Urology and RH Lurie Cancer Center, Northwestern University
Medical School, Chicago, IL 60611, USA.

Id proteins are helix-loop-helix transcription factors that regulate tumor
angiogenesis. In order to identify downstream effectors of Id1 involved in the
regulation of angiogenesis, we performed PCR-select subtractive hybridization on
wild-type and Id1 knockout mouse embryo fibroblasts (MEFs). Here we demonstrate
that thrombospondin-1 (TSP-1), a potent inhibitor of angiogenesis, is a target
of transcriptional repression by Id1. We also show that Id1-null MEFs secrete an
inhibitor of endothelial cell migration, which is completely inactivated by
depletion of TSP-1. Furthermore, in vivo studies revealed decreased
neovascularization in matrigel assays in Id1-null mice compared to their
wild-type littermates. This decrease was completely reversed by a TSP-1
neutralizing antibody. We conclude that TSP-1 is a major target for Id1 effects
on angiogenesis.
