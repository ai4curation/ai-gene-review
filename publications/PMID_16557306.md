Suicide inhibition of alpha-oxamine synthases: structures of the covalent

Alexeev D(1), Baxter RL, Campopiano DJ, Kerbarh O, Sawyer L, Tomczyk N, Watt R,
Webster SP.

Author information:
(1)School of Biology, Swann Building, University of Edinburgh, Mayfield Road,

The irreversible inhibition of 8-amino-7-oxononanoate synthase by
trifluoroalanine involves decarboxylative defluorination of the inhibitor-PLP
aldimine followed by attack of the conjugated imine by the amino group of the
active site lysine to afford a covalently bound difluorinated intermediate which
can subsequently undergo further HF losses and hydrolysis to afford a
2-(pyridoximine phosphate) acetoyl protein adduct.
