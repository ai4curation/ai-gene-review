# MicroRNA-146a represses LRP2 translation and leads to cell apoptosis in Alzheimer's disease.

MicroRNA regulation of transcript expression has been reported in patients with
Alzheimer's disease (AD). Here, we investigate the role of microRNA-146a
(miRNA-146a), a brain-enriched miRNA, which is upregulated in AD patients.
Through analysis of predicted targets of miRNA-146a, low-density lipoprotein
receptor-related protein-2 (Lrp2), a member of the LDLR family that is known to
play a protective role in AD, was identified. Overexpression of miRNA-146a in
SH-SY5Y cells significantly decreased Lrp2 expression, resulting in a reduction
of Akt activation and induction of proapoptotic caspase-3, thereby increasing
cell apoptosis. Thus, specific miRNA-146a regulation may contribute to AD by
downregulating the Lrp2/Akt pathway.
