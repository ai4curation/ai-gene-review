## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1977 Mar;129(3):1375-8. doi: 10.1128/jb.129.3.1375-1378.1977.

Sterol 24(28) methylene reductase in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Neal WD, Parks LW.

Optimal conditions for the 24(28)methylene reductase were obtained. The enzyme
assay provided for unusually high activity; the Km was determined to be 10.8
mum. The enzyme activity was increased in cells grown with ethanol as the
substrate.
