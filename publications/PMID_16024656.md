# The human PAF complex coordinates transcription with events downstream of RNA synthesis.
**Authors:** Zhu B, Mandal SS, Pham AD, Zheng Y, Erdjument-Bromage H, Batra SK, Tempst P, Reinberg D

## Abstract

1. Genes Dev. 2005 Jul 15;19(14):1668-73. doi: 10.1101/gad.1292105.

The human PAF complex coordinates transcription with events downstream of RNA
synthesis.

Zhu B(1), Mandal SS, Pham AD, Zheng Y, Erdjument-Bromage H, Batra SK, Tempst P,
Reinberg D.

Author information:
(1)Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Division of Nucleic Acids Enzymology,
Department of Biochemistry, University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey,
Robert Wood Johnson Medical School, Piscataway, New Jersey 08854, USA.

The yeast PAF (yPAF) complex interacts with RNA polymerase II and coordinates
the setting of histone marks associated with active transcription. We report the
isolation and functional characterization of the human PAF (hPAF) complex. hPAF
shares four subunits with yPAF (hCtr9, hPaf1, hLeo1, and hCdc73), but contains a
novel higher eukaryotic-specific subunit, hSki8. RNAi against hSki8 or hCtr9
reduces the cellular levels of other hPAF subunits and of mono- and
trimethylated H3-Lys 4 and dimethylated H3-Lys 79. The hSki8 subunit is also a
component of the human SKI (hSKI) complex. Yeast SKI complex is cytoplasmic and
together with Exosome mediates 3'-5' mRNA degradation. However, hSKI complex
localizes to both nucleus and cytoplasm. Immunoprecipitation experiments
revealed that hPAF and hSKI complexes interact, and ChIP experiments
demonstrated that hSKI associates with transcriptionally active genes dependent
on the presence of hPAF. Thus, in addition to coordinating events during
transcription (initiation, promoter clearance, and elongation), hPAF also
coordinates events in RNA quality control.
