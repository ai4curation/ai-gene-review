Inner Membrane Protein YhcB Interacts with RodZ Involved in Cell Shape

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, Faculty of Science, Kobe University, Rokko, Kobe,

Depletion of YhcB, an inner membrane protein of Escherichia coli, inhibited the
growth of rodZ deletion mutant showing that the loss of both YhcB and RodZ is
synthetically lethal. Furthermore, YhcB was demonstrated to interact with RodZ
as well as several other proteins involved in cell shape maintenance and an
inner membrane protein YciS of unknown function, using bacterial two-hybrid
system. These observations seem to indicate that YhcB is involved in the
biogenesis of cell envelope and the maintenance of cell shape together with
RodZ.
