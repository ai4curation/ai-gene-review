# Delta signaling mediates segregation of neural crest and spinal sensory neurons from zebrafish lateral neural plate.
**Authors:** Cornell RA, Eisen JS
**Journal:** Development (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1242/dev.127.13.2873](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.127.13.2873)

## Abstract

1. Development. 2000 Jul;127(13):2873-82. doi: 10.1242/dev.127.13.2873.

Delta signaling mediates segregation of neural crest and spinal sensory neurons
from zebrafish lateral neural plate.

Cornell RA(1), Eisen JS.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Neuroscience, Eugene, Oregon 97403, USA. eisen@uoneuro.
uoregon.edu

We examined the role of Delta signaling in specification of two derivatives in
zebrafish neural plate: Rohon-Beard spinal sensory neurons and neural crest.
deltaA-expressing Rohon-Beard neurons are intermingled with premigratory neural
crest cells in the trunk lateral neural plate. Embryos homozygous for a point
mutation in deltaA, or with experimentally reduced delta signalling, have
supernumerary Rohon-Beard neurons, reduced trunk-level expression of neural
crest markers and lack trunk neural crest derivatives. Fin mesenchyme, a
putative trunk neural crest derivative, is present in deltaA mutants, suggesting
it segregates from other neural crest derivatives as early as the neural plate
stage. Cranial neural crest derivatives are also present in deltaA mutants,
revealing a genetic difference in regulation of trunk and cranial neural crest
development.
