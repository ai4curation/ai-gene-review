# KLF5 Interacts with p53 in regulating survivin expression in acute lymphoblastic leukemia.
**Authors:** Zhu N, Gu L, Findley HW, Chen C, Dong JT, Yang L, Zhou M
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M513810200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M513810200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2006 May 26;281(21):14711-8. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M513810200. Epub
2006  Apr 4.

KLF5 Interacts with p53 in regulating survivin expression in acute lymphoblastic
leukemia.

Zhu N(1), Gu L, Findley HW, Chen C, Dong JT, Yang L, Zhou M.

Author information:
(1)The Division of Pediatric Hematology/Oncology, Emory University School of

The Kruppel-like factor 5 (KLF5) is a transcription factor that regulates
cellular signaling involved in cell proliferation and oncogenesis. Here, we
report that KLF5 interacts with tumor suppressor p53 in regulating the
expression of the inhibitor-of-apoptosis protein survivin, which may play a role
in pathological process of cancer. The core promoter region of survivin contains
multiple GT-boxes that have been characterized as KLF5 response elements.
Deletion and mutation analyses as well as chromatin immunoprecipitation and
electronic mobility shift assay indicated that KLF5 binds to the core survivin
promoter and strongly induces its activity. Furthermore, we demonstrated that
KLF5 protein is able to bind to p53 and abrogate the p53-regulated repression of
survivin. Transfection of KLF5 into a KLF5-negative acute lymphoblastic leukemia
cell line EU-8 enhanced survivin expression, and conversely, silencing of KLF5
by small interfering RNA in a KLF5-overexpressing acute lymphoblastic leukemia
cell line EU-4 down-regulated survivin expression. The KLF5 small interfering
RNA-mediated down-regulation of survivin sensitized EU-4 cells to apoptosis
induced by chemotherapeutic drug doxorubicin. These findings identify a novel
regulatory pathway for the expression of survivin under the control of KLF5 and
p53. Deregulation of this pathway may result in overexpression of survivin in
cancer, thus contributing to drug resistance.
