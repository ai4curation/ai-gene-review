# Local caspase activity directs engulfment of dendrites during pruning.
**Authors:** Williams DW, Kondo S, Krzyzanowska A, Hiromi Y, Truman JW
**Journal:** Nat Neurosci (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1038/nn1774](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn1774)

## Abstract

1. Nat Neurosci. 2006 Oct;9(10):1234-6. doi: 10.1038/nn1774. Epub 2006 Sep 17.

Local caspase activity directs engulfment of dendrites during pruning.

Williams DW(1), Kondo S, Krzyzanowska A, Hiromi Y, Truman JW.

Author information:
(1)MRC Centre for Developmental Neurobiology, King's College London, London SE1
1UL, UK. darren.williams@kcl.ac.uk

Pruning is important for sculpting neural circuits, as it removes excessive or
inaccurate projections. Here we show that the removal of sensory neuron
dendrites during pruning in Drosophila melanogaster is directed by local caspase
activity. Suppressing caspase activity prevented dendrite removal, whereas a
global activation of caspases within a neuron caused cell death. A new
genetically encoded caspase probe revealed that caspase activity is confined to
the degenerating dendrites of pruning neurons.
