# c-Src is a PDZ interaction partner and substrate of the E3 ubiquitin ligase Ligand-of-Numb protein X1.
**Authors:** Weiss A, Baumgartner M, Radziwill G, Dennler J, Moelling K
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2007)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2007.09.062](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2007.09.062)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2007 Oct 30;581(26):5131-6. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2007.09.062.
Epub  2007 Oct 8.

c-Src is a PDZ interaction partner and substrate of the E3 ubiquitin ligase
Ligand-of-Numb protein X1.

Weiss A(1), Baumgartner M, Radziwill G, Dennler J, Moelling K.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Medical Virology, University of Zurich, Gloriastrasse 30, 8006
Zurich, Switzerland. andreas.weiss@usz.ch

The very C-terminus of c-Src is a ligand for PDZ domains. In a screen for PDZ
domains that interact with c-Src, we identified one of the PDZ domains of the
Ligand-of-Numb protein X1 (LNX1), a multiple PDZ domain scaffold and RING type
E3 ubiquitin ligase. We demonstrate that the interaction of c-Src with LNX1
depends on the C-terminal PDZ ligand of c-Src. Furthermore, we show that c-Src
phosphorylates LNX1. Moreover, c-Src itself is ubiquitinated by LNX1, suggesting
an interdependent regulation of c-Src and LNX1.
