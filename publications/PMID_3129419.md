# Membrane-associated phospholipase C of Drosophila retina.
**Authors:** Inoue H, Yoshioka T, Hotta Y
**Journal:** J Biochem (1988)
**DOI:** [10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a122246](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a122246)

## Abstract

1. J Biochem. 1988 Jan;103(1):91-4. doi: 10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a122246.

Membrane-associated phospholipase C of Drosophila retina.

Inoue H(1), Yoshioka T, Hotta Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Basic Human Sciences, School of Human Sciences, Waseda
University, Saitama.

Phospholipase C activities against phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate and
phosphatidylinositol have been examined using head homogenate of Drosophila
visual mutants. In many mutants the enzyme activities were found to be reduced.
The activities against both phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate and
phosphatidylinositol were always affected in parallel among the mutants, while
the activities of other enzymes related to phosphatidylinositol metabolism, such
as diacylglycerol kinase, were not. The enzyme was concluded to be
membrane-associated and was activated maximally at low Ca2+ concentration
(10(-7) M), when phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate was used as a substrate,
while the activity obtained with phosphatidylinositol increased with the Ca2+
concentration up to 10(-4) M. The effects of pH on these two enzyme activities
differed to some extent.
