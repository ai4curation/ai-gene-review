Toll-like receptors (TLRs) take part in both the innate and adaptive immune
systems. The role of the transmembrane domain in TLR signaling is still elusive,
while its importance for the TLR activation was clearly demonstrated. In the
present study the ability of the TLR3 transmembrane domain to form dimers and
trimers in detergent micelles was shown by solution NMR spectroscopy. Spatial
structures and free energy magnitudes were determined for the TLR3 transmembrane
domain in dimeric and trimeric states, and two possible surfaces that may be
used for the helix-helix interaction by the full-length TLR3 were revealed.
