# Point mutations in the upstream region of the alpha-galactosidase A gene exon 6 in an atypical variant of Fabry disease.
**Authors:** Ishii S, Sakuraba H, Suzuki Y
**Journal:** Hum Genet (1992)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00207037](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00207037)

## Abstract

1. Hum Genet. 1992 Apr;89(1):29-32. doi: 10.1007/BF00207037.

Point mutations in the upstream region of the alpha-galactosidase A gene exon 6
in an atypical variant of Fabry disease.

Ishii S(1), Sakuraba H, Suzuki Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Clinical Genetics, Tokyo Metropolitan Institute of Medical
Sciences, Japan.

Single point mutations in the upstream region of exon 6 of the
alpha-galactosidase A gene were found in two Japanese cases of the cardiac form
of Fabry disease; 301Arg----Gln (902G----A) in a case that has already been
published and 279Gln----Glu (835C----G) in a new case. They both expressed
markedly low, but significant, amounts of residual activity in COS-1 cells. In
contrast, two unrelated cases with classic Fabry disease were found to have
different point mutations, which showed a complete loss of enzyme activity in a
transient expression assay; 328Gly----Arg (982G----A) in the downstream region
of exon 6 in one case and two combined mutations, 66Glu----Gln
(196G----C)/112Arg----Cys (334C----T), in exon 2 in the other. We conclude, on
the basis of the results recorded in this study and those in previous reports,
that the pathogenesis of atypical Fabry disease is closely associated with point
mutations in the upstream region of exon 6 of the alpha-galactosidase A gene.
