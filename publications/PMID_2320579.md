Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Merck Sharp & Dohme Research Laboratories,

Glioma-derived vascular endothelial cell growth factor (GD-VEGF) is a 46-kDa
dimeric glycoprotein mitogen with apparently greater specificity for vascular
endothelial cells than the well-characterized fibroblast growth factors. The
GD-VEGF cDNA sequence encodes a 190-amino acid residue subunit that is
converted, by removal of an amino-terminal hydrophobic secretory leader
sequence, to the mature 164-residue subunit characterized by direct amino acid
sequencing. The GD-VEGF homodimeric subunit is homologous to the
platelet-derived growth factor A and B chains and its oncogene homologue v-sis.
