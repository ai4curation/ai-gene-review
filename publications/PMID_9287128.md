# Role of the prohormone convertase PC2 in the processing of proglucagon to glucagon.
**Authors:** Rouillé Y, Bianchi M, Irminger JC, Halban PA
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(97)00892-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(97)00892-2)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1997 Aug 11;413(1):119-23. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(97)00892-2.

Role of the prohormone convertase PC2 in the processing of proglucagon to
glucagon.

Rouillé Y(1), Bianchi M, Irminger JC, Halban PA.

Author information:
(1)Laboratoires de Recherche Louis Jeantet, Centre Médical Universitaire,
Geneva, Switzerland. yrouille@infobiogen.fr

Proglucagon is alternatively processed to glucagon in pancreatic alpha-cells, or
to glucagon-like peptide-1 in intestinal L cells. Here, the specificity of PC2,
the major prohormone convertase of alpha-cells, was examined both in vivo and in
vitro. Adenovirus-mediated co-expression of proglucagon and PC2 in GH4C1 cells
resulted in a pattern of processing products very similar to that observed in
alpha-cells. Oxyntomodulin, an intermediate in the processing of proglucagon,
was quantitatively converted to glucagon in vitro by purified recombinant PC2,
in combination with carboxypeptidase E. It is concluded that PC2 is able to act
alone in the pancreatic pathway of proglucagon processing.
