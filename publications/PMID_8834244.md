# Novel trinucleotide deletion in Fabry's disease.
**Authors:** Cariolou MA, Christodoulides M, Manoli P, Kokkofitou A, Tsambaos D
**Journal:** Hum Genet (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF02267068](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02267068)

## Abstract

1. Hum Genet. 1996 Apr;97(4):468-70. doi: 10.1007/BF02267068.

Novel trinucleotide deletion in Fabry's disease.

Cariolou MA(1), Christodoulides M, Manoli P, Kokkofitou A, Tsambaos D.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Genetics, Cyprus Institute of Neurology and Genetics,
Nicosia.

We describe the molecular characterization of a novel, in-frame deletion that is
located in exon 7 of the alpha-galactosidase A gene in a patient with Fabry's
disease. The 3-bp deletion we identified, besides the typical severe clinical
features, also expresses diffuse facial telangiectasias, which is a new
cutaneous marker of Fabry's disease.
