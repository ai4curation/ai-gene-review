# H3K9 histone methyltransferase G9a-mediated transcriptional activation of p21.

We report that H3K9 HMTase G9a activates transcription of the cell cycle
regulatory gene, p21, in p53-null H1299 cells. Positive regulation of p21 by G9a
is independent of its HMTase activity. We demonstrate that G9a upregulates p21
via interaction with PCAF, and provide evidence that the activating complex is
recruited to the p21 promoter upon DNA damage-inducing agent etoposide
treatment. Our study suggests that G9a decreases proliferation and cell
viability by increasing the level of p21-mediated apoptosis. Our results suggest
that G9a functions as a coactivator for p21 transcription, and directs cells to
undergo apoptosis.
