# Expression of AIE-75 PDZ-domain protein induces G2/M cell cycle arrest in human colorectal adenocarcinoma SW480 cells.
**Authors:** Hirai A, Tada M, Furuuchi K, Ishikawa S, Makiyama K, Hamada J, Okada F, Kobayashi I, Fukuda H, Moriuchi T
**Journal:** Cancer Lett (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.canlet.2004.02.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.canlet.2004.02.005)

## Abstract

1. Cancer Lett. 2004 Aug 10;211(2):209-18. doi: 10.1016/j.canlet.2004.02.005.

Expression of AIE-75 PDZ-domain protein induces G2/M cell cycle arrest in human
colorectal adenocarcinoma SW480 cells.

Hirai A(1), Tada M, Furuuchi K, Ishikawa S, Makiyama K, Hamada J, Okada F,
Kobayashi I, Fukuda H, Moriuchi T.

Author information:
(1)Divisions of Cancer-Related Genes, Institute for Genetic Medicine, Hokkaido
University, N-15 W-7, Kita-ku, Sapporo 060-0815, Japan.

AIE-75 has been known as a 75-kDa autoantigen detected in the serum of
autoimmune enteropathy (AIE) and as a colon cancer-related antigen, and now
designated as a gene causative of Usher syndrome type 1C hereditary syndromic
hearing loss. It binds to a novel putative tumor suppressor MCC2 that is
homologous to MCC (mutated in colon cancer) through a PSD-95/Dlg/ZO-1 (PDZ)
domain. To clarify the functional role in colon cancer cells, we transfected
AIE-75 gene into SW480 colon cancer cells which do not express AIE-75.
Expression of AIE-75 suppressed growth of SW480 cells in vitro in correlation
with the expression levels. It was due mainly to G2/M phase cell cycle arrest
associated with mitotic slippage, resulting in emergence of hyperploid
giant-nucleated or multi-nucleated cells. Screening of proteins that bound to
PDZ domains of AIE-75 by a yeast two hybrid system showed that three
serine/threonine phosphatase catalytic subunits (PP2AC-alpha, PP2AC-beta, and
PPP6C) could bind to AIE-75. Since PP2AC is known to regulate G2/M checkpoint,
we suggest that AIE-75 interacts with PP2AC and prevent cells to transit mitotic
phase.
