# Characterization of soluble complexes of the Shigella flexneri type III secretion system ATPase.
**Authors:** Johnson S, Blocker A
**Journal:** FEMS Microbiol Lett (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1574-6968.2008.01284.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1574-6968.2008.01284.x)

## Abstract

1. FEMS Microbiol Lett. 2008 Sep;286(2):274-8. doi:
10.1111/j.1574-6968.2008.01284.x. Epub 2008 Jul 23.

Characterization of soluble complexes of the Shigella flexneri type III
secretion system ATPase.

Johnson S(1), Blocker A.

Author information:
(1)Sir William Dunn School of Pathology, University of Oxford, Oxford, UK.

The conserved ATPase of type III secretion systems is critical to the export of
substrates through the apparatus. We present a characterization of the native
T3SS ATPase, Spa47, from the cytoplasm of Shigella flexneri, demonstrating it to
be in two distinct high-molecular-weight complexes with Spa33: MxiN and MxiK.
