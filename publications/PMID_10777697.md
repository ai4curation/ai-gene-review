# Small heat shock protein 27 (HSP27) associates with tubulin/microtubules in HeLa cells.
**Authors:** Hino M, Kurogi K, Okubo MA, Murata-Hori M, Hosoya H
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.2000.2553](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.2000.2553)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2000 Apr 29;271(1):164-9. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.2000.2553.

Small heat shock protein 27 (HSP27) associates with tubulin/microtubules in HeLa
cells.

Hino M(1), Kurogi K, Okubo MA, Murata-Hori M, Hosoya H.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biological Science, Faculty of Science, Hiroshima University,
Higashi-Hiroshima, 739-8526, Japan.

One of the monoclonal antibodies raised against mitotic HeLa cells (termed as
mH3) recognized a 27-kDa protein and stained microtubules in the mitotic
spindles of HeLa cells. Immunoscreening of a HeLa cDNA library revealed that mH3
antigen is a small heat shock protein, HSP27. Immunoprecipitation analysis using
mH3 suggested that both alpha- and beta-tubulin are associated with HSP27.
Further, sucrose-cushioned ultra centrifugation revealed that HSP27 is
co-sedimented with taxol-stabilized microtubules. These results indicate that
HSP27 associates with tubulin/microtubules in HeLa cells.
