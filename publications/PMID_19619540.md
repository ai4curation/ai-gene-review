# Ankrd17, an ubiquitously expressed ankyrin factor, is essential for the vascular integrity during embryogenesis.
**Authors:** Hou SC, Chan LW, Chou YC, Su CY, Chen X, Shih YL, Tsai PC, Shen CK, Yan YT
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.025)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2009 Sep 3;583(17):2765-71. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.025.
Epub  2009 Jul 18.

Ankrd17, an ubiquitously expressed ankyrin factor, is essential for the vascular
integrity during embryogenesis.

Hou SC(1), Chan LW, Chou YC, Su CY, Chen X, Shih YL, Tsai PC, Shen CK, Yan YT.

Author information:
(1)Graduate Institute of Life Sciences, National Defense Medical Center, Taipei,
Taiwan, ROC.

Ankyrin repeat domain 17 (Ankrd17) encodes an ubiquitously expressed protein
with two clusters of ankyrin repeats. We have used gene targeting strategy to
ablate the Ankrd17 gene in mouse. The Ankrd17-deficient mice died between
embryonic day (E) 10.5 and E11.5 due to cardiovascular defects. Serious
hemorrhages were detected and the vascular smooth muscle cells (vSMCs)
surrounding the vessels were drastically reduced in the Ankrd17-deficient
embryos, suggesting that the vascular maturation was not completed.
Interestingly, vSMC differentiation marker genes were up-regulated in the mutant
embryos. Our data have demonstrated the indispensability of Ankrd17 functioning
for vascular maturation during early development. The Ankrd17-deficient mice
also provide a new animal model for the analysis of the regulatory pathways of
the differentiation of vSMC precursor cells.
