# Purification, characterization and partial amino acid sequences of carnitine palmitoyl-transferase from human liver.
**Authors:** Finocchiaro G, Colombo I, DiDonato S
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0014-5793(90)81354-q](https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(90)81354-q)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1990 Nov 12;274(1-2):163-6. doi: 10.1016/0014-5793(90)81354-q.

Purification, characterization and partial amino acid sequences of carnitine
palmitoyl-transferase from human liver.

Finocchiaro G(1), Colombo I, DiDonato S.

Author information:
(1)Istituto Nazionale Neurologico C, Besta, Dipartimento di Biochemica e
Genetica, Milano, Italy.

Carnitine palmitoyl-transferase has been extracted with 0.5% Tween-20 from human
liver homogenate and purified to homogeneity. The purified enzyme has a native
Mr of 274 kDa. The subunit Mr is of 66 kDa, as shown by SDS-PAGE and immunoblots
obtained with antibodies raised against human CPT. Purified CPT shows high
affinity for palmitoyl-CoA and palmitoyl-carnitine and is not inhibited by
malonyl-CoA. Seven tryptic peptides and the N-terminal of purified human CPT
have been sequenced, and found homologous to rat CPT sequence. Both antibodies
and peptide sequences are important tools for the investigation of the molecular
basis of CPT deficiency in man.
