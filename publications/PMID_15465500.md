# Drapc1 expression during mouse embryonic development.
**Authors:** Jukkola T, Sinjushina N, Partanen J
**Journal:** Gene Expr Patterns (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.modgep.2004.03.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.modgep.2004.03.006)

## Abstract

1. Gene Expr Patterns. 2004 Oct;4(6):755-62. doi: 10.1016/j.modgep.2004.03.006.

Drapc1 expression during mouse embryonic development.

Jukkola T(1), Sinjushina N, Partanen J.

Author information:
(1)Developmental Biology Programme, Institute of Biotechnology, Viikki
Biocenter, P.O. Box 56, Viikinkaari 9, University of Helsinki, Helsinki 00014,
Finland.

We identified the mouse homolog of human DRAPC1 (APCDD1) gene, shown to be a
target of Wnt/beta-catenin signaling pathway in cancer cell lines. Analysis of
its spatiotemporal expression in mouse embryos from E7.5 to E14 showed that
Drapc1 is expressed during development of the extraembryonic structures, nervous
system, vascular system and inner ear. In addition, Drapc1 is expressed in the
mesenchyme of several developing organs at sites of epithelio-mesenchymal
interactions. Drapc1 expression was also found in the hair follicles of the
adult mouse skin. Similarity of Drapc1 expression pattern to location of active
beta-catenin in developing mouse embryo further suggests that mouse Drapc1 is a
novel in vivo target gene of Wnt/beta-catenin signaling pathway.
