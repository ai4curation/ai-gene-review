# FYN-T-FYB-SLP-76 interactions define a T-cell receptor zeta/CD3-mediated tyrosine phosphorylation pathway that up-regulates interleukin 2 transcription in T-cells.
**Authors:** Raab M, Kang H, da Silva A, Zhu X, Rudd CE
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.274.30.21170](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.274.30.21170)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1999 Jul 23;274(30):21170-9. doi: 10.1074/jbc.274.30.21170.

FYN-T-FYB-SLP-76 interactions define a T-cell receptor zeta/CD3-mediated
tyrosine phosphorylation pathway that up-regulates interleukin 2 transcription
in T-cells.

Raab M(1), Kang H, da Silva A, Zhu X, Rudd CE.

Author information:
(1)Division of Tumor Immunology, Department of Cancer Immunology and AIDS, Dana
Farber Cancer Institute, Harvard Medical School, Boston, Massachusetts 02115,
USA.

Protein-tyrosine kinases p56(Lck), SYK, and ZAP-70 and downstream adaptors LAT
and SLP-76 have been implicated as essential components in T-cell activation.
Another lymphoid-specific adaptor FYB/SLAP has also been identified as a
predominant binding partner of SLP-76 and the Src kinase FYN-T, although its
role in the activation process has been unclear. In this study, we demonstrate
that FYN-T selectively phosphorylates FYB providing a template for the
recruitment of FYN-T and SLP-76 SH2 domain binding. This interaction is unusual
in its distinct cytoplasmic localization and its long term stable kinetics of
phosphorylation. Furthermore, we demonstrate for the first time that the
co-expression of all three components of the FYN-T-FYB-SLP-76 matrix can
synergistically up-regulate T-cell receptor-driven interleukin 2 transcription
activity. These findings document the existence of a T-cell receptor-regulated
FYN-T-FYB pathway that interfaces with the adaptor SLP-76 and up-regulates
lymphokine production in T-cells.
