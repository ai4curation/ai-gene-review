# cDNA sequence of human apolipoprotein(a) is homologous to plasminogen.
**Authors:** McLean JW, Tomlinson JE, Kuang WJ, Eaton DL, Chen EY, Fless GM, Scanu AM, Lawn RM
**Journal:** Nature (1987)
**DOI:** [10.1038/330132a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/330132a0)

## Abstract

1. Nature. 1987 Nov 12-18;330(6144):132-7. doi: 10.1038/330132a0.

cDNA sequence of human apolipoprotein(a) is homologous to plasminogen.

McLean JW(1), Tomlinson JE, Kuang WJ, Eaton DL, Chen EY, Fless GM, Scanu AM,
Lawn RM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Cardiovascular Research, Genentech, Inc., South San Francisco,
California 94080.

Lipoprotein(a) is an LDL-like lipoprotein whose concentration in plasma is
correlated with atherosclerosis. The characteristic protein component of
lipoprotein(a) is apolipoprotein(a) which is disulphide-linked to apolipoprotein
B-100. Sequencing of cloned human apolipoprotein(a) complementary DNA shows that
it is very similar to human plasminogen. It contains a serine protease domain
and two types of plasminogen-like kringle domains, one of which is present in 37
copies.
