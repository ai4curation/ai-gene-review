# Crystal structure of 1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate reductoisomerase complexed with cofactors: implications of a flexible loop movement upon substrate binding.
**Authors:** Yajima S, Nonaka T, Kuzuyama T, Seto H, Ohsawa K
**Journal:** J Biochem (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a003105](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a003105)

## Abstract

1. J Biochem. 2002 Mar;131(3):313-7. doi: 10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a003105.

Crystal structure of 1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate reductoisomerase complexed
with cofactors: implications of a flexible loop movement upon substrate binding.

Yajima S(1), Nonaka T, Kuzuyama T, Seto H, Ohsawa K.

Author information:
(1)Department of Bioscience, Tokyo University of Agriculture, Setagaya-ku, Tokyo
156-8502, Japan. yshun@nodai.ac.jp

The key enzyme in the nonmevalonate pathway, 1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate
reductoisomerase (DXR), has been shown to be an effective target of antimalarial
drugs. Here we report the crystal structure of DXR complexed with NADPH and a
sulfate ion from Escherichia coli at 2.2 A resolution. The structure showed the
presence of an extra domain, which is absent from other NADPH-dependent
oxidoreductases, in addition to the conformation of catalytic residues and the
substrate binding site. A flexible loop covering the substrate binding site
plays an important role in the enzymatic reaction and the determination of
substrate specificity.
