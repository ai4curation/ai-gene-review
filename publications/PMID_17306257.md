## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2007 Mar 6;581(5):967-74. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2007.01.084. Epub

Abl-SH3 binding protein 2, 3BP2, interacts with CIN85 and HIP-55.

Le Bras S(1), Moon C, Foucault I, Breittmayer JP, Deckert M.

Author information:
(1)Institut National de la Santé et de la Recherche Médicale, Université de
Nice-Sophia-Antipolis, UMR 576, Hôpital de l'Archet, Route Saint Antoine de
Ginestière, Nice cédex 3, F-06202, France.

The adapter 3BP2 is involved in leukocyte signaling downstream Src/Syk-kinases
coupled immunoreceptors. Here, we show that 3BP2 directly interacts with the
endocytic scaffold protein CIN85 and the actin-binding protein HIP-55. 3BP2
co-localized with CIN85 and HIP-55 in T cell rafts and at the T cell/APC
synapse, an active zone of receptors and proteins recycling. A binding region of
CIN85 SH3 domains on 3BP2 was mapped to a PVPTPR motif in the first proline-rich
region of 3BP2, whereas the C-terminal SH3 domain of HIP-55 bound a more distal
proline-rich domain of 3BP2. Together, our data suggest an unexpected role of
3BP2 in endocytic and cytoskeletal regulation through its interaction with CIN85
and HIP-55.
