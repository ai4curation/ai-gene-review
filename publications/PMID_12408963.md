# Identification of FOXC1 as a TGF-beta1 responsive gene and its involvement in negative regulation of cell growth.
**Authors:** Zhou Y, Kato H, Asanoma K, Kondo H, Arima T, Kato K, Matsuda T, Wake N
**Journal:** Genomics (2002)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 2002 Nov;80(5):465-72.

Identification of FOXC1 as a TGF-beta1 responsive gene and its involvement in
negative regulation of cell growth.

Zhou Y(1), Kato H, Asanoma K, Kondo H, Arima T, Kato K, Matsuda T, Wake N.

Author information:
(1)Division of Molecular and Cell Therapeutics, Department of Molecular
Genetics, Medical Institute of Bioregulation, Kyushu University, 4546
Tsurumihara, Beppu, Oita, 874-0838, Japan.

We cloned the forkhead box C1 (FOXC1) gene, a member of the
forkhead/winged-helix transcription factor family, as a transforming growth
factor-beta1 (TGF-beta1) responsive gene. We showed that TGF-beta1 upregulated
transcription of FOXC1 in several human cancer cell lines. Ectopic expression of
FOXC1 cDNA in HeLa cells, which lack both copies of the FOXC1 allele, restores
the potential of TGF-beta1 to inhibit cell growth by arresting cells in the
G0/G1 phase. In addition, screens of primary endometrial and ovarian cancers
revealed homozygous deletion of FOXC1 in 6.7% of them, one nonsense and one
missense mutation of FOXC1, and transcriptional silencing in 11.7% of primary
cancers. Evidence that a significant fraction of primary cancers exhibited
somatic mutations suggests that FOXC1 functions as a tumor suppressor through
TGF-beta1 mediated signals.
