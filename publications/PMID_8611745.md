# Purification and characterization of a novel enzyme, maltooligosyl trehalose trehalohydrolase, from Arthrobacter sp. Q36.
**Authors:** Nakada T, Maruta K, Mitsuzumi H, Kubota M, Chaen H, Sugimoto T, Kurimoto M, Tsujisaka Y
**Journal:** Biosci Biotechnol Biochem (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1271/bbb.59.2215](https://doi.org/10.1271/bbb.59.2215)

## Abstract

1. Biosci Biotechnol Biochem. 1995 Dec;59(12):2215-8. doi: 10.1271/bbb.59.2215.

Purification and characterization of a novel enzyme, maltooligosyl trehalose 
trehalohydrolase, from Arthrobacter sp. Q36.

Nakada T(1), Maruta K, Mitsuzumi H, Kubota M, Chaen H, Sugimoto T, Kurimoto M, 
Tsujisaka Y.

Author information:
(1)Hayashibara Biochemical Laboratories, Inc., Okayama, Japan.

A novel enzyme, maltooligosyl trehalose trehalohydrolase from Arthrobacter sp. 
Q36 was purified from a cell-free extract to an electrophoretically pure state 
by successive column chromatography on Sepabeads FP-DA13, Butyl-Toyopearl 650M, 
DEAE-Toyopearl 650S, and Toyopearl HW-55S. The enzyme specifically catalyzed the 
hydrolysis of the alpha-1,4-glucosidic linkage that bound the maltooligosyl and 
trehalose moieties of maltooligosyl trehalose. The Km of the enzyme for maltosyl 
trehalose, maltotriosyl trehalose, maltotetraosyl trehalose, and maltopentaosyl 
trehalose was 5.5 mM, 4.6 mM, 7.0 mM, and 4.2 mM, respectively. The enzyme had a 
molecular mass of 62,000 by SDS-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis and a pI of 
4.1 by gel isoelectrofocusing. The N-terminal amino acid of the enzyme was 
threonine. The enzyme showed the highest activity at pH 6.5 and 45 degrees C, 
and was stable from pH 5.0 to 10.0 and up to 45 degrees C. The activity was 
inhibited by Hg2+, Cu2+, Fe2+, and Zn2+.
