    Nat Cell Biol. 2001 Oct;3(10):E229-31. doi: 10.1038/ncb1001-e229.

Glycosylation modifies protein activities in various biological processes. Here,
we report the functions of a novel UDP-sugar transporter (UST74C, an alternative
name for Fringe connection (Frc)) localized to the Golgi apparatus in cellular
signalling of Drosophila. Mutants in the frc gene exhibit phenotypes resembling
wingless and Notch mutants. Both Fringe-dependent and Fringe-independent Notch
pathways are affected, and both glycosylation and proteolytic maturation of
Notch are defective in mutant larvae. The results suggest that changes in
nucleotide-sugar levels can differently affect Wingless and two distinct aspects
of Notch signalling.
