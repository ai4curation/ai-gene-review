# Oxygenation of omega-3 fatty acids by human cytochrome P450 4F3B: effect on 20-hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acid production.
**Authors:** Harmon SD, Fang X, Kaduce TL, Hu S, Raj Gopal V, Falck JR, Spector AA
**Journal:** Prostaglandins Leukot Essent Fatty Acids (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.plefa.2006.05.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plefa.2006.05.005)

## Abstract

1. Prostaglandins Leukot Essent Fatty Acids. 2006 Sep;75(3):169-77. doi:
10.1016/j.plefa.2006.05.005. Epub 2006 Jul 3.

Oxygenation of omega-3 fatty acids by human cytochrome P450 4F3B: effect on
20-hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acid production.

Harmon SD(1), Fang X, Kaduce TL, Hu S, Raj Gopal V, Falck JR, Spector AA.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Carver College of Medicine, 4-403 BSB, University
of Iowa, Iowa City, IA 52242, USA.

Cytochrome P450 (CYP) omega-oxidases convert arachidonic acid (AA) to
20-hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acid (20-HETE), a lipid mediator that modulates
vascular tone. We observed that a microsomal preparation containing recombinant
human CYP4F3B, which converts AA to 20-HETE, converted eicosapentaenoic acid
(EPA) to 20-OH-EPA. Likewise, docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) was converted to
22-OH-DHA, indicating that human CYP4F3B also can oxidize 22-carbon omega-3
fatty acids. Consistent with these findings, addition of 0.5-5 microM EPA, DHA
or omega-3 docosapentaenoic acid (DPA) to incubations containing 0.5 microM
[3H]AA inhibited [3H]20-HETE production by 15-65%. [3H]20-OH-EPA was rapidly
taken up by COS-7 cells, and almost all of the incorporated radioactivity
remained as unmodified 20-OH-EPA. The 20-OH-EPA stimulated luciferase activity
in COS-7 cells that express peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor alpha,
indicating that this EPA metabolite may function as a lipid mediator. These
findings suggest that some functional effects of omega-3 fatty acid
supplementation may be due to inhibition of 20-HETE formation or the conversion
of EPA to the corresponding omega-oxidized product.
