# GcpE is involved in the 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate pathway of isoprenoid biosynthesis in Escherichia coli.
**Authors:** Altincicek B, Kollas AK, Sanderbrand S, Wiesner J, Hintz M, Beck E, Jomaa H
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (2001)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 2001 Apr;183(8):2411-6. doi: 10.1128/JB.183.8.2411-2416.2001.

GcpE is involved in the 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate pathway of
isoprenoid biosynthesis in Escherichia coli.

Altincicek B(1), Kollas AK, Sanderbrand S, Wiesner J, Hintz M, Beck E, Jomaa H.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Biochemistry, Academic Hospital Centre, Justus Liebig
University, D-35392 Giessen, Germany.
Boran.Altincicek@biochemie.med.uni-giessen.de

In a variety of organisms, including plants and several eubacteria, isoprenoids
are synthesized by the mevalonate-independent 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol
4-phosphate (MEP) pathway. Although different enzymes of this pathway have been
described, the terminal biosynthetic steps of the MEP pathway have not been
fully elucidated. In this work, we demonstrate that the gcpE gene of Escherichia
coli is involved in this pathway. E. coli cells were genetically engineered to
utilize exogenously provided mevalonate for isoprenoid biosynthesis by the
mevalonate pathway. These cells were then deleted for the essential gcpE gene
and were viable only if the medium was supplemented with mevalonate or the cells
were complemented with an episomal copy of gcpE.
