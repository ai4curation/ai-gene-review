# Studies on a trace cell lytic activity associated with alpha-lactalbumin.
**Authors:** McKenzie HA, White FH Jr
**Journal:** Biochem Int (1987)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Int. 1987 Feb;14(2):347-56.

Studies on a trace cell lytic activity associated with alpha-lactalbumin.

McKenzie HA, White FH Jr.

alpha-Lactalbumin (alpha-LA) has been examined with a new and sensitive method
for determination of lysozyme activity. Samples of bovine, human, equine, and
rat alpha-LA exhibited cell lytic activity, from 2 X 10(-6) to 45 X 10(-6) of
the specific activity of hen eggwhite lysozyme. The activity was
chromatographically inseparable from bovine and human alpha-LA. Bovine serum
albumin and purified beta-lactoglobulin were inactive. The pH profiles and
reaction kinetics of bovine and human alpha-LA showed differences from those of
the corresponding milk lysozymes, indicating that their lytic activities were
not likely to have resulted from trace lysozyme content. Thus, it appears that a
weak cell lytic activity is inherent to alpha-LA.
