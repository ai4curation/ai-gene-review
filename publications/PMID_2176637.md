# Saccharomyces cerevisiae homoserine kinase is homologous to prokaryotic homoserine kinases.
**Authors:** Schultes NP, Ellington AD, Cherry JM, Szostak JW
**Journal:** Gene (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0378-1119(90)90250-u](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-1119(90)90250-u)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 1990 Dec 15;96(2):177-80. doi: 10.1016/0378-1119(90)90250-u.

Saccharomyces cerevisiae homoserine kinase is homologous to prokaryotic
homoserine kinases.

Schultes NP(1), Ellington AD, Cherry JM, Szostak JW.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Biology, Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston
02114.

The Saccharomyces cerevisiae gene (THR1) encoding homoserine kinase (HK; EC
2.7.1.39) was cloned by complementation in yeast. Disruption of the THR1 gene
results in threonine auxotrophy in yeast. Comparison of the amino acid sequences
of yeast and bacterial HKs reveals substantial similarity.
