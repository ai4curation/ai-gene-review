# miRNA-mediated deadenylation is orchestrated by GW182 through two conserved motifs that interact with CCR4-NOT.

miRNAs recruit the miRNA-induced silencing complex (miRISC), which includes
Argonaute and GW182 as core proteins. GW182 proteins effect translational
repression and deadenylation of target mRNAs. However, the molecular mechanisms
of GW182-mediated repression remain obscure. We show here that human GW182
independently interacts with the PAN2-PAN3 and CCR4-NOT deadenylase complexes.
Interaction of GW182 with CCR4-NOT is mediated by two newly discovered
phylogenetically conserved motifs. Although either motif is sufficient to bind
CCR4-NOT, only one of them can promote processive deadenylation of target mRNAs.
Thus, GW182 serves as both a platform that recruits deadenylases and as a
deadenylase coactivator that facilitates the removal of the poly(A) tail by
CCR4-NOT.
