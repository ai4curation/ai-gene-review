Smad transcription factors lie at the core of one of the most versatile cytokine
signaling pathways in metazoan biology-the transforming growth factor-beta
(TGFbeta) pathway. Recent progress has shed light into the processes of Smad
activation and deactivation, nucleocytoplasmic dynamics, and assembly of
transcriptional complexes. A rich repertoire of regulatory devices exerts
control over each step of the Smad pathway. This knowledge is enabling work on
more complex questions about the organization, integration, and modulation of
Smad-dependent transcriptional programs. We are beginning to uncover
self-enabled gene response cascades, graded Smad response mechanisms, and
Smad-dependent synexpression groups. Our growing understanding of TGFbeta
signaling through the Smad pathway provides general principles for how animal
cells translate complex inputs into concrete behavior.
