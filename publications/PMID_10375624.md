# The elongation factor-1delta (EF-1delta) originates from gene duplication of an EF-1beta ancestor and fusion with a protein-binding domain.
**Authors:** Guerrucci MA, Monnier A, Delalande C, Bellé R
**Journal:** Gene (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0378-1119(99)00153-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0378-1119(99)00153-5)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 1999 Jun 11;233(1-2):83-7. doi: 10.1016/s0378-1119(99)00153-5.

The elongation factor-1delta (EF-1delta) originates from gene duplication of an
EF-1beta ancestor and fusion with a protein-binding domain.

Guerrucci MA(1), Monnier A, Delalande C, Bellé R.

Author information:
(1)Service Commun de Bio-Systématique, Université Pierre et Marie Curie, 9 quai
Saint Bernard, 75005, Paris, France.

The molecular evolution of two components of elongation factor-1 (EF-1),
EF-1beta and EF-1delta was analysed using the distance matrix, the maximum
parsimony and the maximum likelihood methods, after careful alignment of protein
and cDNA sequences. The topology of the phylogenetic trees obtained supports
monophyly of plant EF-1beta and EF-1beta' sequences, and monophyly of higher
eukaryotic animal EF-1beta and EF-1delta sequences. EF-1beta and EF-1delta are
homologous in their C-terminal domain. EF-1delta, which emerged before
arthropods, originates from a beta-type ancestor gene and fusion with a leucine
zipper N-terminal motif. Plant EF-1beta and EF-1beta' correspond to paralogous
genes whose ancestor was most likely duplicated before the emergence of
monocotyledons and dicotyledons.
