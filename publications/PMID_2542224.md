# Stimulation of IS1 excision by bacteriophage P1 ref function.
**Authors:** Lu SD, Lu D, Gottesman M

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1989 Jun;171(6):3427-32. doi: 10.1128/jb.171.6.3427-3432.1989.

Stimulation of IS1 excision by bacteriophage P1 ref function.

Lu SD(1), Lu D, Gottesman M.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Molecular Biology, National Cancer Institute, Bethesda,
Maryland 20205.

Lysogenization by a c1ts variant of coliphage P1, P1c1.100, markedly increased
the frequency of reversion of a galT::IS1 mutation. The formation of Gal+
colonies presumably occurs by microhomologous recombination between the
9-base-pair repeats in galT (CGCCGCTAC) generated by the transposition of IS1.
The responsible P1 gene, ref, has been cloned and sequenced. ref encodes a
22.8-kilodalton protein and is located near the P1 site-specific recombination
function, cre. Expression of ref was repressed by P1 c+. The absence of a
distinctive ribosome-binding site is consistent with a poor translation of ref
from an expression vector in vivo. Placement of a ribosome-binding site before
ref resulted in the extensive synthesis of the Ref protein. Ref stimulated
precise excision in recB or himA cells, but not in recA mutants. Ref was active
in lexA3 mutants, suggesting that the recombination activity of RecA was
directly involved in the reaction. We have constructed a P1c1.100 ref::Tn10
mutant. The absence of Ref did not appear to restrict dramatically the ability
of P1 to grow lytically or to form lysogens. Thus, the role of ref in the
physiology of P1 remains to be determined.
