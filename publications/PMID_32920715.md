OBJECTIVES: To elucidate the role of FSH1 (family of serine hydrolase) in lipid
homeostasis.
RESULTS: Proteins in various species containing alpha/beta hydrolase domain are
known to be involved in lipid metabolism. In silico analysis of the FSH1 gene in
Saccharomyces cerevisiae revealed the presence of alpha/beta hydrolase domain
(ABHD) and a lipase motif (GXSXG), however its function in lipid metabolism
remained elusive. The overexpression of FSH1 in WT and fsh1Δ cells showed a
significant reduction in the cellular phospholipid levels and an increase in the
triacylglycerol levels and lipid droplet (LD) number. Furthermore, the purified
recombinant protein Fsh1p was identified as a lysophospholipase that
specifically acts on lysophosphatidylserine (LPS) and impacts the lipid
homeostasis in S. cerevisiae.
CONCLUSIONS: These results depicted that Fsh1p has a role on lipid homeostasis
and is a lysophospholipase that hydrolyzes lysophosphatidylserine (LPS).
