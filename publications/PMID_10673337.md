# Structure, expression, and chromosome mapping of LATS2, a mammalian homologue of the Drosophila tumor suppressor gene lats/warts.
**Authors:** Yabuta N, Fujii T, Copeland NG, Gilbert DJ, Jenkins NA, Nishiguchi H, Endo Y, Toji S, Tanaka H, Nishimune Y, Nojima H
**Journal:** Genomics (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1006/geno.1999.6065](https://doi.org/10.1006/geno.1999.6065)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 2000 Jan 15;63(2):263-70. doi: 10.1006/geno.1999.6065.

Structure, expression, and chromosome mapping of LATS2, a mammalian homologue of
the Drosophila tumor suppressor gene lats/warts.

Yabuta N(1), Fujii T, Copeland NG, Gilbert DJ, Jenkins NA, Nishiguchi H, Endo Y,
Toji S, Tanaka H, Nishimune Y, Nojima H.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Genetics, Department of Science for Laboratory Animal
Experimentation, Research Institute for Microbial Diseases, Osaka University,
3-1 Yamadaoka, Suita, Osaka, 565-0871, Japan.

We have cloned and characterized LATS2, a novel mammalian homologue of the
Drosophila tumor suppressor gene lats/warts. Northern blot analysis showed
ubiquitous expression of mouse LATS2 (MmLATS2) mRNA, whereas expression of human
LATS2 (HsLATS2) mRNA was enhanced in skeletal muscle and heart. Immunoblotting
analysis of fractionated cell lysates showed HsLats2 to be a nuclear protein. We
mapped the MmLATS2 gene to mouse chromosome 14 by interspecific backcross
analysis. We also mapped the HsLATS2 gene (by fluorescence in situ
hybridization) to the 13q11-q12 region, in which a loss of heterozygosity has
been frequently observed in many primary cancers and to which the tumor
suppressor genes RB and BRCA2 have also been mapped.
