# Grb14 inhibits FGF receptor signaling through the regulation of PLCγ recruitment and activation.
**Authors:** Browaeys-Poly E, Blanquart C, Perdereau D, Antoine AF, Goenaga D, Luzy JP, Chen H, Garbay C, Issad T, Cailliau K, Burnol AF
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2010.09.048](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2010.09.048)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2010 Nov 5;584(21):4383-8. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2010.09.048. Epub
 2010 Oct 8.

Grb14 inhibits FGF receptor signaling through the regulation of PLCγ recruitment
and activation.

Browaeys-Poly E(1), Blanquart C, Perdereau D, Antoine AF, Goenaga D, Luzy JP,
Chen H, Garbay C, Issad T, Cailliau K, Burnol AF.

Author information:
(1)Université Lille 1 Sciences et Technologies, Villeneuve d'Ascq, France.

To decipher the mechanism involved in Grb14 binding to the activated fibroblast
growth factor receptor (FGFR), we used the bioluminescence resonance energy
transfer (BRET) technique and the Xenopus oocyte model. We showed that Grb14 was
recruited to FGFR1 into a trimeric complex containing also phospholipase C gamma
(PLCγ). The presence of Grb14 altered FGF-induced PLCγ phosphorylation and
activation. Grb14-FGFR interaction involved the Grb14-SH2 domain and the FGFR
pY766 residue, which is the PLCγ binding site. Our data led to a molecular model
whereby Grb14 binding to the phosphorylated FGFR induces a conformational change
that unmasks a PLCγ binding motif on Grb14, allowing trapping and inactivation
of PLCγ.
