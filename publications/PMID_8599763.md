# Crystal structure of the PI 3-kinase p85 amino-terminal SH2 domain and its phosphopeptide complexes.
**Authors:** Nolte RT, Eck MJ, Schlessinger J, Shoelson SE, Harrison SC
**Journal:** Nat Struct Biol (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1038/nsb0496-364](https://doi.org/10.1038/nsb0496-364)

## Abstract

1. Nat Struct Biol. 1996 Apr;3(4):364-74. doi: 10.1038/nsb0496-364.

Crystal structure of the PI 3-kinase p85 amino-terminal SH2 domain and its
phosphopeptide complexes.

Nolte RT(1), Eck MJ, Schlessinger J, Shoelson SE, Harrison SC.

Author information:
(1)Howard Hughes Medical Institute and Laboratory of Molecular Medicine,
Children's Hospital, Boston, Massachusetts 02115, USA.

Crystal structures of the amino-terminal SH2 domain of the p85alpha subunit of
phosphatidylinositol (PI) 3-kinase, alone and in complex with phosphopeptides
bearing pTyr-Met/Val-Xaa-Met motifs, show that phosphopeptides bind in the
two-pronged manner seen in high-affinity Lck and Src SH2 complexes, with
conserved interactions between the domain and the peptide segment from
phosphotyrosine to Met+3. Peptide binding requires the rearrangement of a
tyrosyl side chain in the BG loop to create the hydrophobic Met+3 binding
pocket. The structures suggest a mechanism for the biological specificity
exhibited by PI 3-kinase in its interactions with phosphoprotein partners.
