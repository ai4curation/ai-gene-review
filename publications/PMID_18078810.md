# Repression of Smad3 activity by histone demethylase SMCX/JARID1C.
**Authors:** Kim TD, Shin S, Janknecht R
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bbrc.2007.12.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2007.12.013)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2008 Feb 8;366(2):563-7. doi:
10.1016/j.bbrc.2007.12.013. Epub 2007 Dec 17.

Repression of Smad3 activity by histone demethylase SMCX/JARID1C.

Kim TD(1), Shin S, Janknecht R.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, Mayo Clinic, Rochester, MN
55905, USA.

SMCX/JARID1C was recently shown to be a histone H3 lysine 4 (H3K4) demethylase.
Here, we have identified an SMCX isoform that predominantly resides in the
cytoplasm, but still efficiently demethylates trimethylated H3K4. SMCX requires
several functional domains for its demethylase activity and is also capable of
forming homomers through amino acids 204-493. Further, SMCX physically interacts
with Smad3, a mediator of transforming growth factor-beta (TGF-beta), and
overexpression of SMCX inhibits the ability of Smad3 to activate transcription.
Thus, SMCX is a novel Smad3 corepressor that may antagonize the tumor
suppressing activity of the TGF-beta/Smad3 signaling pathway and thereby
contribute to tumorigenesis. Indeed, SMCX is overexpressed in prostate tumors
and seminomas, suggesting that SMCX might be a novel oncogene.
