# Bearded family members inhibit Neuralized-mediated endocytosis and signaling activity of Delta in Drosophila.
**Authors:** Bardin AJ, Schweisguth F
**Journal:** Dev Cell (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.devcel.2005.12.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2005.12.017)

## Abstract

1. Dev Cell. 2006 Feb;10(2):245-55. doi: 10.1016/j.devcel.2005.12.017.

Bearded family members inhibit Neuralized-mediated endocytosis and signaling
activity of Delta in Drosophila.

Bardin AJ(1), Schweisguth F.

Author information:
(1)CNRS UMR 8542, Ecole Normale Supérieure, 46, rue d'Ulm, 75230 Paris Cedex,
France.

Endocytosis of Notch receptor ligands in signaling cells is essential for Notch
receptor activation. In Drosophila, the E3 ubiquitin ligase Neuralized (Neur)
promotes the endocytosis and signaling activity of the ligand Delta (Dl). In
this study, we identify proteins of the Bearded (Brd) family as interactors of
Neur. We show that Tom, a prototypic Brd family member, inhibits Neur-dependent
Notch signaling. Overexpression of Tom inhibits the endocytosis of Dl and
interferes with the interaction of Dl with Neur. Deletion of the Brd gene
complex results in ectopic endocytosis of Dl in dorsal cells of stage 5 embryos.
This defect in Dl trafficking is associated with ectopic expression of the
single-minded gene, a direct Notch target gene that specifies the mesectoderm.
We propose that inhibition of Neur by Brd proteins is important for precise
spatial regulation of Dl signaling.
