# RNase III cleavage is obligate for maturation but not for function of Escherichia coli pre-23S rRNA.
**Authors:** King TC, Sirdeshmukh R, Schlessinger D
**Journal:** Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (1984)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.81.1.185](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.81.1.185)
**PMC:** [PMC344635](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC344635/)

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1984 Jan;81(1):185-8. doi: 10.1073/pnas.81.1.185.

RNase III cleavage is obligate for maturation but not for function of
Escherichia coli pre-23S rRNA.

King TC, Sirdeshmukh R, Schlessinger D.

RNase III makes the initial cleavages that excise Escherichia coli precursor 16S
and 23S rRNA from a single large primary transcript. In mutants deficient in
RNase III, no species cleaved by RNase III are detected and the processing of
23S rRNA precursors to form mature 23S rRNA fails entirely. Instead, 50S
ribosomes are formed with rRNAs up to several hundred nucleotides longer than
mature 23S rRNA. Unexpectedly, these aberrant subunits function well enough to
participate in protein synthesis and permit cell growth. Consistent with the
inference that RNase III cleavages are absolutely required for 23S rRNA
maturation, when 50S ribosomes from a strain deficient in RNase III were
incubated with a ribosome-free extract from a RNase III+ strain, rRNA species
processed by RNase III and species with normal mature 23S rRNA termini were
produced.
