# Human CDC45 protein binds to minichromosome maintenance 7 protein and the p70 subunit of DNA polymerase alpha.
**Authors:** Kukimoto I, Igaki H, Kanda T
**Journal:** Eur J Biochem (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1046/j.1432-1327.1999.00791.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1432-1327.1999.00791.x)

## Abstract

1. Eur J Biochem. 1999 Nov;265(3):936-43. doi: 10.1046/j.1432-1327.1999.00791.x.

Human CDC45 protein binds to minichromosome maintenance 7 protein and the p70
subunit of DNA polymerase alpha.

Kukimoto I(1), Igaki H, Kanda T.

Author information:
(1)Division of Molecular Genetics, National Institute of Infectious Diseases,
Tokyo, Japan. ikuki@nih.go.jp

Budding yeast CDC45 encodes Cdc45p, an essential protein required to trigger
initiation of DNA replication in late G1 phase. We cloned four and one species
of the human Cdc45p homolog cDNA, resulting from different splicing patterns,
from HeLa cell and human placenta cDNA libraries, respectively. A comparison of
the cDNAs and the genomic sequence showed that the longest encoding a 610-amino
acid protein was comprised of 20 exons. One species, which lacks exon 7 and
contains the shorter of two exons 18, was identical with the previously reported
CDC45L cDNA and constituted 24 out of 28 clones from HeLa cells. Splicing was
different in HeLa cells and TIG-1 cells, a human diploid cell line. Human CDC45
protein was found to bind directly in vitro to human minichromosome maintenance
7 protein (hMCM7) and to the p70 subunit of DNA polymerase alpha. The data
support a thesis that human CDC45 acts as a molecular tether to mediate loading
of the DNA polymerase alpha on to the DNA replication complex through binding to
hMCM7.
