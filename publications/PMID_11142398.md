# Carbon source-dependent transcriptional regulation of the mitochondrial glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase gene, GUT2, from Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
**Authors:** Grauslund M, Rønnow B
**Journal:** Can J Microbiol (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1139/w00-105](https://doi.org/10.1139/w00-105)

## Abstract

1. Can J Microbiol. 2000 Dec;46(12):1096-100. doi: 10.1139/w00-105.

Carbon source-dependent transcriptional regulation of the mitochondrial
glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase gene, GUT2, from Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Grauslund M(1), Rønnow B.

Author information:
(1)Danisco Cultor Innovation, Copenhagen, Denmark. g7br@danisco.dk

Cytosolic glycerol kinase (Gut1p) and mitochondrial glycerol-3-phosphate
dehydrogenase (Gut2p) constitute the glycerol utilization pathway in
Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Transcriptional analysis of the GUT2 gene showed that
it was repressed by glucose and derepressed on the non-fermentable carbon
sources, glycerol, lactate and ethanol. Derepression of GUT2 requires the
protein kinase Snflp as well as the heteromeric protein complex, Hap2/3/4/5, and
its putative DNA-binding site (UASHAP) located in the promoter region.
Furthermore, glucose repression of GUT2 requires the negative regulator, Opi1p.
