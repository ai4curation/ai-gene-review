# In vitro reconstitution of the late steps of genetic recombination in E. coli.
**Authors:** Eggleston AK, Mitchell AH, West SC
**Journal:** Cell (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80242-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80242-1)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 1997 May 16;89(4):607-17. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80242-1.

In vitro reconstitution of the late steps of genetic recombination in E. coli.

Eggleston AK(1), Mitchell AH, West SC.

Author information:
(1)Imperial Cancer Research Fund, Clare Hall Laboratories, South Mimms, United
Kingdom.

Purified proteins have been used to reconstitute an in vitro system for the
medial-to-late stages of recombination in E. coli. In this system, RecA protein
formed recombination intermediates that were processed by the actions of the
RuvA, RuvB, and RuvC proteins. RuvAB was found to promote branch migration, to
dissociate the RecA filament, and to modulate the orientation of cleavage of
Holliday junction resolution by RuvC. Monoclonal antibodies directed against
RuvA, RuvB, or RuvC inhibited resolution in the reconstituted system. Specific
protein-protein interactions between the branch migration motor (RuvB) and the
resolvase (RuvC) were also observed. These results provide evidence for
coordinated action during the late stages of recombination, possibly involving
the assembly of a RuvABC branch migration/resolution complex.
