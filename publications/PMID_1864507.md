# Cloning, disruption and sequence of the gene encoding yeast C-5 sterol desaturase.
**Authors:** Arthington BA, Bennett LG, Skatrud PL, Guynn CJ, Barbuch RJ, Ulbright CE, Bard M
**Journal:** Gene (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0378-1119(91)90535-j](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-1119(91)90535-j)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 1991 Jun 15;102(1):39-44. doi: 10.1016/0378-1119(91)90535-j.

Cloning, disruption and sequence of the gene encoding yeast C-5 sterol
desaturase.

Arthington BA(1), Bennett LG, Skatrud PL, Guynn CJ, Barbuch RJ, Ulbright CE,
Bard M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, Indiana University-Purdue University at Indianapolis
46205.

The ERG3 gene from Saccharomyces cerevisiae has been cloned by complementation
of an erg3-2 mutation. ERG3 is the putative gene encoding the C-5 sterol
desaturase required for ergosterol biosynthesis. The functional gene has been
localized on a 2.5-kb HindIII-BamHI fragment containing an open reading frame
comprising 365 amino acids. Gene disruption resulting from a
deletion/substitution demonstrates that ERG3 is not essential for cell viability
or the sparking function.
