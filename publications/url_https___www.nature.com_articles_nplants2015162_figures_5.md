Figure 5: Direct D6PK interaction with phosphatidylinositol phosphates in vitro.
From: Arabidopsis D6PK is a lipid domain-dependent mediator of root epidermal planar polarity
a–c, Protein-lipid overlay assay of lipid strips with 0.5 µg ml–1 GST (b) or GST–D6PK (c) detected by anti-GST antibody. a, Outline of position of lipids on the strip. LPA, lysophosphatidic acid; LPC, lysophosphatidylcholine; PI, phosphatidylinositol (PtdIns); PI(3)P, PtdIns(3)P; PI(4)P, PtdIns(4)P; PI(5)P, PtdIns(5)P; PE, phosphatidylethanolamine; PC, phosphatidylcholine; S1P, sphingosine-1-phosphate; PI(3,4)P2, PtdIns(3,4)P2; PI(3,5)P2, PtdIns(3,5)P2; PI(4,5)P2, PtdIns(4,5)P2; PI(3,4,5)P3, PtdIns(3,4,5)P3; PA, phosphatidic acid; PS, phosphatidylserine. The experiment was performed on three independent membranes revealing highly similar results.
