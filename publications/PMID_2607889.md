# Circadian rhythms of blood clotting time and coagulation factors II, VII, IX and X in rats.
**Authors:** Soulban G, Labrecque G
**Journal:** Life Sci (1989)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0024-3205(89)90015-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-3205(89)90015-5)

## Abstract

1. Life Sci. 1989;45(25):2485-9. doi: 10.1016/0024-3205(89)90015-5.

Circadian rhythms of blood clotting time and coagulation factors II, VII, IX and
X in rats.

Soulban G(1), Labrecque G.

Author information:
(1)Ecole de Pharmacie, Université Laval, Québec, Canada.

The 24-hr variations in clotting times and vitamin K-dependent blood coagulation
factors were studied in rats kept on a 12-hr light-dark cycle (light on:
0600-1800 hours). Clotting times were determined under a binocular microscope by
measuring the time required for the formation of the first fibrin thread.
Factors II, VII and X were analyzed by the prothrombin test while the factor IX
was quantified using the activated partial thromboplastin time assay. Results
indicated that the clotting times were significantly longer during the dark
(activity) period with a peak at 1:00 and a trough at 17:00. Similarly, a
variation was found in factor activity levels: prothrombin (II), factor VII and
factor X had higher activities during the light span (rest period). The highest
activities found at 13:00 and 09:00 were statistically different from the
minimum activity levels obtained at 21:00. Factor IX did not show a significant
circadian variation.
