# Overproduction and purification of the omega subunit of Escherichia coli RNA polymerase.
**Authors:** Gentry DR, Burgess RR
**Journal:** Protein Expr Purif (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1016/1046-5928(90)90050-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/1046-5928(90)90050-9)

## Abstract

1. Protein Expr Purif. 1990 Sep;1(1):81-6. doi: 10.1016/1046-5928(90)90050-9.

Overproduction and purification of the omega subunit of Escherichia coli RNA
polymerase.

Gentry DR(1), Burgess RR.

Author information:
(1)McArdle Laboratory for Cancer Research, University of Wisconsin-Madison
53706.

This paper reports the construction of plasmids which direct the overproduction
of the omega subunit of Escherichia coli RNA polymerase and the subsequent
purification of omega. Useful overproduction is achieved only if the natural
ribosomal binding site region of rpoZ is replaced with the ribosomal binding
site region of bacteriophage T7 gene 10. Overproduction is directed by T7 RNA
polymerase which is provided on a separate plasmid. omega is purified by three
column steps either from the insoluble inclusion body fraction or from the
soluble fractions of lysates. The final yield is approximately 2 mg omega per 10
g cells wet wt. Additionally, we found that recombinant omega is readily cleaved
by an endogenous protease. Sequence analysis of the most prevalent proteolytic
fragment suggested that the protease responsible was the product of the ompT
gene. Cleavage of omega is greatly reduced in ompT- strains.
