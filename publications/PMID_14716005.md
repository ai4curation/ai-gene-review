The lasting effects of neuronal activity on brain development involve
calcium-dependent gene expression. Using a strategy called transactivator trap,
we cloned a calcium-responsive transactivator called CREST (for
calcium-responsive transactivator). CREST is a SYT-related nuclear protein that
interacts with adenosine 3',5'-monophosphate (cAMP) response element-binding
protein (CREB)-binding protein (CBP) and is expressed in the developing brain.
Mice that have a targeted disruption of the crest gene are viable but display
defects in cortical and hippocampal dendrite development. Cortical neurons from
crest mutant mice are compromised in calcium-dependent dendritic growth. Thus,
calcium activation of CREST-mediated transcription helps regulate neuronal
morphogenesis.
