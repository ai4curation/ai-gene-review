# A case of infantile Alexander disease with a milder phenotype and a novel GFAP mutation, L90P.
**Authors:** Suzuki Y, Kanazawa N, Takenaka J, Okumura A, Negoro T, Tsujino S
**Journal:** Brain Dev (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1016/S0387-7604(03)00132-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0387-7604(03)00132-3)

## Abstract

1. Brain Dev. 2004 Apr;26(3):206-8. doi: 10.1016/S0387-7604(03)00132-3.

A case of infantile Alexander disease with a milder phenotype and a novel GFAP
mutation, L90P.

Suzuki Y(1), Kanazawa N, Takenaka J, Okumura A, Negoro T, Tsujino S.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics, Nagoya University Graduate School of Medicine, 65
Tsurumai-cho, Showa-ku, Nagoya 466-8550, Japan. ysuzuki@med.nagoya-u.ac.jp

Alexander disease is a leukoencephalopathy that usually presents during infancy
with developmental delay, macrocephaly and seizures. Several sequencing analyses
have identified mutations in the gene encoding glial fibrillary acidic protein
(GFAP) of patients with Alexander disease. We described a girl who developed
seizures in infancy with atypical CT findings and in whom a novel heterozygous
mutation, L90P (283T --> C), was detected in exon 1 of the GFAP gene. The
neurological deterioration was mild and appeared relatively late for infantile
onset.
