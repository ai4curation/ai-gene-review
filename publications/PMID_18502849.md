Direct involvement of type II secretion system in extracellular translocation of
Shewanella oneidensis outer membrane cytochromes MtrC and OmcA.

Shi L(1), Deng S, Marshall MJ, Wang Z, Kennedy DW, Dohnalkova AC, Mottaz HM,
Hill EA, Gorby YA, Beliaev AS, Richardson DJ, Zachara JM, Fredrickson JK.

Author information:
(1)Microbiology Group, Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, Richland,

MtrC and OmcA are cell surface-exposed lipoproteins important for reducing solid
metal oxides. Deletions of type II secretion system (T2SS) genes reduced their
extracellular release and their accessibility to the proteinase K treatment,
demonstrating the direct involvement of T2SS in translocation of MtrC and OmcA
to the bacterial cell surface.
