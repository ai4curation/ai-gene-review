# The c1 genes of P1 and P7.
**Authors:** Osborne FA, Stovall SR, Baumstark BR

## Abstract

1. Nucleic Acids Res. 1989 Oct 11;17(19):7671-80. doi: 10.1093/nar/17.19.7671.

The c1 genes of P1 and P7.

Osborne FA(1), Stovall SR, Baumstark BR.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, Georgia State University, Atlanta 30303.

The c1 genes of the heteroimmune phages P1 and P7 were sequenced and their
products were compared. P7c1 expression was correlated with the translation in
vitro of a protein whose predicted molecular weight (33,000 daltons) is
indistinguishable from that of the P1c1 repressor. The c1 regions from both P1
and P7 were found to contain open reading frames capable of coding for a
283-amino acid protein whose predicted secondary structure lacks the
helix-turn-helix motif commonly associated with repressor proteins. Two P1c1
amber mutations were localized to the 283-amino acid open reading frame. The
P1c1 and P7c1 sequences were found to differ at only 18 positions, all but two
of which alter the third position of the affected codon and do not alter the
amino acid sequence of the protein. Plasmids expressing the c1 gene from either
phage cause the repression of transcription from a cloned promoter situated
upstream of P1c1.
