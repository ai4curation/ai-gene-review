**Authors:** Mao C, Obeid LM

## Abstract

1. Methods Enzymol. 2000;311:223-32. doi: 10.1016/s0076-6879(00)11085-7.

Yeast sphingosine-1-phosphate phosphatases: assay, expression, deletion,
purification, and cellular localization by GFP tagging.

Mao C(1), Obeid LM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medicine, Medical University of South Carolina, Charleston
29425, USA.

DHS-1-P phosphatases cloned from yeast represent novel lipid phosphatases, which
were not thought to exist in yeast. Identification and characterization of YSR2
and YSR3 have demonstrated that the DHS-1-P phosphatase is an important mediator
in the biosynthesis of sphingolipids and in the maintenance of the balance of
signaling lipid molecules ceramide, sphingosine, and sphingosine-1-P. Methods
introduced here for purification, activity assay, in vivo labeling, and cellular
localization using GFP tagging are expected to facilitate our understanding of
this enzyme.
