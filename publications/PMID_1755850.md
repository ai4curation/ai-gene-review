# Morquio disease: isolation, characterization and expression of full-length cDNA for human N-acetylgalactosamine-6-sulfate sulfatase.
**Authors:** Tomatsu S, Fukuda S, Masue M, Sukegawa K, Fukao T, Yamagishi A, Hori T, Iwata H, Ogawa T, Nakashima Y
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0006-291x(91)91244-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/0006-291x(91)91244-7)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1991 Dec 16;181(2):677-83. doi:
10.1016/0006-291x(91)91244-7.

Morquio disease: isolation, characterization and expression of full-length cDNA
for human N-acetylgalactosamine-6-sulfate sulfatase.

Tomatsu S(1), Fukuda S, Masue M, Sukegawa K, Fukao T, Yamagishi A, Hori T, Iwata
H, Ogawa T, Nakashima Y, et al.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics, Gifu University School of Medicine, Japan.

We cloned and sequenced a full-length cDNA of human placental
N-acetylgalactosamine-6-sulfate sulfatase, the enzyme deficient in Morquio
disease. The 2339-nucleotide sequence contained 1566 nucleotides which encoded a
polypeptide of 522 amino acid residues. The deduced amino acid sequence was
composed of a 26-amino acid N-terminal signal peptide and a mature polypeptide
of 496 amino acid residues including two potential asparagine-linked
glycosylation sites. Expression of the cDNA in transfected deficient fibroblasts
resulted in higher production of this sulfatase activity than in untransfected
deficient fibroblasts. The cDNA clone was hybridized to only a 2.3-kilobase
species of RNA in human fibroblasts. The amino acid sequence of
N-acetylgalactosamine-6-sulfate sulfatase showed a high degree of homology with
those of other sulfatases such as human arylsulfatases A, B or C,
glucosamine-6-sulfatase, iduronate-2-sulfatase and sea urchin arylsulfatase.
