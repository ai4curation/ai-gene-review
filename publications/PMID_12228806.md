Chitin synthesis is a process maintained across the fungal kingdom that, thanks
to the power of genetic manipulation of yeast cells, is now beginning to be
understood. Chitin synthesis is based on the regulation of distinct chitin
synthase isoenzymes whose number ranges from one in Schizosaccharomyces pombe to
seven in some filamentous fungi, such as Aspergillus fumigatus. This high
diversity makes it difficult to find a unique model of regulation. However, the
results available suggest common themes in regulation. The arrival of the
genomic era, together with the development of fungal genetic technology should
allow experimental approaches to this process.
