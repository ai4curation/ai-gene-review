# Ribokinase from E. coli: expression, purification, and substrate specificity.
**Authors:** Chuvikovsky DV, Esipov RS, Skoblov YS, Chupova LA, Muravyova TI, Miroshnikov AI, Lapinjoki S, Mikhailopulo IA
**Journal:** Bioorg Med Chem (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bmc.2006.05.057](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmc.2006.05.057)

## Abstract

1. Bioorg Med Chem. 2006 Sep 15;14(18):6327-32. doi: 10.1016/j.bmc.2006.05.057.
Epub 2006 Jun 19.

Ribokinase from E. coli: expression, purification, and substrate specificity.

Chuvikovsky DV(1), Esipov RS, Skoblov YS, Chupova LA, Muravyova TI, Miroshnikov
AI, Lapinjoki S, Mikhailopulo IA.

Author information:
(1)Shemyakin-Ovchinnikov Institute of Bioorganic Chemistry, Russian Academy of
Sciences, Miklukho-Maklaya 16/10, Moscow 117997, Russia.

Ribokinase (RK) was expressed in the Escherichia coli ER2566 cells harboring the
constructed expression plasmid encompassing the rbsK gene, encoding ribokinase.
The recombinant enzyme was purified from sonicated cells by double
chromatography to afford a preparation that was ca. 90% pure and had specific
activity of 75 micromol/min mg protein. Catalytic activity of RK: (i) is
strongly dependent on the presence of monovalent cations
(potassium>>>ammonium>cesium), and (ii) is cooperatively enhanced by divalent
magnesium and manganese ions. Besides D-ribose and 2-deoxy-D-ribose, RK was
found to catalyze the 5-O-phosphorylation of D-arabinose, D-xylose, and
D-fructose in the presence of ATP, and potassium and magnesium ions; L-ribose
and L-arabinose are not substrates for the recombinant enzyme. A new
radiochemical method for monitoring the formation of
D-pentofuranose-5-[32P]phosphates in the presence of [gamma-32P]ATP and RK is
reported.
