# Assignment of the genes for triose phosphate isomerase to chromosome 6 and tripeptidase-1 to chromosome 10 in Mus musculus by somatic cell hybridization.
**Authors:** Leinwand LA, Kozak CA, Ruddle FH
**Journal:** Somatic Cell Genet (1978)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF01538987](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01538987)

## Abstract

1. Somatic Cell Genet. 1978 Mar;4(2):233-40. doi: 10.1007/BF01538987.

Assignment of the genes for triose phosphate isomerase to chromosome 6 and
tripeptidase-1 to chromosome 10 in Mus musculus by somatic cell hybridization.

Leinwand LA, Kozak CA, Ruddle FH.

Evidence is presented for the assignment of the gene for triose phosphate
isomerase to Mus musculus chromosome 6 and tripeptidase-1 to chromosome 10 by
synteny testing and chromosome assignment in Chinese hamster X mouse somatic
cell hybrid clones. Neither TPI nor TRIP-1 were expressed concordantly with any
known isozyme markers in 45 hybrid clones (13 primary and 32 secondary).
Karyotypic analysis of 21 clones showed that the expression of TPI and
chromosome 6 were concordant in all cases as was expressed of TRIP-1 and
chromosome 10. Both chromosomes were previously unmarked by isozymes.
