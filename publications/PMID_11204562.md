# Factor XII-dependent contact activation on endothelial cells and binding proteins gC1qR and cytokeratin 1.
**Authors:** Joseph K, Shibayama Y, Ghebrehiwet B, Kaplan AP
**Journal:** Thromb Haemost (2001)

## Abstract

1. Thromb Haemost. 2001 Jan;85(1):119-24.

Factor XII-dependent contact activation on endothelial cells and binding
proteins gC1qR and cytokeratin 1.

Joseph K(1), Shibayama Y, Ghebrehiwet B, Kaplan AP.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medicine and Konishi-MUSC Institute for Inflammation Research,
Medical University of South Carolina, Charleston 29425, USA.

Although proteins of the kinin-forming pathway are bound along the surface of
endothelial cells, the mechanism of activation of this proteolytic cascade is
unclear. Endothelial cell surface proteins, gC1qR and cytokeratin 1, are capable
of binding Factor XII and high molecular weight kininogen (HK) in a
zinc-dependent reaction thus we considered the possibility that these proteins
might catalyze initiation of the cascade. Incubation of Factor XII,
prekallikrein, and HK with gC1qR or cytokeratin 1 leads to a zinc-dependent and
Factor XII-dependent conversion of prekallikrein to kallikrein. We also
demonstrate that normal plasma is capable of activating upon interaction with
the cells whereas plasma deficient in Factor XII, prekallikrein and HK do not
activate. Normal plasma activation was inhibitable by antibody to gC1qR and
cytokeratin 1. Thus, gC1qR and cytokeratin 1, represent potential initiating
surfaces for activation of the plasma kinin-forming cascade and may do so as a
result of their expression along cell surfaces.
