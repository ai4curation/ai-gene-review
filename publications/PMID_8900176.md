# Zebrafish Pax9 encodes two proteins with distinct C-terminal transactivating domains of different potency negatively regulated by adjacent N-terminal sequences.
**Authors:** Nornes S, Mikkola I, Krauss S, Delghandi M, Perander M, Johansen T
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.271.43.26914](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.271.43.26914)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1996 Oct 25;271(43):26914-23. doi: 10.1074/jbc.271.43.26914.

Zebrafish Pax9 encodes two proteins with distinct C-terminal transactivating
domains of different potency negatively regulated by adjacent N-terminal
sequences.

Nornes S(1), Mikkola I, Krauss S, Delghandi M, Perander M, Johansen T.

Author information:
(1)Departments of Biochemistry, Institute of Medical Biology, University of
Tromso, 9037 Tromso, Norway.

We describe the isolation of cDNA clones for zebrafish Pax9. Pax9 expression was
initiated at the end of the segmentation period in mesenchymal sclerotome cells
on both sides of the notochord similarly to the corresponding mouse and chick
genes. Two transcripts, Pax9a and -b, are generated by alternative splicing. The
gene contains 4 exons with exon 3 being included in the Pax9a transcript and
spliced out in the Pax9b transcript. The Pax9a and -b proteins are identical for
212 amino acids from the N terminus but contain distinct C-terminal regions of
131 and 58 amino acids, respectively. The paired domain of Pax9 displayed a
binding-site specificity distinct from Pax6 but similar to Pax1 and -2. Both
Pax9a and -b activated a promoter containing a paired domain binding site.
However, this activation was observed when low amounts of Pax9 expression
vectors were used. Higher amounts led to a sharp decrease in the activation and
even turned into repression. Both the distinct C-terminal regions of Pax9a and
-b harbored transcriptional activating domains of different potency not revealed
in the context of the full-length proteins due to a negative influence of the
N-terminal region including the paired domain.
