# High molecular mass exopolyphosphatase from the cytosol of the yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae is encoded by the PPN1 gene.
**Authors:** Andreeva NA, Kulakovskaya TV, Kulaev IS
**Journal:** Biochemistry (Mosc) (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1134/s0006297906090045](https://doi.org/10.1134/s0006297906090045)

## Abstract

1. Biochemistry (Mosc). 2006 Sep;71(9):975-7. doi: 10.1134/s0006297906090045.

High molecular mass exopolyphosphatase from the cytosol of the yeast
Saccharomyces cerevisiae is encoded by the PPN1 gene.

Andreeva NA(1), Kulakovskaya TV, Kulaev IS.

Author information:
(1)Skryabin Institute of Biochemistry and Physiology of Microorganisms, Russian
Academy of Sciences, Pushchino, Moscow Region 142290, Russia.

It has been shown that the high molecular mass exopolyphosphatase localized in
cytosol of the yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae is encoded by the PPN1 gene. This
enzyme is expressed under special culture conditions when stationary phase cells
are passing on to new budding on glucose addition and phosphate excess. The
enzyme under study releases orthophosphate from the very beginning of
polyphosphate hydrolysis.
