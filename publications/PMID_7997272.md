# XL alpha s is a new type of G protein.

## Abstract

1. Nature. 1994 Dec 22-29;372(6508):804-9. doi: 10.1038/372804a0.

XL alpha s is a new type of G protein.

Kehlenbach RH(1), Matthey J, Huttner WB.

Author information:
(1)Institute for Neurobiology, University of Heidelberg, Germany.

Erratum in
    Nature 1995 May 18;375(6528):253.

The GTP-binding proteins are well-known regulators of cellular functions,
including vesicular transport. Cholera toxin, which is known to catalyse
ADP-ribosylation of the alpha s subunit of heterotrimeric G proteins, stimulates
secretory vesicle formation from the trans-Golgi network. Here we describe a new
cholera toxin target, an 'extra large' G protein (XL alpha s; M(r) 92K) which
consists of a new 51K XL-portion linked to a G alpha s truncated at the amino
terminus. XL alpha s is specifically associated with the trans-Golgi network and
occurs selectively in cells containing both the regulated and the constitutive
pathway of protein secretion. Hence, XL alpha s may mediate the effects of
cholera toxin on secretory vesicle formation.
