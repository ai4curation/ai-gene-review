# Cloning of Schizosaccharomyces pombe bio2 by heterologous complementation of a Saccharomyces cerevisiae mutant.
**Authors:** Phalip V, Lemoine Y, Jeltsch JM
**Journal:** Curr Microbiol (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s002849900470](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002849900470)

## Abstract

1. Curr Microbiol. 1999 Dec;39(6):348-0350. doi: 10.1007/s002849900470.

Cloning of Schizosaccharomyces pombe bio2 by heterologous complementation of a
Saccharomyces cerevisiae mutant.

Phalip V(1), Lemoine Y, Jeltsch JM.

Author information:
(1)Laboratoire de Biotechnologie, Ecole Supérieure de Biotechnologie de
Strasbourg, Boulevard Sébastien Brandt, 67400 Illkirch-Graffenstaden, France.

A Saccharomyces cerevisiae mutant affected in the last step of the biotin
biosynthesis pathway was isolated by using a transposon mutagenesis method. The
gene BIO2, encoding a biotin synthase, is shown to be interrupted in this
mutant. Heterologous complementation experiment allowed the cloning and the
characterization of a novel bio gene: bio2, encoding biotin synthase from
Schizosaccharomyces pombe.
