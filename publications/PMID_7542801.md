# A role for CD5 in TCR-mediated signal transduction and thymocyte selection.
**Authors:** Tarakhovsky A, Kanner SB, Hombach J, Ledbetter JA, Müller W, Killeen N, Rajewsky K
**Journal:** Science (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.7542801](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7542801)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1995 Jul 28;269(5223):535-7. doi: 10.1126/science.7542801.

A role for CD5 in TCR-mediated signal transduction and thymocyte selection.

Tarakhovsky A(1), Kanner SB, Hombach J, Ledbetter JA, Müller W, Killeen N,
Rajewsky K.

Author information:
(1)Institute for Genetics, University of Cologne, Germany.

CD5 is a transmembrane protein that is expressed on the surface of T cells and a
subset of B cells. The absence of CD5 rendered thymocytes hyperresponsive to
stimulation through the T cell antigen receptor (TCR) in vitro. Selection of T
cells expressing three distinct transgenic TCRs was also abnormal in
CD5-deficient mice. These observations indicate that CD5 can influence the fate
of developing thymocytes by acting as a negative regulator of TCR-mediated
signal transduction.
