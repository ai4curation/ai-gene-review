# Binding of Vac14 to neuronal nitric oxide synthase: Characterisation of a new internal PDZ-recognition motif.
**Authors:** Lemaire JF, McPherson PS

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2006 Dec 22;580(30):6948-54. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2006.11.061.
Epub  2006 Dec 4.

Binding of Vac14 to neuronal nitric oxide synthase: Characterisation of a new

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology and Neurosurgery, Montreal Neurological Institute,

PDZ domains mediate protein interactions primarily through either classical
recognition of carboxyl-terminal motifs or PDZ/PDZ domain associations. Several
studies have also described internal modes of PDZ recognition, most of which
depend on beta-finger structures. Here, we describe a novel interaction between
the PDZ domain of nNOS and Vac14, the activator of the PtdIns(3)P 5-kinase
PIKfyve. Binding assays using various Vac14 deletion constructs revealed a
beta-finger independent interaction that is based on a novel internal motif.
Mutational analyses reveal essential residues within the motif allowing us to
define a new type of PDZ domain interaction.
