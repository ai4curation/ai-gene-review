# Corynebacterium sp. U-96 contains a cluster of genes of enzymes for the catabolism of sarcosine to pyruvate.
**Authors:** Suzuki H, Tamamura R, Yajima S, Kanno M, Suguro M
**Journal:** Biosci Biotechnol Biochem (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1271/bbb.69.952](https://doi.org/10.1271/bbb.69.952)

## Abstract

1. Biosci Biotechnol Biochem. 2005 May;69(5):952-6. doi: 10.1271/bbb.69.952.

Corynebacterium sp. U-96 contains a cluster of genes of enzymes for the
catabolism of sarcosine to pyruvate.

Suzuki H(1), Tamamura R, Yajima S, Kanno M, Suguro M.

Author information:
(1)Division of Biosciences, Graduate School of Fundamental Life Science,
Kitasato University, Kanagawa. suzuki@sci.kitasato-u.ac.jp

The sarcosine oxidase gene and nearby genes from Corynebacterium sp. U-96 were
determined. The genes for serine hydroxymethyltransferase, the beta, delta,
alpha, and gamma subunits of sarcosine oxidase, serine dehydratase, and
10-formyltetrahydrofolate hydrolase are arranged in this order. This suggests
that the bacteria contain a cluster of genes for the catabolism of sarcosine to
pyruvate. The possibility that the gene cluster is a merit for the cellular
energy demands of the bacteria is discussed. Functional expression of sarcosine
oxidase in Escherichia coli was accomplished, but the beta subunit and the
betadelta complex were expressed at a low level as a soluble protein.
