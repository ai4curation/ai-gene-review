# Identification of bases required for P2 integrase core binding and recombination.
**Authors:** Sylwan L, Frumerie C, Haggård-Ljungquist E
**Journal:** Virology (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.virol.2010.05.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.virol.2010.05.009)

## Abstract

1. Virology. 2010 Sep 1;404(2):240-5. doi: 10.1016/j.virol.2010.05.009.

Identification of bases required for P2 integrase core binding and
recombination.

Sylwan L(1), Frumerie C, Haggård-Ljungquist E.

Author information:
(1)Department of Genetics, Microbiology and Toxicology, Stockholm University,
SE-106 91 Stockholm, Sweden.

Temperate coliphage P2 integrates its genome into the host chromosome upon
lysogenization via a site-specific recombination event mediated by an integrase
belonging to the complex family of tyrosine recombinases. The host integration
site attB (BOB') is localized in the end of the cyaR gene and shares 27
nucleotides with the core of attP (COC'). In the present study we determine the
minimal attB site using an in vivo recombination assay. Ten nt on the left side
(B) are found to be nonessential for recombination. We show that the integrase
has higher affinity for the right side (B') compared to B and that artificial
B'OB' and an attP site with a matching core (C'OC') are efficient substrates for
recombination in vitro. We have analyzed single nucleotides in attB and find
that sequence homology within a non-centrally located quadruplet in the
hypothetical overlap region is essential for efficient recombination in vivo.
