# Lysine144 is essential for the catalytic activity of Saccharomyces cerevisiae transaldolase.
**Authors:** Miosga T, Schaaff-Gerstenschlager I, Franken E, Zimmermann FK
**Journal:** Yeast (1993)
**DOI:** [10.1002/yea.320091111](https://doi.org/10.1002/yea.320091111)

## Abstract

1. Yeast. 1993 Nov;9(11):1241-9. doi: 10.1002/yea.320091111.

Lysine144 is essential for the catalytic activity of Saccharomyces cerevisiae
transaldolase.

Miosga T(1), Schaaff-Gerstenschlager I, Franken E, Zimmermann FK.

Author information:
(1)Institut für Mikrobiologie, Technische Hochschule Darmstadt, Germany.

Replacement of lysine144 by glutamine in the pentose phosphate pathway enzyme
transaldolase of Saccharomyces cerevisiae is associated with the complete loss
of activity indicating the essential role in catalysis. Neither histidine nor
cysteine is important for catalytic activity as proposed for the Candida utilis
enzyme. Also we could not find any evidence for a half-site character of the
enzyme as described for transaldolase of C. utilis. Therefore, the reaction
mechanisms for the two enzymes are different.
