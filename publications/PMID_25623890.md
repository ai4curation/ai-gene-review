CD44 is a complex cell adhesion molecule that mediates communication and
adhesion between adjacent cells as well as between cells and the extracellular
matrix. CD44 pre-mRNA produces various mRNA isoforms through alternative
splicing of 20 exons, among which exons 1-5 (C1-C5) and 16-20 (C6-C10) are
constant exons, whereas exons 6-15 (V1-V10) are variant exons. CD44 V10 exon has
important roles in breast tumor progression and Hodgkin lymphoma. Here we show
that increased expression of hnRNP L inhibits V10 exon splicing of CD44
pre-mRNA, whereas reduced expression of hnRNP L promotes V10 exon splicing. In
addition, hnRNP L also promotes V10 splicing of endogenous CD44 pre-mRNA.
Through mutation analysis, we demonstrate that the effects of hnRNP L on V10
splicing are abolished when the CA-rich sequence on the upstream intron of V10
exon is disrupted. However, hnRNP L effects are stronger if more CA-repeats are
provided. Furthermore, we show that hnRNP L directly contacts the CA-rich
sequence. Importantly, we provide evidences that hnRNP L inhibits U2AF65 binding
on the upstream Py tract of V10 exon. Our results reveal that hnRNP L is a new
regulator for CD44 V10 exon splicing.
