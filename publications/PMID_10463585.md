# Identification of a novel complex between human kallikrein 2 and protease inhibitor-6 in prostate cancer tissue.
**Authors:** Mikolajczyk SD, Millar LS, Marker KM, Rittenhouse HG, Wolfert RL, Marks LS, Charlesworth MC, Tindall DJ
**Journal:** Cancer Res (1999)

## Abstract

1. Cancer Res. 1999 Aug 15;59(16):3927-30.

Identification of a novel complex between human kallikrein 2 and protease
inhibitor-6 in prostate cancer tissue.

Mikolajczyk SD(1), Millar LS, Marker KM, Rittenhouse HG, Wolfert RL, Marks LS,
Charlesworth MC, Tindall DJ.

Author information:
(1)Hybritech Inc., San Diego, California 92121, USA. sdmikolajczyk@beckman.com

Human kallikrein (hK) 2 is an arginine-selective serine protease expressed
predominantly in the prostate that has an 80% sequence identity with
prostate-specific antigen. Expression of hK2 is elevated in the tumor epithelium
compared to benign prostate tissue. We have purified, sequenced, and identified
a novel hK2 complex in prostate tissue consisting of hK2 and a serine protease
inhibitor known as protease inhibitor-6 (PI-6). This 64-kDa SDS-PAGE stable
complex is elevated in the tumor and is approximately 10% of total hK2. No
comparable complex of prostate-specific antigen was detected. PI-6, also known
as cytoplasmic antiprotease, has been characterized as an intracellular
inhibitor of trypsin and chymotrypsin-like proteases, which has high homology to
plasminogen activator inhibitor 1 and 2. The physiological role of PI-6 in the
prostate and its relationship to hK2 and prostate cancer are under
investigation.
