# Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase variants in Hawaii.

## Abstract

1. Hum Hered. 1992;42(5):327-9. doi: 10.1159/000154092.

Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase variants in Hawaii.

Beutler E(1), Westwood B, Kuhl W, Hsia YE.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular and Experimental Medicine, Scripps Research
Institute, La Jolla, CA 92037.

Sequence analysis has been performed on the DNA of 13 glucose-6-phosphate
dehydrogenase (G6PD) deficient males from Hawaii, 6 of Filipino, 6 of Laotian,
and 1 of Chinese extraction. Four different mutations were found: A-->T at cDNA
nt 835, G-->A at nt 871, C-->T at nt 1360, and G-->A at nt 1388. The mutations
at nt 835 and nt 1360 have not been described previously, and the latter, in
particular, appears to be relatively common. The nt 1360 mutation changes the
same codon as is altered in a previously described mutation, G6PD Andalus.
