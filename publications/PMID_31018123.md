The melanization response is an important defense mechanism in arthropods. This
reaction is mediated by phenoloxidases (POs), which are activated by complex
extracellular serine protease (SP) cascades. Here, we investigate the role of
SPs in the melanization response using compound mutants in D. melanogaster and
discover phenotypes previously concealed in single-mutant analyses. We find that
two SPs, Hayan and Sp7, activate the melanization response in different manners:
Hayan is required for blackening wound sites, whereas Sp7 regulates an alternate
melanization reaction responsible for the clearance of Staphylococcus aureus. We
present evidence that Sp7 is regulated by SPs activating the Toll NF-κB pathway,
namely ModSP and Grass. Additionally, we reveal a role for the combined action
of Hayan and Psh in propagating Toll signaling downstream of pattern recognition
receptors activating either Toll signaling or the melanization response.
