# Human tribbles, a protein family controlling mitogen-activated protein kinase cascades.

Control of mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) cascades is central to
regulation of many cellular responses. We describe here human tribbles
homologues (Htrbs) that control MAPK activity. MAPK kinases interact with Trbs
and regulate their steady state levels. Further, Trbs selectively regulate the
activation of extracellular signal-regulated kinases, c-Jun NH2-terminal
kinases, and p38 MAPK with different relative levels of activity for the three
classes of MAPK observed depending on the level of Trb expression. These results
suggest that Trbs control both the extent and the specificity of MAPK kinase
activation of MAPK.
