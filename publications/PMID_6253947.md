# DNA sequence of the int-xis-Pi region of the bacteriophage lambda; overlap of the int and xis genes.
**Authors:** Davies RW
**Journal:** Nucleic Acids Res (1980)
**DOI:** [10.1093/nar/8.8.1765](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/8.8.1765)
**PMC:** [PMC324034](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC324034/)

## Abstract

1. Nucleic Acids Res. 1980 Apr 25;8(8):1765-82. doi: 10.1093/nar/8.8.1765.

DNA sequence of the int-xis-Pi region of the bacteriophage lambda; overlap of
the int and xis genes.

Davies RW.

The DNA sequence of the int and xis genes of lambda bacteriophage, as well as
that of the PI promoter and a region of unknown function beyond this, was
determined by the chain termination procedure. The Pribnow box sequence of the
PI promoter lies just within the xis gene, and both possible sites of mRNA
initiation from PI are within the xis gene. The end of the xis gene in its turn
overlaps the start of the int gene by 23 base pairs, in a different reading
frame. This overlap may play a role in ensuring efficient excision of the
prophage in response to natural induction signals.
