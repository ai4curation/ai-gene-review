## Abstract

1. EMBO J. 1988 Nov;7(11):3465-70. doi: 10.1002/j.1460-2075.1988.tb03221.x.

The human Lyt-3 molecule requires CD8 for cell surface expression.

DiSanto JP(1), Knowles RW, Flomenberg N.

Author information:
(1)Effector Lymphocyte Biology, Memorial Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center, New
York, NY 10021.

We have previously identified a monoclonal antibody, T8/2T8-5H7, which clustered
serologically with CD8 monoclonal antibodies, but lacked reactivity with L cell
transfectants expressing the human CD8 molecule (Lyt-2 homologue). Based on
these observations, we postulated that T8/2T8-5H7 might recognize the human
Lyt-3 gene product. To test this hypothesis, we have isolated a full-length cDNA
encoding the human Lyt-3 molecule and have characterized its product in
additional transfection experiments. The results of these studies indicate that
the human Lyt-3 cDNA encodes a product recognized by the antibody T8/2T8-5H7.
Interestingly, the human Lyt-3 molecule cannot be expressed alone, but requires
the human Lyt-2 homologue for efficient cell surface expression. A heterodimer
composed of the human Lyt-2 and Lyt-3 molecules may have importance in T
cell-target cell interactions.
