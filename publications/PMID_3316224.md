# SAM1, the structural gene for one of the S-adenosylmethionine synthetases in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Sequence and expression.
**Authors:** Thomas D, Surdin-Kerjan Y
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1987)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1987 Dec 5;262(34):16704-9.

SAM1, the structural gene for one of the S-adenosylmethionine synthetases in
Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Sequence and expression.

Thomas D(1), Surdin-Kerjan Y.

Author information:
(1)Laboratoire d'Enzymologie, Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique,
Gif-sur-Yvette, France.

Saccharomyces cerevisiae contains two genes, SAM1 and SAM2, encoding functional
S-adenosylmethionine synthetases. The gene SAM1 was isolated by functional
complementation of a double mutant of S. cerevisiae, and its identity was
confirmed by gene disruption. The cloned gene was used to probe wild type
chromosomal DNA, and two regions hybridizing with SAM1 were found, one of which
is the SAM1 region. The DNA sequence of SAM1 is reported. The translation
product shows a high homology with the one deduced from the sequence of the MetK
gene encoding the SAM synthetase of Escherichia coli.
