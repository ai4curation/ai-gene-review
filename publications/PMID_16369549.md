## Abstract

1. Nat Methods. 2006 Jan;3(1):27-9. doi: 10.1038/nmeth843.

Comment in
    Nat Methods. 2006 Jan;3(1):12-3. doi: 10.1038/nmeth0106-12.

MicroRNAs (miRNAs) are 20-23 nucleotide (nt) RNA molecules that regulate gene 
expression post-transcriptionally. A key step toward understanding the function 
of the hundreds of miRNAs identified in animals is to determine their expression 
during development. Here we performed a detailed analysis of conditions for in 
situ detection of miRNAs in the zebrafish embryo using locked nucleic acid 
(LNA)-modified DNA probes and report expression patterns for 15 miRNAs in the 
mouse embryo.
