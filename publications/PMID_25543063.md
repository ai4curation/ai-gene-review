Recent studies demonstrated an association between the K153R polymorphism in the
myostatin gene with extreme longevity, lower muscle strength and obesity but the
molecular basis of these associations has not been clarified. Here, we show that
the K153R mutation significantly increases the rate of proteolysis of
promyostatin by furin, but has no effect on the activity of the latent complex
or the cleavage of the latent complex by bone morphogenetic protein 1 (BMP-1).
The increased rate of activation of K153R mutant promyostatin may explain why
this polymorphism is associated with obesity, lower muscle strength and
extension of lifespan.
