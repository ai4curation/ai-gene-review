Comment in
    Science. 2007 May 11;316(5826):845-6. doi: 10.1126/science.1143339.

Inositol pyrophosphates are a diverse group of high-energy signaling molecules
whose cellular roles remain an active area of study. We report a previously
uncharacterized class of inositol pyrophosphate synthase and find it is
identical to yeast Vip1 and Asp1 proteins, regulators of actin-related
protein-2/3 (ARP 2/3) complexes. Vip1 and Asp1 acted as enzymes that encode
inositol hexakisphosphate (IP6) and inositol heptakisphosphate (IP7) kinase
activities. Alterations in kinase activity led to defects in cell growth,
morphology, and interactions with ARP complex members. The functionality of Asp1
and Vip1 may provide cells with increased signaling capacity through metabolism
of IP6.
