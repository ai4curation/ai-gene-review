# The fast-mobility isoform of mouse Mcl-1 is a mitochondrial matrix-localized protein with attenuated anti-apoptotic activity.
**Authors:** Huang CR, Yang-Yen HF
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2010.07.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2010.07.013)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2010 Aug 4;584(15):3323-30. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2010.07.013.
Epub  2010 Jul 11.

The fast-mobility isoform of mouse Mcl-1 is a mitochondrial matrix-localized
protein with attenuated anti-apoptotic activity.

Huang CR(1), Yang-Yen HF.

Author information:
(1)Graduate Institute of Life Sciences, National Defense Medical Center,
Academia Sinica, Taipei, Taiwan.

The full-length pro-survival protein Mcl-1 predominantly resides on the outer
membrane of mitochondria. Here, we identified a mitochondrial matrix-localized
isoform of Mcl-1 that lacks 33 amino acid residues at the N-terminus which serve
both as a mitochondrial targeting and processing signal. Ectopically-expressed
Mcl-1 without the N-terminal 33 residues failed to enter the mitochondrial
matrix but retained wt-like activities both for interaction with BH3-only
proteins and anti-apoptosis. In contrast, the mitochondrial matrix-localized
isoform failed to interact with BH3-only proteins and manifested an attenuated
anti-apoptotic activity. This study reveals that import of Mcl-1 into the
mitochondrial matrix results in the attenuation of Mcl-1's anti-apoptotic
function.
