# Mutational analysis of PMP22, MPZ, GJB1, EGR2 and NEFL in Korean Charcot-Marie-Tooth neuropathy patients.
**Authors:** Choi BO, Lee MS, Shin SH, Hwang JH, Choi KG, Kim WK, Sunwoo IN, Kim NK, Chung KW
**Journal:** Hum Mutat (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1002/humu.9261](https://doi.org/10.1002/humu.9261)

## Abstract

1. Hum Mutat. 2004 Aug;24(2):185-6. doi: 10.1002/humu.9261.

Mutational analysis of PMP22, MPZ, GJB1, EGR2 and NEFL in Korean
Charcot-Marie-Tooth neuropathy patients.

Choi BO(1), Lee MS, Shin SH, Hwang JH, Choi KG, Kim WK, Sunwoo IN, Kim NK, Chung
KW.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology and Ewha Medical Research Center, Ewha Womans
University College of Medicine, Seoul 110-783, Korea.

Erratum in
    Hum Mutat. 2004 Oct;24(4):350.

We examined CMT1A duplication of 17p11.2-p12, mutations of PMP22, MPZ (P0), GJB1
(Cx32), EGR2 and NEFL genes in 57 Korean families with patients diagnosed as
having Charcot-Marie-Tooth (CMT) disease. The CMT1A duplication was present in
53.6% of 28 CMT type 1 patients. In the 42 CMT families without CMT1A
duplication, 10 pathogenic mutations were found in 9 families. The 10 mutations
were not detected in 105 healthy controls. Seven mutations (c.318delT
(p.Ala106fs) in PMP22, c.352G>A (p.Asp118Asn), c.449-1G>T (3'-splice site),
c.706A>G (p.Lys236Glu) in MPZ, c.407T>C (p.Val136Ala)[corrected], c.502T>C
(p.Cys168Arg) in GJB1, and c.1001T>C (p.Leu334Pro) in NEFL) were determined to
be novel. The mutation frequencies of PMP22 and MPZ were similar to those found
in several European populations, however, it appeared that mutations in GJB1 are
less frequent in East Asian CMT patients than in Eur opean patients. We
described the identified mutations and phenotype-genotype correlations based on
nerve conduction studies.
