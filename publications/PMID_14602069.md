# Thicker than blood: conserved mechanisms in Drosophila and vertebrate hematopoiesis.
**Authors:** Evans CJ, Hartenstein V, Banerjee U
**Journal:** Dev Cell (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1534-5807(03)00335-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1534-5807(03)00335-6)

## Abstract

1. Dev Cell. 2003 Nov;5(5):673-90. doi: 10.1016/s1534-5807(03)00335-6.

Thicker than blood: conserved mechanisms in Drosophila and vertebrate
hematopoiesis.

Evans CJ(1), Hartenstein V, Banerjee U.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular, Cell, and Developmental Biology, University of
California, Los Angeles, Los Angeles, CA 90095, USA.

Blood development in Drosophila melanogaster shares several interesting features
with hematopoiesis in vertebrates, including spatiotemporal regulation as well
as the use of similar transcriptional regulators and signaling pathways. In this
review, we describe what is known about hematopoietic development in Drosophila
and the various cell types generated and their functions. Additionally, the
molecular genetic mechanisms of hematopoietic cell fate determination and
commitment within Drosophila blood cell lineages are discussed and compared to
vertebrate mechanisms.
