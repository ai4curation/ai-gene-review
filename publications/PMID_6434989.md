# Information for the dorsal--ventral pattern of the Drosophila embryo is stored as maternal mRNA.
**Authors:** Anderson KV, Nüsslein-Volhard C
**Journal:** Nature (1984)
**DOI:** [10.1038/311223a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/311223a0)

## Abstract

1. Nature. 1984 Sep 20-26;311(5983):223-7. doi: 10.1038/311223a0.

Information for the dorsal--ventral pattern of the Drosophila embryo is stored
as maternal mRNA.

Anderson KV, Nüsslein-Volhard C.

Maternal-effect mutations in 10 loci in Drosophila produce totally 'dorsalized'
embryos. Injection of RNA isolated from wild-type embryos into mutants at six
loci partially restores dorsal-ventral polarity. For the mutant snake, injection
of poly(A)+ RNA restores a complete dorsal-ventral pattern.
