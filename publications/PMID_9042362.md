# Cloning and sequencing of trehalose synthase gene from Thermus aquaticus ATCC33923.

## Abstract

Cloning and sequencing of trehalose synthase gene from Thermus aquaticus
ATCC33923.

Tsusaki K(1), Nishimoto T, Nakada T, Kubota M, Chaen H, Fukuda S, Sugimoto T,
Kurimoto M.

Author information:
(1)Amase Institute, Hayashibara Biochemical Laboratories Inc., Okayama, Japan.

The gene encoding trehalose synthase (catalyzing the conversion of maltose into
alpha, alpha-trehalose by transglucosylation) was cloned from Thermus aquaticus
ATCC33923. Sequence analysis revealed a 2892 bp synthase gene and a 963 residue
amino-acid sequence. The 547 N-terminal residues were homologous to the
full-length synthase from Pimelobacter sp. R48 (53.8% identity).
