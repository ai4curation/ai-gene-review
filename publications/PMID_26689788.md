# A nitrous acid biosynthetic pathway for diazo group formation in bacteria.
**Authors:** Sugai Y, Katsuyama Y, Ohnishi Y
**Journal:** Nat Chem Biol (2016)
**DOI:** [10.1038/nchembio.1991](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchembio.1991)

## Abstract

1. Nat Chem Biol. 2016 Feb;12(2):73-5. doi: 10.1038/nchembio.1991. Epub 2015 Dec
21.

A nitrous acid biosynthetic pathway for diazo group formation in bacteria.

Sugai Y(1), Katsuyama Y(1), Ohnishi Y(1).

Author information:
(1)Department of Biotechnology, Graduate School of Agricultural and Life
Sciences, The University of Tokyo, Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo, Japan.

Although some diazo compounds have bioactivities of medicinal interest, little
is known about diazo group formation in nature. Here we describe an
unprecedented nitrous acid biosynthetic pathway responsible for the formation of
a diazo group in the biosynthesis of the ortho-diazoquinone secondary metabolite
cremeomycin in Streptomyces cremeus. This finding provides important insights
into the biosynthetic pathways not only for diazo compounds but also for other
naturally occurring compounds containing nitrogen-nitrogen bonds.
