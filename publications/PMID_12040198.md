# Control of stomatal distribution on the Arabidopsis leaf surface.
**Authors:** Nadeau JA, Sack FD

## Abstract

1. Science. 2002 May 31;296(5573):1697-700. doi: 10.1126/science.1069596.

Control of stomatal distribution on the Arabidopsis leaf surface.

Nadeau JA(1), Sack FD.

Author information:
(1)Department of Plant Biology, Ohio State University, 1735 Neil Avenue,
Columbus, OH 43210, USA.

Stomata regulate gas exchange and are distributed across the leaf epidermis with
characteristic spacing. Arabidopsis stomata are produced by asymmetric cell
divisions. Mutations in the gene TOO MANY MOUTHS (TMM) disrupt patterning by
randomizing the plane of formative asymmetric divisions and by permitting
ectopic divisions. TMM encodes a leucine-rich repeat-containing receptor-like
protein expressed in proliferative postprotodermal cells. TMM appears to
function in a position-dependent signaling pathway that controls the plane of
patterning divisions as well as the balance between stem cell renewal and
differentiation in stomatal and epidermal development.
