## Abstract

Cloning and sequencing of trehalose synthase gene from Pimelobacter sp. R48.

Tsusaki K(1), Nishimoto T, Nakada T, Kubota M, Chaen H, Sugimoto T, Kurimoto M.

Author information:
(1)Amase Institute, Hayashibara Biochemical Laboratories, Okayama, Japan.

The gene encoding trehalose synthase (catalyzing the conversion of maltose into
alpha, alpha-trehalose by intramolecular transglucosylation) was cloned from
Pimelobacter sp. R48. Sequence analysis revealed a 1719-bp synthase gene and a
573-residue amino-acid sequence. The 220 N-terminal residues were homologous to
those of maltases from Saccharomyces carlsbergensis and Aedes aegypti.
