# 4-hydroxycinnamoyl-CoA hydratase/lyase (HCHL)--An enzyme of phenylpropanoid chain cleavage from Pseudomonas.
**Authors:** Mitra A, Kitamura Y, Gasson MJ, Narbad A, Parr AJ, Payne J, Rhodes MJ, Sewter C, Walton NJ
**Journal:** Arch Biochem Biophys (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1006/abbi.1999.1140](https://doi.org/10.1006/abbi.1999.1140)

## Abstract

1. Arch Biochem Biophys. 1999 May 1;365(1):10-6. doi: 10.1006/abbi.1999.1140.

4-hydroxycinnamoyl-CoA hydratase/lyase (HCHL)--An enzyme of phenylpropanoid
chain cleavage from Pseudomonas.

Mitra A(1), Kitamura Y, Gasson MJ, Narbad A, Parr AJ, Payne J, Rhodes MJ, Sewter
C, Walton NJ.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Food Research-Norwich Laboratory, Norwich Research Park,
Norwich, Colney, NR4 7UA, United Kingdom.

The enzyme 4-hydroxycinnamoyl-CoA hydratase/lyase (HCHL), which catalyzes a
hydration and two-carbon cleavage step in the degradation of 4-hydroxycinnamic
acids, has been purified and characterized from Pseudomonas fluorescens strain
AN103. The enzyme is a homodimer and is active with three closely related
substrates, 4-coumaroyl-CoA, caffeoyl-CoA, and feruloyl-CoA (Km values: 5.2,
1.6, and 2.4 microM, respectively), but not with cinnamoyl-CoA or with
sinapinoyl-CoA. The abundance of the enzyme reflects a low catalytic center
activity (2.3 molecules s-1 at 30 degrees C; 4-coumaroyl-CoA as substrate).
