## Abstract

1. Nucleic Acids Res. 1986 Dec 22;14(24):10001-8. doi: 10.1093/nar/14.24.10001.

Nucleotide sequence of Bacillus phage phi 29 genes 14 and 15: homology of gene
15 with other phage lysozymes.

Garvey KJ, Saedi MS, Ito J.

The nucleotide sequence of Bacillus phage phi 29 genes 14 (g14) and 15 (g15)
have been determined and shown to encode proteins with molecular weights of
15,014 and 28,022, respectively. The g14 open reading frame (ORF) was confirmed
by sequencing a sus14(1241) mutant. Gene product 15 (gp15) has considerable
homology with Salmonella phage P22 lysozyme and lesser homology with Escherichia
coli phage T4 lysozyme. Putative translation signals are identified. In
addition, the role of a previously described promoter, B2, is discussed.
