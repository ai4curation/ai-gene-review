# Functional and antigenic properties of complement receptor type 2, CR2.
**Authors:** Mitomo K, Fujita T, Iida K

**DOI:** [10.1084/jem.165.5.1424](https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.165.5.1424)

## Abstract

1. J Exp Med. 1987 May 1;165(5):1424-9. doi: 10.1084/jem.165.5.1424.

Functional and antigenic properties of complement receptor type 2, CR2.

Mitomo K, Fujita T, Iida K.

This is the first report demonstrating that C3d receptor (CR2) has functional
activity in regulating complement cascade. Purified CR2 was examined for its
cofactor activity in factor I-mediated cleavage of membrane-bound iC3b. CR2 plus
C3b inactivator (I) released C3c from EA 125I-iC3b, and the release was
inhibited when CR2 was preincubated with OKB7 monoclonal anti-CR2. Furthermore,
immunoelectroblotting analysis showed crossreactivity of CR2 with 57H anti-CR1.
These results indicate that CR2 has functional and antigenic similarity to CR1,
thus providing a supporting evidence for placement of CR2 as a member of the
recently defined gene family of C3- and C4-regulatory proteins composed of CR1,
C4-binding protein, and factor H.
