# Yeast Ataxin-7 links histone deubiquitination with gene gating and mRNA export.
**Authors:** Köhler A, Schneider M, Cabal GG, Nehrbass U, Hurt E
**Journal:** Nat Cell Biol (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1038/ncb1733](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb1733)

## Abstract

1. Nat Cell Biol. 2008 Jun;10(6):707-15. doi: 10.1038/ncb1733. Epub 2008 May 18.

Yeast Ataxin-7 links histone deubiquitination with gene gating and mRNA export.

Köhler A(1), Schneider M, Cabal GG, Nehrbass U, Hurt E.

Author information:
(1)Biochemie-Zentrum der Universität Heidelberg, Im Neuenheimer Feld 328, 69120
Heidelberg, Germany.

Targeting of a gene to the nuclear pore complexes (NPCs), known as gene gating,
can affect its transcriptional state. However, the mechanism underlying gene
gating is poorly understood. Here, we have identified SAGA-associated Sgf73
(ref. 10), the yeast orthologue of human Ataxin-7 (ref. 11), as a regulator of
histone H2B ubiquitin levels, a modification linked to both transcription
initiation and elongation. Sgf73 is a key component of a minimal
histone-deubiquitinating complex. Activation of the H2B deubiquitinating
protease, Ubp8, is cooperative and requires complex formation with the
amino-terminal zinc-finger-containing domain of Sgf73 and Sgf11-Sus1. Through a
separate domain, Sgf73 mediates recruitment of the TREX-2 mRNA export factors
Sac3 and Thp1 to SAGA and their stable interaction with Sus1-Cdc31. This latter
step is crucial to target TREX-2 to the NPC. Loss of Sgf73 from SAGA abrogates
gene gating of GAL1 and causes a GAL1 mRNA export defect. Thus, Sgf73 provides a
molecular scaffold to integrate the regulation of H2B ubiquitin levels,
tethering of a gene to the NPC and export of mRNA.
