# Phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate 5-kinase Its3 and calcineurin Ppb1 coordinately regulate cytokinesis in fission yeast.
**Authors:** Zhang Y, Sugiura R, Lu Y, Asami M, Maeda T, Itoh T, Takenawa T, Shuntoh H, Kuno T
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M005575200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M005575200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2000 Nov 10;275(45):35600-6. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M005575200.

Phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate 5-kinase Its3 and calcineurin Ppb1 coordinately
regulate cytokinesis in fission yeast.

Zhang Y(1), Sugiura R, Lu Y, Asami M, Maeda T, Itoh T, Takenawa T, Shuntoh H,
Kuno T.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmacology, Kobe University School of Medicine, Kobe
650-0017, Japan.

The ppb1(+) gene encodes a fission yeast homologue of the mammalian calcineurin.
We have recently shown that Ppb1 is essential for chloride ion homeostasis, and
acts antagonistically with Pmk1 mitogen-activated protein kinase pathway. In an
attempt to identify genes that share an essential function with calcineurin, we
screened for mutations that confer sensitivity to the calcineurin inhibitor
FK506 and high temperature, and isolated a mutant, its3-1. its3(+) was shown to
be an essential gene encoding a functional homologue of
phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate 5-kinase (PI(4)P5K). The temperature upshift or
addition of FK506 induced marked disorganization of actin patches and dramatic
increase in the frequency of septation in the its3-1 mutants but not in the
wild-type cells. Expression of a green fluorescent protein-tagged Its3 and the
phospholipase Cdelta pleckstrin homology domain indicated plasma membrane
localization of PI(4)P5K and phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate. These green
fluorescent protein-tagged proteins were concentrated at the septum of dividing
cells, and the mutant Its3 was no longer localized to the plasma membrane. These
data suggest that fission yeast PI(4)P5K Its3 functions coordinately with
calcineurin and plays a key role in cytokinesis, and that the plasma membrane
localization of Its3 is the crucial event in cytokinesis.
