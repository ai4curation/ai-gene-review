Comment in
    Science. 1997 Jul 25;277(5325):534. doi: 10.1126/science.277.5325.534.

Protein kinase B (PKB) is a proto-oncogene that is activated in signaling
pathways initiated by phosphoinositide 3-kinase. Chromatographic separation of
brain cytosol revealed a kinase activity that phosphorylated and activated PKB
only in the presence of phosphatidylinositol-3,4,5-trisphosphate
[PtdIns(3,4,5)P3]. Phosphorylation occurred exclusively on threonine-308, a
residue implicated in activation of PKB in vivo. PtdIns(3,4,5)P3 was determined
to have a dual role: Its binding to the pleckstrin homology domain of PKB was
required to allow phosphorylation by the upstream kinase and it directly
activated the upstream kinase.
