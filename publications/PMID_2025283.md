# The primary structure of human H-protein of the glycine cleavage system deduced by cDNA cloning.
**Authors:** Fujiwara K, Okamura-Ikeda K, Hayasaka K, Motokawa Y
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0006-291x(05)80242-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0006-291x(05)80242-6)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1991 Apr 30;176(2):711-6. doi:
10.1016/s0006-291x(05)80242-6.

The primary structure of human H-protein of the glycine cleavage system deduced
by cDNA cloning.

Fujiwara K(1), Okamura-Ikeda K, Hayasaka K, Motokawa Y.

Author information:
(1)Institute for Enzyme Research, University of Tokushima, Japan.

A full-length cDNA encoding the human H-protein of the glycine cleavage system
has been isolated from a lambda gt11 human fetal liver cDNA library. The cDNA
insert was 1091 base pairs with an open reading frame of 519 base pairs which
encoded a 125-amino acid mature human H-protein with a 48-amino acid
presequence. Human H-protein is 97%, 86%, and 46% identical to the bovine,
chicken, and pea H-protein, respectively.
