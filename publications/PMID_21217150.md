# Mode of DNA binding by SopA protein of Escherichia coli F plasmid.
**Authors:** Komai M, Umino M, Hanai R
**Journal:** J Biochem (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1093/jb/mvq151](https://doi.org/10.1093/jb/mvq151)

## Abstract

1. J Biochem. 2011 Apr;149(4):455-61. doi: 10.1093/jb/mvq151. Epub 2011 Jan 8.

Mode of DNA binding by SopA protein of Escherichia coli F plasmid.

Komai M(1), Umino M, Hanai R.

Author information:
(1)Department of Life Science, College of Science, Rikkyo University, 3-34-1
Nishi-Ikebukuro, Toshima-ku, Tokyo 171-8501, Japan.

The binding of SopA to the promoter region of its own gene, in which four copies
of SopA's recognition sequence, 5'-CTTTGC-3', are arrayed asymmetrically, was
examined in vitro. Titration using electrophoretic mobility shift assay showed
that the stoichiometry of SopA protomers to the promoter-region DNA is 4 and
that the binding is highly co-operative. The co-operativity was corroborated by
EMSA and DNase I footprinting for a number of mutant DNA fragments in which
5'-CTTTGC-3' was changed to 5'-CTTACG-3'. EMSA in the style of circular
permutation showed that SopA bends DNA. Mutation at either outermost binding
site had a different effect on DNA bending by SopA, reflecting the asymmetry in
the arrangement of the binding sites, for which the results of DNase I
footprinting were in agreement. Gel filtration chromatography and analytical
ultracentrifugation of free SopA showed that the protein can exist as a monomer
and oligomers in the absence of ATP. Hence, the results indicate that the
co-operativity in SopA's DNA binding is based on its intrinsic protein-protein
interaction modified by DNA interaction.
