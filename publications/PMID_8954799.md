# Molecular cloning, tissue distribution, and chromosomal localization of human cationic amino acid transporter 2 (HCAT2).
**Authors:** Hoshide R, Ikeda Y, Karashima S, Matsuura T, Komaki S, Kishino T, Niikawa N, Endo F, Matsuda I
**Journal:** Genomics (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1006/geno.1996.0613](https://doi.org/10.1006/geno.1996.0613)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 1996 Dec 1;38(2):174-8. doi: 10.1006/geno.1996.0613.

Molecular cloning, tissue distribution, and chromosomal localization of human
cationic amino acid transporter 2 (HCAT2).

Hoshide R(1), Ikeda Y, Karashima S, Matsuura T, Komaki S, Kishino T, Niikawa N,
Endo F, Matsuda I.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics, Kumamoto University School of Medicine, Japan.

Human cationic amino acid transporter 2 (HCAT2) was isolated from a human
intestine cDNA library. The nucleotide sequence of the coding region predicts a
658-amino-acid protein with a calculated molecular weight of 71,669. As 91% of
the residues are identical with those of the mouse cationic amino acid
transporter 2 (MCAT2), HCAT2 seems to be a human counterpart of MCAT2. We found
no isoform as was present in MCAT2. In Northern blot analysis, a single (9.0 kb)
HCAT2 mRNA transcript was present in various human tissues. The highest level of
expression was observed in skeletal muscle and the lowest level in the kidney.
Hydropathy plots indicated that the translated protein is predicted to have 14
transmembrane domains with three potential N-glycosylation sites. Two patients
with lysinuric protein intolerance (MIM No. 222700) were analyzed for HCAT2 cDNA
but no mutation was detected. The HCAT2 gene was assigned to human chromosome
8p21.3-p22.
