Disruption of the CAR1 gene encoding arginase enhances freeze tolerance of the

Author information:
(1)National Food Research Institute, Tsukuba, Ibaraki 305-8642, Japan.
shimaj@nfri.affrc.go.jp

The effect of intracellular charged amino acids on freeze tolerance in dough was
determined by constructing homozygous diploid arginase-deficient mutants of
commercial baker's yeast. An arginase mutant accumulated higher levels of
arginine and/or glutamate and showed increased leavening ability during the
frozen-dough baking process, suggesting that disruption of the CAR1 gene
enhances freeze tolerance.
