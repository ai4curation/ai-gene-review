# Rat TRH receptor type 2 exhibits higher basal signaling activity than TRH receptor type 1.
**Authors:** Wang W, Gershengorn MC
**Journal:** Endocrinology (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1210/endo.140.10.7159](https://doi.org/10.1210/endo.140.10.7159)

## Abstract

1. Endocrinology. 1999 Oct;140(10):4916-9. doi: 10.1210/endo.140.10.7159.

Rat TRH receptor type 2 exhibits higher basal signaling activity than TRH
receptor type 1.

Wang W(1), Gershengorn MC.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medicine, Weill Medical College of Cornell University, New
York, NY 10021-4896, USA.

Two types of rat TRH receptor (TRH-R1 and TRH-R2) have been identified and shown
previously to exhibit similar binding and stimulated signaling activity via the
phosphoinositide-calcium transduction pathway. Since mouse TRH-R1 exhibits basal
(or constitutive or ligand-independent) signaling activity, we compared basal
signaling by TRH-R1 and TRH-R2. Basal signaling was measured as
receptor-mediated reporter gene induction via different transcription factors.
We found that TRH-R2 exhibited higher basal signaling activity than TRH-R1 via
pathways mediated by transcription factors AP-1, Elk-1 and CREB. Furthermore,
CREB-mediated transcription was directly dependent on the level of TRH-R2
expression and was inhibited by midazolam, a specific inverse agonist of basal
TRH-R signaling. Since TRH-R1 and TRH-R2 exhibit distinct anatomic distributions
in the rat, it is possible that TRH ligand-independent signaling is more
important in tissues/cells in which TRH-R2 is expressed and less important in
tissues in which TRH-R1 is found.
