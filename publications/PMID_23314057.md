Comment in
    Nat Med. 2013 Feb;19(2):133-4. doi: 10.1038/nm.3071.

Left ventricular noncompaction (LVNC) causes prominent ventricular
trabeculations and reduces cardiac systolic function. The clinical presentation
of LVNC ranges from asymptomatic to heart failure. We show that germline
mutations in human MIB1 (mindbomb homolog 1), which encodes an E3 ubiquitin
ligase that promotes endocytosis of the NOTCH ligands DELTA and JAGGED, cause
LVNC in autosomal-dominant pedigrees, with affected individuals showing reduced
NOTCH1 activity and reduced expression of target genes. Functional studies in
cells and zebrafish embryos and in silico modeling indicate that MIB1 functions
as a dimer, which is disrupted by the human mutations. Targeted inactivation of
Mib1 in mouse myocardium causes LVNC, a phenotype mimicked by inactivation of
myocardial Jagged1 or endocardial Notch1. Myocardial Mib1 mutants show reduced
ventricular Notch1 activity, expansion of compact myocardium to proliferative,
immature trabeculae and abnormal expression of cardiac development and disease
genes. These results implicate NOTCH signaling in LVNC and indicate that MIB1
mutations arrest chamber myocardium development, preventing trabecular
maturation and compaction.
