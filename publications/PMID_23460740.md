# Novel function of Trim44 promotes an antiviral response by stabilizing VISA.
**Authors:** Yang B, Wang J, Wang Y, Zhou H, Wu X, Tian Z, Sun B
**Journal:** J Immunol (2013)
**DOI:** [10.4049/jimmunol.1202507](https://doi.org/10.4049/jimmunol.1202507)

## Abstract

1. J Immunol. 2013 Apr 1;190(7):3613-9. doi: 10.4049/jimmunol.1202507. Epub 2013
Mar 4.

Novel function of Trim44 promotes an antiviral response by stabilizing VISA.

Yang B(1), Wang J, Wang Y, Zhou H, Wu X, Tian Z, Sun B.

Author information:
(1)School of Life Sciences, University of Science and Technology of China,
Hefei, Anhui 230026, China.

Virus-induced signaling adaptor (VISA) functions as a critical adaptor in the
regulation of both the production of type I IFNs and the subsequent control of
the innate antiviral response. In this study, we demonstrate that tripartite
motif (Trim)44 interacts with VISA and promotes VISA-mediated antiviral
responses. The overexpression of Trim44 enhances the cellular response to viral
infection, whereas Trim44 knockdown yields the opposite effect. Trim44
stabilizes VISA by preventing VISA ubiquitination and degradation. These
findings suggest that Trim44 functions as a positive regulator of the
virus-triggered immune response by enhancing the stability of VISA.
