**Authors:** Henry RW, Mittal V, Ma B, Kobayashi R, Hernandez N
**Journal:** Genes Dev (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1101/gad.12.17.2664](https://doi.org/10.1101/gad.12.17.2664)

## Abstract

1. Genes Dev. 1998 Sep 1;12(17):2664-72. doi: 10.1101/gad.12.17.2664.

SNAP19 mediates the assembly of a functional core promoter complex (SNAPc)
shared by RNA polymerases II and III.

Henry RW(1), Mittal V, Ma B, Kobayashi R, Hernandez N.

Author information:
(1)Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory, Cold Spring Harbor, New York 11724, USA.

The basal transcription factor SNAPc binds to the PSE, a core element in the RNA
polymerase II and III human snRNA promoters. SNAPc contains at least four
subunits, but it has not been possible to assemble a fully defined recombinant
SNAPc. Here we reconstitute SNAPc from five recombinant subunits, SNAP43,
SNAP45, SNAP50, SNAP190, and a newly identified subunit, SNAP19. This
recombinant complex binds specifically to the PSE and directs both RNA
polymerase II and III snRNA gene transcription. Thus, the same core SNAPc
nucleates the assembly of two classes of initiation complexes.
