# cDNA cloning of a new low-density lipoprotein receptor-related protein and mapping of its gene (LRP3) to chromosome bands 19q12-q13. 2.
**Authors:** Ishii H, Kim DH, Fujita T, Endo Y, Saeki S, Yamamoto TT
**Journal:** Genomics (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1006/geno.1998.5339](https://doi.org/10.1006/geno.1998.5339)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 1998 Jul 1;51(1):132-5. doi: 10.1006/geno.1998.5339.

cDNA cloning of a new low-density lipoprotein receptor-related protein and
mapping of its gene (LRP3) to chromosome bands 19q12-q13. 2.

Ishii H(1), Kim DH, Fujita T, Endo Y, Saeki S, Yamamoto TT.

Author information:
(1)Tohoku University Gene Research Center, Sendai, 981-8555, Japan.

We report herein the cDNA cloning of a novel member of the low-density
lipoprotein receptor (LDLR) family termed LDLR-related protein 3 (LRP3). Human
and rat LRP3 cDNAs encode a 770-amino-acid type 1 membrane protein with the
following regions: a putative signal sequence, two
isoleucine/leucine/valine-rich regions with an RGD sequence, two ligand-binding
repeat regions, a putative transmembrane region, and a proline-rich cytoplasmic
region with a tyrosine-based internalization signal. Fluorescence in situ
hybridization of human chromosomes revealed that the human LRP3 gene mapped to
chromosome bands 19q12-q13.2. LRP3 transcript was detected in a wide range of
human tissues, with the highest expression in skeletal muscle and ovary. Despite
the presence of the ligand-binding repeats, CHO cells transfected with LRP3 did
not bind beta-migrating very-low-density lipoprotein or receptor-associated
protein.
