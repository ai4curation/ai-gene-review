# Protein kinase Cdelta binds TIRAP/Mal to participate in TLR signaling.
**Authors:** Kubo-Murai M, Hazeki K, Sukenobu N, Yoshikawa K, Nigorikawa K, Inoue K, Yamamoto T, Matsumoto M, Seya T, Inoue N, Hazeki O

Protein kinase Cdelta binds TIRAP/Mal to participate in TLR signaling.

Kubo-Murai M(1), Hazeki K, Sukenobu N, Yoshikawa K, Nigorikawa K, Inoue K,
Yamamoto T, Matsumoto M, Seya T, Inoue N, Hazeki O.

Author information:
(1)The Division of Molecular Medical Science, Graduate School of Biomedical

Toll-like receptor (TLR) family members recognize specific molecular patterns
within pathogens. Signaling through TLRs results in a proximal event that
involves direct binding of adaptor proteins to the receptors. We observed that
TIRAP/Mal, an adaptor protein for TLR2 and TLR4, binds protein kinase Cdelta
(PKCdelta). TIRAP/Mal GST-fusion protein and a TIRAP/Mal antibody were able to
precipitate PKCdelta from rat peritoneal macrophage and THP1 cell lysates.
Truncation mutants of TIRAP/Mal showed that the TIR domain of TIRAP/Mal is
responsible for binding. TLR2- and TLR4-mediated phosphorylation of p38 MAPK,
IKK, and IkappaB in RAW264.7 cells were abolished by depletion of PKCdelta.
These results suggest that PKCdelta binding to TIRAP/Mal promotes TLR signaling
events.
