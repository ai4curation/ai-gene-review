We discovered that the wrong data were used for the left graph in Figure 4A, resulting in the left graphs of Figure 4A and 4C to show the same data. This error has been corrected by replacing the plot in Figure 4A with the correct plot showing the data for lincRNA mutants. We have also improved the right graph of Figure 4C by cutting the X axis to better represent the data range. The article has been corrected accordingly, and these corrections do not in any way change our conclusions.
