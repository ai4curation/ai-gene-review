# Molecular basis of mammalian sexual determination: activation of Müllerian inhibiting substance gene expression by SRY.
**Authors:** Haqq CM, King CY, Ukiyama E, Falsafi S, Haqq TN, Donahoe PK, Weiss MA

## Abstract

1. Science. 1994 Dec 2;266(5190):1494-500. doi: 10.1126/science.7985018.

Molecular basis of mammalian sexual determination: activation of Müllerian

(1)Pediatric Surgical Research Laboratory, Massachusetts General Hospital,

Erratum in
    Science 1995 Jan 20;267(5196):317.

The pathway of male sexual development in mammals is initiated by SRY, a gene on
the short arm of the Y chromosome. Its expression in the differentiating gonadal
ridge directs testicular morphogenesis, characterized by elaboration of
Müllerian inhibiting substance (MIS) and testosterone. SRY and MIS each belong
to conserved gene families that function in the control of growth and
differentiation. Structural and biochemical studies of the DNA binding domain of
SRY (the HMG box) revealed a protein-DNA interaction consisting of partial side
chain intercalation into a widened minor groove. Functional studies of SRY in a
cell line from embryonic gonadal ridge demonstrated activation of a
gene-regulatory pathway leading to expression of MIS. SRY molecules containing
mutations associated with human sex reversal have altered structural
interactions with DNA and failed to induce transcription of MIS.
