# TAZ interacts with zonula occludens-1 and -2 proteins in a PDZ-1 dependent manner.
**Authors:** Remue E, Meerschaert K, Oka T, Boucherie C, Vandekerckhove J, Sudol M, Gettemans J
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2010.09.020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2010.09.020)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2010 Oct 8;584(19):4175-80. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2010.09.020.
Epub  2010 Sep 18.

TAZ interacts with zonula occludens-1 and -2 proteins in a PDZ-1 dependent
manner.

Remue E(1), Meerschaert K, Oka T, Boucherie C, Vandekerckhove J, Sudol M,
Gettemans J.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medical Protein Research, VIB, Gent, Belgium.

The transcriptional coactivator TAZ recognizes L/PPxY motifs in transcription
factors like Runx1/2 through its WW domain. We show that the first PDZ domain of
zona occludens-1 (ZO-1) and 2 (ZO-2) interacts with the carboxy-terminal PDZ
binding motif of TAZ. Deletion of this motif abrogates binding. ZO-2 colocalizes
with TAZ in the nucleus of MDCK cells and ZO-2 expression alters TAZ
localization in human embryonic kidney cells. Luciferase assays demonstrate ZO-2
inhibition of TAZ-mediated transactivation. We propose that zonula occludens is
a negative regulator of TAZ and suggest that selected tight junction proteins
control nuclear translocation and activity of TAZ.
