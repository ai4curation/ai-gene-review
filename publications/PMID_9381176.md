# Functional coherence of the human Y chromosome.
**Authors:** Lahn BT, Page DC
**Journal:** Science (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.278.5338.675](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.278.5338.675)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1997 Oct 24;278(5338):675-80. doi: 10.1126/science.278.5338.675.

Functional coherence of the human Y chromosome.

Lahn BT(1), Page DC.

Author information:
(1)Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Whitehead Institute, and Department of
Biology, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, 9 Cambridge Center, Cambridge,
MA 02142, USA.

A systematic search of the nonrecombining region of the human Y chromosome (NRY)
identified 12 novel genes or families, 10 with full-length complementary DNA
sequences. All 12 genes, and six of eight NRY genes or families previously
isolated by less systematic means, fell into two classes. Genes in the first
group were expressed in many organs; these housekeeping genes have X homologs
that escape X inactivation. The second group, consisting of Y-chromosomal gene
families expressed specifically in testes, may account for infertility among men
with Y deletions. The coherence of the NRY's gene content contrasts with the
apparently haphazard content of most eukaryotic chromosomes.
