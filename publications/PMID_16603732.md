# Identification of the MLL2 complex as a coactivator for estrogen receptor alpha.
**Authors:** Mo R, Rao SM, Zhu YJ
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M513245200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M513245200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2006 Jun 9;281(23):15714-20. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M513245200. Epub
2006  Apr 7.

Identification of the MLL2 complex as a coactivator for estrogen receptor alpha.

Mo R(1), Rao SM, Zhu YJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology, Feinberg School of Medicine, Northwestern
University, Chicago, Illinois 60611, USA.

A novel estrogen receptor (ER)alpha coactivator complex, the MLL2 complex, which
consists of MLL2, ASH2, RBQ3, and WDR5, was identified. ERalpha directly binds
to the MLL2 complex through two LXXLL motifs in a region of MLL2 near the C
terminus in a ligand-dependent manner. Disrupting the interaction between
ERalpha and the MLL2 complex with small interfering RNAs specific against MLL2
or an MLL2 fragment representing the interacting region with ERalpha
significantly inhibited the ERalpha transcription activity. The MLL2 complex was
recruited on promoters of ERalpha target genes along with ERalpha upon estrogen
stimulation. Inhibition of MLL2 expression decreased the estrogen-induced
expression of ERalpha target genes cathepsin D and to a lesser extent pS2. In
addition, MCF-7 cell growth was also inhibited by the depletion of MLL2. These
results demonstrate that the ERalpha signaling pathway is critically dependent
on its direct interaction with the MLL2 complex and suggest a central role for
the MLL2 complex in the growth of ERalpha-positive cancer cells.
