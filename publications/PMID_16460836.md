# Contextual utilization of enzymes in discrete AKAP79/150 signalling complexes.

## Abstract

1. Eur J Cell Biol. 2006 Jul;85(7):621-2. doi: 10.1016/j.ejcb.2006.01.004. Epub

Cellular function involves the concerted action of signal transduction enzymes.
Restriction of enzyme location contributes to the fidelity of each cellular
response. A kinase-anchoring proteins (AKAPs) target the cAMP-dependent protein
kinase and other signalling enzymes to defined subcellular locations. We have
developed a new strategy that combines RNA interference of the endogenous
protein and replacement with AKAP79/150 forms unable to anchor selected binding
partners. Using this approach we show that AKAP79/150 coordinates different
enzyme combinations to modulate the activity of two distinct neuronal ion
channels: AMPA-type glutamate receptors and M-type potassium channels.
Utilization of distinct enzyme combinations in this manner provides a means to
expand the repertoire of cellular events that the same AKAP modulates.
