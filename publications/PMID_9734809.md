# The polyphosphate kinase gene of Pseudomonas aeruginosa.
**Authors:** Ishige K, Kameda A, Noguchi T, Shiba T
**Journal:** DNA Res (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1093/dnares/5.3.157](https://doi.org/10.1093/dnares/5.3.157)

## Abstract

1. DNA Res. 1998 Jun 30;5(3):157-62. doi: 10.1093/dnares/5.3.157.

The polyphosphate kinase gene of Pseudomonas aeruginosa.

Ishige K(1), Kameda A, Noguchi T, Shiba T.

Author information:
(1)Biochemicals Division, YAMASA Corporation, Choshi, Chiba, Japan.
yamasabp@choshinet.or.jp

We have cloned and sequenced a gene encoding polyphosphate kinase (PPK) from
Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. The gene immediately follows the hemB gene encoding
porphobilinogen synthase responsible for heme synthesis. The predicted amino
acid sequence of P. aeruginosa PPK is similar to those of PPKs previously
characterized except that it possesses an extra stretch of 46 amino acids at its
N-terminus, which has significant similarity to the Ras-related protein ARA5 of
Arabidopsis thaliana. When P. aeruginosa PPK was overproduced in Escherichia
coli, ATP-dependent polyphosphate-synthesizing activity was drastically
enhanced, confirming that the protein is a PPK.
