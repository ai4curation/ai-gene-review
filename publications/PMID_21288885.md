# Two forms of ribosomal protein L2 of Escherichia coli that inhibit DnaA in DNA replication.
**Authors:** Chodavarapu S, Felczak MM, Kaguni JM
**Journal:** Nucleic Acids Res (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1093/nar/gkq1203](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkq1203)
**PMC:** [PMC3105425](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3105425/)

## Abstract

1. Nucleic Acids Res. 2011 May;39(10):4180-91. doi: 10.1093/nar/gkq1203. Epub
2011  Feb 2.

Two forms of ribosomal protein L2 of Escherichia coli that inhibit DnaA in DNA
replication.

Chodavarapu S(1), Felczak MM, Kaguni JM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, Michigan State University,
East Lansing, MI 48824-1319, USA.

We purified an inhibitor of oriC plasmid replication and determined that it is a
truncated form of ribosomal protein L2 evidently lacking 59 amino acid residues
from the C-terminal region encoded by rplB. We show that this truncated form of
L2 or mature L2 physically interacts with the N-terminal region of DnaA to
inhibit initiation from oriC by apparently interfering with DnaA oligomer
formation, and the subsequent assembly of the prepriming complex on an oriC
plasmid. Both forms of L2 also inhibit the unwinding of oriC by DnaA. These in
vitro results raise the possibility that one or both forms of L2 modulate DnaA
function in vivo to regulate the frequency of initiation.
