# β1-adrenergic receptor antagonists signal via PDE4 translocation.
**Authors:** Richter W, Mika D, Blanchard E, Day P, Conti M
**Journal:** EMBO Rep (2013)
**DOI:** [10.1038/embor.2013.4](https://doi.org/10.1038/embor.2013.4)

## Abstract

1. EMBO Rep. 2013 Mar 1;14(3):276-83. doi: 10.1038/embor.2013.4. Epub 2013 Feb 5.

β1-adrenergic receptor antagonists signal via PDE4 translocation.

Richter W(1), Mika D, Blanchard E, Day P, Conti M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Obstetrics, Gynecology and Reproductive Sciences, University of
California San Francisco, San Francisco, California 94143, USA.

It is generally assumed that antagonists of Gs-coupled receptors do not activate
cAMP signalling, because they do not stimulate cAMP production via
Gs-protein/adenylyl cyclase activation. Here, we report a new signalling pathway
whereby antagonists of β1-adrenergic receptors (β1ARs) increase cAMP levels
locally without stimulating cAMP production directly. Binding of antagonists
causes dissociation of a preformed complex between β1ARs and Type-4 cyclic
nucleotide phosphodiesterases (PDE4s). This reduces the local concentration of
cAMP-hydrolytic activity, thereby increasing submembrane cAMP and PKA activity.
Our study identifies receptor/PDE4 complex dissociation as a novel mechanism of
antagonist action that contributes to the pharmacological properties of β1AR
antagonists and might be shared by other receptor subtypes.
