# TES is a novel focal adhesion protein with a role in cell spreading.
**Authors:** Coutts AS, MacKenzie E, Griffith E, Black DM
**Journal:** J Cell Sci (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1242/jcs.00278](https://doi.org/10.1242/jcs.00278)

## Abstract

1. J Cell Sci. 2003 Mar 1;116(Pt 5):897-906. doi: 10.1242/jcs.00278.

TES is a novel focal adhesion protein with a role in cell spreading.

Coutts AS(1), MacKenzie E, Griffith E, Black DM.

Author information:
(1)Cancer Research UK Laboratories, Beatson Institute for Cancer Research,
Switchback Road, Bearsden, Glasgow, G61 1BD, UK.

Previously, we identified TES as a novel candidate tumour suppressor gene that
mapped to human chromosome 7q31.1. In this report we demonstrate that the TES
protein is localised at focal adhesions, actin stress fibres and areas of
cell-cell contact. TES has three C-terminal LIM domains that appear to be
important for focal adhesion targeting. Additionally, the N-terminal region is
important for targeting TES to actin stress fibres. Yeast two-hybrid and
biochemical analyses yielded interactions with several focal adhesion and/or
cytoskeletal proteins including mena, zyxin and talin. The fact that TES
localises to regions of cell adhesion suggests that it functions in events
related to cell motility and adhesion. In support of this, we demonstrate that
fibroblasts stably overexpressing TES have an increased ability to spread on
fibronectin.
