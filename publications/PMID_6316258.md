# Nucleotide sequence of thrC and of the transcription termination region of the threonine operon in Escherichia coli K12.
**Authors:** Parsot C, Cossart P, Saint-Girons I, Cohen GN
**Journal:** Nucleic Acids Res (1983)
**DOI:** [10.1093/nar/11.21.7331](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/11.21.7331)

## Abstract

1. Nucleic Acids Res. 1983 Nov 11;11(21):7331-45. doi: 10.1093/nar/11.21.7331.

Nucleotide sequence of thrC and of the transcription termination region of the
threonine operon in Escherichia coli K12.

Parsot C, Cossart P, Saint-Girons I, Cohen GN.

The entire threonine operon (thrABC) of Escherichia coli K12 was cloned, and the
nucleotide sequence of the thrC gene and its 3' flanking region was determined.
The translation initiation codon was identified by sequencing the N-terminal
part of threonine synthase, the thrC gene product. Analysis of the deduced
protein sequence (428 amino acid residues) revealed a region of homology, 35
amino acids long, between the three enzymes encoded by the threonine operon.
During examination of the nucleotide sequence of the 1045 base pair fragments
following the thrC gene, we detected some potential rho-independent and
rho-dependent transcription termination signals.
