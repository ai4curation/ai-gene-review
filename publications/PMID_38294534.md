Acute promyelocytic leukemia (APL) is a specific subtype of acute myeloid
leukemia that is distinguished by the chromosomal translocation
t(15;17)(q24;q21), which leads to the fusion of the promyelocytic leukemia (PML)
gene with the retinoic acid receptor alpha (RARA). Recently, we identified a
novel fusion gene in APL, RARA::ankyrin repeat domain 34C (ANKRD34C), identified
its functions by morphological, cytogenetic, molecular biological and multiplex
fluorescence in situ hybridization analyses, and demonstrated the potential
therapeutic effect clinically and experimentally of all-trans retinoic acid
(ATRA); the findings have important implications for the diagnosis and treatment
of atypical APL.
