# Intragenic transcription of a noncoding RNA modulates expression of ASP3 in budding yeast.
**Authors:** Huang YC, Chen HT, Teng SC
**Journal:** RNA (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1261/rna.2177410](https://doi.org/10.1261/rna.2177410)
**PMC:** [PMC2957049](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2957049/)

## Abstract

1. RNA. 2010 Nov;16(11):2085-93. doi: 10.1261/rna.2177410. Epub 2010 Sep 3.

Intragenic transcription of a noncoding RNA modulates expression of ASP3 in
budding yeast.

Huang YC(1), Chen HT, Teng SC.

Author information:
(1)Department of Microbiology, College of Medicine, National Taiwan University,
Taipei 10051, Taiwan.

Inter- and intragenic noncoding transcription is widespread in eukaryotic
genomes; however, the purpose of these types of transcription is still poorly
understood. Here, we show that intragenic sense-oriented transcription within
the budding yeast ASP3 coding region regulates a constitutively and immediately
accessible promoter for the transcription of full-length ASP3. Expression of
this short intragenic transcript is independent of GATA transcription factors,
which are essential for the activation of full-length ASP3, and independent of
RNA polymerase II (RNAPII). Furthermore, we found that an intragenic control
element is required for the expression of this noncoding RNA (ncRNA). Continuous
expression of the short ncRNA maintains a high level of trimethylation of
histone H3 at lysine 4 (H3K4me3) at the ASP3 promoter and makes this region more
accessible for RNAPII to transcribe the full-length ASP3. Our results show for
the first time that intragenic noncoding transcription promotes gene expression.
