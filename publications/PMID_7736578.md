# CDC27Hs colocalizes with CDC16Hs to the centrosome and mitotic spindle and is essential for the metaphase to anaphase transition.
**Authors:** Tugendreich S, Tomkiel J, Earnshaw W, Hieter P
**Journal:** Cell (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0092-8674(95)90336-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0092-8674(95)90336-4)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 1995 Apr 21;81(2):261-8. doi: 10.1016/0092-8674(95)90336-4.

CDC27Hs colocalizes with CDC16Hs to the centrosome and mitotic spindle and is
essential for the metaphase to anaphase transition.

Tugendreich S(1), Tomkiel J, Earnshaw W, Hieter P.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Biology and Genetics, Johns Hopkins School of
Medicine, Baltimore, Maryland 21205, USA.

We have isolated cDNAs and raised antibodies corresponding to the human homologs
of the S. cerevisiae CDC27 and CDC16 proteins, which are tetratrico peptide
repeat (TPR)-containing proteins essential for mitosis in budding yeast. We find
that the CDC27Hs and CDC16Hs proteins colocalize to the centrosome at all stages
of the mammalian cell cycle, and to the mitotic spindle. Injection of
affinity-purified anti-CDC27Hs antibodies into logarithmically growing HeLa
cells causes a highly reproducible cell cycle arrest in metaphase with
apparently normal spindle structure. We conclude that CDC27 and CDC16 are
evolutionarily conserved components of the centrosome and mitotic spindle that
control the onset of postmetaphase events during mitosis.
