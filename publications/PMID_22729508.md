# Vanishing white matter disease: an Italian case with A638G mutation in exon 5 of EIF2B2 gene, an unusual early onset and a long course.
**Authors:** Sambati L, Agati R, Bacci A, Bianchi S, Capellari S
**Journal:** Neurol Sci (2013)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s10072-012-1129-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10072-012-1129-3)

## Abstract

1. Neurol Sci. 2013 Jul;34(7):1235-8. doi: 10.1007/s10072-012-1129-3. Epub 2012
Jun  23.

Vanishing white matter disease: an Italian case with A638G mutation in exon 5 of
EIF2B2 gene, an unusual early onset and a long course.

Sambati L, Agati R, Bacci A, Bianchi S, Capellari S.

We report the clinical description of an Italian patient with c.638A>G mutation
in exon 5 of EIF2B2 gene and a very slow progressive Vanishing White Matter
disease phenotype. Infact, in relation to her causative mutation, our patient
had an unusual early onset and long course. Furthermore, other than standard MRI
examination and spectroscopy study, we report DWI and ADC maps and FA maps
reconstruction from DTI in order to describe brain tissue degeneration in
vanishing white matter disease.
