Deregulation of the evolutionarily conserved Hippo pathway has been implicated
in abnormal development of animals and in several types of cancer. One mechanism
of Hippo pathway regulation is achieved by controlling the stability of its
regulatory components. However, the executive E3 ligases that are involved in
this process, and how the process is regulated, remain poorly defined. In this
study, we identify, through a genetic candidate screen, the SCF(Slmb) E3 ligase
as a novel negative regulator of the Hippo pathway in Drosophila imaginal
tissues via mediation of the degradation of Expanded (Ex). Mechanistic study
shows that Slmb-mediated degradation of Ex is inhibited by the Hippo signaling.
Considering the fact that Hippo signaling suppresses the transcription of ex, we
propose that the Hippo pathway employs a double security mechanism to ensure
fine-tuned homeostasis during development.
