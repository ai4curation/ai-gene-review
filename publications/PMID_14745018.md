# Estrogen receptor beta inhibits 17beta-estradiol-stimulated proliferation of the breast cancer cell line T47D.

Estrogen receptor beta inhibits 17beta-estradiol-stimulated proliferation of the

Ström A(1), Hartman J, Foster JS, Kietz S, Wimalasena J, Gustafsson JA.

Author information:

Erratum in
    Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 2006 May 23;103(21):8298.

Estrogen receptor (ER) beta counteracts the activity of ERalpha in many systems.
In agreement with this, we show in this study that induced expression of ERbeta
in the breast cancer cell line T47D reduces 17beta-estradiol-stimulated
proliferation when expression of ERbeta mRNA equals that of ERalpha. Induction
of ERbeta reduces growth of exponentially proliferating cells with a concomitant
decrease in components of the cell cycle associated with proliferation, namely
cyclin E, Cdc25A (a key regulator of Cdk2), p45(Skp2) (a key regulator of
p27(Kip1) proteolysis), and an increase in the Cdk inhibitor p27(Kip1). We also
observed a reduced Cdk2 activity. These findings suggest a possible role for
ERbeta in breast cancer and imply that ERbeta-specific ligands may reduce
proliferation of ER-positive breast cancer cells through actions on the G(1)
phase cell-cycle machinery.
