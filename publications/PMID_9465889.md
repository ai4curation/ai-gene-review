# Molecular cloning of CISH, chromosome assignment to 3p21.3, and analysis of expression in fetal and adult tissues.
**Authors:** Uchida K, Yoshimura A, Inazawa J, Yanagisawa K, Osada H, Masuda A, Saito T, Takahashi T, Miyajima A, Takahashi T
**Journal:** Cytogenet Cell Genet (1997)

## Abstract

1. Cytogenet Cell Genet. 1997;78(3-4):209-12. doi: 10.1159/000134658.

Molecular cloning of CISH, chromosome assignment to 3p21.3, and analysis of
expression in fetal and adult tissues.

Uchida K(1), Yoshimura A, Inazawa J, Yanagisawa K, Osada H, Masuda A, Saito T,
Takahashi T, Miyajima A, Takahashi T.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Ultrastructure Research, Aichi Cancer Center Research
Institute, Nagoya, Japan.

The murine cytokine inducible SH2-containing (Cis) protein gene (Cish) was
recently cloned and shown to have a growth inhibitory function. We have isolated
cDNAs coding for its human homologue (CISH) and assigned the gene to 3p21.3 by
fluorescence in situ hybridization. Northern blot analysis showed CISH
expression in various epithelial tissues including lung and kidney, in which
tumors frequently exhibit 3p21.3 deletions.
