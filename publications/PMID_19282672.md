# CDK5RAP2 is required for spindle checkpoint function.
**Authors:** Zhang X, Liu D, Lv S, Wang H, Zhong X, Liu B, Wang B, Liao J, Li J, Pfeifer GP, Xu X
**Journal:** Cell Cycle (2009)
**DOI:** [10.4161/cc.8.8.8205](https://doi.org/10.4161/cc.8.8.8205)

## Abstract

1. Cell Cycle. 2009 Apr 15;8(8):1206-16. doi: 10.4161/cc.8.8.8205. Epub 2009 Apr

CDK5RAP2 is required for spindle checkpoint function.

Zhang X(1), Liu D, Lv S, Wang H, Zhong X, Liu B, Wang B, Liao J, Li J, Pfeifer
GP, Xu X.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Cancer Biology, Capital Normal University College of Life

The combination of paclitaxel and doxorubicin is among the most successful
chemotherapy regimens in cancer treatment. CDK5RAP2, when mutated, causes
primary microcephaly. We show here that inhibition of CDK5RAP2 expression causes
chromosome mis-segregation, fails to maintain the spindle checkpoint, and is
associated with reduced expression of the spindle checkpoint proteins BUBR1 and
MAD2 and an increase in chromatin-associated CDC20. CDK5RAP2 resides on the
BUBR1 and MAD2 promoters and regulates their transcription. Furthermore,
CDK5RAP2-knockdown cells have increased resistance to paclitaxel and
doxorubicin, and this resistance is partially rescued upon restoration of
CDK5RAP2 expression. Cancer cells cultured in the presence of paclitaxel or
doxorubicin exhibit dramatically decreased CDK5RAP2 levels. These results
suggest that CDK5RAP2 is required for spindle checkpoint function and is a
common target in paclitaxel and doxorubicin resistance.
