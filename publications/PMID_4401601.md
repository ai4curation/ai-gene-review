## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1972 Apr;110(1):155-60. doi: 10.1128/jb.110.1.155-160.1972.

Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase from Escherichia coli and from a "high-level"
mutant.

Banerjee S, Fraenkel DG.

Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase has been purified to near homogeneity from
wild-type Escherichia coli K-12 and from a mutant previously found to contain
substantially more of the enzyme. The two enzymes are the same in all
characteristics studied thus far: specific activity, kinetics, specificity, and
subunit size.
