# Identification of hTLR10: a novel human Toll-like receptor preferentially expressed in immune cells.
**Authors:** Chuang T, Ulevitch RJ
**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0167-4781(00)00289-x](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0167-4781(00)00289-x)

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 2001 Mar 19;1518(1-2):157-61. doi:
10.1016/s0167-4781(00)00289-x.

Identification of hTLR10: a novel human Toll-like receptor preferentially
expressed in immune cells.

Chuang T(1), Ulevitch RJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Immunology, The Scripps Research Institute, 10550 North Torrey
Pines Road, 92037, La Jolla, CA, USA.

Herein we describe the isolation of a cDNA encoding a novel human Toll-like
receptor (hTLR) that we term hTLR10. Human TLR10 contains 811 amino acid
residues. Deduced amino acid sequence analysis reveals that like the other known
hTLRs (hTLR1-9) it is characterized by a signal peptide followed by multiple
leucine-rich repeats (LRRs), a cysteine-rich domain, a transmembrane sequence
and a cytoplasmic domain homologous to that of the human interleukin-1 receptor.
Phylogenetic analysis indicates that among all the hTLRs, hTLR10 is most closely
related to hTLR1 and hTLR6; the overall amino acid identity is 50% and 49%,
respectively. hTLR10 mRNA is most highly expressed in lymphoid tissues such as
spleen, lymph node, thymus, and tonsil. Expression analysis of cell lines
indicates a predominance in a variety of immune cell types. Thus, hTLR10 is
preferentially expressed in tissues and cells involved in immune responses.
