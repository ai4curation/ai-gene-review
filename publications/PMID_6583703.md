## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1984 Feb;81(4):1189-92. doi: 10.1073/pnas.81.4.1189.

X chromosome-linked muscular dystrophy (mdx) in the mouse.

Bulfield G, Siller WG, Wight PA, Moore KJ.

An X chromosome-linked mouse mutant (gene symbol, mdx) has been found that has
elevated plasma levels of muscle creatine kinase and pyruvate kinase and
exhibits histological lesions characteristic of muscular dystrophy. The mutants
show mild clinical symptoms and are viable and fertile. Linkage analysis with
four X chromosome loci indicates that mdx maps in the Hq Bpa region of the mouse
X chromosome. This gives a gene order of mdx-Tfm-Pgk-1-Ags, the same as for the
equivalent genes on the human X chromosome.
