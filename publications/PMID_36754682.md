Comment on
    Nature. 2022 Oct;610(7930):212-216. doi: 10.1038/s41586-022-05186-y.

In a recent study, Chaaban and Carter use cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) and
an innovative data-processing pipeline to determine the first high-resolution
structure of the dynein-dynactin-BICDR1 complex assembled on microtubules. The
structure of the complex reveals novel stoichiometry and provides new
mechanistic insight into dynein function and mechanism.
