# The N protein of bacteriophage lambda, defined by its DNA sequence, is highly basic.
**Authors:** Franklin NC, Bennett GN
**Journal:** Gene (1979)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0378-1119(79)90011-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-1119(79)90011-8)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 1979 Dec;8(1):107-19. doi: 10.1016/0378-1119(79)90011-8.

The N protein of bacteriophage lambda, defined by its DNA sequence, is highly
basic.

Franklin NC, Bennett GN.

Nucleotide sequence has been determined for the restriction fragments and cloned
DNA from the pL-N-tL1 region of bacteriophage lambda. A unique reading frame for
the N gene is defined by the absence of natural nonsense codons and by the
presence of seven nonsense codons generated by mutations in N. This reading
frame is initiated at two alternative ATG codons, the second of which is
probably the in vivo translation start. Reading is stopped at a single TAG
codon. The protein coded is therefore 133 or, more probably, 107 amino acids
long, rich in lysine, arginine and proline.
