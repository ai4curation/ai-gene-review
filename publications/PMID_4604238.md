## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1974 Aug;119(2):461-8. doi: 10.1128/jb.119.2.461-468.1974.

Clustering of the genes for allantoin degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Lawther RP, Riemer E, Chojnacki B, Cooper TG.

We have shown that allantoin degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae proceeds
exclusively through the intermediate formation of allantoic acid, urea, and
allophanic acid. The number of reactions between allantoic acid and urea,
however, remains obscure owing to our inability to isolate a mutant defective in
ureidoglycolate hydrolase. Structural genes for the enzymes, allantoinase (dal1)
and allantoicase (dal2) are located on chromosome IX promixal to the centromere
in the order dal1-dal2-lysl.
