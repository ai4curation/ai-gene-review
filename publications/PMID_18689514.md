## Abstract

1. Appl Environ Microbiol. 2008 Oct;74(19):6144-6. doi: 10.1128/AEM.02538-07.
Epub  2008 Aug 8.

Bioaccumulation of palladium by Desulfovibrio fructosivorans wild-type and
hydrogenase-deficient strains.

Mikheenko IP(1), Rousset M, Dementin S, Macaskie LE.

Author information:
(1)School of Biosciences, University of Birmingham, Edgbaston, Birmingham B15
2TT, England, United Kingdom.

Wild-type Desulfovibrio fructosivorans and three hydrogenase-negative mutants
reduced Pd(II) to Pd(0). The location of Pd(0) nanoparticles on the cytoplasmic
membrane of the mutant retaining only cytoplasmic membrane-bound hydrogenase was
strong evidence for the role of hydrogenases in Pd(0) deposition. Hydrogenase
activity was retained at acidic pH, shown previously to favor Pd(0) deposition.
