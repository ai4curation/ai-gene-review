Two modes of autoregulation of the murR repressor in Escherichia coli.

Ishii D(1), Ishihama A, Yamamoto K.

Author information:
(1)Department of Frontier Bioscience, Hosei University, Tokyo, Japan.

The murPQ operon of Escherichia coli encoding the enzymes for degradation of
MurNAc is derepressed by MurR (YfeT) in the presence of MurNAc-6-phosphate. MurR
also represses its own expression. We found that murR is stimulated in rich
medium. This activation involves MurR and most of the IIBC components of the
phosphotransferase system, suggesting auto-induction of murR by an activated
form of MurR.
