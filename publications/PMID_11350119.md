# Molecular cloning and mapping of the human nicotinic acetylcholine receptor alpha10 (CHRNA10).
**Authors:** Lustig LR, Peng H, Hiel H, Yamamoto T, Fuchs PA
**Journal:** Genomics (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1006/geno.2000.6503](https://doi.org/10.1006/geno.2000.6503)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 2001 May 1;73(3):272-83. doi: 10.1006/geno.2000.6503.

Molecular cloning and mapping of the human nicotinic acetylcholine receptor
alpha10 (CHRNA10).

Lustig LR(1), Peng H, Hiel H, Yamamoto T, Fuchs PA.

Author information:
(1)The Center for Hearing Sciences, Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore,
Maryland 21205, USA. llustig@bme.jhu.edu

We report the isolation and initial characterization of a new member of the
human nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (nAChR) subunit family, alpha10
(CHRNA10), from both inner-ear neuroepithelium and lymphoid tissue. The cDNA is
1959 nucleotides in length, with a coding region predicting a protein of 451
amino acids that is 90% identical to rat alpha10. The alpha10 gene was localized
to chromosome 11p15.5. Human alpha10 was detected in human inner-ear tissue,
tonsil, immortalized B-cells, cultured T-cells and peripheral blood lymphocytes
using reverse transcriptase-polymerase chain reaction, Northern blot
hybridization, and immunohistochemistry. We also detected the expression of the
human nAChR alpha9 (CHRNA9) mRNA in these same tissues using RT-PCR and Northern
blot hybridization.
