# MiD49 and MiD51, new components of the mitochondrial fission machinery.
**Authors:** Palmer CS, Osellame LD, Laine D, Koutsopoulos OS, Frazier AE, Ryan MT
**Journal:** EMBO Rep (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1038/embor.2011.54](https://doi.org/10.1038/embor.2011.54)
**PMC:** [PMC3128275](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3128275/)

## Abstract

1. EMBO Rep. 2011 Jun;12(6):565-73. doi: 10.1038/embor.2011.54. Epub 2011 Apr 21.

MiD49 and MiD51, new components of the mitochondrial fission machinery.

Palmer CS(1), Osellame LD, Laine D, Koutsopoulos OS, Frazier AE, Ryan MT.

Author information:
(1)La Trobe Institute for Molecular Science, La Trobe University, Plenty Road,
Melbourne 3086, Victoria, Australia.

Mitochondria form intricate networks through fission and fusion events. Here, we
identify mitochondrial dynamics proteins of 49 and 51 kDa (MiD49 and MiD51,
respectively) anchored in the mitochondrial outer membrane. MiD49/51 form foci
and rings around mitochondria similar to the fission mediator dynamin-related
protein 1 (Drp1). MiD49/51 directly recruit Drp1 to the mitochondrial surface,
whereas their knockdown reduces Drp1 association, leading to unopposed fusion.
Overexpression of MiD49/51 seems to sequester Drp1 from functioning at
mitochondria and cause fused tubules to associate with actin. Thus, MiD49/51 are
new mediators of mitochondrial division affecting Drp1 action at mitochondria.
