# Cloning of the fabF gene in an expression vector and in vitro characterization of recombinant fabF and fabB encoded enzymes from Escherichia coli.
**Authors:** Edwards P, Nelsen JS, Metz JG, Dehesh K
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(96)01437-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(96)01437-8)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1997 Jan 27;402(1):62-6. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(96)01437-8.

Cloning of the fabF gene in an expression vector and in vitro characterization
of recombinant fabF and fabB encoded enzymes from Escherichia coli.

Edwards P(1), Nelsen JS, Metz JG, Dehesh K.

Author information:
(1)Oils Division, Calgene Inc., Davis, CA 95616, USA.

Analysis of the beta-ketoacyl-ACP synthase (KAS) encoded by the fabF gene of
Escherichia coli has been hampered by a reported instability of the cloned gene.
Here we describe biochemical characterization of purified, active protein from
the recombinant fabF gene. This enzyme has the properties ascribed to KAS II and
not those of a putative KAS IV reported to be encoded by fabJ, a genomic clone
with DNA sequence identical to that of fabF. We also characterize active protein
from a recombinant fabB gene and suggest that this method may have a general
utility for analysis of KAS enzymes.
