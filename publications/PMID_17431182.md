# Structural basis for substrate delivery by acyl carrier protein in the yeast fatty acid synthase.
**Authors:** Leibundgut M, Jenni S, Frick C, Ban N
**Journal:** Science (2007)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.1138249](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1138249)

## Abstract

1. Science. 2007 Apr 13;316(5822):288-90. doi: 10.1126/science.1138249.

Structural basis for substrate delivery by acyl carrier protein in the yeast
fatty acid synthase.

Leibundgut M(1), Jenni S, Frick C, Ban N.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Molecular Biology and Biophysics, ETH Zurich, 8092 Zurich,
Switzerland.

In the multifunctional fungal fatty acid synthase (FAS), the acyl carrier
protein (ACP) domain shuttles reaction intermediates covalently attached to its
prosthetic phosphopantetheine group between the different enzymatic centers of
the reaction cycle. Here, we report the structure of the Saccharomyces
cerevisiae FAS determined at 3.1 angstrom resolution with its ACP stalled at the
active site of ketoacyl synthase. The ACP contacts the base of the reaction
chamber through conserved, charge-complementary surfaces, which optimally
position the ACP toward the catalytic cleft of ketoacyl synthase. The
conformation of the prosthetic group suggests a switchblade mechanism for acyl
chain delivery to the active site of the enzyme.
