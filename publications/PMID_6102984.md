## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1980 Apr;142(1):69-78. doi: 10.1128/jb.142.1.69-78.1980.

Purification of glutamine synthetase from a variety of bacteria.

Streicher SL, Tyler B.

We have developed two procedures which allow the very rapid purification of
glutamine synthetase (GS) from a diverse variety of bacteria. The first
procedure, based upon differential sedimentation, depends upon the association
of GS with deoxyribonucleic acid in cell extracts. The second procedure, derived
from the method of C. Gross et al (J. Bacteriol. 128:382-389, 1976) for
purifying ribonucleic acid polymerase by polyethylene glycol (PEG)
precipitation, enabled us to obtain high yields of GS from either small or large
quantities of cells. We used the PEG procedure to purify GS from Klebsiella
aerogenes, K. pneumoniae, Escherichia coli, Salmonella typhimurium, Rhizobium
sp. strain 32H1, R. meliloti, Azotobacter vinelandii, Pseudomonas putida,
Caulobacter crescentus, and Rhodopseudomonas capsulata. The purity of the GS
obtained, judged by sodium dodecyl sulfate-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis,
was high, and in many instances only a single protein band was detected.
