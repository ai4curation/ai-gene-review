# ARHGEF19 regulates MAPK/ERK signaling and promotes the progression of small cell lung cancer.

Small cell lung cancer (SCLC) is an aggressive lung tumor subtype with poor
survival. To identify genes that differentially expressed in SCLC with tumor
promotion activity as candidate therapeutic targets, we analyzed the expression
of 50 RhoGEFs family genes in published microarray data of SCLC and normal
tissues (Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) dataset GSE43346). We identified
ARHGEF19, a member of RhoGEFs family, as an overexpressed oncogene in SCLC.
ARHGEF19 is up-regulated in SCLC tissues and ranks first in RhoGEFs family
genes. Enforced ARHGEF19 expression promotes SCLC cell proliferation in vitro
and its knockdown decreases cell proliferation in vitro and in vivo. ARHGEF19-DH
and -PD domain interacts with HRAS and activates the MAPK/ERK pathway in SCLC
cells and SCLC xenografts. Our study presents evidences that ARHGEF19
overexpression promotes SCLC cell growth and activates the MAPK/ERK pathway.
These findings would shed light on the development of new therapeutics for SCLC
management.
