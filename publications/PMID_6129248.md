# Purification and properties of glutamine synthetase from Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
**Authors:** Mitchell AP, Magasanik B
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1983)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1983 Jan 10;258(1):119-24.

Purification and properties of glutamine synthetase from Saccharomyces
cerevisiae.

Mitchell AP, Magasanik B.

We have purified glutamine synthetase over 130-fold from Saccharomyces
cerevisiae. The enzyme exhibits a Km for glutamate of 6.3 mM and a Km for ATP of
1.3 mM in the biosynthetic reaction, with a pH optimum from 6.1 to 7.0. Ten to
twelve 43,000 molecular weight subunits comprise the active enzyme of 470,000
molecular weight. Rabbit antibodies prepared against the purified enzyme were
used to show that induction of enzyme activity correlates with de novo synthesis
of the enzyme subunit.
