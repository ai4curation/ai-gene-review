# PKR protein kinase is activated by hepatitis C virus and inhibits viral replication through translational control.
**Authors:** Kang JI, Kwon SN, Park SH, Kim YK, Choi SY, Kim JP, Ahn BY
**Journal:** Virus Res (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.virusres.2009.01.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.virusres.2009.01.007)

## Abstract

1. Virus Res. 2009 Jun;142(1-2):51-6. doi: 10.1016/j.virusres.2009.01.007. Epub
2009 Feb 2.

PKR protein kinase is activated by hepatitis C virus and inhibits viral
replication through translational control.

Kang JI(1), Kwon SN, Park SH, Kim YK, Choi SY, Kim JP, Ahn BY.

Author information:
(1)School of Life Sciences & Biotechnology, Korea University, 5-1 Anamdong,
Seoul 136-701, Republic of Korea.

Hepatitis C virus (HCV) infection is currently treated with IFNalpha-based
therapy but little is known how IFNalpha inhibits HCV replication. We show here
that HCV JFH1 infection of human hepatoma Huh-7 cells leads to the activation of
IFN-inducible protein kinase PKR and phosphorylation of the translation
initiation factor eIF2alpha. Compared to a control cell HCV replication was
significantly elevated in a PKR-knockdown cell, giving rise to a 10-fold higher
viral titer, and was less sensitive to IFNalpha treatment. Conversely, transient
expression of PKR inhibited HCV replication in a kinase-dependent manner with
concomitant increase of eIF2alpha phosphorylation. Further, expression of a
phospho-mimetic eIF2alpha mutant moderately inhibited HCV replication. Together,
these results demonstrate that PKR is activated by HCV infection and plays a
critical antiviral role through inhibition of viral protein translation.
