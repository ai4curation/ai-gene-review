## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1994 Dec;176(23):7140-7. doi: 10.1128/jb.176.23.7140-7147.1994.

Identification of FtsW and characterization of a new ftsW division mutant of
Escherichia coli.

Khattar MM(1), Begg KJ, Donachie WD.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Cell and Molecular Biology, University of Edinburgh, Scotland.

The product of the ftsW gene has been identified as a polypeptide that, like the
related RodA protein, shows anomalous mobility on sodium dodecyl
sulfate-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis. FtsW is produced at low levels that
can be increased by altering the translation initiation region of the mRNA.
Overproduction of FtsW strongly inhibits cell growth. A new mutant allele,
ftsW201, causes a temperature-dependent block in the initiation stage of cell
division which is similar to the division block in ftsZ mutants. The block in
initiation of division in the ftsW201 allele is shown to be independent of FtsZ
or the FtsZ inhibitor, SulA. In addition, the ftsW201 mutant is hypersensitive
to overproduction of the division initiation protein FtsZ at the permissive
temperature. Our results suggest a role for FtsW in an early stage of division
which may involve an interaction with FtsZ.
