# Galactosialidosis: simultaneous deficiency of esterase, carboxy-terminal deamidase and acid carboxypeptidase activities.
**Authors:** Kase R, Itoh K, Takiyama N, Oshima A, Sakuraba H, Suzuki Y
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0006-291x(90)91572-a](https://doi.org/10.1016/0006-291x(90)91572-a)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1990 Nov 15;172(3):1175-9. doi:
10.1016/0006-291x(90)91572-a.

Galactosialidosis: simultaneous deficiency of esterase, carboxy-terminal
deamidase and acid carboxypeptidase activities.

Kase R(1), Itoh K, Takiyama N, Oshima A, Sakuraba H, Suzuki Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Clinical Genetics, Tokyo Metropolitan Institute of Medical
Science, Japan.

Esterase and deamidase activities at pH 7.0 and carboxypeptidase activity at pH
5.7 were markedly low or deficient in seven galactosialidosis fibroblast strains
with deficient activity of "protective protein" for lysosomal beta-galactosidase
and neuraminidase. No simultaneous deficiency of these three enzyme activities
was observed in other lysosomal disease fibroblasts examined in this study. This
result strongly suggests that "protective protein" is identical with a
multifunctional protein with esterase/deamidase/carboxypeptidase activities and
its mutation in galactosialidosis results in deficiency of these three enzyme
activities.
