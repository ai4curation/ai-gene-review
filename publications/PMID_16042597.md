# Crystal structure of DMGO provides a prototype for a new tetrahydrofolate-binding fold.
**Authors:** Scrutton NS, Leys D
**Journal:** Biochem Soc Trans (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1042/BST0330776](https://doi.org/10.1042/BST0330776)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Soc Trans. 2005 Aug;33(Pt 4):776-9. doi: 10.1042/BST0330776.

Crystal structure of DMGO provides a prototype for a new
tetrahydrofolate-binding fold.

Scrutton NS(1), Leys D.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, University of Leicester, University Road,
Leicester LE1 7RH, UK.

The crystal structure of DMGO (dimethylglycine oxidase) from Arthrobacter
globiformis in complex with folate compounds has revealed a novel THF
(tetrahydrofolate)-binding fold [Leys, Basran and Scrutton (2003) EMBO J. 22,
4038-4048]. This fold is widespread among folate-binding proteins. The crystal
structures of aminomethyltransferase (T-protein), YgfZ and TrmE all reveal
similar THF-binding folds despite little similarity in sequence or function. The
THF-binding site is highly specific for reduced folate compounds and most
members of this fold family enhance the nucleophilic character of the THF N10
position.
