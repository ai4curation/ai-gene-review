# Lipopolysaccharide induces CYP2E1 in astrocytes through MAP kinase kinase-3 and C/EBPbeta and -delta.
**Authors:** Kelicen P, Tindberg N
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M311850200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M311850200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2004 Apr 16;279(16):15734-42. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M311850200. Epub
2003 Dec 11.

Lipopolysaccharide induces CYP2E1 in astrocytes through MAP kinase kinase-3 and
C/EBPbeta and -delta.

Kelicen P(1), Tindberg N.

Author information:
(1)Division of Molecular Toxicology, Institute of Environmental Medicine,
Karolinska Institute, S-171-77 Stockholm, Sweden.

Cytochrome P450 2E1 (CYP2E1) is highly inducible in a subset of astrocytes in
vivo following ischemic or mechanical injury and in vitro by lipopolysaccharide
(LPS) or interleukin-1beta. We have studied the mechanism of induction, and
found that transcriptional activation of CYP2E1 occurred within 3 h, and CYP2E1
dependent catalytic activity was induced more than 4-fold within 5 h. The
induction was sensitive to several tyrosine kinase inhibitors, and was further
modulated by inhibitors of p38 MAP kinase. MAP kinase kinase-3 (MKK3) was
phosphorylated in response to LPS, and expression of constitutively active MKK3,
but not the MAP kinase kinases MEKK1 or MKK1, activated CYP2E1. Transcriptional
activation was mediated through a C/EBPbeta and -delta binding element situated
at -486/-474, and appeared to involve activation of prebound factors as well as
recruitment of newly synthesized C/EBPbeta and -delta. It is thus suggested that
LPS induces MKK3 activation in astrocytes, which in turn stimulates a C/EBPbeta
and -delta binding element to mediate transcriptional activation of CYP2E1.
