The nuclear import of proteins through nuclear pore complexes (NPCs) illustrates
how a complex biological function can be generated by a spatially and temporally
organized cycle of interactions between cargoes, carriers and the Ran GTPase.
Recent work has given considerable insight into this process, especially about
how interactions are coordinated and the basis for the molecular recognition
that underlies the process. Although considerable progress has been made in
identifying and characterizing the molecular interactions in the soluble phase
that drive the nuclear protein import cycle, understanding the precise mechanism
of translocation through NPCs remains a major challenge.
