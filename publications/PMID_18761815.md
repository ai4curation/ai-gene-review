# Identification and characterization of a novel thymus aging related protein Rwdd1.
**Authors:** Kang N, Duan L, Tang L, Liu S, Li C, Li Y, Liu Q, Hu Y, Cui L, He W
**Journal:** Cell Mol Immunol (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1038/cmi.2008.34](https://doi.org/10.1038/cmi.2008.34)
**PMC:** [PMC4651297](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4651297/)

## Abstract

1. Cell Mol Immunol. 2008 Aug;5(4):279-85. doi: 10.1038/cmi.2008.34.

Identification and characterization of a novel thymus aging related protein
Rwdd1.

Kang N(1), Duan L, Tang L, Liu S, Li C, Li Y, Liu Q, Hu Y, Cui L, He W.

Author information:
(1)Department of Immunology, School of Basic Medicine, Peking Union Medical
College, and Institute of Basic Medical Sciences, Chinese Academy of Medical
Sciences, Beijing 100005, China.

By using DDRT-PCR and EST segment ligation, a novel mouse thymus involution
related gene Rwdd1 was identified. The reading frame encoded a protein of 243
amino acid residues which contained an RWD domain at the N terminus. Rwdd1
expression in thymus was decreased in aged and oxidatively stressed mice. It was
found to be expressed extensively in thymocytes and thymic epithelial cells. The
expression level of Rwdd1 could affect the transactivation activity of androgen
receptor (AR) in transiently transfected thymic epithelial cells. However, no
direct interaction could be detected by fluorescence resonance energy transfer
(FRET) analysis. In conclusion, Rwdd1 is a thymus involution related protein
that may indirectly affect AR signaling pathway.
