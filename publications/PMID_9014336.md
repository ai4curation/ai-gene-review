# Purification and characterization of five cuticular proteins from the spider Araneus diadematus.
**Authors:** Norup T, Berg T, Stenholm H, Andersen SO, Højrup P
**Journal:** Insect Biochem Mol Biol (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0965-1748(96)00052-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0965-1748(96)00052-5)

## Abstract

1. Insect Biochem Mol Biol. 1996 Sep-Oct;26(8-9):907-15. doi:
10.1016/s0965-1748(96)00052-5.

Purification and characterization of five cuticular proteins from the spider
Araneus diadematus.

Norup T(1), Berg T, Stenholm H, Andersen SO, Højrup P.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Biology, Odense University, Denmark.

Urea-extractable proteins have been purified from the cephalothoracic cuticle of
mature Araneus diadematus spiders. Two-dimensional gel electrophoresis showed at
least 12 major proteins, with pIs between 4.5 and 8.5. Five proteins were
purified and their primary structure determined, using a combination of mass
spectrometry and Edman degradation. Based on the amino acid sequence the
proteins can be divided into two groups, both characterized by hydrophobic
regions dominated by Ala, Pro and Val. Sequence similarity was observed between
all the spider cuticle proteins and a number of proteins from other arthropod
cuticles. Although the similarity seemed to be confined only to a region in the
central part of the molecules, it does link these very distantly related
species.
