Growing evidence suggests that Alzheimer disease (AD) origins in vascular
lesions. As the crucial mediator of vascular pathology, angiotensin II-induced
significant amyloid production in our laboratory, although amyloid neurotoxicity
depended on phosphorylated tau (p-tau) in recent studies. In the present study,
p-tau levels were significantly elevated by central angiotensin II via glycogen
synthase kinase 3β (GSK 3β) and other tau kinases. Moreover, angiotensin
II-induced cognitive impairment and tau phosphorylation was attenuated by
losartan and a GSK 3β inhibitor. These findings implicate Ang II as a crucial
mediator of AD pathology and a link between cardiovascular events and AD.
