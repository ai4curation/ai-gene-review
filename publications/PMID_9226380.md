# Isolation of a novel human homologue of the gene coding for echinoderm microtubule-associated protein (EMAP) from the Usher syndrome type 1a locus at 14q32.
**Authors:** Eudy JD, Ma-Edmonds M, Yao SF, Talmadge CB, Kelley PM, Weston MD, Kimberling WJ, Sumegi J
**Journal:** Genomics (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1006/geno.1997.4779](https://doi.org/10.1006/geno.1997.4779)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 1997 Jul 1;43(1):104-6. doi: 10.1006/geno.1997.4779.

Isolation of a novel human homologue of the gene coding for echinoderm
microtubule-associated protein (EMAP) from the Usher syndrome type 1a locus at
14q32.

Eudy JD(1), Ma-Edmonds M, Yao SF, Talmadge CB, Kelley PM, Weston MD, Kimberling
WJ, Sumegi J.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology and Microbiology, University of Nebraska Medical
Center, Omaha 68198, USA.

Usher syndrome type 1 (USH1) is an autosomal recessive, genetically
heterogeneous disorder causing severe congenital deafness, retinitis pigmentosa,
and vestibular dysfunction. The USHla locus located on 14q32 has been linked to
the genetic markers D14S250 and D14S78. Using D14S250 and D14S78, we have
isolated two nonchimeric YACs, 878g10 and 844g2, and a single BAC (135i20) and
PAC (194e17) clone and have arranged them into a contig spanning over the
D14S250 and D14S78 markers. The analysis of the YACs, BAC, and PAC revealed that
the physical distance between D14S250 and D14S78 is less than 25 kb. Iterative
cDNA library screening initiated with the EST 219670 found in the vicinity of
the D14S78 marker yielded a cDNA contig. The nucleotide sequence of the cDNA
encodes a protein of 717 amino acids in length, showing a high level of homology
to the Echinoderm 77-kDa microtubule-associated protein (EMAP). The human
homologue of Echinoderm microtubule-associated protein defines a novel human
gene. We propose that the human EMAP is a strong candidate for the USH1a gene
based on its genomic location and the proposed function of the protein.
