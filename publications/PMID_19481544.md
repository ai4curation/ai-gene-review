# The histone methyltransferase, NSD2, enhances androgen receptor-mediated transcription.
**Authors:** Kang HB, Choi Y, Lee JM, Choi KC, Kim HC, Yoo JY, Lee YH, Yoon HG
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2009.05.038](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2009.05.038)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2009 Jun 18;583(12):1880-6. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2009.05.038.
Epub  2009 May 28.

The histone methyltransferase, NSD2, enhances androgen receptor-mediated
transcription.

Kang HB(1), Choi Y, Lee JM, Choi KC, Kim HC, Yoo JY, Lee YH, Yoon HG.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, Institute of Genetic
Science, Center for Chronic Metabolic Disease Research, Yonsei University
College of Medicine, South Korea.

In this study, we discovered that NSD2 specifically interacts with the
DNA-binding domain of androgen receptor (AR) via its HMG domain, and the nuclear
translocation of both NSD2 and AR is enhanced in the presence of ligand.
Furthermore, we also demonstrated that the over expression of NSD2, but not of
NSD2 (DeltaSET) HMT-activity defective mutant, enhanced the mRNA level of PSA in
a dose-dependent manner. A chromatin immunoprecipitation assay showed that NSD2
protein is recruited to the enhancer region of the PSA gene by AR in an
agonist-enhanced manner. Taken together, these results uncover a potential role
for NSD2 in AR-mediated transcription, implicating NSD2 in prostate
carcinogenesis.
