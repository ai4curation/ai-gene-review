# Discovery of novel 3,6-disubstituted 2-pyridinecarboxamide derivatives as GK activators.
**Authors:** Mitsuya M, Kamata K, Bamba M, Watanabe H, Sasaki Y, Sasaki K, Ohyama S, Hosaka H, Nagata Y, Eiki J, Nishimura T
**Journal:** Bioorg Med Chem Lett (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bmcl.2009.03.137](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bmcl.2009.03.137)

## Abstract

1. Bioorg Med Chem Lett. 2009 May 15;19(10):2718-21. doi:
10.1016/j.bmcl.2009.03.137. Epub 2009 Mar 29.

Discovery of novel 3,6-disubstituted 2-pyridinecarboxamide derivatives as GK
activators.

Mitsuya M(1), Kamata K, Bamba M, Watanabe H, Sasaki Y, Sasaki K, Ohyama S,
Hosaka H, Nagata Y, Eiki J, Nishimura T.

Author information:
(1)Banyu Tsukuba Research Institute, Banyu Pharmaceutical Co. Ltd, Okubo,
Tsukuba, Ibaraki, Japan. morihiro_mitsuya@merck.com

A novel class of 3,6-disubstituted 2-pyridinecarboxamide derivatives was
designed based on X-ray analysis of the 2-aminobenzamide lead class. Subsequent
chemical modification led to the discovery of potent GK activators which
eliminate potential toxicity concerns associated with an aniline group of the
lead structure. Compound 7 demonstrated glucose lowering effect in a rat OGTT
model.
