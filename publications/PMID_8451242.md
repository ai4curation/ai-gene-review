# Crystallographic analysis of Thr-200-->His human carbonic anhydrase II and its complex with the substrate, HCO3-.
**Authors:** Xue Y, Vidgren J, Svensson LA, Liljas A, Jonsson BH, Lindskog S
**Journal:** Proteins (1993)
**DOI:** [10.1002/prot.340150110](https://doi.org/10.1002/prot.340150110)

## Abstract

1. Proteins. 1993 Jan;15(1):80-7. doi: 10.1002/prot.340150110.

Crystallographic analysis of Thr-200-->His human carbonic anhydrase II and its
complex with the substrate, HCO3-.

Xue Y(1), Vidgren J, Svensson LA, Liljas A, Jonsson BH, Lindskog S.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, University of Umeå, Sweden.

A complex of carbonic anhydrase (CA) with one of its substrates, bicarbonate,
has been studied crystallographically. Human isoenzyme II was mutated at
position 200 from threonine to histidine, which results in higher affinity for
bicarbonate. The HCO3- ion binds in the active site to the zinc ion as a
pseudo-bidentate ligand which gives the metal a coordination geometry between
tetrahedral and trigonal bipyramide. The water/hydroxide normally bound with
tetrahedral coordination to the zinc is probably replaced by the OH group of the
bicarbonate ion. The importance of residues Thr-199 and Glu-106 in controlling
the binding orientation of HCO3- is discussed as well as the catalytic
mechanism. Both the complex as well as the uncomplexed mutant were studied at
1.9 A resolution.
