# Colorimetric determination of pure Mg(2+)-dependent phosphatidate phosphatase activity.
**Authors:** Havriluk T, Lozy F, Siniossoglou S, Carman GM
**Journal:** Anal Biochem (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.ab.2007.08.037](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ab.2007.08.037)

## Abstract

1. Anal Biochem. 2008 Feb 15;373(2):392-4. doi: 10.1016/j.ab.2007.08.037. Epub

Colorimetric determination of pure Mg(2+)-dependent phosphatidate phosphatase
activity.

Havriluk T(1), Lozy F, Siniossoglou S, Carman GM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Food Science and Rutgers Center for Lipid Research, Rutgers
University, New Brunswick, NJ 08901, U.S.A.

The malachite green-molybdate reagent was used for a colorimetric assay of pure
Mg2(+)-dependent phosphatidate phosphatase activity. This enzyme plays a major
role in fat metabolism. Enzyme activity was linear with time and protein
concentration, and with the concentration of water-soluble dioctanoyl
phosphatidate. The colorimetric assay was used to examine enzyme inhibition by
phenylglyoxal, propranolol, and dimethyl sulfoxide. Pure enzyme and a
water-soluble phosphatidate substrate were required for the assay, which should
be applicable to a well-defined large-scale screen of Mg2(+)-dependent
phosphatidate phosphatise inhibitors (or activators).
