# The eye-specification proteins So and Eya form a complex and regulate multiple steps in Drosophila eye development.
**Authors:** Pignoni F, Hu B, Zavitz KH, Xiao J, Garrity PA, Zipursky SL
**Journal:** Cell (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80480-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80480-8)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 1997 Dec 26;91(7):881-91. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80480-8.

The eye-specification proteins So and Eya form a complex and regulate multiple
steps in Drosophila eye development.

Pignoni F(1), Hu B, Zavitz KH, Xiao J, Garrity PA, Zipursky SL.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biological Chemistry, The School of Medicine, University of
California, Los Angeles 90095, USA.

Erratum in
    Cell 1998 Feb 20;92(4):following 585.

Sine oculis (so) and eyes absent (eya) are required for Drosophila eye
development and are founding members of the mammalian Six and Eya gene families.
These genes have been proposed to act with eyeless (Pax6) to regulate eye
development in vertebrates and invertebrates. so encodes a highly diverged
homeobox transcription factor and eya encodes a novel nuclear protein. We
demonstrate that So and Eya (1) regulate common steps in eye development
including cell proliferation, patterning, and neuronal development; (2)
synergize in inducing ectopic eyes; and (3) interact in yeast and in vitro
through evolutionarily conserved domains. We propose that an So/Eya complex
regulates multiple steps in eye development and functions within the context of
a network of genes to specify eye tissue identity.
