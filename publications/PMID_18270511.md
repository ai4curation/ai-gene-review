# The ARID domain of the H3K4 demethylase RBP2 binds to a DNA CCGCCC motif.

## Abstract

The ARID domain of the H3K4 demethylase RBP2 binds to a DNA CCGCCC motif.

Tu S(1), Teng YC, Yuan C, Wu YT, Chan MY, Cheng AN, Lin PH, Juan LJ, Tsai MD.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemistry, Ohio State University, 100 West 18th Ave., Columbus,
Ohio 43210, USA.

The histone H3 lysine 4 demethylase RBP2 contains a DNA binding domain, the
AT-rich interaction domain (ARID). We solved the structure of ARID by NMR,
identified its DNA binding motif (CCGCCC) and characterized the binding
contacts. Immunofluorescence and luciferase assays indicated that ARID is
required for RBP2 demethylase activity in cells and that DNA recognition is
essential to regulate transcription.
