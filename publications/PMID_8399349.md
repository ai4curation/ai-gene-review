# cDNA cloning and sequence analysis of the human A3 adenosine receptor.

**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (1993)

We report the cloning of the cDNA encoding the homolog of the A3 adenosine
receptor (A3AR) from a human heart library. The receptor shares a surprisingly
low degree of sequence homology to the rat A3AR. Northern analysis of various
human tissues showed the human A3AR gene to be expressed primarily in lung,
liver, kidney and heart, with very little expression in the brain or in skeletal
muscle.
