# Bacteriophage lambda PaPa: not the mother of all lambda phages.
**Authors:** Hendrix RW, Duda RL

## Abstract

1. Science. 1992 Nov 13;258(5085):1145-8. doi: 10.1126/science.1439823.

Bacteriophage lambda PaPa: not the mother of all lambda phages.

Hendrix RW(1), Duda RL.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biological Sciences, University of Pittsburgh, PA 15260.

The common laboratory strain of bacteriophage lambda--lambda wild type or lambda
PaPa--carries a frameshift mutation relative to Ur-lambda, the original isolate.
The Ur-lambda virions have thin, jointed tail fibers that are absent from lambda
wild type. Two novel proteins of Ur-lambda constitute the fibers: the product of
stf, the gene that is disrupted in lambda wild type by the frameshift mutation,
and the product of gene tfa, a protein that is implicated in facilitating tail
fiber assembly. Relative to lambda wild type, Ur-lambda has expanded receptor
specificity and adsorbs to Escherichia coli cells more rapidly.
