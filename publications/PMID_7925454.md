# Gene XV of bacteriophage PRD1 encodes a lytic enzyme with muramidase activity.
**Authors:** Caldentey J, Hänninen AL, Bamford DH
**Journal:** Eur J Biochem (1994)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1432-1033.1994.00341.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1432-1033.1994.00341.x)

## Abstract

1. Eur J Biochem. 1994 Oct 1;225(1):341-6. doi: 10.1111/j.1432-1033.1994.00341.x.

Gene XV of bacteriophage PRD1 encodes a lytic enzyme with muramidase activity.

Caldentey J(1), Hänninen AL, Bamford DH.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Biotechnology, University of Helsinki, Finland.

Bacteriophage PRD1 is a lipid-containing virus that infects a variety of
Gram-negative bacteria, including Escherichia coli. The phage lyses its host by
virtue of a virally-encoded lytic enzyme, the synthesis of which has been
assigned to gene XV on the basis of complementation analysis and experiments
with mutant phages. We report here the cloning of gene XV into an expression
plasmid and the purification of its product, protein P15, to near homogeneity.
The purified protein P15, identified by N-terminal sequence analysis, showed a
strong lytic activity against chloroform-treated Gram-negative cells. No
activity against Gram-positive bacterial species could be detected. The pH
optimum of the enzyme was between 7.0-8.0. Protein P15 was readily inactivated
at temperatures above 4 degrees C, as well as by increasing the ionic strength
of the buffers. The analysis of cell wall digests indicated that P15 is a
glycosidase that cleaves the beta (1-4) linkage between N-acetylmuramic acid and
N-acetylglucosamine, thus displaying muramidase activity.
