# Identification of a human heme exporter that is essential for erythropoiesis.
**Authors:** Quigley JG, Yang Z, Worthington MT, Phillips JD, Sabo KM, Sabath DE, Berg CL, Sassa S, Wood BL, Abkowitz JL
**Journal:** Cell (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.cell.2004.08.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2004.08.014)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 2004 Sep 17;118(6):757-66. doi: 10.1016/j.cell.2004.08.014.

Identification of a human heme exporter that is essential for erythropoiesis.

Quigley JG(1), Yang Z, Worthington MT, Phillips JD, Sabo KM, Sabath DE, Berg CL,
Sassa S, Wood BL, Abkowitz JL.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medicine/Hematology, University of Washington, Seattle 98195,
USA.

FLVCR, a member of the major facilitator superfamily of transporter proteins, is
the cell surface receptor for feline leukemia virus, subgroup C. Retroviral
interference with FLVCR display results in a loss of erythroid progenitors
(colony-forming units-erythroid, CFU-E) and severe anemia in cats. In this
report, we demonstrate that human FLVCR exports cytoplasmic heme and hypothesize
that human FLVCR is required on developing erythroid cells to protect them from
heme toxicity. Inhibition of FLVCR in K562 cells decreases heme export, impairs
their erythroid maturation and leads to apoptosis. FLVCR is upregulated on
CFU-E, indicating that heme export is important in primary cells at this stage.
Studies of FLVCR expression in cell lines suggest this exporter also impacts
heme trafficking in intestine and liver. To our knowledge, this is the first
description of a mammalian heme transporter.
