# Effects of intracellular glutathione on sensitivity of Escherichia coli to mercury and arsenite.
**Authors:** Latinwo LM, Donald C, Ikediobi C, Silver S
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.1997.7911](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.1997.7911)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1998 Jan 6;242(1):67-70. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.1997.7911.

Effects of intracellular glutathione on sensitivity of Escherichia coli to
mercury and arsenite.

Latinwo LM(1), Donald C, Ikediobi C, Silver S.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, Florida A & M University, Tallahassee 32307, USA.
llatinwo@famu.edu

The effect of intracellular glutathione on sensitivity to mercuric cations and
arsenite anions was studied in Escherichia coli mutants that lack glutathione
(gshA) with or without an additional mutation affecting the osmotregulant
trehalose. The absence of glutathione increased cellular sensitivity to both
Hg2+ and AsO2-. The double mutant was more sensitive to Hg2+ than the single
mutant strain. The addition of plasmid resistance determinants of Hg2+ and AsO2-
showed additivity between chromosomal genes and plasmid genes. Mercury
resistance was increased in the plasmid-containing cells but not up to the level
of wild-type cells. Plasmid arsenite resistance was not expressed in the gshA
mutant of E. coli.
