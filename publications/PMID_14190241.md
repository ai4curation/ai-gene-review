GENE-ENZYME RELATIONS IN HISTIDINE BIOSYNTHESIS IN YEAST.

FINK GR.

Each of the histidine mutants of Saccharomyces cerevisiae has been correlated
with a particular step in histidine biosynthesis. The relations of the genes
controlling histidine biosynthesis in yeast appear to be identical with those
found in Neurospora and clearly distinct from those found in Salmonella.
