# An operon encoding a novel ABC-type transport system in Bacillus subtilis.

## Abstract

1. Microbiology (Reading). 1995 Jul;141 ( Pt 7):1781-4. doi:

An operon encoding a novel ABC-type transport system in Bacillus subtilis.

Rodriguez F(1), Grandi G.

Author information:
(1)ENIRICERCHE S.p.A. - Genetic Engineering and Microbiology Laboratories,Milan,
Italy.

Downstream from the surfactin synthetase operon in Bacillus subtilis a new
operon-type structure has been localized which, on the basis of sequence
determination, potentially encodes an ABC-type transport system. The 268 amino
acid protein, the product of orf1, represents the solute-binding component of
the system whereas the orf2 product, a 234 amino acid protein, is the
transmembrane component. Finally orf3 potentially encodes a typical 241 amino
acid ATP-binding protein involved in energy supply. Comparison of the three
proteins with the subunits of other ABC-type systems suggests that this new
system is involved in amino acid transport.
