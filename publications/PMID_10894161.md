# Apo2L/TRAIL-dependent recruitment of endogenous FADD and caspase-8 to death receptors 4 and 5.
**Authors:** Kischkel FC, Lawrence DA, Chuntharapai A, Schow P, Kim KJ, Ashkenazi A
**Journal:** Immunity (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1074-7613(00)80212-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1074-7613(00)80212-5)

## Abstract

1. Immunity. 2000 Jun;12(6):611-20. doi: 10.1016/s1074-7613(00)80212-5.

Apo2L/TRAIL-dependent recruitment of endogenous FADD and caspase-8 to death
receptors 4 and 5.

Kischkel FC(1), Lawrence DA, Chuntharapai A, Schow P, Kim KJ, Ashkenazi A.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Oncology, Genentech, Inc., South San Francisco,
California 94080, USA.

Fas (APO-1/CD95) and tumor necrosis factor receptor 1 (TNFR1) trigger apoptosis
by recruiting the apoptosis initiator caspase-8 through the adaptor FADD. Fas
binds FADD directly, whereas TNFR1 binds FADD indirectly, through TRADD. TRADD
alternatively recruits the NF-kappaB-inducing adaptor RIP. The TNF homolog
Apo2L/TRAIL triggers apoptosis through two distinct death receptors, DR4 and
DR5; however, receptor over-expression studies have yielded conflicting results
on the ligand's signaling mechanism. Apo2L/TRAIL induced homomeric and
heteromeric complexes of DR4 and DR5 and stimulated recruitment of FADD and
caspase-8 and caspase-8 activation in nontransfected cells. TRADD and RIP, which
bound TNFR1, did not bind DR4 and DR5. Thus, Apo2L/TRAIL and FasL initiate
apoptosis through similar mechanisms, and FADD may be a universal adaptor for
death receptors.
