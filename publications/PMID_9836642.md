Comment in
    Science. 1998 Dec 4;282(5395):1836-7. doi: 10.1126/science.282.5395.1836.

Transcription of naked DNA in vitro requires the general transcription factors
and RNA polymerase II. However, this minimal set of factors is not sufficient
for transcription when the DNA template is packaged into chromatin. Here, a
factor that facilitates activator-dependent transcription initiation on
chromatin templates was purified. This factor, remodeling and spacing factor
(RSF), has adenosine triphosphate-dependent nucleosome-remodeling and spacing
activities. Polymerases that initiate transcription with RSF can only extend
their transcripts in the presence of FACT (facilitates chromatin transcription).
Thus, the minimal factor requirements for activator-dependent transcription on
chromatin templates in vitro have been defined.
