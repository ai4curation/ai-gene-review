# Signaling of rat Frizzled-2 through phosphodiesterase and cyclic GMP.
**Authors:** Ahumada A, Slusarski DC, Liu X, Moon RT, Malbon CC, Wang HY
**Journal:** Science (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.1073776](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1073776)

## Abstract

1. Science. 2002 Dec 6;298(5600):2006-10. doi: 10.1126/science.1073776.

Signaling of rat Frizzled-2 through phosphodiesterase and cyclic GMP.

Ahumada A(1), Slusarski DC, Liu X, Moon RT, Malbon CC, Wang HY.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Pharmacology, Diabetes and Metabolic Diseases
Research Center, University Medical Center, SUNY-Stony Brook, Stony Brook, NY
11794-8651, USA.

The Frizzled-2 receptor (Rfz2) from rat binds Wnt proteins and can signal by
activating calcium release from intracellular stores. We show that wild-type
Rfz2 and a chimeric receptor consisting of the extracellular and transmembrane
portions of the beta2-adrenergic receptor with cytoplasmic domains of Rfz2 also
signaled through modulation of cyclic guanosine 3',5'-monophosphate (cGMP).
Activation of either receptor led to a decline in the intracellular
concentration of cGMP, a process that was inhibited in cells treated with
pertussis toxin, reduced by suppression of the expression of the heterotrimeric
GTP-binding protein (G protein) transducin, and suppressed through inhibition of
cGMP-specific phosphodiesterase (PDE) activity. Moreover, PDE inhibitors blocked
Rfz2-induced calcium transients in zebrafish embryos. Thus, Frizzled-2 appears
to couple to PDEs and calcium transients through G proteins.
