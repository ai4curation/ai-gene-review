# Lack of acrosome formation in Hrb-deficient mice.
**Authors:** Kang-Decker N, Mantchev GT, Juneja SC, McNiven MA, van Deursen JM
**Journal:** Science (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.1063665](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1063665)

## Abstract

1. Science. 2001 Nov 16;294(5546):1531-3. doi: 10.1126/science.1063665.

Lack of acrosome formation in Hrb-deficient mice.

Kang-Decker N(1), Mantchev GT, Juneja SC, McNiven MA, van Deursen JM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics and Adolescent Medicine, Mayo Clinic, 200 First 
Street SW, Rochester, MN 55905, USA.

The sperm acrosome is essential for sperm-egg fusion and is often defective in 
men with nonobstructive infertility. Here we report that male mice with a null 
mutation in Hrb are infertile and display round-headed spermatozoa that lack an 
acrosome. In wild-type spermatids, Hrb is associated with the cytosolic surface 
of proacrosomic transport vesicles that fuse to create a single large acrosomic 
vesicle at step 3 of spermiogenesis. Although proacrosomic vesicles form in 
spermatids that lack Hrb, the vesicles are unable to fuse, blocking acrosome 
development at step 2. We conclude that Hrb is required for docking and/or 
fusion of proacrosomic vesicles during acrosome biogenesis.
