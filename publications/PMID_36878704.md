Treatment options for inherited metabolic epilepsies are rapidly expanding with
advances in molecular biology and the genomic revolution. Traditional dietary
and nutrient modification and inhibitors or enhancers of protein and enzyme
function, the mainstays of therapy, are undergoing continuous revisions to
increase biological activity and reduce toxicity. Enzyme replacement and gene
replacement and editing hold promise for genetically targeted treatment and
cures. Molecular, imaging, and neurophysiologic biomarkers are emerging as key
indicators of disease pathophysiology, severity, and response to therapy.
