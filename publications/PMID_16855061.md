Plasma membrane calcium pumps in mouse olfactory sensory neurons.

Weeraratne SD(1), Valentine M, Cusick M, Delay R, Van Houten JL.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, University of Vermont, 109 Carrigan Drive, Burlington,
VT 05405, USA.

We report here the presence of specific plasma membrane calcium pumps (PMCAs) in
mouse olfactory sensory neurons. All 4 isoforms are present as shown by
deconvolution microscopy, and the specific splice variants are identified by
reverse transcriptase (RT)-polymerase chain reaction (PCR). The PMCAs are
present on the cell body, dendrite, knob, and cilia, but the different isoforms
of PMCAs are not identical in their distributions. The PMCAs are positioned to
play a role in calcium clearance after stimulation.
