# Munc13-1 is a presynaptic phorbol ester receptor that enhances neurotransmitter release.
**Authors:** Betz A, Ashery U, Rickmann M, Augustin I, Neher E, Südhof TC, Rettig J, Brose N
**Journal:** Neuron (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0896-6273(00)80520-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0896-6273(00)80520-6)

## Abstract

1. Neuron. 1998 Jul;21(1):123-36. doi: 10.1016/s0896-6273(00)80520-6.

Munc13-1 is a presynaptic phorbol ester receptor that enhances neurotransmitter
release.

Betz A(1), Ashery U, Rickmann M, Augustin I, Neher E, Südhof TC, Rettig J, Brose
N.

Author information:
(1)Max-Planck-Institut für Experimentelle Medizin, Abt. Molekulare
Neurobiologie, Göttingen, Bundesrepublik Deutschland.

Munc13-1, a mammalian homolog of C. elegans unc-13p, is thought to be involved
in the regulation of synaptic transmission. We now demonstrate that Munc13-1 is
a presynaptic high-affinity phorbol ester and diacylglycerol receptor with
ligand affinities similar to those of protein kinase C. Munc13-1 associates with
the plasma membrane in response to phorbol ester binding and acts as a phorbol
ester-dependent enhancer of transmitter release when overexpressed
presynaptically in the Xenopus neuromuscular junction. These observations
establish Munc13-1 as a novel presynaptic target of the diacylglycerol second
messenger pathway that acts in parallel with protein kinase C to regulate
neurotransmitter secretion.
