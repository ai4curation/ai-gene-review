# Identification of the novel proteins Yip4p and Yip5p as Rab GTPase interacting factors.
**Authors:** Calero M, Winand NJ, Collins RN
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(02)02442-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(02)02442-0)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2002 Mar 27;515(1-3):89-98. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(02)02442-0.

Identification of the novel proteins Yip4p and Yip5p as Rab GTPase interacting
factors.

Calero M(1), Winand NJ, Collins RN.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Medicine, Cornell University, Ithaca, NY 14853-6401,
USA.

The Rab GTPases are key regulators of membrane traffic. Yip1p is a membrane
protein of unknown function that has been reported to interact with the Rabs
Ypt1p and Ypt31p. In this study we identify Yif1p, and two unknown open reading
frames, Ygl198p and Ygl161p, which we term Yip4p and Yip5p, as Yip1p-related
sequences. We demonstrate that the Yip1p-related proteins possess several
features: (i) they have a common overall domain topology, (ii) they are capable
of biochemical interaction with a variety of Rab proteins in a manner dependent
on C-terminal prenylation, and (iii) they share an ability to physically
associate with other members of the YIP1 family.
