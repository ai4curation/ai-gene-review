# MurQ Etherase is required by Escherichia coli in order to metabolize anhydro-N-acetylmuramic acid obtained either from the environment or from its own cell wall.
**Authors:** Uehara T, Suefuji K, Jaeger T, Mayer C, Park JT
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1128/JB.188.4.1660-1662.2006](https://doi.org/10.1128/JB.188.4.1660-1662.2006)
**PMC:** [PMC1367226](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1367226/)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 2006 Feb;188(4):1660-2. doi: 10.1128/JB.188.4.1660-1662.2006.

MurQ Etherase is required by Escherichia coli in order to metabolize
anhydro-N-acetylmuramic acid obtained either from the environment or from its
own cell wall.

Uehara T(1), Suefuji K, Jaeger T, Mayer C, Park JT.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Biology and Microbiology, Tufts University School of
Medicine, Boston, Massachusetts 02111, USA. tsuyoshi.uehara@tufts.edu

MurQ is an N-acetylmuramic acid-phosphate (MurNAc-P) etherase that converts
MurNAc-P to N-acetylglucosamine-phosphate and is essential for growth on MurNAc
as the sole source of carbon (T. Jaegar, M. Arsic, and C. Mayer, J. Biol. Chem.
280:30100-30106, 2005). Here we show that MurQ is the only MurNAc-P etherase in
Escherichia coli and that MurQ and AnmK kinase are required for utilization of
anhydro-MurNAc derived either from cell wall murein or imported from the medium.
