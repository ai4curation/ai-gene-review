# Beta-carotene hydroxylase gene from the cyanobacterium Synechocystis sp. PCC6803.
**Authors:** Masamoto K, Misawa N, Kaneko T, Kikuno R, Toh H
**Journal:** Plant Cell Physiol (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1093/oxfordjournals.pcp.a029405](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.pcp.a029405)

## Abstract

1. Plant Cell Physiol. 1998 May;39(5):560-4. doi:
10.1093/oxfordjournals.pcp.a029405.

Beta-carotene hydroxylase gene from the cyanobacterium Synechocystis sp.
PCC6803.

Masamoto K(1), Misawa N, Kaneko T, Kikuno R, Toh H.

Author information:
(1)Biological Laboratory, Faculty of Education, Kumamoto University, Japan.

The ORF sll1468 of Synechocystis sp. PCC6803 was identified as a gene for
beta-carotene hydroxylase by functional complementation in a
beta-carotene-producing Escherichia coli. The gene product of ORF sll1468 added
hydroxyl groups to the beta-ionone rings of beta-carotene (beta, beta-carotene)
to form zeaxanthin (beta, beta-carotene-3,3'-diol). This newly identified
beta-carotene hydroxylase does not show overall amino acid sequence similarity
to the known beta-carotene hydroxylases. However, it showed significant sequence
similarity to beta-carotene ketolases of marine bacteria and a green alga.
