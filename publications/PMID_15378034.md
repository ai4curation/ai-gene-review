## Abstract

Author information:

The origin licensing repressor geminin is a unique bifunctional protein
providing a molecular link between cellular proliferation, differentiation and
genomic stability. Here we report the first molecular structure of human
geminin, determined by EM and image processing at a resolution of 17.5 A. The
geminin molecule is a tetramer formed by two dimers with monomers interacting
via coiled-coil domains. The unusual structural organization of geminin provides
molecular insight into its bifunctional nature.
