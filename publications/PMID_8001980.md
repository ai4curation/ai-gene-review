# Morquio A syndrome: cloning, sequence, and structure of the human N-acetylgalactosamine 6-sulfatase (GALNS) gene.
**Authors:** Morris CP, Guo XH, Apostolou S, Hopwood JJ, Scott HS
**Journal:** Genomics (1994)
**DOI:** [10.1006/geno.1994.1443](https://doi.org/10.1006/geno.1994.1443)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 1994 Aug;22(3):652-4. doi: 10.1006/geno.1994.1443.

Morquio A syndrome: cloning, sequence, and structure of the human
N-acetylgalactosamine 6-sulfatase (GALNS) gene.

Morris CP(1), Guo XH, Apostolou S, Hopwood JJ, Scott HS.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemical Pathology, Adelaide Children's Hospital, North
Adelaide, Australia.

Deficiency of the lysosomal enzyme, N-acetylgalactosamine 6-sulfatase (GALNS;EC
3.1.6.4), results in the storage of the glycosaminoglycans, keratan sulfate and
chondroitin 6-sulfate, which leads to the lysosomal storage disorder Morquio A
syndrome. Four overlapping genomic clones derived from a chromosome 16-specific
gridded cosmid library containing the entire GALNS gene were isolated. The
structure of the gene and the sequence of the exon/intron boundaries and the 5'
promoter region were determined. The GALNS gene is split into 14 exons spanning
approximately 40 kb. The potential promoter for GALNS lacks a TATA box but
contains GC box consensus sequences, consistent with its role as a housekeeping
gene. The GALNS gene contains an Alu repeat in intron 5 and a VNTR-like sequence
in intron 6.
