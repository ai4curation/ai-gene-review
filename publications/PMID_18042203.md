# HAG2/MYB76 and HAG3/MYB29 exert a specific and coordinated control on the regulation of aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis in Arabidopsis thaliana.
**Authors:** Gigolashvili T, Engqvist M, Yatusevich R, Müller C, Flügge UI
**Journal:** New Phytol (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1469-8137.2007.02295.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-8137.2007.02295.x)

HAG2/MYB76 and HAG3/MYB29 exert a specific and coordinated control on the

Gigolashvili T(1), Engqvist M(1), Yatusevich R(1), Müller C(2), Flügge UI(1).

Author information:
(1)Botanisches Institut der Universität zu Köln, Gyrhofstrasse 15, D-50931 Köln,

(2)Abteilung für Chemische Ökologie, Universität Bielefeld, Universitätsstraße

In a previous transactivation screen, two Arabidopsis thaliana R2R3-MYB
transcription factors, HAG2/MYB76 and HAG3/MYB29, along with the already
characterized HAG1/MYB28, were identified as putative regulators of aliphatic
glucosinolate biosynthesis. Molecular and biochemical characterization of
HAG2/MYB76 and HAG3/MYB29 functions was performed using transformants with
increased or repressed transcript levels. Real-time PCR assays, cotransformation
assays and measurements of glucosinolate contents were used to assess the impact
of both MYB factors on the steady-state level of glucosinolate biosynthetic
genes and accumulation of aliphatic glucosinolates. Both HAG2/MYB76 and
HAG3/MYB29 were shown to be positive regulators of aliphatic glucosinolate
biosynthesis. Expression of promoter-beta-glucuronidase (GUS) fusions indicated
GUS activities in both vegetative and generative organs, with distinct
characteristics for each MYB factor. HAG1/MYB28, HAG2/MYB76 and HAG3/MYB29
reciprocally transactivated each other in the control of aliphatic glucosinolate
biosynthesis and downregulated the expression of genes involved in the control
of indolic glucosinolate biosynthesis, pointing to a reciprocal negative
regulation of these two pathways. All three HAG transcription factors exert a
coordinated control on aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis.
