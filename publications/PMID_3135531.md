## Abstract

1. Nucleic Acids Res. 1988 Jul 11;16(13):5727-40. doi: 10.1093/nar/16.13.5727.

Site-directed mutagenesis in the DNA linking site of bacteriophage phi 29
terminal protein: isolation and characterization of a Ser232----Thr mutant.

Garmendia C(1), Salas M, Hermoso JM.

Author information:
(1)Centro de Biología Molecular (CSIC-UAM), Universidad Autónoma, Madrid, Spain.

By site-directed mutagenesis we have changed the serine residue 232 of the phi
29 terminal protein, involved in the covalent linkage to dAMP for the initiation
of replication, into a threonine residue. The mutant terminal protein has been
purified to homogeneity and shown to be inactive in the formation of the
initiation complex; nevertheless, the mutant protein retains its ability to
interact with the phi 29 DNA polymerase and with the DNA. The results obtained
indicate a high specificity in the linking site of the terminal protein.
