# The premetazoan ancestry of cadherins.
**Authors:** Abedin M, King N
**Journal:** Science (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.1151084](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1151084)

## Abstract

1. Science. 2008 Feb 15;319(5865):946-8. doi: 10.1126/science.1151084.

The premetazoan ancestry of cadherins.

Abedin M(1), King N.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular and Cell Biology and Center for Integrative Genomics,
University of California at Berkeley, Berkeley, CA 94720, USA.

Cadherin-mediated cell adhesion and signaling is essential for metazoan
development and yet is absent from all other multicellular organisms. We found
cadherin genes at numbers similar to those observed in complex metazoans in one
of the closest single-celled relatives of metazoans, the choanoflagellate
Monosiga brevicollis. Because the evolution of metazoans from a single-celled
ancestor required novel cell adhesion and signaling mechanisms, the discovery of
diverse cadherins in choanoflagellates suggests that cadherins may have
contributed to metazoan origins.
