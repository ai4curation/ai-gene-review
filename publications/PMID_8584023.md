# Prolactin receptor is associated with c-src kinase in rat liver.
**Authors:** Berlanga JJ, Fresno Vara JA, Martín-Pérez J, García-Ruiz JP
**Journal:** Mol Endocrinol (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1210/mend.9.11.8584023](https://doi.org/10.1210/mend.9.11.8584023)

## Abstract

1. Mol Endocrinol. 1995 Nov;9(11):1461-7. doi: 10.1210/mend.9.11.8584023.

Prolactin receptor is associated with c-src kinase in rat liver.

Berlanga JJ(1), Fresno Vara JA, Martín-Pérez J, García-Ruiz JP.

Author information:
(1)Departamento de Biologia Molecular, Centro de Biologia Molecular Severo
Ochoa, Madrid, Spain.

The mechanism of action of the pituitary hormone PRL was studied in hepatocytes
of lactating rats. PRL receptor immune complexes obtained from liver lysates
have an associated tyrosine kinase activity. The tyrosine kinase has been
identified in isolated hepatocytes as pp60c-src. Incubation of hepatocytes with
PRL induces the association of PRL receptor with pp60c-src and the resultant
stimulation of its tyrosine kinase activity. Furthermore, PRL stimulates the
gene expression of c-fos, c-jun, and c-src. All of these findings support the
idea that the pp60c-src tyrosine kinase participates in the early steps of the
PRL intracellular signaling that promotes cell growth in liver cells.
