# Interaction of Escherichia coli RuvA and RuvB proteins with synthetic Holliday junctions.
**Authors:** Parsons CA, Tsaneva I, Lloyd RG, West SC
**Journal:** Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (1992)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.89.12.5452](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.89.12.5452)

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1992 Jun 15;89(12):5452-6. doi:
10.1073/pnas.89.12.5452.

Interaction of Escherichia coli RuvA and RuvB proteins with synthetic Holliday
junctions.

Parsons CA(1), Tsaneva I, Lloyd RG, West SC.

Author information:
(1)Imperial Cancer Research Fund, Clare Hall Laboratories, South Mimms, Herts,
United Kingdom.

The RuvA, RuvB, and RuvC proteins of Escherichia coli are required for the
recombinational repair of ultraviolet light- or chemical-induced DNA damage. In
vitro, RuvC protein interacts with Holliday junctions in DNA and promotes their
resolution by endonucleolytic cleavage. In this paper, we investigate the
interaction of RuvA and RuvB proteins with model Holliday junctions. Using
band-shift assays, we show that RuvA binds synthetic Holliday structures to form
specific protein-DNA complexes. Moreover, in the presence of ATP, the RuvA and
RuvB proteins act in concert to promote dissociation of the synthetic Holliday
structures. The dissociation reaction requires both RuvA and RuvB and a
nucleotide cofactor (ATP or dATP) and is rapid (40% of DNA molecules dissociate
within 1 min). The reaction does not occur when ATP is replaced by either ADP or
the nonhydrolyzable analog of ATP, adenosine 5'-[gamma-thio]triphosphate. We
suggest that the RuvA and RuvB proteins play a specific role in the branch
migration of Holliday junctions during postreplication repair of DNA damage in
E. coli.
