# Presence of translation elongation factor-1A (eEF1A) in the excitatory postsynaptic density of rat cerebral cortex.
**Authors:** Cho SJ, Jung JS, Ko BH, Jin I, Moon IS
**Journal:** Neurosci Lett (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.neulet.2004.05.036](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neulet.2004.05.036)

## Abstract

1. Neurosci Lett. 2004 Aug 5;366(1):29-33. doi: 10.1016/j.neulet.2004.05.036.

Presence of translation elongation factor-1A (eEF1A) in the excitatory
postsynaptic density of rat cerebral cortex.

Cho SJ(1), Jung JS, Ko BH, Jin I, Moon IS.

Author information:
(1)Department of Microbiology, College of Natural Sciences, Kyungpook National
University, Taegu 702-701, South Korea.

The postsynaptic density (PSD) is a proteinaceous cellular structure that is
specialized for postsynaptic signal transduction. Here, we show that eukaryotic
translation factor-1A (eEF1A; formerly known as eEF-1alpha) is associated with
the excitatory PSD in rat forebrain. Immunoblot analysis showed that eEF1A in
the PSD fraction is enriched over homogenate. Salt (1.0M NaCl), but not
non-ionic detergents such as Triton X-100 (1.0%) and n-octyl glucoside (1.0%),
could dissociate eEF1A from the PSD core. In cultured cortical neurons, eEF1A
was colocalized with postsynaptic markers (PSD95 and SynGAPalpha), but not with
a presynaptic marker (synaptophysin). These results indicate that eEF1A is
present in the PSD of the excitatory synapses.
