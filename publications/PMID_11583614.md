# Composition and functional characterization of yeast 66S ribosome assembly intermediates.
**Authors:** Harnpicharnchai P, Jakovljevic J, Horsey E, Miles T, Roman J, Rout M, Meagher D, Imai B, Guo Y, Brame CJ, Shabanowitz J, Hunt DF, Woolford JL Jr

The pathway and complete collection of factors that orchestrate ribosome
assembly are not clear. To address these problems, we affinity purified yeast
preribosomal particles containing the nucleolar protein Nop7p and developed
means to separate their components. Nop7p is associated primarily with 66S
preribosomes containing either 27SB or 25.5S plus 7S pre-rRNAs. Copurifying
proteins identified by mass spectrometry include ribosomal proteins,
nonribosomal proteins previously implicated in 60S ribosome biogenesis, and
proteins not known to be involved in ribosome production. Analysis of strains
mutant for eight of these proteins not previously implicated in ribosome
biogenesis showed that they do participate in this pathway. These results
demonstrate that proteomic approaches in concert with genetic tools provide
powerful means to purify and characterize ribosome assembly intermediates.
