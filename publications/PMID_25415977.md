Erratum in
    Plant Cell. 2016 May;28(5):1230. doi: 10.1105/tpc.16.00306.

The Arabidopsis thaliana genes PROTEIN INHIBITOR OF ACTIVATED STAT LIKE1 (PIAL1)
and PIAL2 encode proteins with SP-RING domains, which occur in many ligases of
the small ubiquitin-related modifier (SUMO) conjugation pathway. We show that
PIAL1 and PIAL2 function as SUMO ligases capable of SUMO chain formation and
require the SUMO-modified SUMO-conjugating enzyme SCE1 for optimal activity.
Mutant analysis indicates a role for PIAL1 and 2 in salt stress and osmotic
stress responses, whereas under standard conditions, the mutants show close to
normal growth. Mutations in PIAL1 and 2 also lead to altered sulfur metabolism.
We propose that, together with SUMO chain binding ubiquitin ligases, these
enzymes establish a pathway for proteolytic removal of sumoylation substrates.
