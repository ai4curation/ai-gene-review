# High incidence of electrocardiogram abnormalities in young patients with duchenne muscular dystrophy.
**Authors:** Takami Y, Takeshima Y, Awano H, Okizuka Y, Yagi M, Matsuo M
**Journal:** Pediatr Neurol (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2008.08.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2008.08.006)

## Abstract

1. Pediatr Neurol. 2008 Dec;39(6):399-403. doi:
10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2008.08.006.

High incidence of electrocardiogram abnormalities in young patients with
duchenne muscular dystrophy.

Takami Y(1), Takeshima Y, Awano H, Okizuka Y, Yagi M, Matsuo M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics, Kobe University Graduate School of Medicine, Kobe,

Electrocardiogram abnormalities are reported to be complicated in Duchenne
muscular dystrophy. Although Duchenne muscular dystrophy can be genetically
diagnosed in young patients, extensive electrocardiogram studies have not been
reported. Here, electrocardiogram abnormalities were examined in Duchenne
muscular dystrophy cases with dystrophin gene mutations. Sixty-nine patients,
aged </=18 years, received 136 electrocardiogram examinations. Sixty-four
patients (91.3%) displayed one or more abnormalities. Furthermore, patients
adolescent <10 years (84.8% of patients) displayed electrocardiogram
abnormalities, and the most common abnormality was deep Q-waves. Remarkably, the
abnormality incidence of both deep Q-waves and low RV5 + SV1 (R-wave V5 + S-wave
V1) were significantly high in adolescent patients. Although the patterns or
positions of dystrophin gene mutations were compared with electrocardiogram
abnormalities, no predisposing mutation was disclosed. These results indicate
that electrocardiogram abnormalities in Duchenne muscular dystrophy are a result
of dystrophin deficiency, regardless of types of gene mutations. The disease can
be divided into two types: age-dependent and age-independent. Deep Q-waves and
low RV5 + SV1 are proposed as markers of age-dependent cardiac complications.
