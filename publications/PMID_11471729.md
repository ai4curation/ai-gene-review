# Characterization of recombinant yeast exo-beta-1,3-glucanase (Exg 1p) expressed in Escherichia coli cells.
**Authors:** Suzuki K, Yabe T, Maruyama Y, Abe K, Nakajima T
**Journal:** Biosci Biotechnol Biochem (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1271/bbb.65.1310](https://doi.org/10.1271/bbb.65.1310)

## Abstract

1. Biosci Biotechnol Biochem. 2001 Jun;65(6):1310-4. doi: 10.1271/bbb.65.1310.

Characterization of recombinant yeast exo-beta-1,3-glucanase (Exg 1p) expressed
in Escherichia coli cells.

Suzuki K(1), Yabe T, Maruyama Y, Abe K, Nakajima T.

Author information:
(1)Divison of Applied Life Science, Graduate School of Agricultural Science,
Tohoku University, Sendai, Japan.

Yeast exo-beta-1,3-glucanase gene (EXG1) was expressed in Escherichia coli and
the recombinant enzyme (Exg1p) was characterized. The recombinant Exglp had an
apparent molecular mass of 45 kDa by SDS-PAGE and the enzyme has a broad
specificity for beta-1,3-linkages as well as beta-1,6-linkages, and also for
other beta-glucosidic linked substrates, such as cellobiose and pNPG. Kinetic
analyses indicate that the enzyme prefers small substrates such as
laminaribiose, gentiobiose, and pNPG rather than polysaccharide substrates, such
as laminaran or pustulan. With a high concentration of laminaribiose, the enzyme
catalyzed transglucosidation forming laminarioligosaccharides. The enzyme was
strongly inhibited with high concentrations of laminaran.
