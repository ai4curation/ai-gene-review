Castration-recurrent prostate cancer (CRPC) is suspected to depend on androgen
receptor (AR). The AF-1 region in the amino-terminal domain (NTD) of AR contains
most, if not all, of the transcriptional activity. Here we identify EPI-001, a
small molecule that blocked transactivation of the NTD and was specific for
inhibition of AR without attenuating transcriptional activities of related
steroid receptors. EPI-001 interacted with the AF-1 region, inhibited
protein-protein interactions with AR, and reduced AR interaction with
androgen-response elements on target genes. Importantly, EPI-001 blocked
androgen-induced proliferation and caused cytoreduction of CRPC in xenografts
dependent on AR for growth and survival without causing toxicity.
