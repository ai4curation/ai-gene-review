COP9 signalosome (CSN) cleaves the ubiquitin-like protein Nedd8 from the Cul1
subunit of SCF ubiquitin ligases. The Jab1/MPN domain metalloenzyme (JAMM) motif
in the Jab1/Csn5 subunit was found to underlie CSN's Nedd8 isopeptidase
activity. JAMM is found in proteins from archaea, bacteria, and eukaryotes,
including the Rpn11 subunit of the 26S proteasome. Metal chelators and point
mutations within JAMM abolished CSN-dependent cleavage of Nedd8 from Cul1, yet
had little effect on CSN complex assembly. Optimal SCF activity in yeast and
both viability and proper photoreceptor cell (R cell) development in Drosophila
melanogaster required an intact Csn5 JAMM domain. We propose that JAMM
isopeptidases play important roles in a variety of physiological pathways.
