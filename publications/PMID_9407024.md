Comment in
    Genes Dev. 1997 Dec 15;11(24):3279-85. doi: 10.1101/gad.11.24.3279.

We have investigated the role of the RNA Polymerase II (Pol II) carboxy-terminal
domain (CTD) in mRNA 5' capping. Transcripts made in vivo by Pol II with a
truncated CTD had a lower proportion of capped 5' ends than those made by Pol II
with a full-length CTD. In addition, the enzymes responsible for cap synthesis,
RNA guanylyltransferase, and RNA (guanine-7)-methyltransferase bound directly to
the phosphorylated, but not to the nonphosphorylated, form of the CTD in vitro.
These results suggest that: (1) Pol II-specific capping of nascent transcripts
in vivo is enhanced by recruitment of the capping enzymes to the CTD and (2)
capping is co-ordinated with CTD phosphorylation.
