# Distinct conformations of the protein complex p97-Ufd1-Npl4 revealed by electron cryomicroscopy.
**Authors:** Bebeacua C, Förster A, McKeown C, Meyer HH, Zhang X, Freemont PS
**Journal:** Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (2012)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.1114341109](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1114341109)
**PMC:** [PMC3268311](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3268311/)

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 2012 Jan 24;109(4):1098-103. doi:
10.1073/pnas.1114341109. Epub 2012 Jan 9.

Distinct conformations of the protein complex p97-Ufd1-Npl4 revealed by electron
cryomicroscopy.

Bebeacua C(1), Förster A, McKeown C, Meyer HH, Zhang X, Freemont PS.

Author information:
(1)Centre for Structural Biology and Centre for Biomolecular Electron
Microscopy, Division of Molecular Biosciences, Imperial College London, London
SW7 2AZ, United Kingdom.

p97 is a key regulator of numerous cellular pathways and associates with
ubiquitin-binding adaptors to remodel ubiquitin-modified substrate proteins. How
adaptor binding to p97 is coordinated and how adaptors contribute to substrate
remodeling is unclear. Here we present the 3D electron cryomicroscopy
reconstructions of the major Ufd1-Npl4 adaptor in complex with p97. Our
reconstructions show that p97-Ufd1-Npl4 is highly dynamic and that Ufd1-Npl4
assumes distinct positions relative to the p97 ring upon addition of nucleotide.
Our results suggest a model for substrate remodeling by p97 and also explains
how p97-Ufd1-Npl4 could form other complexes in a hierarchical model of
p97-cofactor assembly.
