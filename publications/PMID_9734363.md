# MyD88 is an adaptor protein in the hToll/IL-1 receptor family signaling pathways.
**Authors:** Medzhitov R, Preston-Hurlburt P, Kopp E, Stadlen A, Chen C, Ghosh S, Janeway CA Jr
**Journal:** Mol Cell (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1097-2765(00)80136-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1097-2765(00)80136-7)

## Abstract

1. Mol Cell. 1998 Aug;2(2):253-8. doi: 10.1016/s1097-2765(00)80136-7.

MyD88 is an adaptor protein in the hToll/IL-1 receptor family signaling
pathways.

Medzhitov R(1), Preston-Hurlburt P, Kopp E, Stadlen A, Chen C, Ghosh S, Janeway
CA Jr.

Author information:
(1)Section of Immunobiology and Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Yale University
School of Medicine, New Haven, Connecticut 06520-8011, USA.

The Toll-mediated signaling cascade using the NF-kappaB pathway has been shown
to be essential for immune responses in adult Drosophila, and we recently
reported that a human homolog of the Drosophila Toll protein induces various
immune response genes via this pathway. We now demonstrate that signaling by the
human Toll receptor employs an adaptor protein, MyD88, and induces activation of
NF-kappaB via the Pelle-like kinase IRAK and the TRAF6 protein, similar to
IL-1R-mediated NF-kappaB activation. However, we find that Toll and IL-1R
signaling pathways are not identical with respect to AP-1 activation. Finally,
our findings implicate MyD88 as a general adaptor/regulator molecule for the
Toll/IL-1R family of receptors for innate immunity.
