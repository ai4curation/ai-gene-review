# Oncoprotein p28 GANK binds to RelA and retains NF-kappaB in the cytoplasm through nuclear export.
**Authors:** Chen Y, Li HH, Fu J, Wang XF, Ren YB, Dong LW, Tang SH, Liu SQ, Wu MC, Wang HY
**Journal:** Cell Res (2007)
**DOI:** [10.1038/cr.2007.99](https://doi.org/10.1038/cr.2007.99)

## Abstract

1. Cell Res. 2007 Dec;17(12):1020-9. doi: 10.1038/cr.2007.99.

Oncoprotein p28 GANK binds to RelA and retains NF-kappaB in the cytoplasm
through nuclear export.

Chen Y(1), Li HH, Fu J, Wang XF, Ren YB, Dong LW, Tang SH, Liu SQ, Wu MC, Wang
HY.

Author information:

p28(GANK) (also known as PSMD10, p28 and gankyrin) is an ankyrin repeat
anti-apoptotic oncoprotein that is commonly overexpressed in hepatocellular
carcinomas and increases the degradation of p53 and Rb. NF-kappaB (nuclear
factor-kappaB) is known to be sequestered in the cytoplasm by I kappaB
(inhibitor of NF-kappaB) proteins, but much less is known about the cytoplasmic
retention of NF-kappaB by other cellular proteins. Here we show that p28(GANK)
inhibits NF-kappaB activity. As a nuclear-cytoplasmic shuttling protein,
p28(GANK) directly binds to NF-kappaB/RelA and exports RelA from nucleus through
a chromosomal region maintenance-1 (CRM-1) dependent pathway, which results in
the cytoplasmic retention of NF-kappaB/RelA. We demonstrate that all the ankyrin
repeats of p28(GANK) are required for the interaction with RelA and that the N
terminus of p28(GANK), which contains the nuclear export sequence (NES), is
responsible for suppressing NF-kappaB/RelA nuclear translocation. These results
suggest that overexpression of p28(GANK) prevents the nuclear localization and
inhibits the activity of NF-kappaB/RelA.
