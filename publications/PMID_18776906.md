# Mincle is an ITAM-coupled activating receptor that senses damaged cells.
**Authors:** Yamasaki S, Ishikawa E, Sakuma M, Hara H, Ogata K, Saito T

## Abstract

1. Nat Immunol. 2008 Oct;9(10):1179-88. doi: 10.1038/ni.1651. Epub 2008 Sep 7.

Mincle is an ITAM-coupled activating receptor that senses damaged cells.

Yamasaki S(1), Ishikawa E, Sakuma M, Hara H, Ogata K, Saito T.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory for Cell Signaling, RIKEN Research Center for Allergy and

Comment in
    Nat Immunol. 2008 Oct;9(10):1099-100. doi: 10.1038/ni1008-1099.

Macrophage-inducible C-type lectin (Mincle) is expressed mainly in macrophages
and is induced after exposure to various stimuli and stresses. Here we show that
Mincle selectively associated with the Fc receptor common gamma-chain and
activated macrophages to produce inflammatory cytokines and chemokines.
Mincle-expressing cells were activated in the presence of dead cells, and we
identified SAP130, a component of small nuclear ribonucloprotein, as a Mincle
ligand that is released from dead cells. To investigate whether Mincle is
required for normal responses to cell death in vivo, we induced thymocyte death
by irradiating mice and found that transient infiltration of neutrophils into
the thymus could be blocked by injection of Mincle-specific antibody. Our
results suggest that Mincle is a receptor that senses nonhomeostatic cell death
and thereby induces the production of inflammatory cytokines to drive the
infiltration of neutrophils into damaged tissue.
