**Authors:** Faust LP, Babior BM

**DOI:** [10.1016/0003-9861(92)90135-j](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-9861(92)90135-j)

## Abstract

1. Arch Biochem Biophys. 1992 Apr;294(1):50-4. doi: 10.1016/0003-9861(92)90135-j.

Overexpression, purification, and some properties of the AdoCbl-dependent
ethanolamine ammonia-lyase from Salmonella typhimurium.

Faust LP(1), Babior BM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular and Experimental Medicine, Scripps Research
Institute, La Jolla, California 92037.

Recombinant ethanolamine ammonia-lyase from S. typhimurium has been
overexpressed and purified in large quantities by a simple procedure. The
molecular weight of the native enzyme is about 480 kDa, and it contains two
active sites/molecule as shown by kinetic studies and by titration with CNCbl.
Analysis by sodium dodecyl sulfate-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis confirms
earlier cloning studies indicating that it is composed of two kinds of subunits,
one of MW 31 kDa and the other of MW 50 kDa. These subunits, inactive by
themselves, combine to produce an active enzyme whose composition is most likely
alpha 6 beta 6. The Km for AdoCbl is 0.5 microM, and the turnover number is 55
s-1 per active site at 22 degrees C.
