# A novel Rad24 checkpoint protein complex closely related to replication factor C.

A novel Rad24 checkpoint protein complex closely related to replication factor
C.

Green CM(1), Erdjument-Bromage H, Tempst P, Lowndes NF.

Author information:
(1)Imperial Cancer Research Fund, Clare Hall Laboratories, Hertfordshire, UK.

Erratum in
    Curr Biol 2000 Feb 24;10(4):R171.

Rad24 functions in the DNA damage checkpoint pathway of Saccharomyces
cerevisiae. Here, analysis of Rad24 in whole cell extracts demonstrated that its
mass was considerably greater than its predicted molecular weight, suggesting
that Rad24 is a component of a protein complex. The Rad24 complex was purified
to homogeneity. In addition to Rad24, the complex included polypeptides of 40
kDa and 35 kDa. The 40 kDa species was found by mass spectrometry to contain
Rfc2 and Rfc3, subunits of replication factor C (RFC), a five subunit protein
that is required for the loading of polymerases onto DNA during replication and
repair [3]. We hypothesised that other RFC subunits, all of which share sequence
homologles with Rad24, might also be components of the Rad24 complex. Reciprocal
co-immunoprecipitation studies were performed using extracts prepared from
strains containing epitope-tagged RFC proteins. These experiments showed that
the small RFC proteins, Rfc2, Rfc3, Rfc4 and Rfc5, interacted with Rad24,
whereas the Rfc1 subunit did not. We suggest that this RFC-like Rad24 complex
may function as a structure-specific sensor in the DNA damage checkpoint
pathway.
