# In vivo functional analysis of the Ras exchange factor son of sevenless.
**Authors:** Karlovich CA, Bonfini L, McCollam L, Rogge RD, Daga A, Czech MP, Banerjee U
**Journal:** Science (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.7725106](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7725106)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1995 Apr 28;268(5210):576-9. doi: 10.1126/science.7725106.

In vivo functional analysis of the Ras exchange factor son of sevenless.

Karlovich CA(1), Bonfini L, McCollam L, Rogge RD, Daga A, Czech MP, Banerjee U.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology and Molecular Biology Institute, University of
California, Los Angeles 90024, USA.

The Son of sevenless (Sos) protein functions as a guanine nucleotide transfer
factor for Ras and interacts with the receptor tyrosine kinase Sevenless through
the protein Drk, a homolog of mammalian Grb2. In vivo structure-function
analysis revealed that the amino terminus of Sos was essential for its function
in flies. A molecule lacking the amino terminus was a potent dominant negative.
In contrast, a Sos fragment lacking the Drk binding sites was functional and its
activity was dependent on the presence of the Sevenless receptor. Furthermore,
membrane localization of Sos was independent of Drk. A possible role for Drk as
an activator of Sos is discussed and a Drk-independent interaction between Sos
and Sevenless is proposed that is likely mediated by the pleckstrin homology
domain within the amino terminus.
