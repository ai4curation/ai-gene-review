# Rhophilin-2 is targeted to late-endosomal structures of the vesicular machinery in the presence of activated RhoB.

## Abstract

1. Exp Cell Res. 2006 Dec 10;312(20):3981-9. doi: 10.1016/j.yexcr.2006.08.028.
Epub  2006 Sep 9.

Rhophilin-2 is targeted to late-endosomal structures of the vesicular machinery
in the presence of activated RhoB.

Steuve S(1), Devosse T, Lauwers E, Vanderwinden JM, André B, Courtoy PJ, Pirson
I.

Author information:
(1)Free University of Brussels-Campus Erasme, Institute of Interdisciplinary
Research, 808 route de Lennik-1070 Brussels, Belgium. ssteuve@ulb.ac.be

Rhophilin-2 or p76(RBE), a protein whose expression is induced by the cyclic AMP
pathway in thyrocytes, contains several protein-protein interaction domains
including HR-1, Bro1 and PDZ domains, and is a partner of RhoB in its GTP-bound
form (Eur J Biochem, 269(24): 6241-9, 2002). We here define its subcellular
localization and dissect the significance of its domains. By subcellular
fractionation and colocalization experiments, rhophilin-2 is recruited to
subcellular organelles by activated RhoB-GTP. As for its yeast homologue,
Npi3/Bro1p, the Bro1 domain of rhophilin-2 is necessary to its recruitment to
the vesicular structures, which are not labeled for EEA1 nor Lamp1, but well
with the late endosome marker CD63.
