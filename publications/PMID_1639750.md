# Genomic structure of human midkine (MK), a retinoic acid-responsive growth/differentiation factor.
**Authors:** Uehara K, Matsubara S, Kadomatsu K, Tsutsui J, Muramatsu T
**Journal:** J Biochem (1992)
**DOI:** [10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a123797](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a123797)

## Abstract

1. J Biochem. 1992 May;111(5):563-7. doi: 10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a123797.

Genomic structure of human midkine (MK), a retinoic acid-responsive
growth/differentiation factor.

Uehara K(1), Matsubara S, Kadomatsu K, Tsutsui J, Muramatsu T.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Faculty of Medicine, Kagoshima University.

Midkine (MK) is a product of a retinoic acid-responsive gene and a new
heparin-binding growth/differentiation factor. The coding sequence of human MK
was located on 1.5 kb DNA segment. The structure of the cloned human MK gene was
determined and compared with that of the mouse gene. The coding sequence was
divided into 4 exons, and each exon and exon-intron boundary was highly
homologous to those of the mouse. Furthermore, 170 bases in the upstream region
of the putative transcription initiation sites and 3 blocks of 200-350 bases in
regions further upstream were highly conserved. These sequences are likely to be
involved in developmentally regulated expression of MK.
