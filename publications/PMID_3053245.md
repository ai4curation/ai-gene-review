# Cloning a synthetic gene for human stefin B and its expression in E. coli.
**Authors:** Jerala R, Trstenjak M, Lenarcic B, Turk V
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1988)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0014-5793(88)80541-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(88)80541-6)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1988 Oct 24;239(1):41-4. doi: 10.1016/0014-5793(88)80541-6.

Cloning a synthetic gene for human stefin B and its expression in E. coli.

Jerala R(1), Trstenjak M, Lenarcic B, Turk V.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Jozef Stefan Institute, Ljubljana, Yugoslavia.

A gene coding for human stefin B was synthesized by the solid-phase phosphite
method and cloned in the pUC8 cloning vector. The insert with the verified DNA
sequence was subcloned into two expression vectors and expressed in E. coli as a
fusion protein with beta-galactosidase and as a native protein. The CNBr cleaved
fusion protein and the native recombinant stefin B were inhibitory to papain and
reacted with antibodies against human stefin B.
