A three-subunit Hap complex that interacts with the RNA polymerase II Elongator
was isolated from yeast. Deletions of genes for two Hap subunits, HAP1 and HAP3,
confer pGKL killer-insensitive and weak Elongator phenotypes. Preferential
interaction of the Hap complex with free rather than RNA polymerase
II-associated Elongator suggests a role in the regulation of Elongator activity.
