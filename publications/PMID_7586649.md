# Clinical and molecular analysis of a Japanese boy with Morquio B disease.
**Authors:** Ishii N, Oohira T, Oshima A, Sakuraba H, Endo F, Matsuda I, Sukegawa K, Orii T, Suzuki Y
**Journal:** Clin Genet (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1399-0004.1995.tb04065.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1399-0004.1995.tb04065.x)

## Abstract

1. Clin Genet. 1995 Aug;48(2):103-8. doi: 10.1111/j.1399-0004.1995.tb04065.x.

Clinical and molecular analysis of a Japanese boy with Morquio B disease.

Ishii N(1), Oohira T, Oshima A, Sakuraba H, Endo F, Matsuda I, Sukegawa K, Orii
T, Suzuki Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Clinical Genetics, Tokyo Metropolitan Institute of Medical
Science, Japan.

Morquio B disease was found in a 15-year-old Japanese boy who presented with
progressive generalized skeletal dysplasia without neurological manifestations.
Mild keratan sulfaturia was found, and beta-galactosidase was deficient in
fibroblasts. Gene analysis revealed two mutant alleles, 83Tyr-->His (Y83H) and
482Arg-->Cys (R482C). The former expressed a low enzyme activity (2-5% of
normal), and the latter expressed no detectable enzyme activity.
