Many plasma membrane proteins enter cells by clathrin-independent endocytosis
(CIE). Rab family small GTPases play pivotal roles in CIE and following
intracellular trafficking of cargo proteins. Here, we provide evidence that
TBC1D24, which contains an atypical Rab GAP domain, facilitates formation of
tubular recycling endosomes (TREs) that are a hallmark of the CIE cargo
trafficking pathway in HeLa cells. Overexpression of TBC1D24 in HeLa cells
dramatically increased TREs loaded with CIE cargo proteins, while deletion of
TBC1D24 impaired TRE formation and delayed the recycling of CIE cargo proteins
back to the plasma membrane. We also found that TBC1D24 binds to Rab22A, through
which TBC1D24 regulates TRE-mediated CIE cargo recycling. These findings provide
insight into regulatory mechanisms for CIE cargo trafficking.
