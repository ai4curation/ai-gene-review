Sulfoquinovose is produced by photosynthetic organisms at a rate of 10(10) tons
per annum and is degraded by bacteria as a source of carbon and sulfur. We have
identified Escherichia coli YihQ as the first dedicated sulfoquinovosidase and
the gateway enzyme to sulfoglycolytic pathways. Structural and mutagenesis
studies unveiled the sequence signatures for binding the distinguishing
sulfonate residue and revealed that sulfoquinovoside degradation is widespread
across the tree of life.
