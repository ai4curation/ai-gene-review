# Phage lysin LysK can be truncated to its CHAP domain and retain lytic activity against live antibiotic-resistant staphylococci.
**Authors:** Horgan M, O'Flynn G, Garry J, Cooney J, Coffey A, Fitzgerald GF, Ross RP, McAuliffe O
**Journal:** Appl Environ Microbiol (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1128/AEM.01831-08](https://doi.org/10.1128/AEM.01831-08)

## Abstract

1. Appl Environ Microbiol. 2009 Feb;75(3):872-4. doi: 10.1128/AEM.01831-08. Epub
2008 Dec 1.

Phage lysin LysK can be truncated to its CHAP domain and retain lytic activity
against live antibiotic-resistant staphylococci.

Horgan M(1), O'Flynn G, Garry J, Cooney J, Coffey A, Fitzgerald GF, Ross RP,
McAuliffe O.

Author information:
(1)Teagasc, Moorepark Food Research Centre, Fermoy, County Cork, Ireland.

A truncated derivative of the phage endolysin LysK containing only the CHAP
(cysteine- and histidine-dependent amidohydrolase/peptidase) domain exhibited
lytic activity against live clinical staphylococcal isolates, including
methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus. This is the first known report of a
truncated phage lysin which retains high lytic activity against live
staphylococcal cells.
