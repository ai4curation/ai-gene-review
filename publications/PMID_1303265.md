# Missense glucokinase mutation in maturity-onset diabetes of the young and mutation screening in late-onset diabetes.
**Authors:** Stoffel M, Patel P, Lo YM, Hattersley AT, Lucassen AM, Page R, Bell JI, Bell GI, Turner RC, Wainscoat JS
**Journal:** Nat Genet (1992)
**DOI:** [10.1038/ng1092-153](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng1092-153)

## Abstract

1. Nat Genet. 1992 Oct;2(2):153-6. doi: 10.1038/ng1092-153.

Missense glucokinase mutation in maturity-onset diabetes of the young and
mutation screening in late-onset diabetes.

Stoffel M(1), Patel P, Lo YM, Hattersley AT, Lucassen AM, Page R, Bell JI, Bell
GI, Turner RC, Wainscoat JS.

Author information:
(1)Howard Hughes Medical Institute, University of Chicago, Illinois 60637.

We describe a codon 299 mutation in the glucokinase gene in a British pedigree
with maturity-onset diabetes of the young (MODY) resulting in a substitution of
glycine to arginine. One out of fifty patients diagnosed with classical
late-onset type 2 diabetes mellitus was also found to have this mutation. All
nine relatives of this patient who have inherited the mutation have type 2
diabetes, although six others without the mutation are also present with
diabetes. The discovery that glucokinase mutations can cause MODY and was also
found in ten affected members of a pedigree with type 2 diabetes in which MODY
had not previously been considered indicates that diagnosis based on molecular
pathology will be helpful in understanding the aetiology of type 2 diabetes.
