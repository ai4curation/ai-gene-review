# Biochemical characterization of an ABC transporter LptBFGC complex required for the outer membrane sorting of lipopolysaccharides.
**Authors:** Narita S, Tokuda H
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2009.05.051](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2009.05.051)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2009 Jul 7;583(13):2160-4. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2009.05.051. Epub
 2009 Jun 3.

Biochemical characterization of an ABC transporter LptBFGC complex required for
the outer membrane sorting of lipopolysaccharides.

Narita S(1), Tokuda H.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Molecular and Cellular Biosciences, University of Tokyo, Tokyo,
Japan.

Seven Lpt proteins (A through G) are thought to be involved in
lipopolysaccharide transport from the inner to outer membrane of Escherichia
coli. LptB belongs to the ATP-binding cassette transporter superfamily. Although
the lptB gene lacks neighboring genes encoding membrane subunits, bioinformatic
analyses recently indicated that two distantly located consecutive genes, lptF
and lptG, could encode membrane subunits. To examine this possibility, LptB was
expressed with LptF and LptG. We report here that both LptF and LptG formed a
complex with LptB. Furthermore, an inner membrane protein, LptC, which had been
implicated in lipopolysaccharide transport, was also included in this complex.
