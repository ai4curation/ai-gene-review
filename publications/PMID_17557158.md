# The COL1A1 gene and high myopia susceptibility in Japanese.

The COL1A1 gene and high myopia susceptibility in Japanese.

Author information:
(1)Department of Ophthalmology, Yokohama City University School of Medicine,
Yokohama, Kanagawa, Japan.

The collagen type Iota alpha Iota (COL1A1) gene encodes the extracellular matrix
component, collagen, and resides in candidate MYP5 for high myopia on the
chromosome 17q22-q23.3. This locus has recently been implicated in playing an
important role in the pathogenesis of experimental myopia. We investigated the
association of disruptions of COL1A1 gene with high myopia by analyzing the
frequency of ten SNPs in a Japanese population of 330 subjects with high myopia
of -9.25 D or less and 330 randomized controls without high myopia. Two SNPs
(rs2075555 and rs2269336) were significantly associated with high myopia (P <
0.05, Pc < 0.1). Two different haplotype blocks in COL1A1 were observed by the
pair-wise linkage disequilibrium between the SNPs. The frequency of GGC/GGC
diplotype constructed by the three SNPs (rs2075555, rs2269336, rs1107946) was
significantly high (OR = 1.6) and associated with high myopia (P = 0.028, Pc<
0.084). Together our results provide the first evidence for COL1A1 as a gene
associated with high myopia.
