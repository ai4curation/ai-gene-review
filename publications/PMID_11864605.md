# RNAi related mechanisms affect both transcriptional and posttranscriptional transgene silencing in Drosophila.
**Authors:** Pal-Bhadra M, Bhadra U, Birchler JA
**Journal:** Mol Cell (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s1097-2765(02)00440-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1097-2765(02)00440-9)

## Abstract

1. Mol Cell. 2002 Feb;9(2):315-27. doi: 10.1016/s1097-2765(02)00440-9.

RNAi related mechanisms affect both transcriptional and posttranscriptional
transgene silencing in Drosophila.

Pal-Bhadra M(1), Bhadra U, Birchler JA.

Author information:
(1)Division of Biological Sciences, 117 Tucker Hall, University of Missouri,
Columbia, MO 65211, USA.

Two types of transgene silencing were found for the Alcohol dehydrogenase (Adh)
transcription unit. Transcriptional gene silencing (TGS) is Polycomb dependent
and occurs when Adh is driven by the white eye color gene promoter. Full-length
Adh transgenes are silenced posttranscriptionally at high copy number or by a
pulsed increase over a threshold. The posttranscriptional gene silencing (PTGS)
exhibits molecular hallmarks typical of RNA interference (RNAi), including the
production of 21--25 bp length sense and antisense RNAs homologous to the
silenced RNA. Mutations in piwi, which belongs to a gene family with members
required for RNAi, block PTGS and one aspect of TGS, indicating a connection
between the two types of silencing.
