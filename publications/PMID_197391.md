# Isolation and characterization of yeast mutants defective in intermediary carbon metabolism and in carbon catabolite derepression.
**Authors:** Ciriacy M
**Journal:** Mol Gen Genet (1977)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00330840](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00330840)

## Abstract

1. Mol Gen Genet. 1977 Jul 20;154(2):213-20. doi: 10.1007/BF00330840.

Isolation and characterization of yeast mutants defective in intermediary carbon
metabolism and in carbon catabolite derepression.

Ciriacy M.

Yeast mutants deficient in the constitutive ADHI (adc 1) were used for the
isolation of mutants with deficiencies of the intermediary carbon metabolism,
and of mutants defective in carbon catabolite derepression. Mutants were
recognized by their inability to grow on YEP-glycerol and/or on ethanol
synthetic complete medium. They were either defective in isocitrate lyase
(ic11), succinate dehydrogenase (sdh1), or malate dehydrogenase (mdh1, mdh2),
mdh-mutants could not uniformely be appointed to one of the known MDH isozymes.
Homozygous mdh and sdh1 diploids are unable to sporulate. Three gene loci could
be identified by mutants pleiotropically defective in many or all of the enzymes
tested In ccr 1 mutants, derepression of isocitrate lyase,
fructose-1,6-diphosphatase, ADHII and possibly of the cytoplasmic MDH is
prevented, whereas the mitochondrial TCA-cycle enzymes, succinate dehydrogenase
and malate dehydrogenase, are not significantly affected. CCR2 and CCR3 have
quite similar action spectra. Both genes are obviously necessary for
derepression of all enzymes tested. It could be shown that ccr1, ccr2 and ccr3
mutants are not respiratory deficient.
