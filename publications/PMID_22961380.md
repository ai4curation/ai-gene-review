# Human CWC22 escorts the helicase eIF4AIII to spliceosomes and promotes exon junction complex assembly.
**Authors:** Barbosa I, Haque N, Fiorini F, Barrandon C, Tomasetto C, Blanchette M, Le Hir H
**Journal:** Nat Struct Mol Biol (2012)
**DOI:** [10.1038/nsmb.2380](https://doi.org/10.1038/nsmb.2380)

## Abstract

1. Nat Struct Mol Biol. 2012 Oct;19(10):983-90. doi: 10.1038/nsmb.2380. Epub 2012
 Sep 9.

Human CWC22 escorts the helicase eIF4AIII to spliceosomes and promotes exon
junction complex assembly.

Barbosa I(1), Haque N, Fiorini F, Barrandon C, Tomasetto C, Blanchette M, Le Hir
H.

Author information:
(1)Institut de Biologie de l'Ecole Normale Supérieure, Centre National de la
Recherche Scientifique UMR8197, Paris, France.

The exon-junction complex (EJC) functionally links splicing to subsequent mRNA
localization, translation and stability. Sequence-independent binding of the EJC
core to RNA is ensured by the DEAD-box helicase eIF4AIII. Here, we identified
the splicing factor CWC22 as a new eIF4AIII partner in flies and humans. CWC22
coexists with eIF4AIII in large protein complexes distinct from EJCs.
Recombinant CWC22 directly contacts eIF4AIII and prevents it from binding RNA.
In vitro splicing assays revealed that CWC22 introduces eIF4AIII to spliceosomes
before remodeling to facilitate eIF4AIII incorporation into the EJC. Finally,
using knockdowns in vivo, we showed that CWC22 is essential for EJC assembly. We
elucidated the initial step of EJC assembly and the duality of CWC22 function
that hinders eIF4AIII from nonspecifically binding RNA and escorts it to the
splicing machinery to promote EJC assembly on mature mRNAs.
