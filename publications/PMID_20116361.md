The many faces of the mitochondrial TIM23 complex.

Mokranjac D(1), Neupert W.

Author information:
(1)Institute for Physiological Chemistry and Munich Center for Integrated
Protein Science CIPSM, Ludwig-Maximilians University, Butenandtstr. 5, 81377
Munich, Germany. dejana.mokranjac@med.uni-muenchen.de

The TIM23 complex in the inner membrane of mitochondria mediates import of
essentially all matrix proteins and a large number of inner membrane proteins.
Here we present an overview on the latest insights into the structure and
function of this remarkable molecular machine.
