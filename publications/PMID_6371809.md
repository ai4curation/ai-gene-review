# Identification of glial filament protein and vimentin in the same intermediate filament system in human glioma cells.
**Authors:** Wang E, Cairncross JG, Liem RK
**Journal:** Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (1984)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.81.7.2102](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.81.7.2102)
**PMC:** [PMC345445](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC345445/)

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1984 Apr;81(7):2102-6. doi: 10.1073/pnas.81.7.2102.

Identification of glial filament protein and vimentin in the same intermediate
filament system in human glioma cells.

Wang E, Cairncross JG, Liem RK.

We have used a human glioma cell line (U-251MG) to study the expression and
cytoplasmic organization of vimentin (decamin) and the glial filament protein
(GFP). Four clones of the parental U-251 cultures were isolated and found to
express GFP from 1-2% to 99% of the cells in the population. Double
immunofluorescence microscopy with antibodies to vimentin and GFP has shown
that, in all four clonal cell lines, vimentin-containing filaments are expressed
in most cells as an organized network and, in GFP-positive cells, GFP and
vimentin are associated with the same filament network. Immunoelectron
microscopy with specific antibodies labeled with colloidal gold particles of
various sizes shows that GFP and vimentin are localized in the same filaments.
These findings confirm in vitro studies of the copolymerization of subunits of
different biochemical nature into the same intermediate filament and suggest the
in vivo probability of the coassembly of GFP and vimentin from a possible
soluble pool of monomers.
