# A novel GABRG2 mutation associated with febrile seizures.

## Abstract

1. Neurology. 2006 Aug 22;67(4):687-90. doi: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000230145.73496.a2.

A novel GABRG2 mutation associated with febrile seizures.

Audenaert D(1), Schwartz E, Claeys KG, Claes L, Deprez L, Suls A, Van Dyck T,

Author information:
(1)Neurogenetics Group, Department of Molecular Genetics, Flanders

Mutations in the gene encoding the gamma2 subunit of the gamma-aminobutyric acid
type A receptor (GABRG2) have been reported to cause childhood absence epilepsy
(CAE), febrile seizures (FS), and generalized epilepsy with FS plus (GEFS+). The
authors analyzed GABRG2 in 47 unrelated patients with CAE, FS, and GEFS+ and
identified a novel mutation that cosegregated with FS. Electrophysiologic
studies demonstrated altered current desensitization and reduced benzodiazepine
enhancement in mutant receptors.
