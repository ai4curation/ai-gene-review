## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1990 Jul;172(7):4115-7. doi: 10.1128/jb.172.7.4115-4117.1990.

Enzymes of phosphoinositide synthesis in secretory vesicles destined for the
plasma membrane in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Kinney AJ(1), Carman GM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Food Science, Cook College, Rutgers University, New Brunswick,
New Jersey 08903.

CDP-diacylglycerol synthase, phosphatidylinositol synthase, and
phosphatidylinositol kinase activities were associated with post-Golgi apparatus
secretory vesicles destined for the plasma membrane of Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
These results suggest that the plasma membrane is capable of synthesizing both
CDP-diacylglycerol and phosphatidylinositol as well as phosphorylating
phosphatidylinositol.
