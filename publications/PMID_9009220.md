# Cloning and sequence of full-length cDNAs encoding the human neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (nAChR) subunits beta3 and beta4 and expression of seven nAChR subunits in the human neuroblastoma cell line SH-SY5Y and/or IMR-32.
**Authors:** Groot Kormelink PJ, Luyten WH
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(96)01383-x](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(96)01383-x)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1997 Jan 6;400(3):309-14. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(96)01383-x.

Cloning and sequence of full-length cDNAs encoding the human neuronal nicotinic
acetylcholine receptor (nAChR) subunits beta3 and beta4 and expression of seven
nAChR subunits in the human neuroblastoma cell line SH-SY5Y and/or IMR-32.

Groot Kormelink PJ(1), Luyten WH.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory for Molecular Biology, Celgen, University of Leuven, Belgium.
pgrootko@janbelcl.ssw.jnj.com

Using PCR-based techniques, we have isolated and sequenced the full-length cDNAs
that encode the human neuronal nAChR subunits alpha3,4,5,7 and beta2,3,4 in the
neuroblastoma cell lines SH-SY5Y and/or IMR-32. The predicted nAChR beta3- and
beta4-subunit proteins contain 458 and 498 amino acids, respectively. Except for
the beta2, all the other cloned cDNAs showed differences with the published
sequences. Northern blots show expression of the nAChR subunits alpha3,7 and
beta2,4 in the human neuroblastoma cell line SH-SY5Y, with intensity of the
hybridisation signal decreasing in the order alpha3 > beta4 > beta2 > alpha7.
