# Transcriptional activation of mouse cytosolic chaperonin CCT subunit genes by heat shock factors HSF1 and HSF2.
**Authors:** Kubota H, Matsumoto S, Yokota S, Yanagi H, Yura T
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(99)01437-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(99)01437-4)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1999 Nov 12;461(1-2):125-9. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(99)01437-4.

Transcriptional activation of mouse cytosolic chaperonin CCT subunit genes by
heat shock factors HSF1 and HSF2.

Kubota H(1), Matsumoto S, Yokota S, Yanagi H, Yura T.

Author information:
(1)HSP Research Institute, Kyoto Research Park, 17 Chudoji Minami-machi,
Shimogyo-ku, Kyoto, Japan. kubota@hsp.co.jp

The chaperonin containing TCP-1 (CCT) is a eukaryotic molecular chaperone
consisting of eight subunit species and assists in the folding of cytosolic
proteins. We show here that all eight mouse CCT subunit genes contain sequences
called heat shock elements for binding heat shock transcription factors (HSFs)
by electrophoretic mobility shift assays and that these genes are
transcriptionally activated by HSFs in reporter gene assays using HeLa cells
transiently overexpressing HSFs. These results suggest that HSF1 and/or HSF2
play a role in Cct gene expression.
