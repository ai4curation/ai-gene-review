# Foxh1 is essential for development of the anterior heart field.
**Authors:** von Both I, Silvestri C, Erdemir T, Lickert H, Walls JR, Henkelman RM, Rossant J, Harvey RP, Attisano L, Wrana JL
**Journal:** Dev Cell (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.devcel.2004.07.023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2004.07.023)

## Abstract

1. Dev Cell. 2004 Sep;7(3):331-45. doi: 10.1016/j.devcel.2004.07.023.

Foxh1 is essential for development of the anterior heart field.

von Both I(1), Silvestri C, Erdemir T, Lickert H, Walls JR, Henkelman RM,
Rossant J, Harvey RP, Attisano L, Wrana JL.

Author information:
(1)Samuel Lunenfeld Research Institute, Mount Sinai Hospital, 600 University

The anterior heart field (AHF) mediates formation of the outflow tract (OFT) and
right ventricle (RV) during looping morphogenesis of the heart. Foxh1 is a
forkhead DNA binding transcription factor in the TGFbeta-Smad pathway. Here we
demonstrate that Foxh1-/- mutant mouse embryos form a primitive heart tube, but
fail to form OFT and RV and display loss of outer curvature markers of the
future working myocardium, similar to the phenotype of Mef2c-/- mutant hearts.
Further, we show that Mef2c is a direct target of Foxh1, which physically and
functionally interacts with Nkx2-5 to mediate strong Smad-dependent activation
of a TGFbeta response element in the Mef2c gene. This element directs transgene
expression to the presumptive AHF, as well as the RV and OFT, a pattern that
closely parallels endogenous Mef2c expression in the heart. Thus, Foxh1 and
Nkx2-5 functionally interact and are essential for development of the AHF and
its derivatives, the RV and OFT, in response to TGFbeta-like signals.
