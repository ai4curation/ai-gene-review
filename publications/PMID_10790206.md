Novel mutations in 13 probands with galactokinase deficiency.

Kolosha V(1), Anoia E, de Cespedes C, Gitzelmann R, Shih L, Casco T, Saborio M,
Trejos R, Buist N, Tedesco T, Skach W, Mitelmann O, Ledee D, Huang K, Stambolian

Author information:
(1)Department of Ophthalmology, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia,
Pennsylvania 19104, USA.

Galactokinase is an essential enzyme in the metabolism of galactose. Patients
with deficiencies in galactokinase exhibit early-onset cataracts. We examined
the sequence of the human galactokinase gene (GK1) from 13 patients exhibiting
galactokinase deficiency and identified 12 novel mutations. One of the mutations
occurred in six of the 13 probands examined, and the remaining 11 were unique
mutations. Expression of each of the mutant GK1 genes in Xenopus oocytes
resulted in very low galactokinase activity levels. These results provide
important information regarding the types of GK1 mutations that occur in the
human population.
