# Functional differentiation of structurally similar membrane subunits of the ABC transporter LolCDE complex.
**Authors:** Mizutani M, Mukaiyama K, Xiao J, Mori M, Satou R, Narita S, Okuda S, Tokuda H
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2013)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2012.11.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2012.11.009)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2013 Jan 4;587(1):23-9. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2012.11.009. Epub
2012  Nov 24.

Functional differentiation of structurally similar membrane subunits of the ABC
transporter LolCDE complex.

Mizutani M(1), Mukaiyama K, Xiao J, Mori M, Satou R, Narita S, Okuda S, Tokuda
H.

Author information:
(1)Faculty of Nutritional Sciences, University of Morioka, Takizawa, Japan.

A photo-sensitive amino acid analogue was introduced into an outer membrane
lipoprotein, Pal, and then subjected to photo-crosslinking with the
lipoprotein-specific ABC transporter LolCDE. Pal crosslinked to LolE but not
LolC in vivo despite that both are structurally similar membrane subunits.
LolCDE liganded with Pal containing the photo-sensitive amino acid analogue was
isolated and subjected to in vitro photo-crosslinking. LolE was found to be the
binding site for Pal. ATP binding to LolD decreased the LolE-Pal crosslinking by
decreasing their hydrophobic interaction. ATP hydrolysis in the presence of LolA
completely abolished the LolE-Pal crosslinking and, concomitantly, generated a
new LolA-Pal crosslinked product.
