# Activation of endogenous Cdc42 visualized in living cells.
**Authors:** Nalbant P, Hodgson L, Kraynov V, Toutchkine A, Hahn KM
**Journal:** Science (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.1100367](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1100367)

## Abstract

1. Science. 2004 Sep 10;305(5690):1615-9. doi: 10.1126/science.1100367.

Activation of endogenous Cdc42 visualized in living cells.

Nalbant P(1), Hodgson L, Kraynov V, Toutchkine A, Hahn KM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmacology, University of North Carolina School of Medicine,
Chapel Hill, NC 27599-7365, USA.

Signaling proteins are tightly regulated spatially and temporally to perform
multiple functions. For Cdc42 and other guanosine triphosphatases, the
subcellular location of activation is a critical determinant of cell behavior.
However, current approaches are limited in their ability to examine the dynamics
of Cdc42 activity in living cells. We report the development of a biosensor
capable of visualizing the changing activation of endogenous, unlabeled Cdc42 in
living cells. With the use of a dye that reports protein interactions, the
biosensor revealed localized activation in the trans-Golgi apparatus,
microtubule-dependent Cdc42 activation at the cell periphery, and activation
kinetics precisely coordinated with cell extension and retraction.
