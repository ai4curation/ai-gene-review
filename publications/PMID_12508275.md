# Size isn't everything.
**Authors:** Tyler D, Baker NE
**Journal:** Bioessays (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1002/bies.10211](https://doi.org/10.1002/bies.10211)

## Abstract

1. Bioessays. 2003 Jan;25(1):5-8. doi: 10.1002/bies.10211.

Size isn't everything.

Tyler D(1), Baker NE.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Genetics, Albert Einstein College of Medicine, Bronx, 
NY 10461, USA.

Much progress has been made recently towards uncovering the mechanisms that 
control the size to which organisms and their organs grow, and identifying some 
of the genes responsible. Size control, however, is only half of the equation. 
In growing to the right size, tissues must also grow to the right shape. A 
recent paper suggests that a hitherto overlooked cellular behaviour governs the 
size and shape of a growing tissue, and issues a challenge to developmental 
biologists to identify the molecular mechanisms involved.
