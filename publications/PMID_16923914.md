# Genetic evidence for an interaction of the UbiG O-methyltransferase with UbiX in Escherichia coli coenzyme Q biosynthesis.
**Authors:** Gulmezian M, Zhang H, Javor GT, Clarke CF
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1128/JB.00668-06](https://doi.org/10.1128/JB.00668-06)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 2006 Sep;188(17):6435-9. doi: 10.1128/JB.00668-06.

Genetic evidence for an interaction of the UbiG O-methyltransferase with UbiX in
Escherichia coli coenzyme Q biosynthesis.

Gulmezian M(1), Zhang H, Javor GT, Clarke CF.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry and the Molecular Biology Institute,
University of California, Los Angeles, California 90095-1569, USA.

IS16 is a thiol-sensitive, Q-deficient mutant strain of Escherichia coli. Here,
we show that IS16 harbors a mutation in the ubiG gene encoding a
methyltransferase required for two O-methylation steps of Q biosynthesis.
Complementation of IS16 with either ubiG or ubiX(K-12) reverses this phenotype,
suggesting that UbiX may interact with UbiG.
