# A role for Pyk2 and Src in linking G-protein-coupled receptors with MAP kinase activation.
**Authors:** Dikic I, Tokiwa G, Lev S, Courtneidge SA, Schlessinger J
**Journal:** Nature (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1038/383547a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/383547a0)

## Abstract

1. Nature. 1996 Oct 10;383(6600):547-50. doi: 10.1038/383547a0.

A role for Pyk2 and Src in linking G-protein-coupled receptors with MAP kinase
activation.

Dikic I(1), Tokiwa G, Lev S, Courtneidge SA, Schlessinger J.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmacology, New York University Medical Center, 10016, USA.

The mechanisms by which mitogenic G-protein-coupled receptors activate the MAP
kinase signalling pathway are poorly understood. Candidate protein tyrosine
kinases that link G-protein-coupled receptors with MAP kinase include Src family
kinases, the epidermal growth factor receptor, Lyn and Syk. Here we show that
lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) and bradykinin induce tyrosine phosphorylation of
Pyk2 and complex formation between Pyk2 and activated Src. Moreover, tyrosine
phosphorylation of Pyk2 leads to binding of the SH2 domain of Src to tyrosine
402 of Pyk2 and activation of Src. Transient overexpression of a dominant
interfering mutant of Pyk2 or the protein tyrosine kinase Csk reduces LPA- or
bradykinin-induced activation of MAP kinase. LPA- or bradykinin-induced MAP
kinase activation was also inhibited by overexpression of dominant interfering
mutants of Grb2 and Sos. We propose that Pyk2 acts with Src to link Gi- and
Gq-coupled receptors with Grb2 and Sos to activate the MAP kinase signalling
pathway in PC12 cells.
