# Isolation and characterization of two human H1 histone genes within clusters of core histone genes.
**Authors:** Albig W, Kardalinou E, Drabent B, Zimmer A, Doenecke D
**Journal:** Genomics (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0888-7543(91)90183-f](https://doi.org/10.1016/0888-7543(91)90183-f)

## Abstract

1. Genomics. 1991 Aug;10(4):940-8. doi: 10.1016/0888-7543(91)90183-f.

Isolation and characterization of two human H1 histone genes within clusters of
core histone genes.

Albig W(1), Kardalinou E, Drabent B, Zimmer A, Doenecke D.

Author information:
(1)Institut für Biochemie, Universität Göttingen, Federal Republic of Germany.

Two human H1 histone genes, termed H1.3 and H1.4, were isolated from two cosmid
clones. The H1.4 gene is associated with an H2B gene, whereas genes coding for
all four core histones are located in the vicinity of the H1.3 gene. This
cluster arrangement was found both in the two cosmid clones and on overlapping
bacteriophage clones isolated from an EMBL3 library. In continuation of our
previous analysis of two human H1 genes, this analysis raises the number of
completely sequenced H1 histone genes within clusters of core histone genes to
four.
