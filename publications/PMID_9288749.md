Wnt signaling polarizes an early C. elegans blastomere to distinguish endoderm
from mesoderm.

Comment in
    Cell. 1997 Aug 22;90(4):581-4. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80517-6.

A polarizing signal induces endoderm production by a 4-cell stage blastomere in
C. elegans called EMS. We identified 16 mutations in five genes, mom-1 through
mom-5, required for EMS to produce endoderm. mom-1, mom-2, and mom-3 are
required in the signaling cell, P2, while mom-4 is required in EMS. P2 signaling
downregulates an HMG domain protein, POP-1, in one EMS daughter. The sequence of
mom-2 predicts that it encodes a member of the Wnt family of secreted
glycoproteins, which in other systems activate HMG domain proteins. Defective
mitotic spindle orientations in mom mutant embryos indicate that Wnt signaling
influences cytoskeletal polarity in blastomeres throughout the early embryo.
