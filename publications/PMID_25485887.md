## Abstract

Molecular investigation of iron-sulfur cluster assembly scaffolds under stress.

Blanc B(1), Clémancey M, Latour JM, Fontecave M, Ollagnier de Choudens S.

Author information:
(1)Université Grenoble Alpes , iRTSV-LCBM, F-38000 Grenoble, France.

Fe/S biosynthesis is controlled in Escherichia coli by two machineries, the
housekeeping ISC machinery and the SUF system that is functional under stress
conditions. Despite many in vivo studies showing that SUF is more adapted for
Fe/S assembly under stress, no molecular data supporting this concept have been
provided so far. This work focuses on molecular studies of key actors in Fe/S
assembly, the SufB and IscU scaffolds under oxidative stress and iron
limitation. We show that the IscU Fe2S2 cluster is less stable than the SufB
Fe2S2 cluster in the presence of hydrogen peroxide, oxygen, and an iron
chelator.
