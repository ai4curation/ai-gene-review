# Dysgenesis of cephalic neural crest derivatives in Pax7-/- mutant mice.
**Authors:** Mansouri A, Stoykova A, Torres M, Gruss P
**Journal:** Development (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1242/dev.122.3.831](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.122.3.831)

## Abstract

1. Development. 1996 Mar;122(3):831-8. doi: 10.1242/dev.122.3.831.

Dysgenesis of cephalic neural crest derivatives in Pax7-/- mutant mice.

Mansouri A(1), Stoykova A, Torres M, Gruss P.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Cell Biology, Max Planck Institute for Biophysical
Chemistry, Göttingen, Germany.

Pax7 is a member of the paired box containing gene family. Its expression
pattern suggests a function in cephalic neural crest derivatives, skeletal
muscle and central nervous system development. To understand the role of Pax7
during mouse embryogenesis, we used the homologous recombination technique in
embryonic stem cells and generated Pax7-/- mice. Homozygous animals are born but
die shortly afer weaning. They exhibit malformations in facial structures
involving the maxilla and nose. Our analysis suggests that the observed
phenotype is due to a cephalic neural crest defect. No obvious phenotype could
be detected in the central nervous system and skeletal muscle. Functional
redundancy between Pax7 and Pax3 is discussed.
