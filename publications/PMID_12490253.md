Drosophila melanogaster has been introduced recently as a model organism in
which to study the mechanisms by which drugs of abuse change behavior and by
which the nervous system changes upon repeated drug exposure. Surprising
similarities between flies and mammals have begun to emerge at the behavioral,
neurochemical and molecular levels.
