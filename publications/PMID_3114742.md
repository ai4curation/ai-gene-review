# A second form of the beta subunit of signal-transducing G proteins.
**Authors:** Gao B, Gilman AG, Robishaw JD
**Journal:** Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (1987)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.84.17.6122](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.84.17.6122)

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1987 Sep;84(17):6122-5. doi:
10.1073/pnas.84.17.6122.

A second form of the beta subunit of signal-transducing G proteins.

Gao B, Gilman AG, Robishaw JD.

A complementary DNA (cDNA) has been isolated that encodes a second form of the
beta subunit of signal-transducing guanine nucleotide-binding regulatory
proteins (G proteins). The cDNA corresponds to a 1.8-kilobase mRNA, and
nucleotide sequence analysis indicates that the encoded polypeptide consists of
340-amino acid residues with a Mr of 37,335. Although the deduced polypeptide is
the same size as that reported previously for the beta subunit, 10% of the amino
acid residues are different. Furthermore, the 5' and 3' non-translated regions
of this cDNA show no significant homology with those reported previously for
cDNAs that encode the beta subunit. These data refute prior conclusions that
there is only one form of the G protein beta subunit.
