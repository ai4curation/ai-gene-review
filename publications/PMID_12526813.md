# Epithelial tube morphology is determined by the polarized growth and delivery of apical membrane.
**Authors:** Myat MM, Andrew DJ
**Journal:** Cell (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0092-8674(02)01140-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(02)01140-6)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 2002 Dec 13;111(6):879-91. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(02)01140-6.

Epithelial tube morphology is determined by the polarized growth and delivery of 
apical membrane.

Myat MM(1), Andrew DJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Cell Biology, Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine, 725 
N. Wolfe Street, Baltimore, MD 21205, USA.

Formation of tubes of the correct size and shape is essential for viability of 
most organisms, yet little is understood of the mechanisms controlling tube 
morphology. We identified a new allele of hairy in a mutagenesis screen and 
showed that hairy mutations cause branching and bulging of the normally 
unbranched salivary tube, in part through prolonged expression of huckebein 
(hkb). HKB controls polarized cell shape change and apical membrane growth 
during salivary cell invagination via two downstream target genes, crumbs (crb), 
a determinant of the apical membrane, and klarsicht (klar), which mediates 
microtubule-dependent organelle transport. In invaginating salivary cells, crb 
and klar mediate growth and delivery of apical membrane, respectively, thus 
regulating the size and shape of the salivary tube.
