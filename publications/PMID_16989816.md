# Modulation of LRP6-mediated Wnt signaling by molecular chaperone Mesd.
**Authors:** Li Y, Lu W, He X, Bu G
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2006.09.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2006.09.011)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2006 Oct 2;580(22):5423-8. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2006.09.011. Epub
 2006 Sep 18.

Modulation of LRP6-mediated Wnt signaling by molecular chaperone Mesd.

Li Y(1), Lu W, He X, Bu G.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatrics, Washington University School of Medicine and St.
Louis Children's Hospital, MO 63110, USA.

LRP6 is a Wnt coreceptor at the cell surface. Here, we report that a specialized
molecular chaperone Mesd modulates LRP6-mediated Wnt signaling and how different
LRP6 mutants exhibit differential effects on Wnt signaling. We found that
overexpression of increasing amounts of the full-length LRP6 enhances Wnt
signaling in a dose dependent manner only in the presence of a co-expression of
the molecular chaperone Mesd, which promotes LRP6 folding and maturation to the
cell surface. We also demonstrated that LRP6 mutant lacking the intracellular
domain impedes LRP6 cell surface expression and Wnt signaling in a
dominant-negative fashion by sequestering Mesd from promoting LRP6 folding. Our
results present novel mechanisms by which Mesd and LRP6 modulate Wnt signaling.
