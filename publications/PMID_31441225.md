# S-acylation of CBL10/SCaBP8 by PAT10 is crucial for its tonoplast association and function in salt tolerance.
**Authors:** Chai S, Ge FR, Zhang Y, Li S
**Journal:** J Integr Plant Biol (2020)
**DOI:** [10.1111/jipb.12864](https://doi.org/10.1111/jipb.12864)

## Abstract

1. J Integr Plant Biol. 2020 Jun;62(6):718-722. doi: 10.1111/jipb.12864. Epub
2019  Oct 24.

S-acylation of CBL10/SCaBP8 by PAT10 is crucial for its tonoplast association
and function in salt tolerance.

Chai S(1), Ge FR(1), Zhang Y(1), Li S(1).

Author information:
(1)College of Life Sciences, State Key Laboratory of Crop Biology, Shandong
Agricultural University, Tai'an, 271000, China.

Crop yield is sensitive to salt stresses, for which Calcineurin B-like proteins
(CBLs) are major response factors. This study shows that Arabidopsis CBL10,
through protein S-acylation by protein S-acyl transferase10, targets to the
vacuolar membrane to confer salt tolerance.
