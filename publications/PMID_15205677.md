# Tob38, a novel essential component in the biogenesis of beta-barrel proteins of mitochondria.

## Abstract

18.

Tob38, a novel essential component in the biogenesis of beta-barrel proteins of
mitochondria.

Waizenegger T(1), Habib SJ, Lech M, Mokranjac D, Paschen SA, Hell K, Neupert W,
Rapaport D.

Author information:
(1)Institut für Physiologische Chemie der Universität München, Butenandtstrasse
5, Haus B, 81377 Munich, Germany.

Insertion of beta-barrel proteins into the outer membrane of mitochondria is
mediated by the TOB complex. Known constituents of this complex are Tob55 and
Mas37. We identified a novel component, Tob38. It is essential for viability of
yeast and the function of the TOB complex. Tob38 is exposed on the surface of
the mitochondrial outer membrane. It interacts with Mas37 and Tob55 and is
associated with Tob55 even in the absence of Mas37. The Tob38-Tob55 core complex
binds precursors of beta-barrel proteins and facilitates their insertion into
the outer membrane. Depletion of Tob38 results in strongly reduced levels of
Tob55 and Mas37 and the residual proteins no longer form a complex.
Tob38-depleted mitochondria are deficient in the import of beta-barrel precursor
proteins, but not of other outer membrane proteins or proteins of other
mitochondrial subcompartments. We conclude that Tob38 has a crucial function in
the biogenesis of beta-barrel proteins of mitochondria.
