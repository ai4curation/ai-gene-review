# CcmD is involved in complex formation between CcmC and the heme chaperone CcmE during cytochrome c maturation.
**Authors:** Ahuja U, Thöny-Meyer L
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M410912200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M410912200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2005 Jan 7;280(1):236-43. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M410912200. Epub 2004
Oct 28.

CcmD is involved in complex formation between CcmC and the heme chaperone CcmE
during cytochrome c maturation.

Ahuja U(1), Thöny-Meyer L.

Author information:
(1)Institut für Mikrobiologie, Eidgenössische Technische Hochschule,
Wolfgang-Pauli-Str. 10, CH-8093 Zürich, Switzerland.

CcmD is a small membrane protein involved in heme delivery to the heme chaperone
CcmE during cytochrome c maturation. Here we show that it physically interacts
with CcmE and CcmC, another essential component of the heme delivery system. We
demonstrate the formation of a ternary complex consisting of CcmCDE. A deletion
analysis of individual domains revealed that the central hydrophobic domain is
essential for its function. Moreover, the C-terminal, cytoplasmic domain seems
to require a net positive charge to be functional. Our topology analysis
indicates that CcmD is an integral interfacial membrane protein with its N and C
termini extruding into the cytoplasmic side of the membrane. Interactions of
CcmD with either ferrochelatase, the last heme biosynthetic enzyme, or directly
with heme were not detectable. We postulate a function for CcmD in
protein-protein interaction or membrane protein assembly required for the heme
delivery process.
