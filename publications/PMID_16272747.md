# A fission yeast SNAP-25 homologue, SpSec9, is essential for cytokinesis and sporulation.
**Authors:** Nakamura T, Kashiwazaki J, Shimoda C
**Journal:** Cell Struct Funct (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1247/csf.30.15](https://doi.org/10.1247/csf.30.15)

## Abstract

1. Cell Struct Funct. 2005;30(2):15-24. doi: 10.1247/csf.30.15.

A fission yeast SNAP-25 homologue, SpSec9, is essential for cytokinesis and
sporulation.

Nakamura T(1), Kashiwazaki J, Shimoda C.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, Graduate School of Science, Osaka City University,
Osaka 558-8585, Japan. taronaka@sci.osaka-cu.ac.jp

The soluble NSF attachment protein 25 (SNAP-25) is a component of the SNARE
complex that is essential for regulated exocytosis in diverse cell types. Here,
we identified a fission yeast SNAP-25 homologue, SpSec9. The sec9+ gene was
essential for vegetative growth. sec9 mRNA was detected in vegetative cells and
further increased during sporulation. This increase during sporulation was
dependent on Mei4, a meiosis-specific transcription factor. A
sporulation-deficient sec9 mutant was isolated by random PCR mutagenesis
(sec9-10). The sec9-10 mutant also exhibited temperature sensitivity for growth
and cell division was found to arrest before completion of cell separation at
restrictive temperatures. In sec9-10 cells, the forespore membrane was normally
initiated near spindle pole bodies during meiosis II. However, subsequent
extension of the membrane was severely impaired. These results indicate that
SpSec9 plays an important role both in cytokinesis and in sporulation.
