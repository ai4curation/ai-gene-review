Myoregulin is a recently discovered micropeptide that controls calcium levels by
inhibiting the intracellular calcium pump sarco-endoplasmic reticulum
Ca2+-ATPase (SERCA). Keeping calcium levels balanced in the heart is essential
for normal heart functioning, thus myoregulin has the potential to be a crucial
regulator of cardiac muscle performance by reducing the rate of intracellular
Ca2+ uptake. We provide the first report of myoregulin mRNA expression in human
heart tissue, absence of expression in human plasma, and the effects of
myoregulin on cardiac hemodynamics in an ex vivo Langendorff isolated rat heart
model of ischemia/reperfusion. In this preliminary study, myoregulin provided a
cardio-protective effect, as assessed by preservation of left ventricular
contractility and relaxation, during ischemia/reperfusion. This study provides
the foundation for future research in this area.
