# Membrane localization and function of Vav3 in T cells depend on its association with the adapter SLP-76.
**Authors:** Charvet C, Canonigo AJ, Billadeau DD, Altman A
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M500275200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M500275200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2005 Apr 15;280(15):15289-99. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M500275200. Epub
2005 Feb 11.

Membrane localization and function of Vav3 in T cells depend on its association
with the adapter SLP-76.

Charvet C(1), Canonigo AJ, Billadeau DD, Altman A.

Author information:
(1)Division of Cell Biology, La Jolla Institute for Allergy and Immunology, San
Diego, California 92121, USA. charvet@liai.org

The Vav family of guanine exchange factors plays a critical role in lymphocyte
proliferation, cytoskeletal reorganization, and gene transcription upon
immunoreceptor engagement. Although the role of Vav1 in T cells is well
documented, the role of Vav3 is less clear. We investigated the subcellular
localization of Vav3 during T cell activation. We report here that
phosphorylation of Vav3 on tyrosine residue Tyr(173) is not required for T cell
receptor (TCR)-induced Vav3 membrane translocation or immunological synapse (IS)
recruitment, but mutation of this residue enhanced TCR-induced nuclear factor of
activated T cells (NFAT) activation. However, Vav3 mutants either containing an
Src homology 2 (SH2)-disabled point mutation (R697L) or lacking its SH3-SH2-SH3
domains were unable to bind SLP-76 did not translocate to the membrane or to the
IS and furthermore failed to activate NFAT. Importantly, the membrane
translocation of Vav3 was abrogated in Lck, ZAP-70, LAT, and SLP-76-deficient T
cells, where Vav3 binding to SLP-76 was disrupted. Finally, we confirmed and
underlined the critical role of Vav3 in NFAT activation by knocking down Vav3
expression in Vav1-deficient T cells. Altogether, our data show that TCR-induced
association of Vav3 with SLP-76 is required for its membrane/IS localization and
function.
