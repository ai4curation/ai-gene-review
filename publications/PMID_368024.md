# Five different enzymatic activities are associated with the multienzyme complex of fatty acid oxidation from Escherichia coli.
**Authors:** Pramanik A, Pawar S, Antonian E, Schulz H
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (1979)
**DOI:** [10.1128/jb.137.1.469-473.1979](https://doi.org/10.1128/jb.137.1.469-473.1979)
**PMC:** [PMC218472](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC218472/)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1979 Jan;137(1):469-73. doi: 10.1128/jb.137.1.469-473.1979.

Five different enzymatic activities are associated with the multienzyme complex
of fatty acid oxidation from Escherichia coli.

Pramanik A, Pawar S, Antonian E, Schulz H.

The purified multienzyme complex of fatty acid oxidation from Escherichia coli
was found to possess 3-hydroxyacyl-coenzyme A (CoA) epimerase and
cis-delta3-trans-delta2-enoyl-CoA isomerase activities in addition to the
previously identified enoyl-CoA hydratase, L-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase,
and 3-ketoactyl-CoA thiolase activities. Evidence is presented in support of the
proposed association of all five enzyme activities with one protein which
apparently is composed of two types of subunits and which can exist in several
aggregated forms. The five component enzymes of the complex were rapidly
inactivated by tris(hydroxymethyl)aminomethane, whereas they remained active in
the presence of potassium phosphate.
