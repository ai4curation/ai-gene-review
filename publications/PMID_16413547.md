# LATS2-Ajuba complex regulates gamma-tubulin recruitment to centrosomes and spindle organization during mitosis.
**Authors:** Abe Y, Ohsugi M, Haraguchi K, Fujimoto J, Yamamoto T
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2005.12.096](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2005.12.096)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2006 Feb 6;580(3):782-8. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2005.12.096. Epub
2006 Jan 9.

LATS2-Ajuba complex regulates gamma-tubulin recruitment to centrosomes and
spindle organization during mitosis.

Abe Y(1), Ohsugi M, Haraguchi K, Fujimoto J, Yamamoto T.

Author information:
(1)Division of Oncology, Department of Cancer Biology, Institute of Medical
Science, University of Tokyo, 4-6-1, Shirokanedai, Tokyo 108-8639, Japan.

LATS2 is a human homolog of Drosophila tumor suppressor lats/warts, and encodes
a mitotic kinase whose physiological roles remain to be elucidated. We performed
yeast two-hybrid screening and identified a LIM protein Ajuba, as a binding
partner of LATS2. LATS2 was localized to the centrosomes throughout the cell
cycle and was associated with Ajuba during mitosis, contributing to latter's
mitotic phosphorylation. Depletion of LATS2 or Ajuba impaired centrosomal
accumulation of gamma-tubulin and spindle formation at the onset of mitosis,
suggesting that the LATS2-Ajuba complex regulates organization of the spindle
apparatus through recruitment of gamma-tubulin to the centrosome.
