Heterochromatin protein 1 (HP1) proteins are chromatin-bound transcriptional
regulators. While their chromodomain binds histone H3 methylated on lysine 9,
their chromoshadow domain associates with the H3 histone fold in a region
involved in chromatin remodeling. Here, we show that phosphorylation at histone
H3 threonine 45 and serine 57 within this latter region differentially affects
binding of the three mammalian HP1 isoforms HP1α, HP1β and HP1γ. Both
phosphorylation events are dependent on the activity of the DYRK1A kinase that
antagonizes HP1-mediated transcriptional repression and participates in abnormal
activation of cytokine genes in Down's syndrome-associated megakaryoblastic
leukemia.
