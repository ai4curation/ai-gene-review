The nucleotide sequence and deduced amino acid sequence of the cytochrome cL
gene of Methylobacterium extorquens AM1, a novel class of c-type cytochrome.

Nunn DN(1), Anthony C.

Author information:
(1)Biochemistry Department, University of Southampton, U.K.

The nucleotide sequence and deduced amino acid sequence of the cytochrome cL of
Methylobacterium extorquens (Pseudomonas AM1; Methylobacterium AM1) shows that
this cytochrome c is completely different, except for its haem-binding site,
from all other cytochromes.
