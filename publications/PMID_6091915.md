# The human LDL receptor: a cysteine-rich protein with multiple Alu sequences in its mRNA.
**Authors:** Yamamoto T, Davis CG, Brown MS, Schneider WJ, Casey ML, Goldstein JL, Russell DW
**Journal:** Cell (1984)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0092-8674(84)90188-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0092-8674(84)90188-0)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 1984 Nov;39(1):27-38. doi: 10.1016/0092-8674(84)90188-0.

The human LDL receptor: a cysteine-rich protein with multiple Alu sequences in
its mRNA.

Yamamoto T, Davis CG, Brown MS, Schneider WJ, Casey ML, Goldstein JL, Russell
DW.

The nucleotide sequence of a cloned 5.3 kilobase cDNA for the human low density
lipoprotein receptor revealed five domains in the 839 amino acid protein: 322
NH2-terminal amino acids, extremely rich in disulfide-bonded cysteine residues
(15%) and including an 8-fold repeat of 40 residues that may contain the LDL
binding site; 350 residues homologous to the precursor of mouse epidermal growth
factor; a region immediately outside the plasma membrane, rich in serine and
threonine and the site of O-linked glycosylation; 22 hydrophobic amino acids,
spanning the plasma membrane; and 50 COOH-terminal amino acids, projecting into
the cytoplasm. The mRNA for the receptor contains a 3' untranslated region of
2.5 kilobases that includes multiple copies of the Alu family of repetitive
DNAs. Transfection of simian COS cells with the human LDL receptor cDNA linked
to the SV40 early promoter resulted in expression of functional cell surface
receptors.
