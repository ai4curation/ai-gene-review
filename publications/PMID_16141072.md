This study describes comprehensive polling of transcription start and
termination sites and analysis of previously unidentified full-length
complementary DNAs derived from the mouse genome. We identify the 5' and 3'
boundaries of 181,047 transcripts with extensive variation in transcripts
arising from alternative promoter usage, splicing, and polyadenylation. There
are 16,247 new mouse protein-coding transcripts, including 5154 encoding
previously unidentified proteins. Genomic mapping of the transcriptome reveals
transcriptional forests, with overlapping transcription on both strands,
separated by deserts in which few transcripts are observed. The data provide a
comprehensive platform for the comparative analysis of mammalian transcriptional
regulation in differentiation and development.
