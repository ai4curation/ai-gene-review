# Hoxa1 and Krox-20 synergize to control the development of rhombomere 3.
**Authors:** Helmbacher F, Pujades C, Desmarquet C, Frain M, Rijli FM, Chambon P, Charnay P
**Journal:** Development (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1242/dev.125.23.4739](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.125.23.4739)

## Abstract

1. Development. 1998 Dec;125(23):4739-48. doi: 10.1242/dev.125.23.4739.

Hoxa1 and Krox-20 synergize to control the development of rhombomere 3.

Helmbacher F(1), Pujades C, Desmarquet C, Frain M, Rijli FM, Chambon P, Charnay
P.

Author information:
(1)Unité 368 de l'Institut National de la Santé et de la Recherche Médicale,

The transcription factor genes Hoxa1 and Krox-20 have been shown to play
important roles in vertebrate hindbrain segmentation. In this report, we present
evidence for novel functions of these genes which co-operate in specifying
cellular identity in rhombomere (r) 3. Although Hoxa1 has not been observed to
be expressed rostrally to the prospective r3/r4 boundary, its inactivation
results in (i) the appearance of patches of cells presenting an r2-like
molecular identity within r3, (ii) early neuronal differentiation in r3,
normally characteristic of even-numbered rhombomeres, and (iii) abnormal
navigation of r3 motor axons, similar to that observed in even-numbered
rhombomeres. These phenotypic manifestations become more severe in the context
of the additional inactivation of one allele of the Krox-20 gene, demonstrating
that Hoxa1 and Krox-20 synergize in a dosage-dependent manner to specify r3
identity and odd- versus even-numbered rhombomere characters. In addition, these
data suggest that the control of the development of r3 may not be autonomous but
dependent on interactions with Hoxa1-expressing cells.
