# Molecular abnormality of a Japanese glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase variant (G6PD Tokyo) associated with hereditary non-spherocytic hemolytic anemia.
**Authors:** Hirono A, Fujii H, Hirono K, Kanno H, Miwa S
**Journal:** Hum Genet (1992)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00197272](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00197272)

## Abstract

1. Hum Genet. 1992 Jan;88(3):347-8. doi: 10.1007/BF00197272.

Molecular abnormality of a Japanese glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase variant
(G6PD Tokyo) associated with hereditary non-spherocytic hemolytic anemia.

Hirono A(1), Fujii H, Hirono K, Kanno H, Miwa S.

Author information:
(1)Okinaka Memorial Institute for Medical Research, Tokyo, Japan.

The entire coding sequence of a Japanese class 1 variant (G6PD Tokyo) was
amplified by the polymerase chain reaction from genomic DNA. Nucleotide analysis
by a direct sequencing technique revealed a unique nucleotide substitution (1246
G to A) in exon 10, which predicts a Glu to Lys substitution at the 416th amino
acid. This is another member of a conspicuous mutation cluster surrounding the
putative NADP-binding domain.
