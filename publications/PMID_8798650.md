# Binding of human minichromosome maintenance proteins with histone H3.
**Authors:** Ishimi Y, Ichinose S, Omori A, Sato K, Kimura H
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.271.39.24115](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.271.39.24115)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1996 Sep 27;271(39):24115-22. doi: 10.1074/jbc.271.39.24115.

Binding of human minichromosome maintenance proteins with histone H3.

Ishimi Y(1), Ichinose S, Omori A, Sato K, Kimura H.

Author information:
(1)Mitsubishi Kasei Institute of Life Sciences, 11 Minamiooya, Machida, Tokyo
194, Japan.

Minichromosome maintenance (MCM) proteins play essential roles in eukaryotic DNA
replication, but their biochemical properties remain to be determined. We
detected in HeLa cell extracts six proteins, CDC47, CDC46/MCM5, Cdc21, P1/MCM3,
Mis5, and BM28/MCM2, by their binding to a specific antibody and by partial
sequencing. The human homologs of the MCM2 (BM28), Mis5, Cdc21, and CDC47
proteins were tightly bound to a histone-Sepharose column and purified to near
homogeneity, whereas the P1/MCM3 and CDC46/MCM5 proteins passed through. Among
the four core histones, the human BM28/MCM2, Mis5, Cdc21, and CDC47 proteins had
high affinity for histone H3. Immunoprecipitation with anti-Cdc21 antibody
revealed that these four MCM proteins form complexes. These results are
consistent with the findings that MCM proteins bind with chromatin in vivo.
