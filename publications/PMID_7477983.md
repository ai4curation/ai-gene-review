# New connexin32 mutations associated with X-linked Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease.
**Authors:** Bone LJ, Dahl N, Lensch MW, Chance PF, Kelly T, Le Guern E, Magi S, Parry G, Shapiro H, Wang S
**Journal:** Neurology (1995)

## Abstract

1. Neurology. 1995 Oct;45(10):1863-6. doi: 10.1212/wnl.45.10.1863.

New connexin32 mutations associated with X-linked Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease.

Bone LJ(1), Dahl N, Lensch MW, Chance PF, Kelly T, Le Guern E, Magi S, Parry G,
Shapiro H, Wang S, et al.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology, University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine,
Philadelphia 19104, USA.

Analysis of the connexin32 gene in patients with X-linked Charcot-Marie-Tooth
disease shows mutations distributed throughout the molecule, with all domains
affected except the fourth transmembrane domain and the distal carboxy terminus.
Sequence analysis of DNA from 19 unrelated patients detected six novel mutations
and three previously reported mutations. Identification of additional mutations
extends the distribution of connexin32 mutations in X-linked Charcot-Marie-Tooth
disease and shows that specific mutations recur in additional families.
