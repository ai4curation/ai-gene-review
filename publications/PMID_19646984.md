# Involvement of Pta1, Pcf11 and a KlCYC1 AU-rich element in alternative RNA 3'-end processing selection in yeast.
**Authors:** Seoane S, Lamas-Maceiras M, Rodríguez-Torres AM, Freire-Picos MA
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.042](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.042)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2009 Sep 3;583(17):2843-8. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2009.07.042. Epub
 2009 Jul 30.

Involvement of Pta1, Pcf11 and a KlCYC1 AU-rich element in alternative RNA
3'-end processing selection in yeast.

Seoane S(1), Lamas-Maceiras M, Rodríguez-Torres AM, Freire-Picos MA.

Author information:
(1)Universidade da Coruña, Facultad de Ciencias, Campus da Zapateira S/N, 15071
A Coruña, Spain.

This work reports the involvement of yeast RNA processing factors Pta1 and Pcf11
in alternative 3'-end RNA processing. The pta1-1 and pcf11-2 mutations changed
the predominance of KlCYC1 1.14 and 1.5 kb transcript isoforms. Mutation of the
KlCYC1 3'-UTR AU-rich sequence at positions 679-690 (mutant M1) altered
transcript predominance. Moreover, expression of M1 in the yeast mutants
partially suppressed their effects in the predominance pattern. The combination
of the M1 and M2 (694-698 deletion) mutations abolished the alternative
processing. Pta1 involvement in this selection was confirmed using the Pta1-td
degron strain.
