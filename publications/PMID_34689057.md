Interleukin-11 (IL-11) is a cytokine that has been strongly implicated in the
pathogenesis of fibrotic diseases and solid malignancies. Elevated IL-11
expression is also associated with several non-malignant inflammatory diseases
where its function remains less well-characterized. Here, we summarize current
literature surrounding the contribution of IL-11 to the pathogenesis of
autoimmune inflammatory diseases, including rheumatoid arthritis, multiple
sclerosis, diabetes and systemic sclerosis, as well as other chronic
inflammatory conditions such as periodontitis, asthma, chronic obstructive
pulmonary disease, psoriasis and colitis.
