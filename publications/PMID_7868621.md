## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1995 Mar;177(5):1409-13. doi: 10.1128/jb.177.5.1409-1413.1995.

The nasB operon and nasA gene are required for nitrate/nitrite assimilation in
Bacillus subtilis.

Ogawa K(1), Akagawa E, Yamane K, Sun ZW, LaCelle M, Zuber P, Nakano MM.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Biological Sciences, University of Tsukuba, Ibaraki, Japan.

Bacillus subtilis can use either nitrate or nitrite as a sole source of
nitrogen. The isolation of the nasABCDEF genes of B. subtilis, which are
required for nitrate/nitrite assimilation, is reported. The probable gene
products include subunits of nitrate/nitrite reductases and an enzyme involved
in the synthesis of siroheme, a cofactor for nitrite reductase.
