## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1998 Jan 6;95(1):132-7. doi: 10.1073/pnas.95.1.132.

Drosophila NURF-55, a WD repeat protein involved in histone metabolism.

Martínez-Balbás MA(1), Tsukiyama T, Gdula D, Wu C.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Molecular Cell Biology, National Cancer Institute, Building 37,
Room 5E-26, National Institutes of Health, Bethesda, MD 20892-4255, USA.

The Drosophila nucleosome remodeling factor (NURF) is a protein complex of four
distinct subunits that assists transcription factor-mediated chromatin
remodeling. One NURF subunit, ISWI, is related to the transcriptional regulators
Drosophila brahma and yeast SWI2/SNF2. We have determined peptide sequences and
isolated cDNA clones for a second NURF component (the 55-kDa subunit).
Immunological studies show that p55 is an integral subunit of NURF and is
generally associated with polytene chromosomes. The predicted sequence of p55
reveals a WD repeat protein that is identical with the 55-kDa subunit of the
Drosophila chromatin assembly factor (CAF-1). Given that WD repeat proteins
related to p55 are associated with histone deacetylase and histone
acetyltransferase, our findings suggest that p55 and its homologs may function
as a common platform for the assembly of protein complexes involved in chromatin
metabolism.
