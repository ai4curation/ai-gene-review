This review is intended to provide a broad outline of the biological and
molecular functions of MYC as well as of the larger protein network within which
MYC operates. We present a view of MYC as a sensor that integrates multiple
cellular signals to mediate a broad transcriptional response controlling many
aspects of cell behavior. We also describe the larger transcriptional network
linked to MYC with emphasis on the MXD family of MYC antagonists. Last, we
discuss evidence that the network has evolved for millions of years, dating back
to the emergence of animals.
