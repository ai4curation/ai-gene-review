Bile acids regulate the transcription of genes that control cholesterol
homeostasis through molecular mechanisms that are poorly understood.
Physiological concentrations of free and conjugated chenodeoxycholic acid,
lithocholic acid, and deoxycholic acid activated the farnesoid X receptor (FXR;
NR1H4), an orphan nuclear receptor. As ligands, these bile acids and their
conjugates modulated interaction of FXR with a peptide derived from steroid
receptor coactivator 1. These results provide evidence for a nuclear bile acid
signaling pathway that may regulate cholesterol homeostasis.
