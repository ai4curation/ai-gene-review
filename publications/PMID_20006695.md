# Expression of prestin and Gata-3,-2,-1 mRNA in the rat organ of Corti during the postnatal period and in culture.

Based on observations that mutations of GATA-3 are responsible for the
HDR-syndrome (hypoparathyroidism, deafness, renal defects) and that
GATA-transcription factors have an important role to play in inner ear
development, we hypothesized that these transcription factors may be involved in
regulatory changes of prestin transcription. To prove this, we examined in
parallel the expression of mRNA of prestin and Gata-3,-2 and Gata-1 in the organ
of Corti during early postnatal development of rats and in organotypic cultures.
Remarkable relations are observed between prestin and Gata-3,-2 expression in
organ of Corti preparations in vivo and in vitro: (i) Gata-3,-2 expression
display similar apical-basal gradients as prestin mRNA levels. (ii) The prestin
expression increases between postnatal day two and postnatal day eight by a
factor of about four in the apical and middle segments and by a factor of two in
the basal part. Highly significant Pearson correlation coefficients were
observed between Gata-3,-2 mRNA and prestin levels when the data were evaluated
by regression analyses. (iii) Parallel changes of prestin mRNA and Gata-3,-2
mRNA levels were observed in response to thyroid hormone and to gemfibrozil
application. These observations suggest a regulatory role played by the
Gata-3,-2 transcription factors in prestin expression.
