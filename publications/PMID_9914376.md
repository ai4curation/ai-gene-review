# The POLO kinase is required at multiple stages during spermatogenesis in Drosophila melanogaster.
**Authors:** Herrmann S, Amorim I, Sunkel CE
**Journal:** Chromosoma (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1007/pl00013778](https://doi.org/10.1007/pl00013778)

## Abstract

1. Chromosoma. 1998 Dec;107(6-7):440-51. doi: 10.1007/pl00013778.

The POLO kinase is required at multiple stages during spermatogenesis in
Drosophila melanogaster.

Herrmann S(1), Amorim I, Sunkel CE.

Author information:
(1)Instituto de Biologia Molecular e Celular, Universidade do Porto, Rua do
Campo Alegre 823, P-4150 Porto, Portugal.

The polo gene of Drosophila melanogaster is the founding member of the polo-like
kinase family which is conserved among eukaryotes. POLO has been implicated in
the organisation and function of the mitotic apparatus. Furthermore, POLO has
been shown to be required for normal spermatogenesis. To characterize further
the role of POLO in spermatogenesis, polo mutants were analysed by
immunostaining with specific antibodies and phase contrast microscopy.
Immunofluorescence shows that POLO localises to the centrosomes, the
centromere/kinetochore and the spindle midzone. The meiotic phenotype of various
mutant allelic combinations was also studied in detail. Observation of mutant
live testes indicates cytological abnormalities in all meiotic cell types,
including variable DNA content and multipolar spindles. Primary spermatocytes in
polo mutant testes contain an abnormal DNA content, suggesting failure of
chromosome segregation during gonial division. Immunostaining of polo mutant
cells with alpha-tubulin shows several abnormalities of the meiotic spindle,
including a significantly reduced central spindle. Our results suggest that polo
has multiple functions during spermatogenesis.
