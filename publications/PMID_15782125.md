# Mdm2 and mdmX prevent ASPP1 and ASPP2 from stimulating p53 without targeting p53 for degradation.
**Authors:** Bergamaschi D, Samuels Y, Zhong S, Lu X
**Journal:** Oncogene (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1038/sj.onc.1208535](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.onc.1208535)

## Abstract

1. Oncogene. 2005 May 26;24(23):3836-41. doi: 10.1038/sj.onc.1208535.

Mdm2 and mdmX prevent ASPP1 and ASPP2 from stimulating p53 without targeting p53
for degradation.

Bergamaschi D(1), Samuels Y, Zhong S, Lu X.

Author information:
(1)Ludwig Institute for Cancer Research, University College London, 91 Riding
House Street, London W1W 7BS, UK.

Using various mutants of p53 and mdm2, we demonstrate here that both the DNA
binding and transactivation function of p53 are required for ASPP1 and ASPP2 to
stimulate the apoptotic functions of p53. Mdm2 and mdmx prevent ASPP1 and ASPP2
from stimulating the apoptotic function of p53 by binding and inhibiting the
transcriptional activity of p53. Importantly, mdm2 and mdmx can prevent the
stimulatory effects of ASPP1 and ASPP2 without targeting p53 for degradation.
These data provide a novel mechanism by which mdm2 and mdmx act as potent
inhibitors of p53.
