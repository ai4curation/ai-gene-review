## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1982 Jan;149(1):173-80. doi: 10.1128/jb.149.1.173-180.1982.

Genetic regulation of the glyoxylate shunt in Escherichia coli K-12.

Maloy SR, Nunn WD.

The expression of the glyoxylate shunt enzymes is required for growth of
Escherichia coli on acetate or fatty acids as a sole carbon source. The genes
for the two unique enzymes of the glyoxylate shunt, aceA and aceB, are located
at 90 min on the E. coli K-12 genetic map. Polar mutations in the aceB gene
eliminate aceA gene function, suggesting that these genes constitute an operon
and the direction of transcription is from aceB to aceA. Mu d (Ap lac) fusions
with the aceA gene have been constructed to study the regulation of the ace
operon. Expression of the ace operon is under the transcriptional control of two
genes: the iclR gene, which maps near the ace operon, and the fadR gene, which
maps at 25 min, and is also involved in the regulation of the fatty acid
degradation (fad) regulon. Merodiploid studies demonstrated that both the iclR
and fadR genes regulate the glyoxylate shunt in a trans-dominant manner.
