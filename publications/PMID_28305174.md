Crosslinking of theDrosophila chorion involves a peroxidase.

TheDrosophila chorion contains an endogenous peroxidase activity which remains
inactive until late stage 14 when it catalyzes the crosslinking of the chorionic
proteins. Using explanted follicles developing in vitro, premature, but
otherwise normal crosslinking can be induced with hydrogen peroxide and normal
crosslinking can be prevented with peroxidase inhibitors. Inhibition or
premature activation of the shell peroxidase allows characterization of
chorionic filament specific proteins and establishes new criteria for the
identification of eggshell components.
