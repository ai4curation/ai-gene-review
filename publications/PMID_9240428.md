# Molecular characterization of the novel basic helix-loop-helix protein DEC1 expressed in differentiated human embryo chondrocytes.
**Authors:** Shen M, Kawamoto T, Yan W, Nakamasu K, Tamagami M, Koyano Y, Noshiro M, Kato Y
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.1997.6960](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.1997.6960)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1997 Jul 18;236(2):294-8. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.1997.6960.

Molecular characterization of the novel basic helix-loop-helix protein DEC1
expressed in differentiated human embryo chondrocytes.

Shen M(1), Kawamoto T, Yan W, Nakamasu K, Tamagami M, Koyano Y, Noshiro M, Kato
Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Hiroshima University School of Dentistry, Japan.

The differentiation of human embryo chondrocytes was markedly induced by the
addition of Bt2cAMP to the culture medium. Using this culture system, a novel
human cDNA for a basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) protein (named DEC1) expressed
primarily in the chondrocytes in response to Bt2cAMP was cloned by the
subtractive hybridization method. DEC1 protein consists of 412 amino acids and
exhibits structural similarities to the mammalian HES family, Drosophila hairy,
and Enhancer of split m7 in the bHLH region. Northern blot analysis showed that
DEC1 mRNA was expressed in various tissues including the cartilage, lung,
spleen, and intestine, but not in the brain. These findings suggest that the
bHLH factor DEC is involved in the control of cell differentiation in several
tissues including cartilage.
