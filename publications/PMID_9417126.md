# The G protein beta5 subunit interacts selectively with the Gq alpha subunit.
**Authors:** Fletcher JE, Lindorfer MA, DeFilippo JM, Yasuda H, Guilmard M, Garrison JC
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.273.1.636](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.273.1.636)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1998 Jan 2;273(1):636-44. doi: 10.1074/jbc.273.1.636.

The G protein beta5 subunit interacts selectively with the Gq alpha subunit.

Fletcher JE(1), Lindorfer MA, DeFilippo JM, Yasuda H, Guilmard M, Garrison JC.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmacology, Health Sciences Center, University of Virginia,
Charlottesville, Virginia 22908, USA.

The diversity in the heterotrimeric G protein alpha, beta, and gamma subunits
may allow selective protein-protein interactions and provide specificity for
signaling pathways. We examined the ability of five alpha subunits (alphai1,
alphai2, alphao, alphas, and alphaq) to associate with three beta subunits
(beta1, beta2, and beta5) dimerized to a gamma2 subunit containing an
amino-terminal hexahistidine-FLAG affinity tag (gamma2HF). Sf9 insect cells were
used to overexpress the recombinant proteins. The hexahistidine-FLAG sequence
does not hinder the function of the beta1gamma2HF dimer as it can be
specifically eluted from an alphai1-agarose column with GDP and AlF4-, and
purified beta1gamma2HF dimer stimulates type II adenylyl cyclase. The
beta1gamma2HF and beta2gamma2HF dimers immobilized on an anti-FLAG affinity
column bound all five alpha subunits tested, whereas the beta5gamma2HF dimer
bound only alphaq. The ability of other alpha subunits to compete with the
alphaq subunit for binding to the beta5gamma2HF dimer was tested. Addition of
increasing amounts of purified, recombinant alphai1 to the alphaq in a Sf9 cell
extract did not decrease the amount of alphaq bound to the beta5gamma2HF column.
When G proteins in an extract of brain membranes were activated with GDP and
AlF4- and deactivated in the presence of equal amounts of the beta1gamma2HF or
beta5gamma2HF dimers, only alphaq bound to the beta5gamma2HF dimer. The
alphaq-beta5gamma2HF interaction on the column was functional as GDP, and AlF4-
specifically eluted alphaq from the column. These results indicate that although
the beta1 and beta2 subunits interact with alpha subunits from the alphai,
alphas, and alphaq families, the structurally divergent beta5 subunit only
interacts with alphaq.
