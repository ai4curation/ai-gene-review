# The angiopoietin-like factor cornea-derived transcript 6 is a putative morphogen for human cornea.
**Authors:** Peek R, Kammerer RA, Frank S, Otte-Höller I, Westphal JR
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1074/jbc.M105746200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M105746200)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 2002 Jan 4;277(1):686-93. doi: 10.1074/jbc.M105746200. Epub 2001
Oct 26.

The angiopoietin-like factor cornea-derived transcript 6 is a putative morphogen
for human cornea.

Peek R(1), Kammerer RA, Frank S, Otte-Höller I, Westphal JR.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Immunology, Netherlands Ophthalmic Research
Institute, Amsterdam 1105 BA, The Netherlands. r.peek@ioi.knaw.nl

The human cornea-specific protein cornea-derived transcript 6 (CDT6) is a member
of the angiopoietin gene family. We report the structural and functional
characterization of CDT6. We demonstrate that CDT6 is a secreted protein that
folds into disulfide-linked homotetramers by coiled-coil interactions. The
finding that CDT6 is expressed at high levels in the avascular corneal stromal
layer suggested that the protein, similar to certain angiopoietins, acts as a
negative regulator of angiogenesis. To test this hypothesis and to assay the
effect of the protein on a growing tissue with high vascular density, CDT6 was
expressed in a mouse xenograft model. Expression of CDT6 led to a reduction in
tumor growth and aberrant blood vessel formation by inducing massive fibrosis.
Interestingly, expression of CDT6 also resulted in the deposition of
extracellular matrix components typical for the mature corneal stromal layer.
These observations strongly suggest a role as morphogen for CDT6 in inducing a
corneal phenotype in vivo.
