# Localization of tissue factor in the normal vessel wall and in the atherosclerotic plaque.
**Authors:** Wilcox JN, Smith KM, Schwartz SM, Gordon D
**Journal:** Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (1989)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.86.8.2839](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.86.8.2839)
**PMC:** [PMC287014](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC287014/)

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1989 Apr;86(8):2839-43. doi: 10.1073/pnas.86.8.2839.

Localization of tissue factor in the normal vessel wall and in the
atherosclerotic plaque.

Wilcox JN(1), Smith KM, Schwartz SM, Gordon D.

Author information:
(1)Department of Cardiovascular Research, Genentech, South San Francisco, CA
94080.

Tissue factor (TF)-producing cells were identified in normal human vessels and
atherosclerotic plaques by in situ hybridization and immunohistochemistry using
a specific riboprobe for TF mRNA and a polyclonal antibody directed against
human TF protein. TF mRNA and protein were absent from endothelial cells lining
normal internal mammary artery and saphenous vein samples. In normal vessels TF
was found to be synthesized in scattered cells present in the tunica media as
well as fibroblast-like adventitial cells surrounding vessels. Atherosclerotic
plaques contained many cells synthesizing TF mRNA and protein. Macrophages
present as foam cells and monocytes adjacent to the cholesterol clefts contained
TF mRNA and protein, as did mesenchymal-appearing intimal cells. Significant TF
protein staining was found deposited in the extracellular matrix surrounding
mRNA-positive cells adjacent to the cholesterol clefts and within the necrotic
cores. These results suggest that deposition of TF protein in the matrix of the
necrotic core of the atherosclerotic plaque may contribute to the
hyperthrombotic state of human atherosclerotic vessels.
