# A novel type of binding specificity to phospholipids for rat mannose-binding proteins isolated from serum and liver.
**Authors:** Kuroki Y, Honma T, Chiba H, Sano H, Saitoh M, Ogasawara Y, Sohma H, Akino T
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(97)01022-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(97)01022-3)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1997 Sep 8;414(2):387-92. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(97)01022-3.

A novel type of binding specificity to phospholipids for rat mannose-binding
proteins isolated from serum and liver.

Kuroki Y(1), Honma T, Chiba H, Sano H, Saitoh M, Ogasawara Y, Sohma H, Akino T.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Sapporo Medical University School of Medicine,
Japan. kurokiy@serpent.cc.sapmed.ac.jp

Mannose-binding protein (MBP) belongs to the collectin subgroup of C-type
lectins with specificity for mannose and N-acetylglucosamine sugars. We
investigated whether rat MBPs isolated from serum (S-MBP) and liver (L-MBP)
interact with phospholipids using antibody against each MBP. Both S- and L-MBPs
bound to phosphatidylinositol coated onto microtiter wells in a concentration-
and a Ca2+-dependent manner. L-MBP also bound to phosphatidylglycerol and weakly
to phosphatidylserine. MBPs interacted with liposomes composed of these lipids.
S- and L-MBPs bound to phosphatidylinositol 4-monophosphate. L-MBP also bound to
cardiolipin. These results provide evidence for a novel type of ligand binding
specificity for MBPs, and raise the possibility that phospholipids are ligands
for collectins.
