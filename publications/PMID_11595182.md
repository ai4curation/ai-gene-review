# Guidance of cell migration by the Drosophila PDGF/VEGF receptor.
**Authors:** Duchek P, Somogyi K, Jékely G, Beccari S, Rørth P
**Journal:** Cell (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0092-8674(01)00502-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(01)00502-5)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 2001 Oct 5;107(1):17-26. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(01)00502-5.

Guidance of cell migration by the Drosophila PDGF/VEGF receptor.

Duchek P(1), Somogyi K, Jékely G, Beccari S, Rørth P.

Author information:
(1)European Molecular Biology Laboratory, Developmental Biology Programme, 69117
Heidelberg, Germany.

Directed cell migrations are important for development, but the signaling
pathways and mechanisms responsible for guiding cell migration in vivo are
poorly understood. Migration of border cells during Drosophila oogenesis is a
simple and attractive model system in which to address these questions. We
demonstrate that PVR, a receptor tyrosine kinase related to mammalian PDGF and
VEGF receptors, acts in border cells to guide them to the oocyte. The oocyte is
the source of a ligand for PVR, PDGF/VEGF factor 1 (PVF1). Intriguingly, the
guidance function of PVR is largely redundant with that of EGFR. We present
evidence implicating Rac and the Rac activator Mbc/DOCK180/CED-5 as mediators of
the guidance signal.
