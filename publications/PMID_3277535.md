Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, University of Utah School of Medicine, Salt Lake

Squalene synthetase has been purified to homogeneity from yeast. It is a single
polypeptide of Mr 47,000. This enzyme catalyzes the synthesis of squalene from
farnesyl diphosphate via presqualene diphosphate. In the presence of reduced
pyridine nucleotides, presqualene diphosphate and squalene are produced in a
ratio of 6:1 from either the purified protein or the crude microsomal fraction.
