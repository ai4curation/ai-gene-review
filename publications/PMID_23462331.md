# Four novel GALC gene mutations in two Chinese patients with Krabbe disease.
**Authors:** Yang Y, Ren X, Xu Q, Wang C, Liu H, He X
**Journal:** Gene (2013)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.gene.2013.02.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gene.2013.02.010)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 2013 May 1;519(2):381-4. doi: 10.1016/j.gene.2013.02.010. Epub 2013 Feb
24.

Four novel GALC gene mutations in two Chinese patients with Krabbe disease.

Yang Y(1), Ren X, Xu Q, Wang C, Liu H, He X.

Author information:
(1)Department of Clinical Genetics, Bayi Children's Hospital Affiliated to
General Hospital of Beijing Military Region, Beijing, China.

Krabbe disease (OMIM #245200) is a rare autosomal recessive leukodystrophy
caused by deficiency of galactocerebrosidase (GALC) activity. We identified four
novel mutations of the GALC gene in two unrelated Chinese families with Krabbe
disease: one insertion mutation, c.1836_1837insT, and one nonsense mutation,
c.599C>A (p.S200X), in an infantile patient, and one deletion mutation,
c.1911+1_1911+5delGTAAG, and one missense mutation, c.2041G>A, in an adult
late-onset patient. This is the first identification of GALC mutations in the
Chinese population.
