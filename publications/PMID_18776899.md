# d-Asb11 is an essential mediator of canonical Delta-Notch signalling.
**Authors:** Diks SH, Sartori da Silva MA, Hillebrands JL, Bink RJ, Versteeg HH, van Rooijen C, Brouwers A, Chitnis AB, Peppelenbosch MP, Zivkovic D
**Journal:** Nat Cell Biol (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1038/ncb1779](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb1779)

## Abstract

1. Nat Cell Biol. 2008 Oct;10(10):1190-8. doi: 10.1038/ncb1779. Epub 2008 Sep 7.

d-Asb11 is an essential mediator of canonical Delta-Notch signalling.

Diks SH(1), Sartori da Silva MA, Hillebrands JL, Bink RJ, Versteeg HH, van
Rooijen C, Brouwers A, Chitnis AB, Peppelenbosch MP, Zivkovic D.

Author information:

In canonical Delta-Notch signalling, expression of Delta activates Notch in
neighbouring cells, leading to downregulation of Delta in these cells. This
process of lateral inhibition results in selection of either Delta-signalling
cells or Notch-signalling cells. Here we show that d-Asb11 is an important
mediator of this lateral inhibition. In zebrafish embryos, morpholino
oligonucleotide (MO)-mediated knockdown of d-Asb11 caused repression of specific
Delta-Notch elements and their transcriptional targets, whereas these were
induced when d-Asb11 was misexpressed. d-Asb11 also activated legitimate Notch
reporters cell-non-autonomously in vitro and in vivo when co-expressed with a
Notch reporter. However, it repressed Notch reporters when expressed in
Delta-expressing cells. Consistent with these results, d-Asb11 was able to
specifically ubiquitylate and degrade DeltaA both in vitro and in vivo. We
conclude that d-Asb11 is a component in the regulation of Delta-Notch
signalling, important in fine-tuning the lateral inhibition gradients between
DeltaA and Notch through a cell non-autonomous mechanism.
