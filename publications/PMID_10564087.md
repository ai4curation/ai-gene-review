# Identification of an acid-activated Cl(-) channel from human skeletal muscles.
**Authors:** Kawasaki M, Fukuma T, Yamauchi K, Sakamoto H, Marumo F, Sasaki S
**Journal:** Am J Physiol (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1152/ajpcell.1999.277.5.C948](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpcell.1999.277.5.C948)

## Abstract

1. Am J Physiol. 1999 Nov;277(5):C948-54. doi: 10.1152/ajpcell.1999.277.5.C948.

Identification of an acid-activated Cl(-) channel from human skeletal muscles.

Kawasaki M(1), Fukuma T, Yamauchi K, Sakamoto H, Marumo F, Sasaki S.

Author information:
(1)Second Department of Internal Medicine, Tokyo Medical and Dental University,
Tokyo 113-8519, Japan.

ClC-4 gene was isolated as a putative Cl(-) channel. Due to a lack of functional
expression of ClC-4, its physiological role remains unknown. We isolated a human
ClC-4 clone (hClC-4sk) from human skeletal muscles and stably transfected it to
Chinese hamster ovary cells. Whole cell patch-clamp studies showed that the
hClC-4sk channel was activated by external acidic pH and inhibited by DIDS. It
passed a strong outward Cl(-) current with a permeability sequence of I(-) >
Cl(-) > F(-). The hClC-4sk has consensus sites for phosphorylation by protein
kinase A (PKA); however, stimulation of PKA had no effect on the currents.
hClC-4sk mRNA was expressed in excitable tissues, such as heart, brain, and
skeletal muscle. These functional characteristics of hClC-4sk provide a clue to
its physiological role in excitable cells.
