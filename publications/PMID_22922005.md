# Post-translational modification of the RhoGTPase activating protein 21, ARHGAP21, by SUMO2/3.
**Authors:** Bigarella CL, Ferro KP, Barcellos KS, Martins-de-Souza D, Traina F, Novello JC, Saad ST, Archangelo LF
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2012)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2012.08.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2012.08.012)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2012 Sep 21;586(19):3522-8. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2012.08.012.
Epub  2012 Aug 21.

Post-translational modification of the RhoGTPase activating protein 21,
ARHGAP21, by SUMO2/3.

Bigarella CL(1), Ferro KP, Barcellos KS, Martins-de-Souza D, Traina F, Novello
JC, Saad ST, Archangelo LF.

Author information:
(1)Hematology and Hemotherapy Center, University of Campinas, Campinas, São
Paulo, Brazil.

ARHGAP21 is a 217 kDa RhoGAP protein shown to modulate cell migration through
the control of Cdc42 and FAK activities. In the present work a 250 kDa-ARHGAP21
was identified by mass spectrometry. This modified form is differentially
expressed among cell lines and human primary cells. Co-immunoprecipitations and
in vitro SUMOylation confirmed ARHGAP21 specific modification by SUMO2/3 and
mapped the SUMOylation site to ARHGAP21 lysine K1443. Immunofluorescence
staining revealed that ARHGAP21 co-localizes with SUMO2/3 in the cytoplasm and
membrane compartments. Interestingly, our results suggest that ARHGAP21
SUMOylation may be related to cell proliferation. Therefore, SUMOylation of
ARHGAP21 may represent a way of guiding its function.
