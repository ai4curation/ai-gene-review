# Trafficking of the myrosinase-associated protein GLL23 requires NUC/MVP1/GOLD36/ERMO3 and the p24 protein CYB.
**Authors:** Jancowski S, Catching A, Pighin J, Kudo T, Foissner I, Wasteneys GO
**Journal:** Plant J (2014)
**DOI:** [10.1111/tpj.12408](https://doi.org/10.1111/tpj.12408)

## Abstract

1. Plant J. 2014 Feb;77(4):497-510. doi: 10.1111/tpj.12408. Epub 2014 Jan 24.

Trafficking of the myrosinase-associated protein GLL23 requires
NUC/MVP1/GOLD36/ERMO3 and the p24 protein CYB.

Jancowski S(1), Catching A, Pighin J, Kudo T, Foissner I, Wasteneys GO.

Author information:
(1)Department of Botany, University of British Columbia, Vancouver, BC, V6T 1Z4,
Canada.

Proteins detrimental to endoplasmic reticulum (ER) morphology need to be
efficiently exported. Here, we identify two mechanisms that control trafficking
of Arabidopsis thalianaGLL23, a 43 kDa GDSL-like lipase implicated in
glucosinolate metabolism through its association with the β-glucosidase
myrosinase. Using immunofluorescence, we identified two mutants that showed
aberrant accumulation of GLL23: large perinuclear ER aggregates in the nuclear
cage (nuc) mutant; and small compartments contiguous with the peripheral ER in
the cytoplasmic bodies (cyb) mutant. Live imaging of fluorescently tagged GLL23
confirmed its presence in the nuc and cyb compartments, but lack of fluorescent
signals in the wild-type plants suggested that GLL23 is normally
post-translationally modified for ER export. NUC encodes the MVP1/GOLD36/ERMO3
myrosinase-associated protein, previously shown to have vacuolar distribution.
CYB is an ER and Golgi-localized p24 type I membrane protein component of coat
protein complex (COP) vesicles, animal and yeast homologues of which are known
to be involved in selective cargo sorting for ER-Golgi export. Without NUC,
GLL23 accumulates in the ER this situation suggests that NUC is in fact active
in the ER. Without CYB, both GLL23 and NUC were found to accumulate in cyb
compartments, consistent with a role for NUC in GLL23 processing and indicated
that GLL23 is the likely sorting target of the CYB p24 protein.
