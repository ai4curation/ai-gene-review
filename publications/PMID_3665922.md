IUPAC-IUB Joint Commission on Biochemical Nomenclature (JCBN). Nomenclature and
symbols for folic acid and related compounds. Recommendations 1986.

[No authors listed]

The folates are a group of heterocyclic compounds based on the
4-[(pteridin-6-ylmethyl)amino]benzoic acid skeleton conjugated with one or more
L-glutamate units. The document is a revision of recommendations prepared by the
IUPAC-IUB Commission on Biochemical Nomenclature in 1964. The main changes are a
consequence of the much greater information that now exists about the
stereochemistry of folates. Recommendations on the nomenclature of reduced
compounds and substituents are included, as are proposed symbols and
abbreviations.
