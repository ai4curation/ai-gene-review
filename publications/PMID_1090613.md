# The deoxyribonucleic acid unwinding protein of Escherichia coli. Properties and functions in replication.
**Authors:** Weiner JH, Bertsch LL, Kornberg A
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1975)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1975 Mar 25;250(6):1972-80.

The deoxyribonucleic acid unwinding protein of Escherichia coli. Properties and
functions in replication.

Weiner JH, Bertsch LL, Kornberg A.

The DNA unwinding protein of Escherichia coli (Sigal, N., Delius, H., Kornberg,
T., Gefter, M., and Alberts, B. (1972) Proc. Nat. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 69,
3537-3541) has been purified to homogeneity by a simple procedure which utilizes
its stability to heating. The protein is an asymmetric tetramer of 18,500 dalton
subunits which binds preferentially to single-stranded DNA at a ratio of one
protein molecule per 32 nucleotides. Binding to DNA is complete in less than 10
s at 0 degrees while release of the protein from single-stranded DNA is
relatively slow even at 37 degrees. A simple functional assay for unwinding
protein depends on its essential role in the conversion of phage G4
single-stranded DNA to the replicative form. Unwinding protein stimulates
initiation of replication of all single-stranded phage DNAs. Approximately 300
copies of unwinding protein are present per cell, as estimated by antibody
titration, an amount sufficient to cover substantial lengths of DNA in several
replicating forks.
