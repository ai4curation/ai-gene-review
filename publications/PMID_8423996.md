# Chromosomal localization of genes encoding the transcription factors, c-rel, NF-kappa Bp50, NF-kappa Bp65, and lyt-10 by fluorescence in situ hybridization.
**Authors:** Mathew S, Murty VV, Dalla-Favera R, Chaganti RS
**Journal:** Oncogene (1993)

## Abstract

1. Oncogene. 1993 Jan;8(1):191-3.

Chromosomal localization of genes encoding the transcription factors, c-rel,
NF-kappa Bp50, NF-kappa Bp65, and lyt-10 by fluorescence in situ hybridization.

Mathew S(1), Murty VV, Dalla-Favera R, Chaganti RS.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Cancer Genetics, Memorial Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center, New
York, NY 10021.

We have used fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) to perform precise
chromosomal mapping of the genes encoding the transcription factors c-rel,
NF-kappa Bp50, NF-kappa Bp65, and lyt-10. The previously published assignments
of c-rel and NF-kappa Bp50 have been refined to specific bands. The map position
of lyt-10, inferred from its isolation from a t(10;14)(q24;q32) translocation,
has been confirmed. NF-kappa Bp65 has now been mapped to 11q13, a site of
frequent involvement in aberration in multiple tumor types.
