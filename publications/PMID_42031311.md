Acidithiobacillus caldusis a core functional strain in bioleaching, and sulfide:
quinone oxidoreductase (SQR), as the key initial enzyme in the sulfur oxidation
pathway, directly constrains sulfur metabolism efficiency and mineral leaching
efficacy. In this study, SQR knockout and overexpression engineered strains were
constructed based on CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing technology and pJRD215 plasmid
conjugation transfer. Overexpression of SQR in A. caldus enhanced its acid
tolerance, energy metabolism efficiency, and biofilm stability. By increasing
sulfur metabolic flux, it promoted intracellular energy reserve and active
sulfur level accumulation, thereby enhancing the strain's colonization ability
on mineral surfaces and tolerance to heavy metal toxicity. In chalcocite
bioleaching, the SQR overexpression strain enhanced sulfur metabolism and
thereby increased the extraction of copper ions. Furthermore, integrated
strategy low pH 0.8 stimulation and 2 g/L sulfur supplementation effectively
further stimulated sulfur and iron metabolism in later bioleaching phase, and
the bioleaching efficiency was improved to 58.3%. This study combines the
genetic engineering of key sulfur‑metabolic enzymes and process optimization to
improve the bioleaching efficiency of low‑grade sulfide ores, providing a novel
reference for its efficient utilization.
