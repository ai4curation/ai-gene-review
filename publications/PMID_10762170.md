# Novel missense mutations in the glycogen-branching enzyme gene in adult polyglucosan body disease.
**Authors:** Ziemssen F, Sindern E, Schröder JM, Shin YS, Zange J, Kilimann MW, Malin JP, Vorgerd M
**Journal:** Ann Neurol (2000)

## Abstract

1. Ann Neurol. 2000 Apr;47(4):536-40.

Novel missense mutations in the glycogen-branching enzyme gene in adult
polyglucosan body disease.

Ziemssen F(1), Sindern E, Schröder JM, Shin YS, Zange J, Kilimann MW, Malin JP,
Vorgerd M.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology, Ruhr University Bochum, Germany.

We describe the first non-Ashkenazi patient with adult polyglucosan body disease
and decreased glycogen-branching enzyme (GBE) activity in leukocytes. Gene
analysis revealed compound heterozygosity for two novel missense mutations
Arg515His and Arg524Gln in the GBE gene. Both missense mutations are predicted
to impair GBE activity. This is the first identification of GBE mutations
underlying adult polyglucosan body disease in a non-Ashkenazi family, and
confirms that adult glycogen storage disease type IV can manifest clinically as
adult polyglucosan body disease.
