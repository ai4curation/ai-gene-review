Circulating levels of the bone-derived hormone osteocalcin maintain GPR37+
ventral tegmental area (VTA) GABAergic neurons in a state of readiness. In this
issue of Neuron, Liu et al.1 demonstrate that this bone-to-brain neuroendocrine
circuit is essential for the rapid detection of and the escape response to
visual threats.
