# Direct interaction between alpha-actinin and hepatitis C virus NS5B.
**Authors:** Lan S, Wang H, Jiang H, Mao H, Liu X, Zhang X, Hu Y, Xiang L, Yuan Z
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2003)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0014-5793(03)01163-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0014-5793(03)01163-3)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2003 Nov 20;554(3):289-94. doi: 10.1016/s0014-5793(03)01163-3.

Direct interaction between alpha-actinin and hepatitis C virus NS5B.

Lan S(1), Wang H, Jiang H, Mao H, Liu X, Zhang X, Hu Y, Xiang L, Yuan Z.

Author information:
(1)Key Laboratory of Medical Molecular Virology, Shanghai Medical College, Fudan
University, Shanghai 200032, PR China.

It has been suggested that cellular proteins are involved in hepatitis C virus
(HCV) RNA replication. By using the yeast two-hybrid system, we isolated seven
cDNA clones encoding proteins interacting with HCV RNA polymerase (NS5B) from a
human liver cDNA library. For one of these, alpha-actinin, we confirmed the
interaction by coimmunoprecipitation, immunofluorescent staining and confocal
microscopic analysis. Experiments with deletion mutants showed that domains
NS5B(84-95), NS5B(466-478), and alpha-actinin(621-733) are responsible for the
interaction. Studies of the HCV subgenomic replicon system with small
interference RNA indicate that alpha-actinin is essential for HCV RNA
replication. Our results suggest alpha-actinin may be a component of the HCV
replication complex.
