# Inositol polyphosphate multikinase is a nuclear PI3-kinase with transcriptional regulatory activity.
**Authors:** Resnick AC, Snowman AM, Kang BN, Hurt KJ, Snyder SH, Saiardi A
**Journal:** Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1073/pnas.0506184102](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0506184102)

## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 2005 Sep 6;102(36):12783-8. doi:
10.1073/pnas.0506184102. Epub 2005 Aug 25.

Inositol polyphosphate multikinase is a nuclear PI3-kinase with transcriptional
regulatory activity.

Resnick AC(1), Snowman AM, Kang BN, Hurt KJ, Snyder SH, Saiardi A.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neuroscience, Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine, 725
North Wolfe Street, Baltimore, MD 21205, USA.

Phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate is a major intracellular messenger
molecule thought to be formed almost exclusively by cytosolic,
wortmannin-inhibited phosphoinositide 3-kinase family members. Inositol
polyphosphate multikinase was identified as an enzyme that generates a series of
water-soluble inositol phosphates. We now report the robust, physiologic, and
evolutionarily conserved phosphoinositide 3-kinase activity of inositol
polyphosphate multikinase, which is localized to nuclei and unaffected by
wortmannin. In yeast, this inositol lipid kinase activity physiologically
regulates transcription.
