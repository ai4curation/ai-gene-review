# IL-17RD (Sef or IL-17RLM) interacts with IL-17 receptor and mediates IL-17 signaling.
**Authors:** Rong Z, Wang A, Li Z, Ren Y, Cheng L, Li Y, Wang Y, Ren F, Zhang X, Hu J, Chang Z
**Journal:** Cell Res (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1038/cr.2008.320](https://doi.org/10.1038/cr.2008.320)

## Abstract

1. Cell Res. 2009 Feb;19(2):208-15. doi: 10.1038/cr.2008.320.

IL-17RD (Sef or IL-17RLM) interacts with IL-17 receptor and mediates IL-17
signaling.

Rong Z(1), Wang A, Li Z, Ren Y, Cheng L, Li Y, Wang Y, Ren F, Zhang X, Hu J,
Chang Z.

Author information:
(1)School of Medicine, Department of Biological Sciences and Biotechnology,

Interleukin-17 (IL-17 or IL-17A) production is a hallmark of T(H)17 cells, a new
unique lineage of CD4(+) T lymphocytes contributing to the pathogenesis of
multiple autoimmune and inflammatory diseases. IL-17 receptor (IL-17R or
IL-17RA) is essential for IL-17 biological activity. Emerging data suggest that
the formation of a heteromeric and/or homomeric receptor complex is required for
IL-17 signaling. Here we show that the orphan receptor IL-17RD (Sef, similar
expression to FGF genes or IL-17RLM) is associated and colocalized with IL-17R.
Importantly, IL-17RD mediates IL-17 signaling, as evaluated using a luciferase
reporter driven by the native promoter of 24p3, an IL-17 target gene. In
addition, an IL-17RD mutant lacking the intracellular domain dominant-negatively
suppresses IL-17R-mediated IL-17 signaling. Moreover, IL-17RD as well as IL-17R
is associated with TRAF6, an IL-17R downstream molecule. These results indicate
that IL-17RD is a part of the IL-17 receptor signaling complex, therefore
providing novel evidence for IL-17 signaling through a heteromeric and/or
homomeric receptor complex.
