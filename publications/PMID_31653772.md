Comment on
    J Biol Chem. 2019 Oct 25;294(43):15559-15574. doi: 10.1074/jbc.RA119.008580.

Vault RNAs, found in vault ribonucleoprotein complexes, are known to be one of
many types of small noncoding RNAs (ncRNAs), but their specific function is not
known. A new study identifies a small ncRNA from Trypanosome brucei as a vault
RNA (vtRNA) based on sequence analysis and its association with the canonical
vault component TEP1. Down-regulation of T. brucei vtRNA impairs mRNA splicing
in a permeabilized cell system, suggesting new roles for these enigmatic
biomolecules.
