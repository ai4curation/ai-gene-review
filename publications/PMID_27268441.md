# Involvement of MAK-1 and MAK-2 MAP kinases in cell wall integrity in Neurospora crassa.
**Authors:** Kamei M, Yamashita K, Takahashi M, Fukumori F, Ichiishi A, Fujimura M

## Abstract

1. Biosci Biotechnol Biochem. 2016 Sep;80(9):1843-52. doi:

Involvement of MAK-1 and MAK-2 MAP kinases in cell wall integrity in Neurospora
crassa.

Kamei M(1)(2), Yamashita K(1), Takahashi M(1), Fukumori F(3), Ichiishi A(1),
Fujimura M(1).

Author information:
(1)a Faculty of Life Sciences , Toyo University , Oura-gun , Japan.
(2)c Department of Microbiology , University of Georgia , Athens , GA , USA.
(3)b Faculty of Food and Nutritional Sciences , Toyo University , Oura-gun ,
Japan.

Among three MAPK disruptants of Neurospora crassa, Δmak-1 was sensitive and
Δmak-2 was hypersensitive to micafungin, a beta-1,3-glucan synthase inhibitor,
than the wild-type or Δos-2 strains. We identified six micafungin-inducible
genes that are involved in cell wall integrity (CWI) and found that MAK-1
regulated the transcription of non-anchored cell wall protein gene, ncw-1, and
the beta-1,3-endoglucanase gene, bgt-2, whereas MAK-2 controlled the expression
of the glycosylhydrolase-like protein gene, gh76-5, and the C4-dicarboxylate
transporter gene, tdt-1. Western blotting analysis revealed that, in the
wild-type strain, MAK-1 was constitutively phosphorylated from conidial
germination to hyphal development. In contrast, the phosphorylation of MAK-2 was
growth phase-dependent, and micafungin induced the phosphorylation of
unphosphorylated MAK-2. It should be noted that the phosphorylation of MAK-1 was
virtually abolished in the Δmak-2 strain, but was significantly induced by
micafungin, suggesting functional cross talk between MAK-1 and MAK-2 signalling
pathway in CWI.
