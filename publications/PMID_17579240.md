## Abstract

1. Genetics. 2007 Jun;176(2):1359-62. doi: 10.1534/genetics.107.070649.

DICER-LIKE 1 and DICER-LIKE 3 redundantly act to promote flowering via
repression of FLOWERING LOCUS C in Arabidopsis thaliana.

Schmitz RJ(1), Hong L, Fitzpatrick KE, Amasino RM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, University of Wisconsin, Madison, Wisconsin
53706, USA.

In Arabidopsis thaliana, DICER-LIKE 1 and DICER-LIKE 3 are involved in the
generation of small RNAs. Double mutants between dicer-like 1 and dicer-like 3
exhibit a delay in flowering that is caused by increased expression of the
floral repressor FLOWERING LOCUS C. This delayed-flowering phenotype is similar
to that of autonomous-pathway mutants, and the flowering delay can be overcome
by vernalization.
