# UXT is a novel centrosomal protein essential for cell viability.
**Authors:** Zhao H, Wang Q, Zhang H, Liu Q, Du X, Richter M, Greene MI
**Journal:** Mol Biol Cell (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1091/mbc.e05-08-0705](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.e05-08-0705)
**PMC:** [PMC1289427](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1289427/)

## Abstract

1. Mol Biol Cell. 2005 Dec;16(12):5857-65. doi: 10.1091/mbc.e05-08-0705. Epub
2005  Oct 12.

UXT is a novel centrosomal protein essential for cell viability.

Zhao H(1), Wang Q, Zhang H, Liu Q, Du X, Richter M, Greene MI.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pathology, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, PA 19104,
USA.

Ubiquitously expressed transcript (UXT) is a prefoldinlike protein that has been
suggested to be involved in human tumorigenesis. Here, we have found that UXT is
overexpressed in a number of human tumor tissues but not in the matching normal
tissues. We demonstrate that UXT is located in human centrosomes and is
associated with gamma-tubulin. In addition, overexpression of UXT disrupts
centrosome structure. Furthermore, abrogation of UXT protein expression by small
interfering RNA knockdown leads to cell death. Together, our findings suggest
that UXT is a component of centrosome and is essential for cell viability. We
propose that UXT may facilitate transformation by corrupting regulated
centrosome functions.
