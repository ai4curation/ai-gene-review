# The human homolog T of the mouse T(Brachyury) gene; gene structure, cDNA sequence, and assignment to chromosome 6q27.
**Authors:** Edwards YH, Putt W, Lekoape KM, Stott D, Fox M, Hopkinson DA, Sowden J
**Journal:** Genome Res (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1101/gr.6.3.226](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.6.3.226)

## Abstract

1. Genome Res. 1996 Mar;6(3):226-33. doi: 10.1101/gr.6.3.226.

The human homolog T of the mouse T(Brachyury) gene; gene structure, cDNA
sequence, and assignment to chromosome 6q27.

Edwards YH(1), Putt W, Lekoape KM, Stott D, Fox M, Hopkinson DA, Sowden J.

Author information:
(1)Human Biochemical Genetics Unit, University College London, England.
yedwards@hgmp.mrc.ac.uk

We have cloned the human gene encoding the transcription factor T. T protein is
vital for the formation of posterior mesoderm and axial development in all
vertebrates. Brachyury mutant mice, which lack T protein, die in utero with
abnormal notochord, posterior somites, and allantois. We have identified human T
genomic clones and derived the mRNA sequence and gene structure. There is 91%
amino acid identity between human and mouse T proteins overall and complete
identity across 77 amino acids of the T-box motif within the DNA-binding domain.
Human T expression is very similar to that found for T in other vertebrate
species and is confined to cells derived from the notochord. The human T gene
maps to chromosome 6q27 and is only the second human member of the T-box gene
family to be described.
