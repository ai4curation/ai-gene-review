# Localization of pp60c-src in growth cone of PC12 cell.
**Authors:** Sobue K, Kanda K
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1988)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0006-291x(88)81028-3](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0006-291x(88)81028-3)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1988 Dec 30;157(3):1383-9. doi:
10.1016/s0006-291x(88)81028-3.

Localization of pp60c-src in growth cone of PC12 cell.

Sobue K(1), Kanda K.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurochemistry and Neuropharmacology, Osaka University Medical
School, Japan.

By immunocytochemical and biochemical techniques, we observed the localization
and expression of pp60c-src in nerve growth factor (NGF)-treated PC12 cells.
Immunostaining of pp60c-src is detected in the neuronal soma and the tips of
neurites (growth cones). Immunofluorescence in the neurites is less significant.
High-resolution microscopy reveals that the location of pp60c-src in growth cone
is in good agreement with the adhesive site of growth cone to the substratum.
The pp60c-src kinase activity and the pp60c-src protein level increase 3.1- to
3.5-fold and 2.0-fold during differentiation of PC12 cells, respectively. The
pp60c-src levels in the neurite fraction are also higher than those in the
neuronal soma fraction. These results support the immunocytochemical finding
that pp60c-src is localized in growth cones of differentiated PC12 cells.
Furthermore, we discuss the possible role of pp60c-src in growth cone.
