# Oxidative protein folding is driven by the electron transport system.
**Authors:** Bader M, Muse W, Ballou DP, Gassner C, Bardwell JC
**Journal:** Cell (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0092-8674(00)81016-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(00)81016-8)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 1999 Jul 23;98(2):217-27. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)81016-8.

Oxidative protein folding is driven by the electron transport system.

Bader M(1), Muse W, Ballou DP, Gassner C, Bardwell JC.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, University of Michigan, Ann Arbor 48109-1048, USA.

Disulfide bond formation is catalyzed in vivo by DsbA and DsbB. Here we
reconstitute this oxidative folding system using purified components. We have
found the sources of oxidative power for protein folding and show how disulfide
bond formation is linked to cellular metabolism. We find that disulfide bond
formation and the electron transport chain are directly coupled. DsbB uses
quinones as electron acceptors, allowing various choices for electron transport
to support disulfide bond formation. Electrons flow via cytochrome bo oxidase to
oxygen under aerobic conditions or via cytochrome bd oxidase under partially
anaerobic conditions. Under truly anaerobic conditions, menaquinone shuttles
electrons to alternate final electron acceptors such as fumarate. This
flexibility reflects the vital nature of the disulfide catalytic system.
