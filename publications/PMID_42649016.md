Prosaposin (PSAP), a secreted glycoprotein with neurotrophic activity, and
protectin D1, a docosahexaenoic acid-derived lipid mediator, are endogenous
ligands of G protein-coupled receptor 37 (GPR37). Although PSAP is expressed in
the pituitary gland, the cellular localization and functional significance of
PSAP-GPR37 signaling and protectin D1-associated pathways remain unclear. In
this study, we investigated the localization and potential functions of PSAP,
GPR37, and protectin D1-related factors in the rat pituitary gland, focusing on
adult pituitary stem/progenitor cells (CD9/SRY-box transcription factor 2
[SOX2]-positive cells) in the marginal cell layer (MCL) facing the residual
lumen of Rathke's pouch. In situ hybridization and immunohistochemistry revealed
that PSAP was localized in CD9/SOX2-positive cells in the intermediate lobe-side
MCL (IL-MCL) and in subsets of adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH)- and growth
hormone-producing cells in the anterior lobe (AL) of the pituitary gland. Gpr37
was predominantly detected in CD9/SOX2-positive cells in the IL-MCL and in some
ACTH-producing cells. The protectin D1-associated factors, fatty acid transport
protein 1 (FATP1/SLC27A1) and arachidonate 15-lipoxygenase (ALOX15), colocalized
with CD9/SOX2-positive cells in the IL-MCL. In a pharmacologically induced
prolactinoma model, Gpr37, Slc27a1, and Alox15 expression level was upregulated
in IL-derived CD9-positive cells. Functional analyses showed that PSAP treatment
increased proopiomelanocortin expression in AL-derived CD9-negative cells,
whereas protectin D1 treatment increased Sox2 expression and decreased Gpr37
expression in CD9/SOX2-positive cells. These findings suggest that
ligand-dependent GPR37 signaling may have distinct roles in the pituitary gland:
PSAP-GPR37 signaling may regulate ACTH production, whereas protectin D1-GPR37
signaling may contribute to Sox2 expression in CD9/SOX2-positive cells.
