# The pentafunctional arom enzyme of Saccharomyces cerevisiae is a mosaic of monofunctional domains.
**Authors:** Duncan K, Edwards RM, Coggins JR
**Journal:** Biochem J (1987)
**DOI:** [10.1042/bj2460375](https://doi.org/10.1042/bj2460375)
**PMC:** [PMC1148286](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1148286/)

## Abstract

1. Biochem J. 1987 Sep 1;246(2):375-86. doi: 10.1042/bj2460375.

The pentafunctional arom enzyme of Saccharomyces cerevisiae is a mosaic of
monofunctional domains.

Duncan K(1), Edwards RM, Coggins JR.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, University of Glasgow, U.K.

The nucleotide sequence of the Saccharomyces cerevisiae ARO1 gene which encodes
the arom multifunctional enzyme has been determined. The protein sequence
deduced for the pentafunctional arom polypeptide is 1588 amino acids in length
and has a calculated Mr of 174555. Functional regions within the polypeptide
chain have been identified by comparison with the sequences of the five
monofunctional Escherichia coli enzymes whose activities correspond with those
of the arom multifunctional enzyme. The observed homologies demonstrate that the
arom polypeptide is a mosaic of functional domains and are consistent with the
hypothesis that the ARO1 gene evolved by the linking of ancestral E. coli-like
genes.
