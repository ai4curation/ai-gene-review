β-Etherases selectively cleave β-O-4 arylether bonds, the most common linkage
type in lignin, with the help of glutathione. This way, they allow for a
targeted depolymerization of lignin with selective formation of specific
phenylpropanoid products, which makes β-etherases highly attractive for
application in lignin biorefinery. In this chapter, we give an overview of the
enzymology of this group of glutathione-S-transferases followed by detailed
procedures regarding the production and biochemical characterization of
β-etherases. This includes easy-to-use spectrophotometric assays for kinetic
measurements as well as the determination of enantioselectivity amongst others.
Finally, the application of β-etherases in combination with other enzymes for
lignin depolymerization is reviewed.
