# Identification of two isoforms of the vesicle-membrane fusion protein SNAP-23 in human neutrophils and HL-60 cells.
**Authors:** Mollinedo F, Lazo PA
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (1997)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.1997.6196](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.1997.6196)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 1997 Feb 24;231(3):808-12. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.1997.6196.

Identification of two isoforms of the vesicle-membrane fusion protein SNAP-23 in
human neutrophils and HL-60 cells.

Mollinedo F(1), Lazo PA.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Leukocyte Biology, Facultad de Medicina, Consejo Superior de
Investigaciones Científicas-Universidad de Valladolid, C/Ramón y Cajal, Spain.

We have identified and cloned two isoforms of SNAP-23 in human neutrophils and
human promyelocytic HL-60 cells. SNAP-23A which is similar to the recently
cloned SNAP-23, and a novel isoform, termed SNAP-23B. SNAP-23B results from a
deletion of 159 bp of the SNAP-23A cDNA, encoding a protein of approximately
17.8 kDa. SNAP-23B is identical to SNAP-23A, but lacks fifty-three amino acid
residues, from 90 to 142. This region of SNAP-23A has a target sequence for
posttranslational fatty acid acylation, suggesting that the two isoforms can
differ in their capacity to interact with membranes. The level of SNAP-23 mRNA
is increased during HL-60 neutrophil differentiation. These findings demonstrate
the existence of two SNAP-23 proteins in human neutrophils; and therefore, these
cells have all the proteins needed to carry out the exocytic process.
