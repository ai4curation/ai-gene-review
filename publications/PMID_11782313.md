# Central spindle assembly and cytokinesis require a kinesin-like protein/RhoGAP complex with microtubule bundling activity.

complex with microtubule bundling activity.

Comment in
    Dev Cell. 2002 Jan;2(1):4-6. doi: 10.1016/s1534-5807(01)00113-7.

A late step in cytokinesis requires the central spindle, which forms during
anaphase by the bundling of antiparallel nonkinetochore microtubules.
Microtubule bundling and completion of cytokinesis require ZEN-4/CeMKLP-1, a
kinesin-like protein, and CYK-4, which contains a RhoGAP domain. We show that
CYK-4 and ZEN-4 exist in a complex in vivo that can be reconstituted in vitro.
The N terminus of CYK-4 binds the central region of ZEN-4, including the neck
linker. Genetic suppression data prove the functional significance of this
interaction. An analogous complex, containing equimolar amounts of a CYK-4
ortholog and MKLP-1, was purified from mammalian cells. Biochemical studies
indicate that this complex, named centralspindlin, is a heterotetramer.
Centralspindlin, but not its individual components, strongly promotes
microtubule bundling in vitro.
