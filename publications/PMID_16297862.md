# Autoantigen Ro52 is an E3 ubiquitin ligase.
**Authors:** Wada K, Kamitani T
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.bbrc.2005.11.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbrc.2005.11.029)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2006 Jan 6;339(1):415-21. doi:
10.1016/j.bbrc.2005.11.029. Epub 2005 Nov 14.

Autoantigen Ro52 is an E3 ubiquitin ligase.

Wada K(1), Kamitani T.

Author information:
(1)Department of Cardiology, The University of Texas M. D. Anderson Cancer
Center, Houston, TX 77030, USA.

Anti-Ro/SSA antibodies are classic autoantibodies commonly found in patients
with Sjögren's syndrome, a chronic autoimmune disease characterized by dryness
of the eyes and mouth. The autoantibodies recognize a RING-finger protein, Ro52,
whose function is still unknown. Since many RING-finger proteins have been
identified as E3 ubiquitin ligases, this study was designed to determine whether
Ro52 functions as an E3 ubiquitin ligase. For this purpose, recombinant Ro52 was
purified from bacterial lysate and used to investigate its activity of E3
ubiquitin ligase in vitro. Its enzymatic activity was also tested in HEK293T
cells using wild-type Ro52 and its RING-finger mutant. Our results indicated
that Ro52 ubiquitinates itself in cooperation with E2 ubiquitin-conjugating
enzyme UbcH5B, thereby validating that Ro52 is a RING-finger-type E3 ubiquitin
ligase. Importantly, this ubiquitin modification is predominantly
monoubiquitination, which does not target Ro52 to the proteasome for
degradation.
