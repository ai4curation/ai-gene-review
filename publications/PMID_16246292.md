# A live cell hormone-binding assay on transgenic bacteria expressing a eukaryotic receptor protein.
**Authors:** Romanov GA, Spíchal L, Lomin SN, Strnad M, Schmülling T

## Abstract

1. Anal Biochem. 2005 Dec 1;347(1):129-34. doi: 10.1016/j.ab.2005.09.012. Epub
2005  Sep 30.

A live cell hormone-binding assay on transgenic bacteria expressing a eukaryotic
receptor protein.

Romanov GA(1), Spíchal L, Lomin SN, Strnad M, Schmülling T.

Author information:
(1)Free University of Berlin, Institute of Biology/Applied Genetics, Germany.
gar@ippras.ru

The investigation of hormone-receptor interaction normally needs isolation and
extensive purification of the receptor protein or a particular
receptor-containing fraction. To bypass these time- and resource-consuming
procedures, we have established a live cell-based assay using transgenic
bacteria expressing single eukaryotic receptors. Here we describe some
biochemical features of the Arabidopsis cytokinin receptor CRE1/AHK4 expressed
in Escherichia coli. The data show that the main characteristics of the
ligand-receptor interaction, including binding affinity and ligand specificity,
can be determined using intact bacteria expressing a functional receptor.
