Aplasia of lacrimal and salivary glands (ALSG) is an autosomal dominant
congenital anomaly characterized by aplasia, atresia or hypoplasia of the
lacrimal and salivary systems. Affected individuals present with irritable eyes
and dryness of the mouth with variable expressivity. Mutations in FGF10 were
recently described in ALSG and in lacrimo-auriculo-dento-digital (LADD) syndrome
which are overlapping clinical entities. We present here two families with ALSG
associated with missense mutations (R80S and G138E, respectively) affecting
highly conserved residues in FGF10. The clinical features of these patients
further broaden the knowledge of FGF10-related phenotypes.
