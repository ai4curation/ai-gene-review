Comment on
    Cell. 2006 May 19;125(4):801-14. doi: 10.1016/j.cell.2006.03.032.

In this issue of Cell, Lim et al. (2006) describe a protein-protein interaction
network for inherited human ataxias, a group of diseases characterized by
degeneration of cerebellar Purkinje cells. This protein interactome shows that
the cerebellar ataxias not only share clinical and pathological characteristics
but also have proteins, processes, and pathways in common.
