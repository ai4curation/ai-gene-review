# Crystal structures of S120G mutant and wild type of human nucleoside diphosphate kinase A in complex with ADP.
**Authors:** Giraud MF, Georgescauld F, Lascu I, Dautant A
**Journal:** J Bioenerg Biomembr (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1007/s10863-006-9043-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10863-006-9043-0)

## Abstract

1. J Bioenerg Biomembr. 2006 Aug;38(3-4):261-4. doi: 10.1007/s10863-006-9043-0.
Epub 2006 Sep 1.

Crystal structures of S120G mutant and wild type of human nucleoside diphosphate
kinase A in complex with ADP.

Giraud MF(1), Georgescauld F, Lascu I, Dautant A.

Author information:
(1)Institut de Biochimie et Génétique Cellulaires, UMR 5095 CNRS-Université
Victor Segalen Bordeaux 2, 1 rue Camille Saint-Saëns, 33077 Bordeaux cedex,
France.

Nm23 was the first metastasis suppressor gene identified. This gene encodes a
NDP kinase that also exhibits other properties like histidine protein kinase and
interactions with proteins and DNA. The S120G mutant of NDPK-A has been
identified in aggressive neuroblastomas and has been found to reduce the
metastasis suppressor effect of Nm23. In order to understand the differences
between the wild type and the S120G mutant, we have determined the structure of
both mutant and wild type NDPK-A in complex with ADP. Our results reveal that
there are no significant changes between the two enzyme versions even in the
surroundings of the catalytic histidine that is required for NDP kinase
activity. This suggests that the S120G mutation may affect an other protein
property than NDP kinase activity.
