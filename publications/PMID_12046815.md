# Keratin-9 gene mutation in a family with epidermolytic palmoplantar keratoderma.
**Authors:** Amichai B, Karpati M, Goldman B, Peleg L
**Journal:** J Eur Acad Dermatol Venereol (2002)

## Abstract

1. J Eur Acad Dermatol Venereol. 2002 Mar;16(2):134-6. doi:
10.1046/j.1468-3083.2002.00426.x.

Keratin-9 gene mutation in a family with epidermolytic palmoplantar keratoderma.

Amichai B(1), Karpati M, Goldman B, Peleg L.

Author information:
(1)Department of Dermatology, Huzot Clinic of Kupat Holim, Ashkelon, Israel.

BACKGROUND: Epidermolytic palmoplantar keratoderma is an autosomal dominant
inherited disorder of keratinization.
METHODS: We studied five members of a Jewish family with epidermolytic
palmoplantar keratoderma. Genomic DNA was extracted from leucocytes, and exon 1
of the keratin 9 gene was amplified using polymerase chain reaction techniques.
RESULTS: The mutation was found in exon 1 of the keratin 9 gene in codon 160.
CONCLUSIONS: Like most of the other families with clinical features of
epidermolytic palmoplantar keratoderma the mutation is found in exon 1 of the
keratin 9 gene.
