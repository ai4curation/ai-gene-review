# Upstream induction sequence, the cis-acting element required for response to the allantoin pathway inducer and enhancement of operation of the nitrogen-regulated upstream activation sequence in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
**Authors:** van Vuuren HJ, Daugherty JR, Rai R, Cooper TG
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (1991)
**DOI:** [10.1128/jb.173.22.7186-7195.1991](https://doi.org/10.1128/jb.173.22.7186-7195.1991)
**PMC:** [PMC209224](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC209224/)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1991 Nov;173(22):7186-95. doi: 10.1128/jb.173.22.7186-7195.1991.

Upstream induction sequence, the cis-acting element required for response to the
allantoin pathway inducer and enhancement of operation of the nitrogen-regulated
upstream activation sequence in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

van Vuuren HJ(1), Daugherty JR, Rai R, Cooper TG.

Author information:
(1)Department of Microbiology, University of Stellenbosch, South Africa.

Expression of the DAL2, DAL4, DAL7, DUR1,2, and DUR3 genes in Saccharomyces
cerevisiae is induced by the presence of allophanate, the last intermediate of
the allantoin degradative pathway. Analysis of the DAL7 5'-flanking region
identified an element, designated the DAL upstream induction sequence (DAL UIS),
required for response to inducer. The operation of this cis-acting element
requires functional DAL81 and DAL82 gene products. We determined the DAL UIS
structure by using saturation mutagenesis. A specific dodecanucleotide sequence
is the minimum required for response of reporter gene transcription to inducer.
There are two copies of the sequence in the 5'-flanking region of the DAL7 gene.
There are one or more copies of the sequence upstream of each allantoin pathway
gene that responds to inducer. The sequence is also found 5' of the
allophanate-inducible CAR2 gene as well. No such sequences were detected
upstream of allantoin pathway genes that do not respond to the presence of
inducer. We also demonstrated that the presence of a UIS element adjacent to the
nitrogen-regulated upstream activation sequence significantly enhances its
operation.
