Comment in
    Science. 2007 Oct 26;318(5850):578-9. doi: 10.1126/science.1150704.

mazEF is a toxin-antitoxin module located on many bacterial chromosomes,
including those of pathogens. Here, we report that Escherichia coli
mazEF-mediated cell death is a population phenomenon requiring a quorum-sensing
molecule that we call the extracellular death factor (EDF). Structural analysis
revealed that EDF is a linear pentapeptide, Asn-Asn-Trp-Asn-Asn. Each of the
five amino acids of EDF is important for its activity.
