We identified loss-of-function mutations in ATP6V0A2, encoding the a2 subunit of
the V-type H+ ATPase, in several families with autosomal recessive cutis laxa
type II or wrinkly skin syndrome. The mutations result in abnormal glycosylation
of serum proteins (CDG-II) and cause an impairment of Golgi trafficking in
fibroblasts from affected individuals. These results indicate that the a2
subunit of the proton pump has an important role in Golgi function.
