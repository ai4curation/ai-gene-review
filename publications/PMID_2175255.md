# A Drosophila gene that is homologous to a mammalian gene associated with tumor metastasis codes for a nucleoside diphosphate kinase.
**Authors:** Biggs J, Hersperger E, Steeg PS, Liotta LA, Shearn A
**Journal:** Cell (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0092-8674(90)90496-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/0092-8674(90)90496-2)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 1990 Nov 30;63(5):933-40. doi: 10.1016/0092-8674(90)90496-2.

A Drosophila gene that is homologous to a mammalian gene associated with tumor
metastasis codes for a nucleoside diphosphate kinase.

Biggs J(1), Hersperger E, Steeg PS, Liotta LA, Shearn A.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, Johns Hopkins University, Baltimore, Maryland 21218.

The product of the abnormal wing discs (awd) gene of Drosophila is 78% identical
to the product of the nm23 gene of mammals, which is differentially expressed in
certain metastatic tumors. We present evidence that the awd gene codes for a
nucleoside diphosphate kinase (NDP kinase) and that this Awd/NDP kinase is
microtubule associated. Neuroblasts in Drosophila larvae homozygous for a null
mutation in the awd gene are arrested in metaphase, indicating that
microtubule-associated Awd/NDP kinase plays a critical role in spindle
microtubule polymerization.
