# Nucleotide and deduced amino acid sequence of human cysteinyl-tRNA synthetase.
**Authors:** Cruzen ME, Arfin SM
**Journal:** DNA Seq (1994)
**DOI:** [10.3109/10425179409020847](https://doi.org/10.3109/10425179409020847)

## Abstract

1. DNA Seq. 1994;4(4):243-8. doi: 10.3109/10425179409020847.

Nucleotide and deduced amino acid sequence of human cysteinyl-tRNA synthetase.

Cruzen ME(1), Arfin SM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biological Chemistry, College of Medicine, University of
California, Irvine 92717.

We have determined the nucleotide sequence of a cDNA coding human cysteinyl-tRNA
synthetase. The predicted protein sequence of 638 amino acids has substantial
sequence similarity to the E. coli cysteinyl-tRNA synthetase in the
nucleotide-binding fold region that constitutes the potential active site. The
results are consistent with the hypothesis that evolution of the full complement
of aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases occurred prior to the divergence of prokaryotes
and eukaryotes.
