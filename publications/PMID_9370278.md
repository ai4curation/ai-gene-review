## Abstract

1. Gene. 1997 Oct 1;198(1-2):165-9. doi: 10.1016/s0378-1119(97)00311-9.

Isocitrate lyase localisation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae cells.

Chaves RS(1), Herrero P, Ordiz I, Angeles del Brio M, Moreno F.

Author information:
(1)Departamento de Bioquímica y Biología Molecular, Instituto Universitario de
Biotecnología de Asturias, Universidad de Oviedo, Spain.

The isocitrate lyase from Saccharomyces cerevisiae was only located in the cell
cytoplasm. This protein was found not to be associated with cell organelles,
even under growth conditions that induce peroxisome proliferation. This
conclusion is supported by experiments carried out by damaging the protoplast
plasma membrane with DEAE-dextran, by differential centrifugation of osmotically
lysed protoplast and by using the green fluorescent protein (GFP) of Aequorea
victoria as a reporter fusion tag to localise the subcellular compartment to
which isocitrate lyase is targeted.
