TACAN is an ion channel-like protein that may be involved in sensing mechanical
pain. Here, we present the cryo-electron microscopic structure of human TACAN
(hTACAN). hTACAN forms a dimer in which each protomer consists of a
transmembrane globular domain (TMD) containing six helices and an intracellular
domain (ICD) containing two helices. Molecular dynamic simulations suggest that
each protomer contains a putative ion conduction pore. A single-point mutation
of the key residue Met207 greatly increases membrane pressure-activated
currents. In addition, each hTACAN subunit binds one cholesterol molecule. Our
data show the molecular assembly of hTACAN and suggest that wild-type hTACAN is
in a closed state.
