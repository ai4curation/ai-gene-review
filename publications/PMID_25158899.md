Dosage compensation is a regulatory process that balances the expression of
X-chromosomal genes between males (XY) and females (XX). In Drosophila, this
requires non-coding RNAs and RNA-binding proteins (RBPs) whose specific
functions remain elusive. Here we show that the Drosophila RBP UNR promotes the
targeting of the activating male-specific-lethal complex to the X-chromosome by
facilitating the interaction of two crucial subunits: the RNA helicase MLE and
the long non-coding RNA roX2.
