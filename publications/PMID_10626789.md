# The generation of endostatin is mediated by elastase.
**Authors:** Wen W, Moses MA, Wiederschain D, Arbiser JL, Folkman J
**Journal:** Cancer Res (1999)

## Abstract

1. Cancer Res. 1999 Dec 15;59(24):6052-6.

The generation of endostatin is mediated by elastase.

Wen W(1), Moses MA, Wiederschain D, Arbiser JL, Folkman J.

Author information:
(1)Department of Surgery, The Children's Hospital, Boston, Massachusetts 02115,
USA.

Endostatin, a potent inhibitor of angiogenesis and tumor growth, is a
COOH-terminal fragment of collagen XVIII derived through cleavage of an Ala-His
linkage by an as yet unidentified endostatin-processing enzyme. Endostatin was
originally isolated from the conditioned medium of hemangioendothelioma (EOMA)
cells. By investigating the processing of collagen XVIII to endostatin by EOMA
cells, we show here that the generation of endostatin can be mediated by an
elastase activity. We also show that several members of the elastase family can
act as an endostatin-processing enzyme by specifically cleaving the Ala-His
linkage and releasing endostatin from a precursor molecule. We further suggest
that the generation of endostatin from collagen XVIII is at least a two-step
process, involving a metal-dependent early step and an elastase
activity-dependent final step.
