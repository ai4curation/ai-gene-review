## Abstract

The role of xylulokinase in Saccharomyces cerevisiae xylulose catabolism.

Richard P(1), Toivari MH, Penttilä M.

Author information:
(1)VTT Biotechnology, Espoo, Finland. peter.richard@vtt.fi

Many yeast species have growth rates on D-xylulose of 25-130% of those on
glucose, but for Saccharomyces cerevisiae this ratio is only about 6%. The
xylulokinase reaction has been proposed to be the rate-limiting step in the
D-xylulose fermentation with S. cerevisiae. Over-expression of xylulokinase
encoding XKS1 stimulated growth on D-xylulose in a S. cerevisiae strain to about
20% of the growth rate on glucose and deletion of the gene prevented growth on
D-xylulose and D-xylulose metabolism. We have partially purified the
xylulokinase and characterised its kinetic properties. It is reversible and will
also accept D-ribulose as a substrate.
