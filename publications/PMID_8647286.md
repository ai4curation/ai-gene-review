# Cloning of a melatonin-related receptor from human pituitary.
**Authors:** Reppert SM, Weaver DR, Ebisawa T, Mahle CD, Kolakowski LF Jr
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0014-5793(96)00437-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(96)00437-1)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1996 May 20;386(2-3):219-24. doi: 10.1016/0014-5793(96)00437-1.

Cloning of a melatonin-related receptor from human pituitary.

Reppert SM(1), Weaver DR, Ebisawa T, Mahle CD, Kolakowski LF Jr.

Author information:
(1)Laboratory of Developmental Chronobiology, Massachusetts General Hospital,
Boston, MA 02114, USA.reppert@helix.mgh.harvard.edu

We have cloned an orphan G protein-coupled receptor from a human pituitary cDNA
library using a probe generated by PCR. The cDNA, designated H9, encodes a
protein of 613 amino acids that is 45% identical at the amino acid level to the
recently cloned human Mel(1a) and Mel(1b) melatonin receptors. Structural
analyses of the encoded protein and its gene, along with phylogenetic analysis,
further show that H9 is closely related to the G protein-coupled melatonin
receptor family. Unusual features of the protein encoded by H9 include a lack of
N-linked glycosylation sites and a carboxyl tail >300 amino acids long. H9
transiently expressed in COS-1 cells did not bind [125I]melatonin or
[3H]melatonin. H9 mRNA is expressed in hypothalamus and pituitary, suggesting
that the encoded receptor and its natural ligand are involved in neuroendocrine
function.
