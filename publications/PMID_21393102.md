# TREM-1 interaction with the LPS/TLR4 receptor complex.
**Authors:** Arts RJ, Joosten LA, Dinarello CA, Kullberg BJ, van der Meer JW, Netea MG
**Journal:** Eur Cytokine Netw (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1684/ecn.2011.0274](https://doi.org/10.1684/ecn.2011.0274)

## Abstract

1. Eur Cytokine Netw. 2011 Mar;22(1):11-4. doi: 10.1684/ecn.2011.0274.

TREM-1 interaction with the LPS/TLR4 receptor complex.

Arts RJ(1), Joosten LA, Dinarello CA, Kullberg BJ, van der Meer JW, Netea MG.

Author information:
(1)Radboud University Nijmegen Medical Center; and Nijmegen Institute for
Infection, Inflammation and Immunity (N4i), Nijmegen, The Netherlands.

Triggering receptor expressed on myeloid cells 1 (TREM-1) is an activating
receptor expressed on neutrophils and monocytes that amplifies inflammation
induced by stimulation of pattern-recognition receptors. In this study, several
lines of evidence are presented that TREM-1 interacts with the toll-like
receptor 4 (TLR4) receptor complex, or is a component of this complex. Blocking
anti-TREM-1 antibodies specifically inhibited LPS-induced TNF-α production,
while the alternative approach of blocking TLR4 by a specific inhibitor led to a
down-regulation of the effects of TREM-1 cross-linking. These data are in line
with the TLR4-TREM1 co-localization in human neutrophils and suggests that, at
least some of the biological effects of TREM-1 may be due to its interaction
with the TLR4/LPS-receptor complex.
