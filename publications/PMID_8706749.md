# L-aspartate oxidase from Escherichia coli. I. Characterization of coenzyme binding and product inhibition.
**Authors:** Mortarino M, Negri A, Tedeschi G, Simonic T, Duga S, Gassen HG, Ronchi S
**Journal:** Eur J Biochem (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1432-1033.1996.0418u.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1432-1033.1996.0418u.x)

## Abstract

1. Eur J Biochem. 1996 Jul 15;239(2):418-26. doi:
10.1111/j.1432-1033.1996.0418u.x.

L-aspartate oxidase from Escherichia coli. I. Characterization of coenzyme
binding and product inhibition.

Mortarino M(1), Negri A, Tedeschi G, Simonic T, Duga S, Gassen HG, Ronchi S.

Author information:
(1)Istituto di Fisiologia Veterinaria e Biochimica, Università di Milano, Italy.

This paper reports the biochemical characterization of the flavoprotein
L-aspartate oxidase from Escherichia coli. Modification of a previously
published procedure allowed overexpression of the holoenzyme in an unproteolysed
form. L-Aspartate oxidase is a monomer of 60 kDa containing 1 mol of
noncovalently bound FAD/mol protein. A polarographic and two spectrophotometric
coupled assays have been set up to monitor the enzymatic activity continuously.
L-Aspartate oxidase was subjected to product inhibition since iminoaspartate,
which results from the oxidation of L-aspartate, binds to the enzyme with a
dissociation constant (Kd) equal to 1.4 microM. The enzyme binds FAD by a simple
second-order process with Kd 0.67 microM. Site-directed mutagenesis of the
residues E43, G44, S45, F47 and Y48 located in the putative binding site of the
isoallossazinic portion of FAD reduces the affinity for the coenzyme.
