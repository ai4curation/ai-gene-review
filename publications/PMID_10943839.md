Aggregation and fusion of myoblasts to form myotubes is essential for myogenesis
in many organisms. In Drosophila the formation of syncytial myotubes is seeded
by founder myoblasts. Founders fuse with clusters of fusion-competent myoblasts.
Here we identify the gene dumbfounded (duf) and show that it is required for
myoblast aggregation and fusion. duf encodes a member of the immunoglobulin
superfamily of proteins that is an attractant for fusion-competent myoblasts. It
is expressed by founder cells and serves to attract clusters of myoblasts from
which myotubes form by fusion.
