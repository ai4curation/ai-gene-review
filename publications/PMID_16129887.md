# Roles of ARFRP1 (ADP-ribosylation factor-related protein 1) in post-Golgi membrane trafficking.
**Authors:** Shin HW, Kobayashi H, Kitamura M, Waguri S, Suganuma T, Uchiyama Y, Nakayama K
**Journal:** J Cell Sci (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1242/jcs.02524](https://doi.org/10.1242/jcs.02524)

## Abstract

1. J Cell Sci. 2005 Sep 1;118(Pt 17):4039-48. doi: 10.1242/jcs.02524.

Roles of ARFRP1 (ADP-ribosylation factor-related protein 1) in post-Golgi
membrane trafficking.

Shin HW(1), Kobayashi H, Kitamura M, Waguri S, Suganuma T, Uchiyama Y, Nakayama
K.

Author information:
(1)Department of Physiological Chemistry, Graduate School of Pharmaceutical
Sciences, Kyoto University, Kyoto 606-8501, Japan.

ADP-ribosylation factor (ARF)-related protein 1 (ARFRP1) is a small GTPase with
significant similarity to the ARF family. However, little is known about the
function of ARFRP1 in mammalian cells, although knockout mice of its gene are
embryonic lethal. In the present study, we demonstrate that ARFRP1 is associated
mainly with the trans-Golgi compartment and the trans-Golgi network (TGN) and is
an essential regulatory factor for targeting of Arl1 and GRIP domain-containing
proteins, golgin-97 and golgin-245, onto Golgi membranes. Furthermore, we show
that, in concert with Arl1 and GRIP proteins, ARFRP1 is implicated in the
Golgi-to-plasma membrane transport of the vesicular stomatitis virus G protein
as well as in the retrograde transport of TGN38 and Shiga toxin from endosomes
to the TGN.
