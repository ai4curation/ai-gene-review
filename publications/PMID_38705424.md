DNA methyltransferases (DNMTs) are important epigenetic modification during
spermatogenesis. To further evaluate the pattern of DNMTs in horse testes during
development, we investigated the expression and localization of DNMT1, DNMT3a
and DNMT3b at different time points. The qRT-PCR results showed that DNMT1
expression was maintained in testes tissue from 6-month-old (0.5y) to 2-year-old
(2y) of age and decreased after 3-year-old (3y) (P < 0.01). The expression
levels of DNMT3a and DNMT3b peaked in testes tissue at 3y (P < 0.01). At
4-year-old (4y), the expression of DNMT3a and DNMT3b was decreased and became
similar to that at 0.5y. Immunofluorescence of DNMT1, DNMT3a and DNMT3b on
testis samples confirmed the differential expression and localization of these
three DNA methylation transferases during horse development. Further molecular
biological studies are needed to understand the implications of the expression
patterns of these DNMTs in horse testes.
