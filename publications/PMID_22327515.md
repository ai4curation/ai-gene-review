Comment in
    Mov Disord. 2012 Jul;27(8):963. doi: 10.1002/mds.25110.

Familial idiopathic basal ganglia calcification (IBGC) is a genetic condition
with a wide spectrum of neuropsychiatric symptoms, including parkinsonism and
dementia. Here, we identified mutations in SLC20A2, encoding the type III
sodium-dependent phosphate transporter 2 (PiT2), in IBGC-affected families of
varied ancestry, and we observed significantly impaired phosphate transport
activity for all assayed PiT2 mutants in Xenopus laevis oocytes. Our results
implicate altered phosphate homeostasis in the etiology of IBGC.
