# Ezh2 controls B cell development through histone H3 methylation and Igh rearrangement.

## Abstract

1. Nat Immunol. 2003 Feb;4(2):124-31. doi: 10.1038/ni876. Epub 2002 Dec 23.

Ezh2 controls B cell development through histone H3 methylation and Igh

Comment in
    Nat Immunol. 2003 Feb;4(2):101-3. doi: 10.1038/ni0203-101.

Polycomb group protein Ezh2 is an essential epigenetic regulator of embryonic
development in mice, but its role in the adult organism is unknown. High
expression of Ezh2 in developing murine lymphocytes suggests Ezh2 involvement in
lymphopoiesis. Using Cre-mediated conditional mutagenesis, we demonstrated a
critical role for Ezh2 in early B cell development and rearrangement of the
immunoglobulin heavy chain gene (Igh). We also revealed Ezh2 as a key regulator
of histone H3 methylation in early B cell progenitors. Our data suggest
Ezh2-dependent histone H3 methylation as a novel regulatory mechanism
controlling Igh rearrangement during early murine B cell development.
