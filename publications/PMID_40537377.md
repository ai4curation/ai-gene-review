Cook et al. show that the VPS15 pseudokinase domain binds GTP and sequesters its
covalently-linked N-terminal myristate. Together, this enforces VPS15
conformations that interact with and stabilize inactive VPS34 kinase domain
conformations. Myristate release disrupts this inhibitory interaction and also
helps dock VPS34 on membranes to catalyze phosphatidylinositol-3-phophate (PI3P)
production.
