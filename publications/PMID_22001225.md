# MutL associates with Escherichia coli RecA and inhibits its ATPase activity.
**Authors:** Zhang M, Zhou Y, Li T, Wang H, Cheng F, Zhou Y, Bi L, Zhang XE
**Journal:** Arch Biochem Biophys (2012)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.abb.2011.09.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.abb.2011.09.013)

## Abstract

1. Arch Biochem Biophys. 2012 Jan 15;517(2):98-103. doi:
10.1016/j.abb.2011.09.013.  Epub 2011 Oct 5.

MutL associates with Escherichia coli RecA and inhibits its ATPase activity.

Zhang M(1), Zhou Y, Li T, Wang H, Cheng F, Zhou Y, Bi L, Zhang XE.

Author information:
(1)State Key Laboratory of Virology, Wuhan Institute of Virology, Chinese
Academy of Sciences, Wuhan, China.

Different DNA repair systems are known to cooperate to deal with DNA damage.
However, the regulatory role of the cross-talk between these pathways is
unclear. Here, we have shown that MutL, an essential component of mismatch
repair, is a RecA-interacting protein, and that its highly conserved N-terminal
domain is sufficient for this interaction. Surface plasmon resonance and
capillary electrophoresis analyses revealed that MutL has little effect on
RecA-ssDNA filament formation, but dose down-regulate the ATPase activity of
RecA. Our findings identify a new role for MutL, and suggest its regulatory role
in homologous recombination.
