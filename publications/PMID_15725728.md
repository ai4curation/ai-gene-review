# Netrin-integrin signaling in epithelial morphogenesis, axon guidance and vascular patterning.
**Authors:** Nikolopoulos SN, Giancotti FG
**Journal:** Cell Cycle (2005)

## Abstract

1. Cell Cycle. 2005 Mar;4(3):e131-5. Epub 2005 Mar 18.

Netrin-integrin signaling in epithelial morphogenesis, axon guidance and
vascular patterning.

Nikolopoulos SN(1), Giancotti FG.

Author information:
(1)Cell Biology Program, Sloan-Kettering Institute for Cancer Research, Memorial
Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center, New York, New York 10021, USA.

Netrins and their classical receptors--DCC and neogenin--play key roles in
neuronal guidance. Recent developments identify new roles for netrins in
epithelial and vascular morphogenesis. Netrins accomplish these effects, at
least in part, through binding to integrins and/or activating
integrin-associated kinases such as FAK and Fyn. Here we discuss these recent
findings and propose that integrins and classical netrin receptors cooperate to
regulate multiple aspects of development.
