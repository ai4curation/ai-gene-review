# A gene required for the specification of dorsal-ventral pattern in Drosophila appears to encode a serine protease.
**Authors:** DeLotto R, Spierer P
**Journal:** Nature (1986)
**DOI:** [10.1038/323688a0](https://doi.org/10.1038/323688a0)

## Abstract

1. Nature. 1986 Oct 23;323(6090):688-92. doi: 10.1038/323688a0.

A gene required for the specification of dorsal-ventral pattern in Drosophila
appears to encode a serine protease.

DeLotto R(1), Spierer P.

Author information:
(1)Département de Biologie moléculaire, Université de Genéve, 30, quai Ernest
Ansermet, CH-1211 Geneva, Switzerland.

The maternal effect gene snake is required for the establishment of the
dorsal-ventral axis during the embryonic development of Drosophila. The
molecular cloning of the gene and analysis of a complementary DNA sequence
suggest that the gene encodes a serine protease which is structurally similar to
proteases involved in blood clotting, peptide processing, and complement
fixation pathways.
