# ISX is a retinoic acid-sensitive gatekeeper that controls intestinal beta,beta-carotene absorption and vitamin A production.
**Authors:** Lobo GP, Hessel S, Eichinger A, Noy N, Moise AR, Wyss A, Palczewski K, von Lintig J
**Journal:** FASEB J (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1096/fj.09-150995](https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.09-150995)
**PMC:** [PMC2874479](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2874479/)

## Abstract

1. FASEB J. 2010 Jun;24(6):1656-66. doi: 10.1096/fj.09-150995. Epub 2010 Jan 8.

ISX is a retinoic acid-sensitive gatekeeper that controls intestinal
beta,beta-carotene absorption and vitamin A production.

Lobo GP(1), Hessel S, Eichinger A, Noy N, Moise AR, Wyss A, Palczewski K, von
Lintig J.

Author information:
(1)Department of Pharmacology, School of Medicine, Case Western Reserve
University, Cleveland, Ohio 44160, USA.

The uptake of dietary lipids from the small intestine is a complex process that
depends on the activities of specific membrane receptors with yet unknown
regulatory mechanisms. Using both mouse models and human cell lines, we show
here that intestinal lipid absorption by the scavenger receptor class B type 1
(SR-BI) is subject to control by retinoid signaling. Retinoic acid via retinoic
acid receptors induced expression of the intestinal transcription factor ISX.
ISX then repressed the expression of SR-B1 and the carotenoid-15,15'-oxygenase
Bcmo1. BCMO1 acts downstream of SR-BI and converts absorbed beta,beta-carotene
to the retinoic acid precursor, retinaldehyde. Using BCMO1-knockout mice, we
demonstrated increased intestinal SR-BI expression and systemic
beta,beta-carotene accumulation. SR-BI-dependent accumulation of
beta,beta-carotene was prevented by dietary retinoids that induced ISX
expression. Thus, our study revealed a diet-responsive regulatory network that
controls beta,beta-carotene absorption and vitamin A production by negative
feedback regulation. The role of SR-BI in the intestinal absorption of other
dietary lipids, including cholesterol, fatty acids, and tocopherols, implicates
retinoid signaling in the regulation of lipid absorption more generally and has
clinical implications for diseases associated with dyslipidemia.
