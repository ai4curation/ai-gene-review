## Abstract

1. Mol Gen Genet. 1975 Aug 5;139(2):121-32. doi: 10.1007/BF00264692.

Methionine biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. I. Genetical analysis of
auxotrophic mutants.

Masselot M, De Robichon-Szulmajster H.

In order to analyse how many structural genes are implicated in the specific
steps of the biosynthesis of methionine in Sacch. cerevisiae, a hundred mutants
were studied by complementation. 21 groups were defined named MET1 to MET25.
Neither recombination between independent mutants of the same complementation
group nor linkage between different groups was found. Preliminary to biochemical
studies, mutants of each complementation group were tested for their capacity to
utilize various precursors of methionine.
