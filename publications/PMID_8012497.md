# Two-dimensional polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis isolation and microsequencing of Pseudomonas aeruginosa proteins.
**Authors:** Michéa-Hamzehpour M, Sanchez JC, Epp SF, Paquet N, Hughes GJ, Hochstrasser D, Pechère JC
**Journal:** Enzyme Protein (1993)
**DOI:** [10.1159/000468649](https://doi.org/10.1159/000468649)

## Abstract

1. Enzyme Protein. 1993;47(1):1-8. doi: 10.1159/000468649.

Two-dimensional polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis isolation and microsequencing
of Pseudomonas aeruginosa proteins.

Michéa-Hamzehpour M(1), Sanchez JC, Epp SF, Paquet N, Hughes GJ, Hochstrasser D,
Pechère JC.

Author information:
(1)Département de Génétique et Microbiologie, Centre Médical Universitaire,
Genève, Suisse.

Outer membrane (OM) proteins of beta-lactam-susceptible and -resistant strains
of Pseudomonas aeruginosa were analyzed by 2-D polyacrylamide gel
electrophoresis. Carrier ampholytes, pH 4-8, and immobilized pH gradient (IPG),
pH 3.5-10.0, procedures were used. An acidic-protein spot (pI = 5.2) detected in
susceptible but not in an imipenem-resistant strain was sequenced and
twenty-five N-terminal amino acids had total homology with the OM protein D, the
imipenem-specific porin of P. aeruginosa. A basic-protein spot (pI = 9.0)
detected in ceftazidime-resistant, but not in a susceptible strain was sequenced
and fourteen N-terminal amino acids had homology with a beta-lactamase encoded
by the ampC gene of P. aeruginosa. The IPG procedure allows identification of
more than one hundred proteins of the OM fraction from a single gel. Detection
of beta-lactamase in OM fractions might reflect a periplasmic contamination, but
its anchorage within the OM cannot be ruled out.
