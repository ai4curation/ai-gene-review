# Interleukin-1 beta secretion from cord blood mononuclear cells in vitro involves P2X 7 receptor activation.
**Authors:** Warren AY, Harvey L, Shaw RW, Khan RN
**Journal:** Reprod Sci (2008)
**DOI:** [10.1177/1933719107310710](https://doi.org/10.1177/1933719107310710)

## Abstract

1. Reprod Sci. 2008 Feb;15(2):189-94. doi: 10.1177/1933719107310710. Epub 2007
Dec  18.

Interleukin-1 beta secretion from cord blood mononuclear cells in vitro involves
P2X 7 receptor activation.

Warren AY(1), Harvey L, Shaw RW, Khan RN.

Author information:
(1)Centre for Reproduction and Early Life, Institute for Clinical Research,
University of Nottingham, UK.

The purinergic receptor P2X(7) is activated by extracellular adenosine
5'-triphosphate (ATP) and promotes the efficient release of interleukin-1 beta.
The authors examine protein and molecular expression of the P2X(7) receptor and
its ability to stimulate interleukin-1 beta release in cord blood mononuclear
cells (CBMCs) from placentae of term nonlaboring and laboring women. They show
both mRNA and protein (78 kDa) expression for the P2X( 7) receptor in CBMCs of
parturient and nonparturient women. Costimulation of CBMCs in vitro with
bacterial endotoxin and ATP resulted in significantly (P < .05) enhanced
interleukin-1 beta release in laboring (54.17 +/- 24.78 pg/mL(-1); n = 8)
compared with nonlaboring (13.60 +/- 3.20 pg/mL(-1); n = 7) deliveries. Release
of interleukin-1 beta in both groups was blocked by preincubation with oxidized
ATP, a P2X(7) receptor antagonist. The authors provide evidence for a novel
inflammatory pathway in the release of interleukin-1 beta, which may be linked
to the onset of labor.
