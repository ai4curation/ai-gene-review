# Human mu opiate receptor. cDNA and genomic clones, pharmacologic characterization and chromosomal assignment.
**Authors:** Wang JB, Johnson PS, Persico AM, Hawkins AL, Griffin CA, Uhl GR
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (1994)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0014-5793(94)80368-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(94)80368-4)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1994 Jan 31;338(2):217-22. doi: 10.1016/0014-5793(94)80368-4.

Human mu opiate receptor. cDNA and genomic clones, pharmacologic
characterization and chromosomal assignment.

Wang JB(1), Johnson PS, Persico AM, Hawkins AL, Griffin CA, Uhl GR.

Author information:
(1)Molecular Neurobiology Branch, NIDA, Baltimore, MD 21224.

A human mu opiate receptor cDNA has been identified from a cerebral cortical
cDNA library using sequences from the rat mu opiate receptor cDNA. The human mu
opiate receptor (h mu OR1) shares 95% amino acid identity with the rat sequence.
The expressed mu OR1 recognized tested opiate drugs and opioid peptides in a
sodium- and GTP-sensitive fashion with affinities virtually identical to those
displayed by the rat mu opiate receptor. Effects on cyclic AMP are similar to
those noted for the rat mu opiate receptor. An 18 kb genomic clone hybridizing
with the h mu OR1 cDNA contains 63 and 489 bp exonic sequences flanked by splice
donor/acceptor sequences. Analysis of hybridization to DNA prepared from human
rodent hybrid cell lines and chromosomal in situ hybridization studies indicate
localization to 6q24-25. An MspI polymorphism, producing a 3.7 kb band, may
prove useful in assessing this gene's involvement in neuropsychiatric disorders
involving opiatergic systems.
