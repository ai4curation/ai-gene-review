# Activation of the reaper gene during ectopic cell killing in Drosophila.
**Authors:** Nordstrom W, Chen P, Steller H, Abrams JM
**Journal:** Dev Biol (1996)
**DOI:** [10.1006/dbio.1996.0296](https://doi.org/10.1006/dbio.1996.0296)

## Abstract

1. Dev Biol. 1996 Nov 25;180(1):213-26. doi: 10.1006/dbio.1996.0296.

Activation of the reaper gene during ectopic cell killing in Drosophila.

Nordstrom W(1), Chen P, Steller H, Abrams JM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Cell Biology and Neuroscience, University of Texas Southwestern
Medical Center, Dallas 75235-9039, USA.

The product of the reaper (rpr) gene is required for programmed cell death in
Drosophila. We examined rpr expression during ectopic cell deaths caused by
ionizing radiation or aberrant development. In both instances, dramatic
induction of rpr expression was observed. A genomic fragment upstream of rpr
confers this regulatory behavior upon a lacZ reporter transgene. In a model cell
culture system, conditional expression of REAPER is sufficient to induce massive
apoptosis that can be prevented by the anti-apoptotic protein p35. Overall,
these results suggest that diverse signals converge at, or upstream of,
rpr-associated transcriptional regulatory elements that can function to initiate
a common apoptotic pathway involving ICE-like protease activity.
