1. J Bacteriol. 1980 Mar;141(3):1291-7. doi: 10.1128/jb.141.3.1291-1297.1980.

Beta-alanine synthesis in Escherichia coli.

Cronan JE Jr.

The enzyme, aspartate 1-decarboxylase (L-aspartate 1-carboxy-lyase; EC
4.1.1.15), that catalyzes the reaction aspartate leads to beta-alanine + CO2 was
found in extracts of Escherichia coli. panD mutants of E. coli are defective in
beta-alanine biosynthesis and lack aspartate 1-decarboxylase. Therefore, the
enzyme functions in the biosynthesis of the beta-alanine moiety of pantothenate.
The genetic lesion in these mutants is closely linked to the other pantothenate
(pan) loci of E. coli K-12.
