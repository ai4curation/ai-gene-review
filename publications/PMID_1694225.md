## Abstract

1. J Exp Med. 1990 Jul 1;172(1):379-82. doi: 10.1084/jem.172.1.379.

A single fragment of a malaria merozoite surface protein remains on the parasite
during red cell invasion and is the target of invasion-inhibiting antibodies.

Blackman MJ(1), Heidrich HG, Donachie S, McBride JS, Holder AA.

Author information:
(1)Division of Parasitology, National Institute for Medical Research, London,
United Kingdom.

A complex of polypeptides derived from a precursor is present on the surface of
the malaria merozoite. During erythrocyte invasion only a small fragment from
this complex is retained on the parasite surface and carried into the newly
infected red cell. Antibodies to this fragment will interrupt invasion.
