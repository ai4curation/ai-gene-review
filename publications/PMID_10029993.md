# Disruption of six novel Saccharomyces cerevisiae genes reveals that YGL129c is necessary for growth in non-fermentable carbon sources, YGL128c for growth at low or high temperatures and YGL125w is implicated in the biosynthesis of methionine.

Disruption of six novel Saccharomyces cerevisiae genes reveals that YGL129c is
necessary for growth in non-fermentable carbon sources, YGL128c for growth at
low or high temperatures and YGL125w is implicated in the biosynthesis of
methionine.

Tizón B(1), Rodríguez-Torres AM, Cerdán ME.

Author information:
(1)Departamento de Biología Celular y Molecular, Universidad de La Coruña,
Spain.

Six open reading frames (ORFs) from chromosome, VII, YGL131c, YGL129c, YGL128c,
YGL125w, YGL124c and YGL121c, were disrupted by deletion cassettes with short
flanking regions homologous to the target locus (SFH). YGL129c is necessary for
growth in non-fermentable carbon sources, YGL128c for growth at low or high
temperatures and YGL125w is implicated in the biosynthesis of methionine. With
regard to the other ORFs, basic phenotypic analyses did not reveal any
significant clues about their function.
