# Distribution of phospholipid hydroperoxide glutathione peroxidase (PHGPx) in rat testis mitochondria.
**Authors:** Godeas C, Sandri G, Panfili E
**Journal:** Biochim Biophys Acta (1994)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0005-2736(94)90242-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-2736(94)90242-9)

## Abstract

1. Biochim Biophys Acta. 1994 Apr 20;1191(1):147-50. doi:
10.1016/0005-2736(94)90242-9.

Distribution of phospholipid hydroperoxide glutathione peroxidase (PHGPx) in rat
testis mitochondria.

Godeas C(1), Sandri G, Panfili E.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Biophysics and Macromolecular Chemistry,
University of Trieste, Italy.

The distribution of phospholipid hydroperoxide glutathione peroxidase (PHGPx) in
isolated rat testis mitochondria was investigated, using a reverse sucrose
density gradient centrifugation procedure for the separation of the inner and
outer membranes and the contact sites between the two membranes. The results
indicate that PHGPx is largely localized in the contact sites fraction. This
finding might therefore suggest that the enzyme has more than just an
antioxidant function.
