# An improved method to unravel phosphoacceptors in Ser/Thr protein kinase-phosphorylated substrates.
**Authors:** Molle V, Leiba J, Zanella-Cléon I, Becchi M, Kremer L
**Journal:** Proteomics (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1002/pmic.201000316](https://doi.org/10.1002/pmic.201000316)

## Abstract

1. Proteomics. 2010 Nov;10(21):3910-5. doi: 10.1002/pmic.201000316.

An improved method to unravel phosphoacceptors in Ser/Thr protein
kinase-phosphorylated substrates.

Molle V(1), Leiba J, Zanella-Cléon I, Becchi M, Kremer L.

Author information:
(1)Institut de Biologie et Chimie des Protéines (UMR), CNRS, Université Lyon1,
BioSciences, Lyon-Gerland, Lyon, France. vmolle@ibcp.fr

Identification of the phosphorylated residues of bacterial Ser/Thr protein
kinase (STPK) substrates still represents a challenging task. Herein, we present
a new strategy allowing the rapid determination of phosphoacceptors in kinase
substrates, essentially based on the dual expression of the kinase with its
substrate in the surrogate E. coli, followed by MS analysis in a single-step
procedure. The performance of this strategy is illustrated using two distinct
proteins from Mycobacterium tuberculosis as model substrates, the GroEL2 and
HspX chaperones. A comparative analysis with a standard method that includes
mass spectrometry analysis of in vitro phosphorylated substrates is also
addressed.
