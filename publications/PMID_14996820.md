# The eutD gene of Salmonella enterica encodes a protein with phosphotransacetylase enzyme activity.
**Authors:** Brinsmade SR, Escalante-Semerena JC
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (2004)
**DOI:** [10.1128/JB.186.6.1890-1892.2004](https://doi.org/10.1128/JB.186.6.1890-1892.2004)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 2004 Mar;186(6):1890-2. doi: 10.1128/JB.186.6.1890-1892.2004.

The eutD gene of Salmonella enterica encodes a protein with
phosphotransacetylase enzyme activity.

Brinsmade SR(1), Escalante-Semerena JC.

Author information:
(1)Department of Bacteriology, University of Wisconsin-Madison, Madison,
Wisconsin 53726, USA.

The EutD protein of Salmonella enterica is homologous to the catalytic domain of
the phosphotransacetylase (Pta) enzyme. The Pta-like activity level of the EutD
enzyme compared favorably to that of other Pta enzymes. High-pressure liquid
chromatography and mass spectrometry verified that acetyl-coenzyme A was the
product of the reaction. The EutD protein restored growth of an S. enterica pta
strain on acetate as the source of carbon and energy.
