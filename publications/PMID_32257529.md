Correction to: Bone Research 10.1038/boneres.2017.44, published online 26 September 2017

During a reread of our article previously published in Bone Research, we regrettably found that the second lane of originally published Fig. 7b was labeled incorrectly. It should be siAFF4. And in Figs. 6b and Fig. 7b, the images of a-tublin were inadvertently misused. The correct images have been replaced. We sincerely apologize for this oversight, but it does not affect any of our original conclusions.Fig. 6Fig. 7
