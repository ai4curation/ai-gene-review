# [Molecular cloning of human sperm surface protein P34H gene and semi-quantitative analysis of its expression in testis and epididymidis].
**Authors:** Xia XY, Xu XF, Gao Y, Huang YF
**Journal:** Zhonghua Nan Ke Xue (2003)

## Abstract

1. Zhonghua Nan Ke Xue. 2003 Feb;9(1):24-7.

[Molecular cloning of human sperm surface protein P34H gene and
semi-quantitative analysis of its expression in testis and epididymidis].

[Article in Chinese]

Xia XY(1), Xu XF, Gao Y, Huang YF.

Author information:
(1)College of Life Science, Nanjing Normal University, Nanjing, Jiangsu 210097,
China.

OBJECTIVES: To identify, clone and sequence human sperm protein P34H gene and
perform semi-quantitative analysis of its expression in testis and epididymidis.
METHODS: Total RNA was prepared from human epididymidis tissue. cDNA fragment
encoding human P34H was amplified by RT-PCR using specific primers, and then was
cloned into pGEM-T vector. Inserted P34H gene was sequenced by ABI 377 DNA
Sequencer. Meanwhile, semi-quantitative analysis of P34H expression in testis
and caput, corpus, cauda epididymidis was done by RT-PCR based on beta-actin as
an inner control.
RESULTS: Human sperm protein P34H gene was successfully cloned and its cDNA
sequence was submitted to GenBank(Accession No. AF515625).
CONCLUSIONS: The cloning of P34H gene may be useful for the further basic and
clinical studies on P34H.
