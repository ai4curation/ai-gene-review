# The C. elegans gon-2 gene encodes a putative TRP cation channel protein required for mitotic cell cycle progression.
**Authors:** West RJ, Sun AY, Church DL, Lambie EJ
**Journal:** Gene (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0378-1119(01)00373-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0378-1119(01)00373-0)

## Abstract

1. Gene. 2001 Mar 21;266(1-2):103-10. doi: 10.1016/s0378-1119(01)00373-0.

The C. elegans gon-2 gene encodes a putative TRP cation channel protein required
for mitotic cell cycle progression.

West RJ(1), Sun AY, Church DL, Lambie EJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biological Sciences, 115 Gilman, Dartmouth College, Hanover NH
03755, USA.

The C. elegans gon-2 gene is required for the post-embryonic mitotic cell
divisions of the gonadal precursor cells. A single major transcript of
approximately 6.7 kb is derived from the gon-2 locus. This mRNA encodes a
protein related to the TRP family of cation channels and has a high degree of
similarity to several vertebrate genes, including melastatin. Mutant alleles of
gon-2 affect evolutionarily conserved amino acid residues. Northern analyses
suggest that gon-2 expression is not limited to gonadal tissues.
