# ATPase activity of SopA, a protein essential for active partitioning of F plasmid.
**Authors:** Watanabe E, Wachi M, Yamasaki M, Nagai K
**Journal:** Mol Gen Genet (1992)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF00538693](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00538693)

## Abstract

1. Mol Gen Genet. 1992 Sep;234(3):346-52. doi: 10.1007/BF00538693.

ATPase activity of SopA, a protein essential for active partitioning of F
plasmid.

Watanabe E(1), Wachi M, Yamasaki M, Nagai K.

Author information:
(1)Department of Agricultural Chemistry, University of Tokyo, Japan.

The sopA, B, C genes of the F plasmid play an essential role in plasmid
partitioning during cell division in Escherichia coli. In this paper, the
products of the sopA and sopB genes were isolated and their biochemical
activities studied. [alpha-32P]ATP was cross-linked to the SopA protein by UV
irradiation; this cross-linking was observed only in the presence of magnesium
ion, and was competitively inhibited in the presence of non-radioactive ATP, ADP
and dATP, but not other NTPs or dNTPs. In contrast, no ATP binding activity was
detected for the SopB protein. The SopA protein showed a modest magnesium
ion-dependent ATPase activity and this activity was stimulated in the presence
of DNA. The ATPase activity in the presence of DNA was further stimulated by
addition of the SopB protein. However, the SopB protein alone failed to
stimulate the ATPase activity.
