## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 1993 Mar 29;320(1):9-12. doi: 10.1016/0014-5793(93)81646-h.

Tyr-571 is involved in the T7 RNA polymerase binding to its promoter.

Rechinsky VO(1), Tunitskaya VL, Dragan SM, Kostyuk DA, Kochetkov SN.

Author information:
(1)V.A. Engelhardt Institute of Molecular Biology, Russian Academy of Sciences,
Moscow.

The in vitro studies of three T7 RNA polymerase point mutants suggest that
substitutions of Ala and Thr for Pro-563 and of Ser for Tyr-571 have little
effect on the enzyme catalytic competence, but result in its inability to
utilize the promoter. Both P563A and P563T mutants retain the promoter-binding
ability, whereas the promoter affinity of the Y571S mutant drops drastically.
