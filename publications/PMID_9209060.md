# Characterization of thiI, a new gene involved in thiazole biosynthesis in Salmonella typhimurium.
**Authors:** Webb E, Claas K, Downs DM

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1997 Jul;179(13):4399-402. doi: 10.1128/jb.179.13.4399-4402.1997.

Characterization of thiI, a new gene involved in thiazole biosynthesis in
Salmonella typhimurium.

Webb E(1), Claas K, Downs DM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Bacteriology, University of Wisconsin-Madison, 53706, USA.

Thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP) is a required cofactor in Salmonella typhimurium
that is generated de novo by the condensation of 4-amino-5-hydroxymethyl
pyrimidine (HMP) pyrophosphate and 4-methyl-5-(beta-hydroxyethyl)-thiazole (THZ)
monophosphate. The THZ and HMP moieties are independently synthesized, and
labeling studies have demonstrated probable metabolic precursors to both. We
present herein the initial characterization of thiI, a gene required for THZ
synthesis. We show that thiI is a 1,449-bp open reading frame located at minute
9.65 on the S. typhimurium chromosome and that it encodes a 483-amino-acid
protein with a predicted molecular mass of 55 kDa. Unlike genes in the thiamine
biosynthetic operon at minute 90, thiI is not transcriptionally regulated by
TPP.
