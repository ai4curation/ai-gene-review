# Isolation of two novel human RhoGEFs, ARHGEF3 and ARHGEF4, in 3p13-21 and 2q22.
**Authors:** Thiesen S, Kübart S, Ropers HH, Nothwang HG
**Journal:** Biochem Biophys Res Commun (2000)
**DOI:** [10.1006/bbrc.2000.2925](https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.2000.2925)

## Abstract

1. Biochem Biophys Res Commun. 2000 Jun 24;273(1):364-9. doi:
10.1006/bbrc.2000.2925.

Isolation of two novel human RhoGEFs, ARHGEF3 and ARHGEF4, in 3p13-21 and 2q22.

Thiesen S(1), Kübart S, Ropers HH, Nothwang HG.

Author information:
(1)Max Planck Institut für Molekulare Genetik, Ihnestrasse 73, Berlin-Dahlem,
D-14195, Germany.

RhoGEFs play an important role in various signaling cascades and are implicated
in human conditions like cancer and mental retardation. A database search
combined with screening of a human neuronal teratocarcinoma library identified
two novel RhoGEFs, ARHGEF3 and ARHGEF4 (HGMW-approved symbols). The widely
expressed ARHGEF3 transcript of 3561 nucleotides encodes a polypeptide of 526
amino acids with homology to NET1. The ARHGEF4 gene generates two transcripts of
3665 and 4000 nucleotides that translate into 720 amino acid residues.
Expression of ARHGEF4 is restricted to brain and the encoded protein shows
homology to collybistin. FISH analysis of genomic clones mapped ARHGEF3 to
3p13-21 and ARHGEF4 to 2q22.
