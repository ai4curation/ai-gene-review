# Ribosomal protein S1 functions as a termination factor in RNA synthesis by Qβ phage replicase.
**Authors:** Vasilyev NN, Kutlubaeva ZS, Ugarov VI, Chetverina HV, Chetverin AB
**Journal:** Nat Commun (2013)
**DOI:** [10.1038/ncomms2807](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms2807)

## Abstract

1. Nat Commun. 2013;4:1781. doi: 10.1038/ncomms2807.

Ribosomal protein S1 functions as a termination factor in RNA synthesis by Qβ
phage replicase.

Vasilyev NN(1), Kutlubaeva ZS, Ugarov VI, Chetverina HV, Chetverin AB.

Author information:
(1)Institute of Protein Research of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Pushchino,
Moscow 142290, Russia.

S1 is the largest ribosomal protein, and is vitally important for the cell. S1
is also a subunit of Qβ replicase, the RNA-directed RNA polymerase of
bacteriophage Qβ. In both protein and RNA syntheses, S1 is commonly believed to
bind to a template RNA at the initiation step, and not to be involved in later
events. Here, we show that in Qβ replicase-mediated RNA synthesis, S1 functions
at the termination step by promoting release of the product strand in a
single-stranded form. This function is fulfilled by the N-terminal fragment
comprising the first two S1 domains. The results suggest that S1 might also have
a role other than mRNA binding in the ribosome.
