Fig. 2: SIRV2 gp37 middle/late expression explains its lack of anti-CRISPR activity.
From: Natively expressed AcrIII-1 does not function as an anti-CRISPR protein
a, SIRV2 genome functional partitioning. b, Representative western blot analysis of early (gp48) and middle/late (gp17 and gp37) protein expression in SIRV2-infected cells. c, Schematic of SIRV2 Para-gp29 virus. d, Growth curves of CRISPR-III-B-positive cultures infected with parental SIRV2 or SIRV2 Para-gp29 (MOI, 0.1) with (ACV medium) or without (SCV medium) gp29 induction with 0.2% arabinose. e, The mean ± s.d. extracellular virus titre in cultures from d (36 h after infection). Data are from two biological replicates. f, The genomic context and regulation of vRING, AcrIIIB1 and AcrID homologues in crenarchaeal viruses.
