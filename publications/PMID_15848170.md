# Unrip is a component of SMN complexes active in snRNP assembly.
**Authors:** Carissimi C, Baccon J, Straccia M, Chiarella P, Maiolica A, Sawyer A, Rappsilber J, Pellizzoni L
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2005)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2005.03.034](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2005.03.034)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2005 Apr 25;579(11):2348-54. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2005.03.034.

Unrip is a component of SMN complexes active in snRNP assembly.

Carissimi C(1), Baccon J, Straccia M, Chiarella P, Maiolica A, Sawyer A,
Rappsilber J, Pellizzoni L.

Author information:
(1)Dulbecco Telethon Institute, Institute of Cell Biology (CNR), Monterotondo
Scalo, Rome, Italy.

A macromolecular complex containing survival of motor neurons (SMN), the spinal
muscular atrophy protein, and Gemin2-7 interacts with Sm proteins and snRNAs to
carry out the assembly of these components into spliceosomal small nuclear
ribonucleoproteins (snRNPs). Here we report the characterization of
unr-interacting protein (unrip), a GH-WD protein of unknown function, as a
component of the SMN complex that interacts directly with Gemin6 and Gemin7.
Unrip also binds a subset of Sm proteins, and unrip-containing SMN complexes are
necessary and sufficient to mediate the assembly of spliceosomal snRNPs. These
results demonstrate that unrip functions in the pathway of snRNP biogenesis and
is a marker of cellular SMN complexes active in snRNP assembly.
