**Authors:** Taniguchi H, Ohta H, Ogawa M, Mizuguchi Y
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (1985)
**DOI:** [10.1128/jb.162.2.510-515.1985](https://doi.org/10.1128/jb.162.2.510-515.1985)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1985 May;162(2):510-5. doi: 10.1128/jb.162.2.510-515.1985.

Cloning and expression in Escherichia coli of Vibrio parahaemolyticus
thermostable direct hemolysin and thermolabile hemolysin genes.

Taniguchi H, Ohta H, Ogawa M, Mizuguchi Y.

Two hemolysin genes of Vibrio parahaemolyticus WP1, a thermostable direct (TSD)
hemolysin gene and a thermolabile hemolysin gene, were cloned into the pBR322
vector in Escherichia coli K-12 C600. A large amount of the TSD hemolysin
produced in E. coli K-12 accumulated in the periplasmic space. The TSD hemolysin
gene was localized on a 0.9-kilobase HindIII-BamHI fragment by identifying
qualitatively the production of the TSD hemolysin by a reverse passive
hemagglutination assay in the osmotic shock fluid. The thermolabile hemolysin
gene was isolated on a 1.3-kilobase HindIII-PstI fragment by selection with the
hemolysin on blood agar. Southern blot hybridization and colony hybridization
experiments indicated that the TSD hemolysin gene was present in the chromosomal
DNA of 15 Kanagawa phenomenon-positive strains but not in 14 negative strains,
whereas the thermolabile hemolysin gene was detected in all strains. No
homologous DNA sequences to TSD and thermolabile hemolysin genes were detected
in the chromosomes of Vibrio cholerae, Vibrio vulnificus, non-O1 V. cholerae,
and Vibrio anguillarum.
