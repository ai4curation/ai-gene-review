# Changes in auxin response from mutations in an AUX/IAA gene.
**Authors:** Rouse D, Mackay P, Stirnberg P, Estelle M, Leyser O
**Journal:** Science (1998)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.279.5355.1371](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.279.5355.1371)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1998 Feb 27;279(5355):1371-3. doi: 10.1126/science.279.5355.1371.

Changes in auxin response from mutations in an AUX/IAA gene.

Rouse D(1), Mackay P, Stirnberg P, Estelle M, Leyser O.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biology, University of York, York YO1 5YW, UK.

Transcription of the AUX/IAA family of genes is rapidly induced by the plant
hormone auxin, but evidence that AUX/IAA genes mediate further responses to
auxin has been elusive. Changes in diverse auxin responses result from mutations
in the Arabidopsis AXR3 gene. AXR3 was shown to be a member of the AUX/IAA
family, providing direct evidence that AUX/IAA genes are central in auxin
signaling. Molecular characterization of axr3 gain-of-function and
loss-of-function mutations established the functional importance of domains
conserved among AUX/IAA proteins.
