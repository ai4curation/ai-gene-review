# p14ARF interacts with N-Myc and inhibits its transcriptional activity.
**Authors:** Amente S, Gargano B, Diolaiti D, Della Valle G, Lania L, Majello B
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2007)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2007.01.062](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2007.01.062)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2007 Mar 6;581(5):821-5. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2007.01.062. Epub
2007 Feb 2.

p14ARF interacts with N-Myc and inhibits its transcriptional activity.

Amente S(1), Gargano B, Diolaiti D, Della Valle G, Lania L, Majello B.

Author information:
(1)Department of Structural and Functional Biology, University of Naples
Federico II, Naples, Italy.

In this study, we report that the human p14(ARF) associates in vivo with the
N-Myc and inhibits N-Myc mediated transcriptional activation. We have determined
that the region (aa 140-300) encompassing the N-Myc BoxIII is required for
efficient interaction in vivo. Furthermore, we demonstrate that in the SK-N-BE
neuroblastoma cell line p14(ARF) over-expression delocalized N-Myc from the
nucleoplasm into nucleoli and that N-Myc regions required for interaction with
p14(ARF) are also important for nucleoli co-localization. Finally, we determine
that the N-terminal region of the p14(ARF) protein is involved in binding to
c-Myc and N-Myc proteins.
