Update in

10.1073/pnas.2318601121.

WDR76 is a multifunctional protein involved in many cellular functions. With a
diverse and complicated protein interaction network, dissecting the structure
and function of specific WDR76 complexes is needed. We previously demonstrated
the ability of the Serial Capture Affinity Purification (SCAP) method to isolate
specific complexes by introducing two proteins of interest as baits at the same
time. Here, we applied SCAP to dissect a subpopulation of WDR76 in complex with
SPIN1, a histone marker reader that specifically recognizes trimethylated
histone H3 lysine4 (H3K4me3). In contrast to the SCAP analysis of the
SPIN1:SPINDOC complex, H3K4me3 was copurified with the WDR76:SPIN1 complex. In
combination with crosslinking mass spectrometry, we built an integrated
structural model of the complex which revealed that SPIN1 recognized the H3K4me3
epigenetic mark while interacting with WDR76. Lastly, interaction network
analysis of copurifying proteins revealed the potential role of the WDR76:SPIN1
complex in the DNA damage response.
TEASER: In contrast to the SPINDOC/SPIN1 complex, analyses reveal that the
WDR76/SPIN1 complex interacts with core histones and is involved in DNA damage.
