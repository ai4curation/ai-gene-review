# Expression and purification of the high-affinity phosphate transporter of Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
**Authors:** Berhe A, Fristedt U, Persson BL
**Journal:** Eur J Biochem (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1111/j.1432-1033.1995.tb20426.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1432-1033.1995.tb20426.x)

## Abstract

1. Eur J Biochem. 1995 Jan 15;227(1-2):566-72. doi:
10.1111/j.1432-1033.1995.tb20426.x.

Expression and purification of the high-affinity phosphate transporter of
Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Berhe A(1), Fristedt U, Persson BL.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry, Arrhenius Laboratories for Natural Sciences,
University of Stockholm, Sweden.

The plasma membrane high-affinity phosphate permease of Saccharomyces cerevisiae
has been overproduced as a stable membrane-bound chimeric protein in Escherichia
coli. Construction of a chimera between the permease and a peptide containing 10
consecutive histidine residues allowed selective binding of the chimera to a
chelating column charged with Ni2+, and elution with imidazole in a high state
of purity. Approximately 5 mg purified His10-permease was obtained from 3 g (wet
mass) cells. The purified phosphate permease chimera catalyzes
uncoupler-sensitive phosphate transport after reconstitution into
proteoliposomes.
