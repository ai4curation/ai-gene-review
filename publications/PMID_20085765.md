# Noxa is necessary for hydrogen peroxide-induced caspase-dependent cell death.
**Authors:** Aikawa T, Shinzawa K, Tanaka N, Tsujimoto Y
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2010)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2010.01.026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2010.01.026)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2010 Feb 19;584(4):681-8. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2010.01.026. Epub
2010 Jan 19.

Noxa is necessary for hydrogen peroxide-induced caspase-dependent cell death.

Aikawa T(1), Shinzawa K, Tanaka N, Tsujimoto Y.

Author information:
(1)Department of Medical Genetics, Osaka University Medical School, Osaka,
Japan.

Oxidative stress induces apoptosis or necrosis of many cell types, which can
cause tissue injury. Hydrogen peroxide (H(2)O(2)) induced apoptotic death of
Jurkat cells. This effect was inhibited by overexpression of human Bcl-2, by
silencing of cytochrome c, and by ablation of Bax/Bak, indicating that
H(2)O(2)-induced apoptosis was mediated by the mitochondrial pathway in Jurkat
cells. Treatment with H(2)O(2) caused an increase of Noxa protein, via
activating transcription factor 4-dependent accumulation of Noxa mRNA and
inhibition of Noxa protein degradation. H(2)O(2)-induced apoptosis was strongly
suppressed by silencing of Noxa, indicating that Noxa plays a crucial role in
this form of apoptosis.
