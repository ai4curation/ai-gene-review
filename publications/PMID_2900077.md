# Isolation of a DNA fragment which complements glutamine synthetase deficient strains of S. pombe.
**Authors:** Barel I, Bignell G, Simpson A, MacDonald D
**Journal:** Curr Genet (1988)
**DOI:** [10.1007/BF02427754](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02427754)

## Abstract

1. Curr Genet. 1988 Jun;13(6):487-94. doi: 10.1007/BF02427754.

Isolation of a DNA fragment which complements glutamine synthetase deficient
strains of S. pombe.

Barel I(1), Bignell G, Simpson A, MacDonald D.

Author information:
(1)Department of Genetics, Cambridge, UK.

From a gene bank of S. pombe DNA, a 5.6 kb clone was isolated which complemented
mutants defective in glutamine synthetase (GS) activity. Sub-cloning fragments
of this 5.6 kb clone showed that the complementing activity was localised in a
1.6 kb HindIII-AvaI fragment and a partial DNA sequence revealed an open reading
frame preceded by TATA sequences and a TGACTA sequence. Plasmid constructs
carrying up to 3.4 kb of DNA used to transform gln- strains gave transformants
which showed a wide range of GS activity, in some cases 100 times the wild-type
level. These constructs identify DNA sequences lying downstream from the
putative coding sequence which have effects on the total amount of enzyme
activity, but do not affect the control imposed by the nitrogen source on which
the cells are grown.
