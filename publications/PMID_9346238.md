Comment in
    Cell. 1997 Oct 17;91(2):149-51. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80396-7.

We have reconstituted the ubiquitination pathway for the Cdk inhibitor Sic1
using recombinant proteins. Skp1, Cdc53, and the F-box protein Cdc4 form a
complex, SCFCdc4, which functions as a Sic1 ubiquitin-ligase (E3) in combination
with the ubiquitin conjugating enzyme (E2) Cdc34 and E1. Cdc4 assembled with
Skp1 functions as the receptor that selectively binds phosphorylated Sic1. Grr1,
an F-box protein involved in Cln destruction, forms complexes with Skp1 and
Cdc53 and binds phosphorylated Cln1 and Cln2, but not Sic1. Because the
constituents of the SCF complex are members of protein families, SCFCdc4 is
likely to serve as the prototype for a large class of E3s formed by
combinatorial interactions of related family members. SCF complexes couple
protein kinase signaling pathways to the control of protein abundance.
