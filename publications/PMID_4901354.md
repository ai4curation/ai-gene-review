# Evidence for two genes specifically involved in unsaturated fatty acid biosynthesis in Escherichia coli.
**Authors:** Cronan JE Jr, Birge CH, Vagelos PR
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (1969)
**DOI:** [10.1128/jb.100.2.601-604.1969](https://doi.org/10.1128/jb.100.2.601-604.1969)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1969 Nov;100(2):601-4. doi: 10.1128/jb.100.2.601-604.1969.

Evidence for two genes specifically involved in unsaturated fatty acid
biosynthesis in Escherichia coli.

Cronan JE Jr, Birge CH, Vagelos PR.

Unsaturated fatty acid auxotrophs of Eschericha coli have been divided into two
distinct cistrons by extract complementation and genetic complementation based
on abortive transduction. Lesions in one cistron result in the loss of the
beta-hydroxydecanoyl thioester dehydrase which produces the first biosynthetic
intermediate in unsaturated fatty acid formation. Evidence is presented which
indicates that lesions in the second cistron result in the lack of a second
enzyme specifically involved in the biosynthesis of unsaturated fatty acids.
