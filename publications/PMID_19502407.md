Structural basis for the localization of the chemotaxis phosphatase CheZ by
CheAS.

Hao S(1), Hamel D, Zhou H, Dahlquist FW.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry, University of California-Santa
Barbara, Santa Barbara, CA 93106, USA.

CheA-short interacts with CheZ to localize CheZ to cell poles. The fifth helical
region (residues 112 to 133) from the phosphotransfer domain of CheA interacts
with CheZ and becomes ordered and helical, although it lacks a stable fold in
the CheA fragment comprising residues 98 to 150 alone. One CheA molecule binds
to one CheZ dimer.
