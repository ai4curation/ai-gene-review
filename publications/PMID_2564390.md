# Expression of human asparagine synthetase in Escherichia coli.
**Authors:** Van Heeke G, Schuster SM
**Journal:** J Biol Chem (1989)

## Abstract

1. J Biol Chem. 1989 Apr 5;264(10):5503-9.

Expression of human asparagine synthetase in Escherichia coli.

Van Heeke G(1), Schuster SM.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemistry, University of Nebraska, Lincoln 68588-0304.

Human asparagine synthetase was expressed in Escherichia coli. Synthesis of the
enzyme was demonstrated by immunoblotting and by complementation of asparagine
auxotrophy in E. coli. The recombinant enzyme was shown to have both the
ammonia- and glutamine-dependent asparagine synthetase activity in vitro.
Compared to asparagine synthetase isolated from beef pancreas, the one expressed
in E. coli migrated at a slightly slower rate on a denaturing protein gel. In
contrast with previous reports, the data obtained here strongly suggest that the
active enzyme is a homodimer. The production of soluble and active enzyme was
shown to be highly temperature-dependent. Expression at 37 degrees C yielded no
soluble enzyme, whereas growth at 30 and 21 degrees C favored the production of
soluble asparagine synthetase. The incubation temperature was also important for
complementation of asparagine auxotrophy in E. coli, as growth in the absence of
asparagine occurred at 30 degrees C and not at 37 degrees C.
