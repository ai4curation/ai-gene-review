# Analysis of synphilin-1 and synuclein interactions by yeast two-hybrid beta-galactosidase liquid assay.
**Authors:** Neystat M, Rzhetskaya M, Kholodilov N, Burke RE
**Journal:** Neurosci Lett (2002)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0304-3940(02)00253-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0304-3940(02)00253-7)

## Abstract

1. Neurosci Lett. 2002 Jun 7;325(2):119-23. doi: 10.1016/s0304-3940(02)00253-7.

Analysis of synphilin-1 and synuclein interactions by yeast two-hybrid
beta-galactosidase liquid assay.

Neystat M(1), Rzhetskaya M, Kholodilov N, Burke RE.

Author information:
(1)Department of Neurology, Columbia University, New York, NY 10032, USA.

Synphilin-1 interacts with alpha-synuclein, which has been implicated in the
pathogenesis of Parkinson's disease (PD). By examination of their interactions
quantitatively, with the use of the yeast two-hybrid beta-galactosidase assay,
we find that the synuclein amino acid (aa) 1-65 region is sufficient for an
interaction. A central domain of synphilin-1, aa 349-555, is both necessary and
sufficient for an interaction with alpha-synuclein. We did not observe an effect
of the synuclein A53T mutation, which causes one familial form of PD, on
interactions with synphilin-1. However, the A30P mutation caused an increase in
the interaction between the synuclein aa 1-65 fragment and the synphilin-1
central domain.
