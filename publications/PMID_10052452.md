# Coupling of coat assembly and vesicle budding to packaging of putative cargo receptors.
**Authors:** Bremser M, Nickel W, Schweikert M, Ravazzola M, Amherdt M, Hughes CA, Söllner TH, Rothman JE, Wieland FT
**Journal:** Cell (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80654-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80654-6)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 1999 Feb 19;96(4):495-506. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)80654-6.

Coupling of coat assembly and vesicle budding to packaging of putative cargo
receptors.

Bremser M(1), Nickel W, Schweikert M, Ravazzola M, Amherdt M, Hughes CA, Söllner
TH, Rothman JE, Wieland FT.

Author information:
(1)Biochemie-Zentrum Heidelberg, University of Heidelberg, Germany.

COPI-coated vesicle budding from lipid bilayers whose composition resembles
mammalian Golgi membranes requires coatomer, ARF, GTP, and cytoplasmic tails of
putative cargo receptors (p24 family proteins) or membrane cargo proteins
(containing the KKXX retrieval signal) emanating from the bilayer surface.
Liposome-derived COPI-coated vesicles are similar to their native counterparts
with respect to diameter, buoyant density, morphology, and the requirement for
an elevated temperature for budding. These results suggest that a bivalent
interaction of coatomer with membrane-bound ARF[GTP] and with the cytoplasmic
tails of cargo or putative cargo receptors is the molecular basis of COPI coat
assembly and provide a simple mechanism to couple uptake of cargo to transport
vesicle formation.
