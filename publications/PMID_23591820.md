In eukaryotes, mRNA export involves many evolutionarily conserved factors that
carry the nascent transcript to the nuclear pore complex (NPC). The THO/TREX
complex couples transcription to mRNA export and recruits the mRNA export
receptor NXF1 for the transport of messenger ribonucleoprotein particles (mRNP)
to the NPC. The transcription and export complex 2 (TREX-2) was suggested to
interact with NXF1 and to shuttle between transcription sites and the NPC. Here,
we characterize the dynamics of human TREX-2 and show that it stably associates
with the NPC basket. Moreover, the association of TREX-2 with the NPC requires
the basket nucleoporins NUP153 and TPR, but is independent of transcription.
Differential profiles of mRNA nuclear accumulation reveal that TREX-2 functions
similarly to basket nucleoporins, but differently from NXF1. Thus, our results
show that TREX-2 is an NPC-associated complex in mammalian cells and suggest
that it is involved in putative NPC basket-related functions.
