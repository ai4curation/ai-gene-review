Erratum in
    J Integr Plant Biol. 2018 Oct;60(10):1015. doi: 10.1111/jipb.12710.

LHP1 mediates recruitment of the PRC2 histone methyltransferase complex to
chromatin and thereby facilitates maintenance of H3K27me3 on FLC, a key
flowering repressor gene. Here, we report that the PWWP domain proteins (PDPs)
interact with FVE and MSI5 to suppress FLC expression and thereby promote
flowering. We demonstrated that FVE, MSI5, and PDP3 were co-purified with LHP1.
The H3K27me3 level on FLC was decreased in the pdp mutants as well as in the
fve/msi5 double mutant. This study suggests that PDPs function together with FVE
and MSI5 to regulate the function of the PRC2 complex on FLC.
