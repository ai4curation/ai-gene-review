PTK7 (protein tyrosine kinase 7) is an evolutionarily conserved transmembrane
receptor with functions in various processes ranging from embryonic
morphogenesis to epidermal wound repair. Here, we review recent findings
indicating that PTK7 is a versatile co-receptor that functions as a molecular
switch in Wnt, Semaphorin/Plexin and VEGF signaling pathways. We focus in
particular on the role of PTK7 in Wnt signaling, as recent data indicate that
PTK7 acts as a Wnt co-receptor, which activates the planar cell polarity
pathway, but inhibits canonical Wnt signaling.
