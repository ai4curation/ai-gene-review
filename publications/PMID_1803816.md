The allantoinase (DAL1) gene of Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Erratum in

The allantoinase (DAL1) gene from Saccharomyces cerevisiae has been cloned,
sequenced, and found to encode a 472 amino acid protein with a Mr of 52,028.
DAL1 is expressed in an inducer-independent manner in strain M970 (sigma 1278b
genetic background) and modestly responds to mutation of the dal80 locus.
Expression was also sensitive to nitrogen catabolite repression (NCR).
Correlated with these expression characteristics, the upstream region of DAL1
contained five copies of a sequence that is homologous to the DAL UASNTR element
previously shown to be required for transcriptional activation and NCR
sensitivity of the DAL5 and DAL7 genes. Missing from the DAL1 5' flanking region
were any sequences with significant homology to the DAL7 UIS element required
for response to inducer. These observations further support the roles of UASNTR
and DAL7 UIS in the regulation of allantoin pathway gene expression.
