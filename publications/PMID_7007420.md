## Abstract

1. J Clin Microbiol. 1981 Jan;13(1):163-6. doi: 10.1128/jcm.13.1.163-166.1981.

Variable assimilation of carbon compounds by Candida albicans.

Syverson RE.

A total of 215 typical strains of Candida albicans were studied for their
ability to assimilate 11 carbon compounds. All isolates assimilated lactic acid,
ribitol, succinic acid, methyl alpha-D-glucopyranoside. None of the isolates
assimilated cellobiose and salicin; 1.9% of the isolates assimilated
L-arabinose. Citric acid, glycerol, and L-sorbose were assimilated by greater
than 97% of the isolates, whereas melezitose was assimilated by 81% of the
isolates. Assimilation results depended on duration of incubation, temperature,
and methodology.
