# Episodes of generalized weakness in two sibs with the C164T mutation of the connexin 32 gene.
**Authors:** Panas M, Kalfakis N, Karadimas C, Vassilopoulos D
**Journal:** Neurology (2001)
**DOI:** [10.1212/wnl.57.10.1906](https://doi.org/10.1212/wnl.57.10.1906)

## Abstract

1. Neurology. 2001 Nov 27;57(10):1906-8. doi: 10.1212/wnl.57.10.1906.

Episodes of generalized weakness in two sibs with the C164T mutation of the
connexin 32 gene.

Panas M(1), Kalfakis N, Karadimas C, Vassilopoulos D.

Author information:
(1)Neurogenetics Unit, Department of Neurology, University of Athens, Greece.

Two sibs with Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease had repeated episodes of generalized
weakness. The patients had distal weakness and atrophy as well as findings of
CNS involvement on brain MRI. Both patients bear the C164T mutation of the
connexin 32 gene but no mutations of the genes responsible for hyper- or
hypokalemic periodic paralysis. It is possible that both patients have one
disease with complex phenotype due to abnormal expression of the connexin 32
gene.
