# Mechanistic insights into sulfur relay by multiple sulfur mediators involved in thiouridine biosynthesis at tRNA wobble positions.
**Authors:** Ikeuchi Y, Shigi N, Kato J, Nishimura A, Suzuki T
**Journal:** Mol Cell (2006)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.molcel.2005.11.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2005.11.001)

## Abstract

1. Mol Cell. 2006 Jan 6;21(1):97-108. doi: 10.1016/j.molcel.2005.11.001.

Mechanistic insights into sulfur relay by multiple sulfur mediators involved in
thiouridine biosynthesis at tRNA wobble positions.

Ikeuchi Y(1), Shigi N, Kato J, Nishimura A, Suzuki T.

Author information:
(1)Department of Chemistry and Biotechnology, Graduate School of Engineering,
University of Tokyo, 7-3-1 Hongo, Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo 113-8656, Japan.

The wobble bases of bacterial tRNAs responsible for NNR codons are modified to
5-methylaminomethyl-2-thiouridine (mnm5s2U). 2-thio modification of mnm5s2U is
required for accurate decoding and essential for normal cell growth. We
identified five genes yhhP, yheL, yheM, yheN, and yccK (named tusA, tusB, tusC,
tusD, and tusE, respectively) that are essential for 2-thiouridylation of
mnm5s2U by a systematic genome-wide screen ("ribonucleome analysis"). Efficient
2-thiouridine formation in vitro was reconstituted with recombinant TusA, a
TusBCD complex, TusE, and previously identified IscS and MnmA. The desulfurase
activity of IscS is stimulated by TusA binding. IscS transfers the persulfide
sulfur to TusA. TusE binds TusBCD complex and stimulates sulfur transfer from
TusA to TusD. TusE also interacts with an MnmA-tRNA complex. This study revealed
that 2-thiouridine formation proceeds through a complex sulfur-relay system
composed of multiple sulfur mediators that select and facilitate specific sulfur
flow to 2-thiouridine from various pathways of sulfur trafficking.
