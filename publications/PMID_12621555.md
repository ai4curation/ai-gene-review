# [Cloning of cDNA of TSARG4, a human spermatogenesis related gene].
**Authors:** Xing XW, Li LY, Fu JJ, Zhu WB, Liu G, Liu SF, Lu GX
**Journal:** Sheng Wu Hua Xue Yu Sheng Wu Wu Li Xue Bao (Shanghai) (2003)

## Abstract

1. Sheng Wu Hua Xue Yu Sheng Wu Wu Li Xue Bao (Shanghai). 2003 Mar;35(3):283-8.

[Cloning of cDNA of TSARG4, a human spermatogenesis related gene].

[Article in Chinese]

Xing XW(1), Li LY, Fu JJ, Zhu WB, Liu G, Liu SF, Lu GX.

Author information:
(1)Human Reproductive Engineering Institute, Central South University Xiangya
Medical School, Changsha 410078, China.

To understand molecular mechanism of spermatogenesis, two ESTs BG720564 and
AI700454, were found from SPAG4 (sperm antigen 4), a gene related to dense fiber
protein of outer membrane of the human sperm and mouse spermatocytes gene
AK006225. The gap was filled by polymerase chain reaction, and a 1252 bp
fragment was obtained. This 1252 bp fragment was named TSARG4 (testis and
spermatogenesis related gene 4 (GenBank accession number AF401350). Its opening
reading frame was 94-1233 bp, as was proved by RT-PCR in human testis. TSARG4
gene was located in 20q11.2, and the putative protein was 379 amino acid with a
theoretical molecular weight of 43 081 and isoelectric point of 8.61. The
homologies of amino acid sequences were 74% between TSARG4 and AK006225 gene and
45% between TSARG4 and SPAG4 gene, respectively. The human TSARG4 mRNA is
expressed in a wide range of adult tissues, including testis, whereas the
homologous mouse spermatocytes gene AK006225 is expressed specifically in the
testis.
