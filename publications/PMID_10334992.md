Comment in
    Science. 1999 May 21;284(5418):1285-6. doi: 10.1126/science.284.5418.1285.

Bile acids are essential for the solubilization and transport of dietary lipids
and are the major products of cholesterol catabolism. Results presented here
show that bile acids are physiological ligands for the farnesoid X receptor
(FXR), an orphan nuclear receptor. When bound to bile acids, FXR repressed
transcription of the gene encoding cholesterol 7alpha-hydroxylase, which is the
rate-limiting enzyme in bile acid synthesis, and activated the gene encoding
intestinal bile acid-binding protein, which is a candidate bile acid
transporter. These results demonstrate a mechanism by which bile acids
transcriptionally regulate their biosynthesis and enterohepatic transport.
