# The HtrA (DegP) protein, essential for Escherichia coli survival at high temperatures, is an endopeptidase.
**Authors:** Lipinska B, Zylicz M, Georgopoulos C
**Journal:** J Bacteriol (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1128/jb.172.4.1791-1797.1990](https://doi.org/10.1128/jb.172.4.1791-1797.1990)
**PMC:** [PMC208670](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC208670/)

## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1990 Apr;172(4):1791-7. doi: 10.1128/jb.172.4.1791-1797.1990.

The HtrA (DegP) protein, essential for Escherichia coli survival at high
temperatures, is an endopeptidase.

Lipinska B(1), Zylicz M, Georgopoulos C.

Author information:
(1)Department of Cellular, Viral and Molecular Biology, University of Utah
Medical Center, Salt Lake City 84132.

As a preliminary step in the understanding of the function of the Escherichia
coli HtrA (DegP) protein, which is indispensable for bacterial survival only at
elevated temperatures, the protein was purified and partially characterized. The
HtrA protein was purified from cells carrying the htrA gene cloned into a
multicopy plasmid, resulting in its overproduction. The sequence of the 13
N-terminal amino acids of the purified HtrA protein was determined and was
identical to the one predicted for the mature HtrA protein by the DNA sequence
of the cloned gene. Moreover, the N-terminal sequence showed that the
48-kilodalton HtrA protein is derived by cleavage of the first 26 amino acids of
the pre-HtrA precursor polypeptide and that the point of cleavage follows a
typical target sequence recognized by the leader peptidase enzyme. The HtrA
protein was shown to be a specific endopeptidase which was inhibited by
diisopropylfluorophosphate, suggesting that HtrA is a serine protease.
