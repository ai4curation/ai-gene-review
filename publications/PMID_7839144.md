# Independent human MAP-kinase signal transduction pathways defined by MEK and MKK isoforms.
**Authors:** Dérijard B, Raingeaud J, Barrett T, Wu IH, Han J, Ulevitch RJ, Davis RJ
**Journal:** Science (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1126/science.7839144](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7839144)

## Abstract

1. Science. 1995 Feb 3;267(5198):682-5. doi: 10.1126/science.7839144.

Independent human MAP-kinase signal transduction pathways defined by MEK and MKK
isoforms.

Dérijard B(1), Raingeaud J, Barrett T, Wu IH, Han J, Ulevitch RJ, Davis RJ.

Author information:
(1)Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Massachusetts
Medical School, Worcester 01605.

Erratum in
    Science 1995 Jul 7;269(5220):17.

Mammalian mitogen-activated protein (MAP) kinases include extracellular
signal-regulated protein kinase (ERK), c-Jun amino-terminal kinase (JNK), and
p38 subgroups. These MAP kinase isoforms are activated by dual phosphorylation
on threonine and tyrosine. Two human MAP kinase kinases (MKK3 and MKK4) were
cloned that phosphorylate and activate p38 MAP kinase. These MKK isoforms did
not activate the ERK subgroup of MAP kinases, but MKK4 did activate JNK. These
data demonstrate that the activators of p38 (MKK3 and MKK4), JNK (MKK4), and ERK
(MEK1 and MEK2) define independent MAP kinase signal transduction pathways.
