# FGF-19, a novel fibroblast growth factor with unique specificity for FGFR4.
**Authors:** Xie MH, Holcomb I, Deuel B, Dowd P, Huang A, Vagts A, Foster J, Liang J, Brush J, Gu Q, Hillan K, Goddard A, Gurney AL
**Journal:** Cytokine (1999)
**DOI:** [10.1006/cyto.1999.0485](https://doi.org/10.1006/cyto.1999.0485)

## Abstract

1. Cytokine. 1999 Oct;11(10):729-35. doi: 10.1006/cyto.1999.0485.

FGF-19, a novel fibroblast growth factor with unique specificity for FGFR4.

Xie MH(1), Holcomb I, Deuel B, Dowd P, Huang A, Vagts A, Foster J, Liang J,
Brush J, Gu Q, Hillan K, Goddard A, Gurney AL.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Biology, Genentech Inc., 1 DNA Way, South San
Francisco, CA 94080, USA.

We have identified a novel fibroblast growth factor, FGF-19, the most distant
member of the FGF family described to date. FGF-19 is a high affinity, heparin
dependent ligand for FGFR4 and is the first member of the FGF family to show
exclusive binding to FGFR4. Human FGF-19 maps to chromosome 11 q13.1, a region
associated with an osteoporosis-pseudoglioma syndrome of skeletal and retinal
defects. FGF-19 message is expressed in several tissues including fetal
cartilage, skin, and retina, as well as adult gall bladder and is overexpressed
in a colon adenocarcinoma cell line.
