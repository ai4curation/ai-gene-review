# Tumor suppressor NM23-H1 is a granzyme A-activated DNase during CTL-mediated apoptosis, and the nucleosome assembly protein SET is its inhibitor.
**Authors:** Fan Z, Beresford PJ, Oh DY, Zhang D, Lieberman J
**Journal:** Cell (2003)

Granzyme A (GzmA) induces a caspase-independent cell death pathway characterized
by single-stranded DNA nicks and other features of apoptosis. A GzmA-activated
DNase (GAAD) is in an ER associated complex containing pp32 and the GzmA
substrates SET, HMG-2, and Ape1. We show that GAAD is NM23-H1, a nucleoside
diphosphate kinase implicated in suppression of tumor metastasis, and its
specific inhibitor (IGAAD) is SET. NM23-H1 binds to SET and is released from
inhibition by GzmA cleavage of SET. After GzmA loading or CTL attack, SET and
NM23-H1 translocate to the nucleus and SET is degraded, allowing NM23-H1 to nick
chromosomal DNA. GzmA-treated cells with silenced NM23-H1 expression are
resistant to GzmA-mediated DNA damage and cytolysis, while cells overexpressing
NM23-H1 are more sensitive.
