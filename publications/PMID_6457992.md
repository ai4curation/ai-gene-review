lambda Repressor and cro--components of an efficient molecular switch.

Johnson AD, Poteete AR, Lauer G, Sauer RT, Ackers GK, Ptashne M.

In a lysogen, most genes of phage lambda are repressed; in response to a
transient induction signal, they are efficiently switched on. The switch, which
consists in part of a tripartite operator to which two regulatory proteins bind,
depends not only on DNA--protein interactions, but also on effects transmitted
from one DNA-bound protein to another. lambda Exemplifies a strategy that
facilitates efficient switching between two physiological states in response to
a transient signal.
