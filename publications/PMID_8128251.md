Some cases of hereditary nonpolyposis colorectal cancer (HNPCC) are due to
alterations in a mutS-related mismatch repair gene. A search of a large database
of expressed sequence tags derived from random complementary DNA clones revealed
three additional human mismatch repair genes, all related to the bacterial mutL
gene. One of these genes (hMLH1) resides on chromosome 3p21, within 1
centimorgan of markers previously linked to cancer susceptibility in HNPCC
kindreds. Mutations of hMLH1 that would disrupt the gene product were identified
in such kindreds, demonstrating that this gene is responsible for the disease.
These results suggest that defects in any of several mismatch repair genes can
cause HNPCC.
