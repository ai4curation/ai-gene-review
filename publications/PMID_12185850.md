# Mechanisms of spindle positioning: focus on flies and worms.

Accurate spindle positioning is crucial for spatial control of cell division.
During metazoan development, coordination between polarity cues and spindle
position also ensures correct segregation of cell fate determinants. Converging
evidence indicates that spindle positioning is achieved through interactions
between cortical anchors and the plus ends of microtubules, generating pulling
forces acting on spindle poles. This article discusses recent findings that
indicate how this mechanism might be used for spindle positioning during
Drosophila and Caenorhabditis elegans development.
