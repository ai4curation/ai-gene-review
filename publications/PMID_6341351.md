## Abstract

1. J Bacteriol. 1983 May;154(2):1002-4. doi: 10.1128/jb.154.2.1002-1004.1983.

Cloning of genes that complement yeast hexokinase and glucokinase mutants.

Walsh RB, Kawasaki G, Fraenkel DG.

Genes complementing the glucose-negative fructose-negative Saccharomyces
cerevisiae triple mutant strain (hxkl hxk2 glk1), which lacks hexokinase PI,
hexokinase PII, and glucokinase, were obtained from a pool of yeast DNA in the
multicopy plasmid YEp13.
