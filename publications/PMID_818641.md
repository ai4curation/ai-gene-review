## Abstract

1. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 1976 May;73(5):1684-8. doi: 10.1073/pnas.73.5.1684.

dunce, a mutant of Drosophila deficient in learning.

Dudai Y, Jan YN, Byers D, Quinn WG, Benzer S.

Normal Drosophilia learn to avoid an odorant associated with electric shock. An
X-linked mutant, dunce, has been isolated that fails to display this learning in
spite of being able to sense the odorant and electric shock and showing
essentially normal behavior in other respects.
