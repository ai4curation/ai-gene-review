# X-ray crystal structure of Saccharomyces cerevisiae Pdx1 provides insights into the oligomeric nature of PLP synthases.
**Authors:** Neuwirth M, Strohmeier M, Windeisen V, Wallner S, Deller S, Rippe K, Sinning I, Macheroux P, Tews I
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2009)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2009.06.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2009.06.009)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2009 Jul 7;583(13):2179-86. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2009.06.009.
Epub  2009 Jun 11.

X-ray crystal structure of Saccharomyces cerevisiae Pdx1 provides insights into
the oligomeric nature of PLP synthases.

Neuwirth M(1), Strohmeier M, Windeisen V, Wallner S, Deller S, Rippe K, Sinning
I, Macheroux P, Tews I.

Author information:
(1)Technische Universität Graz, Institut für Biochemie, Graz, Austria.

The universal enzymatic cofactor vitamin B6 can be synthesized as pyridoxal
5-phosphate (PLP) by the glutamine amidotransferase Pdx1. We show that
Saccharomyces cerevisiae Pdx1 is hexameric by analytical ultracentrifugation and
by crystallographic 3D structure determination. Bacterial homologues were
previously reported to exist in hexamer:dodecamer equilibrium. A small sequence
insertion found in yeast Pdx1 elevates the dodecamer dissociation constant when
introduced into Bacillus subtilis Pdx1. Further, we demonstrate that the yeast
Pdx1 C-terminus contacts an adjacent subunit, and deletion of this segment
decreases enzymatic activity 3.5-fold, suggesting a role in catalysis.
