Comment in
    Cell. 1998 Jul 10;94(1):1-4. doi: 10.1016/s0092-8674(00)81213-1.

PCAF histone acetylase plays a role in regulation of transcription, cell cycle
progression, and differentiation. Here, we show that PCAF is found in a complex
consisting of more than 20 distinct polypeptides. Strikingly, some polypeptides
are identical to TBP-associated factors (TAFs), which are subunits of TFIID.
Like TFIID, histone fold-containing factors are present within the PCAF complex.
The histone H3- and H2B-like subunits within the PCAF complex are identical to
those within TFIID, namely, hTAF(II)31 and hTAF(II)20/15, respectively. The PCAF
complex has a novel histone H4-like subunit with similarity to hTAF(II)80 that
interacts with the histone H3-like domain of hTAF(II)31. Moreover, the PCAF
complex has a novel subunit with WD40 repeats having a similarity to
hTAF(II)100.
