# Acetyltransferase p300 regulates NBS1-mediated DNA damage response.
**Authors:** Jang ER, Choi JD, Lee JS
**Journal:** FEBS Lett (2011)
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.febslet.2010.11.034](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2010.11.034)

## Abstract

1. FEBS Lett. 2011 Jan 3;585(1):47-52. doi: 10.1016/j.febslet.2010.11.034. Epub
2010 Nov 23.

Acetyltransferase p300 regulates NBS1-mediated DNA damage response.

Jang ER(1), Choi JD, Lee JS.

Author information:
(1)Department of Molecular Science and Technology, College of Natural Sciences,
Ajou University, Suwon, Republic of Korea.

The role of p300 in DNA damage response is unclear. To understand how
ATM-dependent phosphorylation of p300 affects its function in response to DNA
damage, we present evidence that S106 of p300, which is phosphorylated by ATM,
regulates stability of NBS1 and recruitment into damaged DNA, possibly leading
to regulation of DNA repair. Non-phosphorylatable p300 (S106A) destabilized NBS1
and decreased NBS1-p300 interaction. The recruitment of NBS1 into damaged DNA
was impaired in the presence of S106A. Also, a dominant negative p300 lacking
enzymatic activity induced destabilization of NBS1, suggesting that its
acetyltransferase is required for NBS1 stability. These results indicate that
phosphorylation of p300 can regulate NBS1-mediated DNA damage response, and that
these events occur in an acetylation-dependent manner.
