# Different localization of tau protein kinase I/glycogen synthase kinase-3 beta from glycogen synthase kinase-3 alpha in cerebellum mitochondria.
**Authors:** Hoshi M, Sato M, Kondo S, Takashima A, Noguchi K, Takahashi M, Ishiguro K, Imahori K
**Journal:** J Biochem (1995)
**DOI:** [10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a124965](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a124965)

## Abstract

1. J Biochem. 1995 Oct;118(4):683-5. doi: 10.1093/oxfordjournals.jbchem.a124965.

Different localization of tau protein kinase I/glycogen synthase kinase-3 beta
from glycogen synthase kinase-3 alpha in cerebellum mitochondria.

Hoshi M(1), Sato M, Kondo S, Takashima A, Noguchi K, Takahashi M, Ishiguro K,
Imahori K.

Author information:
(1)Mitsubishi Kasei Institute of Life Sciences, Tokyo.

We examined the subcellular distribution of two glycogen synthase kinase-3
(GSK-3) isoforms in rat cerebellum. Results from immunoelectron microscopy and
subcellular fractionation revealed that one isoform, tau protein kinase I/GSK-3
beta (TPKI/GSK-3 beta), was present in mitochondria, but GSK-3 alpha was not.
Although the two GSK-3 isoforms seem to have similar properties, the difference
of subcellular localization observed here suggests that TPKI/GSK-3 beta fulfills
some specific function in mitochondria.
