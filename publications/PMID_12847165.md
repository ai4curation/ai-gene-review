## Abstract

1. Neurology. 2003 Jul 8;61(1):99-101. doi: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000072482.70963.d7.

Gaucher's disease with Parkinson's disease: clinical and pathological aspects.

Bembi B(1), Zambito Marsala S, Sidransky E, Ciana G, Carrozzi M, Zorzon M,
Martini C, Gioulis M, Pittis MG, Capus L.

Author information:
(1)Unità Operativa Dipartimentale di Malattie Metaboliche, I.R.C.C.S. Burlo
Garofolo, Trieste, Italy. bembi@burlo.trieste.it

The association between type 1 Gaucher disease and PD has been reported in the
literature. The clinical picture is characterized by the predominance of
bilateral akinetic-rigid signs and poor response to levodopa therapy. The
authors describe four patients (two siblings) with type 1 Gaucher disease
presenting with the following signs of typical PD: asymmetric onset of rigidity,
resting tremor, bradykinesia, and a favorable response to Parkinson therapies.
