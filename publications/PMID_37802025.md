Cyclic GMP-AMP synthase (cGAS) binds pathogenic and other cytoplasmic
double-stranded DNA (dsDNA) to catalyze the synthesis of cyclic GMP-AMP (cGAMP),
which serves as the secondary messenger to activate the STING pathway and innate
immune responses. Emerging evidence suggests that activation of the cGAS pathway
is crucial for anti-tumor immunity; however, no effective intervention method
targeting cGAS is currently available. Here we report that cGAS is palmitoylated
by ZDHHC9 at cysteines 404/405, which promotes the dimerization and activation
of cGAS. We further identified that lysophospholipase-like 1 (LYPLAL1)
depalmitoylates cGAS to compromise its normal function. As such, inhibition of
LYPLAL1 significantly enhances cGAS-mediated innate immune response, elevates
PD-L1 expression, and enhances anti-tumor response to PD-1 blockade. Our results
therefore reveal that targeting LYPLAL1-mediated cGAS depalmitoylation
contributes to cGAS activation, providing a potential strategy to augment the
efficacy of anti-tumor immunotherapy.
