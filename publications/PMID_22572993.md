The transcription initiation factor TFIIH is a remarkable protein complex that
has a fundamental role in the transcription of protein-coding genes as well as
during the DNA nucleotide excision repair pathway. The detailed understanding of
how TFIIH functions to coordinate these two processes is also providing an
explanation for the phenotypes observed in patients who bear mutations in some
of the TFIIH subunits. In this way, studies of TFIIH have revealed tight
molecular connections between transcription and DNA repair and have helped to
define the concept of 'transcription diseases'.
