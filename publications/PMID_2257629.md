# let-60, a gene that specifies cell fates during C. elegans vulval induction, encodes a ras protein.
**Authors:** Han M, Sternberg PW
**Journal:** Cell (1990)
**DOI:** [10.1016/0092-8674(90)90495-z](https://doi.org/10.1016/0092-8674(90)90495-z)

## Abstract

1. Cell. 1990 Nov 30;63(5):921-31. doi: 10.1016/0092-8674(90)90495-z.

let-60, a gene that specifies cell fates during C. elegans vulval induction,
encodes a ras protein.

Han M(1), Sternberg PW.

Author information:
(1)Howard Hughes Medical Institute.

Genetic analysis previously suggested that the let-60 gene controls the switch
between vulval and hypodermal cell fates during C. elegans vulval induction. We
have cloned the let-60 gene, and shown that it encodes a gene product identical
in 84% of its first 164 amino acids to ras gene products from other vertebrate
and invertebrate species. This conservation suggests that the let-60 product
contains all the biochemical functions of ras proteins. Extrachromosomal arrays
of let-60 ras DNA cause cell-type misspecification (extra vulval fates)
phenotypically opposite to that caused by let-60 ras loss-of-function mutations
(no vulval fates), and suppress the vulvaless phenotype of mutations in two
other genes necessary for vulval induction. Thus, the level and pattern of
let-60 ras expression may be under strict regulation; increase in let-60 ras
activity bypasses or reduces the need for upstream genes in the vulval induction
pathway.
