1. Executive summary

The nitrogen-regulatory PTS (PTS^Ntr) module is fully satisfiable in P. putida KT2440, and every step is supported by direct experiments in the target strain (not homology transfer). The three module steps map cleanly to three genes:

The primary phosphate flow PEP → EI^Ntr → NPr → EIIA^Ntr was demonstrated in KT2440 by in-vivo phosphorylation-state assays (18296519), and each protein has genetically confirmed function in the strain (PMIDs 17416664, 11489866, 21236318, 26224366, 22434849).

Three curation actions dominate this review: 1. The KEGG bucket ppu02060 conflates two biologically distinct processes — the fructose/sugar PTS (fruBKA) and the regulatory PTS^Ntr. Only the latter belongs to this module; fruB/fruK/fruA should be excluded (fruK is a glycolytic 1-phosphofructokinase, not a PTS component at all). 2. PP_0948 carries a misleading legacy symbol "ptsH"/"HPr" but is the nitrogen-PTS NPr (KEGG ortholog K08485), confirmed by rpoN-operon synteny and His15 mutagenesis. Its symbol should be corrected to ptsO/npr. 3. Phosphate entry is not strictly ptsP-exclusive (a second EI-type enzyme "mtp" and FruB cross-talk provide redundancy), a caveat for the step-1 molecular-player annotation.

2. Target-organism pathway definition

What the module IS (in KT2440): a cytoplasmic, membrane-independent PEP-driven phosphorelay that functions purely in signaling/regulation — it does not transport or phosphorylate any sugar. PEP donates a phosphoryl group to EI^Ntr (PtsP), which relays via the small phosphocarrier NPr (His15) to the terminal regulator EIIA^Ntr (PtsN). The phosphorylation state of EIIA^Ntr (set by the cell's PEP/pyruvate ratio and C/N status) is read out by direct protein–protein interactions with downstream targets. This is the "N/C-sensing" or "nitrogen-related/metabolic" PTS.

Alternate names / database definitions: - PTS^Ntr, PTS(Ntr), "nitrogen-related PTS", "nitrogen-metabolic PTS", "N/C-sensing PTS", "regulatory PTS", "paralogous/branch PTS". - KEGG places all of these genes on map ppu02060 "Phosphotransferase system (PTS)", which is an overview transport map that also contains all sugar PTS proteins — the map name is broader than this module.

Neighboring processes to keep SEPARATE from this module: - Fructose PTS / PTS^Fru (fruBKA, PP_0793–0795): carbohydrate uptake + fructose-1-P phosphorylation. Shares map ppu02060 and cross-talks with PTS^Ntr but is a distinct pathway. - Glycolysis / fructose metabolism (fruK = 1-phosphofructokinase). - σ⁵⁴ (RpoN) regulon / nitrogen assimilation (Ntr/GlnK–NtrBC systems): functionally connected but a separate module. Note NPr/PtsN are genomically embedded in the rpoN operon here, which can invite conflation. - KdpD/KdpE two-component system and KdpFABC K⁺ transport: a target of PtsN, not part of the phosphorelay module.

4. Candidate genes and evidence

4.1 Genes that ARE the module (high confidence)

EI^Ntr — ptsP / PP_5145 (Q88CN5; K08484; EC 2.7.3.9; 759 aa) - Role: PEP-dependent autophosphorylating Enzyme I of the Ntr branch; entry point of the relay. - Domain architecture: N-terminal GAF sensory domain + PEP-utilizer (PEP-utilizers_N/C) catalytic domains — the GAF domain is the presumptive metabolic-signal input (analogous to other EI^Ntr proteins). - Evidence: direct KT2440 genetics — part of the PEP→EI^Ntr→NPr→EIIA^Ntr flow (18296519); ptsP/ptsO/ptsN act in concert to control PHA (17416664). - Caveat: phosphodonor redundancy — a second EI-type ORF "mtp" was noted, and neither ptsP nor mtp single mutants abolished Pu carbon regulation (11489866); FruB cross-talk can also phosphorylate downstream EIIA^Ntr in a ptsP background (18296519). → mark step 1 "covered (PtsP primary) with documented redundancy."

NPr — PP_0948 (Q88PA2; KO K08485 = NPr; 90 aa; PTS-HPr fold; active-site His15) - Role: phosphocarrier relay between EI^Ntr and EIIA^Ntr. - Evidence: H15A substitution abolishes ptsO function in carbon regulation of Pu (11489866) — direct proof of the phospho-His relay; coexpressed with ptsN, mapped adjacent to rpoN (11489866); part of the concerted PHA-controlling cascade (17416664). - Curation caveat (important): the KEGG/GenBank symbol is "ptsH" and name "Phosphocarrier protein HPr", but the ortholog assignment (K08485) and all functional/synteny evidence identify it as the NPr (ptsO/npr). KT2440 has no separate canonical carbohydrate HPr — the fructose branch's HPr activity is fused into the FruB multidomain protein (18296519). → Recommend symbol correction PP_0948: ptsH → ptsO/npr.

EIIA^Ntr — ptsN / PP_0950 (Q88PA0; K02806; 154 aa; PTS_EIIA_2) - Role: terminal phosphorylation-state-dependent regulator acting by direct protein–protein interaction (no sugar phosphorylation). - Direct KT2440 targets: - PtsN~P represses kdpFABC (K⁺ transport) via direct interaction with the sensor kinase KdpD; K⁺-dependent (26224366; modeled in 26159078). - Dephospho-PtsN inhibits pyruvate dehydrogenase (AceE, E1 subunit) — co-IP/MS, co-localization, activity assays (21236318). - Controls central-carbon flux (elevated pyruvate-shunt flux in ptsN mutants; 22434849) and PHA accumulation (17416664). - Modulates the Pu/TOL biodegradation promoter in response to carbon (11489866 30209872). - Opposite phospho-forms act on different targets — a bona fide phosphorylation-state switch.

5. Gaps, ambiguities, and likely over-annotations

  • Module-boundary error in the source bucket: ppu02060 mixes PTS^Ntr with the fructose PTS. The generic module boundary is correct in concept (it explicitly excludes sugar transport), but the local candidate list is contaminated with fruBKA. → module_needs_revision at the gene-membership level.
  • PP_0948 symbol over-propagation: "ptsH/HPr" is a legacy mislabel; the protein is NPr. High-impact for curation because a curator could wrongly assign it to carbohydrate PTS or expect a separate NPr elsewhere (there is none). Genome-wide KO check confirms this: KT2440 has zero genes assigned to the canonical HPr ortholog K02784 and zero to the carbohydrate Enzyme I ortholog K08483 — so the "HPr" label has no legitimate genomic referent, and FruB is the sole sugar-PTS Enzyme I.
  • No intra-genome paralog ambiguity: each PTS^Ntr step is single-copy in KT2440 — ptsP=PP_5145 (K08484), NPr=PP_0948 (K08485), ptsN=PP_0950 (K02806). This contrasts with Rhizobium leguminosarum (two ptsN copies; 22340847), so KT2440 module steps map unambiguously to one gene each.
  • "mtp" is not a distinct Enzyme I: the only other PEP-utilizing enzyme in KT2440 is PEP synthase (ppsA, PP_2082, K01007); there is no second EI/EI^Ntr ortholog. Step-1 redundancy therefore most plausibly operates through FruB cross-talk rather than a bona fide second Enzyme I.
  • fruK broad EC/name: "Phosphofructokinase" — ensure it is annotated as 1-phosphofructokinase (fructose-1-P → fructose-1,6-bisP) and not counted toward any PTS step.
  • EI^Ntr phosphodonor redundancy: step-1 input is not strictly PtsP-exclusive — FruB cross-talk feeds the relay in a ptsP background (18296519), and the historic "mtp" ORF (11489866) has no distinct modern EI ortholog (see above). Curate step 1 as "PtsP primary, with documented redundancy."
  • PP_0949 (UPF0042 ATP-binding, RapZ/YhbJ homolog): present in the operon; associated in other taxa with GlmZ sRNA / RNase adaptor regulation — outside this three-step module but a candidate for a separate module/review.
  • NPr's own direct targets: in E. coli, dephospho-NPr regulates LpxD/LPS and envelope stress (25701731). Whether NPr has PtsN-independent targets in KT2440 is untested (uncertain species transfer) — an open question.
  • Broader-organism inferences (flag as transfer, not direct): E. coli cross-talk→Kdp (26159077); Rhizobium PTS^Ntr→ABC transporters (22340847); P. aeruginosa PtsN redundant phosphorylation and virulence links (37074168). These are weak-to-moderate transfer to KT2440 and should not be asserted as KT2440 facts.
