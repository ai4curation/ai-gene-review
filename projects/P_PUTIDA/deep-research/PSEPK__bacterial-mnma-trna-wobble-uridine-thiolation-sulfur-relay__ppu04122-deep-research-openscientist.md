## 1. Executive Summary

The MnmA 2-thiouridine relay is **fully encoded and should be marked COVERED** in *P. putida* KT2440. Every biochemical step of the experimentally reconstituted *E. coli* relay — IscS → TusA → TusBCD → TusE → MnmA → s²U34 (PMID 16387657) — has a clear ortholog in the KT2440 proteome, and the four central mediators sit in a **syntenic `tusD-tusC-tusB-tusE` operon (PP_3993–PP_3996)**. Because both organisms are gammaproteobacteria and the *tus* genes are co-localized, transfer of the *E. coli* mechanism to KT2440 is **strong**.

The main curation issue is **not gaps but over-scope**: KEGG map04122 lumps three chemically distinct, IscS-fed sulfur pathways (2-thiouridine relay, molybdopterin/Moco biosynthesis, thiamine/4-thiouridine) into one "Sulfur relay system" bucket. Only **7 of the 22 candidate genes** belong to the 2-thiouridine module. The initial **paralog ambiguity** at the entry points was **resolved in Iteration 2** by sequence identity + InterPro signature + operon context: **IscS = PP_0842** (isc operon, IscS-specific domain) and **TusA = PP_2116** (specific Sulphur_carrier_TusA signature) are the relay-committed genes, while PP_2435 and PP_1233 are divergent accessory paralogs. A minor residual issue is **generic EC 2.8.1.- / unnamed** annotations on some Tus subunits (PP_3994/PP_3995).

Evidence is **homology- and synteny-based**; there is, to my knowledge, **no direct KT2440-specific biochemical or genetic characterization** of this relay. Confidence rests on the strength of the *E. coli* model plus KT2440 orthology/synteny.

## 2. Target-Organism Pathway Definition

**Included biochemical process.** ATP-dependent 2-thiolation of the wobble uridine (U34 → s²U34) in tRNA^Glu, tRNA^Lys, tRNA^Gln, catalyzed by the thiouridylase **MnmA (EC 2.8.1.13)**, using sulfur delivered through a protein-only persulfide relay originating from the cysteine desulfurase **IscS (EC 2.8.1.7)**. s²U34 restricts codon–anticodon wobble and is required for accurate decoding of NNR (AAR/CAR/GAR) codons and for recognition by cognate aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases (PMID 16871210, PMID 26791911).

**Neighboring processes to keep SEPARATE:**
- **mnm⁵ side chain (MnmE/MnmG/GidA).** MnmA installs only the 2-thio group; the 5-methylaminomethyl group is added by MnmE (PP: P0A175, 456 aa) and MnmG (Q88RW8, 631 aa), **both present in KT2440** — so the strain makes the complete mnm⁵s²U34. These add carbon/nitrogen (not sulfur) and are outside the sulfur-relay bucket and this module (though adjacent — they finish the same nucleoside).
- **Molybdopterin / Moco biosynthesis** (MoaA/B/C/D/E, MoeB). A separate IscS-fed sulfur pathway that KEGG map04122 also contains.
- **Thiamine biosynthesis & 4-thiouridine (s⁴U8)** (ThiI, ThiS/ThiF). ThiI makes s⁴U8 at position 8 (not the wobble position) and feeds thiamine; distinct from MnmA.
- **Fe–S cluster assembly (Isc/Suf).** Shares IscS as donor but is a different destination.

**Alternate names / database definitions.** The *E. coli* Tus genes were originally *yhhP/yheL/yheM/yheN/yccK* and renamed *tusA/tusB/tusC/tusD/tusE* (PMID 16387657). MnmA is also historically **TrmU / Asm / MnmA thiouridylase**. KEGG module context for this branch corresponds to the "2-thiouridine" portion of map00790/map04122; it is **not** a standalone KEGG MODULE, which is why no module YAML resolved.

## 5. Gaps, Ambiguities, and Likely Over-Annotations

- **No true gaps.** All five relay activities plus the desulfurase are encoded. The module is satisfiable.
- **Sulfur-donor paralogy — RESOLVED (Iteration 2).** Two cysteine desulfurases: **IscS (PP_0842)** vs **IscS-II (PP_2435)** (both EC 2.8.1.7, ~404–405 aa). Three concordant criteria identify **PP_0842 as the relay donor**: 72.5% identity to *E. coli* IscS (P0A6B7) vs 52.7% for PP_2435; only PP_0842 carries the IscS-specific InterPro **IPR010240 (Cys_deSase_IscS)**; and PP_0842 sits in the canonical **isc operon** (PP_0842 *iscS* – PP_0843 *iscU* – PP_0844 *iscA* – PP_0845 *hscB*). PP_2435 lacks the IscS signature and is flanked by a Xre/Res toxin–antitoxin pair and an acyl-CoA dehydrogenase (no *isc/suf/csd* context) → **divergent accessory desulfurase, not the primary relay donor.**
- **Carrier paralogy — RESOLVED (Iteration 2).** Two TusA-family carriers: **TusA (PP_2116)** vs **TusA-I (PP_1233)**. **PP_2116 is the relay carrier**: 57.5% identity to *E. coli* TusA (P0A890) and it uniquely carries the specific InterPro **IPR022931 (Sulphur_carrier_TusA)**. PP_1233 is only 44.3% identical and carries only the generic **IPR001455 (TusA-like)** signature → likely a paralog serving a different sulfur branch (Moco/DsrE2-YeeD-type), consistent with *E. coli* TusA's multifunctionality (PMID 33526615). Both retain the catalytic CxxP persulfide motif (PP_2116 GLN**C**PEP, PP_1233 GLN**C**PLP), so both are functional sulfurtransferases; the discriminator is the family-specific InterPro signature, not the active site.
- **Over-propagation / naming issues.** PP_3994 protein name ("tRNA 5-methylaminomethyl-2-thiouridine synthase, TusC-like") over-claims enzymatic identity — it is a **TusBCD structural subunit (TusC)**, not the synthase (MnmA is). PP_3994/PP_3995 lack gene symbols; TusE and TusA-I carry broad "Sulfurtransferase (EC 2.8.1.-)" terms. These are annotation-transfer artifacts, not functional claims.
- **MnmA is single-copy.** Only one 2-thiouridylase (PP_4014) exists in the proteome, so the terminal-enzyme assignment is unambiguous (no paralog competition).
- **PP_1233 is low-evidence.** It carries only generic GO:0016740 (transferase activity) and UniProt annotationScore 1.0 (lowest tier); keep it excluded from the relay gene set pending characterization.
- **Species-transfer caveat (important).** All functional calls are **inferred from *E. coli*** (orthology + operon synteny + catalytic-residue conservation), **not from KT2440 experiments**. A targeted PubMed search (Iteration 4–5) found **no direct experimental characterization** of the MnmA/Tus relay in *P. putida* KT2440 or in *Pseudomonas* generally. Transfer is **strong** for gammaproteobacteria (short phylogenetic distance, conserved operon, all catalytic residues intact) but must be labeled inferred, not direct.
