How propagation works

A mapping row is asserted on one ARO term and applies to genes (determinant terms) below it. ARO joins the two kinds of term that rows are written on by different relations, so propagation follows two paths:

  • Gene families (e.g. ARO:3000001 beta-lactamase) apply to the family and every is_a descendant: CTX-M-15 is_a CTX-M beta-lactamase is_a … beta-lactamase. Reported as exact or narrower.
  • Resistance mechanisms (e.g. ARO:0001004 antibiotic inactivation) are not ancestors of any gene. A family is linked to a mechanism by participates_in (RO:0000056), and mechanisms nest by is_a, so a mechanism row applies to every gene that reaches it by is_a* ∘ participates_in ∘ is_a*. Reported as participates_in. Until 2026-11-27 only the first path was implemented, so every mechanism row reached zero genes; the CARD team pointed this out.

Other ARO relations are deliberately not followed: confers_resistance_to_antibiotic and confers_resistance_to_drug_class point to the drug (a chemical), part_of/has_part join complex subunits and gene clusters, and the regulation relations join regulators to their targets. None of them makes a gene a member of a mapped family or a participant in a mapped mechanism.

When several mapped terms give the same GO term to an entry, the entry is credited to the most specific one (exact, then narrower, then participates_in). That is one reason a row can show zero; the other is that the entries it reaches already carry the term. Each row says which.

enables rows (green) claim every family member has the activity; related match rows (amber) are broader or cover a minority of members that may differ. Candidate annotations are leads for curators, not assertions.

Review history

Reviewed by the CARD curation team (CARD review of the aro2go table, received 2026-11-27). CARD confirmed that all ARO accessions used were current, that the enzyme-family mappings were right (beta-lactamase, APH, AAC, ANT, CAT, MPH, Erm, trimethoprim-resistant DHFR, sulfonamide-resistant folP, MCR-type phosphoethanolamine transferases), that the FosA rows were appropriately cautious, and that the GO-gap list was accurate. Rows CARD reviewed carry reviewer_label; rows added after that review do not. CARD also pointed out that mechanism rows reached no genes under is_a-only propagation (fixed: they now propagate along participates_in), that ARO:3000560 and ARO:3000202 had been relabelled (updated), and that the separate mphA/mphB rows were redundant with the MPH family row (removed).

Where the curated mapping should live and how it should track ARO releases is discussed in the options note.
