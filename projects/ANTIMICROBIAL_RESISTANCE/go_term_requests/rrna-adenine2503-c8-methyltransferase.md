# GO new term request (draft): 23S rRNA (adenine(2503)-C(8))-methyltransferase activity

Draft for the geneontology/go-ontology new-term-request template. **Not filed.** The CARD team reviewed the gap behind this request and said they would support it.

- **Label:** 23S rRNA (adenine(2503)-C(8))-methyltransferase activity
- **Definition:** Catalysis of the reaction: adenosine(2503) in 23S rRNA + 2 reduced [2Fe-2S]-[ferredoxin] + 2 S-adenosyl-L-methionine = 8-methyladenosine(2503) in 23S rRNA + 5'-deoxyadenosine + L-methionine + 2 oxidized [2Fe-2S]-[ferredoxin] + S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine.
- **Definition references:** RHEA:42632, PMID:19144912
- **Parent(s) (is_a):** GO:0008169 C-methyltransferase activity; GO:0016433 rRNA (adenine) methyltransferase activity
- **Synonyms:** 23S rRNA m8A2503 methyltransferase activity (EXACT); Cfr methyltransferase activity (NARROW)
- **Cross-references:** EC:2.1.1.224; RHEA:42632
- **ARO terms that would map to it:** ARO:3000202 Cfr-like 23S rRNA methyltransferase; ARO:3009650 23S rRNA (adenine(2503)-C(8))-methyltransferase
- **Example gene products:** UniProtKB:Q9FBG4 (Cfr, Mammaliicoccus sciuri); UniProtKB:A5HBL2 (Cfr, Staphylococcus aureus)

## References

- PMID:19144912 — Identification of 8-methyladenosine as the modification catalyzed by the radical SAM methyltransferase Cfr that confers antibiotic resistance in bacteria.
- PMID:10952608 — Identification of a plasmid-borne chloramphenicol-florfenicol resistance gene in Staphylococcus sciuri.
- PMID:17555436 — Acquisition of a natural resistance gene renders a clinical strain of methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus resistant to the synthetic antibiotic linezolid.

## Notes

Radical SAM enzyme; methylation of C8 of A2503 confers the PhLOPSA phenotype (phenicols, lincosamides, oxazolidinones, pleuromutilins, streptogramin A). Sibling of GO:0070040 rRNA (adenine(2503)-C2-)-methyltransferase activity (RlmN, EC 2.1.1.192); Cfr is not correctly annotated to that term. Matches the parent class CARD now uses, ARO:3009650.

All PMIDs, EC numbers and Rhea ids above were checked on 2026-11-27 (PubMed esummary titles; UniProt entries; Rhea search). GO ids were checked against the GO release used by `just validate-mappings`.
