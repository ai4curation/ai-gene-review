# GO new term request (draft): streptogramin B lyase activity

Draft for the geneontology/go-ontology new-term-request template. **Not filed.** The CARD team reviewed the gap behind this request and said they would support it.

- **Label:** streptogramin B lyase activity
- **Definition:** Catalysis of the linearization of a type B streptogramin (cyclic hexadepsipeptide) antibiotic such as virginiamycin S1 by cleavage of the ester bond of its macrolactone ring through an elimination reaction, rather than hydrolysis, producing a linear peptide with an N-terminal dehydrobutyrine.
- **Definition references:** PMID:11467949, PMID:17563376
- **Parent(s) (is_a):** GO:0016835 carbon-oxygen lyase activity
- **Synonyms:** virginiamycin B lyase activity (EXACT); Vgb activity (NARROW)
- **Cross-references:** EC:4.2.99.- (UniProt P17978 Vgb carries EC 4.2.99.-; no full EC number); no Rhea reaction found (Rhea searched for virginiamycin, 2026-11-27)
- **ARO terms that would map to it:** ARO:3000376 streptogramin vgb lyase
- **Example gene products:** UniProtKB:P17978 (Vgb, Staphylococcus aureus)

## References

- PMID:11467949 — Vgb from Staphylococcus aureus inactivates streptogramin B antibiotics by an elimination mechanism not hydrolysis.
- PMID:17563376 — Structural basis for streptogramin B resistance in Staphylococcus aureus by virginiamycin B lyase.

## Notes

Mg2+-dependent seven-bladed beta-propeller. Mechanism is elimination, which is why the parent is a lyase (EC 4.2.99) and not an esterase.
