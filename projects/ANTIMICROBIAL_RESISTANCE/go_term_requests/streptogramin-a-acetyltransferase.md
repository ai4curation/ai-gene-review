# GO new term request (draft): streptogramin A O-acetyltransferase activity

Draft for the geneontology/go-ontology new-term-request template. **Not filed.** This gap was not on the CARD team's list; it is recorded in the mapping file and included for completeness.

- **Label:** streptogramin A O-acetyltransferase activity
- **Definition:** Catalysis of the reaction: a streptogramin A antibiotic (e.g. virginiamycin M1, dalfopristin) + acetyl-CoA = O-acetyl-streptogramin A + CoA, acetylation of the secondary hydroxyl of the antibiotic.
- **Definition references:** PMID:11841212, PMID:12771141
- **Parent(s) (is_a):** GO:0016413 O-acetyltransferase activity
- **Synonyms:** virginiamycin acetyltransferase activity (EXACT); Vat activity (NARROW)
- **Cross-references:** EC:2.3.1.- (UniProt P26839 Vat and P50870 Vat(D) carry EC 2.3.1.-; no full EC number); no Rhea reaction found (Rhea searched for virginiamycin, 2026-11-27)
- **ARO terms that would map to it:** ARO:3000453 streptogramin vat acetyltransferase
- **Example gene products:** UniProtKB:P50870 (Vat(D), Enterococcus faecium); UniProtKB:P26839 (Vat(A), Staphylococcus aureus)

## References

- PMID:11841212 — Crystal structure of Vat(D): an acetyltransferase that inactivates streptogramin group A antibiotics.
- PMID:12771141 — Structural basis of Synercid (quinupristin-dalfopristin) resistance in Gram-positive bacterial pathogens.
