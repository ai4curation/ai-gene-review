## 1. GO:0000750 `replaced_by` misplaces non-kinase participants

**Title:** GO:0000750 replaced_by GO:0071507 moves upstream regulators onto the MAPK cascade

**Body:**

GO:0000750 *pheromone-dependent signal transduction involved in conjugation
with cellular fusion* was obsoleted (go-ontology#31335) as a precomposed term to
be represented as a GO-CAM, with `replaced_by: GO:0071507` *pheromone response
MAPK cascade*.

Because `replaced_by` is applied to existing annotations automatically, every
gene annotated to GO:0000750 is moved into the MAPK cascade itself. That is
correct for the cascade kinases, but not for participants acting upstream of
it. Example: *S. cerevisiae* Arr4/GET3 (IMP, PMID:18261907) is a non-receptor
guanine-nucleotide exchange factor for the G alpha subunit Gpa1. It "promotes G
protein-dependent cellular responses, including mitogen-activated protein
kinase (MAPK) phosphorylation, new-gene transcription, and mating", but it is
not a component of the cascade.

Requests:

1. Re-map such upstream annotations to GO:0062038 *positive regulation of
   pheromone response MAPK cascade*. This follows the pattern of the *S. pombe*
   pheromone GO-CAM gomodel:693b3c0900000536, where GEF activity positively
   regulates the cascade.
2. Where no single term is true of every annotated gene, use `consider` rather
   than `replaced_by` for this term.

## 2. General: check `replaced_by` against all annotated participants

**Title:** Obsoleting precomposed "X involved in Y" terms: check replaced_by covers every annotated gene

**Body:**

A precomposed term asserts two things, X and `part_of` Y. A single-term
`replaced_by` keeps one half. When it is applied automatically, it is only
safe if that term is true of *every* gene annotated to the obsolete class.
Two cases from our review of these terms:

- GO:0051603 *proteolysis involved in protein catabolic process* →
  GO:0030163 *protein catabolic process*: safe, since every annotated gene
  takes part in the catabolic process.
- GO:0000750 → GO:0071507: unsafe for upstream regulators (ticket 1).

Suggestion: before obsoleting a precomposed term with `replaced_by`, list the
distinct molecular roles among its annotated genes. If they are not all
covered by the replacement, use `consider`.

## 3. Caspase-regulator terms are not under their genus

**Title:** GO:0043027 and GO:0008656 are not subclasses of GO:0004869 / GO:0140608

**Body:**

GO:0043027 *cysteine-type endopeptidase inhibitor activity involved in apoptotic
process* is_a only GO:0043028 *cysteine-type endopeptidase regulator activity
involved in apoptotic process*. It is not a subclass of GO:0004869
*cysteine-type endopeptidase inhibitor activity*. Likewise, GO:0008656
*cysteine-type endopeptidase activator activity involved in apoptotic process*
is_a GO:0016505 and GO:0043028, but not GO:0140608 *cysteine-type endopeptidase
activator activity*. None of these terms has a logical definition, and
GO:0043028 has no `part_of` apoptotic process. As a result, genes annotated
only to the apoptosis-specific term (for example XIAP, DIAP1 and CED-4) are not
returned by a query for cysteine-type endopeptidase inhibitor or activator
activity.

Requests:

1. Add `is_a` to the genus and an `intersection_of` logical definition
   (genus `and part_of GO:0006915`).
2. Alternatively, given the obsoletion policy for precomposed terms, obsolete
   them with `consider` pointing at the genus. A single `replaced_by` is unsafe
   here: some annotated genes regulate the caspase only indirectly, and
   *Drosophila* Hid, for example, acts on DIAP1 rather than on a caspase.

## 5. MOMP regulation terms: "involved in apoptotic signaling pathway" is label-only

**Title:** GO:1901028/GO:1901029/GO:1901030: the "involved in" context is not in the logical definition, but the synonyms treat it as narrowing

**Body:**

GO:1901030 *positive regulation of mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization
involved in apoptotic signaling pathway* is defined as `GO:0065007 and
positively_regulates GO:0097345` *mitochondrial outer membrane
permeabilization*. GO:1901029 and GO:1901028 follow the same pattern. The
logical definitions have no `part_of GO:0097190`. The context comes from
GO:0097345 itself, which is defined as MOMP "as part of the apoptotic
signaling pathway" (`part_of GO:0097190`). The "involved in apoptotic
signaling pathway" in the three labels is therefore entailed, not added.

The synonyms disagree. "regulation of MOMP" is EXACT on GO:1901028, and
"positive regulation of MOMP" is EXACT on GO:1901030. But the spelled-out
"positive regulation of mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization" is
BROAD on GO:1901030, and the same holds on GO:1901029. Under the logical
definition, the spelled-out phrase names exactly the defined class.

Requests (either is fine):

1. Relabel the three terms to *(positive / negative) regulation of
   mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization*. Keep the current labels as
   EXACT synonyms, and change the BROAD synonyms to EXACT.
2. Or, if a non-apoptotic MOMP is meant to exist (compare GO:1902686
   *mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization involved in programmed cell
   death*), say so in
   GO:0097345's definition and adjust the logical definitions so they
   distinguish the two.

Why it matters: precomposed "X involved in Y" terms are being retired in
favour of GO-CAM. Labels like these look precomposed, but there is no Y to
move into a model and no broader term to replace them with. In our corpus,
56 reviewer-authored uses and 10 GOA rows were on these terms.

## 6. Two "involved in" terms that name a process: rename, don't obsolete

Both terms are the right grain for the genes that carry them, but their
labels put them in the "X involved in Y" set that is being retired. A rename
would take them out of it without moving any annotation.

**GO:0106074 *aminoacyl-tRNA metabolism involved in translational fidelity*.**
The definition ("Any process which detects an amino-acid acetylated tRNA is
charged with the correct amino acid, or removes incorrect amino acids from a
charged tRNA") is aminoacyl-tRNA editing. GO has no other term for it, and
GO:0002161 *aminoacyl-tRNA deacylase activity* is `part_of` it. There is no
logical definition: "aminoacyl-tRNA metabolism" is not a GO class, and the
nearest genus, GO:0006399 *tRNA metabolic process*, is far broader.

Requests:

1. Relabel to *aminoacyl-tRNA editing* (or *aminoacyl-tRNA proofreading*),
   keeping the current label as an EXACT synonym.
2. Fix the synonym typo "aminoacyl-tRNA editin" → "aminoacyl-tRNA editing",
   and consider making it EXACT.
3. Fix "amino-acid acetylated tRNA" in the definition, which presumably means
   "aminoacylated tRNA".

**GO:0006271 *DNA strand elongation involved in DNA replication*.** The term
names the elongation stage of replication: its EXACT synonym is "DNA
replication elongation", it xrefs Reactome R-HSA-69190 "DNA strand
elongation", and GO:0006272 *leading strand elongation* and GO:0006273
*lagging strand elongation* are `is_a` it. Obsoleting it in favour of
GO:0022616 *DNA strand elongation* would leave those stages under a genus
that also covers strand extension outside replication.

Request: relabel to *DNA replication elongation*, keeping the current label
as an EXACT synonym. The logical definition (`GO:0022616 and part_of
GO:0006260`) can stay.

In our corpus, 49 reviewer-authored uses and 41 GOA rows are on these two
terms.
