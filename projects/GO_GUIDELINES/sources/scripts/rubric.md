# Relevance-to-GO-best-practice rubric

"Best practice" = guidance on how to make, review, or QC Gene Ontology annotations (and GO-CAM models):
which terms/relations/evidence codes/extensions to use, when to annotate or not, propagation rules
(IBA/PAINT, InterPro2GO, ARBA, keywords), annotation consistency/QC, documentation of conventions,
and the ontology-structure principles curators rely on.

- 5: States or establishes guidance/policy directly (a guideline document, consortium decision,
     annotation-practice paper, curator SOP, documented convention).
- 4: Substantial discussion that reaches or clarifies a general practice applicable beyond one case
     (e.g. "how should we annotate X-type situations", a decision recorded for a class of terms).
- 3: Case work that carries a reusable lesson or worked example of a guideline (e.g. a rule fix
     whose discussion explains why a class of mappings is wrong).
- 2: Case-level correction or request with little generalizable guidance; tool/format/ontology
     material only tangentially about annotation practice.
- 1: Not relevant (logistics, admin, empty, software dev unrelated to curation practice, data dumps).

Rationale: one or two sentences, specific about WHAT guidance the item contains (or why it has none).
Name the topic (e.g. "explains the 'NOT' qualifier policy for IBA"), don't just say "relevant".
