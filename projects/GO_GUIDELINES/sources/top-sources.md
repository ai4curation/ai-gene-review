## GO shared Google Drive: score 5 (34)

- [2017-09-12_BC2-Gaudet-2.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1m9v6KbdXs32ihzMjqGJYQpuK1Qf49Gh-) (opus): 2017 GOC training talk stating literature-curation best practices: annotate the conclusion not the assay (e.g. rubidium vs potassium, histone as a non-physiological kinase substrate), interpret results against the research hypothesis, curate gene-by-gene rather than paper-by-paper, beware indirect phenotypes, and the 'GO commandments' on consistency and removing obsolete annotations. Status: older training material derived from the GO Handbook chapter but still consistent with current practice. It is an earlier, shorter version of the 2017-10_02 deck, which is the fuller canonical copy.
- [2017-10_02 GOC_annotation_best_practices-Gaudet.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1gprg-s8gU070iiWD3QbpazOsuEf8Tjhd) (opus): GOC curator training deck on literature-based annotation best practice: annotate the conclusion not the assay, focus on the research hypothesis, phenotype relevance (no IMP to a process without cellular or molecular support, e.g. Tmem234), gene-by-gene curation, removing annotations based on invalidated hypotheses, membrane-part CC conventions, and QC approaches (consistency exercises, taxon constraints, co-occurrence). Status: 2017 training material, still consistent with current guidance. This is the fuller canonical version of the BC2 deck.
- [ARCHIVED - Signaling receptor activity .docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1dZKucnM45V05MYJUkpuf3Epje6mZRxRv3-62Y5JwAHw) (opus): GO-CAM guideline for signaling receptors. A protein ligand enables receptor ligand activity and has_input the receptor, not the reverse; the receptor has_input its effector, not its ligand; links are directly positively regulates. Small-molecule ligands are linked with 'is small molecule activator/inhibitor', and coreceptors (coreceptor activity, e.g. IL2) are handled separately; it also notes how standard annotation differs. Status: archived, with content moved to the GO-CAM MF guidelines (reviewed 2026-03-10). The current canonical copy is in those guidelines, but the content is guideline text, so it stays at 5.
- [Annotation relations - Guidelines for P2GO.xlsx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1DXdjAuNf7u8CKXYlgW5kyzPgq7vnp-Aai0S5Sh63guc) (opus): Usage table for every gene-product-to-term relation. contributes_to is only for irreducible complex activities; acts upstream of (or within) and its positive/negative children cover BP roles whose mechanism is unknown; is_active_in and located_in are used for CC. Complex membership must be part_of, and non-members get protein-containing complex binding instead. colocalizes_with is marked deprecated. It is current, direct curator guidance.
- [Annotation_best_practices-Gaudet.pdf](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B8fqtupTOdsSWXlvOFZxWTNwSkk) (opus): Gaudet's talk on best practice for literature-based GO annotation: what an annotation asserts (the normal in vivo role), focusing on the research hypothesis, and annotating the conclusion rather than the assay, with worked examples (DDFB, FOXL2). It is foundational practice guidance, and the talk is based on the published best-practice paper.
- [Best Practices for predicting GO data .docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1z-6rNJ4tg3M-Ant-2kqDXohPZUlkw9GEZ5qlNZDYj18) (opus): Guidelines for external groups that publish GO predictions: use authoritative GO/GOA data and the latest ontology release; avoid obsolete, do-not-annotate and obsoletion-candidate terms; use the precise relations (enables, involved_in, is_active_in); respect taxon constraints and NOT annotations; avoid redundant predictions to ancestor terms. It is a draft with comments and unfilled sections ('Add section') of what became the GO website page /docs/guidelines-external/. It states the guidance directly, so 5 stands; the published web page is the canonical version.
- [Best Practices for predicting GO data .docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1z-6rNJ4tg3M-Ant-2kqDXohPZUlkw9GEZ5qlNZDYj18) (opus): Guideline for external resources predicting GO annotations: use current ontology, avoid obsolete/do-not-annotate terms, use precise relations (enables, involved_in, is_active_in; contributes_to rarely), respect taxon constraints, no 'regulation of' for process participants, respect NOT annotations, keep a blocklist (exosome, apoptosis), do not exclude IEA/IBA in evaluation. Draft with placeholder sections and comment threads in the OLD LEGACY folder; the canonical version is the published geneontology.org /docs/guidelines-external/ page.
- [Chromatin remodeling annotation guidelines.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=15fDBhmO54zSJms25aV5nX3kgSwsHMnWgdSuPdo-ukyw) (opus): Curator guideline on chromatin terms: histone chaperone, modifier and reader MFs, a table mapping each histone mark to MF and BP terms, and the rule that short-term dynamic transcription regulation is not 'epigenetic regulation of gene expression' without evidence of stability over cell divisions. It also says histones themselves go to chromatin organization rather than remodeling processes, since they are the targets, and lists expected proteins per BP. Status: archived; its slides say the content moved to a Google Doc, which is the canonical current copy.
- [Contributes_to.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1g-2M8XQuINGN6eKVT0Sqm1OsudF_kM2D4jemv-izTks) (opus): Complex working group deck (2020) establishing that MF goes to the subunit that performs it, non-participating subunits are not given contributes_to, and contributes_to is reserved for irreducible molecular machines (shared active site, ATP synthase, complex II, heteromeric channels), with Paul Thomas's post-meeting summary of the rule. A proposal deck whose rule was adopted in the Contributes_to wiki guideline; partly refined by the 2025 GO-CAM complex rules.
- [Copy of MF guidelines for GO-CAM.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1DWvc_g5yH4S8-Tc2rPtaAyPOIkYzITagK1sXysj0CY4) (opus): GO-CAM activity-unit guidelines for signaling receptors, coreceptors and molecular adaptors. A ligand has_input its receptor, never the reverse, and directly positively regulates the receptor. The receptor's input is its effector, not its ligand. A small-molecule ligand is linked with is small molecule activator/inhibitor. It is current, reviewed guidance with a revision history. It is a 'Copy of', so the original 'MF guidelines for GO-CAM' document is the canonical copy; the WIP Standard annotation guidelines are an earlier draft of the same content.
- [Draft Working Group Policy Statement.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1leeuPb1WDgyORZHrxhapW9HKLTPrCi6xsMi-KaZNCIQ) (opus): Draft IEA-WG policy: the GOC does not accept AI-generated annotations, except pre-approved datasets meeting benchmarks; all AI-assisted associations need manual review by a trained curator and must follow GO guidelines (with an experimental evidence code once reviewed). It lists nine failure modes as de facto annotation requirements: taxon constraints, paralog divergence, do-not-annotate terms, redundancy, legacy science, re-adding removed annotations, IEA training feedback loops. Status: working draft whose header says 'Check this instead / Ignore below', apparently superseded by the 'Updating contributing new GO annotations' text. It still states policy, so it stays at 5.
- [GO-CAM Curation Documentation.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=18ihslb7prB6CWtu2yjF-pMHZBTY1-AdXJAu-ZyuyXS4) (opus): GO-CAM curation guide covering the activity as the primary modelling unit, activity units, evidence on each relation, GO term granularity, avoiding redundancy with extensions, handling protein binding, and GO-ECO mappings. It is established curator documentation for building and reviewing models.
- [GO-CAM guidelines.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1vF8JCnUM_JqkxgAvVHHl6ntyXpk3tY4y_NAjRjZ3WJE) (opus): Current (2025) consortium rules for annotating protein-containing complexes in GO-CAM: assign MFs to individual subunits in a causal flow, use the complex as enabler with 'has part' for emergent activities, complex as enabler without parts when the active subunit is unknown, and the GPAD export mapping to enables/contributes_to. A guideline document stating explicit rules.
- [GO-CAM modeling guidelines.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1DS2ObQc1EVcQKAHjKNt-0jzFCSSVLDQ4Osm-1dsTwdA) (opus): Core GO-CAM modelling rules. Activities are the nodes, and each activity has one location and one part_of BP. has_input names the target. Choose the most specific causal relation: causally upstream of, then provides input for, then regulates, then directly regulates. 'Regulates' requires a known control mechanism, and a gene product gets a separate activity node only when the term, target, location, process or timing differs. It states the convention directly, though open reviewer comments show it was still being refined.
- [GOcurators_KW2GOannouncement.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1QoTlEdEPLu5qteDKynRQsf0SiClWEJzNK3rjQgp23xk) (opus): IEA working group decision to retire the UniProt KW2GO pipeline, scored against the WG rubric. Reasons: multi-hop provenance (ARBA to keyword to GO), keyword scope misaligned with GO (regulation vs process), high error rate, redundancy with Rhea2GO/PAINT/ARBA, and metal-ion 'binding' cofactor terms that are not real MFs. It is a recorded consortium decision with rationale that applies to the whole class of keyword-derived IEAs. Status: decision implemented (kept only for viruses per the Fall 2026 deck).
- [HTP_Guidelines_condensed_version.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=19DNZL21UDZWT9Q1NKocpl8XUIEQtH-25) (opus): Condensed GO guideline for annotating high-throughput data: HTP/HMP/HGI/HDA/HEP codes mapped to LTP equivalents, the same quality standard as LTP (most HTP papers should not be annotated, curators may decline unreliable sets), never annotate primary RNAi screens, and proteomics/localization guidance. A guideline document in a FROZEN legacy folder; the published GO HTP documentation is the canonical version.
- [HTP_paper_draft.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1AponRUpFx9mPAdVOpsRdklxOvjAaPPc4) (opus): Draft of the consortium paper 'Annotation of high throughput data using the Gene Ontology' (Attrill et al.), setting out the HTP annotation framework: HTP evidence codes, false-positive handling, quality criteria for when HTP studies may be annotated. An annotation-method paper; this is a legacy draft, and the published Database 2019 paper is the canonical version.
- [IEA WG GOC meeting Fall 2026.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1cEmQivoBNbojaN6IjcDmQd74-VjEuYY4MmSMC2l1M9I) (opus): IEA WG final report with per-pipeline decisions: KW2GO retired except for viruses; SubCell/Rhea/EC2GO replaced by direct mappings for Swiss-Prot and stopped for TrEMBL as multi-hop; UniPathway2GO retired; InterPro2GO, ARBA and UniRule kept and disputed. It also covers concerns about orthology mass transfer, a GO-compliance requirement for groups whose curation is mapped directly, and the protein-binding recommendation (stop the IntAct IPI import, mark top-level binding terms do-not-annotate). Status: current consortium recommendations and decisions.
- [Intracellular_Signaling_Guidelines.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1ukxL3vKfLbV4NAiGSeGg3EbpLKijfXXOr0npHeUYLI4) (opus): Draft guideline defining intracellular signaling cassettes (start/end set by the first activated component), with annotation rules: members annotated to both cassette and pathway, cassette part_of pathway, ligand/receptor/upstream components to 'positive regulation of' the cassette, downstream targets not annotated to the cassette; per-cassette scope notes (SMAD, PI3K/PKB, NF-kB, NO-cGMP). Status: current draft with open comments; it states policy directly, so it stays at 5.
- [Multi-species terms - documentation to review.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=12_2bJFup0Wn9TVYlysfbxFz8YERibJ4OXDB8GRowueU) (opus): Wiki-derived documentation of multi-species process annotation: request terms in the multi-organism node rather than annotating host processes directly, direction-explicit term names, two-taxon taxon column, host = larger and symbiont = smaller organism, standard definition templates, worked examples. Marked 'TO BE REVIEWED' and out of date, since the multi-organism process node was later refactored and obsoleted; content is guideline-level, so 5 with that caveat.
- [New Causal Relations Decision Tree for VPE.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1ODFtV2aEeUeOUDs8dupre-3hUJchLFMMu-kChPKR1Oc) (opus): Curator-agreed decision tree (2022-12-20) for choosing a GO-CAM causal relation between two activities. First pick the relationship type (regulation, provides input for, constitutively upstream, removes input for, undetermined), then the direction of effect, then directness, which yields a specific RO relation. It is current guidance and the adopted outcome of the 2022 causal relations review.
- [Protein oligomerization obsoletion - proposal.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1JxzNwl1fr5Fj-IJy_Fo1lLfAlAQB8IxPDwwYKxIXBZo) (opus): Proposal to obsolete the protein (homo/hetero)oligomerization BP and MF terms, with explicit annotation rules for the whole class of terms. A gene product that mediates assembly goes to protein-containing complex assembly (GO:0065003) with a has_output complex extension. Assembled subunits instead get the CC complex term or a structural or bridging MF, and the annotation is removed if nothing fits. It is labelled a proposal, but it sets class-wide guidance (the terms were later obsoleted), so 5 stands.
- [Regulation Notes.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1mAIocbi6UvG_zbfCrWOUrneia3vl2XkjVPM39tmSx40) (opus): Documented GO-CAM convention (2020 Jamboree) on when to use 'regulates' between two MFs: successive activities with direct state change (MAPKK->MAPK), binding that activates a downstream activity of the same product, and specificity factors controlling gene expression (TFs, miRNAs); and when not to (trafficking-related PTM enzymes). A guideline document stating rules directly; current as GO-CAM practice.
- [Transcription Factors Annotation Guidelines.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1bqCxx7QUblrc9q4PIynbJzoCXv248UWBxJa7fguJyXs) (opus): GO guideline for transcription regulators: separates GTFs, dbTFs and coTFs with recommended MF/BP/CC terms for each, requires experimental evidence of sequence-specific DNA binding plus target regulation for dbTF activity, sets has_input conventions, and gives a four-question checklist (hypothesis, domain/ortholog support, consistency with other annotations, results). Activator plus repressor annotations are noted as not inconsistent. Status: archived (last updated 2023-10-24) and replaced by the published guideline paper (Gaudet et al. 2021, BBA Gene Regulatory Mechanisms), which is canonical; some term labels here are outdated.
- [Transcription coregulator activity.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1Wq-_3w6F2L_CGvneQT8n-pv-Ekk_i2_QpUZJeO_kvxI	1lNJkuoJask1SYt8CgVcBFr3CQ_7D72lp) (opus): GO-CAM activity-unit guideline for transcription coregulators: MF is a child of GO:0003712, has_input is the regulated transcription factor, the occurs_in context is the nucleus, the link to the TF is directly positively/negatively regulates, and the link to target-gene products is indirectly regulates. Status: archived, with no review date filled in; the current version presumably sits in the GO-CAM MF guidelines. The content itself is guideline text, so it stays at 5.
- [TranscriptionProgress.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B_c5g4FezmhfNENjNk5RbjJwQUk) (opus): Transcription working-group guidelines, 2017 revision. Use regulation of gene expression when the only evidence is a change in mRNA levels, and regulation of RNA Pol II transcription only when there is evidence of transcriptional regulation. A full decision tree maps assay type, DNA-binding evidence and ChIP evidence to six annotation patterns (has_direct_input, contributes_to, coincident_with); it does not apply to histones or signaling molecules. It states adopted guidance, though some of its terms and relations are now outdated. Duplicate of the .pptx.pptx copy with the same score; this file, with slide numbering, is the canonical copy.
- [TranscriptionProgress.pptx.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1C5DPbCHhvS8wJF8-xk4fh4jl9NF0GAtxTVBJgB4GUK4) (opus): Duplicate of TranscriptionProgress.pptx with the same 2017 transcription guidelines and decision tree (regulation of gene expression versus regulation of RNA Pol II transcription by assay type and DNA-binding evidence, with extension patterns). It gets the same score as its twin. The single .pptx is the canonical copy, and parts of the content are dated.
- [UCL_Expression_Transcription_Decision_Tree_2016.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B_c5g4FezmhfSzV5OERKcHJ1MUk) (opus): UCL (Huntley and Kramarz) decision tree for annotating gene products that regulate gene expression or transcription. It routes by assay type (reporter assay, nuclear run-on, mRNA levels) and by evidence of sequence-specific DNA binding to BP or MF transcription terms with their extensions. It states the guidance directly. It is the 2016 version, superseded by the 2017 TranscriptionProgress revision, and some terms (nuclear chromatin, the old TF activity terms) are now obsolete.
- [Updating contributing new GO annotations.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1ELFV19lLbGGDtvw-BZrqo4cT7xgaUFJ4eOd2K-tjvcs) (opus): Replacement text for the geneontology.org 'contributing to GO' page: the same evidence standards apply to every annotation set, no unsolicited sets are accepted, contributors must maintain their sets, large-scale or LLM-derived sets are only considered under a pre-agreed framework with curator evaluation, and LLM-assisted associations must be manually reviewed and then get experimental codes. It also gives GAF 2.2 requirements (precise valid terms respecting taxon constraints, precise evidence code, GO rules). Status: current; the Fall 2026 WG deck reports this update as done.
- [Use of relations for GO annotation - GP2term and extensions.pptx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=128U6g91Iv2ej9pcgTSGOowbhjp4GwGXmE4gDTiue1Cc) (opus): Training deck from September 2021 on GP2term relations: the defaults for each aspect, when to use contributes_to, involved_in versus acts upstream of (or within), and is_active_in versus located_in. It also covers annotation extensions (has_input, has_output, occurs_in) and when to add them. It is current guidance stated directly.
- [evCode-proposals.doc](https://drive.google.com/open?id=12ksXkxcceb_IVRxejxC0DOSU9Wt7Bk70) (opus): Evidence Code Proposals draft (Jan 2007 Cambridge GOC) defining how IMP, IGI, IDA, IPI and other codes are applied: evidence codes are not quality measures, author intent decides IMP vs IDA for transfection, with-field semantics for IGI. A guideline document, archived and superseded by current GO/ECO evidence-code documentation.
- [evCode-summary.doc](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1qZm_e_wnfu_yUAg7qbwrJpYwyRRNqD_7) (opus): Summary of the 2007 evidence-code documentation changes: codes are not quality indicators, grouping into experimental/computational/author/curator, IMP vs IDA by author intent, TAS narrowed to cited statements, retreat from the 2006 ISS 'with' restriction, RCA spot-checking, IGC added. Records consortium decisions directly; archived and superseded by current docs.
- [nature accepted version.docx](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1ZIYCORgZUGSCTsA_QvKde7HV0UxPbFQQtK9BqImvGXg) (opus): Accepted manuscript of the PAN-GO paper (Feuermann et al., 'A compendium of human gene functions derived from evolutionary modeling'). It describes the PAINT explicit evolutionary-modelling method: review all experimental GO evidence across a PANTHER family tree, select maximally informative non-redundant terms, and place gain and loss of each function on tree branches to produce IBA annotations. It is an annotation-method paper establishing how IBAs are made. Status: accepted version; the published Nature article is the canonical source, and this docx and the PDF item are copies of the same manuscript.
- [nature accepted version.pdf](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1orCHKRivACLddQTBQd3kJsF6MK3Z3X9V) (opus): PDF copy of the same PAN-GO accepted manuscript as the .docx item. It describes the PAINT evolutionary-modelling method that is the basis of IBA annotations: family-wide evidence review, informative term selection, and explicit gain/loss placement on tree branches. It gets the same score as its duplicate; the published Nature article is the canonical version.

## GO wiki: score 5 (91)

- [11 MAR 2014 PAINT Conference Call](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/11_MAR_2014_PAINT_Conference_Call) (opus): Spells out the PAINT propagation logic for positive vs NOT annotations: NOT propagates only to the annotated term, never to parents; mixed positive/NOT at the same or more general level ignores the NOT; positive general terms still propagate into a clade where a specific term is stopped. Historical (2014) but a self-contained statement of the NOT-propagation rules, so kept at 5.
- [5 May 2020 PAINT Conference Call](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/5_May_2020_PAINT_Conference_Call) (opus): States PAINT policy for GAF 2.2 qualifiers: PAINT ignores gp2term qualifiers (except NOT) when deciding whether an experimental annotation supports an IBD, and IBD/IBA defaults are involved_in (BP), is_active_in (CC), enables or contributes_to (MF). Current-era (2020) explicit policy, kept at 5.
- [Annotating binding](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotating_binding) (opus): Annotation guideline on binding: MF binding terms are for biologically meaningful interactions, IPI with partner in with/from is required for GO:0005515, has_input captures the physiological partner (with/from for the assayed one), prefer specific binding child terms, and do not annotate substrate/cofactor binding. Marked PROPOSAL 2020 / TO BE REVIEWED, but it is the guideline page itself, so 5.
- [Annotating downstream processes](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotating_downstream_processes) (opus): Annotation guideline: annotate the gene product's direct activity rather than downstream consequences of core processes (RNA Pol II, spliceosome examples), request specific 'X involved in Y' terms, treat ligand availability as regulation of the signaling pathway, and revise downstream annotations as knowledge improves. Guideline page flagged TO BE REVIEWED against newer acts_upstream_of relations; still 5.
- [Annotating from phenotypes](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotating_from_phenotypes) (opus): Core guideline for phenotype-based BP annotation: annotations reflect normal function, 'required for' is not 'involved in', beware read-outs, pleiotropy and high-level phenotypes, use acts_upstream_of_or_within when mechanism is unknown, regulation needs MF-level evidence, and revisit old IMP annotations. Current guideline (reviewed 2018), clearly 5.
- [Annotating regulation](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotating_regulation) (opus): Guideline on choosing a process term versus its regulation term: define process boundaries, annotate the parent process (acts_upstream_of_or_within) when unsure, consider allele type and assay for IMP, do not annotate pathway members that activate the next step as regulators, and limiting components are not regulators. Guideline page marked TO BE REVIEWED, but states practice, so 5.
- [Annotation Assigned by](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Assigned_by) (opus): Draft documentation of the assigned_by (GAF col 15) convention: credit the group that made the annotation decision; InterPro2GO is assigned_by InterPro; SPKW2GO, SPSL2GO, HAMAP2GO are UniProtKB; EC2GO credits the source dataset, with matching GO_REFs. Self-contained convention document, though a 2014 draft and partly dated (SPKW annotations since retired), so kept at 5 with that caveat.
- [Annotation Conf. Call 2016-03-22](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Conf._Call_2016-03-22) (opus): Contains the full approved text of GO_REF:0000111 (IC annotations supported by at least one ISS annotation; with/from must list all supporting GO ids) and the matching website documentation, approved on this call; also discusses restricting has_participant to legacy data. Establishes an evidence/reference convention (2016, still current), kept at 5.
- [Annotation Conf. Call 2019-11-19](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Conf._Call_2019-11-19) (opus): Gives explicit reannotation rules for the 'activated by'/'inhibited by' extensions: endogenous direct regulators become a binding annotation that directly_positively/negatively_regulates the activity, binding-term extensions become has_input, competitive inhibitors/decoys directly negatively regulate the downstream activity, and unknown mechanisms are no longer captured. Self-contained rule set (2019), kept at 5.
- [Annotation Conf. Call 2020-02-04](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Conf._Call_2020-02-04) (opus): Records the ontology-editor decision that curators annotate to 'binding' with has_input ChEBI rather than requesting new pre-composed chemical binding terms, and that 'directly regulates' is only used between activities of different gene products; also has_direct_input to has_input and occurs_at to occurs_in. Explicit policy statements (2020); the binding-term criteria were left for future review, so possibly partly superseded.
- [Annotation Conf. Call 2021-08-03](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Conf._Call_2021-08-03) (opus): States the guidelines from the RHEA ontology jamboree: create enzyme terms only for characterised reactions (annotate L-amino acid/D-sugar forms by default), GO MF specificity matches enzyme specificity not every substrate, and NAD(P) terms mean cofactor unknown with narrow xrefs to NAD and NADP reactions. Explicit current MF term/annotation conventions, kept at 5.
- [Annotation Conf. Call 2024-03-12](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Conf._Call_2024-03-12) (opus): Sets out current do-not-annotate subset policy: gocheck_do_not_annotate and do_not_manually_annotate merged into one subset (warning rule GORULE:0000008, Noctua and PAINT to block, QuickGO filter to be removed) and a new obsoletion_candidate subset and warning rule. Self-contained current policy statement, kept at 5.
- [Annotation Extension field: Supplying temporal location information to a GO annotation](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Extension_field%3A_Supplying_temporal_location_information_to_a_GO_annotation) (opus): Guideline page on using exists_during in the annotation extension to record the cell-cycle or developmental stage at which a CC localization is observed, with S. pombe worked examples and a pointer to the BP occurs_in CC pattern. A documented extension convention; archived wiki (2014) and cell-cycle phase terms used are now obsolete, but the content is guidance, so 5.
- [Annotation Extension: Capturing cell and tissue types](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Extension%3A_Capturing_cell_and_tissue_types) (opus): Guideline for adding cell, tissue and anatomy context in extensions: part_of for CC and occurs_in for BP/MF, pipe/comma syntax, the rule that context does not imply exclusivity, and that immortalized cell lines are not evidence of cell-type context, with worked TLR4/Wnt7a examples. A standing guideline page (edited 2021), so 5.
- [Annotation Relations](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Relations) (opus): Current reference page (edited 2026) defining GO-CAM contextual and causal relations and the standard gp2term relations per aspect, including default relations, when to use enables vs contributes to, involved in vs acts upstream of (or within) with directionality, and that colocalizes with is being deprecated. Authoritative current relation-use guidance, so 5.
- [Annotation consistency : ChIP experiments](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_consistency_%3A_ChIP_experiments) (opus): Current guideline (reviewed 2023) that ChIP supports localization to chromatin (or a child) but not direct DNA binding unless the study is premised on direct binding (e.g. known motif). A concise, explicit evidence-interpretation rule, so 5.
- [Apoptosis Curation Manual](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Apoptosis_Curation_Manual) (opus): Curation manual for cell death terms giving a decision tree from cell death down to apoptotic signaling and execution phases, rules for choosing extrinsic vs intrinsic pathways, restricting direct annotation to 'apoptotic process' when the phase is unknown, and adding cell type as an extension rather than using cell-type-specific terms where ISS would be lost. Explicit curator guideline (edited 2023), so 5.

Documented relation-use convention (no examples given), so 5.

Explicit relation-use guidance, though without examples, so 5.

Explicit practice decision on a gp2term relation; review date 2018 but consistent with the current Annotation Relations page, so 5.

Explicit relation-use convention, so 5.
- [Contributes to](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Contributes_to) (opus): Current guideline (reviewed 2023, edited 2026) on the contributes_to qualifier: only for irreducible molecular machines, a three-question checklist, worked examples (ATP citrate lyase, RNA Pol II core, ATP synthase) and out-of-scope rules (single catalytic subunit or homomer uses enables; regulatory subunits get regulator activity; unknown catalytic subunit gets no MF). Core annotation policy, so 5.

Consortium policy directly on point, so 5.
- [Development](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Development) (opus): GO editors' guideline for development terms: the standard x development / morphogenesis / formation / structural organization / maturation part_of structure, standard textual definitions, and how development differs from morphogenesis, maturation and metamorphosis (with imaginal-disc exception). Agreed 2003, page last edited 2018 and still the reference convention; a substantive guideline document.
- [Directly negatively regulates](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Directly_negatively_regulates) (opus): Official GO-CAM relation guideline: use 'directly negatively regulates' only between activities in direct succession with a known mechanism (effect on the enabler, or removal of an activator); do not capture constitutive upstream activities. Current, reviewed February 2023 by named curators.
- [Directly positively regulates](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Directly_positively_regulates) (opus): Official GO-CAM relation guideline: 'directly positively regulates' links successive MFs with no intervening activity where the upstream activity enhances the downstream enabler; constitutive upstream activity is not captured. Current, reviewed February 2023, with EGF/MAPK examples.
- [Elements of an annotation](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Elements_of_an_annotation) (opus): Core current annotation guideline (reviewed 2026-03) covering annotation subject, relations, extensions, evidence, reference and assigned_by, with a detailed NOT policy: NOT propagates down, use only when a property would be expected, not for negative/inconclusive results or with extensions, and IKR for sequence-based NOTs.
- [Enables](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Enables) (opus): Official gene-product-to-term relation guideline: 'enables' when the gene product performs the MF itself; accessory subunits should not get the MF but adaptor or regulator activity instead, with contributes_to for multi-subunit activities. Current documentation (reviewed 2018).
- [Guide to GO Evidence Codes](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guide_to_GO_Evidence_Codes) (opus): Canonical current guide to GO evidence codes: categories, ECO mappings, the principle that evidence codes are not quality measures, multiple annotations with different codes from one paper, and the decision tree. Core annotation guideline (last edit 2025).
- [Guidelines for GO textual definitions](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_for_GO_textual_definitions) (opus): Ontology editors' guideline for textual definitions: genus-differentia form, no plurals/circularity/categorizers, design-pattern standard definitions, and definition cross-reference rules (PMID preferred, RHEA/EC for enzymes, no Reactome). Tagged 'BEING REVIEWED' but reviewed April 2024; current.
- [Guidelines for catalytic activity terms](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_for_catalytic_activity_terms) (opus): Detailed current editor guideline for catalytic activity terms: in vivo substrate specificity, scope of positional/histone terms, bi-directional and multi-step reactions, criteria for obsoleting/merging, labels from EC, '=' reaction definitions, EC/RHEA hierarchy, and GO's NAD(P) convention (annotate both NAD and NADP terms if both used).
- [Guidelines for checkpoints](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_for_checkpoints) (opus): Explicit annotation rule: do not annotate to checkpoint terms from mutations that activate the checkpoint (arrest shows the checkpoint works), only from mutations that inactivate it; repair genes go to 'response to checkpoint' terms. Short but complete, reusable policy for a class of terms.
- [Guidelines for logical definitions](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_for_logical_definitions) (opus): Current (2026) ontology editors' guideline for logical definitions: genus plus minimal necessary-and-sufficient differentiae, no OR, other relations as asserted superclasses, alignment with textual definitions, design-pattern examples, and has_part some binding for MF. Directly states editorial policy.
- [Guidelines for new Binding terms](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_for_new_Binding_terms) (opus): Brief but explicit editor policy: subclasses of GO:0005515 protein binding must represent general protein classes, not specific proteins, and new protein-containing complex binding terms must correspond to a protein-containing complex subclass. Current (2024).
- [Guidelines for new Biological Processes](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_for_new_Biological_Processes) (opus): Editors' guideline for new BP terms: discrete beginning and end in the definition, the is_a vs part_of test (does the whole parent process occur?), draft metabolic primary input/output rules, and disease/mutant processes out of scope. Partly an index of topic links, but the rules stated are real current policy.
- [Guidelines for new Molecular Functions](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_for_new_Molecular_Functions) (opus): Editors' guideline for new MF terms: MF and BP must be orthogonal, 'activity' suffix, in vivo substrate specificity, has_part for compound functions, regulator scope, NAD(P) convention and out-of-scope items. Current (reviewed 2024) though it retains an older 'TO BE REVIEWED' section on binding and grouping terms.
- [Guidelines for using ChEBI terms](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_for_using_ChEBI_terms) (opus): Ontology convention that GO uses the ChEBI major microspecies at pH 7.3, how to find it via RHEA/ChEBI, the build check and allow-list, and how to fix failures. Current editor policy (reviewed 2024, edited 2026).
- [Guidelines from Annotation Camp](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Guidelines_from_Annotation_Camp) (opus): Workshop-derived annotation guidelines on downstream/indirect processes (annotate core processes, remove indirect ones as knowledge grows), avoiding redundant binding annotations, with/from vs extension usage, 'response to' term use and IEP vs IDA. Explicit guidance, but historical (c. 2007-2010) and partly superseded, e.g. its advice to request 'X involved in Y' terms conflicts with current practice.
- [Happens during](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Happens_during) (opus): Current annotation-extension guideline for happens_during: used only for temporal phases (biological phase or life stage), no longer for attaching a process of unclear relation (use part_of when it is part of the process), and exists_during for components. States the convention directly with examples.
- [Has input](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Has_input) (opus): Current guideline for the has_input extension/GO-CAM relation: specific molecular target of an MF or BP with a resolvable ID, direct physical interaction not required, separate annotations for different substrates, distinction from with/from, and what not to capture (redundant with term, currency chemicals, analogs, cofactors). Reviewed 2021.
- [Has output](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Has_output) (opus): Current guideline for the has_output extension: capture specific products more specific than the term, multiple outputs only for one reaction, and do not capture currency chemicals or products already in the definition, with worked examples. Reviewed 2021.
- [Inferred by Curator (IC)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_by_Curator_%28IC%29) (opus): Evidence-code guideline for IC: used when an annotation is inferred from other GO annotations, WITH/FROM must hold the supporting GO ID(s), comma for AND and pipe for OR, and GO_REF:0000036 when several sources are combined. Current policy (reviewed 2018) though its example terms are now obsolete.
- [Inferred from Direct Assay (IDA)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Direct_Assay_%28IDA%29) (opus): Current evidence-code guideline for IDA: IDA vs IMP for heterologous/overexpression experiments, IPI rather than IDA when the binding partner can be identified, IDA for complex isolation via tagged subunit, and GORULEs forbidding IDA on binding/protein binding and WITH entries on IDA.
- [Inferred from Electronic Annotation (IEA)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Electronic_Annotation_%28IEA%29) (opus): Current evidence-code guideline for IEA listing the GOC-sanctioned pipelines (EC, HAMAP, InterPro2GO, RHEA, UniProt keywords and subcellular locations, TreeGrafter, Ensembl Compara, ARBA), mandatory GO_REF and WITH/FROM, ISS/ISO when reviewed by a curator, and the IEA age rule. Notes that several mappings are under review (2026-03).
- [Inferred from Experiment (EXP)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Experiment_%28EXP%29) (opus): Evidence-code guideline: EXP is the parent experimental code and curators should use the specific IDA/IPI/IMP/IGI/IEP codes instead; no WITH/FROM for EXP. Short but directly prescriptive and current.
- [Inferred from Expression Pattern (IEP)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Expression_Pattern_%28IEP%29) (opus): Evidence-code guideline restricting IEP to Biological Process terms (GORULE:0000006), only for normal expression patterns, with a list of overexpression, localisation and binding cases that should use IDA, IMP or IPI instead. Current policy.
- [Inferred from Genetic Interaction (IGI)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Genetic_Interaction_%28IGI%29) (opus): Detailed current evidence-code guideline for IGI: when to use IGI vs IMP (multiple vs single perturbation), suppression/enhancement/synthetic, co-transfection and complementation cases, WITH/FROM rules (pipe vs comma), reciprocal annotations, and cases that must not use IGI (same-gene rescue, miRNA reporter assays).
- [Inferred from Genomic Context (IGC)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Genomic_Context_%28IGC%29) (opus): Official IGC evidence-code page: when operon, synteny and pathway/genome-scale reconstruction justify IGC, that process annotations require the whole process to be present in the organism, and WITH/FROM conventions (neighbour genes or pathway ID; GO_REF for unpublished methods). Current (reviewed 2018, edited 2025); a guideline document, so 5.
- [Inferred from High Throughput Direct Assay (HDA)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_High_Throughput_Direct_Assay_%28HDA%29) (opus): Official HDA evidence-code policy: which assays qualify, proteomics criteria (peptide FDR <1%, at least 2 unique peptides, purification quality checks) and fluorescence-localisation screens, with low-throughput validation annotated as IDA in addition. Current guideline (reviewed 2018); 5.
- [Inferred from High Throughput Experiment (HTP)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_High_Throughput_Experiment_%28HTP%29) (opus): Official HTP evidence-code policy: what counts as high-throughput data, same quality standard as low-throughput, <1% false-positive target, annotate the conclusion not the assay, subsets allowed, no minimum dataset size, no WITH/FROM. Current consortium guideline; 5.
- [Inferred from High Throughput Mutant Phenotype (HMP)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_High_Throughput_Mutant_Phenotype_%28HMP%29) (opus): Official HMP evidence-code policy: never annotate a primary RNAi screen, require off-target controls, replicates, Z-factor >= 0.5 and independent validation, and check that the phenotype directly supports the term. Current guideline (reviewed 2018); 5.
- [Inferred from Key Residues (IKR)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Key_Residues_%28IKR%29) (opus): Official IKR evidence-code page: every IKR row carries NOT, WITH/FROM is mandatory for GO_REF-based IKR, and an experimental NOT (IDA) is used instead or as well when lack of activity is assayed, with a caution about family exceptions (WNK1). Current guideline; 5.
- [Inferred from Mutant Phenotype (IMP)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Mutant_Phenotype_%28IMP%29) (opus): Official IMP evidence-code policy: allele and perturbation comparisons (including inhibitors and RNAi), IMP versus IDA for overexpression/transfection, IMP plus an extension when gene A's mutation informs gene B, and WITH/FROM for allele IDs. Current (edited 2026); 5.
- [Inferred from Physical Interaction (IPI)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Physical_Interaction_%28IPI%29) (opus): Official IPI evidence-code policy: an interactor ID is required in WITH/FROM, IPI must not be used for catalytic activity, unidentified-partner binding uses IDA, and the GORULE checks are listed. Current guideline, though it cites obsolete GO:0043234 and the reciprocal protein-binding rule may be dated; 5.
- [Inferred from Reviewed Computational Analysis (RCA)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Reviewed_Computational_Analysis_%28RCA%29) (opus): Official RCA evidence-code page: RCA is for integrated analysis of large-scale or multi-type data; sequence-only analysis uses ISS, unreviewed predictions use IEA, single experimental results use IPI/IGI/IEP. Current; notes the code label was renamed to 'automatically integrated combinatorial evidence'. Guideline, so 5.
- [Inferred from Sequence Alignment (ISA)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Sequence_Alignment_%28ISA%29) (opus): Official ISA evidence-code policy: the match protein must be experimentally characterised (to stop transitive annotation errors), curator review of the alignment, ISO when orthology is established, mandatory WITH, IEA for any automated step, with worked examples. Current; 5.
- [Inferred from Sequence Model (ISM)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Sequence_Model_%28ISM%29) (opus): Official ISM evidence-code policy: manually reviewed model hits (HMM, PROSITE, InterPro, tRNAscan) use ISM, automated hits use IEA, and curators must judge how functionally specific a model is (e.g. CUB domain is uninformative). - [Inferred from Sequence Orthology (ISO)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Sequence_Orthology_%28ISO%29) (opus): Official ISO evidence-code policy: orthology criteria, mandatory WITH naming experimentally supported orthologs (all paralogs where one-to-many), GO_REF use (e.g. MGI GO_REF:0000096), updating annotations when orthology predictions change, and IEA for bulk transfer. Current; 5.
- [Inferred from Sequence or structural Similarity (ISS)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Inferred_from_Sequence_or_structural_Similarity_%28ISS%29) (opus): Core ISS evidence-code policy: ISS as umbrella for ISA/ISM/ISO, mandatory WITH whose gene product must have experimental or IC support, GO_REF:0000024 for curator analyses, a caution about transferring from HTP-based annotations, and GORULE checks. Current (reviewed 2024); 5.

- [Tips to Produce High Quality Annotations](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Tips_to_Produce_High_Quality_Annotations) (opus): Current (reviewed Nov 2025) annotation best-practice page: curate pathway/gene-centrically, annotate core function only, capture the conclusion not the assay (e.g. wound-healing assay is not wound healing), annotate normal function, check term parentage, remove outdated annotations, and AI-suggested associations need manual review and then take experimental codes. Keep 5.
- [Traceable Author Statement (TAS)](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Traceable_Author_Statement_%28TAS%29) (opus): Evidence-code guideline (reviewed 2018): TAS is for author statements attributed to a cited source, no with/from, not for textbook common knowledge (use IC), NAS for uncited statements, and remove TAS once an experimental annotation to the same term exists. Keep 5.
- [Transmembrane Proteins](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Transmembrane_Proteins) (opus): Annotation guideline allowing experimental membrane localization plus predicted TM domains to support 'integral to membrane' children, with a caution on N-terminal single TM segments acting as signal peptides, and a pipeline plan for legacy annotations. The guidance is direct, but out of date: GO:0016021 and the integral-to-X-membrane terms have since been obsoleted.

## go-annotation issues and PRs: score 5 (8)

- [GOC to decide what is HTP data ](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1390) (opus): Records the GOC decision on what counts as HTP data: hypothesis-driven work is not HTP even if large, exploratory screens are, and lists the characteristics of HTP papers (same workflow over many genes, automated, open-ended, tabular datasets, false positives). The guideline text is quoted in full, keep 5; it is now part of the HTP evidence-code guide.
- [Question about proposed GO rule: IPI should not be used with catalytic activity molecular function terms](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/2131) (opus): Records adoption of gorule-0000007: IPI must not be used for catalytic activity MF terms, scoped to is_a children of catalytic activity (enzyme regulator terms excluded after discussion), with non-compliant annotations filtered. A consortium evidence-code rule adoption, keep 5.
- [Review Noctua annotations using some IEA sources](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/6085) (opus): States a rule change: IEA annotations from UniProt-Keywords, UniProt-SubCell and UniPathway may no longer be used as evidence in Noctua, and affected annotations must be re-supported or generalised. Brief but a direct, current (2025) consortium policy on evidence sources, so 5.
- [Review annotations to GO:0051259 'protein complex oligomerization' and children](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/2521) (opus): Consortium annotation-review ticket announcing the decision that oligomerization/stoichiometry of protein interactions is out of GO scope, with GO:0051259 and children to be obsoleted, InterPro2GO mappings removed and crystallography-based annotations deleted. It states a scope policy for a class of terms directly; the detailed review guidelines are in a linked Google doc. Historical (terms since obsoleted) but the scope rule still applies.
- [Review annotations to co-localizes_with GO:0032991 protein-containing complex  & children](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1940) (opus): Protein Complex WG decision that colocalizes_with is not allowed on protein-containing complex terms, with the 9 May 2018 summary of replacements: remove if no interaction evidence, use an x complex binding term, or protein-containing complex binding with the complex ID in with or has_direct_input. Debate on colocalization evidence follows. Explicit rule and alternatives, keep 5.
- [Review annotations using extension 'occurs_at'](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/2584) (opus): Consortium decision deprecating the occurs_at extension relation, with explicit per-pattern replacement rules (GO CC/CL to occurs_in, SO motif to has_input on a binding term, Pfam removed, promoter/enhancer SO under discussion) and follow-up clarifications (occurs_in for histone-modifier locations, no SO regions on target proteins). Directly establishes extension-relation practice; parts on transcription-regulation extensions were left pending GREEKC input. Historical but still the basis of current usage.
- [Should HTP annotations be allowed sequence-based transfer methods](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1775) (opus): HTP working-group decision that HTP-evidenced annotations may be propagated by ISS/ISA/ISO/IBA, with no new codes, case-by-case manual review and no automated pipelines, followed by the ISS wiki update and P2GO change. States the rule and its documentation, keep 5.
- [obsoletion: drug binding](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/3166) (opus): Obsoletion of 'drug binding' with explicit annotation advice agreed at an annotation call: annotate the biologically relevant ligand when a compound is a proxy, make BP annotations for inhibitor-manipulated pathways only if the gene normally participates, do not annotate binding for gain-of-function drug interactions, and review 'response to x' terms. A consortium decision stating rules for a class of binding annotations; current.
