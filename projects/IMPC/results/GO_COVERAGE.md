## Does IMPC data reach GO?

IMPC allele symbols from the significant calls, looked up in MGI's genotype-phenotype report (`MGI_GenePheno.rpt`) for their MGI allele ids, which are then looked for in the WITH/FROM column of MGI's IMP rows (the allele a mutant-phenotype row was made on).

- MGI phenotype (MP) annotation rows on those alleles: 51,958 (47,569 with no PubMed id, the IMPC data loads; 4,389 from papers), on 11,428 genotypes; 8,072 IMPC alleles resolved to MGI ids
- Positive control: MGI IMP rows whose WITH/FROM names any allele in that report: 26,076 (43%)
- MGI IMP rows made on an IMPC allele: 407 (114 genes), citing a PMID: 407, citing anything else: 0
