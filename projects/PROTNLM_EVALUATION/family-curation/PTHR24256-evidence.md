This family of proteins includes serine proteases with diverse biological functions, primarily involved in immune responses. Members are characterized by their proteolytic activity, with some having a specific role in the melanization-mediated immune response, where they cleave substrates to activate cascades such as the prophenoloxidase system. This process is crucial for pathogen recognition and defense in invertebrates. Other members are implicated in morphogenesis and development, contributing to processes like germ-band retraction and dorsal closure. Some proteins within the family are inactive but still play essential roles in immune responses or development by interacting with other proteins or pathways. The family also includes threonine proteases, which may be involved in proteolysis. The presence of a clip domain in some members suggests a structural feature that is important for their function.
