INTERPRO The Homeobox protein family is characterized by the presence of a homeobox domain, which enables these proteins to bind DNA and regulate gene expression. Members of this family function as transcription factors involved in a variety of developmental and cellular processes. They recognize specific DNA sequences to either activate or repress transcription, playing crucial roles in cell differentiation, organ development, and the establishment of body axes. Some members are involved in cell cycle regulation, acting as transcriptional repressors during specific phases. Others have roles in mating type determination in yeast, pharyngeal development, and the biosynthesis of compounds in pathogenicity. The family includes proteins that regulate hematopoietic differentiation, vascular endothelial cell proliferation, and anterior identity in embryogenesis. They may also interact with signaling pathways such as WNT and NODAL, and some have additional functions like mRNA nuclear export inhibition.
