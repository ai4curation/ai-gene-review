INTERPRO This entry includes ALDH7A1 and related proteins from all domains of life. Mammalian ALDH7A1 (an alpha-aminoadipic semialdehyde dehydrogenase) is homologous to the plant turgor-responsive ALDH protein, an enzyme that protects against various forms of stress, such as salinity, dehydration, and osmotic stress [[cite:PUB00096589], [cite:PUB00096586]]. Mutations in the ALDH7A1 gene cause pyridoxine-dependent epilepsy (PDE) [[cite:PUB00096587]].
