INTERPRO This entry represents Probable quinate permease and related sugar transporters. These proteins encompass a diverse group of integral membrane transport proteins involved in the facilitated transport of a wide variety of substrates across cellular membranes. Members of this family are primarily involved in the uptake of sugars such as glucose, fructose, galactose, and quinic acid, which can be catabolised as carbon sources. These transporters operate with varying affinities, enabling organisms to adapt to different concentrations of extracellular sugars. Some members also participate in symport mechanisms, coupling sugar transport with proton movement. Additionally, certain proteins within this family function as sensors in glucose-signalling pathways, although they do not transport glucose themselves. The family includes proteins that contribute to metabolic processes, adaptation to environmental changes, and the biosynthesis of natural products [[cite:PUB00164864]].
