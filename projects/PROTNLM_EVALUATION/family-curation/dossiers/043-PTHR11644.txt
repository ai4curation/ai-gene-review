INTERPRO The cytidine and deoxycytidylate deaminase family encompasses enzymes that play a crucial role in pyrimidine metabolism. Members of this family are responsible for the hydrolytic deamination of cytidine and 2'-deoxycytidine, converting them into uridine and 2'-deoxyuridine, respectively. This reaction is essential for the recycling of cytidine and for the synthesis of uridine monophosphate (UMP). Some members of the family have also evolved to deaminate the cytosine moiety of certain antibiotics, such as blasticidin S, contributing to antibiotic resistance. The family is characterized by a conserved domain structure, with each monomer typically comprising a small N-terminal alpha-helical domain and two larger core domains that exhibit nearly identical tertiary structures.
