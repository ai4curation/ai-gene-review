INTERPRO The protein family includes mitochondrial and outer membrane metalloproteases involved in quality control and stress response. Mitochondrial members act as part of the inner membrane quality control system, cleaving mistranslated or misfolded proteins and regulating mitochondrial dynamics and apoptosis in response to stress. They play roles in the integrated stress response, lipid metabolism, and energy expenditure. Outer membrane members function as chaperones and proteases, maintaining membrane integrity by assembling or eliminating proteins based on their folding state, and are involved in LPS-assembly protein degradation. The family is characterized by a stress-sensor region that regulates proteolytic activity, often activated by autocatalytic cleavage upon mitochondrial depolarization.
