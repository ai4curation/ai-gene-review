INTERPRO This entry represents a family of inositol 5-phosphatases and similar proteins predominantly found in eukaryotes, including Synaptojanin-1  (SYNJ1), phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate 5-phosphatase A (PI5PA) and inositol polyphosphate 5-phosphatase OCRL from humans. These enzymes act on various phosphoinositides, including phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate, phosphatidylinositol (4,5)-bisphosphate and phosphatidylinositol (3,4,5)-trisphosphate [[cite:PUB00101558], [cite:PUB00101560]]. OCRL regulates traffic in the endosomal pathway by regulating the specific pool of phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate that is associated with endosomes [[cite:PUB00087626]] and is involved in primary cilia assembly [[cite:PUB00101559], [cite:PUB00101557]]. This protein is associated with the oculocerebrorenal syndrome of Lowe and Dent disease 2 [[cite:PUB00065572], [cite:PUB00087629]].
