INTERPRO The non-lysosomal glucosylceramidase family encompasses enzymes that catalyze the hydrolysis of glucosylceramides to glucose and ceramide, playing a crucial role in the metabolism of glycosphingolipids. These glycosphingolipids are involved in various cellular processes including growth, differentiation, adhesion, signaling, and membrane dynamics. Family members also exhibit transglucosylation activity, transferring glucose from glucosylceramides to cholesterol, affecting its solubility and function. Additionally, they can reverse this process under certain conditions. The enzymes are also capable of hydrolyzing and forming bile acid-glucose conjugates, and they can act on galactosylceramides with lower efficiency. Their activity is lipid-dependent and subject to regulation by various factors, including metal ions and specific inhibitors.
