INTERPRO This family of proteins is involved in the RNA interference (RNAi) pathway and plays a critical role in the formation of the RNA-induced silencing complex (RISC). Members of this family are part of the RISC loading complex (RLC), which processes precursor microRNAs (pre-miRNAs) into mature miRNAs and loads them onto Argonaute proteins. These proteins also participate in the production of short interfering RNAs (siRNAs) from double-stranded RNA (dsRNA), which are key in post-transcriptional gene silencing. Additionally, they are implicated in the regulation of translation, either by repressing or altering the cleavage site of pre-miRNAs. Some members have a role in the innate immune response, particularly in viral infections, by interacting with and modulating the activity of proteins involved in interferon signaling.
