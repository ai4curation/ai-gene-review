INTERPRO This entry represents Rhophilin-1/2 (RHPN1/2) and similar proteins predominantly found in vertebrates. RHPN1/2 bind specifically to GTP-Rho and regulate the actin cytoskeleton through interactions with various downstream effector molecules [[cite:PUB00102006]]. RHPN1 is a key regulator of the podocyte cytoskeleton and is essential for glomerular filtration [[cite:PUB00093212]]. The isolated Bro1-like domain of Rhophilin-1 ([interpro:IPR042715]) binds human immunodeficiency virus type 1 (HIV-1) nucleocapsid [[cite:PUB00093211]]. RHPN2 limit stress fibre formation and/or increase the turnover of F-actin structures in the absence of high levels of RhoA activity [[cite:PUB00102006]].
