INTERPRO This entry represents Gemin-5, a protein involved in the assembly and function of small nuclear ribonucleoproteins (snRNPs), which are essential components of the spliceosome, the complex responsible for the splicing of pre-mRNA in eukaryotic cells [[cite:PUB00155015], [cite:PUB00103799]]. They recognise and bind to snRNAs and the 7-methylguanosine cap found on mRNA and snRNA molecules [[cite:PUB00155012], [cite:PUB00155013], [cite:PUB00155014]]. They are part of the SMN complex, facilitating the formation of the core snRNP by delivering snRNAs to the complex. Additionally, they may interact with ribosomes and play a role in the regulation of protein synthesis. In Drosophila, is also described as a nuclear receptor cofactor in ecdysone-regulated gene expression during molting and puparium formation [[cite:PUB00155010]] and it is essential in muscle and neuronal tissues for motor function, including climbing ability and flight [[cite:PUB00155011]].
