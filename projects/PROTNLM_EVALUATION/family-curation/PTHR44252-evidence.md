The L-xylulose reductase family comprises enzymes that catalyze the NADPH-dependent reduction of various sugars and alpha-dicarbonyl compounds. Members of this family participate in the uronate cycle of glucose metabolism and may contribute to cellular osmoregulation in renal tubules by producing xylitol, an osmolyte. Some family members also metabolize endogenous carbonyl compounds, such as those derived from lipid peroxidation, and may play a role in xenobiotic metabolism. The family belongs to the broader short-chain dehydrogenases/reductases (SDR) superfamily, known for their involvement in the metabolism of steroids, prostaglandins, and xenobiotics.
