This group represents protein CABLES (CDK5 and ABL1 enzyme substrate), including CABLES1 and CABLES2 [[cite:PUB00016076], [cite:PUB00015978]]. CABLES1 is a cyclin-dependent kinase binding protein, primarily involved in cell cycle regulation [[cite:PUB00073756]]. CABLES1 binds to different functional domains of p53 and p73 and modifies their cell death-inducing activities [[cite:PUB00033517]]. It may be a tumour suppressor in human ovarian cancer [[cite:PUB00073756]]. CABLES2 is involved in both p53-mediated and p53-independent apoptotic pathways [[cite:PUB00073479]].
