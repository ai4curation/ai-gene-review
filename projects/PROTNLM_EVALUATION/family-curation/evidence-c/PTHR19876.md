This family of proteins is involved in intracellular trafficking, specifically in the transport of proteins from the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) to the Golgi apparatus and from the Golgi back to the ER. Members of this family form part of the coatomer complex, which is a cytosolic protein complex that binds to specific motifs on cargo proteins and vesicles. This complex is essential for the formation of non-clathrin-coated vesicles and is required for vesicle budding from the Golgi. The coatomer complex is also implicated in maintaining Golgi structural integrity and in the processing and recycling of certain receptors, such as the low-density lipoprotein (LDL) receptor. The recruitment of the coatomer to membranes is dependent on ADP-ribosylation factors (ARFs), which are small GTP-binding proteins.
