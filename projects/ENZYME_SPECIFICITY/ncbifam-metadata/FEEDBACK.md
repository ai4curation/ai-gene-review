RefSeq hits are the release table's `n_refseq_protein_hits`. `proposed` is a curator's replacement where one was checked; `GO terms for the model's EC` lists, unreviewed, the terms that cross-reference the model's own EC numbers. `no GO term cross-references EC …` means no GO term carries that EC as a cross-reference; GO often has a term for the reaction all the same, cross-referenced only to Rhea, as `proposed` shows for adenosine deaminase. In the EC_SUSPECT section the suspect EC's terms are evidence for the verdict, not proposals. The RefSeq column is summed over the distinct models in each verdict; a model in two verdicts counts in both, so the rows are not additive.
