# UniProt: F2U5Y1 is named "Lung surfactant protein A"; it is Rosetteless

**Destination:** UniProt (uniprot.org update request).

**Summary.** UniProt F2U5Y1 (*Salpingoeca rosetta*, PTSG_03555) carries the
submitted name "Lung surfactant protein A", inherited from the genome
project's EMBL record EGD82922.1. The protein is **Rosetteless (Rtls)**:
- a forward genetic screen mapped the rosette-defect mutant to this gene
  (PMID:25299189);
- an independent CRISPR allele confirmed it (PMID:32496191).

It is a secreted C-type lectin-like protein with two CTLDs, and it is required
for rosette development. No similarity of function to animal surfactant
protein A has been shown.

**Requested change.** Rename the protein to Rosetteless, gene name *rtls*.
Jumble (F2TWH0) and couscous (F2UJ78) could get their literature names too
(PMID:30556809).
