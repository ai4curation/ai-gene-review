# PANTHER, PTHR10316 / PTHR17616: S. rosetta Yorkie candidate placed in the MAGI-related family

**Destination:** PANTHER team (pantherdb.org feedback).

**Summary.** The 2025 knockout study names PTSG_06057 (UniProt F2UDK1) as the
*S. rosetta* Yorkie homolog (bioRxiv DOI:10.1101/2024.07.13.603360, version
2). PANTHER places F2UDK1 in **PTHR10316:SF68**, "MEMBRANE ASSOCIATED GUANYLATE
KINASE-RELATED". TreeGrafter grafts it onto PTN002569196, an ecdysozoan node,
and it inherits cytoplasm and signal transduction. A different *S. rosetta*
WW-domain protein, F2U5K0 (PTSG_03848), is classed in the YAP1 family
**PTHR17616:SF8**, together with Drosophila yki (Q45VV3) and *Capsaspora*
coYki (A0A0D2WY30).

**Evidence** (`genes/SALRS/yorkie/yorkie-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md`, our own
analysis):
- F2UDK1 has the four Warts/LATS HXRXXS motifs reported for srYki
  (PMID:38729842), and no recognizable TEAD-binding motif.
- In full-length alignments, F2UDK1 scores higher than F2U5K0 against YAP1,
  Yki and coYki. Much of that similarity is in the WW domains.

**Requested change.** Check the classification of F2UDK1 and F2U5K0 with a
phylogeny of holozoan WW-domain proteins. Either PANTHER's call or the
literature's ortholog assignment is wrong.
