# IEP corpus survey

Generated by `projects/IEP/iep_corpus_survey.py`. Two views: raw GOA (`*-goa.tsv`, what GOA ships) and reviewed (`*-ai-review.yaml`, what reviewers concluded).

GO closure computed against GO release **2026-07-26** (OAK adapter `sqlite:obo:go` by default; override with `IEP_GO_ADAPTER`). The coarse branch tallies can move by a row or two between GO releases, as terms are obsoleted or reparented; everything else is release-independent.

**What the reviewer dispositions are.** Every `action` counted below comes from this repository's own `*-ai-review.yaml` files, which are AI-generated reviews produced under the guidance in `CLAUDE.md`. They are not independent adjudication of GO curators. See the [limitations note](../IEP.md#what-flagged-means-and-does-not-mean).
