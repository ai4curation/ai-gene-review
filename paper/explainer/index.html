AI Gene Review · manuscript in progress

Agentic review uncovers large systematic errors in biological knowledge bases

We had an AI agent re-check every Gene Ontology annotation on 10,505 genes against the literature, sequence analysis and curation rules. The errors it found are not scattered. They cluster in particular evidence types, annotation pipelines and parts of the ontology, so a single upstream fix can correct thousands of annotations at once.

Protein binding (GO:0005515) is 53,550 rows (16.3% of everything). It records that an interaction was seen, not what the protein does, so it is removed by policy and left out of every summary number. In 21.3% of those rows the reviewer could replace it with a real function such as adaptor or enzyme regulator.

Background

How a GO annotation gets made

Every annotation is a claim that a gene product has a function, location or role, tagged with an evidence code. The three main routes fail in different ways.

Experimental

IDA · IMP · IPI · IGI · IEP · HDA
  1. A paper reports an experiment.
  2. A curator reads it and picks a term.
  3. One row per gene, per paper.

Fails when the readout is far from the activity: an expression change or a knockout phenotype read as taking part in a process.

Phylogenetic

IBD → IBA
  1. A PAINT curator reads the experimental annotations across a PANTHER family tree.
  2. They place the function at the ancestral node where it arose.
  3. Every descendant inherits it.

Fails when the node is placed too deep, a subfamily changed function, or the seed annotation was wrong.

Electronic

IEA · InterPro2GO · keywords · ARBA · Compara
  1. A mapping links a domain, keyword, rule or ortholog to a term.
  2. It is applied to every matching protein.
  3. It is regenerated each release, unreviewed.

Fails when a term true for some family members is given to all, or context-specific biology is copied across species.

Method

The review system

One structured document per gene. The agent works only from cached sources, and every quote it uses as evidence must appear word for word in the cited paper or record. Validators enforce the schema, real ontology terms and curation policies before a review is merged.

  1. FetchUniProt record, GOA rows, cached papers
  2. ResearchDeep-research reports from several providers
  3. ReviewAgent judges each annotation, cites evidence
  4. ValidateSchema, term IDs, verbatim quotes, policies
  5. MergeReviewed pull request plus history record
  6. ReportSource defects, NOTs and new terms sent upstream

Results

Where the errors are

  • Tree-based propagation is about as reliable as direct assays. IBA rows are accepted 70.1% of the time, IDA 67.5%. Pairwise orthology transfer (ISO) is accepted only 33.0%.
  • The weakest experimental evidence is expression. IEP rows are accepted 13.6% and rejected 31.1%.
  • Electronic terms are usually the right kind but the wrong grain, so they are modified more often than rejected.
  • Biological process is the weakest aspect; cellular component the strongest.
Deep dives

Twelve systematic error classes

Ordered along the life of an annotation: from the experiment, through propagation and electronic mapping, to the ontology itself, the evidence trail, the entity annotated, and finally the tools that inherit the result.

01

Experimental is not the same as functional: the distance from readout to activity

Evidence codes say an experiment was done, not how close its readout is to the protein's own activity.

IEP rows: 13.6% accepted, 31.1% rejected. Molecular readouts support a function term 77% of the time, phenotype readouts 8%.

Phylogeny mostly gets it right, but bad seeds spread

03

Annotation by projection: orthology transfer into the rest of the tree of life

Outside model organisms almost everything is projected from orthologs, carrying context the target lineage lacks.

ISO accepted 33.0% vs IBA 70.1%. Naked mole rat: 1 experimental annotation among ~335,000.

04

Regulating is not participating: keyword mappings and the retirement of SPKW

05

A domain is not a function: InterPro2GO, ARBA and transcription factors

A domain tells you the kind of function, not which one; rules built on domains spread that guess.

InterPro2GO rows: 30.0% modified, the highest of any pipeline. Of ARBA rows not kept, 62% were true but uninformative.
