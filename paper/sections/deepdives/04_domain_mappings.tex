\subsection{A domain is not a function: InterPro2GO and ARBA}\label{sec:dd-domain}
\textbf{Claim.} Signature-based mappings attach a term valid for some family members to all;
association rules (ARBA) OR together thousands of condition sets under generic terms.
\textbf{Data.} InterPro2GO is the most \act{MODIFY}-heavy pipeline (Table~\ref{tab:iea}).
\proj{INTERPRO.md}: 3,652 judged InterPro2GO rows on 1,706 genes; 36 proposed mapping edits over
15 entries (e.g.\ ATP binding and protein phosphorylation on IPR000719).
\proj{ARBA\_REVIEW.md}: 6,051 ARBA rows; of those not kept, 62\% true-but-uninformative
(construction failures) vs 9\% false (consequent failures).
\textbf{Upstream.} Curated rules (\texttt{rules/curated/}) that couple sequence features with
taxon/module context. \todo{status of InterPro proposals.}
