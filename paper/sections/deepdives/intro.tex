\note{Each deep dive follows the same template: (i) the error class in one sentence;
(ii) why the pipeline or practice produces it; (iii) scale in our corpus, from a reproducible
query; (iv) one or two worked genes; (v) what changed upstream (term obsoleted, mapping
repointed, pipeline retired, NOT filed) or what should. Numbers marked \proj{} come from the
project page and must be re-derived by a script under \texttt{paper/scripts/} before submission.
Order follows the life of an annotation: experiment $\rightarrow$ propagation $\rightarrow$
electronic mapping $\rightarrow$ activity semantics $\rightarrow$ the ontology itself
$\rightarrow$ the evidence trail $\rightarrow$ the entity annotated $\rightarrow$ what downstream tools inherit.}
