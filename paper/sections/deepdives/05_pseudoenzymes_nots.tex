\subsection{Lost catalysis: pseudoenzymes and the missing NOTs}\label{sec:dd-pseudo}
\textbf{Claim.} Every automated channel (InterPro2GO, EC2GO, UniRule, ARBA, IBA) assigns
fold-retaining but catalytically dead proteins their family's activity, and only a NOT annotation
stops re-propagation.
\textbf{Data.} \proj{PSEUDOENZYMES.md}: tardigrade Cu/Zn-SOD paralogs, Epe1, EryCII, PqsB,
ActI-ORF2. \proj{TOP\_NOTS.md}: 250 NOT candidates mined from REMOVE/over-annotation reasons
across 19 species, 22 very strong. \proj{UNIPROT\_CAUTION\_NOTE.md}: CAUTION notes as a worklist
rediscovered CRMP/DPYSL, ILK, ROR1.
\textbf{Method highlight.} Catalytic-residue conservation, M-CSA, HMM scoring, structure.
