\subsection{Regulating is not participating: keyword and orthology mappings for processes}\label{sec:dd-kw}
\textbf{Claim.} Coarse mappings from UniProt keywords to GO processes assert that every protein
tagged ``Apoptosis'', ``Autophagy'' or ``Biological rhythms'' participates in that process.
\textbf{Data.} \proj{SPKW.md}: across 12 sub-projects (137 genes), 79--100\% of reviewed
eukaryotic apoptosis, autophagy, rhythm and \textit{S.\ pombe} meiosis keyword rows were
over-annotations; bacterial keyword rows were mostly sound. Corpus-wide, UniProt keyword2GO rows
are \todo{macro} rejected (Table~\ref{tab:iea}). Ensembl Compara orthology rows (mostly BP
transferred from mouse/rat phenotypes) have the lowest acceptance of any pipeline.
\textbf{Upstream.} GOA retired the SPKW pipeline for cellular organisms ($\sim$April 2026)
\todo{confirm, cite}. KW-1110 remapping (\proj{KW\_1110\_TRAF\_KW2GO.md}).
\textbf{Link to principle.} ``Necessity is not participation'' (IMP/IGI non-core rates,
\S\ref{sec:overview}); the AGT/angiotensin-maturation comparator check.
