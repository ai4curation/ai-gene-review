\subsection{Right enzyme, wrong reaction: specificity, promiscuity and non-physiological substrates}\label{sec:dd-enzyme}
\textbf{Claim.} MF terms name the right enzyme class but the wrong substrate, chain length,
cofactor or reaction, and in-vitro promiscuous activity is recorded as function.
\textbf{Data.} \proj{ENZYME\_SPECIFICITY.md}: 14 exemplars incl.\ all ten human mitochondrial
$\beta$-oxidation enzymes (chain-length specificity); LPL1, PHYKPL, EryCIII.
\proj{NONPHYSIOLOGICAL\_REACTIONS.md}: $\sim$15 GO MF terms obsoleted for non-physiological
substrates without criteria; proposal for criteria (selected, pathway-committed vs assay readout).
\textbf{Rule.} Promiscuity tiers: genuine secondary activity (non-core) / real but unknown
relevance (over-annotated) / paralog noise when a dedicated enzyme exists (remove, even IDA).
