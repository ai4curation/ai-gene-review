Gene Description

RBMP1, also called BST4 (bestrophin-like protein 4; locus Cre06.g261750), is a nuclear-encoded, chloroplast-targeted membrane protein of about 668 residues in the green alga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii. Its N-terminal part is a bestrophin (YneE/VCCN1-like) channel domain related to the thylakoid bestrophins BST1-3 and the plant thylakoid chloride channel VCCN1. Its long, intrinsically disordered C-terminus faces the stroma and carries two Rubisco-binding motifs that bind the alpha-helices of the Rubisco small subunit. The protein is found only in the pyrenoid tubules, the thylakoid-derived membranes that cross the Rubisco matrix of the pyrenoid, where it occupies a distinct radial subdomain. Its Rubisco-binding C-terminus is needed to concentrate it in the tubules. RBMP1 assembles in the membrane as pentamers, the typical bestrophin channel stoichiometry, and in larger complexes. It was first proposed to tether the Rubisco matrix to the tubules. However, mutants lacking the protein form normal pyrenoids with tubules and grow normally at air CO2, and the protein does not pull thylakoids into a reconstituted Rubisco condensate in Arabidopsis. Instead, the mutants have a transiently raised energy-dependent quenching during dark-to-light transitions, which points to a more acidic thylakoid lumen, and they grow poorly under fluctuating high light. The current view is therefore that RBMP1/BST4 is a pyrenoid tubule ion channel, possibly for bicarbonate, that helps to balance lumenal ion and proton levels during light changes. Its permeant ion has not been identified, since no currents were recorded in Xenopus oocytes.

Core Functions

Pentameric bestrophin-family ion channel in the thylakoid-derived pyrenoid tubule membrane. It is held in the tubules by Rubisco-binding motifs on its stroma-facing disordered C-terminus. It is proposed to move an anion, possibly bicarbonate, across the tubule membrane and so help balance lumenal ion and proton levels during dark-to-light transitions and fluctuating light. Its permeant ion has not been identified. Its Rubisco binding serves to target it, not to tether the matrix, because mutants keep a normal tubule-matrix interface.

RBMP1 / BST4 (Cre06.g261750; UniProt A0A2K3DMS8) notes

Identity

  • RBMP1 (Rubisco-binding membrane protein 1) and BST4 (bestrophin-like protein 4)
    are the same gene, Cre06.g261750. PMID:39240724
  • The name RBMP1 was coined by Meyer et al. 2020. PMID:33177094
  • UniProt A0A2K3DMS8 is an unreviewed TrEMBL entry ("Uncharacterized protein", 668 aa)
    with InterPro IPR044669 (YneE/VCCN1/2-like), Pfam PF25539 (Bestrophin_2) and PANTHER
    PTHR33281 (UPF0187 protein YneE). The UniProt "Cell membrane" location is an ARBA rule,
    not an observation. Disordered C-terminal regions 437-503 and 546-668 (MobiDB-lite).
  • Naming hazard for the CCM project: projects/CO2_CONCENTRATING_MECHANISMS.md lists
    RBMP1 and BST4 as two separate genes, and modules/pyrenoid_ccm.yaml cites the BST4
    preprint (PMID:38014171) as describing a separate candidate tether. They are one gene.

Domain architecture

  • N-terminal bestrophin channel domain (transmembrane) plus a long disordered C-terminus with
    two Rubisco-binding motifs (RBMs). PMID:39240724
  • Diverged from BST1-3: phenylalanine at the first position of the putative selectivity pore,
    and a separate clade. PMID:39240724
  • Meyer 2020 also called it a predicted bestrophin-family anion channel. PMID:33177094

Localization

  • Pyrenoid tubules in Chlamydomonas (fluorescent tagging, two independent groups).
    PMID:33177094
    PMID:39240724
  • Expansion microscopy places BST4 in a tubule subdomain at an intermediate radius, distinct
    from MITH1 and CAH3. PMID:42465278 (bioRxiv preprint)
  • In Arabidopsis expression, BST4 sits in stroma lamellae thylakoids with the C-terminus facing
    the stroma (protease protection). PMID:39240724
  • Tubule targeting depends on the RBM-bearing C-terminus. PMID:39240724

Rubisco binding

  • Original evidence: AP-MS (cited by Meyer as ref 18, the spatial interactome; the cached text of
    PMID:28938113 does not name Cre06.g261750, so the gene-level hit is in supplementary tables),
    plus the RBM itself. PMID:33177094
  • Y2H: the BST4 C-terminus interacts with RBCS1. WR-to-EE mutation of the RBMs abolishes the
    interaction. FRET shows BST4 and RBCS1 are close in vivo. PMID:39240724

Tether hypothesis: tested and not supported

  • Meyer 2020 model: RBMs on RBMP1/RBMP2 recruit Rubisco to the tubules. PMID:33177094
  • Adler et al. 2024 (peer-reviewed version of the 2023 preprint PMID:38014171):
  • bst4 pyrenoids are normal, with tubules. PMID:39240724
  • BST4 cannot pull thylakoids into an EPYC1-Rubisco condensate in Arabidopsis.
  • Conclusion: "BST4 is a pentameric transmembrane channel found within the pyrenoid tubules but is not crucial for Rubisco matrix tethering."
  • Possible redundancy with other RBM tubule proteins (RBMP2) is not excluded, but there is no
    positive evidence that RBMP1 tethers anything. So molecular adaptor activity (GO:0060090) is
    NOT justified. The Rubisco interaction looks like a targeting mechanism that holds the channel
    in the tubules.

Channel and physiology

  • Pentameric assembly by Slimfield single-molecule counting, an AlphaFold pentamer, and a
    ~1 MDa BN-PAGE complex. PMID:39240724
  • Xenopus oocytes gave no currents with KCl, NaHCO3, K-PEP or K-gluconate, including for
    C-terminal truncations. Permeant unknown. PMID:39240724
  • bst4: no growth defect at air CO2 under continuous light, but a growth defect under
    fluctuating high light. NPQ (qE) is transiently higher during dark-to-light transitions,
    which suggests a lower lumenal pH. PMID:39240724
  • Authors' model: BST4 is a tubule ion channel that contributes to lumen ion homeostasis/pH,
    possibly as an HCO3- channel. PMID:39240724

GOA assessment summary

  • IBA voltage-gated chloride channel activity (from AtVCCN1): the family is right, but chloride
    and voltage gating are not shown, and no Cl- current was detected. Generalize to GO:0005216.
  • IEA chloride transmembrane transport: comes from the IBA MF. Chloride is not shown, so mark
    it as over-annotated.
  • IBA photosynthesis, light reaction: consistent with the bst4 NPQ and fluctuating-light
    phenotypes. Accept.
  • IBA thylakoid membrane: consistent (the tubules are thylakoid-derived). Accept, and add pyrenoid
    tubule (GO:0160223) as NEW.
  • IEA plasma membrane (ARBA): contradicted by every localization study. Remove.
