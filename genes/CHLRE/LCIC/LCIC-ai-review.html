Gene Description

LCIC (low-CO2-inducible protein C; Cre06.g307500) is a soluble chloroplast stromal protein of Chlamydomonas reinhardtii and the closest paralog of LCIB. Both belong to the LCIB/LCIC family (Pfam PF18599), whose members fold like beta-carbonic anhydrases. LCIC is induced when CO2 is limiting and associates tightly with LCIB in a high-molecular-weight complex (about 350 to 440 kDa, roughly 1:1 LCIB:LCIC, probably hexameric). Under limiting CO2 in the light this complex gathers as puncta around the pyrenoid, outside the starch sheath, and it disperses through the stroma when CO2 is high or in the dark. The crystal structure of the LCIC core (residues 40-344) shows a beta-CA fold with one zinc ion per subunit, held by the canonical Cys-His-Cys triad (Cys119, His179, Cys203). The His and Arg residues that are essential for catalysis in active homologs are disordered. Recombinant LCIC, the recombinant LCIB-LCIC complex and the native complex purified from algal cells showed no carbonic anhydrase activity in vitro. LCIB, but not LCIC, has since been shown to complement carbonic anhydrase-deficient yeast and Arabidopsis mutants. So the catalytic contribution of LCIC is unresolved. LCIC is needed for LCIB to move to the pyrenoid periphery, and in an lcib mutant LCIC does not accumulate. The LCIB/LCIC complex is thought to recapture CO2 leaking from the pyrenoid as bicarbonate, or to act as a diffusion barrier. LCIC also interacts with the bestrophin-like proteins LCI11 and Cre16.g662600.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

LCIB-LCIC complex

Definition: A chloroplast stromal protein complex of the beta-carbonic-anhydrase-like proteins LCIB and LCIC, about 350 to 440 kDa and probably hexameric. Under limiting CO2 it relocates to the periphery of the pyrenoid. Found in green algae with a pyrenoid-based CO2-concentrating mechanism.

Justification: The complex is well established by Y2H, co-IP, gel filtration, co-expression and affinity-purification/MS. There is no GO complex term for it, so LCIC can only be placed in the generic GO:0032991.

Parent term: protein-containing complex

Core Functions

Non-catalytic (as far as measured) beta-CA-fold subunit of the stromal LCIB/LCIC complex. LCIC partners with LCIB in a high-molecular-weight, roughly 1:1 hetero-oligomer and is needed for the complex to relocate to puncta at the pyrenoid periphery under limiting CO2. The complex is proposed to recapture CO2 that leaks from the pyrenoid as bicarbonate, or to act as a CO2 diffusion barrier. LCIC binds zinc through a beta-CA Cys-His-Cys triad, but recombinant LCIC and the native LCIB-LCIC complex showed no carbonic anhydrase activity in vitro, so no molecular function is asserted for LCIC itself.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is LCIC a catalytic or a regulatory/structural subunit of the LCIB/LCIC complex? Should the module's carbonate dehydratase activity (GO:0004089) be asserted for LCIB alone, with LCIC recorded only as a complex member, until LCIC-containing complexes are shown to be active?

Suggested experts: Fukuzawa H, Yamano T, Mueller-Cajar O

Q: Does phosphorylation at LCIC Ser160, next to the disordered active-site His161, switch the complex between inactive and active states?

Q: What is the functional significance of the LCIC interaction with the bestrophin-like proteins LCI11 and Cre16.g662600? Is the LCIB/LCIC complex positioned next to the thylakoid bicarbonate channels?

Suggested experts: Mackinder LCM

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Test LCIC alone and LCIB+LCIC co-expression for complementation of the yeast nce103 and Arabidopsis beta-ca5 mutants, as already done for LCIB. Include zinc-ligand (C119A/H179A/C203A) LCIC variants, and measure CA activity of native complex purified from very-low-CO2 cells under reducing and phosphorylation-preserving conditions.

Hypothesis: LCIC contributes catalytic carbonic anhydrase activity to the LCIB/LCIC complex under the right conditions.

Type: genetic complementation and enzyme assay

Experiment: Generate an lcic null mutant (CRISPR or CLiP insertion) and measure growth at air and very low CO2, photosynthetic Ci affinity, and LCIB abundance and localization. Then complement with wild-type or zinc-site-mutant LCIC.

Hypothesis: LCIC is required for CCM function independently of its role in stabilizing and relocating LCIB.

Type: mutant phenotyping and complementation

    separately purified LCIB and LCIC does not reconstitute the complex (full text, not cached, PMC5187666).

Carbonic anhydrase activity: this is the key point for the module

  • Jin et al. 2016 full text (not cached): "CsLCIB, CrLCIB, CrLCIC, recombinant CrLCIB-LCIC complex, and
    native CrLCIB-LCIC complex were inactive under all conditions tested". Only PtLCIB3, PtLCIB4 and FjLCIB
    were active. The inactivity is attributed to disordering of His161 and Arg193 in LCIC. A Ser160
    phosphosite lies next to His161.
  • The cached full-text secondary sources restate this:
    PMID:28938113
    PMID:34791500.
  • Kasili et al. 2023 shows LCIB alone complements CA-deficient yeast (nce103) and Arabidopsis (beta-ca5)
    PMID:36856938.
    The abstract does not test LCIC.
  • Conclusion: there is no direct evidence that LCIC has carbonate dehydratase activity (GO:0004089), and
    in vitro assays of LCIC and the native complex were negative. I therefore did not assert GO:0004089 for
    LCIC, either as enables or as contributes_to. The module annoton lcib_lcic_ca assigns GO:0004089 to the
    complex. For LCIB that is now supportable from complementation data. For LCIC it is not supported. The
    module's own description already says CA activity "has not been robustly measured in vitro".
  • I also considered a NOT carbonate dehydratase activity (IDA) annotation, but did not add one. The
    authors suggest the activity may be regulated (conformational, by phosphorylation, or by complex
    dissociation), and the negative assays are in vitro only.

BP

  • GO has no "CO2-concentrating mechanism" process term. There is no lcic mutant phenotype, and necessity is
    confounded by LCIC loss in lcib mutants. No BP was proposed.

core_functions:
  - description: Non-catalytic (as far as measured) beta-CA-fold subunit of the
      stromal LCIB/LCIC complex. LCIC partners with LCIB in a high-molecular-weight,
      roughly 1:1 hetero-oligomer and is needed for the complex to relocate to
      puncta at the pyrenoid periphery under limiting CO2. The complex is
      proposed to recapture CO2 that leaks from the pyrenoid as bicarbonate, or
      to act as a CO2 diffusion barrier. LCIC binds zinc through a beta-CA
      Cys-His-Cys triad, but recombinant LCIC and the native LCIB-LCIC complex
      showed no carbonic anhydrase activity in vitro, so no molecular function
      is asserted for LCIC itself.
