Gene Description

BST2 (bestrophin-like protein 2; locus Cre16.g663400) is a nuclear-encoded, chloroplast-targeted multi-pass membrane protein of the bestrophin/YneE/VCCN family in the green alga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii. It is one of three closely related paralogs (BST1/Cre16.g662600, BST2 and BST3/Cre16.g663450; more than 80% identical to one another) clustered in a ~130 kb region of chromosome 16. Older literature used the name LCI11 for BST2 and/or BST3. Its nearest land-plant relative is the Arabidopsis thylakoid voltage-dependent chloride channel VCCN1 (about 30% identity). Homology modelling of the BST1-3 group places them in the pentameric bestrophin channel fold, with a positively charged selectivity pore consistent with an anion channel. BST2 transcripts rise within hours of transfer to air-level CO2; this induction is reduced, but less completely than for BST1 and BST3, in mutants lacking the CCM master regulator CIA5. BST2 is expressed more highly than BST1. Fluorescently tagged BST2 lies in the thylakoid membranes of the chloroplast, extends into the pyrenoid thylakoid tubules, and is concentrated near the pyrenoid periphery. In an affinity-purification interactome, BST2 was recovered as an interactor of both BST1 and BST3. RNAi lines in which all three paralogs are knocked down together grow poorly at limiting CO2, have lower affinity for inorganic carbon and accumulate less inorganic carbon, especially at alkaline pH where bicarbonate dominates; loss of BST3 alone has little effect, implying redundancy. BST1-3 are therefore proposed to be redundant thylakoid anion channels that move stromal bicarbonate into the thylakoid lumen, where carbonic anhydrase CAH3 converts it to CO2 for Rubisco in the pyrenoid. No BST2-specific mutant has been described, and bicarbonate (or any anion) conductance has not been measured for any of BST1-3.

Core Functions

Bestrophin-type anion channel, modelled as pentameric, in the chloroplast thylakoid membrane, acting redundantly with (and probably hetero-oligomerizing with) paralogs BST1 and BST3. It is proposed (from triple-knockdown physiology, location and family precedent, not from transport measurements) to conduct stromal bicarbonate into the thylakoid lumen for dehydration by CAH3 in the low-CO2 inducible CO2-concentrating mechanism.

  • Three paralogs clustered on chr 16 PMID:31391312.

  • 80% identical; name LCI11 historically used for BST2 and BST3 PMID:31391312. Note that the 2017 interactome uses LCI11 for Cre16.g663450 (BST3).

  • Closest land-plant homolog Arabidopsis VCCN1, ~30% identity PMID:31391312.
  • Predicted chloroplast transit peptide PMID:31391312.
  • Homology model (done for BST1, applies to the >80%-identical group) PMID:31391312.

BST2-specific data

  • Expression: induced in ambient CO2; CIA5 dependence partial PMID:31391312, whereas BST1 and BST3
    were severely reduced in cia5. Expressed above BST1 PMID:31391312.
  • Localization: Venus fusion (PSAD promoter) in thylakoid membranes, into pyrenoid tubules,
    enriched at pyrenoid periphery PMID:31391312. Native-promoter
    control was done only for BST3.
  • Interactome: BST2 is a prey of BST1 and BST3 baits PMID:28938113. LCIB/LCIC interactions were reported for BST3 and BST1 only
    PMID:28938113.
  • Phenotype: only via triple RNAi PMID:31391312; no BST2 single mutant.

Family-level physiology (shared with BST1)

  • Triple knockdown: poor low-CO2 growth, lower Ci affinity, reduced 14Ci accumulation
    PMID:31391312.
  • pmf effect opposite to Arabidopsis vccn1 PMID:31391312.
  • HCO3- delivery to CAH3 is a proposal PMID:31391312; no transport data for any of
    BST1-3 PMID:39240724.
