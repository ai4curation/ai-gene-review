Gene Description

RBMP2 (Rubisco-binding membrane protein 2; locus Cre09.g416850) is a large (1690 residues, about 165 kDa), nuclear-encoded membrane protein of the chloroplast pyrenoid in the green alga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii. It has an N-terminal region predicted to contact membranes, a central rhodanese-like domain flanked on both sides by predicted transmembrane helices, and a long disordered C-terminal region with six Rubisco-binding motifs, the last at the exact C-terminus. The rhodanese-like domain is non-catalytic: it contains no cysteine, and an aspartate occupies the position of the catalytic cysteine of sulfurtransferases and CDC25 phosphatases. RBMP2 resides in the pyrenoid tubules, the thylakoid-derived membranes that cross the Rubisco matrix, and is concentrated in their central reticulated region. It builds that region. In rbmp2 mutants the cylindrical tubules still enter the matrix but often end bluntly before the centre, the narrow reticulated network is absent, the lumenal carbonic anhydrase CAH3 is mislocalized to the matrix periphery, and growth at very low CO2 is impaired. Overexpression enlarges the reticulated region. Domain deletions separate two steps: the rhodanese-like domain is needed for tubules to extend to the centre, and the membrane-contact and transmembrane regions are needed to convert them into narrow reticulated tubules. The Rubisco-binding motifs bind Rubisco as peptides but are dispensable for forming the reticulated region. RBMP2 was first proposed to tether the Rubisco matrix to the tubules, but its demonstrated role is in shaping the tubule membranes that position CAH3 for CO2 release near Rubisco.

Core Functions

Builds the central reticulated region of the pyrenoid tubules. As an integral membrane protein of the tubules, RBMP2 is needed for the cylindrical tubules to extend to the pyrenoid centre (a step that needs its non-catalytic rhodanese domain) and for converting them into the narrow, minitubule-free reticulated network (a step that needs its membrane-contact and transmembrane regions). This network holds the carbonic anhydrase CAH3 near the middle of the Rubisco matrix. No molecular activity is asserted. Membrane bending by the membrane-contact region and a transmembrane hairpin is proposed but not tested, and the rhodanese domain lacks the catalytic cysteine. Its six Rubisco-binding motifs bind Rubisco as peptides but are not needed for the reticulated region, and a tether role is not established.

Suggested experts: Jonikas MC

Q: Do the six Rubisco-binding motifs of RBMP2 contribute to the tubule-matrix interface redundantly with other RBM proteins (RBMP1/BST4, SAGA1, MITH1)? An rbmp1 rbmp2 double mutant, or ΔRBM RBMP2 in a bst4 background, would test the remaining tether hypothesis.

Domain architecture

  • Wu et al. 2026: PMID:42427617
  • Six RBMs, consistent with a review. PMID:35961043
  • Local check (file:CHLRE/RBMP2/RBMP2-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md): six matches to the
    [D/N]W[R/K]XX[L/I/V/A] consensus at 1126, 1265, 1345, 1592, 1638 and 1685 (the last at the

Rhodanese domain: non-catalytic

  • The whole protein has a single cysteine (Cys498, in the low-complexity N-terminal half).
    The rhodanese domain (775-920) has none. A crude loop scan puts Asp870 (DSYGVE) in the
    position of the catalytic cysteine.
  • Wu et al.'s alignment agrees. PMID:42427617
  • Consequence: no sulfurtransferase (thiosulfate-cyanide sulfurtransferase) or
    CDC25-like phosphatase activity can be inferred. The InterPro rhodanese hit should not
    be turned into an MF annotation by any pipeline. Its role is structural or interaction
    based. PMID:42427617

Localization

  • Pyrenoid tubules, Venus fusion. PMID:33177094
  • Central reticulated region, unlike RBMP1. PMID:33177094
  • Confirmed with expansion microscopy, and the region expands on overexpression. PMID:42427617
  • PMID:42465278 (ExM of pyrenoid membranes) does not mention RBMP2. It shows CAH3
    concentrated in the reticulated region. PMID:42465278

  • Peptide arrays tiling RBMP2 were probed with Rubisco. PMID:33177094

  • GO has no Rubisco-binding MF term, and "protein binding" is uninformative, so no MF was

Tether hypothesis vs membrane shaping

  • Original model: RBMs on RBMP1/RBMP2 recruit Rubisco to the tubules. PMID:33177094
  • RBMP1/BST4 was later shown not to be needed for tubule-matrix contact. The paper raises
    redundancy in general terms but does not name RBMP2. PMID:39240724
  • Wu et al. 2026 (bioRxiv, not yet peer reviewed) is the first functional study of RBMP2:
  • Null phenotype: PMID:42427617
  • Rescue: PMID:42427617
  • Tubules still enter the matrix without RBMP2, so it is not needed for the
    tubule-matrix interface as such. PMID:42427617
  • Overexpression is sufficient to enlarge the region. PMID:42427617
  • Rhodanese domain: PMID:42427617
  • MCP and TM: PMID:42427617
  • RBMs dispensable: PMID:42427617
  • LCI16 binds (IP, AlphaFold model with the rhodanese domain) but is not needed. PMID:42427617
  • Conclusion: RBMP2 is a membrane-shaping factor for the CAH3-hosting reticulated region,
    not an established tether. A tether contribution by its RBMs, redundant with other RBM
    proteins, is still untested.

GO decisions

  • NEW CC GO:0160223 pyrenoid tubule (IDA, PMID:33177094; also PMID:42427617).
  • NEW BP GO:0010027 thylakoid membrane organization (IMP, PMID:42427617). This passes the
    participation test: RBMP2 is in the remodelled membrane, its MCP/TM regions are needed
    for the shape change, and overexpression enlarges the region. It matches the term used
    for SAGA1 and MITH1. GO:0097749 membrane tubulation was considered, but the tubules

    activity is excluded on sequence grounds.

Raw output: analysis_output.txt. Input: ../RBMP2-uniprot.txt (1690 aa).

1. Rhodanese domain has no cysteine

  • The whole 1690-residue protein has a single cysteine, Cys498, which lies in the
    Pro/Ala-rich low-complexity N-terminal half, far outside the rhodanese domain.
  • The PROSITE PS50206 rhodanese domain (775-920) contains no cysteine at all.
  • Active rhodanese domains (thiosulfate sulfurtransferases, CDC25 phosphatases) depend on
    a catalytic cysteine at the start of the active-site loop. A crude [C/D]xxGxx scan
    finds one candidate loop in the domain, D870-SYGVE, i.e. an aspartate where the
    cysteine would sit. This agrees with Wu et al. 2026 (PMID:42427617), whose alignment
    marks a "non-catalytic aspartic acid residue in RBMP2" at the catalytic-cysteine
    position and calls the domain "non-catalytic".
  • Conclusion: the domain cannot perform the persulfide (sulfurtransferase) or
    phosphocysteine (CDC25-type phosphatase) chemistry. Do not annotate
    thiosulfate-cyanide sulfurtransferase activity, sulfurtransferase activity or
    phosphatase activity.

2. Six Rubisco-binding motifs in the C-terminal half

Matches to the Meyer et al. 2020 consensus [D/N]W[R/K]XX[L/I/V/A] (PMID:33177094):
1126 NWRDVI, 1265 DWRQAA, 1345 NWRDQV, 1592 DWRREL, 1638 NWRQQV, and 1685 DWRARV at the
exact C-terminus (C-terminal residue Val, as Meyer et al. note). Six motifs, matching the
count given by Adler et al. 2022 (PMID:35961043) and Wu et al. 2026 (PMID:42427617).

3. Hydrophobic segments (Kyte-Doolittle, 19-aa window, mean >= 1.6)

  • 684-714 (max 2.47): strong candidate transmembrane helix, N-terminal to the rhodanese
    domain.
  • 961-981 (max 1.82): weaker candidate, C-terminal to the rhodanese domain.
  • 191-209 and 403-422 are Ala/Pro-rich low-complexity stretches that only just pass the
    threshold and are unlikely to be real membrane spans.

This crude screen agrees with the DeepTMHMM result reported by Wu et al. 2026
("predicted transmembrane helices (TM) on both sides of the RHO domain"). It is not a
topology prediction; the side of the membrane on which the rhodanese domain sits is not
established here.

Not done

No transit-peptide prediction (TargetP/PredAlgo not available offline). The N-terminus is
Ala-rich and Ser-rich, as is typical of Chlamydomonas chloroplast transit peptides, but
this is not asserted as a result.

core_functions:
  - description: Builds the central reticulated region of the pyrenoid tubules.
      As an integral membrane protein of the tubules, RBMP2 is needed for the
      cylindrical tubules to extend to the pyrenoid centre (a step that needs
      its non-catalytic rhodanese domain) and for converting them into the
      narrow, minitubule-free reticulated network (a step that needs its
      membrane-contact and transmembrane regions). This network holds the
      carbonic anhydrase CAH3 near the middle of the Rubisco matrix. No
      molecular activity is asserted. Membrane bending by the membrane-contact
      region and a transmembrane hairpin is proposed but not tested, and the
      rhodanese domain lacks the catalytic cysteine. Its six Rubisco-binding
      motifs bind Rubisco as peptides but are not needed for the reticulated
      region, and a tether role is not established.
