Gene Description

SAGA2 (StArch Granules Abnormal 2; locus Cre09.g394621) is a large (~190 kDa) nuclear-encoded, chloroplast-targeted pyrenoid protein of Chlamydomonas reinhardtii and a homologue of SAGA1 (about 30% identical). UniProt holds two TrEMBL entries for this locus, A0A2K3DEH1 (1,816 aa) and A0A2K3DEF8 (1,748 aa), which are alternative gene-model products of the same gene. SAGA2 has two consecutive N-terminal CBM20 starch-binding domains, a long coiled-coil middle region and extended disordered segments that carry several copies of the pyrenoid Rubisco-binding motif. Rubisco binds SAGA2 motif peptides in vitro, and SAGA2 binds the Rubisco large and small subunits and the linker EPYC1 in yeast two-hybrid assays. A purified construct of its two CBM20 domains binds starch granules and the starch precursor maltoheptaose and the mimic beta-cyclodextrin in vitro. In the cell SAGA2 forms a fairly uniform shell at the interface between the Rubisco matrix and the starch sheath, and it is depleted from the gaps where thylakoid tubules enter the matrix, which are occupied by SAGA1. The saga2 single mutant grows normally at limiting CO2, has a single pyrenoid with normal tubules, and shows reduced starch coverage of the matrix early in pyrenoid formation that recovers later. SAGA2 acts redundantly with SAGA1 to place the starch sheath on the pyrenoid. The saga1 saga2 double mutant lacks a starch sheath and its starch is spread through the stroma, and either protein restores the sheath. Expressed in Arabidopsis with an EPYC1-Rubisco condensate, SAGA2 increases condensate-associated starch. SAGA2 is proposed to tether starch granules to the matrix and perhaps to prime granule initiation at the matrix surface. It does not take part in forming the pyrenoid tubules.

Core Functions

Links the Rubisco matrix to the starch sheath at the pyrenoid periphery. The two CBM20 domains bind starch and starch precursors, and the Rubisco-binding motifs bind the matrix. SAGA2 covers the matrix surface between tubule entry sites and, working redundantly with SAGA1, places the starch sheath around the pyrenoid, especially early in pyrenoid formation.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should GO add a process term for starch sheath assembly or localization at the pyrenoid (for example "pyrenoid starch sheath assembly"), so that SAGA1 and SAGA2 can be annotated to the process they carry out? The closest existing term, GO:0033037 polysaccharide localization, is used almost only for glycosaminoglycans.

Suggested experts: Jonikas MC

Q: Do SAGA1 and SAGA2 prime starch granule initiation at the matrix surface (GO:0062052 starch granule initiation), as their maltoheptaose binding and the stromal starch in the double mutant suggest, or do they only capture and tether granules made elsewhere?

Q: Which gene model is correct for Cre09.g394621? UniProt has A0A2K3DEH1 (1,816 aa) and A0A2K3DEF8 (1,748 aa) for the same locus, and published papers describe a ~190 kDa protein with four Rubisco-binding motifs.

Q: What is the in vivo role of the SAGA2-EPYC1 interaction seen in yeast two-hybrid, given that EPYC1 binds SAGA2 through the same helices that bind Rubisco?

Decisions

  1. IBA vacuolar transport and membrane (PTN000386078, STBD1 family): REMOVE. The
    node is seeded by mammalian STBD1 (ER-anchored, glycophagy). SAGA2 shares only the CBM20,
    has no membrane anchor, is chloroplast-targeted and acts at the matrix-starch interface,
    away from membranes. Target-specific evidence of divergence, recorded in propagation_review.
  2. IEA carbohydrate binding, starch binding: ACCEPT. Confirmed by the direct binding
    assay (domain-level). Consistent with SAGA1.
  3. NEW pyrenoid (GO:1990732) IDA from SAGA2-Venus localization.
  4. No process term. GO has no starch sheath assembly/localization term. GO:0033037
    polysaccharide localization exists but QuickGO shows only 2 annotations (Chst11,
    glycosaminoglycan), so it is not an established home for starch tethers. Raised as a
    suggested question, consistent with the SAGA1 review. Tubule terms (GO:0160223,
    GO:0010027) are explicitly not applicable to SAGA2.
