Gene Description

HLA3 (High Light Activated 3, also CrMRP1; locus Cre02.g097800) is a full-size ATP-binding cassette transporter of the ABCC (multidrug resistance-associated protein, MRP) subfamily in the green alga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii. It has two transmembrane domains and two nucleotide-binding domains, with one consensus and one degenerate ATP site as is typical of ABCC proteins. HLA3 is one of seven MRP-type genes in the genome but the only one induced by limiting CO2 under control of the CCM master regulator CIA5, and its protein accumulates within hours of transfer to very low CO2. Immunofluorescence, plasma membrane fractionation and fluorescent-protein tagging place HLA3 in the plasma membrane. Loss of HLA3 (RNAi knockdown or insertional mutation) reduces photosynthetic inorganic carbon affinity and intracellular inorganic carbon accumulation, mainly at alkaline pH where bicarbonate is the dominant species, and the defect is strongly enhanced by loss of the chloroplast envelope protein LCIA or the stromal protein LCIB. Ectopic expression raises cellular inorganic carbon accumulation, most strongly when co-expressed with LCIA, and HLA3 expressed in Xenopus oocytes increases bicarbonate uptake. HLA3 therefore supplies external bicarbonate across the plasma membrane as the first step of the algal CO2-concentrating mechanism, acting with LCIA, which moves bicarbonate on into the chloroplast stroma. Whether HLA3 transports bicarbonate itself by an ATP-driven mechanism has not been shown biochemically. In the spatial interactome HLA3 associates with the plasma membrane protein LCI1 and the P-type ATPase ACA4.

Core Functions

Plasma membrane ABC-family (ABCC/MRP-type) transporter that mediates uptake of external bicarbonate into the cytosol under very low CO2, supplying the substrate that LCIA moves into the chloroplast stroma in the CO2-concentrating mechanism. ATP coupling is inferred from the conserved NBDs but not demonstrated.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express wild-type HLA3 and Walker B (E to Q in NBD2) or Walker A (K to M) mutants in Xenopus oocytes or yeast and measure H14CO3- uptake, ATP dependence (vanadate, ATP depletion) and CO2 versus bicarbonate preference; complement the Hin-1 mutant with the same variants.

Hypothesis: HLA3 transports bicarbonate by an ATP-hydrolysis-coupled mechanism through its consensus ATP site.

Type: heterologous transport assay and genetic complementation

Experiment: Purify HLA3 in detergent or nanodiscs, measure basal and bicarbonate-stimulated ATPase activity, and reconstitute into proteoliposomes for vectorial bicarbonate transport.

Hypothesis: Purified HLA3 has bicarbonate-stimulated ATPase activity.

Type: biochemistry

Sequence / domain features (from HLA3-uniprot.txt, plus a quick motif check)

  • Full-size ABCC/MRP architecture: TMD1 (166-384), NBD1 (418-639), TMD2 (711-1003),
    NBD2 (1062-1297); PANTHER PTHR24223:SF443 (ABCC subfamily), CDD ABC_6TM_ABCC_D1/D2 and
    ABCC_MRP_domain1/2; ~12+ predicted TM helices (Phobius). There is no N-terminal TMD0
    in InterPro/CDD, so it resembles the "short" MRPs (MRP4/5-like) rather than MRP1.
  • Motif inspection of the UniProt sequence (done inline, not a deposited analysis):
    NBD1 Walker A GRIAAGKS, NBD1 Walker B LVLLDN (catalytic Glu replaced by Asn),
    NBD1 signature FSGGQ (canonical); NBD2 Walker A GRTGSGKST, NBD2 Walker B ILCLDE
    (canonical), NBD2 signature SLGQ (degenerate). This is the asymmetric
    one-consensus / one-degenerate ATP-site arrangement typical of ABCC proteins; the
    consensus site (NBD2 Walker A/B + NBD1 signature) is intact, so ATP binding and
    hydrolysis are expected.

Expression and regulation

  • Limiting-CO2 inducible; only one of seven Chlamydomonas MRP-type genes controlled by
    CIA5 PMID:19321421.
  • In the CIA5/CO2 RNA-seq study HLA3 falls in CCM cluster 15 with LCIA, LCI1 and CCP1
    PMID:22634760.
  • HLA3 mRNA is reduced in LCIA insertion mutants; LCIA is unaffected in the HLA3 mutant,
    suggesting a chloroplast-to-nucleus signal PMID:26015566.

Localization

  • Originally only predicted (WoLF PSORT) to be plasma membrane PMID:19321421.
  • Direct: anti-HLA3 immunofluorescence at the cell periphery and enrichment in purified
    plasma membrane fractions [PMID:26015566 "a notable enrichment of HLA3 was observed in the
    PM fraction"; "we concluded that HLA3 and LCIA were localized to the PM and CE,
    respectively"].
  • Venus-tagged HLA3 at the plasma membrane in Chlamydomonas, and GFP-tagged HLA3 at the
    plasma membrane in tobacco leaf cells PMID:26538195.
  • UniProt ARBA (ARBA00004128) and the GO:0005774 vacuolar membrane IEA are therefore
    contradicted by direct data (the rule is a family-level transfer from plant vacuolar
    ABCC/MRP glutathione-conjugate pumps).

Function

  • RNAi knockdown (2 constructs): HLA3 knockdown alone gives modest, high-pH-specific loss of
    Ci affinity and Ci uptake in very-low CO2; combined with LCIB mutation or off-target LCIA
    co-knockdown, dramatic loss of growth and Ci uptake at pH 9 PMID:19321421. The authors
    explicitly leave open direct vs. indirect action PMID:19321421.
  • dTALE activation of HLA3 in high-CO2 cells increased Ci accumulation and Ci-dependent O2
    evolution at very low CO2 [PMID:25660294 abstract: "confirms that HLA3 is indeed involved
    in Ci uptake, and suggests it is mainly associated with HCO3(-) transport"]. Abstract only
    in cache.
  • Insertion mutant Hin-1 (complemented by Hin-1C): reduced Ci affinity at pH 9 and lower
    [14C]-Ci accumulation PMID:26015566. HLA3/LCIA double mutant additive. Single HLA3
    overexpression in HC gave only a small Ci accumulation increase; co-overexpression with LCIA
    significantly raised Ci affinity/accumulation PMID:26015566.
  • Heterologous: HLA3 (untagged or GFP-tagged) at the oocyte surface raised H14CO3- uptake
    2.7-fold over water-injected controls PMID:26538195. The
    authors caution the mode (active vs passive) is unresolved PMID:26538195. This is the most direct evidence that HLA3 itself moves
    bicarbonate across a membrane; no ATP-dependence, substrate specificity or purified
    protein data exist.
  • Interactome: HLA3 co-purifies reciprocally with LCI1 and with the P-type ATPase ACA4,
    plus a CaM kinase and CYG63 cyclase as high-confidence interactors PMID:28938113. Not a GO
    complex; recorded as a question only. Blue-native PAGE shows HLA3 in a ~580 kDa complex
    independent of LCIA PMID:26015566.

  • MF: GO:0015106 bicarbonate transmembrane transporter activity (NEW; oocyte uptake +
    in vivo genetics), keep IBA GO:0140359 ABC-type transporter activity; ATP binding and ATP
    hydrolysis IEA accepted (consensus ATP site intact).
  • CC: GO:0005886 plasma membrane (NEW; IDA). REMOVE GO:0005774 vacuolar membrane (IEA, ARBA)
    — contradicted.
  • BP: GO:0015701 bicarbonate transport (NEW); keep transmembrane transport IBA/IEA.
  • Did not add a CCM-level or "response to CO2" process: induction by low CO2 is regulation of
    the gene, not activity of the product.
