Gene Description

NrtC is the ATP-binding component with a C-terminal regulatory extension of the high-affinity NrtABCD nitrate/nitrite transporter in Synechococcus elongatus PCC 7942. Its N-terminal ABC domain participates in energy coupling at the cytoplasmic membrane, while its NrtA-like C-terminal extension is required for normal ammonium-promoted inhibition of transport. The extension is dispensable for basal nitrate/nitrite uptake.

Core Functions

Contributes the ATP-binding component with a C-terminal regulatory extension to ATP-coupled nitrate/nitrite uptake for nitrogen assimilation. The C-terminal extension mediates ammonium-responsive inhibition; domain deletion preserves basal transport.
