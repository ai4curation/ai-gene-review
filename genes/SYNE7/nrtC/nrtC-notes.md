# nrtC review notes

## 2026-10-27: PCC 7942 native-system review

Reviewed all 7 seeded annotation rows for P38045, NCBITaxon:1140 (PCC 7942). Original annotation fields and both raw files were snapshotted before editing and retained. No NEW rows were introduced.

NrtC is the ATP-binding component with a C-terminal regulatory extension of the high-affinity NrtABCD nitrate/nitrite transporter in Synechococcus elongatus PCC 7942. Its N-terminal ABC domain participates in energy coupling at the cytoplasmic membrane, while its NrtA-like C-terminal extension is required for normal ammonium-promoted inhibition of transport. The extension is dispensable for basal nitrate/nitrite uptake.

Annotation decisions:

- GO:0005524 ATP binding: ACCEPT. The target ABC nucleotide-binding domain and the primary sequence comparison support ATP binding. This is the energy-coupling portion of the transporter; ATP binding is distinct from ATP hydrolysis and is not being removed as a duplicate (PMID:8437564).
- GO:0005886 plasma membrane: ACCEPT. NrtB is the integral membrane component; the ATP-binding components NrtC and NrtD act on the cytoplasmic side of this membrane. Native NrtC immunoblots and domain-deletion experiments directly support membrane association (PMID:9341163).
- GO:0015112 nitrate transmembrane transporter activity: MODIFY. nrtC contributes its ATP-binding component with a C-terminal regulatory extension role to the ATP-coupled NrtABCD complex (PMID:8437564, PMID:9341163). ABC-type nitrate transporter activity is the supported descendant of nitrate transmembrane transporter activity. The complex-level annotation is evaluated as a component contribution, not a claim that this subunit alone transports nitrate.
- GO:0015414 ABC-type nitrate transporter activity: ACCEPT. The native NrtABCD system is an ABC transporter for nitrate and nitrite. nrtC contains the ATP-binding machinery and genetically contributes to uptake; this is a complex contribution rather than autonomous transport by a soluble ATPase (PMID:8437564, PMID:9341163).
- GO:0015706 nitrate transmembrane transport: ACCEPT. Native transporter-gene disruption and uptake measurements establish participation in high-affinity nitrate transport, with subunit roles resolved by sequence and domain studies. Nitrate transport remains a useful process assertion alongside the complex activity (PMID:8437564, PMID:9341163).
- GO:0016020 membrane: MODIFY. The evidence localizes the transporter to the cytoplasmic membrane. The parent membrane term is biologically true but can be refined to plasma membrane, already represented in this review.
- GO:0016887 ATP hydrolysis activity: ACCEPT. NrtC and NrtD are the conserved ABC energy-coupling components supported by native transport genetics and nucleotide-binding-domain architecture. ATPase activity is retained as the subunit-level activity; the cited native studies do not separately measure purified nrtC ATPase kinetics. ABC-type nitrate transporter activity is not an is_a descendant of ATP hydrolysis activity in the checked ontology.

Full-text methods identify R2-SPc, a PCC 7942 derivative cured of pUH24. The study measures nitrate transport separately from reduction using narB mutants; residual nitrite utilization is not automatically attributed to NrtABCD because an additional nitrite-specific uptake system exists. Direct ammonium binding, phosphorylation mechanism and a regulatory role for NrtD are not established by these NrtC deletion data.
