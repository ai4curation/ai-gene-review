Gene Description

NarB is the assimilatory ferredoxin-dependent nitrate reductase of Synechococcus elongatus PCC 7942. It transfers electrons from reduced ferredoxin through a [4Fe-4S] cluster to a molybdopterin-containing molybdenum center, reducing nitrate to nitrite in the cytoplasm. Nitrite is subsequently reduced to ammonium by NirA for incorporation into cellular nitrogen.
