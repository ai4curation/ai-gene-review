## 2026-10-27: PCC 7942 native-system review

NarB is the assimilatory ferredoxin-dependent nitrate reductase of Synechococcus elongatus PCC 7942. It transfers electrons from reduced ferredoxin through a [4Fe-4S] cluster to a molybdopterin-containing molybdenum center, reducing nitrate to nitrite in the cytoplasm. Nitrite is subsequently reduced to ammonium by NirA for incorporation into cellular nitrogen.

One normal Falcon deep-research attempt with perplexity-lite fallback failed; no synthetic provider file was created. Manual primary-source review supplies the evidence below. Normal acquisition logs are in `.cache/module-audit-logs/nitrate-syne7-review/`.

Family grounding: target UniProt membership is PTHR43105:SF9, official PANTHER label **NADPH-FE(3+) OXIDOREDUCTASE SUBUNIT ALPHA**, checked against `interpro/panther/panther.obo`. Broad family labels do not determine substrate specificity; a chloroplastic family name does not place a cyanobacterial protein in a chloroplast.

  PCC 7942 NarB catalyzes nitrate reduction with reduced ferredoxin or artificial methyl viologen. Supporting text: "either reduced methyl viologen or reduced ferredoxin could be used as an electron donor for the reduction of nitrate"
- [PMID:15166246 Tuning a nitrate reductase for function. Abstract read; publisher PDF retrieval returned 403. Metal/sulfide stoichiometry and EPR support the bound cofactors; the enzyme is an assimilatory cytoplasmic reductase.
  NarB contains approximately four Fe, four acid-labile sulfides and one Mo per molecule. Supporting text: "Metal and acid-labile sulfide analysis revealed nearest integer values of 4:4:1 (iron/sulfur/molybdenum)/molecule of NarB."

  PCC 7942 NarB contains a molybdopterin Mo center and a [4Fe-4S] cluster. Supporting text: "one bis-molybdopterin guanine dinucleotide (MGD) Mo center and one [4Fe-4S]2+,+ cluster as prosthetic groups."
  The experimental enzyme/donor pair combines PCC 7942 NarB with PCC 6803 ferredoxin. Supporting text: "Both recombinant wild-type Fd from the cyanobacterium Synechocystis sp. PCC 6803 and recombinant, C-terminally Histagged Synechococcus sp. PCC 7942 NR were expressed in Escherichia coli"

  The C-terminal extension of NrtC participates in ammonium-promoted inhibition. Supporting text: "the C-terminal domain of NrtC is involved in the ammonium-promoted inhibition of the nitrate/nitrite transporter."

Annotation decisions:

- GO:0005515 protein binding: MODIFY. The original IPI paper (PMID:14871493) measures a functional ferredoxin complex and ferredoxin-dependent nitrate-to-nitrite catalysis, not merely an uninterpreted interaction. GO:0047889 captures the experimentally supported activity. The inherited spinach interactor is preserved; the abstract also specifies Anabaena PCC 7119 ferredoxin variants, and full-text PMID:28520412 uses PCC 6803 ferredoxin with PCC 7942 NarB. These experiments do not identify an exclusively native donor pair.
- GO:0016020 membrane: UNDECIDED. The proximate source is TreeGrafter PANTHER:PTN000177404. Target spectroscopy and enzymology describe a cytoplasmic assimilatory ferredoxin-dependent enzyme, not a membrane quinone-linked respiratory reductase (PMID:15166246, PMID:28520412). However, soluble enzyme biochemistry alone does not exclude peripheral membrane association, and the node-specific localization evidence was not recovered. Preserve the inherited assertion while leaving its validity unresolved; do not infer a false membrane location solely from the broad nitrate-reductase family.
- GO:0016491 oxidoreductase activity: MODIFY. The broad redox domains support oxidoreductase chemistry, but direct expression and catalysis establish the substrate and ferredoxin donor (PMID:8624415, PMID:14871493). Refine to GO:0047889 rather than using an unspecific redox function.
- GO:0019333 denitrification pathway: REMOVE. UniPathway:UPA00652 propagates a respiratory denitrification assignment to an assimilatory cyanobacterial nitrate reductase. Native physiology and target enzyme studies place NarB in the nitrate-to-nitrite step feeding NirA and nitrogen assimilation, not production of gaseous nitrogen oxides or dinitrogen. The shared nitrate substrate does not justify assigning this protein to denitrification (PMID:8624415, PMID:15166246, PMID:9341163).
- GO:0043546 molybdopterin cofactor binding: ACCEPT. Target metal analysis and Mo spectroscopy, together with the full-text description of the MGD-containing active center, independently support this cofactor-binding annotation (PMID:15166246, PMID:28520412). It is a catalytic cofactor, not an inference from the erroneous quinone EC assignment.
- GO:0051539 4 iron, 4 sulfur cluster binding: ACCEPT. Approximately four Fe and four acid-labile sulfides per protein and the reduced-cluster EPR signal directly support a [4Fe-4S] cluster in PCC 7942 NarB (PMID:15166246).
- GO:0160182 nitrate reductase (quinone) activity: MODIFY. The inherited RHEA:56144/EC:1.7.5.1 mapping specifies quinone-dependent nitrate reduction, whereas expression of the PCC 7942 gene and subsequent donor-binding/catalysis studies establish ferredoxin-dependent nitrate reduction. Replace with GO:0047889 as a donor correction, not an ancestor refinement (PMID:8624415, PMID:14871493, PMID:28520412). The raw UniProt reaction and original GOA fields remain unchanged.

The source quinone reaction/EC and denitrification pathway should be corrected upstream. The soluble cytoplasmic enzyme evidence does not by itself exclude transient membrane binding; the TreeGrafter location remains UNDECIDED. PMID:28520412 uses heterologous PCC 6803 ferredoxin. Residue numbering of N-terminally truncated recombinant constructs was not transferred to full-length UniProt coordinates.
