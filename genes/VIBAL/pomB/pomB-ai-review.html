Gene Description

PomB is the B subunit of the sodium-driven polar flagellar stator of Vibrio alginolyticus. Its N-terminal membrane helix contributes to the PomAB ion pathway, including the essential Asp24, while its periplasmic plug and peptidoglycan-binding region regulate and anchor the stator. Two PomB subunits associate with five PomA subunits. Sodium transport and torque generation are properties of the assembled complex, not autonomous PomB.

pomB research notes

2026-11-06 comparative flagellar audit

Current UniProt O06874 is a species-level V.alginolyticus record, not an accession silently renamed to a particular modern strain. Its complete sequence matches RCSB 8ZYW; PomB has only an addedC-terminalHis6. The SIFTS alternativeA0A7H8DMV8 differs at residue 132 and was rejected as an exact match. PMID 39793043 uses recombinant BL21(DE3)/pLysS production and separately functional V.alginolyticus assays. PMID 18326582 distinguishes native NMB191ΔpomAB/VIO5 from an E.coli hybrid. PMID 37500658 takes DNA fromATCC17749, inserts a cleavage site inPomB for some structural preparations, and tests VaPotAB hybrid function inSalmonellaLT2; those hybrid results are not a nativeVibrio assay. No NEW biological-process row is needed here. GO 0120101 definition requires a stoichiometry update; no incorrect A4B2 count is adopted.

Default Falcon was attempted once and returned HTTP403 authentication failure; the genuine command log is retained in the ignored comparative-panel audit directory. No provider report was fabricated.

All original annotation payloads, including any inherited NEW proposals, are preserved. Only reviewer-authored judgments and synthesis were updated. Identity bridges and construct limits are recorded under analysis/module_audits/bacterial_flagellar_assembly_export/comparative-panel/.
