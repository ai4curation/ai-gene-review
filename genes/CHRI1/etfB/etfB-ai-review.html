Gene Description

EtfB (Csal_0993) is the beta subunit of an electron-transfer flavoprotein (ETF) heterodimer of the halophilic gammaproteobacterium Chromohalobacter salexigens. It belongs to the ETF beta-subunit/FixA family; by similarity to characterized ETFs it binds the structural AMP of the heterodimer, while the FAD sits mainly on the alpha subunit EtfA. etfB is the last gene of a locus with ddhC (N,N-dimethylglycine/sarcosine dehydrogenase), the membrane-anchored ferredoxin gene Csal_0991 and etfA, and its deletion abolishes growth on dimethylglycine as sole nitrogen source while growth on sarcosine and glycine is retained. EtfA-EtfB is therefore thought to carry electrons generated during dimethylglycine conversion to sarcosine to an unidentified respiratory acceptor.

Core Functions

Beta subunit of the EtfA-EtfB heterodimer encoded in the ddhC-Csal_0991-etfA-etfB dimethylglycine degradation locus. It contributes to the electron-carrier activity of the heterodimer, whose FAD is bound mainly by EtfA; donor and acceptor have not been shown biochemically.
