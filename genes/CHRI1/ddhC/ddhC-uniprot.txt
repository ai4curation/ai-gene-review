CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in degradation of glycine betaine (PubMed:32631860).
CC       Catalyzes the demethylation of both N,N-dimethylglycine (DMG) and
CC       sarcosine, releasing formaldehyde and forming glycine as the final
CC       product (PubMed:32631860). Does not show activity toward trimethylamine
CC       (TMA), histamine, glycine betaine (GB) or choline (PubMed:22675587).
CC       The C-N bond in DMG is probably oxidized by removal of a hydride
CC       equivalent to form a labile imine intermediate, which is then
CC       spontaneously hydrolyzed in the presence of water, producing sarcosine
CC       and formaldehyde (PubMed:32631860). The two protons subtracted from DMG
CC       are transferred to the non-covalently bound FAD, resulting in the
CC       reduced form of FAD, which is subsequently reoxidized by coupling with
CC       reduction of the enzyme-bound NAD(P)(+) (PubMed:32631860). Regeneration
CC       of NAD(P)(+) is achieved by electron transfer to the [4Fe-4S] cluster
CC       in the probable membrane-anchored ferredoxin csal_0991
CC       (PubMed:32631860). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22675587,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32631860}.
