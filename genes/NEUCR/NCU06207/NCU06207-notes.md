# N. crassa NCU06207 notes

- NCU06207 is the N. crassa PTHR11863 Erg3-family member and inherits the fungal ERG3 Delta7-sterol C-5 desaturase, ER-membrane and ergosterol-biosynthesis PAINT rows.
- No N. crassa-specific Erg3 deletion, purified-enzyme or localization publication was found in the cached references or in a 2024-2026 literature search; the review therefore relies on the Swiss-Prot by-similarity record and PAINT inheritance from the yeast, Candida and animal SC5D C-5 desaturase clade.
