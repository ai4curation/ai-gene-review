Gene Description

erg-4 (NCU03006) encodes the Neurospora crassa sterol C-24 methyltransferase of the Erg6/SMT family. It is a class I-like S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent methyltransferase required for ergosterol biosynthesis; Neurospora erg-4 mutants are blocked at the C-24 methylation step and fail to make ergosterol. Erg-4 transfers a methyl group from S-adenosyl-L-methionine to lanosterol to form eburicol, placing the enzyme early in the filamentous-fungal route from lanosterol to ergosterol and distinguishing it from budding-yeast Erg6, whose curated substrate is zymosterol.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

lanosterol 24-C-methyltransferase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: lanosterol + S-adenosyl-L-methionine = eburicol + S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine + H(+).

Justification: Neurospora Erg-4 catalyzes the RHEA:52652 lanosterol-to-eburicol reaction. GO:0003838 sterol 24-C-methyltransferase activity is the right parent but is currently defined as the budding-yeast zymosterol-to-fecosterol reaction RHEA:21128.

Parent term: sterol 24-C-methyltransferase activity

Mappings:

    [UniProt:Q9P3R1,
    "Reaction=lanosterol + S-adenosyl-L-methionine = eburicol + S-adenosyl-"].
  • Q9P3R1 is a reviewed PTHR44068:SF1 member alongside S. cerevisiae ERG6,
    S. pombe erg6, C. albicans ERG6, A. fumigatus erg6, and additional fungal
    sterol C-24 methyltransferases. The PAINT activity/localization node
    PTN002226525 and fungal process node PTN000802684 therefore cover the
    Neurospora enzyme. GO:0003838 is currently defined as the RHEA:21128
    zymosterol -> fecosterol reaction, but PAINT uses it for the broader sterol
    C-24 methyltransferase activity class, so this review keeps GO:0003838 and
    proposes a lanosterol-specific child term for the Neurospora core function.
