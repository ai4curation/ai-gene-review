Gene Description

apg-6 encodes the single Neurospora crassa ATG8-family ubiquitin-like modifier. The protein is proteolytically matured and conjugated to phosphatidylethanolamine on phagophore and autophagosome membranes, where the conserved Atg8 lipidation cycle supports autophagosome assembly and maturation and provides the Atg8 platform used by selective autophagy pathways.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is Neurospora crassa APG-6 detectably processed, PE-lipidated, and recruited to phagophore or autophagosome membranes under starvation or selective-autophagy conditions, or is its ATG8 activity still only inferred from conserved fungal homologs?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Tag endogenous APG-6, assay starvation-dependent puncta and vacuolar delivery, and compare wild-type APG-6 with a C-terminal glycine or Atg4 processing-site mutant by lipidation gel shift and autophagy-flux readouts.

Hypothesis: Neurospora APG-6 is proteolytically processed at its conserved C-terminal glycine, PE-lipidated, and recruited to autophagosomal membranes during starvation-induced autophagy.

Type: endogenous tagging and autophagy-flux assay

Experiment: Measure intracolonial hyphal fusion in mutants for other core autophagy genes and in an APG-6 lipidation-site mutant, then test whether restoration of lipidatable APG-6 rescues both fusion and autophagy-flux phenotypes.

Hypothesis: The atg-8 intracolonial hyphal-fusion phenotype is a downstream consequence of impaired Atg8 lipidation-dependent autophagy rather than a direct APG-6 role in plasma-membrane fusion.

Type: genetic complementation and phenotyping

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: Direct APG-6 processing, PE lipidation, and phagophore or autophagosome recruitment have not been experimentally demonstrated in N. crassa.

OPEN BIOLOGY RESIDUAL_SUBGAP

What is known: Q8WZY7 is a canonical PTHR10969 ATG8-family protein, but all current APG-6 molecular-function, process, and localization annotations are PAINT, UniProt SubCell, or similarity inferences from experimentally characterized ATG8-family proteins in other species.

Significance: Direct lipidation and localization evidence would establish whether the filamentous-fungal protein carries the conserved biochemical ATG8 activity that is currently inferred for every GOA row.

What would resolve it: Endogenous APG-6 lipidation, localization, and autophagy-flux assays in wild-type and lipidation-site mutant Neurospora strains.

apg-6 / ATG8 notes

2026-10-21

  • Reviewed the PTHR10969 PAINT rows for the cross-fungal ATG8 family.
    PTN000103522 is the eukaryotic ATG8 node that propagates autophagosome
    membrane, phosphatidylethanolamine binding, autophagosome assembly, mitophagy,
    cellular response to nitrogen starvation, and autophagosome maturation to the
    Neurospora crassa member; PTN000861115 is the Fungi-level node that
    propagates fungal-type vacuole membrane from curated SGD and PomBase seeds.
  • N. crassa APG-6 is a single-copy member of the fungal ATG8 branch in the
    cached PANTHER entries table and UniProt classifies Q8WZY7 in PTHR10969.
    I did not find evidence that the Neurospora protein has diverged from the
    conserved ATG8 lipidation cycle.
  • The direct N. crassa literature hit for atg-8/NCU01545 was the Fu et al.
    deletion-library screen PMID:21666072, which reported that atg-8
    mutants were among strains "apparently required for intracolonial hyphal cell
    fusion without being essential for CAT formation" and that "the phenotypes of
    the ham-4 (NCU00528), atg-8 (NCU01545), nox-1 (NCU02110), ro-11 (NCU08566),
    and ro-6 (NCU9142) mutants have been verified by complementation and RIP
    experiments". This supports an apg-6 loss phenotype but does not establish a
    direct APG-6 activity in cell fusion, so I did not add a cell-fusion GO term.
  • The UniProt record also transfers by-similarity nucleophagy, ER-specific
    autophagy/survival, early secretory-pathway membrane fusion, and Atg8-PE
    tethering/hemifusion statements from S. cerevisiae. These statements were not
    promoted to new N. crassa annotations because the organism-specific evidence
    is absent and the current GOA/PAINT rows already cover the conserved
    autophagosome-membrane function.
  • PubMed/open-web searches for Neurospora crassa apg-6 Atg8 autophagy NCU01545 and 2024-2026 ATG8 papers found NCBI/FungiDB-style gene pages and
    papers that use NCU01545 as an ortholog in comparative figures, but no newer
    primary N. crassa APG-6 biochemical or localization study.
  • Kept the mitophagy and nitrogen-starvation response IBAs as non-core:
    selective autophagy and starvation autophagy use the conserved ATG8 platform,
    but APG-6's direct activity is phosphatidylethanolamine-conjugated
    autophagosome membrane modification.
  • Marked the two generic vacuolar membrane rows as too general because the
    same review accepts the fungal-type vacuole membrane IBA from the
    Fungi-level ATG8 PAINT node.
