# NCU02305 notes

## 2026-11-23 PTHR12001 review

- `NCU02305` is UniProt `Q7S565`, a predicted mitochondrial CoQ-side-chain trans-prenyl diphosphate synthase in `PTHR12001:SF69`.
- PubMed searches for `NCU02305`, Neurospora `COQ1`, `hexaprenyl`, and `ubiquinone` found broad Neurospora mitochondria and ubiquinone papers but no direct NCU02305 product-chain-length or complex-architecture study.
- The EnsemblFungi decaprenyl-diphosphate synthase and heterotetrameric-complex transfers use S. pombe dps1 as a source. Those annotations are too specific for NCU02305 because SF69 mixes several chain lengths and assembly modes: S. cerevisiae COQ1 is hexaprenyl, E. coli IspB is octaprenyl, and S. pombe Dps1 is decaprenyl.
