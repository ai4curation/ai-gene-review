Gene Description

acu-8 encodes the Neurospora crassa member of the conserved fungal Ach1 acetyl-CoA hydrolase/transferase family. Acu-8 is required for growth on acetate and acts in mitochondria as a succinyl-CoA:acetate CoA-transferase that links acetate with acetyl-CoA metabolism while retaining minor acetyl-CoA hydrolase activity.

Core Functions

Catalyzes the succinyl-CoA-dependent transfer of CoA to acetate in the fungal Ach1/Acu-8 family, producing acetyl-CoA during mitochondrial acetate utilization and detoxification.

acu-8 evidence notes

  • acu-8 / P15937 is the Neurospora crassa member of PTHR43609:SF1, the fungal Ach1/acetyl-CoA hydrolase subfamily.
  • PTN000484586 is the key PAINT node for acetyl-CoA hydrolase activity, acetate CoA-transferase activity, and acetate metabolic process in the Ach1 subfamily. This placement is supported for acu-8 because the direct Neurospora mutant has an acetate-utilization phenotype and deficient acetyl-CoA hydrolase activity: [PMID:1357077 An acetate-sensitive mutant of Neurospora crassa deficient in acetyl-CoA hydrolase, "The mutant was shown to be deficient in acetyl-CoA hydrolase and to accumulate acetyl-CoA when supplied with acetate"].
  • Fleck and Brock re-characterized the fungal family as CoA-transferases rather than simple energy-wasting thioester hydrolases: [PMID:19298859 Re-characterisation of Saccharomyces cerevisiae Ach1p: fungal CoA-transferases are involved in acetic acid detoxification, "Ach1p showed highest specific activity for the CoASH transfer from succinyl-CoA to acetate and only a minor acetyl-CoA-hydrolase activity"].
  • PTN000484602 is the Ascomycota mitochondrion node and is appropriate for acu-8; Saccharomyces Ach1p was experimentally enriched with mitochondria and localized to mitochondria by immunofluorescence: [PMID:12606555 Functional characterization and localization of acetyl-CoA hydrolase, Ach1p, in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, "By subcellular fractionation, most of the Ach1p in yeast was distributed with mitochondria and little Ach1p in the cytoplasm"].
  • The generic InterPro CoA-transferase row was reviewed as TOO_GENERAL because the accepted GO:0008775 acetate CoA-transferase activity row already states the Ach1-family specificity.
