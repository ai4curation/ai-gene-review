Neurospora cryo-EM structure 7R81 identifies Q7S2X9 as its 51-residue eL39 protein, entity 74, chain VB (author n1); [the retained RCSB entity record](NCU08990-7R81-entity74.json) explicitly maps the accession. This directly establishes residence in the cytosolic large ribosomal subunit, a more precise cellular location already represented in GOA. The broad Cytoplasm prediction is therefore correct but less precise than the supported ribosomal location. Nuclear assembly of ribosomal subunits does not contradict cytoplasmic residence during translation.
