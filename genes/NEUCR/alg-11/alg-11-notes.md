# alg-11 notes

alg-11 is the Neurospora crassa PTHR45919:SF1 ortholog of S. cerevisiae Alg11.
The completed PTHR45919 family review supports the conserved ALG11 PAINT
assertions for ER-membrane localization, dolichol-linked oligosaccharide
biosynthesis, and GDP-Man:Man3GlcNAc2-PP-Dol alpha-1,2-mannosyltransferase
activity.
