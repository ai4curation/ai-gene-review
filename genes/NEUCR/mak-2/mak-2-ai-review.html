Gene Description

mak-2 encodes the terminal MAP kinase of the Neurospora crassa NRC-1/MEK-2/MAK-2 MAPK cascade, a Fus3/Spk1-like module used for germling and hyphal chemotropic communication, somatic cell fusion, vegetative growth, and sexual development. MAK-2 kinase activity is required for coordinated oscillatory recruitment of MAK-2 and SO to opposing cell tips during chemotropic interactions, and MAK-2 also localizes to nuclei and cytoplasmic cortical complexes in this self-signaling system.

Manual deep research: NEUCR mak-2

mak-2 / NCU02393 encodes a 352 aa PTHR24055 MAP kinase with the canonical
MAPK TEY activation motif and the conserved protein kinase ATP-binding,
Ser/Thr kinase active-site, protein kinase domain, and MAPK signatures in
UniProt. It is the terminal MAPK in the Neurospora crassa NRC-1/MEK-2/MAK-2
module.

The foundational Neurospora study is PMID:15075265. The paper disrupted
mak-2, observed pleiotropic defects including loss of vegetative hyphal
fusion, female sterility, and autonomous ascospore lethality, and showed that
MAK-2 phosphorylation peaks during germ-tube elongation, branching, and
germling fusion. The upstream MAPKKK nrc-1 was required for MAK-2
phosphorylation, placing the two proteins in a shared MAPK cascade.

The strongest live-cell evidence is PMID:19884508. Functional MAK-2-GFP was
cytoplasmic/nuclear when germlings were isolated, then alternated with SO at
cortical complexes in interacting fusion partners. An analog-sensitive MAK-2
variant established that the kinase activity is required for chemotropic
interactions and for coordinated MAK-2/SO oscillation.

PMID:23028357 and PMID:25412208 clarify that MAK-2 acts as a scaffolded module
with NRC-1 and MEK-2. HYM1 is critical for organizing the MAK-2 pathway, and
HAM-5 functions as a MAPK scaffold required for MEK-2/MAK-2 puncta and
oscillation during germling and hyphal fusion.

Recent papers did not change the GOA decisions. PMID:34899653 and
PMID:39283084 connect MAK-2/PR-MAPK signaling to protoperithecium and sexual
development, and PMID:41915693 places MAK-2 upstream of the 2026 EOP-1/SO
pre-fusion oscillator. None supplies evidence that Neurospora MAK-2 executes a
literal pheromone-response GPCR cascade, so the PANTHER Fus3/Spk1-clade IBA
to GO:0071507 pheromone response MAPK cascade is better modified to the
ancestor GO:0000165 MAPK cascade.

mak-2 re-review notes

2026-11-01 PTHR24055 fungal PR-MAPK review

  • mak-2 encodes the Neurospora terminal ERK-type MAP kinase of the
    NRC-1 -> MEK-2 -> MAK-2 cascade. PMID:15075265 showed that MAK-2
    contains the conserved MAPK TEY activation motif, that mak-2 mutants fail
    vegetative hyphal fusion and are female sterile, and that MAK-2
    phosphorylation during early colony development depends on the upstream
    MAPKKK NRC-1.
  • PMID:19884508 provided the key direct functional/localization experiment:
    functional MAK-2-GFP is cytoplasmic/nuclear in isolated germlings and
    oscillates to cortical complexes at interacting conidial anastomosis tube
    tips, while an analog-sensitive MAK-2 variant showed that MAK-2 kinase
    activity is required for chemotropic interactions and for the alternating
    MAK-2/SO oscillation.
  • PMID:23028357 and PMID:25412208 establish MAK-2 as part of a scaffolded
    PR-like MAPK module. HYM1 helps organize NRC-1, MEK-2, and MAK-2, while
    HAM-5 scaffolds the MAK-2 MAPK complex and is needed to localize MEK-2 and
    MAK-2 to oscillatory puncta during germling and hyphal fusion.
  • The PANTHER:PTN000622075 IBDs are broad PTHR24055 MAPK-family transfers
    for Ser/Thr kinase chemistry and active nucleus/cytoplasm compartments. They
    are safe for MAK-2; the catalytic parent is simply less informative than the
    exact imported GO:0004707 MAP kinase activity row.
  • The PANTHER:PTN001172087 IBD is seeded by S. cerevisiae Fus3 and S. pombe
    Spk1, and it correctly identifies the inherited fungal Fus3/Spk1-type MAPK
    clade. Neurospora papers also name the NRC-1/MEK-2/MAK-2 tier as the
    PR-MAPK pathway during sexual development, so the GO:0071507 pheromone response MAPK cascade IBA should be retained even though MAK-2 also runs
    chemotropic self-signaling during germling and hyphal fusion.
  • I searched for 2024-2026 Neurospora mak-2 papers. PMID:34899653,
    PMID:39283084, and PMID:41915693 extend MAK-2/PR-MAPK biology into
    protoperithecium development, VE-1-dependent sexual development, and the
    SO/EOP-1 pre-fusion oscillator. They reinforce the same core pathway rather
    than changing any of the 12 seeded GOA row decisions.
