Neurospora crassa cbr1 encodes a CBR1-like NADH-cytochrome b5 reductase of the flavoprotein pyridine nucleotide cytochrome reductase family. The protein has an N-terminal transmembrane helix followed by conserved FAD- and NADH-binding domains, belongs to the CBR1-like branch of PANTHER NADH-cytochrome b5 reductases, and is inferred from the characterized Saccharomyces cerevisiae ortholog to reduce cytochrome b5 and the iron protein Dph3 from NADH.

Cbr1 is a Dph3 reductase required for the tRNA wobble uridine modification.
findings
statement supporting_text
S. cerevisiae Cbr1 was identified as an NADH-dependent Dph3 reductase, rapidly reduced Dph3 in vitro when NADH was supplied, and provided the Dph3-dependent reducing system for the Dph1-Dph2 radical-SAM enzyme. Upon addition of NADH to initiate the reaction, Dph3 was rapidly reduced by Cbr1
Loss of CBR1 alone partially impaired mcm5s2U tRNA wobble uridine modification, and combined loss of CBR1 and MCR1 further reduced this Dph3-dependent modification. CBR1 deletion alone conferred some resistance to γ-toxin, suggesting that formation of tRNA modifications was partially impaired
