# erg-3 notes

Neurospora crassa erg-3 encodes the PTHR21257 SF52 Delta14-sterol reductase. The present GOA set is computational, with PAINT IBA rows for ER membrane, Delta14-sterol reductase activity and ergosterol biosynthetic process and UniProt/InterPro rows for the same reaction or broader parents. No newer direct N. crassa erg-3 core-function paper was found beyond the family-level evidence already used by PAINT.
