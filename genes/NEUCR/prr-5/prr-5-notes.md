# prr-5 manual notes

## 2026-10-21

Deep research could not be generated in this environment because the configured providers were unavailable, and the seeded Neurospora rows cite no organism-specific PMID. This review therefore focused on the local UniProt record, the PTHR28013 PAINT node, and cached experimental evidence from close fungal PalI/Rim9-family members.

Neurospora prr-5 falls in PTHR28013:SF3 with experimentally studied Aspergillus PalI, Saccharomyces Rim9, Candida Rim9, and Schizosaccharomyces Mac1. The PAINT rows for plasma membrane, cell division site, and growing cell tip all come from `PTN001997363`, the same curated Dikarya node. A. nidulans PalI-GFP is directly observed at the plasma membrane, septae, and polarized hyphal tips, making this node plausible for a filamentous-fungal PalI/Rim9 member such as prr-5.

I did not add process annotations to `prr-5`: UniProt infers a role in alkaline-pH pacc-1 processing by homology, but the current review has no direct Neurospora pH-signaling paper in cache.
