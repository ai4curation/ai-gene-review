  `PTHR19359:SF150` cytochrome b5 with an N-terminal heme-binding domain and a

  biosynthetic process from the CGD/SGD-seeded fungal branch, and

  `PTHR19359:SF14`, away from the reviewed fungal SF150 CYB5 branch, so I did
  not include it as a sterol-pathway target here.
