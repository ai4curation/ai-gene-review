# acon-3 (Neurospora crassa) — curation notes
UniProt Q7SB37 (NCU07617; "Acr1"). Nuclear MEDUSA/MedA-family regulator (DUF7082). Neurospora variant, stage regulation.
- PANTHER PTHR39463 = MEDUSA (the MedA conidiation-regulator family) — cross-species link to Aspergillus medA.
- Acts downstream of fluffy (FL); required for later conidiation stages: fl overexpression [PMID:15126394 "does not bypass the conidiation defect of the acon-3 mutant"]. That paper places FL between acon-2 and acon-3 [PMID:15126394 "fl acts downstream of acon-2 and upstream of acon-3"] but does NOT extend the chain to the con genes — elevated fl expression was [PMID:15126394 "correlated with increased expression of eas, but not con-6 or con-10"]. So acon-3's position relative to the conidiation-specific con genes is not established experimentally.
- Only GOA annotation is nucleus (IBA). Molecular function uncharacterized (no MF asserted). Node PTN002218055 not in local PAINT index, so grounded as GENE_PRODUCT.
