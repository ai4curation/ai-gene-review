| Host localization and virulence target | Mature AVR-Pita bound rice OsCOX11 in yeast two-hybrid and in-vitro pull-down assays. AVR-Pita:YFP co-localized with mitochondrial markers and OsCOX11:mCherry. AVR-Pita or OsCOX11 overexpression increased cytochrome-c-oxidase activity, reduced chitin-triggered ROS/defense-gene responses, and increased blast susceptibility; reduced OsCOX11 dosage increased resistance. (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000026) | **Moderate–high for binding/localization; moderate for the physiological pathway.** Major functional evidence relies on ectopic or constitutive expression, and no catalytic mutant or OsCOX11 cleavage product established that protease activity mediates the effect. | [Han et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12284-021-00453-4) |
| Immune recognition: revised 2024 model | CRISPR/RNAi and infection assays showed that Ptr—not the linked NLR Pi-ta—is required for AVR-Pita recognition. PtrA confers Pi-ta2 resistance and recognized all variants tested; PtrB confers classical Pi-ta resistance but recognized only Tyr191/192 variants. A Tyr→Cys substitution enabled escape from PtrB while retaining PtrA recognition. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000014, pqac-00000043) | **High for genetic receptor assignment and allele specificity.** The historical Pi-ta–AVR-Pita interaction could not be reproduced. (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000013) | [Xiao et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41477-024-01694-z) |
| Field implementation and breeding relevance | The released Taiwanese cultivar Tainung 84 carries PtrA and has maintained strong blast resistance since 2010. PtrA occurred in 8 of 41 surveyed japonica cultivars (19.5%), which showed good field resistance during 2008–2024. Fine mapping delimited the Ptr locus to 140.4 kb. (pqac-00000055, pqac-00000071, pqac-00000072) | **High for field association; not proof that AVR-Pita recognition alone caused durability.** Tainung 84 also carries *Piz-t* and the partial-resistance QTL qBR12_3.3–4.4; the authors therefore support multilocus pyramiding rather than relying solely on PtrA. | [Wang et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40529-024-00444-w) |
