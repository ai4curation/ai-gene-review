Gene Description

SsuE is an NADPH-dependent flavin reductase associated with the alkanesulfonate monooxygenase system of Pseudomonas putida S-313. Heterologous expression of the S-313 gene produced NADPH-dependent FMN reduction, lower FAD reduction and no detectable NADH-supported activity under the tested conditions. SsuE can supply reduced flavin for oxygenase chemistry, but native complementation experiments found it dispensable for tested aliphatic-sulfonate and methionine growth while required for aromatic-sulfonate growth. The alternative reduced-flavin source supporting the former conditions was not identified.
