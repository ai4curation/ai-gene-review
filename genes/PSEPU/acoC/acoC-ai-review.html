Gene Description

AcoC is the lipoyl-bearing E2 acetyltransferase assigned to the acetoin-cleaving system of Pseudomonas putida PpG2. The E2 role is inferred from its conserved lipoyl-bearing architecture and position in the acetoin-cleaving locus. In the proposed E2 role it accepts the E1-derived acetyl group on its lipoyl carrier and transfers it to coenzyme A. Its alpha/beta-hydrolase catalytic-domain architecture differs from classical E2 acetyltransferase folds; a native E3 partner and specific catalytic-residue mechanism remain unresolved.
