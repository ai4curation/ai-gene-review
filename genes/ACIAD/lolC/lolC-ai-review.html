Gene Description

Homodimeric membrane component of the lipoprotein-releasing LolDF complex of Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1, called LolF in biochemical studies and encoded by the genome locus named lolC. LolF binds lipidated cargo, recruits LolA and couples conformational changes driven by the LolD ATPase to cargo release from the inner membrane.

ADP1 lolC / LolF review notes

The genome record names A0ACM8PZZ0/ACIAD2641 lolC; the direct biochemical study names its homodimeric membrane component LolF. Official Q9EYM3 is sequence-identical to the 411-residue target; the 410-residue PDB LolF entities under Q9EYM3 match native positions 1–410. The role is resolved by native ADP1 isolation, structures and sequence identity, not by guessing from the shared PTHR30489:SF0 label. [file:analysis/module_audits/bacterial_lol_lipoprotein_trafficking/aciad-consultation/identity-notes.md]

Native ADP1 chromosomal LolD tagging copurifies the membrane component, and structures show the homodimeric complex. [PMID:42736309, "The LolDF complex forms a homodimer that adopts a characteristic type VII ABC transporter architecture"]. Four membrane helices per protomer provide the cargo cavity and periplasmic carrier-binding regions; the membrane machinery and LolD ATPase together perform release. A contribution MF is appropriate on this positive mechanistic basis. It does not require a separate LolE paralog, and LolF is not an autonomous ATPase.

Direct detergent BamE transfer supports the new contribution to lipoprotein-releasing activity. [PMID:42736309, "To verify ATP-driven lipoprotein transfer to LolA, we performed an in vitro single-cycle transport assay using detergent-purified A baylyi LolDF co-expressed with BamE and purified A baylyi LolA in the presence or absence of ATP and MgCl2."] The endogenous short substrate density is not unequivocal identification of a particular native cargo. Both native depletion/complementation and purified transfer experiments are distinguished from direct whole-pathway flux.

All five original rows remain ordered and exact. The broad membrane term is refined to plasma membrane; the existing transport, outer-membrane localization and membrane-complex rows are retained. One specific release MF is added with contributes_to; no new process is introduced.

Assay limits, research and lineage checks

The primary's charge substitutions were made in BamE; their binding/extraction results do not establish universal rules for all +3-charged lipoproteins. Lnt-silencing viability and structural accommodation suggest acyl-state tolerance but are not a chemically defined assay proving efficient transport of every diacylated substrate. The observed single LolA, unknown endogenous cargo and detergent single-cycle handover are retained as separate facts.
