Gene Description

pcaJ encodes subunit B of the heteromeric beta-ketoadipate:succinyl-CoA transferase (3-oxoadipate CoA-transferase, EC 2.8.3.6) of Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1. With subunit A (PcaI) it forms an alpha2beta2 enzyme that transfers CoA from succinyl-CoA to 3-oxoadipate, giving 3-oxoadipyl-CoA and succinate in the shared lower beta-ketoadipate pathway. pcaJ is the second gene of the pcaIJFBDKCHG operon; the catechol-branch catJ gene encodes a protein of identical sequence, so this UniProt entry represents both loci.
