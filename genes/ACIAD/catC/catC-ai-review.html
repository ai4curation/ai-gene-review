Gene Description

CatC of Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 is muconolactone Delta-isomerase (EC 5.3.3.4), a small (96-residue) cytoplasmic enzyme of the dimeric alpha/beta-barrel fold that forms a homodecamer. It isomerizes (4S)-muconolactone, the product of CatB, to beta-ketoadipate enol-lactone, the third and last reaction of the catechol branch of the beta-ketoadipate pathway before the branch converges with the protocatechuate branch at the enol-lactone hydrolase step. catC is the second gene of the muconate-inducible catBCIJFD operon of the ADP1 ben-cat cluster.
