Gene Description

CatB of Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 is cis,cis-muconate lactonizing enzyme I (muconate cycloisomerase, EC 5.5.1.1), a cytoplasmic member of the mandelate racemase/muconate lactonizing enzyme branch of the enolase superfamily. It cycloisomerizes cis,cis-muconate, the product of CatA ring cleavage, to (4S)-muconolactone in the catechol branch of the beta-ketoadipate pathway, and is encoded first in the muconate-inducible catBCIJFD operon of the ADP1 ben-cat gene cluster. The purified enzyme accepts cis,cis-muconate and some fluoro- and methyl-substituted analogues but not chlorinated muconates, which distinguishes it from the chloromuconate cycloisomerases of chloroaromatic degradation pathways. Like other muconate cycloisomerases it is expected to use a divalent metal, Mn(2+), at the active site.

Evidence summary

  • catB encodes muconate lactonizing enzyme in the A. calcoaceticus catBCDE cluster
    PMID:3003031, and the cloned
    fragment complements P. putida catB mutants PMID:3003031.
  • Substrate specificity of the ADP1 enzyme was measured directly: it converts
    cis,cis-muconate and methyl/fluoro analogues but not chlorinated muconates
    [PMID:9726873 "the muconate cycloisomerases from Pseudomonas putida PRS2000 and
    Acinetobacter \"calcoaceticus\" ADP1"; "but not 2-fluoro-, 2-chloro-, 3-chloro-, or
    2,4-dichloro-cis,cis-muconate"]. This is the basis for removing GO:0018850.
  • catB is induced by muconate via CatM PMID:7592340.
  • Family: NCBIfam TIGR02534 (muconate cycloisomerase), PANTHER PTHR48073:SF2,
    PROSITE MR_MLE motifs. Mn(2+) cofactor in UniProt is by similarity to P. putida
    CatB (P08310), whose Mn(2+) site was measured PMID:6652062.

Review decisions

  • Accept GO:0018849 (core) and GO:0030145.
  • Remove GO:0018850 (directly contradicted for this enzyme), GO:0006518 and
    GO:0009063 (enolase-superfamily over-transfer; consistent with the KT2440 catB review).
  • Refine GO:0003824 to GO:0018849 and GO:0042952 to GO:0019615.
