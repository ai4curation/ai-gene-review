CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required for mobilization of iron from the bacterioferritin
CC       (BFR) complex, composed of BfrB and FtnA in varying proportions;
CC       mobilization requires the [2Fe-2S] cluster of this protein
CC       (PubMed:19575528, PubMed:22812654, PubMed:26368531, PubMed:28318006,
CC       PubMed:30183257). Reduction of the BfrB heme group occurs in the
CC       presence of Bfd, strongly suggesting that the BfrB-Bfd complex allows
CC       heme to mediate electron transfer from FPR to the Fe(3+) iron core in
CC       the BFR prior to its release as Fe(2+) (PubMed:19575528,
CC       PubMed:22812654). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19575528,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22812654, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26368531,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28318006, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30183257,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:19575528}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer (Probable) (PubMed:30183257). Interacts with BfrB; up
CC       to 12 Bfd proteins can bind to the BfrB bacterioferritin complex (BFR)
CC       (PubMed:19575528, PubMed:22812654, PubMed:26368531, PubMed:35327558).
CC       One Bfd protein binds to a BfrB dimer in the BFR, with the [2Fe-2S]
CC       cluster positioned about 22 Angstroms above the heme of BfrB
CC       (PubMed:22812654). Does not interact with FtnA (PubMed:35327558).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19575528, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22812654,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26368531, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35327558,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:30183257}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Increased transcription under iron starvation
CC       (PubMed:12207696). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12207696}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The C-terminal 17 residues are not required for [2Fe-2S]
CC       cluster assembly, interaction with the BfrB bacterioferritin complex
CC       (BFR) or iron mobilization from the BfrB core (PubMed:30183257).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30183257}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: No growth phenotype in iron-sufficient
CC       conditions, in low iron conditions iron accumulates in BFR composed
CC       only of BfrB, the iron is not mobilizable, increased secretion of
CC       pyoverdin siderophore (PubMed:28318006). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28318006}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the Bfd family. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- CAUTION: Was originally thought to not require the [2Fe-2S] cluster for
CC       iron mobilization from BfrB (PubMed:19575528). Subsequent experiments
CC       show the [2Fe-2S] cluster is necessary (PubMed:22812654).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19575528, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22812654}.
