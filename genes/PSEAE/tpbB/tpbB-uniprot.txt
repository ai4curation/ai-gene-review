CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the synthesis of cyclic-di-GMP (c-di-GMP) via the
CC       condensation of 2 GTP molecules. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20300602,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24278422}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Part of the YfiB-TpbB-YfiR (or yfiBNR) system, encoding a
CC       tripartite signaling module that modulates intracellular c-di-GMP
CC       levels (PubMed:20300602, PubMed:22719254). The system is a key
CC       regulator of the small colony variant (SCV) phenotype, and plays an
CC       important role in biofilm formation and in vivo persistence
CC       (PubMed:20300602). The c-di-GMP produced by TpbB/YfiN stimulates the
CC       production of the Pel and Psl exopolysaccharides, which promotes
CC       surface attachment, generates an SCV phenotype and confers resistance
CC       against phagocytosis (PubMed:20300602). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20300602,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22719254}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activity is tightly controlled by YfiR, a small
CC       periplasmic protein, and the OmpA/Pal-like outer-membrane lipoprotein
CC       YfiB (PubMed:20300602, PubMed:22719254). Diguanylate cyclase activity
CC       is inhibited by the specific interaction of YfiR with the TpbB
CC       periplasmic domain and is activated by YfiB, which releases the YfiR-
CC       mediated repression through sequestration of YfiR to the outer membrane
CC       (PubMed:20300602, PubMed:22719254). Release of repression leads to a
CC       conformational shift in TpbB/YfiN that propagates through the PAS and
CC       transmembrane domains to switch the cytoplasmic HAMP domain from an
CC       inactive to an active conformation and activate the C-terminal
CC       catalytic GGDEF domain (PubMed:22719254, PubMed:24278422). Thus,
CC       TpbB/YfiN appears to function by switching between discrete inactive
CC       and active functional states depending on the presence or absence of
CC       bound YfiR (PubMed:22719254). Activity is also controlled by
CC       dephosphorylation of the periplasmic domain by the tyrosine phosphatase
CC       TpbA (By similarity). These two mechanisms of regulation could in
CC       principle work in parallel or as part of the same regulatory pathway
CC       (PubMed:20300602). Does not undergo product feedback inhibition
CC       (PubMed:24278422). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A0H2Z7X0,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20300602, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22719254,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24278422}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Purine metabolism; 3',5'-cyclic di-GMP biosynthesis.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:22719254). Interacts with YfiR
CC       (PubMed:22719254). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22719254}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell inner membrane
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:20300602}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The presence of the HAMP domain is required for dimerization
CC       and catalysis. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24278422}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated at both Tyr residues and Ser/Thr residues (By
CC       similarity). Dephosphorylated and inactivated by TpbA (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A0H2Z7X0}.
