CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription regulator that plays a critical role in the
CC       expression of beta-lactamase AmpC, acting by positive regulation of the
CC       ampC gene (PubMed:16251297, PubMed:23045355, PubMed:25182487,
CC       PubMed:8405939). Acts by binding directly to the promoter region of the
CC       ampC gene (PubMed:25182487, PubMed:8405939). Probably does not regulate
CC       transcription of its own gene (PubMed:16251297). Has a wider role in
CC       the regulation of expression of genes involved in cell wall and capsule
CC       synthesis, proteolysis, quorum sensing, and virulence (PubMed:16251297,
CC       PubMed:22479525). Negatively modulates the MexEF-OprN efflux pump
CC       (PubMed:22479525). Regulates genes that have been acquired by
CC       horizontal gene transfer (PubMed:22479525).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16251297, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22479525,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23045355, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25182487,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8405939}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25182487}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000305}. Membrane
CC       {ECO:0000303|PubMed:25182487}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Up-regulated by beta-lactams (PubMed:25182487). Expression
CC       is negatively regulated by sigma factor RpoN, possibly through promoter
CC       blocking (PubMed:25182487). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25182487}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Effector binding domain recognizes specific cell-wall-derived
CC       muropeptide products, probably acting as a link between cell-wall
CC       recycling/repair and the induction of resistance to beta-lactam
CC       antibiotics. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28079369}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Reduces beta-lactam resistance (PubMed:22479525).
CC       Reduces beta-lactamase activity, at least in part, by reducing beta-
CC       lactamase AmpC expression level (PubMed:22479525). Reduces
CC       pathogenicity in a C.elegans infection model (PubMed:22479525).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22479525}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Orthologs in several species of Enterobacteria, but no
CC       known ortholog in E.coli. {ECO:0000303|PubMed:16251297,
CC       ECO:0000303|PubMed:8405939}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the LysR transcriptional regulatory family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
