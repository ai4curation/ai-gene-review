CC   -!- FUNCTION: Major component of the type IV pilus (T4P) that plays a role
CC       in surface and host cell adhesion, colonization, biofilm maturation,
CC       virulence, and twitching, a form of surface-associated motility
CC       facilitated by cycles of extension, adhesion, and retraction of T4P
CC       fibers (PubMed:26041805, PubMed:31431558, PubMed:9282737). In addition,
CC       plays a critical role in type II secretion of exoenzymes by interacting
CC       with specific components such as XcpT or XcpU (PubMed:9282737).
CC       Modulates host calcium signaling through interaction with host calcium-
CC       modulating cyclophilin ligand (PubMed:23266901).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23266901, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26041805,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31431558, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9282737}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with itself to form a multimeric tubular filament
CC       structure with a diameter of 51 Angstroms (PubMed:39666767). Interacts
CC       with PilJ (PubMed:26041805). Interacts with PilS at the inner membrane
CC       (PubMed:27162347). Interacts with type II secretion system (T2SS)
CC       components XcpT, XcpU and XcpW (PubMed:9282737).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26041805, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27162347,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39666767, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9282737}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: (Microbial infection) The ssRNA phage PP7 infects P.aeruginosa
CC       O1 (PAO1) through the retractile type IV pilus (T4P). PP7 binds to T4P
CC       by interacting with a single pilin subunit PilA through the Pilus-
CC       Interacting Region (PIR) of the phage maturation protein (Mat), and is
CC       brought to the cell surface through pilus retraction powered by ATPases
CC       PilT and PilU. Then Mat enters the cell envelope, causing pilus bending
CC       and pilus detachment, which impairs cell motility and restricts the
CC       pathogen's virulence. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38574145}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Fimbrium {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39666767}. Cell
CC       inner membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27162347}; Single-pass membrane
CC       protein {ECO:0000255}. Host endoplasmic reticulum membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23266901}. Note=Likely secreted through the outer
CC       membrane PilQ secretin channel for surface display.
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:39666767}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: PilA subunits constituting the T4P exhibit a classical pilin
CC       fold featuring an extended N-terminal alpha-helix linked to a C-
CC       terminal globular beta-sheet-containing domain, which are packed
CC       tightly along the pilus. The N-terminal helices constitute the pilus
CC       core where they stabilize the tubular assembly via hydrophobic
CC       interactions. The alpha-helical core of the pilus is surrounded by the
CC       C-terminal globular domain of PilA that coats the outer surface of the
CC       pilus, mediating interactions with the surrounding environment.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39666767}.
CC   -!- PTM: Methylated by prepilin peptidase PilD at the amino group of the N-
CC       terminal phenylalanine once the leader sequence is cleaved.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1429457}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mutants show a significant reduction in the
CC       efficiency of extracellular protein secretion (PubMed:9282737). They
CC       are also defective in twitching motility (PubMed:31431558).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31431558, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9282737}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the N-Me-Phe pilin family. {ECO:0000305}.
