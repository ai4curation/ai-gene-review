## Interpretation

PA0335 is a standalone (monofunctional) HAD-family histidinol-phosphate phosphatase, distinct from
the bifunctional HisB of enterobacteria (P. aeruginosa hisB/PA5143 is the IGP dehydratase and is
essential). Incomplete auxotrophy implies a minor backup phosphatase activity in PAO1, not yet
identified. No CC annotation exists; no direct localization evidence was found, so no NEW CC row.
