CC   -!- FUNCTION: With LigD forms a non-homologous end joining (NHEJ) DNA
CC       repair enzyme, which repairs dsDNA breaks with reduced fidelity.
CC       Stimulates rNTP addition to DSB and end joining (ligation) of linear
CC       DNA by LigD, on 3'-overhangs and probably also 5'-overhangs and blunt
CC       dsDNA breaks. Binds both ends of linear dsDNA protecting it from
CC       exonuclease activity. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20018881}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer, may form higher-order multimers on DNA. Non-
CC       dimerized protein does not stimulate LigD ligase activity. Probably
CC       interacts with LigD. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20018881}.
