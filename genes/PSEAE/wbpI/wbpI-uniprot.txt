CC       diacetamido-2,3-dideoxy-alpha-D-mannuronic acid (UDP-alpha-D-
CC       ManNAc3NAcA). Exhibits high specificity towards the substrate as UDP-
CC       alpha-D-GlcNAc, UDP-alpha-D-GlcNAcA (UDP-2-acetamido-2-deoxy-alpha-D-
CC       glucuronic acid) and UDP-alpha-D-GlcNAc3NAc (UDP-2,3-diacetamido-2,3-
CC       dideoxy-alpha-D-glucose) cannot act as substrates.
