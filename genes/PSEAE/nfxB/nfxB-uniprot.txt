CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional regulator (PubMed:23924707, PubMed:7592337,
CC       PubMed:8878035). Represses expression of target genes, including its
CC       own gene and the mexC-mexD-oprJ efflux operon; thereby plays a role in
CC       conferring multidrug resistance (PubMed:1330820, PubMed:23924707,
CC       PubMed:7592337, PubMed:8878035). Binds regulatory elements in target
CC       genes, including promoters for nfxB and the mexC-mexD-oprJ operon
CC       (PubMed:23924707, PubMed:7592337). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1330820,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23924707, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7592337,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8878035}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotetramer; may represent active DNA-binding repressor form.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23924707}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Represses expression of its own gene, nfxB.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23924707, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7592337,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8878035}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Confers multidrug resistance.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23924707}.
