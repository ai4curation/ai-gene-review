CC   -!- FUNCTION: Part of the H1 type VI secretion system (H1-T6SS) specialized
CC       secretion system, which delivers several virulence factors in both
CC       prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells during infection (PubMed:21325275,
CC       PubMed:24794869). Forms the spike at the tip of the elongating tube
CC       formed by haemolysin co-regulated protein 1/Hcp1 (PubMed:26894531).
CC       Allows the delivery of the Tse6 toxin to target cells where it exerts
CC       its toxicity (PubMed:30177742). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21325275,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24794869, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26894531,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30177742}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms homotrimers (PubMed:21325275). Part of the type VI
CC       secretion system (T6SS) (PubMed:26894531). Interacts with EagT6 and
CC       Tse6; these interactions are required for Tse6 loading onto VgrG1
CC       (PubMed:30177742). Interacts with Hcp1 (PubMed:21325275).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21325275, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26894531,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30177742}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21325275}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Deletion of vgrG1a shows residual type VI
CC       secretion (PubMed:21325275). However, simultaneous deletion of all
CC       three vgrG genes (vgrG1a, vgrG1b and vgrG1c) totally abrogates
CC       bacterial killing (PubMed:24794869). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21325275,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24794869}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the VgrG protein family. {ECO:0000305}.
