# hutG (Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, PA5091, Q9HU92) notes

## 2026-11-11

- Deep research was not run for this gene in this session; no provider file
  exists. The review rests on the reviewed UniProt record and the cached
  abstracts of PMID:16475788, PMID:4290562 and PMID:22225844.
- Function: N-formylglutamate deformylase, measured on the purified protein
  [PMID:16475788 "while Pa5091 catalyzed the hydrolysis of N-formyl-L-glutamate to formate and L-glutamate"].
- P. aeruginosa has two parallel terminal branches from FIGLU to glutamate,
  HutF/HutG and the HutE (PA3175) formimidoylglutamase; either suffices
  [PMID:22225844 "Genetic analysis revealed that the four-step route is dispensable as long as the five-step route is present (and vice versa)."].
- No PANTHER family is assigned in the UniProt record, so no family review applies.
- Exemplar for the two-step HutF/HutG route of the histidine catabolism module.
