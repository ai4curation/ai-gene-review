Gene Description

PA5388 encodes CaiX, the periplasmic L-carnitine-binding protein of the Cbc (choline/betaine/carnitine) ABC importer of Pseudomonas aeruginosa. CaiX has a cleavable signal peptide and an OpuAC-type substrate-binding domain; it binds carnitine with moderate affinity, docks to the shared CbcWV permease-ATPase core, and confers carnitine specificity on it. CaiX is required for carnitine uptake and for growth on carnitine, and with CbcWV it is sufficient for carnitine but not choline or glycine betaine uptake.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

L-carnitine binding

Definition: Binding to L-carnitine.

Justification: CaiX is a carnitine-specific substrate-binding protein, but GO has no carnitine-binding term; the closest parent is quaternary ammonium group binding.

Parent term: quaternary ammonium group binding

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:19919675 : CaiX, which binds carnitine with a moderate affinity and represents the first carnitine-specific SBP to be reported.
