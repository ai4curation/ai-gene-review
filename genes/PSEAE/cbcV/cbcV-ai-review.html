Gene Description

CbcV (PA5376) is the ATP-binding and hydrolyzing component of the Cbc ABC importer in Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. It energizes the CbcW membrane component, while associated binding proteins specify choline, glycine betaine, carnitine and, in characterized CosX-containing combinations, choline-O-sulfate. Its ATPase acts on the cytoplasmic side of the transport assembly by the conserved ABC mechanism. Substrate import supplies nutrients for downstream catabolic enzymes.
