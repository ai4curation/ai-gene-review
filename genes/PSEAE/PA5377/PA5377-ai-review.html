Gene Description

PA5377 (CbcW) is the integral inner-membrane permease subunit of the Cbc (choline/betaine/carnitine) ABC importer, encoded in the cbcXWV operon (PA5378-PA5376) with the substrate-binding protein CbcX and the ATPase CbcV. It has six transmembrane helices and belongs to the ChoW/ProW family of binding protein-dependent permeases. The CbcWV core transporter works with several separately encoded periplasmic binding proteins that set its substrate specificity. With CbcX it takes up choline (and betaine), with BetX glycine betaine, and with CaiX L-carnitine and short-chain acylcarnitines. Cbc imports these quaternary ammonium compounds mainly for use as carbon and nitrogen sources. Transposon mutants in PA5377 cannot grow on choline, glycine betaine or carnitine as sole carbon sources. Expression of cbcXWV is induced by glycine betaine through the activator GbdR.

Core Functions

Integral membrane permease (TMD) subunit CbcW of the CbcWV core ABC importer. With the ATPase CbcV and an interchangeable periplasmic substrate-binding protein (CbcX for choline, BetX for glycine betaine, CaiX for carnitine and short-chain acylcarnitines), it translocates quaternary ammonium compounds across the inner membrane, mainly for use as carbon and nitrogen sources.

    ("weakly basic ... amino or substituted amino group"). Choline, glycine
    betaine and carnitine are quaternary ammonium compounds, and GO places them
    under GO:0015651 quaternary ammonium group transmembrane transporter
    activity and GO:0015418 ABC-type quaternary ammonium compound transporting
    activity, not under amine transporter (runoak ancestors, go.db). The amine
    term is therefore the wrong branch for Cbc.

    group transport in the current ontology. GO:0031460 and GO:1902603 are.

    contributes_to GO:0015418, which covers all three SBP-dependent substrates.
