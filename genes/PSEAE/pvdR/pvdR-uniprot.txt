CC   -!- FUNCTION: Part of the tripartite efflux system PvdRT-OpmQ required for
CC       the secretion into the extracellular milieu of the siderophore
CC       pyoverdine (PVD), which is involved in iron acquisition
CC       (PubMed:19906986, PubMed:21035449, PubMed:22187978, PubMed:23766114).
CC       This subunit is an adapter protein that stimulates the ATPase activity
CC       of PvdT and connects the inner and outer membrane components (By
CC       similarity). The system is responsible for export of newly synthesized
CC       PVD after the final steps of biosynthesis have taken place in the
CC       periplasm (PubMed:21035449). It is also responsible for recycling of
CC       PVD after internalization of ferri-PVD into the periplasm by the outer-
CC       membrane receptor FpvA and release of iron from PVD, thus making PVD
CC       available for new cycles of iron uptake (PubMed:19906986,
CC       PubMed:23766114). In addition, can expel unwanted metals complexed with
CC       PVD from the periplasm into the extracellular medium (PubMed:22187978).
CC       Does not contribute to resistance to antibiotics belonging to the
CC       classes of tetracyclines, aminoglycosides, beta-lactams and macrolides,
CC       and chloramphenicol (PubMed:19906986). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q88F89,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19906986, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21035449,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22187978, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23766114}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of the tripartite efflux system PvdRT-OpmQ, which is
CC       composed of an inner membrane component with both ATPase and permease
CC       domains, PvdT, a periplasmic membrane fusion protein, PvdR, and an
CC       outer membrane component, OpmQ. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19906986,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21035449, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23766114}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Periplasm {ECO:0000305|PubMed:19906986}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression is iron-regulated and under the tight control of
CC       the PVD-specific PvdS sigma factor. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19906986}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mutant shows reduction of extracellular PVD
CC       levels and accumulates PVD in the periplasm (PubMed:19906986). Mutant
CC       lacking pvdRT-opmQ secretes PVD and can import ferri-PVD, but has an
CC       approximately 20-fold increase in the amount of PVD present in the
CC       periplasm (PubMed:23766114). The pvdRT-opmQ deletion mutant also
CC       accumulates newly synthesized PVD in the periplasm (PubMed:21035449).
CC       Mutant accumulates unwanted PVD-metal complexes (PubMed:22187978).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19906986, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21035449,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22187978, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23766114}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Was originally thought to be involved only in PVD
CC       recycling (PubMed:19906986, PubMed:23766114). It was later shown to be
CC       involved in the secretion of both newly synthesized PVD and recycled
CC       PVD (PubMed:21035449). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19906986,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21035449, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23766114}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the membrane fusion protein (MFP) (TC 8.A.1)
CC       family. {ECO:0000305}.
