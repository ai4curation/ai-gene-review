CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the regulation of glucose metabolism. Represses
CC       its own expression as well as that of the gluconate permease GntP
CC       (PubMed:28752954). It employs an effector mediated de-repression
CC       mechanism: in the absence of ligand, GntR binds to the gntR and gntP
CC       promoters and represses their expression. The release of promoter bound
CC       GntR is induced by gluconate and 6-phosphogluconate that bind with
CC       similar apparent affinities to the GntR/DNA complex. The release of
CC       GntR leads to transcription of the genes (PubMed:28752954).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28752954}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Free GntR fails to recognize gluconate and 6-
CC       phosphogluconate, whereas the GntR/DNA complexes recognize both
CC       ligands. It is therefore likely that GntR DNA binding induces
CC       structural changes that permit GntR to recognize effectors.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28752954}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Negatively autoregulated. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28752954}.
