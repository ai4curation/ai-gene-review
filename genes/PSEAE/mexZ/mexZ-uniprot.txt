CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional regulator (PubMed:19589837, PubMed:31527038).
CC       Represses the expression of target genes, including the efflux pump
CC       subunits MexX and MexY, thereby modulating responses to antibiotics
CC       such as aminoglycosides and tetracycline (PubMed:10543738,
CC       PubMed:19589837, PubMed:23441219, PubMed:31527038). Binds to a 20 bp
CC       palindromic sequence in a putative promoter in the mexZ-mexXY
CC       intergenic region (PubMed:15336398, PubMed:19589837, PubMed:20691272,
CC       PubMed:31527038). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10543738,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15336398, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19589837,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20691272, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23441219,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31527038}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: MexZ transcription repression activity is probably
CC       reduced by blocking of its DNA-binding domain by direct interaction
CC       with AmrZ/PA5471. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31527038}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (Probable) (PubMed:15336398). Probably binds to DNA
CC       in dimeric form (PubMed:15336398, PubMed:19589837). Interacts with
CC       antirepressor ArmZ/PA5471; interaction reduces MexZ-DNA binding
CC       (PubMed:19589837, PubMed:23441219, PubMed:31527038).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15336398, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19589837,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23441219, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31527038,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:20691272}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Undetectable in wild-type cells (PubMed:19589837). Up-
CC       regulated by antibiotic tetracycline (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:19589837). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19589837}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Increases expression of mexY, independent of
CC       tetracycline induction, in PAO1161 strain (PubMed:31527038). Increases
CC       expression of mexX about 5-fold, or 18-fold, in the absence or presence
CC       of spectinomycin, respectively, in the PAO1 strain (PubMed:23441219).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23441219, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31527038}.
