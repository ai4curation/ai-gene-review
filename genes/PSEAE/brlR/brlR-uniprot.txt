CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional regulator (PubMed:23687276, PubMed:23935054,
CC       PubMed:24612375). Responsive to the second messenger cyclic di-GMP (c-
CC       di-GMP) and to the virulence factor pyocyanine, which both enhance gene
CC       expression and promoter DNA binding of BrlR (PubMed:24612375,
CC       PubMed:29967320). Activates expression of operons encoding the
CC       multidrug efflux pumps MexAB-OprM and MexEF-OprN and several ABC
CC       transport systems, acting by direct binding to their respective
CC       promoters (PubMed:23687276, PubMed:24612375, PubMed:29180529). Also
CC       acts as a repressor of the two component regulatory system, PhoPQ
CC       (PubMed:23935054). Binds to promoter of its own gene (PubMed:24612375).
CC       Contributes to the antimicrobial tolerance exhibited by biofilms,
CC       acting, at least in part, by activating expression of multidrug efflux
CC       pumps and ABC transporters (PubMed:22730129, PubMed:23687276,
CC       PubMed:29180529). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22730129,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23687276, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23935054,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24612375, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29180529,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29967320}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer (PubMed:24612375). Homodimer; dimer formation enhanced
CC       in the presence of the second messenger, cyclic di-GMP (c-di-GMP)
CC       (PubMed:24612375, PubMed:29967320). Homotetramer; dimer of dimers,
CC       arranged in a head-to-tail fashion, which may reduce DNA-binding
CC       ability (PubMed:29967320). Conformational changes upon binding c-di-GMP
CC       or pyocyanine may facilitate DNA binding (PubMed:29967320).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24612375, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29967320,
CC       ECO:0000303|PubMed:29967320}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Up-regulated by the second messenger cyclic di-GMP (c-di-
CC       GMP), which has been associated with controlling the transition between
CC       a motile and biofilm lifestyle (PubMed:24612375). Up-regulated by the
CC       virulence factor pyocyanine (PubMed:29967320). Expressed during biofilm
CC       formation, under flowing media conditions, after 6 h post initial
CC       attachment and detectable for at least one to six days
CC       (PubMed:22730129, PubMed:24612375). Not detectable prior to 6 h, in the
CC       earliest phase of initial, reversible, cell-surface attachment during
CC       biofilm formation (PubMed:24612375). Expression not detectable when
CC       grown in suspension, also known as free-floating or planktonic growth
CC       (PubMed:22730129). Expression not detectable in biofilms grown under
CC       stationary culture media conditions (PubMed:22730129).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22730129, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24612375,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29967320}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Abolishes high-level biofilm-specific drug
CC       tolerance, including tolerance of tobramycin, norfloxacin,
CC       trimethoprim, tetracycline and kanamycin (PubMed:22730129,
CC       PubMed:23935054). Biofilms are more susceptible to treatment with
CC       hydrogen peroxide (PubMed:22730129). Intracellular norfloxacin
CC       accumulation increases in mutant biofilms (PubMed:29180529). Normal
CC       attachment, formation and architecture of biofilms (PubMed:22730129).
CC       Growth in suspension, known as planktonic growth, is normal in the
CC       absence of antimicrobial agents (PubMed:22730129). Increases resistance
CC       against colistin, in either planktonic or biofilm growth conditions
CC       (PubMed:23935054). No differences in colony morphology or Psl
CC       polysaccharide production (PubMed:22730129). No differences in
CC       motility, such as swimming, swarming, or twitching (PubMed:22730129).
CC       No effect on the susceptibility of planktonic cells to tobramycin or
CC       hydrogen peroxide (PubMed:22730129). Greater than 9-fold decrease in
CC       the transcripts of the ABC-type transport system operon PA1874-PA1877
CC       (PubMed:29180529). Decreases expression of genes involved in carbon
CC       catabolism and metabolism, secreted factors, and type I to III
CC       secretion (PubMed:23687276). Increases expression of the arnBCADTEF
CC       operon (PubMed:23935054). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22730129,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23687276, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23935054,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29180529, ECO:0000303|PubMed:22730129}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Biofilm bacteria exhibit a profound tolerance of various
CC       antimicrobial agents, rendering biofilm cells 10- to 1,000-fold less
CC       susceptible than the same bacteria grown in a planktonic (free-
CC       floating) culture (PubMed:22730129). Second messenger cyclic di-GMP (c-
CC       di-GMP) has been associated with controlling the transition between a
CC       motile and biofilm lifestyle; increased levels contribute to resistance
CC       of exponential-phase planktonic cells to tobramycin and norfloxacin
CC       (PubMed:24612375). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24612375,
CC       ECO:0000303|PubMed:22730129}.
