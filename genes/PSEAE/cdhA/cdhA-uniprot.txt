CC       hydroxybutyrate, L-lactate, ethanol, L-malate and D,L-isocitrate are
CC       not substrates (PubMed:4302217). Is involved in a L-carnitine
CC       degradation pathway that allows P.aeruginosa to grow on L-carnitine as
CC       the sole source of carbon and nitrogen (PubMed:19406895).
