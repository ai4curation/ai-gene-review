Gene Description

CdhA (PA5386) is the soluble NAD(+)-dependent L-carnitine dehydrogenase of Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. It oxidizes L-carnitine to 3-dehydrocarnitine at the entry to aerobic carnitine degradation. CdhC then forms betainyl-CoA and acetoacetate, and CdhB hydrolyzes betainyl-CoA to glycine betaine. The cdh locus is induced during growth on carnitine; PAO1 genetic evidence and curated biochemical assignments support the pathway. The cytoplasmic location is inferred from the curated enzyme family and intracellular reaction context.
