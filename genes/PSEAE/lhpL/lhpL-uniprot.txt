CC   -!- FUNCTION: Bifunctional enzyme catalyzing both the dehydration of trans-
CC       3-hydroxy-L-proline (t3LHyp) to Delta(1)-pyrroline-2-carboxylate
CC       (Pyr2C) and 2-epimerization of t3LHyp to cis-3-hydroxy-D-proline
CC       (c3DHyp). No dehydratase activity with L-proline, trans-4-hydroxy-L-
CC       proline (t4LHyp), cis-4-hydroxy-L-proline (c4LHyp), D-proline, cis-4-
CC       hydroxy-D-proline (c4DHyp), trans-4-hydroxy-D-proline (t4DHyp) or L-
CC       serine as substrates (PubMed:27929065). Displays neither t4LHyp
CC       epimerase nor proline racemase activity (PubMed:17849014). Is likely
CC       involved in a degradation pathway that converts t3LHyp to L-proline,
CC       which would allow P.aeruginosa to grow on t3LHyp as a sole carbon
CC       source (PubMed:24980702, PubMed:27929065).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17849014, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27929065,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:24980702}.
