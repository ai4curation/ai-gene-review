CC   -!- FUNCTION: Kinase involved in the biosynthesis of the core
CC       oligosaccharide region of lipopolysaccharide (LPS) (PubMed:11741974,
CC       PubMed:21810964, PubMed:30237436). Catalyzes the phosphorylation of
CC       heptose I (HepI), the first heptose added to the Kdo2-lipid A module
CC       (PubMed:11741974). Also has protein-tyrosine kinase activity:
CC       autophosphorylates on all Tyr residues; in vitro can phosphorylate
CC       poly(Glu,Tyr) (PubMed:11741974). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11741974,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21810964, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30237436}.
