CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic subunit of the Asp/Glu-tRNA(Asn/Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex GatCAB. GatCAB allows the formation of
CC       correctly charged Asn-tRNA(Asn) or Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the multi-step
CC       transamidation of misacylated Asp-tRNA(Asn) or Glu-tRNA(Gln) in
CC       organisms which lack either or both of asparaginyl-tRNA or glutaminyl-
CC       tRNA synthetases. The reaction takes place in the presence of glutamine
CC       and ATP through an activated phospho-Asp-tRNA(Asn) or phospho-Glu-
CC       tRNA(Gln). This subunit specifically recognizes tRNA(Gln) and
CC       tRNA(Asn), and mediates the transamidation of misloaded Glu and Asp
CC       respectively by catalyzing formation of the activated gamma-phospho-
CC       intermediate and its subsequent aminolysis using ammonia produced by
CC       the gatA subunit. Recognizes the first base pair and the D-loop of its
CC       specific tRNA substrates. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_00121}.
