Gene Description

cdhB (PA5385) encodes the betainyl-CoA thioesterase of Pseudomonas aeruginosa, a hotdog-fold acyl-CoA thioesterase (PANTHER PTHR31793 subfamily SF2) that hydrolyzes betainyl-CoA (N,N,N-trimethylglycyl-CoA) to glycine betaine and free coenzyme A. This is the last step of aerobic L-carnitine catabolism after CdhA and CdhC, and it regenerates the CoA consumed by CdhC. Despite the legacy name "betainyl-CoA thiolase", the protein is a hydrolase, not a thiolase. cdhB is induced by carnitine through CdhR and is required for growth on carnitine.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

betainyl-CoA thioesterase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: N,N,N-trimethylglycyl-CoA + H2O = glycine betaine + CoA + H+.

Justification: The EC 3.1.2.33 (RHEA:45716) activity is experimentally established for CdhB and for the C-terminal region of S. meliloti BcoE-BcoF, but GO has only the broader acyl-CoA hydrolase term.

Parent term: acyl-CoA hydrolase activity
