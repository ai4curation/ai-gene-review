CC   -!- FUNCTION: Displays c-di-GMP-specific phosphodiesterase (PDE) activity
CC       (PubMed:23729646). Seems to play a specific role in nitric oxide (NO)-
CC       induced biofilm dispersion (PubMed:23729646). Enhanced NbdA synthesis
CC       in the presence of NO increases PDE activity, leading to reduced
CC       cellular c-di-GMP levels and biofilm dispersion (PubMed:23729646). Does
CC       not show diguanylate cyclase (DGC) activity (PubMed:23729646).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23729646}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=3',3'-c-di-GMP + H2O = 5'-phosphoguanylyl(3'->5')guanosine +
CC         H(+); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:24902, ChEBI:CHEBI:15377, ChEBI:CHEBI:15378,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:58754, ChEBI:CHEBI:58805; EC=3.1.4.52;

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: PDE activity is stimulated by GTP
CC       (PubMed:23729646). It could also be stimulated by NO (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23729646, ECO:0000305|PubMed:23729646}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell inner membrane
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:23729646}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression is induced in response to nitric oxide (NO)
CC       (PubMed:23729646). Is probably only weakly expressed under normal
CC       conditions (PubMed:23729646). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23729646}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminal MHYT domain might sense NO through a bound metal
CC       ion. {ECO:0000305|PubMed:23729646}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Inactivation of the gene impairs biofilm
CC       dispersion upon exposure to NO but not dispersion in response to
CC       glutamate (PubMed:23729646). Disruption of the gene does not cause a
CC       biofilm-related phenotype under standard conditions (PubMed:16477007).
CC       Mutants exhibit an intermediate virulence phenotype (PubMed:16477007).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16477007, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23729646}.
