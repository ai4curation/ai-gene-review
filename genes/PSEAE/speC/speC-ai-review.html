Gene Description

SpeC (PA4519) is the ornithine decarboxylase of Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, a fold-type III pyridoxal 5'-phosphate decarboxylase of the ODC1-related subfamily that converts L-ornithine directly to putrescine and CO2. It provides one of two redundant putrescine-synthesis routes: genetic analysis showed that SpeC or the arginine decarboxylase SpeA alone supports normal growth, while a speA speC double mutant is a putrescine auxotroph. As a soluble PLP enzyme without export or membrane signals it is expected to act in the cytoplasm.
