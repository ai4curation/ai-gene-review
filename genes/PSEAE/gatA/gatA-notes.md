# PSEAE gatA: biochemical and native-context review

GatA is the glutaminase subunit of the GatCAB tRNA-dependent amidotransferase in Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1; it hydrolyzes glutamine to supply ammonia for transamidation. Its native aminoacyl-tRNA production role is conversion of Asp-tRNA(Asn) to Asn-tRNA(Asn). The purified complex can also use supplied heterologous Glu-tRNA(Gln), a biochemical capacity distinct from native substrate supply.
