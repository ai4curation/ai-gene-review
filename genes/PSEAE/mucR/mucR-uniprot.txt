CC   -!- FUNCTION: Displays both diguanylate cyclase (DGC) and c-di-GMP-specific
CC       phosphodiesterase (PDE) activity (PubMed:23729646). Probably modulates
CC       DGC and PDE activities, and thus c-di-GMP levels, in a growth mode-
CC       dependent manner (PubMed:23729646). May act as a PDE under planktonic
CC       growth conditions and as a DGC in biofilms (PubMed:23729646). During
CC       biofilm formation, it specifically activates alginate biosynthesis via
CC       generation of a localized c-di-GMP pool in the vicinity of the alginate
CC       biosynthesis protein Alg44 (PubMed:19088322).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19088322, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23729646}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Deletion of the gene in the mucoid alginate-
CC       overproducing strain PDO300 results in a nonmucoid phenotype and an

CC       and glutamate (PubMed:23729646). Deletion results in increased swimming
CC       motility (PubMed:28186120). Loss of the gene does not influence
CC       swarming motility (PubMed:19088322). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19088322,
