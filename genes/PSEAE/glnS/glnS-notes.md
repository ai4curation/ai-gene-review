# PSEAE glnS: source-based annotation review

GlnS supplies the direct glutaminyl-tRNA synthetase route in Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, ligating glutamine to tRNA(Gln) using ATP. It acts in cytoplasmic translation alongside a discriminating GluRS; the organism separately uses non-discriminating AspRS and GatCAB for Asn-tRNA(Asn) formation.

## Evidence and scope

The direct GlnRS route is established by homologous-tRNA charging in PAO1 extracts and fractionation, distinct from purification of the GluRS, AspRS and GatCAB proteins in the same paper.

- PMID:14729703: Direct glutaminyl-tRNA biosynthesis and indirect asparaginyl-tRNA biosynthesis in Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. Access: full cached text.
