CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transferase involved in the biosynthesis of the
CC       lipopolysaccharide (LPS) (PubMed:15838026, PubMed:17697256,
CC       PubMed:30047569). Catalyzes the transfer of a polymerized O-antigen
CC       molecule from its polyprenyl diphosphate membrane anchor to a terminal
CC       sugar of the lipid A-core oligosaccharide, finalizing the biosynthesis
CC       of the lipopolysaccharide (PubMed:15838026, PubMed:17697256,
CC       PubMed:30047569). Required for the attachment of both A-band and B-band
CC       O-antigens, two forms of O-antigen produced by P.aeruginosa, onto the
CC       lipid A-core receptors (PubMed:15838026, PubMed:17697256). Important
CC       for cell wall integrity and motility of the bacteria (PubMed:15838026).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15838026, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17697256,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30047569}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=a lipid-linked O antigen + a lipid A-core oligosaccharide = a
CC         lipopolysaccharide + a polyisoprenyl diphosphate.; EC=2.4.99.26;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:15838026, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17697256,
CC         ECO:0000269|PubMed:30047569};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activity does not require ATP and magnesium ions.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21983211, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30047569}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Bacterial outer membrane biogenesis; lipopolysaccharide
CC       biosynthesis. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15838026,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17697256}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17697256}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell inner membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20824106,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30047569}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20824106, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30047569}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Lipopolysaccharide (LPS) from this mutant is
CC       devoid of B-band O-polysaccharides and semirough (PubMed:15838026). The
CC       mutant is also deficient in the production of A-band polysaccharide, a
CC       homopolymer of D-rhamnose (PubMed:15838026). Mutation drastically
CC       affects the swimming and twitching motilities of the bacteria
CC       (PubMed:15838026). Reduced number of pili and/or flagella can be
CC       observed on the cell surfaces of the mutant bacteria compared to their
CC       respective wild-type parent strains (PubMed:15838026). A P.aeruginosa
CC       waaL mutant could not be cross-complemented by E.coli waaL, which
CC       suggests that each of these proteins has specificity for its cognate
CC       core oligosaccharide (PubMed:17697256). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15838026,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17697256}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Was originally reported to have ATPase activity,
CC       required for ligation as an energy source (PubMed:17697256). However,
CC       it was shown later that ligation occurs without ATP and magnesium ions,
CC       and that WaaL cannot catalyze ATP hydrolysis in vitro (PubMed:21983211,
CC       PubMed:30047569). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17697256,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21983211, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30047569}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the O-antigen ligase family. {ECO:0000305}.
