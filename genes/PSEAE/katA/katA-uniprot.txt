CC   -!- FUNCTION: The major expressed catalase protein in strain PAO1 in
CC       stationary phase (PubMed:10368148). Decomposes H(2)O(2) into water and
CC       oxygen; serves to protect cells from the toxic effects of H(2)O(2)
CC       (PubMed:10368148). May facilitate the transition from anaerobic to
CC       aerobic growth (which occurs during biofilm dispersion in infected
CC       patients) (PubMed:24663218). Binds nitric oxide (NO), protecting cells
CC       against this intermediate generated during anaerobic respiration
CC       (PubMed:24663218). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10368148,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24663218}.

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24663218}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Loss of detectable catalase activity in strain
CC       PAO1 in stationary phase during aerobic and anaerobic growth
CC       (PubMed:10368148, PubMed:24663218). Increase in sensitivity to
CC       H(2)O(2), a double katA-ftnA deletion is more sensitive to H(2)O(2)
CC       (PubMed:10368148). When grown in iron-limiting conditions is less
CC       sensitive to H(2)O(2) (PubMed:10368148). Increased sensitivity to
CC       nitric oxide (NO) donor A-NO(2)- in aerobic and anaerobic culture,
CC       decreased biofilm formation (PubMed:24663218).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10368148, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24663218}.
