# PA3175 / hutE (Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, Q9HZ59) notes

## 2026-11-11

- Deep research was not run for this gene in this session; no provider file
  exists. The review rests on the reviewed UniProt record and the cached
  abstracts of PMID:16475788 and PMID:22225844.
- Function: formimidoylglutamase (FIGLU to L-glutamate + formamide), measured
  on the purified protein [PMID:16475788 "The protein Pa3175 is dislocated from the hut operon and was shown to catalyze the hydrolysis of N-formimino-L-glutamate to formamide and L-glutamate."].
- Genetics: the HutE (four-step) and HutFG (five-step) branches are each
  sufficient [PMID:22225844 "Genetic analysis revealed that the four-step route is dispensable as long as the five-step route is present (and vice versa)."].
- Family PTHR11358:SF35 (FORMIMIDOYLGLUTAMASE). The agmatinase and putrescine
  IBAs from PTN000144486 are over-propagations into this diverged subfamily
  and are removed, as for B. subtilis HutG.
- Exemplar for the one-step formamide route in the histidine catabolism module.
