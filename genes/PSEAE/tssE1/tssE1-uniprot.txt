CC   -!- FUNCTION: Core component of the H1 type VI (H1-T6SS) secretion system
CC       that plays a role in the release of toxins targeting both eukaryotic
CC       and prokaryotic species. Plays a role in correct elongation and
CC       stability of TssB1-sheath (PubMed:21873404, PubMed:22906320). Functions
CC       downstream of TssK1 and prevents detachment of TssB1-sheath and its
CC       premature disassembly. Once bound onto the sheath via an interaction
CC       with TssB1, provides an ideal docking point for ClpV1 to be recruited
CC       to the sheath (PubMed:31379775). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21873404,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22906320, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31379775}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with TssB1 (via N-terminus) (PubMed:22906320).
CC       Interacts with TssA1 (PubMed:27288401). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22906320,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27288401}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21873404}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Deletion mutant does not prevent TssB1 assembly
CC       into a sheath-like structure. However, the sheath exhibits an aberrant
CC       phenotype and break itself in smaller pieces.
