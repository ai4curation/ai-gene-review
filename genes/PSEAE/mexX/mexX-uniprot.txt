CC   -!- FUNCTION: Periplasmic adapter component of the MexXY-OprM efflux system
CC       that confers multidrug resistance (PubMed:10543738, PubMed:11083635,
CC       PubMed:15325256). Functions as part of a major efflux pump for
CC       quinolones, macrolides, tetracyclines, lincomycin, chloramphenicol,
CC       aminoglycosides and penicillins (PubMed:10543738, PubMed:11083635).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10543738, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11083635,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15325256}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the MexXY-OprM multidrug efflux complex, composed
CC       of MexX, MexY and OprM. {ECO:0000305|PubMed:15325256}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell inner membrane {ECO:0000305|PubMed:11083635,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:15325256}; Lipid-anchor {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-
CC       ProRule:PRU00303, ECO:0000305}. Periplasm
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:15325256}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Part of the mexX-mexY operon (PubMed:10543738). Up-regulated
CC       by antibiotic tetracycline (at protein level) (PubMed:19589837).
CC       Repressed by transcription factor mexZ; MexZ binds to a 20 bp
CC       palindromic sequence in a putative promoter in the mexZ-mexXY
CC       intergenic region (PubMed:19589837). Expression almost undetectable in
CC       the absence of antibiotic (at protein level) (PubMed:19589837).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10543738, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19589837}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the membrane fusion protein (MFP) (TC 8.A.1)
CC       family. {ECO:0000305}.
