CC   -!- FUNCTION: Structural subunit of the Asp/Glu-tRNA(Asn/Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex GatCAB. GatCAB allows the formation of
CC       correctly charged Asn-tRNA(Asn) or Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the multi-step
CC       transamidation of misacylated Asp-tRNA(Asn) or Glu-tRNA(Gln) in
CC       organisms which lack either or both of asparaginyl-tRNA or glutaminyl-
CC       tRNA synthetases. The reaction takes place in the presence of glutamine
CC       and ATP through an activated phospho-Asp-tRNA(Asn) or phospho-Glu-
CC       tRNA(Gln). This subunit forms a belt that wraps around the interface of
CC       the catalytic subunits A and B to stabilize the complex.
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_00122}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the heterotrimeric GatCAB glutamyl-tRNA(Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex composed of GatA, GatB and GatC.
