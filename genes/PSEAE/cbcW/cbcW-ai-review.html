Gene Description

CbcW (PA5377) is the multipass inner-membrane permease of the shared Cbc ABC importer in Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. It forms the translocation component with the CbcV ATPase and recruits substrate-binding proteins that confer specificity for choline, glycine betaine or carnitine. Heterologous combinations with CosX also support choline-O-sulfate uptake. Substrate recognition and transport require compatible partners; CbcW does not itself hydrolyze ATP or chemically degrade these nutrients.
