# PSEAE gltX: source-based annotation review

GltX is the discriminating glutamyl-tRNA synthetase of Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. It activates glutamate with ATP and transfers it to tRNA(Glu). The characterized enzyme does not glutamylate tRNA(Gln), consistent with direct Gln-tRNA(Gln) synthesis by GlnS in this organism. A conserved zinc-binding region is supported by curated sequence-family inference.

## Evidence and scope

The discriminating substrate preference is measured in the PAO1 enzyme, whereas zinc occupancy is a target sequence-family inference. The E. coli zinc-depletion study is comparative support, not a PAO1 metal-stoichiometry assay.

- PMID:14729703: Direct glutaminyl-tRNA biosynthesis and indirect asparaginyl-tRNA biosynthesis in Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. Access: full cached text.
- PMID:8218204: The glutamyl-tRNA synthetase of Escherichia coli contains one atom of zinc essential for its native conformation and its catalytic activity. Access: abstract only.
