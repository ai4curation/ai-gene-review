CC   -!- FUNCTION: Periplasmic adapter component of the MexAB-OprM efflux system
CC       that confers multidrug resistance (PubMed:25901994, PubMed:30944318,
CC       PubMed:33009415, PubMed:8226684). The MexAB-OprM efflux system has a
CC       broad substrate specificity, acting as a substrate:proton antiporter,
CC       using proton motive force to export substrates (Probable). MexA is
CC       elongated in shape, spanning about half of the periplasm, linking MexB
CC       and OprM stably together, perhaps promoted by binding to peptidoglycan
CC       (PubMed:33009415, PubMed:34070225). Functions as the major efflux pump
CC       for n-hexane and p-xylene efflux (PubMed:9603892). Has been shown in
CC       one study to be involved in the active efflux of the autoinducer N-(3-
CC       oxododecanoyl) homoserine lactone, thereby playing an indirect role in
CC       quorum-sensing; but has been shown in another study not to be involved
CC       in efflux of this autoinducer (PubMed:32715566, PubMed:9973347). Over-
CC       expression of the pump increases antibiotic and solvent efflux
CC       capacities (PubMed:8540696). Implicated in the secretion of the
CC       siderophore pyoverdine (PubMed:7968531, PubMed:8226684).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25901994, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30944318,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32715566, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33009415,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34070225, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8226684,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8540696, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9603892,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9973347, ECO:0000303|PubMed:7968531,
CC       ECO:0000303|PubMed:8226684, ECO:0000305|PubMed:25901994,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:8540696}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Export of antibiotics and solvents is dramatically
CC       decreased in the presence of the protonophore carbonyl cyanide m-
CC       chlorophenylhydrazone (CCCP), therefore probably driven by a proton
CC       gradient (PubMed:25901994, PubMed:8540696). Antibiotic efflux is
CC       inhibited by pyridopyrimidine derivatives, such as ABI-PP, acting by
CC       binding to a hydrophobic pocket in MexB (PubMed:23812586).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23812586, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25901994,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8540696}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the MexAB-OprM multidrug efflux complex, composed
CC       of six MexA subunits forming a hexameric tube, binding to a MexB
CC       trimer, which interact with the trimeric OprM outer membrane channel
CC       protein (PubMed:15117957, PubMed:15226509, PubMed:15325256,
CC       PubMed:30944318). OprM is thought to not directly contact MexB;
CC       instead, MexA joins MexB and OprM by forming a funnel-like hexamer
CC       anchored to the inner membrane (PubMed:15117957, PubMed:15226509,
CC       PubMed:30944318). MexA may initially form a hexameric ring complex with
CC       MexB prior to OprM, then OprM undergoes a conformational change as it
CC       contacts MexA, allowing the periplasmic gate to open (PubMed:25901994,
CC       PubMed:30944318, PubMed:33009415). It is thought that, under high
CC       intracellular substrate concentration, MexB ejects substrate into the
CC       tunnel formed by MexA-OprM; as the substrate level declines,
CC       conformational changes in MexB cause efflux to reduce and stop and the
CC       complex shifts to the closed state (PubMed:30944318, PubMed:33009415).
CC       MexB subunit acts as a substrate:proton antiporter and activity is
CC       enhanced significantly when in complex with MexA and OprM, in vitro
CC       (PubMed:25901994, PubMed:33009415). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15117957,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15226509, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15325256,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25901994, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30944318,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33009415}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell inner membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10671490}; Lipid-anchor {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-
CC       ProRule:PRU00303, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10671490}. Note=The membrane
CC       anchor is not necessary for antibiotic efflux as the protein is
CC       functional when targeted to the periplasm by a foreign signal peptide.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: By growth under severe iron limitation.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Disruption of MexA, MexB and OprM significantly
CC       reduces active efflux of N-(3-oxododecanoyl) homoserine lactone/3-oxo-
CC       C12-HSL/PAI-1, but not N-butyryl homoserine lactone/C4-HSL/PAI-2.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9973347}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: In both X-ray crystal structures approximately the last
CC       100 residues are not ordered.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the membrane fusion protein (MFP) (TC 8.A.1)
CC       family. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- CAUTION: Has been shown in one study to be involved in the active
CC       efflux of the autoinducer N-(3-oxododecanoyl) homoserine lactone
CC       (PubMed:9973347). However, has been shown in another study not to be
CC       involved in efflux of this autoinducer (PubMed:32715566).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32715566, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9973347}.
