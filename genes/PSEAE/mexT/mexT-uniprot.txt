CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription regulator (PubMed:10515918, PubMed:31722511).
CC       Modulates quorum-sensing (QS) signals, acting, at least in part, by
CC       activating the MexEF-OprN efflux pump and repressing pqsE expression
CC       (PubMed:39708318, PubMed:41128522). Activates transcription from
CC       promoters of the MexEF-OprN operon and of the mexS/qrh gene
CC       (PubMed:10515918, PubMed:20727012, PubMed:31722511). Binds to a 15 bp
CC       sequence motif similar to DNA sequence 5'-ATCA(N7)CGAT-3' in regulatory
CC       elements of target genes (PubMed:20727012, PubMed:31722511). Plays a
CC       role in norfloxacin, imipenem, and chloramphenicol resistance
CC       (PubMed:10515918). Probably also down-regulates porin oprD expression
CC       at the post-transcriptional level and thereby confers increased
CC       resistance to imipenem (PubMed:10515918, PubMed:9044268). Plays a role
CC       in virulence (PubMed:39708318, PubMed:41128522). Involved in repressing
CC       expression of virulence factor exoenzyme S, part of the type III
CC       secretion system (T3SS) (PubMed:21075931).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10515918, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20727012,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21075931, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31722511,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39708318, ECO:0000269|PubMed:41128522,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9044268}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotetramer. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31722511}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Abolishes transcription from the mexE promoter
CC       (PubMed:21075931). Increases expression of pqsE (PubMed:41128522).
CC       Increases production of rhamnolipids; phenotype is complemented by
CC       wild-type mexT (PubMed:41128522). Decreases total concentration of
CC       quorum-sensing (QS) signal 2-heptyl-4-quinolone (HHQ) compared with
CC       wild-type (PubMed:41128522). Activity and timing of expression of LasR
CC       during growth are similar to wild-type, whereas RhlR exhibits earlier
CC       and higher activity (PubMed:41128522). Activity of PqsR QS signal
CC       occurs earlier during growth (PubMed:41128522). Approximately 10-fold
CC       increase in Pseudomonas quinolone signal (PQS) concentrations compared
CC       with wild-type in stationary phase (PubMed:39708318, PubMed:41128522).
CC       Increases mortality in C.elegans after culture of L4 larval stage with
CC       deletion mutant (PubMed:41128522). Increases exo-protease activity
CC       (PubMed:39708318). Increases pyocyanine production (PubMed:39708318).
CC       Decreases resistance to ciprofloxacin, but increases aminoglycoside and
CC       colistin resistance; increased colistin resistance is complemented
CC       fully by wild-type mexT, but not F-130 mutant (PubMed:39708318).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21075931, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39708318,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:41128522}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Mutations in mexT are common in P.aeruginosa isolates
CC       from diverse sources, including people with cystic fibrosis (CF),
CC       urinary tract infections, wounds, burns, keratitis, plants, rivers,
CC       soil, and animals (PubMed:39708318). Most frequently affected residues
CC       in clinical CF isolates are all located in or near to the putative
CC       ligand-binding pocket (PubMed:39708318). Mutations in MexT also arise
CC       spontaneously at high frequency in laboratory PAO1 sublines;
CC       unfortunately, this leads to genetic and phenotypic variability that
CC       may affect the reproducibility and validity of research
CC       (PubMed:31859619). Based on sequence comparison of different
CC       P.aeruginosa strains and PAO1 sublines, it seems likely that the
CC       prototypic MexT sequence is shared by PA14, PAK and LES400 strains
CC       (Probable). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31859619, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39708318,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:31859619}.
