# PSEAE aspS: source-based annotation review

AspS is the non-discriminating aspartyl-tRNA synthetase of Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1. It forms Asp-tRNA(Asp) and the Asp-tRNA(Asn) intermediate used by GatCAB to generate Asn-tRNA(Asn). A tRNA-dependent association with GatCAB facilitates intermediate handoff; the bacterial GAD domain contributes to the dynamic architecture of this transamidosome.

## Evidence and scope

PMID:14729703 studies PAO1 enzyme purified after homologous-host overproduction. PMID:25548166 provides the reconstructed transamidosome structure and kinetics; crystallographic assembly is not a claim that every enzyme molecule occupies a permanent native complex.
