# thiI (PA5118, Q9HU66) notes

## Identity

- tRNA sulfurtransferase ThiI, 484 aa, Swiss-Prot entry annotated entirely by
  HAMAP-Rule MF_00021 (no P. aeruginosa experimental evidence in UniProt).
- Domain architecture (UniProt FT, HAMAP): N-terminal ferredoxin-like domain,
  THUMP domain 63..167 (tRNA binding), PP-loop/AANH adenylation domain (ATP
  binding 185..186, 267, 289, 298), and a C-terminal rhodanese domain 405..483
  with the persulfide active-site Cys457 and the redox-active Cys346-Cys457
  disulfide. NCBIfam TIGR04271 (ThiI_C_thiazole) and InterPro IPR026340
  (thiazole biosynthesis domain) match: P. aeruginosa ThiI is the long,
  rhodanese-containing "E. coli / Salmonella type" ThiI.
- Pa Cys457 and Cys346 correspond to E. coli Cys456 and Cys344
  [PMID:10722656 "Mutation of Cys-456 to alanine impairs this sulfurtransferase activity, and the C456A ThiI is incapable of supporting generation of 4-thiouridine in tRNA both in vitro and in vivo."]
  [PMID:11443125 "Substitution of Cys-344, the only cysteine residue conserved among all sequenced ThiI, resulted in the loss of function in vivo and a 2700-fold reduction in activity measured in vitro."]

## Function (family knowledge; enteric orthologues)

- 4-thiouridine (s4U8) in tRNA: ThiI adenylates U8 and transfers the IscS
  persulfide sulfur
  [PMID:10753862 "the sulfane sulfur generated by IscS is transferred sequentially to ThiI and then to tRNA during the in vitro synthesis of s(4)U"]
  [PMID:10753862 "we show that ThiI binds to unmodified E. coli tRNA(Phe)"]
- Thiazole moiety of thiamine: Salmonella thiI mutants require thiazole
  [PMID:9209060 "We present herein the initial characterization of thiI, a gene required for THZ synthesis."]
  and the rhodanese domain alone suffices for this role
  [PMID:21724998 "the C-terminal rhodanese domain of ThiI was sufficient for thiazole synthesis in vivo"]
  [PMID:21724998 "ThiI residues required for 4-thiouridine synthesis were not involved in thiazole biosynthesis"]
  Mechanism of sulfur delivery to ThiS is not settled:
  [PMID:21724998 "Indeed, in vivo results suggest that thiI is required for the formation of the ThiS thiocarboxylate ( 24 ), although ThiI was not required for the in vitro formation of the ThiS thiocarboxylate ( 31 )."]
- The thiazole role is lineage-specific. ThiI without the rhodanese domain
  (Firmicutes, many archaea) makes s4U but is not needed for thiamine:
  [PMID:22773787 "Gene inactivation experiments in B. subtilis indicate the requirement of ThiI and NifZ for the biosynthesis of 4-thiouridine, but not thiamine."]
  [PMID:22904325 "deletion of ThiI (MMP1354) abolished the biosynthesis of s(4)U but not of thiamine"]
  [PMID:21724998 "proteins annotated as ThiI based on containing only the AANH and THUMP domains are unlikely to be involved in thiazole synthesis and would be better annotated as 4-thiouridine sulfurtransferases"]

## Pseudomonas evidence

- No P. aeruginosa thiI paper found (PubMed: thiI AND Pseudomonas in
  title/abstract returns nothing).
- P. putida KT2440 thiI deletion: [PMID:37891428 "As expected, the content of 4-thiouridine in bulk tRNA was drastically reduced in the ΔthiI knockout strain (negative control)."]
  This is the closest genus-level experimental support (s4U only; thiamine not
  tested).

## The PseudoCAP seed, PMID:9209060

- Webb, Claas & Downs 1997 J Bacteriol. The title and abstract are about
  Salmonella typhimurium: [PMID:9209060 "Thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP) is a required cofactor in Salmonella typhimurium"].
  Full text is not in PMC text form (PMC179266 returned no body). The study is
  genetic (thiI mutant, thiazole requirement, mapping, ORF sequence,
  transcriptional regulation). Nothing in the abstract or title mentions
  P. aeruginosa, and a 1997 Salmonella genetics paper cannot have assayed
  PA5118 (PAO1 genome published 2000). The PseudoCAP IDA is therefore an
  orthology transfer coded as direct evidence; the correct code would be ISS
  (with Salmonella ThiI), or the row should be dropped.
- The term itself is plausible for PA5118, because PA5118 has the rhodanese
  domain and Cys457 that Salmonella needs for thiazole synthesis.

## PAINT node PTN001804941

- PTN001804941 is the root (AN0) of PTHR43209 (ThiI family; LUCA-level
  speciation node per projects/TREEGRAFTER/graft_paths_all.tsv). The family
  file lists 289 members, mostly Firmicutes (Streptococcus, Bacillus,
  Staphylococcus, Listeria, Clostridium, Lactobacillus, Mycoplasma) and
  archaea (Saccharolobus, Methanocaldococcus, Methanosarcina, Thermococcus,
  Halobacterium), plus Gammaproteobacteria. 165/289 members are <=440 aa,
  i.e. short ThiI lacking the rhodanese domain.
- IBDs at this node: GO:0002937, GO:0016783, GO:1990228 seeded by E. coli
  P77718 (sound; E. coli has EXP/IMP/IDA for s4U and sulfurtransferase), and
  GO:0052837 thiazole biosynthetic process seeded only by Q9HU66.
- E. coli ThiI itself has GO:0009228 thiamine biosynthetic process IMP
  (PMID:9632726, EcoCyc) in GOA, so a genuine experimental seed for a
  thiamine/thiazole IBD exists, but it belongs at a rhodanese-domain
  (Proteobacteria) subclade, not the family root.

## Decisions summary

- ACCEPT: tRNA 4-thiouridine biosynthesis (IBA), tRNA-uracil-4
  sulfurtransferase activity (IEA), tRNA binding (IEA), cytoplasm (IEA).
- MODIFY: sulfurtransferase activity (IBA, IEA) -> tRNA-uracil-4
  sulfurtransferase activity; RNA binding -> tRNA binding; tRNA
  thio-modification -> tRNA 4-thiouridine biosynthesis; sulfur compound
  biosynthetic process -> thiazole biosynthetic process.
- KEEP_AS_NON_CORE: thiazole biosynthetic process (IDA, IBA, IEA; plausible by
  domain architecture, unshown in P. aeruginosa), thiamine diphosphate
  biosynthetic process, ATP binding, sulfurtransferase complex.
- REMOVE: CCA tRNA nucleotidyltransferase activity (InterPro2GO mapping of the
  AANH/PP-loop domain; ThiI is not a CCA-adding enzyme).
