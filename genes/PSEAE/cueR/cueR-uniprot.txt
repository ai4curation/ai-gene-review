CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional regulator involved in copper homeostasis
CC       (PubMed:20233934, PubMed:30718281, PubMed:31790504). Acts as a
CC       metallosensor (PubMed:30718281). In response to high levels of copper,
CC       it activates the expression of 11 genes, which are grouped into 5
CC       transcriptional units: flcABCDE (PA3515-PA3519), copZ1 (PA3520), mexPQ-
CC       opmE (PA3521-PA3523), copZ2 (PA3574.1) and cueA (PubMed:20233934).
CC       Under copper rich conditions, it also represses the expression of the
CC       H2 type IV secretion system (H2-T6SS), of azu, which encodes the H2-
CC       T6SS-dependent copper-binding effector azurin and of oprC, the azurin-
CC       interacting partner (PubMed:31790504). Acts by binding directly to the
CC       promoter region of its targets (PubMed:20233934, PubMed:31790504).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20233934, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30718281,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31790504}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by metallation (PubMed:30718281).
CC       Activation of CueR occurs via direct Cu(+) transfer from the CopZ metal
CC       chaperones (PubMed:30718281). CueR receives Cu(+) from both CopZ1 and
CC       CopZ2 in vitro, while experiments in vivo indicate that it is
CC       metallated mainly through Cu(+) delivery from CopZ1 (PubMed:30718281).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30718281}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:38776258). Homotetramer (PubMed:38776258).
CC       Can exist both as a dimer as well as a tetramer in solution and in the
CC       protein-DNA complexes (PubMed:38776258). Interacts with the metal
CC       chaperones CopZ1 and CopZ2 in vitro (PubMed:30718281). Interaction with
CC       CopZ1 is stronger and independent of Cu(+) (PubMed:30718281).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30718281, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38776258}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000305|PubMed:30718281,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:31790504}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Transcriptionaly regulated by the quorum-sensing global
CC       regulator LasR, when triggered by its cognate autoinducer, 3OC(12)-HSL
CC       (PubMed:20233934). LasR directly binds to the cueR promoter region
CC       (PubMed:20233934). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20233934}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: It contains an N-terminal DNA binding region and a C-terminal
CC       metal binding region. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25342258}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Deletion of the gene increases the sensitivity of
CC       P.aeruginosa to copper (PubMed:20233934). Deletion does not affect
CC       sensitivity to silver, nickel, cadmium, iron, mercury, zinc, cobalt and
CC       manganese (PubMed:20233934). It increases the levels of Hcp2 and TssA2,
CC       but not of Hcp1 or Hcp3 (PubMed:31790504). The deletion mutant
CC       significantly increases the killing of prey cells (E.coli) relative to
CC       the parent control (PubMed:31790504). It also significantly increases
CC       mouse survival in a mouse model of acute pneumonia (PubMed:31790504).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20233934, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31790504}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: The transcription mechanism of CueR depends not only on
CC       the DNA sequence, but also on the species itself. Distinct differences
CC       are detected between the mechanism of E.coli CueR versus the mechanism
CC       of P.aeruginosa CueR. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38776258}.
