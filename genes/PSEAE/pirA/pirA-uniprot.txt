CC   -!- FUNCTION: Specific receptor for the siderophore ferric enterobactin
CC       (PubMed:15899402). Probably involved in the transport of siderophores,
CC       including host catecholamines such as L-DOPA (PubMed:33350053).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15899402, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33350053}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell outer membrane {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-
CC       ProRule:PRU01360}; Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-
CC       ProRule:PRU01360}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Deletion decreases sensitivity to siderophore-
CC       beta-lactam drug conjugates (PubMed:28137795). In a pvdD and pchEF
CC       double mutant background, in iron restricted culture, able to grow in
CC       the presence of enterobactin or various synthetic catecholates, but
CC       fails to grow when enterobactin receptor pfeA is also deleted
CC       (PubMed:15899402). In a pvdF and pchA double mutant background, in iron
CC       restricted culture, growth inhibited by 60% in the presence of dopamine
CC       and delayed in the presence of other catecholamines such as
CC       epinephrine; growth is inhibited further, but not completely abolished,
CC       when piuA is aso deleted (PubMed:33350053).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15899402, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28137795,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33350053}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the TonB-dependent receptor family.
