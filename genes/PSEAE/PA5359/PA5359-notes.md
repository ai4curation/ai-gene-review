### What PMID:14574114 is about

- The abstract names the gene products that were characterised:
  [PMID:14574114 "Heterologous expression showed that the two alkane hydroxylase
  homologs of P. aeruginosa PAO1 (AlkB1 and AlkB2) oxidize C(12)-C(16)n-alkanes,
  while two rubredoxin (RubA1 and RubA2) and a rubredoxin reductase (RubB)
  homologs can replace their P. putida GPo1 counterparts in n-octane
  oxidation."] and the distribution of the hydroxylase genes
  [PMID:14574114 "The two long-chain alkane hydroxylase genes are present in all
  environmental and clinical isolates of P. aeruginosa strains tested in this
  study."].
- Those five genes have PAO1 loci and UniProt entries of their own: alkB1 =
  PA2574 (Q9I0R2), alkB2 = PA1525 (Q6H941), rubA1/alkG1 = PA5351 (Q9HTK7),
  rubA2/alkG2 = PA5350 (Q9HTK8), rubB/alkT = PA5349 (Q9HTK9) (UniProt query).
  PA5359 is not one of them; it lies about ten loci away, between ubiA (PA5358)
  and phoB (PA5360).
- A QuickGO query for annotations keyed to PMID:14574114 returns exactly one
  row in the whole database: the PseudoCAP IMP on Q9HTJ9 (PA5359). None of the
  five genes the abstract actually describes is annotated from this paper; their
  GO:0043448 rows come from UniProt keyword mapping (GO_REF:0000041) instead.
- A single annotation from an alkane-oxidation paper, landing on a gene the
  abstract does not mention while the five it does mention get nothing, looks
  like a locus-tag slip - PA5349 (rubB/alkT) differs from PA5359 by one digit.
  This cannot be confirmed without the full text, so it is recorded as a
  `SUSPECTED` source defect with `requested_change: CHECK` rather than asserted.

### PAINT node (PA5359-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md)

PTN002217427 is the root of PTHR39335 (Eubacteria speciation node, 7 leaves in
5 organisms): two *Streptomyces coelicolor* lipoproteins (SCO0930, SCO7673),
*Gloeobacter violaceus* glr0303, two *Bradyrhizobium diazoefficiens* proteins
(bll4220, bll5559), a *Solanum lycopersicum* "lipoprotein with Yx(FWY)xxD
motif", and PA5359. The GO:0043448 IBD is sole-seeded by PA5359 and GOA now
holds 764 rows with this node in with/from (7 GO_Central and 757 TreeGrafter).

Whatever the status of the seed, the propagation is not defensible on its own
terms: alkane catabolism requires an alkane hydroxylase system, and this family
is a set of small, signal-peptide-bearing lipoprotein-repeat proteins with no
such component, spanning actinobacteria, a cyanobacterium, an
alphaproteobacterium and a flowering plant. A tomato protein annotated to
alkane catabolic process by descent from a *P. aeruginosa* mutant phenotype is a
clear taxon and role mismatch.
