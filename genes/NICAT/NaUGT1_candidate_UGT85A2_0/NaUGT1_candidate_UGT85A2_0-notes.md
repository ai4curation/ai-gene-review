## 2026-09-20 primary-evidence adjudication follow-up

The focused OpenScientist report was read in full, including its sequence-analysis code. Its negative verdict is contradicted by two primary 2026 studies, not merely by a preferred pathway model. [PMID:42151135, Nicotine biosynthesis is completed by cryptic activating glucosylation] explicitly identifies tobacco UGT1 as UGT709L18 and its supplements map the assayed enzyme to Nitab4.5_0006222g0020.1 / A0A1S3YWH6. That is the very accession the report called an O-glucosyltransferase on the strength of an automated name. Published coding/expressed sequences give a 479-aa native protein, 466 identical positions over 485 global-alignment columns against this target.

[PMID:41928514, Complete biosynthesis of nicotine] provides direct N. attenuata recombinant enzyme and genetic evidence. Independent mapping of its cloning, qPCR, VIGS and sequencing primers identifies g26396. The primer-compatible reconstructed protein contains all 485 target residues unchanged after a 45-aa N-terminal extension. This supports the gene-level reaction and nicotine-pathway assignment; it does not demonstrate activity of the isolated 485-aa form or establish the dominant native start site. See the reproducible bioinformatics/identity-cell.md and RESULTS.md files for exact source URLs, rows, genomic coordinates, hashes and negative-control results.

The previous withdrawal of the nicotine-process NEW was provisional. It is now reinstated with primary IDA support and the explicit construct caveat. GO:0050139 already represents nicotinate-N-glucosyltransferase activity, so the generic UDP-glucosyltransferase row is MODIFY and the duplicative custom term is removed. The process participation check identifies NaUGT1 itself as the catalyst; QuickGO comparators PMT1/PMT3, QPT2 and A622 carry nicotine biosynthesis, and the local GO-CAM index has no target model. Alternative quercetin/iridoid substrate claims remain UNDECIDED because no target-specific negative substrate panel was found.

The report also overlooked characterized plant nicotinate enzymes in [PMID:31611893], mislabeled average-linkage clustering as neighbor joining, and used an identity implementation that can omit terminal overhangs. The report is preserved and marked DISPUTED, with its pathway-exclusion finding OVERTURNED. No duplicate adjudication is needed for the same question.
