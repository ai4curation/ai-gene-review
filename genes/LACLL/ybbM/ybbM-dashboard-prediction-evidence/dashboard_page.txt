why
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by a specific nickel-stress fitness phenotype, with convergent homolog, domain, neighborhood, topology, and STRING evidence supporting an ABC transporter permease. Literature evidence largely measures expression rather than transport activity; cofitness and direct substrate-transport assays are absent. Iron export is retained as putative because FetB annotations and recurring FetA/YbbL associations support it; nickel-specific export is rejected because nickel sensitivity does not identify the transported substrate. The inconsistent bdhA cassette summary does not support assigning oxidoreductase activity.

3 terms
why these terms
The protein is identified as an ABC transporter permease. The trace confirms that evidence from homologs, domains, and topology supports this function. While the annotation mentions 'iron-export', the trace explicitly states this is 'retained as putative'. However, it is the most specific transport activity supported. The trace explicitly rejects 'nickel-specific export' (stating that nickel sensitivity doesn't identify the substrate), so I will not assign nickel transport. 'Nickel tolerance' is mentioned as a phenotype, which corresponds to 'stress response to nickel ion'.
