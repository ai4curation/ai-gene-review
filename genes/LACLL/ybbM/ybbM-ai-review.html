Gene Description

This plasmid-borne Lactococcus lactis gene encodes a 251-residue integral membrane protein of the UPF0014 (YbbM/FetB) family, with seven predicted transmembrane helices and the UPF0014 domain over almost its whole length. It is the permease (transmembrane) half of a two-component bacterial ABC transporter. The adjacent gene encodes an ABC ATP-binding protein 47% identical to Escherichia coli FetA, and the two genes form a cassette carried on lactococcal plasmids among insertion-sequence elements; strain 275 plasmid p275A carries two identical copies. The protein is 59% identical to E. coli FetB, whose FetAB transporter lowers intracellular iron when overexpressed and protects against hydrogen peroxide, and which is proposed to export iron. It falls below the trusted cutoff of the NCBIfam FetB equivalog model, however, and other UPF0014 members transport different substrates. The distant plant homolog STAR2 with its partner STAR1 exports UDP-glucose for aluminium tolerance. The substrate of the lactococcal transporter is therefore unknown. No experiment has been done on this protein or any lactococcal member of the family; expression of streptococcal homologs falls after heat or nitric oxide exposure.

Core Functions

Transmembrane (permease) subunit of a two-component, plasmid-encoded ABC transporter of the UPF0014/FetAB type, working with the adjacent ABC ATP-binding protein. The transported substrate is unknown; iron export is a hypothesis based on E. coli FetAB.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does a strain cured of the plasmid cassette (or a deletion of both copies) accumulate more iron, or become more sensitive to iron, nickel or H2O2, than its parent?

Q: Does the lactococcal permease/ATPase pair complement the H2O2 sensitivity of an E. coli fetAB deletion, or the aluminium sensitivity of a plant star1/star2 or als3 mutant?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Delete both cassette copies in strain 275 (or cure plasmid p275A), then measure intracellular Fe, Ni, Mn and Zn by ICP-MS and growth under iron, nickel and H2O2 stress, with complementation from a plasmid carrying one permease/ATPase pair.

Type: Metal content and sensitivity of a deletion strain

Experiment: Express the lactococcal permease and ATPase in an E. coli ΔfetAB Δfur background and test H2O2 survival and intracellular iron (EPR or ICP-MS), alongside E. coli fetAB as the positive control.

Type: Heterologous complementation

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: The substrate of the lactococcal UPF0014 ABC transporter is unknown. Iron export is shown only indirectly for E. coli FetAB (lower intracellular iron on overexpression), and the plant member of the family exports UDP-glucose; the lactococcal protein falls outside the FetB equivalog. No transport assay, metal-sensitivity test or deletion phenotype exists for any lactococcal member.

BIOLOGY

Gap: Why lactococcal plasmids carry this transporter, sometimes in two copies among IS elements, is not known; no phenotype has been linked to plasmid carriage of the cassette.
