Gene Description

PhaZ contributes to intracellular medium-chain-length poly(3-hydroxyalkanoate) mobilization in strain GPo1, currently classified as Ectopseudomonas oleovorans. It is an alpha/beta-hydrolase associated genetically with degradation of stored polyester. A leaky phaZ mutant retains polymer longer, and a phaC1/phaZ-containing fragment restores turnover-associated phenotypes. Polymer mobilization supports carbon and energy availability during nutrient limitation.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

medium-chain-length poly(3-hydroxyalkanoate) depolymerase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the hydrolysis of ester bonds in medium-chain-length poly(3-hydroxyalkanoate), yielding shorter polyester chains and hydroxyalkanoate products.

Justification: GO:0050527 specifies poly(3-hydroxyoctanoate) with products of size1–5, whereas the GPo1 evidence establishes turnover of native mixed medium-chain-length polyester without a product-resolved purified assay. The broader ester-hydrolase core is supported without asserting those unmeasured details.

Parent term: hydrolase activity, acting on ester bonds
