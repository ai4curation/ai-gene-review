# THET2 TT_C0087: source-bounded annotation review

TT_C0087 encodes the Thermus thermophilus HB27 IscS cysteine desulfurase, a pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent enzyme that mobilizes sulfur from L-cysteine. This biochemical donor capacity does not establish that IscS is the sole native donor or exclude intervening carriers in the cell.
