# THET2 TT_C1373: source-bounded annotation review

TT_C1373 encodes the Thermus thermophilus HB27 SufS cysteine desulfurase. It uses pyridoxal phosphate to mobilize sulfur from L-cysteine, and purified SufS supports C2 thiolation of tRNA wobble uridine by reconstituted MnmA. Its physiological allocation between sulfur pathways and its relative contribution compared with IscS remain unresolved. The same named donor also enhances the separate TtuA/TtuB-dependent tRNA U54 thiolation reaction in vitro.
