# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1.5 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1.5` (Protein 1.5; gp1.5)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03792`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 29 AA (~3.1 kDa)

## Sequence Features and Expression
- **Small uncharacterized Class II peptide**: Gene `1.5` is located between genes `1.4` and `1.6` in the Class II middle region of the bacteriophage T7 genome [PMID:6864790]. It encodes a 29-amino-acid polypeptide of unknown function that is non-essential for T7 growth under standard laboratory conditions.
