# Bacteriophage T7 Gene 18.7 (Outer Membrane Spanin Subunit Rz1 / gp18.7) — Curation Notes

## Overview
Bacteriophage T7 gene *18.7* (`P03788`, 83 aa precursor / 62 aa mature lipoprotein, ~9.2 kDa) is an overlapping reading frame completely embedded in the +1 frame within gene *18.5* (`P03803`) [PMID:6864790, PMID:17900620]. It encodes the outer membrane lipoprotein spanin subunit gp18.7 (the functional equivalent of phage lambda Rz1) [PMID:17900620].

## Structural and Biological Role
- **Outer Membrane Lipoprotein (o-spanin)**: gp18.7 possesses a canonical N-terminal lipoprotein signal peptide (residues 1–21) cleaved by signal peptidase II (LspA), followed by S-diacylglycerol and N-acyl modification of Cys-22 (`LIPID 22`), which anchors the mature proline-rich polypeptide into the inner leaflet of the *Escherichia coli* outer membrane (`GO:0044384`, `host outer membrane`) [PMID:17900620].
- **Periplasmic Spanin Complex with gp18.5**: Summer et al. (2007) established that T7 gp18.7 is the prototype o-spanin (Rz1 equivalent) that forms a periplasm-spanning complex with the inner membrane i-spanin gp18.5 (Rz equivalent) to disrupt the host outer membrane after peptidoglycan degradation during phage lysis (`GO:0044659`, `viral release from host cell by cytolysis`) [PMID:17900620].
