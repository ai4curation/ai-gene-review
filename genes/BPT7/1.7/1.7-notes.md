# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1.7 (Nucleotide kinase) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1.7` (Nucleotide kinase; gp1.7; EC 2.7.4.8 / EC 2.7.4.9)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03781` (Isoforms `P03781-1` Large gp1.7, 196 AA, ~22.2 kDa; `P03781-2` Small gp1.7, 157 AA, ~17.8 kDa initiated at Met40)
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 196 AA

## Biological Function and Mechanism
- **Metal-independent deoxynucleoside monophosphate kinase**: Gene `1.7` is a Class II middle gene located between `1.6` and `1.8` [PMID:6864790]. Tran et al. [PMID:20497505] demonstrated that purified gp1.7 (which exists as both full-length 22.2 kDa and N-terminally truncated 17.8 kDa forms with identical activity, with the active site residing in the C-terminal half) catalyzes the phosphorylation of dGMP and dTMP to dGDP and dTDP, respectively, as well as dideoxythymidylate (ddTMP) to ddTDP, explaining why gene `1.7` confers sensitivity to exogenous dideoxythymidine (ddT) [PMID:18599435, PMID:20497505].
- **Phosphate donor specificity and metal independence**: Unlike canonical nucleoside monophosphate kinases, gp1.7 shares no sequence homology with known kinases, lacks a canonical P-loop nucleotide-binding motif, is completely independent of divalent metal ions, and uses GTP, dGTP, or dTTP—but not ATP—as its phosphoryl donor [PMID:20497505].
- **UniProt caution on defense suppression keywords**: UniProt explicitly notes a `CAUTION` regarding `PMID:33157039`, stating that gp1.7 alone is insufficient to cause bacterial growth arrest or retroribonucleotide/retroribonuclease-associated abortive infection without the bacterial Retron-Eco1 (Ec86) defense system, and that the automated `KW-0899`/`KW-1090` keyword mappings (`GO:0052031`, `GO:0052170`) do not reflect a true host innate immunity suppression role of gp1.7.
