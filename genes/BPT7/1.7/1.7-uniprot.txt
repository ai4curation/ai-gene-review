CC   -!- FUNCTION: Nucleoside monophosphate kinase that catalyzes the
CC       phosphorylation of dGMP and dTMP to dGDP and dTDP, respectively, by
CC       using either GTP, dGTP or dTTP as the phosphate donor
CC       (PubMed:20497505). Other dNTPs and NTPs (including ATP, CTP, UTP, dATP
CC       and dCTP) cannot serve as phosphate donors in the conversion of dTMP to
CC       dTDP, and dAMP, dCMP and dUMP cannot be used as acceptor substrates
CC       (PubMed:20497505). Confers sensitivity of phage T7 growth to
CC       dideoxythymidine (ddT), due to its ability to efficiently catalyze the
CC       conversion of dideoxythymidylate (ddTMP) to ddTDP, which is finally
CC       converted to ddTTP where it is incorporated as a chain-terminating
CC       nucleotide into replicating T7 DNA (PubMed:18599435, PubMed:20497505).
CC       A double mutation in this protein and the RecBCD inhibitor gp5.9
CC       protein allows phage to overcome the retron Ec48 bacteriophage defense
CC       system. This protein alone when overexpressed in E.coli does not cause
CC       growth arrest; Y128C may be a silent mutation (PubMed:33157039).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18599435, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20497505,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33157039}.
