# Escherichia phage T7 gene 2.8 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `2.8` (Protein 2.8; gp2.8)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03795`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 139 AA (~15.6 kDa)

## Sequence Features and Domain Architecture
- **Putative HNH/His-Me finger homing endonuclease**: Gene `2.8` is located between genes `2.5` and `3` in the Class II middle region of the bacteriophage T7 genome [PMID:6864790]. It encodes a 139-amino-acid polypeptide belonging to the HNH (His-Me finger) endonuclease superfamily (`InterPro:IPR003615`, `InterPro:IPR044930`, `Pfam:PF13392`), related to freestanding phage homing endonucleases such as T7 gp3.8, gp5.3, and T3/T7-family HNH nucleases. Its specific DNA cleavage site and biological role during T7 infection have not yet been experimentally determined.
