CC   -!- FUNCTION: The terminase large subunit acts as an ATP driven molecular
CC       motor necessary for viral DNA translocation into empty capsids and as
CC       an endonuclease that cuts the viral genome from the concetamer to
CC       initiate and to end a packaging reaction (By similarity). The terminase
CC       lies at a unique vertex of the procapsid and is composed of two
CC       subunits, a small terminase subunit involved in viral DNA recognition
CC       (packaging sequence), and a large terminase subunit possessing
CC       endonucleolytic and ATPase activities (By similarity). Both terminase
CC       subunits heterooligomerize and are docked on the portal protein to form
CC       the packaging machine (PubMed:23632014). Packaging initiates by TerS
CC       recognizing the packaging sequence in the viral DNA. The nuclease
CC       activity of TerL cuts the viral DNAand the terminase-DNA complex binds
CC       to the portal of a procapsid shell. DNA is translocated into the
CC       capsid, powered by the packaging ATPase in TerL, which continues until
CC       the next site is encountered at which point the motor stops and again
CC       cuts the DNA to release the nucleocapsid filled with a unit-length
CC       genome ('unit length' packaging) (PubMed:19082553, PubMed:23632014).
CC       Direct short terminal repeats at each end of the genome are duplicated
CC       in concert with packaging (PubMed:19082553). {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_04147, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23632014,
CC       ECO:0000303|PubMed:19082553}.
