# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1 (T7 RNA polymerase) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1` (T7 RNA polymerase; gp1; EC 2.7.7.6)
- **UniProt ID**: `P00573`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 883 AA (~98.9 kDa monomer)

## Biological Function and Mechanism
- **Archetypal single-subunit DNA-directed RNA polymerase**: Gene `1` is a Class I early gene transcribed by host *Escherichia coli* RNA polymerase [PMID:6864790]. It encodes a monomeric 99 kDa DNA-dependent 5'-3' RNA polymerase structurally homologous to the Pol I family of DNA polymerases (with an N-terminal promoter-binding/AT-rich recognition domain and C-terminal thumb, palm, and fingers subdomains) [PMID:6708104, PMID:8133519, PMID:7688864].
- **Catalytic mechanism and active-site residues**: Catalyzes Mg2+-dependent de novo RNA synthesis in the 5'-to-3' direction from highly specific 17-23 bp T7 Class II (replication/metabolism) and Class III (morphogenesis/packaging/lysis) promoters without auxiliary transcription factors [PMID:4580764, PMID:8133519]. Active-site residues Asp537 and Asp812 coordinate the catalytic divalent metal ions, while Lys631, Tyr639, and His811 mediate NTP binding and phosphodiester bond formation [PMID:8133519].
- **Roles in viral DNA replication, concatemer packaging, and lysozyme regulation**:
  - Synthesizes RNA transcripts that serve as primers for T7 DNA polymerase (`gp5`) at the primary origin of replication ($\phi1.1\text{A}$/$\phi1.1\text{B}$) [PMID:6864790, PMID:15223315].
  - Bound and allosterically inhibited by T7 lysozyme (`gp3.5`), which stabilizes the initiation conformation relative to the elongation complex, shifting transcription from Class II to Class III promoters and pausing transcription during DNA packaging [PMID:9192997, PMID:15223315].
  - Also required, together with gene `0.3`, for superinfection exclusion of bacteriophage T3 [PMID:916034].
