# Escherichia phage T7 gene 2.5 (Single-stranded DNA-binding protein) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `2.5` (Single-stranded DNA-binding protein; gp2.5)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03696`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 232 AA (~25.7 kDa monomer; forms a homodimer in solution)

## Biological Function and Mechanism
- **Essential OB-fold single-stranded DNA-binding protein**: Gene `2.5` is an essential Class II replication gene [PMID:6864790]. Gp2.5 contains an oligosaccharide/oligonucleotide-binding (OB) fold core domain and an acidic 26-residue C-terminal tail ending in a conserved aromatic residue (Phe232). In solution, gp2.5 forms a stable homodimer stabilized by its C-terminal tail, which mimics ssDNA to prevent non-specific aggregation until ssDNA binding displaces the tail.
- **Replisome coordination**: The exposed acidic C-terminal tail of ssDNA-bound gp2.5 directly interacts with both T7 DNA polymerase (`gp5`/thioredoxin) and the T7 helicase-primase (`gp4`) [PMID:8528255, PMID:30679383], coordinating leading- and lagging-strand synthesis at the replication fork and stimulating primer synthesis and utilization.
- **Homologous DNA strand annealing, recombination, and repair**: Gp2.5 catalyzes homologous DNA base-pairing (strand annealing) and mediates homologous recombination and double-strand break repair of replicating T7 DNA concatemers.
