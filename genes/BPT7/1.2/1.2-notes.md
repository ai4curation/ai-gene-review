# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1.2 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1.2` (Inhibitor of dGTPase; gp1.2)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03780`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 85 AA (~10.1 kDa)

## Biological Function and Mechanism
- **Inhibitor of host dGTP triphosphohydrolase (Dgt)**: Gene `1.2` is located immediately downstream of gene `1.1` near the primary origin of replication [PMID:6864790]. It encodes an 85-AA protein that binds and inhibits the host *Escherichia coli* deoxyguanosine-5'-triphosphate triphosphohydrolase (`dgt`, EC 3.1.5.1), which hydrolyzes dGTP into deoxyguanosine and tripolyphosphate.
- **Role in viral DNA replication**: In *E. coli* `optA1` mutants that overproduce host dGTPase by ~50-fold, T7 `1.2` mutants cannot replicate their DNA because intracellular dGTP pools are depleted; wild-type gp1.2 forms a tight complex with the hexameric host dGTPase to preserve dGTP pools required for viral DNA genome replication [PMID:6864790, PMID:24218612]. Gene `1.2` is also targeted by the F-plasmid exclusion system PifA, which triggers abortive infection upon recognizing gp1.2 (along with gp10).
