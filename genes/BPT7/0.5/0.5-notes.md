# Escherichia phage T7 gene 0.5 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `0.5` (Protein 0.5; gp0.5)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03777`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 47 AA (~4.9 kDa)

## Sequence Features and Expression
- **Class I early hydrophobic peptide**: Gene `0.5` is located in the Class I early region of the T7 genome between `0.4` and `0.6A` [PMID:6864790, PMID:7310871]. It encodes a 47-amino-acid polypeptide featuring a predicted N-terminal signal-like region (residues 1-23) and a hydrophobic transmembrane alpha-helix (residues 24-43) predicted to insert into the host cell inner membrane [PMID:6864790, PMID:7310871]. Its molecular function during infection remains uncharacterized.
