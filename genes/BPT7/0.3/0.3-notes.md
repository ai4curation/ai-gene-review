# Escherichia phage T7 gene 0.3 (Ocr) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `0.3` (Overcome classical restriction, Ocr; gp0.3)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03775`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 117 AA (~13.8 kDa monomer; ~27 kDa homodimer in solution)

## Biological Function and Mechanism
- **B-form DNA mimic and anti-restriction protein**: Gene `0.3` is the first Class I early gene transcribed by host RNA polymerase upon entry of the T7 genome into *Escherichia coli* [PMID:6864790]. Ocr forms an elongated, curved homodimer rich in acidic residues (34 Asp/Glu per monomer) whose surface charge distribution mimics ~24 bp of bent B-form double-stranded DNA [PMID:6257722, PMID:11804597].
- **Inhibition of Type I restriction-modification (R-M) enzymes**: Ocr binds with picomolar affinity ($K_d \approx 10^{-10}\text{ M}$) to the DNA-binding site of host Type I R-M complexes such as EcoKI and EcoBI, competitively blocking DNA binding and completely inhibiting both restriction endonuclease and modification methyltransferase activities [PMID:1095770, PMID:11804597, PMID:12235377, PMID:19074193, PMID:20302878, PMID:32483229].
- **Inhibition of BREX phage defense**: Ocr also binds the BREX defense methyltransferase BrxX (PglX) as a DNA mimic, neutralizing BREX-mediated restriction of phage replication [PMID:32338761, PMID:38989624].
- **Interaction with host RNA polymerase and role in superinfection exclusion**: Cryo-EM studies demonstrate that Ocr can also bind the cleft of host bacterial RNA polymerase and compete with sigma factors to inhibit host transcription [PMID:32039758]. Genetic studies also identified `0.3` (along with gene `1`) as required for exclusion of superinfecting phage T3 particles [PMID:916034].
