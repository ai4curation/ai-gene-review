# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1.6 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1.6` (Protein 1.6; gp1.6)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03793`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 86 AA (~9.8 kDa)

## Sequence Features and Expression
- **Uncharacterized Class II protein**: Gene `1.6` is located between genes `1.5` and `1.7` in the Class II middle region of the bacteriophage T7 genome [PMID:6864790]. It encodes a basic 86-amino-acid polypeptide (`gp1.6`) synthesized during middle gene expression. Although non-essential for plaque formation under standard laboratory growth conditions, its specific molecular function during infection remains unknown.
