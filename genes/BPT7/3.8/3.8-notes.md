## Functional Evidence
- Identified as a potential Class II coding sequence between gene 3.5 and the φOL replication origin/gene 4 region in the complete T7 DNA sequence (`PMID:6864790`).
- Sequence analysis reveals a conserved HNH/His-Me finger endonuclease domain (`Pfam:PF13392` / `InterPro:IPR003615`), typical of phage homing endonucleases found in T7-like podophages (such as T7 gp1.75/gp3.8/gp5.3/gp5.5-5.7 region homologs or T3/yeO3-12 relatives).
- However, no direct biochemical or genetic characterization of T7 gp3.8 endonuclease activity or target site specificity has been published, and the gene has no existing GOA annotations.
