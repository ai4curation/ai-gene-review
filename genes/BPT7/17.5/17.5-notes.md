# Bacteriophage T7 Gene 17.5 (Holin gp17.5) — Curation Notes

## Overview
Bacteriophage T7 gene *17.5* (`P03802`, 67 aa, ~7.4 kDa) encodes the class II holin gp17.5 (protein L), an integral inner-membrane protein with two transmembrane helices [PMID:6864790].

## Biological Role
- **Host Inner Membrane Permeabilization (Holin)**: At a genetically programmed time late in the lytic cycle, gp17.5 oligomerizes in the *Escherichia coli* inner (cytoplasmic) membrane (`GO:0020002`, `host cell plasma membrane`) to form membrane lesions (`GO:0140911`, `pore-forming activity`), collapsing the proton-motive force and allowing the T7 endolysin (gp3.5 amidase) to access and degrade the peptidoglycan layer during host cell lysis (`GO:0044659`, `viral release from host cell by cytolysis`) [PMID:6864790, PMID:17900620].

## GO Annotation Review
- **Accept**: `GO:0140911` (`pore-forming activity`), `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`), and `GO:0020002` (`host cell plasma membrane`).
- **Modify**: Refine `GO:0016020` (`membrane`) to `GO:0020002` (`host cell plasma membrane`), and refine `GO:0019076` (`viral release from host cell`) and `GO:0031640` (`killing of cells of another organism`) to `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`).
