# Escherichia phage T7 gene 0.6A Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `0.6A` (Proteins 0.6A / 0.6B; gp0.6A / gp0.6B)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03778`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 53 AA (~6.2 kDa for gp0.6A); 111 AA (~12.9 kDa for frameshift product gp0.6B)

## Expression and Frameshifting
- **Class I early gene with ribosomal frameshifting**: Located between genes `0.5` and `0.7` in the Class I early region of bacteriophage T7 [PMID:6864790, PMID:7310871]. Standard translation of the `0.6A` reading frame yields a 53-AA basic polypeptide (gp0.6A), while a programmed ribosomal frameshift into the +1 reading frame just upstream of the `0.6A` TGA stop codon produces the 111-AA extended polypeptide gp0.6B [PMID:6864790]. Neither gp0.6A nor gp0.6B is essential for plaque formation in standard laboratory *E. coli* strains, and their biochemical functions remain unknown.
