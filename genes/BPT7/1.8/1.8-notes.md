# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1.8 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1.8` (Protein 1.8; gp1.8)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03794`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 48 AA (~5.8 kDa)

## Sequence Features and Expression
- **Uncharacterized Class II protein**: Gene `1.8` is located between genes `1.7` and `2` in the Class II middle region of the bacteriophage T7 genome [PMID:6864790]. It encodes a 48-amino-acid polypeptide of unknown function that is non-essential for plaque formation under standard laboratory growth conditions.
