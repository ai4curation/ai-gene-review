CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a role in packaging a single copy of genome into the
CC       prohead. The terminase is composed of two subunits (a large and a
CC       small) and the small subunit recognizes a specific sequence in the
CC       viral DNA. Once the DNA is packaged, the terminase detaches from the
CC       connector and the tail replaces it to finish maturation of the virion.
CC       Packaging initiates by TerS recognizing the packaging sequence in the
CC       viral DNA. The nuclease activity of TerL cuts the viral DNA and the
CC       terminase-DNA complex binds to the portal of a procapsid shell. DNA is
CC       translocated into the capsid, powered by the packaging ATPase in TerL,
CC       which continues until the next site is encountered at which point the
CC       motor stops and again cuts the DNA to release the nucleocapsid filled
CC       with a unit-length genome ('unit length' packaging) (PubMed:19082553,
CC       PubMed:3298244). Direct short terminal repeats at each end of the
CC       genome are duplicated in concert with packaging (PubMed:19082553).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:3298244, ECO:0000303|PubMed:19082553}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homooctamer (Probable). Interacts with the terminase large
CC       subunit gp19; the active complex is probably heterooligomeric.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:3036832}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminus contains a helix-turn-helix (HTH) doamin that is
CC       involved in viral DNA binding. {ECO:0000305|PubMed:34598064}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the terminase small subunit family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
