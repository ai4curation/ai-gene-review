CC   -!- FUNCTION: ATP-dependent DNA helicase and primase essential for viral
CC       DNA replication and recombination (PubMed:21606333, PubMed:22977246,
CC       PubMed:32009150). The helicase moves 5' -> 3' on the lagging strand
CC       template (PubMed:2829184, PubMed:9185573), unwinding the DNA duplex
CC       ahead of the leading strand polymerase at the replication fork and
CC       generating ssDNA for both leading and lagging strand synthesis
CC       (PubMed:21606333, PubMed:22977246, PubMed:32009150). ATP or dTTP
CC       hydrolysis propels each helicase domain to translocate 2 nt per step
CC       sequentially along DNA (PubMed:17604719, PubMed:30679383). Mediates
CC       strand transfer when a joint molecule is available and participates in
CC       recombinational DNA repair through its role in strand exchange
CC       (PubMed:8617248, PubMed:9096333). Primase activity synthesizes short
CC       RNA primers at the sequence 5'-GTC-3' on the lagging strand that the
CC       polymerase elongates using dNTPs and providing the primase is still
CC       present (PubMed:6454135, PubMed:9139692). {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
