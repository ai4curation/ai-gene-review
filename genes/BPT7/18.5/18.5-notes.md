# Bacteriophage T7 Gene 18.5 (Inner Membrane Spanin Subunit Rz / gp18.5) — Curation Notes

## Overview
Bacteriophage T7 gene *18.5* (`P03803`, 143 aa, ~16.2 kDa) encodes the inner membrane spanin subunit gp18.5 (the functional ortholog of phage lambda Rz) [PMID:6864790, PMID:17900620]. Gene *18.7* (`P03788`), which encodes the outer membrane lipoprotein spanin subunit (Rz1 equivalent), is completely embedded in the +1 reading frame within gene *18.5* [PMID:17900620].

## Biological Role and Refutation of Legacy Peptidase Annotations
- **Two-Component Spanin Complex for Outer Membrane Disruption**: Summer et al. (2007) demonstrated that T7 gp18.5 and gp18.7 are the prototypes of embedded two-component Rz/Rz1 spanin equivalents in Gram-negative phages [PMID:17900620]. Anchored in the *Escherichia coli* inner membrane (`GO:0020002`, `host cell plasma membrane`) by an N-terminal transmembrane helix (residues 12–32), the periplasmic coiled-coil domain of gp18.5 complexes with the outer membrane lipoprotein gp18.7 to span the periplasm and fuse/disrupt the host outer membrane after peptidoglycan degradation by gp3.5 amidase (`GO:0044659`, `viral release from host cell by cytolysis`) [PMID:17900620].
- **Removal of Obsolete Peptidase Annotations**: Early literature in the 1980s–1990s hypothesized that phage lambda Rz and T7 gp18.5 might be peptidoglycan endopeptidases, leading to legacy UniProt keywords `KW-0645` (`Protease`) and `KW-0378` (`Hydrolase`). Subsequent biochemical and genetic studies (Summer et al., 2007) definitively disproved any proteolytic or enzymatic activity and established that Rz/gp18.5 is a purely structural membrane-fusion spanin subunit [PMID:17900620]. Therefore, `GO:0006508` (`proteolysis`), `GO:0008233` (`peptidase activity`), and `GO:0016787` (`hydrolase activity`) must be marked `REMOVE`.

## GO Annotation Review
- **Accept**: `GO:0020002` (`host cell plasma membrane`) and `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`).
- **Modify**: Refine `GO:0031640` (`killing of cells of another organism`) to `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`).
- **Remove**: `GO:0006508` (`proteolysis`), `GO:0008233` (`peptidase activity`), and `GO:0016787` (`hydrolase activity`).
- No experimental or computational GO annotations are currently assigned in GOA; `existing_annotations` and `core_functions` are therefore left empty pending experimental characterization.
