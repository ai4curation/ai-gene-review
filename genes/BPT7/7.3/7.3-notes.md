## Literature & Function Summary
- **Essential tail assembly and host-adsorption protein ejected at infection initiation** (`PMID:16054667`):
  - Kemp et al. (2005) (`PMID:16054667`) showed that gp7.3 is an essential structural component of the mature T7 virion tail (~25–30 copies per virion) that is lost from the virion upon initiation of infection and likely ejected into the host cell.
  - Phage particles produced in the absence of gp7.3 assemble morphologically normal-looking heads and tails containing DNA, but fail to adsorb productively to *E. coli* cells and are non-infectious (`PMID:16054667`, `file:genes/BPT7/7.3/7.3-uniprot.txt`).
  - Cryo-EM reconstruction (`PDB:9JYZ`) resolves gp7.3 lining the interior lumen of the gp12 tail nozzle.
