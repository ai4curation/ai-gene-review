# Gene 5.5-5.7 (P03787) Notes — Escherichia phage T7 (`BPT7`)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `5.5-5.7`
- **UniProtKB accession**: `P03787` (`V5557_BPT7`, Fusion protein 5.5/5.7)
- **Length**: 169 amino acids (~18.9 kDa)
- **Alternative products**: `P03787-1` (`5.5-5.7`) and `P0DJY3-1` (`5.5`).
- **Domains**:
  - N-terminal gp5.5 suppressor-of-silencing domain (residues 1–98, `InterPro:IPR022611`, `Pfam:PF11247`).
  - C-terminal gp5.7 domain (residues 101–169, `InterPro:IPR024461`, `Pfam:PF13946`, resolved by solution NMR in `PDB:5LGM`).

## Literature & Function Summary
- Produced by a rare -1 ribosomal frameshift near the 3' end of gene 5.5 mRNA that bypasses the gene 5.5 stop codon and continues translation in the -1 frame through gene 5.7 (`PMID:6864790`, `file:genes/BPT7/5.5-5.7/5.5-5.7-uniprot.txt`).
- Combines the N-terminal H-NS anti-silencing module (gp5.5) with the C-terminal gp5.7 module (`PDB:5LGM`), which inhibits host *E. coli* Eσ70 RNA polymerase transcription initiation at T7 early promoters.
