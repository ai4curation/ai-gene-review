# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1.3 (DNA ligase) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1.3` (DNA ligase; gp1.3; EC 6.5.1.1)
- **UniProt ID**: `P00969`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 359 AA (~41.1 kDa)

## Biological Function and Mechanism
- **ATP-dependent DNA ligase**: Gene `1.3` is transcribed on both Class I (early) and Class II (middle) mRNAs [PMID:6864790]. It encodes a monomeric 41 kDa ATP-dependent DNA ligase (EC 6.5.1.1) that catalyzes phosphodiester bond formation between adjacent 3'-hydroxyl and 5'-phosphate termini in nicked double-stranded DNA via a three-step nucleotidyltransfer mechanism: (1) formation of a covalent enzyme-AMP intermediate at the active-site Lys34 residue with release of pyrophosphate, (2) transfer of the adenylyl group to the 5'-phosphate of the DNA nick, and (3) attack by the 3'-OH to seal the nick and release AMP.
- **Role in DNA replication, repair, and recombination**: Seals Okazaki fragments during lagging-strand viral DNA replication and repairs single-strand nicks during DNA repair and homologous recombination. In wild-type *E. coli*, host NAD+-dependent DNA ligase (`LigA`) can substitute for gp1.3, making gene `1.3` essential only in ligase-deficient (`lig-`) host strains [PMID:6864790].
