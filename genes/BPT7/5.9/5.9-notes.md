# Gene 5.9 (P20406) Notes — Escherichia phage T7 (`BPT7`)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `5.9`
- **UniProtKB accession**: `P20406` (`GP59_BPT7`, RecBCD inhibitor gp5.9)
- **Length**: 52 amino acids (~6.0 kDa)
- **Structures**: `PDB:8B1R`, `PDB:9GMB`, `PDB:9GMD` (cryo-EM structures bound to *E. coli* RecBCD and retron-Ec48-RecBCD).
- **InterPro / Pfam**: `InterPro:IPR058007` / `Pfam:PF25708` (`T7_Gp5_9`, Exonuclease V inhibitor Gp5.9).

## Literature & Function Summary
- **Inhibition of host RecBCD (exonuclease V) nuclease-helicase** (`PMID:127051`):
  - Linear double-stranded T7 DNA is vulnerable to degradation by the host RecBCD (exonuclease V) ATP-dependent helicase-nuclease complex.
  - Lin (1992) (`PMID:127051`) cloned and purified the 52-residue acidic T7 gp5.9 protein and demonstrated that it directly binds and inhibits *E. coli* RecBCD (exonuclease V) in vitro and in vivo without requiring ATP hydrolysis.
  - Structural studies (`PDB:8B1R`) show that gp5.9 forms an acidic coiled-coil DNA mimic that blocks the DNA-binding channel of RecBCD.
- **Triggering of the bacterial retron-Ec48 abortive infection defense system** (`PMID:33157039`):
  - Millman et al. (2020) (`PMID:33157039`) demonstrated that the bacterial retron-Ec48 anti-phage defense system associates with RecBCD and "guards" it; binding and inhibition of RecBCD by T7 gp5.9 (or phage lambda Gam) activates the retron-Ec48 effector, triggering abortive infection and cell death (`PDB:9GMB`, `9GMD`).
