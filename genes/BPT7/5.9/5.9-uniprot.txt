CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a role in the inhibition of the host RecBCD complex
CC       (also called exonuclease V) (Probable). A double mutation in this
CC       protein and the nucleotide kinase gp1.7 protein allows phage to
CC       overcome the retron Ec48 bacteriophage defense system. Overexpression
CC       of the wild-type gp5.9 protein in E.coli with an intact Ec48 retron
CC       leads to a dramatic decrease in cell counts consistent with cell death,
CC       the L23P mutant does not have this effect (PubMed:33157039).
