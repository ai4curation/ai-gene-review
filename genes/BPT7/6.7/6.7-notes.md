# Gene 6.7 (P03801) Notes — Escherichia phage T7 (`BPT7`)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `6.7`
- **UniProtKB accession**: `P03801` (`GP67_BPT7`, Morphogenesis protein 6.7)
- **Length**: 88 amino acids (~9.3 kDa; N-terminal initiator Met1 cleaved, mature chain residues 2–88)
- **Structures**: `PDB:9JYY`, `PDB:9JYZ`, `PDB:9JZ0` (high-resolution cryo-EM structures of the T7 virion head/portal-core complex).
- **InterPro / Pfam**: `InterPro:IPR020134` / `Pfam:PF17570` (`T7-like_gp67`, Bacteriophage T7-like, internal head protein gp6.7).

## Literature & Function Summary
- **Internal head / morphogenesis protein ejected at initiation of infection** (`PMID:16054667`):
  - Kemp et al. (2005) (`PMID:16054667`) demonstrated that mature T7 virions contain ~20–26 copies of the internal head protein gp6.7 (`P03801`) and the tail protein gp7.3 (`P03751`), and that both proteins are lost from the virion at the initiation of infection and likely ejected into the host cell.
  - Deletion or amber mutation of gene `6.7` severely reduces infective phage yield, producing particles that assemble and package DNA but exhibit defective head morphogenesis and reduced infectivity (`PMID:16054667`).
