# Escherichia phage T7 gene 0.7 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `0.7` (Protein kinase 0.7; gp0.7; EC 2.7.11.1)
- **UniProt ID**: `P00513`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 359 AA (~40.7 kDa)

## Biological Function and Mechanism
- **Bifunctional serine/threonine protein kinase and host transcriptional shutoff factor**: Gene `0.7` is the rightmost Class I early gene of phage T7 [PMID:6864790, PMID:7310871]. The N-terminal two-thirds of gp0.7 (residues 1-243) encodes an ATP-dependent serine/threonine protein kinase [PMID:1101258, PMID:1310178], whereas the C-terminal region contributes to host RNA polymerase shutoff early in infection [PMID:6864790].
- **Host substrates phosphorylated by gp0.7**:
  - **Host RNA polymerase $\beta'$ subunit (RpoC)**: Phosphorylated at Thr1068, increasing transcription termination at the weak $\text{T}\phi$ terminator and attenuating host transcription [PMID:16672600].
  - **Host mRNA degradosome (RNase E and RhlB)**: Phosphorylated in the C-terminal scaffold domain of RNase E, stabilizing T7 mRNAs synthesized by T7 RNA polymerase against endonucleolytic decay [PMID:11722741].
  - **Host translation apparatus**: Phosphorylates ribosomal protein S1 and initiation/elongation factors IF1, IF2, IF3, and EF-G to remodel host translation in favor of viral transcripts [PMID:1534259, PMID:8022276].
- **Autophosphorylation**: Undergoes intramolecular autophosphorylation at Ser216, which down-regulates its own phosphotransferase activity [PMID:1310178, PMID:22951189].
