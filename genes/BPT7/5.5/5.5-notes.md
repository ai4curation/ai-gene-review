# Gene 5.5 (P0DJY3) Notes — Escherichia phage T7 (`BPT7`)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `5.5`
- **UniProtKB accession**: `P0DJY3` (`SGS_BPT7`, Protein suppressor of silencing gp5.5)
- **Length**: 99 amino acids (~11.1 kDa)
- **Alternative products**: Canonical gp5.5 (`P0DJY3-1`) and frameshift fusion protein gp5.5-5.7 (`P03787-1`).
- **InterPro / Pfam**: `InterPro:IPR022611` / `Pfam:PF11247` (`Bacteriophage_T7_5_5`).

## Literature & Function Summary
- **Anti-silencing via inhibition of host H-NS oligomerization** (`PMID:21764926`):
  - Host nucleoid-associated protein H-NS (`P0ACF8`) silences foreign AT-rich and phage DNA by forming extended nucleoprotein filaments via its central dimer-dimer oligomerization domain.
  - Ali et al. (2011) (`PMID:21764926`) demonstrated that T7 gp5.5 binds directly to the central dimer-dimer oligomerization domain (site 2) of *E. coli* H-NS (`P0ACF8`), preventing higher-order H-NS-DNA complex assembly and relieving H-NS-mediated transcriptional silencing of T7 promoters in vitro and in vivo.
  - Purified gp5.5 also self-associates into higher-order oligomers in solution (`PMID:21764926`) and forms a complex with H-NS and host tRNA-Val (`file:genes/BPT7/5.5/5.5-uniprot.txt`).
- **Programmed -1 ribosomal frameshifting (`GO:0075523`)**:
  - A rare -1 ribosomal frameshift near the 3' end of gene 5.5 mRNA produces the 169-AA gp5.5-5.7 fusion protein (`P03787`, `PMID:6864790`).

## Curation Decisions
- `GO:0075523` (`viral translational frameshifting`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`) -> `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` (describes the cis-acting mRNA frameshifting mechanism that generates the minor 5.5-5.7 fusion product rather than the biochemical function of mature gp5.5).
- **Proposed `NEW` annotations**:
  - `GO:0052031` (`symbiont-mediated perturbation of host defense response`, `involved_in`, IMP `PMID:21764926`): gp5.5 counteracts H-NS-mediated xenogeneic silencing of phage DNA.
  - `GO:0042802` (`identical protein binding`, `enables`, IPI `PMID:21764926`): gp5.5 self-associates/oligomerizes in solution and upon binding H-NS.
