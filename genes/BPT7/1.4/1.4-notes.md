# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1.4 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1.4` (Protein 1.4; gp1.4)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03791`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 51 AA (~5.4 kDa)

## Sequence Features and Expression
- **Class II putative single-pass host membrane protein**: Gene `1.4` is located immediately downstream of the $\text{T}\phi$ early terminator and gene `1.3` in the Class II region of the T7 genome [PMID:6864790]. It encodes a 51-amino-acid hydrophobic polypeptide containing a predicted transmembrane alpha-helix (residues 12-32) expected to insert into the host *Escherichia coli* inner membrane [PMID:6864790]. Its biological function during T7 infection has not been experimentally determined.
