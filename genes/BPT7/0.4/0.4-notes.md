# Escherichia phage T7 gene 0.4 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `0.4` (Host cell division inhibitor gp0.4)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03776`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 51 AA (~5.9 kDa)

## Biological Function and Mechanism
- **Direct inhibitor of host FtsZ**: Expressed ~2 minutes post-infection as a Class I early protein co-transcribed with gene `0.3` on an RNase III-processed bicistronic mRNA [PMID:6864790, PMID:24218612]. Gp0.4 binds directly to the essential *Escherichia coli* cytoskeletal division protein FtsZ and inhibits FtsZ filament assembly in vitro and Z-ring formation in vivo, independent of endogenous FtsZ inhibitors MinC and SulA [PMID:24218612].
- **Competitive fitness advantage in dividing hosts**: By blocking cytokinesis early in infection when only a single phage genome is present, gp0.4 prevents a nascent daughter cell from escaping without a phage genome and preserves the full cytoplasmic resources of the infected cell for T7 progeny production. Resistance to gp0.4 toxicity maps to a 2-AA (Gly-Val) duplication at position 18 of FtsZ (`ftsZ9`) [PMID:24218612].
