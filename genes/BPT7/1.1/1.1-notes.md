# Escherichia phage T7 gene 1.1 Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `1.1` (Protein 1.1; gp1.1)
- **UniProt ID**: `P03779`
- **Taxon**: Escherichia phage T7 (`NCBITaxon:10760`)
- **Length**: 42 AA (~5.2 kDa)

## Sequence Features and Genomic Context
- **Small basic polypeptide near the primary origin of replication**: Gene `1.1` lies immediately downstream of the $\phi1.1\text{A}$ and $\phi1.1\text{B}$ promoters within the primary origin of replication region of the T7 genome [PMID:6864790]. It is transcribed both on early RNAs initiated at Class I promoters and on Class II RNAs initiated at $\phi1.1\text{A}$ and $\phi1.1\text{B}$ [PMID:6864790]. The 42-AA product is rich in basic residues (arginine and lysine), suggesting potential nucleic acid binding, though it is non-essential under standard laboratory growth conditions and its specific biochemical function remains unknown.
