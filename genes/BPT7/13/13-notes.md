# BPT7 gene 13 (gp13, P03723) — Curation Notes

## Summary of Function
Bacteriophage T7 protein gp13 (`P03723`, 138 AA) is a class III morphogenetic protein required for late virion assembly and infectivity [PMID:6864790, PMID:16054667]. Although historically annotated by sequence curation as a probable internal virion or scaffold protein, biochemical analysis of purified T7 virions and `13-` amber mutant particles showed that gp13 is present in small amounts in mature virions and is required for the stable incorporation/organization of the internal head/core proteins (`gp6.7`, `gp14`, `gp15`, and `gp16`) and for efficient tail attachment [PMID:16054667]. Upon adsorption of T7 virions to host *Escherichia coli* cells at the initiation of infection, gp13 is lost/degraded from the virion concomitantly with ejection of the internal core proteins [PMID:16054667].
