CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a role in the inhibition of the host toxin-antitoxin
CC       (TA) system. These modules made by the host and composed of a toxic
CC       gene and a neutralizing gene play role in stress response and
CC       programmed cell death. Gp4.5 interacts with the host protease lon to
CC       neutralize the degradation of the antitoxin that would result in the
CC       activation of the toxin and the destruction of the bacteria.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23478446}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with host lon protease; this interaction prevents
CC       the degradation of antitoxin by lon and thus the toxin activation.
