## Literature & Function Summary
- **Toxin-antitoxin (TA) abortive infection suppression via Lon protease inhibition** (`PMID:23478446`):
  - Sberro et al. (2013) (`PMID:23478446`) discovered the SanaTA toxin-antitoxin system in *Shewanella* sp. ANA-3 and showed that wild-type phage T7 overcomes SanaTA-mediated abortive infection in *E. coli*, whereas T7 mutants lacking gene `4.5` are sensitive to SanaTA restriction.
  - Because the SanaTA antitoxin is rapidly degraded by the host ATP-dependent serine endopeptidase Lon (`P0A9M0`), phage infection and host transcriptional shutoff normally trigger antitoxin depletion and toxin-mediated abortive infection.
  - Co-immunoprecipitation and functional assays demonstrated that T7 gp4.5 physically interacts with and inhibits host Lon protease (`P0A9M0`), preventing Lon-mediated antitoxin degradation and neutralizing TA-mediated bacterial defense (`PMID:23478446`, `file:genes/BPT7/4.5/4.5-uniprot.txt`).
