| Correct identity: **FGSG_03537 in PH-1**? | The PH-1 locus was identified as **TRI5/FGSG_03537**; its cDNA was expressed, deleted, mutated, and complemented in PH-1-derived strains (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000006). | Direct locus-level confirmation supports correspondence to the supplied Q00909 record. The paper does not independently report the UniProt accession or all supplied InterPro/Pfam identifiers. |
| Catalytic reaction and substrate? | Recombinant Tri5 expressed in *E. coli* produced trichodiene; purified wild-type enzyme was assayed with **farnesyl diphosphate (FPP)**. N225D/S229T substitutions in the Mg²⁺-coordinating NSE/DTE motif abolished activity without disrupting the mainly α-helical fold (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000016). | Strong biochemical evidence identifies Tri5 as the FPP cyclase initiating trichothecene biosynthesis. The reaction is **FPP → trichodiene + diphosphate**, distinct from downstream Tri4 oxygenation. |
| How specific is its product profile? | Trichodiene was the major recombinant product, with minor products including premnaspirodiene. Six sesquiterpenes detected in induced fungal cultures—including longifolene, germacrene D, β-copaene, sativene, cyclosativene, and ar-curcumene—were absent from recombinant Tri5 cultures (pqac-00000016). | Tri5 is principally, but not absolutely, product-specific for trichodiene. The six absent fungal products should not be assigned as direct Tri5 products; other sesquiterpene synthases are implicated. |
| Cytosolic or ER-localized? | Tri5 is a **cytosolic enzyme enriched near ER-derived toxisomes**. Deleting TRI5 left undifferentiated ER, whereas wild-type Tri5-GFP and catalytically inactive, structurally intact Tri5 restored toxisome morphology (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017). | The findings support a non-catalytic organizational requirement for Tri5. Physical binding to ER membranes or ER-anchored Tri1, Tri4, or Tri11 is a proposed model, **not a demonstrated direct interaction**. |
| Does RNA5P regulate TRI5 in cis? | Replacing the 834-bp TRI5 upstream region containing RNA5P increased TRI5 sense RNA **2.6-fold**. Ectopic RNA5P overexpression of **more than fivefold** had no significant effect, whereas approximately **15-fold in-situ** overexpression reduced TRI5 and made DON nearly undetectable (pqac-00000019, pqac-00000020). | This is strong evidence for locus-dependent **cis repression**, rather than inhibition by freely diffusible RNA5P. Tri6 and Tri10 suppress RNA5P while supporting TRI5 expression. |
| What did the 2024 ER-remodeling study establish? | Overproduction of the ER-localized enzymes **Tri1 or Tri4** generated toxisome-shaped ER structures in non-inducing medium. FgHog1 was required for typical assembly, whereas the UPR was not (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000025). | This provides current cellular context for the pathway but does **not** demonstrate that Tri5 is an ER membrane protein. Direct ER localization in this study applies to Tri1 and Tri4, not Tri5. |
| What is the crop phenotype of TRI5 disruption? | Three independent TRI5 mutants from each of three *F. graminearum* field isolates—**nine mutants in total**—were tested. Mutants failed to spread beyond inoculated wheat spikelets, were as aggressive as wild type on barley, and caused less maize disease only in the NIV-producing background, not in the two DON-producing backgrounds (pqac-00000026, pqac-00000029). | TRI5-dependent trichothecenes promote within-head spread in wheat, but their contribution is host- and chemotype-dependent. These phenotypes reflect loss of pathway products and do not prove a separate direct virulence activity of the Tri5 protein. |
