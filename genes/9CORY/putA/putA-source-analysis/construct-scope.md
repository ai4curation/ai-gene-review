# CfPutA construct identity and biochemical scope

Verified 2026-11-06 using official RCSB polymer-entity data, PDBe SIFTS and 5UX5 mmCIF, with source URLs and response hashes in construct-bridge.json. Full downloads remain in the ignored audit cache.

The complete 1076-residue polymer entity1 sequence is identical to frozen UniProt A0A1X8XLF1. The deposited sequence begins GHMQEGEQ. mmCIF sequence numbering assigns Gly and His to PDB residues -1 and0; Met is residue1, and the final Ser is1074. Thus UniProt positions3–1076 correspond to the paper-numbered 1074-residue CfPutA segment. Primary PMID28420730 describes a codon-optimized synthetic construct with an N-terminal His8 tag and TEV site, expressed in E. coli, followed by tag cleavage. The leading GH is therefore interpreted as a construct remnant, not a demonstrated natural insertion. The source-derived UniProt sequence is preserved.

No source strain or genomic accession is provided in the PDB source fields inspected. The symbol putA is the literature convention used for this entry, which has no frozen UniProt GN gene-name line. Direct biochemical and structural evidence applies to recombinant CfPutA and its deposited construct; it is not a native Corynebacterium growth, localization or repression experiment. Paper residue numbers require a +2 offset to map onto this frozen UniProt sequence.

The primary assays separately measure PRODH using PMS/DCPIP and GSALDH using P5C/GSAL plus NAD+. Coupled turnover and intermediate trapping use menadione bisulfite. These support quinone-linked proline oxidation and NAD-dependent semialdehyde oxidation without identifying a native quinone. P5C and glutamate semialdehyde interconvert; the aldehyde is the GSALDH substrate. Intermediate trapping and lag-time analysis support channeling but do not establish zero leakage. Ligand-induced dimerization and C-terminal deletion effects are properties measured on recombinant protein, not native transcriptional regulation.
