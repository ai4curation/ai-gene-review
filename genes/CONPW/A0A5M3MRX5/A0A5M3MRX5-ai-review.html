Gene Description

Type-1 NAD-dependent malate dehydrogenase (EC 1.1.1.37) of the brown-rot basidiomycete Coniophora puteana (strain RWD-64-598 SS2), most likely the mitochondrial isozyme. The 339-residue protein has the lactate/malate dehydrogenase NAD-binding Rossmann and C-terminal catalytic domains, with a predicted proton-acceptor histidine and conserved NAD(+)- and substrate-binding residues (NCBIfam TIGR01772, PIRSF000102, CDD cd01337, PANTHER PTHR11540:SF73). It is inferred to catalyse the reversible NAD(+)-dependent oxidation of (S)-malate to oxaloacetate. An N-terminal extension of about 25 residues, rich in arginine, serine and alanine and lacking acidic residues, resembles the mitochondrial presequence of yeast Mdh1p. The protein has no peroxisomal PTS1. It is therefore expected to act in the mitochondrial matrix as the malate-to-oxaloacetate step of the tricarboxylic acid cycle. The only other type-1 MDH in the proteome (A0A5M3MC96) has no presequence and ends in a PTS1 (SKL). Brown-rot fungi secrete oxalic acid made largely by hydrolysis of oxaloacetate. A mitochondrial MDH would feed that pool only indirectly, because in current models malate rather than oxaloacetate leaves the mitochondria. The protein has not been characterized experimentally; all functional statements rest on sequence family membership.

Q: Which C. puteana MDH isozyme supplies the oxaloacetate that is hydrolysed to oxalate? Is it this mitochondrial enzyme, the PTS1-bearing paralog A0A5M3MC96, or an unidentified cytosolic MDH? And is oxaloacetate hydrolase cytosolic in brown-rot basidiomycetes, as it is in Aspergillus niger?

  • Genome from the 31-genome lignin-decay study PMID:22745431.

Mitochondrial or cytosolic?

Mitochondrial. See A0A5M3MRX5-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md:
- N-terminal 32 residues before the GxGGxG core motif (MLARTALRSVASTSRASAFSTSAARLTRVAVL):
5 Arg/Lys, 0 Asp/Glu, 10 Ser/Thr, which is presequence composition. This parallels yeast MDH1,
whose 17-residue extension is a mitochondrial presequence
PMID:3072021.
- No PTS1 (C-terminus FVA). The only other type-1 MDH in the proteome, A0A5M3MC96
(CONPUDRAFT_157993), has no presequence and ends in SKL (PTS1), like yeast peroxisomal MDH3.
- Identity 0.63 to yeast MDH1, 0.48 to yeast cytosolic MDH2.
- No targeting predictor (TargetP/MitoFates) was run, so the presequence call rests on
composition plus homology. No experimental localization exists for any C. puteana MDH.

Oxalate context

  • Brown-rot Fomitopsis palustris: whole-cell extracts have very high MDH activity
    PMID:11553780; most oxalate
    comes from oxaloacetate hydrolysis
    PMID:11553780.
    The authors' model says malate, not oxaloacetate, leaves the mitochondria and is
    oxidized outside
    PMID:11553780.
    MDH isozymes were not separated.
  • A. niger oxaloacetate hydrolase is cytoplasmic
    PMID:3132096.
  • So a mitochondrial matrix MDH runs the TCA cycle oxidation in the matrix. Oxaloacetate
    made there is not directly available to a cytosolic oxaloacetate hydrolase. In the
    Munir model, the oxaloacetate for oxalate is made by an extramitochondrial MDH.
    That points to the peroxisomal paralog A0A5M3MC96 (or an unidentified cytosolic MDH),
    not to this protein. Neither role is tested in C. puteana.

Question

Is A0A5M3MRX5 the mitochondrial or a non-mitochondrial (cytosolic/peroxisomal)
type-1 malate dehydrogenase of C. puteana?

Method

The conserved NAD-binding glycine-rich motif of the lactate/malate DH N-terminal
domain (regex G[AG].GG[IV]G) was used as a fixed point marking the start of
the conserved catalytic core. The residues before it were compared across the
target, its only other type-1 MDH paralog in the reference proteome
(A0A5M3MC96, CONPUDRAFT_157993) and the three S. cerevisiae isozymes (MDH1
mitochondrial, MDH2 cytosolic, MDH3 peroxisomal). The last three residues were
inspected for a PTS1 signal.

Interpretation

  • The target carries an N-terminal extension of roughly 25 residues beyond the
    short (6-7 residue) start of the peroxisomal-type sequences, rich in Arg,
    Ser/Thr and Ala and devoid of Asp/Glu. This is the composition of a
    mitochondrial matrix-targeting presequence, and it parallels yeast MDH1,
    whose 17-residue N-terminal extension is an experimentally established
    mitochondrial presequence (UniProt P17505 TRANSIT 1..17; PMID:3072021).
    No presequence predictor (TargetP, MitoFates) was run here; the call rests on
    composition and on homology to yeast MDH1.
  • The target has no PTS1 (ends in FVA). Its paralog A0A5M3MC96 ends in SKL, a
    canonical PTS1, and lacks the presequence, matching yeast peroxisomal MDH3.
  • Family classifiers agree: the target is in CDD cd01337
    (MDH_glyoxysomal_mitochondrial) and PANTHER PTHR11540:SF73 (MALATE
    DEHYDROGENASE, MITOCHONDRIAL). (The paralog is also placed in a subfamily
    named "MALATE DEHYDROGENASE, MITOCHONDRIAL", SF16, despite its PTS1; PANTHER
    subfamily names are not localization calls.)
  • Conclusion: A0A5M3MRX5 is most likely the mitochondrial MDH of C. puteana,
    not a cytosolic enzyme. No yeast-MDH2-like cytosolic type-1 MDH, and no
    type-2 (IPR010945/IPR011274) MDH, was found among the UniProt entries of
    proteome UP000053558 queried by EC 1.1.1.37 / Pfam PF00056 / PF02866 /
    name "malate" (October 2026). That is a search of the current UniProt
    proteome only; it does not prove the absence of a cytosolic isozyme.
