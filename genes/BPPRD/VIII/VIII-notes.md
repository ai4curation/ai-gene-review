# Gene VIII (DNA terminal protein P8) - Curation Notes

## Overview
- **UniProt ID**: P09009 (`TERM_BPPRD`)
- **Gene symbol**: `VIII` (Protein P8)
- **Organism**: Enterobacteria phage PRD1 (`NCBITaxon:10658`)
- **Function**: Genome terminal protein (TP) covalently attached to the 5'-ends of both strands of the linear dsDNA genome via a phosphodiester bond between a tyrosine hydroxyl and dGMP. Serves as the protein primer for DNA replication initiation by DNA polymerase P1 and accompanies the viral genome into the procapsid during packaging [PMID:1655759, PMID:17202207, file:BPPRD/VIII/VIII-uniprot.txt].

## Key Literature & Evidence
- [PMID:1655759] Shows that P1 catalyzes formation of the P8-dGMP replication initiation complex, which is a prerequisite for full-length phage DNA replication in vitro.
- [PMID:17202207] Discusses the role of the P8-DNA complex in binding the packaging ATPase P9 and procapsid during virion assembly and DNA packaging.
- [PMID:23091024] Shows that when expressed heterologously as a YFP fusion in mammalian COS-7 cells, PRD1 TP localizes to the eukaryotic nucleus; however, PRD1 naturally infects Gram-negative bacteria lacking a nucleus, so `GO:0042025` (host cell nucleus) is an experimental artifact in a non-host system and is not added.
