# Gene IX (DNA packaging ATPase P9) - Curation Notes

## Overview
- **UniProt ID**: P27381 (`VP09_BPPRD`)
- **Gene symbol**: `IX` (Protein P9)
- **Organism**: Enterobacteria phage PRD1 (`NCBITaxon:10658`)
- **Function**: Viral DNA packaging motor ATPase localized at the unique packaging vertex of the capsid. Hydrolyzes ATP to power translocation of the unit-length P8-linked dsDNA genome into the preformed procapsid and remains bound to the mature virion after packaging as a structural vertex component [PMID:17202207, file:BPPRD/IX/IX-uniprot.txt].
