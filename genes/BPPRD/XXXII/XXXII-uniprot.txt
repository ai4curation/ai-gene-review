CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: PRD1 virions are composed of a tail-less icosahedral
CC       capsid of diameter 63 nm, an inner protein-lipid membrane, and a dsDNA
CC       genome which is located inside the lipid vesicle. The DNA is packaged
CC       into a preformed procapsid. The internal membrane plays an active role
CC       in DNA delivery to the host cell by forming a tubular structure used
CC       for injecting the DNA into the host cytoplasm.
