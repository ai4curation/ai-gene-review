# Gene VI (Packaging efficiency factor P6) - Curation Notes

## Overview
- **UniProt ID**: P27379 (`VP06_BPPRD`)
- **Gene symbol**: `VI` (Protein P6)
- **Organism**: Enterobacteria phage PRD1 (`NCBITaxon:10658`)
- **Function**: Minor capsid protein located exclusively at the unique packaging vertex, where it forms a ring-shaped heterododecamer with packaging ATPase P9 anchored to the inner membrane portal proteins P20/P22. Essential for efficient ATP-driven viral DNA genome packaging [PMID:17202207, file:BPPRD/VI/VI-uniprot.txt].

## Curation Decisions
- **ACCEPT**: `GO:0019073` (viral DNA genome packaging, IMP from PMID:17202207).
- **NEW**: `GO:0005198` (structural molecule activity) and `GO:0019068` (virion assembly) supported by [PMID:17202207].
