# Rhodobacter construct and strain scope

Persistent marker: CYTOCHROME-RHOCB-CONSTRUCTS-20261106.

The frozen targets are SB1003 HelX/P36893 (176 aa), Ccl2/Q00501 (149 aa), Ccl1/Q00500 (653 aa), and CycH/D5ALI6 (424 aa). The identities, loci and historical sequence cross-references come from their untouched UniProt records.

PMID28634234 Table 1 construct and strain rows are retained in [the scoped excerpt](PMID28634234-table1-excerpt.md), with URL/hash provenance. The genuine DOE OSTI PDF and full pdftotext extraction are preserved unchanged only in the ignored acquired-sources cache. It identifies MT-SRP1.r1 (ccmI deletion plus ccmFH promoter-up mutation), MD11 (ccmG deletion), and MD14 (ccmH interruption). Soluble CcmG derives from pQE60-helX/pCS1555, without the membrane-anchor region and with a C-terminal His6 tag. The soluble FLAG-CcmH construct pAV10 derives from pCS1604/pRGK291. Table1 removes the signal sequence and C-terminal46 amino acids; the methods prose instead says N-terminal46. Table1 agrees with the independently described C-terminal anchor and retention of assayed Cys42/Cys45. This is a source wording discrepancy, not a corrected plasmid sequence. Purified-protein assays used E. coli expression; complementary native-host redox experiments used complemented R. capsulatus mutants.

PMID18753134 full primary PMC Table1 and methods were inspected at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2573057/ (normal cache remains abstract-only). Exact short excerpt:
> All R. capsulatus mutant strains listed are derivatives of strain MT1131

The complex assays used Strep-CcmF, CcmH-Strep and CcmI-FLAG in the appropriate MT1131-derived mutants. Association in detergent-dispersed membranes establishes a complex; it is not an isolated-subunit ligation assay.

PMID21956106 full cached methods trace full-length His10-CcmI through pSVEN, pMADO3 and pMADO5. The CcmI-1 fragment, not necessarily the full-length original donor, explicitly used MT1131 genomic DNA. The full-length tagged protein complemented MT-SRP1. No deposited full plasmid sequence was recovered to prove identity to the modern SB1003 D5ALI6 sequence.

MT1131 is SB1003-derived, as documented in primary strain tables (https://journals.asm.org/doi/full/10.1128/jb.01080-06); this lineage relationship is not proof of sequence identity. The SB1003 reviews therefore transfer conserved molecular roles with explicit construct/strain limits and do not claim an unmodified SB1003 assay.
