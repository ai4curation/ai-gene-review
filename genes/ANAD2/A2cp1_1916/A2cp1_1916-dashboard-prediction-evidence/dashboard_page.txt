Why this tier
Tier B: named GH57 family assignments and comparative homolog evidence support a family-level annotation, but no homolog is directly biochemically characterized. Available evidence includes comparative summaries, query and homolog domains, one homolog neighborhood, STRING associations, conservation, and topology; experimental fitness, cofitness, and substrate-specific biochemical evidence are absent. Specific alpha-amylase or trehalohydrolase assignments are rejected because the carbohydrate-related context does not distinguish those activities, and STRING explicitly provides only family-level specificity.
