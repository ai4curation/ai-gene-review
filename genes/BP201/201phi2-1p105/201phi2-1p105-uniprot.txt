CC   -!- FUNCTION: Self-assembles to form a proteinaceous shell that encloses
CC       the viral DNA and compartmentalizes proteins and DNA during viral
CC       infection (PubMed:28082593, PubMed:28813669, PubMed:35922510). This
CC       micrometer-scale compartment contains narrow pores and is the site of
CC       viral replication, with the proteins involved in DNA replication
CC       localized inside (PubMed:28082593, PubMed:28813669, PubMed:35922510).
CC       Provides a surface for docking of capsids during packaging
CC       (PubMed:28082593, PubMed:28813669). Probably protects the viral genome
CC       against host defenses (Probable). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28082593,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28813669, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35922510,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:35922510}.
