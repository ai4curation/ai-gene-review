CC   -!- FUNCTION: GDP-Man:Man(3)GlcNAc(2)-PP-Dol alpha-1,2-mannosyltransferase
CC       that operates in the biosynthetic pathway of dolichol-linked
CC       oligosaccharides, the glycan precursors employed in protein asparagine
CC       (N)-glycosylation. The assembly of dolichol-linked oligosaccharides
CC       begins on the cytosolic side of the endoplasmic reticulum membrane and
CC       finishes in its lumen. The sequential addition of sugars to dolichol
CC       pyrophosphate produces dolichol-linked oligosaccharides containing
CC       fourteen sugars, including two GlcNAcs, nine mannoses and three
CC       glucoses. Once assembled, the oligosaccharide is transferred from the
CC       lipid to nascent proteins by oligosaccharyltransferases. Catalyzes, on
CC       the cytoplasmic face of the endoplasmic reticulum, the addition of the
CC       fourth and fifth mannose residues to the dolichol-linked
CC       oligosaccharide chain, to produce Man(5)GlcNAc(2)-PP-dolichol core
CC       oligosaccharide. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P53954}.
