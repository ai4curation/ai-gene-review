# MCD4 notes

## Evidence

- C. albicans MCD4 (orf19.5244) encodes a GPI ethanolamine phosphate transferase 1 homolog. Expressing CaMCD4 rescued growth of the S. cerevisiae conditional mcd4 mutant when native MCD4 was repressed, and CaMCD4 expression markedly reduced abnormal GPI-anchor precursor accumulation in that assay [PMID:31073667, "Expression of this homologue, designated CaMCD4, restored cell growth"; PMID:31073667, "Analysis of radiolabeled lipids showed that the accumulation of abnormal GPI"].
- Conditional repression of the C. albicans MCD4 locus reduced C. albicans growth and caused abnormal morphology in culture and in a silkworm infection model [PMID:31073667, "CaMCD4 expression by Dox led to a decrease in growth and appearance of abnormal"].
