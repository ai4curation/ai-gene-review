Gene Description

ESA1 encodes the Candida albicans ESA1/MYST histone acetyltransferase, a chromatin enzyme and catalytic subunit of NuA4-family complexes. Esa1 acetylates histone H4, especially H4K5 and H4K12 in C. albicans cells, and contributes to NuA4-dependent regulation of nuclear transcription, filamentous growth, temperature adaptation, and genotoxic and oxidative stress resistance.
