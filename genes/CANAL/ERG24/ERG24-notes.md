# ERG24 notes

C. albicans ERG24 was isolated by complementation of the S. cerevisiae erg24 mutant in PMID:11897574; the abstract also reports disruption of both copies, slow-growth and drug-sensitivity phenotypes, reduced mouse pathogenicity, and failed serum-induced germ-tube formation. The core annotation set should therefore retain Delta14-sterol reductase activity, ergosterol biosynthetic process, and ER-membrane localization while keeping germ-tube and xenobiotic phenotypes as non-core readouts of the sterol lesion.

IBA rows from PTN000479179, PTN000479204 and PTN001083003 are consistent with the PAINT placement of fungal Erg24/TM7SF2-family C-14 reductases.
