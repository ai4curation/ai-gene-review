Gene Description

CHS7 encodes a CHS7-family multipass membrane chaperone that supports normal Chs3-dependent chitin synthase III activity and cell-wall chitin production in Candida albicans. By homology with Saccharomyces cerevisiae CHS7 and Neurospora crassa csc-1/CSE-7, the protein is inferred to act at the endoplasmic reticulum membrane as a client-biased export chaperone for the class IV chitin synthase.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

chitin synthase export chaperone activity

Definition: A protein folding chaperone activity that binds or escorts a chitin synthase client in the endoplasmic reticulum to promote its productive folding, ER quality-control evasion, and export toward the Golgi.

Justification: C. albicans CHS7, S. cerevisiae CHS7, and N. crassa csc-1 all support a client-biased export factor for class IV chitin synthases rather than a generic unfolded-protein binder; GO:0044183 is correct but could be refined by a child term for this dedicated CHS7-family activity.

Parent term: protein folding chaperone

PMID:16079341
Candida albicans strains deficient in CHS7, a key regulator of chitin synthase III, exhibit morphogenetic alterations and attenuated virulence.
  • C. albicans CHS7 is required for most cell-wall chitin synthesis.
    "most chitin synthesis in Candida albicans, one of the fungi most commonly isolated from opportunistic mycoses and infections, depends on CHS7."
  • C. albicans chs7 null mutants have reduced chitin synthase activity and reduced chitin.
    "C. albicans chs7Delta null mutants showed reduced levels of chitin and CS activity, and were resistant to Calcofluor."
  • C. albicans CHS7 is functionally related to S. cerevisiae CHS7 but does not export S. cerevisiae Chs3 in a cross-species rescue test.
    "Despite the sequence similarity and functional relationship with ScChs7p, CaChs7p was unable to restore CSIII activity in a Saccharomyces cerevisiae chs7Delta null mutant, because it was unable to direct ScChs3p export from the endoplasmic reticulum."
  • Loss of CHS7 causes yeast-cell and hyphal-colony morphology defects.
    "chs7Delta hyphae were very curved and had irregular lateral walls, resulting in very compact colonies that seemed unable to spread out radially on the surface, unlike the wild-type."
PMID:17559412
Promoter heterozygosity at the Candida albicans CHS7 gene is translated into differential expression between alleles.
  • Both CHS7 alleles contribute to wild-type C. albicans CHS7 expression.
    "Both alleles are expressed in the wild type and contribute to the total levels of the CaCHS7 mRNA."
  • The CHS7 L allele drives lower transcription than the S allele.
    "the presence of this region in the promoter (L allele) significantly reduced the transcription of the gene as compared with its absence (S allele)."
  • Reduced CHS7 expression in heterozygous strains can reduce chitin and alter hypha-induced morphology.
    "The HeteroL strain showed reduced levels of chitin, a partial resistance to calcofluor, and a moderate degree of morphogenetic alterations in hypha-inducing media."
PMID:39926406
CSE-8, a filamentous fungus-specific Shr3-like chaperone, facilitates endoplasmic reticulum exit of chitin synthase CHS-3 (class I) in Neurospora crassa.
  • Neurospora CSE-7 and CSE-8 are Chs7 orthologs.
    "The Neurospora crassa genome encodes two orthologues of Chs7: chitin synthase export (CSE) proteins CSE-7 (NCU05720) and CSE-8 (NCU01814)"
  • Shr3-like proteins, including yeast Chs7, are specialized ER chaperones for plasma-membrane proteins.
    "These proteins function as specialized chaperones that prevent the aggregation of PM proteins at the ER by ensuring proper folding"

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does C. albicans Chs7 physically bind CaChs3 and travel with it beyond the ER, as S. cerevisiae Chs7 does?

Q: Does the naturally weak CHS7 L promoter sensitize specific clinical isolates to wall stress by limiting CaChs7-dependent Chs3 export?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Image functional CaChs3-GFP and CaChs7-mCherry expressed from their native loci in wild-type, chs7-null, and CHS7 rescue strains; combine this with Chs3 co-immunoprecipitation and detergent-solubility assays to distinguish client aggregation from a post-folding export block.

Hypothesis: C. albicans Chs7 directly promotes Chs3 folding and ER export.

Type: chitin-synthase client-folding and ER-export assay

Experiment: Swap the S and L promoter alleles at the native CHS7 locus, then measure CaChs7 abundance, CaChs3 ER retention, chitin synthase III activity, wall chitin, and hyphal colony morphology under basal and wall-stress conditions.

Hypothesis: The CHS7 S/L promoter polymorphism controls chitin synthase III output by making CaChs7 limiting for CaChs3 export.

Type: promoter-allele dosage assay
