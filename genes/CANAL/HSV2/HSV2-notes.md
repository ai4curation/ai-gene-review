# HSV2 notes

## 2026-11-21 fungal PROPPIN review

The C. albicans Q59P11 protein is the local HSV2 representative in the Saccharomycotina Atg21/Hsv2 branch of PTHR11227. UniProt places Q59P11 in PTHR11227, and the PTHR11227-entries snapshot lists it as `Q59P11,SVP1-like protein 2,...,Candida albicans...HSV2`, matching the curated HSV2 orthology rather than the Atg18 branch.

PubMed searches on 2026-11-21 did not find a direct C. albicans HSV2-specific paper; the review therefore treats same-aspect GOA rows as electronic transfers unless direct C. albicans evidence is present in GOA. Conserved PROPPIN PI3P/PI(3,5)P2 binding and adaptor activity remain plausible through PTHR11227, but the root cargo-selective pexophagy, mitophagy, nucleophagy and glycophagy PAINT rows should not be projected from the pan-WIPI/PROPPIN root onto every Atg21/Hsv2 descendant.
