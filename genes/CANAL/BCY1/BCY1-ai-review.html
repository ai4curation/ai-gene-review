Gene Description

BCY1 encodes the sole Candida albicans regulatory subunit of cAMP-dependent protein kinase A. Bcy1 binds cAMP through two cyclic-nucleotide-binding domains and binds the Tpk1/Tpk2 catalytic subunits in the heterotetrameric PKA holoenzyme, thereby restraining catalytic kinase activity until cAMP binding releases active Tpk subunits. This regulation places Bcy1 downstream of Ras1/Cyr1-generated cAMP in control of cAMP-PKA signaling, filamentous growth, carbon-source responses, stress responses, and white-opaque switching. Functional Bcy1-GFP is predominantly cytosolic in glucose, can accompany Tpk2-containing holoenzyme to the vacuolar membrane in glycerol, and is recruited to cytoplasmic P-bodies and stress granules during heat stress.
