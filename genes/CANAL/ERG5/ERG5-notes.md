# ERG5 notes

- UniProt G1UB11 is the C. albicans SC5314 ERG5/CYP61 ortholog and PTHR24286:SF228 member; the record assigns the Rhea reaction for 5-dehydroepisterol C-22 desaturation, ER-membrane localization, and the ergosterol pathway.
- PMID:10390230 purified and reconstituted sterol delta22-desaturase from C. glabrata and noted CYP61 homologs in the C. albicans and S. pombe genome projects. CGD uses this as the publication support for ISS transfer from S. cerevisiae ERG5 to C. albicans ERG5.
- PMID:20547793 is direct C. albicans clinical-isolate support: CA108 has an erg5 sequence insertion, no detectable ergosterol, and ergosta-5,7-dienol as more than 80% of the sterol fraction. The paper notes that complementation remains to be done, so it is direct mutant sterol evidence rather than a purified-enzyme assay.
- PMID:12773383 recovered an ERG5-adjacent transposon insertion in the haploinsufficiency filamentation screen, but the reconstructed one-allele deletion had a wild-type appearance. Mark the starvation/serum filamentation rows as over-annotated and flag the CGD rows for deletion unless independent ERG5 evidence supports them.
- PMID:19824013 is abstract-only in the cache. It reports LC-MS/MS identification of 214 membrane proteins but does not expose the ERG5 peptide or table hit, so leave the CGD plasma-membrane IDA undecided pending table review.
- GO-Central places ergosterol biosynthesis on PTN001209378 for the Dikarya CYP61 node seeded by S. cerevisiae and S. pombe Erg5. C. albicans ERG5 is in the same PTHR24286:SF228 clade and should retain the IBA.
