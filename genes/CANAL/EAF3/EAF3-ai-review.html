Gene Description

Eaf3 is a nuclear MRG-family chromatin protein in Candida albicans. It has an N-terminal Tudor-knot/chromo-like module and a C-terminal MRG interaction domain and belongs to the conserved fungal Eaf3/Alp13 branch that functions in NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complexes and Rpd3S-type histone deacetylase complexes, linking histone acetylation and deacetylation machinery to RNA polymerase II-associated chromatin regulation.

Core Functions

Acts as the Candida Eaf3/MRG-family subunit of the NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complex, contributing to NuA4 histone H4 acetyltransferase activity in nuclear chromatin and to RNA polymerase II-linked transcriptional regulation.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Tag Eaf3 at its endogenous locus and perform reciprocal co-immunoprecipitation or affinity purification with tagged Rco1 and Rpd31, followed by quantitative immunoblotting and mass spectrometry under yeast and hyphal induction conditions.

Hypothesis: C. albicans Eaf3 associates with Rco1 and Rpd31 in a CaRpd31S-like complex but was missed in the initial Rpd3/Rpd31 pulldowns.

Type: reciprocal co-IP/AP-MS

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: Whether C. albicans Eaf3 physically associates with the Rco1/Rpd31-containing CaRpd31S deacetylase complex remains unresolved.

OPEN BIOLOGY CC_DARK

What is known: PAINT supports fungal Eaf3/Rpd3S inheritance and the 2016 Candida study identified an Rco1/Rpd31 complex, but its initial Rpd3/Rpd31 purifications did not recover Eaf3 or Rco1.

Significance: Resolving Eaf3 membership would determine whether the C. albicans Rpd3S-like complex has retained the canonical fungal H3K36me-coupled Eaf3 reader module.

What would resolve it: Directly test Eaf3 association with tagged Rpd31 and Rco1 in yeast and hyphal growth conditions.

EAF3 curation notes

2026-11-06

  • Fetched CANAL/EAF3 from UniProt/GOA. The seed contains 11 rows covering
    broad MRG-domain IEAs, a CGD NuA4 IDA row, an IC from NuA4 membership to
    contributes_to histone H4 acetyltransferase activity, the fungal Rpd3S IBA
    from PTN000853816, and the three broad-root MRG-family IBAs from
    PTN000093963.
  • Checked newer literature for Candida Eaf3 and the Rpd3/Rpd31/Rco1 branch. No
    newer Candida EAF3-specific experiment superseded the 2008 NuA4 curation
    [PMID:18685084, "Based on the Candida Genome Database, orthologues of all but
    one of the subunits of the S. cerevisiae NuA4 complex exist in C. albicans"].
    The local cache drops the rows of Table 4, so I checked the PMC rendering;
    the table lists EAF3/orf19.2660 as the Candida ortholog of budding-yeast
    EAF3.
  • The 2016 Rpd3/Rpd31 paper is relevant but not enough to remove the Rpd3S PAINT
    inference. Xie et al. did not recover Eaf3/Rco1 in their initial Rpd3/Rpd31
    purifications, then used targeted co-IP to show that Rco1 interacts with
    Rpd31 rather than Rpd3 [PMID:27935838, "Rpd31 but not Rpd3 represents the HDAC
    component of the CaRpd31S regulatory complex"]. This supports a divergent
    Candida Rpd31S-like complex, not a demonstrated Eaf3 loss from the complex.
  • PTN000853816 is a fungal PAINT node for GO:0032221 Rpd3S complex seeded by
    direct PomBase alp13 and SGD EAF3 evidence. The donor list is short but
    biologically appropriate, and Candida Eaf3 belongs to the same fungal
    PTHR10880:SF15 Eaf3/Alp13 branch.
  • PTN000093963, the eukaryotic MRG root, needs term-specific handling.
    GO:0035267 NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complex and GO:0006357 regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II remain appropriate for
    Candida Eaf3. GO:1990841 promoter-specific chromatin binding is
    over-propagated from only Arabidopsis MRG seeds and should not be transferred
    to fungal Eaf3 proteins.
  • I did not add GO:0140003 histone H3K36me3 reader activity for this Candida
    review. The term is biologically plausible by analogy to budding and fission
    yeast, but the current Candida seed set lacks direct evidence that C. albicans
    Eaf3 recognizes H3K36-methylated chromatin.
