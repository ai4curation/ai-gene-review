# ERG25 notes

ERG25 is the older Candida albicans C-4 sterol methyl oxidase paralog in
PTHR11863. The primary cached experimental anchor is Kennedy et al. 2000, which
cloned ERG25 by complementation of a Saccharomyces cerevisiae erg25 mutant and
noted the expected non-heme iron histidine clusters plus an ER retrieval signal
[PMID:10783002, "The ERG25 gene encoding the Candida albicans C-4 sterol methyl
oxidase was cloned and sequenced by complementing a Saccharomyces cerevisiae
erg25 mutant with a C. albicans genomic library."].

Re-review notes:

- Accepted PTN000221246 for C-4 methylsterol oxidase activity and ER membrane
  localization.
- Accepted the fungal PTN000933387 ergosterol-biosynthesis IBA.
- Retained iron ion binding as a cofactor-level non-core annotation.
- Marked the InterPro lipid biosynthesis parent as too general because the
  gene already carries ergosterol biosynthetic process.
- Did not assert a catalytic complex annotation: this has direct evidence in
  budding yeast, but C. albicans ERG25 has no direct complex row yet.
