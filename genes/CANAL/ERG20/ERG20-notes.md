# CANAL ERG20 notes

## 2026-11-22 review

- Reviewed the new C. albicans ERG20 stub against Swiss-Prot, CGD-sourced GOA,
  PMID:14653518, PMID:35728770, and the PTHR11525 PAINT sidecar.
- PMID:14653518 is abstract-only. The abstract confirms that Song et al.
  assayed sterol/prenylation-pathway expression in C. albicans treated with
  lovastatin and fluconazole, including the observation that "early part" genes
  such as "HMG1 and ERG20" did not alter expression under the combined treatment
  in mid-logarithmic growth.
- PMID:35728770 is abstract-only. CGD curates ERG20 to fungal biofilm matrix by
  IDA from this LC-MS/MS biofilm proteomics paper; the abstract confirms the
  C. albicans LC-MS/MS experiment but does not list ERG20, so the review keeps
  the experimental annotation as non-core and defers to CGD's full-text read.
- The EnsemblFungi transfer of `contributes_to GO:0004311 geranylgeranyl
  diphosphate synthase activity` appears to have crossed from the S. pombe
  Fps1/Spo9 heteromeric GGPS case into a canonical single-chain Candida FPPS.
  No Candida Spo9-like cofunction evidence was found.
- Literature searching in the 2024-2026 window found newer Candida ergosterol
  and biofilm context papers but no newer direct ERG20 enzymology, localization,
  or GGPS-cofactor evidence.
