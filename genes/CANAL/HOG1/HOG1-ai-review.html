Gene Description

Candida albicans HOG1 encodes the Hog1 stress-activated MAP kinase, the terminal serine/threonine kinase of the Ssk2-Pbs2-Hog1 HOG pathway. Hog1 cycles between cytoplasm and nucleus after activating stress, where it phosphorylates downstream proteins to coordinate adaptation to osmotic stress, oxidative stress, heavy-metal and cationic stress, and several Candida-specific host-associated stresses. In C. albicans, Hog1 also constrains filamentous growth, contributes to cell-wall and chlamydospore phenotypes through cross-talk with the Cek1 pathway, and links basal stress signaling to G1/S and cell-size control.

Definition: Any process that results in a change in state or activity of a cell as a result of lipid peroxidation or oxidized lipid damage.

Justification: The Candida HOG pathway protects cells from oxidized linolenic acid, but the existing GO:0034440 lipid oxidation term describes execution of lipid oxidation rather than a cellular response to lipid-peroxide damage.
