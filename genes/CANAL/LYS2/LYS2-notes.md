# CANAL LYS2 notes

Candida albicans LYS2 encodes the PTHR44845:SF1 L-2-aminoadipate reductase of
the fungal alpha-aminoadipate pathway. The GO-Central IBA rows are on
PTN001186117, seeded by CGD:CAL0000183678, PomBase:SPAP7G5.04c and
SGD:S000000319, and propagate both `GO:0004043` and `GO:0009085`.

## Cached publications

- PMID:9560434 cloned and sequenced the C. albicans LYS2 locus. The pCaLYS2
  plasmid complemented S. cerevisiae and C. albicans lys2 mutants, and
  transformants recovered AAR activity.
- PMID:10647122 sequentially disrupted both C. albicans LYS2 alleles and showed
  loss of AAR activity, lysine auxotrophy, and rescue after transformation with
  LYS2.
- PMID:11717270 expressed recombinant C. albicans Lys2p and showed that it is
  inactive alone but activated by CoA plus C. albicans CLD2 lys2 mutant extract,
  which supplies the Lys5-like phosphopantetheinyl transferase. Mutation of
  activation-domain residues G882 and S884 abolished activity.
- PMID:12756539 mutagenized the C. albicans Lys2p adenylation, NADPH-binding,
  and putative alpha-aminoadipate-binding domains and showed that conserved
  catalytic residues in each domain are required for AAR activity.
- PMID:2050413 and PMID:3062368 are early chromosome-assignment and mapping
  papers. The cached abstracts do not expose the LYS2 functional assay details,
  but the later direct Candida disruption, complementation, and recombinant
  protein papers make the LYS2 activity and lysine-biosynthesis role clear.
- PMID:15546125 is a later S. pombe Lys1/Lys7 activation-domain paper. Its
  cached abstract discusses C. albicans and S. cerevisiae LYS2 as established
  comparators but directly assays S. pombe Lys1p rather than Candida Lys2p.

## Newer literature search

Targeted PubMed searches for `Candida albicans LYS2 alpha-aminoadipate
