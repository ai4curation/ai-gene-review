# C. albicans ERG3 notes

- Reviewed the cached C. albicans ERG3 cloning, deletion, restoration and fluconazole-treatment papers. The GOA experimental rows are all consistent with Erg3 as the late ER-membrane Delta7-sterol C-5 desaturase.
- PMID:10433965 cloned C. albicans ERG3 and restored ergosterol synthesis with the wild-type B311 allele.
- PMID:16127034 and PMID:16436713 support the azole/virulence and in vivo fluconazole phenotypes of erg3 mutants; these phenotypes are downstream of the sterol-desaturase defect and are non-core.
- A 2024-2026 literature search did not find a newer C. albicans ERG3 biochemical or localization paper that changes the GO conclusions.
