# ERG4 notes

C. albicans ERG4 is the predicted ortholog of S. cerevisiae ERG4 and S. pombe erg4/sts1 in PTHR21257:SF31. No direct C. albicans ERG4 core-function paper was cached, so this review relies on UniProt/EC orthology, PANTHER PAINT propagation from PTN000479182 for C-24(28) sterol reductase activity, and the established terminal ERG4 reaction from yeast and fission yeast.

PubMed and local-cache searches on 2026-11-22 did not identify a target-specific C. albicans ERG4 paper that would justify replacing the CGD biological_process ND placeholder with a direct biological-process annotation; the non-ND biological-process rows in GOA are IBA/IEA inferences.
