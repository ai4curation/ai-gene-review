# ERG4 notes

C. albicans ERG4 is the predicted ortholog of S. cerevisiae ERG4 and S. pombe erg4/sts1 in PTHR21257:SF31. No direct C. albicans ERG4 paper was cached, so this review relies on UniProt/EC orthology, PANTHER PAINT propagation from PTN000479182 for C-24(28) sterol reductase activity, and the established terminal ERG4 reaction from yeast and fission yeast. The CGD ND biological-process row is stale because the same record now has supported ergosterol-biosynthetic annotations.
