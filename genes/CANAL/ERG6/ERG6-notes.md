# ERG6 notes (O74198, CAALFM_C302150CA)

- PANTHER family context: ERG6 is a PTHR44068:SF1 member with S. cerevisiae ERG6 (P25087), S. pombe erg6 (O14321), C. albicans ERG6 (O74198), Aspergillus fumigatus erg6 (Q4W9V1), and N. crassa erg-4 (Q9P3R1). The PTHR44068 PAINT table places GO:0003838 sterol 24-C-methyltransferase activity, GO:0005783 endoplasmic reticulum, and the parent GO:0016126 sterol biosynthetic process on PTN002226525; the more specific fungal ergosterol biosynthesis call GO:0006696 is on PTN000802684, seeded by CGD:CAL0000189580, PomBase:SPBC16E9.05, and SGD:S000004467.
- Direct Candida activity: Ganapathy et al. purified native Candida 24-SMT and found zymosterol is the optimal substrate and fecosterol is the product [PMID:20946868 "The product of the enzyme incubated with zymosterol was fecosterol."]. Liu et al. 2023 and later inhibitor papers also treat CaERG6 as a directly assayed sterol 24-C-methyltransferase [PMID:37005772 "CaERG6, a crucial sterol 24-C-methyltransferase involved in the biosynthesis of ubiquitous ergosterol in C. albicans"]; the 2023 Cell Chemical Biology H55 paper and 2024 JMC H55-derivative paper were found in the PubMed search beyond the GOA set and cached.
- Original genetics: Jensen-Pergakes et al. cloned C. albicans ERG6 by complementing S. cerevisiae erg6 [PMID:9593144 "The Candida albicans ERG6 gene was isolated by complementation of a S. cerevisiae erg6 mutant by using a C. albicans genomic library."] and disrupted both Candida alleles, producing sterol-pathway drug phenotypes.
- Local IBA comparison: the completed S. cerevisiae ERG6 review accepts GO:0003838, ER localization, lipid droplet localization, and ergosterol biosynthesis; the completed S. pombe erg6 review accepts GO:0003838, ER, and ergosterol biosynthesis and raises the unresolved question of whether pombe Erg6 also acts on lipid droplets. For Candida, I kept ER as the only core location because the lipid-droplet row is an Ensembl transfer from yeast rather than direct Candida evidence.
- PMID:19824013 plasma-membrane localization: the cached paper is abstract-only and reports a broad plasma-membrane proteomics workflow, not the Erg6 peptide evidence. I left GO:0005886 UNDECIDED rather than removing a CGD experimental row without the full text/supplement.
- PMID:17604452 xenobiotic response: the CaFT study shows altered ERG6 strain fitness in response to fluconazole and other compounds, but this is a membrane-sterol/drug-susceptibility phenotype rather than an Erg6 execution step in a xenobiotic response, so GO:0071466 is marked over-annotated.
