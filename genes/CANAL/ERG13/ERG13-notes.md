- Falcon deep research could not be used because agentapi is unavailable; this review used the cached Candida albicans ERG13 GOA rows, the cached publications for PMID:12773383 and PMID:17604452, the local UniProt flat file, and the local PTHR43323 PAINT/family review.
- Follow-up after PR review read the UniProt-linked Candida-specific PMIDs that were cached or fetchable: PMID:14653518, PMID:15917516, PMID:17588813, PMID:21209325, and PMID:22265407. PMID:15917516 and PMID:22265407 are full text in the cache, PMID:17588813 and PMID:14653518 are abstract-only, and the ERG13-specific UniProt INDUCTION/PTM statements for these papers appear to come from supplementary-scale expression or proteomics datasets rather than main-text ERG13 experiments.
- Literature searches for `Candida albicans ERG13 HMG-CoA synthase`, `A0A1D8PTW6`, `orf19.7312`, and `CAALFM_CR09160CA` found pathway and antifungal-target review coverage of Erg13 but no newer ERG13-specific primary localization or activity paper.
- PTHR43323-review.yaml assesses the PTN000222418 HMG-CoA synthase and acetyl-CoA metabolic process IBD nodes as sound inherited HMG-CoA synthase/mevalonate annotations. It also assesses PTN000933803 as the sound fungal ergosterol biosynthesis node.
- The CGD IMP rows trace to genome-scale haploinsufficiency screens. Uhl et al. screened transposon insertion strains for altered filamentous growth on nutrient-poor Spider medium and on YEPD plus 1% fetal calf serum; Xu et al. screened a barcoded heterozygous deletion pool with inhibitory compounds to detect chemically induced haploinsufficiency. These screens support phenotype-level sensitivity when ERG13 dosage is reduced, but they do not show that Erg13 is a dedicated filamentation, starvation-response, or xenobiotic-response regulator.
- The cached Candida albicans publications PMID:12773383, PMID:14653518, PMID:15917516, PMID:17588813, PMID:17604452, PMID:21209325, and PMID:22265407, the UniProt flat file, and web searches for `Candida albicans ERG13 HMG-CoA synthase`, `A0A1D8PTW6`, `orf19.7312`, and `CAALFM_CR09160CA` do not identify a measured ERG13 cellular compartment; the existing ND cellular-component row therefore remains a current no-direct-data statement.
