Candida albicans MCR1 encodes the MCR1-like member of the fungal NADH-cytochrome b5 reductase family. The protein has the conserved cytochrome-b5 reductase FAD- and NADH-binding regions and a predicted N-terminal transmembrane segment, and is inferred to act as a mitochondrial NADH-dependent cytochrome-b5 reductase.
