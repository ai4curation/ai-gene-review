Gene Description

CAALFM_C301820WA (orf19.9233) encodes an 866-residue glycoside hydrolase family 3 (GH3) beta-glucosidase (EC 3.2.1.21) predicted to hydrolyse terminal, non-reducing beta-D-glucosyl residues. It has the N- and C-terminal GH3 catalytic domains with the GH3 active-site signature, a PA14 domain inserted in the catalytic region, and a C-terminal fibronectin type III-like domain. No signal peptide is predicted. It belongs to the PANTHER beta-glucosidase family PTHR42715 (subfamily SF27) and is a paralog of BGL22. Nothing is known experimentally about its substrate, expression or location. C. albicans does not assimilate cellobiose and has no cellulases, so the protein is not part of a cellulose-utilisation route; its physiological substrate remains to be identified.
