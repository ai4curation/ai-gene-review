# CYB5 notes

## Literature and PAINT

- `CYB5` (`A0A1D8PQP7`, CGD `CAL0000192012`) is the *Candida albicans*
  `PTHR19359:SF150` member and is itself one of the CGD seeds for the fungal
  `PANTHER:PTN000451736` sterol-biosynthesis PAINT node.
- Rogers et al. disrupted *C. albicans* `CYB5` and recovered viable mutants with
  low ergosterol and increased azole/terbinafine sensitivity, supporting the
  CGD `IMP` `GO:0006696 ergosterol biosynthetic process` and `GO:0009055
  electron transfer activity` rows rather than a pleiotropic membrane effect
  [PMID:15328107, "low ergosterol levels"].
- The root CGD `GO:0005575` ND row remains a true gap. UniProt and PAINT both
  infer that CYB5 is at the endoplasmic reticulum membrane, but I did not find a
  C. albicans CYB5 paper with direct localization or topology data.

## Newer Searches

- PubMed/web searches for *Candida* `CYB5` and PTHR19359 found a 2026 full-text
  paper on *N. glabratus* CYB5 [PMID:41891695], which I used for the
  `CANGA/CAGL0L03828g` review. I did not find post-2004 *C. albicans* papers
  with direct CYB5 biochemical or genetic data beyond the Rogers et al. sterol
  phenotype.
