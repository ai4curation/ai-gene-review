# ERG251 notes

ERG251 is the second Candida albicans member of PTHR11863. Unlike ERG25, it is
not represented in the frozen PTHR11863 entries CSV, but the current UniProt
record carries `DR   PANTHER; PTHR11863; STEROL DESATURASE; 1.`.

Key cached papers:

- Li et al. 2023 identified CZ66 as an inhibitor that points to Erg251 as the
  C-4 sterol methyl oxidase responsible for 14-methylsterol production under
  fluconazole stress [PMID:36475771, "The mechanism of action of CZ66 is
  targeting the C-4 sterol methyl oxidase, encoded by the ERG251 gene"].
- Todd et al. 2024 found recurrent ERG251 mutations during fluconazole
  adaptation and showed that ERG251 disruption changes sterol composition and
  has pleiotropic effects on azole susceptibility, filamentation and stress
  responses [PMID:39078851, "The altered sterol composition of the ERG251
  mutants had pleiotropic effects on transcription, filamentation, and stress
  responses"].

Re-review notes:

- Treated the plasma-membrane IDA as a non-core high-throughput proteomic hit.
