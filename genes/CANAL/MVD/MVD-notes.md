# CANAL MVD notes

## 2026-11-22 fungal PTHR10977 review

- `CANAL/MVD` is UniProt `A0A1D8PC43` and PANTHER `PTHR10977:SF3`, matching
  the conserved fungal diphosphomevalonate decarboxylase clade.
- The direct C. albicans publication cloned `MVD`, showed that it is a single
  copy gene, and showed that a C. albicans MVD copy under the `NOP1` promoter
  complements the S. cerevisiae `erg19(ts)` MVD defect
  [PMID:12073030].
- The cached PMID:12073030 record is abstract-only, so the three CGD IGI rows
  are accepted by combining the abstract-level complementation evidence with
  the full-text CGD curatorship.
- Literature searches for newer C. albicans papers found follow-on expression
  studies but no newer direct MVD catalytic, localization or deletion paper
  that changes the GO conclusions.
