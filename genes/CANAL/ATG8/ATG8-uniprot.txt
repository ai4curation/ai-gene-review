CC   -!- FUNCTION: Ubiquitin-like modifier involved in autophagosome formation
CC       (By similarity). The atg8-PE conjugate mediates tethering between
CC       adjacent membranes and stimulates membrane hemifusion, leading to
CC       expansion of the autophagosomal membrane during autophagy (By
CC       similarity). With atg4, mediates the delivery of the autophagosomes to
CC       the vacuole via the microtubule cytoskeleton (By similarity). Required
CC       for selective autophagic degradation of the nucleus (nucleophagy) as
CC       well as for mitophagy which contributes to regulate mitochondrial
CC       quantity and quality by eliminating the mitochondria to a basal level
CC       to fulfill cellular energy requirements and preventing excess ROS
CC       production (By similarity). Also participates in membrane fusion events
CC       that take place in the early secretory pathway (By similarity).
CC       Regulates autophagy-related endoplasmic reticulum stress response
CC       (PubMed:29544880, PubMed:36328097). Also involved in resistance to
CC       oxidative stress (PubMed:27473887). Is essential for virulence
CC       (PubMed:29544880). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P38182,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27473887, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29544880,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36328097}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasmic vesicle, autophagosome membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31284951}; Lipid-anchor
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:31284951}. Vacuole membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31284951}; Lipid-anchor
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:31284951}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Induced during biofilm formation.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22265407}.
CC   -!- PTM: The C-terminal 19 residues are removed to expose Gly-116 at the C-
CC       terminus. The C-terminal Gly is then amidated with
CC       phosphatidylethanolamine by an activating system similar to that for
CC       ubiquitin. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P38182}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Attenuates tolerance to oxidative stress when
CC       PEX1 is also deleted (PubMed:27473887). Leads to hypersensitivity to ER
CC       stress and causes the loss of virulence when YSY6 is also deleted
CC       (PubMed:29544880). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27473887,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29544880}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the ATG8 family. {ECO:0000305}.
