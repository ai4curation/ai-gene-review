Gene Description

Mkc1 is the cell-wall-integrity mitogen-activated protein kinase of Candida albicans. It is activated by Mkk2-dependent phosphorylation in response to cell-wall or membrane perturbation, surface contact, oxidative stress, temperature shifts, and echinocandins, and it promotes fungal cell-wall construction, contact-dependent invasive filamentation, biofilm maturation, and caspofungin-induced chitin remodeling.

Core Functions

MAP kinase activity in the Candida cell-wall-integrity pathway: Mkc1 is the terminal Ser/Thr MAP kinase that responds to Mkk2-dependent activation and drives cell-wall remodeling, contact-dependent invasive growth and biofilm maturation, and compensatory chitin responses during echinocandin stress.
