Gene Description

Candida albicans MKC1 encodes the Slt2/Spm1-family MAP kinase of the cell-wall-integrity pathway. Mkc1 is activated downstream of the Pkc1-Mkk2 module during cell-wall and surface-contact stress, phosphorylates protein substrates as a serine/threonine MAPK, and supports fungal cell-wall construction, chitin-mediated caspofungin remodeling, invasive filamentous growth, biofilm development, thermal and oxidative stress responses, and systemic virulence phenotypes.

MKC1 manual literature triage

Provider deep research was not run for this review. Manual triage combined the
cached CGD/GOA publications, the local UniProt record, the PTHR24055 PAINT
cache, a search for post-2023 Candida albicans MKC1 literature, and the most
recent full-text paper found by that search.

The review is anchored on the original MKC1 cloning and Slt2-complementation
paper PMID:7891715, the direct cell-wall-construction and morphological
transition study PMID:9493378, the contact-activation/biofilm paper
PMID:15800048, the Mkk2-Mkc1 CWI pathway study PMID:26197240, and the 2023
caspofungin/chitin/glucan-unmasking study PMID:37377417. The 2025 Sdd3 study
PMID:39688394 was added as newer support for the upstream Rho1-PKC-MAPK model
that controls Candida chitin synthesis during biofilm formation.

No newer direct MKC1 paper was found that supersedes the CGD experimental rows.
The main electronic-annotation problems are not PAINT problems; they come from
EnsemblFungi copying fission-yeast Pmk1 calcium, cytokinesis, and glucose
signaling outputs onto C. albicans MKC1.
