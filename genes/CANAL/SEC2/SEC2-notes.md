# SEC2 notes

## 2026-11-20 Sec2/Rabin3 fungal-family review

- Reviewed the C. albicans long Sec2 ortholog against the same `PTHR14430`
  family split used for budding yeast and fission yeast.
- Accepted `PANTHER:PTN000374167` GEF transfer and the `IPR040351` InterPro2GO
  GEF row; accepted the fungal `PANTHER:PTN001024876` cell-tip IBA because
  Candida SEC2 is itself experimentally localized at the hyphal Spitzenkorper.
- Accepted the CGD hyphal-growth, Spitzenkorper, and Golgi-to-plasma-membrane
  vesicle rows. Bishop et al. 2010 shows that Sec2 S584 phosphorylation is
  required for stable Spitzenkorper localization and normal hyphal growth, and
  Caballero-Lima et al. 2014 physically associates Sec2p/SEC2 mRNA with
  secretory-vesicle fractions.
- Marked the CGD `molecular_function` ND row for removal because GEF activity
  is no longer unknown, and marked `filamentous growth` as too general relative
  to the same paper's specific `hyphal growth` row.
