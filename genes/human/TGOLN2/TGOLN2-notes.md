# TGOLN2 (TGN46) notes

Deep research providers unavailable (no API keys). Notes from UniProt O43493 and cached literature.

## Identity / topology
- Trans-Golgi network integral membrane protein 2 (TGN46/TGN51/TGN48; human ortholog of rodent TGN38).
  Single-pass type I membrane protein: signal peptide 1-21, large luminal domain 22-381, TM 382-402,
  short cytoplasmic tail 403-437 carrying tyrosine-based (YQRL-type) sorting motifs.
- [UniProt O43493 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane; Single-pass type I membrane protein. Golgi apparatus, trans-Golgi network membrane"]; [UniProt "Primarily in trans-Golgi network. Cycles between the trans-Golgi network and the cell surface returning via endosomes"].
- Localization conclusion: primarily the trans-Golgi network membrane; cycles to the plasma membrane (luminal
  domain becomes extracellular) and recycles back via endosomes/clathrin-coated vesicles. Core location is the TGN.

## Function
- [UniProt "May be involved in regulating membrane traffic to and from trans-Golgi network"]. Implicated in
  cargo sorting and secretory/transport vesicle formation at the TGN (e.g. post-Golgi LH3/collagen trafficking,
  PMID:27435297). Its specific molecular function is not established.
