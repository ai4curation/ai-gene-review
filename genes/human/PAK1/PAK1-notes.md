PAK1 is the canonical human group I p21-activated kinase. It binds GTP-loaded CDC42/RAC, autophosphorylates, and phosphorylates substrates in LIMK/cofilin, JNK/MAPK, RAF, centrosomal microtubule, stress-granule and nuclear DNA-damage pathways.

- PMID:8805275 cloned hPAK1 and showed that constitutively active hPAK1 activates the JNK MAPK pathway.
- PMID:10559936 showed PAK1 phosphorylation of LIMK1 at Thr508 and linked Rac/Cdc42 to cofilin-dependent actin remodeling.
- PMID:27012601 placed PAK1 with GIT1/betaPIX in regulation of gamma-tubulin recruitment and interphase centrosomal microtubule nucleation.
- PMID:23260667 showed that PAK1 phosphorylates MORC2 on Ser739 after DNA damage to support ATPase-coupled chromatin remodeling.
- PMID:25766321 showed PAK1 interaction with RUFY3 and support for gastric-cancer cell migration and invasion.
