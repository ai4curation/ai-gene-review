CC       rare condition characterized by dry conjunctival mucosae, irritable
CC       eyes, epiphora (constant tearing), and xerostomia (dryness of the
CC       mouth), which increases risk of dental erosion, dental caries,
CC       periodontal disease, and oral infections. ALSG has variable
CC       expressivity, and affected individuals may have aplasia or hypoplasia

CC       absence of the lacrimal puncta. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15654336}. Note=The

CC       entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Lacrimo-auriculo-dento-digital syndrome 3 (LADD3)
CC       [MIM:620193]: A form of lacrimo-auriculo-dento-digital syndrome, an

CC       lacrimal and salivary glands and ducts, cup-shaped ears, hearing loss,
CC       hypodontia and enamel hypoplasia, and distal limb segments anomalies.
CC       In addition to these cardinal features, facial dysmorphism,
CC       malformations of the kidney and respiratory system and abnormal
CC       genitalia have been reported. Craniosynostosis and severe syndactyly
CC       are not observed. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16501574,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16630169}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
