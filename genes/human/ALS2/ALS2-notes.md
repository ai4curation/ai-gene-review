# ALS2 evidence notes

## Full review, 2026-09-27

ALS2 is the approved human symbol (HGNC:443), accession Q96Q42. The previous symbol ALS2CR6 and UniProt synonym KIAA1563 were checked alongside ALS2 for current-main directories and open review overlap. All were absent at main 1b2db4e9260aedb54abf17fb9fea71c4aee4cc5e; the three canonical seed files match the exact ordinary seed6 recovery/import. The original 48 annotation source objects, qualifiers, WITH/FROM identifiers and three alternative products are preserved. No NEW annotation is proposed.

The substantive core is the long alsin protein's Rab5 exchange activity in endosomal membrane dynamics. ALS2 supplies a mechanistic step, GDP release that permits Rab5 activation, rather than merely being necessary for neuronal survival. Functional self-association is part of this mechanism. Cytosol and early endosomes are the compact core locations; accepted broad or other subcellular locations remain valid without assigning the same biochemical reaction to every observed compartment. No neuronal death, clinical diagnosis or protein-family membership is treated as a new molecular activity.

- [PMID:15247254] (DOI 10.1074/jbc.M406120200): cached abstract plus original full article at https://mcm.h-its.org/mcm/projects/rab/literature/ALS2Activity.pdf, Methods and Figures 1–7 read. A human brain cDNA library yields ALS2 self-interactors. Tagged proteins are expressed in COS-7 cells; affinity isolation, co-immunoprecipitation, gel filtration, Rab5A binding and loss of preloaded tritiated GDP establish self-associated GEF function. The antibody immunogen is explicitly mouse, and reused construct sources refer to the preceding study; neither host nor antibody species is silently assigned to every insert. - [PMID:15033976] (DOI 10.1074/jbc.M313504200): official identity and original full article at https://mcm.h-its.org/mcm/projects/rab/literature/Alsin.pdf read. Human ALS2 derives from SH-SY5Y/KIAA1563 cDNA. Bacterial VPS9-domain constructs stimulate Rab5 GDP release; human full-length ALS2 coexpression in Sf9 increases Rac-GTP capture, which does not isolate direct Rac exchange catalysis. Human tagged ALS2 localization in mouse NIH3T3 is distinct from endogenous mouse staining and rat E18 hippocampal cultures. The title's Rac-GEF interpretation must be distinguished from the assay actually performed.
- [PMID:16049005] (DOI 10.1074/jbc.M506216200): official identity, cached abstract and complete author-uploaded primary article at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/7696710_ALS2Alsin_regulates_Rac-PAK_signaling_and_neurite_outgrowth read. Human full-length ALS2 is expressed in CHO cells. GST-PAK binding-domain capture measures Rac-GTP, while immunoprecipitated PAK1 phosphorylates myelin basic protein in a separate kinase assay. The DH deletion abolishes the response; isolated DH/PH is insufficient. Rat E18 cortical/hippocampal cultures supply endogenous growth-cone localization and neurite-outgrowth assays, including human ALS2 transfection and dominant-negative Rac/Rab controls. These are positive cellular responses; neither a GTP-hydrolysis assay nor direct ALS2–PAK1 binding is demonstrated.
- [PMID:17409386] (DOI 10.1074/jbc.M610682200): official identity and indexed original full Methods/Results from https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2007/073131/200730014A/200730014A0002.pdf read. Methods expressly says PCR/reverse-transcription PCR-amplified human cDNAs. ALS2 preferentially binds activated Rac1, fails to activate Rac1 under the tested conditions, and is recruited to ruffles/macropinosomes before Rab5-GEF-dependent fusion with transferrin-positive endosomes. Endogenous HeLa ALS2 recruitment is separately demonstrated. This provides direct human corroboration of the main trafficking mechanism and a qualified conflict with the earlier Rac-GEF interpretation, not a reason to erase all positive Rac–PAK results.
- [PMID:30224357] (DOI 10.1074/jbc.RA118.003849): official PubMed identity and indexed full PMC6222102 Methods/Results read. Fresh WT ALS2 preparations, endogenous comparison, gel filtration and detergent/storage controls revise the earlier octamer estimate to a predominant tetramer under these conditions. Human pathogenic-variant constructs are traced to earlier human-cDNA experiments; COS-7, HeLa and NSC-34 are distinct hosts. Variant assembly, stability and localization defects do not fix a universal in-tissue stoichiometry. The gene-level core therefore uses a functional oligomer, not an obligate octamer or homodimer.
- [PMID:15371724] (DOI 10.1097/00001756-200410050-00002): complete abstract and official identity checked. The ARDA antibody raised against an ALS2 RLD peptide marks rodent somatodendritic compartments and human spinal motor-neuron soma/dendrites. The full specificity controls were not recovered. Rat-neuron nuclear/axon negatives are not universal human NOT assertions.
- [PMID:16085057] (DOI 10.1016/j.bbamcr.2004.12.008): complete abstract and official identity checked. Monkey COS-7 overexpression-associated mitochondrial/Golgi disturbances are distinct. No new microtubule-nucleation or mitochondrial activity follows from these phenotypes.
- [PMID:16670179] (DOI 10.1093/brain/awl104): complete abstract and official identity checked. Human G540E clinical segregation and SK-N-BE localization/survival experiments are real primary data. - [PMID:21300063] (DOI 10.1016/j.febslet.2011.01.045): complete abstract and official identity checked. - [PMID:17093100] (DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2084-06.2006): official identity and indexed full PMC2556290 Methods/Results read. ### Ontology and source decisions

Official live AmiGO definitions were read. GO:0005096 requires increased GTP hydrolysis, whereas GO:0005085 describes nucleotide exchange. The fully inspected GDP-release source, [PMID:15247254], is refined to GEF; the corresponding [PMID:12837691] assertion remains unresolved because its complete experiments were not accessible. Likewise GO:0043547 concerns hydrolytic activity, while the human-ALS2 CHO experiment measures Rac-GTP occupancy: the source is refined to positive Rac signaling. GO:0043539 requires kinase binding and activation; downstream PAK1 activity is instead represented by positive regulation of protein kinase activity. These distinctions preserve the positive experiments.

GO:0042803 specifically requires a homodimer. The full self-association paper proposes larger assemblies and competing models, and the later study revises stoichiometry. Generalization to identical protein binding preserves the demonstrated interaction. GO:0032045 describes a functional GEF complex and does not require different subunit species, so the purified/self-associated functional ALS2 unit supports refinement of the generic complex row and the integrated core. No claim of a native ALS2CL heterocomplex is added.

GO:0007032 endosome organization and GO:0016197 endosomal transport have distinct formal scopes; both are supported by the exchange/fusion mechanism. No new process assertion is manufactured from clinical necessity. No matching Q96Q42 activity was found in the local GO-CAM index. No NEW was needed, and no ancestor/descendant duplicate was introduced as a proposed NEW.

PAINT PTN002338289 and PTN002918520 remain ancestral judgments whose exact IBD placement/MSA was not reconstructed. All supplied extant sources are assessed explicitly. Official primary mappings identify mouse Als2 Q920R0/MGI:1921268, rat Als2 P0C5Y8/RGD:1310372, fly Als2 FBgn0037116, and mouse Als2cl MGI:2447532. The exact historical Ensembl peptide version was not recovered. The human target's inclusion is valid experimental grounding, not circularity.
