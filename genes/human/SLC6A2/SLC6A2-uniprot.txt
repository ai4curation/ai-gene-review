CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mediates sodium- and chloride-dependent transport of
CC       norepinephrine (also known as noradrenaline), the primary signaling
CC       neurotransmitter in the autonomic sympathetic nervous system
CC       (PubMed:2008212, PubMed:8125921, PubMed:38750358). Is responsible for
CC       norepinephrine re-uptake and clearance from the synaptic cleft, thus
CC       playing a crucial role in norepinephrine inactivation and homeostasis
CC       (By similarity). Can also mediate sodium- and chloride-dependent
CC       transport of dopamine (PubMed:11093780, PubMed:8125921,
CC       PubMed:39395208, PubMed:39048818). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O55192,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11093780, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2008212,
