# MSRB2 notes

- UniProt Q9Y3D2 is the reviewed human mitochondrial MSRB2 protein in PTHR10173:SF37.
  The record curates a mitochondrial transit peptide, a Cys-based MsrB catalytic
  residue, four ProRule Zn(2+) ligands and EC 1.8.4.12/1.8.4.14 catalytic activities
  by mouse orthology.
- PMID:14699060 characterized the mammalian MsrB family. It localized mouse MsrB2 to
  mitochondria with N-terminal signal and full-length GFP fusions, measured MsrB2
  activity on dabsyl-Met-R-SO, and detected structural zinc in purified MsrB2.
- PMID:18424444 showed that mitochondrial overexpression of MSRB2 in human MOLT-4
  cells protects against hydrogen-peroxide stress. This supports a non-core
  oxidative-stress role downstream of the core Met-R-SO reductase reaction.
- PMID:23911929 used purified mouse MsrB2 to reduce MICAL-oxidized Met46/Met49 in
  actin in vitro, but its cellular macrophage data assign LPS-stimulated actin
  polymerization to cytosolic MsrB1. I kept MSRB2 actin binding as a non-core
  substrate call and marked actin filament polymerization as over-annotated.
- Reactome R-HSA-5676917 and R-HSA-5676934 include MSRB2 in methionine-sulfoxide
  protein repair. Their cytosol event location does not fit mitochondrial MSRB2, and
  the free-Met-R-SO EC/Rhea activity is described as very inefficient, so those rows
  are over-annotation rather than core MSRB2 biology.
- PMID:27499296, PMID:32296183 and PMID:32814053 are high-throughput interactome
  sources for bare GO:0005515 rows. The rows keep their exact IntAct partners but
  are removed under the protein-binding policy.
