Gene Description

ARID2, also called BAF200, is a nuclear DNA-binding subunit that specifies the PBAF branch of mammalian SWI/SNF chromatin-remodeling complexes. It supports PBAF assembly and promoter-selective transcriptional regulation while contributing to remodeling powered by the BRG1 ATPase. Its ARID region binds DNA without an established sequence preference. ARID2 also associates with RAD51 and promotes its recruitment during homologous-recombination repair of DNA double-strand breaks. ARID2-containing assemblies with and without BRG1 have been recovered, indicating that its repair interactions cannot be reduced to a single fixed complex composition.
