CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor for follitropin, the follicle-
CC       stimulating hormone (PubMed:11847099, PubMed:24058690,
CC       PubMed:24692546). Mediates FSH signaling through cAMP/protein kinase A
CC       (PKA) and phosphatidylinositol-3 kinase (PI3K)/protein kinase B (AKT)
CC       pathways to regulate ovarian follicular development and steroidogenesis
CC       (PubMed:24058690). Has diverse extragonadal functions: signals via a
CC       GNAI2-coupled FSHR on osteoclasts and their precursors, resulting in
CC       activation of MEK/ERK, NF-kappa-B, and AKT pathways and thus increases
CC       osteoclastogenesis and bone resorption (By similarity). Regulates
CC       glucose-stimulated insulin secretion in pancreatic beta-cells in a bell
CC       curve manner, with moderate FSH levels promoting insulin secretion via
CC       Galphas/cAMP/PKA pathways while high FSH levels inhibit this effect
CC       through Galphai coupling (By similarity). In adipose tissue, inhibits
CC       thermogenesis by suppressing UCP1 expression and promotes lipogenesis
CC       via CREB/PPARgamma pathways (By similarity). Also affects hepatic
CC       cholesterol metabolism by increasing serum total and low-density-
CC       lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) by inhibiting the expression of LDL
CC       receptor (LDLR) (PubMed:26583582). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P35378,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11847099, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24058690,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24692546, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26583582}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotrimer. Functions as a homotrimer binding the FSH hormone
CC       heterodimer composed of CGA and FSHB (PubMed:24692546,
CC       PubMed:36720854). Interacts with ARRB2 (By similarity). Interacts with
CC       APPL2; interaction is independent of follicle stimulating hormone
CC       stimulation (PubMed:17030088). Interacts with GNAS (PubMed:36720854).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P35378, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17030088,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24692546, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36720854}.
