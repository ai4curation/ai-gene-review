Gene Description

CYP2U1 is a membrane-associated, heme-containing cytochrome P450 expressed in the thymus and brain and localized to the endoplasmic reticulum and mitochondria. It uses NADPH-derived electrons delivered by cytochrome P450 reductase to hydroxylate long-chain fatty acids, forming 19- and 20-HETE from arachidonic acid. It also oxidizes the indole ring of N-arachidonoylserotonin, and recombinant enzyme can metabolize sorafenib and sunitinib. Biallelic loss of function causes hereditary spastic paraplegia 56; patient and mouse studies associate deficiency with disturbed mitochondrial physiology, lipid balance and folate-related metabolism.
