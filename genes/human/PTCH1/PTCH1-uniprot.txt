CC   -!- FUNCTION: Negative regulator of the smoothened signaling pathway, which
CC       acts as a receptor for hedgehog (SHH, IHH and DHH) morphogens
CC       (PubMed:21537345, PubMed:29954986, PubMed:29995851, PubMed:30139912,
CC       PubMed:31127104, PubMed:31555730, PubMed:37611095, PubMed:40128518,
CC       PubMed:9811851). In absence of hedgehog, acts as an inhibitor of
CC       smoothened protein (SMO) by preventing SMO access to cholesterol:
CC       mechanistically, acts as a cholesterol transporter, which depeletes
CC       cholesterol from the outer leaflet of plasma membrane, thereby
CC       preventing cholesterol-binding to the extracellular CRD region of SMO
CC       (PubMed:31555730, PubMed:37611095). Hedgehog-binding inhibits PTCH1
CC       cholesterol transporter activity, promoting SMO activation by
CC       cholesterol and transduction of the smoothened signaling pathway
CC       (PubMed:31548691). Molecular mechanism governing cholesterol transport
CC       are still unclear: may move cholesterol from the outer to the inner
CC       leaflet of the membrane in exchange for potassium or sodium ion export
CC       in the opposite direction (PubMed:37611095). Seems to have a tumor
CC       suppressor function, as inactivation of this protein is probably a
CC       necessary, if not sufficient step for tumorigenesis (PubMed:8658145).
