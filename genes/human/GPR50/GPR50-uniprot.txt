CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor that plays a role in numerous
CC       physiological processes including regulation of energy metabolism,
CC       neurite outgrowth or cell migration (PubMed:19699797). Promotes self-
CC       renewal and neuronal differentiation of neural progenitor cells through
CC       activation of the NOTCH and WNT/beta-catenin signaling pathways (By
CC       similarity). Modulates the KAT5-dependent glucocorticoid receptor
CC       signaling by modulating KAT5 subcellular compartmentalization
CC       (PubMed:21858214). Also plays a role in the activation TGFBR1 in the
CC       absence of TGFBR2 by interfering with FKBP1A binding to TGFBR1, leading
CC       to induction of both canonical and non-canonical SMAD signaling
CC       pathways resulting in inhibition of proliferation or promotion of
CC       migration (PubMed:29572483). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O88495,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16778767, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19699797,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21858214, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29572483}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [C-terminal domain]: Upon cleavage by CAPN1, functions as a
CC       scaffold in the nucleus for interacting partners such as GTF2I to
CC       promote FOS promoter activation. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O88495,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19699797, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21858214,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31900622}.
