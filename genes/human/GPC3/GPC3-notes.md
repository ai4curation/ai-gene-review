## Function
- Glypican-3: GPI-anchored heparan sulfate proteoglycan; core cleaved by a furin-like convertase into
  disulfide-linked alpha/beta chains; processing required for Wnt modulation [PMID:14610063].
- Binds Frizzled directly via its HS chains, promoting Wnt-Frizzled signaling complexes and their
  endocytosis [PMID:24496449].
- Binds DPP4/CD26 and inhibits its peptidase activity [PMID:17549790]. GO:0060422 'peptidyl-dipeptidase'
  denotes C-terminal dipeptide release (ACE-like), whereas DPP4 removes N-terminal dipeptides.
- Mouse (UniProt by similarity): competes with PTCH1 for Hedgehog; the GPC3-SHH complex is endocytosed and
  degraded in lysosomes.
- Loss of function causes Simpson-Golabi-Behmel overgrowth syndrome [PMID:8589713]. Oncofetal HCC antigen;
  AFP among co-IP partners [PMID:39822733].

## Localization
- "GPCs are heparan sulfate proteoglycans (HSPGs) that are linked to the cell surface via
  glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)" [PMID:14610063]; GPI-anchor at Asn554 (UniProt).
- HS-chain assembly in ER/Golgi lumen and lysosomal degradation (Reactome).
- Conclusion: external side of plasma membrane / cell surface (GPI-anchored, fully extracellular); shed soluble
  form; biosynthetic ER/Golgi and lysosomal turnover pools.
