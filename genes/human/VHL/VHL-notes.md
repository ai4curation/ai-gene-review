# VHL notes

- Deep research dispatch failed in this checkout: the generic wrapper only exposed the `claude_code` provider, and both the `perplexity` and `falcon --fallback perplexity-lite` recipes exited before writing a provider file. This review therefore uses the seeded GOA rows, cached GOA PMIDs, cached Reactome records, UniProt, and the local PAINT-loss report.
- Core function: VHL is the substrate-recognition subunit of the Elongin B/C-CUL2-RBX1 Cullin-RING ubiquitin ligase. Its central job is to bind prolyl-hydroxylated HIF-alpha subunits in oxygenated cells and present them for ubiquitylation and proteasomal degradation; Reactome summarizes the mechanism as hydroxylated HIF-alpha subunits being bound by VHL in a complex with Elongin B, Elongin C, CUL2 and RBX1, followed by HIF-alpha ubiquitination and destruction [Reactome:R-HSA-1234176, "HIF-alpha subunits hydroxylated at proline residues are bound by VHL, an E3 ubiquitin ligase in a complex containing ElonginB, Elongin C, CUL2, and RBX1"].
- The old 1995 Elongin paper directly supports VHL binding to Elongin B/C and inhibition of Elongin SIII transcription elongation in vitro [PMID:7660122, "The VHL protein was shown to bind tightly and specifically to the Elongin B and C subunits and to inhibit Elongin (SIII) transcriptional activity in vitro."]. That makes a negative transcription-elongation row defensible as non-core, but VHL itself is not a transcription elongation factor.
- VHL also directly represses the HIF transcriptional arm: FIH-1 binds HIF-1alpha and VHL, and VHL can recruit histone deacetylases to inhibit HIF-1alpha transactivation [PMID:11641274, "VHL also functions as a transcriptional corepressor that inhibits HIF-1alpha transactivation function by recruiting histone deacetylases."].
- RAPTOR/mTORC1 is a supported newer substrate branch but should remain peripheral to the HIF-alpha core function. The 2021 paper reports that "VHL interacts with RAPTOR and increases RAPTOR degradation by ubiquitination, thereby inhibiting mTORC1 signaling" [PMID:34290272].
- MAP1LC3B/autophagy is also direct but peripheral: the 2019 paper reports a VHL LIR motif, MAP1LC3B binding, MAP1LC3B ubiquitination, and inhibition of LC3B-mediated autophagy [PMID:30902965, "pVHL interacts with MAPL1LC3B and inhibits LC3B-mediated autophagy via MAP1LC3B ubiquitination"].
- Several seeded biological-process rows are downstream phenotypes or low-mechanism tumor-gene readouts rather than direct work by VHL. The glioma stem-like-cell paper supports that VHL expression suppresses STAT3/JAK2 expression and tumorigenic behavior [PMID:23338840, "These findings suggest that VHL regulated the tumorigenicity and self-renewal ability of glioma cancer stem cells by inhibiting the JAK/STAT signaling pathway."], but it does not show that VHL directly executes a JAK/STAT signaling step. The PL6 paper shows that reintroduced VHL restores PL6 expression [PMID:17973242, "The restoration of VHL function in vitro in the VHL-deficient CC-RCC cell lines was found to reinstate PL6 expression"], without defining a direct transcriptional mechanism.
- PMID:12169691 is abstract-only in the cache. The abstract directly supports JADE1 binding, partial colocalization with VHL, and protein stabilization by VHL [PMID:12169691, "VHL increases Jade-1 protein half-life up to 3-fold."]. It does not expose enough detail for the seeded mitochondrion row, and the apoptosis/differentiation/cell-morphogenesis rows appear to be over-propagated from the partner's name and tumor-biology context rather than from a direct VHL activity.
- PMID:34431623 assays synthetic VHL-derived peptides and shows amyloid-like properties for two of them [PMID:34431623, "predicted six peptide fragments from pVHL to be amyloidogenic"]. Those peptide biophysics results do not make amyloid fibril formation a normal VHL function.
- There are 80 bare `GO:0005515 protein binding` rows. All are retained as separate `existing_annotations` entries with their original `supporting_entities` under issue #7042 and removed only under the project policy that bare generic protein binding is uninformative.
- `just paint-losses human VHL` reported two PAINT losses on the VHL lineage: an IRD blocking `GO:0005634 nucleus` at `PTN000389250`, and an IRD blocking `GO:0030891 VCB complex`. Neither term has a live IBA row in the seeded VHL review; the live VHL IBA rows all come from `PTN000389241`.
