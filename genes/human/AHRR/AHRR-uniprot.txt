CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mediates dioxin toxicity and is involved in regulation of
CC       cell growth and differentiation. Represses the transcription activity
CC       of AHR by competing with AHR for heterodimer formation with ARNT and
CC       subsequently binding to the xenobiotic response element (XRE) sequence
CC       present in the promoter regulatory region of a variety of genes.
CC       Represses CYP1A1 by binding the XRE sequence and recruiting ANKRA2,
CC       HDAC4 and/or HDAC5. Autoregulates its expression by associating with
CC       its own XRE site. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17890447,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18172554}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 1]: Represses AHR transcription activity
CC       (PubMed:19380484). Also represses transcription mediated by HIF1A and
CC       EPAS1/HIF2A but does not repress NR1I2/PXR or ESR1 transcriptional
CC       activities (PubMed:19380484). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19380484}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 2]: Does not repress AHR transcription activity.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19380484}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterodimer with ARNT; formation of the heterodimer results in
CC       repression of the transcription activity of AHR (PubMed:28904176).
CC       Interacts with ANKRA2, HDAC4 and HDAC5 (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3U1U7, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28904176}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: [Isoform 1]: Interacts with ARNT.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19380484}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: [Isoform 2]: Does not interact with ARNT.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19380484}.
