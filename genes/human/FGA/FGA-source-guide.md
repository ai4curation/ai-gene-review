# FGA integrated evidence guide

FGA supplies structural alpha-chain material to fibrinogen and fibrin. Its assembled alpha-C material also binds tPA and plasminogen and supports plasmin generation. These distinct contributions support the structural and enzyme-substrate adaptor core functions. Thrombin, Factor XIIIa and tPA perform their respective catalytic steps.

The [literature journal](FGA-notes.md), [direct source guide](FGA-direct-source-guide.md) and [database source guide](FGA-database-source-guide.md) give the relevant experiments and access limits. The review contains 131 decisions and 90 complete reference assessments. The two natural splice records are preserved, while two separate cleavage-product classes retain official coordinates and their limits. No quote or NEW annotation is introduced.

The [direct source records](FGA-direct-source-records.yaml), [database source evidence](FGA-database-source-evidence.yaml), [product map](FGA-product-source-map.yaml), [raw processed-product joins](FGA-product-source-joins.yaml), [ontology evidence](FGA-ontology-evidence.yaml) and [phylogeny evidence](FGA-phylogeny-evidence.yaml) provide source identity and interpretation. The review YAML is the authority for final decisions; these records preserve provenance rather than duplicate decisions.

The decisions comprise 76 ACCEPT, 36 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, three MODIFY, six TOO_GENERAL and 10 UNDECIDED. Mixed free-peptide/mature-chain rows and unresolved specific proteomics joins retain their uncertainty. Generic K18 and kinase-substrate interactions remain non-core under the explicit [campaign curation instructions](../../../projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions), which override the generic validator’s REMOVE preference for these supported interactions. Their evidence does not justify inventing a more specific molecular function. Related BioPlex maps are not counted as independent replication.

The final source set contains 87 genuine normal files: 37 publications, 47 Reactome entries and three PANTHER/PAINT files. The 42 additional discovery caches remain private and unchanged. Primary read scopes are explicit; source records do not imply that every figure, supplement or pathway branch was independently examined.

The same-term HDA source limits and the two mixed free-FPA/mature-chain rows remain explicit. Heterotypic adhesion is retained as a non-core contribution within intact fibrinogen by curator deference to the positive original abstract; the full chain-resolved assay remains inaccessible. BioPlex refinement identifies a measured partner class in tagged-ORF detergent-lysate AP-MS, not direct affinity, endogenous colocalization or a regulatory activity. A secretory/intracellular compartment mismatch remains an unresolved physiological limitation.

The feedback assessment uses the original BioPlex 2.0 Methods, “Generation of Stable Cell Lines,” “Affinity-Purification Mass Spectrometry” and “Identification of Interacting Proteins” ([PMID:28514442](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28514442/)); BioPlex 3.0 STAR Methods, “Creation of Stable Cell Lines,” “Affinity Purification of Protein Complexes” and “Identification of High Confidence Interacting Proteins” ([PMID:33961781](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33961781/)); and its 293T network row containing FGA/2243 and YWHAQ/10971. These methods describe tagged constructs, NP40 lysates, gene-level protein grouping and background-aware scoring. They do not establish direct binary binding or endogenous colocalization. The original abstract of [PMID:8100742](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8100742/) directly reports intact-fibrinogen-dependent leukocyte/endothelial adhesion; accepting that bounded complex contribution does not claim recovery of its full text or an alpha-chain receptor interface.

Official strict `is_a`/`part_of` ancestry distinguishes [plasminogen activation](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0031639) and [fibrinolysis](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0042730): neither is an ancestor of the other. Both describe supported aspects of the existing alpha-C cofactor core. The broader structural molecular function additionally covers soluble fibrinogen assembly, so the synthesis avoids a duplicate parent/child molecular-function core.
