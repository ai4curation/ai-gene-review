# FGA integrated evidence guide

FGA supplies structural alpha-chain material to fibrinogen and fibrin. Its assembled alpha-C material also binds tPA and plasminogen and supports plasmin generation. These distinct contributions support the structural and enzyme-substrate adaptor core functions. Thrombin, Factor XIIIa and tPA perform their respective catalytic steps.

The [literature journal](FGA-notes.md), [direct source guide](FGA-direct-source-guide.md) and [database source guide](FGA-database-source-guide.md) give the relevant experiments and access limits. The review contains 131 decisions and 90 complete reference assessments. The two natural splice records are preserved, while two separate cleavage-product classes retain official coordinates and their limits. No quote or NEW annotation is introduced.

The [direct source records](FGA-direct-source-records.yaml), [database source evidence](FGA-database-source-evidence.yaml), [product map](FGA-product-source-map.yaml), [raw processed-product joins](FGA-product-source-joins.yaml), [ontology evidence](FGA-ontology-evidence.yaml) and [phylogeny evidence](FGA-phylogeny-evidence.yaml) provide source identity and interpretation. The review YAML is the authority for final decisions; these records preserve provenance rather than duplicate decisions.

The decisions comprise 76 ACCEPT, 33 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, three MODIFY, six TOO_GENERAL and 13 UNDECIDED. Mixed free-peptide/mature-chain rows and unresolved specific proteomics joins retain their uncertainty. Generic K18 and kinase-substrate interactions remain non-core under the project binding policy; their evidence does not justify inventing a more specific molecular function. Related BioPlex maps are not counted as independent replication.

The final source set contains 87 genuine normal files: 37 publications, 47 Reactome entries and three PANTHER/PAINT files. The 42 additional discovery caches remain private and unchanged. Primary read scopes are explicit; source records do not imply that every figure, supplement or pathway branch was independently examined.
