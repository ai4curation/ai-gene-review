# FGA database source guide

This guide records the sources used for 74 database-derived annotation contexts. Manual decisions reside in `FGA-ai-review.yaml`; the [source map](FGA-database-source-evidence.yaml) retains source keys, indices and target routes without duplicating those decisions. Model coordinates refer to the represented entity, not an experimentally established processing schedule for every natural splice product.

The public ontology evidence contains actual official definitions and strict `is_a`/`part_of` paths used for broad-term decisions, plus the GO:0140767 enzyme-substrate adaptor child relationship to GO:0030674. The refinement uses independent human binding/coupled-activation evidence; it does not infer a new ancestral PAINT placement. The source snapshot records preserve dates and hashes. The general extracellular location remains useful across soluble fibrinogen, fibrin, released peptides and pathological variant-containing material. No single narrower complex term covers that entire source scope.

The curated fibrinopeptide A and mature-chain identifiers are separate from the two natural splice products. The [product-source map](FGA-product-source-map.yaml) preserves all six raw product-specific contexts and their exact seed joins. Experimental alpha-C fragments, cross-linked oligomers and proteolytic truncations remain experimental preparations.
