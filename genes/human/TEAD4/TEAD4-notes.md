# TEAD4 curation notes

- Keep TEAD4's conserved Pol II cis-regulatory DNA binding, transcription-factor activity, nuclear/nucleoplasmic location, YAP/TAZ coactivator binding, VGLL4 coregulator binding, and Hippo-signaling output as the core biology.
- Remove or modify every generic `GO:0005515` row independently while preserving each `WITH/FROM`; rows where the partner is YAP, TAZ, or VGLL4 can move to a more informative transcription coactivator/coregulator binding term.
- Modify `skeletal system development`: the cited mouse FR-19 paper is about an FGF-inducible TEF relative expressed during skeletal muscle differentiation, not skeletal-system development.
- Keep the old human RTEF-1 muscle-development row no stronger than non-core/over-annotated because the cached abstract only reports cloning, phylogenetic analysis, and tissue expression.
- Recover the clearer part of the old muscle biology by modifying the erroneous skeletal-system row to skeletal muscle cell differentiation: mouse FR-19/Tead4 expression increases during C2C12 myoblast differentiation [PMID:8662936 "We have also found that FR-19 mRNA expression increases during mouse C2C12 myoblast differentiation in vitro."] and the original human RTEF-1 paper found that skeletal muscle had the highest abundance of both RTEF-1 and TEF-1 mRNAs [PMID:8921372 "skeletal muscle showed the highest abundance of both mRNAs, with lower levels detected in pancreas, placenta, and heart"].
- Mouse Tead4 has a non-redundant early trophectoderm role upstream of Cdx2, although these mouse genetic data are better recorded as paralog-specific context than as a new human GO process term. Yagi et al. reported that Tead4(-/-) morulae fail to make trophoblast stem cells, trophectoderm, or blastocoel cavities [PMID:17913785 "Consequently, Tead4(-/-) morulae do not produce trophoblast stem cells, trophectoderm or blastocoel cavities"], and Nishioka et al. found that Tead4-null embryos fail to form a blastocoel and lose late Cdx2 and other trophectoderm markers [PMID:18083014 "Tead4-/- embryos died at pre-implantation stages without forming the blastocoel."].
