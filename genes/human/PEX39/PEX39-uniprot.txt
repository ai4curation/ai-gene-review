CC   -!- FUNCTION: Cytosolic peroxin that promotes the peroxisomal import of
CC       proteins containing a type-2 peroxisomal targeting signal (PTS2) by
CC       binding to PEX7 and stabilizing its interaction with PTS2-containing
CC       cargo (PubMed:37160800, PubMed:40739340). PEX39 subsequently transfers
CC       the PEX7-cargo complex to PEX13 at the peroxisomal membrane, enabling
CC       PEX7 to bind PEX13 and facilitating translocation of PTS2-containing
CC       proteins into the peroxisome. Both PEX39 and PEX13 contain a conserved
CC       KPWE motif, which enables competitive binding to PEX7 and ensures
CC       efficient handover of the complex (PubMed:40739340).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37160800, ECO:0000269|PubMed:40739340}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via KPWE motif) with PEX7; stabilizes interaction
CC       of PEX7 with PTS2-containing proteins. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:40739340}.
