# LY6K notes

Deep research (falcon / perplexity-lite) failed; notes from cached publication and UniProt Q17RY6.

- Ly6/uPAR three-finger (snake-toxin-like) protein; signal 1-17, chain 18-138, GPI anchor at Gly138, propeptide 139-165 removed.
- GPI-anchored cell membrane, membrane raft, acrosome (UniProt, by similarity to mouse Q9CWP4).
- Mouse: required for sperm migration into oviduct and ZP binding; interacts with TEX101 (UniProt).
- Cancer-testis antigen; serum biomarker in NSCLC/ESCC; siRNA suppresses cancer cell growth [PMID:18089789 "knocked down of LY6K expression with small interfering RNAs resulted in growth suppression of the lung and esophageal cancer cells"].

Localization conclusion: GPI-anchored plasma membrane (ectodomain exposed on outer leaflet, raft), acrosome in sperm;
shed/soluble form in serum and a cytoplasmic pool in overexpressing tumor cells. Genuinely cell-surface accessible.
