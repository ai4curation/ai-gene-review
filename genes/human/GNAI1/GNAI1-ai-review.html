Gene Description

GNAI1 encodes G alpha i1, a heterotrimeric G protein alpha subunit that transduces signals from Gi-coupled receptors. It binds and hydrolyzes guanine nucleotides and can directly inhibit selected adenylyl cyclase isoforms, reducing cAMP production in receptor- and effector-dependent contexts. In addition to plasma-membrane signaling, Gi1 participates in cortical LGN–NuMA machinery that orients mitotic spindles and localizes to centrosomal and cytokinetic structures. Its cellular effects include neurotransmitter, endocrine and immune-cell responses.
