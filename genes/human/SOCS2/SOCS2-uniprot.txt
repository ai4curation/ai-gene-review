CC   -!- FUNCTION: Substrate-recognition component of a cullin-5-RING E3
CC       ubiquitin-protein ligase complex (ECS complex, also named CRL5
CC       complex), which mediates the ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal
CC       degradation of target proteins, such as EPOR and GHR (PubMed:11781573,
CC       PubMed:21980433, PubMed:25505247, PubMed:31182716, PubMed:34857742).
CC       Specifically recognizes and binds phosphorylated proteins via its SH2
CC       domain, promoting their ubiquitination (PubMed:21980433,
CC       PubMed:25505247, PubMed:31182716, PubMed:34857742, PubMed:37816714).
CC       The ECS(SOCS2) complex acts as a key regulator of growth hormone
CC       receptor (GHR) levels by mediating ubiquitination and degradation of
CC       GHR, following GHR phosphorylation by JAK2 (PubMed:21980433,
CC       PubMed:25505247, PubMed:34857742). The ECS(SOCS2) also catalyzes
CC       ubiquitination and degradation of JAK2-phosphorylated EPOR
CC       (PubMed:11781573). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11781573,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21980433, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25505247,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31182716, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34857742,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37816714}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Substrate-binding is prevented by the covalent
CC       inhibitor MN551 that cross-links with Cys-111 (PubMed:37816714). Also
CC       inhibited by a MN551 derivative, MN714, which contains a
CC       pivaloyloxymethyl that allows cell permeability (PubMed:37816714).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37816714}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11781573, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21980433,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25505247, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31182716,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34857742}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Substrate-recognition component of the ECS(SOCS2) complex,
CC       composed of SOCS2, CUL5, ELOB, ELOC and RNF7/RBX2 (PubMed:16675548,
CC       PubMed:21980433, PubMed:23897481, PubMed:25505247, PubMed:31182716,
CC       PubMed:34857742, PubMed:37816714). Interacts with IGF1R
CC       (PubMed:9727029). Interacts with DCUN1D1 (PubMed:23401859).
