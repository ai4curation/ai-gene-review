# ASPHD1 notes

- Bioinformatics (ASPHD1-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md): ASPH Fe ligand H679 maps to ASPHD1 R304, with no His within ±6 residues, so it is lost; H725 is conserved (H349); most 2-OG contacts are conserved. ASPHD1 is likely non-catalytic. Every site was window-checked.
- The only experimental report is an infection screen [PMID:41751799 "RNAi knockdown of ASPHD1 significantly enhanced ST95 ExPEC internalization (Figure 6D,E) in both fused and unfused BeWo cells."]. It is not annotated: a single knockdown phenotype with no mechanism.
- The affinage record was written with --force: the "E. coli" gate is a false positive (E. coli is the pathogen).
