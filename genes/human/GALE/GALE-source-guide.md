# GALE source guide

## Biological interpretation

GALE is a cytosolic, NAD-bound homodimer with two reversible epimerase activities. It recycles UDP-glucose from UDP-galactose in the Leloir pathway and supplies UDP-galactose in the reverse direction for glycoconjugate synthesis. Its second activity interconverts UDP-GlcNAc and UDP-GalNAc. The latter is shared with endogenous fly GALE; it is not uniquely human (PMID:22654673). NAD binding is part of catalysis, rather than a separate core function. NADH generated by the coupled reporter in PMID:16302980 is not net consumption of NAD by GALE.

The broad galactose-metabolism and catabolism annotations are refined to the existing Leloir-pathway annotation through verified is_a edges: GO:0033499 → GO:0019388 → GO:0006012. The electronic InterPro/UniPathway mapping and human UniProt pathway statement are generic; a specific original UPA00214 definition was not recovered. Independent human enzyme evidence establishes the refinement. Reverse UDP-galactose supply remains explicit in the first catalytic core; no new biosynthetic process annotation is added. QuickGO currently names GO:0033499 “D-galactose catabolic process, Leloir pathway”; older cache labels remain historical.

Five self-interaction annotations are refined to the already accepted GO:0042803 protein homodimerization activity, an is_a descendant of GO:0042802. Human crosslinking and author-defined structural dimers establish the specific assembly. The individual GALE pair/clone tables of the five interaction screens were not inspected; no screen-specific stoichiometry, natural product or orthogonal validation is inferred. Two structured findings under PMID:16302980 hold the shared exact catalytic and crosslinking anchors. Rows and the core link to that reference without duplicating its excerpts.

## Source access and experimental scope

- [PMID:16302980](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1742-4658.2005.05017.x): the cache is abstract-only. Selected indexed original Methods/Results establish human His6-GALE expressed in E. coli, UDP-Gal-to-UDP-Glc kinetics and EDC/BS3 crosslinking. Direct publisher HTTP access failed; this does not change the scope of the retained indexed reading. This paper does not establish the N-acetylated substrate activity.
- [PMID:11279032](https://holdenlab.biochem.wisc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1467/2020/09/99Thoden_et_al_2001.pdf): the cache contains the abstract; selected Methods/Results/Discussion were read in the author-hosted reprint. It supports human UDP-GlcNAc accommodation and prior turnover evidence. Human UDP-GalNAc co-crystallization failed, and the proposed specificity mutagenesis was not completed. The paper describes NADH, while deposited 1HZJ uses the NAD ligand dictionary. Its sequence differs from current canonical GALE at position 180 (A-to-V), despite zero-mutation metadata; no phenotype or natural-product assignment follows from this discrepancy.
- [PMID:22654673](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3359975/): selected original Results/Methods directly assay human GALE transgene controls in fly knockdown lysates for both substrate pairs. This is human-protein evidence in a fly host, not a purified-human assay. The cache contains full XML; figures and supplements were not independently inspected.
- [PMID:10801319](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi000215l): the authentic abstract and official PDB 1EK5/1EK6 sequences, ligands and assemblies were read; subscription full text remained inaccessible. Both 348-residue entities exactly match current canonical GALE. Author-defined dimers contain bound NAD (1EK5) or reduced NAI with UDP-glucose UPG (1EK6). No experimental map inspection is claimed.
- Interaction screens: PMID:16189514 is abstract-only and LOW relevance. Selected retained text for PMID:25416956 lacks Results/Methods and target tables despite its full-text flag. Selected assay/clone passages of PMID:25502805, PMID:31515488 and PMID:32296183 establish dataset methods, not a verified GALE-specific clone or validation result. The review preserves these individual access limits.
- [Reactome R-HSA-70369](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-70369) and [normal complex R-HSA-70363](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-70363) describe the cytosolic GALE:NAD+ dimer. R-HSA-5610036 is the failed V94M reaction, with [mutant complex R-HSA-9631876](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9631876). The cached event summary and retained component pages support model localization; the failed reaction is not evidence of wild-type catalysis. Failed later event requests are retained as access history, not as biological evidence.

## Products and phylogenetic transfer

Official direct FASTAs identify Q14376-1 (348 residues) and Q14376-2 (274 residues). In the short product, canonical residues 1–79 are replaced by MSPLQ. The reviewed full-length and tagged constructs do not establish equivalent activity of that natural short product. Its missing canonical N-terminal contacts do not by themselves prove inactivity.

In the actual PTHR43725 tree, human Q14376 is AN338/PTN002536271 and its ungapped sequence exactly matches canonical GALE. Activity and cytosol originate at AN287/PTN000041817; Leloir participation originates at AN323/PTN000821420. All origins lie on the target lineage. The two retained activity losses, PTN002886021 and PTN001621994, are off that lineage. Target self-donor evidence is valid grounding, and donor count is not an evidence score. The mouse Q8R059 donor GOA supports the ortholog context; its individual primary papers were not read.
