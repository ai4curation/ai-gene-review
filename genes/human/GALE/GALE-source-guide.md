# GALE source guide

## Biological synthesis

Human GALE is a cytosolic homodimer with two reversible UDP-sugar epimerase specificities. Its UDP-galactose/UDP-glucose reaction recycles UDP-glucose in the Leloir pathway and also contributes to galactose-containing nucleotide-sugar supply. Its UDP-GalNAc/UDP-GlcNAc reaction contributes to N-acetylated precursor balance. NAD binding is part of this catalytic mechanism, not a separate physiological core. The coupled dehydrogenase assay in PMID:16302980 produces NADH as its reporter; this is not net NAD consumption by GALE.

PMID:22654673 directly assays human GALE transgene controls in fly knockdown lysates for both substrate pairs. Endogenous fly GALE is also dual-specific. The inherited statement that the second activity is human-specific is superseded; the relevant contrast in PMID:11279032 is with the particular E. coli epimerase. That structural study did not obtain a human UDP-GalNAc co-crystal and did not complete the proposed specificity mutagenesis. Its substrate-accommodation hypothesis is kept distinct from those missing experiments.
