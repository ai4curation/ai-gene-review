# G6PC1 source guide

G6PC1 is the ER-membrane glucose-6-phosphatase catalytic subunit. The review distinguishes its hydrolytic core, condition-dependent phosphotransfer measured in the mouse ortholog, and secondary nutritional, hormonal and drug responses. The 357-residue canonical protein and 176-residue alternative product remain distinct; engineered structural constructs are not natural isoforms.

The current judgments and manual reference assessments are in G6PC1-ai-review.yaml. The companion evidence archive retains original source contexts, source-to-inherited joins, product sequences, actual phylogeny, interaction-table provenance and quotation history. The private full proposal preserves the authored decisions; the compact public projection does not repeat them.

## Primary evidence and access

- [PMID12093795](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12093795/): authentic abstract plus selected original Methods/Results establish the covalent His176 intermediate. Selected published text was read through the [author-hosted reproduction](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11281605_The_catalytic_center_of_glucose-6-phosphatase_-_HIS176_is_the_nucleophile_forming_the_phosphohistidine-enzyme_intermediate_during_catalysis). This is not an independent non-covalent phosphate-ion binding assay or a whole-figure audit.
- [PMID39847333 original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11789071/): selected human structural and biochemical Results/Methods distinguish wild type from H176A substrate-trapping and terminal ALFA constructs. The natural short product was not thereby tested.
- [PMID8407995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8407995/): original mouse Methods, Table I and Results show phosphotransfer in disrupted mouse-expressing COS1 and mouse-liver microsomes. The source is mouse, not rat or human; numerical OCR units are not reproduced and no physiological reverse flux is asserted.
- [PMID32814053 original](https://pub.dzne.de/record/153392/files/DZNE-2020-01389.pdf) and [original supplement ZIP](https://edoc.mdc-berlin.de/id/eprint/19322/2/19322suppl.zip): Table S2 NN1.0 rows 12842 and 9030 contain the WFS1 and RNF11 pairs. The participants are human proteins in a yeast host. Selected original Results and screening Methods, plus exact IntAct records, support the positive associations; construct boundaries and endogenous mechanisms remain unestablished.
- The eight exact rat-donor publications are PMID26883980, 25943649, 14988444, 17158265, 27366200, 19579180, 4303362 and 9813078. Authentic abstracts were read; selected full original Methods/Results were additionally read for 27366200. The carbohydrate-stimulus and G6pc-specific measurement questions in 26883980 and 25943649 remain explicit. Nutritional and insulin effects are conditional on the actual experimental state.

The earlier human cloning, disease-variant and topology sources were read as authentic complete abstracts unless a selected-original scope is named above. An abstract foregrounding SLC37A4 or G6PC3 is not evidence of a wrong-gene curation error. Reactome disease variants are kept distinct from wild type, and the gene in a transcriptional event is distinct from the ER-membrane protein output.
