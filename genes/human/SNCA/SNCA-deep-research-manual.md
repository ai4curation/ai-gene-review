# SNCA (alpha-synuclein, P37840) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this session. This file is a manual
synthesis written from the cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt
record (`SNCA-uniprot.txt`). Each claim carries its source.

## Identity and structure

- 140-aa neuronal protein, the major component of Lewy bodies; originally cloned as
  NACP, the precursor of the non-Abeta component (NAC) of Alzheimer amyloid
  [PMID:8248242 "We tentatively named this 35-amino acid peptide NAC (non-A beta component of AD amyloid) and its precursor NACP"].
- Intrinsically disordered in solution [PMID:8901511 "NACP exists as a mixture of rapidly equilibrating extended conformers and is representative of a class of \"natively unfolded\" proteins"].
- N-terminal imperfect KTKEGV repeats form an amphipathic helix on lipid surfaces; the
  acidic C-terminal tail stays unfolded [PMID:11286556 "The N-terminal region of alphaS binds to synthetic lipid vesicles and detergent micelles in vitro and adopts a highly helical conformation"].
- Native oligomeric state is debated: Bartels et al. reported a helical tetramer
  [PMID:21841800 "endogenous α-synuclein isolated and analysed under non-denaturing conditions from neuronal and non-neuronal cell lines, brain tissue and living human cells occurs in large part as a folded tetramer of about 58 kDa"],
  whereas Fauvet et al. found predominantly disordered monomer in brain
  [PMID:22315227 "both human and rodent α-syn expressed in the central nervous system exist predominantly as an unfolded monomer"].
  Burre et al. reconcile this partly: membrane binding drives multimerization
  [PMID:25246573 "α-synuclein multimerizes into large homomeric complexes upon membrane binding"].

## Physiological function at the presynapse

- Synaptic, raft-dependent localization [PMID:15282274 "alpha-synuclein localizes almost exclusively to the nerve terminal"].
- SNARE chaperone: binds VAMP2 and promotes SNARE-complex assembly; synuclein triple
  knockouts show age-dependent decline [PMID:20798282 "α-synuclein directly bound to the SNARE-protein synaptobrevin-2/vesicle-associated membrane protein 2 (VAMP2) and promoted SNARE-complex assembly"].
  The active form is the membrane-bound multimer [PMID:25246573 "α-synuclein promotes SNARE complex assembly at the presynaptic plasma membrane in its multimeric membrane-bound state, but not in its monomeric cytosolic state"].
- Genetic cooperation with CSPalpha/DNAJC5 requires phospholipid binding
  [PMID:16269331 "alpha-synuclein does not simply substitute for CSPalpha but acts by a downstream mechanism that requires phospholipid binding by alpha-synuclein"].
- Exocytosis: promotes fusion-pore dilation [PMID:28288128 "both overexpressed and endogenous synuclein accelerate the kinetics of individual exocytotic events, promoting cargo discharge and reducing pore closure"];
  facilitates priming and fusion in PC12 cells [PMID:30404828 "Our results show that α-synuclein is involved in vesicle priming and fusion."].
- Dopaminergic transmission: knockout mice show altered paired-pulse DA release
  [PMID:10707987 "alpha-Syn is an essential presynaptic, activity-dependent negative regulator of DA neurotransmission"].
- Endocytosis: with PUFAs promotes clathrin-mediated endocytosis and SV recycling
  [PMID:18980610 "alphaS and PUFAs normally function in endocytic mechanisms and are specifically involved in SV recycling upon neuronal stimulation"].

## Modulation of monoamine transporters and enzymes (peripheral, mostly overexpression)

- DAT inhibition [PMID:12958153 "alpha-synuclein induced a 35% decrease in [3H]DA uptake"]; SERT [PMID:16882008]; NET [PMID:17156375].
- Tyrosine hydroxylase inhibition [PMID:11943812 "observed a dose-dependent inhibition of tyrosine hydroxylase by alpha-synuclein"].
- PLD inhibition is not reproduced [PMID:19146388 "we observed no significant inhibition of PLD by alphaSyn in any of these systems"].
- GPX1 stimulation, single report [PMID:23507046 "α-synuclein enhanced the GPX-1 activity with Kd of 17.3nM"].

## Metal and lipid binding

- High-affinity Cu(II) site at the N-terminus/His50 [PMID:21319811 "α-syn binds Cu(2+) with a K(d) of ≈0.10 nM"]; Cu(I) binding [PMID:25495902].
- Ca2+ binding at the acidic C-terminus [PMID:11312271 "The Ca(2+)-binding site consists of a novel C-terminally localized acidic 32-amino acid domain"].
- Fe and Mg modulate aggregation in vitro [PMID:11850416].
- Not a fatty-acid carrier [PMID:16687662 "We conclude that alphaS is not likely to act as an intracellular FA carrier."].

## Pathology

- Missense mutations and multiplications cause PARK1/PARK4 and DLB (UniProt DISEASE).
- Aggregates into amyloid fibrils, Lewy bodies and glial cytoplasmic inclusions;
  filament structures from MSA [PMID:32461689].

## Note on PMID:22219196

This PMID was supplied to the session as a key SNCA paper. The cached record is
"Glutathione s-transferase omega 1 activity is sufficient to suppress neurodegeneration
in a Drosophila model of Parkinson disease", a study of Drosophila GSTO1 and parkin;
alpha-synuclein is mentioned only in passing (4 mentions, background). It provides no
evidence about SNCA function.
