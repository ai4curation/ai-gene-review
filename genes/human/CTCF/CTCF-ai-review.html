Gene Description

CTCF is a nuclear zinc-finger DNA-binding protein that organizes chromatin loops and regulates transcription. Sequence-specific occupancy at regulatory and boundary elements helps anchor contacts with cohesin and constrains enhancer–promoter communication. CTCF can activate or repress RNAPII transcription according to the locus and cellular context, and contributes to allele-specific organization at imprinted loci. RNA interactions can modulate its chromatin occupancy. CTCF also contributes to mitotic centromere organization. The 727-residue protein contains eleven zinc fingers; the 399-residue alternative product lacks the N-terminal 328 residues, and equivalent DNA recognition or architectural activity has not been established.

Definition: Binding to a protein that catalyzes histone demethylation.

Justification: CTCF associates with the histone demethylase KDM5B in DNase-treated cell extracts. GO:0019899 enzyme binding remains the supported available partner-class term.
