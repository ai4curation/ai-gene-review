# ELF4 evidence notes

Human ELF4 is the 663-residue ETS-family transcription factor Q99607. The official record has one displayed product and no alternative-products block. RUNX1 isoforms are interacting partner products; experimental MEF deletions and the disease-associated ELF4–ERG fusion are separate entities. None is added as a natural ELF4 splice product.

Human MEF DNA binding and promoter transactivation support an integrated transcription-factor mechanism. The original PMID8895518 abstract reports GM-CSF/IL3 promoter transactivation, while PMID14625302 explicitly reports an ELF4-responsive IL8 ETS site. Their full operative Methods were unavailable. PMID10207087 supplies reciprocal and domain-resolved MEF–RUNX1 binding, with cooperative IL3 reporter activation in a COS7 host. These complementary sources support precise polymerase-II transcription annotations without assigning every immune phenotype as a core activity.
