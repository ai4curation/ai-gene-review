Gene Description

CHM encodes Rab escort protein 1 (REP1), a soluble Rab-binding protein that couples Rab geranylgeranylation to membrane delivery. REP1 presents Rab substrates to the RABGGTA–RABGGTB catalytic machinery, remains associated with prenylated Rab products and delivers them to intracellular membranes. It also inhibits GDP release from Rab proteins in biochemical assays. Its defining work is substrate recognition and escort rather than autonomous geranylgeranyl-transfer chemistry. A cytosolic pool supports this trafficking cycle; nuclear localization has also been reported, with its functional significance unresolved. Loss of REP1 causes choroideremia, linking defective Rab processing and trafficking to retinal degeneration.
