Gene Description

COL6A1 encodes the alpha1 chain of collagen VI, a secreted extracellular matrix protein with a short triple-helical region flanked by von Willebrand factor A domains. Alpha1(VI) assembles with other collagen VI chains into triple-helical units that form disulfide-linked dimers and tetramers and extracellular beaded microfibrils. The canonical complex contains alpha1(VI), alpha2(VI) and alpha3(VI); alternative long chains support additional assembly contexts. Collagen VI provides matrix architecture around cells and connects tissue mechanics with cell adhesion and survival. Pathogenic COL6A1 variants cause collagen VI-related muscular dystrophies, including Bethlem and Ullrich phenotypes.
