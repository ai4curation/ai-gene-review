## Structural synthesis and chain scope

Alpha1(VI) contributes structure to collagen VI trimers and beaded extracellular microfibrils. The core describes extracellular matrix structural contribution (GO:0005201), in canonical collagen VI trimers (GO:0005589), located in the extracellular matrix (GO:0031012). Existing GO:0030020 annotations follow the official collagen convention, but the author-chosen core does not assert a mechanically measured tensile property. Beaded collagen VI microfibrils are described biologically without requiring identity with the fibrillin/MFAP-focused GO:0001527 definition. No NEW annotation or additional process is proposed. [PMID:2551668](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2551668/) and [PMID:23869615](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23869615/).

The original 1989 sequence work uses human fibroblast/placental cDNA and amnion peptides. Its two C-terminal variants concern alpha2(VI), not alpha1(VI). Original scans were read by the independent core reviewer; canonical stoichiometry is contextual knowledge in that source. The 2008 experiment uses endogenous human alpha1/alpha2 in SaOS-2 cells with an engineered mouse alpha4 N6–C2 construct. It directly supports secreted disulfide-linked association of the tested alpha1/alpha2/alpha4 combination. Therefore row49 is refined from generic protein complex to GO:0140158, the VWA-rich collagen trimer class, rather than forcing the strictly alpha1/alpha2/alpha3 GO:0005589 definition. The experiment does not establish a native human alpha1/alpha2/alpha4 extracellular microfibril. [PMID:18400749](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18400749/).

The 1994 isolated human-chain experiments distinguish alpha1 and alpha2 ligand behavior. Alpha2, unlike alpha1, binds the tested perlecan/fibronectin/pepsin-VI preparations in the separate solid-phase assays. Both chains bind heparin-Sepharose; that purification observation is not treated as a universal negative or used to manufacture a NEW heparin annotation. Isolated non-triple-helical chains do not refute multichain coassembly. A listed erratum text was not recovered, so no correction scope is claimed. [PMID:8168508](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8168508/).
