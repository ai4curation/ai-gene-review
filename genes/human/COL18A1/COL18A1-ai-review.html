Gene Description

COL18A1 encodes the alpha-1 chain of collagen XVIII, a secreted, nonfibrillar basement-membrane heparan-sulfate proteoglycan that assembles into homotrimers. Its matrix role supports basement-membrane organization and tissue architecture, including the vitreoretinal interface. Alternative amino-terminal forms share the collagenous and carboxy-terminal regions. Proteolytic release of the carboxy-terminal NC1 region and endostatin produces forms with distinct extracellular effects: oligomeric NC1 can promote matrix-dependent endothelial motility and morphogenesis, whereas some endostatin preparations inhibit endothelial proliferation or angiogenesis. These effects depend on oligomerization, preparation and biological context; they are not interchangeable properties of every intact precursor or circulating fragment. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause Knobloch syndrome, with severe ocular abnormalities and variable occipital defects.
