Gene Description

CHSY1 is a type-II Golgi membrane glycosyltransferase that elongates the chondroitin backbone of proteoglycans. Its luminal catalytic region alternately adds glucuronic acid through a beta1,3 linkage and N-acetylgalactosamine through a beta1,4 linkage, using UDP-sugar donors. CHSY1 forms functional polymerase complexes with CHPF or CHPF2, which support complex assembly and activity, while CHSY1 supplies both sugar-transfer reactions. The resulting chains can subsequently be modified by sulfotransferases. CHSY1-dependent chondroitin production contributes to extracellular-matrix organization and skeletal development. A soluble CHSY1 pool has also been detected in primary human fibroblast conditioned medium; its processing and the relationship between chondroitin synthesis and altered NOTCH signaling remain incompletely resolved.

Core Functions

The C-terminal GT7 domain of CHSY1 transfers GalNAc from UDP-GalNAc to the terminal GlcA of a chondroitin acceptor, creating a beta1,4 linkage. This is one of two coupled catalytic steps in Golgi chondroitin backbone elongation. CHPF or CHPF2 supports the polymerase complex, while CHSY1 supplies the sugar-transfer chemistry.
