# SLC43A2 (LAT4) review notes

- LAT4 is a Na+/Cl-/pH-independent, heavy-chain-independent facilitative uniporter for
  branched-chain amino acids (Leu, Ile, Val) plus Phe and Met
  [PMID:30379325 "Uniporter LAT4 selectively transports the branched chain amino acids leucine, isoleucine and valine, and additionally methionine and phenylalanine"].
- Resides at the basolateral membrane of intestinal and renal epithelia, driving the basolateral
  efflux step of transepithelial neutral amino acid (re)absorption; surface density is tuned by a
  Ser274/Ser297 phosphorylation switch
  [PMID:30379325 "dephosphorylation of S274 and phosphorylation of S297 increase LAT4 membrane localization and function"].
