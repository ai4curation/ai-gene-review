# PAK2 notes

Deep research could not be generated in this checkout: Falcon and Perplexity providers were unavailable from `deep-research-client`, and the Claude Code provider was not logged in. Manual review therefore used the cached UniProt record, GOA rows, Reactome records, PAINT slice, and cached primary publications.

PAK2 is a ubiquitous group I p21-activated kinase with two recurring functional modes: full-length CDC42/RAC-regulated Ser/Thr kinase signaling, and a caspase-cleaved PAK2p34 fragment that translocates and contributes to apoptotic signaling.

- PMID:10748018 showed direct PAK2 phosphorylation of MLCK and inhibition of myosin-II RLC phosphorylation.
- PMID:12853446 showed nuclear targeting and proteasomal regulation of the proapoptotic caspase-cleaved PAK2p34 fragment.
- PMID:16617111 showed posttranslational myristoylation of caspase-activated PAK2 and membrane localization that potentiates late apoptotic events.
- PMID:21555521 and PMID:27889207 showed PAK2 phosphorylation of CASP7 and inhibition of apoptotic caspase-7 activity.
- PMID:25753039 placed PAK2 in ZO-1-dependent endothelial junction mechanotransduction.
