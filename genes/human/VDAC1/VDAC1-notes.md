# VDAC1 notes

Deep research providers unavailable in this environment (falcon/perplexity-lite failed); research from
cached publications and the UniProt record.

## Function
- Voltage-gated channel: open state weakly anion-selective, closed state cation-selective
  [file:human/VDAC1/VDAC1-uniprot.txt "The open state has a weak anion selectivity whereas the closed state is"];
  human VDAC1 expressed in yeast forms VDAC-type channels in bilayers [PMID:8420959].
- Dimers act as a phospholipid scramblase, independent of channel activity [PMID:38065946 "dimers of human VDAC1
  and VDAC2 catalyze rapid transbilayer translocation of phospholipids by a mechanism that is unrelated to their
  channel activity"].
- Lipid binding (ceramide verified in full text despite the VDAC2-focused title) [PMID:31015432 "indicating that
  VDAC1 and VDAC2 are genuine mitochondrial CBPs"].
- mPTP component with SPG7/PPIF [PMID:26387735]; Parkin substrate setting mitophagy vs apoptosis [PMID:32047033,
  PMID:20098416].

## Localization conclusion (surface benchmark)
Primarily the **mitochondrial outer membrane** (multi-pass beta barrel). A genuine but minor **plasma-membrane
pool** ("plasmalemmal porin") is reported, including neuronal lipid rafts [PMID:25168729] and as a kringle-5
receptor on endothelial cells [PMID:25296756]; these rows were kept as non-core rather than removed, since
UniProt also records a cell-membrane location. Nucleus (HDA) and exosome (HDA) rows marked as over-annotated
(abundant-protein carry-over).
