CC   -!- FUNCTION: Non-selective voltage-gated ion channel that mediates the
CC       transport of anions and cations through the mitochondrion outer
CC       membrane and plasma membrane (PubMed:10661876, PubMed:11845315,
CC       PubMed:18755977, PubMed:30061676, PubMed:8420959). The channel at the
CC       outer mitochondrial membrane allows diffusion of small hydrophilic
CC       molecules; in the plasma membrane it is involved in cell volume
CC       regulation and apoptosis (PubMed:10661876, PubMed:11845315,
CC       PubMed:18755977, PubMed:8420959). It adopts an open conformation at low
CC       or zero membrane potential and a closed conformation at potentials
CC       above 30-40 mV (PubMed:10661876, PubMed:18755977, PubMed:8420959). The
CC       open state has a weak anion selectivity whereas the closed state is
CC       cation-selective (PubMed:18755977, PubMed:8420959). Binds various
CC       signaling molecules, including the sphingolipid ceramide, the
CC       phospholipid phosphatidylcholine, and the sterols cholesterol and
CC       oxysterol (PubMed:18755977, PubMed:31015432). In depolarized
CC       mitochondria, acts downstream of PRKN and PINK1 to promote mitophagy or
CC       prevent apoptosis; polyubiquitination by PRKN promotes mitophagy, while
CC       monoubiquitination by PRKN decreases mitochondrial calcium influx which
CC       ultimately inhibits apoptosis (PubMed:32047033). May participate in the
CC       formation of the permeability transition pore complex (PTPC)
CC       responsible for the release of mitochondrial products that triggers
CC       apoptosis (PubMed:15033708, PubMed:25296756). May mediate ATP export
