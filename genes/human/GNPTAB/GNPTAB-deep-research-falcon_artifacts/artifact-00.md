| What does the human GNPTAB catalytic architecture reveal? | Cryo-EM resolved the soluble human S1S3 GNPTAB truncation at **3.4 Å** and UDP-GlcNAc-bound soluble human phosphotransferase at **2.9 Å**. S1S3 preserves the four-region Stealth catalytic core and NDD motif but lacks major regulatory and recognition regions, favoring a constitutively open state. (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000042) | Peer-reviewed human structural study. The soluble engineered proteins lack transmembrane regions; S1S3 is not equivalent to native full-length α₂β₂γ₂ GNPT and has reduced protein-substrate selectivity. | Li et al., 2024; published online **22 Aug 2024**. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2024.107706](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2024.107706) |
| How much does S1S3 increase catalytic turnover? | At saturating UDP-GlcNAc, S1S3 versus soluble parent hPTase had *k*cat values of **3.39 vs 0.153 s⁻¹** for α-methyl-mannoside (~22-fold), **1.54 vs 0.119 s⁻¹** for mannobiose (~13-fold), and **0.11 vs 0.012 s⁻¹** for Man₉GlcNAc₂ (~9-fold). Reported values were based on **two independent assays**. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000038) | Peer-reviewed purified-enzyme biochemistry. Artificial or isolated glycan acceptors do not reproduce recognition of intact lysosomal hydrolases; increased turnover may accompany off-target phosphorylation of nonlysosomal glycoproteins. | Li et al., 2024. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2024.107706](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2024.107706) |
| How is GNPT retained in the Golgi, and can it modify cargo beyond soluble hydrolases? | In human-cell knockouts, loss of **GOLPH3/GOLPH3L** destabilized LYSET–GNPT complexes, impaired M6P tagging, and disrupted lysosomal-enzyme maturation and trafficking. Anti-M6P enrichment detected tagged proteins in wild-type but not LYSET-, GNPTAB-, or GOLPH3/GOLPH3L-deficient cells; M6P immunoprecipitation supported direct tagging of the **B4GALT5 ectodomain**, suggesting a degradative lysosomal route for this Golgi protein. (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000025, pqac-00000026) | Peer-reviewed human-cell genetic, biochemical, and trafficking evidence. B4GALT5 expands the candidate substrate space, but the physiological prevalence and tissue relevance of this route remain unresolved. | Brauer et al., 2024; online **25 Nov 2024**. [https://doi.org/10.1038/s44318-024-00305-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44318-024-00305-z) |
| What recent human evidence connects GNPTAB deficiency to ML II/III diagnosis? | Among **20 Chinese probands**, **6** had ML II and **14** had ML III α/β. Variants were found in **35/40 alleles (87.5%)**, including **five novel variants**. Plasma arylsulfatase A was **13–63×** the reference value and HexA approximately **3–4×** higher; urinary glycosaminoglycans were generally normal. (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000033) | Peer-reviewed clinical cohort, but small and ancestry-specific. Five alleles remained unresolved; Sanger sequencing may miss copy-number or deep-intronic variants, and most new variants lacked direct cellular functional validation. | Feng et al., 2024; published **Dec 2024**. [https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-024-05223-x](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-024-05223-x) |
| What did the foundational minimal-domain structure establish about catalysis and specificity? | A minimal zebrafish GNPTAB construct—**87% identical** to human—showed that four dispersed Stealth-family regions assemble the catalytic domain and that both α and β products are required. Removing accessory regions retained catalytic activity but abolished discrimination between lysosomal hydrolases and nonlysosomal glycoproteins; mutations of key donor-pocket residues reduced activity by roughly **50–1,000-fold**. (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000041) | Peer-reviewed crystallographic and biochemical evidence. The structure is zebrafish-derived and engineered; conclusions about human substrate selection require corroboration from intact human complexes. | Gorelik et al., 2022; published **8 Aug 2022**. [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2203518119](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2203518119) |
| Are GNPTAB-directed treatments clinically established? | Engineered S1S3 can increase M6P installation and is less than half the size of full-length hPTase, making it attractive for AAV-oriented M6P engineering; however, genomes above roughly **5 kb** show reduced vector expression, and S1S3 may phosphorylate nonlysosomal proteins. Available structural literature describes gene therapy, antisense approaches, and pharmacological chaperones as investigational, and no relevant disease-targeted interventional GNPTAB trial was identified in the registry search. (pqac-00000045, pqac-00000051) | **Preclinical, not approved therapy.** Increased M6P tagging or cellular rescue does not establish safety, durable in-vivo correction, skeletal/CNS delivery, or clinical efficacy. | Li et al., 2024, [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2024.107706](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2024.107706); Gorelik et al., 2022, [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2203518119](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2203518119) |
