### Licensing, DNA contacts and disease alleles

CDT1 cooperates with ORC and CDC6 to load MCM2–7 onto DNA. Purified human reconstitution separates recruitment from high-salt-resistant loading, and omission of CDT1 disrupts loading. Full-length and later truncated constructs in the 2024 study are distinct preparations. This directly performed co-loader role supports the pre-RC assembly core; it does not give CDT1 an intrinsic ATPase or helicase activity. [PMID:39604729](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39604729/), selected full Results/Fig.1 and expression/loading Methods.

The 2026 human reconstitution tests WT CDT1 and the MGS variants R462Q/E468K; R453W was not successfully purified for the assay. R210C is derived from a fly mutant and is not identified here as a human MGS allele. Recruitment/loading deficits are measured, whereas suggested interface/salt-bridge explanations are structural interpretations. [PMID:41448435 Molecular impacts of Meier-Gorlin syndrome mutations on human origin licensing, "MGS variants of CDT1 specifically reduce MCM recruitment"].

Direct DNA binding is grounded in mouse biochemical experiments and separately corroborated by human recombinant-protein DNA-cellulose assays. Mouse full-length 557-aa Cdt1 and mapped deletions were tested by DNA-cellulose and mobility shift; this does not establish sequence-specific origin recognition. Human 2026 experiments use tagged WT/mutant CDT1, with the DNA-binding control distinguished from cellular synthesis inhibition. [PMID:12192004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12192004/), indexed full primary Methods/Figs.2,4–6; [PMID:41004347](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41004347/), full Materials/Methods and selected Results/Fig.4. The human dynamic-chromatin study’s authentic abstract independently supports G1 chromatin association; its original full body was not recovered. [PMID:17318181](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17318181/).

The InterPro-derived preinitiation-complex assembly assertion is refined to mitotic pre-replicative complex assembly (GO:1902985). Current GO definitions distinguish the licensing/loading step from downstream preinitiation assembly enabling origin opening and helicase activation. These are distinct branches, so this is a functional-stage refinement rather than a claimed direct-parent replacement. The original IEA/source is unchanged and the existing experimental pre-RC assertion is retained.

### Inhibition of synthesis versus checkpoint signaling

CDT1 itself can restrain DNA synthesis. Human-cell experiments and purified human replication systems in PMID:36608667 distinguish this effect from checkpoint signaling and rereplication. CDT1 inhibits established forks and a minimal CMG/Pol-epsilon/RPA system, while Pol-epsilon-only primer extension is unaffected; MCM-contacting domains are required. The purified CDT1 preparation is 2×FLAG human protein from insect cells. ATR/WEE1 inhibition and CDC6-degron experiments provide separate cellular controls. These support an enzyme-inhibitor core and retention of the existing negative replication assertion. They do not provide a CMG-alone unwinding assay or a solved inhibitory structure; the paper explicitly leaves altered helicase–polymerase coupling as an alternative. [PMID:36608667](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36608667/), selected full Results and STAR protein/replication Methods.

The additional human MCF-7 study uses engineered nondegradable, licensing-impaired and geminin-nonbinding CDT1 constructs, with MCM-contacting mutants as controls. EdU/flow-cytometry and binding experiments corroborate synthesis restraint. This engineered overexpression context is not substituted for a measurement of its unperturbed physiological magnitude. [PMID:41004347](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41004347/).

The original 2009 source combines a human truncated CDT1–geminin structure, Xenopus licensing assays and separately described human U2OS overexpression-induced G2 arrest. Its supplementary Fig.S9 pixels and detailed SI Methods were not independently recovered; the explicit main Results description was read. The cell-cycle annotation remains contextual. New human licensing reconstitution shows inhibition with a CDT1–geminin heterotrimer, so an obligatory inhibitory hexamer is not asserted. This qualifies the older mechanism without denying binding or CDT1’s independently demonstrated fork-inhibitory function. [PMID:19906994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19906994/), indexed full main text; [PMID:41365879](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41365879/), full selected Results/Figs.1–3.

The older checkpoint citation measures binding/loading: [PMID:14672932 The regulated association of Cdt1 with minichromosome maintenance proteins and Cdc6 in mammalian cells, "Cdc6 and Cdt1 are mutually dependent on one another"]. Actual primary Methods/Results/Figs.1–6 were read through the indexed author-uploaded article. Those assays support licensing rather than the signal-transduction step required by GO:0000076; that source-specific IDA assertion is over-annotated. The PAINT checkpoint node remains unresolved because the complete ancestral reconstruction and fly experimental grounding were not re-adjudicated. The combined electronic chain is also unresolved despite inspection of the mouse binding source. A short donor list or human self-donor is not a flaw, and licensing perturbation is not proof that CDT1 can never influence checkpoints.

### Mitotic microtubule and kinetochore function

G2-specific CDT1 perturbation after normal S phase, rescue, Hec1-loop mutants and cold-stable attachment measurements separate the mitotic function from prior replication defects. The source states [PMID:22581055 Recruitment of the human Cdt1 replication licensing protein by the loop domain of Hec1 is required for stable kinetochore-microtubule attachment, "human Cdt1 has a separable essential mitotic function"]. Human HeLa experiments are distinguished from PTK2/LLCPK1 localization. Hec1 interaction is supported by human two-hybrid, GST pull-down and endogenous co-IP; NUF2 recovery is co-complex evidence, not a purified binary interface.

Independent purified-protein work demonstrates direct microtubule binding by tag-cleaved full-length human CDT1, using porcine-brain microtubules and no-MT/GST/GFP controls. GFP-truncated TIRF experiments and cellular phosphomutant rescue are separate assays. This supports GO:0008017 in the core. Because this MF is already a descendant of the existing generic protein-binding class, no redundant NEW row is added, and Hec1-binding assertions are preserved as interaction evidence. [PMID:30154187](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30154187/), full Fig.1E/F and selected Results/Methods. Kinetochore is used as an anatomical location, not misfiled as a protein-containing complex.
