Gene Description

GRK5 is a serine/threonine kinase that phosphorylates activated G protein-coupled receptors and regulates their signaling. Phospholipid interactions and a C-terminal amphipathic region support its plasma-membrane association. GRK5 also enters the nucleus and phosphorylates non-receptor substrates, including p53, giving it context-dependent roles in stress responses, survival and proliferation. Its receptor recruitment differs from GRK2, and kinase activity does not imply the same regulatory outcome for every receptor or cell type.
