# LHCGR notes

- Core function: LHCGR is an LH/chorionic-gonadotropin-activated class A GPCR that signals through Gs and cAMP at the plasma membrane.
- Keep LH/CG binding and receptor-activity rows as core. Keep phospholipase C and inositol trisphosphate rows as supported secondary signaling branches.
- Keep ovarian follicle, ovulation, male gonad, and male genital development rows as non-core physiological outcomes of LHCGR signaling rather than the core molecular function itself.
- The `cognition` row from PMID:23227193 is over-annotated. The paper is a CNV screen in Alzheimer's cohorts and does not establish direct LHCGR involvement in a cognitive process.
