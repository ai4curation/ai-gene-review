# LHCGR manual literature synthesis

LHCGR encodes the lutropin-choriogonadotropic hormone receptor, the glycoprotein hormone GPCR for luteinizing hormone and chorionic gonadotropin. UniProt summarizes the core function as "Receptor for lutropin-choriogonadotropic hormone" whose activity is "mediated by G proteins which activate adenylate cyclase" [file:human/LHCGR/LHCGR-uniprot.txt]. This places the main human function at the plasma membrane, where LH/CG binding drives cAMP signaling in gonadal endocrine cells.

The phospholipase C and inositol trisphosphate annotations are retained as secondary LHCGR signaling branches because the PTHR24372 LHCGR PAINT node carries GO:0007200 from mouse and rat seeds and BHF-UCL independently transferred the same PLC/IP3 signaling branch from mouse Lhcgr.

The glycoprotein hormone receptor ectodomain mechanism is shared with TSHR and FSHR: Costagliola et al. showed that a conserved sulfotyrosine motif is required for high-affinity binding in the receptor family, and the abstract says site-directed mutagenesis indicates a similar role in the LH/CG and FSH receptors [PMID:11847099, "the motif, which is conserved in all members of the glycoprotein hormone receptor family, seems to play a similar role in the LH/CG and FSH receptors"]. Reactome summarizes the specific human pathway: LH binds LHCGR in ovary, testes and uterus, and the activated receptor couples to G alpha s to activate adenylate cyclase and increase cAMP [Reactome:R-HSA-391377, "The activated receptor couples to the G protein alpha-s subunit (Gilchrist RL et al, 1996) which activates adenylate cyclase and increases intracellular cAMP levels."].

Human genetic loss-of-function and gain-of-function phenotypes are consistent with the receptor's physiological roles in male gonad development, male genital development, ovarian follicle development, and ovulation, but those are organism-level consequences of LH/CG receptor signaling rather than separate molecular activities. The older male pseudohermaphroditism papers support LHCGR's requirement in Leydig cell function [PMID:7719343, "Male pseudohermaphroditism due to a homozygous missense mutation of the luteinizing hormone receptor gene."] [PMID:9626653, "A homozygous mutation in the luteinizing hormone receptor causes partial Leydig cell hypoplasia"].

The LHCGR cognition annotation points to an Alzheimer's disease copy-number-variation study rather than to receptor signaling in a defined cognitive process. The cached full text describes CNV analyses in AD and candidate regions, not an LHCGR functional perturbation assay [PMID:23227193, "The aim of the present report is to analyze the role of CNVs in AD using data from a unique cohort of clinically characterized and neuropathologically defined cases (AD) and controls (TGen cohort)"].

LHCGR is internalized and trafficked through endosomes after activation. The endosome row is therefore plausible as a non-core receptor-trafficking location, while the repeated plasma-membrane rows describe the core cell-surface signaling site.
