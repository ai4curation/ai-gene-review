CC   -!- FUNCTION: Sequence-specific DNA-binding protein that interacts with
CC       inducible viral and cellular enhancer elements to regulate
CC       transcription of selected genes. AP-2 factors bind to the consensus
CC       sequence 5'-GCCNNNGGC-3' and activate genes involved in a large
CC       spectrum of important biological functions including proper eye, face,
CC       body wall, limb and neural tube development. They can also suppress a
CC       number of genes including MCAM/MUC18 and C/EBP alpha. Is involved in
CC       the negative regulation of Wnt canonical signaling, and is a
CC       transcriptional repressor of MYC (PubMed:33000225). AP-2-alpha is the
CC       only AP-2 protein required for early morphogenesis of the lens vesicle.
CC       Together with the CITED2 coactivator, stimulates the PITX2 P1 promoter
CC       transcription activation. Associates with chromatin to the PITX2 P1
CC       promoter region. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11694877,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12586840, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33000225}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Binds DNA as a dimer. Can form homodimers or heterodimers with
CC       other AP-2 family members. Interacts with WWOX. Interacts with CITED4.
CC       Interacts with UBE2I. Interacts with RALBP1 in a complex also
CC       containing EPN1 and NUMB during interphase and mitosis. Interacts with
CC       KCTD1; this interaction represses transcription activation
CC       (PubMed:19115315, PubMed:33000225). Interacts (via C-terminus) with
CC       CITED2 (via C-terminus); the interaction stimulates TFAP2A-
CC       transcriptional activation. Interacts (via N-terminus) with EP300 (via
CC       N-terminus); the interaction requires CITED2. Interacts with KCTD15;
CC       this interaction inhibits TFAP2A transcriptional activation.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11694877, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11744733,
