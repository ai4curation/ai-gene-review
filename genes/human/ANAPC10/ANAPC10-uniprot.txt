CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome
CC       (APC/C), a cell cycle-regulated E3 ubiquitin ligase that controls
CC       progression through the cell cycle (PubMed:18485873, PubMed:27120157,
CC       PubMed:27509861). APC/C acts by mediating ubiquitination and subsequent
CC       degradation of target proteins: it mainly mediates the formation of
CC       'Lys-11'-linked polyubiquitin chains and, to a lower extent, the
CC       formation of 'Lys-48'- and 'Lys-63'-linked polyubiquitin chains
CC       (PubMed:18485873). APC/C catalyzes assembly of branched 'Lys-11'-/'Lys-
CC       48'-linked branched ubiquitin chains on target proteins
CC       (PubMed:29033132). APC/C is activated by CDC20 in the
CC       metaphase/anaphase transition of cell cycle, targeting the degradation
CC       of cyclin B and securin (PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C is
CC       regulated by the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC), which inhibits APC/C
CC       and delays chromosome segregation (PubMed:27509861).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18485873, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27120157,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27509861, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29033132}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18485873, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29033132}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C),
CC       composed of ANAPC1, ANAPC2, CDC27/ANAPC3, ANAPC4, ANAPC5, CDC16/ANAPC6,
CC       ANAPC7, CDC23/ANAPC8, ANAPC10, ANAPC11, CDC26/ANAPC12, ANAPC13, ANAPC15
CC       and ANAPC16 (PubMed:25043029, PubMed:26083744, PubMed:27120157,
CC       PubMed:27509861). APC/C associates with CDC20 to form the CDC20-APC/C
CC       complex, which is crucial for metaphase/anaphase transition in cell
CC       cycle; this interaction is dependent on APC/C phosphorylation
CC       (PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C associates with mitotic
CC       checkpoint complex (MCC), which inhibits APC/C and delays chromosome
CC       segregation (PubMed:27509861). APC/C interacts with FZR1 and FBXO5
CC       (PubMed:25043029, PubMed:26083744). Interacts (via the C-terminus) with
CC       to CDC27/ANAPC3 (PubMed:11524682). Interacts with PIWIL1; interaction
CC       only takes place when PIWIL1 binds piRNA (By similarity). Interacts
CC       with FBXO43; the interaction is direct (PubMed:34595750).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8K2H6, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11524682,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25043029, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26083744,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34595750}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the APC10 family. {ECO:0000305}.
