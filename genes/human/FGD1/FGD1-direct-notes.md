The clearest direct molecular evidence is the purified-fragment exchange assay in PMID:8969170, Figure 2. Human FGD1 residues 330–710 promote GDP release and nucleotide loading on modified Cdc42Hs. The accompanying small-GTPase pulldown establishes substrate recognition, while the fibroblast assays connect exchange to Cdc42-dependent actin responses. They do not show direct actin binding, GTP-hydrolysis catalysis, or FGD1 kinase activity. The retained actin, filopodium and small-GTPase-binding roles are distinguished from the central exchange mechanism.
