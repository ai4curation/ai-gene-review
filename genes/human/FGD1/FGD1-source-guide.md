# FGD1 evidence guide

The [review YAML](FGD1-ai-review.yaml) contains the final 34 annotation decisions and 15 reference assessments. Its single molecular-function core is CDC42 nucleotide exchange through the DH–PH region, with cytosolic and plasma-membrane-associated pools. Actin organization, filopodial responses and cell shape remain downstream contextual effects rather than separate structural or actin-binding molecular functions.

The [literature journal](FGD1-notes.md), [direct source guide](FGD1-direct-source-guide.md) and [database source guide](FGD1-database-source-guide.md) identify the actual original Methods, Results, captions and database records read. The review preserves all imported source objects and reference identities/findings; it adds one independently verified mouse-donor reference. It introduces no NEW annotation, natural product or publication quotation, in accordance with the [campaign instructions](../../../projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions).

All 19 accepted source files remain preserved privately. The installation subset is ten files: three publications, four Reactome entries and three unchanged PANTHER/PAINT records. The other nine discovery sources are unused. The final decisions are 11 ACCEPT, 14 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, six TOO_GENERAL, two MODIFY and one UNDECIDED. The two replacements preserve CDC42-specific signaling regulation (GO:0032489) and exchange-defined GTPase activation (GO:0090630). The single core process also uses GO:0032489. Current definitions and typed hierarchy evidence are recorded in the ontology sidecar.
