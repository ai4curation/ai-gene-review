# EXT1 evidence and source boundaries

The reviewed evidence distinguishes the human EXT1–EXT2 catalytic assembly, individual recombinant subunits, tagged cellular localization and database models. Supported generic interactions remain non-core under the [campaign instructions](../../../projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions); reference-only supports add no publication quotations. The source-described 746-residue Q16394 product is preserved. Engineered anchor deletions, expression tags, host species and other transcript models are not additional natural products or proof of functional equivalence.

## Catalytic experiments

[PMID:12907669](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12907669/) was recovered as an [author-uploaded original](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/10621872_In_Vitro_Polymerization_of_Heparan_Sulfate_Backbone_by_the_EXT_Proteins). Operative Methods, Results, relevant captions and selected Discussion were read. Human EXT1 residues 29–746 and EXT2 lacking residues 1–46 were expressed in HEK293 with engineered secretion leaders. Anti-FLAG-bound preparations extend free K5-derived oligosaccharides. Single-donor radiolabel incorporation, chain-length chromatography and linkage-sensitive glycosidase digestion support sugar transfer and polymerization. Apparent 160-kDa EXT1 and associated EXT1–EXT2 elution support oligomerization; the endogenous-partner isoelectric-focusing check is reported but not displayed. Neither physiological secretion nor an obligate native EXT1 homodimer is inferred. No original figure pixels or supplementary material were inspected.

The two modern human studies refine that interpretation:

- [PMID:36402845](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-34882-6) uses soluble EXT1 29–746/EXT2 47–718 coexpressed in HEK293F. Purification, structural/mutational Results, FACE assays with defined octa-/nona-saccharide acceptors, HeLa complementation Methods and main captions were read. EXT1 GT-B mutations impair GlcA transfer; both GT-A contributions are positive in these comparisons, with an explicit thermal-stability confound for the EXT1 D565N/D567N complex. The full-length tagged HeLa knockout-rescue experiment is separate from the soluble purified assay.
- [PMID:36593275](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41589-022-01220-2) uses EXT1 28–746/EXT2 46–718. Operative expression, mutagenesis, UDP-Glo, glypican-1 and MALDI Methods, Results/Discussion and relevant main captions were read. Defined oligosaccharide and glypican-associated extension assays assign robust GlcA transfer to EXT1 GT-B and dominant GlcNAc transfer to EXT2 GT-A. Low-yield, proteolyzed EXT1-only material nevertheless gives low qualitative GlcNAc/co-polymerase activity. That result is neither categorical inactivity nor an autonomous rate equivalent to the heterocomplex.

No modern structural figure pixels, raw spectra or supplementary numerical experiments were independently inspected. Exact indexed read scopes are retained in the reference assessments and archived evidence. The older positive GlcNAc-transfer result remains visible alongside the modern differences in constructs, substitutions, stability and assay conditions. Actual chain-extension chemistry supports biosynthetic participation beyond knockout necessity; EXT1 is not assigned every linker or core-protein synthesis step.

Construct, HPLC/labeling, IF and endoH Methods, operative Results and captions were read. Human HeLa-derived EXT1 was expressed in mouse/CHO hosts. N-terminal deletion and glycosylation support the secretory membrane-protein model without a direct protease-protection topology assay. ## The two catalytic reactions and their experimental boundaries

EXT1’s principal reaction is beta-1,4 transfer of glucuronic acid from UDP-GlcA to a GlcNAc-terminated chain. Its second measured capacity is alpha-1,4 transfer of GlcNAc from UDP-GlcNAc to a GlcA-terminated acceptor. Both belong to chain elongation by the EXT1–EXT2 complex; these terms do not assign linker synthesis, sulfation or the entire biosynthetic pathway to EXT1.

In [PMID:12907669](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12907669/), human EXT1 29–746 and EXT2 lacking residues 1–46 were expressed as secreted tagged proteins in HEK293 cells. Affinity-captured EXT1 gave positive single-sugar radiochemical transfer and alternating extension on K5-derived free oligosaccharides. Glycosidase digestion supported the reported linkages. These are direct catalytic observations, not assays of native membrane-associated stoichiometry. The authors’ isoelectric-focusing argument against substantial endogenous heterocomplex contamination was reported as data not shown. Figure 5 caption and Results describe the single-transfer assay; no new figure-pixel or raw-data inspection was performed for this synthesis.

[PMID:36402845](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36402845/) uses human EXT1 29–746/EXT2 47–718 for soluble-complex biochemistry, defined oligosaccharide FACE assays, and separate full-length HeLa knockout/complementation experiments. Both GT-A mutant sets affected GlcNAc transfer, with a modest stability reduction for the EXT1 D565N/D567N preparation. [PMID:36593275](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36593275/) uses human EXT1 28–746/EXT2 46–718, glypican and short-acceptor assays, and assigns dominant GlcNAc transfer to EXT2. EXT1 GT-A effects are smaller or variable there. The latter paper also reports low qualitative activity from separately expressed EXT1, whose low yield and extensive proteolysis prevent quantitative equivalence claims. Thus the studies support secondary EXT1 catalytic capacity while leaving its native quantitative contribution open.

The Golgi setting is independently supported by the accepted source evidence. In [PMID:35137078](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35137078/), full-length human fluorescent constructs colocalize with GM130 in COS-7 cells (Methods and Figure 7A caption); this is human protein in a nonhuman host. It is separate from native human Reactome participant localization and from the membrane-topology evidence discussed above. No engineered soluble construct is treated as a natural secreted product, and no additional natural product or isoform is invented.
