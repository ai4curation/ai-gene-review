# C5 (Complement C5; UniProtKB:P01031) — Curation Notes

## 1. Gene Product Overview & Proteolytic Maturation into Functional Isoforms (`C5a` and `C5b`)
- **Gene / Protein:** `C5` (`HGNC:1331`, `UniProtKB:P01031`), encoding human complement component C5 (1676 aa precursor).
- **PANTHER Classification:** Member of `PANTHER:PTHR11412` (`MACROGLOBULIN / COMPLEMENT`), specifically subfamily `PANTHER:PTHR11412:SF83` (`COMPLEMENT C5`).
- **Two-Stage Proteolytic Processing:**
  1. **Intracellular pro-C5 processing:** Following removal of the signal peptide (residues 1–18) and a tetra-basic linker (`RPRR`, residues 674–677), mature plasma C5 circulates as a ~190 kDa disulfide-linked two-chain glycoprotein composed of the N-terminal **beta chain** (`PRO_0000005985`, residues 19–673) and C-terminal **alpha chain** (`PRO_0000005987`, residues 678–1676) [PMID:106884, PMID:690134].
  2. **Convertase-mediated activation cleavage (Arg751–Leu752):** Upon activation of the classical/lectin (`C4b2b3b`), alternative (`C3bBb3b`), or granzyme K (`GZMK`-initiated `C4b2b3b`) complement cascades, surface-assembled C5 convertases cleave the C5 alpha chain between Arg751 and Leu752 [PMID:12878586, PMID:21217642, PMID:30643019, PMID:39914456], generating two structurally and functionally distinct mature products (`functional_isoforms`):
     - **`C5a` (`PRO_0000005988`, C5a anaphylatoxin, residues 678–751, 74 aa):** A soluble four-helix bundle glycoprotein (`N`-glycosylated at Asn741, position 64 of C5a) released into the extracellular fluid [PMID:690134]. `C5a` acts as a high-affinity agonist of the complement anaphylatoxin receptors `C5AR1` (`CD88`, `UniProtKB:P21730`, coupled to $G\alpha_i$/$G\alpha_o$/$G\alpha_{16}$ and $\beta$-arrestin) and `C5AR2` (`C5L2`, `UniProtKB:Q9P296`, an atypical $\beta$-arrestin-biased receptor lacking G protein coupling) [PMID:8182049, PMID:9553099, PMID:36806352, PMID:37169960, PMID:37852260]. Plasma carboxypeptidases N and B2 rapidly cleave the C-terminal Arg751 (Arg74 of C5a) to yield **`C5a des-Arg`** (residues 678–750), a naturally occurring partial/biased agonist with ~10–100-fold lower potency at `C5AR1` that dampens acute inflammatory signaling while retaining leukocyte chemotactic activity at higher concentrations [PMID:690134, PMID:37852260].
     - **`C5b` (`PRO_0000005989` alpha' chain residues 752–1676 disulfide-linked to the beta chain `PRO_0000005985`):** Undergoes a dramatic conformational rearrangement of its MG3, MG7, MG8, CUB, and thioester-like domain (`TED`/`C5d`) upon release of `C5a` [PMID:22832194]. Unlike `C3` and `C4`, `C5` lacks an internal reactive thioester bond in its TED domain (`C5` is not a covalent opsonin and is not an endopeptidase inhibitor). Instead, nascent `C5b` exposes a transient (~2 min half-life) binding site captured by `C6` to form the stable `C5b6` subcomplex, which sequentially recruits `C7`, heterotrimeric `C8` (`C8A`/`C8B`/`C8G`), and ~18 copies of `C9` to assemble the cytolytic **membrane attack complex (MAC, `GO:0005579`)** pore in target bacterial and cellular membranes [PMID:22832194, PMID:30552328, PMID:30643019, PMID:34667172].

## 2. Ligand-Side Signaling Pathway Audit (`LIGANDS_IN_SIGNALING`)
- **Missing Pathway Annotation (`NEW`):**
  - `GO:0038178` (*complement component C5a signaling pathway*, defined as *"The series of molecular signals generated as a consequence of the complement component C5a binding to a cell surface receptor"*, `is_a GO:0007186` *G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway*) is annotated in human GOA to the receptors `C5AR1` (`P21730`) and `C5AR2` (`Q9P296`), but **not** to the ligand `C5` (`P01031`).
  - Meanwhile, `C5` carries legacy ProtInc `TAS` annotations for the broader parent terms `GO:0007166` (*cell surface receptor signaling pathway*) and `GO:0007186` (*G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway*) citing `PMID:8759757` (Nilsson et al. 1996, showing pertussis toxin-sensitive C5a-induced calcium mobilization and chemotaxis in HMC-1 human mast cells) and `PMID:8898085` (Ames et al. 1996, functional expression of human C5aR and C3aR in *Xenopus* oocytes).
  - We modify `GO:0007166` and `GO:0007186` to `GO:0038178` (*complement component C5a signaling pathway*) and propose `GO:0038178` as a `NEW` core biological process annotation on `C5` (specifically executed by the `C5a` isoform `PRO_0000005988`), supported by cryo-EM structures of the human `C5a`–`C5AR1`–$G_i$ signaling complex [PMID:36806352, PMID:37169960, PMID:37852260] and receptor-binding/signaling studies [PMID:8182049, PMID:8759757, PMID:9553099].
- **Receptor Binding Specificity:**
  - `C5a` binds `C5AR1` via a two-site/three-site mechanism: the helical bundle core of `C5a` engages the sulfated/acidic N-terminal extracellular segment (residues 2–30, especially 21–30) of `C5AR1`, while the C-terminal octapeptide tail of `C5a` (`HKDMQLGR`, terminating in Arg751/Arg74) inserts into the transmembrane orthosteric pocket of `C5AR1` to trigger TM6 outward movement and $G_i$ coupling [PMID:8182049, PMID:9553099, PMID:36806352, PMID:37169960, PMID:37852260].
  - Existing `GO:0001664` (*G protein-coupled receptor binding*, IBA) and `GO:0005102` (*signaling receptor binding*, TAS from `PMID:9218605` and `PMID:9590258`, IPI from `PMID:16452172`) are well supported; `GO:0005102` is refined (`MODIFY`) to `GO:0001664` (*G protein-coupled receptor binding*).

## 3. Erroneous Annotations on `C5`: Chemokine Activity (`GO:0008009`) and Endopeptidase Inhibitor Activity (`GO:0004866`)
1. **`GO:0008009` (*chemokine activity*) (`TAS`, `PMID:10820279`) and `GO:0060326` (*cell chemotaxis*) (`IEA`, `GO:0008009` -> `GO:0060326`) — `REMOVE`:**
   - `C5` carries a legacy ProtInc `TAS` annotation for `GO:0008009` (*chemokine activity*) citing `PMID:10820279` (Luu et al. 2000, *"Differential ability of exogenous chemotactic agents to disrupt transendothelial migration of flowing neutrophils"*), which compared fMLP, IL-8, ENA-78, GRO-$\alpha$, and zymosan-activated plasma/C5a as *"exogenous chemotactic agents"*.
   - Worse, PAINT node `PTN008967461` in `PANTHER:PTHR11412` used `UniProtKB:P01031` (`C5`) and `UniProtKB:P01024` (`C3`) as IBD seeds to propagate `GO:0008009` (*chemokine activity*) across the entire Eumetazoan complement C3/C4/C5 clade!
   - **Why this is false:** `C5a` and `C3a` are **complement anaphylatoxins** (`IPR001840` / `PF01821` four-helix bundle cleavage fragments of $\alpha_2$-macroglobulin/complement family precursors `PTHR11412`) that signal through anaphylatoxin GPCRs (`C5AR1`, `C5AR2`, `C3AR1` in `PTHR24225` `CHEMOTACTIC RECEPTOR`). They are **not chemokines** (which are small Greek-key $\beta$-sheet cytokines of families `PTHR12015` and `PTHR10179` with conserved CC/CXC/C/CX3C disulfide motifs that activate chemokine receptors in `PTHR10489`). Indeed, `PMID:37169960` explicitly notes that cryo-EM structures *"reveal a conserved recognition pattern of anaphylatoxins to the complement receptors that is different from chemokine receptors"*.
   - While `C5a` genuinely mediates leukocyte/neutrophil/mast cell chemotaxis (`GO:0006935` *chemotaxis*, `GO:0030593` *neutrophil chemotaxis*), it is a non-chemokine chemoattractant; both `GO:0008009` (TAS) and the `GO_REF:0000116` GOC-OWL `IEA` for `GO:0060326` (*cell chemotaxis*, inferred solely from `GO:0008009`) must be marked `REMOVE`.
2. **`GO:0004866` (*endopeptidase inhibitor activity*) (`IEA`, `GO_REF:0000002` via `IPR001599`/`IPR002890`) — `REMOVE`:**
   - InterPro2GO maps the broad $\alpha_2$-macroglobulin domains (`IPR001599` `Macroglobln_a2`, `IPR002890` `MG2`) to `GO:0004866` (*endopeptidase inhibitor activity*).
   - While $\alpha_2$-macroglobulin (`A2M`, `P01023`, `PANTHER:PTHR11412:SF165`) and pregnancy zone protein (`PZP`) are genuine bait-region/thioester-trapping endopeptidase inhibitors (and PAINT correctly restricts `GO:0004866` to the `A2M` clade `PTN000892454`), complement `C5` (`P01031`, `PANTHER:PTHR11412:SF83`) lacks both the protease-trapping bait region and the internal reactive thioester (`C5` is cleaved *by* C5 convertase endopeptidases at Arg751–Leu752 to generate `C5a` and `C5b`) [PMID:21217642, PMID:22832194]. Thus `GO:0004866` on `C5` is a classic domain-level InterPro2GO over-annotation and is marked `REMOVE`.
