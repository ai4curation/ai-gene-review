Gene Description

H3-3A encodes histone H3.3, a replacement histone also encoded as an identical protein by H3-3B. H3.3 associates with H4 and forms part of the DNA-contacting histone core of nucleosomes. Its deposition can occur independently of DNA synthesis, allowing histone replacement within chromatin.
