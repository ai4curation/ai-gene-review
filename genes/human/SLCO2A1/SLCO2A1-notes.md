# SLCO2A1 (OATP2A1 / PGT, prostaglandin transporter) — curation notes

UniProt: Q92959 (SO2A1_HUMAN). Gene: SLCO2A1 (HGNC:10955); synonyms OATP2A1, SLC21A2, PGT.
Family: organic anion transporter (TC 2.A.60) / SLCO-OATP family; PANTHER PTHR11388:SF14;
Pfam PF03137 (OATP); InterPro IPR004156 (OATP). 643 aa, 12-TM multi-pass membrane protein
(MFS fold) with a Kazal-like extracellular domain (DOMAIN 438..496).

## Core function: the prostaglandin transporter (PGT)

SLCO2A1 is the prototypical **prostaglandin transporter**. It mediates cellular uptake (and,
bidirectionally, efflux) of prostaglandins and thromboxanes across the plasma membrane.

- UniProt CC FUNCTION: "Mediates the transport of prostaglandins (PGs, mainly PGE2, PGE1,
  PGE3, PGF2alpha, PGD2, PGH2) and thromboxanes (thromboxane B2) across the cell membrane"
  [UniProtKB:Q92959 CC:FUNCTION, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11997326,26692285,8787677].
- Original human cloning/expression: "a full-length clone catalyzed the transport of PGE1,
  PGE2, PGD2, PGF2alpha, and, to a lesser degree, TXB2" [PMID:8787677 abstract]; 82% aa
  identity to rat PGT.
- Reactome: "The human gene SLCO2A1 encodes prostaglandin transporter PGT. It is ubiquitously
  expressed and can transport the protaglandins PGD2, PGE1, PGE2 and PGF2A" [Reactome:R-HSA-879528].

### Mechanism: Na+-independent secondary active anion exchange (PG/lactate antiport)

PGT is NOT a Na+- or proton-coupled symporter. It is an electroneutral/electrogenic
**exchanger** that couples prostaglandin uptake to efflux of intracellular monovalent anions,
principally **lactate** generated by glycolysis:

- "Uses lactate, generated by glycolysis, as a counter-substrate to mediate PGE2 influx and
  efflux" [UniProtKB:Q92959 CC:FUNCTION, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11997326]. Lactate as the driving
  force was shown by Chan et al. (PMID:11997326).
- "PG uptake is dependent upon membrane potential and involves exchange of a monovalent anionic
  substrate (PGs exist physiologically as an anionic monovalent form) with a stoichiometry of
  1:1 for divalent anions or of 1:2 for monovalent anions" [UniProtKB:Q92959 CC:FUNCTION,
  ECO:0000269|PubMed:29204966]. This supports the GO MF "secondary active transmembrane
  transporter activity" (GO:0015291) and BP "sodium-independent organic anion transport"
  (GO:0043252).
- Reactome family statement: OATPs "mediate the sodium-independent transport of a wide range of
  amphipathic organic compounds" [Reactome:R-HSA-879518].
- Catalytic activity (UniProt, Rhea): e.g. prostaglandin E2(out) + 2 (S)-lactate(in) =
  prostaglandin E2(in) + 2 (S)-lactate(out) (RHEA:74383); analogous reactions for PGE1, PGF2α,
  PGD2, TxB2, PGE3, PGH2.
- Kinetics: KM 0.331 µM for PGE2, 7.202 µM for PGE3 [UniProtKB:Q92959 CC:BIOPHYSICOCHEMICAL].

### Physiological role: PG clearance and signal termination

PGT delivers extracellular PGs to the cytoplasm where 15-hydroxyprostaglandin dehydrogenase
(HPGD) oxidizes/inactivates them ("two-step model of PG signal termination"; Nomura et al.
PMID:15044627).

- "Plays a role in the clearance of PGs from the circulation through cellular uptake, which
  allows cytoplasmic oxidation and PG signal termination" [UniProtKB:Q92959 CC:FUNCTION,
  ECO:0000269|PubMed:8787677].
- "The broad tissue distribution and substrate profile of hPGT suggest a role in the transport
  and/or metabolic clearance of PGs in diverse human tissues" [PMID:8787677 abstract].
- Context-dependent roles noted in UniProt (mostly By similarity / specific tissue studies):
  transcellular (vectorial) PG release in epithelia, renal Na+ balance in the collecting duct,
  endometrial decidualization (PMID:16339169), ovulation (PMID:27169804). These are downstream
  physiological consequences of the transport function, not activities PGT itself performs.

## Localization

- Plasma membrane is the core location (CC SUBCELLULAR LOCATION "Cell membrane"; multiple TAS/IEA
  rows). Multi-pass (12 TM).
- Testis/blood-testis barrier: "OATP2A1 localization to the basal membrane of SCs is consistent
  with its role as the prototypical prostaglandin transporter" [PMID:35307651] — IDA basal
  plasma membrane (GO:0009925) in Sertoli cells. "The basal membrane localization of OAT1,
  OAT2, OATP1A2, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OATP2A1, OATP2B1, and OATP3A1-v2" [PMID:35307651].
- Basolateral plasma membrane (GO:0016323) IBA from the OATP clade; in epithelia PGT can be
  apical or basolateral depending on cell type (vectorial release).
- Cytoplasm (GO:0005737) and Lysosome (GO:0005764) are listed by UniProt only By similarity to
  the rat ortholog (Q9EPT5), reflecting intracellular/vesicular pools; no direct human evidence.
  Peripheral to the plasma-membrane transport function.

## Disease (downstream of impaired PG clearance — necessity, not a function PGT performs)

Loss-of-function SLCO2A1 variants cause **primary hypertrophic osteoarthropathy (PHOAR2 /
pachydermoperiostosis; MIM:614441, 167100)** and isolated digital clubbing, and are associated
with chronic enteropathy (CEAS). Mechanism: failure to clear PGE2 → elevated PGE2 → periostosis,
pachydermia, clubbing (UniProt DISEASE; Zhang et al. PMID:22197487; Seifert et al. PMID:22331663;
Diggle et al. PMID:22553128). These are pleiotropic downstream effects of PGE2 accumulation; the
bone/skin remodeling, inflammation and vascular processes are executed by PG receptors and their
effector pathways, not by PGT. Per the participation test, PGT is `involved_in` prostaglandin
transmembrane transport only; the osteoarthropathy etc. are not core functions.
