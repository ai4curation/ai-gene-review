| Mitochondrial tRNA^Thr charging | Purified human mitochondrial ThrRS activated threonine and charged mitochondrial tRNA^Thr(UGU). It discriminated threonine over serine by **383-fold**; G35C or U36C abolished charging, whereas A73C partially reduced it. | Direct human in-vitro biochemistry and tRNA identity-element mutagenesis | (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000032) |
| Translational proofreading | Human TARS2 can misactivate serine. Mutating editing-site **His133/His137** eliminated deacylation of Ser-tRNA^Thr and increased mischarging, establishing post-transfer editing as the critical final safeguard. | Direct human enzyme mutagenesis and biochemical assays | (pqac-00000031) |
| Editing fidelity in vivo | Disabling the corresponding mouse Tars2 editing residues caused Ser-tRNA^Thr accumulation and Thr→Ser mistranslation, impaired mitochondrial translation/OXPHOS, increased ROS, and induced G0/G1 arrest. Heart-restricted editing deficiency produced contractile dysfunction, dilated cardiomyopathy, and fibrosis. | 2023 primary study using mammalian cells and a genetically engineered mouse model | (pqac-00000013) |
| Mitochondrial localization and RNA granules | TARS2 is synthesized as a nuclear-encoded precursor with an N-terminal targeting sequence and imported into mitochondria. Native mitochondrial ThrRS formed foci that colocalized with the mitochondrial RNA-granule marker GRSF1; deleting its C-terminal tRNA-binding region markedly reduced enrichment at GRSF1 foci. | Human-cell localization, microscopy, and domain-deletion evidence | (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000029, pqac-00000035) |
| Cytosolic pool | A 2023 review reports that approximately **10–15%** of TARS2 is cytosolic. This estimate should **not** be interpreted as showing that 10–15% is lysosomal; no quantitative lysosome-versus-mitochondrion partition was established in the cited excerpt. | Secondary-source estimate; compartmental interpretation limited | (pqac-00000036) |
| Threonine-sensitive Rag–mTORC1 signaling | TARS2 residues **301–381** mediate Rag interaction. Four disease variants in this region had reduced abundance (**38.9–65.6% of wild type**) and reduced normalized binding to RagA (**41.3–48.2%**) and RagC (**20.4–50.5%**), supporting impaired amino-acid-dependent mTORC1 activation. | 2023 human-cell co-immunoprecipitation and disease-variant analysis; lower protein stability is a potential confounder | (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000028) |
| Developmental and mTORC1-related effects | Zebrafish tars2 knockdown caused reduced head/cerebellar size, cardiac edema, apoptosis, impaired myelination, and muscle abnormalities; wild-type human TARS2 mRNA rescued major phenotypes. PGC1α decreased and PPARα increased, consistent with altered mTORC1-regulated signaling. | 2023 in-vivo morpholino/rescue model; supportive rather than definitive human-pathway proof | (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000028) |
| Human recessive disease burden | In a 2023 cohort, **18 new individuals from 15 families** carried biallelic TARS2 variants. Across the expanded series, axial hypotonia occurred in **24/27**, elevated lactate in **17/20**, seizures in **14/28**, and cerebellar atrophy in **75%** of those assessed. | Multicenter human genetic/clinical study with functional variant testing | (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000017) |
