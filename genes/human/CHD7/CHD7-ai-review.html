Gene Description

CHD7 is an ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler that binds DNA and nucleosomes and changes histone–DNA contacts. Histone recognition and associations with other chromatin regulators help place its activity at cell-type-specific regulatory regions. CHD7 acts in nuclear chromatin and also has a nucleolar pool associated with active ribosomal DNA and rRNA expression. Human cell models establish roles in neural crest formation and neuroepithelial identity, while pathogenic variants cause CHARGE syndrome with variable craniofacial, sensory, cardiac, genital and neurodevelopmental abnormalities.

The defining function is ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling. Purified human CHD7 assays directly measure ATP hydrolysis, DNA/nucleosome engagement, nucleosome repositioning and altered restriction accessibility (PMID:23134727; PMID:28533432). The WT/K999R control supports intrinsic catalytic coupling. The 2017 Methods pair a correct CHD7 CCDS with a CHD9 UniProt accession; official CCDS and UniProt mapping resolve the intended long CHD7 sequence, not an independently resequenced experimental plasmid.
