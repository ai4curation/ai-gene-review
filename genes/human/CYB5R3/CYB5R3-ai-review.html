Gene Description

CYB5R3 is an FAD-containing NADH–cytochrome-b5 reductase that transfers reducing equivalents from NADH to cytochrome b5. Its N-terminally anchored, myristoylated membrane product acts at the endoplasmic reticulum and mitochondrial outer membrane, whereas a shorter soluble product is expressed during late erythroid maturation and supports methemoglobin reduction. Partner redox systems connect this chemistry to lipid metabolism and vascular nitric-oxide handling. Reconstituted CYB5R3–cytochrome-b5–mARC systems also support nitrite reduction, with mARC performing the terminal molybdenum-dependent reaction. Pathogenic variants can impair stability, FAD organization or nicotinamide-cofactor handling and cause recessive congenital methemoglobinemia.
