# RXFP1 notes

- Core function: RXFP1 is a plasma-membrane relaxin family peptide GPCR that activates adenylate cyclase and cAMP signaling.
- GO has generic peptide- and protein-hormone receptor terms that fit RXFP1 relaxin binding; the `G protein-coupled receptor activity` InterPro row stops at the receptor-family level.
- The AKAP5, CTRP8, RAMP2, and RAMP3 rows are all bare `GO:0005515 protein binding`; they are row-specific interactions but not separate GO molecular functions.
- The InterPro `signal transduction` row is true but too broad once GPCR and hormone-mediated signaling rows are present.
