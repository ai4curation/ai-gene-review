## DNA binding and signaling
PMID:31582430 full text is decisive: human recombinant full-length SMAD2 and its MH1 domain bind SBE/5GC DNA. The E3 insert changes the conformational equilibrium and affinity; it does not make full-length SMAD2 categorically unable to bind DNA. Historical claims based on aggregated GST preparations or the stronger short Δexon3 isoform are not generalized into removals. The mouse mesendoderm assays in that study remain distinguished from its direct human-protein biochemical assays. Existing isoforms Q15796-1/2 are preserved. PMID:9389648, PMID:9702198 and PMID:15350224 support FAST1/FOXH1 complexes, activin response and R-SMAD/co-SMAD heteromers. SMAD2 is a receptor substrate and transcriptional signal transducer, not a receptor kinase.

## Localization
Human Protein Atlas page inspected 2026-10-25: https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000175387-SMAD2/subcellular. It states: "Mainly localized to the nucleoplasm and nucleoli." Cytosol is additionally supported. Thus the experimental nucleolus annotation is retained as non-core, without assigning ribosome biogenesis. Cytosolic and nuclear cycling are independently supported by primary receptor and nuclear-export studies.

## Noncanonical and developmental contexts
PMID:19366699 full text establishes BMP9-induced SMAD2 responses in human pulmonary artery endothelial cells, including Smad2-dependent IL8 and E-selectin transcription. This contextual activity is retained despite SMAD2's usual activin/TGF-beta branch assignment. Primary miRNA processing is treated separately from core transcription and does not imply RNase activity. Tau binding (PMID:24964035), cholesterol-modulated signaling (PMID:17878231), trophoblast invasion (PMID:34432647), and anti-osteogenic BMP3b/GDF10 signaling (PMID:22155034) remain peripheral.

Mouse donor annotation records were queried only to trace original experiments, never quoted as evidence. PMID:14691138 supports SMAD2/4-LEF1 palate signaling. PMID:33548622 and the unmodified Q62432 UniProt record support the context-dependent odontoblast role. By contrast, PMID:14656760 principally maps renal expression and speculates about functions; the exact ureteric-bud developmental transfer is marked over-annotated. The left/right annotation from PMID:17239842 remains unresolved because its cached abstract does not expose the SMAD2-specific manipulation. No experimental annotation was removed because a title foregrounded another gene.
