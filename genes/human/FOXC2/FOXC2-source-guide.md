# FOXC2 source guide

This guide distinguishes original annotation sources from auxiliary corroboration. Selected Methods/Results or captions are identified below; availability of an article is not a claim that every section, supplementary assay or figure image was examined. The source assessments apply to specific claims, so an UNVERIFIED source may also contain verified experiments for other annotations. Original reference identifiers are preserved.

No new publication quotations were added. Supported generic interactions remain non-core when their source does not justify a specific functional refinement; unresolved evidence remains undecided. A partner’s activity, location or tissue role is not automatically transferred to FOXC2.
