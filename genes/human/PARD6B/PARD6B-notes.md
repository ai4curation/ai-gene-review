# PARD6B notes

## 2026-11-15 re-review of source defects

- The `WRONG_REFERENCE` defect on the ComplexPortal NAS row GO:0070160 tight junction
  (PMID:11257119) was withdrawn. The paper does describe PARD6A, not PARD6B
  [PMID:11257119 "a 1,041-bp open reading frame encoding a protein of 346 amino acid residues"],
  but the row is not a citation slip. The Complex Portal records for the PARD6B variants of the
  PAR polarity complex (CPX-6193 PARD6B-PRKCI, CPX-6196 PARD6B-PRKCZ) give their evidence as
  ECO:0005546, inferred by paralogy, and QuickGO by reference shows the same NAS row on all six
  variant complexes (CPX-6183, CPX-6193 to CPX-6197) and on PARD6A, PARD6B, PARD6G, PRKCI, PRKCZ
  and PARD3. ComplexPortal therefore applies the PARD6A paper to the paralogous complexes
  deliberately. The action stays `ACCEPT`: tight junction localization of PARD6B is supported by
  UniProt (by similarity to mouse Pard6b).
