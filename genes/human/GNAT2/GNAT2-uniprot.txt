CC   -!- FUNCTION: Alpha subunit of the heterotrimeric guanine nucleotide-
CC       binding protein (G protein) that transduces signals from the G protein-
CC       coupled cone photoreceptors (blue, green and red cones)
CC       (PubMed:12077706, PubMed:2534964). Functions as a GTPase, cycling
CC       between an active GTP-bound state and an inactive GDP-bound state. Upon
CC       photoactivation, cone photoreceptors promote GDP release and GTP
CC       binding (By similarity). In its active state, GNAT2 stimulates
CC       downstream effectors, including the cone cGMP-phosphodiesterase (PDE6)
CC       complex, to mediate the phototransduction cascade (By similarity).
CC       Activation of the PDE6 complex occurs through binding and repositioning
CC       of the inhibitory PDE6 gamma subunits (PDE6H), thereby promoting
CC       catalytic activity (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04695,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12077706, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2534964}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterotrimeric G proteins are composed of alpha, beta and

CC       site. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04695}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell projection, cilium, photoreceptor outer
CC       segment {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2534964}. Photoreceptor inner segment

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Retinal cone photoreceptors.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2534964}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Achromatopsia 4 (ACHM4) [MIM:613856]: An ocular stationary
CC       disorder due to the absence of functioning cone photoreceptors in the
CC       retina. It is characterized by total colorblindness, low visual acuity,
CC       photophobia and nystagmus. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12077706}. Note=The
CC       disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented in this
CC       entry.
