# GNAT2 (cone transducin alpha, Galpha-t2) notes

## Identity
Human GNAT2, UniProt P19087, 354 aa, G-alpha i/o/t/z subfamily (PANTHER PTHR10218:SF68).
Expressed in all three human cone classes [PMID:2534964 "demonstrate the presence of the Tc alpha peptide in all types of cones"].

## Function
- Cone transducin alpha: couples cone opsins to PDE6 [PMID:12077706 "a heterotrimeric G-protein that couples to the cone visual pigments"]; activation mechanism described there (GDP/GTP exchange, release from beta-gamma, PDE activation). Biochemistry mostly from bovine rod Gt; cone-specific mechanism inferred by homology (82% identity).
- Human truncating alleles cause achromatopsia (ACHM4) [PMID:12077706 "We therefore argue that the mutations represent functional null alleles of GNAT2"].
- Mouse Gnat2 cpfl3 missense: loss of cone ERG [PMID:17065522 "The Gnat2(cpfl3) mutation leads to cone dysfunction"].
- Rat: endogenous cone Talpha translocates out of the outer segment only under bright light (7000 lux) [PMID:17591908].

## Doubtful rows
- Bitter taste and positive regulation of cytosolic Ca2+ (rat IDA PMID:12917372, propagated by IEA and by PAINT to the GNAT2 node): the experiment used a Galpha16 chimera carrying the Galpha-t2 C-terminal tail, read out by calcium, in heterologous cells; the authors only say Galpha-t2 "may also be involved". Not evidence that GNAT2 acts in taste.
- Synapse (mouse IMP PMID:24403163): an ON-RGC AMPA receptor study that used Gnat2 mutant mice as a cone-less background; it is not a localization of Gnat2. Remove.
- G protein-coupled photoreceptor activity (NAS): the opsin's activity, not transducin's. Remove.
- Adenylate cyclase-modulating GPCR pathway: transducin's effector is PDE6; removed as for GNAT1.
- Reactome WNT5A/FZD rows place G alpha t2 at plasma membrane in non-retinal signalling (mouse F9/melanoma literature, not read). In cones the disc membranes are plasma membrane infoldings, so plasma membrane is true; refined to cone photoreceptor disc membrane.
