Gene Description

LIG3 encodes DNA ligase III, an ATP-dependent DNA ligase (EC 6.5.1.1) that seals single-strand nicks in duplex DNA through an enzyme-AMP intermediate. It has an N-terminal PARP-type zinc finger that, with the DNA-binding domain, senses nicks and DNA ends and hands them to the nucleotidyltransferase/OB-fold catalytic core. Alternative translation initiation gives two forms: isoform 1, with an N-terminal mitochondrial transit peptide, is imported into mitochondria, where LIG3 is the only DNA ligase and seals Okazaki fragments during mtDNA replication and nicks in mitochondrial base excision repair; isoform 3 (LIG3alpha) is nuclear and forms a stable BRCT-mediated heterodimer with XRCC1, which imports and stabilizes it. Nuclear LIG3alpha/XRCC1 ligates nicks in short-patch base excision and single-strand break repair, can complete nucleotide excision repair (alongside LIG1), and is the main ligase of the microhomology-biased alternative end-joining pathway of double-strand break repair, being recruited to such breaks by MRN. A germ-cell splice form (LIG3beta) lacks the BRCT domain. The mitochondrial function is the essential one: nuclear LIG3 is dispensable in mouse cells, where LIG1 can substitute, whereas loss of mitochondrial LIG3 depletes mtDNA, and biallelic human LIG3 variants cause a mitochondrial neurogastrointestinal encephalomyopathy-like mtDNA depletion syndrome. LIG3 does not take part in V(D)J recombination.

Key findings

  • One gene, two compartments. Isoform 1 (P49916-1) carries an N-terminal mitochondrial
    transit peptide; isoform 3 (alpha, P49916-3) starts at the downstream ATG and is nuclear;
    isoforms 2/4 (beta, germ-cell splice form) lack the XRCC1-binding BRCT
    PMID:10207110.
  • Activity: ATP-dependent nick sealing; the PARP-like ZnF senses nicks/ends
    [PMID:20518483 "The collective results support a \"jackknife model\" in which the ZnF loads
    ligase III onto nicked DNA and conformational changes deliver DNA into the active site."].
    LigIIIalpha/XRCC1 and LigIIIbeta join nicked and linear duplex substrates
    PMID:19589734.
  • Essential role is mitochondrial: PMID:21390132; PMID:21390131. Human biallelic variants cause MNGIE-like mtDNA depletion
    PMID:33855352.
  • mtDNA replication: Okazaki-fragment ligation PMID:21878356. The
    GOA IMP row for this paper uses "negative regulation of mitochondrial DNA replication",
    which inverts the sign of the knockdown result (knockdown attenuated recovery of mtDNA
    copy number). Recorded as a source defect (CACAO).
  • mtBER: PMID:24674627.
  • Nuclear BER/SSBR with XRCC1 (Reactome; PMID:33087283 review), redundant with LIG1 in
    mouse cells (PMID:21390131).
  • Alt-NHEJ/MMEJ: PMID:15899791; PMID:21816818; translocations reduced
    without nuclear Lig3 PMID:21655080; LIG1 can substitute for some A-EJ PMID:26787905.
  • Not in V(D)J recombination: LIG3 cannot complement LIG4-null pre-B cells (PMID:9809069),
    supporting the NOT annotation.
  • Context: telomere end-joining pathway choice (module and GO-CAM review proposed in ai4curation/ai-gene-review#5904; not on this branch and not used as evidence). The placement of LIG3 (with PARP1)
