| Domain/Region | Location | Key Features | Functional Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signal peptide / precursor segment | Extreme N-terminus; removed during maturation in the secretory pathway | Directs nascent FGFR4 into the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane and supports biosynthetic processing as a type-I single-pass membrane protein | Enables extracellular presentation of the ligand-binding region and delivery of mature FGFR4 to the cell surface |
| Immunoglobulin-like domain D1 (IgI) | N-terminal extracellular region | Contains the N-glycosylation site **Asn112**; together with the acid box, forms an autoinhibitory module rather than the principal ligand-binding surface (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001) | Limits inappropriate receptor activation and helps maintain FGFR4 in a quiescent state; glycosylation influences receptor folding, processing, and localization (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000021) |
| Acid box | Extracellular linker between D1 and D2 | Acidic, serine-rich sequence characteristic of FGFR-family receptors (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000006) | Cooperates with D1 in receptor autoinhibition and modulates accessibility of the D2–D3 ligand-binding module (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004) |
| Immunoglobulin-like domain D2 (IgII) | Central extracellular region | Contains **Asn258**, a reported N-glycosylation site; contributes substantially to the FGF-binding interface and coreceptor interactions (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001) | Directly participates in FGF recognition and formation of ligand–receptor–coreceptor complexes with heparan sulfate or Klotho proteins (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003) |
| Immunoglobulin-like domain D3 (IgIII; FGFR4 c-type configuration) | Membrane-proximal extracellular region | Contains reported N-glycosylation sites **Asn290, Asn311, and Asn322**; combines with D2 to form the principal ligand-binding pocket. Unlike FGFR1–3, FGFR4 has one principal IgIII configuration rather than alternatively spliced b/c isoforms (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000003) | Major determinant of ligand selectivity, including the structurally favored interaction with FGF19; glycosylation contributes to receptor maturation, surface delivery, processing, and cytoprotective signaling (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000021) |
| Transmembrane domain | Single α-helical segment spanning the plasma membrane | Connects the extracellular ligand-binding module to the cytoplasmic signaling region; receptor activation organizes FGFR4 dimers across the membrane (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003) | Transmits ligand- and coreceptor-induced conformational changes across the membrane, bringing intracellular kinase domains into an activation-competent arrangement |
| Juxtamembrane region | Cytoplasmic segment immediately C-terminal to the transmembrane helix | Provides binding interfaces for membrane-associated adaptor proteins, notably FRS2 and CRK/CRKL (pqac-00000002) | Couples activated FGFR4 to FRS2-dependent RAS–MAPK and PI3K–AKT signaling and helps organize receptor-proximal signaling complexes (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000027) |
| Tyrosine-kinase domain—TK1 and TK2 lobes | Intracellular catalytic region | Split protein-kinase architecture containing the ATP-binding pocket and activation loop; ligand-induced dimerization causes trans-autophosphorylation. The hinge-region **Cys552** is uncommon among FGFR paralogues and can be covalently targeted by FGFR4-selective inhibitors (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000047) | Catalyzes ATP-dependent transfer of phosphate to tyrosine residues on FGFR4 and downstream substrates such as FRS2α and PLCγ, initiating MAPK, PI3K–AKT, PLCγ–PKC/Ca²⁺, and STAT signaling (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000027) |
| C-terminal tail | Intracellular region following the kinase domain | Flexible regulatory segment containing phosphorylation-dependent interaction sites; forms part of the signaling platform created by activated FGFR4 (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000016) | Supports recruitment and regulation of downstream effectors and contributes to the magnitude, duration, and routing of FGFR4 signaling, including signaling from plasma-membrane and endosomal compartments (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000020) |
