| Signaling pathway | Key mediators | Downstream effectors | Biological outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RAS–RAF–MEK–ERK (MAPK)** | Activated FGFR4 phosphorylates **FRS2α**, which recruits **GRB2** and **SOS**; SOS activates **RAS**, followed by **RAF** and **MEK** | **ERK1/2** and nuclear transcription factors such as ELK1, FOS, JUN, and ETS-family proteins | Promotes context-dependent proliferation, differentiation, migration, invasion, regenerative responses, and cell-cycle gene expression; persistent activation contributes to oncogenesis (pqac-00000027, pqac-00000028, pqac-00000031) |
| **PI3K–AKT–mTOR** | Phosphorylated **FRS2α** recruits **GRB2** and **GAB1**; GAB1 recruits **PI3K**, leading to PIP3-dependent activation of **AKT** | **mTORC1**, PDK1, TSC2, FOXO proteins, and apoptosis regulators including BAD, BAX, and caspase-9 | Supports cell survival, growth, protein synthesis, metabolism, angiogenesis, and resistance to apoptosis; aberrant FGFR4 signaling can promote tumor progression and metastasis (pqac-00000026, pqac-00000028, pqac-00000031) |
| **PLCγ–PKC–Ca²⁺** | **PLCγ** binds a phosphorylated receptor docking site and is phosphorylated; activated PLCγ hydrolyzes **PIP2** into **IP3** and **DAG** | IP3-induced intracellular **Ca²⁺** release, **PKC**, NFAT, and calcium-dependent signaling proteins | Controls calcium signaling, transcription, differentiation, migration, and receptor regulation; in FGFR4-driven HCC, PLCγ/ERK-dependent calcium entry can support cancer stem-cell self-renewal (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000026, pqac-00000027) |
| **SRC–STAT3 / JAK–STAT** | Activated FGFR4 engages **SRC** and, depending on context, JAK-associated signaling; **STAT3** is phosphorylated and incorporated into signaling complexes | STAT3 dimerization and **nuclear translocation**, followed by transcriptional regulation; STAT1 and STAT5 can also participate in broader FGFR signaling | Regulates survival, proliferation, inflammatory and oncogenic transcription programs. In FGF19-expressing HCC cells, FGFR4, SRC, and STAT3 form an endosomal complex, with SRC required for delivery of the signaling complex and STAT3 to the nucleus (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000027) |


*Table: The table summarizes the four principal signaling branches activated downstream of FGFR4, from proximal adaptors to functional outputs. These pathways explain how receptor activation regulates proliferation, survival, metabolism, calcium signaling, and transcription.*
