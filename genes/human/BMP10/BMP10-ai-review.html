Gene Description

BMP10 is a secreted TGF-beta-superfamily growth factor synthesized as a precursor with an N-terminal prodomain and a C-terminal signaling chain. Proprotein-convertase processing permits receptor activation while the prodomain can remain noncovalently associated. BMP10 engages type I and type II serine/threonine kinase receptors, including ACVRL1/ALK1 and BMPRII, to stimulate SMAD1/5/9 signaling. Its extracellular signaling supports cardiac development and regulates endothelial responses. The processed prodomain-growth-factor complex binds fibrillin-1, contributing to extracellular targeting, and interaction with telethonin has been implicated in secretion and cardiac hypertrophic responses.
