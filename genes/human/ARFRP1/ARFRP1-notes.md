# Curation Notes for Human ARFRP1 (UniProtKB:Q13795, HGNC:662)

## 1. Gene Identity, Orthology, and the `ARL3` Nomenclature Hazard
- **Symbol / Accession**: `ARFRP1` (`UniProtKB:Q13795`, `HGNC:662`; historical aliases `ARP`, `ARP1`), a 201-residue small monomeric Ras/Arf-superfamily GTPase in `PANTHER:PTHR45909:SF1` (ancestral node `PANTHER:PTN000196303`).
- **Critical Homonym Trap (`ARFRP1` vs `ARL3`)**:
  - Budding yeast *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* `ARL3` (`UniProtKB:Q02804`, `SGD:S000005972`, `YPL051W`) is the true 1:1 ortholog of mammalian `ARFRP1` (`UniProtKB:Q13795`) [PMID:9920936, "We have identified a yeast gene (yARL3) encoding a protein that is structurally related (>43% identical) to the mammalian ARF-like protein ARP."].
  - Mammalian `ARL3` (`UniProtKB:P36405`, `HGNC:694`) is an unrelated ciliary GTPase (regulated by ARL13B GEF and RP2 GAP) absent from budding yeast. Literature or automated text-mining that conflates yeast `ARL3` with mammalian ciliary `ARL3` must be strictly separated from `ARFRP1`.
- **PANTHER / InterPro Family Context (`PTHR45909`)**:
  - InterPro's profile HMM (`IPR024156` / `PTHR45909-entries.csv`) cross-matches four single-pass ER membrane signal recognition particle receptor beta subunits (`SRPRB`: human `Q9Y5M8`, mouse `P47758`, rat `Q4FZX7`, *Dictyostelium* `Q54XX1`; classified in PANTHER 19.0 `PTHR11485:SF34` at `PTN002608117`) and *Dictyostelium* `arrL` (`Q54YV7`, `PTHR11711:SF222`). True `PTHR45909:SF1` (`PTN000196303`) is a cohesive clade of soluble/peripheral trans-Golgi network (TGN) GTPases.

## 2. Molecular Mechanism & Subcellular Targeting
- **Intrinsic GTP Binding and Hydrolysis**:
  - Conserves P-loop Walker A `24-GLDNAGKT-31` (`Lys30`, `Thr31`; `T31N` is GDP/exchange-defective dominant-inactive) and Switch II `75-DLGGQ-79` (`Gln79`; `Q79L` is GTPase-deficient constitutively active), plus G4 `134-NKQD-137`.
  - Purified recombinant ARP binds GTPgammaS saturably and hydrolyzes GTP intrinsically without added phospholipids or extracts [PMID:8530503, "Recombinant ARP exhibited specific and saturable GTP gamma S (guanosine 5'-3-O-(thio)triphosphate) binding and, unlike ARF isotypes, GTPase activity in the absence of tissue extracts or phospholipids."].
- **NatC N-terminal Acetylation and SYS1 Receptor Tethering at the Cytosolic Face of the TGN**:
  - Unlike canonical myristoylated Arfs (`Gly2`), ARFRP1 begins with `1-MYTLLSGLYKYMFQ-14` (`Met1-Tyr2`), which is co-translationally N-alpha-acetylated on `Met1` (`FT MOD_RES 1 N-acetylmethionine`, `PMID:22223895`) by the heterotrimeric NatC complex (`NAA30`/`hNaa30`, `NAA35`, `NAA38`) [PMID:28356483, PMID:37891180].
  - The N-terminally acetylated amphipathic helix binds directly to the multipass Golgi integral membrane receptor `SYS1` (`hSys1`) independently of GTP binding; `Y2A` blocks co-immunoprecipitation with SYS1 [PMID:15077113, PMID:32972971, "Consistently, Arfrp1 coimmunoprecipitated with HA-tagged Sys1 and a Y2A mutation on Arfrp1 blocked the interaction (Fig."], `Y2P` shifts ARFRP1-GFP to the cytoplasm [PMID:28356483], and `SYS1` knockout releases/destabilizes endogenous ARFRP1 from the TGN [PMID:31575603].
  - Per `projects/ORGANELLES.md` Rule 7, because ARFRP1 is a soluble cytosolically synthesized protein (lacking any signal peptide or transmembrane segment) shown at the TGN and stably tethered to its cytosolic face by integral partner `SYS1`, we add `GO:0160281` (`cytoplasmic side of trans-Golgi network membrane`) on `IC` (`PMID:31575603`, with `GO:0032588`) while retaining `GO:0005794` (`Golgi apparatus`), `GO:0005802` (`trans-Golgi network`), and `GO:0032588` (`trans-Golgi network membrane`), and keeping `GO:0005829` (`cytosol`) as `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` for the soluble/unrecruited pool.
- **Bifurcated TGN Tethering Cascade & Cargo Sorting**:
  - **Retrograde endosome-to-TGN tethering (`GO:0042147`, `GO:0034067`)**: GTP-bound ARFRP1 functions upstream of both `ARL1` (which recruits GRIP-domain golgins `GOLGA1`/golgin-97, `GOLGA4`/golgin-245, `GCC1`/GCC88, `GCC2`/GCC185 for carrier capture) and `ARL5` (which recruits the multisubunit `GARP` complex `VPS51`-`VPS54` for SNARE assembly), essential for retrograde delivery of TGN38/TGN46, CI-MPR, and Shiga toxin [PMID:16129887, PMID:17127620, PMID:31575603].
  - **Anterograde TGN export (`GO:0043001`, `GO:0006886`)**: ARFRP1 promotes TGN-to-plasma-membrane export of VSV-G [PMID:16129887, PMID:19224922], E-cadherin (`CDH1`) [PMID:18662990], planar-cell-polarity cargo `VANGL2` via clathrin adaptor `AP-1` [PMID:23326640, PMID:29666182], `GLUT2` and `IGF1` in hepatocytes [PMID:22927645], `SNAP25` via `GOPC` in pancreatic beta cells [PMID:33359402], and TGN-to-endosomal/storage sorting of `GLUT4` in adipocytes [PMID:20230794], as well as Golgi lipidation/maturation of chylomicrons and VLDL via an `ARL1`-Golgin-245-`RAB2` pathway [PMID:22505585, PMID:24186947]. Viruses also exploit ARFRP1 pathways: HCV NS5A recruits ARFRP1/SNAP23 for lipid-droplet growth [PMID:27550144] and SARS-CoV-2 Envelope uses an `ARFRP1`/`AP-1` pathway for Golgi-to-lysosome delivery [PMID:38569033].
