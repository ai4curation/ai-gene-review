CC   -!- FUNCTION: Electroneutral divalent metal cation:bicarbonate symporter of
CC       the plasma membrane mediating the cellular uptake of zinc and
CC       manganese, two divalent metal cations important for development, tissue
CC       homeostasis and immunity (PubMed:12504855, PubMed:22898811,
CC       PubMed:23403290, PubMed:26637978, PubMed:29337306, PubMed:29453449).
CC       Transports an electroneutral complex composed of a divalent metal
CC       cation and two bicarbonate anions or alternatively a bicarbonate and a
CC       selenite anion (PubMed:27166256, PubMed:31699897). Thereby, it also
CC       contributes to the cellular uptake of selenium, an essential trace
CC       metal and micronutrient (PubMed:27166256). Also imports cadmium a non-
CC       essential metal which is cytotoxic and carcinogenic (PubMed:27466201).
CC       May also transport iron and cobalt through membranes (PubMed:22898811).
CC       Through zinc import, indirectly regulates the metal-dependent
CC       transcription factor MTF1 and the expression of some metalloproteases
CC       involved in cartilage catabolism and also probably heart development
CC       (PubMed:29337306). Also indirectly regulates the expression of proteins
CC       involved in cell morphology and cytoskeleton organization
CC       (PubMed:29927450). Indirectly controls innate immune function and
CC       inflammatory response by regulating zinc cellular uptake which in turn
CC       modulates the expression of genes specific of these processes
CC       (PubMed:23403290, PubMed:28056086). Protects, for instance, cells from
CC       injury and death at the onset of inflammation (PubMed:18390834). By
CC       regulating zinc influx into monocytes also directly modulates their
CC       adhesion to endothelial cells and arteries (By similarity). Reclaims
CC       manganese from the bile at the apical membrane of hepatocytes, thereby
CC       regulating the activity of the manganese-dependent enzymes through the
CC       systemic levels of the nutrient (PubMed:28481222). Also participates in
CC       manganese reabsorption in the proximal tubule of the kidney
CC       (PubMed:26637978). By mediating the extracellular uptake of manganese
CC       by cells of the blood-brain barrier, may also play a role in the
CC       transport of the micronutrient to the brain (PubMed:26637978,
CC       PubMed:31699897). With manganese cellular uptake also participates in
CC       mitochondrial proper function (PubMed:29453449). Finally, also probably
CC       functions intracellularly, translocating zinc from lysosome to cytosol
CC       to indirectly enhance the expression of specific genes during TCR-
CC       mediated T cell activation (PubMed:19401385).
