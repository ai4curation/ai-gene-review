Gene Description

COL6A2 encodes the alpha2 chain of collagen VI, a secreted extracellular matrix protein with a short collagenous region flanked by von Willebrand factor A domains. In canonical collagen VI, alpha2 associates with alpha1 and alpha3 chains; intracellular dimers and tetramers give rise to extracellular beaded microfibrils that support tissue architecture. The major 2C2 product and two shorter C-terminal splice products share their N-terminal and collagenous regions, but the assembly properties of the shorter products remain incompletely established. Collagen VI occurs around cells and in connective-tissue matrices, including muscle-associated matrix. Pathogenic COL6A2 variants cause collagen VI-related muscular dystrophies, including Bethlem and Ullrich phenotypes.
