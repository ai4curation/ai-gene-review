Gene Description

DNMT3B is a nuclear DNA cytosine-5 methyltransferase that helps establish DNA methylation patterns and regulate gene expression. Catalytically competent forms prefer CpG sites but also methylate non-CpG cytosines under tested conditions. Association with DNMT3L, other DNA methyltransferases and chromatin regulators influences activity and targeting. A distinct, catalytically inactive splice product, DNMT3B3, binds the nucleosome acidic patch and positions DNMT3A-containing complexes on chromatin. DNMT3B-dependent methylation contributes to repeat stability and transcriptional silencing; pathogenic variants cause a subset of immunodeficiency, centromeric instability and facial anomalies syndrome. Context-specific positive effects on gene expression are also reported.
