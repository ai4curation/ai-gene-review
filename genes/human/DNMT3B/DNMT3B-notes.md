The existing broad gene-expression-regulation annotation is refined to include [GO:0045814, negative regulation of gene expression, epigenetic](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0045814), alongside the already supported RNA polymerase II repression and positive BAG1 contexts. DNMT3B performs the methyl-transfer step rather than merely being required for an unrelated outcome. [PMID:22133874](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3248285/) connects mouse Dnmt3b-dependent promoter methylation to Ment repression in MYC-driven lymphoma; inducible deletion supports the relationship, while the human lymphoma arm is correlative. Its Results explicitly leave physical occupancy of individual promoters unresolved. This supports the broad epigenetic mechanism without asserting a purified human promoter assay.
