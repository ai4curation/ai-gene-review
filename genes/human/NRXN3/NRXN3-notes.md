### Identity and scope
- Q9Y4C0 is the canonical neurexin-3-alpha entry (1643 aa; signal peptide 1-27, six laminin G-like/LNS domains with three EGF repeats, TM helix 1569-1589, 54-aa cytoplasmic tail ending ...EYYV, the class II PDZ-binding motif shared with NRXN1/2). Beta isoforms from the internal promoter are a separate UniProt entry, Q9HDB5 (GOA rows in `NRXN3-bioinformatics/NRXN3-beta-isoform-entry-goa.tsv`; reference only, not reviewed here).
- UniProt function text is by similarity to mouse Nrxn1 [file:human/NRXN3/NRXN3-uniprot.txt "Neuronal cell surface protein that may be involved in cell"] (continuing "recognition and cell adhesion"); location [file:human/NRXN3/NRXN3-uniprot.txt "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Presynaptic cell membrane"]. Interactions with APBA1 (Mint1), GRIP2, LNX1 and PATJ (all PDZ proteins) come from the fragmentomics PBM screen (PMID:36115835).
- Gene structure: [PMID:12036300 "the neurexin-3 gene occupying almost 2% of human chromosome 14"]. SS5 produces membrane-anchored vs soluble NRXN3 [PMID:17804423 "Including or excluding exon 23 at SS#5 produces soluble or transmembrane NRXN3 isoforms."]. A GPI-anchored NRXN3 proteoform (AS5, exon 24) exists in interneurons [PMID:35550065 "We find that exon 24 containing (AS5+) NRXN3 proteoforms are specifically expressed in GABAergic interneurons, are GPI-anchored, and are polarized to the presynaptic compartment."]
- Non-neuronal expression: heart-specific splicing [PMID:12379233 "We reported here on the expression of NRXN3 in additional tissues (lung, pancreas, heart, placenta, liver, and kidney) and on the identification and characterization of heart-specific splicing variants of NRXN3."]; vascular neurexins (family-level, beta-neurexin antibody) [PMID:19926856].

### Core biology (with provenance)
- Trans-synaptic adhesion, neurexin-3 specific:
  - Neurexin-3-beta forms CL1 (latrophilin-1) adhesion complexes [PMID:22262843 "CL1-binding to neurexin-2β and -3β also mediates intercellular adhesion."]
  - Neurexin-3-alpha binds NLGN1 and LRRTM2 in trans (HEK aggregation assays) [PMID:31578233 "To test whether A687T alters interactions with NL1–3 and LRRTM2 in trans , we used a HEK cell aggregation assay."] [PMID:31578233 "Under normal circumstances, Nrxn3α WT SS4− interacts with NL1 and LRRTM2"]; A687 modulates NLGN1/3 binding [PMID:39231636 "selectively increased binding to neuroligin-1 and neuroligin-3 without changing binding to neuroligin-2 or LRRTM2"]
  - Dystroglycan via LNS2 [PMID:11470830 "The second LNS domain of all three α-neurexins (neurexin 1α, 2α, and 3α) formed tight Ca2+-dependent complexes with dystroglycan, suggesting that all α-neurexins bind to dystroglycan"]
  - Nanoscale organization [PMID:37543682 "we identify neurexin-3 as a critical presynaptic adhesion molecule that regulates excitatory synapse nano-organization in hippocampus"]
- Trans-synaptic control of postsynaptic AMPA receptors (the best-defined NRXN3-specific function):
  - [PMID:23827676 "constitutive inclusion of SS4 in presynaptic neurexin-3 decreased postsynaptic AMPA, but not NMDA receptor levels, and enhanced postsynaptic AMPA receptor endocytosis"]
  - [PMID:26030848 "extracellular sequences of presynaptic neurexin-3 mediated trans-synaptic regulation of postsynaptic AMPA receptors"]; this is independent of the neurexin tail and of synapse number [PMID:26030848 "suggesting that neurexin-mediated trans-synaptic interactions stabilize postsynaptic AMPARs in cultured hippocampal neurons independent of neurexin-mediated intracellular signaling"]
  - Non-overlapping with NRXN1 (NMDAR) and NRXN2 (no effect) [PMID:31005376 "In contrast, alternative splicing of neurexin-3 at SS4 suppressed AMPA-receptor-mediated, but not NMDA-receptor-mediated, synaptic responses"]
  - Mechanism: Nrxn3SS4+–Cbln2–GluD1 [PMID:34135511 "neurexin-1-cerebellin-2 and neurexin-3-cerebellin-2 complexes differentially regulate NMDA"]; circuit dependence [PMID:36205393 "In the cerebellum, conversely, Nrxn3SS4+-Cbln1 signaling regulated AMPA-receptors"]
  - Mossy fibre: C1ql2 acts via a specific Nrxn3 variant [PMID:38358390 "we demonstrate C1ql2 to exert its functions through direct interaction with a specific splice variant of neurexin-3, Nrxn3(25b+)"]
- Presynaptic release, region/synapse specific:
  - Olfactory bulb, needs intracellular sequences [PMID:26030848 "In cultured neurons and acute slices of the olfactory bulb, however, intracellular sequences of presynaptic neurexin-3 were selectively required for GABA release."]
  - Inhibitory release probability via dystroglycan loop (OB, PFC) [PMID:36997523 "Neurexin-3 enables a normal release probability at inhibitory synapses via a trans-synaptic feedback signaling loop consisting of presynaptic Neurexin-3 and postsynaptic dystroglycan."]
  - Dendrite-targeting interneuron synapses [PMID:35550065 "Nrxn3 AS5 isoform deletion results in a selective impairment of dendrite-targeting interneuron synapses in the dentate gyrus without affecting somatic inhibition or glutamatergic perforant-path synapses."]
  - Sex- and synapse-dependent effects in ventral subiculum [PMID:34879268 "Nrxn3 knockout impairs synapse density, postsynaptic strength, and inhibitory postsynaptic current (IPSC) amplitude at PV-RS synapses in males, but enhances presynaptic release and IPSC amplitude in females"]
  - Human A687T missense (profound ID + epilepsy patient) is a presynaptic gain of function [PMID:31578233 "neurexin-3αA687T unexpectedly produced the first neurexin presynaptic gain-of-function phenotype"]
  - Pan-neurexin (not NRXN3-specific) presynaptic organizer roles: Ca2+ channel coupling [PMID:32134527] and GABA-B receptor signalling [PMID:33888718 "deletion of neurexins rendered neurotransmitter release significantly less sensitive to GABAB-receptor activation"]. These are triple-neurexin deletions; they do not isolate NRXN3 and are not used for NRXN3-specific claims.
- Ribbon synapses: [PMID:39254120 "In both mouse and zebrafish models, the loss of Nrxn3 results in significantly fewer intact ribbon synapses."]
- Knockout: constitutive Nrxn3 alpha/beta KO is largely perinatal lethal [PMID:26030848 "we found that most homozygous Nrxn3α/β KO mice died at birth"]; synapse density normal in hippocampal cultures [PMID:26030848 "consistent with a normal synapse density"].

### Behaviour and human genetics (weighed critically)
- Mouse: Nrxn3 R498W (129S1 strain) enhances observational fear; SST+ ACC deletion reproduces it [PMID:29681532 "Selective deletion of Nrxn3 in somatostatin-expressing (SST+) interneurons in the anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) markedly increased observational fear and impaired inhibitory synaptic transmission from SST+ neurons."] (abstract only). OB deletion impairs olfaction (Aoto 2015).
- ASD CNVs (source of the BHF-UCL IGI rows): four ASD probands with NRXN3 deletions, several inherited from carrier parents without ASD [PMID:22209245 "NRXN3 deletions were found in one father with subclinical autism and in a carrier mother and father without formal ASD diagnoses, indicating issues of penetrance and expressivity at this locus."] The IGI "with" Q9HDB5 reflects deletions removing both alpha and beta exons [PMID:22209245 "three of the four deletions in the ASD cases affect both the alpha and beta isoforms of NRXN3"]. One proband was "gifted" [PMID:22209245 "At age 9, he was identified as “gifted” after being assessed for learning disabilities."]; language findings are a clinical diagnosis of "speech language delays" in one proband who later had near-normal speech.
- Addiction: SNP association with alcohol dependence near SS5 [PMID:17804423 "A splicing site SNP, rs8019381, that is located 23 bp from the SS#5 exon 23 donor site displays association with P = 0.0007 (odds ratio = 2.46)."]; suggestive linkage of opioid dependence to 14q that is ancestry-specific and spans a large region [PMID:17409192 "The 14q peak encompasses the NRXN3 gene (neurexin 3), which was previously identified as a potential candidate gene for addiction."]. Numerous smaller candidate-SNP studies (smoking, impulsivity, borderline traits) exist. Assessment: modest-sized candidate-gene associations and a linkage peak, not genome-wide significant, not consistently replicated, and without a variant-to-function mechanism; they support no GO process annotation (e.g. no "response to drug" or "behavioral response to ethanol" term is warranted).
- Waist circumference GWAS [PMID:19557197 "identified one novel locus associated with WC in the neurexin 3 gene [NRXN3 (rs10146997, p = 6.4x10(-7))]"] — genome-wide significant only in the combined analysis; mechanism unknown; not GO-annotatable.
- Autoimmunity: neurexin-3-alpha autoantibody encephalitis [PMID:27170573 "Neurexin-3α autoantibodies associate with a severe but potentially treatable encephalitis in which the antibodies cause a decrease of neurexin-3α and alter synapse development."]

### Divergences from NRXN1 / NRXN2
- Signal transduction replacement: NRXN1/NRXN2 used GO:0099545; for NRXN3 the child GO:0099557 (…modulating synaptic transmission) is used, because the NRXN3-specific trans-synaptic signal is a modulation of postsynaptic AMPA receptor levels without changes in synapse number (Aoto 2013/2015, Dai 2019/2021), and mouse Nrxn3 already has GO:0099557 by IDA (PMID:34135511, UniProt). GO:0099557 is_a GO:0099545, so it remains consistent with the module.
- Release role: like NRXN2 (and unlike NRXN1's core neurotransmitter secretion), NRXN3 gets NEW GO:0099171; its effect is synapse- and region-specific (required for GABA release in OB, inhibitory release probability in OB/PFC; sex-dependent opposite effects in vSUB). NRXN3 has no neurotransmitter secretion row in GOA.
- PDZ: as for NRXN1, protein binding -> PDZ domain binding, used as the MF of the release core function (intracellular sequences are required for OB GABA release; Aoto 2015). NRXN2 had no such rows.
- Learning IGI: kept non-core like NRXN1, but for a different reason - NRXN1 has biallelic-loss severe ID, whereas the NRXN3 CNV paper reports no learning phenotype; the row is retained only because the term is supported for the gene by mouse data and the IBA (validator flags inconsistent actions per term).
- Social/adult behaviour IGI: kept non-core as for NRXN1/2; penetrance is incomplete (carrier parents without ASD).
