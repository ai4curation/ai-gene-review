CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic alpha subunit of the rod-specific cGMP
CC       phosphodiesterase (PDE6) complex, which hydrolyzes 3',5'-cyclic GMP in
CC       the phototransduction cascade. The PDE6 holoenzyme consists of two
CC       catalytic subunits (PDE6A and PDE6B) and two inhibitory gamma subunits
CC       (PDE6G) (PubMed:20940301). Light-activated GNAT1 relieves gamma
CC       subunit-mediated inhibition, enabling the catalytic subunits to
CC       hydrolyze cGMP and thereby mediate visual signal transduction and
CC       amplification. Decreased cytosolic cGMP levels result in closure of
CC       cGMP-gated cation channels at the plasma membrane, leading to rod
CC       photoreceptor hyperpolarization (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P11541, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20940301}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Tetramer composed of two catalytic chains (alpha and beta) and
CC       two inhibitory chains (gamma). Interacts with GNAT1; two GNAT1-GTP
CC       molecules bind both the catalytic core (PDE6A and PDE6B) and the
CC       inhibitory PDE6G subunits, inducing conformational rearrangements that
CC       relieve inhibition and activate catalysis.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P11541}.

CC   -!- DISEASE: Retinitis pigmentosa 43 (RP43) [MIM:613810]: A retinal
CC       dystrophy belonging to the group of pigmentary retinopathies. Retinitis
CC       pigmentosa is characterized by retinal pigment deposits visible on
CC       fundus examination and primary loss of rod photoreceptor cells followed
CC       by secondary loss of cone photoreceptors. Patients typically have night
CC       vision blindness and loss of midperipheral visual field. As their
CC       condition progresses, they lose their far peripheral visual field and
CC       eventually central vision as well. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10393062,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7493036}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
CC       affecting the gene represented in this entry.
