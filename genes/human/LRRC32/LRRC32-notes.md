# LRRC32 (GARP) notes

Deep research (falcon / perplexity-lite) failed; notes from cached publications and UniProt Q14392.

- Single-pass type I TM protein, LRR ectodomain; on activated Tregs and platelets.
- Tethers latent TGF-beta to surface [PMID:19651619 "GARP or LRRC32, a leucine-rich repeat molecule of unknown function, is critical for tethering TGF-beta to the cell surface."].
- Binds latent TGF-beta (LAP + mature) on Treg surface [PMID:19750484].
- Disulfide links Cys192/Cys331 to TGFB1 prodomain; outcompetes LTBP1; prevents secretion; integrin aVb6/b8 activate TGFb from the complex [PMID:22278742].
- Crystal structure GARP:latent TGF-beta1, presentation to aVb8 [PMID:30361387].
- LAPTM4B regulates GARP-TGFB1 production [PMID:26126825].

Localization conclusion: genuine cell-surface / plasma membrane single-pass type I protein, LRR ectodomain
exposed extracellularly (binds latent TGF-beta). Nucleoplasm HPA row removed (topologically inconsistent).
GARP is a docking/presentation molecule, not a signal-transducing receptor (signaling receptor activity IBA over-annotated).
