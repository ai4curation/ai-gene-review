# EFL1 source and sequence evidence

The replay resolves human Q7Z2Z2 to PANTHER PTHR42908 leaf PTN002534018 in the captured version 19 tree (1,107 nodes). Its 7,508-column aligned sequence yields exactly the 1,120-residue canonical protein after gap removal. All five imported IBA assertions descend from their literal IBD nodes: PTN000562745 for GTPase, cytosol and ribonucleoprotein-complex assertions, and PTN000562912 for cytosolic ribosome assembly and ribosome binding. No same-term negative assertion occurs on the target path. Separate elongation-function loss records are retained without transferring them to the reviewed terms.

Thirteen literal donor identifiers are joined to current records through explicit cross-references. Current record matches and direct experimental records are separate output fields. This reconstructs the phylogenetic evidence; donor count and self-donation are not negative evidence. ARBA00026818 and ARBA00085698 each have a sufficient condition set matched by official target FunFam/InterPro/taxonomic fields. InterPro IPR000795 and IPR005225 include the nucleotide-binding region.

The current IntAct export contains two exact EFL1–SBDS records: [EBI-21463457](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/details/interaction/EBI-21463457), PMID:30545121, is fluorescence polarization (MI:0053), direct interaction, with human factors in vitro; [EBI-56912423](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/details/interaction/EBI-56912423), PMID:39251607, is proximity labeling (MI:1314), proximity (MI:2364), represented by a spoke expansion (MI:1060), in human K562 cells. Participant roles and construct features are retained on their literal sides. The missing 2015 record in this current export is not a contradiction: the original PMID:25991726 directly measures the human pair by anisotropy.

Human EFL1/SBDS anisotropy and yeast kinetic or SAXS experiments are distinguished. The 2024 SBDS-bait proximity hit does not establish a direct interface by itself, nor a separate post-transcriptional regulatory activity for EFL1. No target-specific quantitative screen supplement was recovered.

Official FASTAs reconstruct products Q7Z2Z2-1 (1,120 residues) and Q7Z2Z2-2 (1,069 residues), the latter deleting canonical positions 32–82. This proves sequence identity, not activity equivalence. The historical unversioned NP_078856 reference does not authenticate the actual expressed insert as either current product.

GO:0043023 is a current child of ribosome binding (GO:0043022) and fits the measured 60S interaction. A narrower assembly term describing aggregation of components was not forced onto late eIF6 release. SBDS exchange-factor activity does not make SBDS a catalytic enzyme for enzyme-binding classification, and GTP-dependent protein binding would require a nucleotide-state dependence not established by the pair assays. The specific GTPase/assembly mechanism and the positive generic SBDS association therefore remain distinct levels of description.
