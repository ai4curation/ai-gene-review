# EFL1 evidence notes

## 2026-10-18: cytosolic ribosome maturation

EFL1 is a GTPase that acts with SBDS during late 60S maturation. Human-protein biochemistry measures GTP hydrolysis and SBDS-dependent eIF6 release; removing this anti-association factor permits subunit joining. Ribosome binding, nucleotide binding and hydrolysis belong to this integrated mechanism. They do not establish routine translation elongation or a separate general role in cellular GTP homeostasis. PMID:21536732 and PMID:26479198 support the core mechanism.

PMID:21536732 uses human EFL1 produced in yeast and human SBDS with mouse pre-60S particles or rabbit reticulocyte 60S particles. The mouse genetic perturbation is Sbds loss. Catalytic and omission controls support EFL1-dependent hydrolysis/release, while SBDS K151N can uncouple hydrolysis from eIF6 displacement. PMID:26479198 uses human factors on Dictyostelium particles; the local EFL1 map supports domain-level fitting, not atomic contacts. Static GMPPCP structures do not establish the exact timing of hydrolysis.
