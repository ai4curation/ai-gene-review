Gene Description

TBX20 is a T-box family sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factor expressed in cardiac progenitors, myocardium and endocardium (including endocardial cushions), and in hindbrain branchiomotor/visceromotor neurons. Through its T-box domain it binds brachyury-like half sites and acts as a context-dependent activator or repressor of RNA polymerase II target genes; the long isoform carries C-terminal transactivation and transrepression domains. TBX20 physically cooperates with NKX2-5, GATA4/5, TBX5 and ISL1 to activate cardiac enhancers (e.g. NPPA, GJA5, MEF2C, NKX2-5), and represses TBX2 in prospective chamber myocardium, partly by binding SMAD1/5 and blocking BMP/SMAD-dependent activation. In mouse it is required for heart tube elongation, chamber formation, atrioventricular canal and valve development and cardiomyocyte proliferation, and continues to maintain ion channel and contractile gene programs in adult cardiomyocytes. Human TBX20 variants cause atrial septal defect 4 and are associated with valve defects, tetralogy of Fallot and dilated cardiomyopathy.
