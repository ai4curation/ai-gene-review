CC   -!- FUNCTION: Controls V(D)J recombination during T-cell development by
CC       repressing T-cell receptor (TCR) beta enhancer function (By
CC       similarity). Binds to scaffold/matrix attachment region beta
CC       (S/MARbeta), an ATC-rich DNA sequence located upstream of the TCR beta
CC       enhancer (By similarity). Represses cyclin D1 transcription by
CC       recruiting HDAC1 to its promoter, thereby diminishing H3K9ac, H3S10ph
CC       and H4K8ac levels (PubMed:16166625). Promotes TP53 activation, which
CC       causes cell cycle arrest (By similarity). Plays a role in the
CC       regulation of alternative splicing (PubMed:26080397). Binds to CD44
CC       pre-mRNA and negatively regulates the inclusion of CD44 proximal
CC       variable exons v2-v6 but has no effect on distal variable exons v7-v10
CC       (PubMed:26080397). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8VBU8,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16166625, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26080397}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of a corepressor complex containing BANP, HDAC1, SIN3A,
CC       SIN3B, RBL1 and RBL2 (PubMed:16166625). Forms a trimeric complex in the
CC       nucleus consisting of BANP, HDAC6 and KHDRBS1/SAM68; HDAC6 keeps
CC       KHDRBS1 in a deacetylated state which inhibits the inclusion of CD44
CC       alternate exons (PubMed:26080397). The complex is disrupted by
CC       MAPK1/MAPK3-mediated phosphorylation of BANP which results in BANP
CC       export to the cytoplasm (PubMed:26080397). This facilitates acetylation
CC       of KHDRBS1 and CD44 variant exon inclusion (PubMed:26080397). Interacts
CC       with TP53 (By similarity). Interacts with CUX1/CDP (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with HDAC1 (PubMed:16166625). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8VBU8,
