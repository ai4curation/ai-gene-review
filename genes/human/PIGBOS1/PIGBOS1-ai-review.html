Gene Description

PIGBOS1 (PIGB opposite strand 1, PIGBOS) is a 54-amino-acid microprotein encoded by a small open reading frame on the strand opposite the GPI-anchor biosynthesis gene PIGB. It is widely expressed and has no paralogs. PIGBOS1 is a signal-anchored, single-pass protein of the mitochondrial outer membrane: a short N-terminal segment faces the intermembrane space, a single transmembrane helix anchors it, and a C-terminal region of about 25 residues faces the cytosol. Through this cytosolic region (residues 30-36 are critical) it binds CLCC1, an endoplasmic reticulum chloride channel, and the interaction is concentrated where the ER lies close to mitochondria. PIGBOS1 does not tether the two organelles; loss of the protein leaves ER-mitochondria contacts unchanged. Its best-supported role is to dampen the ER unfolded protein response: cells lacking PIGBOS1 respond to ER stressors with stronger XBP1 splicing, ATF6 activity and UPR target-gene induction and undergo more ER stress-induced apoptosis, overexpression has the opposite effect, and rescue depends on the CLCC1-binding region. PIGBOS1 self-associates. A more recent study reports that the PIGBOS1-CLCC1 interaction also promotes ER calcium release, ER-to-mitochondria calcium transfer, store-operated calcium entry and mitochondrial respiration.

Core Functions

PIGBOS1 is a mitochondrial outer membrane microprotein whose cytosolic C-terminal region binds the ER chloride channel CLCC1 near ER-mitochondria contacts. Through this interaction it dampens the ER unfolded protein response: loss of PIGBOS1 heightens signalling through all three UPR branches and increases ER stress-induced apoptosis, overexpression lowers it, and only CLCC1-binding-competent PIGBOS1 rescues. It does not act as an ER-mitochondria tether.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is PIGBOS1 a regulator of CLCC1 channel activity? CLCC1 has chloride channel activity and a CLCC1-binding-dependent effect of PIGBOS1 on ER calcium release, ER-to-mitochondria calcium transfer and store-operated calcium entry has been reported (PMID:42351194), but whether PIGBOS1 changes CLCC1 conductance directly has not been measured. A positive answer would justify a channel regulator activity term in place of transporter binding.

Q: Should PIGBOS1 be annotated to GO:0044233 mitochondria-associated ER membrane contact site? The PIGBOS1-CLCC1 split-GFP signal lies between ER and outer membrane markers, and GOA gives CLCC1 this term from the same paper, but PIGBOS1 is not a tether and only a fraction of it is bound to CLCC1; outer-membrane contact-site partners such as RMDN3 and MFN2 do not carry the term either.

Q: How does an outer membrane signal-anchor protein with about four intermembrane-space residues copurify HIGD2A and complex III/IV subunits in digitonin-solubilized mitochondria (PMID:34800366)? Is this a post-lysis association or evidence of a second pool?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Electrophysiology of reconstituted CLCC1 (planar bilayer or ER-targeted patch) with and without PIGBOS1 or its 30-36 alanine mutant, to test whether PIGBOS1 directly modulates CLCC1 conductance.

Experiment: Endogenous-level proximity labelling or super-resolution imaging of PIGBOS1 to measure what fraction resides at ER-mitochondria contacts, and whether the fraction changes under ER stress.

Identity

  • 54-aa microprotein encoded by a smORF in exon 2 of a transcript on the opposite strand of PIGB
    (chr15). UniProt: single TM helix (aa 5-25), N-terminus (1-4) in IMS, C-terminus (26-54)
    cytoplasmic; region 30-36 required for CLCC1 binding. Pfam PF23670 / InterPro IPR057394
    (PIGBOS1 family); PAN-GO has 0 IBA annotations. No paralogs.
  • PMID:31653868
  • Translation shown by a tryptic peptide and antibodies:
    PMID:31653868
  • NB: the Chu et al. paper is Nat Commun 2019 (PMID:31653868), not Cell Chem Biol as in the task brief.
    PMID verified by E-utilities and cached full text (PMC6814811).

Localization

  • MOM by fractionation, IF colocalization with Tom20, proteinase K protection assay, split-GFP:
    PMID:31653868
  • Signal-anchor topology:
    PMID:31653868
  • MitoCoP high-confidence mitochondrial proteome (HTP) includes PIGBOS1:
    PMID:34800366
  • ER-mitochondria contacts: PIGBOS-CLCC1 split-GFP signal peaks between the ER and MOM markers, but PIGBOS
    is not a tether (no ERMICC change in KO; overexpression does not remodel contacts).
    PMID:31653868
  • GOA gives CLCC1 (Q96S66) GO:0044233 (MAM contact site, IDA, PMID:31653868) but gives PIGBOS only MOM.
    Comparator check: MOM-side contact-site proteins RMDN3/PTPIP51 (Q96TC7), MFN2 (O95140) and VAPA
    (Q9P0L0) carry no direct GO:0044233 annotation in QuickGO. So I do NOT propose GO:0044233 as NEW;
    the curator's choice (CLCC1 = MAM fraction; PIGBOS = MOM) is consistent with the data.

Interaction with CLCC1

  • IP-MS + SAINT/CRAPome, APEX proximity labelling, reciprocal co-IP, split-GFP, flow cytometry.
  • Region 30-36 critical:
    PMID:31653868
  • Interaction not regulated by ER stress (IP and split-GFP after TM/TG).
  • CLCC1 has GO:0005254 chloride channel activity (IDA PMID:37142673, plus IBA) in GOA, so it qualifies
    as a transmembrane transporter; GO:0044325 transmembrane transporter binding is the informative
    replacement for the bare protein binding row.
  • Homo-oligomerization (human-human and human-rat co-IP); TMDOCK predicts TM homodimer. Not proposed
    as an MF term (binding term of little value).

UPR phenotype (the one BP in GOA)

  • Loss (siRNA KD, CRISPR KO) -> heightened XBP1 splicing, ATF6 reporter, all UPR target genes;
    rescue by siRNA-resistant PIGBOS; overexpression dampens UPR. Directional in both senses, so the
    annotation can be sharpened to negative regulation (GO:1900102).
    PMID:31653868
    PMID:31653868
  • Requires CLCC1 binding: C-terminal truncation and 30-32 AAA mutant fail to rescue:
    PMID:31653868
  • Specific to ER UPR (no effect on mitoUPR with CDDO); increased ER-stress apoptosis (caspase-3,
    PARP cleavage) in KD/KO cells, but staurosporine-induced death unchanged. The apoptosis effect is
    treated as a downstream consequence of heightened UPR; no NEW apoptosis term proposed.

Newer work

  • Aditya & Bera 2026 (PMID:42351194, single lab, HEK293T): PIGBOS promotes IP3R-mediated ER Ca2+
    release, ER-to-mitochondria Ca2+ transfer and SOCE, all dependent on CLCC1-binding C-terminus;
    loss impairs respiration and ATP.
    PMID:42351194
    Mechanism is explicitly unresolved (direct vs via intermediaries), and much is expression-level
    change of Ca2+ machinery. Not proposed as NEW; raised as a suggested question.
  • MitoCoP q-AP-MS (PMID:34800366): FLAG-PIGBOS1 copurified HIGD2A and complex III/IV subunits,
    PMID:34800366
    Hard to reconcile with a MOM signal-anchor protein whose IMS-facing part is only ~4 residues;
    could reflect digitonin-solubilized overexpressed protein. Not used for annotation.

  • 41465308 (review on microproteins), 41277184 (oocyte aging omics), 40287452 (horse astaxanthin),
    31858090 (AD hypothesis), 29100423 (lncRNA biomarker melanoma) - incidental mentions; not used.
