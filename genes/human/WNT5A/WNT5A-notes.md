# WNT5A evidence notes

2026-11-26 because the local deep-research-client install exposed neither the
Falcon nor Perplexity providers. This note is the manually written literature
synthesis for the WNT5A review.

WNT5A is the major noncanonical Wnt ligand in this first PTHR12027 slice. It is
secreted to the extracellular matrix and binds Frizzled receptors, but its
principal outputs are planar-cell-polarity, calcium/JNK/PKC and ROR-dependent
pathways rather than default beta-catenin activation. UniProt explicitly notes
that WNT5A can activate or inhibit canonical Wnt signaling depending on receptor
context; Topol et al. showed ROR2-dependent WNT5A antagonism of
beta-catenin-dependent transcription, while human cell studies in GOA support
FZD4/FZD9/ROR2 receptor engagement, endothelial migration, macrophage
inflammatory signaling and synapse-organization roles [PMID:12952940;
PMID:17986384; PMID:18174455; PMID:27402827].

The PAINT root term `cytokine activity` is the wrong label for a Wnt ligand and
should be refined to receptor-ligand activity. PAINT's canonical Wnt row is
contextually defensible for WNT5A because FZD4 can route WNT5A to
beta-catenin-dependent signaling, but noncanonical Wnt signaling, PCP signaling,
FZD/ROR receptor binding and extracellular region are the better core claims for
WNT5A.

WNT5A has many process rows from perturbation studies in cancer, immune,
vascular, skeletal and neural cells. These often report pathway or phenotype
effects of adding or knocking down WNT5A; they can support non-core signaling
contexts when the ligand itself is the experimental signal, but they should not
be generalized into every downstream cytokine, apoptotic or tissue-development
process. Reactome rows that place WNT5A in clathrin-coated vesicles reflect
internalization of WNT5A-bound receptors rather than an intrinsic WNT5A
location, so those component rows should be marked as trafficking-context
over-annotations.
