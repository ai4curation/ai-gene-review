# DSTN review notes

## 2026-11-27

Reviewed DSTN as part of the PTHR11913 ADF/cofilin family with CFL1 and the
pre-existing CFL2 review.

- Core function: human actin-depolymerizing factor/destrin binds actin and
  severs/depolymerizes filaments. PMID:8399167 cloned human ADF and showed it
  bound actin and severed filaments; PMID:11812157 compared human ADF and
  cofilin directly.
- PAINT: PTN000227258 soundly propagates cytoplasm, actin cytoskeleton, actin
  filament binding and actin filament depolymerization through the whole
  ADF/cofilin family.
- Protein binding: rows naming ACTB or ACTG1 were changed to actin binding.
  Other GO:0005515 rows were removed as uninformative generic binding claims
  without asserting that the underlying high-throughput interactions are false.
- Mouse-derived cortical and synaptic IEA rows were left UNDECIDED because this
  review did not trace human evidence that resolves DSTN as a stable cortical,
  presynaptic, postsynaptic or glutamatergic-synapse component.

No production GO-CAM in `gocams/index.tsv` is centered on DSTN, and no
ADF/cofilin-specific module fit this small two-gene content PR.
