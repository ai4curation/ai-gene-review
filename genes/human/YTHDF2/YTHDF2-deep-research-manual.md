# YTHDF2 manual evidence notes

Falcon and Perplexity-lite were unavailable for this review, so the review uses the reviewed UniProt record, the cached GOA rows, cached primary publications, the YTHDF3 review, and manual synthesis.

## Core m6A-reader and decay role

The high-throughput mRNA-interactome rows from PMID:22658674 and PMID:22681889 support only generic RNA binding and are less informative than the YTH-domain m6A-reader evidence.

PMID:24284625 defines the canonical YTHDF2 activity and location: We further establish the role of YTHDF2 in RNA metabolism, showing that binding of YTHDF2 results in the localization of bound mRNA from the translatable pool to mRNA decay sites, such as processing bodies.

PMID:27558897 identifies one direct decay mechanism: We further show that YTHDF2 recruits the CCR4-NOT complex through a direct interaction between the YTHDF2 N-terminal region and the SH domain of the CNOT1 subunit, and that this recruitment is essential for the deadenylation of m(6)A-containing RNAs by CAF1 and CCR4.

PMID:30930054 identifies a second decay mechanism: HRSP12 functions as an adaptor to bridge YTHDF2 and RNase P/MRP, eliciting rapid degradation of YTHDF2-bound RNAs.

PMID:32492408 places YTHDF2 in the shared YTHDF model: Instead, the DF paralogs act redundantly to mediate mRNA degradation and cellular differentiation.

YTHDF2 is a predominantly cytoplasmic and cytosolic YTHDF paralog that binds m6A-modified mRNAs, promotes mRNA degradation by deadenylation and endoribonucleolytic routes, partitions into P-bodies and cytoplasmic stress granules, and can relocalize to the nucleus after heat shock.

## Non-core molecular and process outputs

PMID:31815440 identifies a weaker non-canonical RNA-reading activity: Our findings, along with recent structural and functional studies of YTH-domain family proteins, support that YTHDF2 is a versatile reader that can recognize m6A, m1A and m5C,4,31 though the binding affinity toward m5C is much weaker than that toward m6A.

PMID:31815440 links YTHDF2 loss to rRNA processing rather than a fully resolved pathway: Additionally, genetic ablation of YTHDF2 resulted in substantial perturbations in rRNA maturation by diminishing the formation of 30S and 12S intermediates from the 47S rRNA precursor.

PMID:32169943 records an induced-pluripotent-stem-cell neural differentiation output: Depletion of YTHDF2 in iPSCs leads to stabilization of these transcripts, loss of pluripotency, and induction of neural-specific gene expression.

PMID:26458103 describes a heat-shock nuclear translational-control role that is stress conditional and non-core for YTHDF2.

The mouse- and zebrafish-derived YTHDF2 rows for oocyte maturation, gamete generation, spermatogonial cell division, regulation of cell adhesion, negative regulation of Notch signaling, embryonic morphogenesis, hematopoietic stem-cell proliferation and differentiation, endothelial to hematopoietic transition, meiotic cell-cycle regulation in oocytes, and stem-cell differentiation are developmental outputs of m6A-dependent turnover and are retained as non-core.

PMID:10508479 identified YTHDF2 as a renal carcinoma antigen recognized by antibody; that does not mean YTHDF2 executes the humoral immune response.

## Condensate biology

PMID:31292544 connects YTHDF2 with partitioning into ribonucleoprotein compartments: The resulting mRNA-YTHDF complexes then partition into different endogenous phase-separated compartments, such as P-bodies, stress granules or neuronal RNA granules.

## Protein binding policy

The GO:0005515 IPI rows are curated physical associations and are removed under the project bare-protein-binding policy, not because the reported interactions are false.
