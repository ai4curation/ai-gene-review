# YBX2 notes

Automated deep research could not run in this workspace: Falcon and the
perplexity-lite fallback were unavailable to `deep-research-client`, and the
only installed provider (`claude_code`) reported a missing login. These notes
therefore record the manual literature pass over the GOA PMID rows and UniProt
text.

Human YBX2/Contrin is the germ-cell-enriched Y-box member. PMID:10100484 cloned
human Contrin as the homologue of mouse MSY2/Xenopus germ-cell Y-box proteins
and reported a cold shock domain, basic/aromatic islands, predicted
nuclear-localization signals, and testis-selective expression by northern and
western blotting. The paper is enough to keep nucleic-acid binding and broad
nucleus/cytoplasm localization, but the abstract mostly transfers the
transcriptional and translational-delay role from non-human homologues. The
three TAS process rows from that paper are therefore kept as non-core or
over-annotated rather than treated as direct human evidence for core functions.

PMID:28970281 is a mouse brown-adipose Ybx2 study. It supports conservation of a
post-transcriptional RNA-stability function in a mammalian paralog but does not
establish cold-induced thermogenesis as a direct human YBX2 process.

The PAINT rows place broad nucleic-acid binding and nucleus on the family.
There are no PAINT losses on the human YBX2 lineage, but the PTHR11544 family is
very broad and includes bacterial cold-shock proteins. The broad GO:0003676 rows
are kept as the right common denominator for the family; the PAINT and
InterPro2GO rows reinforce the same generic nucleic-acid-binding claim.
