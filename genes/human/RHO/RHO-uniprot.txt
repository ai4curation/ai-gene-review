CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled photoreceptor that activates the G protein
CC       transducin (t) signaling pathway in response to light and is essential
CC       for image-forming vision under low-light conditions (PubMed:6140680,
CC       PubMed:7846071, PubMed:8107847). Required for postnatal photoreceptor
CC       cell viability (PubMed:12566452, PubMed:2215617). Activation occurs
CC       when the covalently bound 11-cis-retinal chromophore absorbs a photon
CC       and isomerizes to all-trans-retinal, inducing a conformational change
CC       in the opsin that triggers G protein-mediated phototransduction
CC       (PubMed:28524165, PubMed:26200343, PubMed:28753425, PubMed:8107847).
CC       Signal termination occurs via receptor phosphorylation, which promotes
CC       binding of arrestin (SAG) and displacement of the G protein alpha
CC       subunit (PubMed:28524165, PubMed:26200343).
