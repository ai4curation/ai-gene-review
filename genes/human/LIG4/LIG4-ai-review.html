Gene Description

LIG4 encodes DNA ligase IV, the ATP-dependent DNA ligase (EC 6.5.1.1) of classical non-homologous end joining (NHEJ), the main pathway that repairs DNA double-strand breaks in vertebrate cells. Its N-terminal catalytic region (DNA-binding, adenylation and OB-fold domains) encircles nicked DNA and seals it through lysyl-AMP and DNA-adenylate intermediates, using Mg2+. Two C-terminal BRCT domains with the inter-BRCT linker bind an XRCC4 homodimer; XRCC4 stabilizes LIG4, imports it into the nucleus and stimulates its activity, and LIG4 in turn helps localize XRCC4 and XLF. XRCC4-LIG4 is recruited to Ku- and DNA-PKcs-bound DNA ends and, with XLF and PAXX, forms long- and short-range synaptic complexes in which it ligates the processed ends. The ligase is mechanistically flexible, joining incompatible ends and ligating across short gaps. LIG4 completes the joining step of V(D)J recombination and class switch recombination as well as repair of radiation-induced breaks, and ligates deprotected telomeres into chromosome fusions after loss of TRF2. Loss of Lig4 is late-embryonic lethal in mice, with neuronal apoptosis and blocked lymphocyte development; hypomorphic human mutations cause LIG4 syndrome (immunodeficiency, microcephaly, growth delay, radiosensitivity) and radiosensitive SCID.
