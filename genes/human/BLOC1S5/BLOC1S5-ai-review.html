Gene Description

BLOC1S5, also called MUTED, is a small nonenzymatic subunit of the eight-subunit BLOC-1 complex. It stabilizes the assembly that sorts membrane cargo from early endosomes to melanosomes and other lysosome-related organelles. Human MUTED restores BLOC-1 stability, pigmentation and cargo delivery in muted mouse melanocytes. BLOC-1 also supports selected neuronal cargo-trafficking routes. Biallelic BLOC1S5 defects cause Hermansky-Pudlak syndrome type 11, with hypopigmentation and platelet dense-granule deficiency.
