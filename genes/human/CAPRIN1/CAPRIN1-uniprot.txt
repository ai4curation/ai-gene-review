CC   -!- FUNCTION: mRNA-binding protein that acts as a regulator of mRNAs
CC       transport, translation and/or stability, and which is involved in
CC       neurogenesis, synaptic plasticity in neurons and cell proliferation and
CC       migration in multiple cell types (PubMed:17210633, PubMed:31439799,
CC       PubMed:35979925). Plays an essential role in cytoplasmic stress granule
CC       formation (PubMed:35977029). Acts as an mRNA regulator by mediating
CC       formation of some phase-separated membraneless compartment: undergoes
CC       liquid-liquid phase separation upon binding to target mRNAs, leading to
CC       assemble mRNAs into cytoplasmic ribonucleoprotein granules that
CC       concentrate mRNAs with associated regulatory factors (PubMed:31439799,
CC       PubMed:32302570, PubMed:32302571, PubMed:32302572, PubMed:34074792,
CC       PubMed:36040869, PubMed:36279435). Undergoes liquid-liquid phase
CC       separation following phosphorylation and interaction with FMR1,
CC       promoting formation of cytoplasmic ribonucleoprotein granules that
CC       concentrate mRNAs with factors that inhibit translation and mediate
CC       deadenylation of target mRNAs (PubMed:31439799). In these cytoplasmic
CC       ribonucleoprotein granules, CAPRIN1 mediates recruitment of CNOT7
CC       deadenylase, leading to mRNA deadenylation and degradation
CC       (PubMed:31439799). Binds directly and selectively to MYC and CCND2
CC       mRNAs (PubMed:17210633). In neuronal cells, directly binds to several
