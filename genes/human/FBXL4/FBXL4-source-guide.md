The reviewed core links receptor ubiquitination/proteasomal turnover to negative regulation of mitophagy, while retaining distinct source limits for topology, contextual nuclear staining, pathway-model compartments and pairwise interaction screens.

- [Notes journal](FBXL4-notes.md) and [database notes](FBXL4-database-notes.md): retained historical lane boundaries followed by the combined interpretation.

The primary and database guides retain the finite reading scope of each lane. The combined review applies the accepted whole-reference successors and includes the four shared primary scopes exactly once. Inherited quotations remain in their original positions; an older quotation is not silently revised to erase a subsequently identified conflict.

The single core does not imply exclusive mitochondrial localization. The older intermembrane-space experiments and modeled cytosolic Reactome placements remain uncertain, while HPA nuclear-speck staining remains contextual. The two bounded gaps concern the conflicting topology observations and context-dependent PPTC7 recruitment/mutant/dosage results. No engineered targeting construct is represented as a natural product, and no additional GO annotation or quotation is introduced.
