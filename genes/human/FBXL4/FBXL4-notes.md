## Function

- SCF-FBXL4 sits on the mitochondrial outer membrane and degrades the mitophagy
  receptors NIX and BNIP3 [PMID:37161784 "SCFFBXL4 , an SKP1/CUL1/F-box protein ubiquitin ligase complex, localises to the mitochondrial outer membrane in unstressed cells"].
- Integral, N-anchored outer-membrane protein; protease-accessible in intact
  mitochondria [PMID:36896912 "We determined that FBXL4 functions as an integral outer-membrane protein that forms an SCF-FBXL4 ubiquitin E3 ligase complex."].
- Direct substrate interaction [PMID:37102372 "FBXL4 restricts NIX and BNIP3 levels via direct interaction and protein destabilization"].
- PPTC7 bridges BNIP3/NIX to FBXL4 [PMID:38992176 "a pool of PPTC7 on the mitochondrial outer membrane acts as an adaptor linking BNIP3 and NIX to FBXL4"; PMID:40025034].
- Mouse genetics: Bnip3 or Nix loss rescues Fbxl4-null lethality [PMID:36896912 "knockout of either Bnip3 or Nix rescues metabolic derangements and viability of the Fbxl4-/- mice."].

## Localisation conflict

The original disease paper placed FBXL4 in the intermembrane space
[PMID:23993194 "localizes in the intermembrane space"]; later protease-protection and
alkaline-extraction work places it in the outer membrane (PMID:36896912, PMID:37161784).
The IMS annotation was removed on that basis.

## Disease

Biallelic loss of function causes MTDPS13 (PMID:23993193, PMID:23993194); variants
impair SCF assembly and BNIP3/NIX degradation (PMID:37568009).
