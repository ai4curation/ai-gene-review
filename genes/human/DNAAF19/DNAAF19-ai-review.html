Gene Description

DNAAF19, formerly CCDC103, is a small self-associating microtubule-binding protein that supports axonemal dynein-arm assembly and attachment in motile cilia. Conserved cytoplasmic and axonemal pools support dynein incorporation, enabling ciliary beating and normal left–right patterning. Purified human protein binds and stabilizes microtubules and forms oligomers; native human respiratory material contains apparent dimers. Its precise native polymer arrangement and dynein interface remain unresolved. Human variants cause primary ciliary dyskinesia with variable dynein-arm and laterality defects. Alternative transcripts encode a full-length 242-residue product and a predicted 98-residue product with a distinct short C terminus.

Core Functions

Binds microtubules and self-associates to support assembly and axonemal attachment of outer and inner dynein arms. Direct human recombinant microtubule binding complements human-protein rescue of both arm classes and conserved axonemal localization. Native human respiratory material contains apparent dimers, while recombinant full-length protein and defined fragments form oligomers. Native polymer geometry and the precise dynein/docking interface remain unresolved; native human arm-loss evidence is strongest for outer arms.
