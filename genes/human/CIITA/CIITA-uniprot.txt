CC   -!- FUNCTION: Essential for transcriptional activity of the HLA class II
CC       promoter; activation is via the proximal promoter (PubMed:16600381,
CC       PubMed:17493635, PubMed:7749984, PubMed:8402893, PubMed:40608405). Does
CC       not bind DNA (PubMed:16600381, PubMed:17493635, PubMed:7749984,
CC       PubMed:8402893). May act in a coactivator-like fashion through protein-
CC       protein interactions by contacting factors binding to the proximal MHC
CC       class II promoter, to elements of the transcription machinery, or both
CC       PubMed:8402893, PubMed:7749984, (PubMed:16600381, PubMed:17493635).
CC       Alternatively it may activate HLA class II transcription by modifying
CC       proteins that bind to the MHC class II promoter (PubMed:16600381,
CC       PubMed:17493635, PubMed:7749984, PubMed:8402893). Also mediates
CC       enhanced MHC class I transcription; the promoter element requirements
CC       for CIITA-mediated transcription are distinct from those of
CC       constitutive MHC class I transcription, and CIITA can functionally
CC       replace TAF1 at these genes. Activates CD74 transcription
CC       (PubMed:32855215). Exhibits intrinsic GTP-stimulated acetyltransferase
CC       activity (PubMed:11172716). Exhibits serine/threonine protein kinase
CC       activity: can phosphorylate the TFIID component TAF7, the RAP74 subunit
CC       of the general transcription factor TFIIF, histone H2B at 'Ser-37' and
CC       other histones (in vitro) (PubMed:24036077). Has antiviral activity
CC       against Ebola virus and coronaviruses, including SARS-CoV-2
CC       (PubMed:32855215). Induces resistance by up-regulation of the p41
CC       isoform of CD74, which blocks cathepsin-mediated cleavage of viral
CC       glycoproteins, thereby preventing viral fusion (PubMed:32855215).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11172716, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16600381,
