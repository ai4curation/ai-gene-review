# Human IFT52 (UniProt Q9Y366): functional annotation research report

## Identity and principal function

**Identity check.** The supplied UniProt record identifies Q9Y366 as human intraflagellar transport protein 52 homolog, encoded by **IFT52**; C20orf9, NGD5 and CGI-53 are supplied aliases. Independent studies explicitly investigate human *IFT52*, identify its product as an intraflagellar transport B (IFT-B) subunit, and describe the corresponding GIFT, central and C-terminal architecture. The reviewed literature therefore supports research on the specified protein, not a similarly named gene. The accession and aliases themselves are taken from the supplied UniProt record rather than independently established by the retrieved papers. (ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 1-2, taschner2014crystalstructuresof pages 2-3)

**Functional conclusion.** IFT52 is principally a **nonenzymatic structural organizer and coupling component of the IFT-B complex**. It helps assemble the protein scaffold of intraflagellar transport trains, couples IFT-B subassemblies to one another and to the kinesin-II transport machinery, and thereby supports cilium formation and transport toward the ciliary tip. It is not itself the motor, a transporter with a defined small-molecule substrate, or an enzyme with an established catalytic reaction. Specific cargo associations demonstrated in IFT52-perturbed cells are discussed below; the evidence does not assign IFT52 an independently established, exclusive cargo specificity. (ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 3-5, lacey2023themolecularstructure pages 4-5, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 1-2, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 7-10)

## Molecular mechanism and domains

IFT-B supplies the backbone of polymeric trains moving along ciliary microtubules. Its **IFT-B1 core** and **IFT-B2 peripheral** modules meet at a composite interface containing IFT52 and IFT88 on the B1 side and IFT38 and IFT57 on the B2 side. Human-cell interaction assays identify this four-protein assembly as a binding site for heterotrimeric kinesin-II (KIF3A–KIF3B–KAP3). The IFT52–IFT88 pair also contributes to the interface with IFT-A proteins IFT122–IFT144; these are interactions within a larger transport apparatus, not evidence that IFT52 alone drives retrograde movement. (ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 2-3, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 3-5, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 1-2)

The domain assignments supplied for Q9Y366—IFT52/GIFT, central and C-terminal regions—fit experimental structural work. An **N-terminal GIFT region** contributes to an intact B1–B2 assembly interface: the human disease-associated p.Ala199Thr substitution retains several tested B1 contacts but weakens association with IFT38/IFT57 and kinesin-II. A **central, proline-rich region** associates with IFT70; a conserved algal IFT52 peptide is enclosed by IFT70’s tetratricopeptide-repeat superhelix. Structural models additionally place the central IFT52 region alongside IFT88 at the B1–B2 junction. The **C-terminal region** forms an α-helical heterodimer with IFT46, facilitating incorporation of other IFT-B components. These conclusions combine human interaction assays with structures determined predominantly in *Chlamydomonas* and *Tetrahymena*: their published residue numbers must **not** be applied directly to human Q9Y366. The cropped published domain schematic and IFT70–IFT52 structure illustrate the assignments. (ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 2-3, taschner2014crystalstructuresof pages 7-8, taschner2014crystalstructuresof pages 2-3, lacey2023themolecularstructure pages 4-5, taschner2014crystalstructuresof media 5b5ac48e)

The interaction map is supported by perturbation experiments rather than structure prediction alone. In human HEK293T interaction assays, the disease-associated C-terminal truncation **p.Leu293Alafs*** (reported as L293Afs*) failed to support tested IFT-B contacts, B1–B2 coupling, kinesin-II association and an IFT-A-associated interaction. In IFT52-knockout human retinal pigment epithelial cells it failed to rescue ciliogenesis, whereas wild-type IFT52 rescued it; p.Ala199Thr afforded incomplete rescue. Separately, structural modeling of IFT-B was checked against cross-linking mass spectrometry, scattering, crystallography, mutagenesis and binding assays. (ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 2-3, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 3-5, petriman2022biochemicallyvalidatedstructural pages 4-6)

## Cellular site of action and biological processes

IFT52 acts **inside the cell at the mother centriole/basal body and within the cilium**, rather than as a secreted protein. IFT-B subunits accumulate at the ciliary base, where the transport apparatus is recruited and assembled; IFT-B-containing trains subsequently traverse the ciliary shaft toward the tip. In human hTERT-RPE1 cells, IFT52 knockout eliminates detectable mother-centriole IFT88 and IFT38 and prevents ciliogenesis. A 2025 knockout comparison found that IFT38 can still be recruited to a centriole in non-ciliated **KIF3B**-knockout cells: IFT-B recruitment therefore does not simply follow cilium formation. Another 2025 study found mother-centriole recruitment of IFT-A and IFT-B can occur independently, refining—but not making IFT52 dispensable for—the model of pre-train assembly. (ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 3-5, tasaki2025mutuallyindependentand pages 2-4, tasaki2025assemblyandmother pages 5-10)

The most detailed **in situ** train architecture currently cited here comes from 2023 cryo-electron tomography and structure-guided modeling of *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii* flagella, **not human cilia**. It places IFT52 centrally in the repeating anterograde IFT-B architecture and links its configuration to IFT88 and the B1–B2 interface. This is strong evidence for a conserved structural mechanism, although individual human-train contacts remain an extrapolation. Earlier experiments in *Chlamydomonas* found that IFT52 associates with IFT46 in the cytoplasm and recruits it to basal bodies and flagella, supporting preassembly and targeting as well as transport roles. (lacey2023themolecularstructure pages 4-5, lacey2023themolecularstructure pages 1-2, lv2017intraflagellartransportprotein pages 1-4)

An additional, less-established **extraciliary centrosomal role** has been proposed. A 2019 study reported distal-centriole association of IFT52 with centrin and, particularly in Ift52-null mouse IMCD3 kidney cells, abnormal centrin distribution, microtubule anchorage/dynamics and centrosome cohesion; wild-type but not certain patient-derived variant constructs corrected centriole splitting. These observations merit consideration when interpreting renal phenotypes, but evidence that this mechanism independently causes human disease is weaker than the convergent human-cell evidence for IFT-B assembly and ciliogenesis. (dupont2019humanift52mutations pages 16-21, dupont2019humanift52mutations pages 13-16)

## Signaling and cargo trafficking

IFT52 is best placed **upstream of cilium-dependent signaling**, by sustaining the organelle and the transport machinery that position signaling proteins. In a zebrafish *ift52* knockout, reduced expression of the Hedgehog target genes *ptc2* and *gli1* accompanied ciliary and craniofacial defects, supporting a connection to Hedgehog-dependent development. This is a model-organism phenotype, not proof that IFT52 directly binds Hedgehog components or regulates GLI transcription in human cells. (dupont2019humanift52mutations pages 9-13)

Human-cell results make the distinction more precise. In IFT52-knockout RPE1 cells complemented with the partially functional **Δ139–162** or **p.Ala199Thr** variant, Smoothened agonist still produced broadly normal ciliary **SMO entry and GPR161 exit** among the cilia assessed. Nevertheless, p.Ala199Thr diminished ciliary IFT-B abundance, and variant-expressing cells showed impaired tip localization of **KIF17** and **ICK/CILK1**. Thus the observed defect is in IFT-B assembly/motor coupling and selected tip trafficking; it would be incorrect to claim that these variants demonstrably block every Hedgehog receptor-trafficking step. Downstream GLI processing or transcription was not established by that human-cell receptor assay. Nor do the cited IFT52-specific experiments establish a direct Wnt-signaling, kinase or enzymatic activity for IFT52. (ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 3-5, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 7-10, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 5-7)

## Human genetics, quantitative findings and practical use

Biallelic human **IFT52** variants have been reported in skeletal ciliopathies, including short-rib polydactyly/short-rib thoracic dysplasia and cranioectodermal dysplasia (Sensenbrenner syndrome). A 2019 investigation additionally described an individual with congenital kidney/urinary-tract abnormalities and homozygous **p.Asp259His**. Clinical presentations and variant effects are not interchangeable: p.Arg142* was initially described as a nonsense variant, but subsequent analysis detected exon skipping and a residual, partially functional protein; p.Asp259His retained substantial ciliary-complex behavior in tested cells, despite effects in additional assays. The authors cautioned that modifiers or oligogenic factors could contribute to the renal presentation. (dupont2019humanift52mutations pages 13-16, dupont2019humanift52mutations pages 6-9, dupont2019humanift52mutations pages 16-21, girisha2016ahomozygousnonsense pages 1-2)

Patient-cell findings connect genotype to mechanism. In the original short-rib polydactyly report, affected fibroblasts had an approximately **60% reduction in ciliation**; IFT52 and other IFT-B components were reduced, and detectable ciliary IFT88 was lost. In the 2022 human-cell knockout/rescue experiments, wild-type IFT52 restored cilia, p.Ala199Thr produced milder defects, and L293Afs* did not restore cilium formation. These are informative quantitative and functional observations, **not** population estimates of disease frequency or evidence of a treatment. (zhang2016ift52mutationsdestabilize pages 4-5, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 3-5, zhang2016ift52mutationsdestabilize pages 1-1)

The principal **current application is research and variant interpretation**: sequencing identifies candidate biallelic variants; patient fibroblasts or engineered RPE1 cells permit testing of IFT-B stability, basal-body recruitment, ciliation, ciliary IFT88 and variant-specific rescue. Structural interfaces help interpret why a variant disrupts the scaffold. Phenotypes should be interpreted alongside the precise allele and experimental system, because a partially functional variant can preserve one trafficking readout while impairing another. The sources reviewed do not establish an IFT52-directed clinical therapy. (zhang2016ift52mutationsdestabilize pages 5-6, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 3-5, dupont2019humanift52mutations pages 13-16, ishida2022molecularbasisunderlying pages 5-7)
