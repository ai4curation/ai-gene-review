# KLF5 manual notes

Automated deep research failed for Falcon and the configured Perplexity-lite fallback, so this review uses the seeded GOA references and targeted manual source checks.

KLF5 is a nuclear C2H2 zinc-finger KLF transcription factor. The strongest row-level source in the seeded set is PMID:16595680, whose abstract reports KLF5 binding to the survivin core promoter by ChIP and EMSA and activating that promoter while interacting with p53. Those data support KLF5 as a Pol II cis-regulatory DNA-binding transcriptional activator.

Rows that assert simple human two-hybrid or affinity-capture partners are removed only when the GO term is bare GO:0005515 protein binding. The MRF-binding Ensembl transfer is kept as a non-core mouse Klf5 interaction, while the endothelin-response electronic rows are treated as over-propagated expression-response contexts rather than direct human KLF5 processes.
