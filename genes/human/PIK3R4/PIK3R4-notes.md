# PIK3R4 review notes

Review started from `just fetch-gene human PIK3R4`. The proteostasis network places PIK3R4 under `Autophagy-Lysosome Pathway > Autophagophore initiation and elongation > Class 3 PI3K complex 1, direct > Class 3 PI3K complex 1 component`.

Falcon deep research was requested with `just deep-research-falcon human PIK3R4`, but the provider timed out after 600 seconds and no `PIK3R4-deep-research-falcon.md` file was produced. I am completing the review using the cached primary literature, UniProt record, GOA seed, Reactome context, and notes below.

PIK3R4/VPS15/p150 is the regulatory/scaffolding subunit of class III PI3K complexes with PIK3C3/VPS34 and BECN1. The original human p150 paper showed that p150 is homologous to yeast Vps15p, stably associates with PtdIns 3-kinase, increases lipid kinase activity, and conserves the Vps15p/Vps34p protein-trafficking complex from yeast to human [PMID:8999962 "Recombinant p150 associated with PtdIns 3-kinase in vitro in a stable manner" and "Vps15p.Vps34p complex has been conserved from yeast to man"].

The PN leaf is the ATG14-containing PI3KC3-C1/autophagy-initiation branch. PI3KC3-C1 consists of VPS34, VPS15, BECN1, and ATG14; VPS15 organizes the complex and bridges VPS34 to the ATG14:BECN1 subcomplex [PMID:25490155 "PI3KC3-C1 ... consists of the lipid kinase VPS34, the scaffolding protein VPS15, the tumor suppressor BECN1, and the autophagy-specific subunit ATG14" and "VPS15 organizes the complex and serves as a bridge"]. A recent structural paper places VPS15/PIK3R4 in the ULK1C:PI3KC3-C1 initiation supercomplex and states that canonical bulk and selective autophagy are initiated by PI3KC3-C1 recruitment/activation [PMID:40442316 "All forms of canonical autophagy, bulk and selective, are initiated upon the recruitment and activation ... PI3KC3-C1" and "PI3KC3-C1 contains one copy each of ... VPS15"].

PIK3R4 should be modeled as contributing to complex-level PI3P production, not as the catalytic phosphatidylinositol kinase. Reactome says the ATG14:PIK3C3:PIK3R4:BECN1 complex phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol to PI3P for autophagosome formation [Reactome:R-HSA- PMID:5672012 "The Beclin-1 complex (ATG14:PIK3C3:PIK3R4:BECN1) is essential for autophagosome formation" and "PIK3C3 ... phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol (PI) producing phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PI3P)"]. Reactome also describes PIK3R4 as necessary for PIK3C3 catalytic activity, localization, and stability in PI3P-producing complexes [Reactome:R-HSA- PMID:6798174 "PIK3C3, Vps34 ... is found in intracellular membrane complexes with ... PIK3R4, Vps150, necessary for catalytic activity, localization and stability"].

The UVRAG-containing PI3KC3-C2 branch is also real but is secondary to this PN leaf. The Rab7 paper supports late-endosome localization and early/late endosomal PI3K cycling [PMID:14617358 "The hVPS34/p150 complex colocalized with rab7 on late endosomes" and "link rab7 to the regulation of phosphatidylinositol 3'-kinase cycling between early and late endosomes"]. The UVRAG/BIF-1 paper supports receptor degradation and cytokinesis through a VPS15/VPS34/BECN1/UVRAG/BIF-1 subcomplex, while ATG14L is not required for those C2-like functions [PMID:20643123 "a specific sub-complex containing VPS15, VPS34, Beclin 1, UVRAG and BIF-1 regulates both receptor degradation and cytokinesis" and "ATG14L ... is not required"].

Autophagy process rows are supportable at the macroautophagy/autophagosome-assembly level, but cargo-specific pexophagy is too specific for the accessible human PIK3R4 evidence. The class III PI3K macroautophagy paper showed that increasing PI3P by overexpressing p150 stimulates macroautophagy and class III PI3K antisense inhibits macroautophagy [PMID:10625637 "overexpressing the p150 adaptor, stimulates macroautophagy" and "specific class III PI3K antisense oligonucleotide greatly inhibited the rate of macroautophagy"]. NRBF2 studies further support the ATG14-linked Vps34/Vps15 complex in starvation-induced autophagy [PMID:24785657 "NRBF2 binds to complexes that include Vps34, Vps15, Beclin-1 and ATG-14L" and PMID:24849286 "assembly of the specific Atg14L-Beclin 1-Vps34-Vps15 complex for autophagy induction"].

PIK3R4 also carries protein kinase annotations. UniProt currently curates catalytic protein serine/threonine reactions, Mn(2+) cofactor, and probable autophosphorylation, but the recent structural literature describes VPS15 as a pseudokinase [file:human/PIK3R4/PIK3R4-uniprot.txt "Probably autophosphorylated" and PMID:40442316 "the pseudokinase VPS15"]. I am retaining the protein kinase/ATP-binding rows as non-core rather than making them the central functional claim.

Generic `protein binding` annotations are not useful molecular-function annotations here. NRBF2, ATG14, BECN1, UVRAG, RAB7A, and high-throughput interactome rows should be interpreted as PI3KC3 complex/subcomplex evidence or interaction context, not as a core generic binding function [PMID:19270696 "Atg14L and UVRAG bind to Beclin 1 in a mutually exclusive manner"; PMID:24785657 "NRBF2 directly interacts with Vps15"; PMID:14617358 "hVPS34 and its adaptor protein p150 are rab7 interacting partners"].

Curation stance:
- Core: PI3KC3-C1 and PI3KC3-C2 complex membership, contribution to complex-level 1-phosphatidylinositol-3-kinase activity/PI3P biosynthesis, autophagosome assembly and macroautophagy, autophagosome maturation/endosomal trafficking context, late endosome/autophagosome/membrane/cytosol localization.
- Modify: pexophagy to autophagosome assembly; vacuole terms to human lysosomal/endosomal equivalents; PI3K/AKT signaling to autophagosome maturation.
- Keep as non-core: protein kinase/ATP-binding/protein phosphorylation rows, cilium/axoneme/cytoskeleton context, glucose-starvation response, phagocytic vesicle membrane, cytokinesis.
- Remove: nucleus-vacuole junction as a yeast/vacuole-specific cellular component that does not map to human PIK3R4.
- Mark over-annotated: generic protein binding rows.

## 2026-09-20 full evidence re-review

Reviewed all 66 original source assertions and the one pre-existing NEW proposal. PAINT traces recover PTN000426471 in PTHR17583 for the disputed kinase, vacuolar-trafficking, pexophagy and nucleus-vacuole-junction assertions.

- Full primary PMID:39913640 is decisive new target evidence: human-complex cryo-EM, HPLC and mass spectrometry identify GTP in VPS15, not ATP. The paper states “This structure rules out that VPS15 is a GTP-dependent protein kinase.” Altered P-loop and Mg/guanine-binding geometry prevent phosphotransfer to an external protein acceptor. Removed legacy kinase MF assertions and replaced ATP binding with GTP binding. This is direct structural/chemical evidence, not inference from the word pseudokinase or from a better-known scaffold function. GTP occupancy and myristate jointly control membrane docking and VPS34 activation; the core synthesis now reflects that mechanism.
- Broad protein-phosphorylation BP remains UNDECIDED: a noncatalytic scaffold might contribute to another kinase reaction, and loss of VPS15 catalysis does not logically exclude that weaker process claim. The old abstract-only PMID:8999962 does not establish that alternative mechanism.
- Restored vacuolar protein targeting and late-endosome-to-vacuole transport. GO vacuole includes degradative eukaryotic compartments; the lysosome definition is a small lytic vacuole. Human VPS15/UVRAG complexes support receptor degradation (PMID:20643123). The previous early-to-late replacement changed the transport stage; a human taxon alone does not disprove a vacuolar destination.
- Restored autophagy regulation: VPS15 directly controls activity and membrane orientation of the catalytic complex, and a structural/cofactor contribution is compatible with regulation.
- Pexophagy and the specific nucleus-vacuole contact remain UNDECIDED. A shared execution role can count as selective-autophagy participation without cargo recognition. Conversely, generic late endosomal localization does not establish the nuclear contact. Root coordinates focused adjudication; pexophagy may overlap the pending PIK3C3 complex report.
- Full PMID:27882921 establishes a human VPS15–GM130 cis-Golgi pool and an IFT20 trafficking mechanism. In serum-deprived fibroblasts “there is no co-localization between VPS15 and acetylated α-tubulin in patient and control cells”. This contrasts with the inferred ciliary/axonemal pool, so those localization assertions are UNDECIDED rather than retained as unqualified positives or globally rejected from a single context. Description includes the demonstrated Golgi mechanism; no speculative NEW location/process assertions were added.
- The AKT source assertion was refined to the PI3P-producing complex contribution after inspection of full PMID:21062745 (shared manual primary-check note under ATG14). The paper does not establish a p150-specific AKT pathway endpoint.
- Withdrew the pre-existing NEW autophagosome-assembly child because the source annotations already cover macroautophagy and PI3KC3-C1. The direct assembly mechanism remains in the supported core synthesis, without manufacturing another redundant assertion.
- Generic binding removals are information-content decisions, not refutations of interactions. Global OpenScientist query scan by gene/accession/aliases found no existing report. All non-NEW source metadata remains exact.

## 2026-09-21 protein-phosphorylation report incorporation

Read the complete [focused OpenScientist report](PIK3R4-hypotheses/protein-phosphorylation-complex-contribution/openscientist.md), its citation list, HTML and extracted PDF. GO:0006468 remains UNDECIDED after adjudication. A pseudokinase may perform structural or cofactor work in another enzyme's reaction; being phosphorylated is insufficient, but pseudokinase status does not establish universal absence of participation. The report acknowledges uncertainty while recommending removal/noncore inconsistently, and its Knowledge Gaps states: “Checked: abstract + seed description; unresolved.” It did not obtain the full1997 or2025 key papers. It claims a motif CSV and executed code, but neither is among the two delivered artifacts. No computational result from that claim was adopted.

Full PMID:39913640 supports the existing intrinsic kinase-MF rejection through human nucleotide identification and geometry, not simply the word pseudokinase.
