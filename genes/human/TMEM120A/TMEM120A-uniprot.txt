CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multifunctional protein involved in mechanosensation, and
CC       plays an essential role in lipid metabolism and adipocyte
CC       differentiation (PubMed:26024229, PubMed:36420836). May function as a
CC       potential ion channel involved in sensing mechanical stimuli
CC       (PubMed:35235791). Mediates the mechanosensitivity of the PKD2-TMEM120A
CC       channel complex through direct physical interaction (PubMed:36420836).
CC       TMEM120A seems to affect mechanosensation by inhibiting PIEZO2
CC       channels, possibly by altering cellular lipid content (By similarity).
CC       TMEM120A is structurally similar to a lipid-modifying enzyme, ELOVL7,
CC       and contains a bound coenzyme A molecule, which suggests it might
CC       function as an enzyme in lipid metabolism (PubMed:34374645,
CC       PubMed:34409941, PubMed:34465718). Additionally, implicated in innate
CC       immune response against Zika virus. Acts as a key activator of the
CC       antiviral signaling involving STING1 (PubMed:35013224).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8C1E7, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26024229,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34374645, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34409941,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34465718, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35013224,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35235791, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36420836}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:34374645, PubMed:34409941, PubMed:34465718,
CC       PubMed:35235791). Forms heterooligomer with TMEM120B (PubMed:26024229).
CC       Interacts with PKD2; TMEM120A inhibits PKD2 channel activity through
CC       the physical association of PKD2 with TMEM120A (PubMed:36420836).
CC       Interacts (via C-terminal domain) with STING1; regulates the
CC       trafficking of STING1 from the ER to the ER-Golgi intermediate
CC       compartment to elicit antiviral effects (PubMed:35013224).

CC   -!- CAUTION: Whether TMEM120S functions as a mechanosensitive ion channel

CC       does not exhibit mechanosensitive channel activity and display no
CC       typical ion channel structural characteristics (PubMed:34374645,
CC       PubMed:34409941, PubMed:34465718). One publication, however, suggests
CC       the presence of a potential ion permeation pathway based on molecular
CC       dynamics simulation (PubMed:35235791). Its structural homology to
CC       ELOVL7, leads to suggest than TMEM120A may function as an enzyme
CC       involved in fatty acid metabolism, although its enzymatic activity and
CC       its potential substrates remain unknown (PubMed:34374645,
CC       PubMed:34409941, PubMed:34465718). Whether TMEM120A is an enzyme rather
CC       than an ion channel is still under debate.
