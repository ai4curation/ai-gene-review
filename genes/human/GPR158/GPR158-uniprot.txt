CC   -!- FUNCTION: Metabotropic receptor for glycine that controls synapse
CC       formation and function in the brain (PubMed:36996198). Acts as an
CC       atypical G protein-coupled receptor that recruits and regulates the
CC       RGS7-GNB5 complex instead of activating G proteins (PubMed:31189666,
CC       PubMed:36996198). In absence of glycine ligand, promotes the GTPase
CC       activator activity of RGS7, increasing the GTPase activity of G protein
CC       alpha subunits, thereby driving them into their inactive GDP-bound form
CC       (PubMed:36996198). Glycine-binding changes the conformation of the
CC       intracellular surface, inhibiting the GTPase activator activity of the
CC       RGS7-GNB5 complex, promoting G protein alpha subunits into their active
CC       GTP-bound form and regulating cAMP levels (PubMed:36996198). Also able
CC       to bind taurine, a compound closely related to glycine, but with a two-
CC       fold lower affinity (PubMed:36996198). Glycine receptor-dependent
CC       regulation of cAMP controls key ion channels, kinases and neurotrophic
CC       factors involved in neuronal excitability and synaptic transmission
CC       (PubMed:36996198). Plays a pivotal role in regulating mood and
CC       cognition via its ability to regulate neuronal excitability in L2/L3
CC       pyramidal neurons of the prefrontal cortex (By similarity). Also
CC       involved in spatial learning by regulating hippocampal CA1 neuronal
CC       excitability (By similarity). Acts as a synaptic organizer in the
CC       hippocampus, required for proper mossy fiber-CA3 neurocircuitry
CC       establishment, structure and function: induces presynaptic
CC       differentiation in contacting axons via its interaction with GPC4 (By
CC       similarity). In addition to glycine, may also act as a receptor for
CC       osteocalcin (BGLAP) hormone: osteocalcin-binding initiates a signaling
CC       response that prevents neuronal apoptosis in the hippocampus and
CC       regulates the synthesis of neurotransmitters (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8C419, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31189666,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36996198}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:31189666, PubMed:34793198, PubMed:34815401).
CC       Associates with the RGS7-GNB5 complex, promoting its localization to
CC       the cell membrane and regulating its GTPase activator activity
CC       (PubMed:31189666, PubMed:34793198, PubMed:34815401). Interacts (via
CC       VCPWE motifs) with GNAO1 (PubMed:31189666). Interacts with GPC4 (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with EGFLAM (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:D4A6L0, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8C419,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31189666, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34793198,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34815401}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23451275,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30855200, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31189666}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34793198,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34815401}. Postsynaptic cell membrane
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8C419}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34793198, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34815401}. Presynaptic
CC       cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8C419}; Multi-pass membrane
CC       protein {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34793198, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34815401}.
CC       Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23451275, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30855200}.
CC       Note=Mainly localizes to the postsynaptic membrane, with a small
CC       portion to the presynaptic membrane (By similarity). Trafficks between
CC       the nucleus and the cell membrane; it is unclear how a multi-pass
CC       membrane protein can traffick between the nucleus and the cell membrane
CC       (PubMed:23451275). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8C419,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23451275}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The Cache-like region shares similarity with the Cache domain,
CC       a well-known receptor for amino acids (PubMed:34793198). It acts as a
CC       ligand-binding module that recognizes and binds glycine and taurine
CC       (PubMed:36996198). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34793198,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36996198}.
