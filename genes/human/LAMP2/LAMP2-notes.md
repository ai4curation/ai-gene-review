# LAMP2 notes

Deep research (falcon / perplexity-lite) failed; notes from cached publications and UniProt P13473.

- Type I lysosomal membrane glycoprotein; luminal domain 29-375, TM 376-399, cytoplasmic 400-410 (UniProt).
- Principal location intracellular lysosomes; minor PM fraction in some cells [PMID:2912382 "A fraction of each glycoprotein was also detected on the plasma membrane of U937 and HL-60 cells but not on a variety of other tissue culture cells."]. Shuttles lysosome/endosome/PM (UniProt, PMID:17897319). Surface after degranulation (Reactome platelet/neutrophil degranulation).
- LAMP-2A = CMA receptor [PMID:11082038 "Lamp2a acts as a receptor in the lysosomal membrane for substrate proteins of chaperone-mediated autophagy."]; multimeric translocation complex [PMID:18644871].
- LAMP1/2 inhibit TMEM175 -> lysosomal acidification [PMID:37390818].
- Needed for STX17 recruitment and autophagosome-lysosome fusion [PMID:27628032].
- Stabilizes TAPL [PMID:22641697].

Localization conclusion: lysosomal/late endosomal membrane (type I, luminal domain inside lumen); minor/transient PM pool
with luminal domain exposed extracellularly (CD107b, upregulated on degranulation). Not a constitutive surface protein.
