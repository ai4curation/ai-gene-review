CC   -!- FUNCTION: Lysosomal membrane glycoprotein which plays an important role
CC       in lysosome biogenesis, lysosomal pH regulation and autophagy
CC       (PubMed:11082038, PubMed:18644871, PubMed:24880125, PubMed:27628032,
CC       PubMed:36586411, PubMed:37390818, PubMed:8662539). Acts as an important
CC       regulator of lysosomal lumen pH regulation by acting as a direct
CC       inhibitor of the proton channel TMEM175, facilitating lysosomal
CC       acidification for optimal hydrolase activity (PubMed:37390818). Plays
CC       an important role in chaperone-mediated autophagy, a process that
CC       mediates lysosomal degradation of proteins in response to various
CC       stresses and as part of the normal turnover of proteins with a long
CC       biological half-live (PubMed:11082038, PubMed:18644871,
CC       PubMed:24880125, PubMed:27628032, PubMed:36586411, PubMed:8662539).
CC       Functions by binding target proteins, such as GAPDH, GPX4, NLRP3 and
CC       MLLT11, and targeting them for lysosomal degradation (PubMed:11082038,
CC       PubMed:18644871, PubMed:24880125, PubMed:36586411, PubMed:8662539). In
CC       the chaperone-mediated autophagy, acts downstream of chaperones, such
CC       as HSPA8/HSC70, which recognize and bind substrate proteins and mediate
CC       their recruitment to lysosomes, where target proteins bind LAMP2
CC       (PubMed:36586411). Plays a role in lysosomal protein degradation in
CC       response to starvation (By similarity). Required for the fusion of
CC       autophagosomes with lysosomes during autophagy (PubMed:27628032). Cells
CC       that lack LAMP2 express normal levels of VAMP8, but fail to accumulate
CC       STX17 on autophagosomes, which is the most likely explanation for the
CC       lack of fusion between autophagosomes and lysosomes (PubMed:27628032).
CC       Required for normal degradation of the contents of autophagosomes
CC       (PubMed:27628032). Required for efficient MHC class II-mediated
CC       presentation of exogenous antigens via its function in lysosomal
CC       protein degradation; antigenic peptides generated by proteases in the
CC       endosomal/lysosomal compartment are captured by nascent MHC II subunits
CC       (PubMed:15894275, PubMed:20518820). Is not required for efficient MHC
CC       class II-mediated presentation of endogenous antigens
CC       (PubMed:20518820). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P17046,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11082038, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15894275,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18644871, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20518820,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24880125, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27628032,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36586411, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37390818,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8662539}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform LAMP-2C]: Modulates chaperone-mediated autophagy.
CC       Decreases presentation of endogenous antigens by MHCII. Does not play a
CC       role in the presentation of exogenous and membrane-derived antigens by
CC       MHCII. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26856698}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: (Microbial infection) Supports the FURIN-mediated cleavage of
CC       mumps virus fusion protein F by interacting with both FURIN and the
CC       unprocessed form but not the processed form of the viral protein F.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32295904}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer (PubMed:18644871, PubMed:25342746). Homodimer
CC       (PubMed:25342746). Homotrimer (PubMed:25342746). Forms large
CC       homooligomers (PubMed:18644871). Interacts (via its cytoplasmic region)
CC       with HSPA8; HSPA8 mediates recruitment of proteins with a KFERQ motif
CC       to the surface of the lysosome for chaperone-mediated autophagy
CC       (PubMed:25342746, PubMed:36586411). Interacts with HSP90 in the
CC       lysosome lumen; this enhances LAMP2 stability (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with MLLT11 (PubMed:24880125). Interacts with ABCB9
CC       (PubMed:22641697). Interacts with FURIN (PubMed:32295904). Interacts
CC       with CT55; this interaction may be important for LAMP2 protein
CC       stability (PubMed:36481789). Interacts with TMEM175; inhibiting the
CC       proton channel activity of TMEM175 (PubMed:37390818). Forms a ternary
CC       complex with RAB7A and RUFY4 (via RUN domain); the interaction with
CC       RAB7A is mediated by RUFY4 (via RUN and coiled coil domains) (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P17046,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P17047, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18644871,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22641697, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24880125,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25342746, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32295904,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36481789, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37390818}.
