# PADI6 notes

## 2026-11-28 manual review

PADI6 belongs to the PADI structural family but is not an active protein-arginine deiminase. Two 2024 human structural papers agree with the PAINT IKR on `PTN000089843`: Ranaivoson et al. found a PAD6 homodimer with a substrate-blocked active site and no citrullination of histone H3 or cytokeratin 5 [PMID:38656308, "PAD6 was found to be enzymatically inactive for citrullination using histone H3 and cytokeratin 5 in vitro"], and Williams et al. reported that human PADI6 dimerizes but does not bind Ca2+ or citrullinate standard PADI substrates [PMID:39286527, "human PADI6 dimerises like hPADIs 2-4, however, does not bind Ca2+ and is inactive in in vitro assays against standard PADI substrates"].

The central PADI6 role is structural. Mammalian oocyte cytoplasmic lattices contain PADI6 and SCMC proteins, store maternally provided proteins for the early embryo, and are lost when PADI6 is knocked out [PMID:37922900, "cytoplasmic lattices are composed of filaments with a high surface area, which contain PADI6 and subcortical maternal complex proteins"]. Human PADI6 loss-of-function variants cause female infertility with early embryonic arrest, but those phenotype papers support the importance of the lattice-storage function rather than a separate catalytic or calcium-binding activity [PMID:27545678].

Rows propagated from mouse to tubulin binding, embryonic cleavage, epigenetic programming, cortical granules, and ooplasm were reviewed cautiously: the cytoplasmic-lattice structural and protein-storage terms are retained as the direct functions, while higher-level embryonic phenotypes are treated as over-annotations unless they name the lattice-storage work itself.
