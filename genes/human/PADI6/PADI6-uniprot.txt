CC   -!- FUNCTION: Structural constituent of cytoplasmic lattices, which plays a
CC       key role in early embryonic development (PubMed:37922900,
CC       PubMed:41772195). Cytoplasmic lattices consist in fibrous structures
CC       found in the cytoplasm of oocytes and preimplantation embryos: they are
CC       required to store maternal proteins critical for embryonic development,
CC       such as ribosomal proteins, cytoskeletal tubulins and proteins
CC       controlling epigenetic reprogramming of the preimplantation embryo, in
CC       a poised but restrained state, regulating their availability for early
CC       embryonic development (PubMed:37922900, PubMed:41772195). Cytoplasmic
CC       lattices also tightly control ubiquitination during the oocyte-to-
CC       embryo transition by trapping E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase UHRF1 in a
CC       compact autoinhibited conformation and maintaining SCF ubiquitin ligase
CC       complexes inactive (PubMed:41772195). PADI6 oligomers acts as an
CC       essential structural scaffolding element of cytoplasmic lattices (By
CC       similarity). In contrast to other members of the family, does not show
CC       protein-arginine deiminase activity due to its inability to bind Ca(2+)
CC       (PubMed:38656308, PubMed:39286527). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8K3V4,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37922900, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38656308,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39286527, ECO:0000269|PubMed:41772195}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer; homodimerizes via a head-to tail mechanism
CC       (PubMed:38656308, PubMed:39286527). Structural component of cytoplasmic
CC       lattices, at least composed of PADI6, NLRP2, NLRP5, NLRP7, NLRP14,
CC       TLE6, KHDC3L and OOEP (PubMed:37148315). Cytoplasmic lattices form a
CC       filamentous architecture; the external framework is built from PADI6
CC       decamers and the subcortical maternal complexes (SCMC) (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8K3V4, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37148315,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38656308, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39286527}.

CC   -!- DISEASE: Note=PADI6 variants have been found in a spectrum of
CC       phenotypes characterized by aberrant methylation of multiple imprinted
CC       loci, a condition known as multi-locus imprinting defect or multi-locus
CC       imprinting disturbance (MLID) (PubMed:32928291, PubMed:33221824). MLID-
CC       related phenotype spectrum ranges from intrauterine death to different
CC       types of imprinting disorders, including Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome
CC       (BWS), Silver-Russell syndrome (SRS), and non-specific developmental
CC       and behavioral manifestations (PubMed:32928291, PubMed:33221824).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32928291, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33221824}.
