CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of neuronal acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) that
CC       function as pentameric, ligand-gated cation channels with high calcium
CC       permeability. nAChRs are excitatory neurotrasnmitter receptors formed
CC       by a collection of nAChR subunits. Each nAchR subunit confers
CC       differential attributes to channel properties, including activation,
CC       deactivation and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, cation
CC       permeability, and binding to allosteric modulators (Probable). Forms
CC       heteropentamers with CHRNA9. Expressed in the inner ear, in sympathetic
CC       neurons and in other non-neuronal cells, such as skin keratinocytes and
CC       lymphocytes (PubMed:11752216, PubMed:15531379). nAChR formed by
CC       CHRNA9:CHRNA10 is involved in modulation of auditory stimuli. The
CC       channel is permeable to a range of divalent cations including calcium,
CC       the influx of which may activate a potassium current which
CC       hyperpolarizes the cell membrane. In the ear, mediates synaptic
CC       transmission between efferent olivocochlear fibers and hair cells of
CC       the cochlea, this may lead to a reduction in basilar membrane motion,
CC       altering the activity of auditory nerve fibers and reducing the range
CC       of dynamic hearing (PubMed:11752216). This may protect against acoustic
CC       trauma. May also regulate keratinocyte adhesion (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9UGM1, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11752216,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15531379, ECO:0000305|PubMed:18723036}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by a myriad of ligands such as
CC       acetylcholine (PubMed:11752216). AChR activity is inhibited by the
CC       antagonists alpha-conotoxins RgIA and GeXXA, small disulfide-
CC       constrained peptides from cone snails (PubMed:26395518).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11752216, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26395518}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms homo- or heterooligomeric channels in conjunction with
CC       CHRNA10. The native outer hair cell receptor may be composed of
CC       CHRNA9:CHRNA10 heterooligomers (PubMed:11752216). Found in the
CC       stoichiometric form (CHRNA9)2:(CHRNA10)3 (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9JLB5, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11752216}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Synaptic cell membrane

CC       {ECO:0000255}. Cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9JLB5}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in inner-ear tissue, tonsil, immortalized
CC       B-cells, cultured T-cells and peripheral blood lymphocytes.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11752216, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15531379}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: The heterooligomeric receptor composed of CHRNA9 and
CC       CHRNA10 has an atypical pharmacological profile, binding several non-
CC       nicotinic ligands including strychnine (a glycine receptor antagonist)
CC       and atropine (a muscarinic acetylcholine receptor antagonist).
