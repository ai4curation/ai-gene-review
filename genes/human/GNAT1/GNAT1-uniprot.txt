CC   -!- FUNCTION: Alpha subunit of the heterotrimeric guanine nucleotide-
CC       binding protein (G protein) that transduces signals from the G protein-
CC       coupled photoreceptor rhodopsin (RHO). Essential for normal RHO-
CC       mediated light perception in retinal rod photoreceptors
CC       (PubMed:22190596). Functions as a GTPase, cycling between an active
CC       GTP-bound state and an inactive GDP-bound state. Under dim light
CC       conditions, photoactivated RHO promotes GDP release and GTP binding. In
CC       its active state, GNAT1 stimulates downstream effectors, including rod
CC       cGMP-phosphodiesterase (PDE6) complex, to mediate the phototransduction
CC       cascade (By similarity). Activation of the PDE6 complex occurs through
CC       binding and repositioning of the inhibitory PDE6 gamma subunits
CC       (PDE6G), thereby promoting catalytic activity (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04695, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22190596}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=GTP + H2O = GDP + phosphate + H(+); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:19669,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:15377, ChEBI:CHEBI:15378, ChEBI:CHEBI:37565,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:43474, ChEBI:CHEBI:58189;

CC       PhysiologicalDirection=left-to-right; Xref=Rhea:RHEA:19670;

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Regulated by the GTPase activating protein (GAP)
CC       RGS9, which increases the GTP hydrolysis activity.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04695}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of a heterotrimeric G protein composed of alpha,
CC       beta and gamma subunits (By similarity). The alpha chain contains the
CC       guanine nucleotide-binding site. Forms the transducin heterotrimer
CC       composed of GNAT1, GBB1 and GNGT1; this complex associates with RHO in
CC       rod photoreceptors (By similarity). Interacts with RHO; interaction
CC       promotes GDP release and GTP binding (By similarity). Interacts with
CC       the PDE6 heterotetrameric complex; two GNAT1-GTP molecules bind both
CC       the catalytic core (PDE6A and PDE6B) and the inhibitory PDE6G subunits,
CC       inducing conformational rearrangements that relieve inhibition and
CC       activate catalysis (By similarity). Interacts with RGS9 and PDE6G; RGS9
CC       acts as a GAP for GNAT1 (By similarity). Interacts (when myristoylated)
CC       with UNC119; interaction is required for localization in sensory
CC       neurons (PubMed:21642972). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04695,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21642972}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Photoreceptor outer segment membrane
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04695}; Lipid-anchor
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21642972}. Photoreceptor inner segment
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P20612}. Note=Localizes to disk membranes in the
CC       rod outer segment (OS) (By similarity). Localizes mainly in the outer
CC       segment in the dark-adapted state, whereas is translocated to the inner
CC       part of the photoreceptors in the light-adapted state. During dark-
CC       adapted conditions, in the presence of UNC119 mislocalizes from the
CC       outer segment to the inner part of rod photoreceptors which leads to
CC       decreased photoreceptor damage caused by light.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04695, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P20612}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Rod photoreceptor cells (PubMed:1614872).
CC       Predominantly expressed in the retina followed by the ciliary body,
CC       iris and retinal pigment epithelium (PubMed:22190596).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1614872, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22190596}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: First detected at low levels at approximately
CC       postnatal day 7. Subsequently, expression increases rapidly during the
CC       first month after birth. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22190596}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Night blindness, congenital stationary, autosomal dominant 3
CC       (CSNBAD3) [MIM:610444]: A non-progressive retinal disorder
CC       characterized by impaired night vision, often associated with nystagmus
CC       and myopia. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17584859, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8673138}.
CC       Note=The disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented
CC       in this entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Night blindness, congenital stationary, 1G (CSNB1G)
CC       [MIM:616389]: An autosomal recessive form of congenital stationary
CC       night blindness, a non-progressive retinal disorder characterized by
CC       impaired night vision or in dim light, with good vision only on bright
CC       days. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22190596}. Note=The disease is caused by
CC       variants affecting the gene represented in this entry.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the G-alpha family. G(i/o/t/z) subfamily.
