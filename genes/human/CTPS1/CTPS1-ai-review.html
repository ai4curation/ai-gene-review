Gene Description

CTPS1 is a glutamine-dependent CTP synthase that couples ATP consumption to the amination of UTP, supplying CTP for nucleotide and membrane-lipid synthesis. Its homotetrameric enzyme can assemble into regulated cytoophidia in the cytoplasm and nucleus; substrate-associated human filaments enhance catalytic activity. Increased CTP supply is particularly important for proliferation of activated lymphocytes. CTPS1 also catalyzes protein asparagine deamidation: modification of histone H1 facilitates subsequent acetylation and chromatin relaxation during DNA repair, while IRF3 deamidation limits interferon induction. These catalytic roles are regulated by cellular context and phosphorylation.
