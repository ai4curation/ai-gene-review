CC       PubMed:17189248, PubMed:17463002, PubMed:24870241, PubMed:28459447,
CC       PubMed:34583994). Catalyzes the ATP-dependent amination of UTP to CTP
CC       with either L-glutamine or ammonia as a source of nitrogen
CC       (PubMed:16179339, PubMed:24870241, PubMed:28459447, PubMed:34583994).
CC       CTPS1 CTP synthase activity plays a crucial role in the proliferation
CC       of activated lymphocytes and immunity; additional CTP being required to
CC       meet increased demand for DNA, RNA and lipid membrane biosynthesis in
CC       proliferating lymphocytes (PubMed:24870241, PubMed:8530356). In
CC       addition to CTP synthase activity, also acts as a protein deamidase
CC       that catalyzes the side chain deamidation of specific asparagine
CC       residues of proteins to aspartate (PubMed:40240600). Acts as a negative
CC       regulator of innate immunity by mediating deamidation of 'Asn-85' of
CC       IRF3, preventing IRF3 from binding DNA (By similarity). Facilitates
CC       chromatin relaxation in response to DNA damage by mediating deamidation
CC       of 'Asn-76' and 'Asn-77' of histone H1, thereby promoting subsequent
CC       acetylation of histone H1 at 'Lys-75' (H1K75ac), increasing chromatin
