## Biological scope

The two activity summaries distinguish direct Hsp90 ATPase inhibition/cochaperone activity from binding FLCN within the nutrient-regulated lysosomal signaling complex. FNIP1 contributes positively to the latter complex; the exact original annotation that it directly activates FLCN GAP catalysis remains UNDECIDED. Recruitment, Rag association and nucleotide-state epistasis do not themselves measure a catalytic rate increase.

FNIP1-dependent effects on B-cell development and survival are retained with species, developmental-stage and nutrient limits. The positive-apoptosis ISS term is modified to negative regulation of B-cell apoptotic process: actual mouse loss experiments show increased death, not a pro-apoptotic role for wild-type Fnip1. The human source assertion remains intact. The two 2012 mouse studies have different mTOR/rapamycin outcomes and are not merged into a universal TOR response.
