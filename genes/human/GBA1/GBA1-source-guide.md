# GBA1 source and context guide

## Catalytic function and scope

The core is lysosomal glucosylceramide hydrolysis (GO:0004348), directly contributing to glucosylceramide catabolism (GO:0006680) and ceramide formation (GO:0046513). Saposin-assisted access to membrane lipid and the lumenal catalytic setting are compatible; membrane association does not make GBA1 a transmembrane transporter. Natural-substrate liposome work and cellular salvage experiments independently ground the core. The latter separates GBA1 cleavage from subsequent acid-ceramidase and ceramide-synthase steps. [PMID:9201993](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9201993/); [PMID:19279011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19279011/).

Secondary glucosyl transfer and steryl-glucoside hydrolysis are retained without assuming a fixed net direction in every cell. Original PMID:26724485 Discussion supports baseline degradation and cholesterol-loading-dependent formation, but leaves the causal consequences of altered glucosylcholesterol in Gaucher disease unresolved; the structured biology gap is limited to that question. The accessible PMID:24211208 abstract concerns GlcCer-dependent synthesis, whereas selected original PMID:26724485 Methods/Results distinguish hydrolysis and transfer and distinguish GBA1 from the mouse GBA2 comparator. The former's uninspected Methods are not reconstructed from its abstract. [PMID:24211208](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24211208/); [PMID:26724485](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26724485/).

Three additional sources qualify substrate breadth. The [external original PMID:32144204](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7170530/) Results accompanying Fig. 9E state: “GalCer hydrolysis occurred in the presence of rGBA1”. Its C6-NBD-GalCer assay gives approximately 200-fold lower activity than the GlcCer analog. This is not a comparison with GALC. The [official human GALC record](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P54803/entry) establishes a lysosomal GalCer hydrolase associated with Krabbe disease. The positive GBA1 assay remains valid, while physiological GalCer clearance by GBA1 is unestablished and the annotation is marked over-annotated. PMID:33361282 supports xylose-containing lipid chemistry. PMID:39395789 main-text paragraph27 distinguishes sterol substrates from fluorogenic probes and explicitly allows poor solubility to limit 6-O-palmitoylated substrate degradation. No medaka result is attributed to the last paper. [PMID:32144204](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32144204/); [PMID:33361282](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33361282/); [PMID:39395789](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39395789/).

The GO:0009247 definition describes diacylglycerol glycolipids, yet actual typed children include glycosphingolipid, dolichol-linked and galactolipid biosynthesis. The three non-core sterol-glycoside synthesis contexts retain this ontology-scope question explicitly. GO:0046527 does not require UDP-glucose; no unverified specific child or new physiological pathway is invented. Current definitions and typed-child evidence are preserved in the source archive.

## Partners, delivery and compartments

LIMP-2/SCARB2 and chaperone partners support informative partner-specific binding refinements. The original PGRN interaction remains positive, while its generic protein-binding row is removed for informativeness. PGRN recruitment of HSP70 does not independently establish that PGRN itself meets the folding-chaperone class required by GO:0051087. [PMID:18022370](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18022370/); [PMID:25202012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25202012/); [PMID:27789271](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27789271/).

PMID:40159502 selected structure/Methods use soluble LIMP-2 residues 36–431, not the native bilayer-spanning receptor. The interface supports binding; native topology, low-pH release and a simultaneous saposin complex require distinct evidence. ER/Golgi/TGN localization can accompany delivery without becoming GBA1's principal catalytic compartment. [PMID:40159502](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40159502/).

The actual official Reactome participant chain is [reaction 1605591](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1605591) → [GBA1:SAPC catalyst 1605675](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1605675) → [human GBA1 entity 1605775](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1605775), linked to P04062 chain 40–536 at the lysosomal membrane. Imported GO columns on the entity are not additional experiments.

## Cellular outcomes and experimental limits

The protein-clearance, autophagy and lysosome-organization annotations retain positive effects while separating endpoints from executed chemistry. PMID:26392287 uses human GBA1 in rodents; that host does not make the construct wrong-target. Static LC3/p62 cannot resolve induction versus blocked clearance. PMID:27378698 reports cell-type-dependent flux and lysosome-reformation endpoints, leaving parts of the lipid/signaling mechanism unresolved. Lack of a protease activity alone is not the reason for the two direct protein-catabolism over-annotation decisions. [PMID:26392287](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26392287/); [PMID:27378698](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27378698/).

PMID:23707074 Results (Fig.3) and live-cell Methods measure mitochondrial potential with TMRM in mouse neurons and astrocytes, including oligomycin responses. This supports the specific GO:0051881 replacement for the broader potential term, without a direct membrane-pump assignment. [PMID:23707074](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23707074/).

PMID:25456120 includes wild-type biochemical controls but twin-only MEA comparisons. Protein/dopamine rescue is not firing rescue. Its full main text is accessible. The two NOT contexts remain bounded culture observations; the firing annotation remains UNDECIDED because the inspected comparison does not isolate GBA1-dependent firing relative to wild type, not because the main paper is inaccessible. The protein-abundance result supports regulation without directly measuring degradation rate. Main figure pixels and full supplementary methods were not inspected. [PMID:25456120](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25456120/).

PMID:9101438 selected original Methods/Results/Discussion concern rat epidermal enzyme responses and lipid-barrier maturation. The Results on p. 580 state that “activity was unaffected by 1 d of DHT treatment”; after two days, GCase activity was “75% of control”. These DHT-specific findings differ from the abstract’s summary of accelerating hormones. CBE impairs extracellular lipid maturation despite substantially preserved lamellar bodies and secretion, supporting direct catalysis in barrier establishment. Human rows remain donor transfers, not human hormone assays. [External original article](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/14116755_Glucosylceramide_metabolism_is_regulated_during_normal_and_hormonally_stimulated_epidermal_barrier_development_in_the_rat).

PMID:23580063 now has genuine full text. Selected Methods/Results distinguish strong enzyme inhibition from bounded alpha-synuclein and proteolysis results; rat neuronal proteolysis was not measured. A [2021 correction](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0252975) addresses Fig.6A beta-actin band removal and supplies a corrected panel. It announces no revised enzyme assay. Figure pixels/raw blots were not reanalyzed. [PMID:23580063](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23580063/).

## Natural products and ancestry

Official P04062 version 277 declares five sequences of 536/516/263/449/487 residues, each independently matched to direct FASTA and coordinate reconstruction. The first two arise from alternative initiation, not an invented splice mechanism. PMID:3687939 supports active lysosomal products under its tested conditions. The PMID:8294033/ENA D13287 pseudogene provenance raises a specific uncertainty for product 3: BAA02546.1 is also 263 residues but differs at nine positions. This is neither an established identity nor grounds for deleting an official product. Functions of products 4/5 remain unverified in the selected evidence; this access boundary is not promoted to a field-wide claim that nobody knows. A SUSPECTED/CHECK source-record concern makes product 3 provenance queryable without a deletion or replacement claim. [PMID:3687939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3687939/); [PMID:8294033](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8294033/).

Actual PTHR11069 tree/MSA data place the sequence-identical human AN30/PTN002476139 below AN7/PTN001667738 for both incoming IBA assertions. The two IBD donor sets match the raw records, including legitimate target self-evidence. No NOT/IRD/IKR exception was found. The literal ancestral metadata `Eubacteria`/taxon 2 is retained as family-record provenance, not assigned to human or interpreted as an independently dated evolutionary event. These two specific assertions do not establish every secondary substrate or truncated-product activity.
