CC   -!- FUNCTION: S-adenosyl-L-methionine-binding protein that acts as an
CC       inhibitor of mTORC1 signaling via interaction with the GATOR1 and
CC       KICSTOR complexes (PubMed:29123071, PubMed:35776786). Acts as a sensor
CC       of S-adenosyl-L-methionine to signal methionine sufficiency to mTORC1:
CC       in presence of methionine, binds S-adenosyl-L-methionine, leading to
CC       disrupt interaction with the GATOR1 and KICSTOR complexes and promote
CC       mTORC1 signaling (PubMed:29123071, PubMed:35776786). Upon methionine
CC       starvation, S-adenosyl-L-methionine levels are reduced, thereby
CC       promoting the association with GATOR1 and KICSTOR, leading to inhibit
CC       mTORC1 signaling (PubMed:29123071, PubMed:35776786). Probably also acts
CC       as a S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent methyltransferase (Potential).
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03044, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29123071,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35776786}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the DEPDC5 subunit of the GATOR1 complex;
CC       interaction is disrupted when SAMTOR binds S-adenosyl-L-methionine
CC       (PubMed:29123071, PubMed:29590090, PubMed:35776786). Interacts with the
CC       KICSTOR complex; interaction is disrupted when SAMTOR binds S-adenosyl-
CC       L-methionine (PubMed:29123071, PubMed:35776786). {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_03044, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29123071,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29590090, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35776786}.
