# EXOSC9 notes

EXOSC9/RRP45/PM-Scl-75 is the human RRP45 subunit of the RNA exosome core. The full-length PM/Scl-75c isoforms are incorporated into the exosome, whereas shorter PM/Scl-75a isoforms lack the N-terminal region needed for exosome association [PMID:12788944, "only the longer polypeptide is able to associate with the exosome complex"].

EXOSC9 is non-catalytic despite its RNase PH-like domain. The current human-exosome model assigns RNA hydrolysis to DIS3, DIS3L and EXOSC10/RRP6; the Exo-9 core is a catalytically inactive scaffold and substrate-binding channel [PMID:20531386, "It consists of a nine-subunit catalytically inert core that serves a structural function and participates in substrate recognition."].

AU-rich RNA binding is retained for EXOSC9 because PM-Scl75 was the direct bait in Mukherjee et al. and Anderson et al. [PMID:11782436, "PM-Scl75 protein was found to interact specifically with AREs"]. By contrast, the old NAS 3'-5' RNA exonuclease activity is removed because it attributes the associated exosome activity to the EXOSC9 gene product itself.

The Senataxin/SETX study supports a non-core nuclear-chromosome/focus localization and a physical interaction with SETX; it does not make EXOSC9 a transcription factor or a positive regulator of RNA polymerase II transcription [PMID:24105744, "Rrp45 colocalizes with SETX in distinct foci upon induction of transcription-related DNA damage"]. The Ensembl transcription-factor rows are therefore marked over-annotated rather than accepted.

Reactome ContentService was checked for the RNA exosome localization TAS rows: R-HSA-6791222, R-HSA-9933575, R-HSA-9933585, R-HSA-9934522, R-HSA-9934763, R-HSA-9935282 and R-HSA-9935290 are nucleoplasm events; R-HSA-430028, R-HSA-450422, R-HSA-450431 and R-HSA-450488 are cytosol events; R-HSA-9935824 and R-HSA-9935837 are nucleolus events.

The NAS immune-response row from the PM/Scl autoantibody paper treats antigenicity as if it were EXOSC9 function. The paper is useful for the PM/Scl history but does not show EXOSC9 executing an immune response.

No production GO-CAM in `gocams/index.tsv` names EXOSC9, and no family-specific module was added for the same reason as EXOSC7 and EXOSC8: these three proteins are only part of the larger RNA exosome machinery.
