# PGF manual review notes

Manual synthesis was used for this review because Perplexity deep research was unavailable during PR preparation.

## Core biology

PGF encodes placenta growth factor, a secreted disulfide-linked VEGF-family ligand. UniProt lists four alternative isoforms. PlGF binds FLT1/VEGFR1; PlGF-2 also binds neuropilin-1 and neuropilin-2 in a heparin-dependent manner.

PMID:9467961 is the core paper in the seeded GOA rows. It shows that PlGF binds only Flt1, stimulates DNA synthesis in porcine aortic endothelial cells expressing Flt1, activates MAP kinase in those cells, and does not induce migration or PLC-gamma phosphorylation under the same assay conditions. PMID:14684734 adds direct structural support for PlGF bound to VEGFR1 domain 2.

## Peripheral and unresolved rows

PMID:11329059 showed that PlGF loss impairs angiogenesis in pathological models of ischemia, inflammation, wound healing, and cancer; angiogenesis is kept as a broad, non-core vascular outcome of PlGF/FLT1 signaling.

PGF is not a VEGFR2 ligand in the seeded human evidence. The PMID:20660291 row is a generic protein-binding IPI to FLT1 on a Gremlin/VEGFR2 paper whose cached abstract does not mention PlGF; this review modifies that row to the directly supported FLT1/VEGFR1 binding term and records the cached abstract's limitation.

PMID:21215706 links tumor macrophage polarization and vessel normalization to PlGF down-regulation. That establishes a tumor-microenvironment role for PlGF but is not the cleanest support for core FLT1 growth factor activity.
