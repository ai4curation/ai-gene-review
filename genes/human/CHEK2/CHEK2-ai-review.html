Gene Description

CHEK2 encodes CHK2, an FHA-domain-containing protein serine/threonine kinase that couples DNA damage signaling to checkpoint control, substrate stability and apoptotic responses. ATM-dependent priming and regulated self-association promote activation-loop autophosphorylation. Nuclear CHK2 phosphorylates regulatory proteins including checkpoint phosphatases and factors controlling p53 signaling, transcription and DNA-repair pathway choice. Its action is regulatory rather than direct DNA rejoining. PML-associated nuclear pools and context-dependent extranuclear distributions have been reported. Alternative splicing produces proteins with different domain content and activity; the canonical 543-residue enzyme is not representative of every product.
