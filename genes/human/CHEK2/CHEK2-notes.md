# CHEK2 research notes

## 2026-10-10 substantive review

CHEK2 was authenticated as the literal next unchecked catalog entry after CHD8. The HGNC identity, accession, aliases, complete open-PR file/label inventories and publication queue were checked before intake. All work was isolated in TMP. Normal fetch produced 108 source assertions, 13 alternative products and 60 original reference identities. These source objects remain exact; no NEW assertion is proposed. Existing source caches were selected from actual main byte-for-byte, with fresh normal variants archived separately. Falcon timed out and its explicit fallback failed quota authentication; no provider output was fabricated.

The defining activity is human protein serine/threonine kinase activity. The performed signaling work is substrate phosphorylation, coupled to activation through regulated self-association and autophosphorylation. Direct human biochemical experiments and activation-loop structures support one catalytic core. Existing serine-phosphorylation assertions describe a measured component without asserting exclusive serine specificity or tyrosine activity. Dimerization refinements preserve their separate source records and acknowledge the already existing homodimer assertion; they are not NEW coverage.

Product identity requires unusually careful reading. The 543-residue AF096279.1 translation from PMID:10097108 exactly matches O96017-1, despite an upstream reference-line isoform label. AY551303 from PMID:15361853 exactly matches O96017-4, deletion107–197. Its del2–3 construct retained approximately60% of WT activity in a human U2OS immunoprecipitation assay, contradicting the upstream blanket inactivity note. All thirteen product objects and UniProt bytes remain untouched. Neither altered domain content nor one tested construct establishes inactivity of every other product.

The 31 generic binding assertions were reviewed pair by pair using exact PMID/partner joins, original source reads, official partner identities and literal interaction features. A positive pair plus a verified kinase, phosphatase, ATPase, coregulator or ubiquitin-protease partner supports a class refinement without assigning that catalytic activity to CHEK2. PP2A B subunits remain regulatory subunits rather than being relabeled catalytic phosphatases. Viral processed proteins, peptide-only assays and variant features remain explicit. CARF and TNKS1BP1 exact physical-source links remain unresolved; their positive effects on CHK2 phosphorylation are not substituted for the missing pair evidence. No lack of a database hit is treated as proof of nonbinding.

Repair and spindle assertions are refined to performed regulatory work. The BRCA2/RAD51 source supports regulation of homologous-recombination repair; the broader Reactome BRCA1-phosphorylation branch supports regulation of double-strand break repair. The human CHK2–BRCA1 S988 rescue experiments support regulation of mitotic spindle assembly, with intact spindle-checkpoint function. These are source-specific existing assertions, not redundant new processes, and no direct DNA rejoining or motor activity is assigned.

The CDC25A catabolic assertion remains unresolved. The original positive abstract and exact co-IP record are retained, while the full later study PMID:18480045 challenges a general CHK2 requirement for Ser76/SCF-betaTRCP-dependent turnover in HCT116. This does not erase binding, phosphorylation or other substrate-regulatory roles. FOXM1 and STRAP studies independently support regulation of stability. The original E2F1 journal body was not recovered; its author's authentic Glasgow thesis supplies selected kinase, stability and apoptosis experiments, with a more limited reporter interpretation. It is identified as a distinct primary disclosure rather than impersonating journal full text.

The oxidative-stress/autophagy paper PMID:37943659 remains abstract-limited after bounded genuine retrieval attempts. Its positive ATM–CHK2–TRIM32–ATG7 model is preserved, but exact source-dependent stress, starvation and autophagosome annotations remain U pending full assay/species resolution. The underlying Ser/Thr and serine kinase activities are independently established. The telomeric colocalization/senescence source and the inferred cytoplasmic active pool are also left unresolved at their precise scopes, without title-based rejection or donor-count arguments.

Official HPA ICC/IF and an actual Hep-G2 multichannel image support nucleoplasmic localization. Reactome event graphs separate catalytic, recruitment and substrate roles. IBA reviews retain the actual PTN source entities with unresolved node histories; human self-reference among descendants is not circularity. Optional full_text_unavailable flags are omitted because cache availability and actual external access differ; every reference assessment states the inspected scope. Complete primary bodies, selected sections, abstract-only records and failed challenge responses are distinguished in the source artifact.

Two genuine production GO-CAM models, 654d809000001587 and 654d809000001692, positively place CHEK2 kinase activity in the PMID:37943659 autophagy/stress mechanism. Their exact target activity and evidence fields were inspected from authenticated current-main bytes. The unresolved reviews reflect primary assay-access limits, not a claim that these curated models are wrong or that the pathway lacks CHEK2 coverage.
