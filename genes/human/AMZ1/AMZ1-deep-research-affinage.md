# Affinage mechanistic annotation for AMZ1 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

AMZ1 (archaemetzincin-1) is the founding human member of the archaemetzincin family of zinc-dependent metalloproteases within the metzincin superfamily [PMID:15972818]. Recombinant AMZ1 directly hydrolyzes synthetic substrates and bioactive peptides in vitro, an activity abolished by metalloprotease inhibitors, establishing it as a catalytically active enzyme [PMID:15972818]. Its catalytic domain carries the conserved HEXXHXXGX3CX4CXMX17CXXC motif, comprising an archetypal zinc-binding site and the methionine residue diagnostic of metzincins [PMID:15972818]. Beyond this biochemical characterization, the physiological substrates, cellular localization, and pathway context of AMZ1 have not been characterized in the available corpus.
