CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor for catecholamines that couples to
CC       G(s) proteins to activate adenylate cyclase and cAMP-dependent pathway
CC       (PubMed:10212248, PubMed:12391161, PubMed:15358775). Binds epinephrine
CC       and norepinephrine with approximately equal affinity (PubMed:33093660).
CC       Mediates the activation of Ras via binding with cAMP-dependent RAPGEF2
CC       (PubMed:12391161). As part of the sympathetic nervous system, plays a
CC       role in the physiologic regulation of cardiac functions such as
CC       stimulation of cardiomyocyte contraction (PubMed:11052857). Also
CC       delivers pro-apoptotic signals in cardiomyocytes (By similarity).
CC       Involved in the regulation of sleep/wake behaviors (PubMed:11854867,
CC       PubMed:31473062). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P34971,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10212248, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11052857,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11854867, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12391161,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15358775, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31473062,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33093660}.
