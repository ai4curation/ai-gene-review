# CNGA3 review notes

CNGA3 supplies ligand-binding and pore-forming elements of the cone cyclic nucleotide-gated channel. Human recombinant CNGA3 homomers conduct current, while the physiological cone channel combines CNGA3 with CNGB3. Two independent purified human structural studies resolve three alpha subunits and one beta subunit. Full-length detergent structures and engineered truncated nanodisc constructs are distinct preparations, and expression-plasmid ratios are not measurements of subunit stoichiometry ([PMID:34969976](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34969976/), [PMID:37463923](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37463923/)).
