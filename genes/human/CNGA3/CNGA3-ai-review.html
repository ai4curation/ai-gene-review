Gene Description

CNGA3 is the ligand-binding and pore-forming alpha subunit of the cone photoreceptor cyclic nucleotide-gated cation channel. It assembles with CNGB3 in cone outer-segment membranes, where intracellular cGMP promotes cation entry; light-driven loss of cGMP closes the channel and contributes to the cone electrical response. Human reconstituted complexes contain three CNGA3 subunits and one CNGB3. CNGA3 can also form conductive homomers and respond less effectively to cAMP. Biallelic CNGA3 variants cause achromatopsia and related cone dysfunction. Ortholog studies additionally report neuronal, glial and sensory-tissue expression and context-dependent protein interactions.

Core Functions

CNGA3 contributes cGMP-recognition and pore-forming elements to the cone cyclic nucleotide-gated channel. Together with CNGB3 it couples intracellular cGMP to cation flow across outer-segment membranes, supporting the electrical response underlying cone vision. CNGA3 functions within the physiological heteromer and can also form conductive homomeric channels in expression experiments.

Human recombinant CNGA3 homomers conduct current, while the physiological cone channel combines CNGA3 with CNGB3. Two independent purified human structural studies resolve three alpha subunits and one beta subunit.
