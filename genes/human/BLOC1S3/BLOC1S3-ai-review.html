Gene Description

BLOC1S3, also called BLOS3, is a subunit of the eight-protein BLOC-1 complex. This cytoplasmic complex associates with endosomal membranes and supports sorting of membrane cargo toward lysosome-related organelles, including melanosomes and platelet dense granules. BLOS3 participates in the assembled complex, whose integrity and cargo-trafficking function are disrupted by pathogenic BLOC1S3 variants causing Hermansky-Pudlak syndrome type 8. Conserved BLOC-1 activity also supports neuronal membrane-cargo delivery and neurite development.
