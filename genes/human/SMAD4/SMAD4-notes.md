## Core mechanism
SMAD4 is the shared co-SMAD for activin/TGF-beta/Nodal-responsive SMAD2/3 and BMP-responsive SMAD1/5/9. It is not the receptor kinase and does not require the R-SMAD C-terminal activation mechanism. PMID:15350224 resolves two phosphorylated R-SMADs plus one SMAD4; PMID:12374795 cautions that promoter-associated assemblies can vary in stoichiometry. Direct DNA binding, partner/coactivator recruitment, and signal-dependent transcription are core. PMID:31582430 provides direct human SMAD4 DNA-binding measurements alongside SMAD2. PMID:9702198 and PMID:9389648 establish human FAST1/FOXH1-dependent activin/TGF-beta reporter responses. BMP signaling is directly supported rather than excluded by the activin emphasis.

## Structural versus physiological claims
PMID:10647180 describes sulfate ions at an MH2 crystal interface proposed to mimic recognition of R-SMAD phosphoserines. The sulfate-binding annotation is marked over-annotated as a physiological activity. Self-association is retained as peripheral, but the precise homodimer claim remains unresolved because accessible evidence does not settle that stoichiometry. The collagen-binding transfer is retained as an in-vitro MH2/full-length binding observation from PMID:14559231; physiological contact is not established.

## Tissue and signaling context
Directly read donor references support cardiac/endocardial development (PMID:21330551), cardiac remodeling and negative MEK/ERK regulation (PMID:16151019), phenylephrine/BMP-driven hypertrophy (PMID:26873969), pancreatic ERK activation (PMID:11401854), cardiomyocyte apoptosis (PMID:15949472), limb digit chondrogenesis (PMID:23034633), and renal mesenchymal organization (PMID:15342483). Opposite effects in different tissues are preserved rather than averaged into a universal sign. The endoglin-titled PMID:17628518 full text includes conditional Smad4 experiments and outflow septation defects: it is not a wrong-gene source. Mouse/rat annotation records were used only for source tracing, never as biological evidence.

SMAD4-dependent hepcidin transcription links BMP/TGF-beta/IL6 signals to iron homeostasis (PMID:16330325). miR29a transcription (PMID:23426367) is distinct from SMAD4-independent DROSHA processing. SMAD4 restrains WNT output in PC3 cells (PMID:23538390 full text, TOPflash assays) but stabilizes beta-catenin in chondrocytes (PMID:20097766); both contextual roles are retained. Human ECM and EMT studies were read as SMAD4 functional evidence despite their microRNA-focused titles. Gonadotrope SMAD4/FOXL2 loss compromises FSH synthesis (PMID:24739304), supporting an endocrine reproductive role rather than a claimed cell-autonomous ovarian mechanism.
