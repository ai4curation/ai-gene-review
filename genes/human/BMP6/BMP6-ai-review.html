Gene Description

BMP6 is a secreted growth factor of the TGF-beta superfamily. Its processed ligand engages BMP type I and type II receptor complexes to initiate SMAD1/5/8-dependent and context-dependent noncanonical signaling. It promotes osteogenic differentiation and regulates hepatic hepcidin expression, connecting extracellular signaling with systemic iron balance. BMP6 can form an active heterodimer with BMP2 in experimental systems, and erythroferrone limits BMP6-containing ligand activity by binding extracellular ligand. Receptor use and cellular outcomes depend on tissue context.

The review separates its receptor-binding and signaling role from receptor kinase activity, SMAD transcriptional activity, and erythroferrone-mediated ligand sequestration. One core function describes extracellular growth-factor signaling, including hepcidin-dependent systemic iron regulation. Bone, pancreatic, adrenal and vascular responses are recorded as tissue-specific consequences.

The 81 original annotation objects are preserved, including their evidence codes, source references, supporting entities and the cytokine contributes_to qualifier.
