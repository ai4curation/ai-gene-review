# BEST1 review notes

## Biological synthesis

BEST1 supplies the structure of a homopentameric anion channel. Direct human-protein expression and modern structural work establish calcium-sensitive chloride conductance; separate permeability measurements show substantial bicarbonate conductance. These are two activities of the same pore rather than two different assemblies. Human RPE surface labeling, native macaque/porcine tissue staining and expressed-human-protein localization together support the major basolateral RPE location. The species and host-cell contexts remain explicit (PMID:11904445, PMID:18400985, PMID:35789156, PMID:11050159).

Retinal disease mechanisms vary by allele. Some variants reduce current, some affect trafficking, and a studied recessive-disease combination retains anion-channel function. Neither disease causation nor a paper title justifies treating all BEST1 variants as assembly-defective or all bestrophinopathy as complete loss of current (PMID:18179881, PMID:19853238, PMID:21330666, PMID:26200502, PMID:26720466). N/C-terminal self-interaction is experimentally supported but is kept distinct from channel gating and pentamer stoichiometry (PMID:19372599).

## Neural evidence and the unresolved expression discrepancy

The explicit mouse donor O88870 has functional evidence for Best1-dependent astrocytic glutamate release and modulation of postsynaptic NMDA responses. Cell-type-specific relief of silencing restores the relevant hippocampal effect through astrocytes rather than neurons. These are functional tests, not simply a donor count or an antibody image (PMID:23021213, PMID:25645137). Original glial GABA-release experiments and a later astrocyte-specific conditional knockout support context-dependent tonic inhibition (PMID:20929730, PMID:39266474).

An independent study reports very low brain Best1 transcript/protein and no specific astroglial staining with WT/KO comparisons and positive-tissue controls. This is a substantive localization disagreement, but it does not directly repeat all earlier functional release and rescue assays (PMID:32848599). The positive and negative evidence is retained together. The human glutamate/GABA secretion and plasticity annotations remain orthology-supported and non-core; no unmeasured human neural physiology is asserted.

The presynapse inference cannot be justified merely because a glial protein participates in neurotransmitter release. Later, independent mouse cortical work reports BEST1 at glutamatergic and GABAergic terminals with WT/KO comparisons. That evidence supports non-core retention of the existing presynaptic location, while direct human neuronal localization remains unestablished (PMID:39466647). The 2020 negative astroglial observation does not settle this distinct neuronal compartment question.

Human BEST1 experiments add a separate layer: GAD65 modifies permeation, extracellular GABA activates chloride conductance, and human iPSC-RPE cells show BEST1-dependent glutamate currents and GABA influx under imposed ionic conditions. GABA influx in that assay is not GABA secretion, and permeability does not establish physiological flux direction in intact polarized RPE or endogenous human astrocytes (PMID:39277606). No NEW receptor, neurotransmitter process or redundant transport annotation is proposed.
