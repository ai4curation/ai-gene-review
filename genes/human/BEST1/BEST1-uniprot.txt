CC       across cell membranes when activated by calcium (Ca2+)
CC       (PubMed:11904445, PubMed:12907679, PubMed:18179881, PubMed:18400985,
CC       PubMed:19853238, PubMed:21330666, PubMed:26200502, PubMed:26720466,
CC       PubMed:35789156). Allows the movement of chloride and hydrogencarbonate
CC       (PubMed:11904445, PubMed:12907679, PubMed:18179881, PubMed:18400985,
CC       PubMed:19853238, PubMed:21330666, PubMed:26200502, PubMed:26720466,
CC       PubMed:35789156). Found in a partially open conformation leading to
CC       significantly smaller chloride movement (PubMed:35789156). Upon
CC       F2R/PAR-1 activation, the sequestered calcium is released into the
CC       cytosol of astrocytes, leading to the (Ca2+)-dependent release of L-
CC       glutamate into the synaptic cleft that targets the neuronal
CC       postsynaptic GRIN2A/NMDAR receptor resulting in the synaptic plasticity
