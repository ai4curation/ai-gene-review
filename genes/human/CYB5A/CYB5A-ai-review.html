Gene Description

CYB5A encodes cytochrome b5 type A, a small heme-containing electron carrier. Its membrane-bound form is anchored in the endoplasmic reticulum and exposes a cytoplasmic heme domain to redox partners. An alternatively spliced erythrocyte form lacks the membrane anchor and occurs in the cytosol. Cytochrome b5 supports lipid and steroid metabolism and the maintenance of reduced hemoglobin. Human CYB5A enhances CYP17A1 17,20-lyase activity; disruption can cause methemoglobinemia and impaired androgen synthesis. Electron donation and substrate-dependent conformational effects contribute to its interactions with P450 enzymes.

Core Functions

CYB5A relays electrons through its heme to partner redox enzymes. The ER-anchored form presents its heme domain to cytoplasmic partners, while the mature soluble erythrocyte form supports cytosolic redox metabolism. CYP17A1 17,20-lyase enhancement combines electron donation with substrate-dependent conformational effects.
