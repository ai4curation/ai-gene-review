# F9 source guide

Human factor IX is secreted as a zymogen. Activated FIXa supplies intrinsic-tenase protease chemistry, while FVIIIa supplies cofactor activity. This guide distinguishes performed FX activation from FIX being modified, transported or cleaved by another enzyme. The two natural products and source-specific experimental states remain separate. The [campaign curation instructions](../../../projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions) govern reference-only support without new publication quotations.

## Core synthesis

The core combines [serine-type endopeptidase activity](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0004252) with the performed FX activation step of [intrinsic coagulation](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0007597). FIXa performs the cleavage; FVIIIa enhances it. Independent direct peptide hydrolysis in [PMID:34502392](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34502392/) supports an enabled enzyme activity rather than only an accessory contribution. The human pathway substrate is [F10/P00742](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P00742/entry); the separate Nagoya-paper reconstruction uses bovine FX. Upstream processing of FIX itself is not the reason for assigning zymogen activation.

Calcium coordination and peripheral membrane association are integrated structural and assembly properties. The bounded recent exact tenase-complex ontology query returned no identifier; the assembly is described in prose without inventing a GO complex. Biosynthetic compartments, matrix/exosome detections and engineered variants do not become additional core functions. The natural 423-residue product is not assumed equivalent to the displayed 461-residue precursor or its activated chains.

The synthesis used the operative peptide assay in PMID:34502392 (Results 2.3 and Methods 4.5–4.6), the Materials, purification and coupled activity assay plus Results/Figures 6–7 in PMID:2592373, and the Gla-domain Methods, structural Results and Table I in PMID:14722079.
