# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ANKRD13B (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ANKRD13B functions as a molecular scaffold in early EGFR endocytic sorting, recognizing Lys-63-linked ubiquitin chains on ligand-activated EGFR through its UIM domains [PMID:31985874]. It assembles with the RING-finger E3 ligase RNF11 in vivo, an interaction that depends on the ANKRD13B UIM regions but, atypically for UIM-mediated binding, does not require ubiquitination of RNF11 [PMID:31985874]. Together with the related proteins ANKRD13A and ANKRD13D and the E3 ligases ITCH and RNF11, ANKRD13B participates in the early phases of EGFR endocytosis [PMID:31985874]. Beyond this scaffolding role in ubiquitin-dependent EGFR sorting, no further mechanistic detail has been characterized in the available corpus.
