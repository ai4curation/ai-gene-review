# CSN3 (human kappa-casein, P07498) - review notes

## Identity

- Secreted milk glycoprotein, 182 aa precursor, 20 aa signal peptide. Kappa-casein family
  (Pfam PF00997, PANTHER PTHR11470); unlike the calcium-sensitive caseins it is not a
  member of the phosphoserine-cluster casein Pfam family.
- Heavily O-glycosylated in the C-terminal (caseinomacropeptide) region
  [PMID:7460900 "demonstrated the presence of several (up to 10) prosthetic sugar groups distributed throughout the peptide chain instead of only one in the case of cow and sheep kappa-caseinoglycopeptides"].
- UniProt: chymosin cleavage site 117-118 (para-kappa-casein / macropeptide); disulfide-linked
  heteromultimers with alpha-s1-casein (also Reactome R-HSA-5340124).

## Micelle-stabilizing role

- Calcium-insensitive, prevents Ca-induced precipitation of beta-casein
  [PMID:11949848 "Although calcium-insensitive, kappa-CN is known for its role in preventing the precipitation of beta-CN in the presence of Ca+2"].
- Surface location in human micelles [PMID:7979373 "The relative amount of kappa-casein increased as micelle size decreased, and thus as surface area/volume increased. Since kappa-casein also stabilizes the micelles against precipitation by Ca2+ ions, a surface position for most of the kappa-casein is implied."].
- Regulates micelle size [PMID:12906042 "indicating that the kappa-CN probably occupies a surface position and may regulate micelle size by its relative abundance"].
- Brush layer / steric-electrostatic stabilization [PMID:36903269 "In addition, κ-casein forms a type of “brush” on the surface of the micelle that interacts with the whey and ensures electrostatic repulsion between micelles [7]."].
- General mechanism (bovine) [PMID:9839241 "The stability of the casein micelle is dependent on the presence of kappa-casein (CN) on the surface of the micelle where it functions as an interface between the hydrophobic caseins of the micelle interior and the aqueous environment."].

## Genetics (mouse ortholog)

- Csn3 (Csnk) null mice fail to lactate [PMID:16698927 "these females did not suckle their pups and failed to lactate because of destabilization of the micelles in the lumina of the mammary gland. Thus, kappa-casein is essential for lactation"].
  Contrast with Csn2 KO which lactates normally (PMID:8016126).
- Evolution: kappa + beta-like casein are the minimal micelle set [PMID:36903269 "only κ-casein and an ancient β-casein-like protein are obligatory to form stable casein micelles"].

## Glycan-dependent anti-adhesive activity (non-core)

- Purified human kappa-casein inhibited H. pylori adhesion to gastric mucous cells, fucose-dependent
  [PMID:8523212 "Purified human K-casein, in contrast to K-casein purified from bovine milk, effectively inhibited the cell lineage-specific adhesion of fluoroisothiocyanate-labeled Helicobacter pylori to human gastric surface mucous cells."].
  Ex vivo, single study; no GO annotation exists. Recorded as a suggested question rather than NEW.

## Interactome

- Y2H hits (RBPMS, UBQLN1, UBQLN2, VENTX, ZNF76) from CCSB screens; all intracellular /
  nuclear proteins, not plausible partners of a secreted luminal milk protein. No functional
  interpretation.
