# GNB2 source guide

The generic protein-binding term is removed for lack of functional specificity. Positive experimental evidence is retained in each incoming context and in the exact assay/product projections. No partner function is assigned to GNB2 from association alone.

PMID:20139237: Git5-assisted assembly was measured in Saccharomyces with fission-yeast proteins and a Git3 fragment; this was not purified human-complex reconstitution.

PMID:11238401: The authentic abstract reports Git5–Git11 two-hybrid and co-immunoprecipitation evidence. The accessible PMC article is scanned; its full experimental text was not independently read. Yeast N-terminal architecture is not assigned to human GNB2.

PMID:31300275: Retinal beta1–gamma1 was native bovine material; alpha and some complexes used engineered constructs.

PMID:32814053: Human proteins were tested in yeast; partner accessions remain separate, including two UBE2S records for one gene pair.

The evidence JSON preserves the raw rows, target paths, source hashes and assay fields. Construct, host and participant identity remain distinct.

Publication-cache flags describe the normal cache, not every independently accessible original. All nine missing normal records were acquired through the repository helpers, and access flags were reconciled with those records. The 41 pre-existing sources were preserved unchanged.

PMID:23533145: Table 2 reports a three-protein group (P62879;Q9HAV0;P16520). Its peptide counts do not establish GNB2-exclusive detection; individual peptide spectra were not replayed.

PMID:17897319: Original Table 1 reports beta-2 separately from beta4. Contextual association is retained without claiming unique-peptide or sequence-level verification.
