CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mitochondrial carbonic anhydrase that catalyzes the
CC       reversible conversion of carbon dioxide to bicarbonate/HCO3
CC       (PubMed:24530203, PubMed:8356065, PubMed:16807956, PubMed:16686544,
CC       PubMed:17127057, PubMed:17314045, PubMed:19186056, PubMed:19206230).
CC       Mitochondria are impermeable to HCO3, and thus this intramitochondrial
CC       carbonic anhydrase is pivotal in providing HCO3 for multiple
CC       mitochondrial enzymes that catalyze the formation of essential
CC       metabolites of intermediary metabolism in the urea and Krebs cycles
CC       (PubMed:24530203). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16686544,
