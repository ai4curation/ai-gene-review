## Matrix and tissue evidence

[Zhang et al., 1994](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.124.5.855), Methods p. 856 and Figures 5–9, distinguishes bovine microfibril immunogold from human fetal immunostaining. The [2003 coassembly study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11037003_Fibrillins_Can_Co-assemble_in_Fibrils_but_Fibrillin_Fibril_Composition_Displays_Cell-specific_Differences), Figures 5/6/9/10, independently images human FBN2 microfibrils. Elasticity remains a positive matrix-level inference, prioritized below the broader structural role that also covers nonelastic matrix. No isolated-protein recoil measurement is claimed.

[The osteoblast study](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.201003089) uses mouse loss-of-function cultures and recombinant FBN2–LTBP assays (Figure 4). Matrix sequestration regulates signaling; even double-fibrillin-deficient cultures can mineralize. Neither a generic calcium-phosphate scaffold requirement nor human in-vivo perturbation is inferred.

Human tissue localization is positive but spatially bounded: [15277214](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0002-9440(10)63305-6), Results and Figure 3B, directly assays FBN2 in renal GBM despite its FBN1-focused title; [24899048](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddu276), Figure 2, emphasizes the central elastic layer of Bruch membrane, which does not itself prove basal-lamina incorporation. Its generic basement-membrane decision instead relies on the independent renal and other ocular localization evidence; [25406291](https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.14-15453), Figures 2–5, shows stage-specific ocular deposition including Descemet membrane and anterior lens capsule. Eye-process decisions retain a contextual structural/IEP inference and curator deference, not a demonstrated human morphogenetic perturbation. The two eye terms have no verified strict ancestor path. FBN1/2/3 in the eye paper are paralogs, not FBN2 splice isoforms.

## Binding and proteomics

Stable original locators are [12429738](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M209256200), Figures 8–9; [15131124](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M313672200), Figures 2–4; [18339631](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M707820200), Figure 6 and Table 3; [25034023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2014.06.061), Figure 3; and [26601954](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M115.681775), Figure 6. These experiments test recombinant FBN2 regions, with bovine MAGP partners explicitly retained. BMP binding uses prodomains; it does not by itself establish the sign of downstream signaling. B7ZLY3 identifies human LTBP1, without evidence that this exact transcript product was separately tested.

[23658023](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M113.463372), Table 1, reports FBN2 in human HUES1-derived ECM separately from mouse feeder data. [28675934](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jproteome.7b00191), original supplementary workbook pr7b00191_si_002.xlsx, has sole-FBN2/P35556 rows B557,D5844,D6245,D7043; mixed descriptions remain separate. Extraction/MS identifies the matrix compartment, not intact molecular assembly or natural product boundaries.

[28327460](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biomaterials.2017.03.008), main Table I, swaps fibrillin/fibulin names beside FBN/FBLN symbols. The exact FBN2 supplemental accession join remains unresolved. Independent scaffold evidence supports its generic RCA structural claim, but does not reconstruct the particular HDA identification. This is the reason that HDA remains undecided; no misattribution is asserted. [MatrisomeDB 2](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkac1009) describes database methodology, but its FBN2 target page was unavailable. The interstitial-matrix decision instead retains the matching generic claim using direct human connective-tissue imaging and curator deference, without claiming a recovered database hit.

## Placensin and product boundaries

[32329225](https://doi.org/10.15252/embr.201949530), Results/Figures 2–5 and expression/IP-MS Methods, supports a released human C-terminal product and recombinant biological activity. Methods use 2780–2912, whereas Figure 2 caption says 2779–2912. Reviewed UniProt uses 2780–2912; these are not newly mapped endogenous termini. Furin prediction/co-localization is not a cleavage-dependence experiment. Mouse hepatocyte metabolic responses and human trophoblast invasion are distinct from serum pregnancy associations. The truncated 1,473-aa splice product lacks the entire mapped hormone region.
