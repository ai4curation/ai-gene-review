# FBN2 database review notes

The database lane reviews 30 imported contexts in 16 whole source groups: 10 ACCEPT, 10 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, 9 TOO_GENERAL and one MODIFY. All 60 imported source objects and their ordering remain exact. The lane leaves the direct-paper reviews, description, status, products and core synthesis untouched.

The principal source distinctions are mature matrix fibrillin-2 versus the placensin product; human ligand versus mouse recipient experiments; mouse Fbn2 evidence versus Fbn1-specific BMP findings; current family nomenclature versus the actual release-19 topology; and primary observations versus rule or pathway inference. See the source guide and complete structured decision table.

No normal source is rewritten. Eighteen selected existing normals support this lane (six shared publications, nine Reactome records and three family files); all 44 intake normals are preserved, and unused discovery publications remain private. No new normal publication, annotation or quotation is introduced.

The private provenance records preserve the initial failed tree request, failed Ensembl lookup, unsuccessful legacy GO_REF URL attempts and the subsequent successful official GO_REF pages. Those retrieval limits do not silently become scientific negatives. The reference source titles and all seed fields are retained.
