# FBN2 evidence and product scope

Fibrillin-2 has three retained core molecular activities: extracellular matrix structure, calcium binding in its EGF-like domain architecture, and hormone activity of the released C-terminal product placensin. Matrix-chain and hormone functions are distinct. The two original splice records remain unchanged; the shorter 1,473-aa product lacks the mapped placensin interval.

## Matrix organization and regulatory context

Human FBN2 microfibril and coassembly evidence supports a broad structural role, including nonelastic matrices. The calcium-binding assignment follows target-specific EGF-like domain evidence; it does not assert calcium signaling or affinity at every repeat. In mouse osteoblasts, Fbn2-dependent matrix sequestration of latent TGF-β complexes limits signaling. This provides a scaffold mechanism for the retained regulatory process without assigning receptor catalysis or making fibrillin-2 an obligatory mineralization substrate. Bone and eye outcomes remain contextual non-core biology.

The generic basement-membrane location is supported by actual human renal GBM and Descemet/anterior lens-capsule evidence. The separate Bruch membrane study places FBN2 particularly in its central elastic layer; that measurement is not relabeled as RPE or choriocapillaris basal-lamina incorporation. Original source and tissue limits remain visible in each complete reference assessment.

## Placensin and processing limits

The two applied classes use reviewed UniProt chain mappings: matrix fibrillin-2 at 78–2779 and placensin at 2780–2912. These curated coordinates are not newly measured endogenous termini. The placensin paper's Methods use 2780–2912, whereas its Figure 2 caption says 2779–2912; both statements are preserved. Human trophoblast secretion and serum IP-MS support the released product, while recombinant human ligand was tested in mouse metabolic systems and human trophoblast cells. Neither an endogenous human glucose intervention nor hormone output from the truncated splice product is inferred.

Placensin regulates recipient-cell glucose metabolism; those recipient cells execute the glucose-conversion reactions. Furin prediction and co-localization do not establish cleavage necessity, and internal peptides after proteolytic digestion do not map an intact N terminus. The proposed experiment addresses this bounded biological gap with intact-product mapping and controlled furin perturbation. No processing activity is assigned to the FBN2 substrate.

## Why some same-term decisions differ

The unresolved PMID28327460 HDA target identification retains UNDECIDED because the exact supplementary FBN2 accession join was not recovered. Independent microfibril evidence supports its generic structural classification, but cannot reconstruct that particular screen hit. In contrast, actual main-table/workbook records support the other HDA matrix detections. The inverted fibrillin/fibulin names in the source table are not treated as proof of curator error. The broader PAINT ECM inference concerns the inherited matrix form already represented by microfibril, whereas the HDA extracts do not resolve the intact molecular assembly or processing state.

Broad extracellular location remains informative for precursor, placensin, processing and degradation contexts. Mature-microfibril-only Reactome assertions are true but subsumed by the retained microfibril location. These distinctions follow product and claim scope, not a ranking of evidence codes. Broad structural molecular function is retained because it covers nonelastic matrix organization as well as the contextual elasticity role.
