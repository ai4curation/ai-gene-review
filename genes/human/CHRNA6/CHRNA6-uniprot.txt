CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of neuronal acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) that
CC       function as pentameric, ligand-gated cation channels with high calcium
CC       permeability among other activities. nAChRs are excitatory
CC       neurotrasnmitter receptors formed by a collection of nAChR subunits
CC       known to mediate synaptic transmission in the nervous system and the
CC       neuromuscular junction. Each nAchR subunit confers differential
CC       attributes to channel properties, including activation, deactivation
CC       and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, cation permeability, and
CC       binding to allosteric modulators (Probable). CHRNA6 forms pentameric
CC       channels with CHRNB2, CHRNB3 and CHRNA4 that exhibit high sensitivity
CC       to ACh and nicotine and are predominantly expressed in only a few brain
CC       areas, including dopaminergic neurons, norepirephrine neurons and cells
CC       of the visual system (PubMed:16835356). nAChrs containing CHRNA6
CC       subunits mediate endogenous cholinergic modulation of dopamine and
CC       gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) release in response to nicotine at nerve
CC       terminals. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R0W9, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16835356,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:16835356}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by a myriad of ligands such as
CC       acetylcholine, cytisine and nicotine (PubMed:16835356). CHRNA6 nAChR
CC       activity is inhibited by the antagonists alpha-conotoxin MII and PIA, a
CC       small disulfide-constrained peptides from cone snails.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R0W9, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16835356}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Neuronal AChR is composed of two different types of subunits:
CC       alpha and non-alpha (beta). CHRNA6/alpha-6 subunit can be combined to
CC       CHRNB2/beta-2, CHRNA4/alpha-4 and CHRNB3/beta-3 to give rise to
CC       functional receptors (PubMed:16835356). Heteropentamers containing
CC       CHRNB3 have an stoichiometry of (CHRNA6:CHRNB2)2:CHRNB3
CC       (PubMed:16835356). Interacts with LYPD6 (PubMed:27344019).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R0W9, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16835356,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27344019}.

CC       {ECO:0000255}.
