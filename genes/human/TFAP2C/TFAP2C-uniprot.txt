CC   -!- FUNCTION: Sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factor that
CC       interacts with cellular enhancer elements to regulate transcription of
CC       selected genes, and which plays a key role in early embryonic
CC       development (PubMed:11694877, PubMed:24413532). AP-2 factors bind to
CC       the consensus sequence 5'-GCCNNNGGC-3' and activate genes involved in a
CC       large spectrum of important biological functions (PubMed:11694877,
CC       PubMed:24413532). TFAP2C plays a key role in early embryonic
CC       development by regulating both inner cell mass (ICM) and trophectoderm
CC       differentiation (By similarity). At the 8-cell stage, during morula
CC       development, controls expression of cell-polarity genes (By
CC       similarity). Upon trophoblast commitment, binds to late trophectoderm
CC       genes in blastocysts together with CDX2, and later to extra-embryonic
CC       ectoderm genes together with SOX2 (By similarity). Binds to both closed
CC       and open chromatin with other transcription factors (By similarity).
CC       Involved in the MTA1-mediated epigenetic regulation of ESR1 expression
CC       in breast cancer (PubMed:24413532). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q61312,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11694877, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24413532}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Binds DNA as a dimer (PubMed:11694877). Can form homodimers or
CC       heterodimers with other AP-2 family members (PubMed:11694877).
CC       Interacts with WWOX (PubMed:15548692). Interacts with UBE2I
CC       (PubMed:12072434). Interacts with KCTD1; this interaction represses
CC       transcription activation (PubMed:19115315). Interacts with CITED2 (via
CC       C-terminus); the interaction stimulates TFAP2B-transcriptional activity
CC       (PubMed:11694877, PubMed:12586840). Interacts with CITED4
CC       (PubMed:11744733). Interacts with MTA1 (PubMed:24413532).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11694877, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11744733,
