# TFAP2C (AP-2 gamma, Q92754) — curation notes

Project: NEURAL_CREST_ORIGINS, Tier 4 (missing module members). Review date 2026-10-08.

## Identity and molecular activity

- AP-2 family sequence-specific TF; binds GCC(N3)GGC as homo- or heterodimer via the basic region + helix-span-helix; N-terminal PY/Pro-rich transactivation domain. Originally purified as ERF-1 from MCF-7 cells, binding the ESR1 promoter [PMID:9113991 "This cDNA encodes a protein of 48 kDa previously identified as the AP2gamma transcription factor"; "ERF-1 activates ER gene transcription by binding to the imperfect palindrome CCCTGCGGGG within the promoter of the ER gene"].
- Activator and promoter-specific repressor: AP-2gamma represses Mn-SOD (SOD2) [PMID:11278550 "AP-2alpha and AP-2gamma were more active in this regard than AP-2beta"; "Repression of Mn-SOD by AP-2 was dependent on DNA binding"] while activating c-erbB-3 in the same assay ["another AP-2-responsive gene, c-erbB-3, was transactivated by AP-2"].
- Genome-wide in MCF-7: ChIP-seq motif SCCTSRGGS; primary targets both induced and repressed [PMID:20629094 "a conservative estimate that 8% of genes altered by TFAP2C expression are primary target genes and includes genes that are both induced and repressed by TFAP2C"].
- Cofactors: CITED2 coactivates all AP-2 isoforms and binds the TFAP2C dimerization domain [PMID:11694877 "CITED2 interacts with and co-activates all isoforms of transcription factor AP-2 (TFAP2)"; PMID:12586840 "CITED2 interacted with the dimerization domain of TFAP2C"]. CITED2 with TFAP2C transactivates PITX2C [PMID:22735262 "cooperation between CITED2 and transcription factor AP2-gamma (TFAP2C) in the transactivation of the PITX2C gene"]. KCTD1 represses all three major AP-2s [PMID:19115315 "KCTD1 interacted with three major members of the AP-2 family and inhibited their transcriptional activities"]. SUMOylation via UBC9 dampens activity [PMID:12072434 "sumolation of AP-2 decreases its transcription activation potential"]. MTA1/NuRD complex on the ESR1 promoter [PMID:24413532 "the transcription factor AP-2γ (TFAP2C) and the IFN-γ-inducible protein 16 (IFI16) were components of the complex"; "in MCF7, TFAP2C activated the reporter encoding ERpro315"]. WWOX retains TFAP2C in the cytosol (Reactome R-HSA-8864569).

## Developmental roles (synthesis)

1. **Trophectoderm / placenta (core, conserved in mammals).** Mouse Tfap2c null dies ~E7.5 from extra-embryonic failure [PMID:26811378 "Loss of TFAP2C in mouse leads to developmental defects in the extra-embryonic compartment with lethality at embryonic day (E)7.5"]. In totipotent mouse embryos it binds/activates early TE and ICM genes, then late TE genes with CDX2 [PMID:38243114 "TFAP2C, a trophoblast regulator, bound and activated both early TE and inner cell mass (ICM) genes at the totipotent (two- to eight-cell) stages"]. Human TSC to EVT: early-stage TF that pre-occupies EVT genes [PMID:38346993 "TFAP2C pre-occupies the regulatory elements of the inactive extravillous trophoblast-active genes during the early stage of differentiation"]. Mouse Tfap2c carries GO:0001829 trophectodermal cell differentiation by IMP (QuickGO, PMID:20404091, PMID:38243114); human TFAP2C does not -> NEW GO:0001829 (ISS).
2. **Primordial germ cells (core).** Human: TFAP2C-null hESCs cannot make hPGCLCs; TFAP2C acts upstream of PRDM1 to induce SOX17 and blocks somatic fates [PMID:31875561 "TFAP2C functions in the TFAP2A+ progenitors upstream of PRDM1 to regulate the expression of SOX17"; "This serves to protect hPGCLCs from crossing the Weismann's barrier to adopt somatic cell fates"]; binds the OCT4 naive enhancer [PMID:30590035 "deleting the TFAP2C-bound naive enhancer at the OCT4 locus (also called POU5F1) results in impaired OCT4 expression and a negative effect on hPGCLC identity"]; GATA3/2 + SOX17 + TFAP2C suffice to induce hPGCLCs [PMID:33608411 "GATA3 or GATA2, immediate BMP effectors, combined with SOX17 and TFAP2C, generated hPGCLCs"]. Mouse: PGCs specified but lost by E8.0 in Tfap2c mutants [PMID:19776388 "In the mutant animals, PGCs were specified but were lost around E 8.0."]. Comparator: mouse Prdm1 carries GO:0007281 germ cell development (IGI); mouse Tfap2c carries GO:0030718 (IMP); human PRDM1/SOX17 do not (QuickGO 2026-10-08) -> NEW GO:0007281 (IMP, human data).
3. **Epidermis / mammary / cortex (non-core, mostly redundant with TFAP2A).** Mouse Tfap2a/Tfap2c double epidermal knockout [PMID:18824566 "By conditionally ablating two highly expressed transcription factors, AP-2alpha and AP-2gamma, we unmasked functional redundancies"]; cortical basal progenitors [PMID:19749747 "AP2gamma directly regulated the basal progenitor fate determinants Math3 (also known as Neurod4) and Tbr2"]. Not added as NEW; noted in questions.
4. **Breast cancer.** ESR1/FOXA1/RET regulation [PMID:20629094]; luminal identity [PMID:24835590 "The TFAP2C/AP-2γ transcription factor regulates luminal breast cancer genes"]. Disease context, captured by MF terms only.

## Neural crest: network layer

- Chick (Rothstein & Simoes-Costa 2020, full text): TFAP2C is at the border during gastrula, then lost from crest and restricted to epidermis [PMID:31848212 "although TFAP2C was enriched at the neural plate border, it was lost in the neural crest lineage at later stages and its expression became restricted to the presumptive epidermis"]. Knockdown lowers border genes only [PMID:31848212 "TFAP2C knockdown during induction resulted in a down-regulation of the neural plate border markers MSX1, PAX7, ZIC1, and GBX2 but had no significant effects on the expression of specification genes FOXD3, ETS1, SOX9, and SOX10"]. CUT&RUN: 5863 of 7021 TFAP2C peaks co-occupied with TFAP2A at HH6; co-IP of TFAP2A with TFAP2C in embryo. TFAP2A/C peaks in the TFAP2B locus (feed-forward), and TFAP2B represses TFAP2C cell-autonomously [PMID:31848212 "Thus, TFAP2B-mediated repression of TFAP2C likely drives the assembly of TFAP2A/B heterodimers."]. TFAP2C cannot substitute for TFAP2B in specification [PMID:31848212 "overexpression of TFAP2C does not adequately rescue the expression of specification genes"].
- Zebrafish (Li & Cornell 2007, full text): tfap2c alone has no visible phenotype; tfap2a+tfap2c together abolish crest induction, cell-autonomously, during gastrula [PMID:17258188 "simultaneous inhibition of tfap2a and tfap2c utterly prevents neural crest induction"; "Because tfap2c expression is rapidly down regulated in neural crest, Tfap2a/c function in neural crest induction must occur during gastrula stage."]. Border markers pax3/msxb/zic2b were normal in the double, placing Tfap2a/c "in parallel to or possibly downstream of Pax3, Zic2b, and Msxb, and upstream of Foxd3, Sox9, Sox10, and Snai1b".
- **Synthesis.** The two species disagree on exact epistasis relative to the other border genes (chick: upstream of MSX1/PAX7/ZIC1/GBX2; zebrafish: parallel/downstream of pax3/msxb/zic2b) but agree on what matters for GO: TFAP2C acts only at gastrula/border-induction stage, is gone from the crest before specification, is redundant with TFAP2A, and does not drive specification itself. That is the border layer, so **GO:0014029 neural crest formation** (the term defined as forming the ectodermal border region), not GO:0014034 or GO:0014036. TFAP2C is a DNA-binding TF co-occupying border enhancers with TFAP2A, so it passes the participation test (it does part of the work). Human evidence is absent (no human border model), so evidence code ISS from chick/zebrafish orthologs, matching TFAP2A.
- **Comparator check (QuickGO, 2026-10-08,** NC branch GO:0014029/14030/14031/14032/14033/14034/14036/0001755 with descendants, taxa 9606, 10090, 10116, 9031, 8355, 8364, 7955): zebrafish tfap2c (Q5PQZ4 and TrEMBL isoforms) carries GO:0014032 IGI (PMID:17258188, PMID:17724731, PMID:22241841) and GO:0014036 IGI (PMID:20885782); zebrafish tfap2a carries GO:0014032 IGI and GO:0014036 IMP/IGI; mouse Tfap2a GO:0014032 IMP (PMID:8622766), rat Tfap2a ISO. No TFAP2B, TFAP2C, TFAP2D or TFAP2E in human, mouse, rat, chicken or Xenopus carries any NC term; mouse Tfap2c (Q61312) has none (its null dies before crest forms). So the absence on human TFAP2C is a coverage gap, not a convention; the zebrafish ortholog's terms show GO accepts TFAP2C in crest processes. The zebrafish GO:0014036 is from a double knockdown (IGI), consistent with redundancy; the chick data argue that TFAP2C itself does not act at the specification step, so GO:0014036 is not transferred.
- **Module placement.** Border specification part, as the TFAP2A partner: TFAP2A–TFAP2C heterodimer annoton (GO:0001228, GO:0014029). Edges: TFAP2A/C -> TFAP2B (activation, CUT&RUN peaks in TFAP2B locus); TFAP2B -| TFAP2C (repression, cell-autonomous, likely direct). TFAP2C should not enter the specification part.

## Evolution

- Amphioxus has a single AP-2 expressed in non-neural ectoderm only (TFAP2A review, PMID:12397104), so AP-2's border role is a vertebrate co-option. Rothstein & Simoes-Costa note TFAP2C/TFAP2B expression lies within TFAP2A's domain, "suggesting subfunctionalization of these paralogs over the course of evolution" [PMID:31848212]. TFAP2C kept the ancestral non-neural-ectoderm/epidermis expression and took on the gastrula border-induction share; TFAP2B took the crest-specification share. Mammals additionally use TFAP2C in trophectoderm and germline (placental mammal-specific deployments).
