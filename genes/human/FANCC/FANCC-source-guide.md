# FANCC source guide

Human FANCC is a noncatalytic constituent of the nuclear Fanconi anemia repair complex. The source evidence supports physical complex participation, interstrand cross-link repair, and contextual nonnuclear roles. It does not establish every molecular activity performed by the complex as a FANCC activity. This guide brings together target-specific original experiments, inherited phylogenetic assertions, localization and reaction models, and their distinct limits.

## Core mechanism and the inherited adaptor proposal

The final core retains repair-complex assembly and ICL repair, with nucleoplasm and FA nuclear-complex membership. Human nuclear immunoprecipitation and recombinant human CEF-module work are positive physical evidence. The latter uses Xenopus ID2 and does not isolate FANCC's particular contribution to ubiquitin transfer. Current [GO:0030674](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0030674) explicitly directs complex-scaffold roles to [GO:0140378](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0140378); that distinction does not itself prove that FANCC performs the more specific hold-together step. The inherited adaptor source remains UNDECIDED, and the core omits both adaptor MF and contributes-to ligase rather than silently retaining the old stronger mechanism. This does not deny a structural role for FANCC.

[GO:0036297 ICL repair](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0036297) and [GO:0065003 complex assembly](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0065003) are not ancestors of one another under the saved current is_a/part_of closure. Their inclusion represents the supported repair role and physical assembly contribution, not two independent catalytic activities. [GO:0043240](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0043240) is a protein complex and is represented separately from nucleoplasm. The exact C-specific chromatin IDA remains unresolved; nuclear-complex isolation alone does not resolve chromatin fractionation.

The inherited core quotations are preserved literally. The FANCE-binding and FAA/FAC-association statements support partner association; the E3-ligase quotation describes the complex. They are not newly interpreted as isolated FANCC catalysis or a directly demonstrated FANCC bridge to ID2. The same-role comparator checks and fixed-main GO-CAM index from the accepted direct review remain bounded negative searches, not proof of a universal convention or a missing annotation.

Its old quotation remains contextually interpreted and exact. No fixed whole-complex stoichiometry or isolated FANCC ligase activity is asserted.

## Conserved structural evidence and its revision

[PMID:31666700](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6858856/) describes recombinant full-length chicken subunits expressed in insect cells. Selected Results, cloning/purification and ubiquitination Methods, and Extended Data Figures 1, 3 and 8 captions were independently read. Crosslinking places FANCC near FANCL and FANCE. Whole CEF omission/add-back is not a FANCC-only perturbation, and purified CEF did not readily reassociate with the symmetric subcomplex. This does not demonstrate a fixed human complex stoichiometry or isolated C-mediated recruitment.

[PMID:32939280](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7467173/) Methods and Results §§3.3–3.4 model the prior chicken data, not a second independent human experiment. The later model assigns a region previously attributed to FANCC/FANCE to FANCG. It nevertheless supports a modeled C–E interface and maps a human residue-295 variant to that interface. The mapping predicts a possible defect; no human mutant assay was executed there. Crosslinks used during model building are not independent validation. No new figure pixels, raw maps, coordinates, deposited-model reanalysis or separate structural supplement are claimed here.
