Gene Description

ANKRD17 is a large ankyrin-repeat protein with a type-1 KH domain that occupies nuclear and cytoplasmic pools. Its chromatin-associated pool supports DNA-replication and G1/S cell-cycle regulation and interacts with cyclin E/CDK2 and replication factors. In innate immune signaling, ANKRD17 promotes association of the RNA sensors RIG-I and MDA5 with MAVS and supports NOD1/NOD2-dependent inflammatory responses. It is also implicated in YAP protein stability. Heterozygous loss of function causes a neurodevelopmental syndrome characterized by intellectual disability, speech delay and dysmorphic features.

Core Functions
