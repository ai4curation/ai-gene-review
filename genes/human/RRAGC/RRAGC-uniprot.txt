CC   -!- FUNCTION: Guanine nucleotide-binding protein that plays a crucial role
CC       in the cellular response to amino acid availability through regulation
CC       of the mTORC1 signaling cascade (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:24095279,
CC       PubMed:27234373, PubMed:31601708, PubMed:31601764, PubMed:32612235,
CC       PubMed:34071043, PubMed:36697823, PubMed:37057673). Forms heterodimeric
CC       Rag complexes with RagA/RRAGA or RagB/RRAGB and cycles between an
CC       inactive GTP-bound and an active GDP-bound form: RagC/RRAGC is in its
CC       active form when GDP-bound RagC/RRAGC forms a complex with GTP-bound
CC       RagA/RRAGA (or RagB/RRAGB) and in an inactive form when GTP-bound
CC       RagC/RRAGC heterodimerizes with GDP-bound RagA/RRAGA (or RagB/RRAGB)
CC       (PubMed:24095279, PubMed:31601708, PubMed:31601764, PubMed:32868926).
CC       In its GDP-bound active form, promotes the recruitment of mTORC1 to the
CC       lysosomes and its subsequent activation by the GTPase RHEB
CC       (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:24095279, PubMed:27234373, PubMed:32612235,
CC       PubMed:36697823). This is a crucial step in the activation of the MTOR
CC       signaling cascade by amino acids (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:24095279,
CC       PubMed:27234373). Also plays a central role in the non-canonical mTORC1
CC       complex, which acts independently of RHEB and specifically mediates
CC       phosphorylation of MiT/TFE factors TFEB and TFE3: GDP-bound RagC/RRAGC
CC       mediates recruitment of MiT/TFE factors TFEB and TFE3 (PubMed:32612235,
CC       PubMed:36697823). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20381137,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24095279, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27234373,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31601708, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31601764,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32612235, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32868926,

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms a heterodimer with RRAGA, in a sequence-independent
CC       manner, and RRAGB (PubMed:11073942, PubMed:14660641, PubMed:32868926).
CC       Heterodimerization stabilizes proteins of the heterodimer
CC       (PubMed:11073942). The GDP-bound form of RRAGC (in complex with the
CC       GTP-bound form of RRAGA or RRAGB), interacts with RPTOR, thereby
CC       promoting recruitment of mTORC1 to the lysosomes (PubMed:18497260,
CC       PubMed:31601708, PubMed:31601764). Component of the lysosomal
CC       folliculin complex (LFC), composed of FLCN, FNIP1 (or FNIP2),
CC       RagA/RRAGA or RagB/RRAGB GDP-bound, RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD GTP-bound,
CC       and Ragulator (PubMed:31672913, PubMed:31704029, PubMed:32868926).

CC       interaction with this negative regulator is most probably direct,
CC       preferentially occurs with the inactive GDP-bound form of RRAGB, is
CC       negatively regulated by amino acids and prevents interaction with RPTOR
CC       (PubMed:22575674). The Rag heterodimer interacts with SLC38A9; the
