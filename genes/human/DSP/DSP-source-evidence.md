## Binding records and class grounding

[PMID:11790773](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M108765200) was recovered as an indexed original through [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11567404_Protein_binding_and_functional_characterization_of_plakophilin_2_Evidence_for_its_diverse_roles_in_desmosomes_and_-catenin_signaling), while its normal cache remains abstract-only. The verified PKP2 class chain is human Q99959 GO:0005882 ISS annotation 328040827 → mouse Q9CQ73; rat F1M7L9 ISO annotation 57565728 → MGI:1914701 → the same Q9CQ73 mouse gene. The refinement does not assert DSP kinase activity or direct contact proved by purified reconstitution.

The [18474624](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18474624/) original Figure 3/Results and Methods show PKP2-dependent PKC-alpha/DSP co-IP. The imported bovine PKC-alpha accession remains exact: human host cells and a separately specified human retroviral construct do not establish the species of every FLAG reagent. ## Contextual mechanisms

[31595153](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31595153/) supplies actual human nuclear imaging/fractionation, ChIP/FISH, TRF2 association/dependence and perturbation/rescue observations. These are positive contextual results. They do not establish purified DNA binding, a telomere enzyme or the proposed lamina mechanism. The [28891468](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28891468/) original supplies positive DSP/COPS3 association and DSP-dependent EGFR handling. A negative DSP-NTP binary test does not negate other constructs; COPS3 is noncatalytic and COP9 catalytic activity is not assigned to DSP. Much of the differentiation/rescue analysis perturbs COPS3 rather than DSP.

The [17475244](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17475244/) abstract directly reports DSP depletion, increased human HaCaT proliferation/G1–S progression and altered signaling/protein levels. It does not expose the full target-specific differentiation assay. That precise assertion stays undecided; positive proliferation and independently corroborated stabilization contexts are retained.

## Specificity and access audit

[10908733](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0014-5793(00)01806-8) was recovered as an indexed [author original](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/12410196_Small_proline-rich_protein_1_is_the_major_component_of_cell_envelope_of_normal_human_oral_keratinocytes) beyond its abstract cache. Actual resistant-envelope preparation and Table 2 identify a DSP-derived peptide; transglutaminases perform cross-linking. No isolated joined DSP peptide or DSP catalytic/template/cofactor step was demonstrated. The envelope and differentiation contexts remain positive, while assigning cross-link formation itself is excessive.

[12373648](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC385098/) was recovered in full beyond its abstract cache. All operative Methods/Results/discussion and figure/table text were read, without image interpretation. The human family ECG/Holter, angiographic and genetic results are real. Exact current GO definitions additionally require His–Purkinje-cell attachment/binding or ventricular-cell action-potential modulation, which this study does not measure. These are over-specific annotation interpretations, not false patient data or a negative claim about DSP electrical roles.

Other source-specific full-text gaps are retained explicitly in the YAML reference assessments: 10852826, 16917092, 18343367, 19136012, 21756917, 22781308, 22889254, 23381804 and 23444369. Where independently recovered target evidence establishes the same gene-level claim, curator-supported assertions are retained. Where the exact claim cannot be resolved, UNDECIDED is used. Abstracts from donor studies 11781569, 12951053, 16406610, 25496840 and 35119382 are not described as full-paper reads.

The normal machine cache and its access flags remain unchanged even where a separate genuine original or supplement was recovered. The source guide is a route to that evidence, not an authored replacement for the cited publications.
