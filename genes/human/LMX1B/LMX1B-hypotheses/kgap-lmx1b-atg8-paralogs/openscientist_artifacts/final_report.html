LMX1B and the six human ATG8 paralogs: can LIR-motif determinants narrow the set?

Gene: human LMX1B (UniProt O60663, NCBITaxon:9606) Hypothesis slug: kgap-lmx1b-atg8-paralogs Source: genes/human/LMX1B/LMX1B-ai-review.yaml, focus type free_text Verdict: partially supported, with the central paralog-narrowing claim refuted on its own terms

Summary

LIR-motif determinants cannot discriminate among the six human ATG8 paralogs for LMX1B. The seed hypothesis anticipated this outcome as the expected result if the interface is non-canonical, and that is what the analysis finds. Residues 308-317 are SYTPLAPPQQ and contain exactly one LIR-like core, Y309-T310-P311-L312. It satisfies the bare aromatic/hydrophobic consensus [W/F/Y]-X1-X2-[I/L/V] and fails the curated canonical definition at a single position: X2 is proline, a residue that appears in 0 of 60 cores parsed from ELM-validated LIR instances and that the official ELM regex excludes explicitly. The same core fails the GABARAP interaction motif [W/F]-[V/I]-X2-V at all three defining positions. The ELM server, run independently on O60663, predicts no human-applicable LIR class inside 308-317, and places its only unfiltered canonical LIR predictions at 106-113 and 353-362.

The structural side of the question gives the same answer from a different direction. Contact mapping of 29 human ATG8-peptide complexes at a 4.5 Å heavy-atom cutoff identifies 13 LIR-docking-site positions that read the LIR core. Eight are identical in all six paralogs (I23, P32, V33, K49, K51, L53, R70, F108 in LC3B numbering), and the five that vary do so conservatively (D19/E17/D25, H27/Y25/F33, K30/R28/K36, I34/I32/V40, F52/Y49/F58). The LDS residues that actually differ between paralogs sit on the surfaces that read the flanks, not the core: the N-terminal arm (positions 2, 3, 10, 11), T50 versus K47/R47, V58 versus L55/I55, L63 versus F60, K65 versus F62/W62. Those surfaces are read by an acidic upstream extension in LC3-selective LIRs and by a GIM in GABARAP-selective ones. The LMX1B element has an upstream flank of EGMMAS with no acidic cluster and a downstream flank of APPQQQ, and fails the GIM outright. There is nothing in this motif for the discriminating surface to read, so every paralog is equally compatible with it. No paralog has incompatible pocket residues.

Only abundance narrows the set. In GTEx v8 substantia nigra, GABARAP is at 253.1 median TPM, GABARAPL2 119.1, MAP1LC3A 91.7, GABARAPL1 70.5, MAP1LC3B 69.4, and MAP1LC3C 0.164, which is 1542-fold below GABARAP and at background in every brain region tested. Tissue identity is confirmed by TH at 45.8 TPM and LMX1B at 1.88 TPM, 27- to 160-fold above other brain regions. The plausible endogenous partners are therefore GABARAP, GABARAPL2, MAP1LC3A, GABARAPL1 and MAP1LC3B, indistinguishable from one another on motif grounds; MAP1LC3C is the single paralog that can be set aside, and it is set aside on expression, not on sequence. Two further results qualify the motif assignment itself. The Δ308-317 deletion also removes a Pin1 docking site, a proline-directed kinase site at T310-P311, a class II PxxP and a YxxΦ sorting signal, so the transcriptional phenotype does not isolate ATG8 binding. And a stronger canonical LIR candidate, F356-H357-D358-I359 in the acidic, disordered DSIFHDIDSD, is 97-100% invariant across 151 vertebrate LMX1B entries, lies 39 residues outside the deleted window, and has never been tested.

Executive judgment

Partially supported. The hypothesis is well posed and the analysis it requests is answerable, but the proposition that LIR determinants can narrow the paralog set is false for this motif. The hypothesis itself names this possibility, so the correct reading is that the seed question is resolved rather than left open.

The reasoning rests on three results, each computed in this investigation:

  1. The motif is non-canonical at exactly one position, and that position is the one that matters least for paralog choice. Proline at X2 disqualifies YTPL from the canonical definition, but X2 is read by L53 (invariant), K30/R28 (conservative charge-identical substitution) and H27/Y25/F33 (aromatic or imidazole in all six). A non-canonical X2 therefore predicts weak binding to all six, not selective binding to some.
  2. The LDS floor that reads a LIR core is the same protein in all six paralogs. 8 of 13 core-reading positions are strictly identical, and the remaining 5 preserve charge or aromaticity. The pocket cannot distinguish paralogs for this ligand.
  3. Expression discriminates, and only at one paralog. MAP1LC3C is effectively absent from human brain. Everything else is abundant.

The most important caveats, in order of how much they should change a curator's reading:

  • The ten deleted residues carry at least four other predicted linear motifs. The published Δ308-317 transcriptional phenotype is consistent with ATG8 loss, with loss of a proline-directed phosphorylation switch, or with destabilization of a disordered activation segment. The deletion does not attribute the phenotype to ATG8 binding.
  • A better canonical LIR candidate exists outside the tested window (F356-H-D-I), and it is the one candidate whose acidic flanks could in principle confer paralog preference.
  • No structure of LMX1B bound to any ATG8 exists, and none was built here. All structural statements in this report are contact mapping from other peptides' complexes, stated as such.
  • GTEx substantia nigra is bulk post-mortem tissue RNA standing in for sorted midbrain dopaminergic neurons, which were not accessible programmatically from this environment. It establishes presence or absence, not neuron-intrinsic stoichiometry and not protein abundance.

Key findings

F001. Residues 308-317 contain exactly one LIR-like core, and it fails the canonical consensus at X2

An exhaustive regex scan of all 402 residues of human LMX1B for the core LIR consensus [WFY]xx[ILV] returns three matches in the entire protein and no others: Y107-Q-Q-L in the context LYCKQD-YQQL-FAAKCS, Y309-T-P-L in the context EGMMAS-YTPL-APPQQQ, and F356-H-D-I in the context YGNDSI-FHDI-DSDTSL. The deleted segment 308-317 is SYTPLAPPQQ and contains exactly one of the three, with the aromatic at position 309 and the hydrophobic at position 312.

Scoring that core against the official ELM regexes, fetched from elm.eu.org/elms/elms_index.tsv:

SYTPL fails LIG_LIR_Gen_1 at one position only. The upstream [EDST] anchor is satisfied by S308, the aromatic by Y309 and the hydrophobic by L312, but P311 occupies X2 and the canonical pattern excludes it with [^PG]. This is not a quirk of one regex. Among 60 ELM-curated, experimentally validated LIR instances whose core could be parsed from the UniProt sequence, the observed X2 composition is E 12, I 8, L 8, F 6, T 5, M 4, V 4, A 2, D 2, N 2, R 2, and Q, C, S, H, K at 1 each. Proline at X2: 0 of 60.

The same core also fails the GABARAP interaction motif [W/F]-[V/I]-X2-V (PMID: 28655748) at all three defining positions: Theta is Y, not W or F; X1 is T, not V or I; Gamma is L, not V. The motif therefore carries no GABARAP-selective signature.

Structural context from AlphaFold DB model AF-O60663-F1 (v6): mean pLDDT over 308-317 is 45.7, against 89.2 for the homeodomain (215-275) and 85.2 for the LIM region (40-160). The motif lies in a disordered segment, which is what a functional short linear motif requires. The same model eliminates Y107 from consideration: its pLDDT is 89.9 and its mean heavy-atom contact number is 15.8 against a protein median of 10, so it is folded and buried inside the LIM2 zinc finger and not available for docking.

The authors of the primary study describe the element in the same terms. The commentary states that "LMX1B possesses a non-canonical LC3-interacting region (LIR) in its C-terminus through which it interacts with ATG8 family members" (PMID: 37312406). The X2-proline analysis supplies the residue-level reason for that description.

F002. The LDS residues that read the LIR core are invariant across all six paralogs

Thirty-eight PDB entries listed as experimental support for ELM LIR instances were downloaded and parsed. Twenty-nine contained a human ATG8 chain (identity ≥ 0.90 to a human paralog) bound to a LIR peptide of ≤ 40 residues: MAP1LC3A (4 complexes, FYCO1), MAP1LC3B (6, OPTN/ANK3/ANK2), MAP1LC3C (8, PLEKHM1), GABARAP (7, WDFY3/ANK2/STX17) and GABARAPL1 (4, NBR1/ANK3/ATG16L1-type). Heavy-atom contacts within 4.5 Å were computed for each LIR peptide position, then mapped onto all six paralogs through BLOSUM62 global alignments (identity to LC3B: LC3A 0.82, LC3C 0.53, GABARAPL2 0.39, GABARAP 0.32, GABARAPL1 0.32). No GABARAPL2-peptide complex exists in the PDB, so GABARAPL2 was mapped by alignment only; this is stated rather than papered over.

Of 40 LDS positions contacted in ≥ 2 complexes, 12 are identical in all six paralogs, and those 12 include the HP1 and HP2 floor: I23, P32, V33, D48, K49, K51, L53, V54, P55, I67, R70, F108 (LC3B numbering).

Every substitution that reaches the core preserves charge or aromaticity. Every substitution that genuinely changes chemistry (T50→K47/R47, K65→F62/W62, L63→F60, and the N-terminal arm) acts on the flanks. That split is the structural basis of the known LC3-versus-GABARAP selectivity rule: LC3 paralogs prefer an acidic upstream extension read by the R10/R11 arm, and GABARAPs prefer a GIM read by the K46/K48 surface and the hydrophobic 55/60/62 patch.

Applied to LMX1B: the upstream flank is EGMMAS, with no D or E at positions −1 to −3, and the downstream flank is APPQQQ, proline-rich and neutral. The motif presents neither selectivity signal. LIR determinants cannot discriminate among the six human ATG8 proteins for LMX1B, and no paralog has incompatible pocket residues. This is consistent with the reported binding of tagged LMX1B to all six.

PMID: 28655748 supports this reading from two directions. It states plainly that "how these adaptors/receptors selectively interact with either LC3 or GABARAP families remains unclear", and it shows experimentally that "selective mutation of the X1 and X2 positions either completely abolished the interaction with all LC3 and GABARAPs or increased PLEKHM1-GIM selectivity 20-fold towards LC3B". X1 and X2 identity drives selectivity, and the LMX1B pair (T, P) matches neither the LC3-selective nor the GABARAP-selective combination.

The exact human window EGMMASYTPLAPPQQ occurs in 90 of 120 entries, spanning primates, rodents, carnivores, ungulates and marsupials. Gallus gallus has EGMMTSYTPLAPPQQ, Xenopus tropicalis EGMMTSYAPLAPPQQ, Danio rerio lmx1bb EGMMNSYTPLAPAQQQ and Salmo trutta EGMMNSFTPLAPPQQ. Two things follow. The proline at X2 has been fixed for at least 450 million years of vertebrate evolution, so the element is under selection. Selection does not establish what is being selected for: YTPLAPP also reads as a proline-rich segment compatible with SH3, WW and proline-directed kinase chemistries. And the human LMX1A core Y290-T-A-L also fails the canonical ELM regex, but for a different reason: its upstream residues are M-N-P at −3 to −1, so there is no [EDST] anchor, whereas LMX1B has S308 at −1 but P311 at X2. Both paralogs are reported to bind ATG8 proteins while differing at exactly the position that disqualifies LMX1B's motif. If ATG8 binding were motif-driven at this site, LMX1A with alanine at X2 should bind at least as well as LMX1B.

If LMX1B binds ATG8 proteins through a classical LIR interface, F356-H-D-I is the better structural candidate: it matches the consensus, carries the acidic flanks that engage the paralog-variable N-terminal arm (R10/R11 in LC3A/B, E8/D8 and K46/K48 in GABARAPs), is disordered and exposed, and is more strictly conserved than YTPL. Unlike Y309, it is the one candidate whose flanks could in principle confer paralog preference.

F007. Nuclear ATG8-transcription-factor complexes have independent precedent

Two primary studies outside the LMX1B work report the same class of mechanism. In human ovarian cancer cells, SETD5 methylates nuclear LC3B, and "in the nucleus of human ovarian cancer (OC) cells, methylated LC3B binds the nuclear transcription factor PRDM10 to the promoter regions of autophagy-related genes (ATGs), including ATG2a, ATG7, ATG12, and ATG16L1, to suppress their transcription" (PMID: 40497358). In plants, "interactions between StATG8 and transcription factors AtWRKY40 and AtWRKY60 were detected in both cytoplasmic autophagosomes and the nuclear compartment" (PMID: 40732098).

The cellular-component and biological-process parts of the LMX1B claim, a nuclear ATG8 pool acting at autophagy gene promoters, should not be rejected on prior-implausibility grounds. Both precedents involve transcription factors other than LMX1B, and neither establishes which paralog partners LMX1B in dopaminergic neurons. The PRDM10 case also runs in the opposite direction, suppressing rather than stimulating autophagy gene transcription.

Mechanistic model and interpretation

The question the seed hypothesis poses is whether sequence-level LIR logic can rank six near-identical binding modules against one ligand. The answer is no, and the reason is structural: ATG8 paralogs diverge where they read flanks, and the LMX1B element has no informative flanks.

Two readings of the data are consistent with everything measured here.

Reading A, the non-canonical interface. Y309-T310-P311-L312 is the real contact. Proline at X2 constrains the backbone phi angle and removes an amide hydrogen-bond donor that contributes to beta-strand pairing with ATG8 strand beta2 in every characterized LIR complex. The resulting interface is low-affinity and not superimposable on p62- or FYCO1-type complexes. Because the bound conformation would differ from the canonical one, the usual selectivity rules do not apply at all, and the degeneracy across six paralogs is the predicted consequence. Binding under these conditions most likely depends on avidity, on nuclear concentration, or on an additional contact surface not captured by any motif.

Reading B, the misassigned motif. The ATG8 contact is at F356-H-D-I, which matches the canonical consensus, carries acidic flanks on both sides, is disordered and exposed, and is more strictly conserved than YTPL. The Δ308-317 phenotype then comes from losing the proline-directed phosphorylation switch or the SH3/YxxΦ chemistry in that window, and the ATG8 interaction is a separate, untested property of the 353-362 segment. Under this reading paralog preference might exist after all, because acidic flanks are exactly the feature that the paralog-variable N-terminal arm reads.

The LMX1A comparison bears on both. Both LMX1A and LMX1B are reported to bind ATG8 proteins, and they differ at exactly the position that disqualifies LMX1B's YTPL (Pro versus Ala/Thr). Under Reading A, X2 identity is irrelevant and this is unsurprising. Under Reading B, the shared binding element is the conserved FHDI/FHDL site, which both paralogs retain. Neither reading predicts that LMX1A should bind more weakly, which is what makes a side-by-side LMX1A/LMX1B affinity comparison a useful discriminator.

On scope: LMX1B's core molecular function sits in the homeodomain. A single missense substitution there, V265D, "abolishes DNA binding and thereby the ability to transactivate other genes" and produces glaucoma and semi-lethality in mouse (PMID: 24809698). Reporter studies of nail-patella syndrome alleles place transactivation loss in the homeodomain helices and the N-terminal arm, while "LIM B and truncation mutations retained residual activity" (PMID: 10767331). The ATG8 interaction is a regulatory overlay on a DNA-binding transcription factor. Nothing in this analysis supports LMX1B acting as a selective autophagy cargo receptor. It is also worth recording that the one unbiased affinity purification of LMX1B from dopaminergic cells recovered PSPC1, PSF, GRLF1, DHX9, MYO1C, HSP70 and TMPO (PMID: 23308148), and no ATG8-family protein appeared among them, which bears on the stoichiometry of the nuclear interaction.

Mechanistic scope

Compartment. The nucleus, under basal conditions, with cytosolic engagement appearing on starvation (PMID: 37014324). The nuclear setting is independently plausible: methylated nuclear LC3B binds PRDM10 at autophagy gene promoters in human cells (PMID: 40497358), and nuclear ATG8-transcription-factor complexes occur in plants (PMID: 40732098). These are analogies, not evidence about LMX1B.

Cell type. Human iPSC-derived midbrain dopaminergic neurons and transformed cell lines. LMX1B expression in GTEx is sharply restricted, with substantia nigra 27- to 160-fold above other brain regions, so the mechanism is confined to tissues where LMX1B is present at all.

Which paralogs the mechanism can use. Five of six, on expression grounds. MAP1LC3C is not available in this tissue. Beyond that, the analysis cannot narrow further, and the interface is most likely degenerate across the family.

Direct activity versus downstream phenotype. The direct molecular event under test is a protein-protein contact between a disordered LMX1B segment and the LDS of an ATG8 protein. Everything else reported around it is downstream: autophagy gene transcription, autophagic flux, neuronal resilience. The transcriptional output measured after Δ308-317 is two steps removed from the binding event and is confounded by the other motifs in the deleted window.

What the mechanism is not. It is not LIR-mediated in the canonical sense. Nothing here supports LMX1B acting as a selective autophagy receptor, and nothing requires LMX1B to be an autophagy substrate for the transcriptional effect to occur.

Conflicts and alternatives

A. The resources disagree with the published label, in a specific way. The commentary calls the element a non-canonical LIR (PMID: 37312406). The analysis here gives the reason: proline at X2, absent from all 60 ELM-validated LIR cores, and explicitly excluded by the canonical regex. ELM's own server declines to predict any human-applicable LIR class at 308-317. "Non-canonical" is a fair description and is also a statement that sequence-level LIR logic does not apply, which is why no paralog ranking follows from it.

B. Δ308-317 does not isolate ATG8 binding. The deleted residues carry DOC_WW_Pin1_4, MOD_ProDKin_1 (T310-P311), LIG_SH3_3 and TRG_ENDOCYTIC_2, plus three SH2 motifs. Loss of transcriptional output after deleting all of them is consistent with ATG8 loss, with loss of a proline-directed phosphorylation switch, or with destabilization of a disordered activation segment. Prior work on LMX1B truncations found that "LIM B and truncation mutations retained residual activity" while homeodomain mutations abolished transactivation (PMID: 10767331), so a C-terminal lesion abolishing output is a meaningful result that nonetheless does not identify its own cause.

C. A better canonical candidate lies outside the tested window. F356-H-D-I in DSIFHDIDSD matches the canonical regex unfiltered, carries acidic residues on both flanks, is disordered and exposed, and is FHDI in 147 of 151 vertebrate orthologues with X1 H and X2 D at 100%. It sits 39 residues C-terminal to the deleted window and has never been mutated. If the true ATG8 contact is there, the deletion result and the motif assignment are decoupled.

D. The LMX1A comparison cuts against a motif-driven interpretation. Both LMX1A and LMX1B are reported to bind ATG8 proteins. Their syntenic motifs differ at exactly the position that disqualifies LMX1B's (YTPL versus YTAL/YTTL, Pro in 1 of 165 LMX1A entries). Either X2 identity is irrelevant to this interaction, which argues the interface is non-canonical, or the shared binding runs through a different element, which points at the conserved FHDI/FHDL site.

E. Non-LDS interfaces are not excluded. ATG8 proteins have characterized surfaces outside the LDS, and ubiquitin-like proteins engage partners through them. Nothing here excludes an LDS-independent LMX1B-ATG8 contact, in which case the LIR framing is the wrong frame and selectivity would be governed by residues not examined in this analysis.

F. The tagged six-paralog panel is an overexpression result. Binding of a tagged construct to all six paralogs is exactly what a low-affinity degenerate interface would produce under overexpression, and it is also what a genuine pan-family interaction would produce. The result cannot distinguish the two, which is why endogenous paralog identity needs mass spectrometry rather than co-transfection.

Discriminating tests

Ordered by how decisively each resolves the open question.

  1. Point mutants in place of the deletion. Compare LMX1B Y309A, L312A, P311A and Δ308-317 for ATG8 binding and for transcriptional output on an autophagy-gene reporter. If the point mutants lose binding but retain output, the deletion phenotype comes from a different motif in the window. Resolves conflict B directly.
  2. Test F356. F356A/I359A, alone and combined with Y309A. If the double mutant loses binding and the Y309 single mutant does not, the published motif assignment is wrong. Resolves conflict C.
  3. Peptide ITC against all six paralogs. Synthesize EGMMASYTPLAPPQQQ and YGNDSIFHDIDSDTSL and titrate each against recombinant LC3A, LC3B, LC3C, GABARAP, GABARAPL1 and GABARAPL2. This is the direct test of the seed hypothesis. The prediction from this analysis is that affinities for the 308-317 peptide will lie within roughly 3-fold across all six, and that the peptide may show no measurable binding at all.
  4. Endogenous paralog identity by mass spectrometry. Immunoprecipitate endogenous LMX1B from hiPSC-derived mDAN nuclei and identify the co-precipitating ATG8 using paralog-distinguishing peptides. This answers "which paralogs are endogenous partners" without any motif reasoning. GABARAP is the prior favourite on abundance alone, at 2.1-fold above the next paralog.
  5. LDS-dead ATG8. Test LMX1B binding against LC3B F52A/L53A. If binding survives, the interface is not the LDS and the LIR framing should be dropped.
  6. Individual paralog knockdown in mDANs. Deplete GABARAP, GABARAPL2, MAP1LC3A, GABARAPL1 and MAP1LC3B one at a time and measure LMX1B target-gene transcription. If no single knockdown has an effect, that confirms the functional redundancy the structural analysis predicts.
  7. Swap experiment at X2. Replace YTPL with YTAL (the LMX1A residue) or with a canonical high-affinity core such as WTHL. If the canonical core increases binding, the proline is the limiting determinant and the element is a weak LIR; if nothing changes, the contact is elsewhere.
  8. Side-by-side LMX1A/LMX1B affinity comparison. The two paralogs differ at X2 and are both reported to bind ATG8. Equal affinity argues against a motif-driven interface at this site.
