| Catalytic substrate specificity | PKMYT1 transfers ATP’s γ-phosphate to inhibitory Thr14 and Tyr15 of CDK1/Cdc2, strongly preferring Thr14 and cyclin-B1-bound CDK1; it is substantially narrower in substrate scope than WEE1 and is not established as a general CDK2 kinase. | Recombinant GST-PKMYT1 assays; CDK1 phosphosite mutants; cyclin-bound versus monomeric CDK1; radiometric kinase assays | Thr14 phosphorylation has a much lower apparent ATP *K*ₘ than Tyr15; either phosphosite reduced CDK1–cyclin-B histone-H1 activity ≈10-fold, while dual phosphorylation reduced it ≈100-fold | Liu et al., 1997-02, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1128/MCB.17.2.571) (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002) |
| Localization | PKMYT1 acts on cytoplasmic membrane-associated CDK1 pools at the endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi; residues 378–399 form a hydrophobic membrane-targeting segment, and deletion redirects the protein predominantly to the nucleus. | Cell fractionation, immunofluorescence colocalization with γ-adaptin and PTP1B, deletion mapping in HeLa cells | Membrane-targeting segment spans 22 residues (378–399) | Liu et al., 1997-02, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1128/MCB.17.2.571) (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004) |
| G2/M pathway role | PKMYT1 restrains mitotic entry both catalytically—by inhibitory CDK1 phosphorylation—and noncatalytically—by binding and retaining CDK1–cyclin-B complexes in the cytoplasm. CDC25 phosphatases reverse this inhibition; activated CDK1 and PLK1 participate in feedback that suppresses PKMYT1 as cells enter mitosis. | Biochemical and cell-cycle studies summarized in an authoritative mechanistic review | Overexpression delays mitotic entry; kinase-inactive PKMYT1 retains partial delaying activity, supporting a sequestration mechanism | Schmidt et al., 2017-11, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules22122045) (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000007) |
| **Preclinical—CCNE1 synthetic lethality (2022)** | Genome-scale CRISPR screening identified PKMYT1 inhibition as synthetically lethal with **CCNE1** amplification. Selective oral inhibitor RP-6306/lunresertib activates CDK1 prematurely, forcing CCNE1-overexpressing cells into mitosis during DNA synthesis; combinations with gemcitabine produced durable regressions in amplified tumor models. | CRISPR screens, engineered cell lines, xenografts; no randomized human efficacy evidence | Mechanistic pharmacodynamic endpoint: reduced CDK1-Thr14 phosphorylation without gross loss of CDK1 protein | Gallo et al., 2022-04, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04638-9) |
| **Preclinical—checkpoint-inhibitor resistance (2023)** | PKMYT1 overexpression compensated for loss of other checkpoint kinases by maintaining inhibitory CDK1 phosphorylation, reducing premature mitotic entry and increasing resistance to WEE1, ATR and CHK1 inhibitors. | Cancer-cell overexpression and drug-response experiments | Directional result only: reduced premature entry and shorter mitotic arrest; no human response rate established | Sokhi et al., 2023-11, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2023.1270542) |
| **Preclinical—pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (2024)** | CRISPR screens identified PKMYT1 as a non-mutant PDAC dependency. Genetic or pharmacologic inhibition disrupted G2/M control, increased γH2AX, premature mitosis and apoptosis; TP53-mutant models were more sensitive than TP53-intact models. RP-6306 suppressed cell-line and patient-derived xenografts without overt measured toxicity. | Genome-wide CRISPR screens; KRAS-mutant and wild-type cells; KPC/KC models; cell-line and patient-derived xenografts | Xenograft groups: *n*=6; PDX groups: *n*=8; oral RP-6306 twice daily; mouse weight and ALT/AST showed no overt toxicity | Wang et al., 2024-04, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44321-024-00060-y) (pqac-00000015) |
| **Translational/preclinical—ER-positive breast cancer (2024)** | High or persistent PKMYT1 marked endocrine/CDK4/6-inhibitor resistance. Lunresertib plus gemcitabine was synergistic in p53-deficient, palbociclib-resistant cells and reduced tumors in resistant organoid/PDX models; this was not a therapeutic clinical trial. | Patient transcriptomic cohorts plus resistant cell, organoid and PDX models | 22 ER-positive PDXs analyzed; TP53 alterations reported in up to 58.5% of CDK4/6-inhibitor-resistant metastatic ER-positive cancers; responder/nonresponder PKMYT1 difference *p*=0.056 in a very small subgroup | Chen et al., 2024-05, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-23-0564) (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) |
| **Preclinical—TNBC/low-molecular-weight cyclin E (2024)** | Low-molecular-weight cyclin E increased PKMYT1 abundance and CDK1 inhibitory phosphorylation, creating sensitivity to lunresertib. Treatment promoted premature mitosis, replication-fork defects, chromosome damage, apoptosis and antitumor immune signaling in LMW-E-high but not LMW-E-null models. | TNBC specimens, engineered cells, xenografts, transgenic tumors and patient-derived xenografts | Approximately 70% of TNBCs were reported to express LMW-E; this prevalence is not a clinical response rate | Li et al., 2024-08, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-23-4130) |
| **Clinical—lunresertib status** | Lunresertib/RP-6306 remains investigational, with no approved PKMYT1-directed therapy. The phase 1/1b MYTHIC trial tests monotherapy and combinations with ATR inhibitor camonsertib or WEE1 inhibitor Debio 0123 in biomarker-selected solid tumors; eligible alterations include **CCNE1** amplification and deleterious **FBXW7** or **PPP2R1A** variants. A separate nonrandomized phase 2 study evaluated combinations with gemcitabine, FOLFIRI, trastuzumab or camonsertib. | Registered interventional human trials; efficacy should not be inferred from enrollment or preclinical rationale | MYTHIC: recruiting, estimated enrollment 464; phase 2 NCT05605509: completed, actual enrollment 28; MINOTAUR/FOLFIRI: terminated by sponsor, actual enrollment 38 | ClinicalTrials.gov, [NCT04855656](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04855656) (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012); [NCT05605509](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05605509) (pqac-00000010); [NCT05147350](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05147350) (pqac-00000009) |
