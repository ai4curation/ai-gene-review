CC   -!- FUNCTION: Dioxygenase that plays a key role in active DNA
CC       demethylation, by catalyzing the sequential oxidation of the modified
CC       genomic base 5-methylcytosine (5mC) into 5-hydroxymethylcytosine
CC       (5hmC), 5-formylcytosine (5fC), and 5-carboxylcytosine (5caC)
CC       (PubMed:19372391, PubMed:21496894, PubMed:21778364, PubMed:35798741).
CC       In addition to its role in DNA demethylation, plays a more general role
CC       in chromatin regulation by recruiting histone modifying protein
CC       complexes to alter histone marks and chromatin accessibility, leading
CC       to both activation and repression of gene expression (PubMed:33833093).
CC       Plays therefore a role in many biological processes, including stem
CC       cell maintenance, T- and B-cell development, inflammation regulation,
CC       genomic imprinting, neural activity or DNA repair (PubMed:31278917).
CC       Involved in the balance between pluripotency and lineage commitment of
CC       cells and plays a role in embryonic stem cells maintenance and inner
CC       cell mass cell specification. Together with QSER1, plays an essential
CC       role in the protection and maintenance of transcriptional and
CC       developmental programs to inhibit the binding of DNMT3A/3B and
CC       therefore de novo methylation (PubMed:33833093). May play a role in
CC       pancreatic beta-cell specification during development. In this context,
CC       may function as an upstream epigenetic regulator of PAX4 presumably
CC       through direct recruitment by FOXA2 to a PAX4 enhancer to preserve its
CC       unmethylated status, thereby potentiating PAX4 expression to adopt
CC       beta-cell fate during endocrine lineage commitment (PubMed:35798741).
CC       Under DNA hypomethylation conditions, such as in female meiotic germ
CC       cells, may induce epigenetic reprogramming of pericentromeric
CC       heterochromatin (PCH), the constitutive heterochromatin of
CC       pericentromeric regions. PCH forms chromocenters in the interphase
CC       nucleus and chromocenters cluster at the prophase of meiosis. In this
CC       context, may also be essential for chromocenter clustering in a
CC       catalytic activity-independent manner, possibly through the recruitment
CC       polycomb repressive complex 1 (PRC1) to the chromocenters (By
CC       similarity). During embryonic development, may be required for normal
CC       meiotic progression in oocytes and meiotic gene activation (By
CC       similarity). Binds preferentially to DNA containing cytidine-phosphate-
CC       guanosine (CpG) dinucleotides over CpH (H=A, T, and C), hemimethylated-
CC       CpG and hemimethylated-hydroxymethyl-CpG (PubMed:29276034).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3URK3, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12124344,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19372391, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19372393,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21496894, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21778364,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25284789, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29276034,

CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 1]: Dioxygenase that plays a key role in active DNA
CC       demethylation (PubMed:28531272). Binds to promoters, particularly to
CC       those with high CG content (By similarity). In hippocampal neurons,
CC       isoform 1 regulates the expression of a unique subset of genes compared
CC       to isoform 2, although some overlap exists between both isoforms, hence
CC       differentially regulates excitatory synaptic transmission (By
CC       similarity). In hippocampal neuron cell cultures, isoform 1 controls
CC       both miniature excitatory postsynaptic current amplitude and frequency
CC       (By similarity). Isoform 1 may regulate genes involved in hippocampal-
CC       dependent memory, leading to positive regulation of memory, contrary to
CC       isoform 2 that may decrease memory (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3URK3, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28531272}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 2]: Dioxygenase that plays a key role in active DNA
CC       demethylation (PubMed:28531272). As isoform 1, binds to promoters,
CC       particularly to those with high CG content, however displays reduced
CC       global chromatin affinity compared with isoform 1, leading to decreased
CC       global DNA demethylation compared with isoform 1 (By similarity).
CC       Contrary to isoform 1, isoform 2 localizes during S phase to sites of
CC       ongoing DNA replication in heterochromatin, causing a significant de
CC       novo 5hmC formation, globally, and more so in heterochromatin,
CC       including LINE 1 interspersed DNA repeats leading to their activation
CC       (By similarity). In hippocampal neurons, isoform 2 regulates the
CC       expression of a unique subset of genes compared to isoform 1, although
CC       some overlap between both isoforms, hence differentially regulates
CC       excitatory synaptic transmission (By similarity). In hippocampal neuron
CC       cell cultures, isoform 2 controls miniature excitatory postsynaptic
CC       current frequency, but not amplitude (By similarity). Isoform 2 may
CC       regulate genes involved in hippocampal-dependent memory, leading to
CC       negative regulation of memory, contrary to isoform 1 that may improve
CC       memory (By similarity). In immature and partially differentiated
CC       gonadotrope cells, directly represses luteinizing hormone gene LHB
CC       expression and does not catalyze 5hmC at the gene promoter (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3URK3,
