Gene Description

GRIA4 encodes GluA4, an AMPA receptor subunit that converts glutamate binding into postsynaptic cation currents. Recombinant human flip GluA4 homomers show fast desensitization and recovery in HEK293 recordings. De novo variants cause neurodevelopmental disorder with variable seizures and gait abnormalities.
