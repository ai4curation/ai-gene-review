- Beta subunit of ATP-forming succinyl-CoA synthetase [PMID:17287286 "The SUCLA2 gene encodes the ATP-forming beta subunit of the Krebs cycle enzyme succinyl-CoA ligase."].
- Patient muscle mitochondria lack ADP-forming SCS activity [PMID:15877282 "Markedly decreased succinyl-CoA synthetase activity due to a deleterious mutation in SUCLA2"].
- Recombinant SCS-A uses succinate, not itaconate; itaconate inhibits it (PMID:34492704).
- Malyl-CoA is a side product of promiscuous TCA-cycle enzymes, cleared by CLYBL
  [PMID:40108300 "malyl-CoA, a side product of promiscuous citric acid cycle enzymes"]. The
  GOA "toxic metabolite repair" annotation therefore describes CLYBL's role, not SUCLA2's.
- Loss of SCS causes succinyl-CoA accumulation and hypersuccinylation
  (PMID:33230181, PMID:37819759) and mtDNA depletion in mouse tissues where Sucla2 is
  expressed (PMID:24271779).
- ALAS2 interaction and the proposed succinyl-CoA supply for heme synthesis remain
  hypotheses (PMID:10727444, PMID:14643893).
