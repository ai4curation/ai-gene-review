CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the NADPH-dependent reduction of several pentoses,
CC       tetroses, trioses, alpha-dicarbonyl compounds and L-xylulose
CC       (PubMed:11882650, PubMed:19337691, PubMed:40737316). Can use both NAD
CC       and NADP as cosubstrate but shows higher activity with NADP
CC       (PubMed:11882650). Participates in the uronate cycle of glucose
CC       metabolism (PubMed:11882650). May play a role in the water absorption
CC       and cellular osmoregulation in the proximal renal tubules by producing
CC       xylitol, an osmolyte, thereby preventing osmolytic stress from
CC       occurring in the renal tubules (PubMed:11882650).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11882650, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19337691,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40737316, ECO:0000303|PubMed:11882650}.
