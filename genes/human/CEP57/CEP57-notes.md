# CEP57 evidence journal

## 2026-10-10: substantive ClinGen review

CEP57 is a microtubule-binding centrosomal organizer with a separable protein-anchoring role. The review preserves all 69 normal seeded assertions, five UniProt products and 40 original reference identifiers/titles. Seven genuine normal publication caches add mechanistic context. No NEW annotation is needed: the existing microtubule-binding, cytoskeletal-adaptor and spindle-organization assertions already cover the principal activities.

The most informative anchoring experiment is not a generic depletion phenotype. Human CEP57 binds purified PCNT PACT fragments, recruits PACT to ectopic microtubule-associated CEP57, and supports centrosomal loading. A PCNT–CEP57 chimera probes that physical route. The primary description, “anchor for the PACT domain,” captures this performed work [PMID:30804344, Results/Fig. 4 and subsequent rescue experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30804344/). This does not establish a fixed native ternary bridge or an intrinsic motor activity. The paper explicitly uses human isoform-a NP_055494.2; the five current products are retained without extrapolating identical behavior to all of them.

Direct microtubule evidence has different construct boundaries. The 2008 work uses mouse CEP57 fragments and bovine-brain tubulin; pronounced bundling contributes to its turbidity signal [PMID:18294141](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4351815/). The 2016 study supplies independent human HeLa-cDNA-derived C-terminal microtubule co-sedimentation [PMID:26743940](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26743940/). The 2024 study examines full-length tagged human CEP57 condensation but conducts most detailed reconstitution with engineered Cep57S, residues 56–264 joined to 334–433. Tubulin enrichment and a lower assembly threshold in PEG/taxol-containing assays are genuine measured activities; they are not a demonstration of native full-length processive polymerase activity. Full-length WT versus KR/FF cellular rescue is a separate experiment, and the mutations affect multiple interaction/assembly properties [PMID:38857398, Figs. 1–6 and purification/assembly Methods](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38857398/).

The September 2026 architecture article [DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-77795-4](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-77795-4) was accessible only as an authentic publisher abstract during this review. It is consistent with a structural CEP57 bridge but is not used to assert construct identity, atomic interfaces or controls absent from the accessible text. No publication cache or PMID was invented for it.

The physical kinetochore-attachment IBA remains UNDECIDED. The frog donor Q5FWP9 is currently named cep57l1; that fact does not prove PAINT node misplacement. Its original study explicitly addresses attachment in Xenopus egg extracts. The human 2016 paper reports checkpoint effects but no significant attachment defect. The 2019 human study obtains different checkpoint results under its conditions while acknowledging some kinetochore staining. These observations distinguish physical attachment from checkpoint signaling and PCM integrity; neither donor count nor legitimate target self-inclusion is used to reject an ancestral assertion. Complete PTN001064019/PTN001456386 history was not reconstructed [PMID:17803911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17803911/), [PMID:26743940](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26743940/), [PMID:30804344](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30804344/).

FGF2 trafficking is retained as a distinct contextual role. Actual publisher Main/Figs. 2–5 and Methods show human-library interaction discovery, mouse NIH3T3 depletion and bovine endothelial ligand experiments. The binding-deficient ligand retains selected FGFR/ERK/p70S6K outputs while losing nuclear-associated accumulation and most mitogenicity; an NLS fusion partly rescues the latter. The source therefore supports refining the existing receptor-signaling assertion to protein localization to nucleus, retaining nuclear/perinuclear ambiguity. It does not prove universal nonparticipation in receptor signaling or identify a nuclear-pore motor, importin or dynactin bridge. The inspected Methods do not unambiguously identify the species of every purified construct [PMID:12717444, full primary](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncb979).

The two spermatid-development transfers are over-annotated at the inspected source scope. The exact mouse Q8CEE0 donor uses IEP from PMID:12954732. The original author-uploaded Methods/Results measure developmental expression, enriched germ-cell populations, vitamin-A deficiency and RARalpha-null backgrounds, plus localization and interaction. They do not demonstrate a CEP57-performed developmental step or a CEP57-specific germ-cell perturbation. This is a limit of that evidence chain, not a claim that CEP57 cannot function in spermatids. Mouse full-length GFP localization in CV-1 cells supports the retained broad nuclear/cytoplasmic locations; the source's inconsistent NLS numbering is not propagated [PMID:12954732, original author copy](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/10583157_Tsp57_A_Novel_Gene_Induced_During_a_Specific_Stage_of_Spermatogenesis).

Conversely, independent human target microscopy corroborates the original centrosome assertions even though the target output from the older proteomics sources was not re-extracted. Human gamma-TuRC co-immunoprecipitation supports the gamma-tubulin association at complex resolution; NEDD1-dependent recruitment does not establish a purified binary gamma-tubulin interface [PMID:22508265](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22508265/).
