Gene Description

CEP57 is a coiled-coil centrosomal protein that binds microtubules and helps organize pericentriolar material and the mitotic spindle. Its distinct interaction regions anchor centrosomal proteins, including pericentrin, and support the integrity of the centrosome and centriole engagement. Human CEP57 can self-associate; purified constructs form condensates that concentrate tubulin, while cellular perturbations disrupt microtubule organization. CEP57, also called translokin, binds the 18-kDa form of FGF2 and supports its intracellular trafficking toward the nuclear compartment in mammalian cell models. The relative contribution of its proposed kinetochore checkpoint role varies across experimental systems. Biallelic CEP57 variants cause mosaic variegated aneuploidy syndrome.
