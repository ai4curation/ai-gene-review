Gene Description

CHRNA7 encodes the alpha7 subunit of calcium-permeable nicotinic acetylcholine receptors. Five alpha7 chains can assemble into a rapidly desensitizing, acetylcholine-gated cation channel, and alpha7 can also participate in beta2-containing heteromeric receptors. Neuronal receptors contribute to presynaptic transmitter modulation and postsynaptic excitation, with downstream effects on synaptic plasticity and cognition. Alpha7 is also expressed in non-neuronal cells, where receptor signaling can regulate inflammatory mediator production, endothelial responses and epithelial physiology. Anti-inflammatory signaling need not require ion-channel conduction, and the direction of downstream responses depends on cell type, ligand, receptor assembly and physiological context. Alpha7 interacts with amyloid-beta in experimental systems; effects on amyloid processing and neuronal injury differ among preparations and disease models.
