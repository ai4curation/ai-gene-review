| Domain name | Location / function | Key features | Regulatory significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFAT homology region (NHR; regulatory domain) | N-terminal regulatory region controlling phosphorylation, calcineurin docking, and nucleocytoplasmic trafficking | Contains serine-rich regions, Ser-Pro motifs, calcineurin-docking motifs, and an NLS; distinct from the DNA-binding domain | Multisite phosphorylation retains NFATc1 in the cytoplasm; calcineurin-mediated dephosphorylation produces the active nuclear form (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002) |
| Rel homology region (RHR/RHD; DNA-binding domain) | Conserved central or distal region that contacts DNA | Rel/NF-κB-like DNA-binding fold; recognizes a core 5′-GGAA(A)-3′ motif; monomeric DNA binding is generally weak | Confers sequence recognition, while partners such as AP-1 stabilize binding and determine promoter and cell-context specificity (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000007) |
| Nuclear localization signal (NLS) | Embedded in or functionally coupled to the regulatory NHR; directs nuclear import | Accessibility is conformationally controlled by phosphorylation | Masked in phosphorylated NFATc1 in resting cells and exposed after calcineurin-mediated dephosphorylation, enabling cytoplasm-to-nucleus translocation (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000021) |
| N- and C-terminal transactivation domains (TADs) | Variable terminal regions that recruit transcriptional cofactors and modulate activation or repression | Less conserved than the NHR and RHR; alternative NFATC1 isoforms differ in terminal regions; the C-terminal TAD contributes to isoform-specific activity | Help determine target-gene output and cofactor selection; domain differences contribute to NFATc1-specific cytokine regulation and functional differences from NFATc2 (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000011, pqac-00000046) |
| PxIxIT calcineurin-docking motif | Located in the NHR; provides a high-affinity docking interface for calcineurin | Conserved short linear motif; competitively recognized by the NFAT-inhibitory peptide VIVIT | Positions activated calcineurin to dephosphorylate NFATc1; disruption inhibits dephosphorylation, nuclear import, and NFAT-dependent transcription (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000037) |
| LxVP calcineurin-docking motif | Regulatory region; provides a second calcineurin-contact site | Conserved docking motif distinct from PxIxIT and associated with substrate recognition during active signaling | Cooperates with PxIxIT to support efficient, signal-dependent calcineurin engagement and NFATc1 activation (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000017) |
| Serine-rich regions and Ser-Pro phosphorylation motifs | Distributed through the NHR; form a phosphorylation-sensitive trafficking switch | Contain multiple regulatory phosphoserines, including SRR and SP clusters targeted by GSK3, CK1, DYRK1A, and other kinases | Phosphorylation favors cytoplasmic sequestration or nuclear export; calcineurin removes phosphates to expose the NLS, whereas rephosphorylation terminates signaling (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000013, pqac-00000032) |
| Composite transcription-factor interaction surfaces | Primarily within the RHR and variable TADs; mediate cooperative transcriptional complexes | Interact with AP-1 Fos/Jun and, depending on context, NF-κB, RUNX1, C/EBPα, BATF/IRF4, or other cofactors | Convert relatively weak NFATc1 DNA binding into stable, context-specific regulation; partner availability helps distinguish productive activation from AP-1-independent tolerance or exhaustion programs (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000029, pqac-00000031) |
