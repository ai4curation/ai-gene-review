|---|---|---|---|
| T-cell activation and clonal expansion | Converts antigen-receptor–evoked Ca²⁺ signals into transcription supporting proliferation, survival, and effector function | TCR signaling drives Ca²⁺ entry; Ca²⁺/calmodulin activates calcineurin, which dephosphorylates NFATc1 and enables nuclear import. Nuclear NFATc1 binds GGAA(A)-containing elements, often cooperatively with AP-1; inducible NFATc1/αA also positively autoregulates NFATC1 expression | NFATc1 loss impairs antigen-driven lymphocyte expansion and effector responses. A 2024 mouse study found that NFATc1-deficient CD8⁺ cells failed to sustain inflationary memory responses during latent cytomegalovirus infection (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000055) |
| CD4⁺ T-helper differentiation and type-2 immunity | Promotes TH2 differentiation and production of type-2 cytokines, especially IL-4; contributes to allergic inflammation | NFATc1 cooperates with AP-1 and promoter-specific cofactors to regulate cytokine genes; its transcriptional effects differ from those of NFATc2 | Conditional Nfatc1 deficiency in CD4⁺ T cells reduced TH2 polarization, IgE, mast-cell accumulation, and eosinophilic lung inflammation in an allergic-asthma model reported online in 2024 and published in 2025 (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000025) |
| Dendritic-cell–T-cell communication | Supports dendritic-cell priming of T-cell proliferation and subsequent TH2 differentiation | NFATc1-dependent transcription in CD11c⁺ antigen-presenting cells helps establish an effective priming environment | Deleting Nfatc1 in CD11c⁺ cells weakened T-cell priming, proliferation, and TH2 responses in experimental allergic asthma (pqac-00000025) |
| Persistent CD8⁺ memory responses | Maintains antigen-specific effector-memory and inflationary CD8⁺ populations during persistent infection | Recurrent antigen signaling sustains NFAT-dependent maturation and survival programs rather than being strictly required for initial priming | A 2024 MCMV study found largely preserved acute responses but defective long-term inflation after NFATc1 deletion; combined NFATc1/NFATc2 loss caused the strongest defects and impaired adoptive control of CMV (pqac-00000055) |
| CD8⁺ T-cell chronic stimulation and exhaustion | Inducible NFATc1/αA primarily supports survival and persistence of progenitor-exhausted and exhausted cells; NFATc2 has a stronger role in terminal dysfunction | Chronic TCR signaling induces the P1-promoter-derived NFATc1/αA isoform, promotes TOX expression, limits NFATc2, and shifts the Bim/BCL-2 survival balance; some outputs can occur without AP-1 | In chronic LCMV models, Nfatc1a RNA increased approximately 10–20-fold in progenitor and terminally exhausted cells. NFATc1-deficient virus-specific cells became nearly undetectable by day 15, while enhancer deletion selectively removing inducible α isoforms impaired survival under chronic stimulation (pqac-00000049, pqac-00000050, pqac-00000052, pqac-00000053, pqac-00000054) |
| Osteoclast differentiation and bone resorption | Acts as a master transcriptional regulator of osteoclast formation, fusion, maturation, cytoskeletal organization, and matrix resorption | RANKL–RANK recruits TRAF6 and activates NF-κB/MAPK pathways; immunoreceptor-associated Ca²⁺ signals activate calcineurin–NFATc1. NFATc1 then induces the osteoclast program and is restrained by BCL6, IRF8, MAFB, and LHX2 | A highly cited 2024 review synthesizes evidence that excessive NFATc1 activation drives rheumatoid-arthritis bone erosion and identifies the RANKL–RANK–TRAF6–NF-κB/MAPK–NFATc1 axis as a therapeutic intervention point (pqac-00000044, pqac-00000045) |
| Cardiovascular and valvular development | Required for normal endocardial, heart-valve, septal, and vascular development | Developmental Ca²⁺/calcineurin signaling activates NFATc1-dependent transcription in cardiovascular progenitor and endothelial contexts | Genetic loss in mice causes embryonic lethality associated with cardiovascular defects and organ hypoplasia, indicating a nonredundant developmental role (pqac-00000028, pqac-00000047, pqac-00000060) |
| Vascular remodeling and atherosclerosis | Regulates vascular smooth-muscle proliferation and phenotypic change, endothelial responses, inflammation, angiogenesis, foam-cell formation, and calcification | Calcineurin-dependent nuclear NFATc1 integrates Ca²⁺ signaling with CD137–TRAF6–JNK–AP-1 and OX40 pathways to alter vascular gene expression | Reviews link abnormal NFATc1 signaling to several stages of diabetic atherosclerosis, although much of the evidence is pathway- or family-level rather than uniquely isoform-specific (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000033) |
| Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma | Supports oncogenic transcription, malignant progression, and interactions with epigenetic regulators | Ca²⁺/calcineurin–NFATc1 can activate MYC; NFATc1 also activates EZH2 transcription and forms a phosphorylation-dependent NFATc1–EZH2 complex | NFATc1 and EZH2 rose from metaplastic and premalignant lesions to established PDAC; human tumors showed a positive NFATC1–EZH2 mRNA correlation of r = 0.75, and abundant EZH2 occurred in 70% of specimens with high nuclear epithelial NFATc1 (pqac-00000057, pqac-00000058) |
| Other solid tumors | Can promote angiogenesis, epithelial–mesenchymal transition, invasion, and metastasis, but effects are tumor-context dependent | Reported targets and outputs include VEGF, COX-2, CXCR7, and SNAI1; NFATc1 may also regulate methylation-sensitive promoters | NFATc1 has been linked to colorectal-cancer EMT and metastasis and prostate-cancer growth and invasion, whereas pro-apoptotic FasL regulation suggests tumor-suppressive activity in hepatocellular carcinoma. These opposing observations argue against treating NFATc1 as uniformly oncogenic (pqac-00000041, pqac-00000059, pqac-00000061) |
| Hematologic malignancy and drug resistance | Promotes leukemic maintenance, dedifferentiation, and resistance in selected molecular contexts | Constitutively active NFATc1 cooperates with FLT3-ITD; NFAT-dependent autocrine IL-4 signaling can support kinase-inhibitor resistance | NFATc1 accelerated FLT3-ITD AML in experimental models; depletion or inhibition increased sorafenib sensitivity, while nuclear NFATc1 increased resistance. NFATc1-mediated IL-4 signaling has also been linked to imatinib resistance in CML (pqac-00000057, pqac-00000062) |
| Histone homeostasis and genome stability | Represses histone-gene transcription, limiting potentially toxic histone accumulation | Ca²⁺/calcineurin activates NFATc1, which binds promoter regions of multiple histone genes and suppresses transcription | A 2024 study found that calcineurin inhibition increased histone abundance, whereas NFATc1 repressed histone H3 genes; excess H3 inhibited cell proliferation, suggesting a homeostatic role for the pathway (pqac-00000059) |
| Human pancreatic β-cell biology | Regulates genes associated with β-cell proliferation, identity, and diabetes susceptibility | Nuclear NFATc1 binds or influences accessible enhancer/promoter programs with context-dependent transcriptional partners | In human islets, NFATC1 regulated HNF4A, CDC123, HHEX, GLIS3, and SLC30A8 together with NFATC2, while GIPR and MACF1 were reported as NFATC1-selective; direct binding was more extensively mapped for NFATC2, so NFATC1 directness remains less certain (pqac-00000040) |

It emphasizes recent findings in immunity, exhaustion, bone biology, cancer, and transcriptional homeostasis while distinguishing isoform-specific evidence from broader NFAT-family inference.*
