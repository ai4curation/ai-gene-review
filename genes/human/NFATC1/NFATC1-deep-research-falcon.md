# Comprehensive Research Report: NFATC1 (Nuclear Factor of Activated T-cells, Cytoplasmic 1)

## Gene Identity Verification

The gene **NFATC1** (also known as NFAT2 or NFATc) encodes the transcription factor NFATc1, a member of the calcium-responsive Nuclear Factor of Activated T-cells family in humans (UniProt: O95644) (cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2). The protein domains identified in UniProt—Ig-like fold, Ig E-set, IPT domain, NFAT domain, and p53-like transcription factor DNA-binding superfamily—are consistent with NFATc1's characterized structural and functional properties as a Rel-family transcription factor regulated by calcium-dependent signaling (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 1-3).

## Primary Molecular Function: Transcription Factor Mechanism

### Protein Structure and Domain Organization

NFATc1 exhibits a conserved domain architecture shared among calcium-regulated NFAT proteins (NFATc1–4). The protein consists of several functionally distinct regions described in detail below and summarized in the accompanying table.

The **NFAT homology region (NHR)** at the N-terminus serves as the principal regulatory domain (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 1-3). This region contains multiple serine-rich and serine-proline motifs that are extensively phosphorylated by kinases including GSK3, CK1, DYRK1A, and PKA in resting cells (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6). The NHR also harbors two conserved calcineurin-docking motifs: PxIxIT and LxVP (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 29-33). These short linear sequences mediate high-affinity binding to the calcium-activated phosphatase calcineurin, positioning it to dephosphorylate the regulatory serines. Embedded within or functionally coupled to the NHR is a nuclear localization signal (NLS) whose accessibility is controlled by the phosphorylation state—phosphorylation masks the NLS and retains NFATc1 in the cytoplasm, while dephosphorylation exposes it and permits nuclear import (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, hui2023howdoesnfat3 pages 1-2).

The **Rel homology region (RHR)**, also called the Rel homology domain or DNA-binding domain, represents the conserved central region responsible for DNA recognition (cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2, oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9). This domain shares structural homology with the Rel/NF-κB transcription factor superfamily and recognizes a core consensus DNA sequence of 5′-GGAA(A)-3′ (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 6-8). However, NFATc1's DNA binding as a monomer is relatively weak, and effective transcriptional regulation generally requires cooperative binding with partner transcription factors at composite regulatory elements (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6).

Variable **N- and C-terminal transactivation domains (TADs)** flank the conserved core regions (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 1-3). These less-conserved terminal regions recruit transcriptional cofactors and modulate gene activation or repression. Alternative splicing and promoter usage generate NFATc1 isoforms with distinct terminal sequences, contributing to functional specialization; notably, the C-terminal TAD has been shown to contribute to NFATc1-specific cytokine regulation (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 5-7).

### DNA Binding Specificity and Target Gene Regulation

NFATc1 recognizes the consensus DNA motif 5′-GGAA(A)-3′ through its Rel homology domain (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 6-8). Because monomeric NFAT–DNA binding is weak, productive transcriptional regulation depends on cooperative interactions with other transcription factors, most notably the AP-1 family proteins Fos and Jun, which bind adjacent sites at composite NFAT/AP-1 elements (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 5-7). Depending on cellular context and promoter architecture, NFATc1 also cooperates with NF-κB, RUNX1, C/EBPα, BATF, IRF4, and other factors to achieve target-gene specificity (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 15-16).

Identified direct or regulated target genes of NFATc1 include cytokine genes (IL-2, IL-4, IL-9, GM-CSF), metabolic regulators in pancreatic β-cells (HNF4A, CDC123, HHEX, GLIS3, SLC30A8, GIPR, MACF1), angiogenesis-related genes (VEGF, COX-2, CXCR7), and cell-cycle regulators (CDK4, cyclin, c-MYC) (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 6-8, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 5-7, simonett2021identificationofdirect pages 4-5, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 3-4, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 9-10). In osteoclasts, NFATc1 regulates a master differentiation program including genes for osteoclast fusion, cytoskeletal organization, and matrix resorption (zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 1-3, zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 5-6). The transcription factor TOX, which drives T-cell exhaustion, is also induced by NFATc1 during chronic stimulation (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 1-2, wu2024newopportunitiesto pages 4-6).

## Subcellular Localization and Calcium-Dependent Translocation

### Cytoplasmic Retention in Resting Cells

In resting or unstimulated cells, NFATc1 is highly phosphorylated on serine residues within the regulatory NHR and is retained in the cytoplasm (cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6). This phosphorylation masks or inactivates the nuclear localization signal, preventing nuclear entry and maintaining the transcription factor in an inactive state (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, hui2023howdoesnfat3 pages 1-2).

### Nuclear Translocation Upon Activation

Cell activation triggers an increase in intracellular calcium through calcium influx via plasma membrane channels (including store-operated calcium entry through Orai channels) or release from intracellular stores (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6). Elevated Ca²⁺ binds to calmodulin (CaM), and the Ca²⁺/CaM complex associates with and activates the serine/threonine phosphatase calcineurin. Calcineurin is composed of a catalytic CnA subunit and a regulatory CnB subunit; Ca²⁺ binding to CnB and Ca²⁺/CaM binding to CnA displace the autoinhibitory domain, exposing the active site (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 29-33).

Activated calcineurin docks onto NFATc1 through the conserved PxIxIT and LxVP motifs and dephosphorylates the multiple regulatory serine residues in the NHR (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 10-12, oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 29-33). This dephosphorylation induces a conformational change that exposes the nuclear localization signal, allowing nuclear import machinery to translocate NFATc1 from the cytoplasm into the nucleus (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 10-12, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 17-18). Once in the nucleus, NFATc1 can bind DNA and cooperate with other transcription factors to regulate gene expression (cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2).

### Termination of Signaling and Nuclear Export

Nuclear translocation is reversible. Constitutively active kinases, including GSK3β, CK1, DYRK1A, and JNK, rephosphorylate NFATc1, which masks the NLS and promotes nuclear export, returning the transcription factor to the cytoplasm when calcium signals decline (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6). The balance between calcineurin-mediated dephosphorylation and kinase-mediated rephosphorylation thus determines NFATc1 localization and transcriptional activity (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 10-12). This dynamic equilibrium allows NFAT signaling to decode the duration and amplitude of calcium signals, with sustained Ca²⁺ elevation and nuclear calcineurin activity required for prolonged transcriptional responses (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 10-12).

## Signaling Pathways and Biochemical Interactions

### The Calcium-Calcineurin-NFAT Pathway

The primary signaling pathway regulating NFATc1 is the calcium-calcineurin-NFAT axis. Receptor stimulation (e.g., T-cell receptor, receptor tyrosine kinases, or G-protein-coupled receptors) activates phospholipase C (PLC), which generates IP₃ and triggers store-operated calcium entry (SOCE), raising cytoplasmic Ca²⁺ (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6). The Ca²⁺/calmodulin-activated phosphatase calcineurin then dephosphorylates NFATc1, enabling its nuclear translocation and transcriptional activity (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 10-12, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6).

### Kinase-Mediated Negative Regulation

Multiple kinases oppose calcineurin by phosphorylating NFATc1 and promoting its cytoplasmic retention or nuclear export. Key regulatory kinases include:
- **GSK3 (Glycogen synthase kinase 3)**: Phosphorylates serine-rich regions, masking the NLS (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6)
- **CK1 (Casein kinase 1)**: Contributes to the phosphorylation code controlling localization (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6)
- **DYRK1A**: Phosphorylates the regulatory domain (oliveira2026calcineurinnfatsignalingin pages 6-9)
- **JNK (c-Jun N-terminal kinase)**: Linked to stress and inflammatory pathways and can phosphorylate NFATc1 (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6, cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2)
- **p38 MAPK**: Provides additional nuclear regulation by phosphorylating distinct sites that influence transactivation (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6)

### Cooperative Transcription Factor Interactions

Because NFATc1's intrinsic DNA-binding affinity is modest, its transcriptional output depends critically on protein-protein interactions with cooperating transcription factors:

- **AP-1 (Fos/Jun)**: The most important partner, binding composite NFAT/AP-1 sites at cytokine promoters (IL-2, IL-4) and many other NFAT target genes. Different AP-1 compositions (Jun/Fos vs. Jun/ATF2) confer promoter-specific regulation (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 5-7)
- **NF-κB**: Can cooperate or compete with NFAT depending on the promoter context (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6)
- **RUNX1**: Cooperates with NFAT in hematopoietic differentiation (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6)
- **C/EBPα**: Participates in myeloid-cell regulation (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6)
- **BATF and IRF4**: Cooperate with NFATc1 in chronically activated T cells (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 15-16)

### Upstream Pathway Integration

NFATc1 activity is modulated by additional signaling pathways beyond direct Ca²⁺/calcineurin regulation:
- **CD137-CD137L signaling**: Activates a TRAF6→JNK→AP-1 cascade that cooperates with NFATc1 (cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2)
- **OX40-OX40L signaling**: Regulates NFATc1 in lymphocytes (cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2)
- **RANKL-RANK-TRAF6**: In osteoclasts, this pathway activates NF-κB and MAPK cascades that converge on NFATc1 to drive osteoclastogenesis (zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 1-3, zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 5-6)

### T-Cell Activation, Proliferation, and Differentiation

NFATc1 plays a central role in converting T-cell receptor (TCR)-evoked calcium signals into transcriptional programs supporting T-cell activation, clonal expansion, and effector function (yang2025nfatc1incd4+ pages 1-2, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6). In CD4⁺ T cells, NFATc1 is particularly important for TH2 differentiation and the production of type-2 cytokines, especially IL-4 (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 5-7, yang2025nfatc1incd4+ pages 1-2). A 2025 study demonstrated that conditional deletion of Nfatc1 in CD4⁺ T cells impaired TH2 polarization and reduced eosinophilic lung inflammation, IgE production, and mast-cell accumulation in an allergic asthma model (yang2025nfatc1incd4+ pages 1-2). The inducible NFATc1/αA isoform, generated from the P1 promoter, undergoes positive autoregulation and is preferentially expressed after initial and repeated T-cell stimulation, promoting antigen-driven proliferation and survival (yang2025nfatc1incd4+ pages 1-2, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 1-2).

NFATc1 also functions in dendritic cells (DCs), where it supports the priming of T-cell responses. Deletion of Nfatc1 in CD11c⁺ DCs weakened T-cell priming, proliferation, and subsequent TH2 differentiation in experimental asthma (yang2025nfatc1incd4+ pages 1-2).

### CD8⁺ T-Cell Exhaustion and Chronic Stimulation

Recent research (2024–2026) has revealed a specialized role for the **NFATc1/αA isoform** in CD8⁺ T-cell exhaustion during chronic antigen stimulation. Unlike the constitutive isoforms, NFATc1/αA is strongly induced by persistent TCR signaling in the context of chronic infection or tumor-antigen exposure (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 4-5, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 1-2, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 12-13). In chronic LCMV infection models, Nfatc1a RNA (encoding NFATc1/αA) increased 10–20-fold in both progenitor-exhausted (T_PEX) and terminally exhausted (T_EX) CD8⁺ T cells compared to resting cells (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 4-5, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 5-7). This isoform predominantly localizes to the nucleus after six days of chronic stimulation but is minimally induced by acute stimulation (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 12-13).

Functionally, NFATc1/αA primarily supports the **survival and persistence** of exhausted T cells rather than driving terminal exhaustion directly (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 1-2, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 13-15, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 7-8). NFATc1-deficient virus-specific CD8⁺ T cells failed to sustain long-term responses during chronic LCMV infection, becoming nearly undetectable by day 15 post-infection, whereas acute responses were largely preserved (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 5-7, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 7-8, chaudhry2024nfatsignalingis pages 7-9). NFATc1/αA supports survival partly by repressing the pro-apoptotic protein Bim and maintaining a favorable Bim/BCL-2 balance (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 13-15, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 7-8). It also induces the transcription factor TOX, which orchestrates the exhaustion program, but NFATc1's primary contribution appears to be sustaining exhausted cells in a progenitor-like state rather than promoting terminal dysfunction—a role more strongly associated with NFATc2 (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 1-2, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 13-15, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 5-7, samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 7-8). These findings have implications for improving CAR T-cell and checkpoint-blockade immunotherapies (samperebirlanga2026nonredundantfunctionsof pages 1-2).

A 2024 MCMV study corroborated NFATc1's role in persistent CD8⁺ memory responses, showing that NFATc1 is essential for maintaining inflationary effector-memory populations during latent cytomegalovirus infection, even though acute infection control is largely preserved (chaudhry2024nfatsignalingis pages 7-9).

### Osteoclast Differentiation and Bone Metabolism

NFATc1 is recognized as a **master transcriptional regulator** of osteoclastogenesis (zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 1-3, zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 5-6). Following RANKL binding to RANK on osteoclast precursor cells, TRAF6 activates NF-κB and MAPK pathways while calcium-dependent co-stimulatory signals (e.g., from immunoreceptors) activate calcineurin, which dephosphorylates and activates NFATc1. Nuclear NFATc1 then drives the transcription of osteoclast-specific genes required for differentiation, fusion, maturation, cytoskeletal reorganization (F-actin ring formation), and the degradation of organic and inorganic bone matrix components (zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 1-3, zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 5-6). Inhibitory regulators such as BCL6, IRF8, MAFB, and LHX2 restrain NFATc1 activity under physiological conditions.

In pathological contexts such as rheumatoid arthritis, excessive NFATc1 activation—driven by increased RANKL and inflammatory cytokines from synovial fibroblasts and immune cells—promotes abnormal osteoclast formation and bone destruction (zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 1-3, zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 5-6). A highly cited 2024 review identifies the RANKL–RANK–TRAF6–NF-κB/MAPK–NFATc1 axis as a key therapeutic target for RA-associated bone loss (zheng2024recentadvancesof pages 1-3).

### Cardiovascular Development

NFATc1 is essential for normal cardiovascular development. It is required for endocardial, heart valve, septal, and vascular formation during embryogenesis (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 4-5, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 1-3). Genetic loss of NFATc1 in mice results in embryonic lethality due to cardiovascular defects and organ hypoplasia, demonstrating a nonredundant developmental role (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 1-3, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 4-5).

In adults, NFATc1 also participates in vascular remodeling processes, including vascular smooth muscle cell proliferation and phenotypic transition, endothelial responses, inflammation, angiogenesis, macrophage foam-cell formation, and vascular calcification (cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2). NFATc1 has been implicated in the pathogenesis of diabetic atherosclerosis, where it integrates CD137–TRAF6–JNK–AP-1 and OX40 signaling pathways to alter vascular gene expression (cai2021roleofnfat pages 1-2).

### Cancer

NFATc1's role in cancer is context-dependent and varies by tumor type. In **pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC)**, NFATc1 supports oncogenic transcription and malignant progression (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 10-11, patil2021chromatinindependentinterplayof pages 5-6, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 3-4). Ectopic Ca²⁺/calcineurin–NFATc1 activation promotes MYC overexpression, a key oncogenic driver (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 10-11, sato2024calcineurinnfatc1pathwayrepresses pages 11-12). A 2021 study found that NFATc1 directly activates transcription of the epigenetic regulator EZH2, and the two proteins form a phosphorylation-dependent complex. NFATc1 and EZH2 expression increased progressively from acinar-to-ductal metaplasia through PanIN lesions to established PDAC in mouse models. In human PDAC specimens, 70% with high epithelial nuclear NFATc1 also showed abundant EZH2, and mRNA levels were positively correlated (r = 0.75) (patil2021chromatinindependentinterplayof pages 5-6).

In **colorectal cancer**, NFATc1 promotes epithelial–mesenchymal transition, metastasis, and poor prognosis by targeting SNAI1 and other genes (sato2024calcineurinnfatc1pathwayrepresses pages 11-12, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 3-4). In **prostate cancer**, calcineurin inhibitors suppress proliferation, migration, and invasion, implicating the calcineurin–NFATc1 pathway in progression (sato2024calcineurinnfatc1pathwayrepresses pages 11-12). Conversely, in **hepatocellular carcinoma**, NFATc1 acts as a tumor suppressor by promoting apoptosis through FasL-mediated signaling (sato2024calcineurinnfatc1pathwayrepresses pages 11-12), illustrating that NFATc1's effects are not uniformly oncogenic.

In **hematologic malignancies**, constitutively active NFATc1 cooperates with FLT3-ITD (internal tandem duplication) mutations to accelerate acute myeloid leukemia (AML) development and expand immature blasts (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 10-11, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 8-9). NFATc1 depletion or pharmacologic inhibition with cyclosporin A or VIVIT increases sorafenib sensitivity in FLT3-ITD AML, while nuclear NFATc1 confers sorafenib resistance and promotes dedifferentiation (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 8-9). NFATc1-mediated autocrine IL-4 signaling has also been linked to imatinib resistance in chronic myeloid leukemia (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 8-9).

NFATc1 additionally promotes angiogenesis and lymphangiogenesis through induction of VEGF, COX-2, and CXCR7, processes associated with cancer infiltration and metastasis (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 3-4, kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 10-11).

### Additional Functions

A 2024 study revealed a novel role for the calcineurin/NFATc1 pathway in **histone homeostasis**: NFATc1 binds to the promoters of multiple histone genes and represses histone H3 transcription, limiting potentially cytotoxic histone accumulation. Calcineurin inhibition increased histone levels, and excess histone H3 inhibited cell proliferation, suggesting a protective homeostatic function (sato2024calcineurinnfatc1pathwayrepresses pages 11-12).

In **pancreatic β-cells**, NFATc1 regulates genes associated with β-cell proliferation, identity, and diabetes susceptibility. In human islets, NFATc1 influenced expression of HNF4A, CDC123, HHEX, GLIS3, SLC30A8, GIPR, and MACF1, with some genes regulated specifically by NFATc1 (simonett2021identificationofdirect pages 4-5).

## Pharmacological Targeting

NFATc1 is a validated therapeutic target. The immunosuppressants **cyclosporine A** and **tacrolimus** inhibit calcineurin, preventing NFATc1 dephosphorylation and nuclear translocation, thereby suppressing NFAT-dependent cytokine transcription (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 8-9). However, these agents affect more than 50 calcineurin substrates and cause broad side effects (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 4-5). More selective NFAT inhibitors have been developed:
- **VIVIT**: A peptide that selectively disrupts calcineurin docking to NFAT by competing for the PxIxIT binding site (patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 3-6, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 10-12, patterson2021transcriptionalregulationby pages 8-9)
- **A-285222** and **INCA-6**: Small-molecule direct NFAT inhibitors (kitamura2021isoformselectivenfatinhibitor pages 4-5)

## Conclusions

NFATc1 (NFATC1 gene, UniProt O95644) is a calcium-responsive transcription factor with diverse and context-dependent functions across immune regulation, bone metabolism, cardiovascular development, and cancer biology. Its activity is tightly controlled by a dynamic interplay between calcium-activated calcineurin (which dephosphorylates and activates NFATc1) and multiple kinases (which rephosphorylate and inactivate it), translating transient calcium signals into sustained transcriptional programs. NFATc1 cooperates with partner transcription factors, particularly AP-1, to achieve target-gene specificity.

Recent findings (2023–2026) have highlighted the specialized role of the inducible NFATc1/αA isoform in supporting survival of exhausted CD8⁺ T cells during chronic stimulation, with implications for cancer immunotherapy. Additionally, NFATc1 continues to be recognized as a master regulator of osteoclastogenesis and a key target for treating rheumatoid arthritis–associated bone destruction. Its context-dependent functions in cancer—acting as an oncogenic driver in some tumor types but a tumor suppressor in others—underscore the complexity of NFAT biology and the need for careful therapeutic targeting strategies.
