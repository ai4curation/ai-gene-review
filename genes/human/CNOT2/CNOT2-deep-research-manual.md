# CNOT2 manual literature synthesis

Manual synthesis for the CNOT2/PTHR23326 family review used the fetched CNOT2 UniProt record, CNOT2 GOA rows, the PTHR23326 PAINT slice and the cached literature supporting the GOA rows.

Key primary sources:

- PMID:21299754: CNOT2 depletion destabilizes the human CCR4-NOT complex and reduces CNOT6L-containing deadenylase activity, supporting CNOT2 as a noncatalytic contributor to complex-level poly(A)-specific ribonuclease activity and poly(A)-tail shortening.
- PMID:31320642: recombinant human CCR4-NOT dissection shows that the nonenzymatic CAF40 and NOT modules are sufficient with the catalytic module to stimulate deadenylation, supporting CNOT2/CNOT3 structural participation rather than direct RNA hydrolysis.
- PMID:24121232: structural work resolves the CNOT2-CNOT3 NOT-module heterodimer and its CNOT1 contacts, supporting CCR4-NOT core-complex membership and specific CNOT2-CNOT3 heterodimerization.
- PMID:14707134 and PMID:16712523: human reporter and interaction experiments support a Not-box-dependent transcription-corepressor context for CNOT2.
- PMID:22367759: mouse Cnot2/Cnot3 knockdown and human ESC depletion support regulation of stem-cell population maintenance and extraembryonic-lineage restriction as contextual developmental roles.
