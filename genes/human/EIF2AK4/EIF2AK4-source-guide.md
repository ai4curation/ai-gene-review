# EIF2AK4 source guide

Publication caches preserve the normal acquisition output. Separately recovered original text can provide more experimental detail than a committed abstract-only cache. A cache marked full text can still omit Methods or supplements. The accounts below state the actual scope used; no figure-image interpretation or separately downloaded supplement analysis is asserted. Bibliographic verification is distinct from verification of every annotation attributed to a paper.

### PMID:16121183

Translational control of hippocampal synaptic plasticity and memory by the eIF2alpha kinase GCN2.

Cache: `publications/PMID_16121183.md` (SHA256 `7ac06c9a2d220b71f713a127c0efb39ff493e9c484ab0f1443c3e4519efbb98c`).

Original Methods, Results and captions establish mouse GCN2-knockout effects whose direction depends on stimulation or training strength. Weak stimulation/training enhances plasticity or memory, whereas stronger protocols reveal impaired late plasticity or memory. Human GCN2 antigen used to raise an antibody is not a human functional experiment.

[Original source](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature03897); [PubMed metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16121183/).

Operative locators: Original Methods for mouse genetics, electrophysiology, behavioral testing and polysomes; selected Results and Discussion comparing weak and strong protocols.

### PMID:32610081

Ribosome Collisions Trigger General Stress Responses to Regulate Cell Fate.

Cache: `publications/PMID_32610081.md` (SHA256 `98f8f521abae485759b06ef3838a28f559718588d9722d59308dffa088aa67f3`).

Human MCF10A GCN2 inhibition and shRNA establish collision-induced eIF2alpha phosphorylation dependence. Kinase-dead ZAKalpha rescues this branch, whereas simple glutamine-starvation signaling persists in ZAK-knockout cells. The original did not capture GCN2 or GCN20 on collided ribosomes. Selected Methods, Results and captions were read; supplement files were not inspected. This is cellular signaling evidence, not a purified human enzyme or isoform-resolved experiment.

[Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7384957/); [PubMed metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32610081/).

The unchanged [UniProt record](EIF2AK4-uniprot.txt) is now explicitly referenced in the review. ECO:0000250 is not relabeled as direct human experimental evidence, and none of these statements assigns equal function to all natural products.
