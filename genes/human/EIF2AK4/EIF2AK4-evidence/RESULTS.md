The tree has 1,076 nodes and the alignment has 1,076 sequences; the human target is PTN002475950 and its ungapped 1,649-residue sequence matches Q9P2K8 exactly. All lineage records are retained in `paint.json`, including records outside the six selected terms. No selected same-term loss record occurs on that lineage.

The complete mouse Q9QZ05 and yeast P15442 responses contain 86 and 76 records. All 29 ISS contexts have exact-term donor matches, reproduced in `donor-joins.json`. These joins establish propagation provenance. They do not turn mouse, yeast or expression-host experiments into direct human assays, and an exact donor annotation does not remove the need to assess the primary source.

The first two use InterPro combinations and the third uses three FunFam memberships. Their predicted broad protein-metabolism regulation is not an independent biochemical substrate assay. The review's more specific initiation-control interpretation relies on the experimental GCN2 mechanism.

The replay independently reproduces the previously derived PAINT, donor and ARBA projections. It does not decide ACCEPT, MODIFY, non-core or unresolved status; those biological decisions are recorded in the review and notes. The natural human product variants and experimental construct assignments remain separate from the canonical sequence check.
