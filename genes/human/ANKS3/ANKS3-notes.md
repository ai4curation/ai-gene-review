# ANKS3 notes

- SAM-domain scaffold:
  - ANKS3-SAM polymerizes and binds ANKS6-SAM, shown by crystal structures [PMID:24998259].
  - ANKS3 recruits ANKS6 to Bicc1, forming giant assemblies [PMID:29290488].
  - ANKS3's coiled coil binds Bicc1 and inhibits mRNA binding, relieved by ANKS6, which controls Dand5 decay in laterality [PMID:37733651].
  - A human homozygous variant fails zebrafish rescue [PMID:27417436].
  - ANKS3 binds NEK7 and retains it in the cytoplasm [PMID:26188091], and is present in renal cilia [PMID:26327442].
