# ELANE database provenance results

The offline replay authenticates 275 data/code members and reconstructs 102 source objects, 104 raw contexts, 51 current term definitions and 51 typed `is_a` ancestry records. All captures are fixed snapshots; a successful replay does not claim that every upstream database remains unchanged.

Six PAINT source rows join literal ancestral IBD records on the human P08246 lineage in PTHR24257. The target sequence exactly matches the official 267-aa precursor. The full saved IBD scan finds no loss assertion on that target path. A target appearing among the experimental donors and a short donor list are valid properties of the provenance, not evidence of circularity or weakness.

The current ARBA rule evaluation finds matching condition set 18 for ARBA00027399 and set 1 for ARBA00090615. ARBA00027128 has no matching set among its 280 captured sets. Its historical state was not reconstructed. Independent evidence supports the extracellular location, so current-rule reproducibility is kept separate from the biological conclusion.

Twenty-one Reactome annotation rows have explicit captured paths to human P08246. The paths preserve catalytic routes separately from input/output or granule-cargo routes. Human ELANE appears as a catalyst even where an event has a nonhuman top-level species label. Captured intervals are database processing metadata, not proof of the sequence in a historical assay preparation. The traversal is limited to the captured objects; it makes no exhaustive absence claim about unexpanded branches.

Seven complete current donor queries retain the experimental references and relation fields used for assessment. The rat rolling source uses an inhibitor affecting several proteases, leaving ELANE-specific attribution uncertain. Stimulus-specific mouse phagocytosis results do not establish a universal uptake mechanism. Those scientific judgments are documented in the review and source guide rather than generated by the replay.

The raw contexts preserve two interacting-taxon distinctions that the normal TSV projection collapses. No raw row or source dictionary is invented or removed.
