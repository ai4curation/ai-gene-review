# ELANE biological evidence

ELANE encodes neutrophil elastase, a serine endopeptidase whose substrate cleavage contributes to antimicrobial defense and remodeling of proteins in neutrophils and extracellular material. Direct human-enzyme experiments establish cleavage of plasma proteins, cell-surface substrates, histones and other defense-related proteins. An interaction with a substrate or inhibitor is not automatically a separate receptor, signaling or structural function.

The official protein is a 267-aa precursor. The signal peptide spans residues 1–27, the annotated propeptide 28–29, and the chain feature 30–267. Historical purified preparations and the 1HNE structure include a 218-residue sequence mapping to residues 30–247 with a sequence discrepancy. These boundaries, glycoforms and experimental truncations are distinct from natural splice products; no additional natural splice form is inferred. Native sputum sourcing in the original structure paper is retained despite inconsistent deposition source-method metadata. [PMID:2911584](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2911584/) [PMID:3550808](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3550808/)

## Proteolysis and defense

Human elastase directly fragments kininogens and thrombin-related substrates. The kininogen experiments separate the elastase reaction from subsequent kallikrein-dependent kinin release. The thrombin study separately demonstrates antimicrobial peptide activity; applying a derived peptide in a mouse infection experiment is not an ELANE perturbation. [PMID:12887060](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12887060/) [PMID:20421939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20421939/)

The ecotin study measures bacterial survival after treatment with human elastase, providing direct antibacterial evidence rather than only a requirement inferred from a host knockout. Susceptibility depends on bacterial protection mechanisms and assay context. In the SPIN study, elastase processing of a bacterial inhibitor and MPO-dependent killing are separate steps. [PMID:14705961](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14705961/) [PMID:29306874](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29306874/)

NET formation includes direct work by ELANE: purified enzyme cleaves histones and promotes chromatin decondensation, while human neutrophil experiments place its release and nuclear translocation in a wider MPO-dependent response. This interpretation uses the combined evidence rather than treating an inhibitor with PR3 activity as uniquely specific. The current definition and exact synonyms of GO:0031452 include chromatin decondensation. The separate human-thrombus localization study establishes elastase-positive extracellular DNA structures but does not measure its catalytic activity in those specimens. [PMID:20974816](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20974816/) [PMID:25066128](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25066128/) [PMID:32434865](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32434865/)

GSDMD activation and GSDMB inactivation are distinct substrate reactions. A lytic-death result does not by itself establish all elements of a pyroptotic inflammatory response or ELANE-dependent cytokine maturation. The accepted non-core pathway annotation preserves direct ELANE catalysis while keeping that broader process limit explicit. [PMID:29539421](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29539421/) [PMID:36899106](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36899106/)

## Context-dependent effects and locations

CD40 cleavage in gingival fibroblasts reduces CD40L-stimulated chemokine production, whereas elastase exposure in A549 cells increases IL-8 production. These are different stimulus and cell contexts, not a single contradictory response. The A549 and airway smooth-muscle studies remain at abstract-level access; no missing preparation or mechanistic details are supplied. [PMID:12223522](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12223522/) [PMID:14730209](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14730209/) [PMID:15010259](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15010259/)

The C5a study reports selective suppression of neutrophil responses by purified human leukocyte elastase. Its named alternate-agonist controls are explicit in the lysate/supernatant experiments; the purified-enzyme result is separately summarized as selective. Neither C5a nor its receptor is assigned as the cleaved substrate. Acute calcium-response inhibition leaves the steady-state homeostasis annotation unresolved. [PMID:15140022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15140022/)

ECM-enriched tissue and marrow preparations contain target-specific ELANE peptide records. These support contextual association without establishing a structural matrix role or exclusive localization. A verified urinary-exosome target record is retained independently of an unresolved EPS-urine record whose supplemental target table was not recovered. Missing supplementary evidence is not evidence of contamination or biological absence. [PMID:25037231](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25037231/) [PMID:28344315](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28344315/) [PMID:19056867](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19056867/) [PMID:23533145](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23533145/)

## Annotation boundaries and remaining questions

One source-specific term is removed: the alpha2-plasmin-inhibitor interaction does not support protease binding, because the named partner is a protease inhibitor. The positive cleavage experiment is preserved. The responsible BHF-UCL source row is recorded as a structured term-check request without inventing a replacement specific binding term. [PMID:6980881](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6980881/)

Five source contexts remain uncertain: steady-state calcium homeostasis; ELANE-specific attribution of UVA-associated fibroblast activity; the EPS-urine exosome target record; direct fungal killing in a study whose measured killing assay uses bacteria; and ELANE-specific leukocyte rolling after a multi-protease inhibitor. These uncertainties do not assert absence of the corresponding biological functions.
