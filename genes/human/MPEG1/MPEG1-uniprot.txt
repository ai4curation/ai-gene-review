CC   -!- FUNCTION: Pore-forming protein involved in both innate and adaptive
CC       immunity (PubMed:23753625, PubMed:26402460, PubMed:28422754,
CC       PubMed:30609079, PubMed:31537793, PubMed:33224153). Plays a central
CC       role in antigen cross-presentation in dendritic cells by forming a pore
CC       in antigen-containing compartments, thereby promoting delivery of
CC       antigens for cross-presentation (By similarity). Also involved in
CC       innate immune response following bacterial infection; shows
CC       antibacterial activity against a wide spectrum of Gram-positive, Gram-
CC       negative and acid-fast bacteria (PubMed:23753625, PubMed:26402460,
CC       PubMed:28422754, PubMed:30609079, PubMed:33224153). Reduces the
CC       viability of the intracytosolic pathogen L.monocytogenes by inhibiting
CC       acidification of the phagocytic vacuole of host cells which restricts
CC       bacterial translocation from the vacuole to the cytosol (By
CC       similarity). Required for the antibacterial activity of reactive oxygen
CC       species and nitric oxide (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A1L314, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23753625,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26402460, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28422754,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30609079, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31537793,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33224153}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Macrophage-expressed gene 1 protein, processed form]: Pore-
CC       forming protein that plays a central role in antigen cross-presentation
CC       in dendritic cells by mediating delivery of antigens for cross-
CC       presentation (By similarity). Dendritic cells bridge innate and
CC       adaptive immunity by capturing exogenous antigens on MHC class-I
CC       molecules and presenting them to naive CD8(+) T-cells (By similarity).
CC       Acts by forming a pore in antigen-containing compartments, promoting
CC       the release of antigens into the cytosol, enabling generation of
CC       MHCI:peptide complexes and T-cell priming (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A1L314}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Forms arc- and ring-shaped pre-pores on top of the
CC       membrane at neutral to slightly acidic pH conditions and converts to
CC       pores upon acidification (PubMed:31537793). Undergoes transition from
CC       the pre-pore to the pore in a processive clockwise hand-over-hand
CC       process (By similarity). In the pore state, 2 alpha-helical regions
CC       refold into transmembrane hairpins (TMH1 and TMH2) in each protomer
CC       that form in the ensemble complex giant beta-barrel transmembrane pores
CC       (PubMed:31537793). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A1L314,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31537793}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homooligomer (PubMed:31537793). Predominantly forms a
CC       homooligomeric arc-shaped pore complex instead of complete rings of 16
CC       subunits (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A1L314,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31537793}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: [Isoform 1]: Cytoplasmic vesicle membrane
