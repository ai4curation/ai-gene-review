CC   -!- FUNCTION: Small GTPase that cycles between an active GTP-bound and an
CC       inactive GDP-bound state and mainly functions in vesicle-mediated
CC       endoplasmic reticulum (ER) to Golgi transport. The active GTP-bound
CC       form inserts into the endoplasmic reticulum membrane where it recruits
CC       the remainder of the coat protein complex II/COPII (PubMed:23433038,
CC       PubMed:32358066, PubMed:33186557, PubMed:36369712). The coat protein
CC       complex II assembling and polymerizing on endoplasmic reticulum
CC       membrane is responsible for both the sorting of cargos and the
CC       deformation and budding of membranes into vesicles destined to the
CC       Golgi (PubMed:23433038, PubMed:32358066, PubMed:33186557). In contrast
CC       to SAR1A, SAR1B specifically interacts with the cargo receptor SURF4 to
CC       mediate the transport of lipid-carrying lipoproteins including APOB and
CC       APOA1 from the endoplasmic reticulum to the Golgi and thereby,
CC       indirectly regulates lipid homeostasis (PubMed:32358066,
CC       PubMed:33186557). In addition to its role in vesicle trafficking, can
CC       also function as a leucine sensor regulating TORC1 signaling and more
CC       indirectly cellular metabolism, growth and survival. In absence of
CC       leucine, interacts with the GATOR2 complex via MIOS and inhibits TORC1
CC       signaling. The binding of leucine abrogates the interaction with GATOR2
CC       and the inhibition of the TORC1 signaling. This function is completely
CC       independent of the GTPase activity of SAR1B (PubMed:34290409).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23433038, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32358066,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33186557, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34290409,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36369712}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer; upon association with membrane (PubMed:32358066).
CC       Part of the coat protein complex II/COPII, composed of SEC23/24 and
CC       SEC13/31 heterodimers, that it helps recruit and assemble on
CC       endoplasmic reticulum (ER) membranes at ER exit site (PubMed:32358066).
CC       Interacts with PREB; PREB acts as a guanine nucleotide exchange factor
CC       facilitating the activation of SAR1B by loading it with GTP
CC       (PubMed:32358066). Interacts with SURF4; recruits the cargo receptor
CC       SURF4 and its lipoprotein cargos to COPII-coated ER to Golgi transport
CC       vesicles (PubMed:33186557). Interacts with MIOS; the interaction is
CC       direct, disrupted by the binding of leucine and mediates the
CC       interaction of SAR1B with the GATOR2 complex to negatively regulate the
CC       TORC1 signaling upon leucine deprivation (PubMed:34290409).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9QVY3, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32358066,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33186557, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34290409}.
