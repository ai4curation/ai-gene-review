## Molecular mechanism and evidence boundaries

CD247 performs two related but distinguishable kinds of molecular work: cytoplasmic recruitment of signaling proteins, and physical membrane assembly of receptor modules. LCK phosphorylates CD247 ITAMs; the phosphorylated motifs then engage ZAP70 tandem SH2 domains. CD247 is the docking scaffold, not the kinase. The complete abstract of PMID:7509083 and selected Fig.1/footnote-35 Methods in the author reproduction identify human Jurkat and LCK-deficient J.CaM1 controls. The external copy is https://www.researchgate.net/publication/267042930_Pillars_Article_Sequential_Interactions_of_the_TCR_with_Two_Distinct_Cytoplasmic_Tyrosine_kinases_Science_1994_263_1136-1139 ; access did not extend to a verified complete original paper or supplement. The canonical cache remains abstract-only. PMID:7528772 gives detailed positive/negative purified zeta-peptide and single/tandem-SH2 controls in its abstract. PMID:8901551 further separates kinase binding from activation: dimeric GST-zeta presentation, rather than any free phosphopeptide, increased ZAP70 catalytic output under its conditions.

The receptor-complex adaptor child GO:0030159 is a better existing term for the source GO:0030674 adaptor assertion. Its official definition concerns physical assembly of signaling receptor complexes; its parent is GO:0035591. GO:0005068 is inappropriate because that term concerns intrinsically tyrosine-kinase transmembrane receptors, whereas the TCR uses separate cytoplasmic kinases. Official term pages were read through FlyBase/AmiGO/MGI because no callable OLS tool was available. This is a refinement of an existing activity, with no NEW annotation or process proposed.

The primary membrane evidence is not interchangeable with soluble-tail self-association. PMID:17055436 has a partial canonical HTML extraction. Selected original Results/Methods at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3466601/ distinguish the E. coli-expressed 33-residue transmembrane NMR peptide from full-length human zeta in ER-microsome folding/assembly assays. Mutations can separate dimerization from incorporation into the receptor. PMID:14967045 instead studies purified cytoplasmic domains, with monomer/dimer and dimer/tetramer equilibria at approximately micromolar and millimolar scales. Those observations remain valid non-core experimental annotations; a physiological cytoplasmic tetramer is not asserted.

PMID:31461748 provides the human TCR-CD3 structural assembly, with one alpha-beta receptor, gamma-epsilon, delta-epsilon and zeta-zeta module. The official correction at https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03245-4 was read: it adds omitted subunit sequences and construct information. It does not state that the assembly result was reversed. Canonical access is abstract-only; selected publisher captions and the correction are external evidence, not a cached full Methods claim. The older PMID:9485181 human Jurkat/thymocyte assembly evidence remains useful, but its proposed double-TCR stoichiometry is not the structural model used here. Its reference assessment records that bounded disagreement. No fixed stoichiometry is extrapolated to all receptor states or all gamma-delta subsets.

Human CD247 also assembles with CD16A, as shown in the human NK-cell/COS7 work of PMID:2532305 and the later reconstitution in PMID:28652325. The latter canonical HTML contains abstract and Discussion; separately inspected official Results/captions at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5514760/ describe human gamma-gamma, gamma-zeta and zeta-zeta modules, cotranslation versus post-mixing controls, and 293T surface-expression experiments. Molecular modeling is distinguished from experimental assembly. Human and mouse CD247 are not interchangeable in CD16A assembly, and receptor mutants can bypass normal retention; these results do not imply universal adaptor dependence. The homodimer core therefore summarizes physical assembly and receptor export, while the adaptor core summarizes ITAM-mediated signal recruitment. The Fc-gamma complex membership term retains its curated usage; its definition's conventional gamma-containing composition is not a claim that every human CD16A complex contains the same signaling dimer.
