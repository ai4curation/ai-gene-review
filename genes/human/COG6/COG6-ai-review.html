Gene Description

COG6 is a peripheral Golgi-associated subunit of the conserved oligomeric Golgi complex. It contributes to the lobe-B assembly and recognizes Golgi SNARE proteins through an N-terminal interaction region. These contacts help maintain SNARE abundance and organization, support retrograde membrane traffic and preserve Golgi architecture and glycosylation. Human COG6 deficiency can disrupt COG-complex organization and cause multisystem congenital glycosylation disorders or a hypohidrosis–intellectual disability syndrome. Clinical and biochemical severity varies among alleles. Three annotated protein products differ substantially in length; the shortest ends before the mapped SNARE-binding region.
