CC       maintaining cochlear stereocilia bundles that are involved in sound
CC       detection (PubMed:24619944). Ensures the restriction of TPRN to the
CC       basal region of stereocilia in hair cells (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3TYR5, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24619944}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with TPRN; the interaction restricts TPRN to the
CC       stereocilum basal region. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3TYR5}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell projection, stereocilium
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3TYR5}. Note=Concentrated at the basal taper
CC       region of stereocilia in early postnatal development. In adult hair
CC       cells, concentrated at the basal region but also detected along the
CC       stereocilium shaft. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3TYR5}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Deafness, autosomal recessive, 101 (DFNB101) [MIM:615837]: A

CC       severe hearing loss. Vestibular function is unaffected.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24619944}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
