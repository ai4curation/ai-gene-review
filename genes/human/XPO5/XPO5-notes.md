# XPO5 review notes

PTHR11223 includes the XPO1 and XPO5 export receptor branches. XPO5 supports the shared RanGTP-dependent nuclear export receptor and shuttling localization rows, but its core specificity is pre-miRNA and other structured RNA export rather than the leucine-rich protein NES cargo recognition that dominates XPO1/CRM1.

XPO5 had 46 GOA rows and 46 seeded `existing_annotations` entries. The review keeps one entry per row and preserves every `WITH/FROM` list, including broad PANTHER rows that mix exportin-1 and exportin-5 donors.

The two PMID:19965479 RISC rows were removed because the cited structure is a pre-miRNA:Exp5:RanGTP nuclear export complex, not RISC. The protein-export annotations from the 2002 founding paper and the 2013 SNAG/eEF1A paper were kept as secondary XPO5 cargo biology.
