Gene Description

Cadherin-11 is a type II classical cadherin that mediates calcium-dependent homophilic cell adhesion, particularly in mesenchymal cells. Its extracellular cadherin repeats engage cadherin molecules on adjacent cells, while the canonical cytoplasmic tail recruits catenins at adherens junctions. These contacts coordinate cell organization and collective movement. A truncated splice isoform modulates the intact protein, and proteolytic release produces an extracellular fragment. CDH11 dysfunction perturbs craniofacial development; osteoblast and valve-cell studies also connect its adhesive role to tissue formation and remodeling.
