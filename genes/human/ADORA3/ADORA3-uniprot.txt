CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) for adenosine that plays
CC       significant roles in various physiological processes including immune
CC       regulation, cardioprotection and neuroprotection (PubMed:38627384,
CC       PubMed:40825947, PubMed:9837869). Also acts as a receptor for
CC       adenosines containing N(6)-methylated adenine (m6A) post-
CC       transcriptional modification, which are derived from the degradation of
CC       RNAs (mRNAs, rRNAs and tRNAs): activated by N(6)-methyladenosine (m6A),
CC       N(6),N(6)-dimethyladenosine (m6,6A) and N(6)-isopentenyladenosine (i6A)
CC       (PubMed:33472058, PubMed:39511145). Preferentially couples to the
CC       inhibitory G protein (Gi), leading to the suppression of adenylate
CC       cyclase activity and a reduction in intracellular cyclic AMP levels
CC       (PubMed:33472058, PubMed:38627384). Upon adenosine binding, mediates
CC       cardioprotection in cardiomyocytes through anti-apoptotic effects
CC       primarily via the ERK1/2 pathway as well as the PI3K pathway
CC       (PubMed:9837869). In the central nervous system, participates in the
CC       modulation of synaptic plasticity, including long-term potentiation
CC       (LTP) and long-term depression (LTD) in the hippocampus (By
CC       similarity). In lung mast cells, receptor activation contributes to the
CC       type I allergic response by facilitating mast cell degranulation and
CC       histamine release (By similarity). Highly expressed in inflammatory
CC       cells such as neutrophils and mast cells, inhibits neutrophil
CC       degranulation and reduces superoxide production, thereby modulating
CC       inflammatory responses (PubMed:9164961). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P28647,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q61618, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33472058,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38627384, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39511145,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40825947, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9164961,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9837869}.

CC       receptor is mediated by G proteins which inhibits adenylyl cyclase
CC       (PubMed:8234299). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8234299}.
