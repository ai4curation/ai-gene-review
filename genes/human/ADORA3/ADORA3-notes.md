# ADORA3 (A3 adenosine receptor) review notes

Deep research via falcon/perplexity-lite failed (no provider keys); research from cached publications and UniProt.

- Gi-coupled adenosine receptor [PMID:40825947 "with A1AR and A3AR preferentially coupling to Gi proteins"]; inhibits adenylyl cyclase [PMID:9837869 "Cardiac myocytes transfected with the A 3 receptor cDNA had an enhanced IB-MECA-mediated inhibition of adenylyl cyclase activity."]
- Endogenous RNA-derived ligands: m6A [PMID:33472058 "acts as a ligand for the human adenosine A3 receptor, for which it has greater affinity than unmodified adenosine"], i6A [PMID:39511145 "we identify tRNA-derived N6-isopentenyl adenosine (i6A) as an A3R-selective ligand"]
- Localization: plasma membrane, 7TM, extracellular orthosteric site; flow-cytometric surface expression [PMID:39511145 "The cell surface expression of A3R was measured as described previously with modifications"]. UniProt "Cell membrane" by similarity.
- Expression: lung, liver, kidney, heart; little brain [PMID:8399349 "with very little expression in the brain or in skeletal muscle"]
- Physiology: inhibits neutrophil degranulation [PMID:9164961]; mast-cell degranulation/type I allergy [PMID:33472058]; cardioprotection [PMID:9837869]; osteosarcoma proliferation/migration suppression [PMID:27886186].
- Curation: "activation of adenylate cyclase activity" (PMID:9380026) reflects chronic-agonist AC supersensitization, not direct activation -> over-annotation. "regulation of heart contraction" and "response to wounding" from an ischemia cardioprotection paper -> over-annotations.
