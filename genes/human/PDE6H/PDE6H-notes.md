# PDE6H notes

Session 2026-11-16, review of 21 GOA rows (vision-disorder alignment campaign), last of the five PDE6 genes.

## Function

- Inhibitory gamma subunit of cone PDE6 (Pgamma-c, 83 aa); human cDNA cloned from retina, cone-specific
  by in situ hybridisation [PMID:8786098]. Cone holoenzyme = PDE6C homodimer + two Pgamma-c
  [PMID:39739818]. In that study the complex (PDE6C)2(Pgamma-c)2 was purified and solved, and a
  Pgamma-c C-terminal peptide inhibited trypsin-activated mouse retinal PDE6 (Ki 8.2 uM vs 2.5 uM
  for the rod peptide); the cached text does not state the species of the full-length Pgamma-c.
- Core MF: GO:0004857 enzyme inhibitor activity, as for PDE6G (no specific MF term for
  phosphodiesterase inhibition exists).
- No GO term for the cone PDE6 complex; proposed in the PDE6C review. Row 17 (membrane protein
  complex) is the nearest available.
- Rod and cone Pgamma both increased EGF/thrombin ERK stimulation when transfected into HEK293 cells
  [PMID:11502744]; non-core.
- The RGS9 GAP-enhancing role is documented for rod Pgamma (bovine/mouse); not examined for PDE6H.

## Disease

- ACHM6 (recessive) per UniProt. The cited IMP paper [PMID:15629837] reports only a heterozygous
  5'UTR variant in one family with a distinct cone dystrophy, with increased in vitro translation;
  weak genetic evidence. The ACHM6 nonsense-allele paper is not cached.
