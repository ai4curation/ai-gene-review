CC   -!- FUNCTION: Inhibitory gamma subunit of the cone-specific cGMP
CC       phosphodiesterase (PDE6) complex which hydrolyzes 3',5'-cyclic GMP in
CC       the phototransduction cascade (By similarity). The PDE6 holoenzyme
CC       consists of two catalytic alpha subunits (PDE6C) and two inhibitory
CC       gamma subunits (PDE6H) (By similarity). Light-activated GNAT2 relieves
CC       gamma subunit-mediated inhibition, allowing the catalytic subunits to
CC       hydrolyze cGMP and mediate visual signal transduction and
CC       amplification. The resulting decrease in cytosolic cGMP levels triggers
CC       the closure of cGMP-gated cation channels at the plasma membrane,
CC       leading to hyperpolarization of cone photoreceptors (By similarity).
CC       Inhibition by the gamma subunit is relatively dynamic and less tightly
CC       coupled than in rods, allowing transient access to the catalytic pocket
CC       and facilitating the rapid phototransduction kinetics characteristic of
CC       cones (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P22571,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51160, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P61249}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Tetramer composed of homodimeric catalytic chains (alpha'
CC       subunit/PDE6C) each one accociated with one inhibitory chain (gamma
CC       subunit/PDE6H). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51160}.
