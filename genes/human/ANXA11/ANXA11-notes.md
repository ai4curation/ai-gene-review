## Mechanistic direction and new primary sources

The principal working model is a calcium-controlled connection between membranes and cytoplasmic macromolecular assemblies. Nuclear/cell-cycle localization and a role in terminal cytokinesis are established in older human-cell experiments. More recent primary work directly tests ANXA11-mediated contacts between RNA granules and lysosomal membranes, rather than relying solely on a knockout phenotype. The exact GO molecular-function formulation remains to be checked; a membrane or organelle location does not by itself establish an adaptor or scaffold activity.
