CC   -!- FUNCTION: A component of desmosome cell-cell junctions which are
CC       required for positive regulation of cellular adhesion (PubMed:17559062,
CC       PubMed:38395410). Involved in the interaction of plaque proteins and
CC       intermediate filaments mediating cell-cell adhesion. Required for
CC       proliferation and viability of embryonic stem cells in the blastocyst,
CC       thereby crucial for progression of post-implantation embryonic
CC       development (By similarity). Maintains pluripotency by regulating
CC       epithelial to mesenchymal transition/mesenchymal to epithelial
CC       transition (EMT/MET) via interacting with and sequestering CTNNB1 to
CC       sites of cell-cell contact, thereby reducing translocation of CTNNB1 to
CC       the nucleus and subsequent transcription of CTNNB1/TCF-target genes
CC       (PubMed:29910125). Promotes pluripotency and the multi-lineage
CC       differentiation potential of hematopoietic stem cells
CC       (PubMed:27338829). Plays a role in endothelial cell sprouting and
CC       elongation via mediating the junctional-association of cortical actin
CC       fibers and CDH5 (PubMed:27338829). Promotes cardiomyocyte cell
CC       homeostasis and desmosome junction formation at intercalated disks, as
CC       a result plays a role in the maintenance of cardiac conduction and
CC       heart chamber integrity (By similarity). Positively regulates
CC       pancreatic islet development and maintenance of endothelial cell
CC       barrier integrity in the pancreas, therefore involved in the controlled
CC       release of insulin from islet cells into the circulation in response to
CC       glucose (By similarity). Plays a role in limiting inflammatory
CC       infiltration and the apoptotic response to injury in kidney tubular
CC       epithelial cells, potentially via its role in maintaining cell-cell
CC       adhesion and the epithelial barrier (PubMed:38395410). Acts as a
CC       positive modulator of CSK and EGFR activation via sequestering them
CC       away from lipid rafts, this is independent of its role in desmosome
CC       cell junctions (PubMed:26918609). Also disrupts the localization of
CC       CAV1 to lipid rafts resulting in its distribution throughout the
