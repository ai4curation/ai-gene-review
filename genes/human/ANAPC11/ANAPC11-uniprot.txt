CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome
CC       (APC/C), a cell cycle-regulated E3 ubiquitin ligase that controls
CC       progression through the cell cycle (PubMed:11739784, PubMed:18485873,
CC       PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). Together with ANAPC2, forms the
CC       catalytic component of the E3 ubiquitin ligase complex
CC       (PubMed:11739784, PubMed:18485873, PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861).
CC       APC/C acts by mediating ubiquitination and subsequent degradation of
CC       target proteins: it mainly mediates the formation of 'Lys-11'-linked
CC       polyubiquitin chains and, to a lower extent, the formation of 'Lys-
CC       48'- and 'Lys-63'-linked polyubiquitin chains (PubMed:11739784,
CC       PubMed:18485873). APC/C catalyzes assembly of branched 'Lys-11'-/'Lys-
CC       48'-linked branched ubiquitin chains on target proteins
CC       (PubMed:29033132). APC/C is activated by CDC20 in the
CC       metaphase/anaphase transition of cell cycle, targeting the degradation
CC       of cyclin B and securin (PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C is
CC       regulated by the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC), which inhibits APC/C
CC       and delays chromosome segregation (PubMed:27509861).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11739784, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18485873,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27120157, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27509861,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29033132}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18485873, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29033132}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C),
CC       composed of ANAPC1, ANAPC2, CDC27/ANAPC3, ANAPC4, ANAPC5, CDC16/ANAPC6,
CC       ANAPC7, CDC23/ANAPC8, ANAPC10, ANAPC11, CDC26/ANAPC12, ANAPC13, ANAPC15
CC       and ANAPC16 (PubMed:10922056, PubMed:25043029, PubMed:26083744,
CC       PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C associates with CDC20 to form
CC       the CDC20-APC/C complex, which is crucial for metaphase/anaphase
CC       transition in cell cycle; this interaction is dependent on APC/C
CC       phosphorylation (PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C associates
CC       with mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC), which inhibits APC/C and delays
CC       chromosome segregation (PubMed:27509861). APC/C interacts with FZR1 and
CC       FBXO5 (PubMed:25043029, PubMed:26083744). Interacts with the cullin
CC       domain of ANAPC2 (PubMed:11739784). Interacts with UBE2D2
CC       (PubMed:11739784). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10922056,
