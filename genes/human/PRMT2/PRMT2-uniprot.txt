CC   -!- FUNCTION: Arginine methyltransferase that methylates the guanidino
CC       nitrogens of arginyl residues in proteins such as STAT3, FBL, histone
CC       H4 (PubMed:19405910). Acts as a coactivator (with NCOA2) of the
CC       androgen receptor (AR)-mediated transactivation (PubMed:17587566). Acts
CC       as a coactivator (with estrogen) of estrogen receptor (ER)-mediated
CC       transactivation (PubMed:12039952). Enhances PGR, PPARG, RARA-mediated
CC       transactivation (PubMed:12039952). May inhibit NF-kappa-B transcription
CC       and promote apoptosis (PubMed:16648481). Represses E2F1 transcriptional
CC       activity (in a RB1-dependent manner) (By similarity). May be involved
CC       in growth regulation (By similarity). Involved in PRMT2IP-mediated
CC       inhibition of the microglial inflammatory responses through suppression
CC       of NF-kappa-B signaling, thereby reducing the production of pro-
CC       inflammatory cytokines (PubMed:38892217).
