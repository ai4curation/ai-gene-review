# FLNC source evidence

Filamin C supplies actin-associated structure in striated muscle. The review retains one connected structural core: actin filament binding supporting sarcomere organization at Z-discs, the sarcolemma and costameres. Human target evidence, ortholog transfers, peptide/fragment binding and natural products are kept distinct.

## Structural work and muscle partners

Human FLNC-null induced pluripotent stem-cell cardiomyocytes have disordered actin bundles and reduced sarcomere organization even on patterned matrices. This is positive evidence for the structural organization role; the tested heterozygous variants did not produce the same structural deficit. Selected original Methods/Results and caption text were read, without inspecting figure pixels, videos or separate supplements. The downstream expression and lysosomal changes do not make FLNC a transcription factor or lysosomal enzyme. [PMID:34405687](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34405687/)

Human muscle tissue and differentiating human cultures support endogenous FLNC at Z-discs, sarcoplasm and sarcolemma. The source distinguishes an antibody recognizing alpha/gamma filamin from an alpha-specific antibody: its historical isoform terminology often names paralogs, not modern splice products. Human FLNC fragments tested in rat cardiomyocytes or mouse C2C12 hosts are separate from endogenous human localization. Purified human FLNC/myotilin fragment assays support binding, whereas the proposed alpha-actinin bridge runs through myotilin. Myotilin-fragment perturbation is not a FLNC knockout, and FLNC repeat-20 overexpression did not visibly disrupt assembly. [PMID:11038172](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2192634/)

The original FLNC/FATZ/myotilin study explicitly reports FLNC C-terminal contacts with beta1A integrin, FATZ1 and myotilin. These support structural connections between muscle Z-disc and membrane-associated partners. They do not establish a particular simultaneous ternary assembly or every natural product's affinity. Xin's other ligand interfaces and ankyrin's membrane-associated properties are not transferred to FLNC. This supports the single structural core without inventing a separate adaptor mechanism. [PMID:16076904](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16076904/), [PMID:16631741](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16631741/), [PMID:21223964](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21223964/)

## Binding specificity and product limits

Nine original protein-binding assertions receive evidence-backed replacements: three cytoskeletal-protein-binding contexts (myotilin twice and Xin), FATZ binding, integrin binding, three SH3-domain-binding contexts and ankyrin binding. Exact pair/context identity is preserved. The two remaining generic associations are retained as non-core under the explicit campaign instruction: FBXL22-associated FLNC degradation and RTF2-containing affinity-purified complexes do not establish a more specific FLNC molecular activity.

Six positive IntAct peptide-array records distinguish ABL1 residues 61–121, GRB2 residues 156–215 and CRK residues 132–192/256–296. Official UniProt domain coordinates identify the corresponding SH3 regions; CRK 256–296 is only part of its second annotated SH3 domain. The ABL1 and GRB2 records test FLNC peptides 260–273 and 1749–1762; the CRK records test 260–273. Exact target/construct claims come from the curated experimental records, not an independently recovered primary target table. The original abstract establishes the domain-peptide screen; its PLCgamma1 validation rate is not assigned to FLNC pairs. [PMID:17474147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17474147/), [IntAct Q14315](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/search?query=Q14315), [ABL1](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P00519/entry), [CRK](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P46108/entry), [GRB2](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P62993/entry)

Official UniProt declares Q14315-1 (2725 residues) and Q14315-2 (2692 residues). Both official FASTAs were checked: product 2 exactly equals product 1 after deletion of residues 1734–1766 (VSP_007579). This deletion contains the tested 1749–1762 peptide, while peptide 260–273 is retained. This is a sequence boundary, not proof that product 2 loses all full-length partner binding. The 33-residue splice deletion differs from the 78-residue muscle-filamin insertion discussed in the older localization study. Neither tags, experimental deletions, disease substitutions nor historical paralog names create an additional natural product. [UniProt Q14315](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q14315/entry)

## Contextual locations and donor scope

Human FLNC expressed in rat cardiac cells is cytoplasmic and associated with actin fibers; mutant aggregates do not redefine the normal location. Human HFF1 fraction proteomics independently identifies FLNC in focal adhesions. These studies preserve human construct versus host-species distinctions and do not establish equal localization of both natural products. [PMID:25351925](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25351925/), [PMID:21423176](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3279191/)

Rat D3ZHA0 retains experimental ankyrin-binding, sarcoplasm and sarcolemma evidence from PMID:21223964. Its original abstract supports the ankyrin-G interaction and adult muscle costamere localization, while independent PMID:11038172 supports human muscle compartments. The O70511-7 partner remains a rat Ank3 product; exact construct correspondence to that splice product was not recovered. Mouse Q8VHX6 carries muscle-cell-development evidence from PMID:16914736, whose abstract describes C2C12 differentiation/fusion and defective mouse myogenesis. These are explicit ortholog transfers, not relabeled human experiments. [Rat FLNC](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/D3ZHA0/entry), [Mouse FLNC](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8VHX6/entry), [PMID:16914736](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16914736/)

The cytoplasm assertions are true but covered by retained cytosol; sarcolemma entails membrane. Official is_a/part_of paths support these four TOO_GENERAL decisions. Z-disc/costamere ancestry does not itself entail the cytoskeleton term, so that independently supported structural location is retained without inventing an edge or claiming disjointness. HPA cytosol is retained on curator evidence without a new antibody/image audit. The Reactome summary supports cytosolic association; the detailed entity graph and FLNC stoichiometry were not independently replayed. [Cytosol](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005829), [Sarcolemma](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0042383), [Reactome R-HSA-430347](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-430347)

## Reference-specific access

The normal cache's full-text flag is recorded separately from what was actually read. Identifier/title verification does not claim full reproduction of a paper. No new publication quotations were authored.

- [PMID:11038172](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11038172/): Selected original Methods, Results and Discussion read, including purified human FLNC/myotilin fragment assays and endogenous human muscle localization. Human constructs in rat cardiomyocytes or mouse C2C12 cells are distinct. No figure-pixel or separate supplement audit; historical paralog language is not a modern natural-product assignment.
- [PMID:12393796](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12393796/): PMID/title identity is verified. Selected original author-copy sections establish FLNA/FLNB interactions, but a FLNC-specific assay was not independently recovered. The inherited cytoplasm assertion is retained as biologically correct but TOO_GENERAL using independent target localization; exact source-specific FLNC support remains unverified, without a wrong-paralog accusation.
- [PMID:16076904](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16076904/): Authentic original abstract explicitly reports FLNC C-terminal contacts with beta1A integrin, myotilin and FATZ1. Full pair-specific Methods, simultaneous ternary engagement, stoichiometry and natural-product mapping were not recovered.
- [PMID:16631741](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16631741/): Authentic original abstract and exact human-pair IntAct yeast/far-western records support FLNC-Xin association. Microscopy is separate colocalization evidence. Full original Methods were not recovered; Xin interfaces, splice behavior and partner functions are not transferred to FLNC.
- [PMID:17474147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17474147/): Original abstract and six exact positive IntAct peptide-array pair records read, with official ABL1/CRK/GRB2 SH3 coordinates. Complete primary target tables were not recovered. Curated fragment evidence is explicitly distinguished from the abstract and from an endogenous full-length interaction.
- [PMID:17987659](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17987659/): Original abstract and exact fetched record read; it explicitly identifies the nebulette-FLNC interaction. Full pair-specific construct/species allocation was not recovered. Original human and chicken partner contexts are preserved.
- [PMID:21223964](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21223964/): Original abstract reports ankyrin-G OTBD screening, muscle co-IP and costamere colocalization. Full primary Methods/images were unavailable. Rat Ank3 and donor D3ZHA0 records remain distinct from independent human muscle localization; exact O70511-7 assay mapping is unresolved.
- [PMID:21423176](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21423176/): Selected actual Methods/Results in the normal full-text cache read. Human HFF1 focal-adhesion fractions identify filamin-C with myosin-II-responsive enrichment. Fraction proteomics is not target-specific imaging or an isolated mechanosensing assay.
- [PMID:22972877](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22972877/): Original abstract identifies FLNC as a potential FBXL22 substrate and reports dose-dependent degradation with proteasome-inhibitor attenuation. Confirmed SKP1/CUL1 co-IP is not silently reassigned to FLNC; exact FLNC pair-specific Methods remain unrecovered.
- [PMID:25351925](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25351925/): Selected original author-repository PDF Methods/Results and Fig4 caption read. Tagged full-length human NM_001458 FLNC was tested in rat cardiac cells and additional mouse C2C12 assays. Engineered disease substitutions and pathological aggregation do not define natural product function; no image-pixel or separate SI audit.
- [PMID:33961781](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33961781/): Original study metadata/available text and two exact human-pair IntAct anti-tag affinity-purification records support RTF2-FLNC complex association in HCT116 and HEK293T. The latter is a spoke-expanded edge. No spectrum reanalysis, direct binary affinity or complete pair-specific Methods replay is claimed.
- [PMID:34405687](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34405687/): Selected original BioC Methods/Results/Discussion and caption context read. Isogenic human FLNC-null hiPSC cardiomyocytes show persistent sarcomere/actin disorganization, distinct from heterozygous variant behavior in the same conditions. No figures, videos or separate supplements were audited.
- [PMID:16914736](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16914736/): Original abstract and exact official mouse Q8VHX6 donor annotation read. C2C12 differentiation/fusion and Flnc-deficient mouse myogenesis evidence support the transferred developmental context. Full original Methods were not independently read; no human developmental experiment is asserted.
