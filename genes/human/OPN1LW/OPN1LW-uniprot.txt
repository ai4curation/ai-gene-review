CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled photoreceptor that selectively activates
CC       transducin G alpha protein (G(t)) in response to long-wavelength (red)
CC       light (PubMed:21314100, PubMed:2937147, PubMed:6140680). This
CC       activation leads to increased cGMP phosphodiesterase activity,
CC       resulting in decreased intracellular cGMP and subsequent closure of
CC       cGMP-gated ion channels (PubMed:21314100, PubMed:2937147,
CC       PubMed:6140680). The process is initiated when the opsin-bound cis-
CC       retinal chromophore absorbs a photon and isomerizes to all-trans-
CC       retinal, inducing a conformational change in the opsin that triggers
CC       the G protein-mediated phototransduction cascade (PubMed:21314100,
CC       PubMed:2937147, PubMed:6140680). Mediates visual perception of red
CC       light in cone photoreceptor cells (PubMed:12051694, PubMed:8213841,
CC       PubMed:8666378). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12051694,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21314100, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2937147,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:6140680, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8213841,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8666378}.

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: The red (OPN1LW), green (OPN1MW), and blue (OPN1SW)
CC       photoreceptor pigments are found in cone cells.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2937147}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Maximally activated by long-wavelength light near 560 nm,
CC       corresponding to the red region of the visible spectrum in retinal cone
CC       cell. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2937147}.
CC   -!- PTM: Contains one retinal chromophore covalently bound to a lysine
CC       residue via a Schiff base. Upon light absorption, the bound 11-cis-
CC       retinal isomerizes to all-trans-retinal. Following activation, the
CC       covalently bound all-trans-retinal is released through hydrolysis of
CC       the Schiff base, and OPN1 is regenerated by binding a new 11-cis-
CC       retinal molecule. {ECO:0000269|Ref.6}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated on some or all of the serine and threonine residues
CC       present in the C-terminal region.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Colorblindness, partial, protan series (CBP) [MIM:303900]: An
CC       X-linked color vision defect characterized by a dichromasy in which red
CC       and green are confused, with loss of luminance and shift of brightness
CC       and hue curves toward the short wave end of the spectrum. Dichromasy is
CC       due to the use of only two types of photoreceptors, blue plus red in
CC       deuteranopia and blue plus green in protanopia.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12051694}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
CC       affecting the gene represented in this entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Blue cone monochromacy (BCM) [MIM:303700]: A rare X-linked
CC       congenital stationary cone dysfunction syndrome characterized by the
CC       absence of functional long wavelength-sensitive and medium wavelength-
CC       sensitive cones in the retina. Color discrimination is severely
CC       impaired from birth, and vision is derived from the remaining preserved
CC       blue (S) cones and rod photoreceptors. BCM typically presents with
CC       reduced visual acuity, pendular nystagmus, and photophobia. Patients
CC       often have myopia. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30065301,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34445325, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35769953,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8213841, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8666378}. Note=The
CC       disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented in this
CC       entry.
