# EDN1 Curation Notes (Human Endothelin-1, UniProtKB:P05305)

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Gene symbol / ID**: `EDN1` (`UniProtKB:P05305`, `HGNC:3176`)
- **PANTHER family**: `PANTHER:PTHR13874:SF10` (`ENDOTHELIN-1`, within `PANTHER:PTHR13874` `ENDOTHELIN`; root PAINT node `PANTHER:PTN000356323`, clade node `PANTHER:PTN002676936`).
- **Preprohormone processing**: Synthesized primarily by vascular endothelial cells as a 212-amino-acid preproendothelin-1 (signal peptide residues 1-17). Cleaved at dibasic sites by furin-like proprotein convertases to yield the inactive 38-amino-acid intermediate big endothelin-1 (residues 53-90), which is then specifically cleaved at the Trp21-Val22 bond (residues 73-74) by endothelin-converting enzymes (`ECE1`, `ECE2`) to release the mature bioactive 21-residue bicyclic peptide Endothelin-1 (ET-1, residues 53-73), stabilized by two intramolecular disulfide bonds (`Cys53-Cys67` and `Cys55-Cys63`) [PMID:2649896, PMID:1917960].
- **Signaling mechanism**: Mature ET-1 acts as a potent paracrine/autocrine peptide hormone (`GO:0005179` *hormone activity*, child of `GO:0048018` *receptor ligand activity*) that binds with subnanomolar affinity to both the ET-1/ET-2-selective Endothelin-1 receptor (`EDNRA` / ETA, `GO:0031707` *endothelin A receptor binding*) on vascular smooth muscle cells and the nonselective Endothelin B receptor (`EDNRB` / ETB, `GO:0031708` *endothelin B receptor binding*) on endothelial and smooth muscle cells [PMID:1713452, PMID:8982507, Reactome:R-HSA-375281]. Agonist-bound `EDNRA` and `EDNRB` couple primarily to heterotrimeric Gq/11 proteins (`GNAQ`/`GNA11`) to stimulate phospholipase C-beta (`PLCB1`/`PLCB4`), generating inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate (IP3) and diacylglycerol (DAG) and mobilizing intracellular Ca2+ (`GO:0086100` *endothelin receptor signaling pathway* and `GO:0160135` *phospholipase C-activating endothelin receptor signaling pathway*) [PMID:1917960].
- **Physiological and developmental roles**: Potent vasoconstrictor and positive regulator of arterial and venous smooth muscle contraction (`GO:0014826`, `GO:0045987`) that maintains systemic arterial blood pressure (`GO:0003100`) [PMID:2649896, PMID:8982507]. During embryogenesis, `EDN1` signaling through `EDNRA` on cranial neural crest cells is essential for first and second pharyngeal arch patterning (`GO:0060021` *roof of mouth development*, `GO:0060037` *pharyngeal system development*, `GO:0048706` *embryonic skeletal system development*); pathogenic `EDN1` variants cause auriculocondylar syndrome 3 and isolated question-mark ears [PMID:24290376].

## Ligands in Signaling Pathways Assessment (Tranche 4C)
- In human GOA, `EDN1` carries `GO:0086100` (*endothelin receptor signaling pathway*, IEA from InterPro `IPR001926`) and `GO:0005179` (*hormone activity*, IBA/IEA), so `GO:0086100` passes the ligand check in human, mouse, and rat (though zebrafish `edn1` lacks `GO:0086100`).
- However, on the specific child pathway `GO:0160135` (*phospholipase C-activating endothelin receptor signaling pathway*), `EDN1` is missing (Category B gap), even though [PMID:1917960] directly demonstrated that 10 nM human ET-1 (and ET-2 and ET-3) stimulates phospholipase C to increase IP3 mass and mobilize intracellular Ca2+ in human cells. We therefore propose `NEW` (`IDA`) for `GO:0160135` (*phospholipase C-activating endothelin receptor signaling pathway*) backed by [PMID:1917960] and link `EDN1` to `MODULE:endothelin_receptor_signaling`.
- For the broad parent signaling terms `GO:0007186` (*G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway*, IEA) and `GO:0007202` (*activation of phospholipase C activity*, TAS `PMID:2844576`), we use `MODIFY` to `GO:0160135` (*phospholipase C-activating endothelin receptor signaling pathway*) and `GO:0086100` (*endothelin receptor signaling pathway*).
