CC   -!- FUNCTION: Calcium sensor of the mitochondrial calcium uniporter (MCU)
CC       channel, which senses calcium level via its EF-hand domains
CC       (PubMed:24503055, PubMed:24560927, PubMed:26903221, PubMed:28615291,
CC       PubMed:30699349, PubMed:31397067, PubMed:32494073, PubMed:32667285,
CC       PubMed:32762847, PubMed:32790952). MICU1 and MICU2 form a disulfide-
CC       linked heterodimer that stimulates and inhibits MCU activity, depending
CC       on the concentration of calcium (PubMed:24560927, PubMed:26903221,
CC       PubMed:28615291, PubMed:30699349, PubMed:31397067, PubMed:32148862,
CC       PubMed:32494073, PubMed:32667285, PubMed:32762847, PubMed:32790952). At
CC       low calcium levels, MICU1 occludes the pore of the MCU channel,
CC       preventing mitochondrial calcium uptake (PubMed:32494073,
CC       PubMed:32667285, PubMed:32762847). At higher calcium levels, calcium-
CC       binding to MICU1 and MICU2 induces a conformational change that weakens
CC       MCU-MICU1 interactions and moves the MICU1-MICU2 heterodimer away from
CC       the pore, allowing calcium permeation through the MCU channel
CC       (PubMed:32494073, PubMed:32667285, PubMed:32762847).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24503055, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24560927,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26903221, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28615291,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30699349, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31397067,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32148862, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32494073,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32667285, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32762847,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32790952}.

CC       (PubMed:24560927, PubMed:28615291, PubMed:30699349, PubMed:31397067,
