## Key biology

Human IL18 was cloned as IFN-gamma-inducing factor from human liver cDNA, and
recombinant human IGIF induced IFN-gamma from mitogen-stimulated PBMC and
enhanced NK-cell cytotoxicity [PMID:8666798 "The recombinant human IGIF induced
IFN-gamma production by mitogen-stimulated PBMC and enhanced NK cell
cytotoxicity"].

Natural human IL-18 is generated by proteolytic maturation in the cytosol.
Akita et al. purified natural IL-18 from THP.1 cytosolic extract and showed that
caspase-1 cleaves pro-IL-18 after Asp36 to generate mature IL-18, while
caspase-3 cleaves pro- and mature IL-18 into inactive products [PMID:9334240
"caspase-1, which cleaves prohIL-18 at the Asp36-Tyr37 site to generate the
mature hIL-18"].

Mature IL-18 signals as an extracellular IL-18 receptor agonist. Kato et al.
solved the IL-18 structure and used mutagenesis to define three receptor
activation surfaces [PMID:14528293 "three sites that are important for receptor
activation"], while the later ternary-complex structure showed human IL-18 bound
to IL-18 receptor alpha and beta ectodomains [PMID:25500532 "we present a
crystal structure of human IL-18 bound to the two receptor extracellular
domains"].

GSDMD pores can release mature IL-18 preferentially over pro-IL-18. Xia et al.
showed that the mature IL-1 family cytokines pass the GSDMD pore faster than
their precursors [PMID:33883744 "Mature IL-1beta and IL-18 were secreted
substantially faster than their precursors"].

IL18 is also a direct target of human noncanonical inflammatory caspases: CASP4
and CASP5 cleave pro-IL-18 efficiently, and CASP4 uses the same tetrapeptide
site as CASP1 [PMID:37993714 "Caspase-4 cleaves the same tetrapeptide site in
pro-IL-18 as caspase-1"].

TMED10 supplies another unconventional secretion route for leaderless cargoes
and mediates cargo entry into the ERGIC, but the IL18 review should not turn that
into an IL18 transport annotation because IL18 is cargo, not the transporter
[PMID:32272059 "TMED10 as a protein channel for the vesicle entry and secretion
of many leaderless cargoes"].
