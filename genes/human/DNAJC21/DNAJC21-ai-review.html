Gene Description

DNAJC21 is a J-domain protein involved in large (60S) ribosomal subunit biogenesis through nucleolar RNA association and cytoplasmic cooperation with the Hsp70 chaperone HSPA8 and assembly factors PA2G4 and ZNF622. It associates with precursor 45S rRNA and supports PA2G4 recycling during subunit maturation. Its N-terminal J domain contains the conserved HPD motif, and the protein contains two C2H2 zinc fingers. DNAJC21 occupies nucleolar and cytosolic pools and redistributes toward the nucleus when ribosomal RNA synthesis is inhibited. Alternative transcripts encode three predicted protein isoforms that differ between the zinc fingers. Biallelic pathogenic variants cause a cancer-prone bone marrow failure syndrome associated with defective ribosome biogenesis.

Core Functions

Hsp70-partner activity in cytoplasmic 60S subunit maturation: DNAJC21 associates with HSPA8, PA2G4 and ZNF622 in nuclease-treated human lysates, and the P32A HPD-loop mutation selectively impairs HSPA8 association. DNAJC21 loss perturbs PA2G4 traffic, with excess PA2G4 in 60S/80S fractions of affected-individual cells; wild-type re-expression restores that traffic. Together, these observations support a co-chaperone/assembly-factor contribution to large-subunit biogenesis. They do not directly measure human HSPA8 ATPase stimulation or reconstituted PA2G4 release, establish a purified binary interface, or define fixed complex stoichiometry.

AIGR Review Snapshot

  • Description: DNAJC21 (DnaJ homolog subfamily C member 21; also DNAJA5) is a J-domain co-chaperone of the HSP70 system that functions in ribosome biogenesis, specifically in the maturation of the large (60S) ribosomal subunit. It contains an N-terminal J-domain and two C2H2 zinc fingers within an otherwise disordered C-terminal region. DNAJC21 localizes to the cytoplasm, the nucleus and especially the nucleolus, associates with precursor 45S rRNA, and works with the HSP70 chaperone HSPA8 and the cofactors PA2G4 (a 60S nuclear-export factor) and ZNF622 to drive late nucleolar rRNA biogenesis and cytoplasmic maturation/recycling of the 60S subunit. It is the human counterpart of the yeast 60S-maturation factor Jjj1/Zuo1-like J-protein. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause a cancer-prone bone marrow failure syndrome (BMFS3, Shwachman-Diamond-like), establishing DNAJC21 as a ribosomopathy gene.
