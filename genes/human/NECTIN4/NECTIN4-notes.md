# NECTIN4 curation notes

Deep research (falcon/perplexity-lite) unavailable; notes from UniProt Q96NY8 and cached papers.

- Topology: signal 1-31, extracellular 32-349 (IgV-IgC-IgC), TM 350-370, cytoplasmic 371-510 (afadin-binding C-terminus).
- Adherens junctions with afadin (UniProt, PMID:11544254); homophilic and nectin-1 heterophilic adhesion
  [PMID:22902367 "heterophilic adhesion has been observed between nectin-1 and nectin-3; nectin-1 and nectin-4"].
- Measles virus epithelial receptor via IgV domain [PMID:22048310; PMID:23202587].
- TIGIT ligand inhibiting NK cells [PMID:32503945] - single-lab finding, non-core.
- ADAM17 shedding of entire ectodomain [PMID:15784625 "The soluble Nectin-4 form (43 kDa) is formed by the entire Nectin-4 ectodomain."].

Localization conclusion: plasma membrane (adherens junctions, lateral membrane), single-pass type I;
ectodomain extracellular and surface-accessible (ADC target); shed ectodomain in serum.
