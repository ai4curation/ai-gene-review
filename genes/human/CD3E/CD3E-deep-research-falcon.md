# Comprehensive Research Report: CD3E Gene (CD3 Epsilon Chain)

## Gene Identity Verification

The gene CD3E (UniProt accession P07766) encodes the T-cell surface glycoprotein CD3 epsilon chain in humans (Homo sapiens). This protein is a critical component of the T-cell receptor (TCR)-CD3 complex and matches the specified UniProt entry, confirming we are investigating the correct gene (mariuzza2020thestructuralbasis pages 2-4, menon2023modulatingtcell pages 2-4).

## 1. Protein Structure and Molecular Architecture

### Overall Organization

CD3 epsilon (CD3ε) is a type-I single-pass transmembrane protein that functions as an invariant signaling subunit of the TCR-CD3 complex (chandler2020tcellactivation pages 3-5). The complete TCR-CD3 assembly exhibits a 1:1:1:1 stoichiometry of TCRαβ:CD3γε:CD3δε:CD3ζζ, with CD3ε present in two copies—one paired with CD3γ and one with CD3δ (mariuzza2020thestructuralbasis pages 2-4, mariuzza2020thestructuralbasis pages 1-2).

**Extracellular Domain**: The N-terminal extracellular region contains an immunoglobulin-like domain that forms heterodimers with either CD3δ or CD3γ through parallel β-strand contacts and side-to-side hydrophobic interactions (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 2-3, chandler2020tcellactivation pages 3-5). This domain is essential for assembly and folding of the complete TCR-CD3 complex.

**Connecting Peptide**: A short membrane-proximal segment containing a conserved CxxC motif forms an intrachain disulfide bond that contributes to TCR-CD3 assembly and outside-in signaling transmission (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 2-3).

**Transmembrane Domain**: The single-pass transmembrane helix participates in an eight-helix bundle formed by all TCR-CD3 components. Acidic residues in CD3ε engage basic lysine and arginine residues of TCRαβ through electrostatic interactions, while hydrophobic contacts stabilize the complex (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 2-3, mariuzza2020thestructuralbasis pages 2-4).

**Cytoplasmic Tail**: The intracellular region contains multiple functional motifs arranged sequentially from membrane-proximal to C-terminal: a basic residue-rich sequence (BRS), a di-glycine motif, a proline-rich sequence (PRS), and an immunoreceptor tyrosine-based activation motif (ITAM) with consensus sequence YxxL/I–x6–8–YxxL/I (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8).

### Recent Structural Insights (2023-2024)

Recent cryo-EM structures of the human TCR-CD3 complex resolved the organization of CD3 subunits around the αβTCR, showing that both CD3γε and CD3δε are positioned on the same side of the receptor in both unliganded and peptide-MHC-bound states (natarajan2024insitucellsurface pages 1-2). A 2024 in situ crosslinking study identified an extracellular arrangement of CD3ε′–CD3γ–CD3ε–CD3δ around the αβTCR and demonstrated that the cell-surface complex closely resembles detergent-purified structures (natarajan2024insitucellsurface pages 1-2). Importantly, minimal subunit reorientations occur upon activating antibody or pMHC-tetramer binding, suggesting the absence of large inactive-to-active conformational states in the extracellular regions (natarajan2024insitucellsurface pages 1-2).

A groundbreaking 2024 study identified a highly conserved di-glycine motif (Gly169-Gly170 in human CD3ε) in the cytoplasmic tail, positioned between the BRS and PRS (tercero2024adiglycinemotif pages 1-4). This motif appears critical for transmitting ligand-induced allosteric changes from the TCR to cytoplasmic signaling components. The G169A mutation impaired stimulation-induced Nck binding, reduced CD3ε tyrosine phosphorylation, selectively diminished CD3ζ phosphorylation at Tyr83, and impaired Lck phosphorylation at Ser59 (tercero2024adiglycinemotif pages 1-4, tercero2024adiglycinemotif pages 6-8).

## 2. Primary Molecular Function

### Signal Transduction Module

CD3ε functions as a critical signal transduction adaptor rather than a ligand-binding receptor. Its primary role is to convert extracellular TCR engagement into intracellular biochemical signals (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5).

### Kinase and Adaptor Recruitment

**LCK Recruitment**: The BRS of CD3ε recruits the Src-family kinase LCK through ionic interactions with LCK's unique domain (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8, woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 1-2, xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 6-7). LCK can also bind the RK motif within the ITAM region through its SH3 domain, positioning the kinase near the receptor for ITAM phosphorylation (woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 1-2). This recruitment mechanism is distinctive to CD3ε among CD3 chains and represents a key early event in TCR signaling initiation (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 6-7).

**NCK Recruitment**: The PRS recruits the adaptor protein NCK through NCK's SH3.1 domain (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8, woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 1-2). This interaction becomes accessible following ligand-induced conformational changes that expose the normally cryptic PRS (hartl2020noncanonicalbindingof pages 21-23, woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 13-13). NCK binding connects the TCR to cytoskeletal recruitment, immunological synapse formation, and amplification of proximal signaling including CD3 ITAM and ZAP70 phosphorylation (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8). The NCK-CD3ε interaction is reported to be essential for TCR signaling and immune synapse formation (hartl2020noncanonicalbindingof pages 21-23, tercero2024adiglycinemotif pages 11-14).

### ITAM-Dependent Signaling

CD3ε contains one ITAM that contributes to the total of ten ITAMs in the complete αβ TCR-CD3 complex (CD3γ, CD3δ, and each of the two CD3ε chains contribute one ITAM each, while CD3ζζ contributes six) (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5, chandler2020tcellactivation pages 3-5). Following TCR engagement with cognate peptide-MHC, LCK and FYN phosphorylate tyrosine residues within CD3 ITAMs, creating docking sites for the tandem SH2 domains of ZAP70 (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5, woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 1-2, chandler2020tcellactivation pages 5-7). This initiates the downstream signaling cascade.

Dynamic phosphorylation of the CD3ε ITAM is essential for proper signaling coordination. Recent 2024 studies using phosphomimetic CD3ε variants demonstrated that irreversible tyrosine phosphorylation prevents signal transduction: Y39E reduced NCK binding, Y50E abolished LCK recruitment while increasing NCK binding, and the double mutation permitted ZAP70 binding but reduced both LCK and NCK interactions (woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 1-2). These findings emphasize that CD3ε orchestrates optimal TCR signaling through dynamically regulated phosphorylation states rather than through static protein-protein interactions.

### Allosteric Regulation

CD3ε participates in ligand-induced allosteric changes within the TCR-CD3 complex. In resting T cells, the cytoplasmic domain is held against the plasma membrane through electrostatic interactions between the positively charged BRS and negatively charged phospholipids, sequestering ITAM tyrosines and preventing inappropriate phosphorylation (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 6-7, mariuzza2020thestructuralbasis pages 8-9). Following peptide-MHC engagement, CD3ε dissociates from the membrane, exposing signaling regions and permitting kinase access (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8, xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 6-7). This conformational regulation represents a key mechanism for maintaining signaling quiescence in resting cells while enabling rapid activation upon antigen recognition.

## 3. Cellular Localization and Trafficking

### Plasma Membrane Localization

CD3ε resides primarily at the T-cell plasma membrane as an integral component of the assembled TCR-CD3 complex (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5, menon2023modulatingtcell pages 2-4). The protein is held within this complex through complementary electrostatic interactions in the transmembrane region, allowing the complete receptor to move laterally through the lipid bilayer as one functional unit (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5, menon2023modulatingtcell pages 2-4).

### Assembly and Quality Control

CD3ε is essential for TCR-CD3 complex assembly and surface expression. It acts as a key chaperone that pairs with both CD3γ and CD3δ to form the CD3γε and CD3δε dimers, which are necessary for proper folding of all CD3 components and thymocyte development (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5). TCR-CD3 assembly proceeds sequentially: TCRαβ first associates with CD3δε, then CD3γε joins, and finally CD3ζζ is incorporated (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 2-3). Unpaired or incompletely assembled CD3/TCR subunits are retained in the endoplasmic reticulum and targeted for lysosomal degradation (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5).

### Endosomal Signaling Compartments

A major recent advance reveals that TCR-CD3 signaling is not restricted to the plasma membrane. Following T-cell activation, signaling occurs in specialized intracellular IRAP (insulin-responsive aminopeptidase)/Syntaxin 6-positive endosomal compartments (evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 4-5, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 2-3, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 1-2). These vesicles contain multiple TCR signalosome components including ZAP-70, LAT, LCK, CD3ε, and CD3ζ (evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 4-5).

The TCR-CD3ζ complex is internalized through clathrin- and AP2/dynamin-2-dependent endocytosis into these IRAP-containing endosomes, where CD3ζ remains signaling competent (evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 1-2, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 8-9). IRAP physically associates with CD3ζ and regulates the intracellular availability and signalosome assembly function of this signaling pool (evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 3-4, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 8-9). Importantly, IRAP's S-acylation is required for its interaction with CD3ζ and for effective TCR signaling (evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 3-4, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 8-9).

IRAP-positive endosomes polarize toward the immune synapse in antigen-stimulated T cells, and the intracellular CD3ζ pool is recruited continuously to the dynamic synapse (evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 3-4, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 8-9). This endosomal signaling compartment is particularly important for responses to weak or suboptimal TCR stimulation and contributes to T-cell survival and anti-tumor immunity (evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 8-9, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 5-6, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 9-10).

### Regulated Membrane Association

The cytoplasmic domain of CD3ε exhibits dynamic membrane association regulated by calcium and phosphorylation state (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8, chandler2020tcellactivation pages 5-7). Calcium-mediated disruption of lipid interactions can provide an additional route to ITAM exposure and can activate bystander TCRs to amplify signaling (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8). This regulation is important for both thymocyte development and peripheral T-cell function (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 6-7).

## 4. Signaling Pathways and Biological Processes

### Proximal Signaling Events

CD3ε ITAM phosphorylation initiates a highly orchestrated signaling cascade. Upon peptide-MHC binding and CD3ε membrane dissociation, recruited LCK phosphorylates tyrosine residues within CD3 ITAMs (woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 1-2, xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8, xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 6-7). These phosphorylated ITAMs serve as docking sites for ZAP-70, which is then activated through phosphorylation (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5, woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 1-2, chandler2020tcellactivation pages 5-7).

Phosphorylated CD3ε participates in cooperative binding of NCK through both its SH3.1 and SH2 domains, with the SH2 domain engaging phosphorylated Tyr177 in the ITAM region (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8). This NCK recruitment contributes to cytoskeleton remodeling, immunological synapse formation, and activation of downstream pathways including AKT and ERK1/2 (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8).

### Downstream Signaling Cascades

The CD3ε-initiated signaling cascade propagates through multiple interconnected pathways to activate key transcription factors:

**Calcium Signaling**: TCR activation triggers Ca²⁺ influx through CRAC channels, and elevated calcium disrupts ionic interactions between CD3 cytoplasmic domains and membrane lipids, exposing additional ITAM tyrosines and amplifying signaling (xu2020structuralunderstandingof pages 7-8). This positive feedback loop enhances T-cell sensitivity.

**NFAT, NF-κB, and AP-1 Pathways**: CD3 ITAM phosphorylation ultimately activates these three major transcription factor families through production of second messengers Ca²⁺ and IP3 and activation of Ras (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5, woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 1-2, woessner2024phosphomimeticcd3εvariants pages 13-13). These pathways converge to produce the characteristic transcriptional signature of activated T cells, including cytokine production, proliferation, and effector differentiation.

**Co-stimulation Integration**: Full T-cell activation requires integration of CD3ε-initiated signals with CD28 co-stimulation, which recruits PI3K, Grb2, Gads, Itk/Tec kinases, LCK, and PKCθ to amplify survival, proliferation, and cytokine-producing pathways (chandler2020tcellactivation pages 5-7).

### Regulated Sequence of Signaling Events

Recent evidence supports a model in which CD3ε coordinates a regulated sequence of interactions between TCR subunits and associated effector proteins (tercero2024adiglycinemotif pages 1-4). The di-glycine motif appears to facilitate transmission of allosteric changes that sequentially expose or organize binding sites for LCK, NCK, and downstream effectors, while also regulating phosphorylation of CD3ζ at specific tyrosine residues (tercero2024adiglycinemotif pages 1-4, tercero2024adiglycinemotif pages 6-8). This temporal and spatial organization of the signaling cascade represents a sophisticated mechanism for fine-tuning T-cell activation.

## 5. Clinical Relevance and Therapeutic Applications

### CD3E Deficiency and Immunodeficiency

CD3ε is absolutely essential for T-cell development and function. CD3ε deficiency results in severe combined immunodeficiency (SCID), characterized by T-cell lymphopenia or absent mature T cells, impaired T-cell activation, and defective mitogenesis (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5). CD3ε-null mice completely lack mature thymocytes, and this developmental block can be rescued by reintroducing a CD3ε transgene (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5). Among CD3 subunit deficiencies, ε, ζ, and δ defects are particularly life-threatening and lethal, representing some of the most severe forms of inherited SCID (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 4-5).

### Bispecific T-Cell Engagers

CD3ε serves as a critical target for bispecific T-cell engaging antibodies that redirect cytotoxic T cells toward tumor cells. **Blinatumomab**, which engages CD3ε and CD19, is FDA-approved for acute lymphoblastic leukemia and represents a major clinical success (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 10-11, menon2023modulatingtcell pages 7-8). This bispecific T-cell engager (BiTE) activates polyclonal T cells against CD19-positive B-cell malignancies while concentrating activation at the tumor site to reduce systemic effects (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 10-11).

**Tebentafusp**, an engineered TCR-CD3 scFv fusion protein, is approved for HLA-A*02:01-positive metastatic uveal melanoma, where it activates polyclonal T cells against gp100-positive tumor cells and improves survival outcomes (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 10-11, menon2023modulatingtcell pages 7-8).

**MP0533**, a multispecific CD33×CD123×CD70 CD3-engaging DARPin, represents a next-generation approach for acute myeloid leukemia (AML). This agent is designed to induce selective T-cell-mediated killing of AML leukemic stem cells by requiring coexpression of at least two of three target antigens, thereby improving selectivity and reducing cytokine release compared to single-antigen engagers (bianchi2024thecd33xcd123xcd70multispecific pages 22-23). MP0533 is currently in phase 1 clinical evaluation (bianchi2024thecd33xcd123xcd70multispecific pages 22-23).

### Immunomodulatory Anti-CD3 Antibodies

Anti-CD3 monoclonal antibodies have diverse clinical applications beyond oncology:

**Transplantation**: Muromonab (OKT3) was historically used to treat acute cellular rejection after solid organ transplantation, although its strong agonist activity caused cytokine release syndrome and systemic adverse effects (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 8-10).

**Autoimmune Disease**: Modified anti-CD3 antibodies with reduced agonist activity are being developed for tolerance induction. **Teplizumab**, an Fc-receptor-nonbinding humanized anti-CD3 antibody, promotes regulatory T-cell responses and received FDA approval to delay onset of type 1 diabetes (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 8-10). Clinical studies demonstrated improved insulin production and metabolic control (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 7-8, menon2023modulatingtcell pages 8-10). **Otelixizumab** preserved pancreatic beta cells in recently diagnosed type 1 diabetes patients (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 8-10).

Oral administration of anti-CD3 antibodies has shown promise for promoting tolerogenic responses. Oral OKT3 demonstrated safety in hepatitis C nonresponders with increased regulatory T cells and improved liver enzyme levels, as well as regulatory T-cell induction and improved insulin resistance in nonalcoholic steatohepatitis (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 10-11, menon2023modulatingtcell pages 20-21).

### Novel CAR-T Cell Engineering

Recent research has revolutionized CAR-T cell design by exploring CD3ε as an alternative intracellular signaling domain to the conventional CD3ζ chain. FDA-approved CARs typically combine a costimulatory domain (such as 4-1BB or CD28) with the CD3ζ intracellular domain, which contains three ITAMs (cardenas2023harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-2, minguet2022harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-4).

**CD3ε-Based CARs**: Systematic engineering of CARs containing CD3δ, CD3ε, or CD3γ cytoplasmic tails revealed that these single-ITAM CD3 chains unexpectedly outperformed conventional CD3ζ CARs in vivo (minguet2022harnessingcd3diversity pages 12-14, cardenas2023harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-2, minguet2022harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-4). The CD3ε intracellular domain showed superior tumor therapy in preclinical models, with transcriptomic and proteomic analyses revealing differences in activation potential, metabolism, and resistance to stimulation-induced dysfunction (cardenas2023harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-2, minguet2022harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-4, minguet2022harnessingcd3diversity pages 9-12).

**Mechanistic Optimization**: CD3ε-based CARs require careful engineering of signaling motifs. The ITAM, BRS, and RK motifs are important for CAR-T cytotoxicity, activation, calcium flux, and antigen sensitivity, with the BRS and RK motifs recruiting LCK and providing complementary contributions to signaling (minguet2022harnessingcd3diversity pages 12-14). Dimeric CAR designs improved functionality, particularly for CD3ε CARs which showed poor expression as monomers due to endoplasmic reticulum retention signals (cardenas2023harnessingcd3diversity pages 9-10).

**Dual CD3ζ/CD3ε CARs**: Adding the full CD3ε cytoplasmic domain to CD19 CARs that retain CD3ζ creates a balanced signaling architecture. CD3ε contributes an inhibitory ITAM that recruits the inhibitory kinase CSK, thereby attenuating the CD3ζ/ZAP70/PLCγ1 axis and reducing excessive cytokine release while maintaining cytotoxicity (wu2020multiplesignalingroles pages 9-12, wu2020multiplesignalingroles pages 14-15). Simultaneously, the CD3ε BRS supports AKT-mTORC1 signaling, improving CAR-T persistence (wu2020multiplesignalingroles pages 14-15). This optimization strategy produced better tumor control and survival in xenograft models for both CD19 and mesothelin CARs (wu2020multiplesignalingroles pages 9-12).

A 2023 Nature Immunology study identified the phosphatase SHP-1 as a new CD3δ interaction partner that binds the phosphorylated C-terminal tyrosine of the CD3δ ITAM, attenuating activation signals and potentially preventing exhaustion (cardenas2023harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-2). This finding has implications for understanding how CD3 chain diversity tunes CAR function.

### CD3-Targeting Aptamers

DNA and RNA aptamers targeting CD3ε represent an economical, mass-producible, reversible platform for T-cell modulation. A DNA aptamer selected against human CD3ε displayed low-nanomolar affinity and, after truncation and dimerization, induced CD69 expression in vitro, indicating agonistic T-cell activation (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 11-12). The limited persistence and reversibility of aptamers could help reduce off-target effects compared to antibody-based approaches (menon2023modulatingtcell pages 11-12).

## 6. Current Understanding and Expert Analysis

### Structural Plasticity and Signaling Initiation

The mechanism of TCR activation remains an area of active investigation. Recent structural and biophysical studies support an allosteric model in which peptide-MHC binding triggers conformational changes that propagate from the TCR extracellular region through the transmembrane domain to the cytoplasmic tails (mariuzza2020thestructuralbasis pages 2-4, mariuzza2020thestructuralbasis pages 8-9). However, 2024 in situ crosslinking data indicate minimal large-scale reorientations in the extracellular CD3 subunits upon activation, suggesting that subtle dynamic changes or transmembrane-localized rearrangements are more important than dramatic conformational shifts (natarajan2024insitucellsurface pages 1-2).

The discovery of the di-glycine motif as a critical flexible hinge in the CD3ε cytoplasmic tail provides new mechanistic insight into how allosteric changes are transmitted to recruit signaling proteins (tercero2024adiglycinemotif pages 1-4). This finding bridges structural and functional models by identifying a specific sequence element that enables conformational adaptability.

### Endosomal Signaling Paradigm

The demonstration of IRAP-dependent endosomal TCR signaling represents a paradigm shift in understanding T-cell activation. Rather than viewing endocytosis solely as a mechanism for signal termination, the field now recognizes that internalized TCR-CD3 complexes continue to signal from specialized endosomal platforms that are insulated from plasma membrane phosphatases and may amplify or sustain signaling under suboptimal stimulation conditions (evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 4-5, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 1-2, evnouchidou2020irapdependentendosomalt pages 8-9). This has important implications for understanding T-cell responses to weak antigens, tumor immunity, and potentially for optimizing adoptive T-cell therapies.

### CAR Design Principles

The finding that single-ITAM CD3 chains can outperform the triple-ITAM CD3ζ chain challenges the assumption that more ITAMs necessarily produce stronger or better T-cell activation (cardenas2023harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-2, minguet2022harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-4). Instead, optimal CAR function appears to depend on signal quality, motif composition, and the balance between activating and inhibitory signals. The ability to tune CAR signaling by selecting specific CD3 chain intracellular domains, engineering motif combinations, and incorporating inhibitory modules like CSK-recruiting ITAMs opens new avenues for rational CAR optimization (wu2020multiplesignalingroles pages 9-12, wu2020multiplesignalingroles pages 14-15, cardenas2023harnessingcd3diversity pages 1-2).

### Future Directions

Key areas for future investigation include: (1) determining the precise structural rearrangements in the transmembrane and cytoplasmic regions during activation, (2) elucidating the temporal sequence and spatial organization of CD3ε signalosome assembly, (3) understanding how IRAP-dependent endosomal signaling integrates with plasma membrane signaling to determine overall T-cell responses, (4) translating CD3ε mechanistic insights into improved CAR designs with enhanced safety and efficacy profiles, and (5) developing CD3-targeting agents with tunable agonist versus antagonist activity for treating autoimmune disease, preventing transplant rejection, or enhancing anti-tumor immunity.

## Summary

CD3E encodes the CD3 epsilon chain, an essential signaling component of the T-cell receptor complex in humans. The protein functions as a molecular organizer that couples antigen recognition to intracellular signaling through recruitment of the kinase LCK and the adaptor NCK, ITAM-dependent ZAP-70 activation, and initiation of calcium, NFAT, NF-κB, and MAPK pathways. CD3ε exhibits remarkable structural sophistication, with multiple cytoplasmic motifs (BRS, di-glycine, PRS, ITAM) that coordinate dynamic, allosterically regulated signaling events at both the plasma membrane and in specialized IRAP-positive endosomal compartments. CD3ε deficiency causes severe combined immunodeficiency, while CD3ε-targeting therapeutic approaches—including bispecific T-cell engagers, immunomodulatory antibodies, and next-generation CAR-T cells incorporating CD3ε signaling domains—represent major advances in cancer immunotherapy, transplantation, and autoimmune disease treatment. Recent 2023-2024 studies continue to reveal new mechanistic insights into CD3ε function, including the role of the di-glycine motif in allosteric signal transmission and the optimization of CAR designs through CD3 chain diversification.
