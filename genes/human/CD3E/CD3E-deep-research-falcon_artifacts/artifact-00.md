| Basic residue-rich sequence (BRS) | Membrane-proximal portion of the cytoplasmic tail, upstream of the PRS and ITAM | Binds acidic inner-leaflet phospholipids, reversibly sequestering the tail at the membrane; also recruits LCK and helps position it for CD3-ITAM phosphorylation. | Proline-rich sequence (PRS) | Cytoplasmic tail, between the BRS/di-glycine region and ITAM; normally conformationally cryptic | Becomes accessible during receptor triggering and binds the NCK SH3.1 domain, connecting the TCR to cytoskeletal remodeling, immune-synapse organization, and amplification of proximal signaling. (pqac-00000044, pqac-00000047) |

(pqac-00000031, pqac-00000043) | Indirectly regulates NCK, LCK, CD3ζ, ZAP70, LAT, and ERK signaling | Potential synthetic-receptor tuning site and mechanistic disease-variant candidate; no established clinical intervention currently targets it. (pqac-00000031, pqac-00000043) |

(pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000022) | LCK, FYN, ZAP70, and phosphorylation-state-dependent signalosome proteins | Central to anti-CD3 and bispecific-engager activity and an alternative CAR intracellular signaling module.
