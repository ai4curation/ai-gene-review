# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ARMCX6 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ARMCX6 is an X-linked armadillo repeat-containing protein whose direct mechanistic characterization is limited to its subcellular distribution and a self-associating transmembrane domain. GFP-tagged ARMCX6 localizes to the cytoplasm of HeLa cells [PMID:22993554]. Its transmembrane domain is capable of homotypic self-interaction, with an interface enriched in GxxxG motifs and composed of conserved, coevolved, polar residues [PMID:33209210]. Beyond these observations, no ARMCX6-specific function, substrate, or pathway role has been experimentally established in the available corpus; proposed roles in TRAK2/MIRO-dependent mitochondrial trafficking derive from subfamily-level data rather than direct study of ARMCX6 [PMID:33267763].
