CC   -!- FUNCTION: Sodium-coupled antiporter of neutral amino acids. In a tri-
CC       substrate transport cycle, exchanges neutral amino acids between the
CC       extracellular and intracellular compartments, coupled to the inward
CC       cotransport of at least one sodium ion (PubMed:17094966,
CC       PubMed:23756778, PubMed:26492990, PubMed:29872227, PubMed:34741534,
CC       PubMed:8702519, PubMed:39095408, PubMed:27272177). The preferred
CC       substrate is the essential amino acid L-glutamine, a precursor for
CC       biosynthesis of proteins, nucleotides and amine sugars as well as an
CC       alternative fuel for mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation. Exchanges
CC       L-glutamine with other neutral amino acids such as L-serine, L-
