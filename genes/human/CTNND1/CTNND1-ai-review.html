Gene Description

CTNND1 encodes p120-catenin, an armadillo-repeat protein that binds cadherin cytoplasmic tails and controls cadherin surface stability. At adherens junctions it supports persistent cell-cell adhesion by protecting cadherins from turnover while allowing regulated junction remodeling. Cadherin-free long p120 products can restrain RhoA activation; cadherin engagement and phosphorylation modulate this regulation. Cytoplasmic and nuclear pools interact with signaling proteins and the transcription factor Kaiso. Alternative translation starts and splicing generate products with different N- and C-terminal sequences and distinct patterns of nuclear accumulation and effects on cell morphology.
