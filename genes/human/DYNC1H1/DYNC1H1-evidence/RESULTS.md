The measurements do not imply secretion, physiological RNA regulation or a purified binary interface. In particular, the OpenCell field named `pval` is retained as supplied, and its value is not relabeled as a raw probability. The author-thesis RNA-capture row retains its original group and five numerical columns. Source access and missing supplementary material are described in the [evidence guide](../DYNC1H1-source-evidence.md).

The PSEN2 source reports a positive Y2H pair and reciprocal coimmunoprecipitation. The paper's 140-kDa DYNC1H1-reactive band and unrecovered clone/antibody identity do not authenticate the full heavy chain; the corresponding annotation is now UNDECIDED. Database reference-sequence checksums do not resolve that experimental-identity question.

# Independent partner classification for the PMID:32814053 pairs

The binding observations and partner classifications are separate evidence. The [original study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108050), also available as an [institutional PDF](https://pub.dzne.de/record/153392/files/DZNE-2020-01389.pdf), used non-autoactivating baits, four independent screens, three reporter readouts, and subsequent pairwise retests. Each of the 17 selected pairs has linked IntAct pooling, validated two-hybrid, and array records under [IM-28217](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/search?query=pubid:32814053). These are three stages of the same study, not three independent studies. The original Y2H network contained 13,736 gene-level interactions; the 30,374-interaction extended network additionally incorporated earlier data. Orthogonal DULIP testing covered a subset and is not demonstrated for each pair below.

The table reports independently curated partner identities, not activities transferred to DYNC1H1. Official UniProt FUNCTION, catalytic, feature, and GO fields were inspected; their cited experiments were not all independently reread. Similarity-derived classification is explicitly identified. The ten proposed GO classes are nonobsolete molecular-function descendants of protein binding in the saved official QuickGO responses; their definitions specify the class of bound protein, not regulation of that protein's activity or its physiological pathway.

The three exact alternative sequences above were independently reconstructed from official variant features and matched the saved official FASTAs and IntAct participant ROGIDs. ROGID equality authenticates reference participant identity, not the full experimental ORF or an isoform-discriminating assay. No DYNC1H1 construct boundary is supplied in these selected records; FA2H is the only selected partner with a recorded binding-region feature. No native interaction, subcellular encounter, active-site contact, or partner pathway is inferred from these assays. These limitations apply equally to the refined and generic pairs.
