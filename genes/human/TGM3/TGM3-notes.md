# TGM3 notes

## Manual review notes

- Accept the direct TGase3 enzymology and calcium-binding rows from PMID:11980702, PMID:12679341, PMID:8099584, PMID:21282207, and PMID:27866708.
- The broad `catalytic activity`, `acyltransferase activity`, and `protein modification process` rows are less precise than protein-glutamine gamma-glutamyltransferase activity and peptide crosslinking; use MODIFY with supported replacements instead of keeping them as separate biology.
- Cytoplasm is well supported by UniProt and by TGM3-specific keratinocyte and uncombable-hair-syndrome papers.
- Remove the erythrocyte-membrane rows from PMID:5038456 for TGM3. The title and date make them incompatible with epidermal transglutaminase 3, whereas human TGM3 was cloned as TGase3 in PMID:8099584.
- The 1978 keratinocyte envelope paper is correct for the pathway but not specific enough for the TGM3 gene product; leave TGM3 support on the later biochemical and localization studies.
