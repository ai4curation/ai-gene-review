# TGM3 manual deep research

`just deep-research-perplexity-lite human TGM3` could not be run because the only configured provider in this checkout was `claude_code`, so this file records manual synthesis from cached UniProt, GOA, PAINT, and primary-publication abstracts.

TGM3 encodes the epidermal transglutaminase E zymogen. PMID:8099584 cloned human and mouse TGase3 and showed that the recombinant human proenzyme is activated by limited proteolysis; PMID:11980702 and PMID:12679341 solved calcium-bound structures and showed how calcium binding gates proteolytic activation and catalytic activity. These publications directly support protein-glutamine gamma-glutamyltransferase activity and calcium ion binding.

The main cellular role of TGM3 is cytoplasmic crosslinking of soluble cornified-envelope precursor proteins in upper epidermal layers. PMID:12850301 localized the zymogen in granular and cornified layers and found a diffuse cytoplasmic distribution in differentiating cultured keratinocytes. Reactome's cornified-envelope pathway and UniProt both describe a division in which cytosolic TGM3 makes loricrin/SPRR oligomers that membrane TGM1 can later attach to the growing envelope.

TGM3 also acts in the hair follicle. PMID:27866708 reports UHS-causing mutations in PADI3, TGM3, and TCHH and argues that the three encoded proteins participate in hair-shaft formation. This supports retaining the existing hair-follicle morphogenesis row as a non-core but specific developmental context.

PMID:5038456 is a 1972 isolated-erythrocyte-membrane paper and the three rows derived from it have the wrong biology for TGM3; they most plausibly belong to the transglutaminase-like erythrocyte protein band 4.2 rather than the epidermal TGM3 enzyme. PMID:24643 predates TGM3 cloning and shows keratinocyte envelope transglutaminase activity generally, so the later TGM3-specific rows should carry the support for peptide crosslinking and keratinocyte differentiation instead.
