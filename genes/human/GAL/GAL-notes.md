# GAL Curation Notes (Human Galanin Peptides, UniProtKB:P22466)

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Gene symbol / ID**: `GAL` (`UniProtKB:P22466`, `HGNC:4114`)
- **PANTHER family**: `PANTHER:PTHR16839:SF1` (`GALANIN PEPTIDES`, within `PANTHER:PTHR16839` `GALANIN`; root PAINT node `PANTHER:PTN008359337`).
- **Preprohormone processing**: Synthesized as a 123-amino-acid preprogalanin precursor (signal peptide residues 1-19; propeptide residues 20-32) that is cleaved at dibasic sites by prohormone convertases within neuronal and neuroendocrine secretory granules (`GO:0030141`) to yield two mature secreted peptides:
  1. **Galanin** (residues 33-62; 30 amino acids in human, uniquely carrying a C-terminal non-amidated Ser30 compared with 29-residue amidated galanin in other vertebrates) [PMID:1722333].
  2. **Galanin message-associated peptide (GMAP)** (residues 65-123; 59 amino acids).
- **Signaling mechanism**: Mature galanin acts as a secreted neuropeptide hormone (`GO:0005184` *neuropeptide hormone activity*, child of `GO:0005179` *hormone activity* and `GO:0048018` *receptor ligand activity*) whose strictly conserved N-terminal 1-13 pharmacophore (`GWTLNSAGYLLGP`) binds and activates all three seven-transmembrane G protein-coupled galanin receptors: `GALR1`, `GALR2`, and `GALR3` (`GO:0090663` *galanin-activated signaling pathway*) [PMID:1370155, PMID:24602615, PMID:25691535, Reactome:R-HSA-375280].
  - `GALR1` and `GALR3` couple to pertussis-toxin-sensitive inhibitory Gi/o heterotrimeric G proteins (`GNAI1`/`GNAI2`/`GNAI3`) to inhibit adenylate cyclase and open GIRK potassium channels (`GO:0007193` *adenylate cyclase-inhibiting G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway*).
  - `GALR2` couples primarily to Gq/11 heterotrimeric G proteins (`GNAQ`) to activate phospholipase C-beta, generate IP3, and release Ca2+ from ER stores through IP3 receptors, facilitating BK channels and modulating neuronal excitability (`GO:0007200` *phospholipase C-activating G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway*) [PMID:24602615, Reactome:R-HSA-375280].
- **Physiological roles**: Inhibits glucose-stimulated insulin secretion from pancreatic beta cells (`GO:0032024` / `GO:0046676`) with subnanomolar potency (`ED50 < 1 nM`) [PMID:1370155], modulates nociception and inflammatory pain (`GO:0019233`), regulates feeding behavior, smooth muscle contraction, and hippocampal/cortical seizure susceptibility (de novo p.Ala39Glu mutation in mature galanin causes temporal lobe epilepsy by converting galanin into a `GALR1` antagonist and weakening `GALR2` agonism) [PMID:25691535].

## Key Curation Corrections & Ligands in Signaling Pathways Assessment (Tranche 4C)
1. **Ligand-vs-receptor MF mis-annotation (`GO:0004966` *galanin receptor activity*)**: Human `GAL` is annotated with `GO:0004966` (*galanin receptor activity*, IMP from [PMID:25691535]). In [PMID:25691535], the authors assayed wild-type and p.Ala39Glu mutant **galanin peptide** on cells expressing `GALR1` and `GALR2`; `GAL` is the secreted neuropeptide hormone ligand, not the transmembrane receptor! We `MODIFY` `GO:0004966` to `GO:0005184` (*neuropeptide hormone activity*).
2. **Wrong GPCR second-messenger pathway (`GO:0007189` *adenylate cyclase-activating G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway*)**: Human `GAL` carries a legacy TAS annotation for `GO:0007189` from [PMID:7692313] (a 1995 chromosomal mapping paper), which PAINT then propagated across `PTHR16839` (`PTN008359337`). In reality, `GALR1` and `GALR3` **inhibit** adenylate cyclase via Gi/o (`GO:0007193`) and `GALR2` activates phospholipase C via Gq/11 (`GO:0007200`) [Reactome:R-HSA-375280, PMID:24602615]. We `MODIFY` both the TAS and IBA rows for `GO:0007189` to `GO:0090663` (*galanin-activated signaling pathway*).
3. **Category B pathway gap (`GO:0090663` *galanin-activated signaling pathway*)**: `GO:0090663` currently has only the receptors `GALR2` and `GALR3` annotated in human/mouse/rat and zero ligand annotations across all species. We `MODIFY` the generic parent rows `GO:0007218` (*neuropeptide signaling pathway*, IBA/IEA) and `GO:0007165` (*signal transduction*, IEA) to `GO:0090663` (*galanin-activated signaling pathway*) and add a `NEW` (`IDA`) annotation for `GO:0090663` supported by [PMID:25691535] and [PMID:24602615], linking `GAL` to `MODULE:galanin_signaling`.
