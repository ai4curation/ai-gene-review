Gene Description

ATP5PF encodes coupling factor 6 (F6) of mitochondrial F1F0 ATP synthase (complex V), a small, nuclear-encoded, mitochondrially imported subunit of the peripheral stalk. Together with subunits b, d and OSCP, the stalk acts as a stator that holds the catalytic alpha3beta3 head and subunit a static against the rotating central stalk and c-ring. It is required for functional coupling of the catalytic F(1) and proton-translocating F(o) sectors during oxidative ATP synthesis.
