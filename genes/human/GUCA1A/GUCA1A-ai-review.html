Gene Description

GUCA1A encodes GCAP1, an EF-hand calcium sensor in retinal photoreceptors. It regulates membrane guanylate cyclases to restore cGMP during photoresponse recovery. Pathogenic variants can impair calcium-dependent cyclase control and cause cone or cone-rod dystrophy.

The curated function record states “GCAP1 inhibits RetGC activation” (UniProt P43080, CC:FUNCTION). This calcium-dependent regulation supports the broader regulator core.
