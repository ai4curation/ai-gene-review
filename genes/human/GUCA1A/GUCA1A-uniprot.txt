CC   -!- FUNCTION: Regulates the activity of retinal membrane guanylyl cyclases
CC       (GUCY2D/RetGC1 and GUCY2F/RetGC2) in a Ca(2+)-dependent manner. At low
CC       intracellular Ca(2+) concentrations, GCAP1 binds to and allosterically
CC       activates RetGC, stimulating cGMP synthesis and restoring cGMP levels
CC       in photoreceptors during the recovery phase of phototransduction. At
CC       high Ca(2+) concentrations, GCAP1 inhibits RetGC activation,
CC       contributing to the Ca(2+)-dependent negative feedback loop that
CC       maintains photoreceptor sensitivity (PubMed:18706439, PubMed:19459154,
CC       PubMed:30184081, PubMed:30622141). This regulation is a key event in
CC       recovery of the dark state of both cone and rod photoreceptors
CC       following light exposure (PubMed:18706439, PubMed:19459154). Acts
CC       primarily in cone photoreceptors and also contributes to rod function
CC       (PubMed:18706439, PubMed:19459154). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18706439,

CC   -!- DISEASE: Cone dystrophy 3 (COD3) [MIM:602093]: An autosomal dominant
CC       cone dystrophy. Cone dystrophies are retinal dystrophies characterized
CC       by progressive degeneration of the cone photoreceptors with
CC       preservation of rod function, as indicated by electroretinogram.
CC       However, some rod involvement may be present in some cone dystrophies,
CC       particularly at late stage. Affected individuals suffer from
