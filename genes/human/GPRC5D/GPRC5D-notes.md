## Function
- Orphan family C (metabotropic glutamate receptor-like) G protein-coupled receptor, RAIG4/group 5. "GPRC5D has
  seven putative transmembrane segments and is expressed in the cell membrane" [PMID:11311935]. 7TM, no identified
  ligand; UniProt FUNCTION: GPCR involved in hard keratin expression, role in hair/nail (PMID:15086536).
- Normally expressed only in the hair follicle; in multiple myeloma GPRC5D protein is on CD138+ plasma cells
  independent of BCMA, a validated cell-surface CAR-T/bispecific target [PMID:30918115].
- Homodimer (UniProt, PubMed:38898050/39181182).
- IBA node PTN001025902 (GPRC5 family) carries protein kinase activator activity, signaling receptor complex and
  extracellular exosome, with donors GPRC5A/B (Q9NQ84/Q9NZH0).

## Localization
- Seven-TM multi-pass membrane protein, extracellular N-terminus aa 1-27. Cell membrane [PMID:11311935].
- Conclusion: plasma membrane, cell surface accessible (CAR-T/bispecific target). No surface-exposed large
  ectodomain beyond short loops, but the extracellular loops are the antibody epitopes.
