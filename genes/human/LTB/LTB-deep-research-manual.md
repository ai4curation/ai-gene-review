# LTB manual literature synthesis

Perplexity deep research was unavailable in this workspace, so this file records a manual synthesis from cached primary papers, Reactome events, UniProt, and the existing PTHR11471 family review.

## Core biology

LTB encodes lymphotoxin-beta, a type II TNF-superfamily membrane protein that anchors LT-alpha on the cell surface and supplies the beta subunits of the LT-alpha1-beta2 heterotrimer. The original cloning paper reports that "LT alpha is present on the surface of activated T, B, and LAK cells as a complex with a 33 kd glycoprotein" and that cloning identified that glycoprotein as lymphotoxin beta, "a type II membrane protein with significant homology to TNF, LT alpha, and the ligand for the CD40 receptor" [PMID:7916655].

The LT-alpha1-beta2 heterotrimer binds LTBR, not the TNF death receptors used by soluble TNF and LTA homotrimers. The 2013 structural study states that "LTalpha1beta2 possesses two binding sites for LTbetaR with distinct affinities and that dimerization of LTbetaR by LTalpha1beta2 is necessary and sufficient for signal transduction" [PMID:24248355].

LTB should therefore keep the shared TNF-family cytokine activity, receptor binding, extracellular/cell-surface location, and LTBR complex rows when supported, but TNF/TNFR1/TNFR2 and death-domain apoptosis terms need to be rejected or left unresolved unless the row specifically reflects the LTBR-mediated lymphotoxin-beta receptor pathway.
