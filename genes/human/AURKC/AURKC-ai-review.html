Gene Description

Aurora kinase C is a protein serine/threonine kinase that functions in chromosome passenger complexes during cell division, with an important role in germ cells. Binding to INCENP and phosphorylation regulate its catalytic activity and substrate recognition. AURKC phosphorylates proteins including INCENP, Survivin and histone H3, and its division-stage localization includes chromosomes, the spindle midzone and the midbody. AURKC and Aurora B have overlapping activities, although their complexes and physiological requirements depend on cell type. Human variants that remove the kinase domain cause macrozoospermia-associated male infertility.
