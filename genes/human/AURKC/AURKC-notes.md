# AURKC prospective core synthesis

One molecular activity is sufficient: protein serine/threonine kinase activity (GO:0004674), operating as the catalytic component of an AURKC-containing chromosome passenger complex (GO:0032133). INCENP binding and phosphorylation couple to kinase activation and substrate recognition; Survivin phosphorylation is a demonstrated substrate relationship. These are parts of one kinase mechanism, not separate intrinsic binding, activation and phosphotransfer cores.

A prospective standalone description should identify Aurora kinase C as a chromosome-passenger serine/threonine kinase with prominent germ-cell functions and context-dependent overlap with Aurora B. It phosphorylates protein substrates, including CPC components and histone H3, and changes location through division. AURKC-containing and AURKB-containing complexes are predominantly distinct in the tested somatic-cell system. Human truncating variants that remove the kinase domain cause macrozoospermia-associated infertility. Do not present the mouse double-knockout compensation result as negating this human phenotype, or treat combined B/C inhibition as a human AURKC-specific perturbation.

Candidate core locations are the chromosome centromeric region (GO:0000775), spindle midzone (GO:0051233) and midbody (GO:0030496), with source-qualified localization and no claim of constitutive residence. Put GO:0032133 in `in_complex`, not `locations`. The direct catalytic process is protein phosphorylation (GO:0006468); whether a more informative cytokinesis-regulation process belongs in the core should be resolved against the primary source and the annotation consultation. No NEW assertion is proposed from this draft.

The protein-kinase rows can be refined to GO:0004674 using independent mechanistic evidence while preserving the Aurora C-SV experiment as the original source. GO:0004712 adds tyrosine catalysis; it is not simply a synonym or ancestor of serine/threonine kinase activity. The evidence reviewed supports a source-qualified replacement with the demonstrated specificity, not an absolute statement that no conceivable tyrosine substrate exists. Serine kinase activity (GO:0106310) is itself demonstrated; it need not be replaced simply because a broader dual serine/threonine label is used in the core.

Do not infer that Aurora C-SV and the canonical protein are functionally identical from retained kinase domains. Natural product 2 lacks 1–34, product 3 replaces 1–20 with methionine, and the structural 36–305 construct is engineered. These facts motivate questions about regulation, activation and localization rather than invented product-specific activities.

Useful questions: which substrates and spatial roles discriminate AURKC from AURKB at endogenous abundance in human germ cells; how do the three natural N termini affect stability and localization; and what phylogenetic placement and target evidence underlie the centrosome/spindle-pole IBA assertions? Donor names alone do not resolve the latter. Specific high-throughput protein pairs also need their source tables before confident adjudication.

The canonical review is still PENDING. This draft awaits independent annotation consultation and the additional source-cache recovery; it is not a validated review.
