# PKDREJ notes

Automated deep research could not be generated for this review because the configured provider was unavailable, so this note records manual curation context only.

PKDREJ is a testis-enriched, sperm-associated polycystin/REJ-family multi-pass membrane protein. The original human paper identified a receptor-for-egg-jelly homolog and showed exclusive testis expression [PMID:9949214, "Northern blot analysis showed that PKDREJ was expressed only in testis"], but its acrosome-reaction role in UniProt is transferred from mouse and is not directly assayed in the human cloning paper.

The plasma-membrane, acrosomal-membrane and generic membrane rows are plausible, low-resolution location claims by mouse orthology or sequence features. The nucleus row is weak for this large membrane protein. The root PTHR10877 calcium-channel and mechanosensation rows should not be propagated onto PKDREJ because no reviewed human or mouse evidence shows that PKDREJ itself has calcium-channel activity or directly detects mechanical stimulus. The IPR003915-derived calcium ion binding row should likewise not be kept as evidence that the receptor-like PKDREJ protein itself binds calcium.
