The core functions are direct mRNA 3′-UTR binding and translation activation through protein recruitment. The complete official and normal cached abstract of [PMID:24954906](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24954906/) establishes PAR-CLIP contact with U-rich elements and transcript stabilization; its full Methods were not available. Reporter activity was normalized to transcript abundance, with polysome profiling as an independent endpoint. Human ATXN2 recruits PABPC1 and TENT2/PAPD4; TENT2 performs poly(A) addition. Partial PAPD4 depletion, a reconstructed human full-length cDNA and the engineered 906–1095 fragment establish bounded mechanisms, not the function of every natural product. Figure pixels and complete supplements were not reviewed.

The complete cached Abstract, Methods and Results through the PABP-abundance experiments, plus selected Discussion paragraphs, were read for [PMID:17392519](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17392519/). Endogenous human ATXN2 associates with DDX6 in nuclease-treated lysates and redistributes to stress granules after heat or arsenite. Depletion reduces granule number/size without eliminating residual granules. Overexpression of full-length normal/expanded ATXN2 or its DDX6-binding region disperses visible P-bodies, while depletion lacks an obvious P-body effect. The existing assembly assertion is therefore refined to negative regulation of P-body assembly in that experimental context. This does not establish universal physiological suppression.

Later stress-granule evidence is kept assay-specific. The complete official and normal abstract of [PMID:27240544](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27240544/) distinguishes mouse fibroblast deficiency from human neuroblastoma starvation. The complete abstracts and selected original knockout/construct Methods and initial Results of [PMID:37295429](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37295429/) show that human ATXN2-knockout HeLa cells still form arsenite-induced granules with altered size and number; U2OS domain-deletion experiments are separate. PABPC is the RNA-dependent chaperone, and ATXN2 its client. Complete abstracts, selected Methods/Results and the Figure 7 caption of [PMID:38869059](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38869059/) support an approximately 20% reduction after human-cell ATXN2 depletion and stronger rescue by full-length than engineered 1–1079 protein. These observations do not require universal necessity for assembly.

## 2026-09-30 UTC — First review follow-up
