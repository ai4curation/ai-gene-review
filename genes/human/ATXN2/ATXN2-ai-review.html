Gene Description

Ataxin-2 is a predominantly cytoplasmic RNA-binding protein that associates with messenger ribonucleoproteins and regulates mRNA stability and translation. It contacts U-rich sequences in mRNA 3' untranslated regions and recruits PABPC1 and the cytoplasmic poly(A) polymerase TENT2 to selected transcripts, supporting poly(A)-tail extension and translation. Its RNA- and protein-interaction regions also influence stress-granule composition and assembly in a manner that depends on cell type and stress conditions. Polyglutamine expansion in ATXN2 causes spinocerebellar ataxia type 2.
