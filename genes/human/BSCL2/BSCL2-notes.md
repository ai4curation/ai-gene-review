### Mechanistic synthesis

Seipin is an oligomeric ER membrane protein that contributes physical structure to lipid-droplet assembly and growth. Human structural work reports an anionic-phospholipid-binding lumenal domain and an undecameric assembly ([PMID:30293840](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30293840/), complete cached abstract). Human live-cell work places the seipin–LDAF1 machinery at droplet initiation sites and shows that recruited machinery can redirect those sites ([PMID:31708432](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31708432/), relevant Results/Methods read). Seipin remains ER-associated while LDAF1 can move onto the growing droplet. Human knockout, rescue, endogenous tagging and cargo-exchange experiments connect seipin to functional ER–LD contacts ([PMID:27879284](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27879284/)). These are participation data: seipin supplies part of the organizing apparatus rather than merely being a substrate consumed by another process.

The proposed single core combines phospholipid binding with lipid-droplet formation. GO:0140042 is an `is_a` child of lipid droplet organization and `part_of` lipid storage in the inspected official [PomBase ontology view](https://www.pombase.org/term/GO%3A0140042). The broader processes therefore do not need duplicate core units. The study's language about facilitating triglyceride phase separation does not establish triglyceride-synthesis chemistry by seipin. AGPAT2 catalysis, downstream desaturation changes, and adipocyte lipase activities are not transferred to BSCL2.

The broad GO:0005811 definition allows lipid-droplet-associated proteins. Thus seipin's ER-embedded contact-site position does not make the old term intrinsically false. For the direct PMID:27879284 localization row, GO:0160259, [endoplasmic reticulum membrane-lipid droplet contact site](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0160259), is more precise. The official definition names the contact between the two structures; its links to ER membrane and lipid droplet are `overlaps`, not `is_a`. The candidate changes only this row's proposed review action/term, leaving its source term intact. The original PMID:30970241 localization experiment is not exposed in the abstract and remains UNDECIDED. The broad UniProt location mapping is retained with the contact-site interpretation.

### Experimental boundaries

- **PMID:31708432:** Endogenous human SUM159 tagging and imaging establish assembly sites and localization dynamics. The purified recombinant complex used seipin residues 1–310 with LDAF1 in mammalian expression cells; it was not a direct assay of every full-length named product. LDAF1 destabilization in seipin knockout cells means that the deletion phenotype is not an isolated assay of seipin alone. The purified-complex triglyceride and cryo-EM observations support a model, without demonstrating a chemical lipid-synthetic activity. Selected Results, construct/purification, co-IP, imaging and retargeting Methods were read; figure images and supplementary data were not independently inspected.
- **PMID:27879284:** Human A431 knockout/rescue, endogenous seipin tagging, EM captions and patient-fibroblast experiments were inspected. Initial neutral-lipid synthesis and initial cargo recruitment differ from later contact-dependent delivery and growth. The source notes membrane-preservation limits in EM. These limits are retained instead of treating all contact-site evidence as equivalent to direct membrane continuity imaging.
- **PMID:27564575:** Much of the detailed dynamics and domain analysis is in Drosophila S2 cells. The paper also tests human SUM159 knockout and patient fibroblasts, and human seipin rescues fly depletion. The human support is retained without relabeling every fly assay as human.
- **PMID:30293840:** The complete abstract explicitly reports full-length/lumenal-domain anionic-phospholipid binding. Full affinity, assay-condition and construct details were unavailable. This supports the broad binding function but not phosphatidic-acid exclusivity.
- **PMID:31178403:** The complete abstract describes seipin retargeting, ER–LD necks and rapid contact removal. Individual construct species and full model-membrane protocols were not available, so none is reconstructed from the title or laboratory affiliation.
- **PMID:19278620:** The complete abstract distinguishes 12 seipin-null patient cell lines from eight AGPAT2-deficient lines and nine controls. Droplet number/size and triglyceride changes support storage/organization. Its downstream Delta9-desaturation result does not make seipin a desaturase.
- **PMID:14981520:** The complete abstract directly supports ER membrane localization. N88S/S90L glycosylation/aggregation effects concern pathogenic variants, not a new normal neuronal molecular function.
