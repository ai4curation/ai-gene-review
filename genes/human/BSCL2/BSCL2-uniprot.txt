CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mediates the formation of lipid droplets during the
CC       differentiation and maturation of adipocytes (PubMed:19278620,
CC       PubMed:21533227, PubMed:27564575, PubMed:30293840, PubMed:31708432).
CC       Together with LDAF1, defines the sites of lipid droplet formation in
CC       the endoplasmic reticulum and promotes the aggregation of
CC       triacylglycerol molecules to form the core of a lipid droplet
CC       (nucleation) in the endoplasmic reticulum and drive budding of lipid
CC       droplets into the cytoplasm (PubMed:31708432). BSCL2 and LDAF1 also
CC       regulate protein and lipid delivery from the endoplasmic reticulum into
CC       growing lipid droplets (PubMed:27879284, PubMed:31178403). During early
CC       adipogenesis, BSCL2 inhibits GPAT3 and GPAT4 activity, thereby
CC       preventing phosphatidic acid accumulation that could antagonize PPARG
CC       and block differentiation (By similarity). This inhibition is then
CC       relieved by ADIG during adipocyte maturation, allowing expansion of
CC       lipid droplets (By similarity). Regulates the maturation of ZFYVE1-
CC       positive nascent lipid droplets and the function of the RAB18-ZFYVE1
CC       complex in mediating the formation of endoplasmic reticulum-lipid
CC       droplet contacts (PubMed:30970241). Binds anionic phospholipids
CC       including phosphatidic acid (PubMed:30293840).

CC       severe pyramidal tract signs and spastic paraplegia. SPG17 is a motor
CC       neuron disease overlapping with distal spinal muscular atrophy type 5.

CC   -!- DISEASE: Neuronopathy, distal hereditary motor, autosomal dominant 13
CC       (HMND13) [MIM:619112]: A form of distal hereditary motor neuronopathy,
CC       a heterogeneous group of neuromuscular diseases caused by selective
CC       degeneration of motor neurons in the anterior horn of the spinal cord,
CC       without sensory deficit in the posterior horn. HMND13 is characterized
CC       by distal muscular atrophy primarily affecting the upper limbs. Lower
CC       limb involvement may occur at the same time or later. Clinical features
CC       are highly variable even within families, and include poor fine hand
CC       motor skills, difficulty walking, foot deformities, spasticity and
CC       hyperreflexia. Some HMND13 patients show axonal peripheral neuropathy
