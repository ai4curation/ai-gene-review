## Catalysis, recruitment and the core synthesis

BTK's principal activity is nonreceptor protein tyrosine phosphorylation. [PMID:11606584](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11606584/) reports purified Btk phosphorylation of recombinant PLCgamma2 and a functional requirement for defined PLCgamma2 tyrosines. The human PLCgamma2 rescue was performed in chicken DT40 cells; the abstract does not identify the purified Btk construct species. [PMID:15509800](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15509800/) separately reports Btk-dependent PLCgamma2 activating phosphosites in human Ramos and murine splenic B cells. Calcium/NF-kappaB outputs belong to this pathway mechanism. PLCgamma2 performs the lipid hydrolysis, and ATP binding is part of BTK catalysis rather than a separate core activity. The source phospholipase-activator assertion is retained as a non-core mechanistic description; full original binding-assay access was unavailable.

Own PIP3 recognition is a distinct molecular activity that positions the kinase. [PMID:17823121](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17823121/) reports quantitative PH-domain lipid-binding experiments and PIP3-dependent recruitment in NIH 3T3 cells; [PMID:10196179](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10196179/) and [PMID:10602036](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10602036/) distinguish isolated PH-domain reporters from full-length Btk-GFP. The two core units therefore describe kinase action and PIP3 recognition, without assigning phosphoinositide synthesis or hydrolysis to BTK. Cytosol and stimulus-dependent peripheral plasma-membrane recruitment are compatible locations. Nuclear shuttling in [PMID:11016936](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11016936/) includes wild-type/endogenous evidence and is retained as a contextual localization, not dismissed because deletion mutants accumulate more strongly.

For innate signaling, the normally cached Reactome TIRAP event and [PMID:25704810](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25704810/) support kinase participation. The latter abstract directly names DDX41/STING interactions, DDX41 phosphorylation and interferon effects. The narrower cGAS/STING source term remains UNDECIDED: official [GO:0140896](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0140896) explicitly starts with cGAS/cGAMP, while the inspected abstract centers DDX41 and the relevant full experiment was unavailable. An abstract omission is not a claim that no such experiment exists.

[PMID:34554188](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34554188/) was read at the relevant unique Results, Methods and Discussion passages: purified BTK/NLRP3, kinase-dead K430E and inhibitor controls, target-tyrosine mutants, native-complex/ASC assays, human PBMC/XLA and mouse macrophage reconstitution. Those assays identify BTK as a catalytic participant in assembly, not merely a necessary substrate. The study itself discusses different effects under stronger TLR priming. Its positive NLRP3-assembly annotation is accepted in the demonstrated settings, without claiming permanent mature-complex membership or universal positive inflammatory regulation. Figure images and complete supplements were not inspected.
