Gene Description

BTK is a Tec-family nonreceptor protein tyrosine kinase that couples immune-receptor activation to intracellular signaling. Its PH domain recognizes phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate and recruits a cytosolic pool to membrane signaling assemblies. In B cells, BTK phosphorylates and activates PLCgamma2 within the BLNK-organized receptor pathway, supporting calcium mobilization, NF-kappaB signaling, activation and differentiation. BTK also phosphorylates substrates in innate immune pathways, including DDX41 and NLRP3, with outputs that depend on cell type and stimulation. SH2/SH3-mediated interactions organize signaling partners, and a smaller pool can shuttle through the nucleus. Loss of functional BTK causes X-linked agammaglobulinemia.

ATP-dependent nonreceptor tyrosine phosphorylation transmits immune-receptor signals. BTK activates PLCgamma2 in BCR assemblies and participates in TIRAP-associated innate signaling; direct phosphorylation of NLRP3 supports inflammasome assembly in demonstrated priming/activation settings. B-cell calcium and NF-kappaB responses are downstream consequences of this kinase mechanism, not additional catalytic activities.
