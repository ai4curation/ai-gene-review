CC   -!- FUNCTION: Cell surface glycoprotein that plays a role in cell adhesion
CC       and tumor progression (PubMed:10910050, PubMed:11590190,
CC       PubMed:1378450, PubMed:16204051, PubMed:2022629, PubMed:2803308,
CC       PubMed:8776764). Intercellular adhesion occurs in a calcium- and
CC       fibronectin-independent manner (PubMed:16204051, PubMed:2022629).
CC       Mediates homophilic and heterophilic cell adhesion with other
CC       carcinoembryonic antigen-related cell adhesion molecules, such as
CC       CEACAM5 and CEACAM8 (PubMed:11590190, PubMed:16204051, PubMed:2022629,
CC       PubMed:2803308, PubMed:8776764). Heterophilic interaction with CEACAM8
CC       occurs in activated neutrophils (PubMed:8776764). Plays a role in
CC       neutrophil adhesion to cytokine-activated endothelial cells
CC       (PubMed:1378450). Plays a role in cell migration and cell adhesion to
CC       endothelial cells (PubMed:16204051). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10910050,
