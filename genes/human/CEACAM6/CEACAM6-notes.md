# CEACAM6 (NCA, CD66c) notes

Deep research providers unavailable in this environment; research from the 15 cached publications and
UniProt P40199.

## Topology / localization
- IgV + 2 IgC2 domains; GPI-anchored. UniProt: "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane; Lipid-anchor,
  GPI-anchor ... Apical cell membrane ... Cell surface ... Localized to the apical glycocalyx surface."
- Conclusion: plasma membrane, outer leaflet, entire mature protein extracellular; apical in polarized
  epithelium; stored in neutrophil granules and delivered to the surface on activation; shed/secreted
  forms detected in sputum [PMID:25645918].

## Functions
- Homophilic and heterophilic adhesion with CEACAM8, CEACAM1, CEACAM5 [PMID:11590190, PMID:2803308,
  PMID:8776764]; IgV-domain homo- and heterodimers [PMID:26483485].
- Neutrophil adhesion to cytokine-activated endothelium [PMID:1378450].
- Antibody blockade reduces tumour-cell adhesion, invasion and metastasis [PMID:16204051].
- Inhibits anoikis [PMID:10910050].
