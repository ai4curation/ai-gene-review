# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ANKRD34B (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ANKRD34B (DP58) encodes an ankyrin repeat-containing phosphoprotein whose expression and subcellular distribution are coupled to cell-type-specific differentiation programs [PMID:15358210, PMID:17485271]. It was first identified as a cytosolic phosphoprotein rapidly induced within 30 minutes during bone marrow commitment to dendritic cells, where it migrates at 58 kDa and carries serine, threonine, and tyrosine phosphorylation [PMID:15358210, PMID:17485271]. In neurons, by contrast, it is constitutively expressed as a 52 kDa nuclear phosphoprotein modified predominantly on serine residues, and its ankyrin repeats resemble those of the NF-κB-binding protein BCL3 [PMID:17485271]. Functionally, knockdown in embryonic stem cell-derived mesodermal cells alters neurogenic and adipogenic markers, consistent with a role as a positive regulator of neurogenesis and a negative regulator of adipogenesis [PMID:20184659]. Beyond these expression and knockdown studies, the direct binding partners, catalytic context, and precise molecular mechanism of ANKRD34B have not been characterized in the available corpus.
