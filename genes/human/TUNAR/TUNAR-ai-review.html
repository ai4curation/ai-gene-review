Gene Description

TUNAR encodes pTUNAR (also called BNLN, beta cell and neural cell-regulin), a 48-amino-acid microprotein translated from a transcript long annotated as the long non-coding RNA TUNAR (TUNA, LINC00617; megamind in zebrafish). The peptide is highly conserved across vertebrates and carries a single C-terminal transmembrane helix that anchors it in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane. There it binds sarco/endoplasmic reticulum Ca2+-ATPases: SERCA3 (ATP2A3) in pancreatic beta cells, where the interaction increases at high glucose, and SERCA2 (ATP2A2) in neural cells. Its sequence lacks the xLFxxF motif of the regulin family (phospholamban, sarcolipin, myoregulin, endoregulin) and is unrelated to DWORF, so it appears to be an independently evolved SERCA-binding micropeptide. Altering its level changes ER and cytosolic Ca2+ handling, but the direction differs by cell type. In glucose-stimulated beta cells, overexpression lowers ER Ca2+, raises cytosolic Ca2+, reduces ER stress signalling and enhances glucose-stimulated insulin secretion. In mouse neural cells, overexpression enlarges the ER Ca2+ store and speeds cytosolic Ca2+ clearance, consistent with SERCA2 activation. The mRNA is enriched in insulin-producing beta cells of the islet and in the central nervous system. Loss of the peptide in mouse embryonic stem cells promotes neural differentiation, and its overexpression reduces neurite formation, so pTUNAR acts as a Ca2+-dependent brake on neural differentiation. Whether it activates or inhibits SERCA pump activity directly has not been measured.

Core Functions

pTUNAR is a single-pass ER-membrane micropeptide that binds SERCA Ca2+-ATPases (SERCA3 in pancreatic beta cells, SERCA2 in neural cells) and modulates Ca2+ exchange between the ER lumen and the cytosol. The sign of the effect on SERCA pumping appears to depend on cell type: ER Ca2+ is lowered in glucose-stimulated beta cells but raised in neurons. Pump activity has not been measured directly, so the molecular function is recorded as SERCA (ATPase) binding rather than as activator or inhibitor activity.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does pTUNAR/BNLN change SERCA pump kinetics directly, and in which direction? Beta-cell data (SERCA3; lower ER Ca2+) suggest inhibition, whereas neural data (SERCA2; faster cytosolic Ca2+ clearance) suggest activation. The answer decides whether GO:0042030 ATPase inhibitor activity, GO:0141109 transporter activator activity, or only the direction-neutral GO:1901894 regulation of ATPase-coupled calcium transmembrane transporter activity should be added, in line with ERLN and STRIT1 (DWORF).

Q: Is the opposite sign of the Ca2+ effect explained by SERCA isoform (SERCA3 vs SERCA2b) or by competition with other SERCA regulins expressed in each cell type (e.g. endoregulin with SERCA3)?

Q: Is the extracellular-vesicle pool of pTUNAR (seen for the overexpressed mouse peptide) physiologically relevant, or a by-product of overexpression?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Co-reconstitute purified synthetic pTUNAR with SERCA2b or SERCA3a in proteoliposomes, or co-express them in HEK293 microsomes, and measure Ca2+-dependent ATPase activity (Vmax and KCa), with phospholamban and DWORF as inhibitor and activator controls. Add FRET or crosslinking to test whether pTUNAR occupies the regulin groove.

Hypothesis: pTUNAR directly alters SERCA2b and SERCA3 Ca2+-ATPase kinetics.

Experiment: Mutate the pTUNAR start codon in mouse beta cells or human stem-cell-derived beta cells, leaving the lncRNA intact as in the mESC knockout, and measure ER and cytosolic Ca2+ oscillations, ER stress markers and glucose-stimulated insulin secretion.

Hypothesis: Endogenous pTUNAR is required for normal beta-cell Ca2+ oscillations and GSIS.

TUNAR (pTUNAR / BNLN) review notes

UniProt A0A1B0GTB2 (TUNAR_HUMAN), 48 aa, Swiss-Prot reviewed. HGNC:44088 "TCL1 upstream
neural differentiation-associated RNA". The locus was long annotated as a lncRNA (TUNA,
HI-LNC78, LINC00617; zebrafish megamind). Mouse ortholog A0A1B0GQX2.

Literature found (PubMed esearch "TUNAR micropeptide", "Tunar microprotein", "BNLN micropeptide")

Only two primary papers characterise the peptide:

  1. PMID:34513312 Li et al. 2021 Mol Ther Nucleic Acids (full text cached). Human islets / rat INS-1 /
    mouse and human islets. Names the peptide BNLN.
  2. PMID:35036403 Senis et al. 2021 Front Cell Dev Biol (full text cached, fetched this session).
    Mouse ESCs, N2A, cortical neurons, in utero electroporation. Names it pTUNAR. This is the
    paper behind the UniProt "By similarity" (A0A1B0GQX2) statements and the ISS rows.

Other TUNAR papers (e.g. PMID:33682459 TUNAR lncRNA and Wnt signalling in beta cells;
PMID:29540255 glioma paper, WITHDRAWN) treat TUNAR as an RNA and are not relevant to the
protein's GO annotation.

Key findings

Coding / structure

  • PMID:34513312; the TM domain is
    "extremely conserved and present in all 88 species".
  • Senis: "pTUNAR is a transmembrane protein that localizes in the endoplasmic reticulum and
    interacts with the calcium transporter SERCA2" PMID:35036403.
  • Senis note no regulin xLFxxF motif and no similarity to DWORF PMID:35036403. So TUNAR is NOT a regulin family member by sequence; SERCA regulation would be convergent.

Localization

  • Beta cells (INS-1, GFP-FLAG tags, KDEL-RFP co-stain): PMID:34513312, minor lysosome/mito/peroxisome/Golgi signal; not at cell surface (HiBiT).
  • Mouse: co-localises with SERCA2 in brain and NIH3T3; also detected in small and large
    extracellular vesicles PMID:35036403. EV location is in UniProt (by similarity) but not in GOA; not proposed (single supplementary observation of an overexpressed tagged protein).

SERCA interaction - direction of effect is DISCORDANT

  • Beta cells: pull-down/MS found SERCA3 (ATP2A3, Q93084), confirmed by co-IP in HEK293 and PLA
    in INS-1; interaction increases with high glucose PMID:34513312.
    Overexpression LOWERS ER Ca2+ and RAISES cytosolic Ca2+ -> authors suggest possible SERCA3
    inhibition but explicitly did not test enzyme activity PMID:34513312.
  • Neurons (mouse): co-IP with SERCA2; overexpression INCREASES ER Ca store and speeds cytosolic
    Ca2+ clearance; KO opposite trend -> "possible role of pTUNAR as an activator of SERCA2"
    PMID:35036403. Also no direct ATPase assay, and they note PMCA as alternative.
  • Senis explicitly reconcile as context-dependent: PMID:35036403

Conclusion: evidence supports SERCA binding (two isoforms, two labs, co-IP + PLA) and an effect
on ER/cytosolic Ca2+, but not a direction-specific MF (activator vs inhibitor). Unlike DWORF
(STRIT1: GO:0141109 transporter activator activity) and ERLN (GO:0042030 ATPase inhibitor
activity), there is no purified-system or pump-kinetics measurement. I therefore keep ATPase
binding (GO:0051117) as the core MF and do NOT propose GO:0042030/GO:0141109 or
GO:1901895/GO:1901896. GO:1901894 (direction-neutral regulation of ATPase-coupled calcium
transmembrane transporter activity) is plausible but also not directly measured; raised as a
question rather than NEW.

Downstream physiology

  • GSIS: overexpression increases GSIS in INS-1, mouse (lean and DIO) and human islets;
    ATG-deletion construct has no effect (peptide-dependent); shRNA knockdown of Tunar lowers GSIS
    in mouse islets PMID:34513312
    (note shRNA also removes the lncRNA). Downstream of Ca2+ handling -> non-core.
  • ER stress: BNLN overexpression reduced high-glucose ATF6 reporter activation.
  • Neural: start-codon-to-stop KO mESCs (lncRNA level unaffected) show enhanced neural
    differentiation in teratomas, EBs and neural protocol; overexpression reduces neurite number
    in N2A and cortex PMID:35036403.
    Supports negative regulation of neuron differentiation (ISS, non-core). The ISS row
    "neuron projection morphogenesis" better captured as negative regulation of neuron projection
    development (GO:0010977); overexpression-only evidence.

Annotation decisions (summary)

  • ER / ER membrane (4 rows): ACCEPT.
  • ER calcium ion homeostasis IDA: ACCEPT (core).
  • regulation of cytosolic calcium ion concentration ISS: ACCEPT (direction-neutral; both labs).
  • ATPase binding IPI (SERCA3) and ISS (SERCA2): ACCEPT (core MF, most defensible).
  • positive regulation of insulin secretion involved in cellular response to glucose stimulus IDA: KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
  • negative regulation of neuron differentiation ISS: KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
  • neuron projection morphogenesis ISS: MODIFY -> GO:0010977.
  • No NEW terms.
