CC   -!- FUNCTION: Substrate-recognition component of a cullin-5-RING E3
CC       ubiquitin-protein ligase complex (ECS complex, also named CRL5
CC       complex), which mediates the ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal
CC       degradation of target proteins, such as LZTS1, PSRC1 and SUV39H1
CC       (PubMed:16325183, PubMed:27697924, PubMed:39039081, PubMed:40440427).
CC       The ECS(ASB7) complex acts as a negative regulator of H3K9me3 histone
CC       mark by mediating ubiquitination and degradation of SUV39H1: ASB7 is
CC       recruited to heterochromatin by CBX5 (HP1 alpha), where it associates
CC       with SUV39H1, promoting the ubiquitin-proteasome-mediated degradation
CC       of SUV39H1 (PubMed:40440427). During mitosis, ASB7 is phosphorylated
CC       and inactivated, allowing SUV39H1 activity (PubMed:40440427). The
CC       ECS(ASB7) complex also plays a role in spindle dynamics and genome
CC       integrity by targeting the mitotic progression protein PSRC1 for
CC       proteasomal degradation in a cell cycle-dependent manner
CC       (PubMed:27697924). Also participates in meiosis by mediating the proper
CC       attachment between kinetochores and microtubules (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q91ZU0, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16325183,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27697924, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39039081,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40440427}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16325183, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27697924,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39039081, ECO:0000269|PubMed:40440427}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Substrate-recognition component of the ECS(ASB7) complex,
CC       composed of ASB7, CUL5, ELOB, ELOC and RNF7/RBX2 (PubMed:16325183,
CC       PubMed:27697924, PubMed:39039081, PubMed:40440427). Interacts (via
CC       PxVxL motif 1) with CBX5 (HP1 alpha); leading to its recruitment to
CC       heterochromatin (PubMed:40440427). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16325183,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27697924, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39039081,
