Gene Description

PIK3AP1 encodes BCAP, a multidomain intracellular adaptor linking immune receptors to phosphoinositide 3-kinase signaling. Its phosphorylated motifs and proline-rich regions recruit p85 and other SH2/SH3-containing proteins, including GRB2. The long isoform contains an N-terminal TIR domain that connects BCAP to Toll-like-receptor signalosomes; DBB/ankyrin-containing regions support self-association. Cytosolic BCAP can be recruited to receptor-bearing membranes. Its signaling functions contribute to B-cell activation and to context-dependent control of inflammatory cytokine production in innate immune cells. It lacks the lipid-kinase activity of the p110 enzymes it helps recruit.
