CC   -!- FUNCTION: Poly-ADP-ribosyltransferase that mediates poly-ADP-
CC       ribosylation of proteins and plays a key role in DNA repair
CC       (PubMed:17177976, PubMed:18055453, PubMed:18172500, PubMed:19344625,
CC       PubMed:19661379, PubMed:20388712, PubMed:21680843, PubMed:22582261,
CC       PubMed:23230272, PubMed:25043379, PubMed:26344098, PubMed:26626479,
CC       PubMed:26626480, PubMed:30104678, PubMed:31796734, PubMed:32028527,
CC       PubMed:32241924, PubMed:32358582, PubMed:33186521, PubMed:34465625,
CC       PubMed:34737271). Mediates glutamate, aspartate, serine, histidine or
CC       tyrosine ADP-ribosylation of proteins: the ADP-D-ribosyl group of
CC       NAD(+) is transferred to the acceptor carboxyl group of target residues
CC       and further ADP-ribosyl groups are transferred to the 2'-position of
CC       the terminal adenosine moiety, building up a polymer with an average
CC       chain length of 20-30 units (PubMed:19764761, PubMed:25043379,
CC       PubMed:28190768, PubMed:29954836, PubMed:35393539, PubMed:7852410,
CC       PubMed:9315851). Serine ADP-ribosylation of proteins constitutes the
CC       primary form of ADP-ribosylation of proteins in response to DNA damage
CC       (PubMed:33186521, PubMed:34874266, PubMed:41062835). Specificity for
CC       the different amino acids is conferred by interacting factors, such as
CC       HPF1 and NMNAT1 (PubMed:28190768, PubMed:29954836, PubMed:32028527,
CC       PubMed:33186521, PubMed:33589610, PubMed:34625544, PubMed:34874266).
CC       Following interaction with HPF1, catalyzes serine ADP-ribosylation of
CC       target proteins; HPF1 confers serine specificity by completing the
CC       PARP1 active site (PubMed:28190768, PubMed:29954836, PubMed:32028527,
CC       PubMed:33186521, PubMed:33589610, PubMed:34625544, PubMed:34874266).
CC       Also catalyzes tyrosine ADP-ribosylation of target proteins following
CC       interaction with HPF1 (PubMed:29954836, PubMed:30257210). Following
CC       interaction with NMNAT1, catalyzes glutamate and aspartate ADP-
CC       ribosylation of target proteins; NMNAT1 confers glutamate and aspartate
CC       specificity (By similarity). PARP1 initiates the repair of DNA breaks:
CC       recognizes and binds DNA breaks within chromatin and recruits HPF1,
CC       licensing serine ADP-ribosylation of target proteins, such as histones
CC       (H2BS6ADPr and H3S10ADPr), thereby promoting decompaction of chromatin
CC       and the recruitment of repair factors leading to the reparation of DNA
CC       strand breaks (PubMed:17177976, PubMed:18172500, PubMed:19344625,
CC       PubMed:19661379, PubMed:23230272, PubMed:27067600, PubMed:34465625,
CC       PubMed:34874266). HPF1 initiates serine ADP-ribosylation but restricts
CC       the polymerase activity of PARP1 in order to limit the length of poly-
CC       ADP-ribose chains (PubMed:33683197, PubMed:34732825, PubMed:34795260).
CC       In addition to base excision repair (BER) pathway, also involved in
CC       double-strand breaks (DSBs) repair: together with TIMELESS, accumulates
CC       at DNA damage sites and promotes homologous recombination repair by
CC       mediating poly-ADP-ribosylation (PubMed:26344098, PubMed:30356214).
CC       Mediates the poly-ADP-ribosylation of a number of proteins, including
CC       itself, APLF, CHFR, RPA1 and NFAT5 (PubMed:17396150, PubMed:19764761,
CC       PubMed:24906880, PubMed:34049076). In addition to proteins, also able
CC       to ADP-ribosylate DNA: catalyzes ADP-ribosylation of DNA strand break
CC       termini containing terminal phosphates and a 2'-OH group in single- and
CC       double-stranded DNA, respectively (PubMed:27471034). Required for PARP9
CC       and DTX3L recruitment to DNA damage sites (PubMed:23230272). PARP1-
CC       dependent PARP9-DTX3L-mediated ubiquitination promotes the rapid and
CC       specific recruitment of 53BP1/TP53BP1, UIMC1/RAP80, and BRCA1 to DNA
CC       damage sites (PubMed:23230272). PARP1-mediated DNA repair in neurons
CC       plays a role in sleep: senses DNA damage in neurons and promotes sleep,
CC       facilitating efficient DNA repair (By similarity). In addition to DNA
CC       repair, also involved in other processes, such as transcription
CC       regulation, programmed cell death, membrane repair, adipogenesis and
CC       innate immunity (PubMed:15607977, PubMed:17177976, PubMed:19344625,
CC       PubMed:27256882, PubMed:32315358, PubMed:32844745, PubMed:35124853,
CC       PubMed:35393539, PubMed:35460603). Acts as a repressor of
CC       transcription: binds to nucleosomes and modulates chromatin structure
CC       in a manner similar to histone H1, thereby altering RNA polymerase II
CC       (PubMed:15607977, PubMed:22464733). Acts both as a positive and
CC       negative regulator of transcription elongation, depending on the
CC       context (PubMed:27256882, PubMed:35393539). Acts as a positive
CC       regulator of transcription elongation by mediating poly-ADP-
CC       ribosylation of NELFE, preventing RNA-binding activity of NELFE and
CC       relieving transcription pausing (PubMed:27256882). Acts as a negative
CC       regulator of transcription elongation in response to DNA damage by
CC       catalyzing poly-ADP-ribosylation of CCNT1, disrupting the phase
CC       separation activity of CCNT1 and subsequent activation of CDK9
CC       (PubMed:35393539). Involved in replication fork progression following
CC       interaction with CARM1: mediates poly-ADP-ribosylation at replication
CC       forks, slowing fork progression (PubMed:33412112). Poly-ADP-ribose
CC       chains generated by PARP1 also play a role in poly-ADP-ribose-dependent
CC       cell death, a process named parthanatos (By similarity). Also acts as a
CC       negative regulator of the cGAS-STING pathway (PubMed:32315358,
CC       PubMed:32844745, PubMed:35460603). Acts by mediating poly-ADP-
CC       ribosylation of CGAS: PARP1 translocates into the cytosol following
CC       phosphorylation by PRKDC and catalyzes poly-ADP-ribosylation and
CC       inactivation of CGAS (PubMed:35460603). Acts as a negative regulator of
CC       adipogenesis: catalyzes poly-ADP-ribosylation of histone H2B on 'Glu-
CC       35' (H2BE35ADPr) following interaction with NMNAT1, inhibiting
CC       phosphorylation of H2B at 'Ser-36' (H2BS36ph), thereby blocking
CC       expression of pro-adipogenetic genes (By similarity). Involved in the
CC       synthesis of ATP in the nucleus, together with NMNAT1, PARG and NUDT5
CC       (PubMed:27257257). Nuclear ATP generation is required for extensive
CC       chromatin remodeling events that are energy-consuming
CC       (PubMed:27257257). Plays a role in sister chromatid cohesion by
CC       mediating ADP-ribosylation of RSMC during S phase which promotes the
CC       interaction between RSMC and CDCA5/sororin, leading to enhanced
CC       interaction of CDCA5/sororin with the cohesin complex and promotion of
CC       sister chromatid cohesion (PubMed:41261216).
