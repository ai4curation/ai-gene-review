CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome
CC       (APC/C), a cell cycle-regulated E3 ubiquitin ligase that controls
CC       progression through the cell cycle (PubMed:18485873, PubMed:27120157,
CC       PubMed:27509861). APC/C acts by mediating ubiquitination and subsequent
CC       degradation of target proteins: it mainly mediates the formation of
CC       'Lys-11'-linked polyubiquitin chains and, to a lower extent, the
CC       formation of 'Lys-48'- and 'Lys-63'-linked polyubiquitin chains
CC       (PubMed:18485873). APC/C catalyzes assembly of branched 'Lys-11'-/'Lys-
CC       48'-linked branched ubiquitin chains on target proteins
CC       (PubMed:29033132). APC/C is activated by CDC20 in the
CC       metaphase/anaphase transition of cell cycle, targeting the degradation
CC       of cyclin B and securin (PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C is
CC       regulated by the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC), which inhibits APC/C
CC       and delays chromosome segregation (PubMed:27509861). Has an enzyme-
CC       substrate adapter activity mediating the processive ubiquitination of
CC       specific substrates (PubMed:34942119). Involved in brain development
CC       through the specific ubiquitination and clearance of MKI67 from
CC       constitutive heterochromatin after neuronal progenitors exit mitosis
CC       (PubMed:34942119). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18485873,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27120157, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27509861,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29033132, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34942119}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29033132}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). Component of the
CC       anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C), composed of ANAPC1,
CC       ANAPC2, CDC27/ANAPC3, ANAPC4, ANAPC5, CDC16/ANAPC6, ANAPC7,
CC       CDC23/ANAPC8, ANAPC10, ANAPC11, CDC26/ANAPC12, ANAPC13, ANAPC15 and
CC       ANAPC16 (PubMed:9469815, PubMed:25043029, PubMed:26083744,
CC       PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C associates with CDC20 to form
CC       the CDC20-APC/C complex, which is crucial for metaphase/anaphase
CC       transition in cell cycle; this interaction is dependent on APC/C
CC       phosphorylation (PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C associates
CC       with mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC), which inhibits APC/C and delays
CC       chromosome segregation (PubMed:27509861). APC/C interacts with FZR1 and
CC       FBXO5 (PubMed:25043029, PubMed:26083744). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25043029,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26083744, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27120157,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27509861, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9469815}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18445686}. Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18445686}.
CC       Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton, spindle {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18445686}.
CC       Note=Localizes to spindle during metaphase and to cytoplasmic
CC       microtubules during interphase. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18445686}.
