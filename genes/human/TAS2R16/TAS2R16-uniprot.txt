CC   -!- FUNCTION: Gustducin-coupled receptor implicated in the perception of
CC       bitter compounds in the oral cavity and the gastrointestinal tract.
CC       Signals through PLCB2 and the calcium-regulated cation channel TRPM5.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12379855, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15759003}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with RTP3 and RTP4. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16720576}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16720576};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in a subset of gustducin-positive taste
CC       receptor cells of the tongue. Expressed in circumvallate papillae and
CC       testis (PubMed:16720576). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16720576}.
CC   -!- POLYMORPHISM: Genetic variation in TAS2R16 influences sensitivity to
CC       beta-glucopyranoside tasting (BGLPT) [MIM:617956]. Variant Asn-172
CC       results in greater receptor activation in response to bitter beta-
CC       glucopyranoside compounds including salicin, arbutin and amygdalin
CC       compared to Lys-172 (PubMed:16051168). Variant Lys-172 may influence
CC       risk of alcohol dependence (PubMed:16385453).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16051168, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16385453}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Several bitter taste receptors are expressed in a single
CC       taste receptor cell.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Confers bitter perception of salicin to non-taster mice.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor T2R family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
