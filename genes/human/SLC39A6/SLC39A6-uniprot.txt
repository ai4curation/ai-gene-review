CC   -!- FUNCTION: Zinc-influx transporter which plays a role in zinc
CC       homeostasis and in the induction of epithelial-to-mesenchymal
CC       transition (EMT) (PubMed:12839489, PubMed:18272141, PubMed:21422171,
CC       PubMed:23919497, PubMed:27274087, PubMed:34394081). When associated
CC       with SLC39A10, the heterodimer formed by SLC39A10 and SLC39A6 mediates
CC       cellular zinc uptake to trigger cells to undergo epithelial- to-
CC       mesenchymal transition (EMT) (PubMed:27274087). The SLC39A10-SLC39A6
CC       heterodimer also controls NCAM1 phosphorylation and its integration
CC       into focal adhesion complexes during EMT (By similarity). Zinc influx
CC       inactivates GSK3B, enabling unphosphorylated SNAI1 in the nucleus to
CC       down-regulate adherence genes such as CDH1, causing loss of cell
CC       adherence (PubMed:23919497). In addition, the SLC39A10-SLC39A6
CC       heterodimer plays an essential role in initiating mitosis by importing
CC       zinc into cells to initiate a pathway resulting in the onset of mitosis
CC       (PubMed:32797246). Participates in the T-cell receptor signaling
CC       regulation by mediating cellular zinc uptake into activated lymphocytes
CC       (PubMed:21422171, PubMed:30552163, PubMed:34394081). Regulates the zinc
CC       influx necessary for proper meiotic progression to metaphase II (MII)
CC       that allows the oocyte-to-egg transition (PubMed:25143461).
