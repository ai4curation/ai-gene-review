CC   -!- FUNCTION: Endoplasmic reticulum (ER)-anchored protein that mediates the
CC       formation of contact sites between the ER and endosomes via interaction
CC       with FFAT motif-containing proteins such as STARD3 or WDR44
CC       (PubMed:32344433, PubMed:33124732). STARD3-VAPA interaction enables
CC       cholesterol transfer from the ER to endosomes (PubMed:33124732). Via
CC       interaction with WDR44 participates in neosynthesized protein export
CC       (PubMed:32344433). In addition, recruited to the plasma membrane
CC       through OSBPL3 binding (PubMed:25447204). The OSBPL3-VAPA complex
CC       stimulates RRAS signaling which in turn attenuates integrin beta-1
CC       (ITGB1) activation at the cell surface (PubMed:25447204). With OSBPL3,
CC       may regulate ER morphology (PubMed:16143324). May play a role in
CC       vesicle trafficking (PubMed:11511104, PubMed:19289470).
