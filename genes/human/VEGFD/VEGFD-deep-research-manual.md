# Manual deep research: VEGFD

Perplexity was unavailable during preparation of this VEGF-family review, so this file records the manual literature synthesis used to check VEGFD rows.

- PMID:9435229 identifies VEGF-D as a VEGF homology domain ligand for KDR/VEGFR2 and FLT4/VEGFR3, with receptor tyrosine phosphorylation and endothelial mitogenic activity.
- PMID:17392173 shows that VEGF-D expression induces lymphangiogenesis in a pancreatic beta-cell tumor model.
- PMID:21515745 shows that proteolytic processing is required for VEGF-D to promote tumor angiogenesis, lymphangiogenesis and lymph node metastasis.
- PMID:19275959 supports VEGFD-induced human mast-cell chemotaxis as a specialized inflammatory output.
- PMID:22535492 is the mouse Vegfd GO_REF:0000107 source for dopaminergic neuron differentiation; PMID:27107012 is a pooled yeast two-hybrid study whose VAMP3-VEGFD pair-specific support was not resolved here.
