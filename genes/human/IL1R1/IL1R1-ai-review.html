Gene Description

Interleukin-1 receptor type 1 (IL1R1, CD121a) is the signalling receptor for the IL-1 agonists IL-1alpha and IL-1beta and also binds the antagonist IL-1Ra (IL1RN). It is a single-pass type I transmembrane glycoprotein with three extracellular immunoglobulin-like domains that bind ligand and a cytoplasmic Toll/IL-1 receptor (TIR) domain. Agonist binding recruits the co-receptor IL1RAP; the paired TIR domains then recruit MYD88, IRAK4 and IRAK1/2 (and TOLLIP), leading through TRAF6 and TAK1 to NF-kappaB and p38, ERK and JNK MAPK activation and inflammatory gene expression. IL-1Ra occupies the binding site without recruiting IL1RAP and so blocks signalling. IL1R1 is broadly expressed (fibroblasts, endothelial and epithelial cells, T cell subsets, especially Th1 and Th17), mediates IL-1 costimulation of IFN-gamma production by Th1 cells, can be phosphorylated on Tyr-496 to recruit PI3K, and can associate with PDGFRB in vascular smooth muscle cells. A soluble ectodomain is released by shedding. Gain-of-function variants cause autosomal dominant chronic recurrent multifocal osteomyelitis.

Key findings

  • Cloned type I IL-1 receptor: single-pass integral membrane protein, three extracellular Ig-like
    domains binding IL-1, large cytoplasmic region PMID:2530587.
  • Binds both agonists; pro-IL-1alpha binds without processing, pro-IL-1beta does not
    PMID:2950091.
  • With IL1RAP forms the signalling complex; TIR domains recruit MYD88, IRAK, TOLLIP (UniProt
    FUNCTION).
  • IL1RN binds IL1R1 and blocks IL1RAP recruitment [PMID:9062194 crystal structure; UniProt].
  • Tyr-496 phosphorylation recruits PIK3R1 (UniProt PTM); IL-1beta/PDGF-BB costimulation induces
    IL1R1-PDGFRB association and PI3K/Akt signalling in aortic SMCs PMID:16477012.
  • Th1 cells express IL1R1 and produce IFN-gamma in response to IL-1beta plus IL-12 PMID:10653850.
  • Shed soluble form (sIL1R1) [UniProt PTM, PubMed:8142597].

Curation observations

  • The NAS rows from ComplexPortal (ECO:0005547, complex membership) for negative regulation of
    inflammatory response (PMID:33391283) and negative regulation of IL-1 signalling (PMID:23847621)
    derive from antagonist/decoy complexes (IL1RN:IL1R1). The inhibition is done by IL1RN (or IL1R2);
    IL1R1 is the blocked receptor. Removed.
  • The NAD+ nucleosidase IEA comes from the TIR-domain ProRule. No experimental NADase activity has
    been reported for IL1R1; its TIR domain acts as a signalling scaffold. Marked as over-annotation.
