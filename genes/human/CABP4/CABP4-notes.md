# CABP4 (P57796) review notes

Session 2026-11-16, vision-disorder alignment campaign (dismech: Congenital_Stationary_Night_Blindness,
incomplete form / cone-rod synaptic disorder). Kept consistent with genes/human/CACNA1F.

## Synthesis

- CaBP4 is a calmodulin-like EF-hand protein (EF-hands with predicted Ca2+-binding sites; the
  CaBP family has a disabled EF2 loop [PMID:10625670]).
- Mouse: specifically expressed in photoreceptors and localized to synaptic terminals; Cabp4-/-
  mice have a thin outer plexiform layer, ectopic synapses, ~100-fold less sensitive rod bipolar
  responses, and reduced rod and cone synaptic ERG; CaBP4 binds the Cav1.4 C-terminus and shifts
  Cav1.4 activation to hyperpolarized voltages in transfected cells [PMID:15452577, abstract].
- Human: recessive CABP4 mutations cause CSNB2-like disease [PMID:16960802]; CaBP4 suppresses CDI
  of the primate-specific Cav1.4 delta-exon47 splice variant and enhances activation of full-length
  Cav1.4, which requires exon 47 [PMID:27226626].
- Human CaBP4 in transfected Neuro2A cells is diffusely cytosolic [PMID:19338761].
- MF: GO:0005246 calcium channel regulator activity (it regulates Cav1.4; it is not a channel
  subunit). BP: visual perception; synaptic role at the photoreceptor ribbon synapse.
- Over-annotations: phototransduction (CaBP4 acts at the synapse, not in the outer-segment cascade),
  signal transduction (NAS from a paper that did not characterize CaBP4), extracellular region (NAS,
  no support). Sensory perception of sound (IBA from the CaBP2 hair-cell node) not assessed in the
  cached literature; left UNDECIDED.
