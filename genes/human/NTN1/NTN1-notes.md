# NTN1 notes

## Evidence re-review, 2026-09-20: context and family transfer

The two specific POU-derived DNA-binding/Pol II transcription-factor transfers remain REMOVE based on recognition mechanism and protein architecture, but the broader Pol II transcription-regulation process is UNDECIDED: signaling can regulate transcription without the ligand being a sequence-specific factor. The current PTHR10574 PAINT export actually contains the POU-seeded PTN000180816 rows, so a demonstrated wrong-family tree placement must not be asserted without further upstream inspection. Primary secretion is not exclusivity: a truncated nucleolar netrin-1 associates with ribosomal-gene promoters in cancer cells [PMID:22871610, “Nucleolar localization of a netrin-1 isoform enhances tumor cell proliferation.”]. That ribosome-biogenesis finding does not establish the Pol II-specific molecular functions. Direct mouse work establishes basement-membrane-associated netrin-1 as a commissural guidance substrate [PMID:28780049, “Netrin1 establishes multiple boundaries for axon growth in the developing spinal cord.”]; basement membrane is therefore retained as a core location. The Slit/Robo-focused abstract of PMID:10102268 does not prove that NTN1 was not assayed. Its generic protein-binding annotation is removed for informativeness, not alleged reference misassignment. Description and core function were checked for consistency.

## Ligand activity and signaling pathway review, 2026-10-21

Updated the molecular function on `core_functions` and the three receptor-interaction `GO:0005515` (`protein binding`) `MODIFY` rows (`PMID:19721007`, `PMID:20434207`, `PMID:19196994`) from `GO:0005114` (*netrin receptor binding*) to `GO:0048018` (*receptor ligand activity*), with row-specific verbatim quotes from each publication. Following the `go-ontology#31015` (Jan 2026) reclassification of *receptor ligand activity* to `has_part` *signaling receptor binding*, receptor-specific binding terms record binding only and in GOA are annotated to receptor-side proteins (`DSCAM`, `TUBB1`/`TUBB3`) rather than netrin ligands; the GO-CAM signaling receptor guideline places ligands on `GO:0048018` or a child (`GO:0045499` *chemorepellent activity* / `GO:0042056` *chemoattractant activity*, neither of which alone covers bifunctional Netrin-1). Also proposed `NEW` `GO:0038007` (*netrin-activated signaling pathway*) and added it to `core_functions.directly_involved_in`, resolving the Category A ligand gap identified in `projects/LIGANDS_IN_SIGNALING.md` (`PMID:19196994`, `PMID:19721007`).
