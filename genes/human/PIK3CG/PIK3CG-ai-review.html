Gene Description

PIK3CG encodes p110γ, the catalytic subunit of class IB phosphoinositide 3-kinase. Complexes with p101 or p84/p87 couple G protein and Ras inputs to membrane PIP3 production, supporting leukocyte migration, activation and inflammatory responses. Recruitment and regulation vary with the regulatory subunit and receptor context; PKCβ can also activate p110γ after FcεRI stimulation. The enzyme phosphorylates additional phosphoinositide substrates in biochemical assays and has protein serine kinase activity. In the cardiovascular system, lipid kinase activity, protein phosphorylation and scaffolding of cAMP-regulating complexes contribute distinct effects on receptor trafficking and contractility.
