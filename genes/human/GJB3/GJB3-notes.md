## 2026-11-05 — biological evidence and research scope

Human Cx31 conducts ions and dyes between transfected cells, supporting electrical and chemical coupling ([PMID:16549784](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16549784/)). The original human cloning study identifies keratinocyte expression; its full text remains inaccessible ([PMID:9704026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9704026/)). A mouse donor study reports “Cx31 was localised among type 2 fibrocytes” ([PMID:14595769](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14595769/)); this does not establish the same cellular distribution in humans. Human Protein Atlas metadata independently identifies junctional and punctate intracellular pools, with cell-line and antibody details retained in the [source excerpts](GJB3-external-evidence.md#human-protein-atlas). The puncta are not assigned to a specific organelle.
