CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of a non-conventional N-methyl-D-aspartate (NMDA)
CC       receptors (NMDARs) that function as heterotetrameric, ligand-gated
CC       cation channels with low calcium permeability and low voltage-dependent
CC       block by Mg(2+) (By similarity). During the development of neural
CC       circuits, participates in the synaptic refinement period, restricting
CC       spine maturation and growth (By similarity). Forms glutamatergic
CC       receptor complexes with GluN1 and GluN2 subunits which are activated by
CC       glycine binding to the GluN1 and GluN3 subunits and L-glutamate binding
CC       to GluN2 subunits (By similarity). Forms excitatory glycinergic
CC       receptor complexes with GluN1 alone which are activated by glycine
CC       binding to the GluN1 and GluN3 subunits (PubMed:38598639). GluN3A
CC       subunit also binds D-serine (By similarity). Each GluN3 subunit confers
CC       differential attributes to channel properties, including activation,
CC       deactivation and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, Ca2(+)
CC       permeability, and binding to allosteric modulators (By similarity). By
CC       competing with GIT1 interaction with ARHGEF7/beta-PIX, may reduce
CC       GIT1/ARHGEF7-regulated local activation of RAC1, hence affecting
CC       signaling and limiting the maturation and growth of inactive synapses
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R1M7,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38598639}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterotetramer (PubMed:38598639). Forms heterotetrameric
CC       channels composed of two GluN1/zeta subunits (GRIN1), and two identical
CC       GluN3 subunits (GRIN3A or GRIN3B) (in vitro) (PubMed:38598639). Can
CC       also form heterotetrameric channels that contain at least two GluN1
CC       subunits and at least a combination of one GluN2 and one GluN3 subunits
CC       (in vitro) (By similarity). Does not form functional homomeric channels
CC       (By similarity). Found in a complex with GRIN1, GRIN2A or GRIN2B and
CC       PPP2CB (By similarity). Probably interacts with PPP2CB. No complex with
CC       PPP2CB is detected when NMDARs are stimulated by NMDA
CC       (PubMed:11588171). Interacts (via C-terminus) with GIT1, but not with
CC       GRIA1/GluA1, nor with synaptophysin/SYP; this interaction competes with
CC       GIT1 interaction with ARHGEF7/beta-PIX (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R1M7, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11588171,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38598639}.

CC       density {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R1M7}. Note=Requires the presence of
CC       GRIN1 to be targeted at the plasma membrane.
