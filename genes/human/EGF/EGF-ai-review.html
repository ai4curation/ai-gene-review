Gene Description

EGF encodes a type I transmembrane precursor that is proteolytically processed to release mature epidermal growth factor, a secreted ligand for EGFR-family receptor tyrosine kinases. Mature EGF binds EGFR and promotes ligand-induced receptor dimerization and autophosphorylation, activating ERBB signaling outputs such as the RAS-RAF-MEK-ERK cascade and PI3K-AKT signaling in epithelial and other responsive tissues.
