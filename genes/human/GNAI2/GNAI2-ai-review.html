Gene Description

GNAI2 encodes G alpha i2, a heterotrimeric G protein alpha subunit that transmits receptor signals through guanine-nucleotide exchange and hydrolysis. Activated Gi2 can inhibit selected adenylyl cyclases and also regulate channel, kinase and secretory pathways in a receptor- and cell-dependent manner. It functions at the cytoplasmic plasma-membrane surface, with additional cytosolic, mitotic and neuronal secretory-vesicle pools. Its physiological roles include immune and vascular signaling, endocrine secretion and synaptic regulation.
