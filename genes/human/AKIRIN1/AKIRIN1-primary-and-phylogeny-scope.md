# AKIRIN1 primary evidence and phylogenetic scope

## Identity and actual inheritance

Human AKIRIN1 is Q9H9L7; mouse Akirin1/Mighty is Q99LF1 (MGI:1915300). The mouse source is the same orthologous gene, not Akirin2. The current PTHR13293 tree places human leaf PTN002491310 below PTN002665250. That node carries positive IBDs for lamellipodium assembly, macrophage chemotaxis, myoblast migration, positive myoblast differentiation and negative satellite-cell proliferation. Nuclear/chromatin/cofactor/transcription assertions descend from PTN000329080. The extracted actual target path, IBDs and full-response hash are in AKIRIN1-Q9H9L7-paint-lineage.json. The inherited IRD at PTN002665250 concerns GO:0045089 positive regulation of innate immune response; it does not negate chemotaxis or the broader transcriptional functions.

## Primary evidence and access limits

- PMID:18066067: full relevant Results/Discussion checked. Human HsAkirin1 and HsAkirin2 were expressed in HeLa cells and showed NLS-dependent nuclear localization. The human rescue of the Drosophila immune assay used HsAkirin2. Mouse Akirin1-null MEFs retained the tested IL-1/TLR cytokine response, whereas Akirin2 loss affected selected outputs. Viable Akirin1-null animals and this specific immune result do not refute muscle or motility functions. The old review's direct-human-AKIRIN1 NF-kappaB mechanism was not established by these experiments.
- PMID:19406121: cached abstract and publisher abstract/preview checked; complete article unavailable. Mouse Akirin1 promotes macrophage and myoblast chemotaxis with PI3K-dependent actin reorganization and lamellipodia formation. The experimentally supported mouse annotations in QuickGO cite this paper for GO:0010592, GO:0010759 and GO:0014839. Thus there is positive target-ortholog evidence; nuclear enrichment and an outwardly normal knockout do not contradict these regulatory roles. Detailed direct effector identity remains unestablished here.
- PMID:18255059: abstract checked, complete article unavailable. Mighty overexpression in C2C12 cells promotes earlier cell-cycle withdrawal, myogenic regulator expression, differentiation and myotube hypertrophy. This supports a context-dependent anti-proliferative phase alongside positive myogenesis. The exact mouse MGI IMP source for both negative satellite terms is this paper; unavailable full text is not grounds to reverse the curator.
- PMID:23516508: full Methods, Results and relevant figures checked. Satellite cells were isolated from mouse muscle, but the direct Akirin1 overexpression experiment in Figure 6 uses C2C12 myoblasts. Under the dexamethasone protocol it improves proliferation and differentiation. Myostatin inhibition in other experiments is an upstream perturbation, not an independent direct Akirin1 assay. Different cell states/timing permit this positive proliferation result to coexist with earlier cell-cycle exit in the 2008 experiments.
- PMID:40211850: full relevant Methods/Results checked. In goat muscle satellite cells, Akirin1 plasmid plus control miRNA reduces Pax7/PCNA and EdU/CCK-8 outcomes while increasing myogenic markers and myotubes; miR-345-3p reverses these effects. The gene-specific overexpression experiment corroborates a negative proliferation context without asserting that all Akirin1 perturbations in all states have the same direction.
- PMID:30746755: abstract checked; full article unavailable. Mouse knockout alters fiber-type composition and oxidative phenotype despite absence of a gross phenotype. A promoter-reporter assay supports Akirin1 regulation of MuRF1 transcription. This is supporting transcriptional context, not proof of every proposed physical cofactor mechanism.
- PMID:39856124: full relevant Methods/Results checked. Both human and murine lung models are used, but the Akirin1 perturbation/rescue experiments use mouse lung fibroblasts with 344SQ cancer cells and mouse Akirin1 cDNA. The output is cancer-cell behavior in coculture; it is not a direct negative macrophage-chemotaxis assay. These findings do not negate the 2009 cell-autonomous motility annotations. No additional cancer-process assertion is proposed from this context.
- PMID:25416956: the cache is marked full text but contains mainly introductory/discussion material. Independent IntAct PSICQUIC retrieval verifies three human AKIRIN1-GOPC two-hybrid records for this exact paper (saved TSV and retrieval/hash in AKIRIN1-source-and-term-check.json). The real binary interaction is retained as evidence, but it does not support the old replacement GO:0044877 protein-containing complex binding. The generic protein-binding row is retained as non-core because the binary evidence is credible while its connection to the core transcriptional mechanism remains unresolved.
- PMID:34711951: abstract-only cache. The abstract establishes AKIRIN2-dependent proteasome import, and the existing curated AKIRIN1 UniProt record explicitly distinguishes AKIRIN1 from that activity. No new AKIRIN1 proteasome-binding/import assertion is made, and this comparison is not generalized to absence of all possible proteasome contacts.

## Satellite identity versus terminal myogenesis

QuickGO defines GO:0014816 skeletal muscle satellite cell differentiation as acquisition of satellite-cell features; GO:1902725 negatively regulates this process. It is not synonymous with a satellite-derived myoblast forming a myotube. The authoritative term/edge responses and mouse source rows are saved in AKIRIN1-source-and-term-check.json.

Related primary evidence: Kelly Anne Dyer, *Characterisation of Mighty Expression during Skeletal Muscle Regeneration*, MSc thesis, University of Waikato (2006), [repository](https://hdl.handle.net/10289/2243), [PDF](https://researchcommons.waikato.ac.nz/server/api/core/bitstreams/166160c8-c15b-4595-9a90-759aa8ee4fbd/content). Sections 3.7.1/3.7.2 quantify reduced Sca-1 and CD34 transcripts in reserve-cell and myotube fractions from Mighty-overexpressing C2C12 clones. Sections 4.1/4.2.2 describe smaller reserve-cell populations alongside enhanced fusion and propose altered commitment versus reserve-cell maintenance. This corroborates the compatibility of reduced satellite/reserve identity with positive terminal myogenesis. Marker expression and an in-vitro reserve-cell model are not lineage tracing, and the thesis is not a substitute for the inaccessible full 2008 journal article. The standing curator-supported negative-differentiation transfer is retained with these scope limits; no contradictory target evidence was found.

## Existing research report critically incorporated

The entire Falcon biological report and its evidence-table artifact were read. Its promyogenic cofactor synthesis and explicit separation of AKIRIN1-specific muscle evidence from predominantly Akirin2/Drosophila immune mechanisms are useful. Its general positive-proliferation summary follows the glucocorticoid study and is not universal across conditions. The 2025 fibroblast study was checked directly to distinguish human association/model context from mouse Akirin1 perturbation. Fertility-expression and disease-association findings do not establish additional biological participation. The report supplies synthesis and access leads, not independent experimental proof. No exact OpenScientist hypothesis was cached at review start and no unresolved biological contradiction remains that requires a duplicate or new report.
