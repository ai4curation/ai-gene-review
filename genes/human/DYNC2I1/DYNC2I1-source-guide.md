# DYNC2I1 evidence and source guide

The review preserves 56 imported source assertions and one official 1,066-residue product. It proposes no new GO annotation. This guide identifies the primary evidence, distinguishes repository cache availability from actual original access, and links the [portable database replay](DYNC2I1-bioinformatics/evidence-audit/README.md).

## Identity, location and propagation

[UniProt Q8WVS4](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8WVS4/entry) and [HGNC:21862](https://www.genenames.org/data/gene-symbol-report/#!/hgnc_id/HGNC:21862) identify DYNC2I1, formerly WDR60. The official sequence has 1,066 residues and no alternative-products block. Engineered truncations, binding fragments and disease variants are not promoted to natural isoforms.

The official [PANTHER tree](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/treeinfo?family=PTHR16022), [alignment](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/familymsa?family=PTHR16022) and [PAINT IBD data](https://data.pantherdb.org/ftp/downloads/paint/current/IBD.gaf) reproduce the target sequence and seven literal IBD joins at PTN008562642. Its human descendant evidence includes the target itself, which is expected and not circular. The replay includes the full saved IBD file rather than a hand-written donor summary. It also preserves source-specific IntAct records from the [target query](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/search?query=Q8WVS4), sequence checksums and the [original AP-MS deposit](https://public.ndexbio.org/v2/network/95bc75d5-d1d1-11ee-8a40-005056ae23aa).

The [HPA target page](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000126870-DYNC2I1/subcellular) was read at the per-location level: centrosome and primary cilium are supported, basal body approved, and cytosol uncertain. No image pixels were scored. The positive HPA cilium assertion is retained at its source scope; general centrosomal pools are not erased by more specific ciliary-base evidence. The uncertain cytosol score is not presented as an independent high-confidence soluble pool.

Six [Reactome IFT events](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5625426) trace to Q8WVS4 residues 1–1066 in the cilium entity [R-HSA-5624903](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5624903) or tip entity [R-HSA-5625409](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5625409). The tip is core-relevant turnaround context within the main transport mechanism; these are curated model states, not new human localization or residence-time measurements. The [ciliary-base](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0097546) and [ciliary-tip](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0097542) closures both include cilium and do not include one another. Likewise [cilium assembly](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0060271) and [intraciliary retrograde transport](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0035721) are nonredundant in the saved typed closure.

[InterPro IPR042505](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/entry/InterPro/IPR042505/) provides the family-level electronic mapping. The current [ARBA00028353](https://www.uniprot.org/arba/ARBA00028353) output is biologically consistent with a ciliary protein, but its current condition sets were not fully matched by the captured target cross-references; the historical execution is therefore unresolved, not falsely reconstructed. The mouse donor [Q8C761](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8C761/entry) is identified independently, while direct human studies support the retained target location. The failed protein-identifier query is not treated as absence of a donor.

## Cache and source preservation

All existing publication-cache bytes and flags remain exact. A `full_text_available: true` flag does not guarantee that every table, caption or Methods section is present. In particular, the protected PMID:25036637 and PMID:33961781 representations are shorter than the separately read genuine normal copies, and the tear cache omits its identifying Table II row. These access distinctions are recorded rather than silently replacing shared files. PMID:32229580 remains abstract-only; citation correctness for its limited use is distinct from full-text availability.

The normal-fetched PMID:21530541 is the sole new publication cache proposed here. The normal-fetched metadata sources PMID:12853948, PMID:25492405 and PMID:27021811 were not used for new scientific claims and are not included in the publication payload. Existing provider output is preserved; the new provider and fallback failures are documented in the appended notes. The original notes remain an exact prefix, all 27 inherited row excerpts remain unchanged, and the four excerpts attached to the superseded cores are removed without relocation. There are no new quotations. The source-input archive contains database records, not republished paper full text.
