# NEURL1 (human, O76050) review notes

## Identity
- E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase NEURL1 (Neuralized homolog). PANTHER PTHR12429 (NEURALIZED) / PTHR12429:SF13.
- IBA: ubiquitin protein ligase activity PTN004361013; regulation of Notch signaling PTN000276953; plasma membrane, postsynaptic density, positive regulation of long-term neuronal synaptic plasticity PTN002646352.

## Key findings
- Human NEURL1 overexpression in medulloblastoma decreases JAG1, HES1, HEY1, and induces apoptosis [PMID:20847082].
- In vitro monoubiquitination of JAG1; CPEB3 ubiquitination in neurons (UniProt; mouse).
- N-myristoylated; membrane targeting (UniProt PTM).
- MIB1 rather than Neuralized is the dominant mammalian ligand E3 [PMID:25747658].

## Decisions
- Core: GO:0061630 (Notch-ligand monoubiquitination; regulation of Notch signaling) and neuronal CPEB3 ubiquitination (postsynaptic density; LTP).
- translation regulator activity over-annotated (CPEB3 is the regulator); protein binding (14-3-3 screen) removed.

## Variant-relevant biology
- Neuralized is essential for Delta signaling in Drosophila neurogenesis but minor/redundant in mammals.
