CC   -!- FUNCTION: Orphan receptor involved in vision (PubMed:22325362,
CC       PubMed:24084093). Required for signal transduction through retinal
CC       depolarizing bipolar cells (PubMed:22325362). Acts as an atypical G
CC       protein-coupled receptor that recruits and regulates the R7 group RGS-
CC       GNB5 complexes instead of activating G proteins: promotes the GTPase
CC       activator activity of R7 RGS proteins, increasing the GTPase activity
CC       of G protein alpha subunits, thereby driving them into their inactive
CC       GDP-bound form (By similarity). Associates with components of
CC       metabotropic signaling cascade in retina ON-bipolar neurons, such as
CC       TRPM1 and GRM6: may control the ability of the GRM6 cascade to gate
CC       TRPM1 (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:E9PY61,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22325362, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24084093}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (By similarity). Associates with the R7 group RGS-
CC       GNB5 complexes, composed of an R7 group RGS subunit (RGS6, RGS7, RGS9
CC       or RGS11) and GNB5, promoting their localization to the cell membrane
CC       and regulating the GTPase activator activity of R7 RGS proteins (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with TRPM1 (By similarity). Interacts with GRM6
CC       (By similarity). Interacts with EGFLAM; transsynaptic interaction is
CC       required for synaptic organization of photoreceptor cells (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:E9PY61}.
