CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required for regulation and trafficking of retinal membrane
CC       guanylyl cyclases GUCY2D/RetGC1 and GUCY2F/RetGC2 in photoreceptor
CC       cells (PubMed:21078983, PubMed:21928830, PubMed:27471269,
CC       PubMed:29515371, PubMed:30559291). Mediates the export of retinal
CC       guanylyl cyclases (RetGCs) from the endoplasmic reticulum and inhibits
CC       their basal and GCAP-stimulated catalytic activity during intracellular
CC       transport to the photoreceptor outer segment, thereby preventing
CC       premature activation of RetGC (PubMed:21078983, PubMed:21928830,
CC       PubMed:27471269, PubMed:29515371, PubMed:30559291). Up-regulates the
CC       activity of GUK1, a kinase involved in GMP recycling and, indirectly,
CC       cGMP synthesis (PubMed:29515371). Plays an important role in the
CC       survival of rod and cone photoreceptors in the retina (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8BRE0, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21078983,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21928830, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27471269,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29515371, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30559291}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer (PubMed:30559291). Interacts with GUCY2D and GUCY2F;
CC       negatively regulates their activity and promotes their exit from the
CC       endoplasmic reticulum and trafficking to the photoreceptor outer
CC       segments (PubMed:21078983, PubMed:21928830, PubMed:26100624,
CC       PubMed:27471269, PubMed:29515371, PubMed:30559291). Interacts with
CC       GUK1; up-regulates GUK1 activity (PubMed:29515371).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21078983, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21928830,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26100624, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27471269,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29515371, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30559291}.
