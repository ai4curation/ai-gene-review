# GLA evidence scope and preserved curation provenance

The current review covers 58 distinct source annotations and one 429-residue human product, P06280. All actions were manually assessed before editorial condensation. No new GO assertion is proposed. The core groups one catalytic activity with two substrate classes; receptor cargo, cell preparation and downstream phenotype are kept distinct from the protein's catalytic participation.

All 73 official publication identities were checked. The original 70-reference union supplied 59 abstracts, all read. Selected originals, supplementary records and corrections were read as described below; this is not a claim to have read all 73 full articles. Ten no-abstract records remain scientifically unverified.

## PMID:39940

Selected original human-liver Methods and Results were read, despite absent PubMed abstract. Separate soluble-oligosaccharide and glycolipid assays establish catalysis. Historical preparations do not define modern alternative products.

## PMID:8804427

Original Methods distinguish human cDNA from Sf9 production. Galactose release from labeled liposomal Gb3 establishes chemistry with saposin-B stimulation. The reported monomeric preparation limits universal dimer-requirement claims.

## PMID:1332979

Selected original Methods, Results and captions distinguish human GLA from its CHO host. Receptor affinity, secretion and immunogold localization are positive findings. Presumed TGN structures and crystals reflect overexpression; the receptor column does not independently identify receptor species.

## PMID:3029062

Original human-fibroblast pulse-chase and selective lysosomal-rupture controls distinguish delivery, subsequent maturation and secretion. Altered targeting changes secretion. Precursor and mature bands are processing forms.

## PMID:6256390

Selected original native-size and reducing-SDS measurements support the homodimeric splenic preparation. This observation is retained without requiring every recombinant active preparation to have identical oligomeric state.

## PMID:15003450

Official abstract supports the human homodimeric structure and catalytic residues. Complete original was not recovered; no historical construct-to-current sequence equivalence is claimed.

## PMID:21949853

Original SPR supports direct recombinant-GLA binding to purified human sortilin; engineered HEK293 uptake supports cargo delivery. Physiological receptor shares and exact purified construct boundaries remain unresolved.

## PMID:6313412

Complete abstract reports ConA-associated stabilization and enhanced uptake of placental GLA. Full original is inaccessible. The positive interaction remains; removal concerns generic-binding informativeness, not an alleged assay error.

## PMID:33961781

Literal BioPlex 293T/HCT116 rows and directed releases identify GLA bait and ALG11 prey. Original methods use FLAG-HA affinity purification from lysates. Association does not establish direct contact or a transferred catalytic role.

## PMID:36115835

Original terminal-ten-residue peptide/isolated-PDZ assays and target tables support positive pairs. Only two exact IntAct records have quantified parameters; below-quantification values are not nonbinding. TX1BP3 workbook shorthand remains a candidate name correspondence, not clone-sequence proof.

## PMID:36477203

The original correction repairs figure labels, a missing column and panel arrangement. It announces no replacement interaction table. Figure pixels were not independently inspected.

## PMID:40205054

Original U2OS AP-MS network edge 566462 links TAX1BP3 bait to GLA prey. Global SEC and imaging are not pair-specific direct-contact tests. Official O14907 identity is TAX1BP3; no full construct equivalence is claimed.

## PMID:41039152

The publisher correction replaces a duplicated fourth Methods loss-function equation and corrects x/y subscripts. No AP-MS edge correction is announced; the mathematics was not independently revalidated.

## PMID:15668341

The actual mouse IMP donor source reports iNOS expression and nitration after Gla loss, including an aged comparison without atherosclerosis. These are contextual downstream readouts, not purified NOS inhibition or direct human experiments.

## PMID:23533145

Original supplementary Table 2 row 606 identifies P06280; unique-peptide counts are 1, 2, 8 and 6 across four pools. Supplementary methods establish exosome-enriched urine preparation and mass spectrometry, not topology or a diagnostic abundance difference.

## Inference and product boundaries

The inspected PAINT family and AN5/PTN000155295 assertions support inherited activity, oligosaccharide metabolism, glycoside catabolism and the broad cytoplasmic compartment. Cytoplasm includes organelles. Root lysosome annotations are retained alongside lumen terms. No natural alternative protein product is inferred from processing bands, experimental constructs, replacement enzymes or a historical RNA-editing report.

The two Reactome GLA reactions identify the catalyst through dimer and P06280 component links. The granule-exocytosis model places GLA in input/output cargo sets. Catalysis supports metabolic participation; release as cargo does not justify a new exocytosis assertion. GO-CAM absence is not used as evidence of a gap.

Independent primary enzymology and the shared core support the function underlying variant and inference rows. Individual annotations retain their original reference and literal machine context; the source guide avoids multiplying the same experimental claim across those rows. Positive interaction pairs remain recorded even where generic protein binding is removed as uninformative.
