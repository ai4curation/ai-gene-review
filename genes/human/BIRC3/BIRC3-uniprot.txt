CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multi-functional protein which regulates not only caspases
CC       and apoptosis, but also modulates inflammatory signaling and immunity,
CC       mitogenic kinase signaling and cell proliferation, as well as cell
CC       invasion and metastasis (PubMed:21737329, PubMed:21737330,
CC       PubMed:21931591, PubMed:23453969). Acts as an E3 ubiquitin-protein
CC       ligase regulating NF-kappa-B signaling (PubMed:21737329,
CC       PubMed:21737330, PubMed:21931591, PubMed:23453969). Regulates both
CC       canonical and non-canonical NF-kappa-B signaling by acting in opposite
CC       directions: acts as a positive regulator of the canonical pathway and
CC       suppresses constitutive activation of non-canonical NF-kappa-B
CC       signaling (PubMed:22095281). The target proteins for its E3 ubiquitin-
CC       protein ligase activity include: RIPK1, RIPK2, RIPK3, RIPK4, CASP3,
CC       CASP7, CASP8, IKBKE, TRAF1, and BCL10 (PubMed:21931591,
CC       PubMed:23453969). Acts as a key regulator of TNF-mediated signaling by
CC       mediating ubiquitination of RIPK1, activating the canonical NF-kappa-B
CC       pathway (PubMed:21737329, PubMed:21737330). Acts as an important
CC       regulator of innate immune signaling via regulation of Toll-like
CC       receptors (TLRs), Nod-like receptors (NLRs) and RIG-I like receptors
CC       (RLRs), collectively referred to as pattern recognition receptors
CC       (PRRs) (PubMed:22095281). Protects cells from spontaneous formation of
CC       the ripoptosome, a large multi-protein complex that has the capability
CC       to kill cancer cells in a caspase-dependent and caspase-independent
CC       manner (PubMed:22095281). Suppresses ripoptosome formation by
CC       ubiquitinating RIPK1 and CASP8 (PubMed:21737329, PubMed:21737330).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21737329, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21737330,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21931591, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23453969,
CC       ECO:0000303|PubMed:22095281}.
