# LRCH3 literature notes

## Evidence hierarchy

The strongest direct functional evidence is PMID:29467281. In cultured human RPE
and HEK293T systems, domain mapping placed DOCK7 binding at the LRCH3 LRR region
and MYO6 binding between the LRR and CH regions: [PMID:29467281, “These
experiments showed both MYO6 and DOCK7 can bind to LRCH3; DOCK7 binds to its
leucine‐rich repeats and MYO6 to a region between amino acids 383–648 (Fig 5C).”]
The LRCH3–MYO6 interaction was tested as direct by mammalian two-hybrid assay:
[PMID:29467281, “Indeed, our M2H assay confirmed that LRCH3 interacts directly
with MYO6; the binding site again mapped to amino acids 363–648 and required both
the WWY and RRL motif in the MYO6 CBD, corroborating our earlier SILAC experiments
(Figs 1E and 5D).”]

The same study supports a DOCK7-induced septin displacement (DISP) module rather
than a generic binding role. LRCH3's CH-containing region recruited and
oligomerized SEPT7 in a mitochondrial knock-sideways assay [PMID:29467281,
“Co‐expression of MitoGBD with GFP alone, full‐length GFP‐LRCH3 or GFP‐tagged
domain fragments of LRCH3 in RPE cells caused all to relocalise to the
mitochondria; however, only relocalisation of full‐length LRCH3 or a fragment
containing its calponin homology domain triggered the concomitant recruitment,
and oligomerisation, of SEPT7, from its steady state localisation along actin
filaments to mitochondria (Figs 6A and EV5C).”] Coexpression with the DOCK7 DHR2
region increased the septin-ring phenotype [PMID:29467281, “Strikingly however,
the co‐expression of LRCH3 and the DOCK7 DHR2 domain, which contains the LRCH3
and MYO6 binding sites as well as the RAC1/CDC42 GEF activity of DOCK7, caused a
very dramatic increase in the number of cells (~80%) containing septin ring
structures (Fig 6D).”]

## Interaction and complex boundaries

The DISP model is experimentally well motivated, but its edges are not all
equivalent. LRCH3–MYO6 was supported as direct by mammalian two-hybrid analysis,
whereas LRCH3–DOCK7 topology was mapped by pull-downs and proximity data. Septins
were not recovered in LRCH3 immunoprecipitates, so their relationship is best
described as proximity plus CH-region-dependent relocalization rather than a
proven stable binary interaction [PMID:29467281, “As we were unable to observe
septins in LRCH3 IPs (the same IP was positive for MYO6 and DOCK7; compare Figs
5C and EV5B), we verified this interaction using a knock sideways approach that
targeted GFP‐LRCH3 to mitochondria using a GFP binding domain fused to a
mitochondria‐targeting sequence (MitoGBD).”] Most decisive experiments used
expressed proteins in cultured cells; they do not show that MYO6, LRCH3, DOCK7,
and septins form one constitutive endogenous complex in every tissue.

The other seeded interaction references are systematic screens and should remain
corroborative. PMID:24255178 reports a 749-edge mammalian Hippo interaction
network [PMID:24255178, “We identified 749 protein interactions, including 599
previously unrecognized interactions, and demonstrated that several interactions
with serine and threonine phosphatases were phosphorylation-dependent.”]
PMID:25416956 describes a large binary human interactome [PMID:25416956, “Here,
we describe a systematic map of ?14,000 high-quality human binary protein-protein
interactions.”] PMID:33961781 is explicitly cell-line-specific AP-MS
[PMID:33961781, “Through affinity-purification mass spectrometry, we have created
two proteome-scale, cell-line-specific interaction networks.”] OpenCell combines
endogenous tagging, imaging, and mass spectrometry [PMID:35271311, “We combined
genome engineering, confocal live-cell imaging, mass spectrometry, and data
science to systematically map the localization and interactions of human
proteins.”] The newer U2OS map likewise integrates interaction and imaging data
[PMID:40205054, “Here we construct a global map of human subcellular architecture
through joint measurement of biophysical interactions and immunofluorescence
images for over 5,100 proteins in U2OS osteosarcoma cells.”] These datasets
support context-specific association with DOCK7, MYO6, or DOCK8, but do not on
their own establish direct binding, regulatory direction, or a stable complex.

## Localization, species, paralog, and isoform boundaries

PMID:20805893 provides direct human localization evidence for an expressed
LRCH3 construct in dividing HeLa cells [PMID:20805893, “Similarly, we found that
hLRCH3 accumulates at the cleavage furrow from anaphase onset (Figure S6C),
albeit without significant cortical accumulation being observed at previous
stages of mitosis.”] The paper establishes that the four human paralogs share
LRR-plus-CH architecture [PMID:20805893, “In human, only four highly-related
proteins (hLRCH1-4) simultaneously harbor these two motifs.”] Its depletion,
cortical-stability, spindle-positioning, fertility, and stress phenotypes were
obtained for the single Drosophila dLRCH gene, not human LRCH3, and should not be
transferred.

The focused DISP study itself provides a useful paralog control: [PMID:29467281,
“Although other LRCH family members such as LRCH1 were identified in the MYO6
and DOCK7 BioID data sets, no interaction between LRCH1 and MYO6 was observed,
although LRCH1 did co‐immunoprecipitate DOCK7 (Fig EV4A and B).”] Thus LRCH1's
DOCK8-regulatory biology and functions reported for LRCH2 or LRCH4 are not
evidence for LRCH3. The reviewed literature does not establish an
LRCH3-isoform-specific function: expressed full-length LRCH3 constructs and
partner isoforms used in interaction datasets identify what was tested, not a
function unique to one of the four UniProt LRCH3 isoforms.

## Bottom line for later synthesis

The best-supported human LRCH3 role is an adaptor/regulatory role in the
MYO6–LRCH3–DOCK7 DISP module that reorganizes septin structures in cultured
cells. Direct LRCH3–MYO6 binding, domain-resolved partner recruitment, and the
septin-remodeling phenotype are supported; direct actin binding, universal
endosomal localization, a constitutive four-component complex, and transfer of
other LRCH-paralog functions are not.
