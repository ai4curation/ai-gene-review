# ACD (Q96AP0) Curation Notes — Shelterin Subunit TPP1

## 1. Nomenclature and Isoform Disambiguation

1. **Gene symbol vs. protein alias homonymy (`ACD` vs. `TPP1`)**:
   - Human HGNC symbol `ACD` (*ACD shelterin complex subunit and telomerase recruitment factor*, `UniProtKB:Q96AP0`, `PANTHER:PTHR14487`, subfamily `PANTHER:PTHR14487:SF3` `ADRENOCORTICAL DYSPLASIA PROTEIN HOMOLOG`) encodes the shelterin complex subunit universally referred to in telomere biochemistry as **TPP1** (coined from its three 2004 discovery aliases **TINT1** [PMID:15380063], **PTOP** [PMID:15181449], and **PIP1** [PMID:15231715]).
   - Official HGNC symbol `TPP1` (`UniProtKB:O14773`, `PANTHER:PTHR10574`) instead encodes lysosomal sedolisin-family serine protease **tripeptidyl-peptidase 1** (`CLN2`, `EC 3.4.14.9`). Similarly, in this repository `genes/DANRE/tpp1` (`F8W2M8`) is the zebrafish lysosomal protease (`PTHR10574`), whereas zebrafish's shelterin `PTHR14487` ortholog is `A0A1D5NT19` (`ZFIN:ZDB-GENE-081022-45`).

2. **Alternative promoter isoforms (`TPP1-S` vs. `TPP1-L`) and the −86 residue coordinate offset**:
   - Reviewed UniProt entry `Q96AP0` (sequence version 4, **458 aa**) displays `Isoform 3` (`Q96AP0-3`, MANE Select `ENST00000620761.6` / `RefSeq:NP_001075955.2`), which corresponds to **TPP1-S** (initiating at Met87 of the historical 544-aa reading frame). `Isoform 2` (`Q96AP0-2`, `RefSeq:NP_075065.3`, 455 aa) lacks residues 34–36 (`VSP_060243`).
   - UniProt currently flags the 544-aa cDNA sequences (`AAH16904.1`, `AAS80318.1`, `BAB14658.1`) under `SEQUENCE CAUTION` as *"Erroneous initiation; Extended N-terminus"*. However, Grill et al. 2019 (`PMID:31216472`) demonstrated by ENCODE CAGE/RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, prime-editing/CRISPR isoform knockouts, and testis Western blotting that **TPP1-L (544 aa)** is a genuine, biologically active alternative-promoter isoform predominantly expressed in differentiating male germ cells (spermatids), whereas **TPP1-S (458 aa, `Q96AP0`)** is the predominant isoform in somatic and stem cells (`"Unlike TPP1-S, which activates telomerase, TPP1-L blocks telomere elongation despite containing an intact TEL patch and recruiting telomerase to telomeres"`, `PMID:31216472`). An Arginine-rich motif (`R43/R46/R48` in 544-aa numbering) in the unique 86-aa N-terminal tail of `TPP1-L` restrains telomerase catalysis after recruitment (`PMID:31216472`). We therefore record both `ACD_TPP1_S` (`Q96AP0-3`) and `ACD_TPP1_L` (`GenBank:AAH16904.1`) under `functional_isoforms`, with a `MISSING_ISOFORM` `source_defects` entry requesting UniProt restore `TPP1-L` as an alternative-initiation isoform on `Q96AP0`.
   - **Coordinate translation table (`TPP1-L` 544 aa → canonical `Q96AP0` 458 aa, Δ = −86)**:
     - **NOB (N-terminal of OB) hydrophobic motif**: `L95-R96-P97-W98-I99-R100-E101-L102-I103-L104` (544-aa) → **`L9-R10-P11-W12-I13-R14-E15-L16-I17-L18`** (`FT MOTIF 11..13 /note="PWI"` in `Q96AP0`; contacts TERT TEN–TRAP hydrophobic cleft in `PMID:35201900`, `PMID:31216472`).
     - **USP7-binding peptide motif**: `A129-G130-P131-S132` (544-aa) → **`A43-G44-P45-S46`** in `Q96AP0` (`PMID:25172512`).
     - **TEL patch acidic loop**: `D166-W167-E168-E169-K170-E171` (544-aa) → **`D80-W81-E82-E83-K84-E85`** in `Q96AP0` (`FT VARIANT 84 Missing in DKCA6 and DKCB7`, i.e., historical `ΔK170` in `PMID:25205116`, `PMID:25233904`).
     - **Downstream TEL patch residues**: `R180, L183, L212, E215` (544-aa) → **`R94, L97, L126, E129`** in `Q96AP0` (`PMID:22863003`, `PMID:35201900`).
     - **OB-fold ubiquitination dyad**: `K232, K233` (544-aa) → **`K146, K147`** in `Q96AP0` (`PMID:25172512`).
     - **POT1-binding domain (RD)**: `244–337` (544-aa) → **`158–251`** in `Q96AP0` (`FT REGION 158..251`).
     - **TIN2-binding C-terminal variant `P491T`**: `P491` (544-aa) → **`P405`** in `Q96AP0` (`FT VARIANT 405 P -> T in DKCB7`, `PMID:25233904`).
     - Note that recent clinical sequencing papers keyed to MANE Select `NM_001082486.2` (e.g. `p.S295N` and `p.H358Q` in `PMID:41822348`) use native 458-aa `Q96AP0` coordinates directly (`Ser295` and `His358`).

## 2. Core Molecular Biology & GO Annotation Synthesis

1. **Shelterin molecular adaptor (`GO:0060090` `molecular adaptor activity`, `GO:0070187` `shelterin complex`)**:
   - ACD/TPP1 is one of the six core subunits of mammalian shelterin (`TRF1`, `TRF2`, `RAP1`, `TIN2`, `TPP1`/`ACD`, `POT1`) [PMID:15181449, PMID:15231715, PMID:15380063, PMID:15383534, PMID:16880378]. Its central recruitment domain (`aa 158–251`) binds the C-terminal OB3–HJRL split domain of POT1, while its C-terminal TIN2-interacting helix binds TIN2 (`TINF2`), tethering POT1 to telomeric chromatin (`GO:0070200` / `GO:0070198`) and stabilizing the TRF1–TIN2–TRF2 bridge (`GO:0032202` `telomere assembly`, `PMID:16880378`).
   - Through POT1 recruitment to the 3' single-stranded G-overhang, ACD/TPP1 prevents ATR checkpoint activation and represses aberrant repair/fusions (`GO:0016233` `telomere capping`, `GO:0031848` `protection from non-homologous end joining at telomere`; `PMID:17632522`, `PMID:18185984`, `PMID:22101936`).

2. **ACD/TPP1 does NOT directly bind telomeric DNA (`GO:0042162` `telomeric repeat DNA binding` → `REMOVE`)**:
   - Within mammalian shelterin, only TRF1, TRF2 (dsDNA), and POT1 (ssDNA) directly contact telomeric repeat DNA (`PMID:23685356`). Reconstituted gel-shift assays (`PMID:31216472`: *"Although neither protein bound DNA directly, both TPP1-L and TPP1-S further stabilized the POT1-DNA complex in vitro"*) and the 3.2 Å cryo-EM structure of human telomerase–TPP1–POT1–DNA (`PMID:35201900`: *"Unlike its ciliate TEBPβ homolog (28), TPP1 does not contact the DNA"*) establish that ACD/TPP1 has no intrinsic DNA-binding activity. The `IDA` row from `PMID:23685356` reflects SILAC co-enrichment of the intact 6-subunit shelterin complex on ds-`TTAGGG` beads, which was also used to seed PAINT `PTN002683935`.

3. **ACD/TPP1 is NOT a subunit of the telomerase holoenzyme (`GO:0005697` → `REMOVE`), and canonical `Q96AP0` is NOT a telomerase inhibitor (`GO:0010521` → `REMOVE`)**:
   - `GO:0005697` (`telomerase holoenzyme complex`, `IEA` from `InterPro:IPR019437` `TPP1/Est3`) conflates fungal Est3 and ciliate TEBPβ/p50 (constitutive telomerase holoenzyme subunits) with shelterin subunits ACD/TPP1 and *S. pombe* Tpz1 (`PMID:35201900`: *"While TPP1-POT1 is part of the shelterin complex residing at mammalian telomeres, p50-TEB complex is integral Tetrahymena telomerase holoenzyme subunits"`).
   - `GO:0010521` (`telomerase inhibitor activity`, `IDA` from `PMID:25172512`) is unsupported by `PMID:25172512` (which assayed USP7 deubiquitination, localization, co-IPs, and half-life, with zero telomerase activity assays) and misstates the molecular function of canonical `Q96AP0` (`TPP1-S`), which directly binds TERT (`GO:0070182` `DNA polymerase binding`) via its OB-fold TEL patch (`E82/E83/K84/E85`, `R94`, `L126`, `E129`) and NOB motif (`P11-W12-I13`) to **recruit telomerase and stimulate repeat addition processivity** (`GO:0007004` `telomere maintenance via telomerase`, `GO:0032212` `positive regulation of telomere maintenance via telomerase`; `PMID:17237768`, `PMID:22863003`, `PMID:23103865`, `PMID:35201900`).
   - By contrast, the biological process `GO:0032211` (`negative regulation of telomere maintenance via telomerase`) IS retained (`ACCEPT`): ACD/TPP1 negatively regulates telomerase-mediated lengthening in *cis* as part of the shelterin length-homeostasis bridge by tethering POT1 (`PMID:15181449`, `PMID:15231715`, `PMID:15380063`) and recruiting the CST (`CTC1–STN1–TEN1`) terminator complex (`PMID:19648609`, `PMID:22763445`), and in male germ cells via the `TPP1-L` isoform (`PMID:31216472`). Note that on the `PMID:25172512` `IDA` rows for `GO:0032211`, `GO:0016233`, and `GO:0070203`, `PMID:25172512` only showed that ubiquitination-deficient TPP1 localized normally (`GO:0000781`) and bound telomerase/TIN2/POT1/CST (`GO:0070182`, `GO:0044877`), discussing capping and telomerase control from prior citations; we keep `GO:0032211` and `GO:0016233` consistent with the true gene-level claims while recording `source_defects` on mis-evidenced rows, and `REMOVE` `GO:0070203` (`regulation of establishment of protein localization to telomere`) where `PMID:25172512` explicitly found *"ubiquitination of human TPP1 is not required for its localization to telomeres"* (whereas ACD itself mediates `GO:0070200` `establishment of protein localization to telomere` for POT1 and TERT).

## 3. Evaluation of Affinage Deep Research (`ACD-deep-research-affinage.md`)

- **Metadata & Trust Gates**: `self_evaluation_pairwise: win`, `faith_pct: 85.7%`, `n_discoveries: 12`, `citation_count: 12` (all 12 PMIDs are real PubMed articles on shelterin `ACD`/`Acd`; zero fabricated IDs or `PMID:bio_...` tokens).
- **Strengths**:
  1. **Clean homonym separation**: Zero contamination from lysosomal sedolisin protease `TPP1` (`CLN2`, `O14773`).
  2. **Strong genetics & disease synthesis**: Accurately integrates PTOP–POT1–TIN2 bridging (`PMID:15181449`), TEL patch `ΔK170` bone marrow failure (`PMID:25205116`), mouse `acd` splice mutation and p53-dependent caudal/limb apoptosis (`PMID:15537664`, `PMID:19660449`), p53-independent hematopoietic stem cell depletion (`PMID:24316971`), post-transcriptional dosage compensation in heterozygous LoF carriers (`PMID:33446513`), and IPF-associated destabilizing variants (`PMID:41822348`).
  3. **Apt MF slim concepts**: Although restricted to `goslim_generic`, `GO:0060090` (`molecular adaptor activity`) accurately captures ACD's shelterin-bridging function.
- **Limitations & Failure Modes**:
  1. **Severe symbol/alias retrieval blind spot (`ACD` vs. `TPP1`)**: Because literature retrieval was anchored to `ACD`/`Acd` (or `PTOP`), Affinage missed almost the entire foundational biochemical and structural `TPP1` corpus whose titles/abstracts use `TPP1` without `ACD` — including discovery of POT1–TPP1 telomerase recruitment and repeat-addition processivity and the OB-fold structure (`PMID:17237767`, `PMID:17237768`, `PMID:20231318`), initial definition of the TEL patch (`PMID:22863003`, `PMID:23103865`), discovery of the NOB motif and `TPP1-L` vs. `TPP1-S` isoform specialization (`PMID:31216472`), Hoyeraal-Hreidarsson compound heterozygosity (`PMID:25233904`), CST termination coupling (`PMID:19648609`, `PMID:22763445`), and the cryo-EM structures of human telomerase bound to TPP1–POT1 (`PMID:35201900`, `PMID:35418675`).
  2. **Unflagged mixing of incompatible isoform residue coordinates (−86 offset)**: Keyed to `uniprot_accession: Q96AP0` (458-aa `TPP1-S`), Affinage reports `ΔK170` (`PMID:25205116`), `p.G223V` (`PMID:26345285`), and `p.Q320X` (`PMID:25505254`) in historical 544-aa `TPP1-L` numbering (`Lys84del`, `Gly137Val`, `Gln234Ter` on `Q96AP0`) right alongside `p.S295N` and `p.H358Q` (`PMID:41822348`) in native 458-aa `Q96AP0` numbering, without noting the 86-aa shift.
  3. **Coarse GO/Reactome grounding**: Slim-downcast localization `GO:0000228` (`nuclear chromosome`) loses `GO:0070187` (`shelterin complex`) and `GO:0140445` (`chromosome, telomeric repeat region`), while Reactome grounding (`R-HSA-73894` `DNA Repair`, `R-HSA-5357801` `Programmed Cell Death`) omits Reactome's canonical telomere pathways (`R-HSA-171306` *Packaging Of Telomere Ends*, `R-HSA-171319` *Telomere Extension By Telomerase*).
