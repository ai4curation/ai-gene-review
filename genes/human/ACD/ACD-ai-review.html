Gene Description

Core architectural adaptor and telomerase recruitment subunit of the human six-protein shelterin complex (TERF1, TERF2, TERF2IP/RAP1, TINF2/TIN2, POT1, and ACD, widely called TPP1 in telomere literature and distinct from lysosomal protease TPP1). Canonical somatic ACD/TPP1-S (458 amino acids, Q96AP0-3) contains an N-terminal hydrophobic PWI motif (Pro11-Trp12-Ile13; NOB), an oligonucleotide/oligosaccharide-binding (OB) fold bearing the surface-exposed acidic TEL-patch loop (Asp80-Trp81-Glu82-Glu83-Lys84-Glu85), a central POT1-binding domain, and a C-terminal TIN2-interacting helix. ACD does not contact telomeric repeat DNA directly; instead, it bridges single-stranded G-overhang-binding POT1 to TIN2 and duplex-bound TERF1/TERF2 to cap chromosome ends against ATR DNA damage signaling and non-homologous end joining. During S phase, the NOB PWI peptide and acidic TEL patch dock directly onto the TEN and IFD-TRAP domains of telomerase reverse transcriptase (TERT) to recruit telomerase to telomeres and stimulate processive TTAGGG repeat synthesis, whereas POT1 overhang sequestration, CST (CTC1-STN1-TEN1) recruitment, and an 86-residue N-terminally extended male germ-cell isoform (TPP1-L) restrain telomere over-elongation.

Functional Isoforms

Curated functional classes representing distinct biological activities. These may be splice variants, cleavage products, or other forms with different functions.

TPP1-S (canonical 458-aa somatic isoform) SPLICE VARIANT
ID: ACD_TPP1_S
UNIPROT ISOFORM: Q96AP0-3
Predominant 458-residue isoform expressed in human somatic and stem cells (initiating at Met87 of historical 544-aa numbering). Exposes an unobstructed N-terminal PWI NOB motif (Pro11-Trp12-Ile13) and OB-fold TEL patch (Glu82-Glu83-Lys84-Glu85) that both recruit telomerase to telomeres and activate repeat-addition processivity, while preserving full POT1/TIN2 shelterin bridging and telomere capping.
Isoform-specific terms: positive regulation of telomere maintenance via telomerase
TPP1-L (544-aa N-terminally extended germ-cell isoform) SPLICE VARIANT
ID: ACD_TPP1_L
Alternative 544-residue isoform transcribed from an upstream promoter and containing an 86-amino-acid arginine-rich N-terminal extension preceding Met1 of TPP1-S; constitutes the major isoform in differentiated male germ cells (testes/spermatocytes). Retains shelterin assembly, telomere capping, and TEL-patch-mediated telomerase recruitment, but fails to activate telomerase repeat synthesis, thereby restraining excessive telomere elongation in TERT-positive germ cells.
Isoform-specific terms: negative regulation of telomere maintenance via telomerase

Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ACD (TPP1) is a shelterin-complex scaffold protein that protects chromosome ends and licenses telomere elongation, and its loss couples telomere dysfunction to p53-dependent apoptosis during development and stem-cell maintenance [PMID:15181449, PMID:19660449]. It physically bridges TIN2 and POT1, with the POT1 interaction occurring through the POT1 C-terminus; disrupting this bridge prevents POT1 from localizing to telomeres and deregulates telomere length PMID:15181449. A distinct surface, the TEL patch, is required to recruit telomerase to telomeres: a single-amino-acid deletion (ΔK170) leaves telomeric localization intact but abolishes telomerase recruitment, causing telomere shortening and bone marrow failure in an affected family PMID:25205116. Loss of functional ACD in vivo produces chromosomal fusions both with and without internal telomeric sequence, indicating telomere-protective and broader genome-stability roles PMID:18185984. ACD-mediated telomere dysfunction acts upstream of p53: caudal truncation, limb hypoplasia, and vertebral anomalies in acd-mutant mice are rescued on a p53-null background, whereas hematopoietic stem cells are acutely depleted upon Acd inactivation in a p53-target-driven, cell-type-specific manner not rescued by p53 loss [PMID:19660449, PMID:24316971]. Cells maintain a single functional copy of ACD by post-transcriptionally restoring TPP1 protein to normal levels despite reduced mRNA PMID:33446513. In human disease, separation-of-function and loss-of-function ACD variants cause bone marrow failure and aplastic anemia PMID:25205116, and TPP1-destabilizing missense variants reduce DKC1 expression and shorten telomeres in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis PMID:41822348.

  1. Gene symbol vs. protein alias homonymy (ACD vs. TPP1):
  2. 2. Core Molecular Biology & GO Annotation Synthesis

  1. Shelterin molecular adaptor (GO:0060090 molecular adaptor activity, GO:0070187 shelterin complex):
  2. ACD/TPP1 is one of the six core subunits of mammalian shelterin (TRF1, TRF2, RAP1, TIN2, TPP1/ACD, POT1) [PMID:15181449, PMID:15231715, PMID:15380063, PMID:15383534, PMID:16880378]. Its central recruitment domain (aa 158–251) binds the C-terminal OB3–HJRL split domain of POT1, while its C-terminal TIN2-interacting helix binds TIN2 (TINF2), tethering POT1 to telomeric chromatin (GO:0070200 / GO:0070198) and stabilizing the TRF1–TIN2–TRF2 bridge (GO:0032202 telomere assembly, PMID:16880378).

  3. Through POT1 recruitment to the 3' single-stranded G-overhang, ACD/TPP1 prevents ATR checkpoint activation and represses aberrant repair/fusions (GO:0016233 telomere capping, GO:0031848 protection from non-homologous end joining at telomere; PMID:17632522, PMID:18185984, PMID:22101936).
