## 2026-10-17 — Hedgehog pathway and human phenotype follow-up

The existing GO:0007224 annotation is now ACCEPT and is represented in the single export core. This supersedes its earlier non-core classification: ligand delivery is DISP1's central contribution to Hedgehog signaling. The GO definition names events following Smoothened activation, while curated annotations also include ligand-side participants such as SHH. The valid DISP1 PAINT assertion, zebrafish soluble-ShhN rescue ([PMID:19948063](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2791760/), craniofacial Results), and human DISP1-dependent release ([PMID:34845226](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8630017/), Figures 1–4 and release Methods) jointly support the performed pathway step. No lateral positive-regulation replacement or NEW process is added. The core substrate now identifies human SHH as UniProtKB:Q15465; the cargo is its lipid-modified N-terminal signaling product, not the unprocessed precursor or C-terminal processing domain.

The biological summary now connects ligand release to embryonic brain and craniofacial patterning and to the human midline-malformation spectrum. [PMID:19184110](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2774849/), functional-analysis Methods, truncation Results and family descriptions, reports affected daughters with microform craniofacial findings and normal brain imaging, each inheriting a truncation from a clinically unaffected mother. Functional tests modeled the human changes in murine Disp1 in Drosophila cells; wild-type human DISP1 did not function in that assay. Its full original was not recovered, and the abstract-only flag remains unchanged; no uniform inheritance or severity is inferred.
