CC   -!- DISEASE: Holoprosencephaly 10 (HPE10) [MIM:621143]: A form of
CC       holoprosencephaly, a structural anomaly of the brain in which the
CC       developing forebrain fails to correctly separate into right and left
CC       hemispheres. It is a genetically and clinically heterogeneous disorder
CC       with a wide spectrum of severity, ranging from alobar holoprosencephaly
CC       with severe facial abnormalities, such as cyclopia and proboscis, to
CC       mild forms that include lobar or microform holoprosencephaly, without
CC       cerebral malformations and with mild craniofacial defects. HPE10
CC       inheritance pattern is autosomal recessive. Autosomal dominant
CC       inheritance with incomplete penetrance or oligogenic inheritance have
CC       been reported in some families. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19184110,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38529886}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
CC       affecting the gene represented in this entry.
