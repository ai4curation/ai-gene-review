Gene Description

GPKOW is a nuclear MOS2-family protein with G-patch and KOW domains. It binds RNA and DHX16/hPrp2 and participates in spliceosomal pre-mRNA processing. Pathogenic GPKOW variants cause X-linked developmental disorders involving growth, brain and eye abnormalities.
