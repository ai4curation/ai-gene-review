Gene Description

CHRM3 encodes the M3 muscarinic acetylcholine receptor, a seven-transmembrane receptor that predominantly activates Gq/11, phospholipase C and calcium signaling. It regulates smooth-muscle contraction and exocrine secretion, including intestinal and salivary responses. The receptor can occupy basolateral epithelial membranes, and TMEM147 can retain a pool in the endoplasmic reticulum. Its signaling repertoire is context dependent: human M3 associates with NALCN in heterologous cells, while a Src-dependent, G-protein-independent channel response was studied in a mouse pancreatic beta-cell line.
