# Affinage mechanistic annotation for BAGE4 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

BAGE4 is a cancer/testis antigen whose characterization in the available corpus is limited to expression profiling and immunogenicity, with no molecular mechanism of protein function established. Its transcript is expressed at comparable levels in cancer stem-like (side population) and bulk (main population) cells of lung, colon, and breast adenocarcinoma lines, distinguishing it from cancer/testis genes that are preferentially enriched in cancer stem cells [PMID:23574628]. As an antigen, BAGE4-derived peptides are immunogenic and can elicit immune responses in vivo when delivered as a Pam2Cys-conjugated self-adjuvanting vaccine or as a lipid nanoparticle formulation [PMID:40326329]. Beyond this expression and vaccine-antigen characterization, no biochemical activity, subcellular localization, interaction partner, or pathway role for the BAGE4 protein has been characterized in the available corpus.
