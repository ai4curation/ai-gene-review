CC   -!- FUNCTION: Substrate-recognition component of a BCR (BTB-CUL3-RBX1) E3
CC       ubiquitin ligase complex that terminates RNA polymerase II (Pol II)
CC       transcription in the promoter-proximal region of genes
CC       (PubMed:39504960, PubMed:39667934). The BCR(ARMC5) complex provides a
CC       quality checkpoint during transcription elongation by driving premature
CC       transcription termination of transcripts that are unfavorably
CC       configured for transcriptional elongation: the BCR(ARMC5) complex acts
CC       by mediating ubiquitination of Pol II subunit POLR2A phosphorylated at
CC       'Ser-5' of the C-terminal domain (CTD), leading to POLR2A degradation
CC       (PubMed:35687106, PubMed:38225631, PubMed:39504960, PubMed:39667934).
CC       The BCR(ARMC5) complex acts in parallel of the integrator complex and
CC       is specific for RNA Pol II originating from the promoter-proximal zone:
CC       it does not ubiquitinate elongation-stalled RNA Pol II

CC       fatty acid desaturation by mediating ubiquitination and degradation of
CC       SCAP-free SREBF1 and SREBF2 (PubMed:35862218). Involved in fetal
CC       development, T-cell function and adrenal gland growth homeostasis
