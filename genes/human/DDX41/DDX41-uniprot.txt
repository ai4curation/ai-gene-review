CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multifunctional protein that participates in many aspects of
CC       cellular RNA metabolism. Plays pivotal roles in innate immune sensing
CC       and hematopoietic homeostasis (PubMed:34473945). Recognizes foreign or
CC       self-nucleic acids generated during microbial infection, thereby
CC       initiating anti-pathogen responses (PubMed:23222971). Mechanistically,
CC       phosphorylation by BTK allows binding to dsDNA leading to interaction
CC       with STING1 (PubMed:25704810). Modulates the homeostasis of dsDNA
CC       through its ATP-dependent DNA-unwinding activity and ATP-independent
CC       strand-annealing activity (PubMed:35613581). In turn, induces STING1-
CC       mediated type I interferon and cytokine responses to DNA and DNA
CC       viruses (PubMed:35613581). Selectively modulates the transcription of
CC       certain immunity-associated genes by regulating their alternative
CC       splicing (PubMed:33650667). Binds to RNA (R)-loops, structures
CC       consisting of DNA/RNA hybrids and a displaced strand of DNA that occur
CC       during transcription, and prevents their accumulation, thereby
CC       maintaining genome stability (PubMed:36229594). Also participates in
CC       pre-mRNA splicing, translational regulation and snoRNA processing,
CC       which is essential for ribosome biogenesis (PubMed:36229594,
CC       PubMed:36780110). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q91VN6,
