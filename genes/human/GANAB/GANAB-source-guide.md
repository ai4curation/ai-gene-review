# GANAB source guide

GANAB catalyzes early ER N-glycan glucose trimming as the alpha subunit of glucosidase II. The review separates measured enzyme chemistry, curated ancestry, original target-table observations and client-protein phenotypes.

## Chemistry, complexes and products

[PMID:10929008](https://doi.org/10.1093/glycob/10.8.815) was read as an official abstract and selected original Results/Methods/captions from the author-hosted paper. Both human alpha forms work with beta; alpha1 D542 and alpha2 D564 are the corresponding catalytic positions in current Q14697-1 (944 aa) and Q14697-2 (966 aa). Both complexes cleave Glc1/Glc2 high-mannose substrates under the reported conditions; Glc3 was not cleaved in that assay. Single-human-subunit baculovirus preparations form human/insect heteroenzymes, so their activity does not establish an isolated human alpha-only enzyme. Retention-signal manipulation of beta affects secretion/ER retention. No whole-paper or original-image inspection is claimed.

The current official GO:0106407 definition describes Glc2-to-Glc1 hydrolysis. The original human experiments support this and the subsequent Glc1-to-Man9 reaction, represented in two chemistry cores. The second core and five existing MODIFY recommendations retain the conditional proposal “Glc1Man9GlcNAc2 oligosaccharide glucosidase activity”. The preferred resolution is to reconcile the existing term with its documented broader usage, rather than infer an overlooked activity from a null ontology search.

The actual production [FlyBase GO-CAM 680ad14200001222](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/6b0b93146b8e8883df427181bc3682d61e3f311a/gocams/680ad14200001222/680ad14200001222-src.yaml) has two GO:0106407 activities enabled by Gcs2alpha (FB:FBgn0027588): 680ad14200001292 directly provides input for 680ad14200001231. Their placement between glucosidase I and mannosidase is consistent with the two successive glucose removals established experimentally; the intermediate glycans are not explicit molecule nodes. The ER-lumen assertions separately use yeast P38138, so they must not be described as location evidence from Q14697. The human-accession index search alone missed Q14697 in these donor fields.

The local [Drosophila module](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/6b0b93146b8e8883df427181bc3682d61e3f311a/modules/dmel_n_glycan_processing.yaml) represents both inner-glucose removals under GO:0106407, while the [generated CAZy mapping](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/6b0b93146b8e8883df427181bc3682d61e3f311a/projects/GLYCOBIOLOGY/cazy2go.generated.terms.yaml) records EC:3.2.1.207 → GO:0106407 with ec2go provenance. No model, module or upstream mapping is changed here.

The alpha-D-glucose wording of GO:0090599 conflicts with the beta-D-glucose product in GO:0106407/RHEA and is treated as an unresolved representation issue, not a missing edge or disproved GANAB function.
