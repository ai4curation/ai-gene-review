# COG4 source-grounded review notes

## Biological synthesis

COG4 is an integral, noncatalytic subunit of the conserved oligomeric Golgi tethering complex. One protein-complex-scaffold core captures its multiple COG-subunit contacts and its coordination of Sly1/SCFD1 with syntaxin 5. The original human-subunit assembly analysis supports this refinement of the COG7-source binding assertion; the other positive COG pairs remain separate. The official scaffold definition and its three specific descendants were checked. Ubiquitin-ligase, MAP-kinase and centriole scaffolds do not describe this complex. No NEW molecular function or biological process is added. [PMID:15047703; PMID:19536132]

The original 2004 paper uses human constructs, including COG4 AI634312/SCOD1M4, expressed in rabbit reticulocyte lysate. Pairwise reciprocal co-immunoprecipitation supports contacts with COG1, COG2, COG5 and COG7. The lysate may contain bridging factors, so these experiments do not establish purified binary affinities. The assembly experiments support a structural role without making COG4 the sole bridge responsible for every subunit contact. [PMID:15047703]

The 2009 source uses human COG4 and STX5, recombinant rat rSly1, and endogenous HeLa material. Purified COG4 residues 1–231 bind GST-rSly1. The E53/71A mutant loses Sly1 association while preserving STX5 binding and incorporation into the COG complex; it does not restore retrograde trafficking as wild type does. The independent bounded consultation inspected original supplementary Figure S7: wild-type COG4 supports association of rSly1 with an STX5 mutant that cannot bind Sly1 normally, whereas the COG4 binding mutant does not. This is positive coordination of distinct partners. Autonomous SNARE zippering, membrane fusion and glycosyltransferase activity are not established. [PMID:19536132]

The human HEK293T COG4 knockout and replacement experiments connect complex function to Golgi architecture and glycosylation. Electron microscopy reveals cisternal defects even when some Golgi-marker distributions remain perinuclear. The toxin-transport assays cross several compartments and have receptor-binding limitations; they are interpreted together with the source-specific recruitment and retrograde-transport experiments. COG4 performs structural assembly and recruitment, rather than catalyzing the glycan reactions whose distribution is disturbed. [PMID:27066481; PMID:19536132]

Biallelic COG4 deficiency and the recurrent dominant Saul-Wilson variant have distinct clinical and trafficking contexts. No developmental or disease process is manufactured from these phenotypes. [PMID:19494034; PMID:19651599; PMID:30290151]
