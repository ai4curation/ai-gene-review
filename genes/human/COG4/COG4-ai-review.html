Gene Description

COG4 is a noncatalytic subunit of the eight-subunit conserved oligomeric Golgi tethering complex. It helps assemble the complex and coordinates the Sec1/Munc18 protein SCFD1/Sly1 with the Golgi SNARE syntaxin 5 through distinct binding surfaces. These interactions support retrograde trafficking and maintenance of Golgi architecture, enabling the correct distribution of glycosylation machinery. COG4 occupies both cytosolic and peripheral Golgi-membrane pools. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause a congenital disorder of glycosylation, whereas a recurrent dominant variant causes Saul-Wilson syndrome with a different trafficking disturbance.

Core Functions

Acts as an integral scaffold of the COG tethering complex, organizing COG-subunit contacts and coordinating Sly1 with syntaxin 5 to support Golgi retrograde trafficking and architecture. Human full-length and N-terminal constructs establish the recruitment mechanism; equivalent activity of the 337-residue Cog4S and N-terminally shortened 712-residue product has not been demonstrated.
