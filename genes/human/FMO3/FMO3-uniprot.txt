CC       drugs as well as dietary compounds (PubMed:10759686, PubMed:30381441,
CC       PubMed:32156684). Plays an important role in the metabolism of
CC       trimethylamine (TMA), via the production of trimethylamine N-oxide
CC       (TMAO) metabolite (PubMed:9776311). TMA is generated by the action of
CC       gut microbiota using dietary precursors such as choline, choline
CC       containing compounds, betaine or L-carnitine. By regulating TMAO
CC       concentration, FMO3 directly impacts both platelet responsiveness and
CC       rate of thrombus formation (PubMed:29981269).
