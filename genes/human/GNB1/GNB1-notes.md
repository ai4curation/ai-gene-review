## Function
- G protein beta-1 (WD40 beta-propeller); obligate heterodimer with a prenylated G-gamma. UniProt: "The beta
  and gamma chains are required for the GTPase activity, for replacement of GDP by GTP, and for G
  protein-effector interaction". Gbeta itself has no nucleotide-binding site; GTPase is the alpha subunit.
- Gbeta-gamma effectors: GIRK channels (X-ray GIRK2-betagamma complex; Gbeta Ser98/Trp99 contacts)
  [PMID:23739333]; PLC-beta3 recruitment to membrane [PMID:37991948 in GNAQ cache]; Ras-GRF activation
  [PMID:8717044]; Rhes/AGS1 bind Gbeta1 [PMID:19255495].
- Rod transducin beta subunit (GNB1:GNGT1) in phototransduction (Reactome R-HSA-2485182).
- Many cryo-EM GPCR-G protein structures use Gbeta1gamma2 (PMID:29925951, 34239069, 35501348 ...).
- De novo GNB1 variants cause a neurodevelopmental disorder (GNB1 encephalopathy) - general knowledge, not
  cited from cache.

## Localization
- Membrane anchoring via Gamma subunit geranylgeranyl: "the C-terminus of the Gγ subunit, which contains a
  covalent lipid molecule, a geranylgeranyl group, is pointed directly at the membrane layer as if to function as
  an anchor" [PMID:23739333].
- Conclusion: intracellular, cytoplasmic side of PM (inner leaflet, through Ggamma prenyl); rod disc membranes;
  not surface exposed. Reactome "cytosol" assignments describe the cytoplasmic face.
