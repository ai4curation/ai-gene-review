CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mediates the vitamin K-dependent carboxylation of glutamate
CC       residues to calcium-binding gamma-carboxyglutamate (Gla) residues with
CC       the concomitant epoxidation of vitamin K hydroquinone to vitamin K
CC       epoxide (PubMed:17073445, PubMed:39880952, PubMed:39880037). Couples
CC       epoxidation and carboxylation in the same active site (PubMed:39880952,
CC       PubMed:39880037). Catalyzes gamma-carboxylation of blood coagulation
CC       factors (F2, F7, F9 and F10) and anticoagulants such as PROC, which are
CC       essential for thrombosis(PubMed:17073445, PubMed:39880037). Catalyzes
CC       gamma-carboxylation of osteocalcin/BGLAP, which is essential for bone
CC       metabolism (PubMed:17073445, PubMed:39880952). Catalyzes gamma-
CC       carboxylation of matrix Gla protein (MGP) and other transmembrane
CC       gamma-Gla proteins such as PRRG2, which are essential for calcium
CC       homeostasis and haemostasis (PubMed:17073445, PubMed:39880037).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17073445, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39880037,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39880952}.
