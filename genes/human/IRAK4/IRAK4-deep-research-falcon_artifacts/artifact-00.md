| Category | IRAK4 property | Functional interpretation and evidence | Key citations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | Human IRAK4, a catalytically active serine/threonine kinase | Interleukin-1 receptor-associated kinase 4 is an early signal transducer in MyD88-dependent innate immunity and is distinct from IRAK1, IRAK2, and IRAK3/IRAK-M. | (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001) |
| Domain architecture | N-terminal death domain (DD) | Mediates homotypic death-domain interactions with MyD88 and other IRAKs, recruiting IRAK4 into the Myddosome. DD-only or kinase-inactive IRAK4 can enter initial MyD88 assemblies, showing that recruitment is structurally separable from catalysis. | (pqac-00000037, pqac-00000038, pqac-00000056) |
| Domain architecture | Proline/serine/threonine-rich linker (ProST region) | Connects the death and kinase domains and provides a regulatory, phosphorylation-rich region. Its exact IRAK4-specific mechanistic contribution remains less firmly resolved than those of the DD and kinase domain. | (pqac-00000036, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000041) |
| Domain architecture | C-terminal kinase domain | Binds ATP and transfers phosphate to serine/threonine residues. Myddosome-driven proximity promotes IRAK4 trans-autophosphorylation, after which the kinase domain supports IRAK1/2 recruitment or activation and productive inflammatory signaling. | (pqac-00000031, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000037) |
| Domain architecture | Absence of the extended C-terminal TRAF6-binding region found in IRAK1/2 | IRAK4 primarily recruits MyD88 and initiates IRAK activation; downstream IRAK1/2 proteins provide C-terminal TRAF6-binding functions. This distinguishes IRAK4 from other IRAK-family architectures. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000041, pqac-00000052) |
| Enzymatic reaction | ATP-dependent protein serine/threonine phosphorylation | IRAK4 catalyzes transfer of the ATP γ-phosphate to hydroxyl groups on protein serine or threonine residues. Physiological specificity is strongly imposed by proximity and orientation within the Myddosome rather than by a broad, fully defined linear sequence motif. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000051) |
| Substrate | IRAK4 itself: Thr345/Ser346-associated autophosphorylation | MyD88-dependent clustering brings IRAK4 kinase domains together for trans-autophosphorylation. This occurs after IRAK4 recruitment but before stable IRAK1 incorporation and acts as an energy-dependent Myddosome-maturation switch. | (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000032) |
| Substrate | IRAK1: Thr209 | The best-supported direct IRAK4 target in canonical and DNA-damage-associated signaling. Thr209 phosphorylation initiates IRAK1 conformational activation and enables later activation-loop phosphorylation and autophosphorylation. | (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000016) |
| Substrate | IRAK1: Thr387 | Commonly placed in the hierarchical IRAK4-to-IRAK1 activation sequence, but some accounts assign the later Thr387 event partly or wholly to activated IRAK1 autophosphorylation. It should therefore not be treated as an unequivocal direct IRAK4 site without assay-specific qualification. | (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000060) |
| Substrate | IRAK2 | IRAK4-dependent phosphorylation promotes IRAK2 activation, although the relevant human phosphorylation sites and whether all are direct remain less precisely established than for IRAK1 Thr209. | (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000013) |
| Canonical localization | Cytoplasmic face of activated plasma-membrane receptors | Following activation of IL-1-family receptors or cell-surface TLRs, MyD88 recruits IRAK4 into receptor-proximal Myddosomes; IRAK4 is not a transmembrane, extracellular, or constitutively nuclear protein. | (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000020) |
| Canonical localization | Endosomal receptor-associated Myddosomes | Endosomal MyD88-dependent TLRs, especially TLR7 and TLR9, also recruit IRAK4 into cytoplasmic signalosomes. IRAK4 kinase activity is particularly important for TLR7-associated inflammatory signaling. | (pqac-00000026, pqac-00000046, pqac-00000053) |
| Noncanonical localization | Cytoplasmic IRAK4–IRAK1 complex after DNA double-strand breaks | Ionizing radiation activates IRAK4 in the cytoplasm independently of MyD88 and canonical TLR/IL-1R engagement. IRAK4 phosphorylates IRAK1, after which activated IRAK1 translocates to the nucleus and supports cell survival. | (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000016) |
| Upstream pathway | IL-1-family receptors | IL-1β and related IL-1-family inputs recruit MyD88 through receptor TIR domains, initiating the MyD88 → IRAK4 → IRAK1/2 signaling hierarchy. | (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000026) |
| Upstream pathway | Most Toll-like receptors, but not TLR3 | Cell-surface and endosomal TLRs that use MyD88 recruit IRAK4. TLR3 signals through TRIF without the canonical MyD88–IRAK4 Myddosome, whereas TLR4 uses both MyD88 and TRIF branches. | (pqac-00000027, pqac-00000072) |
| Core signaling sequence | Receptor → MyD88 → IRAK4 → IRAK1/2 → TRAF6 | IRAK4 occupies the MyD88-proximal Myddosome layer, undergoes trans-autophosphorylation, and activates downstream IRAKs. IRAK1/2 then engage TRAF6 and promote nondegradative ubiquitin signaling. | (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000027, pqac-00000061) |
| NF-κB branch | TRAF6 → TAK1–TAB2/3 → IKK → IκB degradation → NF-κB | K63-linked ubiquitin scaffolds recruit TAK1 complexes; IKK activation causes IκB phosphorylation and degradation, permitting RelA/p65 and c-Rel nuclear entry and inflammatory-gene transcription. | (pqac-00000061, pqac-00000063, pqac-00000068) |
| MAPK branch | TAK1 → p38, JNK, and ERK cascades → AP-1 | IRAK4-initiated IRAK–TRAF6 signaling activates MAPK cascades that regulate AP-1-dependent transcription and cytokine-mRNA responses. The quantitative dependence on IRAK4 kinase activity varies by receptor and cell type. | (pqac-00000069, pqac-00000070, pqac-00000073) |
| IRF outputs | IRF5 and IRF7-associated responses | MyD88–IRAK signaling can activate IRF5 and, in plasmacytoid dendritic cells stimulated through TLR7/9, IRF7 and type-I-interferon production. These distal outputs are mediated substantially through downstream IRAK1 rather than direct IRAK4 phosphorylation of the IRFs. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000061, pqac-00000065) |
| Catalytic function | Phosphorylation-dependent maturation of the Myddosome | IRAK4 kinase activity is dispensable for initial MyD88–IRAK4 coassembly but required for autophosphorylation, efficient IRAK1 incorporation, downstream IRAK modification, and robust IL-1R or MyD88-only TLR signaling. | (pqac-00000031, pqac-00000033, pqac-00000058) |
| Scaffold function | Noncatalytic assembly and stabilization of signaling complexes | Kinase-inactive IRAK4 can still recruit into the Myddosome and bind IRAK1/2. Complete IRAK4 loss is consequently more disruptive than catalytic inhibition because it removes both enzymatic and structural functions. | (pqac-00000047, pqac-00000048, pqac-00000053) |
| TLR4-specific scaffold role | Integration of MyD88 and TRIF signals at TRAF6 | In TLR4 signaling, the IRAK4 scaffold supports TRIF-dependent TRAF6, NF-κB, and MAPK activation independently of IRAK4 catalysis, while the TRIF–TRAF3–IRF3 branch remains comparatively IRAK4 independent. | (pqac-00000045, pqac-00000049, pqac-00000064) |
| Functional evidence | Kinase-dead mutants, knockout cells, reconstitution, and selective inhibition | Kinase-inactive IRAK4 or PF-06650833 suppresses IRAK phosphorylation, NF-κB signaling, and cytokine production—especially after TLR7 stimulation—while IRAK4-null cells show broader defects that expose the protein’s additional scaffold role. | (pqac-00000053, pqac-00000055, pqac-00000056) |
| Functional summary | Proximity-activated kinase and receptor-proximal scaffold | IRAK4 converts ligand-induced MyD88 oligomerization into a phosphorylation- and ubiquitin-dependent inflammatory relay. Its principal precise role is to assemble and mature the Myddosome and activate IRAK1/2, not to directly phosphorylate distal NF-κB or MAPK components. | (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000069) |
