## Molecular activity and physiological context

CD70 is a cell-surface TNF-family ligand for CD27. Expression-cloning studies identify the ligand and its T-cell costimulatory action [PMID:8387892; PMID:8120384]. Human deficiency, receptor binding and rescue studies show that CD70 on presenting B cells supports CD27-dependent T-cell activation and expansion [PMID:28011863; PMID:28011864]. This activity is summarized as receptor ligand activity at the plasma membrane in the CD27 signaling pathway. The inspected production GO-CAM `666b894f00000352` already represents P32970 in that role, separate from the CD27 receptor and intracellular effector nodes. No intracellular kinase/adaptor activity is assigned to the ligand.

The broad InterPro TNF-signaling annotation is refined to CD27 signaling: the current GO definitions distinguish the TNF ligand from CD70/CD27. T-cell and broad lymphocyte proliferation inferences are refined to positive regulation of T-cell proliferation. This describes the ligand's costimulatory role; T-cell division occurs in the responding cell. Source labels and identifiers are retained under `term`, with refinements confined to `review`.

The two seeded products P32970-1 and P32970-2 are preserved exactly. The VSP_056416 sequence note alone does not establish an isoform-specific functional distinction, and none is invented.

## Structural and reverse-signaling follow-up — 2026-10-09

The full cached human CD27–CD70 structure paper was read for its binding/structural Results, activation experiments and Methods, with the concentration-dependent Discussion. It directly supports receptor binding and ligand activity: soluble ectodomain preparations bind in SPR, and the crystal contains three CD27 molecules around a CD70 homotrimer. The tested human fragments were CD70 Q45–P193 and CD27 A23–S127; this is not a structure of two complete opposing cell membranes. The structural source now supports the original receptor-binding and ligand-activity decisions and the same core function. No oligomerization annotation or additional core is added. [PMID:34419446](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34419446/).

The cell experiments distinguish binding from productive costimulation. Plate-bound CD70 enhanced anti-CD3-dependent primary T-cell IFN-gamma production and Jurkat AP-1 activity. Soluble constructs were ineffective in the tested reporter conditions, while the Discussion recognizes earlier activity at higher soluble concentrations. Thus the evidence favors effective presentation and higher-order clustering, without declaring every soluble form inactive. Figure legends and reported tables were read; raw structural data and figure pixels were not reanalysed. [PMID:34419446](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34419446/).
