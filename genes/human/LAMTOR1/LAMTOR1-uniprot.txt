CC   -!- FUNCTION: Key component of the Ragulator complex, a multiprotein
CC       complex involved in amino acid sensing and activation of mTORC1, a
CC       signaling complex promoting cell growth in response to growth factors,
CC       energy levels, and amino acids (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:22980980,
CC       PubMed:29158492). Activated by amino acids through a mechanism
CC       involving the lysosomal V-ATPase, the Ragulator plays a dual role for
CC       the small GTPases Rag (RagA/RRAGA, RagB/RRAGB, RagC/RRAGC and/or
CC       RagD/RRAGD): it (1) acts as a guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF),
CC       activating the small GTPases Rag and (2) mediates recruitment of Rag
CC       GTPases to the lysosome membrane (PubMed:22053050, PubMed:22980980,
CC       PubMed:28935770, PubMed:29158492, PubMed:30181260, PubMed:31001086,
CC       PubMed:32686708, PubMed:36476874). Activated Ragulator and Rag GTPases
CC       function as a scaffold recruiting mTORC1 to lysosomes where it is in
CC       turn activated (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:22980980, PubMed:29158492).
CC       LAMTOR1 is directly responsible for anchoring the Ragulator complex to
CC       the lysosomal membrane (PubMed:31001086, PubMed:32686708). LAMTOR1
CC       wraps around the other subunits of the Ragulator complex to hold them
CC       in place and interacts with the Rag GTPases, thereby playing a key role
CC       in the recruitment of the mTORC1 complex to lysosomes (PubMed:28935770,
CC       PubMed:29107538, PubMed:29123114, PubMed:29285400). Also involved in
CC       the control of embryonic stem cells differentiation via non-canonical
CC       RagC/RRAGC and RagD/RRAGD activation: together with FLCN, it is
CC       necessary to recruit and activate RagC/RRAGC and RagD/RRAGD at the
CC       lysosomes, and to induce exit of embryonic stem cells from pluripotency
CC       via non-canonical, mTOR-independent TFE3 inactivation (By similarity).
CC       Also required for late endosomes/lysosomes biogenesis it may regulate
CC       both the recycling of receptors through endosomes and the MAPK
CC       signaling pathway through recruitment of some of its components to late
CC       endosomes (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:22980980). May be involved in
CC       cholesterol homeostasis regulating LDL uptake and cholesterol release
CC       from late endosomes/lysosomes (PubMed:20544018). May also play a role
CC       in RHOA activation (PubMed:19654316). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9CQ22,
