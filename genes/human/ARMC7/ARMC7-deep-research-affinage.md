# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ARMC7 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ARMC7 is a component of the activated human minor spliceosome, where it functions in recognition of the U6atac snRNA cap [PMID:33509932]. ARMC7 forms a complex with RBM48 that directly binds the γ-monomethyl phosphate cap at the 5' end of U6atac snRNA, an interaction that stabilizes the catalytic center of the activated minor spliceosome [PMID:33509932].
