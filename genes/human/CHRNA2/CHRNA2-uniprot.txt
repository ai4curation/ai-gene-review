CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of neuronal acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) that
CC       function as pentameric, ligand-gated cation channels with high calcium
CC       permeability among other activities. nAChRs are excitatory
CC       neurotrasnmitter receptors formed by a collection of nAChR subunits
CC       known to mediate synaptic transmission in the nervous system and the
CC       neuromuscular junction. Each nAchR subunit confers differential
CC       attributes to channel properties, including activation, deactivation
CC       and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, cation permeability, and
CC       binding to allosteric modulators (PubMed:18723036). CHRNA2 forms
CC       heteropentameric neuronal acetylcholine receptors with CHRNB2 and
CC       CHRNB4 and plays a role in nicotine dependence (PubMed:24467848,
CC       PubMed:27493220). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24467848,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27493220, ECO:0000303|PubMed:18723036}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Neuronal AChR is composed of two different types of subunits:
CC       alpha and non-alpha (beta). CHRNA2/alpha-2 subunit can be combined to
CC       CHRNB2/beta-2 or CHRNB4/beta-4 to give rise to functional receptors.
CC       Both CHRNA2:CHRNB2 and CHRNA2:CHRNB4 nAChR complexes are
CC       heteropentamers with two subtypes: LS (low agonist sensitivity) with a
CC       (CHRNA2)3:(CHRNB2/4)2 and HS (high agonist sensitivity) with a
CC       (CHRNA2)2:(CHRNB2/4)3 stoichiometries; the subtypes differ in their
CC       subunit binding interfaces which are involved in ligand binding.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24467848, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27493220}.
