# GARS1 biological and source notes

## 2026-11-04 — Glycine charging, products and cellular pools

GARS1 supplies glycyl-tRNA for cytosolic and mitochondrial translation. Human cDNA expression and aminoacylation experiments establish the catalytic activity; human GlyRS structures and tRNA complexes identify the substrate-recognition mechanism. The enzyme forms a homodimer. The mixed-species coimmunoprecipitation in mouse N2a cells preserves a human–mouse interaction context and is not itself relabeled as a human homodimer assay; independent solution/structural evidence supplies that conclusion (PMID:7753621; PMID:7961834; PMID:7962006; PMID:17545306; PMID:24898252).

Official sequences establish two alternative-initiation products: the 739-residue precursor and the 685-residue product beginning at precursor residue 55. These are not alternative-splicing products. The source labels mRNA1 and mRNA2 describe transcript leaders rather than protein isoform numbers. Leader-dependent translation and localization contribute to the two cellular pools, while the longer product is not necessarily restricted to mitochondria. ER-ribosome proximity in the initiation study does not establish ER-lumen or integral-membrane localization. The predicted 1–36 transit feature and Reactome's 37–739 coordinates are distinct from the 55–739 short product. The structural source's FL 56–739 and SF 114–739 constructs are separately retained, including their engineered mutant context (PMID:26327585; PMID:24898252; UniProtKB:P41250).

The actual PTHR10745 tree places the 739-residue human sequence at AN21/PTN002473369. Glycyl-tRNA ligase activity and cytoplasmic localization trace to AN0/PTN000078673, while the mitochondrial assertions trace to AN3/PTN000078675. Literal IBD evidence and donor sets agree, and no negative assertion occurs on the human lineage. Human self-evidence is legitimate experimental grounding, not circularity. ## Secondary chemistry and unresolved claims

Human GlyRS directly condenses ATP to Ap4A in biochemical assays, with tRNA inhibition. This is measured catalytic capacity; its physiological contribution to cellular Ap4A homeostasis remains unsettled, so it is retained outside the single aminoacylation core. The separate asymmetric Ap4A-hydrolase annotation is unresolved: inspected main Experimental Procedures and Discussion plus selected supplemental Methods/captions did not reveal a human GARS1 Ap4A-to-ATP-plus-AMP assay. A separately added inorganic pyrophosphatase and an E. coli hydrolase discussed in the article do not establish that activity for human GARS1. This is a source-specific uncertainty, not a claim that GARS1 can never hydrolyze Ap4A (PMID:19710017).

The mouse secretory-granule donor presents a different identity problem. The original A61 antibody work detected a 23-kDa protein in dense granules with stimulus-dependent release. Current RefSeq retains its publication and Sgrp23 alias, and the deposited 342-nt fragment maps strongly in reverse-complement orientation within current Gars1 coding sequence. Nevertheless, the deposited plus-strand 114-residue translation is not the current Gars1 peptide, so the identity of the purified granule protein remains unresolved. Neither a wrong-gene assignment nor a clone error or biological antisense transcript is established. The human Ensembl IEA is therefore UNDECIDED (PMID:8939962).

## Neuronal, extracellular and interaction evidence

Selected original human spinal-cord and sural-nerve staining supports an axonal pool. The reported intracellular granules are not automatically secretory granules, and staining does not directly demonstrate local translation or axonal transport. Separate experiments support secretion and an extracellular-vesicle-enriched pool in the reported cell systems. These locations are retained outside the catalytic core and do not imply integral vesicle topology. The disease-mutant Nrp1 interaction is neomorphic and is not assigned as a normal receptor activity of GARS1 (PMID:17035524; PMID:26503042). The official corrigendum adds funding acknowledgements only, with no biological correction (PMID:26789244).

Six positive human interaction contexts in the neurodegenerative-disease interactome retain their exact partners, isoforms and assay records. Their bare protein-binding terms are removed as uninformative, without denying the measured associations or transferring partner functions. The two human–mouse GlyRS interaction rows are handled with the same distinction. No new process annotation is manufactured from necessity, disease phenotype or interacting-partner function (PMID:32814053; PMID:17545306).
