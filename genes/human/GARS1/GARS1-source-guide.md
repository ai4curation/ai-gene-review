## Secondary activity and location limits

[19710017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19710017/) directly supports ATP condensation to Ap4A. Original main Experimental Procedures/Discussion and selected supplemental Methods/captions were inspected for the distinct hydrolysis claim; no human Ap4A-to-ATP-plus-AMP assay was found there. Neither the separate phosphatase reagent nor a bacterial hydrolase discussed in the text resolves that claim. Measured synthesis remains non-core. The hydrolysis IDA is marked over-annotated because the source demonstrates a different reaction; whether human GARS1 can also hydrolyze Ap4A remains a biological question.

Selected original [17035524](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6674701/) Methods/Results, Figure 3 caption and Table 2 text support endogenous human neuronal and axonal localization. Images were not visually inspected. Granules are not identified as secretory granules by that staining alone. [26503042](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature15510) supports extracellular release and a vesicle-enriched pool in the selected extended-data captions; the full purification Methods and images were not inspected. The Nrp1-binding mechanism applies to neuropathy mutants. Its [official correction](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature16499) adds funding acknowledgements only.

The human secretory-granule transfer is traced to mouse [8939962](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8939962/). Original selected Methods/Results and GenBank U64446.1 were inspected. Current RefSeq records preserve historical linkage and the nucleotide fragment maps strongly in reverse-complement orientation to the current coding sequence, but the submitted translation does not establish the identity of the purified 23-kDa protein. The evidence is retained without asserting wrong gene, clone error or an antisense transcript. The [reproducible A61 analysis](GARS1-bioinformatics/A61/RESULTS.md) includes the three official input records, offline scripts, all optimal alignments and independent literal checks. Stimulated release and the five-residue Lys-C observation are from the [selected original article](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/14267156_Purification_of_storage_granule_protein-23_A_novel_protein_identified_by_phage_display_technology_and_interaction_with_type_I_plasminogen_activator_inhibitor) Methods/Results and Figure 2/3 captions; the normal publication cache contains only the abstract.
