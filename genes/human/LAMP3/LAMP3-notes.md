# LAMP3 notes

Deep research (falcon / perplexity-lite) failed; notes from cached publications and UniProt Q9UQV4.

- DC-LAMP: lysosomal distribution in DC; in MIIC before MHC II reaches surface, then perinuclear lysosomes [PMID:9768752 "Confocal microscopy confirmed the lysosomal distribution of the protein."].
- Lung-specific gene TSC403 homologous to LAMPs [PMID:9721848].
- Influenza post-entry host factor [PMID:21810281 "plays an important role in viral post-entry steps"] -> symbiont entry IMP marked over-annotated.
- ATF4/UPR induced, promotes autophagic flux [PMID:25681212].
- Cell surface pool in THP-1 relocates to Salmonella vacuoles [PMID:27329040 "LAMP-3, like LAMP-2, shifts its localization from the cell surface to alongside Salmonella."].
- IBA "establishment of protein localization to organelle" from LAMP2 CMA -> over-annotation.

Localization conclusion: lysosomal/late endosomal (MIIC) membrane, lamellar body membrane in type II pneumocytes; minor
cell-surface pool (luminal domain exposed when at surface). Mostly intracellular.
