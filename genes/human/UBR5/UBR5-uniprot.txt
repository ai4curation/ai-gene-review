CC   -!- FUNCTION: E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase involved in different protein
CC       quality control pathways in the cytoplasm and nucleus (PubMed:29033132,
CC       PubMed:33208877, PubMed:37478846, PubMed:37478862). Mainly acts as a
CC       ubiquitin chain elongator that extends pre-ubiquitinated substrates
CC       (PubMed:29033132, PubMed:37409633). Component of the N-end rule
CC       pathway: ubiquitinates proteins bearing specific N-terminal residues
CC       that are destabilizing according to the N-end rule, leading to their
CC       degradation (By similarity). Recognizes type-1 N-degrons, containing
CC       positively charged amino acids (Arg, Lys and His) (By similarity).
CC       Together with UBR4, part of a cytoplasm protein quality control pathway
CC       that prevents protein aggregation by catalyzing assembly of heterotypic
CC       'Lys-11'-/'Lys-48'-linked branched ubiquitin chains on aggregated
CC       proteins, leading to substrate recognition by the segregase p97/VCP and
CC       degradation by the proteasome: UBR5 is probably branching multiple
CC       'Lys-48'-linked chains of substrates initially modified with mixed
CC       conjugates by UBR4 (PubMed:29033132). Together with ITCH, catalyzes
CC       'Lys-48'-/'Lys-63'-branched ubiquitination of TXNIP, leading to its
CC       degradation: UBR5 mediates branching of 'Lys-48'-linked chains of
CC       substrates initially modified with 'Lys-63'-linked conjugates by ITCH
CC       (PubMed:29378950). Catalytic component of a nuclear protein quality
CC       control pathway that mediates ubiquitination and degradation of
CC       unpaired transcription factors (i.e. transcription factors that are not
CC       assembled into functional multiprotein complexes): specifically
CC       recognizes and binds degrons that are not accessible when transcription
CC       regulators are associated with their coactivators (PubMed:37478846,
CC       PubMed:37478862). Ubiquitinates various unpaired transcription
CC       regulators (MYC, SUPT4H1, SUPT5H, CDC20 and MCRS1), as well as ligand-
CC       bound nuclear receptors (ESR1, NR1H3, NR3C1, PGR, RARA, RXRA and VDR)
CC       that are not associated with their nuclear receptor coactivators
CC       (NCOAs) (PubMed:33208877, PubMed:37478846, PubMed:37478862). Involved
CC       in maturation and/or transcriptional regulation of mRNA by mediating
CC       polyubiquitination and activation of CDK9 (PubMed:21127351). Also acts
CC       as a regulator of DNA damage response by acting as a suppressor of
CC       RNF168, an E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase that promotes accumulation of
CC       'Lys-63'-linked histone H2A and H2AX at DNA damage sites, thereby
CC       acting as a guard against excessive spreading of ubiquitinated
CC       chromatin at damaged chromosomes (PubMed:22884692). Regulates DNA
CC       topoisomerase II binding protein (TopBP1) in the DNA damage response
CC       (PubMed:11714696). Ubiquitinates acetylated PCK1 (PubMed:21726808).
CC       Acts as a positive regulator of the canonical Wnt signaling pathway by
CC       mediating (1) ubiquitination and stabilization of CTNNB1, and (2) 'Lys-
CC       48'-linked ubiquitination and degradation of TLE3 (PubMed:21118991,
CC       PubMed:28689657). Promotes disassembly of the mitotic checkpoint
CC       complex (MCC) from the APC/C complex by catalyzing ubiquitination of
CC       BUB1B, BUB3 and CDC20 (PubMed:35217622). Plays an essential role in
CC       extraembryonic development (By similarity). Required for the
CC       maintenance of skeletal tissue homeostasis by acting as an inhibitor of
CC       hedgehog (HH) signaling (By similarity). Mediates the ubiquitin-
CC       dependent proteasomal degradation of unphosphorylated retinoblastoma
CC       protein RB1 during the G1 phase of the cell cycle, progressively
CC       reducing RB1 concentration in G1 to promote passage through the G1-S
CC       transition (PubMed:39441926). Mediates also the 'Lys-48'-linked
CC       ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of the chromatin regulator
CC       ATAD2, thereby antagonistically regulating the expression of cell
CC       cycle-related genes including PLK1, CDK1, CCNB2, and CCNA2
CC       (PubMed:41978312). Acts cooperatively with TRIP12 to assemble 'Lys-
CC       29'/'Lys-48'-branched ubiquitin chains that promote proteasomal
CC       degradation of substrates that are otherwise protected by
CC       deubiquitination by the activity of OTUD5, which readily cleaves 'Lys-
CC       48' but not 'Lys-29' linkages (PubMed:40128189). Acts as a guardian of
CC       genome integrity by specifically recognizing and polyubiquitinating the
CC       APOBEC3 cytidine deaminases APOBEC3B and APOBEC3H-I in their non-RNA-
CC       bound state, targeting them for proteasomal degradation and thereby
CC       limiting APOBEC3-driven hypermutation of genomic DNA (PubMed:41554709).

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21118991, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21127351,
