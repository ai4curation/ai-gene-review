# UBR5 notes

## GO-CAM 6606056e00000678

The GO-CAM model `6606056e00000678` represents UBR5 as a chromatin ubiquitin protein ligase acting on ligand-bound ESR1 and as part of `GO:0071630` nuclear protein quality control by the ubiquitin-proteasome system.

PMID:37478846 supports UBR5-dependent turnover of an estrogen receptor reporter and estradiol-dependent direct binding of recombinant UBR5 to ER. It does not directly assay ER ubiquitination, and its chromatin ChIP-seq support is for UBR5 at RARA-bound and GR-bound sites rather than ER-bound sites. The ESR1 GO-CAM activity is therefore a plausible but inferred extension of the paper's stronger biochemical and chromatin evidence.

## PAINT

`just paint-losses human UBR5` reported no IRD or IKR loss on the `UniProtKB:O95071` lineage.

## PMID:29033132

The locally cached PubMed abstract for PMID:29033132 establishes the K11/K48-linked chain pathway but does not name UBR5. The open full text supports the UBR5-specific annotations: Figure 5A states "UBR4 and UBR5 assemble most K11/K48-linked chains during quality control.", and the Results section reports that UBR4/UBR5 co-depletion eliminated most K11/K48-linked chains during proteotoxic stress and that UBR5 complexes synthesized K11/K48-linked chains in vitro.

## Human Protein Atlas

Human Protein Atlas entry `ENSG00000104517-UBR5` reports UBR5 as mainly localized to the nucleoplasm with additional cytosol staining: "Mainly localized to the nucleoplasm. In addition localized to the cytosol." The same page lists HPA053688 staining in MCF-7, PC-3, and U2OS cells with nucleoplasm in all three and cytosol in PC-3 and U2OS.
