CC   -!- DISEASE: Osteopetrosis, autosomal recessive 9 (OPTB9) [MIM:620366]: A
CC       form of osteopetrosis, a rare genetic disease characterized by
CC       abnormally dense bone, due to defective resorption of immature bone.
CC       Osteopetrosis occurs in two forms: a severe autosomal recessive form
CC       occurring in utero, infancy, or childhood, and a benign autosomal
CC       dominant form occurring in adolescence or adulthood. Recessive
CC       osteopetrosis commonly manifests in early infancy with macrocephaly,
CC       feeding difficulties, evolving blindness and deafness, bone marrow
CC       failure, severe anemia, and hepatosplenomegaly. Deafness and blindness
CC       are generally thought to represent effects of pressure on nerves. OPTB9
CC       is characterized by increased bone density and bone fragility, as well
CC       as renal failure. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34668226}. Note=The disease may
