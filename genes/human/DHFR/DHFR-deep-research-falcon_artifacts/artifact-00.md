| Location | Direct DHFR chemistry/function | Evidence/qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Cytosol | Canonical chromosome-5 DHFR uses NADPH to reduce 7,8-dihydrofolate (DHF) to tetrahydrofolate (THF), replenishing reduced folate for one-carbon metabolism. It can also reduce synthetic dietary folic acid (FA) first to DHF and then to THF; FA is an ancillary, nonphysiological substrate rather than the principal endogenous substrate. | Human-liver assays measured a DHF Km of approximately 0.05 µM. In the highest-activity liver tested, THF formation from FA was 0.02 versus 26.1 nmol/min/g from DHF—about 1,300-fold slower—despite an FA Km of approximately 0.5 µM (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000045). DHFR inhibition consequently limits reduced-folate pools and downstream nucleotide synthesis (pqac-00000035). |
| Nucleus | At the G1/S boundary and after DNA damage, DHFR joins SHMT1, TYMS and MTHFD1 in the nuclear de novo thymidylate-synthesis complex. TYMS converts dUMP and 5,10-methylene-THF to dTMP and DHF; DHFR then reduces DHF back to THF for continued cycling near DNA-replication sites. | Isolated S-phase nuclei synthesize dTMP from serine or formate. SUMO-dependent nuclear import, association with PCNA and nuclear lamins, and SHMT1-mediated scaffolding support a localized replication-coupled pathway rather than passive diffusion of cytosolic dTMP (pqac-00000031, pqac-00000043). |
| Mitochondria | Canonical DHFR can supply mitochondrial reductase activity that regenerates THF for local folate-dependent metabolism, including de novo dTMP synthesis. | In purified HEK293 mitochondrial fractions, a 2024 study measured DHFR activity of 14.88 nmol/min/mg and identified five peptides unique to canonical DHFR (~30% sequence coverage), but no DHFR2-specific peptide (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000044). Earlier recombinant/knockdown work attributed mitochondrial activity to the distinct chromosome-3 paralog DHFR2/DHFRL1 (pqac-00000016); newer targeted proteomics generally did not detect endogenous DHFR2 protein in adult cells or tissues, although one DHFR2-specific peptide was reported in embryonic heart, so rare developmental expression remains possible (pqac-00000027). These findings establish canonical DHFR in the tested HEK293 mitochondria but should not be generalized uncritically to every human tissue or developmental stage. |


*Table: Direct chemistry and localization evidence for canonical human chromosome-5 DHFR (P00374), separated from the DHFR2/DHFRL1 paralog. The table highlights strong cytosolic and nuclear evidence and the revised, appropriately qualified mitochondrial attribution.*
