CC   -!- FUNCTION: Member of the eIF2 complex that functions in the early steps
CC       of protein synthesis by forming a ternary complex with GTP and
CC       initiator tRNA (PubMed:31836389). This complex binds to a 40S ribosomal
CC       subunit, followed by mRNA binding to form the 43S pre-initiation
CC       complex (43S PIC) (By similarity). Junction of the 60S ribosomal
CC       subunit to form the 80S initiation complex is preceded by hydrolysis of
CC       the GTP bound to eIF2 and release of an eIF2-GDP binary complex (By
CC       similarity). In order for eIF2 to recycle and catalyze another round of
CC       initiation, the GDP bound to eIF2 must exchange with GTP by way of a
CC       reaction catalyzed by eIF-2B (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P05198, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31836389}.
