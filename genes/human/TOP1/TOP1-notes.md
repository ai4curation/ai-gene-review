# TOP1 notes

## 2026-11-27 manual family review

The automated deep-research provider was unavailable in this workspace, so this
review used the fetched GOA rows, UniProt, PAINT, and the cached primary
publications.

- Human TOP1 is the canonical nuclear type IB DNA topoisomerase. Its conserved
  core activity is ATP-independent strand breakage/rejoining that relaxes DNA
  torsion generated by replication and transcription
  [UniProtKB:P11387, "Releases the supercoiling and torsional tension of DNA
  introduced during the DNA replication and transcription by transiently
  cleaving and rejoining one strand of the DNA duplex"].
- PTHR10290 places `GO:0003917`, `GO:0006265`, `GO:0006260`, `GO:0007059`, and
  the ancestral nucleolus call on node `PTN000033230`; `just paint-losses human
  TOP1` reported no IRD/IKR loss on the TOP1 lineage.
- TOP1 is a nuclear/nucleolar enzyme and can remain bound to mitotic
  chromosomes [PMID:9049244, "In mitosis, topoisomerase IIbeta diffused
  completely into the cytosol, whereas topoisomerases I and IIalpha remained
  chromosome bound."].
- The BMAL1/circadian rows were retained as non-core regulatory contexts:
  PMID:22904072 supports TOP1-dependent Bmal1 promoter topology and circadian
  period changes, but not a sequence-specific RNA polymerase II
  cis-regulatory-region binding function.
- The two RNA-binding HDA rows come from broad mRNA interactome-capture screens
  [PMID:22658674; PMID:22681889]. They are useful discovery datasets, but they
  do not establish a direct RNA-binding mechanism for TOP1 separate from
  cotranscriptional chromatin engagement.
- Bare `GO:0005515` protein-binding rows were removed under the protein-binding
  policy while the more specific `GO:0019904` SF2/ASF RS-domain row was retained
  as part of TOP1's experimentally reported secondary kinase interaction
  [PMID:9611241, "at least two distinct domains of Topo I are necessary for
  kinase activity"].
- UniProt records an endothelial F3 pre-mRNA alternative-splicing function for
  the TOP1/CLK SR-protein kinase arm from PMID:19168442. The review used that
  context to explain the non-core SF2/ASF domain-binding row, but did not add a
  direct splicing-process annotation from an abstract-only cache that leaves the
  exact participation of TOP1 in TF splicing unresolved.
