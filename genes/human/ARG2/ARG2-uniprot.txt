CC   -!- FUNCTION: May play a role in the regulation of extra-urea cycle
CC       arginine metabolism and also in down-regulation of nitric oxide
CC       synthesis. Extrahepatic arginase functions to regulate L-arginine
CC       bioavailability to nitric oxid synthase (NOS). Arginine metabolism is a
CC       critical regulator of innate and adaptive immune responses. Seems to be
CC       involved in negative regulation of the survival capacity of activated
CC       CD4(+) and CD8(+) T cells (PubMed:27745970). May suppress inflammation-
CC       related signaling in asthmatic airway epithelium (PubMed:27214549). May
CC       contribute to the immune evasion of H.pylori by restricting M1
CC       macrophage activation and polyamine metabolism (By similarity). In
CC       fetal dendritic cells may play a role in promoting immune suppression
CC       and T cell TNF production during gestation (PubMed:28614294). Regulates
CC       RPS6KB1 signaling, which promotes endothelial cell senescence and
CC       inflammation and implicates NOS3/eNOS dysfunction (PubMed:22928666).
CC       Can inhibit endothelial autophagy independently of its enzymatic
CC       activity implicating mTORC2 signaling (PubMed:25484082). Involved in
CC       vascular smooth muscle cell senescence and apoptosis independently of
CC       its enzymatic activity (PubMed:23832324). Since NOS is found in the
CC       penile corpus cavernosum smooth muscle, the clitoral corpus cavernosum
CC       and the vagina, arginase-2 plays a role in both male and female sexual
CC       arousal (PubMed:12859189). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O08691,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12859189, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22928666,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23832324, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25484082,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27214549, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27745970}.
