# SLC25A34 notes

Reviewed as the reference protein of the PANTHER family PTHR45928 (whose
placement of the yeast Oac1 carriers is disputed; see that family review).
No provider deep research was run for this gene; these notes record the
literature read.

- The only functional study is the yeast Oac1 paper. The human protein could not
  be reconstituted: [PMID:18682385 "The SLC25A34 protein was not expressed in E. coli ."]
- Heterologous rescue of the yeast alpha-IPM export defect:
  [PMID:18682385 "Our finding that the SLC25A34 and SLC25A35 proteins partially rescued the phenotype of the Δ OAC1 Δ LEU4 double mutant in the absence of leucine indicates their ability to transport α-IPM."]
- Physiological substrate unknown:
  [PMID:18682385 "it is likely that α-IPM is not the physiological substrate of SLC25A34 and SLC25A35, which remains to be determined."]
- The single GOA row (mitochondrial inner membrane, UniProt SubCell by similarity)
  is accepted because the carrier fold (three SOLCAR repeats, six TM helices) and
  the functional replacement of an inner-membrane carrier agree.
- A generic transmembrane transporter activity is proposed as NEW (IC); the
  isopropylmalate-specific term is deliberately not proposed for a human protein.
