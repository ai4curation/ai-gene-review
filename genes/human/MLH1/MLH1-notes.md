# MLH1 PTHR10073 review notes

MLH1 was reviewed as the shared MutL scaffold in PTHR10073. It forms MutLalpha with PMS2, MutLbeta with PMS1, and MutLgamma with MLH3, so rows that only establish alternative heterodimers or meiotic/chromatin contexts were kept non-core rather than folded into the core MLH1-PMS2 post-replicative MMR function.

Key papers checked locally: PMID:16873062 localizes the endonuclease active site to the PMS2 DQHA(X)2E(X)4E motif while assaying the assembled human MLH1-PMS2 MutLalpha complex; PMID:33510387 further reconstitutes MutSalpha-MutLalpha initiation and incision; PMID:11292842 maps competition among PMS2, PMS1 and MLH3 for the same MLH1 interaction region; PMID:17715146 shows that MutSalpha/MutSbeta, not MutLalpha, stimulate WRN unwinding. The ComplexPortal meiotic mismatch-repair row from PMID:16873062 is a term/context error rather than evidence that the somatic MutLalpha paper is wrong.
