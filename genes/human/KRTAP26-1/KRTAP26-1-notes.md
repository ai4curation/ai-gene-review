- Description used: KRTAP26-1 (keratin-associated protein 26-1, KAP26.1) is a 210-aa member of the PMG/KRTAP_PMG family of hair keratin-associated proteins. Its expression is restricted to the hair follicle, where in situ hybridization and immunohistochemistry localize it to the well-differentiated portion of the hair cuticle, about 10-15 cell layers above the dermal papilla apex. There it is a matrix constituent of keratinizing cuticle cells that associates with hair keratin intermediate filaments. The ovine KRTAP26-1 orthologue is polymorphic and its variants are associated with wool fibre diameter, curvature and staple length.
