# KAT2A notes

Family pass for PTHR45750, 2026-11-24. KAT2A/GCN5 and KAT2B/PCAF are the two human PTHR45750 paralogues and are alternative GNAT catalytic subunits of SAGA/STAGA/TFTC and ATAC histone acetyltransferase complexes.

No source checked in this review established KAT2A as a direct human cytokine-production, synaptic-plasticity, T-cell-activation, cartilage-development, bone-development, stem-cell-maintenance, cell-projection, mitochondrial-distribution, TNF-response, or NGF-response effector.

KAT2A cGAS/STING evidence supports KAT2A as the acetyltransferase recruited by MYO1F, not as the primary factor positioning cGAS at the plasma membrane.

The KAT2A histone deacetylase-binding rows were left undecided because the cached PMID:12887892 abstract foregrounds PCAF/SIRT2 and does not mention KAT2A or GCN5.
