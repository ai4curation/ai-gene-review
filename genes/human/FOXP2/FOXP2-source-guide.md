# FOXP2 source and assay guide

All 72 original QuickGO contexts and 72 exported rows were reconciled with the genuine 70-row seed. Two pairs differ by nuclear scope and are retained as 72 distinct review contexts. All eleven original extension atoms and partner isoform identifiers remain intact. The [source evidence](FOXP2-source-evidence.yaml) preserves the complete originals and actual raw-to-native-to-reviewed mapping.

FOXP2 is a nuclear transcription factor with direct human regulatory-DNA binding and repression evidence. Target-dependent activation, model-dependent neuronal development and cooperative mouse muscle-development findings remain distinct. Positive pair assays are interpreted within their recorded host, construct and detection method. No new annotation or publication quotation is proposed.

The official record declares nine products and supports eight sequence reconstructions. O15409-3 is explicitly Not described. Historical clone names, the 714-aa BC143867 construct, the 503–584 structural fragment and an annotated 93–715 interaction region are not assigned to natural products without exact construct evidence.

The actual PTHR45796 human target is PTN002483647/AN150, matching the 715-aa displayed sequence. All seven incoming IBA objects join literal IBDs on its path, across four distinct ancestral nodes. The original donor groupings remain exact; human self-donor evidence is legitimate experimental grounding. Current rat vocalization ISO/IBA records are distinguished from an original direct rat experiment.
