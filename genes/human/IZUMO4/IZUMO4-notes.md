# IZUMO4 notes

Deep research (falcon / perplexity-lite) failed in this environment; notes compiled from cached
publications, PubMed search and UniProt (Q1ZYL8).

- Izumo family member with IZUMO domain; signal peptide 1-15, no TM segment; UniProt "Secreted" (ECO:0000305).
- Soluble, broader tissue expression than IZUMO1-3 [PMID:19658160 "Izumo 1-3 are transmembrane proteins expressed specifically in the testis, and Izumo 4 is a soluble protein expressed in the testis and in other tissues."].
- Lost after acrosome reaction in mouse sperm, suggesting intra-acrosomal [PMID:19658160 "Because Izumo 4 is predicted to be a soluble protein, its loss after the AR suggests it could be an intra-acrosomal protein."].
- Forms a dimer-sized complex on sperm (PMID:19658160).
- Human sperm: immunolocalized to acrosomal region; anti-IZUMO4 antibodies reduce cumulus penetration, hemizona binding and sperm penetration assays [PMID:40760720 "IZUMO proteins were immunolocalized in the acrosomal region of acrosome-intact and acrosome-reacted cells."].
- Mouse Izumo4 KO males fertile [PMID:40572022 "these 18 testis-expressed genes are individually dispensable for male reproduction in mice"].
- Nucleus HDA (PMID:21630459, sperm nucleus proteomics) is weak; ARBA membrane IEA wrong (no TM).

Localization conclusion: soluble secretory-pathway protein, acrosomal content (released at acrosome reaction);
not an integral plasma-membrane protein.
