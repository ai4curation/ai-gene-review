CC   -!- FUNCTION: Symporter that cotransports neutral amino acids and sodium
CC       ions from the extracellular to the intracellular side of the cell
CC       membrane (PubMed:11342143, PubMed:19015196, PubMed:33928121). The
CC       transport is electrogenic, pH dependent and partially tolerates
CC       substitution of Na(+) by Li(+) (PubMed:11414754). Preferentially
CC       transports smaller amino acids, such as glycine, L-alanine, L-serine,
CC       L-asparagine and L-threonine, followed by L-cysteine, L-histidine, L-
CC       proline and L-glutamine and L-methionine (PubMed:11414754,
CC       PubMed:33928121). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11342143,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11414754, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19015196,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33928121}.
