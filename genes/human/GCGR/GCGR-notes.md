## Function
- Class B1 GPCR for glucagon; couples mainly to Gs/adenylyl cyclase, also Ca2+ mobilization.
  "GCGR is a G protein-coupled receptor that activates the stimulatory α G protein (Gs) and increases cAMP
  production upon glucagon binding" [PMID:19657311].
- Cloned human receptor "binds [125I]glucagon and transduces a signal leading to increases in the concentration
  of intracellular cyclic adenosine 3',5'-monophosphate" [PMID:8144028].
- Structures: 7TM domain [PMID:23863937], full-length [PMID:28514451], antagonist MK-0893 extra-helical site
  [PMID:27111510], cryo-EM Gs complex with dual agonist [PMID:32371397].
- C-terminal tail phosphorylation needed for glucagon-induced endocytosis; tail region needed for surface
  expression [PMID:9287038].
- Loss-of-function variants (P86S, V368M, biallelic defects) cause Mahvash disease: hyperglucagonemia,
  alpha-cell hyperplasia, hyperaminoacidemia [PMID:19657311, PMID:30294546, PMID:32677665].
- Gly40Ser association with NIDDM [PMID:7773293] and essential hypertension [PMID:8563746] are genetic
  association reports, not process participation.
- RAMP1/2/3 complexes from multiplexed screen [PMID:39083597].

## Localization
- Plasma membrane, extracellular N-terminal domain (aa 26-136) binds glucagon; rapidly internalized after
  ligand binding [UniProt; PMID:9287038]. Mutant and WT GCGR "both expressed on the cell surface"
  [PMID:30294546]. Conclusion: PM, ECD exposed; ligand-induced endocytosis.
