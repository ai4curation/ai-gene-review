CC   -!- FUNCTION: Substrate-recognition subunit of protein phosphatase 2A
CC       (PP2A) that plays a key role in cell cycle by controlling mitosis entry
CC       and exit (PubMed:1849734, PubMed:33108758). Involved in chromosome
CC       clustering during late mitosis by mediating dephosphorylation of MKI67
CC       (By similarity). Essential for serine/threonine-protein phosphatase 2A-
CC       mediated dephosphorylation of WEE1, preventing its ubiquitin-mediated
CC       proteolysis, increasing WEE1 protein levels, and promoting the G2/M
CC       checkpoint (PubMed:33108758). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q6P1F6,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:1849734, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33108758}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: PP2A consists of a common heterodimeric core enzyme, composed
CC       of a 36 kDa catalytic subunit (subunit C) and a 65 kDa constant
CC       regulatory subunit (PR65 or subunit A), that associates with a variety
CC       of regulatory subunits (PubMed:38123684). Proteins that associate with
CC       the core dimer include three families of regulatory subunits B (the
CC       R2/B/PR55/B55, R3/B''/PR72/PR130/PR59 and R5/B'/B56 families), the 48
CC       kDa variable regulatory subunit, viral proteins, and cell signaling
CC       molecules (PubMed:38123684). Interacts with the PP2A C catalytic
CC       subunit PPP2CA (PubMed:38123684). Interacts with the PP2A A subunit
CC       PPP2R1A (PubMed:38123684). Interacts with TP53 (PubMed:17245430).
CC       Interacts with IER5 (PubMed:25816751). Interacts with MFHAS1; the
CC       interaction is direct (PubMed:28609714). Interacts with PABIR1/FAM122A
CC       (via its N-terminus); the interaction is direct and inhibits PP2A
CC       activity (PubMed:27588481, PubMed:33108758, PubMed:38123684). Interacts
CC       with ARPP19; the interaction is direct and inhibits PP2A activity
CC       (PubMed:27588481, PubMed:33108758, PubMed:38123684). Interacts with
