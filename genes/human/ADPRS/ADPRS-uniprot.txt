CC       ribose and acts on different substrates, such as proteins ADP-
CC       ribosylated on serine and threonine, free poly(ADP-ribose) and O-
CC       acetyl-ADP-D-ribose (PubMed:21498885, PubMed:29907568, PubMed:30045870,

CC       PubMed:34625544). Specifically acts as a serine mono-ADP-
CC       ribosylhydrolase by mediating the removal of mono-ADP-ribose attached
CC       to serine residues on proteins, thereby playing a key role in DNA
CC       damage response (PubMed:28650317, PubMed:29234005, PubMed:30045870,
CC       PubMed:33186521, PubMed:34019811, PubMed:34625544). Serine ADP-
CC       ribosylation of proteins constitutes the primary form of ADP-
CC       ribosylation of proteins in response to DNA damage (PubMed:29480802,
CC       PubMed:33186521, PubMed:34625544). Does not hydrolyze ADP-ribosyl-
CC       arginine, -cysteine, -diphthamide, or -asparagine bonds
CC       (PubMed:16278211, PubMed:33769608). Also able to degrade protein free
CC       poly(ADP-ribose), which is synthesized in response to DNA damage: free
CC       poly(ADP-ribose) acts as a potent cell death signal and its degradation
CC       by ADPRHL2 protects cells from poly(ADP-ribose)-dependent cell death, a
