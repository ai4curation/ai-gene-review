CC   -!- FUNCTION: Inhibins/activins are involved in regulating a number of
CC       diverse functions such as hypothalamic and pituitary hormone secretion,
CC       gonadal hormone secretion, germ cell development and maturation,
CC       erythroid differentiation, insulin secretion, nerve cell survival,
CC       embryonic axial development or bone growth, depending on their subunit
CC       composition. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11279126}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Activin A is a homodimer of INHBA that plays a role in
CC       several essential biological processes including embryonic development,
CC       stem cell maintenance and differentiation, haematopoiesis, cell
CC       proliferation and tissue fibrosis (PubMed:3194407, PubMed:16440334).
CC       Signals through type I (such as ACVR1B or ACVR1C) and type II receptors
CC       (such as ACVR2A, ACVR2B or BMPR2) which, upon ligand binding,
CC       phosphorylate SMAD2 and SMAD3 intracellular signaling mediators that
CC       form a complex with SMAD4, translocate to the nucleus and modulate gene
CC       expression (PubMed:10652306, PubMed:24018044). Can also activate
CC       alternative non-canonical intracellular signaling pathways including
CC       the p38 MAPK, extracellular signal-regulated kinases 1/2 (ERK1/2) and
CC       c-Jun N-terminal kinases (JNKs) to modulate cell migration and
CC       differentiation (PubMed:16440334). Alternatively, promotes osteoblastic
CC       differentiation via ACVRL1-SMAD1/5/9 pathway (PubMed:34948289). In
CC       addition, can engage the type I receptor ACVR1 to form an ACVR1-activin
CC       A-type II receptor non-signaling complex (NSC) that renders receptors
CC       unavailable for engagement with BMPs, hence resulting in an apparent
CC       inhibition of ACVR1-mediated BMP signaling (PubMed:26333933).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10652306, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16440334,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24018044, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26333933,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:3194407, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34948289}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Inhibin A is a dimer of alpha/INHA and beta-A/INHBA that
CC       functions as a feedback regulator in the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal
CC       (HPG) axis. Inhibits the secretion of FSH from the anterior pituitary
CC       gland by acting on pituitary gonadotrope cells. Antagonizes activin A
CC       by binding to the proteoglycan, betaglycan, and forming a stable
CC       complex with and, thereby, sequestering type II activin receptors while
CC       excluding type I receptor. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10746731}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Dimeric, linked by one or more disulfide bonds. Inhibin A is a
CC       dimer of alpha/INHA and beta-A/INHBA. Activin A is a homodimer of beta-
CC       A/INHBA. Activin AB is a dimer of beta-A/INHBA and beta-B/INHBB.
CC       Interacts with FST and FSTL3; these interactions prevent activin A
CC       interaction to its type II receptor (PubMed:11279126). Activin A
CC       interacts with ACVR2A (PubMed:10652306). Activin A interacts with BMPR2
CC       (PubMed:24018044). Inhibin A interacts with ACVR1; this interaction
CC       creates a non-signaling complex (NSC) that inhibits ACVR1-mediated BMP
CC       signaling (PubMed:10652306). Inhibin A interacts with ACVR2A
CC       (PubMed:10652306). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10652306,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11279126, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12660162,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12697670, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18768470}.
