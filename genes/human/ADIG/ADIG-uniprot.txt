CC   -!- FUNCTION: Key regulator of lipid storage that promotes the expansion of
CC       lipid droplets during the formation of mature adipocytes. Acts by
CC       forming a dodecameric complex with seipin (BSCL2) when adipocytes have
CC       differentiated and are undergoing maturation. In the dodecameric
CC       complex, ADIG antagonizes the activity of BSCL2 by reducing BSCL2-
CC       mediated triacylglycerol nucleation and relieving BSCL2's inhibition of
CC       GPAT3 and GPAT4. GPAT3 and GPAT4 then translocate to lipid droplets,
CC       promoting the synthesis of triacylglycerols and the expansion of lipid
CC       droplets. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R400}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms a dodecameric complex with seipin (BSCL2). ADIG
CC       selectively binds the dodecameric form of BSCL2: its transmembrane
CC       interacts with the first transmembrane (TM1) of one BSCL2 monomer and
CC       second transmembrane (TM2) of a neighboring BSCL2 monomer.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R400}.
