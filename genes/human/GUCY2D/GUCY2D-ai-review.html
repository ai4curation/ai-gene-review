Gene Description

GUCY2D encodes retinal guanylyl cyclase 1, a membrane enzyme that converts GTP to cGMP in photoreceptors. Calcium-sensitive GCAP regulation supports cGMP replenishment during recovery from light stimulation. Pathogenic variants cause inherited retinal dystrophies.
