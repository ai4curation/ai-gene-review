## PMID:30319355

The reported ER localization is from transfected cells; mutant constitutive activity does not describe normal GC-E.

## PMID:9600905

ATP-specific catalysis required engineered substitutions; nucleotide regulation and nucleotide substrate specificity are different questions.

## PMID:21124868

The positive binding assay uses human cyclase and mouse Ankmy2; it does not establish cyclase-mediated transport.

## PMID:7777544

RetGC1 is a real comparator. Retinal nuclear-layer RNA is distinct from nuclear-envelope protein localization.

## PMID:9153227

Rat constructs were assayed in COS7; native association preparations were bovine. Rat rod immunogold resolved both plasma and disc membranes.

## PMID:21598940

Mouse knockout-resolved native outer-segment preparations distinguish RetGC1 activity; they do not constitute direct human microscopy.

## PMID:8662612

Human wild-type RetGC1 in HEK293 membranes responds to ATP and AMP-PNP; this response alone does not physically map a binding site.

## PMID:12799385

Bovine outer-segment nucleotide-preincubation experiments support regulation; the paper explicitly treats direct ATP binding as speculative, and its kinase-domain structures are models.

## PMID:8900199

Purified bovine outer-segment cyclase showed ATP photoaffinity labeling and phosphotransferase activity. The authors retained a contamination caveat and required domain mutagenesis for definitive intrinsic-kinase attribution.

## Reactome:R-HSA-74885

The human catalyst is the modeled mature Q02846 chain52–1103 in disc membrane. This does not make the incoming canonical GOA records product-specific.

## Reactome:R-HSA-2514859

Direct catalyst membership in the nested cGMP-synthesis reaction distinguishes enzymatic participation from mere pathway presence.
