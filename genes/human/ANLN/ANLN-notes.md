# ANLN notes

## Biology
- Anillin is a conserved contractile-ring scaffold [PMID:10931866; PMID:18158243].
  - Domains: an N-terminal myosin-binding region and actin-binding domain; a C-terminal C2, Rho-binding domain (RBD) and PH domain [PMID:25959226].
  - Binds F-actin: human fragments cosediment with actin [PMID:10931866].
  - Binds myosin II, shown with Xenopus fragments [PMID:15496454].
  - Binds RhoA directly [PMID:18158243; PMID:31105010].
  - Binds PI(4,5)P2 through its PH domain [PMID:22197245].
  - Binds septins [PMID:10931866; PMID:12479805; PMID:36044846].
- Location by stage:
  - Nuclear in interphase; cortical after nuclear envelope breakdown; furrow, then midbody [PMID:10931866].
- Regulation and loss of function:
  - APC/C-Cdh1 substrate [PMID:16040610].
  - Depletion causes cytokinesis failure, abscission failure and multinucleation [PMID:15496454; PMID:16040610].
- Other roles:
  - Human epithelial junction integrity [PMID:25809162].
  - FSGS variants R431C and G618C [PMID:24676636].

## GOA calls
- **Positive regulation of bleb assembly (IMP, PMID:23870127) → MODIFY to negative regulation.** Depletion causes premature and large blebs.
- **Regulation of exit from mitosis (TAS) → REMOVE.** Anillin is an APC/C substrate at exit, not a regulator.
- **Cadherin binding (HDA, BioID proximity) → REMOVE.**
- **Actin binding → MODIFY to actin filament binding.**
- **Non-core:** septin rows (TAS and IBA), podocyte migration (variant gain-of-function), bleb location.
- **IBA rows** come from opisthokont node PTN001853010 (scraps, Mid2, ANI-1, human ANLN); no failure.
- **NEW:** small GTPase binding (RhoA; IDA) and PI(4,5)P2 binding (IDA).
- **Not added:**
  - Myosin II binding: shown with Xenopus proteins only.
  - Actin bundling and ring constriction (PMID:34321459): species of the anillin used is unclear from the cached text.
