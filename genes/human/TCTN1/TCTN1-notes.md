# TCTN1 notes

Deep research: skipped because the providers are unavailable here. The review is based on UniProt Q2MV58 and on PMID:21725307 and PMID:16357211, which were fetched in full text.

## Key findings
- Tctn1 forms a complex with MKS/JBTS proteins [PMID:21725307 "Tctn1 forms a complex with multiple ciliopathy proteins associated with Meckel and Joubert syndromes, including Mks1, Tmem216, Tmem67, Cep290, B9d1, Tctn2 and Cc2d2a."].
- It localizes to the transition zone [PMID:21725307 "In hTERT-RPE1 cells, Tctn1 localized to the transition zone, between the ciliary axoneme and basal body"].
- It is required for a subset of interactors to reach the transition zone [PMID:21725307 "Tctn1−/− MEFs exhibited decreased Mks1 and Tmem67 localization to the transition zone"]. NPHP1 and NPHP4 localize independently of it.
- Its requirement for ciliogenesis is tissue-specific [PMID:21725307 "essential for ciliogenesis in some, but not all, tissues"].
- It regulates Hedgehog signaling downstream of Smo and Rab23 [PMID:16357211]. No NEW Hedgehog term was added, because the effect is most likely a consequence of the ciliary gate function. This is raised as a suggested question.
- TCTN1 has a signal peptide. The extracellular region and Reactome cytosol rows were marked over-annotated.
