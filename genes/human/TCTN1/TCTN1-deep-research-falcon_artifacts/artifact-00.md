Both occurred in recessive pedigrees with Joubert-spectrum neurodevelopmental abnormalities. Rhodopsin recovered faster in transition-zone FRAP, Tctn1 re-expression rescued STX3 sorting, and progressive photoreceptor degeneration followed.
