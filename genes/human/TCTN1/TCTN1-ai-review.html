Gene Description

TCTN1 (tectonic-1) is a conserved signal-peptide protein of the tectonic family. It is a component of the MKS (tectonic-like) complex at the ciliary transition zone, together with TCTN2, TCTN3, MKS1, TMEM216, TMEM67, B9D1, CC2D2A and CEP290. This membrane-spanning assembly acts as a diffusion barrier that keeps the ciliary membrane a distinct signaling compartment. TCTN1 is required for MKS1 and TMEM67 to reach the transition zone, for ciliogenesis in some tissues such as the embryonic node and neural tube, and for ciliary localization of membrane-associated proteins such as ARL13B, adenylyl cyclase 3, Smoothened and PKD2. Through these ciliary roles it is needed for full Hedgehog pathway activation and repression in the neural tube and limb, acting downstream of Smoothened and RAB23. A TCTN1 mutation causes Joubert syndrome.

Core Functions

Component of the MKS/tectonic transition-zone complex. It is required for MKS1 and TMEM67 to localize to the transition zone, for tissue-specific ciliogenesis, and for the diffusion barrier that keeps membrane proteins in or out of the cilium. No specific molecular activity has been defined.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: What is the topology of TCTN1 at the transition zone? Is it held at the extracellular or lumenal face by binding to TCTN2/TMEM216/TMEM67, and is any of it secreted in vivo?

Q: Is the Hedgehog phenotype of Tctn1 mutants entirely explained by loss of ciliary Smoothened and ARL13B, or does TCTN1 have a separate signaling role?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Map TCTN1 topology at the transition zone with protease-protection and surface-labelling assays on non-permeabilized ciliated cells, and test for secreted TCTN1 in conditioned medium from cells expressing untagged endogenous protein.

Experiment: Combine domain-deletion rescue of Tctn1-null MEFs with assays for MKS1/TMEM67 recruitment and ciliary Smoothened entry to map the parts of TCTN1 needed for complex assembly versus gate function.

TCTN1 notes

Deep research: skipped because the providers are unavailable here. The review is based on UniProt Q2MV58 and on PMID:21725307 and PMID:16357211, which were fetched in full text.

Key findings

  • Tctn1 forms a complex with MKS/JBTS proteins PMID:21725307.
  • It localizes to the transition zone PMID:21725307.
  • It is required for a subset of interactors to reach the transition zone PMID:21725307. NPHP1 and NPHP4 localize independently of it.
  • Its requirement for ciliogenesis is tissue-specific PMID:21725307.
  • It regulates Hedgehog signaling downstream of Smo and Rab23 PMID:16357211. No NEW Hedgehog term was added, because the effect is most likely a consequence of the ciliary gate function. This is raised as a suggested question.
  • TCTN1 has a signal peptide. The extracellular region and Reactome cytosol rows were marked over-annotated.
