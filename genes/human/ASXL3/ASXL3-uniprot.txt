CC   -!- FUNCTION: Putative Polycomb group (PcG) protein. PcG proteins act by
CC       forming multiprotein complexes, which are required to maintain the
CC       transcriptionally repressive state of homeotic genes throughout
CC       development. PcG proteins are not required to initiate repression, but
CC       to maintain it during later stages of development. They probably act
CC       via methylation of histones, rendering chromatin heritably changed in
CC       its expressibility (By similarity). Non-catalytic component of the PR-
CC       DUB complex, a complex that specifically mediates deubiquitination of
CC       histone H2A monoubiquitinated at 'Lys-119' (H2AK119ub1)
CC       (PubMed:30664650, PubMed:36180891). The PR-DUB complex is an epigenetic
CC       regulator of gene expression and acts as a transcriptional coactivator,
CC       affecting genes involved in development, cell communication, signaling,
CC       cell proliferation and cell viability (PubMed:30664650,
CC       PubMed:36180891). ASXL1, ASXL2 and ASXL3 function redundantly in the
CC       PR-DUB complex and are essential for chromatin recruitment and
CC       transcriptional activation of associated genes (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8C4A5,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30664650, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36180891}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Core component of the polycomb repressive deubiquitinase (PR-
CC       DUB) complex, at least composed of BAP1, one of ASXL1, ASXL2 or
CC       (probably) ASXL3, and one of MBD5 or MBD6 (Probable). Distinct
CC       combinations of ASXL and MBD proteins may preferentially bind specific
CC       histone modification marks (PubMed:36180891). The PR-DUB core
CC       associates with a number of accessory proteins, including FOXK1, FOXK2,
CC       KDM1B, HCFC1 and OGT; KDM1B specifically associates with ASXL2 PR-DUB
CC       complexes (Probable). Interacts (via PHD domain) with MBD5 and MBD6
CC       (via MBD domain); the interaction is probably direct and mediates
CC       association of MBD proteins with the PR-DUB core (PubMed:36180891).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36180891, ECO:0000305|PubMed:30664650,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:36180891}.
