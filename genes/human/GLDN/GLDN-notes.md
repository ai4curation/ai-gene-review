# GLDN curation notes

Official human identity: HGNC:29514, UniProtKB:Q6ZMI3. All 13 raw contexts are unsuffixed, without negation or annotation extensions.

The official 551-residue Q6ZMI3-1 and 427-residue Q6ZMI3-2 sequences are distinct natural splice products. The shorter sequence exactly matches canonical residues 125–551; it is not a label for the proteolytically released ectodomain. No unique activity is assigned to it here. Historical CAD98018 sequence conflict V376A is retained as source context, not interpreted as a modern canonical variant.

PAINT family PTHR23192 was inspected through its actual tree, alignment, ancestral mapping and IBD records. The target is AN489/PTN002509188. The three IBA placements and all literal donor sets were replayed independently. The cell-surface self-donor is experimentally grounded.

Primary construct boundaries are preserved: rat clone AY266116 in PMID:16039564 is 549 residues; modern human canonical sequence is 551 residues. Recombinant fragments, Fc fusions and assay hosts do not define natural human isoforms. The source-specific assay and access boundaries are recorded in reference reviews.

HPA050873 reports U2OS plasma membrane and MCF-7/SuSa vesicles. These cell-line observations are not universal localization or isoform assignments.

The official Matrisome portal links the human masterlist: sheet1 row63 identifies GLDN/HGNC29514/Q6ZMI3 as Core matrisome / ECM Glycoproteins. An older linked workbook agrees at row62. Category membership is distinct from a specific human peptide or tissue measurement.

No NEW annotations are proposed. The axon IEA decision is restricted to physiological compartment mapping and does not deny extracellular binding to axonal receptors.
