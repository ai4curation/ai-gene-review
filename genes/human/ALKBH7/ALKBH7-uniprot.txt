CC   -!- FUNCTION: RNA demethylase that demethylates N(2)-dimethylguanosine and
CC       N(1)-methyladenosine within mitochondrial pre-tRNA regions. This
CC       demethylation activity plays a crucial role in regulating the
CC       processing of nascent polycistronic mitochondrial RNA, thereby
CC       influencing mitochondrial gene expression and overall mitochondrial
CC       activity (PubMed:34253897). In response to extensive DNA damage is
CC       required for programmed necrosis induced by alkylation and oxidation.
CC       Acts by triggering the collapse of mitochondrial membrane potential and
CC       loss of mitochondrial function that leads to energy depletion and cell
CC       death (PubMed:23666923). Involved in fatty acid metabolism (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9D6Z0,
