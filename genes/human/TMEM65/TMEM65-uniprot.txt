CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mitochondrial protein that plays a critical role in the
CC       sodium-dependent calcium efflux from mitochondrion (PubMed:40200126,
CC       PubMed:40691517). The precise role of TMEM65 in mitochondrial
CC       sodium/calcium exchange is however unclear: TMEM65 associates with the
CC       SLC8B1/NCLX antiporter and probably promotes the sodium/calcium
CC       exchange mediated by SLC8B1/NCLX (PubMed:40200126). According to
CC       another report, TMEM65 functions as the mitochondrial sodium/calcium
CC       antiporter itself: homodimerization drives formation of a pocket
CC       capable of binding calcium (PubMed:40691517). Essential for maintaining
CC       proper cardiac intercalated disk (ICD) structure and function as well
CC       as cardiac conduction velocity in the heart (By similarity). Its
CC       association with SCN1B is required for stabilizing the perinexus in the
CC       ICD and for localization of GJA1 and SCN5A to the ICD (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q4VAE3, ECO:0000269|PubMed:40200126,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40691517}.
