Gene Description

AP1S3 encodes sigma-1C (also called sigma-3), one of three interchangeable small subunits of adaptor protein complex 1 (AP-1), the heterotetrameric clathrin adaptor that selects cargo for transport between the trans-Golgi network and endosomes. AP-1 comprises two large adaptins (gamma and beta-1), a medium adaptin (mu-1A or mu-1B) and a single small adaptin, which in human cells may be sigma-1A, sigma-1B or sigma-1C, so the sigma-1C form is a variant of the same complex rather than a distinct machine. The tetramer is cytosolic at rest and is recruited onto trans-Golgi and endosomal membranes by ARF1 and phosphoinositides, where it forms the inner layer of a clathrin coat, captures transmembrane cargo, rides the budded vesicle to its destination and is released again on uncoating. Within the complex the sigma subunit is the chain that reads acidic dileucine sorting signals, [DE]XXXL[LI], in the cytosolic tails of cargo proteins. Structures containing sigma-1C itself show the two bulky motif residues inserted into adjacent hydrophobic pockets formed by Leu65, Phe67, His85, Val88 and Val98, with Lys60 and Arg61 forming a basic patch that hydrogen-bonds a phosphoserine immediately downstream of the motif, so that phosphorylation of a cargo tail can strengthen its capture. The same surface is occupied, before the tetramer is assembled, by the pseudoGTPase domain of the assembly chaperone AAGAB, which keeps the free sigma subunit folded and soluble and sterically blocks cargo binding until the complex is built. Sigma-1C is a 154-residue longin/roadblock-fold protein with no catalytic activity, and it also contributes to the structural integrity of the tetramer through an interface with the medium adaptin. Loss of sigma-1C is tolerated at the organismal level, consistent with the three sigma-1 paralogues being largely redundant, but it has clear cellular consequences. Cells depleted of it fail to deliver Toll-like receptor 3 to endosomes and lose the type I interferon response to double-stranded RNA, and they form fewer autophagosomes on starvation. In the epidermis, where the gene is abundantly expressed, these defects converge on an inflammatory circuit in which the autophagy receptor p62 accumulates, NF-kappa-B is activated and interleukin-36-alpha is overproduced. Two hypomorphic coding variants - Phe4Cys, which destabilises the protein's hydrophobic core, and Arg33Trp, which weakens the interface with the medium adaptin - are enriched in people with pustular psoriasis; neither lies on the cargo-binding surface, so both act by reducing the amount of functional complex rather than by changing what it selects.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

dileucine sorting signal binding

Definition: Binding to an acidic dileucine sorting signal, a [DE]XXXL[LI] peptide motif in the cytosolic tail of a transmembrane cargo protein, which directs that protein into clathrin-coated vesicles formed by heterotetrameric adaptor protein complexes.

Justification: GO can express the complex-level activity (GO:0035615 clathrin-cargo adaptor activity) and the tyrosine-motif counterpart read by the medium adaptin (GO:0089710 endocytic targeting sequence binding, whose definition requires an essential tyrosine), but it has no term for the dileucine motif read by the small adaptin. That leaves the one activity a sigma subunit demonstrably performs on its own unexpressible, so sigma subunits across AP-1, AP-2 and AP-4 carry either nothing or a bare protein binding row. The gap is now sharply defined by structure: the motif's two bulky residues occupy adjacent pockets on the sigma subunit, and in AP1S3 those pockets are formed by Leu65, Phe67, His85, Val88 and Val98. A sibling of GO:0089710 under protein binding would fit the existing pattern exactly. The absence was checked by searching the GO release served by QuickGO on 'dileucine', 'sorting signal binding', 'cargo adaptor activity', 'signal sequence binding' and 'endosomal sorting signal'; 'dileucine' returns nothing at all.

Parent term: protein binding

assembly chaperone binding

Definition: Binding to an assembly chaperone, a protein that binds an individual subunit or an assembly intermediate of a multi-subunit complex and is required for that complex to form correctly, but that is not itself a component of the finished complex.

Justification: Dedicated assembly chaperones are recognised for ribosomes, proteasomes, nucleosomes and SNARE complexes as well as for the AP-1 and AP-2 adaptors, but GO has no term for the client side of that relationship: searching the ontology on 'assembly chaperone', 'chaperone binding' and 'protein complex assembly' returns only GO:0051087 protein-folding chaperone binding, whose definition is about nascent or unfolded polypeptides. For AP1S3 the relationship is unusually well defined - a 1.68 A co-crystal with the AAGAB pseudoGTPase domain, a 994 A2 interface, a 1:1 complex, and the demonstration that the free subunit is otherwise misfolded - yet GO does not even classify AAGAB itself as a chaperone, so the client-side annotation currently has to borrow a folding-chaperone term. A term of this shape would serve every AP-complex sigma and alpha/gamma subunit, not just this one.

Core Functions

Sigma-1C is the cargo-reading subunit of one of the three ubiquitous AP-1 variants. As part of the assembled tetramer on trans-Golgi and endosomal membranes it supplies the two adjacent hydrophobic pockets that receive the bulky residues of an acidic dileucine [DE]XXXL[LI] sorting signal, together with a basic patch that reads a phosphoserine just downstream of the motif, so that cargo selection can be tuned by phosphorylation. Bridging that captured cargo to the clathrin lattice is a property of the whole complex rather than of this subunit, which is why the clathrin-cargo adaptor activity is recorded as a contribution; what sigma-1C itself brings is the sorting-signal binding surface and a structural role in holding the tetramer together through its interface with the medium adaptin. Structural molecule activity is recorded as the subunit-level function because it is the only one GO can currently express for this protein: the activity it actually performs alone, binding the dileucine sorting signal, has no GO term and is proposed here as a new one.

Current model (mechanistic narrative)

AP1S3 encodes σ1C, a sigma subunit of the AP-1 clathrin adaptor complex that governs endosomal membrane trafficking in keratinocytes, where its loss drives cutaneous autoinflammation [PMID:24791904, PMID:27388993]. AP1S3 is required for endosomal translocation of TLR3, and its depletion mislocalizes TLR3 and blunts downstream TLR3 signaling PMID:24791904. Beyond its keratinocyte role, AP1S3 physically binds the HCV E2 envelope protein and shields it from E6AP-mediated ubiquitin-dependent proteasomal degradation, and a synthetic peptide carrying the AP1S3-recognized motif inhibits HCV infection PMID:27079945.

9. Open questions

  1. Is there any cargo that the σ1C form of AP-1 selects and the σ1A/σ1B forms do not? Nothing
    in the record addresses this, and it is the question that would decide whether AP1S3
    deserves its own process annotations at all rather than inheriting the complex's.
  2. Why is the phenotype epidermal if the gene is widely expressed and the complex redundant?
    The candidate answer in the literature is relative abundance in keratinocytes plus the IL-36
    amplification loop, but no isoform-swap experiment has been done.
  3. Which AP-1 σ variant handles STING? 7R4H used AP1S3; the cellular knockdowns and mutants in
    the same paper used AP1S1. These may be interchangeable, but it has not been tested.
  4. Four isoforms are annotated, two of them NMD candidates, and the MANE-Select transcript
    encodes isoform 4 (C-terminus TMEEYMNKPTF) while the UniProt canonical is isoform 1
    (VSTVSQTMGER). Both structures used isoform 1. Nothing is known about isoform-specific

Interpretation

The cargo-facing surface of AP1S3 is intact and structurally characterised at 1.68-2.34 A:
the hydrophobic pockets that receive the two bulky residues of a [DE]XXXL[LI] sorting
motif (L65, F67, H85, V88, V98, I103) and the basic pair (K60, R61) that reads a
phosphoserine immediately C-terminal to the motif. The same V88/V98 pocket is the site the
assembly chaperone AAGAB occupies before the complex is built, so cargo binding and
chaperone binding are mutually exclusive on this subunit.

Both PSORS15 variants sit outside that cargo surface: F4 in the hydrophobic core (folding)
and R33 at the sigma/mu1A interface (assembly), consistent with both being reported as
destabilising rather than cargo-selective.

  residues inserted into adjacent hydrophobic pockets formed by Leu65, Phe67, His85, Val88 and Val98,
  with Lys60 and Arg61 forming a basic patch that hydrogen-bonds a phosphoserine immediately downstream
  of the motif, so that phosphorylation of a cargo tail can strengthen its capture. The same surface is
  occupied, before the tetramer is assembled, by the pseudoGTPase domain of the assembly chaperone AAGAB,
  which keeps the free sigma subunit folded and soluble and sterically blocks cargo binding until the
  complex is built. Sigma-1C is a 154-residue longin/roadblock-fold protein with no catalytic activity,
  and it also contributes to the structural integrity of the tetramer through an interface with the medium
  adaptin.


  Loss of sigma-1C is tolerated at the organismal level, consistent with the three sigma-1 paralogues
  being largely redundant, but it has clear cellular consequences. Cells depleted of it fail to deliver
  Toll-like receptor 3 to endosomes and lose the type I interferon response to double-stranded RNA, and
  they form fewer autophagosomes on starvation. In the epidermis, where the gene is abundantly expressed,
  these defects converge on an inflammatory circuit in which the autophagy receptor p62 accumulates, NF-kappa-B
  is activated and interleukin-36-alpha is overproduced. Two hypomorphic coding variants - Phe4Cys, which
  destabilises the protein's hydrophobic core, and Arg33Trp, which weakens the interface with the medium
  adaptin - are enriched in people with pustular psoriasis; neither lies on the cargo-binding surface,
  so both act by reducing the amount of functional complex rather than by changing what it selects.
