## Enzyme and product scope

Human GALC removes a galactosyl headgroup from galactolipids. The human cloning/expression study reports increased activity in COS-1 cells; cat RNA was a probe intermediate, not the expressed enzyme ([PMID:8281145](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8281145/)). Independent purification from human urine, with brain and placenta preparations, supports enzyme identity and activity ([PMID:8399327](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8399327/)). Those authentic abstracts were read, but full original assay Methods were not recovered. The old journal's numerical pH and Km statements are historical and are not used in the current judgments.

The current official products are P54803-1 (685 residues), -3 (662), -4 (659) and -5 (589). Direct FASTAs agree with the declared feature reconstructions. This verifies sequences, not equivalent catalytic activity, processing or localization of every product. The early human cDNA papers describe 669 residues; a later promoter paper reports an upstream in-frame ATG 48 bases before the old start. Neither observation silently establishes every historical construct's identity with a current suffix ([PMID:8297359](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8297359/), [PMID:9434153](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9434153/)). Proteolytic fragments and electrophoretic bands are not natural splice products.

## Acidic-compartment mechanism

Selected original Methods, Results and captions of the GALC-SapA paper were read. Its principal structure is a mouse 2:2 complex at 3.6 angstrom; a separate limited human pull-down is distinguished. The structure provides a route between the catalytic region and SapA's lipid cavity, but does not contain a resolved lipid-substrate complex. No universal SapC-negative conclusion is imported ([PMID:29323104](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5764952/)).

The endolysosome paper measures cathepsin substrates and acid-phosphatase activity, not GALC-specific microscopy. Its selected original Methods and Results support the IC setting of an acid hydrolase, with the inference explicit ([PMID:27498570](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27498570/)). The single core uses endolysosome lumen; the broader lysosomal-lumen setting is not repeated as a second ancestor location. Lysosomal lumen is part_of lysosome, while endolysosome lumen is_a lysosomal lumen. The three organelle refinements preserve a true broad location while specifying its lumen.

The cancer article is explicitly a review. It states the correct galactosylcerebroside-to-ceramide conversion but is not represented as a newly read primary IDA assay, despite the literal original evidence code ([PMID:15657896](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15657896/)). Its downstream cancer hypotheses do not create new apoptosis, migration or adhesion annotations.

## Ancestry and donor evidence

The actual PTHR15172 tree has 68 nodes and 35 leaves; the alignment has 1,038 columns. Human AN16/PTN002495607 exactly matches the current 685-residue sequence. All three IBA origins are AN0/PTN001032282, on the actual target path. Later retained activity and lysosome IBDs add a zebrafish donor relative to the incoming records; the original versions remain literal. Self-donors reflect experimental grounding and are not circular evidence.

The current mouse P54818 donor chain includes positive cDNA-expression evidence: normal mouse cDNA raises activity in COS1 cells while the twitcher nonsense construct does not ([PMID:8769874](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8769874/)). A SapA-mutant experiment supports dependence of galactosylceramide degradation on its activator, and a twitcher mass-spectrometry study measures psychosine accumulation ([PMID:11371512](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11371512/), [PMID:11734583](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11734583/)). Authentic abstracts were read; individual full-paper GALC assay panels were not inspected. These donor scopes complement independent human evidence rather than replace it.
