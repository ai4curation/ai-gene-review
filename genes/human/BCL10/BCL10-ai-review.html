Gene Description

BCL10 is a noncatalytic immune signaling adaptor with an N-terminal caspase recruitment domain. Activated CARD-containing proteins, including CARD9, CARD11 and CARD14, nucleate BCL10 assemblies that recruit and organize MALT1 and downstream signaling machinery. These cytoplasmic and membrane-associated CARD-BCL10-MALT1 complexes promote canonical NF-kappaB activation through regulated ubiquitin and kinase signaling. BCL10 self-association also provides a platform for MALT1 activation. The adaptor supports antigen-receptor responses in lymphocytes and selected innate responses, with dependence varying by receptor and cell type. Biallelic loss of BCL10 causes combined immunodeficiency, whereas dysregulated expression or activating signaling assemblies can contribute to lymphoma. Nuclear localization, transcriptional coactivation and effects on apoptosis occur in particular experimental or pathological contexts.
