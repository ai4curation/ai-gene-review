# Affinage mechanistic annotation for DNAJC25 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

DNAJC25 is a cytoplasmic DnaJ/HSP40 co-chaperone family member that acts as a suppressor of cell proliferation and a promoter of apoptosis in hepatocellular carcinoma cells, where its overexpression inhibits colony growth and increases apoptosis [PMID:23205125]. DNAJC25 is itself a substrate of the E3 ubiquitin ligase RNF149, which directs its ubiquitin-mediated degradation, and this regulation contributes to HCC progression [PMID:37958377]. Beyond these overexpression and substrate-level observations, no direct chaperone mechanism, binding partner, or catalytic activity has been characterized for DNAJC25 in the available corpus.
