CC   -!- FUNCTION: DNA-binding protein critical for the DNA damage response,
CC       specifically in repairing double-strand breaks (DSBs) via the classical
CC       non-homologous end joining (NHEJ) pathway. It forms a heterodimer with
CC       XRCC6 (Ku70), creating the Ku70:Ku80 heterodimer (Ku complex), which
CC       serves as a DNA end-binding complex. It primarily binds DSBs and
CC       recruits essential repair factors, assembling the core long-range NHEJ
CC       complex to facilitate the alignment and ligation of broken DNA ends
CC       (PubMed:11493912, PubMed:33854234, PubMed:34352203). This pathway
CC       ensures the rapid repair of cytotoxic and mutagenic DSBs and
CC       contributes to the generation of diversity in T-cell receptors and
CC       antibodies through mechanisms such as V(D)J recombination
CC       (PubMed:9742108). Likely acts as a 5'-deoxyribose-5-phosphate lyase
CC       (5'-dRP lyase), catalyzing the beta-elimination of the 5'-deoxyribose-
CC       5-phosphate at abasic sites near DSBs. This activity cleans the termini
CC       of abasic sites, a common form of nucleotide damage, preparing broken
CC       ends for ligation (PubMed:20383123). It may also possess 3'-5' DNA
CC       helicase activity, although this has not been confirmed in vivo, and
CC       its physiological significance remains unclear (PubMed:7957065). Beyond
CC       DNA repair, the protein contributes to telomere maintenance
CC       (PubMed:29490055). It is also implicated in transcriptional regulation,
CC       acting as a cofactor for various transcription factors
CC       (PubMed:12145306, PubMed:8621488). It plays a role in the regulation of
CC       DNA virus-mediated innate immune response by assembling into the HDP-
CC       RNP complex, a complex that serves as a platform for IRF3
