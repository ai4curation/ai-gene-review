Gene Description

EPG5 (ectopic P-granules autophagy protein 5 homolog) is a large (about 2,580 residues), metazoan-specific cytoplasmic protein that acts at the late, degradative step of macroautophagy. It is a Rab7 effector: it is recruited to late endosomes and lysosomes by direct binding to GTP-Rab7 and the R-SNAREs VAMP7/VAMP8, and it also binds LC3 and the assembled STX17-SNAP29 Qabc SNARE complex on autophagosomes. By bridging these membranes, EPG5 stabilizes STX17-SNAP29-VAMP7/8 trans-SNARE complexes and promotes fusion of autophagosomes with late endosomes/lysosomes, while restricting promiscuous fusion with other endocytic vesicles. Loss of EPG5 causes accumulation of non-degraded autophagosomes. EPG5 also supports Rab7-dependent delivery of endocytic cargo, including CpG DNA, to late endosomes/lysosomes, which is needed for TLR9/TLR7 signalling in B cells. Recessive EPG5 mutations cause Vici syndrome, a multisystem disorder with callosal agenesis, cataracts, cardiomyopathy, hypopigmentation and combined immunodeficiency, and are also linked to a neurodevelopmental disorder with parkinsonism.

Core Functions

Rab7 effector and tethering factor that bridges LC3- and STX17-SNAP29-positive autophagosomes with Rab7/VAMP7/8-positive late endosomes/lysosomes and stabilizes trans-SNARE complexes, determining the fusion specificity that produces degradative autolysosomes.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does EPG5 tether membranes on its own (for example, cluster Rab7- and LC3-decorated liposomes in vitro), or does it act only as a SNARE-assembly chaperone downstream of HOPS-mediated tethering?

Q: Is the endolysosomal CpG-delivery defect of EPG5-deficient cells explained by the same Rab7-effector and fusion-specificity mechanism as the autophagy defect?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Reconstitute tethering and fusion of Rab7/VAMP7 proteoliposomes with LC3-PE/STX17-SNAP29 proteoliposomes using purified full-length EPG5, with and without HOPS, to separate tethering from SNARE-chaperone activity.

Hypothesis: EPG5 bridges Rab7- and LC3-containing membranes independently of HOPS.
