Gene Description

EPG5 is a 2,579-residue cytoplasmic Rab7 effector that coordinates selective fusion of autophagosomes with late endosomes and lysosomes. It associates with the lysosomal surface through Rab7 and VAMP7/8, binds autophagic STX17-SNAP29-containing SNARE assemblies, and engages LC3/GABARAP proteins through tandem LC3-interacting-region motifs, with preferential binding to the GABARAP subfamily. These interactions organize the fusion machinery and restrict inappropriate fusion with other endocytic vesicles; EPG5 deficiency causes accumulation of undegraded autophagic cargo. EPG5 also supports endosome-to-lysosome delivery of internalized nucleic acids, enabling endosomal TLR9 and TLR7 signaling. Biallelic pathogenic variants cause Vici syndrome and a neurodevelopmental disorder with parkinsonism or other movement abnormalities.

Core Functions

Binds autophagic SNARE assemblies and LC3/GABARAP-family proteins at the endolysosomal compartment, coordinating productive autophagosome-lysosome fusion and cargo clearance. SNARE binding is the directly demonstrated specific molecular function; a broader adaptor role remains a mechanistic model because human complex recognition or stabilization has not been distinguished from bringing two partners together.
