Gene Description

EPG5 is a large intracellular protein that helps ensure productive fusion of autophagosomes with late endosomes and lysosomes. Human EPG5 associates with the endolysosomal compartment and binds Rab7, LC3/GABARAP-family proteins and autophagic SNARE assemblies. Its elongated helical architecture and interaction surfaces support a membrane-tethering model that links organelle recruitment and SNARE-complex organization to autophagic cargo clearance. EPG5 also supports delivery of internalized endocytic cargo to lysosomes; in human immune and fibroblast models this enables endosomal TLR9 responses to CpG DNA. Biallelic pathogenic EPG5 variants cause Vici syndrome, a multisystem disorder associated with defective autophagy.
