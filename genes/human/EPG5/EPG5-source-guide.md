## PMID:29130391 — human endocytic trafficking and RAN association

[Original article, DOI 10.1080/15548627.2017.1389356](https://doi.org/10.1080/15548627.2017.1389356); [PMC article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5846549/); [actual author-repository PDF](https://iris.uniroma1.it/retrieve/e3835326-6b0b-15e8-e053-a505fe0a3de9/Piano%20Mortari_Vici_2018.pdf).

Read the main Results, Discussion and operative Methods in the normal original and the 16-page author manuscript. The inspected figure pixels are Figure 3 (PDF page 6, journal page 27), Figure 4 (page 7/page 28), and Figure 7 (page 11/page 32). Figures 1, 2, 5 and 6 were assessed through main text and captions, without separate pixel inspection. Supplementary movies and other supplementary bodies were not inspected.

- Results on CpG trafficking, Figures 2–4, and the Cell stimulation, siRNA and Confocal microscopy Methods: human patient fibroblasts and lymphoblastoid cells, plus EPG5-depleted control fibroblasts, retain internalized CpG in tubular/recycling compartments. Figure 3D–E provides the ATG7 comparison. The paper reports unsuccessful rescue transfection; this is not presented as successful rescue evidence. This supports endosome-to-lysosome cargo delivery, not free-nucleotide transport.
- Results on endosomal association and Figure 4B–D: human EPG5-GFP colocalizes with LAMP2 in fibroblasts/HEK293T cells, compared with EEA1. This is compartment localization, not integral-membrane topology.
- Figures 1E and 5: CpG-triggered cytokine/NFKB responses depend on the delivery pathway in these cells; preserved IL1B responses help bound the interpretation. EPG5 contributes through trafficking, without an established receptor, sensor or kinase activity.
- TLR agonist Methods: the CpG stimulus is a synthetic single-stranded oligodeoxynucleotide. Figure 6 separately uses a double-stranded-DNA baculovirus transduction assay. Its GFP readout integrates multiple steps. The precise GO cellular-response-to-dsDNA attribution therefore remains unresolved; the study did not exclusively use single-stranded DNA.
- EPG5 sequence homology Results, Figure 7D and Co-immunoprecipitation Methods: reciprocal EPG5/RAN association is measured in detergent-solubilized human cell extracts. The partner is RAN, not Rab7. This does not establish a purified binary interaction or a karyopherin mechanism.
- Figure 7E and fractionation Methods: perinuclear/nuclear-envelope staining, emerin costaining and fractionation support the reported location. The Discussion's nuclear transport interpretation remains preliminary; uncertainty about function does not invalidate the location observation.

## PMID:27588602 — human binding evidence and worm reconstitution

[Original article, DOI 10.1016/j.molcel.2016.08.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2016.08.021); [actual publisher supplement, 32 pages](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S1097276516304634-mmc1.pdf); [actual main Figure 6 image](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S1097276516304634-gr6.jpg).

The original main article remains inaccessible. Publisher/API identity metadata, the supplement cover, DOI and article identifier agree. The normal cache remains abstract-only. The original supplement's relevant Results/captions and Methods were read, including Protein expression and purification, In vitro pulldown, immunogold localization, liposome preparation/reconstitution and lipid mixing. Supplementary Figures S3, S4 and S6 and main Figure 6 pixels were inspected. The main Figure 6 operative Results and caption were not recovered; the image alone is not a substitute for that access.

- Figure S3A–D and S3I–N: human tagged EPG5 associates with Rab7/LAMP1-positive compartments and with late-endosomal or hybrid endosome-lysosome membranes by immunogold microscopy. This supports the functional membrane-associated pool without claiming a transmembrane segment.
- Figure S4E: full-length human EPG5 association with Rab7 variants is a cell-extract assay. S4F–G separately tests purified human EPG5 fragments 491–599 and 491–575 against GST-Rab7, supporting direct small-GTPase binding.
- Figure S5V: purified human EPG5(427–1094) binds LC3. S5 mutant results were read in the caption/text; S5 pixels were not separately inspected.
- Figure S6O–Q: human EPG5(427–1094) binds SNAP29/VAMP7/VAMP8 weakly and assembled STX17-SNAP29-VAMP7/8 complexes more strongly; isolated STX17 does not bind in S6O. S6J–K/N show human-cell associations, and S6T measures reduced endogenous STX17–VAMP7 association after EPG5 depletion. These establish SNARE association with explicit construct and assay limits.
- Figure S6U–W and the reconstitution/lipid-mixing Methods: the reconstituted system uses worm EPG-5 and worm SNARE proteins. Rab7 is attached to membranes using an engineered membrane anchor. NBD dequenching measures lipid mixing, not content mixing. The two-color proteoliposome images are not treated here as a quantified, isolated human membrane-bridging assay.

Human-fragment binding, full-length human cell association and worm biochemical reconstitution are distinct evidence scopes. None establishes identical activity for both natural human products. The inherited vesicle-membrane-tethering proposal remains UNDECIDED; this is an unresolved experimental attribution, not a claim that the biological tethering model is false.

## PMID:39809444 — human structure and revised LIR interpretation

[Original article, DOI 10.1080/15548627.2024.2447213](https://doi.org/10.1080/15548627.2024.2447213); [PMC original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12087653/).

These results revise the speculative multivalent interpretation of the 2021 work. No isolated human membrane-bridging assay was established. Main figure pixels, supplementary bodies and simulations were not independently inspected or rerun.
