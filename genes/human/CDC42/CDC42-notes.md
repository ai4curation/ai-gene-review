# CDC42 evidence notes

## Molecular function and biological scope

CDC42 is a Rho-family GTP-controlled signaling switch. Its intrinsic GTP hydrolysis and nucleotide-dependent effector recognition connect spatial signals to actin organization and cell polarity. These are properties of CDC42 itself: kinase, GEF, GAP and actin-nucleation activities belong to other proteins in the associated machinery. The core uses GO:0003925, whose current definition includes small monomeric G proteins. The core separately records GBD/CRIB effector recognition and uses the specific positive-filopodium and epithelial apical/basal-polarity processes; their broader ancestors remain in the annotation review.

The primary WASP study explains how CDC42 binding releases an autoinhibited region, “enabling its interaction with the actin regulatory machinery” ([PMID:10724160](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10724160/), abstract). This supplies a mechanism for actin regulation without making CDC42 an actin polymerase. Recombinant Cdc42 homodimerization and intrinsic hydrolysis are directly described in [PMID:9748241](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9748241/); nucleotide-loading reporters in other papers are not themselves purified hydrolysis assays. Human PAR6 proteins bind active CDC42 and participate in polarity complexes ([PMID:11260256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11260256/)); the Rac-specific ruffling experiment in that abstract is not transferred to CDC42.

Selected full Results in human Caco-2 cells show that CDC42 depletion disrupts junctional and apical/basal markers; constitutive activation also perturbs junctions. Spatial control, rather than simply more activity, is important ([PMID:22891260](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22891260/), Figures 1 and 7). Human endothelial experiments connect fibronectin/NRP1/ABL1 signaling to CDC42 activation and inhibitor-sensitive protrusions ([PMID:26051942](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26051942/), selected Figure 4 Results). These support actin and polarity as core outputs. Their vascular, adhesion and stress-fiber contexts remain non-core.

The exact two seeded alternative-product records are preserved, including legacy secondary accessions. The UniProt record identifies different C termini in the placental and brain products. Experiments on palmitoylated GAP43/paralemmin motifs are not direct assays of CDC42 brain-product lipidation ([PMID:14978216](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14978216/)). Endogenous product targeting remains a useful experimental question.

## Other directly examined cellular contexts

- **Mitosis:** selected full Results, Methods and Figures 4–7 of [PMID:15642749](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15642749/) describe endogenous CDC42 activation, spindle/bridge localization and antibody controls in HeLa S3 cells. CDC42 depletion affects chromosome congression and kinetochore–microtubule attachment. The explicit high-frequency cytokinesis-failure result concerns Ect2 depletion. That result is not transferred to CDC42; the positive-cytokinesis assertion remains unresolved.
- **Phagocytosis:** [PMID:26465210](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26465210/), selected Figure 1 and Methods, uses RAW264.7 macrophages expressing Cdc42G12V. Sustained activation retains actin at abortive cups. Activation and subsequent inactivation have different roles; the exact transgene species was not independently resolved from the selected Methods.
- **Morphology:** full Figure 7 and accompanying Results of [PMID:8625410](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8625410/) compare activated human CDC42, Rac and Rho constructs in porcine endothelial cells. WASP suppresses CDC42-induced lamellipodia and stress-fiber bundling. The four existing morphology assertions are retained as non-core at this construct/species scope.
- **Migration and spreading:** CDC42 RNAi reduces matrix invasion in human MDA-MB231 cells, with modest CXCR4 changes ([PMID:20023705](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20023705/), selected Figure 6). Activated CDC42 counteracts ICAP1-dependent inhibition of NIH3T3 spreading ([PMID:11807099](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11807099/), selected Figures 1, 3 and 4). These are context-dependent phenotypes, not a unique normal-tissue metastasis function.
- **Neuronal/granule locations:** selected full [PMID:21048939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21048939/) shows CDC42/LRRK2 coassociation and soma/neurite staining in differentiated SH-SY5Y cells. The abstract of [PMID:15121898](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15121898/) identifies CDC42 in human Staufen-containing granule preparations. These do not establish CDC42 kinase or RNA-binding activity.
- **Specialized comparisons:** [PMID:30189608](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30189608/), selected Results, separates mouse spermatid localization from CDC42 mutant comparisons in 293T cells. [PMID:23940119](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23940119/), Figure 1G/H, actually measures CDC42 active state in human keratinocytes despite foregrounding RhoA. A paper's title does not identify every protein it tested.

## Three independently examined unusual assertions

The ubiquitin-ligase assertion is removed because the actual selected experiments fail to establish the stated catalytic function. [PMID:21435037](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21435037/), selected full Results and Figures 2 and 8, shows CDC42-dependent enhancement of SopB ubiquitination in yeast, while a CDC42-binding-deficient SopB mutant remains ubiquitinated in HeLa infection. It does not demonstrate CDC42-catalyzed ubiquitin transfer. This judgment is based on the experiments, not a title or a presumed incompatibility with GTPase activity. The same paper's bacterial replication/yeast-growth effects are not relabeled as positive regulation of human cell growth. Exact SptP/SopE2 comparisons remain unresolved.

[PMID:16380373](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16380373/), selected full Figure 4 Results, examines endogenous CDC42 Golgi localization and brefeldin-A redistribution. It supports Golgi apparatus rather than membership of the eight-subunit COG complex. The distinct Golgi-membrane ISS assertion retains its **rat** Q8CFN2 donor.

The storage-vacuole inference has an authentic mouse donor: [PMID:21828338](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21828338/), full Figure 1C/Methods, detects Cdc42 in the VAMP2-marked MIN6 insulin-granule fraction. Secretory granule is the supported refinement. Fractionation does not resolve membrane versus lumen, and this remains an orthology-based human assertion.
