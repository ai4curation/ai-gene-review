Gene Description

CTSK encodes cathepsin K, a papain-family cysteine endopeptidase synthesized as a 329-residue secretory precursor and processed to a mature 215-residue enzyme. It acts in lysosomal compartments and after secretion, with a prominent role in osteoclast degradation of collagen-rich bone matrix. Its proteolysis extends to several extracellular matrix proteins, and glycosaminoglycans modulate its collagenolytic activity. Specialized thyroid pools also participate in thyroglobulin proteolysis and hormone liberation. Loss of CTSK function causes pycnodysostosis, characterized by impaired bone remodeling and brittle, dense bone.
