- Important for the mitochondrial UPR module: in mammals CLPP is NOT required for UPRmt [PMID:27154400 "Remarkably, our results clearly show that mammalian CLPP is neither required for, nor it regulates the UPR(mt) in mammals."]. So GO:0034514 was not added to human CLPP, unlike worm CLPP-1.
