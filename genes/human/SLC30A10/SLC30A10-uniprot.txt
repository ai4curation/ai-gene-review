CC   -!- FUNCTION: Calcium:manganese antiporter of the plasma membrane mediating
CC       the efflux of intracellular manganese coupled to an active
CC       extracellular calcium exchange (PubMed:30755481). Required for
CC       intracellular manganese homeostasis, an essential cation for the
CC       function of several enzymes, including some crucially important for the
CC       metabolism of neurotransmitters and other neuronal metabolic pathways.
CC       Manganese can also be cytotoxic and induce oxidative stress,
CC       mitochondrial dysfunction and apoptosis (PubMed:22341972,
CC       PubMed:25319704, PubMed:26728129, PubMed:27226609, PubMed:27307044).
CC       Could also have an intracellular zinc ion transporter activity,
