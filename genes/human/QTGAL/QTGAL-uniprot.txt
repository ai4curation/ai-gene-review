CC   -!- FUNCTION: Glycosyltransferase that specifically catalyzes
CC       galactosylation of cytoplasmic tRNA(Tyr) modified with queuosine at
CC       position 34 (queuosine(34)) (PubMed:37992713). Galactosylates the
CC       cyclopentene hydroxyl group of queuosine(34) in tRNA(Tyr) to form
CC       galactosyl-queuosine(34) (PubMed:37992713). Mannosylation of
CC       queuosine(34) in tRNA(Tyr) is required to slow-down elongation at
CC       cognate codons UAC and suppress stop codon readthrough, thereby
CC       regulating protein translation (PubMed:37992713).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37992713}.
