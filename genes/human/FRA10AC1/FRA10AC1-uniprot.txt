CC       of FRA10AC1 results in expression of the folate-sensitive fragile site
CC       FRA10A. The number of the CGG repeats normally varies in the population
CC       from 8 to 14. In contrast, individuals cytogenetically expressing the
CC       fragile site have at least 200 CGG repeats (PubMed:15203205). No
CC       distinct phenotype has been associated with expression of FRA10A.
CC       Nevertheless, some studies have proposed that this fragile site
CC       expression might be associated with intellectual disability,
CC       tumorigenesis, or neurological disorders. However, these associations
CC       can be attributed to ascertainment bias. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15203205,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:15203205}.
