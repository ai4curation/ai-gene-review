## 2. Structural & Mechanistic Synthesis (`P29374`, 1257 aa)
1. **RB1 Pocket Recruitment via `957-LVCHE-961` (`LXCXE`) & Bimodal R1/R2 Transcriptional Repression**:
   - Cloned by Fattaey et al. 1993 (`PMID:8414517`) and Otterson et al. 1993 (`PMID:8455946`) as a ~200 kDa nuclear phosphoprotein binding the retinoblastoma (`RB1`) pocket in vivo and displaced by HPV E7.
   - Lai et al. 1999 (`PMID:10490602`) and Lai et al. 2001 (`PMID:11283269`) demonstrated that `ARID4A` bridges `RB1`/`RBL1` (`p107`)/`RBL2` (`p130`) pocket proteins via its canonical `LXCXE` motif (`957-LVCHE-961`, central `Cys959`; co-crystallized with the RB1 pocket in `PDB:7SMC`) to `mSin3A-SAP30-HDAC1/HDAC2` class I histone deacetylase complexes via its C-terminal R2 repression domain, while a separate central R1 domain mediates HDAC-independent transcriptional repression at E2F target promoters during cell-cycle exit (`GO:0003714`, `GO:0070822`, `GO:0017053`, `GO:0000122`). Also purified with `mSin3A` (`PMID:12724404`) and `BRMS1` (`PMID:14581478`).
2. **Multidomain Chromatin & Nucleic Acid Engagement (Sequence-Nonspecific dsDNA + Methyl-Histone Reader)**:
   - **Interdigitated Double Tudor Domain (`aa 4-121`)**: Solution NMR structure (`PMID:24379399`) shows that, unlike methyl-lysine-reading Tudor domains, the `ARID4A` Tudor domain lacks an intact aromatic cage and instead binds dsDNA and ssDNA electrostatically (`Kd` ~10-100 uM) without sequence preference (`GO:0003690`).
   - **PWWP-ARID Supramodule (`aa 170-411`)**: Patsialou et al. 2005 (`PMID:15640446`) DNA-binding site selection and Xu et al. 2021 NMR (`PMID:34506790`) proved that the `ARID4A` ARID domain (`Gly327` in `326-RGTPI-330`, core `Trp365`, `Ser379` at the ARID5 `Thr388` major-groove position) binds dsDNA **without AT- or GC-rich sequence preference**, while a long disordered acidic loop L12 inside the ARID acts as an intramolecular DNA mimic that autoinhibits ARID DNA binding and binds H2A-H2B. Ren et al. 2022 (`PMID:35365808`) solved the 2.05 A crystal structure of the `PWWP-ARID` supramodule and showed that schizophrenia-associated missense variant `p.His411Asp` (`His411` verified at `P29374` position 411) loosens the supramodule, weakens dsDNA binding, and impairs G0/G1 arrest.
   - **Chromobarrel Domain (`aa 568-635`)**: NMR (`PMID:22247551`) and crystal (`PMID:29408527`) structures show an aromatic cage that recognizes `H4K20me3` (and weaker `H3K9me3`/`H3K27me3`/`H3K36me3`/`H3K4me3`), with affinity enhanced when multidomain `ARID4A` engages nucleosomal DNA (`PMID:34506790`).
3. **In Vivo Epigenetic & Coactivator Roles with Paralog `ARID4B` + Reported Breast Cancer RNA Stabilization**:
   - `Arid4a` forms a complex with `Arid4b` at the *Snrpn* promoter to maintain `H3K9me3`, `H4K20me3`, and DNA methylation at the Prader-Willi/Angelman imprinting center (`PMID:17043311`), suppresses myelodysplastic/AML transformation in bone marrow (`PMID:18728284`), and acts as a transcriptional coactivator for `AR` and `RB1` in Sertoli cells to sustain spermatogenesis and blood-testis barrier integrity (`PMID:23487765`).
   - A 2025 study (`PMID:40066676`) reported that `ARID4A` also binds conserved 3'-UTR RNA elements to stabilize `MTSS1`, `TIMP2`, `RB1`, and `PTEN` mRNAs in breast cancer cells; we note this emerging post-transcriptional role in references/questions without over-annotating core chromatin functions until independently replicated.
