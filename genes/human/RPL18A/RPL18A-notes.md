# RPL18A notes

- 2026-11-15 re-review of source defects: the two WRONG_GENE_PRODUCT defects on the Complex Portal NAS rows GO:0006941 striated muscle contraction (PMID:34081545) and GO:1901740 negative regulation of myoblast fusion (PMID:26684695) were withdrawn, and both actions changed from REMOVE to MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. QuickGO by reference shows each term on the complex record ComplexPortal:CPX-7665 ("60S cytosolic large ribosomal subunit, striated muscle variant", which contains RPL3L, Q92901, in place of RPL3) and on every protein subunit of that complex and of its mouse counterpart CPX-7663. RPL18A (Q02543) is a listed participant of CPX-7665. The rows are therefore Complex Portal's standard export of complex-level terms to subunits, not an annotation placed on the wrong ribosomal protein; that the terms over-reach for a constitutive subunit is a review judgement expressed by the action.
