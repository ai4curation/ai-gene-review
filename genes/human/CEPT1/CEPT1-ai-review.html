Gene Description

CEPT1 (choline/ethanolamine phosphotransferase 1) is a dual-specificity, polytopic integral membrane enzyme of the CDP-alcohol phosphatidyltransferase class-I family that catalyzes the final, committed step of both the CDP-choline and CDP-ethanolamine (Kennedy) branches of glycerophospholipid biosynthesis. It transfers a phosphobase group from a CDP-linked donor to the sn-3 hydroxyl of 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol (DAG): using CDP-choline it produces phosphatidylcholine plus CMP (diacylglycerol cholinephosphotransferase activity, EC 2.7.8.2), and using CDP-ethanolamine it produces phosphatidylethanolamine plus CMP (ethanolaminephosphotransferase activity, EC 2.7.8.1). Catalysis requires a divalent metal cofactor, preferring Mg2+ (Mn2+ supports roughly half-maximal activity), coordinated by a conserved di-aspartate CDP-binding motif; the enzyme also has a minor 1-alkenyl/1-alkyl-2-acylglycerol choline phosphotransferase side activity that yields ether (plasmenyl/plasmanyl) choline phospholipids and platelet-activating-factor precursors. CEPT1 is a homodimer with ten transmembrane helices per protomer and a hydrophobic chamber that accommodates the DAG acyl tails during catalysis. It resides in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane and the nuclear (inner) membrane, where channeling of CDP-choline generated by the upstream rate-limiting enzyme CCTalpha drives de novo phosphatidylcholine synthesis. CEPT1 is ubiquitously expressed.

CEPT1 (human) — gene review notes

UniProt: Q9Y6K0 (CEPT1_HUMAN), 416 aa. HGNC:24289. Gene on chromosome 1.

Summary of function

CEPT1 is choline/ethanolamine phosphotransferase 1, the dual-specificity enzyme
that catalyzes the final (committed) step of both the CDP-choline and CDP-ethanolamine
(Kennedy) branches of glycerophospholipid synthesis. It transfers a phosphobase from a
CDP-linked donor to diacylglycerol (DAG):

  • CDP-choline + DAG → phosphatidylcholine (PC) + CMP (EC 2.7.8.2; GO:0004142 diacylglycerol cholinephosphotransferase activity)
  • CDP-ethanolamine + DAG → phosphatidylethanolamine (PE) + CMP (EC 2.7.8.1; GO:0004307 ethanolaminephosphotransferase activity)

It is a polytopic integral membrane enzyme of the CDP-alcohol phosphatidyltransferase
class-I family, resident in the ER and nuclear (inner) membrane, requiring Mg2+ (or, less
efficiently, Mn2+) as cofactor.
