Gene Description

CEP41 is a centrosomal and ciliary protein that supports axonemal tubulin glutamylation and microtubule organization. It associates with the polyglutamylase TTLL6 and promotes its access to the cilium, without an established glutamylase activity of its own. CEP41 localizes to centrioles, basal bodies and cilia; loss of CEP41 can disrupt ciliary modification and architecture while leaving initial ciliogenesis intact. Purified human CEP41 also binds and stabilizes microtubules. In endothelial models, CEP41-dependent glutamylation influences ciliary disassembly and vascular cell behavior. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause Joubert syndrome.

Core Functions

CEP41 associates with TTLL6 and supports its ciliary access, thereby facilitating axonemal tubulin glutamylation. The measured binding/targeting role is noncatalytic; tagged complex association and mammalian localization experiments do not by themselves identify an IFT motor bridge.
