# CSB–PGBD3 source and assay scope

This sibling review covers [UniProt P0DP91](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P0DP91/entry), the 1,061-residue CSB–PGBD3 fusion, under the shared gene symbol ERCC6. Its displayed product has aliases P0DP91-1 and Q03468-2. The two other entries preserved from UniProt identify external full-length CSB Q03468-1 and standalone PGBD3 Q8N328-1; they are not three equivalent fusion variants. Canonical CSB's motor functions remain in the [separate ERCC6 review](../ERCC6/ERCC6-ai-review.yaml).

All eleven QuickGO contexts have schema-backed reviews in [ERCC6__P0DP91-ai-review.yaml](ERCC6__P0DP91-ai-review.yaml). The [complete literal source records](ERCC6__P0DP91-source-records.yaml) preserve source identifiers, qualifiers, extensions, WITH/FROM partners and evidence. They are the same eleven source objects previously reviewed alongside canonical ERCC6. The source record's experimental extensions are retained in the review: STAT1 for the IMP expression and STAT-serine-phosphorylation annotations, and all six targets for the HMP expression annotation. The IGI repair-regulation context retains canonical CSB Q03468 as its partner and remains distinct from the IMP context.

[PMID:22483866](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dnarep.2012.02.004), [original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3340519/): the accepted mechanistic review read operative Methods and Results, including Results 3.3–3.6 and the experiments discussed with Figures 2–4. Distinct full-length human CSB and fusion constructs were expressed in human CSB-null UVSS1KO cell pools. UV RNA-synthesis recovery and damaged-reporter reactivation are repair endpoints; they do not reconstitute a fusion ATPase or excision reaction. Fusion expression increases a STAT1-associated interferon-like program and STAT1 Ser727 phosphorylation; full-length CSB can suppress this program. These are positive regulatory observations, with effective antiviral defense left unresolved. Figure 4's EMSA compares partially purified insect-expressed human fusion and standalone PGBD3 on six MER85 elements with nonspecific competitor. Selected sequence binding is supported; unpublished ChIP-seq is not treated as an independently verified experiment. No separate supplement or figure-pixel inspection is claimed for that accepted reading.

[PMID:26218421](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1005419), [original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4517778/): the accepted primary and database reviews read operative Results, the Methods section on laser micro-irradiation and imaging, and the Figure 3 construct/caption context. Explicit human fusion-RFP in U2OS and fusion-eGFP in HeLa establish nuclear and damage-associated chromatin localization. Rhesus-oocyte immunohistochemistry is a separate experiment; human ovary western blots show expression, not endogenous human oocyte subcellular localization. These observations support broad nucleus without establishing a named nuclear-body class. No separate supplement or figure pixels were inspected.

The [HPA ERCC6 record](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000225830-ERCC6/subcellular) uses antibody HPA071297. Its 141-residue antigen exactly matches fusion residues 472–612 and standalone PGBD3 residues 4–144, while being absent from canonical CSB. The accepted database review inspected the selected U2OS image and recorded the predicted 119.5 kDa western band plus additional bands. This supports a real localization signal but cannot uniquely distinguish the fusion from standalone PGBD3. The finer fusion nucleoplasm and nuclear-body assertions therefore remain UNDECIDED; broad nucleus has independent fusion-construct evidence.
