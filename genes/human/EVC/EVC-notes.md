## Feedback: ciliary compartments and pathway participation

Both broad cilium assertions are now refined to ciliary membrane, using the same positive topology/localization evidence. The mouse-construct limitation remains separate from the probable human IDA source-code query; the imported code, citation and query identity are unchanged. The existing signaling core now includes basal-body-region localization as well as ciliary membrane. Native mouse microscopy, curated UniProt statements and the public human HPA observations support the association, with limited spatial resolution and no inferred basal-body assembly activity. Signal-dependent complex–SMO association sharpens the molecular knowledge gap without demonstrating an individual EVC adaptor/scaffold function (PMID:21356043; PMID:24582806). The source guide supplies exact public paper sections, image URLs and versioned RefSeq accessions.

That controlled negative result is limited to the tested mouse cells and detection methods; it does not settle the independent human HPA signal. Product-resolved and genetically controlled experiments can distinguish cell context from antibody specificity (PMID:21356043).
