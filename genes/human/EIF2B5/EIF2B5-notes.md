# EIF2B5 evidence notes

EIF2B5 encodes the catalytic epsilon subunit of eIF2B. GDP release from eIF2 allows its reuse in cytoplasmic translation initiation. Native human factor preparations, human complex reconstitution and disease-associated human epsilon variants support this mechanism; the rat epsilon-alone assay provides a separate, explicitly nonhuman line of catalytic evidence. These preparations should not be described as an isolated-human-epsilon assay ([PMID:9139680](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9139680/), [PMID:11323413](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11323413/), [PMID:15060152](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15060152/), [PMID:29599213](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29599213/)).

The official displayed human product is Q13144, 721 residues. No alternative-product block or natural isoform suffix is introduced. The 6K72 deposited epsilon sequence matches this reference, but sequence identity alone does not establish contact geometry or isolated activity. The initially incomplete clone described in PMID:8688466 is a historical experimental limitation, not a natural alternative product.

Cytosol is supported by the target Human Protein Atlas page at enhanced location-specific reliability and by the literal human Reactome Q13144 entity. HPA064370 was recorded in MCF-7, PC-3 and U2OS, and HPA069303 in HaCaT, RT-4 and U2OS. This permits refinement of broad cytoplasm annotations while preserving the original source context. Human epsilon–eGFP nuclear exclusion in BHK cells and positive endogenous mouse nuclear staining are distinct observations; neither implies exclusive localization in every condition ([HPA](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000145191-EIF2B5/subcellular), [PMID:12426392](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12426392/), [PMID:12388085](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12388085/)).

Heat and glucose responses remain contextual functions. The rat Q64350 heat donor has direct experimental grounding in PMID:9341116; this supports the conserved broad response claim without making epsilon a thermosensor. The unavailable operative heat-specific details in PMID:15723074 and PMID:12499492 remain reference-access limitations. Glucose-stimulated whole-complex activity in rat pancreatic islets is likewise not a human glucose-receptor assay ([PMID:8567668](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8567668/)).

Positive clinical evidence is retained without equating tissue damage with a direct developmental step. The 93 isolated premature-ovarian-failure subjects in PMID:15507143 were negative for the screened variants; the positive ovarioleukodystrophy cases are in PMID:12707859. Clinical myelin pathology and oligodendrocyte density do not alone demonstrate epsilon-mediated sheath formation or lineage progression. Human glial-progenitor RNAi and GFAP induction support an astrocyte context, with the inaccessible operative details kept explicit ([PMID:15217090](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15217090/), [PMID:15723074](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15723074/)).

The rat epsilon phosphorylation experiments link inhibition of translation-supporting activity to cell death. Resistant mutants protect Rat1/PC12 cells under the tested perturbations; this does not establish an active wild-type epsilon proapoptotic step. The positive-apoptosis annotation is therefore over-specific, rather than refuted solely by a lack of wild-type-overexpression effect ([PMID:11756553](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11756553/)).

Partner-class refinements retain the literal assay scope: the GSK3B assay uses an engineered epsilon peptide, the two-subunit gamma association differs from delta recovery with a larger complex, and screen or co-immunoprecipitation records do not by themselves prove a native binary interface. Reference sequence checksums are not evidence that an assayed insert was sequence-identical. The class assigned to the partner is not a catalytic activity transferred to epsilon.

Five source assertions remain unresolved: endoplasmic-reticulum stress, one myelination assertion, two peptide-hormone responses and T-cell-receptor signaling. They retain their imported identities and qualifiers.
