## Phylogenetic inference and the earlier cholesterol assertion

In the official PTHR15351 version 19 tree, human ERLIN2 leaf PTN002495868 descends from ER-membrane/ERAD node PTN000794169 and SREBP node PTN001282580. Its ungapped MSA equals the canonical 339-residue sequence. The worm ERAD NOT node PTN002692340 is outside this target path. The current cholesterol-binding IBD, PTN002692334, lies in the ERLIN1 clade outside the ERLIN2 path, although aggregate family labels retain the cholesterol term. This is a source-version/ancestry distinction, not evidence of biological loss. Target self-WITH and the number of experimental descendants are not defects.

The earlier ERLIN2 cholesterol IBA is scientifically UNDECIDED. Those findings support analogue interaction without settling the exact cholesterol-binding annotation.
