CC   -!- FUNCTION: Calcium, potassium:sodium antiporter that transports 1 Ca(2+)
CC       and 1 K(+) in exchange for 4 Na(+) (PubMed:26631410). Is involved in
CC       calcium homeostasis in retinal rod photoreceptors and plays a central
CC       role in light adaptation. In darkness, it mediates calcium efflux to
CC       balance the calcium influx driven by cyclic nucleotide-gated (CNG)
CC       channels. Upon illumination, when CNG channels close, it continues to
CC       extrude calcium, leading to a rapid decrease in its cytosolic levels.
CC       This reduction is essential for the feedback mechanisms that regulate
CC       visual signaling and enable adaptation to changing light conditions (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q91WD8,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26631410}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
