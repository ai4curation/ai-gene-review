## Catalysis and core function

[PMID:32915949](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32915949/) provides the decisive biochemical evidence. Its actual Results and construct/purification Methods describe recombinant human FIT2, wild type and catalytic-histidine mutants, radiolabeled substrate assays and product mass spectrometry. FIT2 cleaves fatty-acyl-CoA into acyl-4′-phosphopantetheine and adenosine 3′,5′-bisphosphate. Another microsomal activity produces the subsequent acyl-pantetheine product; that second reaction is not assigned to FITM2. The measured substrate preference does not establish equal activity on every CoA derivative. The ER-luminal orientation of the catalytic region is a topology-based interpretation, not a direct localization measurement from the purified assay.

The official [GO:0010945 definition](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0010945) explicitly includes acyl-CoA substrates. Its short label, coenzyme A diphosphatase activity, therefore describes the measured reaction. A generic lipid-phosphatase-family prediction is not substituted for it. Human cellular complementation connects active FIT2 to ER morphology and normal lipid-droplet formation. Long-term changes in triglyceride synthesis and associated expression were not fully rescued and are separated from the immediate catalytic mechanism. Figure captions and text were read; figure pixels and separate supplements were not independently audited.

## Neutral-lipid binding, storage and the unresolved tether

[PMID:22106267](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22106267/) supports direct detergent-solubilized FIT2 binding to triolein, saturation/competition behavior and binding-mutant comparisons. Accessible indexed Results also describe DAG binding. The official mouse donor [P59266](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P59266/entry) is Fitm2/Fit2 and links this study and N80A perturbation to binding/droplet phenotypes. Complete original Methods and the species of every construct were not recovered; the incoming ISS/IEA contexts are retained as transfers. No purified human binding assay is claimed solely because later literature describes the earlier work that way.

The second core function summarizes triglyceride binding in lipid storage, with associated DAG-binding capacity. Its mechanistic relationship to acyl-CoA diphosphatase activity remains a question, not an established reaction sequence. DAG binding is not DAG hydrolysis.

[Reactome R-HSA-8857686](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8857686) describes FITM2 binding TAGs and partitioning them into droplets. The official [GO:0170007 definition](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0170007) instead requires bringing an ER membrane and a lipid droplet together to establish a contact site. The accessible binding/partitioning experiments do not settle physical membrane bridging. The TAS tether annotation is UNDECIDED, with incomplete full-text access disclosed; absence of a visible assay is not used to REMOVE it. The reaction's ER location is supported independently. Its apparent repeated-paralog-name typo is not reproduced as biological fact or used to dismiss the reaction.

## Mouse donor, triglyceride metabolism and adipocyte differentiation

The genuine normal fetch recovered the HTML full text of [PMID:18160536](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18160536/). Mouse membrane fractionation, mouse constructs expressed in HEK293 cells, tracer partitioning and murine 3T3-L1 shRNA Results were read with the available Methods. Human host cells do not convert the mouse constructs into human protein experiments. FIT2 partitioned newly synthesized triglyceride into droplets without increasing synthesis in the overexpression system. In differentiating knockdown adipocytes, triglyceride biosynthetic flux decreased. The existing broad triglyceride-metabolic involvement is retained as non-core, without calling FITM2 a triglyceride synthase or lipase.

The same original paper reports reduced PPARγ, aP2 and adiponectin in the strongest shRNA1 condition, while shRNA2/3 retained broadly similar marker expression despite reduced droplets. Neither uniformly preserved nor universally blocked differentiation describes those experiments. The two existing differentiation transfers are retained as non-core and are not promoted to direct regulation of adipogenic commitment.

[PMID:24519944](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24519944/) used a later, aP2-driven mouse deletion. The conditional adipocytes retained differentiation markers while producing fewer, larger droplets; this system cannot exclude an earlier developmental role. Its normal cache remains abstract-only; bounded independent primary consultation read the official PMC Methods, Results and captions. It is corroborating context rather than a reason to erase the strongest-shRNA observation.
