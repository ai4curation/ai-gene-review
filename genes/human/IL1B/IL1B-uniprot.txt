CC   -!- FUNCTION: Potent pro-inflammatory cytokine (PubMed:10653850,
CC       PubMed:12794819, PubMed:28331908, PubMed:3920526). Initially discovered
CC       as the major endogenous pyrogen, induces prostaglandin synthesis,
CC       neutrophil influx and activation, T-cell activation and cytokine
CC       production, B-cell activation and antibody production, and fibroblast
CC       proliferation and collagen production (PubMed:3920526). Promotes Th17
CC       differentiation of T-cells. Synergizes with IL12/interleukin-12 to
CC       induce IFNG synthesis from T-helper 1 (Th1) cells (PubMed:10653850).
CC       Plays a role in angiogenesis by inducing VEGF production
CC       synergistically with TNF and IL6 (PubMed:12794819). Involved in
CC       transduction of inflammation downstream of pyroptosis: its mature form
CC       is specifically released in the extracellular milieu by passing through
CC       the gasdermin-D (GSDMD) pore (PubMed:33377178, PubMed:33883744). Acts
CC       as a sensor of S.pyogenes infection in skin: cleaved and activated by
CC       pyogenes SpeB protease, leading to an inflammatory response that
CC       prevents bacterial growth during invasive skin infection
CC       (PubMed:28331908). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10653850,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12794819, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28331908,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33377178, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33883744,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:3920526}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: (Microbial infection) Cleavage by S.pyogenes
CC       cysteine protease SpeB promotes its activation independently of CASP1
CC       (PubMed:28331908, PubMed:32719155). SpeB-mediated maturation of IL1B
CC       plays a dual role depending on infection site: while IL1B inflammatory
CC       response prevents bacterial growth during invasive skin infections, it
CC       promotes streptococcal infection of the nasopharynx by disrupting
CC       colonization resistance mediated by the microbiota (PubMed:28331908,
CC       PubMed:32719155). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28331908,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32719155}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer. In its precursor form, weakly interacts with full-
CC       length MEFV; the mature cytokine does not interact at all
CC       (PubMed:17431422). Interacts with integrins ITGAV:ITGBV and
