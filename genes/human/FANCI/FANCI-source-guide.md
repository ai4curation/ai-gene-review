# FANCI — evidence and source guide

FANCI is a DNA-binding component of the FANCI–FANCD2 (ID2) repair complex. Purified human FANCI binds DNA, and ubiquitination changes the architecture and substrate preference of human ID2. FANCI also promotes FANCD2 monoubiquitination through a regulatory and structural contribution. FANCL and UBE2T perform ubiquitin transfer; FAN1 and POLN supply nuclease and polymerase activities, respectively.
