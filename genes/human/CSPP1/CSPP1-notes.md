## 2026-10-13 ciliary tip reconstitution follow-up

[PMID:39856351, "CSPP1 accumulations decreased growth rate"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12170345/) supplies additional direct evidence for CSPP1-dependent slowing, pausing and regrowth, and for cooperative regulation by ciliary tip proteins. The experiments use tagged human CSPP1 and other human tip proteins except mouse TOGARAM1, with porcine brain tubulin. Minor preparation contaminants are reported; size-exclusion chromatography removed aggregates without establishing oligomeric states. The cryo-ET densities and slow-growth reconstitution support a flexible interacting network, without assigning every density to one protein, proving a fixed complex stoichiometry or directly reconstituting human axonemes.

ARMC9 lacks autonomous microtubule binding in the tested conditions but performs a scaffold/regulatory role: it is recruited by CSPP1 and TOGARAM1 and changes their measured effects on dynamics. This supports the regulatory partner class in GO:0005519, whose definition does not require the partner itself to bind microtubules autonomously. Conversely, the new study does not reproduce CSPP1–TOGARAM1 co-immunoprecipitation or CSPP1 colocalization with TOGARAM1/CCDC66 in reconstitution. These negatives remain alongside the earlier positive cellular association assays; the review does not claim universal purified direct contacts.

The core now separates direct stabilization at microtubules, with observed ciliary context, from the independently established central-spindle MyoGEF recruitment role in cytokinesis. It does not assert that luminal stabilization causes that recruitment, or invent a guanine nucleotide exchange or adapter activity. Centrosome and pole imaging remains accepted in source rows and described biologically without forcing an untested activity-location edge. The existing cell-division refinement overlaps an existing cytokinesis assertion; the real metaphase-arrest phenotype remains recorded with its mechanism unresolved. No additional NEW process term is introduced.

The original notes, machine assertions, source caches and three official products are preserved. The optional request to add an ordinary enables qualifier is declined under the annotation-reviewer instruction to preserve inert qualifier values; its original omission is unchanged. The biological summary retains long/short functional distinctions; construct equivalence limits remain in the product and source-specific assessments. The earlier CEP104 review is a related consistency follow-up, not edited in this CSPP1 change.
