CC   -!- FUNCTION: Integral mitochondrial outer-membrane protein that mediates
CC       the formation of mitochondria-associated endoplasmic reticulum
CC       membranes (MAMs) (PubMed:33972548). In turn, mediates angiogenesis and
CC       neoangiogenesis through interference with intracellular Ca(2+)
CC       communication and regulation of the vascular endothelial growth factor
CC       receptor KDR/VEGFR2 expression at both mRNA and protein levels
CC       (PubMed:33972548). Also acts as an activator of hypoxia-induced
CC       mitophagy, an important mechanism for mitochondrial quality and
CC       homeostasis, by interacting with and recruiting LC3 protein family to
CC       mitochondria (PubMed:22267086, PubMed:24671035, PubMed:24746696,
CC       PubMed:27653272). Mechanistically, recruits DRP1 at ER-mitochondria
CC       contact sites leading to DRP1 oligomerization and GTPase activity to
CC       facilitate mitochondrial fission during hypoxia (PubMed:27145933,
CC       PubMed:33978709). Additionally, plays a role in hepatic ferroptosis by
CC       interacting directly with glutathione peroxidase/GPX4 to facilitate its
CC       recruitment into mitochondria through TOM/TIM complex where it is
CC       degraded by mitophagy (PubMed:36828120). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22267086,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24671035, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24746696,

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33972548, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33978709,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36828120}.
