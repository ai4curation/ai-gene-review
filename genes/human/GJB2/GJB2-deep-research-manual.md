# GJB2 manual evidence and source boundaries

Connexin-26 supplies the subunits of an intercellular channel. Its single channel core covers molecular exchange, electrical coupling and structural assembly. Cargo delivery to the surface is a different relationship from doing the delivery.

## Selected primary evidence

Source excerpts appear in the review; the separately accessed calcium-wave passage is reproduced here and cited from its annotation. Full-cache availability and actual reading scope are separate: selected original sections are not a claim to have inspected every figure or supplement.

Plaque imaging includes WT/mutant GFP-4C constructs; M34A/deletion structural preparations are distinct. Conflicting Alexa598/568 labels remain unresolved.

## Clinical and emerging context

Reference assessments preserve historical variant interpretations. The M34T conclusion of PMID:14694360 is explicitly marked disputed against PMID:31160754, rather than silently rewritten as a current clinical classification. Papers without an abstract or inspected body remain scientifically unverified despite verified identifiers.

[PMID:41514193](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12970211/) informs a question, not a new function. Selected original text reports nuclear observations but describes human-cell ChIP alignment to a mouse genome and inconsistent IP-MS sample provenance. These reporting issues require clarification; they do not prove fabrication. ChIP/co-IP and reporter effects do not establish direct sequence-specific DNA contact. No nuclear, DNA-binding or transcription-regulator annotation is proposed.
