# TRMT1L family review notes

TRMT1L is the vertebrate TRMT1 paralog in PTHR10631. It retains Trm1-family tRNA
guanine dimethyltransferase activity but differs from TRMT1 in substrate
position: two 2025 papers resolved TRMT1L as the enzyme that installs m2,2G27 on
tyrosine tRNAs [PMID:39786990, "TRMT1L represents the elusive enzyme catalyzing
m2,2G at position 27 in tyrosine tRNAs"; PMID:39786998, "TRMT1L is responsible
for catalyzing N2,N2-dimethylguanosine (m22G) solely at position 27 of
tRNA-Tyr-GUA"].

The same 2025 work supersedes the 2021 model in which TRMT1L was proposed to
methylate cytosolic tRNA-Ala(AGC) isodecoders at G26 [PMID:33499731, "TRMT1L,
whose activity was unknown, is responsible for methylating a subset of cytosolic
tRNAAla(AGC) isodecoders at position 26"]. The old location evidence from that
paper remains useful, but the catalytic assignment is recorded in
`references[].findings` as overturned by the G27 studies.

Hwang et al. also found that TRMT1L binds 28S rRNA and a subset of tRNAs and
interacts with a Rix1 ribosome-biogenesis component [PMID:39786998, "binds to
the 28S rRNA as well as to a subset of tRNAs"]. Because this activity is
unmodeled by current GOA rows and its relationship to the tRNA acp3U phenotype
is unresolved, the review leaves it as a suggested question rather than adding
a process term.
