CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multi-isomeric modular protein which forms a linking network
CC       between organelles and the actin cytoskeleton to maintain the
CC       subcellular spatial organization. As a component of the LINC (LInker of
CC       Nucleoskeleton and Cytoskeleton) complex involved in the connection
CC       between the nuclear lamina and the cytoskeleton. The nucleocytoplasmic
CC       interactions established by the LINC complex play an important role in
CC       the transmission of mechanical forces across the nuclear envelope and
CC       in nuclear movement and positioning (PubMed:34818527). Specifically,
CC       SYNE2 and SUN2 assemble in arrays of transmembrane actin-associated
CC       nuclear (TAN) lines which are bound to F-actin cables and couple the
CC       nucleus to retrograde actin flow during actin-dependent nuclear
CC       movement. May be involved in nucleus-centrosome attachment. During
CC       interkinetic nuclear migration (INM) at G2 phase and nuclear migration
CC       in neural progenitors its LINC complex association with SUN1/2 and
CC       probable association with cytoplasmic dynein-dynactin motor complexes
CC       functions to pull the nucleus toward the centrosome; SYNE1 and SYNE2
CC       may act redundantly. During INM at G1 phase mediates respective LINC
CC       complex association with kinesin to push the nucleus away from the
CC       centrosome. Involved in nuclear migration in retinal photoreceptor
CC       progenitors. Required for centrosome migration to the apical cell
CC       surface during early ciliogenesis. Facilitates the relaxation of
CC       mechanical stress imposed by compressive actin fibers at the rupture
CC       site through its nteraction with SYN2 (PubMed:34818527).
