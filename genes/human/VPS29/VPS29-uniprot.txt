CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the commander complex that is essential for
CC       endosomal recycling of transmembrane cargos; the commander complex is
CC       composed of the CCC subcomplex and the retriever subcomplex
CC       (PubMed:37172566, PubMed:39587083, PubMed:38062209, PubMed:38459129).
CC       Component of the retriever complex, which is a heterotrimeric complex
CC       related to retromer cargo-selective complex (CSC) and essential for
CC       retromer-independent retrieval and recycling of numerous cargos such as
CC       integrin alpha-5/beta-1 (ITGA5:ITGB1) (PubMed:28892079,
CC       PubMed:37172566, PubMed:39587083, PubMed:38062209, PubMed:38459129).
CC       Component of the retromer cargo-selective complex (CSC). The CSC is
CC       believed to be the core functional component of retromer or respective
CC       retromer complex variants acting to prevent missorting of selected
CC       transmembrane cargo proteins into the lysosomal degradation pathway.
CC       The recruitment of the CSC to the endosomal membrane involves RAB7A and
CC       SNX3. The SNX-BAR retromer mediates retrograde transport of cargo
CC       proteins from endosomes to the trans-Golgi network (TGN) and is
CC       involved in endosome-to-plasma membrane transport for cargo protein
CC       recycling. The SNX3-retromer mediates the retrograde endosome-to-TGN
CC       transport of WLS distinct from the SNX-BAR retromer pathway. The SNX27-
CC       retromer is believed to be involved in endosome-to-plasma membrane
CC       trafficking and recycling of a broad spectrum of cargo proteins. The
CC       CSC seems to act as recruitment hub for other proteins, such as the
CC       WASH complex and TBC1D5. Required to regulate transcytosis of the
CC       polymeric immunoglobulin receptor (pIgR-pIgA) (PubMed:15247922,
CC       PubMed:21725319, PubMed:23563491). In the endosomes, retriever complex
CC       drives the retrieval and recycling of NxxY-motif-containing cargo
CC       proteins by coupling to SNX17, a cargo essential for the homeostatic
CC       maintenance of numerous cell surface proteins associated with processes
CC       that include cell migration, cell adhesion, nutrient supply and cell
CC       signaling (PubMed:28892079, PubMed:39587083). The recruitment of the
CC       retriever complex to the endosomal membrane involves CCC and WASH
CC       complexes (PubMed:28892079). Involved in GLUT1 endosome-to-plasma
CC       membrane trafficking; the function is dependent of association with
CC       ANKRD27 (PubMed:24856514). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24856514,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28892079, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37172566,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38062209, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38459129,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39587083, ECO:0000303|PubMed:15247922,
CC       ECO:0000303|PubMed:21725319, ECO:0000303|PubMed:23563491}.

CC       selective complex (CSC) mediates the exit of human papillomavirus from
CC       the early endosome and the delivery to the Golgi apparatus.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25693203}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the commander complex consisting of the CCC
CC       subcomplex and the retriever subcomplex (PubMed:37172566,
CC       PubMed:28892079, PubMed:39587083, PubMed:38062209, PubMed:38459129).
CC       Component of the heterotrimeric retriever complex formed by VPS26C,
CC       VPS29 and VPS35L; within the complex interacts with VPS35L
CC       (PubMed:37172566, PubMed:28892079, PubMed:31712251, PubMed:39587083,
CC       PubMed:38062209, PubMed:38459129). Component of the heterotrimeric
CC       retromer cargo-selective complex (CSC), also described as vacuolar
CC       protein sorting subcomplex (VPS), formed by VPS26 (VPS26A or VPS26B),
CC       VPS29 and VPS35 (PubMed:11102511, PubMed:16737443, PubMed:17891154,
CC       PubMed:28892079). The CSC has a highly elongated structure with VPS26
CC       and VPS29 binding independently at opposite distal ends of VPS35 as
CC       central platform (By similarity). The CSC is believed to associate with
CC       variable sorting nexins to form functionally distinct retromer complex
CC       variants. The originally described retromer complex (also called SNX-
CC       BAR retromer) is a pentamer containing the CSC and a heterodimeric
CC       membrane-deforming subcomplex formed between SNX1 or SNX2 and SNX5 or
CC       SNX6 (also called SNX-BAR subcomplex); the respective CSC and SNX-BAR
CC       subcomplexes associate with low affinity. The CSC associates with SNX3
CC       to form a SNX3-retromer complex. The CSC associates with SNX27, the
CC       WASH complex and the SNX-BAR subcomplex to form the SNX27-retromer
CC       complex (PubMed:21725319, PubMed:23563491). Interacts with VPS26A,
CC       VPS35, SNX1, SNX2, SNX3, SNX27, WASHC5 (PubMed:11102511,
CC       PubMed:20923837, PubMed:23563491, PubMed:28892079, PubMed:30213940).
CC       Interacts with TBC1D5; this interaction is blocked by VPS35L in the
CC       retriever complex (PubMed:23331060, PubMed:37172566). Interacts with
CC       SNX17; the interaction is indirect; SNX17 (via its C-terminus)
CC       interacts with the retriever complex (via VPS26C and VPS35L)
CC       (PubMed:39587083). Interacts with VPS26B and ANKRD27 (By similarity).
