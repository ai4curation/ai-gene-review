# CXCL8 (Interleukin-8 / C-X-C motif chemokine 8) Curation Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol:** `CXCL8` (formerly `IL8`)
- **UniProtKB:** `P10145`
- **PANTHER family / subfamily:** `PANTHER:PTHR12015` (`SMALL INDUCIBLE CYTOKINE A`) / `PANTHER:PTHR12015:SF200` (`INTERLEUKIN-8`)
- **Proteolytic processing & proteoforms:** Synthesized as a 99-amino-acid precursor with a 22-residue N-terminal signal peptide (residues 1-22). Cleavage yields the 77-residue secreted form `CXCL8(1-77)` (residues 23-99, `[AVLPR]SAKELRCQCI...`), which undergoes N-terminal proteolytic processing by extracellular proteases (including thrombin, plasmin, MMP-9, PRTN3, and ELANE) into more potent N-terminally truncated isoforms such as `CXCL8(6-77)` (72 residues, `SAKELRCQCI...`, the major monocyte-derived form) and `CXCL8(9-77)` (`ELRCQCI...`), retaining the essential N-terminal `ELR` (`Glu-Leu-Arg`) motif immediately preceding the `CXC` disulfide-bridged core (`PMID:1891716`, `PMID:8399143`, `PMID:36725964`).

## Receptor Binding and Signaling Pathway (`GO:0038112`)
- **Cognate signaling receptors:** `CXCR1` (`IL8RA`, `UniProtKB:P25024`) and `CXCR2` (`IL8RB`, `UniProtKB:P25025`) (`PMID:1840701`, `PMID:8662698`, `PMID:32610344`, `PMID:37433790`).
- **Structural mechanism:** Cryo-EM structures of human `CXCL8` bound to `CXCR2–Gi` (`PDB:6LFO`, `PDB:6LFM`; `PMID:32610344`) and `CXCR1–Gi` (`PDB:8IC0`; `PMID:37433790`), together with solution NMR studies (`PMID:29143165`), define a two-site binding mechanism:
  1. **Chemokine recognition site I (CRS1):** The N-loop and third beta-strand of `CXCL8` dock onto the N-terminal extracellular domain of `CXCR1`/`CXCR2`, conferring binding affinity and receptor specificity.
  2. **Chemokine recognition site II (CRS2):** The N-terminal `ELR` motif (`Glu4-Leu5-Arg6` of mature 72-residue IL-8) inserts into the orthosteric transmembrane pocket of `CXCR1`/`CXCR2`, engaging `Arg203`/`Arg199` (ECL2) and `Asp265`/`Asp261` (TM6) to allosterically trigger outward movement of TM6 and coupling to heterotrimeric `Gi` (`GNAI1`/`GNAI2`/`GNAI3`) (`PMID:32610344`, `PMID:37433790`).
- **Glycosaminoglycan (GAG) immobilization:** `CXCL8` binds heparin and endothelial heparan sulfate proteoglycans (`GO:0008201`) via basic residues in its C-terminal alpha-helix and proximal N-loop, establishing immobilized intravascular and interstitial chemotactic gradients required for neutrophil arrest, transendothelial migration, and directional chemotaxis (`PMID:11564821`, `PMID:24501198`, `PMID:36725964`).
- **Atypical receptor interaction:** `CXCL8` also binds the non-signaling atypical chemokine receptor `ACKR1` (`DARC`) on erythrocytes and post-capillary venular endothelium, which acts as a chemokine buffer/sink and mediates endothelial transcytosis (`Reactome:R-HSA-374251`, `PMID:36725964`).

## Key Curation Decisions
1. **Signaling pathway (`GO:0038112`):** `CXCL8` already carries `GO:0038112` (*interleukin-8-mediated signaling pathway*, `IDA` from `PMID:8399143`), which is `ACCEPT`ed as a core function and additionally supported by `PMID:1840701`, `PMID:8662698`, `PMID:32610344`, and `PMID:37433790`. Broad parent signaling terms (`GO:0007165` *signal transduction*, `GO:0007186` *G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway*, `GO:0035556` *intracellular signal transduction*) are `MODIFY`ed to `GO:0038112`.
2. **Molecular functions (`GO:0008009`, `GO:0005153`, `GO:0008201`):** `GO:0008009` (*chemokine activity*), `GO:0005153` (*interleukin-8 receptor binding*), and `GO:0008201` (*heparin binding*) are `ACCEPT`ed as core molecular functions. More general parent terms `GO:0005125` (*cytokine activity*) and `GO:0045236` (*CXCR chemokine receptor binding*) are `MODIFY`ed to `GO:0008009` and `GO:0005153`, respectively.
3. **Mis-attributed `PMID:25411203` annotations (`GO:0016020` *membrane* and `GO:0045236`):** Full-text inspection of `PMID:25411203` (*Cutting edge: GPR35/CXCR8 is the receptor of the mucosal chemokine CXCL17*) confirms that the paper characterizes `CXCL17` and the membrane GPCR `GPR35` (which the authors propose naming `CXCR8`), not `CXCL8` (IL-8). Because `CXCL8` is a soluble secreted protein (`GO:0005576`) and not a membrane protein, `GO:0016020` (*membrane*) is `REMOVE`d.
4. **PANTHER PAINT (`PTN002564680` in `PTHR12015`):** `PTN002564680` (the root chordate chemokine node in `PTHR12015`) propagates `GO:0008009` (*chemokine activity*), `GO:0045236` (*CXCR chemokine receptor binding*), `GO:0030593` (*neutrophil chemotaxis*), `GO:0006954` (*inflammatory response*), `GO:0071222` (*cellular response to lipopolysaccharide*), `GO:0061844` (*antimicrobial humoral immune response mediated by antimicrobial peptide*), and `GO:0005576` (*extracellular region*), but lacks an IBD for `GO:0070098` (*chemokine-mediated signaling pathway*); furthermore, `GO:0038112` (*interleukin-8-mediated signaling pathway*) should be scoped specifically to `PANTHER:PTHR12015:SF200` (`INTERLEUKIN-8`).
