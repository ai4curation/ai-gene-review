CC   -!- FUNCTION: Factor Xa is a vitamin K-dependent glycoprotein that converts
CC       prothrombin to thrombin in the presence of factor Va, calcium and
CC       phospholipid during blood clotting (PubMed:22409427, PubMed:39880037).
CC       Factor Xa activates pro-inflammatory signaling pathways in a protease-
CC       activated receptor (PAR)-dependent manner (PubMed:24041930,
CC       PubMed:30568593, PubMed:34831181, PubMed:18202198). Up-regulates
CC       expression of protease-activated receptors (PARs) F2R, F2RL1 and F2RL2
CC       in dermal microvascular endothelial cells (PubMed:35738824). Triggers
CC       the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines, such as MCP-1/CCL2 and
CC       IL6, in cardiac fibroblasts and umbilical vein endothelial cells in
CC       PAR-1/F2R-dependent manner (PubMed:30568593, PubMed:34831181). Triggers
CC       the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines, such as MCP-1/CCL2, IL6,
CC       TNF/TNF, IL-1beta/IL1B, IL8/CXCL8 and IL18, in endothelial cells and
CC       atrial tissues (PubMed:24041930, PubMed:35738824, PubMed:9780208).
CC       Induces expression of adhesion molecules, such as ICAM1, VCAM1 and
CC       SELE, in endothelial cells and atrial tissues (PubMed:24041930,
CC       PubMed:35738824, PubMed:9780208). Increases expression of
CC       phosphorylated ERK1/2 in dermal microvascular endothelial cells and
CC       atrial tissues (PubMed:24041930, PubMed:35738824). Triggers activation
CC       of the transcription factor NF-kappa-B in dermal microvascular
CC       endothelial cells and atrial tissues (PubMed:24041930,
CC       PubMed:35738824). Activates pro-inflammatory and pro-fibrotic responses
CC       in dermal fibroblasts and enhances wound healing probably via PAR-
CC       2/F2RL1-dependent mechanism (PubMed:18202198). Activates barrier
CC       protective signaling responses in endothelial cells in PAR-2/F2RL1-
CC       dependent manner; the activity depends on the cleavage of PAR-2/F2RL1
CC       by factor Xa (PubMed:22409427). Up-regulates expression of plasminogen
