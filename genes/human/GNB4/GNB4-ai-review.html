Gene Description

GNB4 is a 340-residue WD-repeat beta subunit of heterotrimeric G proteins. Together with gamma and alpha subunits, it supports GPCR signaling and effector regulation. GNB4 is present in peripheral-nerve axons and Schwann cells. Dominant missense variants can impair signaling and cause Charcot-Marie-Tooth neuropathy.

Biological synthesis

GNB4 provides the beta-subunit assembly and signaling interface in alpha–beta–gamma G-protein complexes, coupling GPCR activation to downstream responses. [PMID:11842130; PMID:23434117]

Official UniProt declares one 340-residue product and a processed chain spanning residues 2–340. This processing is not an alternative splice product. [UniProt:Q9HAV0]

Methods, Results and Figure 4 identify human clone AF300648.1 in COS7 cells. Wild type enhances IP3 signaling, but not maximal calcium response comparably. Separate HEK293T stability assays do not support mutant degradation. PMID:23434117

Human beta4/gamma2/alphaoA expression in rat neurons “resulted in heterotrimer formation.” PMID:11842130
