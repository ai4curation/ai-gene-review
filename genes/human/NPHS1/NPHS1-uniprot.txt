CC   -!- FUNCTION: Seems to play a role in the development or function of the
CC       kidney glomerular filtration barrier. Regulates glomerular vascular
CC       permeability. May anchor the podocyte slit diaphragm to the actin
CC       cytoskeleton. Plays a role in skeletal muscle formation through
CC       regulation of myoblast fusion (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9QZS7, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R044}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Self-associates (via the Ig-like domains) (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with CD2AP (via C-terminal domain) (By similarity). Interacts
CC       with NPHS2 (PubMed:11562357). Interacts with MAGI1 (via PDZ 2 and 3
CC       domains) forming a tripartite complex with IGSF5/JAM4 (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with DDN; the interaction is direct (By similarity). Also
CC       interacts (via the Ig-like domains) with KIRREL1/NEPH1 and KIRREL2; the
CC       interaction with KIRREL1 is dependent on KIRREL1 glycosylation (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with KIRREL3 (By similarity). Forms a complex
CC       with ACTN4, CASK, IQGAP1, MAGI2, SPTAN1 and SPTBN1 (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase regulatory subunit PIK3R1;
CC       the interaction is reduced by high glucose levels (PubMed:28955049).
CC       Interacts (via N-terminus) with GAPVD1 (via Ras-GAP or VPS9 domains)
CC       (PubMed:29959197). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9QZS7,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R044, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11562357,
