# FERMT1 integrated evidence guide

Kindlin-1's central mechanism is beta-integrin-tail binding at focal adhesions, with cooperation in integrin activation and cell-matrix attachment. The core uses integrin binding (GO:0005178), focal adhesion (GO:0005925), positive regulation of integrin activation (GO:0033625), and cell-matrix adhesion (GO:0007160). The two process terms have no ancestor/descendant relationship under the inspected strict is_a/part_of hierarchy. Focal adhesion is a cellular anatomical entity, not a protein-containing complex.

The original human evidence in [PMID:14634021](../../../publications/PMID_14634021.md) supports endogenous focal-adhesion/conditional ruffle localization and beta-integrin-tail association. The association assay uses cell lysate, so it is not described as a two-purified-protein affinity measurement. Its kindlin-alone activation result is distinguished from talin cooperation in [PMID:19804783](../../../publications/PMID_19804783.md). The latter source separates human functional constructs from mouse purified structural domains. Human keratinocyte evidence in [PMID:21356350](../../../publications/PMID_21356350.md) supports overlapping kindlin-1/kindlin-2 contributions to integrin activation and attachment; overlapping functions do not imply interchangeability.

The [direct source guide](FERMT1-direct-source-guide.md) records the actual original Methods/Results recovered for the three direct publication groups, including limitations of cached full-text flags. The [database source guide](FERMT1-database-source-guide.md) records eleven mouse donor chains, the three literal PAINT joins, InterPro/rule provenance and the additional original evidence. Full manual reference objects are preserved as whole objects in the integrated review. No abstract is silently rewritten into full text.

The [direct decision table](FERMT1-direct-decisions.yaml) and [database decision table](FERMT1-database-decisions.yaml) retain complete source keys and zero-based indices. All 31 approved review successors are preserved. Broad migration row 14 is TOO_GENERAL because the separately retained positive row 29 carries its verified strict descendant GO:1903691. The latter remains non-core, not withdrawn. TGF-beta production row 24 is refined to the already present signaling-regulation term GO:0030511; it does not create a new annotation.

Contextual Wnt/TGF-beta signaling, hair cycling, stem-cell behavior, basement-membrane organization, polarity and migration are kept outside the narrow core. The donor experiments and human corroboration remain distinguished. Nuclear import and direct actin-filament binding remain UNDECIDED where the recovered source does not isolate those specific mechanisms; neither uncertainty negates positive observed localization or signaling.

The [phylogeny evidence](FERMT1-phylogeny-evidence.yaml) preserves actual family labels and positive IBD strings without pretending that the failed tree/MSA requests verified ancestry. The [donor-chain evidence](FERMT1-propagation-evidence.yaml) exposes exact orthology sources. Target self-WITH is expected and donor count is not used as a quality metric.

The [product map](FERMT1-database-product-map.yaml) preserves all four natural splice products and exact sequence-reconstruction facts. No new processed product or common activity for every splice form is inferred.

All 21 normal sources are retained byte-for-byte: the 19 accepted intake files, exact main PMID24681597 and genuinely fetched PMID16876785. Ten are current reference/family dependencies and eleven preserve the intake discovery record.
