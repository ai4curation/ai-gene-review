CC   -!- FUNCTION: DNA-binding transcription factor that specifically binds to
CC       the upstream regulatory region of type I interferon (IFN) and IFN-
CC       inducible genes and regulates their expression (PubMed:39013473,
CC       PubMed:7687740). Mainly acts as a transcription repressor, repressing
CC       expression (PubMed:39013473). Also acts as an activator for several
CC       genes including H4 and IL7 (PubMed:15226432, PubMed:9540062).
CC       Constitutively binds to the ISRE promoter to activate IL7
CC       (PubMed:15226432). Involved in cell cycle regulation through binding
CC       the site II (HiNF-M) promoter region of H4 and activating transcription
CC       during cell growth (PubMed:9540062). Antagonizes IRF1 transcriptional
CC       activation (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P23906,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15226432, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39013473,
