CC   -!- FUNCTION: Organic zwitterion/cation transporter with apparent
CC       specificity for amino acids and their derivatives. Has low affinity for
CC       its substrates and may regulate their flux across the plasma membrane
CC       at high substrate concentrations (PubMed:33124720). Bidirectionally
CC       transports carnitine and acetylcarnitine, possibly regulating their
CC       cytosolic abundance and further fatty acid catabolism via beta
CC       oxidation (PubMed:33124720). Displays high transport activity toward
CC       zwitterionic substrates such as glycine betaine and diet-derived
CC       ergothioneine and carnosine. Can transport cations having an indole
CC       skeleton such as thiamine with lower efficiency. Does not transport
CC       agmatine (PubMed:15028572, PubMed:33124720). The transport mechanism,
CC       symport with sodium or facilitated diffusion allosterically regulated
CC       by sodium, remains to be elucidated (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15028572, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33124720,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:33124720}.
