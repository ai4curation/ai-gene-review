CC   -!- FUNCTION: May be involved in transcriptional regulation. May mediate
CC       some of the neuroprotective peptide VIP-associated effects involving
CC       normal growth and cancer proliferation. Positively modulates WNT-beta-
CC       catenin/CTNN1B signaling, acting by regulating phosphorylation of, and
CC       thereby stabilizing, CTNNB1. May be required for neural induction and
CC       neuronal differentiation. May be involved in erythroid differentiation
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9Z103}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via N-terminal region) with beta-catenin/CTNNB1
CC       (via the central armadillo domains); interaction is direct and
CC       stabilizes CTNNB1 by modulating its phosphorylation by glycogen
CC       synthase kinase-3 beta GSK3B. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9Z103}.
