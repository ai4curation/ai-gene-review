# PADI3 notes

## 2026-11-28 manual review

PADI3 is the hair-follicle/epidermal PADI family enzyme and catalyzes calcium-dependent peptidyl-arginine deimination. Human loss-of-function work placed PADI3, the transglutaminase TGM3, and their substrate TCHH in the same hair-shaft pathway [PMID:27866708, "The two enzymes PADI3 and TGM3, responsible for posttranslational protein modifications, and their target structural protein TCHH are all involved in hair shaft formation"]. The mouse PAD cloning paper cited by one TAS row is adequate as legacy support for the conserved PAD chemistry, but it does not add a human PADI3-specific role beyond the curated family/electronic support.

The PANTHER nucleus row appears to be inherited from the PADI4 histone branch without direct evidence that PADI3 has the same nuclear role. The seven bare `protein binding` rows come from interactome capture and were removed under the protein-binding policy. The two `identical protein binding` rows were kept as peripheral support because the PADI3 UniProt interaction block records repeated self-interaction experiments.

The PADI3 HPA GO_REF:0000052 annotation is an IDA Human Protein Atlas immunofluorescence curation to GO:0005829 cytosol; this supports resolving diffuse cytoplasmic localization to the cytosol child term.
