# MAFA notes

`just deep-research human MAFA --provider perplexity` failed because the provider was not available in this checkout, and retrying the available `claude-code` provider failed because Claude Code was not logged in. I therefore reviewed the cached GOA, UniProtKB, PANTHER and publication evidence manually.

MAFA is a nuclear large-Maf bZIP transcription factor in pancreatic beta-cell transcriptional programs. UniProt records activation of insulin gene expression, binding to the insulin C1/RIPE3b enhancer element, binding to TRE-type MARE DNA, and homodimer or heterodimer formation.

PMID:12011435 cloned human `mafA` and reports that hMafA "can specifically bind the insulin enhancer element RIPE3b and activate insulin-gene expression." PMID:12368292 independently identified MafA as a beta-cell-specific, glucose-regulated RIPE3b-binding activator and showed strong insulin-promoter activation in reporter assays.

PMID:12917329 is mouse beta-cell work but is useful for the chromatin/nuclear interpretation because it reports MafA ChIP within the endogenous insulin enhancer and that MafA, MafB and c-Maf can each bind and activate insulin C1 element-mediated expression.

PMID:17941991 used an engineered liver-cell context. It shows that beta-cell transcription factors including MafA can drive production and secretion of insulin after adenoviral expression in Hepa1-6 or primary liver cells, while L-arginine stimulates secretion through a nitric-oxide-dependent pathway in those engineered cells. This supports MAFA transcription-factor activity in the assay, but not a direct endogenous role for the MAFA protein in the insulin secretion process.
