# ARPC1B: literature and annotation assessment

## Structural role in a regulated actin-nucleating complex

The human platelet study identified p41-Arc by peptide sequencing and cDNA cloning, with clone 527050 listed in the Methods. Its Results distinguish p41-Arc from the previously identified human Sop2 homologue; the two are related proteins, not interchangeable names for the same experimental reagent. The p41/p40 bands yielded identical peptide maps, and their summed abundance supported a stoichiometric subunit of the seven-protein assembly. The paper reports “the seven polypeptide subunits of the complex are present in a 1:1:1:1:1:1:1 stoichiometry”. Its cellular imaging used other subunits, including Arp3, p34 and p21; this is complex-level localization, not direct ARPC1B immunofluorescence. [PMID:9230079, original Methods and p41 Results](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.138.2.375).

The reconstitution paper provides complementary functional evidence: its complete abstract explicitly describes expression of the human seven-subunit complex in insect cells and distinct p41 effects on nucleation and stimulation. It assigns the strongest filament-binding role to p34/p20. Full original Methods were not recovered through the inspected publisher/author routes. The existing ARPC1B filament-binding annotation is therefore retained as a contribution within the complex, not an isolated p41 binding interface. The core likewise records contributes_to_molecular_function and does not turn ARPC1B into an autonomous nucleator. [PMID:11741539](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1097-2765(01)00393-8).

## LegK2 recognition: a specific interaction outside the core function

The original PMID:25944859 Figure 1 Results and vector/assay Methods resolve the human target. ARPC1B and ACTR3 constructs came from the human ORFeome resource, and ARPC1B was positive in binary yeast assays and affinity copurification from HEK293T cells. The latter result includes the literal passage “Flag-tagged ARPC1B (45 kDa), but not Flag-tagged ARP3, was copurified”. GST and LegK1 controls, plus continued ARPC1B binding to kinase-dead LegK2, distinguish the interaction from catalytic activity. The initial library bait showed residual self-activation despite 3-aminotriazole; that limitation is kept separate from the subsequent targeted tests.
