Gene Description

ARPC1B is the p41-Arc WD-repeat subunit of the Arp2/3 complex, a seven-subunit actin-nucleating assembly. It contributes structural organization and regulated activity of the complex, supporting branched actin networks and cortical actin organization in human cells. Complexes containing ARPC1B have distinct assembly and disassembly properties from ARPC1A-containing complexes. ARPC1B acts within the cytoplasmic actin machinery rather than as an autonomous actin nucleator.

Core Functions

ARPC1B supplies a structural subunit of the Arp2/3 complex that supports branched actin-network assembly and contributes to mother-filament engagement. In human cortical actin systems it enables organization of protrusive and cortical networks as part of this complex, rather than acting as an autonomous nucleator.

Structural role in a regulated actin-nucleating complex

The human platelet study identified p41-Arc by peptide sequencing and cDNA cloning, with clone 527050 listed in the Methods.
