CC   -!- FUNCTION: ATP-gated nonselective transmembrane cation channel that
CC       requires high millimolar concentrations of ATP for activation
CC       (PubMed:17483156, PubMed:25281740, PubMed:9038151). Upon ATP binding,
CC       it rapidly opens to allow the influx of small cations Na(+) and Ca(2+),
CC       and the K(+) efflux (PubMed:17483156, PubMed:20453110, PubMed:28235784,
CC       PubMed:39262850). Also has the ability to form a large pore in the cell
CC       membrane, allowing the passage of large cationic molecules
CC       (PubMed:17483156). In microglia, may mediate NADPH transport across the
CC       plasma membrane (PubMed:39142135). In immune cells, P2RX7 acts as a
CC       molecular sensor in pathological inflammatory states by detecting and
CC       responding to high local concentrations of extracellar ATP. In
CC       microglial cells, P2RX7 activation leads to the release of pro-
CC       inflammatory cytokines, such as IL-1beta and IL-18, through the
CC       activation of the NLRP3 inflammasome and caspase-1 (PubMed:26877061).
CC       Cooperates with KCNK6 to activate NLRP3 inflammasome (By similarity).
CC       Activates death pathways leading to apoptosis and autophagy
CC       (PubMed:21821797, PubMed:23303206, PubMed:28326637). Activates death
CC       pathways leading to pyroptosis (By similarity).
