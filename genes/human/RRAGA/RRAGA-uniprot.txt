CC   -!- FUNCTION: Guanine nucleotide-binding protein that plays a crucial role
CC       in the cellular response to amino acid availability through regulation
CC       of the mTORC1 signaling cascade (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:24095279,
CC       PubMed:25936802, PubMed:31601708, PubMed:31601764, PubMed:38103557).
CC       Forms heterodimeric Rag complexes with RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD and
CC       cycles between an inactive GDP-bound and an active GTP-bound form:
CC       RagA/RRAGA is in its active form when GTP-bound RagA/RRAGA forms a
CC       complex with GDP-bound RagC/RRAGC (or RagD/RRAGD) and in an inactive
CC       form when GDP-bound RagA/RRAGA heterodimerizes with GTP-bound
CC       RagC/RRAGC (or RagD/RRAGD) (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:24095279,
CC       PubMed:25936802, PubMed:31601708, PubMed:31601764, PubMed:32868926). In
CC       its GTP-bound active form, promotes the recruitment of mTORC1 to the
CC       lysosomes and its subsequent activation by the GTPase RHEB
CC       (PubMed:20381137, PubMed:25936802, PubMed:31601708, PubMed:31601764).
CC       Involved in the RCC1/Ran-GTPase pathway (PubMed:9394008). May play a
CC       direct role in a TNF signaling pathway leading to induction of cell
CC       death (PubMed:8995684). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20381137,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24095279, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25936802,
