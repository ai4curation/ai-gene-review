CC   -!- FUNCTION: Pore-forming subunit of two major inward rectifying Ca(2+)
CC       channels at the plasma membrane: Ca(2+) release-activated Ca(2+) (CRAC)
CC       channels and arachidonate-regulated Ca(2+)-selective (ARC) channels
CC       (PubMed:16807233, PubMed:17442569, PubMed:19182790, PubMed:19622606,
CC       PubMed:19706554, PubMed:20354224, PubMed:32415068). Assembles with
CC       ORAI1 and ORAI2 to form hexameric CRAC channels that mediate Ca(2+)
CC       influx upon depletion of endoplasmic reticulum Ca(2+) store and channel
CC       activation by Ca(2+) sensor STIM1, a process known as store-operated
CC       Ca(2+) entry (SOCE). Various pore subunit combinations may account for
CC       distinct CRAC channel spatiotemporal and cell-type specific dynamics.
CC       ORAI1 mainly contributes to the generation of Ca(2+) plateaus involved
CC       in sustained Ca(2+) entry and is dispensable for cytosolic Ca(2+)
CC       oscillations, whereas ORAI2 and ORAI3 generate oscillatory patterns.
CC       CRAC channels assemble in Ca(2+) signaling microdomains where Ca(2+)
CC       influx is coupled to calmodulin and calcineurin signaling and
CC       activation of NFAT transcription factors recruited to ORAI1 via AKAP5.
CC       CRAC channels are the main pathway for Ca(2+) influx in T cells and
CC       promote the immune response to pathogens by activating NFAT-dependent
CC       cytokine and chemokine transcription (PubMed:16807233, PubMed:17442569,
CC       PubMed:19182790, PubMed:19706554, PubMed:20354224, PubMed:32415068).
CC       Assembles with ORAI1 to form channels that mediate store-independent
CC       Ca(2+) influx in response to inflammatory metabolites arachidonate or
CC       its derivative leukotriene C4, termed ARC and LRC channels respectively
CC       (PubMed:19622606, PubMed:32415068). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16807233,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17442569, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19182790,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19622606, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19706554,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20354224, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32415068}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: CRAC channels are regulated by fast Ca(2+)-
CC       dependent inactivation (FCDI), a mechanism that limits Ca(2+) influx
CC       and cell toxicity. ORAI3 subunit displays prominent FCDI
CC       (PubMed:19706554). Inhibited by lanthanides such as Gd(3+) ions
CC       (PubMed:16807233). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16807233,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19706554}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Oligomerizes in homomeric and heteromeric ORAI complexes.
CC       Native CRAC channels most likely consist of hexameric ORAI heteromers,
CC       implying that diverse ORAI1, ORAI2 and ORAI3 subunit combinations with
CC       distinct biophysical properties can operate in a cell-type specific way
CC       (PubMed:17442569, PubMed:32415068). ARC channels are heteropentamers
CC       consisting of three ORAI1 and two ORAI3 subunits (PubMed:19622606).
CC       Interacts with STIM1; this regulates channel activity (PubMed:19706554,
CC       PubMed:32415068). Interacts with CRACR2A/EFCAB4B.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17442569, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19622606,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19706554, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20418871,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32415068}.
