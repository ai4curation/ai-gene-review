# DSE evidence notes

DSE directly changes uronic-acid stereochemistry within chondroitin chains, generating iduronic-acid-containing dermatan sulfate. One catalytic core combines chondroitin-glucuronate 5-epimerase activity with dermatan-sulfate proteoglycan biosynthesis at the Golgi membrane. The positive ER-associated pool remains explicit in the biological description and annotations. The [source guide](DSE-source-guide.md) links the original evidence and distinguishes repository cache availability from separate original access.

## Chemistry and physiological participation

[PMID:16505484](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16505484/) identifies the bovine spleen enzyme and separately assays sequence-verified human full-length SART2/DSE with a C-terminal His tag. Tritium release and carbon-14 product mapping establish the GlcA-to-IdoA reaction on chondroitin. Metabolic labeling additionally shows increased iduronic acid and iduronate-rich blocks in cellular CS/DS chains. DSE therefore performs the chemical modification itself; its biosynthetic role does not rest only on necessity or a disease phenotype.

[PMID:19004833](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19004833/) tests tagged human wild-type and mutant proteins and shows that N-glycosylation supports activity. Its proposed 2009 active-site mechanism is a model, not the same evidential claim as the measured net epimerization. The later human structure in [PMID:33815739](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8006597/) revises that model and identifies a metal-binding environment. The structure is not a substrate cocrystal. DSE's own glycosylation does not make it a glycosyltransferase, and a proposed transient bond-cleavage step does not establish net glycan-degrading lyase activity. These features belong to the same catalytic mechanism rather than separate core functions.

The electronic heparan-sulfate pathway assignment is rejected on a positive substrate distinction. The original [PMID:7092807](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1163673/) compares the dermatan/chondroitin and heparan epimerase systems under their respective substrate, pH and cofactor conditions. [EC 5.1.3.19](https://iubmb.qmul.ac.uk/enzyme/EC5/1/3/19.html) and [RHEA:21084](https://www.rhea-db.org/rhea/21084) describe the DSE reaction; [GLCE/O94923](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/O94923/entry) instead acts on N-sulfated heparosan under [EC 5.1.3.17](https://iubmb.qmul.ac.uk/enzyme/EC5/1/3/17.html). The current DSE UniProt pathway statements do contain the suspect heparan assignment, so it is documented as a target-source mapping error rather than silently ignored. The heparan pathway itself is valid. The Rhea chondroitin 4′-sulfate equation does not imply an exclusive sulfation requirement, because primary DSE assays also use unsulfated chondroitin.

## Localization, products and experimental constructs

The original [PMID:10679095](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10679095/) studies human SART2/DSE fused C-terminally to GFP. COS7 microscopy and sucrose-gradient fractionation support a predominant ER and partial Golgi distribution. Human MCF-7 transfectants show a similar organelle pattern but lack the vesicle pattern seen in COS7. These are positive tagged-human-protein observations in specified hosts, not direct measurements of untagged native occupancy. Broad ER and Golgi assertions are refined to their supported membrane compartments while contextual vesicle annotations are retained as non-core. Hydrophobicity predictions are not experimentally resolved membrane topology.

All five scanned pages of PMID:7092807 were inspected. Its enzyme preparation is from cultured human skin fibroblasts, whereas bovine, chick and rat materials have other substrate or reagent roles. More than 90% of activity is recovered in membrane fractions; the highest specific activity is in the microsomal pellet, and detergent plus salt solubilizes a substantial fraction. This supports membrane association but does not separate ER from Golgi. The study predates molecular DSE/DSEL identification, so later recombinant human DSE work supplies the gene-level connection. The HPA XML inspected for DSE contains no subcellular assay section and supplies no additional localization claim.

The [official Q9UL01 record](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9UL01/entry) contains one displayed 958-residue sequence and no alternative-products block. Signal peptide 1–22 is sequence-predicted; the annotated processed chain spans 23–958. The soluble 23–775 and 23–894 proteins used in the structural study are experimental constructs, not natural splice products. Only the 23–775 construct yielded the catalytic-domain crystal; the extension toward the membrane used cross-link-constrained modeling. No distinct natural-product function or obligatory oligomer is inferred.

## Phylogenetic and pathway evidence

The PTHR15532 target alignment matches all 958 residues of Q9UL01. Its human leaf PTN002496188 descends from the recorded IBD node PTN001036588 along a 17-node path. Three literal IBDs cover epimerase activity, dermatan-sulfate metabolism and chondroitin-sulfate metabolism. Their source identities are preserved: bovine P0C2H4 and human Q9UL01 provide target-family activity grounding, while MGI:2442948/Q0VBN2 is mouse Dsel, not mouse Dse. A shared ancestral function can be supported across those paralogs; neither donor count nor legitimate target self-inclusion is a failure.

The complete abstract of [PMID:22496542](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22496542/) reports reduced brain CS/DS iduronic acid and altered sulfation after mouse Dsel loss. Full original access was unavailable, so no unseen assay or supplementary detail is attributed to that source. The direct human DSE reaction and cellular chain modifications independently support the target's biosynthetic function. The broad dermatan metabolism IBA is refined to biosynthesis; chondroitin-chain metabolism remains valid because DSE chemically modifies that chain rather than merely consuming an unrelated substrate.

The actual [ARBA00095326 rule](https://www.uniprot.org/arba/ARBA00095326) matches both required FunFam identifiers and the Primates lineage in the official target record. Reactome's [DS-GAG pathway](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2022923) contains the [epimerization reaction](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2022052), whose catalyst links through the DSE/DSEL alternative-enzyme set to Q9UL01 at the Golgi membrane. That set does not establish a required DSE–DSEL complex. The inspected GO-CAM index has no DSE activity; absence alone is not an annotation gap.
