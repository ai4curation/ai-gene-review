Gene Description

ASDURF (ASNSD1 upstream open reading frame protein) is a 96-amino-acid microprotein translated from an upstream open reading frame (uORF) in the 5' leader of the ASNSD1 mRNA, upstream of and separate from the ASNSD1 main coding sequence. The peptide was first detected by peptidomics in K562 cells, at roughly 10-2000 copies per cell, and a tagged construct localizes to the cytoplasm. ASDURF is structurally related to beta-prefoldins. It is a subunit of the PAQosome (R2TP/prefoldin-like complex), a cytoplasmic chaperone assembly involved in the biogenesis of several multiprotein complexes. Within it, ASDURF joins PFDN2, PFDN6, UXT, URI1 and PDRG1 to form the heterohexameric prefoldin-like module. Purified ASDURF binds the prefoldin-like complex directly. ASDURF is upregulated in MYC- and MYCN-driven medulloblastoma and is required for the survival of MYC-driven medulloblastoma cells, where its loss phenocopies loss of prefoldin-like subunits. Loss of ASDURF or PFDN2 causes overlapping post-transcriptional changes in the proteome, with cell-cycle proteins prominent among them. No catalytic or chaperone activity has been shown for ASDURF itself.

Core Functions

Structural subunit of the prefoldin-like module of the PAQosome (R2TP/prefoldin-like chaperone complex) in the cytoplasm. ASDURF is a beta-prefoldin-like peptide that, together with PFDN2, PFDN6, UXT, URI1 and PDRG1, forms the heterohexameric prefoldin-like module. No molecular function term is assigned: no chaperone or other activity has been shown for ASDURF on its own, and bare protein binding would not be informative.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does ASDURF replace a canonical beta-prefoldin position in the prefoldin-like hexamer (making it obligatory for PAQosome assembly), or is it a substoichiometric or exchangeable subunit?

Q: Which PAQosome client complexes (for example RNA polymerase II, snoRNPs, PIKK complexes) depend specifically on ASDURF, and does that explain the post-transcriptional cell-cycle proteome signature seen after its loss?

Q: Does translation of the ASDURF uORF also regulate translation of the downstream ASNSD1 coding sequence in cis, independent of the peptide's trans function?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Assemble the prefoldin-like module from recombinant subunits with and without ASDURF, and test assembly and client binding (for example RPB5/POLR2E or unfolded model substrates). This would show whether ASDURF is structurally required and whether the module has holdase activity.

Experiment: Delete ASDURF without changing the ASNSD1 coding sequence (start-codon mutation in the endogenous locus). Then measure PAQosome integrity and the assembly of known client complexes by quantitative proteomics.

ASDURF (L0R819) review notes

2026-10-03 — initial review (MICROPROTEINS Tier 2)

Identity. ASDURF ("ASNSD1 upstream open reading frame protein", HGNC:53619) is a
96-aa peptide encoded by an upstream ORF in the 5' leader of the ASNSD1 mRNA. It is a
separate UniProt entry (L0R819) with its own HGNC symbol, so it has its own folder.
UniProt lists the host ASNSD1 protein (Q9NWL6) as the "external" alternative product.

Literature found (PubMed E-utilities, 2026-10-03). Query
ASDURF OR "ASNSD1 uORF" OR (ASNSD1 AND (uORF OR upstream open reading frame)) returned 6
hits; the relevant ones are PMID:23160002, PMID:31738558, PMID:38176414 (+ bioRxiv
preprint PMID:37205492 of the same work) and the Mol Cell preview PMID:38242097. A
PAQosome query (18 hits) found no further ASDURF-specific primary work.

Discovery / localization — PMID:23160002 (Slavoff et al. 2013, full text)

  • Detected by peptidomics in K562 as "ASNSD1-SEP"; quantified by isotope dilution
    PMID:23160002.
  • FLAG-tagged construct with native UTRs gives cytoplasmic signal in HEK293T
    PMID:23160002.
    Overexpression with tag; fine for a broad CC term.

PAQosome subunit — PMID:31738558 (Cloutier et al. 2020, J Proteome Res; abstract only)

  • PMID:31738558
  • PMID:31738558
  • PMID:31738558
  • Basis of ComplexPortal CPX-6145 (paqosome_human) annotations. Full text not cached;
    defer to ComplexPortal curators.

Medulloblastoma dependency — PMID:38176414 (Hofman et al. 2024 Mol Cell, full text)

  • CRISPR screens of non-canonical ORFs; ASNSD1-uORF KO reduces viability of MYC-driven
    medulloblastoma lines, rescued by WT but not start-site-mutant cDNA.
  • Co-IP/MS with prefoldin subunits, endogenous co-IP with PFDN6, GST pulldown
    PMID:38176414.
  • DepMap/PRISM co-dependency clusters it with PFDL not canonical PFD
    PMID:38176414.
  • KO of ASNSD1-uORF or PFDN2 gives an overlapping post-transcriptional proteome change
    (cell-cycle proteins), with little RNA change. Actin/tubulin unaffected.
  • Interpretation: independent confirmation of PFDL/PAQosome membership. Cancer cell
    survival is a context-specific dependency, not a GO BP to assert.

Caller's context check

  • Chen et al. 2020 Science (PMID:32139545, cached full text) does not mention ASNSD1 or
    ASDURF in its cached text; it did not report the PAQosome association. The PAQosome
    finding is Cloutier et al. 2020 (Coulombe lab), and the CRISPR-screen context is Hofman
    et al. 2024 (Prensner lab). Not cited in the review.
