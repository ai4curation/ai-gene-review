CC   -!- FUNCTION: Secreted antioxidant enzyme that acts as the primary
CC       scavenger of superoxide radicals in the extracellular space, converting
CC       them to hydrogen peroxide and thereby protecting tissues from oxidative
CC       damage (PubMed:12933702, PubMed:15761197). Also preserves nitric oxide
CC       bioavailability by preventing the formation of harmful peroxynitrite
CC       (PubMed:12933702). Stored in intracellular pre-formed vesicles of
CC       macrophages and neutrophils and released upon stimulation by
CC       inflammatory mediators (PubMed:31326693). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12933702,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15761197, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31326693}.
