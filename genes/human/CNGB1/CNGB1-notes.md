## Channel-bearing products and sensory signaling

The structural beta sequence is NP_001288.3 residues 456–1251, with full-length functional comparisons separately reported [PMID:34699778]. The older electrophysiology uses human HA-tagged beta with bovine alpha in Xenopus oocytes, not an entirely human receptor [PMID:24164424]. Actual bovine experiments measure sodium/calcium permeability, rather than relying on calcium block as proof of permeation [PMID:10352032].

The human olfactory preparation contains two CNGA2, one CNGA4 and one CNGB1 and exhibits cAMP-dependent activation plus calcium/calmodulin feedback. It also uses residues 456–1251, an engineered 796-aa sequence [PMID:41131016]. Original rat olfactory cloning defines a distinct 858-aa B1b product and directly tests native-like sodium/potassium conduction and gating [PMID:10377344]. These assays support one contributed cyclic-nucleotide-gated cation-channel core, while retaining the original ligand-specific rows.

The four exact human products remain RCNC2B/Q14028-1 (1,251 aa), RCNC2A/Q14028-2 (623 aa), GARP2/Q14028-3 (299 aa), and Q14028-4 (1,245 aa). Official FASTAs match independent VAR_SEQ reconstruction.
