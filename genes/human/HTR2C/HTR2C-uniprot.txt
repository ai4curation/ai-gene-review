CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor for 5-hydroxytryptamine
CC       (serotonin) (PubMed:12970106, PubMed:18703043, PubMed:19057895,
CC       PubMed:29398112, PubMed:7895773). Also functions as a receptor for
CC       various drugs and psychoactive substances, including ergot alkaloid
CC       derivatives, 1-2,5,-dimethoxy-4-iodophenyl-2-aminopropane (DOI) and
CC       lysergic acid diethylamide (LSD) (PubMed:19057895, PubMed:29398112).
CC       Ligand binding causes a conformation change that triggers signaling via
CC       guanine nucleotide-binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the
CC       activity of downstream effectors (PubMed:18703043, PubMed:29398112).
CC       HTR2C is coupled to G(q)/G(11) G alpha proteins and activates
CC       phospholipase C-beta, releasing diacylglycerol (DAG) and inositol
CC       1,4,5-trisphosphate (IP3) second messengers that modulate the activity
CC       of phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase and promote the release of Ca(2+) ions
CC       from intracellular stores, respectively (PubMed:18703043,
CC       PubMed:29398112). Beta-arrestin family members inhibit signaling via G
CC       proteins and mediate activation of alternative signaling pathways
CC       (PubMed:29398112). Regulates neuronal activity via the activation of
CC       short transient receptor potential calcium channels in the brain, and
CC       thereby modulates the activation of pro-opiomelanocortin neurons and
CC       the release of CRH that then regulates the release of corticosterone
CC       (By similarity). Plays a role in the regulation of appetite and eating
CC       behavior, responses to anxiogenic stimuli and stress (By similarity).
CC       Plays a role in insulin sensitivity and glucose homeostasis (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P34968,
