CC   -!- FUNCTION: Bidirectional uniporter involved in the facilitative
CC       transport of nucleosides and nucleobases, and contributes to
CC       maintaining their cellular homeostasis (PubMed:10722669,
CC       PubMed:12527552, PubMed:12590919, PubMed:16214850, PubMed:21795683,
CC       PubMed:9396714, PubMed:9478986). Functions as a Na(+)-independent,
CC       passive transporter (PubMed:9478986). Involved in the transport of
CC       nucleosides such as inosine, adenosine, uridine, thymidine, cytidine
CC       and guanosine (PubMed:10722669, PubMed:12527552, PubMed:12590919,
CC       PubMed:16214850, PubMed:21795683, PubMed:9396714, PubMed:9478986). Also
CC       able to transport purine nucleobases (hypoxanthine, adenine, guanine)
CC       and pyrimidine nucleobases (thymine, uracil) (PubMed:16214850,
CC       PubMed:21795683). Also mediates nicotinamide uptake supporting
CC       homeostasis and modulating its biological activities (PubMed:39885119).
CC       Can also transport urate (PubMed:40367900). Involved in nucleoside
CC       transport at basolateral membrane of kidney cells, allowing liver
CC       absorption of nucleoside metabolites (PubMed:12527552). Mediates apical
CC       nucleoside uptake into Sertoli cells, thereby regulating the transport
CC       of nucleosides in testis across the blood-testis-barrier
CC       (PubMed:23639800). Mediates both the influx and efflux of hypoxanthine
CC       in skeletal muscle microvascular endothelial cells to control the
CC       amount of intracellular hypoxanthine available for xanthine oxidase-
CC       mediated ROS production (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O54699,
