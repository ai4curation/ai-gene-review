Gene Description

CAMK2D encodes the delta subunit of calcium/calmodulin-dependent protein kinase II (CaMKII), a serine/threonine kinase that assembles with other CaMKII subunits into dodecameric holoenzymes. Calcium-bound calmodulin relieves autoinhibition and permits Thr287 autophosphorylation, which makes the kinase partly calcium-independent. Delta is broadly expressed and is the predominant CaMKII of the heart, where the cytoplasmic deltaC splice variant tunes excitation-contraction coupling by phosphorylating calcium-handling and channel proteins, including the ryanodine receptor RyR2, phospholamban, the L-type calcium channel and the sodium channel Nav1.5, and also phosphorylates titin to lower passive myocardial stiffness. The nuclear deltaB variant phosphorylates HDAC4, promoting its nuclear export and MEF2-dependent hypertrophic gene expression. Elevated or persistent CaMKII delta activity increases sarcoplasmic reticulum calcium leak, alters action potentials and drives maladaptive remodelling in hypertrophy and heart failure. With CaMKII gamma, delta is one of the ubiquitous isoforms implicated in interferon-gamma-induced STAT1 Ser727 phosphorylation.

Findings

Catalysis, structure and holoenzyme

  • CaMKII delta is a Ca2+/calmodulin-activated Ser/Thr kinase; the CaMKIIdelta/Ca2+/CaM crystal
    structure shows how CaM binding releases the regulatory segment and how Thr287 is presented to
    a neighbouring kinase domain PMID:20668654.
  • All four isoforms are dodecamers PMID:14722083. Delta is the predominant cardiac CaMKII
    PMID:12676813.

Cardiac excitation-contraction coupling

  • deltaC phosphorylates RyR2 and phospholamban in vivo PMID:12676814; overexpression increases SR Ca2+ leak/sparks and reduces SR load
    PMID:12676813.
  • deltaC binds and phosphorylates Nav1.5 at Ser516/Thr594, shifting gating toward loss of
    function PMID:22514276.
  • beta-IV-spectrin targets CaMKII to excitable membranes and Nav channels (mouse)
    PMID:20877009.
  • CaMKII phosphorylates titin PEVK and N2B elements and lowers passive force; delta/gamma DKO and
    deltaC transgenic hearts were used PMID:23283722.

Nuclear deltaB and hypertrophy

  • deltaB is nuclear and phosphorylates HDAC4 Ser210, derepressing MEF2; knockdown blunts
    hypertrophic responses PMID:17179159.

Over-reaching rows

  • Electrical-coupling and heart-rate-by-conduction rows (IC/ISS) extrapolate from Nav1.5 gating
    and AP prolongation; no gap-junction or conduction data for CAMK2D were found.
  • "regulation of cell growth" (NAS, PMID:9060999) rests on a speculative sentence in a variant
    cloning paper.
  • LTP rows are an alpha/beta neuronal property; no delta-specific LTP evidence was found.

STAT1 (module context)

  • PMID:11972023 identifies the ubiquitous gamma/delta isoforms as the STAT1 TAD-interacting
    kinases PMID:11972023. The antibodies and
    purification did not separate gamma from delta, so no delta-specific NEW annotation is
    proposed; recorded as a suggested question.
