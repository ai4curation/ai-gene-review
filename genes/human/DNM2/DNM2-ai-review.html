Gene Description

Dynamin-2 is a widely expressed mechanochemical GTPase that assembles on membranes and couples GTP hydrolysis to membrane remodeling and vesicle scission. Its pleckstrin-homology domain binds membrane lipids, while its proline-rich region engages recruitment and regulatory partners. It supports receptor-mediated endocytosis and recycling-endosome scission during autophagic membrane formation, and participates in cortactin-dependent actin-bundle remodeling. Cytosolic, membrane-associated and specialized neuronal or adhesion pools provide distinct cellular contexts for this machinery.
