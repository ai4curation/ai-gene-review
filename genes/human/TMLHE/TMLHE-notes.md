# TMLHE Notes

TMLHE is the mitochondrial first-step enzyme of endogenous L-carnitine synthesis. Vaz et al. purified rat epsilon-N-trimethyllysine hydroxylase, used rat peptides to identify the human and mouse cDNAs, and confirmed by heterologous expression that the rat and human cDNAs encode the enzyme [PMID:11431483]. Monfregola et al. then showed that human TMLH-a is the active, mitochondrially imported Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate-dependent isoform, with its first 15 amino acids necessary and sufficient for mitochondrial import and with His389 required for activity [PMID:15754339]. Later stereochemical work with recombinant human TMLH showed that the reaction forms (2S,3S)-3-hydroxy-N-epsilon-trimethyllysine from (2S)-N-epsilon-trimethyllysine [PMID:27965989, PMID:28045275].

The GOA file is complete, not header-only: it carries 21 rows. The exact `GO:0050353 trimethyllysine dioxygenase activity`, `GO:0045329 carnitine biosynthetic process`, mitochondrial root, and matrix rows are correct. `GO:0016491 oxidoreductase activity` is true but too general because the exact dioxygenase row exists. The PANTHER IBA rows match PTHR10696: PTN000075473 carries carnitine biosynthesis on the eukaryotic carnitine-biosynthesis clade shared with BBOX-like enzymes, and PTN001642737 carries TMLHE-specific mitochondrial and trimethyllysine dioxygenase annotations.

Nava et al. provide the main IMP support: human TMLHE variants were loss-of-function and increased the upstream substrate trimethyllysine in patient plasma [PMID:23092983]. Disease association was not turned into additional GO process annotations.
