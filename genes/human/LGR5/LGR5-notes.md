# LGR5 notes

Deep research (falcon / perplexity-lite) failed; notes from cached publications and UniProt O75473.

- LRR 7TM glycoprotein-hormone-receptor-like protein; ectodomain binds R-spondins [PMID:22815884 "RSPO binds to the extracellular domain of LGR4 and LGR5"].
- Potentiates canonical Wnt via LRP6 phosphorylation [PMID:21693646 "mediate the potentiation of Wnt/β-catenin signaling by enhancing Wnt-induced LRP6 phosphorylation"].
- NOT G-protein/beta-arrestin coupled [PMID:21693646 "neither receptor is coupled to heterotrimeric G proteins or to β-arrestin"] -> GPCR activity and GPCR signaling REMOVED.
- Crystal structure RSPO1-LGR5 ecto 2:2 [PMID:23809763].
- Facultative Wnt receptor component [PMID:21727895].
- Constitutively internalized, steady-state in TGN via retromer [PMID:23439653 "reaches a steady-state distribution in the TGN within 2 h"]; poorly expressed at PM.

Localization conclusion: plasma membrane multipass receptor (ectodomain exposed for R-spondin binding), but
constitutively endocytosed so predominantly intracellular (trans-Golgi network) at steady state.
