# USP18 notes

## 2026-11-26 curation

- Automated deep research did not complete for this run: `just deep-research-falcon human USP18 --fallback perplexity-lite` found neither a working Falcon nor Perplexity provider and reported only `claude_code` as available. I used cached primary publications, UniProt, Reactome summaries, and the GO-CAM review for manual synthesis instead.
- The core catalytic activity is ISG15 deconjugation. Malakhov et al. showed that UBP43/USP18 efficiently cleaves ISG15 fusions and native ISG15 isopeptide-linked conjugates, establishing USP18 as an ISG15-specific protease [PMID:11788588, "UBP43 can efficiently cleave only ISG15 fusions including native ISG15 conjugates linked via isopeptide bonds"]. HERC5/cGAS work later supplied a direct cGAS ISGylation substrate and an opposite USP18 activity in the cGAS-STING pathway [PMID:38421872, "the E3 HERC5 catalyzes the ISGylation of cytoplasmic cGAS at lysine 21, 187, 219, and 458, whereas Ubl carboxy-terminal hydrolase 18 removes the ISGylation of cGAS"].
- USP18 also has deubiquitinase activity on selected ubiquitinated protein substrates, but this is target-specific rather than its defining family role. Reactome captures TAK1:TAB1 deubiquitination, and Zhou et al. 2024 directly showed USP18-dependent removal of polyubiquitin chains from SLC7A11 and SLC7A11 stabilization [PMID:38959043, "USP18 appears to be a bona fide DUB for SLC7A11 stabilization at posttranslational levels"].
- USP18 has two major noncatalytic immune-signaling roles. Malakhova et al. showed that UBP43/USP18 binds IFNAR2 and blocks receptor-associated JAK1 independently of its ISG15 isopeptidase activity [PMID:16710296, "Ubp43 specifically binds to the IFNAR2 receptor subunit and inhibits the activity of receptor-associated JAK1 by blocking the interaction between JAK and the IFN receptor"]. Arimoto et al. later showed that STAT2 recruits USP18 to IFNAR2 [PMID:28165510, "We found that STAT2 recruits USP18 to the type I IFN receptor subunit IFNAR2 via its constitutive membrane-distal STAT2-binding site"]. Separately, Zhang et al. showed USP18 recruits USP20 to STING and that USP20, not USP18, catalyzes STING deubiquitination in that branch [PMID:27801882, "USP18 did not deubiquitinate STING in vitro but facilitated USP20 to catalyze deubiquitination of STING in a manner independent of the enzymatic activity of USP18"].
- Loss-of-function human genetics support USP18 as a physiologic brake on type I interferon signaling. Patient fibroblasts from Meuwissen et al. showed excessive IFN-induced inflammatory gene expression that could be rescued with USP18 [PMID:27325888, "patient fibroblasts displayed severely enhanced IFN-induced inflammation, which was completely rescued by lentiviral transduction of USP18"].
- Localization is isoform-aware. Full-length USP18 is cytoplasmic, while an N-terminally truncated human isoform, USP18-sf, has enhanced nuclear deISGylation activity [PMID:22170061, "USP18-sf shows different subcellular distribution compared with the full-length protein and enhanced deISGylation activity in the nucleus"].
