CC   -!- FUNCTION: Interferon-induced ISG15-specific protease that plays a
CC       crucial role for maintaining a proper balance of ISG15-conjugated
CC       proteins in cells (PubMed:11788588, PubMed:38421872). Regulates protein
CC       ISGylation by efficiently cleaving ISG15 conjugates linked via
CC       isopeptide bonds (PubMed:11788588, PubMed:38421872). Acts as a negative
CC       regulator of cGAS-STING signaling be mediating deISGylation of CGAS
CC       (PubMed:38421872). Regulates T-cell activation and T-helper 17 (Th17)
CC       cell differentiation by deubiquitinating TAK1, likely to keep TAK1-TAB
CC       complexes in steady conditions (PubMed:23825189). In turn, restricts
CC       activation of NF-kappa-B, NFAT, and JNK as well as expression of IL2 in
CC       T-cells after TCR activation (PubMed:23825189). Acts as a molecular
CC       adapter with USP20 to promote innate antiviral response through
CC       deubiquitinating STING1 (PubMed:27801882). Involved also in the
CC       negative regulation of the inflammatory response triggered by type I
CC       interferon (PubMed:27325888, PubMed:28165510). Upon recruitment by
CC       STAT2 to the type I interferon receptor subunit IFNAR2 interferes with
CC       the assembly of the ternary interferon-IFNAR1-IFNAR2 complex and acts
CC       as a negative regulator of the type I interferon signaling pathway
CC       (PubMed:28165510). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11788588,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23825189, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27325888,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27801882, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28165510,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38421872}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 2]: Has enzymatic activity similar to isoform 1 and
CC       interferes with type I interferon signaling. Major deISGylation enzyme
CC       for nuclear proteins (PubMed:22170061). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22170061}.
