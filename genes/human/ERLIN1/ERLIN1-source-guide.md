# ERLIN1 direct functional evidence

## ER membranes and the erlin assembly

[PMID:16835267](https://doi.org/10.1242/jcs.03060): the original publisher PDF's Results on ER colocalization/internal detergent-resistant membranes and Methods for constructs, fractionation and cholesterol depletion were read. Human ERLIN1 was cloned from U937 cells and expressed in HeLa; experiments with human proteins in mouse 3T3 cells are separate. Cholesterol depletion after hypotonic disruption affects internal membrane detergent resistance differently from whole-cell treatment. Engineered N-terminal fragments are targeting probes, not natural products. No figure pixels or separate supplements were inspected.

[PMID:19240031](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M809801200): the actual Molecular Properties and IP3-receptor ERAD Results, recovered Plasmids/shRNA/TEM Methods and selected captions were read. Mouse SPFH1 BC011220 and mouse SPFH2 constructs remain mouse proteins in HeLa hosts. Native Rat-1/alphaT3-1 experiments are distinguished from endogenous HeLa glycosylation. The selected Figure 2C caption does not establish its cell context. The original large-complex size is an estimate; it is not a modern structural subunit count. No complete-Methods, figure-pixel or supplemental-data read is claimed.

## Ligase and associated-protein interactions

[PMID:21343306](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M110.211326): original XML Results, Figure 2 discussion and construct/immunoprecipitation Methods show human SPFH1 association with human SYVN1/HRD1 and AMFR/gp78 in CHO-7 cells. The SPFH1 result differs from SPFH2 for TRC8 and sterol-triggered HMGCR turnover. Co-immunoprecipitation supports association with an E3 ligase but does not establish purified binary contacts, ubiquitin-transfer catalysis or a universal ERAD substrate relationship. No figure pixels or independent supplementary data were inspected.

[PMID:22119785](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb2383): original Results and experimental-design passages describe tagged-bait affinity purification, mass spectrometry and interaction scoring in human HEK293 cells. Actual [IntAct](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/) records EBI-25769871/EBI-25770016 identify the ERLIN1–ERLIN2 pair; EBI-25770037 identifies ERLIN1–SYVN1 and EBI-25770556 ERLIN1–AMFR. Detergent dependence and the global validation rate do not establish the precise condition or confidence score for every pair. The separate Online Methods/supplementary files were not independently read. Independent PMID:21343306 supports the two specific E3-association refinements.

[PMID:18468998](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M801860200): original Table 3 lists ERLIN1 in the HEK293 ADRA1D tandem-affinity preparation (four peptides, 16% coverage). IntAct EBI-21277437 independently identifies that pair. The association is retained as an ancillary assembly observation. Receptor physiology and dystrophin/syntrophin mechanisms do not establish an ERLIN1-specific signaling role. Operative TAP Methods, raw spectra and figure pixels were not independently read.

## Cholesterol binding and SREBP regulation

[PMID:24217618](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.201305076): original Results and binding, cell-culture, lipid and biochemical Methods were read. Recombinant human ERLIN1 produced in S2 cells and immobilized through its tag directly binds radiolabeled cholesterol, with competition, saturation and cooperativity. Native mouse-liver complex binding is a separate experiment. Human HeLa ERLIN1 depletion raises SREBP-responsive cholesterol/fatty-acid synthesis transcripts and lipid abundance; processing, localization, SCAP inhibition and Insig turnover connect the phenotype to regulation. FLAG-SREBP processing and Insig turnover use HEK293 cells; HeLa nuclear staining and the fatostatin experiment are distinct assays. A scaffolding mechanism is one proposed model, not an established unique mechanism. These are not direct synthesis-flux measurements. ERLIN2-specific FRAP observations and mixed-species SCAP/Insig preparations are not treated as pure human ERLIN1 experiments. No figure pixels or separate supplementary data were inspected.

[PMID:34572057](https://doi.org/10.3390/cells10092408): the cached review's erlin microdomain discussion was read as secondary context. The internal membrane-raft decision also rests on the original PMID:16835267 fractionation/cholesterol-depletion observations; it does not depend on the review alone.

## Independent human ERAD evidence

[PMID:19751772](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbamcr.2009.09.004): genuine fetched full text, actual cell/RNAi/construct Methods and stimulated-IP3-receptor Results were read. Human muscarinic-receptor-expressing HeLa cells with endogenous ERLIN1 depletion show impaired receptor ubiquitination and degradation; the calcium response and the tested IκBα degradation control remain intact. ERLIN2 depletion also partly reduces ERLIN1, and the rescue construct is mouse SPFH2. Selected other model ERAD substrates differ in response, so no universal ERAD requirement is inferred. The human ERLIN1 perturbation is independent of the rodent source experiments in PMID:19240031. No figure pixels or separate supplements were inspected.

The 348-residue displayed human ERLIN1 sequence is the identified natural product. None of these engineered fragments, expression hosts or complex-size estimates establishes another natural isoform. No publication quotation is added.

## Modern originals and core activity boundaries (2026-10-24)

**Integral complex structure and cargo recruitment.** [Veronese et al., PMID:38782601](https://doi.org/10.26508/lsa.202402620), Results/Figures 1–3 and 6–8, Discussion and the cell/knockout/rescue, interaction, lipid and AlphaFold Methods, was read from the actual author-manuscript text. Endogenous human HeLa interactions and double ERLIN1/ERLIN2 knockout with combined rescue support erlin-complex-dependent TMUB1-L/RNF170 association. TMUB1 association with RNF185/TMEM259 persists, so the effect is not simply universal loss of detected interaction. These experiments do not resolve individual ERLIN1 contact surfaces or demonstrate purified ERLIN1 bridging two partners. The Discussion explicitly permits separate complexes containing TMUB1 or RNF170. Rescue cloning provides coding-region primers without independently resolving the original template provenance. AlphaFold uses human sequences; the adjacent-subunit interfaces are predictions. The tested interface mutants are ERLIN2-FLAG, including G48V as an analogy to ERLIN1 G50V. No exact whole-complex subunit count is inferred from this study.

The structural activity is represented by [GO:0140378](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0140378), whose integral-complex-component definition is more direct than a second, redundant adaptor activity. Its coupling to selective protein turnover uses separate primary evidence. [Lu et al., PMID:21610068](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M111.251983), Experimental Procedures, Results including Priority of Binding to Activated IP3 Receptors, Figures 4–5 captions and Discussion, was read from recovered indexed original text. In human mHeLa cells, ERLIN2 depletion inhibits RNF170 recruitment whereas RNF170 depletion does not prevent erlin-complex recruitment. This orders the complex upstream of the ligase; it is not an isolated ERLIN1 recruitment perturbation. Native alphaT3-1/Rat-1 experiments are rodent, while SH-SY5Y association and mHeLa depletion provide distinct human evidence. The 257-aa RNF170 constructs are mouse even when expressed in HeLa. The ligase assay establishes RNF170 chemistry, not ERLIN1 enzymatic activity. The normal PMID:21610068 cache remains unchanged and abstract-only; manual reviewer access is broader because the operative original was recovered separately.

[Wang et al., PMID:19751772](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbamcr.2009.09.004), Figure 6/Results, supplies positive quality-control evidence: combined SPFH1/SPFH2 depletion modestly stabilizes misfolded A1AT-NHK and TTR-D18G in human HEK/HeLa assays, while other tested reporters differ. This supports the current [GO:0036503 ERAD quality control pathway](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0036503), without reclassifying activated IP3 receptors as misfolded substrates or claiming a universal ERAD requirement. Figure 7 reports no effect on the tested sterol-induced HMGCR degradation; interaction with gp78/HRD1 does not automatically transfer ERLIN2-specific HMGCR claims to ERLIN1.

**Sterol binding and regulatory coupling.** The previously assessed [PMID:24217618](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.201305076) separates recombinant human ERLIN1 sterol binding from native mouse complex assays and cellular SREBP endpoints. In PMID:38782601, actual lipid, morphology and inhibitor-rescue Results support restraint of cholesterol esterification. Sterol sequestration/accessibility to SOAT1 and effects on Insig regulation remain mechanistic alternatives. They do not constitute a direct ERLIN1 cholesterol-transport assay.

[PMID:37683630](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2023.08.013), actual [PMC10914638](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10914638/), Results/Figures 6–7 captions, Discussion/Limitations and operative cell, virus, perturbation, interaction, pulse-chase and lipid Methods, was read independently of its unchanged abstract-only normal cache. Human A549 experiments support USP25-dependent erlin stabilization and contextual effects on SREBP processing, HMGCR abundance, total cholesterol and influenza output. Figure 7J's labeled-cholesterol efflux assay uses shERLIN2, and Figure 7D's T65I mutant is ERLIN2. HMGCR abundance and total cholesterol do not directly measure de-novo sterol synthetic flux. ERLIN1 is not assigned USP25 deubiquitinase activity.

**Disease and virus-specific boundaries.** [Cogan et al., PMID:39367212](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00439-024-02702-0), participant/DNA/RNA and protein-modeling Methods, variant Results, Discussion and Figures 1–2 captions, was read from the actual manuscript. The 13-person, six-family series supplies positive clinical and patient RNA-splicing evidence for the investigated variants. Effects on complex assembly are predictions from human-sequence AlphaFold models and a bacterial SPFH/FtsH analogy, not experimentally measured assembly defects or stoichiometry. The 2026 [PMID:41887216 abstract](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2026.03.001) reports a later structural study, but its operative body was not recovered or read here; no quantitative structural result from that abstract is adopted in the cores.

[Whitten-Bauer et al., PMID:31810281](https://doi.org/10.3390/cells8121555), Methods and full Results/Discussion, was read from the genuine full original cache. Human Huh-7 depletion separates an effect on establishing HCV RNA replication and efficient particle production from tested entry, initial translation and established-replicon maintenance. Different depletion efficiencies limit an absolute ERLIN1/ERLIN2 specificity claim. These HCV findings and the influenza context above are not generalized into a universal proviral or antiviral core function.
