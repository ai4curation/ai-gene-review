Gene Description

CHRM4 encodes the M4 muscarinic acetylcholine receptor, a seven-transmembrane acetylcholine receptor that preferentially couples to Gi/o proteins and inhibitory cyclic-AMP signaling. It contributes to neuronal cholinergic modulation and motor-circuit regulation. Different agonists can recruit different Gi subtypes with different efficacy. Plasma-membrane and neuronal synaptic localization support receptor signaling, whereas physical association with receptor activity-modifying proteins does not by itself establish a particular native signaling complex.

Core Functions

Binds acetylcholine and activates heterotrimeric G proteins, preferentially engaging Gi/o-linked signaling with inhibition of cyclic-AMP production. Coupling depends on ligand, receptor abundance and cellular effector context; the receptor does not itself catalyse the downstream enzymatic reaction.
