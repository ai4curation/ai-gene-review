# EYA4: coactivation, phosphatase activity and localization

EYA4 has two supported molecular roles. [PMID:19606496](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19606496/), abstract Results, reports EYA4–SIX3 co-immunoprecipitation, SIX3-dependent nuclear recruitment and cooperative reporter-gene activation. The committed [UniProt O95677](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/O95677/entry) CC:SUBUNIT independently curates this functional interaction with experimental evidence from that paper. These positive results support DNA-binding transcription factor binding and transcriptional coactivation. Full Methods and historical construct sequences remain unavailable: the literal GOA partner Q62233 denotes mouse Six3, and no purified human–human binary-affinity assay or isoform-resolved result is claimed. The earlier generic-binding-only interpretation overlooked the explicitly reported functional assay.

The [2024 phosphatase study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-45683-4), Methods for protein purification and phosphatase assays, directly measures human EYA4 residues 367–639 on PLK1-pY425, PLK1-pY445 and H2AX-pY142 peptides. The described purification uses poly-His/TVMV tagging, Ni-NTA, Fast Q and Superdex 75; the inspected Methods do not identify the expression host. This isolated domain matches current isoforms 1 and 2, without establishing equivalent activity for all full-length isoforms.

The same study's Results and Figure 2 distinguish EYA4-specific perturbation from EYA1 depletion and pan-EYA inhibition. EYA4 depletion reduces PLK1-pT210 relative to total PLK1 and reduces phosphorylation of TCTP and CDC25C, with a nonsignificant CyclinB result. The EYA4 catalytic mutant and docking mutants support coupling between EYA4 phosphatase activity and PLK1 activation. EYA4 therefore performs the dephosphorylation step in positive regulation of protein kinase activity; PLK1 performs the downstream kinase reactions. No kinase activity is assigned to EYA4. Figure 1G and the Figure 3 Results connect endogenous G2 centrosomal EYA4 with centrosomal PLK1, pericentrin accumulation and centrosome separation. The isolated pY445 PRM comparison is a nonsignificant trend; predicted PLK1 conformational changes remain a model. Selected main text and captions were read, without new experimental-image or supplementary-data inspection.

Developmental sources retain their distinct scopes. [Mouse Eya4 deletion](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI32899), Results, gives birth-stage palate maturation delay and later middle-ear/eustachian-tube abnormalities. [Zebrafish eya4 perturbation](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.012237), Methods and Results, uses two splice-blocking morpholinos, mismatch controls, otic/canal/hair-cell endpoints and downstream atp1b2b rescue. The publisher-provided original preview was read through ResearchGate. These data support conserved developmental participation without direct EYA4 DNA binding. Embryonic morphogenesis is an is_a descendant of anatomical structure morphogenesis; inner ear development is not, so only the former replaces the broad TAS term.
