# EYA4: molecular activity, localization and developmental evidence

Human EYA4 has directly measured phosphotyrosine-peptide phosphatase capacity. In the [2024 original](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-45683-4), a purified human EYA4 367–639 domain hydrolyzes PLK1-pY425, PLK1-pY445 and H2AX-pY142 peptides. The inspected Methods describe poly-His/TVMV tagging, Ni-NTA, Fast Q and Superdex 75 purification, and a magnesium-containing phosphate-release assay. They do not explicitly identify the enzyme expression host. The paper's full-length cellular constructs and the isolated domain are separate preparations; the current 367–639 sequence is present in products 1 and 2, but the historical plasmid was not resequenced here and activity was not independently established for all five natural products.

The same original examines EYA4 in human HeLa and 293T cells. Endogenous EYA4 and PLK1 colocalize at G2 HeLa centrosomes, with an EYA4-siRNA comparison in Figure 1G. Genetic perturbation, phosphotyrosine assays and PLK1 activation experiments support this context. The pY445 PRM comparison alone is a nonsignificant trend, and pan-EYA benzarone inhibition cannot independently establish target specificity. H2AX-pY142 is a positive biochemical control; it is not an EYA4-specific cellular DNA-repair experiment. Selected operative Methods, Results and captions were read. Figure pixels and supplementary files were not inspected.

[Human Protein Atlas EYA4](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000112319-EYA4/subcellular) provides independent nuclear localization evidence. The actual HPA004805 HeLa, Rh30 and U2OS images show diffuse/granular nuclear signal; HPA reports approved nucleoplasm localization. Variable extranuclear signal prevents an exclusive-location claim. The full 89-residue antigen matches O95677-5 exactly; products 1, 3 and 4 retain a 62-residue exact segment and product 2 a 63-residue segment. These polyclonal-antibody images neither identify the stained product nor establish product-5-specific localization. They also do not resolve a particular nuclear-body subtype.

The broad nuclear annotations are therefore refined to nucleoplasm. The cytoplasmic annotations are refined to the experimentally described centrosomal pool, without asserting that all extranuclear EYA4 is centrosomal. Current GO places nucleoplasm within nucleus but does not place centrosome under cytoplasm in the inspected typed closure; the latter replacement follows direct compartment evidence rather than a claimed ancestor relation.

The [PAINT family PTHR10190](https://www.pantherdb.org/panther/family.do?clsAccession=PTHR10190) contains human EYA4 leaf PTN002468882, whose ungapped alignment exactly matches the 639-residue displayed sequence. Positive IBDs at PTN000022641 support tyrosine phosphatase, nucleus, differentiation and positive DNA-repair regulation. PTN002565156 supports embryonic morphogenesis and inner-ear development. All these nodes lie on the actual target path, without a target-path NOT. Some current developmental IBD donor lists include zebrafish evidence absent from the imported WITH lists; this is a source-version distinction, not a reason to invalidate the placement.

The conserved repair interpretation combines that placement with actual substrate chemistry. [PMID:19234442](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19234442/) tests Eya1/3 affinity preparations, H2AX phosphorylation and repair-factor association. Its Eya3 240–573 negative pSer comparison is not an EYA4 experiment; human depletion and murine rescue are also kept separate. The 2024 human EYA4-domain H2AX-pY142 assay corroborates the inherited biochemical capacity without by itself demonstrating a cellular EYA4 repair outcome.

Developmental evidence is organism-specific. [Mouse Eya4 deletion](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI32899) causes a palatal maturation delay at birth and later middle-ear/eustachian-tube defects. Embryonic inner-ear expression is a separate observation; the authors report apparently normal inner-ear anatomy as data not shown. Middle-ear pathology is not substituted for direct inner-ear developmental evidence. Actual selected publisher Methods, Results and captions were read, without figure pixels or supplements.

In the [zebrafish original](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.012237), two eya4 splice-blocking morpholinos, mismatch controls and downstream atp1b2b mRNA rescue establish a regulatory contribution to otic-vesicle/canal and sensory hair-cell development. The inspected Methods and Results came from the publisher-provided full-text preview available through [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/23265916_Eya4_regulation_of_NaK-ATPase_is_required_for_sensory_system_development_in_zebrafish). This is not a human-product experiment or direct atp1b2b promoter-binding assay. No figure pixels, movies or supplementary material were read.

The imported rat source is Eya4/RGD:1307099, currently A0A8I6AS60 and ENSRNOP00000097961.2. Its inner-ear-development assertion is IEP from [PMID:11159937](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11159937/). The PMID, DOI and abstract were verified, but the operative original was inaccessible. No rat assay detail is inferred from the title or human deafness abstract, and expression alone is not treated as proof that the product performs developmental work. The retained non-core developmental inference also rests on the independent zebrafish experiments and actual PAINT placement.

[InterPro IPR028472](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/entry/InterPro/IPR028472/) is an actual target protein match. The target protein-match response and official EC reaction were inspected; the direct IPR-entry and Rhea RDF requests failed. ## Molecular activity and unresolved coupling

One tyrosine-phosphatase activity accounts for the inspected EYA4 chemistry. The [human-domain experiment](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-45683-4) directly measures PLK1-pY425, PLK1-pY445 and H2AX-pY142 peptide dephosphorylation. Human EYA4 perturbation and endogenous G2 colocalization support a centrosomal PLK1-regulatory context; the isolated pY445 PRM trend is not independently significant. Molecular-dynamics predictions about the PLK1 connecting loop are hypotheses, not measured conformational dynamics, and a nonphosphorylatable Y445F mutant does not directly measure endogenous phosphosite occupancy.

The DNA-repair facet combines valid PAINT inheritance with the H2AX peptide reaction; EYA4-specific cellular repair outcomes remain to be established. Neither this inference nor nuclear staining transfers the EYA1/EYA3 cellular assays to EYA4. Nucleoplasm and centrosome describe separately observed protein pools, without implying that every natural product occupies both compartments or that domain activity establishes native-product equivalence. The developmental and association evidence remains separate from this catalytic unit.
