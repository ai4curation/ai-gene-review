## 2026-10-25 — molecular activity synthesis

A single protein tyrosine phosphatase unit captures the experimentally measured human EYA4 domain chemistry. PLK1-pY425/pY445 and H2AX-pY142 are direct peptide substrates in the inspected original. EYA4 depletion, endogenous G2 centrosomal colocalization and PLK1 activation readouts connect this chemistry to a human cellular mechanism, while the pY445 PRM comparison alone is nonsignificant and pan-EYA inhibitor effects are not EYA4-specific. The synthesis does not assert intrinsic Ser/Thr catalysis or a separate generic-binding activity.

The positive DNA-repair-regulation facet retains the accepted phylogenetic inference, corroborated by EYA4-domain H2AX chemistry. It is not an EYA4-specific cellular repair experiment. Nucleoplasm is supported by independent HPA imaging; centrosome by the human G2 experiments. No fixed complex, predominant location or equivalence of all five products is inferred. The questions separate native H2AX substrate attribution, natural-product activity/localization and quantitative PLK1-pY445 turnover.
