# EYA4 source review notes

## 2026-10-24 — activity, compartment and developmental evidence

The human EYA4 367–639 domain has directly measured phosphotyrosine-peptide phosphatase capacity in PMID:38360978. Its purified domain, full-length cellular constructs and endogenous G2 HeLa centrosomal staining are distinct preparations. The inspected purification Methods do not name the enzyme expression host. The H2AX-pY142 control corroborates biochemical capacity, while the conserved repair interpretation also draws on the actual PAINT placement; it does not establish an EYA4-specific cellular repair phenotype by itself.

The human HPA004805 images support nucleoplasmic localization with variable extranuclear signal. The complete antigen matches current product 5, while long segments are shared by the other four products. Neither the imaging nor the historical constructs establish the native location or activity of all five products. Centrosomal localization is supported independently by the 2024 endogenous-cell experiments; this is an evidence-based refinement of broad cytoplasm, not a claimed GO ancestor relation.

Mouse palatal and middle-ear defects, zebrafish otic development and the rat expression assertion retain their distinct organism and assay limits. The rat operative original was not recovered. The earlier PMID:9887327 visual-perception and morphogenesis assertions remain unresolved because their supporting original passages were unavailable. Conserved developmental inferences do not convert those uninspected source-specific passages into verified experiments.

The independently recovered PMID:19606496 abstract positively supports an EYA4–SIX3 association. Its Methods and construct sequence remain unavailable, so the mouse Six3 WITH accession is retained without inferring the EYA4 construct species, isolated binary binding or product specificity. The supported generic association remains non-core.

## 2026-10-25 — molecular activity synthesis

A single protein tyrosine phosphatase unit captures the experimentally measured human EYA4 domain chemistry. PLK1-pY425/pY445 and H2AX-pY142 are direct peptide substrates in the inspected original. EYA4 depletion, endogenous G2 centrosomal colocalization and PLK1 activation readouts connect this chemistry to a human cellular mechanism, while the pY445 PRM comparison alone is nonsignificant and pan-EYA inhibitor effects are not EYA4-specific. The synthesis does not assert intrinsic Ser/Thr catalysis or a separate generic-binding activity.

The positive DNA-repair-regulation facet retains the accepted phylogenetic inference, corroborated by EYA4-domain H2AX chemistry. It is not an EYA4-specific cellular repair experiment. Nucleoplasm is supported by independent HPA imaging; centrosome by the human G2 experiments. No fixed complex, predominant location or equivalence of all five products is inferred. The questions separate native H2AX substrate attribution, natural-product activity/localization and quantitative PLK1-pY445 turnover.
