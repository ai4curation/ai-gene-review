CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of neuronal acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) that
CC       function as pentameric, ligand-gated cation channels with high calcium
CC       permeability among other activities. nAChRs are excitatory
CC       neurotrasnmitter receptors formed by a collection of nAChR subunits
CC       known to mediate synaptic transmission in the nervous system and the
CC       neuromuscular junction. Each nAchR subunit confers differential
CC       attributes to channel properties, including activation, deactivation
CC       and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, cation permeability, and
CC       binding to allosteric modulators (PubMed:20881005, PubMed:8663494). Has
CC       an accessory rather than functional role and is only able to form
CC       functional nAChRs when co-assembled with another beta subunit
CC       (PubMed:20881005, PubMed:8663494). Participates in pentameric
CC       assemblies along with CHRNA3, CHRNA4, CHRNB2 and CHRNB4
CC       (PubMed:20881005, PubMed:8663494). Increases receptor sensitivity to
CC       acetylcholine and nicotine when associated with CHRNA4 and CHRNB2
CC       (PubMed:8663494). Plays a role in nicotine addiction (PubMed:20881005).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20881005, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663494}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by a myriad of ligands such as
CC       acetylcholine, cytisine, nicotine, choline and epibatidine.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20881005, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663494}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Neuronal AChR that forms heteropentamers composed of two
CC       different type of subunits: alpha and non-alpha (beta). CHRNA5/alpha-5
CC       subunit is only able to form functional nAChRs when co-assembled with
CC       another alpha subunit, can be combined to CHRNA4/alpha-4 or
CC       CHRNA3/alpha-3 and CHRNB4/beta-4 or CHRNB2/beta-2 to give rise to
CC       functional receptors (PubMed:20881005, PubMed:8663494). Interacts with
CC       LYPD6 (PubMed:27344019). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20881005,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27344019, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663494}.

CC       {ECO:0000255}. Cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P32297}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
