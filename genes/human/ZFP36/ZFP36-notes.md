# ZFP36 notes

ZFP36/TTP is the human prototype of the TTP-family tandem CCCH zinc-finger RNA-binding proteins. It binds AU-rich elements in target 3'-UTRs and recruits mRNA decay machinery to destabilize those transcripts: Lai et al. showed that TTP binds the TNF 3'-UTR ARE and promotes TNF mRNA deadenylation [PMID:10330172, "TTP can bind directly to the AU-rich element (ARE) in TNF-alpha mRNA"], Lai et al. then showed that TTP and related CCCH proteins promote turnover of ARE-containing TNF, GM-CSF, and IL3 RNAs [PMID:11279239, "Co-expression of the CCCH proteins caused the rapid turnover of these ARE-containing mRNAs"], and Fabian et al. mapped a C-terminal TTP motif that binds CNOT1 and is required for efficient CCR4-NOT-dependent deadenylation [PMID:23644599, "Mutations at the CNOT1-TTP interface impair TTP-mediated deadenylation"].

The PAINT node `PTN002648882` is the main family node for the vertebrate TTP-like decay proteins. It has direct human ZFP36 evidence for `GO:0035925` mRNA 3'-UTR AU-rich region binding, `GO:0160134` protein-RNA sequence-specific adaptor activity, and `GO:0000289` nuclear-transcribed mRNA poly(A) tail shortening [PTHR12547-paint.tsv, "PTHR12547	PTN002648882	GO:0160134	F	IBD	false"]. The same node carries `GO:0000900` mRNA regulatory element binding translation repressor activity only from two worm genes, so the human rows that inherit `GO:0000900` are treated as over-propagated.

ZFP36 also couples ARE-bound mRNAs to P-bodies, decapping, and other decay routes. Franks and Lykke-Andersen showed that TTP and BRF-1 deliver ARE-mRNAs to processing bodies [PMID:17369404, "TTP and BRF-1 deliver ARE-mRNAs to processing bodies"], and Fenger-Gron et al. showed that TTP can enhance decapping of an ARE RNA in vitro [PMID:16364915, "TTP enhances decapping of a target AU-rich element (ARE) RNA in vitro"]. TTP is regulated by phosphorylation and 14-3-3 binding, including p38/MK2-dependent regulation of stress-granule association [PMID:15014438, "14-3-3 binding excludes TTP from SGs and inhibits TTP-dependent degradation of ARE-containing transcripts"], but those kinase and stimulus rows are regulatory context around ZFP36 rather than ZFP36 participation in MAPK cascades.

Many rows in this GOA slice are bare `GO:0005515` protein-binding rows from interaction screens or individual mRNA-decay cofactors. Those IPI rows are removed under the project protein-binding policy because the term records no molecular specificity; the useful biology is captured by ARE binding, protein-RNA sequence-specific adaptor activity, CCR4-NOT recruitment, deadenylation, decapping, P-body localization, and specific non-core binding terms such as 14-3-3 protein binding.
