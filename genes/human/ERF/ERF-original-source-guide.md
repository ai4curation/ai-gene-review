# ERF original sources and database provenance

The database assertions concern human ERF/P50548. The canonical protein has 548 residues. The reported 473-residue product deletes canonical residues 1–75, overlapping the ETS domain; the evidence below does not establish equivalent activity of the two products.

## Direct human assays and interaction records

- [Sgouras et al., 1995 original](https://www.embopress.org/doi/pdf/10.1002/j.1460-2075.1995.tb00160.x): cDNA-isolation Results, Functional analysis of ERF, Figure 2A–D and Libraries/plasmids, Cell lines/transfection and EMSA Methods. The actual PDF text and Figure 2 pixels were inspected. Human 548-residue ERF represses HeLa CAT reporters; ETS-site deletion and GAL4 tethering distinguish recognition and active repression. Figure 2D is a GAL4-binding control, not an ERF–H28 gel. The original reports its initial Southwestern result as data not shown. No natural shorter-product equivalence is established.
- [Luck et al., 2020 original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7169983/): main Results, Generation and characterization of HuRI. [Author data](https://www.interactome-atlas.org/download) have twelve method records for four ERF pairs, using ERF clone 2592/P50548-1. Exact partners are Q08117-2, Q96EZ8-1 and Q9UGI0; MEOX2 clone 824 is labeled P50222 in the author deposit. [IntAct](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/search?query=P50548%20AND%20Q6FHY5) records the exact GOA ERF–Q6FHY5 pair under PMID:32296183, with IM-25472-32649, IM-25472-7688 and IM-25472-118431. Current MEOX2 sequences have 303 versus 304 residues and are not declared identical. The observations are yeast two-hybrid interactions, not proof of an endogenous complex or a more specific activity. Complete supplementary Methods and target-pair mammalian orthogonal validation were not recovered.
- [Jolma et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2012.12.009): metadata and the directly attributed [JASPAR MA0760.1 dataset](https://jaspar.elixir.no/matrix/MA0760.1/) identify human ERF/P50548 and HT-SELEX. The exact position-frequency matrix was retrieved. The full Methods, construct boundaries and supplement were not recovered. The sequence-specific DNA-binding judgment also uses the independent 1995 original and does not claim a full-text audit of the 2013 study.

No new publication quotations were added and the normal source caches were not rewritten. The 473-residue ERF product deletes residues 1–75, including part of the mapped ETS domain; the experiments above do not establish its equivalent function.
