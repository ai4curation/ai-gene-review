Gene Description

HAAO is a cytosolic, non-heme Fe(II)-dependent dioxygenase that cleaves 3-hydroxyanthranilate to form 2-amino-3-carboxymuconate semialdehyde (ACMS). ACMS can spontaneously cyclize to quinolinate, supplying a precursor for NAD+ synthesis through the kynurenine pathway of tryptophan degradation. ACMSD can divert this intermediate by decarboxylation. Biallelic loss-of-function HAAO variants impair NAD+ synthesis and cause congenital malformations.

  • CC: cytosol (GO:0005829).
