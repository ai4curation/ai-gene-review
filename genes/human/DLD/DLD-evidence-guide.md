# DLD evidence and source scope

This augmentation preserves the 84 historical annotation source objects, including the prior glycine-cleavage proposal, and incorporates five genuine additional GOA rows. One separately proposed endopeptidase annotation brings the total to 90. The three official natural products remain distinct from processed chains, engineered fragments and disease variants. Primary source access, rather than a cache’s nominal full-text flag alone, determines the claims below.

## Catalytic functions and products

The main activity regenerates oxidized lipoyl cofactors on E2 or GCSH proteins using FAD and NAD+. The pyruvate, 2-oxoglutarate and branched-chain ketoacid process definitions include all three coupled reactions, including DLD’s regeneration step. DLD does not itself perform the E1 decarboxylation. Human biochemical evidence includes [reconstitution and activity measurements](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20160912/), [mutant E3 assays and calorimetry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20385101/) and [E3/E3BP structure](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16442803/). These support one shared redox unit across enzyme systems.

[The OADH paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29191460/) remains abstract-only: it explicitly reports recombinant human DHTKD1 recruitment of DLST and DLD. Its E1 substrate preference and enamine-associated oxidant chemistry are not DLD-specific properties. [The human-liver BCKDH paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3593587/) also remains abstract-only; it reports dissociable E3 and recovery of overall complex activity upon adding it. Existing well-established target assertions are retained without inventing inaccessible Methods.

The prior glycine-cleavage proposal remains TAS-level evidence. In [reaction R-HSA-5694018](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5694018), catalyst 5694028 identifies [active unit 5694020](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5694020), the DLD dimer with FAD. Its [DLD component 69978](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-69978) resolves to P09622 residues 36–509 in matrix. DLD performs lipoyl regeneration rather than merely being consumed. Same-role LPD1 and LpdA annotations support this convention; checked ancestry does not duplicate another retained process. No target occurrence was found in the checked GO-CAM index. The local glycine module cites this review and is not independent evidence.

The new [endopeptidase annotation](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0004175) describes conditional cleavage, supported by [human recombinant WT/mutant frataxin-substrate experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1851069/) and independently by [human DLD preparations cleaving human IκBε](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5746455/). The former was read through indexed original Results, figure legends and Methods despite failed article downloads; its protected cache is still abstract-only. The latter cache omits critical Results/Methods, which were recovered separately. Urea/refolding, reaction conditions and reported WT/mutant preparation limits are retained. Mouse N9/brain stress experiments are not direct normal human microglial physiology. No additional NF-κB process or protease location is proposed. The retained NEW molecular-function assertion records the conditional biochemical activity; it is not a second core function. Native human deployment and substrates are recorded as a BIOLOGY knowledge gap, recognizing the existing IκBε experiments rather than claiming that no candidate cellular substrate is known.

The current serine-type peptidase definitions require a catalytic triad. The first paper instead supports a serine/glutamate dyad, with H450A increasing activity. Therefore GO:0004175 is used without deleting the biochemical serine dependence. Mature-protein E431/D444/H450/S456 correspond to precursor E466/D479/H485/S491; the later paper’s reported S456A name is not independently remapped to its clone. The engineered 126-residue fragment is not natural isoform 2.

[Official UniProt P09622](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P09622/entry) and independent official sequence reconstruction give products of 509, 410 and 461 residues. Isoform 2 deletes residues 1–99; isoform 3 deletes 147–194. Canonical precursor processing removes 1–35. Sequence changes alone do not establish the alternative products’ activity or localization.

## Localization and annotation specificity

Mitochondrion annotations are refined at the gene level to the established matrix pool, while each source’s assay resolution remains explicit. An organelle proteomics experiment is not described as a matrix-resolved assay. In particular, [the human-muscle phosphoproteomics cache](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20833797/) is marked full text but lacks detailed Methods and target supplemental assignments. The matrix refinement uses independent evidence. The [human mitochondrial proteome study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34800366/) provides methodological context, without a new target-peptide or phosphosite reanalysis.

[The nuclear OGDH study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29211711/) directly detects a small DLD pool in human HEK293/U251 nuclei. Its OGDH-dependent association with KAT2A supplies succinyl-CoA; KAT2A performs histone succinyl transfer. The [current HPA page](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000091140-DLD/subcellular) reports supported nucleoplasm staining and a principal mitochondrial pool. These secondary compartments remain compatible with the matrix refinement.

[The sperm study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15888450/) provides positive homolog evidence for the four contextual localizations: hamster acrosomal and flagellar principal-piece staining with antigen-preabsorption controls (Fig. 2), mouse acrosomal staining (Fig. 3A/B), and hamster detergent/shearing/exocytosis experiments resolving acrosomal matrix (Fig. 4). This original was read through an author-deposited indexed copy in addition to the newly fetched abstract cache. The mouse donor GOA labels extend beyond the explicitly mouse assay resolution; the hamster experiments supply the corresponding compartment evidence. Human anti-E3 staining failed, and anti-whole-PDH positivity is not DLD-specific. HPA's human principal-piece and primary-cilium staining both have uncertain reliability. These are separate observations: rodent motile sperm flagella do not establish human primary-cilium localization. No sperm process or universal natural-product localization is inferred.
