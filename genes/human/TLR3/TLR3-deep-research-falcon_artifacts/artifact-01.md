(pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) | **TAK1** activates the **IKK complex** (IKKα, IKKβ, and NEMO/IKKγ), causing IκBα phosphorylation and release of NF-κB p50/p65. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000023) | TAK1 activates the **IKK complex**, leading to IκB degradation and nuclear translocation of NF-κB. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000023) |
| **MyD88-dependent amplification—emerging 2025 model** | Although TRIF remains indispensable, activated TLR3 was reported to associate with **MyD88** and induce MyD88 oligomerization in macrophages; this oligomerization required TLR3 and was strongly dependent on TRIF. (pqac-00000059, pqac-00000060) | MyD88 contributes to phosphorylation of **NF-κB p65** and **IκBα** and may sustain later TBK1 phosphorylation. Its absence reduces—but does not eliminate—TLR3 responses, whereas TRIF loss abolishes responsiveness. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000060) | Amplification of NF-κB activity and production of IL-6, TNF-α, and IFN-β. This revises the traditional “exclusively TRIF-dependent” model, but presently rests mainly on mouse-macrophage evidence and requires broader validation. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000059) |
| **Positive regulation by TRIM56** | **TRIM56** associates early with TRIF following TLR3 stimulation. Its coiled-coil domain and C-terminal residues 434–610 provide a signaling scaffold; this activity is independent of its RING E3-ligase function. (pqac-00000054, pqac-00000056) | Phosphorylation of TRIM56 **Ser471, Ser475, and Ser710** is required for potentiation; Ser710 supports TRIF association. TRIM56 enhances both IRF3 and NF-κB branches without enhancing unrelated TNF-α or IL-1β pathways. (pqac-00000054, pqac-00000055) | Strengthens type I interferon, interferon-stimulated gene, chemokine, and antiviral-state induction specifically downstream of extracellular dsRNA–TLR3–TRIF signaling. (pqac-00000055, pqac-00000056) |
| **TRIF–RIPK1–FADD–caspase-8 apoptosis** | The TRIF RHIM engages **RIPK1**, which recruits **FADD** and promotes assembly of a death-inducing signaling complex. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000023) | FADD promotes oligomerization and activation of **caspase-8**, followed by executioner-caspase signaling. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000061) | Programmed apoptotic death of infected, stressed, or—in some settings—malignant cells; the magnitude and consequence depend on cell type and competing survival signals. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000035) |
| **TRIF–RIPK3–MLKL necroptosis** | TRIF engages **RIPK3** through RHIM-dependent interactions, particularly when caspase-8 activity is absent or inhibited. (pqac-00000019, pqac-00000023) | RIPK3 activation and autophosphorylation lead to phosphorylation and oligomerization of **MLKL**; activated MLKL translocates to and disrupts cellular membranes. (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000061) | Lytic **necroptotic cell death**, release of inflammatory cellular contents, and potential amplification of host defense or pathological tissue injury. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000023) |
