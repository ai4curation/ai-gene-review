Gene Description

ATG12 is a ubiquitin-like protein that works as a permanent modification rather than a reversible mark. It is activated by ATG7 acting as an E1 enzyme, carried by ATG10 as an E2, and conjugated through its C-terminal glycine to Lys130 of ATG5, where it remains constitutively - there is no deconjugating enzyme and essentially no other substrate. The ATG12-ATG5 conjugate that results is itself an enzyme: it has E3 ligase-like activity for the second autophagy conjugation cascade, promoting attachment of ATG8-family proteins to membrane phospholipid. ATG12 makes an identifiable contribution to that activity, since the high-affinity binding surface for ATG3, the E2 that carries the activated ATG8 protein, lies on ATG12 and not on ATG5. Bound to the N-terminal region of ATG16L1, the conjugate is delivered to the phagophore and drives the membrane expansion that builds an autophagosome. The same conjugate has a second, later role: bound instead to TECPR1, which competes with ATG16L1 for it, it acts at the autolysosome to promote autophagosome-lysosome fusion. ATG12 is also a point at which viruses engage the autophagy machinery, associating with RIG-I, MDA5 and MAVS to dampen type I interferon production and being required for translation of incoming hepatitis C virus RNA.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

phagophore membrane

Definition: The membrane system of a phagophore, comprising its two closely apposed lipid bilayers and the continuous rim that joins them, which expands by lipid acquisition and closes to form the double membrane of an autophagosome.

Justification: The four GO:0034045 assertions on this gene all describe the ATG12-ATG5 conjugate acting on the elongating phagophore bilayer, the location it reaches via ATG16L1. GO has autophagosome membrane and omegasome membrane but no phagophore membrane, so such annotations split between a term for the finished organelle and GO:0034045, which asserts via bounding_layer_of a bounding membrane the PAS does not have. See GO issue #29437 recommendation R3 and projects/CONDENSATES/GO_0034045-annotation-audit.md. Same term proposed in the ATG9A, WIPI2 and ATG16L1 reviews.

Core Functions

Acts as a ubiquitin-like modifier: activated by the E1 enzyme ATG7, transferred by the E2 ATG10, and conjugated through its C-terminal glycine to Lys130 of ATG5, where it remains permanently. This single conjugation event is what ATG12 exists to do, and it creates the functional unit of the second autophagy conjugation cascade.

Molecular Function:
protein tag activity

As half of the ATG12-ATG5 conjugate, contributes E3 ligase-like activity for attachment of ATG8-family proteins to membrane phospholipid. The contribution is specific and structurally located: the high-affinity binding site for the E2 enzyme ATG3 is exclusive to ATG12, so it is the subunit that engages the carrier of the activated ATG8 protein. Neither subunit has the activity alone, which is why the qualifier is contributes_to.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: ATG12 uses contributes_to for the Atg8-family ligase activity, which is the correct qualifier for a subunit of an E3-like conjugate. ATG5 and ATG16L1 do not. Should the three be harmonised, and is contributes_to the right answer for all of them given that the ATG3-binding surface is exclusive to ATG12?

Q: TECPR1 and ATG16L1 bind the ATG12-ATG5 conjugate mutually exclusively, sending it to two different membranes for two different jobs. Is that switch regulated, and if so by what - is there a signal that converts assembly-competent conjugate into fusion-competent conjugate?

Q: ATG12 is conjugated once, to one substrate, and never removed. Is the ubiquitin-like machinery here doing anything a constitutive fusion protein could not, or is the conjugation step a vestige of a system that was once regulated?

Q: Five of the eighteen protein binding annotations (CRX, MEOX2, IER2, SERTAD1, SERTAD2) come from a single binary interactome screen and involve transcription factors and immediate-early proteins. Have any been followed up, and should unreplicated high-throughput hits carry the same annotation weight as the ATG5 and ATG3 interactions?
