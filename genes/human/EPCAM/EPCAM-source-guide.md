# EPCAM source guide

This guide records original-paper access and the scope of the experiments used in the EPCAM review (2026-10-24). A publication cache's `full_text_available` flag describes the automated fetch, which can differ from separately recovered original material. Public links below identify the actual papers, data or author copies; no publication text has been added as quotations.

## Sources with remaining original-access limits

* [PMID:15195135](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.onc.1207610): abstract and Nature preview inspected; complete original unavailable. Independent original evidence resolves plasma/lateral membrane and contextual proliferation/transcription claims, without pretending that the original assays were inspected.
* [PMID:15922867](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15922867/): verified abstract reports E-FABP expression changes; original article was not recovered. No E-FABP catalytic or binding activity is transferred to EPCAM.
* [PMID:16054130](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16054130/): verified abstract describes rat experiments and human/rat tissue colocalization. Human CLDN7 association and lateral localization have independent original support. The exact older tight-junction localization remains unresolved.
* [PMID:1729376](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1729376/): murine cloning is the title's focus, but the actual abstract also describes human myeloma-cell FACS. Main original unavailable; no wrong-species attribution is inferred.
* [PMID:11487543](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11487543/): human intestinal vesicle abstract inspected, publisher original unavailable. Urinary-exosome data independently establish the general EPCAM compartment, while the older intestinal assay remains unverified.
* [PMID:16545622](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.modgep.2006.02.001): official metadata and abstract report mouse developmental expression profiling, including Tacstd1. Main text unavailable; the transferred ureteric bud development claim remains undecided.
* [PMID:19098912](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.283): verified PubMed abstract used only for the explicit genomic EPCAM-read-through/MSH2-silencing disease mechanism. Complete original was not inspected. This is not a DNA-repair or methyltransferase activity of EpCAM protein.

## Database and phylogenetic provenance

The [official UniProt record](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P16422.json) and [independent FASTA](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P16422.fasta) agree exactly on the displayed 314-residue precursor. Signal peptide 1–23 is followed by chain 24–314 (PRO_0000022467); the membrane helix is 266–288. The extracellular and cytoplasmic topology segments are 24–265 and 289–314. The record lists no alternative products. Shed EpEX and released EpICD are processing products; the study's recombinant tail boundaries do not automatically define the endogenous cleavage site.

The actual PTHR14168 PAINT assertions and [current PANTHER family tree](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/treeinfo?family=PTHR14168) place human P16422 below PTN008351906, PTN002680918 and PTN000366716. The last lies on the EPCAM side of the EPCAM/TACSTD2 duplication. RGD:621365 is rat Epcam/O55159; MGI:1861606 is mouse Tacstd2/Q8BGV3, a legitimate descendant source for deeper ancestral assertions. Human target evidence in its own IBA source set is expected. The cadherin-adhesion concern is the scope of the donor's experimental assertion, not a missing node, an invalid clade, donor count, or target self-inclusion.

The [current HPA record](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000119888-EPCAM/subcellular) reports supported plasma-membrane localization, with HPA067463 staining in MCF-7 and no staining in the displayed CACO-2/U2OS samples. [SL-0153](https://rest.uniprot.org/locations/SL-0153.json) maps lateral membrane to GO:0016328, [SL-0265](https://rest.uniprot.org/locations/SL-0265.json) maps tight junction to GO:0005923, and [IPR043406](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/entry/InterPro/IPR043406/) maps the EPCAM/Trop-2 family broadly to membrane. Correct source mappings do not resolve every biological specificity question.

Current [GO:0048863](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0048863) concerns acquisition of stem-cell features, distinguishing it from the lineage-output experiment reviewed here. [GO:0098641](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0098641) combines cadherin binding with an adhesion role and inherits the trans-cell contact requirement of [GO:0098632](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0098632); the human Y2H pair supports only [GO:0045296](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0045296). [GO:0050839](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0050839) describes binding to a cell adhesion molecule and does not itself assert that EPCAM forms a trans adhesive bond.
