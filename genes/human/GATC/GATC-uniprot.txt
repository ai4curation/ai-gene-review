CC   -!- FUNCTION: Structural subunit of the mitochondrial Glu-tRNA(Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex GatCAB. GatCAB allows the formation of
CC       correctly charged Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the transamidation of
CC       misacylated Glu-tRNA(Gln) in the mitochondria (PubMed:19805282). The
CC       reaction takes place in the presence of glutamine and ATP through an
CC       activated gamma-phospho-Glu-tRNA(Gln) (By similarity). This subunit
CC       probably forms a belt that wraps around the interface of the catalytic
CC       subunits A and B to stabilize the complex (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03149, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19805282,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:19805282}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Subunit of the heterotrimeric GatCAB amidotransferase (AdT)
CC       complex, composed of A (QRSL1), B (GATB) and C (GATC) subunits.
