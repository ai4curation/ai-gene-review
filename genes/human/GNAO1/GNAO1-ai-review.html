Gene Description

GNAO1 encodes the Go alpha subunit, an abundant neuronal heterotrimeric G protein that switches between GDP- and GTP-bound states to transmit receptor signals. Go participates in neurotransmitter-receptor control of channels, vesicle release and cAMP signaling, with different alpha isoforms and cellular contexts determining the output. Cellular suppression of cAMP can involve beta/gamma availability and pathway coupling rather than direct inhibition of every cyclase isoform. Go is prominent at neuronal plasma membranes and synapses and also regulates endocrine secretory-vesicle docking.

Core Functions

A heterotrimeric G alpha molecular switch couples activated GPCRs to intracellular effectors through GDP/GTP exchange and GTP hydrolysis; receptor and effector selectivity remain protein- and context-dependent.
