CC       generated by peroxisomal oxidases to water and oxygen, thereby
CC       protecting cells from the toxic effects of hydrogen peroxide
CC       (PubMed:7882369). Promotes growth of cells including T-cells, B-cells,
CC       myeloid leukemia cells, melanoma cells, mastocytoma cells and normal
CC       and transformed fibroblast cells (PubMed:7882369).
