Gene Description

RGS2 is a regulator of heterotrimeric G protein signaling that accelerates GTP hydrolysis on G alpha subunits, particularly Gq, thereby limiting receptor-driven signaling. It associates with the cytoplasmic surface of the plasma membrane and also occupies cytoplasmic and nuclear compartments; alternative translation initiation generates a shorter product with mitochondrial localization. RGS2 regulates vascular and cardiac signaling and can inhibit adenylyl cyclase and translation through mechanisms distinct from its GAP activity. Its physiological effects depend on receptor, cell type, expression level and protein isoform.
