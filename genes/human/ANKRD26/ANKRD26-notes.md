- [PMID:22666460, ANKRD26 and its interacting partners TRIO, GPS2, HMMR and DIPA regulate adipogenesis in 3T3-L1 cells](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0038130). Original Results, figures, Discussion and Methods read; the canonical cache remains abstract-only. Human ANKRD26 residues 1212–1710 were expressed in human 293T cells for co-immunoprecipitation. The four partners originated from a mouse embryo library. The four interactions are supported, but this does not establish a simultaneous four-partner complex or catalytic activity of ANKRD26. TRIO is a GEF; GPS2 and CCDC85B/DIPA are transcriptional coregulators; HMMR has intracellular cytoskeletal roles. Mouse 3T3-L1 knockdown supports an adipogenic phenotype. Cytoplasmic redistribution of GPS2/DIPA used the ANKRD26 fragment; the authors could not answer whether full-length protein produced that redistribution. Keep construct and species distinctions explicit. Supporting abstract text: “identified by yeast two-hybrid and immunoprecipitation assays”.
- [PMID:21399614, Novel asymmetrically localizing components of human centrosomes identified by complementary proteomics methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3102290/). Canonical extracted main text read; it omits detailed candidate tables/most Results despite its full-text availability flag. Do not claim reconstruction of the original ANKRD26-specific image. Later direct studies independently confirm and refine the centrosomal location.
- [PMID:33350495, ANKRD26 recruits PIDD1 to centriolar distal appendages to activate the PIDDosome following centrosome amplification](https://www.hollandlab.com/_files/ugd/cb5e85_fa11a565cb124429b1495f02b80877a6.pdf). Original recruitment, domain mapping, complementation and response-specific Results read in the author-hosted article; remaining Methods/supplements not fully inspected. ANKRD26 recruits PIDD1 through a coiled-coil region; deleting that region separates centrosomal localization from PIDD1 recruitment and downstream signaling. ANKRD26 is an upstream docking factor, not caspase-2 or the PIDD1 autoprotease. This study found normal ciliation frequency and no general requirement for DNA-damage-induced arrest. These are context-limited findings, not universal absence-of-function assertions.
- [PMID:33350486, Centriolar distal appendages activate the centrosome-PIDDosome-p53 signalling axis via ANKRD26](https://link.springer.com/article/10.15252/embj.2020104844). Original PIDD1 binding/recruitment, deletion-rescue Results and Discussion read; complete Methods/supplements not inspected. Yeast two-hybrid maps a PIDD1-interacting segment at ANKRD26 residues 911–1181. Its deletion preserves ANKRD26 localization but loses PIDD1 recruitment and PIDDosome activation in human RPE1 cells. Recruitment and PIDD1 autoproteolysis are separable requirements. This study's DNA-damage and ciliary-length observations have different assays from PMID:33350495; preserve that context rather than declaring either paper wrong.
- [PMID:36794499, ANKRD26 is a new regulator of type I cytokine receptor signaling in normal and pathological hematopoiesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10388282/). Identity, abstract, main cell-model Methods, receptor-interaction/internalization Results and Discussion read; supplements uninspected. Human ANKRD26 associates with MPL, EPOR and G-CSFR in heterologous co-IP and proximity assays, and modulates cytokine responses in human UT7/primary-cell models. Human ANKRD26 overexpression in murine Ba/F3 cells inhibits ligand-triggered receptor internalization. The authors explicitly leave direct binary receptor binding and the internalization mechanism unresolved. Neither receptor-associated kinase activity nor direct binding to a purified receptor follows from these experiments.
- [PMID:38493476, Ankrd26 is a retinoic acid-responsive plasma membrane-binding and -shaping protein critical for proper cell differentiation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38493476/). Official full abstract and publisher-indexed initial Results read; full Methods and detailed biochemical assays not yet inspected. The abstract reports an N-terminal amphipathic element mediating membrane association/bending, clustering in response to retinoic acid, and defects caused by an AML-associated truncation. Do not assign the precise experimental species, lipid selectivity or curvature preference without the relevant methods.
- [PMID:39882846, A hierarchical pathway for assembly of the distal appendages that organize primary cilia](https://cdn.elifesciences.org/articles/85999/elife-85999-v2.pdf). Original ANKRD26-specific Results, model and Discussion read; full Methods/supplements not yet inspected. ANKRD26 affects the outer CEP83 region and PIDD1 recruitment. Its loss delays ciliation at 24 hours, with recovery by 48 hours, while length and ARL13B defects persist. This refines older endpoint studies; it does not demonstrate that ANKRD26 directly performs every ciliogenesis step or binds ARL13B.

## Follow-up: membrane mechanism and term definitions

The [author-uploaded original article for PMID:38493476](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379037085_Ankrd26_is_a_retinoic_acid-responsive_plasma_membrane-binding_and_-shaping_protein_critical_for_proper_cell_differentiation) resolved the earlier Methods gap. DNA constructs, purification, liposome preparation, cosedimentation, and the relevant Results/Figure 3 were read. Human constructs derive from NM_014915.2; human residues 45–217 preferentially bind small versus large vesicles. Mouse experiments are separately identified. Purified N-terminal modules also reshape membranes; the AML truncation retains curvature preference despite impaired anchoring and shaping. This supports evaluating curvature sensing separately from bending, without inferring lipid specificity.

Official GO definitions checked: [GO:0140090 membrane curvature sensor activity](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0140090), [GO:0030674 protein-macromolecule adaptor activity](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0030674), and [GO:0140378 protein complex scaffold activity](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0140378). The latter requires an integral structural component holding a complex together; PIDD1 docking should not automatically become that assertion. [GO:0097539 ciliary transition fiber](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0097539) includes centriolar distal appendage as a synonym, but has a ciliary complex definition. Preserve the original centrosome assertion while considering the precise localization in core functions. [GO:0001221 transcription coregulator binding](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0001221) describes interaction with coregulators and does not imply that ANKRD26 itself binds DNA or performs transcriptional coregulation.
