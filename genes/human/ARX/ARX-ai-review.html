Gene Description

ARX is a paired homeobox transcription factor that binds cis-regulatory DNA and activates or represses gene expression according to target and cellular context. It acts in the nucleus and participates in neuronal development, pancreatic alpha-cell specification and selected intestinal endocrine lineages. ARX can cooperate with the histone demethylase PHF8 in activating KDM5C; histone demethylation is performed by the associated enzymes. Pathogenic ARX variants cause a spectrum of X-linked neurodevelopmental disorders, including developmental and epileptic encephalopathy, Partington syndrome and lissencephaly with abnormal genitalia. Developmental effects and endocrine subtype requirements differ among experimental systems.

Core Functions

Recognizes cis-regulatory DNA and regulates RNA polymerase II transcription, repressing targets such as LMO1/SHOX2 and enhancing KDM5C activation together with PHF8. This nuclear activity contributes to neuronal development and selected pancreatic and intestinal endocrine differentiation programs; direct downstream targets for every developmental context remain unresolved.
