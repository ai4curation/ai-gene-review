# MGAT4C notes

MGAT4C was cloned as a human GnT-IV homolog from the 12q21 region commonly deleted in
pancreatic cancer (PMID:10570912). UniProt curates EC 2.4.1.145 and the GnT-IV RHEA
reaction on the reviewed record, but the human literature is thinner than for MGAT4A and
MGAT4B.

MGAT4C's reviewed UniProt record places it in the same alpha1,3-arm beta1,4-GlcNAc
branch-forming family as MGAT4A and MGAT4B.

Reactome uses MGAT4C as a putative GnT-IV participant in the branch-addition events.
Those rows are kept, with the direct catalytic statement coming from the reviewed UniProt
record and PANTHER rather than from human MGAT4C biochemistry.
