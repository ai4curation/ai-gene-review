# MGAT4C notes

MGAT4C was cloned as a human GnT-IV homolog from the 12q21 region commonly deleted in
pancreatic cancer (PMID:10570912). UniProt curates EC 2.4.1.145 and the GnT-IV RHEA
reaction on the reviewed record, but the human literature is thinner than for MGAT4A and
MGAT4B.

MGAT4C's reviewed UniProt record places it in the same alpha1,3-arm beta1,4-GlcNAc
branch-forming family as MGAT4A and MGAT4B.

Reactome uses MGAT4C as a putative GnT-IV participant in the branch-addition events.
Those rows are kept, with the direct catalytic statement coming from the reviewed UniProt
record and PANTHER rather than from human MGAT4C biochemistry. The same Reactome summary
erroneously calls MGAT4C a putative chromosome 2 gene; Furukawa et al. cloned it from the
commonly deleted 12q21 region in pancreatic cancer (PMID:10570912).

Sakamoto et al. cloned chicken GnT-VI/Q9DGD1 from hen oviduct and showed that it catalyzed the
alpha-1,6-arm GnT-VI reaction, whereas human GnT-IV-homologue/MGAT4C-transfected COS-1 cells had no
detectable GnT-VI activity (PMID:10962001). That negative result rules out GnT-VI activity for MGAT4C
but does not directly assay the alpha-1,3-arm GnT-IV reaction.
