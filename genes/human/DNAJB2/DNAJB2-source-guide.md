# DNAJB2 source and assay guide

DNAJB2 is human HGNC:5228, UniProt P25686, also called HSJ1/HSPF3. The review preserves 41 historical source assertions and separately incorporates five newly retrieved GOA assertion triples. The [current normal GOA export](DNAJB2-goa.tsv) is used for validation; the [historical export](source-snapshots/2026-10-17-main-16996128-DNAJB2-goa.tsv) preserves the original bytes. The [source provenance](DNAJB2-source-provenance.yaml) lists the exact 41-to-40-triple transition and five current additions. Review actions apply to biological claims and retain source-specific limitations.

## Mechanism and product scope

The [official UniProt record](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P25686/entry) identifies P25686-3 as the displayed 324-residue HSJ1b product and P25686-2 as the 277-residue HSJ1a product. Both retain the J domain and two ubiquitin-interacting motifs. HSJ1a retains canonical residues 1–274, replaces GGR at 275–277 with DVF, and lacks residues 278–324, including the long product’s prenylated Cys321. Product identity was checked against the official individual FASTAs. This does not authenticate every older named HSJ1a expression clone as the exact modern 277-residue sequence.

The [GO:0140309 definition](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0140309) requires prevention of aggregation plus delivery to an acceptor or location. Binding an unfolded protein alone is insufficient. [GO:0042026](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0042026) requires restoration of biological activity, whereas [GO:0140036](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0140036) combines modification-dependent recognition with an adaptor role. These distinctions separate ATPase activation, antiaggregation, functional recovery and client sorting.

The second core now explicitly uses GO:0140309 for the combined antiaggregation-and-delivery function. The live GO:0140036 annotation still records modification-dependent recognition and adaptor activity, which supplies one mechanism of that coupled unit. No third core, NEW annotation row or `contributes_to` molecular-function qualifier is required. The five historical holdase replacement recommendations remain reviewable on their retired source rows.

The two core activities describe a coordinated system rather than a universal outcome. The J domain can promote degradation while UIM-dependent preservation retards it for the same Atx3 client. HSJ1b overexpression can enhance rhodopsin inclusions in its ER context. Neither effect invalidates antiaggregation in the separately demonstrated systems.

## Binding records and localization

[PMID:25036637](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4104544/) supplies LUMIER Methods and the literal human MLF1–DNAJB2 record **EBI-9364639 / IM-22301-375**: Renilla-tagged MLF1 and FLAG-tagged DNAJB2 in HEK293T cells. [PMID:33961781](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8165030/) supplies the HCT116 AP-MS record **EBI-54862867 / IM-29278-19149**: tagged MLF1 bait and DNAJB2 prey, with spoke expansion. Both pairs’ sequence/taxon identifiers match the current canonical proteins. These are positive lysate associations, not purified direct-contact measurements. MLF1’s tentative transcription-factor designation does not justify a more specific binding activity from these experiments. The independently recovered original Methods are distinct from the partial normal cache extractions.

The PMID:21625540 annotation’s WITH/FROM is **P25686-2**. Its exact self-association assay remains unresolved; current IntAct and MINT records did not supply it. The verified primary abstract of the inherited [DNAJB2a extension study](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddad058) independently reports wild-type and mutant polydisperse oligomers. This supports retention of gene-level association while leaving original-pair attribution and oligomer stoichiometry open.

The [HPA DNAJB2 record](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000135924-DNAJB2/subcellular) and its [official XML](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000135924.xml) report enhanced-reliability nuclear-membrane ICC/IF localization in A-431, U-251MG and U2OS, with an siRNA validation record. The record was read; image pixels were not independently scored, and no splice-product identity is inferred from the antibody signal.

Positive binding is preserved under the [project’s explicit policy](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/16996128f5e52475aeffd6b3a573cdb94b1ff9bc/projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions). Genericity is not a reason to remove an experimentally positive association. Specific refinements require the actual partner and activity context.
