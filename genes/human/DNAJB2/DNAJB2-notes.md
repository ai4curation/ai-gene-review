# DNAJB2 review notes

## 2026-10-17 — existing-review augmentation

The historical review contains 41 source assertions. All remain unchanged outside their reviewer-supplied assessments. Five additional assertion triples were genuinely retrieved from current GOA and are reviewed separately, giving 46 contexts. These are source additions rather than `NEW` proposals. The original GOA, UniProt and Falcon files are preserved; the contemporary normal GOA export is provided as [DNAJB2-current-goa.tsv](DNAJB2-current-goa.tsv). Earlier curation remains available in repository history. No previous notes file existed at the input boundary.

The biological synthesis now has two coupled activities: Hsp70 ATPase activation and ubiquitin-dependent client recognition/triage. The former broad folding core repeated those mechanisms and overstated the assigned reporter experiments. [PMID:21231916](https://doi.org/10.1042/BJ20101247) found that DNAJB2a suppresses aggregation but does not improve luciferase refolding. Independent [PMID:20889486](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20889486/) supports J-domain-dependent recovery of mutant Parkin recruitment and mitophagy-related function. This supports client-specific folding recovery, without turning antiaggregation into a refolding assay or generalizing the negative luciferase result to all clients.

The four obsolete GO:0051082 source assertions and the general transporter assertion are refined to GO:0140309. Its delivery requirement is supported by the independent primary abstract of [PMID:15936278](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2005.04.058), which describes HSJ1 client recognition, aggregation prevention and proteasome sorting. The individual SOD1 and luciferase experiments supply binding/antiaggregation context; they are not falsely described as direct delivery measurements. The activity is integrated with client recognition and triage, not treated as an unrelated third core.

The two MLF1 associations are retained as non-core positive evidence. The historical self-product WITH/FROM assertion from PMID:21625540 remains unresolved at the exact assay level, but the independently verified [DNAJB2a extension study](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddad058) reports wild-type as well as mutant polydisperse oligomers. That supports a gene-level self-association without establishing a homodimer or retroactively verifying the original pair. This follows the [project’s explicit curation instructions](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/16996128f5e52475aeffd6b3a573cdb94b1ff9bc/projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions) for preserving positive generic binding when a more informative specific activity is not justified.

Two inherited removals claiming that PMID:9553041 concerns only a different gene are corrected. The full original genuinely tests a human HSJ1 J domain in engineered WT1 fusions. Its growth effects depend on the fusion context and WT1 DNA-binding function, so assigning them to native full-length DNAJB2 is marked over-annotated. The experimental observation is preserved. Likewise, constitutive expression is not a reason to deny response to unfolded protein: the older cloning paper is limited evidence, while later target studies establish action on misfolded clients.

Current HPA ICC/IF records resolve the previous access-based uncertainty about nuclear-membrane localization, without assigning the antibody signal to a specific splice product. The official products remain P25686-3/HSJ1b, 324 residues, and P25686-2/HSJ1a, 277 residues. Historical 274-residue constructs and engineered domains are distinguished from those natural products.

Four short literal publication anchors occur once each in the YAML cores. Full source access, assay/construct limits and public locators are in the [source guide](DNAJB2-source-guide.md). Publication cache flags remain unchanged even where an original was recovered separately. The existing provider report is preserved rather than replaced with manually authored provider text.
