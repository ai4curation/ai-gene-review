ARMC9 is an 818-residue LisH/armadillo-repeat protein that organizes a ciliary protein interaction network. Human cell localization places it at the basal body and daughter centriole; mammalian ciliary localization is dynamic, with Hedgehog-dependent tip enrichment in the mouse cell systems studied. Its partners include TOGARAM1, CSPP1 and CCDC66. The strongest mechanistic evidence now distinguishes its structural contribution to a complex from autonomous microtubule binding or polymerase activity. Biallelic variants cause Joubert syndrome 30, with the characteristic hindbrain malformation and variable neurological, ocular and other ciliopathy features. No enzyme activity is inferred from altered tubulin modifications, mitochondrial phenotypes, or association with an enzyme/assembly factor.
