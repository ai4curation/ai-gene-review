CC   -!- FUNCTION: Scaffold protein that plays an essential role in cytoplasmic
CC       stress granule formation which acts as a platform for antiviral
CC       signaling (PubMed:23279204, PubMed:32302570, PubMed:32302571,
CC       PubMed:32302572). Plays an essential role in stress granule formation
CC       (PubMed:27022092, PubMed:32302570, PubMed:32302571, PubMed:32302572,
CC       PubMed:35977029). Stress granules are membraneless compartments that
CC       store mRNAs and proteins, such as stalled translation pre-initiation
CC       complexes, in response to stress (PubMed:32302570, PubMed:32302571,
CC       PubMed:32302572). Promotes formation of stress granules phase-separated
CC       membraneless compartment by undergoing liquid-liquid phase separation
CC       (LLPS) upon unfolded RNA-binding: functions as a molecular switch that
CC       triggers RNA-dependent LLPS in response to a rise in intracellular free
CC       RNA concentrations (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q13283,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23279204, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27022092,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32302570, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32302571,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32302572, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35977029}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Under physiological conditions, G3BP2 adopts a
CC       compact state that is stabilized by intramolecular interactions between
CC       the RG-rich and the acidic regions that inhibit phase separation. Upon
CC       stress, polysomes disassemble and mRNAs are released in an unfolded
CC       protein-free state. Binding of unfolded mRNA to G3BP2 outcompetes the
CC       intramolecular interactions and RNA-bound G3BP2 adopts an expanded
CC       conformation in which the RG-rich region becomes exposed to engage in
CC       protein-protein and protein-RNA interactions, allowing physical cross-
CC       linking of RNA molecules to form protein-RNA condensates, leading to
CC       liquid-liquid phase separation (LLPS). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q13283}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms homooligomers (PubMed:23279204). Forms heterodimers with
CC       G3BP1 (PubMed:23279204). Interacts with NFKBIA (via N-terminus)
CC       (PubMed:10969074). Interacts (via NTF2 domain) with USP10; inhibiting
CC       stress granule formation (PubMed:23279204, PubMed:27022092,
CC       PubMed:31981475). Interacts (via NTF2 domain) with CAPRIN1; promoting
CC       stress granule formation (PubMed:27022092). Associates (via RG-rich
CC       region) with 40S ribosome subunits (PubMed:27022092). Interacts with
CC       PABPC1 (PubMed:23279204). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10969074,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23279204, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27022092,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31981475}.
