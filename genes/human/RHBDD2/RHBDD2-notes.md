# RHBDD2 curation notes

Deep-research services were unavailable for this family when reviewed. The
manual synthesis below uses the cached UniProt, GOA, PANTHER/PAINT and
publication records.

RHBDD2 is the reviewed human RHBDD2 branch in PTHR11009 and differs from the
three reviewed human DERL paralogs. Its UniProt record places it by similarity
at the Golgi/cis-Golgi network membrane and cautions that the protein is related
to the S54 peptidase family but lacks the conserved active sites, making
peptidase activity unsupported [file:human/RHBDD2/RHBDD2-uniprot.txt, "lacks the
conserved active sites, suggesting that it has no peptidase activity"].

The PTHR11009 PAINT root PTN000109990 includes DERL1, DERL2, DERL3 and RHBDD2.
Its ER membrane and ERAD IBDs fit the three human DERL paralogs but over-propagate
into the Golgi RHBDD2 branch, so the family review proposes single-gene IRDs at
the RHBDD2 leaf. The signal-recognition-particle binding IBA in the cached GOA
file appears stale because GO:0005047 is no longer present in the current
PTHR11009 PAINT slice.
