# RHBDD2 curation notes

Deep-research services were unavailable for this family when reviewed. The
manual synthesis below uses the cached UniProt, GOA, PANTHER/PAINT and
publication records.

RHBDD2 is the reviewed human RHBDD2 branch in PTHR11009 and differs from the
three reviewed human DERL paralogs. Ahmedli et al. directly placed mouse Rhbdd2
at the Golgi in retinal fractions and by GM130 colocalization and reported a
homozygous human R85H variant that co-segregated with autosomal recessive
retinitis pigmentosa in one family [PMID:23386608, "only the S 2 fraction (the
Golgi membrane fraction) contains an appreciable amount of RHBDD2 protein";
PMID:23386608, "We found a homozygous mutation in the RHBDD2 gene, R85H, that
co-segregates with disease in a family affected with arRP"]. The
reviewed UniProt record places human RHBDD2 at the Golgi/cis-Golgi network
membrane and cautions that the protein is related to the S54 peptidase family
but lacks the conserved active sites, making peptidase activity unsupported
[file:human/RHBDD2/RHBDD2-uniprot.txt, "lacks the conserved active sites,
suggesting that it has no peptidase activity"].

The PTHR11009 PAINT root PTN000109990 includes DERL1, DERL2, DERL3 and RHBDD2.
Its ER membrane, ERAD and signal-recognition-particle-binding IBDs fit the three
human DERL paralogs but over-propagate into the Golgi RHBDD2 branch, so the
family review proposes single-gene IRDs at the RHBDD2 leaf.
