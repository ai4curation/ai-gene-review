# DZIP1L evidence notes

## 2026-10-18: initial source-qualified review

DZIP1L is associated with centrioles and ciliary basal bodies and with SEPTIN2. Human evidence supports regulation of polycystin localization, while effects on cilium assembly vary by cell type and organism. The 2009 human study requires combined DZIP1/DZIP1L depletion for the reported reduction in ciliogenesis; human localization uses GFP-DZIP1L IMAGE4940443 in hTERT-RPE1. The 2017 study includes endogenous human reciprocal SEPTIN2 co-immunoprecipitation and N-terminal fragment mapping. Patient B155 fibroblasts need not lose cilia to show altered PC1/PC2 distribution. Mouse and zebrafish phenotypes are kept distinct. These results support one integrated SEPTIN2-binding and ciliary-organization core without assigning SEPTIN2 GTP hydrolysis to DZIP1L (PMID:19852954; PMID:28530676).

The 2018 mouse study separately supports Cby positioning and ciliary-bud formation. Mouse Dzip1l and Cby constructs in HEK293 cells remain mouse-protein experiments. The mutant cells retain Tctn2 and IFT recruitment despite defects in other steps. Thus neither universal cilium absence nor a generalized failure of every recruitment event is claimed (PMID:29487109).
