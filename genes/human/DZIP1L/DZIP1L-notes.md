## 2026-10-18: initial source-qualified review

Human evidence supports regulation of polycystin localization, while effects on cilium assembly vary by cell type and organism. The 2009 human study requires combined DZIP1/DZIP1L depletion for the reported reduction in ciliogenesis; human localization uses GFP-DZIP1L IMAGE4940443 in hTERT-RPE1.
