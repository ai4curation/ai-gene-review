This guide links the evidence behind the localized and binding-class assertions. The [preserved source-evidence record](DZIP1L-source-evidence.yaml) retains the detailed source boundaries, and the [reproducible source audit](DZIP1L-bioinformatics/evidence/RESULTS.md) derives interaction identities, propagation, per-location HPA confidence and product distinctions from saved inputs. ## Human transition-zone evidence

[Lu et al., PMID:28530676](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5687889/) reports endogenous DZIP1L staining with two antibodies. The Results describing Figure 4j–k distinguish human dermal-fibroblast colocalization with TCTN1 from human RPE-1 3D-SIM showing nearby DZIP1L/TCTN1 signals. The immunofluorescence Methods specify the antibodies, fixation and structured-illumination acquisition. This supports **GO:0035869 ciliary transition zone**, in addition to independently retained centriole and basal-body locations. The reported approximately 120-nm lateral resolution is not a molecular contact measurement, an exclusive-residence claim or a purified gate-complex assay. The text and Methods were read; no new figure-pixel inspection is claimed.

The current [transition-zone definition](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0035869) and typed ancestry distinguish it from [centriole](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005814) and [ciliary basal body](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0036064). One authored IDA/located_in row captures this measured target location. No new biological-process term follows from the definition's discussion of gating. The same study's human families and [official DZIP1L disease record](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8IYY4/entry) support autosomal-recessive polycystic kidney disease 5 context.

[Glazer et al., PMID:19852954](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2799895/) independently supports tagged human DZIP1L at the ciliary base and the combined DZIP1/DZIP1L depletion phenotype. The archived human IMAGE4940443 clone has the long-product architecture but three differences from the current displayed sequence. [Wang et al., PMID:29487109](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5897601/) uses mouse Dzip1l constructs and mutant cells to examine Cby positioning and ciliary-bud formation. These source-specific results do not imply that every human product or every cell type has identical behavior.

## Positive binding and independent partner class

[GO:0008092](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0008092) covers binding to a cytoskeletal protein component. The [microtubule-cytoskeleton definition](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0015630) includes associated proteins. Thus a documented centrosomal or centriolar component can qualify without itself polymerizing into a filament; mere transient localization would not establish that class. [GO:0097110](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0097110) identifies binding to a signaling scaffold. These are partner-class terms, not assertions that DZIP1L performs the partner's activity.

## Access and confidence distinctions

The PMID:29892012 machine cache marks full text available but contains only partial article text, omitting operative Methods/Results. A separately attempted PMC representation is a challenge page and was excluded. The review therefore keeps its explicit incomplete-access flag and makes no mutation-mechanism claim from that missing text. The positive human DZIP1L–CEP76 record is independently checked in IntAct, with corroborating positive pairs in PMID:25416956 and PMID:31515488 and a separately established CEP76 class. This is sufficient for the bounded class-binding assertion; citation correctness and availability remain separate assessments. Genuine cache bytes and flags are preserved.
