Gene Description

Complement factor H is a secreted complement regulator that recognizes C3b and host-associated polyanions. Its N-terminal domains support factor-I-mediated C3b cleavage and accelerate decay of the alternative-pathway C3 convertase, while C-terminal recognition domains help concentrate this protection on host surfaces. The shorter splice product FHL-1 shares the first seven complement-control domains and regulatory activity but lacks the C-terminal recognition region of full-length factor H. Factor H also associates with pentraxins, oxidation-modified proteins and selected microbial surface proteins in context-dependent interactions. Human CFH variants can impair complement control and predispose to renal or retinal disease. In mouse neurons, intracellular factor H can additionally protect against oxidative CNS injury through a C-terminal-domain-dependent mechanism.
