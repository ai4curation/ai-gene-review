CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of neuronal acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) that
CC       function as pentameric, ligand-gated cation channels with high calcium
CC       permeability among other activities. nAChRs are excitatory
CC       neurotrasnmitter receptors formed by a collection of nAChR subunits
CC       known to mediate synaptic transmission in the nervous system and the
CC       neuromuscular junction. Each nAchR subunit confers differential
CC       attributes to channel properties, including activation, deactivation
CC       and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, cation permeability, and
CC       binding to allosteric modulators (PubMed:31488329, PubMed:31708116).
CC       CHRNA3 forms heteropentameric neuronal acetylcholine receptors with
CC       CHRNB2 and CHRNB4, with CHRNA5, and CHRNB3 as accesory subunits
CC       (PubMed:20881005, PubMed:8663494). CHRNA3:CHRNB4 being predominant in
CC       neurons of the autonomic ganglia, it is known as ganglionic nicotinic
CC       receptor (PubMed:31488329). CHRNA3:CHRNB4 or CHRNA3:CHRNA5:CHRNB4 play
CC       also an important role in the habenulo-interpeduncular tract,
CC       modulating the mesolimbic dopamine system and affecting reward circuits
CC       and addiction (By similarity). Hypothalamic CHRNA3:CHRNB4 nAChR
CC       activation by nicotine leads to activation of POMC neurons and a
CC       decrease in food intake (By similarity). Also expressed in the
CC       urothelium where it modulates reflex bladder activity by increasing
CC       intracellular calcium through extracellular influx and basal ATP
CC       release (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04757,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R4G9, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20881005,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31488329, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31708116,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663494}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Neuronal AChR is composed of two different types of subunits:
CC       alpha and beta. CHRNA3/Alpha-3 subunit can be combined to CHRNA5/alpha-
CC       5, CHRNB2/beta-2 CHRNB3/beta-3 or CHRNB4/beta-4 to give rise to
CC       functional receptors (PubMed:20881005, PubMed:31488329,
CC       PubMed:8663494). Forms stoichiometries such as (CHRNA3)2:(CHRNB4)3 or
CC       (CHRNA3:CHRNB4)2:CHRNB3 (PubMed:11118490). Part of a complex composed
CC       of STUB1/CHIP, VCP/p97, CHRNA3, and UBXN2A that modulates the
CC       ubiquitination and endoplasmic reticulum-associated degradation (ERAD)
CC       of CHRNA3 (PubMed:26265139). Within the complex UBXN2A acts as a
CC       scaffold protein required for the interaction of CHRNA3 with VCP/p97,
CC       this interaction also inhibits CHRNA3 ubiquitination by STUB1/CHIP and
CC       subsequently ERAD (PubMed:26265139). Interacts with UBXN2A (via SEP
CC       domain), the interaction is required for the interaction of CHRNA3 in
CC       the STUB1:VCP:UBXN2A complex (PubMed:26265139). Interacts with RIC3;
CC       which is required for proper folding and assembly (PubMed:16120769).
CC       Interacts with LYPD6 (PubMed:27344019). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11118490,

CC       and secondary CAKUT (BAIPRCK) [MIM:191800]: An autosomal recessive

CC       insufficiency. Additional autonomic features are impaired pupillary
CC       reflex and orthostatic hypotension. The disease manifests in utero or
CC       early childhood. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31708116}. Note=The disease is
CC       caused by variants affecting the gene represented in this entry.
