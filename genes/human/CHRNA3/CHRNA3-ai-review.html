Gene Description

Human alpha3 contributes principal ligand-binding faces to heteromeric ganglionic receptors with beta4 or beta2, sometimes including accessory alpha5 or beta3. Reconstituted alpha3beta4 and alpha3beta4beta3 stoichiometries are context-specific. These pentameric membrane proteins couple extracellular acetylcholine recognition to cation flux and neuronal excitability. Alpha3 is a major autonomic ganglion receptor component, and loss-of-function variants cause bladder dysfunction and dysautonomia.
