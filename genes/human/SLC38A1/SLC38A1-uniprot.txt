CC   -!- FUNCTION: Symporter that cotransports short-chain neutral amino acids
CC       and sodium ions from the extracellular to the intracellular side of the
CC       cell membrane (PubMed:10891391, PubMed:20599747). The transport is
CC       elctrogenic, pH dependent and driven by the Na(+) electrochemical
CC       gradient (PubMed:10891391). Participates in the astroglia-derived
CC       glutamine transport into GABAergic interneurons for neurotransmitter
CC       GABA de novo synthesis (By similarity). May also contributes to amino
CC       acid transport in placental trophoblasts (PubMed:20599747). Also
CC       regulates synaptic plasticity (PubMed:12388062).
