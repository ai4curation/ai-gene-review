# Comprehensive Research Report: FGF21 (Fibroblast Growth Factor 21)

## Gene and Protein Identity

FGF21 (gene ID: FGF21, UniProt: Q9NSA1) encodes human Fibroblast Growth Factor 21, a member of the fibroblast growth factor superfamily that functions as an endocrine hormone rather than a traditional paracrine growth factor (geng2020thetherapeuticpotential pages 1-2, flippo2021metabolicmessengersfgf21 pages 5-6). The protein belongs to the heparin-binding growth factors family, though unlike canonical FGFs, it exhibits weak heparin affinity, which enables it to circulate systemically and function as an endocrine hormone (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 1-2, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4). FGF21 consists of 181 amino acids in its mature secreted form and is classified within the endocrine FGF subfamily (FGF15/19, FGF21, and FGF23) that regulates metabolic homeostasis (salgado2021fgf21andchronic pages 1-2).

## Primary Function and Mechanism of Action

### Endocrine Signaling Molecule

FGF21 functions primarily as a stress-inducible endocrine hormone that coordinates systemic energy metabolism in response to nutritional and metabolic challenges (geng2020thetherapeuticpotential pages 1-2, flippo2021metabolicmessengersfgf21 pages 5-6, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3). Unlike enzymes, FGF21 has no catalytic activity or substrate specificity; instead, it acts as a signaling molecule that communicates metabolic states between tissues (chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 1-2). Its primary role is to integrate and communicate hepatic nutrient status to peripheral organs, coordinating adaptive responses to fasting, protein restriction, carbohydrate excess, cold exposure, and cellular stress (flippo2021metabolicmessengersfgf21 pages 5-6, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4).

### Metabolic Regulation

FGF21 regulates multiple aspects of energy homeostasis including: (1) glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity, (2) lipid metabolism and fatty acid oxidation, (3) energy expenditure and thermogenesis, and (4) macronutrient preference and feeding behavior (yan2021therolesand pages 2-3, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3, zhang2025researchprogresson pages 2-3). During fasting, FGF21 promotes hepatic fatty acid oxidation and ketogenesis while supporting peripheral glucose uptake and utilization (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, tillman2020fgf21anemerging pages 9-10). In adipose tissue, it enhances insulin sensitivity, promotes glucose uptake via GLUT1, stimulates lipolysis and adiponectin secretion, and induces browning of white adipose tissue (chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3, tillman2020fgf21anemerging pages 9-10, kaur2022multiorganfgf21fgfr1signaling pages 2-3).

## Receptor Complex and Signaling Mechanism

### Receptor Components

FGF21 signals through a heterodimeric receptor complex consisting of an FGF receptor (FGFR) and the obligate co-receptor β-klotho (KLB) at the plasma membrane (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 1-2, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3). FGFR1c is the predominant receptor isoform mediating metabolic effects, though FGFR2c and FGFR3c can contribute in specific contexts (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, zhang2025researchprogresson pages 2-3, kaur2022multiorganfgf21fgfr1signaling pages 1-2). The receptor complex forms through a dual-binding mechanism: the C-terminal domain of FGF21 binds β-klotho with relatively high affinity (Kd ~20-40 nM), while the N-terminal domain engages the FGFR with much weaker direct affinity (>10 μM), requiring cooperative assembly for effective signaling (an2023heparinisessential pages 7-8, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4).

A 2023 study revealed that heparin plays an essential role in optimal FGF21 signaling by supporting FGFR1 dimerization and regulating β-klotho cellular stability, adding mechanistic detail to how this receptor complex is assembled and maintained (an2023heparinisessential pages 7-8). Neither FGFR nor β-klotho can be activated by FGF21 independently; both components are required for functional signaling (chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 1-2, flippo2021metabolicmessengersfgf21 pages 1-2).

### Downstream Signaling Pathways

Upon receptor engagement, FGFR1 undergoes autophosphorylation and activates multiple intracellular signaling cascades (kaur2022multiorganfgf21fgfr1signaling pages 1-2, geng2020thetherapeuticpotential pages 3-4). The MAPK/ERK pathway is activated through phosphorylation of the adaptor protein FRS2α, which recruits GRB2 and SOS, leading to RAS activation and downstream ERK1/2 phosphorylation (kaur2022multiorganfgf21fgfr1signaling pages 2-3, geng2020thetherapeuticpotential pages 3-4, edirisinghe2024decodingfgffgfrsignaling pages 1-3). The PI3K/Akt pathway is engaged through FRS2α-GRB2-mediated recruitment of GAB1, resulting in PI3K activation and AKT phosphorylation (kaur2022multiorganfgf21fgfr1signaling pages 2-3, edirisinghe2024decodingfgffgfrsignaling pages 1-3, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4).

AMPK signaling represents a critical metabolic pathway downstream of FGF21, mediating effects on glucose uptake, mitochondrial biogenesis, and fatty acid oxidation (kaur2022multiorganfgf21fgfr1signaling pages 2-3, tan2023targetingfgf21in pages 14-15, zhang2025researchprogresson pages 3-4). FGF21 activates an AMPK-SIRT1-PGC-1α axis that enhances mitochondrial respiration and metabolic efficiency (zhang2025researchprogresson pages 3-4, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3). Additional signaling mechanisms include PLCγ-mediated calcium signaling, STAT pathway activation, and modulation of mTOR, NF-κB, and autophagy-related pathways (tan2023targetingfgf21in pages 14-15, zhang2025researchprogresson pages 3-4, edirisinghe2024decodingfgffgfrsignaling pages 1-3).

While ERK phosphorylation serves as a robust pharmacodynamic marker of FGF21 receptor activation, researchers emphasize that ERK alone cannot explain FGF21's diverse metabolic effects, and the complete molecular mechanisms linking receptor activation to tissue-specific metabolic outcomes remain areas of active investigation (geng2020thetherapeuticpotential pages 3-4, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3).

## Localization and Tissue Distribution

### Production and Secretion

FGF21 is synthesized as a secretory protein that is processed through the endoplasmic reticulum and COPII vesicle secretory pathway before being released into the extracellular space and circulation (flippo2021metabolicmessengersfgf21 pages 1-2). The liver is the predominant source of circulating FGF21 under basal physiological conditions in both rodents and humans (zhang2025researchprogresson pages 2-3, flippo2021metabolicmessengersfgf21 pages 1-2, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4). However, FGF21 can also be produced by multiple other tissues in stimulus-dependent contexts: brown adipose tissue during cold exposure, white adipose tissue during feeding or metabolic stress, skeletal muscle during exercise or mitochondrial dysfunction, and pancreas in response to metabolic signals (tucker2023fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, spann2022thenuancedmetabolic pages 10-11, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 1-2).

FGF21 functions as an extracellular hormone rather than an intracellular protein; it circulates in the bloodstream with a relatively short half-life of approximately 0.5-1.5 hours and acts on distant tissues by binding cell-surface receptor complexes (salgado2021fgf21andchronic pages 2-3, flippo2021metabolicmessengersfgf21 pages 1-2, salgado2021fgf21andchronic pages 1-2). Non-hepatic production often serves autocrine or paracrine functions, while hepatic FGF21 primarily acts through endocrine mechanisms (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, spann2022thenuancedmetabolic pages 10-11).

### Target Tissues

The tissue specificity of FGF21 action is determined primarily by the restricted expression pattern of β-klotho rather than the more ubiquitous FGFR expression (aaldijk2023biologicalandpharmacological pages 8-11, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4). The principal target tissues where β-klotho is expressed include adipose tissue (both white and brown), liver, pancreas, and specific regions of the central nervous system (aaldijk2023biologicalandpharmacological pages 8-11, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3, salgado2021fgf21andchronic pages 2-3, edirisinghe2024decodingfgffgfrsignaling pages 14-15). In rodents, β-klotho expression has been detected in discrete brain regions including the suprachiasmatic nucleus, nucleus of the solitary tract, ventromedial hypothalamus, paraventricular nucleus, and other areas involved in metabolic regulation (spann2022thenuancedmetabolic pages 7-8, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 1-2). However, human brain expression of β-klotho appears to be sparse or uncertain compared to robust rodent expression, representing a species difference relevant to translating preclinical findings (aaldijk2023biologicalandpharmacological pages 8-11).

Adipose tissue represents the clearest direct peripheral target of circulating FGF21, where it regulates glucose uptake, insulin sensitivity, lipolysis, thermogenesis, and adiponectin secretion (salgado2021fgf21andchronic pages 2-3, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, kaur2022multiorganfgf21fgfr1signaling pages 1-2). The heart also expresses β-klotho and FGFR1 and responds to FGF21 with cardioprotective effects (spann2022thenuancedmetabolic pages 7-8, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4).

## Transcriptional Regulation

### Nutrient-Sensing Pathways

Hepatic FGF21 expression is controlled by multiple nutrient-sensing transcription factors that respond to different metabolic states (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, spann2022thenuancedmetabolic pages 3-5, tan2023targetingfgf21in pages 1-2). During fasting and lipid flux, peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor alpha (PPARα) serves as the principal transcriptional regulator, with CREBH providing additional support (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, spann2022thenuancedmetabolic pages 3-5, purcbandurko2026fgf21andshbg pages 5-7, salgado2021fgf21andchronic pages 2-3). PPARα activation by free fatty acids induces hepatic FGF21 transcription as part of the adaptive response to fasting that includes increased fatty acid oxidation, ketogenesis, and gluconeogenesis (zhang2025researchprogresson pages 2-3, salgado2021fgf21andchronic pages 2-3, chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3, rhyu2021newlydiscoveredendocrine pages 16-17).

Carbohydrate or fructose excess induces FGF21 through carbohydrate response element binding protein (ChREBP), often with PPARα cooperation required for the full glucose response (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, spann2022thenuancedmetabolic pages 3-5, tan2023targetingfgf21in pages 1-2, salgado2021fgf21andchronic pages 2-3). This carbohydrate-responsive induction represents a feedback mechanism to limit further simple sugar intake (chen2022thepotentialfunction pages 2-3).

### Stress-Responsive Pathways

Protein restriction and amino acid deprivation activate the GCN2-eIF2α-ATF4 pathway, markedly increasing FGF21 transcription to support metabolic reprogramming during protein insufficiency (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, tan2023targetingfgf21in pages 1-2). Mitochondrial stress and endoplasmic reticulum stress activate ATF4 and CHOP transcription factors, which strongly induce FGF21 as part of the integrated stress response (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4, tan2023targetingfgf21in pages 1-2, rhyu2021newlydiscoveredendocrine pages 16-17). Post-transcriptionally, CNOT6L can negatively regulate FGF21 by promoting mRNA decay (tan2023targetingfgf21in pages 1-2).

Species differences exist in fasting responses: mice show robust FGF21 induction after 6-12 hours of fasting, while humans may require more prolonged fasting for significant circulating increases (spann2022thenuancedmetabolic pages 3-5, zhang2025researchprogresson pages 2-3).

## Recent Developments (2023-2024)

### Mechanistic Advances

Recent structural and biochemical studies have refined understanding of FGF21 receptor biology. A 2023 PNAS study demonstrated that heparin is essential for optimal FGF21 cellular signaling and for maintaining β-klotho stability at the cell surface, revealing a previously underappreciated component of the receptor activation mechanism (an2023heparinisessential pages 7-8, guo2024novelfgf21analogues pages 6-6). Structure-based optimization efforts have led to engineered FGF21 analogues with improved pharmacokinetic properties and enhanced binding affinity for the β-klotho/FGFR complex (guo2024novelfgf21analogues pages 6-6).

### Clinical Translation

Therapeutic development has focused on long-acting FGF21 analogues for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH), hypertriglyceridemia, and related cardiometabolic conditions (guo2024novelfgf21analogues pages 6-6, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 11-13). The HARMONY phase 2b trial evaluated efruxifermin (an FGF21 analogue) for NASH in 2023, while pegozafermin has shown benefits across hepatic and cardiometabolic endpoints including reduced liver fat and fibrosis, improved MASH resolution, substantially lower triglycerides, and increased HDL cholesterol (guo2024novelfgf21analogues pages 6-6, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 11-13). Phase 3 trials (ENLIGHTEN-Fibrosis and ENLIGHTEN-Cirrhosis) are evaluating histological outcomes in advanced liver disease (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 11-13).

A 2023 systematic review and meta-analysis of eight randomized controlled trials found that FGF21 analogues significantly reduced fasting insulin, body weight, and total cholesterol, but showed no significant aggregate effects on fasting glucose, HbA1c, HOMA index, blood free fatty acids, or systolic blood pressure (carbonetti2023useoffgf21 pages 12-13). The quality of evidence ranged from moderate to very low due to imprecision and indirection issues, indicating need for additional high-quality clinical trials (carbonetti2023useoffgf21 pages 12-13).

### Expanded Applications

Recent research has expanded potential FGF21 applications beyond diabetes and NAFLD to include cardiovascular disease, kidney disease, neurodegeneration, and other metabolic complications (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 11-13, rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 2-4). New mechanistic insights into antifibrotic effects include direct or indirect modulation of hepatic stellate cells and regulation of TGF-β/Smad, NF-κB, Wnt/β-catenin, and cellular stress pathways (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 11-13). Receptor-targeted biologics such as BFKB8488A, which selectively activate the FGFR1/β-klotho complex, represent alternative therapeutic strategies that have shown reductions in hepatic fat, triglycerides, and body weight with favorable adiponectin increases (rama2026fibroblastgrowthfactor pages 11-13).

FGF21 is a liver-derived endocrine hormone that functions as a metabolic stress signal coordinating adaptive responses across multiple organs. It operates through a unique receptor complex requiring both FGFR1 and β-klotho, activating MAPK/ERK, PI3K/Akt, and AMPK pathways to regulate glucose and lipid metabolism, energy expenditure, and feeding behavior. Its expression is controlled by nutrient-sensing transcription factors (PPARα, ChREBP) and stress-responsive pathways (ATF4/CHOP), enabling integration of diverse metabolic signals. Recent advances have clarified receptor mechanisms, identified heparin's supporting role, and advanced clinical development of FGF21-based therapeutics for MASH and metabolic diseases, though optimal clinical efficacy and patient selection remain areas of ongoing investigation.
