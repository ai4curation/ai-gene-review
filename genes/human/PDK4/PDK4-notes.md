## Manual review notes

The Falcon/Perplexity deep-research providers were unavailable here, so this file
records the manual PDK4 literature synthesis for the review.

- Core function: PDK4 is a mitochondrial PDHA1 kinase. PMID:8798399 cloned the
  fourth human PDK isoform and placed it in the mitochondrial PDK family;
  PMID:18658136 then showed that human PDK4 has unusually robust basal activity
  without the PDC core.
- PAINT: there are no IRD/IKR losses on the PDK4 lineage. The PTN000236514 PDC
  phosphorylation node fits PDK4. The narrower PDK4 insulin-signaling IBD at
  PTN004327935 rests on a mouse Pdk4 insulin physiology seed and was treated as
  over-annotated for human PDK4 because it asserts a receptor-signaling pathway
  rather than the PDC phosphorylation step.
- PMID:14966024 measured increased PDK4 mRNA after a 40-hour fast in human
  skeletal muscle. That supports fasting induction of the gene, not direct
  participation of the protein product in the cellular response to starvation.
- PMID:21852536 supports secondary effects on PDH flux and de novo lipogenesis
  in detached cells after PDK4 overexpression; these rows were kept as non-core
  consequences of core PDC inhibition.
