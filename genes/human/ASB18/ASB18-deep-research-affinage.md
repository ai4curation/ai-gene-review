# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ASB18 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ASB18 is a substrate-recognition subunit of a Cullin5-RING E3 ubiquitin ligase complex [PMID:16325183]. Through the BC box and Cul5 box motifs within its SOCS box domain, ASB18 binds Cullin5 (Cul5) and Rbx2 to assemble a complex that possesses E3 ubiquitin ligase activity [PMID:16325183]. Beyond this assembly and activity, no substrates or downstream physiological roles for ASB18 have been characterized in the available corpus.
