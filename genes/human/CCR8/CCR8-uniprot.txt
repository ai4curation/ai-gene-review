CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor that can bind a variety of
CC       chemokines, such as CCL1, CCL8, CCL16, CCL18 (PubMed:23999500,
CC       PubMed:35041514). Regulates monocyte and eosinophil chemotaxis.
CC       Undergoes internalization upon CCL18 binding, leading to induced
CC       migration and calcium flux of highly polarized Th2 cells
CC       (PubMed:23999500). In microglial cells, promotes phagocytosis with
CC       CCL18 through NF-kappa-B and Src signaling pathways (PubMed:35041514).
CC       Stimulation of the CCL1-CCR8 signaling axis protects the gut from acute
CC       intestinal damage (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P56484,
