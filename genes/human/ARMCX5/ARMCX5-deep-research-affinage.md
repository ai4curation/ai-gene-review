# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ARMCX5 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ARMCX5 is an X-chromosomal armadillo repeat-containing protein whose loss is associated with neurodevelopmental and seizure phenotypes. In mice, deletion of a 0.35 Mb Xq22.1 subregion containing Armcx5 (alongside Gprasp1, Gprasp2, and Bhlhb9) recapitulates the Xq22.1 syndrome—respiratory failure, neonatal lethality, cleft palate, and seizures—placing Armcx5 within the minimal critical region for this syndrome, though its individual contribution is not isolated from the co-deleted genes [PMID:24569167]. Direct molecular mechanisms specific to ARMCX5 have not been characterized in the available corpus; beyond its assignment to GPRASP/ARMCX subfamily 1 [PMID:33267763] and the epigenetic regulation of its hippocampal expression [PMID:32772228], no biochemical activity or validated binding partner for ARMCX5 itself has been established.
