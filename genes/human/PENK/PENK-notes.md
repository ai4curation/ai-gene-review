# PENK Curation Notes (Human Proenkephalin-A, UniProtKB:P01210)

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Gene symbol / ID**: `PENK` (`UniProtKB:P01210`, `HGNC:8831`)
- **PANTHER family**: `PANTHER:PTHR11438:SF3` (`PROENKEPHALIN-A`, within `PANTHER:PTHR11438` `PROENKEPHALIN`; root PAINT node `PANTHER:PTN000153716`, clade node `PANTHER:PTN002606001`).
- **Preprohormone processing**: Synthesized in striatal medium spiny neurons, spinal cord dorsal horn interneurons, peripheral sensory/enteric neurons, and adrenal medullary chromaffin cells as a 267-amino-acid polyprotein precursor (signal peptide residues 1-24) packaged into the lumen of neuronal dense-core vesicles (`GO:0099013`) [PMID:7057924]. Cleaved at flanking dibasic sites by prohormone convertases (PC1/PCSK1, PC2/PCSK2) and carboxypeptidase E to yield:
  - Four copies of **Met-enkephalin** (`YGGFM`, residues 100-104, 107-111, 136-140, 210-214)
  - One copy of **Leu-enkephalin** (`YGGFL`, residues 230-234)
  - One copy of the octapeptide **Met-enkephalin-Arg-Gly-Leu** (`YGGFMRGL`, residues 186-193)
  - One copy of the heptapeptide **Met-enkephalin-Arg-Phe** (`YGGFMRF`, residues 261-267)
  - Larger intermediate peptides including **peptide E**, **BAM-18**, **BAM-22P**, and **BAM8-22** (which additionally activates the sensory neuron GPCR `MRGPRX1` via Gq) [PMID:7057924, PMID:36302898].
- **Signaling mechanism**: Mature enkephalin peptides act as endogenous opioid peptide hormones/neuropeptides (`GO:0001515` *opioid peptide activity*, child of `GO:0005184` *neuropeptide hormone activity* and `GO:0048018` *receptor ligand activity*) that bind and activate the delta-type (`OPRD1`) and mu-type (`OPRM1`) opioid receptors (`GO:0031628` *opioid receptor binding*, `GO:0038003` *G protein-coupled opioid receptor signaling pathway*) [PMID:7057924, Reactome:R-HSA-373076]. Agonist-bound `OPRD1` and `OPRM1` couple to pertussis-toxin-sensitive inhibitory Gi/o heterotrimeric G proteins (`GNAI1`/`GNAI2`/`GNAI3`/`GNAO1`) to inhibit adenylate cyclase (`GO:0031635` *adenylate cyclase-inhibiting opioid receptor signaling pathway*), activate GIRK potassium channels, and inhibit presynaptic voltage-gated Ca2+ channels, suppressing nociceptive neurotransmission (`GO:0019233` *sensory perception of pain*) [Reactome:R-HSA-373076].

## Ligands in Signaling Pathways Assessment (Tranche 4C)
- Human `PENK` already carries `GO:0038003` (*G protein-coupled opioid receptor signaling pathway*, TAS from `Reactome:R-HSA-373076`) and `GO:0001515` (*opioid peptide activity*, IBA from `PTN000153716` and IEA), so `GO:0038003` passes the ligand check in human, mouse, and rat (though zebrafish `penka` and `penkb` lack `GO:0038003` because PAINT `PTN000153716` only places `GO:0007218` *neuropeptide signaling pathway*).
- However, on the canonical Gi/o effector child pathway `GO:0031635` (*adenylate cyclase-inhibiting opioid receptor signaling pathway*), `PENK` is missing in human, mouse, and rat (Category B gap; only `OPRK1` is annotated).
- We `MODIFY` the broad signaling rows `GO:0007218` (*neuropeptide signaling pathway*, IBA/IEA) and `GO:0007165` (*signal transduction*, IEA) to `GO:0038003` (*G protein-coupled opioid receptor signaling pathway*) and `GO:0031635` (*adenylate cyclase-inhibiting opioid receptor signaling pathway*), and add a `NEW` (`TAS`) annotation for `GO:0031635` backed by `Reactome:R-HSA-373076`, linking `PENK` to `MODULE:opioid_receptor_signaling`.
- Note on `PMID:15538397` (`GO:0006915` *apoptotic process*, `GO:0030262` *apoptotic nuclear changes*, `GO:0005634` *nucleus*): [PMID:15538397] characterizes a non-secreted nuclear isoform of proenkephalin in stressed/tumor cells that sensitizes cells to apoptosis; we keep `GO:0005634` as `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` and mark the downstream apoptosis phenotype rows as `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`.
