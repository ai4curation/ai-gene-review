CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Met-enkephalin]: Neuropeptide that competes with and mimic
CC       the effects of opiate drugs. They play a role in a number of
CC       physiologic functions, including pain perception and responses to
CC       stress. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:7057924}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Leu-enkephalin]: Neuropeptide that competes with and mimic
CC       the effects of opiate drugs. They play a role in a number of
CC       physiologic functions, including pain perception and responses to
CC       stress. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:7057924}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Met-enkephalin-Arg-Phe]: Met-enkephalin-Arg-Phe neuropeptide
CC       acts as a strong ligand of Mu-type opioid receptor OPRM1. Met-
CC       enkephalin-Arg-Phe-binding to OPRM1 in the nucleus accumbens of the
CC       brain increases activation of OPRM1, leading to long-term synaptic
CC       depression of glutamate release. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P22005}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [PENK(114-133)]: Increases glutamate release in the striatum
CC       and decreases GABA concentration in the striatum.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04094}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Neuropeptide BAM22]: Neuropeptide that mediates stress-
CC       induced opioid analgesia (By similarity). Also possesses non-opioid
CC       action by acting as a ligand for MRGPRX1 receptor to induce itch in a
CC       histamine-independent manner (PubMed:11850634).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04094, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11850634}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Neuropeptide BAM8-22]: Neuropeptide that induces itch in a
CC       histamine-independent manner as well as nociceptive sensations
CC       (PubMed:11850634, PubMed:28223516). Acts as a ligand for MRGPRX1
CC       receptor in sensory neurons of dorsal root ganglion: MRGPRX1-binding in
CC       the peripheral nervous system elicits itch and scratching, while
CC       MRGPRX1-binding in the central nervous system dampens chronic pain
CC       (PubMed:11850634, PubMed:21593341, PubMed:28223516, PubMed:36302898,
CC       PubMed:37591889, PubMed:39725036). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11850634,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21593341, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28223516,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36302898, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37591889,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39725036}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [PENK(237-258)]: Increases glutamate release in the striatum.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04094}.
