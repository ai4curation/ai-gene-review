# TCF7 notes

## 2026-11-26 curation

- Automated deep research did not run in this workspace: `just deep-research human TCF7 --provider perplexity` reported that `perplexity` was unavailable and only `claude_code` was configured. I used cached GOA evidence, UniProt, Reactome, PANTHER family data, and cached primary papers for manual synthesis instead.
- TCF7 belongs to the compact PTHR10373 TCF/LEF HMG-box family. The core family architecture is an HMG DNA-binding domain plus a conserved amino-terminal beta-catenin-binding region; Dazap2 work summarized this family-level mechanism as nuclear Tcf/Lef factors that bind TLE/Groucho without Wnt and switch to beta-catenin complexes after Wnt activation [PMID:19304756, "Vertebrate Tcf/Lef proteins perform two well-characterized functions: in association with beta-catenin they activate gene expression, and in the absence of Wnt ligands they bind TLE/Groucho proteins to act as transcriptional repressors"].
- The TCF7/TCF1-specific experimental rows are relatively sparse compared with LEF1 and TCF7L2, so the PAINT IBA rows provide important orthology support for core HMG-box RNA polymerase II cis-regulatory DNA binding, chromatin activity, canonical Wnt signaling, and beta-catenin-TCF complex membership. The mouse Tcf paper directly showed that Tcf proteins bind the canonical Tcf motif, complex with beta-catenin, and activate transcription downstream of Wnt stimulation [PMID:9488439, "The mTcf-3 and mTcf-4 proteins bind a canonical Tcf DNA motif and can complex with the transcriptional coactivator beta-catenin"].
- Reactome nucleoplasm rows correspond to modeled CTNNB1:TCF/LEF promoter-binding events for Wnt target genes, so they were kept as core nuclear localization rows rather than deduplicated.
- Most IntAct `GO:0005515` rows were removed only under the bare-protein-binding policy. The UniProt PMID:9783587 row with Xenopus XGrg-4 as its `WITH/FROM` partner was instead modified to `GO:0001222` because the paper shows the TCF/Groucho-family corepressor interaction; rows with different partners were left as distinct review entries.
