| LEPR form/state | Principal compartment | Mechanism and functional interpretation | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LEPRb (Ob-Rb; long signaling isoform)** | Plasma membrane; especially important in specialized hypothalamic neurons | Long cytoplasmic tail recruits JAK2 through Box1/Box2. Activated JAK2 phosphorylates Tyr985 (SOCS3 negative feedback and SHP2–GRB2–ERK), Tyr1077 (STAT5), and Tyr1138 (principally STAT3). Tyr1138–STAT3 is the dominant STAT-dependent pathway in mouse physiology; STAT3 also induces *SOCS3*, completing feedback inhibition. (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000016) | Established receptor-signaling mechanism, supported strongly by biochemical and mouse genetic evidence; human biallelic loss-of-function genetics validates its physiological importance. |
| **LEPRa and other short membrane isoforms** | Plasma membrane and endosomal trafficking compartments; LEPRa is also expressed in choroid-plexus epithelium | Share the leptin-binding ectodomain but have short cytoplasmic tails containing Box1 without the complete LEPRb signaling apparatus; they can weakly activate JAK2 in overexpression systems but are **not equivalent to fully signaling-competent LEPRb**. A proposed LEPRa–LRP1 complex mediates leptin uptake/transcytosis at the choroid plexus. (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000033) | Limited signaling role is established; the transport model is tentative because the 2023 evidence is a bioRxiv preprint using genetically modified mice, rat choroid-plexus cells, and transfected cell lines—not human in-vivo validation. |
| **Soluble LEPR (LepRe plus shed ectodomain)** | Extracellular fluid and blood plasma | A secreted extracellular-domain isoform produced by alternative splicing, together with proteolytically shed membrane-receptor ectodomain, forms the principal circulating leptin-binding protein. It can sequester leptin acutely and stabilize or transport ligand over longer periods, but it lacks a transmembrane/cytoplasmic signaling apparatus. (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000024) | Established ligand-binding/regulatory form; not a cell-autonomous signaling receptor. |
| **Nascent/biosynthetic LEPRb** | Endoplasmic reticulum, then secretory pathway en route to the plasma membrane | Newly synthesized LEPRb is folding-prone and subject to SEL1L–HRD1 ER-associated degradation. Defective quality control causes aggregation, ER retention, reduced surface delivery, and weaker leptin signaling; the human obesity-associated C604S receptor is folding-defective. The ER is therefore a biogenesis/quality-control compartment, **not the normal site of leptin signal transduction**. (pqac-00000028, pqac-00000029, pqac-00000030) | Peer-reviewed 2024 evidence is primarily from mouse POMC-neuron genetics and engineered-cell experiments, with human relevance supported by analysis of the C604S disease variant. |


*Table: Comparison of signaling-competent LEPRb, short membrane isoforms, soluble receptor, and the biosynthetic ER pool. The table distinguishes established plasma-membrane signaling from tentative transport functions and intracellular quality control.*
