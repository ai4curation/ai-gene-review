## GO:0003924 GTPase activity — ACCEPT
GNA11 is a guanine-nucleotide-binding G alpha subunit that switches between GDP- and GTP-bound states and hydrolyzes bound GTP. Receptor/effector biochemistry and nucleotide-state experiments support the intrinsic molecular switch. This is distinct from the GAP activity of RGS proteins; activated or nucleotide-defective mutants are controls, not substitutes for the wild-type function.
Sources: PMID:31073061, PMID:15331768

## Mammalian phototransduction evidence follow-up

Read normally recovered full PMID24958088 (PMC4282707) and PMID24870805 (PMC4037210). The earlier chicken-only rationale was incomplete. Retain a secondary visible-light phototransduction role for human GNA11 as comparative functional inference, not a direct human retinal assay. Full PMID24958088 Fig. 4 isolates endogenous mouse Gnaq and Gna11 contributions in melanopsin-expressing Neuro-2A cells: either supports the calcium response, whereas joint silencing eliminates it. Fig. 5 combines native retinal knockdown with pupil measurements and supports overlapping Gnaq/Gna11/Gna14 participation without separately establishing each isoform as necessary in vivo. This mammalian evidence supplements the older chicken source PMID12196552. PMID24870805 preserves responses in tested single/double knockout combinations and discusses compensation or alternative signaling; this limits necessity claims, not the demonstrated participation capacity. Neither experiment establishes an exclusive human isoform assignment or every ipRGC subtype.

The precise human GNA11 outer-segment localization remains unresolved. PMID12196552 reports chicken Gq/11 immunolocalization with G11-associated opsin signaling. Full PMID24958088 adds adult mouse retinal localization, including Gnaq-selective cone-photoreceptor staining and cross-reactive Gnaq/11 staining, but the reported cone/photoreceptor-layer signal does not independently resolve this exact human subcompartment for each isoform. Its melanopsin experiments concern inner-retinal ganglion cells, not rod/cone outer segments. PMID24870805 examines neonatal ipRGC expression and function, which likewise does not settle outer-segment residence. These limitations do not demonstrate absence.

The precise human GNA11 contribution to circadian entrainment remains unresolved after mammalian source review. Chicken pineal phase shifting in PMID12196552 is a different photoreceptive implementation. PMID24958088 measures cellular phototransduction and pupillary responses, not circadian entrainment. Full PMID24870805 actually tests mouse wheel-running rhythms: the tested mutant combinations retain light-dark entrainment and light-pulse phase shifting; Gnaq/Gna11 double mutants show a small reduction of period lengthening in constant light. That combined-genotype effect does not separately establish human GNA11 entrainment, and normal entrainment does not demonstrate absence of a redundant contribution. Do not equate melanopsin participation with proof of every downstream circadian role.
