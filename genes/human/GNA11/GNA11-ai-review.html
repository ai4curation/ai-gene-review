Gene Description

GNA11 encodes G alpha 11, a Gq-family heterotrimeric G protein alpha subunit. Receptor-stimulated GDP/GTP exchange activates PLC beta signaling, linking extracellular stimuli to inositol trisphosphate, diacylglycerol and calcium-dependent responses. G11 acts on the cytoplasmic plasma-membrane surface and shares some physiological roles with Gq, including endocrine, vascular and developmental signaling. Receptor specificity, cellular expression and compensatory overlap determine which paralog contributes in each tissue.
