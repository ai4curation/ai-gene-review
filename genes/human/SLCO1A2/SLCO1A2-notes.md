# SLCO1A2 (OATP1A2 / OATP-A, UniProt P46721) — curation notes

## Identity and family
- HGNC symbol SLCO1A2; UniProt P46721 (SO1A2_HUMAN); gene also historically SLC21A3, OATP, OATP1, OATP-A. 670 aa, 12-transmembrane multi-pass plasma-membrane protein.
- Member of the **organic anion transporting polypeptide (OATP/SLCO) family** (TC 2.A.60), MFS fold. PANTHER PTHR11388 (ORGANIC ANION TRANSPORTER), subfamily PTHR11388:SF16 [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "PANTHER; PTHR11388:SF16; SOLUTE CARRIER ORGANIC ANION TRANSPORTER FAMILY MEMBER 1A2"]. InterPro IPR004156 (OATP), IPR020846 (MFS_dom). Contains a Kazal-like domain (433–488) with three disulfide bonds [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "DOMAIN          433..488"].
- "Belongs to the organo anion transporter (TC 2.A.60) family." [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Belongs to the organo anion transporter (TC 2.A.60) family"].

## Core molecular function: Na+-independent polyspecific organic anion uptake transporter (anion exchanger)
- **Na(+)-independent transporter** of a broad range of amphipathic organic anions [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Na(+)-independent transporter that mediates the cellular uptake of a broad range of organic anions such as the endogenous bile salts cholate and deoxycholate, either in their unconjugated or conjugated forms (taurocholate and glycocholate)"].
- Transport is **not** Na+-coupled and **not** proton-symport; OATPs act as **anion exchangers**, with **bicarbonate (HCO3-) as the probable counteranion** that exchanges for organic anions [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Hydrogencarbonate/HCO3(-) acts as the probable counteranion that exchanges for organic anions"]. Family-level study: OATP/Oatp-mediated transport is paralleled by bicarbonate efflux and the transporters probably act as anion exchangers [PMID:19129463 "OATP/Oatp-mediated substrate transport is paralleled by bicarbonate efflux"], [PMID:19129463 "mediate cellular uptake of a wide variety of amphipathic organic compounds such as bile salts, steroid conjugates, oligopeptides, and a large list of drugs, probably by acting as anion exchangers"]. Because uptake is driven by exchange with an outwardly directed counter-anion gradient, the activity fits **secondary active transmembrane transporter activity (GO:0015291)** (antiport mechanism).
- **pH-sensitive**: low extracellular pH stimulates transport (increased substrate affinity) [PMID:19129463 "a low extracellular pH stimulated transport activity"]; [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Shows a pH-sensitive substrate specificity which may be ascribed to the protonation state of the binding site and leads to a stimulation of substrate transport in an acidic microenvironment"]. A conserved His-107 in TM3 is essential for pH sensitivity [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "A conserved histidine residue in the third TMD (His-107) may play an essential role in the pH sensitivity"].
- Optimum pH ~6.5 for estrone 3-sulfate, taurocholate, prostaglandin E2 and T4 [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Optimum pH is 6.5 with estrone 3-sulfate, taurocholate, prostaglandin"].

## Substrate spectrum (multispecific)
- **Bile acids / bile salts** (endogenous): cholate, deoxycholate; conjugated taurocholate (KM 60 uM), glycocholate (KM 19 uM), taurochenodeoxycholate, tauroursodeoxycholate [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "KM=60 uM for taurocholate"]. UniProt curates Rhea catalytic activities for taurocholate(out)=taurocholate(in) etc. [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Reaction=taurocholate(out) = taurocholate(in)"].
- **Steroid conjugates**: dehydroepiandrosterone 3-sulfate (DHEAS; KM 6.6 uM), estrone 3-sulfate, 17beta-estradiol 17-O-(beta-D-glucuronate) [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Transports dehydroepiandrosterone 3-sulfate (DHEAS), a major circulating steroid secreted by the adrenal cortex, as well as estrone 3-sulfate and 17beta-estradiol 17-O-(beta-D-glucuronate)"].
- **Thyroid hormones**: T3 (3,3',5'-triiodo-L-thyronine) and T4 [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Capable of thyroid hormone transport (both T3 or 3,3',5'-triiodo-L-thyronine, and T4 or L-tyroxine)"]. Direct transport assays (PubMed:20358049) establish iodothyronine transport; this supports **thyroid hormone transmembrane transporter activity (GO:0015349)** — a function not currently in GOA.
- **Prostaglandin E2** [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Also transports prostaglandin E2"].
- **all-trans-retinol (atROL)**: apical uptake across retinal pigment epithelium, essential to the visual cycle [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Mediates apical uptake of all-trans-retinol (atROL) across human retinal pigment epithelium"].
- **Neuropeptides**: substance P/TAC1 (KM 76 uM) and vasoactive intestinal peptide/VIP (KM 73 uM) — proposed reuptake of peptide neurotransmitters released from retinal neurons [PMID:25132355 "Substance P and vasoactive intestinal peptide were identified as substrates for OATP1A2 and OATP2B1"].
- **Drugs/xenobiotics**: fexofenadine, methotrexate, paclitaxel, rosuvastatin/pravastatin, doxorubicin, ouabain, ciprofloxacin, bilirubin and many more (large DrugBank list). Methotrexate/paclitaxel disposition [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "May play an important role in plasma and tissue distribution of the structurally diverse chemotherapeutic drugs"]. Transport inhibited by the grapefruit component naringin [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Transport activity is inhibited by the grapefruit juice component naringin"].

## Biological processes
- Na+-independent organic anion transport (GO:0043252) and bile acid/bile salt transport (GO:0015721) are direct restatements of the MF. Responsible for intestinal absorption of bile acids (by similarity) [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Responsible for intestinal absorption of bile acids"].
- Drug/xenobiotic uptake transport is a major pharmacological role (Drug ADME). Mechanistically this is **xenobiotic transmembrane transport (GO:0006855)**, not xenobiotic metabolism — OATP1A2 transports drugs, it does not metabolize them.
- Proposed roles in blood-brain / blood-CSF barrier transport of organic anions and signal mediators, and hormone/neurosteroid uptake by neural cells [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Plays roles in blood-brain and -cerebrospinal fluid barrier transport of organic anions and signal mediators"].

## Subcellular location (tissue-dependent polarization)
- Plasma membrane, multi-pass [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane"]. HPA IDA and PMID:25132355 confirm plasma-membrane localization.
- **Apical/luminal** in classic absorptive/barrier tissues: brush-border of enterocytes, luminal membrane of BBB capillary endothelium; apical RPE uptake of retinol. Reactome models apical plasma membrane.
- **Basal** in Sertoli cells (blood-testis barrier): [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Localized to the basal membrane of Sertoli cells"]; [PMID:35307651 "The basal membrane localization of OAT1, OAT2, OATP1A2, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OATP2A1, OATP2B1, and OATP3A1-v2"]. So the "basolateral plasma membrane" IBA (is_active_in) is consistent with basal Sertoli localization but polarization is tissue-dependent; apical is equally documented.

## Expression
- Higher in brain than liver/kidney; brain neurons (cortex, hippocampus), retina, placental trophoblasts, lung, testis (basal Sertoli), liver, prostate, eye [file:human/SLCO1A2/SLCO1A2-uniprot.txt "Higher expression in the brain than in liver and kidney"]. HPA tissue-enhanced: brain, choroid plexus, liver, salivary gland.

## Protein interactions (GO:0005515 rows)
- The 22 IPI protein-binding rows (all PMID:36115835, Gogl et al. quantitative PDZ/PBM fragmentomics) are PDZ-domain proteins (DLG1-4, MPDZ, PATJ, SCRIB, PDZK1, MAGI1/2, GRIP1/2, TJP1, WHRN, LNX1/2, NHERF4/PDZD3, FRMPD2, GRID2IP, APBA1/2, IL16) binding the **C-terminal PDZ-binding motif** of OATP1A2 (the entry's own INTERACTION block lists these same IntAct pairs). This is a scaffolding/membrane-anchoring interaction set measured by a systematic affinity screen. As bare `GO:0005515 protein binding` these are uninformative per the protein-binding policy and are removed; the underlying biology (C-terminal PBM → PDZ scaffold) is recorded here and as a suggested question.

## Variants / pharmacogenetics
- Non-synonymous variants (T13, Y128, I135, D172, T187, S668; I355V) alter drug disposition / CNS drug entry (PMID:15632119). Not function-defining for this review.

## Curation decisions summary
- Core MF: **GO:0015291 secondary active transmembrane transporter activity** (polyspecific anion-exchange mechanism) and **GO:0015125 bile acid transmembrane transporter activity** (best-characterized endogenous substrate class). Both current and IBA/experimentally supported.
- Generic `GO:0022857 transmembrane transporter activity` rows → TOO_GENERAL (more specific MF asserted). `GO:0016020 membrane`, `GO:0055085 transmembrane transport` → TOO_GENERAL.
- `GO:0006805 xenobiotic metabolic process` → MODIFY to `GO:0006855 xenobiotic transmembrane transport` (lateral: transport not metabolism).
- NEW: `GO:0015349 thyroid hormone transmembrane transporter activity` (direct T3/T4 transport, PubMed:20358049 + curated Rhea catalytic activities).
- Obsoleted grouping terms GO:0008514 / GO:0015347 (sodium-independent organic anion transmembrane transporter activity) were deliberately obsoleted by GO as "unnecessary and confusing grouping" terms — not re-proposed; raised as a suggested question instead.
