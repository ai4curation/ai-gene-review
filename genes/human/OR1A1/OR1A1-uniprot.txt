CC   -!- FUNCTION: Olfactory receptor activated by various compounds, such as 3-
CC       methyl-2,4-nonanedione, dihydrojasmone, citronellol and carvone
CC       (PubMed:17601748, PubMed:27642093, PubMed:27916747, PubMed:28656349,
CC       PubMed:35686936). Ligand-binding causes a conformation change that
CC       triggers signaling via G(s)-class of G alpha protein GNAL/G(olf),
CC       mediating activation of adenylate cyclase activity and increased
CC       production of cAMP, initiating a neuronal response that triggers the
CC       perception of a smell (PubMed:27916747). Acts as a selective receptor
CC       for 3-methyl-2,4-nonanedione, a key food odorant associated with a
CC       prune note in oxidized wine and is an aroma determinant in tea and
CC       apricots (PubMed:27916747). Also responds specifically to certain
CC       nitromusks, notably musk ambrette, and to a lesser extent, musk
CC       tibetene and musk xylene (PubMed:29632183).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17601748, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27642093,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27916747, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28656349,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29632183, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35686936}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Highly activated by synthetic agonists with bis-
CC       acetylene rods combined with an aryl spacer, such as aryl bis-
CC       acetylene. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:40523847}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17601748,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27642093}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the olfactory epithelium.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17601748}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor 1 family.
