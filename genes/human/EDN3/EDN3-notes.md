# EDN3 Curation Notes (Human Endothelin-3, UniProtKB:P14138)

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Gene symbol / ID**: `EDN3` (`UniProtKB:P14138`, `HGNC:3178`)
- **PANTHER family**: `PANTHER:PTHR13874:SF11` (`ENDOTHELIN-3`, within `PANTHER:PTHR13874` `ENDOTHELIN`; root PAINT node `PANTHER:PTN000356323`).
- **Preprohormone processing**: Synthesized as a 238-amino-acid preproendothelin-3 (signal peptide residues 1-17), cleaved at dibasic sites by furin-like proprotein convertases to big endothelin-3 (residues 97-137, 41 aa), and cleaved at the Trp21-Ile22 bond (residues 117-118) by endothelin-converting enzymes (`ECE1`) to yield mature 21-residue bicyclic Endothelin-3 (`[Thr2,Phe4,Thr5,Tyr6,Lys7,Tyr14]endothelin`, residues 97-117), stabilized by two intramolecular disulfide bonds (`Cys97-Cys111` and `Cys99-Cys107`) [PMID:2649896, PMID:1917960].
- **Signaling mechanism**: Mature ET-3 functions as a secreted peptide hormone (`GO:0005179` *hormone activity*, `GO:0048018` *receptor ligand activity*) that binds the Endothelin B receptor (`EDNRB` / ETB, `GO:0031708` *endothelin B receptor binding*) with subnanomolar affinity equipotent to ET-1 and ET-2, while exhibiting lower affinity for `EDNRA` (ETA) [PMID:1713452, PMID:8982507, Reactome:R-HSA-375281]. Binding of ET-3 to `EDNRB` (and at higher concentrations `EDNRA`) activates heterotrimeric Gq/11 proteins and phospholipase C-beta to generate IP3 and mobilize intracellular Ca2+ (`GO:0086100` *endothelin receptor signaling pathway* and `GO:0160135` *phospholipase C-activating endothelin receptor signaling pathway*) [PMID:1713452, PMID:1917960].
- **Physiological and developmental roles**: Essential paracrine signal for the proliferation, survival, and migration of `EDNRB`-expressing enteric neural crest precursors (forming the myenteric and submucosal plexuses of the distal gut) and melanoblasts (`GO:0048484` *enteric nervous system development*, `GO:0030318` *melanocyte differentiation*); loss-of-function mutations in `EDN3` cause Waardenburg syndrome type 4B (Waardenburg-Shah syndrome) and congenital central hypoventilation / Hirschsprung disease. In the vasculature, ET-3 contracts venous smooth muscle via `EDNRB` and elicits systemic pressor and endothelial vasodilator responses (`GO:0014826`, `GO:0003100`) [PMID:2649896, PMID:8982507].

## Ligands in Signaling Pathways Assessment (Tranche 4C)
- Unlike `EDN1` and `EDN2` (which carry InterPro IEA annotations for `GO:0086100` *endothelin receptor signaling pathway*), human `EDN3` lacks both `GO:0086100` (*endothelin receptor signaling pathway*) and `GO:0160135` (*phospholipase C-activating endothelin receptor signaling pathway*) in GOA (Category A gap!), carrying only generic `GO:0007166` (*cell surface receptor signaling pathway*, IEA) and `GO:0007165` (*signal transduction*, TAS `PMID:2649896`).
- We `MODIFY` `GO:0007166` and `GO:0007165` to `GO:0086100` (*endothelin receptor signaling pathway*) and `GO:0160135` (*phospholipase C-activating endothelin receptor signaling pathway*), and add `NEW` (`IDA`) annotations for `GO:0086100` (backed by [PMID:1713452]) and `GO:0160135` (backed by [PMID:1917960]), linking `EDN3` to `MODULE:endothelin_receptor_signaling`.
