CC   -!- FUNCTION: Uniporter involved in the facilitative transport of
CC       nucleosides and nucleobases, and contributes to maintaining their
CC       cellular homeostasis (PubMed:10722669, PubMed:10755314,
CC       PubMed:12527552, PubMed:14759222, PubMed:15037197, PubMed:17379602,
CC       PubMed:21795683, PubMed:26406980, PubMed:27995448, PubMed:35790189,
CC       PubMed:8986748). Functions as a Na(+)-independent transporter
CC       (PubMed:8986748). Involved in the transport of nucleosides such as
CC       adenosine, guanosine, inosine, uridine, thymidine and cytidine
CC       (PubMed:10722669, PubMed:10755314, PubMed:12527552, PubMed:14759222,
CC       PubMed:15037197, PubMed:17379602, PubMed:26406980, PubMed:8986748,
CC       PubMed:39737929). Also transports purine nucleobases (hypoxanthine,
CC       adenine, guanine) and pyrimidine nucleobases (thymine, uracil)
CC       (PubMed:21795683, PubMed:27995448). Also mediates the uptake of
CC       nicotinamide; contributes to maintaining nicotinamide homeostasis but
CC       also regulated nicotinamide biological roles (PubMed:39885119).
CC       Mediates basolateral nucleoside uptake into Sertoli cells, thereby
CC       regulating the transport of nucleosides in testis across the blood-
CC       testis barrier (By similarity). Regulates inosine levels in brown
CC       adipocytes tissues (BAT) and extracellular inosine levels, which
