# Xu et al. 2007: IL-6 experiment

Article: *XB130, a Novel Adaptor Protein for Signal Transduction*. PMID:17412687. DOI: [10.1074/jbc.M701684200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M701684200).

The [full paper hosted by ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/6408798_XB130_a_Novel_Adaptor_Protein_for_Signal_Transduction) was inspected through web retrieval on 2026-09-08. The repository PMID cache contains only the abstract; this short source excerpt records the additional full-text observation, not a claim that the cache contains the full paper.

In the Results subsection on endogenous XB130 in human lung epithelial cells, the authors report cytokine measurements following XB130 siRNA in BEAS-2B cells:

> In addition to IL-8, the protein level of IL-6 was also reduced by siRNA treatment.

This supports an IL-6-production regulatory effect in that cell context. It does not establish a direct cytokine-producing activity or a constitutive proinflammatory role in every tissue or species.
