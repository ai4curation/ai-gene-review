CC   -!- FUNCTION: Secreted cytokine-like protein (PubMed:12847263). Binds to
CC       the scavenger receptor MARCO (PubMed:12847263). Can also bind to
CC       pathogens including the Gram-positive bacterium L.monocytogenes, the
CC       Gram-negative bacterium P.aeruginosa, and yeast (PubMed:12847263).
CC       Strongly inhibits phospholipase A2 (PLA2G1B) activity
CC       (PubMed:24213919). Seems to have anti-inflammatory effects in
CC       respiratory epithelium (By similarity). Also has anti-fibrotic activity
CC       in lung (PubMed:24213919). May play a role in fetal lung development
CC       and maturation (PubMed:24213919). Promotes branching morphogenesis
CC       during early stages of lung development (PubMed:24213919). In the
CC       pituitary, may inhibit production of follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH)
CC       and luteinizing hormone (LH) (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q920H1, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12847263,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24213919}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer; disulfide-linked (PubMed:24213919). Monomer
CC       (PubMed:24213919). Interacts with APOA1 (PubMed:12847263).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12847263, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24213919}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12847263}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Highly expressed in lung and trachea
CC       (PubMed:12175512, PubMed:12406855, PubMed:12847263). Detected
CC       throughout the airway epithelium in lung, with slightly higher
CC       expression in large airways (PubMed:12406855). Found in lung submucosal
CC       gland acinus where it localizes to serous-like cells (PubMed:12406855).
CC       Probably expressed in club cells of the bronchioles (PubMed:12847263).
CC       Not detected in other tissues tested (PubMed:12847263).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12175512, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12406855,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12847263}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in fetal lung.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12175512}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the secretoglobin family. UGRP subfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
