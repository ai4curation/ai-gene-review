Gene Description

MAP1LC3B (LC3B) is a ubiquitin-like protein of the Atg8 family (LC3 subfamily) and the most widely used marker of autophagic membranes. Newly made pro-LC3B is cleaved by ATG4 proteases after Gly120 to give cytosolic LC3B-I, which the E1-like ATG7, the E2-like ATG3 and the ATG12-ATG5-ATG16L1 E3-like complex conjugate to phosphatidylethanolamine on the phagophore, forming membrane-anchored LC3B-II; ATG4 also removes it again. On the phagophore and autophagosome, LC3B promotes membrane expansion and serves as a docking platform: its hydrophobic LIR-docking site binds LC3-interacting region motifs of selective autophagy receptors (for example SQSTM1, PHB2, BNIP3, AMBRA1, TEX264), core autophagy proteins and regulators, coupling cargo capture to the autophagic membrane and supporting mitophagy and ER-phagy. Atg8-family proteins are collectively needed for efficient autophagosome formation and especially for autophagosome-lysosome fusion, where the GABARAP subfamily predominates. LC3B was originally identified as a light chain of microtubule-associated proteins MAP1A/MAP1B and binds microtubules; it also binds ceramide during ceramide-induced lethal mitophagy and stimulates JMY-mediated actin nucleation on autophagic membranes.
