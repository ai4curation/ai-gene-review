CC   -!- FUNCTION: Retina-specific kinase involved in the regulation of
CC       phototransduction, playing a key role in terminating the light-
CC       activated rhodopsin (RHO) signal in rod photoreceptors. Following light
CC       activation, it phosphorylates RHO, thereby promoting binding of S-
CC       arrestin (SAG) which then blocks transducin-mediated opsin signaling
CC       (PubMed:15946941). Is also involved in terminating light-activated
CC       opsin signals in cone photoreceptors, primarily acting on medium- and
CC       long-wave-sensitive opsins (PubMed:12601058). In cone photoreceptors,
CC       opsin phosphorylation promotes binding of C-arrestin (ARR3) (By
CC       similarity). Fast signal desensitization is essential for scotopic
CC       vision and permits rapid adaptation to changes in illumination (By
CC       similarity). May play a role in the maintenance of the outer nuclear
CC       layer in the retina (By similarity) (PubMed:12601058).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P28327, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9WVL4,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12601058, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15946941}.
