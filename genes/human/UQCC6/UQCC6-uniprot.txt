CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required for the assembly and stability of the mitochondrial
CC       ubiquinol-cytochrome c reductase complex (complex III (CIII) or
CC       cytochrome b-c1 complex), a multisubunit transmembrane complex that is
CC       part of the mitochondrial electron transport chain (ETC) which drives
CC       oxidative phosphorylation (PubMed:32161263). Mediates early complex III
CC       biogenesis (By similarity). Participates in regulating the levels of
CC       electron transport chain proteins, and therefore energy supply, in
CC       response to changes in energy demand (By similarity). Also required for
CC       cytochrome c oxidase complex (complex IV) assembly (PubMed:34969438).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8BTC1, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32161263,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34969438}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with UQCRC1 (PubMed:32161263). Interacts with UQCRQ
CC       (By similarity). Interacts with UQCC5 (PubMed:34969438). Forms a
CC       complex, named COMB/coordinator of mitochondrial CYTB biogenesis,
CC       composed of UQCC1, UQCC2, UQCC4, UQCC5 and UQCC6; stabilizes nascent
CC       cytochrome b/MT-CYB and promotes its membrane insertion. Forms a
CC       complex, named COMA, composed of UQCC1, UQCC2 and UQCC4; activates MT-
CC       CYB translation. Forms a complex, named COMC, composed of UQCC1, UQCC2;
CC       UQCC3 and UQCC4; mediates MT-CYB hemylation and association with the
CC       first nuclear-encoded complex III subunit UQCRQ (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with MT-CYB (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8BTC1}.
