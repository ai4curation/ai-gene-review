# FLNA source evidence and biological boundaries

This manual evidence synthesis accompanies the [review](FLNA-ai-review.yaml) and [source/context guide](FLNA-source-guide.md). Annotation-reviewer and core-function-synthesizer criteria were applied to every seeded assertion. Original evidence codes, partners, qualifiers, order and natural products are preserved. Existing supported generic associations are retained as non-core under the explicit project instruction; no functional subtype is inferred merely from a partner name. No new annotation or supporting-text quotation is added. References below support paraphrased assessments, with actual source access distinguished from curated evidence retained on trust.

## Core actin scaffold

The actin-binding core describes F-actin binding and structural crosslinking. Human FLNA recombinants, full-length human FLNA purified from HEK293 cells, actin co-sedimentation and gelation support the gene-level activity in [PMID:25358863](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms6325). The related human recombinant and M2/A7 experiments in [PMID:10051605](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10051605/) anchor target identity even though the original bulk platelet/leiomyoma filamin preparation does not independently establish isotype purity. FLNA itself supplies the crosslinks. C-terminal deletion can impair dimerization as well as partner binding, so it is not a selective RalA- or CRMP1-binding experiment. Rat CRMP1 and mouse neuronal assays are distinguished from human FLNA reagents. The older [PMID:2391361](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2391361/) and [PMID:3138234](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3138234/) evidence was verified at abstract level; their full Methods and modern residue coordinates are not claimed. The independent human assays support retaining those core assertions.

Human HFF1 focal-adhesion proteomics names filamin-1 separately from filamin-B/C in [PMID:21423176](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21423176/). Location does not make FLNA a motor or show that every recovered protein executes adhesion assembly. The core uses cytosol, cortical cytoskeleton and focal adhesion, without adding redundant NEW annotations. Official definitions are [actin filament binding](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0051015), [actin crosslink formation](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0051764), [cytosol](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005829), [cortical cytoskeleton](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0030863) and [focal adhesion](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005925). No additional kinase, phosphatase, GAP, GEF, transcription-enzyme or actin-depolymerase core is asserted.

## Receptor-tail adaptor core

A second core captures a distinct structural activity: bringing receptor tails and the actin network into functional contact. [GO:0030674 protein-macromolecule adaptor activity](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0030674) covers this coordinated connection. It is not an ancestor or descendant of the retained actin-filament-binding molecular function under the official is_a/part_of paths.

Actual [PMID:21926999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21926999/) Results reconstitute F-actin–FLNA networks containing beta7-integrin cytoplasmic tails. Applied shear or myosin-generated stress changes integrin-tail association with the network. Human FLNA-repeat/integrin-tail structural and biochemical experiments in [PMID:17690686](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17690686/) independently establish the human ligand-binding side; the cellular host in that study includes CHO cells. This is positive evidence for connecting the cytoskeleton to receptor tails, rather than an inference from two unrelated binding lists. The first core describes actin crosslinks; the second describes the receptor-tail connection.

The 21926999 supplementary protein-production/species protocols were not replayed. The core does not declare every reconstituted component human, map experimental deletions onto natural products, or generalize the same linkage to all GPCR, SK2 or GPIb contexts. The four ligand alternatives in PMID:19828450 do not establish simultaneous occupancy on intact FLNA. No additional biological-process term or GOA annotation is introduced. Source-specific generic binding decisions remain unchanged under the campaign instruction.

## Receptors, channels and specialized interactions

Source-specific binding methods remain explicit in each reason. Exact IntAct pairs can establish curated association, co-complex membership or proximity without demonstrating purified binary affinity. Peptide/repeat NMR, calorimetry and binding experiments are distinguished from intact endogenous receptor occupancy. Spoke expansion, crosslinking and proximity ligation are not silently described as direct binary contact. Species and processed viral-chain identifiers remain unchanged.

[PMID:24951510](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4103345/) supplies actual human SK2/FLNA fragment and cell experiments plus mouse cardiomyocyte knockdown, channel-surface measurements and apamin-sensitive currents. These support channel regulation through trafficking; altered gating is not required. Delivery versus removal is not fully separated. The calcium-sensitive alpha-actinin2/CaM effect is not assigned to FLNA. Cardiac repolarization remains downstream inference, and Z-line imaging is directly mouse; human atrial Western blot is expression evidence, not human Z-disc imaging. Separate supplementary Methods and figure pixels were not inspected.

[PMID:17536008](https://academic.oup.com/mend/article/21/9/2242/2738634) supports PMA-induced D3 receptor sequestration in restored A7 but not FLNA-deficient M2 cells, although receptors reach the surface in both. This establishes a contextual sequestering role without FLNA kinase activity or universal delivery dependence. [PMID:18322202](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18322202/) supports Fc gamma RI surface retention and lysosomal protection in human systems. Bafilomycin rescue supports stabilization, whereas initial delivery occurs without FLNA and recycling remains a qualified model. The GO:0045184 directed-movement assertion is therefore UNDECIDED; GO:0034394 is not a formal descendant that could justify TOO_GENERAL. The older [PMID:1833070](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1833070/) abstract directly describes ligand-sensitive receptor/actin-binding-protein association in U937 and receptor-transfected COS cells.

[PMID:12393796](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12393796/) was subsequently recovered as original primary text: human FLNA/FLNB fragment two-hybrid, human NT2 co-IP and cytoplasmic colocalization resolve earlier species/access uncertainty. No migration-compensation mechanism, purified affinity or natural-product-specific heterodimer function follows.

## Platelet and process specificity

Human FLNA/GPIb attachment, recombinant and native platelet complex evidence in [PMID:16293600](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1895705/) supports contextual receptor-complex and activation roles. Human platelet Syk association and separate mouse FlnA-loss experiments in [PMID:20713593](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20713593/) corroborate receptor/scaffold organization. FLNA binds the kinase; it does not acquire kinase activity. Intrinsic coagulation GO:0007597 describes the coagulation-factor cascade, which these receptor-cytoskeleton experiments do not establish FLNA performing. That mapping is over-annotated without denying haemostatic function. Specific megakaryocyte-development and calcium-store-release contexts remain UNDECIDED rather than treating missing inspected experiments as proof of absence. The MDS proteomics in [PMID:23382103](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23382103/) includes FLNA immunoblot validation but no FLNA-specific causal perturbation.

Rat-fragment and human M2/A7 experiments in [PMID:16291724](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16291724/) support receptor/GTPase scaffolding and cellular migration/spreading; scratch assays are not organismal wound-repair demonstrations. [PMID:22121117](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22121117/) reports impaired, not absent, ciliation in human mosaic patient cells. [PMID:25358863](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25358863/) semaphorin evidence includes mouse knockdown with human FLNA rescue and human receptor-reconstituted cells, not only worm biology.

## Nuclear, transcriptional and donor contexts

[PMID:15684392](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC548007/) supports FOXC1 association, nuclear FLNA and PBX1a distribution in distinct human and mouse experiments. PBX1a import is contextual; FOXC1 remains nuclear in both M2 and A7. Antibody/fractionation evidence is not replaced by a claim that all nuclear FLNA is a cleavage fragment. [PMID:22307607](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3277164/) supports cellular negative Pol I regulation and nucleolar retention. Its isolated in-vitro immunodepletion Results versus Fig2F-caption sign discrepancy remains unresolved; it is not silently corrected or used to erase independent cellular evidence.

The cytosolic Myb/clathrin/FLNA association in [PMID:18548008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2486420/) is real, but is distinct from the replication/transcription-regulatory nuclear Myb complex represented by [GO:0031523](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0031523). This term mapping is over-annotated while the contextual spindle process is retained with B-Myb-perturbation limits. [PMID:26157139](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4566247/) supports FLNA organizing HDAC5 localization and tubulin deacetylation; HDAC5 is the enzyme. Engineered fragments are not natural FLNA products.

The rat donor A0A8I5ZV49 and ENSRNOP00000082035 identify the same protein. [PMID:22948144](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3488041/) rat-brain FLNA co-IP supports complex association, not the HCN1/protocadherin purified SPR result as a FLNA assay. [PMID:20167606](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20167606/) rat mesangial experiments distinguish endogenous protein from an expressed human C-terminal construct. Recruitment into a Smad-containing promoter-associated scaffold/shuttle supports contextual transcription involvement beyond necessity alone; direct Smad3 contact, DNA binding and the exact post-arrival Pol II step remain unresolved.

[PMID:22076441](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22076441/) abstract evidence supports rat radial scaffolds/cortical migration, but not an independently resolved positive proliferation direction. [PMID:22872576](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3512009/) selected primary sections support actin organization and developmental Sertoli-barrier/junction maintenance; GO:1902396 includes maintenance and does not require a motor. Rat synaptic and mature dendritic pools in [PMID:26972007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26972007/) and [PMID:22434607](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3393847/) differ in developmental/imaging context, not a universal exclusion. Mouse [PMID:23525008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3672938/) supports podosome localization; Amotl2 perturbation is not attributed to FLNA. Donor transfers remain transfers rather than relabeled human experiments.

## Extracellular pools and unresolved extensions

Urinary exosome target inclusion in the original-author table for [PMID:19056867](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19056867/) and Table1 of [PMID:21362503](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3085584/) was independently checked. For [PMID:20458337](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20458337/) and [PMID:23533145](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23533145/), exact FLNA supplement rows were not recovered; retention explicitly combines curated HDA support and independent target-vesicle evidence. Whole-exosome proteomics is distinct from a small MHC-II co-IP subset. Vesicle detection does not establish conventional secretion, a cancer-specific change or a universal extracellular core function.

The GO:0005642 annulate-lamella extension on the genuine FLNA-FilGAP association differs from the peripheral lamellae described in selected actual [PMID:16862148](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16862148/) Results. The CL:0000639 extension on one [PMID:20458337](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20458337/) context officially names a basophil cell of the anterior pituitary, not a B cell. Both source fields are preserved and flagged as questions. The binding or exosome term is not rejected solely because its extension is unresolved. The PMID:28297718 DND1 nuclear extension likewise remains source-specific uncertainty despite independently supported nuclear crosslink association in PMID:30021884.

The PMID:17408621 exact MINT fragment/assay record points to the original Figure3A cyclin-B1 experiment, while the retained source partner O95067 is officially CCNB2. This concrete accession/assay discrepancy remains UNDECIDED; it is stronger than a title mismatch but does not prove all B2 experiments absent. No source partner is silently replaced.

## Process participation clarified after review

For [PMID:20167606](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20167606/), the positive contribution is recruitment of Smad factors into a nuclear, promoter-associated FLNA complex. The existing rat-to-human transcription assertion is retained contextually because this scaffold/shuttle supplies part of the process. ChIP does not establish direct FLNA-DNA contact, and the experiment does not separate initiation, elongation or mRNA stability after promoter arrival. Those limits do not convert the process assertion into a claim of FLNA polymerase activity. The experimentally generated C-terminal fragment is distinct from a natural splice product.

For [PMID:26157139](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4566247/), HDAC5 C-terminal interaction interference reduces both enzyme accumulation at injured mouse axon tips and local tubulin deacetylation. Human M2/A7 experiments distinguish the FLNA-dependent deacetylation response from HDAC5 nuclear export. FLNA therefore has a measured local scaffold/positioning role, while HDAC5 performs the chemistry. This is stronger than a requirement inferred only from an injury phenotype and does not create a universal axon-growth function.

The two retained process assertions differ from the rejected nuclear Myb-complex mapping: the latter names a compartment-specific complex that the actual cytosolic Myb-Clafi association does not establish. No action changed in this follow-up; the positive structural rationale is now explicit.
