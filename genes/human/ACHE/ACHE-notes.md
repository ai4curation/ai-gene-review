# ACHE evidence notes


## 2026-10-26: complete source-specific acetylcholine panel audit

Human mutagenesis and curated catalytic statements establish acetylcholine + water → choline + acetate (PMID:1517212, PMID:8349597). This terminates transmitter exposure and provides choline for reuse; neither physical binding to CHT nor synthesis of acetylcholine is inferred. Isoform-specific anchoring supports extracellular, basal-lamina and plasma-membrane localization. Nuclear accumulation in apoptotic SK-N-SH cells (PMID:11985878) and Golgi colocalization in osteoblastic cells (PMID:15454088) retain the root organelle claims.

Direct human AChE binding to human collagen IV and mouse laminin is measured in PMID:12524166. Its self-association experiment is described as potentially nonspecific aggregation; independent curated isoform dimers/tetramers support oligomerization rather than relying on that experiment alone. Human AChE amyloid-promoting activity (PMID:8608006) and crosslinking (PMID:9493175; brain-enzyme species unspecified in abstract) support ancillary amyloid association without establishing human disease causality. ENO1 binding is distinguished from activation by an isolated AChE-R peptide (PMID:18194455).

Full PMID:17038428 actually studies mouse Ache knockout, 23 versus 29°C exposure, sympathetic ganglion currents and pharmacological rescue. Retain human cold-thermogenesis transfer as non-core, age/species-limited signaling homeostasis; AChE is not a heat-generating enzyme. Osteoblast development, APP metabolism, fine perinuclear location and three nonclassical process claims remain individually unresolved where full relevant evidence was not recovered. Adhesion and Golgi claims are independently explicit in the accessible osteoblast abstract and are not rejected with them.
