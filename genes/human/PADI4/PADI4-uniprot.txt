CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the citrullination/deimination of arginine residues
CC       of proteins such as histones, thereby playing a key role in histone
CC       code and regulation of stem cell maintenance (PubMed:15339660,
CC       PubMed:15345777, PubMed:16567635, PubMed:19153223, PubMed:21245532,
CC       PubMed:25622091). Citrullinates histone H1 at 'Arg-54' (to form
CC       H1R54ci), histone H3 at 'Arg-2', 'Arg-8', 'Arg-17' and/or 'Arg-26' (to
CC       form H3R2ci, H3R8ci, H3R17ci, H3R26ci, respectively) and histone H4 at
CC       'Arg-3' (to form H4R3ci) (PubMed:15339660, PubMed:15345777,
CC       PubMed:16567635, PubMed:21245532). Acts as a key regulator of stem cell
CC       maintenance by mediating citrullination of histone H1: citrullination
CC       of 'Arg-54' of histone H1 (H1R54ci) results in H1 displacement from
CC       chromatin and global chromatin decondensation, thereby promoting
CC       pluripotency and stem cell maintenance (PubMed:15339660,
CC       PubMed:15345777, PubMed:16567635, PubMed:21245532). Promotes profound
CC       chromatin decondensation during the innate immune response to infection
CC       in neutrophils by mediating formation of H1R54ci (PubMed:18209087).
CC       Citrullination of histone H3 prevents their methylation by CARM1 and
CC       HRMT1L2/PRMT1 and represses transcription (PubMed:15345777). Involved
CC       for the formation of neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs) by mediating
CC       citrullination of histone H3 and initiating decondensation of chromatin
CC       and nuclear membrane rupture (PubMed:19153223, PubMed:25622091,
CC       PubMed:28574339, PubMed:32170015). NETs are mainly composed of DNA
CC       fibers and are released by neutrophils to trap pathogens during
CC       inflammation (PubMed:19153223). Citrullinates EP300/P300 at 'Arg-2142',
CC       which favors its interaction with NCOA2/GRIP1 (PubMed:15731352).
