CC   -!- FUNCTION: Assembly factor involved in the biogenesis of the
CC       mitochondrial-specific ribosomes (mitoribosomes) (PubMed:28892042,
CC       PubMed:30215512, PubMed:31666358). Specifically associates with
CC       intermediates of the mitochondrial ribosome large subunit (mt-LSU) and
CC       is required for proper ribosome assembly, possibly preventing premature
CC       association of the large and small ribosomal subunits (PubMed:28892042,
CC       PubMed:30215512, PubMed:31666358). Thereby, indirectly regulates
CC       mitochondrial translation (PubMed:28892042, PubMed:30215512,
CC       PubMed:31666358). It is also required for complete assembly of the
CC       mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I (PubMed:31666358). May also
CC       function in DNM1L-mediated mitochondrial fission (PubMed:29083303).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28892042, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29083303,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30215512, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31666358}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with intermediates of the mitochondrial ribosome
CC       large subunit (mt-LSU) (via MALSU1 as part of a complex that also
CC       contains NDUFAB1); regulates mitochondrial ribosomes assembly
CC       (PubMed:28892042, PubMed:30215512, PubMed:31666358, PubMed:35177605).
CC       Interacts with MRPL4 (PubMed:30215512). Interacts with MRPS27
CC       (PubMed:30215512). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28892042,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30215512, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31666358,
