# EARS2 evidence notes

## 2026-10-18: initial annotation review

EARS2 uses ATP to glutamylate both mitochondrial tRNA-Glu and mitochondrial tRNA-Gln. The latter is a substrate for the separate GatCAB amidotransferase; EARS2 does not itself supply that amidation. Recombinant transit-sequence-deleted human EARS2 was tested with native bovine and human placental mitochondrial tRNAs, while full-length C-terminal EGFP fusion was used for human-cell mitochondrial targeting. GatCAB knockdowns in the paper are not EARS2 knockdowns (PMID:19805282).

All 22 imported source objects remain intact. Ten are accepted, eleven are refined and zinc binding remains undecided. The two catalytic activities are represented separately, with a shared mitochondrial charging process. Broad nucleotide binding is refined to ATP binding. Current GO marks glutamyl-tRNA aminoacylation GO:0006424 obsolete; the three imported IDs are preserved and their reviews propose GO:0070127. The ontology change does not contradict the biochemical observations. No new process assertion is added from disease necessity.

Matrix localization is supported by the positive curated EXP/ECO:0000269 assertion and the official SL-0170 mapping. The inspected 19805282 main text establishes mitochondrial targeting, but the detailed supplementary Methods were unavailable and the specific matrix assay was not independently reconstructed. This access limit is not negative evidence against the curator's positive finding. Broad mitochondrial annotations are refined using that target-level matrix support rather than relabelling whole-organelle experiments as matrix assays.

The genuine human MitoCoP supplement identifies EARS2 group 6714 in Table S1(A), row 333, and S1(B), row 301. The group contains Q5JPH6, H3BTB7 and Q5JPH6-2 and has measured mitochondrial enrichment, spatial clustering and import dependence. These are not separate measurements of each natural product. The matrix GO flag is imported prior annotation. Its peripheral carbonate/sonication category is operational: approximately 86.43% membrane-associated and 18.01% integral, without assigning a membrane face or excluding matrix-side association (PMID:34800366).

The [portable analysis](EARS2-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md) reconstructs all three PAINT IBD joins on the exact 523-residue target lineage. Current donor records retain their evidence codes, and target self-donation is legitimate experimental grounding. Rhea 23540 and 64612 distinguish the two tRNA substrates; 18397 is the broader Glx reaction. ARBA00026962 still outputs mitochondrion, although the precise current FunFam trigger has not been independently reconstructed. That trace limit does not overturn separate target evidence. Each GO_REF is assessed as a methodological reference with LOW biological relevance rather than a primary experiment.

The zinc-binding inference comes from a genuine IPR033910 match. In the current family alignment, E. coli Zn ligands C98/C100/C125/H127 correspond to human C140/C142/C167/N169. The three cysteines are retained and the fourth site differs. Neither this correspondence nor the absence of a target metal feature establishes loss of binding. No direct human occupancy measurement was recovered, so zinc binding remains unresolved and is not promoted into the core.

The official products have 523 and 534 residues and differ at the C terminus. The NP_001077083.1 reference parent named in the primary study exactly matches Q5JPH6-1, but sequencing of the complete expressed insert is not established. Shared upstream sequence does not prove equivalent natural-product activity or localization. Biallelic EARS2 variants are associated with infantile leukoencephalopathy involving the thalamus and brainstem and elevated lactate, with differing disease severity. The clinical abstract supports disease context, not a new mechanistic pathway or tissue-development annotation (PMID:22492562).
