# MEF2A curation notes

Automated Falcon deep research, with the `perplexity-lite` fallback, was unavailable in this worktree. This note records the manual evidence synthesis used for the MEF2A review.

MEF2A is one of the human RSRF/MEF2 MADS-box transcription factors originally cloned from human muscle. The early human cloning work showed that the proteins are present in muscle-specific DNA-binding complexes, share an amino-terminal MADS DNA-binding domain, and act as MEF2 site-dependent transcriptional activators [PMID:1516833]. The related RSRF study established that human RSRF proteins bind CTA(A/T)4TAG DNA sites as dimers and can dimerize with one another [PMID:1748287]. MEF2A structural work solved both the solution structure and crystal structure of the dimeric MEF2A core bound to a MEF2 DNA target, so direct sequence-specific DNA binding and dimerization are core molecular functions [PMID:10835359; PMID:10715212].

Direct MEF2A target/promoter evidence comes from multiple contexts. PITX2a directly interacts with MEF2A and cooperates on the atrial natriuretic factor promoter in a MEF2-site-dependent way [PMID:15466416]. HAND1 is recruited to promoters through physical interaction with MEF2 proteins and MEF2/HAND1 activates MEF2-dependent promoters synergistically [PMID:16043483]. MEF2A occupies the c-Jun promoter in monocyte/macrophage cells, where MEF2A/MEF2D dimers associate either with HDAC1/HDAC7 or with p300 in a differentiation-dependent way [PMID:20590529]. ChIP and reporter experiments also support HZF1 as a direct MEF2A target [PMID:21468593].

HDAC and kinase regulation are real regulatory inputs to the MEF2A transcription factor. HDAC4 and HDAC5 bind MEF2A and repress its transcriptional activity [PMID:10487761; PMID:10748098]. Smad2 physically associates with MEF2A and cooperates with MEF2 in a p38-dependent transcriptional assay [PMID:11160896]. ERK5/BMK1 and p38 signaling phosphorylate MEF2A and modulate MEF2-dependent transcription; p38 phosphorylates Thr-312/Thr-319 and enhances expression from MEF2A-MEF2D heterodimers [PMID:9753748; PMID:9858528; PMID:10849446].

Some biological-process rows are peripheral outputs rather than the core molecular function. Calcium-dependent dephosphorylation of MEF2A switches Lys-403 from SUMOylation to acetylation and inhibits postsynaptic dendritic claw differentiation, so neuronal calcium-response and dendrite-morphogenesis rows are retained as context-specific [PMID:16484498]. Nfix forms a complex with PKC theta that binds/phosphorylates/activates MEF2A in fetal myogenesis [PMID:20178747]. The BHF cardiac hypertrophy rows from PMID:24161931 could not be verified from the cached abstract, so they are left undecided rather than overruled.
