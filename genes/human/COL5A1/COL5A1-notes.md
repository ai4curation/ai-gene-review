# COL5A1 evidence and curation notes

These notes summarize the literature on COL5A1, with experiment, species and precursor-product limits kept explicit. Automated literature synthesis was unavailable, so the assessment uses manually read sources.

## Matrix structure, assembly and products

COL5A1 supplies alpha1(V), commonly in the alpha1(V)2alpha2(V) heterotrimer and heterotypic collagen fibrils. Human EDS studies report abnormal fibril diameters, reduced type V secretion and disproportionately reduced matrix deposition/fibril number. The 15095409 abstract explicitly compares COL5A1-haploinsufficient cells with COL1A1-haploinsufficient OI cells; it is not a wrong-gene reference. Full originals 11278977/15095409 remain unavailable, so their exact biosynthesis assertions are UNDECIDED rather than treating secretion/deposition amounts as the performed chemical step. Intrinsic chain assembly can be work of formation; the review does not deny it simply because collagen is a precursor. [PMID:11278977](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11278977/), [PMID:15095409](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15095409/)

The complete 15383546 abstract reports mouse Col5a1 loss and failure of ordinary fibril formation, independently supporting initiation/structural work. This is not relabeled as a human assay. The integrated core uses existing GO:0030020, type V trimer and collagen fibril organization, rather than separate duplicate structural, receptor or processing cores. GO:0030020 explicitly permits extracellular-matrix collagens. [PMID:15383546](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15383546/)

PMID:20705069 treats V and XI as “a single collagen type with multiple tissue-specific isoforms” (Structure section), including alpha1(V)-containing type V trimers among the major V/XI isoforms. Its particular alpha1(XI)2alpha2(V) example is not a claim that alpha1(V) is absent from this family. The bovine cartilage/disc/meniscus study PMID:19103590 independently identifies alpha1(V) in crosslinked V/XI polymers and partially resolves alpha1(XI)/alpha1(V)/alpha3(XI) hybrids. Current GO:0005592 enumerates canonical alpha1(XI)/alpha2(XI)/alpha1(II), so the two existing source assertions are generalized to GO:0005583 (fibrillar collagen trimer). This is an existing-assertion correction, not a new coverage claim; the more specific hybrid composition is retained in the reasons and ontology question. [PMID:20705069](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2996481/), [PMID:19103590](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19103590/)

The PMID:9042913 abstract independently links a C-propeptide cysteine substitution to failed mutant-chain trimer incorporation. This supports performed assembly work by the chain itself; the original biosynthesis assertions in PMID:11278977 and PMID:15095409 remain unresolved at their own assay scope because their full Methods were not recovered.

Both official products have 1838 residues and were independently reconstructed against genuine FASTAs. Isoform 2 replaces 1690–1711 in the C-propeptide; current annotations place signal 1–37, mature chain 38–1605 and C-propeptide 1606–1838. The mature-chain sequence is shared, but equal precursor selection, secretion and processing have not thereby been tested. The 9042913 abstract’s disease C-propeptide substitution is separate from these normal splice variants. [PMID:9042913](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9042913/)

## Source-specific interactions

The [original 20979576 accepted manuscript](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/47565572/) was genuinely accessed as indexed author-uploaded text, with actual bait/library Methods, partner Table 1, selected co-IP controls, SPR and processing Results read. It uses the human alpha1(V) N-propeptide and engineered domains/constructs. Fibronectin, MMP2 and tenascin-C positive binary/domain evidence supports the respective partner-class refinements GO:0001968, GO:0002020 and GO:0050840, without an in-vivo colocalization, catalytic modulation or exact active-site requirement. TIMP1 and PCPE1 remain supported non-core binding; an inhibitor or processing enhancer is not automatically an enzyme. BMP1 processing assays are distinguished from the screen hits: no uninspected BMP1 binary retest is claimed. A model of matrix bridging is not made into a new adaptor function. The genuine selected abstract remains unchanged.

The canonical counterpart identities support protease binding (GO:0002020) and protein phosphatase binding (GO:0019903), respectively. These are AP-MS co-complex observations, including spoke-expanded MMP2 records; direct contact, native encounter and enzyme regulation remain unestablished. The N-propeptide/domain retests in PMID:20979576 supply a separate experimental context. The 2025 U2OS paper’s specific COL5A1/MMP2 entry remains unverified and is retained without a more specific claim. The interaction index preserves four matched records and eight source assertions without a current match; lack of a current match is not negative evidence against the original PMID:20979576 or PMID:9099729 observations. [PMID:28514442](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28514442/), [PMID:33961781](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33961781/), [PMID:40205054](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40205054/)

The [original 9099729 Methods/Results](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/14117045/) use pepsinized human amniotic-membrane collagen V and rat NG2, with solid-phase and rotary-shadowing assays. Preserve the rat Q00657 partner, processed preparation and positive proteoglycan-core interaction. The 2203476 and 8900172 complete abstracts support heparin and PDGF binding respectively; full original Methods/preparation species remain uninspected, and aggregate affinities are not assigned to a particular human splice product. ## Applied matrix cues and contextual processes

Recovered [14970208 human Methods/Results](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/5781672/) distinguish COL5A1-deficient from COL3A1-deficient fibroblasts, purified human collagen V/III supplementation and integrin perturbations. They support matrix/fibronectin organization and a scoped integrin-ligand context, not purified alpha1(V)–integrin affinity. The broader fiber-organization assertion is retained because fibronectin and collagen networks are both involved.

The [original 18305566 indexed paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/5546012/) uses human dermal fibroblasts and collagen V supplementation. Scratch-edge attachment stability and migration rescue are positive; this is not a separate quantitative adhesion assay. IGFBP1 Transwell is a different experiment. The epidermal-cell-specific wound-spreading assertion is refined to GO:0044319 (wound healing, spreading of cells); no epidermal cell was tested. The narrower fibroblast-migration term specifies pseudopodium extension/retraction, a mode not resolved by these saved assays, so it is not forced. Integrin organization/localization was assessed by immunofluorescence, whereas metabolic labeling concerned collagen; chemical integrin biosynthesis is not demonstrated. That exact process assertion is marked over-annotated while preserving every positive matrix, integrin, attachment and migration observation. The matrix-dependent movement restores a continuous monolayer surface, matching this wound-spreading parent without implying epidermal cells.

Actual 23154389 Methods/Results expose human ESCs to printed collagen V combinations under Wnt3a/ActivinA induction. The factorial effect reduces SOX17/DNA endoderm output; separate HEK293 adhesion controls are not ESC differentiation assays. Retain this applied matrix-cue effect as non-core, without claiming a universal endogenous receptor pathway or all-product equivalence. [PMID:23154389](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23154389/)

The 16431952 original indexed human examinations and separate mouse time-course Methods/Results support corneal/lid architecture and fibril changes. Human COL5A1 and COL5A2 cases stay separate. The 9683580 complete abstract supplies human splice/skin-disease evidence; proposed mutant incorporation is qualified. The 16430677 abstract is an Achilles-tendon association, not a demonstrated allele-specific assembly mechanism. These contextual observations are kept distinct from the performed matrix core. [PMID:16431952](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16431952/), [PMID:9683580](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9683580/), [PMID:16430677](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16430677/)
