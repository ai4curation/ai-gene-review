Gene Description

COL5A1 encodes the alpha1 chain of type V collagen, a secreted fibrillar matrix component that helps initiate collagen fibrils and regulate their diameter and organization. Alpha1(V) commonly assembles with alpha2(V) into type V heterotrimers that coassemble with type I collagen; tissue-specific V/XI hybrid assemblies also occur. Its retained N-terminal region interacts with matrix proteins and contributes to fibril organization and cell–matrix behavior, whereas the C-propeptide participates in precursor assembly before processing. Reduced or altered alpha1(V) disrupts connective-tissue architecture and causes classical Ehlers–Danlos syndrome, with skin, joint and ocular manifestations. Alternative splicing changes a segment of the C-terminal propeptide.
