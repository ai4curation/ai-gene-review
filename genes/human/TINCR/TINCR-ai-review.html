Gene Description

TINCR encodes an 87-amino-acid ubiquitin-like microprotein (also called TUBL or pTINCR) translated from the main open reading frame of the TINCR transcript on chromosome 19, which was long classified as the long noncoding RNA "terminal differentiation-induced ncRNA". The protein is a single beta-grasp ubiquitin-like domain with a C-terminal SUMO-interacting motif; it is conserved in mammals, has been detected in the cornified stratum corneum and in other epithelia, and is induced by keratinocyte differentiation and by UV-induced DNA damage in a TP53-dependent manner. Endogenous protein is found in the nucleus and at cell-cell junctions, and overexpressed protein is also cytoplasmic. It binds SUMO1 and SUMO2/3 non-covalently through its SIM and associates with the Rho GTPase CDC42, increasing CDC42 SUMOylation and GTP loading, which promotes actin remodelling, junction formation and epithelial (keratinocyte) differentiation. Loss of the protein delays skin wound closure in mice and accelerates UVB-induced squamous cell carcinoma, and its expression suppresses growth of squamous carcinoma cells; in normal keratinocytes it has instead been reported to promote cell-cycle progression.

Core Functions

SUMO-binding ubiquitin-like microprotein that promotes epithelial (keratinocyte) differentiation. Through its C-terminal SUMO-interacting motif it binds SUMO1/2/3 and associates with CDC42, increasing CDC42 SUMOylation and GTP loading, which drives actin remodelling, cell-cell junction formation and expression of differentiation markers.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: How does a non-catalytic SUMO-binding ubiquitin-like protein increase CDC42 SUMOylation? Does it recruit a SUMO E2/E3 to CDC42, protect SUMO-CDC42 from SENPs, or act as a SUMO-loaded adaptor? The answer decides whether an adaptor or enzyme-regulator MF term is appropriate.

Q: Why does the protein promote proliferation in normal keratinocytes (PMID:34351912) but suppress proliferation in transformed epithelial and squamous carcinoma cells (PMID:36369429, PMID:36899004)?

Q: Is the CDC42 interaction direct? Only co-immunoprecipitation has been shown, so a small GTPase binding MF is not yet asserted.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Binding assays with purified pTINCR (wild type and SIM mutant), SUMO2 and CDC42 in GDP and GTP states, plus in vitro SUMOylation of CDC42 with and without pTINCR.

Hypothesis: pTINCR binds CDC42 directly and SUMO-dependently.

Experiment: Compare start-codon knockout and SIM mutant knock-in in primary human keratinocytes, measuring cell-cycle progression, CDC42-GTP and differentiation markers in the same cells.

Hypothesis: The differentiation and proliferation phenotypes reflect one pathway acting in different cell states.

TINCR (A0A2R8Y7D0) review notes

2026-10-08 — MICROPROTEINS Tier 2 review (claude-code)

Identity

  • HGNC TINCR (HGNC:14607), formerly LINC00036 / PLAC2; long annotated as the lncRNA
    "terminal differentiation-induced ncRNA". UniProt A0A2R8Y7D0 (Swiss-Prot, PE1), 87 aa,
    a single ubiquitin-like (beta-grasp) domain (PS50053, 14-83) with a C-terminal
    SUMO-interacting motif (79-83). MANE Select NM_001396408.1 now carries the CDS, so the
    ORF is the main ORF of the TINCR transcript (not an alt-ORF of another host gene); the
    folder name follows the HGNC symbol.
  • Crystal structure PDB 7MRJ (res 2-87) from PMID:36899004.
  • Names in the literature: TUBL (Nita 2021), pTINCR (Boix 2022), TINCR microprotein
    (Morgado-Palacin 2023).

Existence

  • Stratum corneum proteomics PMID:32012357.
  • Endogenous FLAG knock-in mice and targeted proteomics PMID:34351912.

Function evidence

  • Proliferation (Nita 2021, PMID:34351912): overexpression in NHEKs increased S phase; ORF
    frameshift (1-bp del, RNA structure preserved) abolishes the effect in mouse primary
    keratinocytes PMID:34351912. Human NHEK siRNA knockdown decreased S phase.
    Frameshift mice: delayed wound closure PMID:34351912. Nita found that
    two of three siRNAs did not affect involucrin induction, i.e. no differentiation effect.
  • Differentiation (Boix 2022, PMID:36369429): start-codon KO HaCaT/MCF7 cells fail to
    differentiate; pTINCR binds SUMO1/2/3 non-covalently via its SIM (GST pull-down) and
    co-IPs with CDC42, increases CDC42 SUMOylation and GTP-CDC42; SIM mutant inactive
    PMID:36369429. In tumour /
    epithelial lines pTINCR decreases proliferation PMID:36369429.
  • Tumour suppressor in SCC (Morgado-Palacin 2023, PMID:36899004): TP53/UV-induced; Tincr KO
    mice get more UVB SCC.
  • So the direction of the proliferation effect differs between normal keratinocytes (Nita,
    pro-proliferative) and transformed epithelial cells (Boix, Morgado-Palacin, anti-
    proliferative). Neither group explains the discrepancy; mechanism of the Nita effect is
    unknown.

Localization

  • Endogenous: nucleus and cell-cell junctions in HaCaT/MCF7 PMID:36369429. Overexpressed FLAG-TUBL: cytoplasm in HeLa PMID:34351912. IHC in SCC is cytoplasmic.

Decisions

  • Locations: all accepted (nucleus, cytoplasm, anchoring junction).
  • Keratinocyte proliferation IMP: kept as non-core (context-dependent; opposite direction in
    transformed cells; molecular route unknown).
  • Wound healing ISS (from mouse A0A1B0GRQ3 frameshift mice): non-core.
  • NEW: SUMO binding GO:0032183 (direct, SIM-dependent, PMID:36369429); positive regulation of
    epithelial cell differentiation GO:0030858 (start-codon KO + rescue; it is the pTINCR
    protein that acts, via CDC42). Did not propose small GTPase binding as an MF (co-IP only;
    direct binding not shown) nor protein sumoylation regulation (mechanism unknown: TINCR is
    not an E3; it may act as a SUMO-loaded adaptor).
