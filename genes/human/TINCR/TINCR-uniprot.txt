CC   -!- FUNCTION: Promotes epithelial differentiation by enhancing the
CC       sumoylation and activation of CDC42 (PubMed:36369429). Promotes
CC       keratinocyte proliferation (PubMed:32012357). This contributes to the
CC       maintenance of skin homeostasis by enhancing wound healing after skin
CC       injury (By similarity). Acts as a tumor suppressor in epithelial tumors
CC       (PubMed:36369429, PubMed:36899004). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A1B0GRQ3,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32012357, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36369429,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36899004}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via SUMO-interacting motif) with SUMO1 and SUMO2;
CC       the interactions increase TINCR protein stability (PubMed:36369429).
CC       Interacts with CDC42; the interaction promotes CDC42 sumoylation and
CC       activation (PubMed:36369429). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36369429}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36369429}. Cell
CC       junction {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36369429}. Cytoplasm
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A1B0GRQ3}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Detected in the stratum corneum, the outermost
CC       layer of the epidermis (at protein level) (PubMed:32012357). Detected
CC       in skin, sweat and sebaceous glands (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:36369429). Expressed in cutaneous basal keratinocytes with a
CC       prominent up-regulation in more differentiated spinocellular and
CC       granular skin layers (PubMed:36899004). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32012357,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36369429, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36899004}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Up-regulated upon cellular stress such as exposure to UV
