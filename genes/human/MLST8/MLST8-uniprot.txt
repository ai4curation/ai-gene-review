CC   -!- FUNCTION: Subunit of both mTORC1 and mTORC2, which regulates cell
CC       growth and survival in response to nutrient and hormonal signals
CC       (PubMed:12718876, PubMed:15268862, PubMed:15467718, PubMed:24403073,
CC       PubMed:28489822). mTORC1 is activated in response to growth factors or
CC       amino acids (PubMed:12718876, PubMed:15268862, PubMed:15467718,
CC       PubMed:24403073). In response to nutrients, mTORC1 is recruited to the
CC       lysosome membrane and promotes protein, lipid and nucleotide synthesis
CC       by phosphorylating several substrates, such as ribosomal protein S6
CC       kinase (RPS6KB1 and RPS6KB2) and EIF4EBP1 (4E-BP1) (PubMed:12718876,
CC       PubMed:15268862, PubMed:15467718, PubMed:24403073). In the same time,
CC       it inhibits catabolic pathways by phosphorylating the autophagy
CC       initiation components ULK1 and ATG13, as well as transcription factor
CC       TFEB, a master regulators of lysosomal biogenesis and autophagy
CC       (PubMed:24403073). The mTORC1 complex is inhibited in response to
CC       starvation and amino acid depletion (PubMed:24403073). Within mTORC1,
CC       MLST8 interacts directly with MTOR and enhances its kinase activity
CC       (PubMed:12718876). In nutrient-poor conditions, stabilizes the MTOR-
CC       RPTOR interaction and favors RPTOR-mediated inhibition of MTOR activity
CC       (PubMed:12718876). As part of the mTORC2 complex, transduces signals
CC       from growth factors to pathways involved in proliferation, cytoskeletal
CC       organization, lipogenesis and anabolic output (PubMed:15467718,
CC       PubMed:35926713). mTORC2 is also activated by growth factors, but seems
CC       to be nutrient-insensitive (PubMed:15467718, PubMed:35926713). In
CC       response to growth factors, mTORC2 phosphorylates and activates AGC
CC       protein kinase family members, including AKT (AKT1, AKT2 and AKT3), PKC
CC       (PRKCA, PRKCB and PRKCE) and SGK1 (PubMed:15467718, PubMed:35926713).
CC       mTORC2 functions upstream of Rho GTPases to regulate the actin
CC       cytoskeleton, probably by activating one or more Rho-type guanine
CC       nucleotide exchange factors (PubMed:15467718). mTORC2 promotes the
CC       serum-induced formation of stress-fibers or F-actin (PubMed:15467718).
CC       mTORC2 plays a critical role in AKT1 activation by mediating
CC       phosphorylation of different sites depending on the context, such as
CC       'Thr-450', 'Ser-473', 'Ser-477' or 'Thr-479', facilitating the
CC       phosphorylation of the activation loop of AKT1 on 'Thr-308' by
CC       PDPK1/PDK1 which is a prerequisite for full activation
CC       (PubMed:15467718). mTORC2 regulates the phosphorylation of SGK1 at
CC       'Ser-422' (PubMed:15467718). mTORC2 also modulates the phosphorylation
CC       of PRKCA on 'Ser-657' (PubMed:15467718). Within mTORC2, MLST8 acts as a
CC       bridge between MAPKAP1/SIN1 and MTOR (PubMed:31085701).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12718876, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15268862,
