CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transporter for the divalent cation Zn(2+) (PubMed:10681536,
CC       PubMed:29791142, PubMed:30914478). Mediates the influx of Zn(2+) into
CC       cells from extracellular space. The Zn(2+) uniporter activity is
CC       independent of H(+)-driving force, but is modulated by extracellular pH
CC       and membrane potential. Also transports other divalent cations Zn(2+),
CC       Cd2(+), Cu2(+), Co2(+) in the order of decreasing affinity,
CC       respectively (PubMed:29791142, PubMed:30914478). In the skin, aids in
CC       the differentiation of keratinocytes in the epidermis (By similarity).
