| **SLC26A1 is a major determinant of human sulfate homeostasis** | A patient homozygous for **p.Leu275Pro** had plasma sulfate **138–159 μM (mean 148 μM)** versus approximately **300 μM** physiologically. (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010) | The patient had a 13-mm renal stone, but its composition was unavailable. (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010) | Participants had CKD, although subanalysis suggested the effect was not explained by reduced eGFR. (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000024) | Functional assays recreated the corresponding human changes in a **mouse Slc26a1 construct** expressed in HEK293T cells.
