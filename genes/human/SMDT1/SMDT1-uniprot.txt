CC   -!- FUNCTION: Essential regulatory subunit of the mitochondrial calcium
CC       uniporter complex (uniplex), a complex that mediates calcium uptake
CC       into mitochondria (PubMed:24231807, PubMed:26774479, PubMed:27099988,
CC       PubMed:30454562, PubMed:31080062, PubMed:32315830, PubMed:32494073,
CC       PubMed:32762847, PubMed:32790952, PubMed:33296646). Required to bridge
CC       the calcium-sensing proteins MICU1 with the calcium-conducting subunit
CC       MCU (PubMed:24231807, PubMed:30454562, PubMed:32494073,
CC       PubMed:32762847, PubMed:32790952). Acts by mediating activation of MCU
CC       and retention of MICU1 to the MCU pore, in order to ensure tight
CC       regulation of the uniplex complex and appropriate responses to
CC       intracellular calcium signaling (PubMed:27099988, PubMed:31080062,
CC       PubMed:32315830, PubMed:33296646). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24231807,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26774479, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27099988,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30454562, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31080062,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32315830, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32494073,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32762847, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32790952,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33296646}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the uniplex complex, composed of MCU, EMRE/SMDT1,
CC       MICU1 and MICU2 (or MICU3) in a 4:4:1:1 stoichiometry (PubMed:24231807,
CC       PubMed:27099988, PubMed:31080062, PubMed:32315830, PubMed:32494073,
CC       PubMed:32762847, PubMed:32862359, PubMed:33296646). The number of
CC       EMRE/SMDT1 molecules is hovewer variable, ranging from 1 to 4 copies
CC       per uniplex complex, leading to uniplex complexes with distinct
CC       gatekeeping profiles (PubMed:32315830). Interacts (via its C-terminal
CC       poly-Asp tail) with MCUR1; the interaction is direct (PubMed:27184846).
CC       Unprocessed form interacts (via transit peptide) with MAIP1
CC       (PubMed:27642048). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24231807,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27099988, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27184846,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27642048, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31080062,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32315830, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32494073,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32762847, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32862359,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33296646}.

CC       transmembrane region mediates interaction with MCU and is required to
CC       activate the calcium-conducting pore in the uniporter complex.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27099988}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The poly-Asp region at the C-terminus mediates interaction with
CC       the polybasic region of MICU1. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27099988}.
CC   -!- PTM: Undergoes proteolytic degradation in neurons: degraded by AFG3L2
CC       and SPG7 before SMDT1/EMRE assembly with the uniporter complex,
CC       limiting the availability of SMDT1/EMRE for MCU assembly and promoting
CC       efficient assembly of gatekeeper subunits with MCU (PubMed:27642048,
CC       PubMed:28396416, PubMed:31097542). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27642048,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28396416, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31097542}.
