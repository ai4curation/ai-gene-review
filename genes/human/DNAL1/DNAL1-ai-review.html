Gene Description

DNAL1 is a leucine-rich-repeat light-chain component of axonemal outer dynein arms. Its associations with dynein heavy chains and alpha-tubulin support the integrity and assembly of the ciliary motor apparatus. Biallelic pathogenic variants can destabilize DNAL1 and impair these associations, causing primary ciliary dyskinesia with defective outer dynein arms.

DNAL1 evidence notes

DNAL1 is a light-chain component of the outer dynein arm. Human binding and disease evidence supports heavy-chain association, alpha-tubulin association and a structural contribution to arm assembly or stability [PMID:15845866; PMID:21496787]. These are complementary features of one motor-complex role, without evidence that DNAL1 itself is an ATPase or autonomous motor.

Primary source scope

PMID:15845866, Identification and analysis of axonemal dynein light chain 1 in primary ciliary dyskinesia patients was independently inspected in the UNC-hosted original PDF, Methods (Expression analysis; Co-immunoprecipitation analysis), Results and Figure 2/3 text. Human 190-residue EGFP-DNAL1 expressed in HEK293T cells was mixed with pig tracheal high-salt axonemal extract, and DNAH5 was recovered relative to an EGFP control. This is positive mixed-species extract association, not purified human binary affinity. Human Northern expression in testis and mouse in-situ expression in node, airway and ependyma are separate observations. The negative screen in 86 PCD patients does not contradict the subsequently identified pathogenic DNAL1 allele.

PMID:21496787, Primary ciliary dyskinesia caused by homozygous mutation in DNAL1 was independently inspected in selected Methods, Results and Figure 1/3/4 text. Human wild-type/N150S DNAL1-Myc was expressed in HEK293T cells and combined with rat tracheal high-salt axonemal extract. Matching DNAL1 amounts required different cell-extract inputs. N150S reduced heavy-chain and alpha-tubulin co-recovery by approximately 80%; the separate cellular decay assay established reduced stability. Respiratory cilia from affected individuals lacked normal outer arms. The work supports the structural interaction role but does not directly measure a purified human ternary bridge, ATP-independent human tether mechanics or mechanosensing. Both central publication caches are genuinely abstract-only; the separately recovered originals support the assay detail recorded here, without altering those caches.

PMID:29601588, ZMYND10 stabilizes intermediate chain proteins in the cytoplasmic pre-assembly of dynein arms was inspected in Results (ZMYND10 interactions and DNAI1 stability), Figure 4, supplementary Figure S7 captions, and the plasmid/co-immunoprecipitation/stability Methods. These operative sections name DNAI1. The current DNAL1 annotation and ZMYND10 curated protein record nevertheless attribute a DNAL1 association to this paper. The exact target-specific provenance remains unresolved. A current IntAct/MINT nonmatch is not a negative assay, and neither a wrong-gene verdict nor nonbinding is inferred.

PMID:32296183, A reference map of the human binary protein interactome supplies the HuRI Methods context. The exact positive pair is DNAL1 Q4LDG9–LRRC40 Q9H9A6, including EBI-24553346 (IM-25472-137459), EBI-23484276 and EBI-25039784. Six sequence/taxon hashes across the three records match the displayed 190-residue DNAL1 and 602-residue LRRC40 proteins. A sufficient-binding-region feature covers DNAL1 residues 40–190; it does not identify the molecule as natural isoform 3. The positive generic binding is retained as non-core under the scoped project instruction, without inferring an endogenous complex or specific functional class from partner identity alone.
