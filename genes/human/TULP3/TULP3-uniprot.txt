CC   -!- FUNCTION: Protein-protein adapter that drives transport of G protein-
CC       coupled receptors into primary cilia by mediating association between
CC       the intraflagellar transport complex A (IFT-A) and G protein-coupled
CC       receptors FFAR4, GPR45 and GPR161 (PubMed:11375483, PubMed:20889716,
CC       PubMed:31761534, PubMed:36775821). Acts as a negative regulator of the
CC       Shh signaling by promoting trafficking of GPR161 to cilia
CC       (PubMed:20889716, PubMed:36775821). During adipogenesis, regulates
CC       ciliary trafficking of FFAR4 in preadipocytes (PubMed:31761534).
CC       Promotes transport of GPR45 into primary cilia of neurons in
CC       paraventricular nucleus (PVN) of the hypothalamus (By similarity).
CC       Binds to phosphorylated inositide (phosphoinositide) lipids; both IFT-
CC       A- and phosphoinositide-binding properties are required to regulate
CC       ciliary G protein-coupled receptor trafficking (PubMed:11375483,
CC       PubMed:20889716). Besides its role in cilium localization, acts as a
CC       regulator of AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) activity in response
CC       to calorie restriction: binds lithocholic acid, a metabolite generated
CC       in response to calorie restriction, promoting TULP3 ability to activate
CC       SIRT1, which then deacetylates the ATP6V1E1 subunit of the V-ATPase
CC       complex, leading to (1) V-ATPase complex inhibition on lysosomes and
CC       (2) AMPK activation via the AXIN1-STK11/LKB1 axis (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O88413, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11375483,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20889716, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31761534,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36775821}.
