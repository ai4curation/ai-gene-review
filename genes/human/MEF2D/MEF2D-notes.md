# MEF2D curation notes

Automated Falcon deep research, with the `perplexity-lite` fallback, was unavailable in this worktree. This note records the manual evidence synthesis used for the MEF2D review.

MEF2D is the fourth human MEF2 MADS-box transcription factor. The original hMEF2D paper cloned alternatively spliced products of a single gene and showed that hMEF2D can activate transcription through sequence-specific MEF2-site binding, recapitulating endogenous tissue-specific MEF2 activity [PMID:8269842]. Broader human MEF2 family work supports homo- and heterodimeric DNA binding to CTA(T/A)4TAG/A sites for MEF2A-D proteins [PMID:1516833; PMID:1748287; PMID:8575763].

MEF2D works with MEF2A in human monocyte/macrophage terminal differentiation. MEF2A/MEF2D heterodimers are abundant in macrophage-differentiated HL60 cells, MEF2A occupies the c-Jun promoter, the dimers interact with HDAC1 and HDAC7, and differentiated macrophages show p300 association with MEF2A [PMID:20590529]. The MEF2A-D heterodimer response to p38 also supports dimerization and MEF2-dependent transcription by mixed MEF2 dimers [PMID:9858528].

MEF2D regulation partly parallels MEF2A and MEF2C but is isoform-selective. BMK1/ERK5 phosphorylates and activates MEF2D but not MEF2B [PMID:10849446]. MEF2D is phosphorylated, acetylated, SUMOylated and deacetylated by class I/II HDAC/SIRT proteins in studies cited by UniProt, but the GOA molecular-function rows in this batch mostly reduce to DNA binding, HDAC binding, dimerization, and DNA-binding transcription-factor partner binding.

The vascular smooth muscle proliferation row from the miR-665 paper is kept as over-annotated: the abstract supports MEF2D as one of two miR-665 targets whose forced expression can attenuate miR-665's anti-proliferative effect, but that perturbation does not establish MEF2D as a direct executor of vascular smooth-muscle-cell proliferation [PMID:29118903]. The human adult-heart-development row is likewise expression/splicing evidence during cardiac maturation, not direct MEF2D activity in the process [PMID:10458488].
