# SUCLG2 (Q96I99) notes

## 2026-11-11 first review

Written as part of the evolutionary review of the succinyl-CoA synthetase families; see genes/yeast/LSC2/LSC2-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md.

- SUCLG2 is the GTP-specific beta subunit; with SUCLG1 it forms the GDP-forming enzyme, crystallised as the human heterodimer [PMID:32627745 "Human GTP-specific SCS (GTPSCS), an αβ-heterodimer, was produced in Escherichia coli"].
- Phylogeny: SUCLG2 is below PAINT node PTN000929335 with pig, mouse, invertebrate, choanoflagellate and Dictyostelium subunits; 38 of the 42 leaves below that node have Gln at position 20 of the mature subunit (Gln57 of the human precursor). SUCLA2 and the fungal A-type beta subunits are in the other daughter clade of a duplication that predates animals [file:yeast/LSC2/LSC2-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md "The duplication that produced the A-type and G-type genes predates the divergence of animals, fungi and Dictyostelium, because Dictyostelium, Monosiga, Mortierellomycota and Basidiobolus each have one gene of each type in the trees"]. (Corrected 2026-11-12: Gln20 is not confined to this clade and does not by itself mark GDP specificity; see below.)
- NOT GO:0004775 (ADP-forming) IBA accepted. Evidence is descent, the GTP-specific pig enzyme, in which Gln20 contacts guanine [PMID:16481318 "interactions of the guanine base with a glutamine residue (Gln-20beta) and with backbone atoms provide the specificity"] plus the pigeon enzyme [PMID:9765290 "Two succinyl-CoA synthetases, one highly specific for GTP/GDP and the other for ATP/ADP, have been purified to homogeneity from pigeon liver and breast muscle"]. No assay of the human enzyme with ADP was read; recorded as a suggested question.
- ATP binding (IEA from InterPro IPR013815, ATP-grasp subdomain) modified to GTP binding. The signature is named for the fold; in the G-type subunit the fold binds GTP [PMID:16481318 "GTP is bound in the ATP-grasp domain"]. This InterPro2GO mapping is wrong for the characterised GTP-specific members (pig and human SUCLG2). A curated rule conditioned on the clade node PTN000929335 would also need to exclude the misplaced Xanthomonas (Q8P676) and Ustilago (A0A0D1E3R8) leaves, and for the unassayed members it rests on descent (reworded 2026-11-12; the line used to say the mapping is wrong for the whole clade). It must not be conditioned on the Gln20 site, because Gln20 also occurs in an ATP-specific subunit (corrected 2026-11-12).
- GO:0042709 succinate-CoA ligase complex IBA marked too general beside GO:0045244 (GDP-forming), the same treatment as LSC2, S. pombe SPCC1620.08 and now SUCLA2.
- Protein binding IPI rows removed under the policy. The HSC20 (HSCB) rows are a yeast two-hybrid hit confirmed by co-immunoprecipitation, of unknown significance [PMID:24606901 "we identified interacting partners of the cochaperone HSC20"].
- Reactome CLPXP rows (R-HSA-9838035, R-HSA-9838289): matrix location accepted; the cached summaries do not name SUCLG2, so client status was not checked.
- Direct localization not in GOA [PMID:25370487 "Specificity of SUCLG2 antiserum was supported by co-localization with mitotracker orange staining of paraformaldehyde-fixed human fibroblast cultures, delineating the mitochondrial network."].

### Affinage

## Retrospective audit corrections (2026-11-12)

A retrospective audit of the succinyl-CoA synthetase reviews found errors in this review. Each was checked against the source before it was changed. No annotation action changed.

- Gln20 is not a marker of GDP specificity. The Blastocystis enzyme has Gln at the position of pig Gln20 and is ATP-specific [PMID:18452512 "Although analysis of residues inside the nucleotide-binding site suggests that Blastocystis SCS is GTP-specific, we demonstrate that it is ATP-specific."]. The reworked family analysis finds Gln20 outside the clade below PTN000929335 as well [file:yeast/LSC2/LSC2-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md "Gln at position 20 is not sufficient for GTP specificity, and is not required for GTP use or preference."]. The description, the core function, the NOT GO:0004775 reason, the GO:0004776 IBA reason, a suggested question and the first suggested experiment were reworded. They now say that the characterised GTP-specific enzymes have Gln20 and that the residue contacts guanine in the pig enzyme, not that it sets specificity.
- The statement that GDP specificity is a derived state inside the G clade was removed. It depended on PANTHER's placement of plant subunits on the SUCLG2 side, which the sequence trees do not support. Whether Gln20 arose on the stem of the G-type lineage or within it is not resolved.
- NOT GO:0004775 (IBA): still ACCEPT. The reason now rests on descent from the PAINT loss node, the GTP-specific pigeon and pig enzymes [PMID:8401211 "this combination of subunits forms a productive enzymatic complex having GTP specificity"] and the human enzyme being characterised as GTP-specific in the curated UniProt function text. The RESULTS.md sentence quoted before no longer exists and was replaced. No assay of the human enzyme with ADP was read.
- Itaconate. PMID:34492704 was cited in the proposed new term as support for itaconate-CoA ligase activity, but it reports that pig heart SCS-G did not convert itaconate [PMID:34492704 "neither ATP- nor GTP-specific SCS is able to use itaconate under the same conditions as succinate in vitro"]. The quote was removed. The bioRxiv preprint of PMID:40108300 (doi 10.1101/2024.09.25.614871, version 1) was read and reports that recombinant human SUCLG1/SUCLG2 forms itaconyl-CoA with a catalytic efficiency 15-fold lower than with succinate, so the proposed term is kept with the conflict stated. The description now says itaconyl-CoA formation is reported but contested. The CLYBL abstract sentence was removed from the GO:0004776 EXP row.
- Knowledge gap. The gap said no GTP-requiring process had been tied to the enzyme. In beta cells one has [PMID:31315053 "mtGTP synthesized by succinyl-CoA synthetase (SCS) is hydrolyzed via mitochondrial PEPCK (PEPCK-M) to make phosphoenolpyruvate"], and isoform-specific knockdown separates the two enzymes [PMID:17403370 "siRNA suppression of the GTP-producing pathway (DeltaSCS-GTP) reduced glucose-stimulated insulin secretion (GSIS) by 50%, while suppression of the ATP-producing isoform (DeltaSCS-ATP) increased GSIS 2-fold"]. The gap is narrowed to what remains open and its dark_aspect changed from BP_DARK to RESIDUAL_SUBGAP, because the TCA-cycle role is core in the same review. Insulin secretion is a downstream effect and no process annotation is proposed from it.
- Tissue expression. The description said SUCLG2 is most abundant in liver and kidney, without a source. It now follows the UniProt tissue-specificity statement, and a finding quoting that line was added. Only pigeon data give enzyme ratios by tissue [PMID:9765290 "the ratios of GTP-specific to ATP-specific activities in kidney, brain, and heart are approximately 7, 1, and 0.1, respectively"], so no human ratio is stated; the description says the ratio varies between tissues [PMID:15234968 "both proteins are widely expressed in mammals with the relative amounts varying from tissue to tissue"].
- HSC20. The cached text of PMID:24606901 holds only the Introduction and Discussion. The Results (PubMed Central, PMC6550293) say the interaction was confirmed by co-immunoprecipitation, and the authors note a C-terminal lysine pattern [PMID:24606901 "SUCLG2, a protein identified in our screen, which may provide succinyl-CoA consumed in the first step of heme biosynthesis, contains a similar consensus at its C-terminus"]. The two rows stay REMOVE under the protein-binding policy. The suggested questions on HSC20 and on SDHB were merged into one.
- References. PMID:34415331 was described as truncated in the cache. The abstract is complete and states the SDHB and complex II result; it is now VERIFIED with a finding. PMID:28380382 changed from VERIFIED to UNVERIFIED because SUCLG2 is not visible in the cached abstract. The PMID:9765291 dating finding stays DISPUTED and now also cites published support [PMID:18452512 "it is clear that a taxonomically broad range of eukaryotes possess two distinct forms of SCSβ"]. Affinage record: correctness changed from DISPUTED to LOW_QUALITY, with the DISPUTED status moved to its GO grounding finding; its one supporting quote on the mitochondrion IBA row was kept beside the primary paper it was verified against. (Removed later on 2026-11-12: a record rated LOW_QUALITY should not support an annotation row; the row keeps its primary support. The validator's advisory warning that no annotation cites the deep research file is accepted.)
- Mitochondrion rows on IBA, IEA and NAS evidence: the sentence about an assay not resolving the sub-compartment was replaced by what each source rests on. GO:0006104 rows: the reason now says why the term is kept beside its child GO:1901289.
- Status changed from INITIALIZED to DRAFT.
- Blastocystis follow-up. The 2008 paper explained the ATP specificity by two rim lysines [PMID:18452512 "GTP is destabilized by interaction with Lys 42 and Lys 110"]. The same groups revised this in 2019 [PMID:31282474 "The crystal structures show that the enzyme is specific for ADP/ATP because of interactions between the nucleotide and the binding site"]: Gln20 and the linker between the two ATP-grasp subdomains read the adenine base [PMID:31282474 "of the linker that make the enzyme ATP-specific"], while the double rim mutant [PMID:31282474 "binds GTP better than ATP according to kinetic assays"]. PMID:31282474 was added as a reference (abstract only). The suggested experiment now names the linker beside Gln20 and the two rim positions and includes a linker-swap construct.
- GO:0006104 rows: the child GO:1901289 is defined by breakdown of succinyl-CoA, so the reason now says it names only the direction that consumes succinyl-CoA (the forward direction of the TCA cycle), not "the oxidative direction".
- Knowledge gap provenance: PMID:31315053 added for the PEPCK-M statement.
- PMID:28380382: the open full text (PMC12285277) was read. SUCLG2 is not named in the main text; the HSC20 partners are in Table S1 and in IntAct (IM-25655), which were not checked. Correctness stays UNVERIFIED.
- Not done: the published text of PMID:40108300 was not read. Only the abstract of PMID:31282474 was read.
- Cross-file consistency pass (2026-11-12). GO:0003824 catalytic activity (IEA) stays TOO_GENERAL; its reason is now worded the same way in all eleven subunit reviews of the family.
