# DRC4 review notes

## 2026-10-18 — source-preserving review

DRC4 is the current human symbol for HGNC:4166/O95995; GAS8 and GAS11 are previous names. The qualified main tree contained no review or history under any of those names, so this is a new DRC4 review rather than a rename. Ordinary isolated fetching produced 170 source assertions from 171 raw GOA contexts. The duplicate context is the same axonemal IDA assertion from PMID:26387594 supplied by MGI and UniProt. Every seeded source dictionary, qualifier and supporting entity is preserved. The decisions are 32 ACCEPT, 40 MODIFY, 97 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE and 1 UNDECIDED; no NEW annotation is proposed.

The independent core consultation and the annotation review converge on one N-DRC scaffold mechanism. Human loss of DRC4 removes DRC3 from axonemes, disrupts doublet alignment and reduces bending amplitude while some dynein-arm markers remain present. Detailed scaffold geometry is resolved in algae; the human respiratory reconstruction identifies the homologous subunits and places predicted models into density. These are complementary evidence levels, not interchangeable human atomic-interface experiments. [PMID:26387594](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26387594/), [PMID:37258679](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37258679/).

The integrated core records scaffold activity, axoneme assembly, cilium movement, its regulation and DRC3 localization/retention. These processes are not redundant in the current typed GO closure. Axoneme assembly is below microtubule cytoskeleton organization; flagellated sperm motility is below cilium movement. Thus the broader organization and specific sperm context are explained within this unit without adding another activity or duplicate process entry. Axonemal anatomy remains a location, separately from membership in the N-DRC/axonemal nexin-link complex. None of this implies motor ATPase activity or loss of every dynein arm.

Conserved microtubule binding supports the scaffold mechanism. The 2007 source reports single-particle binding and GMAD/IMAD effects, but its full construct Methods were not recovered. The 2000 abstract explicitly identifies a human GAS11 fragment associated with microtubules; a title foregrounding trypanosomes does not negate that experiment. The 2006 original distinguishes human tagged protein and human cell/airway detection from purified mouse protein tested with rodent Golgi membranes. These sources support binding and accessory localization without assigning every assay to a current human splice product. [PMID:17366626](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17366626/), [PMID:10969087](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10969087/), [PMID:16643277](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0854.2006.00411.x).

The Smoothened evidence remains positive and context-dependent. Human C-terminal GAS8 fragments interacted with human SMO in yeast two-hybrid assays. The subsequent co-immunoprecipitation and cellular experiments used mouse Gas8, including an engineered GMAD–CT construct whose GRK2-dependent signaling effect was significant; the full-length construct's effects were variable and nonsignificant. Mouse knockdown and zebrafish rescue experiments support the stated signaling context. A later mouse gene-trap study found motile-ciliary defects but normal tested SAG-induced Smo accumulation and developmental patterning. Its residual N-terminal Gas8–beta-geo fusion prevents an assumption of a clean protein-null comparison. Neither study erases the other, and no separate universal signaling core or kinase/GEF activity is asserted. [PMID:21659505](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3149358/), [PMID:27472056](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27472056/).

Current HPA ICC/IF records support nonexclusive plasma-membrane, Golgi, cytosolic, primary-ciliary and sperm-segment pools. The cytoplasm refinements use independent human cytosolic records and do not pretend the original mouse assays resolved cytosol. The sperm localization records and a human infertility original support the flagellar context. Loss of a C-terminal antibody signal in that patient cannot distinguish absent protein from a truncated product, and the reported splice effect was predicted rather than measured in patient RNA. [HPA GAS8/DRC4](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000141013-GAS8/subcellular), [PMID:37950557](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37950557/).

All 109 HuRI partner assertions have literal IntAct records and matching parent-sequence checksums. Nineteen partners also have separately recorded sufficient-binding-region features; a parent checksum does not erase those fragment boundaries. The KRT27 BioPlex association has independent HuRI corroboration. The interaction extract is factual, while the review supplies the biological judgment. Positive pairs without an established narrower class remain non-core under the authorized project binding policy, not false positives. Binary interaction plus a verified class supports the bounded refinements; the partner's catalytic, transcriptional or cytoskeletal function is never transferred to DRC4.

The four final class corrections make this distinction explicit. TCEA2 assists elongation and stalled-polymerase recovery, EAF1 stimulates ELL/ELL2, and HEXIM2 sequesters P-TEFb. Those functions do not establish the DNA-bound transcription-factor/coregulator relationship required by the current coregulator terms, so these pairs remain positive non-core interactions. CDC37 instead has an established client-binding co-chaperone role and is directly observed contacting an unfolded kinase intermediate. Its verified DRC4 binary pair supports protein-folding chaperone binding without requiring DRC4 to be a folding client or a chaperone itself. The exact current definitions are preserved in [the ontology response](source-evidence/partner-class-definitions.json). [CDC37 official record](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q16543/entry), [PMID:27339980](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27339980/).

Seven IBA assertions were traced to literal IBD records and the target's ancestral path in PTHR31543. Their descendant evidence includes human, mouse, worm and fish contexts as applicable. A target appearing among the descendants is expected and is not circularity; donor count is not used to grade the phylogenetic judgment. The positive mouse sources for microtubule binding, Rab3B association and sperm/ciliary localization remain species-scoped. The single unresolved annotation is negative regulation of proliferation from PMID:9790751: its authentic human GAS11 abstract is relevant, but target-specific full Methods were unavailable, so no false-experiment or wrong-gene claim is made.

The two natural products are O95995-1 (478 residues) and O95995-2 (453 residues, deletion of residues 1–25). Both sequences were independently reconstructed. Antibody detection of two forms does not establish functional equivalence, and historical fragments or engineered mutants are not relabeled as natural isoforms.
