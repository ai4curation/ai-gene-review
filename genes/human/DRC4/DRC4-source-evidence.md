## Interaction identity and functional class

The [target data](source-evidence/target-data.json) and [source selection](source-evidence/selection.json) retain all 112 IPI assertions. There are literal current IntAct matches for all 109 HuRI pairs and the KRT27 BioPlex pair. SMO and DRC1 have positive original evidence even though this current IntAct query did not return their matching records. Absence from that query is not negative biological evidence.

[HuRI PMID:32296183](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32296183/) was read at the main generation/characterization section. Three assay configurations, repeated pairwise testing and sequence confirmation support binary interactions. Dataset-level MAPPIT/GPCA validation is not assigned to each DRC4 pair. All parent/reference ROGIDs match the corresponding official target/partner sequences. Nineteen partners have separately recorded sufficient-binding-region features; exact ranges remain visible in the data. Examples that affect class interpretation are TRIM41 residues 86–630, excluding its RING region, and MID2-2 residues 21–705, retaining its RING. Isoform suffixes are authenticated separately, not assumed equivalent from names.

[BioPlex PMID:33961781](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33961781/) supplies KRT27 bait and DRC4 prey in a spoke-expanded anti-tag capture record. Its independently observed HuRI pair supports the bounded cytoskeletal-protein-binding refinement. The protected main cache is marked full text, but its usable content does not include a complete Methods read. The separate larger normal-fetch variant is retained only as research provenance; main bytes are not replaced.

Partner classes come from exact current UniProt identities, functions and relevant domains, interpreted against GO definitions. The replacement terms describe what DRC4 binds, not activities performed by the partner. In particular, keratin binding is not automatically binding to an assembled intermediate filament, a kinase inhibitor is not a kinase, and a complex subunit does not automatically possess the complex's catalytic activity. Positive associations without a justified narrower class remain non-core.

The final bounded class review is documented by the unmodified [official definitions](source-evidence/partner-class-definitions.json) and their [source receipt](source-evidence/partner-class-definitions-source.json). GO:0003712 requires regulation via binding to DNA-bound transcription factors, and GO:0001221 describes the corresponding coregulator relationship. TCEA2, EAF1 and HEXIM2's verified elongation roles do not establish that class. GO:0051087 instead describes binding to a client-folding/transport chaperone. CDC37's official functional record and [PMID:27339980](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27339980/) abstract/captions and selected main introduction establish its kinase-client contact; the structural paper is partner-class evidence, not a DRC4 assay. No DRC4 folding-client mechanism is required or asserted.

## Phylogeny, localization and products

The [reproducible extract](source-evidence/README.md) records all seven literal IBD joins and DRC4's 18-node target path in PTHR31543. The relevant ancestors are PTN000774884 and PTN002834877; no IRD occurs on the captured target path. Human self-descendant evidence is expected and is not circular. Mouse Q60779, fish F1QNW4 and worm Q18689 source records were examined with their actual organism boundaries; a small donor list is not treated as weak phylogenetic support. Full donor papers were not all reread where only the literal source identity/term was needed.

The [official HPA page](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000141013-GAS8/subcellular) still uses GAS8. Its XML maps to O95995/current DRC4. Antibody HPA041311 records supported ICC/IF localization in A-431, U-251MG, U2OS, ASC52telo, serum-starved hTERT-RPE1, RPTEC/TERT1 and sperm contexts. Exact locations and image URLs are retained in the extract. These are record-level observations; image pixels were not reinterpreted and no isoform specificity is claimed. Cytosol is a nonexclusive human refinement, not a relabeling of every historical donor experiment.

Current official products are 478 and 453 residues, with the latter deleting residues 1–25. Sequence reconstruction matches both official FASTAs. None of the source fragments, truncation mutants or antibody bands is automatically identified as the 453-residue product. The N-DRC core uses axoneme as anatomical location and axonemal nexin link as complex membership; their relation does not justify overwriting the original anatomy qualifiers.

## Acquisition and reproduction limits

Genuine normal caches and every preexisting main cache are preserved without hand editing. Abstract-only flags remain literal even where a separately recovered original was read. Conversely, a full-text flag is not proof that omitted Methods were inspected. Falcon and fallback failures are documented in the execution packet; no synthetic provider report is supplied.

The public extractor uses only the Python standard library and explicit source paths. It verifies hashes before deriving pair identity, fragment annotations, HPA records and PAINT descent. It does not generate curation actions or infer native interactions. The raw source URLs and hashes are public; historical exact input files can also be supplied through the documented path map. Live URLs may have changed since capture, in which case the checksum failure is intentional. No authentication keys or hidden service are needed for saved-input reproduction.
