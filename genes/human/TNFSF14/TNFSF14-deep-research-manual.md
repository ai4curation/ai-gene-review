# TNFSF14 manual literature synthesis

Perplexity deep research was unavailable in this workspace, so this file records a manual synthesis from cached primary papers, Reactome events, UniProt, and the existing PTHR11471 family review.

## Core biology

TNFSF14 encodes LIGHT/HVEM-L, a TNF-family type II membrane ligand that can also be shed. The LIGHT discovery paper reports that HVEM binds "secreted lymphotoxin alpha (LTalpha) and LIGHT" and that LIGHT "also engages the receptor for the LTalphabeta heterotrimer" [PMID:9462508], establishing binding to TNFRSF14/HVEM and LTBR. An independent HVEM-L paper used HVEM-Fc to identify a soluble TNF-related protein and found that it "specifically bound to HVEM-Fc with an affinity of 44 nM" [PMID:9765287].

LIGHT can costimulate T cells and signal through LTBR/HVEM-associated NF-kappaB pathways, but it is not the FASLG or TRAIL death-receptor ligand. The PTHR11471 family review does not place TNFSF14 in the FASLG/TNFSF10 death-domain subfamilies. Death-receptor apoptosis rows need direct TNFSF14 evidence, while broad TNF-family cytokine, TNF receptor superfamily binding, plasma membrane, extracellular region, LTBR binding, HVEM binding, and NF-kappaB activation rows are the expected core or near-core assertions.
