CC       recognition receptor recognizing pathogen- and damage-associated
CC       molecular patterns (PAMPs and DAMPs) to induce innate immune responses
CC       via downstream signaling pathways (PubMed:10835634, PubMed:15809303,
CC       PubMed:16622205, PubMed:17292937, PubMed:17478729, PubMed:20037584,
CC       PubMed:20711192, PubMed:23880187, PubMed:27022195, PubMed:29038465,

CC       with LY96 to mediate the innate immune response to bacterial
CC       lipopolysaccharide (LPS) (PubMed:27022195). Also involved in LPS-
CC       independent inflammatory responses triggered by free fatty acids, such
CC       as palmitate, and Ni(2+) (PubMed:20711192). Mechanistically, acts via
CC       MYD88, TIRAP and TRAF6, leading to NF-kappa-B activation, cytokine
