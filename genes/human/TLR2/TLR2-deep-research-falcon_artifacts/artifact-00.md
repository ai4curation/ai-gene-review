| Receptor complex | Ligand type | Specific examples | Microbial source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TLR2/TLR1 heterodimer** | PAMP: triacylated bacterial lipoproteins/lipopeptides | Pam3CSK4; triacylated lipoproteins; lipoarabinomannan and lipomannan. The third lipid chain occupies a hydrophobic channel in TLR1, providing acylation-state specificity. CD14 can facilitate ligand delivery. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000025) | Gram-negative and Gram-positive bacteria, mycobacteria, and *Mycoplasma* spp. |
| **TLR2/TLR6 heterodimer** | PAMP: diacylated lipoproteins/lipopeptides | Pam2CSK4, FSL-1, and MALP-2; commonly associated with responses to lipoteichoic-acid-containing preparations. TLR6 lacks the TLR1-like channel for a third acyl chain, favoring diacylated ligands. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000029) | Gram-positive bacteria and *Mycoplasma* spp.; FSL-1 is modeled on a *Mycoplasma* lipopeptide. |
| **TLR2/TLR4 heterodimer or receptor association** | PAMP/DAMP: context-dependent and incompletely established | Some atypical, low-endotoxic LPS preparations; hemoglobin and matrix metalloproteinase-2 have also been associated with TLR2/TLR4 signaling. Direct heterodimeric recognition and physiological relevance require further confirmation. (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000026) | Reported for atypical bacterial LPS; hemoglobin and MMP-2 are host-derived danger-associated signals rather than microbial products. |
| **TLR2/TLR10 heterodimer** | PAMP: bacterial surface glycolipids/LPS; specificity remains incompletely defined | *Helicobacter pylori* LPS and related bacterial signals have been linked to TLR2/TLR10-dependent NF-κB activation and cytokine production, but precise ligand-binding rules remain unresolved. (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000026) | Principally reported for *Helicobacter pylori*. |
| **TLR2 homodimer—proposed** | PAMP: bacterial cell-wall material | Peptidoglycan and lipoteichoic acid have been assigned to TLR2 homodimers in some studies. However, purified-material responses can reflect contaminating lipoproteins, and the physiological signaling competence of TLR2 homodimers remains controversial. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000025, pqac-00000027) | Primarily Gram-positive bacterial cell walls. |
| **TLR2 homodimer—proposed** | PAMP: fungal polysaccharides | Zymosan and β-glucans have been associated with TLR2 responses, often through cooperation with Dectin-1 or other C-type lectin receptors rather than direct recognition by an isolated TLR2 homodimer. (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000027) | Fungal and yeast cell walls, including *Candida* and *Saccharomyces* spp. |
| **TLR2 with CLEC2D** | PAMP: fungal β-glucans | β-glucans; CLEC2D/TLR2 heterodimers display greater β-glucan-binding ability than TLR2 homodimers and suppress IRF5-mediated IL-12 production through MyD88 degradation. (pqac-00000027, pqac-00000030) | Fungi, experimentally including *Candida albicans*. |
| **TLR2 with accessory receptors** | PAMP: structurally diverse microbial particles | Zymosan and β-glucans with Dectin-1; selected diacylated ligands with CD36; bacterial lipopeptides presented by CD14. CD14, CD36, CD44, integrins, and lectin receptors can control delivery, avidity, or signaling rather than replace the canonical TLR partner. (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000027) | Bacteria, fungi, and yeasts, depending on the accessory receptor and ligand. |
| **TLR2 complex; partner context-dependent or unresolved** | DAMP: extracellular host proteins and matrix fragments | HMGB1, heat-shock proteins, human β-defensin-3, and hyaluronan fragments. These are proposed endogenous danger signals, but direct binding and exclusion of microbial contaminants are not equally established for every candidate. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000041) | None—released, exposed, or fragmented during host-cell stress, injury, or inflammation. |
| **TLR2 complex; partner context-dependent or unresolved** | DAMP: disease-associated protein aggregates | Amyloid-β and oligomeric α-synuclein, associated with microglial or inflammatory activation in neurodegenerative settings. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000007, pqac-00000041) | None—host-derived aggregates associated with Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s disease biology. |


*Table: TLR2 recognizes molecular patterns rather than catalyzing a substrate reaction; receptor partners and accessory proteins determine much of its specificity. The table distinguishes well-established lipopeptide recognition from proposed or context-dependent PAMP and DAMP assignments.*
