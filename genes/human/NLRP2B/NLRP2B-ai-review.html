Gene Description

NLRP2B (pyrin-only protein 4, POP4; formerly the pseudogene NLRP2P/CLRX.1/NOD24) is a 45-amino-acid protein encoded on chromosome Xp11.21 by a processed retrocopy of an NLRP2/NLRP7-like gene that is restricted to Old World primates. Its open reading frame corresponds to the first 45 codons of NLRP2 and is predicted to fold into only the first two alpha-helices of a pyrin domain (PYD); it is about 80% identical to the N-terminal region of the pyrin-only protein POP2 (PYDC2). The ORF is under purifying selection across hominids, and the mRNA is detected in many tissues and in leukocytes and is induced by lipopolysaccharide in monocytic cells. The endogenous protein has not been detected directly. When expressed ectopically, POP4 is diffusely cytoplasmic and partly nuclear, and moves into the nucleus on TNF stimulation. It inhibits NF-kappaB RelA/p65 transactivation at or downstream of RelA/p65 by reducing RelA/p65 phosphorylation at Ser-536, and thereby lowers TNF and IL-6 release from macrophages stimulated through TLR2 or TLR4; siRNA knockdown in human THP-1 cells increases the release of these cytokines. Unlike POP2, it lacks the acidic residues and helix 4 needed to block ASC-dependent inflammasomes and does not inhibit NLRP3 inflammasome-driven IL-1beta release. In HeLa cells under cycloheximide treatment it slows S/G2 progression and, with TNF, increases apoptosis.

Core Functions

Dampens NF-kappaB RelA/p65-dependent inflammatory gene expression downstream of Toll-like receptor and TNF receptor stimulation, by reducing activating phosphorylation of RelA/p65 at Ser-536 and so limiting RelA/p65 TAD1 transactivation, which lowers TNF and IL-6 production by macrophages. The molecular activity responsible (which kinase or phosphatase is affected, and whether POP4 binds RelA/p65 directly) is unknown, so no molecular function term is assigned. Unlike its paralog POP2, it does not inhibit ASC-dependent inflammasomes.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is the 45-residue POP4 protein made from the endogenous NLRP2B locus? All functional data come from ectopic expression of the ORF, the tagged protein was not detectable by western blot, and HPA reports no protein. Detection of the endogenous peptide (for example by targeted mass spectrometry in LPS-stimulated monocytes) would confirm the product that the annotations describe.

Q: What does POP4 bind to reduce RelA/p65 Ser-536 phosphorylation - RelA/p65 itself, an IKK or other Ser-536 kinase, or a phosphatase? Only an answer to this would allow a molecular function term to be assigned.

Q: Should NLRP2B also carry a general NF-kappaB signalling regulation term (for example GO:0043124 negative regulation of canonical NF-kappaB signal transduction), or are the stimulus-specific TNF and TLR terms the intended representation?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Ribosome profiling and targeted mass spectrometry (or an endogenous epitope knock-in) in LPS-stimulated THP-1 cells or primary monocytes, where NLRP2B mRNA is induced.

Hypothesis: The endogenous NLRP2B ORF is translated in human myeloid cells.

Type: proteomics / ribosome profiling

Experiment: Affinity purification-mass spectrometry of tagged POP4 at near-endogenous levels, followed by in vitro kinase assays with IKKbeta/TBK1 and RelA TAD1 substrate in the presence of recombinant POP4.

Hypothesis: POP4 limits RelA/p65 Ser-536 phosphorylation by binding a specific kinase or RelA/p65 itself.

Type: interaction proteomics and in vitro kinase assay

(GO:0043124) raised as a question instead. Comparator: paralog PYDC2/POP2 (Q56P42) carries
the positive versions of both NOT rows (GO:0032691, GO:1900226), so the NOT rows are informative.
