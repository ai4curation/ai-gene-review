CC       preventing RELA/p65 phosphorylation at 'Ser-536', thereby inhibiting
CC       its transcriptional activity. Through NF-kappa-B regulation may control

CC       a role in modulation of innate immunity. May also play a role in cell
CC       cycle progression and apoptotic process. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24871464}.
