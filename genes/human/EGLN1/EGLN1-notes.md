# EGLN1 evidence notes

EGLN1/PHD2 couples oxygen availability to HIF-alpha stability by hydroxylating HIF-alpha degradation-domain prolines. VHL-dependent ubiquitination and proteasomal turnover occur downstream; EGLN1 performs the hydroxylation step. Human biochemical evidence and human-cell HIF responses support one integrated oxygen-sensing function, with cytosolic and nuclear pools (PMID:11598268; PMID:19604478; PMID:16956324; PMID:19631610). The official UniProt record links pathogenic variants to familial erythrocytosis. A HIF1B wording error in an official FUNCTION sentence is not used as substrate evidence.

The original HPH-2 experiment used AF229245 lacking residues 76–177, whereas later tPHD2 experiments used residues 181–426. Neither is equated with every natural product. The three official proteins are 426, 404 and 325 residues; their IDs and sequence notes remain unchanged. The 404-residue product deletes 338–359, and the 325-residue product replaces 58–175 with a 17-residue segment. Hydroxylation and 2-oxoglutarate turnover are separate readouts; crystal substitutes and engineered variants do not establish all-product equivalence. The original-source and core assessments were independently checked before integration.

The peptide/PDZ survey requires measurement-level interpretation (PMID:36115835). Its complete target selection yields 133 measurements and 11 quantified positive domains. Of 21 imported partners, IL16, DLG3 and WHRN have a positive original-table domain; the other 18 have only censored measurements. Only DLG3 PDZ2 is also the positive domain in the current IntAct construct record. IL16 PDZ4 and WHRN/DFNB31 PDZ1 are independent original-table support, not positive calls for the different recorded domains. Binding intensity, not pKd, exceeds the 0.05 threshold. These three are single DAPF measurements; their pKd estimates use the study's 18 micromolar conversion assumption rather than independent row-level FP values. WHRN's intensity is just above threshold. No native full-length complex, sequenced insert, natural-isoform equivalence or strong affinity is inferred. Censoring is uncertainty, not demonstrated nonbinding.

The interaction refinements identify independently established partner classes: HIF bHLH transcription factors, FKBP8 enzyme, LIMD1 signaling scaffold and CBFA2T3 corepressor. They do not give EGLN1 those partners' activities. Binding outside a catalytic domain does not invalidate enzyme binding. Processed NS5A is distinguished from polyprotein enzyme domains. Supported generic associations without a justified informative replacement remain non-core under the explicit CLINGEN_MENDELIAN task instruction; unavailable or ambiguous operative evidence remains unresolved.

Mouse/rat neuronal experiments support contextual postsynaptic localization, spine remodeling and FLNA turnover (PMID:26972007). They are not endogenous human-neuron assays. Rat cortical HCA stress shows PHD1, not PHD2, knockdown protection (PMID:19587290); dopaminergic mitochondrial-stress experiments support a distinct PHD2-dependent context (PMID:26818499). These conditional results do not establish universal loss of neuronal PHD2 function.

The mouse positive-transcription donor assertion was traced to the original ATP13A2 experiment. PHD2 inhibition or knockdown increases ATP13A2 RNA/protein, opposing the transferred positive-regulator sign (PMID:26818499). qPCR measures steady-state RNA and does not itself establish transcription rate, so no opposite transcription assertion is proposed. Separate measured iron effects remain contextual. Likewise, HIF responses to oxygen do not demonstrate maintenance of intracellular oxygen concentration; the narrow homeostasis assertion is over-annotated. PDE4D association is supported, but direct inhibition versus abundance or hydroxylation-dependent effects remains unresolved (PMID:22975349).

HPA's location-specific score supports cytosol and labels vesicles uncertain. The nuclear pool is independently supported, and source-specific broad cytoplasm statements retain their measured resolution. Five Reactome roots yield nine literal paths to Q9GZT9 in cytosol; these modeled paths do not confer VHL ligase or proteasome activity on EGLN1. PAINT target ancestry, literal IBDs and metal-binding residues are verified. The unavailable ARBA predicate set remains explicitly unresolved while the enzyme reaction is independently established.
