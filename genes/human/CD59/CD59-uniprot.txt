CC   -!- FUNCTION: Potent inhibitor of the complement membrane attack complex
CC       (MAC) action, which protects human cells from damage during complement
CC       activation (PubMed:11882685, PubMed:1698710, PubMed:2475111,
CC       PubMed:2475570, PubMed:2606909, PubMed:9053451). Acts by binding to the
CC       beta-haipins of C8 (C8A and C8B) components of the assembling MAC,
CC       forming an intermolecular beta-sheet that prevents incorporation of the
CC       multiple copies of C9 required for complete formation of the osmolytic
CC       pore (PubMed:11882685, PubMed:1698710, PubMed:36797260).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11882685, ECO:0000269|PubMed:1698710,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2475111, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2475570,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2606909, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36797260,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9053451}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: The soluble form from urine retains its specific complement
CC       binding activity, but exhibits greatly reduced ability to inhibit
CC       complement membrane attack complex (MAC) assembly on cell membranes.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8670172}.

CC   -!- PTM: N- and O-glycosylated. The N-glycosylation mainly consists of a
CC       family of biantennary complex-type structures with and without
CC       lactosamine extensions and outer arm fucose residues. Also significant
CC       amounts of triantennary complexes (22%). Variable sialylation also
CC       present in the Asn-43 oligosaccharide. The predominant O-glycans are
CC       mono-sialylated forms of the disaccharide, Gal-beta-1,3GalNAc, and
CC       their sites of attachment are probably on Thr-76 and Thr-77. The GPI-
CC       anchor of soluble urinary CD59 has no inositol-associated phospholipid,
CC       but is composed of seven different GPI-anchor variants of one or more
CC       monosaccharide units. Major variants contain sialic acid, mannose and
CC       glucosamine. Sialic acid linked to an N-acetylhexosamine-galactose arm
CC       is present in two variants. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18780401,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8670172}.
CC   -!- PTM: Glycated (PubMed:10805801). Glycation is found in diabetic
CC       subjects, but only at minimal levels in nondiabetic subjects. Glycated
CC       CD59 lacks MAC-inhibitory function and confers to vascular
CC       complications of diabetes (PubMed:10805801).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10805801}.
