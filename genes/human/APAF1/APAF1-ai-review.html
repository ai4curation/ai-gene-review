Gene Description

APAF1 is a cytosolic NB-ARC/WD40 scaffold that couples mitochondrial cytochrome c release to initiator caspase activation. Cytochrome c and dATP/ATP binding relieve the autoinhibited ADP-bound APAF1 monomer and promote APAF1 oligomerization into the apoptosome, where APAF1 CARD domains recruit procaspase-9 CARDs and concentrate CASP9 for activation of the intrinsic apoptotic caspase cascade.
