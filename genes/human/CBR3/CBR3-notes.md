# CBR3 review notes

## Family context

CBR3 is the human PTHR43963:SF3 paralog of CBR1. It retains the same
short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase scaffold and a cytosolic NADPH-dependent
carbonyl/quinone reductase activity, but direct biochemical comparisons show a
narrower substrate spectrum than CBR1 and no significant prostaglandin 9-keto
reductase or 15-hydroxyprostaglandin dehydrogenase activity.

`just paint-losses human CBR3` found no IRD or IKR loss on the human CBR3
lineage.

## Evidence notes

- No falcon or perplexity deep-research provider was available while seeding the
  CBR family; the CBR3 review therefore uses the UniProt entry, GOA rows,
  cached primary CBR3 biochemical literature, Reactome and the PTHR43963 PAINT
  cache.
- All CBR3 protein-binding GOA rows in this family are bare GO:0005515 IPI rows
  from high-throughput interactome screens and do not identify a molecular
  function more specific than the policy-excluded root protein-binding term.
- The tear-fluid extracellular-region row is a high-throughput body-fluid
  proteomics hit and is peripheral to the directly observed cytosolic
  localization and cytosolic reductase activity.
- CBR3 V244M assays used menadione to measure allele-dependent quinone
  reduction. Later CBR3 work showed low or no menadione activity, so menadione
  is sufficient for the molecular quinone-reductase activity but not for
  physiological vitamin K or phylloquinone catabolism.
- The Alzheimer disease copy-number-variation paper reports human genetic
  association data. It does not test CBR3 protein activity in cognition.
