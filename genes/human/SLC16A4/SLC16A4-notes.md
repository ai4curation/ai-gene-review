# SLC16A4 (MCT5) curation notes

- UniProt O15374, "Probable monocarboxylate transporter" [file:human/SLC16A4/SLC16A4-uniprot.txt CC:FUNCTION]. Historical names MCT5/MCT4; HGNC SLC16A4.
- Member of the SLC16/MCT family (MFS, monocarboxylate porter TC 2.A.1.13), 12-TM topology, cloned by sequence homology by Price, Jackson & Halestrap 1998 [PMID:9425115 "the cloning and sequencing of four new full-length MCT-like sequences from human cDNA libraries, which we have denoted MCT3, MCT4, MCT5 and MCT6"].
- Orphan transporter: no demonstrated substrate. Felmlee et al. 2020 SLC16 review classes MCT5 among the orphans [PMID:32144120 "MCT5, 13, and 14 still have no known substrates and therefore remain orphan transporters"].
