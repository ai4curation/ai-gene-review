CC   -!- FUNCTION: Associates with and recruits the catalytic subunit of the
CC       calcineurin complex (PPP3CA/Calcineurin A) to the plasma membrane,
CC       where it functions as a dynamic scaffold and regulator of PPP3CA
CC       activity thereby modulating calcineurin signaling (PubMed:41224739). In
CC       brown adipocytes, regulates thermogenesis by binding to PPP3CA,
CC       promoting the dephosphorylation of the PKA regulatory subunit, PRKAR2B.
CC       This modulation of PRKAR2B phosphorylation enhances PKA activity,
CC       thereby stimulating heat thermogenesis (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8K1L6, ECO:0000269|PubMed:41224739}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via PxIxIT motif), when phosphorylated on Thr-44
CC       with PPP3CA; leading to enhanced recruitment of PPP3CA to the plasma
CC       membrane and modulation of calcineurin signaling.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28881575, ECO:0000269|PubMed:41224739}.

CC   -!- DOMAIN: Contains a combination of two SLiMs (short linear interaction
CC       motifs) motifs, LxVP and PxIxIT, forming the composite LxVPxIxIT motif.
CC       SLiMs motifs are small interaction modules within intrinsically
CC       disordered regions of proteins that interact with specific domains.
CC       This arrangement facilitates dephosphorylation of CLMB through a
CC       multimeric mechanism in which one CLMB recruits PPP3CA via PxIxIT
CC       binding to enable dephosphorylation of other CLMB molecules via their
CC       LxVP motif. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:41224739}.
CC   -!- PTM: N-myristoylation, in combination with reversible S-acylation
CC       promote the association of CLMB with membranes.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:41224739}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated. Phosphorylation and dephosphorylation of CLMB
CC       regulate its interaction with PPP3CA, controlling its recruitment to
CC       the plasma membrane and modulate calcineurin signaling. Phosphorylation
CC       at Thr-44 increases CLMB binding to PPP3CA. Dephosphorylated by PPP3CA
CC       requires two CLMB molecules per PPP3CA allowing one CLMB to bind PPP3CA
CC       via its PxIxIT and the other CLMB to undergo LxVP-dependent
CC       dephosphorylation. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:41224739}.
