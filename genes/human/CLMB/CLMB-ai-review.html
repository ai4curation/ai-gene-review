Gene Description

Calcimembrin (CLMB; formerly C16orf74, also MICT1) is a 76-aa intrinsically disordered microprotein of the DUF4597 family that lacks transmembrane segments and associates with the cytoplasmic face of the plasma membrane through N-myristoylation of Gly2 and reversible S-acylation of Cys7 and Cys14. It carries a composite LxVPxIxIT calcineurin-docking motif (33-LDVPDIIIT-41) whose PxIxIT part, when Thr44 is phosphorylated, binds the catalytic subunit of calcineurin (PPP3CA) with very high affinity, recruiting calcineurin to membranes. CLMB is itself dephosphorylated by calcineurin through its LxVP motif in multimeric complexes, so the CLMB:calcineurin ratio, CLMB phosphorylation and dynamic acylation tune membrane calcineurin signalling. It is highly expressed in brown and beige adipocytes and induced by cold; there it limits calcineurin-mediated dephosphorylation of the PKA regulatory subunit RIIbeta, prolonging beta3-adrenergic PKA signalling and promoting thermogenesis, as shown in mouse brown adipose tissue and human beige adipocytes. CLMB is overexpressed in pancreatic and other carcinomas, where high expression is associated with invasion and poor prognosis.

Core Functions

Lipid-anchored calcineurin docking protein: via its phospho-regulated composite LxVPxIxIT motif CLMB binds the calcineurin catalytic subunit PPP3CA with high affinity and, through N-myristoylation and dynamic S-acylation, tethers calcineurin to the plasma membrane, shaping membrane calcineurin signalling (CLMB is itself a calcineurin substrate).

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is CLMB net-inhibitory to calcineurin (sequestering it from RIIbeta, as proposed in brown adipocytes, PMID:40355556) or a membrane-targeting scaffold that promotes dephosphorylation of LxVP-only membrane substrates (PMID:41224739)? The answer decides between protein phosphatase inhibitor activity and an adaptor/regulator MF.

Q: Should the thermogenesis annotations use positive regulation of cold-induced thermogenesis (GO:0120162) rather than adaptive thermogenesis, given that CLMB modulates beta3-adrenergic PKA signalling rather than producing heat itself?

Q: Is the UNC119 interaction (repeated Y2H hits) a physiological route for transporting myristoylated CLMB between membranes, analogous to other UNC119 cargo?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: In human brown/beige adipocytes, replace endogenous CLMB with wild-type, G2A (non-myristoylated), IxIT-mutant or T44A CLMB and measure membrane calcineurin, RIIbeta phospho-Ser, PKA activity and UCP1-dependent respiration after beta3-agonist stimulation.

Hypothesis: CLMB-dependent membrane recruitment of calcineurin alters RIIbeta phosphorylation in human brown adipocytes.

Type: genetic rescue with structure-function mutants

CLMB (calcimembrin; C16orf74; MICT1) - curation notes

Identity

  • UniProt Q96GX8, HGNC:23362, symbol CLMB; synonyms C16orf74, MICT1. 76 aa canonical
    isoform (V1); isoform 2 (V3) lacks residues 1-12 (and thus the myristoylation site).
  • Canonical, annotated ORF (not an alt-ORF peptide), so the standard folder convention applies.
  • Family: DUF4597 (Pfam PF15366, InterPro IPR027864), PANTHER PTHR37455. No TM segment;
    membrane association via lipidation (N-myristoyl Gly2, S-palmitoyl Cys7/Cys14).
  • Mouse ortholog Q8K1L6 (1190005I06Rik / Mict1).

Most hits are cancer prognostic / bioinformatic signature papers. Mechanistic papers:

    Lipidation: PMID:41224739

    adipocytes (adipose MSC derived): PMID:40355556
    Human MICT1 in plasma membrane fraction of HEK293: PMID:40355556
    Mechanism: MICT1 binds PP2B via PxIxIT, limiting PP2B-RIIb interaction / RIIb dephosphorylation,
    prolonging PKA activity. G2A (non-myristoylated) MICT1 is inactive.

Interpretation

  • MF: best described as a calcineurin (PP2B) docking/anchoring protein: binds CNA via a
    phospho-regulated high-affinity PxIxIT, and as a lipid-anchored protein targets calcineurin to
    membranes -> protein phosphatase 2B binding (GO:0030346) plus protein-membrane adaptor activity
    (GO:0043495). It is also a calcineurin substrate (no GO MF for "being a substrate").
    Whether its net effect is inhibitory (sequestering CN from RIIb; Sul) or a membrane-targeting
    scaffold that can promote dephosphorylation of LxVP-only membrane substrates (Cyert) is
    context-dependent; I did not assert protein phosphatase inhibitor/regulator activity.
  • UNC119 Y2H hits (3 high-throughput screens): UNC119 is a carrier for N-myristoylated cargo,
    so this is plausibly a genuine lipid-cargo interaction, but it says nothing about CLMB's own
    function; bare protein binding -> REMOVE per project policy.
  • BP adaptive thermogenesis: supported in human beige adipocytes (OE) and mouse KO; the role is
    modulatory (PKA signalling tuning). Accepted as annotated; regulation term
    (GO:0120162 positive regulation of cold-induced thermogenesis) raised as a question.
  • Cancer roles (PDAC invasion, prognostic marker) are not GO-annotatable functions.
