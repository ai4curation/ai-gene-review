Gene Description

FASLG (Fas ligand, CD95L, TNFSF6) is a type II transmembrane TNF-family cytokine. Its trimeric extracellular TNF-homology domain binds the FAS/CD95 death receptor on neighboring cells to assemble a FADD- and initiator-caspase-containing DISC and trigger extrinsic apoptotic signaling during immune homeostasis, cytotoxic lymphocyte killing, activation-induced T-cell death, and peripheral tolerance. The membrane precursor traffics through secretory lysosomes to the cell surface, can be shed to a soluble receptor-binding ectodomain, and can be further cleaved by ADAM10 and SPPL2A to liberate a nuclear FasL intracellular domain with transcriptional effects.
