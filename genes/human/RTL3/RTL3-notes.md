# RTL3 (Q8N8U3) notes

Domesticated Ty3/gypsy sushi-ichi retrotransposon Gag gene; Gag CA-like domain + C-terminal CCHC zinc knuckle (res 443-462); no RT/integrase; in human the -1-frame protease ORF does not overlap gag so the annotated protein is non-catalytic [PMID:38507667 "in human RTL3, a −1 frameshift would not be sufficient to produce a fusion protein, because the stop-free regions containing the two domains do not overlap"].
Family defined in [PMID:16093683 "three Mar proteins have conserved the Gag CCHC zinc finger motif, suggesting a role in nucleic acid binding"].
Primary function: nuclear gene-regulatory factor in chondrocytes; required for SOX9/COL2A1 and matrix synthesis; activity needs the CCHC knuckle [PMID:33043724 "Gene regulatory activity of RTL3 requires the c-terminal CCHC zinc-finger binding domain"; "ATDC5Rlt3−/− chondrocytes demonstrated a noticeable reduction in matrix deposition"]. Reduced in osteoarthritic cartilage.
Mouse ortholog Zcchc5 nuclear CCHC protein, restrains muscle satellite-cell self-renewal [PMID:27446912 "ZFP354C and ZCCHC5 decreased self-renewal capacity when overexpressed"].
HPA IF: nucleoplasm (main). Direct DNA/RNA targets of human protein untested.
Calls: nucleic acid binding ACCEPT; zinc ion binding ACCEPT; nucleus ACCEPT (IEA+ISS); NEW positive regulation of chondrocyte differentiation (GO:0032332) and positive regulation of transcription by RNA Pol II (GO:0045944).
