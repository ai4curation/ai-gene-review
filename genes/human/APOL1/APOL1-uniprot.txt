CC   -!- FUNCTION: May play a role in lipid exchange and transport throughout
CC       the body. May participate in reverse cholesterol transport from
CC       peripheral cells to the liver. A component of trypanosome lytic factor
CC       of human serum; plays a crucial role in killing Trypanosoma brucei by
CC       forming pores in parasite lysosomal membranes and sensitizing T.brucei
CC       to oxidation-stimulated osmotic lysis (PubMed:12621437,
CC       PubMed:16020735, PubMed:19997494, PubMed:26645690, PubMed:7723792).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12621437, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16020735,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19997494, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26645690,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7723792}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: In plasma, interacts with APOA1 and mainly associated with
CC       large high density lipoprotein particles.

CC       resistance-associated protein from Trypanosoma brucei rhodesiense; the
CC       interaction results in inactivation of APOL1 pore-forming activity and
CC       T.brucei rhodesiense resistance to lysis by normal human serum.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12621437, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19997494}.
