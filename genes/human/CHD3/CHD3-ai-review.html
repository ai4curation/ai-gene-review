Gene Description

CHD3 (Mi-2alpha) is an ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler that changes nucleosome positioning and DNA accessibility. It forms CHD3-containing NuRD assemblies and contributes remodeling activity alongside the histone-deacetylase subunits. Its tandem PHD fingers recognize histone H3 tails, with sensitivity to their modification state. CHD3 acts mainly on nuclear chromatin and can support either transcriptional repression or activation in a target- and cell-dependent manner. Human stem-cell studies link CHD3-dependent chromatin accessibility to cranial neural crest specification. A smaller centrosomal pool associates with pericentrin and contributes to centrosomal protein retention and spindle organization. Pathogenic CHD3 variants can alter ATPase activity or its coupling to remodeling and cause a neurodevelopmental disorder with prominent speech and language impairment.
