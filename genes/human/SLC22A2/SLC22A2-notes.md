# SLC22A2 (OCT2 / hOCT2) — curation notes

UniProt: O15244 (S22A2_HUMAN), 555 aa, HGNC:10966. Gene synonym OCT2.

## Summary of function

SLC22A2 encodes **organic cation transporter 2 (OCT2)**, a 12-transmembrane-domain
member of the SLC22 family within the major facilitator superfamily
[UniProtKB:O15244 CC:SIMILARITY, "Belongs to the major facilitator (TC 2.A.1) superfamily. Organic cation transporter (TC 2.A.1.19) family."].
It is an **electrogenic, Na+-independent, membrane-potential-driven, bidirectional
(facilitated) polyspecific organic-cation uniporter** — NOT a coupled symporter
[UniProtKB:O15244 CC:FUNCTION, "Electrogenic voltage-dependent uniporter that mediates the transport of a variety of organic cations such as endogenous bioactive amines, cationic drugs and xenobiotics"; "Functions as a Na(+)-independent, bidirectional uniporter"; "Cation cellular uptake or release is driven by the electrochemical potential, i.e. membrane potential and concentration gradient"].
Cloning/characterisation showed "cation transport by hOCT2 was pH independent,
electrogenic, and polyspecific" [PMID:9260930].

## Physiological roles

**1. Renal basolateral organic-cation uptake (primary role).** OCT2 is
predominantly expressed on the **basolateral membrane of the renal proximal
tubule**, where it carries out the **first step of tubular secretion of organic
cations** from blood into the tubular cell
[PMID:12395288 "The electrogenic cation transporters OCT1 and OCT2 in the basolateral membrane of renal proximal tubules mediate the first step during secretion of organic cations"; PMID:9260930 "hOCT2 mediates the first step in cation reabsorption"; UniProtKB:O15244 CC:FUNCTION "Predominantly expressed at the basolateral membrane of hepatocytes and proximal tubules and involved in the uptake and disposition of cationic compounds by hepatic and renal clearance from the blood flow"]. Endogenous
renal substrates include **creatinine** (clinically important — basis of
drug–creatinine interactions) [PMID:15212162 "only hOCT2 stimulated creatinine uptake when expressed in HEK293 cells"; "hOCT2, but not hOCT1, is responsible for the basolateral membrane transport of creatinine in the human kidney"], N-1-methylnicotinamide
(NMN), TEA and MPP+ [PMID:9260930 "hOCT1 and hOCT2 mediate tracer influx of N-1-methylnicotinamide (NMN), tetraethylammonium (TEA), and 1-methyl-4-phenylpyridinium (MPP)"].
UniProt CAUTION notes that most studies place it basolateral in proximal tubule,
with some evidence for luminal/apical localisation in the distal tubule.

**2. CNS monoamine handling.** OCT2 is expressed in neurons of the cerebral
cortex, subcortical nuclei and dopaminergic substantia nigra, where it mediates
low-affinity, Na+-independent **interstitial/extraneuronal uptake of monoamine
neurotransmitters** (dopamine, noradrenaline, serotonin, histamine, adrenaline,
tyramine) [PMID:9687576 "electrogenic transport of norepinephrine, histamine, dopamine, serotonin, and the antiparkinsonian drugs memantine and amantadine was demonstrated"; "hOCT2 plays a physiological role in the central nervous system by regulating interstitial concentrations of monoamine neurotransmitters"; PMID:16581093 "OCT2 and OCT3 subtypes share a similar monoamine preference profile, with higher transport efficacies for epinephrine and histamine than for the other monoamines"]. It is NOT a presynaptic high-affinity
reuptake transporter (that is DAT/NET/SERT); OCT2 is the low-affinity
extraneuronal/"uptake-2"-type system. The dopaminergic neuromodulators
cyclo(his-pro) and salsolinol are "endogenous key substrates" linked to
dopaminergic cell integrity [PMID:17460754 "salsolinol were the endogenous key substrates of the sodium-independent organic cation transporter OCT2"].

**3. Other substrates (polyspecific).** Choline (low affinity, Km ~26 mM in the
newest systematic study) [PMID:40972734 "SLC22A1-3 were confirmed as choline transporters, albeit with low affinity"]; thiamine/vitamin B1 [PMID:24961373]; polyamines agmatine/putrescine (bidirectional, pH-dependent) [PMID:21128598 "OCT2 and MATE1 provide bidirectional agmatine transport"; "Putrescine accumulation was pH- and concentration-dependent in hOCT2-HEK cells (K(m) = 11.29 ± 4.26 mM) but not hOCT1-HEK cells"]; acetylcholine at the apical membrane of airway ciliated cells (luminal ACh release) [PMID:15817714 "ACh is transported by OCT1 and OCT2 but not by OCT3"; "OCT2 mediates electrogenic uptake and efflux of ACh"]; guanidine [PMID:12089365]; and the non-amine organic anions prostaglandin E2 / F2α (high affinity, PGE2 Km 28.9 nM) [PMID:11907186 "The K(m) values of PGE(2) uptake by hOAT1, hOAT2, hOAT3, hOAT4, hOCT1, and hOCT2 were 970, 713, 345, 154, 657, and 28.9 nM, respectively"].

## Pharmacology (major pharmacogene)

OCT2 mediates renal secretion of many cationic drugs: metformin, cisplatin and
oxaliplatin (OCT2-mediated proximal-tubule uptake underlies **cisplatin
nephrotoxicity** — a downstream consequence of transport, not a function OCT2
performs), amantadine, memantine, cimetidine, famotidine, ranitidine, TEA, and
nucleoside/antiviral RTI drugs (lamivudine, abacavir, AZT) [PMID:19141712]. ADMA
and L-arginine are low-affinity, high-capacity substrates (L-arginine Km >10 mM,
i.e. physiologically marginal; dedicated cationic-amino-acid transporters CAT2A/2B
do this job) [PMID:23864433 "ADMA and L-arginine are substrates of human CAT2A, CAT2B, OCT2 and MATE1"].

## Localisation beyond kidney

Basal membrane of Sertoli cells / blood–testis barrier (peritubular myoid and
Leydig cells) [PMID:35307651 "localized to PMCs and LCs with minor positive staining at the basal membrane of SCs"]; luminal side of brain microvessel
endothelium (blood–brain barrier, MPTP transport) [PMID:20477935 "both OCT1 and OCT2 were mainly expressed on the luminal side of brain microvessel endothelial cells"]; airway ciliated-cell apical membrane; detected in urinary exosomes by proteomics
(PMID:19056867 — expected shedding of a renal plasma-membrane protein, not a
functional location).

## Regulation

Tyrosine phosphorylation (YES1 kinase, proline-rich PESPR motif) activates
transport; inhibited by cGMP [PMID:12395288]; a candidate interactor IRIP/YciO
(PMID:16024787) inhibits OCT2 activity.
