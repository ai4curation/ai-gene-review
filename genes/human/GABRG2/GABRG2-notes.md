## 2026-11-02 — Private whole-gene synthesis

Human gamma2 contributes to heteromeric GABA-A receptor pore and allosteric architecture. Human alpha/beta/gamma2 structures and functional currents support the core channel role; orthosteric GABA recognition is principally at beta/alpha interfaces, while the alpha/gamma interface supports benzodiazepine modulation. Engineered loops, purification tags and chimeric assay components remain distinct from natural splice products ([PMID29950725](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29950725/), [PMID30602789](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30602789/)).

Direct official sequences resolve long 475, short 467 and inserted-segment 515 products. PMID30602789 Methods explicitly uses the long P18507-2 backbone; a reference metadata range ending467 is not evidence that it used the short splice form. Selected native human-brain results identify gamma2 in major receptor assemblies, while a separate auxiliary map retains a gamma-x assignment. Mass spectrometry detects short and long forms without resolving the splice product of each map ([PMID39843743](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39843743/)).

Postsynaptic clustering and receptor trafficking are supported with assay-specific limits. Human receptor-containing cells form functional contacts in the reduced co-culture system, but gamma2 alone is not sufficient and the JoVE protocol reuses earlier material ([PMID23909897](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23909897/), [PMID25489750](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25489750/)). Selected original neurexin Results explicitly place gamma2-containing clusters on dendritic surfaces, with MAP2 and live antibody labeling; the inspected Methods describe E18 rat hippocampal cultures. This supports location without assigning the neurexin/neuroligin synaptogenic step to gamma2 ([PMID15620359](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15620359/)).

Gamma2 association with KIF21B and transport-vesicle delivery supports a non-core trafficking pool, with full-method and imaging limits retained ([PMID25172774](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25172774/)). Rat gamma2 immunogold localization includes synaptic and lower-density extrasynaptic membrane, so gamma2 is not confined to synapses ([PMID9464994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9464994/)). Histamine modulation depends on heteromer composition and differs from direct histamine gating of beta homomers; no gamma2 histamine-binding site is asserted ([PMID18281286](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18281286/)).

One human GABARAPL1 partner assertion remains unresolved: the original accessible abstract concerns GABARAP, and later mouse GABARAPL1/gamma2 experiments do not establish the original human context ([PMID9892355](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9892355/), [PMID33436612](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33436612/)).
