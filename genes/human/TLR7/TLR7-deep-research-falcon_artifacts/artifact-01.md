| Component/Protein | Type | Role in pathway |
|---|---|---|
| TLR7 activation | Endosomal pattern-recognition receptor | Processed ssRNA fragments and guanosine occupy complementary ectodomain sites, promoting TLR7 homodimerization and juxtaposition of cytosolic TIR domains. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000048, pqac-00000051) |
| MyD88 | TIR-domain adaptor | Principal adaptor recruited to activated TLR7; nucleates assembly of the Myddosome and recruits IRAK-family kinases. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000018) |
| TIRAP / TRAM | Bridging or accessory adaptors | TIRAP has been reported to facilitate TLR7–MyD88 signaling and IRF7 phosphorylation. Evidence also implicates TRAM in a parallel interferon-producing branch, although canonical TLR7 signaling is MyD88-dependent and the generality of TRAM involvement remains less established. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000020) |
| IRAK4 | Serine/threonine kinase | Recruited into the Myddosome; phosphorylates and activates downstream IRAK proteins, especially IRAK1. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000022) |
| IRAK1 | Serine/threonine kinase | Becomes activated within the Myddosome, dissociates, and engages TRAF6; it also contributes to the IRF7-dependent interferon branch. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000018) |
| TRAF6 | Adaptor and E3 ubiquitin ligase | Builds non-degradative K63-linked ubiquitin signaling scaffolds that promote TAK1 activation; also participates in IRF7 activation. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017) |
| TAK1 with TAB1/2/3 | MAP3 kinase complex | Integrates upstream ubiquitin-dependent signals and activates the IKK–NF-κB and MAPK–AP-1 inflammatory branches. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000020) |
| IKK complex | Kinase complex | Phosphorylates IκB proteins, causing their degradation and releasing NF-κB for nuclear translocation. IKK-family activity also contributes to interferon-regulatory signaling. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017) |
| NF-κB | Transcription factor | Induces inflammatory-response genes, including cytokines and chemokines, after translocating to the nucleus. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000018) |
| IRF7 | Interferon-regulatory transcription factor | Major TLR7-linked interferon regulator, particularly in plasmacytoid dendritic cells; phosphorylation enables nuclear entry and induction of type I interferon genes. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018) |
| Type I interferons | Downstream output | IFN-α and IFN-β establish antiviral programs and amplify innate and adaptive immunity; excessive production can reinforce autoimmune inflammation. (pqac-00000057, pqac-00000061) |
| Pro-inflammatory cytokines and chemokines | Downstream output | NF-κB and MAPK/AP-1 signaling induces inflammatory mediators that recruit and activate immune cells; dysregulated output contributes to inflammatory pathology. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000030) |


*Table: This table traces signaling from endosomal TLR7 activation through Myddosome-associated kinases and transcription factors to type I interferon and inflammatory outputs. It also distinguishes the canonical MyD88 pathway from less-established accessory-adaptor contributions.*
