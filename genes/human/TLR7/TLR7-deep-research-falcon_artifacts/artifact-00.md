| Domain/Region | Location | Function/Description |
|---|---|---|
| Signal peptide / precursor segment | Extreme N terminus; directs the nascent protein into the secretory pathway | Targets TLR7 to the endoplasmic reticulum (ER), where the receptor enters the intracellular trafficking pathway leading to endosomes and endolysosomes. The mature receptor is therefore positioned primarily in intracellular membranes rather than at the plasma membrane. (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000012) |
| N-terminal ectodomain (ECD) | Endosomal/endolysosomal lumen; N-terminal to the transmembrane helix | Large ligand-sensing region composed predominantly of leucine-rich repeats. It binds RNA-derived products and synthetic agonists, and ligand occupancy promotes formation of the signaling-competent TLR7 homodimer. (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000033) |
| Leucine-rich repeat array | Throughout the ectodomain; approximately 26 LRRs forming a curved, horseshoe-like scaffold | Generates a large parallel β-sheet on the concave face and α-helical elements on the convex face. This architecture provides the surfaces and pockets used for nucleoside, oligoribonucleotide, and small-molecule recognition. It aligns with the UniProt/InterPro annotations for typical LRRs and the LRR-domain superfamily. (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000033, pqac-00000034) |
| Z-loop / insertion region | Ectodomain between LRR14 and LRR15 | Flexible regulatory segment that must undergo endolysosomal proteolytic cleavage for efficient RNA recognition and receptor activation. Failure to cleave this region prevents productive dimerization and microbial-RNA sensing. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000033) |
| Proteolytic cleavage site and mature ectodomain fragments | Within the Z-loop in acidic endolysosomal compartments | Cleavage yields N- and C-terminal ectodomain fragments that remain associated as a functional receptor. This compartment-dependent maturation helps restrict signaling to endolysosomes and limits inappropriate recognition of self RNA elsewhere in the cell. (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000053) |
| Cys-rich flanking region / intramolecular disulfide linkage | Ectodomain, including cysteines flanking the processed regions | After Z-loop cleavage, the ectodomain fragments remain linked by a disulfide bond reported between Cys98 and Cys475. This preserves the mature ligand-binding architecture and corresponds to the cysteine-rich flanking-region annotation supplied for Q9NYK1. (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000033) |
| Primary ligand-binding site (site 1) | Pocket at the interface of two ectodomains | Recognizes guanosine and guanosine-like small molecules; synthetic heterocyclic agonists can occupy the same general interface. Binding contributes directly to dimer stabilization and receptor activation. Important modeled contacts include aromatic/hydrophobic residues and hydrogen bonding with residues such as Asp555 and Thr586. (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000034, pqac-00000051) |
| RNA-binding site (site 2) | Concave surface of the ectodomain, spatially distinct from the nucleoside/small-molecule pocket | Binds short uridine-containing ssRNA fragments. At least a short U-rich oligoribonucleotide—described as a three-nucleotide segment containing a UU motif—cooperates with guanosine occupancy at site 1, providing dual-ligand control of activation rather than recognition of one long, invariant RNA sequence. (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000048, pqac-00000051) |
| Ligand-induced homodimer interface | Formed between two ectodomains after ligand engagement | Produces an M-shaped symmetrical homodimer. Agonist binding reduces the separation between membrane-proximal C termini from roughly 53 Å to roughly 23 Å, bringing the cytoplasmic signaling regions close enough to initiate downstream signaling; antagonists can instead stabilize an inactive open conformation. (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000034, pqac-00000039) |
| Single-pass transmembrane helix | Spans the endosomal membrane between the luminal ectodomain and cytosolic tail | Anchors TLR7 in intracellular membranes and mechanically couples luminal ligand-induced dimerization to juxtaposition of the cytosolic TIR domains. (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000036) |
| C-terminal Toll/interleukin-1 receptor (TIR) domain | Cytosolic C terminus | Conserved signaling domain that recruits TIR-containing adaptor proteins—principally MyD88—after receptor dimerization. This initiates Myddosome assembly and signaling through IRAK4/IRAK1, TRAF6 and TAK1 toward NF-κB, MAPK/AP-1 and IRF7-dependent inflammatory and type-I-interferon responses. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000032) |


*Table: This table maps the principal structural regions of human TLR7 (UniProt Q9NYK1) to their intracellular positions and mechanistic roles. It highlights endolysosomal maturation, dual-site RNA-product recognition, ligand-induced homodimerization, membrane anchoring, and TIR-domain signal initiation.*
