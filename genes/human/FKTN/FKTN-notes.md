The core function is the first ribitol-phosphate transfer onto GalNAc C3 of the core M3 O-mannosyl glycan on alpha-dystroglycan. FKTN performs this transfer itself, before the FKRP and RXYLT1 steps; it is not merely required for a downstream phenotype. Original NMR/biochemistry and human loss/rescue support the chemistry [PMID:26923585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26923585/), with independent donor-transfer evidence [PMID:27194101](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27194101/). GO:0016780 accurately describes substituted-phosphate transfer. The polymer-specific GO:0047356 was examined but not guessed as a replacement; the specificity question remains explicit. The normal substrate is phosphorylated core M3, while separate GalNAc–GlcNAc–pNp NMR acceptors lack mannose phosphate. No universal artificial-acceptor phosphate requirement is asserted.
