CC       for tyrosine-kinase-based signaling related to cell adhesion
CC       (PubMed:24574519). As a focal adhesion protein, plays a role in
CC       embryonic fibroblast migration (By similarity). May play an important
CC       role in integrin beta-1 or B cell antigen receptor (BCR) mediated
CC       signaling in B- and T-cells. Integrin beta-1 stimulation leads to
CC       recruitment of various proteins including CRKL and SHPTP2 to the
CC       tyrosine phosphorylated form (PubMed:9020138). Promotes adhesion and
CC       migration of lymphocytes; as a result required for the correct
CC       migration of lymphocytes to the spleen and other secondary lymphoid
CC       organs (PubMed:17174122). Plays a role in the organization of T-cell F-
CC       actin cortical cytoskeleton and the centralization of T-cell receptor
CC       microclusters at the immunological synapse (By similarity). Negatively
CC       regulates cilia outgrowth in polarized cysts (By similarity). Modulates
CC       cilia disassembly via activation of AURKA-mediated phosphorylation of
CC       HDAC6 and subsequent deacetylation of alpha-tubulin (PubMed:17604723).
CC       Positively regulates RANKL-induced osteoclastogenesis (By similarity).
CC       Required for the maintenance of hippocampal dendritic spines in the
CC       dentate gyrus and CA1 regions, thereby involved in spatial learning and
CC       memory (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A8I3PDQ1,
