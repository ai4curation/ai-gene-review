CC   -!- FUNCTION: Pore-forming subunit of the cone cyclic nucleotide-gated
CC       channel. Mediates cone photoresponses at bright light converting
CC       transient changes in intracellular cGMP levels into electrical signals.
CC       In the dark, cGMP levels are high and keep the channel open enabling a
CC       steady inward current carried by Na(+) and Ca(2+) ions that leads to
CC       membrane depolarization and neurotransmitter release from synaptic
CC       terminals. Upon photon absorption cGMP levels decline leading to
CC       channel closure and membrane hyperpolarization that ultimately slows
CC       neurotransmitter release and signals the presence of light, the end
CC       point of the phototransduction cascade. Conducts cGMP- and cAMP-gated
CC       ion currents, with permeability for monovalent and divalent cations.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10888875, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12815043,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34969976, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37463923}.

CC       helices and a beta-roll of eight beta-strands from CNGA3 and CNGB3

CC   -!- DOMAIN: The ion conduction pathway consists of S5, S6 and pore helices
CC       from CNGA3 and CNGB3 subunits. It contains a central hydrophobic gate
CC       that opens upon cNMP binding. CNGB1 displays an additional charged
CC       arginine gate below the central gate to regulate ion permeation.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34969976}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Stargardt disease 1 (STGD1) [MIM:248200]: An autosomal
CC       recessive form of Stargardt disease, a retinal degenerative disease
CC       characterized by macular dystrophy, progressive bilateral atrophy of
CC       the foveal retinal pigment epithelium, and accumulation of fluorescent
CC       flecks around the macula and/or in the central and near-peripheral
CC       areas of the retina. STGD1 patients typically lose central vision in
CC       their first or second decade of life. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15712225}.
CC       Note=The disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented
CC       in this entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Achromatopsia 3 (ACHM3) [MIM:262300]: An autosomal recessive,
CC       ocular stationary disorder due to the absence of functioning cone
CC       photoreceptors in the retina. It is characterized by total
CC       colorblindness, low visual acuity, photophobia and nystagmus.
CC       Achromatopsia type 3 patients manifest severe myopia.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10888875, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10958649,
