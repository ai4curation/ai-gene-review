# PAK3 notes

Deep research could not be generated in this checkout: Falcon and Perplexity providers were unavailable from `deep-research-client`, and the Claude Code provider was not logged in. Manual review therefore used the cached UniProt record, GOA rows, Reactome records, PAINT slice, and cached primary publications.

PAK3 is the neuron-enriched human group I PAK. Its direct experimental evidence in the GOA set is smaller than PAK1/PAK2, but it shares CDC42/RAC-regulated Ser/Thr kinase activity and the group I PAK phosphorylation of MAPK6/MAPK4, and review/ortholog evidence supports PAK3 roles in neuronal morphogenesis and synapse organization.

- PMID:21177870 showed that group I PAKs, including PAK3, phosphorylate ERK3/MAPK6 and ERK4/MAPK4 on their activation-loop serines and activate MK5 signaling.
- PMID:19036346 reviewed evidence that PAK1, PAK2 and PAK3 are expressed in brain and regulate neuronal fate, axon guidance, neuronal polarization, neuronal migration and synaptic plasticity.
