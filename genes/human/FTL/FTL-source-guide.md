# FTL source guide

This guide distinguishes actual selected original text or target tables from curated interaction records, abstracts and bibliography-only sources. It now accompanies genuine native acquisition and exact reconciliation of 73 original contexts, all 16 TSV columns and 73 native seed objects. The accepted scientific assessment has been applied to the genuine seed; validation and publication status are recorded separately in the notes. Normal cache availability must not be confused with access to separately retained originals. No new quotations were added.

## Core and source interpretation

Human L-chain shells and H/L heteropolymers support iron incorporation, mineralization and storage. Human deficiency/rescue demonstrates composition-dependent homeostasis. These are distinct from rapid catalytic oxidation at H-chain ferroxidase centers. Ferritin is autophagic cargo; receptor association, turnover and cargo necessity do not automatically make FTL autophagy machinery. The observed 2014, 2015 and 2019 variant effects remain study-specific.

The retained PTHR11431 tree places the exact 175-aa human sequence at AN31/PTN002478947. All three incoming IBA terms join positive AN0/PTN000153092 IBDs with matching descendants. A target appearing in the cytoplasm donor set is valid grounding. Cytoplasm is more general than the retained cytosol location via part_of; this scope decision does not reject its node placement.
