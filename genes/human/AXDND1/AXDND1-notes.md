# AXDND1 notes

## 2026-10-05 review (PAINT, affinage)

- Two independent mouse knockouts are male-sterile (PMID:34759295, PMID:35386379). A third (PMID:38997255) adds a spermatogonial-balance defect. Human MMAF frameshift: [PMID:40457935 "These findings indicate that AXDND1 is essential for the organization of flagellar axoneme and provide direct evidence that AXDND1 is a MMAF gene in humans, thus expanding the phenotypic spectrum of AXDND1 frameshift mutations."]
- Manchette: it forms in knockouts and then becomes abnormal [PMID:34759295 "In the early elongating spermatids (step 8–9), we observed a discernable manchette structure formation in both WT and Axdnd1−/− mice (Fig. 6I)."]. GO:1905198 manchette assembly is kept as non-core because no manchette-organization term exists. The localization data conflict: PMID:38997255 saw manchette microtubules, while PMID:35386379 saw no colocalization. No NEW manchette CC is added.
- NEW sperm flagellum (IDA, PMID:40457935). The signal is in control flagella and absent in the frameshift patient.
- The cytosol row is an HPA cell-line call and is kept as non-core. All four cytoplasm rows are accepted.
