## 24 October 2026: catalytic role and outstanding mechanisms

Glutathione persulfide is the chemically transformed substrate: purified human ETHE1 consumes oxygen and produces sulfite in the matched enzyme assay. The same step is therefore both glutathione-conjugate metabolism and a component of sulfide oxidation. It does not assign ETHE1 the initial oxidation of free H2S or the downstream production of sulfate. The measured iron center is part of this catalytic mechanism; no separate binding or self-association mechanism is needed to describe it.

The catalytic and localization evidence comes from distinct experiments. PMID:23144459 used an engineered Δ11/C-terminal His6 preparation, whereas PMID:25596185 used the GPVD-plus-21–254 preparation. PMID:14732903 supplies native human mitochondrial protection and soluble-fraction evidence. Their combination supports the matrix reaction without asserting activity of every shorter sequence record, a compulsory dimer, or a nuclear enzymatic role. Metal occupancy, expressed shorter products and antibody-specific nuclear signal remain useful targets for the proposed experiments.
