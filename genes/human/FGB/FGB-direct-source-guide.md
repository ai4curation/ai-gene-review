# FGB direct evidence guide

The [review YAML](FGB-ai-review.yaml) is the decision authority. The [source records](FGB-direct-source-records.yaml) retain every direct source key, zero-based index, method and locator without duplicating decision prose. [Product boundaries](FGB-product-source-boundaries.yaml) distinguish parent-source identities from curated processing metadata; [literature notes](FGB-notes.md) explain the biological synthesis.

Evidence is assessed at the claim's actual scope. Human beta-chain assembly supports structural complex participation. Intact-fibrinogen receptor or cellular responses are not isolated beta-chain activities. Positive matrix classification can be supported independently at gene level while a particular unavailable high-throughput target/sample record remains uncertain. The external platelet-surface location is more precise than its cell-surface parent (GO:0009897 part_of GO:0009986); the broader structural molecular function still covers soluble-complex assembly beyond matrix incorporation.

For generic binding, the positive cellular association is retained as non-core unless a more informative function is demonstrated. GO:0046790 requires a virion, so an expressed core-protein construct does not establish it. GO:0051087 describes binding to a chaperone and is compatible with the beta chain being a client. These decisions follow the project's [curation instructions](../../../projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions); no new publication quotations are introduced.
