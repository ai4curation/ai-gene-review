# FGB integrated evidence guide

The [review YAML](FGB-ai-review.yaml) is the authority for final decisions. FGB supplies beta-chain structure to fibrinogen and fibrin. Its contextual platelet, cell-adhesion, chaperone-client and extracellular-fraction associations are distinguished from an autonomous beta-chain catalytic or receptor-binding function.

The [literature journal](FGB-notes.md), [direct evidence guide](FGB-direct-source-guide.md) and [database evidence guide](FGB-database-source-guide.md) describe the experiments and source limits. The review contains 113 decisions, 82 complete reference assessments, one structural core and two separately described cleavage products. All imported source identities/order remain unchanged; no NEW assertion or publication quotation is added.

The final normal-source set contains 80 genuine files: 33 publications, 44 Reactome entries and three unchanged PANTHER/PAINT files. The other 29 discovery sources remain private and unchanged. Current family identifiers are preserved alongside explicit evidence from the older API tree and exact human sequence; the version difference is not hidden by replacing the accepted normal files.

The decisions comprise 65 ACCEPT, 28 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, five TOO_GENERAL, one MODIFY and 14 UNDECIDED. The modified cortex assertion is replaced by an evidence-supported external-surface location. A positive generic binding association remains non-core under the standing project instructions. Same-term action differences preserve distinctions between generic structural biology and unresolved source-specific high-throughput observations.

The core uses GO:0072378, blood coagulation, fibrin clot formation, matching the accepted FGB process assertions and its structural contribution to the fibrin network. This is a scope alignment with the evidence, not a claim that pathway participation requires catalysing every step. The ontology evidence records the selection.
