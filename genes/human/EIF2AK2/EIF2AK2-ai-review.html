Gene Description

EIF2AK2 (PKR) is an interferon-inducible cytosolic serine/threonine kinase, one of the four vertebrate eIF2alpha kinases. It has two N-terminal double-stranded RNA-binding motifs and a C-terminal kinase domain. Binding of dsRNA (viral replication intermediates or structured host RNAs), the protein activator PRKRA/PACT, or ISGylation drives PKR homodimerization and autophosphorylation in the activation loop (Thr446/Thr451). Activated PKR phosphorylates eIF2alpha (EIF2S1) on Ser51, which blocks eIF2B-mediated recycling of eIF2 and represses cap-dependent translation initiation. This triggers the integrated stress response, including preferential ATF4 translation, and shuts down viral and host protein synthesis. Through this translational control PKR restricts many RNA and DNA viruses and amplifies IFN-beta induction. Viruses encode many PKR antagonists (E3, K3L, NS5A, E2, US11, NS1, TAR RNA), and cellular inhibitors (NPM1, NCK1, DNAJC3, TARBP2, DUS2) restrain its basal activity. PKR also phosphorylates secondary substrates (MKK6, PPP2R5A, ILF3, TP53, CDK1 Tyr4, among others) and contributes to p38 MAPK and NF-kappaB signalling, apoptosis and growth control. A minor nuclear pool exists. Heterozygous variants cause leukoencephalopathy with episodic neurological regression (LEUDEN) and dystonia 33.

description: EIF2AK2 (PKR) is an interferon-inducible cytosolic serine/threonine kinase, one of the four vertebrate eIF2alpha kinases. It has two N-terminal double-stranded RNA-binding motifs and a C-terminal kinase domain. Binding of dsRNA (viral replication intermediates or structured host RNAs), the protein activator PRKRA/PACT, or ISGylation drives PKR homodimerization and autophosphorylation in the activation loop (Thr446/Thr451). Activated PKR phosphorylates eIF2alpha (EIF2S1) on Ser51, which blocks eIF2B-mediated recycling of eIF2 and represses cap-dependent translation initiation. This triggers the integrated stress response, including preferential ATF4 translation, and shuts down viral and host protein synthesis. Through this translational control PKR restricts many RNA and DNA viruses and amplifies IFN-beta induction. Viruses encode many PKR antagonists (E3, K3L, NS5A, E2, US11, NS1, TAR RNA), and cellular inhibitors (NPM1, NCK1, DNAJC3, TARBP2, DUS2) restrain its basal activity. PKR also phosphorylates secondary substrates (MKK6, PPP2R5A, ILF3, TP53, CDK1 Tyr4, among others) and contributes to p38 MAPK and NF-kappaB signalling, apoptosis and growth control. A minor nuclear pool exists. Heterozygous variants cause leukoencephalopathy with episodic neurological regression (LEUDEN) and dystonia 33.
