CC   -!- FUNCTION: Serine protease that initiates the GZMK pathway of the
CC       complement system, a cascade of proteins directly activated by CD8(+)
CC       T-cells that leads to phagocytosis and breakdown of pathogens and
CC       signaling that strengthens the adaptive immune system (PubMed:3262682,
CC       PubMed:39814882, PubMed:39914456). GZMK is specifically secreted by
CC       CD8(+) T-cells and mediates both recognition and initiation steps of
CC       GZMK complement pathway (PubMed:39914456). First acts as a pattern
CC       recognition receptor, which specifically recognizes and binds heparan
CC       sulfate glycosaminoglycans on the pathogen surface to drive
CC       opsonization (PubMed:39914456). It then initiates the complement
CC       pathway cascade by catalyzing cleavage and activation of C2 and C4, the
CC       next components of the complement pathway (PubMed:39814882,
CC       PubMed:39914456). GZMK-mediated complement activation is an important
CC       contributor to tissue inflammation (PubMed:24711407, PubMed:33271118,
CC       PubMed:35704599, PubMed:39614076, PubMed:39814882, PubMed:39914456).
CC       Also able to cleave and activate F2R/PAR1 and PAR2/F2RL1, possibly
CC       promoting interleukin release (PubMed:21760880, PubMed:26936634,
CC       PubMed:35881628). May also regulate apoptosis by catalyzing cleavage
CC       and activation of BID, thereby promoting cytochrome C release from
CC       mitochrondria (PubMed:17308307). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17308307,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21760880, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24711407,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26936634, ECO:0000269|PubMed:3262682,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33271118, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35704599,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35881628, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39614076,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39814882, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39914456}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Specifically inhibited by D-Phe-Pro-Arg-
CC       chloromethylketone (PPACK) synthetic drug.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10480954, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39814882}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:3262682,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39914456}. Cell surface
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24711407, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39914456}. Cytoplasmic
CC       granule {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1460416}. Note=Constitutively secreted by
CC       CD8(+) T-cells in the absence of TCR stimulation (PubMed:39914456).
CC       Specifically binds heparan sulfate glycosaminoglycans on the surface of
CC       pathogens (PubMed:39914456). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39914456}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Specifically expressed by CD8(+) T-cells.

CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00274}.

CC       activation of complement proteins C3 and C5 in addition to C2 and C4
CC       (PubMed:39814882). However, cleavage of C3 and C5 is probably indirect,
CC       as GZMK-mediated cleavage of C2 and C4 generates the formation of C3
CC       and C5 convertases that cleave and activate C3 and C5.
