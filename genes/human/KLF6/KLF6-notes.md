# KLF6 manual notes

Automated deep research failed for Falcon and the configured Perplexity-lite fallback, so this review uses the seeded GOA references and targeted manual source checks.

KLF6 is a nuclear C2H2 zinc-finger KLF transcriptional activator. PMID:18755691 reports direct binding and activation of the ATF3 promoter by KLF6 in prostate cancer cells, and the original CPBP cloning paper reports a human C2H2 zinc-finger protein that binds a TATA-less core-promoter element and activates reporters (PMID:9083102). The PMID:24634218 full text supports a specific coactivator mechanism in which KLF6 binds RELA/p65 in the nucleus and contributes to a subset of NF-kappaB target promoters.

The old Bcd abstract supports B-cell expression and oncogenic transformation but does not demonstrate that KLF6 directly executes B-cell differentiation, so that broad NAS process row is over-annotated. HPA fibrillar-center and nucleolar locations are retained as observed, non-core nuclear subcompartments rather than treated as the defining location of KLF6 transcriptional activity.
