CC   -!- FUNCTION: Constitutively open nonselective divalent cation-conducting
CC       channels which mediate the influx of Ca(2+), Mg(2+), Mn(2+), Ba(2+),
CC       and Ni(2+) into the cytoplasm, leading to membrane depolarization
CC       (PubMed:11535825, PubMed:19436059, PubMed:21278253). Impermeable to
CC       zinc ions (PubMed:21278253). In addition, forms heteromultimeric ion
CC       channels with TRPM3 which are permeable for calcium and zinc ions
CC       (PubMed:21278253). Plays an essential role for the depolarizing
CC       photoresponse of retinal ON bipolar cells (PubMed:19878917,
CC       PubMed:19896109). In the dark, tonic release of glutamate activates the
CC       G protein-coupled receptor for glutamate, GRM6, its activation induces
CC       the release of G(o) protein and the beta-gamma G protein dimer. Both
CC       subunits can interact and inactivate the TRPM1 channel. A light onset,
CC       induces decrease in glutamate release and deactivation of GRM6 leading
CC       to channel opening and membrane depolarization (By similarity). May
CC       play a role in metastasis suppression (PubMed:9537257).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q2TV84, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11535825,

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the retina where it localizes to the
CC       outer plexiform layer. Specifically, it is expressed in retinal bipolar
CC       cells (BPCs) of the ON subtype (PubMed:30027108). Highly expressed in
CC       benign melanocytic nevi and diffusely expressed in various in situ
CC       melanomas, but not detected in melanoma metastases. Also expressed in
CC       melanocytes and pigmented metastatic melanoma cell lines. In
CC       melanocytes expression appears to be regulated at the level of
CC       transcription and mRNA processing. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11112417,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19896109, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30027108,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9537257}.
