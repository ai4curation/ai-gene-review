# OAS2 notes

OAS2 has two OAS domains and synthesizes 2-5A from ATP after dsRNA activation,
with the C-terminal domain carrying the catalytic site. Merold et al. directly
showed that "dimerization and myristoylation localize OAS2 to Golgi" membranes
[PMID:40412389], so the Golgi membrane rows are core-supporting rather than
over-propagated membrane calls.

The family-level membrane row is kept for OAS2 and OAS1 p46 but pruned from
OAS3 and OASL. The PTHR11258 nucleoplasm row is seeded by OAS1/OAS3 and is
marked as an OAS2 over-propagation because the reviewed OAS2 record localizes it
to cytoplasm, perinuclear cytoplasm and Golgi membrane, not nucleus.

OAS2's regulation-of-lactation rows are kept as non-core by similarity from
mouse Oas2. They are not used as evidence for the antiviral OAS/RNASEL module.
