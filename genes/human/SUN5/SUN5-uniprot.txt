CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required for male fertility (PubMed:38870534). Plays an
CC       essential role in maintenance of sperm head-to-tail attachment during
CC       late stages of spermiogenesis, via promoting secure attachment of the
CC       nuclear envelope to the flagellum coupling apparatus (Probable)
CC       (PubMed:38870534). Component of the LMNB1/SUN5/SEPT12 bridge that
CC       connects the sperm proximal centriole to the implantation fossa, the
CC       bridge functions to prevent detachment of the proximal centriole from
CC       the posterior nucleus during spermiogenesis (PubMed:38870534). Required
CC       for localization of SEPT12 at the implantation fossa of sperm cells
CC       during spermiogenesis (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9DA32,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38870534, ECO:0000305|PubMed:28945193}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Probable homotrimer. Interacts with DNAJB13. Interacts with
CC       SEPT12 and LMNB1; the interactions act as a bridge between LMNB1 and
CC       the Septin complex; which functions to maintain the sperm head-to-tail
CC       junction during late stages of spermiogenesis (PubMed:38870534).
CC       Interacts with SEPT2 (PubMed:38870534). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9DA32,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38870534}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       Q8TC36; Q9NPE6: SPAG4; NbExp=3; IntAct=EBI-13068796, EBI-10819434;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus inner membrane
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:27640305}; Single-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9DA32}. Golgi apparatus
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9DA32}. Note=During spermiogenesis, traffics
CC       through the Golgi apparatus before reaching the round spermatid inner
CC       membrane of the nuclear envelope and later migrates to the coupling
CC       apparatus of the sperm during sperm head elongation and
CC       differentiation. Expressed at the sperm implantation fossa in
CC       spermatids during spermiogenesis. In mature spermatozoa, is localized
CC       to the coupling apparatus of the sperm head and tail in the
CC       implementation fossa (By similarity). Expressed at the neck region in
CC       mature sperm (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9DA32,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:27640305}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Sperm (at protein level) (PubMed:27640305). Widely
CC       expressed (PubMed:12621555). Conflictingly shown to be specifically
CC       expressed in testis (PubMed:21711156). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12621555,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21711156, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27640305}.
CC   -!- PTM: Highly glycosylated in the Golgi apparatus during spermiogenesis.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9DA32}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Spermatogenic failure 16 (SPGF16) [MIM:617187]: An infertility
CC       disorder caused by spermatogenesis defects and characterized by
CC       abnormally shaped spermatozoa in the semen of affected individuals.
CC       Most spermatozoa are made up of headless tails, while a small
CC       proportion has an abnormal head-tail junction. A few spermatozoa are
CC       made up of tailless heads. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27640305,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28541472, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28945193,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29298896}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
CC       affecting the gene represented in this entry.
