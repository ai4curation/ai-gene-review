Gene Description

BAGE (B melanoma antigen 1) encodes a putative 43-residue protein, one of a family of nearly identical BAGE sequences in the juxtacentromeric regions of chromosomes 9, 13, 18 and 21. It is a cancer/testis antigen: silent in normal somatic tissues apart from testis, but re-expressed in melanomas and other cancers, where promoter hypomethylation accompanies expression. Its peptide AARAVFLAL, presented on HLA-Cw16, is recognized by autologous cytolytic T lymphocytes. A signal peptide is predicted, but no biochemical function is known.

OPEN BIOLOGY WHOLLY_DARK

What is known: Established: a 43-residue ORF whose signal-peptide segment AARAVFLAL is presented on HLA-Cw16 to cytolytic T cells, so the N terminus is translated in melanoma. Expression is restricted to testis and tumours and is controlled by promoter methylation. UniProt predicts a 1-17 signal peptide and a 26-residue mature chain from residues 18-43. Not established: whether that mature chain is produced, stable or secreted, any binding partner with functional follow-up, or any cellular function. The BAGE-like/PF08180 family assignment has no known functional signature, Pharos classifies BAGE as Tdark, and PAN-GO assigns no evolutionary-model annotations.

Affinage mechanistic annotation for BAGE (human)

Current model (mechanistic narrative)

BAGE encodes a 43-amino-acid miniprotein that functions as a cancer-testis antigen, defined by the CTL-recognized peptide AARAVFLAL presented on HLA-Cw1601 to trigger cytolytic T lymphocyte killing of human melanoma cells PMID:7895173. Its expression is governed primarily by an epigenetic switch: BAGE loci reside in juxtacentromeric regions of chromosomes 9, 13, 18, and 21 and are densely methylated in normal somatic tissues but hypomethylated in the large majority of tumors, with promoter-region hypomethylation causally driving re-expression in cancer PMID:15704127. Consistent with methylation-based silencing, BAGE transcription in negative cells can be reactivated pharmacologically by the DNA demethylating agent 5-aza-2'-deoxycytidine PMID:9816341, and BAGE-locus hypomethylation is a consistent event in colorectal neoplastic transformation that distinguishes tumor from matched normal tissue PMID:18541613. The family arose through hominoid-lineage chromosomal rearrangements that duplicated an 84-kb fragment containing part of MLL3 into juxtacentromeric regions, and dN/dS analysis indicates the coding sequences are under selective pressure consistent with functional protein products PMID:12676563. Beyond its antigenicity and epigenetic regulation, no intrinsic biochemical or cellular function for the BAGE protein has been characterized in the available corpus.

2026-10-05 review (PAINT, affinage)

  • 43-aa cancer/testis antigen: PMID:7895173
  • UniProt's 'Secreted' is a curator inference (ECO:0000305) from a predicted signal peptide, so the extracellular region row is non-core. AARAVFLAL maps to residues 2-10 inside that predicted signal peptide; the HLA-Cw16 epitope establishes N-terminal translation but not production of the mature 18-43 chain.
  • All 5 GO:0005515 rows come from one interactome screen (PMID:32814053) and are removed under policy.
  • No core function; recorded a WHOLLY_DARK knowledge gap. BAGE2-5 are paralogs in the same BAGE-like family and are reviewed separately; that family has no known functional signature, and UniProt cross-references corroborate BAGE as Pharos Tdark with 0 PAN-GO annotations.
