| Stage | Molecular Components | Mechanism | Key Outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Antigen recognition and receptor triggering | Peptide–MHC, TCRαβ, CD3δε (including CD3D), CD3γε, CD3ζζ, CD4 or CD8 | TCRαβ recognizes peptide–MHC; CD3D does not bind antigen directly. Ligand binding, receptor clustering, phosphatase exclusion, allostery, and mechanical force are complementary proposed mechanisms that make CD3 signaling tails accessible. CD3D contributes through its structural coupling to TCRα and the assembled receptor. (pqac-00000057, pqac-00000059, pqac-00000060) | Converts extracellular antigen recognition into a signaling-competent TCR–CD3 state while preserving antigen specificity. |
| 2. CD3D ITAM exposure | CD3D cytoplasmic tail; inner-leaflet membrane lipids | CD3D contains one ITAM with consensus **YxxL/I–x₆₋₈–YxxL/I**. Triggering increases kinase access to its two tyrosines; membrane organization and receptor conformation regulate accessibility. The exact coupling mechanism remains incompletely resolved. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000019) | Creates an accessible phosphorylation-dependent signaling platform on the cytosolic face of the T-cell membrane. |
| 3. ITAM phosphorylation | LCK, FYN, CD3D ITAM | Src-family kinases phosphorylate the **two ITAM tyrosines**. CD3D contributes one of the ten ITAMs in the complete αβ TCR–CD3 complex; most CD3D ITAM tyrosines are unphosphorylated in resting naïve T cells. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000032) | Produces a doubly phosphorylated ITAM that can recruit tandem-SH2 signaling proteins and encode signal strength. |
| 4. ZAP70 recruitment and activation | Doubly phosphorylated CD3 ITAMs, ZAP70, LCK | ZAP70 binds phosphorylated ITAMs through its tandem SH2 domains and is activated by LCK-dependent phosphorylation and autophosphorylation. CD3D functions as part of the collective CD3 ITAM array rather than as an autonomous receptor. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000036) | Initiates the proximal tyrosine-kinase cascade and couples CD3 phosphorylation to downstream signalosome formation. |
| 5. LAT–SLP76 signalosome assembly | ZAP70, LAT, SLP76, GADS, GRB2, PLCγ1, ITK | Activated ZAP70 phosphorylates LAT and SLP76. These adaptors assemble a membrane-associated signalosome that recruits enzymes and adaptors, including PLCγ1, ITK, GADS, and GRB2. (pqac-00000034, pqac-00000041, pqac-00000045) | Branches the proximal CD3 signal into calcium, DAG, MAPK, cytoskeletal, metabolic, and survival pathways. |
| 6. Ras–ERK–AP-1 branch | GRB2, SOS and/or RASGRP1, RAS, RAF, MEK1/2, ERK1/2, FOS, JUN | LAT-associated GRB2–SOS and DAG-responsive RASGRP1 activate RAS; RAS activates RAF→MEK→ERK. ERK induces FOS, while RAC–JNK contributes JUN; FOS/JUN form AP-1. (pqac-00000042, pqac-00000043, pqac-00000045) | Promotes AP-1-dependent genes controlling activation, proliferation, differentiation, and cytokine production. |
| 7. IP3–Ca²⁺–NFAT branch | PLCγ1, PIP2, IP3, endoplasmic-reticulum Ca²⁺ stores, STIM1, ORAI/CRAC channels, calmodulin, calcineurin, NFAT | PLCγ1 cleaves PIP2 into IP3 and DAG. IP3 induces Ca²⁺ release and store-operated Ca²⁺ entry; Ca²⁺–calmodulin activates calcineurin, which dephosphorylates NFAT and permits its nuclear entry. (pqac-00000041, pqac-00000043, pqac-00000047) | NFAT cooperates with AP-1 to induce IL-2 and other activation genes; NFAT without adequate AP-1 can instead favor anergy-associated programs. |
| 8. DAG–PKCθ–NF-κB branch | DAG, PKCθ, CARMA1–BCL10–MALT1 complex, TRAF6, IKK, IκB, NF-κB | DAG activates PKCθ, promoting assembly of the CBM complex and activation of IKK. IKK-dependent IκB degradation releases NF-κB for nuclear translocation. (pqac-00000043, pqac-00000044, pqac-00000047) | Drives inflammatory, survival, proliferation, and effector-gene expression. |
| 9. PI3K–AKT–mTOR branch | PI3K, PIP2, PIP3, AKT, TSC1/2, mTORC1/2 | PI3K converts PIP2 to PIP3, recruiting PH-domain signaling proteins and activating AKT; AKT and TSC1/2-dependent regulation engage mTOR complexes. This arm is strengthened by costimulatory input such as CD28. (pqac-00000034, pqac-00000043, pqac-00000045) | Supports metabolic reprogramming, protein synthesis, growth, survival, proliferation, and differentiation. |
| 10. Integrated transcriptional response | NFAT, AP-1, NF-κB; additional CREB and MEF2 inputs | The calcium, MAPK, PKCθ, and metabolic branches converge in the nucleus. Signal amplitude, duration, costimulation, inhibitory receptors, phosphatases, and the broader ten-ITAM array determine the final program; no unique downstream pathway is known to be exclusive to CD3D. (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000040, pqac-00000047) | Induces cytokines such as IL-2, clonal expansion, survival, differentiation, cytotoxic or helper effector functions, and context-dependent tolerance or anergy. |


*Table: This table traces signaling from antigen-triggered TCR–CD3 activation through phosphorylation of CD3D’s two-tyrosine ITAM and the major downstream transcriptional branches. It distinguishes CD3D’s contribution to the collective CD3 signaling apparatus from functions of the receptor complex as a whole.*
