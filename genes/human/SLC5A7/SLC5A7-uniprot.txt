CC   -!- FUNCTION: High-affinity Na(+)-coupled choline transmembrane symporter
CC       (PubMed:11027560, PubMed:11068039, PubMed:12237312, PubMed:12969261,
CC       PubMed:17005849, PubMed:23132865, PubMed:23141292, PubMed:27569547,
CC       PubMed:40972734). Functions as an electrogenic, voltage-dependent
CC       transporter with variable charge/choline stoichiometry
CC       (PubMed:17005849). Choline uptake and choline-induced current is also
CC       Cl(-)-dependent where Cl(-) is likely a regulatory ion rather than
CC       cotransported ion (PubMed:11068039, PubMed:12237312, PubMed:17005849).
CC       Plays a critical role in acetylcholine (ACh) synthesis by taking up the
CC       substrate choline from the synaptic cleft into the presynaptic nerve
CC       terminals after neurotransmitter release (PubMed:27569547).
CC       SLC5A7/CHT1-mediated choline high-affinity transport in cholinergic
CC       neurons is the rate-limiting step for production of ACh, thereby
CC       facilitating communication by subsequent action potentials
CC       (PubMed:11027560). Localized predominantly in presynaptic terminal
CC       intracellular organelles, and translocated to the plasma membrane in
CC       active form in response to neuronal activity (PubMed:12969261,
CC       PubMed:15953352). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11027560,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11068039, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12237312,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12969261, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15953352,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17005849, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23132865,

CC   -!- DISEASE: Neuronopathy, distal hereditary motor, autosomal dominant 7
CC       (HMND7) [MIM:158580]: A form of distal hereditary motor neuronopathy, a
CC       heterogeneous group of neuromuscular disorders caused by selective
CC       degeneration of motor neurons in the anterior horn of the spinal cord,
CC       without sensory deficit in the posterior horn. The overall clinical
CC       picture consists of a classical distal muscular atrophy syndrome in the
CC       legs without clinical sensory loss. The disease starts with weakness
CC       and wasting of distal muscles of the anterior tibial and peroneal
CC       compartments of the legs. Later on, weakness and atrophy may expand to
CC       the proximal muscles of the lower limbs and/or to the distal upper
CC       limbs. HMND7 is characterized by onset in the second decade of
CC       progressive distal muscle wasting and weakness affecting the upper and
CC       lower limbs and resulting in walking difficulties and hand grip. There
CC       is significant muscle atrophy of the hands and lower limbs. The
CC       disorder is associated with vocal cord paresis due to involvement of
CC       the tenth cranial nerve. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23141292}. Note=The
CC       disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented in this
CC       entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Myasthenic syndrome, congenital, 20, presynaptic (CMS20)
CC       [MIM:617143]: A form of congenital myasthenic syndrome, a group of
CC       disorders characterized by failure of neuromuscular transmission,
CC       including pre-synaptic, synaptic, and post-synaptic disorders that are
CC       not of autoimmune origin. Clinical features are easy fatigability and
CC       muscle weakness. CMS20 is an autosomal recessive, pre-synaptic form
CC       characterized by severe hypotonia and episodic apnea soon after birth,
CC       generalized limb fatigability and weakness, delayed walking, ptosis,
CC       poor sucking and swallowing. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27569547}. Note=The
CC       disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented in this
CC       entry.
