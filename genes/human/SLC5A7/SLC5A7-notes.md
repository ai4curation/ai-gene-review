# SLC5A7 evidence notes


## 2026-10-26: complete source-specific acetylcholine panel audit

Human transporter assays establish sodium-dependent high-affinity choline uptake (PMID:11027560, PMID:11068039); this supplies substrate for CHAT and therefore participates in acetylcholine biosynthesis even though CHT does not acetylate choline. The normal full PMC recovery for PMID:17005849 reports variable charge/choline relationships and a chloride regulatory interpretation: do not impose a universal fixed ion stoichiometry or equate every current with stoichiometric transport. Newer structural work does not turn chloride dependence into proof of one universal transport ratio.

The GOA Reactome neurotransmitter-transport and bile-acid-transport collection contexts are overbroad for the actual choline-uptake reaction; this is not an experimentally established absence of all alternative substrates. A neurotransmitter-cycle label does not identify acetylcholine as the transported substrate. PMID:11904763 specifically localizes rat CHT to motoneuron perikarya, dendrites and axons, independently supporting the inherited neuronal compartments. Vesicle/endosome membrane pools coexist with active surface uptake.
