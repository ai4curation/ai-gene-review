Gene Description

DNASE1L3 is a calcium- and magnesium-dependent DNase I-family endonuclease that degrades extracellular DNA, including nucleosomal chromatin associated with apoptotic cell-derived microparticles and released neutrophil extracellular traps. Its C-terminal region supports access to protein- and lipid-associated DNA substrates, helping clear self-DNA and limit anti-DNA autoimmunity. Intracellular DNASE1L3 can also cleave nuclear chromatin into internucleosomal fragments in dying cells; this contribution depends on the cellular and death-induction context.
