Gene Description

SPAAR (small regulatory polypeptide of amino acid response; formerly the lncRNA LINC00961) is a mammalian microprotein of 90 aa (canonical) or 75 aa (short isoform, conserved with mouse) with a single transmembrane helix, a short lumenal N-terminus and a cytoplasmic C-terminus. It resides in the membrane of late endosomes and lysosomes, where it associates with the lysosomal V-type proton ATPase (interacting with the V0 a1 and a2 subunits ATP6V0A1/ATP6V0A2). SPAAR selectively restrains mTORC1 activation by amino acids, but not by growth factors: it favours a tightly bound V-ATPase-Ragulator-Rag GTPase supercomplex that fails to recruit mTORC1 to the lysosome. In mouse skeletal muscle the peptide is downregulated after acute injury, and its loss enhances mTORC1 activation and muscle regeneration. The peptide is expressed in lung, heart, skeletal muscle and endothelium; overexpression in endothelial cells promotes network formation, while the host RNA has separable, opposing effects on angiogenesis.

Core Functions

Lysosome/late-endosome membrane microprotein that acts as a negative regulator of amino-acid-induced mTORC1 activation. SPAAR associates with the lysosomal V-ATPase (ATP6V0A1/ATP6V0A2) and favours a tightly bound V-ATPase-Ragulator-Rag GTPase supercomplex, preventing mTORC1 recruitment to the lysosome in response to amino acids but not growth factors. It is an interactor and regulator of the V-ATPase, not a subunit of the proton pump; no molecular-function term currently captures this regulatory activity.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: What molecular activity does SPAAR exert on the V-ATPase-Ragulator-Rag assembly: does it directly bridge the V0 a subunit to Ragulator/Rag, or allosterically lock the V-ATPase in an amino-acid-insensitive state? The answer determines whether a molecular adaptor or an enzyme/GTPase regulator MF term is appropriate.

Q: Is the lysosomal V-ATPase interaction cell-type specific? HA-SPAAR pull-down in endothelial cells did not recover V-ATPase subunits, and the pro-angiogenic effect of SPAAR overexpression may operate through a different mechanism (e.g. SYNE1).

Q: Does the primate-specific 90-aa isoform 1 share the localisation and mTORC1-regulatory function of the conserved 75-aa isoform 2, to which UniProt attributes the function?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Generate peptide-specific (start-codon mutant, lncRNA-preserving) knockout endothelial cells or mice, as done for muscle, and test angiogenesis, lysosomal mTORC1 recruitment after amino-acid restimulation, and rescue with wild-type versus V-ATPase-binding-deficient SPAAR.

Hypothesis: SPAAR's pro-angiogenic effect in endothelial cells depends on its mTORC1-regulatory function at the lysosome.

Type: genetic knockout and rescue

SPAAR (LINC00961-encoded microprotein) — review notes

2026-09-30 — initial review (claude-code)

Identity

  • UniProt A0A1B0GVQ0 (SPAR_HUMAN), 90 aa canonical (isoform 1); isoform 2 lacks residues
    1-15 (alternative initiation, VSP_058780). HGNC:27244; synonyms LINC00961, SPAR.
    Mouse ortholog UniProt A0A1B0GSZ0.
  • Topology (UniProt, from PMID:28024296): lumenal N-terminus (1-18), single TM helix
    (19-39), cytoplasmic C-terminus (40-90). Pfam PF22004 / InterPro IPR054161 (SPAR);
    no other domains, no catalytic motif.
  • UniProt FUNCTION is annotated to isoform 2 (the short, human/mouse-conserved form).
    Lee et al. 2021: "Two isoforms of the SPAAR protein have been experimentally
    characterized: a short form present in human and mouse, and a long form present in
    human but not in mouse" PMID:34946813. The 5' elongated long form arose de novo in
    primates PMID:34946813. GOA rows are on the canonical accession without isoform qualifiers.

Primary evidence: Matsumoto et al. 2017 Nature (PMID:28024296; cached abstract only)

  • Localisation + v-ATPase interaction: PMID:28024296.
  • Specificity for amino-acid input: PMID:28024296.
  • Peptide-specific KO mouse (lncRNA preserved): PMID:28024296; muscle regeneration: PMID:28024296.
  • UniProt summary of the full-text mechanism: SPAAR interacts with ATP6V0A1 and ATP6V0A2
    and "acts by promoting the formation of a tightly bound supercomplex composed of the
    lysosomal V-ATPase, Ragulator and Rag GTPases, preventing recruitment of mTORC1"
    (UniProt CC FUNCTION, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28024296). I could not read the full text; this
    mechanistic detail is taken from UniProt's curated record.
  • Commentaries: Tajbakhsh 2017 PMID:28388426; Matsumoto et
    al. 2017 Cell Cycle feature (PMID:28319435, title "SPAR, a lncRNA encoded mTORC1
    inhibitor").

Endothelial / angiogenesis work (Baker lab)

  • Spencer et al. 2020 [PMID:31990292, full text cached]. LINC00961 locus is endothelial
    enriched; the locus produces two molecules with opposing effects: PMID:31990292. SPAAR ORF overexpression in HUVECs increased network formation
    PMID:31990292. Evidence for the peptide's angiogenic role is
    overexpression only; knockdown/locus KO removes both RNA and peptide.
  • Endogenous SPAAR peptide not detected in basal HUVECs PMID:31990292.
  • HA-SPAAR pull-down in HUVECs did not recover v-ATPase subunits: PMID:31990292. Top hit SYNE1 (nesprin-1). This is a single overexpression AP-MS
    with no follow-up; not enough for an MF/BP annotation.
  • Spiroski et al. 2021 PMID:33478078: whole-locus KO mice — sex-specific growth delay,
    smaller LV volumes, larger infarct risk area. Locus KO cannot separate lncRNA vs peptide
    function; not usable for peptide-specific GO annotation.

Evolution

  • Lee, Wacholder, Carvunis 2021 PMID:34946813: orthologs in marsupials and monotremes
    ("SPAAR has existed at least since the emergence of mammals"), low primary sequence
    conservation but conserved structure.

Other literature

  • Many LINC00961 cancer papers (miRNA sponge models, e.g. PMID:30825207 HCC) treat the
    transcript as a lncRNA and do not address the peptide. Not used for GO.

Curation reasoning

  • Complex membership vs binding. SPAAR is an interactor/regulator of the lysosomal
    v-ATPase, not a subunit (v-ATPase subunit inventory is ATP6V1/ATP6V0/accessory
    ATP6AP1/2; SPAAR is not among them and the interaction was not seen in HUVECs). GOA's
    colocalizes_with GO:0046611 correctly avoids asserting membership; ACCEPT it with that
    reading. Do not propose part_of.
  • MF. No MF term captures "negative regulator of amino-acid-induced mTORC1 lysosomal
    recruitment by stabilising v-ATPase-Ragulator-Rag supercomplex". ATPase binding
    (GO:0051117) would only restate the interaction, and there is no evidence SPAAR alters
    v-ATPase catalytic/pump activity, so no ATPase regulator term. Leave MF unset in core
    function; flagged as a question.
  • BP. Core: GO:1904262 negative regulation of TORC1 signaling (IMP). GO:0071230
    cellular response to amino acid stimulus is acceptable (SPAAR acts specifically on the
    amino-acid arm of mTORC1 activation). GO:0043416 regulation of skeletal muscle tissue
    regeneration is a downstream tissue phenotype of the peptide-specific KO in mouse; keep
    as non-core.
  • No NEW annotations proposed: angiogenesis evidence is overexpression-only in one study,
    with locus-level confounding.
