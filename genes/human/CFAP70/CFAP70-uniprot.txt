CC   -!- FUNCTION: Axoneme-binding protein that plays a role in the regulation
CC       of ciliary motility and cilium length. Essential for the assembly of
CC       the sperm flagellum and the correct shaping of the sperm head, during
CC       spermiogenesis.Contributes in part to flagellar biogenesis by
CC       stabilizing QRICH2, promoting the cytoplasmic preassembly of CFAP61 and
CC       CFAP91, and regulating the manchette localization of axoneme-associated
CC       proteins such as IFT88, DNAI1 and CFAP61.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:D3YVL2}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with MYCBPAP, CFAP65, DNAI1, DNAI2, QRICH2, CFAP61
