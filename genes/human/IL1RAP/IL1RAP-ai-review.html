Gene Description

IL1RAP (IL-1 receptor accessory protein, IL-1R3, IL-1RAcP) is a type I transmembrane protein of the IL-1 receptor family with three extracellular Ig-like domains and a cytoplasmic TIR domain. It does not bind cytokine on its own; instead it is recruited by a cytokine-bound primary receptor and supplies the second TIR domain of the signalling heterodimer. It is the shared co-receptor for IL-1alpha/IL-1beta (with IL1R1), IL-33 (with IL1RL1/ST2) and IL-36alpha/beta/gamma (with IL1RL2). Ligand-induced juxtaposition of the two receptor TIR domains recruits MYD88 and TOLLIP-IRAK complexes, nucleating the Myddosome that drives NF-kappaB and MAPK activation and inflammatory gene expression. IL1RAP also joins IL-1-bound IL1R2 in a non-signalling decoy complex, and a secreted splice isoform raises the affinity of soluble IL1R2 for IL-1, aiding IL-1 neutralisation. In neurons (shown in mouse), IL1RAP and its AcPb isoform act as a trans-synaptic adhesion partner of presynaptic PTPRD that promotes synapse formation.

Functional Isoforms

Curated functional classes representing distinct biological activities. These may be splice variants, cleavage products, or other forms with different functions.

membrane-bound IL-1RAcP (mIL-1RAcP and neuronal AcPb) SPLICE CLASS
ID: IL1RAP_MEMBRANE
UNIPROT ISOFORM: Q9NPH3-1, Q9NPH3-5
Transmembrane forms. Isoform 1 is the signalling co-receptor that is recruited by cytokine-bound IL1R1, IL1RL1 or IL1RL2 and supplies the second TIR domain of the receptor heterodimer; it carries the core coreceptor and IL-1/IL-33/IL-36 signalling functions of the gene. The AcPb isoform (Q9NPH3-5) has a distinct C-terminus and, in mouse neurons, acts with isoform 1 as a trans-synaptic partner of PTPRD.
Isoform-specific terms: coreceptor activity interleukin-1-mediated signaling pathway
soluble IL-1RAcP (sIL-1RAcP) SPLICE VARIANT
ID: IL1RAP_SOLUBLE
UNIPROT ISOFORM: Q9NPH3-2
Secreted isoform lacking the transmembrane and TIR regions. It associates with secreted ligand-bound IL1R2 and raises its affinity for IL-1 by about two orders of magnitude, making soluble IL1R2 a potent IL-1 inhibitor (PMID:23847621; UniProt isoform 2 FUNCTION, PubMed:12530978). It therefore acts opposite to the membrane isoform, and it is the basis for keeping negative regulation of interleukin-1-mediated signaling pathway (GO:2000660) on this gene. Isoform 3 (sIL-1RAcP-beta, Q9NPH3-3) is also secreted but has no characterised function.
Isoform-specific terms: negative regulation of interleukin-1-mediated signaling pathway

is enables. IL1RAP also contacts IL-1beta in the ternary complex (PMID:22426547). The enables rows
are accepted, the convention is recorded in their reason, and the question of contributes_to for
accessory chains is raised in suggested_questions.
