CC   -!- FUNCTION: Microtubule-based anterograde translocator for membranous
CC       organelles, such as primary cilium and centriole. Shows a plus end-
CC       directed microtubule sliding activity in vitro. Plays a role in primary
CC       cilia formation, probably by mediating transport of proteins into
CC       primary cilia. Mediates smoothened (SMO) translocation into primary
CC       cilia in response to hedgehog morphogens. Plays a role in centriole
CC       cohesion and subdistal appendage organization and function. Regulates
CC       the formation of the subdistal appendage via recruitment of DCTN1 to
CC       the centriole. Also required for ciliary basal feet formation and
CC       microtubule anchoring to mother centriole.
