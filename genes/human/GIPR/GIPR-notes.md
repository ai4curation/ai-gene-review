## Function
- Class B1 GPCR for glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP). Human islet receptor clones showed
  high-affinity GIP binding; "GIP binding to both forms of the receptor induced a dose-dependent increase in
  intracellular cAMP levels (EC50 values of 0.6-0.8 nmol/l) but no elevation of cytoplasmic calcium
  concentrations" [PMID:7556958]. Insulinoma clone coupled to adenylate cyclase and did not raise Ca2+
  [PMID:7589426].
- GIP binds in a surface groove of the ECD [PMID:17715056]; truncated GIP(3-30)NH2 is a competitive
  antagonist at human GIPR in COS-7 cAMP assays [PMID:26572091].
- GWAS: GIPR variants associated with 2-h glucose, reduced insulin secretion and diminished incretin effect
  [PMID:20081857].
- RAMP complexes from multiplexed screen [PMID:39083597].

## Localization
- Seven-TM receptor, ECD aa 22-138 extracellular. "N-glycosylation is required for cell surface expression"
  (UniProt, PMID:22412906). Conclusion: plasma membrane, ligand-binding ECD exposed.
