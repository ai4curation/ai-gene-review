# Affinage mechanistic annotation for APOC4 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

APOC4 encodes a small apolipoprotein within the apolipoprotein E/C-I/C-II gene cluster, comprising 127 residues with a 25-residue signal peptide and two predicted amphipathic alpha-helical domains, expressed at low levels in human liver [PMID:8530039]. The amphipathic helical architecture is consistent with lipid association characteristic of exchangeable apolipoproteins, and genetic variation at the locus links apoC-IV to triglyceride metabolism [PMID:8530039, PMID:10996355]. Beyond this molecular characterization and its association with lipid traits, no enzymatic activity, receptor interaction, or reconstituted mechanism for apoC-IV has been characterized in the available corpus.
