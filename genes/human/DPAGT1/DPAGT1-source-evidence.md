## Binding and propagation

The HTT pair records annotate an HTT sufficient-binding region of 1–546 and modifications in residues 18–38. These are participant features distinct from parent sequence identifiers/checksums. DPAGT1's partner field is not evidence that every native product was tested. The original Y2H protocol excludes reporter-autoactivating baits and uses multiple growth/reporters. This positive pair supports non-core binding; neither a specific physiological role nor a narrower functional class is established by the screen. Genericity alone is not grounds for removal or over-annotation.

PAINT node PTN000060284 carries the membrane and GPT-activity IBD assertions at the eukaryotic ancestor. The official PTHR10571 tree places human Q9H3H5 at leaf PTN002471652 below it. The human experimental descendant among the IBD sources is legitimate target grounding. The membrane claim is refined to ER membrane using target-specific evidence; the catalytic claim is accepted. No donor-count or circularity objection is made.

IPR033895 maps the specific GPT activity and LLO process. IPR000715 maps a broader membrane/phosphotransferase family encompassing other substrate chemistries; human target evidence supplies the specific reaction and compartment. The current Golgi IEA row transfers from P42867 and ENSMUSP00000056282; the literal mouse record is MGI IPI, PMID:36074821, with Q8BH26. Its unresolved source scope is explicitly separated from the independently supported ER pool.

## Reaction and ontology boundaries

[GO:0003975](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0003975) uses Dol-P as acceptor (EC 2.7.8.15). [GO:0003976](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0003976) uses lysosomal-enzyme D-mannose (EC 2.7.8.17). The full 1982 source and current definitions establish this difference.

The typed GO closure places LLO synthesis GO:0006488 within protein N-linked glycosylation GO:0006487. One catalytic core therefore represents the performed step, and the old broad process row is refined without introducing a duplicate NEW process. GO:0042803 is the homodimerization child of identical-protein binding GO:0042802; the refinement does not assert a particular intersubunit bond. ER membrane GO:0005789 reaches membrane GO:0016020 and intracellular membrane-bounded organelle GO:0043231 through the checked is_a/part_of closure.

[Reactome R-HSA-446191](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-446191) → catalyst 449231 → protein [449262](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-449262) → reference entity 55898 identifies human Q9H3H5, residues 1–408, in ER membrane. [R-HSA-4549334](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-4549334) → catalyst 9631918 → defined mutant set 4549346 links to normal reaction 446191. Its loss-of-function framing is not proof that every clinical variant has reduced purified turnover. The source summary's glycosidic-linkage shorthand is not substituted for the chemically explicit phosphotransfer reaction.

GO-CAM `65c57c3400000687`, activity `65d7e4ac00000377`, already models Q9H3H5 enabling the catalytic activity, part of LLO synthesis and occurring in ER membrane. No new process assertion is proposed.
