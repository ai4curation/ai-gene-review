# DYNC2H1 evidence and source guide

This guide separates the preserved repository caches from independently accessed originals and identifies the evidence behind the material decisions. The review contains 46 imported source assertions, three official alternative products and no new annotation. The [reproducible evidence analysis](DYNC2H1-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md) reconstructs database records; it does not generate biological verdicts.

## Molecular identity and propagation

[UniProt Q8NCM8](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8NCM8/entry) identifies three official products. The primary sequence is Q8NCM8-1 (4,307 residues); Q8NCM8-2 has the Q3273-to-QIIGLKSW change and is 4,314 residues. Q8NCM8-3 deletes residues 736–4122, retaining the N-terminal 735 and C-terminal 185 residues. It is 920 residues long and lacks the AAA motor ring and stalk. The short product is not assigned full-length motor competence. Names, accessions and inherited sequence notes are preserved.

The official [PANTHER gene-information service](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/geneinfo?geneInputList=Q8NCM8&organism=9606) resolves the target to PTHR10676:SF352. Its [tree](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/treeinfo?family=PTHR10676) and [alignment](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/familymsa?family=PTHR10676), together with the literal [PAINT IBD source](https://data.pantherdb.org/ftp/downloads/paint/current/IBD.gaf), are saved in the replay bundle with exact hashes. The 787-node tree/alignment reproduce Q8NCM8-1 and confirm target descent through PTN002472914 and PTN000743491. The initially retrieved UniProt-linked PTHR46532 topology lacked the target; that lookup remains protected provenance rather than evidence against the IBA. The sibling dynein-1 IRDs are not exclusions on the target path.

The GO:0060294 decision challenges functional placement rather than node validity. The [current term](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0060294) is `is_a` [cilium movement](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0003341), and `part_of` both cilium-dependent cell motility and cell motility. Dynein-2 moves cargo along the axoneme; axonemal dyneins generate ciliary bending. The review also explicitly recognizes IFT-powered protozoan surface gliding, where movement along a cilium transmits traction. Thus this decision does not deny all IFT involvement in cell locomotion: it distinguishes cargo movement along a cilium from the ontology's motion of the cilium itself. Motile-cilium/9+2-cilium locations and positive assembly/retrograde-transport assertions remain intact.

The [Reactome IFT entries](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5625426) are traced through explicit complex/dimer membership to Q8NCM8 residues 1–4307 in the cilium or ciliary-tip compartment. The replay contains six source reaction roots and twelve target paths. These are model assertions, not twelve independent localization experiments. Current InterPro rules and the ARBA evidence are assessed at their own scope; the cytoplasmic ARBA output is not described as a fully reconstructed historical rule execution when its current conditions do not match the retrieved target cross-references.
