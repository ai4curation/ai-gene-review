Gene Description

BLOC1S1, also called BLOS1 or GCN5L1, is a small shared subunit of the BLOC-1 and BORC trafficking complexes. In BLOC-1 it helps maintain the assembly that organizes endosomal cargo delivery and lysosome-related organelle biogenesis. In BORC it supports complex stability and ARL8-dependent outward positioning of lysosomes from the cytoplasmic surface of the lysosomal membrane. BLOC1S1 also has cytosolic and mitochondrial pools and promotes protein acetylation associated with respiratory regulation; cellular acetylation experiments have not yet resolved whether it provides an intrinsic acetyl-transfer catalytic site or supports other components of the acetylation machinery.
