CC   -!- FUNCTION: Peptide hormone that functions as endogenous ligand for the G
CC       protein-coupled apelin receptor (APLNR/APJ), that plays a role in the
CC       regulation of normal cardiovascular function and fluid homeostasis
CC       (PubMed:25639753, PubMed:28137936, PubMed:35817871). Functions as a
CC       balanced agonist activating both G(i) protein pathway and beta-arrestin
CC       pathway of APLNR (PubMed:35817871). Downstream G proteins activation,
CC       apelin can inhibit cAMP production and activate key intracellular
CC       effectors such as ERKs (PubMed:35817871). On the other hand, APLNR
CC       activation induces beta-arrestin recruitment to the membrane leading to
CC       desensitization and internalization of the receptor (PubMed:35817871).
CC       Required for mesendodermal differentiation, blood vessels formation and
CC       heart morphogenesis during early development and for adult
CC       cardiovascular homeostasis (PubMed:25639753, PubMed:28137936). Acts as
CC       a motogen by promoting mesendodermal cell migration during gastrulation
CC       by binding and activating APLNR. Acts as an early embryonic regulator
CC       of cellular movement with a role in migration and development of
CC       cardiac progenitor cells. May act as a chemoattractant for the
CC       activation of angioblast migration toward the embryonic midline, i.e.
CC       the position of the future vessel formation, during vasculogenesis.
CC       Positively regulates sinus venosus (SV)-derived endothelial cells
CC       migration into the developing heart to promote coronary blood vessel
CC       sprouting. Plays a role in placental vascular development; promotes
CC       placental trophoblast invasion and spiral artery remodeling in the
CC       uterus. Involved in the regulation of maternal cardiovascular
CC       homeostasis to prevent gestational hypertension and for potent
CC       cardioprotective functions during heart failure. Mediates myocardial
CC       contractility in an ERK1/2-dependent manner (By similarity).

CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P0DP76, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25639753,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28137936, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35817871}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24316148,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28137936}. Secreted, extracellular space
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28137936}. Note=Found in blood plasma
CC       (PubMed:28137936). Found in serum of pregnant mice, peaking at
CC       midgestation; indicating a maternal and zygotic origin of circulating
CC       APELA during pregnancy (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P0DMC4}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the intima of blood vessels
CC       (PubMed:28137936). Expressed in endothelial cells in blood vessels in
CC       the heart and lung (PubMed:28137936). Expressed in cytotrophoblasts and
CC       syncytiotrophoblasts of first-trimester placental tissue and term
CC       placentas (at protein level) (PubMed:28663440). Not detected in smooth
CC       muscle cells or cardiomyocytes (at protein level) (PubMed:28137936).
CC       Expressed in kidney (PubMed:25639753). Expressed in blood vessels
CC       (PubMed:28137936). Expressed in embryonic (ESCs) and induced (iPSCs)
CC       pluripotent stem cells (PubMed:25639753). Most highly expressed in
CC       undifferentiated embryonic stem cell and is rapidly down-regulated
CC       during differentiation (PubMed:24316148). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24316148,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25639753, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28137936,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28663440}.
