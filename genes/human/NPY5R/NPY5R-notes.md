# NPY5R curation notes

Deep research (falcon/perplexity-lite) unavailable; notes from UniProt Q15761 and cached papers.

- Class A GPCR; high affinity for NPY, PYY, (2-36)NPY, (LP)NPY; low for (13-36)NPY and rat PP
  [PMID:8824284 "pharmacological analysis shows that the rat and human Y5 receptors have high affinity for the peptides that elicit feeding"].
- Gi/o coupled: inhibits adenylate cyclase (UniProt FUNCTION).
- Cardiac morphogenesis IMP rows (PMID:19593212) rest on a linkage/association study of adult LV traits
  in hypertensives (full text available) - no morphogenesis assay; removed.
- Most process IEAs are rat Ensembl Compara transfers of physiological phenotypes.

Localization conclusion: plasma membrane, 7-TM (N-terminus and ECLs extracellular); neuronal
subdomains (presynapse, neuron projection, possibly primary cilium) from rodent data.
