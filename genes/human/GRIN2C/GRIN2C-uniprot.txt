CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of N-methyl-D-aspartate (NMDA) receptors (NMDARs)
CC       that function as heterotetrameric, ligand-gated cation channels with
CC       high calcium permeability and voltage-dependent block by Mg(2+)
CC       (PubMed:26875626, PubMed:36309015). Participates in synaptic plasticity
CC       for learning and memory formation by contributing to the slow phase of
CC       excitatory postsynaptic current and long-term synaptic potentiation (By
CC       similarity). Channel activation requires binding of the
CC       neurotransmitter L-glutamate to the GluN2 subunit, glycine or D-serine
CC       binding to the GluN1 subunit, plus membrane depolarization to eliminate
CC       channel inhibition by Mg(2+) (PubMed:26875626, PubMed:36309015). NMDARs
CC       mediate simultaneously the potassium efflux and the influx of calcium
CC       and sodium (By similarity). Each GluN2 subunit confers differential
CC       attributes to channel properties, including activation, deactivation
CC       and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, Ca2(+) permeability, and
CC       binding to allosteric modulators (PubMed:26875626).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P35438, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q01098,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26875626, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36309015}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterotetramer (PubMed:36309015). Forms heterotetrameric
CC       channels composed of two GluN1/zeta subunits (GRIN1), and two identical
CC       GluN2/epsilon subunits (GRIN2A, GRIN2B, GRIN2C or GRIN2D) or GluN3
CC       subunits (GRIN3A or GRIN3B) (in vitro) (PubMed:26875626,
CC       PubMed:36309015). In vivo, the subunit composition may depend on the
CC       expression levels of the different subunits (Probable). Interacts with
CC       PDZ domains of PATJ and DLG4 (By similarity). Interacts (via PDZ-
CC       binding motif) with SNX27 (via PDZ domain); the interaction is required
CC       for recycling to the plasma membrane when endocytosed and prevent
CC       degradation in lysosomes (PubMed:21300787).
