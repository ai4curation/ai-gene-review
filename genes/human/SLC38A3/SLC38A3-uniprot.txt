CC   -!- FUNCTION: Symporter that cotransports specific neutral amino acids and
CC       sodium ions, coupled to an H(+) antiporter activity (PubMed:10823827).
CC       Mainly participates in the glutamate-GABA-glutamine cycle in brain
CC       where it transports L-glutamine from astrocytes in the intercellular
CC       space for the replenishment of both neurotransmitters glutamate and
CC       gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) in neurons and also functions as the
CC       major influx transporter in ganglion cells mediating the uptake of
CC       glutamine (By similarity). The transport activity is specific for L-
CC       glutamine, L-histidine and L-asparagine (PubMed:10823827). The
CC       transport is electroneutral coupled to the cotransport of 1 Na(+) and
CC       the antiport of 1 H(+) (By similarity). The transport is pH dependent,
CC       saturable, Li(+) tolerant and functions in both direction depending on
CC       the concentration gradients of its substrates and cotransported ions
CC       (PubMed:10823827). Also mediates an amino acid-gated H(+) conductance
CC       that is not stoichiometrically coupled to the amino acid transport but
CC       which influences the ionic gradients that drive the amino acid
CC       transport (By similarity). In addition, may play a role in nitrogen
CC       metabolism, amino acid homeostasis, glucose metabolism and renal
CC       ammoniagenesis (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9DCP2,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9JHZ9, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10823827}.

CC       neurodevelopmental impairment, and poor prognosis. Development is
CC       normal prior to seizure onset, after which cognitive and motor delays
CC       become apparent. DEE102 is an autosomal recessive form characterized by
CC       onset of variable types of seizures in infancy.
