CC   -!- FUNCTION: Reversibly inhibits the activity of ATP2A1/SERCA1 and
CC       ATP2A2/SERCA2 in sarcoplasmic reticulum by decreasing the apparent
CC       affinity of the ATPase for Ca(2+) (PubMed:11781085, PubMed:9575189).
CC       Also inhibits the activity of ATP2A3/SERCA3 (By similarity). Modulates
CC       calcium re-uptake during muscle relaxation and plays an important role
CC       in calcium homeostasis in muscle. Required for muscle-based, non-
CC       shivering thermogenesis (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9CQD6,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11781085, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9575189}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homooligomer (PubMed:36523160). Can also form heterooligomers
CC       with other sarcoplasmic/endoplasmic reticulum calcium ATPase (SERCA)
CC       regulators ARLN, ERLN, PLN and STRIT1/DWORF (PubMed:36523160). Monomer
CC       (By similarity). Interacts with calcium ATPase ATP2A1/SERCA1. Interacts
CC       as a monomer with ATP2A2/SERCA2; the interaction decreases ATP2A2
CC       Ca(2+) affinity (PubMed:28890335). Interacts with VMP1; VMP1 competes
CC       with PLN and SLN to prevent them from forming an inhibitory complex
CC       with ATP2A2 (PubMed:28890335). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P42532,
