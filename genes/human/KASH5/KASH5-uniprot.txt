CC   -!- FUNCTION: As a component of the LINC (LInker of Nucleoskeleton and
CC       Cytoskeleton) complex, involved in the connection between the nuclear
CC       lamina and the cytoskeleton. The nucleocytoplasmic interactions
CC       established by the LINC complex play an important role in the
CC       transmission of mechanical forces across the nuclear envelope and in
CC       nuclear movement and positioning. Required for telomere attachment to
CC       nuclear envelope in the prophase of meiosis (PubMed:35587281). Required
CC       for rapid telomere prophase movements implicating a SUN1/2:KASH5 LINC
CC       complex in which SUN1 and SUN2 seem to act at least partial
CC       redundantly. Required for homolog pairing during meiotic prophase in
CC       spermatocytes and probably oocytes. Essential for male and female
CC       gametogenesis (PubMed:35587281). Recruits cytoplasmic dynein to
CC       telomere attachment sites at the nuclear envelope in spermatocytes. In
CC       oocytes is involved in meiotic resumption and spindle formation.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q80VJ8, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35587281}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Core component the LINC complex which is composed of inner
CC       nuclear membrane SUN domain-containing proteins coupled to outer
CC       nuclear membrane KASH domain-containing nesprins. SUN and KASH domain-
CC       containing proteins seem to bind each other promiscuously; however,
CC       differentially expression of LINC complex constituents is giving rise
CC       to specific assemblies. At least SUN1/2-containing core LINC complexes
CC       are proposed to be hexameric composed of three protomers of each KASH
CC       and SUN domain-containing protein (By similarity). Interacts with SUN1;
CC       this interaction mediates its telomere localization by forming a
CC       SUN1:KASH5 LINC complex (PubMed:35708642). Component of a probable
CC       SUN2:KASH5 LINC complex. Self-associates. Interacts with DYNC1H1,
CC       DCTN1, DYNC1I1/2 and PAFAH1B1; suggesting the association with the
CC       dynein-dynactin motor complex (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q80VJ8, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35708642}.
