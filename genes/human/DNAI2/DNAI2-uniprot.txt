CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of dynein, a family of motor proteins essential for
CC       movement along microtubules (By similarity). Required for structural
CC       and functional integrity of cilia (By similarity). Part of the dynein
CC       complex of respiratory cilia. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q23FU1}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Dynein consists of at least two heavy chains and a number of
CC       intermediate and light chains. Part of the ciliary outer dynein arms
CC       (ODAs), at least consisting of dynein axonemal heavy chains, light
CC       chains and intermediate chains (By similarity). The ODAs interact with
CC       DNAAF9; this interaction inactivates the dyneins (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with DNAAF2 (By similarity). Interacts with DNAAF6/PIH1D3
CC       (PubMed:28176794). Interacts with HEATR2; probably involved in outer
CC       arm dynein assembly (PubMed:25232951). Interacts with CFAP53 (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with CFAP70 and DNAI1 (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A2AC93, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q23FU1,
