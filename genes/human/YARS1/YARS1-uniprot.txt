CC   -!- FUNCTION: Tyrosine--tRNA ligase that catalyzes the attachment of
CC       tyrosine to tRNA(Tyr) in a two-step reaction: tyrosine is first
CC       activated by ATP to form Tyr-AMP and then transferred to the acceptor
CC       end of tRNA(Tyr) (Probable) (PubMed:25533949). Also acts as a positive
CC       regulator of poly-ADP-ribosylation in the nucleus, independently of its
CC       tyrosine--tRNA ligase activity (PubMed:25533949). Activity is switched
CC       upon resveratrol-binding: resveratrol strongly inhibits the
CC       tyrosine--tRNA ligase activity and promotes relocalization to the
CC       nucleus, where YARS1 specifically stimulates the poly-ADP-
CC       ribosyltransferase activity of PARP1 (PubMed:25533949).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25533949, ECO:0000305|PubMed:16429158,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:9162081}.
