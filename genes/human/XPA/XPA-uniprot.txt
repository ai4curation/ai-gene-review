CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in DNA nucleotide excision repair (NER). Initiates
CC       repair by binding to damaged sites with various affinities, depending
CC       on the photoproduct and the transcriptional state of the region.
CC       Required for UV-induced CHEK1 phosphorylation and the recruitment of
CC       CEP164 to cyclobutane pyrimidine dimmers (CPD), sites of DNA damage
CC       after UV irradiation (PubMed:19197159). During NER stimulates the 5'-3'
CC       helicase activity of XPD/ERCC2 and the DNA translocase activity of
CC       XPB/ERCC3 (PubMed:31253769). Connects XPD/ERCC2 and XPB/ERCC3 during
CC       NER, retaining DNA near the XPB/ERCC3 active site, and stabilizing the
CC       complex in a different conformation than in transcribing TFIIH
CC       (PubMed:31253769). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19197159,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31253769}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with GPN1. Interacts with RPA1 and RPA2; the
CC       interaction is direct and associates XPA with the RPA complex.
CC       Interacts (via N-terminus) with CEP164 upon UV irradiation. Interacts
CC       with HERC2. During NER binds XPD/ERCC2 and XPB/ERCC3 of transcription
CC       factor IIH (TFIIH), bridging the 2 proteins (PubMed:31253769).
