CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of N-methyl-D-aspartate (NMDA) receptors (NMDARs)
CC       that function as heterotetrameric, ligand-gated cation channels with
CC       high calcium permeability and voltage-dependent block by Mg(2+)
CC       (PubMed:26875626, PubMed:27616483, PubMed:28126851, PubMed:9489750).
CC       Participates in synaptic plasticity for learning and memory formation
CC       (By similarity). Channel activation requires binding of the
CC       neurotransmitter L-glutamate to the GluN2 subunit, glycine or D-serine
CC       binding to the GluN1 subunit, plus membrane depolarization to eliminate
CC       channel inhibition by Mg(2+) (PubMed:26875626, PubMed:27616483,
CC       PubMed:28126851, PubMed:9489750). NMDARs mediate simultaneously the
CC       potassium efflux and the influx of calcium and sodium (By similarity).
CC       Each GluN2 subunit confers differential attributes to channel
CC       properties, including activation, deactivation and desensitization
CC       kinetics, pH sensitivity, Ca2(+) permeability, and binding to
CC       allosteric modulators (PubMed:26875626, PubMed:28095420,
CC       PubMed:28126851, PubMed:9489750). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P35438,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q14957, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26875626,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27616483, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28095420,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28126851, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9489750}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterotetramer. Forms heterotetrameric channels composed of
CC       two GluN1/zeta subunits (GRIN1), and two identical GluN2/epsilon
CC       subunits (GRIN2A, GRIN2B, GRIN2C or GRIN2D) or GluN3 subunits (GRIN3A
CC       or GRIN3B) (in vitro) (PubMed:26875626, PubMed:28126851,
CC       PubMed:36959261, PubMed:9489750). In vivo, the subunit composition may
CC       depend on the expression levels of the different subunits (Probable).
CC       Interacts with PDZ domains of PATJ and DLG4 (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26875626, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28126851,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36959261, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9489750, ECO:0000305}.

CC   -!- DISEASE: Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 46 (DEE46)
CC       [MIM:617162]: A form of epileptic encephalopathy, a heterogeneous group
CC       of severe early-onset epilepsies characterized by refractory seizures,
CC       neurodevelopmental impairment, and poor prognosis. Development is
CC       normal prior to seizure onset, after which cognitive and motor delays
CC       become apparent. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27616483}. Note=The disease is
CC       caused by variants affecting the gene represented in this entry.
