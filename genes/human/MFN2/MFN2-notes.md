# MFN2 notes

Deep research: not run (falcon times out in this environment; perplexity-lite unavailable). Review based on cached publications and UniProt.

- Fusion GTPase: [PMID:11181170 "Formation of mitochondrial filaments and networks required a wild-type Mfn2 GTPase domain"]; catalytic mutants inactive [PMID:28114303 "the corresponding Mfn2 mutants Mfn2E230A and Mfn2R259A, failed to elongate mitochondria"].
- ER-mitochondria tethering proposal [PMID:19052620 "Thus, mitofusin 2 tethers ER to mitochondria"]; contested in later literature, so used only as a MODIFY target (GO:0140474) on that paper's row, not as a core function.
- Parkin receptor [PMID:23620051 "A broad role for Mfn2 as Parkin receptor"].
- HSG anti-proliferative role is fusion-independent [PMID:15322553 "rHSG-induced anti-proliferation was independent of mitochondrial fusion"]; PASMC miR-17 study gives opposite sign (positive regulation of proliferation) - kept non-core.
- GO:0160204 (mitochondrion-mitochondrion outer membrane tether activity) would describe trans-tethering, but no MFN2-specific direct tethering assay is in the cached set; not asserted.
