## PMID:21044324 — actual full cache

Human HeLa, U2OS, HDF and SH-SY5Y experiments are distinguished from monkey COS-7 work. Three antibodies gave similar staining; only the N-terminal 217-2 antibody was optimized for endogenous immunoblotting. Peptide/preimmune specificity controls are reported, some data not shown. Interphase ASPM is predominantly nuclear, and this study did not detect an interphase centrosomal pool. After nuclear-envelope breakdown it forms a pole-proximal ring around, rather than extensively overlapping, gamma-tubulin. Late mitotic protein localizes at central-spindle minus ends and a narrow midbody ring.

Nocodazole removes spindle-associated ASPM while retaining gamma-tubulin; taxol produces ASPM-positive aster centers and separate ASPM-negative anastral gamma-tubulin foci. This supports microtubule-dependent localization and should not be converted into a constitutive centriolar-core claim.

Two independent U2OS siRNAs (ASP1 and ASP2) reduce spindle-pole ASPM, with different efficacy. The paper did not detect a matching decrease of total interphase protein. Live-cell orientation/cytokinesis analysis used ASP1 and GL3 control, pooling four experiments (94 and 126 divisions). The paper's word 'asymmetric' denotes geometric division orientation relative to the dish, not an independently measured daughter-cell fate. The cytokinesis deficit exceeds the effect of altered orientation alone. No new apoptosis-regulation process is inferred from downstream caspase activation.

Patient fibroblasts with IVS25+1G>T retain an apparently stable protein but show markedly reduced pole localization; cDNA reveals a cryptic splice event deleting a tripeptide at the C terminus. The body and one figure legend disagree about upstream/downstream wording; the sequenced body says upstream. Keep current canonical protein length rather than repeating the isolated 3447-aa typo. Human C-terminal D1–D3 fragments (3177–3477, 3256–3477 and 3315–3477) expressed in HeLa and COS-7 cause dominant-negative spindle/cytokinesis defects. These are fragments, not evidence that canonical isoform 2 has all the same effects.

The spindle-organization and spindle-positioning processes are supported by actual structural localization plus perturbations. The discussion offers several possible late-cytokinesis mechanisms; it does not establish a direct motor, actin-binding enzyme or cytokinesis kinase function for ASPM. Later PMID:28883092 provides a useful compensation context rather than a reason to erase these experimental annotations.

## PMID:23152892 — mouse donor evidence

[Original PLOS article](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0049303): complete Results through Figure 6 and targeted Methods read. Native mouse oocytes and mouse embryonic fibroblasts were studied. ASPM decorates the meiotic spindle and co-immunoprecipitates with calmodulin; the immunoprecipitation control is unrelated rabbit IgG. This is cellular association, not an isolated purified binary-binding assay. One ASPM morpholino reduces the measured protein signal and disrupts spindle morphology/progression; uninjected and control-morpholino controls are included. No rescue experiment is claimed. Spindle-checkpoint machinery is discussed as a hypothesis, not measured directly. These data support the mouse donor biology for existing human orthology transfers; they are not direct human oocyte assays.

This species boundary was independently checked. Endogenous human ASPM is recruited to newly generated minus ends in the cellular experiment. ## 2026-09-29 — PR3501 follow-up

The earlier final-synthesis paragraph records the initial publication. This follow-up retains all 19 original source assertions, the existing NEW ISS minus-end-binding assertion, both products and one core. The only action change is row 14 (zero-based): spindle localization becomes MODIFY with establishment of mitotic spindle orientation. Current decisions are 11 ACCEPT, 6 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, 1 MODIFY, 1 UNDECIDED and 1 NEW. All original nine literal supporting quotations and cached source bytes are preserved.

The [official GO:0000132 record](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0000132) defines mitotic spindle alignment relative to other cellular structures. Its parents include GO:0051294 establishment of spindle orientation and GO:0040001 establishment of mitotic spindle localization; the displayed ancestry reaches the original GO:0051653 spindle localization. The term is not human-specific: the human restriction belongs to the experiments. The original U2OS study measures alignment relative to the dish, and the human knockout/interface experiments in [PMID:28436967](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5458804/) corroborate mitotic misorientation. This is a refinement of the source assertion, not a second NEW process row.

The core now names ASPM's active outputs. Actual normal XML Results in PMID:28436967 show that purified short mouse ASPM with calmodulin slows minus-end growth and blocks outgrowth at higher concentrations, and that ASPM recruits katanin and enhances its severing activity. Mutation-based recruitment experiments separate this assistance from the severase chemistry supplied by katanin. The statement that other MAPs were not detected supports autonomous ASPM end tracking in that preparation; it does not convert the preparation into isolated human ASPM. Endogenous tagged human ASPM co-purifies with calmodulin and katanin and is recruited to newly exposed minus ends. Those human observations support the conserved mechanism without directly measuring isolated human minus-end growth inhibition. The complete primary Results paragraphs for these targeted experiments were reread; figure pixels, movies, raw spectra and complete supplementary datasets remain unread.

[GO:0031115](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0031115) denotes reduced microtubule polymerization; its parents are regulation terms, with an explicit negatively_regulates link to GO:0046785 microtubule polymerization. It is not an orientation ancestor or a synonym for depolymerization. The purified mouse growth-blocking experiment meets participation directly, not merely through necessity. Nevertheless, this bounded human follow-up retains the existing conserved binding inference and makes its outputs explicit in prose without adding a second NEW process assertion or another core BP. This is a conservative scope and species-inference boundary, not a claim that ASPM is passive or that ortholog inference is invalid.

For [PMID:27562601](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5048379/), the complete normal abstract and indexed original ASPM/CITK Results, Figure 4 legend and adjacent rescue/paclitaxel Results, plus cell-culture and transfection/RNAi/rescue Methods were read. The CITK rescue construct is mouse, and the later EB3 dynamics experiments primarily interrogate CITK. These are targeted human-cell data supporting the orientation refinement, not an isolated ASPM polymerase assay. The normal cache remains abstract-only and its availability flags remain accurate; external indexed reading is recorded separately.

The cytokinesis question is explicit. In PMID:21044324, failures also occur in geometrically symmetric divisions, and asymmetric division does not fully account for the excess failures. Thus cytokinesis is not dismissed as simply caused by misorientation. The Discussion presents alternative executing mechanisms involving central-spindle organization, astral microtubules or coupling to the cortex, without resolving which late-cytokinesis step ASPM directly performs. No NEW cytokinesis BP follows solely from depletion and localization.
