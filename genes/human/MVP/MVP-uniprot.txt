CC   -!- FUNCTION: Structural component of the vault particle, which is a
CC       massive ribonucleoprotein complex that may play a role in cargo
CC       transport and signal transduction (PubMed:40691181). Oligomerizes to
CC       form a cage-like vault complex, which can house several minor vault
CC       components including PARP4 (PubMed:40691181). Vaults may play a role in
CC       nucleo-cytoplasmic transport (PubMed:15133037). Down-regulates IFNG-
CC       mediated STAT1 signaling and subsequent activation of JAK
CC       (PubMed:16418217). Down-regulates SRC activity and signaling through
CC       MAP kinases (PubMed:16441665). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15133037,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16418217, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16441665,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40691181}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: The vault is a massive cage-like structure; it consists of a
CC       dimer of half-vaults, with each half-vault comprising 39 identical MVP
CC       subunits (PubMed:40691181). The two half-vaults assemble to form the
CC       complete vault, where minor vault components such as PARP4, TEP1 and
CC       vault RNAs (vRNAs) may bind to the interior (PubMed:40691181,
CC       PubMed:12177006, PubMed:16373071). Interacts (via MVP repeats 3 and 4)
CC       with PARP4; each MVP subunit binds a single PARP4 molecule, allowing
CC       each vault to house up to 78 PARP4 molecules (PubMed:40691181).
CC       Interacts (via MVP shoulder) with nucleotide ligands, including NAD+
CC       and ADP (PubMed:40691181). Interacts with TEP1 (PubMed:10551828).
CC       Interacts (via MVP 3) with PTEN and activated MAPK1 (PubMed:12177006).
CC       The phosphorylated protein interacts with the SH2 domains of PTPN11 and
CC       SRC (PubMed:15133037, PubMed:16441665). Interacts with APEX1
CC       (PubMed:18809583). May interact with ZNF540 (PubMed:16815308).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10551828, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12177006,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15133037, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16373071,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16441665, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16815308,
