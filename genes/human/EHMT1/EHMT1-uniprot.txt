CC   -!- FUNCTION: Histone methyltransferase that specifically mono-, di- and
CC       trimethylates 'Lys-9' of histone H3 (H3K9me1, H3K9me2 and H3K9me3,
CC       respectively) in euchromatin (PubMed:12004135). H3K9me represents a
CC       specific tag for epigenetic transcriptional repression by recruiting
CC       HP1 proteins to methylated histones (PubMed:12004135). Also weakly
CC       methylates 'Lys-27' of histone H3 (H3K27me) (PubMed:12004135). Also
CC       required for DNA methylation, the histone methyltransferase activity is
CC       not required for DNA methylation, suggesting that these 2 activities
CC       function independently (By similarity). Probably targeted to histone H3
CC       by different DNA-binding proteins like E2F6, MGA, MAX and/or DP1
CC       (PubMed:12004135). During G0 phase, it probably contributes to
CC       silencing of MYC- and E2F-responsive genes, suggesting a role in G0/G1
CC       transition in cell cycle (PubMed:12004135). Involved in the
CC       differentiation of myoblastic precursors into brown adipose cells:
CC       following recruitment to chromatin by PRDM16, mediates formation of
