Gene Description

CDKN2A encodes distinct tumor-suppressive proteins through alternative first exons and reading frames. The 156-residue p16INK4A protein (P42771) is an ankyrin-repeat inhibitor that binds CDK4 and CDK6, limits cyclin D-dependent kinase activity and restrains RB-dependent progression from G1 into S phase. Nuclear and cytoplasmic p16 pools contribute to control of proliferation and stable arrest in senescence contexts. The unrelated p14ARF protein (Q8N726-1) instead regulates the MDM2–p53 pathway. These proteins share a locus but do not share a primary sequence or interchangeable biochemical functions.

p14ARF SPLICE VARIANT
ID: CDKN2A_P14ARF
UNIPROT ISOFORM: Q8N726-1
The unrelated 132-residue alternative-reading-frame product regulates MDM2 and p53. PMID:9724636 establishes human ARF/MDM2-dependent regulation; PMID:35944929 demonstrates MDM2 ligase inhibition using engineered N32 ARF/GFP/MDM2 fusion constructs in pull-down and lysine-discharge experiments. The fragment/fusion assay is not an untagged full-length experiment, and the function is not transferred to smARF (Q8N726-2), p16 or other untested products. ARF–spinophilin association and the mouse ARF apoptosis experiments do not establish those activities for P42771.
