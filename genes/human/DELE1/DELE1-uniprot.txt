CC   -!- FUNCTION: Protein kinase activator that acts as a key activator of the
CC       integrated stress response (ISR) following various stresses, such as
CC       iron deficiency, mitochondrial stress or mitochondrial DNA breaks
CC       (PubMed:32132706, PubMed:32132707, PubMed:35388015, PubMed:37327776,
CC       PubMed:37550454, PubMed:37832546, PubMed:38340717). Detects impaired
CC       protein import and processing in mitochondria, activating the ISR
CC       (PubMed:35388015). May also required for the induction of death
CC       receptor-mediated apoptosis through the regulation of caspase
CC       activation (PubMed:20563667). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20563667,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32132706, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32132707,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35388015, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37327776,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37550454, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37832546,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38340717}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [DAP3-binding cell death enhancer 1]: Protein kinase
CC       activator that activates the ISR in response to iron deficiency: iron
CC       deficiency impairs mitochondrial import, promoting DELE1 localization
CC       at the mitochondrial surface, where it binds and activates EIF2AK1/HRI
CC       to trigger the ISR. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37327776}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [DAP3-binding cell death enhancer 1 short form]: Protein
CC       kinase activator generated by protein cleavage in response to
CC       mitochondrial stress, which accumulates in the cytosol and specifically
CC       binds to and activates the protein kinase activity of EIF2AK1/HRI
CC       (PubMed:32132706, PubMed:32132707, PubMed:37327776, PubMed:37550454,
CC       PubMed:37832546, PubMed:38340717). It thereby activates the integrated
CC       stress response (ISR): EIF2AK1/HRI activation promotes eIF-2-alpha
CC       (EIF2S1) phosphorylation, leading to a decrease in global protein
CC       synthesis and the induction of selected genes, including the
CC       transcription factor ATF4, the master transcriptional regulator of the
CC       ISR (PubMed:32132706, PubMed:32132707, PubMed:37327776,
CC       PubMed:37550454, PubMed:37832546). Also acts as an activator of PRKN-
CC       independent mitophagy: activates the protein kinase activity of
CC       EIF2AK1/HRI in response to mitochondrial damage, promoting eIF-2-alpha
CC       (EIF2S1) phosphorylation, leading to mitochondrial localization of
CC       EIF2S1 followed by induction of mitophagy (PubMed:38340717).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32132706, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32132707,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37327776, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37550454,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37832546, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38340717}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with DAP3. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20563667}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: [DAP3-binding cell death enhancer 1]: Interacts (via TPR
CC       repeats) with EIF2AK1/HRI; activating the protein kinase activity of
CC       EIF2AK1/HRI, thereby promoting the integrated stress response (ISR).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37327776}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: [DAP3-binding cell death enhancer 1 short form]: Homooctamer;
CC       oligomerization is required to activate EIF2AK1/HRI (PubMed:37550454).
CC       Interacts (via TPR repeats) with EIF2AK1/HRI; activating the protein
CC       kinase activity of EIF2AK1/HRI, thereby promoting the integrated stress
CC       response (ISR) (PubMed:32132706, PubMed:32132707, PubMed:37550454,
CC       PubMed:38340717). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32132706,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32132707, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37550454,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38340717}.

CC   -!- PTM: [DAP3-binding cell death enhancer 1]: Unstable protein in absence
CC       of stress: imported in the mitochondrial matrix following processing by
CC       the mitochondrial-processing peptidase (MPP), where it is degraded by
CC       LONP1 (PubMed:37327776). Stabilized in response to iron deficiency:
CC       iron deficiency impairs mitochondrial import, promoting localization at
CC       the mitochondrial surface and stabilization (PubMed:37327776). Cleaved
CC       by OMA1 in response to mitochondrial stress, generating the DAP3-
CC       binding cell death enhancer 1 short form (DELE1(S) or S-DELE1) that
CC       accumulates in the cytosol and activates the protein kinase activity of
CC       EIF2AK1/HRI (PubMed:32132706, PubMed:32132707, PubMed:37327776,
CC       PubMed:38340717). Protein cleavage by OMA1 can take place at different
CC       positions, and apparently does not require a specific sequence motif
CC       (PubMed:32132707). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32132706,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32132707, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37327776,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38340717}.
CC   -!- PTM: [DAP3-binding cell death enhancer 1 short form]: Ubiquitinated and
CC       degraded by the SIFI complex once the mitochondrial stress has been
CC       resolved, thereby providing stress response silencing
CC       (PubMed:38297121). Within the SIFI complex, UBR4 initiates ubiquitin
CC       chain that are further elongated or branched by KCMF1
CC       (PubMed:38297121). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38297121}.
