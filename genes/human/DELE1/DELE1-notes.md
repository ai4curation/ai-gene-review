# DELE1 notes

Deep research: not run (falcon/perplexity-lite unavailable). Review based on cached publications and UniProt.

- OMA1 cleaves DELE1; short form activates HRI in cytosol [PMID:32132706 "stress-induced activation of OMA1 causes DELE1 to be cleaved into a short form that accumulates in the cytosol, where it binds to and activates HRI via its C-terminal portion."]
- Associated with inner membrane before cleavage [PMID:32132707 "DELE1, a little-characterized protein that we found was associated with the inner mitochondrial membrane."]
- Iron deficiency: import arrest, full-length DELE1 on mitochondrial surface activates HRI; degraded by LONP1 at steady state [PMID:37327776].
- DELE1-CTD oligomerizes [PMID:37550454]; HRI branch drives mitophagy [PMID:38340717].
- No GO:0034514 on DELE1 in GOA; not added — the established triggers are depolarization/import failure and iron deficiency, not shown to be matrix protein misfolding specifically.
