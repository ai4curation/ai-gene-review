CC   -!- FUNCTION: Regulatory subunit of anion-selective CLCNKA:BSND and
CC       CLCNKB:BSND heteromeric channels involved in basolateral chloride
CC       conductance along the nephron to achieve urine concentration and
CC       maintain systemic acid-base homeostasis, and in the stria vascularis of
CC       the inner ear to establish the endocochlear potential necessary for
CC       normal hearing (PubMed:11734858, PubMed:12111250, PubMed:12574213,
CC       PubMed:16849430, PubMed:18776122, PubMed:19646679, PubMed:20538786,
CC       PubMed:26013830). Most likely acts as a chaperone that allosterically
CC       regulates proper sorting of CLCNKA:BSND and CLCNKB:BSND channels at the
CC       basolateral plasma membrane domain and functional switch to ion
CC       conducting state. Mediates constitutive opening of channel common gates
CC       (PubMed:11734858, PubMed:12111250, PubMed:12574213, PubMed:16849430,
CC       PubMed:18776122, PubMed:19646679, PubMed:20538786, PubMed:26013830).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11734858, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12111250,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12574213, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16849430,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18776122, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19646679,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20538786, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26013830}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with CLCNK channels. Forms heteromers with CLCNKA in
CC       the thin ascending limb of Henle and with CLCNKB in the thick ascending
CC       limb and more distal segments. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12111250}.
