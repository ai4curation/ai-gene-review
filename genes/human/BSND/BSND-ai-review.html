Gene Description

Barttin is a small integral membrane accessory subunit of the ClC-Ka and ClC-Kb chloride channels, encoded by BSND. It associates with these pore-forming subunits and controls channel activation, surface abundance and polarized membrane sorting. Functional ClC-K/barttin complexes support renal chloride reabsorption and the ionic environment required for inner-ear hearing. Wild-type human barttin preferentially sorts basolaterally in polarized epithelial expression models; pathogenic variants can impair conductance, trafficking or sorting through distinct mechanisms. Loss of barttin function causes Bartter syndrome with sensorineural deafness.
