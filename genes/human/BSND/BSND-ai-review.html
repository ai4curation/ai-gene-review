Gene Description

Barttin, encoded by BSND, is an integral membrane accessory subunit of the ClC-Ka and ClC-Kb chloride channels. It associates with these pore-forming subunits and regulates channel activation, surface abundance and polarized membrane sorting. Barttin modifies cooperative channel gating, favoring chloride conductance at physiological potentials; its palmitoylation at Cys54 and Cys56 supports efficient activation. Trafficking and activation can be separated experimentally and by disease-associated variants. ClC-K/barttin complexes support renal chloride reabsorption and potassium recycling in the inner ear. Wild-type human barttin preferentially sorts basolaterally in polarized epithelial models. Loss of barttin function causes Bartter syndrome with sensorineural deafness, while some hypomorphic variants predominantly impair hearing.
