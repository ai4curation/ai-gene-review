CC   -!- FUNCTION: Essential for normal spermatogenesis and male fertility.
CC       Required for normal sperm head morphology, acroplaxome formation,
CC       acrosome attachment, and acrosome granule stability. May anchor and
CC       stabilize acrosomal adherence to the acroplaxome at least in part by
CC       facilitating the presence of F-actin in the subacrosomal space (By
CC       similarity). May play an important role in formation and fusion of
CC       Golgi-derived vesicles during acrosome biogenesis (PubMed:32923619).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9QY84, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32923619}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via N-terminus) with TES (via LIM domain 2).
CC       Heterodimer with TES; the heterodimer interacts with ENAH to form a
CC       heterotrimer (PubMed:21278383). Interacts with ACTL9 (PubMed:33626338).
CC       Interacts with CYLC1; the interaction may be relevant for proper
CC       acrosome attachment to the nuclear envelope (PubMed:38573307).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21278383, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33626338,
