CC   -!- FUNCTION: Essential component of the OST-alpha/OST-beta complex, a
CC       heterotetramer that acts as the intestinal basolateral transporter
CC       responsible for bile acid export from enterocytes into portal blood
CC       (PubMed:16317684, PubMed:41606314, PubMed:41606328). The OST-alpha/OST-
CC       beta complex efficiently transports the major species of bile acids
CC       (taurocholate) (PubMed:16317684, PubMed:41606314, PubMed:41606328).
CC       Taurine conjugates are transported more efficiently across the
CC       basolateral membrane than glycine-conjugated bile acids (By
CC       similarity). In the complex, SLC51A/OST-alpha adopts a G protein-
CC       coupled receptor fold and constitutes a bile acid recognition site
CC       (PubMed:41606314, PubMed:41606328). Can also transport steroids such as
CC       estrone 3-sulfate and dehydroepiandrosterone 3-sulfate, therefore
CC       playing a role in the enterohepatic circulation of sterols
CC       (PubMed:16317684). Able to transport eicosanoids such as prostaglandin
CC       E2 (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R000,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q90YM5, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16317684,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:41606314, ECO:0000269|PubMed:41606328}.

CC   -!- DOMAIN: In the complex, two SLC51A/OST-alpha units form the homodimeric
CC       core, with two SLC51B/OST-beta units bound to the periphery
CC       (PubMed:41606314, PubMed:41606328). SLC51A/OST-alpha adopts a G
CC       protein-coupled receptor fold and contains a unique cysteine-rich loop
CC       with seven palmitoylation sites to constitute a bile acid recognition
CC       site (PubMed:41606314, PubMed:41606328). A positive cavity in
CC       SLC51A/OST-alpha connects the bile acid site and facilitates the
CC       transmembrane translocation of bile acids through SLC51B/OST-beta
CC       (PubMed:41606314, PubMed:41606328). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:41606314,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:41606328}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The cysteine-rich loop is highly palmitoylated.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:41606314, ECO:0000269|PubMed:41606328}.
CC   -!- PTM: Palmitoylation of the cysteine-rich loop is required to form a
CC       lateral substrate-binding groove for recognition of bile acids.
