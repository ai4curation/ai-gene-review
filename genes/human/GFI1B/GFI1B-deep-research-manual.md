# GFI1B: selected primary evidence

GFI1B recognizes regulatory DNA and recruits transcriptional corepressors. The evidence below separates human natural products, engineered reagents and murine experiments; it does not assign every perturbation phenotype a direct molecular role.

## Repression, promoter context and products

[PMID:22399799](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22399799/) distinguishes human p32 and p37 in erythroid systems. The short-product depletion and comparative recruitment experiments support hematopoietic repression. The p37 overexpression condition also increases endogenous p32. Synthetic methylated peptides and K8A mutants test recruitment requirements; they do not quantify endogenous Lys8 modification. The observed p39 band has no identified transcript.

[PMID:9566867](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9566867/) uses murine cDNA and mouse cells. Its p21 repression and inhibition of IL6-induced arrest have the opposite sign from the incoming negative-G1/S assertion. [PMID:12351384](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12351384/) also uses murine cDNA, including in human recipient cells. Its artificial consensus-site reporter and SOCS1 promoter have different responses; activation cannot be generalized across promoters.

## FOG1 partner discrepancy

The [original PMID:15920471 article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/sj.emboj.7600702), Results accompanying Figure 1D, states: “FOG‐1 antibodies immunoprecipitated GATA‐1 but not TAL‐1, Ldb1 or Gfi‐1b”. This is a reported mouse MEL assay, not a universal no-interaction claim. Its positive GATA1/Gfi1b complex is distinct. The literal incoming Q8IX07 accession identifies FOG1/ZFPM1 and remains unchanged pending a source-context check. The local publication cache contains the abstract; this statement comes from the independently accessed original.

## Human DNA-binding reagent

The [original supplemental workbook for PMID:28473536](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/files/S-EPMC8009048/NIHMS1670407-supplement-Supplemental_tables_S1-6.xlsx) supplies the following derived target index, using one-based Excel row numbers:

| Target | Protein row | Displayed-sequence coordinates | Motif model rows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GFI1B | S1:785 | residues 148–330 | S2:7543/7548 |

The 183-residue extended DNA-binding reagent exactly matches positions 148–330 of the 330-residue product. It is neither the full-length p37 protein nor p32. S2 contains methylated and unmethylated selection models; no GFI1B-specific S3 classification or native promoter occupancy is inferred. Methods describe recombinant bacterial protein selection on random DNA. The more specific regulatory-DNA judgment also uses independent promoter evidence and the actual ancestral assertion.

## Positive interaction assays and partner classes

[PMID:16713569](https://barabasi.com/media/pub_imports/files/190.pdf) reports the inherited-ataxia interaction screen. Six exact positive IntAct records preserve the NELL2, CPAP/CENPJ and JAG2 pairs in Y2H and HEK293T pull-down assays. CPAP records [EBI-952822](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/interaction/EBI-952822) and [EBI-946647](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/intact/interaction/EBI-946647) supply pair-specific evidence; the study-wide validation rate does not. The 1–330 target feature is not a clone-sequence or isoform crosswalk. [CPAP FUNCTION](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9HC77/entry) establishes its microtubule-regulatory class, matching [GO:0005519](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005519). The signaling terms [GO:0038023](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0038023) and [GO:0048018](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0048018) instead require signal transmission or a change in receptor activity; binding NELL2 or putative ligand JAG2 demonstrates neither. None of these assays establishes endogenous colocalization or a cytoskeletal role for GFI1B.

## Phylogenetic scope

The asserted PAINT book is [PTHR24393](https://data.pantherdb.org/PANTHER19.0/books/PTHR24393/tree.mia), while the current UniProt cross-reference is PTHR23226:SF419. Both are retained as different source assertions. The actual 330-residue target AN43/PTN002550783 descends from the four-function AN0/PTN002811836 node and the hematopoiesis AN29/PTN008616677 node. Donor count and target self-evidence do not weaken those assertions. No replacement family assignment is made.

## GATA1 binding and lineage regulation

[PMID:16177182](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16177182/), Methods “Plasmid constructs and site-directed mutagenesis” and Results accompanying Figures 6 and 8, tests human GFI1B with tagged mouse GATA1 in 293T cells and GATA1-dependent recruitment in human K562 cells. The positive cellular association supports transcription-factor binding independently of the retained FOG1 source query. Discussion also reports no interaction in the tested yeast two-hybrid system; purified direct affinity is not established.

The human p32 experiments in PMID:22399799 support erythroid regulation. [PMID:23927492](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23927492/) and [PMID:24325358](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24325358/) describe altered transcriptional activity and abnormal megakaryocyte development or marker expression in human GFI1B disease contexts; only their abstracts were inspected. These observations refine the conserved hemopoiesis assertion to two regulatory lineages, without creating a new ancestral node or assigning both products identical roles.
