# GFI1B research notes

## 2026-11-05: evidence synthesis

GFI1B is primarily a sequence-specific transcriptional repressor with a promoter-dependent activation context. Human p32 and p37 are distinct natural products; the shorter sequence deletes displayed residues 171–216. The p39 band in PMID:22399799 has no defined transcript. Historical constructs, bacterial DNA-binding fragments and SNAG mutants are retained as experimental reagents.

The incoming FOG1 partner needs a source-context check: Q8IX07 is ZFPM1, and PMID:15920471 separates the tested FOG1 complex from GATA1/Gfi1b. The cell-cycle term also requires a directional check because PMID:9566867 reports prevention of arrest. These are two different source issues; no partner is silently replaced and no opposite human process is added.

The asserted PTHR24393 ancestry was read directly, with the current PTHR23226 UniProt cross-reference retained separately. Context-dependent activation and legitimate self-evidence remain represented. Nuclear-matrix association is non-core; broad transcription regulation is redundant with its supported directional descendants. Positive generic interaction observations remain recorded even where their uninformative term is removed.

Selected original articles, direct product sequences and the original SELEX workbook are indexed in the manual evidence page and source ledger. Abstract-only access is distinguished from externally accessed original text. No new process or natural product is inferred from disease necessity alone.

## 2026-11-05: transcription-factor binding and lineage refinement

Human GFI1B association with GATA1 and GATA1-dependent promoter recruitment support transcription-factor binding independently of the questionable FOG1 partner field (PMID:16177182; PMID:15920471). The FOG1 source query is retained. Human p32 perturbation and the megakaryocyte disease studies support more precise regulation of erythrocyte and megakaryocyte differentiation (PMID:22399799; PMID:23927492; PMID:24325358), with product and assay limits preserved.

The CPAP association remains a measured partner-class binding function outside the repression core. Its positive Y2H and mammalian pull-down records differ from an inference that binding a ligand makes GFI1B a receptor. Neither physiological colocalization nor a role in centriole regulation is established. The p37 and p32 entries now use functional-class mappings without adding products or product-exclusive GO terms.
