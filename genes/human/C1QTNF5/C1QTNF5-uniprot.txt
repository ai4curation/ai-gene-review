CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in cellular energy homeostasis through the
CC       regulation of 5'-AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) phosphorylation
CC       and activation (PubMed:19651784, PubMed:34887495). In myocytes, it
CC       induces adiponectin receptors-independent AMPK phosphorylation and
CC       activation, thereby increasing the phosphorylation of acetyl-CoA
CC       carboxylase (ACC) and stimulating fatty acid oxidation and glucose
CC       uptake (PubMed:19651784). In the retinal pigment epithelium (RPE), it
CC       acts as antagonist of adiponectin receptor protein 1 (ADIPOR1) and
CC       negatively regulates ADIPOR1-dependent AMPK activation
CC       (PubMed:34887495). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19651784,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34887495}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotrimer (via collagen-like domain) (PubMed:22892318,
CC       PubMed:24531000). Can form higher order oligomers, such as bouquet-like
CC       octadecamers, by supercoiling of the trimers (PubMed:22892318,
CC       PubMed:24531000). Interacts with ERFE/C1QTNF15 (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with MFRP (PubMed:17122143, PubMed:24531000). In the retinal
CC       pigment epithelium (RPE), interacts with ADIPOR1; the interaction down-
CC       regulates ADIPOR1 activity and subsequent AMPK activation
CC       (PubMed:34887495). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8K479,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17122143, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22892318,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24531000, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34887495}.
