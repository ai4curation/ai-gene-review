Gene Description

CEP290 (NPHP6) is a very large (~290 kDa) coiled-coil protein of the centrosome, centriolar satellites and proximal ciliary transition zone. At the transition zone it binds axonemal microtubules directly through a central myosin-tail-homology region and the ciliary membrane through an N-terminal amphipathic helix, so it can bridge the two in the Y-link region. It organizes recruitment of MKS-module proteins to the TZ downstream of RPGRIP1L/MKS5, and it is part of the ciliary gate that controls membrane-protein entry and exit. In centriolar satellites it works with PCM1, CEP131 and CCDC66. CP110 restrains its ciliogenic activity, and it is needed for ciliary vesicle maturation, Rab8a recruitment and BBSome integrity together with NPHP5. Mutations cause Leber congenital amaurosis, Joubert, Senior-Loken and Meckel-Gruber syndromes and nephronophthisis.

Core Functions

CEP290 is a structural component of the proximal ciliary transition zone. It binds microtubules directly through its central myosin-tail-homology region and membranes through an N-terminal amphipathic helix, which allows it to bridge the axoneme and ciliary membrane at the Y-links. It assembles MKS-module proteins at the TZ downstream of RPGRIP1L/MKS5 and acts in the ciliary gate that controls membrane-protein entry and exit.
