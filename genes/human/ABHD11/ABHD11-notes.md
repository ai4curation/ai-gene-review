# Curation Notes for Human `ABHD11` (`UniProtKB:Q8NFV4`)

## 1. Gene Identity, Genomic Context, and Sequence Coordinate Crosswalk

- **Gene / Accession**: Human `ABHD11` (synonyms `WBSCR21`, `PP1226`), `UniProtKB:Q8NFV4` (`ABHDB_HUMAN`, `sv=2`, 306 aa canonical isoform `Q8NFV4-1`), `PANTHER:PTHR46118:SF4` (`PROTEIN ABHD11`).
- **Chromosomal locus**: 7q11.23 within the Williams-Beuren syndrome (WBS) hemizygously deleted critical region (`PMID:12073013` Merla et al. 2002, "Here, we report the characterization of WBSCR16, WBSCR17, WBSCR18, WBSCR20A, WBSCR20B, WBSCR20C, WBSCR21, WBSCR22, and WBSCR23, nine novel genes contained in the WBS commonly deleted region or its flanking sequences."). No single-gene Mendelian disease is caused by isolated `ABHD11` loss; WBS arises from contiguous multigene 7q11.23 deletion.

## 2. Primary Physiological Mechanism: Mitochondrial Matrix Glutaryl-Lipoyl Thioesterase Maintaining OGDHc and 2-Oxoglutarate Metabolism

1. **Subcellular localization and OGDHc association (`PMID:32792488`, `PMID:34800366`, `PMID:32579589`)**:
   - Morgenstern et al. 2021 (`PMID:34800366`, MitoCoP) independently classifies endogenous human ABHD11 in the high-confidence mitochondrial proteome.
   - Bailey et al. 2020 (`PMID:32792488`) demonstrated by confocal colocalization with MitoTracker and OGDH, mitochondrial Proteinase K protection in mitoplasts, HA-affinity co-immunoprecipitation, and mass spectrometry that ABHD11 resides in the **mitochondrial matrix** (`GO:0005759`) and physically associates with the **2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase complex** (`GO:0045252`, subunits OGDH and DLST).
2. **Catalytic mechanism — glutaryl-lipoyl thioesterase (`PMID:32792488`, `PMID:40664791`, `PMID:41184294`)**:
   - Bailey et al. 2020 (`PMID:32792488`) showed that genetic depletion of `ABHD11` or covalent active-site inhibition with ML226 (`PMID:23658947`) selectively abolishes functional lipoylation on K110 of OGDHc-E2 (`DLST`) without reducing total OGDH/DLST/DLD abundance or impairing lipoic acid synthesis (`LIAS`/`LIPT1`/`LIPT2`), causing rapid accumulation of 2-oxoglutarate (`2-OG`), reduction of `2-OG` to L-2-hydroxyglutarate (`L-2-HG`), inhibition of 2-OG-dependent dioxygenases (PHDs/HIF-1alpha hydroxylation and TET DNA 5hmC hydroxymethylation) in cancer cells, and reduced mitochondrial respiration. Reconstitution requires catalytically intact ABHD11 (`S141A`/`Ser132Ala` and `H296A`/`His287Ala` fail to rescue DLST lipoylation or p-nitrophenyl acetate hydrolysis).
   - Grice et al. 2025 (`PMID:40664791`) resolved the endogenous chemical reaction by LC-MS/MS and synthetic peptide enzymology: glutaryl-lipoyl thioester adducts (`Lpglu`) form continuously on the reactive thiols of lipoylated K110 of OGDHc-E2 (`DLST`), and purified human ABHD11 acts directly as a **glutaryl-lipoyl thioesterase** (`GO:0016790` `thiolester hydrolase activity`), hydrolyzing the S-glutaryl-lipoyl thioester bond to release free glutarate and regenerate catalytically competent dihydrolipoamide on DLST (blocked by ML226). Crucially, ABHD11 does **not** cleave lysine-glutaryl amides (`Kglu`, a SIRT5 substrate), glutaryl-CoA, oleoyl-CoA, or arachidonoyl-CoA (`PMID:27544863`, `PMID:40664791`).
   - In primary human and mouse T cells (`PMID:40664791`, `PMID:41184294`), ABHD11 inhibition impairs OGDHc/TCA cycle flux without accumulating `2-HG`, rewiring reductive carbon flux into unsaturated glycerolipids and 24,25-epoxycholesterol/LXR signaling, skewing human CD8+ T cells toward central memory (`TCM`) and dampening effector cytokine production.
   - **In-repo `GO:0006103` comparator check**: Curated `genes/human/LIAS/LIAS-ai-review.yaml` carries `GO:0009249` (*protein lipoylation*) and does not carry `GO:0006103` (`genes/human/LIPT1/` contains only `LIPT1-uniprot.txt` without GOA or an `-ai-review.yaml`—likewise listing `GO:0009249` without `GO:0006103`—and `LIPT2` is not yet in-repo), whereas curated `genes/human/DLST/DLST-ai-review.yaml` and `genes/human/DLD/DLD-ai-review.yaml` carry `GO:0006103` (accepted on `DLST` and refined to descendant `GO:0120551` on `DLD`) alongside `GO:0006099` and `GO:0045252`. Because `ABHD11` physically resides in OGDHc (`part_of` `GO:0045252`) to continuously repair catalytic `S8`-glutaryl-lipoamide adducts rather than installing lipoate de novo, `NEW` `IMP` `GO:0006103` aligns with OGDHc complex curation.

## 3. Critical Evaluation of Generic Esterase vs Claimed Diacylglycerol/Triacylglycerol Lipase Annotations

- **Generic carboxylic ester hydrolysis (`GO:0052689`)**: Purified full-length human ABHD11 hydrolyzes short-chain chromogenic carboxylic esters (*p*-nitrophenyl butyrate `pNPC4` in `PMID:27544863` and *p*-nitrophenyl acetate in `PMID:32792488` and `PMID:40664791`) in a `Ser132`-dependent, ML226-sensitive manner. This is a genuine active-site esterase side-activity (`KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` on the `IBA` row for `GO:0052689`), whereas the physiological substrate is the S-glutaryl-lipoyl thioester on OGDHc (`NEW` `IDA` for `GO:0016790` `thiolester hydrolase activity`).
- **Refutation of physiological diacylglycerol / lipid-hydrolase claims (`GO:0016298`, `GO:0120516`, `GO:0006629`)**:
  - Navia-Paldanius et al. 2016 (`PMID:27544863`, "Our fluorescent activity assays designed for natural lipase substrates revealed no activity of hABHD11 towards mono- or diacylglycerols.") directly tested natural monoacylglycerol and diacylglycerol substrates on human ABHD11 and detected **zero** activity.
  - Escoubet et al. 2020 (`PMID:32579589`) reported in vitro hydrolysis of 1-stearoyl-2-arachidonoyl-*sn*-glycerol to 2-arachidonoylglycerol (`2-AG`) using an N-terminally truncated recombinant construct (`37..306`), which prompted UniProt to rename `Q8NFV4` "sn-1-specific diacylglycerol lipase ABHD11" (`EC 3.1.1.116`) and project `RHEA:33275`/`RHEA:38507`/`RHEA:48596` (`GO:0120516` IEA). However, Escoubet et al.'s own in vivo lipidomics demonstrated that `Abhd11` knockout mice have **wild-type endogenous 2-AG levels** across tissues (`PMID:32579589`, "In vivo preclinical studies demonstrated that knock-out ABHD11 mice have a similar 2AG level as WT mice and exhibit a lean metabolic phenotype."), and changes in glycerolipid saturation upon ABHD11 loss in cells are secondary consequences of blocked OGDHc flux driving reductive glutamine carboxylation (`PMID:32792488`, `PMID:40664791`, `PMID:41184294`). Per `projects/ENZYME_SPECIFICITY.md`, `GO:0016298` (`IDA`), `GO:0120516` (`IEA`), and `GO:0006629` (`IBA`) are all `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED`, with an upstream `UNIPROT` `WRONG_TERM` `source_defects` entry on `GO:0120516`.
  - All 22 `GO:0005515` (`protein binding`) IPI rows from HuRI (`PMID:32296183`) on isoform `Q8NFV4-4` represent unverified yeast two-hybrid binary hits with non-mitochondrial nuclear/cytosolic/endosomal prey proteins and are `REMOVE` per repository protein-binding policy.
