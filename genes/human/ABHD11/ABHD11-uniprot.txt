CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the hydrolysis of diacylglycerol in vitro and may
CC       function as a key regulator in lipid metabolism, namely by regulating
CC       the intracellular levels of diacylglycerol (PubMed:32579589). 1,2-
CC       diacyl-sn-glycerols are the preferred substrate over 1,3-diacyl-sn-
CC       glycerols (By similarity). The enzyme hydrolyzes stearate in preference
CC       to palmitate from the sn-1 position of 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerols (By
CC       similarity). Maintains the functional lipoylation of the 2-oxoglutarate
CC       dehydrogenase complex (OGDHc) through its interaction with the OGDHc by
CC       preventing the formation of lipoyl adducts (PubMed:32792488). In
CC       addition, is also required for the expansion and differentiation of
CC       embryonic stem cells (ESCs) (By similarity).
