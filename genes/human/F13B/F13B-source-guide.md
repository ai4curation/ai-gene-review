# F13B sources and biological boundaries

F13B encodes the noncatalytic B subunit of plasma factor XIII. The evidence separates secretion and assembly of the B subunit from transglutaminase chemistry performed by F13A1. B-containing A2B2 is the circulating proenzyme assembly; the activated A-chain complex is a different state.

## Human B secretion and assembly

[Thomas et al., PMID:26247044](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4521963/) provides the most directly inspected B-specific experiments. Wild-type and mutant pEZ-MO1-FXIIIB constructs were expressed in HEK293T cells. The investigators collected cleared extracellular medium and lysates, measured B antigen, and added recombinant A2 to secreted material to measure reconstituted A2B2 antigen relative to B antigen. Methods “Cell culture, transfection, and protein expression and quantification” and “Expression and reconstitution study,” Results of the same names and Figures 1/3 captions were read. These are secretion and assembly assays, not an assay of B catalysis. The confocal ER/Golgi experiments concern biosynthetic transit and mutant retention. Figure captions and their reported quantitative results were read; pixels and supplementary images were not inspected. The genuine normal cache contains complete main text; a new XML request used an unacceptable response type and failed, without affecting that available original.

[PMID:2334637](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2141.1990.tb02585.x) is available here only as its original clinical abstract. It reports familial human B deficiency, reduced plasma A and a short disappearance half-life of infused A, while platelet A remained normal. This is positive carrier/stability evidence with uninspected full Methods. It is not an experiment showing B makes fibrin cross-links.

## The 2016 A-chain experiment and component-level evidence

The full [PMID:27363989 article and supplement](https://doi.org/10.1002/humu.23041) were recovered in an [official Bonn thesis](https://bonndoc.ulb.uni-bonn.de/xmlui/bitstream/handle/20.500.11811/7478/4941.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y). Its reproduced article begins with “Coagulation Factor XIIIA Subunit Missense Mutations Affect Structure and Function at the Various Steps of Factor XIII Action.” The inspected Methods express sequence-verified human F13A1 constructs in COS-1 cells. Results examine A-chain activity, alpha-2-antiplasmin incorporation, fibrin cross-linking and clot properties. The supplementary alpha-2-antiplasmin assay uses commercial A2B2 (Fibrogammin) as a positive reference preparation. It does not isolate B-chain chemistry. The main Introduction describes B as the protective carrier and describes its release during activation. Main text, selected captions and supplementary assay Methods were read; no figure pixels or unrelated thesis chapters were inspected.

The current [ComplexPortal CPX-6231](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/complexportal/complex/CPX-6231) explicitly identifies two P00488 and two P05160 subunits, with F13A1 designated as enzyme and the overall complex evidence labeled curator-inferred. Its “XIIIa” name/function wording must not erase the inactive-versus-activated assembly distinction. Whole-complex positive controls may legitimately support component-level process participation. Absence of an isolated B perturbation does not establish an evidence-code error. The imported F13B clot-formation IDA is retained with its explicit complex-level scope, and the unsupported wrong-evidence-code defect record is removed. The B participation and A2B2 association remain supported independently. No source code, term or normal record is rewritten, and no upstream report has been submitted.

## Core synthesis and the molecular-function boundary

The one core describes noncatalytic B-dependent assembly and maintenance of the plasma A-subunit pool. It uses the already supported [fibrin-clot formation process](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0072378), extracellular region and broad transferase-complex context. Blood coagulation is an ancestor of that process, so it is not repeated in the same core. The complex label applies to precursor assembly, not persistent B membership in the activated A-plus-calcium state. No protein-glutamine transferase activity is assigned to B, even with a contributes_to qualifier.

The decisive B-specific original is [PMID:26247044](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4521963/): Methods on cell culture/expression and expression/reconstitution; Results on secreted antigen and heterotetramer assembly; Figures 1 and 3 caption text. Those passages were independently read for synthesis. Added recombinant A2 and secreted B are measured by heterotetramer antigen relative to total B; this demonstrates assembly rather than a catalytic assay or a measured delivery route. The clinical and donor-rescue sources remain abstract-only. The earlier 2016 A-subunit original and actual Reactome component objects preserve the A-versus-B and activation-state distinction. No new original acquisition or image-pixel read occurred in this phase.

## Protein stabilization: explicit assessment

The current [GO:0050821 protein stabilization](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0050821) definition concerns maintaining protein structure and integrity and preventing degradation or aggregation. Its direct parent is [GO:0031647 regulation of protein stability](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0031647). It is not an ancestor or descendant of either blood coagulation or fibrin-clot formation, so redundancy would not prohibit a justified annotation. It is a biological-process term and would not itself supply the omitted molecular-function slot.

F13B is a plausible performer, not the substrate of the proposed stabilization: its binding/assembly contribution acts on A. However, the directly inspected human [reconstitution experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4521963/) measures A2B2 antigen relative to B antigen (Methods “Expression and reconstitution study,” corresponding Results and Figure 3 caption). The [human deficiency abstract](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2141.1990.tb02585.x) measures disappearance of infused A, and the [human-B rescue in mice](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12185-007-0005-z) reports plasma A abundance and cross-linking. The complete Methods of those historical studies remain unavailable. These observations establish the positive maintenance role but do not distinguish clearance of intact A from protection of A against degradation or aggregation. A NEW assertion of GO:0050821 is therefore deferred on this evidence, without asserting that the function is absent.

The comparator check does not justify automatic gap filling. At this observation, exact-term/descendant QuickGO queries returned no GO:0050821 annotation for human [VWF/P04275](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P04275/entry), the circulating factor-VIII protective partner, or [IGFALS/P35858](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P35858/entry), the IGF-complex subunit associated with prolonged circulating half-life. [AHSP/Q9NZD4](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9NZD4/entry) is a useful positive contrast: it carries the term and prevents alpha-hemoglobin aggregation/precipitation. The first two are closer plasma-persistence comparators; the third has a directly different anti-aggregation mechanism. Annotation absence does not prove a prohibition, but it prevents inferring that all protective circulating partners routinely receive the term. The pinned repository GO-CAM index and cached models contain no P05160/F13B entry; that finite search supplies neither a contrary modeled role nor evidence for a new assertion.
