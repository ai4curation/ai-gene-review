## 2026-10-25 — Structural contribution to the A2B2 precursor

The broad molecular function [structural molecule activity, GO:0005198](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005198) is now assigned specifically to the B-containing precursor complex. The current definition concerns structural integrity. In the human B-expression/reconstitution experiment, fixed recombinant A2 and secreted wild-type or mutant B produced an A2B2 antigen endpoint normalized to B antigen; several B mutants selectively reduced assembly. That target-specific complex-assembly phenotype supports a broad structural contribution, while leaving the precise stabilizing mechanism unresolved. A NEW IMP annotation records the mutant evidence and the core names the same molecular function. [PMID:26247044](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4521963/), Methods “Cell culture, transfection, and protein expression and quantification” and “Expression and reconstitution study,” corresponding Results and Figure 3 caption.

This does not assign transglutaminase activity to B or place it in the activated A-plus-calcium complex. It also does not resolve the previously deferred protein-stabilization mechanism. A broad structural contribution and a specific degradation/aggregation or dissociation-prevention mechanism have different evidence requirements.
