Gene Description

CHST6 encodes corneal N-acetylglucosamine 6-O-sulfotransferase, a type II Golgi membrane enzyme that transfers sulfate from PAPS to the 6-hydroxyl group of GlcNAc in glycan acceptors. This reaction contributes to keratan sulfate chain biosynthesis and the sulfation pattern of corneal proteoglycans. The human enzyme can also sulfate mucin glycans in a heterologous expression assay. Biallelic loss of CHST6 function or cornea-specific expression causes macular corneal dystrophy.
