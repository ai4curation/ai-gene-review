# PDYN Curation Notes (Human Proenkephalin-B / Prodynorphin, UniProtKB:P01213)

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Gene symbol / ID**: `PDYN` (`UniProtKB:P01213`, `HGNC:8820`)
- **PANTHER family**: `PANTHER:PTHR11438:SF4` (`PROENKEPHALIN-B`, within `PANTHER:PTHR11438` `PROENKEPHALIN`; root PAINT node `PANTHER:PTN000153716`, clade node `PANTHER:PTN002606008`).
- **Preprohormone processing**: Synthesized in striatonigral medium spiny neurons, hippocampal dentate gyrus granule cells (mossy fibers), hypothalamic magnocellular neurons, and spinal cord dorsal horn neurons as a 254-amino-acid polyprotein precursor (signal peptide residues 1-20) packaged into neuronal dense-core vesicles in axon terminals and dendrites [PMID:10098940]. Cleaved at dibasic and monobasic sites by prohormone convertases (PC1, PC2) and carboxypeptidase E into multiple bioactive opioid peptides bearing an N-terminal Leu-enkephalin (`YGGFL`) core followed by basic C-terminal address extensions:
  - **Alpha-neoendorphin** (residues 175-184) and **beta-neoendorphin** (residues 175-183)
  - **Dynorphin A(1-17)** (residues 207-223), **Dynorphin A(1-13)** (residues 207-219), and **Dynorphin A(1-8)** (residues 207-214)
  - **Leumorphin** / **Dynorphin B-29** (residues 226-254) and **Dynorphin B** / **rimorphin** (residues 226-238)
  - **Leu-enkephalin** (`YGGFL`, cleaved from the N-termini of neoendorphin, dynorphin A, and dynorphin B).
- **Signaling mechanism**: Mature dynorphin and neoendorphin peptides function as secreted opioid neuropeptide hormones (`GO:0001515` *opioid peptide activity*, child of `GO:0005184` *neuropeptide hormone activity* and `GO:0048018` *receptor ligand activity*) that act preferentially and with subnanomolar affinity as the natural endogenous ligands for the kappa-type opioid receptor (`OPRK1` / KOR; and with lower selectivity `OPRM1` and `OPRD1`) (`GO:0031628` *opioid receptor binding*, `GO:0038003` *G protein-coupled opioid receptor signaling pathway*) [PMID:9047294]. Agonist-bound `OPRK1` couples to pertussis-toxin-sensitive inhibitory Gi/o heterotrimeric G proteins (`GNAI1`/`GNAI2`/`GNAI3`) to inhibit adenylate cyclase (`GO:0031635` *adenylate cyclase-inhibiting opioid receptor signaling pathway*), open GIRK potassium channels, and inhibit voltage-gated Ca2+ channels, thereby suppressing presynaptic glutamate and dopamine release (`GO:0007270` *neuron-neuron synaptic transmission*) and modulating pain perception (`GO:0019233`) and stress/aversion circuits [PMID:9047294, PMID:10098940, Reactome:R-HSA-373076].

## Ligands in Signaling Pathways Assessment (Tranche 4C)
- Unlike `PENK` (which carries a TAS annotation for `GO:0038003` *G protein-coupled opioid receptor signaling pathway*), human `PDYN` lacks **both** `GO:0038003` (*G protein-coupled opioid receptor signaling pathway*) and `GO:0031635` (*adenylate cyclase-inhibiting opioid receptor signaling pathway*) in GOA (Category A gap!), carrying only the parent `GO:0007218` (*neuropeptide signaling pathway*, IBA from `PTN000153716` and IEA).
- We `MODIFY` both rows of `GO:0007218` (*neuropeptide signaling pathway*) to `GO:0038003` (*G protein-coupled opioid receptor signaling pathway*) and `GO:0031635` (*adenylate cyclase-inhibiting opioid receptor signaling pathway*), and add `NEW` annotations for `GO:0038003` (`IDA`, backed by [PMID:9047294]) and `GO:0031635` (`TAS`, backed by `Reactome:R-HSA-373076` and [PMID:9047294]), linking `PDYN` to `MODULE:opioid_receptor_signaling`.
