# CARD11: receptor-responsive signaling assembly

CARD11 (CARMA1) organizes intracellular signaling in lymphocytes. Its best-supported molecular function is signaling adaptor activity: activated CARD11 nucleates BCL10 assembly and recruits MALT1 and ubiquitin-signaling components, connecting antigen receptors to canonical NF-kappaB. The central mechanistic observation is physical organization of a functional complex, not only loss of a downstream response after perturbation. The 2013 reconstitution describes **“substoichiometric CARMA1”** (PMID:24074955, *Structural architecture of the CARMA1/Bcl10/MALT1 signalosome*), while the HOIP study explains **“bringing substrate to enzyme”** (PMID:27777308, *Molecular Determinants of Scaffold-induced Linear Ubiquitinylation*). CARD11 supplies the organizing platform; HOIP, MALT1 and upstream kinases supply their respective catalytic activities.

The review retains one integrated adaptor core. CARD-domain recognition, self-association, regulated membrane association and recruitment of BCL10/MALT1/HOIP are parts of that operation. Existing canonical NF-kappaB and TCR signaling assertions have direct mechanistic support. The broader immune, IL-2, costimulatory and activation outcomes remain contextual functions. No new annotation is proposed, and no comparator-based gap is presumed.

## Assembly and construct boundaries

The 2013 study uses purified human CARMA1 and BCL10 fragments and reconstituted MALT1-containing assemblies. CARMA1 seeds the BCL10 filament at substoichiometric concentrations; cotransfected cellular complexes and endogenous lymphoma complexes support the assembly model. CARMA1/BCL10 interface modeling is distinguished from the resolved BCL10 filament. Constructs and preparations differ in oligomer size, so the review does not assert one obligatory native full-length stoichiometry.

The 2019 work provides direct CARD11 evidence despite its CARD9-focused title. Purified CARD11 constructs are human sequences; the mammalian full-length and inhibitory-domain-deletion CARD11 reporter constructs use mouse residues 1–1159. CARD9 supplies the high-resolution structures. Human CARD11 filament-containing fractions template BCL10, whereas small oligomer fractions are much less effective. The paper refines the earlier small-oligomer interpretation; both support self-association and nucleation, without proving that a resolved CARD9 architecture is an atomic structure of human CARD11 (PMID:31296852).

## Locations and cellular outputs

CARMA1 is reported to be **“constitutively associated with lipid rafts”** (PMID:12154360, *CARMA1 is a critical lipid raft-associated regulator*). The CD26 study independently places a CARD11-containing complex at rafts following costimulation (PMID:17287217). Broad cytoplasm is compatible with intracellular membrane association and does not mean an exclusively free soluble pool. Reactome-derived cytosol annotations retain the curated GOA compartment. The complete cached reaction summaries provide pathway context, but most do not contain a separate cytosol field; they cannot independently resolve the balance of soluble and membrane-associated protein.

The localization study PMID:14673152 supplies direct recruitment evidence in human Jurkat-derived JPM50.6 cells conjugated to SEE-presenting Raji cells. Tagged wild-type CARMA1 accumulates at the contact, whereas L808P is defective. The same study shows that the contact still forms in CARMA1-deficient cells: signaling assembly and synapse formation are distinct claims. Human cDNA sequence comparisons and reconstitution experiments are explicit in the normal cached main text. Its complete main Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion were read; figure placeholders do not supply the omitted captions or images. The exact original mouse ortholog donor experiment was not reconstructed.

The human atopy study supports a contextual TORC1 signaling assignment. Human CARD11 constructs in Jurkat cells and patient lymphocytes show reduced phospho-S6; patient cells have impaired ASCT2 upregulation, and glutamine partly restores the phospho-S6 response. Some cellular activation responses improve under combined cytokine and glutamine conditions. The direct CARD11–ASCT2 coupling mechanism remains unresolved, and these culture experiments are not a clinical supplementation trial. CARD11 is not assigned glutamine transport or TOR kinase activity (PMID:28628108).

## Human disease contexts

Three directly cited primary studies distinguish different germline mechanisms. Activating heterozygous variants produce constitutive CARD11/NF-kappaB signaling and BENTA; the original study includes four affected people, patient-cell phenotypes and mutant reconstitution in lymphocyte models (PMID:23129749). Increased B-cell output and transformation mechanisms are partly interpretive; the paper does not establish inevitable malignancy. Biallelic loss of function is supported by the human homozygous truncation and reconstitution study of severe combined immunodeficiency (PMID:23561803, abstract-only). Heterozygous hypomorphic variants in the atopy study interfere with wild-type signaling, distinguished from simple haploinsufficiency through coexpression and null-variant controls (PMID:28628108). These disease mechanisms concern particular variants and do not turn disease outcomes into new GO functions.

For the BENTA study, selected unique Results, Discussion and Methods were read, including patient genetic evidence, CARD11 aggregation/NF-kappaB assays and primary human T-cell transfections. The biallelic-deficiency source provides a complete abstract with explicit functional reconstitution but no recovered full Methods or images. No supplementary clinical tables or original images were inspected for either study.

## Binding decisions and uncertainties

The twenty partner-specific binding records retain every original accession, including CK1alpha P48729-1, CBLB Q13191-1 and the mouse Map4k1 partner P70218. Independent target evidence supports refining BCL10 associations to the existing CARD domain binding term. Region-mapped associations with CK1alpha, HPK1 and PKC-delta support protein kinase binding, without kinase catalysis or generic kinase activation. The HOIP substrate–enzyme corecruitment experiment supports the existing signaling adaptor activity. MALT1 co-complex association is not automatically an independently demonstrated binary CARD11–MALT1 protease-binding interface. The CD26-tail and CBLB associations remain supported generic non-core interactions.

This follows the [published project-specific standing binding policy](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/5c7ad2560590ffc6ed5ebe701ebff240510bc0ca/projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md): retain supported generic binding as non-core when a finer activity is not established. The original fragmentomics target rows for PTEN and RSK1 remain uncertain because the exact target constructs and measurements were not recovered. A CARD11 PDZ domain recognizing a partner peptide must not be relabeled as CARD11 binding a partner PDZ domain.

GMP kinase activity remains unresolved. The official activity requires ATP-dependent phosphorylation of GMP to GDP. The original abstract describes a GUK domain **“with homology to guanylate kinase”** (PMID:11278692), but no target nucleotide-turnover experiment was recovered. Domain architecture neither proves catalysis nor proves its absence. The two derived nucleotide-metabolism annotations therefore share the uncertainty. Likewise, a CARD-to-apoptosis domain mapping needs an actual target apoptotic endpoint; transformed-cell viability or NF-kappaB signaling does not alone identify the mode of death. The neural-stem-cell exosome assignment remains uncertain because the target peptide/table was not inspected, not because CARD11 was absent from the paper's abstract.
