CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms a heterodimer with SLC3A2 that functions as a sodium-
CC       independent transporter for large neutral amino acids (PubMed:9751058,
CC       PubMed:10049700, PubMed:11557028, PubMed:10574970, PubMed:11564694,
CC       PubMed:12117417, PubMed:12225859, PubMed:15769744, PubMed:18262359,
CC       PubMed:25998567, PubMed:30867591). Functions as an obligatory amino
CC       acid antiporter, mediating the exchange of amino acids and amino acid-
CC       related compounds across the plasma membrane (PubMed:11557028,
CC       PubMed:12117417, PubMed:12225859, PubMed:11847106, PubMed:17896864).
CC       Also mediates sodium-independent exchange of iodothyronines with
CC       neutral and aromatic L-alpha-amino acids (PubMed:11564694,
CC       PubMed:12225859). Mediates exchange of L-tryptophan with the tryptophan
CC       metabolites L-kynurenine and 3-hydroxy-L-kynurenine (PubMed:17896864).
CC       Mediates cellular uptake of iodothyronines, including thyroxine (T4),
CC       triiodothyronine (T3), reverse triiodothyronine (rT3) and 3,3'-diiodo-
CC       L-thyronine (3,3'-T2), and iodotyrosines including 3-iodo-L-tyrosine
CC       (MIT) and 3,5-diiodo-L-tyrosine (DIT) (PubMed:26305885,
CC       PubMed:38661522). Involved in the uptake of methylmercury (MeHg) when
CC       administered as L-cysteine or D,L-homocysteine complexes, thereby
CC       contributing to metal ion homeostasis and toxicity (PubMed:12117417).
CC       Involved in the cellular activity of low-molecular-weight nitrosothiols
CC       through stereoselective transport of L-nitrosocysteine (L-CNSO) across
CC       the plasma membrane (PubMed:15769744). When associated with LAPTM4B,
CC       the heterodimer is recruited to lysosomes, where it promotes leucine
CC       uptake into these organelles and thereby contributes to mTORC1
CC       activation (PubMed:25998567). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10049700,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10574970, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11557028,
