CC   -!- FUNCTION: Assembly factor that mediates the formation of some
CC       mitochondrial respiratory supercomplexes (respirasomes), thereby
CC       promoting oxidative phosphorylation and energy metabolism
CC       (PubMed:27545886, PubMed:30428348, PubMed:33727070, PubMed:36198313).
CC       Acts as a molecular adapter that associates with both mitochondrial
CC       respiratory complexes III (CIII) and IV (CIV), promoting their
CC       association (PubMed:27545886, PubMed:36198313). Mediates the formation
CC       of various mitochondrial respiratory supercomplexes, such as
CC       MCIII(2)IV(2), composed of two CIII and two CIV, and the CS-respirasome
CC       (MCI(1)III(2)IV(2)), composed of one CI, two CIII and two CIV
CC       (PubMed:27545886, PubMed:30428348). Not involved in the formation of
CC       the canonical respirasome (MCI(1)III(2)IV(1)), composed of one CI, two
CC       CIII and one CIV (By similarity). The formation of different
CC       respirasomes is important for cell adaptation to oxygen conditions and
CC       prevent metabolic exhaustion: supercomplexes mediated by COX7A2L/SCAF1
CC       are required to maintain oxidative phosphorylation upon low oxygen
CC       conditions and promote metabolic rewiring toward glycolysis
CC       (PubMed:36198313). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q99KD6,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27545886, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30428348,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33727070, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36198313}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the mitochondrial respiratory complexes III
CC       (CIII) and IV (CIV), promoting their association.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27545886}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion inner membrane
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:27545886}; Single-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q99KD6}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: By estrogen (PubMed:9418891). Expression is induced by ATF4
CC       downstream of the EIF2AK3/PERK-mediated unfolded protein response,
CC       thereby increasing formation of respiratory chain supercomplexes
CC       (PubMed:31023583). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31023583,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9418891}.
CC   -!- POLYMORPHISM: Genetic variations affect COX7A2L expression in human
CC       population (PubMed:36253618). The rs10183278 variant leads to increased
CC       COX7A2L expression in the muscle and is associated with lower body fat
CC       and improved cardiorespiratory fitness (PubMed:36253618). Myotubes
CC       harboring this insertion display more mitochondrial respiratory
CC       supercomplexes and increased respiration (PubMed:36253618). The genetic
CC       variant rs10183278 is characterized by an insertion in the 3'
CC       untranslated region of COX7A2L transcripts, which specifically promotes
CC       mRNA stability and expression (PubMed:36253618).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36253618}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the cytochrome c oxidase VIIa family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
