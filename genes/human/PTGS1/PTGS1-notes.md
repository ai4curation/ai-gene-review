# PTGS1 notes

PTGS1/COX-1 is the constitutive human prostaglandin G/H synthase in PTHR11903:SF6. Human recombinant PGHS-1 and PGHS-2 purified from baculovirus had both cyclooxygenase and peroxidase activities (PMID:7947975), and immunoelectron microscopy places PGHS-1 and PGHS-2 on the lumenal surfaces of the ER and nuclear envelope (PMID:9545330). PTHR11903 PAINT activity/process terms at PTN000935189 are correct for PTGS1/PTGS2, while the generic cytoplasm row is less informative than ER and nuclear-envelope membrane localization.
