# SMIM15 review notes

## Identity

SMIM15 (previous symbol C5orf43; UniProt Q7Z3B0, SIM15_HUMAN) is a 74-residue (8.6 kDa)
protein. UniProt: predicted helical TM segment at residues 20-40 (ECO:0000255); predicted
coiled coil 42-74; residues 55-74 disordered with a lysine/arginine-rich stretch. Common
variant L23F (rs4546328) lies in the TM helix. Pfam PF15086 (UPF0542), PANTHER PTHR28644
(SMIM15 family). UniProt subcellular location "Membrane; Single-pass membrane protein" is
curator inference (ECO:0000305). PE level 1.

## Literature search (2026-10-03)

signature in AML, a diet/atherosclerosis transcriptome study in primates, and a camelid

protein function, localisation or interactions exists. The cancer/expression papers
anticipated in the brief are of this signature-list type and carry no functional
