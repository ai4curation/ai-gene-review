Gene Description

Caveolin-3 is the muscle-enriched caveolin isoform and an oligomeric component of the caveolar coat at the sarcolemma. Together with cavins, it organizes membrane microdomains and coordinates recruitment and trafficking of selected proteins. Its interactions with dysferlin and MG53 connect caveolar organization to muscle membrane repair. Channel-specific assemblies regulate calcium currents and potassium-channel surface turnover, including recruitment of NEDD4-2 to hERG. Disruption of caveolin-3 abundance or trafficking can impair muscle membrane organization and cause caveolinopathy.

Channel mechanisms and physiological limits

The potassium-channel study combines heterologous hERG experiments with native rat/rabbit association and neonatal rat depletion.
