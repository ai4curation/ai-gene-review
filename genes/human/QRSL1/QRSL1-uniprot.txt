CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic subunit of the mitochondrial Glu-tRNA(Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex GatCAB (Probable) (PubMed:19805282). GatCAB
CC       allows the formation of correctly charged Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the
CC       transamidation of misacylated Glu-tRNA(Gln) in the mitochondria. The
CC       reaction takes place in the presence of glutamine and ATP through an
CC       activated gamma-phospho-Glu-tRNA(Gln) (By similarity). This subunit
CC       probably catalyzes the hydrolysis of the amido donor Gln, releasing
CC       ammonia that is sequestered within a channel in the GatCAB complex and
CC       made available to subunit B for downstream aminolysis of Glu-tRNA(Gln)
CC       (Probable). Required for the stability of GATB (By similarity).
