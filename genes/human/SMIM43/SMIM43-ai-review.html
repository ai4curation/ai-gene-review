Gene Description

SMIM43 (NEMEP, Nodal enhanced mesendoderm micropeptide; formerly annotated as TMEM155) is a 63-amino-acid microprotein with a single N-terminal transmembrane helix, translated from an upstream open reading frame in a transcript previously regarded as non-coding or as encoding a larger, unconfirmed 130-residue protein. In mouse, the orthologous transcript (Gm11549) is a direct target of Nodal/Activin signaling, and the micropeptide localizes to the plasma membrane, where it binds the class I facilitative glucose transporters GLUT1 (SLC2A1) and GLUT3 (SLC2A3) through its transmembrane segment and a short C-terminal motif (His51-Phe57). NEMEP increases cellular glucose uptake and glycolytic flux in a binding-dependent manner, and in mouse embryonic stem cells it is required for mesendoderm differentiation, apparently by supplying the increased glucose uptake that this transition requires. The human protein is translated, shares about 94% identity with the mouse peptide, binds human GLUT1 and GLUT3, and raises glucose consumption and glycolysis when overexpressed in human hepatoma cells; its role in human development has not been examined.

Core Functions

Plasma membrane microprotein that binds the facilitative glucose transporters GLUT1 (SLC2A1) and GLUT3 (SLC2A3) via its single transmembrane helix and a C-terminal H51-F57 motif and enhances their glucose uptake, increasing glycolytic flux. In mouse ES cells this Nodal-induced boost of glucose import supports mesendoderm differentiation.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does NEMEP change the intrinsic kinetics (Vmax, Km) of GLUT1/GLUT3, or does it act by increasing their surface abundance or stability? This decides between transporter activator activity and a trafficking/stabilization role.

Q: Is SMIM43 expressed and required during human gastrulation-stage differentiation (e.g. hESC-derived mesendoderm), and is it Nodal/Activin-responsive in human cells?

Q: What is the function of TMEM155/SMIM43 upregulation reported in immune-cell transcriptomic studies (acute HIV infection PBMCs), given that previous annotations were based on an incorrect 130-residue ORF?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Reconstitute GLUT1 or GLUT3 in proteoliposomes with and without synthetic NEMEP (WT versus H51-F57 deletion) and measure glucose transport kinetics.

Type: in vitro reconstitution transport assay

Experiment: Knock out SMIM43 in human ES cells and assay 2-deoxyglucose uptake and mesendoderm marker induction after Activin A treatment, together with surface biotinylation of GLUT1/GLUT3.

Type: CRISPR knockout in hESCs

Decisions

  • Plasma membrane: ACCEPT (both rows).
  • Transmembrane transporter binding (IPI x2, human proteins): ACCEPT; IEA ACCEPT.
  • GO:0010828: MODIFY -> GO:0046326 positive regulation of D-glucose import across plasma
    membrane (uptake of glucose/2-DG into cells is what was measured).
  • Mesendoderm development: KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. Mouse-only necessity evidence; NEMEP acts by
    supplying glucose uptake (metabolic support), not as a developmental regulator per se.
    Not tested in human cells.
  • NEW MF GO:0141109 transporter activator activity: proposed. Binds GLUT1/3 and increases
    their transport activity in a binding-dependent manner. Comparator (QuickGO, 2026-09-30): the SERCA-regulating
    microproteins carry regulator-type MFs on their pump partner: DWORF P0DN84 GO:0008047
    enzyme activator activity (ISS); SLN O00631 and PLN P26678 GO:0004857 enzyme inhibitor
    activity (ISS). For a non-enzymatic transporter partner the analogous term is GO:0141109.
    Caveat: authors say "likely"; no reconstituted/proteoliposome assay.
