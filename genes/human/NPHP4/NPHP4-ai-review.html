Gene Description

NPHP4 (nephrocystin-4, nephroretinin) is a large, conserved, soluble protein built from repeated C2-like and immunoglobulin-like domains. It lies at the distal ciliary transition zone close to the membrane and at the basal body, centrosome and epithelial cell-cell junctions. NPHP4 is the central member of the NPHP1-4-8 (NPHP) module: it binds NPHP1 directly and links it to RPGRIP1L. In Chlamydomonas, NPHP4 is an essential part of the transition-zone barrier that controls the membrane and soluble protein composition of the cilium. In C. elegans, NPHP-4 acts redundantly with the MKS module to attach the basal body and transition zone to the membrane and to build the ciliary gate. At junctions, NPHP4 supports timely tight-junction formation and epithelial morphogenesis. Through Inturned and DAAM1 it organizes the subapical actin network of multiciliated cells. It also regulates signaling: it stabilizes JADE1 and moves it to the nucleus to inhibit canonical Wnt signaling, and it controls PYK2-dependent phosphorylation of NPHP1. NPHP4 mutations cause nephronophthisis type 4 and Senior-Loken syndrome.
