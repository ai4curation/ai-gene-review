Gene Description

CHRNA4 encodes the alpha4 subunit of neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptors. Alpha4 commonly assembles with beta2 into pentameric acetylcholine-gated cation channels, contributing ligand-recognition interfaces and the shared transmembrane pore. Receptor activation depolarizes neurons and can modulate presynaptic transmitter release. The two major alpha4beta2 stoichiometries differ in agonist sensitivity, conductance and calcium permeability; accessory subunits further alter receptor behavior. Alpha4-containing receptors occur in somatodendritic and presynaptic compartments and respond to nicotine. Pathogenic pore-domain variants can alter receptor behavior and cause autosomal dominant sleep-related hypermotor epilepsy. A recent study also reports ventricular alpha4beta2 receptor activity in rodent models and pharmacological modulation of conduction in explanted failing human hearts.
