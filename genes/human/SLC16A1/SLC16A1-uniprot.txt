CC   -!- FUNCTION: Bidirectional proton-coupled monocarboxylate transporter
CC       (PubMed:12946269, PubMed:32946811, PubMed:33333023). Catalyzes the
CC       rapid transport across the plasma membrane of many monocarboxylates
CC       such as lactate, pyruvate, acetate and the ketone bodies acetoacetate
CC       and beta-hydroxybutyrate, and thus contributes to the maintenance of
CC       intracellular pH (PubMed:12946269, PubMed:33333023). The transport
CC       direction is determined by the proton motive force and the
CC       concentration gradient of the substrate monocarboxylate. MCT1 is a
CC       major lactate exporter (By similarity). Plays a role in cellular
CC       responses to a high-fat diet by modulating the cellular levels of
CC       lactate and pyruvate that contribute to the regulation of central
CC       metabolic pathways and insulin secretion, with concomitant effects on
CC       plasma insulin levels and blood glucose homeostasis (By similarity).
CC       Facilitates the protonated monocarboxylate form of succinate export,
CC       that its transient protonation upon muscle cell acidification in
CC       exercising muscle and ischemic heart (PubMed:32946811). Functions via
CC       alternate outward- and inward-open conformation states. Protonation and
CC       deprotonation of 309-Asp is essential for the conformational transition
CC       (PubMed:33333023). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P53986,
