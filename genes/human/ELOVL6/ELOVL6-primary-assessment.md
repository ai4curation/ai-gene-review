## Chemistry and pathway scope

GO:0042761 defines a very-long-chain fatty acid by an aliphatic tail containing more than 22 carbons; GO:0035338 and GO:0042759 use 13-22 tail carbons for long-chain species. Papers conventionally report total chain carbon numbers. C18 alone does not establish a VLCFA product; C24 clearly qualifies. An enzyme can perform an upstream synthesis step without catalyzing the terminal step. Reactome R-HSA-75876 includes the C16-to-C18 cycle in the route that continues to longer products; its own >20-carbon prose is not substituted for the GO boundary. GO:0036109 currently contains a chemical typo (omega-6/C18H32O2 for alpha-linolenic acid). The intended ALA route is omega-3, 18:3, as independently described in Reactome R-HSA-2046106. Source term ID and label are preserved.

## ELOVL6-specific scope

PMID:25003994 full text and [Figure 2D](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/figure/image?size=large&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0101823.g002), image SHA256 `90adb941576b4dbc3f7e8972206799c84e88992086a71f4e456ec6501b598b2d`, show purified proteoliposome-reconstituted ELOVL6 overwhelmingly preferring C16:0. Other substrate bars are near baseline; the figure does not establish an all-conditions negative for the weak PUFA activities detected in the 2010 membrane assay. KAR co-reconstitution stimulates condensation even without KAR catalysis; active KAR plus NADPH gives additional stimulation. The authors discuss the lack of definitive physical-interaction evidence and possible detergent-micelle artifacts. Thus functional membership in the elongation machinery is supported without claiming a resolved binary interface or stoichiometry.

PMID:31529722 full manuscript was read from [Tsukuba](https://tsukuba.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/56034/files/HEP_71-5-1609.pdf), SHA256 `1a3f35d415d51660e710e96372f1235f24bae047b2f28cbeb3aa5f8efd95f46f`. Figure 5 and its Results describe Elovl6-CerS4 colocalization and co-immunoprecipitation in HEK293 cells, alongside mouse liver lipidomics identifying the C18:0 ceramide pool. The study concerns mouse Elovl6 physiology and supports ortholog-conserved sphingolipid precursor synthesis; it does not show human ELOVL6 catalyzing CerS4's amide-bond reaction.

PMID:26214738 full text and Extended Data Figure 1 show human ELOVL6 rescue of fly dElovl6 lethality and establish the C16:0/C18:0 pathway. Fly mitochondrial-outer-membrane localization is not silently transferred to the human protein. PMID:26628376 studies mouse thermogenesis: ELOVL6-dependent lipid remodeling supports brown-adipose adaptation, with mitochondrial lipid composition and TFR1 signaling offered as mechanisms whose relative contributions remain unresolved. The tissue-specific process remains non-core, without treating lack of direct human assays as refutation.

IntAct HuRI records verify HMOX1, LCP2 and RTP2 pairings. The PMID:20937905 partner Q96G23 is CERS2, correcting the earlier CERS6 description. An empty current IntAct query for that paper does not negate the full-paper co-immunoprecipitation finding.
