Gene Description

SIX6 (OPTX2) is a SIX/sine oculis family homeodomain transcription factor of the Six3/6 (Optix) subfamily, closely related to SIX3 and encoded in the SIX1-SIX4-SIX6 cluster on chromosome 14q23. It is expressed in the developing and adult neural retina, optic stalk/optic nerve region, ventral hypothalamus and pituitary. Through its Six domain and homeodomain it binds cis-regulatory DNA (for example the Shh SBE2 forebrain enhancer) and acts mainly as a transcriptional repressor, recruiting Groucho/TLE (TLE1, AES/TLE5) and Dachshund corepressors via its Six domain. In mouse, Six6-Dach complexes directly repress cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor genes such as p27Kip1, expanding early retinal and pituitary progenitor pools; Six6 loss causes retinal and pituitary hypoplasia, and with Six3 it contributes to eye-field specification. In humans, 14q22-q23 deletions encompassing SIX6 are associated with bilateral anophthalmia and pituitary anomalies, homozygous truncation causes complex microphthalmia with optic disc anomalies and retinal dystrophy, and common coding variants (e.g. Asn141His) at the SIX1/SIX6 locus are associated with optic nerve cup-to-disc ratio and primary open-angle glaucoma.

Biological roles / disease

  • Retinal and pituitary progenitor proliferation PMID:12130660.
  • Eye field specification with Six3 PMID:12441302.
  • 14q22-q23 deletions with SIX6 hemizygosity: anophthalmia + pituitary anomalies PMID:10512683; but no SIX6
    point mutations found in 173 MAC patients PMID:15505031. Homozygous truncation -> complex microphthalmia (ODRMD) [PMID:23167593; abstract unavailable].
  • POAG / cup-disc ratio association; zebrafish six6a knockdown reduces optic nerve volume; Asn141His and Leu205Arg hypomorphic PMID:24875647.
