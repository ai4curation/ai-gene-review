Gene Description

GNA14 encodes G alpha 14, a Gq-family heterotrimeric G protein alpha subunit that couples selected receptors to phospholipase C beta signaling. Its nucleotide-binding and helical domains cooperate in effector recognition, and receptor-dependent activation generates intracellular inositol phosphate signals. G14 participates in selected chemokine and inflammatory receptor pathways, with coupling distinct from the broader expression patterns of Gq and G11. Native tissue-specific receptor and effector combinations remain less extensively characterized than heterologous cellular coupling.
