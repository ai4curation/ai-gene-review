# GNB3 (G protein beta-3) notes

## Identity
Human GNB3, UniProt P16520, WD-repeat G protein beta family. Widely expressed; in the
retina it is the beta subunit of cone transducin and of the ON-bipolar Go heterotrimer.

## Retina (mostly mouse)
- Cone transducin is Galpha-t2/Gbeta3/Ggamma-t2 [PMID:23516284 "a heterotrimeric G-protein, cone transducin, comprising Gαt2, Gβ3 and Gγt2 subunits"].
- Gnb3-/- cones: Galpha-t2 and Ggamma-t2 reduced 4-6 fold in the outer segment, responses with normal kinetics but ~4-fold lower sensitivity; main role is to set transducin levels [PMID:23516284 "These results suggest that the main role of Gβ3 in cones is to establish optimal levels of transducin heteromer in the outer segment"].
- ON-bipolar cells: mGluR6 activates Galpha-o/Gbeta3/Ggamma13; Gbeta-gamma dialysis closes TRPM1 [PMID:22586107; dimer identity not GNB3-specific]; TRPM1 co-immunoprecipitates with Gbeta3 and both Galpha-o and Gbeta-gamma close the channel (mouse rod bipolar cells) [PMID:26883481].
- Human: biallelic GNB3 mutations cause autosomal-recessive CSNB with ON-bipolar dysfunction and reduced cone sensitivity [PMID:27063057].

## Non-retinal rows
- C825T splice variant (GNB3-s) association with hypertension [PMID:9425898]: a genetic association, and GTPgammaS binding of the heterotrimer, not GTPase activity of beta.
- Human GNB3 BAC transgenic mice (overexpression of the risk allele) gain adiposity and metabolic syndrome [PMID:29206867]; source of many IGI regulation rows. Indirect overexpression phenotypes; marked over-annotated. NOT rows (feeding, locomotion) report null phenotypes of that same overexpression model.
- Gbeta subunits have no GTPase; the GTPase row is removed.
