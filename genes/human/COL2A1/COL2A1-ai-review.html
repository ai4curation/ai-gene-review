Gene Description

COL2A1 encodes the pro-alpha-1(II) collagen chain, a major structural component of cartilage and vitreous extracellular matrices. Alpha-1(II) chains assemble into type-II collagen homotrimers and also supply the alpha-3 chain of type-XI collagen. Following secretion and removal of propeptides by extracellular procollagen proteinases, collagen molecules assemble into cross-linked fibrils that organize and support the matrix. Alternative splicing produces forms with different N-terminal sequences and a shorter C-terminal product. Disruption of collagen-II abundance or architecture causes skeletal dysplasias and connective-tissue disease with ocular and auditory manifestations.
