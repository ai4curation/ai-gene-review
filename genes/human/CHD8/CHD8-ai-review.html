Gene Description

CHD8 is a nuclear ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler that binds DNA and nucleosomes and changes nucleosome position and accessibility. Its interactions with transcriptional regulators, histones and chromatin-associated proteins support both repression and activation at different loci, including beta-catenin-responsive genes and Pol II/III transcriptional contexts. CHD8-dependent chromatin regulation also influences alternative RNA splicing and neurodevelopmental and enteric phenotypes.
