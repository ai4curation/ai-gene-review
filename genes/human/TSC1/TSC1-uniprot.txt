CC   -!- FUNCTION: Non-catalytic component of the TSC-TBC complex, a
CC       multiprotein complex that acts as a negative regulator of the canonical
CC       mTORC1 complex, an evolutionarily conserved central nutrient sensor
CC       that stimulates anabolic reactions and macromolecule biosynthesis to
CC       promote cellular biomass generation and growth (PubMed:12172553,
CC       PubMed:12271141, PubMed:12906785, PubMed:15340059, PubMed:24529379,
CC       PubMed:28215400). The TSC-TBC complex acts as a GTPase-activating
CC       protein (GAP) for the small GTPase RHEB, a direct activator of the
CC       protein kinase activity of mTORC1 (PubMed:12906785, PubMed:15340059,
CC       PubMed:24529379). In absence of nutrients, the TSC-TBC complex inhibits
CC       mTORC1, thereby preventing phosphorylation of ribosomal protein S6
CC       kinase (RPS6KB1 and RPS6KB2) and EIF4EBP1 (4E-BP1) by the mTORC1
CC       signaling (PubMed:12271141, PubMed:24529379, PubMed:28215400,
CC       PubMed:33215753). The TSC-TBC complex is inactivated in response to
CC       nutrients, relieving inhibition of mTORC1 (PubMed:12172553,
CC       PubMed:24529379). Within the TSC-TBC complex, TSC1 stabilizes TSC2 and
CC       prevents TSC2 self-aggregation (PubMed:10585443, PubMed:28215400). Acts
CC       as a tumor suppressor (PubMed:9242607). Involved in microtubule-
CC       mediated protein transport via its ability to regulate mTORC1 signaling
CC       (By similarity). Also acts as a co-chaperone for HSP90AA1 facilitating
CC       HSP90AA1 chaperoning of protein clients such as kinases, TSC2 and
CC       glucocorticoid receptor NR3C1 (PubMed:29127155). Increases ATP binding
CC       to HSP90AA1 and inhibits HSP90AA1 ATPase activity (PubMed:29127155).
CC       Competes with the activating co-chaperone AHSA1 for binding to
CC       HSP90AA1, thereby providing a reciprocal regulatory mechanism for
CC       chaperoning of client proteins (PubMed:29127155). Recruits TSC2 to
