## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

AKAIN1 (C18orf42) is a neural-tissue-enriched protein that engages the cAMP-dependent protein kinase A (PKA) signaling system through direct binding to PKA regulatory subunits, specifically the type II regulatory subunits, as shown by reciprocal immunoprecipitation and pull-down of the endogenous CRISPR-tagged protein in Neuro2a cells [PMID:25653177].
