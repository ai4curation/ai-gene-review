# B2M (beta-2-microglobulin) review notes

Deep research not run (providers unavailable); research from cached publications and UniProt.

- Invariant light chain of MHC class I [UniProt "Component of the class I major histocompatibility complex (MHC)."]; structural molecule activity [PMID:36104323 "crucial for MHC I assembly"]
- Required for HFE surface expression -> iron homeostasis [PMID:9465039 "This mutation eliminates the ability of HFE to associate with beta2-microglobulin (beta2m) and prevents cell-surface expression."]
- Free/secreted: urine/serum [PMID:7554280]; antibacterial activity of free B2M [PMID:23144825 "An antibacterial protein (about 12 kDa) was isolated from human amniotic fluid"]; amyloid in dialysis [PMID:28468825]; pro-aging factor [PMID:26147761]
- Localization: cell surface (part of MHC-I, external side), extracellular region (shed free B2M), ER/Golgi assembly+transit. SURFACE-EXPOSED as MHC-I ectodomain; also abundant secreted/extracellular. ESAT-6 blocks surface export [PMID:25356553; UniProt line quoted].
- Class II IBAs (rows 12,113,114,137) and peptide antigen binding IBA (133) are over-annotations from the lumped MHC class I/II PANTHER node PTN000460629 — B2M is the class I light chain, not a class II subunit and does not bind the antigenic peptide. Lysosomal membrane IBA (49) similarly class-II-pathway -> over-annotated.
- Protein binding (22-41) removed per policy; identical protein binding (self) -> homodimerization.
