# B2M (P61769) Curation Notes

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Gene symbol**: `B2M` (Beta-2-microglobulin; UniProt `P61769`; NCBI Taxon `9606`).
- **Architecture**: 119-amino-acid precursor (residues 1-20 signal peptide; residues 21-119 mature 99-residue secreted chain, ~11.7 kDa) folding into a single immunoglobulin-like C1-set beta-sandwich domain stabilized by an intrachain disulfide bond (`Cys45-Cys100`) [file:human/B2M/B2M-uniprot.txt]. Lacks a transmembrane domain and associates noncovalently with the alpha-3 and alpha-1/alpha-2 domains of heavy chains [PMID:36104323, Reactome:R-HSA-983146].
- **Core Function 1 — Obligate Structural Subunit of MHC Class I and Class I-Like Antigen-Presenting Complexes**:
  - Serves as the invariant light chain (`GO:0005198` `structural molecule activity`) of classical MHC class Ia molecules (`HLA-A`, `HLA-B`, `HLA-C`; `GO:0042612` `MHC class I protein complex`), non-classical MHC class Ib molecules (`HLA-E`, `HLA-F`, `HLA-G`; `GO:0032398` `MHC class Ib protein complex`), lipid-presenting `CD1a`/`CD1b`/`CD1c`/`CD1d` glycoproteins, and vitamin B metabolite-presenting `MR1` [PMID:36104323, PMID:35705051, PMID:28636952, PMID:15718280, PMID:12118248, PMID:23051753].
  - In the endoplasmic reticulum lumen (`GO:0005788`), `B2M` pairs with newly synthesized heavy chains and enters the multiprotein MHC class I peptide-loading complex (`GO:0042824`, comprising `TAP1`/`TAP2`, tapasin, `ERp57`/`PDIA3`, and calreticulin) to support peptide proofreading and assembly (`GO:0002502`) prior to anterograde transport via ER-to-Golgi vesicles (`GO:0012507`) and the Golgi apparatus (`GO:0000139`, `GO:0005794`) to the external side of the plasma membrane (`GO:0009897`) [PMID:21263072, PMID:35948544, PMID:36104323].
- **Core Function 2 — Obligate Structural Subunit of Non-Antigen-Presenting MHC Class I-Related Receptors (`HFE` and `FCGRT`/FcRn)**:
  - Forms heterodimers with the hereditary hemochromatosis protein `HFE` (`GO:1990712` `HFE-transferrin receptor complex`), enabling `HFE` exit from the ER, cell-surface expression, and binding to `TFRC` (TfR1) and `TFR2` to modulate transferrin-mediated iron uptake and systemic iron sensing [PMID:9465039, PMID:9546397, PMID:9990067, PMID:15965644, PMID:18353247, PMID:20618438]. The classic hemochromatosis mutation `HFE C282Y` (and `Q283P`) abolishes `B2M` association, trapping `HFE` in the ER [PMID:9465039, PMID:15965644].
  - Pairs with the `FCGRT` alpha chain to form the neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) heterodimer, which mediates pH-dependent recycling and transcytosis of IgG and albumin (curated in detail on `FCGRT`).
- **Extracellular Shedding and Pathological Amyloidosis**:
  - Because `B2M` lacks a membrane anchor, it dissociates from cell-surface heavy chains and circulates as a soluble monomer in serum, urine, colostrum, tears, and amniotic fluid (`GO:0005576` `extracellular region`) [PMID:7554280, PMID:16502470, PMID:22664934, PMID:23144825].
  - In chronic renal failure and long-term hemodialysis, impaired renal clearance leads to elevated circulating `B2M`, which can undergo Cu(II)-induced, acid-induced, or proteolysis-induced (`DeltaN6`) domain swapping and self-association (`GO:0042802`, `GO:0042803`, `GO:0051289`) into amyloid fibrils (`GO:1990000`) deposited in osteoarticular tissues (dialysis-related amyloidosis) [PMID:17108084, PMID:18579777, PMID:19172750, PMID:21131979, PMID:21220305, PMID:28468825].

## Key Curation Decisions on Existing Annotations
1. **PANTHER `PTN000460629` Over-Propagated MHC Class II Terms (`REMOVE`)**:
   - `GO:0002503` (`peptide antigen assembly with MHC class II protein complex`), `GO:0019886` (`antigen processing and presentation of exogenous peptide antigen via MHC class II`), `GO:0023026` (`MHC class II protein complex binding`), `GO:0042613` (`MHC class II protein complex`), `GO:0005765` (`lysosomal membrane`), and `GO:0031902` (`late endosome membrane`) are all propagated from MHC class II alpha/beta and HLA-DM chains within the broad Ig-like C1-set PANTHER family. `B2M` is never a subunit of MHC class II complexes.
   - `GO:0042605` (`peptide antigen binding`, IBA) is also `REMOVE`: `B2M` is a single Ig-like C1-set domain that underpins the alpha-1/alpha-2 platform and binds the alpha-3 domain of MHC class I heavy chains, but does not form the peptide-binding groove or directly bind the antigenic peptide.
2. **IBA Annotations Seeded by `B2M` Descendants (`KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`)**:
   - `GO:0050778` (`positive regulation of immune response`, IBA) includes `UniProtKB:P61769` (`B2M`) and `MGI:MGI:88127` (`B2m`) among its `supporting_entities`, reflecting experimental descendant grounding at the PAINT node. Retained as `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` alongside `GO:0050870` (`positive regulation of T cell activation`, IBA).
3. **Bare `GO:0005515` (`protein binding`) (`REMOVE`)**:
   - All 20 IPI rows for bare `GO:0005515` (covering `CD1A`, `CD1B`, `HFE`, `MR1`, `HLA-E`, `TAP1`, `CD244`, `PCNA`, `CIAPIN1`) are marked `REMOVE` per the repository's protein-binding policy; the biologically informative complex memberships (`GO:0042612`, `GO:0032398`, `GO:0042824`, `GO:1990712`) and structural activity (`GO:0005198`) are captured directly.
4. **ComplexPortal MHC Class Ib Annotations on `GO:0032398` (`ACCEPT`) and `GO:0002477` (`MODIFY` → `GO:0002476`)**:
   - Six papers (`PMID:10429675`, `PMID:10843695`, `PMID:15718280`, `PMID:28636952`, `PMID:35705051`, `PMID:9892618`) are co-annotated by ComplexPortal to `GO:0032398` (`MHC class Ib protein complex`) and `GO:0002477` (`antigen processing and presentation of exogenous peptide antigen via MHC class Ib`). Three of these (`PMID:15718280` on `HLA-G`, `PMID:28636952` on `HLA-F`, `PMID:35705051` on `HLA-E`) directly study non-classical MHC class Ib molecules, whereas the other three (`PMID:10429675` on `HLA-Cw4`, `PMID:10843695` on `HLA-A2`, `PMID:9892618` on `HLA-A2`) study classical MHC class Ia molecules. Importantly, all three MHC class Ib structures present endogenous self, leader, or intracellular peptides (histone H2A self-peptide in `HLA-G` [PMID:15718280], endogenous human proteomic peptides in `HLA-F` [PMID:28636952], and MHC class Ia VL9 leader peptides or intracellular pathogen peptides in `HLA-E` [PMID:35705051]). Therefore, all 6 `GO:0002477` rows are marked `MODIFY` to `GO:0002476` (`antigen processing and presentation of endogenous peptide antigen via MHC class Ib`) with `source_defects` (`WRONG_TERM` / `REPOINT`), while `GO:0032398` is retained as `ACCEPT`.
5. **Topology Refinement of `GO:0005886` (`plasma membrane`) and `GO:0016020` (`membrane`) (`MODIFY` → `GO:0009897`)**:
   - Because `B2M` is a secreted 99-residue luminal/extracellular beta chain lacking a transmembrane domain or lipid anchor that localizes to the cell surface exclusively via noncovalent association with the extracellular alpha-3 domain of heavy chains, all 32 `GO:0005886` (`plasma membrane`) rows are marked `MODIFY` → `GO:0009897` (`external side of plasma membrane`), and `GO:0016020` (`membrane`) is modified to `GO:0009897` (`external side of plasma membrane`) and `GO:0000139` (`Golgi membrane`).
6. **Bacterial Membrane Potential Dissipation in `PMID:23144825` (`GO:0003254`, `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED`)**:
   - `PMID:23144825` demonstrates that amniotic-fluid `B2M` kills bacteria (`L. monocytogenes`, `E. coli`) by dissipating bacterial membrane potential (`GO:0050829`, `GO:0050830`), not by regulating physiological eukaryotic membrane depolarization; `GO:0003254` (`regulation of membrane depolarization`) is marked `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED` with `source_defects`.
