CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the class I major histocompatibility complex
CC       (MHC). Involved in the presentation of peptide antigens to the immune
CC       system. Exogenously applied M.tuberculosis EsxA or EsxA-EsxB (or EsxA
CC       expressed in host) binds B2M and decreases its export to the cell
CC       surface (total protein levels do not change), probably leading to
CC       defects in class I antigen presentation (PubMed:25356553).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25356553}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterodimer of an alpha chain and a beta chain. Beta-2-
CC       microglobulin is the beta-chain of major histocompatibility complex
CC       class I molecules. Polymers of beta 2-microglobulin can be found in
CC       tissues from patients on long-term hemodialysis. B2M alone (not in
CC       complex with HLA-I) interacts with M.tuberculosis EsxA (ESAT-6) and an
CC       EsxA-EsxB (CFP-10) complex; the tripartite complex can be detected in
CC       the host endoplasmic reticulum (PubMed:25356553). The B2M-EsxA complex
CC       can be detected in patients with pleural tuberculosis and is stable
CC       from pH 4.0 to 8.0 and in the presence of 2M NaCl (PubMed:25356553).
