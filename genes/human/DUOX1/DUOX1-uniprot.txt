CC   -!- FUNCTION: Generates hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) which is required for the
CC       activity of thyroid peroxidase/TPO and lactoperoxidase/LPO
CC       (PubMed:10806195, PubMed:15972824). Plays a role in thyroid hormone
CC       synthesis (PubMed:10806195). Also required for lactoperoxidase-mediated
CC       antimicrobial defense at the surface of mucosa (PubMed:12824283).
CC       Antimicrobial agent hypothiocyanite (OSCN-), which is produced by LPO
CC       from DUOX1-derived H2O2, promotes influenza virus inactivation by
CC       reducing viral binding to and entry into host cells (PubMed:34168077).
CC       Promotes antiviral immunity by increasing airway cytokine levels,
CC       promoting innate immune cell recruitment and reducing airway epithelial
CC       cell apoptosis (By similarity). In response to bacterial stimuli,
CC       activated by ATP and promotes EGFR/ERK signaling, ADAM17 activation,
CC       and EGFR ligand shedding, leading to enhanced CXCL8/IL8 expression and
CC       secretion in airway epithelia (PubMed:19386603). Synthesizes NAADP from
CC       its reduced NAADPH form which promotes Ca(2+) signaling during T cell
CC       activation (PubMed:34784249). In addition to its oxidase activity, has
CC       also been shown to have peroxidase activity through its N-terminal
CC       peroxidase-like domain (PubMed:11514595). However, another study showed
CC       that the isolated peroxidase-like domain does not bind heme and has no
CC       intrinsic peroxidase activity (PubMed:19460756).
