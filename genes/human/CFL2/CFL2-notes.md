### Performed actin activity and muscle scope

Human CFL2 directly binds and severs actin filaments. PMID:35440100 Figure 4 counts individual severing events after purified human CFL2 addition, complementing its structural analysis of the actin-bound protein. The NMR assembly explicitly uses rabbit skeletal actin. TIRF cofilin constructs were expressed in bacteria and their His tags cleaved, leaving an extra N-terminal glycine. The different CFL1 and CFL2 concentrations were chosen to obtain comparable severing rates; they are not used here to assert an unconditional potency ratio. Bulk polymerization in this experiment reports severing-generated filament ends, not an intrinsic actin-polymerase function.

The seeded broad actin-binding assertion is therefore refined to GO:0003789, actin filament severing activity. One core combines this activity with the already present severing and depolymerization processes. The specific F-actin-binding assertions remain accepted. No additional process is manufactured from disease necessity, and no isoform-specific source field is added. Comparisons called cofilin isoforms in these papers often compare the paralogs CFL1 and CFL2, not the three alternative CFL2 products.

PMID:19752190 was recovered externally in genuine full text while its normal abstract cache remains unchanged. Human cardiac cDNA supplied GST-CFL2 residues 1–166; direct bulk pyrene assays establish depolymerization, and human quadriceps microscopy colocalizes CFL2 with alpha-actinin at the Z disc. The kit actin species is not stated in the inspected Methods. Muscle LIM protein directly binds CFL2 and modifies its activity. PMID:24860983 distinguishes that full-length MLP-a association from the short MLP-b product, which does not bind CFL2 directly; its title is not evidence of a wrong-gene citation.

PMID:17160903 full text supplies control human I-band staining, with an antibody that cross-reacts with CFL1 and a precise longitudinal pattern reported without displayed data. Patient A35T muscle has reduced CFL2 and excess F-actin; bacterial expression tests altered solubility, not a direct mutant-versus-WT depolymerization rate. The existing IMP regulatory assertion is retained with independent direct CFL2 biochemistry. Z disc is part of I band in the checked ontology, so the two original locations are not rejected as mutually exclusive.

PMID:25373779 adds a location boundary: endogenous CFL2 in cultured neonatal rat cardiomyocytes occurs between Z discs near thin-filament pointed ends, with depletion/rescue effects on filament-length organization. This does not negate human quadriceps Z-disc staining. The core does not assert universal or exclusive Z-disc residency, and the recombinant protein details delegated to an uninspected supplement are not labeled human.
