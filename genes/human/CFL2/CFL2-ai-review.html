Gene Description

Cofilin-2 is an actin-binding protein enriched in striated muscle that promotes filament severing and depolymerization. Its direct interaction with actin changes filament organization and supports turnover of sarcomeric thin filaments. Cofilin-2 localizes to the muscle actin cytoskeleton, including the Z-disc region, and interacts with muscle LIM protein, which modulates its activity. Human CFL2 variants can cause congenital myopathy with abnormal actin accumulation and sarcomeric organization.
