# ANKRD53 notes

- Mitotic function from a single HeLa siRNA study [PMID:26820536, abstract only]. DDA3/PSRC1 recruits ANKRD53 to the spindle, and ANKRD53 counteracts DDA3 for MT polymerization. Depletion causes a mitotic delay, unaligned chromosomes, reduced spindle MT polymerization, SAC activation and binucleation.
- All five IBAs come from node PTN001192898, seeded only by ANKRD53 itself.
- Calls:
  - Spindle and spindle pole: ACCEPT.
  - Positive regulation of MT polymerization and regulation of mitotic spindle organization: ACCEPT, core.
  - Chromosome alignment and regulation of mitotic cytokinesis: KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, as likely consequences of reduced spindle dynamics.
  - DDA3 IPI → MODIFY to GO:0008092 cytoskeletal protein binding.
- Adipocyte role [PMID:41654016, full text]: ANKRD53 binds ACSL1 and promotes its mitochondrial localization to couple lipolysis with beta-oxidation. Single study, so no NEW term; raised as a question.
