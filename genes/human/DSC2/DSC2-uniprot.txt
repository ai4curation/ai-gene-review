CC   -!- FUNCTION: A component of desmosome cell-cell junctions which are
CC       required for positive regulation of cellular adhesion
CC       (PubMed:33596089). Promotes timely incorporation of DSG2 into desmosome
CC       intercellular junctions and promotes interaction of desmosome cell
CC       junctions with intermediate filament cytokeratin, via modulation of DSP
CC       phosphorylation (PubMed:33596089). Plays an important role in
CC       desmosome-mediated maintenance of intestinal epithelial cell
CC       intercellular adhesion strength and barrier function (PubMed:33596089).
CC       Positively regulates wound healing of intestinal mucosa via promotion
CC       of epithelial cell migration, and also plays a role in
CC       mechanotransduction of force between intestinal epithelial cells and
CC       extracellular matrix (PubMed:31967937). May contribute to epidermal
CC       cell positioning (stratification) by mediating differential
CC       adhesiveness between cells that express different isoforms. May promote
CC       p38MAPK signaling activation that facilitates keratinocyte migration
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P55292,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31967937, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33596089}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with DSP, PKP2 and JUP (PubMed:21062920). Interacts

CC       activation (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P55292,
