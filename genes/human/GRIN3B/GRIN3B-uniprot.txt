CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of a non-conventional N-methyl-D-aspartate (NMDA)
CC       receptors (NMDARs) that function as heterotetrameric, ligand-gated
CC       cation channels with low calcium permeability and low voltage-dependent
CC       block by Mg(2+) (By similarity). Forms glutamatergic receptor complexes
CC       with GluN1 and GluN2 subunits which are activated by glycine binding to
CC       the GluN1 and GluN3 subunits and L-glutamate binding to GluN2 subunits
CC       (By similarity). Forms excitatory glycinergic receptor complexes with
CC       GluN1 alone which are activated by glycine binding to the GluN1 and
CC       GluN3 subunits. GluN3B subunit also binds D-serine and, in the absence
CC       of glycine, activates glycinergic receptor complexes, but with lower
CC       efficacy than glycine (By similarity). Each GluN3 subunit confers
CC       differential attributes to channel properties, including activation,
CC       deactivation and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, Ca2(+)
CC       permeability, and binding to allosteric modulators (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8VHN2, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q91ZU9}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms heterotetrameric channels that contain at least two
CC       GluN1 subunits and at least a combination of one GluN2 and one GluN3
CC       subunits (in vitro) (By similarity). Forms heterotetrameric channels
CC       composed of two GluN1/zeta subunits (GRIN1), and two identical GluN3
CC       subunits (GRIN3A or GRIN3B) (in vitro) (Probable). Does not form
CC       functional homomeric channels (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q91ZU9, ECO:0000305}.
