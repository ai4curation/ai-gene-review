CC       Catalytic component of the polycomb repressive deubiquitinase (PR-DUB)
CC       complex, a complex that specifically mediates deubiquitination of
CC       histone H2A monoubiquitinated at 'Lys-120' (H2AK119ub1)
CC       (PubMed:20436459, PubMed:25451922, PubMed:30664650, PubMed:35051358).
CC       Does not deubiquitinate monoubiquitinated histone H2B (PubMed:20436459,
