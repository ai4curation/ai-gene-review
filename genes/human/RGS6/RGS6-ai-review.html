Gene Description

RGS6 is a multidomain regulator of heterotrimeric G protein signaling in the R7 RGS subfamily. Its RGS domain accelerates G alpha inactivation, and its G gamma-like domain binds the atypical beta subunit GNB5 to assemble a signaling-regulatory complex. The RGS6–GNB5 complex has directly demonstrated GAP activity toward Go. DEP-domain partners and splice variation influence membrane, cytoplasmic and nuclear localization. RGS6 participates in neuronal signaling and in termination of cardiac muscarinic responses; native mouse experiments establish control of M2-dependent acetylcholine-activated potassium currents.

Core Functions

The GNB5-bound RGS6 complex accelerates Go GTP hydrolysis and limits receptor signaling. Mouse genetics establishes rapid termination of M2-mediated cardiac potassium currents; this does not identify every native G alpha substrate or imply universal dependence on R7BP.
