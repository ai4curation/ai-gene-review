All original annotation payloads, evidence codes, references and qualifiers were preserved. No new annotations were manufactured to populate a module. Actual Falcon and fallback research attempts failed authentication/quota; this is manual literature research, not provider-generated prose.

The whole record was reviewed, including secondary physiology and localization. Existing assertion specificity is judged independently of evidence code. Native, heterologous, mutant and orthology-based results are distinguished below.

## GO:0005096 GTPase activator activity — ACCEPT
RGS6–GNB5 purified complexes accelerate Go GTP hydrolysis (PMID:10521509), and native mouse Rgs6 deletion prolongs M2-linked GIRK signaling. These support GAP activity without assigning RGS4-like substrate breadth to every RGS6 complex. The conserved RGS domain and specific GNB5 partnership support the cross-species assignment.
Sources: PMID:10521509, PMID:20884879, PMID:24318880

## GO:0005634 nucleus — KEEP_AS_NON_CORE
Human RGS6 splice products exhibit different nuclear/cytoplasmic partitioning, and R7BP-dependent trafficking can redistribute R7–GNB5 assemblies. Retain the nuclear pool with product/partner dependence; this is not universal localization of every RGS6 product.
Sources: PMID:12761221, PMID:15897264

## GO:0005654 nucleoplasm — KEEP_AS_NON_CORE
The existing nucleoplasmic assignment is compatible with independently observed nuclear RGS6 products. Retain this contextual pool, distinguishing the human imaging-based assignment from its conserved mouse inference and from the predominant membrane signaling role.
Sources: PMID:12761221

## GO:0005737 cytoplasm — ACCEPT
RGS6 has cytoplasmic and soluble pools supported by splice-product localization and RGS6–GNB5 biochemical studies. Membrane recruitment and nuclear redistribution coexist with these pools rather than disproving them.
Sources: PMID:10521509, PMID:12761221, PMID:15897264

## GO:0005829 cytosol — ACCEPT
RGS6 has cytoplasmic and soluble pools supported by splice-product localization and RGS6–GNB5 biochemical studies. Membrane recruitment and nuclear redistribution coexist with these pools rather than disproving them.
Sources: PMID:10521509, PMID:12761221, PMID:15897264

## GO:0005886 plasma membrane — ACCEPT
Membrane recruitment of R7 RGS–GNB5 complexes, including RGS6 binding to R7BP, supports the plasma-membrane signaling pool. Retain the supported compartment without manufacturing a topology-specific replacement from a recruitment assay.
Sources: PMID:15897264, PMID:20884879

## GO:0007186 G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway — ACCEPT
RGS6 performs termination of GPCR signaling through its GAP complex. Native mouse M2 experiments support a specific cholinergic implementation; purified Go-directed activity establishes the molecular role independently.
Sources: PMID:10521509, PMID:20884879

## GO:0008277 regulation of G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway — ACCEPT
The regulation term captures the established RGS6 complex function. The physiological consequence depends on partner, receptor and cellular context; this broad process remains useful alongside positive regulation of GTPase activity.
Sources: PMID:10521509, PMID:20884879

## GO:0016020 membrane — MODIFY
The established membrane-associated signaling pool can be specified as plasma membrane, already separately annotated. This refinement does not exclude additional membrane compartments or erase the soluble pool.
Sources: PMID:15897264

## GO:0035556 intracellular signal transduction — MODIFY
For the represented core signaling function, RGS6 is specifically a regulator of GPCR signaling. Refine the very broad intracellular signal-transduction term to that established process rather than keep it merely as peripheral.
Sources: PMID:10521509, PMID:20884879

## GO:0043005 neuron projection — KEEP_AS_NON_CORE
The R7-family neuronal localization inference is compatible with neurite-associated RGS6–SCG10 function and neuronal RGS expression. Retain neuron projection as a contextual conserved location, not evidence that every human/mouse splice product occupies every neuronal projection.
Sources: PMID:12140291

## GO:0043547 positive regulation of GTPase activity — ACCEPT
RGS6 directly accelerates G alpha GTPase activity in the purified RGS6–GNB5 complex. This positive regulation of GTP hydrolysis is the mechanism by which receptor signaling is terminated.
Sources: PMID:10521509

## GO:0003924 GTPase activity — MODIFY
The G alpha substrate hydrolyzes GTP; RGS6 accelerates that reaction in partnership with GNB5. Refine the misassigned intrinsic GTPase activity to the existing GAP function.
Sources: PMID:10521509

## GO:0005515 protein binding — REMOVE
Remove uninformative bare protein binding under the project policy while retaining the specific GNB5-dependent GAP complex and supported biological functions.
Source handling: original payload retained; no fabricated supporting quote.
