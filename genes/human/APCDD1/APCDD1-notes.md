# Curation Notes for Human `APCDD1` (`UniProtKB:Q8J025`, `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2`)

## 1. Protein Identity, Architecture, and Crystal Structure Synthesis
- **Gene / Accession**: Human `APCDD1` (*adenomatosis polyposis coli down-regulated 1*; legacy alias `B7323`, `DRAPC1`), `UniProtKB:Q8J025` (514 aa, sequence version 1), classified in `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2` (`PROTEIN APCDD1`).
- **Topology & Post-Translational Maturation**: Single-pass type I transmembrane glycoprotein with a cleavable N-terminal signal peptide (`aa 1–26`, containing `Leu9` in its hydrophobic core), a large extracellular domain (`aa 27–492`), a C-terminal transmembrane helix (`aa 493–513`), and a minimal C-terminal stub (`Arg514`) (`PMID:20393562`, `PMID:37155902`). Mature full-length human APCDD1 runs at ~68 kDa (monomer) and ~130 kDa (homodimer) in cell extracts, is N-glycosylated at three conserved sequons (`Asn100`, `Asn168`, `Asn319`), and is not measurably shed into conditioned medium (`PMID:20393562`).
- **Crystal Structure & Ligand-Capture Mechanism (`PMID:37155902`, `PDB:8E0P`, `8E0R`, `8E0W`)**: Hsieh et al. (2023) solved X-ray crystal structures (to 1.95 Å) of the glycosylated secreted extracellular domain of mouse APCDD1 (`UniProtKB:Q3U128`, 514 aa, sharing identical residue numbering across `Cys52–Cys466` and `Asn100`/`Asn168`/`Asn319` with human `Q8J025`). The ECD comprises two homologous antiparallel beta-barrel domains (`ABD1` and `ABD2`) oriented ~90° apart and linked by six conserved disulfide bridges (three intradomain in ABD1, two intradomain in ABD2, and one interdomain bridge `Cys52–Cys466`). While ABD1's interior cavity is occluded (~35 Å³), ABD2 forms a ~350 Å³ hydrophobic pocket that co-purifies with stearic acid (C18:0, by reversed-phase HPLC-MS) and accommodates palmitoleate (`PAM`, C16:1), mediating detergent-sensitive capture of lipidated human WNT7A (`PMID:37155902`).
- **Re-evaluation of Reported LRP5 Binding (`PMID:20393562` vs. `PMID:37155902`)**: Shimomura et al. (`PMID:20393562`) reported co-immunoprecipitation in Triton X-100 cell lysates and *E. coli*-expressed unglycosylated `GST-APCDD1(28-486)` pulldown with both `WNT3A` (`UniProtKB:P56704`) and `LRP5` (`UniProtKB:O75197`), which IntAct exported as two generic `GO:0005515` (`protein binding`) IPI rows. Crucially, *E. coli* expression cannot form the six native disulfide bonds or three N-glycans of the APCDD1 ECD. When Hsieh et al. (`PMID:37155902`) directly tested folded, glycosylated mammalian-expressed APCDD1 ECD (in both `APCDD1-AP` and `AP-APCDD1` orientations, alongside positive-control `DKK1-AP`/`AP-DKK1`) against biotinylated extracellular `PE1-4` domains of LRP5 and LRP6, they detected **zero binding**, concluding that APCDD1 inhibits Wnt signaling by binding lipidated WNT ligands rather than LRP5/6 coreceptors. Accordingly:
  - `GO:0005515` IPI with `UniProtKB:P56704` (`WNT3A`) is refined via `MODIFY` to `GO:0017147` (`Wnt-protein binding`).
  - `GO:0005515` IPI with `UniProtKB:O75197` (`LRP5`) is set to `REMOVE` under both the generic `protein_binding_policy` and the direct biochemical refutation for folded ectodomains in `PMID:37155902`.
- **Dual BMP Pathway Inhibition (`PMID:32320685`)**: Vonica et al. (2020) showed that human `APCDD1` also suppresses BMP2-induced BRE-luciferase activity (~5-fold), blocks BMP4-stimulated nuclear phospho-Smad1 accumulation, reduces cell-surface levels of type I BMP receptor `BMPR1A` (`Alk3`), and co-immunoprecipitates with `BMPR1A` (whereas it does not inhibit Activin-induced Smad2 signaling), supported in vivo by *Xenopus laevis* `apcdd1` morpholino knockdown/rescue and zebrafish `apcdd1l` genetics. We therefore propose a conservative `NEW` annotation to `GO:0030514` (`negative regulation of BMP signaling pathway`, `IDA`, `PMID:32320685`).

## 2. Subcellular Localization & Disease Variant (`p.Leu9Arg`)
- Wild-type human APCDD1 resides at the `plasma membrane` (`GO:0005886`, `ACCEPT`) of Wnt-receiving cells (`PMID:20393562`).
- Heterozygous mutation `c.26T>G` (`p.Leu9Arg`) in the signal peptide causes autosomal-dominant hereditary hypotrichosis simplex (`HHS1`, `OMIM:605185` / `146520`). `L9R` prevents signal-peptide cleavage, arrests mutant APCDD1 in the endoplasmic reticulum (`ER`), and traps co-expressed wild-type APCDD1 in the ER via transmembrane-dependent homodimerization (`GO:0042802` `identical protein binding`, `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`), depleting functional APCDD1 from the cell surface (`PMID:20393562`, `PMID:32320685`). Per `projects/ORGANELLES.md`, ER transit of wild-type APCDD1 and ER retention of the pathogenic `L9R` mutant do **not** represent steady-state wild-type ER residence (`GO:0005783` is not added).
