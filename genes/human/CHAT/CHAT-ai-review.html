Gene Description

Choline acetyltransferase catalyses transfer of an acetyl group from acetyl-CoA to choline, producing acetylcholine. It supplies the neurotransmitter in cholinergic neurons, including motor-neuron terminals, and is essential for normal neuromuscular transmission. Human CHAT produces proteins with distinct N-terminal extensions. The common smaller enzyme is principally cytoplasmic and can shuttle through the nucleus; the larger primate product is enriched in neuronal nuclei. Nuclear ChAT can affect choline-transporter expression in human neuronal cells, although the molecular connection remains unresolved. Pathogenic variants impair acetylcholine synthesis and can cause congenital myasthenic syndrome with episodic apnea.
