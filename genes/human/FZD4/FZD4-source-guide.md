# FZD4 source guide

This guide describes the evidence behind the FZD4 review and its important experimental limits. The [evidence archive](FZD4-source-evidence.yaml) retains all 134 original annotation contexts, their literal donor and partner identifiers, the six extension atoms in five contexts, the canonical sequence and source provenance. Current decisions, reference assessments and core functions belong in the review; they are not duplicated in the archive.

## Receptor identity and products

The official reviewed human record, [UniProt Q9ULV1](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9ULV1/entry), identifies a 537-residue FZD4 precursor with an extracellular cysteine-rich region, seven transmembrane segments and cytoplasmic signaling motifs. It declares no alternative natural products. Signal-peptide processing, isolated extracellular domains, intracellular peptides, stabilizing substitutions and disease variants used in experiments are distinct from natural isoforms. Sequence identity does not establish identical behavior across these engineered constructs.

## Wnt and Norrin receptor functions

Two complementary receptor functions organize the synthesis. Human FZD4 functional rescue supports Wnt-dependent canonical signaling, with ligand and cellular context retained. The original [PMID:28733458](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28733458/) Methods, Results, Figure 6 and Table 4 include FZD4 responses to Wnt9a/9b even though other Frizzled receptors receive prominent treatment in the abstract. This is functional pathway output, not a purified ligand-affinity measurement. [PMID:20802536](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20802536/) tests human HA-FZD4 in hamster CHO cells, Wnt5a-dependent responses and receptor trafficking. Xenopus calcium-branch and rodent neuronal experiments retain their separate systems and do not by themselves establish a particular human FZD4 G-protein coupling mechanism.

Norrin is a non-Wnt ligand. The authentic abstract of [PMID:15035989](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15035989/) supports high-affinity Norrin–FZD4 recognition and the FZD4/LRP receptor system; complete original text was not recovered. [PMID:17955262](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17955262/) supports cell-surface binding and signaling within its abstract-level access. [PMID:19837032](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19837032/) supplies selected original mouse endothelial signaling and barrier evidence, including cerebellar vascular-barrier integrity. These observations do not resolve every older cerebellar morphogenesis or extracellular-matrix-input assertion. Short excerpts supporting the receptor roles, regulation and response interpretations have exact normal-cache byte locators in the evidence archive. The retinal role is included in the Norrin core because FZD4 supplies the endothelial signal-reception step, not merely because mutations cause a vascular phenotype.

## Interactions and constructs

- **Amyloid-beta:** the original figure legends of [PMID:18234671](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18234671/) explicitly identify FZD4 CRD with beta40 in Figure 1C. Available beta42/aggregate panels name FZD5. The unresolved FZD4-specific contexts remain unresolved without calling the curators wrong from incomplete access.
- **NHERF1 and Dishevelled:** [PMID:20802536](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20802536/) tests the FZD4 terminal V537A substitution, NHERF1 PDZ2, receptor association and recombinant overlay. Literal hamster NHERF1 and mouse Dvl2 partner records are preserved without equating host species to every construct. Original Table II in [PMID:19388021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19388021/) reports FZD4 internal-peptide competition IC50 values of 2.2, 0.9 and 1.8 micromolar for human DVL1, DVL2 and DVL3 PDZ domains; these are not dissociation constants.
- **SFRP1:** selected original Figure 6 and Methods in [PMID:19277043](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19277043/) assay expressed human FZD4 extracellular-Fc and human SFRP1 constructs. Association does not transfer the paper's separate androgen-receptor findings to a specific FZD4 mechanism.
- **Haenig screen:** [PMID:32814053](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32814053/) Table S2 NN1.0 rows 3664, 3663, 3666, 3665 and 10468 contain the FZD4–DMWD, A2M, HTT, DNM2 and SPRED1 pairs. Human proteins were tested in yeast and pairwise reporter retesting. DMWD 316–649, HTT fragments/mutation-tagged contexts, the DNM2 suffix and bait/prey orientation remain explicit. Table S4 has no FZD4 pair-specific mammalian DULIP validation.
- **PDZ interactome:** [PMID:36115835](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36115835/) Supplementary Dataset 1 tests the human FZD4 terminal peptide 528–537, KPGKGSETVV, against human PDZ domains. Original domain measurements support 13 of the 21 partner contexts; eight remain unresolved. Original pKd zero entries and the literal IntAct `kd:1.0x10(molar)` field are not treated as measured 10-M affinity or proof of no interaction. IL16, FRMPD2, CIPP and LNX1 have positive original-domain rows beyond the zero-valued imported subset. For raw 26, incoming Q86UT5 remains distinct from the retained IntAct Q86UT5-2 participant; neither identifier is silently substituted. Peptide/domain binding does not establish full-length endogenous complexes.
- **Association and turnover:** original Figure 4 and supplementary Figure S3 of [PMID:14688793](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14688793/) support FZD4 self-association and FZD4–FZD9 complementation in expressed constructs; reporter proximity does not fix physiological stoichiometry. [PMID:22575959](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22575959/) directly assays FZD4 ubiquitination and surface regulation, but the accessible co-immunoprecipitation panels do not resolve the exact FZD4–ZNRF3 pair.

## Locations, process scope and propagation

Wild-type surface localization is supported separately from mutant ER trapping and engineered structural constructs. The FZD4 comparator in original Figure 2 of [PMID:19643732](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19643732/) supports surface residence despite the paper's FZD5-focused internalization results. HPA provides supported antibody-localization metadata; individual images were not independently scored. ARBA supplies a computational cell-surface prediction, not a new experiment or a completed target-condition audit.

Mouse donor evidence supports specialized synaptic and junctional contexts. The indexed original Results/Figure 1 of [PMID:16501258](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16501258/) supports hippocampal Fzd4/PSD-95 colocalization, and the official Figure 4 caption of [PMID:15195140](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15195140/) supports epithelial-contact localization. The abstract of [PMID:25424568](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25424568/) supports neuronal morphogenesis but does not expose the precise dendrite-localization assay, leaving those transferred location contexts unresolved. These donor observations are not relabeled as human experiments.

The actual PTHR11309 tree and alignment place human FZD4 beneath all six relevant IBDs: five at PTN000139394 and the retina-development assertion at PTN002601720. The target alignment reproduces all 537 residues. Current incoming and retained main/global donor sets differ for raw 36, 65, 72 and 88 and match for raw 50 and 77; both source versions remain preserved. Donor count and legitimate target self-membership are not defects. Biological relevance is assessed separately from correct node inheritance.

All 28 Reactome records were read as the existing cellular-component contexts. The literal extension groups remain attached to their original rows: raw 39 has input UniProtKB:P41221; raw 47 occurs in CL:0002608 and is part of GO:0016055 in one group; raw 59 and 86 occur in CL:0002608; raw 124 retains its original `part_of` CL:0002608 relation. ## Functional specificity and disputed source provenance

The positive [PMID:36115835 supplementary workbook](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-022-33018-0/MediaObjects/41467_2022_33018_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx) measurements directly name the PDZ domains tested against the FZD4 terminal peptide. Thirteen positive contexts are refined to PDZ domain binding; eight unresolved contexts remain unchanged. Selected row identifiers and numerical measurements are available in the compact source archive. The [PMID:20802536 original](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20802536/) separately establishes the NHERF1 PDZ2 interface, whereas its Figure 6 Dvl2 recruitment experiment does not map the Dvl2 interface. The two Norrin partner rows are refined to cytokine binding. Other positive associations retain their original experimental scope under the [immutable project policy](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/7d80d6aef16b7ef0246a8833cf3e51a8e262b4dd/projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions).

The transcription terms describe regulation, not transcription-factor molecular activity. Human FZD4-dependent Topflash/TOP-FOP output is positive evidence for that regulation. The original [PMID:14688793 Figure 4h caption](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncb1081/figures/4) identifies the SuperTOPflash experiment even though the complete article is unavailable. The receptor's membrane location does not refute regulation through beta-catenin signaling.

[GO:0071300](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0071300) explicitly includes altered gene expression. The authentic [PMID:12429992 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12429992/) reports FZD4 induction after ATRA in human NT2 cells, supporting that non-core response. It does not establish that FZD4 executes neuronal differentiation. Both original ISS rows name WNT7B/P56706 rather than a Frizzled ortholog; two source-defect records request an upstream donor/evidence check without replacing either original identifier. The source problem and the independently supported response are separate judgments.

The checked is_a ancestry for [Norrin signaling](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0110135) does not include cytokine-mediated signaling. The broad cytokine context therefore remains non-core; no unverified formal edge is used to justify a TOO_GENERAL action. Selected mouse cerebellar results in PMID:19837032 establish vascular integrity and stability; they do not independently expose the older donor's generation-and-organization experiment, so the cerebellar morphogenesis annotation remains unresolved.

External numerical claims remain source-qualified. The [PMID:19388021 original Table II](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19388021/) is an indexed original table; its internal-peptide IC50 values are not affinity constants. The [Haenig original article and supplements](https://edoc.mdc-berlin.de/id/eprint/19322/) provide Table S2 NN1.0. No invented normal-cache quotation substitutes for those separately read original tables.
