# KDM5A curation notes

Automated Falcon deep research, with the `perplexity-lite` fallback, was unavailable in this worktree. This note records the manual evidence synthesis used for the KDM5A review.

KDM5A/RBP2 is a JARID1-family nuclear histone demethylase. Iwase et al. identified the KDM5 family as H3K4me3 demethylases and showed that RBP2/KDM5A demethylates H3K4me3 [PMID:17320160]. Tu et al. solved the KDM5A ARID domain, identified a CCGCCC DNA motif, and showed that ARID DNA recognition is required for transcriptional regulation in a reporter assay [PMID:18270511]. Wang et al. showed that the C-terminal KDM5A PHD finger recognizes H3K4me3/2 and that this reader activity becomes leukemogenic when fused to NUP98 [PMID:19430464].

The CLOCK-BMAL1 and RB1 rows describe specialized contexts. KDM5A/JARID1a coactivates CLOCK-BMAL1 by inhibiting HDAC1 independently of its demethylase activity in mouse and fly circadian systems [PMID:21960634]. Mouse Jarid1a and Jarid1b contribute to retinoblastoma-mediated H3K4 demethylation and gene silencing during senescence [PMID:22615382]. These were retained as non-core regulatory outputs.
