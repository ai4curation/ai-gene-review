CC   -!- FUNCTION: ATP-dependent transporter of the ATP-binding cassette (ABC)
CC       family that hydrolyzes ATP to enable the active transport of organic
CC       anions conjugated with glutathione, glucuronate or sulfate. Operates at
CC       the plasma membrane where it mediates the efflux of substrates from the
CC       cytoplasm into the extracellular space. The rate of transport is in
CC       some way coupled to the reduced glutathione (GSH) intracellular levels
CC       (PubMed:10064732, PubMed:11114332, PubMed:16230346, PubMed:8640791,
CC       PubMed:7961706, PubMed:9281595). Mediates the systemic release of
CC       glutathione-conjugated leukotriene C4 (LTC4) from myeloid cells such as
CC       mast cells, as part of the inflammatory response (PubMed:10064732,
CC       PubMed:11114332, PubMed:7961706, PubMed:8640791). Exports S-
CC       geranylgeranyl-glutathione (GGG) in lymphoid cells and stromal
CC       compartments of lymphoid organs. ABCC1 (via extracellular transport)
CC       with GGT5 (via GGG catabolism) establish GGG gradients within lymphoid
CC       tissues to position P2RY8-positive lymphocytes at germinal centers in
CC       lymphoid follicles and restrict their chemotactic transmigration from
CC       blood vessels to the bone marrow parenchyma (By similarity). Mediates
CC       basolateral export of GSH-conjugated R- and S-prostaglandin A2
CC       diastereomers in polarized epithelial cells (PubMed:9426231). Can
CC       export both GSH and its oxidized form GSSG in a protective mechanism
CC       that limits cellular damage especially during oxidative stress.
CC       Regulates GSH and GSSG release from astrocytes to maintain glutathione
CC       redox equilibrium in the brain (PubMed:16230346, PubMed:8640791).
CC       Transports signaling molecules. Appears to selectively recognize cyclic
CC       2'3'-GMP-AMP (cGAMP) produced by mammals but not 3'3'-cGAMP produced by
CC       bacteria. Mediates 2'3'-cGAMP export and limits intracellular cGAMP-
CC       mediated cGAS-STING signaling and type I interferon synthesis
CC       (PubMed:36070769, PubMed:39765229). Exports sphingosine 1-phosphate
CC       from mast cells independently of their degranulation (PubMed:17050692).
CC       Confers resistance to anticancer drugs by mediating ATP- and GSH-
CC       dependent drug export (PubMed:9281595). Similarly to MRP2, transports
CC       various steroids, bile acids and bilirubin conjugates (PubMed:8640791,
CC       PubMed:9355767). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O35379,
