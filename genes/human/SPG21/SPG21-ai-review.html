Gene Description

Maspardin is a small, deeply conserved and ubiquitously expressed protein that partitions between the cytosol and the cytosolic face of late endosomal and lysosomal membranes, where it acts as an effector of the small GTPase RAB7A, binding preferentially to its GTP-loaded form. Through that binding it keeps RAB7A on retromer- and TBC1D5-positive late endosomes rather than on lysosomes; when maspardin is absent, RAB7A redistributes to the lysosomal membrane, loses access to its GTPase-activating protein TBC1D5 and stays GTP-bound, which recruits the anterograde adaptor FYCO1 and shifts lysosomes toward the cell periphery. The same redistribution disturbs Rag-mediated presentation of the transcription factor TFEB to mTORC1, so that TFEB is under-phosphorylated and accumulates in the nucleus; canonical mTORC1 substrates such as ULK1, S6K1 and 4E-BP1 are unaffected. Maspardin is built almost entirely from an alpha/beta hydrolase fold, but no enzymatic activity has ever been demonstrated for it: the fold retains its G-x-S-x-G nucleophile elbow while sequence and structural analyses agree that the acid and histidine partners of the catalytic triad are absent, and the fold appears instead to serve as a protein-interaction module. It was originally isolated as a binding partner of the CD4 cytoplasmic tail. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause SPG21 (Mast syndrome), an autosomal recessive complicated hereditary spastic paraplegia with dementia, thin corpus callosum and cerebellar and extrapyramidal signs.

Core Functions

Maspardin is an effector of RAB7A. It binds the GTP-loaded form of the GTPase from the cytosol and is thereby recruited to late endosomal and lysosomal membranes, and it holds RAB7A on retromer- and TBC1D5-positive late endosomes rather than on lysosomes. Loss of maspardin moves RAB7A onto the lysosomal membrane, away from its GAP TBC1D5, so RAB7A remains GTP-bound; that in turn recruits the anterograde adaptor FYCO1 and drives lysosomes toward the cell periphery, and it disturbs Rag-mediated presentation of TFEB to mTORC1 so that TFEB is under-phosphorylated and enters the nucleus. Maspardin does not interact with the Rag GTPases directly and does not affect canonical mTORC1 substrates. Its alpha/beta hydrolase fold is used as a protein-interaction module, not as a catalytic domain.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Has maspardin ever been assayed for catalytic activity? Every source from 2003 to 2026 states that it lacks the catalytic triad and is therefore probably not an enzyme, but all of those statements are sequence or structure inferences, and the G-x-S-x-G nucleophile elbow (Gly107-Ala108-Ser109-Leu110-Gly111) is intact. A single activity-based protein profiling experiment would convert a twenty-two-year-old prediction into a fact.

Suggested experts: Craig Blackstone, Juan S Bonifacino

Q: Should the recurrent p.Ala108Pro allele be reinterpreted? It sits inside the nucleophile elbow, immediately before Ser109, and is currently explained purely as a misfolding and degradation defect. Is its residual activity in TFEB rescue assays consistent with a folding defect alone?

Suggested experts: Marielle Boonen, Thomas Jacqmin

Q: By what mechanism does maspardin hold RAB7A on retromer-positive late endosomes? Bridging to TBC1D5 or VPS35 is not detected in co-immunoprecipitations that do recover the maspardin-RAB7 complex, so the effector output is undefined.

Suggested experts: Marielle Boonen, Juan S Bonifacino

Q: Is the CD4 interaction worth retaining as a molecular-function annotation? It is a real 2001 two-hybrid result with domain mapping, never replicated and never followed up, and it is propagated as an IBA across a family far older than CD4 itself.

Suggested experts: Waldemar Kolanus

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express and purify recombinant human maspardin and subject it to activity-based protein profiling with fluorophosphonate and related serine-hydrolase probes, alongside a panel of generic ester, thioester and lipid substrates. Include a Ser109Ala mutant and a well-characterised active ABHD enzyme as controls. A negative result with an intact, probe-accessible elbow would close a gap that has been assumed shut since 2003; a positive result would redirect the whole field.

Hypothesis: Maspardin retains no catalytic activity, and Ser109 is not a reactive nucleophile.

Type: Activity-based protein profiling with substrate panel

Experiment: Reconstitute prenylated RAB7A on liposomes or giant unilamellar vesicles with and without purified maspardin, and measure GDI-mediated extraction, TBC1D5-stimulated GTP hydrolysis, and lateral partitioning of RAB7A. Pair this with proximity-dependent biotinylation from maspardin in HeLa cells to recover partners that survive crosslinking but not co-immunoprecipitation.

Hypothesis: Maspardin acts on RAB7A by restricting its extraction from the membrane or its access to TBC1D5, rather than by bridging it to another protein.

Type: In vitro membrane reconstitution plus proximity labelling

Experiment: Map the CD4-binding and RAB7A-binding surfaces of maspardin by mutagenesis and test whether they overlap; in parallel, test whether maspardin orthologues from a species lacking CD4 (for example the Spodoptera littoralis homologue) bind RAB7 orthologues. If the surfaces coincide, the CD4 annotation is a lineage-specific repurposing rather than a separate function.

Hypothesis: The CD4 interaction is a vertebrate-specific elaboration of a conserved RAB7A-binding surface.

Type: Structure-guided mutagenesis with cross-species binding assays

SPG21 (maspardin) review notes

Why this gene was selected

SPG21 is a case where the protein's fold asserts more than the evidence supports. Maspardin carries
an alpha/beta hydrolase (ABHD) domain, is a member of the AB hydrolase superfamily in UniProt, and sits
in Pfam PF00561 (Abhydrolase_1) - yet in twenty-five years no catalytic activity has been reported for
it, and a 2026 J Cell Biol paper still opens by calling it a protein of unknown function:
PMID:41400694 and
PMID:41400694. GOA's molecular-function content is a single
GO:0005515 protein binding from ten interactome screens, plus a GO:0042609 CD4 receptor binding
pair from 2001.

The catalytic-residue question, checked rather than assumed

The nucleophile elbow is intact. In Q9NZD8 the sequence at residues 107-111 is G-A-S-L-G, the canonical
G-x-S-x-G motif, with Ser109 at the nucleophile position; UniProt annotates the AB hydrolase-1 domain at
residues 87-159. So this is not a case of a degenerate fold.

The rest of the triad is not. Four independent sequence analyses, spanning the original disease paper to
a 2025 structural-prediction analysis, all report that the acid and histidine partners of the triad are
absent:

  • PMID:14564668
  • PMID:20661613
  • PMID:19184135
  • PMID:40833810

The original CD4 paper had already framed the domain as non-catalytic and repurposed:
PMID:11113139

What is still missing, and why I have not written "pseudoenzyme" as a finding. No assay has ever been
run. Every statement above is a sequence or structure inference, and the authors of the mouse-knockout
paper say so explicitly: PMID:20661613. An invertebrate comparative study hedges the same way:
PMID:22729480. The convergence of four independent
analyses is strong, and it is reinforced by the fact that the retained Ser109 sits inside the recurrent
pathogenic missense allele p.Ala108Pro, whose effect is protein misfolding and degradation rather than loss
of a catalysis - but converging predictions are not a negative experiment. This goes in
suggested_questions and knowledge_gaps, not into core_functions as an asserted pseudoenzyme call.

What maspardin does do: RAB7A effector

Two 2025-2026 papers, from independent laboratories, converge on the same molecular description.

Bonifacino group: PMID:40833810 and PMID:40833810, with the specificity result
PMID:40833810.

Boonen group: PMID:41400694 and PMID:41400694, with the mechanism running through RAB7
positioning: PMID:41400694 and PMID:41400694.

So the honest molecular function is small GTPase binding (GO:0031267; GO merged "Rab GTPase binding"
into it) - specifically binding GTP-loaded RAB7A - and the honest statement of what remains dark is the
effector output: nobody knows what maspardin does to RAB7A once bound, or how binding keeps RAB7A on
retromer/TBC1D5-positive late endosomes rather than on lysosomes.

The CD4 annotation

GO:0042609 CD4 receptor binding is a real result: a two-hybrid screen with the CD4 cytoplasmic tail
recovered ACP33, and the interaction was mapped to CD4's C-terminal hydrophobic residues
PMID:11113139. It is not overturned,
and I have not removed it. But it is not the conserved core function: maspardin is ubiquitously expressed,
is conserved to insects and beyond (CD4 is vertebrate-specific), the disease is neurological rather than
immunological, and the IBA row is seeded solely by this human gene's own IPI (WITH/FROM
PANTHER:PTN002698091|UniProtKB:Q9NZD8), so the phylogenetic assertion projects a vertebrate-specific
partner across a family that predates it. Marked non-core with that reasoning stated.

Curation position taken

  • GO:0005515 protein binding (10 IPI rows from interactome screens) → MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED.
    This is where maspardin's whole molecular function currently lives in GOA, and it says nothing.
  • GO:0042609 CD4 receptor binding (IBA + IPI) → KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. Real, unreplicated in 25 years,
    taxon-restricted partner, not the conserved function.
  • GO:0005829 cytosol (IBA, IEA, 2x IDA) → ACCEPT. Maspardin genuinely partitions between cytosol
    and the endolysosomal membrane.
  • GO:0010008 endosome membrane (IEA) → ACCEPT as the informative location.
  • GO:0016020 membrane (IEA) → MODIFY to endosome membrane; correct but uninformative.
  • GO:0030140 trans-Golgi network transport vesicle and GO:0005794 Golgi apparatus →
    KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. From the 2001 work; the compartment that the current mechanism runs through is
    the late endosome/lysosome.
  • GO:0007166 cell surface receptor signaling pathway (ARBA IEA) → MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED; a machine
    generalisation of the CD4 story.
  • GO:0050851 antigen receptor-mediated signaling pathway (IC) → KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
  • New: RAB7A binding as GO:0031267 small GTPase binding, late endosome, and the TFEB/mTORC1 and
    lysosome-motility processes.

Open question for experts

Has anyone ever assayed maspardin for hydrolase activity? The pseudoenzyme call has been repeated for
twenty-two years on sequence grounds alone, and the retained, mutation-hit nucleophile elbow is exactly
the configuration in which a fold-retaining protein sometimes turns out to retain residual activity.

    id: GO:0031267

  description: >-
    Maspardin is an effector of RAB7A. It binds the GTP-loaded form of the GTPase from the cytosol and is
    thereby recruited to late endosomal and lysosomal membranes, and it holds RAB7A on retromer- and
    TBC1D5-positive late endosomes rather than on lysosomes. Loss of maspardin moves RAB7A onto the
    lysosomal membrane, away from its GAP TBC1D5, so RAB7A remains GTP-bound; that in turn recruits the
    anterograde adaptor FYCO1 and drives lysosomes toward the cell periphery, and it disturbs Rag-mediated
    presentation of TFEB to mTORC1 so that TFEB is under-phosphorylated and enters the nucleus. Maspardin
    does not interact with the Rag GTPases directly and does not affect canonical mTORC1 substrates. Its
    alpha/beta hydrolase fold is used as a protein-interaction module, not as a catalytic domain.

  knowledge_gaps:
  - gap_statement: >-
      Whether maspardin is a pseudoenzyme is asserted but not demonstrated. No hydrolase, esterase, lipase
      or any other catalytic assay has ever been performed on maspardin, in twenty-two years. Every
      statement that it lacks enzymatic activity - including the ones in the 2025 and 2026 papers - is a
      sequence or structure inference, repeated from the 2003 disease paper onward.
    boundary: >-
      What is established is the fold and one half of the machinery. Maspardin is a bona fide member of the
      alpha/beta hydrolase superfamily (Pfam PF00561, InterPro IPR000073), with parallel beta strands and an
      intact nucleophile elbow: residues 107-111 of Q9NZD8 are G-A-S-L-G, the canonical G-x-S-x-G motif,
      with Ser109 at the nucleophile position, inside the UniProt AB hydrolase-1 domain at 87-159. Four
      independent alignment and structure-prediction analyses agree that the acid and histidine partners of
      the triad are absent, and the original CD4 study already described the domain as non-catalytic and
      functioning as a peptide-binding module. What is missing is a single negative experiment.
    gap_kind:
    - BIOLOGY
    dark_aspect: RESIDUAL_SUBGAP
    status: OPEN
    significance: >-
      A retained, unmutated nucleophile elbow in a protein with no measured activity is exactly the

      substrate panel of lipid and small-molecule esters, would settle it either way; a competitive
