Gene Description

MBTPS2 encodes site-2 protease (S2P), an intramembrane zinc metalloprotease of the M50 family. It is a polytopic membrane protein in the Golgi with cytosolic N- and C-termini, an HEIGH zinc-binding motif, and an Asp ligand buried within membrane-spanning segments. S2P acts after site-1 protease (MBTPS1) in regulated intramembrane proteolysis. Once S1P has trimmed the lumenal domain of a membrane-bound transcription factor, S2P cleaves within its transmembrane helix, which depends on a helix-destabilizing Asn-Pro motif in the substrate. This releases the cytosolic N-terminal bZIP domain, which enters the nucleus. Its principal substrates are SREBP-1 and SREBP-2, which drive cholesterol and fatty acid synthesis and uptake, and ATF6/ATF6B, which induce ER chaperones in the unfolded protein response. S2P also cleaves the CREB3-family factors OASIS (CREB3L1), BBF2H7 (CREB3L2) and CREB-H (CREB3L3). S2P is essential for cholesterol prototrophy and the ATF6 branch of the ER stress response. X-linked hypomorphic variants cause IFAP syndrome, keratosis follicularis spinulosa decalvans, X-linked Olmsted syndrome and osteogenesis imperfecta type XIX. In the bone disease, defective processing of OASIS, ATF6 and SREBP impairs collagen secretion and osteoblast differentiation.

Core Functions

Intramembrane zinc metalloendopeptidase that performs the second, membrane-embedded cleavage in regulated intramembrane proteolysis of S1P-trimmed membrane-bound bZIP transcription factors, releasing their cytosolic domains; principal substrates are SREBP-1/-2 (sterol-regulated lipid synthesis) and ATF6/ATF6B (ER unfolded protein response), with CREB3-family factors (OASIS/CREB3L1, CREB3L2, CREB3L3) as further substrates.

Identity and structure

  • 519 aa polytopic membrane protein; N and C termini cytosolic; "Belongs to the peptidase M50A
    family" (UniProt; MEROPS M50.001; PANTHER PTHR13325:SF3).
  • Zinc metalloprotease with HEIGH motif within a hydrophobic segment and Asp467 as third zinc ligand
    [PMID:10419520 "The HEIGH sequence of S2P, which contains two potential zinc-coordinating residues,
    is contained within a long hydrophobic segment."; "Aspartic acid 467, located approximately 300
    residues away from the HEIGH sequence, appears to provide the third coordinating residue for the
    active site zinc."].
  • Cloned by complementation of CHO M19 cells PMID:9659902.

Mechanism

  • Second, sterol-independent cleavage of SREBP within TM1 after S1P PMID:8674110; cleaves Leu-Cys bond; needs
    Asn-Pro helix-breaking motif in the substrate TM PMID:10805775.
  • Substrate must first be trimmed by S1P (S2P acts on short-lumenal-domain intermediates).
  • ATF6 second cleavage; S2P-null cells fail to induce GRP78 PMID:11163209.
  • Also OASIS/CREB3L1, CREB3L2, CREB3L3 (Reactome R-HSA-8874201) PMID:16417584.
  • Site of action: Golgi (Reactome; UniProt Golgi membrane by similarity/inference from PMID:16417584).

  • KFSD-X, X-linked Olmsted syndrome, and X-linked osteogenesis imperfecta type XIX PMID:27380894.
  • S2P is not needed for lysosomal enzyme trafficking (contrast with S1P/GNPTAB) PMID:30046013.
