Gene Description

CHD4 (Mi-2beta) is an ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler that engages nucleosomal DNA and histones to change nucleosome positioning and accessibility. It is a remodeling subunit of CHD4-containing NuRD assemblies and also forms the ADNP/HP1-associated ChAHP complex. DNA-binding regulators and chromatin readers recruit these assemblies to selected genomic regions, where CHD4 supports context-dependent transcriptional repression and, in other settings, transcriptional competence. HDAC subunits provide the associated deacetylation activity. Nuclear CHD4 also supports local transcriptional control and homologous recombination at DNA lesions. Developmental consequences include regulation of erythroid gene expression and neuronal maturation. A smaller cytoplasmic and centrosomal pool associates with pericentrin, with behavior distinct from CHD3. Pathogenic CHD4 variants cause a neurodevelopmental disorder.
