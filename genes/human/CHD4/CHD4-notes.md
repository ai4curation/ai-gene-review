### Performed molecular work and products

PMID:28977666 directly measures purified human CHD4 ATP hydrolysis and nucleosome repositioning, with recombinant/tagged constructs and chicken or human histone preparations. Its use of “isoform” for CHD3 versus CHD4 concerns paralogs, not the two current CHD4 splice products. PMID:32543371 reconstructs full-length human CHD4 on nucleosomes containing Xenopus histones and Widom601 DNA. The motor engages DNA near SHL +2 and contacts histones; the observed lack of terminal-DNA unwrapping under the tested nucleotide-analogue conditions is not a universal helicase-negative result. The structural paper explicitly identifies its full-length human construct as Q14839-1. Both current product identities are preserved; this assignment does not establish that the other product was tested.

The authentic original PMID:9790534 author PDF distinguishes human Mi2beta from Mi2alpha by peptide sequences and tests NuRD remodeling separately from deacetylation. Its Fig.6 HDAC1 pull-down of in-vitro-translated Mi2beta strengthens the binding conclusion. PMID:33283408 links the CHD4 C terminus to the GATAD2-containing NuRD assembly. Pooled cross-links and older MBD2/3 preparations do not show every paralog combination in a single NuRD particle.

Repression is established in multiple contexts: human ZIP/ZGPAT recruitment at EGFR (PMID:19644445), human CHD4 with mouse TBX5 constructs and mouse cardiac complexes (PMID:26859351), ADNP-mediated ChAHP recruitment (PMID:29795351), and human erythroid CHD4 rescue downstream of ZNF410 (PMID:33301730). ZNF410 binds the CHD4 gene; it is not shown to recruit CHD4 protein to globin. Other NuRD contexts support positive transcriptional regulation, including the mouse erythroid study PMID:19927129 and acute mouse ESC CHD4/MBD3 experiments in PMID:41632506. Those contexts do not require a separate autonomous coactivator headline.

### Repair, localization and propagation

PMID:25593309 directly assays CHD4-dependent DR-GFP repair, damage recruitment, local transcriptional silencing and RAD51 loading at FokI lesions. Its AsiSI RAD51 experiment targets ZMYND8, and the recruitment rescue is ZMYND8 rescue; neither is relabeled as a CHD4 experiment. CHD4 supplies chromatin work rather than recombinase chemistry.

Human RPE1 imaging in PMID:17626165 establishes nuclear, cytoplasmic and centrosomal CHD4 pools. CHD4 and CHD3 depletion have different effects on centrosomal pericentrin; a CHD3 anchoring mechanism is not transferred to CHD4. Authentic HPA XML and visually inspected A431/U2OS images corroborate nucleoplasmic localization. Reactome event locations are kept separate from partner enzymes and substrate reactions.
