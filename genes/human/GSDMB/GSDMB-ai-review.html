Gene Description

Gasdermin-B, a member of the gasdermin family of pore-forming proteins and the only human gasdermin with no rodent ortholog. Like its paralogs it is an autoinhibited two-domain cytosolic protein in which the C-terminal domain masks the membrane-binding surface of the N-terminal domain. Granzyme A delivered by cytotoxic T and natural killer cells cleaves the interdomain linker at Lys244, releasing the N-terminal fragment, which oligomerizes into a 27-subunit transmembrane pore. Recognition is unusually specific: granzyme A must dimerize and engages an exosite on the GSDMB C-terminal domain, and the mouse enzyme does this inefficiently. Pore competence is determined by alternative splicing rather than by cleavage. Granzyme A cleaves every splice isoform, but only isoforms that translate exon 6 assemble a functional pore, because exon-6-derived elements form the belt that the oligomer needs; isoforms with a deleted or modified exon 6 yield N-terminal fragments that cannot lyse cells and can act dominant-negatively over those that can. Different isoforms also read different lipids and therefore different target membranes: the canonical exon-6-containing form binds inner-leaflet phosphoinositides and sulfatide and permeabilizes the human plasma membrane, whereas another isoform instead binds cardiolipin and lipid A and kills Gram-negative bacteria directly rather than lysing the host cell. Shigella flexneri counters this by secreting the E3 ligase IpaH7.8, which ubiquitinates GSDMB for proteasomal degradation. GSDMB is expressed mainly in gastrointestinal and airway epithelium and in derived tumours, is induced by interferon-gamma, and polymorphisms at the locus are associated with asthma and inflammatory bowel disease.

Core Functions

Gasdermin-B is an autoinhibited cytosolic protein that cytotoxic lymphocytes convert into a pore. Granzyme A, which must dimerize and engage an exosite on the GSDMB C-terminal domain, cleaves the interdomain linker at Lys244; the released N-terminal fragment binds inner-leaflet phosphoinositides of the target-cell plasma membrane and oligomerizes into a 27-subunit transmembrane pore, permeabilizing the cell and causing pyroptosis. This activity is gated by alternative splicing rather than by cleavage - granzyme A cleaves every isoform, but only isoforms translating exon 6 provide the belt elements the oligomer needs, so Q8TAX9-4 (GSDMB3) and Q8TAX9-6 (GSDMB4) are pore-competent while Q8TAX9-1, Q8TAX9-2 (GSDMB2), Q8TAX9-3 (GSDMB1) and Q8TAX9-5 are not, and the non-competent fragments inhibit the competent ones dominant-negatively. Cleavage by neutrophil elastase or caspases yields shorter, non-cytotoxic fragments and is inhibitory. Interferon-gamma raises GSDMB expression.

Q: Do the non-pore-competent isoforms (Q8TAX9-1, Q8TAX9-2, Q8TAX9-3) still traffic to the plasma membrane after cleavage? Their dominant-negative inhibition of the competent isoforms implies they co-assemble, which would mean the plasma membrane location is not isoform-restricted even though pore formation is. This determines whether localization annotations should carry an isoform qualifier at all.

Suggested experts: Judy Lieberman

Q: Is the cardiolipin/lipid A versus phosphoinositide lipid preference determined by the exon-6 region at all? Q8TAX9-1 and Q8TAX9-4 differ only at Asn221 and by the loss of residues 234-242, and Q8TAX9-6 carries that same 234-242 loss yet remains host-cell pore-competent, so the lipid switch may not map to the deletion that explains pore competence.

Suggested experts: Neal M. Alto, Osnat Herzberg

Q: Does exon-6-containing GSDMB damage mitochondria as well as the plasma membrane? PMID:36899106 reports concomitant mitochondrial damage from exon-6-containing N-terminal constructs, while PMID:36157507 found essentially no GSDMB-NT in the mitochondrial fraction and no cytochrome c release. This has not been reconciled.

Q: Which GSDMB isoforms are expressed in the tissues where GSDMB matters clinically - airway epithelium in asthma and gut epithelium in IBD - as opposed to the tumour cell lines where isoform composition has been measured? The asthma and IBD risk polymorphisms sit in the C-terminal domain, so their effect may be on granzyme A exosite engagement rather than on pore formation.

GSDMB (Q8TAX9) review notes

Why this gene was selected

GSDMB is the gasdermin whose pore-forming competence is isoform-dependent, and the
question put to this review was: which splice isoforms are pore-competent, and is
granzyme-A-triggered pyroptosis a general GSDMB property or restricted to exon-6-containing
isoforms? GOA carries none of this: five family-level IBAs and six localization rows, with
no isoform qualification anywhere. GSDMB is also the only human gasdermin with no rodent
ortholog, which matters for how the mouse literature can be read.

The isoform framework resolves this. UniProt now curates the two profiles as isoform
properties: Q8TAX9-4 binds host inner-leaflet phosphoinositides (PI4P, PI5P, PI(4,5)P2) and
sulfatide and lyses host cells, while Q8TAX9-1 binds lipid A and cardiolipin, kills
Gram-negative bacteria, and explicitly does not bind host inner-leaflet
phosphoinositides. Gong et al. used the 416-residue isoform, i.e. Q8TAX9-4; Hansen et al.
used isoform 1. So the two groups were, in an important sense, working on different proteins.
This is the single most consequential fact for curating GSDMB, and GOA currently expresses
none of it.

Adjudication taken in this review

  1. Pyroptotic pore formation is accepted as a core GSDMB function, since four
    laboratories agree on it including two independent cryo-EM pore structures, but the
    isoform restriction is written into every relevant reason, and the pore-competence
    NEW annotation carries isoform: Q8TAX9-4.
  2. Antibacterial defence is accepted as core, not demoted. GSDMB is one of the few
    gasdermins with direct bactericidal evidence on the target gene itself (PMID:34022140),
    and GSDMB appears in its own WITH/FROM for GO:0042742 - which, per project guidance,
    marks experimental grounding on the target rather than circularity.
  3. The phosphoinositide-binding IBAs are accepted, because unlike GSDMC there is
    direct GSDMB measurement behind them (PMID:28154144, and UniProt's curation of isoform 4),
    with the isoform conflict recorded in reason.
  4. Phosphatidylserine binding is demoted to non-core. It is the one lipid term with no
    GSDMB-specific measurement in either direction: its WITH/FROM is mouse Gsdmd plus the
    family node only, Chao tested phosphoinositides/sulfatide/cardiolipin, and the word
    phosphatidylserine does not appear anywhere in the Hansen full text. It is a pure
    GSDMD-to-family transfer.
  5. Cardiolipin binding and Gram-negative killing are proposed as NEW, tagged
    isoform: Q8TAX9-1, because they are the distinctive, well-evidenced GSDMB activities
    that GOA is missing entirely.

  description: Gasdermin-B is an autoinhibited cytosolic protein that cytotoxic lymphocytes convert
    into a pore. Granzyme A, which must dimerize and engage an exosite on the GSDMB C-terminal domain,
    cleaves the interdomain linker at Lys244; the released N-terminal fragment binds inner-leaflet

    rather than by cleavage - granzyme A cleaves every isoform, but only isoforms translating exon

    the non-competent fragments inhibit the competent ones dominant-negatively. Cleavage by neutrophil
    elastase or caspases yields shorter, non-cytotoxic fragments and is inhibitory. Interferon-gamma
    raises GSDMB expression.
