Gene Description

SMPD4 (neutral sphingomyelinase 3, nSMase3) is a magnesium-dependent, neutral sphingomyelin phosphodiesterase (EC 3.1.4.12) that hydrolyses membrane sphingomyelin to ceramide and phosphocholine. Despite its name it is a distinct enzyme unrelated by sequence to the SMPD2/SMPD3 (nSMase1/nSMase2) extended-calcineurin-like family. It is a C-tail-anchored, single-pass integral membrane protein of the endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus, and it also resides at the outer nuclear envelope where it associates with nuclear pore complex proteins (it was independently identified as the nuclear envelope transmembrane protein NET13). Its catalytic activity is Mg2+-dependent with a neutral pH optimum, is stimulated by phosphatidylserine and by tumour necrosis factor, and is inhibited by scyphostatin. By generating ceramide from sphingomyelin, SMPD4 contributes to membrane sphingolipid homeostasis and to endoplasmic reticulum organization and ER-to-nuclear-envelope crosstalk, and it participates in stress-response signalling (DNA damage, TNF) linked to apoptosis and cell-cycle/mitotic progression. It is highly expressed in heart and skeletal muscle, where it mediates TNF-stimulated oxidant production. Bi-allelic loss-of-function variants in SMPD4 cause an autosomal-recessive neurodevelopmental disorder with microcephaly, arthrogryposis, and structural brain anomalies (NEDMABA).

CAUTION — GO:0050290 is the WRONG MF term for SMPD4

  • GOA carries many GO:0050290 sphingomyelin phosphodiesterase D activity annotations (IBA, IEA-InterPro,
    IEA-GO_REF:2, IDA PMID:25180167, IDA PMID:16517606). GO:0050290 = EC 3.1.4.41, SMase D, reaction
    "sphingomyelin = ceramide 1-phosphate + choline" (a phospholipase-D-type cleavage found in venom/bacteria).
  • SMPD4 is a type-C sphingomyelinase (EC 3.1.4.12), reaction "sphingomyelin = ceramide + phosphocholine".
    UniProt states EC=3.1.4.12 explicitly. [file:human/SMPD4/SMPD4-uniprot.txt "EC=3.1.4.12"]
  • The correct GO MF term is GO:0004767 sphingomyelin phosphodiesterase activity (EC:3.1.4.12; verified in go.db).
  • Therefore GO:0050290 annotations are MODIFY → GO:0004767. The experimental (IDA/EXP) ones are not removed
    (they demonstrate the real phosphocholine-releasing activity); the term label is just wrong.
  • This is a known family-wide GO curation artifact: nSMases are recurrently mis-mapped to the SMase-D term.
