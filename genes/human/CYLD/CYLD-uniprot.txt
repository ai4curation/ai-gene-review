CC   -!- FUNCTION: Deubiquitinase that specifically cleaves 'Lys-63'- and linear
CC       'Met-1'-linked polyubiquitin chains and is involved in NF-kappa-B
CC       activation and TNF-induced necroptosis (PubMed:18313383,
CC       PubMed:18636086, PubMed:19481526, PubMed:26670046, PubMed:26997266,
CC       PubMed:27458237, PubMed:27591049, PubMed:27746020, PubMed:29291351,
CC       PubMed:32185393). Negatively regulates NF-kappa-B activation by
CC       deubiquitinating upstream signaling factors (PubMed:12917689,
CC       PubMed:12917691, PubMed:19481526, PubMed:32185393). Contributes to the
CC       regulation of cell survival, proliferation and differentiation via its
CC       effects on NF-kappa-B activation (PubMed:12917690). Negative regulator
CC       of Wnt signaling (PubMed:20227366). Inhibits HDAC6 and thereby promotes
CC       acetylation of alpha-tubulin and stabilization of microtubules
CC       (PubMed:19893491). Plays a role in the regulation of microtubule
CC       dynamics, and thereby contributes to the regulation of cell
CC       proliferation, cell polarization, cell migration, and angiogenesis
CC       (PubMed:18222923, PubMed:20194890). Required for normal cell cycle
CC       progress and normal cytokinesis (PubMed:17495026, PubMed:19893491).
CC       Inhibits nuclear translocation of NF-kappa-B (PubMed:18636086). Plays a
CC       role in the regulation of inflammation and the innate immune response,
CC       via its effects on NF-kappa-B activation (PubMed:18636086). Dispensable
CC       for the maturation of intrathymic natural killer cells, but required
CC       for the continued survival of immature natural killer cells (By
CC       similarity). Negatively regulates TNFRSF11A signaling and
CC       osteoclastogenesis (By similarity). Involved in the regulation of
CC       ciliogenesis, allowing ciliary basal bodies to migrate and dock to the
CC       plasma membrane; this process does not depend on NF-kappa-B activation
