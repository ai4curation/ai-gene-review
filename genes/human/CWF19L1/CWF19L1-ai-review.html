Gene Description

CWF19L1 is a nucleoplasmic CWF19-family protein that associates with spliceosomal proteins and supports pre-mRNA splicing. Human cell experiments connect it to U5 snRNP and PRPF19-complex components and show altered alternative splicing after its depletion. A conserved Drn1-like capacity to stimulate RNA lariat debranching is inferred from phylogeny and yeast biochemistry; direct activation of human DBR1 by CWF19L1 has not been established. The canonical protein contains 538 amino acids, while alternative splicing produces shorter 242- and 401-residue forms. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause a recessive ataxia syndrome with intellectual disability and cerebellar hypoplasia.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Compare purified human DBR1 alone and with each sequence-verified CWF19L1 product using branched RNA, with protein-alone controls, matched protein concentrations and stability measurements. In parallel, test rescue of splicing defects in CWF19L1-depleted cells without assuming that the biochemical and cellular phenotypes share one mechanism.
