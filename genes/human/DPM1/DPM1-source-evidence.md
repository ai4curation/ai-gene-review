## Preparation and identification scope

The original Catalan Methods are paraphrased here; no new verbatim source passage is reproduced. Printed pages 97–100 describe CTAB/digitonin treatment and repeated washing of the nuclear preparation, with microscopy assessment. The procedure-efficiency estimate of approximately 99% is distinct from the 2011 article abstract’s reported 99.9% nuclear purity; these are not interchangeable measurements. Printed pages 112–113 describe tryptic LC-MS/MS, the human Swiss-Prot search, FDR 0.05 and a minimum of two peptides including at least one unique peptide. The figure caption describes a blank-gel-band control.

The target table provides positive detection in the stated preparation. It does not establish permanent nuclear residence, an import mechanism or a nuclear catalytic role for DPM1. Microscopy of the preparation is not DPM1-specific immunolocalization. The evidence therefore supports a contextual, non-core localization assessment with these limits.
