# XPNPEP1 notes

## 2026-11-26 review

- PANTHER family: `PTHR43763`, subfamily `PTHR43763:SF6`.
- No IRD or IKR PAINT loss falls on the human XPNPEP1 lineage.
- The current human GOA has no IBA rows, so this review does not need a family-review
  exception or PAINT pruning request.
- XPNPEP1 is the soluble cytosolic AP-P: Cottrell et al. cloned full-length human
  cytosolic aminopeptidase P and showed that recombinant enzyme hydrolyzes the
  X-Pro bond in bradykinin and substance P [PMID:11106490].
- Li et al. solved the human XPNPEP1 structure and showed that the enzyme is a
  homodimer with two catalytic manganese ions per subunit [PMID:18515364].
- Rao et al. found that CQ31/CQ04 target PEPD and XPNPEP1 and that dual inhibition
  of those M24B aminopeptidases is sufficient for CARD8 inflammasome activation
  in sensitive blood-cell lines [PMID:35165443]. This makes the programmed-cell-death
  row plausible but not core.
- The EPS-urine exosome row rests on a high-throughput proteomic survey
  [PMID:23533145]. It is kept as a non-core exported pool; cytosol remains the
  core location.
