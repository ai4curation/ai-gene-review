# XPNPEP1 manual deep research

XPNPEP1 is the human soluble, cytosolic aminopeptidase P/Xaa-Pro aminopeptidase.
The 1997 XPNPEPL cDNA paper identified a 623 amino acid human homolog of
X-prolyl aminopeptidases [PMID:9465902]. The full-length cytosolic enzyme was
then cloned and expressed; recombinant AP-P cleaved the X-Pro bond in bradykinin
and substance P and was activated preferentially by manganese [PMID:11106490].

The human enzyme is a manganese-dependent M24B metallopeptidase. Its crystal
structure revealed a homodimer whose catalytic domains coordinate two Mn(II) ions
per subunit; interface mutagenesis supported the importance of dimerization for
full activity [PMID:18515364].

The M24B inhibitor study adds a cell-biological role downstream of the peptidase
reaction. A CQ31 photoaffinity analog enriched only PEPD and XPNPEP1 from HEK
293T lysates, CQ04 inhibited recombinant XPNPEP1 activity, and combined PEPD/XPNPEP1
loss or inhibition prevented catabolism of proline-containing peptides, weakly
inhibiting DPP8/9 and activating CARD8 pyroptosis in sensitive cells [PMID:35165443].

XPNPEP1 is primarily cytosolic. A single high-throughput EPS-urine exosome study
detected the protein in expressed prostatic secretion exosomes [PMID:23533145],
which is credible as a vesicle pool but not the principal compartment of the enzyme.
