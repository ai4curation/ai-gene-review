Gene Description

CEP250, also called C-Nap1, is a large coiled-coil centrosomal protein concentrated at the proximal ends of parental centrioles. It forms cohesive assemblies and anchors components of the centrosomal linker, maintaining interphase centriole proximity and organizing the centrosomal accumulation of linker proteins. Its association with the centrosome changes during the cell cycle and is regulated by phosphorylation, including modification by NEK2. Human CEP250 variants are associated with retinal and auditory disease; experimental ciliary phenotypes depend on cell type and perturbation context.
