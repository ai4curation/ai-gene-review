Gene Description

DIAPH1 is a diaphanous-related formin that couples Rho-family signaling to actin filament assembly. Its FH1–FH2 region promotes actin assembly, while an N-terminal regulatory region and C-terminal autoregulatory segment restrain activity until relieved by activated Rho proteins. Cytosolic and membrane-associated pools organize actin structures; additional nuclear, centrosomal and spindle-associated pools occur in specific cellular settings. DIAPH1 also participates in receptor-linked cytoskeletal signaling and PKD2 localization. Disruption of its regulation or expression affects platelet architecture, hearing and neurodevelopment. Alternative splicing produces three protein isoforms with differences in their internal sequences.
