# TNFSF11 manual literature synthesis

Perplexity deep research was unavailable in this workspace, so this file records a manual synthesis from cached primary papers, Reactome events, UniProt, and the existing PTHR11471 family review.

## Core biology

TNFSF11 encodes RANKL/TRANCE/OPGL, a type II TNF-family ligand for TNFRSF11A/RANK and the decoy receptor TNFRSF11B/OPG. It is a membrane protein with a soluble ectodomain, and the PTHR11471 family review places it in SF3 as the subfamily that "binds the non-death-domain receptor RANK (TNFRSF11A) to drive TRAF6-mediated osteoclast differentiation".

Primary osteoclast papers directly establish the RANKL/RANK/OPG axis. PMID:9568710 reports that "OPG ligand (OPGL) binds to a unique hematopoeitic progenitor cell that is committed to the osteoclast lineage and stimulates the rapid induction of genes that typify osteoclast development" and that OPG blocks OPGL effects in vitro and in vivo. PMID:9730902 then shows that "TRANCE expression by osteoblasts appears to be both necessary and sufficient for hormone-mediated activation of mature osteoclasts, and TRANCE-R is likely to be a receptor for signal transduction for activation of the osteoclast and its survival."

The original TRANCE cloning paper also supports the immune arm: soluble human TRANCE "induced c-Jun N-terminal kinase (JNK) activation in T cells" and the transcript was up-regulated by TCR stimulation [PMID:9312132]. Keep direct RANKL cytokine/receptor-binding and osteoclast differentiation/activation rows; avoid projecting generic TNF death-receptor apoptosis terms onto RANKL.
