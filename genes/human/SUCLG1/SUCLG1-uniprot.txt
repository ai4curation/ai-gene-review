CC   -!- FUNCTION: Succinyl-CoA synthetase functions in the citric acid cycle
CC       (TCA), coupling the hydrolysis of succinyl-CoA to the synthesis of
CC       either ATP or GTP and thus represents the only step of substrate-level
CC       phosphorylation in the TCA (PubMed:34492704, PubMed:40108300). The
CC       alpha subunit of the enzyme binds the substrates coenzyme A and
CC       phosphate, while succinate binding and specificity for either ATP or
CC       GTP is provided by different beta subunits (By similarity). Also able
CC       to act as an itaconyl- and malyl-CoA synthetase (PubMed:40108300).
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03222, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34492704,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40108300}.
