# OAS3 notes

OAS3 is the three-OAS-domain paralog. Domain I is non-catalytic but binds long
dsRNA, and domain III carries the catalytic activity. Recombinant human OAS3
"synthesizes considerably longer 2-5As than previously reported" and can activate
RNase L intracellularly [PMID:25275129].

The macrophage perturbation paper is useful for OAS1/OAS3 effects on chemokine
output, but it does not make OAS3 a direct RIG-I or MDA5 signaling component.
Those RLR and chemokine-production rows are marked over-annotated to avoid
turning a downstream transcript phenotype into pathway membership.

PTHR11258 propagates a membrane IBA to OAS3 from an OAS2-seeded root row. No
direct OAS3 membrane evidence was found in the cached record set, so the family
review requests an IRD at the OAS3 clade while leaving dsRNA binding, 2-5A
synthetase activity, cytosol, nucleoplasm and antiviral process terms intact.
