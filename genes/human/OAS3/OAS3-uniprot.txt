CC   -!- FUNCTION: Interferon-induced, dsRNA-activated antiviral enzyme which
CC       plays a critical role in cellular innate antiviral response. In
CC       addition, it may also play a role in other cellular processes such as
CC       apoptosis, cell growth, differentiation and gene regulation. Catalyzes
CC       preferentially the dimerization of ATP to form 2'-5'-diadenylate (2-
CC       5A), but can also form the trimeric 2'-5'-oligoadenylate. The
CC       oligoadenylate then binds to the inactive monomeric form of
CC       ribonuclease L (RNase L) leading to its dimerization and subsequent
CC       activation. Activation of RNase L leads to degradation of cellular as
CC       well as viral RNA, resulting in the inhibition of protein synthesis,
CC       thus terminating viral replication. Can mediate the antiviral effect
CC       via the classical RNase L-dependent pathway or an alternative antiviral
CC       pathway independent of RNase L. Displays antiviral activity against
CC       Chikungunya virus (CHIKV), Dengue virus, Sindbis virus (SINV) and
CC       Semliki forest virus (SFV). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19056102,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19923450, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9880533}.
