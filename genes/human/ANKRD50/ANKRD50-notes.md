# ANKRD50 notes

- ANKRD50 was found in the VPS35 interactome, and its RNAi depletion phenocopies loss of SNX27-retromer: GLUT1 is lost from the surface and routed to lysosomes [PMID:25278552, full text]. It is an essential component of the SNX27-retromer-WASH supercomplex and binds several parts of it directly and cooperatively. The cargoes include SLC1A4, and VPS35 D620N weakens retromer's association with ANKRD50 [PMID:27909246, abstract].
- Endocytic recycling (IMP): ACCEPT, core. ANKRD50 is a component of the machinery, so this is participation. Endosome (IEA): ACCEPT.
- VPS35 IPI → MODIFY to GO:1905394 retromer complex binding. Dengue-virus IPI → REMOVE.
- Candidate late-onset PD gene in a family exome study [PMID:40494419]; no GO term drawn.
- PTHR24173 (shared with ANKRD16 and ANKRD28) is a large mixed family whose annotated nodes do not reach ANKRD50 (PAN-GO 0). The family files are not committed, consistent with the ANKRD16 and ANKRD28 reviews.
