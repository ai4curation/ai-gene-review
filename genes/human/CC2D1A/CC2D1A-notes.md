## Transcription and historical construct provenance

[PMID:12917378](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6740452/) originally characterized a mouse-brain 595-residue Freud-1 clone with rat Htr1a reporters and rat L6/RN46A cells. Recombinant-protein DNA-binding and reporter controls support rodent sequence recognition and repression. The human sequence comparison does not itself identify an experimentally tested current human product. Selected Methods, cloning results and captions were read from a primary-paper reproduction after official access failed; the normal cache is abstract-only. The curator's historical product mapping remains unresolved. This warrants a provenance question, not a claim of paralog misattribution. Htr1a and SV40 reporter experiments also do not positively establish the snRNA specificity of GO:1905381; this does not establish that CC2D1A cannot regulate snRNA.

Separate human evidence comes from [PMID:27914010](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5479700/). Selected official Methods, Results and captions describe endogenous and tagged Freud-1 in HEK-293 and SK-N-SH cells, Brg1 interaction, promoter ChIP and depletion experiments. In HEK-293 cells, Freud-1 depletion reduces Brg1 recruitment, while substantial HTR1A derepression requires combined depletion. In SK-N-SH cells, recruitment is mutually dependent and either depletion increases HTR1A expression. These support human nuclear transcriptional repression with cell-dependent partners. ChIP alone does not prove direct DNA contact, and Brg1 ATPase or HDAC chemistry must not be assigned to CC2D1A. The antibody recognizes multiple products; no isoform-specific core function is inferred.

PAINT IBA assertions are ancestral-node judgments. Target self-inclusion in WITH/FROM is expected when its experimental annotations helped seed that judgment. Neither self-inclusion nor a short donor list establishes circularity. The nuclear transcriptional role is independently corroborated; historical human IDA provenance and snRNA specificity are separate questions.

## Noncatalytic signaling coordination

[PMID:18662999](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2546995/) tests full-length human Aki1 with human PDK1/Akt constructs. Selected official Methods, Results and captions were inspected. Sequential immunoprecipitation with mock/S6K controls supports a ternary complex; translated, immunopurified Aki1 binds recombinant partners. Depletion weakens PDK1–Akt association and Akt Thr308 phosphorylation, without a comparable effect on Ser473 or intrinsic PDK1 activity. PIP3 production and Akt membrane-recruitment controls distinguish coordination from upstream PI3K activation. EGF versus IGF-1/PDGF controls and concentration-dependent reversal limit generalization. The evidence supports noncatalytic signaling-adaptor activity, not kinase activity or universal growth-factor regulation. The separately accessed primary Results report that immunoprecipitated Aki1 accelerates recombinant PDK1-mediated Akt phosphorylation in the presence of PIP3. This is a biochemical kinase assay, not a CC2D1A depletion-rescue experiment or fully purified binary binding reconstitution. The normal PMID:18662999 cache remains abstract-only; its brief evidence anchor does not substitute for the separately accessed Methods and Results.

[GO:0035591 signaling adaptor activity](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0035591) concerns bringing signaling molecules together to coordinate their functions and is a child of protein–macromolecule adaptor activity. This is a candidate core molecular function supported by the mechanism above. A new process annotation is not inferred merely from apoptosis, viability or disease phenotypes.

The original NF-kappaB source, PMID:12761501, is a human cDNA overexpression screen. Its cached abstract does not expose the CC2D1A-specific table. [PMID:20529849](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2915672/) independently identifies the screened clone and tests human CC2D1A expression, reporter activity, IKK immunoprecipitate activity and target-gene expression. Selected Methods/Results include expression and pathway-knockdown controls. Ubc13 depletion/rescue tests dependence on Ubc13, not endogenous CC2D1A necessity. The evidence supports a context-dependent positive canonical-pathway effect. It does not establish CC2D1A kinase or ubiquitin-ligase activity, direct TRAF2/TAK1 recruitment, or a universal physiological stimulus. The normal PMID:20529849 cache contains XML-derived text. Its complete available nonduplicated Methods, Results, main figure captions and Discussion were read; no figure pixels or supplementary experiments were independently inspected. The authors did not resolve a physiological activating stimulus in the cell systems examined.
