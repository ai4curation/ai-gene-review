Gene Description

COL6A3 encodes the alpha3 chain of collagen VI, an extracellular matrix protein with an extended array of N-terminal von Willebrand factor A domains, a short collagenous region and C-terminal globular domains. Long alpha3 products associate with alpha1 and alpha2 chains to form collagen VI units that assemble into dimers, tetramers and extracellular beaded microfibrils. Specific N- and C-terminal regions support higher-order assembly and matrix deposition. Human recombinant N-terminal domains bind heparin and hyaluronan. Proteolytic processing releases C-terminal fragments, including C5/endotrophin; experimentally studied fragments differ from the intact matrix chain. Alternative splicing produces five recorded human products, two of which lack the collagenous and C-terminal regions. Pathogenic COL6A3 variants cause collagen VI-related muscular dystrophies.

Core Functions

Alpha3(VI) contributes the long structural chain of canonical alpha1/alpha2/alpha3 collagen VI units. N-terminal and C-terminal interfaces support extracellular end-to-end tetramer association and beaded-microfibril deposition. The two short products lacking the collagenous and C-terminal regions have not been shown to substitute for the assembled long chain.
