CC   -!- FUNCTION: Essential sperm cell-surface protein required for
CC       fertilization by acting as a ligand for IZUMO1R/JUNO receptor on egg.
CC       The IZUMO1:IZUMO1R/JUNO interaction is a necessary adhesion event
CC       between sperm and egg that is required for fertilization but is not
CC       sufficient for cell fusion. The ligand-receptor interaction probably
CC       does not act as a membrane 'fusogen'. Acts a ligand for the human-
CC       specific oolemma epitope FCRL3/MAIA during fertilization
CC       (PubMed:36070373). FCRL3/MAIA replaces IZUMO1R/JUNO as IZUMO1 receptor
CC       after sperm-egg adhesion, which permits species-specific gamete fusion
CC       (PubMed:36070373). Plays a critical role in sperm-oolemma binding prior
CC       to plasma membrane fusion. Can mediate cell-cell fusion in cultured
CC       mammalian cells independently of its binding to IZUMO1R/JUNO (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9D9J7,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36070373}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer, homodimer; disulfide-linked and homooligomer;
CC       depending on the context (PubMed:27309808, PubMed:27309818). Interacts
CC       with IZUMO1R/JUNO. IZUMO1 and IZUMO1R/JUNO form a complex with 1:1
CC       stoichiometry (PubMed:27309808, PubMed:27309818). In gamete
CC       recognition, IZUMO1R/JUNO first binds to monomeric IZUMO1 (By
CC       similarity). The weak, but specific interaction with IZUMO1R/JUNO
CC       induces IZUMO1 homodimerization (By similarity). The process follows a
CC       tight binding phase where IZUMO1 bends the entire structure towards the
CC       sperm membrane side through a thiol-disulfide exchange reaction (By
CC       similarity). The molecule no longer binds to IZUMO1R/JUNO and instead
CC       binds to a putative second oocyte receptor (By similarity). Interacts
CC       with ACE3 (By similarity). Part of a oolemmal binding multimeric
CC       complex (IZUMO1 complex) composed at least of IZUMO1 and GLIPR1L1; the
CC       complex assemblage is influenced by the maturation status of the male
CC       germ cell. Interacts with GLIPR1L1. Interacts with FREY; the
CC       interaction retains IZUMO1 at the endoplasmic reticulum membrane and
CC       coordinates IZUMO1 complex assembly (By similarity). Interacts with
CC       FCRL3/MAIA (via extracellular domain); the interaction replaces
CC       IZUMO1R/JUNO as IZUMO1 receptor after sperm-egg adhesion
CC       (PubMed:36070373). Interacts with WDR54 (By similarity). Forms a
CC       complex with SPACA6 and TMEM81 on spermatocyte cell membrane
CC       (PubMed:39423812). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q6AY06,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9D9J7, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27309808,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27309818, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36070373,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39423812}.
