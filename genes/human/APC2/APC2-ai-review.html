Gene Description

APC2 (APCL) is a large, brain-enriched APC-family protein that associates with microtubules and actin-rich structures and binds beta-catenin. Its C-terminal regions support microtubule lattice association, interactions with growing plus ends and DYNLL2-dependent clustering. These activities help organize neuronal microtubules and dendritic morphology; local stabilization can coexist with ongoing microtubule growth and shrinkage. APC2 also reduces beta-catenin abundance and beta-catenin-dependent transcription. Perinuclear, Golgi and peripheral actin-associated pools have been observed in human cells. Biallelic loss of APC2 causes a neurodevelopmental disorder with lissencephaly and subcortical heterotopia.
