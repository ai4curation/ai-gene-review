# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ANKRD45 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ANKRD45 is a two-ankyrin-repeat-containing protein implicated in cell division, where it dynamically relocates during mitosis and concentrates at the midbody ring during cytokinesis [PMID:31208154]. It is required for cell proliferation: depletion by siRNA or CRISPR/Cas9 induces apoptosis in cultured cells, and zebrafish ankrd45 mutants show liver defects [PMID:31208154]. Beyond its midbody-ring localization and pro-survival/proliferative requirement, no molecular mechanism, binding partner, or substrate for ANKRD45 has been characterized in the available corpus.
