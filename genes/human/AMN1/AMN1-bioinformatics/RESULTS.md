## Interpretation

- **Every donor of both IBA rows carries a UniProt F-box domain.** Neither yeast donor is yeast AMN1 (P38285).
- **All six PTHR13318:SF254 members (PROTEIN AMN1 HOMOLOG) lack an F-box and are 249-258 residues long.** No IBA donor is in SF254.
- **Family drift.** Two GO:0031146 seeds (Q8W104, Q8BH16) and one GO:0019005 seed (Q8W104) are now classified in PTHR13382 in UniProt, and SGD:S000002714 (Q06640) has no PANTHER assignment. Yet PTHR13318-paint.tsv still lists them as seeds of PTN002547163. This is version drift since the IBD was made, and it further weakens the inference for AMN1.
- **No F-box in AMN1.** UniProt annotates none, and InterPro, Pfam and SMART detect only leucine-rich repeats. At most 25 of the 48 FBXL15 F-box positions align to the AMN1 N-terminus, with 6 identities. AMN1 therefore has no recognizable F-box, the SKP1-binding module that makes FBXL proteins SCF substrate receptors. This is a sequence-level inference; SKP1 binding has not been tested.
