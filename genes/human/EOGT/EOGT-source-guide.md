## Inherited and database assertions

- **PAINT:** both imported IBA assertions are placed at **PTN001841612**. The pinned IBD file identifies fly FBgn0264672 for ER lumen and fly FBgn0264672 plus mouse MGI:2141669 for catalytic activity. The live [PANTHER tree](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/treeinfo?family=PTHR20961) and [MSA](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/familymsa?family=PTHR20961), both version 19, place human Q5NDL2 below this node. The ungapped human leaf exactly matches the canonical protein, with no matching NOT assertion on its path in the pinned August 2026 IBD snapshot. Human, mouse and fly donors are assigned to PTHR20961:SF148, officially named EGF DOMAIN-SPECIFIC O-LINKED N-ACETYLGLUCOSAMINE TRANSFERASE. This verifies the inherited claim's location; the short donor list is not treated as weakness.
- **Mouse donor identity:** [Q8BYW9](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8BYW9.json) maps to MGI:2141669 and ENSMUSP00000061610.5. Current [QuickGO mouse annotations](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/annotation/search?geneProductId=Q8BYW9&limit=200) include IDA GO:0097363 and GO:0006493 citing PMID:22310717.
- **Fly donor identity:** [Q9VQB7](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9VQB7.json) maps to FBgn0264672. Current [QuickGO fly annotations](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/annotation/search?geneProductId=UniProtKB:Q9VQB7&limit=200) include direct ER-lumen and catalytic assertions from PMID:22158438. Initial FlyBase-form API requests returned identifier-format errors; these errors were not treated as absence of donor evidence.
- **Location mapping:** [SL-0096](https://rest.uniprot.org/locations/SL-0096.json) maps directly to GO:0005788. Human canonical signal and HDEL features are consistent with the inherited location; shorter products require separate assessment.
- **InterPro:** [IPR007657](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/entry/InterPro/IPR007657/) is the Glycosyltransferase 61 family and [IPR049625](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/entry/InterPro/IPR049625/) its catalytic domain. Both map to broad GO:0016757. These entries also encompass enzymes with different substrate specificities. Direct EOGT evidence supports refinement to the already present GO:0097363.
- **Reaction mapping:** [Rhea:48904](https://www.rhea-db.org/rhea/48904) and [Rhea:48908](https://www.rhea-db.org/rhea/48908) describe GlcNAc transfer from UDP-GlcNAc to protein serine and threonine, respectively. They support reaction identity, not EGF specificity or subcellular location on their own.
- **Term specificity:** [GO:0097363](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0097363) is a current molecular function and an is_a descendant of GO:0016757. [GO:0006493](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0006493) permits subsequent sugar-chain elongation. Its current child [GO:7770074](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:7770074) explicitly excludes elongation. That narrower definition does not adequately cover the secretory-pathway glycan reported in the mammalian donor source; retaining the parent process does not assign downstream galactosyltransferase activity to EOGT.

Database snapshots were captured on 2026-10-24. Original evidence remains source- and preparation-specific. No additional process assertion is inferred from disease association, fly rescue, or discussion hypotheses.


## Ontology term verification

Fresh [QuickGO term responses](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/ontology/go/terms/GO:7770074,GO:0006493) and the [OLS term record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/api/ontologies/go/terms?iri=http%3A%2F%2Fpurl.obolibrary.org%2Fobo%2FGO_7770074), retrieved on 2026-10-24, both identify GO:7770074 as an active biological-process term, **protein O-linked glycosylation via N-acetylglucosamine**. OLS records creation on 2026-05-07 and links [ontology issue 32044](https://github.com/geneontology/go-ontology/issues/32044). The term is a current direct `is_a` child of GO:0006493. Its numeric range does not make it invalid.

The [saved official responses](EOGT-bioinformatics/ontology-term-check.json) make this check inspectable without network access. The current GO:0006493 descendants were also checked: other direct process children distinguish the initiating sugar (mannose, fucose, glucose, galactose, xylose, arabinose or GalNAc), rather than providing a generic serine-only or threonine-only alternative. GO:7770074 remains the relevant GlcNAc-specific comparator, but its definition excludes extension into an oligosaccharide. The accepted parent therefore covers the initiating EOGT step while allowing the later glycan extension reported in the mammalian source; it does not assign that extension reaction to EOGT.

## Core scope

The single core combines the accepted catalytic reaction, ER-lumen location and protein O-linked glycosylation process. Direct human activity is established for the canonical 527-residue protein; inherited ER localization is corroborated by fly and mouse originals. No claim is made that every shorter human product has the same activity or location.
