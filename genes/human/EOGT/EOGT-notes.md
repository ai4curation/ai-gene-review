# EOGT biological and annotation notes

EOGT adds O-linked N-acetylglucosamine to extracellular EGF-like domains as secretory-pathway proteins pass through the endoplasmic reticulum. It uses UDP-GlcNAc as donor and is distinct from intracellular OGT. Direct human activity was demonstrated for the 527-residue KC347596.1 clone on Notch EGF-repeat reporters in fly S2 cells (PMID:23671640, Figure 1 and Methods). Fly purified-enzyme and targeting experiments establish folded-EGF substrate specificity and ER retrieval (PMID:22158438, Figures 2–3). Mouse purified-enzyme and cellular assays provide additional ortholog evidence (PMID:25488668, Methods and Results associated with Figures 1–2). Original-paper links and exact reading limits are in [the source guide](EOGT-source-guide.md).

Biallelic EOGT variants segregate with Adams-Oliver syndrome (PMID:23522784). Functional follow-up of corresponding substitutions used mouse EOGT, including when HEK293T cells were the host. Mouse W207S is unstable and prone to proteasomal degradation; mouse R377Q retains ER localization and acceptor binding but has impaired UDP-GlcNAc binding (PMID:25488668, Results associated with Figures 5–8). These engineered disease-substitution constructs and the truncation mimic do not establish the behavior of natural human splice products. Human genetics and fly rescue do not by themselves identify the endogenous human substrate or tissue mechanism responsible for the syndrome.

The three natural products are preserved separately: 527, 208 and 443 residues. Reconstruction and independent FASTA comparison are documented in [the product analysis](EOGT-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md). Only the canonical product exactly matches the human clone tested in PMID:23671640. The shorter products lack the canonical DYD region, and the 208-residue product also lacks HDEL. Retained signal sequences or HDEL motifs alone do not establish localization or catalytic competence. Reduced activity of the engineered DYD-to-AYA mutant is not a direct assay of either natural shorter product.

Both PAINT assertions trace to PTN001841612, with the human target below that node in the current version-19 tree. The exact canonical sequence matches its ungapped MSA leaf, and no matching NOT record occurs on that path in the pinned IBD snapshot. The mouse and fly source identities and direct annotation citations were checked without treating donor count as evidential strength.

All eight imported annotations are retained as separate source assertions. Seven are accepted; the broad InterPro molecular-function annotation is refined to the existing protein O-acetylglucosaminyltransferase activity. No new process annotation is proposed. The parent protein O-linked glycosylation process is retained because the current GlcNAc-specific child excludes elongation, whereas mammalian secretory-pathway O-GlcNAc can be galactosylated (PMID:22310717, abstract). This assigns EOGT the initiating transfer rather than downstream galactosylation. The older mouse paper's full Methods were unavailable; independent human and mouse originals establish the catalytic function.
