Gene Description

Dynamin-1 is a large, predominantly neuronal GTPase that converts nucleotide hydrolysis into membrane remodeling during endocytosis. Its pleckstrin-homology domain binds phosphoinositides, its stalk supports self-assembly, and its proline-rich tail recruits SH3-domain partners. Assembly at membrane necks couples GTPase-domain interactions to constriction and vesicle scission, supporting receptor-mediated uptake and presynaptic membrane retrieval. Dynamin-1 also influences endocytic pit and vesicle dimensions. Alternative splicing changes an internal segment and the C-terminal tail; pathogenic variants can disrupt neuronal membrane trafficking and cause developmental and epileptic encephalopathy.
