| The protein localizes to centrosomal and ciliary microtubule-organizing regions. | Immunofluorescence detected endogenous TCTEX1D2 around centrioles and at the ciliary base; cycling cells also showed MTOC, spindle-pole, centrosomal, and cytokinetic-bridge localization. | TCTEX1D2 contributes to ciliogenesis and ciliary homeostasis. | siRNA depletion in human hTERT-RPE cells reduced the ciliated-cell fraction; another study reported longer cilia without an obvious block in ciliogenesis.
