# TNFSF15 manual literature synthesis

Perplexity deep research was unavailable in this workspace, so this file records a manual synthesis from cached primary papers, Reactome events, UniProt, and the existing PTHR11471 family review.

## Core biology

TNFSF15 encodes TL1A/VEGI, a TNF-family type II membrane or secreted ligand. The early VEGI paper describes "a novel member of the tumor necrosis factor (TNF) family" from a human umbilical vein endothelial-cell cDNA library and reports that the soluble form suppressed angiogenesis by directly inhibiting endothelial-cell proliferation [PMID:9872942].

The key receptor paper identifies the immune receptor pair: "TL1A is a TNF-like ligand for DR3 and TR6/DcR3 and functions as a T cell costimulator" and the abstract reports that TL1A binds DR3/TNFRSF25 and decoy receptor TR6/DcR3/TNFRSF6B, activates NF-kappaB/apoptosis in DR3-expressing cell lines, and costimulates T cells [PMID:11911831].

TNFSF15 is therefore a cytokine and tumor necrosis factor receptor superfamily ligand whose specific core receptor is TNFRSF25/DR3, with TNFRSF6B acting as a decoy receptor. Do not treat it as a FAS/TRAIL/RANK/TNF ligand; those receptor-specific downstream processes are confined to other PTHR11471 subfamilies.
