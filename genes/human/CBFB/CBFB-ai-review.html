Gene Description

Core-binding factor subunit beta is a non-DNA-contacting transcriptional cofactor that heterodimerizes with RUNX proteins. Binding stabilizes the RUNX Runt-domain conformation and enhances sequence-specific DNA binding, allowing promoter-dependent activation or repression. Cellular experiments also show that CBFB protects RUNX3 from ADP-ribosylation-dependent ubiquitination and degradation. These nuclear complexes regulate developmental programs including hematopoiesis and skeletal development. HIV-1 Vif can recruit CBFB to stabilize an APOBEC-targeting ubiquitin-ligase assembly. Heterozygous pathogenic variants cause a skeletal disorder resembling cleidocranial dysplasia; the somatic CBFB–MYH11 leukemia fusion has distinct properties from the normal protein.

CBFB evidence and annotation review

2026-10-04 — Whole-gene review

CBFB is the non-DNA-contacting beta subunit of core-binding factor.
