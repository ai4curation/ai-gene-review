Gene Description

XRCC3 (X-ray repair cross-complementing protein 3) is one of the five human RAD51 paralogs, a RecA/RAD51-family protein with an N-terminal domain and a C-terminal Walker A/B ATPase domain. All of its characterized activities are exerted as the obligate partner of RAD51C in the CX3 (RAD51C-XRCC3) heterodimer, and CX3 also assembles with RAD51D-XRCC2 into the X3CDX2 (DX2-CX3) tetramer, with RAD51 into an autoinhibited RAD51-X3C octamer, and with BCDX2 into a BCDX2-CX3-RAD51 supercomplex. Within these assemblies XRCC3 contacts RAD51 directly; X3CDX2 caps the 5' end of RAD51-ssDNA filaments (XRCC2 forming the cap) and stabilizes them, and XRCC3-containing complexes stimulate RAD51-mediated strand exchange and D-loop formation. CX3 binds Holliday junctions and fork junctions, acts downstream of RAD51 recruitment in BRCA1-BRCA2-dependent homologous recombination, and influences late steps of recombination, including gene-conversion tract length and GEN1-associated Holliday junction resolution in mitotic cells. During replication stress XRCC3 associates with nascent DNA at stalled forks, contributes to protection of nascent strands, and, uniquely among the paralog complexes, promotes ATP-hydrolysis-dependent restart of stalled and collapsed forks; ATM/ATR phosphorylation of XRCC3 Ser225 is required for RAD51 chromatin loading, HR repair and recovery of collapsed (but not stalled) forks. CX3 also recruits the FANCM translocase to R-loops to suppress transcription-replication conflicts. XRCC3 participates in recombination-mediated telomere trimming (t-loop deletion / t-circle formation) and is found in mitochondria, where RAD51C/XRCC3 associates with the nucleoid and supports mtDNA replication and copy number. XRCC3-deficient cells are hypersensitive to DNA cross-linking agents and ionizing radiation and are chromosomally unstable; germline variants are associated with breast cancer susceptibility.

Core Functions

As the RAD51-contacting subunit of CX3 and of the X3CDX2 (DX2-CX3) assembly, XRCC3 regulates the RAD51 nucleoprotein filament: X3CDX2 stably caps the 5' end of RAD51-ssDNA filaments and anchors them, and XRCC3-containing complexes stimulate RAD51-mediated strand exchange and D-loop formation. This acts downstream of RAD51 recruitment in BRCA1-BRCA2-dependent homologous recombination repair of DNA double-strand breaks. Strand-exchange stimulation is assigned contributes_to because it is a property of the paralog complexes.
