Gene Description

CHST3 encodes chondroitin 6-O-sulfotransferase 1, a type II Golgi membrane sulfotransferase that transfers sulfate from PAPS to C6 of N-acetylgalactosamine residues in chondroitin chains. This modification contributes to the sulfation pattern of extracellular and cell-surface chondroitin sulfate proteoglycans. The recombinant human enzyme also accepts keratan sulfate in vitro. Biallelic loss of CHST3 activity causes a skeletal dysplasia with joint dislocations and progressive spinal involvement.
