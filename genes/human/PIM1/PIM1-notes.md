# PIM1 curation notes

Deep research providers were unavailable in this worktree: Falcon and Perplexity failed before creating provider files, so this review uses the fetched GOA, UniProt, PAINT, Reactome and PMID caches plus manual inspection.

PIM1 is the best-supported human PIM kinase in this family. Direct rows support serine/threonine phosphorylation, DEPDC5-dependent TORC1 activation, and phosphorylation of GBP1 in the IFN-gamma antibacterial pathway. The BMX/Etk and RPS19 protein-binding rows are productive interactions and were converted to activator/kinase functions; BioPlex bare protein-binding rows with no functional consequence were removed. HPA and Reactome localization rows were retained as locations rather than made core signaling functions.
