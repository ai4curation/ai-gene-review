# ASAH2B review notes

## Sources
- UniProt: "Putative inactive neutral ceramidase B"; PE2 (transcript level); a partial paralog of ASAH2 from segmental duplication.
- Affinage: no mechanistic findings.
- PMID:17334805 (abstract): ASAH2L expression falls with age and in LOAD.
- Bioinformatics (`ASAH2B-bioinformatics/asah2_coverage.py`): ASAH2B aligns only to ASAH2 residues 636-780 (137/145 identical). It lacks the S354 nucleophile and all four Zn-binding residues, keeping one C-terminal Ca-binding site.

## Decisions
- REMOVE: N-acylsphingosine amidohydrolase activity and the three ceramide/sphingosine process rows (all IEA). The fragment cannot form the catalytic site.
- UNDECIDED: cytoplasm and plasma membrane (IEA ARBA family-location transfers; there is no localization data, and the ASAH2 signal anchor is absent).
- Knowledge gap: wholly dark; even protein existence is uncertain.
