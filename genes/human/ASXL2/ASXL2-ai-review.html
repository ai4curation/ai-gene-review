Gene Description

ASXL2 is a nuclear chromatin regulator with HARE-HTH, DEUBAD and atypical PHD regions. It forms a regulatory subunit of BAP1-containing PR-DUB complexes and stimulates BAP1-mediated removal of ubiquitin from histone H2A. ASXL2 also supports transcriptional coactivation through nuclear-receptor and chromatin-associated interactions, including experimentally established PPAR signaling. These activities influence developmental gene expression and cell differentiation.

Core Functions

ASXL2 binds BAP1 through its DEUBAD regulatory region and stimulates BAP1-mediated deubiquitination of histone H2A at lysine 119. As a regulatory subunit of nuclear PR-DUB, it promotes substrate recognition and helps maintain the complex. BAP1 supplies the hydrolytic activity; ASXL2 supplies the activating interaction. Its PHD-dependent MBD5/6 interactions support complex assembly and chromatin association within this integrated regulatory role.
