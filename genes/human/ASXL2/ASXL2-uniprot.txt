CC   -!- FUNCTION: Putative Polycomb group (PcG) protein. PcG proteins act by
CC       forming multiprotein complexes, which are required to maintain the
CC       transcriptionally repressive state of homeotic genes throughout
CC       development. PcG proteins are not required to initiate repression, but
CC       to maintain it during later stages of development. They probably act
CC       via methylation of histones, rendering chromatin heritably changed in
CC       its expressibility (By similarity). Involved in transcriptional
CC       regulation mediated by ligand-bound nuclear hormone receptors, such as
CC       peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma (PPARG). Acts as
CC       coactivator for PPARG and enhances its adipocyte differentiation-
CC       inducing activity; the function seems to involve differential
CC       recruitment of acetylated and methylated histone H3. Non-catalytic
CC       component of the PR-DUB complex, a complex that specifically mediates
CC       deubiquitination of histone H2A monoubiquitinated at 'Lys-119'
CC       (H2AK119ub1) (PubMed:30664650, PubMed:36180891). The PR-DUB complex is
CC       an epigenetic regulator of gene expression and acts as a
CC       transcriptional coactivator, affecting genes involved in development,
CC       cell communication, signaling, cell proliferation and cell viability
CC       (PubMed:30664650, PubMed:36180891). ASXL1, ASXL2 and ASXL3 function
CC       redundantly in the PR-DUB complex (By similarity) (PubMed:30664650).
CC       The ASXL proteins are essential for chromatin recruitment and
CC       transcriptional activation of associated genes (By similarity). ASXL1
CC       and ASXL2 are important for BAP1 protein stability (PubMed:30664650).
CC       {ECO:0000250, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8BZ32,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21047783, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30664650,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36180891}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Core component of the polycomb repressive deubiquitinase (PR-
CC       DUB) complex, at least composed of BAP1, one of ASXL1, ASXL2 or
CC       (probably) ASXL3, and one of MBD5 or MBD6 (PubMed:24634419,
CC       PubMed:30664650, PubMed:36180891). Distinct combinations of ASXL and
CC       MBD proteins may preferentially bind specific histone modification
CC       marks (PubMed:36180891). The PR-DUB core associates with a number of
CC       accessory proteins, including FOXK1, FOXK2, KDM1B, HCFC1 and OGT; KDM1B
CC       specifically associates with ASXL2 PR-DUB complexes (Probable)
CC       (PubMed:30664650). Interacts (via PHD domain) with MBD5 and MBD6 (via
CC       MBD domain); the interaction is probably direct and mediates
CC       association of MBD proteins with the PR-DUB core (PubMed:36180891).
CC       Interacts with PPARA and PPARG (PubMed:21047783).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21047783, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24634419,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30664650, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36180891,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:24634419}.
