CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multifunctional blue, copper-binding (6-7 atoms per molecule)
CC       glycoprotein. It has ferroxidase activity oxidizing Fe(2+) to Fe(3+)
CC       without releasing radical oxygen species. It is involved in iron
CC       transport across the cell membrane (PubMed:16150804). Copper ions
CC       provide a large number of enzymatic activities. Oxidizes highly toxic
CC       ferrous ions to the ferric state for further incorporation onto apo-
CC       transferrins, catalyzes Cu(+) oxidation and promotes the oxidation of
CC       biogenic amines such as norepinephrin and serotonin (PubMed:14623105,
CC       PubMed:4643313, PubMed:5912351). Provides Cu(2+) ions for the
CC       ascorbate-mediated deaminase degradation of the heparan sulfate chains
CC       of GPC1 (By similarity). Has glutathione peroxidase-like activity, can
CC       remove both hydrogen peroxide and lipid hydroperoxide in the presence
CC       of thiols (PubMed:10481051). Acts as an inhibitor of the peroxidase
CC       activity of MPO (By similarity). Also shows NO-oxidase and NO2 synthase
CC       activities that determine endocrine NO homeostasis (PubMed:16906150).
