CC       activation through the antigen receptor (PubMed:35767951). Acts as a
CC       positive regulator of T-cell coactivation upon binding to DPP4. The
CC       first PTPase domain has enzymatic activity, while the second one seems
CC       to affect the substrate specificity of the first one. Upon T-cell

CC       activation threshold to limit autoreactivity. {ECO:0000250,
