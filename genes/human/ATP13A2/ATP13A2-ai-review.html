Gene Description

ATP13A2 is a P5B-type ATPase that uses ATP hydrolysis to export polyamines, especially spermine, from late endolysosomal compartments into the cytosol. Its membrane-associated N-terminal region binds phosphatidic acid and phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate, coupling the transporter to its endolysosomal lipid environment. ATP13A2-dependent lysosomal homeostasis affects autophagic trafficking, exosomal secretion and neuronal survival. Loss-of-function variants cause Kufor-Rakeb syndrome and other neurodegenerative phenotypes. Potassium-dependent membrane ATPase activity and cellular potassium-surrogate transport have also been reported.

ATP13A2 manual annotation review

ATP13A2 is an ATP-dependent P5B polyamine transporter of the late endolysosomal membrane system. The principal function is export of polyamines, especially spermine, toward the cytosol. The review retains the original 120 annotation source objects and all three alternative products (Q9NQ11-1, Q9NQ11-2 and Q9NQ11-3). It proposes one transport core and no new annotations.

This is not described as homogeneous purified-protein transport, nor as an isoform-exclusive function.
