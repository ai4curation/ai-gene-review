# KLK2 notes

Deep research (falcon / perplexity-lite) failed; notes from cached publications and UniProt P20151.

- Trypsin-like, arginine-selective S1 serine protease (hK2); signal peptide 1-18, activation propeptide 19-24.
- Purified active from seminal plasma [PMID:9042371 "the recovered free hK2 is a trypsin-like enzyme"].
- Activates pro-PSA, pro-hK2 and pro-uPA [PMID:10209959 "Unlike PSA, hK2 activates pro-PSA , pro-hK2 and the zymogen form of urokinase-type plasminogen activator (uPA)"]; recombinant pro-PSA activated by hK2 [PMID:9261179].
- Complexes with serpins PCI, PAI-1, ACT, PI-6 [PMID:10209959; PMID:10463585].
- Blood pressure IBA from KLK1-like clade judged over-annotation (prostate-restricted).

Localization: secreted, extracellular (seminal plasma); not cell-surface anchored.
