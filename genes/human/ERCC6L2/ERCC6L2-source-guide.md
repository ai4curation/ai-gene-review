## Core activity and product scope

One core combines a bounded DNA-unwinding activity with the established contribution to classical nonhomologous end joining. The biochemical and cellular evidence are complementary; purified end joining was not reconstituted, and the exact coupling of the DNA-melting reaction to the joining machinery remains unresolved.

[PMID:41741470](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69843-w), [original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13013651/): Results Figure 5 and Methods for protein purification, DNA melting and electrophoretic mobility-shift assays establish short-overhang duplex melting by purified human ERCC6L2. Supplementary Table 3's WT sequence exactly matches the 1,550-residue Q5T890-1 product; the purified mutant changes K154 to alanine. This is distinct from the cellular K154R mutant. Figure 5 pixels were independently inspected during core synthesis. ATP enhances melting relative to AMP-PNP and no ATP, and the K154A mutant loses that enhancement while retaining DNA binding. These controls support an ATP-hydrolysis-coupled component by inference; the inspected experiment does not separately quantify phosphate or ADP release. Residual melting occurs without hydrolyzable ATP. RPA or mtSSB is required under the tested conditions. The assay uses four-nucleotide 5′ or 3′ overhang substrates and does not establish general unwinding of all duplex structures, a directional motor rate, or exonucleolytic resection. The protein sequence proof was read; no additional supplementary experiments are claimed inspected.

[PMID:32355287](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41422-020-0328-3), [original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7608219/): Results Figures 3–5 and cell-line, cloning, laser and co-immunoprecipitation Methods establish damage-site recruitment, association with end-joining factors and a contribution to repair-complex loading. Human U2OS and HEK293T experiments are distinct from mouse B-cell class-switch and XLF-redundant V(D)J joining experiments and mouse MEF ligase recruitment. The human construct ending at residue 1,561 and mouse construct ending at residue 1,537 are not the purified 1,550-residue biochemical reagent. DNA-independent co-immunoprecipitation does not establish every pair as a purified binary interaction. The direct role is repair-factor and DNA-end handling, not DNA-ligase chemistry. Text and captions were independently read; no additional figure pixels or complete supplementary experiment set were inspected for this synthesis.

[PMID:27185855](https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.20151183), [original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4886357/): Results Figures 4–5 and 9–10, with cDNA cloning, GFP/forced-NLS, microirradiation and repair-assay Methods, distinguish the 1,561-residue nuclear product from the mostly cytoplasmic 712-residue product. Only the long product rescues patient-cell phleomycin sensitivity in the tested assay; forced nuclear localization does not restore the short product's damage recruitment. The condition-specific V(D)J precision and homologous-recombination results do not exclude the later partly redundant end-joining contribution. These observations support the description's product boundaries, not automatic extension of the purified 1,550-residue activity to every product. Text and captions were read; no figure pixels were inspected here.

[PMID:37014751](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2023.112329), [original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12003248/): Figure 1 centromere localization/DNA-binding-fragment experiments, Figure 4 PCNA-binding experiments and the Discussion limitations support the description's centromere and PCNA statements. Independent core reading included centromere Results, DNA-binding-fragment and end-processing observations, and the authors' limitation on interpreting PCNA-mutant function beyond localization. The accepted annotation packet supplies the detailed PCNA peptide/pull-down/ITC/crystal scope. These data do not establish a separate catalytic centromere activity or a general chromatin-remodeling mechanism. No new figure or supplement inspection is claimed here.

Current [GO:0003678 DNA helicase activity](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0003678) requires ATP-hydrolysis-driven DNA unwinding. It is applied here to the supported ATP-dependent component of the measured reaction, with the assay limitation stated in the core itself. It does not describe the entire residual ATP-independent melting signal. Current [GO:0097680 classical nonhomologous end joining](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0097680) captures the established end-joining contribution; the description retains the unresolved mechanistic link and the broader cellular effects observed in 2026. Its broader DNA-repair ancestors are omitted to avoid redundancy.

[GO:0000729 DNA double-strand break processing](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0000729) specifically describes 5′→3′ exonucleolytic resection and is not assigned to duplex melting. No separate ATP-hydrolysis, translocase, chromatin-remodeler, centromere-catalysis or PCNA-activation core is proposed. Nuclear/centromeric localization remains represented by the accepted annotations and biological description; no location field silently equates the purified 1,550-residue reagent with the separately localized 1,561-residue construct.

## Resection regulation and feedback revision (2026-10-24)

[PMID:37014751](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2023.112329), Results Figure 5A–G and phleomycin/native-BrdU Methods, supports increased pRPA and exposed ssDNA after ERCC6L2 loss in human U2OS cells, without a corresponding increase in gamma-H2AX. CtIP, BLM or BRCA1 depletion reduces that excess processing. The PCNA aPIP region at approximately 790–811 is in the middle of the tested 1,561-residue construct, separate from the C-terminal recruitment/condensate context.

[PMID:41741470](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69843-w), Results Figures 3–4 with SMART, AsiSI, ChIP and knockdown Methods, adds measured resection tracks, break-associated ssDNA and MRE11 spreading. NBN loss and MRE11/CtIP perturbations define the reported pathway relationships. Unchanged recruitment measurements are distinguished from increased spreading. These Results and Methods were read as text/captions; no additional Figure 3/4 pixels or supplementary experiments were inspected during this feedback assessment.

[PMID:40913148](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-025-01760-4), [original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12527916/), provides a contrasting prolonged-ATM-inhibition context. Actual official Nature Results and Figures 3–7 captions, together with cell-line, construct, SMART, AsiSI, fractionation, co-immunoprecipitation, decay/ubiquitination and FRAP Methods, were read. In human U2OS cells under 24-hour ATM inhibition, ERCC6L2 loss lowers resection, while condensate-associated CtIP stabilization, RNF138 depletion and complementation support a regulatory contribution. The study declares a 1,561-residue full-length protein, ER-N 1–712 and ER-C 713–1,561, including an NLS-FUS chimera; exact clone sequence equivalence was not independently established. These are cellular experiments, not purified resection or helicase reconstitution. Figure pixels, movies and the supplementary experiment set were not inspected.

Current [GO:1903775 regulation of DNA double-strand break processing](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:1903775) accommodates this context-dependent direction. Its typed ancestry is regulation of DNA metabolism, and it regulates GO:0000729; the replacement of broad damage response is a lateral functional correction. ERCC6L2 is not assigned exonucleolytic resection chemistry. The regulation annotation remains separate from the catalytic core because coupling of the C-terminal CtIP mechanism to purified helicase activity has not been demonstrated.

One new molecular-function annotation records the measured ATP-dependent component of short-overhang unwinding specifically for Q5T890-1. It is supported by PMID:41741470 and the exact protein-sequence proof already described above, and is absent from the 24 imported assertions. It does not add a process annotation or extend the activity to other products. Current [GO:0036121 double-stranded DNA helicase activity](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0036121) additionally specifies dsDNA-dependent ATP hydrolysis; that specificity is not independently resolved by the inspected assay. The bounded GO:0003678 assertion follows the evidence, rather than a desire to eliminate a validation warning.
