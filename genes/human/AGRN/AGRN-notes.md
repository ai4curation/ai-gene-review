## Synaptic signaling and matrix evidence

- **PMID:18848351**, cached primary abstract: LRP4 recognizes agrin, complexes with MuSK and mediates activation. This directly supports agrin's activator function; it does not make agrin a kinase. Exact affinity and assembly stoichiometry are not derived from this abstract. **PMID:23178848** is a structural review, not another primary stoichiometry experiment. An AGRN-LRP4 pair of heterodimers should not be relabeled a measured ternary 2:2 agrin/LRP4/MuSK complex.
- **PMID:15340048**, cached abstract plus Discussion: pulse-and-wash experiments establish subsequent MuSK/AChR phosphorylation and clustering. The cache does not provide all Methods/Results. The supported quote reports the complete result: “a single 5-min agrin pulse, followed by extensive washing, triggered long-lasting MuSK and AChR phosphorylation and efficient AChR clustering.”
- **PMID:9151673**, full cached primary: recombinant chick agrin NtA fragments bind laminins and change AChR cluster geometry in cultured chick myotubes. The N-terminal fragment competes with the full-length protein's cluster-size effect. Human and mouse sequence conservation supports transfer; this is not a direct purified-human binding assay or a gene-knockout experiment.
- **PMID:9417121**, cached primary abstract: solid-phase radioligand binding, tissue copurification and coimmunoprecipitation support dystroglycan binding. The generic protein-binding annotation is refined to the specific interaction, even though an ISS row already carries that term. Organism details not stated in the accessible abstract are not invented.
- **PMID:9652404**, cached human primary abstract: adult lung/kidney basement-membrane accumulation and human sequence conservation support location and NtA inference. Its full article was not recovered. The cytoskeletal structural activity remains UNDECIDED: a matrix tether and a signaling organizer are not automatically a cytoskeletal structural constituent, but an inaccessible full source does not justify categorical rejection.
- **PMID:19631309**, cached abstract and primary PubMed figure descriptions: human p.Gly1709Arg preserves the tested MuSK activation, cluster counts and dystroglycan binding, while injected mutant destabilizes native rat NMJs. Maintenance and initial cluster induction must be distinguished. The full linked correction at [PMC2796872](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2796872/), DOI `10.1016/j.ajhg.2009.09.010`, corrects an incomplete printed pedigree panel and states the online version was unaffected. It does not invalidate the functional findings.
- **PMID:1314621** supports inactive alternative forms in the reported aggregation assay; the abstract does not warrant the inherited universal 1000-fold numerical comparison or an exclusive z8 requirement across all functions.
- **PMID:17586728** describes agrin as the neurotrypsin substrate, with an active signaling fragment after cleavage. Neurotrypsin performs proteolysis. The fragment/Na-K-ATPase excitability mechanism, also described in human UniProt, supports retaining broad chemical synaptic transmission and synaptic signaling instead of replacing these solely with developmental processes.

### Full human GBM paper and terminology

The entire nine-page publisher article **PMID:9405491** was read at [the publisher PDF](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/002215549804600104). Human immunostaining and immunoelectron microscopy directly establish GBM localization. The sixfold agrin/perlecan comparison concerns molarity in the tested crude glomerular extracts, not all human tissue or a whole-body ratio. Discussion presents renal charge filtration, mechanical linkage and protease inhibition as possible functions rather than measured outcomes.

The introduction discusses acetylcholine receptor clustering and cites earlier voltage-dependent sodium-channel clustering work. It does not establish a muscarinic GPCR signaling mechanism. Current [GO:0007213](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0007213) specifically describes a G-protein-coupled acetylcholine receptor pathway. The source's positive agrin mechanism supports MODIFY to receptor clustering. The sodium-channel-clustering TAS assertion is retained as non-core and explicitly remains a citation of earlier work, not a direct assay in the GBM paper.

Current GO:0005200 concerns structural integrity of a cytoskeletal structure, whereas extracellular receptor-induced cytoskeleton organization is separately supported. Its exact source remains unresolved rather than declared biologically impossible.

## Glycan donor audit

The historical [MGI comparative Agrn graph](https://www.informatics.jax.org/homology/GOGraph/Agrn), generated 2023-03-10, traces mouse Agrn glycan-binding assertions to **PMID:12773545**, MGI:5486565 / J:196117. The [PubMed primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12773545/) and the author-posted paper were checked. Methods/Results describe chick agrin expressed in HEK293/EBNA cells, glycosidase treatments and serine-cluster substitutions; vitreous-body material is also examined. These experiments locate **covalent** HS/CS attachment sites. They do not settle noncovalent ligand recognition by the core protein. This is a source-scope problem to resolve, not proof that all agrin–glycan binding is false or a claim that the historical mouse annotation must be a wrong-gene error.

[PMID:17649979](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17649979/), with indexed Results/Discussion from [PMC2111043](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2111043/), provides distinct direct binding evidence. Chick P31696 G3 splice variants were examined by calorimetry and NMR. The measured calcium affinities are approximately 260 and 503 micromolar; calcium-dependent sialic-acid affinities are approximately 928 and 1472 micromolar under the tested conditions. HS/heparin binding is calcium-independent. These isolated-domain measurements establish biochemical capacities but not human physiological affinities or a dominant endogenous sialic-acid ligand role. Sialic binding is retained as non-core; calcium binding is retained. The exact intact-HSPG partner assertion remains UNDECIDED because free HS binding and covalent HS attachment do not by themselves resolve which whole proteoglycan was recognized. CS binding likewise remains UNDECIDED rather than inferred directly from glycosylation.

InterPro IPR001881 in the separate calcium IEA is a calcium-binding EGF-like domain mapping, distinct from these G3 measurements. The generic metal-ion keyword mapping is refined to calcium. Calcium-dependent receptor clustering alone would not have established direct metal binding.
