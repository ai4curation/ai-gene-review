## Glycan donor audit

The historical [MGI comparative Agrn graph](https://www.informatics.jax.org/homology/GOGraph/Agrn), generated 2023-03-10, traces mouse Agrn glycan-binding assertions to **PMID:12773545**, MGI:5486565 / J:196117. The [PubMed primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12773545/) and the author-posted paper were checked. Methods/Results describe chick agrin expressed in HEK293/EBNA cells, glycosidase treatments and serine-cluster substitutions; vitreous-body material is also examined. These experiments locate **covalent** HS/CS attachment sites. They do not settle noncovalent ligand recognition by the core protein. This is a source-scope problem to resolve, not proof that all agrin–glycan binding is false or a claim that the historical mouse annotation must be a wrong-gene error.

[PMID:17649979], with indexed Results/Discussion from [PMC2111043](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2111043/), provides distinct direct binding evidence. Chick P31696 G3 splice variants were examined by calorimetry and NMR. The measured calcium affinities are approximately 260 and 503 micromolar; calcium-dependent sialic-acid affinities are approximately 928 and 1472 micromolar under the tested conditions. HS/heparin binding is calcium-independent. These isolated-domain measurements establish biochemical capacities but not human physiological affinities or a dominant endogenous sialic-acid ligand role. Sialic binding is retained as non-core; calcium binding is retained. The exact intact-HSPG partner assertion remains UNDECIDED because free HS binding and covalent HS attachment do not by themselves resolve which whole proteoglycan was recognized. CS binding likewise remains UNDECIDED rather than inferred directly from glycosylation.

InterPro IPR001881 in the separate calcium IEA is a calcium-binding EGF-like domain mapping, distinct from these G3 measurements. The generic metal-ion keyword mapping is refined to calcium. Calcium-dependent receptor clustering alone would not have established direct metal binding.
