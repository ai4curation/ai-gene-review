# FXYD2 source and context guide

This review covers 25 original human P54710 contexts. The associated [evidence archive](FXYD2-source-evidence.yaml) retains every original object, all 16 atoms in six conjunctive extension groups, the literal offline forecast joins, actual native mappings and source-specific judgments. Genuine repository acquisition produced all 25 native rows and 28 normal source files; eight existing files were reused unchanged and 20 were acquired.

## What the principal experiments establish

Human FXYD2b expression with human alpha1/beta1 in Xenopus oocytes supports condition-dependent Na,K-pump regulation, and human alpha1/beta1/FXYD2 cryo-EM structures establish complex membership. These are distinct from sodium-channel regulation and from attributing alpha-subunit ATP hydrolysis, ion binding or gating directly to gamma. The renal magnesium-wasting association does not identify FXYD2 as a magnesium transporter.

The two IBA rows have real root-node placements in PTHR14132: human leaf PTN002493856 descends from PTN000364858, and the current 66-residue target exactly matches the actual family alignment. All literal source identifiers are retained. The channel-specific inference is refined on functional-type grounds after reading donor assays. Positive-export inheritance remains unresolved because the relevant conditional target mechanism is not established; mixed effects do not prove functional loss, and donor count is not an evidential ranking.

## Natural products and historical constructs

UniProt documents P54710-1/A (66 residues) and P54710-2/B (64 residues). The latter replaces canonical MTGLSMDG at residues 1–8 with MDRWYL; both products match direct official FASTA. Sequence identity does not establish equivalent expression or function. The functional paper's FXYD2b construct and structural accession AAG37906.1 are not silently equated with a current product without a literal construct-sequence join. Historical 58-residue cloning and 66/68-residue genomic descriptions are preserved as historical reports. G41R is a disease variant, not a natural third product. The urinary-exosome paper's old isoform-1/NP_001671 designation is not current-product evidence without the missing peptide sequence.

## Review boundaries

The four unresolved rows concern ATPase activation, molecular-adaptor activity, proton transport and conditional positive sodium export. Their donor/source evidence remains intact. Four broad annotations are TOO_GENERAL only because retained sodium export/potassium import or plasma-membrane terms entail them through checked is_a-only paths. No NEW annotation or extra catalytic assertion is introduced. One short, verified Results excerpt from PMID:33231612 supports the ATPase-regulator core. It concerns apparent intracellular Na affinity with intracellular K, rather than isolated gamma catalytic activity; its exact native-cache byte offsets and original Results locator are retained in the archive.

The archive carries the reviewed evidence and the [notes](FXYD2-notes.md) retain the dated private proposal and subsequent acquisition record. Validation and final status are reported only after their actual execution.
