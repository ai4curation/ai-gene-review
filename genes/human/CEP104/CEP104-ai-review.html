Gene Description

CEP104 is a TOG-domain-containing microtubule regulator at distal centrioles and ciliary tips. Its TOG domain binds tubulin, while full-length human CEP104 can stabilize microtubule plus ends by suppressing both growth and shortening. Recruitment by EB proteins and ciliary partners such as CCDC66 and CSPP1 shapes this activity; TOGARAM1 can overcome growth inhibition within the tip-protein network. CEP104 supports normal cilium length and architecture, and biallelic variants cause Joubert syndrome.

Core Functions

Stabilizes microtubule plus ends by limiting tubulin loss and growth. Ciliary partners recruit CEP104 and tune its TOG-dependent activity within the tip-protein network. Mapped TOG-function rescue links this microtubule-regulatory machinery to ciliary elongation, without establishing an initiating role.
