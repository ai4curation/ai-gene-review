CC   -!- FUNCTION: Inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate-gated calcium channel that, upon
CC       inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate binding, mediates calcium release from the
CC       endoplasmic reticulum (ER) (PubMed:10620513, PubMed:27108797).
CC       Undergoes conformational changes upon ligand binding, suggesting
CC       structural flexibility that allows the channel to switch from a closed
CC       state, capable of interacting with its ligands such as 1,4,5-
CC       trisphosphate and calcium, to an open state, capable of transferring
CC       calcium ions across the ER membrane (By similarity). Cytoplasmic
CC       calcium released from the ER triggers apoptosis by the activation of
CC       CAMK2 complex (By similarity). Involved in the regulation of epithelial
CC       secretion of electrolytes and fluid through the interaction with AHCYL1
CC       (By similarity). Part of a complex composed of HSPA9, ITPR1 and VDAC1
CC       that regulates mitochondrial calcium-dependent apoptosis by
CC       facilitating calcium transport from the ER lumen to the mitochondria
CC       intermembrane space thus providing calcium for the downstream calcium
CC       channel MCU that directly releases it into mitochondria matrix (By
CC       similarity). Regulates fertilization and egg activation by tuning the
CC       frequency and amplitude of calcium oscillations (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P11881, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P29994,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10620513, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27108797}.
