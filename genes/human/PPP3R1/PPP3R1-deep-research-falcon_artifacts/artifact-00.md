| Ca²⁺ sensing and EF-hands | CnB is the **regulatory, not catalytic**, calcineurin subunit. It contains four EF-hands and binds CnA even without Ca²⁺; Ca²⁺-saturated CnB regulates CnA more strongly. Activity, pull-down and circular-dichroism assays supported this mechanism. A later synthesis assigns paired high-affinity (submicromolar) and low-affinity (10–50 µM) sites and reports that low-affinity-site occupancy lowers the holoenzyme’s substrate *K*ₘ; CnA supplies phosphatase catalysis. (pqac-00000022) | Authoritative review integrating biochemical and cellular studies across mammalian systems; not a direct PPP3R1 perturbation experiment. | [10.3390/cells12182269](https://doi.org/10.3390/cells12182269), 2023 |
