# Human PPP3R1: functional annotation and evidence report

## Identity and primary molecular function

**PPP3R1**, identified in the question by UniProt accession **P63098**, encodes **calcineurin B1 (CnB1)**, the calcium-binding *regulatory* subunit of calcineurin, also called protein phosphatase 2B or PPP3. This assignment is consistent with independent reviews, which distinguish PPP3R1 from **PPP3R2** (calcineurin B2) and from **PPP3CA, PPP3CB and PPP3CC** (catalytic calcineurin A isoforms). Its four calcium-binding EF-hand motifs agree with the supplied domain annotations. The cited literature verifies the gene–protein assignment and family; P63098 itself is the identifier supplied in the question, rather than an accession independently checked against a database record here. (fonodi2024roleofprotein pages 15-16, roy2020identifyingnewsubstrates pages 1-3, nolze2023calcineurinisa pages 1-3)

**The primary function of PPP3R1 is to sense intracellular Ca²⁺ and regulate the activity and positioning of its catalytic partner, calcineurin A—not to catalyze a reaction itself.** Calcineurin A supplies the metal-dependent phosphatase active site and removes phosphate from selected *phosphoserine/phosphothreonine residues on proteins*. NFAT transcription factors are its best-characterized substrates; TFEB is another established substrate. Consequently, substrate specificity belongs to the assembled, regulated phosphatase complex and its substrate-docking interactions, not to an intrinsic enzymatic activity of isolated CnB1. (roy2020identifyingnewsubstrates pages 1-3, fonodi2024roleofprotein pages 15-16, nolze2023calcineurinisa pages 1-3, xia2024smurf1controlsthe pages 11-13)

CnB contains two pairs of EF-hands with different calcium affinities: approximately **10–50 µM** for the low-affinity pair and **submicromolar** affinity for the high-affinity pair, as summarized by Roy and Cyert. Their mechanistic synthesis proposes that calcium binding to CnB promotes subsequent Ca²⁺–calmodulin binding to CnA, displacement of CnA autoinhibitory elements and maximal phosphatase activity. It also reports that N-terminal myristoylation of CnB promotes enzyme thermal stability; this modification should not, by itself, be interpreted as proof that CnB1 is constitutively membrane-bound. In biochemical experiments, calcium-free CnB still bound CnA and supported weaker phosphatase regulation than calcium-saturated CnB, demonstrating that complex assembly and calcium-dependent activation are related but distinct properties. (roy2020identifyingnewsubstrates pages 3-4, li2009calcineurinregulatorysubunit pages 1-2)

The experimental contexts most relevant to functional annotation are summarized below; **mouse gene-deletion results are not presented as direct human knockout evidence**. (li2009calcineurinregulatorysubunit pages 1-2, mencarelli2018calcineurinbin pages 1-2, reed2020deletionofcalcineurin pages 1-7, xia2024smurf1controlsthe pages 8-11)

## Where the protein acts and which pathways it controls

**Localization is stimulus- and partner-dependent.** Calcineurin is predominantly **intracellular and cytoplasmic** under basal conditions, although a smaller nuclear pool has been reported. Its defining activity is therefore not extracellular secretion: it couples local intracellular calcium signals to protein dephosphorylation. In mouse cardiomyocytes, endogenous CnB associates with CIB1, which helps position the complex at the **sarcolemma** near an L-type Ca²⁺ channel during hypertrophic stimulation. These data establish a context-specific membrane-associated pool, not an exclusively membrane-localized human PPP3R1 protein. (masaki2022decodingthephosphatase pages 4-5, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 8-10, heineke2010cib1isa pages 4-6, heineke2010cib1isa pages 6-7)

The canonical pathway is **Ca²⁺ → CnB1/Ca²⁺–calmodulin-regulated calcineurin A → NFAT dephosphorylation → NFAT nuclear import and transcriptional response**. NFAT itself moves from cytoplasm to nucleus after dephosphorylation; this should not be confused with a requirement that all CnB1 translocate to the nucleus. This pathway connects calcium signals to immune-cell activation and tissue-specific transcriptional programs. Calcineurin can recognize additional proteins through docking interactions, so NFAT is the best-defined substrate family rather than its only possible target. (nolze2023calcineurinisa pages 1-3, thiel2021ca2+microdomainscalcineurin pages 8-10, fonodi2024roleofprotein pages 15-16)

A second well-supported pathway involves **TFEB**, whose calcineurin-dependent activation promotes transcription associated with lysosomal biogenesis and autophagy. In mice with Schwann-cell-specific *Ppp3r1* deletion, nerve injury produced less early nuclear TFEB, reduced autophagic flux and slower clearance of myelin debris, despite little effect on developmental myelination. The TFEB nuclear-localization difference was observed at **three days** after nerve crush and was no longer detectable at **seven days**, a useful indication that the requirement is time- and context-dependent. (reed2020deletionofcalcineurin pages 21-25, reed2020deletionofcalcineurin pages 1-7)

## Recent mechanistic research, 2023–2024

A **2024 primary study** added unusually direct, PPP3R1-specific spatial and interaction evidence to the lysosomal model. After experimentally induced lysosomal damage, immunofluorescence showed **LGALS3/galectin-3-dependent recruitment of PPP3R1 to lysosomes**. Binding experiments found a damage-enhanced PPP3R1–PPP3CB association; PPP3R1 associated *indirectly* with galectin-3 and bound TFEB in interaction assays. PPP3R1 knockdown weakened the galectin-3–SMURF1 interaction. In the authors’ model, PPP3R1 supports assembly and TFEB positioning, whereas **PPP3CB is the catalytic subunit** responsible for TFEB-directed phosphatase activity. The work is mechanistic cell-study evidence, not a demonstration that targeting PPP3R1 treats lysosomal disease in patients. The article appeared in *Autophagy* **20:735–751 (2024)**; its DOI contains its earlier publication identifier, **10.1080/15548627.2023.2267413**. (xia2024smurf1controlsthe pages 8-11, xia2024smurf1controlsthe pages 11-13)

For broader physiological context, a **2023 review** concluded from knockout and inhibitor studies that calcineurin/NFAT signaling participates in vascular smooth-muscle responses, including proliferation, migration and remodeling. This is evidence about **calcineurin signaling across subunits and cell types**, not proof that changing human PPP3R1 alone produces each vascular phenotype. The review was published **13 September 2023**. (nolze2023calcineurinisa pages 1-3, nolze2023calcineurinisa pages 3-4)

## Physiological evidence and real-world application

Genetic perturbation strengthens the assignment of CnB1 as a necessary regulatory component. CD4⁺ T-cell-specific *Ppp3r1/Cnb1* deletion in **mice** caused spontaneous colitis associated with increased IL-12 responsiveness, **JAK2–STAT4 signaling** and IFN-γ production. This shows that CnB1-dependent signaling also restrains a particular inflammatory response; it does **not** mean that CnB1 directly dephosphorylates STAT4. In separate mouse-heart and cultured-cardiomyocyte experiments, CIB1–CnB binding and CIB1-dependent sarcolemmal positioning correlated with NFAT reporter activation under hypertrophic stimulation. Deleting *Ppp3r1* in mouse embryonic fibroblasts also prevented CIB1 overexpression from stabilizing calcineurin A, supporting a role for CnB1 in maintaining its catalytic partner. (mencarelli2018calcineurinbin pages 10-11, mencarelli2018calcineurinbin pages 1-2, heineke2010cib1isa pages 4-6, heineke2010cib1isa pages 6-7)

**Clinical implementation targets the calcineurin complex, not PPP3R1 selectively.** Cyclosporine and tacrolimus are established calcineurin-inhibiting immunosuppressants used, for example, to prevent allograft rejection; related agents are used for immune-mediated disease. Their immunophilin–drug complexes block calcineurin-dependent signaling, including NFAT activation. Because these drugs inhibit complex activity rather than selectively deleting CnB1, their benefits or adverse effects cannot be assigned solely to PPP3R1. The 2023 vascular review discusses hypertension and renal salt-handling effects among relevant limitations. (nolze2023calcineurinisa pages 3-4, roy2020identifyingnewsubstrates pages 1-3)

## Evidence boundaries and key sources

**Interpretation:** The strongest molecular annotation is **intracellular EF-hand Ca²⁺ sensor and regulatory/scaffolding partner of catalytic calcineurin A**. Direct PPP3R1-specific evidence supports complex assembly, localized recruitment and requirements revealed by conditional mouse deletion. Much of the clinically relevant NFAT literature tests the holoenzyme or nonspecific calcineurin inhibitors, while the newer lysosomal mechanism has not been established here as a human clinical intervention. Reported calcium affinities characterize CnB EF-hand pairs in biochemical analyses; they are not measurements of calcium concentrations at every cellular site. (li2009calcineurinregulatorysubunit pages 1-2, roy2020identifyingnewsubstrates pages 3-4, xia2024smurf1controlsthe pages 8-11, nolze2023calcineurinisa pages 3-4)
