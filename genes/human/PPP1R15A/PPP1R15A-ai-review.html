Gene Description

PPP1R15A encodes GADD34, a stress-inducible regulatory subunit of protein phosphatase 1 (PP1). Its mRNA carries inhibitory uORFs and is translated, and transcribed via ATF4/CHOP, when eIF2alpha is phosphorylated during the integrated stress response. GADD34 binds the PP1 catalytic subunit through a C-terminal bipartite KVRF/RARA motif and recruits phosphorylated eIF2alpha (EIF2S1) through a conserved central substrate-binding region, forming a holophosphatase that dephosphorylates eIF2alpha Ser51 (Ser52 in precursor numbering). This restores eIF2B-mediated recycling and global protein synthesis, providing negative feedback that ends the PERK/ISR translational block and allows recovery from stress; prolonged activity also permits synthesis of pro-apoptotic CHOP targets. An N-terminal amphipathic helix anchors GADD34 peripherally on the cytoplasmic face of the ER membrane (and to a lesser extent mitochondria), targeting PP1alpha there; the protein is short-lived and degraded by the proteasome. GADD34 also interacts with LYN, SMAD7 and other partners, and contributes to restriction of some viruses.

  PPP1R15A encodes GADD34, a stress-inducible regulatory subunit of protein phosphatase 1 (PP1). Its mRNA
  carries inhibitory uORFs and is translated, and transcribed via ATF4/CHOP, when eIF2alpha is phosphorylated
  during the integrated stress response. GADD34 binds the PP1 catalytic subunit through a C-terminal bipartite
  KVRF/RARA motif and recruits phosphorylated eIF2alpha (EIF2S1) through a conserved central substrate-binding
  region, forming a holophosphatase that dephosphorylates eIF2alpha Ser51 (Ser52 in precursor numbering).
  This restores eIF2B-mediated recycling and global protein synthesis, providing negative feedback that
  ends the PERK/ISR translational block and allows recovery from stress; prolonged activity also permits
  synthesis of pro-apoptotic CHOP targets. An N-terminal amphipathic helix anchors GADD34 peripherally
  on the cytoplasmic face of the ER membrane (and to a lesser extent mitochondria), targeting PP1alpha
  there; the protein is short-lived and degraded by the proteasome. GADD34 also interacts with LYN, SMAD7
  and other partners, and contributes to restriction of some viruses.
