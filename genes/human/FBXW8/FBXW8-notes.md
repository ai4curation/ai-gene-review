# FBXW8 notes

## 2026-11-15 ORGANELLES Golgi batch 2 (Golgi apparatus rows)

- Rows: IDA (PMID:21572988) and IEA (GO_REF:0000044, SL-0132), both `located_in`, both stay ACCEPT; the Golgi
  stays in the second core function's locations.
- PMID:21572988 is abstract-only in the cache; full text read in PMC (PMC3091842, DOI 10.1371/journal.pbio.1001060)
  via the PubMed full-text tool. Not quoted in supporting_text. What I read: endogenous Fbxw8 in rat cerebellar
  granule neurons overlaps GM130 and TGN38 (cis and trans Golgi) and not Hsp60 or PDI; the same in hippocampal
  neurons; the signal is lost on Fbxw8 RNAi; GFP-Fbxw8 co-localizes with mCherry-GT; Fbxw8 is in the cytoplasmic
  fraction "which includes Golgi membranes". Fbxw8 or Cul7 knockdown disperses the Golgi (GM130, TGN38, EM),
  rescued by Grasp65 knockdown; OBSL1 localizes Cul7, not Fbxw8, to the Golgi.
- No sub-compartment: overlaps both cis and trans markers; soluble, recruited substrate receptor. Assay in rat
  neurons; FBXW8 is also cytoplasmic (PMID:17205132).
