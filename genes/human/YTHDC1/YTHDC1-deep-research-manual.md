# YTHDC1 manual evidence notes

Falcon and Perplexity-lite were unavailable for this review, so the review uses the reviewed UniProt record, the cached GOA rows, cached Reactome records, cached primary publications, and manual synthesis.

## Core nuclear m6A-reader role

The high-throughput mRNA-interactome rows from PMID:22658674 and PMID:22681889 support only generic RNA binding and are less informative than the YTH-domain m6A-reader evidence.

PMID:20167602 connected the YTH domain to RNA binding and YTHDC1-dependent splice-site choice: The presence of the binding motif in alternative exons is necessary for YT521-B to directly influence splice site selection in vivo.

PMID:25242552 structurally established m6A recognition by YTHDC1: Here we present the crystal structures of the YTH domain of YTHDC1, a member of the YTH domain family, and its complex with an m(6)A-containing RNA.

PMID:26876937 identified the direct splicing-factor mechanism: YTHDC1 promotes exon inclusion in targeted mRNAs through recruiting pre-mRNA splicing factor SRSF3 (SRp20) while blocking SRSF10 (SRp38) mRNA binding.

PMID:28984244 identified a direct mRNA export role: Here, we show that the m6A-binding protein YTHDC1 mediates export of methylated mRNA from the nucleus to the cytoplasm in HeLa cells.

PMID:27602518 established a non-core XIST role: A systematic comparison of m6A-binding proteins shows that YTH domain containing 1 (YTHDC1) preferentially recognizes m6A residues on XIST and is required for XIST function.

PMID:29799838 shows the mouse oocyte consequence of Ythdc1 loss: Strikingly, loss of YTHDC1 leads to extensive alternative polyadenylation in oocytes, altering 3' UTR length. Furthermore, YTHDC1 deficiency causes massive alternative splicing defects in oocytes.

YTHDC1 is a nuclear and nuclear-speckle m6A reader that couples methylated pre-mRNAs and mRNAs to splice-site recognition, regulated splicing, and mRNA export from the nucleus.

The mouse-derived YTHDC1 rows for brown fat cell differentiation, post-transcriptional regulation of gene expression, and molecular sequestering activity are conserved but non-core outputs of the nuclear m6A reader and splicing/export functions.

## Reactome PAXT rows

The Reactome PAXT events place YTHDC1 in the nucleoplasm as part of nuclear poly(A)+ RNA surveillance and decay complexes, but the rows only assert nucleoplasm for this review.

## Protein binding policy

The GO:0005515 IPI rows are curated physical associations and are removed under the project bare-protein-binding policy, not because the reported interactions are false.
