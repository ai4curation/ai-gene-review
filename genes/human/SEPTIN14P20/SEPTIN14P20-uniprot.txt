CC       of RNA stabilizers ELAVL1/HUR, MATR3 and PABPC1 with m6A-containing
CC       mRNAs (PubMed:32245947). Contributes to MYC stability by enhancing
CC       binding of IGF2BP1 to m6A-containing MYC mRNAs and increasing
CC       recruitment of RNA stabilizing proteins to m6A-containing MYC mRNAs
CC       (PubMed:32245947). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32245947}.

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Detected in colon (at protein level).
