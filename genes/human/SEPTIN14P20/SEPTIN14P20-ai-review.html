Gene Description

SEPTIN14P20 encodes RBRP (RNA-binding regulatory peptide), a 71-amino-acid peptide translated from a transcript of the septin 14 pseudogene 20 locus on chromosome 20q13.33, which was previously annotated as the long non-coding RNA LINC00266-1 (C20orf69). The peptide is the retained septin-14 reading frame of this pseudogene: residues 1-47 are identical to the C-terminal coiled-coil region of SEPTIN14 (residues 383-429), and only a 24-residue C-terminal tail is unique. RBRP has been reported in colorectal, breast, ovarian and nasopharyngeal cancer cells and in colorectal tissue, at higher levels in tumours than in adjacent normal tissue, but independent confirmation of the endogenous product is lacking. In the single functional study, RBRP bound the KH3-KH4 di-domain of the m6A reader IGF2BP1 in an RNA-independent manner (abolished by a G19A substitution), increased IGF2BP1 binding to m6A-modified RNA in vitro and in cells, and increased the association of IGF2BP1 and the RNA stabilisers ELAVL1/HuR, MATR3 and PABPC1 with m6A-marked MYC mRNA. This stabilised MYC mRNA and promoted colorectal cancer cell proliferation, migration, invasion and xenograft growth.

Core Functions

Rests on a single study of a pseudogene-encoded peptide whose existence UniProt rates as uncertain.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Which tryptic peptides were observed in the endogenous MS identification of RBRP? Only peptides from the unique C-terminal tail (residues 48-71, e.g. HPDPYEFLLLR or HPGFNEELSPC) distinguish RBRP from SEPTIN14, and the search was run against a custom RBRP-only database.

Q: Does the C-terminal coiled-coil region of SEPTIN14 itself, which contains the same Gly-19-equivalent residue (SEPTIN14 Gly-401), bind IGF2BP1? If so, the binding surface is a septin property rather than a novel peptide function.

Q: Has the RBRP-IGF2BP1 interaction or the MYC mRNA stabilisation effect been reproduced by an independent laboratory?

SEPTIN14P20 / RBRP (C0HM01): relationship to the parental septin

Script: align_to_septin14.py (Biopython local alignment, BLOSUM62, gap open -11 / extend -1;
sequences fetched live from UniProt REST). Raw output: output.txt.

Results (from output.txt)

  • RBRP vs human SEPTIN14 (Q6ZU15, 432 aa): RBRP residues 1-47 align to SEPTIN14 residues
    383-429 with 47/47 identities (100%). The remaining RBRP residues 48-71
    (PDPYEFLLLRKIKHPGFNEELSPC) do not align; SEPTIN14 instead ends ...DTKKDKHRKK.
  • RBRP vs SEPTIN7 (Q16181): weak, 13/33 identities (39%) over the coiled-coil region,
    i.e. ordinary septin-family similarity.
  • RBRP Gly-19, the residue whose G19A mutation abolishes IGF2BP1 binding (PMID:32245947),
    lies in the SEPTIN14-identical segment (it corresponds to SEPTIN14 Gly-401).

Interpretation

  • The RBRP ORF is the retained septin-14 reading frame of the SEPTIN14P20 pseudogene: the
    first two-thirds of the peptide reproduce the C-terminal coiled-coil region of SEPTIN14
    exactly, and only a 24-residue C-terminal tail is unique.
  • This contradicts the statement in PMID:32245947 that no matching proteins exist for RBRP.
  • Consequence for existence evidence: an antibody or MS peptide confined to residues 1-47
    cannot distinguish RBRP from SEPTIN14 (or a SEPTIN14 fragment). The anti-RBRP antigen
    (TDTKKDKHPDPY, residues 41-52) spans the junction, with 8 SEPTIN14-shared and 4 unique
    residues. Only tryptic peptides from the unique tail (e.g. HPDPYEFLLLR, HPGFNEELSPC) would be
    RBRP-specific; the paper does not list which peptides were observed, and its database search
    was against a custom RBRP database, so cross-identification cannot be ruled out from the text.
  • The IGF2BP1-binding determinant (Gly-19) is septin-14 sequence, so the binding surface is not
    novel to RBRP. Whether SEPTIN14's own C-terminal region binds IGF2BP1 is untested.
