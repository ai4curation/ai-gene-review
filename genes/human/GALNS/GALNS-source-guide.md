# GALNS source guide

GALNS removes 6-sulfate groups during lysosomal degradation of chondroitin and keratan glycans. One molecular activity, GO:0043890, covers both natural sugar classes. The current review contains 15 source annotations and one separately identified inherited proposal for keratan sulfate proteoglycan catabolism. ## Catalytic evidence and specificity

PMID:22940367 directly measures purified human GALNS on synthetic 4-MU-sulfate and 4-MU-Gal6S. The latter assay couples desulfation to beta-galactosidase reporter release and includes omission controls. Synthetic aryl-sulfate turnover supports the arylsulfatase IBA as a real, non-core catalytic capability; it does not establish a physiological aryl substrate. The authors explicitly describe substantial substrate-recognition promiscuity; the non-core arylsulfatase judgment retains that measured catalytic capability. The formylglycine/calcium active site and homodimer are structural findings. The GalNAc product pose is nonproductive, so it is not described as a productive sulfated-substrate pose.

PMID:820716 assays crude human kidney extracts on chondroitin-6-sulfate oligosaccharides and corrects a separate small arylsulfatase B contribution. It does not establish GALNS 4-sulfatase activity or intact-polymer cleavage. Subtracting ARSB contamination also does not prove that GALNS can never hydrolyze a 4-sulfated substrate. The 4S and 6S terms name different positional chemistries. A lateral correction is possible when an actual mapping error is established; non-descendance is not itself a reason to prohibit MODIFY. PMID:15962010 actually assays GALNS cDNA activity and SUMF1 enhancement; the detailed supplementary GALNS substrate protocol remains unread. PMID:8325655 remains abstract-only after bounded original-source checks, leaving its specific 4-sulfatase assertion UNDECIDED.

The [author-uploaded original of PMID:18285341](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/5567525_Distinct_Effects_of_N-Acetylgalactosamine-4-sulfatase_and_Galactose-6-sulfatase_Expression_on_Chondroitin_Sulfates), DOI [10.1074/jbc.M707967200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M707967200), provides the critical per-gene distinction. The cached abstract collectively links ASB-or-GALNS manipulation to CS and C4S outcomes. The selected GALNS-specific Results, Table 2 and Figure 2 caption report total CS changes but no C4S increase after silencing and no C4S change after overexpression. Table 1 separately records enzyme activities, including the effective GALNS manipulation and essentially unchanged ASB activity. The narrower Results govern the current interpretation, and the apparent abstract-versus-Results discrepancy is explicit. These cellular measurements do not demonstrate universal absence of a GALNS 4S side reaction.

The original selected Methods identify human GALNS expression and siRNA in MCF-7 cells. The explicit 4-MU-sulfate recipe is for ASB; detailed GALNS assay chemistry refers to earlier work. The normal cache remains abstract-only. Original text and the Figure 2 caption were inspected externally, but figure images and the prior assay recipe were not. Unchanged keratan abundance in this cell assay is not affirmative keratan-degradation evidence.
