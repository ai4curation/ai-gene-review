Gene Description

CNKSR2 is a multidomain neuronal scaffold that organizes postsynaptic protein assemblies and positions signaling partners near membranes. Its long C-terminal form binds PDZ-containing proteins through a terminal motif and supports complexes containing Densin and PSD-95. Mammalian neuronal studies link CNK2-mediated recruitment of regulators such as Vilse and TNIK to postsynaptic organization and spatial control of signaling. Human CNK2 also binds Raf, Rlf and EGFR. Alternative products differ in internal domains and the long C-terminal interaction region, so these activities are not established uniformly for every product. Pathogenic variants cause Houge-type X-linked syndromic intellectual developmental disorder, with developmental and speech delay and early-onset seizures.
