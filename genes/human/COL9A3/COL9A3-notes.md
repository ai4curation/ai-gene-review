# COL9A3 evidence notes

COL9A3 encodes the 684-residue alpha3(IX) precursor. The official record supplies one displayed sequence and no alternative-product description, and the seeded review has no alternative-products field. Signal/chain features and sequence identity were checked directly. Disease-associated truncations or engineered deletion constructs are not additional natural products.

## Structural and assembly evidence

PMID:8660302 analyzes human fetal articular cartilage. The actual purification, peptide sequencing, Results and Figures 4–5 identify alpha3 COL2 connections to type II N- and C-telopeptides and alpha3 NC1. These same-chain intermolecular links are genuine, while the native material remains a network of heterotrimeric collagen IX rather than a purified autonomous alpha3 dimer. PMID:17880280 independently reports alpha3-only full-length material mainly as monomers/dimers after expression in insect cells. The engineered long deletion constructs behave differently from full chains, and short constructs do not demonstrate the same trimerization competence. These limits favor a non-core homodimerization judgment alongside the main heterotrimeric structural activity.

PMID:27897211 directly resolves three distinct IX assembly-domain chains, attached to engineered type I collagen host–guest sequences. It establishes an alpha3-containing assembly/stagger interface, not the structure of intact collagen IX. The paper calls the domain NC2; its sequence overlaps a differently labelled current UniProt region. Preserve both source descriptions and resolve the nomenclature explicitly before treating labels as equivalent coordinates. PMID:8586434's complete authentic abstract establishes human alpha3 peptide sequencing, cloning and the heterotrimer composition; its original full text remains unavailable after bounded publisher/API recovery attempts.

A single integrated structural core links type IX trimer membership, extracellular matrix residence and matrix organization. Native cross-links and assembly interfaces supply a physical participation premise. Processing by hydroxylases, chaperones or cross-linking enzymes is not assigned as an enzymatic activity of alpha3. Alpha1 NC4 heparin, COMP and complement experiments are not transferred to the short alpha3 N-terminal region. No NEW annotation is proposed from another chain's activity or from the uncurated interaction inventory.

## Human proteomic evidence

PMID:25037231 uses serial extraction of human colorectal tissues and liver material, followed by digestion and LC-MS/MS. The original supplementary workbook identifies COL9A3/Q14050 at row 128 in both the complete-data and matrisome sheets, with 14 total unique peptides. This supports ECM detection.
