CC       membrane independently of pH or a Na(+) gradient. Major substrates are
CC       the iodothyronines T3 and T4 and to a lesser extent rT3 and 3,3-
CC       diiodothyronine (3,3'-T2) (PubMed:16887882, PubMed:18337592,

CC       PubMed:40680733). Acts as an important mediator of thyroid hormone
CC       transport, especially T3, through the blood-brain barrier (Probable)
CC       (PubMed:28526555). Mediates the efflux of 3,5-diiodo-L-tyrosine (DIT)
CC       and 3-iodo-L-tyrosine (MIT) (PubMed:38661522).
