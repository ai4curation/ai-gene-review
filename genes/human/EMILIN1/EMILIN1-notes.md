# EMILIN1 evidence notes

EMILIN1 combines a matrix-organizing role with integrin-mediated cell anchorage. The structural evidence includes interactions with elastin and fibulins, deposition rescue and organized extracellular assemblies. The adhesion evidence includes purified human alpha4beta1 binding, ligand-domain mutagenesis and receptor-blocking experiments. These are complementary activities; EMILIN1 is not the alpha4beta1 receptor heterodimer or the LOX enzyme that performs oxidative crosslinking. [PMID:12456677](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12456677/), [PMID:14701737](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14701737/), [PMID:18463100](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18463100/), [PMID:28717224](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28717224/).
