CC   -!- FUNCTION: Associates with SLC3A2 to form a functional heterodimeric
CC       complex that translocates small and large neutral amino acids with
CC       broad specificity and a stoichiometry of 1:1. Functions as amino acid
CC       antiporter mediating the influx of extracellular essential amino acids
CC       mainly in exchange with the efflux of highly concentrated intracellular
CC       amino acids (PubMed:10391915, PubMed:11311135, PubMed:11847106,
CC       PubMed:12716892, PubMed:15081149, PubMed:15918515, PubMed:29355479,
CC       PubMed:33298890, PubMed:34848541). Has relatively symmetrical
CC       selectivities but strongly asymmetrical substrate affinities at both
CC       the intracellular and extracellular sides of the transporter
CC       (PubMed:11847106). This asymmetry allows SLC7A8 to regulate
CC       intracellular amino acid pools (mM concentrations) by exchange with
CC       external amino acids (uM concentration range), equilibrating the
CC       relative concentrations of different amino acids across the plasma
CC       membrane instead of mediating their net uptake (PubMed:10391915,
CC       PubMed:11847106). May play an essential role in the reabsorption of
CC       neutral amino acids from the epithelial cells to the bloodstream in the
CC       kidney (PubMed:12716892). Involved in the uptake of methylmercury
CC       (MeHg) when administered as the L-cysteine or D,L-homocysteine
CC       complexes, and hence plays a role in metal ion homeostasis and toxicity
CC       (PubMed:12117417). Involved in the cellular activity of small molecular
CC       weight nitrosothiols, via the stereoselective transport of L-
CC       nitrosocysteine (L-CNSO) across the transmembrane (PubMed:15769744).
CC       Imports the thyroid hormone diiodothyronine (T2) and to a smaller
CC       extent triiodothyronine (T3) but not rT 3 or thyroxine (T4)
CC       (PubMed:26305885). Mediates the uptake of L-DOPA (By similarity). May
CC       participate in auditory function (By similarity). Mediates the uptake
CC       of leucine and 3-iodo-L-tyrosine (MIT) (PubMed:26305885). Mediates the
CC       uptake of 3,5-diiodo-L-tyrosine (DIT) (PubMed:38661522).
