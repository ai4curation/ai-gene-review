# CLDN6 notes

Deep research providers unavailable in this environment; research from UniProt P56747 and the cached
publications. Human experimental literature in GOA is limited to HCV-entry work plus interactome screens.

## Topology / localization
- Tetraspan claudin (4 TM, 2 extracellular loops, cytoplasmic termini with PDZ-binding motif).
- UniProt: "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell junction, tight junction ... Cell membrane; Multi-pass membrane protein".
- Receptor-active claudin pools associating with CD81 are basolateral rather than junctional in polarized
  hepatoma cells [PMID:20375010 "Claudin-1 associated with CD81 at the basolateral membrane of polarized
  HepG2 cells"] — relevant to surface accessibility.
- Oncofetal expression: fetal epithelia, silent in adult, re-expressed in germ-cell/ovarian tumours
  (CAR-T and antibody target).

## Functions
- Tight junction strand component: UniProt "Plays a major role in tight junction-specific obliteration of
  the intercellular space"; binds ZO-1/ZO-2/ZO-3.
- HCV entry receptor (host-factor role) [PMID:20375010; UniProt "Acts as a receptor for hepatitis C virus
  (HCV) entry into hepatic cells"].
