Gene Description

Endoregulin (ERLN, also known as ELN and SMIM6) is a 62-amino-acid single-pass transmembrane micropeptide of the endoplasmic reticulum membrane and a member of the regulin family of sarco/endoplasmic reticulum Ca2+-ATPase (SERCA) regulators that also includes phospholamban (PLN), sarcolipin (SLN), myoregulin (MRLN), another-regulin (ARLN) and DWORF (STRIT1). It has a short cytosolic N-terminal region, a C-terminal transmembrane helix carrying the conserved SERCA-binding motif, and a short luminal tail. Unlike the muscle regulins, it is expressed mainly in non-muscle epithelia and endothelia, where its distribution overlaps that of SERCA3. The peptide binds SERCA in the same transmembrane groove as phospholamban and inhibits the pump: the mouse peptide lowers the apparent Ca2+ affinity of SERCA3a and SERCA2b in cell homogenates, the purified human peptide lowers the maximal turnover rate of purified SERCA1a in reconstituted membranes, and its expression reduces ER Ca2+ stores in cultured cells. Endoregulin also forms homo-oligomers and hetero-oligomers with other regulins, while binding SERCA as a monomer. No loss-of-function phenotype or disease association has been reported.

Core Functions

Endoregulin is a single-pass ER membrane micropeptide that binds the SERCA Ca2+-ATPase in its transmembrane regulatory groove (the phospholamban site) and inhibits it. This reduces Ca2+ pumping into the ER in the non-muscle epithelial and endothelial cells where it is expressed, mainly alongside SERCA3. Direct inhibition has been shown with the purified human peptide (reduced SERCA turnover) and with the mouse peptide in cells (reduced apparent Ca2+ affinity of SERCA3a/SERCA2b).

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does endoregulin inhibit SERCA by lowering apparent Ca2+ affinity (as reported for mouse ELN with SERCA3a in cell homogenates) or by lowering Vmax (as reported for the human peptide with SERCA1a in proteoliposomes)? Is the difference due to species, SERCA isoform or assay system?

Suggested experts: Seth L Robia, Howard S Young

Q: Is endogenous human endoregulin protein detectable in the SERCA3-expressing epithelia and endothelia in which its mRNA is found, and does it co-purify with SERCA3 there?

Suggested experts: Douglas M Anderson, Eric N Olson

Q: Does hetero-oligomerisation with other regulins (for example SLN or DWORF) sequester endoregulin away from SERCA in cells that co-express them?

Suggested experts: Seth L Robia

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Knock out ERLN in a human epithelial cell line that expresses SERCA3 (for example an airway or intestinal epithelial line) and measure ER Ca2+ content with an ER-targeted ratiometric indicator, and SERCA KCa/Vmax in microsomes. Rescue with wild-type versus TM-helix binding-motif mutant endoregulin.

Hypothesis: Endogenous endoregulin tonically inhibits SERCA3 and limits ER Ca2+ stores in epithelial cells.

Experiment: Co-reconstitute purified human endoregulin with purified human SERCA3a and SERCA2b and measure Ca2+-dependent ATPase activity, to resolve the KCa-versus-Vmax discrepancy between the mouse and human studies.

Hypothesis: Human endoregulin inhibits its physiological partner SERCA3 by the same mechanism observed with SERCA1a.

Identity

  • Human ERLN, 62 aa, single predicted TM helix (UniProt TRANSMEM 25..45), cytosolic
    N-terminus, short luminal C-terminal tail. Former symbols C17orf110, SMIM6. Mouse
    ortholog Q3U0I6 (Elnl / 1110017F19Rik), 56 aa.
  • GOA (human): only two rows, both GO:0005789 ER membrane (IEA from UniProt SubCell;
    ISS from mouse Q3U0I6). PAN-GO: no IBA. No human MF or BP annotation at all.
  • Mouse Q3U0I6 GOA: IDA ER membrane, IPI protein binding (SERCA2b), IDA
    acts_upstream_of_or_within GO:1901895 negative regulation of ATPase-coupled calcium
    transmembrane transporter activity - all from PMID:27923914.

Discovery - mouse (PMID:27923914, Anderson et al. 2016 Sci Signal; PubMed-verified)

  • Found by screening the mouse genome for the SERCA-binding motif
    PMID:27923914.
  • ER colocalisation with SERCA2b in COS-7 cells
    PMID:27923914.
  • Co-IP with SERCA2b, competed by PLN (same binding groove)
    PMID:27923914.
  • Ca uptake: lowers apparent Ca affinity (KCa) of SERCA3a, no Vmax change
    PMID:27923914.
  • Expression: non-muscle epithelia, overlapping SERCA3
    PMID:27923914.
  • All constructs in this paper are mouse (mouse genome screen, 56-aa ELN).

Human-sequence experimental evidence

  • PMID:34445594 (Rathod et al. 2021 IJMS, Young lab): recombinant HUMAN ELN
    PMID:34445594

    PMID:34445594
    lowers Vmax, not KCa
    PMID:34445594.

    inhibits the pump. Note the mechanistic discrepancy with the mouse data (KCa effect on

    PMID:34445594.
  • PMID:36523160 (Phillips et al. 2023 Biophys J, Robia lab): human sequences
    PMID:36523160;
    ELN homo- and hetero-oligomerises with PLN, SLN, ALN, DWORF (FRET in cells). This is

    but bind SERCA as monomers
    PMID:31449798.
    Species of the ELN construct is not stated in the cached text; the 2023 paper later
    comments that mouse sequences are satisfactory models, implying earlier work used mouse.
  • PMID:39921961 (Cunningham et al. 2025 Cell Calcium; abstract only): ELN lowers ER Ca2+
    content in live cells
    PMID:39921961.
    Species of construct not stated in abstract.

Physiology

  • No loss-of-function or disease data
    PMID:34445594.

Comparators (QuickGO, 2026-09-30)

  • PLN (P26678): GO:0042030 ATPase inhibitor activity IDA/IBA/ISS/IEA; GO:0004857 ISS;
    GO:0051117 ATPase binding ISS; GO:1901894 etc.
  • SLN (O00631): GO:0004857 enzyme inhibitor activity ISS; GO:1901895 IDA; GO:1901894 IDA.
  • So the SERCA-inhibitory regulins carry an inhibitor MF and a negative regulation of
    ATPase-coupled calcium transporter activity BP. ERLN is in the same role (the peptide
    itself binds and inhibits the pump) - participation test is satisfied: ELN is the
    regulator performing the regulatory step, not a substrate.

Decisions

  • Both ER membrane rows: ACCEPT (mouse IDA + human localisation consistent).
  • NEW MF GO:0042030 ATPase inhibitor activity (IDA, PMID:34445594 human peptide,
    purified system; mouse data corroborate). Considered GO:0141110 transporter inhibitor
    activity, but PLN precedent and the assay (ATPase turnover) favour GO:0042030.
  • NEW BP GO:1901895 negative regulation of ATPase-coupled calcium transmembrane
    transporter activity (mirrors mouse IDA and SLN IDA).
  • Not proposed: identical protein binding (homo-oligomerisation) - binding-only term,
    functional significance unclear; ER calcium ion homeostasis - only abstract-level
    live-cell data; tissue-level processes - none established.
