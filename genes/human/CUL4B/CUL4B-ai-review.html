Gene Description

CUL4B is a cullin scaffold that organizes DDB1-associated RING E3 ubiquitin ligases. It connects substrate-receptor assemblies to RBX1-containing catalytic machinery, supporting substrate ubiquitination and proteasomal turnover. CRL4B also supports nondegradative H2AK119 monoubiquitination and cooperates with PRC2 in repressive chromatin regulation. CUL4B-containing DDB2 complexes participate in damaged-DNA recognition and nucleotide-excision repair. CUL4B is enriched in the nucleus, with context-dependent cytoplasmic pools, and shares some functions with CUL4A while also assembling distinct regulatory complexes. Alternative products differ at the N terminus, including the nuclear-localization region.
