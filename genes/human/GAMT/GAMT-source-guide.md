# GAMT: reaction, compartments and evidence scope

GAMT methylates guanidinoacetate using S-adenosylmethionine (SAM), producing creatine and S-adenosylhomocysteine (SAH). The strongest selected human evidence is the liver activity experiment in [PMID:8651275](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1914613/): scanned original pages 915–917 include radiomethyl transfer, chromatographic identification of creatine, substrate-free blanks and reference-enzyme controls. The patient deficiency observations complement this positive reaction assay. They are not the sole basis for assigning catalytic activity or creatine biosynthesis.

The normal publication cache for that paper is abstract-only. The original scanned pages were read separately; the endpoint labeled as a PDF returned HTML and is excluded from the original-page evidence. [PMID:9325156](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9325156/) supplies an authentic complete abstract describing the human reaction and gene mapping, with full Methods and figures unavailable. Its negative result for a coding mutation in jittery mice does not contradict human GAMT activity.

## One reaction, two process facets

[Reactome R-HSA-71286](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-71286) explicitly connects SAM and guanidinoacetate inputs to SAH, creatine and a proton. Catalyst [R-HSA-71276](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-71276) resolves to human Q14353 and cytosol. The official HTML reaction and participant records were inspected; failed ContentService requests are not represented as successful JSON evidence. [Creatine metabolism](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-71288) includes downstream transport, phosphorylation and creatinine formation, but GAMT performs the biosynthetic methylation step. Broad methyltransferase and creatine-metabolism annotations are therefore refined to the verified specific descendants.

The inherited core-only SAM-catabolism term describes another aspect of this same reaction. A current exact-term comparator search found experimental rat Gamt support and mouse Gamt, Gnmt and Pcmt1 annotations; the term is used for enzymes performing SAM-dependent methyl transfer. The verified typed ancestors do not make it redundant with creatine biosynthesis. This retains one core with two process facets, without adding a NEW annotation or a quantitative methyl-donor-homeostasis claim. The cached GO-CAM index has no Q14353/GAMT match; this is a dated index observation, not a statement about every live model.

## Positive interaction records and an unresolved process

BioPlex2 and BioPlex3 293T tables contain the GAMT–FARS2 pair, at original lines 19992 and 63913 respectively. Tagged-protein affinity-purification mass spectrometry supports co-association without establishing direct binary binding or a defined GAMT regulatory role. Absence of that edge from the inspected HCT116 network is not a universal biological negative. The source methods and selected text are from [PMID:28514442](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28514442/) and [PMID:33961781](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33961781/).

The retained HuRI PSI dataset for [PMID:32296183](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32296183/) contains Q14353-1–Q969Q5 at lines 10815, 31161 and 103319, and Q14353-1–Q9HCM9-2 at lines 2887, 61333, 77131, 149193, 165485 and 170884. These are array, prey-pooling and validated two-hybrid records from human ORFs in yeast. Nine records do not mean nine independent biological interactions. The exact target clone, partner suffix and historical publication placeholder remain in provenance. These positive observations do not define a more informative GAMT molecular activity, so the four bare protein-binding annotations are removed as uninformative, without denying the associations.

The muscle-contraction TAS remains UNDECIDED. [PMID:8547310](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8547310/) has an accessible human-cloning/clinical abstract, but its full supporting experiments were not recovered. Hypotonia and creatine deficiency do not by themselves show that GAMT performs the force-generating contractile step. The prior confident over-annotation and citation-defect interpretations are preserved historically and replaced with this explicit access limitation.
