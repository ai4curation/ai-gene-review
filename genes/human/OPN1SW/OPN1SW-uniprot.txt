CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled photoreceptor that selectively activates
CC       transducin G alpha protein (G(t)) in response to short-wavelength
CC       (blue-violet) light, thereby stimulating the G(t)/transducin protein
CC       pathway (PubMed:2937147, PubMed:6140680). This activation leads to
CC       increased cGMP phosphodiesterase activity, resulting in decreased
CC       intracellular cGMP and subsequent closure of cGMP-gated ion channels
CC       (By similarity). The process is initiated when the opsin-bound cis-
CC       retinal chromophore absorbs a photon and isomerizes to all-trans-
CC       retinal, inducing a conformational change in the opsin that triggers
CC       the G protein-mediated phototransduction cascade (By similarity).
CC       Signal termination occurs via receptor phosphorylation, which promotes
CC       binding of cone arrestin (ARR3) and displacement of the G protein alpha
CC       subunit (By similarity). Required for the maintenance of cone outer
CC       segment organization in the ventral retina, but not essential for the
CC       maintenance of cone photoreceptor function (By similarity). Involved in
CC       ensuring the correct abundance and localization of retinal membrane
CC       proteins (By similarity). Mediates visual perception of blue light
CC       (PubMed:1386496, PubMed:1531728, PubMed:23022137). May contribute to
CC       increased spectral sensitivity under dim light conditions (By
CC       similarity). Is not UV-sensitive (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:D5H3X0, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P04000,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51491, ECO:0000269|PubMed:1386496,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:1531728, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23022137,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2937147, ECO:0000269|PubMed:6140680}.

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: The red (OPN1LW), green (OPN1MW), and blue (OPN1SW)
CC       photoreceptor pigments are found in cone cells (PubMed:2937147).
CC       Expressed throughout the epidermis and dermis, primarily in the stratum
CC       granulosum in the facial and abdominal skin (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:30168605). Expressed in dermal fibroblasts (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:31380578). Expressed in melanocytes (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:31730232). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2937147,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30168605, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31380578,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31730232}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Maximally activated by short-wavelength light near 420 nm,
CC       corresponding to the blue-violet region of the visible spectrum in
CC       retinal cone cell (PubMed:6140680). Induced by ultraviolet A light in
CC       dermal fibroblasts. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31380578,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:6140680}.
CC   -!- PTM: Contains one retinal chromophore covalently bound to a lysine
CC       residue via a Schiff base. Upon light absorption, the bound 11-cis-
CC       retinal isomerizes to all-trans-retinal. Following activation, the
CC       covalently bound all-trans-retinal is released through hydrolysis of
CC       the Schiff base, and OPN1 is regenerated by binding a new 11-cis-
CC       retinal molecule. {ECO:0000269|Ref.10}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated by GRK1; phosphorylation is activated by light.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51491}.

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23022137}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
