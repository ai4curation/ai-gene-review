The [official caveola-assembly definition, GO:0070836](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO%3A0070836), describes components aggregating, arranging and bonding to form a caveola. Its parent is GO:0044854, plasma membrane raft assembly, under membrane-raft assembly and organization. The definition describes structural construction and does not restrict participation to a catalyst or integral membrane protein. Selected complete Results, captions and Methods of PMID:24013648, Molecular composition and ultrastructure of the caveolar coat complex, provide CAVIN1-specific participation evidence. Human cavin constructs, crosslinking, partial crosslink reduction, velocity gradients and isolation establish oligomeric coat units; endogenous and tagged human-cell localization controls place cavin-1 throughout the caveolar bulb. Human cavin-2 and cavin-3 coassembly in mouse fibroblast knockout backgrounds depends on cavin-1. These combined observations identify an organizing structural contribution, beyond a knockout merely preventing an outcome. Human HeLa/RPE coat experiments are distinguished from the mouse knockout cellular backgrounds. A purified direct CAVIN1–caveolin contact, complete atomic lattice geometry and independent CAVIN1 membrane bending are not established by these experiments.

The PMID:11139612 cache remains abstract-only. Separately indexed official primary Methods and selected Results/captions were read, with no claim to figure pixels or a complete paper.
