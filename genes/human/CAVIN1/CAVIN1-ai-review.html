Gene Description

CAVIN1 (PTRF) is a peripheral caveolar coat protein that oligomerizes and associates with caveolins and other cavins on the cytoplasmic surface of caveolae. It supplies a structural framework for the coat and is important for stable caveola formation in adipose, muscle and other cells. Human biallelic loss causes congenital generalized lipodystrophy with muscular dystrophy and secondary caveolin deficiency. A smaller nuclear pool is observed in human adipocytes. Its conserved transcript-release role is supported principally by murine biochemical and cellular studies, in which PTRF recognizes nascent ribosomal RNA and facilitates release of paused RNA polymerase I complexes and transcriptional recycling.
