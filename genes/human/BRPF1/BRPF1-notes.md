## Scientific synthesis

BRPF1 supplies a chromatin-reader and scaffold/activator role in alternative KAT6A/MOZ, KAT6B/MORF and KAT7/HBO1 assemblies. KAT6A and KAT6B are related alternative catalytic partners; the complex name does not require both enzymes in every particle. BRPF1 bridges the KAT proteins to ING and MEAF6 factors, increases activity on nucleosomal substrates, and helps select substrate context. Acetyl transfer is performed by the KAT subunits. The activator core uses the already sourced GO:0010698 rather than attributing an intrinsic HAT reaction to BRPF1. [PMID:18794358](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18794358/), [PMID:24065767](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3792477/), [PMID:27939640](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27939640/).

A second, coupled core captures histone-reader activity. Purified human BRPF1 bromodomain structures and mutagenesis establish recognition of acetylated H2A and H4 peptides. PZP experiments independently show H3 recognition with an unmethylated Lys4 and chromatin engagement by the assembled complex. ING5 is the source of H3K4me3-directed targeting in that complex; this is not intrinsic H3K4me3 recognition by the BRPF1 PZP domain. Two binding rows are refined to GO:0140054 histone H2A reader activity and GO:0140008 histone H4 reader activity; their exact GOA partner IDs are preserved. The peptide structures do not distinguish among genes encoding identical histone sequences. The broad core GO:0140566 covers the complementary recognition modules without multiplying overlapping core entries. [PMID:25281266](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4252766/), [PMID:24065767](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3792477/).

The three H4 catalytic labels already carry `contributes_to`. Their weaker assertion must not be mistaken for independent BRPF1 catalysis. The 2006 original assay remains unavailable beyond the abstract. Later actual Results distinguish BRPF1-complex acetylation of free H3/H4 from preferential H3 acetylation in chromatin, whereas HBO1-JADE complexes target chromatin H4K5/8/12. That distinction justifies uncertainty about the precise original sites, not categorical removal of all possible free-histone contribution. [PMID:16387653](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16387653/), [PMID:24065767](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3792477/).

Nuclear function and chromatin association are directly supported. The 2008 primary indexed Results also show cytoplasmic tagged BRPF1 when expressed alone, with nuclear relocalization on partner coexpression. This is a conditional non-core compartment; it does not establish a separate cytoplasmic biochemical function or quantify endogenous distribution. Runx-responsive reporter coactivation with MOZ is likewise context dependent: Gal4-BRPF1 alone lacked activation potential. The publisher erratum swaps H2A/H2B labels in Fig.5D/E/G and BR2/BR14 labels in Fig.6E; no claim here depends on those uncorrected labels. [PMID:18794358](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2573306/), [publisher erratum](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1128/MCB.01901-08).

The ZMYND8-centered paper explicitly reports focal nuclear BRPF1 in its broad bromodomain screen. Its title is not a reason to reject the BRPF1 localization row. The fuller actual Results do not place BRPF1 in the ZMYND8 damage-recruitment class; no ZMYND8 repair mechanism is assigned to BRPF1. The 2013 genomic mapping antibody recognizes both BRPF1 and BRPF2, so the ChIP map is not presented as BRPF1-exclusive. [PMID:25593309](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4298138/), [PMID:24065767](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3792477/).

The broad existing developmental annotation is retained as non-core context, supported by the human variant study and disruption of the chromatin machinery. This is not a new instructive-patterning claim. The original hemopoiesis annotation remains UNDECIDED because neither the source-specific BRPF1 mechanism nor the later mouse primary assays were examined sufficiently to settle it. KAT6A phenotypes are not automatically transferred to BRPF1. [PMID:27939640](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27939640/).

## 2026-10-03 — reader specificity and the chromatin-remodeling relation

The H2A reader identifier was checked independently against the official ontology views: [GO:0140054](https://flybase.org/cgi-bin/cvreport.pl?cvterm=GO%3A0140054&rel=is_a) is correctly named histone H2A reader activity. Selected Methods and Results in the authenticated fuller source confirm human BRPF1 residues 629–742, modified peptides and ITC experiments. The current [GO:0006338 definition](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0006338) begins: “A dynamic process of chromatin reorganization resulting in changes to chromatin structure.” The decisive additional ontology assertion is [GO:0140993 histone modifying activity](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0140993) **part_of** GO:0006338. This is a molecular-function-to-biological-process relation, not an is_a claim between two process terms. The older [GO:0016570 histone modification](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0016570) is explicitly obsolete because it represents a molecular function. Thus a historical sibling comparison does not justify excluding histone-modifying mechanisms from the current remodeling process.

BRPF1's participation is supported by its own contribution to complex assembly, activation and chromatin engagement. In the inspected [PMID:24065767 Results](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24065767/), PZP-domain deletions disrupt histone association and chromatin acetylation while leaving free-histone acetylation intact. The same study distinguishes H3 targeting by BRPF1 complexes from H4 targeting by JADE complexes. The [PMID:27939640 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27939640/) independently reports deficient H3K23 acetylation in the pathogenic-variant context. The retained GO:0006338 annotations and core process links therefore refer to a scaffold/reader contribution to histone modification; KAT6A, KAT6B or KAT7 performs acetyl transfer. Neither an intrinsic BRPF1 ATPase nor a direct nucleosome-sliding experiment is claimed. Both core descriptions now state this mechanistic boundary explicitly.

The PZP evidence for recognition of H3 with unmethylated Lys4 remains explicit in the accepted broad histone-reader row and core.
