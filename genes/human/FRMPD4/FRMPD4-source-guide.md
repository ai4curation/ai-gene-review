# FRMPD4 source and assay guide

This guide accompanies genuine FRMPD4 acquisition through the repository's native tools. All 76 original GOA contexts, all 16 columns of the 76-row export and all 76 genuine native seed objects have been reconciled by exact source key. Actual TSV physical lines and stored indices are recorded separately from the preserved private forecast. Original partners, qualifiers, evidence codes and null extensions remain intact. No new annotations or quotations are proposed.

The main functional evidence uses human FRMPD4 constructs alongside rodent neurons and tissue. The current official sequence is 1322 residues; no alternative-products declaration was found. Experimental fragments, disease truncations and rodent immunoreactive bands are not treated as separate natural human products.

## Measurement and orientation audit

For PMID:36115835, all 53 original partner contexts were joined to the original supplementary measurements and retained curated records. Thirty-one have positive quantitative support and 22 remain unresolved because their matched original signals are below the assay thresholds. A composite zero or the imported `kd:1.0x10(molar)` value is not interpreted as a physical affinity; `negative=false` does not establish detectability. No unresolved row is removed or declared biologically absent.

Twenty-seven positive contexts measure the human FRMPD4 C-terminal motif (1313–1322) binding a partner PDZ domain and support a PDZ-domain-binding refinement. Four positive contexts measure FRMPD4’s own PDZ domain (67–160) binding the partner peptide and retain generic binding. TJP3 is the critical reverse-orientation example. LNX1, PARD3B and GRIP2 have useful positive author rows beyond finite-parameter coverage in the exact curated records. Domain-specific missing cells remain missing.
