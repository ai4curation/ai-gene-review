## Catalytic identity

PMID12711671 directly identifies human ELAC2 as a tRNA 3′-processing endonuclease. Human testis cDNA encoded the 826-residue protein; an intein expression/purification system in E. coli yielded active recombinant enzyme. Cleavage of a human pre-tRNAArg was mapped after its discriminator nucleotide. Vector-derived material and GroEL controls addressed the bacterial preparation background. The preparation contained copurifying proteins, so its exact purity should not be overstated. Engineered N- and C-terminal halves were also assayed; these are experimental constructs, not the four natural splice products. The C-terminal half retained cleavage of the full precursor. Pig liver and yeast experiments are distinct corroboration, not human assays. Cancer-associated substitutions did not uniformly eliminate catalytic activity; disease association does not define a separate molecular function.

PMID15317868 independently studies recombinant human long ELAC2, human short ELAC1, the engineered human ELAC2 halves, yeast long enzyme and pig liver material. Full human long enzyme processes a pre-tRNA and a minimal pre-tRNA substrate. Neither separated half reproduced the full enzyme's guide-dependent target-RNA cleavage. Magnesium/manganese dependence was experimentally varied; different species and constructs had different responses. The guided synthetic-RNA assays do not by themselves establish physiological general mRNA degradation. The paper explicitly leaves endogenous mediation of an earlier cellular reporter result unresolved.

PMID16361254 and PMID18809514 remain abstract-level reads after unsuccessful full-body access. Their abstracts explicitly describe human long-enzyme substrate/kinetic or mutagenesis experiments; no wrong-gene or absent-activity claim follows from missing Methods. The exact constructs, residue boundaries and all controls were not recovered. Human direct catalysis is independently established by the two accessible earlier originals and the independent 2024 consultation.

## Natural products and translation initiation

PMID21559454 used human ELAC2 coding sequence with its native upstream context and C-terminal EGFP in stable inducible 293 cells, with a 143B replication context. Native-start-context protein localized to nuclei and mitochondria. Optimizing the first start codon favored mitochondria; deleting the first 15 amino acids favored nuclei. These experiments support alternative translation initiation and targeting, without functionally identifying official splice products Q9BQ52-2, -3 or -4. An 811-aa translation-start form, the predicted 17–826 processed chain, the 826-aa displayed precursor and the 31–826 recombinant enzyme are different entities. This paper is a localization/targeting study, not a new direct nuclease assay. Its interpretation of older tagged constructs is context-specific, not proof against every cytosolic ELAC2 observation.

PMID21593607 author-upload original: endogenous antibody localization in four human cell lines supports both nuclear and mitochondrial pools; formaldehyde fixation and tagging contexts are documented. HeLa RNAi and processing-site assays support mitochondrial tRNA 3′ maturation. An engineered M1A initiation mutant is not an official splice product. The RNAi results do not make ELAC2 the RNase P 5′ nuclease. The retrieved Methods/Results text was read; original figure pixels and supplements were not inspected by this owner.

PMID21857155 author-upload original: optimized-start C-terminal EYFP in fixed HeLa cells colocalized with mitochondria. RNAi, junction assays and mitochondrial RNA sequencing distinguish ELAC2-associated 3′ processing from RNase P-associated 5′ processing. The text reports ELAC2/PTCD1 association. Imaging does not independently resolve the matrix. The reported global 3′-read ratio p value for ELAC2 is 0.0581; it should not be silently represented as below 0.05. No new translation, respiration, methyltransferase or ribosome-assembly annotation follows from these perturbations. Figure pixels were not inspected.

## Localization and high-throughput sources

PMID34800366: the authentic Table S1 was extracted from the Europe PMC supplementary ZIP. Sheet A row341 identifies Q9BQ52 as the simplified accession, within a group also containing Q9BQ52-4, Q9BQ52-2 and other accessions. ELAC2 is MitoCoP-positive, subtractive Class1, spatial Cluster1 and importomics Class1; it meets experimental pool criteria rather than only literature supplementation. Sheet B row309 contains the ELAC2 gene-level entry. These data support mitochondrial localization but do not distinguish the splice products within the protein group. The paper combines human cell-line fractionation, quantitative mass spectrometry and TOMM40 perturbation; its subcellular classification is not a direct activity assay. An initial PMC attachment route returned challenge HTML despite HTTP200 and was excluded.

PMID24703694 remains abstract-level after access attempts. The abstract explicitly identifies ELAC2 among RNA-processing proteins associated with mitochondrial nucleoids. This is positive source-specific evidence; it is not evidence that ELAC2 replicates DNA, transcribes RNA or assembles ribosomes. Exact original construct, cell/species and assay details are unresolved here and must not be invented.

PMID22658674 and PMID22681889 report human poly(A)-RNA capture studies. The retrieved material establishes study design but does not recover the ELAC2-specific original supplement row for either source. The general fact that ELAC2 recognizes RNA is independently supported by catalytic and structural work; that does not authenticate a particular mRNA-capture measurement or establish an mRNA-regulatory process. Retain the distinction between biochemical RNA recognition and unresolved source-specific capture details.

## Human disease and processing

PMID23849775: patient fibroblast and muscle processing-junction assays found increased uncleaved mitochondrial tRNA-containing precursors. Lentiviral wild-type ELAC2 complementation rescued the fibroblast processing defect. RNA-seq and Northern blots examined mature RNAs and processing intermediates; mature tRNA abundance did not simply track the degree of processing impairment. The yeast Thr513Ile experiment is a separate ortholog model of the human Thr520Ile substitution. Translation and respiratory abnormalities are downstream consequences; they do not justify adding those processes as direct ELAC2 functions. The authors explicitly discuss differences from transient HeLa RNAi results and alternative mechanisms. The expression construct was described as wild-type cDNA, not as a mapped set of four splice isoforms.
