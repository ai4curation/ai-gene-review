## Actin crosslinking, splicing and calcium

- [PMID:23557398, Foley and Young](https://doi.org/10.1042/BJ20121824): the native cache is abstract-only. A public [author manuscript](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Paul-Young-14/publication/236112733_An_analysis_of_splicing_actin-binding_properties_heterodimerization_and_molecular_interactions_of_the_non-muscle_a-actinins/links/5cc6e349299bf1209787a952/An-analysis-of-splicing-actin-binding-properties-heterodimerization-and-molecular-interactions-of-the-non-muscle-a-actinins.pdf) was inspected, including supplementary Methods on PDF pages 29–30. Figure 2 compares purified human ACTN1/ACTN4 constructs against human platelet actin. Full-length purification tags were removed. The exon 19 splice choice changes calcium sensitivity. Figures 3–4 support heterodimer formation in tested settings, with cell-line exceptions; fragment pull-downs retained their fusion tags. Human cell-line and murine tissue experiments are distinct. The manuscript's 893-residue construct numbering is not a verified mapping to the current 892-residue canonical UniProt sequence.
- [PMID:26879394, Murphy et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/1873-3468.12101): publisher full text was read; the native cache retains the abstract. Purified human ACTN1 actin-binding-domain variants were compared with wild type using human platelet actin, followed by HeLa-cell measurements. R46Q increased affinity and bundling, but the variants differed. R46Q and E225K retained calcium inhibition. These assays do not support a universal calcium-insensitive disease mechanism or identical changes in gross cell architecture.
- [PMID:31365757, O'Sullivan et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/1873-3468.13562): publisher full text was read; the native cache retains the abstract. Rod/CaM-domain variants increased bundling to varying degrees; L547P was not a significant increase in the reported assay. Calcium binding was broadly retained, and full-length mutant proteins retained calcium-dependent inhibition. The paper appeared online in 2019 and in a 2020 issue. Actin species was not independently verified from the unread supplementary detail.
- [PMID:26020004, Backman](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4435478/): full-text XML is cached. Isolated human C-terminal constructs representing brain, non-muscle and smooth-muscle splice forms were expressed in bacteria. The brain and non-muscle constructs bound one calcium ion with reported affinities of 49 ± 9 and 56 ± 18 μM; no binding was detected for the smooth-muscle construct. These measurements concern fragments under the assay conditions, not whole-cell calcium thresholds.
- [PMID:27272015, Drmota Prebil et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4895382/): full-text XML is cached. NMR, calorimetry, mass spectrometry and mutagenesis establish one functional calcium-binding site in the EF1 region of the isolated non-muscle CaM-like domain. The full-length neck-fastening mechanism is a structural model rather than a direct observation of full-length inhibited ACTN1.

## Platelet architecture and disease experiments

[PMID:23434115, Kunishima et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2013.01.015) links dominant ACTN1 variants to macrothrombocytopenia and assays their cytoskeletal effects. Full Methods and Results were read in an eight-page article-in-press PDF; supplements were not read. Human platelet-derived constructs were tested in CHO cells, and Q32K/V105I were tested in mouse fetal-liver megakaryocytes. Mutant cells produced fewer, larger proplatelet tips, but the fraction of megakaryocytes extending proplatelets over four days was unchanged. The sampled human platelets showed apparently normal aggregation, clot retraction and spreading. This does not establish a universal negative gene-product role in aggregation. Dominant-negative and integrin-signalling explanations discussed in the article are not treated as proven mechanisms.

[PMID:24069336](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0074728) provides an independent ACTN1 variant and platelet/cytoskeletal context. Its annotation-level findings are reviewed against the cached article. The native NOT platelet-activation assertion is preserved, but the scope of any normal-response measurement must remain distinct from a claim that normal ACTN1 never participates in activation.

## Postsynaptic anchoring

[PMID:29429936, Matt et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5963734/) has a substantial cached full text. Figure 1 uses recombinant full-length alpha-actinin-1 and domain constructs to demonstrate direct PSD-95 association. The neuronal depletion experiments target alpha-actinin-1, -2 and -4 together. Wild-type alpha-actinin-1 rescue and PSD-95-binding mutants E53K and E213K/D217K connect this interaction to postsynaptic PSD-95/AMPAR retention. Thus the ACTN1-specific evidence is not inferred solely from a pan-alpha-actinin phenotype. The structural role is supported in cultured neurons without claiming unique or exclusive ACTN1 function across all synapses.

## Additional source-resolution checks

- The depositing laboratory's [Addgene 11908 record](https://www.addgene.org/11908/) links PMID:11223950 to human ACTN1, NM_001102, fused to C-terminal eGFP. The study used mouse fibroblasts as host cells. This resolves construct identity without supplying the unread full Methods or every annotated binding experiment.
- [PMID:17944866, full article mirror](https://www.academia.edu/29662306/Direct_binding_of_%CE%B1_actinin_enhances_TRPP3_channel_activity), Figure 8: human ACTN1 and ACTN2 fragments were tested by yeast two-hybrid against TRPP3. The channel-current experiments instead used chicken-gizzard alpha-actinin, so their functional effects are not specifically assigned to human ACTN1. The native cache is abstract-only.
- [PMID:1629252, primary-article scan](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=300843be65197084880cde38f01d7e2811ceee00&repid=rep1&type=pdf): Methods and Results show BM75.2 immunoreactivity and alpha-actinin enrichment in human-neutrophil pseudopods. The inspected reagent checks do not independently discriminate ACTN1 from ACTN4. This leaves the specific source assignment unresolved without establishing a wrong-gene annotation.
- The [Reactome PARVB reaction](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-430308) explicitly includes cytosolic ACTN1. The [platelet release event](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-481007) outputs set R-HSA-481033, whose alpha-actinin subset R-HSA-349778 includes extracellular ACTN1. These are curated model assertions; their inspection does not reanalyse the original peptide tables or resolve the separate alpha-granule-lumen evidence.
