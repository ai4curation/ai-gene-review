CC   -!- FUNCTION: Dual function regulator of gene expression; regulator of
CC       transcription and modulator of alternative splicing (PubMed:30698747).
CC       General coactivator of nuclear receptor-induced gene expression,
CC       including genes activated by the glucocorticoid receptor NR3C1
CC       (PubMed:30698747). Binds to a subset of pre-mRNAs and to components of
CC       the spliceosome machinery to directly modulate basal alternative
CC       splicing; involved in simple and complex cassette exon splicing events
CC       (PubMed:30698747). Binds its own pre-mRNA and regulates its alternative
CC       splicing and degradation; one of the alternatively spliced products is
CC       a stable intronic sequence RNA (sisRNA) that binds the protein to
CC       regulate its ability to affect splicing (PubMed:27899669,
CC       PubMed:36533631). Binding of the sisRNA stimulates phase separation and
CC       localization to nuclear speckles, which may contribute to activation of
CC       nuclear receptor-induced gene expression (PubMed:36533631). May also
CC       indirectly modulate alternative splicing (PubMed:30698747). Regulates
CC       transcription of genes involved in heart development, neuronal cell
CC       function, protein localization and chromatin localization (By
CC       similarity). Regulates splicing of genes involved in neurogenesis and
CC       chromatin organization (By similarity). Essential for central nervous
CC       system development (By similarity). Required for the estrogen-dependent
CC       expression of ESR1 target genes (PubMed:21454576). Can act in
CC       cooperation with MED1 (PubMed:21454576). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3UL36,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21454576, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27899669,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30698747, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36533631}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with MED1; the interaction is direct
CC       (PubMed:21454576). Interacts with PUF60, U2AF2 and JMJD6; may interact
CC       with other proteins involved in RNA processing and splicing
CC       (PubMed:30698747). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21454576,

CC   -!- INDUCTION: Post-transcriptionally regulated by autoregulatory feedback
CC       loop (PubMed:27899669, PubMed:36533631). ARGLU1 protein binds ARGLU1
CC       pre-mRNA and stimulates alternative splicing to produce two alternative
CC       RNA molecules (PubMed:27899669, PubMed:36533631). The first includes an
CC       additional exon between exons 2 and 3 and is rapidly degraded by
CC       nonsense mediated decay (PubMed:27899669, PubMed:36533631). The second,
CC       a stable intronic sequence RNA (sisRNA), retains the entirety of intron
CC       2 and is able to bind ARGLU1 protein preventing it from stimulating
CC       alternative splicing (PubMed:27899669, PubMed:36533631).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27899669, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36533631}.
