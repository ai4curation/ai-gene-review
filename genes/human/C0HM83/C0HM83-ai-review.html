Gene Description

SHMOOSE (Small Human Mitochondrial ORF Over SErine tRNA) is a 58-aa microprotein encoded by a short open reading frame in human mitochondrial DNA. The ORF lies on the heavy-strand-encoded sense at m.12234-12410, overlapping the genes for tRNA-Ser(AGY) (MT-TS2) and tRNA-Leu(CUN) (MT-TL2) and the 5' end of MT-ND5 in a different reading frame. It contains no codons whose meaning differs between the standard and vertebrate mitochondrial genetic codes, so it encodes the same peptide whether translated by mitochondrial or cytosolic ribosomes, and the site of its translation is unknown. The peptide has a predicted N-terminal amphipathic helix, a disordered proline-rich C-terminal half and no recognisable mitochondrial targeting sequence. Endogenous SHMOOSE has been detected in neuronal mitochondrial and nuclear fractions (absent from cells lacking mtDNA), by mass spectrometry of peptides from mitochondria, and in cerebrospinal fluid. Synthetic SHMOOSE binds the MICOS core subunit mitofilin (IMMT/MIC60) and, when added to neuronal cells, increases basal oxygen consumption and protects against oligomeric amyloid-beta toxicity; these effects come from added peptide, and the role of the endogenous peptide has not been tested by loss of function. The m.12372G>A (rs2853499) haplogroup U marker changes Asp47 to Asn (D47N) and was associated with Alzheimer disease risk and brain imaging traits, but this association, which cannot separate the peptide from the overlapping mtDNA sequence and linked haplogroup variants, does not by itself establish what the peptide does.

Core Functions

SHMOOSE is a mitochondrial-DNA-encoded microprotein found in mitochondria, where its only reported partner is the MICOS core subunit mitofilin (IMMT). Its molecular activity is unknown: the binding, respiration and neuroprotection data come from adding synthetic peptide, and no loss-of-function test of the endogenous peptide exists, so no molecular function or process is asserted.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is SHMOOSE translated by mitochondrial or cytosolic ribosomes? Its codons read the same under both genetic codes, and its start codon lies within the tRNA-Ser(AGY) sequence, so translation would seem to require an RNA that escapes normal tRNA punctuation processing of the heavy-strand transcript. Which RNA species carries the intact ORF?

Q: Does endogenous SHMOOSE associate with mitofilin/MICOS, and on which side of the inner membrane? If so, does it change MICOS assembly or crista junction structure, which would support a more specific function term than protein binding?

Q: Is the nuclear signal real (imaging, protease protection, other antibodies), and how would a mitochondrially encoded peptide reach the nucleus?

Q: Is the Alzheimer disease association of m.12372G>A caused by the D47N peptide change rather than by linked haplogroup U variants or effects of the variant on the overlapping RNA?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Ribosome profiling of mitochondrial versus cytosolic ribosomes (or mitoribosome inhibitor sensitivity of endogenous peptide levels) to find where SHMOOSE is translated.

Experiment: Loss of function of the endogenous peptide, e.g. base editing of the start codon or an internal stop in cybrid or mtDNA-edited cells, choosing edits silent for MT-TS2/MT-TL2 and MT-ND5, followed by respirometry and cristae imaging.

Experiment: Cybrid lines that differ only at m.12372 (D47N), to separate the variant from its haplogroup background.

Experiment: Protease-protection and immuno-EM localisation of endogenous SHMOOSE, with co-IP of the endogenous peptide and MICOS subunits (MIC60, MIC19, MIC10).

C0HM83 (SHMOOSE) review notes

Identity

  • UniProt C0HM83 (SHMOS_HUMAN), "Protein SHMOOSE" / "Small human mitochondrial ORF over
    serine tRNA", 58 aa, PE1, encoded on mtDNA (OG Mitochondrion). UniProt gives no gene symbol,
    so the folder and gene_symbol use the accession (alt-ORF convention, projects/MICROPROTEINS.md).
  • Essentially one primary paper: Miller et al. 2023 Mol Psychiatry PMID:36127429, plus an
    erratum [PMID:36658336, abstract only, content of correction not available]. Later PubMed hits
    (search "SHMOOSE", 2026-10-03: 8 records) are reviews or plasma-level association studies,
    e.g. PMID:41883858 (Mediterranean diet vs plasma SHMOOSE/humanin, same USC group). No
    independent replication of the mechanistic findings found.

ORF location and genetic code (bioinformatics, C0HM83-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md)

  • The UniProt sequence maps once on rCRS, + (heavy-strand-encoded) sense, m.12234-12410 (incl. TAA
    stop); overlaps MT-TS2 (tRNA-Ser(AGY)), MT-TL2 (tRNA-Leu(CUN)) and the 5' end of MT-ND5 in a
    different frame. Same coordinates are given in PMID:41883858.
  • No TGA/ATA/AGA/AGG codons, so standard and vertebrate mitochondrial codes give the identical
    58-aa product. Paper agrees: PMID:36127429. So the
    code cannot tell whether it is made by mitoribosomes or cytosolic ribosomes (unlike MOTS-c),
    and the authors leave this open PMID:36127429.
  • My own inference (not tested): the start codon lies inside tRNA-Ser(AGY). Standard tRNA
    punctuation processing of the H-strand polycistron would separate the start from the rest of the
    ORF, so translation would need an unprocessed or alternatively processed RNA. Flag as a question.
  • m.12372G>A (rs2853499) changes codon 47 GAC->AAC (D47N); synonymous for ND5 per the paper
    PMID:36127429.

Existence

  • Endogenous peptide: WB ~6 kDa in mito and nuclear fractions of SH-SY5Y; absent in rho0 cells; two
    peptides by MS after IP from mitochondria [PMID:36127429 "SHMOOSE was detected in neuronal
    mitochondria and nuclei fractions at the predicted ~6kDa molecular weight"; "in rho zero cells
    (i.e., cells without mtDNA), SHMOOSE was not detected"]. Detected in CSF by ELISA.

Variant association (not function)

  • D47N associated with AD in ADNI with modest effect PMID:36127429. rs2853499 is a haplogroup U marker; authors concede
    PMID:36127429. The same base also lies in the overlapping ND5 sequence (synonymous)
    and in the H-strand transcript, so association alone cannot attribute risk to the peptide. Genetic
    association is not a GO function.

Functional experiments (all with exogenous synthetic peptide)

  • Mitofilin (IMMT, Q16891) binding: spiked synthetic peptide into lysate -> IP-MS (98 proteins, IMMT a
    top hit), reciprocal co-IP after treating cells with 1 uM peptide, and dot blot with recombinant
    proteins PMID:36127429. siRNA IMMT abolished peptide effect on superoxide. D47N still binds.
  • Exogenous peptide raised basal OCR ~20% and MTT signal; protected SH-SY5Y against oligomeric Abeta42;
    ICV in rats changed hypothalamic transcriptome. These are pharmacological responses to added peptide,
    not loss-of-function of the endogenous product; no knockout/knockdown of endogenous SHMOOSE exists
    (mtDNA editing would be needed).

GO decisions

  • GO:0005515 protein binding IPI (IMMT) -> REMOVE per project policy (no informative MF; there is no
    "MICOS complex binding" GO term in QuickGO search). Interaction is reported in description.
  • mitochondrion IDA + IEA -> ACCEPT.
  • nucleus IDA + IEA -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (fractionation-only, one study, no function there).
  • No NEW: OCR/neuroprotection data are exogenous-peptide pharmacology; participation test not met.

SHMOOSE (C0HM83) ORF location and genetic-code check

Scripts: locate_orf.py (output locate_orf.out), overlaps.py (output overlaps.out).
Both fetch the human mtDNA reference (rCRS, NC_012920.1) from NCBI at run time.

  • The 58-aa UniProt sequence is found once, on the heavy-strand-encoded (+) sense of rCRS,
    starting at m.12234 (ATG) and ending with a TAA stop at m.12408-12410.
  • Annotated features overlapped (all + strand): MT-TS2 (tRNA-Ser(AGY), 12207-12265),
    MT-TL2 (tRNA-Leu(CUN), 12266-12336) and the 5' end of MT-ND5 (12337-14148), in a reading
    frame different from ND5 (offset reported in overlaps.out).
  • The ORF contains no codon whose meaning differs between the standard (table 1) and
    vertebrate mitochondrial (table 2) codes (no TGA, ATA, AGA or AGG), so it gives the same
    58-aa product whether translated by cytosolic or mitochondrial ribosomes. The genetic code
    therefore does not tell us where SHMOOSE is translated (contrast MOTS-c).
  • Codon 47 occupies m.12372-12374 (GAC = Asp in rCRS); the m.12372G>A (rs2853499) allele
    gives AAC = Asn, i.e. the D47N variant.
