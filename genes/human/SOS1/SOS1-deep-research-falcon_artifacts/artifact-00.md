| Allosteric positive feedback | — | Newly formed RAS–GTP binds REM, favors an open catalytic configuration, recruits/retains SOS1 at the membrane, and promotes further RAS activation. | Produces localized, potentially switch-like or pulsatile RAS activation; opposed by ERK/RSK phosphorylation and receptor internalization. | Strong structural, biochemical, single-molecule, and modeling support. | Autoinhibition and negative feedback | — | Histone/DH/PH elements restrict access to the REM allosteric site; ERK/RSK-dependent phosphorylation of the tail reduces productive GRB2-mediated recruitment. | Maintains low basal activity in the cytosol and limits signaling after pathway activation. | Strong structural evidence for the closed state and cellular evidence for phosphorylation-dependent feedback; individual phosphosite effects can be context-dependent. | Direct pathway position | — | SOS1 is the exchange catalyst between activated receptors/adaptors and RAS–GTP formation; its immediate product is active RAS, not phosphorylated ERK or AKT. | Operates primarily at the cytoplasmic face of the plasma membrane, with signaling behavior shaped by receptor clusters, lipids, RAS density, and endocytosis. | Reconstitution and structural evidence directly support this pathway position.
