CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transmembrane protein predominantly expressed on hepatocytes
CC       that plays a key role in endocytosis of plasma glycoproteins that lack
CC       terminal sialic acid residues. Specifically recognizes terminal
CC       galactose and N-acetylgalactosamine residues, facilitating the
CC       clearance of desialylated glycoproteins from circulation. Plays thereby
CC       a role in a variety of physiological processes, such as removal of
CC       desialylated platelets, elimination of activated lymphocytes and
CC       maintenance of serum glycoprotein homeostasis (PubMed:25485912). Plays
CC       a role in the removal of desialylated platelets by activating a
CC       feedback loop regulating thrombopoietin (TPO) expression via the JAK2-
CC       STAT3 signaling pathway (PubMed:25485912). Contributes also to
CC       recognition and elimination of activated lymphocytes by hepatocytes
CC       that can act as cytotoxic effectors (PubMed:21656538). May also play a
CC       physiological role in the regulatory network of lipid homeostasis (By
CC       similarity). Upon ligand binding, the receptor-ligand complex is
CC       internalized and trafficked to a sorting organelle, where dissociation
CC       occurs. The receptor is then recycled back to the cell surface.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P34927, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12171918,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21656538, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25485912}.
