# ASGR1 (P07306) Curation & Research Notes

## 1. Gene & Protein Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `ASGR1` (aliases: `CLEC4H1`, HL-1, H1)
- **UniProt accession**: `P07306` (*Homo sapiens*, `NCBITaxon:9606`), 291 amino acids
- **Protein identity**: Asialoglycoprotein receptor 1 (major subunit H1 of the hepatic Ashwell-Morell receptor).
- **Isoforms**:
  - **Isoform H1a (`P07306-1`, canonical)**: 291 aa single-pass type II transmembrane protein localized to the basolateral (sinusoidal) plasma membrane of hepatocytes (`[Isoform H1a]: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12171918}; Single-pass type II membrane protein.`, `file:genes/human/ASGR1/ASGR1-uniprot.txt`).
  - **Isoform H1b (`P07306-2`)**: Replaces residues 24–63 spanning the transmembrane signal-anchor domain with a single Asp residue (`24–63 -> D`, `VSP_044480`) due to alternative splicing, producing a soluble secreted protein (`sASGPR`) detected in human serum (`[Isoform H1b]: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20886072}.`, `file:genes/human/ASGR1/ASGR1-uniprot.txt`).

## 2. Domain Architecture & Post-Translational Modifications
- **Topological domains** (`file:genes/human/ASGR1/ASGR1-uniprot.txt`):
  - **Cytoplasmic N-terminal tail (residues 1–40)**: Contains a canonical tyrosine-based clathrin-coated pit sorting/internalization signal `YQDL` at residues 5–8 (`FT   MOTIF           5..8 /note="Endocytosis signal"`), a phosphorylation site at Ser-12 (`Phosphoserine`), and a palmitoylated cysteine at Cys-36 (`S-palmitoyl cysteine`). The cytoplasmic tail of ASGR1 (H1) contains basolateral plasma membrane sorting information (`[PMID:10734069, "We have generated chimeric proteins with the cytoplasmic domain of either the asialoglycoprotein receptor H1 or the transferrin receptor, two basolateral proteins, fused to the transmembrane and exoplasmic segments of aminopeptidase N, an apical protein, and analyzed them in Madin-Darby canine kidney cells."]`).
  - **Transmembrane signal-anchor (residues 41–61)**: Single-pass type II transmembrane helix (`FT   TRANSMEM        41..61 /note="Helical; Signal-anchor for type II membrane protein"`).
  - **Extracellular stalk / neck region (residues 62–160)**: Contains a coiled-coil region (residues 61–123) that mediates oligomerization into homo-oligomers and hetero-oligomers with ASGR2 (`[PMID:29069408, "This data parallels previous reports demonstrating that co-expression of ASGR1 results in ASGR2 incorporation into ASGR1/ASGR2 heterodimers that are then exported from the ER and are ultimately targeted to the plasma membrane"]`).
  - **C-type lectin domain (CTLD, residues 161–278; `PROSITE:PS50041`)**: Extracellular C-terminal carbohydrate-recognition domain (CRD) stabilized by three intrachain disulfide bonds (153–164, 181–275, 252–267) and coordinating three $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ ions (residues 191, 197, 216, 240, 242, 243, 253, 254, 265, 266, 278; `PDB:1DV8`).
- **S-Palmitoylation and Endocytic Trafficking**:
  - Yik et al. (`PMID:12171918`) demonstrated that cytoplasmic palmitoylation of ASGR1 (Cys-36) and ASGR2 (Cys-54, Cys-58) is required for efficient clathrin-dependent endocytosis, ligand-receptor dissociation in early/sorting endosomes, and delivery of desialylated glycoprotein ligands to lysosomes (`[PMID:12171918, "We conclude that palmitoylation of the ASGP-R is required for its efficient endocytosis of ligand by the clathrin-dependent endocytic pathway and, in particular, for the proper dissociation and delivery of ligand to lysosomes."]`).

## 3. Carbohydrate Specificity (QPD vs. EPN Motif) & Oligomeric Function
- **QPD Galactose/GalNAc Specificity Motif**:
  - At the primary $\text{Ca}^{2+}$-binding site (site 2) of the C-type lectin domain, ASGR1 carries the tripeptide motif **QPD** (`Gln240-Pro241-Asp242` in sequence `...NWRPEQPDDWYGHGLGGGEDCAHFTDDGRWNDDVCQRPYRWVCETELDKASQEPPLL`), which hydrogen-bonds to the equatorial 3-OH and axial 4-OH configuration of terminal **D-galactose (Gal)** and **N-acetyl-D-galactosamine (GalNAc)** residues (`[file:genes/human/ASGR1/ASGR1-uniprot.txt, "Transmembrane protein predominantly expressed on hepatocytes that plays a key role in endocytosis of plasma glycoproteins that lack terminal sialic acid residues. Specifically recognizes terminal galactose and N-acetylgalactosamine residues, facilitating the clearance of desialylated glycoproteins from circulation."]`).
  - By contrast, mannose/fucose-specific C-type lectins carry an **EPN** (`Glu-Pro-Asn`) motif at the corresponding position, which coordinates the equatorial 3-OH and 4-OH of D-mannose, L-fucose, and N-acetylglucosamine.
- **Receptor Assembly and Cargo Uptake**:
  - The native human hepatic asialoglycoprotein receptor is a hetero-oligomer of major subunit ASGR1 (H1) and minor subunit ASGR2 (H2), though ASGR1 can also assemble into functional homo-oligomers on the hepatocyte surface (`[PMID:29069408, "This data parallels previous reports demonstrating that co-expression of ASGR1 results in ASGR2 incorporation into ASGR1/ASGR2 heterodimers that are then exported from the ER and are ultimately targeted to the plasma membrane"]`).
  - Tanowitz et al. (`PMID:29069408`) showed that ASGR1 mediates receptor-mediated endocytosis of GalNAc-conjugated antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) in hepatocytes and that ASGR1 homo-oligomers are sufficient for GalNAc-ASO uptake whereas ASGR2 alone is retained intracellularly and rapidly degraded (`[PMID:29069408, "Collectively, this implies that ASGR2 behaves similarly in MHT cells as in other heterologous cell lines in which its primary translational product is degraded, and therefore its expression is non-contributory to ASO uptake, and further that ASGR1 homooligomers are sufficient for GalNAc-mediated ASO uptake."]`).

## 4. Phylogenetic Context & IBA Propagation Analysis
- **PANTHER Classification**:
  - In the PANTHER classification (`interpro/panther/PTHR22803/PTHR22803-entries.csv`), `ASGR1` (`P07306`) and `ASGR2` (`P07307`) are placed in the broad C-type lectin receptor family `PANTHER:PTHR22803` (`MANNOSE, PHOSPHOLIPASE, LECTIN RECEPTOR RELATED`).
  - Because `PTHR22803` encompasses functionally divergent C-type lectin subfamilies—including EPN-motif mannose/fucose-binding myeloid pattern-recognition receptors (MRC1, CD209/DC-SIGN, CD207/Langerin, CLEC4E/Mincle, CLEC4D/MCL, CLEC6A/Dectin-2), prolectin (CLEC17A), low-affinity IgE Fc receptor (FCER2/CD23), lithostathine/REG proteins, and snake venom snaclecs—PAINT ancestral node annotations in `PTHR22803` cause severe over-propagation onto `ASGR1` and `ASGR2`:
  1. **`PANTHER:PTN004568004` (`GO:0005537` D-mannose binding; `GO:0042806` fucose binding)**:
     - Seeded by human `CLEC17A` (`UniProtKB:Q6ZS10`, Prolectin), which binds terminal mannose and fucose. Propagating `D-mannose binding` and `fucose binding` to `ASGR1` and `ASGR2` is biologically incorrect (`PROPAGATION_BAD` / `FUNCTIONAL_DIVERGENCE`, `OVER_PROPAGATED`), as ASGR1 and ASGR2 possess the canonical galactose-specific QPD motif (Gln240-Pro241-Asp242 in ASGR1) and selectively bind terminal D-galactose and GalNAc rather than D-mannose or L-fucose.
  2. **`PANTHER:PTN001105054` (`GO:0038187` pattern recognition receptor activity; `GO:0006955` immune response)**:
     - Seeded by human and mouse myeloid C-type lectin pattern-recognition receptors (`UniProtKB:Q9ULY5` *CLEC4E*/Mincle, `MGI:MGI:1298389` *Clec4e*, `UniProtKB:Q8WXI8` *CLEC4D*/MCL, `UniProtKB:Q6EIG7` *CLEC6A*/Dectin-2, `MGI:MGI:1861231` *Cd209b*, `MGI:MGI:1861232` *Cd209d*), human *FCER2* (`UniProtKB:P06734`), *Drosophila* lectins (`FB:FBgn0016675`, `FB:FBgn0053532`, `FB:FBgn0053533`), and zebrafish `ZFIN:ZDB-GENE-061207-22`.
     - Propagating `pattern recognition receptor activity` and `immune response` to `ASGR1` and `ASGR2` is an over-propagation across functionally diverged paralogs (`PROPAGATION_BAD` / `FUNCTIONAL_DIVERGENCE`, `OVER_PROPAGATED`). ASGR1 and ASGR2 are hepatocyte-specific house-keeping endocytic scavenger receptors that clear endogenous desialylated plasma glycoproteins (including vWF) and aged desialylated platelets (activating hepatic thrombopoietin expression via JAK2-STAT3 signaling, `PubMed:25485912` in `file:genes/human/ASGR1/ASGR1-uniprot.txt`) from circulation (`PMID:8439566`, `PMID:29069408`); this homeostatic glycoprotein and platelet turnover is not an innate or adaptive `immune response` (`GO:0006955`) or pathogen-associated pattern-recognition activity.
     - Note that `ASGR1` also carries a direct `IDA` annotation to `GO:0038187` (`pattern recognition receptor activity`) from `PMID:29069408`. Inspection of the full text of `PMID:29069408` shows that the study examined hepatocyte uptake of synthetic GalNAc-conjugated and unconjugated phosphorothioate antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) via ASGR1 and never assayed pathogen recognition or innate immune PRR activity. Both the `IDA` and `IBA` rows for `GO:0038187` must therefore be set to `REMOVE` (with a `WRONG_TERM` `source_defects` entry on the `PMID:29069408` row).
  3. **`PANTHER:PTN001105053` (`GO:0009897` external side of plasma membrane)**:
     - Seeded by `UniProtKB:Q9NNX6` (human *CD209*), `MGI:MGI:1916415` (*Clec10a*), `MGI:MGI:3588267` (*Cd209e*), and `MGI:MGI:95497` (*Fcer2a*). Because ASGR1 (and ASGR2) are type II single-pass plasma membrane proteins whose C-terminal C-type lectin carbohydrate-recognition domains project into the extracellular space on the external face of the hepatocyte plasma membrane, this IBA annotation (`is_active_in`) is accurate and represents a core localization (`ACCEPT`, `NO_FAILURE_CORE`).
