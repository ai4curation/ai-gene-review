# ATF5 notes

Deep research: not run (falcon times out after 600 s in this environment and perplexity-lite is not installed). Review based on cached publications, UniProt, Reactome and PubMed lookups.

- Mammalian UPRmt transcription factor, functional counterpart of C. elegans ATFS-1 [PMID:27426517 "ATF5 expression can rescue UPR(mt) signaling in atfs-1-deficient worms requiring the same UPR(mt) promoter element identified in C. elegans."]
- Required for stress-induced HSP60/mtHSP70/LONP1 transcription [PMID:27426517 "Impressively, transcription of all four genes was significantly reduced when ATF5 was impaired by two different shRNAs"]
- Reactome curates ATF5 + HSF1 at HSPD1/HSPE1 and HSPA9 promoters [Reactome:R-HSA-9857071].
- Other targets: BCL2 [PMID:21212266], CHOP via AARE1 [PMID:20654631]; C/EBPB cofactor in adipogenesis [PMID:24216764]; centrosomal PCM ring [PMID:26213385].
- GOA lacked GO:0034514; added as NEW (IMP, PMID:27426517). Comparator: worm ATFS-1 carries GO:0034514.
- bZIP partner IPIs (CEBPA, CEBPG) modified to protein heterodimerization activity; other protein binding removed.
