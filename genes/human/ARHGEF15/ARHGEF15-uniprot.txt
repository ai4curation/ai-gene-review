CC   -!- FUNCTION: Guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) that activates RhoA,
CC       playing a role in the regulation of actin cytoskeleton organization
CC       (PubMed:36929019). Is involved in the regulation of vascular smooth
CC       muscle contractility (PubMed:12775584). Additionally, it negatively
CC       regulates excitatory synapse development by suppressing the synapse-
CC       promoting activity of EPHB2 (By similarity). Does not activate RAC1 or
CC       CDC42 (PubMed:36929019). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q5FWH6,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12775584, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36929019}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with EPHB2 (By similarity). Interacts with EPHA4
CC       (PubMed:12775584). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q5FWH6,

CC   -!- DISEASE: Brain small vessel disease 5 with osteoporosis (BSVD5)

CC       cerebral infarctions associated with brain imaging abnormalities such
