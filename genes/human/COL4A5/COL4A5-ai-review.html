Gene Description

COL4A5 encodes the alpha-5 chain of type-IV collagen, a secreted component of basement-membrane networks. Alpha-5 participates in alpha3/alpha4/alpha5 and alpha5/alpha5/alpha6 collagen protomers, whose terminal-domain interactions support matrix architecture in tissues including the kidney, skin and intestine. Pathogenic COL4A5 variants cause X-linked Alport syndrome. Isolated recombinant human NC1 and a synthetic pentastatin peptide bind integrin in experimental assays and perturb pulmonary endothelial barrier function. The identity, physiological production and endogenous roles of naturally released bioactive fragments remain incompletely resolved; these isolated-fragment experiments do not establish or exclude the same activity in assembled collagen.
