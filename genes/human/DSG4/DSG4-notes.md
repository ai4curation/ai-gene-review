## What DSG4 is

Desmoglein-4 is the fourth desmosomal cadherin, a single-pass type I membrane
glycoprotein with calcium-binding extracellular cadherin domains and a cytoplasmic tail
that binds plakoglobin (JUP). Unlike DSG1 and DSG3, its expression is concentrated in
the hair follicle — inner root sheath, matrix and precortex — with lower expression in
other epithelia.
[file:human/DSG4/DSG4-uniprot.txt "In scalp follicles, present in the inner root sheath (IRS) and all layers of the matrix and precortex (at protein level)"]

## Evidence

Compound heterozygous DSG4 mutations cause autosomal recessive monilethrix; the W823X
mutant loses affinity for plakoglobin, implicating DSG4-plakoglobin coupling in
desmosome integrity in the hair shaft.
[PMID:21495994 "we show that the mutant DSG4 protein with the mutation p.W823X severely affects the affinity to plakoglobin protein, which may contribute to disruption of desmosomes in the patient's hair shaft"]
UniProt records the interaction and the colocalization.
[file:human/DSG4/DSG4-uniprot.txt "Note=Colocalizes with JUP at desmosomes."]

Localization conclusion: plasma membrane and desmosome, cadherin ectodomain
extracellular. The cornified-envelope rows derive from Reactome cornification pathway
membership and an ARBA rule, not from a DSG4 localization experiment, and DSG4 protein
is documented in the follicle rather than in the cornified layer.

Lymphocyte-migration rows come from rodent orthologs (rat Q6W3B0, mouse Q7TMD7) and are
recorded by UniProt only "By similarity"; they are kept as non-core.
