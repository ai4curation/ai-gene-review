CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor specific to ghrelin, an appetite-
CC       regulating peptide hormone commonly found in stomach (PubMed:35027551,
CC       PubMed:39833471). Upon activation, stimulates appetite and promotes
CC       growth hormone secretion (PubMed:10604470, PubMed:11322507,
CC       PubMed:35027551, PubMed:39833471). Ghrelin binding causes a
CC       conformation change that triggers signaling via guanine nucleotide-
CC       binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the activity of downstream
CC       effectors, such as phospholipase C (By similarity). GHSR is coupled to
CC       G(q) G proteins and mediates production of diacylglycerol (DAG) and
CC       inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate (IP3) (By similarity). Also binds other
CC       growth hormone releasing peptides (GHRP) (e.g. Met-enkephalin and GHRP-
CC       6) as well as non-peptide, low molecular weight secretagogues (e.g. L-
CC       692, 429, MK-0677, adenosine) (PubMed:10604470, PubMed:11322507). Also
CC       plays a role in synaptic plasticity independently of ghrelin by forming
CC       a heteromer with the dopamine receptors DRD1 and DRD2 in neurons (By
CC       similarity). Following DRD1 activation by dopamine in hippocampal
CC       neurons, GHSR acts as a coreceptor, resulting in DRD1 coupling to G(q)
CC       at the expense of G(s), initiating hippocampal synaptic plasticity
CC       involved in memory and learning (By similarity). Following DRD2
CC       activation by dopamine in lumbosacral defecation centers, GHSR acts as
CC       a coreceptor, resulting in DRD1 coupling to G(q) at the expense of
CC       G(i)/G(o), regulating defecation (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O08725, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q99P50,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10604470, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11322507,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35027551, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39833471}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by octanoylated ghrelin; octanoylation
CC       is essential for full activation of the receptor (PubMed:35027551).
CC       Stimulated by anamorelin, which binds to the same binding sites as
CC       ghrelin (PubMed:39833471). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:35027551,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39833471}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the heterotrimeric G protein complex composed
CC       of 3 units, alpha, beta and gamma; this complex may consist of GNB1 and
CC       GNG2 (PubMed:35027551, PubMed:39833471). Interacts with DRD1; forms a
CC       heteromer with DRD1 in hippocampal neurons (By similarity). Interacts
CC       with DRD2; forms a heteromer with DRD2 in neurons (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q99P50, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35027551,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39833471}.
