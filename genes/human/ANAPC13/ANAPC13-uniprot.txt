CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome
CC       (APC/C), a cell cycle-regulated E3 ubiquitin ligase that controls
CC       progression through the cell cycle (PubMed:15060174, PubMed:18485873,
CC       PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C acts by mediating
CC       ubiquitination and subsequent degradation of target proteins: it mainly
CC       mediates the formation of 'Lys-11'-linked polyubiquitin chains and, to
CC       a lower extent, the formation of 'Lys-48'- and 'Lys-63'-linked
CC       polyubiquitin chains (PubMed:15060174, PubMed:18485873). APC/C
CC       catalyzes assembly of branched 'Lys-11'-/'Lys-48'-linked branched
CC       ubiquitin chains on target proteins (PubMed:29033132). APC/C is
CC       activated by CDC20 in the metaphase/anaphase transition of cell cycle,
CC       targeting the degradation of cyclin B and securin (PubMed:27120157,
CC       PubMed:27509861). APC/C is regulated by the mitotic checkpoint complex
CC       (MCC), which inhibits APC/C and delays chromosome segregation
CC       (PubMed:27509861). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15060174,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18485873, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27120157,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27509861, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29033132}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15060174, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18485873,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29033132}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C),
CC       composed of ANAPC1, ANAPC2, CDC27/ANAPC3, ANAPC4, ANAPC5, CDC16/ANAPC6,
CC       ANAPC7, CDC23/ANAPC8, ANAPC10, ANAPC11, CDC26/ANAPC12, ANAPC13, ANAPC15
CC       and ANAPC16 (PubMed:16364912, PubMed:25043029, PubMed:26083744,
CC       PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C associates with CDC20 to form
CC       the CDC20-APC/C complex, which is crucial for metaphase/anaphase
CC       transition in cell cycle; this interaction is dependent on APC/C
CC       phosphorylation (PubMed:27120157, PubMed:27509861). APC/C associates
CC       with mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC), which inhibits APC/C and delays
CC       chromosome segregation (PubMed:27509861). APC/C interacts with FZR1 and
CC       FBXO5 (PubMed:25043029, PubMed:26083744). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16364912,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25043029, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26083744,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27120157, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27509861}.
