# MSRB3 notes

- UniProt Q8IXL7 is the reviewed human MSRB3 protein in PTHR10173:SF56. The
  displayed isoform 1 is the ER MsrB3A form; isoform 2 substitutes a different
  N-terminus and is the mitochondrial MsrB3B form.
- PMID:14699060 directly assayed purified human MSRB3 forms on dabsyl-Met-R-SO,
  detected zinc in MsrB3, localized MsrB3A to the ER through its N-terminal signal
  plus KAEL retention motif, and localized MsrB3B to mitochondria through its
  alternative N-terminal signal.
- PMID:21185009 identified autosomal-recessive DFNB74 families with MSRB3
  loss-of-function alleles. Cys89Gly abolishes zinc binding and reductase activity,
  while Arg19X specifically disrupts the mitochondrial isoform.
- PMID:15914630 supports oxidative-stress protection as a cellular outcome for
  MsrB1/MSRB2/MSRB3 in human lens cells. I kept the InterPro oxidative-stress row
  as non-core because the direct MSRB3 mechanism is Met-R-SO repair.
- PMID:25416956 is a proteome-scale human binary-interactome screen. The four
  IntAct GO:0005515 rows preserve their individual WITH/FROM interactors but are
  removed under the protein-binding policy.
- Reactome R-HSA-5676917 and R-HSA-5676934 correctly include MSRB3 in
  methionine-sulfoxide protein repair but use a cytosol event location and a
  low-efficiency free-Met-R-SO activity that should not be core MSRB3 assertions.
