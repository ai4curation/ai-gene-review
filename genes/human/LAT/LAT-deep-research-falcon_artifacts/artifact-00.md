| Pathway | Key LAT-interacting proteins | Mechanism of activation | Functional outcome in T cells |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NFAT** | PLCγ1; GADS–SLP-76; ITK | ZAP70-dependent LAT phosphorylation creates the LAT–GADS–SLP-76–PLCγ1 signalosome. Activated PLCγ1 hydrolyzes PIP2 to IP3 and DAG; IP3 triggers ER Ca²⁺ release and store-operated Ca²⁺ entry, activating calcineurin, which dephosphorylates NFAT and permits its nuclear entry. (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000025, pqac-00000031) | NFAT cooperates with other transcription factors to induce IL-2 and broader activation programs. LAT deficiency disrupts PLCγ1 activation, Ca²⁺ mobilization, NFAT activation, and IL-2 transcription despite preserved receptor-proximal ZAP70 activation. (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000036) |
| **AP-1 / RAS–MAPK** | GRB2; SOS1; PLCγ1–RasGRP1; GADS–SLP-76–VAV1 | Phosphorylated LAT recruits GRB2–SOS1, promoting RAS activation and the RAF–MEK–ERK cascade. PLCγ1-derived DAG also activates RasGRP1, while LAT-associated VAV1 can support RAC–JNK signaling; ERK/JNK outputs activate FOS/JUN AP-1 complexes. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000026, pqac-00000031) | Drives activation-associated chromatin and transcriptional programs, cytokine expression, proliferation, differentiation, and effector function. A 2024 mutational screen found that defective LAT variants usually impaired AP-1 and NFAT together, indicating coordinated rather than independently modular pathway control. (pqac-00000028, pqac-00000035) |
| **NF-κB** | PLCγ1; GADS–SLP-76; PKCθ-associated machinery | LAT-dependent recruitment and activation of PLCγ1 generates DAG, which activates PKCθ and downstream IKK signaling, leading to NF-κB activation. LAT supplies the proximal scaffold rather than catalyzing the lipid reaction itself. (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000031) | Supports transcription of activation, survival, inflammatory-cytokine, and effector genes; together with NFAT and AP-1, it contributes to productive T-cell activation rather than anergy. (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000031) |
| **Ca²⁺ signaling** | PLCγ1; GADS–SLP-76; ITK | PLCγ1 binds LAT principally at phospho-Y132 and is stabilized/activated within the LAT signalosome. PLCγ1 converts PIP2 into IP3 and DAG; IP3 opens ER IP3 receptors, and ER-store depletion promotes sustained STIM–ORAI-mediated Ca²⁺ entry. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000031) | Produces rapid and sustained cytosolic Ca²⁺ signals needed for calcineurin–NFAT activation, IL-2 expression, proliferation, migration, cytokine production, and effector responses. LAT-deficient cells retain proximal receptor phosphorylation but lose normal Ca²⁺ mobilization. (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000025) |


*Table: This table maps LAT signalosome components to the NFAT, AP-1/MAPK, NF-κB, and calcium branches of T-cell receptor signaling. It highlights LAT’s role as a nonenzymatic scaffold that coordinates otherwise distinct downstream outputs.*
