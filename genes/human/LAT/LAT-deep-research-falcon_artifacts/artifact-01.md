| Feature | Human LAT annotation | Functional significance | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | **Linker for activation of T-cells family member 1**; gene symbol **LAT**; UniProt **O43561**; also called pp36 or p36–38 | Confirms the target as human LAT, a T-cell-receptor-associated transmembrane adaptor—not another gene sharing a similar symbol | LAT is consistently described as the 36–38-kDa transmembrane signaling adaptor downstream of ZAP70 (pqac-00000030, pqac-00000047) |
| Protein size | Approximately **233 amino acids**; apparent molecular mass **36–38 kDa** | The high apparent mass relative to sequence length is characteristic of this heavily modified, largely disordered adaptor | Sequence-wide mutational studies extended through residue 233; authoritative reviews identify LAT as a 36–38-kDa protein (pqac-00000045, pqac-00000040) |
| Overall architecture | Short extracellular N terminus, one N-terminal transmembrane segment, and a long tyrosine-rich cytoplasmic tail | Provides membrane anchoring while exposing a large intracellular signaling platform | Structural and functional analyses describe a compact N-terminal membrane region followed by an extensive cytoplasmic signaling region (pqac-00000040, pqac-00000041) |
| Transmembrane region | Single N-terminal transmembrane helix; residues **L11–G12–L13** are among experimentally interrogated membrane-region residues | Membrane integration positions LAT near activated TCR–LCK–ZAP70 complexes | Recent sequence–function mapping and AlphaFold analysis identified the membrane region as critical for surface trafficking and activation (pqac-00000041, pqac-00000052) |
| Palmitoylation sites | **C26 and C29** are S-palmitoylated membrane-proximal cysteines; C26 has the stronger demonstrated requirement | Promotes membrane-microdomain distribution and efficient LAT tyrosine phosphorylation; C26 mutation can abolish raft localization, whereas C29 mutation has a partial effect | Supported by biochemical literature and recent structural/sequence synthesis (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000042) |
| Intrinsically disordered region | Most of the cytoplasmic tail is **intrinsically disordered**, with distributed short linear motifs and conserved charge patterning rather than a globular catalytic domain | Enables multivalent, phosphorylation-dependent assembly of the LAT signalosome; functional regions span **more than 40%** of the sequence | A 2024 pooled single-cell mutational map identified broad functional regions, interaction motifs, and important negative-charge distribution (pqac-00000043, pqac-00000044) |
| Y132 | ZAP70-phosphorylated **Y132** is the principal direct docking site for **PLCG1/PLCγ1** | Couples TCR signaling to PIP2 hydrolysis, IP3/Ca²⁺–NFAT signaling, and DAG-dependent pathways | Interaction mapping and AlphaFold-Multimer analysis support selective PLCG1 binding at pY132 (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000021) |
| Y110, Y171, Y191 and Y226 | Phosphorylated sites can recruit **GRB2-family adaptors**—GRB2, GADS and/or GRAP—with overlapping specificity; Y110 and Y171 contribute to GRB2 binding, while Y171 and Y191 are especially important for GADS engagement | Connect LAT to SOS1–RAS signaling and, through GADS, to SLP-76; multivalent binding also promotes LAT clustering | Recent interaction modeling and proximity-labeling show permissive, interdependent binding across these sites rather than four fully independent modules (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) |
| S224 and S241 | Candidate DNA-PKcs-regulated phosphoserines within conserved S/Q motifs; **S224 and S241** were reported as DNA-PKcs substrates | Reported to regulate LAT tyrosine phosphorylation and signalosome composition; S241A disrupted recruitment of PLCγ1, GADS, SLP-76 and SOS1 and impaired IL-2 production | Evidence is from a **2025 bioRxiv preprint** and should be treated as provisional pending peer review (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000036) |
| Plasma membrane microdomains | LAT is an integral plasma-membrane protein enriched in cholesterol- and sphingolipid-associated signaling microdomains | This is a principal site of early LAT phosphorylation and signalosome assembly following TCR engagement | Palmitoylation-dependent membrane positioning is tightly linked to LAT phosphorylation and function (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000013) |
| Immunological synapse | Activated LAT accumulates in microclusters at the T-cell–antigen-presenting-cell interface | Spatially concentrates PLCγ1, GRB2, GADS–SLP-76 and other effectors, supporting signal propagation and persistence | Imaging and trafficking studies identify the immune synapse as LAT’s major activation-associated functional destination (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000013) |
| Intracellular vesicles | A substantial—and in some studies more abundant—LAT pool resides in intracellular vesicles | Serves as a trafficking reservoir delivered later to the synapse, helping sustain signaling after rapid activation of surface LAT | Vesicular LAT is recruited after plasma-membrane LAT and participates in temporally regulated synapse signaling (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012) |
| Golgi and trans-Golgi network | Endocytosed LAT undergoes retrograde transport to the Golgi/TGN and subsequent anterograde delivery toward the immune synapse | The Golgi functions as a polarized trafficking hub rather than the exclusive site of LAT signaling | Retrograde transport involves **Rab6 and syntaxin-16**; anterograde transport involves **VAMP7 and GMAP210** (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000015) |
