## Functional role and its mechanistic limit

Patient dermal fibroblasts in the 2019 study retain normal ciliation frequency, but their cilia are longer and bulbous, with IFT88/IFT46 tip accumulation and relatively preserved IFT122 distribution. The independent 2022 human hTERT-RPE1 knockout/rescue study corroborates elongated cilia and IFT88/ICK tip accumulation. Full-length FAM149B1-EGFP rescues length and IFT88 distribution. The engineered 1–179 fragment improves the IFT88 endpoint without correcting length. Neither 1–179 nor 180–582 restores normal length; intact full-length protein is required in the tested rescue assay. These results separate the measured outputs without defining the behavior of the natural 528-residue product.

[Cilium assembly, GO:0060271](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0060271), is retained for the imported experimental and phylogenetic contexts. A normal fraction of ciliated cells does not exclude defective assembly or extension of the structure. The same normal-frequency observation is already recorded in UniProt VAR_083866, alongside altered assembly and protein localization. Generalizing to its organization parent solely on that observation would discard supported specificity. Current CILK1 and IFT88 assembly annotations and their committed reviews provide consistent comparison, without transferring their established kinase or transport-machinery activities to FAM149B1. CDC14A's regulatory assignment depends on a distinct phosphatase mechanism; a comparable FAM149B1 mechanism is not demonstrated, so no regulation term is substituted.

[Protein localization to cilium, GO:0061512](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0061512), expressly includes maintenance at a location within the cilium and fits the protein-distribution defect and rescue without requiring defective initial entry. Actual current `is_a` and combined `is_a`/`part_of` ancestor responses show that neither assembly nor protein localization is an ancestor of the other. They represent distinct endpoints of one supported ciliary role; the assembly/organization parent-child pair is not duplicated in the core.

The 2019 tagged HEK293 co-immunoprecipitation and 2022 tagged HEK293T association/domain mapping support FAM149B1 association with BROMI and CCRK. They do not establish a purified binary interface or native stoichiometry. Adaptor and cargo-adaptor definitions were considered in the direct lane, but bridging, motor-cargo coupling and kinase regulation were not demonstrated. A cyclin-like CCRK role and regulation of IFT turnaround remain mechanistic hypotheses. Static tip enrichment is not a direct measurement of trafficking rate. The core therefore represents the accepted process role while leaving molecular function, location and complex unasserted. Its biological knowledge gap concerns the molecular step, not an alleged lack of ciliary biology or merely an absent ontology label.

The [campaign instructions](../../../projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions) authorize retention of supported generic binding as non-core when no specific replacement is established, and prohibit new publication quotation anchors. The supported binding review remains exact; reference-only citations and precise source locators supply the evidence trail.
