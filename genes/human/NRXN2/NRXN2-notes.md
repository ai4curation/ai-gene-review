# NRXN2 (human, Q9P2S2) curation notes

## 2026-09-30 — initial review

### Identity and scope
- Q9P2S2 is the canonical neurexin-2-alpha entry (1712 aa; signal peptide 1-28, six LNS/laminin G-like domains with three EGF repeats, TM helix 1637-1657, cytoplasmic tail 1658-1712). The beta isoforms from the internal promoter are a separate UniProt entry, P58401 (its GOA rows are in `NRXN2-bioinformatics/NRXN2-beta-isoform-entry-goa.tsv`, reference only, not reviewed).
- UniProt Ca2+-binding residues by similarity in LNS2 (335, 352, 420), LNS4 (779, 796, 854), LNS6 (1189, 1206, 1288, 1290); heparan sulfate at Ser1400 [file:human/NRXN2/NRXN2-uniprot.txt "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Presynaptic cell membrane"].
- Human GOA (38 rows) is almost entirely IBA/ISS/IEA/TAS; the only human experimental rows are IMP behaviour annotations from one family (PMID:21424692). Most ISS rows come from mouse Nrxn2 (E9Q7X7), whose sources are BHF-UCL IDA/IPI from PMID:21424692 and MGI IGI from PMID:12827191 (checked in QuickGO).
- The Falcon deep-research report was used for retrieval only. It presents the "anti-synaptogenic" role as settled and does not mention the conflicting data or retractions (below).

### Core biology (with provenance)
- Neurexin-2-alpha binds NLGN2 and LRRTM2 and clusters postsynaptic components; ASD truncation removes LNS6 and abolishes both [PMID:21424692 "Whereas wild-type neurexin2α-CFP induced robust clustering of glutamatergic components YFP-LRRTM2 and PSD-95, and GABAergic components NLGN2 and gephyrin at dendrite contact sites"] [PMID:21424692 "unlike the high affinity binding of wild-type neurexin2α-CFP, the delA mutant expressed in COS cells was unable to bind either LRRTM2-AP or NLGN2-Fc"]
- IgSF21 is an NRXN2-alpha-selective ligand (LNS1), recruiting axonal NRXN2-alpha in trans to drive inhibitory presynaptic differentiation [PMID:28864826 "Interestingly, IgSF21-Fc interacted only with NRX2α and not with any other NRX isoforms"] [PMID:28864826 "Together, these data indicate that IgSF21 binds and recruits axonal NRX2α in a trans-interaction manner and that this interaction is essential for the synaptogenic activity of IgSF21."] The same paper frames NRXN2-alpha as both receptor and ligand [PMID:28864826 "NRX2α acts as a receptor that mediates presynaptic organization signals induced by postsynaptic organizers such as NLGs19, 22, calsyntenin-335 and IgSF21 but also acts as a ligand that induces postsynaptic organization"]
- Adhesion: neurexin-2-beta cells aggregate with latrophilin-1 cells [PMID:22262843 "Strikingly, we found that cells expressing either of these two neurexins formed aggregates with CL1-expressing cells, again dependent on a lack of an insert in SS4 in the case of neurexin-2β"]; neurexin-2-beta Ca2+ site needed for neuroligin-1 adhesion [PMID:20064387 "was blocked by a single amino-acid substitution in neurexin-2β that abolishes Ca2+-binding to the neurexin LNS domain, and that also abolishes neuroligin-1 mediated cell adhesion"]
- Dystroglycan via LNS2 [PMID:11470830 "The second LNS domain of all three α-neurexins (neurexin 1α, 2α, and 3α) formed tight Ca2+-dependent complexes with dystroglycan, suggesting that all α-neurexins bind to dystroglycan"]
- Overexpressed neurexin-2-beta suppresses GABAergic transmission [PMID:20471353 "Thus, contrary to expectations, increasing neuronal neurexin-2β expression decreased the function of GABAergic but not glutamatergic synapses, without altering synapse numbers."]
- Alpha-neurexins collectively required for release, not synapse formation [PMID:12827191 "Using triple-knockout mice, we show that alpha-neurexins are not required for synapse formation, but are essential for Ca2+-triggered neurotransmitter release."] [PMID:17347997 "Therefore, alpha-neurexins are not essential for the formation of the vast majority of synapses in vivo but rather regulate the function of these synapses."] But Nrxn2-alpha alone is dispensable in secretory cells [PMID:17035546 "both WT and SKO2 genotypes showed virtually identical results in all of the parameters that were examined and are referred to collectively as controls"]

### NRXN2-specific in vivo phenotypes — and the conflict
- Neocortex (Missler lab): [PMID:25745399 "Using patch-clamp recordings in Nrxn2α knockout brains, we observe reduced spontaneous transmitter release at excitatory synapses in the neocortex."] [PMID:25745399 "Inhibitory transmission as well as synapse densities and ultrastructure remain unchanged in the neocortex of both models."]
- Hippocampus (Sudhof lab): [PMID:36608123 "Unexpectedly, we find that in the hippocampus, the loss of Nrxn2 causes an increase both in the number of excitatory synapses and in the presynaptic release probability, suggesting a function in restricting synapse assembly."] [PMID:36608123 "Parallel measurements of inhibitory postsynaptic currents (IPSCs) did not uncover any change in the synaptic strength or the presynaptic release probability of inhibitory synapses"]
- Lin 2023 explicitly disputes the Born 2015 mouse line ("the mouse line used in this paper was generated originally in our laboratory but found to not represent an Nrxn2 deletion"). The Nrxn2-alpha line (Missler 2003) was also used by Dachtler.
- Integrity status (checked in PubMed, 2026-09-30):
  - PMID:36608123 (Lin 2023 Sci Adv): 2025 expression of concern; in March 2026 the EoC was removed and an erratum published (PMID:41824587), editors stating "the authors' original results are maintained". Cited, but reference marked DISPUTED.
  - PMID:36961922 (Lin et al. 2023 PNAS, "Neurexin-2 restricts synapse numbers and restrains the presynaptic release probability by an alternative splicing-dependent mechanism"): RETRACTED (PMID:38442183). Not fetched, not cited.
  - PMID:28472659 (Chen et al. 2017): retracted; not cited (as for NRXN1).
- Behaviour: [PMID:25423136 "We report that Nrxn2α KO mice displayed deficits in sociability and social memory when exposed to novel conspecifics."] [PMID:36941261 "conditional deletion of Nrxn2 from the hippocampus and cortex (Nrxn2 cKO) results in behavioral abnormalities, including reduced social preference and increased nestlet shredding behavior"] [PMID:36941261 "we observed unprovoked spontaneous reoccurring electrographic and behavioral seizures in Nrxn2 cKO mice"] [PMID:38702381 "Emx1-Nrxn2 cKO mice exhibited reversal learning deficits, indicating their inability to adopt new action strategies."] [PMID:26595880 "Nrxn2α HET mice also express a lack of long-term object discrimination, indicating a deficit in cognition."]
- Human: one ASD boy with heterozygous NRXN2 frameshift, inherited from a father with language delay [PMID:21424692 "This mutation was inherited from the father who had a severe language delay (did not speak until 5 years of age)."]

### Decisions (38 GOA rows + 2 NEW)
- ACCEPT (12): plasma membrane (IBA + 4 Reactome TAS), presynaptic membrane (IEA, ISS), presynaptic active zone membrane IBA, trans-synaptic protein complex IBA, neuroligin family protein binding (IBA, ISS), cell adhesion molecule binding ISS.
- MODIFY (4): neuron cell-cell adhesion (IEA, TAS) -> GO:0099560 synaptic membrane adhesion; signal transduction (IBA, ISS) -> GO:0099545 trans-synaptic signaling by trans-synaptic complex.
- MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED (5): calcium channel regulator activity ISS; vocal learning (IEA, IMP) and vocalization behavior (IEA, IMP).
- KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (17): transmembrane signaling receptor activity (IBA, ISS); chemical synaptic transmission; neurotransmitter secretion; nervous system development; synapse assembly; learning; adult behavior x3; social behavior x2; postsynaptic membrane assembly; gephyrin clustering x2; neuroligin clustering; PSD-95 clustering.
- NEW (2): GO:0098632 cell-cell adhesion mediator activity (ISS, PMID:22262843); GO:0099171 presynaptic modulation of chemical synaptic transmission (ISS, PMID:36608123). Comparator for 0099171: SynGO has mouse Nrxn2 (PMID:36608123) and mouse Nrxn3 (PMID:26030848) on this term; human NRXN1/NRXN3 do not carry it.

### Where this diverges from NRXN1
- GO:0007269 neurotransmitter secretion: ACCEPT (core) for NRXN1, KEEP_AS_NON_CORE here. The IGI source is combined alpha-neurexin deletion; NRXN2 alone is dispensable in secretory cells and its release phenotype changes sign between neocortex and hippocampus. The direction-neutral GO:0099171 (NEW) is used as the core process instead.
- Third core function: NRXN1 used PDZ domain binding (GO:0030165) + neurotransmitter secretion. NRXN2 has no protein-binding rows and no cached NRXN2-specific CASK/Mint evidence, so no PDZ MF is asserted; the core function is modulation of release/synapse number with no MF.
- Synapse assembly kept non-core as for NRXN1, but the reason differs: NRXN2 both promotes (coculture, IgSF21) and restricts (hippocampus) synapse assembly.
- Social behaviour kept non-core as for NRXN1, but mouse support is much stronger for NRXN2 (Nrxn1-alpha KOs are socially normal; Nrxn2-alpha KOs are not).
- Core trans-synaptic organizer function adds the NRXN2-alpha-selective ligand IgSF21.
