Gene Description

CEBPA encodes C/EBPalpha, a basic leucine-zipper transcription factor that recognizes cis-regulatory DNA as a homodimer or heterodimer and coordinates differentiation with proliferative restraint. Its N-terminal regulatory regions and interacting transcription factors shape promoter-specific activation and repression. C/EBPalpha drives granulocytic and adipocyte gene programs and contributes to differentiated functions in liver and lung. The p42 and N-terminally truncated p30 products share the C-terminal DNA-binding and dimerization region but differ in regulatory potency; an N-terminally extended product is associated with nucleolar rDNA regulation. C/EBPalpha also associates with cell-cycle regulatory proteins, linking transcriptional control to cell-cycle arrest in a product- and phosphorylation-dependent manner. Disruptive CEBPA variants impair myeloid differentiation and predispose to acute myeloid leukemia.

Core Functions

Promoter-complex interactions, including E2F repression, couple this program to proliferative restraint. Promoter partners and the N-terminal product context determine output; p30 is not assumed to match p42 potency.
