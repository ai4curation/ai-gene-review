# FANCM evidence and interpretation guide

FANCM combines ATP-driven remodeling of branched nucleic acids with recruitment of DNA-repair machinery to chromatin. These activities are complementary. MHF-assisted fork remodeling and FANCM:FAAP24 hybrid remodeling were tested in different protein preparations; a result from one preparation is not automatically assigned to the other. The three annotated natural products are also distinct from engineered fragments and patient alleles.

The review retains historical quotations and findings for provenance. Some older conclusions in the notes and reference histories are superseded by the dated reassessment: RNA-containing substrates are positively supported, normal RAD51 foci do not exclude downstream SDSA participation, and chromatin enrichment in tested cells does not exclude all other locations. New judgments appear in annotation reviews and the current biological summary.

Four-way-junction binding and ATP-dependent junction processing remain accepted. The actual [GO:0009378 definition](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0009378) is compatible with the observed branched substrate output; it does not require a claim of unrestricted linear-duplex unwinding. [GO:0045003](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0045003) names noncrossover SDSA, whereas [GO:0035825](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0035825) describes equal-material exchange. This does not establish biological disjointness: reciprocal recombination is a narrow synonym and crossover a related synonym, so GO:0035825 is not restated as crossover-only. Positive mechanistic evidence motivates the SDSA refinement.

## Database context, interactions and natural products

[UniProt Q8IYD8](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8IYD8/entry) supplies the exact source protein and natural-product records. The 669-residue product has a substitution at residue 669 and lacks residues 670–2048. The 2022-residue product lacks residues 228–253 in the motor region. Engineered FANCM 1–754 and the patient p.Gln1701* product are different entities. Neither a shared gene symbol nor an overlapping antibody epitope establishes equivalent activity.

[HPA FANCM subcellular evidence](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000187790-FANCM/subcellular) assigns supported nucleoplasm localization using HPA055144. Actual A-431 and U-2 OS image fields were inspected; PC-3 was assessed from metadata only. The antigen maps to displayed residues 1802–1886, absent from the 669-residue product, and does not distinguish the two longer products.

All 13 cited Reactome events were traced to FANCM's own compartment-bearing entity rather than assigned the enclosing complex's compartment by assumption. Twelve nuclear events include Q8IYD8 residues 1–2048 in nucleoplasm. The [cytosolic PKR-regulation event R-HSA-9835411](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9835411) reaches the cytosolic FANCM:FAAP24 entity. That contextual location is retained as non-core. The event's FANCC/FA-pathway perturbation evidence does not demonstrate isolated FANCM inhibition of PKR. Traces record representative routes to each reached entity, not every possible duplicate graph route.

## Functional refinements from the complete PR review

The structured core now includes [R-loop processing, GO:0062176](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0062176), supported by the existing human FANCM:FAAP24 hybrid-flap and telomeric R-loop experiments [PMID:26593718](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26593718/), Figure 6 and Biochemical Analysis; [PMID:31138795](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31138795/), Figure 7b and In vitro R-loop resolution assays. A reference-only IC/NEW proposal records that process. The enzyme performs the disassembly step; the claim is not based solely on a knockout phenotype. Hybrid binding is not added as a separate molecular function from these catalytic assays.

[Chromatin-protein adaptor activity, GO:0140463](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0140463) expresses the established FA core recruitment role more precisely than chromatin binding alone. The seven pairwise association contexts retain their individual decisions: MHF and FAAP24 aid FANCM DNA engagement, whereas the recruitment core concerns FA core cargo. The description restores supported family, phosphorylation, ovarian-expression and ovarian-insufficiency biology; natural-product functional equivalence remains an experimental question.

Shared Reactome access scope, consolidated from twelve repeated reference notes: the prior traversal records representative routes rather than every duplicate route; unresolved off-target branches remain in the evidence packet. Cited primary papers were not all independently read. These model records are not substituted for original experiments. Event-specific judgments and the distinct cytosolic event caveat remain in each reference review.
