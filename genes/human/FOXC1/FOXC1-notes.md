## Review follow-up: biological synthesis and source boundaries

Human WT reporter and endogenous promoter experiments support nuclear FOXC1 transcriptional activation, including regulation of FOXO1 in ocular cells ([PMID:11179011](../../../publications/PMID_11179011.md), [PMID:17993506](../../../publications/PMID_17993506.md)). The original domain-mapping study also demonstrates a transferable inhibitory region. Its residues 215–366 attenuate activation-domain-driven reporter output but did not reduce the tested constitutive SV40 baseline without an activation domain. This is positive evidence for modular attenuation; it does not by itself establish a separate full-length DNA-binding repressor mechanism ([PMID:11782474](../../../publications/PMID_11782474.md), original Results/Figure 5E and Discussion linked in the source guide).

Interaction assays have distinct scopes. The NFIA-associated signal in the original nuclear-extract/probe screen supports refinement to RNA polymerase II-specific DNA-binding transcription-factor binding because NFIA's class is established. It does not inherit PBX1's separate co-immunoprecipitation or inhibitory result, and NFIA did not impair FOXC1 activity in the tested cells ([PMID:15684392](../../../publications/PMID_15684392.md)). The GLI2 cancer experiments support increased DNA-binding activity in that setting; they do not replay the separate mouse core-promoter-binding donor assay ([PMID:26565916](../../../publications/PMID_26565916.md), [PMID:25808752](../../../publications/PMID_25808752.md)).

Developmental effects retain their tissue context. FOXC1 variants cause anterior segment dysgenesis and increased glaucoma susceptibility. Mouse corneal stromal experiments establish corneal avascularity and transparency, which is distinct from ocular lens transparency ([PMID:11782474](../../../publications/PMID_11782474.md), [PMID:22171010](../../../publications/PMID_22171010.md)). Three structured, unreported source queries now identify the two human lens transfers and the exact mouse donor assertion. No replacement is guessed. A niche cell can regulate another cell’s differentiation; non-cell-autonomy alone does not establish over-annotation. The unresolved issue is the exact differentiation endpoint in the inaccessible assays ([PMID:24590069](../../../publications/PMID_24590069.md)).

The revised questions and experiments address biological mechanisms. Source-access follow-up remains in the evidence map and here: the original FOXC1 assay attributed to PMID:11237714; the P297S letter PMID:19793056; the PMID:14506133 bending experiment; the exact PMID:25609649 FOXC1–PBX1 pair; the mouse PMID:28223138 negative-transcription assay; the PMID:24590069 differentiation assays; any lens-specific support beyond PMID:22171010; and the precise core-promoter assay in PMID:25808752.
