## 2026-11-04 — Biological synthesis and review clarification

GATA6 binds regulatory GATA elements and can activate or repress transcription. Human binding/reporter studies support recognition and activation, whereas endogenous fibroblast ChIP, site mutation and TNC repression establish a repressive context ([PMID:19666519](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19666519/); [PMID:18177748](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18177748/)). Endogenous adrenal complexes contain both natural initiation products and MED1; the tested coactivator response does not establish a universal activity ranking ([PMID:19497978](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19497978/)).

Human pancreatic agenesis genetics and loss of DNA binding/reporter activation in tested missense proteins support dosage-sensitive developmental regulation ([PMID:22158542](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22158542/)). This informs the biological description and conserved activity links. No new pancreas-specific GO assertion is made without a separate process/ontology assessment; absence from GOA alone neither proves nor disproves a curation gap.

Specialized lineage and stimulus outputs remain distinct from the conserved transcriptional mechanism. Endothelial survival effects depend on growth-factor conditions ([PMID:21127043](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21127043/)). Skin programming includes separable keratinocyte and sebocyte contexts ([PMID:33082341](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33082341/)). Mouse sinoatrial lineage regulation supports pacemaker development, without establishing GATA6 as a contractile effector ([PMID:33443158](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33443158/)). The source guide contains the experimental distinctions and the reasons for non-core or over-annotated process decisions.

Human intestinal differentiation remains uncertain: the proposed transition role is compatible with the observations, but its human causal basis is unresolved ([PMID:9566909](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9566909/)). UNDECIDED records that claim-specific uncertainty; it is not restricted to unavailable publications. Independent nuclear and molecular evidence can resolve those different claims.
