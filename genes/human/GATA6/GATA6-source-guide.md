# GATA6 source guide

This guide makes the evidence behind the annotation reasons portable. The review contains 92 original contexts, one core synthesis, two natural initiation products and no NEW annotation. Full original records and actual native joins are retained in [the evidence archive](GATA6-evidence.yaml). Source assessments distinguish the material actually read from uninspected figures, supplements and constructs.

## Shared interpretation

A reference-only `supported_by` link refers to the same source assessment and scope recorded here; it does not imply an additional experiment. The main activity is sequence-directed PolII regulation. Binding, occupancy, transcriptional output and downstream cellular effects are distinguished. Existing experimental attributions are preserved when an inaccessible panel limits independent verification. A title focused on another regulator is not evidence of misattribution.

## Selected original evidence

### PMID:10608869

[10608869 original record](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.274.53.38004). Original Methods/Results and Fig. 6; no image inspection. Comparable-protein EMSA controls are reported as data not shown.

### PMID:18177748

[18177748 original record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbagrm.2007.11.012). Human fibroblast TNC RNA/protein, reporter, GATA-site mutation, DNA-affinity and endogenous ChIP evidence agree. Transfected coding provenance is not inferred from recipient species.

### PMID:19497978

[19497978 original record](https://doi.org/10.1210/en.2009-0006). H295R coIP includes both endogenous forms. M147L is engineered; steroid-output knockdown targets MED1, not GATA6. ChIP and promoter tests support transcription.

### PMID:19666519

[19666519 original record](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0904744106). Human EMSA/HeLa reporters separate N466H binding from activation. E486del remains intranuclear. Mouse enhancer transgenes test sites, not GATA6 deletion; detailed SI was inaccessible.

### PMID:21127043

[21127043 original record](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M110.176925). Human endothelial perturbation and mouse rescue are distinct. Growth-factor occupancy differs from hypoxia. TGFB1/2 RNA effects, mouse-promoter/rat-cell testing and conditional survival remain separate.

### PMID:33082341

[33082341 original record](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-18784-z). Human SebE6E7, follicle explants and organoids supply complementary contexts. NM_005257.5 is stated without modern-product alignment; the results do not establish embryonic or uniformly promaturation effects.

### PMID:9566909

[9566909 original record](https://doi.org/10.1128/MCB.18.5.2901). Human expression/nuclear observations and Xenopus reporters address different claims. The proposed transition role remains unresolved; transcript decline is not functional exclusion.

### PMID:16199874

[16199874 original record](https://doi.org/10.1128/MCB.25.20.8960-8970.2005). Fig. 5D tests GATA6 with Hey1/2/L. Recipient HEK293T cells do not establish coding species. Reporter repression is distinct from purified affinity or natural-product exclusivity.

### PMID:11889139

[11889139 original record](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.200106057). Selected original Methods/Results distinguish human GATA6/aortic cells, murine NFATc1 and rat promoter. Deletion reagents are engineered; no coronary anatomy is inferred.

### PMID:33443158

[33443158 original record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7817143/). Mouse lineage deletions establish SAN programming; TBX3 site-dependent reporters, ChIP and nuclear-extract EMSA support regulatory work. Exact construct and ChIP-cell details need the unavailable supplement.

### PMID:25068583

[25068583 original record](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0103097). Methods and Fig. 3: fetal-heart cells, not an hESC model. RNA-UTR targeting and the miR10a differentiation arm remain distinct.

### PMID:28473536

[28473536 original record](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaj2239). Original S1 row 298: 143-residue eDBD, matching long residues 367–509 and short 221–363. S2 rows 8278/8283: methylated/unmethylated selection models. S3 row 692: no bisulfite data.

## Review clarifications

The two PMID:33082341 excerpts now distinguish keratinocyte epidermal programming from sebocyte differentiation; the latter does not imply increased terminal lipid output. The seven excerpts total at most 15 words from any one source, including the two excerpts from that paper.

The core retains nucleoplasm because the exact human GATA6 participant is present in the curated SFTPA promoter-binding event [R-HSA-5685296](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5685296), consistent with the HPA IDA and other nuclear evidence. The HPA images were not independently inspected. Nucleus is the enclosing compartment, not a competing localization.

Both UniProt initiation products remain in `alternative_products`. The current `functional_isoforms.type` enum has no alternative-translation-initiation value; neither product is relabeled as a splice variant, cleavage product or modification state. Their existing functional descriptions retain the context-dependent comparison.

The evidence archive uses repository-relative paths for current normal sources and stable checksums plus artifact names for historical private snapshots. Those snapshots are not bundled as public files; their hashes preserve identity without claiming that a local workstation path is a portable link. Primary source URLs and experimental locators remain in this guide.
