# ENTPD1 annotation review notes

The annotation checkpoint evaluates 70 imported source contexts, retaining their order, identifiers, evidence, source references, qualifiers and supporting entities. Seventy-one raw QuickGO records become 70 normal rows because two exosome records collapse when the normal TSV omits their differing raw annotation ids and one cell-type extension. Both raw records remain preserved in the acquisition evidence; no annotation or product is silently deleted.

The review has 53 ACCEPT, eight MODIFY, three KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, one REMOVE and five UNDECIDED decisions. Broad membrane rows are refined to plasma membrane; generic hydrolase is refined to apyrase; coagulation/platelet-aggregation rows are refined to negative regulation of platelet aggregation on the independent endothelial ADP-hydrolysis experiment. NDP/NTP class activities are retained because the human substrate panel establishes breadth. A single-substrate experiment does not require narrowing the whole-gene class.

Supported RanBPM binding is retained as non-core without assigning CD39 an unsupported adapter function. Caveolar enrichment is also non-core. The remaining uncertainties concern cell adhesion, CDP-specific activity, two narrow UDP donor/Rhea chains, and the unrecovered ENTPD1 entry in the exosome supplementary table. The separate PAINT UDP assertion is accepted on actual ancestral placement; no human UDP assay is claimed.

[The original-evidence guide](ENTPD1-reference-guide.md) provides public URLs and precise read limits. Independent originals resolve source-only access gaps where they establish the same biological claim. An ACCEPT or MODIFY does not mean every cited original was fully available. Six explicit reviewer full-text-unavailable flags describe actual access limits; recovered originals are not mislabeled from the normal fetcher's cache flags.

Two unreported source defects are recorded: the incorrect DOI in UniProt's PMID8626624 reference block, and a request to check mouse basement-membrane localization. The latter follows actual Methods/Results/captions and an independent peer review. It does not imply wrong orthology, gene-name confusion, a universal absence of matrix association or a new NTPDase2 annotation. Original source fields and caches remain unchanged.

The six natural products are preserved separately. Native placental protein preparations, 517/306-residue placental cDNAs, and synthetic human/rat deletion constructs are distinct. Sequence reconstruction matches each independent FASTA; loss or retention of a segment is not alone a functional assay.

The normal source set contains sixteen accepted publication caches plus three genuine donor-paper additions. The genuine Falcon/perplexity-lite research invocation failed during bootstrap; no provider report was fabricated. Curated interpretation uses the inspected originals and database provenance instead. No new supporting-text quotations are added.

The completed review includes one independently synthesized apyrase core, connecting successive ATP/ADP hydrolysis and inorganic phosphate release to cell-surface nucleotide catabolism and a contribution to inhibition of platelet aggregation. AMP-to-adenosine conversion belongs to CD73. PMID:8529670, PMID:8996251 and PMID:10636909 provide the directly inspected core evidence; PMID:8955160 remains abstract-only. The six products are not presumed to share every catalytic or localization property. The final normal validation and preservation receipts are kept separately; DRAFT status reflects remaining advisories and is not a statement that any imported row is unreviewed. Publication and campaign credit require separate verification.

The [reproducible product analysis](ENTPD1-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md) includes exact input snapshots, a standard-library reconstruction script and independent human/rat FASTA comparisons. Its unchanged-residue projections do not predict activity or localization.
