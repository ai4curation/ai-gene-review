The review has 53 ACCEPT, eight MODIFY, three KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, one REMOVE and five UNDECIDED decisions. Independent originals resolve source-only access gaps where they establish the same biological claim. An ACCEPT or MODIFY does not mean every cited original was fully available. The latter follows actual Methods/Results/captions and an independent peer review. It does not imply wrong orthology, gene-name confusion, a universal absence of matrix association or a new NTPDase2 annotation. Original source fields and caches remain unchanged.
