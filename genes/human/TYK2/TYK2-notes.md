# TYK2 notes

Deep research providers unavailable in this environment (falcon/perplexity-lite failed); research done
from cached publications and the UniProt record.

- Non-receptor PTK; no TM domain [PMID:2216457 "Hydrophobicity analysis of our sequence does not identify a
  transmembrane domain"].
- Binds and phosphorylates IFNAR1 [PMID:7526154 "the p135tyk2 tyrosine kinase directly binds and tyrosine
  phosphorylates this receptor subunit"]; structure of FERM-SH2 with IFNAR1 box2 [PMID:24704786].
- Phosphorylates STAT1 Tyr701 [PMID:7657660]; catalytic activity required for STAT3/IFNAR1 phosphorylation
  [PMID:10542297].
- Pathways: type I IFN [PMID:1386289], IL-10 [PMID:7543512], IL-12/IL-23 [PMID:7528775].
- Unverified: IFN-gamma (PMID:8232552 abstract assigns it to JAK1/JAK2), IL-5 (PMID:7888666, PMID:1495999
  abstracts mention only JAK2/IL5RA) -> UNDECIDED.

## Localization conclusion
Intracellular: peripheral protein on the cytoplasmic side of the plasma membrane, docked on cytokine
receptor ICDs [PMID:24704786 "constitutively associate with membrane proximal peptide motifs found on the
intracellular side of cytokine receptors"], plus a cytosolic pool. Not exposed at the cell surface.
Generic plasma membrane / membrane terms modified to GO:0031234.
