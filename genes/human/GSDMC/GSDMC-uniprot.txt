CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Gasdermin-C]: This form constitutes the precursor of the
CC       pore-forming protein: upon cleavage, the released N-terminal moiety
CC       (Gasdermin-C, N-terminal) binds to membranes and forms pores,
CC       triggering pyroptosis. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32929201,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34012073}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Gasdermin-C, N-terminal]: Pore-forming protein that causes
CC       membrane permeabilization and pyroptosis (PubMed:27281216,
CC       PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073). Produced by the cleavage of
CC       gasdermin-C by caspase CASP8 in response to death signals
CC       (PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073). After cleavage, moves to the plasma
CC       membrane where it strongly binds to membrane inner leaflet lipids
CC       (PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073). Homooligomerizes within the
CC       membrane and forms pores of 10-15 nanometers (nm) of inner diameter,
CC       triggering pyroptosis (PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27281216, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32929201,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34012073}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: [Gasdermin-C]: The full-length protein before
CC       cleavage is inactive: intramolecular interactions between N- and C-
CC       terminal domains mediate autoinhibition in the absence of activation
CC       signal (PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073). The intrinsic pyroptosis-
CC       inducing activity is carried by the released N-terminal moiety
CC       (Gasdermin-C, N-terminal) following cleavage by caspase CASP8
CC       (PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32929201,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34012073}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: [Gasdermin-C, N-terminal]: Homooligomer; homooligomeric ring-
CC       shaped pore complex containing 27-28 subunits when inserted in the
CC       membrane. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q5Y4Y6}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: [Gasdermin-C]: Cytoplasm, cytosol
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11223543}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: [Gasdermin-C, N-terminal]: Cell membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32929201}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q5Y4Y6}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed mainly in trachea and spleen
CC       (PubMed:11223543). In the esophagus, expressed in differentiating cells
CC       and probably in differentiated cells. Also detected in gastric
CC       epithelium (PubMed:19051310). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11223543,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19051310}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression in induced by STAT3 and CD274/PD-L1 in response
CC       to hypoxia. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32929201}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Intramolecular interactions between N- and C-terminal domains
CC       are important for autoinhibition in the absence of activation signal
CC       (By similarity). The intrinsic pyroptosis-inducing activity is carried
CC       by the N-terminal domain (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q5Y4Y6}.
CC   -!- PTM: Cleavage by CASP8 relieves autoinhibition by releasing the N-
CC       terminal moiety (Gasdermin-C, N-terminal) that initiates pyroptosis
CC       (PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073). The cleavage site is unclear
CC       (PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073). According to a publication, it
CC       takes place after Asp-240 in response to alpha-ketoglutarate
CC       (PubMed:34012073). Another paper reports cleavage by CASP8 after Asp-
CC       365 (PubMed:32929201). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32929201,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34012073}.
CC   -!- PTM: Palmitoylated. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38599239}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the gasdermin family. {ECO:0000305}.
