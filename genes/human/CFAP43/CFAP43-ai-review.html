Gene Description

CFAP43 is a conserved axonemal protein with an N-terminal WD-repeat region and an extended C-terminal coiled-coil region. Together with CFAP44 it forms the tether/tether-head complex associated with inner dynein arm f and the doublet microtubule surface. Its structural integration links axonemal organization to coordinated ciliary beating. Human respiratory axoneme structures identify CFAP43 in this machinery, while human infertility and mouse loss-of-function studies establish a requirement for normal sperm flagellar architecture and motility. Respiratory cilia can remain assembled while their beat frequency and fluid transport are altered. The relative requirements for this complex vary across tissues and organisms, and the functional differences among the five human alternative products remain unresolved.

Core Functions

CFAP43 supplies part of the tether/tether-head structure with CFAP44 along axonemal doublet microtubules in motile cilia and sperm flagella. Native human respiratory-axoneme models identify the canonical-sequence protein in conserved inner-dynein-arm docking machinery. In Tetrahymena, removing FAP43 destabilizes the position of retained I1 motor domains; mouse respiratory cilia can remain grossly assembled while Cfap43 loss changes beat frequency and transport. These complementary structural and functional observations support a conserved mechanical contribution to coordinated beating, including frequency modulation, rather than an intrinsic motor or kinase activity. The precise frequency-setting mechanism has not been isolated in human. Sperm axoneme organization and sperm movement are tissue manifestations of this structural role; severe sperm architecture defects do not imply universal failure of cilium initiation.

CFAP43 is an axonemal structural protein. The native human respiratory-axoneme model in PMID:37258679(https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06140-2), human structure section and model-building Methods, identifies the conserved tether/tether-head apparatus. Official PDBe 8J07 entity 81 maps chains i6/i8 to Q8NDM7, residues 1–1665. This connects experimentally constrained structure to the human target. It does not mean every residue was resolved or every alternative product was tested. The canonical-sequence model is distinguished from the five preserved UniProt products.

The detailed algal Results show FAP43 integrated with FAP44 in a structural assembly. PMID:29514928(https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.E18-02-0142), Figure 1 and cryo-ET Results, independently removes the tether/tether-head in Tetrahymena fap43 mutants while I1 dynein remains visible. PMID:29687140(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29687140/), native-tag and waveform Results, retains cilia number/length but changes waveform in Tetrahymena.
