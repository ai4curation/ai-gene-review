# CFAP43 research notes

## 2026-10-10 — substantive annotation review

CFAP43 is an axonemal structural protein. The native human respiratory-axoneme model in [PMID:37258679](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06140-2), human structure section and model-building Methods, identifies the conserved tether/tether-head apparatus. Official PDBe 8J07 entity 81 maps chains i6/i8 to Q8NDM7, residues 1–1665. This connects experimentally constrained structure to the human target. It does not mean every residue was resolved or every alternative product was tested. The canonical-sequence model is distinguished from the five preserved UniProt products.

The detailed algal Results show FAP43 integrated with FAP44 in a structural assembly. [PMID:29514928](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.E18-02-0142), Figure 1 and cryo-ET Results, independently removes the tether/tether-head in Tetrahymena fap43 mutants while I1 dynein remains visible. Algal FAP244 redundancy explains why many Chlamydomonas fap43 repeats retain this structure. [PMID:29687140](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29687140/), native-tag and waveform Results, retains cilia number/length but changes waveform in Tetrahymena. Neither organism is a direct human construct assay. The detailed prestroke map in [PMID:39743588](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08337-5) is bovine sperm; its CFAP43/CFAP44 helices participate in the inner-arm docking network. Dynein motor properties are not CFAP43 enzymatic activities.

These results support one NEW molecular function, GO:0005200 structural constituent of cytoskeleton, and one integrated structural core. All 13 original assertions are retained. Existing sperm axoneme assembly includes organization as well as initiation: [PMID:29449551](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-02792-7), Figures 2 and 4–5, demonstrates human and mouse sperm architectural defects; the mouse figure describes the axoneme as “fully disorganized”. Respiratory cilia need not fail to form. [PMID:31884020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ydbio.2019.12.010), selected primary Results, tracheal imaging Methods and Movie S4/S5 legends, instead separates altered mouse beat frequency from reduced fluid transport. The indexed primary text was accessible, although a subsequent direct publisher opening returned 403 and the normal cache remains abstract-only. No complete PDF or every figure was read.
