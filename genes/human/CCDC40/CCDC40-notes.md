# CCDC40 manual research notes

CCDC40 contributes structure to the motile-cilium axoneme. The most informative synthesis is one CCDC39–CCDC40 structural unit that organizes the 96-nm repeat and the recruitment or retention of inner dynein-arm and regulatory components. It is not a dynein ATPase, a demonstrated active cargo transporter, or an extracellular protein merely because cilia move extracellular fluid. Human structural evidence supports refining the existing unknown molecular-function row to structural constituent of cytoskeleton, GO:0005200. No NEW annotation is proposed.

## Primary mechanisms and species boundaries

The original [CCDC40 study](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.727), PMID:21131974, combines human patient genetics and respiratory-cell phenotypes with mouse and zebrafish experiments. The complete cached scientific prose and Methods were read. Human mutant cilia show defective inner-arm/regulatory organization and ineffective motion; outer-arm markers are retained. Direct Ccdc40 antibody localization to tracheal cilia and apical cytoplasm is mouse evidence, supported by the lnks mutant control. The human localization experiment in Figure 5 detects CCDC39 recruitment, not CCDC40 itself. Zebrafish depletion/rescue supports heart and visceral laterality; this is not a human embryo or human cardiomyocyte assay. The paper's earlier structural-scaffold hypothesis is now reinforced by direct human structural data. Its alternative preassembly/transport suggestions do not independently establish motor activity. No figure pixels, movies or complete supplements were inspected.

[PMID:37258679](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37258679/) provides the critical direct human structural evidence. Selected complete human Results, captions and Methods were read, alongside the complete abstract. Patient ID03 has a homozygous CCDC40 exon 1–3 deletion; ID04 is the separate CCDC39 mutant. Patient-derived epithelial/organoid axonemes lose the 96-nm repeat, including its coiled coil, while outer-arm docking and the 24-nm repeat remain. Radial spokes can enter the axoneme but fail to attach periodically. This supports a structural contribution to organization rather than merely an indirect requirement for healthy cilia. The human models integrate cryo-EM density, structure prediction and algal structural guidance; not every contact is a directly demonstrated human interface. The earlier algal FAP59/FAP172 findings are contextual, not substituted for the human CCDC40 experiment. Complete figure pixels and supplements were not inspected.

[PMID:23255504](https://doi.org/10.1002/humu.22261) was read at selected complete human Results, relevant Methods and localization passages. CCDC40-mutant cross-sections lose inner arms while retaining outer arms. The report gives CCDC40-specific frequency measurements, not just altered waveforms. It also reports sperm dysmotility in one CCDC40 patient but does not display those data. That supports retaining the existing sperm-motility assertion as non-core with its presentation limit. CCDC39 and radial-spoke staining in CCDC40-mutant cells must not be relabeled as direct CCDC40 antibody localization. Preserved radial-spoke staining does not establish normal periodic attachment or function.

[PMID:26387594](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26387594/) centers on GAS8 but includes an actual Ccdc40 lnks mouse tracheal experiment showing loss of GAS8 localization, as well as discussion of the earlier human CCDC40 findings.
