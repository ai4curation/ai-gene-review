| Pan et al., 2023 | High RBPJ was associated with PD-1⁺, TIM-3⁺, granzyme-B-low exhausted CD8⁺ T cells in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). RBPJ knockdown or inhibition of the RBPJ transcriptional complex with RIN1 reduced exhaustion markers and improved antitumor activity. (pqac-00000049, pqac-00000050) | Tumor-infiltrating CD8⁺ T cells and HCC cells; patient datasets, mouse models, and cell-based experiments | Supports RBPJ-complex inhibition as a candidate strategy to reduce immune exhaustion and enhance anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapy; RIN1 also suppressed tumor-cell PD-L1, L-kynurenine production, and mTOR signaling. Evidence remains preclinical. | RBPJ-associated transcription promotes inhibitory-receptor expression and an exhausted T-cell state; RIN1 disrupts this program, while RBPJ knockdown lowers PD-1 and TIM-3. (pqac-00000049, pqac-00000050) |
| Zhang et al., 2023 | Ubc9-dependent SUMOylation of RBPJ at K110 and K195 stabilized the protein, enhanced its transcriptional activity, and increased CIITA and MHC class II expression in dendritic cells. (pqac-00000065, pqac-00000067) | LPS-activated dendritic cells and a mouse DSS-induced colitis model | Identifies the Ubc9–RBPJ–CIITA axis as a potential inflammatory-disease target. Dendritic-cell Ubc9 deficiency impaired antigen presentation and CD4⁺ T-cell priming and reduced colitis severity. | SUMOylation protects RBPJ from ubiquitin-mediated proteasomal degradation; stabilized RBPJ binds the *CIITA* promoter and promotes the MHC-II antigen-presentation program. (pqac-00000065, pqac-00000066) |
| Ng et al., 2023 | Jagged1 was identified as a direct RBPJ target that feeds back to suppress TNF-driven inflammatory osteoclastogenesis. Recombinant Jagged1 inhibited human inflammatory osteoclast formation without broadly suppressing TNF-induced inflammatory genes. (pqac-00000059, pqac-00000061) | Macrophages and osteoclast precursors exposed to TNF; mouse and human cultures, with comparison of rheumatoid arthritis and systemic lupus erythematosus samples | Suggests that restoring Jagged1 signaling could limit inflammatory bone loss, including rheumatoid-arthritis-associated erosion, while potentially preserving broader immune responses. | TNF induces RBPJ-dependent *JAG1* transcription; Jagged1 then establishes negative feedback that restrains TNF-mediated—but not RANKL-mediated—osteoclast differentiation. (pqac-00000059, pqac-00000061) |
| Xia et al., 2024 | RBPJ amplified TNF-driven macrophage inflammation by suppressing homeostatic TGFβ signaling. RBPJ loss reduced TNF-induced cytokines and interferon-stimulated genes and lowered circulating inflammatory mediators in mice. (pqac-00000062) | Macrophage inflammatory responses and a TNF-induced chronic-inflammation mouse model | Positions RBPJ-mediated repression of TGFβ signaling as a possible intervention point in TNF-dependent inflammatory disease; therapeutic translation has not yet been established. | RBPJ binds the *TGFBR1* and *TGFBR2* promoters and represses receptor expression, weakening TGFβ-mediated restraint of TNF responses; RBPJ may also directly regulate inflammatory genes. (pqac-00000062) |
| Chen et al., 2024 | RBPJ mRNA was lower in PBMCs from 83 newly diagnosed rheumatoid arthritis patients than in 70 healthy controls and correlated inversely with DAS28, CRP, and ESR. Synovial analysis identified 562 genes differing between RBPJ-low and RBPJ-high groups. (pqac-00000057, pqac-00000058) | Human rheumatoid arthritis PBMCs and the GSE89408 synovial-tissue transcriptomic dataset | Suggests RBPJ as a candidate disease-activity biomarker and mechanistic link to immune dysregulation and bone destruction, but the observational design does not establish causality or clinical utility. | RBPJ-associated genes were enriched in T-cell-receptor signaling, Th1/Th2 and Th17 differentiation, PI3K–AKT and cytokine signaling, lymphocyte differentiation, and osteoclastogenesis. (pqac-00000057, pqac-00000058) |
