CC       PubMed:27234298, PubMed:28965847, PubMed:35831023). When smoothened
CC       signaling is initiated, GLI transcription factors dissociate from SUFU,
CC       translocate to the nucleus and activate expression of target genes
CC       (PubMed:24311597, PubMed:28965847, PubMed:35831023). In absence of
CC       smoothened signaling, the SUFU-GLI3 complex is recruited to cilia,
CC       leading to the efficient processing of full-length GLI3 into
CC       transcription repressor GLI3R: SUFU participates to GLI3 processing by
