Gene Description

GPT encodes cytosolic alanine aminotransferase ALT1, a homodimeric pyridoxal 5'-phosphate-dependent enzyme. It catalyzes reversible transamination of L-alanine and 2-oxoglutarate to pyruvate and L-glutamate, linking alanine turnover with glucose and amino-acid metabolism. Human liver protein has been purified and characterized, and ALT1 is detected in tissues including liver and kidney. Alanine breakdown supplies pyruvate for further metabolism, while reverse flux can produce alanine. ALT1 can also be detected in plasma and extracellular vesicle preparations; these observations do not establish secretion as its defining physiological function. The related mitochondrial enzyme is encoded by GPT2.

It directly executes alanine turnover; net flux is context dependent. Native human liver ALT1 is a homodimer; extracellular detection is not its defining metabolic role.

2026-10-25 identity, chemistry and compartment

Normally seeded human P24298 with alias GPT. All 12 original assertions are reviewed, preserving every machine-sourced field; no NEW rows. GPT/ALT1 is distinct from mitochondrial GPT2/ALT2. Current UniProt membership is PTHR11751:SF308; this identity is not inferred merely from a plausible family name.

Primary study PMID:1931970 purifies native human liver cytosolic ALT and determines sequence and assembly: an active enzyme mass near 114 kDa is consistent with a homodimer of approximately 55-kDa subunits. Primary study PMID:19360321 directly compares human ALT1/ALT2 activities toward alanine/pyruvate and distinguishes their kinetic parameters. The core reaction is reversible alanine + 2-oxoglutarate to pyruvate + glutamate, with PLP as the catalytic cofactor. A broad transaminase assertion is refined to the established alanine-specific function. Alanine catabolism is direct catalytic participation, not inferred solely from clinical marker levels.

The original PMID:11863375 mainly describes cloning GPT2 and its recombinant activity; this does not warrant deleting the correctly assigned human GPT activity. Independent purified native ALT1 evidence establishes the claim, and abstract emphasis alone cannot show curator misattribution.

Human cell fractionation in PMID:19360321 reports ALT1 in cytosol and ER fractions, with no mitochondrial detection. Retain the established cytosolic location and refine general cytoplasm to cytosol; do not manufacture an ER annotation from one fractionation result or assert all ALT1 pools have exclusive cytosolic localization. Reactome R-HSA-70523 and R-HSA-70524 describe the two directions of the same reaction. The GPT2 Reactome R-HSA-507749 PDB3IHJ cofactor observation is used only as homologous structural corroboration of PLP chemistry, never as evidence that GPT is mitochondrial.

The extracellular-region assertion is independently supported by isoenzyme-specific human plasma detection in PMID:19360321. Retain it non-core without inventing an active secretion mechanism or an extracellular physiological catalytic role. The urinary-exosome HDA assertion from PMID:19056867 is retained as a curated contextual observation; the target peptide table was not independently reconstructed from the cached abstract. A cytosolic enzyme need not be dismissed as contamination when detected outside its usual compartment.
