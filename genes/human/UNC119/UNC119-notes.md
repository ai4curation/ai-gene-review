# UNC119 notes

- Human UNC119/UNC119A is the human SF5 member of PTHR12951, the PDE6D/UNC119 myristoyl-binding carrier family; it has an Ig-like C-terminal hydrophobic pocket that directly binds acylated GNAT1/transducin-alpha peptides [PMID:21642972 "The co-crystal structure of UNC119 with the lauroylated Tα peptide at 2.0 Å (PDB ID: 3RBQ) showed that the pocket easily accommodates a lauroyl (C12) moiety"].
- UNC119A and UNC119B share myristoylated cargo binding biochemistry, but have divergent cellular specificity. UNC119A localizes to centrosomes in RPE cells, whereas UNC119B concentrates at the transition zone/proximal cilium and is the paralog required for NPHP3 ciliary delivery [PMID:22085962 "LAP-UNC119a localized to the centrosome, as determined by colocalization with pericentrin"; "ARL3, UNC119b (but not UNC119a), and the ARL3 GAP Retinitis Pigmentosa 2 (RP2) are required for NPHP3 ciliary targeting"].
- The current PAINT model already respects that split: PTHR12951 root lipid binding and lipoprotein transport gains reach both human paralogs, while the root cilium and cilium assembly gains are explicitly stopped from reaching the UNC119A clade by PTN002658567 IRDs.
- UNC119A has a separate Src-family kinase adaptor arm through its N terminus: it binds and activates LCK/FYN in T-cell signaling [PMID:14757743 "Unc119 is a receptor-associated activator of Src-type kinases"] and FYN/RAB11A in cytokinesis [PMID:23535298 "Our results showed that UNC119a interacts sequentially with Fyn and Rab11a and is necessary for the completion of cytokinesis"].
- The mouse ortholog is also annotated to negative regulation of clathrin-dependent and caveolin-mediated endocytosis. The human record has a related but distinct Abl/Crk bacterial-uptake arm [PMID:19381274 "Unc119 interacts with Abl family kinases and inhibits their kinase activity"; "Shigella infectivity increases in Unc119-deficient epithelial and macrophage cells"], so these imported endocytosis rows were kept as peripheral rather than made core.
- No module or GO-CAM review was added. The existing primary-cilium module models the ARL3-UNC119B-RP2 NPHP3 delivery route and should stay UNC119B-specific; no cached production GO-CAM covers the UNC119A photoreceptor or cytokinesis functions.
