Gene Description

Cyclin D2 is a noncatalytic regulatory subunit of cyclin-dependent kinases that couples growth signals to G1 progression and entry into S phase. Its association with CDK4 or CDK6 promotes retinoblastoma-family protein phosphorylation by the kinase subunit, relieving restraint on cell-cycle progression. Cyclin D2-containing complexes redistribute between the cytoplasm and nucleus, with nuclear-envelope and chromatin-associated populations observed during cell-cycle entry. Cyclin D2 abundance is controlled by ubiquitin-dependent proteolysis. Pathogenic CCND2 variants cause megalencephaly-polymicrogyria-polydactyly-hydrocephalus syndrome 3.
