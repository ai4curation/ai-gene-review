## Stability, proliferation and correction limits

PMID:33854239 was read at complete abstract and selected complete Results/Discussion/caption scope. D2 protein accumulation, increased half-life and joint D1/D2/D3 expression are distinct from the more detailed D1-centred rescue, substrate ubiquitination and CDK2 redistribution experiments. The combined human U2OS D-cyclin overexpression is not a D2-only experiment; mouse embryo and tumour data remain separate. The review uses this source for the existing broad cyclin statements and stability context, with stronger D2-specific primary support for the core mechanism.

The original PMID:24130168 abstract reports miR-29, CCND2 and MMP2 in gastric cancer. The exact CCND2 perturbation/rescue controls and the 2024 correction text could not be read. Independent official PubMed reciprocal links and the publisher establish the erratum DOI <https://doi.org/10.1093/carcin/bgae070>, but its corrected material and impact remain unresolved. The three source-specific proliferation, apoptosis and positive-G1/S assertions therefore remain UNDECIDED. This does not declare the paper retracted or the phenotypes false, and it does not invalidate independently supported general G1/S activity.
