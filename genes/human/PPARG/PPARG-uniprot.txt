CC       heterodimers with the retinoic acid receptor and acts as a key
CC       regulator of biological processes, such as adipocyte differentiation,
CC       lipid metabolism, glucose homeostasis and beta-oxidation of fatty acids
CC       (PubMed:16150867, PubMed:20829347, PubMed:23525231, PubMed:8702406,
CC       PubMed:8706692, PubMed:9065481). Activated by lipid ligands: binds
CC       peroxisome proliferators, such as hypolipidemic drugs, and fatty acids,
CC       such as prostaglandin J2 metabolites (PubMed:16150867, PubMed:20829347,
CC       PubMed:23525231, PubMed:8702406, PubMed:8706692, PubMed:9065481).
CC       Ligand-binding results in a conformational change in the receptor,
CC       promoting dissociation of repressors and recruitment of coactivators,
CC       and subsequent activation of target gene expression (PubMed:16150867,
CC       PubMed:20829347, PubMed:23525231, PubMed:8702406, PubMed:8706692,
CC       PubMed:9065481). Specifically binds to DNA specific PPAR response
CC       elements (PPRE) and modulates the transcription of its target genes,

CC       of gut homeostasis by suppressing NF-kappa-B-mediated pro-inflammatory
CC       responses (PubMed:20829347). Plays a role in the regulation of
CC       cardiovascular circadian rhythms by regulating the transcription of
CC       BMAL1 in the blood vessels (By similarity).
