Gene Description

COL10A1 encodes the alpha-1 chain of type X collagen, a secreted short-chain collagen produced by hypertrophic chondrocytes in presumptive mineralization zones of hyaline cartilage. Three chains assemble through their C-terminal NC1 domains into homotrimers that contribute to extracellular matrix organization during skeletal development. Type X collagen forms higher-order networks in vitro and associates with collagen fibrils in tissues. Its isolated human NC1 trimer binds calcium. Dominant pathogenic COL10A1 variants cause Schmid metaphyseal chondrodysplasia.

Manual assessment: COL10A1

All 34 machine source objects, their order and 17 reference identities are retained. The seed has no alternative-product section. There are 35 decisions: 16 ACCEPT, 12 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, six MODIFY and one NEW. The new assertion is calcium binding from a directly tested human NC1 construct, not an imported source assertion or a new process. The source index records literal interaction/product, donor, Reactome and target-table provenance; it is authored documentation, not independent experimental evidence.

Matrix structure and calcium

The integrated core is extracellular matrix structural contribution by the type X homotrimer, coupled to matrix organization. The current GO:0030020 comment explicitly permits ECM collagens. Human proteomics verifies COL10A1 in ECM-enriched material but does not measure isolated tensile mechanics. Type X is not recast as a conventional fibrillar collagen: GO:0005599 itself distinguishes in-vitro hexagonal lattices from in-vivo fibril association. The original chick study reports matrix-associated type X and self-assembly of purified protein; only its complete abstract was accessible, and the PDF-only PMC record plus failed PDF retrieval are retained (PMID:1860888).

Selected original author-uploaded manuscript windows for PMID:11839302 were read in addition to the genuine abstract cache. The host is ResearchGate, with an author upload and apparent proof/manuscript header; this is not described as the final publisher PDF. The Methods use the human C-terminal 161-residue NC1 domain with an N-terminal His6 tag in E. coli TG-1. The 2-angstrom structure resolves residues 549–680; the tag and the first 28 construct residues are disordered. The structural calcium cluster is corroborated by inductively coupled plasma analysis after Chelex-treated-buffer dialysis and an EDTA pre-dialysis comparison. Bulk calcium abundance and the structural cluster are different measurements; no fixed native full-length stoichiometry is inferred.

This warrants non-core NEW GO:0005509, with no typed ancestor/descendant overlap among the 34 originals. Both narrower current descendants describe synaptic calcium buffering and do not fit. Calcium binding does not establish mineral nucleation, a mineralization process or a second core function. The human clinical W651R report supports skeletal involvement but is not a direct mutant-assembly experiment (PMID:8554571). The inherited trimer/organization/location assertions remain supported; historical PAINT trees and ancestral-node placement were not inspected.

Positive binding, products and source granularity

Twelve binding assertions join to 39 literal positive 42-field interaction records: separate 2014 and 2020 sources and distinct isoforms are retained. Official product sequences and ROGIDs agree for the target and all partners. COL10A1 is the 680-residue precursor in these yeast two-hybrid records; mature secreted trimers and endogenous cell context were not demonstrated. The validated-Y2H records have neutral/neutral experimental roles, whereas array/pooling records may designate bait/prey; no uniform bait assignment is imposed. The 2014 dataset's orthogonal validation subsets do not establish pair-specific orthogonal validation for these interactions (PMIDs:25416956 and 32296183).

Five class refinements are source-scoped. Canonical BANP and BANP-2 retain positive binding and are refined to RNA polymerase II-specific DNA-binding transcription-factor binding. The complete original PMID:34234345 abstract directly establishes the mouse/human BANP CGI-promoter class; the 469-residue BANP-2 retains its BEN domain but alters other regions, so exact isoform activity and interface specificity remain untested. NRF1-4 retains its DNA-binding region despite an insertion within the broader activation region. ZNF410-3 retains its five DNA-binding zinc fingers despite its C-terminal deletion. These products support the same transcription-factor-binding class without assigning transcriptional regulation to COL10A1. MESD has an independently curated folding/transport chaperone function, supporting chaperone binding; the screen does not demonstrate that MESD folds collagen X.

The remaining seven positive binding assertions stay non-core under the campaign policy. VSNL1 is a calcium sensor, not calmodulin. SGTB cochaperone association with Hsp70 does not make SGTB an Hsp70 protein or independently establish the folding/transport activity required for a more precise class here. Ubiquilin shuttles are not the assembled proteasome; their positive canonical/isoform and cross-source assertions remain distinct. CIDEB lipid transfer during droplet fusion is not chemical catalysis merely because material is transferred. None of these associations is rejected solely because native compartments differ, nor used to invent a collagen nuclear/proteostasis pathway.
