### Matrix structure and calcium

The integrated core is extracellular matrix structural contribution by the type X homotrimer, coupled to matrix organization. The current GO:0030020 comment explicitly permits ECM collagens. Human proteomics verifies COL10A1 in ECM-enriched material but does not measure isolated tensile mechanics. The original chick study reports matrix-associated type X and self-assembly of purified protein; only its complete abstract was accessible, and the PDF-only PMC record plus failed PDF retrieval are retained (PMID:1860888).

Selected original author-uploaded manuscript windows for PMID:11839302 were read in addition to the genuine abstract cache. The host is ResearchGate, with an author upload and apparent proof/manuscript header; this is not described as the final publisher PDF. The Methods use the human C-terminal 161-residue NC1 domain with an N-terminal His6 tag in E. coli TG-1. The 2-angstrom structure resolves residues 549–680; the tag and the first 28 construct residues are disordered. The structural calcium cluster is corroborated by inductively coupled plasma analysis after Chelex-treated-buffer dialysis and an EDTA pre-dialysis comparison. This warrants non-core NEW GO:0005509, with no typed ancestor/descendant overlap among the 34 originals. Both narrower current descendants describe synaptic calcium buffering and do not fit.
