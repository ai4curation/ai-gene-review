# GEMIN5 source guide

GEMIN5 selects RNA substrates for snRNP assembly and regulates translation. Four direct molecular activities are represented: snRNA recognition, cap recognition, SMN-mRNA 3′-UTR binding and ribosome binding. Cap binding does not require obligatory SMN membership; ribosome binding does not imply an integral ribosomal subunit or a proven elongation-stalling mechanism. Positive SMN-specific translation and broader repression remain separated by experimental context.

The 73 incoming annotation contexts are retained. Sixteen generic protein-binding terms are removed while their observed partner pairs remain recorded. Six refinements specify mRNA binding, viral-IRES 5′-UTR binding, DEAD-box helicase binding, eIF4E binding or gems. Broad splicing, complex assembly and nuclear-body assertions are represented by supported, more specific claims. No new annotation rows or natural products are introduced.
