CC   -!- FUNCTION: Inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate-gated calcium channel that upon
CC       inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate binding transports calcium from the
CC       endoplasmic reticulum lumen to cytoplasm. Exists in two states; a long-
CC       lived closed state where the channel is essentially 'parked' with only
CC       very rare visits to an open state and that ligands facilitate the
CC       transition from the 'parked' state into a 'drive' mode represented by
CC       periods of bursting activity (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9Z329}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Ca(2+)(in) = Ca(2+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:29671,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:29108; Evidence={ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9Z329};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate-gated calcium channel
CC       activity is increased by cAMP that occurs independently of PKA
CC       activation. ATP and cytosolic calcium modulate the open probability
CC       (Po) predominantly by facilitating extended 'bursting' activity of the
CC       channel (By similarity). Inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate-gated calcium
CC       channel activity is inhibited by CALM1 in a calcium-dependent manner
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P29995,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9Z329}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotetramer (By similarity). Interacts with CABP1

CC       similarity). Interacts with BCL2L10 (PubMed:27995898). Interacts with
CC       TRPC4 (By similarity). Interacts with CHGA and CHGB (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P29995, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8WN96,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12032348, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27995898}.
