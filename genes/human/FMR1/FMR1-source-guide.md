# FMR1 source guide

This guide accompanies the [review](FMR1-ai-review.yaml), [journal](FMR1-notes.md), and [structured source evidence](FMR1-source-evidence.yaml). It records what was actually available and what the experiments establish. Primary-source reading was divided among Codex reviewers, followed by cross-review of all four annotation groups; this does not mean every reviewer read every original paper.

## Context and source preservation

The genuine fetch produced 253 original QuickGO records and 250 stored annotations. Three projection pairs (raw 33/34,174/175,235/236; zero-based) share exported seed keys. Both originals remain in the evidence file. All original terms, evidence codes, references, qualifiers and partner fields are retained. Eight original extension groups contain 12 conjunctive atoms and are represented through the schema's extensions slot, preserving relation/target/order. The two RNAcentral targets are synthetic structural RNAs, not endogenous human transcripts. The neuroblast extension retains CL:0000031, whose preferred label is neuroblast (sensu Vertebrata).

The source set contains 85 immutable normal files:82 publications and three PTHR10603 files. Eighteen existing main files were hydrated before genuine fetch and remain exact. Additional source qualification and genuine acquisition expanded the initial 69-file set to85. A successful full-text download is not a claim that its complete content was read. The six later donor publications are retained for checkable provenance; their newly acquired bodies were not silently promoted to inspected experiments. The initial source-access map remains a dated 69-source observation, superseded in scope by this 85-source inventory.

Manual source research was used after known provider authentication/quota failures in this campaign. No generated-provider report is fabricated. Supporting entries contain reference IDs without new publication quotations, following the standing [ClinGen project instruction](../../../projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md). That instruction also governs generic binding: supported generic associations remain non-core, supported specific classes are refined, and unresolved source contexts remain undecided. This is an explicit user instruction, not a change to the default validators.

## Biological decisions and assay boundaries

The 250 stored decisions are 81 ACCEPT, 139 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, 8 MODIFY, 6 TOO_GENERAL and 16 UNDECIDED. Three distinct core activities cover selected-mRNA recognition, translation regulation and molecular-condensate scaffolding. Developmental, viral, nuclear/chromatin and channel effects are retained with source-specific scope rather than treated as universal extensions of those cores.

The positive AMPA-receptor-internalization annotation from the original 2015 source is refined to negative regulation of internalization. The measured rescue reduces the elevated internalized/total-receptor signal; this is not an isolated rate-constant measurement. The 2014 chromatin paper supplies separate corroboration and is not substituted for the original 2015 citation. Positive filopodium assembly is generalized to regulation of assembly because the donor's combined filopodia/spine protrusion assay does not isolate a positive assembly rate. The presynaptic-translation IBA remains undecided: its phylogenetic placement is genuine, but the inspected channel/exocytosis study does not measure local translation. This is an access/mechanistic limit, not a donor-count argument or an asserted ancestral loss.

FMRP can repress or enhance translation depending on the RNA and regulatory system. RNA structure, translation, stability and localization were measured separately and are not interchangeable. The short-RNA annealing experiments do not establish universal RISC necessity; the FMRP-deficient-cell study provides relevant counterevidence to that universal claim. Chromatin recognition and damage-response recruitment are not catalytic DNA repair. A source's mRNA localization or protein association is not automatically evidence for direct protein contact.

Native human assays, tagged human protein in rodent hosts, rodent proteins in human hosts, and cell-free fragments are distinguished. The human FMRP 445–632/CAPRIN1 607–709 condensate experiments do not assay every full-length natural product. CNOT7 performs the deadenylation in that system. The 2024 FXR1 study includes real FMRP network experiments, but its actomyosin-signaling conclusion is not transferred to FMRP. Its FMR1 knockdown did not reproduce the tested A549 stress-fiber phenotype.

## Phylogenetic evidence

The actual PTHR10603 tree and MSA each contain 149 entries. The human target PTN002471935 reconstructs the 632-residue displayed sequence exactly. All 11 incoming IBA origin/term joins occur on its actual path. The 11-record normal PAINT slice is derived from the original IBD GAF, whose URL, retrieval time and hash are preserved in the evidence file, alongside the official tree/MSA receipts. PTN001318100 and PTN000063980 are ancestral nodes; they are not individual pairwise donors.

Tree/MSA and IBD are separately pinned release snapshots. In particular, nucleus raw 156 has different donor/date fields between original QuickGO and original IBD; neither is rewritten. Target self-membership and a short donor list are not treated as circularity or weak evidence. Node placement establishes the propagation path; it does not replace biological interpretation of the asserted function.
