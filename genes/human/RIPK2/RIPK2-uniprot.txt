CC   -!- FUNCTION: Serine/threonine/tyrosine-protein kinase that plays an
CC       essential role in modulation of innate and adaptive immune responses
CC       (PubMed:14638696, PubMed:17054981, PubMed:21123652, PubMed:28656966,
CC       PubMed:9575181, PubMed:9642260). Acts as a key effector of NOD1 and
CC       NOD2 signaling pathways: upon activation by bacterial peptidoglycans,
CC       NOD1 and NOD2 oligomerize and recruit RIPK2 via CARD-CARD domains,
CC       leading to the formation of RIPK2 filaments (PubMed:17054981,
CC       PubMed:17562858, PubMed:21123652, PubMed:22607974, PubMed:28656966,
CC       PubMed:29452636, PubMed:30026309). Once recruited, RIPK2
CC       autophosphorylates and undergoes 'Lys-63'-linked polyubiquitination by
CC       E3 ubiquitin ligases XIAP, BIRC2 and BIRC3, as well as 'Met-1'-linked
CC       (linear) polyubiquitination by the LUBAC complex, becoming a
CC       scaffolding protein for downstream effectors (PubMed:22607974,
CC       PubMed:28545134, PubMed:29452636, PubMed:30026309, PubMed:30279485,
CC       PubMed:30478312). 'Met-1'-linked polyubiquitin chains attached to RIPK2
CC       recruit IKBKG/NEMO, which undergoes 'Lys-63'-linked polyubiquitination
CC       in a RIPK2-dependent process (PubMed:17562858, PubMed:22607974,
CC       PubMed:29452636, PubMed:30026309). 'Lys-63'-linked polyubiquitin chains
CC       attached to RIPK2 serve as docking sites for TAB2 and TAB3 and mediate
CC       the recruitment of MAP3K7/TAK1 to IKBKG/NEMO, inducing subsequent

CC       released from NF-kappa-B inhibitors and translocates into the nucleus
