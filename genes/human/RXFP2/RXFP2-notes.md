# RXFP2 notes

- Core function: RXFP2 is a plasma-membrane relaxin/INSL3 family peptide receptor whose conserved dominant branch is Gs/adenylate-cyclase signaling.
- GO has generic peptide- and protein-hormone receptor terms that fit RXFP2 binding to INSL3 and relaxin-family peptides.
- PMID:16569707 supports a human RXFP2 inhibitory branch through GalphaoB in transfected HEK293T cells, so the adenylate cyclase-inhibiting GPCR row is retained as a secondary signaling branch.
- Searches for human RXFP2 evidence supporting direct oocyte maturation, negative regulation of cell population proliferation, or negative regulation of apoptotic process did not find primary human RXFP2 work comparable to the rat Lgr8/Rxfp2 oocyte and germ-cell studies; those three rat-propagated process rows are kept as over-annotations for human RXFP2.
- The negative search used PubMed and cached-publication queries for `human RXFP2 oocyte maturation`, `human RXFP2 cell proliferation`, `human RXFP2 apoptosis`, `LGR8 human oocyte`, `LGR8 human germ cell`, and the three rat-propagated GO labels; it found human RXFP2 signaling papers, including the GalphaoB cAMP branch in PMID:16569707, but not primary human RXFP2 work for the rat-propagated developmental or survival processes.
