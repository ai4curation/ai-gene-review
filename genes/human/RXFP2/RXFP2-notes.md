# RXFP2 notes

- Core function: RXFP2 is a plasma-membrane relaxin/INSL3 family peptide receptor whose conserved branch is Gs/adenylate-cyclase signaling.
- GO has no specific RXFP2 or INSL3 receptor term in the local cache. Keep the closest supported generic receptor and peptide-hormone-binding terms.
- Mark rat-propagated oocyte maturation, Gi, proliferation, and apoptosis rows as over-annotated because the human record and Reactome RXFP2 event support Gs peptide-hormone receptor activity, not those downstream tissue contexts.
