# ID2 notes

Automated Falcon and Perplexity-lite deep research were unavailable in this runtime, as recorded in `ID1-notes.md`;
these notes summarize the cached primary literature, UniProt record, and PANTHER PAINT data used for the manual review.

## Evidence reviewed

- **Conserved ID-family mechanism.** ID2 is on `PTHR11723` node `PTN000919548` with ID1, ID3 and ID4 for the
  conserved nuclear HLH transcription-regulator inhibitor mechanism. The node has no IRD or IKR loss on the human
  ID2 lineage.
- **CLOCK/BMAL1 regulation.** Ward et al. showed that ID2 binds CLOCK and BMAL1, that the ID2 HLH domain is
  required to inhibit CLOCK-BMAL1 transactivation, and that ID2 overexpression reduces CLOCK and BMAL1 nuclear
  localization [PMID:20861012].
- **Polycystin-dependent ID2 localization.** Li et al. linked PC1/PC2 to ID2 nuclear localization and cell-cycle
  regulation; the accessible abstract indicates that polycystin loss promotes nuclear accumulation of ID2, where
  ID2 can regulate the p21 promoter [PMID:16311606].
- **Scope.** Many ID2 biological-process rows are mouse ISS/Ensembl projections of developmental or circadian
  phenotypes. Those are reviewed as context-specific outputs of ID2-mediated transcription-factor inhibition, not
  as separate core molecular functions.
