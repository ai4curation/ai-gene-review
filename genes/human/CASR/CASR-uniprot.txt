CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor that senses changes in the
CC       extracellular concentration of calcium ions and plays a key role in
CC       maintaining calcium homeostasis (PubMed:17555508, PubMed:19789209,
CC       PubMed:21566075, PubMed:22114145, PubMed:22789683, PubMed:23966241,
CC       PubMed:25104082, PubMed:25292184, PubMed:25766501, PubMed:26386835,
CC       PubMed:32817431, PubMed:33603117, PubMed:34194040, PubMed:34467854,
CC       PubMed:7759551, PubMed:8636323, PubMed:8702647, PubMed:8878438). Senses
CC       fluctuations in the circulating calcium concentration: activated by
CC       elevated circulating calcium, leading to decreased parathyroid hormone
CC       (PTH) secretion in parathyroid glands (By similarity). In kidneys, acts
CC       as a key regulator of renal tubular calcium resorption (By similarity).
CC       Ligand binding causes a conformation change that triggers signaling via
CC       guanine nucleotide-binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the
CC       activity of downstream effectors (PubMed:38632411). CASR is coupled
CC       with different G(q)/G(11), G(i)/G(o)- or G(s)-classes of G proteins
CC       depending on the context (PubMed:38632411). In the parathyroid and
CC       kidney, CASR signals through G(q)/G(11) and G(i)/G(o) G proteins:
CC       G(q)/G(11) coupling activates phospholipase C-beta, releasing
CC       diacylglycerol (DAG) and inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate (IP3) second
CC       messengers, while G(i)/G(o) coupling mediates inhibition of adenylate
CC       cyclase activity (PubMed:38632411, PubMed:7759551). The G protein-
CC       coupled receptor activity is activated by a co-agonist mechanism:
CC       aromatic amino acids, such as Trp or Phe, act concertedly with divalent
CC       cations, such as calcium or magnesium, to achieve full receptor
CC       activation (PubMed:27386547, PubMed:27434672, PubMed:32817431,
CC       PubMed:33603117, PubMed:34194040). Acts as an activator of the NLRP3
CC       inflammasome via G(i)/G(o)-mediated signaling: down-regulation of
CC       cyclic AMP (cAMP) relieving NLRP3 inhibition by cAMP (PubMed:32843625).
CC       Acts as a regulator of proton-sensing receptor GPR68 in a seesaw
CC       manner: CASR-mediated signaling inhibits GPR68 signaling in response to
CC       extracellular calcium, while GPR68 inhibits CASR in presence of
CC       extracellular protons (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P48442,

CC   -!- DOMAIN: The extracellular regions of the homodimer interact in a side-
CC       by-side fashion while facing opposite directions (PubMed:27386547,

CC       (PubMed:27386547, PubMed:27434672). In the inactive configuration, the
CC       venus flytrap modules of both protomers are in the open conformation
CC       associated with the resting state (open-open) and the interdomain cleft
CC       is empty (PubMed:27434672). In addition, each protomer contains three
CC       anions, which reinforce the inactive conformation, and one calcium ion
CC       (PubMed:27434672). In the active configuration, both protomers of
CC       extracellular regions have the closed conformation associated with
CC       agonist-binding (closed-closed) (PubMed:27386547, PubMed:27434672). The
CC       ligand-binding cleft of each protomer is solely occupied by an aromatic
CC       amino-acid (PubMed:27386547, PubMed:27434672). Calcium is bound at four
CC       novel sites, including one at the homodimer interface (PubMed:27386547,
CC       PubMed:27434672). Agonist-binding induces large conformational changes
CC       within the extracellular region homodimer: first, the venus flytrap
CC       module of each protomer undergoes domain closure (PubMed:27386547,

CC       each other, resulting in an expansion of the homodimer interactions
