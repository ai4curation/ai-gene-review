Gene Description

CNOT1 is the large organizing subunit of the CCR4-NOT complex. Its repeated helical domains hold catalytic deadenylases and regulatory modules together and provide binding surfaces for RNA-associated recruitment factors, including tristetraprolin, GW182/TNRC6 and Nanos proteins. These assemblies coordinate poly(A)-tail shortening, mRNA decay and translational repression. CNOT1 acts in the cytoplasm and P-bodies and also participates in nuclear receptor-dependent transcriptional repression. It supplies scaffold and recruitment functions, while CNOT6/CNOT6L and CNOT7/CNOT8 provide the complex’s nuclease chemistry. Cellular effects on stem-cell identity and survival depend on this broader control of gene expression.

Core Functions

CNOT1 is a structural scaffold of CCR4-NOT that holds catalytic deadenylases and regulatory modules together and recruits them through interfaces for RNA-associated factors. Its MIF4G domain positions CAF1 with an accessible active site, while other regions organize the NOT and CNOT9 modules. Human reconstitution and recruitment experiments demonstrate coordinated mRNA deadenylation and silencing. CNOT1 contributes to the assembled RNase activity without supplying nuclease catalysis itself. The work uses full-length proteins and engineered domains; the four natural products have not been compared equivalently.
