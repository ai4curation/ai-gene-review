| **Processed matrix mineralization — moderate-to-high mechanistic confidence** | Secreted DMP1 is a precursor processed by **BMP1/tolloid-like metalloproteinases** at X–Asp bonds into approximately **37-kDa N-terminal** and **57-kDa C-terminal** products. Native full-length, highly phosphorylated DMP1 inhibited hydroxyapatite formation in a cell-free system, whereas the highly phosphorylated 57-kDa fragment nucleated mineral; the N-terminal region can inhibit nucleation. Thus activity depends on cleavage, phosphorylation, concentration, collagen context, and assay conditions—not a simple universal “mineralization promoter” label. | Biochemical isolation, proteolysis studies, and controlled cell-free hydroxyapatite assays; strong for processing and context-dependent activity, but principally rat/bovine proteins rather than native human DMP1. | 2004, [10.1074/jbc.M314114200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M314114200); 2007, [10.1177/154405910708601202](https://doi.org/10.1177/154405910708601202) (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000028, pqac-00000029) |
| **Osteocyte–FGF23 bone–kidney axis — high causal confidence; mechanism incomplete** | DMP1 acts locally in osteocytes as a **negative regulator of FGF23 production** and supports osteocyte maturation. DMP1 loss enhances osteocytic FGFR signaling and raises FGF23; circulating FGF23—not DMP1—then signals through renal FGFR–αKlotho complexes, reduces proximal-tubule phosphate reabsorption and 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin-D production, and causes phosphate wasting.
