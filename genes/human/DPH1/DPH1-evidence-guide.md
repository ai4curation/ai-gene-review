The binding-record ROGIDs are `hcvtBckAa6QAiPOZppYB8Yl1MTQ9606` (DPH1) and `M7mQ08NDYxuawmUEKRgjSdIr+Og9606` (TFCP2). They match the checked parent/reference sequences; this checksum agreement is not independent authentication of an archived experimental ORF. The records have no annotated bait-region feature; no inference about unrecorded tags, all isoforms or native stoichiometry is made. The verified [GO:0140297 definition](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0140297) requires a DNA-binding transcription-factor partner, whose class is supported independently by the official TFCP2 FUNCTION field.

Current typed ontology closures establish nucleoplasm→nucleus, cytosol→cytoplasm, GO:0140297→protein binding, and GO:0120513→catalytic complex/protein-containing complex. The nuclear refinement specifies a positive nucleoplasmic pool without claiming that the entire nuclear distribution excludes chromosomes or nucleolus. Broad complex terms are refined to the existing defined synthase complex; no duplicate NEW is proposed.

Other reference scope is deliberately limited. PMID:11013075 is a verified abstract describing the OVCA1/RBM8 interaction screen; publisher access did not yield its full Methods. PMID:8603384 and PMID:8616839 provide historical transcript/candidate-gene context from abstracts, with OVCA2 explicitly separate. PMID:15489334 is an abstract-only MGC resource; PMID:16625196 has a full XML cache but only its sequencing abstract/metadata were used. PMID:25558065 and PMID:29362492 are abstract-only clinical context. PMID:26220823 has a full XML cache, but only its cohort abstract was used here. None supplies invented target biochemical assays or additional developmental-process annotations.

The only newly reproduced source excerpts occur once each in the YAML: seven words from PMID:10519411 and eight from PMID:31463593. Notes and this guide paraphrase rather than repeat those excerpts. Public primary URLs and exact record identifiers remain usable independently of the execution workspace.
