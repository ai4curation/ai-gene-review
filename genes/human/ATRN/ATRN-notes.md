# ATRN notes

## 2026-10-05 review (PAINT, affinage)

- Two isoforms [PMID:10811918 "The mRNA isoform for membrane attractin splices over the LINE-1 exon and includes five exons encoding transmembrane and cytoplasmic domains with organization and coding potential almost identical to that of the mouse gene."].
- No DPPIV activity [PMID:17261078 "This indicates that the multidomain protein attractin acts as a receptor or adhesion protein rather than a protease."].
- Signaling receptor activity (TAS 1999 and mouse IEA) is UNDECIDED: the genetics place mahogany between Agouti and MCRs, but no cited source shows receptor signalling. A 2025 preprint (affinage) instead proposes ATRN as an MGRN1 adaptor for MC1R/MC4R degradation; it is not citable from the cache.
- Extracellular and plasma membrane rows are accepted (isoform-specific). Exosome (HDA) rows are non-core.
- No NEW terms. The CNS myelination role rests on necessity evidence only (rodent mutants, a human leukodystrophy family).
