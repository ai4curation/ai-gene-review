CC   -!- FUNCTION: ATP-dependent transporter of the ATP-binding cassette (ABC)
CC       family that actively extrudes physiological compounds, and xenobiotics
CC       from cells. Lipophilic anion transporter that mediates ATP-dependent
CC       transport of glucuronide conjugates such as estradiol-17-beta-o-
CC       glucuronide and GSH conjugates such as leukotriene C4 (LTC4)
CC       (PubMed:12527806, PubMed:15256465). May regulate the transport of
CC       organic compounds in testes across the blood-testis-barrier (Probable).
CC       Mediates intercellular propagation of antiviral immune signaling in
CC       early stages of infection (By similarity). In RNA virus-infected cells,
CC       oligoadenylate synthase senses viral dsRNA and generates 2',5'-
CC       oligoadenylates (2-5A) which act as second messengers to activate
CC       RNASEL and type I interferon signaling to inhibit viral replication (By
CC       similarity). This innate signaling pathway is locally extended and
CC       amplified by ABCC10, which exports 2-5A from virus-infected cells to
CC       cross-activates RNASEL in uninfected neighboring cells and confers
CC       protection against viral infection (By similarity). Mediates multidrug
CC       resistance (MDR) in cancer cells by preventing the intracellular
CC       accumulation of certain antitumor drugs, such as, docetaxel and
CC       paclitaxel (PubMed:15256465, PubMed:23087055). Does not transport
CC       glycocholic acid, taurocholic acid, MTX, folic acid, cAMP, or cGMP
CC       (PubMed:12527806). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R4P9,
