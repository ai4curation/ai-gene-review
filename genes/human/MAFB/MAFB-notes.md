# MAFB notes

`just deep-research human MAFB --provider perplexity` failed because the provider was not available in this checkout, and retrying the available `claude-code` provider failed because Claude Code was not logged in. I therefore reviewed the cached GOA, UniProtKB, PANTHER and publication evidence manually.

MAFB is a nuclear large-Maf bZIP transcription factor. UniProt describes MAFB as a transcriptional activator or repressor, required in several differentiation contexts, and as a DNA-binding homodimer or heterodimer with other bZIP factors.

PMID:8620536 directly identified MafB as an Ets-1 interaction partner that represses Ets-1 transactivation of erythroid promoters and inhibits erythroid differentiation. PMID:20581830 directly identified human MAFB as a negative regulator of type I interferon transcription and an IRF3 antagonist at IFN promoters.

PMID:27181683 supports abducens nerve formation as a developmental context: human MAFB loss-of-function variants cause Duane retraction syndrome and Mafb-knockout mice confirm the abducens-nerve pathology. PMID:8001130 is the older mouse kreisler paper linking Mafb/kr to hindbrain and inner-ear development.

PMID:27208706 is mouse and human keratinocyte work. It supports a non-core role for MAFB in keratinocyte differentiation but not direct participation in cornified-envelope assembly; MAFB is a transcription factor upstream of the envelope genes rather than an assembly factor.

The ComplexPortal BACH1/BACH2 rows cite Bach or Keap1/Nrf2 review papers centered on small Maf proteins, and the ATF4 integrated-stress-response row cites an ATF4 mitochondrial-stress paper that does not mention MAFB. Those rows are treated cautiously and not promoted into the MAFB core.
