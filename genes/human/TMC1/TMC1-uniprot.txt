CC   -!- FUNCTION: Pore-forming subunit of the mechanotransducer (MET) non-
CC       selective cation channel complex located at the tips of stereocilia of
CC       cochlear hair cells and that mediates sensory transduction in the
CC       auditory system (By similarity). The MET complex is composed of two
CC       dimeric pore-forming ion-conducting transmembrane TMC (TMC1 or TMC2)
CC       subunits, and aided by several auxiliary proteins including LHFPL5,
CC       TMIE, CIB2/3 and TOMT, and the tip-link PCDH15 (By similarity). MET
CC       channel is activated by tension in the tip-link extending from the side
CC       wall of one stereocilium to the tip of the adjacent shorter
CC       stereocilium, where the channel is located (By similarity). TMC1 MET
CC       channel is highly permeable to calcium and likely transports monovalent
CC       cations (By similarity). Also involved in vestibular hair cells
CC       transduction current (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R4P5}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Ca(2+)(in) = Ca(2+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:29671,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:29108; Evidence={ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R4P5};
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms the MET channel composed of TMC dimer (TMC1 or TMC2),
CC       TMIE, TOMT, CIB (CIB2 or CIB3), LHFPL5 and PDH15 (PubMed:30138589,
CC       PubMed:34089643). The interaction of TMC1 and TMC2 with TOMT is
CC       required for the transportation of TMC1/2 into the stereocilia of hair
CC       cells. Interacts (via N-terminus) with both isoforms CD1 and CD3 of
CC       PCDH15 (By similarity). Can form a heterodimer with TMC2, TMC5 or TMC7
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R4P5,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30138589, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34089643}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R4P5};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000305}. Note=Localized to the
CC       stereocilia tips of the cochlear hair cells.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8R4P5}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Detected in fetal cochlea, and at low levels in
CC       placenta and testis.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: TMC1 is structurally similar to TMEM16 channel and may include
CC       ten transmembrane (TM) domains with the ion-conducting pore placed
CC       between TM4 and TM7. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30138589}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Deafness, autosomal dominant, 36 (DFNA36) [MIM:606705]: A form
CC       of non-syndromic sensorineural hearing loss. Sensorineural deafness
CC       results from damage to the neural receptors of the inner ear, the nerve
CC       pathways to the brain, or the area of the brain that receives sound
CC       information. DFNA36 is a bilateral hearing loss, and begins at 5-10
CC       years of age. It progresses to profound deafness within 10-15 years.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11850618, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25388789}. Note=The
CC       disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented in this
CC       entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Deafness, autosomal recessive, 7 (DFNB7) [MIM:600974]: A form

CC       results from damage to the neural receptors of the inner ear, the nerve
CC       pathways to the brain, or the area of the brain that receives sound
