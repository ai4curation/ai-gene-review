CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of a multiprotein complex required for the assembly
CC       of the RNA endonuclease module of the integrator complex
CC       (PubMed:39032489, PubMed:39032490). Associates with INTS9 and INTS11 in
CC       the cytoplasm and blocks the active site of INTS11 to inhibit the
CC       endonuclease activity of INTS11 before formation of the full integrator
CC       complex (PubMed:39032489, PubMed:39032490). Following dissociation of
CC       WDR73 of the complex, BRAT1 facilitates the nuclear import of the
CC       INTS9-INTS11 heterodimer (PubMed:39032489). In the nucleus, INTS4 is
CC       integrated to the INTS9-INTS11 heterodimer and BRAT1 is released from
CC       the mature RNA endonuclease module by inositol hexakisphosphate (InsP6)
CC       (PubMed:39032489). BRAT1 is also involved in DNA damage response;
CC       activates kinases ATM, SMC1A and PRKDC by modulating their
CC       phosphorylation status following ionizing radiation (IR) stress
CC       (PubMed:16452482, PubMed:22977523). Plays a role in regulating
CC       mitochondrial function and cell proliferation (PubMed:25070371).
CC       Required for protein stability of MTOR and MTOR-related proteins, and
CC       cell cycle progress by growth factors (PubMed:25657994).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16452482, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22977523,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25070371, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25657994,
