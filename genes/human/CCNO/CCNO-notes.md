### Identity and the historical glycosylase name

The normal UniProt caution and the official NCBI gene record explicitly distinguish this cyclin from uracil-DNA glycosylase. UDG2/UNG2 are historical names for CCNO, while current UNG2 denotes an isoform of the separate UNG gene. Consequently, old repair-enzyme descriptions are not evidence for CCNO glycosylase activity. This identity correction is also discussed by the 2014 kinase study, although its own negative glycosylase experiments are described as data not shown and are not treated as a independently inspectable biochemical dataset. No DNA-repair function is added.

The 1993 paper remains correctly identified as PMID:8419333, despite its historical enzyme interpretation. Its complete available abstract documents cell-cycle-regulated human transcript/protein abundance, promoter binding and turnover, using HeLa S3 and WI38 cells. Those observations establish regulation of CCNO expression; they do not, by themselves, establish a mitotic step performed by CCNO. The original full paper was not accessible. Its IDA mitotic-process annotation remains UNDECIDED, rather than being removed because of the naming history. [PMID:8419333; normal UniProt P22674; official NCBI gene 10309]

### Molecular activity: cyclin-dependent kinase activation

The official publisher Methods, Results, captions and Discussion of PMID:25364462 were read. The study amplified human NM021147 CCNO and NM001798 CDK2 cDNAs into tagged expression constructs, verified their sequences, and expressed them in HEK293 cells. Reciprocal tagged co-immunoprecipitation and association of tagged CCNO with endogenous CDK2 support complex formation. Anti-CDK2 immunoprecipitates phosphorylated histone H1 more strongly after CCNO expression. IgG immunoprecipitation and checks for recovered CDK2 were included. CCNO S81A retained partial stimulation and is not an inactive control. The observation fits the current GO:0061575 definition requiring CDK binding and activation. It supports refinement of the existing GO:0016538 regulator assertion and a single matching core molecular function. It does not make CCNO a catalytic kinase or establish a mitotic-progression endpoint. [PMID:25364462, DOI:10.3892/ol.2014.2530]

The available official abstract of PMID:18927588 reports cyclin O-associated active CDK complexes, mainly involving Cdk2, in the context of intrinsic apoptosis in lymphoid cells. That context is kept separate from the human recombinant/overexpression assays and from physiological airway differentiation. The paywalled original body has not been read, and no new apoptosis annotation is proposed. [PMID:18927588, DOI:10.1038/cdd.2008.145]

The 2025 paper was also recovered as the official author-repository PDF at https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/221295/1/PIIS2211124724014682.pdf. Selected complete Results, Methods subsections, Discussion and limitations were read as text. Its exact mouse constructs are pWPI-mCCNO R135A and pWPI-mCCNO K190A, mutations of conserved cyclin-fold residues. Validation expressed myc-tagged WT or mutant CCNO in HEK293 cells, immunoprecipitated CCNO with anti-myc and measured associated histone H1 phosphorylation; expression/loading controls were reported. Loss of immunoprecipitate activity is not loss of intrinsic CCNO catalysis. The authors call these CDK-dead CCNO versions, but kinase-dead CCNO is misleading shorthand and is not used for the scientific conclusion. Their failure to rescue mouse brain multiciliation is reported separately from the human-cell work. Supplementary figure pixels were not inspected. The authors explicitly leave the physiological CDK partner and substrates in multiciliated cells unresolved. [PMID:39740663, DOI:10.1016/j.celrep.2024.115117]

### Multiciliation and the distinction from mitosis

The complete normal abstract of PMID:24747639 describes recessive variants in 16 affected individuals, patient-cell defects in mother-centriole generation/placement and greatly reduced numbers of motile cilia. Residual cilia retained axonemal motors and did not show obvious beating defects. The human findings are distinct from the separate Xenopus morpholino experiment. The abstract explicitly reports CCNO in apical cytoplasm. The full original paper was not claimed accessible. [PMID:24747639]

The 2015 mouse study provides deuterosome and centriole-amplification context, but its evidence is not described as human. Selected official indexed Results and Methods were read; a direct PMC request encountered a browser challenge. Normal source recovery will establish the final cache flag and permit further inspection. [PMID:25712475, DOI:10.15252/embj.201490805]

The 2025 human experiment used an H9 embryonic stem-cell line with a homozygous 14-bp CCNO deletion, producing a truncated product. WT and mutant cells could generate lung progenitors/airway basal cells and acquire early MCC markers FOXJ1, RFX2 and RFX3, but mutant cells failed to produce normal SAS6-positive procentrioles and multiple cilia. Patient airway TEM findings corroborated impaired centriole amplification. These are differentiation-progression defects, not evidence that CCNO initially specifies epithelial fate. The tagged human c.431_444del constructs used in separate HEK293T expression checks are distinguished from endogenous H9-derived airway measurements. No figure pixels, supplementary patient tables or raw images were reanalyzed. [PMID:39740663]

In mouse brain MCCs, the same paper places CCNO around entry into an S-like differentiation phase, reports nuclear enrichment during early amplification/growth, and examines p27 and RB phosphorylation as stage markers. This variant amplifies centrioles without DNA replication or mitosis. Mouse tracheal tissues partially compensate for CCNO absence, unlike mouse brain and human airway cells; the proposed alternative-cyclin compensation is not experimentally settled. These observations support the existing multiciliated epithelial differentiation and cilium-assembly annotations, but do not establish ordinary mitotic G1/S activity. Conversely, they do not disprove a mitotic role in another context. The mitotic IBA remains UNDECIDED without reconstruction of its original IBD placement.

GO:1903251 currently has GO:0160108 among its ancestors through epithelial differentiation/development. The broad animal-development IEA is therefore retained as non-core, rather than rejected because the immediate experiment concerns cells. GO:0060271 and GO:1903251 are not ancestors of one another in the inspected current ontology. Both existing processes can describe distinct aspects of this single regulatory activity. No NEW molecular function or biological process is proposed.

### Localization and the generic interaction

PMID:28860486 actually assayed CCNO despite emphasizing CNTD2 in its title. Selected complete human Results and immunohistochemistry/antibody-validation Methods were read from the full normal cache. Six paired tumor/adjacent-normal samples supported CCNO nucleolar concentration. The reported general increase in CCNO protein did not meet statistical significance in the larger paired-tissue analysis. No nucleolar enzymatic activity or oncogenic-driver role is inferred. This target-specific human localization corroborates the original nucleolar IDA, HPA mapping and UniProt vocabulary IEA. It also supports the broader nuclear compartment; these remain non-core localization assertions.

The broad cytoplasmic rows are supported by the human multiciliation study. Target self-accessions in the nucleus/cytoplasm IBA descendant evidence are legitimate experimental grounding, not circularity. The original PAINT node history for microtubule-organizing-center activity was not reconstructed. Centriole defects alone do not locate CCNO at that center, so this site-of-action assertion remains UNDECIDED without asserting a conflicting localization.

The exact HuRI partner Q08117-2 is preserved. The normal reviewed UniProt entry lists the same CCNO–TLE5 isoform-2 edge with three experiments and IntAct records EBI-2556878/EBI-11741437. Selected cached HuRI Methods establish the binary-screen and pairwise-verification design. The target supplement row and the specific assay configuration have not been independently inspected. The curated edge is retained as KEEP_AS_NON_CORE under the explicit ClinGen project binding override in projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md, without inventing a TLE5-dependent transcriptional mechanism or finer binding class.
