CC   -!- FUNCTION: Costimulatory molecule that belongs to the immunoglobulin
CC       superfamily that plays an important role in T-lymphocyte activation
CC       (PubMed:12196291, PubMed:7694363). Acts as the primary auxiliary signal
CC       augmenting the MHC/TCR signal in naive T-cells by acting as a ligand
CC       for the CD28 receptor which is constitutively expressed on the cell
CC       surface of T-cells (PubMed:12196291, PubMed:7694363). May play a
CC       critical role in the early events of T-cell activation and
CC       costimulation of naive T-cells, such as deciding between immunity and
CC       anergy that is made by T-cells within 24 hours after activation
CC       (PubMed:7527824). Also involved in the regulation of B cells function,
CC       plays a role in regulating the level of IgG(1) produced. Upon CD40
CC       engagement, activates NF-kappa-B signaling pathway via phospholipase C
CC       and protein kinase C activation (By similarity). Also acts as an
CC       inhibitor of T-cell activation by acting as a ligand for CTLA4, a decoy
CC       receptor, thereby blocking CD28-mediated T-cell priming
CC       (PubMed:11279501). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P42082,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11279501, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12196291,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7527824, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7694363}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 2]: Interferes with the formation of CD86 clusters,
CC       and thus acts as a negative regulator of T-cell activation.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:7527824}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: (Microbial infection) Acts as a receptor for adenovirus
CC       subgroup B. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16920215}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:12606712). Interacts with MARCH8
CC       (PubMed:12582153). Interacts (via cytoplasmic domain) with PHB1 and
CC       PHB2; the interactions increases after priming with CD40 (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P42082,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12582153, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12606712}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: (Microbial infection) Interacts with adenovirus subgroup b
CC       fiber protein. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16920215}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: (Microbial infection) Interacts with Orthopoxvirus OPG038/M2
CC       protein, inhibiting the interaction with CTLA4 and CD28.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30918073}.
