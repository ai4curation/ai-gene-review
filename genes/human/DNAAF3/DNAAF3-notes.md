# DNAAF3 review notes

## 2026-10-17

The initial review preserves all eight normally seeded source dictionaries, covering nine raw QuickGO records. Two dated ISS records project to the same stored DynAP assertion and are collapsed by the existing ETL, not by manual curation. Three localization rows are accepted; five assembly rows are refined to outer and inner arm assembly. There are five official predicted products, one integrated assembly core and no NEW annotation.

Human respiratory-cell dynein-arm loss, zebrafish phenotypes and Chlamydomonas rescue/complex experiments in PMID:22387996 establish the assembly work. The full original Methods were read separately from the incomplete normal cache. In particular, Figure 7 HA-IC2 co-immunoprecipitation retains HCα/β with reduced HCγ in pf22; the phenotype is not absence of all heavy/intermediate-chain association. Chlamydomonas inner arm f is retained. Tagged PF22 localization is algal; no human localization experiment or universal isoform function is inferred.

The DynAP donor is literal Xenopus Q32NQ7, with direct IDA from PMID:30561330. Original Figure 2C and the Methods on Xenopus cDNA cloning/imaging provide the positive source evidence. The human supplemental staining targets other proteins. Actual PAINT descent and the family/SL mappings are verified. Current IBD and QuickGO donor lists differ in the presence of zebrafish; both support the same node/terms, and neither donor count nor target experimental grounding is treated as circularity.

The independent core consultation and owner source review agree on a single outer/inner dynein-arm maturation unit. PMID:38261620 strongly supports the candidate arginine methyltransferase model through structure and algal mutant proteomics. PMID:39969973 provides comparative limits and explicitly unresolved catalytic versus noncatalytic assembly mechanisms. A purified-human or catalytic-dead rescue experiment is not reported in these evaluated sources. The candidate mechanism remains in the biological description and a BIOLOGY knowledge gap; no precise type-II activity, direct motor activity or autonomous chaperone function is assigned.

Five official product sequences were independently reconstructed. Named isoform 2 is accession Q8N9W5-5 and has a predicted NMD caution. Product -6 is a 47-residue N-terminal extension, so historical Leu108 corresponds to canonical Leu61. Historical numbering and sequence compatibility are not natural-product functional equivalence.

Genuine normal caching ultimately succeeded for the newer primary papers after retained rate-limit failures. PMID:30561330 is an exact reuse of a genuine normal cache shared with the DNAAF11/DNAAF2 work. Existing main sources, machine outputs and the frozen bootstrap remain unchanged.
