## 2026-11-28 NGF family review

Perplexity deep research was unavailable for this family, so this review used manual
triage of the fetched GOA rows, the human UniProt record, cached publications, Reactome
events, and the PTHR11589 PAINT node file.

BDNF is the TrkB-biased human member of PTHR11589. The human record and the JBC
processing paper, PMID:11152678, support a secreted neurotrophin precursor that can be
cleaved in the trans-Golgi network or immature secretory vesicles to produce mature BDNF.
PMID:19467646 provides direct human extracellular evidence by detecting mature and proBDNF
in whole saliva.

The exact mouse-derived localization rows under axon, dendrite, presynapse, glutamatergic
synapse and neuronal dense-core vesicle were not verified from accessible evidence for
human BDNF and are left UNDECIDED. The broader secretory-vesicle row is retained as
peripheral because PMID:11152678 explicitly places precursor cleavage in immature
secretory vesicles.

The PAINT family nodes for extracellular region, growth factor activity, receptor tyrosine
kinase signaling, neuronal survival and synaptic modulation fit BDNF biology. The
GO:0038180 nerve growth factor signaling pathway IBA is not a BDNF pathway and is marked
REMOVE with a family-level TERM_SCOPING_PROBLEM.
