# Manual deep research: VEGFB

Perplexity was unavailable during preparation of this VEGF-family review, so this file records the manual literature synthesis used to check VEGFB rows.

- PMID:9751730 shows that human VEGF-B binds selectively to FLT1/VEGFR1 and stimulates plasminogen-activator pathway changes in endothelial cells, supporting extracellular VEGFR1 ligand activity and VEGF receptor signaling.
- PMID:20228789 identifies a VEGFB-specific role in endothelial fatty-acid uptake mediated by endothelial VEGFR1 and neuropilin-1, rather than a canonical VEGFA-like sprouting angiogenesis role.
- PMID:19275959 directly reports chemotaxis of human lung mast cells and mast-cell lines toward VEGF-B167, supporting the mast-cell chemotaxis branch as peripheral inflammatory biology.
- PMID:19483306 supports an extracellular tenascin-X/VEGFB interaction but not a more specific molecular function than receptor binding.
- PMID:32296183 and PMID:40205054 are large-scale interaction-map papers whose exact KLHL12-VEGFB pair-specific validation was not resolved here.
