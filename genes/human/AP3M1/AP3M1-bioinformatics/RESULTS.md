## What this shows

- The AP-3 medium subunit carries a cargo-binding site: 10 AP3M1 residues contact the bound LAMP1 YxxPhi peptide in the human AP-3 cryo-EM structure.
- It is the *same* site as the AP-2 one: 9 of those 10 residues fall on alignment columns that are also AP2M1 cargo-pocket positions in the mu2/TGN38 crystal structure.
- The site is structurally conserved but chemically divergent: AP3M1 is identical to AP2M1 at only 5 of 14 pocket positions while aligning to a residue at 14 of 14, which is the structural basis for the different YxxPhi preferences of the mu subunits.
- Mouse Ap3m1 matches human AP3M1 at 14 of 14 of these positions, so the ISS/IEA transfers GOA makes from mouse Ap3m1 to AP3M1 are not crossing a diverged cargo-binding site.
