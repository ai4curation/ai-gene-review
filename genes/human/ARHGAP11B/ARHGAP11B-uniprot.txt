CC   -!- FUNCTION: Hominin-specific protein that promotes development and
CC       evolutionary expansion of the brain neocortex (PubMed:25721503,
CC       PubMed:27957544, PubMed:30484771, PubMed:32554627, PubMed:33938018).
CC       Able to promote amplification of basal progenitors in the
CC       subventricular zone, producing more neurons during fetal
CC       corticogenesis, thereby playing a key role in neocortex expansion
CC       (PubMed:25721503). Promotes the proliferation of basal progenitors by
CC       inhibiting the mitochondrial permeability transition pore (mPTP):
CC       delays the opening of the mPTP via interaction with ADP:ATP
CC       translocase, thereby increasing mitochondrial Ca(2+) concentration and
CC       inducing glutamine catabolism, which is required for basal progenitor
CC       proliferation (PubMed:31883789). Does not possess GTPase activator
CC       activity: the absence of GTPase activator activity is required to
CC       promote amplification of basal progenitors during neocortex development
CC       (PubMed:25721503, PubMed:27957544). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25721503,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27957544, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30484771,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31883789, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32554627,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33938018}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with ADP:ATP translocase components SLC25A4/ANT1 and
CC       SLC25A5/ANT2. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31883789}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion matrix
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31883789}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Specifically expressed in radial glia cells of
CC       cortical neural stem and progenitor cells (NPCs) during neocortex
CC       development. Preferentially expressed in apical and basal radial glia
CC       (PubMed:25721503). Not detected in cortical neurons and cortical plate
CC       (PubMed:25721503). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25721503}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: When expressed in embryonic mouse neocortex, promotes
CC       basal progenitor generation and self-renewal, and can increase cortical
CC       plate area and induce gyrification (PubMed:25721503, PubMed:33938018).
CC       Mice exhibit increased neocortical size and upper-layer neuron numbers
CC       persisting into adulthood (PubMed:33938018). Moreover, mice display
CC       altered neurobehavior, characterized by an increased memory flexibility
CC       and a reduced anxiety level (PubMed:33938018). When expressed in the
CC       developing neocortex of the gyrencephalic ferret, strongly increases
CC       proliferative basal radial glia, a progenitor cell type thought to be
CC       instrumental for neocortical expansion, resulting in extension of the
CC       neurogenic period and an increase in upper-layer neurons
CC       (PubMed:30484771). As consequence, the postnatal ferret neocortex
CC       displays increased neuron density in the upper cortical layers and
CC       expands in both the radial and tangential dimensions (PubMed:30484771).
CC       Expression in fetal neocortex of the common marmoset increases the
CC       numbers of upper-layer neurons, promoting enlargement of the neocortex
CC       and inducing its folding (PubMed:32554627).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25721503, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30484771,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32554627, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33938018}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: ARHGAP11B arose from partial duplication of ARHGAP11A on
CC       the human lineage after separation from the chimpanzee lineage, but
CC       before the divergence from Neandertals. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25721503,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28580430}.
CC   -!- CAUTION: Although it contains a Rho-GAP domains, does not have GTPase
CC       activator activity (PubMed:25721503, PubMed:27957544). The absence of
CC       GTPase activator activity is required to promote amplification of basal
CC       progenitors (PubMed:27957544). Restoring this activity abolishes
CC       ability to promote amplification of basal progenitors during neocortex
CC       development (PubMed:27957544). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25721503,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27957544}.
