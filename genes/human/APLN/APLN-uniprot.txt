CC   -!- FUNCTION: Peptide hormone that functions as endogenous ligand for the G
CC       protein-coupled apelin receptor (APLNR/APJ), that plays a role in
CC       cadiovascular homeostasis (PubMed:10525157, PubMed:22810587,
CC       PubMed:35817871, PubMed:38428423). Functions as a balanced agonist
CC       activating both G(i) protein pathway and beta-arrestin pathway of APLNR
CC       (PubMed:22810587, PubMed:38428423). Downstream G proteins activation,
CC       apelin can inhibit cAMP production and activate key intracellular
CC       effectors such as ERKs (PubMed:22810587, PubMed:35817871,
CC       PubMed:38428423). On the other hand, APLNR activation induces beta-
CC       arrestin recruitment to the membrane leading to desensitization and
CC       internalization of the receptor (PubMed:22810587, PubMed:38428423).
CC       Apelin blunts cardiac hypertrophic induction from APLNR on response to
CC       pathological stimuli, but also induces myocardial hypertrophy under
CC       normal conditions (PubMed:22810587, PubMed:38428423). Apelin-36
CC       dissociates more hardly than (pyroglu)apelin-13 from APLNR (By
CC       similarity). Involved in the regulation of cardiac precursor cell
CC       movements during gastrulation and heart morphogenesis (By similarity).
CC       Has an inhibitory effect on cytokine production in response to T-cell
CC       receptor/CD3 cross-linking; the oral intake of apelin in the colostrum
CC       and the milk might therefore modulate immune responses in neonates (By
CC       similarity). Plays a role in early coronary blood vessels formation (By
CC       similarity). Mediates myocardial contractility in an ERK1/2-dependent
CC       manner (By similarity). May also have a role in the central control of
CC       body fluid homeostasis by influencing vasopressin release and drinking
CC       behavior (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q4TTN8,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R0R3, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9R0R4,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10525157, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22810587,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35817871, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38428423}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: (Microbial infection) Endogenous ligand for the apelin
CC       receptor (APLNR), an alternative coreceptor with CD4 for HIV-1
CC       infection (PubMed:11090199). Inhibits HIV-1 entry in cells coexpressing
CC       CD4 and APLNR (PubMed:11090199). Apelin-36 has a greater inhibitory
CC       activity on HIV infection than other synthetic apelin derivatives
CC       (PubMed:11090199). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11090199}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9TUI9}.
CC       Secreted, extracellular space {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22810587,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38428423}. Note=Abundantly secreted in the
CC       colostrum. Lower level in milk. Decreases rapidly within several days
CC       after parturition in milk, but is still detectable even in commercial
CC       milk. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9TUI9}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the brain with highest levels in the
CC       frontal cortex, thalamus, hypothalamus and midbrain (PubMed:10617103).
CC       Secreted by the mammary gland into the colostrum and the milk.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10617103}.
CC   -!- PTM: Several active peptides may be produced by proteolytic processing
CC       of the peptide precursor. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9TUI9}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the apelin family. {ECO:0000305}.
