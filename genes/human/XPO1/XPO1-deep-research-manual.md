# XPO1 manual synthesis

XPO1/CRM1 is a RanGTP-dependent karyopherin export receptor in the exportin-1 branch of PTHR11223. It recognizes leucine-rich nuclear export signal cargos, forms export complexes with RanGTP in the nucleus, moves them through the nuclear pore, and releases them in the cytoplasm after RanGTP hydrolysis.

Core function: XPO1 is the CRM1 export receptor for leucine-rich nuclear export signal cargos; it binds RanGTP and NES-containing cargos in the nucleus, carries them through the nuclear pore, and releases them in the cytoplasm after RanGTP hydrolysis.

PMID:9368044 cloned human CRM1, localized a human CRM1-GFP fusion to the nucleus, and showed leptomycin B-sensitive export of an NES reporter. PMID:12724356 and PMID:12773398 support CRM1-dependent ribosomal subunit export, including direct RanGTP-dependent binding of human NMD3 to CRM1 and a requirement for CRM1 in 40S and 60S export. PMID:22250199 places the cofactor NLP1 in CRM1/RanGTP/NES cargo complex assembly, and PMID:29666234 shows POST-dependent CRM1 export of UBQLN4-bound polyubiquitinated substrates.

The same export cycle gives XPO1 experimentally supported presence in the nucleoplasm, cytosol, nucleus and cytoplasm; UniProt additionally records Cajal body and nucleolus residence during U3 snoRNA and ribosomal RNP export.

Human XPO1 also exports pre-ribosomal subunits, selected viral and cellular ribonucleoprotein cargos, FMRP-associated mRNAs, and UBQLN4-bound polyubiquitinated substrates, but these are cargo-specific branches of the core RanGTP-dependent CRM1 export mechanism.

Generic protein-containing-complex and ribonucleoprotein-complex rows describe true CRM1 cargo-export assemblies but are too cargo-agnostic to be core functional summaries.

GOA rows that transfer rat Xpo1 response-to-salt, triglyceride, xenobiotic, transcription-factor-binding and protein-domain-binding annotations to human XPO1 overstate cargo-context biology as functions of the general export receptor.

A high-throughput membrane row was treated as over-annotation: XPO1 is a soluble karyopherin that shuttles through nuclear pores and between soluble nuclear and cytoplasmic compartments, not an integral or resident membrane protein.

The old annulate-lamellae and kinetochore rows were kept as peripheral cellular-component observations rather than as core sites of CRM1 cargo loading.

Rows that assert only GO:0005515 protein binding were removed under the bare-protein-binding policy because they do not name a cargo-specific XPO1 molecular function.
