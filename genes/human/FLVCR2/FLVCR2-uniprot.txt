CC   -!- FUNCTION: Choline uniporter that specifically mediates choline uptake
CC       at the blood-brain-barrier (PubMed:38302740, PubMed:38778100,
CC       PubMed:40972734). Responsible for the majority of choline uptake across
CC       the blood-brain-barrier from the circulation into the brain (By
CC       similarity). Choline, a nutrient critical for brain development, is a
CC       precursor of phosphatidylcholine, as well as betaine (By similarity).
CC       Also mediates transport of ethanolamine (PubMed:38778100). Choline and
CC       ethanolamine transport is not coupled with proton transport and is
CC       exclusively driven by the choline gradient across the plasma membrane
CC       (PubMed:38778100). However, the presence of an inwardly directed proton
CC       gradient enhances choline uptake (By similarity). Also acts as a heme b
CC       transporter (PubMed:20823265, PubMed:32973183). Required to regulate
CC       mitochondrial respiration processes, ATP synthesis and thermogenesis
CC       (PubMed:32973183). At low heme levels, interacts with components of
CC       electron transfer chain (ETC) complexes and ATP2A2, leading to
CC       ubiquitin-mediated degradation of ATP2A2 and inhibition of
CC       thermogenesis (PubMed:32973183). Upon heme binding, dissociates from
CC       ETC complexes to allow switching from mitochondrial ATP synthesis to
CC       thermogenesis (PubMed:32973183). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q91X85,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20823265, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32973183,
