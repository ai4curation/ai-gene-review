Gene Description

C1QA is the A chain of the secreted complement recognition protein C1q. Six copies each of the A, B and C chains assemble into collagen-like stalks and heterotrimeric globular heads. The A-chain globular module binds immunoglobulins, and assembled C1q recognizes clustered IgG or IgM on target surfaces. Within the C1 complex, this recognition couples to activation of the associated C1r and C1s proteases and initiation of the classical complement cascade. C1QA also contributes to recognition of altered-self surfaces and receptor-mediated immune regulation by assembled C1q, including phosphatidylserine recognition and CD33 signaling. In mouse models, C1qa-dependent complement activity participates in synaptic remodeling and glial responses.
