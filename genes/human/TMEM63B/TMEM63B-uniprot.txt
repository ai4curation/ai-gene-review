CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mechanosensitive cation channel with low conductance and high
CC       activation threshold (PubMed:37543036, PubMed:38127458). Osmosensitive
CC       cation channel preferentially activated by hypotonic stress
CC       (PubMed:37543036, PubMed:38127458). Also acts as a phospholipid
CC       scramblase in response to changes in membrane structure: upon changes
CC       in membrane curvature and thickness, alters its conformation and
CC       translocates phospholipids, such as phosphatidylcholine and
CC       sphingomyelin, thereby controlling plasma membrane lipid distribution
CC       (PubMed:39217145, PubMed:39424995, PubMed:39716028). Forms a
CC       heterodimer with SLC19A2, which mediates phospholipid scramblase
CC       activity following Ca(2+) stimulation (By similarity). Expressed in
CC       excitatory neurons of the subfornical organ and functions as a thirst
CC       receptor that mediates neuronal response to hyperosmolality to drive
CC       thirst and drinking behavior (By similarity). Facilitates intestinal
CC       motility by promoting proliferation of intestinal stem cells (By
CC       similarity). Essential for the baby's first breath and respiration
CC       throughout life (PubMed:38127458). Upon lung inflation conducts cation
CC       currents in alveolar type 1 and 2 cells triggering lamellar body
CC       exocytosis and surfactant secretion into airspace (PubMed:38127458).
CC       Acts as an osmosensor in cochlear outer hair cells (OHCs) where it
CC       mediates calcium influx and regulatory volume decrease response (By
CC       similarity). Required for the maintenance of OHC morphology, OHC
CC       survival and normal hearing (By similarity).
