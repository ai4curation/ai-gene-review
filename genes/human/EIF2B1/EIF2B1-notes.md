# EIF2B1 review notes

## 2026-10-20: evidence and scope

EIF2B1 is the alpha subunit of eIF2B. The integrated core describes its structural and regulatory contribution to the cytosolic nucleotide-exchange complex and cytoplasmic translation initiation. Purified human alpha homodimers and reconstitution with the other four subunits in PMID:29599213 provide participation evidence. Alpha-free complexes retain activity, and isolated alpha is not assigned autonomous exchange or hydrolase activity. PMID:27023709 includes a human translation comparator within a study whose detailed structural work concerns Schizosaccharomyces pombe; the translation endpoint is distinguished from a direct alpha-only GDP-exchange assay.

The distinction is not a requirement that a catalytic protein function in isolation.
