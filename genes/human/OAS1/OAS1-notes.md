# OAS1 notes

OAS1 is the single-OAS-domain, dsRNA-activated 2-5A synthetase. Reviewed OAS1,
OAS2 and OAS3 all keep GO:0001730, while OASL keeps the curated NOT row because
its OAS-like domain binds dsRNA but does not synthesize 2-5A
[PMID:9826176, "it was devoid of 2'-5'OAS activity typical of the"].

OAS1 has two major cellular forms. The p42 isoform remains diffuse, while p46 is
prenylated and targeted to membranous SARS-CoV-2 replication organelles, where it
blocks viral replication [PMID:34581622, "A prenylated dsRNA sensor protects
against severe COVID-19."]. The broad `membrane` row is therefore true but kept
as non-core beside the more central cytosolic and p46 endomembrane functions,
whereas mitochondrial and nuclear fraction rows are retained as non-core pools.

PTHR11258's root 2-5A synthetase and dsRNA-binding IBD rows work for OAS1/OAS2/OAS3,
but the root membrane and nucleoplasm rows need pruning from OAS3/OASL and
OAS2/OASL respectively. The type-I-IFN and IL-27 PAINT process rows overstate
OAS proteins as pathway members when they are induced antiviral effectors.
