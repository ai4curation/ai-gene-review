# Human MAP2K5/MEK5 (UniProt Q13163): Functional-Annotation Research Report

## Executive summary

The requested target is unambiguous: **human MAP2K5 encodes dual-specificity mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase 5 (MEK5/MKK5; UniProt Q13163)**. The canonical protein is reported as 448 amino acids and approximately 50.1 kDa, with an N-terminal PB1 interaction domain and a C-terminal STE-family protein-kinase domain. These features agree with the supplied UniProt identity and InterPro domain assignments; literature concerning other genes or organisms was excluded. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 64-68, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 64-68)

MEK5 has an unusually narrow established physiological specificity. Activated principally by MEKK2/MAP3K2 and MEKK3/MAP3K3, it phosphorylates the activation-loop **TEY motif of ERK5/MAPK7 at Thr218 and Tyr220**. ERK5 remains the only firmly established direct physiological substrate of MEK5. MEK5 therefore functions primarily as the dedicated MAP2K of the **MEKK2/3–MEK5–ERK5 signaling module**, rather than as a broadly acting kinase. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 68-72, gil2023mapkinaseerk5 pages 50-53, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 68-72)

The strongest physiological interpretation is that MEK5 couples mitogenic and mechanical/stress inputs to ERK5-dependent responses, especially endothelial survival, flow sensing and vascular integrity. Cancer, drug resistance and metabolic associations are important research areas, but most such evidence concerns the entire MEK5–ERK5 axis rather than a uniquely demonstrated action of MAP2K5. Selective research inhibitors exist, but the reviewed evidence did not identify an approved MEK5-directed therapy or a selective MEK5 inhibitor with demonstrated efficacy in a human interventional trial. (drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 1-2, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 77-80, drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 11-12)

### Domain architecture

The canonical protein contains an N-terminal **PB1 domain**, approximately residues 18–109, followed by a kinase domain at approximately residues 166–409. This matches the supplied PB1-like/PB1_MAP2K5, kinase-like and dual-specificity MAPK-kinase annotations. Within the PB1 region, residues 18–25 have been associated with ERK5 binding and residues 64–68 with MAP3K2/3 binding, allowing MEK5 to connect an upstream MAP3K with its downstream MAPK through separable interfaces. Interactions with SQSTM1 and PARD6A have also been reported, although their importance to the core catalytic reaction is less established. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 64-68, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 64-68)

## 2. Primary molecular function and catalytic reaction

### Reaction

MEK5 is an ATP-dependent dual-specificity protein kinase. Its defining reaction can be summarized as:

**ATP + ERK5/MAPK7 → ADP + phosphorylated ERK5/MAPK7**

MEK5 phosphorylates both a threonine and a tyrosine in ERK5’s activation-loop **Thr-Glu-Tyr (TEY)** motif—Thr218 and Tyr220 in human ERK5. Evidence summarized from biochemical work indicates preferential phosphorylation of Thr218 first, followed by Tyr220; phosphorylation of both sites is required for full ERK5 catalytic activation. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 68-72, gil2023mapkinaseerk5 pages 50-53)

### Substrate specificity

The current evidence supports **ERK5/MAPK7 as the only firmly recognized direct physiological substrate of MEK5**, and MEK5 as the only recognized MAP2K that directly activates ERK5. Accordingly, downstream targets such as MEF2-family transcription factors should be annotated as ERK5 substrates or pathway outputs—not as direct MEK5 substrates. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 68-72, gil2023mapkinaseerk5 pages 50-53, hoang2017oncogenicsignalingof pages 1-5)

This narrow specificity is reinforced structurally by PB1-mediated docking. The PB1 scaffold raises the effective local concentration of MEKK2/3, MEK5 and ERK5 and helps insulate this cascade from canonical ERK1/2, JNK and p38 modules. (drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 2-4, hoang2017oncogenicsignalingof pages 1-5)

## 3. Activation mechanism and pathway position

MEK5 occupies the middle tier of a conventional three-kinase MAPK cascade:

**stimulus/receptor → MAP3K2/MEKK2 or MAP3K3/MEKK3 → MAP2K5/MEK5 → MAPK7/ERK5 → cytoplasmic and nuclear responses**.

MEKK2 and MEKK3 bind the MEK5 PB1 domain and phosphorylate MEK5 at **Ser311 and Thr315** within its kinase domain. MEKK2 is generally described as having greater MEK5-binding affinity and as a prominent activator, but upstream usage varies with stimulus and cell type. MEKK1 is not regarded as a corresponding MEK5 activator. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 68-72, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 68-72, drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 2-4)

Reported inputs include EGF and other growth factors, VEGF, PDGF, FGF-2, neurotrophins, serum, inflammatory cytokines, osmotic and oxidative stress, hypoxia, ischemia and fluid shear stress. WNK1 has been placed further upstream in some EGF-responsive contexts: WNK1 depletion reduced ERK5 activation, whereas WNK1 overexpression increased it in HEK293 cells. This is pathway-level evidence and does not make WNK1 a direct substitute for the established MEKK2/3-to-MEK5 activation step. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 68-72, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 68-72)

## 4. Cellular location and trafficking

MEK5 is reported to be **predominantly cytosolic**, consistent with its role in assembling upstream MAP3Ks and cytosolic ERK5 into a signaling complex. Its PB1 domain provides the key organizing interfaces in that compartment. (fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 64-68)

A critical distinction is that the prominent stimulus-dependent nuclear translocation described in this pathway is chiefly a property of **ERK5**, not proof that MEK5 itself becomes a nuclear kinase. Inactive ERK5 is retained in a compact cytosolic state; MEK5-dependent activation exposes ERK5 trafficking determinants and promotes ERK5 nuclear entry. Reported ERK5 trafficking regions include an NLS at residues 505–539 and an NES at 771–816. These sequences belong to ERK5 and must not be assigned to MAP2K5. (fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 64-68, fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 64-68, hoang2017oncogenicsignalingof pages 1-5)

Thus, the best-supported compartmental model is: **MEK5 executes its primary phosphorylation reaction mainly in the cytosolic signaling complex; activated ERK5 then carries part of the signal into the nucleus.**

## 5. Isoforms and structure–function evidence

Four alternatively spliced forms have been reported, although historical α/β nomenclature is not completely consistent across publications. The most studied comparison is between a full-length, approximately 50-kDa form and an approximately 40-kDa form lacking 89 N-terminal residues, including important PB1-region information. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 64-68, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 64-68)

In HEK293 transfection and co-immunoprecipitation experiments, both principal forms could associate with ERK5, but only the full-length form efficiently activated ERK5 and induced its nuclear translocation. The shortened form interfered with association of the activating form with ERK5 and behaved as a dominant-negative regulator. Phosphomimetic substitution at MEK5 Ser311/Thr315 activated ERK5 when introduced into the full-length form but not equivalently in the shortened form. These results demonstrate that activation-loop phosphorylation alone is insufficient for productive signaling when the required N-terminal docking architecture is absent. Cameron et al., published 9 January 2004; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M308755200. (cameron2004differentialroleof pages 1-1)

## 6. Biological processes and physiological interpretation

Activation of ERK5 by MEK5 has been linked to cell survival, proliferation, differentiation, motility, angiogenesis and stress adaptation. These broad phenotypes should be interpreted as outputs of the **MEK5–ERK5 module**, because many experiments directly perturb ERK5 rather than separately resolving MEK5’s contribution. (gil2023mapkinaseerk5 pages 50-53, drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 1-2)

The strongest physiological context is vascular endothelium. Laminar shear stress activates an atheroprotective endothelial program in which the MEK5–ERK5 axis contributes to mechanotransduction, survival, barrier integrity and anti-inflammatory transcription. Reviews regard this as a protective stress-response pathway not only in endothelium but also in mechanically challenged bone, cartilage and muscle. This creates an important therapeutic tension: inhibiting the axis may be useful in tumors that exploit it for survival, but systemic inhibition could impair normal vascular or tissue-protective signaling. (drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 1-2)

The cascade also responds to oxidative and osmotic stress, ischemia and hypoxia. These observations support annotation as a signaling integrator rather than as an enzyme serving a constitutive metabolic pathway. (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 68-72, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 68-72)

## 7. Disease relevance and quantitative evidence

### Cancer

Cancer literature commonly reports elevated expression or activation of the pathway. Historical quantitative findings include a **22-fold increase in MAP2K5 expression** in apoptosis-resistant MCF7 breast-carcinoma cells and approximately **4–5-fold higher MEK5 protein** in DU145 prostate-cancer cells than in comparator transformed lines; elevated expression was also observed in MCF7 and T47D cells. These are cellular-expression observations, not proof that MAP2K5 is an oncogenic driver in patients. (cameron2004differentialroleof pages 7-8)

A review reported strong MEK5 expression in more than one-third of prostate tumors and expression in over half of breast tumors in one study, while emphasizing that relationships among abundance, kinase activation, tumor morphology and prognosis remained incompletely established. The same caution applies to associations with metastasis, epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition and treatment resistance. Drew et al., January 2012; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbcan.2011.10.002. (drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 1-2)

Genetic or pharmacological disruption of upstream MEKK2 inhibited growth of MDA-MB-231 and BT474 xenografts and reduced triple-negative breast-cancer metastasis in experimental models. This supports vulnerability of the broader cascade, but it does not establish that selective inhibition of MEK5 itself will reproduce the result clinically. Hoang et al., January 2017; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.canlet.2017.01.034. (hoang2017oncogenicsignalingof pages 1-5)

### Human genetics and complex traits

Open Targets links MAP2K5 to smoking behavior, skeletal-system abnormalities, diabetes, type 2 diabetes and metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease, with displayed aggregate association scores of approximately **0.31–0.42**. These scores integrate heterogeneous evidence and should be treated as prioritization signals rather than proof of direct causation or therapeutic tractability. (OpenTargets Search: -MAP2K5)

## 8. Recent developments, emphasizing 2023–2024

Recent literature has expanded the biological setting of the pathway more than it has altered the core biochemical annotation.

### Human pluripotent stem cells

A 2024 study reported that ERK5 supports autocrine mitogenic balance and cell-fate specification in human pluripotent stem cells. The work supports a role for MEK5-dependent ERK5 activation in maintaining signaling and transcriptional programs, but the principal experimentally interrogated molecule was ERK5; it does not establish a new direct MAP2K5 substrate. Song et al., September 2024; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stemcr.2024.07.007.

### Breast-cancer network pharmacology

A 2024 systems-pharmacology analysis constructed perturbed protein-interaction networks and prioritized **genistein** among plant polyphenols, together with candidate combinations involving established breast-cancer drugs. This is a computational prioritization of interventions affecting the MEK5–ERK5 network, not evidence that genistein selectively binds MEK5 or that the combinations are clinically effective. Odongo et al., February 2024; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13040-024-00357-1.

### Coronary-artery-disease functional genomics

Schnitzler et al. integrated CAD GWAS, endothelial epigenomics, enhancer-to-gene maps and CRISPRi Perturb-seq in telomerase-immortalized human aortic endothelial cells. The screen targeted expressed genes within 500 kb of CAD signals, achieved an average **40% expression reduction**, identified **50 transcriptional programs**, and connected **41 genes at 43 CAD signals** to endothelial programs. It recovered a flow-response program and endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition, reinforcing the importance of endothelial mechanotransduction; however, the retrieved study sections did **not** provide a MAP2K5-specific perturbation result. It should therefore be cited as current contextual evidence, not direct validation of MAP2K5 as a CAD gene. Published February 2024; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07022-x. (schnitzler2024convergenceofcoronary pages 9-11, schnitzler2024convergenceofcoronary pages 11-14, schnitzler2024convergenceofcoronary pages 1-4)

The study’s broader quantitative results included strong support for the 41-gene set by PoPS (**rank-sum P = 2.5 × 10⁻53**). CCM2 knockdown increased GOSR2 by **122%** (P = 8.9 × 10⁻7), while GOSR2 knockdown increased an ER-stress program by **94%** and reduced a focal-adhesion/JUN program by **23%**. These data concern neighboring endothelial programs rather than direct MEK5 biochemistry. (schnitzler2024convergenceofcoronary pages 9-11)

## 9. Current applications and pharmacology

The principal real-world application is as a **research target and pathway biomarker**, using genetic perturbation, phospho-ERK5 readouts and small-molecule probes to investigate stress signaling, endothelial biology and tumor-cell adaptation.

BIX02188 and BIX02189 are commonly used MEK5 inhibitors. Reported biochemical IC50 values are **4.3 nM** and **1.5 nM**, respectively. Both suppress ERK5 phosphorylation and MEF2-driven transcription without inhibiting several tested control kinases, including MEK1, MEK2, ERK2 and JNK2; however, CSF1R is a reported off-target, and cellular or organismal selectivity cannot be inferred from a limited kinase panel. (fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 77-80, drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 11-12)

Older reagents require caution. U0126 can inhibit MEK5 dose-dependently, while PD98059 partially inhibits it, so studies using these compounds cannot automatically attribute effects solely to MEK1/2–ERK1/2. Conversely, XMD8-92, ERK5-IN-1, AX15836 and BAY-885 are primarily ERK5-directed compounds and should not be called MEK5 inhibitors. Some ERK5 kinase inhibitors can paradoxically promote ERK5 nuclear localization or transcriptional activity and may fail to phenocopy ERK5 genetic deletion. (fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 77-80, drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 12-14, drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 11-12)

No selective MEK5 inhibitor was identified as an approved drug or as having demonstrated efficacy in a human interventional trial in the evidence reviewed here. Consequently, therapeutic claims remain **preclinical**, and no clinically validated MAP2K5 biomarker, companion diagnostic or standard-of-care implementation can presently be assigned.

## 10. Expert assessment and annotation recommendations

1. **Primary function:** annotate MAP2K5 as the dedicated dual-specificity MAP2K that activates ERK5/MAPK7 by phosphorylation of Thr218 and Tyr220. This is substantially better supported than broad descriptions such as “regulates proliferation.” (fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 68-72, gil2023mapkinaseerk5 pages 50-53)
2. **Localization:** annotate the protein as predominantly cytosolic, acting in PB1-organized signaling complexes. Do not transfer ERK5’s NLS/NES or stimulus-dependent nuclear translocation to MEK5. (fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 64-68, fernandez2021resumendetesis. pages 64-68)
3. **Pathway:** the core module is MEKK2/3→MEK5→ERK5. Inputs and outputs are context dependent, but substrate specificity at the MEK5 step is unusually restricted. (fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 68-72, drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 2-4)
4. **Isoforms:** preserve isoform-specific annotations. The shortened N-terminal form may bind ERK5 yet inhibit productive signaling, demonstrating that physical association does not equal catalytic activation. Historical α/β labels should be reconciled against the exact transcript or UniProt isoform sequence used. (fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 64-68, cameron2004differentialroleof pages 1-1)
5. **Disease claims:** distinguish direct MAP2K5 evidence from ERK5-axis evidence. Expression correlations, GWAS links and pathway sensitivity do not by themselves establish causality or clinical actionability. (OpenTargets Search: -MAP2K5, drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 1-2)
6. **Therapeutic outlook:** the pathway is attractive in cancers that exploit stress-survival signaling, but endothelial and tissue-protective functions predict a potentially narrow safety window for systemic inhibition. Available inhibitors remain research tools, and clinical validation is absent in the retrieved record. (drew2012mek5erk5pathwaythe pages 1-2, fernandez2021resumendetesis.a pages 77-80)

## Conclusion

Human MAP2K5/Q13163 is a cytosolic, PB1-containing STE-family MAP2K whose precise function is to activate ERK5/MAPK7 through dual phosphorylation of its TEY activation loop. MEKK2 and MEKK3 activate MEK5 at Ser311/Thr315, while PB1-mediated assembly confers pathway specificity. The protein’s best-supported physiological role is as the central relay of a mitogen- and stress-responsive pathway, particularly in endothelial mechanotransduction and tissue-protective signaling. Cancer and complex-trait associations are biologically plausible and experimentally active areas, but translation remains preclinical. The central annotation should therefore emphasize the highly specific **MEKK2/3–MEK5–ERK5 reaction**, with broader phenotypes and therapeutic claims recorded as context-dependent pathway consequences rather than intrinsic molecular functions.
