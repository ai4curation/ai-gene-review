# ID4 notes

Automated Falcon and Perplexity-lite deep research were unavailable in this runtime, as recorded in `ID1-notes.md`;
these notes summarize the cached primary literature, UniProt record, Reactome summaries, and PANTHER PAINT data used
for the manual review.

## Evidence reviewed

- **Conserved ID-family mechanism.** ID4 is on `PTHR11723` node `PTN000919548` with ID1, ID2 and ID3 for the
  conserved nuclear HLH transcription-regulator inhibitor mechanism. The node has no IRD or IKR loss on the human
  ID4 lineage.
- **Direct human ID4 mechanism.** Pagliuca et al. cloned human ID4, noted that it lacks the basic region found in
  DNA-binding bHLH transcription factors, and showed that ID4 inhibited MyoD-dependent transactivation from the
  muscle creatine kinase E-box enhancer [PMID:7665172].
- **Nucleoplasm.** Reactome `R-HSA-9612534` supports nucleoplasmic ID2 and ID4 in the TGF-beta receptor pathway;
  the row is treated as localization support rather than as a distinct core TGF-beta signaling function. HPA also
  contributes an ID4 nucleoplasm localization row that agrees with the nuclear function.
