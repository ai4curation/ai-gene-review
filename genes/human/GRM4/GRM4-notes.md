## Function
- Group III metabotropic glutamate receptor mGluR4; class C GPCR coupled to Gi/o, inhibiting adenylyl cyclase.
  Human mGluR4 in CHO cells: agonists "reduction of forskolin-stimulated cyclic AMP (cAMP)" with L-AP4 EC50 0.2 uM
  [PMID:8738157]; group III cloning and pharmacology [PMID:9473604].
- Dual signaling: "inhibition of cAMP formation and activation of the mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK)
  pathway"; GRK2 desensitizes MAPK arm [PMID:15102938].
- Forms homodimers and mGlu2-mGlu4 heterodimers [PMID:37286794].
- Interacts with PICK1 (UniProt). Predominantly presynaptic autoreceptor; strongly expressed in cerebellum.

## Localization
- Multi-pass (7TM) cell-membrane protein with large extracellular Venus flytrap glutamate-binding domain. Cell
