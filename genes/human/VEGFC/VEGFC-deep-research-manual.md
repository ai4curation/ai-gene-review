# Manual deep research: VEGFC

Perplexity was unavailable during preparation of this VEGF-family review, so this file records the manual literature synthesis used to check VEGFC rows.

- PMID:8617204 identifies human VEGF-C as a ligand for FLT4/VEGFR3 and KDR/VEGFR2, with receptor tyrosine autophosphorylation and endothelial-cell migration activity.
- PMID:9233800 shows that proteolytic processing changes VEGFC activity and receptor specificity, with mature VEGF-C activating endothelial migration, proliferation and vascular permeability.
- PMID:20145116 and PMID:23878260 provide structural support for VEGFC binding to VEGFR2 and VEGFR3, respectively.
- PMID:19275959 supports VEGFC-induced human mast-cell chemotaxis as a specialized inflammatory output.
- PMID:19147132, PMID:19412173, PMID:16618418, and PMID:26934950 are the rodent source papers behind several GO_REF:0000107 transfers. Those rows are kept as non-core because they are experimentally grounded but describe endometriosis, high-salt blood-pressure, cholangiocyte, or mesenchymal stem-cell contexts outside core human VEGFC receptor-ligand biology.
