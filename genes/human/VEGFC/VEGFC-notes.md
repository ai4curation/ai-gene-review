# VEGFC manual review notes

Manual synthesis was used for this review because Perplexity deep research was unavailable during PR preparation.

## Core biology

VEGFC is a secreted VEGF-family precursor. Proteolytic processing trims the N- and C-terminal propeptides around the VEGF homology domain and changes receptor specificity: partially processed species act mainly through VEGFR3/FLT4, whereas fully processed VEGFC can also activate VEGFR2/KDR.

PMID:8617204 purified and cloned human VEGFC as the Flt4/VEGFR3 ligand and showed that it induces tyrosine autophosphorylation of VEGFR3 and VEGFR2 and stimulates bovine capillary endothelial-cell migration. PMID:9233800 showed that stepwise proteolysis increases activity toward VEGFR3, that only fully processed VEGFC activates VEGFR2, and that mature VEGFC increases vascular permeability, endothelial migration and endothelial proliferation.

The later structural papers map the two core receptor interfaces: PMID:20145116 crystallized VEGFC with the VEGFR2 high-affinity site and PMID:23878260 crystallized VEGFC with VEGFR3 domains D1-D2 and analyzed VEGFR3 activation. Together these support VEGFR2/VEGFR3 binding and downstream VEGF receptor signaling.

## Peripheral and unsupported rows

PMID:19275959 showed that VEGFC induces human mast-cell chemotaxis. That supports chemoattractant and mast-cell chemotaxis rows but as an inflammatory cell context, not the central VEGFR3 lymphangiogenic role.

Several IEA rows come from mouse or rat phenotypes and name very specific organismal readouts, such as xenobiotic response, osteoblast differentiation, blood pressure, epithelial-cell proliferation, protein secretion, and mesenchymal stem-cell proliferation. The current human review keeps the direct receptor-ligand mechanism and avoids elevating those phenotypic transfers to core human VEGFC functions.
