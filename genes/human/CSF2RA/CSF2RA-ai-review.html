Gene Description

CSF2RA encodes the ligand-recognizing alpha subunit of the granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (GM-CSF) receptor. Its membrane form binds GM-CSF and assembles with the common beta subunit CSF2RB to form a signaling receptor that supports myeloid-cell proliferation and activation through pathways including JAK-STAT. Alternative splicing produces forms with different cytoplasmic tails or extracellular and membrane regions, including a soluble ligand-binding receptor. Defective GM-CSF receptor responses can impair pulmonary surfactant homeostasis and cause hereditary pulmonary alveolar proteinosis.

Alu-insertion receptor with increased ectodomain release SPLICE VARIANT
ID: CSF2RA_ALU_RELEASE_PRONE
UNIPROT ISOFORM: P15509-7
The 434-aa Alu-GMRalpha precursor contains a 34-aa extracellular juxtamembrane insertion. The complete PMID:17681666 abstract reports preserved ligand binding and preferential proteolytic ectodomain release in recombinant assays. The shedding is performed by proteases; this class does not assign protease activity or identify a native cleavage product sequence.
