## PAINT

All three IBDs occur at AN0/PTN002934155, ancestral to the exact human sequence. The retained human experimental annotations legitimately ground its self-donor; no family loss record was found in this snapshot.

## PMID:28814342

The donor study uses mouse conditional genetics and lineage markers. Septal Gsx2 loss reduces proliferative SVZ progenitors and olfactory interneurons; the authors discuss indirect effects on basal progenitor proliferation. This is conserved developmental evidence, not a direct human assay.
