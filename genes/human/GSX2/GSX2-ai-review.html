Gene Description

GSX2 is a homeodomain transcription factor that regulates gene expression in developing forebrain progenitors. It binds regulatory DNA and acts predominantly as a transcriptional repressor in human lateral ganglionic eminence-like progenitors. Its effects depend on cellular context and DNA-site organization. Recessive pathogenic variants disrupt basal ganglia development.
