Gene Description

ASNS is a cytosolic enzyme that makes L-asparagine from L-aspartate, glutamine and ATP. Its N-terminal glutaminase domain supplies nitrogen to the C-terminal synthetase domain, coupling glutamine hydrolysis to asparagine formation; human structural studies support a homodimer. The enzyme supplies asparagine for cellular metabolism and growth. Its expression responds to nutrient availability, and particular cancer-cell experiments link ASNS to survival during glucose deprivation and some drug treatments. Biallelic pathogenic variants cause asparagine synthetase deficiency, a severe neurodevelopmental disorder with microcephaly, developmental impairment and seizures.
