CC   -!- FUNCTION: Cell surface receptor that regulates diverse cellular
CC       processes including cell proliferation, differentiation, migration, and
CC       apoptosis (PubMed:12958365, PubMed:19416857). Initiates BMP, inhibin,
CC       and TGF-beta signaling pathways by interacting with different ligands
CC       including TGFB1, BMP2, BMP5, BMP7 or GDF5 (PubMed:18184661).
CC       Alternatively, acts as a cell surface coreceptor for BMP ligands,
CC       serving to enhance ligand binding by differentially regulating
CC       BMPR1A/ALK3 and BMPR1B/ALK6 receptor trafficking (PubMed:19726563).
CC       Promotes epithelial cell adhesion, focal adhesion formation and
CC       integrin signaling during epithelial cell spreading on fibronectin
CC       (PubMed:22562249). By interacting with the scaffolding protein beta-
CC       arrestin2/ARRB2, regulates migration or actin cytoskeleton and promotes
CC       the activation of CDC42 as well as the inhibition of NF-kappa-B
CC       (PubMed:19416857, PubMed:19325136). In gonadotrope cells, acts as an
CC       inhibin A coreceptor and regulates follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH)
CC       levels and female fertility (By similarity). Plays a role in the
CC       inhibition of directed and random cell migration in epithelial cells by
CC       altering the actin cytoskeletal organization (PubMed:19416857).
CC       Participates in epithelial-mesenchymal transformation (EMT) upon
CC       binding to BMP2 or TGFB2, by activating the PAR6/SMURF1/RHOA pathway
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P26342,
