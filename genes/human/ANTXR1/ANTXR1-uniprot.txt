CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a role in cell attachment and migration. Interacts with
CC       extracellular matrix proteins and with the actin cytoskeleton and
CC       thereby plays an important role in normal extracellular matrix (ECM)
CC       homeostasis. Mediates adhesion of cells to type 1 collagen and gelatin,
CC       reorganization of the actin cytoskeleton and promotes cell spreading.
CC       Plays a role in the angiogenic response of cultured umbilical vein
CC       endothelial cells. May also act as a receptor for PLAU. Upon ligand
CC       binding, stimulates the phosphorylation of EGFR and ERK1/2
CC       (PubMed:30241478). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15777794,
