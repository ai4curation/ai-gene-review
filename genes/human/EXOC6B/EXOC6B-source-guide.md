# EXOC6B: sources, products and assay boundaries

The Sec15B identity is important throughout this review. EXOC6B/Q9Y2D4 is distinct from EXOC6/Sec15A. A generic historical “Sec15” label is sufficient for neither a paralog-specific interaction nor an identical human mechanism. The displayed 811-residue sequence and the separate 672-residue UniProt product remain as supplied; the experiments below do not establish functional equivalence of those products.

The inspected main Methods/Results do not supply the recovered sec15 accession or distinguish the two human paralogs. The Sec15 antibody is referred to Yeaman 2003; the listed siRNA coordinates are for Sec5, Sec8 and snapin, not Sec15. Supplementary Figure S1 and movies were not accessed. Cytokinesis has positive process evidence with unresolved paralog attribution. Membrane fission has a second uncertainty: the paper places vesicle delivery/fusion before cell cleavage, without identifying an exocyst-performed step in scission. A structural or cofactor contribution could count as participation, but necessity for cytokinesis alone does not establish it.
