CC   -!- FUNCTION: Has a role in the formation and organization of the
CC       extracellular matrix. In the eye, it functions as a mediator of
CC       dexamethasone-induced matrix deposition in the trabecular meshwork, the
CC       tissue responsible for the outflow of the ocular aqueous humor and for
CC       the maintenance of intraocular pressure (PubMed:21199193). Is a
CC       negative regulator of angiogenesis in the cornea, and plays a major
CC       role in maintaining corneal avascularity and transparency
CC       (PubMed:25622036). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21199193,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25622036}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotetramer; disulfide-linked. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11682471}.

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Highly expressed in the cornea (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:11682471). Expression is restricted to the stromal layer
CC       (PubMed:11682471). Also detected at the junction between the corneal
CC       stromal layer and the conjuctiva. Not detected in the sclera
