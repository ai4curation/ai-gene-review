## PMID:18515362 — Dual roles of Gln137 of actin revealed by recombinant human cardiac muscle alpha-actin mutants.

## PMID:22590617 — Subdomain location of mutations in cardiac actin correlate with type of functional change.

Full cached Methods/Results/Discussion inspected; PubMed verifies title/PMID/DOI. Mutations in human ACTC cDNA; recombinant baculovirus/Sf9 proteins purified with DNase-I affinity. Polymerization fluorescence includes2.5% pyrene-labelled alpha-skeletal actin tracer. Nucleotide/phosphate assays are actin assays, not myosin ATPase.

Pi-release kinetics combine ATP hydrolysis, release and filament turnover; they are not isolated chemistry rate constants. R312H instability and phalloidin stabilization may influence comparative motility. DNase-I inhibition reports folding; do not infer chaperone activity because CCT folds actin.

## PMID:37009486 — Mutagenic analysis of actin reveals the mechanism of His161 flipping that triggers ATP hydrolysis.

Human cardiac WT/A108G/P109A/Q137A in baculovirus/Sf9 retain16-aa N-terminal Strep-tag extension; Physarum fragmin F1 expressed inE.coli stabilizes F-form actin. Structural active-site evidence concerns intrinsic actin-bound ATP, not motor stepping. The authors describe the Q137A structure/low-ATPase discrepancy and propose water fluctuations; this is an interpretation, not a direct fluctuation measurement. No new whole-cell developmental claim follows.
