# DST source and evidence guide

This guide separates repository cache availability from actual source inspection. The review retains 68 imported source objects from 69 raw contexts and nine official products, and adds one separately supported dynactin-binding annotation. Source identity, qualifiers and cached full-text flags are unchanged. Exact quoted support is confined to three core entries; this guide provides locators and access limits, not substitute primary evidence.

## Propagation, model and assay boundaries

The [PTHR23169 tree](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/treeinfo?family=PTHR23169), [family alignment](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/familymsa?family=PTHR23169) and [ancestral IBD file](https://data.pantherdb.org/ftp/downloads/paint/current/IBD.gaf) give the literal evidence path. The source manifest pins exact snapshots. The human leaf PTN000747957 descends from both cited nodes PTN002753757 and PTN002753862; all nine IBDs are reproduced. Mouse Dst/Q91ZU6, MACF1, plectin and epiplakin remain separate donors. A short donor list or the target’s own experimental grounding is not a propagation failure.

The [Reactome event R-HSA-2213192](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2213192) contains the [BPAG1e:Plectin input](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2220789), whose [DST-3 component](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2220815) is in cytosol. The embedded Q03001-3 record retains 7570-residue coordinates, whereas the current official product is 2649 residues. The target-resolved modeled location is used; those coordinates are not used for sequence reconstruction or an independently measured soluble pool.

The public [replay script and instructions](DST-bioinformatics/README.md) recompute exact interaction joins, two OpenCell accession groups, the peptide/product matches and PAINT lineage from genuine saved inputs. Its hashes and source URLs make the analyzed versions explicit. ROGID matches authenticate database reference sequences; assayed fragment features still govern experimental scope. Positive generic binding without a justified narrower subclass follows the task-specific CLINGEN retention policy.

Current official definitions distinguish [cytoskeletal adaptor activity](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0008093) from [dynactin binding](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0034452): neither is an ancestor of the other. The former retains the integrated transport role, while the latter records the demonstrated contact with the [DCTN1/p150 partner](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q14203/entry) and dynactin-containing complexes. Dynactin binding is a child of broad cytoskeletal protein binding; the existing broad replacements concern other partners, so their source rows are not repurposed. [Keratinocyte migration](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0051546) is a typed descendant of cell migration and the original cell motility term, and is distinct from maintenance of cell polarity. [RNA polymerase binding](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0070063) covers a polymerase molecule or complex, without requiring a host polymerase or catalytic-site contact.
