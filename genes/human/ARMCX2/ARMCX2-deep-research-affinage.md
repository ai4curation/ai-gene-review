# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ARMCX2 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ARMCX2 (ALEX2) is an X-chromosome-encoded armadillo repeat protein originally identified by cDNA cloning, with transcripts broadly expressed across human tissues and reduced or lost in several epithelial carcinomas including lung, prostate, colon, pancreas, and ovarian tumors [PMID:11162520]. In cisplatin-resistant ovarian cancer cells, the ARMCX2 promoter acquires CpG hypermethylation that transcriptionally silences the gene; treatment with the demethylating agent 2-deoxy-5'-azacytidine restores ARMCX2 expression and resensitizes cells to cisplatin, linking epigenetic silencing of ARMCX2 to acquired chemoresistance [PMID:22249249]. As a member of the GPRASP/ARMCX subfamily, ARMCX2 carries a mitochondrial targeting transmembrane domain and, in complex with TRAK2 and MIRO, participates in axonal mitochondrial trafficking and regulation of mitochondrial size and division [PMID:33267763]. Beyond these observations, direct mechanistic characterization of ARMCX2's molecular activity has not been established in the available corpus.
