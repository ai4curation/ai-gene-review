CC   -!- FUNCTION: Substrate-recognition component of a cullin-5-RING E3
CC       ubiquitin-protein ligase complex (ECS complex, also named CRL5
CC       complex), which mediates the ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal
CC       degradation of target proteins, such as DAB1 and IRS1
CC       (PubMed:16127460). Specifically recognizes and binds phosphorylated
CC       proteins via its SH2 domain, promoting their ubiquitination (By
CC       similarity). The ECS(SOCS7) complex acts as a key regulator of reelin
CC       signaling by mediating ubiquitination and degradation of phosphorylated
CC       DAB1 in the cortical plate of the developing cerebral cortex, thereby
CC       regulating neuron positioning during cortex development (By
CC       similarity). Functions in insulin signaling and glucose homeostasis
CC       through IRS1 ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal degradation
CC       (PubMed:16127460). Also inhibits prolactin, growth hormone and leptin
CC       signaling by preventing STAT3 and STAT5 activation, sequestering them
CC       in the cytoplasm and reducing their binding to DNA (PubMed:15677474).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8VHQ2, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15677474,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16127460}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16127460}.

CC       STAT5 (PubMed:15242778, PubMed:15677474, PubMed:16127460). Interacts
