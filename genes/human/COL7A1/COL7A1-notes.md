# COL7A1 review notes

COL7A1 supplies the alpha1 chain of collagen VII anchoring fibrils, which join epithelial basement membranes to underlying connective tissue. Human skin preparations and sequence-defined recombinant experiments support one integrated structural function. NC1 binds matrix ligands, and the C-terminal region participates in antiparallel assembly. These interactions contribute structure directly; collagen VII is not assigned the proteolytic or trafficking activities of the enzymes and receptors that process or transport it. [PMID:18599485; PMID:11274208; PMID:9245798]

## Structural mechanism and molecular specificity

The 2008 experiments combine authentic human dermal suprastructures with reconstitution using human full-length 2,944-residue collagen VII and mini-collagens. Collagen I fibrils were made from embryonic chicken tendon. Full-length and an NC1-containing mini-collagen bound those fibrils; a shortened construct lacking the helix-proximal von Willebrand A segment did not. Binding was dose dependent and corroborated by selective cross-linker tag transfer. The native human skin association resisted denaturants, but the authors explicitly leave its covalent chemistry unresolved. Resistance is not reported here as proof of a particular native covalent bond. [PMID:18599485]

Purified human amnion NC1 also binds laminin 332 and mouse EHS collagen IV, with reciprocal solid-phase, solution co-immunoprecipitation and conformation controls. This matrix-to-matrix attachment does not establish direct integrin binding through a laminin bridge. The separate 2006 abstract reports weaker monomeric collagen I affinity; a monomer assay and a reconstituted fibril assay need not give the same apparent interaction strength. The original 2006 full download was not obtained. [PMID:9245798; PMID:16563355]

## NC2 structure and the inhibitor prediction

The original human NC2/COL construct contains the 161-residue NC2 region and 186 adjacent collagenous residues. Expressed in human 293 cells, it forms disulfide-linked higher-order assemblies; removal of the collagenous region and specified C-terminal cysteine mutations distinguish trimer formation from antiparallel assembly. These are actual structural participation experiments, not only knockout necessity. [PMID:11274208]

The same original paper contradicts the automatic Kunitz inhibitor mapping at the tested scope. NC2/COL did not protect NC1 from trypsin or chymotrypsin at 10-fold or 50-fold excess, whereas aprotinin did. Thrombin/plasmin negatives were reported without displayed data, and isolated NC2 was also reported inactive. The original Results and Figure 6 caption differ in the aprotinin ratio; the conclusion depends on the positive control, not selecting one ratio. Other proteases or processing states remain open. The IEA is removed without a new universal NOT annotation or an all-product negative claim. Both actual InterPro mapping records are preserved.
