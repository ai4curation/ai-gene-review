Gene Description

TBX5 is a T-box transcription factor of the TBX2/3/4/5 subfamily, expressed in the developing heart (atria, left ventricle, conduction system, epicardium) and forelimb. Its N-terminal T-box domain binds T-box elements (AGGTGTGA) in Pol II promoters and enhancers, and TBX5 mainly activates targets such as NPPA, GJA5/Cx40, FGF10, MYH6, ID2 and PITX2. It works cooperatively with partner cardiac transcription factors: it binds NKX2-5 (heterodimer on composite DNA elements), GATA4 and MEF2C, and interacts with SALL4 in limb and heart patterning. Coactivators TAZ/YAP1 and acetylation by KAT2A/KAT2B enhance its activity, and acetylation promotes nuclear retention. TBX5 shuttles between nucleus and cytoplasm, where the PDZ-LIM protein PDLIM7 (LMP4) tethers it to actin and represses it. In development TBX5 is required for forelimb bud initiation through direct activation of Fgf10, for atrial and ventricular septation, for posterior (inflow) heart growth, and in a dosage-sensitive way for patterning and maturation of the atrioventricular node and ventricular conduction system. In the adult atrium it maintains rhythm through a TBX5-PITX2 network. Heterozygous loss-of-function variants cause Holt-Oram syndrome (upper-limb radial-ray defects with atrial/ventricular septal defects and conduction block), and variants are associated with atrial fibrillation and dilated cardiomyopathy.

Identity and structure

  • T-box transcription factor, 518 aa; T-box DNA-binding domain residues 58-238 (UniProt FT
    DNA_BIND, PROSITE PRU00201; structure PMID:26926761). PANTHER PTHR11267:SF28
    (T-BOX TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR TBX5), TBX2/3/4/5 subfamily.
  • Binds T-box elements (TBE, consensus AGGTGTGA). EMSA with a synthetic site
    PMID:12499378.
  • Crystal structure of TBX5 + NKX2-5 DNA-binding domains on a 19-bp NPPA target
    PMID:26926761; also a TBX5 homodimer.
  • Two cooperating NLSs PMID:14519429.
  • Acetylation of K339 by KAT2A/KAT2B potentiates activity and nuclear retention
    PMID:29174768.
  • Nucleocytoplasmic shuttling controlled by the PDZ-LIM protein PDLIM7/LMP4, which tethers TBX5 to
    actin and represses its activity [PMID:16880269 "In the presence of LMP4, Tbx5 shuttles dynamically between the nucleus and cytoplasm"; "LMP4 acts as a repressor of Tbx5 activity"]; zebrafish
    Pdlim7 balance of Tbx5 activity at the AV boundary PMID:19895804.

Molecular function: sequence-specific Pol II activator with cardiac TF partners

  • NKX2-5: Y2H with Nkx2-5 bait isolated Tbx5; the two bind the Nppa promoter in tandem and synergise
    PMID:11431700.
    HOS missense mutants lose NKX2-5 interaction and synergy PMID:12499378.
    ANF and Cx40 (Gja5) promoters are cooperatively activated with Nkx2-5 and are dosage-sensitive targets PMID:11572777.
    Consistent with the NKX2-5 review in this repo, which MODIFYs TBX5 protein-binding rows to GO:0061629.
  • GATA4: physical interaction disrupted by GATA4 G296S and by HOS TBX5 missense mutations
    PMID:12845333; GATA4 N285S
    also disrupts it PMID:24000169.
  • MEF2C: direct interaction through DNA-binding domains, synergistic MYH6 activation PMID:19204083.
  • SALL4: Tbx5 regulates Sall4 and the two interact positively and negatively in forelimb and heart
    PMID:16380715.
  • Coactivators TAZ (WWTR1) and YAP1 PMID:16332960;
    in beta-catenin-driven cancer cells a beta-catenin-YAP1-TBX5 complex activates BCL2L1/BIRC5 PMID:23245941.
  • KAT2A/KAT2B bind and acetylate TBX5 PMID:29174768.

Biological roles

  • Holt-Oram syndrome (heart-hand): TBX5 haploinsufficiency [PMID:8988164; PMID:8988165 "We conclude that TBX5 is critical for limb and heart development and suggest that haploinsufficiency of TBX5 causes Holt-Oram syndrome"].
    Strict criteria require preaxial radial-ray defects PMID:16183809.
  • Forelimb: mouse Tbx5 null embryos lack forelimb buds; Tbx5 directly activates Fgf10
    PMID:12490567;
    conditional deletion confirms requirement for bud formation and outgrowth PMID:12736217.
  • Heart: Tbx5 del/del causes posterior (atrial/sinoatrial) hypoplasia; haploinsufficiency reduces
    ANF and Cx40 PMID:11572777. Ventricular septation set by a Tbx5 gradient PMID:19727199.
  • Conduction system: required for AV bundle/bundle-branch patterning and AV node maturation
    PMID:15289437; Tbx5-Nkx2-5-Id2 pathway specifies ventricular conduction system PMID:17604724.
  • Adult atrial rhythm: Tbx5 -> Pitx2 incoherent feed-forward network; adult deletion causes AF
    PMID:27582060. Human TBX5 variants associated with AF and DCM [PMID:27035640; PMID:25963046].
  • Overexpression inhibits cardiomyocyte proliferation (chick, partly non-cell-autonomous) PMID:11161571
    and cell growth of cultured cells PMID:12237100; proepicardial migration affected by both gain and
    loss PMID:15138308. These are dosage/overexpression readouts and treated as non-core.
