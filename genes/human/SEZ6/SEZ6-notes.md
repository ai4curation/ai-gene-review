# SEZ6 (human, Q53EL9) curation notes

Deep research providers were unavailable; evidence from UniProt and the single cached publication.
Only one GOA reference (PMID:32296183, a large interactome screen) exists for this gene, so most
rows are IBA/IEA.

## Localization (benchmark-relevant)
- Single-pass type I plasma membrane protein with a large extracellular CUB/sushi region:
  UniProt `SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000250}; Single-pass type I membrane protein.
  Note=Localized on dendrites and in the synaptic and postsynaptic fraction.`
- Dendritic spine and dendritic shaft accepted; ER, neuronal cell body, perinuclear region and
  apical dendrite kept as non-core (biosynthetic pool or finer subcompartments).
- Conclusion: neuronal cell surface, ectodomain exposed (consistent with its use as an ADC /
  T-cell-engager target in small-cell lung cancer).

## Function
- UniProt: "May play a role in cell-cell recognition and in neuronal membrane signaling";
  "Seems to be important for the achievement of the necessary balance between dendrite elongation
  and branching"; "Involved in the development of appropriate excitatory synaptic connectivity".
- No molecular function term is justified by current evidence: the only annotated MF was generic
  protein binding (removed per policy), so the core function entry carries locations and processes
  but no molecular_function. Identifying a ligand is listed under suggested experiments.
