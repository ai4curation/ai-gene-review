RTL8A (Q9BWD3) — Functional Annotation Report

Gene: RTL8A (synonyms: CXX1B, FAM127B, MAR8A, Mart8A) Protein: Retrotransposon Gag-like protein 8A UniProt: Q9BWD3 · HGNC: 24514 · Organism: Homo sapiens Family: RTL/SIRH (Mart) family of domesticated retroelement genes; FAM127 family Domain: RTL1/1-8/LDOC capsid-like (InterPro IPR032549; Pfam PF16297)

1. Summary (Answer to the Research Question)

RTL8A is not an enzyme, transporter, or classical signaling molecule. It is a small (113-residue, ~13 kDa) protein corresponding to a single, truncated retroviral/retrotransposon Gag "capsid" (CA) domain that has been domesticated from a Ty3/gypsy sushi-ichi-related LTR retrotransposon in the eutherian lineage. Functionally it behaves as a capsid-derived protein–protein interaction / adaptor module. Its two best-documented molecular roles are:

  1. Regulation of the ubiquitin–proteasome shuttle factor UBQLN2 — RTL8 binds UBQLN2 and drives its translocation into the nucleus/nucleolus to ubiquitin-rich protein-quality-control (PQC) compartments; UBQLN2 reciprocally stabilizes RTL8 (35247097).
  2. Antagonistic regulation of the related capsid protein PEG10 — RTL8 hetero-oligomerizes with the PEG10 N-terminal capsid domain, is packaged into PEG10 virus-like particles (VLPs), and inhibits PEG10 VLP formation/release while raising intracellular PEG10; it is also the obligatory bridge that lets UBQLN2 complex with PEG10 and be recruited into cytoplasmic stress granules under stress (39775359; 40680123).

Thus RTL8A acts at the interface of endogenous retroelement (PEG10) capsid biology and cellular proteostasis (UBQLN2 / ubiquitin–proteasome system / stress granules), operating in both the nucleolus/nucleus and cytoplasmic stress granules.

Identity verification: The gene symbol, organism (human), FAM127/RTL family membership, and the PF16297 capsid-like domain are all internally consistent with the primary literature. This is a genuine, correctly identified human protein — not a case of symbol ambiguity. The only caveat is that RTL8A is one of three near-identical paralogs (see §6), so published functional work refers to "RTL8" collectively rather than resolving RTL8A individually.

2. Molecular Identity and Origin

RTL8A belongs to the RTL (Retrotransposon Gag-Like) / SIRH (Sushi-Ichi Retrotransposon Homolog) family, also called the Mart genes. Eutherian mammals possess 11 RTL/SIRH genes "presumably derived from a certain retrovirus", which were co-opted ("domesticated") and now function in the placenta, brain, and innate immune system in a eutherian-specific manner (Kaneko-Ishino & Ishino, 37892118). These genes derive from a Ty3/gypsy LTR-retroelement with homology to the pufferfish sushi-ichi retrotransposon (Henke et al., 25888968).

Within this family, RTL8A corresponds to Mart8 / CXX1. Full-length family members such as PEG10 (Mart2) and RTL1 (Mart1) retain a multidomain Gag(-Pol)-like organization and can form virus-like particles and bind nucleic acids (Segel et al., Science 2021, 34413232). RTL8 is a "truncated form of PEG10 that shares homology with the N-terminal gag-like capsid domain" (Campodonico et al., 39775359) — i.e., it retains essentially only the capsid (CA) module.

3. Structure (Experimental Inference)

UniProt Q9BWD3 is a single 113-residue chain whose annotation is limited to the chain feature and a single natural variant (rs2498776 at position 48); it carries no catalytic-motif annotation, consistent with a non-enzymatic protein.

Bioinformatic/structural analysis performed in this study:

  • AlphaFold model (Q9BWD3): mean pLDDT = 88.0, with a very-high-confidence folded core at residues ~21–100 (per-20-residue mean pLDDT 90–98) flanked by a lower-confidence, likely flexible N-terminus (res 1–20, pLDDT 64.6) and C-terminus (res 101–113, pLDDT 77.1). This is consistent with a single compact capsid-like (CA) fold plus disordered tails.
  • Homology scan vs PEG10 (Q86TG7): a crude ungapped comparison yields ~27% identity aligning to the PEG10 capsid region, independently supporting that the RTL8A fold is a diverged retroviral Gag capsid (CA) domain (consistent with 39775359).

RTL8A lacks the CCHC zinc-knuckle/nucleocapsid, the aspartyl-protease (DSG) motif, and the reverse-transcriptase/integrase regions found in full retroelements. Mechanistically, the folded CA-like core provides the oligomerization/interaction surface that mediates incorporation into PEG10 capsids and binding to UBQLN proteins, while the flexible termini likely mediate additional partner contacts.

4. Primary Molecular Function

4.1 A capsid-derived protein-interaction / adaptor module (not an enzyme)

RTL8A has no catalytic activity. Its function is structural/regulatory: it uses a retroviral capsid-like domain as an interaction surface. Curated interaction data (UniProt/IntAct) list binding partners PEG10 (isoform 2), UBQLN2, UBQLN1, and CCDC102B, matching the interactions characterized experimentally below.

4.2 Regulation of UBQLN2 subcellular localization and proteostasis

In an unbiased mass-spectrometry screen for UBQLN2 interactors, RTL8 was identified, and the UBQLN2–RTL8 interaction was confirmed both in vitro (recombinant proteins) and in vivo (mouse brain tissue) (Mohan et al., J Biol Chem 2022, 35247097). Key experimental findings:

  • RTL8 localizes to nucleoli under endogenous and overexpressed conditions.
  • Co-expression of RTL8 promotes nuclear translocation of UBQLN2, including under heat-shock stress, and the two colocalize in ubiquitin-enriched subnuclear structures containing PQC components.
  • The effect is specific to UBQLN2 and does not extend to the paralogous brain ubiquilins UBQLN1 and UBQLN4.
  • UBQLN2 reciprocally stabilizes RTL8 protein levels in human cell lines and mouse brain.

Because UBQLN2 is a ubiquitin–proteasome shuttle factor central to protein quality control (and mutated in ALS/FTD), RTL8A functions as a spatial regulator of the UBQLN2 branch of the ubiquitin–proteasome system, directing UBQLN2 to nuclear/nucleolar PQC sites where it can clear aberrant nuclear proteins.

4.3 Antagonism of PEG10 capsid / virus-like-particle biology

PEG10 forms virus-like particles (VLPs) implicated in its roles in placentation and neurological disease. Campodonico et al. (39775359) showed:

  • RTL8 alone cannot form VLPs but is incorporated into PEG10-derived particles.
  • RTL8 binds the N-terminal capsid domain of PEG10.
  • RTL8 co-expression decreases PEG10 VLP abundance and increases intracellular PEG10, and modulating RTL8 changes VLP output in cells that naturally produce them — i.e., RTL8 inhibits PEG10 VLP formation/release.

This defines a novel antagonistic relationship between two domesticated retroelement genes, with RTL8A acting as a "poison-subunit"–like capsid partner that caps or disrupts productive PEG10 capsid assembly/egress.

4.4 Bridging PEG10 and UBQLN2 at stress granules

Mohan et al. (2024 preprint 39484508; 2025 40680123) integrated these two activities: "RTL8 confers on UBQLN2 the ability to complex with and regulate PEG10," and PEG10 — a core stress-granule component that forms VLPs within granules — can recruit UBQLN2 into stress granules only when RTL8 is present. Changing UBQLN2, RTL8, or PEG10 levels remodels stress-granule disassembly kinetics and translation recovery. RTL8A is therefore an obligatory adaptor coupling PEG10 capsid condensates to the UBQLN2 proteostasis machinery.

5. Localization (Where It Acts)

  • Nucleolus / nucleus: endogenous and overexpressed RTL8 localizes to nucleoli; with UBQLN2 it concentrates in ubiquitin-rich subnuclear PQC bodies (35247097). This is the site of its UBQLN2-directing function.
  • Cytoplasmic stress granules: under stress, RTL8 is found with PEG10 and UBQLN2 in stress granules, where PEG10 forms VLPs (40680123).
  • Site of PEG10 antagonism: RTL8 is incorporated into cytoplasmic PEG10 capsid particles and influences their intracellular abundance and release (39775359).

RTL8 is broadly expressed in many of the same tissues as PEG10, including human brain (39775359).

6. Paralog Caveat (Important for Interpretation)

The human genome encodes three near-identical RTL8 paralogs — RTL8A/B/C (= CXX1B/A/C = Mart8A/B/C) — on the X chromosome, all producing ~13 kDa proteins of nearly indistinguishable sequence; the mouse orthologs are Cxx1a,b,c / Mart8b,c,a (25888968). Because the proteins are essentially identical, the antibody-, overexpression-, and mass-spectrometry-based functional studies above characterize "RTL8" as a group and do not resolve a function unique to RTL8A. RTL8A should therefore be understood as sharing the functions documented for the RTL8 trio.

7. Pathway Context

RTL8A sits at the intersection of two pathways:

  • Proteostasis / ubiquitin–proteasome system: via UBQLN2, routing PQC activity to nuclear/nucleolar compartments and modulating UBQLN2 availability at stress granules — relevant to ALS/FTD, where UBQLN2 is a disease gene (35247097; 40680123).
  • Endogenous retroelement capsid pathway: via PEG10, as a negative regulator of PEG10 VLP formation/release (39775359). PEG10 itself is implicated in placentation, ALS, and Angelman syndrome, so RTL8A is positioned as a cell-intrinsic modulator of PEG10-driven biology.

8. Supported vs. Refuted Hypotheses

Supported (by direct experimental evidence): - RTL8 physically binds UBQLN2 (in vitro + mouse brain) and drives its nuclear/nucleolar translocation to PQC sites (35247097). - RTL8 binds the PEG10 capsid domain, is packaged into PEG10 VLPs, and inhibits VLP formation/release (39775359). - RTL8 is required for PEG10-dependent recruitment of UBQLN2 to stress granules (40680123).

Supported (by structural/evolutionary inference): - RTL8A is a domesticated, capsid-only retroelement Gag fragment (AlphaFold + homology; 37892118 25888968 39775359).

Refuted / not supported: - RTL8A is an enzyme, transporter, or nucleic-acid-packaging protein — no evidence; it lacks the catalytic and nucleocapsid modules of full retroelements.

9. Limitations and Future Directions

  • Functional data derive largely from overexpression and interaction studies; endogenous loss-of-function (knockout/knockdown) phenotypes specific to RTL8A are not established.
  • Paralog resolution (RTL8A vs B vs C) is unaddressed experimentally.
  • No experimental 3D structure exists; the capsid-like fold is predicted (AlphaFold) and inferred from PEG10 homology.
  • The physiological consequences in brain/placenta and the disease relevance (ALS/FTD via UBQLN2; PEG10-linked disorders) remain to be defined in vivo.
