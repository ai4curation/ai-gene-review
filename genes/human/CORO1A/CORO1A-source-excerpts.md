## PMID:11094157

[Original source](https://febs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1016/S0014-5793(00)02220-1); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0014-5793(00)02220-1). Results 3.1, indexed line 149.

> coronin did not co-precipitate with anti-p85 immunocomplexes

## PMID:17442961

[Original source](https://doi.org/10.4049/jimmunol.178.9.5769); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4049/jimmunol.178.9.5769). Results, Figure 10 assay; indexed lines 1944–1947.

> FL GST-coronin1 significantly inhibited Arp2/3-dependent nucleation

Purified full-length human GST-coronin inhibits the Arp2/3/VCA assay. The isolated WD construct does not; dominant-negative cell phenotypes may affect multiple coronins.
