# ERCC5 source and access guide

The review distinguishes actual original evidence from database inference and from successful download status. Normal publication-cache bytes and their availability metadata were not changed when originals were recovered separately. The reading limits below apply to the reviewed experiments, including uninspected figures and supplements.

## Phylogenetic and rule evidence

The actual [PANTHER PTHR16171 tree](https://www.pantherdb.org/panther/family.do?clsAccession=PTHR16171) places human PTN002497156 below the Eukaryota assertion node PTN000792422. The ungapped target alignment exactly matches the 1,186-aa P28715 sequence. Three IBD assertions were traced: GO:0006289, GO:0000112 and GO:0000014, with no target-path NOT. A single yeast donor is legitimate phylogenetic support; human self-WITH is legitimate descendant evidence, not circularity. Yeast donor experiments and independent human experiments retain their separate species and preparation scopes.

## Human pathway participants and catalysts

All 11 source Reactome roots have actual forward routes to [human entity 67447](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-67447), reference entity 67448, P28715, modeled residues 1–1186 in nucleoplasm. Location roots are 5689317, 5690213, 5690988, 5690990, 5690991, 6782138, 6782141, 6782204, 6782211 and 6782224. The separate pathway root is 6781827. Neighboring polymerase recruitment or ERCC1–ERCC4 incision does not confer those activities on XPG.

Two catalysts identify XPG itself: [GG-NER event 5690988](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5690988) → catalyst 5691069 → active unit 67447; and [TC-NER event 6782224](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6782224) → catalyst 6782216 → active unit 67447. Both have DNA endonuclease activity. [TC-NER pathway 6781827](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6781827) contains dual-incision branch 6782135 and the XPG incision event, establishing an executed step. This does not independently verify every upstream recognition or backtracking model or imply access to PMID:16246722.

## Localization, terms and natural products

The official [HPA ERCC5 record](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000134899-ERCC5/subcellular) reports supported nucleoplasm localization: HPA045845 in U-251MG and HPA050374 in A-431, U-251MG and U2OS. It explicitly cautions that the antibodies can recognize proteins from multiple genes. HPA image pixels were not inspected. Controlled human XPG nuclear-foci experiments and explicit Reactome target compartments independently corroborate the location. The P100 fraction in PMID:26833090 is not purified chromatin.

Current QuickGO definitions were checked for the authored refinements: [bubble DNA binding](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0000405), [DNA endonuclease activity](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0004520), [homologous double-strand-break repair](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0000724), [nucleoplasm](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005654), [chromatin](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0000785), [enzyme activator activity](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0008047), [TFIIH-class factor binding](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0001097) and [TFIIH holo-complex binding](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0062058). DNA endonuclease is not currently a formal descendant of GO:0016788 in the captured graph; that refinement specifies the demonstrated chemistry without inventing an ontology edge. The factor-3 complex definition has a yeast example but is not defined as yeast-only.

## Core reaction and repair-complex scope

The core uses current [DNA endonuclease activity](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0004520), [nucleotide-excision repair](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0006289), [nucleoplasm](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005654) and [nucleotide-excision repair complex](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0000109). The complex term covers NER-protein assemblies; it does not require a permanent preassembled complex. Human incision assemblies [5690214](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5690214) and [6782219](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6782219) each explicitly contain ERCC5 entity 67447, the active unit of their respective DNA-endonuclease catalysts. PMID:11259578 Methods and Results Figures 3–6 supply independent functional human complex evidence with ionic-strength limits. The core preserves the distinct natural products and keeps HR recruitment and NTHL1 activation separate from nuclease chemistry.

## Follow-up scope checks

The full binding refinement retains bubble DNA binding together with double-stranded and single-stranded DNA binding. PMID:12644470 Figure 4 resolves duplex and short single-stranded junction contacts; Figure 2 competition distinguishes these from unrestricted isolated-strand binding. The current official is-a/part-of closures show that [TFIIH-class factor binding](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0001097) and [TFIIH holo-complex binding](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0062058) are not ancestors or descendants of each other. Their separate subunit-versus-holo-complex evidence is retained.

PMID:11259578 Figure 6 Results explicitly retain functionally active XPG with TFIIH after 150 mM KCl washing and lose that interaction at 300 mM KCl. This is evidence for a condition-dependent repair subassembly, compatible with the absence of a permanent all-factor repairosome. The original Methods and Results were reread for this distinction; no new figure-pixel inspection is claimed. PMID:15358233 remains abstract-only but explicitly reports FLAG-XPG-dependent stimulation of NTH1 glycosylase/AP-lyase activity. The unread supplement and figure pixels for PMID:32821917 remain the boundary on adjudicating its damaged-DNA binding assertion.
