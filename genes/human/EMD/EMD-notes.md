# EMD notes

## Primary function

EMD encodes emerin, a LEM-domain tail-anchored protein that resides primarily
at the nuclear inner membrane. The cleanest molecular function annotation is
actin binding: Holaska et al. purified emerin-associated actin from HeLa nuclear
extracts and showed that recombinant emerin caps pointed ends of F-actin
[PMID:15328537, "Emerin binds F-actin with high affinity"].

This actin-binding activity is part of nuclear-envelope organization rather
than a generic cytoskeletal process. The same study concluded that emerin helps
build a cortical actin network at the inner nuclear membrane [PMID:15328537,
"We propose that emerin contributes to the formation of an actin-based cortical
network at the nuclear inner membrane"].

EMD also contributes to nuclear-envelope stiffness in a NEMP1/emerin complex.
Tsatskis et al. showed EMERIN/NEMP1 biochemical association and measured softer
nuclear envelopes after EMERIN knockdown by atomic-force microscopy
[PMID:32923640, "Knockdown of EMERIN softened the NE in both T24 and SHP77
cells"].

## Secondary roles

Emerin has secondary signaling and cytoskeletal links:

- It binds beta-catenin through an APC-like domain and limits beta-catenin
  accumulation in the nucleus, which supports the existing Wnt-pathway rows as
  real but not the central structural function [PMID:16858403, "Emerin
  interacts with beta-catenin through a conserved adenomatous polyposis coli
  (APC)-like domain."].
- A fraction of emerin reaches the outer nuclear membrane and peripheral ER in
  fibroblasts and links the centrosome to the nuclear envelope through tubulin
  [PMID:17785515, "a significant fraction of emerin is located at the outer
  nuclear membrane and peripheral ER"].

## Spindle-associated membrane caveat

PMID:19494128 is about Samp1/TMEM201, not EMD. The abstract makes the contrast
explicit: "another integral inner nuclear membrane protein, emerin, was absent
from the spindle-associated membranes." The emerin spindle pole and cortical ER
rows that cite this paper are therefore unsupported.
