Gene Description

RAB1B is the Rab GTPase of the early secretory pathway. Like all Rabs it is a nucleotide switch: prenylated at its C terminus, held in the cytosol by GDI when inactive, delivered to membranes and loaded with GTP by an exchange factor, and returned to the GDP state by a GTPase-activating protein. In the GTP state it recruits tethering factors that capture COPII-derived carriers leaving endoplasmic reticulum exit sites and hold them at the ER-Golgi intermediate compartment and cis-Golgi for fusion. Sustaining that flux is what keeps the Golgi ribbon compact, and knocking RAB1B down fragments the Golgi. RAB1B is largely redundant with its paralog RAB1A, and the genetics reflect this: phenotypes such as the block in herpes simplex virus 1 glycoprotein trafficking appear when both are removed and not when RAB1B alone is. Because autophagosome formation draws membrane and machinery from the early secretory pathway, RAB1B activity and functional ER exit sites are also required for autophagosomes to form, a requirement that sits upstream of the phagophore rather than on it. RAB1B is a favoured target of intracellular pathogens: Legionella pneumophila effectors recruit it to the bacterial vacuole, act as its exchange factor and covalently modify it.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: The GO:0090557 NOT annotation cites PMID:23885123, whose cached full text does not mention Rab1 anywhere. Does the supporting evidence sit in supplementary data, and if so which figure? A negative annotation is only useful if its basis can be traced.

Q: RAB1B and RAB1A are functionally redundant, and PMID:21680502 records this correctly with a paired IMP NOT and IGI on the same term. How many other RAB1B annotations transferred from RAB1A would fail a single-knockdown test, and should redundant paralog pairs be annotated as a unit?

Q: Legionella pneumophila DrrA/SidM acts as a RAB1 exchange factor and AMPylates the switch II region. Should host proteins targeted this way carry a molecular function annotation reflecting that they are substrates of pathogen effectors, or is that properly the pathogen's annotation?

Q: RAB1B's IBAs are anchored at three PANTHER nodes for different aspects (PTN001292747, PTN000634320, PTN001178334), which is the expected PAINT pattern. But the neuronal cell body assertion carries is_active_in rather than located_in. Is that qualifier warranted for a housekeeping secretory-pathway Rab, and how does PAINT decide between the two when the descendant evidence is localization?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Single versus double knockdown of RAB1A and RAB1B across the phenotypes currently annotated to RAB1B alone - Golgi morphology, ER-to-Golgi cargo transport, autophagosome formation - to establish which are genuinely RAB1B-specific and which require loss of the redundant pair, and so which annotations should carry IGI rather than IMP.

Experiment: Immunoprecipitation of RAB1B from intestinal epithelial monolayers with and without JAM-A knockdown, together with transepithelial resistance measurement after RAB1B knockdown, to resolve whether the negative barrier annotation is supported and to supply evidence for the proposed epithelial barrier term.

    GOA carries a NOT|involved_in annotation of this gene to GO:0090557, which is defined for
