CC   -!- POLYMORPHISM: SMIM1 is responsible for the Vel blood group system (VEL)
CC       [MIM:615264]. The Vel antigen is present on red blood cells from all
CC       people except rare Vel-negative individuals who can form antibodies to

CC       negative individuals are homozygous for a 17-nucleotide frameshift

CC       the 17-nucleotide frameshift deletion and a missense variant at
CC       position 51. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23563608}.
