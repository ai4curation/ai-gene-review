CC   -!- FUNCTION: Pore-forming subunit of the mechanosensitive non-specific
CC       cation Piezo channel required for rapidly adapting mechanically
CC       activated (MA) currents and has a key role in sensing touch and tactile
CC       pain (PubMed:37590348). Piezo channels are homotrimeric three-blade
CC       propeller-shaped structures that utilize a cap-motion and plug-and-
CC       latch mechanism to gate their ion-conducting pathways
CC       (PubMed:37590348). Expressed in sensory neurons, is essential for
CC       diverse physiological processes, including respiratory control,
CC       systemic metabolism, urinary function, and proprioception (By
CC       similarity). Mediates airway stretch sensing, enabling efficient
CC       respiration at birth and maintaining normal breathing in adults (By
CC       similarity). It regulates brown and beige adipose tissue morphology and
CC       function, preventing systemic hypermetabolism (By similarity). In the
CC       lower urinary tract, acts as a sensor in both the bladder urothelium
CC       and innervating sensory neurons being required for bladder-stretch
CC       sensing and urethral micturition reflexes, ensuring proper urinary
CC       function (PubMed:33057202). Additionally, PIEZO2 serves as the
CC       principal mechanotransducer in proprioceptors, facilitating
CC       proprioception and coordinated body movements (By similarity). In inner
CC       ear hair cells, PIEZO1/2 subunits may constitute part of the
CC       mechanotransducer (MET) non-selective cation channel complex where they
CC       may act as pore-forming ion-conducting component in the complex (By
CC       similarity). Required for Merkel-cell mechanotransduction (By
CC       similarity). Plays a major role in light-touch mechanosensation (By
CC       similarity). In kidney, during blood volume challenges, regulates
CC       calcium dynamics and maintains renin production, thereby serving as an
CC       essential regulator of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS)
CC       and systemic blood volume control (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8CD54, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33057202,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37590348}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotrimer; the homotrimer forms a propeller-shaped Piezo
CC       channel with a cation-ion conducting pore (PubMed:37590348).
CC       Heterotrimeric interaction may occur between PIEZO1 and PIEZO2 (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with STOML3 (By similarity). Interacts with
CC       TMC7; the interaction inhibits PIEZO2-conducted mechanically activated
CC       currents (By similarity). Interacts with TMC1; the interaction may be
CC       part of the MET complex (By similarity). Interacts with MDFIC (via C-
CC       terminus); the interaction prolongs Piezo channel inactivation
CC       (PubMed:37590348). Interacts with MDFI (via C-terminus); the
CC       interaction prolongs Piezo channel inactivation (PubMed:37590348).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8CD54, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37590348}.
