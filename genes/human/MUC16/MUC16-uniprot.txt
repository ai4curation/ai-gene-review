CC   -!- FUNCTION: Thought to provide a protective, lubricating barrier against
CC       particles and infectious agents at mucosal surfaces. {ECO:0000250}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Binds to MSLN. Binding to MSLN mediates heterotypic cell
CC       adhesion. This may contribute to the metastasis of ovarian cancer to
CC       the peritoneum by initiating cell attachment to the mesothelial
CC       epithelium via binding to MSLN.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane; Single-pass type I membrane
CC       protein. Secreted, extracellular space. Note=May be liberated into the
CC       extracellular space following the phosphorylation of the intracellular
CC       C-terminus which induces the proteolytic cleavage and liberation of the
CC       extracellular domain.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in corneal and conjunctival epithelia (at
CC       protein level). Overexpressed in ovarian carcinomas and ovarian low
CC       malignant potential (LMP) tumors as compared to the expression in
CC       normal ovarian tissue and ovarian adenomas.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11369781, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12218296,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16384952}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Up-regulated in ovarian cancer cells.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12218296}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Composed of three domains, a Ser-, Thr-rich N-terminal domain,
CC       a repeated domain containing between 12 and 60 partially conserved
CC       tandem repeats of 156 amino acids and a C-terminal transmembrane
CC       contain domain with a short cytoplasmic tail.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11786729}.
CC   -!- PTM: Heavily O-glycosylated; expresses both type 1 and type 2 core
CC       glycans. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12734200}.
CC   -!- PTM: Heavily N-glycosylated; expresses primarily high mannose and
CC       complex bisecting type N-linked glycans. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12734200}.
CC   -!- PTM: May be phosphorylated. Phosphorylation of the intracellular C-
CC       terminal domain may induce proteolytic cleavage and the liberation of
CC       the extracellular domain into the extracellular space.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9276028}.
CC   -!- PTM: May contain numerous disulfide bridges. Association of several
CC       molecules of the secreted form may occur through interchain disulfide
CC       bridges providing an extraordinarily large gel-like matrix in the
CC       extracellular space or in the lumen of secretory ducts.
CC   -!- POLYMORPHISM: The number of repeats is highly polymorphic and can be
CC       composed between 12 and 60 extracellular mucin repeats.
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:11786729}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Antigen that is the basis for a widely used serum assay
CC       for the monitoring of patients with ovarian epithelial cancer. Due to
CC       lack of sensitivity for stage I disease and lack of specificity, it is
CC       of little value in the detection of early ovarian cancer. Due to its
CC       similarly elevated levels in some nonmalignant conditions, it is not
CC       specific enough to be used for population screening.
