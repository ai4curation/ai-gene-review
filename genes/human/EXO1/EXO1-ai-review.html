Gene Description

EXO1 (exonuclease 1) is a nuclear, Mg2+-dependent structure-specific nuclease of the XPG/RAD2 (FEN1-like) family. Its N-terminal nuclease domain carries a robust 5'->3' exonuclease that is most active on blunt or 5'-recessed duplex DNA, plus a FEN1-like 5'-flap endonuclease and an RNase H activity; its long, disordered C-terminal region binds partner proteins (MSH2, MLH1, MSH3, PCNA). In DNA mismatch repair EXO1 is the excision nuclease: activated by MutSalpha, it removes the error-containing strand from a nick 5' to the mismatch, and with MutLalpha, PCNA and RFC it also supports excision directed from a 3' nick. In double-strand break repair EXO1, recruited by MRN and CtIP and stimulated by BLM and RPA, performs long-range 5'->3' end resection that creates the 3' single-stranded tails needed for homologous recombination and ATR signalling; ATM- and CDK-dependent phosphorylation tunes how far it resects. At telomeres lacking POT1-TPP1, NBS1 recruits EXO1 to resect the C-strand, feeding alternative end joining. Mouse genetics also implicate Exo1 in meiosis, somatic hypermutation and class switch recombination.
