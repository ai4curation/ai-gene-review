Gene Description

CLDN16 encodes claudin-16, a four-pass membrane protein that contributes to cation-selective paracellular channels at renal epithelial tight junctions. It cooperates with claudin-19 in junctional complexes that support magnesium and calcium reabsorption in the thick ascending limb. Its C-terminal PDZ-binding motif engages ZO-1 and supports junctional targeting. Junctional composition and cellular background influence the measured ion selectivity, and claudin-14 can reshape claudin-16/19-containing junctions. Pathogenic variants can impair targeting, partner interactions or permeability, causing familial hypomagnesemia with hypercalciuria and nephrocalcinosis.

Core Functions

Claudin-16 supplies a membrane component of cation-permeable tight-junction channels together with claudin-19. This junctional pathway permits ion movement between epithelial cells and supports renal magnesium and calcium reabsorption. PDZ-mediated anchoring and claudin interactions organize its junctional residence and function.
