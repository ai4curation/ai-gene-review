CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms paracellular channels: coassembles with CLDN19 into
CC       tight junction strands with cation-selective channels through the
CC       strands, conveying epithelial permeability in a process known as
CC       paracellular tight junction permeability (PubMed:16234325,
CC       PubMed:18188451, PubMed:28028216). Involved in the maintenance of ion
CC       gradients along the nephron. In the thick ascending limb (TAL) of
CC       Henle's loop, facilitates sodium paracellular permeability from the
CC       interstitial compartment to the lumen, contributing to the lumen-
CC       positive transepithelial potential that drives paracellular magnesium
CC       and calcium reabsorption (PubMed:10390358, PubMed:11518780,
CC       PubMed:14628289, PubMed:16528408, PubMed:28028216).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10390358, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11518780,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14628289, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16234325,
