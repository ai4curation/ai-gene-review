CC   -!- FUNCTION: Negative regulator of the mitochondrial calcium uniporter
CC       (MCU), a channel that mediates calcium uptake into the mitochondrial
CC       matrix (PubMed:31533452). MCUB is required to limit mitochondrial
CC       calcium overload during stress (PubMed:31533452). Acts as a dominant-
CC       negative regulator that displaces MCU from the functional uniplex
CC       complex and thereby decreases the association of calcium sensors MICU1
CC       and MICU2, preventing channel gating (PubMed:31533452). Mitochondrial
CC       calcium homeostasis plays key roles in mitochondrial metabolism
CC       (PubMed:31533452). Acts as an important regulator of mitochondrial
CC       metabolism in response to stress in muscle cells: induced in response
CC       to fasting, leading to restrict mitochondrial calcium uptake, resulting
CC       in reprogramming of mitochondria toward fatty acid oxidation preference
CC       (By similarity). Acts as a regulator of macrophage polarization during
CC       skeletal muscle regeneration: inhibition of mitochondrial calcium
CC       uptake drives differentiation of macrophages with anti-inflammatory
CC       profile, promoting the differentiation and fusion of satellite cells
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q810S1,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31533452}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homooligomer (By similarity). Associates with the uniplex
CC       complex, composed of MCU, MICU1, MICU2 and EMRE/SMDT1, inhibiting its
CC       activity (PubMed:24231807). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q810S1,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24231807}.
