# PRKAB1 notes

## AMPK beta scaffold

PRKAB1 encodes the beta-1 scaffold subunit of AMPK. The core complex is an
alpha/beta/gamma heterotrimer: the alpha subunit carries the catalytic kinase
domain, the beta subunit binds glycogen and bridges the other two subunits, and
the gamma subunit binds adenine nucleotides
[PMID:21399626, "AMPK is a heterotrimeric complex comprising an α catalytic subunit and two regulatory subunits (β and γ)1.";
PMID:25412657, "AMPK consists of an α-subunit with an N-terminal kinase domain (KD) and a regulatory C-terminus, a glycogen-binding β-subunit, and an AMP/ADP/ATP-binding γ-subunit"].
Direct beta-1 structural work crystallized human alpha1/beta1/gamma1 AMPK
[PMID:29480465, "full-length human AMPK α1β1γ1"; PMID:30478170,
"determined the crystal structures of fully active phosphorylated AMPK α1β1γ1"].

PAINT asserts the five PTHR10343 root terms on PRKAB1: nucleus, cytoplasm,
nucleotide-activated protein kinase complex, protein kinase binding, and signal
transduction. The complex and protein-kinase-binding rows are correct for the
human AMPK beta branch. Cytoplasm is also correct. Nucleus/nucleoplasm and broad
signal transduction are real but secondary relative to the core heterotrimeric
energy-sensing scaffold function.

## Peripheral rows

The positive regulation of cold-induced thermogenesis rows trace to mouse AMPK
beta-1/beta-2 adipocyte knockouts. The phenotype is real: deleting both beta
subunits in adipocytes produced cold intolerance and a brown-adipose defect
[PMID:27411013, "found that iβ1β2AKO mice\nwere cold intolerant and resistant to β-adrenergic activation of BAT and beiging"].
This is a tissue-level role of beta-containing AMPK complexes rather than a
separate core molecular function of PRKAB1.

Reactome:R-HSA-9931292 describes metformin-activated AMPK phosphorylating the ER
substrate PD-L1/CD274 [Reactome:R-HSA-9931292, "AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK), activated in response to metformin, directly binds with PD-L1 (CD274) and phosphorylates S195 of PD-L1."]. The row places PRKAB1 in the
endoplasmic-reticulum lumen, but the beta subunit is cytosolic/nuclear and the
Reactome event is consistent with a kinase acting from the cytosolic face, not
with a soluble ER-lumen pool.

The many GO:0005515 rows were reviewed uniformly under the project
protein-binding policy. They may be true physical interactions, but the bare
term is not an informative molecular function and is removed without claiming
the underlying interaction is false.
