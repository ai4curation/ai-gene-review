# ARHGEF33 notes

- PubMed recall (6 hits, 2026-10-04). The only functional study is PMID:33293601:
  - Mouse Arhgef33 (cloned from cerebellum cDNA) activates RhoA in a Rhotekin-RBD pull-down in HEK293 cells.
  - It causes contraction and ROCK-dependent inhibition of neurite extension.
  - Zebra finch retinal expression in parafoveal Müller glia.
- Other hits are omics or association mentions (cerebellar spheroids, colorectal WGS, lung cancer ROH, specialized-tissue proteomics, C/EBPdelta targets).
- GOA: GEF activity (IEA, InterPro DH) → ACCEPT, supported by the mouse ortholog data.
- The PANTHER family PTHR46944 has no IBA rows on the human gene.
