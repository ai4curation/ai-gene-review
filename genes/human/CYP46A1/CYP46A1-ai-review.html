Gene Description

CYP46A1 (cholesterol 24-hydroxylase, cholesterol 24S-hydroxylase, CH24H; EC 1.14.14.25) is a microsomal, heme-thiolate cytochrome P450 monooxygenase expressed predominantly in brain neurons. Anchored to the endoplasmic reticulum membrane by a single N-terminal transmembrane helix, it catalyzes the O2- and NADPH-cytochrome P450 reductase-dependent hydroxylation of cholesterol (with S stereochemistry) at C-24 of the side chain, producing (24S)-hydroxycholesterol. This is the principal, rate-limiting route for eliminating cholesterol from the central nervous system: unlike cholesterol, 24S-hydroxycholesterol readily crosses membranes and the blood-brain barrier, is exported to the plasma and delivered to the liver, where it can enter the alternative bile-acid pathway. 24S-hydroxycholesterol also acts as a signaling oxysterol and nuclear-receptor (LXR) ligand and helps regulate neuronal cholesterol homeostasis and cerebral cholesterol biosynthesis. As a P450 with a conformationally flexible active site, CYP46A1 has broad in vitro substrate specificity, further oxidizing sterol intermediates (7-dehydrocholesterol, desmosterol, cholestanol, 7alpha-hydroxycholesterol), C21 steroid hormones (progesterone, testosterone), and xenobiotics. It is a well-characterized drug target in Alzheimer's disease and other neurodegenerative disorders.

Biological role: CNS cholesterol elimination

  • CYP46A1 is the principal, rate-limiting route for eliminating cholesterol from the
    brain. 24S-hydroxycholesterol (24S-HC), unlike cholesterol, can cross membranes and
    the blood-brain barrier, is exported to plasma/liver and enters the bile-acid pathway.
    PMID:10377398
  • The reaction is also the first step in enzymatic degradation of brain cholesterol.
    PMID:14640697
  • Expressed predominantly in the brain; localized to neurons.
    PMID:10377398 and PMID:10377398
  • 24S-HC is exported to the liver and converted to bile acids/salts; only a minor bile
    acid source but critical for disposing of excess brain cholesterol.

  cholesterol homeostasis and cerebral cholesterol biosynthesis. As a P450 with
  a conformationally flexible active site, CYP46A1 has broad in vitro substrate
  specificity, further oxidizing sterol intermediates (7-dehydrocholesterol,
  desmosterol, cholestanol, 7alpha-hydroxycholesterol), C21 steroid hormones
  (progesterone, testosterone), and xenobiotics. It is a well-characterized drug
  target in Alzheimer's disease and other neurodegenerative disorders.
