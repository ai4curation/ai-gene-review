CC   -!- FUNCTION: Small GTPase that cycles between an active GTP-bound and an
CC       inactive GDP-bound state and mainly functions in vesicle-mediated
CC       endoplasmic reticulum (ER) to Golgi transport. The active GTP-bound
CC       form inserts into the endoplasmic reticulum membrane where it recruits
CC       the remainder of the coat protein complex II/COPII. The coat protein
CC       complex II assembling and polymerizing on endoplasmic reticulum
CC       membrane is responsible for both the sorting of cargos and the
CC       deformation and budding of membranes into vesicles destined to the
CC       Golgi (PubMed:23433038, PubMed:32358066, PubMed:36369712). The GTPase
CC       activity of SAR1 by controlling the timing of COPII budding regulates
CC       the size of the formed vesicles and is important for cargo selection
CC       depending on their size (PubMed:32358066). Together with SEC16A, forms
CC       the organized scaffold defining endoplasmic reticulum exit sites
CC       (ERES), some specific domains of the endoplasmic reticulum where COPII
CC       vesicles form (PubMed:17005010). In addition to its role in vesicle
CC       trafficking, can also function as a leucine sensor regulating TORC1
CC       signaling and more indirectly cellular metabolism, growth and survival.
CC       In absence of leucine, interacts with the GATOR2 complex via MIOS and
CC       inhibits TORC1 signaling. The binding of leucine abrogates the
CC       interaction with GATOR2 and the inhibition of the TORC1 signaling. This
CC       function is completely independent of the GTPase activity of SAR1B
CC       (PubMed:34290409). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17005010,
