# EDAR evidence notes

EDAR recognizes EDA-A1 and transmits a cytokine signal through a membrane receptor with an intracellular death domain. The death-domain name does not make cell death the primary physiological function. The central evidence supports developmental NF-kappaB signaling; JNK and overexpression-associated death require their experimental context.

PMID:10431241 established the human sequence, expression and disease association. Its receptor–ligand interpretation was a hypothesis at that time. Later positive studies support the modern receptor classification. PMID:12270937 concerns XEDAR experiments but explicitly states the prior EDAR disease and ligand findings; its EDAR TAS is retained without treating XEDAR assays as EDAR assays.
