# EDAR evidence notes

EDAR recognizes EDA-A1 and transmits a cytokine signal through a membrane receptor with an intracellular death domain. The death-domain name does not make cell death the primary physiological function. The central evidence supports developmental NF-kappaB signaling; JNK and overexpression-associated death require their experimental context.

PMID:10431241 established the human sequence, expression and disease association. Its receptor–ligand interpretation was a hypothesis at that time. Later positive studies support the modern receptor classification. PMID:12270937 concerns XEDAR experiments but explicitly states the prior EDAR disease and ligand findings; its EDAR TAS is retained without treating XEDAR assays as EDAR assays.

The complete PMID:42308199 notice was read alongside the relevant PMID:27144394 Methods, Results and Fig. 1C caption. The original article remains a preserved source with invalid status. Removing its unreliable source-specific support does not remove the independently verified EDA–EDAR interaction.

The human extracellular-domain screen in PMID:32822567 used multivalent fusion reagents. Its EDAR pairs were recovered from the actual source spreadsheets, not inferred from the main paper's network narrative. The selected EDAR ECD is 27–187, EDA is 63–391 and LILRB1 is 24–461. Binding in both orientations and controlled triplicate retests support the positive pair assertions. The study does not establish physiological LILRB1 activation by EDAR, and EDAR has no entry in the inspected SPR statistics workbook.

Mouse apical localization is a conserved polarized-cell context rather than a universal surface distribution. Current MGI records preserve the donor's positive plasma-membrane and apical IDAs. The source abstract describes lumen/acinar localization, but the unavailable full microscopy methods and images were not reconstructed. The existing PMID:19619491 cache retains its original full-text flag; actual content supplies Introduction and Discussion, with detailed Methods and Results absent.
