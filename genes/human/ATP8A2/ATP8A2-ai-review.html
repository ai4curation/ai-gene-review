Gene Description

ATP8A2 is a P4-type ATPase that couples ATP hydrolysis to the inward movement of aminophospholipids between membrane leaflets. Its principal substrate is phosphatidylserine, with weaker phosphatidylethanolamine transport. ATP8A2 forms a complex with the accessory subunit CDC50A/TMEM30A, which supports folding, exit from the endoplasmic reticulum and localization to Golgi and plasma membranes. Its lipid-transport activity maintains membrane asymmetry and supports neuronal and sensory-cell function.
