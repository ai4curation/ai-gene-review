# XPNPEP2 manual deep research

XPNPEP2 is human membrane-bound aminopeptidase P, a distinct gene product from
the soluble cytosolic XPNPEP1 enzyme. The original human study cloned membrane-bound
AP-P cDNAs by RT-PCR from kidney and lung poly(A)+ RNA and inferred that soluble
and membrane-bound AP-P are encoded by distinct genes or by transcripts with
different C termini [PMID:9375790].

The biochemical evidence for human XPNPEP2 comes from a recombinant soluble
ectodomain. Molinaro et al. expressed glycosylated human membrane-bound AP-P and
showed that it was functional against internally quenched fluorescent substrates
with affinities similar to its natural kinin substrates [PMID:15361070]. The
UniProt record curates the mature protein as a signal-peptide-containing,
GPI-anchored plasma membrane metalloprotease.

Reactome places XPNPEP2 in a set of GPI-anchored cell-surface proteins that can
be released by GPLD1, consistent with an ectoenzyme whose catalytic domain faces
the extracellular region [Reactome:R-HSA-8940388]. Two urinary exosome proteomic
studies detected XPNPEP2 in vesicles from epithelial cells facing the urinary
space [PMID:19056867, PMID:21082674]; this is credible peripheral localization
evidence rather than a separate XPNPEP2 mechanism.
