# XPNPEP2 notes

## 2026-11-26 review

- PANTHER family: `PTHR43763`, subfamily `PTHR43763:SF4`.
- No IRD or IKR PAINT loss falls on the human XPNPEP2 lineage.
- The current human GOA has no IBA rows, so this review does not need a family-review
  exception or PAINT pruning request.
- XPNPEP2 is the human membrane-bound AP-P paralog. Venema et al. cloned the
  human membrane-bound cDNA from kidney and lung RNA and showed that the soluble
  and membrane-bound human forms are distinct gene products [PMID:9375790].
- Molinaro et al. engineered a soluble form of human membrane-bound AP-P and
  showed that it was a functional enzyme on kinin-like internally quenched
  substrates [PMID:15361070]. This directly supports a new bradykinin catabolic
  process row.
- The membrane row from the 1997 cDNA paper is true but less specific than plasma
  membrane, because UniProt and Reactome place mature XPNPEP2 as a GPI-anchored
  cell-membrane enzyme.
- The two urinary exosome rows are both retained as non-core locations for a
  GPI-anchored urinary-tract membrane protein [PMID:19056867, PMID:21082674].
