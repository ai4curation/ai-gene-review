CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the regulation of phototransduction, playing a
CC       key role in signal termination after rhodopsin (RHO) has been activated
CC       by light in rod photoreceptors. Binds to photoactivated and
CC       phosphorylated RHO, preventing its interaction with transducin (GNAT1)
CC       and terminating transducin-mediated opsin signaling (By similarity).
CC       May play a role in preventing light-dependent degeneration of retinal
CC       photoreceptor cells (PubMed:9565049). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08168,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:9565049}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer. Homodimer. Homotetramer (PubMed:21288033). Interacts
CC       with RHO (via the phosphorylated C-terminus) (PubMed:26200343,
CC       PubMed:28753425). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21288033,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26200343, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28753425}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Photoreceptor inner segment
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P20443}. Cell projection, cilium, photoreceptor
CC       outer segment {ECO:0000269|PubMed:3720866}. Membrane
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P20443}; Peripheral membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P20443}. Note=In dark-adapted retina, it is
CC       predominatly localized to the outer nuclear layer and photoreceptor
CC       inner segments (By similarity). Localized to photoreceptor outer
CC       segments in light-exposed retina. During light exposure it first
CC       accumulates in the proximal region of photoreceptor outer segments, and
CC       moves toward the distal tip of the outer segment as light exposure is
CC       prolonged (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P20443}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Detected in retina, in the proximal portion of the
CC       outer segment of rod photoreceptor cells (at protein level).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:3720866}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The C-terminus interferes with binding to non-phosphorylated
CC       RHO. Interaction with phosphorylated RHO triggers displacement of the
CC       C-terminus and leads to a conformation change that mediates high-
CC       affinity RHO binding. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08168}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Night blindness, congenital stationary, Oguchi type 1 (CSNBO1)
CC       [MIM:258100]: A non-progressive retinal disorder characterized by
CC       impaired night vision, often associated with nystagmus and myopia.
CC       Congenital stationary night blindness Oguchi type is an autosomal
CC       recessive form associated with fundus discoloration and abnormally slow
CC       dark adaptation. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15234147,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22665972, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7670478,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9452120}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
CC       affecting the gene represented in this entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Retinitis pigmentosa 47 (RP47) [MIM:613758]: A retinal
CC       dystrophy belonging to the group of pigmentary retinopathies. Retinitis
CC       pigmentosa is characterized by retinal pigment deposits visible on
CC       fundus examination and primary loss of rod photoreceptor cells followed
CC       by secondary loss of cone photoreceptors. Patients typically have night
CC       vision blindness and loss of midperipheral visual field. As their
CC       condition progresses, they lose their far peripheral visual field and
CC       eventually central vision as well. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9565049}.
CC       Note=The disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented
CC       in this entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Retinitis pigmentosa 96 (RP96) [MIM:620228]: An autosomal
CC       dominant form of retinitis pigmentosa, a retinal dystrophy belonging to
CC       the group of pigmentary retinopathies. Retinitis pigmentosa is
CC       characterized by retinal pigment deposits visible on fundus examination
CC       and primary loss of rod photoreceptor cells followed by secondary loss
CC       of cone photoreceptors. Patients typically have night vision blindness
CC       and loss of midperipheral visual field. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28549094,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33047631}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
CC       affecting the gene represented in this entry.
