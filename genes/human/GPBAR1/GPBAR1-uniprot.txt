CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor for bile acid (PubMed:12419312,
CC       PubMed:12524422, PubMed:32698187, PubMed:32747649, PubMed:35858343).
CC       Bile acid-binding causes a conformation change that triggers signaling
CC       via guanine nucleotide-binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the
CC       activity of downstream effectors, such as adenylate cyclase
CC       (PubMed:12419312, PubMed:12524422, PubMed:32698187, PubMed:32747649,
CC       PubMed:35858343). GPBAR1 is coupled to G(s) G proteins and mediates
CC       activation of adenylate cyclase activity (PubMed:12419312,
CC       PubMed:12524422, PubMed:32698187, PubMed:32747649, PubMed:35858343).
CC       Activated by bile acids, such as lithocholate, deoxycholate,
CC       chenodeoxycholate and cholate, in descending order (PubMed:12524422,
CC       PubMed:32698187). Apart from their role in lipid dietary absorption and
CC       cholesterol catabolism, bile acids act as an important signaling
CC       molecule, involved in processes, such as energy expenditure or tissue
CC       inflammation (PubMed:26541439). GPBAR1-mediated signaling promotes
CC       energy expenditure and adiposity reduction in brown adipose tissue by
CC       activating adenylate cyclase, leading to DIO2 activation (By
CC       similarity). Involved in bile acid promoted GLP-1 secretion (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q80SS6,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12419312, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12524422,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32698187, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32747649,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35858343, ECO:0000303|PubMed:26541439}.
