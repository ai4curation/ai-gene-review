## Function
- TGR5/M-BAR, class A GPCR for bile acids coupled to Gs/adenylyl cyclase. "Bile acids induced rapid and
  dose-dependent elevation of intracellular cAMP levels in BG37-expressing cells" [PMID:12419312]. TGR5 "is
  responsive to bile acids as a cell-surface receptor"; bile acids induce receptor internalization, ERK
  activation, GTPgammaS binding and cAMP [PMID:12524422].
- Cryo-EM TGR5-Gs structures [PMID:32747649, PMID:32698187]; biased signaling structures [PMID:35858343].
- Physiology (by similarity to mouse): energy expenditure in brown adipose (DIO2), GLP-1 secretion.
- Interaction with mortalin (HSPA9) in cholangiocarcinoma [PMID:31978385]; RAMP2 screen [PMID:39083597].

## Localization
- Seven-TM receptor, extracellular N-terminus aa 1-21; cell surface receptor that internalizes upon bile-acid
  stimulation [PMID:12524422]. Conclusion: plasma membrane (cell surface), with ligand-induced endocytic pool.
