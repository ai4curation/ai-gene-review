CC   -!- FUNCTION: Adapter protein implicated in the regulation of a large
CC       spectrum of both general and specialized signaling pathways
CC       (PubMed:15696159, PubMed:16511572, PubMed:36732624). Binds to a large
CC       number of partners, usually by recognition of a phosphoserine or
CC       phosphothreonine motif (PubMed:15696159, PubMed:16511572,
CC       PubMed:36732624). Binding generally results in the modulation of the
CC       activity of the binding partner (PubMed:16511572). Promotes
CC       inactivation of WDR24 component of the GATOR2 complex by binding to
CC       phosphorylated WDR24 (PubMed:36732624). Participates in the positive
CC       regulation of NMDA glutamate receptor activity by promoting the L-
CC       glutamate secretion through interaction with BEST1 (PubMed:29121962).
CC       Reduces keratinocyte intercellular adhesion, via interacting with PKP1
CC       and sequestering it in the cytoplasm, thereby reducing its
CC       incorporation into desmosomes (PubMed:29678907). Plays a role in
CC       mitochondrial protein catabolic process (also named MALM) that promotes
CC       the degradation of damaged proteins inside mitochondria
CC       (PubMed:22532927). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15696159,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16511572, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22532927,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29121962, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29678907,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36732624}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:17085597). Forms heterodimers with SFN,
CC       YWHAB or YWHAQ (By similarity). Part of a complex that contains DSG3,
CC       PKP1, YAP1 and YWHAG; the complex is required for localization of DSG3
CC       and YAP1 to the cell membrane in keratinocytes (PubMed:31835537).
CC       Interacts with YAP1 (By similarity). Interacts with SAMSN1 (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with RAF1, SSH1 and CRTC2/TORC2
CC       (PubMed:10433554, PubMed:15159416, PubMed:15454081). Interacts with
CC       ABL1 (phosphorylated form); the interaction retains it in the cytoplasm
CC       (PubMed:15696159). Interacts with GAB2 (PubMed:19172738). Interacts
CC       with MDM4 (phosphorylated); negatively regulates MDM4 activity toward
CC       TP53 (PubMed:16511572). Interacts with PKA-phosphorylated AANAT and
CC       SIRT2 (PubMed:11427721, PubMed:18249187). Interacts with the 'Thr-369'
CC       phosphorylated form of DAPK2 (PubMed:26047703). Interacts with PI4KB,
CC       TBC1D22A and TBC1D22B (PubMed:23572552). Interacts with SLITRK1
CC       (PubMed:19640509). Interacts with LRRK2; this interaction is dependent
CC       on LRRK2 phosphorylation (PubMed:28202711). Interacts with MARK2 and
CC       MARK3 (PubMed:16959763). Interacts with MEFV (PubMed:27030597).
CC       Interacts with ENDOG, TSC2 and PIK3C3; interaction with ENDOG weakens
CC       its interaction with TSC2 and PIK3C3 (PubMed:33473107). Interacts with
CC       (phosphorylated) WDR24 (PubMed:36732624). Interacts with BEST1; this
