CC   -!- FUNCTION: ATP-dependent DNA helicase that unwinds double-stranded
CC       (ds)DNA in a 3'-5' direction (PubMed:24816114, PubMed:25901030,
CC       PubMed:9388193, PubMed:9765292). Participates in DNA replication and
CC       repair (PubMed:12019152, PubMed:21325134, PubMed:23509288,
CC       PubMed:34606619). Involved in 5'-end resection of DNA during double-
CC       strand break (DSB) repair: unwinds DNA and recruits DNA2 which mediates
CC       the cleavage of 5'-ssDNA (PubMed:21325134). Stimulates DNA 4-way
CC       junction branch migration and DNA Holliday junction dissolution
CC       (PubMed:25901030). Binds single-stranded DNA (ssDNA), forked duplex DNA
CC       and Holliday junction DNA (PubMed:20639533, PubMed:24257077,
CC       PubMed:25901030). Unwinds G-quadruplex DNA; unwinding occurs in the 3'-
CC       5' direction and requires a 3' single-stranded end of at least 7
CC       nucleotides (PubMed:18426915, PubMed:9765292). Helicase activity is
CC       higher on G-quadruplex substrates than on duplex DNA substrates
CC       (PubMed:9765292). Telomeres, immunoglobulin heavy chain switch regions
CC       and rDNA are notably G-rich; formation of G-quadruplex DNA would block
CC       DNA replication and transcription (PubMed:18426915, PubMed:9765292).
CC       Negatively regulates sister chromatid exchange (SCE) (PubMed:25901030).
