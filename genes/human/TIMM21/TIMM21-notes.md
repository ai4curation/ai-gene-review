# TIMM21 notes

## ND literature-search citations (2026-11-06)

- existing_annotations[22], GO:0003674 molecular_function (ND, GO_REF:0000015), currently ACCEPT: may be stale.
  The falcon deep-research report (TIMM21-deep-research-falcon.md) cites human-focused literature that
  describes a non-enzymatic molecular role for TIMM21: it is an accessory TIM23 subunit that, with ROMO1,
  defines the TIM23SORT form of the translocase and supports lateral insertion of inner-membrane proteins
  (Crameri et al. 2024, Mol Cell Biol, DOI:10.1080/10985549.2024.2353652; Reyes et al. 2018, EMBO Mol Med,
  DOI:10.15252/emmm.201708698). It also cites a human complexome study (Guerrero-Castillo et al. 2021,
  DOI:10.15252/embj.2021108648) and TIMM21 overexpression rescue in NARP cybrids (Aiyar et al. 2014,
  DOI:10.1038/ncomms6585). If these papers identify an MF for human TIMM21 (and not only complex
  membership), the ND is stale. A curator should check. The action was not changed.
