# BICRA manual research and annotation review

## 2026-10-01 — biological synthesis

BICRA/GLTSCR1 is a defining, noncatalytic GBAF/ncBAF subunit. It is mutually exclusive with BICRAL, contributes to complex composition, and associates with BRD4 and chromatin. The central synthesis is GBAF-dependent chromatin remodeling and transcription regulation. The ATPase activity measured in a BICRA immunoprecipitate belongs to an assembly containing SMARCA2/SMARCA4; it is not evidence that isolated BICRA hydrolyses ATP. Consequently the core uses `contributes_to_molecular_function`, with GBAF under `in_complex` and nucleoplasm under `locations`.

[PMID:29374058](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29374058/) provides the principal biochemical support. The existing normal cache contains fuller text than the recovered abstract-only alternative and was preserved. Its cached Results and Discussion show stable complex association, gradient separation from canonical BAF and PBAF, mutually exclusive BICRA/BICRAL occupancy, effects on complex composition, chromatin association and BRD4 co-immunoprecipitation. BICRA knockout impairs PC3 proliferation and colony formation but does not produce the same viability effect in several other cell lines. In LNCaP cells it increases MYC expression in a BRD4-sensitive context. Transcriptional effects therefore should not be described as uniformly activating.

[PMID:21555454](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21555454/) supports association between tagged GLTSCR1 and endogenous BRD4 in human 293T cells and positive regulation of selected BRD4 target transcripts in C33A cells. Selected indexed official PMC Results were read beyond the normal abstract. The viral reporter alone is complicated by a small reduction in E2 protein, but PIM2, CCND1 and DCPS transcript results provide separate support. NSD3/JMJD6 promoter ChIP and NSD3-associated methylation are not BICRA assays. Nuclear-targeting details concern BRD4 constructs and are not proof that every endogenous BICRA isoform was localized.

[PMID:30510198](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30510198/) establishes BICRA/BICRAL within GBAF and a role for the complex in naive mouse ESC identity. The principal functional interventions affect BRD9. Selected Discussion notes that Gltscr1 deletion alone did not impair pluripotency and proposes paralog compensation. The two developmental annotations are retained as non-core, explicitly qualified complex-associated roles; they do not claim a unique BICRA requirement in human stem cells. This is neither a wrong-gene citation nor evidence that compensated BICRA cannot participate.

The NCK1/CRK rows from [PMID:17474147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17474147/) and CRK row from [PMID:35044719](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35044719/) retain their exact source partners. Complete abstracts and selected bait-table/Results material were read, but the exact BICRA supplemental pair entries were not inspected. These three rows are UNDECIDED, not removed because the gene is missing from an abstract or main-text search. The two independently supported BRD4 interaction rows are KEEP_AS_NON_CORE under the user's action definitions; generic wording alone is not evidence that an interaction is false.

The official [Reactome9932443](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9932443) and [Reactome9933236](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9933236) pages explicitly place the assembly reactions in human nucleoplasm. Their complete normal summaries and selected official identity/compartment/participant sections were read. The location reasons use that compartment evidence and do not substitute an assembly assertion for a localization assertion.

No claim is made that all molecular details remain unknown in the current literature.
