# BICRA manual research and annotation review

## 2026-10-01 — biological synthesis

BICRA/GLTSCR1 is a defining, noncatalytic GBAF/ncBAF subunit. It is mutually exclusive with BICRAL, contributes to complex composition, and associates with BRD4 and chromatin. The central synthesis is GBAF-dependent chromatin remodeling and transcription regulation. The ATPase activity measured in a BICRA immunoprecipitate belongs to an assembly containing SMARCA2/SMARCA4; it is not evidence that isolated BICRA hydrolyses ATP. Consequently the core uses `contributes_to_molecular_function`, with GBAF under `in_complex` and nucleoplasm under `locations`.

[PMID:29374058](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29374058/) provides the principal biochemical support. The existing normal cache contains fuller text than the recovered abstract-only alternative and was preserved. Its cached Results and Discussion show stable complex association, gradient separation from canonical BAF and PBAF, mutually exclusive BICRA/BICRAL occupancy, effects on complex composition, chromatin association and BRD4 co-immunoprecipitation. BICRA knockout impairs PC3 proliferation and colony formation but does not produce the same viability effect in several other cell lines. In LNCaP cells it increases MYC expression in a BRD4-sensitive context. Transcriptional effects therefore should not be described as uniformly activating.

[PMID:21555454](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21555454/) supports association between tagged GLTSCR1 and endogenous BRD4 in human 293T cells and positive regulation of selected BRD4 target transcripts in C33A cells. Selected indexed official PMC Results were read beyond the normal abstract. The viral reporter alone is complicated by a small reduction in E2 protein, but PIM2, CCND1 and DCPS transcript results provide separate support. NSD3/JMJD6 promoter ChIP and NSD3-associated methylation are not BICRA assays. Nuclear-targeting details concern BRD4 constructs and are not proof that every endogenous BICRA isoform was localized.

[PMID:30510198](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30510198/) establishes BICRA/BICRAL within GBAF and a role for the complex in naive mouse ESC identity. The principal functional interventions affect BRD9. Selected Discussion notes that Gltscr1 deletion alone did not impair pluripotency and proposes paralog compensation. The two developmental annotations are retained as non-core, explicitly qualified complex-associated roles; they do not claim a unique BICRA requirement in human stem cells. This is neither a wrong-gene citation nor evidence that compensated BICRA cannot participate.

The NCK1/CRK rows from [PMID:17474147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17474147/) and CRK row from [PMID:35044719](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35044719/) retain their exact source partners. Complete abstracts and selected bait-table/Results material were read, but the exact BICRA supplemental pair entries were not inspected. These three rows are UNDECIDED, not removed because the gene is missing from an abstract or main-text search. The two independently supported BRD4 interaction rows are KEEP_AS_NON_CORE under the user's action definitions; generic wording alone is not evidence that an interaction is false.

The official [Reactome9932443](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9932443) and [Reactome9933236](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9933236) pages explicitly place the assembly reactions in human nucleoplasm. Their complete normal summaries and selected official identity/compartment/participant sections were read. The location reasons use that compartment evidence and do not substitute an assembly assertion for a localization assertion.

## Current biological interpretation after feedback, 2026-10-01 UTC

BICRA is a noncatalytic GBAF/ncBAF subunit with two connected roles: it helps specify the remodeling complex, and it connects BRD4 with that machinery. The [2011 BRD4 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21555454/) reports human tagged-GLTSCR1/endogenous-BRD4 co-immunoprecipitation and an interaction network linking BRD4 to chromatin-remodeling components. The [2018 GBAF study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29374058/) establishes stable complex membership, composition and BRD4 association. The normal UniProt subunit annotation integrates these as a BRD4-to-GBAF bridge. This combined evidence supports GO:0030674 protein-macromolecule adaptor activity for the two BRD4 rows and a corresponding core function. It does not establish purified binary contacts throughout the assembly. The distinct complex-remodeling core retains contributes_to_molecular_function: BICRA is not the ATPase.

The UniProt INTERACTION section positively corroborates BICRA partners NCK1 (NbExp 3) and CRK (NbExp 2). The historical reasons were incomplete because they omitted that independent support. Those curated pair records are now cited explicitly. The remaining uncertainty concerns the exact BICRA peptide and study-specific evidence in the original 2007/2022 supplementary records; the counts alone do not assign individual experiments to each source row, and direct IntAct access failed. These three source assertions remain UNDECIDED under the supplied access-limitation rule, with no claim that the interactions are absent or wrong. Nor does a contact with a signaling adaptor establish that BICRA itself performs an adaptor function in that signaling pathway.

[GO:0140537](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0140537) currently states: “This activity does not occur at the promoter.” The positive cellular-transcript result remains accepted, but it does not locate BICRA-mediated activation relative to the promoter. NSD3/JMJD6 ChIP cannot supply that missing BICRA-specific distinction. The molecular-function row remains UNDECIDED; GO:0003713 is not substituted without evidence of the corresponding locus-associated mechanism. This is a specific mechanistic uncertainty, not a rejection of the paper's positive transcription result.

[Barish et al., PMID33232675](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7820627/) extends the biological picture beyond cultured-cell transcription. The human cohort has BICRA variants associated with neurodevelopmental abnormalities. Zebrafish bicra loss produces craniofacial phenotypes, while fly experiments identify a corresponding ncBAF assembly and context-dependent chromatin effects. These findings support developmental relevance in the description and reference findings. They do not independently establish BICRA-specific maintenance of human stem cells, or negate BICRAL compensation in the earlier mouse ESC work. Accordingly the two existing stem-cell/differentiation assertions remain non-core with their original species and complex-level limits; no new developmental process annotation is manufactured from necessity evidence.

GBAF is described biologically as a member of the mammalian SWI/SNF complex family. The reviewed GO identifiers distinguish GO:0140288 from GO:0016514 as sibling assemblies under their superfamily; the GBAF refinements therefore preserve the experimental complex identity without claiming a strict ontology child relationship. This biological use of SWI/SNF does not assert membership in every canonical BAF or PBAF assembly.

This appended interpretation supersedes the earlier KEEP_AS_NON_CORE decisions for the two BRD4-binding rows and the earlier omission of UniProt corroboration for NCK1/CRK. The previous journal, validation and publication entries remain unchanged historical records. All 22 source annotation objects and both alternative products remain exact. No new annotation row is added. The only action changes are the two evidence-backed BRD4 refinements; redundant reviewer reference lists already represented by supported_by are removed.

The current follow-up read complete cached interaction abstracts, selected 2011 indexed primary Results, the relevant canonical 2018 GBAF Results, UniProt SUBUNIT/INTERACTION/disease sections, current official ontology definitions and selected indexed 2020 developmental-paper sections.
