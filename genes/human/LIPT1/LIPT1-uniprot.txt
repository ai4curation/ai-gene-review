CC       (PubMed:29987032). Unable to catalyze the transfer of octanoyl from
CC       octanoyl-GCSH to PDCE2 (PubMed:29987032). In vitro, it is also able to
CC       catalyze the transfer of the lipoyl group from lipoyl-AMP to the
CC       specific lysine residue of lipoyl domains of lipoate-dependent enzymes
CC       but this reaction may not be physiologically relevant (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29987032, ECO:0000305|PubMed:29987032}.
