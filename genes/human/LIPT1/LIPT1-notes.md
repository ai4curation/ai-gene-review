- Physiological activity: lipoyl transfer from lipoyl-GCSH to E2 subunits (RHEA:16413)
  [PMID:29987032 "the physiologically relevant LIPT1 enzyme activity is transfer of lipoyl moieties from the H protein of the glycine cleavage system to the E2 subunits"].
- Cannot transfer octanoyl from octanoyl-GCSH (UniProt, from PMID:29987032).
- In vitro lipoyl-AMP transfer (GO:0017118) retained as over-annotation.
- GO:7770091 lipoyl-GcvH:protein N-lipoyltransferase activity exists in current GO (QuickGO)
  but not in the configured release; recorded as proposed term.
- Patients: loss of E2 lipoylation, GCSH lipoylation spared [PMID:24256811].
- PANTHER PTHR12561:SF3 (shared with E. coli LplA and B. subtilis LplJ, which are ligases).
