# TGM2 notes

## Manual review notes

- Keep protein-glutamine gamma-glutamyltransferase, glutaminase, and monoaminyltransferase molecular functions as direct catalytic activities.
- Keep `GTP binding` and the alpha1-adrenergic receptor/PLC pathway rows as real but non-core signaling evidence. Remove the old `GTPase activator activity` and small-GTPase-signaling transfers from guinea pig TGM2 because Gh/TGM2 binds and hydrolyzes GTP itself; the rows do not establish a GAP activity toward another GTPase.
- Mitochondrion is a root-organelle term with direct human support in PMID:24349085 and an agreeing PAINT node, so it should be accepted. The ER row propagated from ER calcium release is different: the paper does not localize TGM2 to the ER, only to a cytosolic/mitochondrial signaling axis that drains ER calcium stores.
- Treat `chromatin remodeling` as over-annotated. TGM2 writes serotonyl and dopaminyl marks on histone H3, but those annotations establish covalent histone modification, not ATP-dependent nucleosome remodeling.
- Preserve every IntAct `protein binding` row separately. Several share the same PMID but differ in WITH/FROM and must remain individual rows.
- The 1985 and 1993 CACAO keratinocyte-transglutaminase rows describe the membrane anchoring of TGM1 and should not be kept as TGM2 plasma-membrane evidence.
