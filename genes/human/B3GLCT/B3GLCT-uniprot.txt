CC   -!- FUNCTION: Beta-1,3-glucosyltransferase involved in one of the two
CC       pathways responsible for protein O-linked fucosylation, a unique post-
CC       translational modification of cysteine-knotted proteins that regulates
CC       various biological processes. This pathway targets proteins with
CC       Thrombospondin type-1 (TSP1) repeats (TSR) in the endoplasmic
CC       reticulum. It starts with POFUT2, which attaches fucose via an O-
CC       glycosidic bond to a conserved serine or threonine residue. B3GLCT
CC       extends this modification by transferring a glucose molecule from UDP-
CC       glucose to the fucose. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16899492}.
