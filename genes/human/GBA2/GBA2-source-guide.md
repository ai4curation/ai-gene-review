# GBA2 source guide

GBA2 is a nonlysosomal glucosylceramidase. Its single core function is GlcCer hydrolysis and catabolism; substrate-transfer capacity and downstream cytoskeletal/developmental outcomes are distinguished from that role.

The [manual experimental synthesis](GBA2-deep-research-manual.md) provides original article links, section locators, species/construct details and assay limits. It separates the directly measured GalCer-analog hydrolysis result from its unresolved physiological relevance, and distinguishes ER/Golgi ortholog observations from human cell-surface activity.

## Biological evidence

The selected human expression, mouse knockout, sterol-exchange and localization experiments are organized in the synthesis. The normal caches for PMID:17080196 and PMID:32144204 contain abstracts, although selected original full-text sections were independently accessible. The cache flags remain truthful; they do not erase the separate original reading. Other reference assessments distinguish complete main text from selected sections, and available-but-unread portions from inaccessible material.

The natural human products are Q9HCG7-1 (927 residues), Q9HCG7-2 (877) and Q9HCG7-3 (640). Direct FASTAs match the official alternative-sequence reconstructions. Sequence identity alone does not establish equivalent activity or localization; historical mouse/human reagents are not automatically mapped to every natural product.

The [reviewed human UniProt record](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9HCG7/entry) independently curates GalCer hydrolysis, bile-acid glucosyl transfer and reported plasma-membrane localization with publication-linked evidence. Three short record anchors are placed once in the review; related contexts link to the same record without repeating excerpts. These curated statements complement the original assay reading and do not prove universal topology or physiological GalCer clearance. The purified-human-enzyme abstract of PMID:9111029 also directly supports bile-acid glucoside cleavage, with its negative GlcCer result confined to those assay conditions.

The proposed GO:0016139 → GO:0016137 → GO:0005975 refinement was checked against [official typed paths](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/ontology/go/terms/GO%3A0016139/paths/GO%3A0005975?relations=is_a%2Cpart_of). Carbohydrate chemistry and glycoside catabolism therefore retain their individual evidence; no unverified hierarchical replacement is made.
