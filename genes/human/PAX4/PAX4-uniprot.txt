CC   -!- FUNCTION: DNA-binding transcription factor that plays a central role in
CC       the differentiation and development of type B pancreatic cells, which
CC       are located within the pancreatic islets of Langerhans responsible for
CC       the production and release of insulin (By similarity). Mainly acts as a
CC       transcription repressor that binds to a common element in the glucagon,
CC       insulin and somatostatin promoters and inhibits their expression (By
CC       similarity). Prevents differentiation of type A pancreatic cells,
CC       probably by repressing expression of ARX (By similarity). Competes with
CC       another transcription factor, PAX6, for this same promoter binding site
CC       (By similarity). Also plays a key role in intestinal enteroendocrine
CC       cell differentiation by (1) directly promoting differentiation of
CC       enterochromaffin cells and (2) preventing differentiation of other
CC       intestinal enteroendocrine cell types (PubMed:37883554). Prevents
CC       differentiation of other intestinal enteroendocrine cell types by
CC       repressing the expression of ARX, the master transcription factor for
CC       intestinal type-L, -N, -D, -I and -G enteroendocrine cell
CC       differentiation (PubMed:37883554). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P32115,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37883554}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 2]: Isoform 2 appears to be a dominant negative form
CC       antagonizing PAX4 transcriptional activity.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11263967}.

CC   -!- DISEASE: Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2D) [MIM:125853]: A multifactorial
CC       disorder of glucose homeostasis caused by a lack of sensitivity to
CC       insulin. Affected individuals usually have an obese body habitus and
CC       manifestations of a metabolic syndrome characterized by diabetes,
CC       insulin resistance, hypertension and hypertriglyceridemia. The disease
CC       results in long-term complications that affect the eyes, kidneys,
CC       nerves, and blood vessels. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11723072}. Note=Disease
CC       susceptibility may be associated with variants affecting the gene
CC       represented in this entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Diabetes mellitus, ketosis-prone (KPD) [MIM:612227]: An
CC       atypical form of diabetes mellitus characterized by an acute initial
CC       presentation with severe hyperglycemia and ketosis, as seen in classic
CC       type 1 diabetes, but after initiation of insulin therapy, prolonged
CC       remission is often possible with cessation of insulin therapy and
CC       maintenance of appropriate metabolic control. Metabolic studies show a
CC       markedly blunted insulin secretory response to glucose, partially
CC       reversible with the improvement of blood glucose control. Variable
CC       levels of insulin resistance are observed, especially in obese
CC       patients. Pancreatic beta-cell autoimmunity is a rare finding.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15509590}. Note=Disease susceptibility is
CC       associated with variants affecting the gene represented in this entry.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Maturity-onset diabetes of the young 9 (MODY9) [MIM:612225]: A
CC       form of diabetes that is characterized by an autosomal dominant mode of
CC       inheritance, onset in childhood or early adulthood (usually before 25
CC       years of age), a primary defect in insulin secretion and frequent
CC       insulin-independence at the beginning of the disease.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17426099}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
CC       affecting the gene represented in this entry.
