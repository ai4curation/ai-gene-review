## Source ledger from the annotation consultant

The following ledger records the consultant's own bounded reading and attributes the separate SOD3 primary consultation explicitly. It does not claim that root read every listed paper or experiment. - **10419525:** complete abstract. Recombinant human ATP7A and CXXC mutants were tested in cultured mammalian cells, including in-vitro vesicle copper transport and copper-hyperaccumulation phenotypes. This is direct transport evidence. The abstract does not describe a SOD assay. Full Methods and the listed 1999 erratum body were not read.
- **10484781:** complete abstract. Exofacial tag/surface-antibody uptake directly supports basal and copper-enhanced ATP7A surface cycling and the C-terminal dileucine endocytic signal. No complete Methods claim.
- **10497213:** complete abstract. HAH1/ATOX1 interaction is tested with both Menkes and Wilson metal-binding regions. Reversibility and metal-specificity work described specifically for Wilson is not transferred wholesale to ATP7A.
- **10567439:** complete abstract. Actual human breast localization differs between nonlactating and lactating tissue. Copper delivery to milk is proposed, not directly measured in the available abstract.
- **11092760:** complete abstract. ATP7A co-expression rescues tyrosinase activity and melanogenesis in Menkes fibroblasts; copper chelation and the catalytic Asp1044 substitution impair that rescue. ATP7A supplies copper; tyrosinase performs pigment chemistry.
- **12812980:** complete abstract. ATP7A is the cargo undergoing TGN/cell-surface cycling and Rac1-sensitive internalization. Its necessity as cargo does not create a NEW endocytosis or trafficking-machinery process assignment.
- **14572476, 15035611, 15670166:** complete abstracts. These are ATP7A metal-binding-domain structural/NMR studies, including human domain constructs and HAH1-to-domain Cu(I) transfer. They support domain ligand recognition, not full-length membrane flux or a permanently stable chaperone complex.
- **16051599:** complete abstract. AIPP1/PDZD11 interaction is found in a human library screen and confirmed in mammalian cells. The exact minimal PDZ-domain experiment was not read, so generic association is retained non-core rather than automatically replaced solely because the partner contains a PDZ domain.
