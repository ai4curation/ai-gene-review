## Structure / topology
- Six-TM protein with intracellular N- and C-termini; [PMID:10588738 "encodes a 339-amino acid protein with six potential membrane-spanning regions flanked by hydrophilic amino- and carboxyl-terminal domains"].
- Cryo-EM: homotrimer; heme b in TMD at the extracellular leaflet; mAb120.545 Fabs bind extracellular helices [PMID:32409586 "STEAP1 adopts a reductase-like conformation and interacts with the Fabs through its extracellular helices"].
- Lacks the N-terminal cytoplasmic F420H2:NADP+ oxidoreductase (FNO)-like NADPH-binding domain present in STEAP2-4.

## Function
- [PMID:32409586 "STEAP1 lacks an intracellular NADPH-binding domain and does not exhibit cellular ferric reductase activity"]
- STEAP4-NADPH-domain/STEAP1 chimera reduces Fe(III); proposed to act as ferric reductase within STEAP heterotrimers (speculative) [PMID:32409586 "suggesting that STEAP1 functions as a ferric reductase in STEAP heterotrimers"].
- Heme b coordinated by H175 and H268 [PMID:32409586 "Strictly conserved histidine residues H175 and H268 coordinate the central iron moiety of the heme prosthetic group"].

## Localization
- [PMID:10588738 "Protein analysis located STEAP at the cell surface of prostate-cancer cell lines"]; IHC at cell-cell junctions of prostate secretory epithelium.
- Plasma membrane by confocal (PMID:32409586). Endosome membrane only by similarity to mouse (UniProt ECO:0000250).
- Conclusion: plasma membrane (multi-pass), extracellular loops/helices exposed at cell surface (antibody/ADC target); N/C-termini cytoplasmic.
