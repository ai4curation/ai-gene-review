| **Wu et al., February 2023**, *Cell Death & Disease* — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41419-023-05653-y) | Immunoprecipitation–mass spectrometry of human sperm identified DNAH12 and DNAH1 as candidate DNALI1-associated proteins. DNAH12 remained detectable in DNALI1-mutant sperm, although DNAH1 and DNAH7 were lost. (pqac-00000035, pqac-00000037) | Supports an IDA assembly relationship and a hierarchy in which DNAH12 localization does not require DNALI1. Candidate association by IP–MS did not establish direct DNAH12–DNALI1 binding or assign DNAH12 to a native dynein subtype. |
| **Geng et al., 19 July 2024**, *iScience* — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.110366) | Biallelic DNAH12 variants occurred in **3/1,532 (0.2%)** infertile Chinese men. In patient AY0749, flagellar DNAH12 was absent and SPAG6 was largely lost, whereas DNAH1 and DNAI2 were preserved; sperm showed severe asthenozoospermia and central-pair loss. (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000049) | Human genetics and sperm localization support a causal flagellar role, especially in central-pair integrity. Only three cases were found and only one underwent detailed structural and protein analysis; preservation of DNAH1 differs from later patients. |
| **Yang et al., eLife reviewed preprint 27 August 2024; version of record 27 March 2025** — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.100350) | Screening **796** infertile men identified recessive DNAH12 variants in six unrelated families. Patient sperm and two mouse models showed DNAH12 loss, impaired IDAs and central-pair loss. Mouse-testis reciprocal co-IP demonstrated association with DNALI1 and DNAH1; DNAH12 loss prevented their flagellar recruitment. Tracheal ciliary motility and ultrastructure were not detectably affected. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000027, pqac-00000032) | Strongest mechanistic evidence that DNAH12 acts upstream in sperm-flagellar IDA assembly. The respiratory-cilia conclusion rests mainly on mouse observations and absence of reported PCD in affected humans; it does not exclude subtle or tissue-specific ciliary effects. |
| **Hu et al., January 2025**, *Cell Communication and Signaling* — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12964-025-02043-z) | IQUB-associated radial-spoke-1 loss reduced DNAH12 and DNAH7 in human patient and knockout-mouse sperm, placing these IDA proteins near RS1. (pqac-00000042) | Independent but indirect support for DNAH12 coupling to the radial-spoke–IDA neighborhood. IQUB—not DNAH12—was perturbed, and the study did not demonstrate direct binding or uniquely assign DNAH12 to a specific dynein species. |


*Table: Four primary studies define DNAH12 as a sperm-flagellar inner-dynein-arm assembly factor while highlighting unresolved questions about direct motor activity, exact dynein-subtype assignment, and respiratory-cilia relevance.*
