Gene Description

DOCK11 is a large CDC42-directed guanine-nucleotide exchange factor of the DOCK-D family. Its DHR2 region promotes nucleotide exchange, while additional N-terminal contacts with active CDC42 support feedback activation. DOCK11 couples CDC42 signaling to actin-rich protrusion organization and contributes to immune-cell morphology and homeostasis. Positive filopodium formation is demonstrated during T-cell spreading, but the direction and shape of protrusion changes depend on cell type and stimulation. Mouse Dock11 also supports the marginal-zone B-cell compartment. Human loss-of-function variants cause immune dysregulation and hematologic abnormalities.

Core Functions

DOCK11 activates CDC42 by promoting guanine-nucleotide exchange, coupling small-GTPase signaling to actin-rich protrusion formation. Human DOCK11 constructs support CDC42 exchange activity, and patient-cell, knock-in and Jurkat knockdown/rescue experiments support a positive role in filopodium assembly during T-cell spreading on fibronectin. Protrusion and migration outcomes depend on cell type and assay conditions; this is not a universal prediction for every immune-cell state.
