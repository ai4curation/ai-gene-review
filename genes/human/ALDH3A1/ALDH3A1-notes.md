# ALDH3A1 (human, P30838) review notes

- Deep research: no provider deep-research file is available (no API keys in this session); the review
  rests on UniProt P30838, the cached literature and Reactome.
- Core: cytosolic NAD(P)+ aldehyde dehydrogenase for medium-chain and aromatic aldehydes
  [PMID:12943535 "Human ALDH3A1 demonstrated high substrate specificity for medium-chain (6 carbons and more) saturated and unsaturated aldehydes, including 4-hydroxy-2-nonenal"];
  corneal crystallin and UV absorber
  [PMID:17158879 "ALDH3A1 can reduce protein inactivation and/or aggregation not only by detoxification of reactive aldehydes but also by directly absorbing UV energy"].
- CoQ: in the ubiquinone module only as a rescuer of yeast hfd1
  [PMID:27693056 "Inactivation of the HFD1 gene in yeast resulted in Q deficiency, which was rescued by the human enzyme ALDH3A1"].
  This is heterologous capacity, not evidence of a role in human CoQ biosynthesis, so no ubiquinone or
  4-hydroxybenzaldehyde dehydrogenase term is proposed; raised as a suggested question and experiment.
- Membrane (IBA and Compara) marked over-annotated: the ALDH3 node's membrane term comes from anchored
  ALDH3A2/HFD1, and ALDH3A1 has no anchor. 3-chloroallyl aldehyde dehydrogenase (IBA) marked over-annotated.
