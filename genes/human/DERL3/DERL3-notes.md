# DERL3 curation notes

Deep-research services were unavailable for this family when reviewed. The
manual synthesis below uses the cached UniProt, GOA, PANTHER/PAINT, Reactome and
publication records.

DERL3 is the inducible glycoprotein-ERAD paralog of DERL2. Oda et al. described
DERL2 and DERL3 as UPR-regulated mammalian ERAD components and found that
overexpression of either protein accelerated, whereas knockdown of either
blocked, misfolded-glycoprotein degradation [PMID:16449189, "Overexpression of
Derlin-2 or -3 accelerated degradation of misfolded glycoprotein, whereas their
knockdown blocked degradation"]. The proteins associated with EDEM and p97,
placing them between luminal glycoprotein recognition and cytosolic extraction.

The PMID:22607976 GO rows come from the slowly-folding transthyretin quality
control branch. That paper frames STT3B-dependent post-translational
N-glycosylation as a mark that can send TTR into EDEM3-mediated ERAD
[PMID:22607976, "STT3B-dependent posttranslational N-glycosylation is part of a
triage-salvage system"]. This supports an ERAD role for DERL3 in that branch,
but not the claim that DERL3 itself executes protein N-linked glycosylation.

DERL3 also shares the stress-induced pre-emptive quality-control SRP/SRPR
interaction detected for Derlin-family proteins [PMID:26565908, "Derlin family
proteins (Derlins), which are ER-associated degradation components"]. As for
DERL2, the current PAINT slice no longer carries the cached GO:0005047 IBA, and
the GO:0030968 UPR IBA should not be treated as UPR signal-transducer evidence.
