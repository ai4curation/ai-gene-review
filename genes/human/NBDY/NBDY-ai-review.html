Gene Description

NBDY (NoBody, non-annotated P-body dissociating polypeptide) is a 68-amino-acid, intrinsically disordered microprotein translated from a small open reading frame in the LINC01420 transcript, previously annotated as a long non-coding RNA. It is conserved among mammals but has no homology to larger proteins or known domains. NBDY binds directly to the enhancer of decapping EDC4, through a short motif (residues 22-41, with Leu30 and Trp34 essential) that contacts the EDC4 N-terminal WD40 region and, more weakly, its C-terminal self-association domain; its C-terminal proline-rich segment also binds the EVH1 domain of DCP1A. Through these contacts it associates with the cytoplasmic mRNA decapping complex and localizes to processing bodies (P-bodies). NBDY acts as a negative regulator of P-body assembly: raising its level disperses P-bodies, and its loss increases P-body number. Its phosphorylation at Thr40 during mitosis (downstream of CDK activity) and at Ser61 after EGF receptor signalling (by protein kinase C) is required for P-body dissolution under those conditions, and the unphosphorylated protein can form liquid condensates with RNA in vitro that phosphorylation dissolves. NBDY does not change the catalytic activity of the decapping complex in vitro, but its loss alters the half-lives of more than 1,400 transcripts. DCP2 substrates are stabilized or destabilized depending on their 5' UTRs, which requires the EDC4 interaction; other transcripts change through secondary effects on gene expression.

Core Functions

NBDY is a disordered microprotein that binds the decapping enhancer EDC4 and negatively regulates P-body assembly. It binds EDC4 through its 22-41 motif, and its effect is tuned by phosphorylation at Thr40 in mitosis and Ser61 after EGF signalling. It competes with EDC4 self-association to disperse P-bodies into sub-microscopic decapping complexes. The protein has no known catalytic activity, and no specific GO molecular function term describes this regulatory binding, so molecular_function is left unset.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should NBDY be annotated as part of the RNA decapping complex (GO:0098745)? It co-purifies with and binds EDC4 and DCP1A directly, but it does not change the complex's in vitro activity, and it may be a regulatory binder rather than a stoichiometric subunit.

Q: How does one EDC4-binding microprotein both stabilize and destabilize different DCP2 substrates, and is the 5' UTR-length dependence a direct effect on substrate access to DCP2?

Q: Is the in vitro NBDY-RNA coacervation (critical concentration about 150 uM) relevant at endogenous NBDY concentrations, or is phospho-regulation of P-bodies mediated entirely through EDC4?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Measure decapping of specific DCP2 substrates (e.g. EPC2, GJC1) in vitro with purified decapping complex, with and without recombinant NBDY and full-length capped transcripts carrying native 5' UTRs.

Type: in vitro biochemistry

Experiment: Determine the stoichiometry of endogenous NBDY relative to EDC4 and DCP2 by quantitative proteomics, and test whether phospho-mutants (T40A, S61A) change EDC4 self-association in cells.

Type: quantitative proteomics / co-immunoprecipitation

Gene product

  • 68-aa intrinsically disordered microprotein encoded by a smORF in the lncRNA-annotated
    locus LINC01420 (Xq). UniProt: "Was previously thought to be non-coding but has been
    shown to be translated and functional." Whole chain predicted disordered (MobiDB-lite);
    C-terminal proline-rich region.
  • Conserved in mammals only; no homology to known domains
    PMID:27918561.

Discovery paper: D'Lima et al. 2017 (PMID:27918561, full text cached)

  • Affinity purification-MS: "only twelve proteins were specifically enriched >2-fold by
    NoBody, all of which are components of mRNA decapping and 5'-to-3' mRNA decay complexes".
  • Direct binding to EDC4: GST pull-down plus benzophenone photo-crosslinking
    PMID:27918561.
    Residues 22-41 necessary and sufficient; alanine scan: L30 and W34 required.
  • EDC4 knockdown abolishes co-purification of other decapping factors
    PMID:27918561.
  • P-bodies: overexpression disperses P-bodies (not degradation of components), requires the
    peptide not the RNA; siRNA knockdown increases P-bodies 1.5-2x
    PMID:27918561.
  • Localization: at low expression co-localizes with EDC4 in P-bodies
    PMID:27918561.
  • NMD substrate (Calu-6 p53 mRNA): knockdown DECREASED, overexpression INCREASED steady-state
    p53 mRNA. Authors explicitly say decay rate not measured
    PMID:27918561
    and interpret as negative regulation of decay
    PMID:27918561.
    => The GOA IDA "nuclear-transcribed mRNA catabolic process" (GO:0000956) is based on this
    steady-state measurement. NBDY does not catalyse any step of mRNA breakdown; the direction of
    the effect (if anything) is inhibitory. Not participation.

Na et al. 2020 Biochemistry (PMID:33059440, full text cached)

  • Endogenous NBDY binds EDC4 WD40 domain directly; DCP1A EVH1 binds NBDY C-terminal polyproline.
  • NBDY KO HEK293T: 3x more P-bodies, rescued by re-expression; decreased viability.
  • TimeLapse-seq: >1400 half-lives changed (1200 stabilized, 226 destabilized)
    PMID:33059440.
  • DCP2 substrates stabilized OR destabilized depending on 5' UTR length; requires EDC4
    interaction (W34A).
  • NBDY does not change in vitro decapping activity of immunopurified complex
    PMID:33059440.
  • Non-DCP2 targets stabilized indirectly through reduced synthesis of NMD factor transcripts.
  • => Best process term for this: GO:0043488 regulation of mRNA stability ("Includes processes
    that both stabilize and destabilize mRNAs"). Used as MODIFY replacement for GO:0000956.

Na et al. 2021 JACS (PMID:34346674, full text)

  • NBDY + RNA undergo LLPS in vitro (complex coacervation with polyU; needs PEG crowder;
    critical concentration 150 uM - far above cellular).
  • Phosphorylation: T40 at G2/M (CDK-dependent), S61 after EGF (PKC; PKCalpha phosphorylates in
    vitro). Non-phosphorylatable mutants abrogate mitotic / EGF-induced P-body loss.
    PMID:34346674.
  • Strengthens GO:0010607 negative regulation of cytoplasmic mRNA processing body assembly.
  • In vitro RNA LLPS not proposed as NEW (reductionist, high concentration; RNA binding not
    shown to be sequence-specific or relevant in cells).

Bloch et al. 2023 PLoS One (PMID:36877681) - EDC4 two-hybrid; NBDY-EDC4 interaction in cells

requires EDC4 N-terminal WD40 region. Supports EDC4 binding.

Cheng et al. 2025 RNA (PMID:40360209)

  • NBDY 22-41 binds EDC4 C-terminal domain directly and competes EDC4-EDC4 self-association;
    disrupts P-bodies without affecting tethered mRNA decay
    PMID:40360209.
    Further argues against NBDY participating in mRNA catabolism per se.

Liu & Wang 2023 JACS Au (PMID:37772172): MD simulation of ATP effect on NBDY clusters. Low

relevance, computational only.

LINC01420 cancer papers: treat the locus as lncRNA; not about the NBDY protein. Not used.
