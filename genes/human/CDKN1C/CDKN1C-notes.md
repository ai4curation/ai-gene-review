# CDKN1C notes

## 2026-10-10 — primary-source annotation audit

CDKN1C/p57 is a CIP/Kip cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor. The representative core is nuclear CDK inhibition coupled to restraint of the mitotic cell cycle. The biochemical source describes p57 as intrinsically disordered [PMID:11746698, "largely unfolded"]. The foundational study reports cyclin-dependent binding and G1 arrest; its complete Methods were not recovered. The current interpretation does not borrow a p27 crystal structure to assert an atomic p57 ATP-blocking mechanism. Full Results/Methods in [PMID:19170105](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2981436/) independently compare human and mouse p57, cyclin-binding mutants, CDK2/histone-H1 kinase output and NPAT phosphorylation. The human WI38 reporter and HeLa depletion experiments are distinct from Cos7 overexpression and mouse MEF experiments.

Human genetics requires opposite disease mechanisms. The IMAGe source supports increased growth suppression [PMID:22634751, "suggesting a gain-of-function mechanism"]. Its human FLAG-CDKN1C/PCNA co-IP and Drosophila expression experiments are different assays. Wild-type p57 co-precipitates endogenous PCNA, whereas tested IMAGe alleles disrupt association; the study does not directly establish every downstream effect of changed ubiquitination or protein stability. BWS loss of function should not be assigned to IMAGe. Predominantly maternal expression is a property of the imprinted locus, not evidence that p57 protein creates that imprint.

### Developmental and transcriptional context

The electronic mouse transfers were traced to experimental records rather than inferred from the donor identifier alone. [PMID:19276117](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2664003/) directly compares p57-null and p27-knock-in mice: most skeletal, lens, intestinal, growth and reproductive defects improve; renal and placental abnormalities persist with altered expression patterns. These support contextual developmental roles, while the experiments do not isolate a single mechanism for every organ. [PMID:21729874](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3209650/) directly examines mutant placental architecture and stage-dependent growth, with explicit mouse strain and intrauterine-competition limits.

[PMID:9784491](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC317217/) compares p57/p27 mutant lens and placenta with BrdU, differentiation markers and histology. These assays establish restraint of cycling during differentiation, rather than the anaphase/telophase-to-G1 transition in GO:0007096. Its source-specific replacement is the existing negative-mitotic-cell-cycle term; no NEW process is proposed. The p27 replacement experiment in PMID:19276117 retains maternal-allele expression at the p57 locus. It does not show p57 protein establishing or propagating epigenetic imprints, so the genomic-imprinting protein annotation is removed on that specific source premise.

[PMID:14671317](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC307617/) examines mouse dopamine-neuron maturation and Nurr1 cooperation in MN9D cells. CDK-defective p57 retains cooperative maturation activity, and Nurr1 reporter suppression is promoter-context dependent. [PMID:15889154](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1142590/) Figure 6 directly tests mouse-p57 adenoviral rescue of MBT-1-deficient myeloid progenitors; human TF-1 expression kinetics are separate corroboration. Both existing developmental annotations are retained outside the representative core.

[PMID:20106982](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2843230/) supports transcriptional regulation but requires assay distinctions: Ad-p57 expresses mouse cdkn1c, whereas endogenous HeLa depletion independently affects CTD phosphorylation. Kinase experiments use immunopurified cellular complexes, and bacterial E2F1 binding assays use crude lysates. Neither experiment establishes sequence-specific DNA binding by p57. The specific RNAPII transcriptional process is retained as contextual; a second vague inhibitor core is unnecessary.

In [PMID:16943770](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2906388/), p57 is induced by TGF-beta and p57 depletion restores human limbal-cell BrdU/MTT proliferation. This directly supports downstream antiproliferative participation. It does not distinguish that from increased signaling propagation or feedback, so the positive-pathway-regulation specificity is marked over-annotated. Positive feedback could act downstream of the receptor; its existence is not excluded. Nuclear and cytoplasmic localization are retained, with the nucleus as the principal functional setting.
