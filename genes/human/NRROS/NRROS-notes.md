# NRROS (LRRC33) curation notes

Deep research (falcon/perplexity-lite) unavailable; notes from UniProt Q86YC3 and cached papers.

- Topology: signal 1-18, extracellular LRR region 19-650, TM 651-671, cytoplasmic 672-692 (type I).
- Milieu molecule for proTGF-beta1 [PMID:29909984 "LRRC33 on the surface of microglia functions as a milieu molecule and enables highly localized, integrin-αVβ8-dependent TGF-β activation."].
- Surface pool is the 120 kDa glycoform only; much of LRRC33 intracellular (PMID:29909984).
- ER colocalization with calnexin (PMID:32197075); disease variants impair active TGF-beta1 delivery.
- ROS hypothesis refuted: [PMID:29909984 "no difference was seen between Lrrc33−/− and WT macrophages in ROS generation"]; Cybb loss does not rescue.
- TLR inhibition (PMID:23545260) probably secondary to TGF-beta (UniProt).

Localization conclusion: dual - plasma membrane/cell surface (functional mature glycoform, type I, LRR
ectodomain extracellular) plus a large ER-membrane pool. Surface level is low/regulated (PMA increases).
