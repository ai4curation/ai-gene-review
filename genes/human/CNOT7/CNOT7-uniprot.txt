CC   -!- FUNCTION: Has 3'-5' poly(A) exoribonuclease activity for synthetic
CC       poly(A) RNA substrate (PubMed:19276069, PubMed:20634287,
CC       PubMed:31439799). Its function seems to be partially redundant with
CC       that of CNOT8 (PubMed:19605561). Catalytic component of the CCR4-NOT
CC       complex which is one of the major cellular mRNA deadenylases and is
CC       linked to various cellular processes including bulk mRNA degradation,
CC       miRNA-mediated repression, translational repression during
CC       translational initiation and general transcription regulation
CC       (PubMed:19276069, PubMed:20634287, PubMed:31439799). During miRNA-
CC       mediated repression the complex also seems to act as translational
CC       repressor during translational initiation (PubMed:20065043). Additional
CC       complex functions may be a consequence of its influence on mRNA
CC       expression (PubMed:19276069, PubMed:23236473). Associates with members
CC       of the BTG family such as TOB1 and BTG2 and is required for their anti-
CC       proliferative activity (PubMed:19276069, PubMed:23236473).
