CC   -!- FUNCTION: Bridge-like lipid transfer protein that functions as
CC       molecular bridges between endoplasmic reticulum and the membranes
CC       targeted for lipid delivery (PubMed:35491307, PubMed:40269155). Forms a
CC       tunnel with multiple beta-grooves that allows the transport of
CC       phospholipids (By similarity). Provides phosphatidylethanolamine for
CC       glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI) anchor synthesis in the endoplasmic
CC       reticulum (PubMed:35015055). Plays a role in endosomal trafficking and
CC       endosome recycling (PubMed:30906834). Also involved in the actin
CC       cytoskeleton and cilia structural dynamics (PubMed:30906834). Acts as a
CC       regulator of phagocytosis (PubMed:31540829). Required for the formation
CC       of endoplasmic reticulum-plasma membrane junctions which are critical
CC       for lipid exchange (PubMed:40269155).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A0K3AWP8, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30906834,
