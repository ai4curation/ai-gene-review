CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the transfer of the pyrophosphoryl group from ATP
CC       to the 1-hydroxyl of ribose-5-phosphate to form 5-phospho-ribosyl-1-
CC       pyrophosphate (PRPP), a key intermediate in the biosynthesis of several
CC       metabolites including nucleotides, dinucleotides and some amino acids
CC       (PubMed:37248548, PubMed:40295500, PubMed:8387514). Involved in the
CC       production of newly synthesized ATP/GTP through the purine biosynthesis
CC       pathway (PubMed:40295500). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37248548,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40295500, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8387514}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by magnesium (PubMed:8387514). Activated
CC       by inorganic phosphate (Pi) that binds D-ribose 5-phosphate binding
CC       site (PubMed:37248548, PubMed:8387514). Inhibited by GDP
CC       (PubMed:8387514). Feedback inhibition by purine nucleotides is less
CC       sensitive than inhibition by 2,3-diphosphoglycerate (PubMed:8387514).
CC       ADP can regulate enzyme activity through competitive inhibition and
CC       allosteric inhibition by affecting the conformation of regulatory
CC       flexible (RF) loop (PubMed:37248548). The ADP of allosteric site can
CC       interact with RF loop, leading to the regulation of ATP conformation in
CC       active site (PubMed:37248548). Competitive inhibition of ADP by binding
CC       to the ATP active site in the hexamer where also binds magnesium
CC       (PubMed:37248548). Thermal inactivation at 55 degrees Celsius
CC       (PubMed:8387514). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37248548,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8387514}.
