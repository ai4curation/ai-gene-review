CC   -!- FUNCTION: Probable substrate-recognition component of a SCF-like ECS
CC       (Elongin-Cullin-SOCS-box protein) E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase complex
CC       which mediates the ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal
CC       degradation of target proteins. Promotes differentiation and maturation
CC       of the vascular lineage by an oxygen-dependent mechanism (By
CC       similarity). Also acts as a negative regulator of GPS1, a component of
CC       the COP9 signalosome (CSN) multiprotein complex, thereby inhibiting the
CC       serine phosphorylation of IRS1 (By similarity). Regulates IRS4 levels
CC       by directing its degradation via ubiquitination and thereby decreases
CC       the downstream signal of IRS4 (PubMed:21955513). Plays a critical role
CC       during early vascular development and proper placentation.
CC       Mechanistically, negatively regulates the transcriptional regulator
CC       inhibitor of DNA binding 2/ID2 expression through polyubiquitination
CC       and proteasome dependent degradation (PubMed:24586788).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9WV71, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21955513,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24586788}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with HIF1AN. Component of an ECS (Elongin BC-CUL2/5-
CC       SOCS-box protein) E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase complex formed of CUL2 or
CC       CUL5, Elongin BC (ELOB and ELOC), RBX1 and ASB4. Interacts with IRS4
CC       (PubMed:21955513). Interacts with ID2 (PubMed:24586788).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9WV71, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21955513,
