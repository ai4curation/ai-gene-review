Gene Description

CNTNAP2 encodes Caspr2, a single-pass neuronal membrane protein with a large extracellular adhesion domain and a short intracellular interaction tail. Caspr2 organizes contactin-associated Kv1 potassium-channel assemblies at juxtaparanodes of myelinated axons and also occupies context-dependent synaptic and intracellular trafficking pools. Its extracellular domain binds contactin family proteins under defined conditions, while its terminal peptide binds PDZ domains. These interactions support neuronal membrane organization and circuit maturation. Biallelic loss of CNTNAP2 causes severe neurodevelopmental disease with seizures and language impairment; broader common-variant associations have more limited causal resolution.
