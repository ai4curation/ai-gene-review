CC   -!- FUNCTION: Receptor for the C-X-C chemokine CXCL17, which plays a key
CC       role in lymphocyte homing (PubMed:39293486, PubMed:40279398). Ligand
CC       binding causes a conformation change that triggers signaling via
CC       guanine nucleotide-binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the
CC       activity of downstream effectors, such as RhoA pathway
CC       (PubMed:39293486). Activation by CXCL17 on lymphocytes activates the
CC       RhoA pathway, which regulates cytoskeletal dynamics and integrins,
CC       mediating lymphocyte recruitment into the respiratory, upper
CC       gastrointestinal, biliary and genito-urinary tracts (PubMed:39293486).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39293486, ECO:0000269|PubMed:40279398}.
