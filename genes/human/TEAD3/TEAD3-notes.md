# TEAD3 curation notes

- Keep conserved TEAD-family DNA-binding, Pol II transcription-regulator, nuclear/nucleoplasmic, transcription-regulator-complex, Hippo-signaling, and YAP/TAZ coactivator-binding functions.
- Preserve and review each `GO:0005515` row independently; YAP/TAZ rows can be modified to `transcription coactivator binding`, whereas unrelated high-throughput interaction rows stay uninformative.
- Do not propagate the C. elegans EGL-44/EGL-46 neuroblast annotations as human TEAD3 functions. The cached abstract explicitly places that experiment in C. elegans.
- Treat the old placenta-expression/female-pregnancy row as over-annotated: human TEAD3 binds a placental enhancer, but that does not make the gene product a direct participant in the pregnancy process.
