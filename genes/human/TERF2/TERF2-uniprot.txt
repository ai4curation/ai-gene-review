CC       Component of the shelterin complex (telosome) that is involved in the
CC       regulation of telomere length and protection (PubMed:16166375).
CC       Shelterin associates with arrays of double-stranded 5'-TTAGGG-3'
CC       repeats added by telomerase and protects chromosome ends; without its
CC       protective activity, telomeres are no longer hidden from the DNA damage
CC       surveillance and chromosome ends are inappropriately processed by DNA
CC       repair pathways (PubMed:16166375). Together with DCLRE1B/Apollo, plays
CC       a key role in telomeric loop (T loop) formation by generating 3'
CC       single-stranded overhang at the leading end telomeres: T loops have
CC       been proposed to protect chromosome ends from degradation and repair
CC       (PubMed:20655466). Required both to recruit DCLRE1B/Apollo to telomeres
CC       and activate the exonuclease activity of DCLRE1B/Apollo
