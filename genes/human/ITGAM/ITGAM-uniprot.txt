CC   -!- FUNCTION: Integrin ITGAM/ITGB2 is implicated in various adhesive
CC       interactions of monocytes, macrophages and granulocytes as well as in
CC       mediating the uptake of complement-coated particles and pathogens
CC       (PubMed:20008295, PubMed:9558116). It is identical with CR-3, the
CC       receptor for the iC3b fragment of the third complement component. It
CC       probably recognizes the R-G-D peptide in C3b. Integrin ITGAM/ITGB2 is
CC       also a receptor for fibrinogen and factor X. It recognizes P1 and P2
CC       peptides of fibrinogen gamma chain. Regulates neutrophil migration
CC       (PubMed:28807980). In association with beta subunit ITGB2/CD18,
CC       required for CD177-PRTN3-mediated activation of TNF primed neutrophils
CC       (PubMed:21193407). Integrin ITGAM/ITGB2 is also a receptor for ICAM1
CC       ligand ensuring adhesion between stimulated neutrophils and stimulated
CC       endothelial cells (PubMed:1980124). May regulate phagocytosis-induced
CC       apoptosis in extravasated neutrophils (By similarity). May play a role
CC       in mast cell development (By similarity). Required with TYROBP/DAP12 in
CC       microglia to control production of microglial superoxide ions which
CC       promote the neuronal apoptosis that occurs during brain development (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P05555, ECO:0000269|PubMed:1980124,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20008295, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21193407,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28807980, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9558116, ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterodimer of an alpha and a beta subunit. ITGAM associates
CC       with ITGB2 (Probable). Found in a complex with CD177 and ITGB2/CD18
CC       (PubMed:21193407). Interacts with JAM3 (PubMed:12208882,
CC       PubMed:15194813). Interacts with THBD (PubMed:27055590). Interacts with
CC       complement factor H/CFH; this interaction mediates adhesion of
CC       neutrophils to pathogens leading to pathogen clearance
CC       (PubMed:20008295, PubMed:9558116). Interacts with TMEM268; this
CC       interaction inhibits ITGAM degradation via the endosome-lysosome
CC       pathway (PubMed:38730209). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12208882,
