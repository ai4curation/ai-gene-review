CC   -!- FUNCTION: As a component of the GATOR1 complex functions as an
CC       inhibitor of the amino acid-sensing branch of the mTORC1 pathway
CC       (PubMed:23723238, PubMed:25457612, PubMed:29590090, PubMed:29769719,
CC       PubMed:31548394, PubMed:35338845). In response to amino acid depletion,
CC       the GATOR1 complex has GTPase activating protein (GAP) activity and
CC       strongly increases GTP hydrolysis by RagA/RRAGA (or RagB/RRAGB) within
CC       heterodimeric Rag complexes, thereby turning them into their inactive
CC       GDP-bound form, releasing mTORC1 from lysosomal surface and inhibiting
CC       mTORC1 signaling (PubMed:23723238, PubMed:25457612, PubMed:29590090,
CC       PubMed:29769719, PubMed:35338845). In the presence of abundant amino
CC       acids, the GATOR1 complex is negatively regulated by GATOR2, the other
CC       GATOR subcomplex, in this amino acid-sensing branch of the TORC1
CC       pathway (PubMed:23723238, PubMed:25457612, PubMed:29769719). Within the
CC       GATOR1 complex, DEPDC5 mediates direct interaction with the nucleotide-
CC       binding pocket of small GTPases Rag (RagA/RRAGA, RagB/RRAGB, RagC/RRAGC
CC       and/or RagD/RRAGD) and coordinates their nucleotide loading states by
CC       promoting RagA/RRAGA or RagB/RRAGB into their GDP-binding state and
CC       RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD into their GTP-binding state (PubMed:29590090,
CC       PubMed:35338845). However, it does not execute the GAP activity, which
CC       is mediated by NPRL2 (PubMed:29590090). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23723238,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25457612, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29590090,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29769719, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31548394,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35338845}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Within the GATOR complex, component of the GATOR1 subcomplex,
CC       made of DEPDC5, NPRL2 and NPRL3 (PubMed:23723238, PubMed:25366275,
CC       PubMed:29590090, PubMed:35338845). GATOR1 mediates the strong
CC       interaction of the GATOR complex with small GTPases Rag (RagA/RRAGA,
CC       RagB/RRAGB, RagC/RRAGC and/or RagD/RRAGD) heterodimers
