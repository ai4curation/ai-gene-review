Gene Description

DEPDC5 is the large regulatory subunit of GATOR1, a complex that suppresses mTORC1 signaling during amino acid deprivation. At the lysosomal surface, DEPDC5 organizes the NPRL2–NPRL3 module and contacts Rag GTPases in distinct binding states. It helps coordinate substrate engagement and Rag nucleotide loading, while NPRL2 provides the catalytic arginine finger for GTP hydrolysis. KICSTOR recruits GATOR1 to lysosomes, enabling nutrient signals to control this growth-regulatory pathway.

2026-10-17 — human DEPDC5 source review

Human DEPDC5 is the large GATOR1 subunit. The biochemical distinction is between binding and organizing Rag GTPases, which DEPDC5 performs, and the catalytic arginine finger supplied by NPRL2. Human GATOR1 structures and reconstitution experiments support one core: small GTPase binding, contribution to complex GAP activity, and negative regulation of TORC1 signaling at the lysosomal surface. DEPDC5 alone was inactive in the tested GAP assay. This does not imply that DEPDC5 is dispensable for complex function (PMID:23723238(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23723238/), PMID:29590090(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29590090/), PMID:35338845(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35338845/)).

KICSTOR recruits GATOR1 to lysosomes. Several recruitment experiments image NPRL2 as a complex marker, while the earlier study images tagged DEPDC5 itself. Native human placental proteomics separately identifies DEPDC5 in enriched lysosomal membranes. These observations support membrane association; they do not establish membrane-spanning topology. Mouse neuronal cytosolic/perinuclear staining and corroborating human neural-culture experiments identify another observed pool, with the localization study’s stated preliminary limitation (PMID:28199306(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28199306/), PMID:17897319(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17897319/), PMID:23542697(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23542697/)).

The eight HuRI annotations all refer to the 559-residue O75140-2 product. Each exact human pair has validated two-hybrid, array and pooling records, with sequence checksums matching the current target and partner. The main study describes quadruplicate retesting and sequence confirmation; no pair-specific orthogonal-validation result is claimed. Binding to USP2, CCT3 and the identified MEOX2 clone supports finer partner-class terms. The other five positive associations remain non-core. None establishes a native cellular complex, full-length DEPDC5 activity, or a transcription, repair or phosphatase mechanism for DEPDC5 (PMID:32296183(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32296183/)).

The exact MEOX2 partner is Q6FHY5, an unreviewed 303-residue sequence differing from reviewed MEOX2 by one glutamine deletion outside the homeodomain.
