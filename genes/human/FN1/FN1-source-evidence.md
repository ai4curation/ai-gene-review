# FN1 evidence and source reconciliation

Fibronectin supplies extracellular structural material and distinct ligand-binding surfaces. The review represents five core activities: extracellular matrix structural contribution, integrin binding, collagen binding, heparin binding and proteoglycan binding. [The source guide](FN1-source-guide.md) gives the actual access and assay boundaries; [the context archive](FN1-source-contexts.json) preserves the original review and complete current source projection.

The structural core rests on fibronectin polymerization and scaffold maintenance, including actual human-cell matrix-blockade experiments. Separate reconstitution experiments establish retention of thrombospondin and collagen fibrils. Integrin binding is supported by defined human receptor/domain assays. The FNIII10 RGD mechanism is not generalized to α4β1 or every other receptor. Collagen, heparin and proteoglycan recognition have independent molecular evidence and are not each assigned speculative developmental processes.

The previous authored extracellular-matrix-organization proposal is retained with individually rebuilt primary IMP support from PMID:12388756. Fibronectin itself provides the scaffold, beyond merely being required as a substrate. Same-role comparator annotations and official process ancestry were checked, and the exact existing GO-CAM index had no FN1 entry. The two other authored proposals are retired: fibrinogen binding was supported by a fibrin paper, and broad coagulation duplicated the existing source-specific GO:0072378 process. Older positive fibrinogen-binding literature prevents a universal negative conclusion. Their complete original objects remain in the archive.

The 235 active rows comprise 223 typed current source contexts, eleven retained historical machine-source contexts and the rebuilt authored proposal. Counts are 53 ACCEPT, 114 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, 42 MODIFY, 22 UNDECIDED, three TOO_GENERAL and one NEW. There are no remaining REMOVE decisions. This is the result of individual source review; current-response absence, a different gene in a title, an unavailable experiment or a missing database pair is not used as a blanket removal rule.

Seventy generic-binding contexts were individually assessed, including two historical source rows. The resulting 34 non-core retentions, 26 specific replacements and ten uncertainties preserve each original partner and evidence context. Enzyme binding identifies the partner class rather than assigning catalysis to FN. Processed viral chains, inactive propeptides, co-complex assays and co-migrating overlay bands are distinguished. The separate nonbinding and location reviews retain source-specific soluble, matrix, fragment, donor and fusion boundaries.

Source reconciliation preserves the old fetched GOA, UniProt and provider snapshots. The archive records all 196 original annotations, every one of the 222 current original QuickGO objects, each OR branch, all conjunctions and the final index map. Only authored slots 194 and 195 are removed from the active array; all 193 historical machine-source rows remain represented. Current source extensions and partner fields enrich their matched historical rows, while genuinely distinct contexts are added individually.

The 17 alternative-product identities are retained with verified sequences. Corrections address inverted EDA/EDB statements, unsupported plasma-product assignments, incomplete sequence-feature lists and a truncated synonym. No product is given a new experimental function. Existing full-length and fragment results retain their construct limits.

The archive preserves all 149 inherited reference/text associations. Exact deterministic matching supports 147; two strings attributed to UniProt are absent from that record. Additional source alignment identifies five FBLN1C1 excerpts attached to FN-specific endpoints and a GlnA1 excerpt attached to DnaK. These are archived individually, together with three associations on the retired authored proposals/core. The 138 remaining active excerpts are byte-identical to their originals; no quotation is generated, extended, normalized in storage or transferred to an appended annotation.

Unresolved questions remain explicit. The exact soluble-fibrinogen-complex, selected protease/self-binding, monocyte readout and several bacterial-pair assays are not reconstructed from titles or general dataset validation. These annotations remain undecided with authentic source identity and the limits of the accessible evidence documented.


The historical heart-development IBA is also retained as a separate source-backed row with its exact older four-donor group. The current three-donor row remains unchanged. Both represent the same verified ancestral developmental placement with contextual scope; changing a donor list does not establish target loss or a new human experiment. Joint validation uses the [explicit immutable-snapshot manifest](FN1-goa-snapshots.yaml), preserving both source files and all earlier review evidence.
