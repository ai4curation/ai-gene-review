# FN1 source access and evidence guide

This guide records what was inspected for the FN1 annotation augmentation. The [source evidence](FN1-source-evidence.md) explains the biological decisions; the [context archive](FN1-source-contexts.json) preserves the earlier review, current original source objects, exact projection and all inherited quotation associations. The older fetched [UniProt record](FN1-uniprot.txt), GOA table and provider report are preserved unchanged.

## Source identity and annotation contexts

The target is human FN1, HGNC:3778, UniProt P02751. The current [QuickGO annotation service](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/annotation/search?geneProductId=UniProtKB%3AP02751&limit=200) supplied 222 original contexts. The archive records every original object and assigned-by, partner and extension field. Forty original contexts have extensions. Alternative OR branches are represented as separate annotations, while each conjunction remains together, following [GAF 2.2 column 16](https://geneontology.org/docs/go-annotation-file-gaf-format-2.2/). This produces 223 current typed contexts, including 48 extension groups and 55 atoms.

The final 235 rows also retain eleven historical machine-source contexts not represented by the current response, including the older heart-development donor group, and one individually re-evaluated authored matrix-organization assertion. Absence from a newer response is not a retirement criterion. Only the two unsupported or redundant authored proposals are retired. The archive supplies the proposed-slot-to-final-index map; the earlier indices used below are zero-based and remain unambiguous there.

Predicate identifiers are CURIE strings, with typed target terms. Official OLS/QuickGO metadata resolved BFO:0000050, BFO:0000066, RO:0002233, RO:0002565 and RO:0002411 and all target labels. The exact BFO part-of IRI was used rather than an anomalous returned `obo_id`. Original source relation names and OR/conjunction objects are preserved alongside their schema representation.

The exact hashes and paths of all 148 normal dependencies are in `normal_source_identity_manifest` in the context archive. Existing normal sources were preserved; six previously absent publications were acquired through the repository fetcher. A further needed donor paper, PMID:36812915, was hydrated from its existing Git object. A normal cache's `full_text_available` flag is an acquisition fact, not a claim that every section, figure or supplement was read.

## Reactome and evolutionary evidence

Forty-seven selected [Reactome content-service](https://reactome.org/ContentService/) event paths were followed to their physical-entity leaves. A cytosolic parent set does not overwrite a leaf compartment: the actual FN1–ALK monomer/dimer leaves are plasma-membrane entities. All twelve fusion-derived contexts remain non-core and do not assign ALK catalysis to native FN1. Soluble plasma/precursor/release contexts are distinguished from assembled matrix; only the thirteen source-supported matrix refinements are retained. Reactions consuming FN or acting on another participant do not give FN the enzyme's function.

The actual PTHR46708 family has 218 tree nodes and 218 aligned sequences. Human PTN002498994 contains the exact 2477-aa P02751 sequence. All six incoming IBA origins are PTN002709787, on the actual target path, with positive original IBD evidence. All 16 preserved normal PAINT rows match the original GAF, including five outside the current API node projection. Neither a short donor list nor the target appearing among sources is treated as invalidating the ancestry. Family records are retained unchanged; no additional GO assertion is manufactured from other family branches.

## Products and quotations

All 17 official product accessions and reconstructed FASTA identities are preserved. The displayed P02751-15 sequence contains EDA and EDB and is not labelled a plasma-specific product. Three incomplete VSP lists and a truncated synonym are corrected from the official table. Domain boundaries and splice boundaries are distinguished: VSP_060349 replaces residues 1722–1813 with TI. No binding or localization experiment is assigned to every product.

All 149 earlier quotation associations remain in the archive. Of these, 138 remain active byte-for-byte. Eleven are individually archived with reasons: two absent UniProt strings, five excerpts about a different intervention, one wrong-partner excerpt and three associations attached to retired authored assertions/core. The valid enolase paper title is retained for the unresolved OppD row solely as source identification. No new quotation is introduced.
