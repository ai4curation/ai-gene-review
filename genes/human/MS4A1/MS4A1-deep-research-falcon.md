# Human MS4A1 (CD20): functional annotation and research report

## Identity and principal conclusion

**Identity verified.** The specified human UniProt accession **P11836** corresponds to the protein CD20, encoded by **MS4A1**, not to another MS4A-family member. CD20 is a four-pass cell-surface protein of the MS4A family; its experimentally resolved topology agrees with the supplied CD20-like transmembrane, MS4A and CD20 domain annotations. The best-supported physiological annotation is **an organizer of B-cell plasma-membrane receptor nanodomains that helps maintain the resting state and controls access to B-cell receptor (BCR) signaling**. It has no established catalytic reaction, transported substrate or identified physiological ligand. Although it affects calcium responses, its proposed function as an *intrinsic calcium-conducting pore* remains unproven. [UniProt P11836](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P11836/entry); [Kläsener et al., *PNAS*, 9 February 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021342118); [Rougé et al., *Science*, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz9356). (pavlasova2020theregulationand pages 2-4, klasener2021cd20asa pages 1-2, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 1-2, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 4-5)

## Location and molecular architecture

CD20 functions principally **in the plasma membrane of developing and mature B lymphocytes**, appearing from the late pre-B stage and generally disappearing as cells terminally differentiate into antibody-secreting plasmablasts and plasma cells. Its four membrane-spanning helices place both termini in the cytosol and two loops outside the cell, making the larger extracellular loop accessible to therapeutic antibodies. A 2020 cryo-electron microscopy study resolved antibody-bound human CD20 at **3.3 Å** as a tightly packed *dimer* of four-helix subunits; purified CD20 bound two rituximab Fab fragments per dimer, whereas obinutuzumab Fab bound at a 1:2 Fab:CD20 ratio. Oligomeric states and associated proteins may vary in native membranes, so the isolated antibody-bound structure should not be taken to describe every cellular assembly. [Pavlasova and Mraz, *Haematologica*, June 2020](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2019.243543); [Rougé et al., *Science*, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz9356). (pavlasova2020theregulationand pages 2-4, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 2-3)

The inspected structural **Figure 2** shows the closely packed four-helix subunits and dimer interface; neither presents an obvious central transmembrane pore. This is structural evidence *against the particular pore configurations observed*, rather than proof that CD20 cannot regulate a separate channel or adopt another conformation. [Rougé et al., *Science*, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz9356). (rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 2-3, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 4-5, rouge2020structureofcd20 media 6925c4b6)

## Primary biological function: organization of BCR signaling

In resting human B cells, the **IgM- and IgD-class BCRs occupy distinct membrane nanodomains**. Proximity assays placed CD20 near the IgD-BCR-associated compartment, together with a CD19/CD81 module and other surface proteins; after experimental activation, its proximity shifted toward IgM-BCR. In human Ramos B cells, CRISPR deletion of *MS4A1* increased IgM-BCR proximity to the CD19 coreceptor, phosphorylation of **SYK and AKT**, and expression of activation markers **CD69 and CD86**. Loss of either CD19 or the BCR modified the CD20-knockout phenotype, while conditional CD20 re-expression restored surface markers and the resting phenotype. These perturbation-and-rescue results support a *gatekeeper* role: CD20 helps preserve an IgD-associated membrane organization that restrains inappropriate IgM-BCR/CD19 signaling. [Kläsener et al., *PNAS*, 9 February 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021342118). (klasener2021cd20asa pages 2-4, klasener2021cd20asa pages 1-2)

Rituximab exposure also rearranged receptors and transiently activated signaling in cultured human naïve B cells. In blood samples collected during rituximab administration to patients with rheumatoid arthritis, surface IgM initially rose, while CD19 rapidly decreased despite B cells still being detectable. With longer CD20 loss, Ramos cells showed a PAX5-to-BLIMP-1-associated change and plasma-cell-like characteristics. The latter fate experiment was largely in a **lymphoma cell line**, however, and should not be interpreted as showing that normal human B cells routinely become plasma cells after CD20 blockade. [Kläsener et al., *PNAS*, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021342118). (klasener2021cd20asa pages 4-5, klasener2021cd20asa pages 2-4, klasener2021cd20asa pages 1-2)

CD20 is thus connected to **BCR–CD19–SYK–PI3K/AKT signaling** and B-cell activation thresholds, rather than being established as a stand-alone receptor with a known extracellular ligand. Earlier human lymphoma-cell experiments also linked CD20 depletion to changes in BCR-associated **LYN, SYK and ERK** phosphorylation and BCR-triggered calcium signals; these findings establish pathway participation but do not by themselves identify a direct biochemical reaction performed by CD20. [Pavlasova and Mraz, *Haematologica*, June 2020](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2019.243543); [Kläsener et al., *PNAS*, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021342118). (pavlasova2020theregulationand pages 4-5, klasener2021cd20asa pages 1-2)

## Calcium signaling: an important unresolved distinction

Older overexpression, knockdown and antibody-crosslinking experiments suggested that CD20 participates in calcium conductance or calcium mobilization. A December 2024 MS4A-family review describes CD20 as an ion channel, but this is an interpretation of earlier functional assays, **not direct identification of an ion-permeation pathway in human CD20**. Conversely, the 2020 structure shows a sealed monomeric four-helix bundle and a closely packed dimer interface, and the authors conclude that neither provides a plausible ion-permeation route. [Luo et al., *Frontiers in Immunology*, 9 December 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2024.1481494); [Rougé et al., *Science*, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz9356). (luo2024ms4asuperfamilymolecules pages 2-3, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 1-2, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 3-4, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 2-3, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 4-5)

The human genetic counterexample is especially informative: B-cell lines made from a patient **without surface CD20** still generated intracellular calcium flux after IgM- or IgG-BCR crosslinking. Other cell-line and mouse studies report reduced calcium responses after CD20 depletion. The defensible annotation is therefore **modulator of BCR-associated calcium signaling, mechanism unresolved**—not a confirmed Ca²⁺ transporter with a defined substrate-transport reaction. [Kuijpers et al., *Journal of Clinical Investigation*, January 2010](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI40231); [Pavlasova and Mraz, *Haematologica*, June 2020](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2019.243543). (kuijpers2010cd20deficiencyin pages 2-4, kuijpers2010cd20deficiencyin pages 4-5, pavlasova2020theregulationand pages 4-5)

## Human loss-of-function evidence and biological processes

A patient with biallelic *MS4A1* splice-disrupting variants lacked detectable surface CD20 yet retained **normal numbers of circulating B cells**. The patient had low IgG and very few class-switched memory B cells (**<2%**, compared with a mean of **7.1%** in the cited age-matched group), and showed impaired responses to pneumococcal polysaccharides but preserved anti-tetanus-toxoid IgG after boosters. Patient B cells proliferated to tested stimuli but produced little IgG in vitro. This identifies a nonredundant role in **effective humoral immunity, particularly T-cell-independent polysaccharide antibody responses**, without establishing that CD20 is essential for initial B-cell production. CD20-deficient mouse immunization experiments corroborated impaired T-independent responses, although model-dependent differences in T-dependent responses have also been reported. The human inference is limited by the exceptional rarity of genetically confirmed deficiency. [Kuijpers et al., *Journal of Clinical Investigation*, January 2010](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI40231); [Pavlasova and Mraz, *Haematologica*, June 2020](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2019.243543). (kuijpers2010cd20deficiencyin pages 1-2, kuijpers2010cd20deficiencyin pages 2-4, kuijpers2010cd20deficiencyin pages 4-5, pavlasova2020theregulationand pages 2-4)

Additional human evidence relates IgD to CD20-containing membrane organization: a **2023 study of eight people from three families** with heterozygous *IGHD* variants found that IgD-negative naïve B cells expressed less CD20, CD19 and CD21 and responded less well to several tested stimuli. Because the causal perturbation was **IGHD, not MS4A1**, this supports coupling between IgD-associated B-cell state and CD20 abundance; it does not independently prove that CD20 caused those differences. [Dirks et al., *Frontiers in Immunology*, 26 January 2023](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2023.1096019). (dirks2023igdshapesthe pages 1-2)

## Expression beyond conventional B cells

CD20 is **not absolutely B-cell-exclusive**. A November 2024 study reported CD20 on approximately **3–5% of circulating human T cells** and distinguished two possible origins: transfer of CD20-containing membrane from B cells (*trogocytosis*) and endogenous expression. T cells acquired CD20 plus other B-cell markers during experimental coculture, but naturally occurring human CD20-positive T cells in blood and cerebrospinal fluid lacked those accompanying markers; sorted CD20-positive T cells contained *MS4A1* RNA. These results support endogenous production in **at least some** human T cells, while leaving CD20’s specific signaling function there unresolved. [von Essen et al., *Frontiers in Immunology*, 22 November 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2024.1487530). (essen2024theoriginof pages 1-2, essen2024theoriginof pages 3-6)

A 2024 report also describes CD20-dependent predator-odor sensing in **mouse** olfactory neurons. That is not evidence that **human** MS4A1/P11836 serves as an olfactory receptor, and it should not displace the experimentally supported human B-cell annotation. [Jiang et al., *Nature Communications*, April 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-47698-3); [Luo et al., *Frontiers in Immunology*, 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2024.1481494). (luo2024ms4asuperfamilymolecules pages 2-3)

## Clinical use and recent translational developments

**CD20 is an accessible cell-surface target, not necessarily the direct signaling mechanism that makes its therapies effective.** In B-cell malignancies, rituximab and other anti-CD20 antibodies deplete CD20-bearing cells through combinations of complement-dependent cytotoxicity, antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity and phagocytosis, and, for some antibodies or settings, direct cell death. Rituximab is used in B-cell lymphoma regimens; obinutuzumab and ofatumumab differ in epitope and effector profile. More recently, **CD20×CD3 bispecific antibodies** recruit T cells against malignant B cells; CD20-directed CAR T cells remain an investigational approach in the 2024 review. CD20 on mature B cells but usually not long-lived plasma cells also helps explain why depletion need not directly eliminate existing antibody-producing plasma cells. [Dabkowska et al., *Frontiers in Immunology*, 4 April 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2024.1363102); [Rougé et al., *Science*, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz9356). (dabkowska2024advancementsincancer pages 1-2, dabkowska2024advancementsincancer pages 5-6, dabkowska2024advancementsincancer pages 6-8, dabkowska2024advancementsincancer pages 8-9, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 1-2)

Anti-CD20-mediated B-cell depletion is also used in **multiple sclerosis**. In the 2023 ALITHIOS ofatumumab extension report, the efficacy-analysis population comprised **1,882** people; the continuously treated group had an annualized relapse rate of approximately **0.05** over the reported follow-up, and **78.8%** met the reported no-evidence-of-disease-activity criteria during the extension period. These are **extension-study estimates**, not evidence that one anti-CD20 agent outperforms all others. Indeed, a December 2024 network meta-analysis of **15 randomized trials** found no statistically significant difference in annualized relapse rates between ublituximab and the other monoclonal antibodies it compared; indirect comparisons have their own limitations. [Hauser et al., *Multiple Sclerosis Journal*, September 2023](https://doi.org/10.1177/13524585231195346); [Moloney et al., *Frontiers in Neurology*, 6 December 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2024.1479476). (hauser2023efficacyandsafety pages 1-3, hauser2023efficacyandsafety pages 5-7, moloney2024comparativeefficacyand pages 1-2)

**Antigen abundance and resistance are active research areas.** CD20 loss or altered epitope expression can compromise antibody-based targeting, while other resistance mechanisms also exist; the accessible material for a February 2024 mosunetuzumab study included its experimental supplement rather than the full main results, so a precise incidence of CD20-negative relapse is **not asserted here**. In a separate study published online in **2024**, salinomycin or monensin increased surface CD20 **2.2–4-fold in Raji cells**, with upregulation also tested in primary CLL samples (**n=24** for salinomycin; **n=13** for monensin) and DLBCL samples (**n=10** for salinomycin). The agents increased anti-CD20 activity in experimental systems, but affected other surface proteins too; this is **preclinical target-modulation research, not an established patient treatment**. [Schuster et al., *Blood*, February 2024](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2023022348); [Torun et al., *Haematologica*, published online December 2024](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2024.285826); [Dabkowska et al., *Frontiers in Immunology*, 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2024.1363102). (schuster2024lossofcd20 pages 1-7, schuster2024lossofcd20 pages 7-19, torun2024potassiumsodiumcationcarriers pages 7-10, dabkowska2024advancementsincancer pages 5-6)

**Functional-annotation judgment:** annotate human MS4A1/CD20 as a **B-cell plasma-membrane, four-pass MS4A protein organizing IgD-associated receptor nanodomains and modulating BCR/CD19 activation, with a demonstrated contribution to optimal antibody responses**. Record calcium signaling as associated but do **not** assign a definitive calcium-channel pore, transported substrate, enzyme activity, or human olfactory-receptor function on the present evidence. [Kläsener et al., *PNAS*, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021342118); [Kuijpers et al., *Journal of Clinical Investigation*, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI40231); [Rougé et al., *Science*, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz9356). (klasener2021cd20asa pages 1-2, kuijpers2010cd20deficiencyin pages 2-4, rouge2020structureofcd20 pages 4-5)
