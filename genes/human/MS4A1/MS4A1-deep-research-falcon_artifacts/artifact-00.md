| **Four-pass membrane dimer:** Cryo-EM resolved human CD20 as two tightly associated four-transmembrane bundles forming a compact double-barrel dimer; the extracellular loops present paired antibody epitopes (pqac-00000027, pqac-00000053). | **High-confidence structural evidence** for purified, antibody-bound CD20. The oligomeric arrangement in an unliganded native B-cell membrane could be more dynamic. | Rougé et al., [10.1126/science.aaz9356](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz9356); 2020 |
| **Organizer of the resting B-cell surface:** In human Ramos cells and primary naïve B cells, CD20 localized near the IgD-BCR/CD19 compartment. CRISPR loss reorganized IgM-BCR and CD19, increased SYK–AKT signaling and transient activation; conditional re-expression restored the resting phenotype (pqac-00000014). | **Strong mechanistic evidence**, including loss and rescue. Ramos lymphoma cells are not fully equivalent to normal naïve B cells, but primary-cell and rituximab experiments support the membrane-nanodomain “gatekeeper” model. | Kläsener et al., [10.1073/pnas.2021342118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021342118); 2021 |
| **Required for optimal humoral immunity:** A patient with biallelic splice-disrupting *MS4A1* variants had normal circulating B-cell numbers but markedly reduced class-switched memory B cells, low IgG and impaired pneumococcal-polysaccharide responses; anti-tetanus responses were preserved. Patient-derived cultured B-cell lines retained IgM/IgG-BCR-induced Ca²⁺ flux (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000045). | **Direct human genetic evidence**, but based on one exceptionally rare patient. It supports a nonredundant role in T-cell-independent antibody responses and shows that CD20 is not universally required for BCR-triggered Ca²⁺ mobilization. | Kuijpers et al., [10.1172/JCI40231](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI40231); 2010 |
| **Intrinsic Ca²⁺ channel activity is not established:** Historical expression, silencing and crosslinking studies associated CD20 with Ca²⁺ conductance or BCR-linked Ca²⁺ flux (pqac-00000020). However, the 3.3-Å structure showed a tightly sealed monomeric bundle and dimer interface with no plausible ion-permeation pathway (pqac-00000027, pqac-00000039). | **Best current interpretation:** CD20 regulates Ca²⁺ signaling indirectly through BCR organization or a larger channel/signaling complex rather than acting as a demonstrated pore. Definitive native-membrane electrophysiology and identification of partner channels remain needed. | Rougé et al., [10.1126/science.aaz9356](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaz9356); 2020; Pavlasova & Mraz, [10.3324/haematol.2019.243543](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2019.243543); 2020 |
| **Expression extends to a small human T-cell subset:** Human CD20-negative T cells acquired CD20 and other B-cell proteins through trogocytosis, but circulating and CSF CD20⁺ T cells lacked those co-transferred markers. Naïve CD20⁺ T cells contained *MS4A1* mRNA, and FOXO1 inhibition increased CD20, supporting endogenous expression in at least some cells (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000049). | **Moderate evidence** from small donor cohorts. Human CD20⁺ T cells may arise through both endogenous synthesis and transfer; their CD20-specific molecular function remains unresolved. Murine olfactory or T-cell findings should not be generalized to human MS4A1 without direct evidence. | von Essen et al., [10.3389/fimmu.2024.1487530](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2024.1487530); 2024 |
| **Experimental restoration of target abundance:** Salinomycin and monensin increased surface CD20 by **2.2–4-fold** in human Raji cells at subtoxic concentrations and increased CD20 in primary CLL (salinomycin, *n*=24; monensin, *n*=13) and DLBCL samples (*n*=10) ex vivo. They enhanced rituximab, ofatumumab and CD20-CAR-T activity in experimental systems; benefit in mice was lost after *MS4A1* disruption (pqac-00000031). | **Promising preclinical evidence only:** effects involve MYC-dependent *MS4A1* regulation and broader surface-protein changes, not selective CD20 modulation. Clinical efficacy and safety have not been demonstrated. | Torun et al., [10.3324/haematol.2024.285826](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2024.285826); published online 2024 |
