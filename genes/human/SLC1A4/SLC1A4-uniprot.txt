CC   -!- FUNCTION: Sodium-coupled antiporter of neutral amino acids. In a tri-
CC       substrate transport cycle, exchanges neutral amino acids between the
CC       extracellular and intracellular compartments, coupled to the inward
CC       cotransport of at least one sodium ion (PubMed:24808181,
CC       PubMed:27272177, PubMed:34630942, PubMed:8910405). Exchanges neutral
CC       amino acids such as L- and D-serine, L-threonine, L-asparagine and L-
CC       alanine in a bidirectional way (PubMed:14502423, PubMed:26041762,
CC       PubMed:27272177, PubMed:34630942, PubMed:8101838, PubMed:8340364,
CC       PubMed:8910405). Involved in homeostasis of D-serine, a coagonist of
CC       synaptic NMDA receptors. In astrocytes at excitatory synapses, mediates
CC       electrogenic D-serine influx coupled to L-serine efflux and L-serine
CC       shuttling to neurons for de novo D-serine synthesis (By similarity).
CC       Displays sodium- and amino acid-dependent but uncoupled channel-like
CC       anion conductance with a preference SCN(-) > NO3(-) > I(-) > Cl(-)
