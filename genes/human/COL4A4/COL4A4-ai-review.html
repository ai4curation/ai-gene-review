Gene Description

COL4A4 encodes the alpha4 chain of type IV collagen, a secreted structural component of basement membranes. It assembles with alpha3 and alpha5 chains into collagen IV protomers whose terminal interactions organize the glomerular basement-membrane network. The collagenous region and C-terminal NC1 domain contribute to this architecture; COL4A4 variants are associated with thin-basement-membrane nephropathy and Alport-spectrum kidney disease. Isolated recombinant human NC1, termed tetrastatin, binds integrin alphaVbeta3 and inhibits melanoma-cell growth and invasion in experimental systems. A synthetic C-terminal peptide, QS-13, binds alpha5beta1 and reduces endothelial migration and experimental vascularization, a fragment activity distinct from the intact structural chain.

COL4A4 evidence notes

COL4A4 provides the alpha4 chain of the alpha3/alpha4/alpha5 basement-membrane collagen network. Its isolated human NC1 domain also has experimentally demonstrated integrin-binding activity. Basement-membrane structure is its established primary role. The domain activity is retained as a context-specific experimental finding; it does not establish that intact COL4A4 constitutively presents the same ligand surface.

Structural and clinical evidence

Human P53420 has 1,690 residues, signal sequence 1–38, mature chain 39–1690 and currently annotated NC1 domain 1465–1690. No alternative-product comment was present in the authenticated official record. The mature chain participates in the alpha3/alpha4/alpha5 collagen IV network. The original human sequence paper uses its own NC1 domain-length convention; its complete abstract is available, not full Methods. PMID:7523402(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7523402/)

The complete positive NC1-hexamer abstract reports alpha3/alpha4/alpha5 composition, including alpha4–alpha4 crosslinked dimers and association supporting a shared protomer. This is not an alpha4 homotrimer. Publisher attempts failed; a successful Elsevier HTTP response contained metadata only. Exact full Methods/species/construct scope remain uninspected, and the selected shared abstract is preserved. PMID:12193605(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12193605/)

Actual human NC1 Methods/Results show that isolated alpha4 was monomeric in solution and crystallized in a noncanonical dodecamer; the authors question its physiological relevance. An equimolar alpha3/alpha4/alpha5 mixture yielded alpha5 crystals, while SPR measurements concerned alpha3/alpha5. The structural contacts inform the matrix contribution but do not demonstrate native alpha345 reconstitution or an autonomous alpha4 adaptor mechanism. Row 36 is refined to the existing collagen structural term, without introducing a scaffold function. The official GO:0030020 comment permits extracellular-matrix collagen proteins; the type IV network is not described as fibrillar collagen or as a directly measured isolated-chain tensile assay. PMID:30443360(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30443360/)

The publisher corrigendum corrects an author name and affiliation only. Its operative text does not alter the experimental conclusions.
