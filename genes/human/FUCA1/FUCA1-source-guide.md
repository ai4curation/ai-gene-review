# FUCA1 source guide

Human FUCA1 (P04066; HGNC:4006) is a 466-residue tissue alpha-L-fucosidase. Its 31-residue signal peptide and mature chain are processing features. The common Arg281/Gln281 alleles and experimental residues 6–466 construct are not alternate splice products.

## Catalytic and substrate evidence

[PMID:19666478](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19666478/) explicitly characterizes human FUCA1 alongside FUCA2. Its infection-associated secretion and adhesion conclusions concern FUCA2. [PMID:4074382](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4074382/) supplies human fibroblast activity/processing evidence through the accessible abstract; it does not establish a cytosolic enzyme.

[PMID:9741689](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9741689/) separates bovine kidney purified-enzyme assays from human fibroblast glycolipid turnover. The existing ISS donor Q2KIM0 is bovine FUCA1 and has an IDA annotation to this paper. NAGA acts before efficient defucosylation of the tested linear intermediate. FUCA1 is not described as efficiently hydrolysing every branched blood-group substrate.

[PMID:27491075](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27491075/) is a mouse knockout study. Its storage data support conserved core-defucosylation biology; AGA steric inhibition is cited background. The inherited human IMP/NEW row is archived rather than repeated as a human experiment. A separate IC annotation now infers human process participation from established catalytic activity and the explicit human lysosomal reaction; the mouse study remains corroboration rather than a human IMP experiment. Comparator queries found human MAN2B1 and GLB1 annotations to this process; FUCA1 catalyses a step rather than merely being a substrate. No local GO-CAM index hit was found for P04066/FUCA1.
