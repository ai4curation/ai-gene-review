  The AK4P3 ORF is near-identical to AK4 with the catalytic P-loop intact, so the
  sequence gives NO evidence of pseudogenisation at the protein level. Whether the
  locus is expressed and translated is a separate question this analysis cannot answer.
  AK4P3 keeps AK4's Gln159, the residue that makes AK4 inactive as an adenylate
  kinase in vitro. The sequence therefore predicts AK4's enzymology, which is NOT an
  active AMP kinase.
