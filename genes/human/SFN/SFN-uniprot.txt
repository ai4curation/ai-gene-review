CC   -!- FUNCTION: Adapter protein implicated in the regulation of a large
CC       spectrum of both general and specialized signaling pathways
CC       (PubMed:15731107, PubMed:22634725, PubMed:28202711, PubMed:37797010).
CC       Binds to a large number of partners, usually by recognition of a
CC       phosphoserine or phosphothreonine motif (PubMed:15731107,
CC       PubMed:22634725, PubMed:28202711, PubMed:37797010). Binding generally
CC       results in the modulation of the activity of the binding partner
CC       (PubMed:15731107, PubMed:22634725, PubMed:28202711, PubMed:37797010).
CC       Promotes cytosolic retention of GBP1 GTPase by binding to
CC       phosphorylated GBP1, thereby inhibiting the innate immune response
CC       (PubMed:37797010). Also acts as a TP53/p53-regulated inhibitor of G2/M
CC       progression (PubMed:9659898). When bound to KRT17, regulates protein
CC       synthesis and epithelial cell growth by stimulating Akt/mTOR pathway
CC       (By similarity). Acts to maintain desmosome cell junction adhesion in
CC       epithelial cells via interacting with and sequestering PKP3 to the
CC       cytoplasm, thereby restricting its translocation to existing desmosome
CC       structures and therefore maintaining desmosome protein homeostasis
CC       (PubMed:24124604). Also acts to facilitate PKP3 exchange at desmosome
CC       plaques, thereby maintaining keratinocyte intercellular adhesion
CC       (PubMed:29678907). May also regulate MDM2 autoubiquitination and
CC       degradation and thereby activate p53/TP53 (PubMed:18382127). Upstream
CC       regulator of YAP1 localization in keratinocytes: binds and sequester
CC       YAP1 in the cytoplasm, limiting YAP1-dependent transcription and
CC       driving Hippo signaling and cell differentiation (By similarity).
