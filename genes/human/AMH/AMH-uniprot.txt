CC   -!- FUNCTION: The anti-Muellerian hormone (AMH) plays an important role in
CC       several reproductive functions (PubMed:14742691, PubMed:34155118,
CC       PubMed:3754790, PubMed:8469238). Anti-Muellerian hormone binds and
CC       activates AMHR2, its specific type-II receptor, that heterodimerizes
CC       with type-I receptors (ACVR1 and BMPR1A) to regulate target gene
CC       expression through downstream SMAD protein signal transduction
CC       (PubMed:20861221, PubMed:34155118). Produced and secreted by Sertoli
CC       cells of the male fetus, anti-Muellerian hormone induces Muellerian
CC       duct regression during male fetal sexual differentiation
CC       (PubMed:34155118, PubMed:3754790, PubMed:8469238). In female, it is
CC       produced by granulosa cells of the preantral and small antral follicles
CC       and acts as a negative regulator of the primordial to primary follicle
CC       transition and decreases FSH sensitivity of growing follicles
CC       (PubMed:14742691). Also plays a role in Leydig cell differentiation and
CC       function (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P27106,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14742691, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20861221,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34155118, ECO:0000269|PubMed:3754790,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8469238}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: [Anti-Muellerian hormone]: Homodimer; disulfide-linked
CC       (PubMed:2974034, PubMed:39805014, PubMed:8469238). Anti-Muellerian
CC       hormone (AMH) is secreted as part of a non-covalent heterotetrameric
CC       complex consisting of AMH prodomain and anti-Muellerian hormone
CC       homodimers (PubMed:2974034, PubMed:39805014, PubMed:8469238). After
CC       proteolytic processing, the dimeric AMH prodomain remains non-
CC       covalently associated with the active homodimeric anti-Muellerian
CC       hormone, amplifying its biological activity (PubMed:2974034,
CC       PubMed:39805014, PubMed:8469238). The AMH prodomain homodimer is
CC       displaced upon binding of anti-Muellerian hormone to its receptor
CC       (PubMed:20861221, PubMed:39805014). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20861221,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2974034, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39805014,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8469238}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2974034,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:3754790}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: In ovaries, AMH is detected in granulosa cells of
CC       early growing follicles. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14742691}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Detected in primary follicles and continued to be
CC       expressed in follicles in the antral stage (PubMed:14742691). The
CC       highest level of AMH expression is present in granulosa cells of
CC       secondary, preantral and small antral follicles <4 mm in diameter,
CC       whereas expression is lost from follicles at sizes >8 mm
CC       (PubMed:14742691). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14742691}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Plasma AMH levels in polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
CC       patients are two- to threefold higher than in women with normal
CC       ovaries. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11779604}.
CC   -!- PTM: The preproprotein is proteolytically processed to generate the AMH
CC       prodomain and the anti-Muellerian hormone (PubMed:20861221,
CC       PubMed:2974034, PubMed:8469238). Both homodimerize covalently, and
CC       homodimers associate to form a non-covalent heterotetrameric complex
CC       (PubMed:20861221, PubMed:2974034, PubMed:8469238).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20861221, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2974034,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8469238}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Persistent Muellerian duct syndrome 1 (PMDS1) [MIM:261550]: A
CC       form of male pseudohermaphroditism characterized by a failure of
CC       Muellerian duct regression in otherwise normal males.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8162013, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8872466}. Note=The
CC       disease is caused by variants affecting the gene represented in this
CC       entry.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the TGF-beta family. {ECO:0000305}.
