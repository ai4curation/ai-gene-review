# APOBEC4 notes

- APOBEC4 is a distinct AID/APOBEC subfamily with conserved zinc-coordinating motifs, testis-biased [PMID:16082223].
- Human APOBEC4:
  - Non-mutagenic in bacteria and yeast [PMID:21568845].
  - No in vitro deaminase activity; weak ssDNA binding; mostly cytoplasmic when overexpressed; enhances many promoters, including the HIV-1 LTR [PMID:27249646].
- An siRNA nucleolar-function screen hit, confirmed but not followed up [PMID:38976757].
- Chicken APOBEC4 is antiviral against NDV [PMID:31991164]. Not used: avian overexpression.
- PubMed recall (16 hits for "APOBEC4", 2026-10-04) found no knockout or testis study.
