APOBEC4 (Q8WW27): is the zinc deaminase motif catalytically competent?

Hypothesis slug: kgap-apobec4-zinc-deaminase-motif-competence Gene: human APOBEC4, UniProt Q8WW27, gene directory genes/human/APOBEC4 Focus type: free_text, source genes/human/APOBEC4/APOBEC4-ai-review.yaml

Summary

Human APOBEC4 is not predicted to be deamination-competent, but the mechanism the seed hypothesis proposes for that conclusion is refuted. The hypothesis asks whether the zinc-binding pocket is geometrically assembled and accessible, and anticipates that it is not. It is. Superposing the zinc-site anchor atoms of APOBEC3A (PDB 5SWW) onto the AlphaFold model AF-Q8WW27-F1 v6 gives 0.40 Å RMSD over 10 atoms, and the whole deaminase core superposes at 1.36 Å over 128 Cα pairs. A zinc transplanted through that transform sits 2.24 Å from His93 ND1, 2.50 Å from Cys127 SG and 1.98 Å from Cys134 SG, with the Glu95 carboxylate at 4.04 Å. Every one of those values falls inside the range measured in eight experimental active-site structures. The deaminase core is modelled at mean pLDDT 94.5, so this is not a low-confidence region, and the four catalytic positions are 97.9-98.7% invariant across 233 vertebrate orthologs against a 62.8% background.

Two structural metrics that initially appeared to show catalytic incompetence failed their controls during this investigation and are withdrawn. Solvent-probe accessibility at the zinc does not separate active from inactive enzymes: AID (5W0R), APOBEC1 (6X91) and APOBEC3A 5KEG all score 0.000. Steric occlusion of the substrate channel does not either: transplanting the same ssDNA by the same superposition onto apo structures of active enzymes gives 66 close contacts for APOBEC1, 27 for APOBEC3A and 13 for AID, against 35 for the APOBEC4 model. The metric compares apo with holo. After both retractions, no conformation-independent structural or evolutionary evidence positively supports catalytic incompetence, and the case rests on two negative experimental assays (PMID: 27249646, PMID: 21568845), both of which used ssDNA or genomic DNA while APOBEC4's physiological substrate is unknown.

What survives as a structural statement is narrower and concerns substrate affinity. The APOBEC3A Trp98 that stacks the −1 base becomes Asn124, the loop7 specificity pair Asp131/Tyr132 becomes Thr160/His159, loop1 His29 becomes Thr63, and the cleft within 14 Å of the zinc carries 4 Lys+Arg residues, the fewest of any structure examined (references span 6-9). Two independent constraint measures agree on the same split: every metal ligand sits at the 93rd-99th AlphaMissense percentile while the substrate-reading positions sit at the 63rd (His65) and 47th (Asn124). Separately, and outside the hypothesis, APOBEC4 carries a second candidate metal site at Cys28/Cys31/His34/Cys87 that no other characterised AID/APOBEC deaminase has. It makes GO:0008270 zinc ion binding the best-supported molecular function for this gene and GO:0004126 cytidine deaminase activity an over-annotation.

Executive judgment

Partially supported, with the stated mechanism refuted.

The hypothesis reaches a defensible bottom line and gives the wrong reason for it.

Right: human APOBEC4 is not demonstrated to be deamination-competent, and two direct experimental tests failed to detect activity.

Wrong: the prediction that the active-site pocket is not geometrically assembled. It is assembled, in the canonical position, at high model confidence, and under strong purifying selection.

Two of the three structural metrics tried here, zinc burial and substrate-channel occlusion, do not separate active from inactive enzymes and were withdrawn after controls. A curator should treat any occlusion-based or burial-based argument in the existing review with the same suspicion.

New, outside the hypothesis: a second candidate metal site (Cys28, Cys31, His34, Cys87) that survives three independent controls and changes the GO:0008270 recommendation from one site to two.

Key findings

The zinc site is assembled, canonical and conserved

The 367-residue sequence contains His93-Pro94-Glu95 and Pro126-Cys127-x(6)-Cys134. Superposing the zinc-site anchor atoms (His ND1/CG/CB/CA plus two Cys SG/CB/CA) of APOBEC3A 5SWW onto APOBEC4 gives RMSD 0.40 Å over 10 atoms; whole-core superposition gives 128 Cα pairs at 1.36 Å. The transplanted zinc lands at 2.24 Å (His93 ND1), 2.50 Å (Cys127 SG) and 1.98 Å (Cys134 SG), against an experimental Zn-His ND1 range of 2.03-2.34 Å. The Glu95 carboxylate sits 4.04 Å from the metal, matching APOBEC3G at 3.87 and 4.08 Å and APOBEC2 at 3.82 Å. Ligand-ligand distances in APOBEC4 (3.33, 3.80 and 3.80 Å) fall inside the 3.12-4.33 Å range of eight active-site structures.

The second cysteine ligand is Cys134, not the adjacent Cys133: the Cys127-Cys133 SG-SG distance is 7.67 Å, which is geometrically impossible for a shared metal, while Cys127-Cys134 gives 3.80 Å. This matches the Cx6C spacing and the chicken His97/Glu99/ Pro130/Cys131/Cys138 assignment reported in PMID: 31991164, and it matches UniProt's own Zn(2+) binding annotations at 93, 127 and 134 with the proton donor at 95.

Across 226 non-redundant tetrapod orthologs (87 Mammalia, 118 Aves, 11 Lepidosauria, 3 Testudines, 1 Crocodylia, 5 Amphibia; median 45.0% identity to human), the catalytic positions are near-invariant: His93 99.1%, Glu95 98.2%, Pro126 99.1%, Cys127 98.7%, Cys134 98.7%. Background positions in the same alignment run at 4.0-5.3%. An expanded 233-sequence alignment restricted to residues 1-172 gives the same picture: His93 98.7%, Glu95 97.9%, Cys127 97.9%, Cys134 97.9% against a 62.8% median background.

Two structural metrics failed their controls

Zinc solvent accessibility. Probe-accessible fraction at the metal: APOBEC3A 4XXO 0.092, APOBEC3A 5SWW 0.042, APOBEC3G-CTD 3IR2 0.015, APOBEC4 0.005, and 0.000 for AID 5W0R, APOBEC1 6X91, APOBEC3A 5KEG and APOBEC2 2NYT. Active enzymes span the whole range, so metal burial cannot be used as an argument for incompetence.

Substrate-channel occlusion. The same 12-nucleotide ssDNA from 5SWW (108 heavy atoms), placed by the same zinc-anchor Kabsch superposition onto each structure, gives these counts of DNA atoms within 2.8 Å of protein: APOBEC1 apo 6X91 66, APOBEC2 2NYT 40, APOBEC4 model 35, APOBEC3A apo 4XXO 27, AID apo 5W0R 13, APOBEC3G-CTD apo 3IR2 3, and APOBEC3A holo 5SWW self-control 3. APOBEC4 sits inside the active-enzyme apo range and below APOBEC1. The worst single contact in APOBEC4 is 0.73 Å against 0.40 Å for APOBEC1 and 0.79 Å for apo APOBEC3A. Loop1 of APOBEC4 (residues 58-68), which produced most of the modelled contacts, has the lowest pLDDT of any functional element (mean 81.7, minimum 63.4) against 93-95 for the catalytic helix, the PCxxC strand and loop7. The claim that the APOBEC4 substrate channel is occluded is withdrawn.

The surviving structural difference is substrate engagement

Structure-based mapping of APOBEC3A functional residues onto APOBEC4 preserves the metal machinery (H70→H93, E72→E95, C101→C127, C106→C134) and changes the substrate readers: Trp98→Asn124 (Cα deviation 0.55 Å; across orthologs 31% Asn, 46% Tyr), loop1 His29→Thr63 (0.83 Å; 39% Thr, 49% Asn), loop7 Tyr132→His159 (2.22 Å) and Asp131→Thr160 (2.34 Å).

Cleft composition within 14 Å of the zinc gives one clean discriminator and one that fails. Net charge fails: active APOBEC3G-CTD apo is net −2, the same as inactive APOBEC2, and APOBEC4 is −1. The basic-residue count works: APOBEC4 has 4 Lys+Arg, the lowest of any structure examined, while APOBEC3A, APOBEC3G-CTD, AID, APOBEC1 and APOBEC2 span 6-9. That predicts weak ssDNA engagement, which matches the measured result in PMID: 27249646.

Human population data give the same split. AlphaMissense mean pathogenicity against a protein-wide mean of 0.435: Cys127 0.945 (99th percentile), Cys87 0.943 (99th), Cys31 0.937 (98th), Cys134 0.931 (98th), Cys28 0.922 (96th), His34 0.916 (96th), Glu95 0.916 (96th), His93 0.885 (93rd). Substrate-reading positions are unconstrained: His65 0.511 (63rd), Asn124 0.317 (47th), with Asn124 only 32.2% conserved across 233 orthologs. gnomAD v4 adds nothing usable at the gene level: all loss-of-function constraint fields for ENSG00000173627 are null, oe_mis is 0.850 (observed 375, expected 441.2), and the 53 predicted loss-of-function variants (366 alleles in roughly 1.46 million, commonest p.Asp222ThrfsTer11 at allele count 183) include 0 homozygotes, which carries no weight because fewer than one is expected under Hardy-Weinberg at that frequency.

A second candidate metal site, outside the hypothesis

Cys28, Cys31, His34 and Cys87 place four side-chain donors on a common sphere of radius 2.41 Å, 15.6 Å from the catalytic zinc. That radius matches Zn-S at 2.3 Å and Zn-N at 2.0 Å. The donors are mutually 3.35-4.26 Å apart, too far for a disulfide at 2.05 Å. Local pLDDT is 91-96. The arrangement passes three controls.

Independent predictions. AlphaFold models of 9 taxonomically spread orthologs, predicted separately from divergent sequences, reproduce it.

A methodological correction to my own metric: a four-point equidistant fit is exact by construction, so the "zero spread" first reported carries no information. The circumsphere radius replaces it, and the conclusion survives.

Mechanistic model

The activity under test is zinc-dependent hydrolytic deamination of a cytosine base in a polynucleotide: a Zn-bound hydroxide attacks C4 of the target cytosine while a glutamate shuttles protons. The test separates into two parts that this investigation answered differently.

Part one, the metal centre and proton shuttle. Direct result: assembled and canonical. The His plus two Cys triad and Glu95 are in the geometry seen in active enzymes and under strong purifying selection in both orthologs and human missense data.

Part two, substrate engagement. Direct result: divergent in residue identity at every element checked, with the fewest basic cleft residues of any APOBEC examined and no evolutionary or population constraint at those positions. Inferred, not shown: that this predicts weak, non-specific polynucleotide binding and no 5'-TC preference. The steric version of this claim, that the channel is physically blocked, failed its control.

A third part the hypothesis did not anticipate. The Cys28/Cys31/His34/Cys87 site is under the same constraint as the catalytic site and could support a structural or regulatory metal-dependent function with no deaminase turnover. APOBEC2 (PDB 2NYT) is the precedent: a paralog with an intact His98/Cys128/Cys131 zinc site and no deaminase activity.

Everything else in the APOBEC4 literature sits downstream of, or independent from, this activity. Promoter and LTR enhancement (PMID: 27249646), Newcastle disease virus restriction in chicken (PMID: 31991164), nucleolar and ribosome biogenesis effects (PMID: 38976757), testis-enriched expression (PMID: 16082223) and SARS-CoV-2 expression correlations (PMID: 34234167, PMID: 34276680) are cellular phenotypes or expression observations. None demonstrates deaminase turnover, and in the APOBEC3A case the analogous phenotype survived catalytic inactivation.

GO curation implications

All of the following are leads requiring curator verification.

GO:0004126 cytidine deaminase activity, and any C-to-U deaminase MF term: do not assert. The only positive support is the 2005 motif prediction (PMID: 16082223), which the abstract labels as a suggestion from motif conservation. Two direct experimental tests are negative (PMID: 27249646, PMID: 21568845). An IEA or ISS annotation of this term on APOBEC4 is paralog-driven over-annotation propagated from an InterPro CMP/dCMP deaminase domain match. The defensible actions are removal, or retention with a NOT qualifier referencing 27249646. The structural work here does not rescue the term: an assembled zinc site is necessary and not sufficient, and APOBEC2 is the standing counterexample.

GO:0008270 zinc ion binding: the strongest MF lead, now on two sites. Site 1 is the His93 + Cys127 + Cys134 triad, assembled in the canonical position (0.40 Å anchor RMSD to APOBEC3A), 97.9-98.7% invariant across 233 orthologs, and already annotated by UniProt at 93, 127 and 134 with Glu95 as proton donor. Site 2 is Cys28, Cys31, His34 and Cys87, at 2.41 Å circumsphere radius, 98.3% invariant, AlphaMissense 0.916-0.943, reproduced in 9 independent ortholog models, the only metal-compatible quadruple of 11 in the protein, and absent from APOBEC3A, APOBEC3G, AID and APOBEC2. Appropriate evidence code is ISS or a computational code, since no direct metal-binding measurement on APOBEC4 protein has been published. A curator should not upgrade to IDA without ICP-MS, PAR or metal-titration data, and that measurement should now be framed as a stoichiometry question (one metal or two). For a residue-level annotation, the candidate ligand sets are 93/127/134 and 28/31/34/87. Only the first is currently annotated.

Do not record any claim that the APOBEC4 substrate channel is sterically occluded. That claim appeared in an earlier draft of this report and failed its apo control.

Avoid "protein binding" as a terminal recommendation. Zinc ion binding is both better-supported and more informative.

GO:0016554 cytidine to uridine editing and the UniProt "mRNA processing" keyword: flag as database carry-over. No editing substrate has been identified for any APOBEC4 ortholog, and the spermatogenesis-editing rationale of 16082223 is weakened by viable Apobec4 knockout mice (PMID: 42975197).

Candidate replacement BP leads, both single-paper and needing verification: GO:0045944 positive regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II (27249646, overexpression-based) and GO:0042254 ribosome biogenesis (38976757, siRNA screen with the deaminase-independent mechanism shown mainly for APOBEC3A).

CC GO:0005737 cytoplasm: weak lead from ectopic expression in HeLa (27249646).

Conflicts and alternatives

The seed's own mechanism conflicts with the structure. The hypothesis anticipates that the zinc pocket is not assembled. It is. A curator who adopts the hypothesis as written would record a structural claim this analysis contradicts.

The conservation data cut against a simple pseudo-enzyme reading. More than 300 million years of near-invariance at His93, Glu95, Cys127 and Cys134, now joined by Cys28, Cys31, His34 and Cys87 at the same level, is hard to reconcile with a fully decayed protein. Three alternatives remain open: APOBEC4 binds metal for a structural or regulatory purpose without turnover (the APOBEC2 precedent); APOBEC4 is a conditional enzyme requiring a partner, a post-translational modification or an untested substrate; or APOBEC4 acts on a substrate that is not ssDNA, in which case the ssDNA-based negatives are uninformative.

Paralog confusion is a live risk. 38976757 reports APOBEC3A and APOBEC4 together and the mechanistic work is largely on APOBEC3A, so the APOBEC3A mechanism should not be transferred. 25904160 reports APOBEC4 expression changes alongside APOBEC3B in myeloma, which is transcriptional co-regulation.

Organism-specific differences. Chicken lacks APOBEC1 and APOBEC3 entirely (31991164), so chicken APOBEC4 may carry functions the human gene does not. The chicken antiviral result should not be transferred.

Loop flexibility defeated one of my own analyses. The AlphaFold model is apo, and the control showed the occlusion reading is an apo artifact. Two of the three structural metrics tried, zinc burial and substrate occlusion, do not separate active from inactive enzymes.

Knowledge gaps

  1. No measured zinc stoichiometry for APOBEC4. Checked: UniProt binding-site annotations and the structural models here, both inferential. Matters because GO:0008270 is the recommended term and there are now two candidate sites. Resolved by purified-protein ICP-MS or a metal-displacement titration.

  2. No experimental APOBEC4 structure. Checked: RCSB has no APOBEC4 entry; the only models are AlphaFold predictions. Matters because every geometric statement in this report rests on prediction. Resolved by a crystal or cryo-EM structure, ideally of the full-length protein rather than the 61-177 deaminase domain alone, since the domain construct would miss the second site.

  3. Physiological substrate unknown. Checked: 27249646 (ssDNA, negative), 21568845 (genomic DNA mutator, negative), 16082223 (mRNA, predicted only). Matters because the negatives only exclude the substrates tested. Resolved by RNA and DNA C-to-U sequencing in an APOBEC4-overexpressing versus knockout background.

  4. The role of the conserved His65. Checked: 93.6% conserved across 233 orthologs and sitting where the target cytosine would land in the apo model, but AlphaMissense ranks it 0.511 (63rd percentile), which is weak for a functional residue. Matters because the apo-artifact explanation is now the leading one. Resolved by an H65A mutant tested for ssDNA binding and deamination.

  5. Whether Cys133 or Cys134 is the second ligand. Checked: resolved computationally here (Cys127-Cys133 SG-SG 7.67 Å, geometrically impossible; Cys134 gives 3.80 Å) and consistent with chicken Cys138 (31991164). Matters because mutagenesis targeting the wrong cysteine would produce an uninterpretable negative.

  6. Whether a GO NOT qualifier is warranted or whether removal is cleaner. Checked: GO's conventions require a specific negative reference; 27249646 supplies one for in vitro ssDNA deamination only. A curator should decide between a scoped NOT cytidine deaminase activity (IDA, 27249646) and silent removal of the ISS annotation.

  7. Whether the Cys28/Cys31/His34/Cys87 site binds a metal, and which one. Checked: geometry in the human model and in 9 ortholog models, 233-ortholog conservation, AlphaMissense, PAE, an 11-quadruple within-protein null, and a cluster scan of 5 experimental APOBEC structures. Not described in 16082223, 31991164 or any other paper found here, and absent from UniProt Q8WW27. Every line of evidence is predictive. Resolved by ICP-MS or X-ray anomalous scattering on recombinant full-length APOBEC4 with a C28S/C31S double mutant as the control. A Cys3His site would be expected to hold Zn(II), but geometry alone does not exclude Fe or another divalent metal.

  8. The Apobec4-specific mouse fertility outcome. Checked: 42975197 lists Apobec4 among 39 testis-enriched genes and reports all 38 lines viable with 30 fertile lines covering 31 genes, but the abstract does not itemize which gene each outcome belongs to. Resolved by reading the paper's per-gene table.

Discriminating tests

Ranked by how much each would move the curation decision per unit of effort.

  1. Metal stoichiometry of recombinant full-length APOBEC4 by ICP-MS, with H93A, C127A/C134A and C28S/C31S as separate negatives. Validates or kills the GO:0008270 recommendation and distinguishes one metal from two. Use full-length protein.
  2. Deamination panel across substrate classes (ssDNA, ssRNA, dsDNA bubble, 5-methyl-C, tRNA, pre-rRNA fragments) on purified protein, with APOBEC3A as positive control and an APOBEC4 E95A mutant as specificity control. This is the single experiment that would convert "unresolved" into a defensible annotation either way.
  3. Basic-cleft restoration mutants. APOBEC4 has 4 Lys+Arg in the cleft against 6-9 in the references. Add back 2-3 basic residues at the positions occupied by APOBEC3A's cleft lysines and re-measure ssDNA binding by fluorescence anisotropy. This tests the surviving structural claim and replaces the loop-swap experiment proposed on the strength of the withdrawn occlusion result.
  4. Catalytic-dead rescue in the ribosome biogenesis assay. Re-run the 38976757 nucleolar assay with APOBEC4 wild type versus E95A. If E95A rescues, the cellular function is deaminase-independent and the MF annotation should stay off regardless.
  5. C-to-U editing scan in testis. Compare RNA and DNA from wild-type and Apobec4 knockout mouse testis (lines exist from 42975197). A null result in the tissue of highest expression would be the strongest available in vivo negative.
  6. AlphaFold3 or Boltz prediction of APOBEC4 with two zinc ions and a ssDNA oligo, compared to the same prediction for APOBEC3A. Cheap, and tests both whether the second site holds a metal when one is supplied and whether loop1 opens when a substrate is present.

Limitations

Every structural statement in this report rests on AlphaFold predictions. RCSB has no APOBEC4 entry. Side-chain rotamers are predicted, no metal is present in any model, and the models are apo.

Two metrics were rejected after controls, and one statistic was withdrawn as uninformative. A four-point equidistant fit is exact by construction, so the "zero spread" first reported at the second metal site carries no information; the circumsphere radius replaces it and the conclusion survives. Both self-corrections are recorded here rather than deleted.

Conservation and AlphaMissense measure constraint, not catalysis. Near-invariance at the metal ligands shows the site matters; it does not show that APOBEC4 turns over a substrate.

The two experimental negatives used ssDNA and genomic DNA. They do not exclude activity on an untested substrate, under an untested cofactor condition, or with an untested post-translational modification.

gnomAD gene-level constraint for APOBEC4 is unavailable: all loss-of-function fields are null and whole-gene missense constraint is absent (oe_mis 0.850). The per-residue AlphaMissense signal is a local feature against an otherwise unconstrained background.

Host-side HTTPS was blocked (403) in this environment, so Phenix could not be run on downloaded coordinates. All geometry was computed in the Python executor with an explicit Kabsch superposition, a Shrake-Rupley style probe sampler and an exact circumsphere solve. The file provenance/AF-Q8WW27.pdb is a 134-byte error page left by that failed download and holds no coordinates.
