## Database and original-evidence guide

ERRβ has a sequence-specific DNA-binding domain and activates RNA-polymerase-II transcription. Human long and short constructs have been tested directly; the natural 500-residue product remains distinct. Steroid-specific receptor/binding/signaling assertions are unresolved. Pharmacological modulation is positive evidence and is not excluded by the description of ERRβ as an orphan receptor.
