CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of neuronal acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) that
CC       function as pentameric, ligand-gated cation channels with high calcium
CC       permeability among other activities. nAChRs are excitatory
CC       neurotrasnmitter receptors formed by a collection of nAChR subunits
CC       known to mediate synaptic transmission in the nervous system and the
CC       neuromuscular junction. Each nAchR subunit confers differential
CC       attributes to channel properties, including activation, deactivation
CC       and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, cation permeability, and
CC       binding to allosteric modulators (PubMed:22361591, PubMed:27698419,
CC       PubMed:29720657, PubMed:38454578). CHRNB2 forms heteropentameric
CC       neuronal acetylcholine receptors with CHRNA2, CHRNA3, CHRNA4 and
CC       CHRNA6, as well as CHRNA5 and CHRNB3 as accesory subunits
CC       (PubMed:16835356, PubMed:20881005, PubMed:22361591, PubMed:27698419,
CC       PubMed:29720657, PubMed:38454578, PubMed:8663494). Found in two major
CC       stoichiometric forms,(CHRNA4)3:(CHRNB2)2 and (CHRNA4)2:(CHRNB2)3, the
CC       two stoichiometric forms differ in their unitary conductance, calcium
CC       permeability, ACh sensitivity and potentiation by divalent cation
CC       (PubMed:27698419, PubMed:29720657, PubMed:38454578). Heteropentameric
CC       channels with CHRNA6 and CHRNA4 exhibit high sensitivity to ACh and
CC       nicotine and are predominantly expressed in only a few brain areas,
CC       including dopaminergic neurons, norepirephrine neurons and cells of the
CC       visual system. nAChrs containing CHRNA6 subunits mediate endogenous
CC       cholinergic modulation of dopamine and gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA)
CC       release in response to nicotine at nerve terminals (By similarity).
CC       Also forms functional nAChRs with other subunits such as CHRNA7:CHRNB2,
CC       mainly expressed in basal forebrain cholinergic neurons
CC       (PubMed:33239400, PubMed:38161283). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P12390,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16835356, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20881005,

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by a myriad of ligands such as
CC       acetylcholine, cytisine, nicotine, choline and epibatidine
CC       (PubMed:12527804, PubMed:16835356, PubMed:17132685, PubMed:20881005,
CC       PubMed:27698419, PubMed:29720657, PubMed:38454578). Channel
CC       potentiation by calcium is stoichiometry-selective, CHRNA4:CHRNB2 nACh
CC       receptor is achieved by calcium association with topographically
CC       distinct sites framed by anionic residues within the CHRNA4 subunit and
CC       between the CHRNA4 and CHRNB2 subunits (PubMed:38454578). Oligomeric
CC       amyloid-beta protein 42 activates specifially CHRNA7:CHRNB2 nAchRs
CC       (PubMed:33239400). nAChR activity is inhibited by the antagonist alpha-
CC       conotoxins BuIA, PnIA, PnIC, GID and MII, small disulfide-constrained
CC       peptides from cone snails (PubMed:15609996, PubMed:38161283).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12527804, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15609996,

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Neuronal AChR is a heteropentamer composed of two different
CC       types of subunits: alpha and beta. CHRNB2/Beta-2 subunit can be
CC       combined to CHRNA2/alpha-2, CHRNA3/alpha-3 or CHRNA4/alpha-4,
CC       CHRNA5/alpha-5, CHRNA6/alpha-6 and CHRNB3/beta-3 to give rise to
CC       functional receptors. CHRNA2:CHRNB2 and CHRNA4:CHRNB2 nAChR complexes
CC       exist in two subtypes: LS (low agonist sensitivity) with a
CC       (CHRNA2/4)3:(CHRNB2)2 and HS (high agonist sensitivity) with a
CC       (CHRNA2/4)2:(CHRNB2)3 stoichiometry; the subtypes differ in their
CC       subunit binding interfaces which are involved in ligand binding
CC       (PubMed:12527804, PubMed:16835356, PubMed:17132685, PubMed:22361591,
CC       PubMed:27493220, PubMed:27698419, PubMed:29720657, PubMed:38454578).
CC       Cells produce predominantly an (CHRNA4)3:(CHRNB2)2 nAChR. The
CC       stoichiometric form (CHRNA4)2:(CHRNB2)3 expression is selectively up-
CC       regulated by nicotine and has lower single channel conductance and
CC       calcium permeability (PubMed:17132685). Also part of the stoichiometric
CC       forms: (CHRNA4:CHRNB2)2:CHRNB3 or (CHRNA6:CHRNB2)2:CHRNB3
CC       (PubMed:16835356). Can form heteropentamers with CHRNA7, mainly found
CC       in basal forebrain cholinergic neurons (PubMed:33239400,
CC       PubMed:38161283). Interacts with RIC3; which is required for proper
CC       folding and assembly (PubMed:16120769). Interacts with LYPD6
CC       (PubMed:27344019). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P12390,
