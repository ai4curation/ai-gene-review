## Biological synthesis

β2 contributes pore-lining helices and the complementary orthosteric pocket surface to assembled neuronal nicotinic receptors. It does not form an autonomous channel. A single core therefore uses contributes_to GO:0022848, with plasma-membrane/acetylcholine-channel-complex location and the directly performed receptor-signaling and cation-transmembrane-transport processes. Actual ACh density at human alpha4–β2 interfaces supports the binding contribution; mutant sensitivity by itself would not distinguish affinity from gating (PMID:38454578; PMID:15964197). Human structures and reconstitution support the underlying channel capability (PMID:27698419; PMID:29720657; PMID:8906617).

Species, construct and stoichiometry limits are explicit. Structural constructs can contain loop deletions, tags or Bril insertions; functional full-length recordings are distinguished from those structures. The alpha7/beta2 source uses a defined concatemer with NACHO in Xenopus oocytes, not a universal native pentamer. Calcium permeability, calcium-only current and calcium-dependent potentiation are different measurements. Human concatemer experiments support calcium conduction with composition-dependent permeability (PMID:17132685; PMID:20881005). No separate calcium-binding MF or unsupported new process is proposed.
