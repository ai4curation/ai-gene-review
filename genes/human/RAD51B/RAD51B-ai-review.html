Gene Description

RAD51B (RAD51L1, REC2) is one of the five human RAD51 paralogs, RecA/RAD51-family proteins with Walker A/B ATP-binding motifs that work as accessory factors to the RAD51 recombinase. It is found only in the heterotetrameric BCDX2 complex (RAD51B-RAD51C-RAD51D-XRCC2), where it pairs with RAD51C as the BC subcomplex. RAD51B is not part of the RAD51C-XRCC3 (CX3) or XRCC3-RAD51C-RAD51D-XRCC2 (X3CDX2) complexes. Purified RAD51B binds single- and double-stranded DNA, prefers Holliday-junction DNA, and hydrolyses ATP in a DNA-dependent manner. Within BCDX2 it is one of two catalytically active ATPase subunits. The coupled ATPases of RAD51B and RAD51C drive high-affinity binding of BCDX2 to single-stranded DNA, and BCDX2 then stimulates nucleation and extension of RAD51 nucleoprotein filaments on RPA-coated DNA. Its flexible C-terminal domain is thought to engage RAD51 during filament nucleation. BCDX2 acts in the BRCA1-BRCA2-dependent homologous recombination pathway, downstream of BRCA2 recruitment and upstream of RAD51 loading. It also binds replication-fork and Holliday-junction DNA and, together with RAD51, protects stalled and reversed replication forks from nucleolytic degradation. Rad51b loss is embryonic lethal in mice. In human cell lines RAD51B-deficient cells have the mildest homologous recombination defects of the five paralogs. RAD51B is a breast cancer susceptibility locus and a recurrent translocation partner of HMGA2 in uterine leiomyoma.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

DNA recombinase loader activity

Definition: Binding to a DNA strand exchange protein (recombinase) and to single-stranded DNA so as to promote nucleation and extension of the recombinase nucleoprotein filament, including displacement of the single-stranded DNA-binding protein that otherwise occludes the substrate. The loader is not itself a strand exchange protein and does not catalyse strand exchange.

Justification: Same proposal as in the RAD51C review. GO has the process GO:0000730 DNA recombinase assembly but no molecular function for the mediator activity that carries it out. As a result, the RAD51 paralogs carry borrowed terms such as GO:0140664 ATP-dependent DNA damage sensor activity, which describes a sensor rather than a loader. BCDX2, in which RAD51B provides one of the two coupled ATPase sites, is a defining example.

Parent term: single-stranded DNA binding

Core Functions

As the BCDX2-specific subunit, RAD51B forms the BC subcomplex with RAD51C. Their coupled ATPase cycle drives high-affinity single-stranded DNA binding by BCDX2, which then acts as a recombination mediator: it stimulates nucleation and extension of RAD51 filaments on RPA-coated ssDNA, downstream of BRCA2 and upstream of RAD51 loading, during homologous recombination repair of DNA double-strand breaks.
