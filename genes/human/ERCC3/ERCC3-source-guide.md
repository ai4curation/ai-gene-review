## Ontology and event interpretation

[GO:0043138](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0043138) currently has a 3′–5′ helicase label but a definition describing the opposite direction; [OLS](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/ontologies/go/classes/http%253A%252F%252Fpurl.obolibrary.org%252Fobo%252FGO_0043138) independently reproduces the inconsistency. The actual [EC5.6.2.4](https://enzyme.expasy.org/EC/5.6.2.4) record describes 3′–5′ movement. Imported IDs/labels remain intact; assay direction comes from the actual human original.

[GO:0015616](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0015616) describes ATP-driven DNA movement and is currently unrestricted MF. It is used for the principal motor refinement, while [GO:0016887](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0016887) retains separately measured ATP chemistry. Neither is inferred to be the other’s current ontology ancestor.

[Reactome 73776](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-73776) models promoter escape. Current [GO:0006367](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0006367) and its initiation parent explicitly distinguish clearance from initiation, while GO:0006368 starts after clearance. [GO:0001111](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0001111) therefore supplies the source-specific promoter-clearance replacement; its ancestry includes transcription by Pol II, not initiation or elongation.

The backtracking discussion in Reactome 9684118 uses a bacterial UvrD analogy. Retaining an independently measured human helicase capacity does not certify every modeled human backtracking step. The 9613494/9613497 event objects also use nucleotide-product representations that should not replace actual XPB phosphate-release chemistry; no claim of AMP/PPi production by XPB is made.
