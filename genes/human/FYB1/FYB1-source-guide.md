# FYB1 source guide

FYB1/ADAP coordinates signaling proteins, membrane recruitment and actin-associated adhesion responses. The review distinguishes its intracellular adaptor activity from direct receptor binding, the inside-out activation of integrins from ligand-initiated outside-in signaling, and natural products from the tagged fragments used in individual experiments.

## Mechanism and major annotation decisions

[PMID:10747096](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10747096/), selected original Methods, Results and Figures 3–9, supports human FYB association with Ena/VASP and SLP-76/Nck/WASP machinery at the actin interface. It does not establish direct FYB–TCR binding. The existing signaling-receptor-binding assertion is therefore changed laterally to signaling adaptor activity, GO:0035591. The biochemical complex observation is an association result, not proof that all partners bind one another directly.

[PMID:16980616](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16980616/), selected original Methods and Results/Figures 3–7, separates plasma-membrane recruitment of Rap1 machinery from Rap1 nucleotide loading. [PMID:17403904](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1900018/), Methods and Figures 4 and 7, distinguishes human primary T-cell/Jurkat observations from mouse experiments and SKAP-mediated linkage from direct ADAP–RIAM contact. These studies support signaling organization and recruitment, not a FYB guanine-nucleotide-exchange activity.

[PMID:19996090](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2844019/), indexed original Methods and Results/Figures 2–3/6, provides mouse ADAP outside-in platelet evidence under shear, including direct integrin activation that fails to rescue the defect. [PMID:15572693](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC533979/), indexed Methods/Results/Figure 5, instead studies mouse Pram1. Official donor annotation connects that separate protein to the integrin-signaling IBD. TCR-driven inside-out adhesion alone is not used as evidence for the ligand-initiated GO:0007229 definition. The selected indexed original sections were actually read; failed direct PMC, bodyless XML and failed Europe PMC responses remain excluded as full-text evidence.

The actual PTHR16830 tree and alignment place human FYB1 at AN66/PTN002497673 with the exact 783-residue sequence. AN3/PTN001047914 and AN0/PTN002314453 are genuine ancestors. Mouse Pram1, mouse Fyb1 and human FYB2 donors retain their distinct identities. A single donor is not intrinsically weak evidence, and self-membership is not circularity.

Official InterPro IPR035540 maps the human C-terminal hSH3 region. Authentic original abstracts [PMID:15843031](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15843031/) and [PMID:16831444](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16831444/) support acidic phospholipid binding, making GO:0005543 more informative than generic lipid binding. Full quantitative constructs and conditions were not inspected. No exclusive individual phosphoinositide preference or current product specificity is inferred, and an altered conventional SH3 recognition surface does not erase independently supported noncanonical SKAP binding.

## Compartments and products

Human FYB membrane recruitment and cytosolic association are supported by the selected primary experiments and curated Reactome event summaries. The two Reactome events support cytosol; their full participant trees were not recovered. Auxiliary broad pathways supply metadata only and create no new process annotation.

The nuclear UniProt comment is a PROSITE-ProRule prediction. PMID:9207119 describes putative localization motifs; PMID:9671755's FYB imaging is cytoplasmic/perinuclear, whereas its separate nuclear observation concerns SKAP55. PMID:19807924's nuclear label is explicitly imported from UniProt, not a new imaging result. Both nuclear annotations remain UNDECIDED rather than being rejected from absence of evidence.

[PMID:21881001](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3233801/), selected original Methods/Results/Figures, supports a specialized podocyte cell-contact context. Human and mouse endogenous localization, mouse kidney two-hybrid fragments and tagged MDCK assays remain separate. The broad complex row is non-core; a mouse MIST–SLAP-130–SKAP55 association abstract and independent human functional/purification evidence do not imply that every observed interactor forms one stable entity.

UniProt documents three exact sequences of 783, 829 and 839 residues. Historical short/long FYB proteins, phosphorylation-site mutants, tags and isolated domains are not automatically assigned to a current natural product. Distinct functional contributions remain unresolved.
