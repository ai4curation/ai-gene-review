# IL34 (Interleukin-34) Curation Notes

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Gene symbol**: `IL34` (`UniProtKB:Q6ZMJ4`, 242 aa; signal peptide residues 1–20, mature secreted chain residues 21–242).
- **Protein family**: `PANTHER:PTHR28606` (`INTERLEUKIN-34`, subfamily `PANTHER:PTHR28606:SF1` `INTERLEUKIN-34`).
- **Discovery and core mechanism**:
  - Identified in a functional screen of the human secreted proteome as a homodimeric four-helix-bundle cytokine that binds the macrophage colony-stimulating factor 1 receptor (`CSF1R`, `UniProtKB:P07333`) on human monocytes and stimulates monocyte viability [PMID:18467591, "One of the highly selective proteins was a previously undescribed ligand, designated interleukin-34 (IL-34), which stimulates monocyte viability but does not affect responses in a wide spectrum of other assays. In a separate functional screen, we used a collection of extracellular domains of transmembrane proteins to discover the receptor for IL-34, which was a known cytokine receptor, colony-stimulating factor 1 (also called macrophage colony-stimulating factor) receptor."], as well as `CSF1R`-dependent `ERK1/2` (`MAPK1`/`MAPK3`) phosphorylation in human monocytes [Reactome:R-HSA-6787820, "Like CSF1, IL34 stimulation of CSF1R leads to phosphorylation of extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK) 1 and 2 in human monocytes (Lin et al. 2008)."].
  - Crystal structures of free `IL34` and the `IL34:CSF1R` complex showed that `IL34` adopts an antiparallel four-helix bundle fold homologous to `CSF1` and binds the N-terminal immunoglobulin domains of `CSF1R` in an assembly similar to `CSF1:CSF1R` [PMID:22483114, "Here, the crystal structure of dimeric IL-34 reveals a helical cytokine fold homologous to CSF-1, and we further show that the complex architecture of IL-34 bound to the N-terminal immunoglobulin domains of CSF-1R is similar to the CSF-1/CSF-1R assembly."].
  - In human peripheral blood monocytes, `IL34` signals via `CSF1R` to drive differentiation into immunosuppressive M2-like (`CD14(high) CD163(high) CD1a(-)`, `IL-10(high) IL-12(low)`) macrophages [PMID:23409120, "We report here that IL-34 induces the differentiation of monocytes into CD14(high) CD163(high) CD1a(-) Mφ (IL-34-Mφ). Upon LPS stimulation, IL-34-Mφ exhibit an IL-10(high) IL-12(low) M2 profile and express low levels of the costimulatory molecules CD80 and CD86."].
  - In hepatocellular carcinoma (`HCC`), `IL34` is a direct target of `miR-28-5p` that mediates tumor-associated macrophage (`TAM`) infiltration (the cached entry is abstract-only; GOA curators annotated `GO:0010759`, `GO:0043410`, `GO:0061514`, and `GO:0120041` from the full text) [PMID:26754294, "Through gene expression profiles and bioinformatics analysis, we identified interleukin-34 (IL-34) as a direct target of miR-28-5p, and the effects of miR-28-5p deficiency on HCC growth and metastasis was dependent on IL-34-mediated tumor-associated macrophage (TAM) infiltration."].
  - Unlike ubiquitously expressed `CSF1`, `IL34` expression is tissue-restricted—produced predominantly by epidermal keratinocytes and central nervous system neurons—where it is non-redundantly required for the development, survival, and proliferation of Langerhans cells and microglia via `CSF1R` [PMID:30066957]. `IL34` also interacts with receptor-type protein-tyrosine phosphatase zeta (`PTPRZ1`) [Reactome:R-HSA-8981657, "It can also bind Receptor-type protein-tyrosine phosphatase zeta (PTPRZ1), a cell surface chondroitin sulfate (CS) proteoglycan."] and syndecan-1 (`SDC1`), which modulate its bioavailability and signaling [PMID:30066957; Reactome:R-HSA-9009558, "IL34 dimers can bind to the chondroitin sulphate of SDC1. Subsequently, SDC1 modulates IL34-induced CSF1R signaling pathways (Segaliny et al. 2015)."].
