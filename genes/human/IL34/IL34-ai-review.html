Gene Description

Interleukin-34 (IL34) is a secreted homodimeric four-helix-bundle cytokine that functions as a tissue-restricted agonist of the macrophage colony-stimulating factor 1 receptor (CSF1R). Produced predominantly by epidermal keratinocytes and central nervous system neurons, IL34 binds the extracellular immunoglobulin-like D2-D3 junction of CSF1R to induce receptor dimerization, tyrosine autophosphorylation, and activation of downstream MAPK/ERK, PI3K-AKT, FAK, and STAT3 signaling cascades. Through CSF1R signaling, IL34 promotes the survival, proliferation, chemotaxis, and differentiation of monocytes, tissue macrophages, epidermal Langerhans cells, and brain microglia, and also interacts with syndecan-1 (SDC1) and receptor-type tyrosine-protein phosphatase zeta (PTPRZ1) at the cell surface.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: How do co-receptors syndecan-1 (SDC1) and receptor-type protein-tyrosine phosphatase zeta (PTPRZ1) quantitatively partition IL34 signaling between CSF1R-dependent myeloid/microglial trophic responses and CSF1R-independent signaling in neural and tumor tissues?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Single-molecule TIRF imaging and quantitative phosphoproteomics of primary human monocytes and induced pluripotent stem cell-derived microglia stimulated with equimolar homodimeric IL34, homodimeric CSF1, or heterodimeric IL34:CSF1 in the presence and absence of SDC1 heparan sulfate chains to compare CSF1R dimer lifetime, internalization kinetics, and STAT3/ERK1/2/FAK phosphorylation dynamics.

Hypothesis: SDC1 binding concentrates IL34 at the plasma membrane and prolongs CSF1R surface residency compared with CSF1, producing distinct temporal profiles of ERK1/2 and FAK activation.

IL34 (Interleukin-34) Curation Notes

Summary of Gene Function

  • Gene symbol: IL34 (UniProtKB:Q6ZMJ4, 242 aa; signal peptide residues 1–20, mature secreted chain residues 21–242).
  • Protein family: PANTHER:PTHR28606 (INTERLEUKIN-34, subfamily PANTHER:PTHR28606:SF1 INTERLEUKIN-34).
  • Discovery and core mechanism:
  • Identified in a functional screen of the human secreted proteome as a homodimeric four-helix-bundle cytokine that binds the macrophage colony-stimulating factor 1 receptor (CSF1R, UniProtKB:P07333) on human monocytes with higher affinity than CSF1, stimulating CSF1R tyrosine phosphorylation, ERK1/2 (MAPK1/MAPK3) phosphorylation, monocyte survival/proliferation, and bone marrow macrophage colony (CFU-M) formation [PMID:18467591, "When added to primary human monocytes, purified recombinant human IL-34... stimulated tyrosine phosphorylation of a ~165 kD protein (Fig. 2B) and the phosphorylation of extracellular signal-regulated kinase 1 and 2 (ERK1/2)"].
  • Crystal structures of free IL34 and the IL34:CSF1R (D1–D3) ternary complex showed that IL34 adopts an antiparallel four-helix bundle fold homologous to CSF1 and bridges the D2–D3 junction of CSF1R to drive noncovalent receptor homodimerization [PMID:22483114, "We now present the crystal structures of human IL-34 and the ternary complex between human IL-34 and the N-terminal三个 immunoglobulin-like domains (D1-D3) of human CSF-1R"].
  • In human peripheral blood monocytes, IL34 signals via CSF1R to drive differentiation into immunosuppressive M2-like (CD14+ CD163+, IL-10^high IL-12^low) macrophages [PMID:23409120, "We show that IL-34 induces the differentiation of human monocytes into CD14high CD163high macrophages that produce high levels of IL-10 and low levels of IL-12"].
  • In hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), tumor-secreted IL34 binds CSF1R on tumor-associated macrophages to activate ERK1/2 (MAPK1/MAPK3) and FAK (PTK2) cascades, promoting macrophage proliferation and chemotaxis [PMID:26754294, "IL-34 stimulated the proliferation and chemotaxis of macrophages by activating the focal adhesion kinase (FAK) and extracellular signal-regulated kinase 1/2 (ERK1/2) signaling pathways via CSF1R"].
