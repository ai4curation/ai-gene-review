| aspect | best-supported annotation | evidence type | caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity / target verification | **ALPI** in **Homo sapiens** encodes **intestinal-type alkaline phosphatase (IAP)**, a brush-border alkaline phosphatase distinct from **ALPL**, which encodes tissue-nonspecific alkaline phosphatase (TNAP). ALPI is the intestinal isozyme relevant to UniProt **P09923**. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000008) | Human genetics, biochemical review, enzyme-family analysis | Literature on alkaline phosphatase often mixes isozymes; ALPI-specific conclusions should not be transferred from ALPL without support. |
| Protein architecture / localization | Human ALPI is synthesized as a precursor with an N-terminal signal peptide, contains the phosphatase domain, forms an active homodimer, and is displayed on the **intestinal brush border** as a **GPI-anchored** ectoenzyme; it is also released from microvilli into the lumen and can enter blood. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000008) | Human mutation paper, 2024 review | Some structural details are inferred from family/structural modeling rather than a dedicated modern human ALPI crystal structure. |
| Catalytic reaction | ALPI is a **metalloenzyme phosphomonoesterase** that hydrolyzes phosphate esters, including removal of phosphate from **lipid A** of LPS and from extracellular nucleotides. (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000007, pqac-00000008) | Human genetics, mechanistic biochemistry | Precise catalytic constants vary by substrate, assay, and whether native or engineered enzyme is tested. |
| Metal dependence / family | ALPI belongs to the alkaline phosphatase family and uses catalytic metal ions characteristic of the family, including **Zn2+** dependence highlighted in mechanistic studies. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002) | Enzyme kinetics / family studies | Much direct metal-site evidence comes from comparative or engineered-enzyme work, not exclusively native intestinal ALPI. |
| Physiological substrates | Best-supported substrates/functions include **LPS**, **ATP/ADP/AMP**, **UDP** and other nucleotides; reviews also discuss activity toward **PPi**. For intestinal homeostasis, the strongest support is for **LPS detoxification** and **extracellular nucleotide dephosphorylation**. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000006) | Primary biochemical studies, reviews, human mutation paper | Evidence strength is uneven across substrates; PPi is more central to ALPL/TNAP biology than to the core validated human ALPI phenotype. |
| Main molecular function | ALPI detoxifies pro-inflammatory microbial and host-derived phosphorylated molecules, especially converting highly stimulatory **bisphosphorylated LPS** into a less stimulatory form and degrading luminal pro-inflammatory nucleotides. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000008) | Human genetic mechanism, reviews | “Detoxification” is mechanistically strongest for specific LPS chemotypes and inflammatory contexts. |
| Pathways / biological processes | ALPI participates in **host–microbiota homeostasis**, **TLR4/LPS response control**, **purinergic signaling modulation** via adenosine generation from ATP/ADP/AMP, and supports **epithelial barrier** function and microbiota composition. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000007) | Reviews, in vitro/in vivo mechanistic studies, human genetics | Barrier and microbiome effects are partly indirect downstream consequences of dephosphorylation activity. |
| Tissue / cell expression | ALPI is highly expressed in **intestinal epithelial enterocytes**, concentrated at the **apical brush border**; stool activity reflects luminal enzyme output. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000007) | Human biopsy/stool data, cell biology, review | Broader extra-intestinal detection in blood is reported, but the principal physiological site is the intestinal mucosa. |
| Human genetic evidence | **Biallelic loss-of-function ALPI mutations** were identified as a Mendelian cause of severe inflammatory bowel disease. Mutations impaired stability, dimerization, catalytic activity, cell-surface expression, or GPI anchoring, and patient stool ALPI activity was absent/reduced. (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000007, pqac-00000008) | Human primary genetics with functional validation | Current evidence comes from a small number of families, so penetrance and phenotypic breadth remain incompletely defined. |
| Transcript complexity | Human ALPI has at least two alternatively spliced transcripts; one studied variant retained detectable ALP activity whereas another lacked detectable activity and structural elements near the active site. (pqac-00000004) | Transcript/structure-functional study | Functional relevance of transcript variation in vivo remains uncertain. |
| Recent 2024 developments | 2024 literature emphasized broader anti-inflammatory mechanisms, including links to purinergic signaling, oxidative stress, barrier regulation, microbiota modulation, and possible autophagy-related effects; 2024 animal studies continued testing exogenous IAP in colitis and metabolic/inflammatory settings. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000003) | 2024 reviews and preclinical studies | These developments mostly extend mechanism and therapeutic interest rather than redefine ALPI’s core validated enzymatic role. |
| Translational / clinical status | Clinical translation focuses on **exogenous alkaline phosphatase therapies** (bovine IAP or recombinant/engineered AP such as recAP/ilofotase alfa), especially for **ulcerative colitis** and **sepsis-associated AKI**. Trials include completed phase 2 UC and SA-AKI studies and a terminated phase 3 SA-AKI trial. These agents are **not native endogenous human ALPI**. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010) | Trial registries, therapeutic enzyme study | Clinical evidence concerns pharmacologic AP products, often engineered or nonhuman, so efficacy/safety data should not be interpreted as direct evidence about native ALPI biology. |


*Table: This table summarizes the best-supported functions, localization, substrates, pathways, and translational relevance of human ALPI/IAP, while clearly distinguishing it from ALPL/TNAP. It is useful as a compact evidence-based annotation scaffold for downstream gene interpretation.*
