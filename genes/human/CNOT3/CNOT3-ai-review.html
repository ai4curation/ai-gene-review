Gene Description

CNOT3 is a noncatalytic subunit of the CCR4–NOT complex that connects translation to mRNA turnover. Its N-terminal region recognizes ribosomes with a vacant E-site and contacts the P-site tRNA, while its C-terminal region assembles with CNOT2 on the CNOT1 scaffold. Recognition of particular tRNA features helps recruit CCR4–NOT to slowly decoded transcripts and promotes their decay. The CNOT3-containing NOT module also supports efficient deadenylation by the complex’s catalytic subunits. CNOT3 acts in cytosolic RNA-regulatory assemblies, including context-dependent P-body pools, and has a nuclear pool associated with transcription regulation. Its RNA-regulatory functions support stem-cell maintenance and tissue-specific developmental and metabolic programs. Heterozygous pathogenic variants can cause a neurodevelopmental disorder with developmental delay, intellectual disability and hypotonia.

Molecular role and core synthesis

CNOT3 is a noncatalytic recognition and assembly subunit of CCR4–NOT. Its C-terminal NOT module forms a heterodimer with CNOT2 and assembles on the CNOT1 scaffold. Human recombinant-complex experiments show that this module helps the exonucleases perform efficient poly(A) shortening; that structural contribution supports the existing process assertions without assigning intrinsic RNA hydrolysis to CNOT3. The 2019 isolated NOT-module RNA-binding result is negative, but it concerns C-terminal constructs and does not contradict later N-terminal tRNA recognition. [PMID:24121232; PMID:31320642]

In a human translation system, CNOT3 occupies the ribosomal E-site and contacts both ribosomal subunits and the P-site tRNA. tRNA D-arm and CNOT3 contact mutations alter recruitment; selective ribosome profiling and decay reporters connect this recognition to transcript turnover. The 2023 reconstituted study uses recombinant human CCR4–NOT with rabbit ribosomes, while the 2024 work includes human HEK293T lysates and cells. These distinct contexts support two specific molecular functions: protein-tRNA adaptor activity (GO:0180014) and ribosome binding (GO:0043022). One integrated adaptor core describes their coordinated biological role. A separate plain tRNA-binding annotation would repeat the same recognition observation and is not proposed. [PMID:37653243; PMID:39571015]

Both proposed MFs have current official definitions and typed is_a coverage checks. Neither is an ancestor or descendant of a seeded term or the other proposal. There is no additional biological-process proposal: the existing deadenylation and mRNA-catabolism assertions already capture the performed work. The actual source-main GO-CAM index contains no CNOT3/O75175 entry. Lack of a model is not used as positive evidence for an annotation gap.
