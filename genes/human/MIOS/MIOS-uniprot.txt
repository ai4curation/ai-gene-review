CC   -!- FUNCTION: As a component of the GATOR2 complex, functions as an
CC       activator of the amino acid-sensing branch of the mTORC1 signaling
CC       pathway (PubMed:23723238, PubMed:26586190, PubMed:27487210,
CC       PubMed:35831510, PubMed:36528027). The GATOR2 complex indirectly
CC       activates mTORC1 through the inhibition of the GATOR1 subcomplex
CC       (PubMed:23723238, PubMed:26586190, PubMed:27487210, PubMed:35831510,
CC       PubMed:36528027). GATOR2 probably acts as an E3 ubiquitin-protein
CC       ligase toward GATOR1 (PubMed:36528027). In the presence of abundant
CC       amino acids, the GATOR2 complex mediates ubiquitination of the NPRL2
CC       core component of the GATOR1 complex, leading to GATOR1 inactivation
CC       (PubMed:36528027). In the absence of amino acids, GATOR2 is inhibited,
CC       activating the GATOR1 complex (PubMed:25263562, PubMed:25457612,
CC       PubMed:26586190, PubMed:27487210). Within the GATOR2 complex, MIOS is
CC       required to prevent autoubiquitination of WDR24, the catalytic subunit
CC       of the complex (PubMed:35831510). The GATOR2 complex is required for
CC       brain myelination (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8VE19,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23723238, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25263562,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25457612, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26586190,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27487210, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35831510,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36528027}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: The GATOR2 complex is negatively regulated by the
CC       upstream amino acid sensors CASTOR1 and SESN2, which sequester the
CC       GATOR2 complex in absence of amino acids (PubMed:25263562,
CC       PubMed:26586190, PubMed:26972053, PubMed:27487210, PubMed:35831510). In
CC       the presence of abundant amino acids, GATOR2 is released from CASTOR1
CC       and SESN2 and activated (PubMed:25263562, PubMed:26586190,
CC       PubMed:26972053, PubMed:27487210, PubMed:35831510).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25263562, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26586190,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26972053, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27487210,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35831510}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the GATOR2 subcomplex, composed of MIOS, SEC13,
CC       SEH1L, WDR24 and WDR59 (PubMed:23723238, PubMed:35831510,
CC       PubMed:36528027). The GATOR2 complex interacts with CASTOR1 and
CC       CASTOR2; the interaction is negatively regulated by arginine
CC       (PubMed:26972053). CASTOR1 and CASTOR2 convey leucine availability via
CC       direct interaction with MIOS (PubMed:35831510). The GATOR2 complex
CC       interacts with SESN1, SESN2 and SESN3; the interaction is negatively
CC       regulated by amino acids (PubMed:25263562, PubMed:25457612,
CC       PubMed:26586190). Interacts with SAR1A and SAR1B; the interaction is
CC       direct, disrupted by leucine and mediates the interaction of SAR1A or
CC       SAR1B with the GATOR2 complex to negatively regulate the TORC1
CC       signaling upon leucine deprivation (PubMed:34290409).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23723238, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25263562,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25457612, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26586190,
