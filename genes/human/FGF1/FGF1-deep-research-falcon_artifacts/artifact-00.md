| Extracellular space and extracellular matrix | FGF1 lacks an N-terminal signal peptide and bypasses the ER–Golgi pathway. Cellular stress—including heat, serum deprivation, and hypoxia—promotes regulated unconventional secretion involving membrane remodeling or translocation. Secreted FGF1 is retained locally by heparan-sulfate proteoglycans (HSPGs). (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000034, pqac-00000036, pqac-00000039) | S100A13, p40 synaptotagmin-1, AHNAK2, Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase, Tec kinase, phosphatidylserine, HSPGs (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000034, pqac-00000036, pqac-00000037) | Acts as a local autocrine/paracrine ligand; HSPGs concentrate and present FGF1 to FGFR1–FGFR4, supporting receptor assembly, dimerization, kinase activation, and downstream mitogenic, survival, angiogenic, and repair responses. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000015) |
| Plasma membrane and early endosomes | Extracellular FGF1 binds an FGFR–heparan-sulfate complex and undergoes receptor-mediated endocytosis. A vesicular membrane potential and cellular trafficking machinery permit subsequent movement from early endosomes into the cytosol. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) | FGFR1 or FGFR4, heparan sulfate, PI3K, HSP90, p38 kinase, vesicular proton gradient (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) | Initiates canonical FGFR signaling at the membrane and provides an internalized FGF1 pool that can reach the cytosol and nucleus; receptor kinase activity is required for canonical signaling but apparently not for FGF1 membrane translocation itself. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000024) |
| Cytoplasm | FGF1 may remain intracellular before secretion or enter the cytosol after receptor-mediated uptake and endosomal escape. Nuclear-localization-sequence mutants remain cytosolic yet retain substantial anti-apoptotic activity. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000020) | HSP90, LRRC59, gelsolin, nucleophosmin, p53; FGF1 binds the p53 DNA-binding domain directly. (pqac-00000056, pqac-00000057) | Provides an intracrine, FGFR-kinase-independent survival signal. It reduces caspase-3/7 activity and PARP cleavage, preserves mitochondrial membrane potential, and suppresses selected p53-dependent intrinsic-apoptosis responses upstream of mitochondrial outer-membrane permeabilization. (pqac-00000057, pqac-00000059, pqac-00000060, pqac-00000061) |
| Nucleus | After endosomal escape, FGF1 is imported through N-terminal/bipartite nuclear-localization sequences and classical transport machinery. Nuclear accumulation peaks at approximately six hours; PKCδ phosphorylation at Ser130 and a leucine-rich nuclear-export sequence facilitate export and subsequent degradation. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) | FGF1 NLS motifs, LRRC59, karyopherin-α1, karyopherin-β1, Ran, exportin-1, PKCδ, nucleolin (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) | Supports context-dependent intracrine functions associated with survival and neurotrophic responses. Nuclear entry is experimentally separable from anti-apoptotic activity in differentiated cells, because NLS-deficient FGF1 remains protective; therefore, not all intracellular FGF1 functions require nuclear localization. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000059, pqac-00000062) |
