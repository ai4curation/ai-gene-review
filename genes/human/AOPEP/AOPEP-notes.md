# AOPEP notes

- AOPEP (aminopeptidase O, C9orf3) is an M1 zinc metallopeptidase.
  - The catalytic motif is intact by UniProt similarity annotation: H479/H483/E502 zinc ligands, E480 base, Y586.
- **The only enzymology paper is withdrawn.** Díaz-Perales et al. 2005 [PMID:15687497] was withdrawn by JBC in 2019 [PMID:30808004]. It is the same withdrawal batch as the AMZ1 archaemetzincin paper (see genes/human/AMZ1).
  - Its claims (aminopeptidase substrates; angiotensin III → IV) are not used.
  - The withdrawal is why metalloaminopeptidase activity is left UNDECIDED, while metallopeptidase activity and zinc binding are accepted on the motif.
- Localization: a mouse GFP-ApO fusion is nucleolar in COS7 cells, with a mapped nucleolar localization signal; spliced isoforms lack it [PMID:17803194].
  - The PAINT nucleolus IBD (PTN000898498, Eumetazoa) is seeded only by mouse.
  - Human Protein Atlas IF (api search_download, gene AOPEP, fetched 2026-10-04) reports "Cell Junctions" (Approved). It is not cited in the review because there is no cached source. It is worth a curator's look against the nucleolar call.
  - Not in OpenCell.
- Disease: biallelic loss-of-function variants cause recessive dystonia [PMID:34596301]; many later case series.
- The GWAS hits (atrial fibrillation, PCOS) are not used.
