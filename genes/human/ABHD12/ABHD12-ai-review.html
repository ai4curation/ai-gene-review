Gene Description

ABHD12 is a membrane-associated serine hydrolase that degrades lysophosphatidylserine and oxidized phosphatidylserine. It is enriched at the endoplasmic reticulum and controls bioactive lipid pools implicated in neural and immune homeostasis. The human enzyme also hydrolyzes monoacylglycerols, including the endocannabinoid 2-arachidonoylglycerol; the importance of this activity depends on cell type and activation state. Its catalytic serine, aspartate and histidine residues and glycosylation support lipid hydrolysis. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause PHARC, a progressive disorder featuring polyneuropathy, hearing loss, ataxia, retinitis pigmentosa and cataract.
