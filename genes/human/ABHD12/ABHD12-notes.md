## Catalytic and compartment evidence

- [PMID:22969151, original PMC3466009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3466009/): local cache is abstract-only. Indexed primary Methods/Results were recovered by the query `"PMC3466009" "Results" "HEK293"`. Human ABHD12 cDNA was expressed in HEK293 cells; glycerol-production assays establish MAG hydrolysis, with catalytic S246/D333/H372 mutant controls. These are human constructs, not mouse enzymes inferred from a human host. The tested DAG/TAG negatives bound the broad acylglycerol process to MAG catabolism. The early statement about lack of lysophospholipase activity is not a universal headgroup exclusion: the tested LPA/glycerol-readout context differs from later lysoPS fatty-acid assays.
- [PMID:25290914, PMC4188605](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4188605/): complete cached primary article read. Figure 4 directly tests human ABHD12 as a control despite the BAT5/ABHD16A title. The readout is fatty-acid release from 1-AG/1-LG and 1-oleoyl-lysoPS, with LPA, DAG and TAG comparisons. Exact cached result: “hABHD12 catalyzed oleic acid release from LPS but not from LPA”. This source supports the lysoPS-specific MF and glycerophospholipid catabolism. It does not provide the current phosphocholine-specific interpretation of GO:0004622.
- [PMID:30237167, PMC6217928](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6217928/): full cached Methods, Results, Figures 3–7 and limitations read. Human ABHD12 WT/S246A/mock HEK293T membrane preparations support lysoPS and MAG hydrolysis. C10–C24 1-MAG substrates probe chain-length preference; only commercially available C16/C18 lysoPS species were tested here. The authors explicitly allow membrane accessibility/solubility to contribute to apparent kinetics, because the enzyme was not purified. Deglycosylation with detergent and detergent-only comparisons support glycosylation-dependent activity with that assay limitation. Endogenous human MCF7 fractionation and microscopy are distinguished from mouse brain, Neuro-2a and primary macrophage experiments. A dominant ER pool does not prove universal absence from every other membrane.
- [PMID:30643283, PMC6420073](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6420073/): full cached primary source read. Human WT/S246A/mock comparisons establish oxidized-PS hydrolysis, with negligible activity against the tested unoxidized PS. Methods quantify both free fatty acid and lysoPS products. The corresponding experimentally sourced UniProt reactions RHEA:59364/59368/59372 retain sn-1 acyl and release oxidized sn-2 fatty acid, supporting A2-type chemistry. Oxidized-PS accumulation and cytokine outputs were tested in mouse macrophages and under severe acute brain inflammatory challenge; human THP1 lipid responses are a separate experiment. No generic apoptosis, phagocytosis or ferroptosis process is inferred just because these lipids affect those outcomes.

The two integrated cores describe the distinct lysoPS and oxidized-PS reactions. Their process nodes already occur in the seed, and the A2-type MF is the refinement of row 1, not an ungrounded added process. GO:0046475 is used for the lysoPS reaction and GO:0006660 for the distinct oxidized-PS reaction; these are related processes attached to different catalytic units, not duplicate new annotations. MAG hydrolysis remains supported but contextual. This includes a physiological possibility, not merely an in-vitro artifact.

## Additional primary sources and species boundaries

- [PMID:20797687, PMC2933347](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2933347/): primary identity/abstract confirms the human familial PHARC association. Its historical endocannabinoid framing predates the stronger lysoPS mechanism.
- [PMID:23297193, PMC3557017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3557017/): indexed Results/Figure 5 read using `"PMC3557017" "Table" "lyso"`. Mouse Abhd12 cDNA is expressed in HEK293T; knockout mouse brain demonstrates predominant lysoPS turnover and additional LPI hydrolysis. Bulk MAG/LPC/LPE hydrolysis was unchanged, which does not exclude recombinant capacity or local flux. The specific LPE panel result remains incompletely resolved here, so row 33 is UNDECIDED. The explicit LPI result supports row 37 as non-core.
- [PMID:25580854, PMC4301979](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4301979/): indexed Results were recovered with `"PMC4301979" "ABHD12" "lymphoblast" "extracellular"`. PHARC-subject lymphoblasts have reduced ABHD12/lysoPS-lipase activity and altered secreted lysoPS; mouse macrophage experiments establish the opposing ABHD16A-generation/ABHD12-degradation axis. Protease-protection and glycosylation comparisons support lumenal/extracellular orientation, not a soluble cytosolic catalytic domain. This does not by itself distinguish ER lumen from every possible cell-surface pool.
- [PMID:30420694, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30420694/): verified primary abstract reports selective DO264-dependent lysoPS elevation in mouse brain and primary human macrophages. Infection and hearing comparisons are mouse results. These support context-dependent inflammatory regulation; exact assay-level selectivity details were not newly verified from full text.
- [PMID:33571455, PMC7611549](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7611549/): indexed full Results/Table 1/Figure 1 and 6 descriptions were read through title and `"PMC7611549" "HEK293T" "human ABHD12"` searches. Human enzyme in HEK293T membranes prefers very-long-chain synthetic R-Me-lysoPS substrates. These are methyl esters; carboxylesterase conversion makes them useful surrogates/prodrugs, but they should not be silently described as unmodified natural substrates. Mouse knockout tissue and mast-cell experiments, and human THP1 lipid treatments, are separate scopes.
- [PMID:41983263, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41983263/): the 2026 primary abstract reports activity profiling, DO264-induced 2-AG elevation/arachidonate decrease and distinct CB2R/PGE2 cytokine branches in microglial states. The full construct/species details were not inspected. It makes a blanket claim of no physiological MAG role inappropriate; it does not establish equivalent flux in every human tissue.
- [PMID:40496808, published PMC12150055](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12150055/): verified published identity and abstract report neuronal ABHD12 expression protecting nerves in a sarmopathy model. This is considered as neuroimmune context and a question about lipid mediators, not proof of human native-PS hydrolysis or a new axon-maintenance GO assertion. The published article is used rather than creating a separate citation to its earlier preprint.

## Neuronal ortholog source audit

The [MGI comparative source graph](https://www.informatics.jax.org/homology/GOGraph/Abhd12) links mouse Q99LR1 to PMID:27307232 for protein depalmitoylation, cytoplasm and dendrite cytoplasm, and to PMID:22632720 for AMPA-complex membership.

[PMID:27307232, PMC5015780](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5015780/) was recovered as indexed original Methods/Results with the title and ABHD12, and `"PMC5015780" "Figure 3" "ABHD12"`. Mouse Abhd12 NM_024465 is explicitly in the cDNA screen. Figure 1 measures reduced PSD-95 palmitate labeling after ABHD12 coexpression in HEK293T, with a smaller effect than ABHD17. Figure 3A compares FLAG-tagged proteins in rat hippocampal neurons. The detailed neuronal catalytic-mutant, endogenous-substrate and knockdown work concerns ABHD17; it should not be borrowed for ABHD12. Thus the protein-hydrolase and dendritic assertions are retained as bounded secondary annotations, while the broad cytoplasm term remains true for the established intracellular membrane pool. Cytoplasm is not a synonym for soluble cytosol. No source failure is inferred from the ABHD17-centered title.

[PMID:22632720](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22632720/) was subsequently recovered as the [original institutional PDF](https://www.sfb746.uni-freiburg.de/Publications/Schulte/17_ampar-proteome_neuron2012.pdf), also available from the [Fakler laboratory](https://www.sfb746.uni-freiburg.de/Publications/Fakler/schwenk-2012.pdf). Table 1 (journal page 624) explicitly identifies the mouse protein accession Q99LR1/ABHD12. Results page 622 describe total-brain membrane preparations from adult rats, wild-type mice and antibody-target-knockout mice, with multiple anti-GluA antibodies and IgG controls. Figure 1B distinguishes rat-membrane antibody/IgG comparisons from mouse wild-type/knockout comparisons. The protein identifier does not establish the species of every preparation. The donor evidence now resolves row 17 as non-core complex membership. No human receptor-gating function is inferred.

## Ontology, propagation and curated pathway checks

Live [GO:0120560](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0120560) specifies hydrolysis of 1-acyl-sn-glycero-3-phospho-L-serine; [GO:0004622](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0004622) instead specifies phosphocholine. [GO:0004623](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0004623) is A2-type glycerophospholipase activity, cleaving the sn-2 acyl ester. The [GO:0046475 definition](https://www.informatics.jax.org/vocab/gene_ontology/GO:0046475) includes glycerophosphate derivatives with at least one acyl/alkyl/alkenyl group, so lysoPS qualifies. [GO:0008474](https://amigo.geneontology.org/amigo/term/GO:0008474) is a protein-palmitate hydrolysis activity, distinct from lipid ester hydrolysis.

The seeded GO:0098734 label is obsolete. AmiGO still displayed its August 6 snapshot, but the later official [obsoletion notice 1174](https://github.com/geneontology/go-announcements/issues/1174) and [closed ontology issue 32290](https://github.com/geneontology/go-ontology/issues/32290) explain that this is molecular function, not a separate BP. The source term is preserved and the inferred BP row removed; the underlying GO:0008474 is retained separately. Direct QuickGO API access failed; that failure is not claimed as an ontology result.

All PAINT rows preserve PTN000957701 and record only that node in the structured propagation assessment. The target's appearance among descendants is legitimate self-evidence, not circular. The imported PTHR12277 inventory places human Q8N2K0 and mouse Q99LR1 in SF61 and ABHD12B in SF69; no source node tree/MSA was reconstructed or invented from this inventory. No Q8N2K0/ABHD12 hit was found in the local GO-CAM index. That absence was not used to propose a process. Existing broad membrane/ER assertions retain their original evidence resolution.

Cached [R-HSA-5694462](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5694462) and [R-HSA-426048](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-426048) describe the MAG-to-arachidonate reaction and its parent pathway. Its compartment is retained as curated pathway context; the more direct ER evidence is the core location. No new Reactome record was authored or substituted.
