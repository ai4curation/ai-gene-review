# CAV1: membrane structure, partner organization and enzyme regulation

Caveolin-1 contributes physical structure to caveolae and organizes selected membrane-associated proteins. These functions are related but distinguishable. Integral protomer contacts stabilize the caveolin oligomer; transient recruitment of channels and signaling proteins is a separate adaptor function. Direct nitric-oxide synthase inhibition supplies a third, experimentally defined molecular activity. Neither channel conduction nor the catalytic activities of an associated kinase, ATPase, protease or ubiquitin ligase belong to CAV1 merely because it binds that protein.

## The structural membrane component

Human HeLa caveolar-coat isolation, cross-linking and imaging place CAV1 and CAV2 with cavin proteins around caveolar bulbs. The isolated coat is detergent sensitive without stabilization. Cavin ratios and sedimentation behavior describe the preparation, not an invariant composition of every native caveola. EHD2 and pacsin2 were excluded under the reported purification conditions; this is not a universal negative interaction experiment. Selected original Results, captions and Methods were read, but original figure pixels and every supplementary table were not reanalyzed (PMID:24013648).

The 2022 cryo-EM study adds direct evidence for CAV1's own structural work. Tagged, sequence-verified human CAV1 expressed in E. coli and purified in detergent forms an 8S disc of eleven tightly associated protomers. Refinements using alternative symmetries did not improve the map. Contacts extend across oligomerization, intramembrane and pin regions; Arg54Ala disrupts the 8S assembly. The first 48 residues are unresolved in the model, not deleted from the full-length experimental protein. The beta-barrel is on the cytoplasmic side and is not evidence of ion-channel activity. A native eleven-subunit rule, intact-cell molecular packing and physiological modification/partner states remain unestablished by this bacterial preparation (PMID:35544577).

The 2025 study independently tests membrane insertion. Purified human CAV1 fusion protein is surface active in monolayers and incorporates into preformed liposomes after outer-leaflet lipid extraction. Soluble StayGold, peripheral SNAP25 and transmembrane Syntaxin1A controls behave differently. Coflotation and vesicle-size measurements support replacement of membrane-leaflet material by the CAV1 assembly. In contrast, cholesterol counts beneath the disc, proposed lipid movement through the barrel, changes in scaffolding-domain exposure and most curvature analyses arise from coarse-grained or atomistic simulations. The authors explicitly cannot conclusively attribute bilayer-shape changes to CAV1 because control simulated membranes also undulate. Reconstitution, modeled lipid behavior and a directly demonstrated native bending mechanism must therefore remain separate (PMID:40359049).

GO:0140378 protein complex scaffold activity captures the integral structural contribution in the core synthesis. The official comment on GO:0030674 distinguishes such structural scaffolds from adaptors that bring separate macromolecules into coordinated contact. This is not a substitution of a signaling term merely because both are colloquially called scaffolds. Same-role annotation checks for CAVIN1, CLTC and BIN1 did not recover a complete current exact-term comparison; partial or failed displays do not establish systematic absence. CLTC has structural-molecule precedent, while the PomBase Ent1/Rtn1 membrane-bending precedents concern different physical mechanisms. The precise current definition of GO:0180020 was not fully retrieved. No new membrane-bending annotation is proposed. The source-annotation representation of the structural scaffold is raised as a question rather than inventing a NEW row from presumed curator omission.

## Recruitment and regulation of partners

Human HMEC/HEK293 experiments and separate mouse lung endothelial knockout/rescue experiments connect CAV1 to TRPC1, IP3R3 and calcium entry. A CAV1 scaffolding-domain deletion weakens particular associations yet increases entry under the tested conditions. Human salivary-cell experiments show that CAV1 retains TRPC1 at the surface, while STIM1-dependent rearrangement activates the channel. Excess CAV1 can inhibit the response when STIM1 availability is limiting. These data establish coordinated retention and recruitment, not universal activation or absolute bridge necessity. CAV1 does organizing work; TRPC1 conducts ions and STIM1 senses stores (PMID:19052258; PMID:19897728).

The adaptor core uses GO:0030674 and remains separate from the integral coat scaffold. The three inherited/broad molecular-adaptor assertions are refined to this existing more specific activity. This does not assert a failure in the uninspected PAINT ancestral node. Channel-binding refinements preserve the original channel partner rather than assigning channel activity. Tyr14-phosphorylated CAV1 can bind purified PLCG1/VAV2 SH2 domains, supporting SH2-domain-binding refinements. CAV1's own Tyr14 phosphorylation does not make it a kinase. PTP1B association and substrate trapping support phosphatase binding while retaining CAV1 as the substrate (PMID:20808760; PMID:12176037; PMID:16388599).

Generic protein-binding annotations are retained as non-core when a specific association is supported, consistent with the project's standing instruction. A co-IP or curated interaction edge does not establish direct binary contact, a fixed complex or a downstream biological mechanism. Source partners and product identifiers remain exact. The PAK1 row remains unresolved because the original reagent-versus-partner distinction was not established. The multi-partner cavin/EHD2 row remains unresolved because positive EHD2 evidence was not recovered. The prostacyclin-synthase enzyme-binding row remains unresolved: its cited review's abstract does not expose the target experiment and no independent exact-edge evidence was established. These are source-access limits, not title-based rejection or paralog-misattribution claims.

Selected binding-source primary controls support PAR1, FAS/LC3B, FAS/BID, VCP/UBXD1, MCL1, coat components, COX2, ABCG1, RAC1, NPC1 and RSV matrix associations. Important limits include rat VCP constructs in human-cell experiments, murine partners explicitly marked as cross-species in UniProt, and RSV infectivity effects demonstrated for CAV2 rather than automatically for CAV1. Original high-throughput target tables remain uninspected where noted; the corresponding reference assessments remain UNVERIFIED even when independent same-edge evidence supports retention. No blanket conversion of all screen associations into direct binding was made.

## Direct enzyme inhibition

The 1997 NOS study combines GST-CAV1 binding, control fragments and peptides, purified NOS inhibition and full-length cellular coexpression. Methods distinguish bovine eNOS, rat nNOS and human iNOS; the CAV1 clone's exact species was not independently traced. The physiological meaning of iNOS peptide inhibition remains more limited than the cellular eNOS result. The 1998 study shows reductase-dependent inhibition, calmodulin reversibility and differential effects on cytochrome-c versus ferricyanide reduction. It argues against reducing all inhibition to one oxygenase motif or a simple competition narrative. These primary results support NOS inhibitor activity, with no universal claim for every native NOS isoform or an atomic membrane-bound complex (PMID:9325253; PMID:9712842).

Human CAV1-GFP and endothelial experiments link CAV1/Src association to phosphorylation-dependent regulation. C156S changes oligomerization and location as well as association; it is not a clean test of a single binding interface. Src activity is inferred from phosphorylation readouts in this source, not purified enzyme kinetics. Mouse-null endothelial rescue is separately identified (PMID:25006397). NOS inhibition is a core activity; additional partner-specific kinase/channel regulatory outcomes remain non-core rather than being multiplied into many redundant core units.

## Locations and contextual processes

Caveolae and the plasma membrane are central locations. Golgi, ER, endosomal, vesicular and focal-adhesion pools are retained as trafficking or contextual locations when independently supported. Cytoplasm is broader than soluble cytosol. The actual Dsg2 study shows CAV1 at HaCaT cell borders and relative intracellular redistribution, not a generic fluorescence atlas (PMID:26918609). The Ecm29 paper uses caveolin-marked endosomes as a comparison population largely lacking Ecm29/proteasomes; it is not the separate VCP-dependent degradation study (PMID:20682791). MLC1, not PACSIN2, is the partner in the human/rat astrocyte trafficking paper; its abstract foregrounds MLC1 localization, so exact CAV1 marker panels are not claimed (PMID:19931615).

The complete Reactome summaries were read. Thyroid-reaction participants do not make CAV1 a peroxidase, oxidase or hormone-synthetic enzyme. FOXO-mediated expression makes CAV1 the expression product, not the transcription factor. The two lipid-droplet location assertions are not resolved by the cached CAV1/eNOS event summaries and remain uncertain. Production GO-CAM 645d887900001414 explicitly models CAV1 protein sequestering with TLR4 as input, based on the same mouse-to-human evidence as the source assertion. This agrees with retaining that particular context and does not imply generic sequestration of every partner (PMID:19265160).

Mouse HC11 and mammary-gland experiments provide independent support for the existing mammary-development, negative JAK/STAT and negative MAPK regulatory contexts. Human hTERT-HME1 experiments show that combined estrogen/progesterone increases CAV1, estrogen alone does not, and progesterone alone mildly lowers it. Involution remains uncertain: sustained STAT5 phosphorylation after weaning did not demonstrate a corresponding prolonged-lactation morphology. These selected external primary Results are distinct from the abstract-only canonical cache (PMID:12388746).

Other unresolved donor assertions require the original experiment, cell type and endpoint, particularly specific developmental, ciliary, metabolic, necroptotic and cardiac-electrophysiology processes. A donor list is not a PAINT tree; the target appearing among descendants is not circular evidence. No node-loss mechanism, new taxon restriction or donor-count failure was invented. The peptidase-activator lead is especially sensitive to whole-heart versus raft-localized activity and peptide inhibition; it is not resolved by a general caveolin/MMP association. The source objects are retained intact with explicit uncertainty.

## Human genetics and product boundaries

The normal reference for recessive generalized lipodystrophy reports one homozygous p.Glu38X proband among four screened and loss of fibroblast CAV1. Its exact abstract anchor is “ablates CAV1 expression in skin
fibroblasts” (PMID:18211975). Both initiation products are affected. Full primary images and detailed Methods were not recovered.

The dominant partial-lipodystrophy report describes two unusual heterozygous variants in a limited series/families. The -88delC variant is in the 5-prime UTR, with a proposed translational effect, not an experimentally demonstrated truncation in that study. The full selected Results/Methods do not establish a universal dominant loss-of-function mechanism (PMID:18237401). The PAH study supplies familial segregation, a de novo frameshift, unaffected carriers and reduced patient fibroblast/lung CAV1. Reduced abundance and predicted C-terminal anchoring defects are distinguished; penetrance is incomplete and mouse-null findings are separate from the human patient experiments (PMID:22474227).

The frozen project associations remain exactly four: recessive generalized lipodystrophy and dominant PAH are Definitive; dominant lipodystrophy and dominant ALS are Limited. This review does not upgrade the limited associations or infer a GO process from the disease name. The two UniProt products remain Q03135-1 and Q03135-2 with the original VSP_018692 mapping. The beta product begins downstream through alternative initiation, not an inferred splice event. The available source studies do not establish that every described mechanism applies equally to both products.

## Follow-up: trafficking scope and evidence anchors

The membrane-raft location is now explicit in the partner-adaptor core. Independent human membrane-domain observations support that location, including CAV1/BENE redistribution and VEGFR2-associated fractions (PMID:11294831; PMID:23262137). Raft fractionation is not a universal morphological identification of caveolae. Broad vesicle organization is retained as non-core; the structural core continues to identify the more informative coat-assembly work. GO:0016050 covers vesicle assembly, arrangement and disassembly, whereas GO:0072584 specifically describes uptake into caveolae followed by carrier formation. No new scission activity is inferred.

The previous PMID:19931615 access limit is superseded by selected primary Methods, Results and captions from the [author-hosted paper reproduction](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/40025044_MLC1_trafficking_and_membrane_expression_in_astrocytes_Role_of_caveolin-1_and_phosphorylation). Fig. 8 compares CAV1-GFP with GFP in human U251 cells: “decrease in the expression of the 36-kDa MLC1 component”. This target perturbation supports context-dependent non-core retention, with inhibitory overexpression effects distinguished from direct uptake/scission measurement. Rat association experiments remain separate. Image pixels and supplements were not inspected; the canonical cache is still abstract-only.

Lipid storage and metabolic chemistry are different endpoints. The current GO:0019915 definition concerns accumulation and maintenance of lipid reserves. Human CAV1-deficiency lipodystrophy and CAV1-containing human adipocyte caveolar preparations corroborate the broad physiological storage context (PMID:18211975; PMID:15219854). They do not independently establish a particular triglyceride-conversion step or regulation of fatty-acid chemical metabolism. The existing mouse storage transfer is retained non-core with explicit curator deference, while the exact donor metabolic endpoints remain undecided. This is neither a claim that CAV1 synthesizes lipids nor a newly proposed process based on disease necessity alone.

All fifteen existing MODIFY decisions now have exact cached text anchors. The PLCG1/VAV2 refinements use a recombinant SH2-domain array, phosphopeptide pull-downs from lysates and cellular co-IP of full-length or SH3-SH2-SH3 VAV2; the assays are not conflated into a purified single-domain pull-down. CAV1 supplies the phosphorylated ligand, not the partner's catalytic activity (PMID:20808760). PTP1B, channel-binding, RAC1 and adaptor refinements likewise preserve the original partner and biological direction. Additional compact anchors document selected experimental locations, assembly and NOS inhibition. Repeated assertions still cite the same independent localization evidence where their original target panel is uninspected; a short fragment from an unrelated partner assay is not manufactured to fill every row. The quote audit counts every supporting-text occurrence plus notes, retaining at most 25 words from any source, and no action is changed to satisfy a quotation quota.

Peptidase-activator assertions remain undecided. Retention of a protease in a membrane compartment, whole-heart activity and local raft activity are different observations; without the original activity/localization controls they do not demonstrate, or disprove, an intrinsic CAV1-dependent increase in peptidase catalysis. Similarly, the hERG result supports a broad repolarizing-current context under curator deference but leaves native contraction-coupled cardiac action potentials unresolved. The description names CAV2 explicitly, and the electronic raft row now correctly identifies its mouse donor separately from the neighboring bovine ISS row.
