It recognizes non-phosphorylated FGFR juxtamembrane sequences, enabling constitutive receptor association, and can also recognize phosphorylated NPXY-containing peptides from receptors such as TRKA, TRKB, and RET. This domain therefore couples membrane receptors to the FRS2 signaling scaffold and is a candidate site for direct small-molecule inhibition. | (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000007, pqac-00000046, pqac-00000055, pqac-00000056) |
| C-terminal intrinsically disordered tail with tyrosine-phosphorylation sites | Extended C-terminal region, approximately 400 amino acids | FGFR-mediated phosphorylation converts this tail into a multivalent docking platform. Phosphotyrosines recruit PTPN11/SHP2 and GRB2—Y346 is reported as an SHP2-binding site, whereas Y196, Y306, Y349, and Y392 contribute to GRB2-associated complexes—thereby linking receptors to GRB2–SOS–RAS–MAPK and GAB1–PI3K–AKT signaling. The tail also integrates negative feedback through ERK-dependent threonine phosphorylation and CBL-associated ubiquitination. | (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000029, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000046) |
| Cys61–Cys80 disulfide bridge within the PTB domain | Between Cys61 and Cys80 in the PTB-domain fold | Emerging structural and biophysical evidence indicates that this intradomain bridge stabilizes the native PTB structure and reshapes its folding and ligand-recognition energy landscape. Reduction lowers unfolding free energy by approximately 3 kcal/mol, shifts the unfolding midpoint from about 2.5 M to 0.9 M guanidinium chloride, and markedly weakens FGFR1-peptide affinity, suggesting possible redox-sensitive regulation. Because these findings are recent and derive from biochemical PTB-domain studies, their physiological relevance in intact human cells remains to be established. | (pqac-00000047, pqac-00000048, pqac-00000049, pqac-00000052) |
