| Kinase class and domain | Dual-specificity MAPK kinase in the STE kinase group, containing a conserved protein-kinase catalytic domain and ATP-binding machinery. “Dual specificity” denotes phosphorylation of both threonine and tyrosine in a MAPK activation loop. | **High for class/domain:** curated sequence annotation; **moderate-to-high for mechanism:** established MAPK-cascade biochemistry. | UniProt P46734; Roux & Blenis, 2004-06 (pqac-00000001) |
| Catalytic reaction | Transfers phosphate from ATP to threonine and tyrosine in a cognate p38 MAPK activation loop: **ATP + p38 → ADP + dual-phosphorylated p38**. | **High:** biochemical consensus and phospho-specific experiments; cited evidence does not supply detailed kinetic constants. | Roux & Blenis, 2004-06; Maik-Rachline et al., 2021-12 (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002) |
| MKK3 activation residue | **Thr222 (T222)** phosphorylation is used experimentally as a marker of activated human MEK3/MKK3. Leukemia-associated mutant constructs showed reduced pT222 and reduced downstream p38 phosphorylation. This assay supports an activation-associated site but does not define every regulatory event. | **Moderate-to-high:** phospho-specific immunoblotting of expressed proteins in HEK293 cells rather than purified-enzyme kinetics. | Ayala-Marin et al., 2021-11 (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000012) |
| Principal substrates | Best-supported substrates are **p38-family MAPKs**. MKK3 and MKK6 phosphorylate the conserved **TGY** motif; p38α/MAPK14 is activated at **Thr180 and Tyr182**. MKK3 can also activate p38β, but context-dependent preferences do not justify strict isoform exclusivity. | **High for p38-family specificity and p38α T180/Y182; moderate for individual isoform preferences.** | Maik-Rachline et al., 2021-12; Morales-Martínez & Vega, 2024-01 (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003) |
| Upstream activation | MKK3 lies below MAP3Ks including MEKK1–4, MLK2/3, DLK, ASK1, Tpl2/Cot and TAK1. Inputs include oxidative stress, UV radiation, hypoxia, ischemia and inflammatory cytokines such as IL-1 and TNF-α. Exact wiring varies by stimulus and cell type. | **High at pathway level; variable for particular MAP3K–MKK3 links in individual tissues.** | Roux & Blenis, 2004-06 (pqac-00000001) |
| Subcellular localization | MKK3 has been observed in **both cytoplasm and nucleus**; cytoplasmic predominance occurs in some tumor-cell contexts. Localization is dynamic and context dependent, not confined to one compartment. | **Moderate-to-high:** cellular localization studies plus context-specific tumor immunohistochemistry. | Ben-Levy et al., 1998-09; Pu et al., 2024-06 (pqac-00000007) |
| Core biological role | Serves as the middle tier of a stress-responsive **MAP3K → MKK3 → p38** cascade, converting stress and cytokine inputs into p38 activation. Downstream outcomes include stress adaptation, inflammatory signaling, differentiation, apoptosis and proliferation control, depending on cell context and signal dynamics. | **High for pathway position; moderate for individual phenotypic outcomes because they are context dependent.** | Roux & Blenis, 2004-06; Maik-Rachline et al., 2021-12 (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002) |
| 2024 glioma finding | Retrospective multi-cohort analyses associated higher MAP2K3 expression with higher glioma grade and shorter overall and progression-free survival; reported 1-, 3- and 5-year prognostic AUCs were **0.89, 0.92 and 0.92**. High expression also correlated with immune infiltration and checkpoint expression. These findings do not prove causality or prospective treatment prediction. | **Exploratory/moderate:** transcriptomic, immunohistochemical and computational analyses with limited functional assays; no prospective clinical validation. | Pu et al., 2024-06 (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000007, pqac-00000010) |
| Development status and limitations | MAP2K3 is a plausible research target and candidate biomarker, but evidence remains predominantly preclinical. No MAP2K3-selective approved therapy or validated companion diagnostic is established in the reviewed evidence. MKK6 redundancy, context-dependent tumor effects and uncertain p38-isoform preferences complicate translation. | **Low clinical maturity:** mechanistic and association evidence without demonstrated direct clinical utility; database disease associations are not proof of causality. | Open Targets, accessed 2026-09-24; Morales-Martínez & Vega, 2024-01; Pu et al., 2024-06 (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000005) |
