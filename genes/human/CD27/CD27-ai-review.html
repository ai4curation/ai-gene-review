Gene Description

CD27 (TNFRSF7) is a type I transmembrane costimulatory receptor of the tumor necrosis factor receptor superfamily. Its extracellular region recognizes CD70, while its short cytoplasmic tail couples ligand engagement to intracellular signaling partners including TRAF2. Cell-surface CD27 supports T-cell activation and expansion together with antigen-receptor signals, and contributes to context-dependent B-cell differentiation, antibody responses, survival and T-cell memory programs. Soluble CD27 also occurs extracellularly. Recessive loss of human CD27 impairs immune control of Epstein–Barr virus and causes lymphoproliferative syndrome 2.
