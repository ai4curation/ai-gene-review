CC   -!- FUNCTION: Costimulatory immune-checkpoint receptor expressed at the
CC       surface of T-cells, NK-cells and B-cells which binds to and is
CC       activated by its ligand CD70/CD27L expressed by B-cells
CC       (PubMed:28011863). The CD70-CD27 signaling pathway mediates antigen-
CC       specific T-cell activation and expansion which in turn provides immune
CC       surveillance of B-cells (PubMed:28011863). Mechanistically, CD70
CC       ligation activates the TRAF2-PTPN6 axis that subsequently inhibits LCK
CC       phosphorylation to promote phenotypic and transcriptional adaptations
CC       of T-cell memory (PubMed:38354704). In addition, activation by CD70 on
CC       early progenitor cells provides a negative feedback signal to leukocyte
CC       differentiation during immune activation and thus modulates
CC       hematopoiesis (By similarity). Negatively regulates the function of Th2
CC       lymphocytes in the adipose tissue (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P41272, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28011863,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38354704}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:1655907, PubMed:34419446). Interacts with

CC       PubMed:9692890). Interacts ith PTPN6 (PubMed:38354704).
