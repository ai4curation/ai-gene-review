# COL3A1 review notes

COL3A1 encodes the alpha-1(III) chain of fibrillar type-III collagen.

## Structural and ligand functions

Two molecular cores capture distinct performed work. The structural core is GO:0030020, with collagen type III trimer, extracellular matrix and collagen fibril organization. The current GO comment explicitly accommodates matrix collagens. Human COL3A1 variant studies distinguish secretion, chain association and matrix incorporation, and report altered dermal fibril architecture (PMID:7487954; PMID:9036918). The structural peptide study examines a synthetic native-sequence human C-terminal segment, with alternative cystine-knot conformations, rather than a uniquely resolved full physiological molecule (PMID:18805790). Actual mouse cartilage/meniscus experiments link collagen III to fibril architecture and tissue biomechanics (PMID:31655293). A force assay on an isolated human chain is not claimed. Collagen supplies matrix structure; modifying enzymes and procollagen peptidases perform its chemical processing.

The ligand core is GO:0048018. Original human acid-soluble collagen III binds LAIR1 with receptor and blocking controls, activates a receptor reporter and causes ITIM-dependent inhibition of degranulation (PMID:16754721). The functional receptor step supports refinement of the existing generic pair, without adding a redundant NEW. Complementary mouse GPR56/ADGRG1 experiments demonstrate reciprocal interaction, receptor-dependent inhibition of neuronal migration and RhoA activation (PMID:21768377). The commercial collagen preparation is not assigned a species/catalogue absent from the inspected Methods. Human Fc tagging does not identify the species of the fused receptor. Mouse pial-membrane and cortical results support developmental context, not another direct human affinity assay (PMID:22235340). The 2017 human clinical source retains its positive curated IPI mapping with its unavailable binding-method limitation (PMID:28258187).

## Binding and product scope

Protease, integrin, PDGF and SMAD binding remain supported secondary capabilities. The original SMAD source genuinely reports mouse fetal-orofacial protein interactions using Y2H and GST-MH2 pull-downs; it is not merely a promoter-binding study (PMID:14559231). VWF is an adhesion molecule that bridges collagen matrix to platelet receptors, allowing informative class refinements for two positive pairs (PMID:16912226; PMID:2839553). The latter title does not negate collagen binding: its point is the importance of an additional VWF binding site in subendothelial adhesion.

Aegyptin binding remains a positive non-core interaction. Two exact MINT records map COL3A1 and mosquito O01949; original experiments support collagen/peptide recognition (PMID:20015075). The soluble collagen reagent species is unspecified in the inspected Methods, while the mixed Horm fibrils used elsewhere are horse tendon. Neither aegyptin's inhibitory pharmacology nor MMP12's proteolysis becomes a collagen activity. No exact export match for another source pair is not evidence that the primary positive result is false.

Informative classes are used when the actual source and partner identity support them; native compartment assumptions do not erase a supported interaction.

Official P02461-1 has 1466 residues. P02461-2 has 1163 residues and deletes displayed residues 847–1149. Both reconstruct exactly from the current official sequence/VAR_SEQ data. This does not establish equal activity. Displayed precursor/mature-chain boundaries and the shorter product's behavior are kept distinct, and none of the original annotations has been silently relabeled to an isoform.

## Source-specific unresolved processes

The covalent peptide-cross-linking row is UNDECIDED. Its source establishes receptor clustering, whereas GO:0018149 requires covalent links between protein chains. Collagen III does have interchain covalent links; structural and tissue evidence support that architecture. The uncertainty is the exact process/citation allocation, not a blanket biological denial. Biosensor immobilization is not a collagen-catalyzed reaction (PMID:16754721; PMID:18805790; PMID:31655293).

Several precise assertions remain unresolved after retaining the measured positive findings: chondrocyte differentiation and joint morphogenesis versus postnatal matrix mechanics; heart development versus adolescent coronary injury; TGF-beta pathway, cytokine/radiation/wound responses versus induced collagen abundance; a wound-healing marker versus a demonstrated repair step; and gene expression, TGF-beta production, fibroblast proliferation, elastic-fiber assembly and endochondral morphogenesis in a gain-of-function disease model. These decisions do not demand intrinsic catalysis or signaling from structural participants. They ask which step the product performs in the exact asserted process.

Tsk2 was initially an unidentified mouse mutation associated with collagen-promoter output, dermal cell counts and smaller bodies/femurs. The original available abstract describes examined lungs as normal (PMID:17960558). Later genetic/complementation work identifies a Col3a1 C33S gain-of-function allele (PMID:25330296). The positive phenotypes remain valid, but are not automatically normal reference-protein activities. Structural skin/growth context is retained; the exact lung, expression and several downstream process allocations remain unresolved. The radiation source uses a rat-tail collagen-I contraction lattice, separate from its measured human collagen-III secretion (PMID:14736764). The wound reporter measures promoter-driven EGFP, with post-translational timing limits (PMID:32979396). Angiotensin challenge establishes vascular susceptibility but does not yet resolve the precise response work performed by collagen (PMID:23630948).

Bone-marrow summary-table MGUS non-detection does not negate a positive individual MGUS sample in the detailed table. Proteomic RCA tensile assignments are collagen classifications corroborated by independent structure/mechanics, not claims that each study measured tensile force.

Some nominally full caches contain selected Introduction/Discussion rather than full Methods/Results. Exact target-table support remains unverified for the cardiac glycoproteomics and protocol-comparison sources (PMID:27559042; PMID:28675934); this is recorded in reference assessments. Failed XLSX downloads are HTML challenges and were not interpreted as spreadsheets. The positive location/class is retained from curated and independently supported collagen evidence without claiming an uninspected target table. Human cell-derived ECM Discussion explicitly identifies collagen III (PMID:28327460). The porcine cardiac source remains porcine and preserves its colocalizes_with qualifier (PMID:22261194).

A target in its own PAINT evidence is legitimate grounding. Historical PTN tree/MSA/IBD placements were not reconstructed; current experimental premises are not relabeled as historical-node proof. The positive aegyptin interaction is retained. Wound-healing sources receive different non-core versus unresolved judgments because the human contextual source and mouse deposition-marker experiment have different support.

## 2026-10-12 finite source and process reassessment

The original LAIR1 main Results, Figure 5 caption and Methods use cross-linking for receptor clustering, whereas current GO:0018149 requires covalent protein links. Row 59 is therefore removed as a source-specific term/assay mismatch. This does not negate covalent collagen-III network architecture. The existing ligand core and independent structural evidence remain unchanged.

Row 58 is marked over-annotated because mutant-induced downstream fibrotic regulation is not sufficient evidence for a normal gene-product-production role. The C33S-versus-wild-type comparison, equal transfection efficiencies and increased RNA/protein output are preserved. No categorical rule excludes extracellular proteins from regulatory participation. Rows 88, 102, 103 and 109 remain undecided for their specific production, proliferation, elastic-fibre and endochondral claims: a gain-of-function effect does not itself distinguish an amplified normal activity from mutant-specific pathology, and the earlier full Methods remain unrecovered. The later full PMID:25330296 was actually read; source access and biological interpretation are separate limits. The endochondral donor also contains an electronic-rule record, so its premise is not represented as only a femur-length measurement.

Row 95 is independent of Tsk2: PMID:31655293 examines postnatal cartilage/meniscus matrix organization and biomechanics in normal heterozygotes. The limb-joint annotation remains non-core. Collagen III performs structural work in the matrix being organized, and the morphogenesis definition does not require gross joint deformity. Age/sex limits and proposed versus measured mechanisms remain explicit.

The two VWF cell-adhesion-molecule-binding refinements remain. Current GO:0050839 does not impose its narrower child's cell-surface restriction; official VWF function describes the positive collagen-to-platelet adhesive bridge. Soluble location and absence of a previously found VWF-binding annotation are not disproof of the partner class. ADGRG1 binding is refined to receptor-ligand activity using the existing mouse functional experiments, preserving the original human IPI assay-access limit. Six short, nonduplicated primary anchors now make the key source statements checkable. The exact human vein Table 1 detection remains a NaCl-fraction localization result, not a tensile assay.

This dated assessment supersedes the affected earlier decisions.

## Source-specific response reassessment — 2026-10-12

The original human Tenon-fibroblast study measures collagen-III secretion after beta irradiation by conditioned-medium ELISA, normalized to cell number. Its functional contraction lattice uses rat-tail collagen I. The Discussion proposes post-radiation fibrosis as a possible interpretation, without testing collagen III's contribution to that response. The GO:0009314 assertion is therefore marked over-annotated at this source's production-assay scope, superseding the earlier unresolved allocation; the positive secretion result and separately supported wound-matrix role remain [PMID:14736764].

This is not a blanket exclusion of expression-derived response annotations. The GO manual-annotation guide allows contextual IEP use and asks curators to consider the gene product's known role; an abundance change alone does not automatically establish functional participation ([Biological process, expression studies](https://academic.oup.com/database/article/doi/10.1093/database/bat054/338636)). Full access permits the source-specific judgment here, but does not require every other uncertain process to become negative.

The wound-reporter allocation remains unresolved: actual mouse corneal injury produces Col3a1-associated signal and scar deposition, while the reporter tracks transcription and the treatments act through other cells or vesicles. Collagen's possible structural repair contribution is retained as a genuine interpretation question [PMID:32979396]. The separate chondrocyte-differentiation allocation also remains unresolved. Normal heterozygous mouse cartilage/meniscus experiments demonstrate matrix architecture and biomechanics during postnatal maturation, supporting the retained limb-joint organization judgment, but do not resolve chondroblast acquisition of specialized chondrocyte features [PMID:31655293]. Neither uncertainty is attributed to missing full text.

The VWF binding refinements remain supported at the parent definition's scope. A curator question now explicitly asks whether VWF's soluble adhesive-bridge role fits the intended cell-adhesion-molecule class; no unverified claim that all current annotations require a cell-surface partner is made.
