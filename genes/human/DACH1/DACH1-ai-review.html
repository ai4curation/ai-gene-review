Gene Description

DACH1 (Dachshund homolog 1) is a nuclear transcriptional regulator of the Ski/Sno/Dachshund family and the vertebrate ortholog of Drosophila dachshund. It has an N-terminal Dachbox-N (DD1) domain whose crystal structure shows a winged-helix/forkhead-like DNA-binding fold, and a C-terminal Dachbox-C coiled-coil. DACH1 acts mainly as a transcriptional corepressor: it binds NCoR/HDAC3 and SIN3A corepressor complexes, partners with SIX homeodomain factors (SIX1, SIX6) as a component of the conserved Six-Eya-Dach network that controls precursor proliferation in organogenesis, and represses TGF-beta/SMAD4, AP-1/c-Jun (cyclin D1) and FOX-dependent transcription. EYA phosphatase activity can switch SIX1-DACH complexes from repression to activation through coactivator (CBP) recruitment. DACH1 also binds chromatin and DNA directly through DD1 and represses genes carrying a DACH1-responsive element, recruiting TCERG1. It is expressed in developing eye, limb, kidney and brain, and is a tumor suppressor in breast, prostate and glioma contexts, where it restrains proliferation and tumor-initiating activity (e.g. by repressing cyclin D1 and FGF2).

Core Functions

DACH1 is a nuclear transcriptional corepressor: recruited by DNA-bound partners (SIX homeodomain factors, SMAD4, c-Jun) it brings NCoR/HDAC3 and SIN3A corepressor complexes to target promoters via its Dachbox-N (DS) domain, repressing genes such as cyclin D1, p27Kip1 and TGF-beta/SMAD targets; in the Six-Eya-Dach network EYA phosphatase activity can switch SIX1-DACH from repression to activation.

  • NCoR and SMAD4: PMID:14525983; PMID:14525983

DNA binding (secondary; evaluated carefully)

  • Structure: PMID:12057194
  • Biochemistry: PMID:12215533
  • Reporter/ChIP: PMID:20956529
  • Assessment: direct DNA binding is supported by three independent lines (structure, in vitro binding, DRE reporters + ChIP-seq), and the curators' IDA rows were made from full text, so DNA-binding TF annotations are accepted. However, naked-DNA affinity is reported as low and no high-resolution intrinsic motif exists; DACH1 ChIP peaks co-localize with FOX motifs, so some "sequence-specific" binding may reflect partner tethering. The corepressor function is therefore listed as the first core function, and DNA-binding repression as a second, more weakly characterised mode.

Tumor suppression

  • Glioma FGF2 repression PMID:21750150; breast cancer DNA synthesis/cyclin D1 PMID:16980615. Downstream proliferation/migration terms kept as non-core.

Module

Module annoton GO:0003712 (transcription coregulator activity) is consistent; this review's core MF is the more specific child GO:0003714 transcription corepressor activity (NEW), plus GO:0001227.
