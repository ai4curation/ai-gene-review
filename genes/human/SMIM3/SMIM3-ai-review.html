Gene Description

SMIM3 (small integral membrane protein 3; also NID67, NGF-induced differentiation clone 67) encodes a 60-residue protein with a single predicted central transmembrane helix and little else; it is conserved in mammals (rat and human about 85% identical). The gene was identified in rat PC12 cells as a primary-response gene induced by NGF and FGF, and also by forskolin, calcium ionophore and ATP; its mRNA is most abundant in heart, ovary and adrenal gland. In vitro translation confirms that the open reading frame yields a ~6 kDa product. No molecular activity, binding partner of functional consequence, or biological process has been established. Its similarity to other small single-helix membrane proteins that modulate ion channels led to the suggestion of a channel-regulatory role, which remains untested. In acute myeloid leukemia, SMIM3 mRNA is elevated and associated with poorer survival, and shRNA knockdown slowed growth of AML cell lines, but this has not been linked to a molecular mechanism. The mouse ortholog marks one end of the Cd74-Nid67 interval used to model the 5q- myelodysplastic syndrome, whose phenotype is attributed to Rps14 haploinsufficiency rather than to Nid67.

description: SMIM3 (small integral membrane protein 3; also NID67, NGF-induced differentiation clone 67)
  encodes a 60-residue protein with a single predicted central transmembrane helix and little else; it
  is conserved in mammals (rat and human about 85% identical). The gene was identified in rat PC12 cells
  as a primary-response gene induced by NGF and FGF, and also by forskolin, calcium ionophore and ATP;
  its mRNA is most abundant in heart, ovary and adrenal gland. In vitro translation confirms that the
  open reading frame yields a ~6 kDa product. No molecular activity, binding partner of functional consequence,
  or biological process has been established. Its similarity to other small single-helix membrane proteins
  that modulate ion channels led to the suggestion of a channel-regulatory role, which remains untested.
  In acute myeloid leukemia, SMIM3 mRNA is elevated and associated with poorer survival, and shRNA knockdown
  slowed growth of AML cell lines, but this has not been linked to a molecular mechanism. The mouse ortholog
  marks one end of the Cd74-Nid67 interval used to model the 5q- myelodysplastic syndrome, whose phenotype
  is attributed to Rps14 haploinsufficiency rather than to Nid67.
