CC   -!- FUNCTION: SNAREs (soluble N-ethylmaleimide-sensitive factor-attachment
CC       protein receptors) are essential proteins for intracellular membrane
CC       fusion. SNAREs localized on opposing membranes assemble to form a
CC       trans-SNARE complex, an extended, parallel four-helix bundle whose
CC       assembly releases energy that drives membrane fusion. The core SNARE
CC       complex typically consists of four alpha-helical domains, three from
CC       target membrane SNAREs (t-SNAREs) and one from a vesicle SNARE (v-
CC       SNARE) (PubMed:10036234, PubMed:24227526, PubMed:26771955,
CC       PubMed:28265073). Syntaxin-11/STX11 is an atypical lipid-anchored t-
CC       SNARE that forms a SNARE complex with the t-SNARE SNAP23 and the v-
CC       SNARE VAMP8. This SNARE complex plays a critical role in the regulated
CC       exocytosis of cytotoxic granules by natural killer (NK) cells and
CC       cytotoxic T-lymphocytes (PubMed:17525286, PubMed:19804848,
CC       PubMed:19884660, PubMed:24227526, PubMed:26771955, PubMed:28265073). In
CC       macrophages, STX11 regulates stimulus-dependent vesicular transport of
CC       TLR4 from recycling endosomes to the plasma membrane by cooperating
CC       with SNAP23, thereby playing a critical role in microbial component
CC       recognition and the induction of innate and adaptive immunity (By
CC       similarity). In skeletal muscle satellite cells, STX11 cooperates with
CC       its cognate SNAREs VAMP4 and SNAP23 to facilitate CD36 vesicular
CC       transport from endosomes to the plasma membrane, an essential step in
CC       muscle regeneration (By similarity). In another proposed mechanism,
CC       STX11 may function as a regulator of the SNARE VTI1B on late endosomes
CC       to regulate trafficking from late endosomes to lysosomes
CC       (PubMed:21388490). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9D3G5,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10036234, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17525286,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19804848, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19884660,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21388490, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24227526,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26771955, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28265073}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms a SNARE complex with VAMP4 and SNAP23; involved in
CC       vesicular transport from endosomes to the plasma membrane
CC       (PubMed:10036234). Forms a SNARE complex with VAMP8 and SNAP23; in
CC       activated cytotoxic T-lymphocytes (PubMed:28265073). Interacts with
CC       STXBP2; the interaction is direct and promotes the assembly of
CC       functional STX11/SNAP23/VAMP8 SNARE complexes mediating membrane fusion
CC       in cytoxic granule exocytosis (PubMed:19804848, PubMed:19884660,
CC       PubMed:24459464, PubMed:24910990, PubMed:26771955, PubMed:28265073). In
CC       vitro, forms a SNARE complex with the v-SNARE VAMP2 (PubMed:10036234).
CC       Interacts with VTI1B; the interaction is direct and could regulate the
CC       SNAP receptor activity of VTI1B on late endosomes (PubMed:21388490).
