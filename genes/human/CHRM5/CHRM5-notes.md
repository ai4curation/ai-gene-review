M5 normally favors phosphoinositide signaling, but native human A2058 melanoma cells expressing sequenced M5 exhibit approximately 50% inhibition of forskolin-stimulated cAMP (PMID:8663391). The inhibition is pertussis-toxin insensitive and extracellular-calcium dependent, so retain the non-core process without calling it a universal Gi mechanism. The phosphatidylinositol-dephosphorylation term is refined to the PLC-linked muscarinic process rather than assigning a lipid-phosphatase enzyme activity.

Mouse M5 knockout reduces carbachol-stimulated gastric acid/histamine secretion (PMID:15691866), independently supporting the gastric process. Whole-stomach versus isolated-mucosa expression cautions against a direct parietal-cell assignment. M3–M5 co-IP/BRET (PMID:20398705) supports GPCR-partner binding in transfected HEK293T cells without establishing obligatory native dimers. Retain curated neuronal and membrane locations without using peripheral or melanoma contexts as exclusions.
