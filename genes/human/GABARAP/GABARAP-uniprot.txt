CC   -!- FUNCTION: Ubiquitin-like modifier that plays a role in intracellular
CC       transport of GABA(A) receptors and its interaction with the
CC       cytoskeleton (PubMed:9892355). Involved in autophagy: while LC3s are
CC       involved in elongation of the phagophore membrane, the GABARAP/GATE-16
CC       subfamily is essential for a later stage in autophagosome maturation
CC       (PubMed:15169837, PubMed:20562859, PubMed:22948227, PubMed:34597140).
CC       Through its interaction with the reticulophagy receptor TEX264,
CC       participates in the remodeling of subdomains of the endoplasmic
CC       reticulum into autophagosomes upon nutrient stress, which then fuse
CC       with lysosomes for endoplasmic reticulum turnover (PubMed:31006538).
CC       Participates in selective ER-phagy through interaction with UBAC2,
CC       promoting recruitment of UBAC2-associated ER fragments to
CC       autophagosomes (PubMed:39284914). In addition to autophagy, also
CC       regulates lysosome biogenesis via interaction with FNIP1 (or FNIP2):
CC       interaction sequesters the FLCN-FNIP complex and prevents non-canonical
CC       mTORC1 signaling cascade activation, leading to activation of
