Gene Description

GSDME (DFNA5) is a gasdermin precursor whose N-terminal cleavage product forms membrane pores, executing pyroptotic cell death. Caspase-3 and granzyme B can activate it by cleavage after Asp270. The active fragment can also permeabilize mitochondria and amplify apoptotic signaling. Deafness-associated aberrant splicing produces toxic truncations distinct from the physiological cleavage product.

Core Functions

The activated N-terminal product forms membrane pores that execute pyroptotic cell death and provide a route for transmembrane transport. Mitochondrial permeabilization can amplify intrinsic apoptotic signaling.

GSDME review notes

Biological synthesis

In human CEM-C7 cells, GSDME-dependent mitochondrial permeabilization amplifies apoptosis; the mouse Apaf1 experiment is a separate test. PMID:30976076(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30976076/).

Granzyme B can directly activate the human protein; lytic outcome and total cell death are distinct readouts. PMID:32188940(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32188940/).

The familial deafness mutation alters splicing, whereas the physiological N-terminal product is released proteolytically. PMID:9771715(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9771715/).

The mutation-associated MAPK and cell-death observations remain contextual. They are not assigned to the basal precursor. PMID:26236191(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26236191/).

HEK293T mutant-versus-wild-type comparisons show JNK/ERK activation and partial rescue by SP600125. The signaling response belongs to this disease-construct experiment; the experiment does not establish a normal MAPK-regulator role. PMID:26236191(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26236191/).
