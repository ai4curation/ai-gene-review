CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required for mannose-6-phosphate-dependent trafficking of
CC       lysosomal enzymes (PubMed:36074821, PubMed:36074822, PubMed:36096887).
CC       LYSET bridges GlcNAc-1-phosphate transferase (GNPTAB), to the membrane-
CC       bound transcription factor site-1 protease (MBTPS1), thus allowing
CC       proteolytic activation of the GNPTAB. GNPTAB is involved in the
CC       regulation of M6P-dependent Golgi-to-lysosome trafficking of lysosomal
CC       enzymes (PubMed:36074821, PubMed:36074822, PubMed:36096887). LYSET is
CC       thus an essential factor for maturation and delivery of lysosomal
CC       hydrolases (PubMed:36074822). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36074821,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36074822, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36096887}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: (Microbial infection) Essential for infection by muliple
CC       viruses, including SARS-CoV-2, that utilize activated cathepsins for
CC       entry after M6P-dependent lysosomal transport.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36074821}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with GNPTAB; this interaction is important for
CC       proper localization of GNPTAB in Golgi stacks (PubMed:36074821,
CC       PubMed:36096887). Interacts with MBTPS1 (PubMed:36096887).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36074821, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36096887}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Golgi apparatus membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33252156, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36074821,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36096887}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000255}. Note=Colocalizes with GNPTAB and GNPTG in Golgi
CC       apparatus cisternae. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36074821}.
