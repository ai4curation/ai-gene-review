# DNASE2 (O00115) curation notes

No deep-research provider was available for this session; these notes were
assembled manually from cached primary literature (Europe PMC searches, all PMIDs
verified and cached with `ai-gene-review fetch-pmid`).

## Identity and biochemistry

- Human DNase II (DNase II alpha), 360 aa, signal peptide + lysosomal acid
  endonuclease; cDNA cloned in 1998 [PMID:9446563 "encoded a single polypeptide of 360
  amino acids (signal peptide, 16; propeptide, 91; mature protein, 253)"]
  [PMID:9812984 "The full-length human cDNA has now been cloned"].
- Expressed ubiquitously [PMID:9446563 "DNase II is ubiquitously expressed in human tissues"].
- Catalytic domain belongs to the phospholipase D superfamily (two HKD-like
  half-sites) [PMID:16150810 "DNase II exhibits a catalytic domain common to the
  phospholipase D superfamily"].
- Recombinant human wild-type DNase II digests plasmid DNA at pH 5; patient
  variants (G116A, D121V, exon-4 deletion) lose activity; patient fibroblasts lack
  activity, rescued by WT cDNA [PMID:29259162 "lysates of fibroblasts from F1:V-1 and
  F2:II-4 demonstrated a marked reduction of DNase II activity against the same substrate"].
- A 2024 report claims salivary/purified DNase II also digests RNA in mildly acidic
  conditions [PMID:39329709 "showing activity to digest both DNA and RNA effectively"].
  Single report; not adopted as a GO function here.

## Localization

- Lysosome; mouse DNase II processed (cathepsin L-dependent) and activated in
  lysosomes, detected in macrophage lysosomes [PMID:23516607 "These results indicate that
  DNase II is processed and activated in lysosomes"]. Note the abstract describes the
  mouse protein; GOA records an IDA on human O00115 from this paper.
- Porcine enzyme: processing in lysosomes but not needed for activation [PMID:19292869].
- Soluble luminal enzyme: signal peptide, no transmembrane segment, acid pH optimum,
  luminal processing -> lysosomal lumen (IC, added as NEW).
- Also secreted (saliva, PMID:39329709) and detected in urinary/prostatic exosome
  proteomics (PMID:23533145, HDA) - non-core.

## Biological roles (mostly mouse genetics)

- Digests DNA of engulfed apoptotic cells in macrophage lysosomes/phagolysosomes
  [PMID:12524536 "when apoptotic cells were phagocytosed by macrophages, their DNA was
  degraded by DNase II"].
- Digests nuclei expelled from erythroid precursors in fetal liver macrophages; Dnase2-/-
  mice die of anemia [PMID:11375492 "DNase II is indispensable for definitive
  erythropoiesis in mouse fetal liver"]; acts non-cell-autonomously from macrophages.
  The anemia is an interferon-driven consequence (rescued by Ifnar1/STING/cGAS loss,
  as reviewed in PMID:29259162), so DNASE2 is not itself an erythropoiesis participant.
- Undigested DNA -> cGAS/STING type I IFN and TNF-driven polyarthritis
  [PMID:17066036 "if macrophages cannot degrade mammalian DNA from erythroid precursors
  and apoptotic cells, they produce TNF-alpha"].
- Cell-autonomous lysosomal clearance of damaged nuclear DNA delivered by autophagy
  [PMID:25284779 "Removal of damaged DNA by Dnase2a required nuclear export and
  autophagy-mediated delivery of the DNA to lysosomes"]; mtDNA escaping autophagy in
  DNase II-deficient cardiomyocytes triggers TLR9 inflammation [PMID:22535248].
- Generates TLR9 ligands from CpG-A / bacterial DNA in endolysosomes of DCs
  [PMID:25600358 "TLR9 responds to DNA fragments generated by DNase II"].
- Efferocytosis-induced macrophage proliferation (mouse BMDM and in vivo): DNase II
  hydrolyses apoptotic-cell DNA in the phagolysosome to oligonucleotides that drive
  DNA-PKcs -> mTORC2 (RICTOR) -> MYC signalling [PMID:34784501 "the phagolysosomal enzyme
  that digests AC-DNA into oligonucleotides"; "Silencing Dnase2a resulted in decreased EdU
  incorporation into AC+ macrophages"]. DNase II provides the signal (substrate
  conversion); it is not a signal transducer. Not annotated as a NEW process here.

## Human disease

- Biallelic hypomorphic DNASE2 variants cause autoinflammatory-pancytopenia syndrome
  (type I interferonopathy) [PMID:29259162]; JAK inhibition used therapeutically
  [PMID:31775019, letter, no abstract in cache].

## Evolution

- DNase II family is an ancient eukaryotic trait associated with phagotrophy
  [PMID:18226927 "It probably evolved together with phagocytosis, specifically to
  facilitate DNA degradation and bacteriotrophy"]; C. elegans NUC-1 degrades
  apoptotic-cell DNA in the engulfing step [PMID:10716942].
