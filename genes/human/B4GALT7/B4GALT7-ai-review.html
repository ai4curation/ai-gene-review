Gene Description

B4GALT7 encodes a 327-residue, manganese-dependent type-II Golgi membrane glycosyltransferase of the GT7 family. Its lumen-facing catalytic domain transfers galactose from UDP-galactose to xylose attached to a serine of a proteoglycan core protein, forming a beta1,4 linkage. This first galactose is the second sugar of the shared tetrasaccharide linker used to assemble chondroitin sulfate, dermatan sulfate, heparan sulfate and heparin proteoglycans. Defective linker synthesis impairs proteoglycan glycanation and extracellular-matrix organization; biallelic pathogenic variants cause a spondylodysplastic Ehlers-Danlos syndrome.
