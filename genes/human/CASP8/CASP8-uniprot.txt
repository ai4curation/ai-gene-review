CC   -!- FUNCTION: Thiol protease that plays a key role in programmed cell death
CC       by acting as a molecular switch for apoptosis, necroptosis and
CC       pyroptosis, and is required to prevent tissue damage during embryonic
CC       development and adulthood (PubMed:23516580, PubMed:35338844,
CC       PubMed:35446120, PubMed:8681376, PubMed:8681377, PubMed:8962078,
CC       PubMed:9006941, PubMed:9184224). Initiator protease that induces
CC       extrinsic apoptosis by mediating cleavage and activation of effector
CC       caspases responsible for FAS/CD95-mediated and TNFRSF1A-induced cell
CC       death (PubMed:23516580, PubMed:35338844, PubMed:35446120,
CC       PubMed:8681376, PubMed:8681377, PubMed:8962078, PubMed:9006941,
CC       PubMed:9184224). Cleaves and activates effector caspases CASP3, CASP4,
CC       CASP6, CASP7, CASP9 and CASP10 (PubMed:16916640, PubMed:8962078,
CC       PubMed:9006941). Binding to the adapter molecule FADD recruits it to
CC       either receptor FAS/TNFRSF6 or TNFRSF1A (PubMed:8681376,
CC       PubMed:8681377). The resulting aggregate called the death-inducing
CC       signaling complex (DISC) performs CASP8 proteolytic activation
CC       (PubMed:9184224). The active dimeric enzyme is then liberated from the
CC       DISC and free to activate downstream apoptotic proteases
CC       (PubMed:9184224). Proteolytic fragments of the N-terminal propeptide
CC       (termed CAP3, CAP5 and CAP6) are likely retained in the DISC
CC       (PubMed:9184224). Also cleaves and activates BID, thereby promoting
CC       cytochrome C release from mitochrondria (By similarity). In addition to
CC       extrinsic apoptosis, also acts as a negative regulator of necroptosis:
CC       acts by cleaving RIPK1 at 'Asp-324', which is crucial to inhibit RIPK1
CC       kinase activity, limiting TNF-induced apoptosis, necroptosis and
CC       inflammatory response (PubMed:31827280, PubMed:31827281). Also able to
CC       initiate pyroptosis by mediating cleavage and activation of gasdermin-C
CC       and -D (GSDMC and GSDMD, respectively): gasdermin cleavage promotes
CC       release of the N-terminal moiety that binds to membranes and forms
CC       pores, triggering pyroptosis (PubMed:32929201, PubMed:34012073).
CC       Initiates pyroptosis following inactivation of MAP3K7/TAK1 (By
CC       similarity). Also acts as a regulator of innate immunity by mediating
CC       cleavage and inactivation of N4BP1 downstream of TLR3 or TLR4, thereby
CC       promoting cytokine production (By similarity). May participate in the
CC       Granzyme B (GZMB) cell death pathways (PubMed:8755496). Cleaves PARP1
CC       and PARP2 (PubMed:8681376). Independent of its protease activity,
CC       promotes cell migration following phosphorylation at Tyr-380
CC       (PubMed:18216014, PubMed:27109099). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O89110,
