CC   -!- FUNCTION: Regulator of STK3/MST2 and STK4/MST1 in the Hippo signaling
CC       pathway which plays a pivotal role in organ size control and tumor
CC       suppression by restricting proliferation and promoting apoptosis
CC       (PubMed:29063833). The core of this pathway is composed of a kinase
CC       cascade wherein STK3/MST2 and STK4/MST1, in complex with its regulatory
CC       protein SAV1, phosphorylates and activates LATS1/2 in complex with its
CC       regulatory protein MOB1, which in turn phosphorylates and inactivates
CC       YAP1 oncoprotein and WWTR1/TAZ. Phosphorylation of YAP1 by LATS1/2
CC       inhibits its translocation into the nucleus to regulate cellular genes
CC       important for cell proliferation, cell death, and cell migration. SAV1
CC       is required for STK3/MST2 and STK4/MST1 activation and promotes cell-
CC       cycle exit and terminal differentiation in developing epithelial
CC       tissues. Plays a role in centrosome disjunction by regulating the
CC       localization of NEK2 to centrosomes, and its ability to phosphorylate
CC       CROCC and CEP250. In conjunction with STK3/MST2, activates the
CC       transcriptional activity of ESR1 through the modulation of its
CC       phosphorylation. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16930133,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19212654, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21076410,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21104395, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29063833}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. Stabilized through interaction with STK3/MST2 or
CC       STK4/MST1 (PubMed:15688006, PubMed:16930133, PubMed:19212654,
CC       PubMed:28087714, PubMed:29063833). Interacts (via SARAH domain) with
CC       isoform 1 of NEK2 (PubMed:21076410). Interacts with ESR1 only in the
CC       presence of STK3/MST2 (PubMed:21104395). Interacts with WTIP and AJUBA
CC       (PubMed:20303269). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15688006,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16930133, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19212654,
