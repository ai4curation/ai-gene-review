# Research & Curation Notes: human ABI1 (UniProtKB:Q8IZP0)

## 1. Identity & Domain Architecture (`PTHR10460:SF2`)
- **Gene / Protein**: `ABI1` (Abl interactor 1; aliases `SSH3BP1`, `E3B1`, `Nap1BP`, `AblBP4`), canonical sequence `Q8IZP0-1` (508 aa, `sequence_version: 4`), PANTHER subfamily `PANTHER:PTHR10460:SF2` (`ABL INTERACTOR 1`).
- **Structural modules**:
  - **N-terminal WAVE-binding (WAB) / α-helical bundle domain** (~residues 18–79) overlapping a **t-SNARE coiled-coil homology / HHR region** (residues 45–107): mediates assembly of the heteropentameric WAVE regulatory complex (WRC; `GO:0031209` `SCAR complex`) by forming a trimeric four-helix bundle with the WHD/α1 helix of WAVE1/2/3 (`WASF1`/`WASF2`/`WASF3`) and BRK1/HSPC300 (`PMID:15048123`, `PMID:15143189`, `PMID:21107423`). Also mediates Syntaxin-1 (`STX1A`) and CaMKIIα binding (`PMID:15143189`, `PMID:22993434`) and contains `Tyr53` (phosphorylated by ABL1 during postsynapse-to-nucleus translocation; conserved with rat `Q9QZM5` `Tyr53`, `PMID:17304222`) and `Ser88` (phosphorylated by CaMKIIα, `PMID:22993434`).
  - **Central disordered proline-rich / PxxDY & phosphoregulatory region**:
    - `Tyr213` (`MOD_RES 213`, phosphorylated by ABL1) and proximal PXXP motif bind the Abl SH2 and SH3 domains in trans to allosterically regulate non-myristoylated c-Abl kinase (`PMID:18328268`) and engage the C-terminal SH2 domain of PI3K regulatory subunit p85 (`PIK3R1`) during macropinocytosis (`PMID:20598684`).
    - `Ser216` (`MOD_RES 216`) is phosphorylated by CDK1/cyclin B at mitotic entry to attenuate Abl-mediated WRC phosphorylation and F-actin assembly (`PMID:21900237`); both `Tyr213` and `Ser216` are dephosphorylated by PTEN to promote calpain-dependent ABI1 turnover during epithelial polarization (`PMID:32673396`).
    - Polyproline + `PxxDY` motifs mediate direct binding to the SH3 domain of EPS8 and EPS8-like proteins (`EPS8`, `EPS8L1`, `EPS8L2`, `EPS8L3`; `PMID:9010225`, `PMID:10499589`, `PMID:14565974`), erythroid α-spectrin (`SPTA1`; `PMID:9593709`), and the EVH1 domain of Mena (`ENAH`; `PMID:12672821`, `PMID:17101133`), while the inter-domain linker between the proline-rich region and SH3 domain is required to bridge c-Abl to WAVE2 (a feature shared with `ABI2` but absent in `ABI3`/NESH, `PMID:17101133`, `PMID:26428302`).
  - **C-terminal SH3 domain** (residues 446–505; conserved SH3-domain tyrosine `Tyr455` in 508-aa `Q8IZP0-1`, corresponding to `Tyr398` in shorter splice isoforms in `PMID:21320496`): binds proline-rich regions of c-Abl (`ABL1`), Arg (`ABL2`), SOS1 (`SOS1`) in the EPS8–ABI1–SOS1 Rac-GEF complex (`PMID:10499589`, `PMID:11003655`), N-WASP (`WASL`; `PMID:16155590`), TOCA-1 (`FNBP1L`; `PMID:19798448`), and postsynaptic scaffold ProSAP2/SHANK3 (`PMID:17304222`).
- **Alternative splicing**: UniProt `Q8IZP0` lists 12 splice isoforms. Exon 10 inclusion vs skipping (`PMID:20479892`) switches Rac1 binding and macropinocytosis regulation. Chromosomal translocation `t(10;11)(p11.2;q23)` fuses `KMT2A`/`MLL` to `ABI1` in acute myeloid leukemia (`PMID:9694699`).

## 2. Core Molecular & Cellular Functions
1. **Obligate Subunit of the WAVE Regulatory Complex (`GO:0031209` `SCAR complex`)**:
   - Directly binds WAVE1/WAVE2 (`WASF1`/`WASF2`), NCKAP1 (`NAP1`, or hematopoietic `NCKAP1L`/`HEM1`), CYFIP1/2 (`SRA1`/`PIR121`), and BRK1 (`HSPC300`) (`PMID:15048123`, `PMID:16417406`, `PMID:21107423`, `PMID:26428302`).
   - Required for WRC stability, Rac1-dependent translocation to the leading edge / lamellipodia and filopodia tips (`GO:0030027`, `GO:0032433`; `PMID:11516653`, `PMID:15048123`, `PMID:21482783`), and Arp2/3-mediated branched actin filament assembly.
2. **Signaling Adaptor & Kinase Regulator (`GO:0035591`, `GO:0017124`, `GO:0019901`)**:
   - Bridges c-Abl (`ABL1`) to WAVE2 and Mena (`ENAH`) to promote site-specific tyrosine phosphorylation (`PMID:12672821`, `PMID:17101133`, `PMID:26428302`), while `pTyr213` + PXXP motifs also allosterically restrain non-myristoylated c-Abl in trans (`PMID:18328268`).
   - Scaffolds the EPS8–ABI1–SOS1–PIK3R1 complex to couple RTK/Ras/PI3K signaling to Rac1 activation and relieves EPS8 autoinhibition for actin barbed-end capping (`PMID:9010225`, `PMID:10499589`, `PMID:12515821`, `PMID:14565974`, `PMID:15558031`).
   - Cooperates with Cdc42/TOCA-1 (`FNBP1L`) and N-WASP (`WASL`) in EGFR endocytosis, macropinocytosis, and actin-dependent vesicle trafficking (`PMID:16155590`, `PMID:19798448`, `PMID:20598684`).

## 3. Evaluation of Affinage Deep Research (`ABI1-deep-research-affinage.md`)
- Affinage captures the core mammalian ABI1 literature well (WRC assembly `PMID:15048123`, `PMID:15143189`, `PMID:21482783`; EPS8–ABI1–SOS1–PI3K Rac-GEF scaffold `PMID:10499589`, `PMID:12515821`; N-WASP endocytic activation `PMID:16155590`; `pTyr213`/`Ser216`/PTEN regulation `PMID:18328268`, `PMID:21900237`, `PMID:32673396`; hematopoietic SFK/STAT3/NF-κB derepression `PMID:30213875`; KMT2A-ABI1 AML fusion `PMID:9694699`), with 50 real numeric PMIDs and zero `PMID:bio_...` tokens.
- **Critical issues identified during curatorial audit**:
  1. **Gene-symbol homonym collision (`PMID:38683723`)**: Line 86 cites *The Plant Journal* (2024) on *Arabidopsis thaliana* `ABI1` (`ABA INSENSITIVE 1`, a clade A PP2C protein phosphatase imported into plant nuclei by SAD2 during abscisic acid / coronatine stomatal signaling), which is completely unrelated to human `ABI1` (`PTHR10460`; plant WAVE complex orthologs are `ABIL1`–`ABIL4`, `PMID:15659634`).
  2. **Inverted domain ownership in narrative**: Line 24 states *"ABI1 binds the WHD domain of WAVE2 and an N-terminal WAVE-binding domain of WAVE1"*, inverting `PMID:15143189` (the N-terminal WAVE-binding `WAB` domain is on ABI1, and it binds the Wave homology domain `WHD` of WAVE1).
  3. **Non-human model organism scope**: Lines 54, 66, 67 (`PMID:19168673`, `PMID:23209429`, `PMID:23028145`) summarize *C. elegans* `abi-1` and line 82 (`PMID:23558677`) summarizes *Xenopus* `abi1` inside a human-only synthesis, and Affinage's `goslim_generic` grounding (`GO:0060090`, `GO:0098772`, `GO:0140110 transcription regulator activity`) is both too coarse for curation and overstates ABI1's indirect Myc/Max cofactor role as transcription regulator activity.
  4. **Metadata/residue nuances**: Line 80 dates `PMID:35198891` (*iScience*, 2022) as `2012`, and line 78 cites `Y398` from `PMID:21320496` without noting that this SH3-domain tyrosine corresponds to `Tyr455` on canonical 508-aa `Q8IZP0-1`.

## 4. Key GOA Annotation Audit Notes
- **Bare `GO:0005515` (`protein binding`) rows**: Per repository protein-binding policy, none are accepted as bare `GO:0005515`. Interactions with kinases (`ABL1` `P00519`, `ABL2` `P42684` in `PMID:11418237`, `PMID:17101133`, `PMID:18328268`, `PMID:20598684`, `PMID:9593709`) are `MODIFY` → `GO:0019901` (`protein kinase binding`); SH3-mediated adaptor interactions (`EPS8`/`EPS8L1-3`, `NCK1`, `BAIAP2`/IRSp53, `PIK3R1`/`PIK3R3`, `VAV1`/`VAV2`, `RASA1`, `SOCS7`, `CBLB`, `NCF1`) where SH3/SH2 adaptor or SH3-domain binding is demonstrated are `MODIFY` → `GO:0017124` (`SH3 domain binding`) or `GO:0035591` (`signaling adaptor activity`) as specifically supported by the cited reference; `SPTA1` (`PMID:9593709`) is `MODIFY` → `GO:0017124` (`SH3 domain binding`); structural WRC co-complex co-IPs (`NCKAP1` `Q9Y2A7`, `NCKAP1L` `P55160`) and high-throughput interactome hits (`CACNA1A` `PMID:21078624`, BioPlex/OpenCell/Hein/KRAS interactomes `PMID:26496610`, `PMID:31980649`, `PMID:33961781`, `PMID:35271311`, `PMID:40205054`) are `REMOVE` as uninformative bare protein binding (with WRC membership already captured by `GO:0031209` `SCAR complex`).
- **Root organelle `GO:0005783` (`endoplasmic reticulum`, `TAS`, `PMID:9593709`)**: `PMID:9593709` only observed cytoplasmic punctate GFP fluorescence upon overexpression in NIH3T3 cells ("characteristic of the reticulovesicular system") without any ER marker or endogenous ER localization. Per `projects/ORGANELLES.md`, a `TAS` synthesis row resting solely on GFP-fusion overexpression is `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED`.
- **`Reactome:R-HSA-2130194` (`TAS`, `GO:0005829` `cytosol`)**: `GO:0005829` is `ACCEPT` (WRC resides in the cytosol prior to plasma membrane recruitment), and we note in `reference_review` / `source_defects` (`WRONG_STATEMENT` on `REACTOME`) that `R-HSA-2130194`'s summary text erroneously expands `ABL` as *"Abelson interactor-1 (ABL) tyrosine kinase"* instead of Abelson tyrosine kinase (`ABL1`).
- **`GO:0008154` (`actin polymerization or depolymerization`, `NAS`, `PMID:11516653`)**: `MODIFY` to `GO:0034314` (`Arp2/3 complex-mediated actin nucleation`) / `GO:0030036` (`actin cytoskeleton organization`) — ABI1 does not polymerize actin directly, and `PMID:11516653` localized EYFP-Abi1 to lamellipodia/filopodia tips rather than assaying polymerization directly.
