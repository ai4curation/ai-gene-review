CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic subunit of the tRNA-specific adenosine-34 deaminase
CC       complex, composed of the ADAT2 catalytic subunit and the ADAT3
CC       regulatory subunit, which deaminates adenosine-34 (the first, also
CC       called wobble position of the anticodon) to inosine in many tRNAs.
CC       Inosine-34 allows the decoding of 3 different nucleotides at the third
CC       position of mRNA codons, as inosine is able to pair with U, C, and A.
CC       Binding of the ADAT2-ADAT3 complex to tRNA is mediated by the N-
CC       terminus of ADAT3 which rotates with respect to the catalytic domain of
CC       the complex, formed by ADAT2 and the ADAT3 C-terminal domain, to
CC       position the tRNA anticodon stem-loop correctly in the ADAT2 active
CC       site. The ADAT2-ADAT3 complex is required for radial migration of
CC       projection neurons in the developing brain cortex, and the catalytic
CC       activity of the complex is necessary for this function.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q6P6J0}.
