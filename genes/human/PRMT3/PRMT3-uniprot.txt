CC   -!- FUNCTION: Protein-arginine N-methyltransferase that catalyzes both the
CC       monomethylation and asymmetric dimethylation of the guanidino nitrogens
CC       of arginine residues in target proteins, and therefore falls into the
CC       group of type I methyltransferases (PubMed:22795084, PubMed:23445220,
CC       PubMed:25728001, PubMed:31378783, PubMed:33495566, PubMed:39513743).
CC       Catalyzes the asymmetric arginine dimethylation at multiple sites in
CC       the Arg/Gly-rich region of small ribosomal subunit protein uS5/RPS2
CC       (PubMed:22795084). Also appears to methylate other ribosomal proteins
CC       (By similarity). May regulate retinoic acid synthesis and signaling by
CC       inhibiting ALDH1A1 retinal dehydrogenase activity (PubMed:33495566).
CC       Contributes to methylation of histone H4 'Arg-3', a specific tag for
CC       epigenetic transcriptional activation (PubMed:25728001,
CC       PubMed:31378783, PubMed:39513743). Mediates asymmetric arginine
CC       dimethylation of histone H4 'Arg-3' (H4R3me2a) in the promoter region
CC       of miRNA miR-3648, to promote its transcription and osteogenesis
CC       (PubMed:31378783). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q922H1,
