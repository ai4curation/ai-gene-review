# FARS2 evidence guide

This is an authored source and locator guide. No new publication quotations were added. Original enzyme, interaction and localization evidence was assessed together with database provenance. Normal publication caches remain unchanged; private recoveries supplement their access limits. Provider research failed during intake and no provider report was manufactured. For PMID:22833457, original PDF text was readable through web extraction; the separate 919-byte local download is an HTTP403 failure body, not a valid PDF. Actual returned web text is preserved separately from that failed request.

## Enzyme evidence

**PMID:10329163.** The normal cache is abstract-only. The [author-uploaded original](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/12969863_Expression_and_characterization_of_a_human_mitochondrial_phenylalanyl-tRNA_synthetase) was recovered through indexed web access despite direct HTTP 403 responses. Read original Results on expression and physical properties, ATP:pyrophosphate exchange, tRNA aminoacylation/Table 1, and Methods on cloning, protein purification, exchange and aminoacylation. The private captured read spans include original lines 455–928, 1108–1373 and 1836–2302. Recombinant human enzyme directly catalyzes charging. The bacterial transcript and bovine mitochondrial tRNA substrates do not establish cellular localization of the expressed human protein. The targeting/cleavage boundary was predicted; no exact natural processing site or isoform is asserted. Functional tRNA recognition is demonstrated by charging/steady-state kinetics, not an equilibrium binding measurement.

**PMID:19549855.** Read the complete cached original main text, including Results, Discussion and Methods ([original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2700156/)). Human mitochondrial and cytosolic PheRS were separately tested with bacterial/yeast tRNA substrates. Human mitochondrial PheRS directly charges phenylalanine and also meta-tyrosine in vitro. Cytosolic/bacterial editing findings are not transferred to FARS2. Figure pixels were not inspected.

**PMID:22833457.** The normal cache is abstract-only. Recovered the [original nine-page article PDF](https://scispace.com/pdf/mitochondrial-phenylalanyl-trna-synthetase-mutations-4b0bo4yofy.pdf) and [author-uploaded text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230570767_Mitochondrial_phenylalanyl-tRNA_synthetase_mutations_underlie_fatal_infantile_Alpers_encephalopathy). Read functional analysis of recombinant mtPheRS variants, table-linked kinetic results, and aminoacylation/ATP:pyrophosphate exchange Methods (PDF pp.5–7, text lines 241–280 and 439–493; author text Results lines 684–779). Wild-type human enzyme provides the positive activity comparator. Changes in ATP, amino acid and tRNA kinetics are distinguished from structural predictions. Patient fibroblasts did not reproduce the reported respiratory/translation defect; disease-associated necessity does not itself license additional GO processes.

Official [QuickGO GO:0006432](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0006432) marks the imported term obsolete and explains that it duplicates GO:0004826, with biological process coverage in GO:0006418. The GO:0008033 definition concerns pre-tRNA maturation before aminoacylation. Consequently, the phenylalanyl-tRNA aminoacylation and tRNA-processing assertions are refined to GO:0006418; original imported IDs and labels remain exact.

## Catalytic pathway and family evidence

Both normal Reactome records and current ContentService objects were read. [R-HSA-379726](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-379726) contains [R-HSA-380203](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-380203), which connects through catalystActivity 379675 to physicalEntity 379565. That entity is human O95363, carries matrix compartment GO:0005759, and supplies GO:0004826 catalytic activity. The reaction consumes phenylalanine, tRNA(Phe) and ATP and produces charged tRNA, AMP and diphosphate. The inspected target route supports FARS2's own step, without assigning other pathway catalysts. The pathway's general protein-import citation PMID:11121736 was not independently read and supplies no target-specific evidence here.

The actual PANTHER tree and MSA place O95363 at PTN002480027 in PTHR11538:SF41. The 22-node ancestry path passes PTN000165932 and PTN000165999; the ungapped target MSA reproduces the official 451-residue sequence. The PAINT file places Phe ligase, aminoacylation and cytoplasm assertions at PTN000165932, and mitochondrial location at PTN000165999. Their dates are 2023-01-10, 2025-09-02, 2026-05-28 and 2026-05-28, respectively. Function-loss assertions at PTN000711660 and the cytosolic PheRS complex at PTN000165935 are off the target path. Target self-WITH is legitimate descendant experimental grounding; donor number is not an evidence-strength test. The aminoacylation assertion remains biologically supported while its obsolete term is refined to GO:0006418.

The complete official GO_REF:0000002, 0000024, 0000033, 0000044 and 0000120 method descriptions were checked against their target records. Method titles supply provenance, not gene-specific experiments; merged electronic routes are not independent experimental replication.

## Product and synthesis scope

UniProt displays a 451-residue precursor but specifies no exact mature cleavage boundary or curated alternative products. RefSeq and Ensembl transcript/protein models, predicted recombinant boundaries and Reactome's FARS2(1-?) entity do not establish additional natural products. No synthetic O95363-1, guessed isoform or natural processing coordinate is supplied.

The core is ATP-dependent phenylalanyl-tRNA synthesis. tRNA and ATP binding are catalytic substrate interactions. The cytosolic enzyme's editing function is not transferred to FARS2. Meta-tyrosine charging remains an observed in-vitro substrate property, and disease consequences remain biological context. No additional process or activity is inferred from interaction partners or necessity phenotypes.


## Term specificity and the remaining biochemical question

The broad nucleotide-binding, aminoacyl-tRNA-ligase and tRNA-charging assertions are true but subsumed by retained ATP binding (GO:0005524), phenylalanine-tRNA ligase activity (GO:0004826) and tRNA aminoacylation for protein translation (GO:0006418). Both cytoplasm assertions are likewise subsumed by the positively supported mitochondrion annotation. These five decisions use TOO_GENERAL; the original source objects, PAINT placement and unresolved ARBA eligibility remain intact. The independently informative mitochondrion rows remain accepted. The core now lists mitochondrion alongside matrix and explicitly identifies matrix placement as a curated inference.

The binding records are now authored YAML, with each IntAct method represented as a list item. All 81 records and every original field are retained, including empty author-release locators, exact partner suffixes and accession-limit caveats.

In PMID:19549855, Results under “Discrimination Between Cognate and Noncognate Substrates” establish alternative-substrate charging by the human mitochondrial enzyme. Methods under “tRNA Aminoacylation” specify bacterial or yeast tRNA substrates. The Discussion proposes a mitochondrial contribution to meta-tyrosine incorporation. These experiments do not measure FARS2-dependent incorporation into mitochondrial proteins in living human cells. The knowledge gap and proposed experiment are bounded to that distinction; they add no physiological activity, editing function or oxidative-stress annotation. This is not a claim that a comprehensive later-literature search found no relevant study.
