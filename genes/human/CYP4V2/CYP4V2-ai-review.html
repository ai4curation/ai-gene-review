Gene Description

CYP4V2 is a heme-containing cytochrome P450 associated with the endoplasmic reticulum. It uses oxygen and electrons supplied by cytochrome P450 reductase to hydroxylate fatty acids, including laurate, myristate, palmitate, eicosapentaenoate and docosahexaenoate. Terminal hydroxylation predominates, with smaller penultimate-carbon products observed for the polyunsaturated substrates. CYP4V2 is expressed in several tissues, including retinal pigment epithelium and cornea. Biallelic pathogenic variants cause Bietti crystalline dystrophy; its fatty-acid chemistry may contribute to retinal lipid homeostasis, although the decisive endogenous substrate and disease mechanism remain incompletely resolved.

Core Functions

Heme-dependent terminal hydroxylation of long-chain fatty acids, including myristate, palmitate, EPA and DHA, uses P450 reductase to deliver NADPH-derived electrons and produces omega-hydroxy fatty acids. The physiological retinal substrate balance remains unresolved.

CYP4V2 review notes

2026-10-16 — Fatty-acid chemistry and ocular disease

CYP4V2 is an ER-associated, heme-containing fatty-acid hydroxylase. Reconstituted human enzyme directly makes terminal hydroxy products from laurate, myristate and palmitate. The inspected kinetic Results favor myristate, while the C8 assay was negative. The paper’s use of “medium-chain” includes C14, whereas current GO places C14 in its C13–C22 long-chain class; the separate C6–C12 term captures the positive laurate reaction. PMID:19661213(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19661213/)

Purified human CYP4V2 also forms 20-hydroxy-EPA and 22-hydroxy-DHA. Actual Figure 5 separates biosynthetic standards, minus-NADPH controls and CYP4V2 turnover. Smaller 19-hydroxy-EPA and 21-hydroxy-DHA products establish a secondary penultimate-carbon outcome; they were not separately quantified. Terminal-product quantitation used saturated same-chain standards, and substrate solubility/inhibition prevented exhaustive kinetics. These data support three complementary catalytic activities without asserting equal physiological importance. PMID:22772592(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22772592/)

The same study reports endogenous ARPE-19 CYP4V2 colocalization with calreticulin and ocular epithelial staining. The original main Methods/Results were read, but the supplemental localization figure was not recovered: a URL ending in PDF returned download/challenge HTML. The predicted N-terminal membrane anchor is distinct from experimentally resolved orientation. H331P-transduced HepG2 cells had reduced protein and altered PUFA pools; this does not establish the entire retinal disease mechanism or directly compare turnover per equal amount of variant enzyme. The initial disease-discovery abstract establishes autosomal-recessive Bietti crystalline dystrophy, while its suggested fatty-acid/steroid functions were hypotheses later refined by direct chemistry. PMID:15042513(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15042513/)

Pathways and interactions

Actual Reactome catalyst/entity/reference paths identify the full 525-residue CYP4V2 protein converting DHA to 22-hydroxy-DHA in ER membrane. Both the retinoid-cycle and endogenous-sterol parent pathways contain this child. The substrates are neither retinoids nor sterols under the checked GO definitions, so those two process mappings overstate the specific reaction. This is not a claim that every possible retinoid/sterol substrate has been experimentally excluded.
