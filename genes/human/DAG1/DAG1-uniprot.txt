CC   -!- FUNCTION: The dystroglycan complex is involved in a number of signaling
CC       events and processes including laminin deposition and extracellular
CC       matrix assembly, acetylcholine receptor clustering, sarcolemmal
CC       stability, cell survival, peripheral nerve myelination, nodal
CC       structure, cell migration, epithelial polarization, and epithelium
CC       branching morphogenesis (By similarity). Required for the formation of
CC       photoreceptor ribbon synapses, and long-term maintenance of inhibitory
CC       synapses in cerebellar Purkinje cells (By similarity). Also involved in
CC       the positive regulation of cartilage formation through agrin (AGRN)
CC       binding and up-regulation of SOX9, a transcription factor that plays a
CC       key role in chondrocytes differentiation (PubMed:26290588).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q62165, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26290588}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Alpha-dystroglycan]: Extracellular peripheral glycoprotein
CC       that acts as a receptor for extracellular matrix proteins containing
CC       laminin-G domains. As a receptor for laminin is involved in
CC       extracellular matrix assembly, and activation of the PI3K/AKT pathway
CC       regulating cell apoptotic signals in muscle (By similarity). Binding of
CC       laminin LAMA1 to alpha-dystroglycan also initiates a signaling cascade
CC       in which Src kinases, c-Src or c-Fyn, phosphorylate syntrophin
CC       modifying its interaction with the adapter protein GRB2; this triggers
CC       recruitment of guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor SOS1 and activation of
CC       RAC1, finally resulting in c-Jun phosphorylation by MAPK8/JNK1 (By
CC       similarity). As a receptor for laminin LAMA1 is also involved in
CC       epithelium branching morphogenesis in salivary glang and lung (By
CC       similarity). Receptor for laminin-2 (LAMA2) and agrin in peripheral
CC       nerve Schwann cells (By similarity). Also acts as a receptor for
CC       laminin LAMA5 (By similarity). In muscle cells, it is a receptor for
CC       laminin-1 (also known as laminin-111 or EHS laminin) and is involved in
CC       the stimulation of agrin-induced acetylcholine receptor (AChR)
CC       clustering, and formation of the synaptic basement membrane. It is
CC       required for acetylcholinesterase (AChE) localization at the
CC       neuromuscular junctions (NMJ) through its binding with perlecan (HSPG2)
CC       and is, therefore, involved in the down-regulation of colinergic
CC       synaptic transmission (By similarity). In the retina, it is required
CC       for the formation of photoreceptor ribbon synapses through its
CC       interaction with pikachurin (EGFLAM) (By similarity). Involved in the
CC       positive regulation of cartilage formation through agrin (AGRN) binding
CC       and up-regulation of SOX9, a transcription factor that plays a key role
CC       in chondrocytes differentiation (PubMed:26290588).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O18738, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q28685,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q62165, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26290588}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Beta-dystroglycan]: Transmembrane protein that plays
CC       important roles in connecting the extracellular matrix to the
CC       cytoskeleton. Acts as a cell adhesion receptor in both muscle and non-
CC       muscle tissues. Receptor for both DMD and UTRN and, through these
CC       interactions, scaffolds axin to the cytoskeleton. Also functions in
CC       cell adhesion-mediated signaling and implicated in cell polarity.
