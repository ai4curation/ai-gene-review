# DAG1 biological evidence notes

Dystroglycan is a processed receptor system. The official Q14118 sequence is an 895-residue precursor, with signal peptide 1–29, alpha chain 30–653 and beta chain 654–895. These chains have complementary functions and are recorded as cleavage products, not splice isoforms. ([UniProt Q14118](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q14118/entry)) Glycosylated alpha-DG presents the matrix-ligand-binding surface; beta-DG anchors it in the membrane and engages intracellular partners. One integrated laminin-receptor core captures this physical linkage and its contribution to matrix organization. ([PMID:20044576](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20044576/)) ([PMID:23940118](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23940118/)) Developmental, synaptic and signaling effects retain their tissue and experimental contexts rather than becoming universal core activities.

The distinction between intrinsic maturation and cleavage by another enzyme is decisive. The 2008 SEA study supports intramolecular precursor cleavage but does not establish the catalytic triad in the serine-endopeptidase definition. Cleavage mutants retain or enhance laminin binding and assembly, so cleavage is not described as universally necessary for ligand competence. ([PMID:17905726](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17905726/)) In the separate MMP-9 study, murine beta-DG is the cleaved substrate and human MMP-9 performs proteolysis. ([PMID:19946898](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19946898/)) Neither experiment assigns glycan-transfer activity to DAG1. Public original locators and precise read limits are in [the evidence guide](DAG1-evidence-index.yaml).

Beta-chain partner evidence has different levels of resolution. Utrophin and dystrophin are established cytoskeletal partners. ([PMID:10767429](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10767429/)) ([PMID:11495720](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11495720/)) Human DAG1–CLASP co-immunoprecipitation is distinguished from chick epiblast perturbation, and complex association is not described as a purified binary interface. ([PMID:23940118](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23940118/)) The NCK1 screen tests a synthetic peptide corresponding to alpha-chain residues 300–313; its interaction with intracellular SH3 domains does not establish accessibility in the native mature receptor. ([PMID:17474147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17474147/)) The ankyrin records test a human beta tail against rat ankyrin-G. ([PMID:19109891](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19109891/)) Positive AGR2/AGR3 yeast interactions and the caveolin-3 tail interaction are retained with their assay limits. ([PMID:12592373](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12592373/)) ([PMID:10988290](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10988290/)) No partner enzyme activity is transferred to DAG1.

The platelet original remains inaccessible. ([PMID:18341635](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18341635/)) General cytoskeletal interplay in its abstract is insufficient to reconstruct individual actin, tubulin, MAP2 or vinculin assays and the fine localization/anchoring endpoints. These assertions remain unresolved. A separately inspected human ACTN1–DAG1 curated BioGRID record supports that specific interaction, and plasma-membrane localization is independently established. ([BioGRID 616031](https://thebiogrid.org/interaction/616031)) ([PMID:8017170](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8017170/)) Neither a missing current interaction record nor an unavailable original is evidence of nonbinding.

The human 8UF4 structure supplies direct evidence for calcium coordination. One modeled ion is contacted by five native DAG1 residues in a recombinant 491–748 fragment. Refolding and purification used calcium-rich buffers. This supports contextual calcium binding while leaving native occupancy, affinity and physiological regulation unresolved. It is independent of calcium dependence on the ligand side of an adhesion assay; the paper’s wording about both molecules is kept distinct from the single calcium modeled in the current deposit. ([PMID:39305901](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39305901/)) ([8UF4 coordinates](https://files.rcsb.org/download/8UF4.cif))

Compartment annotations are interpreted by product and source. ER/Golgi locations describe normal receptor maturation and trafficking, and extracellular alpha-derived fragments can be released. ([PMID:17905726](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17905726/)) ([PMID:18201566](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18201566/)) Nuclear and cytoplasmic beta-chain pools are supported in cultured human cells without establishing a universal nuclear function. ([PMID:18764929](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18764929/)) Two defective-POMT models instead label alpha-DG 30–653 cytosolic; their summations do not supply an operative localization measurement, so those particular assignments remain unresolved. ([R-HSA-5615556](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5615556), [R-HSA-5615604](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5615604)) A separate beta-tail observation does not answer that question.

The human parotid exosome workbooks contain IPI00028911.1/DAG1 with two sequences and two spectra. Both measured peptides match the alpha chain. ([PMID:19199708](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19199708/)) This supports a preparation-level observation with contamination and product limits, not intact beta-chain cargo or a required extracellular-vesicle function. The prostatic-secretion survey has relevant main methods, but attempted target spreadsheet files were HTML/error bodies; no target values were inferred from them. ([PMID:23533145](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23533145/)) The guide gives the original parotid download links, sheet names, rows and literal peptide cells.

The actual PTHR21559 tree and alignment reproduce the 895-residue target. All nine IBD assertions lie on its ancestral path. ([PANTHER PTHR21559](https://www.pantherdb.org/panther/family.do?clsAccession=PTHR21559), [GO_REF:0000033](https://geneontology.org/GO_REF/0000033.html)) The authentic full GAF was captured on October 12 and reused; this is not presented as a new October 16 download. Human descendant evidence within an IBA is legitimate, and a short donor list is not a defect. Complete current donor queries distinguish experimental provenance from propagated records; donor paper identifiers in the guide identify the curators’ sources, not papers independently read here.
