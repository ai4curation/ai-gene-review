# STIM1 notes

Deep research providers unavailable (no API keys). Notes from UniProt Q13586 and cached literature.

## Identity / topology
- Single-pass type I membrane protein of the endoplasmic reticulum. UniProt topology: lumenal (labelled
  "Extracellular") N-terminal region 23-213 containing the EF-hand pair and SAM domain; TM 214-234;
  cytoplasmic region 235-685 containing the SOAR/CAD domain, coiled coils and the polybasic/EB1 tip.
- The lumenal EF-hand senses ER Ca2+; store depletion triggers oligomerization and a conformational switch
  exposing SOAR/CAD, which traps and gates ORAI channels across ER-PM junctions.

## Function
- UniProt FUNCTION (ECO:0000269): "Acts as a Ca(2+) sensor that gates two major inward rectifying Ca(2+) channels at the plasma membrane: Ca(2+) release-activated Ca(2+) (CRAC) channels and arachidonate-regulated Ca(2+)-selective (ARC) channels... Plays a role in mediating store-operated Ca(2+) entry (SOCE)."
- [PMID:16005298] STIM is the ER Ca2+ sensor essential for store-depletion-triggered Ca2+ influx; "translocation of STIM1 into puncta that accumulated near the plasma membrane".
- [PMID:19249086 "STIM1 clusters and activates CRAC channels via direct binding of a cytosolic domain to Orai1"].
- [PMID:17517596 "Stromal interaction molecule 1 (STIM1) has recently been identified ... as an endoplasmic reticulum (ER) Ca 2+ sensor that responds to ER Ca 2+ store depletion and activates Ca 2+ channels in the plasma membrane (PM)"].
- [PMID:26322679] ER-PM junction proteomics: "The ER-resident Ca2+ sensor protein, STIM1".

## Localization conclusion
Core location is the ER membrane (and cortical ER at ER-PM junctions; SR membrane in muscle). STIM1 gates
ORAI from the ER side across the ER-PM gap; it is NOT primarily a plasma-membrane protein. A minor
glycosylated surface pool has been reported (several IDA "plasma membrane" rows) and is kept as non-core.

## Disease
Gain-of-function STIM1 → tubular aggregate myopathy / Stormorken syndrome [PMID:23332920, PMID:25326555];
loss-of-function → CRAC channelopathy with immunodeficiency; also enamel maturation [PMID:24621671].
