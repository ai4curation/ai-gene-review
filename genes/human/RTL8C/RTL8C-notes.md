# RTL8C notes

One of three near-identical X-linked human paralogs (RTL8A/B/C = FAM127B/C/A); truncated Gag capsid-only module, no CCHC/protease/RT [PMID:39775359 "RTL8 resembles a truncated form of PEG10 that shares homology with the N-terminal gag-like capsid domain"]. Studied collectively (RTL8C representative); paralogs indistinguishable by western [PMID:33277362].
Function: capsid-derived adaptor bridging PEG10 and UBQLN2. RTL8 binds PEG10 N-terminal capsid, incorporated into PEG10 VLPs, inhibits VLP formation/release [PMID:39775359]. Promotes UBQLN2 nuclear/nucleolar translocation [PMID:35247097 "RTL8 localizes to nucleoli"; "RTL8 promotes nuclear translocation of UBQLN2"]. Essential for UBQLN2-mediated PEG10 turnover and UBQLN2 recruitment to stress granules [PMID:40680123 "RTL8 confers on UBQLN2 the ability to complex with and regulate PEG10"; "RTL8 is an essential partner for the UBQLN2-mediated turnover of PEG10"].
