Gene Description

RGS4 is a GTPase-activating regulator of heterotrimeric Gi/o and Gq signaling. Its RGS domain binds the activated G alpha subunit and stabilizes the transition state for GTP hydrolysis, shortening receptor-driven signaling. RGS4 acts at the cytoplasmic plasma-membrane surface and in the cytoplasm, with regulated nuclear shuttling. It modulates neurotransmitter-receptor control of calcium and potassium channels and can restrain cardiac hypertrophic signaling; receptor and cellular context determine its physiological effects.
