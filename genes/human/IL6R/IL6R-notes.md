# IL6R notes

Deep research with Falcon/Perplexity-lite was unavailable in the shared runtime, so this review was assembled from `just fetch-gene` output, cached publications, UniProt P08887 and GO-CAM 5f46c3b700002685.

- IL6R is the ligand-specific alpha chain for IL-6. IL-6 first binds IL-6Ralpha and is then presented to gp130/IL6ST in the hexameric signaling receptor complex [PMID:12829785 "IL-6 is first engaged by IL-6Ralpha and then presented to gp130"].
- Membrane IL6R and IL6ST support classic signaling, while IL-6 bound to soluble IL6R activates gp130 on cells that do not express membrane IL6R [PMID:11884403 "IL-6 complexed with sIL-6R ... induced rapid tyrosine phosphorylation of gp130"].
- Soluble IL6R is generated by both alternative splicing and ectodomain shedding; serum proteomics and mutagenesis support ADAM10/ADAM17 cleavage of the membrane receptor as a major source [PMID:28060820 "sIL-6R can be efficiently generated by limited proteolysis, predominantly by the metalloproteases ADAM10 and ADAM17"].
- CNTF can use membrane-bound or soluble human IL6R as a substitute alpha receptor for the gp130:LIFR signaling dimer, so the CNTF receptor-complex annotations are secondary but real rather than wrong [PMID:12643274 "human CNTF can use both the membrane-bound and the soluble human IL-6R as a substitute for its cognate alpha-receptor"].
- The PANTHER IBA rows that assign IL-11 receptor activity, IL-11 binding or IL-11-mediated signaling to IL6R are over-propagated from the type-I cytokine receptor family. Human IL-11 signaling uses IL11RA with IL6ST/gp130, not IL6R.

## GO-CAM 5f46c3b700002685

- The model's use of soluble isoform `UniProtKB:P08887-2` is appropriate for IL6 trans-signaling.
- The activity term on soluble IL6R is less ideal: the model types the soluble extracellular isoform with `GO:0004915` interleukin-6 receptor activity, whose definition is transmembrane-signaling oriented. A final GO-CAM consistency PR should note that soluble IL6R presents IL-6 to gp130 and acts as an agonistic signaling-receptor activator in this model rather than as the membrane alpha receptor.
