# VPS35 (Q96QK1) Curation Notes

## Overview & Complex Architecture
- **VPS35** (`Q96QK1`, ~92 kDa, 796 aa) is the central alpha-helical solenoid scaffold of the mammalian **retromer cargo-selective complex (CSC)** (`GO:0030906`), a conserved heterotrimer composed of **VPS35**, **VPS29**, and either **VPS26A** or **VPS26B** ([PMID:11102511, "The data suggest a model in which hVps35 serves as the core of a multimeric complex by binding directly to hVps26, hVps29, and SNX1."]; [PMID:17891154]; [PMID:27889239]; [PMID:30224749]; [PMID:37172566]).
- VPS35 forms an elongated right-handed alpha-helical solenoid containing 17 HEAT-like repeats (~34 alpha-helices):
  - Its **N-terminal region** (residues 1–172, notably the evolutionarily conserved `PRLYL` motif at residues 104–108) directly binds the C-terminal arrestin-like subdomain of **VPS26A** or **VPS26B** ([PMID:11102511]; [PMID:16732284]; [PMID:27889239]).
  - Its **C-terminal half** (residues 476–780) wraps around the metallophosphoesterase-fold scaffold subunit **VPS29** ([PMID:17891154, "Here we report the crystal structure of a VPS29-VPS35 subcomplex showing how the metallophosphoesterase-fold subunit VPS29 (refs 14, 15) acts as a scaffold for the carboxy-terminal half of VPS35."]).
  - On endosomal membranes, retromer dimers assemble into arch-like oligomers over membrane-bound sorting nexins, with antiparallel homodimerization of the VPS35 C-terminal domain forming the apex of the arch while VPS26 dimers dock onto membrane-associated SNX3 or SNX-BAR subunits ([PMID:30224749]).
- Unlike VPS29 (which is shared with the Retriever complex `VPS26C:VPS35L:VPS29`), **VPS35 is exclusive to the retromer CSC**; Retriever uses the distant paralog **VPS35L** (`C16orf62`) ([PMID:28892079]; [PMID:37172566]).

## Core Biological Functions
1. **Endosome-to-Trans-Golgi Network (TGN) Retrograde Transport (`GO:0042147`)**:
   - Retromer retrieves acid hydrolase sorting receptors—most prominently the cation-independent mannose 6-phosphate receptor (**CI-MPR** / `IGF2R`) and **sortilin** (`SORT1`)—from early/maturing endosomes back to the TGN, preventing their delivery to and degradation in lysosomes ([PMID:15078903, "The hVps35 subunit of retromer interacts with the cytosolic domain of the CI-MPR. This interaction probably occurs in an endosomal compartment, where most of the retromer is localized."]; [PMID:15078903, "Depletion of retromer by RNA interference increases the lysosomal turnover of the CI-MPR, decreases cellular levels of lysosomal hydrolases, and causes swelling of lysosomes."]; [PMID:22431521]).
   - In conjunction with **SNX3** (`GO:0042147`), retromer mediates retrograde endosome-to-TGN retrieval of the Wnt sorting receptor **Wntless** (`WLS`/`Evi`), which is required for secretion of Wnt morphogens ([PMID:18193037, "Following dissociation from Wingless, Wntless is internalized and returns to the Golgi apparatus in a retromer-dependent manner."]; [PMID:21725319]; [PMID:27889239]).
2. **Endocytic Recycling (`GO:0032456`), D1 Dopamine Receptor Binding (`GO:0031748`), and Transcytosis (`GO:0045056`)**:
   - Coupled with the endosomal sorting nexin **SNX27** (which binds VPS26A via its PDZ domain) and the actin-nucleating **WASH complex** (which binds the VPS35 C-terminal tail via FAM21/WASHC2C), retromer mediates direct endosome-to-plasma membrane recycling of numerous cell-surface cargoes, including the beta-2 adrenergic receptor (**ADRB2**), dopamine receptor D1 (**DRD1**, via direct binding `GO:0031748`), glucose transporter **GLUT1** (`SLC2A1`), divalent metal transporter **DMT1-II** (`SLC11A2`), and AMPA/NMDA glutamate receptors ([PMID:20164305]; [PMID:20923837]; [PMID:21602791, "β2AR recycling, as measured by antibody efflux following agonist removal, was strongly inhibited by multiple siRNA sequences targeting either VPS26 or VPS35."]; [PMID:23563491]; [PMID:24705354]; [PMID:27460146]).
   - In polarized epithelial cells, retromer promotes basolateral-to-apical transcytosis (`GO:0045056`) of the polymeric immunoglobulin receptor (**pIgR**-pIgA) ([PMID:15247922, "Here, we show a function for the mammalian Vps35-Vps29-Vps26 retromer subcomplex in promoting pIgR-pIgA transcytosis."]).
3. **Mitochondria-Derived Vesicle (MDV) Trafficking & Mitochondrial Dynamics (`GO:0099073`, `GO:0099074`)**:
   - VPS35 also localizes to mitochondria-derived vesicles (`GO:0099073`), mediating delivery of MAPL/MUL1-containing MDVs to peroxisomes and facilitating turnover of mitochondrial dynamin-like protein 1 (**DLP1** / `DNM1L`) complexes via MDV-mediated transport to lysosomes (`GO:0099074`) ([PMID:26618722, "VPS35 mutants showed increased interaction with dynamin-like protein (DLP) 1, which enhanced turnover of the mitochondrial DLP1 complexes via the mitochondria-derived vesicle-dependent trafficking of the complexes to lysosomes for degradation."]).

## Disease Genetics & Parkinson Disease (PARK17)
- Heterozygous missense mutations in `VPS35`, most notably **p.Asp620Asn (D620N)** and **p.Arg524Trp (R524W)**, cause autosomal-dominant late-onset Parkinson disease (**PARK17**, MIM:614203) ([PMID:24705354]; [PMID:25533483]; [PMID:26618722]; [PMID:27385586]; [PMID:27460146]).
- The **D620N** mutation resides in the C-terminal region of VPS35 and does not disrupt assembly of the VPS35-VPS29-VPS26A trimer; instead, it decreases affinity for the WASH complex subunit **FAM21** (`WASHC2C`), impairing WASH recruitment, ATG9A trafficking, autophagosome maturation, DRD1 surface recycling, and AMPA receptor trafficking, while enhancing interaction with mitochondrial DLP1 (`DNM1L`) and causing excessive mitochondrial fragmentation ([PMID:24705354]; [PMID:26618722]; [PMID:27460146]).
