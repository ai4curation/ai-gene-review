## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ANKRD12 (ANCO-2) functions as a transcriptional co-repressor that modulates co-activator activity at nuclear receptor target genes [PMID:18377363]. Through its conserved C-terminal domain it binds the N-terminal transactivation domain of the co-activator ADA3 and co-localizes with subunits of the P/CAF complex (ADA3, ADA2α/β, P/CAF) at nuclear dots, repressing ADA3-mediated transcriptional co-activation [PMID:18377363]. Beyond this single interaction and its functional consequence on nuclear receptor reporters, no further mechanistic detail has been characterized in the available corpus.
