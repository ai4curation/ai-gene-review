CC   -!- FUNCTION: Electroneutral antiporter that translocates urate across the
CC       apical membrane of proximal tubular cells in exchange for monovalent
CC       organic or inorganic anions (PubMed:12024214, PubMed:22194875,
CC       PubMed:35144162, PubMed:35462902). Involved in renal reabsorption of
CC       urate and helps maintaining blood levels of uric acid (PubMed:12024214,
CC       PubMed:22194875). Mediates urate uptake by an exchange with organic
CC       anions such as (S)-lactate and nicotinate, and inorganic anion Cl(-)
CC       (PubMed:12024214). Other inorganic anions such as Br(-), I(-) and
CC       NO3(-) may also act as counteranions that exchange for urate
CC       (PubMed:12024214). Also mediates orotate tubular uptake coupled with
CC       nicotinate efflux and to a lesser extent with lactate efflux, therefore
CC       displaying a potential role in orotate renal reabsorption
CC       (PubMed:21350910). Orotate transport is Cl(-)-dependent
CC       (PubMed:21350910). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12024214,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21350910, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22194875,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35144162, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35462902}.
