Gene Description

WIPI2 is a seven-bladed beta-propeller of the PROPPIN family and one of the two mammalian orthologues of yeast Atg18. It has no catalytic activity; it works as an adaptor that converts a lipid signal into the recruitment of a protein complex. Two phosphoinositide pockets built around a conserved FRRG motif read phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate, which is generated on omegasomes - endoplasmic-reticulum-connected platforms - when starvation activates the ULK1 kinase complex and the class III PI3-kinase. Bound to that lipid at the membrane, WIPI2 binds ATG16L1 and so delivers the ATG12-ATG5-ATG16L1 conjugation machinery to the growing phagophore, where it drives lipidation of ATG8-family proteins and expansion of the nascent autophagosomal membrane. The lipid-reading and complex-binding surfaces are separable: an FRRG-to-FTTG mutant that cannot bind the lipid still binds ATG16L1. WIPI2 also helps hold isolation membranes against the ER through its interaction with the ULK1-RB1CC1 complex. It marks the phagophore and leaves before the autophagosome matures, and depleting it causes omegasomes to accumulate rather than autophagosomes to stall. Beyond bulk starvation-induced autophagy, the WIPI2b isoform binds the phagophore membrane forming around cytosolic Salmonella and is required for the autophagic response to that infection. Biallelic loss-of-function variants cause an autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder with short stature and skeletal anomalies.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

phagophore membrane

Definition: The membrane system of a phagophore, comprising its two closely apposed lipid bilayers and the continuous rim that joins them, which expands by lipid acquisition and closes to form the double membrane of an autophagosome.

Justification: WIPI2 is a peripheral membrane protein on the cytoplasmic face of this bilayer, so its membrane association is the substance of the annotation rather than an incidental detail, and GO:0061908 phagophore records a structure rather than a membrane. GO has autophagosome membrane (GO:0000421) and omegasome membrane (GO:1903349) but no phagophore membrane, which is why such annotations accumulated on GO:0034045 phagophore assembly site membrane - a term that asserts via bounding_layer_of a bounding membrane the PAS does not have. Seven of the forty-five annotations on this gene sit on that term. See GO issue #29437 recommendation R3 and projects/CONDENSATES/GO_0034045-annotation-audit.md. This is the same term proposed in the ATG9A review.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does WIPI2 leave the phagophore at closure, or is it displaced earlier, and what displaces it? The NOT autophagosome and NOT autophagosome maturation annotations record that it is gone by the mature organelle but not when or how.

Q: The six WIPI2 isoforms differ at the N and C termini, and only isoform 4 (WIPI2b) has a documented function in ATG16L1 recruitment and antibacterial autophagy. What do the other five do, and are any of the annotations on this entry isoform-restricted in a way GOA does not record?

Q: Is human WIPI2 actually required for pexophagy, nucleophagy and glycophagy, or are those three IBA annotations generic transfers from yeast and fly PROPPINs? Each is currently asserted from family membership alone.

Q: WIPI2 and WIPI1 are both mammalian Atg18 orthologues and both bind PtdIns3P. What makes WIPI2 the one that recruits ATG16L1, and is the division of labour a property of the propeller or of the flanking sequences?
