## 1. Reference-sequence length audit (run before any conservation number)

The ACTL10 review showed that a Swiss-Prot entry starting mid-fold manufactures fake
substitutions out of residues the sequence never reaches. So the panel is length-audited
first, and the audit is required to flag the known-short entry before it may clear ACTG2.

Conventional-actin modal length: **377** aa (P60709 375, P63261 375, P68133 377, P68032 377, P62736 377, P63267 376).

Entries shorter than 85% of that: **{'Q5JWF8': 245}** (ACTL10 is the entry the ACTL10 review traced to a lost initiator; it is flagged here by
the same criterion, which is what licenses trusting the rest of the panel).

ACTG2 is **376** aa and matches every reviewed one-to-one orthologue: Actg2 Rattus norvegicus 376 aa, Actg2 Mus musculus 376 aa, ACTG2 Gallus gallus 376 aa.
So ACTG2's own reference sequence is full length and no part of this analysis is a
truncation artefact.

## 3. ACTG2's own filament structure and its bound nucleotide

PDB **8V2O** (wild-type ACTG2 filament) chain **C** gives **67** inter-protomer interface residues under the same 4.0 A criterion, i.e. ACTG2 is observed as a multi-protomer filament in
its own right rather than inferred to be one. That count is deliberately not compared with
the 38 of section 2: 6DJO is a four-protomer reconstruction and 8V2O a five-protomer one, so the chosen chain has a different number of neighbours.
The comparable statement is the one in section 2, where the same 38 positions are scored
across every sequence.

Bound heteroatoms per ACTG2 structure, read from the coordinates:

| PDB | ligands (residue name: copies) |
|---|---|
| 8V2O | ADP: 5, HIC: 5, MG: 5 |
| 8V2Z | ADP: 5, HIC: 5, MG: 5 |
| 8V30 | ADP: 5, DTH: 5, EEP: 5, HIC: 5, HYP: 5, MG: 5 |

`HIC` is 4-methyl-histidine: the recombinant human-cell-expressed ACTG2 used for these
structures carries the SETD3 methyl mark at His74 in every protomer, which independently
corroborates UniProt's experimentally-evidenced MOD_RES 74. `DTH`/`EEP`/`HYP` in 8V30 are
phalloidin components, matching that structure's need for phalloidin to polymerise R40C.

Every ACTG2 filament structure carries **ADP**, not ATP, in every protomer. Following the
principle used by the merged ACTR1A/ACTR1B/ACTR5 reviews (annotate the ligand actually
observed), the directly-supported nucleotide term for ACTG2 is ADP binding; ATP binding is
supported instead by UniProt's own catalytic-activity reaction and by the source paper's
statement that hydrolysis happened during sample preparation.

The donor of ACTG2's ISS block is **P08023** = ACTA_CHICK, gene **ACTA2**, Gallus gallus, 377 aa (Swiss-Prot) - "Actin, aortic smooth muscle".
The chicken orthologue that was available and not used is **P63270** = ACTH_CHICK, gene **ACTG2**, 376 aa (Swiss-Prot).

- ACTG2 ISS rows from that donor (**6**): GO:0005737, GO:0010628, GO:0030027, GO:0030175, GO:0044297, GO:0090131
- human ACTA2 ISS rows from the same donor (**5**): GO:0010628, GO:0030027, GO:0030175, GO:0044297, GO:0090131
- shared (**5**): GO:0010628, GO:0030027, GO:0030175, GO:0044297, GO:0090131
- on ACTG2 only (**1**): GO:0005737

So the transfers are not unique to ACTG2: the donor's true human orthologue already
carries them, and the ACTG2 copies are the same inference aimed at the other smooth
muscle actin.
