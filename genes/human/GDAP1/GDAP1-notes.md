# GDAP1 biological notes

## 2026-11-05

The strongest mechanistic synthesis is membrane-associated organelle fission, with a single existing mitochondrial-fission core [PMID:16172208](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16172208/). The source-specific fusion and localization limits are retained in the annotation reasons and [selected evidence](GDAP1-external-evidence.md).

GST-fold similarity is not sufficient to choose a native catalytic function. Positive engineered conjugation and ligand-binding findings remain visible alongside their construct and assay limits [PMID:27841286](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27841286/), [PMID:42520938](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42520938/). Natural product 2 is sequence-supported; the HuRI identifier is not proof of an isoform-specific physiological role [PMID:15489334](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15489334/), [PMID:32296183](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32296183/).

Neuropathy genetics supplies disease context without converting every clinical consequence into GDAP1's direct work. Detailed source access and preservation records are kept in the companion evidence files.

## 2026-11-05 — source and compartment follow-up

Partner-class binding capacity and native encounter are separate questions. The positive screen pairs remain, with explicit membrane-access limits for CLPP and compartment limits for selected other partners. The [selected source evidence](GDAP1-external-evidence.md#locally-checkable-partner-class-excerpts) now supplies compact curated or primary anchors; primary TAGLN2 and BOK studies replace the earlier partner-GO rationale without assigning their activities to GDAP1.

The mitochondrial-fission core now uses only the mitochondrial outer membrane. Positive peroxisomal and liposome-deformation evidence remains in the [membrane source discussion](GDAP1-external-evidence.md#membrane-dynamics), with unresolved wild-type construct identity and translation-mixture limits. Three broad location terms are refined to established compartments. Native catalytic chemistry and endogenous ligand identity remain a biological question, distinct from those source-access limits.
