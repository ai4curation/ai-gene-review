Gene Description

BMPR1A (ALK3) is a single-pass cell-surface BMP receptor with an extracellular ligand-binding domain and an intracellular serine/threonine kinase domain. It recognizes BMP-family ligands in complexes with type II receptors; activation of its kinase couples extracellular signals to receptor-regulated SMADs. Ligand preference and output depend on receptor partners and cellular context. This signaling supports skeletal and cardiovascular development, differentiation and hepatic hepcidin regulation. BMPR1A also supplies type I receptor signaling in the AMH pathway during Müllerian-duct regression.
