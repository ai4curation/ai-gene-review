Gene Description

CASP7 is a short-prodomain executioner caspase that is synthesized as a cytosolic zymogen and activated by initiator caspases, granzyme B, CASP1, or calpain cleavage into an Asp-directed cysteine endopeptidase. In apoptosis, mature CASP7 cleaves PARP1, p23, YY1, claspin, and other nuclear and cytosolic substrates, using a positively charged N-terminal exosite that can bind RNA to accelerate PARP1 and RNA-binding-protein proteolysis. CASP7 is inhibited by IAP proteins such as XIAP/BIRC4 and BIRC6 and by PAK2 phosphorylation, and it also has non-apoptotic roles in inflammasome/NF-kB signaling and SMPD1 activation for membrane-pore repair.

Core Functions

CASP7 is an Asp-directed cysteine endopeptidase that is activated by intersubunit-linker cleavage and cleaves PARP1, p23, YY1, claspin, and other substrates during apoptotic execution.
