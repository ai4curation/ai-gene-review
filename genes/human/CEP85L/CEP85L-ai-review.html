Gene Description

CEP85L is a centrosomal protein concentrated around the proximal region of the mother centriole. It associates with CDK5 and helps retain this kinase at the centrosome, linking centrosomal protein organization to the microtubule cytoskeleton used during neuronal migration. CEP85L depletion disrupts cortical neuronal migration in mice and alters centrosomal CDK5 localization and phosphorylation in human cultured cells. Heterozygous pathogenic CEP85L variants cause posterior-predominant lissencephaly. The effects of individual alternative products and the molecular mechanism coupling CEP85L to CDK5 activation remain incompletely resolved.

Core Functions

CEP85L associates with CDK5 and supports its localization at the proximal mother-centriole region. This organizational role connects centrosomal migration-associated proteins to the microtubule organization used during cortical neuronal migration. Human cultured-cell association and recruitment assays complement mouse cortical knockdown/rescue evidence. The observed CDK5 phosphorylation changes support an activation model, while the direct molecular route and any purified binary interface remain unresolved.
