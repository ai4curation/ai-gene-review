CC   -!- FUNCTION: SNAREs (soluble N-ethylmaleimide-sensitive factor-attachment
CC       protein receptors) are essential proteins for intracellular membrane
CC       fusion. SNAREs localized on opposing membranes assemble to form a
CC       trans-SNARE complex, an extended, parallel four-helix bundle whose
CC       assembly releases energy that drives membrane fusion. The core SNARE
CC       complex typically consists of four alpha-helical domains, three from
CC       target membrane SNAREs (t-SNAREs) and one from a vesicle SNARE (v-
CC       SNARE) (PubMed:10036234, PubMed:30929742). VAMP2 is a v-SNARE
CC       assembling into a SNARE complex with the t-SNAREs SNAP25 and STX1A to
CC       mediate the fusion of synaptic vesicles with the presynaptic plasma
CC       membrane in neurotransmitter release (PubMed:30929742). Also functions
CC       in compensatory endocytosis for synaptic vesicle recycling (By
CC       similarity). In adipocytes, it is involved in the insulin-dependent
CC       exocytosis of GLUT4 storage vesicles (By similarity). Independently of
CC       its SNARE activity, it could modulate the gating characteristics of the
CC       delayed rectifier voltage-dependent potassium channel KCNB1 (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P63044,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P63045, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10036234,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30929742}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of the SNARE core complex containing SNAP25, VAMP2 and
CC       STX1A; this complex constitutes the basic catalytic machinery of the
CC       complex neurotransmitter release apparatus (By similarity). Recruited
CC       to the SNARE complex following binding of the SNARE complex component
CC       STX1A to STXBP1 (By similarity). This complex binds to CPLX1. Interacts
CC       with POPDC1 and STX4 (By similarity). Interacts with VAPA and VAPB.
CC       Interacts with WDFY2, PRKCZ and PRKCI (PubMed:17313651). Forms a
CC       complex with WDFY2 and PRKCZ (PubMed:17313651). Interacts (via N-
CC       terminus) with KCNB1 (via N-terminus and C-terminus); stimulates the
CC       channel inactivation rate of KCNB1 (By similarity). Interacts with
CC       SEPT8; the interaction inhibits interaction of VAMP2 with SYP.
CC       Interacts with SYP; the interaction is inhibited by interaction with
CC       SEPT8 (By similarity). Interacts with PICALM (PubMed:21808019,
CC       PubMed:22118466). Interacts with alpha-synuclein/SNCA
CC       (PubMed:20798282). Interacts with STX3 (By similarity). In vitro, forms
CC       a SNARE complex with the t-SNARE STX11 (PubMed:10036234).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P63044, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P63045,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10036234, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17313651,
