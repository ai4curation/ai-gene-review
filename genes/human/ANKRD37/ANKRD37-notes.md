- 158 aa, four ANK repeats and a putative NLS. It is cytoplasmic and nuclear: transfected in HEK293 [PMID:15809689], and in mouse it moves from the spermatid cytoplasm to the sperm nucleus [PMID:21723927]. HPA gives nucleoplasm and cytosol. All location rows are ACCEPT.
- Cytoplasm IBA: node PTN002794086, seeded by mouse Ankrd37 (MGI:3603344) and Q7Z713 itself, NO_FAILURE_CORE. The PTHR24201 PAINT files are already on main and unchanged.
- HIF-1 target [PMID:19491311]. Fem1b ubiquitinates mouse Ankrd37 and lowers its level [PMID:21723927]; an earlier version of that work (PMID:19171189) was withdrawn and is not cited.
- Phenotypes without mechanism: autophagy in RKO cells [PMID:32679233] and trophoblast invasion [PMID:35218282]. No GO term drawn from them.
- HuRI IPIs with HIF1AN (FIH) and ZNF76 are REMOVE. A possible FIH-hydroxylation link is raised as a question.
