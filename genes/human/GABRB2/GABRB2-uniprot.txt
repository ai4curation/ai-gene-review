CC   -!- FUNCTION: Beta subunit of the heteropentameric ligand-gated chloride
CC       channel gated by gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), a major inhibitory
CC       neurotransmitter in the brain (PubMed:19763268, PubMed:27789573,
CC       PubMed:29950725, PubMed:8264558). GABA-gated chloride channels, also
CC       named GABA(A) receptors (GABAAR), consist of five subunits arranged
CC       around a central pore and contain GABA active binding site(s) located
CC       at the alpha and beta subunit interface(s) (PubMed:29950725). When
CC       activated by GABA, GABAARs selectively allow the flow of chloride
CC       anions across the cell membrane down their electrochemical gradient (By
CC       similarity). Chloride influx into the postsynaptic neuron following
CC       GABAAR opening decreases the neuron ability to generate a new action
CC       potential, thereby reducing nerve transmission (By similarity). GABAARs
CC       containing alpha-1 and beta-2 or -3 subunits exhibit synaptogenic
CC       activity; the gamma-2 subunit being necessary but not sufficient to
CC       induce rapid synaptic contacts formation (PubMed:23909897,
CC       PubMed:25489750). Extrasynaptic beta-2 receptors contribute to the
CC       tonic GABAergic inhibition (By similarity). Beta-containing GABAARs can
CC       simultaneously bind GABA and histamine where histamine binds at the
CC       interface of two neighboring beta subunits, which may be involved in
CC       the regulation of sleep and wakefulness (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08219, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P63138,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19763268, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23909897,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25489750, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27789573,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29950725, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8264558}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heteropentamer, formed by a combination of alpha (GABRA1-6),
CC       beta (GABRB1-3), gamma (GABRG1-3), delta (GABRD), epsilon (GABRE), rho
CC       (GABRR1-3), pi (GABRP) and theta (GABRQ) chains, each subunit
CC       exhibiting distinct physiological and pharmacological properties
CC       (PubMed:29950725, PubMed:8264558). Interacts with UBQLN1 (By
CC       similarity). May interact with KIF21B (By similarity). Identified in a
CC       complex of 720 kDa composed of LHFPL4, NLGN2, GABRA1, GABRB2, GABRG2
CC       and GABRB3 (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P63138,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29950725, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8264558}.
