# GABRB2: evidence and source scope

GABRB2 encodes a beta subunit of the ionotropic GABA-A receptor. Human recombinant structures and functional comparisons identify its contribution to GABA-binding interfaces and the shared chloride pore; native human brain assemblies independently establish beta2 membership. The central mechanism is receptor-mediated chloride conductance and postsynaptic signaling. The review keeps one core function and adds no annotation.

The [review](GABRB2-ai-review.yaml) contains all current judgments, the core and 40 individual reference assessments. The [source archive](GABRB2-source-evidence.yaml) preserves all 66 original assertions, literal source contexts and dates, four current product sequences, seven actual IBD placements and the urinary-fraction extension. It does not repeat the current decisions, core or reference assessments.

## Receptor mechanism and natural products

Selected original [PMID:29950725](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29950725/) Results and Methods establish human alpha1/beta2/gamma2 receptor architecture with functional comparisons. The cryo-EM construct has engineered intracellular loops, so it is not assigned to a current natural splice accession. Selected native human-brain [PMID:39843743](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39843743/) Results/Methods establish beta2-containing and mixed beta2/beta3 assemblies in alpha1-selected neurosurgical samples. Mixed positions, sample selection and unresolved splice identities limit extrapolation; no direct beta2–neuroligin interaction or beta2/beta2 lamotrigine site is inferred.

Official sequence records identify displayed P47870-2 as 512 residues, P47870-1 as 474, P47870-3 as 313 and P47870-4 as 372. All four direct FASTAs and feature-based reconstructions agree. The original [PMID:19763268](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19763268/) instead reports short experimental forms of 314 and 373 residues. These historical lengths are preserved without silently equating them to the current sequences. Human-brain transcript observations, engineered minigenes, tagged receptor assays and T365A comparisons have distinct scopes. The short experimental forms lack TM4 but produced currents with alpha1; domain architecture alone does not establish functional loss.
