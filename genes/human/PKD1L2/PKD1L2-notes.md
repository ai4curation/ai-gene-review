# PKD1L2 notes

Automated deep research could not be generated for this review because the configured provider was unavailable, so this note records manual curation context only.

PKD1L2 is a large multi-pass polycystin-family membrane protein. UniProt describes only a tentative G protein-coupled receptor role and a possible interaction of the C terminus with GNAS and GNAI1; the entry does not cite a direct PKD1L2 ion-channel assay. The current GOA rows are therefore dominated by family-level propagation from the PTHR10877 root and InterPro mappings to the polycystin domain.

The root PANTHER membrane assertion is safe for PKD1L2 because the canonical protein has many predicted transmembrane helices. The root calcium-channel and mechanical-stimulus assertions are not safe for this subfamily: the well-characterized channel activities are in PKD2, PKD2L1, and the PKD1L3/PKD2L1 heteromer, while PKD1L2 has no comparable direct calcium-channel or mechanotransduction evidence.

The IPR000922 automatic carbohydrate-binding row is plausible as a non-core domain annotation for the N-terminal lectin-like module. IPR003915-derived `calcium ion binding`, however, should not be kept as evidence that the poorly characterized PKD1L2 protein itself binds calcium.
