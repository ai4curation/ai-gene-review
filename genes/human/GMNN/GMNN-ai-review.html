Gene Description

Geminin restricts DNA replication licensing by binding CDT1 and preventing productive MCM engagement. It also regulates transcription through AP4-associated repression and selected Hox proteins. APC/C-dependent degradation during mitosis limits its abundance. Stabilizing GMNN variants can cause autosomal-dominant Meier-Gorlin syndrome, linking replication control to human growth.
