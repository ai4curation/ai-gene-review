Gene Description

Geminin restricts DNA replication licensing by binding and inhibiting CDT1. It also regulates transcription through promoter-associated repressor complexes and inhibition of selected Hox proteins. Its abundance and subcellular distribution vary with cell-cycle and binding-partner context.
