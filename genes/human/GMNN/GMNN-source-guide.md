# GMNN source guide

Selected primary Methods, Results and figure descriptions were inspected. Cached-text availability and privately accessible originals are separate facts; normal caches are preserved. No claim is made to have read every cited paper in full.

- **PMID:11125146**: The author-hosted original and Figure 3–4 were inspected. Human Geminin is full-length; functional licensing assays use Xenopus extracts.
- **PMID:16924111**: Mouse constructs and endogenous human assays were separated; see Methods and AP4–Gem–SMRT results.
- **PMID:19906994**: Human fragments and Xenopus extracts support inhibition. Oligomeric switching is a model, not a demonstrated universal endogenous state.
- **PMID:20129055**: Flag-GemL26A occupies origins by ChIP. Geminin inhibits the CDT1–HBO1 complex without directly binding HBO1.
- **PMID:21543332**: Geminin-GFP localization depends on Idas coexpression; this does not determine constitutive endogenous distribution.
- **PMID:22615398**: HOXC9 structural and DNA-competition experiments differ from full-length HOXD9 reporter assays.
- **PMID:24217620**: FUCCI uses a Geminin fragment; the nuclear claim is corroborated independently.
- **PMID:41365879**: Purified human assays and binding-preserving truncations support CDT1 sequestration from MCM2. Routine CDT1ΔN and separate full-length experiments remain distinct; the steric model is docking-informed and native stoichiometry unresolved.
- **PMID:25416956**: Human ORF interactions were screened in yeast and retested pairwise. The exact current partner records preserve fragment and isoform labels; study-wide orthogonal validation is not assigned individually to these pairs.
- **PMID:31515488**: The variant study compares mutant and wild-type interactions in yeast. Its GMNN feature records remain separate from the unsuffixed GO annotation; no disease effect or assayed canonical-sequence equivalence is inferred.
- **PMID:32296183**: Human ORFs were tested in yeast; the recorded fragments and isoform labels remain distinct.
- **PMID:33961781**: Original tables corroborate all three incoming pairs in HEK293T, with directed bait/prey roles. These are co-purification associations, not independent demonstrations of direct contact. The replication-complex extension remains literal.

Access limits: PMID:9635433 and PMID:17318181 remain abstract-only in this reading. Positive independently established functions are retained without reconstructing unavailable assays. The exact PMID:23708001 contact remains a question.

Current HPA XML reports supported main nucleoplasm localization and additional cytosol/basal-body locations. Reactome event participants retain their own compartments even when an event-level compartment differs. Leaf-detail requests returned HTTP 403; complete accession-level leaf replay is not claimed.

GO:0140311 describes binding that prevents partner engagement and is the selected mechanistic refinement for CDT1. It descends from GO:0140313, not GO:0140678; no ontology ancestry between those two alternatives is asserted. CDT1 remains a licensing factor, not an enzyme. GO:0140416 describes prevention of transcription-regulator chromatin engagement; AP4-associated corepression remains distinct.
