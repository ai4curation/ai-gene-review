Gene Description

RPGRIP1L (also called NPHP8, MKS5 or FTM) is a large coiled-coil and C2-domain protein of the ciliary transition zone, related to the photoreceptor protein RPGRIP1. It sits at the basal body and transition zone, distal to CEP290, and binds NPHP4 directly as part of the NPHP1-NPHP4-NPHP8 module. RPGRIP1L acts as the upstream scaffold for assembling the transition zone: MKS- and NPHP-module proteins depend on it to reach the transition zone and form the ciliary diffusion barrier. In photoreceptors it binds RPGR at the connecting cilium, and the common A229T variant, which weakens RPGR binding, modifies retinal degeneration. Loss of RPGRIP1L causes Joubert syndrome, Meckel syndrome and nephronophthisis.

Core Functions

RPGRIP1L is the upstream assembly hub of the ciliary transition zone. It localizes to the TZ distal to CEP290 and binds NPHP4 directly within the NPHP1-NPHP4-NPHP8 module. It is required for recruitment of both MKS- and NPHP-module proteins (and, in worms, CEP-290) to the TZ, and so for TZ gate formation and cilium-dependent signalling. In photoreceptors it partners RPGR at the connecting cilium. It has no characterized enzymatic activity.
