## Key findings
- Binds NPHP4 directly within the NPHP1-4-8 module: [PMID:21565611 "We find that NPHP4 directly binds both NPHP1 and NPHP8 in vitro"]
- TZ position distal to CEP290: [PMID:28401750 "RPGRIP1L was distal to CEP290 and presented a similar width."]
- Assembly hub (worm ortholog MKS-5): [PMID:26982032 "MKS-5 (mammalian Rprgrip1L/Rpgrip1 orthologue) is essential for the assembly of all known TZ proteins (including CEP-290)"]
- MKS and NPHP modules rely on it for TZ recruitment: [PMID:28401750 "components of both the MKS and NPHP modules which rely on it for their recruitment to the TZ"]
- RPGR binding; the A229T modifier weakens it: [PMID:19430481 "the GFP-tagged A229T mutant protein showed considerably lower (∼80%) ability to immunoprecipitate RPGR"]
- TBXA2R binding (single overexpression study): [PMID:19464661 "We found KIAA1005 as a novel interacting protein of the TP alpha and TP beta C-terminal region"]
