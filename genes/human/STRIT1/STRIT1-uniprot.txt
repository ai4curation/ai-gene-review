CC   -!- FUNCTION: Enhances the activity of ATP2A1/SERCA1 ATPase in sarcoplasmic
CC       reticulum by displacing ATP2A1/SERCA1 inhibitors, thereby acting as a
CC       key regulator of skeletal muscle activity (By similarity). Also
CC       enhances the activity of the ATP2A2/SERCA2 ATPase (PubMed:33581112).
CC       Does not directly stimulate SERCA pump activity (By similarity). Binds
CC       preferentially to the phosphorylated E1 and E2 conformational forms of
CC       ATP2A2 which predominate at high Ca(2+) concentrations during the
CC       systolic phase of the cardiac cycle (By similarity). Competes with
CC       ATP2A2 inhibitor phospholamban (PLN) for binding to ATP2A2 and
CC       displaces PLN (PubMed:33581112). Can activate ATP2A2 directly in the
CC       absence of PLN (PubMed:33581112). Also enhances sarcoplasmic reticulum
CC       Ca(2+) uptake and myocyte contractility by displacing the SERCA
CC       inhibitory peptides sarcolipin (SLN) and myoregulin (MRLN) (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P0DN83,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33581112}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homooligomer (PubMed:36523160). Can also form heterooligomers
CC       with other sarcoplasmic/endoplasmic reticulum calcium ATPase (SERCA)
CC       regulators ARLN, ERLN, PLN and SLN (PubMed:36523160). Monomer (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with ATP2A1/SERCA1; the interaction results in
CC       activation of ATP2A1 (By similarity). Interacts as a monomer with
CC       ATP2A2/SERCA2; the interaction results in activation of ATP2A2
CC       (PubMed:33581112, PubMed:36523160). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P0DN83,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33581112, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36523160}.
