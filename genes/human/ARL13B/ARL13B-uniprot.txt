CC   -!- FUNCTION: Cilium-specific protein required to control the microtubule-
CC       based, ciliary axoneme structure. May act by maintaining the
CC       association between IFT subcomplexes A and B. Binds GTP but is not able
CC       to hydrolyze fit; the GTPase activity remains unclear. Required to
CC       pattern the neural tube. Involved in cerebral cortex development:
CC       required for the initial formation of a polarized radial glial
CC       scaffold, the first step in the construction of the cerebral cortex, by
CC       regulating ciliary signaling. Regulates the migration and placement of
CC       postmitotic interneurons in the developing cerebral cortex. Plays a
CC       role in ciliar trafficking of phosphatidylinositol phosphatase INPP5E
CC       in ciliogenesis (PubMed:38219074). May regulate ARF6- and RAB22A-
CC       dependent endocytic recycling traffic (PubMed:23223633).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23150559, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23223633,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38219074}.

CC       ARL13B localization to the cilium membrane. Interacts with actin; the
