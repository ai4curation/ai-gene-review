# EIF2B3 evidence notes

EIF2B3 encodes the gamma subunit of cytosolic eIF2B. Gamma associates with epsilon and helps assemble the exchange-factor complex that regenerates eIF2–GTP for translation initiation. Human tagged-subunit assembly, patient-cell exchange assays and reconstituted human complexes support this contribution. They do not establish that isolated human gamma is an autonomous guanine-nucleotide exchange enzyme. The integrated core therefore records contribution to GEF activity in the eIF2B complex, with cytoplasmic translation initiation and cytosol as its process and functional location. [PMID:11323413](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M011788200), [PMID:15054402](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.ejhg.5201189), [PMID:15060152](https://doi.org/10.1128/MCB.24.8.3295-3306.2004), [PMID:29599213](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaq0939).
