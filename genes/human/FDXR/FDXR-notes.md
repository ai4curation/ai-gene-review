# FDXR notes

## 2026-10-26 — integrated functional synthesis

FDXR transfers electrons from NADPH to ferredoxins. One ferredoxin-NADP+ reductase activity accounts for electron supply to mitochondrial steroidogenic cytochrome P450 enzymes and the ferredoxin-dependent COQ6 reactions in ubiquinone biosynthesis. These are contributions of the reductase itself, rather than an inference from its being required for a downstream phenotype. The activity-centered core therefore uses GO:0004324 with the two existing biosynthetic-process terms. The mechanistic COQ6 reconstitution used reconstructed tetrapod-ancestral FDXR/FDX2 and short-chain intermediates; it is not described as a purified extant-human assay or evidence that FDXR forms a stable metabolon (PMID:2845396; PMID:38425362; PMID:38885337).

Human endogenous imaging and exact MitoCoP P22570-containing target groups support mitochondrial residence. The core uses the organelle-level location. Retained matrix and inner-membrane annotations carry their individual Reactome-model and yeast-ortholog inference boundaries. Human confocal imaging also reports nuclear/cytoplasmic signal; mitochondrial residence is not exclusive localization (PMID:29040572; PMID:34800366; PMID:9727014).

All seven curated splice-product objects are retained. The numbering in PMID:41511346 differs from UniProt: paper isoform 7 corresponds to P22570-4, while paper isoform 4 corresponds to P22570-7. In tagged MCF7 experiments the former is predominantly cytosolic, while paper isoforms 1 and 4 overlap MitoTracker. Altered electrophoretic mobility and targeting do not establish an exact endogenous cleavage site, equivalent activity of all products, or a defined cytosolic substrate. Knockout effects on DNA-damage responses do not by themselves establish direct DNA-repair activity. The exact sequence and source joins are in [FDXR-isoform-source-joins.yaml](FDXR-isoform-source-joins.yaml).

The clinical description is limited to biallelic FDXR-associated mitochondrial disease with optic atrophy and neurological impairment, supported by the patient study. Its corrigendum changes three family genotype labels in Figure 1 and does not describe a correction to Figure 2 localization. The later adrenal-like-cell and mouse findings support steroid physiology while leaving enzyme-specific versus secondary redox effects separate (PMID:29040572; PMID:29554255; PMID:38885337).
