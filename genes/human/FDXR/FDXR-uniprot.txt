CC       mitochondrial P450 systems including cholesterol side chain cleavage in
CC       all steroidogenic tissues, steroid 11-beta hydroxylation in the adrenal
CC       cortex, 25-OH-vitamin D3-24 hydroxylation in the kidney, and sterol C-
CC       27 hydroxylation in the liver (By similarity). Also acts as a
CC       ferredoxin--NADP(+) reductase essential for coenzyme Q biosynthesis:
CC       together with FDX2, transfers the electrons required for the
CC       hydroxylation reaction performed by COQ6 (PubMed:38425362).
