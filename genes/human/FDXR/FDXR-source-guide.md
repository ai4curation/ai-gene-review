# FDXR evidence guide

The review combines one NADPH-dependent ferredoxin-reduction activity with its electron-supply roles in steroid and ubiquinone biosynthesis. All source annotations and seven curated splice products are retained. Reference-only citations identify evidence without claiming a new verbatim quotation was validated.

| Claim | Original evidence and precise scope | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Ferredoxin-NADP+ reductase activity and steroid electron supply | [PMID:2845396](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2845396/), original PNAS pages 7104–7108, especially page 7104 electron-transfer description and the cDNA/splicing Results. [PMID:38885337](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38885337/), Introduction/Figure 1A and Results on patient-derived adrenal-like cells and the Fdxr mutant mouse. | The cloning paper does not compare the two splice enzymes. Steroid output in patient cells and mice is not a purified enzyme-specific assay. |

The measured electron-transfer role supports conserved participation; no stable FDXR complex membership is asserted. |
| Human mitochondrial localization | [PMID:29040572](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29040572/), FDXR/MitoTracker Results and Figure 2C–D/Methods.
