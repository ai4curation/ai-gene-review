# ACP1 manual review notes

## Evidence pass

- `just fetch-gene human ACP1` seeded 20 GOA rows in `ACP1-ai-review.yaml`, and `ACP1-goa.tsv` has 21 lines including the header. The fetch also added the ACP1-specific PMID caches for PMID:10336608, PMID:1587862, PMID:9038134, PMID:9499402 and PMID:9705307.
- Automated deep research did not produce a provider report in this checkout: the Falcon provider could not run because `agentapi` was unavailable, and the family Falcon fallback had neither an Edison nor Perplexity client/key. I therefore used the seeded UniProt/GOA/PANTHER records, PAINT, and cached publications for this family pass.
- PTHR11717 has one reviewed human member, ACP1/P24666, in PTHR11717:SF34. `just fetch-panther-paint PTHR11717` recorded one positive IBD, `PTN000197212 -> GO:0004725`, with ACP1 itself and bacterial, yeast, fission yeast, Drosophila and rat seeds. The only negative PAINT rows in the fetched table are Drosophila `IRD` calls at `PTN000197306` and `PTN002271066`, so no loss blocks the human ACP1 lineage.
- The UniProt FUNCTION and CATALYTIC ACTIVITY lines support the two active human isoforms: isoforms 1 and 2 hydrolyze O-phospho-L-tyrosyl-protein and generic phosphate monoesters, whereas isoform 3 lacks phosphatase activity. This matches PMID:1587862 for the active A/B products and PMID:10336608 for the inactive C splice form.
- PMID:9705307 is abstract-only in the local cache. Its abstract directly identifies the two human LMW-PTP isoenzymes as phosphotyrosine phosphatases and uses the structure to explain substrate-specificity differences in the variable loop, so it supports ACP1's protein-tyrosine phosphatase function.
- PMID:9038134 is abstract-only in the local cache. The abstract reports that Lck thiophosphorylation caused a twofold activation of wild-type LMPTP and that Tyr-131 is important for catalytic activity. It supports the active LMPTP-B enzyme, while UniProt's curated catalytic-activity line ties the paper specifically to the generic acid-phosphatase reaction.
- PMID:10940933 is abstract-only in the local cache, but the Wiley abstract states that most of the studied intracellular PTPases were found in cytosol and many were enriched at the plasma membrane. I also checked the full-text copy surfaced by the DOI/PMID: in the LMPTP paragraph it states "LMPTP was recovered exclusively in the soluble fraction after ultracentrifugation" and describes "this preferential location close to the plasma membrane." The endogenous localization therefore supports cytoplasm as the stable compartment and cytoplasmic-side-of-plasma-membrane as a real but peripheral/recruitable signal.
- PMID:11971983 was not in the ACP1 GOA source list but was fetched to evaluate the orthology-based `GO:0017124` SH3 domain binding row. The abstract says the alpha II-spectrin SH3 domain specifically binds the ACP1/LMW-PTP A isoform and not the B or C isoforms.
- PMID:9499402 directly reports recruitment of LMW-PTP to EphB1 receptor complexes; PMID:15846844 and PMID:25556234 describe broad viral-protein interaction screens. All three rows assert only bare `GO:0005515`, so they are removed by the generic-protein-binding policy without claiming that the reported interaction is false.
- PMID:19056867 and PMID:23533145 are exosome proteomics studies. The available cached text describes high-throughput urinary or EPS-urine exosome proteome surveys but does not name ACP1 in the body text, consistent with the GOA rows coming from supplementary tables.
- QuickGO showed that the rat GO:0007268 source rows on UniProtKB:P41498 are RGD IEP and NAS rows from PMID:10480315. That PubMed abstract reports abundance of rat LMW-PTP in nerve-ending fractions and suggests an important role in synaptic function, but the full rat source was not read here, so the Ensembl-Compara GO:0007268 transfer and its derived GOC GO:0045202 row are left undecided.
- PMID:10336608 shows that LMPTP-B inhibits PDGF-induced c-fos induction, and PMID:9499402 reports EphB1-dependent LMW-PTP recruitment to receptor complexes. Those leads were preserved as gaps/questions rather than a NEW PDGF or Eph biological-process annotation because the abstract-only records do not identify an endogenous direct substrate in human cells.

## Family/module scope

PTHR11717 is coherent for low-molecular-weight protein-tyrosine phosphatase activity, but it is small and has only one reviewed human member in this pass, so there is no multi-part human module to add. No cached GO-CAM names ACP1/P24666.
