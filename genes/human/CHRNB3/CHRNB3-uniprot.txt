CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of neuronal acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) that
CC       function as pentameric, ligand-gated cation channels with high calcium
CC       permeability among other activities. nAChRs are excitatory
CC       neurotrasnmitter receptors formed by a collection of nAChR subunits
CC       known to mediate synaptic transmission in the nervous system and the
CC       neuromuscular junction. Each nAchR subunit confers differential
CC       attributes to channel properties, including activation, deactivation
CC       and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, cation permeability, and
CC       binding to allosteric modulators (PubMed:11118490, PubMed:16835356,
CC       PubMed:35889515). Has an accessory rather than functional role and is
CC       only able to form functional nAChRs when co-assembled with another beta
CC       subunit (PubMed:11118490, PubMed:16835356). Participates in pentameric
CC       assemblies along with CHRNA3, CHRNA4, CHRNA6, CHRNB2 and CHRNB4
CC       (PubMed:11118490, PubMed:16835356). Modulates receptor assembly and
CC       increases receptor sensitivity to nicotine when associated with CHRNB2,
CC       CHRNA4 and/or CHRNA6 as well as CHRNA3 and CHRNB4 (PubMed:11118490,
CC       PubMed:16835356). Seems to play a role in nicotine addiction
CC       (PubMed:11118490, PubMed:16835356). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11118490,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16835356, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35889515}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by a myriad of ligands such as
CC       acetylcholine, cytisine, nicotine, choline and epibatidine.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11118490, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16835356}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Neuronal AChR seems to be composed of two different type of
CC       subunits: alpha and beta (PubMed:11118490, PubMed:35889515).
CC       CHRNB3/beta-3 subunit is only able to form functional nAChRs when co-
CC       assembled with another beta subunit. Participates in pentameric
CC       assemblies along with CHRNA4/alpha-4 and CHRNB2/beta-2 subunits and
CC       with CHRNA6/alpha-6 as well, forming stoichiometries such as
CC       (CHRNA3:CHRNB4)2:CHRNB3, (CHRNA4:CHRNB2)2:CHRNB3 or
CC       (CHRNA6:CHRNB2)2:CHRNB3 (PubMed:11118490, PubMed:16835356).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11118490, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16835356,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35889515}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Synaptic cell membrane
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O70174}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000255}. Cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O70174}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
