Gene Description

Human beta3 is an accessory subunit incorporated alongside principal alpha and complementary beta subunits. Reporter-mutant studies support one beta3 in recombinant alpha3beta4beta3; beta3-containing alpha6beta2 concatamers provide a distinct functional assembly. Beta3 alone is not a channel or a conventional principal acetylcholine-binding site. These pentameric membrane proteins couple extracellular acetylcholine recognition to cation flux and neuronal excitability. Beta3 influences receptor assembly, expression and pharmacology and contributes to dopaminergic terminal receptor populations.
