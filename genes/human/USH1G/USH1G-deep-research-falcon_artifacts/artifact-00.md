| Structural element or feature | Binding partner/process | Operating compartment | Evidence and functional consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Overall 461-aa, ~52-kDa SANS protein** | Multivalent assembly of Usher and spliceosomal protein complexes | Cytoplasm, sensory cilia, hair-cell stereocilia and nucleus | **Non-enzymatic scaffold/adaptor—not an enzyme or transporter.** Its primary function is to organize, position and transfer protein–RNA complexes (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000018) |
| **Three N-terminal ankyrin repeats** | Direct binding to IFT52 and IFT57; association with other IFT-B components | Photoreceptor ciliary base/periciliary region | Yeast two-hybrid, pull-down/capture and cellular assays support direct binding; *Ush1g* knockout reduced IFT20, IFT52 and IFT57 abundance in the mouse photoreceptor ciliary compartment, implicating SANS in IFT-B positioning rather than transport catalysis (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000011) |
| **Central region: CENTn1, CENTn2 and CENTc; partly intrinsically disordered** | MYO7A binding and linkage of Usher complexes to cytoskeletal/trafficking machinery | Hair-cell stereocilia and retinal cytoplasm/periciliary apparatus | The central region binds the N-terminal MyTH4–FERM region of MYO7A; SANS thereby contributes to hair-bundle cohesion and mechanosensitive tip-link-complex assembly, with mutant mice showing disorganized bundles, hearing impairment and vestibular dysfunction (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000014) |
| **CENTn1/CENTn2 interaction surfaces** | Sequential/competitive interactions with PRPF31 and PRPF6 | Nucleus—Cajal bodies and nuclear speckles | FRET and structure prediction mapped PRPF31 mainly to CENTn1 and PRPF6 to CENTn1/CENTn2; PRPF6 binding is predicted to convert an intrinsically disordered CENTn2 “Janus” segment into a short α-helix. Pathogenic variants perturb these interactions (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000007) |
| **Central-region-mediated spliceosomal scaffold activity** | Transfer of U4/U6.U5 tri-snRNP complexes; spliceosome assembly and possible snRNP recycling | From Cajal bodies to nuclear speckles | SANS depletion delays spliceosome assembly and causes complex-A accumulation, altering constitutive and alternative splicing. The evidence supports a carrier/scaffold role in intranuclear tri-snRNP transfer, not RNA catalysis by SANS itself (pqac-00000006) |
| **SAM domain plus C-terminal class-I PDZ-binding motif** | Harmonin/USH1C binding and assembly of the Usher protein network | Hair-cell stereocilia, cytoplasmic/periciliary complexes and SANS condensates | Harmonin binds the SANS SAM–PDZ-binding region; this interaction supports multivalent hair-bundle/tip-link scaffolding, and harmonin overexpression enlarges dynamic SANS condensates (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000021) |
| **NLS at residues 213–224; candidate NLS at 436–447** | Karyopherin-dependent nuclear import | Nuclear envelope, nucleoplasm, Cajal bodies and nuclear speckles | K213E significantly reduced nuclear localization in HeLa and HEK293T cells; R447W had cell-type-dependent effects. Wild-type tagged SANS was approximately 70% nuclear under the tested conditions, indicating regulated rather than exclusively nuclear localization (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000022, pqac-00000023) |
| **NES at residues 181–195** | CRM1/exportin-dependent nuclear export | Nucleus-to-cytoplasm shuttling | L195E significantly increased nuclear enrichment and nuclear droplets; leptomycin-B experiments supported CRM1-dependent export (pqac-00000019, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000022) |
| **NES at residues 406–420 within the SAM region** | Nuclear export and maintenance of cytoplasmic SANS availability | Nucleus-to-cytoplasm shuttling | Truncating or frameshift variants lacking this NES—notably S243* and S278Pfs*71—were significantly enriched in nuclei, potentially coupling defective nuclear RNA processing with loss of cytoplasmic/ciliary scaffold function (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000021) |
| **Dynamic multivalent/condensate-forming behavior** | PRPF31- and harmonin-associated assemblies; proposed liquid–liquid phase separation | Nuclear speckles, Cajal bodies and cytoplasmic/ciliary-associated puncta | Mobile SANS puncta were distinguished from aggresomes and enlarged with PRPF31 or harmonin overexpression. Condensation is a plausible organizing mechanism, but its physiological necessity remains less established than the interaction and localization data (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000019) |
