# FAM20C integrated evidence and source guide

These are agent-authored source notes. The 584-residue natural product and the 252-residue product lacking residues 1–332 remain separate from the propeptide, mature 93–584 chain and engineered kinase fragments. None of the reviewed assays establishes equivalent activity or localization of both natural products.

## Catalysis and substrate recognition

[PMID:22582013](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1217817), Figures 1–3, identifies human FAM20C as a secretory-pathway kinase, with WT/D478A controls, casein phosphorylation and matrix-protein substrates. The main article reports a serine preference with weaker threonine/tyrosine phosphorylation. Its supplemental Methods, Table S1 and Figure S5 were not recovered. The exact antithrombin/SERPINC1 binding context therefore remains unresolved. Independently measured APOL1, CHGB and fibrinogen phosphorylation in the 2015 study provides separately labeled support for those partners; it is not a claim to have read the missing 2012 table.

[PMID:23754375](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1309211110), main Figures 2/3 and Supporting Information Figure S5, separates a worm ceFam20 crystal from human FAM20C biochemical assays. The human S5A/B panels compare Mn-ATP and Mg-ATP kinetics. The structure demonstrates the conserved metal-binding arrangement in the worm protein, while human activity and mutational experiments support its functional relevance. No human manganese-bound crystal or measured cellular metal occupancy is claimed.

[PMID:25789606](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.06120), Figures 1–5 and Methods, tests human enamel proteins and purified FAM20A–FAM20C preparations. AMTN, AMBN and ENAM phosphorylation is supported by kinase-dead and substrate-site controls. FAM20A is the activating pseudokinase; FAM20C performs phosphate transfer. Cellular co-IP, co-elution and a purified 2:2 assembly do not establish universal native stoichiometry. Mouse ameloblast-like cells and mammary tissue remain distinct from human U2OS and recombinant human proteins.

[PMID:26091039](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2015.05.028), Figures 4/5 and Supplemental Experimental Procedures, provides target-specific substrate tests and cellular phosphoproteomics. Actual main Figure 5F identifies IGFBP1 Thr193 **and** Ser194 in the m/z 669.74 peptide. Supplemental Figure S6F examines a different peptide and has a Thr193-or-Ser194 ambiguity plus Ser199. The latter does not cancel the main threonine result. Corresponding short peptides were negative under the tested conditions, so substrate context matters. Purchased fibrinogen provenance is not a sequence-verified isolated human α-chain construct.

The supported generic substrate interactions are retained as non-core under the standing campaign exception. Phosphorylation can establish transient enzyme–substrate engagement without establishing stable binding affinity or a new adaptor activity. Association with FAM20A, S1P or COPII proteins likewise does not transfer those partners' regulatory, protease or coat functions to FAM20C.

Current [peptidyl-serine phosphorylation](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0018105) and [peptidyl-threonine phosphorylation](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0018107) are non-ancestral descendants of protein phosphorylation under the inspected is_a/part_of closure. They refine the broad process using directly performed chemistry. The separately retained serine and serine/threonine kinase terms are not treated as ontology synonyms.

## Secretory-pathway pools and processing

[PMID:29858230](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.201798699), Figures 2–4 and Methods, demonstrates FAM20C-dependent Ero1α Ser145 phosphorylation using purified human kinase and WT/D478A/substrate controls. Ero1α performs the oxidoreductase reaction. The Ero1α-KDEL substrate and separate-coculture experiments do not by themselves identify a FAM20C ER-retention signal or exclude every extracellular reaction.

[PMID:32149426](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.2019103841), Figures 1–4, Methods and Table EV1, identifies PDI Ser357 phosphorylation and reversible WT FAM20C-mApple redistribution from Golgi to ER during thapsigargin stress in human HepG2 cells. Engineered FAM20C-KDEL retention is a separate experiment. WT stress localization plus independent precursor topology supports an ER membrane pool, without proving a freely soluble mature 93–584 ER species.

[PMID:34349020](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2100133118), Figures 1–6 and Supporting Information Methods, distinguishes intracellular membrane-associated protein from secreted Asp93-starting protein. Human-cell extraction and fluorescence-protease-protection experiments support a lumen-facing kinase domain. S1P performs proteolytic processing; FAM20C performs phosphorylation, including controlled Ser106 autophosphorylation. ΔTM, ΔPP, 23–584, 141–578 and engineered HRV3C-site constructs are not the natural 252-residue product. SEC23A/SEC24A association and RUSH transport establish cargo behavior, not a FAM20C coat role. Human HeLa/HEK293T trafficking and mouse MC3T3-E1 mineralization are separate contexts.

## Integration and core representation

The two complete source lanes are combined without changing imported source fields,
reference identities, findings or natural-product objects. Shared reference assessments
retain both labeled read scopes verbatim, including different supplementary and pixel
coverage. The sole review overlay further specifies the Reactome parent-process row
as peptidyl-serine and peptidyl-threonine phosphorylation, using the same human main
Figure 5F evidence. It does not claim that either process is a current typed descendant
of post-translational protein modification.

One catalytic core represents the serine/threonine reactions and the independently
supported serine-specific reaction. The currently separate GO branches do not turn
overlapping chemistry of the same enzyme into two biological activities. The core
uses GO:0004674 because its definition includes both measured reactions; the narrower
source serine annotations remain intact. Mn/Mg-supported catalysis is a facet of this
unit. Positive mouse calcium binding remains non-core and is not converted into
calcium-dependent kinase activity.

The two protein-phosphorylation processes are non-ancestral under the captured current
is_a/part_of closure. Enamel mineralization is additionally retained because FAM20C
performs phosphate transfer to enamel matrix proteins, supported by human biochemical
controls and conserved enamel phenotypes. The broader biomineral-tissue process is
not duplicated in the core. FAM20A activation, S1P cleavage, COPII transport and the
redox/chaperone activities of substrates remain partner roles rather than extra
FAM20C cores.

Core locations distinguish the membrane precursor's lumen-facing kinase domain from
secreted active preparations. The extracellular location does not establish when every
substrate is phosphorylated relative to secretion. The unresolved soluble mature
93–584 ER-lumen model remains an explicit question; neither it nor engineered KDEL
retention is silently promoted to a demonstrated native processing state. Natural
252-product expression, localization and activity remain separate open questions.
