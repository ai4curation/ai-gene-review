CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the coat protein complex II (COPII) which
CC       promotes the formation of transport vesicles from the endoplasmic
CC       reticulum (ER) (PubMed:10214955, PubMed:17499046, PubMed:18843296,
CC       PubMed:20427317, PubMed:41887218). The coat has two main functions, the
CC       physical deformation of the endoplasmic reticulum membrane into
CC       vesicles and the selection of cargo molecules for their transport to
CC       the Golgi complex (PubMed:10214955, PubMed:17499046, PubMed:18843296,
CC       PubMed:20427317, PubMed:41887218). Plays a central role in cargo
CC       selection within the COPII complex and together with SEC24D may have a
CC       different specificity compared to SEC24A and SEC24B (PubMed:17499046,
CC       PubMed:18843296, PubMed:20427317, PubMed:41887218). May more
CC       specifically package GPI-anchored proteins through the cargo receptor
CC       TMED10 (PubMed:20427317). May also be specific for IxM motif-containing
CC       cargos like the SNAREs GOSR2 and STX5 (PubMed:18843296).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10214955, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17499046,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18843296, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20427317}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: COPII is composed of at least five proteins: the Sec23/24
CC       complex, the Sec13/31 complex and Sar1 (PubMed:10075675,
CC       PubMed:10214955, PubMed:17499046). Interacts with TMED2 and TMED10
CC       (PubMed:20427317). Interacts with GOSR2 (via IxM motif) and STX5 (via
CC       IxM motif); recruits GOSR2 and STX5 into COPII-coated vesicles
CC       (PubMed:18843296). Interacts with DDHD1 (PubMed:17428803). Interacts
CC       with STING1 (via ER-exit motif); interaction is direct and promotes
CC       STING1 association with COPII vesicles and endoplasmic reticulum exit
CC       (PubMed:30842662, PubMed:41887218). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10075675,
