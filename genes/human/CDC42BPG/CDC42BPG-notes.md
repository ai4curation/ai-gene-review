# CDC42BPG notes

Per-provider deep research was attempted for ROCK1 with `perplexity`, but the local deep-research client reported that only the unavailable `claude_code` provider was installed. This family-slice review therefore uses the fetched UniProt, GOA, PAINT and PMID caches as the evidence base.

CDC42BPG encodes the smaller DMPK-like CDC42-binding kinase in the PTHR22988 AGC kinase family. It has fewer direct GOA rows than ROCK1/ROCK2 or CDC42BPA/CDC42BPB, so manual review should be conservative about transferred actomyosin and cytokinesis functions unless the row's own evidence or a close MRCK branch supports them.

## Curation summary

The completed review keeps CDC42BPG's experimentally supported serine/threonine kinase activity and leading-edge/cytoplasmic localization, accepts the conserved MRCK-family actomyosin organization IBA, and removes high-throughput TBK1 generic protein-binding rows. Cytosol and broad cytoskeleton rows were left `UNDECIDED` because the cached evidence did not directly establish those exact CDC42BPG locations.
