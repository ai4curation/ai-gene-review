# KDM5B curation notes

Automated Falcon deep research, with the `perplexity-lite` fallback, was unavailable in this worktree. This note records the manual evidence synthesis used for the KDM5B review.

KDM5B/PLU-1 is a nuclear JARID1-family H3K4me2/3 demethylase and corepressor. PLU-1 was shown to be a transcriptional corepressor for BF-1/FOXG1 and PAX9 before the demethylase activity was identified [PMID:12657635]. Xiang et al. and later KDM5B biochemical work support the KDM5B H3K4 demethylase activity [PMID:18048344; PMID:24952722; PMID:26645689; PMID:28262558]. The flexible loop L1 of the KDM5B ARID domain is important for DNA binding and the PHD1 finger binds unmodified H3K4 during H3K4me2/3 demethylation [PMID:20403335; PMID:24952722].

Several rows are Ensembl Compara transfers to phenotypic or stress-response terms. The cached human KDM5B evidence did not verify the fibroblast-growth-factor, fungicide-response, lens-fiber-differentiation or ribonucleoprotein-complex claims, so these are left `UNDECIDED` instead of inferred from generic KDM5B chromatin biology.
