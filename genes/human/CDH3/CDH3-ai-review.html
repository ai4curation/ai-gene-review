Gene Description

P-cadherin (cadherin-3) is a type I classical cadherin that mediates calcium-dependent homophilic cell-cell adhesion. Its extracellular cadherin domains recognize P-cadherin on adjoining cells, while its intracellular region associates with beta-catenin in adhesive junctions. It is expressed in epithelial compartments including basal cells and hair follicles. P-cadherin supports follicular keratinocyte proliferation, maintenance of the hair growth phase and follicular pigmentation through context-dependent signaling relationships. Biallelic CDH3 defects cause sparse hair with progressive macular dysfunction, and can also cause ectodermal dysplasia with limb abnormalities.
