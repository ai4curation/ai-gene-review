Gene Description

Human ARF is the 132-residue p14ARF tumor suppressor encoded in an alternative reading frame of CDKN2A, structurally unrelated to p16INK4A. Full-length ARF binds and inhibits MDM2, supporting p53 stability, cell-cycle arrest and senescence. It also associates with other regulatory proteins, modulates SUMO ligases and can promote apoptosis in defined contexts. ARF occupies nucleolar and nucleoplasmic pools that redistribute with stress and binding partners. Internally initiated smARF lacks residues 1–47; mitochondrial localization of short and full-length products depends on the construct and cellular context and is not uniformly exclusive.
