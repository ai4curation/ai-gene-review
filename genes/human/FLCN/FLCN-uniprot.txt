CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multi-functional protein, involved in both the cellular
CC       response to amino acid availability and in the regulation of glycolysis
CC       (PubMed:17028174, PubMed:18663353, PubMed:21209915, PubMed:24081491,
CC       PubMed:24095279, PubMed:31672913, PubMed:31704029, PubMed:32612235,
CC       PubMed:34381247, PubMed:36103527, PubMed:37079666). GTPase-activating
CC       protein that plays a key role in the cellular response to amino acid
CC       availability through regulation of the non-canonical mTORC1 signaling
CC       cascade controlling the MiT/TFE factors TFEB and TFE3 (PubMed:17028174,
CC       PubMed:18663353, PubMed:21209915, PubMed:24081491, PubMed:24095279,
CC       PubMed:24448649, PubMed:31672913, PubMed:31704029, PubMed:32612235,
CC       PubMed:36103527, PubMed:37079666). Activates mTORC1 by acting as a
CC       GTPase-activating protein: specifically stimulates GTP hydrolysis by
CC       RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD, promoting the conversion to the GDP-bound
CC       state of RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD, and thereby activating the kinase
CC       activity of mTORC1 (PubMed:24095279, PubMed:31672913, PubMed:31704029,
CC       PubMed:32612235, PubMed:37079666). The GTPase-activating activity is
CC       inhibited during starvation and activated in presence of nutrients
CC       (PubMed:31672913, PubMed:32612235). Acts as a key component for non-
CC       canonical mTORC1-dependent control of the MiT/TFE factors TFEB and
CC       TFE3, while it is not involved in mTORC1-dependent phosphorylation of
CC       canonical RPS6KB1/S6K1 and EIF4EBP1/4E-BP1 (PubMed:21209915,
CC       PubMed:24081491, PubMed:31672913, PubMed:32612235). In low-amino acid
CC       conditions, the lysosomal folliculin complex (LFC) is formed on the
CC       membrane of lysosomes, which inhibits the GTPase-activating activity of
CC       FLCN, inactivates mTORC1 and maximizes nuclear translocation of TFEB
CC       and TFE3 (PubMed:31672913). Upon amino acid restimulation, RagA/RRAGA
CC       (or RagB/RRAGB) nucleotide exchange promotes disassembly of the LFC
CC       complex and liberates the GTPase-activating activity of FLCN, leading
CC       to activation of mTORC1 and subsequent cytoplasmic retention of TFEB
CC       and TFE3 (PubMed:31672913). Indirectly acts as a positive regulator of
CC       Wnt signaling by promoting mTOR-dependent cytoplasmic retention of
CC       MiT/TFE factor TFE3 (PubMed:31272105). Required for the exit of
CC       hematopoietic stem cell from pluripotency by promoting mTOR-dependent
CC       cytoplasmic retention of TFE3, thereby increasing Wnt signaling
CC       (PubMed:30733432). Acts as an inhibitor of browning of adipose tissue
CC       by regulating mTOR-dependent cytoplasmic retention of TFE3 (By
CC       similarity). Involved in the control of embryonic stem cells
CC       differentiation; together with LAMTOR1 it is necessary to recruit and
CC       activate RagC/RRAGC and RagD/RRAGD at the lysosomes, and to induce exit
CC       of embryonic stem cells from pluripotency via non-canonical, mTOR-
CC       independent TFE3 inactivation (By similarity). In response to flow
CC       stress, regulates STK11/LKB1 accumulation and mTORC1 activation through
CC       primary cilia: may act by recruiting STK11/LKB1 to primary cilia for
CC       activation of AMPK resided at basal bodies, causing mTORC1 down-
CC       regulation (PubMed:27072130). Together with FNIP1 and/or FNIP2,
CC       regulates autophagy: following phosphorylation by ULK1, interacts with
CC       GABARAP and promotes autophagy (PubMed:25126726). Interaction with
CC       GABARAP also inhibits the non-canonical mTORC1 signaling cascade:
CC       GABARAP sequesters FLCN, thereby inactivating mTORC1 and promoting
CC       nuclear translocation of TFEB and TFE3 (PubMed:34597140,
CC       PubMed:39423796). Required for starvation-induced perinuclear
CC       clustering of lysosomes by promoting association of RILP with its
CC       effector RAB34 (PubMed:27113757). Regulates glycolysis by binding to
CC       lactate dehydrogenase LDHA, acting as an uncompetitive inhibitor
CC       (PubMed:34381247). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8QZS3,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17028174, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18663353,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21209915, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24081491,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24095279, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24448649,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25126726, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27072130,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27113757, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30733432,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31272105, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31672913,
