# ERCC2 source guide

This guide links each directly cited paper to the experimental evidence used in the annotation review. Access to a main article is distinguished from access to a target-specific supplementary record, and figure captions from inspected image data. Publication-cache availability flags are preserved independently of these assessments.

The experimentally characterized full-length human protein and canonical database entities refer to the 760-residue XPD product. The 405-residue splice product is not assumed to share these functions. Human proteins expressed in insect or bacterial hosts remain human preparations; yeast donor experiments are identified separately.

## Primary evidence and access limits

- [Human core-TFIIH study, PMID:31253769](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6599211/): Results/Figures 1–4 and Methods on constructs, purification, helicase and triplex displacement. Recombinant human full-length XPD in TFIIH supports ATP-driven 5′–3′ DNA unwinding and DNA contact. The structural scaffold is lesion-free; damage-specific binding and every proposed proofreading step are not independently established by that structure. Main text/captions were read; the additional Figure 1 and Supplementary Figure1 image inspection is described above.
- [Human XPD functional study, PMID:19934020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2806454/): Figures 4–6; Methods on purification, NER incision, helicase, ATPase and transcription. ATP hydrolysis and DNA unwinding were separate measurements. XPD contributes to TFIIH transcription structure; CDK7 supplies the associated kinase activity. Engineered mutants/truncations are not equated with the natural 405-aa product.

Full-length human XPD-GFP is functional in human XP-D fibroblasts; the location is not exclusively nucleoplasmic.
- [Yeast RAD3 damaged-DNA binding, PMID:8132553](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0021-9258(17)37193-4): actual [author-upload original](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/15065807_Negative_superhelicity_promotes_ATP-dependent_binding_of_yeast_RAD3_protein_to_ultraviolet-damaged_DNA), Methods and Results/Figures 1, 3, 4, published pp8303–8308. ## PAINT, domains, chemistry and target localization

The official PTHR11472 tree places human leaf PTN002479324 beneath both actual asserted nodes: PTN000158192 (DNA helicase) and PTN000158239 (damaged-DNA binding, nucleus, nucleotide-excision repair, RNA polymerase II transcription). All five exact IBD records were inspected. The human aligned sequence matches canonical P18074 at all 760 residues; no NOT record occurs on the saved target ancestry. Yeast RAD3 leaf PTN000158285 descends from PTN000158239 and its current GOA damaged-DNA IDA points to PMID:8132553. No donor-count heuristic or self-WITH circularity claim is used. [PANTHER tree source](https://www.pantherdb.org/services/oai/pantherdb/treeinfo?family=PTHR11472).

The imported six InterPro entries are present in the official target UniProt cross-references. Their mappings support DNA/helicase/ATP functions; generic domain descriptions do not establish RNA catalysis. The IPR001945 entry contains an incongruous XPG/ERCC5 introductory passage; its RAD3/XPD identity, target membership and GO mappings are independently verified, so that unrelated endonuclease prose is not transferred to ERCC2. [InterPro IPR001945](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/entry/InterPro/IPR001945/).

[Rhea13065](https://www.rhea-db.org/rhea/13065) specifies ATP hydrolysis. [EC5.6.2.3](https://enzyme.expasy.org/EC/5.6.2.3) specifies ATP-coupled 5′–3′ DNA helicase; generic replication-fork examples do not assign ERCC2 a replication-specific role. Current QuickGO definitions and ancestry were inspected for all authored replacement terms. The helicase refinement describes the informative motor activity; it does not claim GO:0043139 is an is_a child of the broad phosphorus-anhydride hydrolase term.

The actual [ERCC2 Human Protein Atlas record](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000104884-ERCC2/subcellular) and XML report antibody HPA069266, supported nucleoplasmic localization in SiHa, U-251MG and U2OS cells and additional cytosol in U-251MG. Structured assay data and reliability were read, not image pixels; these observations are not isoform-resolved.

## Natural products and reconstruction

[UniProt P18074](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P18074/entry) reports P18074-1, the 760-residue canonical protein, and P18074-2. The three recorded edits for P18074-2 delete canonical residues1–24, replace414–429 with QAQHCGSSRNQKRSHP, and delete430–760. Applying these explicit edits yields405 residues. This is a sequence reconstruction, not an assay of expression, stability, localization, catalytic activity or TFIIH incorporation. Patient-specific truncations and engineered K48R or other mutant constructs are separate experimental products.

## Ontology and annotation scope

Current [GO:0035315](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0035315) has direct epidermal-cell and neuron-differentiation parents. Its name alone does not establish the cell type relevant to the inaccessible TTD review. [GO:0005669](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0005669) denotes TFIID, whereas [GO:0000439](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/term/GO:0000439) describes the seven-subunit TFIIH core including XPD; PMID:27193682 explicitly used TBP in place of TFIID. The ATP-hydrolysis annotations retain the separately measured chemistry. The broad phosphorus-anhydride hydrolase annotation is refined to GO:0016887, whose current is_a ancestry includes GO:0016818; the directional helicase activity remains independently represented.
