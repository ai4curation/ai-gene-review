CC   -!- FUNCTION: ATP-dependent 5'-3' DNA helicase (PubMed:31253769,
CC       PubMed:8413672, PubMed:9771713). Component of the general transcription
CC       and DNA repair factor IIH (TFIIH) core complex, not absolutely
CC       essential for minimal transcription in vitro (PubMed:10024882,
CC       PubMed:17466626, PubMed:9771713). Required for transcription-coupled
CC       nucleotide excision repair (NER) of damaged DNA; recognizes damaged
CC       bases (PubMed:17466626, PubMed:23352696, PubMed:9771713). Sequestered
CC       in chromatin on UV-damaged DNA (PubMed:23352696). When complexed to
CC       CDK-activating kinase (CAK), involved in transcription by RNA
CC       polymerase II. In NER, TFIIH acts by opening DNA around the lesion to
CC       allow the excision of the damaged oligonucleotide and its replacement
CC       by a new DNA fragment. The ATP-dependent helicase activity of XPD/ERCC2
CC       is required for DNA opening. Involved in DNA lesion verification
CC       (PubMed:31253769). In transcription, TFIIH has an essential role in
CC       transcription initiation. When the pre-initiation complex (PIC) has
CC       been established, TFIIH is required for promoter opening and promoter
CC       escape. Phosphorylation of the C-terminal tail (CTD) of the largest
CC       subunit of RNA polymerase II by the kinase module CAK controls the
CC       initiation of transcription. XPD/ERCC2 acts by forming a bridge between
CC       CAK and the core-TFIIH complex. The structure of the TFIIH
CC       transcription complex differs from the NER-TFIIH complex; large
CC       movements by XPD/ERCC2 and XPB/ERCC3 are stabilized by XPA which allow
CC       this subunit to contact ssDNA (PubMed:31253769, PubMed:33902107).
CC       Involved in the regulation of vitamin-D receptor activity. As part of
CC       the mitotic spindle-associated MMXD complex it plays a role in
CC       chromosome segregation. Might have a role in aging process and could
CC       play a causative role in the generation of skin cancers.
