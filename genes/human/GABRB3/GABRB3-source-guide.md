## Localization, donor experiments and source limits

The mouse [PMID:17021187](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1806703/) was read in the genuine full native cache for beta3 separately from beta2. It reports six-week dysfunction and approximately 24-week afferent/hair-cell loss; no beta3 six-week histopathology or early developmental series was performed, and beta3 progression was explicitly not tested. Formation versus maintenance therefore remains unresolved, and alpha5/beta2 timing is excluded. Images, supplementary figures and numeric Table1 cells were not inspected. Genuine original [PMID:27129275](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27129275/) selected Methods/Results and [PMID:25489750](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25489750/) beta3 contact experiments were read with their construct/species limits. Other original donor papers retain their exact individual access limits in the review assessments.

[PMID:9039914](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9039914/) identifies an epsilon subunit and alpha/beta assembly, without resolving the precise beta3 clone. Independent beta3 evidence supports the accepted biological claims while that original source limit remains visible. The complete main text of [PMID:33234346](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7855156/) was read with the relevant captions.
