- Dark: no substrate known. Mouse knockout has no muscle phenotype [PMID:41549311 "Therefore, Asb5 KO has no effect on proliferation, differentiation, or self-renewing properties of MuSCs."]. The zebrafish double paralog knockout has a heart phenotype (PMID:38003559, PMID:40141403, both cached and cited). No developmental process term is added: the zebrafish phenotype is from orthologs with functional redundancy, and no mechanism is known.
