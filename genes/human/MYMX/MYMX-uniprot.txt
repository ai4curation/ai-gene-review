CC   -!- FUNCTION: Myoblast-specific protein that mediates myoblast fusion, an
CC       essential step for the formation of multi-nucleated muscle fibers
CC       (PubMed:28569745, PubMed:35642635). Involved in membrane fusion
CC       downstream of the lipid mixing step mediated by MYMK (By similarity).
CC       Acts by generating membrane stresses via its extracellular C-terminus,
CC       leading to drive fusion pore formation. Acts independently of MYMK (By
CC       similarity). Involved in skeletal muscle regeneration in response to
CC       injury by mediating the fusion of satellite cells, a population of
CC       muscle stem cells, with injured myofibers (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q2Q5T5, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28569745,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35642635}.
