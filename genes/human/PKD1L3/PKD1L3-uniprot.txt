CC   -!- FUNCTION: Pore-forming subunit of a heterotetrameric, non-selective
CC       cation channel that is permeable to Ca(2+) (PubMed:19464260,
CC       PubMed:23212381). Also shows permeability towards NA(1+), K(+) and
CC       Mg(2+) (PubMed:23212381). Heterotetrameric complex channel is activated
CC       by external low pH and Ca(2+), but opens only when the extracellular pH
CC       rises again and after the removal of acid stimulus (PubMed:19464260,
CC       PubMed:23212381). May act as a sour taste receptor in gustatory cells;
CC       however, its contribution to sour taste perception is unclear in vivo
CC       and may be indirect (PubMed:19812697). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19464260,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19812697, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23212381}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Ca(2+)(in) = Ca(2+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:29671,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:29108; Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:19464260};
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Na(+)(in) = Na(+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:34963,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:29101; Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:23212381};
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=K(+)(in) = K(+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:29463, ChEBI:CHEBI:29103;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:23212381};
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Mg(2+)(in) = Mg(2+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:29827,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:18420; Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:23212381};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: The non-selective cation channel is gated
CC       following an off-response property by acid: gated open after the
CC       removal of acid stimulus, but not during acid application
CC       (PubMed:19464260). Regulation of non-selective cation channel activity
CC       by external Ca(2+) is bimodal, first sensitizing and subsequently
CC       inactivating the current (PubMed:26066678).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19464260, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26066678}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterotetramer with PKD2L1, composed of 3 subunit of PKD2L1
CC       and 1 subunit of PKD1L3. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23212381}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23212381};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255}. Note=Interaction with PKD2L1
CC       is required for localization to the cell membrane.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23212381}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Highly expressed in placenta, weakly in heart and
CC       lung. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12782129}.
CC   -!- PTM: Autoproteolytically processed at the GPS region of the GAIN-B
CC       domain; this cleavage modulates receptor activity.
CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00098}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the polycystin family. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- CAUTION: PKD1L3 and PKD2L1 has been initially identified as sour taste
CC       receptor in type III gustatory cells, but its role in sour taste
CC       reception is controversial (By similarity). Some data confirm this
CC       hypothesis in human: in 2 patients with sour ageusia that are
CC       unresponsive to sour stimuli, but show normal responses to bitter,
CC       sweet, and salty stimuli, expression of PKD1L3 and PKD2L1 is absent in
CC       the anterior part of the tongue. However, a number of experiments have
CC       recently shown that the sour taste receptor activity is probably
CC       indirect: mice lacking Pkd1l3 do not show defects in sour taste
CC       perception (By similarity). Moreover, the Pkd1l3-Pkd2l1 heteromer, when
CC       expressed in cells does not respond to acid stimuli used to evoke
CC       proton currents in taste cells (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q2EG98, ECO:0000305|PubMed:19812697}.
