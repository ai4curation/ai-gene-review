# PKD1L3 notes

Automated deep research could not be generated for this review because the configured provider was unavailable, so this note records manual curation context only.

PKD1L3 is a PKD1-like channel-forming partner for PKD2L1. Yu et al. used PKD1L3/TRPP3 electrophysiology and mutagenesis to show that PKD1L3 affects ion selectivity in the acid-activated channel, supporting the conclusion that PKD1L3 directly coassembles into the pore-forming acid-activated cation channel at the membrane/cell surface rather than serving only as a trafficking or sensory accessory subunit; this supports the cation-channel, cation-transport, plasma-membrane, cell-surface, generic membrane, and cation-channel-complex rows, while leaving the extracellular-exosome HDA row peripheral and the signaling-receptor-complex row over-annotated [PMID:23212381, "PKD1L3 functions as a channel-forming subunit and associates with TRPP3 in a 1:3 stoichiometry to form an acid-activated heteromeric channel"].

The sour-taste annotations need to distinguish heterologous acid-gated channel activity from an in vivo sour-receptor claim. Horio et al. generated Pkd1l3, Pkd2l1, and double-knockout mice and concluded that Pkd2l1 contributes only partly to sour taste and that non-PKD receptors must also participate [PMID:21625513, "These findings suggest that PKD2L1 partly contributes to sour taste responses in mice and that receptors other than PKDs would be involved in sour detection"]. The same study found normal chorda tympani responses in Pkd1l3 knockout mice and normal glossopharyngeal responses in single and double knockouts, so positive sour-taste process and sour-taste receptor rows are over-annotations for PKD1L3.

The PTHR10877 root membrane assertion is safe for PKD1L3, and the root calcium-channel assertion is not egregious because PKD1L3 is directly assayed as a channel-forming PKD2L1 partner. The root `detection of mechanical stimulus` IBA is too broad: the PKD1L3 literature in this review supports an acid-gated heteromeric channel, not a direct mechanical-stimulus detection role.

The single `GO:0005515 protein binding` row records the PKD1L3-PKD2L1/TRPP3 interaction from the acid-gated channel assembly paper. The interaction is genuine, but the bare protein-binding term is removed under the repository protein-binding policy while the channel-complex and cation-channel rows retain the functional conclusion.
