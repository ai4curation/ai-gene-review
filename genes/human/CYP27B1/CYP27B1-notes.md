# CYP27B1 notes

Deep research (falcon) attempted 2026-10-10 and failed (timeout); manual literature review from cached GOA papers.

- Cloning, activity and VDDR1 null alleles [PMID:9415400 "These findings establish the molecular genetic basis of VDDR-1"].
- Recombinant human enzyme, mitochondrial, adrenodoxin-dependent, activated by cardiolipin; also acts on 25(OH)D2 and CYP24A1 intermediates [PMID:22862690]; 24R,25(OH)2D3 is a good substrate [PMID:10518789].
- PDDR missense mutants inactive [PMID:10518789 "completely abolished 1alpha-hydroxylase activity"].
- Extrarenal autocrine roles: keratinocytes [PMID:12496369], mammary epithelium [PMID:16549446], bone [PMID:17023519]; induction by LPS in dendritic cells [PMID:12855575], IFN-gamma in monocytes [PMID:9282826].
- Location: Reactome "mitochondrial outer membrane" modified to inner membrane (type I mitochondrial P450, adrenodoxin partner, cardiolipin activation).
- Vitamin D catabolic process (IBA/IEA) marked over-annotated: activation, not catabolism.
- dismech `Vitamin_D-Dependent_Rickets_Type_1A` binds GO:0004498 calcidiol 1-monooxygenase activity — consistent. ClinGen Definitive (AR) added there.
