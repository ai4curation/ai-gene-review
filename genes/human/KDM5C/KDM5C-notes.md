# KDM5C curation notes

Automated Falcon deep research, with the `perplexity-lite` fallback, was unavailable in this worktree. This note records the manual evidence synthesis used for the KDM5C review.

KDM5C/SMCX is a nuclear JARID1-family H3K4me2/3 demethylase. Iwase et al. used SMCX as the founding human example for the JARID1 H3K4me3 demethylase family and showed disease-associated variants that reduce demethylase or H3K9me3-binding functions [PMID:17320160]. Tahiliani et al. isolated SMCX complexes containing REST, HDAC1/2 and G9a, showed SMCX and REST co-occupancy at neuron-restrictive silencing elements, and showed that SMCX depletion derepresses target genes while increasing H3K4 trimethylation [PMID:17468742].

The PMID:18078810 abstract emphasizes a cytoplasmic SMCX isoform and is not enough on its own to verify the seeded IDA nucleus row from that paper. The row is retained because KDM5C has curated UniProt nuclear localization and the GO curator had access to the full paper.
