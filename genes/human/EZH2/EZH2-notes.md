## 2026-10-25 — Mechanistic feedback clarification

EZH2 methylation and partner recognition remain distinct. The catalytic core retains GO:0140951: its current definition explicitly covers three successive transfers from unmethylated H3K27 to H3K27me3. Wild-type PRC2 activity on the three starting methylation states and the different Y641/A677G preferences are measured in PMID:22323599 (Results accompanying Figures 3–4 and main Materials and Methods). H1.4 methylation in human anti-EZH2 immunoprecipitates and JARID2 K116 methylation by PRC2 preserve the broader protein-lysine activity across all four imported contexts (PMID:19144645, Figure 1E and Experimental Procedures; PMID:25620564, Figure 1 and Results). EED is the methyllysine reader, not EZH2.

The RNA core and broad RNA refinements now name lncRNA binding. Purified human PRC2 binds RepA/Hes1 antisense probes and Gtl2 RNAs, while isolated human EZH2 directly shifts Hes1 antisense RNA in PMID:21172659 (Figure 5 and protein-purification/RNA-assay Methods). PMID:35682829 measures an isolated EZH2 loop contacting HOTAIR1–140 (Results 2.4 and Methods 4.1–4.3). Weak, low-specificity contact does not establish lncRNA sequence exclusivity; it also does not demonstrate another RNA class. Mouse pri-miR-208b RNA-ChIP is not silently converted to a purified-human assay. RNA-dependent recruitment or catalytic regulation remains context dependent. The five natural products remain functionally distinct questions, not equivalent tested reagents.

In the DAB2IP system, EZH2 represses a Ras GAP and increases Ras-GTP signaling; this is not positive regulation of GTP hydrolysis (PMID:20154697, Figure 5 and corresponding Results/Methods). The imported process is therefore refined to positive regulation of Ras protein signal transduction. DAB2IP reconstitution is an important causal control. The correction PMID:38383797 replaces a Figure 1g control micrograph and does not correct the separate Figure 5 experiments. EZH2 contributes upstream repression, not Ras catalysis.

All four heterochromatin-formation contexts now specify the facultative H3K27-associated process.
