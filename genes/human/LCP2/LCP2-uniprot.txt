CC   -!- FUNCTION: Adapter protein primarily involved in signaling pathways
CC       within T-cells, as well as other immune cells such as platelets, mast
CC       cells, and natural killer (NK) cells (PubMed:11313406,
CC       PubMed:33159816). Plays a crucial role for transducing signal from the
CC       T-cell receptor (TCR) after antigen recognition leading to T-cell
CC       activation. Mechanistically, once phosphorylated by the kinase ZAP70,
CC       mediates interactions with the guanine-nucleotide exchange factor VAV1,
CC       the adapter protein NCK and the kinase ITK (PubMed:8673706,
CC       PubMed:8702662). In turn, stimulates the activation of PKC-theta/PRKCQ
CC       and NF-kappa-B transcriptional activity in response to CD3 and CD28
CC       costimulation (PubMed:11313406). Also plays an essential role in AGER-
CC       induced signaling pathways including p38 MAPK and ERK1/2 activation
CC       leading to cytokine release and pro-inflammatory responses
CC       (PubMed:33436632). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11313406,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33436632, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8673706,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8702662}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with SLA. Interacts with CBLB (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with GRB2 (PubMed:7706237). Interacts with SHB
CC       (PubMed:12084069). Interacts with PRAM1 (PubMed:11301322). Interacts
CC       (via SH2 domain) with CD6 (via tyrosine phosphorylated C-terminus)
CC       (PubMed:16914752, PubMed:24584089). Interacts with FYB1 and the
CC       phosphorylated form of FYB2 (PubMed:27335501). Interacts with 14-3-3
CC       adapter/YWHAZ; this phosphorylation leads to YWHAZ proteolytic
CC       degradation (PubMed:33159816). Interacts with VAV1; this interaction
CC       plays a role in TCR-mediated cytokine production (PubMed:15144186).
CC       Interacts with AGER; this interaction plays an important role in AGER-
CC       mediated pro-inflammatory responses and cytokine release
CC       (PubMed:33436632). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q60787,
