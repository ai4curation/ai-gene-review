Gene Description

DICER1 encodes a multidomain RNase III enzyme that cleaves double-stranded RNA and precursor microRNA hairpins into small regulatory RNAs. Its paired RNase III domains, PAZ region and RNA-binding domains coordinate substrate recognition and cleavage. Dicer works with Argonaute proteins and the cofactors TARBP2 or PRKRA in RNA processing and guide loading, linking small-RNA production to post-transcriptional gene regulation. Cytosolic Dicer supports this pathway, with nuclear and perinuclear pools varying by cellular context. Dicer-dependent regulatory RNAs influence development and differentiation, including Schwann-cell maturation and myelination. Alternative splicing produces structurally distinct proteins; t-Dicer has an altered C terminus and asymmetric RNA-cleavage behavior.

  domains coordinate substrate recognition and cleavage. Dicer works with Argonaute proteins and the cofactors
  TARBP2 or PRKRA in RNA processing and guide loading, linking small-RNA production to post-transcriptional
  gene regulation. Cytosolic Dicer supports this pathway, with nuclear and perinuclear
  pools varying by cellular context. Dicer-dependent regulatory RNAs influence development and differentiation,
  including Schwann-cell maturation and myelination. Alternative splicing produces structurally distinct
  proteins; t-Dicer has an altered C terminus and asymmetric RNA-cleavage behavior.
