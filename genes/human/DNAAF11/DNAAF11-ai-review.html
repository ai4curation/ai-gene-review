Gene Description

DNAAF11 (LRRC6) is a leucine-rich-repeat and CS-domain protein that supports assembly and axonemal incorporation of the outer and inner dynein arms required for motile-cilia and sperm-flagellar beating. It associates with other dynein assembly factors and localizes to cytoplasmic dynein axonemal particles (DynAPs). Human airway and sperm studies also report ciliary or flagellar localization, with differences between airway preparations. Loss of DNAAF11 disrupts dynein-arm formation and causes primary ciliary dyskinesia, often with laterality defects and impaired fertility.
