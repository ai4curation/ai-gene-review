# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ARRDC2 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ARRDC2 is a mechanosensitive regulator of skeletal muscle size whose expression is tuned to mechanical load: its mRNA is rapidly induced in skeletal muscle within one day of mechanical unloading by hindlimb suspension, returns to baseline upon reloading, and is suppressed by mechanical loading beyond ambulatory levels, with the unloading-induced response being more pronounced in aged than young muscle [PMID:38314723]. Gain-of-function establishes a causal role downstream of this regulation: adenoviral overexpression of ARRDC2 in C2C12 myotubes is sufficient to reduce myotube diameter and drives transcriptomic changes consistent with muscle atrophy and disuse [PMID:38314723]. Beyond this mechanosensitive expression and pro-atrophic activity, the molecular mechanism by which ARRDC2 acts — its binding partners, biochemical activity, and the pathway through which it reduces myotube size — has not been characterized in the available corpus.
