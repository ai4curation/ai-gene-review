# ARRDC2 review notes

## Sources
- The affinage record (trust gates clear) covers only the muscle-atrophy study (PMID:38314723). It omits the alpha-arrestin literature:
  - PMID:23236378 (full text): plasma membrane and cytoplasmic vesicles, mCherry-tagged ARRDC2 in HEK293T cells.
  - PMID:21191027 (abstract): ARRDC1-4 bind HECT ubiquitin ligases and ESCRT components.
- UniProt: WWP1 binding (via WW domains). The ARRDC2 sequence (Q8TBH0) contains two PPxY motifs, PPEY and PPLY.
- Partners: EPN2 and ZRANB1 (proteome-scale Y2H).

## Decisions
- ACCEPT:
  - Plasma membrane (IBA, IDA); cytoplasmic vesicle (IDA).
  - Cytoplasm (IBA), with the propagation review citing PTN000127176.
- MODIFY: molecular adaptor activity (IBA) → protein-macromolecule adaptor activity.
- REMOVE: EPN2 and ZRANB1 protein binding (policy).
- NEW: ubiquitin protein ligase binding (IPI, PMID:21191027), the core MF.
