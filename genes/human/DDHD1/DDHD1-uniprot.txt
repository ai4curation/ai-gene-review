CC   -!- FUNCTION: Phospholipase A1 (PLA1) that hydrolyzes ester bonds at the
CC       sn-1 position of glycerophospholipids producing a free fatty acid and a
CC       lysophospholipid (Probable) (PubMed:20359546, PubMed:22922100). Prefers
CC       phosphatidate (1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphate, PA) as substrate in
CC       vitro, but can efficiently hydrolyze phosphatidylinositol (1,2-diacyl-
CC       sn-glycero-3-phospho-(1D-myo-inositol), PI), as well as a range of
CC       other glycerophospholipid substrates such as phosphatidylcholine (1,2-
CC       diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine, PC), phosphatidylethanolamine (1,2-
CC       diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphoethanolamine, PE), phosphatidylserine (1,2-
CC       diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phospho-L-serine, PS) and phosphatidylglycerol
CC       (1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phospho-(1'-sn-glycerol), PG) (Probable)
CC       (PubMed:20359546). Involved in the regulation of the endogenous content
CC       of polyunsaturated PI and PS lipids in the nervous system. Changes in
CC       these lipids extend to downstream metabolic products like PI phosphates
CC       PIP and PIP2, which play fundamental roles in cell biology (By
CC       similarity). Regulates mitochondrial morphology (PubMed:24599962).
CC       These dynamic changes may be due to PA hydrolysis at the mitochondrial
CC       surface (PubMed:24599962). May play a regulatory role in
CC       spermatogenesis or sperm function (PubMed:24599962).
