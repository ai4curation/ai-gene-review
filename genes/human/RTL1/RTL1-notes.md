# RTL1 / PEG11 (human, A6NKG5) — review notes
SIRH2; eutherian-specific; DLK1-DIO3 cluster, 14q32.2; full gag-pol (1358 aa). 3 GOA rows, no experimental human GOA. No deep-research file (falcon unavailable; no openscientist report in dir).
Human disease (verified):
- Kagami-Ogata syndrome (KOS): upd(14)pat / maternal-allele lesions; excessive RTL1 is primary factor [PMID:26377239 "excessive RTL1 expression and absent MEG expression constitute the primary underlying factor for the phenotypic development"; PMID:18176563 "excessive RTL1 expression and decreased DLK1 and RTL1 expression are relevant to upd(14)pat-like and upd(14)mat-like phenotypes"]. KOS features: coat-hanger ribs, bell-shaped thorax, abdominal wall defects, placentomegaly, polyhydramnios [PMID:25689926].
- Temple syndrome (TS): upd(14)mat / paternal-allele lesions; reduced RTL1; pre/postnatal growth failure, hypotonia, early puberty, short stature [PMID:24891339].
- RTL1 is the major gene for the skeletal-muscle features of both syndromes [PMID:32878913 "RTL1 is the major gene responsible for the muscle defects"].
Placental/muscle roles by orthology to mouse Rtl1 (PMID:18176565, 32878913). NEW: GO:0039660 structural constituent of virion (ISS; mouse RTL1 forms capsid-like particles PMID:34413232). Protease present but not shown active -> suggested_questions.

## Deep research integration (OpenScientist, 2026-10-24)

Report read as leads; each point checked against cached publications.

Contradiction found and corrected in the review text:
- The report says RTL1 is "produced by the trophoblast cell layers that surround fetal capillary endothelial cells", citing Shiura 2021. Shiura 2021 says the opposite: "It should be noted that, in contrast to Peg10, Rtl1 is only expressed in endothelial cells (Sekita et al., 2008)." [PMID:34559199]. Human placenta IHC agrees: "IHC examinations identified RTL1, DLK1 and DIO3 protein expressions in the vascular endothelial cells and pericytes of chorionic villi, but not in the cytotrophoblasts, syncytiotrophoblasts, and stromal cells" [PMID:22917972]. The trophoblast-layer sentence in Shiura is about PEG10. Description now states endothelial/pericyte expression.
- The report's "acts extracellularly" claim has no primary support; not used.

Verified findings added:
- Mouse KO placental pathology [PMID:28111885 "the severe clogging of fetal capillaries was associated with KO fetuses showing mid-fetal lethality with internal bleeding"]. Added to the placenta development IBA.
- Ectopic PEG11 in transgenic mouse muscle gives hypertrophy [PMID:26474044 "show that they also exhibit a muscular hypertrophy phenotype"]. Note the report calls RTL1 "contributory but insufficient" relative to DLK1; the paper says "both DLK1 and PEG11 act together".
- Sheep PEG11 protein is full length in muscle nuclear fractions, and the authors infer the protease is "either inactive or did not have autolytic activity" [PMID:20072617]. Supports not asserting peptidase activity.
- RTL1as miRNA control [PMID:26138477 "Seven miRNAs, including miR-127 , are processed from a maternally expressed antisense Rtl1 transcript ( Rtl1as ) and regulate Rtl1 levels through RNAi-mediated post-transcriptional degradation."]; ~2.5-fold RTL1 in KOS [PMID:26377239 "This suggests that the RTL1 expression level is ~2.5 times increased in the absence of functional RTL1as."]. Already reflected in the description.
- Sheep callipyge 200-fold PEG11 mRNA [PMID:15298706 "PEG11 mRNA abundance in the gluteus medius was 200-fold and 13-fold greater in +Mat/CLPGPat lambs"]; context only.

No action changes. No change implied for mouse/rat beyond the same endothelial-expression wording (see rat Rtl1 notes).
