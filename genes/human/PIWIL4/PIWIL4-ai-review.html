Gene Description

PIWIL4 (HIWI2) is a PIWI-clade Argonaute protein that in the mammalian male germline acts as the nuclear effector of the piRNA pathway. It is loaded with secondary, predominantly transposon-antisense piRNAs generated by PIWIL2-catalysed slicing in cytoplasmic nuage (piP-bodies), then enters the nucleus of fetal prospermatogonia, where it engages nascent transposon transcripts and directs de novo DNA methylation and heterochromatin formation at LINE1 and related elements. Unlike PIWIL1 and PIWIL2, its slicer activity is dispensable. Loss of the mouse ortholog Miwi2 causes transposon derepression and male sterility. PIWIL4 is also broadly expressed in somatic and cancer cells, where it is largely cytoplasmic and binds tRNA-derived small RNAs.
