CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a central role during spermatogenesis by repressing
CC       transposable elements and preventing their mobilization, which is
CC       essential for the germline integrity (By similarity). Acts via the
CC       piRNA metabolic process, which mediates the repression of transposable
CC       elements during meiosis by forming complexes composed of piRNAs and
CC       Piwi proteins (By similarity). The PIWIL4-piRNA pathway acts in the
CC       nucleus and mediates silencing of active transposons: engages with
CC       nascent transposable element transcripts and governs the piRNA-directed
CC       DNA methylation and subsequent repression of transposons (By
CC       similarity). In contrast to PIWIL1 and PIWIL2, does not show
CC       endonuclease activity (By similarity). Directly binds piRNAs, a class
CC       of 24 to 30 nucleotide RNAs that are generated by a Dicer-independent
CC       mechanism and are primarily derived from transposons and other repeated
CC       sequence elements (By similarity). Associates with secondary piRNAs
CC       antisense and PIWIL2/MILI is required for such association (By
CC       similarity). The piRNA process acts upstream of known mediators of DNA
CC       methylation (By similarity). Plays a key role in the piRNA
CC       amplification loop, also named ping-pong amplification cycle, by acting
CC       as a 'slicer-incompetent' component that loads cleaved piRNAs from the
CC       'slicer-competent' component PIWIL2 and target them on genomic
CC       transposon loci in the nucleus (By similarity). May be involved in the
CC       chromatin-modifying pathway by inducing 'Lys-9' methylation of histone
CC       H3 at some loci (PubMed:17544373). In addition to its role in germline,
CC       PIWIL4 also plays a role in the regulation of somatic cells activities
CC       (By similarity). Plays a role in pancreatic beta cell function and
CC       insulin secretion (By similarity). Involved in maintaining cell
CC       morphology and functional integrity of retinal epithelial through
CC       Akt/GSK3alpha/beta signaling pathway (PubMed:28025795). When
CC       overexpressed, acts as an oncogene by inhibition of apoptosis and
CC       promotion of cells proliferation in tumors (PubMed:22483988).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q4G033, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8CGT6,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17544373, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22483988,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28025795}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with PRMT5 and WDR77. Interacts (when methylated on
CC       arginine residues) with TDRD1, TDRKH/TDRD2 and TDRD9. Interacts with
CC       MOV10L1 (By similarity). Interacts with TEX15 and SPOCD1 (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8CGT6}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25038252,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28025795}. Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15489334,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25038252, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28025795}.

CC       during meiosis. PIWIL2/MILI is required for nuclear localization (By
