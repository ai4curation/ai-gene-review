CC   -!- FUNCTION: Reversibly inhibits the activity of ATP2A2/SERCA2 in cardiac
CC       sarcoplasmic reticulum by decreasing the apparent affinity of the
CC       ATPase for Ca(2+) (PubMed:28890335). Binds preferentially to the ATP-
CC       bound E1 conformational form of ATP2A2 which predominates at low Ca(2+)
CC       concentrations during the diastolic phase of the cardiac cycle (By
CC       similarity). Inhibits ATP2A2 Ca(2+) affinity by disrupting its
CC       allosteric activation by ATP (By similarity). Modulates the
CC       contractility of the heart muscle in response to physiological stimuli
CC       via its effects on ATP2A2. Modulates calcium re-uptake during muscle
CC       relaxation and plays an important role in calcium homeostasis in the
CC       heart muscle. The degree of ATP2A2 inhibition depends on the oligomeric
CC       state of PLN. ATP2A2 inhibition is alleviated by PLN phosphorylation
CC       (By similarity). Also inhibits the activity of ATP2A3/SERCA3 (By
CC       similarity). Controls intracellular Ca(2+) levels in elongated
CC       spermatids and may play a role in germ cell differentiation (By
CC       similarity). In the thalamic reticular nucleus of the brain, plays a
CC       role in the regulation of sleep patterns and executive functioning (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P61012,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P61014, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22427649,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22707725, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28890335}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homopentamer (PubMed:16043693, PubMed:16897780). Can also form
CC       heterooligomers with other sarcoplasmic/endoplasmic reticulum calcium
CC       ATPase (SERCA) regulators ARLN, ERLN, SLN and STRIT1/DWORF
CC       (PubMed:36523160). Monomer (By similarity). Interacts with HAX1
CC       (PubMed:17241641). Interacts as a monomer with ATP2A2; the interaction
CC       decreases ATP2A2 Ca(2+) affinity (PubMed:28890335, PubMed:36523160).
CC       Interacts with VMP1; VMP1 competes with PLN and SLN to prevent them
