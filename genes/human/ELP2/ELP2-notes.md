# ELP2 evidence notes

ELP2 is a WD40-containing component of Elongator. Human ELP4-FLAG purification identifies endogenous ELP2 in the assembled complex, and human tagged ELP2 N-terminal deletion reduces association with ELP1/ELP3. These experiments support an assembly contribution; they do not establish an independent ELP2 enzyme (PMID:22854966; PMID:25960406).

The conserved tRNA wobble-uridine modification role is supported by the ELP2-specific InterPro family, Elongator membership and the reviewed literature. The human record describes cm5U formation by Elongator. The chemical activity of the machinery must remain distinct from the activity of ELP2 alone (PMID:27847465; UniProtKB:Q6IA86). Yeast structural, microtubule-binding and H3K14 results are not rephrased as direct human ELP2 assays.
