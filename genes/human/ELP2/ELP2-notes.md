# ELP2 evidence notes

ELP2 is a WD40-containing component of Elongator. Human ELP4-FLAG purification identifies endogenous ELP2 in the assembled complex, and human tagged ELP2 N-terminal deletion reduces association with ELP1/ELP3. These experiments support an assembly contribution; they do not establish an independent ELP2 enzyme ([PMID:22854966](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3463322/), Experimental Procedures, Affinity Purification; Results, Identification of the Components of the Human Elongator Complex, Fig. 1A; [PMID:25960406](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/276148381_The_Elp2_Subunit_Is_Essential_for_Elongator_Complex_Assembly_and_Functional_Regulation), Results, human N17 deletion/co-immunoprecipitation, Fig. 3C).

The conserved tRNA wobble-uridine modification role is supported by the ELP2-specific InterPro family, Elongator membership and the reviewed literature. The human record describes cm5U formation by Elongator. The chemical activity of the machinery must remain distinct from the activity of ELP2 alone ([PMID:27847465](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5088202/), Elongator organization and substrate-specificity sections; [UniProtKB:Q6IA86](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q6IA86/entry), Function). Yeast structural, microtubule-binding and H3K14 results are not rephrased as direct human ELP2 assays.

The 20 imported annotations remain 20 separate source objects and match all 20 normal GOA contexts. Actions are 7 ACCEPT, 6 MODIFY and 7 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. The three cytoplasm-to-cytosol and three nucleus-to-nucleoplasm refinements consistently use independent target-specific HPA evidence; they do not claim that the original antibody experiments or SL mapping resolved those compartments. Cytosol is the main location and nucleoplasm is additional. No exclusive localization or PML-body subtype is inferred.

Historical RNA polymerase II association and transcription experiments require separate interpretation. [PMID:11714725](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11640079_Purification_and_Characterization_of_the_Human_Elongator_Complex) assayed the human complex with calf-thymus polymerase (RNA polymerase II interaction Methods and Fig. 2), associated it with early elongation complexes, and localized ELP1/ELP3 (Immunostaining Methods and Fig. 3). It did not perform the transcription-rescue experiment previously attributed to it in the review. [PMID:11818576](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC122174/) directly included anti-Elp2 immunofluorescence (Methods, Immunofluorescence; Results, Cellular Localization; Fig. 3). Its Functional Analysis and Fig. 5 report acetyl-CoA-dependent rescue of chromatin transcription in Elongator-depleted nuclear extract, despite failed stimulation in a fully defined reconstituted system. The Discussion leaves a possible initiation contribution open. These are positive complex-level observations with limited mechanistic resolution, not isolated ELP2 catalysis.

The predominant modern function is tRNA U34 modification. [PMID:27847465](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5088202/), substrate-specificity discussion and Conclusions, explains how tRNA rescue can bypass some whole-cell transcription-related phenotypes in yeast, while leaving distinct transcription functions possible. Secondary translation defects cannot simply be assumed to explain purified polymerase binding or acute nuclear-extract add-back. Consequently, the four imported transcription-era annotations remain non-core on their positive biochemical and complex-association evidence, with the uncertain physiological and transcription-step specificity stated explicitly. The imported contributes_to qualifier on polymerase binding remains intact.

The protein-kinase-binding transfer has a literal mouse Q91WG4 experimental donor ([PMID:10954736](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC27739/), Results, StIP1 Associates with JAKs, Fig. 4; trimolecular-complex tests, Fig. 5). Murine full-length StIP1, its WD7-deleted form and GST/HA fragments must remain distinct from the seven current human products. Positive JAK association does not establish a stable ternary STAT3–ELP2–JAK scaffold, which the inspected study did not recover.
