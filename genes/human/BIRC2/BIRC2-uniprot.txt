CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multi-functional protein which regulates not only caspases
CC       and apoptosis, but also modulates inflammatory signaling and immunity,
CC       mitogenic kinase signaling, and cell proliferation, as well as cell
CC       invasion and metastasis (PubMed:21737329, PubMed:21737330,
CC       PubMed:21931591, PubMed:23453969). Acts as an E3 ubiquitin-protein
CC       ligase regulating NF-kappa-B signaling (PubMed:21737329,
CC       PubMed:21737330). Regulates both canonical and non-canonical NF-kappa-B
CC       signaling by acting in opposite directions: acts as a positive
CC       regulator of the canonical pathway and suppresses constitutive
CC       activation of non-canonical NF-kappa-B signaling (PubMed:20651737). The
CC       target proteins for its E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase activity include:
CC       RIPK1, RIPK2, RIPK3, RIPK4, CASP3, CASP7, CASP8, TRAF2, DIABLO/SMAC,
CC       MAP3K14/NIK, MAP3K5/ASK1, IKBKG/NEMO, IKBKE and MXD1/MAD1
CC       (PubMed:18082613, PubMed:21737329, PubMed:21737330, PubMed:21931591,
CC       PubMed:23453969, PubMed:29452637). Acts as a key regulator of TNF-
CC       mediated signaling by mediating ubiquitination of RIPK1: RIPK1
CC       ubiquitination both activates the canonical NF-kappa-B pathway and
CC       prevent RIPK1-mediated cell death by promoting RIPK1 degradation by the
CC       proteasome (PubMed:21737329, PubMed:21737330, PubMed:29452637). Can
CC       also function as an E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase of the NEDD8
CC       conjugation pathway, targeting effector caspases for neddylation and
CC       inactivation (PubMed:21145488). Acts as an important regulator of
CC       innate immune signaling via regulation of Toll-like receptors (TLRs),
CC       Nod-like receptors (NLRs) and RIG-I like receptors (RLRs), collectively
CC       referred to as pattern recognition receptors (PRRs) (PubMed:20651737).
CC       Protects cells from spontaneous formation of the ripoptosome, a large
CC       multi-protein complex that has the capability to kill cancer cells in a
CC       caspase-dependent and caspase-independent manner (PubMed:21737329,
CC       PubMed:21737330). Suppresses ripoptosome formation by ubiquitinating
CC       RIPK1 and CASP8 (PubMed:21737329, PubMed:21737330). Can stimulate the
CC       transcriptional activity of E2F1 (PubMed:21653699). Plays a role in the
CC       modulation of the cell cycle (PubMed:15665297).
