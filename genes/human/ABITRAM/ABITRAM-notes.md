# Research Notes: human ABITRAM (UniProtKB:Q9NX38)

## 1. Nomenclature, Sequence, and Domain Architecture
- **Official HGNC Symbol**: `ABITRAM` (`HGNC:1364`; *Actin-binding transcription modulator*).
- **Synonyms**: `C9orf6`, `FAM206A`, `Simiate`.
- **UniProtKB**: `Q9NX38` (`ABITM_HUMAN`, reviewed Swiss-Prot entry; 181 amino acids, 20,378 Da, sequence version 1).
- **PANTHER Family & Subfamily**: `PANTHER:PTHR13651` (`PROTEIN ABITRAM`), sole subfamily `PANTHER:PTHR13651:SF0` (`PROTEIN ABITRAM`).
- **Fold & Evolutionary Origin**: Single-domain cytosolic/nuclear protein matching `IPR039169` (Abitram), `IPR033753` (`GCV_H/Fam206`), `PF01597` (`GCV_H`), and `SSF51230` (`Single hybrid motif` / `Gene3D:2.40.50.100` barrel-sandwich hybrid fold). Phylogenetic analyses in Derlig et al. 2013 (`PMID:24349419`) showed that Simiate/ABITRAM arose in early eukaryotes (~1.7–1.85 billion years ago, prior to the divergence of Bikonta and Unikonta) from a lipoyl-carrier H-protein-like fold (`GCSH` ancestor) but lacks a mitochondrial transit peptide and lacks the canonical `ES(V/I)KA` lipoylation motif of glycine cleavage system H-protein (`GCSH`). No experimental 3D structure has been determined (`0` PDB structures across `PTHR13651`), and no catalytic or point-mutant active/binding residues have been mapped experimentally.

## 2. Literature Search & Primary Evidence Dissection (Species Attribution)
Queries executed across PubMed and EuropePMC (`"ABITRAM"`, `"FAM206A"`, `"Simiate"`, `"C9orf6"`, `"Q9NX38"`) recovered five primary functional studies on mammalian Simiate/ABITRAM from the Dahlhaus group (`PMID:24349419`, `PMID:24782708`, `PMID:26013685`, `PMID:35787638`, `PMID:41617053`), one protocol/validation paper (`PMID:27167171`), and five high-throughput human binary/AP-MS interactome screens (`PMID:15607035`, `PMID:25416956`, `PMID:28514442`, `PMID:32296183`, `PMID:33961781`).

Careful dissection of species-specific reagents and cell models across the Dahlhaus-lab studies shows:
1. **Direct experiments on endogenous human `ABITRAM` (HEK-293 cells)**:
   - `PMID:24349419` (Derlig et al. 2013, *PLoS ONE*): Immunofluorescence in human HEK-293 cells showed endogenous Simiate (`ABITRAM`) localizes to somata and colocalizes with the splicing factor `SC35` (`SRSF2`) in nuclear speckles (Fig. 5E). Blocking endogenous human ABITRAM in HEK-293 cells via Chariot-shuttled anti-Simiate antibodies caused dose-dependent compaction/agglomeration of nuclear speckles (`SC35` 3D reconstructions) and rapid `TUNEL`-positive apoptosis within 3 hours (up to ~80% epitope blockade causing >99% cell death; Fig. 8; protocol further detailed in `PMID:27167171`). UniProt `Q9NX38` also notes siRNA/antibody depletion lethality (`PMID:24349419`).
   - `PMID:24782708` (Derlig et al. 2014, *Front Cell Neurosci*): In human HEK-293 cells, endogenous ABITRAM colocalizes with Lifeact-RFP-labeled F-actin specifically in lamellipodia (`GO:0030027`, Fig. 1D–E) and colocalizes with endogenous focal adhesion kinase (`PTK2` / `FAK`) in nuclei (`GO:0005634`, Fig. 8A–B,D). Expression of recombinant mouse `GFP-Simiate` in HEK-293 cells additionally showed recruitment to filopodia tips (`GO:0032433`), reduced variance of F-actin FRAP recovery half-times in lamellipodia, and reduced filopodia tip movement velocity (`PMID:24782708`).
   - `PMID:35787638` (stellmacher/Dahlhaus group 2022, *Sci Rep*): Recombinant GST-Simiate pulled down recombinant `GFP-FAK1` from transfected human HEK-293 cell lysates.
2. **Orthologous rodent biochemical and neuronal assays (`mouse` `Q80ZQ9` and `rat` `D4AE08`)**:
   - Purified recombinant mouse `Simiate` (`Q80ZQ9`, sharing 80.8% global Needleman–Wunsch sequence identity [156/193] and 86.2% [156/181] over ungapped aligned columns with human `Q9NX38`; see `genes/human/ABITRAM/ABITRAM-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md`) directly binds monomeric G-actin (`GO:0003785`) in pulldown assays, directly cosediments with pre-polymerized F-actin (`GO:0051015`) without depolymerizing filaments, reduces spontaneous G-actin polymerization by 29% in ultracentrifugation cosedimentation assays while buffering cellular F-actin FRAP turnover variance and stabilizing F-actin-associated proximal dendritic branches (`GO:0030833`, `PMID:24782708`).
   - In mouse brain slices and retinal sections (`PMID:24349419`, `PMID:26013685`, `PMID:41617053`), endogenous mouse Simiate is enriched in the core domain of nuclear speckles (`GO:0016607`), co-precipitates with cytosolic and nuclear `FAK1` (preferentially the 80 kDa calpain-cleaved `FAK80` fragment in nuclei, `PMID:35787638`), and its mRNA contains FMRP recognition motifs and co-immunoprecipitates with FMRP (`PMID:24349419`).
   - In primary E17–E18 rat hippocampal neurons (`PMID:24349419`, `PMID:24782708`, `PMID:35787638`), endogenous Simiate localizes to nuclei, nuclear speckles, somata, dendrites (`GO:0030425`), dendritic growth cones (`GO:0030426`), and filopodia tips (`GO:0032433`), while elevated somatic or nuclear Simiate expression promotes proximal dendritic branching and primary dendrite morphogenesis (`GO:0048813`) and elevates active RNAP2 and hnRNP A2/B1 nuclear volumes (`PMID:35787638`).
3. **High-throughput human protein-interaction screens (`GO:0005515` `IPI` rows)**:
   - `PMID:15607035`: Y2H screen against hepatitis C virus `NS5A` (`UniProtKB:P27958-PRO_0000037576`).
   - `PMID:25416956` (Rolland et al. 2014 Y2H) & `PMID:32296183` (Luck et al. 2020 HuRI Y2H): binary interactions with `HMBOX1` (`Q6NT76`), `PUS10` (`Q3MIT2`), `MESD` (`Q14696`), and `PGCKA1` (`Q8IY42`).
   - `PMID:28514442` (BioPlex 2.0) & `PMID:33961781` (BioPlex 3.0): AP-MS co-complex association with `PUS10` (`Q3MIT2`).
   - None of these high-throughput interactome hits has been biochemically validated for functional mechanism; per the generic protein-binding policy, all eight bare `GO:0005515` rows are marked `REMOVE` as uninformative.

## 3. Evaluation of Affinage Deep Research (`ABITRAM-deep-research-affinage.md`)
- **Trust Gates**: Clear — all 5 citations (`PMID:24349419`, `PMID:24782708`, `PMID:26013685`, `PMID:35787638`, `PMID:41617053`) are real peer-reviewed PubMed IDs with no `PMID:bio_` bioRxiv tokens, retractions, or fabricated claims.
- **Strengths**: Accurately captures all five Dahlhaus-lab functional studies, including the 2022 `FAK1`/`FAK80` nuclear/growth-cone interplay (`PMID:35787638`) and the 2026 STED super-resolution study showing Simiate enrichment in the core domain of nuclear speckles (`PMID:41617053`), neither of which is cited yet in human or mouse GOA.
- **Defects & Caveats**:
  1. **Species conflation**: Presents biochemical assays on recombinant mouse Simiate, immunohistochemistry in mouse brain/retina (`Fmr1-/-` mice, STED imaging), and dendritogenesis assays in rat primary hippocampal neurons as direct findings on human `ABITRAM`, without distinguishing what was tested on human cells (endogenous localization to nuclear speckles, lamellipodia, and nuclei plus antibody-blockade speckle compaction/apoptosis in HEK-293 cells).
  2. **Imprecise FMRP interaction wording**: States `"ABITRAM mRNA contains FMRP recognition motifs and the protein co-precipitates with FMRP"`, whereas `PMID:24349419` demonstrated that *Simiate mRNA* co-immunoprecipitates with FMRP (`"to verify an association of FMRP and Simiate mRNA in vivo, FMRP was immunoprecipitated from brain homogenates followed by RT-PCR experiments"`), not a protein–protein co-precipitation between ABITRAM protein and FMRP! (Recorded on the Affinage reference via `finding_status: DISPUTED` with `superseded_by: [PMID:24349419]` and `reference_review` `correctness: LOW_QUALITY`.)
  3. **Coarse `goslim_generic` GO grounding**: Maps molecular activity to `GO:0008092` (`cytoskeletal protein binding`) and localization to `GO:0005654` (`nucleoplasm`) / `GO:0005829` (`cytosol`), losing the experimentally established specific terms `GO:0003785` (`actin monomer binding`), `GO:0051015` (`actin filament binding`), and `GO:0016607` (`nuclear speck`). Following Affinage evaluation rules, none of Affinage's `goslim_generic` IDs are imported.

## 4. Summary of Annotation Review Decisions
- **Core Molecular Functions (`ACCEPT`)**: `GO:0003785` (`actin monomer binding`, IBA/ISS/IEA) and `GO:0051015` (`actin filament binding`, IBA/ISS/IEA) — conserved across mammalian/metazoan ABITRAM (`PTN000342113` and `Q80ZQ9`).
- **Core Biological Process (`ACCEPT`)**: `GO:0030833` (`regulation of actin filament polymerization`, IBA/ISS/IEA).
- **Contextual / Neuronal & Protrusion Processes (`KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`)**: `GO:0051489` (`regulation of filopodium assembly`, IBA/ISS/IEA) and `GO:0048813` (`dendrite morphogenesis`, IBA/ISS/IEA) — biologically valid downstream manifestations of ABITRAM's actin-regulatory and nuclear speckle functions in filopodia and developing neurons, rather than its universal cell-autonomous biochemical activity.
- **Cellular Components**:
  - `ACCEPT`: `GO:0005634` (`nucleus`, IBA/ISS/IEA — also directly shown for endogenous human ABITRAM in HEK-293 in `PMID:24349419`, `PMID:24782708`), `GO:0016607` (`nuclear speck`, ISS/IEA — directly shown for endogenous human ABITRAM with SC35 in HEK-293 in `PMID:24349419`), `GO:0030027` (`lamellipodium`, IBA/ISS/IEA — directly shown for endogenous human ABITRAM in HEK-293 in `PMID:24782708`), `GO:0032433` (`filopodium tip`, IBA/ISS/IEA).
  - `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`: `GO:0030425` (`dendrite`, IBA/IEA) and `GO:0030426` (`growth cone`, ISS/IEA) — specialized neuronal compartments where mammalian ABITRAM acts during arborization.
- **Generic Protein Binding (`REMOVE`)**: All 8 `GO:0005515` (`protein binding`, IPI) rows (`PMID:15607035`, `PMID:25416956`, `PMID:28514442`, `PMID:32296183`, `PMID:33961781`) removed per the protein-binding policy because bare `GO:0005515` conveys no specific molecular function.
- **Nuclear Speckle Mechanism & `GO:0035063` (`nuclear speck organization`) Evaluation**: Existing BP `GO:0035063` (`nuclear speck organization`) and a potential `GO:0005198` (`structural molecule activity`) child were evaluated for the nuclear-speckle role. Although endogenous human ABITRAM localizes to SC35-positive nuclear speckles in HEK-293 cells (`PMID:24349419`), mouse STED nanoscopy resolves Simiate to the speckle core domain (`PMID:41617053`), and acute Chariot-antibody blockade in HEK-293 cells causes speckle rounding/compaction followed by apoptosis (`PMID:24349419`, `PMID:27167171`), these residence and perturbation findings do not yet distinguish direct structural scaffolding (`GO:0005198`) from spliceosomal/`FAK80` recruitment or regulatory maintenance; accordingly, `proposed_new_terms` is left empty and `GO:0035063` is withheld from `NEW` annotations pending mechanistic resolution.
