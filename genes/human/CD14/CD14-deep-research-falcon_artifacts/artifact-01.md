| Ligand / cargo | Classification | Origin | CD14-related biological significance | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lipopolysaccharide (LPS; endotoxin) | PAMP | Outer membrane of Gram-negative bacteria | Principal and best-characterized CD14 ligand. LBP extracts an LPS monomer and transfers it to membrane or soluble CD14; CD14 then presents it to TLR4–MD-2, promoting TLR4 dimerization, inflammatory signaling, and receptor endocytosis. CD14 can also facilitate cytosolic LPS delivery for noncanonical inflammasome activation. | (pqac-00000052, pqac-00000055, pqac-00000060) |
| Peptidoglycan | PAMP | Bacterial cell wall | Directly binds CD14 and supports recognition or presentation of bacterial cell-wall material to pattern-recognition pathways; the precise downstream receptor and response depend on cellular context. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000043) |
| Lipoteichoic acid (LTA) | PAMP | Cell wall of Gram-positive bacteria | CD14 captures or presents LTA and can facilitate TLR2-associated inflammatory responses, extending its function beyond the LPS–TLR4 system. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000041) |
| CpG-rich microbial DNA | PAMP | Unmethylated CpG motifs enriched in bacterial and viral DNA | Reported CD14 ligand/cargo that may facilitate nucleic-acid uptake or presentation to endosomal pattern-recognition receptors, including TLR9. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000043) |
| Double-stranded RNA / poly(I:C)-like RNA | PAMP or experimental PAMP mimic | Viral replication intermediates or synthetic dsRNA analogues | Listed among CD14-associated microbial ligands; may support uptake and delivery to intracellular RNA-sensing pathways, although direct binding and pathway dependence are less firmly characterized than for LPS. | (pqac-00000038) |
| Microbial phospholipids and lipoprotein-related ligands | PAMP | Bacterial membranes | Broadens CD14-mediated recognition of microbial membrane material and can facilitate signaling through CD14-dependent TLR systems, including TLR2-family complexes. | (pqac-00000039, pqac-00000041) |
| Oxidized phospholipids, especially oxPAPC | DAMP | Oxidation of host membrane phospholipids during tissue injury, oxidative stress, atherosclerosis, or inflammation | CD14 binds and internalizes oxPAPC. This can promote TLR-independent NLRP3 inflammasome activation and IL-1β/IL-18 release, sometimes without cell death. | (pqac-00000042, pqac-00000068, pqac-00000069) |
| S100A9 | DAMP / alarmin | Released or extracellularly accumulated during myeloid-cell activation, tissue damage, and inflammation | Reported endogenous CD14-recognized signal that can connect sterile injury to innate inflammatory signaling; the exact CD14-dependent pathway varies by context. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000068) |
| Mitochondrial DNA | DAMP | Released from damaged or dying host cells and disrupted mitochondria | Reported CD14-associated damage signal; may facilitate uptake or presentation of mitochondrial nucleic acids to intracellular innate sensors, linking cellular injury to inflammation. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000068) |
| Heat-shock proteins | DAMP / alarmin | Released or exposed during cellular stress, necrosis, or tissue injury | Reported CD14 ligands that may enhance recognition of stressed or damaged tissue and modulate TLR-associated inflammatory responses. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000068) |
| Fibronectin | DAMP / extracellular-matrix signal | Extracellular matrix, particularly fragments or altered forms generated during tissue damage and remodeling | Reported CD14-recognized endogenous signal that may couple matrix injury or remodeling to innate immune activation. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000068) |
| Biglycan | DAMP / extracellular-matrix proteoglycan | Extracellular matrix; soluble biglycan can be released during tissue injury or remodeling | Interacts with CD14 and can participate in TLR2- or TLR4-associated sterile inflammatory signaling. | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000041) |
| β-Amyloid | DAMP / aggregated host peptide | Aggregates derived from amyloid precursor protein, particularly in neurodegenerative tissue | CD14 binds or captures β-amyloid and contributes to its uptake by microglia and other myeloid cells, potentially affecting both aggregate clearance and neuroinflammatory signaling. | (pqac-00000039, pqac-00000045) |


*Table: This table distinguishes CD14’s best-established microbial ligand, LPS, from additional reported PAMPs and endogenous DAMPs. Because CD14 is a nonenzymatic capture, transfer, and trafficking co-receptor, these molecules are more accurately described as ligands or cargoes rather than catalytic substrates.*
