Gene Description

AUTS2 is a nonenzymatic regulator of transcription and neuronal cytoskeletal organization. In the nucleus, it participates in noncanonical PRC1 complexes containing PCGF3 or PCGF5 and recruits the histone acetyltransferase EP300/P300 and casein kinase II. P300 recruitment promotes transcriptional activation, while recruited CK2 can inhibit RING1B-dependent histone H2A ubiquitination. The AUTS2 HX-repeat region contributes to P300 association. Mouse studies also support cytoplasmic roles in Rac1/Cdc42 signaling, actin organization, neuronal migration and neurite extension. Its nuclear and cytoplasmic activities depend on cellular context and protein form.

Core Functions

Contributes to transcription coactivation in noncanonical PRC1 complexes by binding and recruiting EP300/P300. Human AUTS2 tethered to a reporter promotes RNA polymerase II transcription in a P300- and PRC1-dependent manner. In mouse brain and derived motor neurons, NRF1 associates with AUTS2-containing PRC1 and directs its occupancy at a subset of active genes. AUTS2 supplies recruitment; P300 and CK2 supply the associated acetyltransferase and kinase activities.
