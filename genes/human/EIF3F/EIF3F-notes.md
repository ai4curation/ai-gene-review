## 2026-10-21: activated NOTCH1 and DTX1 evidence

The DUB summary now explicitly includes the positive activated-NOTCH1 and DTX1 findings curated by UniProt from PMID:21124883. The earlier blanket wording about physiological substrate specificity was too broad: activated NOTCH1 is a supported cellular substrate context. Partial depletion of endogenous human EIF3F increased modified activated Notch constructs, with rescue by murine wild-type EIF3F but not its MPN mutant (Results and Figure 2). DTX1 enabled the observed association between eIF3f and activated Notch constructs (Results and Figures 5–6). Notch reporter cocultures provide additional functional evidence (Figure 7). These cellular experiments, their murine constructs and the separate human GST-EIF3F bacterial cleavage assay remain distinguished.

The same original Discussion explicitly leaves the precise monoubiquitinated Notch substrate form unsettled and acknowledges a possible associated DUB (sections A DUB Acting during Notch Activation and eIF3f Is a DUB). Those limits qualify the mechanism; they do not erase the positive Notch findings or establish inactivity. The broader GO:0101005 decision remains, because the inspected experiments do not settle human metal dependence or thiol chemistry. No new Notch-process annotation, DUB location or complex is inferred. The second core retains its term and references while describing this evidence more precisely.
