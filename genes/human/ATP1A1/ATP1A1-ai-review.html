Gene Description

ATP1A1 encodes the catalytic alpha1 subunit of the sodium/potassium ATPase, a widely expressed P-type ion pump in the plasma membrane. Its transmembrane ion-binding sites and cytoplasmic ATPase domains couple ATP hydrolysis to export of three sodium ions and import of two potassium ions. Alpha1 assembles with a beta subunit and can associate with FXYD regulatory proteins. This electrogenic exchange maintains sodium and potassium gradients that support membrane potential, cell-volume control and secondary transport. Membrane polarity and partner-dependent regulation vary between tissues.
