## Function
- STEAP family metalloreductase. It has an N-terminal cytoplasmic FNO-like NADPH-binding domain and a 6-TM
  ferric reductase domain that binds FAD and heme b. The UniProt FUNCTION text is by similarity (ECO:0000250)
  to mouse Q8BWB6 and human STEAP4.
- Mouse Steap2 reduces Fe(III) and Cu(II) and stimulates iron and copper uptake [PMID:16609065 "Steap2, Steap3, and Steap4 not only reduce iron but also copper"]. These experiments used murine homologs expressed in HEK-293T cells.

## Localization
- Human STAMP1 (= STEAP2) GFP fusion: [PMID:12095985 "STAMP1 is localized to the Golgi complex, predominantly to the trans-Golgi network, and to the plasma membrane"]. It is also seen in vesicular tubular structures that colocalize with EEA1.
- Mouse Steap2 colocalizes with Tf/TfR1 endosomes [PMID:16609065 "However, Steap2 colocalizes nearly completely with Tf and TfR1"].
- Native prostate cancer tissue: [PMID:37966111 "more than 85% of all tumor samples throughout all disease progression stages showed more than 75% of all tumor cells as positive for cell surface STEAP2"]. The extracellular loops are recognized by a therapeutic CAR-T (AZD0754). Surface expression is hard to achieve outside prostate cells.
- Conclusion: plasma membrane (multi-pass, with extracellular loops exposed and the NADPH domain cytoplasmic), plus TGN and early/recycling endosomes.
