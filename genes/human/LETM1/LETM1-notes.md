## K+/H+ versus Ca2+/H+

- Original K+/H+ exchanger assignment, then a Ca2+/H+ proposal from a fly RNAi screen
  and liposome reconstitution [PMID:19797662 "Letm1 is a mitochondrial Ca2+/H+ antiporter"; PMID:24344246].
- In intact cells LETM1 does not set Ca2+ extrusion [PMID:24898248 "We conclude that NCLX, but not LETM1, mediates Ca(2+) extrusion from mitochondria."].
- TMBIM5 is the mitochondrial Ca2+/H+ exchanger [PMID:36321428 "we demonstrate that TMBIM5, but not LETM1, is the long-sought mitochondrial CHE"].
- Patient variants abolish K+ efflux [PMID:36055214 "bi-allelic LETM1 variants are associated with defective mitochondrial K+ efflux, swollen mitochondrial matrix structures"].

Decision: K+/H+ antiport is the core activity; Ca2+/H+ antiport and calcium export were
marked as over-annotations (not removed, because reconstitution and PINK1
phosphorylation data, PMID:29123128, show a Ca2+ transport capacity in vitro).

## Membrane organisation

LETM1 deforms proteoliposome membranes (PMID:32139798) and its knockdown causes swelling
and cristae disorganisation (PMID:18628306).
