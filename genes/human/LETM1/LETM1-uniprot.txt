CC       developmental delay and variably impaired intellectual development with
CC       speech delay apparent from infancy. Affected individuals have
CC       hypotonia, poor feeding, poor overall growth, and respiratory distress

CC       cardiomyopathy, diabetes mellitus, combined oxidative phosphorylation

CC       abnormalities on brain imaging. Death in childhood may occur.
