# DNMT1 source evidence and access

This guide provides source locators and scope limits for the review. It is an authored explanation, not independent experimental support. Publication identifiers and cache flags remain those of the genuine downloaded sources. A full-text flag is not proof that the cache includes every experimental section; separately recovered originals are distinguished below.

## Catalysis, readers and chromatin

[PMID:21163962](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1195380): human full-length 1–1616 and truncated constructs, alongside separately identified mouse complexes, establish CpG substrate chemistry and autoinhibition. The Results kinetics do not support an invariant twofold substrate preference. [PMID:26070743](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2015.06.001): Methods describe human 351–1600, tag removal and a C-terminal cloning extension; the LC-MS assay is independent of the crystal structure. [PMID:36414620](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-34779-4): Results and Methods distinguish human DNMT1 351–1616, isopeptide-linked structural H3Ub2 and disulfide kinetic analogs, human rescue and Xenopus replication extracts. Paired K18/K23 recognition is supported; the K14-specific original remains inaccessible.

The [human heterochromatin original](https://diposit.ub.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/9d727630-340f-4893-a197-5f6d13ca6409/content) provides Methods on pp. 37175–37178 and loss/rescue Results on pp. 37179–37183. No isolated catalytic-dependence claim is made. GO:0051718 is a specific child of cytosine-5 methyltransferase activity. The three retained process terms have no pairwise typed ancestor relationship; distinct biological support is still required and is given in the review.

The target's own experimental evidence is legitimate descendant grounding, not circularity. The unchanged historical identities and their present-day source counterparts are recorded separately.

The supported [HPA DNMT1 record](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000130816-DNMT1/subcellular) identifies nucleoplasm by immunofluorescence. Image pixels were not independently read. Nucleoplasm excludes chromosomes by definition, but the positive HPA summary is not a chromosome-exclusion experiment. Both nucleoplasm and chromatin are therefore preserved in the core and in five broad nuclear-row refinements. The sixth nuclear row, from PMID:24492612, retains its more precise pericentric heterochromatin pool alongside nucleoplasm; actual Results and Figure 4B caption identify endogenous human DNMT1/PHF20L1/5mC in HeLa pericentric heterochromatin. This descendant already covers chromatin. The source-specific broad-resolution and natural-product limits remain explicit. Current chromatin is part_of the live chromosome term, and neither chromatin nor pericentric heterochromatin has a typed nucleus ancestor; biological nuclear localization is not inferred from a nonexistent edge. [Reactome entity 212181](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-212181) explicitly represents human P26358 1–1616 in nucleoplasm. Its paths through defined set 212315, complex 212288 and catalyst 212303 were checked. SUMOylation and miR-21 regulation do not make DNMT1 the modifying enzyme or its own RNA substrate. The location belongs to the explicitly represented protein entity. [MitoCoP/OpenCell extraction](DNMT1-bioinformatics/README.md) records raw hashes, public downloads and reproducible target selection.
