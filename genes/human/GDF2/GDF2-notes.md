# GDF2 biological notes

## 2026-11-05

BMP9 is synthesized as a precursor and its mature ligand can remain associated with a prodomain. The observed 4YCG complex contains human mature ligand and a mouse prodomain; this mixed-species structure is not an entirely human precursor experiment. [PMID:25751889](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25751889/)

Context-dependent vascular responses justify retaining positive and negative regulatory annotations separately. [PMID:17068149](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17068149/), [PMID:20406889](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20406889/)

Joint Gdf2/Bmp10 physiology does not isolate GDF2-specific intracellular iron handling. [PMID:39453386](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39453386/)

Barrier improvement leaves junction-assembly specificity unresolved. [PMID:32561494](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32561494/)

The [source guide](GDF2-source-guide.md) keeps construct, assay, erratum and target-identification limits next to primary locators. The [evidence archive](GDF2-source-evidence.json) preserves literal contexts and the partial nature of flat projections from OR expressions.

### Receptor recognition and redox state

Direct binding to ALK1 supports mature BMP9 receptor recognition; receptor-domain NMR and docking remain distinct evidence. Covalent and noncovalent solution dimers retain signaling activity but differ in persistence and proteolytic susceptibility. These biochemical states do not establish additional natural splice products or endogenous clearance rates. [PMID:22799562](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22799562/); [PMID:25237187](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25237187/).
