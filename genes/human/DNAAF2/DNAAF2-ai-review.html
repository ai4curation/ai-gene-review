Gene Description

DNAAF2, also called KTU, is a cytoplasmic dynein assembly factor that helps build outer and inner axonemal dynein arms before their delivery to motile cilia and flagella. It associates with other assembly factors and chaperone machinery, supporting heavy-chain stability and incorporation into dynein complexes. A cytosolic pool exchanges with dynamic dynein axonemal particles in vertebrate multiciliated cells. Loss of DNAAF2 disrupts respiratory-ciliary and sperm motility and causes primary ciliary dyskinesia.

DNAAF2 biological and evidence notes

DNAAF2/KTU helps build axonemal dynein arms in the cytoplasm. Human KTU deficiency affects outer and inner arms. The discriminating Chlamydomonas experiment shows that residual heavy chain fails to associate with intermediate/light chains even though their smaller subcomplex forms. This supports performed preassembly work and a heavy-chain stability contribution, rather than assigning dynein motor activity to an assembly factor PMID:19052621(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19052621/).

The supported core is one integrated arm-preassembly function. Human respiratory fractionation and HPA support a cytoplasmic/cytosolic pool. Tagged Xenopus Ktu occupies dynamic DynAP foci with dyneins and assembly factors; human HEATR2/LRRC6 imaging in the same study is not human DNAAF2 imaging. Cytosol and DynAP are retained as two supported pools, without exclusivity or a stable-complex interpretation PMID:23872636(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23872636/), PMID:30561330(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30561330/), PMID:33263282(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33263282/), HPA DNAAF2.

Targeted DYX1C1 and PIH1D3 interaction assays are positive. DNAAF2 also associates with DYX1C1 isoform a in human-cell LUMIER and proteomics. The proposed R2SD particle remains hypothetical; the 2018 client-stabilization and liprin-alpha2 assembly assays concern PIH1D2/R2SP. Mouse Ktu–DNAI2/Hsp70 extract associations are not purified binary human reactions, and Hsp90 was negative in that assay PMID:23872636(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23872636/), PMID:28041644(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28041644/), PMID:29844425(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29844425/), PMID:19052621(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19052621/).

The imported interaction partner Q7Z6J8 is UBE3D. Its positive associations are represented by a 2016 TAP spoke, 2021 BioPlex records and the 2025 primary U2OS graph edge 608480. These data do not establish DNAAF2 ubiquitination or a specific ligase-recognition mechanism. Positive generic associations are retained as non-core under the scoped project instruction PMID:27173435(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27173435/), PMID:33961781(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33961781/), PMID:40205054(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40205054/).
