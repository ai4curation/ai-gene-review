# DNAAF2 biological and evidence notes

DNAAF2/KTU helps build axonemal dynein arms in the cytoplasm. Human KTU deficiency affects outer and inner arms. The discriminating Chlamydomonas experiment shows that residual heavy chain fails to associate with intermediate/light chains even though their smaller subcomplex forms. This supports performed preassembly work and a heavy-chain stability contribution, rather than assigning dynein motor activity to an assembly factor [PMID:19052621](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19052621/).

The supported core is one integrated arm-preassembly function. Human respiratory fractionation and HPA support a cytoplasmic/cytosolic pool. Tagged Xenopus Ktu occupies dynamic DynAP foci with dyneins and assembly factors; human HEATR2/LRRC6 imaging in the same study is not human DNAAF2 imaging. Cytosol and DynAP are retained as two supported pools, without exclusivity or a stable-complex interpretation [PMID:23872636](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23872636/), [PMID:30561330](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30561330/), [PMID:33263282](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33263282/), [HPA DNAAF2](https://www.proteinatlas.org/ENSG00000165506/subcellular).

Targeted DYX1C1 and PIH1D3 interaction assays are positive. DNAAF2 also associates with DYX1C1 isoform a in human-cell LUMIER and proteomics. The proposed R2SD particle remains hypothetical; the 2018 client-stabilization and liprin-alpha2 assembly assays concern PIH1D2/R2SP. Mouse Ktu–DNAI2/Hsp70 extract associations are not purified binary human reactions, and Hsp90 was negative in that assay [PMID:23872636](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23872636/), [PMID:28041644](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28041644/), [PMID:29844425](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29844425/), [PMID:19052621](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19052621/).

The imported interaction partner Q7Z6J8 is UBE3D. Its positive associations are represented by a 2016 TAP spoke, 2021 BioPlex records and the 2025 primary U2OS graph edge 608480. These data do not establish DNAAF2 ubiquitination or a specific ligase-recognition mechanism. Positive generic associations are retained as non-core under the scoped [project instruction](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/d06a9a79482eeea9597a393455fb67fe5151be5b/projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions) [PMID:27173435](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27173435/), [PMID:33961781](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33961781/), [PMID:40205054](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40205054/).

The imported CFAP300 pair cites PMID:29727692. Selected original Methods and target-name searching did not identify its binding experiment. The companion PMID:29727693 explicitly supplies the DNAAF2 yeast two-hybrid/co-immunoprecipitation evidence. The imported source identity remains unchanged and its attribution is flagged, while the independently positive interaction is retained [PMID:29727692](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29727692/), [PMID:29727693](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29727693/).

All four PAINT IBDs occur on the reconstructed human lineage; the 837-residue target sequence exactly matches its alignment row. The source list includes valid target experimental grounding. Mouse and zebrafish donor context is preserved: zebrafish single ktu-mutant sperm can retain normal movement despite subtle inner-arm defects, whereas Kupffer-vesicle motility is abnormal and pih1d2/ktu double mutants show stronger defects. This context does not negate the human preassembly role [PMID:23525783](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23525783/), [PMID:29741156](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29741156/). Human PIH1D1 chromatin biology is separate family context and is not transferred to DNAAF2 [PMID:22368283](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22368283/).

The official products are Q9NVR5-1, 837 residues, and Q9NVR5-2, 789 residues, whose internal deletion is canonical residues 622–669. Both sequences were independently reconstructed against official FASTAs. The respiratory Western doublet was not an accession-resolved activity test; neither equivalent activity nor inactivity is established for the natural shorter product [UniProt Q9NVR5](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9NVR5/entry), [PMID:19052621](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19052621/).

No new annotation was added. The extracellular-location inference is rejected because the upstream process describes fluid moved by epithelial cilia, not a secreted DNAAF2 molecule. The mechanistic core omits an unestablished folding or motor molecular function and a named stable R2SD complex.
