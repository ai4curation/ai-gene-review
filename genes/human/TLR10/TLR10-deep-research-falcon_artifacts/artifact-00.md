| Ligand/PAMP or stimulus | Receptor complex | Cellular location | Reported biological effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Viral double-stranded RNA (dsRNA) | TLR10 homodimer proposed; MyD88 recruited | Endosomal membrane | Direct binding has been reported *in vitro* under acidic conditions; associated with MyD88 recruitment and suppression of IRF7-dependent type-I interferon production. Its status as a physiological ligand remains provisional. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000008, pqac-00000017) |
| FSL-1, a synthetic diacylated lipopeptide | TLR2/TLR10 proposed; canonical comparator is TLR2/TLR6 | Primarily plasma membrane | TLR10 knockdown altered FSL-1-induced inflammatory outputs, including IL-8, IL-1, and CCL20. This establishes regulatory involvement but not definitive direct binding by TLR10. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000040) |
| Pam3Cys/Pam3CSK4, synthetic triacylated lipopeptides | TLR2/TLR10 heterodimer proposed; canonical comparator is TLR2/TLR1 | Plasma membrane | Pam3CSK4 enhances TLR2–TLR10 association and can promote MyD88 recruitment; TLR10 has also reduced Pam3Cys-induced IL-8 in some systems, consistent with context-dependent modulation of TLR2 signaling. (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000050, pqac-00000054) |
| Lipopolysaccharide (LPS) | TLR2/TLR10 proposed in some pathogen contexts; experimentally linked to TLR4–TLR10 trafficking/crosstalk | Plasma membrane followed by cytoplasmic vesicles or early endosomes | LPS induces TLR10 internalization in neutrophils and regulates its expression through TLR4, ROS, and NF-κB. Knockdown or antibody studies show altered cytokine release, but LPS is not established as a direct TLR10 ligand. (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000031) |
| Flagellin | TLR10-containing complex proposed; partner not resolved | Presumed plasma membrane | Flagellin-responsive cytokines decrease after TLR10 knockdown in some human cell systems, suggesting regulatory participation; direct recognition and receptor stoichiometry remain unproven. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000040) |
| HIV-1 gp41 | TLR10-containing complex; exact dimer partner unresolved | Plasma membrane proposed | Reported to trigger TLR10-associated NF-κB activation and IL-8 production. Direct ligand specificity and the required receptor partner need further validation. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000017) |
| Influenza A virus components or viral RNA–protein complexes | TLR10-containing complex; TLR2 cooperation proposed in some reports | Cell surface and/or endosomal compartments | TLR10 loss-of-function weakens some influenza-induced inflammatory responses, whereas endosomal TLR10 may regulate nucleic-acid signaling. The exact viral molecular ligand is unresolved. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000027) |
| *Listeria monocytogenes* PAMPs | TLR2/TLR10 heterodimer proposed | Plasma membrane of epithelial or myeloid cells | TLR10 can promote CCL20 and IL-8 production and host defense in some models; silencing increased bacterial viability. Other systems support broader inhibitory effects, indicating cell-context dependence. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000058, pqac-00000062) |
| *Helicobacter pylori* components, including atypical LPS-associated stimuli | TLR2/TLR10 heterodimer proposed | Plasma membrane of gastric epithelial and immune cells | Reported to enhance NF-κB-associated inflammatory signaling in gastric-cell models, although TLR10 can also restrain TLR2 responses. Direct binding to a chemically defined *H. pylori* ligand has not been firmly established. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000053, pqac-00000058) |
| *Borrelia burgdorferi* PAMPs | TLR2/TLR10 proposed | Plasma membrane | TLR10 engagement or expression has been associated with altered inflammatory cytokine production in response to *B. burgdorferi*. The responsible microbial molecule and direct binding mechanism remain unresolved. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002) |


*Table: Summary of candidate TLR10 ligands or stimuli, proposed dimer partners, sites of action, and reported immune effects. Most assignments remain provisional because no endogenous ligand–receptor pair has been definitively validated.*
