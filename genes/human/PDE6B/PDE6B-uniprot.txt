CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic beta subunit of the rod-specific cGMP
CC       phosphodiesterase (PDE6) complex, which hydrolyzes 3',5'-cyclic GMP in
CC       the phototransduction cascade. The PDE6 holoenzyme consists of two
CC       catalytic subunits (PDE6A and PDE6B) and two inhibitory gamma subunits
CC       (PDE6G) (PubMed:20940301). Light-activated GNAT1 relieves gamma
CC       subunit-mediated inhibition, enabling the catalytic subunits to
CC       hydrolyze cGMP and thereby mediate visual signal transduction and
CC       amplification (PubMed:8394174). Decreased cytosolic cGMP levels result
CC       in closure of cGMP-gated cation channels at the plasma membrane,
CC       leading to rod photoreceptor hyperpolarization (Probable). Involved in
CC       retinal circadian rhythm photoentrainment via modulation of UVA and
CC       orange light-induced phase-shift of the retina clock (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P23440, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20940301,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8394174, ECO:0000305}.
