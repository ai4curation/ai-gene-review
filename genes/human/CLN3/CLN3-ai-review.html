Gene Description

CLN3 (battenin) is a multipass endolysosomal membrane protein whose loss causes juvenile neuronal ceroid lipofuscinosis. It conducts chloride and contributes to lysosomal ion balance and degradative function. A detailed trafficking study identified cytosolic-region binding to active Rab7 and RILP, linking CLN3 to the positioning and trafficking of late endosomal compartments. CLN3 deficiency also disrupts lipid distribution, lysosomal metabolite clearance and neuronal physiology. Cellular lipid-binding and transport observations complement these roles, while the exact mechanism of glycerophosphodiester clearance and proposed soluble glycolipid transfer remains unresolved.
