# KAT2B notes

Family pass for PTHR45750, 2026-11-24. KAT2A/GCN5 and KAT2B/PCAF are the two human PTHR45750 paralogues and are alternative GNAT catalytic subunits of SAGA/STAGA/TFTC and ATAC histone acetyltransferase complexes.

No source checked in this review established KAT2B as a sequence-specific RNA polymerase II transcription regulatory-region DNA-binding protein or as a cyclin-dependent protein kinase inhibitor.

No source checked in this review established KAT2B as a human A-band, I-band, actomyosin, vasodilation, neuron-projection-development, parathyroid-hormone-response, or memory effector.

No KAT2B publication checked in this review used acetyl phosphate as the acyl donor.
