# KAT2B notes

Family pass for PTHR45750, 2026-11-24. KAT2A/GCN5 and KAT2B/PCAF are the two human PTHR45750 paralogues and are alternative GNAT catalytic subunits of SAGA/STAGA/TFTC and ATAC histone acetyltransferase complexes. [PMID:18838386 "We show that human ATAC complexes incorporate in addition to GCN5 or PCAF"]

The KAT2B/PCAF sequence-specific DNA-binding, CDK-inhibitor, A-band, I-band, actomyosin, vasodilation, memory, neuron-projection-development, parathyroid-hormone-response, and embryonic-development rows were judged unsupported transfers beside the reviewed human record, which curates PCAF as a nuclear/cytoplasmic GNAT-family acetyltransferase with centrosomal PLK4, MAPRE1/EB1, ACLY, RRP9, TBX5, and FSP1 substrate contexts.
