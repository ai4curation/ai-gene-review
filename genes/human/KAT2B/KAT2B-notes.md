# KAT2B notes

Family pass for PTHR45750, 2026-11-24. KAT2A/GCN5 and KAT2B/PCAF are the two human PTHR45750 paralogues and are alternative GNAT catalytic subunits of SAGA/STAGA/TFTC and ATAC histone acetyltransferase complexes. [PMID:18838386 "We show that human ATAC complexes incorporate in addition to GCN5 or PCAF"]
