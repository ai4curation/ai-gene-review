# GSK3A notes

Automated deep research was attempted with `just deep-research-falcon human GSK3A --fallback perplexity-lite`, but neither the Falcon provider nor the Perplexity-lite fallback was available in this checkout. This review therefore uses the fetched UniProt and GOA records, cached GOA publications, Reactome entries, the existing PANTHER PTHR24057 family review, the existing GSK3B review, and the Wnt module.

GSK3A encodes glycogen synthase kinase-3 alpha, one of the two human GSK-3 paralogs. PANTHER places the protein in `PTHR24057:SF14`, the vertebrate GSK3A subfamily. The fetched PAINT-loss report showed no IRD or IKR losses on the GSK3A lineage, so the root serine/threonine kinase placement and the vertebrate Wnt/protein-turnover placements do not need pruning for this paralog. The alpha and beta paralogs share the catalytic GSK-3 core and differ at the termini: GSK3A has the N-terminal Gly-rich extension and inhibitory Ser21, while GSK3B is inhibited at Ser9.

The core molecular function is ATP-dependent protein serine/threonine kinase activity. UniProt describes GSK3A as a constitutively active protein kinase and records generic protein-serine, tau-serine and tau-threonine phosphorylation reactions [file:human/GSK3A/GSK3A-uniprot.txt "Constitutively active protein kinase"; file:human/GSK3A/GSK3A-uniprot.txt "L-seryl-[protein] + ATP"; file:human/GSK3A/GSK3A-uniprot.txt "L-seryl-[tau protein] + ATP"; file:human/GSK3A/GSK3A-uniprot.txt "L-threonyl-[tau protein] + ATP"]. Its activity is activated by Tyr279 phosphorylation and inhibited by AKT1-mediated Ser21 phosphorylation downstream of insulin [file:human/GSK3A/GSK3A-uniprot.txt "Activated by phosphorylation at Tyr-279"; file:human/GSK3A/GSK3A-uniprot.txt "Phosphorylated by AKT1 at Ser-21"]. Fang et al. showed that protein kinase A also phosphorylates and inactivates the alpha and beta isoforms at these inhibitory N-terminal sites [PMID:11035810 "Protein kinase A physically associates with, phosphorylates, and inactivates both isoforms of GSK-3"].

GSK3A and GSK3B are the two human paralogs that can occupy the GSK3 kinase role in the beta-catenin destruction complex. UniProt explicitly lists GSK3A in APC/AXIN/beta-catenin phosphorylation and in Wnt regulation [file:human/GSK3A/GSK3A-uniprot.txt "In Wnt signaling"]. The current Wnt module models a reusable Axin/APC/CK1/GSK3 destruction-complex role and this review extends that GSK3 family representative list to include UniProtKB:P49840.

Glycogen/insulin regulation is GSK3A-specific in human skeletal muscle cells. Ciaraldi et al. reported that reducing GSK3A expression improved insulin stimulation of glycogen synthase fractional velocity and glucose uptake, while overexpression impaired both insulin responses and led to the conclusion that GSK3alpha is an important regulator of muscle insulin action [PMID:17569761 "GSK3alpha is an important regulator of muscle insulin action"].

The RICTOR branch is a direct phosphorylation-coupled protein-turnover output. Koo et al. found that GSK3 associated with RICTOR and directly phosphorylated Thr1695 in a CDC4 phosphodegron, enabling FBXW7 interaction, ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation [PMID:25897075 "GSK3 was associated with rictor and directly phosphorylated the Thr-1695 site"]. This supports GSK3A annotations to RICTOR ubiquitination/degradation as real but substrate-specific.

The 2026 STARD3 paper supports the cholesterol-transport GOA branch as a new non-core substrate branch. Eichler et al. found that GSK3alpha and GSK3beta redundantly phosphorylate STARD3 Ser209 in its Phospho-FFAT motif, enabling STARD3/VAP-dependent ER to late endosome/lysosome contacts [PMID:41741634 "GSK3α and GSK3β have a redundant activity"].

The amyloid-beta and tau annotations are biologically plausible for GSK3A but should not be multiplied into every Alzheimer downstream phenotype. Phiel et al. concluded that GSK-3alpha is required for maximal APP processing and amyloid-beta production [PMID:12761548 "GSK-3alpha, which is required for maximal processing of APP"]. The tau-protein kinase term is kept from UniProt and the family review, while bare tau binding is non-core because it is secondary to the kinase activity.

GSK3A has several broader neuronal, apoptotic, cardiac, adrenergic, glucocorticoid and mitophagy annotations. These describe pathway contexts or mouse-orthology transfers for a pleiotropic kinase. They are retained as non-core when the claim is specific, marked over-annotated when the propagation reaches beyond a direct human GSK3A mechanism, and removed only for the obsolete `GO:1903749` protein-localization-to-mitochondrion row.

The large block of IntAct `GO:0005515` protein binding rows is uninformative relative to the substrate and pathway annotations above, so every bare protein-binding row is removed under the project protein-binding policy without disputing the recorded interactions.
