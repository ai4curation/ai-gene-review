## Identity and topology
- Glycoprotein NMB (osteoactivin, DC-HIL, HGFIN); type I transmembrane glycoprotein of the PMEL/NMB family.
  Signal peptide 1-22, extracellular domain 23-498 with RGD cell-attachment motif (64-66), TM, cytoplasmic tail
  520-572 (UniProt).
- "GPNMB belongs to the premelanosome protein (PMEL)/NMB family" [PMID:29336782].

## Function
- DC-HIL on APCs binds syndecan-4 on activated T cells via heparinase-sensitive saccharides and inhibits T-cell
  activation, cytokine secretion and S-phase entry [PMID:19350579].
- ADAM10 sheds the ectodomain; "the shed GPNMB/OA ECD is capable of inducing endothelial cell migration in vitro"
  [PMID:20711474].
- EGFR:GPNMB heterodimer recruits BRK downstream of HB-EGF (LINK-A lncRNA study) [PMID:26751287].
- Neuroprotective extracellular fragments in ALS/ischaemia models [PMID:22891158, PMID:25010402].
- Biallelic truncating variants cause amyloidosis cutis dyschromica [PMID:29336782].

## Localization
- Plasma membrane (type I, ECD outside), melanosome membrane (proteomics, PMID:12643545, 17081065), early endosome
  membrane (PMID:29336782); ectodomain shed to extracellular space.
- Conclusion: PM with ECD exposed (antibody-drug conjugate target), plus melanosome/endosome pools and shed ECD.
