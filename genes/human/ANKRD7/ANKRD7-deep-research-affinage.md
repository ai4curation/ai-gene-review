# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ANKRD7 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ANKRD7 (TSA806) is a testis-specific ankyrin-repeat protein implicated in Sertoli cell maturation during spermatogenesis [PMID:8812458, PMID:19277502]. Its mRNA is expressed specifically in the testis among adult human tissues, and the protein contains multiple contiguous ankyrin (cdc10/swi6) repeats [PMID:8812458]. Within the testis, ANKRD7 expression is restricted to Sertoli cells, where it localizes to the nucleus and is induced in a maturation-dependent manner beginning around 20 days postpartum as Sertoli cells differentiate into their mature form [PMID:19277502]. Beyond this expression and localization profile, no enzymatic activity, binding partners, or direct functional mechanism have been characterized in the available corpus.
