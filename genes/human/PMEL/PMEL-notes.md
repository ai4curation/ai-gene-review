# PMEL (Pmel17/gp100) curation notes

Deep research (falcon/perplexity-lite) unavailable; notes from UniProt P40967 and cached papers.

- Single biosynthetic route ER -> Golgi -> endosomes -> melanosomes [PMID:17991747]; rapid ER/cis-Golgi
  processing [PMID:15096515]; transiently at cell surface (UniProt, PMID:16760433, 30988362).
- Sorted onto MVB ILVs (CD63, ApoE) [PMID:11694580; PMID:26387950].
- Processing: PC cleavage, ADAM10/17 S2 [PMID:19047044], BACE2 juxtamembrane [PMID:23754390], gamma-secretase.
- RPT domain forms amyloid at acidic pH, promotes eumelanin formation [PMID:19666488].
- ME20-S secreted soluble form [PMID:8179825]; exosome pool [PMID:26387950].

Localization conclusion: predominantly intracellular - ER/Golgi (biosynthetic), multivesicular
premelanosome ILVs and melanosome lumen (functional amyloid). Only transient/minor plasma membrane pool;
not a stable cell-surface protein.
