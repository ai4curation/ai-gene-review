CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms physiological amyloids that play a central role in
CC       melanosome morphogenesis and pigmentation. The maturation of
CC       unpigmented premelanosomes from stage I to II is marked by assembly of
CC       processed amyloidogenic fragments into parallel fibrillar sheets, which
CC       elongate the vesicle into a striated ellipsoidal shape. In pigmented
CC       stage III and IV melanosomes, the amyloid matrix serves as a platform
CC       where eumelanin precursors accumulate at high local concentrations for
CC       pigment formation. May prevent pigmentation-associated toxicity by
CC       sequestering toxic reaction intermediates of eumelanin biosynthesis
CC       pathway. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11694580, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21962903,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26387950, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26694611,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28272432, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30988362}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Represents a potent melanoma-specific antigen. Among melanoma
CC       non-mutated self-peptides, G9-154 (KTWGQYWQV), G9-209 (ITDQVPFSV) and
CC       G9-280 (YLEPGPVTA), appear to act as immunodominant common epitopes
CC       that stimulate anti-tumor immune response mediated by HLA-A-restricted
CC       cytotoxic T cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:7706734,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8022805}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer; disulfide-linked. Dimerization in the endoplasmic
CC       reticulum and early Golgi prevents premature fibril formation. The
CC       dimers are resolved to monomers in late- or post-Golgi compartments
CC       (PubMed:26694611). Heterooligomer; amyloid-type. Processed
CC       amyloidogenic fragments assemble into fibrils that further organize
CC       into beta-sheet quaternary amyloid structures (PubMed:28272432,
