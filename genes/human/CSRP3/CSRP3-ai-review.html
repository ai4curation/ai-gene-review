Gene Description

CSRP3 encodes muscle LIM protein (MLP), a striated-muscle protein with two LIM domains in its full-length form. MLP binds and cross-links actin filaments and interacts with alpha-actinin and telethonin at the Z disc, supporting sarcomere integrity and the mechanical behavior of cardiac muscle. Nuclear MLP associates with myogenic transcription factors, while a short splice product shows distinct interaction and differentiation effects. Altered MLP function is associated with cardiomyopathy.

Short MLP-b splice class SPLICE VARIANT
ID: CSRP3_SHORT_SPLICE_PRODUCT
UNIPROT ISOFORM: P50461-2
The current official product is 58 residues, retaining the first 37 residues followed by a 21-residue alternative tail. The historical JN898958.1/AFH66949.1 construct in PMID:24860983 is 59 residues and adds one terminal histidine relative to the current sequence. That historical product binds MLP-a and associates with alpha-actinin/telethonin in tissue assays, but lacks the tested direct CFL2, MyoD and myogenin interactions and attenuates myogenic differentiation and MLP-a/CFL2 effects. These historical functional results are not assumed to be experimentally identical for the current 58-residue sequence.
