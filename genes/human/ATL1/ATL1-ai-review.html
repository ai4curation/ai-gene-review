Gene Description

Atlastin-1 is a membrane-anchored dynamin-like GTPase that promotes fusion of endoplasmic reticulum membranes. Nucleotide-dependent self-association and conformational changes couple GTP hydrolysis to membrane tethering and fusion, helping build and maintain a connected tubular ER network. ATL1 also associates with ER-shaping proteins such as reticulons, REEP proteins and spastin. Its function is particularly important in neurons, and pathogenic variants cause forms of hereditary spastic paraplegia.
