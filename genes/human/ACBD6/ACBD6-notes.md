# ACBD6 (human, UniProtKB:Q9BR61) Curation Notes

## Identity and Domain Architecture
- **Gene / Protein**: Human `ACBD6` (`UniProtKB:Q9BR61`, sequence version 1, 282 aa; `HGNC:23339`; `NCBITaxon:9606`).
- **PANTHER Subfamily**: `PANTHER:PTHR24119:SF0` (*ACYL-COA-BINDING DOMAIN-CONTAINING PROTEIN 6*).
- **Modular Structure**: Soluble monomeric intracellular protein lacking signal peptides, transmembrane helices, lipid anchors, or peroxisomal targeting signals (`PMID:18268358`, `PMID:26290611`, `PMID:37951597`). Contains:
  - **N-terminal Acyl-CoA-Binding (ACB) domain** (residues 42–127; solution NMR structure `PDB:2COP`; `InterPro:IPR000582`): four-helix bundle with conserved acyl-CoA binding pocket residues `Phe46` (`αH1`), CoA 3'-phosphoadenosine headgroup triad `Tyr69`/`Lys73` (`αH2`) and `Lys95` (`αH3`), regulatory loop phosphorylation sites `Ser106` and `Ser108` (`PMID:20068231`, `PMID:30642881`), and essential acyl-chain cavity residue `Tyr114` (`αH4`) (`PMID:26621918`, `PMID:30642881`).
  - **C-terminal Ankyrin-repeat (ANK) domain** (`ANK1` residues 191–220, `ANK2` residues 224–253; `InterPro:IPR002110`): mediates direct protein-protein interaction with the C-terminal catalytic domain of human `NMT1` (`UniProtKB:P30419`) and `NMT2` (`UniProtKB:O60551`) (`PMID:26621918`, `PMID:32108178`).

## Synthesized Biology
1. **Selective Fatty-Acyl-CoA Carrier & Phospholipid Remodeling Regulator (`GO:0000062`, `GO:0036151`)**:
   - Recombinant human `ACBD6` binds medium- and long-chain fatty-acyl-CoAs (`C6`–`C20`) with a strong preference for unsaturated long-chain thioesters (`C18:1-CoA`, `C18:2-CoA`, `C20:4-CoA`) over saturated `C16:0-CoA`, and does not bind unesterified fatty acids (`PMID:18268358`, `PMID:26290611`). Deleting the C-terminal ANK repeats (`ACBD6-ΔC`) leaves acyl-CoA binding affinity and chain-length preference intact (`PMID:18268358`).
   - By sequestering free acyl-CoAs, `ACBD6` prevents detergent-like acyl-CoA toxicity and gates acyl-donor transfer to membrane lysophospholipid acyltransferases such as `LPCAT1` during Lands-cycle re-acylation of lysophosphatidylcholine (`LPC`) into phosphatidylcholine (`PC`) (`PMID:26290611`). In `ACBD6.KO` human cells, de novo acyl-CoA synthesis is unaltered, whereas `LPCAT`-mediated `PC` formation from `LPC` and neutral lipid droplet stores are significantly reduced (`PMID:36551154`).
2. **Allosteric Activator & Competitive Protector of N-Myristoyltransferases (`GO:0008047`)**:
   - `ACBD6` physically associates with `NMT2` (stronger) and `NMT1` in human cells (`PMID:26621918`, `PMID:32296183`, `PMID:33961781`, `PMID:40205054`).
   - Binding of the C-terminal ANK module to `NMT2` is necessary and sufficient to shield the myristoyl-CoA (`C14:0-CoA`) active site from competitive inhibition by abundant cellular palmitoyl-CoA (`C16:0-CoA`) or synthetic competitors (`PMID:26621918`, `PMID:32108178`). Grafting the `ACBD6` ANK module onto `ACBD1` transfers `NMT`-stimulatory activity to the chimera (`PMID:32108178`).
   - Acyl-CoA binding to the N-terminal ACB pocket (`Phe46`, `Tyr114`) acts as a positive allosteric effector of the ANK module; pocket mutants `Y114A` and `FKKY-AAAA` fail to stimulate `NMT2` (`PMID:26621918`, `PMID:30642881`, `PMID:32108178`). Phosphorylation of `Ser106` (stoichiometrically phosphorylated during mitosis, `PMID:20068231`) and `Ser108` in the ACB loop increases acyl-CoA binding capacity and further potentiates `NMT` stimulation (`PMID:30642881`).
3. **Subcellular Localization vs Pathogen Hijacking & False Plant PAINT Propagation**:
   - In uninfected human and mammalian cells, `ACBD6` resides in the **cytosol** (`GO:0005829`, core site of `NMT1`/`NMT2` holoenzyme assembly and soluble acyl-CoA buffering) and **nucleus** (`GO:0005634`, non-core pool with no established nuclear-specific activity), and is absent from peroxisomes (unlike `ACBD5`) and uninfected cytosolic lipid droplets (`PMID:25604091`, `PMID:37951597`).
   - During *Chlamydia trachomatis* infection, host `ACBD6` is exhaustively depleted from the host nucleus, recruited onto lipid droplets, and translocated into the bacterial inclusion lumen where it buffers acyl-CoAs for the bacterial acyltransferase `CT775` (`PMID:25604091`). Lipid droplet association is strictly pathogen-induced and not an uninfected human `ACBD6` location.
   - `PANTHER:PTN001191022` `IBA` assertions for `GO:0005783` (`endoplasmic reticulum`) and `GO:0005886` (`plasma membrane`) are false over-propagations seeded exclusively by *Arabidopsis thaliana* Class-II membrane ACBPs `ACBP1` (`AT5G53470`, grouped inside `PTHR24119:SF0`) and `ACBP2` (`AT4G27780`, `PTHR24119:SF4`), which possess a land-plant-specific N-terminal transmembrane helix absent from soluble metazoan `ACBD6` (`PMID:12650615`).
4. **Human Disease Genetics (`PMID:32108178`, `PMID:37951597`)**:
   - Bi-allelic loss-of-function truncations (including `p.Glu63Ter`, `p.Tyr72Ter`, `p.Lys73fs`, `p.Gln94Ter`) and ANK-domain missense variants (`p.Asp201Gly`, `p.His216Arg`) cause autosomal recessive `ACBD6`-related neurodevelopmental disorder with progressive and complex movement disorders (`NEDPM`, `MIM:620785`: dystonia, parkinsonism, ataxia, microcephaly, intellectual disability).
   - While metabolic radiolabeling in two patient fibroblast lines (`PMID:32108178`) and `ACBD6.KO` HeLa cells (`PMID:36551154`) showed reduced myristate incorporation and competitor sensitivity, click-chemistry proteomics (`YnMyr`) in `PMID:37951597` revealed *higher* `YnMyr` incorporation across 68 co- and 18 post-translationally N-myristoylated proteins in patient fibroblasts (and globally elevated `YnMyr` labeling alongside specific depleted neurodevelopmental targets in `acbd6`-knockout zebrafish and *Xenopus*), indicating complex dysregulation of acyl-CoA competition/salvage rather than uniform loss of all N-myristoylation.
