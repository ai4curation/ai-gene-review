# FANCF source guide

FANCF is an integral organizing subunit of the Fanconi anemia core complex. Human assembly-mutant and complementation experiments support its structural contribution to interstrand cross-link repair; conserved hydroxyurea-response evidence preserves broader replication-stress scope. FANCL and UBE2T perform ubiquitin transfer. No FANCF nuclease, polymerase or ubiquitin-transfer activity is inferred from its presence in a reaction complex.
