# CDC42BPB notes

Per-provider deep research was attempted for ROCK1 with `perplexity`, but the local deep-research client reported that only the unavailable `claude_code` provider was installed. This family-slice review therefore uses the fetched UniProt, GOA, PAINT and PMID caches as the evidence base.

CDC42BPB encodes MRCK beta, a CDC42-binding paralog of CDC42BPA in the PTHR22988 AGC kinase family. Manual review should keep direct MRCK beta evidence separate from inferences through MRCK alpha or ROCK1/ROCK2 and avoid promoting broad cytoskeletal phenotypes into unsupported core biological-process annotations.

## Curation summary

The completed review keeps MRCK beta as a CDC42-regulated serine/threonine kinase that acts at plasma-membrane and actomyosin pools to support cell migration. Direct and transferred actomyosin rows were accepted, ZO-1/leading-edge localization was kept as a peripheral site, and cytosol, cytoskeleton, anchoring-junction and broad signal-transduction rows without exact accessible support were left `UNDECIDED`.
