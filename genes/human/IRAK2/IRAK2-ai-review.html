Gene Description

IRAK2 (interleukin-1 receptor-associated kinase-like 2) is a cytosolic member of the Pelle/IRAK family with an N-terminal death domain, a kinase-like domain in which Asn replaces the catalytic Asp, and a C-terminal region with TRAF6-binding motifs. After IL-1 receptor or Toll-like receptor engagement, MYD88 recruits IRAK4 and the MYD88-IRAK4 death-domain assembly recruits IRAK2 (or IRAK1) to form the top layer of the helical Myddosome. IRAK4 phosphorylates IRAK2, which then engages TRAF6 and promotes its K63-linked ubiquitination, leading to NF-kappaB and MAPK activation and inflammatory cytokine production. IRAK2 is partly redundant with IRAK1; in mouse macrophages it dominates sustained signalling because, unlike IRAK1, it is not degraded, whereas in human macrophages IRAK1 plays the larger role. Human IRAK2 shows only negligible autophosphorylation and can carry IL-1 signals as a scaffold; mouse studies report weak, atypical kinase activity whose physiological substrates are unknown. It also acts in TLR4 signalling with the adaptor TIRAP (Mal).

description: IRAK2 (interleukin-1 receptor-associated kinase-like 2) is a cytosolic member of the Pelle/IRAK
  family with an N-terminal death domain, a kinase-like domain in which Asn replaces the catalytic Asp,
  and a C-terminal region with TRAF6-binding motifs. After IL-1 receptor or Toll-like receptor engagement,
  MYD88 recruits IRAK4 and the MYD88-IRAK4 death-domain assembly recruits IRAK2 (or IRAK1) to form the
  top layer of the helical Myddosome. IRAK4 phosphorylates IRAK2, which then engages TRAF6 and promotes
  its K63-linked ubiquitination, leading to NF-kappaB and MAPK activation and inflammatory cytokine production.
  IRAK2 is partly redundant with IRAK1; in mouse macrophages it dominates sustained signalling because,
  unlike IRAK1, it is not degraded, whereas in human macrophages IRAK1 plays the larger role. Human IRAK2
  shows only negligible autophosphorylation and can carry IL-1 signals as a scaffold; mouse studies report
  weak, atypical kinase activity whose physiological substrates are unknown. It also acts in TLR4 signalling
  with the adaptor TIRAP (Mal).
