# ID3 notes

Automated Falcon and Perplexity-lite deep research were unavailable in this runtime, as recorded in `ID1-notes.md`;
these notes summarize the cached primary literature, UniProt record, Reactome summaries, and PANTHER PAINT data used
for the manual review.

## Evidence reviewed

- **Conserved ID-family mechanism.** ID3 is on `PTHR11723` node `PTN000919548` with the other human ID proteins for
  the conserved nuclear HLH transcription-regulator inhibitor mechanism. The node has no IRD or IKR loss on the
  human ID3 lineage.
- **Family mechanism.** The Id-family review by Ling et al. summarizes the mechanism: Id proteins lack the basic
  residues needed for DNA binding and inhibit E-protein homodimers and E-protein/tissue-specific bHLH heterodimers
  by HLH-mediated dimerization [PMID:25248477].
- **CLOCK/BMAL1 binding.** The ID2 clock paper also found direct ID3 interaction with CLOCK and BMAL1 in mammalian
  two-hybrid assays [PMID:20861012].
- **Reactome.** The Reactome `R-HSA-9613460` nucleoplasm row places ID1 and ID3 in a TGF-beta-stimulated
  endothelial-cell event; it supports a nuclear/nucleoplasmic location but is not enough on its own to make the
  angiogenesis context a separate core function.
