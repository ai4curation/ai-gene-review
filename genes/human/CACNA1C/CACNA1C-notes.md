# CACNA1C functional review

CACNA1C encodes the alpha1C pore of the L-type calcium channel CaV1.2. Human channel expression, pore mutagenesis and electrophysiology establish voltage-dependent calcium conductance. Cardiac calcium entry contributes to electrical excitation and triggers RyR2-mediated release from the sarcoplasmic reticulum. The same channel unit is modulated by auxiliary subunits, calmodulin, phosphorylation and alternative splicing. This synthesis separates the calcium-conducting pore from its regulators and from the downstream release pore. [PMID:8392192](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8392192/), [PMID:8099908](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8099908/), [PMID:27481713](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27481713/), [PMID:34072954](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34072954/).
