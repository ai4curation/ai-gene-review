CC   -!- FUNCTION: May have a pivotal role in cell differentiation of different
CC       cell types. Signaling could be triggered by the binding of a lectin-
CC       like ligand to the CD24 carbohydrates, and transduced by the release of
CC       second messengers derived from the GPI-anchor. Modulates B-cell
CC       activation responses. Promotes AG-dependent proliferation of B-cells,
CC       and prevents their terminal differentiation into antibody-forming cells
CC       (PubMed:11313396). In association with SIGLEC10 may be involved in the
CC       selective suppression of the immune response to danger-associated
CC       molecular patterns (DAMPs) such as HMGB1, HSP70 and HSP90. Plays a role
CC       in the control of autoimmunity (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P24807, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11313396}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with LYN and FGR in a cell-type specific manner.
CC       Binds FRG in a small cell cancer line, LYN in the erythroleukemia cell
CC       line K-562 and in Burkitt's lymphoma. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11313396,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8753773}.
