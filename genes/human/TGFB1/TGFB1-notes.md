# TGFB1 notes

Deep research providers unavailable (no API keys). Notes from UniProt P01137 and cached literature.

## Identity / localization
- Transforming growth factor beta-1. Synthesized as a proprotein cleaved in the Golgi into the
  Latency-associated peptide (LAP) and the mature TGF-beta-1 cytokine, which remain non-covalently associated
  as the small latent complex, stored in the extracellular matrix and at the cell surface via LTBP1,
  LRRC32/GARP and LRRC33/NRROS.
- [UniProt P01137 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: [Transforming growth factor beta-1]: Secreted"]; LAP is "Secreted, extracellular space, extracellular matrix".
- Activation: integrins (ITGAV:ITGB6/ITGB8) distort LAP and release active TGF-beta-1 [UniProt "results in distortion of the Latency-associated peptide chain and subsequent release of the active TGF-beta-1"].
- Localization conclusion: secreted, extracellular space/matrix and tethered at the cell surface in the latent
  complex; signals by binding cell-surface TGF-beta receptors. It is not an intrinsic membrane or cytosolic
  protein; nuclear/cytoplasmic and neuronal-compartment annotations are minor/indirect pools.

## Function
- [UniProt "Multifunctional protein that regulates the growth and differentiation of various cell types"].
- Secreted cytokine/growth factor; binds TGF-beta receptors (type I/II/III), activating SMAD signaling. Highly
  pleiotropic: fibrosis/ECM, immune regulation (Treg, IgA switching), EMT, proliferation control, development,
  wound healing. Most process annotations are legitimate downstream roles of this one signaling ligand and are
  marked non-core.
