Gene Description

ATP1A2 encodes the catalytic alpha2 subunit of the sodium/potassium ATPase. In association with beta subunits, it uses ATP to export sodium and import potassium across the plasma membrane. Its ion gradients support excitability, muscle calcium handling and glial neurotransmitter clearance. Loss of pump function is associated with familial hemiplegic migraine and related neurological phenotypes.
