Gene Description

Histone H1.4 is a replication-dependent nuclear linker histone encoded in the chromosome 6p22.2 histone cluster. It binds nucleosomal DNA and contributes to chromatin organization across compacted and transcriptionally active states. Post-translational modifications tune its chromatin association and regulatory contacts. Heterozygous C-terminal frameshift variants cause Rahman syndrome, a neurodevelopmental disorder in which overgrowth is variable.
