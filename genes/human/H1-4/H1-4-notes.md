## Biological evidence and review follow-up, 2026-11-07

H1.4 occupancy spans chromatin states. Human fetal H1s-4 is relatively depleted from active chromatin, while H1.4 K34 acetylation is enriched at active promoters. The historical H1s-4 identity is resolved by HGNC:4718. [PMID:10997781; PMID:22465951]

K26 methylation supports HP1 binding, whereas neighboring S27 phosphorylation prevents it. Chromatin tethering remains a proposed consequence. [PMID:16127177]

The imaging study reports "H1.0 and H1.4 were distributed throughout the nucleus." This broad pool does not exclude HPA nuclear-speck staining; phospho-antibody cross-reactivity limits separate speck confirmation. [PMID:38530350]

Rahman-associated frameshifts produce altered tails; their consequences should not be equated with ordinary H1.4 loss. The functional study expressed human constructs in rat hippocampal neurons, and overgrowth was not obligatory among affected individuals. [PMID:34788807]
