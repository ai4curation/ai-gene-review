# FERMT3 integrated evidence guide

The central function is integrin-tail binding coupled to integrin activation at adhesive contacts. The isolated F1-loop phospholipid interaction is experimentally supported and refines the lipid-binding annotation; it does not establish a full-length membrane orientation or a separate phosphoinositide-specific program. The [literature journal](FERMT3-notes.md) connects human patient experiments, mouse donor mechanisms, localization and remaining questions.

The [direct source guide](FERMT3-direct-source-guide.md) and [database source guide](FERMT3-database-source-guide.md) identify the original sections actually inspected. Their [direct](FERMT3-direct-decisions.yaml) and [database](FERMT3-database-decisions.yaml) decision tables preserve every source key and zero-based row index. The integrated review contains all 31 decisions: 10 ACCEPT, 17 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, one MODIFY and three UNDECIDED. No source is silently replaced by independent corroboration: the exosome row and both Reactome location rows retain their specific uncertainty. The broad membrane row separately records related positive primary evidence with an unresolved final published join.

All 17 complete reference objects are retained from the accepted assessments, including the four added donor/source papers. The [phylogeny evidence](FERMT3-phylogeny-evidence.yaml) distinguishes literal IBD joins and actual sequence matches from unverified tree topology. Target self-WITH is expected; donor count is not a confidence measure. The [natural-product map](FERMT3-database-product-map.yaml) and [experimental-construct table](FERMT3-product-context.yaml) preserve different kinds of sequence evidence without assigning every activity to both natural products.

The cached GO-CAM index had no FERMT3/Q86UX7 match at this inspection. That absence is not an annotation-gap claim. The imported Reactome rows describe cargo location, not participation in an exocytosis step. All 28 selected normal sources and both target machine files are preserved; provider failures are recorded in the original journal and no provider report is manufactured. Canonical validation and publication are separate steps.
