# FERMT3 evidence guide

Kindlin-3 binds integrin cytoplasmic tails and contributes to integrin activation at adhesive contacts. The [literature journal](FERMT3-notes.md) connects patient-cell experiments, ortholog mechanisms, localization and splice-product evidence. Its follow-up section addresses the recent isoform literature and the limits of the reported extracellular locations.

The [direct source guide](FERMT3-direct-source-guide.md) and [database source guide](FERMT3-database-source-guide.md) identify the inspected sections and figures. The [direct](FERMT3-direct-decisions.yaml) and [database](FERMT3-database-decisions.yaml) source indexes retain the source keys and zero-based annotation indices; the direct index also retains methods, locators and unresolved target-table joins. Decisions and their reasons are maintained in [FERMT3-ai-review.yaml](FERMT3-ai-review.yaml).

The [phylogeny evidence](FERMT3-phylogeny-evidence.yaml) separates matched PAINT assertions and sequence alignments from the unverified target-to-node tree path. The [natural-product map](FERMT3-database-product-map.yaml) records the two splice products, while the [construct table](FERMT3-product-context.yaml) distinguishes natural sequences from tagged proteins, isolated domains and synthetic peptides.

The exosome annotation and both Reactome location annotations retain source-specific uncertainty because their FERMT3 target results could not be inspected. The Reactome entries describe cargo locations; they do not establish that kindlin-3 performs an exocytosis step. The NK-cell membrane-proteome row also retains its unresolved published-table join, separately from the independent localization evidence.

## Feedback source locators

The canonical review is the decision authority. This follow-up supports the plasma-membrane refinement and two functional splice classes; it does not change the imported source tuples or the two machine-derived alternative-product objects.

- **PMID:42574321**: [Liverpool author manuscript](https://livrepository.liverpool.ac.uk/3200026/1/2026_Lee_Blood.pdf), [repository record](https://livrepository.liverpool.ac.uk/id/eprint/3200026), [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2026034274). Methods manuscript lines 136–171; Results 175–235 and Figures 1–2 establish full-length human cellular rescue/recruitment. Figure 3 and Results 236–246 concern isolated PH domains. Mouse HSPC complementation and human transcript associations are separate scopes. Figures 1–3 pixels and Figures 1–6 captions were inspected; expanded supplementary methods were not.
- **PMID:18280249**: [publisher original](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0006291X08002489), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18280249/). Complete abstract and indexed cloning Methods/initial Results describe URP2SF/663 residues. Full functional Results/figures remain unavailable. The abstract's apoptosis direction differs from the broad UniProt wording; the four-residue comparison was not established by the accessed material.

This is clinical/activation evidence; no categorical whole-paper absence claim is made from the partial read. The direct HTML download was a security challenge and is excluded as operative evidence.
