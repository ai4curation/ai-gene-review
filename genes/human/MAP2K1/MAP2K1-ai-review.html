Gene Description

MAP2K1 encodes MEK1, a dual-specificity MAP kinase kinase in the RAF-MEK-ERK cascade. Activated RAF-family kinases phosphorylate MEK1, which in turn phosphorylates the threonine and tyrosine activation-loop residues of ERK1/2 MAP kinases from cytosolic and scaffolded signaling complexes. MEK1 couples receptor and RAS/RAF inputs to ERK-dependent control of proliferation, differentiation, survival, cytoskeletal remodeling, and endomembrane dynamics.
