# LONRF2 review notes

## Evidence blockers and boundaries

- The decisive direct functional evidence is PMID:37474791. It establishes LONRF2
  as a protein-quality-control ubiquitin ligase and identifies misfolded TDP-43,
  hnRNP M1 and artificial misfolded proteins as substrates. The local cache is
  abstract-only, so claims about chain linkage, individual domain mutants, catalytic
  residues or precise degradation machinery should await full-text verification
  [PMID:37474791, "Here, we show that LONRF2 is a bona fide protein quality control
  ubiquitin ligase induced in post-mitotic senescent cells."]
- PMID:42442502 provides independent, direct substrate evidence in a different
  biological context: LONRF2 interacts with ALDOA and induces its ubiquitylation
  and degradation in endometrial cancer cells. The glycolysis and tumor phenotypes
  are context-specific rather than the universal core function [PMID:42442502,
  "LONRF2 interacted with ALDOA and induced its ubiquitination and degradation."]
- PMID:32814053 is a systematic yeast two-hybrid interaction screen. Its many
  LONRF2 partners are candidate binary interactors, not established LONRF2 substrates
  or a physiological complex [PMID:32814053, "Here, we report on an interactome
  map that focuses on neurodegenerative disease (ND), connects ∼5,000 human proteins
  via ∼30,000 candidate interactions and is generated by systematic yeast two-hybrid
  interaction screening of ∼500 ND-related proteins and integration of literature
  interactions."]
- LONRF2 has a Lon N-terminal substrate-binding domain but lacks the AAA+ ATPase
  and protease catalytic domains of Lon proteases. The historical alias
  "neuroblastoma apoptosis-related protease" must not be used to infer peptidase
  activity. A reproducible domain analysis confirms the absence of the targeted
  Lon catalytic architecture while finding that the N-terminal RING-like call is
  substantially less well supported than the conserved C-terminal RING
  [file:human/LONRF2/LONRF2-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md, "More decisively, the targeted
  presence matrix finds no AAA+ ATPase domain/core (IPR003593/IPR003959), Lon protease
  family call (IPR004815), peptidase S16 active site (IPR008268), or Lon proteolytic
  domain (IPR008269) in LONRF2."]. The cached PANTHER prose is explicitly unchecked
  LLM-generated text and is not evidence.
- Direct LONRF2 evidence must not be conflated with LONRF1. PMID:36888978 studies
  Lonrf1 expression and treats LONRF2's neuronal protein-quality-control activity
  as a contrast; it does not demonstrate that LONRF1 shares LONRF2 substrates or
  physiological roles.

## Direct functional model

- LONRF2 binds and ubiquitylates abnormally structured TDP-43 and hnRNP M1 and
  artificial misfolded proteins [PMID:37474791, "LONRF2 binds and ubiquitylates
  abnormally structured TDP-43 and hnRNP M1 and artificially misfolded proteins."]
  The paper's hnRNP M1 name is mapped gene-centrically to human HNRNPM/P52272; the
  cached abstract does not identify a tested UniProt isoform.
- Under unperturbed conditions LONRF2 is predominantly neuronal; loss in mice
  causes age-dependent motor-neuron degeneration and cerebellar ataxia. Mouse
  iPSC-derived motor neurons lacking LONRF2 have reduced survival, shortened neurites
  and accumulation of pTDP-43 and G3BP1 [PMID:37474791, "Mouse induced pluripotent
  stem cell-derived MNs lacking LONRF2 showed reduced survival, shortening of neurites
  and accumulation of pTDP-43 and G3BP1 after long-term culture."]
- The 2026 ALDOA study broadens the substrate spectrum beyond damaged RNA-binding
  proteins and supports a general E3-ligase mechanism, while its effects on glycolysis,
  migration, invasion and tumor growth remain endometrial-cancer-specific.
  [PMID:42442502, "LONRF2 overexpression inhibited the growth, glycolysis, migration,
  and invasion of KLE cells."]

## Isoforms and architecture

- UniProtKB Q1L5Z9 annotates two isoforms. Isoform 1 is the 754-aa canonical protein.
  Isoform 2 lacks residues 1-243 (`VSP_023079`). This removes TPR1 (23-58), TPR2
  (59-91), TPR3 (197-230), and the N-terminal portion of TPR4 (231-264), but retains
  the RING finger (449-487) and Lon N-terminal domain (528-737). No isoform-specific
  GO annotation or functional comparison was found. [file:human/LONRF2/LONRF2-uniprot.txt,
  alternative-products and feature-table records]
- The N-terminal deletion means assays using an unspecified LONRF2 construct should
  not automatically be generalized to both isoforms. Neither direct paper's cached
  abstract identifies the tested isoform.

## Curation implications

- Ubiquitin protein ligase activity, misfolded-protein recognition and protein
  quality control are directly supported and form the mechanistic core.
- Neurite maintenance and neuronal survival are important physiological consequences,
  but `neuron apoptotic process` is a poor literal rendering of the evidence: LONRF2
  loss reduces survival rather than demonstrating that LONRF2 executes neuronal
  apoptosis.
- `Ribonucleoprotein complex binding` is compatible with recognition of abnormal
  TDP-43 and hnRNP M1 assemblies, but the direct paper specifically supports
  misfolded-protein recognition rather than generic binding to normal RNP complexes.
- ALDOA ubiquitylation/degradation is suitable as a proposed new substrate-specific
  annotation or supporting example, while negative regulation of glycolysis should
  remain non-core and context-qualified to endometrial cancer.
