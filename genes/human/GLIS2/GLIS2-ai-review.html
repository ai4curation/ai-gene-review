Gene Description

GLIS2 is a GLIS-family protein with five C2H2 zinc fingers. It binds regulatory DNA and acts predominantly in the nucleus. Promoter context permits activation or repression. Corepressor and beta-catenin interactions contribute to its regulatory activity. GLIS2 supports renal epithelial homeostasis, and biallelic disruption is associated with nephronophthisis.
