Gene Description

NKX2-5 (CSX) is an NK-2 class homeodomain transcription factor, the vertebrate ortholog of Drosophila tinman, expressed in cardiac progenitors of the first and second heart fields and in embryonic and adult myocardium, as well as in spleen mesenchyme and pharyngeal/thyroid primordia. Its homeodomain binds NKE (TNAAGTG) elements in promoters and enhancers of cardiac genes such as NPPA/ANF, GATA4, MYL2 and miR-143/145; a C-terminal autorepressive domain makes it a modest activator on its own, and it activates or represses targets in combination with partner transcription factors, notably GATA4 and TBX5 (plus SRF/myocardin and coactivators such as FBLIM1/Cal and CAMTA2). Activity is modulated by casein kinase II phosphorylation and SUMOylation. In the mouse it is required for heart looping, ventricular myocyte specialization, second heart field/outflow tract development and formation of the atrioventricular node and conduction system in a dosage-dependent manner. Heterozygous loss-of-function variants in humans cause secundum atrial septal defect with progressive atrioventricular conduction block, and also ventricular septal defect, tetralogy of Fallot and other congenital heart defects; variants are also associated with congenital asplenia and thyroid dysgenesis.
