# Affinage mechanistic annotation for ARL9 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

ARL9 is a small GTPase that regulates cell proliferation and motility, with loss-of-function evidence linking it to cancer cell behavior and neural progenitor dynamics [PMID:39776316, PMID:25818835]. siRNA knockdown in AGS gastric adenocarcinoma cells reduces proliferation, migration, and invasion, establishing ARL9 as a promoter of gastric cancer cell growth and metastatic behavior [PMID:39776316]. In vivo morpholino knockdown in the Xenopus optic tectum alters neural progenitor cell proliferation or neuronal differentiation, marking ARL9 as a candidate neurogenic regulator [PMID:25818835]. Beyond these loss-of-function phenotypes, the biochemical mechanism of ARL9 is undefined: its effectors, substrates, upstream regulators, and the consequences of its non-canonical switch residue have not been characterized in the available corpus.
