# PADI2 notes

## 2026-11-28 manual review

The automated Falcon/Perplexity deep-research step was unavailable, so the PADI2 review was curated directly from the local UniProt record, the seeded GOA references, PANTHER metadata, and the cached papers.

PADI2 is a calcium-dependent protein-arginine deiminase. The original human cloning paper produced active recombinant human PAD type II [PMID:12392711, "A recombinant PAD protein subsequently produced in Escherichia coli proved to be enzymatically active"], Nakayama-Hamada et al. compared the Ca2+-dependent activities of human PADI2 and PADI4 [PMID:15629448, "we compared the properties of two PADIs, human PADI2 and human PADI4"], and Slade et al. trapped PAD2 in apo, intermediate calcium-bound, and holoenzyme conformations [PMID:25621824, "PAD2 binds calcium in a stepwise fashion"].

The PADI2-specific histone branch is supported by estrogen-stimulated recruitment to ER target promoters: Zhang et al. found H3R26 citrullination on chromatin and concluded that recruited PAD2 citrullinates H3R26, causing local chromatin decondensation and target-gene activation [PMID:22853951, "PAD2 then citrullinates H3R26, which leads to local chromatin decondensation and transcriptional activation"]. PADI2 also citrullinates the inflammatory chemokines CXCL10 and CXCL11, reducing CXCR3 signaling and T-cell chemotaxis; these immunoregulatory rows are direct but peripheral to the conserved catalytic activity [PMID:18645041, "rabbit PAD and human PAD2"].

The Reactome azurophil-granule row points at an event summary that describes MPO-rich neutrophil granules but does not name PADI2, so those compartment annotations were left unresolved rather than asserted as wrong from an incomplete cached event. The HEP substantia-nigra and urinary-exosome HDA rows likewise do not establish a molecular function of PADI2.
