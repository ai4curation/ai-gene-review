# COG7 review notes

## Performed molecular role

COG7 supplies a COG5-contacting structural interface in lobe B of the COG complex. Original reconstruction uses human cDNAs in rabbit reticulocyte lysate: COG5 and COG7 together promote COG6 incorporation, with the lobe-B subunits supporting optimal COG8 incorporation. Possible endogenous factors limit claims of purified binary directness. The original COG5-pair assertion is refined to GO:0140378 protein complex scaffold activity; other COG associations remain separately represented. There is one integrated core and no NEW annotation. [PMID:15047703]

The later structure uses K. lactis fragments. Separate human COG7 interface tests show impaired COG5 association for N-terminal deletions and the I17/F21/V41-to-Asp mutant. That mutant reduces recovered lobe-B assembly about twofold; it does not cause a claimed complete collapse. Glycosylation-rescue experiments test COG5 mutants, not a COG7 rescue construct. These distinctions support a COG7-specific assembly interpretation without importing COG4's independently demonstrated bridging mechanism. The official scaffold definition and its three specialized descendants were read; ubiquitin-ligase, MAPK and centriole scaffold terms do not fit this activity. [PMID:25331899]

COG7–Sly1 association in the original source is positive but weak. The Methods identify recombinant rat Sly1, matching the Q62991 source partner. Human-host identity does not convert that construct into human SCFD1. COG4-specific purified binding and syntaxin coordination are not assigned to COG7. The missing exact pair in the queried interaction export is not negative evidence against the original experiment. [PMID:19536132]
