# PIM3 curation notes

Deep research providers were unavailable in this worktree: Falcon and Perplexity failed before creating provider files, so this review uses the fetched GOA, UniProt, PAINT, PMID caches and manual inspection.

PIM3 is reviewed as a PIM-family serine/threonine kinase with a broad retained cytoplasm location. The direct human colon-cancer paper supports BAD Ser112 phosphorylation and apoptosis resistance, and the shared PIM-family p27 paper supports the mitotic-cell-cycle IBA. The BAD protein-binding IPI row remains undecided because the cached abstract shows co-localization and phosphorylation rather than an explicit IPI-grade physical-interaction assay.
