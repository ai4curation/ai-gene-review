# PIM3 curation notes

Deep research providers were unavailable in this worktree: Falcon and Perplexity failed before creating provider files, so this review uses the fetched GOA, UniProt, PAINT, PMID caches and manual inspection.

PIM3 is reviewed as a PIM-family serine/threonine kinase with a cytosolic Human Protein Atlas location and broader cytoplasm rows treated as too general. The direct human colon-cancer paper supports BAD Ser112 phosphorylation and apoptosis resistance, and the shared PIM-family p27 paper supports the mitotic-cell-cycle IBA. The BAD protein-binding IPI row was converted to the protein serine/threonine kinase activity because the cited paper supports BAD phosphorylation rather than generic binding as the functional PIM3 assertion.
