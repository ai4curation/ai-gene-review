CC   -!- FUNCTION: Glycosylphosphatidylinositol (GPI)-anchored cell surface
CC       glycoprotein that interacts via its N-terminal immunoglobulin domain
CC       with cell surface receptors including CD244/2B4 or CD2 to regulate
CC       immune cell function and activation (PubMed:12007789, PubMed:19494291,
CC       PubMed:27249817, PubMed:9841922). Participates in T-cell signaling
CC       transduction by associating with CD2 and efficiently bringing the Src
CC       family protein kinase LCK and LAT to the TCR/CD3 complex
CC       (PubMed:19494291). In turn, promotes LCK phosphorylation and subsequent
CC       activation (PubMed:12007789). Induces the phosphorylation of the
CC       cytoplasmic immunoreceptortyrosine switch motifs (ITSMs) of CD244
CC       initiating a series of signaling events that leads to the generation of
CC       the immunological synapse and the directed release of cytolytic
CC       granules containing perforin and granzymes by T-lymphocytes and NK-
CC       cells (PubMed:27249817). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12007789,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19494291, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27249817,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9841922}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with CD2 (PubMed:19494291). Interacts with CD244;
CC       this interaction is possible not only on different cells (trans
CC       interaction) but also on the same cell (cis interaction)
CC       (PubMed:27249817, PubMed:9841922). Interacts with LCK
CC       (PubMed:12007789). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12007789,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19494291, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27249817,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9841922}.
