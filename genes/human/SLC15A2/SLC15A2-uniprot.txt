CC   -!- FUNCTION: Proton-coupled amino-acid transporter that transports
CC       oligopeptides of 2 to 4 amino acids with a preference for dipeptides
CC       (PubMed:16434549, PubMed:18367661, PubMed:7756356). Transports neutral
CC       and anionic dipeptides with a proton to peptide stoichiometry of 2:1 or
CC       3:1 (By similarity). In kidney, involved in the absorption of
CC       circulating di- and tripeptides from the glomerular filtrate
CC       (PubMed:7756356). Can also transport beta-lactam antibiotics, such as
CC       the aminocephalosporin cefadroxil, and other antiviral and anticancer
CC       drugs (PubMed:16434549). Transports the dipeptide-like aminopeptidase
CC       inhibitor bestatin (By similarity). Also able to transport carnosine
CC       (PubMed:31073693). Involved in innate immunity by promoting the
CC       detection of microbial pathogens by NOD-like receptors (NLRs) (By
CC       similarity). Mediates transport of bacterial peptidoglycans across the
CC       plasma membrane or, in macrophages, the phagosome membrane: catalyzes
CC       the transport of certain bacterial peptidoglycans, such as muramyl
CC       dipeptide (MDP), the NOD2 ligand (PubMed:20406817).
