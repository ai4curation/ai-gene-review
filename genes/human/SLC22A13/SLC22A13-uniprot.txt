CC   -!- FUNCTION: Anion antiporter that mediates the transport of urate,
CC       orotate and nicotinate in exchange for organic or inorganic anions
CC       (PubMed:18411268, PubMed:31780526, PubMed:35144162, PubMed:35462902).
CC       Translocates urate and orotate across the apical membrane of proximal
CC       tubule epithelial cells and involved in urate renal reabsorption
CC       (PubMed:18411268, PubMed:31780526, PubMed:35144162). Possibly involved
CC       in orotate renal reabsorption and nicotinate intestinal reabsorption
CC       (PubMed:18411268, PubMed:35144162). Mediates urate uptake by an
CC       exchange with organic anions such as (S)-lactate, succinate,
CC       glutathione and nicotinate (PubMed:18411268). Urate and orotate
CC       transports are Cl(-)-dependent (PubMed:35144162, PubMed:35462902).
CC       Shows similar transport characteristics as the urate/orotate renal
CC       antiporter SLC22A12/URAT1 and may act as a compensator of
CC       SLC22A12/URAT1 in certain conditions (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18411268, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31780526,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35144162, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35462902,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:35144162, ECO:0000305|PubMed:35462902}.
