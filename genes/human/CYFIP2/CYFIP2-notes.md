# CYFIP2 evidence notes

CYFIP2 is reviewed as a Rac-responsive WAVE regulatory-complex component. The 44 seeded assertions and all imported source, qualifier and isoform fields are preserved; no NEW annotation is proposed. The two refinements concern Rac1 binding and the actual fibronectin-coated-plate adhesion assay. The latter measures cell-matrix adhesion, not cell-cell adhesion (PMID:15048733, Results 2.2–2.3 and Methods 4.7).

## Direct activity and complex context

Human CD4-cell Rac1/CYFIP2 co-immunoprecipitation supports small GTPase binding, without purified binary-affinity proof (PMID:15048733, Figure 2 and Methods 4.4). The literal human ABI3/CYFIP2 MINT record IM-10986-8 points to anti-bait co-IP in HUVEC and Figure 4D of PMID:17101133. The abstract and curated record support retention despite unavailable full text. PMID:26428302 additionally shows HUVEC ABI/WAVE complexes, but its PIR121/Sra1 antibody is not CYFIP2-exclusive. Neither limitation proves a wrong-gene annotation.

The integrated core combines small GTPase binding, SCAR/WAVE membership and regulation of actin polymerization. PMID:21107423 directly studies Sra1/CYFIP1-containing purified WRC; it supports family-level activation without complex disruption, not CYFIP2-specific measured kinetics. WAVE/ARP2/3 performs downstream nucleation. No autonomous actin nucleation, GTP hydrolysis, or unsupported adapter activity is assigned to CYFIP2.

## FMRP association and products

PMID:11438699 tests an in-vitro-translated human CYFIP2 fragment against GST-FMRP and describes cellular localization. Its deposited AF160973.1/AAD45723.1 translation exactly matches current Q96F07-2. PMID:24658146 detects endogenous human CYFIP2 with FMRP, but RNase strongly disrupts the association; its direct purified interaction is nucleolin–FMRP. These findings support association, not an inferred CYFIP2 RNA-binding or translational-repressor activity. The specific splicing-factor-binding term is not a generic RNA-binding-protein partner class and is not used here.

Both official products were reconstructed and matched independently downloaded FASTAs: Q96F07-1 is 1278 residues; Q96F07-2 is 1253 residues after deletion of displayed residues 558–582. The 2017 paper expresses human CYFIP2 in rat E18 hippocampal neurons, not solely a rat gene product. Its stated length and S582/S968/T1067 numbering match product 2, corresponding to S607/S993/T1092 in the displayed product; this is strong numbering correspondence rather than complete historical clone sequencing (PMID:28692454, constructs/neuronal Methods and mutant Results). Equal activity of the two products has not been demonstrated. Imported source isoform omissions remain unchanged.

## Neuronal and context-specific outcomes

The mouse retrolinkin study distinguishes single CYFIP2 depletion, which affects dendritic branching, from combined CYFIP1/2 depletion, which impairs the reported TrkB internalization endpoint. Signaling experiments also perturb retrolinkin or WAVE1. These are conserved contextual roles, not human IDA or intrinsic receptor-kinase activity (PMID:27605705, Results and construct/pull-down/neuronal/uptake Methods). The 2017 spine/neurite phenotypes likewise do not prove a universal complex-disassembly mechanism.

For apoptosis, the actual PAINT IBD lies on the CYFIP2 path and its human self-donor is legitimate. The 2007 original directly shows several apoptosis readouts after human CYFIP2 expression in DLD1 cells, while depletion did not abolish p53-mediated apoptosis. Its Discussion reports a negative earlier ectopic experiment in PMID:10449408. Because the 1999 main text remains unavailable, the older IDA assertion is UNDECIDED rather than rejected; the later positive contextual phenotype supports non-core retention of the IBA. The 2007 report of the old experiment is not represented as an independent reread of that original. PMID:18387192 is a proteolytic reporter-screen hit, not evidence of CYFIP2 protease catalysis.

## Locations and propagation

The broad cytoplasm annotations are retained because observed soluble, particulate and perinuclear pools are not all equivalent to cytosol. HPA XML supports cytosol and additional cell-dependent compartments; no HPA images were inspected. The six Reactome source roots have 16 captured target paths through CYFIP2 [cytosol] and Q96F07. The bounded capture has 181 objects and leaves 74 non-target frontier IDs unexpanded; it is not a full download of every neighboring pathway.

The original urinary-exosome database contains CYFIP2/NP_055191 with one peptide, reference 2 (PMID:19056867; zero-based HTML row 331, one-based row 332). This supports contextual fraction detection without requiring extracellular catalytic activity. The NK membrane, B-cell exosome and EPS-urine target supplements were not recovered; their positive source assertions are retained with explicit curator deference and independent compartment corroboration, not falsely described as table-verified (PMIDs:19946888, 20458337, 23533145).

The actual PTHR12195 tree contains 226 nodes and the exact displayed CYFIP2 sequence. All five positive source IBDs map to the target path. Two current negative records at PTN002638922 concern SCAR-complex membership (IKR, 2021-05-29) and translation regulation (IRD, 2024-11-20). The SCAR negative record is not silently erased or treated as overriding independent current human complex evidence; its reconciliation is a specific curator question. Current rat-donor additions are recorded separately from preserved original WITH/FROM lists. Complete donor pages, target InterPro matches and literal interaction sequence hashes were checked; donor count and self-evidence were not used to reject propagation.
