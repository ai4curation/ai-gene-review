# Reproduced EGFR evidence

The saved-input replay resolves 603 IPI source assertions against a 1,942-record IntAct snapshot and selects 726 literal records. These are evidence joins, not 726 independently validated native interactions. Assay method, negative flag, species, construct features and pair identity remain necessary for interpreting each record.

The supplementary peptide table contains one P00533 row. It reports the human EGFR 992–1007 peptide, sequence PTDSNFYRALMDEEDM, binding isolated AP2M1 MHD with a reported dissociation constant of 2.4 ± 0.1 μM. This is an isolated-domain/cargo-peptide measurement; intact AP-2-complex binding is an explicitly stated inference from that cargo interface, not an additional measured result. [PMID:37100772](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37100772/).

The current GOA has 969 raw rows and 958 distinct full contexts. All are represented exactly among the non-retired imported objects. Forty-eight historical objects remain archived in place with their biological decisions unchanged, and the two inherited NEW assertions remain distinct. The source replay checks metadata coverage and lifecycle, not the biological validity of a term.

The independent source-chain records cover 190 Reactome annotations through 181 event roots. The selected paths distinguish EGFR catalytic participation, receptor cargo, complexes, candidate membership and mutant/chimeric contexts. PAINT placement independently joins the 16 historical/current IBA objects to ten positive IBD assertions at three target ancestors. These database proofs are interpreted alongside the primary-source evidence in the review; they do not substitute for it.
