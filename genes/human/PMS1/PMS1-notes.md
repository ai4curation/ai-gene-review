# PMS1 PTHR10073 review notes

PMS1 was reviewed as the human MLH1-PMS1 MutLbeta partner. The accepted human claim is complex formation, not catalytic mismatch repair: PMID:10748105 detected MutLbeta in human epithelial cells, and PMID:11292842 showed that PMS1 competes with PMS2 and MLH3 for MLH1.

The GO Central contributes_to DNA endonuclease row from PMID:33510387 was checked against the full text. That paper defines mammalian MutLalpha as MLH1.PMS2 and mentions MLH1-PMS1 only as the yeast MutLalpha complex, so the human PMS1 row is recorded as a confirmed GO Central DB_Object_ID defect that should point to human PMS2. The PAINT endonuclease IBD at PTN000008284 consequently needs an IRD on the deuterostome PMS1 branch, PTN000008324, while the broader MLH1-PMS1 MutLbeta complex rows remain valid.
