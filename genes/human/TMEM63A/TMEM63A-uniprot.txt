CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mechanosensitive cation channel with low conductance and high
CC       activation threshold (PubMed:30382938, PubMed:31587869,
CC       PubMed:37543036). In contrast to TMEM63B, does not show phospholipid
CC       scramblase activity (PubMed:39716028). Acts as a regulator of lysosomal
CC       morphology by mediating lysosomal mechanosensitivity (By similarity).
CC       Important for the baby's first breath and respiration throughout life
CC       (PubMed:38127458). Upon lung inflation conducts cation currents in
CC       alveolar type 1 and 2 cells triggering lamellar body exocytosis and
CC       surfactant secretion into airspace (PubMed:38127458). Also acts as an
CC       osmosensitive cation channel preferentially activated by hypotonic
CC       stress (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q91YT8,
