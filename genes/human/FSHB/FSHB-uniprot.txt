CC   -!- FUNCTION: Hormone-specific beta-subunit of the heterodimeric
CC       glycoprotein hormone FSH; associates with the common alpha-subunit CGA
CC       to form the mature hormone, which binds and activates the follicle-
CC       stimulating hormone receptor FSHR, a G protein-coupled receptor on
CC       ovarian granulosa cells and testicular Sertoli cells, stimulating cAMP
CC       signaling (PubMed:2494176, PubMed:24692546). Follitropin is involved in
CC       follicle development and spermatogenesis in reproductive organs
CC       (PubMed:407105, PubMed:8220432). The beta-subunit determines receptor-
CC       binding specificity (By similarity). Required for ovarian follicle
CC       maturation beyond the preantral/antral stage, granulosa cell
CC       proliferation, follicular estradiol production via aromatase induction,
CC       and ovarian androgen and estrogen synthesis; essential for female
CC       fertility (By similarity). In osteoclasts, activates ERK and AKT via
CC       GNAI2 to regulate bone remodeling (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q60687, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24692546,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2494176, ECO:0000269|PubMed:407105,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8220432}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterodimer. The active follitropin is a heterodimer composed
CC       of an alpha chain/CGA shared with other hormones and a unique beta
CC       chain/FSHB shown here. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15662415,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24692546, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2494176}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       P01225; P01215: CGA; NbExp=4; IntAct=EBI-1030645, EBI-718913;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2494176}.
CC       Note=Efficient secretion requires dimerization with CGA.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2494176}.
CC   -!- DISEASE: Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism 24 with or without anosmia
CC       (HH24) [MIM:229070]: A form of hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, a group
CC       of disorders characterized by absent or incomplete sexual maturation by
CC       the age of 18 years, in conjunction with low levels of circulating
CC       gonadotropins and testosterone and no other abnormalities of the
CC       hypothalamic-pituitary axis. HH24 is characterized by primary
CC       amenorrhea in women, oligo or azoospermia with low to normal
CC       testosterone levels in men, and infertility.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8220432, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9271483,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9280841}. Note=The disease is caused by variants
CC       affecting the gene represented in this entry.
CC   -!- PHARMACEUTICAL: Available under the names Gonal-F or Metrodin HP
CC       (Serono) and Puregon (Organon). Used in the treatment of infertility in
CC       women with proven hypopituitarism or who have not responded to
CC       clomifene; or in superovulation treatment for assisted conception (such
CC       as in vitro fertilization). Metrodin HP is also used in the treatment
CC       of hypogonadotrophic hypogonadism in men for the stimulation of
CC       spermatogenesis.
