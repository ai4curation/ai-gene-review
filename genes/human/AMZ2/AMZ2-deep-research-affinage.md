# Affinage mechanistic annotation for AMZ2 (human)

## Current model (mechanistic narrative)

AMZ2 (archaemetzincin-2) is a functional zinc metalloprotease of the metzincin class that cleaves synthetic substrates and bioactive peptides in vitro, an activity abolished by metalloprotease inhibitors and dependent on a canonical HEXXH zinc-binding motif accompanied by the metzincin-characteristic methionine and four conserved cysteines distinguishing it from other human metalloproteases [PMID:15972818]. It is predominantly expressed in testis and heart [PMID:15972818]. A homozygous missense variant (p.Thr174Ala) that abolishes AMZ2 protein in spermatozoa is associated with severely vacuolated sperm heads, implicating AMZ2 as a candidate causative gene for vacuolated sperm head formation [PMID:37459044]. Beyond its catalytic characterization in vitro [PMID:15972818] and this sperm phenotype [PMID:37459044], the physiological substrates and the molecular pathway linking AMZ2 proteolysis to sperm head morphogenesis have not been characterized in the available corpus.
