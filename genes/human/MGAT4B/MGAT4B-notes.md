# MGAT4B notes

MGAT4B/GnT-IVb is the second human GnT-IV isozyme. The 1998 cloning paper identified a
second mammalian GnT-IV cDNA and confirmed that its expression produced GnT-IV activity
(PMID:10372966). Oguri et al. 2006 compared recombinant MGAT4A and MGAT4B and found similar
acceptor specificity but lower donor and acceptor affinity for MGAT4B, supporting the same
core EC 2.4.1.145 reaction while making MGAT4A the stronger physiological contributor
(PMID:17006639).

The supported core is the type II Golgi-membrane transfer of GlcNAc from UDP-GlcNAc to the
GlcNAc-beta1,2-Man-alpha1,3 arm of complex and hybrid N-glycans. Broader
glycosyltransferase and acetylglucosaminyltransferase rows are true but less informative
once GO:0008454 is present.

MGAT4B also has three bare protein-binding rows. The SLC35A3 interaction is biologically
interesting because Song et al. place the UDP-GlcNAc transporter upstream of GnT-IV-dependent
N-glycan branching (PMID:34981577), but the row itself still says only GO:0005515 protein
binding. The other two rows come from high-throughput interactome screens. All three are
removed under the bare-protein-binding policy.

The PAINT ER and ERGIC calls are seeded from the MGAT4D/GnT1IP clade and should not be
projected as active locations of the catalytic MGAT4B isozyme.
