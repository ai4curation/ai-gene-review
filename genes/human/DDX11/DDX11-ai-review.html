Gene Description

DDX11 is an ATP-dependent DNA helicase associated with nuclear chromatin. Purified human protein unwinds DNA in the 5-prime to 3-prime direction, using accessible single-stranded loading regions and showing substrate-dependent activity on forked duplexes, D-loops and selected G-quadruplexes. DDX11 supports replication under stress and sister-chromatid cohesion, with both catalytic and protein-association contributions under investigation. A nucleolar pool participates in ribosomal chromatin regulation. Pathogenic variants cause Warsaw breakage syndrome.
