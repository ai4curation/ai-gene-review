Gene Description

SLC18A3 encodes the vesicular acetylcholine transporter (VAChT), a proton-coupled antiporter that accumulates acetylcholine inside cholinergic synaptic vesicles using the gradient generated by vesicular V-ATPase. Its cytoplasmic sorting signals govern retrieval and delivery to secretory vesicles. Vesicular filling determines the acetylcholine available for quantal release and supports neuromuscular and central cholinergic transmission. Purified human VAChT also transports serotonin in reconstituted liposomes, but a native serotonergic role has not been established by that assay.
