CC   -!- FUNCTION: ATP-dependent transporter of the ATP-binding cassette (ABC)
CC       family that actively extrudes physiological compounds and xenobiotics
CC       from cells (PubMed:11856762, PubMed:12523936, PubMed:15364914,
CC       PubMed:38886124, PubMed:40138312). Transports a range of endogenous
CC       molecules that have a key role in cellular communication and signaling,
CC       including cyclic nucleotides such as cyclic AMP (cAMP) and cyclic GMP
CC       (cGMP), bile acids, steroid conjugates, urate, and prostaglandins
CC       (PubMed:11856762, PubMed:12523936, PubMed:12835412, PubMed:12883481,
CC       PubMed:15364914, PubMed:15454390, PubMed:16282361, PubMed:17959747,
CC       PubMed:18300232, PubMed:26721430, PubMed:38886124, PubMed:40138312).
CC       Mediates the ATP-dependent efflux of glutathione conjugates such as
CC       leukotriene C4 (LTC4) and leukotriene B4 (LTB4) too. The presence of
CC       GSH is necessary for the ATP-dependent transport of LTB4, whereas GSH
CC       is not required for the transport of LTC4 (PubMed:17959747). Mediates
CC       the cotransport of bile acids with reduced glutathione (GSH)
