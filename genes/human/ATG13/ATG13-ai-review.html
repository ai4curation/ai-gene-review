Gene Description

ATG13 is a non-catalytic regulatory subunit of the ULK1/ULK2 (Atg1) protein kinase complex, which also contains FIP200 (RB1CC1) and ATG101 and is the most upstream initiator of macroautophagy in mammals. ATG13 has an N-terminal HORMA domain that heterodimerizes with ATG101, and a long intrinsically disordered C-terminal region that binds FIP200 and, through a C-terminal MIT-interacting motif, the ULK1/2 MIT domain; it also carries an LC3-interacting region that binds Atg8-family proteins. ATG13 bridges ULK1/2 to FIP200, stabilizes ULK and stimulates its kinase activity. The complex forms constitutively in the cytosol and is recruited to ER-associated phagophore assembly sites, where it coassembles with the class III PI3K complex I. mTORC1 phosphorylates ATG13 and ULK1 to keep the complex inactive under nutrient-rich conditions. ATG13 is required for autophagosome formation, ATG9 trafficking and mitophagy, during which it is recruited to damaged mitochondria.

description: ATG13 is a non-catalytic regulatory subunit of the ULK1/ULK2 (Atg1) protein kinase complex,
  which also contains FIP200 (RB1CC1) and ATG101 and is the most upstream initiator of macroautophagy
  in mammals. ATG13 has an N-terminal HORMA domain that heterodimerizes with ATG101, and a long intrinsically
  disordered C-terminal region that binds FIP200 and, through a C-terminal MIT-interacting motif, the
  ULK1/2 MIT domain; it also carries an LC3-interacting region that binds Atg8-family proteins. ATG13
  bridges ULK1/2 to FIP200, stabilizes ULK and stimulates its kinase activity. The complex forms constitutively
  in the cytosol and is recruited to ER-associated phagophore assembly sites, where it coassembles with
  the class III PI3K complex I. mTORC1 phosphorylates ATG13 and ULK1 to keep the complex inactive under
  nutrient-rich conditions. ATG13 is required for autophagosome formation, ATG9 trafficking and mitophagy,
  during which it is recruited to damaged mitochondria.
