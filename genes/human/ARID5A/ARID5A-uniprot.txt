CC   -!- FUNCTION: DNA-binding protein that may regulate transcription and act
CC       as a repressor by binding to AT-rich stretches in the promoter region
CC       of target genes (PubMed:8649988). May positively regulate chondrocyte-
CC       specific transcription such as of COL2A1 in collaboration with SOX9 and
CC       positively regulate histone H3 acetylation at chondrocyte-specific
CC       genes. May stimulate early-stage chondrocyte differentiation and
CC       inhibit later stage differention (By similarity). Can repress ESR1-
CC       mediated transcriptional activation; proposed to act as corepressor for
CC       selective nuclear hormone receptors (PubMed:15941852). As an RNA-
CC       binding protein, involved in the regulation of inflammatory response by
CC       stabilizing selective inflammation-related mRNAs, such as STAT3 and
CC       TBX21 (By similarity). Also stabilizes IL6 mRNA (PubMed:32209697).
CC       Binds to stem loop structures located in the 3'UTRs of IL6, STAT3 and
CC       TBX21 mRNAs; at least for STAT3 prevents binding of ZC3H12A to the mRNA
CC       stem loop structure thus inhibiting its degradation activity.
CC       Contributes to elevated IL6 levels possibly implicated in autoimmunity
CC       processes. IL6-dependent stabilization of STAT3 mRNA may promote
CC       differentiation of naive CD4+ T-cells into T-helper Th17 cells. In CD4+
CC       T-cells may also inhibit RORC-induced Th17 cell differentiation
CC       independently of IL6 signaling. Stabilization of TBX21 mRNA contributes
CC       to elevated interferon-gamma secretion in Th1 cells possibly implicated
CC       in the establishment of septic shock (By similarity). Stabilizes
CC       TNFRSF4/OX40 mRNA by binding to the conserved stem loop structure in
CC       its 3'UTR; thereby competing with the mRNA-destabilizing functions of
CC       RC3H1 and endoribonuclease ZC3H12A (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q3U108, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15941852,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32209697, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8649988}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with SOX9 (By similarity). Interacts with ESR1
CC       (PubMed:15941852). Interacts with RORC (By similarity).
