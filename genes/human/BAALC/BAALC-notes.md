## 2026-10-05 revision (reviewer round 1)

- Raft (core) vs PSD/synapse (non-core): both rest on rat evidence. Rafts follow from the lipidation motif in every expressing cell type, whereas the PSD is neuron-only. Endogenous rat localization: [PMID:15659234 "The gene was expressed widely in the frontal part of the brain, and the protein was localized to the synaptic sites and was increased in parallel with synaptogenesis."]
- The NEW term is now GO:0031435 (MAP3K1, Q13233). An adaptor term is not asserted because recruitment or bridging has not been shown.
