# ERCC6 original-source and database guide

All 139 primary contexts have been reviewed. The eleven external CSB–PGBD3 assertions are shown in the separate public table below and in linked supporting data; they are not appended as NEW annotations. This guide consolidates accepted source consultations; it does not claim new original acquisition during integration. Normal-cache bytes remain unchanged and reviewer access is distinguished from cache flags. No publication quotations were added.

The five broad canonical nucleus contexts are consistently refined to nucleoplasm using independently read human imaging. Conditional nucleolar localization remains separate; neither the nucleus refinement nor the HPA puncta establish a named nuclear-body class. The tagged fusion retains separate nucleus support and unresolved finer compartments.

## Core-function evidence

These four activity descriptions concern canonical human CSB. They describe different measured properties of the same protein. The separately assessed CSB–PGBD3 fusion is not assigned these motor activities.

- **ATP hydrolysis.** [PMID:12560492, DOI 10.1093/nar/gkg164](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkg164), Materials and Methods (protein purification, ATPase and nucleotide-binding assays), Results and Figures 2–5, directly measures DNA-stimulated phosphate release from ATP by insect-expressed human CSB.
