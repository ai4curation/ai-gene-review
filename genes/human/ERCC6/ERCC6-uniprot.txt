CC   -!- FUNCTION: Essential factor involved in transcription-coupled nucleotide
CC       excision repair (TC-NER), a process during which RNA polymerase II-
CC       blocking lesions are rapidly removed from the transcribed strand of
CC       active genes (PubMed:16246722, PubMed:20541997, PubMed:22483866,
CC       PubMed:26620705, PubMed:32355176, PubMed:34526721, PubMed:38316879,
CC       PubMed:38600235, PubMed:38600236). Plays a central role in the
CC       initiation of the TC-NER process: specifically recognizes and binds RNA
CC       polymerase II stalled at a lesion, and mediates recruitment of
CC       ERCC8/CSA, initiating DNA damage excision by TFIIH recruitment
CC       (PubMed:32355176, PubMed:34526721, PubMed:38600235, PubMed:38600236).
CC       Upon DNA-binding, it locally modifies DNA conformation by wrapping the
CC       DNA around itself, thereby modifying the interface between stalled RNA
CC       polymerase II and DNA (PubMed:15548521). Acts as a chromatin remodeler
CC       at DSBs; DNA-dependent ATPase-dependent activity is essential for this
CC       function (PubMed:16246722, PubMed:9565609). Plays an important role in
CC       regulating the choice of the DNA double-strand breaks (DSBs) repair
CC       pathway and G2/M checkpoint activation; DNA-dependent ATPase activity
CC       is essential for this function (PubMed:25820262). Regulates the DNA
CC       repair pathway choice by inhibiting non-homologous end joining (NHEJ),
CC       thereby promoting the homologous recombination (HR)-mediated repair of
CC       DSBs during the S/G2 phases of the cell cycle (PubMed:25820262).

CC       responses thus preventing premature entry of cells into mitosis

CC       RIF1 accumulation at DSBs thereby promoting BRCA1-mediated HR
