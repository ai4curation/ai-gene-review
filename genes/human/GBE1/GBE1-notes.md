# GBE1 biological notes

## 2026-11-04

GBE1 supplies the branching reaction during glycogen synthesis. The human functional and structural studies support this catalytic role; disease-associated loss of branching explains polyglucosan accumulation without requiring a second core activity ([PMID:8613547](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8613547/), [PMID:26199317](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26199317/)). The human 38–700 crystallization construct is distinct from the current 702-residue canonical product.

The STBD1 association remains a positive biochemical observation, while its downstream mechanism for GBE1 is unresolved. The viral NSP16 screen pair supports a bounded enzyme-binding classification, not an infected-cell role ([PMID:24837458](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24837458/), [PMID:36217029](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36217029/)).

Two original urinary-vesicle datasets identify GBE1. These support contextual fraction occurrence alongside its primary cytosolic role; they do not establish extracellular catalysis or vesicle topology ([PMID:19056867](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19056867/), [PMID:23533145](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23533145/)). Cation binding remains unresolved. The source-specific evidence and limits are in the [source guide](GBE1-source-guide.md) and [selected external evidence](GBE1-external-evidence.md).

## Review follow-up — 2026-11-04

A separate oligosaccharide-binding assertion now records the observed noncatalytic pocket, while the branching-enzyme core remains singular ([PMID:26199317](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddv280)). Human cDNA complementation independently supports branching function ([PMID:8463281](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8463281/)). UniProt assigns a monomer; this is distinguished from a newly measured solution assembly.

The NSP16 interaction retains its positive HIS3 evidence and partner-class refinement. STBD1 association remains positive despite removal of its uninformative generic term. Cation binding remains unresolved after the documented domain, structural-text and UniProt checks; a speculative experiment is withdrawn because missing mapping grounds do not establish a biological knowledge gap.

The [selected source evidence](GBE1-external-evidence.md) now exposes exact table fields, original download links and PAINT ancestry. The [provenance index](GBE1-source-evidence.yaml) points to the immutable lossless records rather than repeating the raw API matrix.
