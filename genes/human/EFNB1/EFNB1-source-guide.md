## Products and interpretation

The official displayed sequence is 346 residues. Its three annotated chains are 28–346 (mature), 218–346 (CTF) and 260–346 (ICD), with the short starts inferred. These are processing states, not alternative splice isoforms. The 1–240 tagged extracellular reagent in PMID:35922511, human expression constructs in PMID:16930449 and mouse cortical/tagged-domain work in PMID:19515908 remain source-specific. Positive nuclear localization is not a direct human transcription-factor assay.

All 66 source dictionaries, evidence codes and qualifiers remain intact. The generic-binding policy is the explicit [ClinGen curation instruction](../../../projects/CLINGEN_MENDELIAN.md#curation-instructions), rather than a blanket rejection of valid associations. Binding to an established receptor class does not itself assert receptor agonism.
