Gene Description

CHRNA1 encodes the alpha1 subunit of the skeletal-muscle nicotinic acetylcholine receptor. Two alpha1 chains join beta1, delta and either fetal gamma or adult epsilon to form a ligand-gated cation channel at the postsynaptic neuromuscular junction. Alpha1 contributes acetylcholine-recognition interfaces and pore-gating elements that convert motor-neuron transmitter release into endplate depolarization and muscle contraction. The assembled receptor also conducts calcium, with permeability influenced strongly by the complementary subunits. Pathogenic variants can impair receptor expression or alter gating and agonist affinity, causing congenital myasthenic syndromes; severe biallelic defects can cause fetal akinesia. Human splice products differ: the P3A-containing alpha+ product was not incorporated into functional receptors in the tested heterologous system.

Core Functions

The current displayed P02708-2 functional product must be distinguished from P3A-containing P02708-1; the latter was not incorporated into functional receptors in the original human-product expression study.
