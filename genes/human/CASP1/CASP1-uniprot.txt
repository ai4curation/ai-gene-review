CC   -!- FUNCTION: Thiol protease involved in a variety of inflammatory
CC       processes by proteolytically cleaving other proteins, such as the
CC       precursors of the inflammatory cytokines interleukin-1 beta (IL1B) and
CC       interleukin 18 (IL18) as well as the pyroptosis inducer Gasdermin-D
CC       (GSDMD), into active mature peptides (PubMed:15326478, PubMed:15498465,
CC       PubMed:1574116, PubMed:26375003, PubMed:32051255, PubMed:37993714,
CC       PubMed:7876192, PubMed:9334240). Plays a key role in cell immunity as
CC       an inflammatory response initiator: once activated through formation of
CC       an inflammasome complex, it initiates a pro-inflammatory response
CC       through the cleavage of the two inflammatory cytokines IL1B and IL18,
CC       releasing the mature cytokines which are involved in a variety of
CC       inflammatory processes (PubMed:15326478, PubMed:15498465,
CC       PubMed:1574116, PubMed:32051255, PubMed:7876192). Cleaves a
CC       tetrapeptide after an Asp residue at position P1 (PubMed:15498465,
CC       PubMed:1574116, PubMed:7876192). Also initiates pyroptosis, a
CC       programmed lytic cell death pathway, through cleavage of GSDMD
CC       (PubMed:26375003). In contrast to cleavage of interleukin IL1B,
CC       recognition and cleavage of GSDMD is not strictly dependent on the
CC       consensus cleavage site but depends on an exosite interface on CASP1
CC       that recognizes and binds the Gasdermin-D, C-terminal (GSDMD-CT) part
CC       (PubMed:32051255, PubMed:32109412, PubMed:32553275). Cleaves and
CC       activates CASP7 in response to bacterial infection, promoting plasma
CC       membrane repair (PubMed:22464733). Upon inflammasome activation, during
CC       DNA virus infection but not RNA virus challenge, controls antiviral
CC       immunity through the cleavage of CGAS, rendering it inactive
CC       (PubMed:28314590). In apoptotic cells, cleaves SPHK2 which is released
CC       from cells and remains enzymatically active extracellularly
CC       (PubMed:20197547). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15326478,
