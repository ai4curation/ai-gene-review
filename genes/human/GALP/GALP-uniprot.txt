CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 1]: Hypothalamic neuropeptide which binds to the G-
CC       protein-coupled galanin receptors (GALR1, GALR2 and GALR3). Involved in
CC       a large number of putative physiological functions in CNS homeostatic
CC       processes, including the regulation of gonadotropin-releasing hormone
CC       secretion.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 2]: Exhibits potent and dose-dependent
CC       vasoconstrictor and anti-edema activity in the cutaneous
CC       microvasculature, a physiologic effects which does not appear to be
CC       mediated via GALR1 or GALR2. Exhibits antimicrobial activity against
CC       Gram-negative bacterias, inducing bacterial membrane blebbing
CC       (PubMed:23537644). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23537644}.
