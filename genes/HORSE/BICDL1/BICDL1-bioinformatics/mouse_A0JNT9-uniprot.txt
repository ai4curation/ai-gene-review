CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as an adapter protein linking the dynein motor complex
CC       to various cargos and converts dynein from a non-processive to a highly
CC       processive motor in the presence of dynactin. Facilitates the
CC       interaction between dynein and dynactin and activates dynein
CC       processivity (the ability to move along a microtubule for a long
CC       distance without falling off the track). Predominantly recruits 2
CC       dyneins, which increases both the force and speed of the microtubule
CC       motor (PubMed:29420470, PubMed:33734450, PubMed:36071160). Component of
CC       secretory vesicle machinery in developing neurons that acts as a
CC       regulator of neurite outgrowth. Regulates the secretory vesicle
CC       transport by controlling the accumulation of Rab6-containing secretory
CC       vesicles in the pericentrosomal region restricting anterograde
CC       secretory transport during the early phase of neuronal differentiation,
CC       thereby inhibiting neuritogenesis. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20360680,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29420470, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33734450,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36071160}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of a tripartite complex with dynein and dynactin, acts an
CC       adapter linking the dynein motor complex and dynactin (PubMed:29420470,
CC       PubMed:33734450, PubMed:36071160). Interacts with KIF1C. Interacts with
CC       RAB6A and RAB6B; interaction is specific to Rab6.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20360680, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29420470,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33734450, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36071160}.
