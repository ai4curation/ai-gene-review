Gene Description

MAP2K2 (MEK2) is a dual-specificity protein kinase in the RAF–MEK–ERK signaling cascade. It phosphorylates the activation-loop threonine and tyrosine residues of ERK MAP kinases, linking upstream signaling to changes in cellular proliferation and differentiation.
