CC       infection (PubMed:20424185, PubMed:25573892).

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000305|PubMed:25573892};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Detected in developing macrophages at 28 hours
CC       post-fertilization (PubMed:20424185). Continues to be expressed in
CC       macrophages at 2 and 6 days post-fertilization (dpf) (PubMed:25573892).
CC       Also detected in neutrophils at 2 and 6 dpf (PubMed:25573892).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20424185, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25573892}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Viable with no significant effect on survival
CC       (PubMed:25573892). Macrophage and neutrophil counts are normal
CC       (PubMed:25573892). Macrophage migration in response to cxcr3.2-
CC       independent stimuli is not affected, although the basal migration rate
CC       is reduced (PubMed:25573892). Macrophage migration to sites of
CC       bacterial infection by S.typhimurium or M.marinum is reduced
CC       (PubMed:20424185, PubMed:25573892). Dissemination of bacterial
CC       infection to distant sites and granuloma formation is also reduced
CC       (PubMed:25573892). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20424185,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25573892}.
