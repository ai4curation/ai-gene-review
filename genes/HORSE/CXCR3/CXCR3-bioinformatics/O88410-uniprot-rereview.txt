CC   -!- FUNCTION: Receptor for the C-X-C chemokines CXCL9, CXCL10 and CXCL11,
CC       and mediates the proliferation, survival and angiogenic activity of
CC       mesangial cells through a heterotrimeric G protein signaling pathway.
CC       Probably promotes cell chemotaxis response (By similarity). Receptor
CC       for chemokine CCL21 (PubMed:10201891, PubMed:9653165). Upon activation
CC       by PF4, induces activated T-lymphocyte migration mediated via
CC       downstream Ras/extracellular signal-regulated kinase (ERK) signaling.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P49682, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10201891,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9653165, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9790904}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homomer. Forms heteromers with ACKR4 (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with PF4/CXCL4 (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P49682}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000250}; Multi-pass membrane
