CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multi-functional protein, involved in both the cellular
CC       response to amino acid availability and in the regulation of glycolysis
CC       (PubMed:23582324, PubMed:27913603, PubMed:32612235). GTPase-activating
CC       protein that plays a key role in the cellular response to amino acid
CC       availability through regulation of the non-canonical mTORC1 signaling
CC       cascade controlling the MiT/TFE factors TFEB and TFE3 (PubMed:23582324,
CC       PubMed:27913603, PubMed:32612235). Activates mTORC1 by acting as a
CC       GTPase-activating protein: specifically stimulates GTP hydrolysis by
CC       RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD, promoting the conversion to the GDP-bound
CC       state of RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD, and thereby activating the kinase
CC       activity of mTORC1 (By similarity). The GTPase-activating activity is
CC       inhibited during starvation and activated in presence of nutrients (By
CC       similarity). Acts as a key component for non-canonical mTORC1-dependent
CC       control of the MiT/TFE factors TFEB and TFE3, while it is not involved
CC       in mTORC1-dependent phosphorylation of canonical RPS6KB1/S6K1 and
CC       EIF4EBP1/4E-BP1 (PubMed:27913603, PubMed:32612235). In low-amino acid
CC       conditions, the lysosomal folliculin complex (LFC) is formed on the
CC       membrane of lysosomes, which inhibits the GTPase-activating activity of
CC       FLCN, inactivates mTORC1 and maximizes nuclear translocation of TFEB
CC       and TFE3 (By similarity). Upon amino acid restimulation, RagA/RRAGA (or
CC       RagB/RRAGB) nucleotide exchange promotes disassembly of the LFC complex
CC       and liberates the GTPase-activating activity of FLCN, leading to
CC       activation of mTORC1 and subsequent cytoplasmic retention of TFEB and
CC       TFE3 (By similarity). Indirectly acts as a positive regulator of Wnt
CC       signaling by promoting mTOR-dependent cytoplasmic retention of MiT/TFE
CC       factor TFE3 (PubMed:31272105). Required for the exit of hematopoietic
CC       stem cell from pluripotency by promoting mTOR-dependent cytoplasmic
CC       retention of TFE3, thereby increasing Wnt signaling (By similarity).
CC       Involved in the control of embryonic stem cells differentiation;
CC       together with LAMTOR1 it is necessary to recruit and activate
CC       RagC/RRAGC and RagD/RRAGD at the lysosomes, and to induce exit of
CC       embryonic stem cells from pluripotency via non-canonical, mTOR-
CC       independent TFE3 inactivation (PubMed:30595499). Acts as an inhibitor
CC       of browning of adipose tissue by regulating mTOR-dependent cytoplasmic
CC       retention of TFE3 (PubMed:27913603). In response to flow stress,
CC       regulates STK11/LKB1 accumulation and mTORC1 activation through primary
CC       cilia: may act by recruiting STK11/LKB1 to primary cilia for activation
CC       of AMPK resided at basal bodies, causing mTORC1 down-regulation (By
CC       similarity). Together with FNIP1 and/or FNIP2, regulates autophagy:
CC       following phosphorylation by ULK1, interacts with GABARAP and promotes
CC       autophagy (By similarity). Interaction with GABARAP also inhibits the
CC       non-canonical mTORC1 signaling cascade: GABARAP sequesters FLCN,
CC       thereby inactivating mTORC1 and promoting nuclear translocation of TFEB
CC       and TFE3 (By similarity). Required for starvation-induced perinuclear
CC       clustering of lysosomes by promoting association of RILP with its
CC       effector RAB34 (By similarity). Regulates glycolysis by binding to
CC       lactate dehydrogenase LDHA, acting as an uncompetitive inhibitor (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8NFG4,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23582324, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27913603,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30595499, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31272105,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32612235}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: GTPase-activating activity is inhibited in the
CC       folliculin complex (LFC), which stabilizes the GDP-bound state of
CC       RagA/RRAGA (or RagB/RRAGB), because Arg-164 is located far from the
CC       RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD nucleotide pocket (By similarity). Disassembly
CC       of the LFC complex upon amino acid restimulation liberates the GTPase-
CC       activating activity (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8NFG4}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via C-terminus) with FNIP1 or FNIP2 (via C-
CC       terminus). Component of the lysosomal folliculin complex (LFC),
CC       composed of FLCN, FNIP1 (or FNIP2), RagA/RRAGA or RagB/RRAGB GDP-bound,
CC       RagC/RRAGC or RagD/RRAGD GTP-bound, and Ragulator. Interaction with
CC       FNIP1 or FNIP2 mediates indirect interaction with the PRKAA1, PRKAB1
CC       and PRKAG1 subunits of 5'-AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK).
CC       Interacts with HSP90AA1 in the presence of FNIP1. Interacts with HSP70,
CC       STUB1, CDC37, AHSA1, CCT2, STIP1, PTGES3 and PPP5C (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with GABARAP; interaction takes place in the presence of
CC       FNIP1 and/or FNIP2 (By similarity). Interaction with GABARAP sequesters
CC       FLCN, thereby inactivating mTORC1 (By similarity). Interacts with RILP;
CC       the interaction is direct and promotes association between RILP and
CC       RAB34 (By similarity). Interacts with KIF3A and KIF3B (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with lactate dehydrogenase LDHA, but not LDHB; the
CC       interaction is direct, may preferentially bind LDHA dimers rather than
CC       tetramers, and regulates LDHA activity, acting as an uncompetitive
CC       inhibitor. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8NFG4}.

CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed throughout embryogenesis
CC       (PubMed:19850877). At 5.5 dpc, expression is restricted to
CC       extraembryonic tissues; by 6.5 dpc, expressed in both embryonic and
CC       extraembryonic tissues (PubMed:19850877). Strong expression is observed
CC       in certain tissues including neural ectoderm, headfold and limb buds,
CC       while it is weakly expressed in the surrounding endoderm and heart
CC       (PubMed:19850877). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19850877}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylation by ULK1 modulates the interaction with GABARAP and
CC       is required to regulate autophagy. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8NFG4}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Embryonic lethality at 5.5-6.5 dpc, showing
CC       defects in the visceral endoderm (PubMed:18974783, PubMed:19850877).
CC       Heterozygous knockout mice appear normal at birth but develop kidney
CC       cysts and solid tumors as they age, probably caused by activation of
CC       the mTOR pathway (PubMed:19850877). Conditional deletion in kidney
CC       leads to development of polycystic kidneys and renal neoplasia, caused
CC       by activation of the mTOR pathway (PubMed:18182616, PubMed:18974783,
CC       PubMed:32612235). Mice lacking Tfeb and Flcn in the kidney do not show
CC       any abnormality in the kidney, suggesting that the kidney phenotype
CC       observed in Flcn knockout mice is due to constitutive activation of
CC       Tfeb (PubMed:32612235). Conditional deletion in heart causes cardiac
CC       hypertrophy with deregulated energy homeostasis leading to dilated
CC       cardiomyopathy: defects are caused by mTORC1 up-regulation
CC       (PubMed:24908670). Conditional deletion in adipose tissue leads to
CC       browning of white adipose tissue (WAT) caused by deregulation of mTORC1
CC       that relieves cytoplasmic retention of Tfe3, leading to direct
CC       induction of the Ppargc1b/PGC-1 transcriptional coactivators, drivers
CC       of mitochondrial biogenesis and the browning program (PubMed:27913603).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18182616, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18974783,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19850877, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24908670,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27913603, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32612235}.
