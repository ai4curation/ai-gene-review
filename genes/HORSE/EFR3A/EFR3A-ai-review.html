Gene Description

EFR3A-family protein with a strongly conserved downstream scaffold region. The selected horse sequence lacks the N-terminal cysteine cluster used by mammalian EFR3 proteins for palmitoylation-dependent plasma-membrane anchoring, making the localization and membrane-recruitment function of this protein form uncertain.
