CC   -!- FUNCTION: Regulatory subunit of the voltage-gated calcium channel that
CC       gives rise to L-type calcium currents. Increases CACNA1B peak calcium
CC       current and shifts the voltage dependencies of channel activation and
CC       inactivation. Increases CACNA1C peak calcium current and shifts the
CC       voltage dependencies of channel activation and inactivation.
CC       {ECO:0000256|ARBA:ARBA00045432}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of a calcium channel complex consisting of a pore-
CC       forming alpha subunit (CACNA1C) and the ancillary subunits CACNB3 and
CC       CACNA2D1. The channel complex contains alpha, beta, gamma and delta
CC       subunits in a 1:1:1:1 ratio. Interacts with CACNA2D4. Interacts with
CC       FASLG. Interacts with CBARP; prevents the interaction of CACNB3 with
CC       the alpha subunit CACNA1C thereby negatively regulating the activity of
CC       the corresponding calcium channel. {ECO:0000256|ARBA:ARBA00046590}.
