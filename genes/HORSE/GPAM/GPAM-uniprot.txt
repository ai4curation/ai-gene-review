CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mitochondrial membrane protein that catalyzes the essential
CC       first step of biosynthesis of glycerolipids such as triglycerides,
CC       phosphatidic acids and lysophosphatidic acids. Esterifies acyl-group
CC       from acyl-coenzyme A (acyl-CoA) to the sn-1 position of glycerol-3-
CC       phosphate, to produce lysophosphatidic acid. Has a narrow hydrophobic
CC       binding cleft that selects for a linear acyl chain. Catalytic activity
CC       is higher for substrates with a 16-carbon acyl chain.
CC       {ECO:0000256|ARBA:ARBA00049585}.
