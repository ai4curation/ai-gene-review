CC   -!- FUNCTION: SNAREs (soluble N-ethylmaleimide-sensitive factor-attachment
CC       protein receptors) are essential proteins for intracellular membrane
CC       fusion. SNAREs localized on opposing membranes assemble to form a
CC       trans-SNARE complex, an extended, parallel four-helix bundle whose
CC       assembly releases energy that drives membrane fusion. The core SNARE
CC       complex typically consists of four alpha-helical domains, three from
CC       target membrane SNAREs (t-SNAREs) and one from a vesicle SNARE (v-
CC       SNARE) (PubMed:10036234, PubMed:11354632, PubMed:8663154,
CC       PubMed:9070898). SNAP23 is a t-SNARE (target-SNARE) that resides on
CC       target membrane and functions in the fusion of transport vesicles with
CC       the plasma membrane in immune cells (PubMed:30811271). In macrophages,
CC       regulates the stimulus-dependent vesicular transport of TLR4 from
CC       recycling endosomes to the plasma membrane by cooperating with STX11
CC       and thereby plays a critical role in the recognition of microbial
CC       components and the induction of innate and adaptive immunity
CC       (PubMed:30811271). In skeletal muscle satellite cells, SNAP23
CC       cooperates with its cognate SNAREs VAMP4 and STX11 to facilitate CD36
CC       vesicular transport from endosomes to the plasma membrane, an essential
CC       step in muscle regeneration (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O09044, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10036234,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11354632, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30811271,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663154, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9070898}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotetramer (via coiled-coil domain), also forms
CC       heterotetramers with STX4 and VAMP3 (PubMed:12556468). Forms a SNARE
CC       complex composed of STX4, SNAP23 and VAMP7 that functions in lysosomal
CC       exocytosis (By similarity). Forms a SNARE complex with VAMP4 and STX11;
CC       involved in vesicular transport from endosomes to the plasma membrane
CC       (PubMed:10036234, PubMed:24910990, PubMed:30811271). Found in a complex
CC       with VAMP8 and STX1A (PubMed:12130530). Found in a complex with VAMP8
CC       and STX4 in pancreas (By similarity). Interacts with STX12 (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with ZDHHC13 (via ANK repeats) (By similarity).
