Gene Description

OMA1 is a mitochondrial membrane zinc metalloprotease involved in stress-responsive protein processing and quality control. Its conserved catalytic architecture supports cleavage of mitochondrial substrates; mammalian OMA1 controls OPA1 processing and signals mitochondrial stress through DELE1.
