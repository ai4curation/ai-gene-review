Gene Description

sor encodes the sulfur oxygenase/reductase (SOR, EC 1.13.11.55) of the thermoacidophilic, chemolithoautotrophic and facultatively anaerobic crenarchaeon Acidianus ambivalens (Sulfolobales). SOR is a soluble cytoplasmic enzyme, induced under aerobic growth, that catalyses an oxygen-dependent disproportionation of elemental sulfur: one part of the sulfur is oxygenated to sulfite while another part is reduced to hydrogen sulfide, with thiosulfate arising mainly non-enzymatically from sulfite and sulfur. It is considered the initial enzyme of sulfur oxidation in Acidianus, supplying sulfite and thiosulfate to downstream membrane-bound oxidoreductases, although an ancillary "recycling" role has also been proposed. Twenty-four identical ~35 kDa subunits assemble into a thermostable hollow sphere (432 symmetry, ~871 kDa) enclosing a positively charged nanocompartment; each subunit has an inward-facing active-site pocket containing a low-potential mononuclear non-haem iron bound by a 2-His-1-carboxylate facial triad (His86, His90, Glu114) and an essential cysteine persulfide (Cys31). Substrate enters through apolar chimney-like channels and pores; Zn2+ inhibits the enzyme by binding in a side channel.

Core Functions

Cytoplasmic iron-dependent sulfur oxygenase/reductase: within the internal cavity of a 24-subunit hollow sphere, each active site (mononuclear non-haem Fe with a 2-His-1-carboxylate triad plus a Cys31 persulfide) disproportionates elemental sulfur in an O2-dependent reaction to sulfite and hydrogen sulfide, initiating sulfur oxidation in Acidianus ambivalens.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is SOR the obligatory first step of elemental sulfur oxidation in A. ambivalens in vivo, or mainly a recycling enzyme acting on sulfur produced by other pathways (e.g. sulfide:quinone or thiosulfate oxidation)?

Q: How are the reactive products (sulfite, H2S, thiosulfate) channelled from the SOR nanocompartment to downstream membrane enzymes (sulfite:acceptor oxidoreductase, TQO), and does A. ambivalens SOR associate with the membrane as reported for the A. tengchongensis ortholog?

Q: Should GO's "aerobic sulfur oxidation" (GO:0070220), currently defined by the sulfur dioxygenase reaction, be broadened or given a sibling so that O2-dependent SOR-initiated sulfur oxidation in archaea and bacteria can be captured precisely?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Construct an sor deletion (or CRISPR knockdown) in a genetically tractable sulfur-oxidizing Sulfolobales host and measure growth and sulfate production on S0 under aerobic chemolithoautotrophic conditions.

Hypothesis: If SOR initiates sulfur oxidation, its loss will abolish or severely reduce aerobic growth on elemental sulfur and sulfate formation.

Experiment: Immunogold electron microscopy and cell fractionation of aerobically grown A. ambivalens, combined with crosslinking/pull-down, to detect SOR association with the cytoplasmic membrane and with TQO or sulfite oxidoreductase.

Hypothesis: A fraction of SOR is membrane-associated in proximity to downstream oxidoreductases, enabling product channelling.

Automated deep research was unavailable for this review (falcon and openai providers
both failed authentication). No -deep-research-*.md file exists. The notes below were

Acidianus ambivalens" / "... Desulfurolobus") and the cached publications in
publications/. Most cached papers are abstract-only; PMID:21747782 (Veith et al. 2011)
and PMID:36191023 (cryo-EM, 2022) have full text.

Identity

  • 309 aa (308 after Met removal) homo-24-mer, ~871 kDa; Pfam PF07682 (SOR), dimeric
    alpha/beta-barrel fold. Organism formerly Desulfurolobus ambivalens; facultatively
    anaerobic, chemolithoautotrophic thermoacidophile (Sulfolobales).
  • EC 1.13.11.55; Rhea:13957 (4 S + O2 + 4 H2O = 2 H2S + 2 sulfite + 6 H+).

Activity

  • Purified from aerobically grown cells; absent from anaerobic cells. "In the presence of
    oxygen but not under a hydrogen atmosphere, the enzyme simultaneously produced sulfite,
    thiosulfate, and hydrogen sulfide from sulfur" PMID:2493451. Thiosulfate is mostly a
    non-enzymatic product: "thiosulfate was produced mainly in a chemical reaction between
    sulfite and sulfur" PMID:2493451. Optimum 85 C, range 50-108 C.
  • sor transcript 38-42x higher in aerobically grown cells PMID:1522063.
  • Described as "the initial enzyme in the sulphur-oxidation pathway of Acidianus
    ambivalens" PMID:15030315; same framing in PMID:21747782.
  • Physiological role debate: PMID:31188531 Heterologous SOR + TQO restored sulfur
    oxidation in S. acidocaldarius DSM639 (but not autotrophy).

Iron centre and active site

  • One Fe per subunit, high-spin ferric EPR g=4.3, Em -268 mV; "mononuclear iron centre"
    PMID:15030315.
  • Mutagenesis: H86A, H90A, E114A inactive with no iron; C31A/S inactive with normal iron
    PMID:15970399.
  • 1.7 A crystal structure PMID:16484493.
  • Zn2+ inhibits by binding a His in a side channel, Hg2+/IAA bind active site cysteines
    PMID:21747782.

Quaternary structure / localization

  • Icosatetramer, 432 symmetry, hollow sphere with internal nanocompartment
    [PMID:15698962; PMID:16484493 "Twenty-four monomers form a large hollow sphere enclosing
    a positively charged nanocompartment."].
  • Cytoplasmic: PMID:21747782. Kletzin 1989 called it a "soluble" enzyme PMID:2493451. Note that
    in Acidianus tengchongensis the orthologous SOR was also found associated with the
    cytoplasmic membrane PMID:15691910; not shown for
    A. ambivalens.

GO considerations

  • GO:0033755 sulfur oxygenase/reductase activity (IEA, EC mapping) – correct, core.
  • GO:0005737 cytoplasm (IEA SubCell) – correct.
  • Missing: GO:0019417 sulfur oxidation (definition "addition of oxygen to elemental
    sulfur" – exactly the oxygenase half-reaction SOR performs). Comparator check: GO:0019417
    is carried by >2000 sulfur-oxidizing enzymes (e.g. SoxA family, by IEA), so the term is
    used for the enzymes performing sulfur oxidation steps; SOR itself catalyses the step.
  • NOT GO:0070220 aerobic sulfur oxidation: its definition ties it to the sulfur
    dioxygenase reaction (a distinct enzyme, EC 1.13.11.18), so although SOR-dependent sulfur
    oxidation is O2-dependent, the term does not fit. Ontology pitfall for archaeal SORs.
  • Missing: GO:0005506 iron ion binding – crystal structure + mutagenesis (IDA). The UniProt
    entry carries KW Iron / Metal-binding and FT BINDING Fe (86, 90, 114) but no KW2GO or
    UniRule metal-binding row was propagated into GOA.
  • Only 5 proteins in all of GOA carry GO:0033755 (all IEA); no experimental GO annotations
    for any SOR despite four decades of biochemistry and multiple structures – archaeal
    under-annotation pattern.
