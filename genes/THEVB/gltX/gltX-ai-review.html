Gene Description

GltX is the non-discriminating class I glutamyl-tRNA synthetase of the cyanobacterium Thermosynechococcus vestitus BP-1, historically named Thermosynechococcus elongatus BP-1. It uses ATP to glutamylate both tRNA(Glu) and tRNA(Gln), with higher catalytic efficiency for tRNA(Glu). Glu-tRNA(Gln) supplies the intermediate for subsequent GatCAB-dependent glutamine formation on tRNA. Zinc is absent from its reported crystal structure, but whether the native enzyme binds zinc weakly in solution remains unresolved.

2026-10-26 BP-1 substrate and zinc review

Q8DLI5/tll0506/PDB2CFO identify Thermosynechococcus vestitus BP-1, historically T. elongatus BP-1. Thermus thermophilus in PMID:16876193 is the discriminating-enzyme comparator, not the target. Normal additional fetch of PMID:16876193 yielded the abstract; a publisher access attempt did not expose full text. The abstract explicitly establishes kinetic synthesis of both Glu-tRNA(Glu) and Glu-tRNA(Gln). The exact publication-linked UniProt record supplies the 13-fold efficiency preference. Live GO definitions distinguish GO:0004818 (tRNA(Glu)) from GO:0050561 (tRNA(Gln)); both are replacements for the existing broad ligase row, so no redundant NEW row is added. SerS GO:0004828, in contrast, already explicitly covers tRNA(Sec).

Read full PMID:25686371 Results and zinc-release experiments. Its approximately one-zinc measurement is for Ec(Te)-GluRS, an E. coli scaffold carrying the Te domain. Native Te structure 2CFO has no resolved zinc; the authors propose weak binding and possible crystallization loss. No zinc requirement is not the same as no zinc binding. The native full-length zinc-binding claim therefore remains UNDECIDED; it is not converted into a negative assertion or a core cofactor requirement.
