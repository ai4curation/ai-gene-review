Gene Description

McoA (locus PputGB1_2665) is a 1,131-aa multicopper oxidase of the manganese-oxidizing bacterium Pseudomonas putida GB-1. It has a hydrophobic N-terminal segment, described as a Sec signal peptide, a bacterial Ig-like (Big_9) region, and C-terminal cupredoxin domains with multicopper oxidase copper-binding motifs whose arrangement differs from that of the second GB-1 Mn-oxidizing multicopper oxidase, MnxG. Genetic analysis shows that McoA and MnxG are each sufficient for oxidation of soluble Mn(II), and of free or ligand-complexed Mn(III), to insoluble Mn(III/IV) oxides, and only deletion of both abolishes oxidation under standard conditions; the unrelated secreted heme peroxidase MopA is a third, conditional Mn oxidase. mcoA is positively regulated by MnxR and is activated at higher Mn(II) concentrations than mnxG, in a subset of cells, so that McoA contributes to Mn oxide biomineralization where MnxG activity is saturated or inhibited. The purified enzyme has not been characterized and its cellular location has not been determined directly.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

manganese oxidase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 2 Mn(2+) + O2 + 2 H2O = 2 MnO2 + 4 H(+), proceeding through Mn(III) intermediates, in which soluble Mn(II) is oxidized to insoluble manganese(III/IV) oxide with molecular oxygen as electron acceptor.

Justification: EC 1.16.3.3 (manganese oxidase), a multicopper-oxidase activity found in many Mn(II)-oxidizing bacteria, has no GO term; ec2go maps only EC 1.16.3.1 (ferroxidase) and the generic EC 1.16.3.- parent GO:0016724. Bacterial Mn(II)-oxidizing MCOs (Bacillus MnxG, P. putida GB-1 MnxG and McoA, Leptothrix MofA, Pedomicrobium MoxA) can currently only be annotated to the generic parent. Distinct from GO:0016689 manganese peroxidase activity, which uses H2O2.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on metal ions, oxygen as acceptor

Core Functions

Copper-dependent multicopper oxidase that oxidizes Mn(II), and free or ligand-complexed Mn(III), to insoluble Mn(III/IV) oxide, presumably coupling this to O2 reduction; one of two MCOs each sufficient for Mn oxidation in P. putida GB-1, contributing mainly at higher Mn(II) concentrations or where MnxG is limited. Activity is inferred from deletion genetics and family membership; the enzyme has not been purified.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is the N-terminal hydrophobic segment of McoA (Phobius transmembrane helix 12-33) a cleaved Sec signal peptide or an uncleaved membrane anchor, and where does McoA act relative to the cell surface oxides?

Q: Do McoA and MnxG act cooperatively (as suggested by the higher Mn oxidation rate of the wild type than of either single mutant), or in different cells?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Heterologous expression and purification of McoA to determine copper content and Mn(II)/Mn(III) oxidation kinetics, and comparison of its Mn(II) affinity and substrate inhibition with MnxG.

Experiment: N-terminal sequencing or fractionation of epitope-tagged McoA to test signal-peptide cleavage and localization (periplasm, outer membrane, supernatant).

Function (genetic)

  • Independently sufficient for Mn(II)/Mn(III) oxidation; double knockout with mnxG
    abolishes oxidation PMID:23124227.
  • Strain retaining only McoA oxidizes Mn(III)-L complexes PMID:29706936.
  • Expressed at higher Mn(II) and in colony centres; relieves MnxG substrate inhibition
    PMID:40470839; PMID:40122939.
  • Not purified/characterized PMID:42757987.

Paralogy

  • Not a duplication product of MnxG PMID:42757987;
    MopA is an unrelated heme peroxidase (see mnxG notes).

  • copper ion binding IEA -> ACCEPT; oxidoreductase activity IEA -> MODIFY to GO:0016724.
  • Proposed new term "manganese oxidase activity" (EC 1.16.3.3, no GO term).

CMMMech record notes (data/records/manganese.yaml; not edited)

  • Evidence text says McoA was "shown by clean deletion and overexpression in Wright et al.
    (2018) to oxidize Mn(II)". The clean deletion is from Geszvain et al. 2013
    (PMID:23124227); I found no McoA overexpression in Wright 2018 (it studied Mn(III)-L
    oxidation by single-oxidase strains). The edge "Mutant analysis of mcoA demonstrates
    independent Mn(II) oxidase activity" is fine as genetics; "oxidase activity" is inferred,
    not measured on the protein.
