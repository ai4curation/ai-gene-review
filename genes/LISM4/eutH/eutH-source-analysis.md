The native Listeria mutant had normal initial macrophage uptake, reduced recovery at the early vacuolar time point, and similar recovery to wild type later in infection. Neutralizing vacuoles with concanamycin A removed the early difference. These findings support an acid-associated physiological role; neither radiolabeled ethanolamine transport nor purified-protein reconstitution was performed. The rich-medium growth control is LB, not growth on ethanolamine as sole carbon or nitrogen source. The paper's historical diffusion interpretation concerns the uncharged ethanolamine fraction. The frozen UniProt entry instead describes probable protonated ethanolamine diffusion by similarity. Neither formulation establishes transported protonation state or coupling for this 10403S protein. The reviewed activity therefore specifies ethanolamine translocation without an ion mechanism.
