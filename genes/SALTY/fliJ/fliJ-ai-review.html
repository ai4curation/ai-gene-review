Gene Description

FliJ is the small coiled-coil central stalk of the Salmonella flagellar export ATPase assembly. It promotes formation of FliI hexamer rings and binds the cytoplasmic linker of FlhA to activate efficient membrane-potential-driven export of axial flagellar proteins. FliJ also recruits unladen export chaperones for recycling. Its export-support role is distinct from torque generation by the flagellar motor and from autonomous ATP hydrolysis. It associates with the cytoplasmic face of the inner-membrane export apparatus.

fliJ research notes

2026-11-06 comparative flagellar audit

P0A1K1 is the LT2 reference protein. RCSB 3AJW entity1 is its exact 147-residue sequence with an added N-terminal GSH. PMID21934659 tests Salmonella SJW backgrounds and tagged interaction constructs; exact structural sequence identity does not relabel those physiological experiments LT2. PMID17088562 distinguishes recycling of unladen export chaperones from generic protein-folding chaperone activity. ATP hydrolysis belongs to FliI. No new process annotation was manufactured from necessity.

Default Falcon was attempted once and returned HTTP403 authentication failure; the genuine command log is retained in the ignored comparative-panel audit directory. No provider report was fabricated.

All original annotation payloads, including any inherited NEW proposals, are preserved. Only reviewer-authored judgments and synthesis were updated. Identity bridges and construct limits are recorded under analysis/module_audits/bacterial_flagellar_assembly_export/comparative-panel/.

The motor-activity claim is removed rather than laterally rewritten: export activation is a different function. A separate NEW GO 0140677 row records the direct Salmonella binding-dependent activation evidence. This is consistent with the PSEPK ortholog motor rejection while permitting stronger direct activity confidence for the Salmonella exemplar.
