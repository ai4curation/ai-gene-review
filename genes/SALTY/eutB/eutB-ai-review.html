Gene Description

EutB is the catalytic large subunit of the adenosylcobalamin-dependent ethanolamine ammonia-lyase of Salmonella Typhimurium LT2. In the assembled EutB/EutC enzyme it converts ethanolamine to acetaldehyde and ammonium, initiating ethanolamine catabolism. Its active-site barrel and substrate/cofactor organization are conserved with the structurally characterized E. coli enzyme. The isolated Salmonella subunits are inactive; catalytic assignment to EutB does not imply autonomy from EutC. The enzyme is incorporated into ethanolamine microcompartments through EutC-associated targeting and interacts with the EutA reactivase.

eutB: LT2 lyase review

2026-11-06. Official frozen target P19264, taxon99287. Pristine seed/raw baseline is salmonella-lyase/seed-baseline.json in ignored audit cache. Every original annotation non-review payload is preserved; no NEW rows are added. A normal Falcon attempt failed HTTP403; this is manual curation, not a fabricated provider report.

  • PMID1550360: Historical purified Salmonella enzyme, with inactive separated subunits and active assembled alpha6-beta6 enzyme. Cached abstract only; exact construct termini are not available. Frozen UniProt assigns the study to LT2, but the older cloned sequences differ from current reference sequences; do not equate every residue of the historical preparation with current canonical records. Modern LT2 localization evidence independently supports gene/strain mapping.

  • PMID2197274: Historical cloning of the two Salmonella subunits. The abstract reports452/286 residues, whereas current LT2 EutB/EutC are453/298 residues; frozen UniProt records explicit conflicts against old AAA27063/AAA27062 translations. Used for enzyme/gene identity and reaction class, not exact current construct sequence.

  • PMID20519496: E. coli structural study with an N-terminally truncated beta subunit and cobalamin analogs/substrates. Active-site and interfacial cofactor organization are homolog inferences for SALTY, supported by frozen HAMAP/domain assignments; not a Salmonella crystal structure or a measurement of its native assembly stoichiometry.

  • PMID22428024: Full text: LT2-derived EutC1–19 reporter targeting in Salmonella, plus recombinant LT2 shell proteins in E. coli. Signal-peptide reporters and recombinant shells are distinguished from direct localization of unmodified native full-length EutC.

  • PMID27063436: Full text: native LT2 EutQ deletion and recombinant E. coli C2566 nanocompartments, with EutC1–19 cargo reporters and EutM/EutQ interactions. It is not restricted to the recombinant host named in the title.

  • PMID27450681: Cached abstract and curated UniProt document EutC/EutE targeting peptides and EutS contacts in recombinant nanocompartments. The precise native LT2 arrangement is evaluated using later2025 imaging and EutQ interaction studies; do not infer the complete native assembly mechanism from minimal recombinant shells.

  • PMID41032589: Full text: native LT2 fluorescently tagged proteins, scarless genomic deletions and EutQ/cargo peptide interactions support EutBC and EutE incorporation. EutC2–19 and EutE2–21 deletions disrupt incorporation. This supersedes the2013 external-EutBC spatial hypothesis; it does not invalidate all cofactor-recycling physiology in that older paper.

  • PMID35785499: Full text: LT2-derived JE6583 immunogold EAL association, EutA/EAL interactions and cognate complementation. The reported EutB peptide16–23 DVKEVLAK matches the frozen sequence. The printed EutC peptide128SEISK133 has five residues for six coordinates and does not exactly match current128–133 SEISDK; this is not used as sequence-identical construct proof.

  • PMID23585538: The external-EutBC arrangement was an explicitly inferential genetic model, superseded for LT2 by later localization and targeting studies. Cofactor-recycling hypotheses remain distinct from the rejected spatial placement.

GO decisions: the amino-acid-process mapping is a category error for amino-alcohol deamination. EutB is the catalytic chain in an obligate complex; EutC contributes cofactor/subunit architecture. Source-specific probable qualifier corrections preserve original enables rows. The2013 exterior model does not establish present LT2 topology. No NEW process term is needed; existing catabolism rows reflect catalytic-complex participation.
