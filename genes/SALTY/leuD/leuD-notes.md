# Salmonella Typhimurium leuD (P04787) curation notes

Provider deep research was not run for this gene. These notes record the
literature read for the review (cached abstracts under `publications/`).

- Native isomerase: 51 kDa LeuC plus 23.5 kDa LeuD, copurifying; in vitro
  complementation of leuC and leuD extracts; Km for 2-isopropylmalate 0.3 mM
  [PMID:7026530 "The native isopropylmalate isomerase was shown to have a Km for its substrate alpha-isopropylmalate of 3 x 10(-4)M."].
- Cryptic paralog NewD can replace LeuD when supQ is mutated [PMID:374346].
- The EXP row for GO:0003861 (UniProt, PMID:7026530) shows activity of the
  complex; reviewed as contributes_to via a CONFIRMED WRONG_QUALIFIER source
  defect, as are the IBA and IEA rows.

  ortholog carries the cytosol IDA rows).
