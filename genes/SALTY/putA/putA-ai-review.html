Gene Description

putA encodes the trifunctional proline utilization A protein (PutA) of Salmonella typhimurium, a 1320-residue homodimeric flavoenzyme. Its FAD-dependent proline dehydrogenase domain oxidises L-proline to 1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate, transferring electrons to membrane quinones, and its C-terminal NAD+-dependent L-glutamate gamma-semialdehyde dehydrogenase domain oxidises the semialdehyde to L-glutamate, allowing proline to be used as a carbon and nitrogen source. An N-terminal ribbon-helix-helix domain makes PutA the autogenous repressor of the divergent putA and putP genes. In the absence of proline PutA binds operator DNA in the control region in the cytoplasm; proline reduction of the FAD drives it to associate peripherally with the cytoplasmic face of the inner membrane, where it acts as an enzyme and the operon is derepressed.
