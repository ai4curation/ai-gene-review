Gene Description

FadB is the multifunctional alpha subunit of the cytoplasmic FadBA fatty acid oxidation complex in Salmonella Typhimurium LT2. Its hydratase and NAD-dependent (3S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase domains perform consecutive beta-oxidation reactions; cis-3/trans-2 enoyl-CoA isomerization and hydroxyacyl-CoA epimerization provide additional substrate-processing activities. FadB cooperates with the FadA thiolase to shorten fatty-acyl-CoA chains.
