# PIKFYVE (donkey, A0A9L0JQ61) notes

## 2026-10-14 DONKEY40 review

- Record: TrEMBL, 460 aa, "Phosphoinositide kinase, FYVE-type zinc finger containing" (Ensembl), PANTHER PTHR46715:SF1, donkey genome project only (PMID:33293529). Only domain annotated: DEP (277-352). 9 GOA rows, all IEA: 7 TreeGrafter (GO_REF:0000118, PTN001347166), 1 combined (PtdIns3P 5-kinase), 1 InterPro2GO (DEP -> intracellular signal transduction).
- UniProt lists seven donkey PIKFYVE entries; five are 2052-2108 aa (full-length kinase models, e.g. A0A8C4L106) and two are short (A0A9L0JQ61 460 aa, A0A9L0KIN8 449 aa). Checked via the UniProt REST search (gene:PIKFYVE AND organism_id:9793) on 2026-10-14.
- Sequence: against canonical human Q9Y2I7, [file:EQUAS/PIKFYVE/PIKFYVE-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md "Paired coverage is 21.93% of the human sequence and 100.0% of the donkey sequence."]; against human isoform 3, [same file, "The donkey sequence aligns to human isoform Q9Y2I7-3 (462 residues) with 96.08% identity among 459 paired residues"]. The donkey entry has the isoform 3 splice junction (NSLQHPQEN replacing human 109-203) and the isoform 2-4 C-terminal GRR truncation at 548 [human record VAR_SEQ VSP_040109/VSP_040110/VSP_040111, "Missing (in isoform 2, isoform 3 and isoform 4)"].
- Consequence: no PIPK catalytic domain (human 1758-2084), no chaperonin-like domain, and six of eight FYVE zinc-ligand cysteines absent. The FYVE finger is what targets full-length PIKFYVE to late endocytic membranes [human record, "Interaction of FYVE-type domain with phosphatidylinositol 3-"... "phosphate (PtdIns(3)P) is necessary for targeting to the membranes of the late endocytic pathway"].
- Full-length human PIKFYVE function (for the gene, not this isoform): sole PtdIns3P 5-kinase; PtdIns5P synthesis; endolysosome maturation; neutrophil chemotaxis/ROS/phagosome-lysosome fusion (PubMed:28779020); melanosome biogenesis (PubMed:29584722); inhibited by apilimod/YM201636.
- Decisions: kinase MF rows REMOVE (domain architecture); kinase-dependent BP rows REMOVE; vesicle CC rows UNDECIDED (no data; FYVE targeting unavailable); DEP->signal transduction MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. No core function: the short isoform's function is not characterized (recorded as a knowledge gap). This is a review of the protein product A0A9L0JQ61, not a claim that the donkey PIKFYVE gene lacks kinase function.
- ProtNLM2 GO: phosphatidylinositol phosphate biosynthetic process (GO:0046854).
