CC   -!- FUNCTION: Dual specificity kinase implicated in myriad essential
CC       cellular processes such as maintenance of endomembrane homeostasis, and
CC       endocytic-vacuolar pathway, lysosomal trafficking, nuclear transport,
CC       stress- or hormone-induced signaling and cell cycle progression
CC       (PubMed:23086417). The PI(3,5)P2 regulatory complex regulates both the
CC       synthesis and turnover of phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate
CC       (PtdIns(3,5)P2) (PubMed:17556371, PubMed:41639454). Sole enzyme to
CC       catalyze the phosphorylation of phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate on the
CC       fifth hydroxyl of the myo-inositol ring, to form (PtdIns(3,5)P2)
CC       (PubMed:17556371, PubMed:41639454). Also catalyzes the phosphorylation
CC       of phosphatidylinositol on the fifth hydroxyl of the myo-inositol ring,
CC       to form phosphatidylinositol 5-phosphate (PtdIns(5)P)
CC       (PubMed:22621786). Has serine-protein kinase activity and is able to
CC       autophosphorylate and transphosphorylate. Autophosphorylation inhibits
CC       its own phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate 5-kinase activity, stimulates
CC       FIG4 lipid phosphatase activity and down-regulates lipid product
CC       formation (PubMed:33098764). Involved in key endosome operations such
CC       as fission and fusion in the course of endosomal cargo transport
CC       (PubMed:22621786). Required for the maturation of early into late
CC       endosomes, phagosomes and lysosomes (PubMed:30612035). Regulates
CC       vacuole maturation and nutrient recovery following engulfment of
CC       macromolecules, initiates the redistribution of accumulated lysosomal
CC       contents back into the endosome network (PubMed:27623384). Critical
CC       regulator of the morphology, degradative activity, and protein turnover
CC       of the endolysosomal system in macrophages and platelets (By
CC       similarity). In neutrophils, critical to perform chemotaxis, generate
CC       ROS, and undertake phagosome fusion with lysosomes (PubMed:28779020).
CC       Plays a key role in the processing and presentation of antigens by
CC       major histocompatibility complex class II (MHC class II) mediated by
CC       CTSS (PubMed:30612035). Regulates melanosome biogenesis by controlling
CC       the delivery of proteins from the endosomal compartment to the
CC       melanosome (PubMed:29584722). Essential for systemic glucose
CC       homeostasis, mediates insulin-induced signals for endosome/actin
CC       remodeling in the course of GLUT4 translocation/glucose uptake
CC       activation (By similarity). Synthesis if (PtdIns(3,5)P2) is required
CC       for the cGAS-STING pathway (PubMed:41639454). Supports microtubule-
CC       based endosome-to-trans-Golgi network cargo transport, through
CC       association with SPAG9 and RABEPK (By similarity). Mediates EGFR
CC       trafficking to the nucleus (PubMed:17909029).
