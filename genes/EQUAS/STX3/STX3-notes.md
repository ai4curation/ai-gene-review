### Identity of the benchmark accession (correction)

- The earlier notes called A0A9L0J901 the canonical, membrane-anchored isoform A. That is
  not right. Syntaxin 3A and 3B share exons 1-8 and differ in the C-terminal half of the SNARE
  motif and the transmembrane anchor, encoded by alternative exons 9A/B, 10A/B and 11A/B
  [PMID:18683220 "the C-termini of syntaxin 3A and 3B differ and are generated by the splicing of three different exons, 9A/B, 10A/B and 11A/B"].
- New segment analysis (`STX3-bioinformatics/compare_isoforms_rereview.py`,
  `RESULTS-rereview.md`): A0A9L0J901 is 100% identical to mouse syntaxin 3B over residues
  226-262 (48.6% to 3A) but 92.6% identical to the 3A anchor segment (36% to 3B). It is a
  3B-SNARE-half + 3A-anchor combination, not one of the described forms 3A/3B/3C/3D
  [PMID:7598732]. Same length as 3A (289 aa); Habc, full SNARE motif and tail anchor intact;
  no catalytic residues apply (SNARE).
- It is Ensembl transcript ENSEAST00005047497 (not canonical) of gene ENSEASG00005007989.
  UniProt's gene-centric canonical for donkey STX3 is A0A8C4LHZ8 (289 aa, syntaxin 3A form);
  A0A8C4PQW9 (287 aa) is the syntaxin 3B form. No donkey STX3 entry is reviewed.
- The 92% identity to human isoform A reported in RESULTS.md is real, but it averages near
  identity outside the alternative exon with ~49% identity inside it.
