CC   -!- FUNCTION: E2 conjugating enzyme that catalyzes the covalent conjugation
CC       of the C-terminal Gly of ATG8-like proteins (GABARAP, GABARAPL1,
CC       GABARAPL2 or MAP1LC3A) to the amino group of phosphatidylethanolamine
CC       (PE)-containing lipids in the membrane resulting in membrane-bound
CC       ATG8-like proteins which is one of the key steps in the development of
CC       autophagic isolation membranes during autophagosome formation
CC       (PubMed:18768753). Cycles back and forth between binding to ATG7 for
CC       loading with the ATG8-like proteins and binding to E3 enzyme, composed
CC       of ATG12, ATG5 and ATG16L1 to promote ATG8-like proteins lipidation (By
CC       similarity). Also play a role as a membrane curvature sensor that
CC       facilitates LC3/GABARAP lipidation by sensing local membrane stress
CC       associated with lipid-packing defects as occurs with high molar
CC       proportions of conical lipids or strident membrane curvature
CC       (PubMed:24747438). Interacts with negatively-charged membranes
CC       promoting membrane tethering and enhancing LC3/GABARAP lipidation (By
CC       similarity). Also acts as an autocatalytic E2-like enzyme by catalyzing
CC       the conjugation of ATG12 to itself in an ATG7-dependent manner, this
CC       complex thus formed, plays a role in mitochondrial homeostasis but not
CC       in autophagy (PubMed:20723759). ATG12-ATG3 conjugation promotes late
CC       endosome to lysosome trafficking and basal autophagosome maturation via
CC       its interaction with PDCD6IP (PubMed:25686249). ATG12-ATG3 conjugate is
CC       also formed upon viccina virus infection, leading to the disruption the
CC       cellular autophagy which is not necessary for vaccinia survival and
CC       proliferation (PubMed:22024753). Promotes primary ciliogenesis by
CC       removing OFD1 from centriolar satellites via the autophagic pathway
CC       (PubMed:24089205). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NT62,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18768753, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20723759,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22024753, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24089205,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24747438, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25686249}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. Interacts with ATG7; this interaction forms an E1-
CC       E2 complex that is essential for the transfer of GABARAP thioester from
CC       ATG7 to ATG3 and disrupts interaction with the E3 enzyme complex (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with ATG12; this interaction is ATG7-dependent
CC       (PubMed:20723759), essential for phosphatidylethanolamine (PE)-
CC       conjugated ATG8-like proteins formation (PubMed:18321988) and also
CC       mediates the autoconjugation of ATG12 on ATG3 (PubMed:18321988,
CC       PubMed:20723759). Interacts with FNBP1L. Interacts with the E3 enzyme
CC       complex composed of 4 sets of ATG12-ATG5 and ATG16L1 (400 kDa); this
CC       interaction disrupts interaction with ATG7 and promotes ATG8-like
CC       proteins lipidation. Interacts with GABARAP and MAP1LC3A. Interacts
CC       with the ATG12-ATG5 conjugate; this interaction inhibits ATG8-like
CC       proteins lipidation (By similarity). Interacts (ATG12-ATG3 conjugate
CC       form) with PDCD6IP (via the BRO1 domain); this interaction is bridged
CC       by ATG12 and promotes multiple PDCD6IP-mediated functions such as
CC       endolysosomal trafficking, macroautophagy and exosome biogenesis
CC       (PubMed:25686249). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NT62,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18321988, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20723759,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25686249}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       Q9CPX6; Q9CQY1: Atg12; NbExp=5; IntAct=EBI-2911810, EBI-2911788;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NT62}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: ATG3 protein translation is increased by EIF5A.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29712776}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The membrane-curvature-sensing motif targets curved membranes.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24747438}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminal region works in concert with its geometry-
CC       selective amphipathic helix (AH) to promote LC3-PE conjugation activity
CC       only on the target membrane. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NT62}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The LC3 interacting regions (LIR) motif mediates interaction
CC       with GABARAP and MAP1LC3A in a beta-sheet conformation-dependent
CC       manner. The LIR motif is required for LC3 lipidation and ATG3~LC3
CC       thioester formation. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NT62}.
CC   -!- PTM: Conjugated to ATG12 at Lys-243 (PubMed:20723759). ATG12-
CC       conjugation plays a role in regulation of mitochondrial homeostasis and
CC       cell death, while it is not involved in PE-conjugation to ATG8-like
CC       proteins and autophagy (PubMed:20723759).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20723759}.
CC   -!- PTM: Cleaved by CASP8 upon death ligand binding such as tumor necrosis
CC       factor-alpha. CASP8 cleavage blocks survival-related autophagy and
CC       favors apoptosis. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NT62}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Homozygous mice lacking the Atg3 gene are at
CC       Mendelian frequency, their body weightis lower than expected and they
CC       die within 1 d after birth. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18768753}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the ATG3 family. {ECO:0000305}.
