CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor that acts as a master regulator of
CC       melanocyte survival and differentiation as well as melanosome
CC       biogenesis (PubMed:10587587, PubMed:22647378, PubMed:27889061,
CC       PubMed:9647758). Binds to M-boxes (5'-TCATGTG-3') and symmetrical DNA
CC       sequences (E-boxes) (5'-CACGTG-3') found in the promoter of
CC       pigmentation genes, such as tyrosinase (TYR) (PubMed:10587587,
CC       PubMed:22647378, PubMed:27889061, PubMed:9647758). Involved in the
CC       cellular response to amino acid availability by acting downstream of
CC       MTOR: in the presence of nutrients, MITF phosphorylation by MTOR
CC       promotes its inactivation (PubMed:36608670). Upon starvation or
CC       lysosomal stress, inhibition of MTOR induces MITF dephosphorylation,
CC       resulting in transcription factor activity (PubMed:36608670). Plays an
CC       important role in melanocyte development by regulating the expression
CC       of tyrosinase (TYR) and tyrosinase-related protein 1 (TYRP1)
CC       (PubMed:10587587, PubMed:22647378, PubMed:27889061, PubMed:9647758).
CC       Plays a critical role in the differentiation of various cell types,
CC       such as neural crest-derived melanocytes, mast cells, osteoclasts and
