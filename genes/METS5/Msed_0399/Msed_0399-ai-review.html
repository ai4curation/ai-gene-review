Gene Description

Msed_0399 encodes a 70-kDa bifunctional enzyme of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. It has an N-terminal NAD+-dependent (S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase region and a C-terminal enoyl-CoA hydratase (crotonase) domain, and it belongs to the same multifunctional-enzyme family as bacterial FadB. The purified native and recombinant enzyme hydrates crotonyl-CoA to (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA and oxidises that product to acetoacetyl-CoA. It uses NAD+ but not NADP+, and it does not act on the (R) stereoisomer. Both activities also work on C8 substrates, and the enzyme is most active with (E)-2-octenoyl-CoA. It was first identified as the crotonyl-CoA hydratase/(S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle, and with three other enzymes it converts 4-hydroxybutyrate to acetyl-CoA in vitro. Later kinetic, proteomic and comparative-genomic work showed that it is not the main enzyme for either step in vivo. The separate dehydrogenase Msed_1423 and the promiscuous hydratase Msed_2001 carry most of the flux, and the Msed_0399 gene is missing from several autotrophic Sulfolobales. The protein is abundant in both autotrophically and heterotrophically grown cells, and its gene lies in a cluster of beta-oxidation-like genes. Its main physiological role may therefore lie in short- to medium-chain acyl-CoA (fatty acid) metabolism, but this has not been tested in vivo.

Core Functions

NAD+-dependent oxidation of (S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoAs to 3-oxoacyl-CoAs by the N-terminal dehydrogenase region, with C4 ((S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA to acetoacetyl-CoA) and C8 substrates. The enzyme does not use NADP+. In vitro it can perform the (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA oxidation step of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle. In vivo, Msed_1423 carries most of that flux, and Msed_0399 may act mainly in short- to medium-chain acyl-CoA metabolism.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should Msed_0399 be annotated to carbon fixation (GO:0015977, or the proposed 'carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle') when Liu et al. estimate that it carries a small flux at best, and the gene is absent from several autotrophic Sulfolobales? The orthologous fusion protein is the sole candidate for these steps in the dicarboxylate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle of Ignicoccus and Pyrobaculum.

Q: Is Msed_0399 a beta-oxidation enzyme in M. sedula? What is its chain-length limit beyond C8, and is its gene cluster induced on fatty acids?

Q: UniProt's ARBA00008750 SIMILARITY line places the enoyl-CoA hydratase in the N-terminal section, but the Pfam domains (and the original paper) place it C-terminally. Should the rule's positional text be corrected for archaeal fusions with the reversed (dehydrogenase-first) domain order?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Delete the Msed_0399 ortholog in a genetically tractable Sulfolobales species (or in M. sedula if genetics allow). Compare autotrophic growth and growth on fatty acids, and measure crotonyl-CoA hydratase and (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase activities in extracts.

Hypothesis: Msed_0399 is dispensable for autotrophic growth but contributes to fatty acid catabolism.

Experiment: Assay recombinant Msed_0399 with C10 to C16 (E)-2-enoyl-CoAs and (S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoAs to define its chain-length range. Use quantitative proteomics to test whether it is induced on fatty acids.

Domain architecture

  • UniProt features: 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase NAD-binding domain (Pfam
    PF02737, residues 2-181), two 3HCDH C-terminal domains (PF00725, 183-277 and
    299-389), and an enoyl-CoA hydratase/isomerase domain (PF00378, 407-650). So the
    dehydrogenase is N-terminal and the crotonase is C-terminal.
  • This matches Ramos-Vera et al. PMID:21169482.
  • The UniProt SIMILARITY line from ARBA00008750 says the reverse ("In the
    N-terminal section; belongs to the enoyl-CoA hydratase/isomerase family"). This
    contradicts the entry's own Pfam domain coordinates. It is a text-level ARBA
    error that does not reach GO.
  • PANTHER PTHR23309:SF49 ("peroxisomal bifunctional enzyme"), the same
    multifunctional-enzyme family as E. coli FadB and human EHHADH. The domain order is
    reversed relative to FadB PMID:21169482.

Biochemistry

  • Identification: the enzyme was purified from autotrophically grown cells PMID:21169482. The
    recombinant enzyme catalysed both steps PMID:21169482.
  • Cofactor: NAD+, not NADP+ PMID:21169482. The protein
    also binds NAD+ in the purification PMID:21169482.
  • So GO:0003857 (3S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase (NAD+) activity (EC 1.1.1.35)
    fits. GO:0008691 3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase activity is defined with NADP+
    (EC 1.1.1.157) and does not fit, even though the paper and BRENDA use EC 1.1.1.157.
  • The NAD+ binding IEA (InterPro IPR006176) is therefore correct here. This contrasts
    with the default NAD binding error on the NADPH enzyme Msed_0709 (ARCHAEA pattern 6).
  • Expression: PMID:21169482. The activity is not autotrophy-specific.
  • In-vitro reconstitution: Msed_0406, Msed_1321, Msed_0399 and Msed_0656 PMID:24532060, with NAD+ in the assay.
  • Substrate range [PMID:32218776 "In hydratase reaction, Msed_0399 was promiscuous and
    could hydrate not only crotonyl-CoA, but also (E)-2-octenoyl-CoA and acrylyl-CoA
    (Table 2)."; "Similarly, it acted as dehydrogenase with both C4 and C8
    (S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoAs (Table 2)."; "Interestingly, the enzyme was most active with
    octenoyl-CoA, thus suggesting that Msed_0399 may participate in β-oxidation, in
    addition to autotrophic CO2 fixation."].
  • The hydratase therefore acts on (2E)-enoyl-CoAs from C3 to C8. GO:0018812
    3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydratase activity ((2E)-enoyl-CoA; no stereochemistry stated)
    is the right level. GO:0120092 (C4 only) is too narrow, and GO:0080023 is the
    (3R)-specific reaction, while this enzyme is (S)-specific.

  • Dehydrogenase: PMID:32218776. Msed_1423
    (UniProt A4YGM9) clusters with Msed_1424 and Msed_1426. Msed_1423 and Msed_0389 have
    kcat/Km values 3- and 20-fold higher than Msed_0399 for (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA.
  • Conclusion of the same paper: PMID:32218776.
  • Distribution: PMID:32218776.
  • Genomic context: PMID:32218776. Caveat:
    PMID:32218776.
  • Hydratase: PMID:33472982; PMID:33472982. The protein is abundant in autotrophic
    cells (89% of the level of Msed_2001), so its low in-vivo share reflects low Vmax, not

  • In the dicarboxylate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle of Ignicoccus and Pyrobaculum, the
    orthologous fusion protein is the only candidate and is thought to do both steps

  • Core MF: GO:0003857 (NAD+-specific, (S)-specific, C4 to C8 tested) and GO:0018812
    (C-terminal crotonase domain). Both are intrinsic activities of the protein. Neither
