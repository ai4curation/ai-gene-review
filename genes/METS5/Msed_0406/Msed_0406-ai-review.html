Gene Description

Msed_0406 encodes 4-hydroxybutyrate--CoA ligase 1 (HBCL1), an AMP-forming class I adenylate-forming acyl-CoA synthetase (ANL superfamily) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. In the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle it activates 4-hydroxybutyrate to 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA using ATP and CoA. This is the first step of the branch that converts 4-hydroxybutyrate, via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase, crotonyl-CoA hydratase/3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase and acetoacetyl-CoA thiolase, into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. A recombinant enzyme with these three partners converts 4-hydroxybutyrate to acetyl-CoA in vitro. The enzyme is promiscuous for short (C2-C5) carboxylic acids. Its catalytic efficiency in vitro is highest with propionate and butyrate, then valerate, acetate and 3-hydroxypropionate, and lowest with 4-hydroxybutyrate (Km about 2 mM). Even so, it is the physiologically relevant 4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase: its rate on 4-hydroxybutyrate is about eightfold higher than that of its paralog Msed_0394 (HBCL2), which prefers butyrate and valerate. Both genes are constitutively expressed. It is a soluble, crystallised enzyme; the single predicted transmembrane segment lies within the conserved N-terminal domain.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 4-hydroxybutanoate + ATP + CoA = 4-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA + AMP + diphosphate.

Justification: This is EC 6.2.1.40 (RHEA:23128), and GO has no term for it (OLS and QuickGO searches for 4-hydroxybutyrate and 4-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA ligase terms return none). The neighbouring step of the same cycle already has GO:0043955 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase activity. The activity is required in both archaeal 4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycles (3HP/4HB in Sulfolobales and Thaumarchaeota, DC/4HB in Thermoproteales and Desulfurococcales). 4HB is a hydroxy acid, not a fatty acid, so the parent should be CoA-ligase activity rather than short-chain fatty acid-CoA ligase activity.

Parent term: CoA-ligase activity

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:23258541 : Recombinant versions of these enzymes catalyzed the ligation of CoA to 4HB

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: Same proposal as in the Msed_0709 review. GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal 3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate, and Msed_0406 acts in that 4HB branch.

Parent term: carbon fixation

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:18079405 : This intermediate was reduced to 4-hydroxybutyrate and converted into two acetyl-CoA molecules via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase.

Core Functions

ATP-dependent (AMP-forming) ligation of CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate to give 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA (EC 6.2.1.40, RHEA:23128). This step opens the acetyl-CoA-regenerating branch of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle. 4HB is a kinetically poor substrate (Km about 2 mM), but Msed_0406 is the faster of the two M. sedula 4HB-CoA ligases.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should GO record every measured in-vitro substrate of a promiscuous archaeal acyl-CoA ligase as an enables annotation? For Msed_0406 the best in-vitro substrate (propionate) is not a cycle intermediate, and the physiological substrate (4HB) is the poorest one and has no GO term, so the annotation set currently describes the enzyme by its non-physiological activities only.

Q: Should the EC 6.2.1.2 (RHEA:48340) to GO:0031956 mapping be reviewed? EC 6.2.1.2 covers butanoate and other C4-C11 acids, while GO defines medium-chain as C6-C12, so butyrate- and valerate-only activities are mapped to a term whose definition excludes them.

Q: Is the GO:0004321 fatty-acyl-CoA synthase activity (EC 2.3.1.86) assignment on PANTHER node PTN008683229 (PTHR43605, ACSM-type acyl-CoA synthetases) an error to be corrected in PAINT?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Delete Msed_0406 and Msed_0394 singly and together, for example with a Sulfolobales genetic system, and compare growth on H2/CO2 with heterotrophic growth on peptides. Measure 4HB-CoA ligase activity and 4HB accumulation in the mutant extracts.

Hypothesis: Msed_0406, but not Msed_0394, is required for autotrophic growth of M. sedula.

Experiment: Using the 8BIQ structure, mutate candidate pocket residues (for example Ala249, Lys250, Ala321) and determine kcat/Km for 4HB, propionate and butyrate.

Hypothesis: Residues lining the acyl pocket determine the 4HB versus propionate preference of Msed_0406.

Pathway context

  • Cycle discovery PMID:18079405
  • In the cycle, propionyl-CoA is made from 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA (dehydratase +
    acryloyl-CoA reductase), not from free propionate, and acetyl-CoA is regenerated by
    thiolysis. So the acetate and propionate activities are not cycle steps. They are
    real in-vitro activities with no demonstrated physiological role.

GO assessment

  • Physiological activity: 4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase (EC 6.2.1.40, RHEA:23128). GO
    has no term (OLS/QuickGO searches for "4-hydroxybutyrate", "hydroxybutyrate-CoA
    ligase", "4-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA" returned none). Precedent: GO:0043955
    3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase activity exists for the neighbouring Msed_1456
    step. Propose a new term.
  • GO:0031956 medium-chain fatty acid-CoA ligase activity is defined for C6-C12 acids.
    The IDA and IEA rows came from EC 6.2.1.2 / RHEA:48340 (butyrate, valerate). Msed_0406
    has no measured activity on C6 or longer, so GO:0031955 short-chain fatty acid-CoA
    ligase activity (fewer than 6 C; parent of GO:0050218) is the correct term. This is
    an EC-to-GO naming mismatch: EC 6.2.1.2 is named "butanoate--CoA ligase" /
    medium-chain acyl-CoA ligase with a C4-C11 range, whereas GO medium-chain means C6-C12.
  • GO:0004321 fatty-acyl-CoA synthase activity is the fatty acid synthase reaction
    (EC 2.3.1.86, acetyl-CoA + malonyl-CoA + NAD(P)H), not an acyl-CoA synthetase.
    "Synthase" vs "synthetase" mix-up in the PANTHER TreeGrafter node (PTN008683229).
    Remove.
  • GO:0006633 fatty acid biosynthetic process: archaeal membranes are isoprenoid ether

    Mammalian ACSM-seeded TreeGrafter propagation. Remove.
  • GO:0016020 membrane: from a predicted TM helix (105-125), inside the N-terminal ANL
