Gene Description

Msed_0406 encodes 4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase (AMP-forming; 4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA synthetase), the main enzyme that activates 4-hydroxybutyrate to 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle of the thermoacidophilic archaeon Metallosphaera sedula. The step opens the branch of the cycle in which 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA is dehydrated, hydrated, oxidised and cleaved to two acetyl-CoA, regenerating the cycle's starting molecule and supplying acetyl-CoA to biosynthesis. The gene is induced during autotrophic growth on H2 and CO2. Msed_0406 is a soluble member of the medium-chain acyl-CoA synthetase (ACSM) family of the ANL adenylate-forming superfamily. In vitro it has broad short-chain specificity, activating propionate, butyrate, valerate, acetate and 3-hydroxybutyrate faster than 4-hydroxybutyrate, but its physiological role is defined by the cycle. A second, slower 4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase, Msed_0394, is also present.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 4-hydroxybutanoate + ATP + CoA = 4-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA + AMP + diphosphate.

Justification: EC 6.2.1.40 and RHEA:23128 describe this reaction, the 4-hydroxybutyrate activation step of the archaeal 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate and dicarboxylate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycles. No GO molecular function term exists for it (QuickGO search, 2026-11-02). Msed_0406 is an experimentally characterised example.

Parent term: short-chain fatty acid-CoA ligase activity

Supporting Evidence:

  • file:METS5/Msed_0406/Msed_0406-uniprot.txt : Xref=Rhea:RHEA:23128
  • PMID:23258541 : Recombinant versions of these enzymes catalyzed the ligation of CoA to 4HB

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice via 3-hydroxypropionate and propionyl-CoA to succinyl-CoA, which is reduced to 4-hydroxybutyrate; 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA is then dehydrated and converted via crotonyl-CoA and acetoacetyl-CoA to two molecules of acetyl-CoA.

Justification: The existing GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus pathway ending in malyl-CoA cleavage to glyoxylate, which does not include the 4-hydroxybutyrate branch used by Sulfolobales and Thaumarchaeota. Enzymes of that branch, such as Msed_0406, can currently be annotated only to the generic parent GO:0015977.

Parent term: carbon fixation

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:24532060 : This cycle adds two CO2 molecules to acetyl coenzyme A (acetyl-CoA) to generate 4HB, which is then rearranged and cleaved to form two acetyl-CoA molecules.

Core Functions

ATP-dependent 4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase (AMP-forming) activity, activating 4-hydroxybutyrate to 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA in the 4-hydroxybutyrate branch of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle. The enzyme has broad short-chain acyl-CoA synthetase activity in vitro; the physiological substrate is set by the pathway.
