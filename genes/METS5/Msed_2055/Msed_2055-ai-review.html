Gene Description

Msed_2055 encodes the coenzyme B12-binding (beta, small) subunit of the adenosylcobalamin-dependent methylmalonyl-CoA mutase (MCM) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. The 155-residue protein consists of a single Rossmann-type B12-binding domain, homologous to the C-terminal cofactor-binding domain of canonical bacterial and mitochondrial MCM alpha chains, and carries the conserved DXHXXG-SXL-GG motif whose histidine is the expected lower axial ligand to the cobalt of adenosylcobalamin. The substrate-binding TIM-barrel domain is supplied by the remotely encoded catalytic subunit Msed_0638. The recombinant mutase is soluble and active only when both subunits are coexpressed, and forms an alpha2beta2 heterotetramer that converts (R)-methylmalonyl-CoA to succinyl-CoA, a step in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle. Msed_2055 transcription is induced under autotrophic growth. The overlapping downstream gene Msed_2056 encodes a MeaB/ArgK-like G-protein, a candidate cofactor-loading chaperone.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal 3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half, which includes the methylmalonyl-CoA mutase step, and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate. Its enzymes therefore have no specific process term. The same term is proposed in the Msed_0709 and Msed_0638 reviews.

Parent term: carbon fixation

Core Functions

Coenzyme B12-binding (beta) subunit of the adenosylcobalamin-dependent methylmalonyl-CoA mutase. With the catalytic subunit Msed_0638 it forms an alpha2beta2 heterotetramer that rearranges (R)-methylmalonyl-CoA to succinyl-CoA in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle. Msed_2055 binds the cofactor, with the DXHXXG histidine as the expected axial cobalt ligand, and activity requires the partner subunit.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should the InterPro2GO mapping from IPR006159 (MCM C-terminal B12-binding domain) to isomerase activity be restricted to fused MCM chains, or changed to contributes_to, given that in split-subunit mutases such as M. sedula Msed_2055 and IcmB the matching protein is a non-catalytic cofactor-binding subunit?

Q: Does the MeaB/ArgK-like G-protein Msed_2056, whose ORF overlaps Msed_2055, load adenosylcobalamin onto the beta subunit and protect the mutase from inactivation, as MeaB does for bacterial MCM?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Purify Msed_2055 alone or as the reconstituted complex, and measure adenosylcobalamin binding by UV-visible and EPR spectroscopy. Compare wild type with an H33 mutant, and test whether the H33 mutant complex retains mutase activity.

Hypothesis: Msed_2055 binds adenosylcobalamin through His33 in the base-off/His-on mode.

Experiment: Coexpress Msed_0638, Msed_2055 and Msed_2056, and measure activity, cofactor occupancy and inactivation rate during turnover, with and without GTP.

Hypothesis: Msed_2056 increases cofactor loading or the stability of the Msed_0638/Msed_2055 mutase.

Annotation reasoning

  • GO:0031419 cobalamin binding (IEA, InterPro): ACCEPT, core. This is the subunit's own
    activity: the domain, the His-on motif and the paper's own naming ("coenzyme
    B12-binding subunit") all point to it. Inferred, not measured on the isolated subunit.
  • GO:0016853 isomerase activity (IEA, IPR006159): a whole-complex catalytic term on a
    cofactor-binding subunit (ARCHAEA pattern 16; cf. pattern 8 proteasome alpha). The
    mutase is an isomerase, and the subunit does do part of the work (it holds the
    AdoCbl whose Co-C homolysis starts the radical rearrangement, and supplies the axial
    His), but it does not catalyse alone. MODIFY to GO:0004494 methylmalonyl-CoA mutase
    activity, to be carried as contributes_to (core_functions), on the experimental
    ground that activity needs both subunits PMID:22752162.
  • GO:0046872 metal ion binding: His33 coordinates the cobalt of cobalamin, so the term is
    not false, but the metal is bound only as part of the cofactor and cobalamin binding
    is the informative statement. KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, as in the human MTR review (the human
    MMUT review accepted it).
  • NEW GO:0015977 carbon fixation (IDA, PMID:22752162), as for Msed_0638. Participation
    test: the subunit supplies the cofactor-binding domain the catalytic step depends on
    (the "cofactor" shape). Comparator: Msed_0638 and the other cycle enzymes carry the
    same term in their reviews. GO:0043427 (Chloroflexus cycle) is not used; the proposed
    term "carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle" (Msed_0709) is
    reused.

Interpretation: the single DXHXXG match (D31-G36, DGHDRG) lies in the PROSITE
B12-binding domain (residues 20-148), with SIL at 78 and GG at 108 downstream in the
spacing of the class I B12-binding Rossmann fold (SXL ~ 40-45 and GG ~ 70-80 residues
after the His). This matches the DXHXXG-SXL-GG motif reported for M. sedula MCM-beta by

the motif. Motif assignment is by pattern and spacing only; no structure was modelled.

proposed_new_terms:
- proposed_name: carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle
  proposed_definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA
    is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are
    reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate.
    4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two
    molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in
    ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.
  justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined
    as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal
    3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half, which includes the methylmalonyl-CoA mutase
    step, and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate. Its enzymes therefore have
    no specific process term. The same term is proposed in the Msed_0709 and Msed_0638
    reviews.
