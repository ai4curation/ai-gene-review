Gene Description

Msed_0638 encodes the catalytic (alpha, large) subunit of the coenzyme B12-dependent methylmalonyl-CoA mutase (MCM) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. The 553-residue protein consists of the substrate-binding TIM-barrel domain of the MCM/isobutyryl-CoA mutase family and lacks the C-terminal B12-binding domain that is fused to it in bacterial and mitochondrial MCMs. That domain is supplied by a separate, remotely encoded subunit, Msed_2055 (MCM-beta), which carries the DXHXXG-SXL-GG cobalamin-binding motif. The recombinant enzyme is soluble and active only when both subunits are coexpressed, and forms an alpha2beta2 heterotetramer that converts (R)-methylmalonyl-CoA to succinyl-CoA. Together with the adjacent methylmalonyl-CoA epimerase gene Msed_0639, it converts the (S)-methylmalonyl-CoA formed by propionyl-CoA carboxylation into succinyl-CoA, closing the 3-hydroxypropionate half of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle. Its transcription is induced under autotrophic growth.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal 3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half, which includes the methylmalonyl-CoA epimerase and mutase steps, and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate. Its enzymes therefore have no specific process term. The same term is proposed in the Msed_0709 review.

Parent term: carbon fixation

Core Functions

Catalytic (alpha) subunit of the adenosylcobalamin-dependent methylmalonyl-CoA mutase. With the B12-binding subunit Msed_2055 it forms an alpha2beta2 heterotetramer that rearranges (R)-methylmalonyl-CoA to succinyl-CoA in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle. Msed_0638 supplies the substrate-binding TIM barrel, and activity requires the partner subunit.

Identity and structure

  • UniProt A4YEG1 (unreviewed), 553 aa, "Methylmalonyl-CoA mutase", EC 5.4.99.2.
    InterPro IPR006099 / IPR006098 / IPR016176 (MCM alpha/beta catalytic TIM-barrel),
    NCBIfam TIGR00641 (acid_CoA_mut_N), PANTHER PTHR48101:SF1 (MCM large subunit),
    CDD cd03680 (MM_CoA_mutase_ICM_like). The protein contains only the N-terminal
    substrate-binding TIM-barrel domain (Pfam PF01642, residues 31-546) and lacks the
    C-terminal Rossmann-type B12-binding domain that is fused to the catalytic domain in
    bacterial/human MCM alpha chains [file:METS5/Msed_0638/Msed_0638-uniprot.txt
    "Methylmalonyl-CoA mutase alpha/beta chain catalytic"].
  • The B12-binding domain is encoded separately by Msed_2055 (UniProt A4YIE3,
    155 aa, "Cobalamin B12-binding domain protein", PANTHER PTHR48101:SF3; UniProt REST
    query 2026-11-01). Msed_2056 (A4YIE4, 314 aa, "LAO/AO transport system ATPase",
    PANTHER PTHR43087) is a MeaB/ArgK-like G-protein chaperone homolog.
  • This split architecture (catalytic TIM-barrel subunit + separate B12-binding
    subunit) resembles isobutyryl-CoA mutase IcmA/IcmB rather than the classic bacterial
    MCM alpha/beta, in which the alpha chain carries both domains.

    substrate and the cofactor" is not how classic heterodimeric MCMs work (the alpha

Conclusions

  • Msed_0638 is the catalytic (alpha, large) subunit of a B12-dependent alpha2beta2
    methylmalonyl-CoA mutase; Msed_2055 is the B12-binding beta subunit. Activity was
    shown for the coexpressed recombinant complex, and transcription is induced under
    autotrophy, so the enzyme acts in the 3HP/4HB cycle (recombinant activity + expression,
    not genetic proof).
  • GO: GO:0004494 accepted (catalytic subunit carrying the substrate-binding site,
    active only within the alpha2beta2 complex). Cobalamin binding: the cofactor sits at the
    interface of the TIM barrel and the separate B12-binding subunit; the His ligand motif is
    on Msed_2055. Kept as non-core. Carbon fixation GO:0015977 added (NEW) as for siblings.
  • Module change needed: the mcm annotons should include Msed_2055 (A4YIE3) as the
    B12-binding subunit (complex), and the knowledge gap about these steps should be revised
    given PMID:22752162.
