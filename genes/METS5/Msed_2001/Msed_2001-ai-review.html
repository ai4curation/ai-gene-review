Gene Description

Msed_2001 encodes the 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA dehydratase (EC 4.2.1.116) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. It is a soluble enzyme of the enoyl-CoA hydratase (crotonase) family and works in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle of the Sulfolobales. In the first half of the cycle it dehydrates 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA to acryloyl-CoA, which acryloyl-CoA reductase then reduces to propionyl-CoA. The same active site reversibly hydrates crotonyl-CoA to (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA at a similar rate; it does not use the (R) stereoisomer. Quantitative proteomics and kinetics indicate that this promiscuous enzyme carries almost all of the cell's 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA dehydratase activity and most of its crotonyl-CoA hydratase activity, the latter being a step of the second half of the cycle that regenerates acetyl-CoA. The crystal structure with CoA shows the conserved crotonase catalytic glutamates (Glu113, Glu133) and a small enoyl pocket lined by bulky aromatic residues, suited to short-chain substrates. Reported oligomeric states differ (monomer, hexamer, octamer). The enzyme is conserved in autotrophic Sulfolobales and is unrelated to the dehydratase domain of the Chloroflexus propionyl-CoA synthase.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal 3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half of that route, uses unrelated enzymes for it, and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate and crotonyl-CoA. Msed_2001 catalyses steps in both halves of the archaeal cycle. This proposal repeats the one in the Msed_0709 review.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: UniProt annotates the preceding enzyme, Msed_1456, to GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle by IDA, and ARBA spreads the term across archaea. Yet the term is defined as the Chloroflexus glyoxylate-forming cycle. Should GO broaden GO:0043427, or create a separate 3HP/4HB-cycle term, so that enzymes of the two cycles are annotated consistently?

Q: What is the true oligomeric state of Msed_2001? Gel filtration gave a monomer, the crystal structure and size exclusion gave a hexamer, and Loder et al. reported an octamer. Does it form a complex with 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase (Msed_1456) in vivo?

Q: Should PANTHER separate the archaeal 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA dehydratase clade from the ECHS1-named subfamily PTHR11941:SF54, so that TreeGrafter stops propagating fatty acid beta-oxidation to it?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Delete Msed_2001 in M. sedula or a genetically tractable Sulfolobales relative, for example Sulfolobus acidocaldarius with a reconstituted pathway or its ortholog in a related autotroph. Compare autotrophic and heterotrophic growth, and measure 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA dehydratase and crotonyl-CoA hydratase activities in extracts.

Hypothesis: Msed_2001 is required for autotrophic growth of M. sedula and is the main crotonyl-CoA hydratase in vivo.

Experiment: Test recombinant Msed_2001 with C6 to C16 trans-2-enoyl-CoAs to define its chain-length limit. Compare Msed_2001 protein levels and growth of a deletion strain on fatty acids versus peptides.

Hypothesis: Msed_2001 contributes to fatty acid or butyrate catabolism during heterotrophic growth.

Pathway context

  • 3HP/4HB cycle PMID:18079405
  • Activity is not regulated by growth mode: PMID:19429610. So the enzyme is present during heterotrophic growth too; any heterotrophic role is unknown.
  • Chloroflexus uses an unrelated trifunctional propionyl-CoA synthase for the same reaction
    (PMID:19429610 abstract), so the archaeal and Chloroflexus cycles evolved independently.
  • Process term: GO:0043427 "carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle" is defined as the
    Chloroflexus glyoxylate-forming cycle (OLS definition checked). Following the Msed_0709
    review, use GO:0015977 carbon fixation and the proposed term "carbon fixation by
    3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle". Note: QuickGO shows UniProt has annotated
    Msed_1456 (A4YGR1) and S. tokodaii STK_07830 to GO:0043427 by IDA (PMID:18165310), and
    ARBA (GO_REF:0000117) puts GO:0043427 on many archaeal enzymes, so UniProt treats the term
    as covering the archaeal cycle. Flagged as a suggested question rather than resolved here.

Fatty acid beta-oxidation (GO:0006635, TreeGrafter)

  • Source: PANTHER PTN000235214; the subfamily is PTHR11941:SF54 "ENOYL-COA HYDRATASE,
    MITOCHONDRIAL" (named for ECHS1), so the term is the mitochondrial crotonase's role.
  • GO:0006635 definition: complete oxidation of a long-chain fatty acid. Msed_2001 has a
    short-chain pocket (PMID:30013155), is characterized only on C3/C4 substrates, and is
    accounted for by the CO2-fixation cycle (PMID:33472982). No study shows fatty acid
    degradation by M. sedula or a role of Msed_2001 in it. In S. acidocaldarius, beta-oxidation
    genes sit in a dedicated FadR-regulated cluster PMID:30948713;
    Msed_2001 is instead the universally conserved cycle enzyme of autotrophic Sulfolobales.
  • The crotonyl-CoA hydration it catalyses is the same chemistry as the last round of
    beta-oxidation, but in M. sedula it runs in the anabolic direction of the cycle. So the
    term is a family-level over-annotation; it cannot be ruled out entirely (the enzyme could
    in principle hydrate crotonyl-CoA during butyrate/fatty acid catabolism), so
    MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED rather than REMOVE.
  • Pattern for the catalogue: same shape as pattern 1 (a recruited paralog inherits the
    ancestral family's process) — here crotonase family -> fatty acid beta-oxidation.

  proposed_definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA
    is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are
    reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate.
