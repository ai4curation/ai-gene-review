Gene Description

Msed_0148 encodes the biotin carboxyl carrier protein (BCCP, beta subunit, 18.6 kDa) of the acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. This bifunctional biotin-dependent enzyme catalyses both carboxylations of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle. The holoenzyme is a complex of about 560 kDa, probably (alpha beta gamma)4. Its other subunits are the biotin carboxylase (Msed_0147, encoded by the gene just upstream and translationally coupled to Msed_0148) and the carboxyltransferase (Msed_1375). The C-terminal biotinyl domain carries the covalently attached biotin prosthetic group. The biotin carboxylase subunit carboxylates this biotin with ATP and bicarbonate, and the carboxybiotin then delivers the carboxyl group to acetyl-CoA or propionyl-CoA at the carboxyltransferase site. As in other Sulfolobales, the residue after the biotinylated lysine is a serine rather than the usual methionine. The M. sedula biotin protein ligase (Msed_2010) biotinylates this subunit in vitro, and poor biotinylation by heterologous ligases limits the activity of the recombinant enzyme. The gene is strongly induced under autotrophic growth. Small biotinylated proteins of this kind are abundant in autotrophic Sulfolobales but absent from archaea that use other CO2-fixation pathways.

Core Functions

Biotin carboxyl carrier subunit of the acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase. Its covalently bound biotin is carboxylated by the biotin carboxylase subunit (Msed_0147) and carries the carboxyl group to acetyl-CoA at the carboxyltransferase subunit (Msed_1375), forming malonyl-CoA in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: GO has no molecular-function term for the carboxyl-carrier role of a BCCP (by analogy with GO:0000036 acyl carrier activity). Should GO add a "biotin carboxyl carrier activity" term, so that BCCP subunits need not be annotated only through contributes_to the whole-enzyme activity?

Q: GO:0009317 acetyl-CoA carboxylase complex is defined by fatty acid biosynthesis. Should it be generalized, or a new complex term made, to cover the archaeal bifunctional acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Biotinylate recombinant Msed_0148 with Msed_2010 and map the modified residue by LC-MS/MS. Test K133A and S134M variants for biotinylation by Msed_2010 and by E. coli BirA, and test reconstituted holoenzyme activity with acetyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA.

Hypothesis: Lys133, in the AMKS motif, is the single biotinylation site of Msed_0148, and the serine at position +1 is what restricts biotinylation to Sulfolobales-type biotin protein ligases.

Subunit-level function and GO

BCCP carries the covalently bound biotin. BC carboxylates it, and the carboxybiotin arm moves to the
CT site, where the carboxyl group is transferred to acetyl-CoA or propionyl-CoA. BCCP thus supplies the
cofactor the reaction depends on: it does part of the work (the "cofactor" case in CLAUDE.md), so
contributes_to the overall activities and involvement in carbon fixation pass the participation test.

GO has no molecular-function term for "carboxyl carrier activity"; GO:0009374 biotin binding describes
non-covalent binding of free biotin and is not used for a covalently attached prosthetic group.
E. coli AccB is annotated enables GO:0003989 (IMP, IBA) and part_of GO:0009317. Here I use
contributes_to GO:0003989 and GO:0004658 (NEW, IDA from the purified complex, PMID:12581213) and
involved_in GO:0015977 carbon fixation. No CC term (see Msed_0147 notes on GO:0009317).

Open points

  • No direct experiment (mass spectrometry or mutagenesis) has located the biotinylated lysine in
    M. sedula BCCP; K133 is a sequence-based prediction.
  • NCBI esummary was down on 2026-11-01. PMID:12581213, PMID:9973333, PMID:12533469 and PMID:27315782 were
    cached with the repo's normal fetch pipeline, using metadata taken from efetch XML.
