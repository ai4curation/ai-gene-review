Gene Description

Msed_0147 encodes the biotin carboxylase (alpha) subunit of the acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. This biotin-dependent enzyme is the carboxylating enzyme of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle of the Sulfolobales. The purified enzyme is a stable complex of about 560 kDa, probably (alpha beta gamma)4. It is made of this 57 kDa biotin carboxylase, the 18.6 kDa biotin carboxyl carrier protein (Msed_0148) and the 57 kDa carboxyltransferase (Msed_1375). The single enzyme carboxylates acetyl-CoA to malonyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA to (S)-methylmalonyl-CoA at similar rates, using ATP and bicarbonate. Within the complex the alpha subunit performs the first half-reaction: the ATP-dependent carboxylation of the biotin carried on the beta subunit (biotin carboxylase, EC 6.3.4.14). It has an N-terminal ATP-grasp biotin carboxylation domain and a C-terminal BC domain. The three subunit genes are strongly induced under autotrophic growth. Because archaea have no fatty acids, the enzyme serves CO2 fixation rather than lipid synthesis. Together with the reductase steps that follow, the M. sedula enzyme has been expressed in Pyrococcus furiosus to make 3-hydroxypropionate from CO2.

Core Functions

Biotin carboxylase subunit of the acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase. It catalyses the ATP-dependent carboxylation of the biotin prosthetic group on the biotin carboxyl carrier protein (Msed_0148). This is the first half-reaction of the acetyl-CoA carboxylation that starts the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: GO:0009317 acetyl-CoA carboxylase complex is defined as catalysing the first step in long-chain fatty acid biosynthesis, and GO has no propionyl-CoA carboxylase complex term. Should the definition be generalized, or a term created for the archaeal bifunctional acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase complex, so that its three subunits can be annotated to a specific complex?

Q: Which divalent cation does the M. sedula biotin carboxylase use in vivo? The UniProt Co(2+) cofactor statement comes from an ARBA rule, not from data on this enzyme.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express Msed_0147 alone and assay bicarbonate-dependent ATP hydrolysis with free D-biotin and with BCCP (Msed_0148) biotinylated in vitro by Msed_2010. Measure ADP or phosphate formation, with and without acetyl-CoA.

Hypothesis: The isolated alpha subunit catalyses ATP-dependent carboxylation of free biotin or of the biotinylated beta subunit, independently of the carboxyltransferase.

Identity

  • UniProt A4YD22 (TrEMBL, unreviewed), 510 aa, "Biotin carboxylase", EC 6.3.4.14, locus Msed_0147.
  • Domains: biotin carboxylation domain (PS50979, 3-451), ATP-grasp (PS50975, 122-320); Pfam
    Biotin_carb_N, CPSase_L_D2, Biotin_carb_C. PANTHER PTHR45007:SF1. [file:METS5/Msed_0147/Msed_0147-uniprot.txt]
  • Gene neighbour: Msed_0148 (BCCP, beta subunit); the two genes appear translationally coupled
    PMID:23530213.
    The carboxyltransferase (gamma) subunit is encoded elsewhere, Msed_1375.

The enzyme

  • The carboxylase of the 3HP/4HB cycle was purified from M. sedula. It is one enzyme that acts on both
    acetyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA, a ~560 kDa complex of a 57 kDa biotin carboxylase (alpha), a 57 kDa
    carboxyltransferase (gamma) and an 18.6 kDa biotin carrier (beta), probably (alpha beta gamma)4
    PMID:12581213
    PMID:12581213
    Km 0.06 mM acetyl-CoA, 0.07 mM propionyl-CoA, 0.04 mM ATP, 0.3 mM bicarbonate (abstract).
  • Earlier: biotin-dependent ACC and PCC activities in autotrophic M. sedula extracts, and a small
    biotin-carrying protein PMID:9973333.
  • Acidianus brierleyi ortholog: alpha4beta4gamma4, bifunctional; ATP-binding residues are in the BC
    subunit PMID:12533469.
  • Pathway role: key carboxylating enzyme of the cycle PMID:18079405;
    promiscuous PMID:19684143.
  • Gene assignment and induction: PMID:24532060.
  • Heterologous function: Msed_0147-0148 + Msed_1375 (E1) expressed in Pyrococcus furiosus gave ACC
    activity (phosphate release from acetyl-CoA, ATP, bicarbonate) and, with E2/E3, 3-HP from CO2
    PMID:23530213.
  • ACC is a high flux-control step and is inhibited by malonyl-CoA, succinyl-CoA, ADP and CoA
    PMID:27771364.

  • Archaea have no fatty acids, so this ACC does not feed fatty-acid synthesis PMID:9973333.

  • Subunit expression: recombinant alpha and gamma subunits made in E. coli were insoluble and inactive;
    active recombinant holoenzyme was obtained only in P. furiosus with the M. sedula biotin protein ligase
    PMID:27315782.

Subunit-level function

The BC subunit performs the first half-reaction: ATP-dependent carboxylation of the biotin on BCCP
(GO:0004075 biotin carboxylase activity, EC 6.3.4.14). The overall acyl-CoA carboxylase activities
(GO:0003989, GO:0004658) belong to the alpha-beta-gamma complex, so the BC subunit contributes_to
them. No individual-subunit assay has been published for the M. sedula enzyme; the half-reaction
assignment rests on the BC domain architecture and on the A. brierleyi homology analysis.

GO annotation decisions

  • GO:0004075 biotin carboxylase activity (IEA EC) - ACCEPT, core.
  • GO:0005524 ATP binding - KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (substrate binding, part of BC activity).
  • GO:0000166 nucleotide binding - MODIFY to ATP binding.
  • GO:0046872 metal ion binding - KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (ATP-grasp Mg2+; the ARBA Co2+ cofactor line is unsupported).
  • NEW: contributes_to GO:0003989 and GO:0004658; involved_in GO:0015977 carbon fixation.
  • CC: GO has no propionyl-CoA carboxylase complex term, and GO:0009317 acetyl-CoA carboxylase complex
    is defined as catalysing "the first step in long-chain fatty acid biosynthesis", which does not fit an
    archaeal enzyme in a CO2-fixation cycle. No CC term is added, and no complex is set in core_functions
    (the generic GO:1902494 catalytic complex would add little). Raised as a suggested question.
