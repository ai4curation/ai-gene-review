Gene Description

Msed_1456 encodes 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase (EC 6.2.1.36) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. It is an AMP-forming acyl-CoA synthetase of the ANL (adenylate-forming) superfamily that works as a homotetramer of about 78 kDa subunits. It activates 3-hydroxypropionate to 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA with ATP and CoA, releasing AMP and pyrophosphate. This is the first step of the reductive conversion of 3-hydroxypropionate to propionyl-CoA in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle of the Sulfolobales. The enzyme has high affinity for 3-hydroxypropionate (Km 180 uM) and ATP (Km 45 uM), and uses propionate with similar catalytic efficiency. Acrylate is used about 10-fold less efficiently, and acetate and butyrate are poor substrates. Its activity is induced about eightfold under autotrophic growth. Although it falls in the acetyl-CoA synthetase sequence family, acetate activation in M. sedula is carried out mainly by a separate paralog. Recombinant M. sedula enzyme is soluble only when co-expressed with the next enzyme of the cycle, 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA dehydratase (Msed_2001), with which it interacts in yeast two-hybrid assays.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal 3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate, so its enzymes, including this 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase, have no specific process term. The same term is proposed in the Msed_0709 review.

Parent term: carbon fixation

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should GO:0043427 be restricted explicitly to the Chloroflexus bicycle (or made a grouping term) and a sibling term created for the 3HP/4HB cycle? Existing GO:0043427 rows on Sulfolobales enzymes would then need to be moved.

Q: Does free propionate activation by Msed_1456 have a physiological role, for example in scavenging propionate during heterotrophic or mixotrophic growth?

Q: Should the acetyl-CoA ligase family signature IPR011904 / TIGR02188 continue to map to acetyl-CoA synthetase activity and acetyl-CoA biosynthesis for crenarchaeal members whose substrate has diverged, such as 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Co-purify native or co-expressed HPCS and HPCD and test complex formation by size-exclusion chromatography with multi-angle light scattering or cross-linking mass spectrometry. Compare coupled-assay kinetics with free enzymes and with added exogenous 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA to look for channelling.

Hypothesis: Msed_1456 and 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA dehydratase (Msed_2001) form a stable complex that channels 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA.

Experiment: Delete or knock down Msed_1456 with a Sulfolobales genetic system and compare growth on H2/CO2 with growth on yeast extract or peptides, with complementation.

Hypothesis: Msed_1456 is required for autotrophic but not heterotrophic growth of M. sedula.

Identity

  • Msed_1456 is 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase (HPCS), EC 6.2.1.36, 661 aa,
    an AMP-forming acyl-CoA synthetase (ANL superfamily; UniProt "Belongs to the
    ATP-dependent AMP-binding enzyme family"). InterPro places it in the
    acetyl-CoA ligase family (IPR011904, TIGR02188 Ac_CoA_lig_AcsA) and PANTHER
    PTHR24095 (acetyl-coenzyme A synthetase), which is why acetyl-CoA synthetase
    terms are propagated to it.
  • Identified by purification of the native enzyme from autotrophically grown
    M. sedula and an internal peptide PMID:18165310.

Activity and substrate specificity

  • Reaction PMID:18165310.
  • Kinetics PMID:18165310.
  • Specificity: 3HP and propionate are equivalent; acrylate ~10-fold lower
    kcat/Km; acetate and butyrate only weakly used, attributed to low affinity
    PMID:18165310
    PMID:18165310.
  • Independent comparison by Hawkins et al. PMID:23258541.
  • The M. sedula acetate-activating enzyme is a different paralog, Msed_1353
    PMID:23258541.
    This, plus the weak acetate affinity of Msed_1456, makes the acetyl-CoA
    synthetase activity and acetyl-CoA biosynthesis IEA rows family-level
    over-annotation (ARCHAEA pattern 1-like: a substrate-diverged ANL member
    inheriting the family-founder name).
  • Homotetramer of 78 kDa subunits PMID:18165310.
  • Induced under autotrophy PMID:18165310.

Pathway

  • Activates 3-hydroxypropionate, the first committed step of the reductive
    conversion 3HP -> 3HP-CoA -> acryloyl-CoA -> propionyl-CoA, in the first half of
    the 3HP/4HB cycle. Acetyl-CoA is regenerated in the second half via
    4-hydroxybutyrate PMID:18079405.
    Msed_1456 itself does not produce acetyl-CoA.
  • GO:0043427 "carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle" (OLS, checked
    2026-10-31): "An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway by which two
    molecules of carbon dioxide are fixed to form glyoxylate ... Malyl-CoA is
    thought to be cleaved to acetyl-CoA, the starting molecule, and glyoxylate".
    This is the Chloroflexus pathway; M. sedula lacks malyl-CoA lyase
    PMID:18165310.
    No GO term for the 3HP/4HB cycle exists (OLS search). Following the
    Msed_0709 review, the IDA/IEA GO:0043427 rows are MODIFIED to the NTR
    "carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle" plus
    GO:0015977 carbon fixation. The activity evidence of the IDA is sound.
  • Recombinant M. sedula HPCS is insoluble alone in E. coli; it is
    soluble only when co-expressed with 3HP-CoA dehydratase Msed_2001, and the
    two interact in yeast two-hybrid
    PMID:27771364
    PMID:27771364.
    Possible substrate channelling; not annotated (Y2H only).

Other

  • PMID:27722922 and PMID:27300329 (engineering of part of the cycle in
    E. coli / in vitro) were cached but are not used; 27722922 is abstract-only.

proposed_new_terms:
- proposed_name: carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle
  proposed_definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA
    is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are
    reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate.
    4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two
    molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in
    ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.
  justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as
    the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal
    3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate,
    so its enzymes, including this 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase, have no specific
    process term. The same term is proposed in the Msed_0709 review.
