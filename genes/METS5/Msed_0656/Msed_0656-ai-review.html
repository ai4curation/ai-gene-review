Gene Description

Msed_0656 encodes an acetoacetyl-CoA beta-ketothiolase (acetyl-CoA C-acetyltransferase, EC 2.3.1.9) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. It is a 395-residue member of the thiolase family with the conserved thiolase N- and C-terminal domains. In the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle of the Sulfolobales, it catalyses the final step, the CoA-dependent thiolytic cleavage of acetoacetyl-CoA into two acetyl-CoA. One acetyl-CoA re-enters the cycle as the CO2 acceptor and the other is released for biosynthesis. The gene was first assigned by its similarity to bacterial thiolases and by its induction under autotrophic growth. The recombinant enzyme cleaves acetoacetyl-CoA with a Km of 0.18 mM and a high specific activity (about 140 U/mg at 70 C), making it the fastest enzyme of the acetyl-CoA-regenerating branch, and it supports in vitro reconstitution of the conversion of 4-hydroxybutyrate to acetyl-CoA. M. sedula has several other thiolase paralogs, at least one of which is also induced in autotrophy and may contribute to the reaction.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. Msed_0656 catalyses the acetyl-CoA-regenerating final step of the archaeal cycle, which has no counterpart in that term. This proposal repeats the one in the Msed_0709 review.

Core Functions

CoA-dependent thiolytic cleavage of acetoacetyl-CoA into two acetyl-CoA (EC 2.3.1.9), the final step of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle. It regenerates the acetyl-CoA acceptor and releases one acetyl-CoA as the net product of each turn.

Q: Does Msed_0656 also supply acetoacetyl-CoA for the archaeal mevalonate pathway, as the ARBA "Isoprene biosynthesis" keyword implies, or does a different paralog do so?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Quantify the thiolase paralogs by proteomics in autotrophic cells. Assay recombinant Msed_0386 and Msed_0396 kinetically, and if genetics allow, delete Msed_0656 in a tractable Sulfolobales host and measure acetoacetyl-CoA thiolase activity and autotrophic growth.

Hypothesis: Msed_0656 is the main acetoacetyl-CoA thiolase of the autotrophic cycle.

Msed_0656 (A4YEH9) notes

Automated deep research was unavailable for this review. These notes come from my own
reading of the cached full texts (PMID:24532060, PMID:21169482, PMID:32218776), the
UniProt record and the GO term definitions in OLS. No -deep-research-*.md file was
created.

Identity

  • 395 aa, unreviewed TrEMBL entry, named "Acetyl-CoA acetyltransferase" (EC 2.3.1.9).
  • Thiolase family (Pfam Thiolase_N PF00108 and Thiolase_C PF02803, PROSITE PS00737
    THIOLASE_2, NCBIfam TIGR01930 AcCoA-C-Actrans, CDD cd00751). PANTHER PTHR18919:SF107.
  • UniProt keywords from ARBA: Acyltransferase, Isoprene biosynthesis. UniProt also
    carries an ARBA lipid metabolic process (GO:0006629) line that is not in the GOA file.
  • M. sedula has several thiolase homologs (Msed_1647, Msed_1290, Msed_0396, Msed_0386,
    Msed_0271, Msed_0270) [PMID:32218776 table 1]. Msed_0386 and Msed_0396 sit in a
    crotonase/dehydrogenase gene cluster PMID:32218776.

Identification (Ramos-Vera et al. 2011, PMID:21169482)

  • Msed_0656 was assigned by similarity to the bacterial enzyme and by co-regulation, and
    the experiment was done on the Thermoproteus ortholog PMID:21169482. PMID:21169482 That sentence refers to Tneu_0249.
  • PMID:21169482

Biochemistry (Hawkins et al. 2014, PMID:24532060)

  • Recombinant His-tagged Msed_0656 was produced aerobically in E. coli and assayed by the
    substrate-dependent loss of CoA (DTNB) at 70 C PMID:24532060
  • Km for acetoacetyl-CoA PMID:24532060. It is
    the fastest enzyme of the branch PMID:24532060.
  • It is one of the four enzymes used to reconstitute 4HB to acetyl-CoA in vitro
    PMID:24532060.
  • Liu et al. 2020 call it the characterized beta-ketothiolase, and note that a paralog
    (Msed_0386) may also contribute PMID:32218776.

Assessment

  • GO:0003985 acetyl-CoA C-acetyltransferase activity (2 acetyl-CoA = CoA +
    acetoacetyl-CoA; OLS-verified) is the core MF. It is supported experimentally for
    Msed_0656 itself (PMID:24532060), and the IEA row is accepted. The direct evidence is
    for the thiolytic direction, which is the physiological direction in the cycle.
  • GO:0016746 and GO:0016747 are ancestors of GO:0003985 (OLS). They are correct but
    general, so both rows are MODIFIED to GO:0003985.
  • Process: NEW GO:0015977 carbon fixation. It catalyses the last step of the cycle,
    which regenerates the acetyl-CoA acceptor.
  • Open question: archaea make isoprenoids by the mevalonate pathway, which starts from
    acetoacetyl-CoA. Msed_0656, or a paralog, may also supply acetoacetyl-CoA for
    isoprenoid synthesis, as the ARBA keyword hints, but no evidence for this was found.
