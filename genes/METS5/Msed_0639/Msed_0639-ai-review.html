Gene Description

Msed_0639 encodes the methylmalonyl-CoA epimerase (MCE) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. It is a 140-residue protein of the vicinal oxygen chelate (VOC, glyoxalase-like) fold. The recombinant enzyme is a homodimer that is activated by the divalent cations Ni2+, Co2+ and Mg2+, and has a high specific activity (218 umol/min/mg at 75 C). It interconverts (S)- and (R)-methylmalonyl-CoA. In the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle it converts the (S)-methylmalonyl-CoA formed by propionyl-CoA carboxylation into the (R)-epimer, which the adjacent-gene product, methylmalonyl-CoA mutase (Msed_0638 with Msed_2055), then rearranges to succinyl-CoA. Its transcription is induced under autotrophic growth. Single copies of the gene are conserved across Sulfolobaceae genomes.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal 3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half, which includes the methylmalonyl-CoA epimerase step, and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate. Its enzymes therefore have no specific process term. The same term is proposed in the Msed_0709 review.

Parent term: carbon fixation

Conclusions

  • Msed_0639 is the recombinantly verified homodimeric, divalent-cation-activated
    methylmalonyl-CoA epimerase of the 3HP/4HB cycle, converting the (S)-methylmalonyl-CoA
    made by propionyl-CoA carboxylase to (R)-methylmalonyl-CoA for MCM; transcription is
    induced under autotrophy. No genetic (knockout) evidence exists.
  • GO:0004493 accepted; GO:0046491 L-methylmalonyl-CoA metabolic process kept as non-core (the
    enzyme interconverts the two methylmalonyl-CoA epimers, so L-methylmalonyl-CoA is its
    substrate or product in either direction; note the GO definition itself mixes 'L-' and
    'S-', whereas L-methylmalonyl-CoA is conventionally the (R)-epimer used by MCM); GO:0015977 carbon fixation NEW.
  • Metal activation (Ni, Co, Mg) is consistent with the VOC fold but the bound metal in
    vivo is not known; no metal-binding term is proposed.
