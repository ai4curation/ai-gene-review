Gene Description

Msed_1375 encodes the carboxyltransferase (gamma) subunit of the acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. This biotin-dependent enzyme catalyses both carboxylations of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle: acetyl-CoA to malonyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA to (S)-methylmalonyl-CoA, at similar rates. The purified holoenzyme is a complex of about 560 kDa, probably (alpha beta gamma)4. It is made of the biotin carboxylase (Msed_0147), the biotin carboxyl carrier protein (Msed_0148) and this 57 kDa carboxyltransferase, which is encoded at a separate locus. The protein is a single-chain carboxyltransferase of the propionyl-CoA carboxylase beta type, with N- and C-terminal CoA carboxyltransferase domains on a crotonase fold. In the complex it performs the second half-reaction: it transfers the carboxyl group from carboxybiotin on the carrier subunit to the acyl-CoA. Sequence analysis of the Acidianus brierleyi ortholog places the acyl-CoA and carboxybiotin binding sites in this subunit, so it is the subunit that sets the dual acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA specificity. The gene is strongly induced under autotrophic growth.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: GO has no term for the acyl-CoA carboxytransferase half-reaction of biotin-dependent carboxylases (carboxybiotin-[carrier protein] + acetyl-CoA or propionyl-CoA = biotin-[carrier protein] + malonyl-CoA or methylmalonyl-CoA; EC 2.1.3.15 for acetyl-CoA). Should one be created as a child of GO:0016743, by analogy with GO:0004075 for the biotin carboxylase half-reaction?

Q: The PANTHER PCC beta-chain node (PTN000523576) propagates propionyl-CoA carboxylase activity with enables to carboxyltransferase subunits. Should TreeGrafter and PAINT annotations of CT subunits use contributes_to? And should the archaeal bifunctional clade also carry acetyl-CoA carboxylase activity?

Q: Which residues of the gamma subunit allow it to accept acetyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA equally, when bacterial AccD/AccA and mitochondrial PCCB are substrate-selective?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express soluble Msed_1375 (for example in P. furiosus or Sulfolobus acidocaldarius) and assay the reverse half-reaction, malonyl-CoA or methylmalonyl-CoA + free biotin to acyl-CoA + carboxybiotin, with kinetic constants for both acyl-CoAs.

Hypothesis: The gamma subunit alone catalyses carboxyl transfer between a carboxylated acyl-CoA and biotin, and its own substrate preference matches that of the holoenzyme.

Identity

  • UniProt A4YGI1 (TrEMBL), 524 aa, locus Msed_1375. Name "Acetyl-CoA carboxylase carboxyltransferase
    subunit alpha / ... beta / propionyl-CoA carboxylase carboxyltransferase subunit"; keyword Transferase.
  • CoA carboxyltransferase N-terminal (PS50980, 10-267) and C-terminal (PS50989, 271-506) domains; Pfam
    Carboxyl_trans (PF01039); crotonase-fold; PANTHER PTHR43842:SF2 (propionyl-CoA carboxylase beta chain).
    This is a single-chain fusion of the AccD-like and AccA-like CT halves, as in PCC beta subunits.
    [file:METS5/Msed_1375/Msed_1375-uniprot.txt]
  • Encoded away from the Msed_0147-Msed_0148 pair.

The enzyme

  • Gamma subunit (57 kDa) = carboxytransferase of the purified M. sedula acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA
    carboxylase, which acts equally on both acyl-CoAs PMID:12581213.
  • A. brierleyi ortholog: acyl-CoA and carboxybiotin binding sites in the CT subunit PMID:12533469.
    So the CT subunit determines the dual acetyl-/propionyl-CoA substrate range.
  • Induced autotrophically PMID:24532060.

  • Part of E1 expressed in P. furiosus PMID:23530213.

  • Recombinant gamma made in E. coli was insoluble and inactive, and an earlier attempt to add
    E. coli-made gamma to the native enzyme changed nothing PMID:27315782.
    Active recombinant ACC (His-tag on the gamma N-terminus) was purified from P. furiosus co-expressing the
    M. sedula biotin protein ligase PMID:27315782.

Subunit-level function and GO

The CT subunit catalyses the second half-reaction: transfer of the carboxyl group from carboxybiotin-BCCP
to acetyl-CoA (EC 2.1.3.15 in the bacterial ACC nomenclature) or propionyl-CoA. GO has no specific term
for this acyl-CoA carboxytransferase half-reaction; the nearest correct parent is GO:0016743 carboxyl- or
carbamoyltransferase activity. (GO:0047154 methylmalonyl-CoA carboxytransferase activity is the
transcarboxylase reaction with pyruvate, a different reaction.)

GOA rows:
- GO:0004658 propionyl-CoA carboxylase activity (TreeGrafter, enables): the activity is real but belongs
to the alpha-beta-gamma complex; the subunit contributes. MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. A NEW contributes_to
row was added for GO:0003989. contributes_to GO:0004658 is recorded only in core_functions, because the
validator treats a NEW row on a term already in GOA as a duplicate, whatever its qualifier. The TreeGrafter node also misses the acetyl-CoA activity, which in
this archaeal enzyme is equal to the propionyl-CoA activity.
- GO:0016874 ligase activity (InterPro2GO from IPR011763): the subunit's own chemistry is a carboxyl
transfer, not ATP-coupled ligation; ATP is consumed by the BC subunit. MODIFY to GO:0016743.
- NEW involved_in GO:0015977 carbon fixation.

Open points

  • NCBI esummary was down on 2026-11-01. The uncached PMIDs (12581213, 9973333, 12533469, 27315782)
    were cached with the repo's normal fetch pipeline, using metadata taken from efetch XML.
  • I found no structure of an archaeal CT of this enzyme; the basis for the equal acetyl-/propionyl-CoA specificity is unknown.
