Gene Description

Msed_1993 encodes the NADPH-dependent malonic semialdehyde reductase of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. It reduces malonate semialdehyde (3-oxopropanoate) to 3-hydroxypropionate, the second reductive step of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle of the Sulfolobales, immediately after malonyl-CoA reduction by the malonyl-CoA/succinyl-CoA reductase Msed_0709. The enzyme was partially purified from autotrophically grown cells, identified by peptide mass fingerprinting, and characterized as a recombinant protein. It is a homotrimeric, metal-independent member of the 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase family with an N-terminal Rossmann-fold dinucleotide-binding domain. It prefers NADPH (NADH supports about 20% of the activity) and is specific for malonate semialdehyde; succinate semialdehyde and simple aliphatic aldehydes are not reduced. Its activity is about five-fold higher in autotrophically than in heterotrophically grown cells. Expressed together with the cycle's carboxylase and malonyl-CoA reductase, it allowed engineered Pyrococcus furiosus to make 3-hydroxypropionate from CO2.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal cycle shares only the first half with it, and its malonic semialdehyde reductase (Msed_1993) is unrelated to the Chloroflexus enzyme. The same term is proposed in the Msed_0709 review.

GO term choices

  • GO:0035527 3-hydroxypropionate dehydrogenase (NADP+) activity — OLS definition:
    "3-hydroxypropanoate + NADP+ = 3-oxopropanoate + H+ + NADPH". 3-Oxopropanoate is malonate
    semialdehyde, so this is exactly the reaction (written in the oxidative direction, as GO
    does for reversible dehydrogenases). Ancestors: GO:0016616 -> GO:0016614 -> GO:0016491.
    So the module's MF term fits. The NAD+ sibling GO:0047565 describes the minor (20%) NADH
    activity and is not needed.
  • GO:0050104 L-gulonate 3-dehydrogenase activity (TreeGrafter, PTN000592773): this is the
    lambda-crystallin/CRYL1 activity of the PANTHER subfamily name. No one has tested
    L-gulonate with Msed_1993; the protein's substrate is a C3 oxo acid, not a hexonate, and
    there is no glucuronate/uronate pathway context in this archaeon. Over-propagation from the
    family -> REMOVE.
  • GO:0006631 fatty acid metabolic process (InterPro2GO from the 3HCDH signatures): the family
    mapping assumes 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase in beta-oxidation. Msed_1993 acts on a free
    aldehyde, not an acyl-CoA, in CO2 fixation. Marked as over-annotation (same treatment as
    fatty acid beta-oxidation on the sibling Msed_2001).
  • GO:0070403 NAD+ binding (IPR006176): the Rossmann domain binds the nicotinamide cofactor,
    but the physiological cofactor is NADPH; MODIFY to GO:0050661 NADP binding.
  • GO:0016491 / GO:0016616: correct but general -> MODIFY to GO:0035527.
  • NEW GO:0015977 carbon fixation (IDA, PMID:19684143), following the sibling reviews; GO:0043427
    (Chloroflexus cycle) not used. The proposed "carbon fixation by
    3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle" term is reused verbatim from Msed_0709.

Open questions

  • Does Msed_1993 have any L-gulonate 3-dehydrogenase or 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase
    activity (never tested)?
  • Structural basis for the switch from acyl-CoA to free semialdehyde substrate in this
    3HCDH-family member.
