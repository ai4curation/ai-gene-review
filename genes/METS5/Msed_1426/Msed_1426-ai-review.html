Gene Description

Msed_1426 encodes the NADPH-dependent acryloyl-CoA reductase (EC 1.3.1.84) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. It reduces acryloyl-CoA to propionyl-CoA, the last of three reactions that convert 3-hydroxypropionate to propionyl-CoA in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle of the Sulfolobales; the two preceding reactions are catalysed by 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA synthetase (Msed_1456) and 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA dehydratase (Msed_2001). The enzyme was enriched from autotrophically grown cells and identified by peptide mass fingerprinting; its activity is more than ten-fold higher in autotrophic than in heterotrophic cells and appears to limit the rate of 3-hydroxypropionate reduction. It is a soluble member of the medium-chain zinc-containing alcohol dehydrogenase family. The recombinant Sulfolobus tokodaii ortholog is a monomer that contains about one zinc per subunit, uses NADPH but not NADH, and does not reduce crotonyl-CoA, an intermediate of the second half of the cycle. Recombinant M. sedula enzyme, obtained by co-expression with the neighbouring succinic semialdehyde reductase Msed_1424, has comparable kinetics. The archaeal enzyme is unrelated to the reductase domain of the trifunctional propionyl-CoA synthase of Chloroflexus, indicating independent evolution of the two 3-hydroxypropionate-based cycles.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal cycle shares only the first half with it, and even there uses separate, unrelated enzymes (such as Msed_1426) in place of the Chloroflexus trifunctional propionyl-CoA synthase. The same term is proposed in the Msed_0709 review.

Parent term: carbon fixation

Core Functions

NADPH-dependent reduction of acryloyl-CoA to propionyl-CoA in the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle, completing the conversion of 3-hydroxypropionate to propionyl-CoA. A soluble zinc-containing medium-chain dehydrogenase family enzyme that does not use NADH and does not reduce crotonyl-CoA.
