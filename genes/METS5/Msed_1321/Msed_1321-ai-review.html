Gene Description

Msed_1321 encodes the 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase (EC 4.2.1.120) of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. This is the key enzyme of the 4-hydroxybutyrate part of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate autotrophic CO2-fixation cycle of the Sulfolobales. It dehydrates 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA to crotonyl-CoA, which is then hydrated, oxidised and cleaved into two acetyl-CoA. The 507-residue protein belongs to the HpaB/PvcC/4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase family, which has an acyl-CoA dehydrogenase-like fold. By homology with the clostridial enzyme, it is expected to carry FAD and a [4Fe-4S] cluster and to use radical chemistry. The recombinant enzyme, produced anaerobically in Escherichia coli, is a homotetramer with a Km of 0.15 mM for 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA and a low specific activity (about 2.2 U/mg at 70 C). Together with 4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase, it is rate-limiting for the acetyl-CoA-regenerating branch. Unlike the clostridial enzyme, it is relatively oxygen tolerant, with a half-life of about 4 days in air. Its transcript is strongly induced (about 27-fold) under autotrophic growth. The submitter's name, vinylacetyl-CoA Delta-isomerase, refers to a partial reaction documented for the clostridial homolog but not tested for this protein.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage, and it has no 4-hydroxybutyrate branch. Msed_1321 catalyses the 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase step that defines the archaeal cycle. This proposal repeats the one in the Msed_0709 review.

Summary: InterPro2GO mapping from the acyl-CoA dehydrogenase/oxidase-like superfamily signatures (IPR009100 N-terminal and middle domain, IPR036250 C-terminal domain, IPR046373 middle domain) to oxidoreductase activity acting on CH-CH donors.
Reason: These are fold-level superfamily signatures that are shared by acyl-CoA dehydrogenases, acyl-CoA oxidases, HpaB-type flavin monooxygenases and the 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratases. The family-specific InterPro entries (IPR004925, IPR024719, IPR024674 HpaB/PvcC/4-BUDH) carry no such mapping. The net reaction catalysed by Msed_1321 is the dehydration of 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA to crotonyl-CoA (PMID:24532060). This is a hydro-lyase reaction (GO:0043721, under GO:0016836) with no net electron transfer, so it is not a CH-CH oxidoreduction. In the clostridial paradigm, FAD only transiently oxidises the substrate within a radical mechanism and is regenerated each turnover. No CH-CH dehydrogenase activity has been reported for Msed_1321 or for its characterised homologs. This is a superfamily-fold over-propagation that contradicts the known reaction class.

Core Functions

Dehydration of 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA to crotonyl-CoA (EC 4.2.1.120), the key and rate-limiting step of the acetyl-CoA-regenerating branch of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle. It lies between 4-hydroxybutyrate-CoA ligase (Msed_0406) and crotonyl-CoA hydration.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does Msed_1321 have vinylacetyl-CoA Delta-isomerase activity, as the clostridial AbfD does? Should UniProt rename the entry 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase (EC 4.2.1.120), with EC 5.3.3.3 kept only as a secondary activity?

Q: Does the M. sedula enzyme contain FAD and a [4Fe-4S] cluster like AbfD, and what structural features account for its much greater oxygen tolerance?

Q: Should InterPro2GO stop mapping the acyl-CoA dehydrogenase-like fold superfamilies (IPR009100, IPR036250, IPR046373) to GO:0016627, given that they also cover the 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase and HpaB monooxygenase families, which are not CH-CH oxidoreductases?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Assay purified recombinant Msed_1321 with vinylacetyl-CoA, and follow crotonyl-CoA formation by HPLC. Determine flavin and iron/acid-labile sulfide content, and record EPR and UV-visible spectra.

Hypothesis: Msed_1321 is bifunctional, with vinylacetyl-CoA Delta-isomerase activity as well as 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase activity, and carries FAD and a [4Fe-4S] cluster.

Experiment: Delete or knock down Msed_1321 in M. sedula, or its ortholog in a genetically tractable autotrophic Sulfolobales relative. Compare autotrophic and heterotrophic growth and measure 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase activity in extracts.

Hypothesis: Msed_1321 is essential for autotrophic growth.

Msed_1321 (A4YGC7) notes

Automated deep research was unavailable for this review. These notes come from my own
reading of the cached full texts (PMID:24532060, PMID:21169482, PMID:32218776), the
UniProt record, the GO term definitions in OLS, and web searches on the clostridial
4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase (AbfD). No -deep-research-*.md file was created.

Identity

  • 507 aa, unreviewed TrEMBL entry. The submitter named it "Vinylacetyl-CoA
    Delta-isomerase" (EC 5.3.3.3). BRENDA cross-references it under EC 4.2.1.120
    (4-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA dehydratase), and MetaCyc has MONOMER-13736.
  • Domains: Pfam HpaB_N (PF11794) and HpaB (PF03241), InterPro IPR004925
    HpaB/PvcC/4-BUDH. This is the 4-hydroxyphenylacetate 3-monooxygenase /
    4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase family, which has the acyl-CoA dehydrogenase-like
    fold. PANTHER PTHR36117:SF3.
  • UniProt keywords FAD and Flavoprotein (ARBA). No cofactor line.
  • Sequence observation from the UniProt file: a CxxxC motif (Cys101, Cys105) and
    His293/Cys299. This fits the kind of cysteine/histidine ligand set expected for the
    [4Fe-4S] cluster of the clostridial enzyme. I did not align it to AbfD here, so treat
    this as unverified.
  • Paralog Msed_1220 lacks catalytic residues PMID:21169482.

Biochemistry (Hawkins et al. 2014, PMID:24532060)

  • Recombinant Msed_1321 was produced anaerobically in E. coli and was active
    PMID:24532060
  • Homotetramer PMID:24532060.
  • Much less oxygen-sensitive than the clostridial enzyme PMID:24532060.
  • Activity assay: a coupled assay. Msed_0406 makes 4HB-CoA, and Msed_0399 (crotonyl-CoA
    hydratase/(S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase) turns the crotonyl-CoA product
    over with NAD+ PMID:24532060. The readout therefore depends on crotonyl-CoA
    being formed from 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA, which is the GO:0043721 reaction.
  • Kinetics: Km 0.15 mM for 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA PMID:24532060, and the rate is low PMID:24532060.
  • The four recombinant enzymes (Msed_0406, Msed_1321, Msed_0399, Msed_0656) together
    convert 4HB to acetyl-CoA in vitro PMID:24532060.
  • Strongly induced in autotrophy PMID:24532060.
  • The vinylacetyl-CoA isomerase activity was NOT assayed for Msed_1321 in the cached
    paper.

Context (Ramos-Vera et al. 2011, PMID:21169482; Liu et al. 2020, PMID:32218776)

  • The dehydratase is the key, well-conserved enzyme of the 4HB part of both crenarchaeal
    cycles PMID:21169482
  • Msed_1321 was named as the gene behind the high dehydratase activity of autotrophic
    extracts PMID:21169482.
  • The protein is abundant and slow PMID:32218776.

Clostridial paradigm (AbfD)

  • Scherf & Buckel 1993, Eur J Biochem 215:421-429. The IUBMB EC 4.2.1.120 entry lists it
    as PMID 8344309. It describes the C. aminobutyricum enzyme as an iron-sulfur and
    FAD-containing 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase/vinylacetyl-CoA delta3-delta2-isomerase.
    NCBI E-utilities were down during this session ("address table is empty"), so I could
    not cache this PMID. It is not cited as a reference in the review.
  • Rhea and IUBMB say the clostridial enzyme has an intrinsic vinylacetyl-CoA isomerase
    activity, and that its mechanism probably runs through vinylacetyl-CoA or a shared
    dienoxy intermediate. This comes from web search (IUBMB EC 4.2.1.120, Rhea), not from
    a cached paper.

Assessment

  • GO:0043721 (4-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA = (2E)-butenoyl-CoA + H2O; OLS-verified, under
    hydro-lyase activity) is the core MF. It is supported by experiment on recombinant
    enzyme (PMID:24532060) and missing from GOA, so it is added as NEW (IDA).
  • GO:0050393 vinylacetyl-CoA delta-isomerase: this IEA comes from the submitter's EC
    5.3.3.3 name. The activity is plausible because the clostridial homolog has it, but
    it has not been tested for Msed_1321 and it is not the physiological step of the cycle.
    The row is kept as non-core.
  • GO:0016627 oxidoreductase acting on CH-CH: an InterPro mapping from the acyl-CoA
    dehydrogenase-like fold superfamilies. The net reaction is a dehydration (lyase), not
    a redox reaction. FAD acts only transiently in the radical mechanism. The row is
    removed.
  • Process: NEW GO:0015977 carbon fixation, with the 3HP/4HB cycle proposed term reused
    from Msed_0709.

  proposed_definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA
