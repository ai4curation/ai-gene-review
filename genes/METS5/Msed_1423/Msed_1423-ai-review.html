Gene Description

Msed_1423 encodes a 391-residue monofunctional (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase of the thermoacidophilic crenarchaeon Metallosphaera sedula. It has an N-terminal Rossmann-fold NAD-binding domain followed by two 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase C-terminal domains, the architecture of the bacterial Hbd/FadB'-type short-chain 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenases, and it lacks the enoyl-CoA hydratase domain of its bifunctional paralog Msed_0399. The purified recombinant enzyme oxidises (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA to acetoacetyl-CoA with NAD+. It has a very low Km for (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA (about 5 uM) and for NAD+ (about 30 uM). NADP+ is accepted only with a Km of about 5 mM, so the enzyme is effectively NAD+-specific, and it is inactive with the C8 substrate (S)-3-hydroxyoctanoyl-CoA. Its catalytic efficiency is about 20-fold higher than that of Msed_0399, its gene is up-regulated under autotrophic and mixotrophic growth, and it lies in a gene cluster with other enzymes of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle (succinic semialdehyde reductase Msed_1424 and acryloyl-CoA reductase Msed_1426). Homologs are present in almost all Sulfolobales and are missing only from the heterotrophic Sulfolobus acidocaldarius. Msed_1423 is therefore considered the main enzyme for the (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase step in the acetyl-CoA-regenerating half of this cycle. The protein is presumed cytoplasmic.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle

Definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate. 4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.

Justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal 3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half of that route, uses unrelated enzymes for it, and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate, crotonyl-CoA and (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA. Msed_1423 catalyses the (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase step of that regeneration half, which has no counterpart in the Chloroflexus cycle. This proposal repeats the one in the Msed_0709 review.

Parent term: carbon fixation

Core Functions

NAD+-dependent oxidation of (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA to acetoacetyl-CoA, the dehydrogenase step in the acetyl-CoA-regenerating half of the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2-fixation cycle. The enzyme has a very low substrate Km, strongly prefers NAD+ over NADP+, and is inactive with C8 substrates. It is the main enzyme for this step in M. sedula.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Msed_1423 accepts NADP+ with a similar Vmax but a 170-fold higher Km (5.1 mM versus 0.03 mM for NAD+). Is the enzyme strictly NAD+-dependent in vivo, given cellular NAD+/NADP+ ratios in M. sedula, so that GO:0003857 rather than GO:0008691 (NADP+-defined) is the correct activity term for the cycle step?

Q: PANTHER places Msed_1423 in PTHR48075:SF5 (3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase), and InterPro2GO maps its domains to fatty acid metabolic process. Should the IPR006108/IPR006176 to GO:0006631 mapping be restricted, since many archaeal members of this family are short-chain enzymes of autotrophic cycles that do not act on fatty acids longer than C4?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Delete the Msed_1423 ortholog in a genetically tractable autotrophic Sulfolobales species and compare autotrophic and heterotrophic growth, measuring (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase activity in extracts of the mutant.

Hypothesis: Msed_1423 is required for autotrophic growth but not for heterotrophic growth.

Experiment: Assay purified Msed_1423 with (R)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA and with C6 (S)-3-hydroxyhexanoyl-CoA to define its stereospecificity and chain-length limit.

Hypothesis: Msed_1423 is specific for the (S) stereoisomer and for C4 substrates.

proposed_new_terms:
- proposed_name: carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle
  proposed_definition: An autotrophic carbon dioxide fixation pathway in which acetyl-CoA
    is carboxylated twice by acetyl-CoA/propionyl-CoA carboxylase, and the products are
    reductively converted via 3-hydroxypropionate and succinyl-CoA to 4-hydroxybutyrate.
    4-Hydroxybutyrate is then converted via 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydratase into two
    molecules of acetyl-CoA. The pathway occurs in autotrophic Sulfolobales and in
    ammonia-oxidizing Thaumarchaeota.
  justification: GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is defined as
    the Chloroflexus cycle that releases glyoxylate via malyl-CoA cleavage. The archaeal
    3HP/4HB cycle shares only the first half of that route, uses unrelated enzymes for
    it, and regenerates acetyl-CoA via 4-hydroxybutyrate, crotonyl-CoA and
    (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA. Msed_1423 catalyses the (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA
    dehydrogenase step of that regeneration half, which has no counterpart in the
    Chloroflexus cycle. This proposal repeats the one in the Msed_0709 review.
