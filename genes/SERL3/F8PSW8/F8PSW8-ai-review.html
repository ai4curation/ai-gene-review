Gene Description

Uncharacterised metal-dependent enzyme of the phosphoenolpyruvate mutase/isocitrate lyase superfamily (Pfam PF13714) from the brown-rot basidiomycete Serpula lacrymans var. lacrymans (dry rot fungus), encoded by locus SERLA73DRAFT_105238 and the only member of this superfamily in the reference proteome. Members of the superfamily act on alpha-oxycarboxylate substrates, cleaving or forming C-C or P-C bonds in an (alpha/beta)8 barrel whose active site is covered by a mobile gating loop and requires Mg2+ or Mn2+. The protein retains the complete catalytic set of the lyase/hydrolase branch, including the gating-loop lysine and the catalytic cysteine, together with the serine that marks oxaloacetate acetylhydrolase rather than 2,3-dimethylmalate lyase or 2-methylisocitrate lyase. Its closest relative with a known physiological context is the equivalent single-copy protein of another brown-rot fungus, Gloeophyllum trabeum, whose oxaloacetase gene is upregulated at the advancing hyphal front during wood decay. The substrate of this protein has not been determined experimentally, and no basidiomycete enzyme of this branch has been characterised as a purified protein.

Core Functions

Metal-dependent cleavage of an alpha-oxycarboxylate substrate by the lyase/hydrolase branch of the phosphoenolpyruvate mutase/isocitrate lyase superfamily. The reaction is inferred from the complete conserved active site; the substrate is not established, so no more specific molecular function is asserted.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is the single-copy PF13714 protein of brown-rot basidiomycetes the oxaloacetase whose expression tracks the hyphal front in Postia placenta and Gloeophyllum trabeum? This protein is 82.1% identical to the G. trabeum protein, so one characterisation would settle the family.

Q: Does the marker serine retain its predictive value outside the ascomycete and plant proteins in which it was derived and tested? The Penicillium paralog Pc18g05100 carries the serine but conferred no oxalate production on yeast, which is a counterexample within the ascomycetes.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express and purify the protein and measure activity against oxaloacetate, (2R,3S)-dimethylmalate, 2-methylisocitrate and D-threo-isocitrate with Mg2+ and Mn2+. This is the experiment that would replace the removed EC 4.1.3.1 with a real assignment.

Experiment: Delete or silence SERLA73DRAFT_105238 in Serpula lacrymans and measure secreted oxalate during wood or mineral-substrate colonisation; loss of oxalate would identify the gene as the basidiomycete oxaloacetase and would also test the pathway that CMMMech's humboldtine and malachite records assume.

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: Which reaction this enzyme catalyses is unknown. It is the single PEP mutase/isocitrate lyase superfamily protein of Serpula lacrymans and carries the serine that marks oxaloacetate acetylhydrolase, yet it is more similar overall to 2,3-dimethylmalate lyase than to any characterised oxaloacetase, and no basidiomycete member of this branch has been purified and assayed. Only enzymology on the protein can settle it.

F8PSW8 (Serpula lacrymans SERLA73DRAFT_105238) — curation notes

Identity

UniProtKB:F8PSW8, 358 aa, locus SERLA73DRAFT_105238 of Serpula lacrymans
var. lacrymans strain S7.3 (dry rot fungus), NCBITaxon:936435, reference
proteome UP000008063. Pfam PF13714 (PEP_mutase) only; no gene symbol, so the
folder is named by accession. It is the only PF13714 protein in the proteome.

This entry is the S. lacrymans counterpart of C. puteana A0A5M3MM75: the two
are the same single-copy basidiomycete gene, and the reasoning below is the same
analysis applied to this sequence.

The "Isocitrate lyase (EC 4.1.3.1)" name is wrong

The name and EC come from PROSITE-ProRule PRU10119 alone. PS00161
(K-[KR]-C-G-H-[LMQR]) is the superfamily catalytic-cysteine motif and matched
2,3-dimethylmalate lyase, E. coli 2-methylisocitrate lyase and the petal death
protein as well as the characterised oxaloacetases, so it does not discriminate;
the FT ACT_SITE 180 placed by the rule is that cysteine.

  • No ICL domain: genuine ICLs carry PF00463 and are 428-590 aa; this is 358 aa
    with PF13714 only.
  • S. lacrymans' real isocitrate lyases are F8QF23 (547 aa) and F8PZU2 (539 aa).
  • Identity to real ICLs is background-level (28.2% to A. nidulans AcuD, 29.5%
    to M. tuberculosis Icl1) against 44.6-46.9% to characterised OAHs.

Method and residue table: [file:CONPW/A0A5M3MM75/A0A5M3MM75-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md].

What it probably is, and why the review stops short of saying so

The OAH specificity serine (Ser-308 in C. parasitica numbering) is present,
the position where dimethylmalate lyase and methylisocitrate lyase have a proline
PMID:20558740;
swapping it in B. cinerea OAH "reduced its OAH activity 40-fold and increased
the (2 R ,3 S )-dimethylmalate lyase activity 15-fold"
PMID:20558740,
and Joosten et al. proposed it as the superfamily marker for OAH activity
PMID:17654546.
The metal-binding and catalytic residues (Asp-126/155/157, Glu-184, Lys-190,
Cys-192, Glu-259, Asn-282, Tyr-111/His-182) are all conserved, and the family
requires a divalent metal
PMID:20558740.

The physiology fits: oxalate biosynthesis in wood-rotting basidiomycetes runs
through oxaloacetase, which has a "pivotal role in yielding oxalate from
oxaloacetate via the acetate-recycling" routes in Fomitopsis palustris
PMID:11553780,
and in two brown-rot fungi "oxaloacetase expression was up upregulated for both
fungi" near the hyphal front, separately from the "paralogous of isocitrate lyases"
PMID:27543342
PMID:27543342. The nearest relative in the
comparison set is the equivalent G. trabeum protein S7Q6Y6 (82.1% identical).

Against calling it OAH outright: it is more similar to A. niger
2,3-dimethylmalate lyase (51.9%) than to any characterised OAH (44.6-46.9%),
several active-site neighbourhoods differ from the ascomycete OAHs (Thr-113 to
Ser; Glu-259 window VCFIEGVKT against VGLLEGFRS), and no basidiomycete
oxaloacetase has been characterised as a cloned protein. The review therefore
removes the isocitrate lyase claim, keeps the superfamily-level catalytic
activity, and asserts no EC.

  • GO:0004451 isocitrate lyase activity (IEA, EC mapping): REMOVE.
  • GO:0003824 catalytic activity (IEA, InterPro): ACCEPT.

    (resource OTHER).

CMMMech context

CMMMech's humboldtine.yaml and malachite.yaml describe A0A5M3MM75 and F8PSW8
as basidiomycete "oahA homologs" auto-annotated as isocitrate lyase. The
auto-annotation statement is accurate; the oahA identification is a defensible
sequence inference, not an established fact, and the mineralogical evidence cited
there (Vlasov et al. 2023) includes no enzyme assay or gene perturbation that
could test it.
