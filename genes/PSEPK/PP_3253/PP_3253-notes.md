# PP_3253 curation notes

- Q88HV0 is explicitly named a putative type 2 glutamate--cysteine ligase
  [file:PSEPK/PP_3253/PP_3253-uniprot.txt, "RecName: Full=Putative
  glutamate--cysteine ligase 2"].
- The exact record qualifies the activity as weak
  [file:PSEPK/PP_3253/PP_3253-uniprot.txt, "ATP-dependent carboxylate-amine
  ligase which exhibits weak"] and has protein-existence level 3, inferred
  from homology.
- PP_3253 belongs to the type 2/YbdK family, PTHR36510:SF1, whereas canonical
  KT2440 GshA belongs to the unrelated type 1 PTHR38761:SF1 family.
- The exact activity and modified-amino-acid process remain undecided pending
  target-protein or genetic evidence. PP_3253 is tracked as a possible
  alternative for the first biosynthetic reaction, not as a third pathway
  step.
- The corrected OpenScientist gene-level request exhausted the full 7,200-second
  provider timeout and produced no report.
- Purified E. coli YbdK showed glutamate--cysteine ligase activity, but its
  catalytic rate was approximately 500-fold slower than authentic gamma-GCS
  [PMID:15211520 "The catalytic rate was found to be approximately 500-fold
  slower than that reported for authentic gamma-GCS."]. This supports the weak
  family activity while leaving physiological relevance unresolved.
- No core function is asserted for PP_3253 on the current evidence. The
  broad C-N ligase class is retained as non-core, and the substrate-specific
  activity remains undecided.
