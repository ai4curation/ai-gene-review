# treY curation notes

- Q88FN6 is an unreviewed record with a submitted maltooligosyl trehalose
  synthase name [file:PSEPK/treY/treY-uniprot.txt, "SubName:
  Full=Maltooligosyl trehalose synthase"] and EC 5.4.99.15
  [file:PSEPK/treY/treY-uniprot.txt, "EC=5.4.99.15"].
- The TreY-specific InterPro and PANTHER assignments support the exact
  glucosylmutase activity, but the target record contains no biochemical
  experiment. The completed OpenScientist review likewise found no direct
  target-strain experiment
  [file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__trey_trez_trehalose_biosynthesis__ppu00500-deep-research-openscientist.md,
  "No experimental (PE=1) evidence in the target strain"].
- The reusable reaction assignment is independently grounded by the cloned
  *Arthrobacter* sp. Q36 treY/treZ pair
  [PMID:8605217 "encoding maltooligosyltrehalose synthase (TreY) and
  maltooligosyltrehalose trehalohydrolase (TreZ), respectively"] and purified
  Q36 TreY activity
  [PMID:8611744 "catalyzes the conversion of maltooligosaccharide into the
  non-reducing saccharide, maltooligosyl trehalose"].
- Trehalose biosynthesis is core. Alpha-glucan catabolism is retained as
  non-core context because the coupled TreY/TreZ route consumes an alpha-glucan
  terminus, while the generic carbohydrate process is redundant.
- PTHR10357 is a broad alpha-amylase family; PTHR10357:SF216 is the precise
  TreY-related subfamily. No PTN is asserted from the TreeGrafter-only GOA
  evidence.
