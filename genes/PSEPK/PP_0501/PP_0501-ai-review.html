Functional Annotation of PP_0501 (Q88QJ1): A Cytoplasmic NAD-Dependent Nucleotide-Sugar Epimerase/Dehydratase in Pseudomonas putida KT2440

Gene: PP_0501 · UniProt: Q88QJ1 (Q88QJ1_PSEPK) · Organism: Pseudomonas putida strain KT2440 (ATCC 47054 / DSM 6125 / NCIMB 11950) · Protein length: 310 aa (~33.3 kDa) · Protein existence: Predicted (level 4)

Summary

PP_0501 encodes a soluble, cytoplasmic NAD⁺-dependent nucleotide-sugar (NDP-sugar) epimerase/dehydratase belonging to the extended short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase (SDR) superfamily, of which UDP-galactose-4-epimerase (GalE) is the archetype. This assignment rests on an unambiguous constellation of sequence and structural diagnostics: an N-terminal Rossmann dinucleotide-binding fingerprint (GGAGFIG, residues 10–16, matching the TGxxGxxG consensus), the catalytic Tyr146–Lys150 (YxxxK) couple that defines the family's active site, an acidic residue (Asp34) positioned to hydrogen-bond the adenosine ribose (a signature of NAD rather than NADP preference), and a high-confidence AlphaFold model (mean pLDDT = 94.9) that reproduces the characteristic two-lobed GalE fold. The domain architecture is consistent across databases: Pfam PF01370 (Epimerase), InterPro IPR001509 / IPR050177 / IPR036291, Gene3D 3.40.50.720 + 3.90.25.10, SUPFAM SSF51735, eggNOG COG0451, and PANTHER PTHR43245.

The enzyme functions in the cytoplasm as a producer of activated sugar donors that feed the biosynthesis of cell-surface glycans — lipopolysaccharide (LPS) O-antigen and/or exopolysaccharide (EPS). The protein has no signal peptide, lipobox, or transmembrane segment, consistent with a soluble cytosolic role generating NDP-sugars upstream of membrane-associated glycosyltransfer and export. Critically, although sequence-database pipelines (KEGG) best-hit-annotate PP_0501 as UDP-glucose 4-epimerase (EC 5.1.3.2, K01784), the housekeeping Leloir-pathway 4-epimerase role in KT2440 is filled by a distinct paralog, galE (PP_3129), which carries the canonical gene name. PP_0501 is therefore a divergent, accessory paralog whose exact NDP-sugar substrate has not been experimentally established.

The most specific functional signal comes from genomic context. PP_0501 overlaps by 8 bp with, and is co-oriented with, the immediately upstream PP_0500, annotated as dTDP-4-dehydrorhamnose reductase (RmlD; K00067; EC 1.1.1.133) — a committed enzyme of the dTDP-L-rhamnose pathway. This tight translational coupling, together with a STRING functional-partner network dominated by sugar-nucleotide biosynthesis enzymes (galU, udg, galE, rmlC, rfbC/D, algD, and a putative sugar transferase), points to a role in 6-deoxyhexose (rhamnose-type) NDP-sugar metabolism rather than in canonical UDP-Glc/UDP-Gal interconversion. In short: PP_0501 is confidently placed at the mechanistic level (an NAD-dependent transient-oxidation NDP-sugar epimerase/dehydratase of the GalE/SDR family, acting in the cytoplasm to supply glycosyl donors for surface polysaccharides), while its precise substrate remains an open, testable question.

Key Findings

Finding 1 — PP_0501 is a single-domain extended-SDR NAD-dependent nucleotide-sugar epimerase/dehydratase

The 310-residue, ~33.3 kDa soluble protein carries all diagnostic motifs of the extended SDR / UDP-galactose-4-epimerase (GalE) superfamily:

  1. N-terminal Rossmann dinucleotide-binding fingerprint — GGAGFIG at residues 10–16, matching the canonical TGxxGxxG glycine-rich loop that cradles the pyrophosphate of the NAD cofactor. The mature N-terminus (MADAPILITGGAGFIG…) begins directly with the Rossmann βαβ fold.
  2. Conserved catalytic Tyr146–Lys150 couple — the YxxxK motif (YAVDK) that is the signature catalytic dyad of SDR enzymes; the tyrosine acts as the catalytic acid/base and the lysine lowers the tyrosine pKa and binds the nicotinamide ribose.
  3. NAD (not NADP) specificity determinant — an acidic residue (Asp34) positioned to hydrogen-bond the 2′- and 3′-hydroxyls of the adenosine ribose, the classic discriminator that excludes the 2′-phosphate of NADP.

These features are corroborated by a consistent, multi-database domain architecture: Pfam PF01370 (Epimerase); InterPro IPR001509 (NAD-dependent epimerase/dehydratase), IPR050177 (Lipid A modification metabolic enzyme cluster), IPR036291 (NAD(P)-binding Rossmann superfamily); Gene3D 3.40.50.720 + 3.90.25.10 (UDP-galactose 4-epimerase domain architecture); SUPFAM SSF51735; eggNOG COG0451; PANTHER PTHR43245.

The family to which PP_0501 belongs has a conserved structure and mechanism. As Zhang et al. state, "These NDP-sugar 4-epimerases (NS-4Es) play critical roles in diverse carbohydrate metabolic pathways across all domains of life and exhibit conserved structures and mechanism" PMID: 42425474. The archetypal reaction is defined by GalE: "UDP-glucose 4-epimerase (GalE) is a key enzyme in the Leloir pathway that catalyzes the reversible conversion of UDP-glucose to UDP-galactose and plays a central role in galactose metabolism" PMID: 42250718. The mechanism proceeds by transient oxidation of the sugar C4′ hydroxyl by the tightly bound NAD⁺ to a 4-keto intermediate, non-stereospecific reduction from the opposite face, and release of the epimerized (or otherwise modified) product — with the cofactor recycled in situ, so no net redox change occurs.

Finding 2 — PP_0501 is a paralog of the housekeeping galE (PP_3129); its precise NDP-sugar substrate is undefined

The KT2440 genome assigns two genes to KEGG orthology K01784 (UDP-glucose 4-epimerase, EC 5.1.3.2): PP_3129, which carries the explicit gene name galE, and PP_0501. PP_3129 bears the canonical galE name, marking it as the housekeeping Leloir-pathway 4-epimerase, while PP_0501 is a divergent paralog assigned by best-hit similarity only. Because a single organism rarely needs two identical housekeeping GalE enzymes, this gene duplication strongly suggests functional divergence of PP_0501 toward a specialized substrate.

This caution is warranted because the NS-4E family is functionally promiscuous across evolution. The review documents that "a plethora of homologous 4-epimerases has been reported with activities on a variety of other nucleotide sugars, including UDP-D-N-acetyl-galacto/glucosamine, UDP-xylose/L-arabinose, UDP-galacturonic/glucuronic acid, dTDP-L-rhamnose/6-deoxy-L-talose and/or GDP-hexoses" PMID: 42425474. The same review expanded the recognized specificity classes from three to seven, underscoring that a best-hit KEGG label of "UDP-glucose 4-epimerase" is an unreliable predictor of the true substrate of a divergent paralog. Consistent with this, UniProt classifies Q88QJ1 as "Predicted" with no experimental EC number.

Finding 3 — Genomic context couples PP_0501 to dTDP-6-deoxysugar (rhamnose-type) biosynthesis

The strongest substrate-level clue is positional. PP_0501 (coordinates 590,754–591,686, + strand) overlaps by 8 bp with the immediately upstream PP_0500 (589,877–590,761, + strand). PP_0500 is annotated by KEGG as dTDP-4-dehydrorhamnose reductase (RmlD; K00067; EC 1.1.1.133) — the committed, final enzyme of the dTDP-L-rhamnose biosynthetic pathway. An 8-bp overlap with shared orientation is a hallmark of translational coupling within a single operon, implying the two gene products act in the same pathway and are co-regulated.

Importantly, KT2440 already possesses a complete canonical rhamnose pathway elsewhere in the genome (rmlC at PP_0265/PP_1782; dTDP-glucose 4,6-dehydratase rmlB at PP_1785; rmlD at PP_1784). PP_0500–PP_0501 therefore constitute a second, accessory deoxysugar-modifying unit, likely dedicated to a specific surface-glycan structure rather than to the core rhamnose supply. dTDP- and UDP-6-deoxysugars (rhamnose, fucose, and their derivatives) are classic building blocks of bacterial O-antigen/LPS and exopolysaccharides. As one study frames the pathway class, the work "aimed to better understand the biosynthesis of the 6-deoxy sugars, fucose and rhamnose" PMID: 40511930. This places PP_0501 most plausibly as an epimerase/dehydratase acting on a dTDP- or UDP-linked (possibly 6-deoxy) hexose destined for a surface glycan.

Finding 4 — PP_0501 functions in the cytoplasm as a soluble metabolic enzyme

The 310-aa sequence has no N-terminal Sec/Tat signal peptide, no lipobox, and no hydrophobic transmembrane segment; the N-terminus (MADAPILITGGAGFIG…) begins directly with the Rossmann βαβ fold. NDP-hexose epimerases/dehydratases of the GalE/SDR family are canonically soluble cytosolic enzymes that generate activated (nucleotide-linked) sugar donors upstream of membrane-associated glycosyltransfer and export steps. eggNOG COG0451 members (nucleotide-sugar epimerase/dehydratase) are cytoplasmic. The subcellular location of PP_0501's catalytic activity is therefore the cytoplasm, where it contributes to the pool of activated sugar precursors; the polysaccharide products it ultimately supplies are assembled at the inner membrane and displayed on the cell surface.

Finding 5 — PP_0501 sits within the activated-sugar biosynthesis network feeding cell-surface glycans; AlphaFold confirms a high-confidence Rossmann fold

A STRING (v12, P. putida KT2440) analysis places PP_0501 firmly inside the sugar-nucleotide biosynthesis network. Its high-confidence functional partners are:

Mechanistic Model / Interpretation

Integrating the five findings yields a coherent picture. PP_0501 is a cytoplasmic, NAD⁺-dependent, transient-oxidation NDP-sugar epimerase/dehydratase that operates within the activated-sugar supply network for surface-glycan biosynthesis.

The consensus interpretation is that PP_0501 acts on a dTDP- or UDP-linked (possibly 6-deoxy) hexose — most plausibly a rhamnose-pathway intermediate or a related deoxysugar — feeding a specific cell-surface glycan structure. This is distinct from the housekeeping UDP-Glc↔UDP-Gal interconversion, which is handled by galE/PP_3129. The two-paralog arrangement (one canonical GalE, one divergent accessory enzyme physically coupled to RmlD) is the central mechanistic insight of this investigation.

Limitations and Knowledge Gaps

  1. No direct experimental characterization. Q88QJ1 has UniProt evidence level 4 ("Predicted"). No purified-enzyme assay, kinetic data, crystal structure, or knockout phenotype exists specifically for PP_0501. All functional claims below the family level are inferential.
  2. Exact substrate is undetermined. The NS-4E family acts on at least seven substrate classes (UDP-Glc/Gal, UDP-GlcNAc/GalNAc, UDP-Xyl/Ara, UDP-GlcA/GalA, dTDP-rhamnose/6-deoxy-talose, GDP-hexoses). Genomic context favors a dTDP-/UDP-(6-deoxy)hexose, but this is a hypothesis, not a measured activity. Whether PP_0501 is an epimerase, a dehydratase, or bifunctional is not established.
  3. Cofactor preference is inferred, not measured. The Asp34 ribose contact indicates NAD⁺ over NADP⁺, but this has not been confirmed biochemically.
  4. Operon boundaries and regulation are predicted. The 8-bp overlap with PP_0500 strongly implies co-transcription, but transcriptomic/RNA-seq confirmation of a single mRNA and shared promoter is lacking.
  5. STRING associations are computational. High-confidence scores reflect co-occurrence, co-expression, and text-mining signals, not physical complexes or validated pathway membership.
  6. Product glycan identity unknown. Whether the downstream product is LPS O-antigen, a specific EPS, or another surface glycan has not been resolved for this particular enzyme.

Proposed Follow-up Experiments / Actions

  1. Recombinant enzyme assay with a substrate panel. Express and purify His-tagged PP_0501; screen candidate NDP-sugars (UDP-Glc, UDP-Gal, UDP-GlcNAc, UDP-GalNAc, UDP-GlcA, dTDP-glucose, dTDP-4-keto-6-deoxyglucose, dTDP-rhamnose intermediates, GDP-mannose) by capillary electrophoresis or LC-MS to detect epimerization/dehydration. This is the single most decisive experiment.
  2. Cofactor determination. Measure bound cofactor (NAD⁺ vs NADP⁺) by denaturation/HPLC and test activity dependence on added cofactor; mutate Asp34 to test the ribose-discrimination hypothesis.
  3. Genetic operon test. RT-PCR / RNA-seq across the PP_0500–PP_0501 junction to confirm co-transcription; construct clean deletions of PP_0501 (and the PP_0500–PP_0501 pair) and characterize LPS/EPS by SDS-PAGE silver stain, glycosyl-composition GC-MS, and immunoblotting for O-antigen changes.
  4. Complementation/epistasis with galE (PP_3129). Test whether PP_0501 can complement a galE deletion (to probe overlap with housekeeping activity) and whether double mutants reveal specialized function.
  5. Structural validation. Solve a crystal or cryo-EM structure (or refine the AlphaFold model against experimental data) with bound cofactor and substrate analog to confirm the active-site geometry and substrate specificity determinants.
  6. Phenotypic profiling. Assess the PP_0501 mutant for biofilm formation, surface adhesion, phage sensitivity, and stress tolerance — readouts sensitive to O-antigen/EPS composition changes.

Conclusion

PP_0501 (Q88QJ1) is confidently identified — at the mechanistic and structural level — as a cytoplasmic, NAD⁺-dependent nucleotide-sugar epimerase/dehydratase of the extended-SDR (GalE) superfamily, carrying the full complement of Rossmann NAD-binding, YxxxK catalytic, and NAD-specificity motifs and a high-confidence GalE-fold AlphaFold model. It generates activated sugar donors in the cytoplasm to supply the biosynthesis of cell-surface polysaccharides. While databases best-hit-label it UDP-glucose 4-epimerase, that housekeeping function is carried by the paralog galE (PP_3129); PP_0501's operonic coupling to the dTDP-4-dehydrorhamnose reductase PP_0500 and its sugar-nucleotide STRING network instead implicate it in 6-deoxyhexose (rhamnose-type) NDP-sugar biosynthesis for LPS O-antigen and/or exopolysaccharide. Its exact substrate remains experimentally undetermined and is the priority target for future work.
