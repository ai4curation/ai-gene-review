# uppS notes

- UniProt assigns Q88MH6 the eight-condensation reaction that produces
  undecaprenyl diphosphate from FPP and IPP
  [file:PSEPK/uppS/uppS-uniprot.txt "Catalyzes the sequential condensation of isopentenyl"].
- The product is a shared precursor for peptidoglycan, lipopolysaccharide, and
  other envelope glycans, so peptidoglycan synthesis is a valid downstream use
  rather than the sole core process
  [file:PSEPK/uppS/uppS-uniprot.txt "UPP is the precursor of glycosyl carrier lipid in"].
- PANTHER places Q88MH6 and E. coli UppS in PTHR10291:SF0, but the official
  subfamily label is "DEHYDRODOLICHYL DIPHOSPHATE SYNTHASE 2". Do not use that
  misleading identifier as the module's family descriptor.
