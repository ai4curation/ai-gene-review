NicC performs oxidative decarboxylation, consuming NADH and molecular oxygen and yielding 2,5-DHP plus carbon dioxide. FAD binding is integral to its core activity, not a peripheral function. The native pathway does not pass through 2,6-DHP. PMID:27218267 was read through the publisher abstract (the local PubMed cache has bibliographic metadata only); both Bordetella and Pseudomonas homologs were assayed and the Pseudomonas FAD-bound structure was solved. No title fragment is used as mechanistic evidence.
PMID:18678916 full main text was read at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2516282/ . Cloned nicC produced a 42-kDa protein converting 6HNA to 2,5DHP with added NADH and FAD, independently establishing catalytic participation beyond the mutant phenotype. The local cache initially remained abstract-only. Full cached PMID:38104959 describes the Pseudomonas H47Q variant: its introduction incorrectly says 2,6-DHP, contradicted by Scheme 1 and product-coupling assays identifying 2,5-DHP. The crystallographic 2-mercaptopyridine ligand was a contaminant and non-substrate, not evidence for a new natural reaction.
