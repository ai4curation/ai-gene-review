# mutS curation notes

MutS (Q88ME7; PP_1626) is the experimentally supported mismatch-recognition
component of KT2440 MMR. The direct study used permanent `mutS` deletion and
resolved a hierarchy of corrected mismatch classes
[PMID:31599106 "permanent deletion of mutS or transient loss of mutL activity"].

The core functions retain both mismatched-DNA binding and ATP-dependent DNA
damage sensor activity: the first captures substrate recognition, while the
second captures the ATP-driven signaling transition. Generic damaged-DNA and
duplex-DNA binding terms were marked over-annotated.
