# mutS curation notes

MutS (Q88ME7; PP_1626) is the experimentally supported mismatch-recognition
component of KT2440 MMR. The direct study used permanent `mutS` deletion and
resolved a hierarchy of corrected mismatch classes
[PMID:31599106 "permanent deletion of mutS or transient loss of mutL activity"].

The core function is represented once as ATP-dependent DNA damage sensor
activity. Generic damaged-DNA and duplex-DNA binding terms were marked
over-annotated, while mismatched-DNA binding remains accepted as the
substrate-specific supporting function.
