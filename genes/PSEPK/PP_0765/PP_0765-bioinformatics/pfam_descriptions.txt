PF06980 Protein of unknown function (DUF1302)
This entry represents a family of predicted outer membrane beta barrel proteins.  These proteins remain uncharacterised. They are approximately 600 residues long. These proteins are likely part of an exporter system that includes an MMPL exporter, a DUF1329 protein [pfam:PF07044] family protein that likely acts as a LolA-like chaperone.

PF07044 Protein of unknown function (DUF1329)
This family consists of several hypothetical bacterial proteins of around 475 residues in length. The majority of family members are from Pseudomonas species but the family also contains sequences from Shewanella oneidensis and Thauera aromatica.
