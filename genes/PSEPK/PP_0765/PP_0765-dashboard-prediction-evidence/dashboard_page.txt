why
The 74.3%-identical PA3923 homolog directly binds laminin (KD 6.9 nM), and its disruption reduces laminin adherence and biofilm formation on laminin-coated surfaces. The 98.7%-identical P. putida homolog is curated as a putative adhesin. Query DUF1302 coverage of 97.8% and a predicted 18-stranded outer membrane beta-barrel support this assignment. Fitness defects on Tween 20, butyl stearate, and quaternary ammonium compounds do not establish transport or catalytic activity.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by direct ligand-binding characterization of a 74.3%-identical homolog, corroborated by curated adhesin annotations, query domain evidence, and outer membrane topology. Additional evidence includes condition-specific fitness, weak cofitness (maximum 0.440), conserved neighborhood associations, and STRING context. The generic STRING porin label and distant dipeptide-transporter similarity do not establish channel or transport activity; the putative qualifier reflects transfer of laminin specificity from a homolog.
