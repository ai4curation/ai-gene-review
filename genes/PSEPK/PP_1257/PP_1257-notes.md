# PP_1257 curation notes

- PP_1257 is assigned to 1-pyrroline-4-hydroxy-2-carboxylate deaminase EC
  3.5.4.22 [file:PSEPK/PP_1257/PP_1257-uniprot.txt,
  "SubName: Full=1-pyrroline-4-hydroxy-2-carboxylate deaminase"].
- Rhea 10560 specifies conversion of
  4-hydroxy-1-pyrroline-2-carboxylate to 2,5-dioxopentanoate and ammonium.
- Watanabe et al. directly characterized the P. putida Pyr4H2C deaminase and
  showed that disruption eliminates growth on L- and D-hydroxyproline
  [PMID:22833679, "disruption of Pyr4H2C deaminase genes led to loss of growth
  on L-hydroxyproline"].
- The TreeGrafter DapA activity is removed as a paralog transfer within the
  shared DapA-like fold. The exact EC assignment and the PP_1255-proR locus
  distinguish the hydroxyproline deaminase role.
