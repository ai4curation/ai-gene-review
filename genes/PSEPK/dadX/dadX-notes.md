# dadX curation notes

Q88CB2 is an experimentally verified alanine-specific racemase. The 2013 study
states that DadX "showed narrow substrate specificity, clearly preferring only
the alanine stereoisomers as the substrates" and that its catalytic
efficiencies with L- and D-alanine were six- and nine-fold higher,
respectively, than those of KT2440 Alr.
[PMID:23995642, "the putative catabolic alanine racemase DadX showed narrow
substrate specificity, clearly preferring only the alanine stereoisomers as the
substrates"; "DadX did show 6- and 9-fold higher k cat / K m values than Alr
with l - and d -alanine, respectively."]

The reaction is reversible, and the protein has a cytosol IEA, making DadX the
best current candidate for cytoplasmic D-alanine production. However, the
target paper describes a catabolic dadAX locus and does not report a DadX
knockout, D-alanine auxotrophy, cell-wall composition, or peptidoglycan flux.
[PMID:23995642, "Often referred to as a 'catabolic racemase' ( 45 ), DadX
enzymes are proposed to serve a role in the catabolism of l -alanine"]

Accordingly, alanine racemase activity is core, but GO:0030632 D-alanine
biosynthetic process remains undecided for the target. The module records a
real candidate-level hole rather than treating reversible chemistry or
UniRule transfer as proof of physiological direction.
