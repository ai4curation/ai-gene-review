# adk review notes

P0A136 is the KT2440 adenylate kinase by its exact UniProt record. A bacterial
homolog study summarizes its central chemistry: [PMID:37418448, "interconversion of ATP, AMP, and ADP in all living organisms."] This is homolog-level support, not direct experimental evidence for P0A136.

P0A136 maps only to the heterogeneous parent `PTHR23359` nucleotide kinase
family, so adenylate kinase specificity is asserted on the exact leaf annoton.
The exact UniProt record supports the core cytoplasm location.
