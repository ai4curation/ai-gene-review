# xthA curation notes

- Q88IV9 is named exonuclease III/AP endonuclease VI and belongs to the AP/ExoA
  family [file:PSEPK/xthA/xthA-uniprot.txt,
  "Exodeoxyribonuclease III / apurinic/apyrimidinic endodeoxyribonuclease VI"].
- AP-endonuclease activity and BER placement are transferred by ISS from the
  reviewed E. coli XthA exemplar UniProtKB:P09030. E. coli ExoIII directly
  cleaves beside AP sites [PMID:16524897,
  "Both are endowed with AP endonucleolytic activity"].
- A characterized Mycobacterium XthA homolog also has phosphatase and
  phosphodiesterase activities [PMID:25748880,
  "3'-5' exonuclease, phosphatase, and phosphodiesterase activities"]. These
  comparative activities justify an end-processing knowledge gap but are not
  transferred as exact blocked-end functions to untested PSEPK XthA.
