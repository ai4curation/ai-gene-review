# aldB-I curation notes

- Q88QE9 / PP_0545 is PTHR43111:SF1, the AldB/AcoD-like family. Verified family exemplars include Pseudomonas ExaC Q9ZAA1 and Cupriavidus AcoD P46368.
- AldB-I is not classical acetylating EutE: EutE is PTHR11699/IPR012408 and catalyzes acetyl-CoA formation, whereas AldB-I is a 506-aa non-acylating EC 1.2.1.3 enzyme predicted to form acetate.
- All three QuickGO rows were reviewed. GO:0140087 is proposed as a more precise function; the ethanolamine-process assignment remains an explicit locus-based hypothesis.
- No completed OpenScientist artifact was present for aldB-I. Existing Asta retrieval was sparse and not used as primary evidence.
