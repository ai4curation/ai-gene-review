PP_3298 (Q88HQ5), CinA C-terminal / PncC family: active-site assessment

Gene: PSEPK PP_3298, UniProt Q88HQ5, NCBITaxon:160488 (Pseudomonas putida KT2440) Hypothesis slug: kgap-pp3298-cinac-pncc-active-site Source file: genes/PSEPK/PP_3298/PP_3298-ai-review.yaml Focus type: free_text Iterations completed: 3. Findings recorded: 11. Papers read: 8.

Summary

Verdict: partially supported, with a competing interpretation that the curation decision should follow.

The seed hypothesis asked two things: whether the PP_3298 active site is assembled, and whether PP_3298 is therefore an active NMN deamidase or a divergent paralog. The first question has a clean answer. PP_3298 retains all four residues that site-directed mutagenesis showed to be essential in characterised PncC (S25, Y52, K57, R139, corresponding to E. coli S29, Y56, K61, R142), and the AlphaFold model places them in the geometry measured in the Agrobacterium fabrum PncC crystal structure 2A9S: Ser OG to Lys NZ at 2.87 Å against 2.90 Å in the crystal, Tyr OH to Arg CZ at 4.01 Å against 3.82 Å. pLDDT at all four positions is 94.2 to 98.6. The alternative reading, that PP_3298 is a decayed pseudo-enzyme with a disassembled site, is refuted. The discriminating comparison is E. coli YdeJ, a PncC-fold homolog at 38.5% identity that has no measurable NMN deamidase activity and has lost exactly the two substrate-binding residues: Tyr56 is Phe67 and Arg142 is Cys152. Sequence identity alone does not separate PP_3298 (39.0%) from the inactive YdeJ (38.5%), so the residue-level mapping carries the test.

The second question resolves against the seed framing. PP_3298 is probably not the NMN deamidase of P. putida KT2440. That role is already filled by PP_1628 (Q88ME5), which is 53.5% identical to E. coli PncC, carries KEGG orthology K03743 / EC 3.5.1.42, sits at 1826561..1827043 directly upstream of recA (PP_1629) and recX (PP_1630) in the ancestral cinA-recA arrangement, and has STRING edges to nadC (0.993), nadD (0.991), pncB (0.977) and ushA (0.970). PP_3298 has a single STRING partner above 0.7 (nadE, 0.711) and sits between an outer membrane lipoprotein, a TetR regulator and an RND efflux pump, with no cofactor-metabolism neighbours. The two proteins are the only PF02464 members in proteome UP000000556, and they fall in different UniRef50 clusters.

Three independent computations point at a lineage-specific shift in PP_3298 rather than either redundancy or decay. Across 82 Pseudomonas PF02464 sequences (41 per clade), the PP_1628-like and PP_3298-like clades are fixed in opposite states at nine pocket-lining positions (all q < 1.3e-6, each with a shift of more than 50 percentage points) while the catalytic tetrad shows no clade difference at all (q > 0.38). PP_3298 matches 2A9S at only 11 of 23 pocket-lining positions against 19 of 23 for PP_1628. The PP_3298 pocket shell carries 57 side-chain heavy atoms, net formal charge +1 and hydrophobic fraction 0.48, against 46 to 50 atoms, net +2 and 0.33 to 0.37 for the three canonical pockets. The honest functional call is an active CinA_C/PncC-fold Ser/Lys amidohydrolase with an unestablished substrate.

One premise of the seed hypothesis cannot be satisfied and should be corrected in the source review. There is no substrate-bound PncC/CinA_C structure anywhere in the PDB. All 13 PF02464-containing entries are apo or carry ATP, Mg, Na or ADP-ribose, and the ADP-ribose binds the fused COG1058 pyrophosphatase domain, not the PncC site. The NMN-contacting residues the hypothesis asks to transfer were defined by docking plus mutagenesis, which is weaker evidence than the hypothesis assumed.

Key findings

F001 and F002: the catalytic apparatus of PP_3298 is present and correctly assembled

Global Needleman-Wunsch alignment (BLOSUM62, gap open -11, extend -1) of Q88HQ5 (167 aa) against E. coli PncC/YgaD (P0A6G3, 165 aa) gives 39.0% identity over 163 aligned positions. The four residues whose substitution abolishes E. coli PncC activity map to S25, Y52, K57 and R139 in PP_3298, all identical to the reference. The same four map to S27, Y54, K59, R139 in PP_1628 at 53.5% identity, and to S33, Y60, K65, R147 in the 2A9S canonical numbering.

The negative control is informative. E. coli YdeJ (P31131, 38.5% identity to PncC) is one of the three E. coli PncC homologs, and NMN deamidase activity was confirmed only for ygaD (PMID: 21953451). YdeJ retains S40 and K72, so the Ser/Lys dyad survives, but Tyr56 has become Phe67 and Arg142 has become Cys152. Those are precisely the two residues whose mutation abolishes NMN binding while leaving catalysis-competent protein behind.

Geometry was measured directly in the AlphaFold DB v6 models and in 2A9S chain A (1.75 Å X-ray).

A 2.87 Å Ser OG to Lys NZ distance is a hydrogen bond, which is what a Ser/Lys amidohydrolase dyad requires. The PP_3298 values reproduce the crystal to within 0.2 Å on both measurements.

The limitation is quaternary structure. In 2A9S the pocket lies at the dimer interface: chain B residues Thr42, Ser47, Ser48 and Val51 are within 5.6 Å of the chain A tetrad centroid, and 19 chain B atoms fall within 8 Å of it. A monomeric AlphaFold model tests only the intrasubunit half of the site.

F003, F006 and F008: PP_1628 is the canonical PncC of this organism, and PP_3298 has no NAD-pathway context

UniProt search of proteome UP000000556 for Pfam PF02464 returns exactly two proteins. PP_1628 (160 aa, submitted name "NMN amidohydrolase") and PP_3298 (167 aa, submitted name "CinA domain protein"). Both carry InterPro IPR008136 (CinA_C), NCBIfam TIGR00199 (PncC_domain) and Gene3D 3.90.950.20. Neither is fused to a COG1058 domain, so KT2440 has no full-length CinA protein.

Two independent context measures, genomic neighbourhood and functional association network, place NAD recycling on PP_1628 and leave PP_3298 without a pathway anchor. The cinA-recA operon arrangement that PP_1628 reproduces is the ancestral context for this family: in Streptococcus pneumoniae, recA lies in a competence-inducible operon whose first gene is cinA (PMID: 7538190). PP_3298 inherits neither the NAD context nor the competence context.

STRING edges derive partly from genomic context, co-occurrence and text mining rather than from experiment, so this is database-level association evidence. Genomic context is suggestive and cannot by itself rule out NMN deamidase activity for a protein whose active site is intact.

F004: residue conservation does not separate the two clades

Across 491 Pfam PF02464 entries from the genus Pseudomonas (478 unique sequences), 3-mer profile similarity splits the family cleanly into a PP_1628-like clade (n = 341) and a PP_3298-like clade (n = 137). Needleman-Wunsch mapping of the four essential residues onto a seeded random subsample of 70 sequences (35 per clade, numpy RNG seed 0) gives:

A motif-based scan of all 478 unique sequences agrees at the Ser, Tyr and Lys positions (PP_3298-like clade: Ser 111/111, Tyr 129/130, Lys 128/130 among sequences where the motif was detected). Purifying selection has held the catalytic tetrad in the PP_3298 lineage across the whole genus. If the lineage had drifted to a non-enzymatic role, loss of Tyr56 or Arg142 would be expected, as happened in YdeJ. The conservation is evidence for retained amidohydrolase chemistry; it does not identify the substrate, since a related pyridine nucleotide would use the same tetrad.

F007: nine outer-shell pocket positions are fixed in opposite states between the clades

Pocket positions were defined geometrically as residues with a side-chain atom within a given radius of the catalytic tetrad centroid in 2A9S chain A. The 7 Å shell contains S31, C32, Y58, K63, A108, R145, R149; the 9 Å shell adds E30, T33, V56, T57, T59, N60, A79, V80, M88, V104, T105, I107. E30, C32, T33, V56 and M88 also show no clade difference. Near-complete fixation (100% in 41 sequences at four positions) is selection on the alternative state, not neutral drift. The pattern fits an amidohydrolase that has retained its chemistry while shifting substrate recognition, and it argues against both "canonical NMN deamidase" and "decayed pseudoenzyme". Loss of Arg149, which lies in the 7 Å shell and is a second basic residue in a pocket that binds a phosphorylated pyridine nucleotide, is the single change most likely to alter ligand preference.

F010: the PP_3298 pocket is bulkier, more hydrophobic and one basic charge short

The final analysis defined each pocket from that model's own tetrad centroid rather than by transfer from 2A9S, so it is a structural measurement rather than an alignment projection. The three canonical PncC pockets are near identical on every axis. PP_3298 is the outlier on every axis: 11 to 24% more side-chain bulk, one fewer basic side chain (the second-shell Arg149 of 2A9S is Met143 in PP_3298), and the polar positions Thr57 and Asn60 replaced by Val51 and Pro54.

PP_3298 and YdeJ fail the canonical-PncC test in different directions. YdeJ has lost the catalytic machinery itself and carries a net-negative pocket, which matches its measured lack of NMN deamidase activity. PP_3298 keeps the complete Ser/Lys/Tyr/Arg machinery but presents it inside a smaller-volume, less electropositive cavity. For a substrate carrying a 5'-phosphate like NMN, losing a second-shell basic residue and adding side-chain bulk is the direction expected for a shift in substrate preference.

F011: no primary literature exists on PP_3298

A PubMed search for "PP_3298 Pseudomonas putida KT2440 gene function" returned no papers. Across the whole investigation eight papers were read (PMIDs 34199271, 24530526, 23776507, 21953451, 7592458, 7538190, 31874073, 10606814), and none reports an experiment on PP_3298 or Q88HQ5. PncC enzymology comes from E. coli, Shewanella and Agrobacterium/Rhizobium proteins, and the only negative control with measured activity is E. coli YdeJ. Every functional statement about PP_3298 is therefore prediction-level, and no IDA, IMP or IPI evidence code is justifiable.

Mechanistic scope and interpretation

The immediate molecular activity under test is hydrolysis of a carbon-nitrogen amide bond by a Ser/Lys dyad. In characterised PncC the Ser nucleophile is activated by the Lys, while Tyr and Arg hold the substrate: the S29A and K61Q variants still bind NMN in the micromolar range but cannot catalyse (PMID: 34199271), which separates the catalytic pair from the binding pair. PP_3298 has all four, correctly spaced, so competence for amide hydrolysis on a small nucleotide-like substrate is supported at the level of structure prediction.

What the model cannot reach is which amide. The nine clade-fixed substitutions sit outside the first catalytic shell, in the positions that would contact the nicotinamide ring and the ribose-phosphate. Removal of the second-shell Arg149 takes positive charge out of a pocket that in PncC must accommodate a 5'-phosphate, and the added bulk reduces available volume. That is a directional observation about substrate preference, not a proof.

Everything downstream of the activity is inference. The NAD cycle phenotype of pncC loss in Salmonella (PMID: 7592458), where mutants retain 2% of wild-type deamidase and NMN accumulation inhibits DNA ligase, belongs to the pathway, and transferring it to PP_3298 would attribute a downstream phenotype to a gene with no measured activity and no pathway context. Pfam PF02464 is also found as the C-terminal domain of full-length CinA, fused to a COG1058 ADP-ribose pyrophosphatase (PMID: 23776507). PP_3298 is a standalone PF02464 protein, so the COG1058 chemistry is not available to it.

Conflicts and alternatives

Redundant paralog. PP_3298 could simply be a second NMN deamidase, with the nine clade-fixed positions reflecting drift under relaxed selection after duplication. Against this: drift produces scattered, lineage-specific substitutions, not nine positions fixed in the opposite state across an entire 137-member clade at q < 1.3e-6 while the catalytic tetrad stays invariant. Selection that strong on pocket-lining positions, with the catalytic positions untouched, is the signature of a maintained but different function.

Degraded pseudo-enzyme. Refuted by the active-site geometry. YdeJ shows what a dead PncC site looks like: Tyr to Phe, Arg to Cys, 8.37 Å between the residues that should be 3.8 Å apart, and net pocket charge −1. PP_3298 shows none of that.

Competence or DNA-damage role rather than metabolic. The CinA family's historical association is with the competence regulon and recA (PMID: 7538190). In P. putida it is PP_1628, not PP_3298, that keeps the recA adjacency, which weakens this alternative for PP_3298 specifically.

Moonlighting on a non-NMN pyridine or nucleotide amide. Consistent with every observation here and currently untested. This is the alternative the discriminating tests below target.

Paralog confusion as a curation risk. The concrete failure mode for this gene review is family-level annotation transfer from PF02464 / TIGR00199 PncC_domain. Both P. putida proteins carry the same domain signatures, and an automated pipeline will assign EC 3.5.1.42 to both. The repository should record explicitly that PP_1628 is the better-supported assignment and that PP_3298 belongs to a distinct clade.

Database carry-over. The UniProt submitted name for PP_3298 is "CinA domain protein", which is appropriately non-committal, while PP_1628's is "NMN amidohydrolase". KEGG assigns K03743 only to PP_1628. The database records are consistent with this report's conclusion and do not need correcting; the risk is in downstream propagation, not in the primary records.

Limitations and knowledge gaps

No substrate-bound structure exists for any PncC or CinA_C protein. All 13 PF02464-containing PDB entries were enumerated through the InterPro API and annotated from the RCSB data API: 2A9S (A. fabrum, 1.75 Å, only Cl and water), 4CT8/4CT9/4CTA/4UOC/4UUW/4UUX (T. thermophilus CinA, with ATP, Mg, Na and ADP-ribose on the COG1058 domain), and 5KOL, 5KVK, 5V01, 5VU3, 6L19, 6MR3 (structural genomics, apo). None contains NMN, NaMN, nicotinamide riboside or any pyridine mononucleotide. E. coli PncC has no PDB entry at all. Every NMN contact assignment in the field rests on docking plus mutagenesis. What would resolve it: a co-crystal or cryo-EM structure of any PncC-family protein with NMN bound.

No assay, no deletion phenotype, no localisation for PP_3298. PubMed returns nothing on the gene. This sets a hard ceiling on the evidence code any annotation can carry. What would resolve it: a purified-protein deamidase assay and a ΔPP_3298 strain.

The substrate encoded by the nine clade-fixed positions is unknown. Nothing in the data identifies a candidate. The charge and bulk changes say "different ligand" without saying which. What would resolve it: a substrate panel on purified protein, or metabolomics on a deletion strain.

Monomeric models. PncC is a homodimer and the pocket receives contributions from the partner subunit (19 chain B atoms within 8 Å of the chain A tetrad centroid in 2A9S). The pocket physicochemistry in F010 was computed on AlphaFold monomers, so the interface half of each pocket is absent from those numbers. What would resolve it: AlphaFold-Multimer dimer models, or re-running the shell analysis on the 2A9S biological assembly for all five proteins.

Unfiltered pLDDT in the pocket analysis. The four tetrad positions in PP_3298 have pLDDT 94.2 to 98.6, which is high, but the surrounding shell residues were used without a confidence cut-off, so side-chain placement confidence is unquantified in the F010 numbers.

Clade sampling. The clade split used 3-mer profile similarity on 478 unique Pseudomonas sequences, and the statistics used a seeded random subsample (41 + 41), not a curated phylogeny with a tree. The split is clean and the effect sizes are large, so a tree is unlikely to reverse the conclusion, but the analysis does not establish the duplication's age or whether the PP_3298 clade extends beyond the genus and its close relatives.

Discriminating tests

Ranked by how sharply each would resolve the competing interpretations.

  1. Direct NMN deamidase assay on purified PP_3298, side by side with PP_1628 and E. coli PncC. Measure ammonia release or NaMN formation. A kcat/Km gap of two orders of magnitude or more against PP_1628 would settle the competing interpretation in one experiment. This is the single most informative test and the cheapest.
  2. Substrate panel against the purified protein. Nicotinamide riboside, nicotinamide, NAD, NADP, nicotinamide hypoxanthine dinucleotide and related linear amides. Designed to find the substrate the remodelled pocket prefers.
  3. Single and double deletion phenotyping. A ΔPP_3298 strain should show no NAD salvage defect if the interpretation here is right; ΔPP_1628 should. A double mutant distinguishes partial redundancy from independence. Growth on NMN as sole pyridine source is the readout, following the Salmonella pncC design (PMID: 7592458).
  4. Chimeric pocket swap. Transplant the nine clade-fixed positions from PP_1628 into PP_3298 and assay. If NMN deamidase activity appears or rises, the nine positions are the specificity determinant, which is the core structural claim of this report.
  5. Untargeted metabolomics on the deletion strain. If the substrate is an unexpected amide, accumulation in ΔPP_3298 is the most direct route to identifying it without guessing a panel.
  6. Co-crystallisation or cryo-EM of any PncC-family protein with NMN bound. This closes the field-wide gap that forced every contact assignment here to be inferential.

Curation leads

All items below require curator verification.

Correct a premise in the source review. The hypothesis text states that NMN-contacting residues can be read from "substrate-bound crystal structures". They cannot. PMID: 24530526 states it explicitly: "Analysis of the PncC structure in complex with a modeled NMN substrate supported our conclusion, and enabled us to propose the catalytic mechanism." Verify this snippet against the abstract.

Reassign the canonical PncC role within the organism. PP_1628 (Q88ME5) carries KEGG K03743 / EC 3.5.1.42, the recA adjacency and the NAD salvage STRING cluster. If the repository currently carries NMN deamidase as a core function on PP_3298, flag it as over-attributed and move the primary assignment to PP_1628.

Candidate GO term change for PP_3298. Generalise GO:0019159 to GO:0016811 as the molecular function, retaining GO:0019159 only as an explicitly prediction-level annotation with a note that a better-supported paralog exists. Treat GO:0019365 as unsupported for PP_3298.
