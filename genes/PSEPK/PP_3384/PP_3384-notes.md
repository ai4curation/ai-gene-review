# PP_3384 evidence notes

The requested OpenScientist run finished without leaving a final gene report, so this
manual note records the evidence used for curation. Q88HH4 belongs to the specific
Gad-subunit-3 family IPR027056 and has a Tat signal. The homologous experimentally
cloned complex contains a third "24-kDa subunit of unknown function" [PMID:9352901].
The protein is therefore curated as a noncatalytic or mechanistically unresolved
complex contributor, not as an independently active gluconate dehydrogenase.
