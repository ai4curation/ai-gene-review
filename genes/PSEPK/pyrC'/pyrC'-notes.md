# pyrC' curation notes

## 2026-08-31

PyrC' (Q88D29; PP_4999) is a lineage-specific inactive dihydroorotase-like
partner of PyrB, not the active pyrC product. The primary P. putida study reports
that the protein lacks histidines required for DHOase catalysis, fails to
complement an E. coli pyrC auxotroph, and forms a dodecameric ATCase complex of
six active PyrB plus six inactive PyrC' chains. Its proposed role is maintaining
the native ATCase assembly and activity [PMID:7896697 "The proposed function for
the vestigial DHOase is to maintain ATCase activity by conserving the
dodecameric assembly of the native enzyme."].

The UniProt/InterPro/PANTHER-derived hydrolase, allantoinase, metal-binding, and
purine-catabolism annotations therefore reflect ancestral-fold propagation and
should not be retained as catalytic claims. The broad pyrimidine-biosynthesis
annotation is directionally correct, but de novo UMP biosynthetic process is the
more precise pathway context.
