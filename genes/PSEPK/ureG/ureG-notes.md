# ureG curation notes

## 2026-08-11

- Q88J00 is reviewed UniProt UreG/PP_2849, a G3E-family P-loop GTPase
  (`PTHR31715:SF0`) with GTPase and nickel-binding annotations.
- Physiological bicarbonate-dependent urease activation requires nucleotide
  hydrolysis, and the critical nucleotide site was localized to UreG
  [PMID:10500143, "The critical nucleotide-binding site was localized to UreG"].
- The separate `GTP binding` term present in UniProt is not added as a new GOA
  row because nucleotide binding is integral to the retained GTPase activity.
- UreG is activation machinery, not a stable subunit of mature urease; it is
  directly involved in urea catabolism but does not contribute to the mature
  complex's catalytic MF.
