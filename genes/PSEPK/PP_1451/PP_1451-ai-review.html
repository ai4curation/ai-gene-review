Gene Description

PP_1451 is a small Sec-exported YafK/LdtF-family cysteine hydrolase predicted to act in the periplasm. Exact PANTHER-subfamily correspondence to Pseudomonas aeruginosa LdtPae3 supports hydrolysis of the amide bond that covalently links OprI-like outer-membrane lipoproteins to peptidoglycan. This reverses the tether-forming reaction of an ErfK-family L,D-transpeptidase; the substrate-specific assignment remains an orthology inference for KT2440.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

peptidoglycan-protein cross-link hydrolase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the hydrolysis of the amide bond that covalently links a protein, such as a Braun-lipoprotein-like outer-membrane lipoprotein, to a peptide stem of peptidoglycan.

Justification: GO:0018104 represents formation of a peptidoglycan-protein cross-link, but no molecular-function term identifies the reverse hydrolysis reaction. Generic carboxypeptidase activity does not specify the protein-peptidoglycan tether substrate.

Parent term: hydrolase activity
