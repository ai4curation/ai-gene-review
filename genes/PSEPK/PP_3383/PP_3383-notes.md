# PP_3383 evidence notes

The requested OpenScientist run finished without leaving a final gene report, so this
manual note records the evidence used for curation. Q88HH5 is the FAD-containing
dehydrogenase subunit of the three-subunit Gad complex based on its conserved protein
architecture, local operon, and homology to the experimentally cloned complex in
*Erwinia cypripedii* [PMID:9352901, "complete structural genes for a 68-kDa dehydrogenase
subunit, a 47-kDa cytochrome c subunit, and a 24-kDa subunit of unknown function"].
The broad PTHR46056 long-chain-alcohol-oxidase family is not used as an exact selector.
