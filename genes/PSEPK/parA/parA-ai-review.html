Gene Description

ParA (PP_0002) is the ATPase of the Pseudomonas putida ParABS chromosome-partitioning system, encoded immediately next to parB beside oriC. It is a 263-residue deviant Walker-type (ParA/MinD-family) P-loop ATPase related to B. subtilis Soj, with the KGGVGKT Walker A box at residues 11-17. It is 84% identical to P. aeruginosa ParA, whose purified protein hydrolyses ATP, dimerizes and binds ParB. In P. putida, parA mutants produce anucleate cells, mainly in minimal medium as cultures enter stationary phase. Together with ParB, ParA stabilises a plasmid carrying an oriC-region parS-like sequence in E. coli, showing that the P. putida proteins have partition activity of their own.

Core Functions

Deviant Walker-type ATPase of the ParABS system that, with ParB bound at the oriC-region parS sites, segregates replicated chromosome origins. The activity is inferred from the P. aeruginosa ortholog and the conserved Walker A box; the partition role is shown in P. putida.

Notes

(parA-notes.md)

parA (PP_0002, P0A149), Pseudomonas putida KT2440

2026-10-23: initial review

Identity. PP_0002 is the chromosomal ParA of the ParABS partition system, encoded next to
parB (PP_0001) at the start of the genome beside oriC. UniProt still names the Swiss-Prot entry
"Uncharacterized protein PP_0002" (sequence version 1, 2005), with only a curated similarity
note: "SIMILARITY: To B.subtilis soj." [file:PSEPK/parA/parA-uniprot.txt]. NCBI names the WP_
protein "ParA family protein" from SPARCLE architecture 11439703.

GOA has no annotation at all. The Pfam match (AAA_31, PF13614) has no InterPro2GO mapping and
the PANTHER subfamily (PTHR13696:SF52, "PARA FAMILY PROTEIN CT_582") brings no IBA. NCBI's
architecture GO (ATP binding, ATP hydrolysis activity) is the only GO this protein has anywhere.

Paralog caution. KT2440 also carries an orphan ParA-family protein, ParI, on a genomic
island, which interferes with IncP-7 plasmid partition PMID:22925377. The evidence below is specific to the oriC-adjacent
PP_0002, not to ParI.

P. putida genetics.
- parA and parB mutants give anucleate cells, but only in minimal medium at the entry into
stationary phase PMID:11832517. The authors link this to division without ongoing replication.
- parAB deletion and ParAB overproduction both raise anucleate-cell frequencies in deceleration
phase, and an oriC-region parS-like sequence stabilises a mini-F in E. coli only with ParAB
PMID:11849535.

Orthology to P. aeruginosa ParA. 84% identity over the full length, with an identical deviant
Walker A box (KGGVGKT, 11-17) [file:PSEPK/parA/parA-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md]. Purified P.
aeruginosa ParA hydrolyses ATP PMID:25139949. The DNA-binding arginines of B. subtilis Soj
are conserved in P. putida ParA PMID:18077387, but DNA binding has not been tested in either Pseudomonas, so it is not
proposed.

Decisions. NEW ATP hydrolysis activity (ISO from P. aeruginosa ParA) and NEW chromosome
segregation (IMP, P. putida mutants). ParA does work in segregation as the partition ATPase, so
the process term passes the "does the gene do any of the work" test.

NCBI/CDD comparison (see parA-pgap-predictions-review.yaml and projects/REFSEQ_GO.md):
both NCBI terms are correct and absent from GOA. This is the clearest case so far of CDD
architecture GO filling a complete GOA gap.

and Q9HT11 and Q9HT12 (P. aeruginosa parA/parB, PA5563/PA5562). Global alignment,
BLOSUM62, gap open -10, extend -0.5.

protein P. putida length P. aeruginosa length identical positions identity motif P. putida position P. aeruginosa position
parA 263 262 219/262 83.6% deviant Walker A (KGGxGK[ST]) KGGVGKT 11-17 KGGVGKT 11-17
parB 290 290 229/290 79.0% ParB CTP-binding box II (GERRxRA) GERRWRA 91-97 GERRWRA 91-97

Interpretation

The P. putida proteins are close orthologs of the P. aeruginosa proteins, at
84% (ParA) and 79% (ParB) identity over their full length. Both genes sit at
the same place in the two genomes, at the start of the chromosome next to oriC.

The motifs that define the two activities are identical and at the same
positions in both species. ParA has the deviant Walker A box KGGVGKT at 11-17,
the ATP-binding signature of the ParA/MinD family. ParB has the CTP-binding box
GERRWRA at 91-97, which coordinates the CTP phosphates in characterized ParB
CTPases.

This is the basis for transferring the biochemical evidence measured on the
P. aeruginosa proteins to the P. putida proteins: ATP hydrolysis by ParA
(PMID:25139949) and parS-stimulated CTP hydrolysis by ParB (PMID:38843873).
