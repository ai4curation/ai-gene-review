Gene Description

SoxD (PP_0324) is the small delta subunit of the cytosolic SoxABDG sarcosine oxidase complex. It is a complex component rather than an independent enzyme and contributes to tetrahydrofolate-coupled conversion of sarcosine to glycine.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

sarcosine oxidase (5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate-forming) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: sarcosine + tetrahydrofolate + oxygen = glycine + 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate + hydrogen peroxide.

Justification: The SoxD structural subunit contributes to the heterotetrameric reaction, whose folate-coupled chemistry is absent from GO.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:9185627 : only the heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidases can use tetrahydrofolates as substrates

soxD curation notes
