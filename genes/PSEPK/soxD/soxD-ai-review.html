Gene Description

SoxD (PP_0324) is the small (111-residue) delta subunit of the cytosolic heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase SoxABDG. In the characterised orthologous enzyme the delta subunit carries a single zinc ion bound by a Cys3His zinc-finger motif and has no flavin or folate cofactor of its own. It is a component of the complex that oxidises sarcosine and transfers its methyl group to tetrahydrofolate, forming glycine, 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate and hydrogen peroxide. In KT2440 this catabolic role is inferred from conserved subunit identity and pathway context; the strain-specific creatine study cites SoxBDAG as background rather than directly characterizing the purified complex.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

sarcosine oxidase (5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate-forming) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: sarcosine + (6S)-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + O2 = (6R)-5,10-methylene-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + glycine + H2O2.

Justification: This proposal describes the complete folate-coupled reaction of heterotetrameric SoxABDG (EC 1.5.3.24; RHEA:70455), distinct from the glycine/formaldehyde-producing GO:0008115 reaction. It is an ontology proposal for the assembled complex, not the activity of each isolated subunit. The schema permits proposed_molecular_function together with contributes_to_molecular_function, but that syntactic permission does not make a complex reaction subunit-specific. The core therefore retains contribution to GO:0016647; beta cores additionally identify the separately defined imine-forming catalytic subreaction. Other subunits retain only their supported own functions. The target locus is inferred from subunit-specific signatures and genomic context; target-specific folate product partition has not been measured.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors, oxygen as acceptor

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:16934831 : In the absence of H 4 folate, turnover occurs at a somewhat slower rate and the sarcosine imine is hydrolyzed to glycine and formaldehyde.

Core Functions

SoxD supplies the zinc-binding delta structure of the enzyme, not a flavin active site. This subunit contributes to the assembled SoxABDG oxidation of sarcosine with oxygen. In characterized orthologous complexes, tetrahydrofolate accepts the one-carbon unit to form 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate and glycine; without folate, imine hydrolysis yields glycine and formaldehyde. KT2440 product partition and autonomous subunit catalysis have not been measured. The oxygen-dependent parent expresses complex contribution without asserting that this subunit independently performs the full folate-coupled reaction.

    + glycine + H2O2.'
  justification: This proposal describes the complete folate-coupled reaction of heterotetrameric SoxABDG (EC
    1.5.3.24; RHEA:70455), distinct from the glycine/formaldehyde-producing GO:0008115 reaction. It is an ontology
    proposal for the assembled complex, not the activity of each isolated subunit. The schema permits proposed_molecular_function
    together with contributes_to_molecular_function, but that syntactic permission does not make a complex
    reaction subunit-specific. The core therefore retains contribution to GO:0016647; beta cores additionally
    identify the separately defined imine-forming catalytic subreaction. Other subunits retain only their supported
    own functions. The target locus is inferred from subunit-specific signatures and genomic context; target-specific
    folate product partition has not been measured.
  proposed_parent:
    id: GO:0016647
    label: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors, oxygen as acceptor
  supported_by:
  - reference_id: PMID:9185627
    supporting_text: The results show that only the heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidases can use tetrahydrofolates
      as substrates
  - reference_id: PMID:16934831
    supporting_text: In the absence of H 4 folate, turnover occurs at a somewhat slower rate and the sarcosine
      imine is hydrolyzed to glycine and formaldehyde.
