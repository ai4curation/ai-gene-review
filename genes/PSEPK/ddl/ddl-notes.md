# ddl/PP_5673 curation notes

A0A140FWM5 is an unreviewed but internally consistent D-alanine-D-alanine
ligase assignment. UniProt records EC 6.3.2.4, the exact D-Ala-D-Ala-forming
reaction, cytoplasmic localization, and peptidoglycan biosynthesis.
[UniProtKB:A0A140FWM5, "Reaction=2 D-alanine + ATP = D-alanyl-D-alanine + ADP +
phosphate +"; "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm"; "PATHWAY: Cell wall biogenesis;
peptidoglycan biosynthesis."]

The exact GO:0008716 activity and GO:0009252 process are accepted from
concordant HAMAP, UniRule, InterPro, Rhea, and pathway evidence. Broad
nucleotide binding, ATP binding, acid-amino-acid ligase activity, and metal ion
binding are ancillary or over-broad relative to the exact reaction.

UniProt places PP_5673 in PTHR23132:SF23, the DdlB-like branch, but the local
reviewed PANTHER member table does not include this unreviewed accession.
PP_5673 and Q88N74 are therefore represented as two exemplars of one DdlB-like
module variant. Their relative expression, essentiality, and condition-specific
deployment remain unresolved.
