# rbsR curation notes

Q88K35 is a LacI-family DNA-binding transcriptional repressor encoded between
the transporter genes and the `rbsK-rbsD` catabolic genes. This makes regulation
of the local locus plausible, but no KT2440 operator, effector, or regulon was
established in the fetched inputs.

The broad PANTHER family is LacI-related, whereas the assigned subfamily is
PurR-related. The review therefore supports generic DNA-binding transcription
repressor activity without asserting ribose as the effector or naming a
specific target regulon
[file:PSEPK/rbsR/rbsR-uniprot.txt, "DR   PANTHER; PTHR30146:SF148; HTH-TYPE
TRANSCRIPTIONAL REPRESSOR PURR-RELATED; 1."].

OpenScientist recovered ortholog literature for RbsR-family repression but no
direct PP_2457 study. It explicitly states: "No dedicated biochemical study of
the *P. putida* protein exists in the literature searched; the mechanism is
inferred from well-characterized RbsR orthologs that share both the domain
architecture and the operon context:"
[file:PSEPK/rbsR/rbsR-deep-research-openscientist.md]. This supports retaining
the generic repressor function while leaving the local operator, effector, and
regulon as questions rather than annotations.
