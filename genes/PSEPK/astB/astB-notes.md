# astB curation notes

- Q88EI5 is assigned the exact EC 3.5.3.23/RHEA:19533 hydrolysis of
  N2-succinyl-L-arginine to N2-succinyl-L-ornithine, ammonium, and carbon
  dioxide [file:PSEPK/astB/astB-uniprot.txt "succinylornithine, ammonia and
  CO(2)."].
- Comparative Pseudomonas genetics assigns the homologous aruB gene to
  N2-succinylarginine dihydrolase [PMID:9393691 "N2-succinylarginine
  dihydrolase"]. The exact activity and L-arginine catabolic process are core;
  broader arginine metabolism is retained as non-core context.
