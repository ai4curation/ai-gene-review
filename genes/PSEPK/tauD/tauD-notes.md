# tauD curation notes

- PMID:22221834 directly studies KT2440 TauD and reports that it "catalyzes the
  hydroxylation of taurine yielding sulfite and aminoacetaldehyde."
- The reviewed exact record gives the complete 2-oxoglutarate-dependent
  reaction [file:PSEPK/tauD/tauD-uniprot.txt, "Reaction=taurine +
  2-oxoglutarate + O2 = aminoacetaldehyde + sulfite +"].
- The enzyme is tetrameric [PMID:22221834, "show that both TauD(Pp) and
  TauD(Ec) are tetramers in"], but `identical protein binding` is too generic
  to represent that assembly as a core function.
- Iron binding and broad dioxygenase/oxidoreductase terms are mechanistically
  correct but subordinate to the substrate-specific taurine dioxygenase term.
