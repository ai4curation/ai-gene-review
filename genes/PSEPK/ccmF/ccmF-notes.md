# ccmF literature notes

- System I separates CcmABCD/CcmE heme handling from a CcmF/H-mediated heme reduction and ligation step [PMID:19721088 "ligation of the heme to CXXCH"].
- CcmF, CcmH, and CcmI form a membrane-integral multisubunit heme-ligation complex [PMID:18753134 "membrane-integral multisubunit heme ligation complex"].
- GO:0004408 was considered, but current evidence does not isolate holocytochrome-c synthase activity to CcmF alone rather than the CcmF/H machinery.
