Gene Description

HolD is the divergent psi accessory subunit of the Pseudomonas putida KT2440 clamp-loader system, encoded at PP_0696. It forms a complex with HolC/chi and is inferred to connect and stabilize the accessory assembly on the DnaX-containing loader. Purified KT2440 chi–psi binds single-stranded DNA under low-salt conditions; experiments on homologous Pseudomonas complexes attribute this binding to the psi N-terminal region. The literature uses a corrected noncanonical start predicting a 261-residue native protein, whereas Q88Q02 retains an older 291-residue translation. The characterized fusion insert corresponds to database residues 32–291 and does not establish a function for the upstream extension.

Core Functions

Contributes the divergent psi accessory subunit to the chi–psi clamp-loader assembly, supporting efficient loading and replisome organization. The identity is grounded by the KT2440 expression insert; the loader mechanism is inferred from characterized Pseudomonas homologs, with the corrected native start distinguished from the longer database translation.
