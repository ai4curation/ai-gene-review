# purA review notes

Q88DD8 is the KT2440 adenylosuccinate synthetase by its exact UniProt record.
The E. coli homolog structure directly resolves the IMP, guanine-nucleotide,
and Mg2+ binding context: [PMID:9000627, "enzyme occur in loops that bind to Mg2+, GDP, IMP and hadacidin."] This is homolog-level mechanistic support, not direct experimental evidence for Q88DD8.

The exact UniProt/HAMAP cytoplasm statement supports a core cytoplasm location.
