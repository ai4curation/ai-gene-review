# acoA curation notes

- Identity: Q88QD9 / PP_0555, the E1 alpha subunit encoded in the KT2440 `aco` locus.
- The only GOA phylogenetic selector is `PANTHER:PTN002264059`. It propagates pyruvate dehydrogenase activity and pyruvate decarboxylation across an acetoin-specific paralog and was not accepted as substrate-specific evidence.
- PMID:7813883 identifies the corresponding P. putida PpG2 AcoA as acetoin-system E1 alpha. PMID:2061286 establishes an alpha2-beta2 AcoA/AcoB E1 in the protein-level H16 exemplar.
- No completed gene-level OpenScientist artifact was present for acoA. Research was not restarted; the review uses cached primary literature, UniProt, GOA, and verified exemplars.
