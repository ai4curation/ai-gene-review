# rbsB curation notes

Q88K38 is the signal-peptide-bearing substrate-binding protein at the start of
the PP_2454-PP_2459 `rbs` locus. The neighboring ATPase, permease, ribokinase,
and D-ribose pyranase make D-ribose import the strongest pathway assignment,
but the current PANTHER subfamily label is D-allose-related and should not be
used as independent proof of ligand specificity
[file:PSEPK/rbsB/rbsB-uniprot.txt, "DR   PANTHER; PTHR46847:SF1;
D-ALLOSE-BINDING PERIPLASMIC PROTEIN-RELATED; 1."].

The OpenScientist retrieval found ortholog and locus evidence but no direct
Q88K38 binding experiment. The review therefore retains D-ribose uptake as a
well-supported inference, not direct KT2440 biochemistry
[file:PSEPK/rbsB/rbsB-deep-research-openscientist.md, "No direct biochemistry
on *P. putida* Q88K38."]. A possible ribose-chemotaxis role is left as a
question because it is established in other bacteria but not demonstrated for
this KT2440 protein.
