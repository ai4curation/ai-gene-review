Gene Description

ccmE encodes a membrane-anchored periplasmic heme chaperone of cytochrome c maturation system I. Binds heme transiently and covalently as a membrane-anchored periplasmic chaperone and delivers it to the heme-ligation machinery. Its mechanistic assignment in Pseudomonas putida KT2440 is inferred from conserved features and characterized system I orthologs.

2026-11-06 system I comparative reassessment

Binds heme transiently and covalently as a membrane-anchored periplasmic chaperone and delivers it to the heme-ligation machinery. This KT2440 assignment is an ortholog inference from the frozen family/domain record and characterized E. coli or other system I proteins; no direct KT2440 assay is cited.

Read the full 2023 E. coli structures (PMID:37626034), 2025 CcmB efflux work (PMID:40026579) and CcmCD crosslink mapping (PMID:40167305). The studies disagree about the heme conduit. ATP-dependent holo-CcmE release and covalent loading remain supported; heme contact, engineered overexpression, and proposed channel models are distinguished from purified vectorial transport. Broad intracellular heme handoff is not equivalent to membrane translocation. The 2025 review PMID:40460019 is abstract-only and is not used to settle this disagreement.
