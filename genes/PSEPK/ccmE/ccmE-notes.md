# ccmE literature notes

- CcmE is a periplasmic heme chaperone that binds heme covalently, with CcmC required for holo-CcmE formation [PMID:10339610 "CcmE is a periplasmic heme chaperone that binds heme covalently"].
- System I models place loaded holo-CcmE upstream of the CcmF/H heme-ligation step [PMID:19721088 "CcmFH-mediated reduction"].
