# cycH literature notes

- A characterized Bradyrhizobium CycH ortholog is required for accumulation of nearly all detectable c-type cytochromes [PMID:8231805 "All of the c-type cytochromes detectable"].
- The PSEPK protein and the characterized ortholog share the exact PANTHER PTHR47870:SF4 assignment, supporting a conservative process-level ISS annotation.
- The direct molecular activity and any substrate-presentation mechanism remain unresolved, so no molecular function is proposed.
