# vdh curation notes

Vdh is the PP_3357 NAD-dependent vanillin dehydrogenase in the KT2440 ferulate
route. The target entry assigns vanillin dehydrogenase and EC 1.2.1.67
[file:PSEPK/vdh/vdh-uniprot.txt]. The primary KT2440 study reports that "The
enzyme activities of Fcs and Vdh were determined in protein extracts" and that
separate inactivation of vdh was among the disruptions that abolished growth
on ferulic acid [PMID:12764569].

The two broad oxidoreductase annotations are replaced with GO:0050608 because
the substrate-specific NAD+-dependent function is directly supported. The same
mutant study supports participation in ferulate catabolism.
