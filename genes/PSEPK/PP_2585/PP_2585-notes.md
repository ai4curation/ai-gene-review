# PP_2585 curation notes

PP_2585 (Q88JR4) is an unreviewed aldehyde dehydrogenase-family protein with
the KGSADH-specific InterPro assignment IPR044151. UniProt predicts both the
NAD+-coupled RHEA:47152 and NADP+-coupled RHEA:11296 reactions; neither is
supported by a direct PP_2585 assay
[file:PSEPK/PP_2585/PP_2585-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR044151; ALDH_KGSADH."].

The module/pathway/taxon OpenScientist report places PP_2585 outside the local
garD/PP_3599/PP_3602 aldarate locus and notes nearby amino-acid/polyamine
catabolism candidates. This is useful for hypothesis generation but does not
establish a physiological pathway. PP_2585 is therefore retained as an
unresolved KGSADH-family paralog, not assigned to aldarate or polyamine
catabolism
[file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__glucarate_galactarate_catabolism__ppu00053-deep-research-openscientist.md].
