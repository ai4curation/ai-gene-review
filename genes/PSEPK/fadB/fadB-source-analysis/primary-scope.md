## PMID32826213: exact native loci, fitness rather than purified kinetics

Authoritative full article acquired from https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7580535/?report=xml on2026-11-06. It returned article HTML; full bytes and extraction remain in ignored audit cache. The existing publication cache was not refreshed. Figure2 and accompanying text describe KT2440 pooled transposon fitness under fatty-acid growth conditions.

Two short exact excerpts are “fadB homolog PP_2136 showed severe fitness defects” and “primary long-chain thiolase appears to be the fadA homolog PP_2137”. The first paragraph associates FadB with C6+ use and FadA with C8+ use; the separate shorter-substrate discussion qualifies hexanoate defects as moderate and describes redundancy. These are native fitness observations, not substrate-resolved isolated enzyme kinetics. Fitness and the independently curated FadBA subunit architecture support a matched native pair inference, not measured stoichiometry or arbitrary pair interchangeability.

The same primary identifies PP_0368 and PP_0370 as major fatty-acyl-CoA dehydrogenase candidates under the tested conditions. It does not establish PP_1893 as the dominant native entry enzyme. PP_1893 remains a FadE-clade chemical-function inference.

For the phenylacetate-region nomenclature, the primary identifies paaJ as PP_3275. This conflicts with the older review's argument based on a frozen PP_3280 record named paaJ and carrying PcaF-related signatures. The discrepancy is not resolved by renaming or modifying either source. A dedicated PaaJ route and a FadA beta-oxidation role do not constitute a direct negative test of FadA participation in another pathway. GO0010124 is therefore unresolved rather than categorically excluded.

## Epimerase: exact claim versus PHA endpoint

PMID36763117 is a full KT2440 primary study of PHA precursor routes; its statement about absent FadBA epimerase function cites Fiedler2002 (PMID12115060). The accessible12115060 abstract explicitly tests Pseudomonas oleovorans FadBA coexpressed with PHA synthase in E. coli. A failure to efficiently supply R-hydroxyacyl-CoA for polymer accumulation is not equivalent to a negative purified assay of the GO0008692 S/R-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA interconversion on KT2440 Q88L02. No exact sequence/construct bridge or target-specific kinetic exclusion was established. Conversely, positive E. coli epimerase evidence plus HAMAP assignment does not resolve that conflicting comparative literature conclusively. UNDECIDED preserves this distinction without assigning a PHA-production role.
