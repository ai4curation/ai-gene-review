Gene Description

PP_0908 encodes a 365-residue DUF1513 (PF07433; PIRSF028101/UCP028101; COG3490) protein of unknown function. Almost the whole mature chain is one DUF1513 domain, which Gene3D and SUPERFAMILY place in the YVTN-repeat / quinoprotein amine dehydrogenase beta-chain beta-propeller fold. The N-terminus (MLRRQALKLG...) is a hydrophobic export signal with a conserved twin-arginine (Tat-like) RRxxLK motif; DeepTMHMM predicts a cleavable signal peptide at residues 1-25 and no transmembrane segment or beta-barrel. There is no lipobox. The mature protein is therefore most likely a soluble periplasmic protein, although that has not been tested. PP_0908 is the last gene of a tightly packed same-strand cluster, PP_0913-PP_0912-PP_0911-PP_0910-PP_0909-PP_0908. That cluster encodes two exported imelysin-like proteins (PP_0913 and PP_0909, Peptidase_M75 / PF09375) and an exported di-haem oxidoreductase/putative peroxidase (PP_0910, DHOR / PF06537). The arrangement is syntenic with the Fur-regulated P. aeruginosa imelysin operon PA4370-PA4373, in which PA4373 is the 78%-identical DUF1513 orthologue. The cluster has no EfeU/FTR1-family permease. The only FTR1-family protein in KT2440, PP_0180, is encoded elsewhere. The cluster therefore resembles the "outer-membrane" imelysin clusters that lack EfeU, not a complete EfeUOB system. Fitness Browser curators reannotated the P. fluorescens orthologues (81% identity) as the "outer membrane component of uptake system, probably for ferrous iron", because they are cofit with their efeO-like and DHOR neighbours. This family-level inference is also the source of the Pfam DUF1513 description. In KT2440, transposon mutants lose fitness in fusaric acid (fitness -2.9 to -2.0 in six experiments). Fusaric acid chelates metals, which fits an iron-related role. However, PP_0908 lies 155 bp upstream of the co-directional RND efflux genes PP_0907-PP_0906, so a polar effect cannot be excluded. No experiment has been reported on PP_0908 or on any DUF1513 protein. Its molecular function, ligand and substrate are unknown. Association with an iron-acquisition module is a well-supported hypothesis, not an established function.

Core Functions

Exported (Sec/Tat signal peptide), probably periplasmic, beta-propeller DUF1513 protein of unknown molecular function. It is encoded in a conserved, Fur-regulated (in P. aeruginosa) gene cluster with two imelysin-like (EfeO/IrpA-type) proteins and a di-haem putative peroxidase, a module proposed to act in iron acquisition. No GO molecular function, process or location term is assigned, because none is established for this protein or family.

Gap: The molecular function of DUF1513 is unknown. No member of the family has been purified, structurally characterized, or tested for metal, haem or partner-protein binding. Whether the beta-propeller binds iron, binds the imelysin-like partners, or has an enzymatic activity is unknown.

BIOLOGY

It has not been tested in KT2440. The cluster lacks an EfeU/FTR1 permease, so the iron species (Fe2+ or Fe3+) and the inner-membrane route, if any, are unknown.

PP_0908 (Q88PE2), Pseudomonas putida KT2440: research report on the DUF1513 / COG3490 protein

Summary

PP_0908 has never been studied experimentally, and its biochemical function is unknown. Searches of the web and the literature found no publication that names PP_0908 or reports an experiment on any DUF1513 protein. Everything below is inference from the protein's sequence and domains, from the genes around it, and from database classifications.

  • Protein: a 365-residue protein with a cleavable N-terminal signal peptide (an export signal). Almost all of it is a single DUF1513 domain, which is predicted to fold into a ring-shaped "β-propeller" structure.
  • Function: Pfam's curators classify DUF1513 proteins as part of an EfeUOB-like iron uptake system. PP_0908 sits in a tight cluster of genes, PP_0910–PP_0909–PP_0908, whose neighbours encode an EfeO/imelysin-like protein and a protein with cytochrome c and peroxidase-like domains. Together these support a hypothesis that PP_0908 is an accessory part of a system in the periplasm (the space between the two membranes) for taking up or processing ferrous iron.
  • What remains unknown: the reaction it catalyses (if any), what it binds, and whether it binds a cofactor.

Interpreting the fold. A β-propeller fold does not by itself imply an enzyme. The same fold serves as a binding scaffold (WD40 proteins), a lactonase or gluconolactonase, or the substrate-binding subunit of a quinoprotein. KEGG's weaker motif hits ("Lactonase", "choice_anch_I") are consistent with a generic propeller and do not establish an activity. The match to the quinoprotein amine dehydrogenase β-chain is structural only. The quinone cofactor in those enzymes sits on a different subunit, so the match does not imply that PP_0908 is a dehydrogenase.

N-terminus. The protein begins MLRRQALKLGSVLLSALTLGGW…. This is a hydrophobic signal peptide, and RRQALKL loosely resembles the twin-arginine (Tat) consensus (S/T)-R-R-x-F-L-K. This is my own reading of the sequence; no prediction tool was run. Together with GenBank's "exported" annotation, it suggests export across the inner membrane, possibly by the Tat pathway, which exports already-folded proteins that often carry cofactors. A Tat route is plausible but unconfirmed. SignalP 6 or TatP should be run to test it.

Localisation caveat. Pfam calls DUF1513 proteins the "outer membrane component". However, PP_0908 shows no feature of an outer-membrane β-barrel, such as alternating amphipathic β-strands or a C-terminal aromatic signature. Its fold is a soluble propeller. The most defensible prediction is a soluble periplasmic protein, or at most one loosely attached to a membrane. Its outer-membrane location should be treated as unverified.

The strong phylogenetic co-occurrence score (0.775) means that PP_0908, PP_0909 and PP_0910 orthologues tend to be present or absent together across genomes. That pattern is a hallmark of a functional module, not just neighbouring genes.

Why this points to iron.
- The M75 partner. Peptidase M75 (imelysin) domains define EfeO. EfeO is the periplasmic, cupredoxin-associated component of the EfeUOB ferrous iron transporter, which in B. subtilis is essential for high-affinity uptake of ferrous and ferric iron (Miethke et al. 2013, BBA 1833:2267, PMID:23764491; this is one of the two references Pfam cites for DUF1513). The same family includes P19/FtrA, the copper-binding protein that oxidises iron in the bacterial Ftr system.
- The Ftr model in Rubrivivax gelatinosus. Steunou et al. 2022 propose that the copper protein FtrA (P19-like) oxidises Fe²⁺ while reducing its bound copper. The ferric iron then passes via FtrB to the permease FtrC, and the electrons are recycled by FtrD, which acts as an electron sink (Steunou et al. 2022, FEBS J 289:6286, doi:10.1111/febs.16476; structures PDB 7R4Z, 7R5P). The equivalent Bordetella system, FtrABCD (Brickman & Armstrong 2012, Mol Microbiol; PubMed 23647104), is iron- and pH-responsive and required for ferrous iron use under acidic conditions (ECU theses on the Brucella/Bordetella Ftr system).
- The di-haem cytochrome c partner. PP_0910's domains suggest a periplasmic redox protein. In this kind of module it could plausibly carry electrons released by iron oxidation, similar to the electron-sink role proposed for FtrD.

Important differences from the classic systems. KT2440 has no annotated EfeO (K07224) or EfeB/DyP peroxidase (K16301). Its only FTR1/EfeU-family permease, PP_0180 (K07243), is encoded far from this cluster. So the PP_0908–PP_0910 cluster is at most an EfeUOB-like or Ftr-like periplasmic module with a non-canonical makeup. No paper has linked PP_0908 to any of the defined components (EfeO, EfeB, FtrA, FtrB, FtrD) one-to-one.

Implications for GO annotation

  • No experimental GO terms are justified.
  • If annotating at all, use the cellular-component term periplasmic space (GO:0042597), and only after a signal-peptide prediction tool confirms export. A process term such as iron ion transport would rest only on family-level inference plus operon context. It should be treated as a hypothesis, not proposed as a new annotation, unless fitness data in KT2440 (Fitness Browser) support it.
  • Do not transfer quinoprotein dehydrogenase, lactonase or peptidase activities from the structural superfamily matches. Peptidase_M75 is a domain of the neighbouring PP_0909 protein, not of PP_0908.

6. Suggested experiments and open questions

  1. Fitness data: look up PP_0908, PP_0909, PP_0910 and PP_0180 in the Fitness Browser (organism "Putida"). Check for co-fitness and for phenotypes under iron limitation, chelators such as 2,2′-dipyridyl, low pH, or copper stress.
  2. Gene deletions: measure growth of ΔPP_0908 and Δ(PP_0908–0910) strains with Fe²⁺ (plus ascorbate) as the sole iron source at acidic pH, with and without a pvd (pyoverdine) background. Pyoverdine is P. putida's main siderophore and would otherwise mask a defect in ferrous uptake.
  3. Localisation: fractionate PhoA/GFP fusions or use cell-fractionation with immunoblotting. Test Tat dependence in a ΔtatC strain.
  4. Biochemistry: purify the mature protein and test whether it binds metals (Fe, Cu) and whether it interacts with PP_0909 and PP_0910, for example by pull-down or crosslinking.
  5. Regulation: test whether the PP_0910–0908 operon is induced by iron limitation through Fur.

Conclusion

PP_0908 is an exported DUF1513 beta-propeller protein of unknown molecular function. It is encoded in a conserved imelysin / di-haem peroxidase gene cluster that is plausibly involved in iron acquisition. Neither the iron link nor the location has been tested for any DUF1513 protein.

  • Synteny with P. aeruginosa. The cluster matches PA4370 (IcmP imelysin), PA4371 (DHOR), PA4372 (imelysin), PA4373 (DUF1513) and PA4374–PA4375 (RND). Xu et al. 2011 (PMID:21799754) call PA4370–PA4373 the "Fur-regulated imelysin operon".
  • Is it an EfeUOB/FTR1 system? Not exactly.
  • There is no EfeU/FTR1 permease in the cluster. The only FTR1-family protein in KT2440 is PP_0180 (cytochrome c + FTR1), which is encoded elsewhere.
  • There is no DyP-type EfeB (PF04261) in KT2440. The "efeB-like" partner is the DHOR protein PP_0910.
  • The "efeO-like" partners are imelysin-only proteins with no cupredoxin domain.
  • This is the EfeU-less, "outer-membrane" type of imelysin cluster described by Xu et al. The substrate of this type, Fe2+ or Fe3+, is unknown.

Signal peptide

  • Predictions. DeepTMHMM (dashboard) predicts SP 1–25, with no TM segment and no beta-barrel. UniProt's SignalP run gives no signal feature.
  • Tat-like motif. The N-terminus is MLRRQALKLGSVLLSALTLGGW, with an RRQALK twin-arginine-like motif. It is conserved in PA4373 (MLRRHVIGLG). PROSITE Tat profiles hit the 32.4% and 32.6% identical Silicibacter and Rhizobium homologues.
  • No lipobox. There is no Cys in the first 30 residues.
  • Location. The most likely location is the periplasm. The Pfam / Fitness Browser "outer membrane component" label is not supported by the architecture.

Fitness

  • just fetch-fitness failed. The Fitness Browser returned 403 and there is no local FEBA database.
  • The dashboard's embedded data (frozen in PP_0908-dashboard-prediction-evidence/dashboard_call.json) show KT2440 fitness of −2.93 to −1.97 (|t| 4.2–7.2) in six R2A + fusaric acid 480 µg/mL experiments.
  • Fusaric acid is a metal chelator, but it is probably also an efflux substrate. PP_0908 sits directly upstream of the co-directional RND pump PP_0907–PP_0906, so polar effects on that pump are a real alternative explanation.
  • Orthologue phenotypes are pleiotropic (P. fluorescens GW456-L13: vancomycin, Zn, Ni, D-cycloserine), with no iron-limitation condition shown.

  • No experimental paper on PP_0908, PA4373 or any DUF1513 protein was found (deep research report plus Europe PMC searches).
