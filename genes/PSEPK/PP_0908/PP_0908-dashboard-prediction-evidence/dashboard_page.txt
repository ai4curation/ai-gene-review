why
Curated homologs at 63.0–81.4% identity associate DUF1513 with probable ferrous iron uptake and conserved EfeO-like/EfeB-like partners; corresponding iron-related neighbors recur around the query. Query fitness decreases under fusaric acid exposure (−2.93 to −1.97), supporting stress-related importance without identifying a transported substrate. Query topology predicts a cleavable signal peptide at residues 1–25 and no membrane-spanning segments, supporting a soluble exported component. Ferrous iron specificity remains putative; no catalytic activity or EC number is established.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by reproducible stress-specific fitness data for a 100%-identity homolog, supplemented by curated uptake-system assignments and conserved iron-related neighborhoods. InterProScan and STRING support DUF1513/UCP028101 family membership; manuscript homologs provide expression or regulatory observations without biochemical characterization. The curated outer membrane assignment is constrained by query topology indicating a soluble exported protein; integral membrane transport and amine dehydrogenase annotations are unsupported.

why these terms
The protein is described as 'associated with a putative ferrous iron uptake system', which supports a role in iron ion transport (BP). It is specifically linked to 'ferrous iron', suggesting ferrous iron binding (MF), although general iron ion binding is also supported. The protein is described as a 'soluble exported' protein, and the trace notes that it is 'constrained by query topology indicating a soluble exported protein' and rejects an 'outer membrane' assignment. In the context of a bacterial uptake system, a soluble exported protein is typically located in the periplasm.
