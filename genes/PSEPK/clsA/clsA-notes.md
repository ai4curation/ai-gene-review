# clsA curation notes

Reviewed UniProt Q88C19 is cardiolipin synthase A. It contains two PLD
phosphodiesterase domains and is assigned the PG+PG cardiolipin reaction
RHEA:31451 [UniProtKB:Q88C19, "Reaction=2 a 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phospho-(1'-sn-glycerol) = a"].

P. putida KT2440 lipidomics detects cardiolipin and the pathway reconstruction
includes two cardiolipin synthase loci [PMID:21895997, "cls, cls‐2 – cardiolipin synthase"].
This supports the pathway context but does not resolve the condition-specific
division of labor among KT2440 paralogs.

The exact synthase activity, cardiolipin biosynthesis, and inner-membrane
location are appropriate core annotations. Comparative deletion lipidomics is
needed to distinguish ClsA from ClsB and candidate ClsC-like enzymes.
