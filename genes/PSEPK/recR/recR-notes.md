# recR curation notes

## 2026-08-08 pathway pass

The Q88F32 HAMAP record assigns RecR to RecBC-independent recombinational repair
and states that it may act with RecF and RecO [file:PSEPK/recR/recR-uniprot.txt,
"It may act with RecF and RecO."]. P. aeruginosa structural work directly
describes RecR complexes with RecF and RecO that facilitate RecA loading
[PMID:29633970, "It forms complexes with RecF and RecO that can facilitate the
loading of RecA onto ssDNA in the RecFOR pathway."].

GO:0000730 is proposed by ISS from reviewed E. coli RecR UniProtKB:P0A7H6, with
the genus-level paper supplying independent mechanistic support.
