# sdhA notes

## OpenScientist adjudication

The sdhA OpenScientist report identified Q88FA7/PP_4191 as the succinate
dehydrogenase flavoprotein subunit and captured the SdhABCD Complex II
architecture, but its active-site, flavinylation, and SdhE mechanistic
statements are transferred from E. coli/Serratia biochemistry and conserved
domain annotations.

The report is useful as homology- and operon-level corroboration, not as direct
P. putida KT2440 biochemical evidence. Flavinylation by SdhE is conserved in
bacteria and annotated by UniProt, but remains a useful KT2440-specific
experimental check.
