# hutU curation notes

- Q88CZ6 is a reviewed urocanate hydratase (EC 4.2.1.49) converting trans-urocanate to 4-imidazolone-5-propanoate. [file:PSEPK/hutU/hutU-uniprot.txt, "Reaction=4-imidazolone-5-propanoate = trans-urocanate + H2O"]
- The record assigns one bound NAD+ per subunit and places HutU at step 2/3 from histidine to N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate. [file:PSEPK/hutU/hutU-uniprot.txt, "Note=Binds 1 NAD(+) per subunit."] [file:PSEPK/hutU/hutU-uniprot.txt, "N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate from L-histidine: step 2/3."]
- Local PANTHER data place Q88CZ6 in the urocanate-hydratase-specific subfamily PTHR12216:SF4. [file:interpro/panther/PTHR12216/PTHR12216-entries.csv, "Q88CZ6,Urocanate hydratase"]
- Same-species cloning and expression produced catalytically active HutU.
  [PMID:7901006 "produced a catalytically active protein"]
- P. putida urocanase mechanism experiments support water addition rather than
  an internal oxidation-reduction reaction, while showing that NAD+ is
  required. [PMID:6110440 "water addition across the conjugated double bond
  system of urocanate, rather than an internal oxidation--reduction process,
  yet NAD+ is required"]
- The 1.14 A P. putida structure is a symmetric homodimer with tightly bound
  NAD+ in each subunit. [PMID:15313616 "The enzyme is a symmetric homodimer of
  2 x 557 amino acid residues with tightly bound NAD+ cofactors."]
- The P. putida hut-locus study identifies hutU as urocanase and reports
  urocanate-inducible expression. [PMID:2842309 "hutU (which encodes urocanase)
  expression was induced by urocanate"]
- These primary records independently verify the OpenScientist synthesis,
  provide direct evidence anchors for GO:0016153, and support a new GO:0051287
  NAD binding annotation.
