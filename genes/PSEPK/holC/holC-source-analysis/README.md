# KT2440 chi–psi identity and construct mapping

PMID21955458 explicitly amplified holC and holD from P. putida KT2440 and sequenced the cloned genes. Its official Additional file9 primers are preserved as PDF and unmodified text extraction. Each primer annealing suffix maps uniquely to the full official AE015451.2 chromosome; coordinates and strand are in identity-proof.json. Stop codons were changed from TGA to TAA without changing terminal amino acids.

HolC is PP_0979, AAN66604.1, Q88P74: its full142-residue translation exactly matches frozen UniProt. The psi primers map PP_0696, AAN66321.1, Q88Q02, not neighboring PP_0978. The current Q88Q02 annotation has291 residues. The primary expression insert begins at the second codon after the corrected TTG start at809524 and corresponds to current residues32–291 (260 native residues), expressed as a GST fusion and cleaved during purification. The corrected native protein is261 residues, initiating with methionine at that TTG. The old upstream30 residues were not assayed. Do not describe the experiment as testing the full291-residue database protein, and do not silently replace the immutable UniProt record.

The selected genomic regions are transparently derived from the official record and retain exact reference coordinates and the original-file hash. Reproduce the retained-region protein/construct checks with `uv run python genes/PSEPK/holC/holC-source-analysis/verify_identity.py`. The whole-chromosome primer uniqueness computation and full downloaded chromosome are logged in `.cache/module-audit-logs/chromosomal-replication-20261106/`.

PMID21955458 directly demonstrates KT2440 chi–psi ssDNA binding. Mechanistic chimeras and psi truncation in the P. aeruginosa/E. coli comparison localize direct DNA binding to psi; a complex-level measurement is not standalone HolC DNA-binding evidence. The chi protein bridges SSB and the loader; psi stabilizes and connects the chi accessory assembly to the minimal DnaX–delta–delta-prime loader. The latter mechanistic transfer to KT2440 is explicit, rather than claiming every assay was performed on KT2440.
