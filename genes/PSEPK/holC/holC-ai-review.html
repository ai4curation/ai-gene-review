Gene Description

HolC is the chi accessory subunit of the Pseudomonas putida KT2440 DNA polymerase III clamp-loader system. It forms a complex with the psi subunit and is inferred to couple SSB-coated templates to the replicase through the conserved chi–SSB interface. The KT2440 chi–psi complex binds single-stranded DNA, although the corresponding direct DNA-binding activity is assigned to psi in mechanistic studies of Pseudomonas homologs. HolC supports efficient replication without being a universally conserved minimal-loader subunit.
