# ttg2D curation notes

- Q88P91 is structurally characterized and belongs to the exact MlaC PANTHER subfamily [file:PSEPK/ttg2D/ttg2D-uniprot.txt "PANTHER; PTHR36573:SF1; INTERMEMBRANE PHOSPHOLIPID TRANSPORT SYSTEM BINDING PROTEIN MLAC"].
- The local Ttg2D name is retained while the module records its MlaC-family periplasmic carrier role.
- There are no existing GOA rows; phospholipid binding, intermembrane transfer, and periplasmic localization are synthesized core assertions.
- OpenScientist identifies PDB 4FCZ and lipid-bound PDB 5UWB as structures of
  Q88P91 itself, providing direct target-level support for a lipid-binding
  cavity [file:PSEPK/ttg2D/ttg2D-deep-research-openscientist.md
  "Direct structural evidence for the target protein (PDB 5UWB)"].
- The quantitative lipid stoichiometry and promiscuity derive from the
  P. aeruginosa ortholog, and the report states that direction is unresolved
  specifically in P. putida. These are not promoted to target-specific core
  claims [file:PSEPK/ttg2D/ttg2D-deep-research-openscientist.md
  "Transport directionality not settled organism-specifically"].
