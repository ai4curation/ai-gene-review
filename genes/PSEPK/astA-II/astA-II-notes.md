# astA-II curation notes

- Q88EI2 carries the alpha-chain-specific signatures `IPR017651` and
  `TIGR03245`, supporting assignment as the alpha-like component of split AstA
  [file:PSEPK/astA-II/astA-II-uniprot.txt
  "InterPro; IPR017651; Arg/Orn_succinylTfrase_asu";
  "NCBIfam; TIGR03245; arg_AOST_alph"].
- Reproducible global alignments independently support that assignment: Q88EI2
  is 85.8% identical across aligned residues to Pseudomonas aeruginosa alpha
  chain P80357, versus 40.4% to beta chain P80358
  [file:PSEPK/astA-I/astA-I-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md].
- The homologous purified Pseudomonas enzyme contains two nonidentical chain
  types and is heterotetrameric [PMID:7523119 "After SDS/PAGE two subunits of
  35 kDa and 37 kDa were evident, indicating that the enzyme is a
  heterotetramer."]. Therefore Q88EI2 contributes to, rather than independently
  enables, GO:0008791.
