PF25226 Domain of unknown function (DUF7844)
This domain is found in uncharacterised bacterial proteins. It is predicted to fold onto a structure with significant similarity to Glucose-regulated metallo-peptidase M90.  This domain is frequently found in combination with [pfam:PF13387].

PF13387 Lnb N-terminal periplasmic domain
This domain, previously known as DUF4105, is found N-terminal in Lipoprotein N-acyltransferase Lnb from Bacteroides and related uncharacterised bacterial proteins. Lnb functions as a lipoprotein N-acyltransferase in its native cell background [[cite:PUB00158892]]. It is composed of two domains, N-terminal (this entry) and C-terminal alpha- helical transmembrane domain. The domain represented by this entry contains a highly conserved histidine residue and a well-conserved NCT motif. Its predicted structure has a significant similarity to the papain-like cysteine peptidases. In addition, the putative catalytic triad Asn-His-Cys is highly conserved. This domain is also found in combination with other domains.
