# Results

The Table S2 lysC_xba_R reverse primer matches the terminal PARQGE of PP_4473/Q88EI9, followed by a stop codon. The hom_nhe_R reverse primer matches terminal RVEQLN of Hom/PP_1470/Q88MU8, followed by a stop. It does not match PP_0664/Q88Q34. The thrB_ecoRI_F primer matches MSVFTPVT at the N terminus of ThrB/PP_0121/Q88RK8, and thrB_not_R matches its terminal LHLPFAL. The thrC_ecor_F primer matches MRYISTR at the N terminus of ThrC/PP_1471/Q88MU7; thrC_xba_R matches terminal HGNRGKPL. Neither terminal ThrC primer matches PP_0662/Q88Q36.

The supplementary caption reports theoretical sizes of 44.6, 46.2, 51.6 and 35.2 kDa for LysC, Hom, ThrC and ThrB, consistent with these four proteins. Taken with native KT2440 cloning in the main text, the independent terminal matches distinguish the named targets from the shorter Hom and alternative ThrC-like paralogs. This is a terminal-primer identity bridge, not verification of the entire cloned insert. SDS-PAGE of soluble/total extracts shows recombinant production; it does not measure purified-enzyme catalysis, cofactor preference or feedback kinetics.

Native LysC/Hom overproduction failed to increase threonine in the tested engineered context. Native ThrB/ThrC coexpression with heterologous feedback-resistant E. coli ThrA increased production in the single-plasmid context. These are joint engineering results, not individual knockout necessity or paralog-specific rescue. The paper's statement that bifunctional ThrA complements LysC, Asd and Hom overstates its chemistry: ThrA supplies aspartokinase and homoserine dehydrogenase, not aspartate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase.
