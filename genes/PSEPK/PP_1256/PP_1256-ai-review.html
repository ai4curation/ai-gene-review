Gene Description

PP_1256 is a predicted alpha-ketoglutaric-semialdehyde dehydrogenase-family enzyme and candidate terminal 2,5-dioxopentanoate oxidoreductase in the KT2440 hydroxyproline-catabolism locus. Its KGSADH-family assignment supports alpha-ketoglutaric-semialdehyde chemistry, while its cofactor preference and contribution relative to the other KT2440 paralogs remain unresolved.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

2,5-dioxovalerate dehydrogenase [NAD(P)+] activity

Definition: Catalysis of 2,5-dioxopentanoate oxidation to 2-oxoglutarate using either NAD+ or NADP+ as electron acceptor.

Justification: GO:0047533 specifies NADP+, while bacterial KGSADH orthologs and the KT2440 records support the same substrate-specific reaction with either cofactor.

Parent term: aldehyde dehydrogenase [NAD(P)+] activity

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:17202142 : KGSADH-II and KGSADH-III showed similar high substrate specificity for alphaKGSA and different coenzyme specificity

PP_1256 curation notes

  • Q88NF5 is an EC 1.2.1.26 alpha-ketoglutaric-semialdehyde dehydrogenase-family
    protein predicted to use NAD+ or NADP+
    [file:PSEPK/PP_1256/PP_1256-uniprot.txt, "RecName: Full=2,5-dioxovalerate dehydrogenase"].
  • Its physical linkage to PP_1255, PP_1257, and proR makes this paralog the
    leading terminal-step candidate, but PP_2585 and PP_3602 carry the same EC
    assignment and no target-specific experiment has resolved pathway flux.
  • The substrate-specific GO:0047533 annotation is accepted; broad
    oxidoreductase mappings are marked as over-annotations.
