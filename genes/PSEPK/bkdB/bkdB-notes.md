# bkdB curation notes

- Q88EQ0/PP_4403 is the lipoyl-bearing BCKDH E2 component. The exact PANTHER
  subfamily is BCKDH-specific, and the lpdV OpenScientist report identifies
  BkdB as E2 in the `bkdAA-bkdAB-bkdB-lpdV` operon
  [file:PSEPK/lpdV/lpdV-deep-research-openscientist.md, "**E2 dihydrolipoyl
  transacylase (bkdB)**"].
- Generic acetyltransferase/acyltransferase annotations are modified to
  GO:0043754, dihydrolipoamide branched chain acyltransferase activity.
