# sucD (Q88FB3, PP_4185) notes

## Retrospective audit corrections (2026-11-12)

A retrospective audit of the succinyl-CoA synthetase reviews found the points below. This file did not exist before; earlier reasoning is in the review YAML and its history records.

- GO:0004776 succinate-CoA ligase (GDP-forming) activity (IEA): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE changed back to ACCEPT. The earlier reason called GDP formation a secondary activity "as for E. coli SucD". That transferred the E. coli kinetic ranking to an enzyme for which no ranking is known. The family rule records synthesis of either nucleotide [file:PSEPK/sucD/sucD-uniprot.txt "coupling the hydrolysis of succinyl-CoA to the synthesis of either ATP or GTP"], and specificity belongs to the beta subunit [file:PSEPK/sucD/sucD-uniprot.txt "nucleotide specificity is provided by the beta subunit"]. The nearest assayed enzyme is P. aeruginosa SucCD [PMID:10671455 "Scs synthesizes essentially an equimolar amount of ATP and GTP"]. Identity computed from UniProt sequences by global alignment (BLOSUM62): alpha subunit Q88FB3 against P. aeruginosa Q51567 is 287 of 294 residues (98%); beta subunit Q88FB2 against P. aeruginosa P53593 is 363 of 388 (94%) and against E. coli P0A836 298 of 388 (77%).
- Limits of the P. aeruginosa result. Only the abstract was read; the full text is not cached. The abstract gives an end-point result and no kinetic constants, and it says the enzyme copurifies with nucleoside diphosphate kinase [PMID:10671455 "Scs was previously shown to copurify with Ndk, presumably as a complex."]. The relative use of ADP and GDP by the P. putida enzyme is unknown, so neither activity is ranked above the other.
- core_functions now has two entries, GO:0004775 and GO:0004776, both as contributes_to_molecular_function, with in_complex GO:0042709 so that both subunits name the same complex. Both entries now carry locations GO:0005829 cytosol, to match the partner subunit SucC and the E. coli subunits. This gene has no cytosol annotation, so the location is an inference from the soluble SucC/SucD complex and the absence of signal or transmembrane features in SucD, and the core function description says so (an earlier version of this line said no location is given).
- GO:0042709 succinate-CoA ligase complex: TOO_GENERAL changed back to ACCEPT. The generic term is the one that covers an enzyme forming ATP or GTP. The earlier reason said the TOO_GENERAL action matched the other reviewed members of the family; it did not match the partner subunit SucC. QuickGO (checked 2026-11-12) shows PseudoCAP annotated the P. aeruginosa subunits to GO:0042709 by IDA from PMID:10671455. GO:0009361 stays ACCEPT; the enzyme forms ATP.
- GO:0003824 catalytic activity: MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED changed to TOO_GENERAL. The term is true and is entailed by the accepted ligase activities.
- Two findings on the UniProt reference quoted CATALYTIC ACTIVITY lines whose only evidence is an ARBA rule. They now quote the HAMAP-rule FUNCTION statement.
