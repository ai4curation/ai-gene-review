# polA curation notes

- Q88RK6 is a full-length bacterial DNA polymerase I with polymerase,
  3-prime-to-5-prime exonuclease, and 5-prime-to-3-prime exonuclease activities
  [file:PSEPK/polA/polA-uniprot.txt,
  "exhibits 3'-5' and 5'-3' exonuclease activity"].
- The PTHR10133 family label supports DNA polymerase I, but PTHR10133:SF27 is
  labeled DNA polymerase nu. The module therefore requires the exact polymerase
  MF and does not use SF27 as a selector.
- The available target evidence does not establish direct double-strand-break
  repair involvement; that process annotation is retained as over-annotated.
