# liuC curation notes

- UniProt evidence: UniProt identifies Q88FM3 as methylglutaconyl-CoA hydratase. [file:PSEPK/liuC/liuC-uniprot.txt "SubName: Full=Methylglutaconyl-CoA hydratase"].
- A structurally characterized bacterial ortholog belongs to the 3-methylglutaconyl-CoA hydratase family, supporting the reaction-specific assignment beyond the broad crotonase fold. [PMID:27271456 "LiuC, a member of the 3-methylglutaconyl CoA hydratases (MGCH)."].
- Comparative growth evidence shows that P. putida uses leucine and isovalerate. [PMID:16820476 "while both species were able to utilize leucine and isovalerate."].
- The same study reports that P. putida does not grow on the tested acyclic terpenes, so the electronic isoprenoid-catabolic-process annotation is removed rather than transferred from bifunctional P. aeruginosa Liu machinery. [PMID:16820476 "P. fluorescens, but not P. putida, could grow on acyclic terpenes"].
