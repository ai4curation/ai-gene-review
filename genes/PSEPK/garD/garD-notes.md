# garD curation notes

- Q88GW6 has concordant GarD-specific evidence: EC 4.2.1.42, TIGR03248,
  IPR017654, PTHR30536:SF1, and PTN002021951
  [file:PSEPK/garD/garD-uniprot.txt, "RecName: Full=Galactarate dehydratase"].
- The D-galacturonate-process annotation is kept as non-core. KT2440 Udh was
  biochemically characterized with galacturonate and supplies D-galactarate to
  GarD through the oxidative uronate route
  [PMID:19060141, "For all three enzymes, the turnover number (k(cat)) with
  glucuronate as a substrate was higher than that with galacturonate"].
- The generic lyase term is true but adds no useful information beyond the
  substrate-specific molecular function.
