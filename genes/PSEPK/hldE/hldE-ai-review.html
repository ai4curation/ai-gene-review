Gene Description

HldE is a bifunctional enzyme in ADP-heptose precursor biosynthesis. Its N-terminal kinase phosphorylates D-glycero-D-manno-heptose 7-phosphate at C-1, and its C-terminal adenylyltransferase converts the resulting heptose 1-phosphate to ADP-D-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose. Any subsequent C-6 epimerization to the L,D stereoisomer is performed by a separate HldD enzyme.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

ADP-D-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose biosynthetic process

Definition: The chemical reactions and pathways resulting in the formation of ADP-D-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose.

Justification: HldE forms the D,D-heptose nucleotide sugar directly, whereas the only specific GO process term names the downstream L,D stereoisomer. KT2440 lacks a resolved HldD ortholog, so the D,D endpoint must be representable without asserting terminal C-6 epimerization.

Parent term: nucleotide-sugar biosynthetic process

hldE curation notes

  • Q88D93 is a bifunctional HldE whose kinase domain forms
    D-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose 1,7-bisphosphate and whose
    adenylyltransferase domain forms ADP-D-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose
    [file:PSEPK/hldE/hldE-uniprot.txt, "yielding
    ADP-D-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose"].
  • The target-genome analysis found no credible HldD/GmhD ortholog by KEGG,
    UniProt/UniRef, RefSeq, or genome-level similarity search
    [file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__adp-heptose-biosynthesis__ppu00541-deep-research-openscientist.md,
    "is a genome-confirmed GAP in KT2440"]. No broad NAD-dependent epimerase is
    assigned to fill this step.
  • GO:0097171 names the downstream ADP-L,D-heptose endpoint and remains
    UNDECIDED. GO has no process term for the directly produced ADP-D,D-heptose
    endpoint, so a new child of GO:0009226 is proposed without inventing an id.
