# ureB curation notes

## 2026-08-11

- Q88J05 is reviewed UniProt UreB/PP_2844, the 105-aa beta structural chain of
  the UreABC complex [`ureB-uniprot.txt`, "Urease subunit beta"].
- The historical review at `86cf4fd8e9` was used only for comparison. Its
  complex membership survives current review, but the individual `enables`
  qualifier for urease activity does not.
- Structural evidence places urease chemistry in the alpha/UreC binickel site,
  while UreB is part of the alpha-beta-gamma assembly
  [PMID:8718850, "Urease ... is an (alpha beta gamma)3 trimer"].
- Q88J05 is curated as a contributor to `GO:0009039`, a member of
  `GO:0035550`, and a direct participant in urea catabolism.
