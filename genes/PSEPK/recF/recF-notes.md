# recF curation notes

## 2026-08-08 pathway pass

The Q88RW7 HAMAP record states that RecF preferentially binds single-stranded
linear DNA and is required for normal replication and SOS inducibility
[file:PSEPK/recF/recF-uniprot.txt, "RecF binds preferentially to single-stranded,
linear DNA."]. RecF is a mediator rather than a repair DNA polymerase, so the
imported repair-synthesis annotation is removed.

Comparative E. coli genetics separates RecJ/RecQ gap extension from RecFOR-
dependent RecA loading [PMID:35653392, "the loading of RecA (mediated by
RecFOR)"]. GO:0000730 is proposed by ISS from reviewed E. coli RecF
UniProtKB:P0A7H0, not from the module assembled during this curation.
