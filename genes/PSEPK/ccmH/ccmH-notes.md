# ccmH literature notes

- CcmH contributes stereospecificity to thiol-disulfide processing while CcmG contributes reduction efficiency [PMID:28634234 "CcmH ensures stereo-specificity"].
- CcmH participates with CcmF and CcmI in a membrane-integral multisubunit heme-ligation complex [PMID:18753134 "membrane-integral multisubunit heme ligation complex"].
