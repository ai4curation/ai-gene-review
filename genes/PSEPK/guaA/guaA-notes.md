# guaA review notes

Q88P21 is the KT2440 glutamine-hydrolyzing GMP synthase by its exact UniProt
record. The corresponding bacterial gene-product relationship is directly
established in E. coli: [PMID:3894345, "gene, catalyzes the synthesis of GMP from XMP."] This is homolog-level support, not direct experimental evidence for Q88P21.

TreeGrafter-only cytosol is retained as non-core. The exact UniProt record has
no subcellular-location statement, so no core location is asserted.
