why
The 89.7%-identical P. putida homolog is identified as T6SS effector Tke2, although its reported experiment measures Rsm protein binding rather than catalytic activity. Query domains include PAAR, RHS repeats, an Rhs-associated core and a C-terminal LHH domain; STRING associations with VgrG proteins corroborate T6SS involvement. No substrate-specific fitness data or direct nuclease characterization is supplied. The prePAAR motif label alone does not establish DNA deaminase activity.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by convergence of the close homolog's T6SS effector identification, query PAAR/RHS domain architecture and independent STRING associations with VgrG proteins. Available evidence includes computational and experimental homolog summaries, domain annotations, neighborhoods, STRING and topology predictions; the Fitness Browser hit supplies only a YD-repeat annotation, without fitness measurements or cofitness. Specific WHH nuclease and DNA deaminase assignments are rejected because neither catalytic activity nor substrate specificity is experimentally established, and the query's terminal domain is labeled LHH. The comparative adhesin assignment has weaker support than the convergent T6SS evidence.

The protein is explicitly identified as a 'type VI secretion system effector protein'. This supports the biological process of protein secretion by the type VI secretion system (GO:0033103), as effectors are the cargo of this system. The reasoning trace explicitly rejects specific molecular functions, such as WHH nuclease activity and DNA deaminase activity, stating they are not experimentally established. No other molecular functions or cellular components are supported by the text.
