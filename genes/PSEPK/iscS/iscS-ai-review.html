Gene Description

IscS is a pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent cysteine desulfurase that mobilizes sulfur from cysteine for delivery to multiple acceptor pathways, including iron-sulfur cluster assembly, cofactor biosynthesis, and tRNA thiolation. In the MnmA branch, IscS supplies sulfur upstream of the Tus carrier relay.
