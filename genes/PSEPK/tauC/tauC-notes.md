# tauC curation notes

- Q88RA2 is submitted as the TauABC permease
  [file:PSEPK/tauC/tauC-uniprot.txt, "SubName: Full=Taurine ABC transporter
  permease subunit"].
- The exact record predicts a multi-pass cell-membrane protein
  [file:PSEPK/tauC/tauC-uniprot.txt, "Multi-pass membrane protein"].
- TauC contributes to, but does not independently enable, the complex-level
  taurine transporter activity. The broad membrane and transmembrane-transport
  annotations are therefore not used as core terms.
- A sulfur-starvation response is plausible for Tau systems but remains
  undecided for the exact KT2440 protein without direct expression evidence.
