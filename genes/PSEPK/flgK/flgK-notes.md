# flgK curation notes

- Q88ES2 is the KT2440 FlgK hook-associated protein 1 and a structural component of the hook-filament junction. [file:PSEPK/flgK/flgK-uniprot.txt, "RecName: Full=Flagellar hook-associated protein 1"]
- Comparative structural work supports a conserved axial-protein scaffold, but the available crystal structure is from *Burkholderia pseudomallei*, not Q88ES2. [PMID:25645451, summarized in file:PSEPK/flgK/flgK-deep-research-openscientist.md]
- FlgK and FlgL connect the hook to the filament and are required late in flagellar assembly. [PMID:9168617, summarized in file:PSEPK/flgK/flgK-deep-research-openscientist.md]
- FlgN-dependent export and the post-hook substrate switch are conserved mechanistic evidence, not direct KT2440 FlgK assays. [PMID:10320579; PMID:10478459, summarized in file:PSEPK/flgK/flgK-deep-research-openscientist.md]
- The review therefore retains structural molecule activity, bacterial-type flagellum assembly, and hook-filament-junction localization while keeping the lack of direct Q88ES2 experiments explicit.
