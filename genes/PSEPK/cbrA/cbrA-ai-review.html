Gene Description

cbrA (PP_4695) encodes the sensor histidine kinase of the CbrA/CbrB two-component system, an atypical integral cytoplasmic-membrane protein in which an N-terminal solute/sodium symporter (SLC5) domain of about 13 transmembrane helices is joined through a STAC linker to cytoplasmic PAS, DHp and CA kinase domains. CbrA autophosphorylates on His766 and transfers the phosphoryl group to the receiver aspartate of its cognate response regulator CbrB; no CbrA phosphatase activity toward CbrB~P has been detected. The SLC5 domain specifically binds and takes up L-histidine, driven by the proton gradient, but L-histidine does not alter the kinase activities in vitro and the proximal signal sensed by CbrA remains unknown. Through CbrB, CbrA drives sigma-54-dependent expression of the CrcZ and CrcY small RNAs that antagonise Hfq/Crc translational repression, so the CbrA/CbrB system controls carbon catabolite repression and the balance of carbon and nitrogen utilization in pseudomonads and related Pseudomonadaceae.
