# aacs review notes

Q88IC8 is an unreviewed TrEMBL entry (PE 3). EC 6.2.1.16, InterPro, NCBIfam,
and PANTHER converge on acetoacetate-CoA ligase chemistry, but direct activity
or pathway-flux evidence for the exact KT2440 protein was not found in the
completed evidence set.

Purified *Zoogloea ramigera* acetoacetyl-CoA synthetase requires ATP and CoA and
produces acetoacetyl-CoA, AMP, and pyrophosphate [PMID:7140777, "The enzyme
absolutely required ATP, CoA"; "for the activation of acetoacetate, yielding
acetoacetyl-CoA, AMP and pyrophosphate in equimolar amounts."]. In
*Sinorhizobium meliloti*, acetoacetyl-CoA synthetase rather than CoA transferase
activates acetoacetate in the PHB cycle [PMID:10735852, "These findings suggest
that acetoacetyl-CoA synthetase, rather than CoA transferase, activates
acetoacetate to acetoacetyl-CoA in the S. meliloti poly-3-hydroxybutyrate
cycle."]. These studies support the reusable bacterial reaction, not a direct
KT2440 pathway annotation.

The broad lipid metabolic process IEA is marked over-annotated because the
family mapping does not establish lipid metabolism for Q88IC8. Evidence for
mammalian AACS in lipogenesis is a separate lineage-specific context and is not
transferred to this bacterial candidate.
