Gene Description

Ech (PP_3358) is the feruloyl-CoA hydratase/lyase of the non-beta-oxidative hydroxycinnamate catabolic route of Pseudomonas putida KT2440. A member of the enoyl-CoA hydratase/isomerase (crotonase) superfamily, it hydrates the double bond of feruloyl-CoA and cleaves the resulting beta-hydroxy thioester by a retro-aldol reaction to vanillin and acetyl-CoA. It acts after the feruloyl-CoA synthetase Fcs and supplies vanillin to the vanillin dehydrogenase Vdh; the three genes are clustered, and inactivation of ech abolishes growth on ferulic acid. Crystal structures of the KT2440 protein have been solved in complex with CoA.

ech curation notes

Ech is the PP_3358 hydroxycinnamoyl-CoA hydratase/lyase between Fcs and Vdh in
the KT2440 ferulate route. The target entry assigns the composite reaction and
reports CoA-bound crystal structures [file:PSEPK/ech/ech-uniprot.txt]. Direct
genetics establishes the pathway role: "The essential involvement of fcs, ech
and vdh in the catabolism of ferulic acid in P. putida KT2440 was proven by
separately inactivating each gene" PMID:12764569. The ech mutant was among the
strains unable to grow on ferulic acid in that study.

The TreeGrafter isoprenoid-catabolism annotation is inconsistent with this
substrate chemistry and target-strain phenotype. It is replaced with ferulate
catabolism, while the generic catalytic activity is replaced with the exact
feruloyl-CoA hydratase/lyase activity.
