Gene Description

TreZ is the malto-oligosyltrehalose trehalohydrolase of the TreY/TreZ route. It hydrolyzes the alpha-1,4 linkage adjacent to the trehalose moiety of maltooligosyltrehalose, releasing trehalose and a shortened alpha-glucan.
