- Q88FN8 is an inferred malto-oligosyltrehalose trehalohydrolase

  Full=Malto-oligosyltrehalose trehalohydrolase"].
- The record describes hydrolysis of the alpha-1,4 linkage adjacent to the
  trehalose moiety [file:PSEPK/treZ/treZ-uniprot.txt, "Reaction=hydrolysis of
  (1->4)-alpha-D-glucosidic linkage"] and places it in trehalose biosynthesis
  [file:PSEPK/treZ/treZ-uniprot.txt, "PATHWAY: Glycan biosynthesis; trehalose
  biosynthesis."].
- The exact activity and pathway are core. Broad glycosidase and carbohydrate
  terms are redundant, and predicted cytoplasmic localization is retained as
  non-core.
- PTHR43651 is a broad glucan-enzyme family; PTHR43651:SF11 is the precise
  TreZ subfamily. No PTN is asserted because GOA contains no locally verified
  PAINT IBD support for this target.
