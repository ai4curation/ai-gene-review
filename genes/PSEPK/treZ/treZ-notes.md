# treZ curation notes

- Q88FN8 is an inferred malto-oligosyltrehalose trehalohydrolase
  [file:PSEPK/treZ/treZ-uniprot.txt, "RecName:
  Full=Malto-oligosyltrehalose trehalohydrolase"].
- The record describes hydrolysis of the alpha-1,4 linkage adjacent to the
  trehalose moiety [file:PSEPK/treZ/treZ-uniprot.txt, "Reaction=hydrolysis of
  (1->4)-alpha-D-glucosidic linkage"] and places it in trehalose biosynthesis
  [file:PSEPK/treZ/treZ-uniprot.txt, "PATHWAY: Glycan biosynthesis; trehalose
  biosynthesis."].
- The exact activity, pathway, and cytoplasmic location are accepted as core;
  the location is explicitly present in the target record
  [file:PSEPK/treZ/treZ-uniprot.txt, "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm"]. Broad
  glycosidase and carbohydrate terms are redundant.
- The reusable reaction assignment is independently grounded by the cloned
  *Arthrobacter* sp. Q36 treY/treZ pair
  [PMID:8605217 "encoding maltooligosyltrehalose synthase (TreY) and
  maltooligosyltrehalose trehalohydrolase (TreZ), respectively"] and purified
  Q36 TreZ activity
  [PMID:8611745 "specifically catalyzed the hydrolysis of the
  alpha-1,4-glucosidic linkage that bound the maltooligosyl and trehalose
  moieties"].
- PTHR43651 is a broad glucan-enzyme family; PTHR43651:SF11 is the precise
  TreZ subfamily. No PTN is asserted because GOA contains no locally verified
  PAINT IBD support for this target.
