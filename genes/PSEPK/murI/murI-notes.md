# murI curation notes

Q88PW2 is the specific glutamate racemase expected to supply D-glutamate for
cell-wall precursor synthesis. UniProt states that it "Provides the
(R)-glutamate required for cell wall biosynthesis" and assigns the pathway
"Cell wall biogenesis; peptidoglycan biosynthesis."
[UniProtKB:Q88PW2, "Provides the (R)-glutamate required for cell wall
biosynthesis"; "PATHWAY: Cell wall biogenesis; peptidoglycan biosynthesis."]

The specific GO:0008881 molecular function and GO:0009252 process are core.
The generic amino-acid racemase and racemase/epimerase terms are true parents
but add no useful specificity.

No target-specific knockout or biochemical paper was identified before the
OpenScientist run. Species-level confidence therefore rests on the reviewed
HAMAP/UniProt assignment and exact reaction mapping, not direct KT2440
experimentation.
