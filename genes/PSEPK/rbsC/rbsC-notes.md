# rbsC curation notes

Q88K36 is an inner-membrane permease in the PP_2454-PP_2459 `rbs` locus.
UniProt predicts seven transmembrane helices; this is not treated as a settled
family topology because the experimentally mapped E. coli ortholog has ten.
Unlike the ambiguous RbsB and RbsA-I family labels, its PANTHER subfamily is
specifically named RbsC
[file:PSEPK/rbsC/rbsC-uniprot.txt, "DR   PANTHER; PTHR32196:SF72; RIBOSE IMPORT
PERMEASE PROTEIN RBSC; 1."].

RbsC contributes the membrane pathway to the collective RbsABC transporter; it
does not independently enable the complete ATP-coupled transport activity. The
submitted EC/hydrolase wording is not interpreted as intrinsic ATP hydrolysis
by the permease subunit.

OpenScientist found no direct KT2440 experiment and states, "No direct
experimental characterization in *P. putida*."
[file:PSEPK/rbsC/rbsC-deep-research-openscientist.md]. Its mechanistic details
come from the E. coli ortholog, so they support the family-level complex model
but do not turn the KT2440 substrate assignment into direct evidence.
