- The reviewed Q88QQ4 record defines the EC 2.7.1.170/Rhea 24952 reaction and
  reports a target-strain fosfomycin-sensitive deletion phenotype
  [file:PSEPK/anmK/anmK-uniprot.txt, "Cells lacking this gene accumulate
  anhMurNAc and are fosfomycin sensitive"].
- The full-text P. putida pathway paper places AnmK upstream of the
  MupP-AmgK-MurU shortcut [PMID:28351914, "the anhMurNAc kinase (AnmK) that
  phosphorylates anhMurNAc, yielding MurNAc 6P, is conserved"].
- The existing GO molecular-function rows are broad. The exact substrate and
  ring-opening chemistry are retained in prose and proposed as a new term.
