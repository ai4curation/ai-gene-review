# bglX curation notes

- UniProt assigns Q88N13 to glycoside hydrolase family 3, predicts a signal
  peptide, periplasmic localization, and beta-glucosidase reaction
  [file:PSEPK/bglX/bglX-uniprot.txt "Belongs to the glycosyl hydrolase 3 family"].
- bglX is not mapped to KEGG ppu00946. It may hydrolyze environmental beta-
  glucosides, but no evidence connects it specifically to the five-step
  naringenin-aglycone pathway.
- The OpenScientist run ended with a DNS connection failure and produced no
  research artifact. No flavonoid-process annotation is proposed.
