# ccmB literature notes

- Direct membrane-vesicle work found that CcmAB is dispensable for heme export [PMID:10708391 "the activity of neither the CydDC nor CcmAB transporters is essential for haem export to the periplasm"].
- Biochemical analysis instead links the CcmAB complex to ATP-dependent processing and release of holo-CcmE [PMID:17419738 "incomplete processing of CcmE"].
