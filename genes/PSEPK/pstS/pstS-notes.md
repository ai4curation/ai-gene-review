# pstS curation notes

- Q88JJ3 is the substrate-binding component encoded at PP_2656 in the complete
  PP_2656-PP_2659 Pst-like operon. UniProt assigns phosphate import, PstS-family
  membership, and PstSACB complex composition by homology. [file:PSEPK/pstS/pstS-uniprot.txt,
  "Part of the ABC transporter complex PstSACB involved in phosphate import."]
- The signal peptide and mature PBP domain support periplasmic localization.
  [file:PSEPK/pstS/pstS-uniprot.txt, "SIGNAL          1..29"]
  [file:PSEPK/pstS/pstS-uniprot.txt, "DOMAIN          30..310"]
- The completed pathway/taxon report distinguishes this operon from the
  Pho-linked PP_5326-PP_5329 locus and finds no direct evidence that the
  PP_2656 operon is the physiologically dominant high-affinity system.
  [file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__bacterial_pst_phosphate_uptake__ppu02010-deep-research-openscientist.md,
  "The second Pst-type operon PP_2656-2659"]
- A same-species PRS2000 study establishes PstSCAB-dependent phosphate uptake,
  but it does not identify Q88JJ3 or directly test KT2440. [PMID:16232467 "The
  pstSCAB genes of Pseudomonas putida PRS2000, encoding the phosphate
  (Pi)-specific transport (Pst) system, were cloned."]
