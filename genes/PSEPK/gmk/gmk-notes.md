# gmk review notes

Q88C87 is the KT2440 guanylate kinase by its exact UniProt record. Direct
bacterial homolog evidence is available from the primary purification study
[PMID:5333666, "Purification and properties of guanylate kinase from Escherichia coli."] This is homolog-level support, not direct experimental evidence for Q88C87.

The exact UniProt record explicitly states cytoplasm, which is accepted and
used as the core location. TreeGrafter-only cytosol is retained as non-core.
