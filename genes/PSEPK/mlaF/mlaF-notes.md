# mlaF curation notes

## 2026-09-01 OpenScientist integration

The report correctly identifies Q88P94/PP_0958 as the MlaFEDB ATPase and finds
the complete conserved ABC nucleotide-binding/hydrolysis motif set
[file:PSEPK/mlaF/mlaF-deep-research-openscientist.md, "A motif scan of the
actual 269-residue *P. putida* MlaF (Q88P94) confirms a complete, intact ABC
nucleotide-binding active site"]. It also places the nucleotide-binding dimer
on the cytoplasmic face of the inner membrane
[file:PSEPK/mlaF/mlaF-deep-research-openscientist.md, "MlaF is a **peripheral
cytoplasmic-membrane protein**: the NBDs sit on the **cytoplasmic (inner) face
of the inner membrane**"].

The report's categorical retrograde-pathway language was not promoted. It
acknowledges that "precisely how ATP hydrolysis is coupled to the direction and
steps of lipid movement is still debated and not fully resolved". The review
therefore assigns ATP hydrolysis directly to MlaF and direction-neutral
intermembrane phospholipid transfer to the assembled system.
