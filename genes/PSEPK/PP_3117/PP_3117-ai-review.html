Gene Description

PP_3117 encodes ImuA, an accessory component of the LexA2-regulated ImuA-ImuB-DnaE2 damage-tolerance cassette in Pseudomonas putida KT2440. In the closely matched PaW85 background, loss of imuA compromises alkylation-damage tolerance when AlkA repair is absent. Its precise molecular mechanism in this organism remains unresolved. Myxococcus ImuA can bind DNA and hydrolyze ATP, but those measurements have not established the same activities for Q88I84. The specific ImuA assignment distinguishes this protein from the canonical SulA cell-division inhibitor.
