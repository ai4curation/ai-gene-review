# glgP curation notes

- Q88CY8 is an unreviewed, computationally annotated alpha-1,4-glucan phosphorylase with EC 2.4.1.1/RHEA:41732. [file:PSEPK/glgP/glgP-uniprot.txt, "DE   RecName: Full=Alpha-1,4 glucan phosphorylase"]
- The reaction releases alpha-D-glucose 1-phosphate from an alpha-1,4-glucan chain. [file:PSEPK/glgP/glgP-uniprot.txt, "Reaction=[(1->4)-alpha-D-glucosyl](n) + phosphate"]
- The RuleBase prose calls the reaction rate-limiting and invokes organismal glucose homeostasis. Those metazoan-style claims were not carried into the PSEPK description because they have no KT2440-specific support.
- PLP binding is retained as non-core cofactor context. [file:PSEPK/glgP/glgP-uniprot.txt, "Name=pyridoxal 5'-phosphate"]
- Purified P. aeruginosa PAO1 GlgP produced alpha-glucose 1-phosphate from maltohexaose, and the deletion strain accumulated more trehalose and alpha-glucan. This is direct support for a close Pseudomonas homolog, not a direct assay of Q88CY8. [PMID:33872310, "Thus, GlgP is a glycogen phosphorylase whose absence leads to the increased accumulation of trehalose and α-glucan in vivo."]
- Direct assays should distinguish GlgA/GlgB glycogen from the neighboring GlgE-dependent alpha-glucan as the physiological substrate.
