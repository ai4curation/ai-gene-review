# mlaD curation notes

## 2026-09-01 OpenScientist integration

The report correctly identifies Q88P92/PP_0960 and supports a single
N-terminal membrane anchor, one MlaD/MCE domain, phospholipid binding, and a
periplasm-facing interface within MlaFEDB
[file:PSEPK/mlaD/mlaD-deep-research-openscientist.md, "Q88P92 has one Mce/MlaD
domain (Pfam **PF02470**, residues **39–116**) — the short, single-ring MlaD
type"]. These findings were integrated into the review.

Two claims were not promoted. First, the report presents retrograde transport
as the settled physiological direction, but later acknowledges that direction
and ATP coupling remain debated
[file:PSEPK/mlaD/mlaD-deep-research-openscientist.md, "The precise
**directionality** (retrograde vs. bidirectional) and the exact **role of ATP
hydrolysis** remain debated even in model organisms"]. The gene review and
module therefore use direction-neutral phospholipid transfer. Second, the
adjacent ttg2 solvent-tolerance context does not establish a direct
MlaD-specific toluene-tolerance function, so it remains pathway context rather
than a core annotation.
