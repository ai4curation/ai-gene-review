# hutI curation notes

- Q88CZ9 is a reviewed imidazolonepropionase (EC 3.5.2.7) that hydrolyzes 4-imidazolone-5-propanoate to N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate. [file:PSEPK/hutI/hutI-uniprot.txt, "Reaction=4-imidazolone-5-propanoate + H2O = N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate"]
- The record identifies HutI as step 3/3 to N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate. It does not catalyze the later terminal conversion to glutamate. [file:PSEPK/hutI/hutI-uniprot.txt, "N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate from L-histidine: step 3/3."]
- One zinc or iron ion is predicted per subunit, but the physiologically dominant metal is unresolved. [file:PSEPK/hutI/hutI-uniprot.txt, "Note=Binds 1 zinc or iron ion per subunit."]
- Local PANTHER data place Q88CZ9 in PTHR42752:SF1, an imidazolonepropionase-related subfamily. [file:interpro/panther/PTHR42752/PTHR42752-entries.csv, "Q88CZ9,Imidazolonepropionase"]
- Generic hydrolase terms were marked over-annotated rather than propagated independently from GO:0050480.
