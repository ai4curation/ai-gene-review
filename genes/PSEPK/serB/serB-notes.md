# serB curation notes

Q88DB8 is an unreviewed, homology-inferred phosphoserine phosphatase, so its
target product line is not treated as independent confirmation of the GOA
calls
[file:PSEPK/serB/serB-uniprot.txt
"RecName: Full=Phosphoserine phosphatase"].

The positive inference combines the target's reaction-discriminating
NCBIfam SerB assignment
[file:PSEPK/serB/serB-uniprot.txt "TIGR00338; serB"] and Mg2+ cofactor line
with characterized orthologs. A bacterial SerB was biochemically characterized
for conversion of O-phospho-L-serine to L-serine
[PMID:25037224
"Phosphoserine phosphatase (PSP), a key essential metabolic enzyme involved in conversion of O-phospho-l-serine to l-serine, was characterized in this study."],
while the archaeal SerB-family structure directly establishes the conserved
Mg2+-dependent phosphoaspartyl mechanism
[PMID:11342136
"PSP and P-type ATPases utilize a common mechanism that involves Mg(2+)-dependent phosphorylation and autodephosphorylation at an aspartyl side chain in the active site."].
