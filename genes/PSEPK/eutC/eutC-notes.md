# eutC curation notes

- Q88QF2 / PP_0542 is the reviewed KT2440 EutC small subunit; P19636 and P19265 are verified protein-level EutC exemplars.
- All seven QuickGO rows were reviewed. The amino-acid-process mapping and both microcompartment localizations were removed; EutBC activity and cobalamin binding require `contributes_to` at the individual-subunit level.
- GOA cites `PANTHER:PTN002446609` for GO:0009350. The canonical PAINT cache for PTHR39330 instead resolves GO:0009350 to `PTN002217404`, seeded by P19636 and P19265. The GOA PTN is preserved as source provenance but is not presented as a canonical ancestral node.
- The completed OpenScientist report was integrated for subunit structure and the KT2440 shell-family inventory. Claims not independently needed for curation were not promoted.
