Gene Description

QueA is the cytoplasmic S-adenosylmethionine:tRNA ribosyltransferase-isomerase of Pseudomonas putida KT2440. It acts after bacterial Tgt has inserted preQ1 at tRNA position 34, transferring and rearranging the ribose moiety of S-adenosyl-L-methionine onto preQ1-tRNA to produce epoxyqueuosine-tRNA, the substrate for QueG.
