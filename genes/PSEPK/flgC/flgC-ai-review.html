Gene Description

FlgC is a proximal-rod subunit of the flagellar basal body in Pseudomonas putida KT2440. It forms part of the rigid axial structure linking the membrane rotor to the distal rod and transmits torque toward the hook and filament. Its role is structural rather than an independent catalytic activity.

2026-11-06 comparative flagellar audit

Family/domain evidence supports conserved structural assignments; comparative Salmonella or other bacterial assays are not presented as KT2440 experiments. Exact subunit counts and interaction geometries are not transferred as target measurements.

The normal Falcon research attempt failed (HTTP 403; provider output was not fabricated). This entry records manual review of the cached primary/comparative literature.

PMID:33931760 resolves the Salmonella basal-body rod; the target-specific NCBIfam/InterPro assignment anchors the inference. Proximal FlgB/FlgC/FlgF and distal FlgG have distinct structural positions. Old database copy-number statements are not target-specific stoichiometry.

General flagellar motility does not by itself establish swarming. The electronically transferred swarming term is qualified against the KT2440 physiological context in PMID:36409076, not rejected because an experimental source is unavailable.

PMID:33931760 purified basal bodies from engineered Salmonella Typhimurium TH25631 (including a hook deletion, elevated FlhDC and a FliG ΔPAA variant). This supports the resolved rod architecture, not an unmodified KT2440 native basal-body measurement.
