why
PA3923, a 47.7%-identical homolog, binds laminin directly (KD 6.9 nM) and supports adhesion and biofilm formation on laminin-coated surfaces. Curated homologs at 48.0–48.4% identity are annotated as putative DUF1302 adhesins. The query has a nearly full-length DUF1302 domain and a predicted outer membrane beta-barrel with a cleavable signal peptide. Laminin specificity remains untested in the query, warranting 'putative'; detergent fitness defects support a surface-associated role without establishing transport activity.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by direct biochemical characterization of laminin binding in the 47.7%-identical PA3923 homolog. Homolog experiments, curated adhesin annotations, query DUF1302 and topology predictions, fitness, cofitness, neighborhood, and STRING evidence are available; cofitness is weak and neighborhood does not establish a specific mechanism. A specific OmpC/OmpF/PhoE porin assignment is rejected because the STRING orthogroup label is less functionally discriminating than the characterized adhesin homolog and does not establish channel activity.
