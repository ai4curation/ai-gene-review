# PP_1255 curation notes

- The UniProt product name is cis-4-hydroxy-D-proline oxidase EC 1.5.1.-
  [file:PSEPK/PP_1255/PP_1255-uniprot.txt, "SubName: Full=Cis-4-hydroxy-D-proline oxidase"].
- The FAD-dependent oxidoreductase/DAO fold and placement immediately upstream
  of PP_1256, PP_1257, and proR support the hydroxyproline pathway role.
- Watanabe et al. directly characterized the P. putida enzyme as a homomeric,
  FAD-containing D-hydroxyproline dehydrogenase with high substrate specificity
  and activity with artificial electron acceptors [PMID:22833679, "the former
  is a homomeric enzyme only containing FAD as a prosthetic group"].
- The physiological electron acceptor remains unresolved, so the incomplete
  EC 1.5.1.- assignment should not be sharpened to an NAD(P)-dependent reaction.
- GO lacks a substrate-specific molecular-function term for this reaction.
  GO:0016491 is therefore used only as the ontology handle for an exact
  substrate-specific prose description; no exact MF annotation is proposed.
- The sole GOA row is cytoplasm. It is retained as non-core rather than used as
  module-level context.
