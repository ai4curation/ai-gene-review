# pstB1 curation notes

- Q88JJ0 is the ATPase encoded at PP_2659 beside PP_2656 pstS, PP_2657 pstC,
  and PP_2658 pstA. UniProt assigns ATP-dependent energy coupling and explicit
  PstSACB complex composition by HAMAP. [file:PSEPK/pstB1/pstB1-uniprot.txt,
  "Responsible for energy coupling to the transport system."]
  [file:PSEPK/pstB1/pstB1-uniprot.txt, "The complex is composed of two ATP-binding proteins (PstB)"]
- This supports an ISS `part_of` annotation to the ABC transporter complex and
  `in_complex` in the core function, while ATP hydrolysis remains the direct
  leaf molecular function.
- The completed pathway/taxon report identifies PP_2656-PP_2659 as a second
  Pst-like operon lacking the Pho-linked context of PP_5326-PP_5329. Its
  activity under phosphate limitation is not established. [file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__bacterial_pst_phosphate_uptake__ppu02010-deep-research-openscientist.md,
  "unknown physiological trigger"]
- The PRS2000 Pst study does not identify Q88JJ0 or directly validate the
  PP_2656 operon. [PMID:16232467 "The pstSCAB genes of Pseudomonas putida
  PRS2000, encoding the phosphate (Pi)-specific transport (Pst) system, were cloned."]
