Gene Description

PP_2049 encodes a metal-dependent alcohol-dehydrogenase-family enzyme in Pseudomonas putida KT2440 with directly demonstrated NAD-dependent oxidation of (R)-3-hydroxybutyrate to acetoacetate. Recombinant enzyme favors the R enantiomer, while native deletion and complementation establish an important role in growth on R-3-hydroxybutyrate and R-1,3-butanediol. PP_2049 is also required for growth on 4-hydroxybutyrate under tested conditions, but direct oxidation of that distinct substrate to succinate semialdehyde remains unresolved.

PP_2049 evidence notes

2026-10-26 — complete target and source audit

Exact Q88L86 corresponds to PP_2049, not the historical gbd PP_2650. Full PMID:32256468 provides native knockout dependence during growth on 4HB and 1,4-BDO but unsuccessful whole-extract 4HB assays. The results leave both free-acid oxidation and alternative CoA-dependent routes possible. Full PMID:37067433 concerns evolved NB10, transcriptional changes and downstream GCL engineering; it does not resolve the substrate of purified PP_2049.

Newly retrieved full PMID:42328986 resolves a distinct activity: His-tagged KT2440 PP_2049 expressed in E. coli K12 MG1655, purified by Ni-NTA, oxidizes R-3HB with NAD. The Results report "equimolar NADH and acetoacetate (AA) were generated for both PP_2049 and PP_3073". Methods independently confirm acetoacetate by HPLC. Fig. 4 also supplies native deletion and complementation. The measured activity was 2.13 U/mg on R-3HB under the reported conditions (37 C, pH 8, 10 mM substrate, 0.9 mM NAD). Minor S-3HB activity does not mean strict stereochemical exclusion; the tested R/S-1,3-diols had no detected activity. No corresponding purified 4HB assay was reported in the inspected study.

Live QuickGO confirms GO:0003858 encodes the R-3HB/NAD reaction. It is a descendant of GO:0016616 and GO:0016491 but not of GO:0004022; the latter MODIFY is explicitly a substrate-specific chemistry refinement, not a fabricated ontology ancestry. All three broad existing rows can carry the precise activity, so no NEW annotation is needed. Catalytic metal binding remains a family-supported property without a measured metal-occupancy claim.

PMID:42847685 led to the companion paper; its cached abstract alone was never treated as a PP_2049 assay. Normal publisher URL fetching returned 403 for the two 2026 papers, while the canonical PMID:42328986 retrieval succeeded with full PMC text. No publication cache was hand-edited.

Workflow: all original annotation fields, order and flags and both raw source hashes were captured before changes. Actual Falcon and fallback provider attempts failed; no provider report was fabricated. Normal publication caching and bacterial fitness retrieval were attempted; FEBA was unreachable and no fitness artifact was created. Manual primary/domain review supplies the decisions. No new annotation row was added.
