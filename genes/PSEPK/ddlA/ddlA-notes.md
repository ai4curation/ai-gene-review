# ddlA curation notes

Q88EV6 has a reviewed HAMAP assignment to D-alanine-D-alanine ligase A. UniProt
records the reaction forming D-alanyl-D-alanine from two D-alanine molecules
and ATP, places the enzyme in the cytoplasm, and assigns peptidoglycan
biosynthesis.
[UniProtKB:Q88EV6, "Reaction=2 D-alanine + ATP = D-alanyl-D-alanine + ADP +
phosphate +"; "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm"; "PATHWAY: Cell wall biogenesis;
peptidoglycan biosynthesis."]

ATP and metal binding are ancillary to the specific ligase activity. Cytoplasm
is retained as the supported location; the parallel cytosol IEA is redundant
for this bacterial soluble enzyme and is not repeated in the core summary.

PANTHER assigns Q88EV6 to PTHR23132:SF25, the DdlA branch. This supports a
family-level DdlA-like variant in the reusable module, but does not establish a
distinct physiological condition for its use in KT2440.
