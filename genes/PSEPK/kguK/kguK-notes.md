# kguK evidence notes

The requested OpenScientist run finished without leaving a final gene report, so this
manual note records the evidence used for curation. Q88HH9 is a PfkB-family kinase in
the conserved kgu locus. The ordered pathway describes 2KGA as "phosphorylated into
2-keto-6-phosphogluconate by the 2-keto-6-phosphogluconate kinase KguK"
[PMID:39770733]. The exact MF remains attached to its existing EC-derived IEA rather
than being upgraded to experimental biochemical evidence from a growth phenotype.
