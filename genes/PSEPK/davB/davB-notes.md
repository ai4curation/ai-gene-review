# davB curation notes

- Q88QV1 is the `davB` locus, but its current UniProt product is automatically
  assigned as tryptophan 2-monooxygenase
  [file:PSEPK/davB/davB-uniprot.txt, "RecName: Full=Tryptophan
  2-monooxygenase"].
- The KT2440 pathway study instead assigns DavB to oxidation of lysine to
  5-aminopentanamide [PMID:31064836, "the oxidation of lysine to
  5-aminopentanamide by DavB and its subsequent deamination to 5AVA by DavA"].
- Purified KT2440 DavB directly catalyzes oxidative decarboxylation of L-lysine
  [PMID:25012259, "DavB is a FAD-dependent monooxygenase that catalyzes
  oxidative decarboxylation of l-lysine"].
- The gene identity, local DavA/DavB pathway context, and GO:0050067 assignment
  support lysine 2-monooxygenase as the core function. Direct comparative
  L-lysine versus L-tryptophan kinetics would still sharpen the specificity
  boundary.
