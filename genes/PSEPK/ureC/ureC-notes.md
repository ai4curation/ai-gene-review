# ureC curation notes

## 2026-08-11

- Q88J04 is reviewed UniProt UreC/PP_2845, the 567-aa alpha chain with EC
  3.5.1.5 and nickel-binding annotation [`ureC-uniprot.txt`, "Urease subunit alpha"].
- The UreC-equivalent alpha chain contains the urease binickel active center
  [PMID:8718850, "each alpha-subunit having an (alpha beta)8-barrel domain
  containing a binickel active center"]. Nickel binding is therefore retained
  for UreC but removed from UreA.
- `hydrolase activity` and `hydrolase activity, acting on carbon-nitrogen (but
  not peptide) bonds` are compatible ancestors but add no information beside
  `urease activity`; they are retained as true non-core ancestors.
- The mature activity belongs to UreABC, but UreC supplies the catalytic center
  and is represented as enabling urease activity rather than merely contributing.
