why
Query InterProScan identifies TruD through HAMAP, PANTHER, PFAM and its conserved catalytic site. Fitness Browser homologs at 77% identity are annotated TruD, and Dickeya homologs at 40.9–41.7% explicitly identify tRNA pseudouridine(13) synthase. The 99.7%-identity STRING homolog belongs to the TruD U13 orthogroup. Fitness phenotypes and the phenol-tolerance insertion do not establish a distinct molecular activity; no EC number or exclusive tRNA species specificity is assigned.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by convergent query-specific TruD signatures, homolog locus annotations and the near-identical STRING homolog's orthogroup assignment. Literature summaries, fitness, neighborhood, STRING and topology evidence are present, but no supplied manuscript directly biochemically characterizes a close homolog and no cofitness data are provided. The distant xylose-receptor comparison is rejected because it conflicts with the extensive TruD domain evidence and predicted cytoplasmic topology; heterogeneous growth phenotypes do not justify assigning a metabolic substrate or phenol-detoxification function.
