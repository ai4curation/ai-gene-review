# flgG curation notes

- Q88ER8 is the KT2440 FlgG family protein and the structural subunit of the distal flagellar rod. [file:PSEPK/flgG/flgG-uniprot.txt, "The rod consists of about 26 subunits of FlgG in the"]
- Classical *Salmonella* work places FlgG in the distal rod immediately before the rod-hook junction. [PMID:2129540, "FlgG is known to lie within the distal portion of the rod, in the region where it is surrounded by the L and P rings, just before the rod-hook junction."]
- Comparative stoichiometry supports a polymeric structural role, but the approximately 26-subunit count has not been measured in KT2440. [PMID:2181149, "The distal rod protein (FlgG) is present at approximately 26 subunits"]
- Deletion of the KT2442 PP4329-PP4397 flagellar cluster eliminates flagella and motility, grounding Q88ER8 in the species-level apparatus while not isolating its individual contribution. [PMID:30338358, "flagella-forming gene cluster PP4329-PP4397"]
- The OpenScientist report found strong domain, sequence, and locus evidence for the FlgG subtype but no direct KT2440 structural or mutational study. [file:PSEPK/flgG/flgG-deep-research-openscientist.md]
- The review therefore proposes structural molecule activity and bacterial-type flagellum assembly while keeping swarming behavior non-core and species-specific.
