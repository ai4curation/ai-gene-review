# hutG curation notes

- Q88D00 is an unreviewed, predicted N-formylglutamate deformylase carrying EC 3.5.1.68. [file:PSEPK/hutG/hutG-uniprot.txt, "SubName: Full=N-formylglutamate deformylase"] [file:PSEPK/hutG/hutG-uniprot.txt, "EC=3.5.1.68"]
- The sequence has HutG/N-formylglutamate-amidohydrolase-specific signatures IPR010247, IPR007709, TIGR02017, and PF05013. [file:PSEPK/hutG/hutG-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR010247; HutG_amidohyd."] [file:PSEPK/hutG/hutG-uniprot.txt, "DR   NCBIfam; TIGR02017; hutG_amidohyd; 1."]
- In the KT2440 terminal route, HutG is curated as acting on the
  N-formyl-L-glutamate produced by HutF and releasing glutamate plus formate.
  This is the two-enzyme formate branch, not the alternative one-step release
  of formamide. [file:PSEPK/hutF/hutF-uniprot.txt,
  "formimidoylglutamate deiminase activity"]
  [file:PSEPK/hutG/hutG-uniprot.txt, "N-formylglutamate deformylase activity"]
- The mapped P. putida hut locus directly identifies hutG as specifying
  N-formylglutamate amidohydrolase. [PMID:2842309 "hutG, which specifies
  N-formylglutamate amidohydrolase"]
- No matching target entry was found in the local PANTHER member cache. The
  locus paper supports gene identity and pathway membership, but no purified
  KT2440 HutG assay was identified in this pass.
