## Pages103–104: genomic scope and Pta activity

> P. putida KT2440 only encodes a pta (PP0774, annotated as phosphate acetyltransferase; Fig. 2A) and completely lacks an acetate kinase gene.

> we did not detect any significant activity, suggesting that the enzymatic activity was dependent on an intact pta gene.

> These results suggest that Pta is indeed active in P. putida KT2440 in a growth-phase dependent fashion, but its relevance (if any) on the cell physiology remains uncertain.

## Page104, Figure3: engineered coupling

> Acetyl-P was used as the substrate for the heterologous AckA bioreaction transplanted from E. coli MG1655

Interpretation: the native KT2440 pta/PP0774 activity is demonstrated by cell-free extracts and a mini-Tn5 mutant. ATP/acetate coupling involves introduced E. coli AckA. Native reverse-route acetyl-CoA biosynthesis is not established by these assays. This does not negate reversible phosphotransacetylase chemistry or rule out other acetyl-phosphate sources.
