# PP_0892 curation notes

Q88PF8 is an unreviewed phospholipase-family protein assigned to the PANTHER
ClsB subfamily. Its current record contains two Cls-like CDD domains and two
Pfam/SMART PLD-domain calls, but only one PROSITE PLD call
[UniProtKB:Q88PF8, "PTHR21248:SF23; CARDIOLIPIN SYNTHASE B"].

The exact cardiolipin synthase annotation is entirely computational. The
protein is therefore treated as a ClsB-like candidate, not as an established
additional KT2440 pathway implementation. The broad phosphatidyltransferase
assignment is plausible, while exact substrate chemistry remains unresolved.

Purified-protein assays with PG and PE, active-site HKD substitutions, and
lipidomics in `clsA`/`clsB` deletion backgrounds would discriminate active
cardiolipin synthesis from another PLD-family function.
