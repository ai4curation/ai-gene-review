Gene Description

LptB is the cytosolic ATPase subunit of the inner-membrane LptB2FG ABC transporter. ATP binding and hydrolysis by the LptB dimer power extraction of lipopolysaccharide from the inner membrane and its transfer into the periplasmic Lpt bridge.

Core Functions

The LptB dimer hydrolyzes ATP on the cytoplasmic face of the inner-membrane extractor, coupling nucleotide turnover to LPS release by LptF/LptG. The KT2440 assignment is supported by the LptB-specific TIGR04406/IPR030921 signatures and homolog biochemical/structural work; a generic ABC ATPase match alone does not determine cargo.
