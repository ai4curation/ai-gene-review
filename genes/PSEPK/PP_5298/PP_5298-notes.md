# PP_5298 curation notes

PP_5298/Q88C85 is assigned EC 3.5.1.94 and Rhea 19737 and is described as
hydrolyzing the gamma-glutamyl linkage of gamma-glutamyl-GABA
[file:PSEPK/PP_5298/PP_5298-uniprot.txt]. Independent PAINT evidence places
GO:0033969 on PTN000230196 from experimentally characterized E. coli PuuD
P76038 [file:interpro/panther/PTHR43235/PTHR43235-paint.tsv]. This supports
removal of the transferred L-glutamine-metabolism annotation.

The E. coli pathway paper includes "hydrolysis of the gamma-glutamyl linkage of
gamma-glutamyl-gamma-aminobutyrate" as the terminal deglutamylation reaction
[PMID:15590624]. KT2440 also contains PP_2179 and PP_3598 with the same exact EC
assignment, so relative in-vivo flux remains unresolved.
