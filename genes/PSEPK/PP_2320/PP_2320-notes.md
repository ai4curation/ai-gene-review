# PP_2320 curation notes

## 2026-09-01 evidence review

PP_2320/Q88KH0 is assigned to PTHR30582:SF24 and is a close ortholog of PAO1
PA2854/Q9HZZ0. PA2854 directly anchors OprI to peptidoglycan in live
Pseudomonas aeruginosa [PMID:42100858, "We document that the gene product of
PA2854 is the catalyst that performs this transformation between the cell wall
and the outer-membrane lipoprotein OprI in live Pseudomonas aeruginosa."]. The
same reaction was reconstituted with purified PA2854, OprI, and synthetic
peptidoglycan [PMID:42100858, "Furthermore, we reconstitute this reaction in
vitro with the use of purified recombinant PA2854, OprI, and synthetic samples
of the cell-wall peptidoglycan, in each case documenting the attachment of the
side-chain ε-amino group of Lys83 of OprI to the peptide stem of the
peptidoglycan."].

The nearby KT2440 oprI locus PP_2322 is corroborative only. PP_2321 intervenes
between PP_2320 and oprI, and the species-aware report does not establish a
defined operon. The function transfer therefore rests primarily on orthology,
domain architecture, exact PANTHER subfamily, and direct PA2854 experiments,
not on local gene proximity.
