# mpl curation notes

- Q88QE7 is assigned to the Mpl subfamily with the exact EC 6.3.2.45/Rhea
  29563 reaction [file:PSEPK/mpl/mpl-uniprot.txt, "Reutilizes the intact
  tripeptide L-alanyl-gamma-D-glutamyl-"]
- The P. putida pathway paper states that the recovered tripeptide is returned
  to peptidoglycan biosynthesis by ligation to UDP-MurNAc [PMID:28351914, "The
  tripeptide is directed to PGN biosynthesis through ligation onto
  UDP-MurNAc"].
- The target assignment is family-based rather than a direct KT2440 Mpl assay;
  the review does not overstate the evidence level.
