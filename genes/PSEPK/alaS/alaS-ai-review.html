Gene Description

AlaS is a cytosolic class-II alanyl-tRNA synthetase that attaches alanine to tRNA(Ala) using ATP. Its conserved editing machinery hydrolyzes mischarged aminoacyl-tRNAs, including Ser-tRNA(Ala) and Gly-tRNA(Ala), helping preserve translation fidelity. A conserved zinc-binding architecture supports its biochemical function.
