# speB curation notes

- Q88KU3 is an unreviewed, PE-3 agmatinase candidate with submitted EC 3.5.3.11,
  TIGR01230 assignment, and a predicted binuclear manganese site. TIGR01230
  does not distinguish it from the conflicting PP_4523 ureohydrolase paralog.
  [file:PSEPK/speB/speB-uniprot.txt, "SubName: Full=Agmatinase",
  "NCBIfam; TIGR01230; agmatinase", "Binds 2 manganese ions per subunit."]
- The completed OpenScientist module+ppu00330+PSEPK report assigns PP_2196 to
  K01480 and distinguishes it from PP_4523, a likely guanidinobutyrase paralog.
  This strengthens but does not experimentally prove substrate specificity.
  [file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__bacterial_putrescine_biosynthesis__ppu00330-deep-research-openscientist.md]
- Agmatinase is a recognized manganese-dependent direct route from agmatine to
  putrescine. [PMID:40673658, "There are currently three known routes from
  agmatine to putrescine, including direct conversion by agmatinase."]

Residual uncertainty: Q88KU3 needs comparative enzyme assays against agmatine,
arginine, and 4-guanidinobutyrate to close the paralog-specificity gap.
