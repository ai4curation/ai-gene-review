# hbdH review notes

Q88IC6 is an unreviewed TrEMBL entry (PE 3). EC 1.1.1.30 and IPR011294 support
the molecular-function call, but direct biochemical or genetic evidence for
this exact KT2440 protein was not found in the completed evidence set.

A structurally characterized *Pseudomonas putida* D-3-hydroxybutyrate
dehydrogenase belongs to the short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase family
[PMID:17958702, "D-3-Hydroxybutyrate dehydrogenase from Pseudomonas putida
belongs to the family of short-chain dehydrogenases/reductases."]. In
*Pseudomonas aeruginosa* PAO1, BdhA converts R-3-hydroxybutyrate to acetoacetate
during growth on that substrate [PMID:26311173, "Essential to this utilization
is the NAD+-dependent dehydrogenase BdhA that converts (R)-3-HB into
acetoacetate"]. These are homolog-level support and are not treated as direct
evidence for Q88IC6.

The PAO1 study also notes that *Pseudomonas* generally does not produce PHB,
despite using its degradation product R-3-hydroxybutyrate [PMID:26311173,
"Bacteria belonging to the genus Pseudomonas do not generally produce PHB but
are capable of using the PHB degradation product (R)-3-hydroxybutyrate"]. Thus
extracellular uptake and intracellular storage-polymer-derived entry are both
kept as contextual possibilities, without asserting a PHB source in KT2440.
