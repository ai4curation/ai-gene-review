# glgX curation notes

- Q88FN4 is an unreviewed sequence-derived record named glycogen debranching enzyme and assigned the older EC 3.2.1.33 classification. [file:PSEPK/glgX/glgX-uniprot.txt, "DE   SubName: Full=Glycogen debranching enzyme"]
- The specific GlgX signature IPR011837 and GO:0004135 support branch-point hydrolysis, but characterized bacterial GlgX is now classified more precisely as EC 3.2.1.196 / GO:0120549, limit dextrin alpha-1,6-maltotetraose-hydrolase activity. [file:PSEPK/glgX/glgX-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR011837; Glycogen_debranch_GlgX."]
- E. coli GlgX preferentially hydrolyzes branches of three or four glucose residues. [PMID:15687211, "high specificity for hydrolysis of chains consisting of"]
- Purified P. aeruginosa PAO1 GlgX preferentially released a DP4 maltooligosaccharide from glycogen. This supports the more precise activity for a close Pseudomonas homolog, while Q88FN4 remains unassayed. [PMID:33872310, "MALDI-MS showed that the enzyme preferentially liberated a maltooligosaccharide with a DP of 4"]
- The broad O-glycosyl hydrolase term is retained as non-core because it is a true parent class, not used as a substitute for the branch-specific function.
- No direct assay of Q88FN4 was found; substrate preference between GlgA-derived glycogen and GlgE-derived alpha-glucan remains open.
