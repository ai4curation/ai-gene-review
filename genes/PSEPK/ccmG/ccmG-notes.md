# ccmG literature notes

- Pseudomonas CcmG and CcmH form a redox pathway in which mixed-disulfide intermediates connect the two proteins [PMID:20544959 "CcmG/CcmH-based redox pathway"].
- Rhodobacter biochemistry also shows direct CcmG reduction of apocytochrome and resolution of CcmH-apocytochrome mixed disulfides [PMID:28634234 "CcmG can efficiently reduce the disulfide bond of apocytochrome c"].
- The review therefore represents CcmG-to-CcmH redox cooperation without asserting one invariant direct-substrate order across bacteria.
