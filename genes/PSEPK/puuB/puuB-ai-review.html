Gene Description

PuuB is the predicted gamma-glutamylputrescine oxidase of KT2440. Its exact PANTHER subfamily and close correspondence to experimentally characterized E. coli PuuB support oxidation of gamma-glutamylputrescine to 4-(gamma-glutamylamino)butanal. No dedicated GO molecular-function term currently captures this reaction.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

gamma-glutamylputrescine oxidase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the oxidation of gamma-L-glutamylputrescine with oxygen and water to form 4-(gamma-L-glutamylamino)butanal, hydrogen peroxide, and ammonium.

Justification: The experimentally characterized Rhea 28414 reaction lacks a substrate-specific GO molecular-function term, forcing PuuB annotations to generic oxidoreductase activity.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH2 group of donors, oxygen as acceptor

puuB curation notes

Q88K44 belongs to PTHR13847:SF275 together with reviewed E. coli PuuB P37906
[file:interpro/panther/PTHR13847/PTHR13847-entries.csv]. The characterized E.
coli pathway explicitly includes "oxidization of gamma-glutamylputrescine"
PMID:15590624. This combination supports the predicted target reaction, while
the absence of a KT2440 assay is retained in the description and experiment
proposal.

The live GO ontology has no substrate-specific term for this reaction. The
verified parent is GO:0016641, oxidoreductase activity acting on a CH-NH2 donor
with oxygen as acceptor. The proposed child is named oxidase activity to match
the oxygen-dependent chemistry and the target UniProt product name.
