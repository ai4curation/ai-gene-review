The bacterial enzyme chemistry is directly summarized by an E. coli structure:
[PMID:17330300, "from nucleoside triphosphates to nucleoside diphosphates."] This is homolog-level support, not direct experimental evidence for Q88PK1.

The exact UniProt record supports the core cytoplasm location. UTP and CTP
biosynthetic roles remain valid non-purine functions in the gene review but are
