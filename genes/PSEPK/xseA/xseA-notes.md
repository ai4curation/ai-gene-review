# xseA curation notes

Q88P26 is a reviewed Exonuclease VII large subunit with EC 3.1.11.6 and an
explicit XseA/XseB heterooligomer assignment. The gene review accepts the
complex activity and DNA-catabolic process. Its cytoplasm annotation is refined
to cytosol, matching the more specific location on the obligate XseB partner
without retaining redundant parent and child locations. KEGG places XseA in
mismatch repair, but no direct KT2440 experiment was found, so mismatch repair
is modeled as a candidate module role rather than added as a gene-level GO
claim.

## OpenScientist adjudication

The OpenScientist report corroborated the XseA catalytic-subunit assignment,
bidirectional single-stranded-DNA exonuclease chemistry, and XseA/XseB complex
architecture using characterized orthologs. Its additional mismatch-repair,
DNA-protein-crosslink-repair, and exact-stoichiometry claims are not supported
by a direct Q88P26 experiment, so they were retained as research context rather
than promoted to target-specific GO assertions.
