# xseA curation notes

Q88P26 is a reviewed Exonuclease VII large subunit with EC 3.1.11.6 and an
explicit XseA/XseB heterooligomer assignment. The gene review accepts the
complex activity and DNA-catabolic process. KEGG places XseA in mismatch
repair, but no direct KT2440 experiment was found, so mismatch repair is
modeled as a candidate module role rather than added as a gene-level GO claim.
