# mccB curation notes

- UniProt evidence: Q88FM4 carries the methylcrotonoyl-CoA carboxylase beta-chain PANTHER subfamily and carboxyltransferase domains. [file:PSEPK/mccB/mccB-uniprot.txt "PANTHER; PTHR22855:SF13; METHYLCROTONOYL-COA CARBOXYLASE BETA CHAIN, MITOCHONDRIAL; 1."].
- The automated mitochondrial suffix belongs to the family label and is not a localization claim for this bacterial protein.
- A characterized Pseudomonas methylcrotonoyl-CoA carboxylase consists of the two liuB/liuD products, supporting assignment of MccB as the beta carboxyltransferase partner of MccA. [PMID:16272386 "The two subunits of MCase corresponded to liuB/liuD"].
- MccB contributes to the complete MCC reaction and complex; it does not independently perform the MccA biotin-carboxylase/carrier half-reaction.
