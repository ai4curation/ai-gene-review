Gene Description

PLP-dependent carboxyspermidine/carboxynorspermidine decarboxylase. In KT2440, NspC is adjacent to the CASDH candidate PP_2928. Comparative subfamily and synteny evidence strongly supports a terminal carboxyspermidine-to-spermidine role, but no direct KT2440 biochemical or genetic experiment establishes that pathway assignment.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

carboxyspermidine/carboxynorspermidine decarboxylase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the decarboxylation of carboxyspermidine to spermidine or carboxynorspermidine to sym-norspermidine.

Justification: NspC is assigned EC 4.1.1.96 and RHEA:34095/RHEA:34099, but GO currently provides only the broad carboxy-lyase parent GO:0016831.

Parent term: carboxy-lyase activity

nspC evidence notes

2026-08-11

The exact KT2440 record assigns NspC to the carboxyspermidine/
carboxynorspermidine decarboxylase subfamily and gives EC 4.1.1.96. Its adjacency
to PP_2928 matches the paired dehydrogenase/decarboxylase organization of the
alternative pathway established in Vibrio cholerae. PMID:19196710

No direct KT2440 NspC assay is available. The current GO molecular-function
annotation is only the broad carboxy-lyase parent, so a specific EC 4.1.1.96
term is proposed.
