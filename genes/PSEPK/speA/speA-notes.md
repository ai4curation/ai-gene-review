# speA curation notes

- Q88QC7 is a reviewed, PE-3 biosynthetic arginine decarboxylase with exact
  EC 4.1.1.19/RHEA:17641 chemistry, PLP and magnesium cofactors, and the
  PTHR43295:SF9 SpeA assignment. These fields are homology-derived rather than
  a KT2440 assay. [file:PSEPK/speA/speA-uniprot.txt, "Catalyzes the biosynthesis
  of agmatine from arginine.", "PTHR43295:SF9; BIOSYNTHETIC ARGININE
  DECARBOXYLASE"]
- Same-genus genetics supports alternative SpeA and SpeC routes: a P. aeruginosa
  double mutant was putrescine auxotrophic, and P. chlororaphis polyamine
  production was eliminated only in the speA speC double mutant.
  [PMID:12634339, "A mutant defective in both speA and speC was a putrescine
  auxotroph."] [PMID:28862813, "Polyamine production was reduced in the speC
  mutant, and was eliminated in the speAspeC mutant."]
- The completed OpenScientist module+ppu00330+PSEPK report identifies PP_0567 as
  the high-confidence SpeA candidate but found no KT2440 functional study.
  Separate gene-level and generic-module report files did not materialize.
  [file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__bacterial_putrescine_biosynthesis__ppu00330-deep-research-openscientist.md]

Residual uncertainty: native KT2440 flux through SpeA versus SpeC and its
environmental regulation remain unmeasured.
