# bhbP review notes

Q88IC5 is an unreviewed TrEMBL entry (PE 4). Its D-beta-hydroxybutyrate permease
name is submitter supplied, while Phobius independently supports a multi-pass
membrane topology. The Gnt-family GO mapping is not sufficient to establish
gluconate as the physiological substrate. The focused review therefore keeps a
broad monocarboxylate transporter MF, replaces the gluconate BP with broad
carboxylic acid transmembrane transport, and does not propose a new
D-3-hydroxybutyrate-specific term.

The gene is adjacent to hbdH and aacs, but neighborhood alone does not establish
transport specificity or justify a direct ketone-body catabolism annotation.
