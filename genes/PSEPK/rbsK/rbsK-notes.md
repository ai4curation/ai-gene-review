# rbsK curation notes

Q88K34 has convergent HAMAP, EC, Rhea, InterPro, and PANTHER support for
ribokinase activity. The assigned reaction is D-ribose plus ATP to D-ribose
5-phosphate, ADP, and a proton
[file:PSEPK/rbsK/rbsK-uniprot.txt, "CC       Reaction=D-ribose + ATP = D-ribose
5-phosphate + ADP + H(+);"].

The broad kinase and phosphorus-group-transfer terms are retained only as
non-core parents. Cytosol is used in the synthesized core; the redundant
cytoplasm parent is not repeated there. No KT2440-specific substrate-spectrum
experiment was present in the fetched inputs.

OpenScientist found extensive ortholog enzymology but states, "Direct
enzymatic assay of the *P. putida* protein has not been published"
[file:PSEPK/rbsK/rbsK-deep-research-openscientist.md]. The report therefore
reinforces the specific HAMAP/Rhea-supported reaction without upgrading it to
direct KT2440 evidence or importing ortholog kinetic values.
