# exaE / PedR2 curation notes

UniProt retains the name ExaE, while the direct KT2440 study renames PP_2672
PedR2 and establishes it as the cognate response regulator of PedS2
[PMID:30158283, "the sensor histidine kinase PedS2 phosphorylates its cognate
LuxR-type response regulator PedR2"].

Phosphorylated PedR2 activates pedE and contributes to pedH repression
[PMID:30158283, "PedR2, which in turn not only activates pedE gene transcription
but is also involved in repression of pedH"]. This supports the exact
response-regulator MF and transcription-regulation BP.
