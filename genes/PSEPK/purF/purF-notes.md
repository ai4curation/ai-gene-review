# purF curation notes

- UniProt identifies Q88LD5 as Amidophosphoribosyltransferase [file:PSEPK/purF/purF-uniprot.txt "RecName: Full=Amidophosphoribosyltransferase"].
- Retain magnesium binding and L-glutamine metabolic process as non-core: glutamine is
  the amide donor consumed by the PurF reaction, while de novo IMP biosynthesis is the
  defining pathway role.
- Mark purine nucleobase biosynthetic process as over-annotated because this route
  produces the nucleotide IMP rather than a free purine nucleobase; GO:0009113 is not
  a broad parent of `GO:0006189`.
