Gene Description

PP_4336 encodes MotC, the A-type (MotA-like) subunit of the MotCD flagellar stator of Pseudomonas putida KT2440. It is an inner-membrane protein with predicted transmembrane helices, an N-terminal MotA_N region and a MotA/TolQ/ExbB proton-channel domain (Pfam PF01618), and it is 83% identical to Pseudomonas aeruginosa MotC. Together with the MotB-like MotD (PP_4335), encoded immediately downstream in the flagellar gene cluster and cotranscribed with cheB1, it forms one of the two stators that power the polar flagella; the second is MotAB (PP_4905-PP_4904). MotA-family subunits form the membrane ring of the stator that conducts protons and engages the FliG torque helix of the rotor. In KT2440 the MotCD stator is dispensable for swimming in liquid but is required for efficient spreading through semisolid agar, where its loss leads to sessile, nonmotile cell clusters; cells lacking both stators are nonmotile. Proton coupling is inferred from the MotA/MotB-type channel architecture; the KT2440 study directly measures motility rather than ion flux.

Core Functions

A-type subunit of the proton-driven MotCD flagellar stator: in the inner membrane it contributes to the stator proton channel that couples proton influx to torque on the rotor, supporting swimming of KT2440 through structured (semisolid) environments. Proton conductance and detailed channel architecture are inferred from homologous stators; native KT2440 motility is directly tested.
