Gene Description

UreE is a cytoplasmic nickel-binding metallochaperone for urease maturation. It is inferred to supply metal to the UreD-UreF-UreG activation machinery for installation of the UreC dinickel active site; it is not a general protein- folding chaperone.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

urease metallocenter assembly

Definition: The assembly of the carbamylated dinickel catalytic center of urease in the preformed urease apoprotein.

Justification: UreD, UreE, UreF, and UreG act directly in urease activation, whereas GO:0043419 urea catabolic process describes the downstream reaction and GO:0065003 protein-containing complex assembly describes protein-subunit assembly rather than metallocenter maturation.

ureE curation notes

2026-08-11

  • Q88J03 is reviewed UniProt UreE/PP_2846, a cytoplasmic nickel-binding urease
    accessory protein.
  • Purified orthologous UreE binds nickel and was proposed to donate it during
    urease activation [PMID:8318889, "UreE binds intracellular nickel ion and
    functions as a nickel donor during metallocenter assembly into the urease apoprotein"].
  • protein folding and unfolded protein binding arise from chaperone-like
    architecture rather than demonstrated generic folding activity. They are not
    propagated into the core function. Generic complex assembly is likewise
    secondary to the specific metallocenter-delivery role.
  • The historical Asta-backed review at 86cf4fd8e9 accepted all transferred
    chaperone terms; current curation narrows Q88J03 to nickel metallochaperoning.
