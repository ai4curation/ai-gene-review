# guaB review notes

Q88P22 is the KT2440 IMP dehydrogenase by its exact UniProt record and belongs
to `PTHR11911:SF111`, not the broader family that also contains GMP reductases.
The bacterial guaB assignment and chemistry are directly established in E.
coli: [PMID:2860637, "catalyzes the synthesis of XMP by the NAD+ dependent oxidation of IMP."] This is homolog-level support, not direct experimental evidence for Q88P22.

GuaB directly commits IMP to GMP biosynthesis. GTP biosynthesis is downstream,
so it is retained in the annotation audit but excluded from the core-function
process list. No location is asserted because the exact UniProt record has no
subcellular-location statement.
