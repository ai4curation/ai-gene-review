# mupP curation notes

- PP_1764/Q88M11 was directly identified and biochemically characterized as
  the MurNAc-6-phosphate phosphatase of the KT2440 anabolic recycling route
  [PMID:28351914, "specifically converts MurNAc 6-phosphate to MurNAc"].
- The TreeGrafter phosphoglycolate-phosphatase and DNA-repair annotations arise
  from the broader HAD phosphatase placement and are contradicted by the direct
  target-specific evidence; both are removed.
- Cytosol is retained only in the gene review. It is not duplicated as generic
  module-level context.
