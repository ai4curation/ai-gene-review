# bkdAB curation notes

- Q88EQ1/PP_4402 is the BCKDH E1 beta subunit. UniProt records an alpha2-beta2
  E1 heterotetramer, while the lpdV OpenScientist report places BkdAB in the
  target-strain BCKDH operon
  [file:PSEPK/lpdV/lpdV-deep-research-openscientist.md, "The **bkd operon**
  encodes the four proteins of the BCKAD multienzyme complex"].
- The TreeGrafter `response to nutrient` term is marked over-annotated: BkdAB
  is a catalytic subunit whose operon is nutrient regulated, not itself a
  nutrient-response regulator.
