# proR curation notes

- The existing review is retained: Q88NF3 is experimentally characterized as a
  PLP-independent 4-hydroxyproline epimerase, not a general proline racemase
  [PMID:23995642, "strong epimerization activity with hydroxyproline as the substrate"].
- The reviewed UniProt reaction is trans-4-hydroxy-L-proline to
  cis-4-hydroxy-D-proline (Rhea 21152), with substantially higher catalytic
  efficiency measured in the reverse direction
  [file:PSEPK/proR/proR-uniprot.txt, "Reaction=trans-4-hydroxy-L-proline = cis-4-hydroxy-D-proline"].
- This pathway pass links ProR to the adjacent PP_1255/PP_1257/PP_1256 route
  while preserving the earlier gene-level Falcon evidence.
