# ureD curation notes

## 2026-08-11

- Q88J07 is reviewed UniProt UreD/PP_2842 and is assigned to the conserved
  UreD family (`PTHR33643:SF1`).
- UreD binds the UreABC apoprotein and stabilizes an activation-competent
  conformation [PMID:7909161, "We propose that UreD is a chaperone protein
  which stabilizes a urease apoprotein conformation that is competent for
  nickel incorporation"].
- The full-text UreF study reports that MBP-UreD binds divalent nickel but also
  emphasizes its scaffold role [PMID:22369361, "The working hypothesis for
  urease activation suggests that UreD enhances the activation efficiency of
  urease apoprotein while also acting as a scaffold"].
- Nickel binding remains `UNDECIDED`: the orthologous MBP fusion bound both Ni
  and more tightly bound Zn, and Q88J07 has not been tested directly. It is not
  promoted to the core function or generalized to nickel homeostasis.
