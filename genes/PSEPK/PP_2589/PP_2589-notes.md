# PP_2589 curation notes

PP_2589/Q88JR0 is an NAD(P)-linked aldehyde dehydrogenase with a
PuuC/GABA-aldehyde-dehydrogenase conserved-domain assignment
[file:PSEPK/PP_2589/PP_2589-uniprot.txt]. KEGG K09472 and pathway context make
it the strongest current KT2440 candidate for the PuuC reaction, but no target
assay establishes turnover of 4-(gamma-glutamylamino)butanal
[file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__gamma-glutamyl-putrescine-catabolism__ppu00330-deep-research-openscientist.md].

The E. coli Puu pathway includes "dehydrogenation of
gamma-glutamyl-gamma-aminobutyraldehyde" [PMID:15590624], establishing the
reaction but not the KT2440 locus. The gene review therefore retains only the
generic aldehyde-dehydrogenase function and proposes an exact GO term without
assigning that proposed specificity to PP_2589.
