# xseB curation notes

Q88QG5 is a reviewed Exonuclease VII small subunit with EC 3.1.11.6 and an
explicit XseA/XseB heterooligomer assignment. Cytoplasm was marked redundant
because the same GOA record includes the more specific cytosol location.
Mismatch repair remains a module-level candidate role pending direct KT2440
evidence.

## OpenScientist adjudication

The OpenScientist report corroborated that Q88QG5 is the noncatalytic small
subunit contributing to Exonuclease VII activity. It also made a false
genomic-context claim: Q88P26 XseA is at `PP_1027`, not `PP_0528`, and is not
adjacent to `xseB` at `PP_0529`; `PP_0528` encodes IspA. The asserted adjacent
operon and its proposed validation experiment were therefore rejected. The
ortholog-based mismatch-repair role remains a module candidate.
