# mutY curation notes

- Q88R48 removes adenine from 8-oxoG:A mismatched DNA and leaves an AP site
  [file:PSEPK/mutY/mutY-uniprot.txt,
  "Hydrolyzes free adenine bases from 7,8-dihydro-8-oxoguanine:adenine mismatched double-stranded DNA"].
- The transferred 8-oxoG DNA N-glycosylase annotation is removed because that
  term denotes excision of the oxidized guanine itself, which is the MutM role.
- Mismatch repair is marked over-annotated, not false: MutY recognizes a
  mismatch, but its lesion-specific N-glycosylase reaction initiates BER.
