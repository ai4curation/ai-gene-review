# nagZ curation notes

- Q88KZ4 is assigned to the glycoside-hydrolase-3 NagZ subfamily and is
  predicted to remove terminal GlcNAc from recycled peptidoglycan fragments
  [file:PSEPK/nagZ/nagZ-uniprot.txt, "cleaving the terminal beta-1,4-linked
  N-acetylglucosamine"].
- The cached P. putida pathway paper explicitly places NagZ in cytoplasmic
  anhydromuropeptide processing [PMID:28351914, "their processing within the
  cytoplasm, yielding anhMurNAc, GlcNAc, and peptides by the
  N-acetylglucosaminidase NagZ"].
- PANTHER PAINT node PTN000766292 provides IBD support for the specific
  GO:0016231 beta-N-acetylglucosaminidase activity
  [file:interpro/panther/PTHR30480/PTHR30480-paint.tsv].
- This is strong pathway/family evidence, not a target-specific biochemical
  assay; the review does not transfer P. aeruginosa AmpC regulation to KT2440.
