# pstC curation notes

- Q88JJ2 is the PP_2657 membrane subunit of the PP_2656-PP_2659 Pst-like
  operon. UniProt supports a phosphate-translocation role but qualifies it as
  probable, so the review models a contribution to the assembled permease.
  [file:PSEPK/pstC/pstC-uniprot.txt, "probably responsible for the translocation of the substrate across the membrane"]
- The completed pathway/taxon report separates PP_2656-PP_2659 from the
  Pho-linked PP_5326-PP_5329 Pst locus and leaves the second operon's
  physiological role unresolved. [file:projects/P_PUTIDA/deep-research/PSEPK__bacterial_pst_phosphate_uptake__ppu02010-deep-research-openscientist.md,
  "candidate_uncertain"]
- The PRS2000 Pst study is same-species pathway evidence, not a direct test of
  Q88JJ2 or KT2440. [PMID:16232467 "The pstSCAB genes of Pseudomonas putida
  PRS2000, encoding the phosphate (Pi)-specific transport (Pst) system, were cloned."]
