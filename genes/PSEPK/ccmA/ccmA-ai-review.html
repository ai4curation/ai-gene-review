Gene Description

CcmA is the cytoplasmic ATPase of the inner-membrane CcmAB complex in cytochrome c maturation system I. ATP hydrolysis supports CcmAB-dependent processing and release of holo-CcmE during c-type cytochrome biogenesis; the molecular substrate or conformational transition coupled to CcmAB remains unresolved.

ccmA literature notes

  • CcmAB is not required for heme association with membrane vesicles, arguing against literal heme export as the core CcmA function PMID:10708391.
  • Purified CcmAB hydrolyzes ATP, and loss of hydrolysis blocks cytochrome c synthesis while leaving CcmE incompletely processed PMID:17419738.
