# ccmA literature notes

- CcmAB is not required for heme association with membrane vesicles, arguing against literal heme export as the core CcmA function [PMID:10708391 "the activity of neither the CydDC nor CcmAB transporters is essential for haem export to the periplasm"].
- Purified CcmAB hydrolyzes ATP, and loss of hydrolysis blocks cytochrome c synthesis while leaving CcmE incompletely processed [PMID:17419738 "found it to have ATP hydrolysis activity"].
