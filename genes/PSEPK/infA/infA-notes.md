# infA curation notes

## Primary evidence

The IF1 A-site and decoding-center description is grounded in the bacterial
30S-IF1 crystal structure, not inferred from the KT2440 HAMAP rule. The study
reports that IF1 occludes the ribosomal A site and changes A1492/A1493 and helix
44 conformation [PMID:11228145, "Binding of IF1 occludes the ribosomal A site
and flips out the functionally important bases A1492 and A1493 from helix 44 of
16S RNA"].

Real-time assembly measurements in *E. coli* identify a kinetically favored
route in which IF3 and IF2 arrive first and IF1 subsequently stabilizes the 30S
preinitiation complex [PMID:22562136, "IF3 and IF2 are the first factors to
arrive, forming an unstable 30S-IF2-IF3 complex. Subsequently, IF1 joins and
locks the factors in a kinetically stable 30S PIC"]. This is evidence for a
favored kinetic route, not proof that all bacterial initiation complexes obey a
strict universal factor-binding order.
