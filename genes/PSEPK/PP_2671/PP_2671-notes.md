# PP_2671 / PedS2 curation notes

PP_2671 is PedS2, the membrane sensor histidine kinase of the PedS2/PedR2
lanthanide switch. The full-text study directly identifies PP_2671 as PedS2 and
shows that PedS2 phosphorylates PedR2 without lanthanum [PMID:30158283, "the
sensor histidine kinase PedS2 phosphorylates its cognate LuxR-type response
regulator PedR2"].

The direct input remains unresolved. Lanthanum lowers PedS2 kinase output, but
the study leaves open direct metal binding versus an indirect periplasmic
interaction. No lanthanide-binding MF is therefore assigned.
