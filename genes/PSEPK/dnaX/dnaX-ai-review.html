Gene Description

DnaX is the ATPase and organizing subunit of the Pseudomonas putida KT2440 DNA polymerase III clamp-loader assembly. It cooperates with delta and delta-prime subunits to load the beta sliding clamp onto primed DNA and couples polymerase cores at the replication fork. DnaX does not catalyze DNA polymerization. The presence and processing mechanism of a separate gamma product are not established here.
