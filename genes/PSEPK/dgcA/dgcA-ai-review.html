Gene Description

DgcA (PP_0310) is the catalytic flavoprotein of the dimethylglycine dehydrogenase system of Pseudomonas putida KT2440. It is a 686-residue soluble protein with an N-terminal NADH:flavin oxidoreductase (Old Yellow Enzyme-type) TIM-barrel domain and a C-terminal FAD/NAD(P)-binding domain, and it is a full-length ortholog (81% identity) of the characterized Chromohalobacter salexigens enzyme DdhC. By that orthology, DgcA oxidatively demethylates N,N-dimethylglycine to sarcosine and formaldehyde, using FAD, and passes the electrons to the adjacent membrane-anchored 2[4Fe-4S] ferredoxin DgcB (PP_0311). DgcB is thought to reduce the electron transfer flavoprotein encoded by the neighbouring PP_0312/PP_0313 genes. This is the dimethylglycine-to-sarcosine step of glycine betaine catabolism, which in Pseudomonas aeruginosa requires the orthologous dgcAB genes. The KT2440 protein has not been purified or assayed directly.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

N,N-dimethylglycine/sarcosine dehydrogenase (ferredoxin) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reactions: N,N-dimethylglycine + H2O + oxidized 2[4Fe-4S]-[ferredoxin] = sarcosine + formaldehyde + reduced 2[4Fe-4S]-[ferredoxin] + 2 H+, and sarcosine + H2O + oxidized 2[4Fe-4S]-[ferredoxin] = glycine + formaldehyde + reduced 2[4Fe-4S]-[ferredoxin] + 2 H+.

Justification: EC 1.5.7.3 and RHEA:74167/RHEA:74155 describe the DgcA/DdhC reaction, but GO has no corresponding term. The only dimethylglycine and sarcosine dehydrogenase terms (GO:0047865, EC:1.5.8.4; GO:0008480, EC:1.5.8.3) are defined with electron-transfer flavoprotein as the direct acceptor, whereas in the DgcAB system the ETF acts after the DgcB ferredoxin. The closest existing term is the generic parent GO:0033694.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors, iron-sulfur protein as acceptor

Core Functions

Soluble FAD-containing dehydrogenase that, by orthology to DdhC, oxidatively demethylates N,N-dimethylglycine to sarcosine and formaldehyde and passes the electrons to the membrane-anchored 2[4Fe-4S] ferredoxin DgcB (EC 1.5.7.3). This is the dimethylglycine step of glycine betaine catabolism. Sarcosine demethylation, seen for DdhC in vitro, has not been tested for the KT2440 enzyme. GO has no term for EC 1.5.7.3 itself; the exact activity is proposed as "N,N-dimethylglycine/sarcosine dehydrogenase (ferredoxin) activity" (see proposed_new_terms), a child of the term used here.

Notes

(dgcA-notes.md)

dgcA curation notes

  • Q88R24 is the soluble flavoprotein component of the Pseudomonas DgcAB system. The OxRdtase_FMN_N fold name alone does not establish FMN binding.
  • The characterized DgcA-like Q1QYW1 enzyme carries noncovalently bound FAD [PMID:32631860, "a monomer of 79 kDa with a noncovalently bound flavin adenine dinucleotide"].
  • Pseudomonas genetics and isotope tracing support DgcAB-dependent conversion of dimethylglycine to sarcosine. [PMID:17951379, "conversion of DMG to sarcosine."]
  • UniProt's EC 1.5.8.- assignment implies a flavin acceptor, while the
    pathway model uses the more specific ferredoxin-linked EC 1.5.7.3 reaction
    and places DgcB before ETF. GO:0047865 is retained as the best available
    substrate-specific term, with a new ferredoxin-linked child term proposed to
    capture the acceptor distinction.
