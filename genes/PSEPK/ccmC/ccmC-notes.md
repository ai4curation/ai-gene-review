# ccmC literature notes

- CcmC is strictly required for transfer and covalent attachment of heme to the CcmE chaperone [PMID:10339610 "it is the only Ccm protein that is strictly required for heme transfer and attachment to CcmE"].
- This direct CcmC-to-CcmE role supports heme handling and binding but not a generic transmembrane heme-transporter molecular function.
