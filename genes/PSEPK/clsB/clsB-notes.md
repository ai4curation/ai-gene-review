# clsB curation notes

Q88HT9 is assigned by current UniProt/HAMAP as cardiolipin synthase B, with two
PLD domains and the PG+PG reaction RHEA:31451
[UniProtKB:Q88HT9, "Full=Cardiolipin synthase B"].

P. putida KT2440 lipidomics detects cardiolipin and reconstructs two named
cardiolipin synthase loci [PMID:21895997, "cls, cls‐2 – cardiolipin synthase"].
The paper does not establish which locus supplies cardiolipin in each growth
phase.

The exact synthase activity and cardiolipin biosynthetic process are supported
for this named family member. Relative activity and possible secondary
phosphatidyltransferase chemistry should be tested against the other paralogs.
