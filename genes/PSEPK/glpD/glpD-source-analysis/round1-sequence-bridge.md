# Q88NY0–P13035 sequence bridge

Frozen full-length UniProt sequences were aligned with Biopython PairwiseAligner in global mode (match2, mismatch−1, gap-open−10, gap-extension−0.5); inputs and hashes are in round1-sequence-identity.json.

**Measured alignment result:** Q88NY0 is 514 residues and P13035 is 501 residues. There are 286 identical positions among 500 aligned residue pairs (57.2% identity); counting all 515 alignment columns gives 55.53% identity. Thus “about57%” uses the paired-residue denominator, not the complete gapped alignment length. Both frozen records assign PTHR11985:SF15 and the FAD-dependent glycerol-3-phosphate-dehydrogenase architecture.

This close sequence bridge, the curated target quinone reaction, and native KT2440 pathway description support transfer of the experimentally established E. coli plasma-membrane association (PMID18296637) as ISS. The target pathway literature calls GlpD membrane-bound (PMID25827416); it is not itself a target topology experiment. The absence of a transmembrane helix does not supply positive evidence of membrane binding. Cytoplasmic-face orientation, bilayer depth and oligomerization remain ortholog-derived; numerical sequence similarity alone cannot establish those details.
