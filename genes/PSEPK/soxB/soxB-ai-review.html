Gene Description

SoxB (PP_0323) is the beta subunit of the cytosolic heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase SoxABDG. It belongs to the FAD-dependent (D-amino-acid-oxidase-like) oxidoreductase fold and, in characterised orthologous enzymes, carries the FAD at which sarcosine is oxidised to its imine and a covalently bound FMN that passes electrons to oxygen, producing hydrogen peroxide. Together with the folate-binding alpha subunit and the small delta and gamma subunits, it forms an enzyme that transfers the oxidised methyl group of sarcosine to tetrahydrofolate, yielding glycine and 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate, and releases formaldehyde in the folate-free reaction. In KT2440 this catabolic role is inferred from conserved subunit identity and pathway context; the strain-specific creatine study cites SoxBDAG as background rather than directly characterizing the purified complex.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

sarcosine oxidase (5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate-forming) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: sarcosine + (6S)-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + O2 = (6R)-5,10-methylene-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + glycine + H2O2.

Justification: This proposal describes the complete folate-coupled reaction of heterotetrameric SoxABDG (EC 1.5.3.24; RHEA:70455), distinct from the glycine/formaldehyde-producing GO:0008115 reaction. It is an ontology proposal for the assembled complex, not the activity of each isolated subunit. The schema permits proposed_molecular_function together with contributes_to_molecular_function, but that syntactic permission does not make a complex reaction subunit-specific. The core therefore retains contribution to GO:0016647; beta cores additionally identify the separately defined imine-forming catalytic subreaction. Other subunits retain only their supported own functions. The target locus is inferred from subunit-specific signatures and genomic context; target-specific folate product partition has not been measured.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors, oxygen as acceptor

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:9185627 : The results show that only the heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidases can use tetrahydrofolates as substrates
  • PMID:16934831 : In the absence of H 4 folate, turnover occurs at a somewhat slower rate and the sarcosine imine is hydrolyzed to glycine and formaldehyde.

sarcosine oxidase (imine-forming) activity

Definition: Catalysis of oxygen-dependent oxidation of sarcosine to the corresponding sarcosine imine, with reduction of molecular oxygen to hydrogen peroxide. In heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase, this catalytic subreaction is localized to beta-bound FAD and FMN and is established in the assembled enzyme. It excludes subsequent imine hydrolysis and transfer of the one-carbon fragment to tetrahydrofolate.

Justification: Sarcosine reduces beta-bound FAD to FADH2; the two electrons pass through beta-bound FMN to reduce one O2 to H2O2. This defines the oxidation step before alpha-associated processing of the imine. GO:0008115 additionally specifies glycine/formaldehyde production, while the separate folate-coupled proposal specifies the complete heterotetramer reaction. Neither is a precise name for this beta-localized catalytic subreaction. The activity proposal does not assert that an isolated beta preparation is catalytically competent. The beta-site assignment is inferred from the specific SoxB family and characterized U-96/other heterotetrameric homologs; this target subunit has not been isolated and assayed.

Core Functions

Supplies the beta-localized catalytic oxidation of sarcosine to its imine at FAD, followed by electron transfer through FMN to oxygen, forming hydrogen peroxide within the assembled sarcosine oxidase. The proposed imine-forming activity records this catalytic subreaction, not autonomous activity of purified beta. The separate contributes_to function records participation in collective enzyme turnover. Subsequent alpha-associated folate capture yields glycine and 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate; it is excluded from the beta-specific proposal. The beta-site assignment is inferred from the specific SoxB family and characterized U-96/other heterotetrameric homologs; this target subunit has not been isolated and assayed. Isolated beta and beta/delta constructs lacked flavin under the reported U-96 expression conditions, so active-site localization does not prove stand-alone turnover.
