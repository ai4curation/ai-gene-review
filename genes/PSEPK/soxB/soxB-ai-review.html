Gene Description

SoxB (PP_0323) is the beta subunit of the cytosolic heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase SoxABDG. It belongs to the FAD-dependent (D-amino-acid-oxidase-like) oxidoreductase fold and, in characterised orthologous enzymes, carries the FAD at which sarcosine is oxidised to its imine and a covalently bound FMN that passes electrons to oxygen, producing hydrogen peroxide. Together with the folate-binding alpha subunit and the small delta and gamma subunits, it forms an enzyme that transfers the oxidised methyl group of sarcosine to tetrahydrofolate, yielding glycine and 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate, and releases formaldehyde only when folate is absent. In KT2440 the complex acts in sarcosine catabolism downstream of creatine and glycine betaine degradation.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

sarcosine oxidase (5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate-forming) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: sarcosine + (6S)-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + O2 = (6R)-5,10-methylene-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + glycine + H2O2.

Justification: This is the physiological reaction of heterotetrameric SoxABDG-type sarcosine oxidases (EC 1.5.3.24, RHEA:70455). GO:0008115 sarcosine oxidase activity is defined by the formaldehyde-forming reaction (EC 1.5.3.1, RHEA:13313), which these enzymes perform only when folate is absent, so no current GO term captures transfer of the oxidised methyl group to tetrahydrofolate.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors, oxygen as acceptor

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:9185627 : The results show that only the heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidases can use tetrahydrofolates as substrates
  • PMID:16934831 : In the absence of H 4 folate, turnover occurs at a somewhat slower rate and the sarcosine imine is hydrolyzed to glycine and formaldehyde.

Core Functions

Catalytic flavoprotein (beta) subunit of the heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase SoxABDG, which converts sarcosine, tetrahydrofolate and O2 to glycine, 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate and H2O2 (EC 1.5.3.24); sarcosine is oxidised at its FAD and electrons pass via its covalent FMN to oxygen. The folate-free formaldehyde-forming reaction (GO:0008115) is a conditional secondary activity of the complex.
