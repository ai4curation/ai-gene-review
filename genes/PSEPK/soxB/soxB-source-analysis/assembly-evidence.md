# Assembly dependence of Corynebacterium U-96 sarcosine oxidase

Primary source: Suzuki et al. (2005), PMID:15914915, DOI:10.1271/bbb.69.952,
page 955, Results, “Purification and characterization of recSO-U96”.
Official publisher PDF: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bbb/69/5/69_5_952/_pdf

Verbatim excerpt (PDF typography and line wrapping normalized; visually checked):

> Moreover, no flavin was found to be bound to these protein samples.

Context and interpretation (reviewer analysis, not a quotation): pSO expresses
recombinant U-96 heterotetramer; pSOβ expresses beta alone; pSOδβ expresses
beta/delta with the affinity tag on delta. The beta and beta/delta constructs
produced low soluble yields; the latter eluate contained a 12–14-fold molar
excess of delta over beta. Further purification made these proteins insoluble.
The authors consequently connect coordinated four-subunit synthesis to flavin
incorporation. This is direct evidence for a construct-dependent assembly and
cofactor-loading problem, not proof that beta can never become active under any
conditions. It does not demonstrate autonomous beta-subunit oxidation, still less
the complete folate-coupled reaction. Catalytic-site location in the assembled
structure is insufficient to infer either autonomous activity.

Scope: this is the Corynebacterium sp. U-96 enzyme, not KT2440 protein. The KT2440
assignment combines conserved subunit identity with the orthologous structure and
chemistry. Raw PDF, pdftotext extraction and inspected page image remain in the
