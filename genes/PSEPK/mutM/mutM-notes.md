# mutM curation notes

- Q88CQ5 is a bifunctional Fpg/MutM enzyme that recognizes oxidized purines,
  including 8-oxoG, and then cleaves the resulting AP-site backbone
  [file:PSEPK/mutM/mutM-uniprot.txt,
  "Has a preference for oxidized purines"; "Cleaves the DNA backbone by beta-delta elimination"].
- The review therefore records two core functions: oxidized-purine DNA
  N-glycosylase activity and class I AP-site endonuclease/AP-lyase activity.
- Generic glycosylase and AP-endonuclease parent terms are not used in place of
  those more informative leaf activities.
