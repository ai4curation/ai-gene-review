Gene Description

NirB is the 859-residue catalytic large-subunit homolog of the NAD(P)H-linked nitrite reductase in Mycolicibacterium smegmatis. It combines an N-terminal pyridine-nucleotide/FAD-binding region with a conserved siroheme/[4Fe-4S] catalytic region and is associated with the neighboring NirD-family small subunit MSMEG_0428. Family and conserved-site assignments support reduction of nitrite to ammonium in nitrogen assimilation, and native nitrogen-limitation studies place nirBD under GlnR control. The precise physiological donor preference and cofactor occupancy of purified mc2 155 NirBD remain unmeasured in the sources examined.

2026-10-29: native context and conserved chemistry

All eight original annotation payloads and both raw records are retained. Falcon authentication failed in the recorded foundation attempt; this is manual literature review, not a generated provider report.

Specific target NCBIfam/PIRSF assignments identify NirB despite an ARBA-only ferredoxin-sulfite name and reaction. [file:MYCS2/nirB/nirB-uniprot.txt "NCBIfam; TIGR02374; nitri_red_nirB; 1."] The generic nitrite/sulfite fold does not determine substrate or donor. A probable upstream query records the mismapped sulfite function; no experiment excluding all sulfite turnover is claimed.

[DOI:10.1186/1471-2164-14-301 "the nitrite reductase NirBD enzyme is up regulated by GlnR in nitrogen limitation"] This primary study uses mc2 155 WT/ΔglnR and provides regulatory context, not purified enzyme specificity. Its historical NarGHJI attribution is superseded for aerobic nitrate assimilation by the direct NasN studies. PMID24266988 uses SMR5/MH1 and must retain that strain distinction. PMID19332834 reports the direct nirB loss phenotype in M. tuberculosis, not this M. smegmatis target.

PIRSR supports the siroheme/[4Fe-4S] catalytic center and PIRNR supports FAD. NADP-specific binding remains UNDECIDED because the general pyridine-nucleotide domain does not resolve donor preference. The separate BFD-like domain suggests a further two-iron center; its occupancy is unverified, not asserted absent. The specific four-iron refinement is therefore bounded to the supported site. No NEW rows or native purified kinetics were added.
