# PYD3 (Q96W94, Lachancea kluyveri) notes

## 2026-10-23 review

Added as the characterized exemplar of the AcyI/M20-class beta-alanine
synthase for the `pyrimidine_degradation` module. Deep research could not be
run in this session (no provider API key); the review rests on the cached
abstracts of the four papers cited by UniProt.

- Genetics: pyd3 mutant cannot grow on N-carbamyl-beta-alanine; PYD3 confers
  that growth on S. cerevisiae [PMID:11454750 "it enabled growth on N-carbamyl-beta-alanine as the sole nitrogen source"].
- Two unrelated eukaryotic beta-alanine synthase classes [PMID:18448119 "is catalyzed by two beta-alanine synthase (beta ASase, EC 3.5.1.6) subfamilies"]:
  nitrilase-type (animals, plants, Dictyostelium; PTHR43674) and the fungal
  M20 type (PTHR32494:SF5), which resembles bacterial N-carbamyl
  amidohydrolases [PMID:11454750 "more similar to bacterial N-carbamyl amidohydrolases"].
- Structure: homodimer, di-zinc catalytic domain [PMID:14534321]; Glu159 and
  Arg322 essential [PMID:17916556].
- The InterPro-derived linear-amidine hydrolase term (GO:0016813) was removed:
  beta-ureidopropionase activity is a linear-amide hydrolase (GO:0016811).
