KdpB (CCNA_01664) is the membrane-embedded P-type ATPase subunit of the KdpABC potassium uptake system in Caulobacter vibrioides NA1000. Conserved type-IA ATPase architecture, the annotated phosphorylatable Asp314, and ATP- and magnesium-binding features support an ATP-driven catalytic cycle coupled to potassium uptake by the assembled complex. Native KdpABC deletion impairs growth in potassium-limited medium, while the detailed ATPase, transport kinetics and coupling stoichiometry of this strain have not been measured in the cited study. KdpE regulates expression of the locus, and KdpD modifies that regulation in a growth-phase-dependent manner.
