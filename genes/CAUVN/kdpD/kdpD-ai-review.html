Gene Description

KdpD (CCNA_01666) is a membrane-associated histidine-kinase-family regulator of the KdpE response regulator in Caulobacter vibrioides NA1000. Native deletion, complementation, point-mutant and promoter-reporter experiments support both positive and negative control, with a proposed kinase contribution during early exponential growth and a predominant KdpE-directed phosphatase role later in potassium-limited cultures. These catalytic assignments are inferred from genetics rather than purified NA1000 phosphotransfer or dephosphorylation measurements. KdpE-dependent transcription persists without KdpD, suggesting another phosphorylation source whose identity is unresolved. The protein lacks the recognizable GAF domain found in Escherichia coli KdpD; a conserved motif-based proposal for serine-kinase regulation of KdpB has not been tested in NA1000.
