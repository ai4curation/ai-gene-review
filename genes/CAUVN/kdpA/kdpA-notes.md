KdpA (CCNA_01663) is the potassium-selective membrane subunit of the high-affinity KdpABC uptake system in Caulobacter vibrioides NA1000. Native deletion and fitness experiments establish the KdpABC system as an important contributor to growth under potassium limitation. Conserved KdpA signatures and curated homology support potassium recognition and delivery to the coupled KdpB ATPase; this subunit-level mechanism has not been reconstituted with the NA1000 complex. KdpA does not supply the ATP-hydrolyzing catalytic center. Kup and other uptake capacity permit residual growth when Kdp is absent.
