Gene Description

KdpE (CCNA_01667) is the receiver-domain and OmpR/PhoB-type DNA-binding response regulator that activates the kdpABCDE potassium uptake locus in Caulobacter vibrioides NA1000. Native ChIP-seq identifies the kdp promoter as its confident direct target, and deletion, complementation, promoter reporters and Asp56 mutants support phosphorylation-dependent transcriptional activation under potassium limitation. KdpD influences KdpE signaling but is not the only proposed phosphorylation source. Broader transcriptome changes do not establish direct binding to every affected gene. KdpE is a soluble regulator acting on intracellular chromosomal DNA, rather than an ion-translocating pump subunit.
