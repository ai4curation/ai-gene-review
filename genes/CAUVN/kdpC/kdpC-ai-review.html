Gene Description

KdpC (CCNA_01665) is a single-pass membrane accessory subunit of the KdpABC potassium uptake complex in Caulobacter vibrioides NA1000. KdpC-family conservation and the native requirement for the KdpABC locus under potassium limitation support a structural contribution to the assembled transport machinery. Homolog structures place the large soluble domain on the periplasmic side, although this orientation has not been measured for NA1000 KdpC. The ATP-hydrolyzing catalytic center resides in KdpB. An older model assigning KdpC an ATP-dependent catalytic-chaperone role remains uncertain for this protein.
