Gene Description

DesB is a gallate dioxygenase, a homodimeric type II (class III, LigB-family) extradiol ring-cleavage dioxygenase in which an N-terminal LigB-like catalytic domain is fused to a C-terminal LigA-like domain, so that a single polypeptide corresponds to both subunits of the two-subunit protocatechuate 4,5-dioxygenase. It specifically cleaves the aromatic ring of gallate with incorporation of both atoms of O2 to give 4-oxalomesaconate, which is then converted to 4-carboxy-4-hydroxy-2-oxoadipate and cleaved to pyruvate and oxaloacetate by the lower protocatechuate 4,5-cleavage pathway enzymes. The active site contains non-heme Fe(II), and substrate binding reorganizes its coordination geometry. In the lignin-degrading sphingomonad strain SYK-6, DesB acts in the main route for syringate (syringyl-unit) catabolism, in which syringate is O-demethylated via 3-O-methylgallate to gallate before ring cleavage, and it is one of two dioxygenases (with LigAB) that degrade gallate. It is functionally equivalent to the GalA gallate dioxygenase of gallate-degrading pseudomonads.

Identity

  • DesB, 418 aa, gallate dioxygenase. Unreviewed TrEMBL entry (PE 4), but the protein was purified
    and characterized in PMID:16030198 (abstract-only in cache).
  • Two-domain fusion of the LigB-type (class III extradiol dioxygenase, Pfam PF02900) catalytic domain
    and a LigA-like domain (Pfam PF07746); CDD cd07950 Gallate_Doxase_N / cd07923 Gallate_dioxygenase_C.
    PMID:16030198
    Same architecture as P. putida GalA (genes/PSEPK/galA).
  • Homodimer: PMID:16030198

Function

  • PMID:16030198
    Km gallate 66.9 uM, Vmax 42.7 U/mg. This is exactly GO:0036238 gallate dioxygenase activity
    (gallate + O2 = (1E)-4-oxobut-1-ene-1,2,4-tricarboxylate).
  • Fe(II): not stated in the DesB abstract. Inferred from family: the LigB active-site Fe
    PMID:15262932
    and the related SYK-6 type II extradiol dioxygenase DesZ requires Fe2+
    PMID:15262932
    P. putida GalA experimentally uses Fe2+.

Pathway / in vivo

  • Syringate -> 3-O-methylgallate (DesA) -> gallate (LigM) -> OMA (DesB) -> LigU/LigJ/LigK lower pathway.
    PMID:16030198
  • PMID:16030198
  • Earlier evidence of a gallate dioxygenase in SYK-6 extracts: PMID:15262932
