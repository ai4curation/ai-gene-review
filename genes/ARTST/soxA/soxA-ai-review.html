Gene Description

soxA of Arthrobacter sp. TE1826 encodes a monomeric sarcosine oxidase (MSOX), a soluble cytoplasmic flavoenzyme of about 43 kDa that catalyzes the oxidative demethylation of sarcosine (N-methylglycine) with molecular oxygen, producing glycine, formaldehyde and hydrogen peroxide (EC 1.5.3.1). It carries one FAD per chain, attached covalently through Cys318; an N-terminal Rossmann-type G-X-G-X-X-G motif (Gly13-Gly18) and Asp36 contact the flavin. Mutation of the attachment cysteine leaves an enzyme whose weak activity depends on noncovalently bound free FAD. Unlike the heterotetrameric bacterial sarcosine oxidases, monomeric sarcosine oxidases do not channel the methyl group to tetrahydrofolate and release free formaldehyde. soxA lies in a gene cluster with a regulator gene (soxR), creatininase (crnA) and creatinase (creA), consistent with a role in the final step of creatinine and creatine degradation to glycine. The protein belongs to the MSOX/MTOX family of FAD-dependent amine oxidases.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is soxA induced by creatinine or creatine through SoxR, and does an Arthrobacter TE1826 soxA mutant fail to grow on creatinine, creatine or sarcosine as sole nitrogen or carbon source? Such data would justify a process annotation (e.g. GO:0006602 creatinine catabolic process); the gene cluster alone does not.

Q: Do the residues and structural features identified in the Bacillus sp. B-0618 monomeric sarcosine oxidase crystal structures (substrate-binding site, flavin attachment geometry) carry over to the TE1826 enzyme? They have not been transferred to this review.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Delete soxA in Arthrobacter sp. TE1826, test growth on creatinine, creatine and sarcosine, and measure soxA transcript levels in response to these substrates, with and without soxR.

Experiment: Solve the crystal structure of the TE1826 enzyme, or a sarcosine-analog complex of it, to confirm the active-site residues directly rather than by homology to the Bacillus enzyme.

    the module (modules/bacterial_dimethylglycine_sarcosine_catabolism.yaml).
  • Bacillus sp. B-0618 MSOX structural work (separate UniProt entry) was not transferred to this protein.
  • Actions: cytoplasm ACCEPT; GO:0008115 ACCEPT (core); GO:0050660 ACCEPT; GO:0016491 and GO:0016647
    TOO_GENERAL (both strict ancestors of the accepted GO:0008115; MODIFY to GO:0008115 would duplicate that row).

    process is suggested only by the gene cluster PMID:9563845, with no physiological data, so left as a
    suggested question.
