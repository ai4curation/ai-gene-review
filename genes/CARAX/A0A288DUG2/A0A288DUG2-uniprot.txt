CC   -!- FUNCTION: Together with PDHA1 forms the heterotetrameric E1 subunit of
CC       the pyruvate dehydrogenase (PDH) complex. The PDH complex catalyzes the
CC       overall conversion of pyruvate to acetyl-CoA and CO(2), and thereby
CC       links cytoplasmic glycolysis and the mitochondrial tricarboxylic acid
CC       (TCA) cycle. It contains multiple copies of three enzymatic components:
CC       pyruvate dehydrogenase (E1), dihydrolipoamide acetyltransferase (E2)
CC       and dihydrolipoamide dehydrogenase (E3). The E1 subunit catalyzes both
CC       the thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP)-dependent decarboxylation of pyruvate
CC       and the reductive acetylation of a lipoyl group covalently linked to
CC       the lipoyl-bearing domains of E2. {ECO:0000256|ARBA:ARBA00094116}.
