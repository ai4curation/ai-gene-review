CC   -!- FUNCTION: Together with PDHB forms the heterotetrameric E1 subunit of
CC       the pyruvate dehydrogenase (PDH) complex in testis. The PDH complex
CC       catalyzes the overall conversion of pyruvate to acetyl-CoA and CO(2),
CC       and thereby links cytoplasmic glycolysis and the mitochondrial
CC       tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle. It contains multiple copies of three
CC       enzymatic components: pyruvate dehydrogenase (E1), dihydrolipoamide
CC       acetyltransferase (E2) and dihydrolipoamide dehydrogenase (E3). The E1
CC       subunit catalyzes both the thiamine pyrophosphate (TPP)-dependent
CC       decarboxylation of pyruvate and the reductive acetylation of a lipoyl
CC       group covalently linked to the lipoyl-bearing domains of E2.
CC       {ECO:0000256|ARBA:ARBA00094144}.
