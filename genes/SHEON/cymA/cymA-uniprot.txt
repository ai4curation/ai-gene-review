CC   -!- FUNCTION: Quinol dehydrogenase involved in several anaerobic electron
CC       transfer pathways (PubMed:10613864, PubMed:12196158, PubMed:12899636,
CC       PubMed:19387485, PubMed:19837833, PubMed:24435070, PubMed:9023196).
CC       Acquires electrons from the membrane quinone pool and mediates their
CC       transfer to several periplasmic terminal reductases and redox shuttles,
CC       including the fumarate reductase FccA, the small tetraheme cytochrome
CC       (STC), the c-type cytochrome MtrA, the nitrate reductase NapA (either
CC       through NapB or directly), the nitrite reductase NrfA and probably also
CC       the DmsE subunit of dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) reductase
CC       (PubMed:12196158, PubMed:12899636, PubMed:19387485, PubMed:19837833,
CC       PubMed:22458729, PubMed:26175726). Required for growth on fumarate and
CC       on DMSO, and for the reduction of iron(III), manganese(IV), nitrite and
CC       nitrate (PubMed:10613864, PubMed:12899636, PubMed:9023196). Not
CC       essential for growth on trimethylamine-N-oxide (TMAO) (PubMed:10613864,
CC       PubMed:9023196). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10613864,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12196158, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12899636,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19387485, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19837833,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22458729, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24435070,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26175726, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9023196}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22458729}.

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22458729, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9023196}; Single-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000255}; Periplasmic side
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:12196158, ECO:0000305|PubMed:12899636,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:22458729}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression is induced in fumarate- and TMAO-grown cells.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9023196}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mutant loses the ability to grow with fumarate as
CC       a terminal electron acceptor and to reduce iron(III), manganese(IV) and
CC       nitrate (PubMed:10613864, PubMed:19387485, PubMed:9023196). It cannot
CC       grow with DMSO as the sole electron acceptor and utilize nitrite
CC       (PubMed:12899636, PubMed:19387485). Deletion of the gene also severely
CC       suppresses selenite reduction (PubMed:24435070). It retains the ability
CC       to grow with O(2) or TMAO as electron acceptors (PubMed:10613864,
CC       PubMed:9023196). Insertion mutant shows a greatly reduced ability to
CC       degrade the xenobiotic compound RDX in comparison with the wild-type
CC       strain (PubMed:22260206). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10613864,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12899636, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19387485,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22260206, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24435070,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9023196}.
CC   -!- BIOTECHNOLOGY: CymA plays an important role in the anaerobic
CC       degradation of the xenobiotic compound hexahydro-1,3,5-trinitro-1,3,5-
CC       triazine (RDX), a cyclic nitramine explosive commonly used for military
CC       applications that is responsible for severe soil and groundwater
CC       contamination (PubMed:22260206). Therefore, supplementary data on the
CC       specific electron transfer mechanisms occurring in Shewanella that
CC       increase the rate and extent of RDX ring-cleavage products is suitable
CC       to develop better remediation strategy for RDX contaminated soils and
CC       groundwater (PubMed:22260206). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22260206}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: To simplify its purification, a soluble form of CymA was
CC       produced by removing the N-terminal membrane anchor (PubMed:12196158,
CC       PubMed:12899636). The truncated form is active and can complement the
CC       deletion mutant (PubMed:12196158, PubMed:12899636).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12196158, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12899636}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the NapC/NirT/NrfH family. {ECO:0000305}.
