# MR-1 CcmB identity and assay limits

Persistent marker: CYTOCHROME-SHEON-CCMB-20261106.

Frozen UniProt Q8EK41 is the 228-aa SO_0262 CcmB of Shewanella oneidensis MR-1, taxon 211586. Primary PMID40026579 Table S2 lists MR-1, its HG0262 ccmB deletion and pHGEN-Ptac-CcmB/His6 expression constructs. The genuine supplementary PDF was acquired through EuropePMC supplementaryFiles by the original-panel reviewer. The [scoped Table S2 excerpt](PMID40026579-tableS2-excerpt.md) retains the necessary identity rows with URL/hash provenance; the full PDF and text are preserved unchanged only in the ignored acquired-sources cache. The main paper controls the C-terminal His6 tag against the native untagged protein. This identifies the target gene and expression design, not a deposited full plasmid sequence.

The experiments measure decreased cellular heme and increased extracellular heme by cellular assays/HPLC during CcmB overproduction, including Ccm-system deletion backgrounds. They support conditional heme-export capacity in the host. They do not directly reconstitute inner-membrane flux by purified CcmB, measure every host export factor, or eliminate an outer-membrane transit requirement. Tag equivalence is not purified transport reconstitution.

PMID40167305 maps a CcmCD heme channel in E. coli and questions general CcmB conduit assignment. It does not directly assay MR-1 CcmB under the overproduction conditions of PMID40026579. Conditional MR-1 efflux capacity is therefore retained as non-core, while the native transmembrane conduit remains unresolved. The core assertion is participation of membrane CcmB in the Ccm maturation machinery, not a universal standalone heme pump.
