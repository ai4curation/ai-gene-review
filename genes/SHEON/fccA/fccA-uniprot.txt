CC   -!- FUNCTION: Flavocytochrome that catalyzes the reduction of fumarate to
CC       succinate (PubMed:12196158, PubMed:12899636, PubMed:22458729). Is
CC       essential for fumarate respiration during anaerobic growth, acting as
CC       the terminal reductase (By similarity). Receives electrons from the
CC       membrane-bound tetraheme c-type cytochrome CymA (PubMed:12196158,
CC       PubMed:12899636, PubMed:19837833, PubMed:22458729). Under ferric iron-
CC       reducing conditions, can be part of an electron transport chain to
CC       ferric iron, by accepting electrons from CymA and transferring them to
CC       the c-type cytochrome MtrA (PubMed:19837833). Also plays an important
CC       role in the reduction of selenite to elemental selenium
CC       (PubMed:24435070). May act as a transient electron storage protein
CC       (PubMed:19837833). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q07WU7,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12196158, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12899636,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19837833, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22458729,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24435070}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=2 Fe(III)-[cytochrome c] + succinate = fumarate + 2 Fe(II)-
CC         [cytochrome c] + 2 H(+); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:77903, Rhea:RHEA-COMP:10350,

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mutants reduce more ferric iron than the wild
CC       type, but this reduction is not coupled to an accelerated growth
CC       (PubMed:19837833). Selenite reduction by the mutant is severely
CC       suppressed, with a 60% decrease in the reduction rate within the
CC       initial 12 hours (PubMed:24435070). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19837833,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24435070}.
