Gene Description

PrpF (SO_0342) is a 397-residue, cofactor-independent methylaconitate isomerase of the 2-methylcitric acid cycle of propionate catabolism in Shewanella oneidensis MR-1. In this organism the prp locus encodes the Fe/S aconitase-like 2-methylcitrate dehydratase AcnD together with PrpF instead of the cofactor-less dehydratase PrpD. AcnD dehydrates 2-methylcitrate to a methylaconitate isomer (proposed to be 4-methyl-cis-aconitate) that aconitase cannot hydrate, and PrpF isomerizes it to 2-methyl-cis-aconitate, the substrate that aconitase converts to 2-methylisocitrate. The two genes are needed together to restore growth on propionate of a Salmonella enterica prpD mutant, and neither works alone. PrpF has no dehydratase or hydratase activity of its own. It is a homodimer with the diaminopimelate epimerase-like (PrpF/DUF453, Pfam PF04303) fold: two alpha/beta domains with the active site in the cleft between them. The crystal structure with trans-aconitate bound places Lys73 as the catalytic base, with Cys107 nearby (Cys107 is oxidized to a sulfinic acid in the crystal), and both residues are required for activity in vivo. The purified protein slowly isomerizes trans-aconitate to cis-aconitate in vitro, a model reaction that shows its cis/trans and double-bond isomerase chemistry.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

methylaconitate isomerase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the isomerization of a methylaconitate isomer produced by aconitase-like 2-methylcitrate dehydratase (AcnD) to 2-methyl-cis-aconitate.

Justification: PrpF's physiological reaction has no GO term. The closest term, GO:0047614 aconitate delta-isomerase activity, describes the unmethylated in vitro model reaction only. EC 5.3.3.- and RHEA:37751 (2-methyl-trans-aconitate = 2-methyl-cis-aconitate) are the current reaction representations, but the identity of the AcnD product (4-methyl-cis-aconitate versus 2-methyl-trans-aconitate) is not settled. The definition is therefore written in terms of the product, 2-methyl-cis-aconitate.

Parent term: intramolecular oxidoreductase activity, transposing C=C bonds

Core Functions

Cofactor-independent isomerase that converts the methylaconitate isomer made by the Fe/S 2-methylcitrate dehydratase AcnD (proposed to be 4-methyl-cis-aconitate) into 2-methyl-cis-aconitate, the substrate of aconitase. Lys73 acts as the catalytic base in the inter-domain active site of the homodimer. The physiological activity is established by in vitro assay with the AcnD product and by in vivo mutant complementation (PMID:29145506). Isomerization of trans-aconitate to cis-aconitate is a weaker model-substrate activity (PMID:17567742).

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is the AcnD product 4-methyl-cis-aconitate (requiring a double-bond migration) or 2-methyl-trans-aconitate (requiring cis/trans isomerization)? The answer decides whether RHEA:37751, as asserted in UniProt, or a positional-isomerase reaction describes PrpF.

Q: Which PTHR43709:SF2 members besides PrpF carry the AcnD-partner methylaconitate isomerase function? SF2 also holds methylitaconate isomerase (Mii) and fungal aconitate delta-isomerase (Adi1), and the PAINT node assigns only the EC 5.3.3-level term.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Determine the structure of the AcnD reaction product by NMR, using authentic standards of 4-methyl-cis-aconitate and 2-methyl-trans-aconitate, and measure PrpF kinetics (kcat/Km) on each isomer and on trans-aconitate.

Type: in vitro enzyme kinetics and NMR

Experiment: Delete prpF in S. oneidensis MR-1 itself (the existing in vivo data come from heterologous complementation in S. enterica) and test growth on propionate, and whether methylaconitate accumulates.

Type: gene deletion and growth phenotyping

What is inferred, not established

  • The chemical identity of the AcnD product (4-methyl-cis-aconitate) is the authors'
    inference. UniProt's RHEA:37751 (2-methyl-trans-aconitate = 2-methyl-cis-aconitate)
    is tagged ECO:0000269 to PMID:17567742, but that paper assayed only unmethylated
    trans-aconitate. The exact substrate isomer is therefore open.
  • No subcellular localization study exists. A cytoplasmic location is likely (soluble,
    no signal peptide) but not annotated here.

Evolutionary grounding

  • PANTHER PTHR43709 (ACONITATE ISOMERASE-RELATED), subfamily SF2 ("DUF453 DOMAIN
    PROTEIN"). SF2 is heterogeneous: it also holds Mii (Q0QLE6, 3-methylitaconate
    isomerase, Eubacterium barkeri), Adi1 (fungal aconitate delta-isomerase), B. subtilis
    YraM and Cupriavidus Q937N7. The family review
    (interpro/panther/PTHR43709/PTHR43709-review.yaml) flags this heterogeneity.
  • PAINT: a single IBD of GO:0016863 at PTN002017920, seeded only by Q8EJW4. The curator
    deliberately placed only the EC 5.3.3-level parent and no substrate-specific term.
    This should be respected when transferring to other SF2 members: GO:0016863 is
    transferable within the clade, but the methylaconitate/2-MCC substrate role is
    PrpF-specific. That role is supported by the TIGR02334/IPR012709 PrpF signature
    and by the acnD-prpF operon context, not by SF2 membership.
  • For the BORPE Q7VZI5 comparison: Q7VZI5 shares SF2, but it is equidistant to PrpF,
    Mii and Adi1, so only the GO:0016863-level activity should be considered
    transferable without further phylogenetic or genomic-context evidence (e.g. an
    adjacent acnD).
