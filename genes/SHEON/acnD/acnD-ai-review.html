Gene Description

AcnD (SO_0343) is an 867-residue iron-sulfur dehydratase of the aconitase/IPM isomerase family that replaces the cofactor-free 2-methylcitrate dehydratase PrpD in the 2-methylcitric acid cycle of propionate catabolism in Shewanella oneidensis MR-1. It is encoded in the prp locus next to prpF. AcnD dehydrates (2S,3S)-2-methylcitrate, and less efficiently citrate, but does not use 2-methylisocitrate or isocitrate, so it performs only the first, dehydration half of an aconitase-type reaction and cannot replace aconitase in the tricarboxylic acid cycle. Its methylaconitate product differs from the 2-methyl-cis-aconitate made by PrpD and is not a substrate for aconitases. The product is proposed to be 4-methyl-cis-aconitate, though UniProt and Rhea record it as 2-methyl-trans-aconitate, and its structure has not been determined directly. The partner isomerase PrpF converts it to 2-methyl-cis-aconitate, which aconitase then hydrates to 2-methylisocitrate. AcnD and PrpF together, but neither alone, restore growth on propionate of a Salmonella enterica prpD mutant. Activity requires anoxic reconstitution of a [4Fe-4S] cluster with iron, sulfide and reductant, is lost with iron chelators and ferricyanide or on exposure to air, and the protein conserves the three cluster-ligating cysteines of mitochondrial aconitase.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

AcnD-type 2-methylcitrate dehydratase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the dehydration of (2S,3S)-2-methylcitrate to water and a methylaconitate isomer other than 2-methyl-cis-aconitate, which requires isomerization (by PrpF) before hydration by aconitase.

Justification: GO:0047547 2-methylcitrate dehydratase activity specifies 2-methyl-cis-aconitate as product (the PrpD reaction, EC 4.2.1.79, RHEA:17725). The AcnD product differs from the PrpD product and is not an aconitase substrate (PMID:29145506). It is recorded as 2-methyl-trans-aconitate by UniProt/Rhea (EC 4.2.1.117, RHEA:26522), but the authors infer 4-methylaconitate. Because the exact structure is unresolved, the term is defined by substrate and by the product's non-identity with 2-methyl-cis-aconitate. Name and definition match the proposal made for the Pseudomonas putida ortholog (genes/PSEPK/acnA-II). This is the same reaction as the existing proposal in projects/RHEA/rhea2go.sssom.yaml for RHEA:26522 / EC 4.2.1.117 ('2-methylcitrate dehydratase (2-methyl-trans-aconitate forming) activity', also grounded on Q8EJW3 and PMID:14702315); the two should be submitted as one term. The product-neutral name is preferred because PMID:29145506 disputes the 2-methyl-trans-aconitate product that the RHEA-based name fixes.

Parent term: hydro-lyase activity

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is the AcnD product 4-methyl-cis-aconitate or 2-methyl-trans-aconitate? The answer decides whether EC 4.2.1.117 / RHEA:26522, as asserted in UniProt, correctly describes AcnD.

Q: Should UniProt retain the EC 4.2.1.3 citrate = D-threo-isocitrate catalytic activity for AcnD, given that the cited paper found no activity with isocitrate?

Q: Which residue differences from TCA-cycle aconitases (for example the Asn following a cluster-ligating Cys, noted by Grimek and Escalante-Semerena) make AcnD accept 2-methylcitrate and release the product rather than rehydrating it?
