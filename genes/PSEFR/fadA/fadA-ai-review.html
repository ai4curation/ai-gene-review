Gene Description

FadA, historically FaoB, is the beta thiolase subunit of the Pseudomonas fragi B-0771 fatty-acid oxidation complex. Its catalytic center cleaves 3-ketoacyl-CoA with CoA, releasing acetyl-CoA and a shortened acyl-CoA. Productive thiolase activity depends on association with the FadB/FaoA alpha partner under the tested reconstitution conditions. Two copies of each chain form a soluble heterotetramer; the complex supplies the final three reactions of beta-oxidation rather than the initial acyl-CoA oxidation step.
