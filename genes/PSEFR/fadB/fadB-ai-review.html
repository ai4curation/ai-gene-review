Gene Description

FadB, historically FaoA, is the alpha subunit of the soluble Pseudomonas fragi B-0771 fatty-acid oxidation complex. Its N-terminal hydratase and C-terminal NAD-dependent dehydrogenase process enoyl-CoA substrates through the middle two reactions of beta-oxidation. The alpha chain remains catalytically active outside the alpha2beta2 complex and supports productive thiolysis by its FadA/FaoB partner. The complex also exhibits enoyl-CoA isomerase and hydroxyacyl-CoA epimerase capabilities; epimerization does not suffice for complete degradation of all unsaturated substrates.

The two chains cover the final three reactions, not all four steps of a native oxidation route. Native strain physiology, isolated chains, assembled catalytic activity and structure-derived channeling are treated separately.

Pf 1997 shows isolated beta inactivity and assembled-complex rescue; this qualifies the catalytic activity without automatically proving that a gene-level enables qualifier is wrong. Phenylacetate catabolism on beta is over-annotated because a shared thiolase fold does not establish that distinct pathway.

Reverse-written reactions are not evidence against catabolic direction. Existing annotations are never used as supported_by evidence. NEW process rationale records the actual catalytic participant, same-role comparison, parents and nonredundancy.
