## 2. Critical Source-Defect Audit on `GO:0030178` (`IDA`, `PMID:20393562`)
- `CHICK/APCDD1-goa.tsv` carries three annotations each to `GO:0005886` (`plasma membrane`), `GO:0017147` (`Wnt-protein binding`), and `GO:0030178` (`negative regulation of Wnt signaling pathway`). All nine rows represent the genuine conserved core function and location of `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2` across Tetrapoda (flanked phylogenetically by *Xenopus laevis* `Q66KI8` and mammalian `Q8J025`/`Q3U128`), so **all nine rows are `ACCEPT`** in accordance with `AGENTS.md` and `action_consistency.yaml` (which judge claims about the gene rather than evidence codes).
- **Host-Organism Misattribution in GOA (`source_defects`)**: Row `GO:0030178` (`IDA`, `PMID:20393562`, `assigned_by: UniProt`) was attached to chicken `UniProtKB:Q5R2I8` (and subsequently ingested as a PAINT seed at `PANTHER:PTN002826827`).
