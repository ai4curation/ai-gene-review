Gene Description

Chicken SYK is a cytoplasmic non-receptor protein tyrosine kinase with tandem SH2 domains that couple phosphorylated receptor motifs to intracellular signaling. In DT40 B cells, SYK-dependent phosphorylation of BLNK links receptor or oxidative stimulation to downstream signaling assemblies. Native chicken heterophil experiments also implicate SYK in Fc-receptor-dependent degranulation. Its molecular architecture supports conserved receptor recruitment and substrate phosphorylation, while broader myeloid, developmental and hemostatic functions rely to varying degrees on mammalian comparative evidence. Avian thrombocytes and mammalian platelets differ in cell structure and signaling outputs, so they do not establish identical cellular implementations.
