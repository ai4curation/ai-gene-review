CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of neuronal acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) that
CC       function as pentameric, ligand-gated cation channels with high calcium
CC       permeability. nAChRs are excitatory neurotrasnmitter receptors formed
CC       by a collection of nAChR subunits. Each nAchR subunit confers
CC       differential attributes to channel properties, including activation,
CC       deactivation and desensitization kinetics, pH sensitivity, cation
CC       permeability, and binding to allosteric modulators. Forms
CC       heteropentamers with CHRNA9. Expressed in the inner ear, in sympathetic
CC       neurons and in other non-neuronal cells, such as skin keratinocytes and
CC       lymphocytes. nAChR formed by CHRNA9:CHRNA10 is involved in modulation
CC       of auditory stimuli. The channel is permeable to a range of divalent
CC       cations including calcium, the influx of which may activate a potassium
CC       current which hyperpolarizes the cell membrane. In the ear, mediates
CC       synaptic transmission between efferent olivocochlear fibers and hair
CC       cells of the cochlea, this may lead to a reduction in basilar membrane
CC       motion, altering the activity of auditory nerve fibers and reducing the
CC       range of dynamic hearing (By similarity). This may protect against
CC       acoustic trauma. May also regulate keratinocyte adhesion (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9GZZ6,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9UGM1}.
