Gene Description

Chicken BLNK, also called BASH or SLP-65, is a noncatalytic adaptor expressed prominently in bursal B cells. BCR stimulation promotes its tyrosine phosphorylation and assembly with signaling proteins. Multiple phosphorylated docking sites on the same BLNK molecule organize PLCgamma and other effectors, linking SYK-dependent receptor signals to calcium and Rac–JNK responses. Its cytoplasmic and membrane-associated complexes perform scaffold work rather than kinase or phospholipase catalysis. Re-expression of BLNK can restrain Rel-driven transformation of primary chicken lymphocytes.

Full BCR-panel annotation reassessment (2026-10-26): CHICK/BLNK

All 14 baseline annotations were assessed, including functions outside BCR signaling. Original terms, evidence, qualifiers, source partners and flags are unchanged. The panel acquisition performed real Falcon/fallback attempts and publication caching; provider attempts failed and no provider prose was fabricated. Prior substantive human BLNK source work was rechecked and retained where sound.

Primary 9834055 identifies chicken BASH. Full 12456653 separately maps human phosphorylation and tests chicken BLNK mutants (Y91/Y103/Y115/Y194/Y205, with residual phosphorylation prompting an additional site test) in DT40. These are not interchangeable residue-number sets. Full 18245482 shows Rel binding to BLNK promoter DNA and suppression of primary chicken lymphocyte transformation after BLNK restoration; it is not evidence for BLNK binding a Rel protein. Bare binding rows are removed under the information-content policy, with the original partners preserved. A specific cell-cycle claim exceeds colony/transformation readouts and is marked over-annotated, without denying the tumor-suppressive phenotype.

The RTK-pathway IBA at PTN001019935 remains unresolved: BCR/SYK signaling is not receptor-tyrosine-kinase signaling, but that distinction does not itself disprove a broader ancestral adaptor function. The single Clnk evidence source is not weak by donor count. No new annotations were proposed.

Final source recovery

Normal recovery obtained full PMID:18369315 and the author manuscript for PMID:11606584. Earlier abstract-only access descriptions for those sources are superseded. The recovered texts support Syk–SLP65 feedback and purified BLNK/PLCgamma2 assembly; mixed-species construct boundaries remain in force.
