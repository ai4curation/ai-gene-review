Gene Description

Twist is the single sea urchin member of the Twist family of basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) transcription factors, with a TWIST1-type bHLH domain and the C-terminal WR motif characteristic of the family. In the sea urchin embryo twist is transcribed in the skeletogenic mesoderm lineage (the large micromere-derived primary mesenchyme cells, PMCs) from the late blastula, is maintained in PMCs through gastrulation, and is later also expressed in the non-skeletogenic (secondary) mesenchyme. It is activated downstream of the PMC specifier Alx1 and feeds back positively on alx1 expression, forming a positive-feedback loop that maintains the PMC regulatory state after the transient initiating inputs have faded. Twist knockdown in the congener Lytechinus variegatus delays PMC ingression (epithelial-mesenchymal transition), with a specific failure of the de-adhesion step, disrupts PMC migration and syncytial fusion, lowers transcripts of the biomineralization genes msp130, sm50 and sm30, and abolishes the larval calcite skeleton; overexpression produces supernumerary and mis-positioned spicules. Later roles in secondary mesenchyme derivatives (pigment and muscle cells) are also reported. The protein is the echinoderm orthologue of the mesoderm and EMT regulators Twist1/Twist2 of vertebrates and Drosophila Twist.

Core Functions

Twist-family bHLH transcription factor of the skeletogenic mesoderm (primary mesenchyme cell) lineage that, downstream of Alx1 and in a positive-feedback loop with it, maintains the PMC regulatory state and drives the transcriptional programmes for ingression (epithelial-mesenchymal transition, specifically de-adhesion), PMC fusion and calcite biomineralization. Functional evidence is from the congener Lytechinus variegatus; the S. purpuratus protein is the 92%-identical orthologue.
