Gene Description

Erg is an ETS-family transcription factor of the ERG/FLI1 subfamily, with an N-terminal PNT (pointed/SAM) domain and a C-terminal ETS winged-helix DNA-binding domain. In the sea urchin embryo it is zygotically activated in the large micromere-derived skeletogenic mesenchyme lineage (the primary mesenchyme cells), immediately downstream of the lineage-founding regulators alx1, ets1, tbr and tel, together with the homeobox genes hex and tgif. Erg and Hex drive each other's transcription in an interlocking positive feedback loop (erg-hex, hex-tgif) that dynamically locks down the skeletogenic regulatory state once the transient initiating inputs have faded, and Erg is in turn a feed-forward driver, together with Alx1, Ets1, Tbr, Tel, Hex, FoxB and Dri, of the battery of differentiation genes that build the larval calcite endoskeleton. Knockdown of erg depresses transcripts of hex and of the downstream differentiation genes. Around the time the skeletogenic cells finish ingressing, erg is additionally switched on in the oral non-skeletogenic mesoderm, where it is confined by the aboral mesoderm regulators Gcm and GataE. In Lytechinus variegatus the erg ortholog is also required for the epithelial-mesenchymal transition of the skeletogenic cells, with Snail upstream and hex downstream of it.

Core Functions

ETS-domain sequence-specific transcription factor that, in the skeletogenic mesenchyme (primary mesenchyme cell) lineage, forms a positive feedback loop with Hex to lock down the lineage regulatory state and acts as a feed-forward driver of the skeletogenic differentiation (biomineralization) gene battery.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is Erg a direct activator of hex and of the skeletogenic differentiation genes (which would justify GO:0001228 / GO:0000978 with IDA), or does it act through intermediates? A cis-regulatory dissection of the hex or an Erg-dependent differentiation-gene module has not been reported for S. purpuratus.

Q: Does erg knockdown in S. purpuratus produce the ingression (EMT) defect reported for L. variegatus erg morphants, and what is the skeletal phenotype? This would determine whether a positive regulation of epithelial to mesenchymal transition annotation is warranted for the S. purpuratus gene product.

Q: What is the function of the later oral non-skeletogenic mesoderm expression of erg (with hex and ets1/2), and does the same erg-hex loop operate there?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: ChIP-qPCR/ChIP-seq of tagged Sp-Erg in primary mesenchyme cells, combined with GFP reporter analysis of the hex cis-regulatory region with mutated ETS (GGAA/T) sites, to test direct Erg input into hex and the differentiation genes.

Experiment: Time-lapse imaging of ingression and skeletal spicule formation after erg morpholino knockdown in S. purpuratus, with hex and biomineralization-gene QPCR at matched time points.

Identity

  • UniProt Q6R7X7 (Q6R7X7_STRPU, unreviewed/TrEMBL, 498 aa) is "Transcription
    factor Erg" deposited by Rizzo & Arnone (EMBL AY508725 / AAR92036.1, Dec 2003,
    submission title "Role of the sea urchin Erg homolog in mesenchyme formation").
    The RX line is PMID:11493577 (Zhu et al. 2001, the PMC EST project), which
    produced the EST from which the cDNA was recovered; that paper does not
    discuss erg as such. RefSeq NP_999833 / GeneID 397069; the CTD cross-reference
    is to human ERG (2078). Community symbol: Erg (Sp-Erg, erg in GRN papers).
  • Domain architecture (UniProt FT): PNT/SAM-pointed domain 91-176 (PROSITE
    PS51433), ETS winged-helix DNA-binding domain 328-408 (PS50061), disordered
    N-terminal and central regions. CDD cd08531 "SAM_PNT-ERG_FLI-1", i.e. the
    ERG/FLI1 subfamily of ETS factors. PANTHER PTHR11849 (family label not written
    from memory). An earlier Lytechinus variegatus genomic fragment of the erg
    homolog defined the ERG/FLI-1-specific "R" region PMID:1457815.
  • Identity check: the accession, the GRN gene "erg" (Oliveri 2008 ref 9 = Rizzo
    et al. 2006) and the Rizzo/Arnone "Sp-Erg" are the same gene; there is a
    single ERG-subfamily ets gene in the S. purpuratus genome
    PMID:16997294. No identity doubt.

Expression

  • Genome-wide ets survey: PMID:16997294 Sp-Erg is not in the maternal set ("Five ets genes (Sp-Ets1/2,
    Sp-Tel, Sp-Pea, Sp-Ets4, Sp-Erf) are also maternally expressed"), i.e. it is
    zygotically activated in the skeletogenic micromere lineage.
  • Later, oral non-skeletogenic mesoderm (NSM): PMID:23261933. Oral NSM erg
    is confined by the aboral regulators: PMID:23261933.

Place in the skeletogenic micromere GRN (Oliveri, Tu, Davidson 2008)

  • Position: immediately downstream of the four double-negative-gate targets
    (alx1, ets1, tbr, tel), in the state-stabilization subcircuit:
    PMID:18413610
  • Inputs: hex feeds back on erg ("the outputs of hex go to erg"); erg is
    downstream of ets1/alx1 per the GRN diagram (Fig. 3A). Perturbation basis:
    PMID:18413610.
  • Outputs to differentiation genes (feed-forward architecture):
    [PMID:18413610 "these differentiation genes require as drivers products of all
    of the now familiar components of the skeletogenic regulatory state ( alx1 ,
    ets1 , tbr , tel , erg , hex , foxb , dri )" ... "Almost all of the linkages to
    the differentiation genes are feeds forward (A > C; A > B; B > C)" ... "The alx1
    and erg genes play both roles, but the ets1 gene ( 9 ) is used in these gene
    batteries only as the A driver"].
  • Interpretation by the authors: PMID:18413610
  • Not shown in the cached text: a morphological (skeleton-minus) phenotype for
    erg MASO in S. purpuratus. The skeleton-minus panels of Fig. 3 are for tbr,
    hex, tgif, ets1 and alx1; erg's effects in Oliveri 2008 are at the level of
    transcript changes (Fig. S3, Table S1). No cis-regulatory analysis of an
    Erg-dependent module is cached, so all Sp evidence for Erg activity is
    perturbation-level (IMP), not direct (IDA).

      perturbation, and the morphological consequence of erg knockdown (skeleton phenotype, ingression)
      is not documented in the cached literature.
    boundary: The erg-hex positive feedback and the requirement of erg for differentiation-gene transcript
      levels are established by perturbation; the erg -> hex link is independently seen in L. variegatus,
      where erg is also required for EMT.

    - BIOLOGY
suggested_questions:
- question: Is Erg a direct activator of hex and of the skeletogenic differentiation genes (which would
    justify GO:0001228 / GO:0000978 with IDA), or does it act through intermediates? A cis-regulatory
    dissection of the hex or an Erg-dependent differentiation-gene module has not been reported for S.
    purpuratus.
- question: Does erg knockdown in S. purpuratus produce the ingression (EMT) defect reported for L. variegatus
    erg morphants, and what is the skeletal phenotype? This would determine whether a positive regulation
    of epithelial to mesenchymal transition annotation is warranted for the S. purpuratus gene product.
- question: What is the function of the later oral non-skeletogenic mesoderm expression of erg (with hex
    and ets1/2), and does the same erg-hex loop operate there?

    into hex and the differentiation genes.
