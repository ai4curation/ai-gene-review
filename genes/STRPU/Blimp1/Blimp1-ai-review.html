Gene Description

Sp-Blimp1 (blimp1/krox, formerly SpKrox1) is the purple sea urchin orthologue of the vertebrate PRDM1/Blimp-1 transcription factor, with an N-terminal PR/SET domain and four C-terminal C2H2 zinc fingers. The gene produces two alternatively transcribed isoforms: the early 1b form (this entry), transcribed zygotically from about 6-9 h after fertilization, and the late 1a form expressed only from gastrulation in the midgut and hindgut. Blimp1b is a central node of the endomesoderm gene regulatory network. It is activated by beta-catenin/Tcf (downstream of Wnt8) and by Otx, and represses its own gene through Blimp1 sites in its cis-regulatory module; together with wnt8, which it directly activates, this autorepressive feedback subcircuit produces an expanding torus of expression that sweeps outward from the skeletogenic micromeres through the non-skeletogenic mesoderm to the veg2 endoderm. Blimp1 acts as an activator on the wnt8, otx (beta1/2 module) and even-skipped cis-regulatory systems and as a repressor of its own gene, of hesC and of notch, thereby releasing delta transcription in the mesoderm and coordinating the outward progression of Wnt and Notch signalling. Blocking blimp1 translation blocks endomesoderm specification; in the endoderm it participates in the blimp1-otx-gatae positive feedback loop and drives brn1/2/4 and tgif expression. Blimp1 isoforms are also reported to regulate germline determinants such as vasa and nanos.

Core Functions

Sequence-specific transcriptional repression through Blimp1 consensus sites of its own gene (autorepression that extinguishes the centre of the expanding torus), of hesC (releasing delta transcription in the non-skeletogenic mesoderm) and of notch, coordinating the outward progression of Wnt8 and Notch signalling across the vegetal plate.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should Blimp1's control of the wnt8 ligand gene and of the hesC/notch genes be represented as positive regulation of canonical Wnt signaling and regulation of Notch signaling, or (as here) only as transcriptional regulation with 'has input' wnt8/hesC/notch? Blimp1 acts one transcriptional step upstream of each pathway, and Peter & Davidson 2011 report that the Blimp1-site mutation does not affect a whole-locus wnt8 BAC.

Q: Does Sp-Blimp1 bind its mapped consensus sites directly? No EMSA or ChIP has been reported for the sea urchin protein; all site assignments rest on mutagenesis and the vertebrate Blimp-1 consensus.

Q: Which of the Blimp1 splice isoforms (1a, 1b, or the additional variants described by Reyes et al. 2025) corresponds to the germline function on vasa/nanos, and does it map to this accession (the 1b form)?

Q: Is the PR/SET domain of Sp-Blimp1 catalytically inactive, as in vertebrate PRDM1, and does repression of hesC involve recruitment of histone-modifying corepressors as suggested for the persistent silencing in skeletogenic mesenchyme?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: ChIP-qPCR or EMSA with recombinant Sp-Blimp1 zinc fingers on the wnt8 module, the blimp1b CR2 autorepression sites, the hesC intronic site and the notch site, converting functional site assignments into direct binding evidence.

Experiment: Immunolocalization or a Blimp1-GFP fusion to document nuclear localization and protein persistence relative to the extinction of blimp1b transcription in the centre of the torus, which constrains the kinetics of autorepression.

Experiment: Isoform-specific morpholinos (1a versus 1b exon junctions) with endoderm and germline markers to partition the endomesoderm and germ-cell functions between the two transcription units.

Blimp1 (Sp-blimp1/krox, formerly SpKrox1; UniProt Q2VF23; GeneID 751833) - curation notes

Identity of the UniProt entry

  • Q2VF23 is an unreviewed TrEMBL entry (753 aa) whose single source is the mRNA DQ177152
    "Blimp1/Krox1b", submitted with Livi & Davidson 2007 (RX PubMed=16798107). It therefore
    represents the early, cleavage-stage 1b splice/transcription variant of the
    blimp1/krox gene that is the endomesoderm-GRN node (RefSeq NP_001073021, GeneID 751833).
    The PR/SET domain (aa 86-209) and four C2H2 zinc fingers (aa 458-564) match the
    description of the gene product as "a zinc finger transcription factor of the SET family
    of proteins" PMID:18061160.
  • Naming: "The blimp1/krox gene of Strongylocentrotus purpuratus, formerly krox1, encodes zinc
    finger transcription factors which play a central role in both early and late endomesoderm
    specification" PMID:16581059. The original cloning paper named it SpKrox1 PMID:9025071. Peter & Davidson 2011 call the early form blimp1b PMID:21623371.
  • Confidence in the accession-to-gene link: high (the accession's own literature citation is
    the Blimp1/Krox isoform paper from the Davidson lab).

What the gene is

Blimp1/Krox is the sea urchin orthologue of the vertebrate PRDM1/Blimp-1 PR-domain zinc-finger
transcription factor. It is expressed zygotically from cleavage and acts both as a
transcriptional activator and as a transcriptional repressor, depending on the target
cis-regulatory module PMID:19104065.

Two alternatively transcribed/spliced forms exist: the early 1b form (this accession) and the
late gut-specific 1a form PMID:16581059. The 1a form is driven by a separate midgut/
hindgut module that carries Brn1/2/4, Otx and Blimp1 sites PMID:16798107.

Expression

  • Earliest transcripts in the macromeres/vegetal plate PMID:9025071; by the Davidson-lab in situ series, cleavage-stage expression
    is in "the large micromeres and veg2 descendents" PMID:16581059, then in the ring of
    mesoderm cells, then the blastopore region/posterior archenteron, and finally midgut and
    hindgut of the pluteus PMID:16581059.
  • The pattern is a moving torus: expressed first in skeletogenic micromeres, then the
    non-skeletogenic mesoderm, then veg2 endoderm, shutting off centrally each time
    PMID:18061160.

Place in the GRN

Inputs into blimp1b (the early cis-regulatory module)

  • beta-catenin/Tcf (from Wnt8 signalling), Otx and Blimp1 itself (autorepression); confirmed by
    site mutagenesis in a BAC-GFP knock-in PMID:18061160.
  • Tcf sites act as a Groucho-mediated toggle: mutating them causes ectopic ectodermal
    reporter expression PMID:18061160.
  • Autorepression was first inferred from morpholino data PMID:16581059 and then reconstructed
    at the cis-regulatory level PMID:17975065.
  • Later (24 h) blimp1b in veg2 endoderm depends on an Eve-controlled signal (V2) from veg1
    PMID:21623371. Delta/Notch is
    required to clear blimp1b from mesoderm PMID:21623371.

Outputs (direct targets with mapped sites)

  • wnt8 (activation). Predicted from perturbation, then shown by site mutation
    PMID:16289024;
    summarized as "Blimp1 is a direct input essential for wnt8 transcription" PMID:18061160.
    In the micromere lineage this is Blimp1's only essential early job PMID:18413610.
    Caveat: Peter & Davidson 2011 found that the Blimp1-site mutation that reduces a small
    wnt8 construct "does not affect expression of a bacterial artificial chromosome expression
    construct containing the whole genomic wnt8 cis-regulatory system" and exclude the model in
    which loss of Blimp1 clears wnt8 from mesoderm PMID:21623371. The activator function on
    the wnt8 module is therefore real at the module level but not the sole driver of wnt8 in the
    endogenous locus.
  • otx (beta1/2 module) (activation, AND logic with Gatae and Otx) [PMID:15110718 "It
    requires gatae, otx, and krox inputs, as predicted, and it operates as an "AND" logic
    processor in that removal of any one of these inputs essentially destroys activity"].
  • hesC (repression through an intronic Blimp1 site) [PMID:19104065 "Mutation of this site
    in a hesC reporter construct caused expression to remain strong in the NSM territory";
    "blimp1 mRNA overexpression nearly abolished expression of the hesC BAC-GFP reporter"]. This
    permits delta transcription in the NSM ("Blimp1 thus represses the repressor of delta ,
    thereby permitting its transcription").
  • notch (repression) PMID:19104065.
  • even-skipped (activation, with Tcf) PMID:18061160.
  • blimp1 itself (autorepression; see above).
  • Later endoderm outputs from perturbation (no sites mapped): brn1/2/4 and tgif
    PMID:21623371; the
    blimp1b-otx-gatae positive feedback loop PMID:21623371.

Loss-of-function phenotype

  • blimp1 MASO blocks endomesoderm specification PMID:18061160; the 1b form "is
    required for the early events of endomesoderm specification" PMID:16581059. Blimp1 MASO
    also leaves hesC on in the NSM and lowers delta ~3-fold PMID:19104065.

Summary of the subcircuit

PMID:19104065

Other roles

  • Germline: Blimp1 isoforms regulate germline determinants (vasa, nanos) in S. purpuratus and
    the sea star PMID:40024498. Abstract only; isoform not mappable to this accession with certainty, so recorded
    as a question rather than an annotation.
  • Starfish orthologue AmKrox has a conserved vegetal ring expression PMID:12915305.

Curation decisions (2026-09-26)

  • IBA/IEA rows for DNA-binding TF activity, cis-regulatory DNA binding, nucleus, regulation/
    negative regulation of Pol II transcription and repressor activity: ACCEPT (all confirmed by
    the cis-regulatory work above).
  • cytoplasm IBA: KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (family-level inference, no sea urchin evidence; site of
    action is nuclear).
  • cell fate commitment IBA and cellular developmental process IEA: MODIFY to the specific
    GO:0001714 endodermal cell fate specification.
  • NEW: GO:0001228 activator activity (IDA; wnt8/otx module site mutagenesis), GO:0045944
    (IDA), GO:0001714 endodermal cell fate specification (IMP; blimp1 MASO). Positive regulation
