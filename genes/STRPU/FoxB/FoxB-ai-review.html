Gene Description

Sp-FoxB (originally cloned as Spfkh1) is the purple sea urchin forkhead box B class transcription factor: a 360-residue, basic (pI 9.96) protein with an N-terminal winged-helix Fork-head DNA-binding domain, a nuclear localization signal and a transactivation region. It is a regulatory gene of the skeletogenic micromere / primary mesenchyme cell lineage, activated in the late tier of the skeletogenic gene regulatory network by inputs from the double-negative-gate outputs Tbr and Alx1 (in an Ets1-Alx1 feed-forward arrangement), alongside dri and foxo. Together with Alx1, Ets1, Tbr, Tel, Erg, Hex and Dri its product is required as a driver of the skeletogenic differentiation gene batteries that build the larval biomineral skeleton, and in Lytechinus the orthologue is required for the apical-constriction step of primary mesenchyme cell epithelial-mesenchymal transition. Additional, later expression domains outside the skeletogenic lineage have been reported, and its upstream cis-regulatory region shows signatures of directional selection in natural populations.

Core Functions

Sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factor of the skeletogenic (primary mesenchyme) lineage, activated by Tbr and Alx1 in the late tier of the skeletogenic GRN, whose product helps drive transcription of the biomineralization differentiation gene batteries that build the larval skeleton.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Which skeletogenic differentiation genes are direct FoxB targets? Oliveri et al. 2008 list foxb among the required drivers, but no FoxB binding site has been validated by cis-regulatory mutagenesis, and the PMC CRM atlases (Shashikant et al. 2018) do not report a FoxB motif.

Q: Is the Lytechinus variegatus finding that foxb is required for the apical-constriction step of primary mesenchyme cell EMT reproducible in S. purpuratus? If so, GO:0010718 (positive regulation of epithelial to mesenchymal transition), already proposed for alx1 and ets1, would also apply to Sp-FoxB.

Q: What are the later, non-skeletogenic expression domains of Sp-FoxB (Minokawa et al. 2004; Tu et al. 2006) and do they reflect separate regulatory functions, e.g. in the oral ectoderm or gut, as the Wnt/hedgehog-responsive promoter sites proposed in 1999 might suggest?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Morpholino knockdown of Sp-foxb followed by NanoString/QPCR of the biomineralization battery (msp130, sm50, sm30, p16, p19, cyp1) and whole-embryo skeletal phenotyping at 48 h, to quantify which differentiation genes depend on FoxB in S. purpuratus and whether a skeleton-minus or ingression-defective phenotype results.

Experiment: EMSA or CUT&RUN with tagged Sp-FoxB on the msp130 and msp103L cis-regulatory modules, to test whether the feed-forward Ets1/Alx1/FoxB wiring involves direct FoxB occupancy of effector-gene CRMs.

FoxB (Sp-FoxB / Spfkh1, Q9XZM6) — curation notes

Identity

  • UniProt Q9XZM6 (TrEMBL, 360 aa), "Winged helix transcription factor Forkhead-1", gene name
    fkh1 (EMBL AAD34014.1), GeneID 373496. The RX citation is the original cloning paper
    PMID:10433970, which places the gene in forkhead Class II
    PMID:10433970;
    Class II of the old Kaufmann/Knochel scheme corresponds to the FoxB subclass.
  • Mapping to the GRN gene: NCBI Gene 373496 (the GeneID cross-referenced from this UniProt entry,
    checked with eutils esummary on 2026-10-03) carries the alias "FoxB". Minokawa et al. 2004
    re-examined the expression of "SpFoxb" as one of four regulatory genes PMID:15183312, i.e. the gene was already known in 2004, consistent with it being one
    of the three fox genes known before the genome survey PMID:17081512. Garfield et al. 2012 analysed the
    "FoxB" upstream cis-regulatory region PMID:23017024, matching the cis-regulatory region sequenced by
    David et al. 1999. Confidence that fkh1/Q9XZM6 is the foxb node of the Davidson skeletogenic GRN:
    HIGH (GeneID alias + the single Class II / FoxB forkhead gene in the genome + continuity of the
    Spfkh1 -> SpFoxb -> Sp-foxb naming in the Caltech papers).
  • Domains (UniProt DR): Fork_head IPR001766 (Pfam PF00250, PROSITE PS50039, residues 13-107);
    PANTHER PTHR11829 (family), subfamily SF377. No GO-CAM contains this gene
    (gocams/index.tsv has no STRPU rows).

Molecular features (David et al. 1999, abstract only in cache)

  • Single ORF encoding DNA-binding domain, NLS and transactivation domain PMID:10433970; 40.7 kDa, pI 9.96.
  • Promoter region contains many predicted TF binding sites, including sites for Wnt/beta-catenin
    and hedgehog pathway effectors PMID:10433970. This 1999 inference of an
    endodermal role was made before the GRN work placed the gene in the skeletogenic lineage.

Place in the skeletogenic (micromere/PMC) GRN

  • foxb is a late-tier regulatory gene of the skeletogenic micromere lineage, downstream of the
    double-negative-gate outputs tbr and alx1 PMID:18413610. Nam & Davidson
    2012 describe the same wiring PMID:22238426.
  • Oliveri et al. 2008 originally proposed a feed-forward (Ets1 > Alx1 > target) regulation of
    Sp-foxb together with the biomineralization genes msp130 and msp103L; Shashikant et al. 2018
    recall this PMID:29558892.
  • Output: FoxB is listed among the regulatory-state components whose products are required as
    drivers of the skeletogenic differentiation gene batteries (perturbation data in Oliveri 2008
    Table S1, not in the cached text) PMID:18413610. These differentiation genes are the biomineral genes
    PMID:18413610.
  • Expression: the GRN papers describe foxb as transcribed in the micromere/skeletogenic lineage
    (Oliveri 2008 Figs. 2, 3, 6). Minokawa 2004 reports additional expression domains
    PMID:15183312, but the cached record is abstract-only, so
    the later (oral ectoderm/stomodaeal, endodermal) domains mentioned in the orchestrator's brief
    cannot be quoted from the cache and are not asserted in the review.

PMC epithelial-mesenchymal transition (Lytechinus variegatus)

  • Saunders & McClay 2014 knocked down 13 skeletogenic TFs in L. variegatus PMID:24598159 and
    found foxb required for PMC EMT PMID:24598159, specifically in the apical
    constriction sub-circuit PMID:24598159.
  • This is the Lv orthologue, so it is recorded here and in suggested_questions rather than as an
    S. purpuratus NEW annotation.

Curation decisions (2026-10-03)

  • IBA rows (0000981, 0000978, 0005634, 0006357) accepted: family-level winged-helix DNA binding
    and nuclear TF function are consistent with the cloning paper and GRN role.
  • IEA generic rows (0003677, 0003700, 0006355) MODIFY to the specific RNA pol II terms already on
    the gene / proposed; 0043565 and 0005634 (IEA) accepted.
  • IBA 0030154 cell differentiation MODIFY -> GO:0070169; IBA 0009653 kept as non-core.
  • NEW: GO:0045944 (IMP, Oliveri 2008) and GO:0070169 (IMP, Oliveri 2008), both minimal and
    resting on the perturbation data summarised in Oliveri 2008.
  • Not proposed: EMT terms (Lv data only), endoderm terms (1999 in silico promoter inference only),
    activator MF GO:0001228 (no reporter/site-mutation evidence for FoxB itself).
