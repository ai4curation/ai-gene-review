Does S. purpuratus Gcm bind the gataE cis-regulatory module directly?

Gene: Gcm, Strongylocentrotus purpuratus (NCBITaxon:7668), UniProt Q8MMJ4, NP_999826.1. Hypothesis slug: kgap-gcm-gatae-sites. Focus type: free_text. Source: genes/STRPU/Gcm/Gcm-ai-review.yaml.

Summary

No Gcm binding site is predicted anywhere in the gataE regulatory domain. A scan of 57,225 bp spanning the complete gataE intergenic and genic space (20 kb upstream to 37 kb downstream of the transcription start on NW_022145613.1, Spur_5.0) finds exactly one match to the GCM consensus ATGCGGRY: ATGCGGAT at -12,448 on the minus strand. That match scores 5.09 bits on the JASPAR GCM2 matrix MA0767.2, against an empirical p = 1e-4 genomic threshold of 10.48 bits, and the adenine it carries at the degenerate R position occurs 0 times in 21,467 counts at that position in the GCM1 HT-SELEX matrix MA0646.2. The same pipeline recovers both sea urchin Gcm sites that have cis-perturbation support as the single top-scoring position in their own loci: gcm -4,062 (ATGCGGGC, 14.63 bits) and pks -1,437 (ATGCGGGT, 15.20 bits), both above the p = 1e-5 threshold of 13.24 bits. Rescoring all three loci with three independent GCM-family matrices, without restricting to exact consensus, gives 0, 0 and 1 stringent hits in gataE; the single hit is ATACGGGTAG at +36,541, which breaks the invariant GCGG core at a base-read position and has zero occurrences in 238,569 bp of the orthologous Lytechinus variegatus region.

The consensus model is the right one to apply to this protein. Parsing PDB 1ODH (murine GCMa GCM domain residues 1-174 bound to its octameric DNA target, 2.85 Å, Cohen et al. 2003) gives 18 residues within 4.0 Å of DNA, four of which contact bases. Q8MMJ4 retains 12 of the 18 and three of the four base-reading residues (N99, N101, H103 in Q8MMJ4 numbering). The only base-contact substitution is mouse I100 to T136. The alternative that gataE lacks a canonical site because the sea urchin protein reads a different sequence is therefore closed off, with one stated uncertainty at position I100/T136.

The negative is stated at the strength the data support, and the power of the test was measured rather than assumed. At the measured genomic rates a 57,225 bp window expects 1.49 ATGCGGRY and 0.50 ATGCGGGY, so P(0 observed) is 0.226 and 0.609. A random window of that size carries at least one p = 1e-5 hit 68.2% of the time. A best-score statistic does not flag the validated gcm site either (p = 0.357 to 0.614 across three matrices), while pks1 is flagged (p = 0.067 to 0.109). Motif analysis at this locus size withholds support for a direct mechanism at gataE and does not refute one. The discriminating observation is the identity of the matches, not their density: 2 of 2 cis-perturbation-validated targets carry a perfect ATGCGGGY, and 0 of 1 for gataE. For curation the consequence does not depend on the power of the negative. A statement that Gcm directly drives gataE needs positive evidence of a mapped site or of occupancy, and neither exists in this species.

Executive judgment

Over-annotated with respect to the word "direct".

The requirement of Gcm for gataE expression is well supported by morpholino knockdown, expression kinetics and Notch-independence, and nothing here touches that. The statement that Gcm directly drives gataE has no mapped site behind it in the literature, gains no support from sequence, and the motif analysis also lacks the power to refute it. The asymmetry is what matters for curation: the analysis finds no candidate site capable of supporting a direct mechanism, while the two genes with cis-perturbation evidence for direct Gcm input each carry a canonical high-scoring site that the same scan pulls out as the top position in its locus.

Direct binding is predicted at no position in the gataE locus. The one consensus match, at -12,448 on the minus strand, is predicted to be a non-binder on PWM grounds, falls outside every Sp/Lv conserved patch, and the observed count of 1 is below the Poisson expectation of 1.49.

Stated plainly, as the seed hypothesis anticipated: motif analysis cannot distinguish a functional site from background here. This was quantified. At a relaxed threshold (p = 1e-4) all three loci, including both positive controls, sit at genomic background with observed/expected hit ratios of 0.98 to 1.89. Discrimination appears only at p = 1e-5. Even there, the count-level and best-score statistics do not reach significance for the validated gcm site. Transcription factors occupy degenerate and low-affinity sites in vivo, and tethering through a partner protein leaves no site at all. The positive controls were carried through every step for exactly this reason.

Key findings

F1. The scan recovers both validated sea urchin Gcm sites as top-scoring outliers

The pipeline was validated before any gataE conclusion was drawn. Scanning the gcm locus (NW_022145598.1, TSS-17.6 kb to +33.4 kb, the BAC 33-O18 equivalent, 51,001 bp) for ATGCGGRY returns exactly one perfect 8/8 match, ATGCGGGC on the plus strand at NCBI-frame -4,220. The spacing from that match to the 7/8 element tTGCGGGC (+11 bp) and to the ATGaGGGT element of the G59 fragment (+171 bp) reproduces the -4,062 / -4,051 / -3,891 landmarks of Ransick and Davidson exactly, so the two coordinate frames differ by a constant 158 bp and the site recovered is the functionally validated one. The pks1 locus (LOC588806, NW_022145610.1, -10 kb to +24.5 kb, 34,461 bp) returns three perfect matches, one of which, ATGCGGGT at -1,437, lies inside the -1.5 kb module whose Gcm sites Calestani and Rogers mutated.

Scored against a log-odds model built on a 1,499,503 bp S. purpuratus genomic background (A 0.315, C 0.186, G 0.188, T 0.311), the gcm site scores 14.63 bits and the pks site 15.20 bits, both above the p = 1e-5 genomic threshold of 13.24 bits. The background density of ATGCGGRY in that 1.5 Mb is 39 occurrences, 1 per 38.4 kb, which is 0.43 times the i.i.d. expectation.

F2. No GCM consensus site above background exists anywhere in 57 kb of the gataE regulatory domain

The gataE regulatory domain was bounded from the Spur_5.0 annotation rather than assumed. GATAe occupies 329,575-361,799 on the minus strand of NW_022145613.1; the 5' neighbour LOC580755 begins 15.2 kb upstream of the gataE TSS and the 3' neighbour LOC577787 ends 4.0 kb downstream, so the entire intergenic plus genic space is about 51 kb. A 57,225 bp window covers all of it.

In that window there is exactly one perfect ATGCGGRY: ATGCGGAT on the minus strand at -12,448. Observed density is 1 per 57.2 kb against a genomic background of 1 per 38.4 kb, so the count sits at or below chance (Poisson expectation 1.49, P(X≥1) = 0.77). Restricting to ATGCGGGY, the variant used by both functional sites and the only variant supported by the GCM1 HT-SELEX data, gataE has zero matches in 57 kb while the genomic rate is 1 per 115 kb. The JASPAR MA0646.2 counts at the R position are G 21,439, C 18, T 10, A 0, so the gataE variant is effectively a non-binder in the available binding data. By PWM the site scores 5.09 bits, below the p = 1e-4 threshold of 10.48 and far below 14.63 and 15.20 bits. A BLAST of the 500 bp around the site against Lytechinus sequence returned only (ATC)n microsatellite matches, placing it in low-complexity repeat.

F3. Q8MMJ4 retains the base-reading residues of the GCMa-DNA structure, so ATGCGGRY is the right consensus

PDB 1ODH was parsed directly and protein residues within 4.0 Å of DNA atoms were computed: 18 residues, of which four contact bases (N63, N65, H67, I100) and the remainder contact the phosphate backbone or sugar. A Needleman-Wunsch alignment (BLOSUM62, gap open -11, extend -1) of Q8MMJ4 (794 aa) to mouse Gcm1 P70348 (436 aa) gives 160 identities overall and maps the contacts as follows.

The gcm positive control passes and reproduces the published Sp/Lv relationship: the aligned Lv sequence is AATAACATGCGGGCATGTC against Sp CAACAAATGCGGGCATGTT, with the 11-mer ATGCGGGCATG and the GGCGCC flank identical. The pks +7,111 case is conserved for the wrong reason: frame-1 translation of that segment matches SpPks1 exactly, so it is a coding exon under protein constraint, and its PWM score is 4.52 bits.

The limiting result is pks -1,437. That site was validated by mutagenesis, and its 8-mer has zero occurrences anywhere in 323,246 bp of the orthologous L. variegatus region. Echinoid cis-regulatory sites turn over fast enough between these two species that absence of conservation is not evidence of absence of function. The conservation arm therefore adds a consistent negative for gataE and cannot carry the argument on its own.

F5. Sp/Lv conservation lacks the resolution to call an 8 bp site, so three statistics were tested and two were discarded

Three conservation statistics were calibrated on the validated gcm -4,062 site before any was used.

Patch-level 16-mer sharing over 300 bp windows finds 16 conserved patches covering 9,050 bp of the 57,225 bp gataE domain (locus median 0.0067, maximum 0.280 of 16-mers shared), none of which contains an ATGCGGRY, with the gataE candidate at a local value of 0.000. The control also scores 0.000, at the 0th percentile of its own locus, so the metric fails. The published conserved block is AACATGCGGGCATGT, 15 bp, one base short of a shared 16-mer.

Seed-and-extend longest exact match in a 308 bp window gives the gcm control 15 bp against a within-locus null median of 17 bp and a 95th percentile of 25 bp, empirical p = 0.88; gataE gives 13 bp, p = 1.0. The control fails again.

Only the site-centred test of F4 passes. Reporting the patch analysis as informative would be wrong, and it is reported here with its failed control attached.

Mechanistic model and interpretation

The immediate molecular activity under test is sequence-specific binding of the Q8MMJ4 GCM domain to DNA within the gataE regulatory region. Everything else in the published chain, pigment cell specification, gataE transcript levels after Gcm knockdown, the timing of gataE activation relative to gcm, is downstream of that activity or independent of it.

Three mechanisms remain compatible with all the evidence collected.

Indirect regulation. Gcm specifies the pigment cell precursor state, and gataE expression follows from that state through other transcription factors. The kinetics and Notch-independence that motivated the original direct call are equally consistent with a one-step indirect route.

Direct action through a non-consensus site. GCM-family proteins can occupy degenerate sites that a consensus scan rejects. This was tested directly by rescoring with three matrices rather than counting exact consensus, and gataE still carries no hit surviving in more than one matrix at p = 1e-5. It remains live at lower stringency, where no locus separates from background. The gataE domain contains bare ATGCGG cores inside three Sp/Lv conserved patches (-9,775 to -9,200, -4,225 to -3,675, +20,200 to +21,025) that are not followed by the RY half. Those are the sequences to test first.

Direct action through tethering. Gcm could be recruited to gataE Module 10 by a partner such as Six1, which is in a positive intergenic feedback loop with Gcm, leaving no Gcm site in the DNA.

The structural arm sets the scope of the motif model. Q8MMJ4 retains N99, N101 and H103, the side chains that read bases in the mouse GCMa complex, so ATGCGGRY is the right model. The one base contact that changes, mouse I100 to T136, sits at a position contacting both backbone and base and could shift preference at the degenerate positions. That is the single route by which the sea urchin protein might tolerate ATGCGGAT better than mouse and human matrices predict, and it is untested.

How the literature bears on the sequence result

Two papers provide the positive controls that make the gataE negative interpretable. PMID: 20122918 mutated predicted SpGcm, SpGataE and SpKrl sites within the pks -1.5 kb region and showed loss of activation; the scan independently places a perfect ATGCGGGT at -1,437 inside that region at 15.20 bits. PMID: 22509525 showed that the two critical elements of the gcm late module are consensus matches to Gcm and Six1 sites; the scan places the single ATGCGGRY of the whole 51 kb gcm locus at the validated position, at 14.63 bits, and the Six1 palindrome 154 bp away. Both sites are the top-scoring position in their locus. The gataE domain has nothing comparable.

PMID: 23261933 is the source of the claim under test and asserts gataE is a direct Gcm target. The evidence behind that assertion is regulatory, not occupancy-based, and the seed hypothesis records as much.

PMID: 17570356 matters twice. It defines the gataE modules, and it defines them by exactly the Sp/Lv patch-conservation method reproduced here. The patch analysis recovers 16 such patches in the 57 kb domain against the 25 that Lee et al. found in a BAC, and none contains a GCM consensus. That observation is reported with the caveat that the method fails its own positive control for 8 bp sites.

PMID: 36519720 supplies independent support for the conservation caveat from the biology: PmGcm is not expressed in PmPks1-positive cells in Patiria miniata, and Gcm knockdown does not abrogate pigment gene expression there. Regulatory connections in this network turn over, which is the same conclusion reached here from the failure of the pks site to be conserved in L. variegatus.

GO curation implications

These are leads requiring curator verification, not finished annotations.

Narrow the free-text statement. A statement that Gcm "directly drives gataE" should be rewritten. The supportable form is that Gcm is required for normal gataE expression in pigment cell precursors, with the evidence coming from knockdown, expression kinetics and Notch-independence, and that no Gcm binding site has been mapped in the gataE regulatory region. The curated text should carry that qualification explicitly, because the sequence analysis produces no candidate site.

Keep the sequence-specific DNA binding capability, re-anchor its evidence. A molecular function term in the region of GO:0000978 (RNA polymerase II cis-regulatory region sequence-specific DNA binding) or GO:0000981 (DNA-binding transcription factor activity, RNA polymerase II-specific) is supported for Gcm. The supporting evidence should cite the gcm late module (PMID: 22509525) and the pks -1.5 kb module (PMID: 20122918), where sites were mutated and function was lost. It should not be anchored to gataE.

Biological process. Positive regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II (GO:0045944) is supportable at IMP level from the morpholino and cis-perturbation work on gcm, pks and six1. For gataE specifically, an IMP-level regulatory annotation is defensible; a statement implying promoter or enhancer occupancy at gataE is not.

Do not fall back on "protein binding". GO:0005515 is not an appropriate recommendation here and is not proposed. If the direct relationship is retained in any form, it needs an experiment measuring occupancy or affinity.

Limitations and knowledge gaps

No binding data exist for this protein. No ChIP, DNase footprint, EMSA or SELEX data exist for S. purpuratus Gcm. Every statement about what Q8MMJ4 binds is transferred from mouse and human GCM1/GCM2 and from Drosophila gcm. This is the central limitation, and it is the reason the seed hypothesis framed the question as a motif analysis in the first place.

The published module coordinates could not be retrieved. The full text of PMID: 17570356, which holds the coordinates of gataE Module 10 and Module 24, is publisher-restricted. Europe PMC returned HTTP 500 for PMC2031225 and NCBI efetch db=pmc did not serve it. The scan therefore covers the whole 57 kb domain rather than the modules. This makes the negative broader and less precise. A curator with the figures could check whether the match at -12,448 falls inside either module; the conservation profile computed here says it falls in no Sp/Lv conserved patch, and the published modules were selected from exactly such patches.

The motif test has limited power at this locus size. P(0 ATGCGGGY in 57,225 bp) = 0.609 under the measured genomic rate, and the validated gcm site is not significant by a best-score statistic (p = 0.357 to 0.614 across three matrices). The negative withholds support for a direct mechanism and does not refute one. Only an occupancy measurement resolves this.

Cross-species conservation has too little resolution for an 8 bp site. Two locus-wide statistics failed the gcm positive control: patch-level 16-mer sharing gave the functional site a local value of 0.000 at the 0th percentile of its own locus, and the longest exact Sp/Lv match in a 308 bp window gave 15 bp against a null median of 17 bp (p = 0.88). Only the site-centred test passed. This is reported as measured.

The I100 to T136 substitution is untested. No measurement exists of how this change affects GCM-domain base preference, so a shifted sea urchin consensus cannot be excluded. It is the one route by which ATGCGGAT might be tolerated.

Resources that could not be accessed programmatically

Stated plainly, with no result fabricated in their place.

  • Europe PMC fullTextXML returned HTTP 500 for PMC2031225 and PMC3561781. NCBI efetch db=pmc served PMC3561781 but not PMC2031225, which is publisher-restricted.
  • The NCBI Datasets ortholog API returned an empty object for gcm, gatae and pks1. L. variegatus orthologs were identified instead by tblastn of the S. purpuratus proteins, then by GeneID lookup and esummary coordinates.
  • bl2seq through the NCBI BLAST URL API returned no RID for 34 kb to 429 kb subject payloads. Pairwise comparison was implemented locally by seed-and-extend and shared k-mer counting.
  • An initial BLAST of the gataE site region against core_nt returned only (ATC)n microsatellite hits and showed core_nt lacks L. variegatus genomic scaffolds, carrying only PREDICTED mRNAs.

Proposed follow-up experiments and actions

ChIP-seq or CUT&RUN for Gcm in S. purpuratus mesenchyme blastula, with the gcm -4,062 and pks -1,437 sites as internal positive controls. A peak over the gataE Module 10 region settles the direct call. Absence of a peak where both controls score refutes it. This is the single most efficient experiment, because it tests occupancy directly and carries its own controls.

EMSA or fluorescence anisotropy of the recombinant Q8MMJ4 GCM domain on ATGCGGAT against ATGCGGGC and ATGCGGGT. HT-SELEX or a protein binding microarray on the S. purpuratus GCM domain, to produce a species-specific matrix in place of the transferred mouse, human and fly matrices. This would replace the weakest input to the whole analysis.

Mutagenesis of the ATGCGGAT at -12,448 in the gataE BAC-GFP reporter. The site is 12.4 kb upstream, outside the characterised modules, so a negative result is informative only alongside the ChIP experiment.

A timed double perturbation, Gcm morpholino with a gataE reporter driven by Module 10 alone, to establish whether that module responds to Gcm at all before asking which sequence mediates it.

Methods and provenance

Sequences were retrieved from NCBI nuccore by E-utilities (Spur_5.0 for S. purpuratus, Lvar_3.0 for L. variegatus). The motif is ATGCGGRY as used by Ransick and Davidson. Matrices are JASPAR MA0767.2 (GCM2), MA0646.2 (GCM1, HT-SELEX) and MA1840.2 (Drosophila gcm), converted to log-odds against a mononucleotide background measured on 1,499,503 non-N S. purpuratus genomic bases (A 0.315, C 0.186, G 0.188, T 0.311). Thresholds are empirical quantiles of 3.0 million background scores taken on both strands. The structure is PDB 1ODH, with contacts computed at a 4.0 Å cutoff. Protein alignment used Needleman-Wunsch with BLOSUM62, gap open -11, extend -1.
