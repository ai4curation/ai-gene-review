Gene Description

Sp-FoxA is the purple sea urchin orthologue of the FoxA/HNF3 class of forkhead-box transcription factors, with a winged-helix Fork-head DNA-binding domain, a Forkhead N-terminal region and a FoxA-type C-terminal region. It is a canonical endoderm regulatory gene: zygotic transcription begins at about 11 h post-fertilization in the whole veg2 endomesoderm lineage, is switched off in the prospective non-skeletogenic mesoderm at 18-20 h so that only the veg2 endoderm keeps expressing it, and later extends to veg1 endoderm, the gut after gastrulation and a patch of oral ectoderm where the mouth forms. Four cis-regulatory modules integrate a Tcf/beta-catenin toggle switch (spatial restriction to the endoderm), Su(H), Hox11/13b, Otx and Brachyury activating inputs, and a single FoxA site through which the protein represses its own gene. In the endoderm FoxA represses the mesoderm regulator gcm and thereby excludes mesodermal (pigment cell) fate from endodermal cells, helps maintain the endoderm-mesoderm boundary and provides regulatory functions essential for specification of the foregut endoderm; it also activates hedgehog. Loss of FoxA yields excess pigment and other mesenchymal cells, failure of gut formation and absence of the mouth.

Core Functions

Sequence-specific transcriptional repression in veg2 endoderm cells, acting on its own gene (through a FoxA site in cis-regulatory module I) and on the mesoderm regulator gcm, which excludes the mesodermal (pigment/mesenchymal) program from endoderm cells and maintains the endoderm-mesoderm boundary.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is FoxA repression of gcm direct (FoxA sites in the gcm cis-regulatory system), or mediated through the Notch/Su(H) input that activates gcm? The transplantation experiments establish the functional relationship but not the cis-regulatory mechanism.

Q: Should FoxA also be annotated as a transcriptional activator (GO:0001228)? Activation of hedgehog and the post-gastrular requirement for gut-specific gene expression are reported, but the cached primary evidence is abstract-level or second-hand, so no activator row was proposed.

Q: Does the oral-ectoderm (stomodaeum) function of foxa warrant a separate mouth development annotation, given that it is a distinct, non-endodermal regulatory network?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Cis-regulatory analysis of the gcm locus in the endoderm, mutating candidate FoxA sites in a gcm reporter and combining with foxa MASO, to test whether gcm repression by FoxA is direct.

Type: cis-regulatory analysis

Experiment: ChIP-seq or DAP-seq for Sp-FoxA at 24-30 hpf to define its direct target set in the endoderm (gut differentiation genes, hedgehog, adhesion and intercalation genes) and separate direct activation from indirect requirements.
