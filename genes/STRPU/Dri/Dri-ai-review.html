Gene Description

Dri (Spdeadringer, Spdri) is an ARID-class transcription factor of the ARID3/Dead ringer/Bright subfamily, with a central ARID DNA-binding domain and a C-terminal REKLES domain. In the sea urchin embryo it is expressed in two separate phases, each following prior territorial specification: first, from about 12 h until ingression, in the skeletogenic descendants of the large micromeres (the primary mesenchyme cell lineage), where it is activated by the skeletogenic regulators Ets1, Alx1 and Tbr; and second, from about 20 h, in the oral ectoderm, where it is released by the Gsc-controlled repressor gate and remains expressed in the oral "face" epithelium far into development. In the skeletogenic lineage Dri is a direct transcriptional activator, together with Ets1, of the terminal skeletogenic differentiation gene battery (for example cyclophilin, whose cis-regulatory module requires intact Dri target sites), and its cell-autonomous function is needed for skeletogenic gene expression before ingression and for spicule formation. In the oral ectoderm Dri is part of the central regulatory network that maintains oral ectoderm identity; when it is knocked down, oral-specific features are lost and the aboral marker spec1 spreads around the entire ectoderm, and the oral-side patterning information that the primary mesenchyme cells need to complete skeletogenesis is not presented. Knockdown also causes failure of gastrulation, through indirect, non-autonomous effects rather than a defect in endomesoderm specification.

Core Functions

Direct, sequence-specific transcriptional activator, acting together with Ets1, of the terminal skeletogenic differentiation gene battery in the primary mesenchyme cell lineage (for example the cyclophilin gene Sp-cyp1), thereby driving skeletogenic gene expression before ingression and spicule formation.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does Dri act as a repressor of aboral ectoderm genes (spec1 and its regulators) in the oral ectoderm, or only as an activator of oral regulatory genes, with aboral repression being indirect?

Q: Is the response of the spdri reporter to Dri a direct positive autoregulatory loop, and does it operate in both expression phases?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Dri ChIP-seq (or ChIP-qPCR on candidate modules such as Sp-cyp1 and oral ectoderm regulatory genes) in S. purpuratus at 18-24 h to identify direct targets in the PMC and oral ectoderm phases and to establish the sign of Dri input in the oral ectoderm.

Experiment: Electrophoretic mobility shift assays with purified Dri ARID domain on the mapped Sp-cyp1 218-bp module to confirm sequence-specific binding of the sites required for reporter expression.

Dri (Strongylocentrotus purpuratus, UniProt Q8MQH7) - curation notes

Identity

  • UniProt Q8MQH7 (DRI_STRPU, Swiss-Prot reviewed, 490 aa, EMBL AY130972 /
    AAM81746) is Spdeadringer (Spdri), deposited by the Davidson lab; its single RX
    line is PMID:12941621 (Amore et al. 2003), the paper that cloned and named the
    gene. The community symbol in the GRN literature is dri / Spdri; here the
    folder alias is Dri.
  • Domain architecture (UniProt FT): ARID DNA-binding domain at 202-294 (PROSITE
    PS51011) and a C-terminal REKLES domain at 389-479 (PS51486), the two-domain
    architecture diagnostic of the ARID3 / Dead ringer / Bright subfamily (InterPro
    IPR045147 "ARI3A/B/C"). PANTHER PTHR15348:SF0 (label not written from memory).
    No identity doubt: the accession, the RX paper and the GRN gene are the same
    entity.

Expression (biphasic)

  • PMID:12941621
  • Timing matters for the choice of GO terms: PMID:12941621 Dri is therefore not a specification gene in either
    territory; it is deployed after the skeletogenic and oral-ectoderm regulatory
    states are already installed, and acts to drive differentiation-gene
    batteries (PMCs) or to maintain/elaborate the oral regulatory state.
  • UniProt DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE (from the same paper): later expression in apical
    plate and ciliary band of the post-gastrular embryo.

Place in the skeletogenic (PMC) GRN

  • Inputs: dri is downstream of the double-negative-gate regulators. In the
    Oliveri/Tu/Davidson 2008 synthesis: PMID:18413610 Cis-regulatory dissection of the spdri locus confirms the
    inputs: PMID:18718463
  • Outputs: Dri, with Ets1, is a driver of the terminal skeletogenic
    differentiation gene battery. PMID:12941621 PMID:18413610
  • Direct, positive cis-regulatory input (the key MF evidence): on the Sp-cyp1
    (cyclophilin) 218-bp module, PMID:16574094 The authors generalise: PMID:16574094
  • Loss of function: PMID:12941621 Micromere-transplant chimeras separate the
    autonomous from the non-autonomous requirement: PMID:12941621

Place in the oral ectoderm GRN

  • PMID:12941621
  • Knockdown converts the whole ectoderm to aboral character: PMID:12941621 Su et al. 2009 cite this as one
    of the perturbations showing the oral GRN controls the aboral repressor:
    PMID:19268450
  • Position in the Su 2009 oral ectoderm GRN: dri belongs to the set released by
    the Gsc "repressor A" gate (the oral-ectoderm analogue of the HesC gate):
    PMID:19268450 Its later domain is the oral "face":
    PMID:19268450
  • Cis-regulation (Mahmud & Amore 2008): a 4.7 kb fragment (-3456;+389) with a
    GFP reporter reproduces both expression phases, and PMID:18718463 The reporter responds to Dri itself,
    implying autoregulation.

Pleiotropic / indirect effects (not annotated)

  • Gastrulation failure after dri MASO is indirect: PMID:12941621 It is attributed partly to a late micromere
    signalling function needed to clear SoxB1 from the prospective endoderm and
    partly to a non-autonomous oral ectoderm effect. Not a Dri participation in
    gastrulation; no BP term proposed.

GOA audit (all electronic)

  • Six rows, three IBA (PAINT, PTN000394188) and three IEA (InterPro ARID
    domain; ARBA/UniProt subcellular). DNA binding, nucleus and regulation of
    transcription by RNA polymerase II are all correct for an ARID-class TF; DNA
    binding is under-specific and the literature supports the RNA polymerase II
    cis-regulatory-region sequence-specific child. No GO-CAM contains Q8MQH7

Decisions

  • NEW GO:0001228 (IDA, PMID:16574094): direct positive cis-regulatory input on
    Sp-cyp1 is shown by site mutation and reporter loss.
  • NEW GO:0045944 (IMP, PMID:12941621; IDA, PMID:16574094).
  • NEW GO:0070169 positive regulation of biomineral tissue development (IMP,
    PMID:12941621): autonomous requirement for skeletogenic gene expression and
    failure of spiculogenesis; matches the convention used for Alx1 and Ets1.
  • NEW GO:0001715 ectodermal cell fate specification (IMP, PMID:12941621) for
    the oral-ectoderm role: Dri is part of the GRN that establishes/maintains
    oral (vs aboral) ectodermal identity; the project convention (Otx, Eve,
    SoxB1) uses this term for sub-ectodermal regional fates. GO:0060834
    oral/aboral axis specification exists but carries no annotations in QuickGO,
    so it is raised as a question rather than asserted.
  • Not proposed: mesodermal cell fate specification (Dri follows specification),
    gastrulation (indirect), EMT (dri is needed until ingression but no ingression
    defect is described as Dri-autonomous).
