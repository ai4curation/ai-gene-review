Gene Description

Alx1 is a paired-class (Q50) homeodomain transcription factor of the Cart1/Alx3/Alx4 subfamily, with a C-terminal OAR motif and a lineage-specific 41-residue "Domain 2" (D2) motif that distinguishes it from its paralogue Alx4. In the sea urchin embryo it is transcribed exclusively in the large micromere lineage, the founder cells of the skeletogenic primary mesenchyme cells (PMCs), from the first interphase after the large micromeres are born. Its expression is cell-autonomous, is driven by Ets1 and by Alx1 itself, and is confined to the micromere lineage by the Pmar1/HesC double-negative gate: HesC represses alx1 everywhere else in the embryo through defined HesC sites in the alx1 cis-regulatory system. Alx1 binds palindromic and TAAT half-site sequences as a dimer and acts as a direct activator of PMC regulatory genes (including its own gene) and of a large battery of terminal effector genes, including those required for epithelial-mesenchymal transition (ingression) and for calcium carbonate biomineralization. Morpholino knockdown blocks PMC specification and produces skeleton-less embryos in which the micromere domain instead expresses the non-skeletogenic mesoderm regulator gcm, while ectopic alx1 activates the skeletogenic program in other lineages. Alx1 is a conserved regulator of skeletal cell identity across echinoderms, and vertebrate Alx3/Alx4/Cart1 proteins play analogous roles in craniofacial and limb skeletogenesis.

Core Functions

Sequence-specific, dimer-forming paired-class homeodomain transcriptional activator that institutes and stabilizes the skeletogenic (primary mesenchyme cell) regulatory state in the large micromere lineage: it auto-activates its own gene, drives the intermediate PMC transcription factors, represses the alternative non-skeletogenic mesoderm regulator gcm, and directly activates the effector genes for ingression (EMT) and calcite biomineralization.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is Alx1 a direct transcriptional repressor of gcm (and of its own gene at high concentration), which would justify GO:0001227 / GO:0000122 annotations, or is the exclusion of the non-skeletogenic mesoderm fate mediated indirectly through Alx1-activated intermediates?

Q: Does the D2 domain, which is required for skeletogenic function and alters DNA-binding properties, act by changing dimerization/half-site binding or by recruiting a specific coactivator?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Mutational dissection of the gcm cis-regulatory module (which already has defined Su(H) sites) for Alx1 binding sites, combined with Alx1 ChIP-qPCR, to test direct repression by Alx1 in the micromere lineage.

Experiment: Co-immunoprecipitation or proximity labeling of tagged Sp-Alx1 in PMCs, with and without the D2 domain, to identify the cofactors that distinguish Alx1 from Alx4 in skeletogenic function.

Alx1 (Strongylocentrotus purpuratus, UniProt Q7Z0W3) - curation notes

Identity

  • UniProt Q7Z0W3 (Q7Z0W3_STRPU, unreviewed/TrEMBL, 430 aa) is the S. purpuratus
    Alx1 protein deposited by the Ettensohn lab (EMBL AAP34698.1); its single RX
    line is PMID:12756175, the paper that named the gene. UniProt's automated
    RecName "Homeobox protein aristaless-like 4" is a family-level ARBA name and
    should not be read as a claim that this is the Alx4 paralogue: the sea urchin
    genome has a separate alx4 gene, and Alx1 is distinguished from Alx4 by the
    D2 domain (see below). The community symbol is Alx1 (older literature also
    writes alx-1).
  • Domain architecture (UniProt FT): paired-class homeodomain at 116-176
    (PROSITE PS50071), C-terminal OAR motif at 407-420, large N-terminal
    disordered region. PANTHER PTHR24329 (label not written from memory here).

Place in the endomesoderm GRN

Alx1 is one of the four regulatory genes (with ets1, tbr, tel) plus delta that
are the direct targets of the Pmar1/HesC double-negative gate and that initiate
the skeletogenic (large micromere / primary mesenchyme cell, PMC) regulatory
state.
