Gene Description

Hnf6 (SpHnf6, also cloned as SpOnecut) is the single sea urchin member of the ONECUT class of homeodomain transcription factors, a 483-residue protein with one CUT domain and a C-terminal homeodomain, orthologous to vertebrate HNF6/ONECUT1. Its expression in the embryo is triphasic. It is transcribed maternally and its transcripts are distributed throughout the embryo before gastrulation; during this phase it acts in the skeletogenic (large micromere/primary mesenchyme cell) lineage as an early, ubiquitously present "booster" input into the biomineralization differentiation gene battery (sm50, pm27, msp130), without being required for any of the lineage-specifying PMC regulatory genes (alx1, ets1, pmar1, tbrain) or for early micromere signaling; it is also needed for expression of the mesodermal regulator gatac. After gastrulation it becomes an early, Nodal-independent component of the oral ectoderm gene regulatory network, where it is required to maintain the oral ectoderm regulatory state; in its absence the aboral marker spec1 spreads over the whole ectoderm. Finally its expression resolves to a trapezoidal band at the oral/aboral ectoderm boundary that exactly prefigures the neurogenic ciliated band, where it persists through the end of embryogenesis and where it is required for normal ciliated band formation. The ciliary-band phase of expression is conserved in the sea star AmHNF6, and ONECUT genes in other deuterostomes are expressed in the central nervous system.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

ciliary band development

Definition: The process whose specific outcome is the progression of the ciliary band over time, from its formation to the mature structure. The ciliary band is a neurogenic band of ciliated ectodermal cells at the boundary of oral and aboral ectoderm in echinoderm and other deuterostome larvae, used for swimming and feeding.

Justification: hnf6/onecut is the canonical marker of the echinoderm ciliary band and is required for its normal formation, yet GO has no term for this larval structure; the closest available terms (nervous system development, epithelium development) do not capture it.

Parent term: anatomical structure development

Core Functions

Ubiquitously present early transcriptional activator ("booster") of the skeletogenic differentiation gene battery (sm50, pm27, msp130) in the primary mesenchyme cell lineage, acting alongside the lineage-specific regulators without itself specifying the lineage.

Nodal-independent early component of the oral ectoderm gene regulatory network that maintains the oral ectoderm regulatory state and excludes aboral (spec1-positive) character; its expression later resolves to the ciliated band at the oral/aboral boundary, whose formation also requires Hnf6.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does Hnf6 bind directly to the cis-regulatory modules of sm50, pm27 or msp130, or is its "booster" input into the skeletogenic differentiation battery indirect?

Q: Which ciliated band genes depend on Hnf6, and does its ciliated band role reflect a conserved ONECUT function in neurogenic ectoderm shared with the chordate CNS expression of onecut genes?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Mutate predicted ONECUT (CUT/homeodomain) sites in the characterised sm50 and msp130 cis-regulatory modules in reporter constructs and compare expression with wild-type constructs in normal and Hnf6-morpholino embryos; confirm occupancy by ChIP with an anti-Hnf6 antibody or tagged Hnf6 at mesenchyme blastula.

Hypothesis: Hnf6 is a direct activator of PMC biomineralization effector genes.

Hnf6 (Sp-Hnf6 / SpOnecut; UniProt Q6UAY6) — curation notes

Identity

  • UniProt Q6UAY6 (TrEMBL) "One cut domain family member", gene hnf6, EMBL AAQ81630.1, NCBI GeneID 378468.
    The RX line of the UniProt record cites Otim et al. 2004 (PMID:15328009), i.e. the accession is the
    cDNA cloned by the Davidson lab for the GRN work, so there is no identity doubt between the accession
    and the GRN node.
  • The same gene was cloned independently as "SpOnecut" by Poustka et al. 2004
    PMID:15230963.
    The UniProt sequence is 483 aa, matching.
  • Domain architecture: one CUT domain (residues 272-358) plus a C-terminal homeodomain (387-447),
    the defining ONECUT-class arrangement PMID:15328009.
    PANTHER PTHR14057 (official name "TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR ONECUT"), subfamily SF47 ("HOMEOBOX PROTEIN ONECUT").
  • Sea star orthologue AmHNF6 confirms the family placement
    PMID:15661644.

Expression (triphasic)

  1. Maternal and global: PMID:15328009
  2. Oral ectoderm: after gastrulation the gene is part of the oral ectoderm GRN; it is one of the
    nodal-independent early oral ectoderm genes
    PMID:19268450.
  3. Ciliated band: PMID:15328009;
    Poustka et al. independently found PMID:15230963
    and called it PMID:15230963.
    (Poustka did not detect the earlier ubiquitous maternal phase; Otim et al. did by QPCR/WMISH.)
    The ciliary-band phase is conserved in the sea star
    PMID:15661644.

Function in the GRN (loss-of-function, morpholino)

  • Skeletogenic (PMC) lineage: Hnf6 is an early, ubiquitously present input into the PMC
    differentiation gene battery, but not into the PMC regulatory genes
    PMID:15328009.
    It is not needed for the micromere signal
    PMID:15328009.
    Oliveri et al. 2008 place it in the skeletogenic GRN as a driver of differentiation genes
    PMID:18413610
    and describe its role as an ancillary booster rather than a lineage-specifying input
    PMID:18413610.
    Summarised by the same group as PMID:15661644.
  • Mesoderm: PMID:15328009
    (not annotated: single downstream regulatory gene, direct/indirect unknown).
  • Oral ectoderm: PMID:15328009.
    In the Su et al. 2009 oral/aboral ectoderm GRN model, hnf6 is one of four early components of the
    oral regulatory state and is nodal-independent
    PMID:19268450;
    hnf6 knockdown, like dri or gsc knockdown, lets the aboral marker spec1 spread over the whole ectoderm
    PMID:19268450.
  • Ciliated band: PMID:15328009

Curation decisions

  • GOA has only two IEA rows (DNA binding; nucleus). Both are consistent with a CUT+homeodomain TF.
    DNA binding is modified to the more informative GO:0000981; nucleus accepted (inferred from
    domain content and TF function; no sea urchin immunolocalisation published).
  • NEW: GO:0000981 (IMP, Otim 2004), GO:0045944 positive regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II
    (IMP; knockdown abolishes activation of sm50/pm27/msp130), GO:0070169 positive regulation of biomineral
    tissue development (IMP; the activated genes are the spicule-matrix/biomineralization battery, as for
    the sibling Dri review), GO:0001715 ectodermal cell fate specification (IMP; maintenance of oral
    ectoderm specification, spec1 spreading on knockdown; same term used for Dri).
  • Not annotated: ciliated band formation (no GO term exists; proposed as a new term), gatac
    dependence (single downstream regulatory gene), micromere signaling (explicitly negative).
  • The proposed GO:0000981 is used rather than the activator child GO:0001228 because no
    cis-regulatory analysis demonstrates direct binding of Hnf6 to a target module; the activator
    inference rests on loss-of-function.
  • Only abstracts are cached for Otim 2004, Otim 2005 and Poustka 2004; Oliveri 2008 and Su 2009 are
    full text. A PubMed search (Otim O[Author] AND Davidson EH[Author]) returned no separate Hnf6
    cis-regulatory paper, so none is cited.

    The process whose specific outcome is the progression of the ciliary band over time,
    from its formation to the mature structure. The ciliary band is a neurogenic band of
    ciliated ectodermal cells at the boundary of oral and aboral ectoderm in echinoderm
