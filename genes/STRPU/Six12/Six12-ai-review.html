Gene Description

Six1/2 is the single sea urchin member of the SIX1/SIX2 subgroup of SIX-class homeodomain transcription factors (sine oculis family), with an N-terminal Six domain that mediates cofactor binding, a central homeodomain that recognises the GGGTATCA-type Six consensus, and a His-rich C-terminus. In the purple sea urchin embryo it is a late-tier regulatory gene of the aboral non-skeletogenic mesoderm (pigment cell precursor) regulatory state, switched on in the mesenchyme blastula after gataE, downstream of Delta/Notch signalling, Gcm and GataE, together with its cofactor Eya. Acting in the nucleus as a sequence-specific activator, Six1/2 provides a positive input through a Six site in the late cis-regulatory module of gcm, forming with Gcm a positive intergenic feedback loop that sustains gcm expression in specified pigment cell precursors after the initiating Delta signal has ceased; its knockdown lowers gcm output about three-fold but does not prevent pigment cell formation. Later it is expressed with pax6, eya, dach and soxE in the aboral veg2-derived cells at the archenteron tip and in the left coelomic pouch, where its expression depends on BMP signalling and is excluded from the right side by Nodal; in the related sea urchin Lytechinus variegatus this coelomic pouch Pax6/Six3/Six1/2/Eya/Dach1 subcircuit directs the homing of the small micromeres.

Core Functions

Sequence-specific transcriptional activator in the nucleus of aboral non-skeletogenic mesoderm (pigment cell precursor) cells. Acting through a Six consensus element in the late cis-regulatory module of gcm, and with its cofactor Eya, Six1/2 sustains gcm transcription after the initiating Delta/Notch signal has ceased, closing a gcm-gataE-six1/2 positive feedback loop that stabilises the aboral mesoderm regulatory state; knockdown lowers peak gcm output about three-fold without abolishing pigment cells.

  • GOA has only electronic rows (5 IBA from PAINT node PTN000044272, 4 IEA). All are
