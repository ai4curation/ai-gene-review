CC   -!- FUNCTION: Secreted effector that enables the plant pathogenic fungus to
CC       manipulate host defenses for successful infection (PubMed:18452583).
CC       Binds chitin, but not to any other glycan, including the N-linked
CC       glycan chitobiose (PubMed:20724636). Outcompetes host immune receptor
CC       for chitin binding through intrachain LysM dimerization
CC       (PubMed:18452583, PubMed:20724636). During infection, sequesters chitin
CC       oligosaccharides that are released from the cell walls of invading
CC       hyphae to prevent elicitation of host immunity (PubMed:20724636).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18452583, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20724636}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms homodimers. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23840930}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18452583}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression is up-regulated during plant infection.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18452583}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The LysM (lysin motif) domains are small globular domains
CC       involved in binding chitin in eukaryotes. ECP6 contains 3 LysM domains.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23840930}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: LysM1 and LysM3 domains cooperate to bring two chitin-binding
CC       regions together, composing a novel type of binding groove in which one
CC       chitin tetramer is nearly completely buried and engaged in many non-
CC       covalent interactions, including 12 hydrogen bonds. The LysM1-LysM3
CC       interdomain groove binds chitin with ultrahigh affinity.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23840930}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: LysM2 domain also contains a functional chitin-binding site.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23840930}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Leads to a significant reduction in virulence on
CC       tomato. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18452583}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: In plants, chitin acts as a microbe-associated molecular
CC       pattern (MAMP) that is recognized by lysin motif (LysM)-containing
CC       plant cell surface-localized pattern recognition receptors (PRRs) that
CC       activate a plethora of downstream immune responses. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the secreted LysM effector family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
