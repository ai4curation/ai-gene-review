CC   -!- FUNCTION: Sodium-exporting ATPase (PubMed:23414762, PubMed:29986883,
CC       PubMed:31311978, PubMed:36180431). Required for the extrusion of Na(+)
CC       from the intraerythrocytic parasites to maintain a low cytosolic
CC       concentration of Na(+) (PubMed:23414762, PubMed:25322084,
CC       PubMed:25453091, PubMed:29555632, PubMed:36180431).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23414762, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25322084,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25453091, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29555632,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29986883, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31311978,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36180431}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Na(+)(in) + ATP + H2O = Na(+)(out) + ADP + phosphate + H(+);
CC         Xref=Rhea:RHEA:14633, ChEBI:CHEBI:15377, ChEBI:CHEBI:15378,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:29101, ChEBI:CHEBI:30616, ChEBI:CHEBI:43474,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:456216; EC=7.2.2.3;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:23414762, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29986883,
CC         ECO:0000269|PubMed:31311978, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36180431};
CC       PhysiologicalDirection=left-to-right; Xref=Rhea:RHEA:14634;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000305};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Inhibited by cipargamin and other spiroindolone
CC       compounds (PubMed:23414762, PubMed:29986883, PubMed:36180431).
CC       Inhibited by 4-cyano-3-methylisoquinoline derivatives MB14 and MB10 but
CC       not RK18 (PubMed:31311978). Inhibited by (+)-SJ733, a
CC       dihydroisoquinolone compound (PubMed:25453091, PubMed:36180431).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23414762, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25453091,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29986883, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31311978,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36180431}.
CC   -!- BIOPHYSICOCHEMICAL PROPERTIES:

CC         KM=0.23 mM for ATP (in the presence of 150-153 mM of Na(+))
CC         {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29986883};

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20813948,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23414762, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29986883,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8813672}; Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed throughout the asexual cycle, including
CC       trophozoites, early schizonts and free merozoites (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:8813672). In the synchronized cultures of asexual stage
CC       parasites, relatively low expression is observed shortly after invasion
CC       (PubMed:11145964). Expression increases at 8 hours, peaks at 16 hours
CC       and decreases slightly by 32 hours after invasion (PubMed:11145964).
CC       Maximal levels are observed in mature asexual stages 40 hours after
CC       invasion (PubMed:11145964). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11145964,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8813672}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: ATP4 inhibitors cause swelling and partial lysis of
CC       infected erythrocytes, probably due to the osmotic swelling of the
CC       intracellular parasites because of Na(+) accumulation in the parasite
CC       cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25453091, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29555632,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31311978}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the cation transport ATPase (P-type) (TC 3.A.3)
CC       family. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- CAUTION: Was initially described as Ca(2+)-transporting ATPase
CC       (PubMed:11145964). In the later studies, Ca(2+) transport function has
CC       not been demonstrated (PubMed:20813948, PubMed:23414762).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11145964, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20813948,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23414762}.
