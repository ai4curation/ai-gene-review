CC   -!- FUNCTION: Nutrient transporter (PubMed:25733858, PubMed:32764664).
CC       Substrate transport is pH-dependent (PubMed:25733858). Can transport
CC       arginine, lysine, histidine, peptides, histamine and spermidine
CC       (PubMed:25733858, PubMed:32764664). Involved in maintaining the osmotic
CC       homeostasis of the digestive vacuole (PubMed:26351679,
CC       PubMed:26420308). Required for the normal asexual intraerythrocytic
CC       proliferation of parasites (By similarity). Can transport Fe(2+) and
CC       Fe(3+) (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9N623,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25733858, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26351679,

CC   -!- POLYMORPHISM: The 3D7 strain is chloroquine-sensitive in contrast to
CC       the 7G8 and Dd2 strains which are chloroquine-resistant. Compared to
CC       the 7G8 and Dd2 strains, the CRT protein from the 3D7 strain shows
CC       lower chloroquine transport rates and higher capacity for the transport
CC       of natural substrates (PubMed:25733858, PubMed:31776516,
CC       PubMed:32764664). In contrast to CRT proteins from 7G8 and Dd2 strains
CC       that can transport lumefantrine and mefloquine, the CRT protein from
CC       the 3D7 strain does not show transport activity for these drugs
CC       (PubMed:35507548). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25733858,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31776516, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32764664,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35507548}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Can function as a drug transporter (PubMed:25733858,
CC       PubMed:32764664). Can transport chloroquine, quinidine and verapamil
CC       (PubMed:25733858, PubMed:32764664). Drug-resistance-conferring
CC       mutations reduce capacities for the transport of the natural substrates
CC       (PubMed:25733858, PubMed:32764664). Active transport requires a
CC       directed pH gradient and a membrane potential (PubMed:25733858).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25733858, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32764664}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the CRT-like transporter family. {ECO:0000305}.
