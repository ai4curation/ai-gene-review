# treS (Stutzerimonas stutzeri CJ38, Q9LAS5) curation notes

## Sources

- No deep-research provider run was made for this gene; the review rests on
  the fetched UniProt record and the cached PubMed abstract.
- GOA has no annotations for Q9LAS5. One NEW molecular-function row is
  proposed from the characterization paper. [PMID:15654636, "Activity
  analysis, using either maltose or trehalose as a substrate, showed a
  reversible reaction."]
- The enzyme does not act on starch or maltooligosaccharides, and the
  recombinant protein converts maltose to trehalose at 72% yield.
  [PMID:15654636, "There was also no detectable activity of related enzymes
  with liquid starch and maltooligosaccharides as substrates."]
- Q9LAS5 = GenBank AAF26837.1, 689 aa, matching the 689-codon ORF in the
  paper. UniProt assigns it to PTHR10357 at family level only, with the
  TIGR02455 TreS_stutzeri and IPR012665 signatures, the same placement as
  P. putida KT2440 TreSA (Q88IT1).
