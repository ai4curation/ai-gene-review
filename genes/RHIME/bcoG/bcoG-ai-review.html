Gene Description

BcoG is a beta-keto acid cleavage enzyme of Sinorhizobium meliloti that converts 3-dehydrocarnitine and acetyl-CoA to betainyl-CoA and acetoacetate. This reaction connects the carnitine-dehydrogenase and betainyl-CoA-hydrolase activities of the fused BcoE-BcoF protein. Purified-enzyme chemistry was measured with the 3D0a2 protein, whose sequence matches the strain 1021 reference through Rm2011. Native GBB utilization differs between strains, so this catalytic identity does not establish growth of strain 1021 on GBB.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

3-dehydrocarnitine:acetyl-CoA trimethylamine transferase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 3-dehydrocarnitine + acetyl-CoA = N,N,N-trimethylglycyl-CoA + acetoacetate.

Justification: EC 2.3.1.317 (RHEA:47044) is measured for BcoG and P. aeruginosa CdhC; GO has only the unrelated 3-keto-5-aminohexanoate cleavage term for the family.

Parent term: acyltransferase activity, transferring groups other than amino-acyl groups
