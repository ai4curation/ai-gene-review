CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multifunctional enzyme that catalyzes the NAD(+)-dependent
CC       oxidation of L-carnitine to 3-dehydrocarnitine and the cleavage of
CC       betainyl-CoA (N,N,N-trimethylglycyl-CoA) into glycine betaine and
CC       coenzyme A (PubMed:30670548). Can also hydrolyze L-carnitinyl-CoA, but
CC       with much lower efficiency (PubMed:30670548). Is involved in a L-
CC       carnitine degradation pathway that allows R.meliloti to grow on L-
CC       carnitine as the sole source of carbon and nitrogen (PubMed:30670548).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30670548}.
