CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the oxidation of methanol to formaldehyde or
CC       formate in the presence of lanthanides (Ln). Is a key enzyme in
CC       methane/methanol metabolism, allowing M.fumariolicum to grow on methane
CC       as the sole carbon and energy source. Can also act on other primary
CC       alcohols in vitro, such as ethanol, 1-propanol, 1-butanol, and 1-
CC       hexanol, but is not able to oxidize secondary alcohols and acetaldehyde
CC       (PubMed:24034209). Uses a specific cytochrome cL, encoded by the
CC       adjacent gene in the locus, as electron acceptor (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:C5B120, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24034209}.
