Gene Description

LpdC is an FAD-containing homodimeric dihydrolipoyl dehydrogenase that catalyses reversible electron transfer between NAD(H) and protein-bound lipoyl groups. It serves as E3 in pyruvate and branched-chain ketoacid dehydrogenase complexes and, in the reductive direction, supplies electrons to the DlaT–AhpD–AhpC antioxidant relay. Purified LpdC also reduces quinones. Proteomic ligand-binding and heterologous infection experiments support additional host-interaction capacities, distinct from its central metabolic and redox functions.

The module boundary is AhpC peroxide reduction plus specifically supported regeneration chains, not all thioredoxin clients or all oxidative-stress physiology. Reconstituted activity does not establish an exclusive physiological donor or growth requirement.

Lpd is the FAD/NAD-dependent E3 enzyme, supplying electrons in the reductive direction to the lipoyl relay and oxidizing reduced lipoyl groups during ketoacid metabolism. PMID:11560483 also reports quinone reduction and transhydrogenation in vitro. These capacities are retained with assay scope.

Full PMID:21238944 was read from the normally cached PMC3040420 text. Native lpdC loss produces major metabolic and nitrosative-stress defects but increases resistance to the tested H2O2 challenge; this does not negate reconstituted peroxide reduction, nor establish an obligatory peroxide-survival pathway.
