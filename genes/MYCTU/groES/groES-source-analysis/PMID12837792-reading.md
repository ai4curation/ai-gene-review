Verbatim excerpt from Results and Discussion:
> calcium binds to the acidic side chains of the roof loops

Paraphrase: the mycobacterial Cpn10 structure coordinates calcium near acidic roof-loop residues, including Glu55. Compared with E. coli GroES it has two additional acidic residues (Asp51/Asp53). DLS detects calcium-dependent size changes in both proteins, consistent with heptamer/tetradecamer equilibrium; the exact E. coli coordination site is proposed, not directly determined. The discussion's proposed ATP transport by Cpn10 assemblies is a hypothesis, not a nucleotide-binding measurement. The paper therefore supports target metal binding but cannot establish ATP binding or independent ATPase activity for Mtb GroES. Its proposed physiological role for the isolated Cpn10 tetradecamer is not equivalent to demonstrated GroEL2–GroES folding-cage stoichiometry.
