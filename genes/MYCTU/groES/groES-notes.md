# MYCTU groES review — 2026-11-07

GroES (Cpn10) is the essential cochaperonin of Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Its heptameric lid binds the chaperonin and supports the ATP-driven protein-folding cage, with GroEL2 supplying the ATPase machinery. Metal ions bind the Cpn10 dome region and influence its conformation and oligomerization. GroES is heat inducible and has also been detected among extracellular plasminogen-binding proteins.
