CC   -!- FUNCTION: CRISPR (clustered regularly interspaced short palindromic
CC       repeat) is an adaptive immune system that provides protection against
CC       mobile genetic elements (viruses, transposable elements and conjugative
CC       plasmids). CRISPR clusters contain sequences complementary to
CC       antecedent mobile elements (spacers) and target invading nucleic acids.
CC       CRISPR clusters are transcribed and processed into CRISPR RNA (crRNA).
CC       The type III-A Csm effector complex binds crRNA and acts as a crRNA-
CC       guided RNase, DNase and cyclic oligoadenylate synthase; binding of
CC       target RNA cognate to the crRNA is required for all activities
CC       (Probable). This CRISPR-Cas system protects bacteria against
CC       transformation with plasmids containing DNA homologous to its spacer
CC       regions (PubMed:29979631). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29979631,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:29979631}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Processes pre-crRNA into individual crRNA units; accurate
CC       cleavage of pre-crRNA depends on a 3' stem-loop and the sequence of the
CC       bases in the stem. The mature crRNA is unusual for type III-A systems
CC       as it does not undergo 3' processing after Cas6 cleavage. Mature crRNA
CC       is about 71 nucleotides (nt long) with an 8 nt 5' handle and 28 nt
CC       repeat with a 10 nt stem-loop at the 3' end.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29979631}.
