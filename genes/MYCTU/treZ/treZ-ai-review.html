Gene Description

TreZ (Rv1562c) is the malto-oligosyltrehalose trehalohydrolase of Mycobacterium tuberculosis, a cytosolic glycoside hydrolase family 13 (GH13) enzyme with an N-terminal E-set domain. It hydrolyses the alpha-1,4 glucosidic bond that joins the trehalose unit to the rest of maltooligosyltrehalose, which TreY (Rv1563c) forms, releasing free trehalose and an alpha-1,4-glucan two glucose units shorter. This completes the TreY-TreZ route from glycogen-like alpha-glucans, one of three mycobacterial trehalose-biosynthesis pathways. Disrupting treZ does not affect capsular alpha-glucan or glycogen production, or growth and persistence in mice.
