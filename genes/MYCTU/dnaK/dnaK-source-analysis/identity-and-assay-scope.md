# Mycobacterial protein identity and assay scope

Official UniProt records identify H37Rv DnaK P9WMJ9/Rv0350 (625 residues), GrpE P9WMT5/Rv0351 (235), DnaJ1 P9WNV9/Rv0352 (395), and DnaJ2 P9WNV7/Rv2373c (382). Exact deposited protein-sequence comparison establishes that PDB8GB3 entity1 matches all 625 DnaK residues and entity2 matches all 235 GrpE residues, without substitutions or extensions. This supports transfer of the purified-complex results in PMID38253530 to those exact protein sequences; it does not change the host strain of the cellular experiments.

PMID27872278 purified Mtb DnaK, both J proteins and GrpE; it measured DnaK ATP turnover and refolding with systematically varied cofactors. J1 and J2 differ in activation and optimal stoichiometry. The cellular deletions/complementation in this paper and the 2024 structural paper used M. smegmatis mc²155 derivatives. They are not native H37Rv essentiality experiments. The accessible 2016 text does not identify the cloning DNA donor strain, and its supplement could not be retrieved (publisher 403, PMC download challenge, EuropePMC non-OA response). No exact assay-sequence bridge is claimed for J1/J2: their reviews explicitly infer conserved function to the H37Rv reference proteins from species-level biochemical work and independently established loci/family architecture.

PMID19470749 full PMC methods/results were inspected separately through web access because the normal publication fetch remains abstract-only. Recombinant Rv0350 was tested alongside Rv0440; capsule immunoblots, mass spectrometry and surface detection support DnaK localization. The overall CFU assay did not establish a DnaK adhesin effect, despite a small shift in the highest-occupancy microscopy bin. The existing NOT adhesion annotation is retained with this experimental context. Fibronectin-binding and plasminogen-activation claims from PMID17849409 remain unresolved without its full assay text; the abstract clearly supports plasminogen binding but foregrounds GlnA1 for fibronectin.
