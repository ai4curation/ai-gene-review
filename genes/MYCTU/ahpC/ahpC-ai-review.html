Gene Description

AhpC is a thiol-dependent peroxiredoxin that reduces hydrogen peroxide, organic hydroperoxides and peroxynitrite. Its oxidized catalytic cysteines can be regenerated through an AhpD/lipoyl-DlaT/Lpd relay or a thioredoxin C/thioredoxin-reductase system. These alternative donor chains are biochemically characterized, while their relative physiological contributions depend on conditions and other antioxidant systems. AhpC contributes to resistance to peroxynitrite and has redox-dependent oligomeric assemblies.
