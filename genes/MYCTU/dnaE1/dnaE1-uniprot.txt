CC       responsible for most of the replicative synthesis in bacteria. Pol III
CC       also exhibits 3' to 5' exonuclease activity. The alpha chain is the DNA

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: The Pol III holoenzyme complex contains at least 10 different
CC       subunits organized into 3 functionally essential subassemblies: the Pol
CC       III core, the beta sliding clamp processivity factor and the clamp-
CC       loading complex. The Pol III core (subunits alpha, epsilon and theta)
CC       contains the polymerase and the 3'-5' exonuclease proofreading
CC       activities. The polymerase is tethered to the template via the dimeric
CC       beta sliding clamp processivity factor. The clamp loader (also called
CC       gamma complex) assembles the beta sliding clamp onto the primed
CC       template and plays a central role in the organization and communication
CC       at the replication fork. The clamp-loading complex contains delta,
CC       delta', psi and chi, and 3 copies of either or both of two different
CC       DnaX proteins, gamma and tau. The DNA replisome complex has a single
CC       clamp loader (3 tau and 1 each of delta, delta', psi and chi subunits)
CC       which binds 3 Pol III cores (1 core on the leading strand and 2 on the
CC       lagging strand) each with a beta sliding clamp dimer (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with the beta-sliding clamp (DnaN) (PubMed:21219854). Co-
CC       immunoprecipitates with DarG in the presence and absence of darT
CC       (PubMed:32634279). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P10443,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21219854, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32634279}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000250}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the DNA polymerase type-C family. DnaE
CC       subfamily. {ECO:0000305}.
