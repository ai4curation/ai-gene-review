Gene Description

TreY (Rv1563c) is the maltooligosyl trehalose synthase of Mycobacterium tuberculosis, a cytosolic glycoside hydrolase family 13 (GH13) enzyme. It catalyses an intramolecular transglycosylation that converts the reducing-end alpha-1,4-linked glucose of a glycogen-like alpha-1,4-glucan into an alpha,alpha-1,1-linked terminal glucose, forming maltooligosyltrehalose. The partner hydrolase TreZ (Rv1562c), encoded by the adjacent gene, then releases free trehalose. The TreY-TreZ route is one of three trehalose-biosynthesis pathways in mycobacteria, alongside OtsA-OtsB (from glucose 6-phosphate and UDP-glucose) and TreS (from maltose).

Core Functions

Converts the reducing-end alpha-1,4 linkage of a glycogen-like alpha-1,4-glucan to an alpha,alpha-1,1 linkage, forming maltooligosyltrehalose for hydrolysis by TreZ in the TreY-TreZ trehalose biosynthesis route.
