# cysK1: H37Rv enzyme chemistry

CysK1 is a cytosolic PLP-dependent O-acetylserine sulfhydrylase of Mycobacterium tuberculosis H37Rv. It combines O-acetylserine and sulfide to produce L-cysteine. A CysE C-terminal peptide occupies its substrate-binding site and inhibits catalysis. This OAS-dependent enzyme is distinct from the OPS- and sulfur-carrier-preferring paralog CysM.

PMID17567578: Abstract and deposited 2Q3B sequence/structures: direct CysK1 catalysis, PLP chemistry and inhibitory CysE tetrapeptide. The peptide alone does not demonstrate assembly of a full CysE-CysK complex. UniProt assigns H37Rv; PDB has species-level source and exact full reference sequence plus GSH.

PMID38311199: Abstract-only direct study of mycobacterial SAT oligomers and regulatory complex. Reports reduced SAT activity in the complex. Exact construct and complex stoichiometry remain unavailable; no inference that the naturally short N terminus is a gene-model error.

PMID16000837: Direct E. coli enzyme evidence supports the inherited secondary CysK desulfhydrase activity at PAINT node PTN000034535, not a direct H37Rv assay.
