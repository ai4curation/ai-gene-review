The official symbol trxB (Rv3913, P9WHH1) denotes thioredoxin reductase, called TrxB2 or TrxR in the literature; it is not the small TrxB1 thioredoxin. PMID:16301794 and curated UniProt establish the FAD/NADPH-dependent reductase.
