# cysE: H37Rv enzyme chemistry

CysE is the cytosolic serine acetyltransferase of Mycobacterium tuberculosis H37Rv. It transfers an acetyl group from acetyl-CoA to L-serine, generating O-acetylserine for the CysK1-dependent cysteine biosynthetic route. Its oligomeric state and interaction with CysK influence activity; the mycobacterial regulatory complex can reduce serine acetyltransferase activity.

PMID23483228: Direct purified Rv2335 SAT kinetics. Normal cache abstract-only; publisher full methods independently inspected and documented in the source-analysis identity record. H37Rv genomic clone, sequence-verified and His6-tagged.

PMID17567578: Abstract and deposited 2Q3B sequence/structures: direct CysK1 catalysis, PLP chemistry and inhibitory CysE tetrapeptide. The peptide alone does not demonstrate assembly of a full CysE-CysK complex. UniProt assigns H37Rv; PDB has species-level source and exact full reference sequence plus GSH.

PMID38311199: Abstract-only direct study of mycobacterial SAT oligomers and regulatory complex. Reports reduced SAT activity in the complex. Exact construct and complex stoichiometry remain unavailable; no inference that the naturally short N terminus is a gene-model error.

PMID41053365: Full primary H37Rv deletion/complementation and infection/stress phenotypes. These are not used to infer direct protein acetylation or add virulence/oxidative-stress process annotations.
