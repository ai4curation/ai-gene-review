Gene Description

Rv3914, officially named trxA and commonly called thioredoxin C or TrxC in the biochemical literature, is a small dithiol-disulfide oxidoreductase. It accepts reducing equivalents from the NADPH-dependent thioredoxin reductase Rv3913 and transfers them to protein substrates including AhpC. Purified TrxC also supports reduction of glutathione disulfide and mycothiol disulfide in coupled assays. Its principal role is intracellular redox homeostasis; the MPT46 protein has also been identified in culture filtrate.
