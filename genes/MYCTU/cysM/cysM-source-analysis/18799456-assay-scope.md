Table 2 reports OAS activity as ND, while OPS turnover is 0.1 per second at 37°C. The product assay used 5mM acceptor, 4mM sodium sulfide, 5micrograms enzyme in 2 mL, HEPES pH 7.4, and acid-ninhydrin detection. Its cysteine calibration range was 0.04–0.8mM; no formal detection limit is stated. Slow OAS aminoacrylate formation is a separate measurement. The discussion describes minor OAS activity, but does not supply a positive complete-reaction endpoint.

The pET28a construct has a cloning-induced Thr2Ala substitution and a residual Gly-Ser-His prefix after His6 removal. Independent deposited entity 3DKI confirms these differences from the frozen P9WP53 sequence. Do not generalize this assay into proof that native P9WP53 has exactly zero OAS activity under all conditions.
