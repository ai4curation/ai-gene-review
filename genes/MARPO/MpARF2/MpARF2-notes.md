PMID:26020919 reports that MpARF2 lowered the Gal4 reporter below control in tobacco BY-2, establishing repressor capacity. The 2020 full paper, normally recovered at `url:https://edepot.wur.nl/523089` (PMID:32415296), adds native inducible MpARF2-mediated repression of auxin targets and conditional-loss growth defects, DNA competition, and LFG-dependent MpTPL association. Crucially, FRET–FLIM was conducted in Arabidopsis mesophyll protoplasts and LFG-peptide reporters in tobacco cells. The native physiological and heterologous direct-interaction results are complementary, not the same experiment. MpARF2 therefore is an active repressor as well as a competitor, not an obligate activator or merely a passive DNA blocker. The inferred essentiality from failed heritable knockout recovery is not equated to universal cell lethality.
