## Mechanism and assay boundaries

[PMID:31744875, A ligand-independent origin of abscisic acid perception](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6900503/): “The Marchantia receptor showed 30% to 50% basal activity when analyzed with the native PP2C or with the standardized HAB1”. The receptor is native-sequence MpPYL1. Read the methods: the phosphatase is ΔN MpABI1, lacking residues 1–224, not an intact native protein; Arabidopsis HAB1 is also N-terminally truncated. Enzyme inhibition uses pNPP and manganese; the result establishes PP2C inhibition, not a specific endogenous Marchantia substrate. ABA enhances an existing basal inhibitory activity. Neither ABA-independent activity nor a narrow dynamic range negates ABA binding/receptor function.

[PMID:41984838, Evolutionary-based remodeling of ABA receptors reveals the structural basis of hormone perception and regulation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13099562/): wild-type MpPYL1 is monomeric by SEC; binary MpPYL1–HAB1 and ternary MpPYL1–ABA–HAB1 crystal structures were solved. The reported 68 nM ABA Kd is measured by titrating the HAB1–MpPYL1 mixture and is not an isolated receptor affinity. The engineered MpPYL1Cs variant has enforced dimerization and altered ABA affinity; do not assign its properties to wild type. The wild-type receptor has both ligand-independent and ligand-enhanced inhibitory behavior.

[PMID:30442644, Archetypal Roles of an Abscisic Acid Receptor in Drought and Sugar Responses in Liverworts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6324230/): native Marchantia knockout and overexpression experiments establish ABA-responsive growth/transcription and desiccation phenotypes. MpPYL1 directly participates as receptor/inhibitor. These phenotypes do not mean it directly synthesizes sugars or LEA proteins. This paper describes interaction with MpABI1A; do not resolve sex-linked phosphatase accessions from the name alone.

## Localization audit and decisions

All eight seeded rows reviewed independently. GO:0004864 refined to GO:0004865 for the demonstrated PP2C target. ABA binding, signaling receptor activity, and ABA-activated signaling accepted. All four nucleus/cytoplasm rows remain UNDECIDED consistently across IBA and IEA. The cached PAINT table confirms PTN001581255 IBD assertions for these locations, which are plausible. This review has no evidence of target-specific loss and does not challenge that ancestral placement. However, no target localization experiment was identified in the inspected full texts. The 2019 MpPYL1-GFP experiments quantify fusion expression by immunoblot and physiological phenotypes, not cellular distribution. The 2026 work studies purified protein. UniProt location CC text is ARBA-only. Nuclear localization of a partner is not localization evidence for MpPYL1. Native compartment assays would resolve this uncertainty.

No NEW process annotations are proposed; core signaling is already present and the receptor itself performs the ligand-dependent phosphatase-inhibition step. Stomatal processes demonstrated in heterologous angiosperms are not transferred to Marchantia, which lacks stomata.
