# Itpr3 (rat, Q63269) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research API was available; this synthesis was compiled by hand from the cached
publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record (`Itpr3-uniprot.txt`).

## Identity and structure
- Rat IP3R3 is 2,670 residues with ~62-64% identity to IP3R1/IP3R2 and eight predicted membrane-spanning
  segments at the C-terminus [PMID:8388391 "Rat IP3R-3 is 2670 amino acids in size, has 62 and 64% identity with IP3R-1 and IP3R-2"].
- UniProt: N-terminal cytoplasmic domain, juxtamembrane domain and TM domain; homotetramer by similarity;
  IP3-, Ca2+-, ATP- and Zn2+-binding residues by similarity to human ITPR3 (Q14573) structures
  [file:rat/Itpr3/Itpr3-uniprot.txt "Homotetramer (By similarity)."].

## Channel activity
- Recombinant rat IP3R3 recorded in planar bilayers: 74-80 pS, open probability 30-40%, apparent IP3 affinity 0.40 microM,
  low-affinity cooperative ATP site [PMID:15533917 "InsP3R3 has a low-affinity (2 mM) ATP modulatory site"].
- Rat IP3R3 recorded in the native ER/outer nuclear membrane of Xenopus oocytes; >90% of channels were rat homotetramers
  [PMID:10694253 "were contributed by homotetramers of r-InsP3R-3 monomers"].
- Ligand binding: IP3 > Ins(1,3,4,5)P4 > InsP6 [PMID:8388391 "it bound IP3 as well as inositol 1,3,4,5-tetrakisphosphate and inositol hexakisphosphate"].

## Regulation
- PKA phosphorylates IP3R3 in RINm5F cells and increases IP3 binding and Ca2+ release
  [PMID:17373911 "binding affinity and IP(3)-induced Ca2+ release activity were enhanced in permeabilized cells that were pre-treated with forskolin or PKA."].
- PKC phosphorylation reduces IP3R3 activity [PMID:17320950 "whereby activated PKC dampens IP3R-3 activity"].
- cAMP sensitises all IP3R subtypes, including rat IP3R3, independently of PKA [PMID:20189985 "high concentrations of cAMP similarly sensitize all IP(3)R isoforms to IP(3)"].
- Bok binds IP3R3 only weakly [PMID:23884412 "Bok binds most strongly to IP3R1 and IP3R2, and barely to IP3R3"].

## Location
- ER: recombinant IP3R3 in ER [PMID:8388391]; RINm5F IP3R3 co-fractionates with calnexin
  [PMID:19116207 "IP3R3 appeared in the same fractions as calnexin, an integral ER protein"]; FRAP shows free diffusion in ER membranes
  [PMID:15537642; PMID:21550988 "IP(3)R1 and IP(3)R3 were uniformly distributed within the membranes of the endoplasmic reticulum (ER)"].
- Apical concentration in intestinal epithelium [PMID:8288584 "with the highest concentration of staining occurring adjacent to the apical brush border of villus cells."].
- Neuronal terminals in rat forebrain; Schwann cells [PMID:10096607; PMID:17091480].

## Tissue distribution
- Predominant subtype in rat islets and RINm5F cells [PMID:8388391; PMID:17320950 "RINm5F cells who express almost exclusively (approximately 90%) the IP3R-3"].
- Dominant IP3R of rat colonic epithelium [PMID:18005397].
