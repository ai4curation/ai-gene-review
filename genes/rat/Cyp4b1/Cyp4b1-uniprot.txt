CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the omega- and (omega-1)-hydroxylation of saturated
CC       fatty acids and n-alkanes (PubMed:2229008). Also bioactivates
CC       xenobiotics such as the procarcinogen 3-methoxy-4-aminoazobenzene and
CC       the cyclic arylamines 2-aminofluorene and 2-aminoanthracene (By
CC       similarity). In addition, catalyzes O-dealkylation of 7-ethoxycoumarin
CC       (PubMed:2229008). Functions at the interface of xenobiotic and
CC       endobiotic metabolism, contributing both to fatty acid and hydrocarbon
CC       oxidation and to the bioactivation of diverse xenobiotics
CC       (PubMed:2229008). Preferentially hydroxylates the terminal carbon
CC       (omega-hydroxylation) of medium-chain fatty acids (C8-C10), with
CC       regioselectivity decreasing as chain length increases. C8 fatty acids
CC       and C7 n-alkanes display optimal chain length for regioselective omega-
CC       hydroxylation, and n-alkanes (C7-C10) are generally turned over faster
CC       and with higher regioselectivity than the corresponding fatty acids. In
CC       addition to omega/omega-1 hydroxylation, also catalyzes alpha-, beta-,
CC       gamma- and delta-hydroxylation of mid-chain length fatty acids, whereas
CC       fatty alcohols of the same chain length are almost exclusively
CC       hydroxylated at the omega, omega-1 and omega-2 positions (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P15128,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q64462, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2229008}.
