# Kcnj11 (rat, P70673) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this session; this summary was assembled
manually from the cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record.

## Identity

Kcnj11 encodes Kir6.2 (ATP-sensitive inward rectifier potassium channel 11), a 390-aa
two-transmembrane (M1, pore loop, M2) member of the inward rectifier (Kir, TC 1.A.2.1)
family, KCNJ11 subfamily (UniProt P70673; RGD:69247; GeneID 83535).

## Molecular function

- Rat Kir6.2 was cloned and, co-expressed with SUR, reconstitutes the beta-cell KATP
  current [PMID:8607800 "Co-expression of rat Kir6.2 and sulfonylurea receptor in human
  embryonic kidney cells generated a potassium current with the properties of the
  beta-cell ATP-sensitive potassium channel."].
- Kir6.2 with SUR2 reconstitutes cardiac/skeletal muscle KATP [PMID:8630239 "Coexpression
  of SUR2 and the inward rectifier K+ channel subunit Kir6.2 in COS1 cells reconstitutes
  the properties of K(ATP) channels described in cardiac and skeletal muscle."].
- Channel is a hetero-octamer of four Kir6 and four SUR subunits [PMID:14672537
  "Functional KATP (ATP-sensitive potassium) channels are hetero-octamers of four Kir6
  (inwardly rectifying potassium) channel subunits and four SUR (sulphonylurea receptor)
  subunits."]; Kir6.2 directly binds the SUR2A C-terminal region [PMID:14672537].
- Cryo-EM (rat Kir6.2 with hamster SUR1): "KATP channel opening is stimulated by PIP2 and
  inhibited by ATP" and opening "widens the inhibitory ATP binding pocket to disfavor ATP
  binding" [PMID:38509107]. ATP-binding residues (48, 50, 330) annotated in UniProt.
- Pore requirement: the GFG->AFA pore mutant Kir6.2AFA abolished SUR2A-induced K+ current
  in H9C2 cells [PMID:20123112 "Kir6.2AFA, a mutant form of Kir6.2 with largely decreased
  K(+) conductance, abolished the effect of SUR2A on K(+) current"].
- Slide-helix V64M in Kir6.2 reduces ATP sensitivity and glibenclamide block
  [PMID:28842488]. Charge interactions between Kir6 distal C-terminus and SUR2 NBD2
  transmit nucleotide/opener regulation [PMID:25236767].
- Native rat ventricular myocytes express Kir6.1 and Kir6.2 and two KATP conductances
  [PMID:17097686].

## Trafficking and partners

- Ankyrin-B binding motif in Kir6.2 C-terminus required for membrane targeting
  [PMID:19805355 "Kir6.2 lacking this motif displays aberrant channel targeting due to
  loss of association with the membrane adapter ankyrin-B (AnkB)."].
- Hsp90/Hsc70/Hsp40 co-IP with HA-Kir6.2 in INS-1 cells but not in COS cells lacking SUR1;
  authors conclude chaperones interact preferentially with SUR1 [PMID:20427569].
- Glucose deprivation increases surface Kir6.2 via AMPK in beta cells [PMID:19720793].
- SUR2-containing channels cycle through endosomes; Kir6.2 is in sarcolemmal and
  endosome-enriched fractions of rat heart and moves to the sarcolemma after ischemia
  [PMID:20971764].
- Kir6.2 Q52E corrects trafficking of select SUR1 mutants [PMID:23695995].

## Localization (rat)

Sarcolemma of ventricular myocytes [PMID:12738227], T-tubule striated pattern
[PMID:15647111], intercalated disk enrichment [PMID:23066018], DRG neuron plasma
membrane, axons, nuclear envelope [PMID:20102598], acrosome of spermatids
[PMID:21328565].

## Physiology

Pancreatic beta-cell KATP (Kir6.2/SUR1) couples glucose metabolism to membrane
excitability and insulin secretion [PMID:20427569]; channel opening hyperpolarizes and
inhibits insulin release (pharmacological activators of Kir6.2/SUR1 inhibit insulin
release, PMID:15163199). Expression-change studies (estradiol, testosterone, nicotine,
TNF/Ang II, iptakalim) report Kir6.2 mRNA regulation but not Kir6.2 activity in those
responses [PMID:23032400, PMID:18950632, PMID:22403787, PMID:19460779, PMID:15964031].
