# Pecam1 (rat, Q3SWT0) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this session; this synthesis was written by the
reviewer from cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record
(`Pecam1-uniprot.txt`). Each statement carries its provenance.

## Identity and architecture

- Rat Pecam1 (PECAM-1, CD31), UniProtKB Q3SWT0, 678 aa, RGD:61927, GeneID 29583.
- Type I transmembrane glycoprotein: signal peptide 1-17, extracellular region 18-589 with six
  Ig-like C2-type domains, single TM helix 590-610, cytoplasmic tail 611-678 carrying an ITIM motif
  (658-663) with Tyr-660 [UniProtKB:Q3SWT0 "MOTIF 658..663 /note="ITIM motif""].
- Family: Ig superfamily; PANTHER PTHR11481 (Fc receptor-like family), subfamily SF5
  "PLATELET ENDOTHELIAL CELL ADHESION MOLECULE" [UniProtKB:Q3SWT0].

## Adhesion

- Homophilic adhesion requires the membrane-distal Ig domain 1 [PMID:9252369 "the NH2-terminal
  (membrane-distal) immunoglobulin domain of CD31 is necessary but not sufficient to support stable
  homophilic adhesion"]; CD31 also has heterophilic partners [PMID:9252369 "CD31 is capable of
  mediating both homophilic and multiple heterophilic adhesive interactions"].
- UniProt (by similarity to human/mouse): trans-homodimer via Ig domains 1-2; heterophilic binding to
  CD177 in neutrophil transmigration [UniProtKB:Q3SWT0 FUNCTION/SUBUNIT].

## Leukocyte transendothelial migration (diapedesis)

- Human in vitro: anti-PECAM or soluble PECAM blocks monocyte/neutrophil transmigration; leukocytes
  arrest on the apical surface over the junction [PMID:8340753 "transmigration, which, based on these
  studies, requires PECAM-1"].
- Rat in vivo: anti-PECAM-1 antibody blocked neutrophil accumulation in peritoneum and lung
  [PMID:8248808 "PECAM-1 is required for neutrophil transmigration in vivo"].
- Rat mesentery intravital microscopy: domain 1-specific antibody suppressed IL-1beta-induced
  extravasation but not rolling or firm adhesion [PMID:10861081 "The anti-PECAM-1 mAb suppressed
  leukocyte extravasation, but not leukocyte rolling or firm adhesion, elicited by IL-1beta in a
  dose-dependent manner"].

## ITIM signaling and SHP-2

- In rat RBL-2H3 basophilic cells PECAM-1 becomes tyrosine-phosphorylated after FcepsilonRI
  aggregation [PMID:9148965].
- Tyrosine-phosphorylated PECAM-1 binds SHP-2 (PTPN11) via the SHP-2 SH2 domains [PMID:9388260 "A
  fusion protein containing the two SH2 domains of SHP-2 precipitated PECAM-1 from cell lysates and
  also directly bound to phosphorylated PECAM-1."].
- In rat brain endothelial RBE4 cells, PECAM-1 cross-linking recruited SHP-2 and blocked
  ICAM-1-induced RhoA activation, stress fibre formation and cortactin phosphorylation
  [PMID:17662049 "PECAM-1 cross-linking per se did not activate RhoA (Fig. 1f, lane c), but completely
  inhibited ICAM-1-induced RhoA activation"].

## Mechanosensing

- PECAM-1 is the force-transmitting component of the PECAM-1/VE-cadherin/VEGFR2 mechanosensory complex
  [PMID:16163360 "PECAM-1 (which directly transmits mechanical force), vascular endothelial cell
  cadherin (which functions as an adaptor) and VEGFR2 (which activates phosphatidylinositol-3-OH
  kinase) comprise a mechanosensory complex"].

## Expression / regulation (rat)

- IFN-gamma lowers and TGF-beta raises PECAM-1 expression in rat liver sinusoidal endothelial cells
  and mononuclear phagocytes; surface expression measured by flow cytometry [PMID:18466611].
- CD31 is upregulated in LPS-injured rat lung [PMID:22592748, abstract only].
