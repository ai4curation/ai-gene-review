# Casp3 review notes

## 2026-09-05 — SFT binding assessment and annotation suitability

The GO:0005123 SFT prediction is UNC, rather than automatically NPI or CNN. The current review marks death receptor binding over-annotated because DISC association does not establish direct receptor contact. The accessible rat spinal-cord-injury abstract describes Fas association with FADD, caspase-8, cFLIP and caspase-3 in a DISC [PMID:17518537, “forming a death-inducing signaling complex (DISC)”]. This supports complex association but does not resolve the direct-contact question. PubMed and the publisher landing page were checked; the latter lists restricted full-text access, and the cached record has `full_text_available: false`. Failure to verify the full experiment cannot justify declaring the binding prediction refuted. The main gene review and experimental GOA annotation are unchanged.

The supported SFT wrapper now records an explicit gene/term exception for `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED` with a source-based UNC rationale. A different rejection action or accepted negation does not inherit this exception. This follows the PredictionAssessmentEnum uncertainty definition; it is not an assertion that direct binding is absent.

**Action changes.**
- Enzyme activator activity (IBA): MODIFY -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. This edit was carried over from the interrupted session. GO:0008656 is an activator (binding) term, but caspase-3 activates procaspases by catalysis. The only seed for the IBD node is the rat PLA2-activator IMP, which comes from inhibitor epistasis [PMID:12492473 "caspase-3 is downstream of ceramide in activating the brain plasmalogen-selective phospholipase A2"].
- Negative regulation of cytokine production (IEA, ISO): REMOVE -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. Caspase-3 proteolysis suppresses cytokine signalling [PMID:30878284 "activated caspase-3 cleaved cGAS, MAVS, and IRF3 to prevent cytokine overproduction"; PMID:36002459 "caspase-3 can mediate the cleavage of NF-κB members p65/RelA, RelB, and c-Rel via its protease activity"].
- Pyroptotic inflammatory response and its positive regulation (IEA, ISO): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. The old reason cited only gasdermin D inactivation. Caspase-3 also activates GSDME [UniProtKB:P55213 "Also involved in pyroptosis by mediating cleavage and activation of gasdermin-E (GSDME)"].
- Cellular response to staurosporine (IEA, ISO), neurotrophin TRK receptor signaling, response to wounding, DNA damage response, response to UV (ISO): REMOVE -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. Caspase-3 does act in these contexts as the executioner, so the annotations are stimulus-context over-annotations rather than wrong. Their propagation_review blocks were changed to TERM_SCOPING_PROBLEM.
- Sensory perception of sound (ISO, mouse): REMOVE -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE [PMID:11374883 "Mice that lack caspase-3, which functions in apoptosis, were generated by gene targeting and shown to undergo hearing loss."].
- Heart development, T cell homeostasis, negative regulation of activated T cell proliferation (ISO, mouse): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE -> UNDECIDED. No cached source supports them, and the cached Casp3-knockout immunology paper covers only B cells [PMID:12970760].
