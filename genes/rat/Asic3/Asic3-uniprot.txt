CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms pH-gated heterotrimeric sodium channels that act as
CC       postsynaptic excitatory receptors in the nervous system
CC       (PubMed:10842183, PubMed:11120882, PubMed:11587714, PubMed:9261094).
CC       Upon extracellular acidification, these channels generate a biphasic
CC       current with a fast inactivating and a slow sustained phase
CC       (PubMed:10842183, PubMed:11120882). ASIC3 is more sensitive to protons
CC       and gates between closed, open, and desensitized states faster than
CC       other ASICs (PubMed:11120882). Displays high selectivity for sodium
CC       ions but can also permit the permeation of other cations
CC       (PubMed:10842183, PubMed:11120882). As a neuronal acid sensor, probably
CC       contributes to mechanoreception, acid nociception, and heat nociception
CC       (By similarity). By forming heterotrimeric channels with ASIC2,
CC       generates a biphasic current with a fast inactivating and a slow
CC       sustained phase, which in sensory neurons is proposed to mediate the
CC       pain induced by acidosis that occurs in ischemic, damaged or inflamed
CC       tissues (PubMed:10842183, PubMed:11120882, PubMed:28396446,
CC       PubMed:9368048). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q6X1Y6,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10842183, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11120882,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11587714, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28396446,

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Inhibited by the diuretic drug amiloride
CC       (PubMed:10842183, PubMed:9261094). Inhibited by gadolinium ions
CC       (PubMed:10842183). Inhibited by extracellular Ca(2+) (PubMed:11120882,
CC       PubMed:12526774). Activated by lactate (PubMed:11528414,
CC       PubMed:12526774). Salicylic acid, diclofenac and aspirin inhibit the
CC       sustained current component (PubMed:11588175). Activated by the
CC       vertebrate neuropeptides NPFF and NPSF, and the related FMRFamide
CC       (PubMed:11587714, PubMed:12668052). Specifically and reversibly
CC       inhibited by the a sea anemone toxin APETx2 (PubMed:15044953). ASIC3-
CC       containing channels are potentiated by the cono-RFamide CNF-Tx1.1, and
CC       probably CNF-Tx1.2 and CNF-Tx1.3 (AC P0DL71) (PubMed:28396446).

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Can form homotrimeric channels (PubMed:10842183).
CC       Heterotrimer; forms functional heterotrimers producing channel with
CC       different properties (PubMed:11587714). Forms heterotrimers with ASIC2;
CC       gives rise to a biphasic current with a sustained current which
CC       discriminates poorly between Na(+) and K(+) (PubMed:10842183,
CC       PubMed:11587714, PubMed:9368048). Interacts with STOM; inhibits ASIC3
CC       acid-evoked current (PubMed:22850675). Interacts with LIN7B (via PDZ
CC       domain); increases ASIC3 expression at the plasma membrane (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with MAGI1 (via PDZ domain); probably regulates
CC       ASIC3 (By similarity). Interacts with GOPC (via PDZ domain); probably
CC       regulates ASIC3 (By similarity). Interacts with DLG4 (via PDZ domain);
CC       reduces ASIC3 expression at the plasma membrane (PubMed:15317815).
