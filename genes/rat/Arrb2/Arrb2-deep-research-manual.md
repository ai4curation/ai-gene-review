# Arrb2 (rat, UniProt P29067): manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this review. This file was written by hand
from the cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record
(`genes/rat/Arrb2/Arrb2-uniprot.txt`). Every assertion carries a provenance tag.

## Identity

- Rat beta-arrestin-2 (arrestin-3), 410 aa, gene Arrb2 (RGD:2157, GeneID 25388). It belongs
  to the arrestin family [UniProtKB:P29067 "Belongs to the arrestin family."], with arrestin N
  and C domains (Pfam PF00339, PF02752).
- Cloned from rat brain as a novel beta-arrestin with 78% identity to beta-arrestin-1
  [PMID:1517224 "Overall, beta-arrestin2 exhibits 78% amino acid identity
  with beta-arrestin1."].

## Canonical function: GPCR desensitization and internalization

- In reconstituted systems, beta-arrestin-2 quenched coupling of the phosphorylated
  beta2-adrenergic receptor to Gs [PMID:1517224 "beta-Arrestin2 was equipotent to
  beta-arrestin1 and specifically inhibited beta 2AR function."].
- UniProt summary of the mechanism [UniProtKB:P29067 "During homologous desensitization,
  beta-arrestins bind to the GPCR-phosphorylated receptor and sterically preclude its
  coupling to the cognate G protein"].
- Endocytic adaptor (clathrin-associated sorting protein) binding clathrin and AP-2
  [UniProtKB:P29067 "The beta-arrestins target many receptors for internalization by acting
  as endocytic adapters (CLASPs, clathrin-associated sorting proteins) and recruiting the
  GPRCs to the adapter protein 2 complex 2 (AP-2) in clathrin-coated pits (CCPs)."]. The
  rat protein's LIEF motif mutant loses clathrin binding (UniProt MUTAGEN 374..377, PMID:10097102).
- In rat vascular smooth muscle cells AT1R stimulation forms an endogenous c-Src/beta-arrestin/AP-2
  complex [PMID:15498833 "c-Src stabilizes the agonist-independent association between
  beta-arrestin2 and the beta-subunit of AP-2"].
- Receptor-specific evidence in rat cells: alpha1B-adrenergic receptor endocytosis depends
  mostly on beta-arrestin-2 [PMID:18523139 "RNA interference showed that beta-arrestin 2 plays
  a prominent role in alpha1b-AR endocytosis."]; mu-opioid receptor endocytosis in striatal
  neurons is blocked by dominant-negative arrestin-3 [PMID:16120787]; FSH receptor recruits
  endogenous beta-arrestins in Sertoli cells [PMID:16899567]; AT1R binds beta-arrestin-2 in
  aorta [PMID:19522833]; PAF receptor [PMID:23911909]; D1 dopamine receptor in striatal
  neurons [PMID:19200235].
- Endosomal trafficking: class B receptor/beta-arrestin complexes travel to endosomes
  [PMID:14757828; PMID:25016018 "We find a novel role for MAPK in the B2R/β-arrestin-2
  complex formation, receptor trafficking and signaling mediated by an ERK1/2
  regulatory motif in the hinge domain of the rat β-arrestin-2 (PET(178)P)"].

## Arrestin-dependent signalling (scaffold)

- ERK1/2 scaffold [UniProtKB:P29067 "Acts as a signaling scaffold for MAPK pathways such as
  MAPK1/3 (ERK1/2) and MAPK10 (JNK3)."]. In rat VSMCs beta-arrestin-2 siRNA reduced AT1R
  ERK activation by angiotensin and SII [PMID:19254952 "Activation by both ligands is
  diminished by depletion of beta-arrestin2 by small interfering RNA"], and mediated an
  anti-apoptotic ERK/p90RSK + PI3K/AKT pathway converging on BAD [PMID:19171933].
- Rat striatal neurons: D1 receptor, beta-arrestin-2 and p-ERK co-immunoprecipitate
  [PMID:19200235]. Rat astrocytes: kappa-opioid sustained ERK and proliferation require
  beta-arrestin-2 [PMID:19014370]. Rat hippocampal neurons: beta-blocker biased agonism of a
  beta-arrestin-2/ERK calcium signal [PMID:21078978]. Rat kidney fibroblasts: SII-AT1R ERK
  and ECM synthesis [PMID:28274926]. PAF receptor/ERK/MMP-2 in VSMCs [PMID:23911909].
- AKT/GSK3 scaffold with PP2A (mouse in vivo) [PMID:16051150; PMID:18191226].
- MDM2 binding by oligomeric beta-arrestin-2 and nucleocytoplasmic shuttling [PMID:17984062
  "the interaction with and subsequent titration out of the nucleus of the protooncogene Mdm2
  specifically require beta-arr2 oligomers"].
- Opposite to beta-arrestin-1 in TRPV4 ubiquitination [PMID:20650893 "Surprisingly, knocking
  down β-arrestin 2 led to an increase in angiotensin-induced ubiquitination of TRPV4."].
- Centrosome/primary cilium; binds 14-3-3 [PMID:19008961].
- Heterodimerizes with alpha-arrestin ARRDC1 for Itch-driven Notch degradation [PMID:23886940].

## Physiology (downstream, mostly perturbation phenotypes)

- Intrathecal beta-arrestin-2 siRNA enhances morphine analgesia and reduces tolerance
  [PMID:21926413]. Morphine changes Arrb2 mRNA in brain [PMID:12614678]. Diurnal Arrb2
  expression in nucleus accumbens [PMID:34919270].
- Neuroprotection via mGluR7/ERK in sevoflurane neurotoxicity [PMID:26621121].

## Citation problems noticed

- PMID:157224 (cited by RGD for GPCR signalling and postsynaptic membrane IDA) is a 1979
  bone-resorbing-factor paper; almost certainly a typo of PMID:1517224.
- PMID:12890920 (cited for D2 receptor domain-specific binding) is a dystrophin/reperfusion
  report with no arrestin content in the abstract.
- PMID:24719556 (cited for positive regulation of epithelial cell apoptotic process) is a
  mesenteric artery contractility study; the full text has no epithelial apoptosis data.
