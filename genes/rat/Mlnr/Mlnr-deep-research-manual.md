# Mlnr (rat, UniProt A0ACM8Q218): manual literature research

No deep-research provider was available in this session; this summary was compiled
manually from cached publications (`publications/PMID_*.md`) and PubMed searches.

## Identity and nomenclature

- UniProt A0ACM8Q218 (TrEMBL, unreviewed, PE 3) is the Ensembl-derived rat entry
  ENSRNOP00000017317, gene `Mlnr`, RGD:621262, GeneID 252859, RefSeq NP_852029.3,
  352 aa, rat chromosome 19.
- NCBI Gene 252859 lists symbol `Mlnr` ("motilin receptor") with alias `Trhr2`
  and other designation "thyrotropin releasing hormone receptor 2". The mouse
  ortholog (GeneID 170732, chr 8) is still named `Trhr2`.
- The protein is the rodent TRH receptor type 2 (TRH-R2): it matches InterPro
  IPR002120 (TRH receptor), PRINTS PR01654 (TRHRECEPTOR2) and PANTHER
  PTHR46061 (THYROTROPIN-RELEASING HORMONE RECEPTOR), CDD cd14995 7tmA_TRH-R.
  The literature on this gene is entirely TRH-R2 literature.
- Rodents lost the true motilin receptor: "We speculate that the MLNR gene became a
  pseudogene before the divergence of the squirrel and other rodents about 75 mya"
  [PMID:19696113]. The `Mlnr` symbol on this locus therefore reflects an
  orthology-based renaming of the rodent Trhr2 locus, not motilin-receptor function.
  No motilin binding by this protein has been reported.

## Function

- Cloning (rat brain): "A new subfamily member of thyrotropin releasing hormone (TRH)
  receptor gene, TRHR2, was isolated from rat brain cDNAs." "specific binding of TRH
  to TRHR2 was observed with Kd value of 9 nM" in transfected COS cells; cyclo(His-Pro)
  did not bind [PMID:9735333, abstract only].
- Signalling: rat TRH-R1 and TRH-R2 "exhibit similar binding and stimulated signaling
  activity via the phosphoinositide-calcium transduction pathway"; TRH-R2 has higher
  basal (constitutive) signalling via AP-1, Elk-1 and CREB reporters, inhibited by the
  inverse agonist midazolam [PMID:10499553].
- Mouse TRH-R2 (92% identical to rat) "signals via the phosphoinositide pathway like
  mTRH-R1"; RGS4 suppresses basal and TRH-stimulated signalling, consistent with Gq
  coupling [PMID:11181534].
- TRH-R2 "is more rapidly internalized than TRH-R1" and both are cell-surface receptors
  [PMID:12683933 review; PMID:12130552].
- Selective TRH-R2 agonists exist (PMID:16162016, PMID:17035026, PMID:21302359) and
  are CNS-active (analeptic, anticonvulsant), consistent with brain-restricted expression.

## Expression and localization

- Brain-specific 9.5 kb mRNA [PMID:9735333].
- TRH-R2 mRNA widely distributed in brain (thalamus, cortex, cerebellum, reticular
  formation); TRH-R2 immunoreactivity in histaminergic neuronal cell bodies of the
  tuberomammillary nucleus (TMN), where TRH-R1 was not detected [PMID:17760865, full text].
  The authors propose a TRH-TRH-R2-histamine pathway that contributes to TRH-induced
  suppression of feeding and possibly arousal; the receptor's role is inferred from
  co-localization, not from receptor-specific perturbation.
- No TRH-R2 expression in pancreas/beta-cell lines [PMID:15171688].

## Gaps

- No knockout phenotype for rat TRH-R2 is reported in the cached literature.
- Physiological significance of constitutive signalling is unknown.
- No human ortholog of TRH-R2 exists (humans have TRHR only).
