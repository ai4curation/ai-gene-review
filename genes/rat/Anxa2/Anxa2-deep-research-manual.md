# Anxa2 (rat, UniProt Q07936) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this review; this file was compiled by
hand from cached publications in `publications/`, PubMed metadata and the UniProt
record `Anxa2-uniprot.txt`. Quotations are verbatim from the cited sources.

## Identity and family

- Annexin A2 (p36, calpactin I heavy chain), 339 aa in rat, four annexin repeats
  (PF00191; PROSITE PS51897) and an N-terminal tail that carries the S100A10-binding
  site (residues 2-24) and the Src (Tyr24) and PKC (Ser26) phosphorylation sites
  (UniProt FT lines, by similarity to human P07355).
- UniProt: "Belongs to the annexin family." and "Calcium-regulated membrane-binding
  protein whose affinity for calcium is greatly enhanced by anionic phospholipids."
- Subunit: "Heterotetramer containing 2 light chains of S100A10/p11 and 2 heavy
  chains of ANXA2/p36 (By similarity)."

## Molecular activities

### Ca2+-dependent anionic phospholipid binding, PI(4,5)P2 binding
- [PMID:14734570 "In liposome sedimentation assays, we show that recombinant annexin
  2 binds to phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate (PtdIns-4,5P(2)) but not to
  other poly- and mono-phosphoinositides."] Affinity ~5 uM, enhanced by Ca2+.
  Experiments used rat basophilic leukemia (RBL) cells expressing annexin 2-GFP.
- [PMID:19193640 "The proteins are characterized by their ability to bind and order
  membrane phospholipids, particularly membranes enriched in cholesterol, with
  binding being most commonly but not invariably regulated by Ca2+"]

### Actin binding and regulation of actin dynamics
- [PMID:16601677 "we further demonstrate that annexin 2 specifically inhibits
  filament elongation at the barbed ends."] (abstract only)
- [PMID:14734570 "Annexin 2 is also an F-actin-binding protein"]
- [PMID:19193640 "Annexin 2 forms a heterotetrameric complex with S100A10 in vivo,
  and it was this complex that was first reported to bundle actin filaments in
  vitro."]

### Cell-surface plasminogen/tPA co-receptor (with S100A10)
- [PMID:11739291 "Recently, annexin II was identified as a coreceptor on endothelial
  cells for plasminogen and tissue plasminogen activator."] Recombinant annexin II
  increased plasmin generation on HUVECs and improved patency of thrombosed rat
  carotid arteries.
- [PMID:14702107 "homozygous annexin II-null mice displayed deposition of fibrin in
  the microvasculature and incomplete clearance of injury-induced arterial
  thrombi."] and "t-PA-dependent plasmin generation at the endothelial cell surface
  was markedly deficient"; neoangiogenesis also reduced.

## Rat-specific cell biology

- v-Src transformation of Rat-1 fibroblasts (PMID:19193640): annexin 2 is
  phosphorylated by v-Src, relocalizes to cortex, ruffles, macropinosomes and
  Rab11-positive perinuclear recycling endosomes; siRNA knockdown blocks v-Src
  targeting to the plasma membrane, FAK-Y576 phosphorylation, morphological
  transformation and anchorage-independent growth.
  ["In cells depleted of annexin 2 by small interfering RNA, v-Src becomes
  activated at the permissive temperature but does not target to the plasma
  membrane or to perinuclear vesicles, and cell transformation does not occur."]
- Lung alveolar type II cells (PMID:18332131, abstract only): annexin A2 "plays a role
  in lung surfactant secretion"; co-IP with Rab14.
- Kidney ureteric bud (PMID:23525114): dentin phosphoprotein (DSPP-derived) binds
  annexin 2 and 6; colocalization at the cell membrane.
- AQP2 immunoaffinity complex from rat papilla (PMID:15823548): annexin A2 among 11
  co-purifying proteins.
- Neuronal rafts (PMID:14499952, abstract only): plasminogen-binding proteins enriched
  in hippocampal-neuron rafts.
- Thyroid (PMID:9022675): annexin II levels rise in hypothyroid rats, interpreted as an
  indirect TSH-mediated effect.

## Interpretation

Core: Ca2+-dependent binding to anionic phospholipids (PS, PI(4,5)P2) at the
cytoplasmic face of the plasma membrane and endosomes; actin filament binding and
regulation of cortical actin; S100A10 heterotetramer formation; as the AnxA2-p11
tetramer on the endothelial cell surface, promotion of tPA-dependent plasminogen
activation. Many ISO-propagated process terms (transcription, neurogenesis, lung
development, collagen fibril organization) are indirect or weakly supported.
