# Klf15 (rat, Q5FVT6) - manual literature research

Deep-research API providers were not available in this session, so this file records a manual
literature survey (PubMed searches plus the cached publications in `publications/`). It is not an
automatically generated deep-research report.

## Identity

- Rat Klf15 (RGD:70918, GeneID 85497), UniProtKB Q5FVT6 (TrEMBL, unreviewed), 415 aa.
- Member of the Sp/KLF C2H2 zinc-finger family; three C-terminal C2H2 zinc fingers (residues
  ~320-407, PROSITE PS50157) form the DNA-binding domain; the N-terminal ~300 residues are a
  regulatory/transactivation region with a disordered segment (151-216).
- Original name KKLF, "kidney-enriched Kruppel-like factor" [PMID:10982849 "A novel member of the
  Cys2-His2 zinc finger gene designated KKLF (for "kidney-enriched Krüppel-like factor")"].

## Molecular function: sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factor

- Binds GC-rich elements; the human KKLF consensus is GGGGNGGNG [PMID:10982849 "A gel mobility shift
  assay revealed sequence-specific binding of recombinant KKLF and MAZ proteins to the CLC-K1 GA
  element"].
- Activator of target promoters:
  - GLUT4 (Slc2a4) in adipocytes/myocytes, synergizing with MEF2A and requiring a KLF15 site
    [PMID:12097321 "this activity requires an intact KLF15-binding site proximal to the MEF2A site"].
  - KChIP2 (Kcnip2) in heart, ChIP-verified, requires the zinc-finger domain; also shown in neonatal rat
    ventricular myocytes [PMID:22367544 "Adenoviral overexpression of Klf15 in neonatal rat ventricular
    myocytes robustly induced KChIP2"].
  - Nephrin/podocin in podocytes [PMID:22493483 "We found that KLF15 is able to bind directly to the
    promoter of nephrin and podocin and stimulate expression of these two key podocyte differentiation
    markers"].
  - BMPER in endothelial cells (human, gel shift) [PMID:19767294 "KLF15 binds directly to a predicted
    KLF-binding element at -284 bp within the BMPER promoter"].
  - PEPCK in rat hepatocytes [PMID:15649433 "Forced expression of KLF15 in cultured hepatocytes increased
    both the expression and the promoter activity of the gene for phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase
    (PEPCK)"], and amino-acid-degrading enzyme genes with PGC1alpha [PMID:20393151].
  - Cardiac lipid-flux genes via p300 recruitment [PMID:24407292 "An important mechanism by which KLF15
    regulates its direct transcriptional targets is via interaction with p300 and recruitment of this
    critical co-activator to promoters"].
- Repressor activity in other contexts:
  - Blocks MAZ activation of CLC-K1 [PMID:10982849 "KKLF coexpression with MAZ appeared to block the
    activating effect of MAZ"].
  - Represses rhodopsin/IRBP promoters [PMID:15277472].
  - Represses CTGF in rat cardiac fibroblasts by preventing P/CAF recruitment [PMID:18586263 "KLF15
    inhibits recruitment of the co-activator P/CAF to the CTGF promoter with no significant effect on
    Smad3-DNA binding"].
  - Inhibits GATA4, MEF2 [PMID:17438289] and MRTF-A/B and myocardin [PMID:22586493] activity.
  - Inhibits p300-mediated acetylation of p53 [PMID:20375365 "KLF15 activation inhibits p300-mediated
    acetylation of p53"].

## Location

- Nuclear [PMID:10982849 "immunohistochemistry revealed the nuclear localization of KKLF protein"];
  the zinc fingers contain the NLS [PMID:22586493 "When all three zinc fingers are fused to eGFP, eGFP is
  restricted to the nucleus"].

## Physiological roles (mostly mouse genetics, some rat cell work)

- Anti-hypertrophic in heart: overexpression in neonatal rat ventricular cardiomyocytes inhibits
  hypertrophy; KO mice develop exaggerated hypertrophy under pressure overload [PMID:17438289].
- Anti-fibrotic: represses CTGF in neonatal rat ventricular fibroblasts; KO hearts more fibrotic
  [PMID:18586263]. Rat pressure-overload model: KLF15 regulates TGF-beta, CTGF, MRTF-A and collagen
  [PMID:25633973, abstract only, not cached].
- Metabolic: fasting gluconeogenesis via amino-acid catabolism gene expression [PMID:17403374
  "KLF15-/- liver and skeletal muscle show markedly reduced mRNA expression of amino acid-degrading
  enzymes"]; glucose uptake via GLUT4 [PMID:12097321].
- Circadian control of cardiac repolarization via KChIP2 [PMID:22367544].
- Podocyte differentiation [PMID:22493483].
- Expression responses: endothelin-1 downregulates Klf15 mRNA in neonatal rat cardiomyocytes
  [PMID:18406357 "Consistent with our microarray data, Klf3, Klf11 and Klf15 were downregulated in
  response to ET-1"]; ET-1 also downregulates KLF15 in endothelial cells [PMID:19767294].
