# Research & Curation Notes: rat Abi1 (UniProtKB:Q9QZM5)

## 1. Identity & Sequence Numbering (`PTHR10460:SF2`)
- **Gene / Protein**: Rat `Abi1` (Abl interactor 1; `RGD:621008`, synonym `e3B1`), canonical sequence `Q9QZM5` (476 aa, `sequence_version: 3`), PANTHER subfamily `PANTHER:PTHR10460:SF2` (`ABL INTERACTOR 1`), 1:1 ortholog of human `ABI1` (`Q8IZP0`) and mouse `Abi1` (`Q8CBW3`).
- **Domain & Residue Coordinates on 476-aa `Q9QZM5`**:
  - N-terminal WAVE-binding / t-SNARE coiled-coil homology region (residues 45–107), containing **`Tyr53`** (`MOD_RES 53`, `TKAYTTQS`), whose ABL1-dependent phosphorylation is required for NMDA-induced translocation of Abi1 from the postsynaptic density to the nucleus (`Y53A` blocks nuclear entry and traps Abi1 perinuclearly without disrupting synaptic localization; `PMID:17304222`).
  - Central disordered proline-rich region (`MOD_RES 208` `Tyr208` and `MOD_RES 211` `Ser211`, homologous to human/mouse `Tyr213` and `Ser216` due to a 5-aa offset around residues 150–158).
  - C-terminal SH3 domain (residues 414–473; conserved SH3 tyrosine `Tyr423`, `MOD_RES 423`), which directly binds the proline-rich region of postsynaptic scaffold ProSAP2/SHANK3 (`Q9JLU4`, `PMID:17304222`).

## 2. Direct Experimental Biology in *Rattus norvegicus*
- **Postsynaptic Density Organization, Nanoscale Spine Actin Architecture & Synapse-to-Nucleus Signaling (`PMID:10995551`, `PMID:17304222`, `PMID:25293574`)**:
  - Courtney et al. (`PMID:10995551`, abstract-only locally) demonstrated enrichment of Abi1, Abi2, and Abl in synaptosomes and growth cone particles in the developing and adult **mouse** nervous system (note UniProt exported a direct `EXP` `GO:0014069` row on rat `Q9QZM5` citing `PMID:10995551`, flagged via `source_defects` `WRONG_EVIDENCE_CODE`/`CHECK` while retaining `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` alongside direct rat PSD data).
  - Proepper et al. (`PMID:17304222`, *EMBO J*, full text available) demonstrated in rat hippocampal neurons and brain fractions that Abi1 localizes to neurites, growth cones, dendritic spines, and the postsynaptic density (PSD) of excitatory glutamatergic synapses; directly binds a proline-rich consensus motif in ProSAP2/Shank3 via the Abi1 C-terminal SH3 domain; knockdown by RNAi causes excessive dendritic branching and immature filopodia-like spine morphology with reduced mature synapses; and NMDA receptor stimulation triggers Abl-dependent `Tyr53` phosphorylation and reversible nuclear translocation of Abi1, where it associates with the Myc/Max (`P52164`) complex to enhance E-box reporter transcription.
  - Chazeau et al. (`PMID:25293574`, *EMBO J*, full text available) used super-resolution single-molecule imaging in rat hippocampal neurons to show that the WAVE complex (including Abi1 and Nap1) is immobilized in nanodomains immediately adjacent to the postsynaptic density (`GO:0014069`, `GO:0098978`), spatially segregated from peripheral spinous formin/elongation zones to drive branched actin nucleation and dendritic spine protrusion.
- **Conserved Core WRC & Adaptor Functions**:
  - Supported by `IBA` (`PTN000048906`) and `ISO` projections from human `Q8IZP0` and mouse `MGI:104913` (`Q8CBW3`) for `GO:0031209` (`SCAR complex`), `GO:0035591` (`signaling adaptor activity`), `GO:0017124` (`SH3 domain binding`), `GO:0030296` (`protein tyrosine kinase activator activity`, retained as `ACCEPT` by curator deference alongside `mouse/Abi1` and `human/ABI1` while noting substrate selectivity in `PMID:12672821`, context-dependent inhibition of nonmyristoylated c-Abl by coexpressed full-length ABI1 in `PMID:18328268`, and `mouse/Abi2` for future family-level harmonization; note that QuickGO `Abi1-goa.tsv` for `Q9QZM5` lacks `GO:0061098`, so `GO:0061098` is omitted from `core_functions[2].directly_involved_in` per `core_function_process_not_in_annotations` rather than manufactured as `action: NEW`), `GO:0030027` (`lamellipodium`), `GO:0032433` (`filopodium tip`), and `GO:0072673` (`lamellipodium morphogenesis`).
