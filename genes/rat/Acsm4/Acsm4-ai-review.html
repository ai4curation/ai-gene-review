Gene Description

Acsm4 (O-MACS, olfactory-specific medium-chain acyl-CoA synthetase) encodes a mitochondrial AMP-forming acyl-CoA synthetase of the medium-chain (ACSM) subfamily of the ANL adenylate-forming enzyme superfamily. It carries an N-terminal mitochondrial transit peptide and the AMP-binding and AMP-binding C domains, and catalyses the ATP-dependent ligation of free medium-chain fatty acids (C6-C12, e.g. hexanoate, octanoate, decanoate, dodecanoate) to coenzyme A, forming the acyl-CoA thioester with release of AMP and diphosphate. This activation step precedes the use of the fatty acid in downstream metabolism. Recombinant tagged protein localizes to mitochondria. In rat, expression is restricted to the olfactory epithelium, where the transcript is found in a dorso-medial zone (zone 1) in olfactory sensory neurons, supporting cells and basal cells, and is already present in the olfactory placode at embryonic day 11.5. Its physiological substrates in the olfactory epithelium and its biological role there are not known.

Biology

  • O-MACS is a medium-chain acyl-CoA synthetase. Its expression is restricted to the olfactory epithelium (OE), in zone 1. PMID:12709059
  • Activity: PMID:12709059. UniProt records C6-C12 substrates (hexanoate, octanoate, decanoate, dodecanoate; EC 6.2.1.2) as ECO:0000269 from this paper.

  • Role: the authors only speculate. PMID:12709059

Key curation issue: synthase vs synthetase

  • GO:0004321 fatty-acyl-CoA synthase activity is the fatty acid synthase reaction (EC 2.3.1.86). Acsm4 is an acyl-CoA synthetase, a ligase (EC 6.2.1.2).
  • The RGD IDA row from PMID:12709059 is the only seed of the PAINT IBD on PTHR43605 root node PTN001944787 (PTHR43605 PTN001944787 GO:0004321 F IBD false RGD:727928). That IBD sends the wrong term to every family member, including mouse and human ACSM4 and the PSEPK PP_2213 precedent.
  • Both rat rows are marked REMOVE. Source defects are recorded: GO_CENTRAL on PTN001944787 (CONFIRMED) and RGD on the IDA row (PROBABLE, since only the abstract was read).

Other decisions

  • GO:0006633 fatty acid biosynthetic process (IBA): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. The seeds are MGI IDAs on Acsm1/Acsm3 from PMID:11470804, which says the products are PMID:11470804. A ligase makes acyl-CoA, not fatty acid. The likely intended term is GO:0046949 fatty-acyl-CoA biosynthetic process.
  • GO:0006637 acyl-CoA metabolic process: MODIFY to GO:0046949.
  • GO:0015645 fatty acid ligase activity: MODIFY to GO:0031956, which the gene already carries.
  • GO:0005549 odorant binding (NAS): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. The paper offers it only as a hypothesis, and enzyme substrate engagement is not annotated as a binding function. It is not removed, because the RGD curator saw the full text.
  • Mitochondrial matrix (IBA, PTN001292823; seeds Acsm3 and bovine ACSM1, both matrix by fractionation PMID:11470804): ACCEPT. The core function location stays at mitochondrion because the matrix location has not been tested on Acsm4 itself.
