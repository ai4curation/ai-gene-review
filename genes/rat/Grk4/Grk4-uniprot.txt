CC   -!- FUNCTION: Specifically phosphorylates the activated forms of G protein-
CC       coupled receptors, such as ADRB2, DRD1 and DRD3 (By similarity). Such
CC       receptor phosphorylation initiates beta-arrestin-mediated receptor
CC       internalization, and signaling events leading to their desensitization
CC       (By similarity). Mediates phosphorylation of DRD1 in the absence of
CC       dopamine activation, resulting in constitutive desensitization (By
CC       similarity). Plays an important role in the regulation of renal sodium
CC       handling and blood pressure (PubMed:16636192).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P32298, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16636192}.
