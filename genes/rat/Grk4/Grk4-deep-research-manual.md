# Grk4 (rat, P70507) - manual literature review

No deep-research API was available for this review; this summary was written by hand from the
cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and PubMed searches. Abstract-only sources are marked.

## Identity

- Rat Grk4 (Gprk2l), UniProt P70507, RGD:61858, GeneID 59077. G protein-coupled receptor kinase of the
  GRK4 subfamily (GRK4/5/6) of the AGC Ser/Thr kinase family; N-terminal RGS-homology (RH) domain, central
  protein kinase domain, C-terminal AGC-kinase extension [file:rat/Grk4/Grk4-uniprot.txt "Belongs to the
  protein kinase superfamily. AGC Ser/Thr protein kinase family. GPRK subfamily."].
- Two rat splice variants: GRK4A (575 aa, testis) and GRK4B (545 aa, lacking exon VI; kidney outer
  medulla) [PMID:9607785, abstract only, "Rat GRK4B (545 amino acids) delineates a new variant that is
  identical to GRK4A except for a 31-amino acid deletion in the N-terminal domain"].

## Catalytic activity

- Both rat variants phosphorylate rhodopsin in vitro, the standard surrogate GRK substrate
  [PMID:9607785, abstract only, "GRKs4A and B are both functional protein kinases, as demonstrated in a
  rhodopsin phosphorylation assay."]. Rhodopsin is a retinal GPCR and is not a physiological substrate of
  a testis/kidney/brain kinase; the assay establishes GPCR kinase activity generally.
- Kinase activity is required for mGlu1 desensitization and agonist-dependent mGlu1a phosphorylation was
  documented [PMID:11099476, abstract only, "Kinase activity was necessary for desensitizing mGlu(1a)
  receptor and agonist-dependent phosphorylation of this receptor was also documented."].
- Human GRK4 isoforms phosphorylate agonist-activated D3 receptor in human proximal tubule cells
  [PMID:19520868, "GRK4-γ and GRK4-α mediated a 3- and 2-fold increase in the phosphorylation of
  agonist-activated D 3 receptor, respectively."]; UniProt transfers ADRB2, DRD1, DRD3 phosphorylation by
  similarity from human P32298.

## Receptor desensitization and internalization

- mGlu1 in cerebellar Purkinje cells: GRK4 mediates homologous desensitization; antisense knockdown in
  cultured Purkinje cells impairs desensitization; receptor and GRK4 colocalize and redistribute on agonist
  exposure [PMID:11099476, abstract only, "Here we show that GRK4 is expressed in cerebellar Purkinje cells,
  where it regulates mGlu(1) metabotropic glutamate receptors"].
- GRK4 knockdown in Purkinje cells reduces agonist-dependent mGlu1 internalization; beta-arrestin mediates
  the downstream MAP kinase activation [PMID:12519791, abstract only, "A major involvement of GRK4 and of
  beta-arrestin in agonist-dependent receptor internalization and MAP kinase activation, respectively, was
  documented in cerebellar Purkinje cells using an antisense treatment to knock down GRK4"].
- Rat IEC-6 intestinal cells: D1 receptor rapidly desensitizes; heparin and an anti-GRK4-6 antibody
  prevent it, so GRK4 and/or GRK6 are implicated (the reagent does not discriminate the two)
  [PMID:15166006, abstract only, "GRK4 and 6 appear to be involved in agonist-mediated responsiveness and
  desensitization."].

## Renal physiology and hypertension (rat)

- SHR have ~90% higher renal GRK4 and serine-phosphorylated D1R; renal cortical GRK4 antisense increases
  sodium excretion and attenuates the age-related rise in blood pressure in SHR but not WKY
  [PMID:16636192, abstract only, "These studies provide direct evidence of a crucial role of renal GRK4 in
  the D1 receptor control of sodium excretion and blood pressure in genetic hypertension."].
- Combined GRK4 + AT1R antisense in SHR [PMID:18957817, "selective renal reduction of GRK4 expression by
  infusion of GRK4 As-Odns increases UnaV, attenuates the increase in arterial BP with age"].
- GRK4 and D3R co-localize in proximal and distal tubules and glomeruli of WKY rat kidney
  [PMID:19520868, "GRK4 was strongly expressed in the proximal tubules (S 1 and S 3 segments), the distal
  convoluted tubules, and the glomeruli."].

These physiological effects are downstream consequences of renal dopamine-receptor desensitization; they
are not processes executed by the kinase.

## Localization

- UniProt (by similarity to human): cytoplasm and cell cortex; palmitoylated; at the cell surface and in
  the cytoplasm under basal conditions, internalizes on receptor stimulation.
- Human proximal tubule cells: GRK4 in cytoplasm and cell membrane, in lipid rafts [PMID:19520868, "GRK4 is
  also distributed in both the cytoplasm and the cell membrane of hPTCs."].
- Rat Purkinje cells (dendrite, cell body) per RGD IDA rows from PMID:11099476 (abstract only).

## PMID:17908240

Willets et al. 2007 (M1 mAChR desensitization in rat hippocampal neurons, full text available) does not
mention GRK4; it studies GRK2 and lists GRKs 2, 3, 5 and 6 as the GRKs identified in these cultures
[PMID:17908240, "GRKs are widely expressed in the brain and we have identified GRKs 2, 3, 5 and 6 in our
hippocampal cultures (Willets et al. 2004)."]. It provides no evidence about Grk4 function.
