## 1. Identity

Rat *Slc18a2* (RGD:3694, GeneID 25549) encodes the synaptic vesicular amine transporter
VMAT2 (SVAT/MAT), a 515-residue 12-transmembrane protein of the SLC18 family within the
major facilitator superfamily. It was cloned twice in 1992: by expression cloning of
reserpine-sensitive serotonin transport activity
[PMID:1438304 "A cDNA for a rat vesicular monoamine transporter, designated MAT, was isolated by expression cloning in a mammalian cell line (CV-1)"],
and by selection in MPP+ — the latter clone (VMAT1/*Slc18a1*) was adrenal-specific, with
"a distinct but highly related protein" in brainstem monoamine neurons
[PMID:1505023 "We have detected messenger RNA transcripts for this transporter only in the adrenal gland. Monoamine cell populations in the brain stem express a distinct but highly related protein."].
The paralogue VMAT1 (rat *Slc18a1*, RGD:3693) differs pharmacologically and in substrate
affinity, notably for histamine and tetrabenazine
[PMID:8125935 "SVAT has a higher affinity than CGAT for monoamine substrates, in particular for histamine"],
[PMID:8125935 "SVAT shows approximately 10-fold greater sensitivity to tetrabenazine than CGAT"].

## 2. Molecular function: monoamine/H+ antiport

VMAT2 is a secondary active transporter that exchanges a cytosolic cationic monoamine for
two luminal protons, using the proton-motive force generated by the vesicular V-ATPase.
Uptake into the lumen requires ATP and a proton gradient
[PMID:1438304 "In cells permeabilized with digitonin, uptake was ATP dependent with an apparent Km of 1.3 microM. Uptake was abolished by the proton-translocating ionophore carbonylcyanide p-trifluoromethoxyphenylhydrazone and with tri-(n-butyl)tin, an inhibitor of the vacuolar H(+)-ATPase."]
The curated UniProt FUNCTION states the stoichiometry explicitly
[UniProtKB:Q01827 "Electrogenic antiporter that exchanges one cationic monoamine with two intravesicular protons across the membrane of secretory and synaptic vesicles."]
with Rhea reactions for serotonin, dopamine and histamine.

Substrate range: serotonin (preferred, sub-micromolar Km), dopamine, norepinephrine,
epinephrine and histamine
[PMID:1438304 "The rank order of potency to inhibit uptake was reserpine > tetrabenazine > serotonin > dopamine > norepinephrine > epinephrine. Direct comparison of [3H]monoamine uptake indicated that serotonin was the preferred substrate."],
[PMID:8860238 "Transport of (3)H-histamine by both VMAT1 and VMAT2 was reserpine-sensitive but only transport by VMAT2 was inhibited by tetrabenazine."]
Polyamines are NOT substrates — spermine/spermidine are handled by SLC18B1/VPAT, and the
same study assayed purified reconstituted rat VMAT2 as the comparator
[PMID:25355561 "we found that neither VMAT2 nor VAChT transport Spd and Spm"],
[PMID:25355561 "The cDNAs of rat VMAT2 and human VAChT were described previously"].
Heterologous expression of rat VMAT2 in yeast reconstitutes proton-driven serotonin
transport and dihydrotetrabenazine binding, and identifies acriflavine as an additional
substrate
[PMID:20007701 "cerevisiae cells expressing rVMAT2 displayed time-dependent and proton-driven [ 3 H]serotonin transport"],
[PMID:20007701 "Expression of rVMAT2 confers resistance to acriflavine and to the parkinsonian toxin 1-methyl-4-phenylpyridinium (MPP + ) by their removal into the yeast vacuole."]
Residues required for transport are known from mutagenesis
[PMID:25355561 "In the case of rat VMAT2, replacement of Asp33, Lys139, Asp400, or Asp427 markedly decreased serotonin transport activity"].

Pharmacology: reserpine is a competitive substrate-site blocker, tetrabenazine a
VMAT2-selective non-competitive inhibitor; Phe136 contributes to tetrabenazine affinity
[PMID:20007701 "The results suggest that either one of the aromatic residues Phe and Tyr at position 136 may contribute to rVMAT2 sensitivity toward the inhibitor TBZ, and is most likely needed for an efficient binding of the inhibitor to the protein."]

**Not a Na+/Cl- symporter.** The plasma-membrane reuptake transporters (SERT/SLC6A4,
DAT/SLC6A3, NET/SLC6A2) are Na+/Cl--coupled; VMAT2 is a H+ antiporter and operates on the
vesicle membrane. The two transporter classes were explicitly contrasted at cloning
[PMID:1438304 "The MAT cDNA clone defines a vesicular monoamine transporter representing a distinct class of neurotransmitter transport molecules."]

## 3. Localization

- Synaptic vesicles and large dense-core vesicles of central monoaminergic neurons, by
  immunogold EM
  [PMID:7568015 "We find that both immunoperoxidase and immunogold labeling for VMAT2 localize to DCVs and small synaptic vesicles in axon terminals, the trans-Golgi network of neuronal perikarya, tubulovesicles of smooth endoplasmic reticulum, and potential sites of vesicular membrane recycling."],
  [PMID:7568015 "In axon terminals, immunogold labeling for VMAT2 was preferentially associated with DCVs at sites distant from typical synaptic junctions."]
- Somata and dendrites of midbrain dopamine neurons (SN, VTA), on SER-like tubulovesicles,
  SSVs and DCVs — and explicitly **not** on the plasma membrane
  [PMID:8753875 "In dopaminergic dendrites, VMAT2 was extensively localized to tubulovesicles that resembled saccules of SER, and less frequently localized to isolated small synaptic vesicles (SSVs) or large dense-core vesicles (DCVs)."],
  [PMID:8753875 "In contrast, there was no detectable immunolabeling for VMAT2 along the nuclear or plasma membranes of perikarya in either region, and only rare immunogold labeling for VMAT2 along saccules of rough endoplasmic reticulum."]
- Sorted directly to LDCVs of the regulated secretory pathway by cytoplasmic C-terminal
  signals, conferring somatodendritic exocytosis
  [PMID:16301178 "In neuroendocrine PC12 cells, VMAT2 localizes exclusively to large dense-core vesicles (LDCVs), and we now show that cytoplasmic signals target VMAT2 directly to LDCVs within the biosynthetic pathway. In neurons, VMAT2 localizes to a population of vesicles that we now find undergo regulated exocytosis in dendrites."]
- Axon terminals and boutons; VMAT2 segregates from VGLUT1 and selects a distinct
  recycling pathway
  [PMID:20534840 "VMAT2 segregates partially from VGLUT1 in the boutons of midbrain dopamine neurons, indicating the potential for distinct release sites."]
- C1 adrenergic neurons of the rostral ventrolateral medulla, differentially in soma versus
  axon
  [PMID:18973576 "Immunoreactivity for VMAT2 occurred in 31% of C1 soma, with a high proportion of these in the caudal part of the RVLM."]
- Peripheral/neuroendocrine: gastric ECL cells (histamine storage) and pancreatic beta
  cells. Notably, rat tissue mast cells appear to lack both VMAT isoforms
  [PMID:8860238 "the apparent absence of VMAT1 and VMAT2 in tissue mast cells"].

## 4. Protein partners and regulation

- Tyrosine hydroxylase and aromatic amino acid decarboxylase form a complex with VMAT2 on
  synaptic vesicles, coupling dopamine synthesis to vesicular loading
  [PMID:19903816 "In rat striata, TH and AADC co-immunoprecipitate with VMAT(2)"],
  [PMID:19903816 "Finally, the coupling between synthesis and transport of DA into vesicles was impaired in the presence of fragments involved in the VMAT(2)/TH/AADC interaction."]
- Hsc70/Hspa8 binds the N-terminus and third cytoplasmic loop and inhibits transport
  [PMID:19457116 "A direct binding was observed between the amino terminus and the third cytoplasmic loop of VMAT2, as well as, a region containing the substrate binding and the carboxy-terminal domains of Hsc70."],
  [PMID:19457116 "Recombinant Hsc70 produced a dose-dependent inhibition of VMAT2 activity."]
- The first luminal loop senses vesicular amine content and triggers G-protein (Go2/Gq)
  dependent down-regulation of its own uptake
  [PMID:16926160 "vesicular content is involved in the activation of vesicle associated G-proteins via a structure sensing the luminal monoamine content. The first luminal loop of VMATs may represent a G-protein-coupled receptor that adapts vesicular filling."]
- CAPS1/CAPS2 promote VMAT1/VMAT2-mediated uptake
  [PMID:19008227 "We conclude that CAPS proteins are involved in optimizing vesicular monoamine uptake and storage mediated by VMAT1 and VMAT2."]
- Casein kinase II phosphorylates Ser512/Ser514 (UniProt refs PubMed:9045708, 22673903).

## 5. Physiology, drugs and toxicants

- Vesicular dopamine storage capacity tracks VMAT2 protein level and determines the
  behavioural/neurochemical response to cocaine; high-responder rats have more accumbal
  VMAT2 and more vesicular dopamine
  [PMID:18315567 "A one-way anova revealed that the levels of both the small and the large VMAT-2 protein were significant less in the nucleus accumbens of LR than of HR"],
  [PMID:18315567 "The nucleus accumbens of LR was marked by smaller levels of dopamine inside storage vesicles than the nucleus accumbens of HR"],
  [PMID:18315567 "Given that vesicular monoamine transporters-2 (VMAT-2) control the amount of dopamine inside storage vesicles (Pothos et al. 2000; Pothos 2002), the levels of the accumbal VMAT-2 were also measured."]
- Cocaine redistributes VMAT2-containing vesicles away from synaptosomal membranes via D2
  receptors, lowering dopamine transport and release
  [PMID:19038779 "the redistribution of VMAT-2 and associated vesicles away from synaptosomal membranes is associated with a decrease in total DA transport and DA content in the membrane-associated VMAT-2-containing subcellular fraction"]
- Methylphenidate kinetically upregulates vesicular dopamine sequestration while lowering
  membrane-associated VMAT2 immunoreactivity
  [PMID:18992277 "in vitro MPD incubation kinetically upregulated VMAT-2 in membrane-associated vesicles, increased vesicular DA content, and increased exocytotic DA release"]
- Methamphetamine damages dopaminergic and serotonergic terminals, with VMAT2 used as a
  terminal marker [PMID:21159748].
- Toxicant sequestration: VMAT2 confers resistance to MPP+ by moving it into vesicles
  [PMID:1505023 "the vesicular transport of biogenic amines modulates susceptibility to N-methyl-4-phenylpyridinium (MPP+), the active metabolite of the neurotoxin N-methyl-1,2,3,6-tetrahydropyridine that produces a model of Parkinson's disease"].
- Expression responses (mRNA/protein down- or up-regulation) to manganese welding fumes
  [PMID:20224926], zinc/paraquat [PMID:20553223], fasting in ECL cells
  [PMID:17536021 "significantly decreased expression of the cellular L-histidine uptake transporter SN2 and of the vesicular monoamine transporter 2 (VMAT-2) responsible for histamine uptake into secretory vesicles"],
  and flattened corticosterone rhythm [PMID:19494803].
- Pancreatic beta cells: tetrabenazine depletes pancreatic dopamine and alters
  glucose-stimulated insulin secretion, i.e. VMAT2 modulates insulin release via vesicular
  monoamine handling
  [PMID:18577569 "these data suggest that VMAT2 regulates in vivo glucose homeostasis and insulin production, most likely via its role in vesicular transport and storage of monoamines in beta-cells"].

## 6. Summary of core function

VMAT2 is the vesicular monoamine/2H+ antiporter of the synaptic-vesicle and dense-core
vesicle membrane. It converts the V-ATPase proton gradient into a steep luminal monoamine
gradient, thereby (i) loading serotonin, dopamine, noradrenaline, adrenaline and histamine
into releasable vesicles, setting quantal size; (ii) defining which vesicles can release
monoamines, including dendritic LDCVs that mediate somatodendritic dopamine release; and
(iii) incidentally protecting cells by sequestering cationic toxicants such as MPP+.
Its activity is tuned by luminal filling via a G-protein-coupled mechanism, by Hsc70, by
CAPS proteins, and by its physical coupling to the dopamine-synthetic enzymes TH and AADC.
