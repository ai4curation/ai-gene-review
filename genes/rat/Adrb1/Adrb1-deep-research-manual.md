# Adrb1 (rat, P18090) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this review; this file was assembled by hand from
cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record `Adrb1-uniprot.txt`. Every claim
carries a provenance tag.

## Identity and family

- Rat beta-1 adrenergic receptor, 466 aa, class A (rhodopsin-like) GPCR; UniProt P18090, RGD:2059,
  GeneID 24925. [UniProtKB:P18090 "Belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor 1 family. Adrenergic
  receptor subfamily. ADRB1 sub-subfamily."]
- Cloned from a rat genomic library; single-copy gene, one N-glycosylation site (Asn15) and three
  PKA consensus sites (Ser296, Ser301, Ser401); mRNA highest in pineal gland, moderate in other brain
  regions and heart. [PMID:1695899 "The rat beta 1-AR gene encodes a protein of 466 amino acids that
  contains one consensus site for N-linked glycosylation (Asn-15) and three consensus sites for
  cAMP-dependent protein kinase phosphorylation (Ser-296, Ser-301, and Ser-401)."]
- Seven helical TM segments (58-81, 96-121, 129-154, 177-199, 222-245, 313-338, 347-366), long
  cytoplasmic C-tail ending in an E-S-K-V class I PDZ ligand. [UniProtKB:P18090 FT TRANSMEM / MOTIF]

## Molecular function: Gs-coupled beta1-adrenergic receptor

- Expressed rat receptor binds iodocyanopindolol with KD 24 pM and stimulates adenylyl cyclase with
  potency isoproterenol > norepinephrine = epinephrine (beta1 profile). [PMID:1695899 "adenylylcyclase
  was stimulated by beta-adrenergic receptor agonists with the rank order of potency of isoproterenol
  greater than norepinephrine = epinephrine, consistent with properties expected of the beta 1-AR
  subtype"]
- Native rat beta1-AR is coupled positively to adenylyl cyclase in anterior pituitary and shows
  constitutive activity (inverse agonism of CGP 20712A). [PMID:18202135 "The present data suggest that
  beta(1)-AR in the anterior pituitary is positively coupled to adenylyl cyclase but is constitutively
  active in a pertussis toxin-sensitive manner."]
- Mouse knockout: loss of beta1-AR abolishes isoproterenol-stimulated chronotropy and inotropy and
  sharply reduces cardiac adenylyl cyclase stimulation. [PMID:8693001 "this lack of responsiveness is
  accompanied by markedly reduced stimulation of adenylate cyclase in cardiac membranes from beta 1-AR
  -/- mice"]
- Partial agonist clenbuterol binds rat beta1 with KD 38 nM. [PMID:6289140 "Equilibrium dissociation
  constants were 38 and 6.3 nM for beta 1 and beta 2 adrenoceptors, respectively."]

## PDZ-ligand interactions, Ras activation and trafficking

- The C-terminal PDZ ligand binds the PDZ domain of the cAMP-dependent Ras GEF CNrasGEF (RAPGEF2),
  coupling Gs/cAMP to Ras activation; beta2-AR does not. [PMID:12391161 "The beta1 adrenergic receptor
  (beta1-AR) binds to the PDZ domain of the cyclic AMP (cAMP)-dependent Ras exchange factor, CNrasGEF,
  via its C-terminal SkV motif."]
- Receptor colocalizes with CNrasGEF at the plasma membrane, including in primary cortical neurons.
  [PMID:12391161 "a substantial fraction of CNrasGEF colocalizes with the β1-AR at the plasma membrane
  in HEK-293T cells"]
- PDZ proteomic array: binds PSD-95, MAGI-2, GIPC, CAL (GOPC), SAP97 and MAGI-3; the rat V466A (paper:
  V477A, human numbering) mutation abolishes MAGI-3 binding; MAGI-3 restrains ERK1/2 activation but
  not cAMP. [PMID:16316992 "The beta1AR carboxyl terminus was found to bind specifically to the first
  PDZ domain of MAGI-3, with the last four amino acids (E-S-K-V) of beta1AR being the key determinants
  of the interaction."]
- MAGI-2 binding (human beta1) enhances agonist-induced internalization. [PMID:11526121 "The beta1AR
  carboxyl terminus binds with high affinity to the first PDZ domain of MAGI-2"]
- Heterodimerizes with alpha2A-AR (human). [PMID:12529373 "We have found that the beta(1)-adrenergic
  receptor (beta(1)AR) and alpha(2A)-adrenergic receptor (alpha(2A)AR) heterodimerize when coexpressed
  in cells."]
- Recycles to the plasma membrane after endocytosis. [PMID:21540189 "Like β1AR, which recycles to the
  plasma membrane, GPER co-traffics with transferrin+, Rab11+ recycling endosomes."]

## Cardiac physiology

- In mouse, cardiac inotropy and chronotropy driven by beta-agonists are mediated almost exclusively
  by beta1. [PMID:10358009 "beta-AR stimulation of cardiac inotropy and chronotropy is mediated almost
  exclusively by the beta1-AR, whereas vascular relaxation and metabolic rate are controlled by all
  three beta-ARs (beta1-, beta2-, and beta3-AR)"]
- Rat atria/ventricle: noradrenaline (ICI118551 present) acts through beta1 on inotropy and I(Ca-L),
  blunted by PDE4. [PMID:19133992 "PDE4 blunts the beta(1)-adrenoceptor-mediated inotropic effects."]
- Rat chromaffin cells: beta1 potentiates L-type Ca2+ current via PKA. [PMID:12514203 "suggesting that
  potentiation is associated with a PKA-mediated phosphorylation of L-channels (approximately 40%
  L-current increase) through beta(1)-ARs"]
- Rat heart rate depends on beta1 tone (atenolol bradycardia). [PMID:9089896 "beta 1-adrenergic
  receptor blockade with atenolol (Aten) lowered pre-exercise fH and (fHmax) to comparable values in A
  and in NA rats"]; [PMID:18931026 "Five days of atenolol treatment decreased MAP similarly in SGX and
  Sham rats."]
- Cardiomyocyte hypertrophy: beta1 more effective than beta2; requires endocytosis/Src/Akt.
  [PMID:18194989 "Cardiac hypertrophy by activation of the beta-adrenergic receptor (beta AR) is
  mediated more efficiently by the beta1-AR than by the beta2-AR."]
- Cardiomyocyte apoptosis: beta1 (not beta2) stimulation induces apoptosis (mouse myocytes); beta1
  siRNA reduces apoptosis after MI in rat. [PMID:11171998 "Stimulation of beta(1)-AR, but not
  beta(2)-AR, markedly induced myocyte apoptosis"]; [PMID:17143192 "The protective mechanism involved
  reduction of cardiomyocyte apoptosis in the beta1 siRNA-treated hearts."]
- Greater glycogenolysis during beta1 than beta2 stimulation in perfused rat heart. [PMID:17911346
  "The greater glycogenolysis was observed during beta(1)- than beta(2)-AR stimulation"]

## Thermogenesis and metabolism

- beta1 KO mice are cold-intolerant and fail to mount diet-induced thermogenesis. [PMID:22728333 "the
  β(1) signaling pathway mediates most of the SNS stimulation of adaptive thermogenesis"]
- Triple beta1/2/3 knockouts are obese and cold sensitive (cannot apportion to beta1 alone).
  [PMID:12387862 "Hence, beta-adrenergic signalling is essential for the resistance to obesity and
  cold"]; [PMID:12161655 "These findings establish that betaARs are necessary for diet-induced
  thermogenesis"]
- Hepatocyte overexpression of beta1 or beta2 raises lipid content (rat liver aging). [PMID:22457517
  "in vitro overexpression of β1- and β2-AR subtypes in hepatocytes from young rodents increased
  cellular lipid content"]

## Nervous system

- Sleep/wake: human short-sleep ADRB1 variant; knock-in mice sleep less; ADRB1+ dorsal pons neurons
  promote wakefulness. [PMID:31473062 "In vivo, the mice carrying the same mutation demonstrated short
  sleep behavior."]
- Memory retrieval: hippocampal beta1 required for intermediate-term contextual memory retrieval
  (mouse); BLA beta1 knockdown impairs auditory fear memory retrieval (rat). [PMID:15066288 "The role
  of norepinephrine in retrieval requires signaling through the beta(1)-adrenergic receptor in the
  hippocampus."]; [PMID:17961922 "memory retrieval was impaired as beta(1)-AR protein expression was
  prevented"]
- Synaptic plasticity: beta1 agonist dobutamine rescues prefrontal LTP in malnourished rats; beta1
  paired with 5 Hz stimulation produces ERK-dependent CA1 LTP. [PMID:21848869]; [PMID:10595521]
- Presynaptic beta1 on GABAergic terminals in periaqueductal gray: beta1 antagonists increase sIPSC
  frequency. [PMID:18262504 "Metoprolol, a specific beta1-adrenoceptor antagonist, also reversibly
  enhanced sIPSC frequency."]
- Found in substance-P-negative large dense-core vesicles in DRG neurons. [PMID:21321602]

## Papers with weak or indirect support for beta1

- PMID:7912752 (PVN-applied agonists and urine output): antidiuresis was mainly beta2-mediated.
  ["It was suggested that there were predominantly beta 2-adrenoceptors mediating antidiuresis in SHR."]
- PMID:11408541: zinterol (beta2) effects on I(Ca,L) were not modified by a beta1 antagonist.
- PMID:18306009: 700 microM landiolol (far above beta1 affinity) contracts trachea via Rho-kinase;
  no evidence the effect is beta1-mediated.
- PMID:21177871 (AKAP-PKA disruptor FMP-API-1): uses isoproterenol (non-selective) in rat neonatal
  cardiac myocytes and Langendorff hearts; describes the beta-AR-Gs-adenylyl cyclase-PKA cascade but
  does not discriminate beta1 from beta2. ["Stimulation of EPs with PGE1 or of β-adrenoceptors (β-AR)
  with adrenergic agonists, such as norepinephrin (NE) or isoproterenol, activates the G protein Gs,
  which in turn stimulates adenylyl cyclase (AC)."]
