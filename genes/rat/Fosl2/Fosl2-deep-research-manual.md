# Fosl2 (rat, P51145) - manual literature research

No deep-research provider was available; this summary was compiled by hand from cached
publications (`publications/PMID_*.md`), PubMed searches, and the UniProt record.

## Identity and structure
- Fos-related antigen 2 (Fra-2), 326 aa, Fos subfamily of bZIP transcription factors; bZIP domain 124-187
  [file:rat/Fosl2/Fosl2-uniprot.txt "Belongs to the bZIP family. Fos subfamily."].
- Fos proteins act as heterodimers on AP-1 sites [PMID:11340164 "All members of this family act by binding to AP-1 sites as heterodimeric complexes with other proteins"].
- Fra-2 lacks the strong C-terminal transactivation domain of c-Fos, so it can repress or activate
  [PMID:21367864 "FRA-2 can act to suppress transcription, for example at promoters induced by c-JUN homodimers"; "However, in heterodimeric combination with JUND, FRA-2 can enhance transcription relative to JUND homodimers"].

## Rat pineal gland (circadian/adrenergic system)
- Cloned from rat pineal; mRNA and protein rise at night via adrenergic->cAMP signalling
  [PMID:7592994 "Both Fra-2 mRNA and protein are absent during the day and increase robustly at night on a circadian basis"].
- Fra-2 is the rhythmic component of pineal AP-1 DNA binding [PMID:12358739 "displays a circadian rhythm in its DNA binding in the pineal gland, which results from the rhythmic expression of Fra-2"; PMID:10987819].
- Dominant-negative Fra-2 transgenic rat (NATDNF2): Fra-2 autoactivates its own promoter; Dio2 is negatively and Cd24 positively linked to Fra-2; Fra-2 is not needed for melatonin synthesis
  [PMID:11340164 "suggests that DII is subject to negative regulation by a Fra-2-containing AP-1 binding complex"; "consistent with the interpretation that Fra-2 does not play an essential role in the pineal gland in melatonin production"].
- Rgs4 is a directly repressed target: ChIP/EMSA on a variant AP-1 (AP-1R) element, mutation abolishes repression, and FRA-2 loading displaces CBP
  [PMID:21367864 "FRA-2 protein preferentially associated with a proximal Rgs4 AP-1R sequence as demonstrated by ex vivo ChIP and in vitro EMSA analysis"; "transcriptional repression was blocked by mutation of the AP-1R sequence"].

## Rat osteoblasts
- PGE2/PKA induces Fra-2, which activates C/EBPbeta [PMID:15299028 "over-expression of Fra-2 significantly increased C/EBPbeta promoter activity in PGE2-induced osteoblasts"].
- Fra2 is among AP-1 proteins on the collagenase-3 promoter AP-1 site after bFGF [PMID:10830307].

## Other rat expression studies (IEP-level only)
Hypoxia in PC12 cells (PMID:15892448, DN screen), restraint stress (PMID:17936518), conditioned taste aversion
(PMID:18374904), pregnancy/labor and progesterone (PMID:12080023), granulosa cells LH/FSH (PMID:10934195), forskolin
and cholera toxin in astrocytes (PMID:11113530, PMID:11295234), IL-1/cAMP in thyroid cells (PMID:18280640), light in SCN
(PMID:11146118), photoperiod (PMID:16962714), estradiol in prostate (PMID:12021174), mechanical loading in condylar
cartilage (PMID:16373449). These show induction of an immediate-early gene, not function.

## Mouse ortholog (source of RGD ISO projections)
- Chondrocyte hypertrophy [PMID:15509771 "Primary Fosl2-/- chondrocytes exhibit decreased hypertrophic differentiation"].
- Osteoclast survival/size via LIF [PMID:18548006 "we show that LIF is a direct transcriptional target of Fra-2 and c-Jun"].
- Osteoblast differentiation, osteocalcin/Col1a2 targets [PMID:20837772].
- Osteoblast Fra-2 controls Adipoq/osteocalcin and systemic glucose [PMID:24046454].
- Adipocyte turnover, PPARgamma2 repression [PMID:24464219]; leptin activation [PMID:22326952].
- B cell development via Foxo1/Irf4 [PMID:28566276]; Treg repression [PMID:32610127]; Th17 network [PMID:23021777].
- Ectopic expression causes lung fibrosis [PMID:18641127]; osteoblast Fra-2 drives systemic inflammation via OPN [PMID:30181393].
