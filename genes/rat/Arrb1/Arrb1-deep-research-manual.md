# Arrb1 (rat beta-arrestin-1, arrestin-2; UniProt P29066): manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this review. This file is a manual synthesis
from the cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and the UniProt record
(genes/rat/Arrb1/Arrb1-uniprot.txt). Every claim carries inline provenance.

## Identity and family

- Rat Arrb1 encodes beta-arrestin-1 (also called arrestin-2), a 418-aa member of the arrestin
  family with arrestin N- and C-domains (Pfam PF00339, PF02752; PANTHER PTHR11792:SF22
  BETA-ARRESTIN-1) [file:rat/Arrb1/Arrb1-uniprot.txt "Belongs to the arrestin family."].
- The rat cDNA was cloned together with beta-arrestin-2 from a rat brain library
  [PMID:1517224 "One of the cDNAs is the rat homolog of bovine beta-arrestin (beta-arrestin1)."].
- Expression: neuronal tissues and spleen, low elsewhere
  [PMID:1517224 "beta-Arrestin1 and beta-arrestin2 are predominantly localized in neuronal tissues and in the spleen."].

## Core activity 1: binding phosphorylated, activated GPCRs and desensitization

- Purified beta-arrestin-1 quenches coupling of GRK-phosphorylated beta2-adrenergic receptor
  to Gs in reconstituted systems [PMID:1517224 "beta-Arrestin2 was equipotent to beta-arrestin1 and specifically inhibited beta 2AR function."].
- A phosphorylated V2 vasopressin receptor C-tail peptide (V2Rpp) binds and conformationally
  activates rat beta-arrestin-1, releasing its C-terminus and enhancing clathrin binding
  [PMID:17513300 "V(2)Rpp binds specifically to beta-arrestin1 causing significant conformational changes"].
- Crystal structure of active beta-arrestin-1 with V2Rpp [PMID:23604254 "Here we report the crystal structure of β-arrestin-1 (also called arrestin-2) in complex with a fully phosphorylated 29-amino-acid carboxy-terminal peptide derived from the human V2 vasopressin receptor (V2Rpp)."]; the protein used was rat beta-arrestin-1 [PMID:23604254 "GST-tagged rat β-arrestin1 in the pGEX4T vector was transformed into BL21(DE3) cells"].
- Cryo-EM structures with C5aR1, CXCR4, M2R and D6R phosphopeptides/receptors define a
  P-X-P-P phospho-motif recognised by a K-K-R-R-K-K N-domain groove
  [PMID:37209686 "These structures help identify a P-X-P-P type phosphorylation motif in GPCRs that interacts with a spatially organized K-K-R-R-K-K sequence in the N-domain of βarrs."];
  rat beta-arrestin-1 was used [PMID:37209686 "Full length rat βarr1, βarr2WT and bovine βarr2DM were cloned into pGEX-4T3 vector"].
  Atypical engagement through M2R ICL3 [PMID:38175886 "Our structures of M2R-βarr1 and M2Rpp-βarr2 underscore that the key interaction interface and the activation mechanism remains conserved despite distinct domains on the receptor being used to engage βarrs."].
- Receptor-specific binding in rat systems: FSH receptor in Sertoli cells
  [PMID:16899567 "a significant FSH-binding activity was co-immunoprecipitated with the endogenous beta-arrestins from agonist-stimulated but not from untreated Sertoli cell extracts."];
  alpha1A/alpha1B adrenergic receptors [PMID:18523139 "the agonistinduced interaction of the alpha1a-AR with beta-arrestins was much weaker than that of the alpha1b-AR."].
- Physiological desensitization: in rat mesenteric arterial smooth muscle, arrestin2
  (beta-arrestin-1) is needed for P2Y2 receptor desensitization
  [PMID:20705669 "siRNA-mediated knockdown of arrestin2 attenuated UTP-stimulated P2Y-receptor desensitization, whereas arrestin3 depletion did not."].
- PDE4 recruitment limits cAMP at activated beta2AR
  [PMID:12399592 "We show that beta-arrestins coordinate both processes by recruiting PDEs to activated beta2-adrenergic receptors in the plasma membrane of mammalian cells."].

## Core activity 2: clathrin/AP-2 cargo adaptor for receptor endocytosis

- UniProt summary [file:rat/Arrb1/Arrb1-uniprot.txt "The beta-arrestins target many receptors for internalization by acting as endocytic adapters (CLASPs, clathrin-associated sorting proteins)"].
- beta-arrestin is a beta2-appendage-selective AP-2 adaptor
  [PMID:15728179 "the only other beta2 appendage selective adaptor, beta-arrestin"].
- Clathrin binding increases on activation [PMID:17513300 "we also show a functional consequence of the release of the C terminus of beta-arrestin1 by enhanced clathrin binding."].
- Endosomal receptor/beta-arrestin-1 complexes (bradykinin B2R)
  [PMID:25016018 "Here we show, in live cells, differential dynamic regulation of endosomal bradykinin B2 receptor (B2R) complexes with either β-arrestin-1 or -2."].
- PAR2 endocytosis and signalosome [PMID:10725339 "The complex, which contains internalized receptor, beta-arrestin, raf-1, and activated ERK, is required for ERK1/2 activation."].

## Core activity 3: signaling scaffold (ERK and others)

- mGlu1 receptor-driven MAPK activation in Purkinje cells requires beta-arrestin
  [PMID:12519791 "beta-arrestin is directly involved in glutamate-stimulated MAP kinase activation by acting as a signaling molecule."].
- Adrenal AT1R-driven sustained ERK and aldosterone production (rat in vivo)
  [PMID:19289825 "This pathway results in sustained ERK activation and subsequent up-regulation of steroidogenic acute regulatory protein"].
- Reciprocal regulation: in rat VSMCs, beta-arrestin-1 knockdown increases early AngII-ERK
  [PMID:19254952 "In β-arrestin1 siRNA-transfected cells, ANG stimulation evokes a kinetic pattern of ERK activation similar to control but with significantly elevated levels of activation at the 2-min time point"];
  and opposes beta-arrestin-2 anti-apoptotic signaling [PMID:19171933 "We have also found that β-arrestin-1 functionally opposes this signaling (33)."; "knocking-down β-arrestin-1 leads to decreases in apoptotic reagent-induced caspase-3 cleavage even in the absence of stimulation with AngII"].
- Cofilin pathway scaffolding downstream of PAR2 [PMID:17500066 "Beta-arrestins can interact with cofilin, LIMK, and chronophin and colocalize with them in membrane protrusions"].
- Estrogen receptor-alpha/beta-arrestin-1 complex in neurons
  [PMID:19339617 "E2 stimulation induces the formation of a ERαβ-arrestin-1 receptor complex"].
- Insulin downregulates beta-arrestin-1 and impairs IGF-1/GPCR MAPK signaling
  [PMID:12167719 "Ectopic expression of wild-type beta-arrestin-1 in insulin-treated cells in which endogenous beta-arrestin-1 had been downregulated rescued IGF-I- and LPA-stimulated MAP kinase phosphorylation."].

## Core activity 4: E3 ubiquitin ligase adaptor

- TRPV4 down-regulation in rat VSMCs [PMID:20650893 "β-Arrestin 1 interacts with, and acts as an adaptor for AIP4, an E3 ubiquitin ligase responsible for TRPV4 ubiquitination."].
- Notch degradation via ARRDC1-ITCH [PMID:23886940 "we show that β-arrestins are necessary for Itch-Notch interaction and for Itch-driven ubiquitylation and degradation of Notch."].
- MDM2-p53 scaffold [PMID:21857681 "β-arrestin-1 (ARRB1), activated via β(2)-adrenoreceptors, facilitates AKT-mediated activation of MDM2 and also promotes MDM2 binding to, and degradation of, p53, by acting as a molecular scaffold."].

## Nuclear / transcriptional roles (non-core)

- Delta-opioid-induced nuclear translocation; p300 recruitment at p27/c-fos promoters
  [PMID:16325578 "betaarr1 translocates to the nucleus and is selectively enriched at specific promoters such as that of p27 and c-fos, where it facilitates the recruitment of histone acetyltransferase p300"].
- Dishevelled/LEF [PMID:11742073 "These results identify betaarr1 as a regulator of Dvl-dependent LEF transcription"].
- CREB complex for GDNF (C6 glioma) [PMID:21223624 "treatment with ADs significantly increased β-arrestin1/CREB interaction."].

## Localization

- Postsynaptic sites in rat brain [PMID:1517224 "immunoelectron microscopy shows that the beta-arrestins are appropriately situated at postsynaptic sites to act in concert with beta ARK to regulate G protein-coupled neurotransmitter receptors."].
- Recruited from cytoplasm to plasma membrane [PMID:16899567 "FSH induces the recruitment of the endogenous GRK 2 and beta-arrestin 1/2 from the cytoplasm to the plasma membrane of rat primary Sertoli cells."].
- Peripheral sensory terminals [PMID:14519533 "In the glabrous skin, strong betaArr1 immunoreactivity was detected in nerve fibers in the dermal nerve plexus and dermal papillae."].

## Expression responses and phenotypes (peripheral)

- Morphine lowers Arrb1 mRNA in locus coeruleus [PMID:12614678 "In locus coeruleus, both acute and chronic morphine treatments resulted in significant decreases (over 50%) in beta-arrestin 1 mRNA level"].
- Astrocyte survival [PMID:24378649 "This indicates that β-arrestin 1 is important to protect astrocytes from apoptosis."].
- Thioredoxin-1 interaction in PC12 cells [PMID:25486571 "the downregulation of β-arrestin-1 suppressed the cell viability"].
- Sevoflurane neurotoxicity [PMID:26621121 "this neuroprotective role of LAP4 could be partially reduced by β-arr1 small interfering RNA (siRNA) or β-arr2 siRNA transfection."].

## Problems found in source annotations

- The mouse phototransduction annotation (PMID:11853756, propagated to rat by ISO) comes from a
  paper on rod arrestin (Sag) knockout mice, not beta-arrestin-1
  [PMID:11853756 "mice with rod arrestin deleted (arrestin -/-)"].
- The mouse "cysteine-type endopeptidase inhibitor activity" (PMID:19915011, propagated by ISO)
  is a phenotype-to-function mapping; the paper describes an ERK1/2-p90RSK-Bad mechanism
  [PMID:19915011 "beta-Arrestin 1 is further found to mediate the antiapoptotic effect of GLP-1 in beta-cells through the ERK1/2-p90RSK-phosphorylation of Bad."].
