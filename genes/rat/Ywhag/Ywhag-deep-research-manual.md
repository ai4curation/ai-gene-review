# Ywhag (rat 14-3-3 protein gamma, UniProt P61983) - manual literature summary

No deep-research provider was available for this session; this summary was compiled by hand from
cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md), PubMed searches and the UniProt record.

## Identity and family
- 247-aa member of the 14-3-3 family; rat RGD:62002, GeneID 56010. Belongs to the 14-3-3 family
  [UniProt P61983 "Belongs to the 14-3-3 family."].
- Forms homodimers and heterodimers with other 14-3-3 isoforms [UniProt "Homodimer (By similarity)"].

## Molecular function: phosphoserine/phosphothreonine motif reader
- UniProt: "Binds to a large number of partners, usually by recognition of a phosphoserine or
  phosphothreonine motif. Binding generally results in the modulation of the activity of the binding
  partner."
- LRRK2: 14-3-3 gamma, theta and zeta bound LRRK2 phosphopeptides, with highest affinity for
  PKA-phosphorylated pS1444 [PMID:24351927 "Experiments of direct binding to the three 14-3-3 isotypes
  gamma, theta, and zeta with phosphopeptides encompassing pS910, pS935, or pS1444 demonstrated the
  highest affinities to phospho-S1444"]. Binding decreased LRRK2 kinase activity in vitro.
- CaV2.2 alpha1B C-tail contains "a phosphoserine-containing motif that directly binds to 14-3-3"
  [PMID:16982421]; isoform used not stated in abstract.
- IGF-1R C-terminal serine autophosphorylation creates 14-3-3 sites [PMID:12482592].
- PKC: 14-3-3gamma interacts with and is phosphorylated by PKC isoforms in human VSMC [PMID:10433554
  "We also demonstrated interaction between 14-3-3gamma and several PKC isoforms"]. Originally described
  as "a putative regulatory protein for protein kinase C" [PMID:8381897].
- MuSK: 14-3-3 gamma co-purifies with MuSK and represses synaptic gene transcription in myotubes
  [PMID:15604144].

## Rat-specific experimental work
- Rat brain fractionation: gamma is present in synaptic membranes and isolated synaptic junctions
  [PMID:7964746 "the eta, epsilon, and gamma isoforms but not the beta and zeta isoforms are associated
  with isolated synaptic junctions"].
- Neuron-enriched expression, axonal transport to nerve terminals [PMID:8381897; UniProt tissue
  specificity].
- Rat adipocytes: 14-3-3gamma is the 14-3-3 isoform that binds AS160 (TBC1D4) insulin-dependently and is
  enriched (3-fold) on immunoisolated GLUT4 vesicles after insulin; AS160 PTB-domain constructs reduce
  14-3-3gamma binding to AS160 and inhibit GLUT4 vesicle fusion [PMID:21454690 "14-3-3γ was enriched on
  insulin-stimulated vesicles"; "Both 14-3-3β and 14-3-3γ precipitated AS160, but the γ isoform was much
  more effective and precipitated higher amounts"]. Full text read.

## Sequestration / localization control (mostly human/mouse)
- WDR24 (GATOR2): AMPK phosphorylation of WDR24 S155 on glucose starvation creates a 14-3-3gamma site;
  YWHAG depletion makes mTORC1 resistant to glucose starvation [PMID:36732624 "we found that WDR24
  specifically bound 14-3-3γ (Fig. 5f)"].
- PKP1: 14-3-3gamma binds pS155-PKP1 in the cytoplasm and destabilises keratinocyte cohesion
  [PMID:29678907 "destabilized intercellular cohesion of keratinocytes by reducing its incorporation into
  desmosomes"] - i.e. negative, not positive, regulation of cell-cell adhesion.
- MondoA-Mlx: 14-3-3 binding to MondoA MCR III retains the complex in cytoplasm [PMID:12446771].
- Mitochondria: during Mieap-induced MALM, 14-3-3gamma enters mitochondria and is needed for elimination
  of oxidized mitochondrial proteins [PMID:22532927 "This result indicated that the 14-3-3γ protein is
  present within mitochondria."].
- Subcellular survey in COS-7: "14-3-3γ is only localized to the nucleus" [PMID:31906564], contrasting
  with the predominantly cytosolic localisation reported elsewhere.

## Weak or indirect evidence
- PMID:11824616 (Down syndrome fetal brain proteomics) only reports reduced 14-3-3 gamma levels and
  speculates on neuronal differentiation and synaptic plasticity; it is the source of human IMP rows to
  regulation of neuron differentiation / synaptic plasticity.
- PMID:38377992 (UFL1/PD-1 in T cells) is source of human IDA rows to T-cell processes; abstract does not
  mention 14-3-3.

## Disease (human ortholog)
- Human YWHAG de novo variants cause developmental and epileptic encephalopathy (not used for GO).

## Actin binding
- No study showing direct actin binding by 14-3-3 gamma was identified in the cached literature or a brief PubMed search (the query is confounded by gamma-actin); the rat ISS row to actin binding derives from the 14-3-3 eta paralog (P68510).
