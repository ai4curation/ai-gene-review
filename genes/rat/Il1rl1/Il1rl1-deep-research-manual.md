# Il1rl1 (rat, Q62611) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available; this synthesis was written by the reviewer from the
cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record `Il1rl1-uniprot.txt`.

## Identity and gene structure
- Rat Il1rl1 (Fit-1, T1/ST2), RGD:2894, GeneID 25556. Two products from alternative promoter
  usage and 3' processing: membrane-bound Fit-1M (isoform A, ST2L) and secreted Fit-1S
  (isoform B, sST2) [PMID:8131748 "Two different mRNA isoforms are expressed from the Fit-1
  gene"; "The Fit-1S mRNA of fibroblasts directs, instead, the synthesis of a secreted protein
  consisting of only the extracellular domain"].
- Fit-1 was first found as a Fos-responsive delayed-early gene of rat fibroblasts
  [PMID:8131748 "Fit-1 is directly regulated by the estrogen-inducible transcription factor
  Fos-ER and that it belongs to the family of delayed early genes"].
- Domain architecture (UniProt): signal peptide 1-26, three Ig-like C2 domains, single TM helix
  332-354, cytoplasmic TIR domain 379-539.

## Molecular function
- Ligand-binding receptor chain for IL-33; with coreceptor IL1RAP forms the signalling complex
  that recruits MYD88/IRAK/TRAF6 (UniProt, by similarity to human Q01638 / mouse P14719).
- Soluble ST2 acts as a decoy receptor for IL-33 [PMID:17492053 "sST2 blocked antihypertrophic
  effects of IL-33, indicating that sST2 functions in myocardium as a soluble decoy receptor"];
  in rat VSMCs [PMID:24265755 "corroborating the results found in the aortas of animals fed a
  HFD in which sST2 acts as a decoy receptor for IL-33"].
- The UniProt EC 3.2.2.6 / TIR NADase statement derives only from the PROSITE-ProRule PRU00204
  TIR rule (ECO:0000255). NADase activity is established for SARM1 and plant/bacterial TIRs; no
  study reports NAD+ hydrolysis by an IL-1 receptor-family TIR domain. Treated as unsupported.

## Rat-specific experimental findings
- Cardiac IL-33/ST2 system is mechanically induced and cardioprotective [PMID:17492053 "Thus,
  IL-33/ST2 signaling is a mechanically activated, cardioprotective fibroblast-cardiomyocyte
  paracrine system"]. Abstract only.
- ET-1 induces Il1rl1 mRNA in neonatal rat cardiac myocytes as a second-phase gene
  [PMID:18406357 "for interleukin 1 receptor-like 1 (Il1rl1), a second phase gene (upregulation
  was inhibited by cycloheximide; Fig. 2B)"].
- High-fat diet raises aortic sST2 and lowers ST2L [PMID:24265755 "HFD group showed an increase
  in aortic soluble sST2 and a decrease in the transmembrane isoform (ST2L) levels"].
- ST2 is secreted by rat cardiosphere-derived cells and inhibits their proliferation
  [PMID:20014349 "ST2 acted as an autocrine factor since it was secreted into media by CDCs and
  had an inhibitory effect on rat CDCs proliferation"].
