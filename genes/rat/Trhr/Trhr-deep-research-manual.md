# Trhr (rat, UniProt Q01717) - manual literature summary

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled by hand from cached
publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and the UniProt record.

## Identity
- Rat thyrotropin-releasing hormone receptor 1 (TRH-R1), 412 aa, rhodopsin-like
  (class A) GPCR with seven predicted transmembrane helices (UniProt Q01717; RGD:3904;
  GeneID 25570). Rats also have a second TRH receptor, TRH-R2 (Trhr2), which is
  absent from humans.
- Cloned from GH4C1/GH3 pituitary cells; expression of its mRNA in Xenopus oocytes
  gave a TRH-evoked conductance [PMID:1317787 "mRNA synthesized in vitro from this
  cDNA was injected into Xenopus oocytes, and a characteristic conductance response to
  TRH was detected by voltage clamp recording"].
- Independent cloning showed a biphasic Ca2+-dependent chloride current, the read-out of
  Gq/PLC/IP3 Ca2+ release in oocytes [PMID:1377915 "application of the peptide induced a
  biphasic Ca(2+)-dependent chloride current"].
- In COS-7 cells TRH raised inositol phosphate production [PMID:8387312 "TRH (100 nM)
  produced an approximately twofold increase in total IP production"].

## Isoforms
- TRH-R(412) and TRH-R(387) arise by retention/splicing of a 52-bp intron in the
  C-terminal tail; both are fully functional [PMID:1334485 "both cDNAs encode fully
  functional TRH receptors"]. The short form is expressed in brain and pituitary
  [PMID:7509436].

## Signalling
- TRH receptors couple to Gq/11 and stimulate PLC, Ca2+ mobilisation and PKC
  [PMID:7527898 "TRH and GnRH receptors are each coupled to G proteins of the Gq/11
  family"]. In GH pituitary cells, immunoblocking showed Gq/11 confer TRH-responsive
  PLC activity [PMID:8861541 "G(q) and/or G(11) confer throliberin (TRH) responsive
  phospholipase C (PL-C) activity in these cells"].

## Dimerization and phosphorylation
- Epitope-tagged rat TRH receptors in HEK293 and GHFT cells form homodimers; TRH
  increases dimer abundance and phosphorylation independent of PLC signalling
  [PMID:12023974 "10-30% of receptors were isolated as hemagglutinin/FLAG-receptor
  dimers under basal conditions"]. (Abstract only.)

## Brain expression (PMID:17760865, full text)
- TRH-R1 is mainly hypothalamic and brain stem, TRH-R2 is widespread. In the TMN,
  histaminergic neurons expressed TRH-R2 but not TRH-R1 [PMID:17760865 "we identified
  TRH-R2, but not TRH-R1, within the cell bodies of histaminergic neurons within the
  TMN"]. So the TRH-induced, histamine-mediated anorexia studied there is not attributable
  to TRH-R1 at the TMN; this paper gives no basis for a feeding-behaviour annotation to Trhr.
