# Trhr (rat) curation notes

- 2026-10-28: Review created. Fetched Q01717 (TRHR_RAT, RGD:3904, GeneID 25570); GOA has
  17 rows. Only one GOA PMID (PMID:12023974, abstract only). Added cloning/expression and
  Gq-coupling papers (PMID:1317787, 1377915, 1334485, 7509436, 8387312, 8861541, 7527898)
  and the task-requested PMID:17760865 (full text).
- Core: TRH receptor activity (GO:0004997) at plasma membrane, signalling via Gq/11 to PLC
  (GO:0007200) [PMID:8861541 "G(q) and/or G(11) confer throliberin (TRH) responsive
  phospholipase C (PL-C) activity in these cells"].
- PMID:17760865 is a negative expression result for TRH-R1 in histaminergic TMN neurons
  [PMID:17760865 "we identified TRH-R2, but not TRH-R1, within the cell bodies of
  histaminergic neurons within the TMN"]; no NEW annotation proposed from it.
- GO:0031334 positive regulation of protein-containing complex assembly (IDA, PMID:12023974):
  the observation is that the ligand TRH increases receptor homodimer abundance; that is
  agonist-modulated self-association, not the receptor regulating assembly of other
  complexes. Marked over-annotated; homodimerization captured as GO:0042803.
- Physiological outputs (TSH/prolactin secretion) not proposed as NEW: no rat Trhr-specific
  experimental evidence was read here.
