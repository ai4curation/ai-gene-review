CC   -!- FUNCTION: Precursor of a serum protease that activates the lectin
CC       pathway of the complement system, a cascade of proteins that leads to
CC       phagocytosis and breakdown of pathogens and signaling that strengthens
CC       the adaptive immune system. The lectin complement system is activated
CC       following association of lectins, such as MBL2, FCN1, FCN2 or FCN3, to
CC       carbohydrates on the pathogen surface. MASP2 is cleaved and activated
CC       by MASP1 in response to lectin-binding to pathogen carbohydrates. Can
CC       activate prothrombin to thrombin. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O00187}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Mannan-binding lectin serine protease 2 B chain]: Serine
CC       protease component of the lectin complement pathway, which catalyzes
CC       cleavage and activation of C2 and C4, the next components of the
CC       complement pathway. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O00187}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY: [Mannan-binding lectin serine protease 2 B chain]:

CC         Evidence={ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O00187};
