# Curation Notes: Rat `Arhgef2` (`UniProtKB:Q5FVC2`, `RGD:1304659`)

- **Gene / Protein**: Rat `Arhgef2` (*GEF-H1* / *Lfc*; `UniProtKB:Q5FVC2`, `sv=1`, 985 aa; `PANTHER:PTHR13944:SF20`).
- **Conserved Residue Coordinates (`Q5FVC2`, `sv=1`)**: `Cys53` (`ZN_FING` `39-86`), `Glu243` (DH CR1), `Tyr395` (DH CR3), and `Ser885` (`882-RRRSLP-887` 14-3-3 phosphoswitch).
- **Direct Rat Experimental Literature & Cross-Species RGD ISO Projections**:
  - Kang et al. (`PMID:19208802`) demonstrated in rat brain and hippocampal neurons that endogenous rat GEF-H1/Lfc associates with AMPA receptor subunit GluR1 (`Gria1`, `P19490`) in the postsynaptic density of glutamatergic synapses and negatively regulates spine density and length via a RhoA signaling cascade (grounding `NEW` `GO:0035025` `IMP`).
  - RGD `ISO` projections on `rat/Arhgef2` include canine `ARHGEF2` (`RGD:12388091` = `UniProtKB:Q865S3`, `PMID:22711822`) and porcine `ARHGEF2` (`RGD:13864640` = `B2DCZ9`, `PMID:23389627`). Notably, `GO:0035022` (`positive regulation of Rac protein signal transduction`, `ISO` from `RGD:13864640`) is marked `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED` (`SOURCE_BAD` / `ROLE_CONFLATION`) because purified Lfc/GEF-H1 lacks Rac1 nucleotide exchange activity (`PMID:8910315`, `PMID:9890991`).
