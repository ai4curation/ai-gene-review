# Bhmt (rat, UniProt O09171) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available; this synthesis was written by the reviewer from cached
publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record `Bhmt-uniprot.txt`.

## Identity and activity

- Rat Bhmt encodes betaine--homocysteine S-methyltransferase 1 (EC 2.1.1.5), a 407-aa zinc
  methyltransferase of the Hcy-binding (homocysteine S-methyltransferase) domain family.
  UniProt: "Converts betaine and homocysteine to dimethylglycine and methionine, respectively. This
  reaction is also required for the irreversible oxidation of choline."
- Kinetics of the recombinant rat enzyme: KM 106.4 uM for L-homocysteine and 333.3 uM for glycine
  betaine [UniProt O09171, citing PMID:12487625].
- Site-directed mutagenesis of rat liver BHMT: "All the mutants obtained showed a reduction of the enzyme
  activity, the most dramatic effect being that of Glu(159), followed by Tyr(77) and Asp(26)."
  [PMID:12487625]
- Structure of rat liver BHMT: "It presents a Zn(2+) atom linked to three essential Cys residues." and
  "The asymmetric unit contains the whole functional tetramer showing point symmetry 222."
  [PMID:15099744]; "Tyr160 is the fourth ligand coordinated to Zn, which is replaced by Hcy upon binding."
- Human BHMT does not use S-adenosylmethionine (SAM) as donor; the paralog BHMT-2 uses S-methylmethionine.
  "S-adenosylmethionine does not inhibit BHMT but is a weak inhibitor of BHMT-2" [PMID:18230605].

## Localization

- Mainly cytosolic, with a small nuclear pool: "Nuclear BHMT is an active homotetramer in normal liver,
  although the total enzyme activity in this fraction is markedly lower than in the cytosol."
  Oxidative stress "induces BHMT nuclear translocation" [PMID:28288879].
- Liver cytosol fractions contain abundant BHMT-1 identified by MALDI-TOF [PMID:19239903].
- Detected in serum of alcohol-fed rats (proteomics) [PMID:23083309], consistent with release from
  injured liver rather than secretion.

## Physiology / regulation

- Hepatic BHMT-1 protein rises in ethanol-fed rats (64% increase at 4 weeks, not significant at 8 weeks)
  [PMID:25684186] and further with betaine supplementation [PMID:19239903], where it is hypothesised to
  "drive the remethylation of homocysteine to generate methionine".
- Pathway context: BHMT is one of two hepatic homocysteine remethylation routes (the other being
  folate/B12-dependent methionine synthase), and its first product, dimethylglycine, is the first step of
  betaine (and hence choline) catabolism to sarcosine.

## Reported association of uncertain significance

- Co-purification of BHMT with FDH (Aldh1l1) and CPS1 on soybean trypsin inhibitor affinity resin,
  eluting at ~300 kDa on gel filtration [PMID:17669278, abstract only]. BHMT alone is a ~180 kDa
  tetramer; the physiological relevance of the "complex" is not established.
