CC   -!- FUNCTION: This is one of the three pore-forming subunits of the
CC       heterotrimeric epithelial sodium channel (ENaC), a critical regulator
CC       of sodium balance and fluid homeostasis (PubMed:8188647,
CC       PubMed:9118951). ENaC operates in epithelial tissues, where it mediates
CC       the electrodiffusion of sodium ions from extracellular fluid through
CC       the apical membrane of cells, with water following osmotically
CC       (PubMed:8382172, PubMed:9118951). It plays a key role in maintaining
CC       sodium homeostasis through electrogenic sodium reabsorption in the
CC       kidneys. Additionally, ENaC is essential for airway surface liquid
CC       homeostasis, which is crucial for proper mucus clearance (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P37088, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8382172,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Na(+)(in) = Na(+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:34963,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:29101; Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:8188647,
CC         ECO:0000269|PubMed:8382172, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Originally identified and characterized by its
CC       inhibition by the diuretic drug amiloride. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8188647,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8382172, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterotrimer; containing an alpha/SCNN1A, a beta/SCNN1B and a
CC       gamma/SCNN1G subunit (PubMed:8188647, PubMed:9118951). Interacts with
CC       WWP1 (via WW domains). Interacts with WWP2 (via WW domains); inhibits
CC       the channel. Interacts with BPIFA1; the interaction is indirect via
CC       SCNN1B and inhibits the proteolytic processing of SCNN1A and SCNN1G and
CC       the activation of ENaC (By similarity). Interacts with the full-length
CC       immature form of PCSK9 (pro-PCSK9) (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P37088, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Apical cell membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11352848, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11773057,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8188647, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000269|PubMed:7929098,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8175716}. Cell projection, cilium
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P37088}. Cytoplasmic granule
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P37088}. Cytoplasm
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29453757}. Cytoplasmic vesicle, secretory vesicle,
CC       acrosome {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29453757}. Cell projection, cilium,
CC       flagellum {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29453757}. Note=In multi-ciliated cells,
CC       located on cilia. In non-ciliated epithelial cells, restricted to
CC       apical surfaces. In epidermis, located nearly uniformly in the
CC       cytoplasm in a granular distribution. In sebaceous glands, observed
CC       only in the cytoplasmic space in between the lipid vesicles. In eccrine
CC       sweat glands, mainly located at the apical surface of the cells facing
CC       the lumen (By similarity). In skin, in arrector pili muscle cells and
CC       in adipocytes, located in the cytoplasm and colocalized with actin
CC       fibers (By similarity). In spermatogonia, spermatocytes and round
CC       spermatids, located in the cytoplasm (PubMed:29453757). Prior to
CC       spermiation, location shifts from the cytoplasm to the spermatid tail
CC       (PubMed:29453757). In spermatozoa, localizes at the acrosome and the
CC       central region of the sperm flagellum (PubMed:29453757).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P37088, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11773057,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29453757}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Detected in kidney, lung and testis (at protein
CC       level). In the testis, detected within the seminiferous tubules but not
CC       in the interstitial cells (at protein level).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29453757}.
CC   -!- PTM: Ubiquitinated. Can be ubiquitinated at multiple sites and undergo
CC       monoubiquitination and polyubiquitination. Ubiquitination by NEDD4 or
CC       NEDD4L inhibits the ENaC channel through endocytosis, intracellular
CC       retention and degradation of its individual subunits.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11323714, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12424229,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12654927, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14556380,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8665844, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9351815}.
CC   -!- PTM: N-glycosylated. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11773057}.
CC   -!- PTM: ENaC is activated through the proteolytic maturation of its
CC       subunits. Furin cleaves the SCNN1A subunit, which results in a stepwise
CC       increase in the open probability of the channel due to the release of
CC       an inhibitory tract (By similarity). BPIFA1, which is recruited by the
CC       SCNN1B subunit, prevents the proteolytic activation of ENaC (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P37088,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q61180}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the amiloride-sensitive sodium channel
CC       (TC 1.A.6) family. SCNN1A subfamily. {ECO:0000305}.
