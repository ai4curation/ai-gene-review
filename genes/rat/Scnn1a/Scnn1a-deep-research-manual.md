## Identity

- UniProt P37089 (SCNNA_RAT), 698 aa; RGD:3639; NCBI GeneID 25122.
- Alpha subunit of the amiloride-sensitive epithelial sodium channel (ENaC), DEG/ENaC family
  (InterPro IPR001873, IPR004724; Pfam PF00858; PANTHER PTHR11690:SF124).
- Topology: cytoplasmic N-terminus (1-110), TM1 (111-131), large extracellular domain (132-589)
  containing the furin sites and GRIP inhibitory tract, TM2 (590-610), cytoplasmic C-terminus
  (611-698) with the PY motif (669-673) [file:rat/Scnn1a/Scnn1a-uniprot.txt].

## Channel function

- Expression cloning from rat colon: "It forms a channel that has the electrophysiological and
  pharmacological properties characteristic of the epithelial Na+ channel" [PMID:8382172].
- Beta and gamma were identified "by functional complementation of the alpha-subunit of the rat
  epithelial Na+ channel"; ENaC "constitutes the rate-limiting step for sodium reabsorption in
  epithelial cells that line the distal part of the renal tubule" [PMID:8107805].
- Co-expression of RCNaCh2 (beta) with RCNaCh1 (alpha) "increased the channel activity 18 +/- 5-fold"
  [PMID:8188647].
- Alpha alone: "the α-subunit is unique in that it is capable of forming a homo-trimeric structure
  that conducts Na(+) ions"; recombinant rat alpha in CHO cells gave amiloride-sensitive current
  [PMID:26394093].
- PHA1-type glycine mutation: "Single channel recordings of channels containing the mutant alpha
  subunit (alphaG95S) directly demonstrate a striking reduction in P(o)" [PMID:9118951].
- Stoichiometry: "Our results support a tetrameric arrangement of ENaC subunits where 2alpha, 1beta,
  and 1gamma come together around central pore." [PMID:17562820]; biochemical heterotetramers
  [PMID:12136275]. (Later cryo-EM structures of human ENaC show a 1:1:1 heterotrimer; not cached here.)

## Location

- Rat kidney: "alpha-ENaC was localized mainly in a zone in the apical domains"; immuno-EM showed
  ENaC "in both the apical plasma membrane and intracellular vesicles" [PMID:11352848].
- Rat colon: apical labelling of RCNaCh1 in Na+-depleted rats [PMID:8188647].
- MDCK: "apical, but not basal, membrane localization of ENaC" [PMID:11773057].
- Cortical actin: alpha-ENaC colocalizes with cortical F-actin and "F-actin specifically binds to
  the carboxyl terminus of alpha-ENaC" [PMID:16356937].
- Neurons: alpha-ENaC in somata and proximal dendrites of SON/PVN magnocellular neurons; benzamil
  "reversibly reduced a steady-state inward current" [PMID:22045317].
- Sperm/testis: cytoplasmic pools in germ cells; acrosome and flagellum of epididymal sperm
  [PMID:29453757]; flagellar midpiece [PMID:18298571].

## Regulation

- Nedd4 WW domains bind the alpha C-terminal PPPAY motif [PMID:8665844]; hypoxia reduces apical ENaC
  via Nedd4-2 ubiquitination [PMID:24093724]; ubiquitination of renal alpha-ENaC in vivo [PMID:32174140].
- SGK1 activates ENaC via alpha Ser621 [PMID:15234985]. CK2 sites are on beta/gamma, not alpha
  [PMID:18308722].
- Hormonal/expression regulation (aldosterone, angiotensin II, dexamethasone, testosterone, dietary
  salt): PMID:16426574, PMID:16332930, PMID:19732740, PMID:16263802, PMID:17873005, PMID:27413634,
  PMID:17485228, PMID:21423289, PMID:18305093.

## Physiology

- Lung: alpha-ENaC siRNA reduced baseline lung fluid absorption by ~30% and abolished terbutaline
  stimulation [PMID:16258001].
- Salt taste: taste-cell-specific ENaCalpha knockout mice showed "a complete loss of salt attraction
  and sodium taste responses" [PMID:20107438].
- Blood pressure: renal and vascular ENaC in Guytonian BP control (review) [PMID:24419567].
