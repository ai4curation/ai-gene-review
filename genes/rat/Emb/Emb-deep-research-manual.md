# Emb (embigin, gp70) - rat - manual literature research

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled by hand from cached
publications (publications/PMID_*.md), the UniProt record (O88775) and PubMed searches.

## Identity and structure
- Rat embigin (O88775, gene Emb, synonym Gp70) is a 328-aa type I transmembrane
  glycoprotein with a signal peptide (1-33), an extracellular region with two Ig-like
  domains (67-160, 159-254), one TM helix (265-285) and a short cytoplasmic tail
  (286-328) [file:rat/Emb/Emb-uniprot.txt]. It belongs to the basigin family of IgSF
  proteins with basigin (BSG/CD147) and neuroplastin (TCDB 8.A.23).
- Purified from rat erythrocytes as the 70-kDa N-glycoprotein cross-linked to MCT1 by DIDS;
  sequencing identified it as "the rat equivalent of the membrane-spanning mouse
  teratocarcinoma glycoprotein GP-70" [PMID:9169423].

## Ancillary protein (chaperone) of monocarboxylate transporters
- In rat erythrocytes MCT1 is associated with embigin, whereas in rabbit erythrocytes it
  associates with basigin [PMID:9169423; PMID:15917240; PMID:20695846].
- MCT2 co-immunoprecipitates with gp70 rather than CD147, confirmed by CFP/YFP FRET:
  "MCT2, which is pCMBS-insensitive, was found to co-immunoprecipitate with gp70 rather than
  CD147" [PMID:15917240]. The authors conclude "ancillary proteins are required to maintain
  the catalytic activity of MCTs as well as for their translocation to the plasma membrane"
  [PMID:15917240].
- In Xenopus oocytes (endogenous basigin, no embigin), "expression of active MCT2 at the
  plasma membrane of oocytes was significantly enhanced by co-expression of exogenous
  embigin"; a C-terminally truncated MCT2 "showed no detectable expression at the oocyte
  plasma membrane unless co-expressed with embigin" [PMID:20695846]. Embigin also modulates
  MCT2 inhibitor sensitivity (AR-C155858) via TMs 3/6 and the C-terminus of MCT2
  [PMID:20695846].
- DIDS cross-links MCT1 Lys38/Lys290 to embigin Lys160/Lys164; mutation of Cys180/Cys182
  (Ig domain 2 disulfide) abolishes cross-linking; a structural model places the embigin
  TM helix adjacent to MCT1 TMs 3 and 6 [PMID:19473976].
- Rat Gp70 interacts with MCT7 (Slc16a6) by BiFC and enhances MCT7-mediated taurine
  uptake ("increased by 148% and 233% in tandem with expression of CD147 and GP70")
  [PMID:35257743].

## Neuromuscular junction
- Emb mRNA is strongly upregulated in denervated rat muscle; in innervated muscle the
  protein is "preferentially localized to neuromuscular junctions"; muscle overexpression
  promotes nerve terminal sprouting, and knockdown accelerates terminal sprout retraction,
  with embigin proposed to enhance NCAM-dependent neuromuscular adhesion [PMID:19164284,
  abstract only].

## Adhesion
- Mouse embigin transfected into L cells enhanced integrin-dependent cell-substratum
  adhesion ("one role of embigin appears to be promotion of integrin-mediated
  cell-substratum adhesion") [PMID:8432389]. Rat embigin was identified as a gene expressed
  during prostate/mammary regression and proposed as "a regulator of cell/ECM interactions"
  [PMID:9438341, abstract only]. No study shows homophilic adhesion by embigin.

## Not found
- No evidence for embigin in axons, axon guidance, or dendrite self-avoidance (functions
  of other members of the broad IgSF PANTHER node PTN001296633, e.g. Dscam, fasciclin II).
