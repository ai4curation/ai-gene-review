## Identity and family
- Eukaryotic translation elongation factor 1A-1, 462 aa, TRAFAC translation-factor
  GTPase superfamily, EF-Tu/EF-1A subfamily (UniProt P62630; InterPro IPR004539,
  IPR000795; NCBIfam TIGR00483). Paralog: Eef1a2 (neurons, muscle).
- [file:rat/Eef1a1/Eef1a1-uniprot.txt "Translation elongation factor that catalyzes the GTP-dependent binding of aminoacyl-tRNA (aa-tRNA) to the A-site of ribosomes during the elongation phase of protein synthesis."]

## Core function: translational elongation
- GTP-bound eEF1A delivers aa-tRNA to the ribosomal A site; eEF1B2 recycles the GDP form
  [PMID:31468715 "GDP needs to be actively exchanged for GTP by the guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) eEF1B2, in order to enable eEF1A to participate in another round of elongation."]
- Rat eEF1A1 tRNA binding assayed directly [PMID:31468715 "In contrast, TIP30 did not affect tRNA binding by eEF1A1 (Appendix Fig S2)."]
- TIP30 competes with eEF1B2 [PMID:31468715 "Increasing concentrations of recombinant TIP30 decreased binding between eEF1A1 and eEF1B2 in a GST‐pulldown assay (Fig 4A)."]
- Nuclear exclusion and Exp5 export via aa-tRNA (human/other cells) [PMID:12426392 "all three elongation factors (eEF1A, eEF1B and eEF2) and the termination factor eRF1 are strictly excluded from nuclei."]
- eEF1A trapped on stalled ribosomes is degraded via RNF14/RNF25 [PMID:36638793 "Here, we elucidate a surveillance pathway which ultimately results in ubiquitination and degradation of eEF1A trapped on the ribosome by ternatin-4."]

## Non-canonical roles (evidence mostly from other species or perturbation)
- mRNA 3'UTR binding (rat recombinant protein, EMSA) [PMID:19501573 "Full length recombinant rat eEF1alpha, and independently domains I and III, formed complexes with the mRNA."]
- Actin bundling [PMID:20643906 "5 nM narciclasine is sufficient to impair eEF1A-related actin bundling activity"]
- Sphingosine kinase activation (human) [PMID:18263879 "we examined the potential role of eEF1A in regulation of the SKs and show that eEF1A is able to directly increase the activity of SK1 and SK2 approximately 3-fold in vitro."]
- EGF-induced ZPR1 interaction and nuclear redistribution [PMID:9852145 "EGF treatment induces the interaction of ZPR1 with eEF-1alpha and the redistribution of both proteins to the nucleus."]
- Lysosomal membrane in CMA regulation (rat liver lysosomes) [PMID:20797626 "Susceptibility of EF1α to treatment with an exogenous protease (trypsin) confirmed its association with the cytosolic side of the lysosome membrane"]
- Lipotoxic death requires eEF1A-1 (CHO, H9c2) [PMID:16319173 "eEF1A-1 involvement in lipotoxicity was confirmed in H9c2 cardiomyoblasts, in which small interfering RNA-mediated knockdown also conferred palmitate resistance."]
- Synapses: PSD fraction [PMID:15265584 "Immunoblot analysis showed that eEF1A in the PSD fraction is enriched over homogenate."]; gephyrin clusters after overexpression [PMID:23839781 "Overexpression of eEF1A in cultured hippocampal neurons results in a significant increase in number, size and density of postsynaptic gephyrin clusters after 21 days in vitro."]
- Isoform switch in neurons [PMID:11907030 "During postnatal brain development the level of peptide elongation factor-1A (eEF1A-1) expression declines and that of the highly homologous isoform, eEF1A-2, increases in neurons."]
