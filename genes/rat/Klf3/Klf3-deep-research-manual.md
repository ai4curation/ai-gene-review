# Klf3 (rat) - manual literature research

No deep-research API provider was available in this session, so this summary was compiled manually from
PubMed searches and the cached publications in `publications/`. Most experimental work on KLF3/BKLF was
done in mouse (and some in human cells); the only rat-specific paper in GOA is PMID:18406357.

## Identity and domain structure
- Rat Klf3 (UniProt D4A4V3, TrEMBL; RGD:1593290) encodes a 344-aa Krueppel-like factor with three C-terminal
  C2H2 zinc fingers. It is 97.4% identical to mouse BKLF/KLF3 (Q60980) and has an identical zinc-finger
  region, the PVDLT CtBP-binding motif and conserved sumoylation lysines K10/K197
  [file:rat/Klf3/Klf3-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md].

## DNA binding
- BKLF was cloned as a major CACCC-box-binding activity in mouse erythroid cells
  [PMID:8657145 "we searched for additional CACCC-box transcription factors that might be active in murine erythroid cells"];
  [PMID:8657145 "BKLF binds with high affinity and preferentially over Sp1 to many CACCC sequences of erythroid cell expressed genes"].
- KLF3 occupies the Klf8 promoters in vivo [PMID:18687676 "Chromatin immunoprecipitation experiments confirm that Klf3 occupies both Klf8 promoters in vivo."]
  and the C/ebpalpha promoter [PMID:18391014 "Chromatin immunoprecipitations confirm that Klf3 binds the C/ebpalpha promoter in vivo."].

## Transcriptional repression mechanism
- The N-terminus is a repression domain that recruits CtBP2 through a PxDLS-type motif
  [PMID:9724649 "We show that mCtBP2 recognizes a related motif in the minimal repression domain of BKLF, and the integrity of this motif is required for repression activity."].
- FHL3 is an additional cofactor [PMID:12556451 "We show here that BKLF also recruits the four and a half LIM domain protein FHL3."].
- Sumoylation contributes to full repression [PMID:15684403 "both recruitment of CtBP and sumoylation are required for full repression by BKLF"].
- Review: [PMID:21360637 "KLF3 is a transcriptional repressor that binds the cofactor C-terminal binding protein, which in turn recruits a large repressor complex to mediate transcriptional silencing."]

## Biological roles (mouse knockouts)
- Erythropoiesis: KLF3 acts as a feedback repressor of a subset of KLF1/EKLF targets
  [PMID:22711990 "KLF3 is required for normal erythropoiesis, and we conclude that the impaired erythroid maturation is due, at least in part, to the combined effects of the derepression of a cohort of KLF3-dependent genes in KLF3-deficient erythroid cells."].
- Adipogenesis: [PMID:18391014 "Klf3 knockout mice have less white adipose tissue, and their fat pads contain smaller and fewer cells."];
  [PMID:18391014 "A Klf3 mutant that does not bind CtBP cannot block adipogenesis."]
- Klf family cross-regulation: [PMID:18687676 "Here we show that Bklf or Klf3 (Basic Krüppel-like factor) represses the Klf8 (Krüppel-like Factor 8) gene in vivo."]
- B-cell development is also reported in the review PMID:21360637 (not separately examined here).

## Rat-specific data
- In neonatal rat cardiac myocytes, endothelin-1 transiently decreases Klf3 mRNA
  [PMID:18406357 "Whereas the decrease in expression of Klf3 was transient with minimal expression at ∼ 30 min (Fig. 1G)"].
  This is an expression response only; no Klf3 protein function in the endothelin response was tested
  [PMID:18406357 "Clearly, the genes which each of the Klfs regulates in cardiac myocytes remain to be established."].
