CC   -!- FUNCTION: Structural component of the vault particle, which is a
CC       massive ribonucleoprotein complex that may play a role in cargo
CC       transport and signal transduction (By similarity). Oligomerizes to form
CC       a cage-like vault complex, which can house several minor vault
CC       components including PARP4 (By similarity). Vaults may play a role in
CC       nucleo-cytoplasmic transport (By similarity). Down-regulates IFNG-
CC       mediated STAT1 signaling and subsequent activation of JAK (By
CC       similarity). Down-regulates SRC activity and signaling through MAP
CC       kinases (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q14764}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: The vault is a massive cage-like structure; it consists of a
CC       dimer of half-vaults, with each half-vault comprising 39 identical MVP
CC       subunits (PubMed:18044992, PubMed:19150846). The two half-vaults
CC       assemble to form the complete vault, where minor vault components such
CC       as PARP4, TEP1 and vault RNAs (vRNAs) may bind to the interior (By
CC       similarity). Interacts (via MVP repeats 3 and 4) with PARP4; each MVP
CC       subunit binds a single PARP4 molecule, allowing each vault to house up
CC       to 78 PARP4 molecules (By similarity). Interacts (via MVP shoulder)
CC       with nucleotide ligands, including NAD+ and ADP (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with TEP1 (By similarity). Interacts (via MVP 3) with PTEN
CC       and activated MAPK1 (By similarity). The phosphorylated protein
CC       interacts with the SH2 domains of PTPN11 and SRC (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with APEX1 (By similarity). May interact with ZNF540 (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q14764,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18044992, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19150846}.
