CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms voltage-independent, pH-gated trimeric sodium channels
CC       that act as postsynaptic excitatory receptors in the nervous system,
CC       playing a crucial role in regulating synaptic plasticity, learning, and
CC       memory (PubMed:11448963, PubMed:9062189, PubMed:9360943). Upon
CC       extracellular pH drop this channel elicits transient, fast activating,
CC       and completely desensitizing inward currents (PubMed:12198124,
CC       PubMed:9062189, PubMed:9360943). Displays high selectivity for sodium
CC       ions but can also permit the permeation of other cations
CC       (PubMed:11448963, PubMed:9062189, PubMed:9360943). Regulates more or
CC       less directly intracellular calcium concentration and CaMKII
CC       phosphorylation, and thereby the density of dendritic spines
CC       (PubMed:15369669). Modulates neuronal activity in the circuits
CC       underlying innate fear (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q6NXK8,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11448963, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12198124,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15369669, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9062189,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9360943}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform ASIC1a]: Permeable to other cations including
CC       calcium, lithium and potassium. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9062189,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9360943}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform ASIC1b]: pH activation and steady-state inactivation
CC       are shifted to more acidic values (PubMed:12198124). Forms channels
CC       that are not permeable to calcium as it discrimates stronger between
CC       monovalent cations (PubMed:9707631). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12198124,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9707631}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform ASIC-beta2]: Has no pH-gated sodium channel activity
CC       per se but can associate with other ASICs and regulate their pH-
CC       sensitivity. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11588592}.

CC   -!- DOMAIN: The second transmembrane domain (TM2) is a discontinuous alpha-
CC       helix disrupted by the GAS motif, which forms the selectivity filter by
CC       adopting an extended, belt-like conformation aligned approximately
CC       parallel to the membrane plane. This peptide belt encircles the waist
CC       of the channel and divides TM2 into two discontinuous helical segments.
CC       The distal helical segment of TM2 interacts with the cytoplasmic
CC       portion of the first transmembrane domain (TM1) from a neighboring
CC       subunit, contributing to the structural and functional integrity of the
CC       channel. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q1XA76}.
