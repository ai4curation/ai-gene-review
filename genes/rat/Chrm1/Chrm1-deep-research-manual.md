# Chrm1 (rat, UniProt P08482) - manual literature research

No deep-research API was available for this session; this summary was compiled by hand
from cached publications (`publications/PMID_*.md`) and the UniProt record.

## Identity and family

- Muscarinic acetylcholine receptor M1 (ACM1_RAT), 460 aa, class A (rhodopsin-like)
  seven-transmembrane GPCR; UniProt: "Belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor 1 family.
  Muscarinic acetylcholine receptor subfamily. CHRM1 sub-subfamily."
  [file:rat/Chrm1/Chrm1-uniprot.txt]
- Rat M1 cDNA was among the first muscarinic receptor genes cloned, from rat cerebral cortex
  [PMID:3037705 "Complementary DNAs for three different muscarinic acetylcholine receptors were
  isolated from a rat cerebral cortex library, and the cloned receptors were expressed in
  mammalian cells"].

## Molecular function and signalling

- UniProt FUNCTION: "Primary transducing effect is Pi turnover." [file:rat/Chrm1/Chrm1-uniprot.txt]
- Cloned rat M1 expressed in a muscarinic-null rat cell line couples to G proteins and
  drives both PI hydrolysis and pertussis-toxin-sensitive adenylate cyclase inhibition
  [PMID:2846274 "The cloned receptors mediated both adenylate cyclase inhibition and
  phosphoinositide hydrolysis, but by different mechanisms."; "Pertussis toxin blocked the
  inhibition of adenylate cyclase"].
- Rat m1 expressed in CHO cells mediates carbachol-stimulated PI hydrolysis; the conserved
  Cys98/Cys178 extracellular disulfide is needed for ligand binding
  [PMID:1317867 "display no carbachol-mediated hydrolysis of phosphoinositides"].
- In native rat hippocampal neurons, M1 is the principal receptor coupling Gq/11 to PLC;
  methacholine evokes IP3/DAG signals, PKCepsilon membrane recruitment and MARCKS
  translocation; GRK2/GRK6 and PKC regulate M1 desensitization depending on synaptic activity
  [PMID:17908240 "M1 mACh receptors are the major subtype signalling through Gq/11 to
  phospholipase C (PLC) and phosphoinositide hydrolysis in the hippocampus"].

## Localization

- Rat striatum EM: M1 in most neurons, enriched in spiny dendrites and postsynaptic densities;
  a few M1-positive axon terminals form asymmetric synapses
  [PMID:8182478 "A small number of m1-immunoreactive axon terminals were observed, all forming
  asymmetrical synapses."].
- Rat hippocampus: somatodendritic M1 in pyramidal and granule cells
  [PMID:7751967 "m1 is widely expressed in somata and dendrites of pyramidal neurons and granule
  cells in dentate gyrus."].
- Rat basolateral amygdala EM: M1 on pyramidal perikarya, dendrites and spines plus axon
  terminals [PMID:23559406 "M1R immunoreactivity (M1R-ir) was also seen in axon terminals, most
  of which formed asymmetrical synapses."].

## Physiology

- Rat neuromuscular junction: pirenzepine-sensitive (M1-type) presynaptic modulation of evoked
  ACh release [PMID:12534975 "the M1 receptor-selective antagonist pirenzepine (10 micro m)
  reduced evoked neurotransmission"]. Pharmacological, abstract-only.
- Mouse knockout (ortholog): loss of muscarinic M-current suppression in sympathetic neurons
  [PMID:9371842]; selective memory defects and reduced hippocampal LTP [PMID:12483218];
  contribution, with M3, to pilocarpine-induced salivation [PMID:15266016].
