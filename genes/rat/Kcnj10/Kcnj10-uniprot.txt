CC   -!- FUNCTION: May be responsible for potassium buffering action of glial
CC       cells in the brain (PubMed:7608203). Inward rectifier potassium
CC       channels are characterized by a greater tendency to allow potassium to
CC       flow into the cell rather than out of it. Their voltage dependence is
CC       regulated by the concentration of extracellular potassium; as external
CC       potassium is raised, the voltage range of the channel opening shifts to
CC       more positive voltages. The inward rectification is mainly due to the
CC       blockage of outward current by internal magnesium. Can be blocked by
CC       extracellular barium and cesium (PubMed:7608203). In the kidney,
CC       together with KCNJ16, mediates basolateral K(+) recycling in distal
CC       tubules; this process is critical for Na(+) reabsorption at the tubules
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P78508,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7608203}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=K(+)(in) = K(+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:29463, ChEBI:CHEBI:29103;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:20807765, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7608203};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Channel activity is strongly regulated by
CC       variations of cytosolic pH; channels are activated by alkaline and
CC       inhibited by acidic pH values (PubMed:20807765). Activated by
CC       phosphatidylinositol 4,5 biphosphate (PtdIns(4,5)P2) (PubMed:18690035).
CC       Inhibited by Ba(2+) and Cs(+) (PubMed:7608203).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18690035, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20807765,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7608203}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotetramer (PubMed:38355723). In kidney cells, it forms
CC       heteromeric channels with Kir5.1/KCNJ16; this interaction is required
CC       for KCNJ16 localization to the basolateral membrane. Interacts with
CC       MAGI1, alone and possibly as a heteromer with KCNJ16; this interaction
CC       may facilitate KCNJ10/KCNJ16 potassium channel expression at the
CC       basolateral membrane in kidney cells (By similarity). Interacts with
CC       PATJ (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P78508,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9JM63, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38355723}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P78508}; Multi-
CC       pass membrane protein {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P78508}. Basolateral cell
CC       membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P78508}. Note=In kidney distal
CC       convoluted tubules, located in the basolateral membrane where it
CC       colocalizes with KCNJ16. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P78508}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Predominantly expressed in the brain, including in
CC       glial cells of the cerebellum and forebrain. Expressed at lower levels
CC       in the kidney, and other peripheral tissues.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:7608203, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7624316}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the inward rectifier-type potassium channel
CC       (TC 1.A.2.1) family. KCNJ10 subfamily. {ECO:0000305}.
