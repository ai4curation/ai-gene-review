## 2026-09-27 OpenScientist lipase-family follow-up

The focused secondary-lipolysis report resolved the remaining Pnlip family carry-overs. The four IBA rows at PTN000906454 now trace to vascular-lipase paralogs rather than classical pancreatic lipase evidence: LPL/LIPC/LIPG for glycerophospholipid PLA1 activity, LPL for lipoprotein lipase activity, LIPC/LIPG for HDL particle remodeling, and LPL/LIPC for fatty-acid biosynthesis. Pnlip's meaningful digestive reaction remains colipase-dependent hydrolysis of dietary triglyceride. I moved the LPL, HDL-remodeling and fatty-acid-biosynthesis rows from UNDECIDED to REMOVE, and marked PLA1 as over-annotated rather than a clean absence because rat pancreatic lipase may retain weak in-vitro sn-1 phospholipid turnover.

The same report also traced GO:0061365 positive regulation of triglyceride lipase activity through rat ISO -> mouse ISO -> human PNLIP IDA PMID:9631512. The human source characterizes recombinant pancreatic lipase's own colipase dependence and interfacial activation, not regulation of a separate triglyceride lipase, so the rat ISO is now removed as a term-scoping/role-conflation error. GO:0004806 already captures the direct triglyceride-lipase activity.
