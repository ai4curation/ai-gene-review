# Csn3 (rat kappa-casein, P04468) - review notes

- cDNA: 21-aa signal peptide, 157-aa mature protein; little homology to ruminant mature kappa-casein [PMID:6094580 "However, little homology was apparent when the mature kappa-casein protein sequences from ovine or bovine sources were compared with rat kappa-casein."]
- Described as the micelle-stabilizing casein [PMID:6094580 "An 820-nucleotide-long cDNA clone for the kappa-casein (the casein micelle-stabilizing protein) from rat mammary gland was isolated"]
- Prolactin-induced expression [PMID:6094580 "Prolactin appears to modulate the production of kappa-casein mRNA."]
- Human milk: surface location implied [PMID:7979373 "Since kappa-casein also stabilizes the micelles against precipitation by Ca2+ ions, a surface position for most of the kappa-casein is implied."]
- Mouse Csn3 KO [PMID:16698927 "However, these females did not suckle their pups and failed to lactate because of destabilization of the micelles in the lumina of the mammary gland."]
- UniProt: chymosin cleavage site 115-116 (no evidence tag, by similarity); O-glycosylation and phosphosites by similarity.
- Core MF per coordinator: GO:0140777 protein-containing complex stabilizing activity (OLS-verified; imperfect fit) + proposed term "casein micelle stabilizing activity".

## Deep research integration (OpenScientist, 2026-10-24)

All PMIDs cited by the report were fetched into publications/ and the points used were
checked against the abstracts.

- No contradictions with the review. The report's primary function (surface "hairy brush"
  stabilization of the micelle) matches core GO:0140777 (family convention for
  kappa-casein), protein stabilization and lactation.
- Added PMID:41200845 ("κ-Casein inhibits amorphous aggregation of β-casein": kappa-casein
  blocks calcium-induced beta-casein aggregation in vitro) to GO:0140777 and the IBA
  protein stabilization row, and PMID:12778565 (stabilizing kappa-casein brush) and a
  report quote to GO:0140777. The same PMID:41200845 support was added to the bovine,
  human and mouse kappa-casein reviews.
- Not used: chymosin cleavage at Phe115-Leu116 (chymosin's activity; kappa-casein is the
  substrate), macropeptide bioactivities (PMID:19744534, 41370273), expression markers
  (PMID:15215175, 8813725), self-association and micelle-size studies (PMID:39106061,
  18069739), bovine variant conservation (PMID:41957694).
- No action changes.
