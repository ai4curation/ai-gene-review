CC   -!- FUNCTION: This is one of the three pore-forming subunits of the
CC       heterotrimeric epithelial sodium channel (ENaC), a critical regulator
CC       of sodium balance and fluid homeostasis (PubMed:8107805,
CC       PubMed:8188647, PubMed:9118951). ENaC operates in epithelial tissues,
CC       where it mediates the electrodiffusion of sodium ions from
CC       extracellular fluid through the apical membrane of cells, with water
CC       following osmotically (PubMed:8107805, PubMed:8188647, PubMed:9118951).
CC       It plays a key role in maintaining sodium homeostasis through
CC       electrogenic sodium reabsorption in the kidneys. Additionally, ENaC is
CC       essential for airway surface liquid homeostasis, which is crucial for
CC       proper mucus clearance (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51170,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8107805, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8188647,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Na(+)(in) = Na(+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:34963,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:29101; Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:8107805,
CC         ECO:0000269|PubMed:8188647};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Originally identified and characterized by its
CC       inhibition by the diuretic drug amiloride. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8107805,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8188647}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the heterotrimeric epithelial sodium channel
CC       (ENaC) composed of an alpha/SCNN1A, a beta/SCNN1B and a gamma/SCNN1G
CC       subunit (PubMed:8107805, PubMed:8188647, PubMed:9118951). Interacts
CC       with WWP1 (via WW domains) (By similarity). Interacts with WWP2 (via WW
CC       domains); inhibits the channel (By similarity). Interacts with the
CC       full-length immature form of PCSK9 (pro-PCSK9); inhibits ENaC by
CC       promoting its proteasomal degradation (By similarity). Interacts with
CC       BPIFA1; the interaction is indirect via SCNN1B and inhibits the
CC       proteolytic maturation of SCNN1A and SCNN1G and the activation of ENaC
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51170,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8107805, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8188647,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Apical cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8188647,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9351815}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51170}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated on serine and threonine residues. Aldosterone and
CC       insulin increase the basal level of phosphorylation.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9501257}.
CC   -!- PTM: Ubiquitinated. Can be ubiquitinated at multiple sites and undergo
CC       monoubiquitination and polyubiquitination. Ubiquitination by NEDD4 or
CC       NEDD4L inhibits the ENaC channel through endocytosis, intracellular
CC       retention and degradation of its individual subunits.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11323714, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12424229,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12654927, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8665844,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9351815}.
CC   -!- PTM: ENaC is activated through the proteolytic maturation of its
CC       subunits. Furin cleaves the SCNN1G subunit first, followed by cleavage
CC       by prostasin (PRSS8), which results in a stepwise increase in the open
CC       probability of the channel due to the release of an inhibitory tract.
CC       BPIFA1, which is recruited by the SCNN1B subunit, prevents the
CC       proteolytic activation of ENaC. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51170}.
CC   -!- PTM: N-glycosylated. N-linked glycans are processed to complex type
CC       during ENaC complex assembly and transport to the plasma membrane.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9WU39}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the amiloride-sensitive sodium channel
CC       (TC 1.A.6) family. SCNN1G subfamily. {ECO:0000305}.
