# Emp3 (rat, Q9QYW5) - manual literature research

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled by hand from cached
publications and PubMed searches (2026-10-28).

## Identity
- Rat Emp3 (Swiss-Prot Q9QYW5, RGD:621094, GeneID 81505), 163 aa, member of the
  PMP-22/EMP/MP20 tetraspan family (Pfam PF00822 PMP22_Claudin; PANTHER PTHR10671:SF8).
  Four predicted TM helices (4-24, 66-86, 100-120, 139-159) and two predicted
  N-glycosylation sites (N46, N56) on the first extracellular loop (UniProt).
- Protein existence is at transcript level only in rat; UniProt function text is
  "Probably involved in cell proliferation and cell-cell interactions."

## Rat-specific literature
- Cloned as "gene 133" from the rat mammary line LA7 [PMID:10570147 "The first, called
  133, is the rat ortholog of the human epithelial membrane protein 3 (EMP3)"]. Antisense
  experiments indicated that gene 133 negatively controls DMSO-induced dome formation
  [PMID:10570147 "the expression of gene 133 blocks the expression of the Na(+) channel
  genes, thus preventing formation of the domes"]. Abstract only cached.

## Ortholog (human EMP3, P54852) literature
- Overexpression of any EMP (EMP-1, -2, -3, PMP-22) in HEK-293 cells induced blebbing and
  caspase-dependent death; EMPs co-associate with the P2X7 C terminus [PMID:12107182
  "the constitutive overexpression of any of the EMPs in HEK-293 cells led to cell
  blebbing, annexin V binding, and cell death, by a caspase-dependent pathway"]. This is
  the probable basis of the human apoptotic process / bleb assembly annotations that RGD
  and Ensembl Compara project to rat. Abstract only cached.
- In glioblastoma cells EMP3 stabilises EGFR by restraining its trafficking to RAB7+ late
  endosomes; it interacts with endocytic proteins and TBC1D5 [PMID:37936247 "EMP3 promotes
  EGFR/CDK2 signaling by regulating the trafficking and enhancing the stability of
  EGFR"]. EMP3 resides at the plasma membrane and in intracellular compartments
  [PMID:37936247 "EMP3 resides in both compartments"].
- Review: EMP3 is a tetraspan membrane protein regulating several membrane receptors
  (EGFR, CD44, integrin/TGF-beta pathway components) in GBM [PMID:34067658].
- EMP3 carries the MAM blood group antigen on red cells; MAM-null cells have low CD44 and
  erythroid progenitors give increased reticulocyte yield [PMID:36789459].
