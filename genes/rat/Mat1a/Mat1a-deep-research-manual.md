# Mat1a (rat) manual literature summary

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled manually from cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record.

## Enzyme and reaction
Rat Mat1a encodes the catalytic subunit of liver methionine adenosyltransferase (MAT I/III; EC 2.5.1.6), which makes S-adenosylmethionine (SAM), the main methyl donor, from methionine and ATP [PMID:10873471 "Most of the transmethylation reactions use the same methyl donor, S-adenosylmethionine (SAM), that is synthesised from methionine and ATP by methionine adenosyltransferase (MAT)"]. The reaction is an SN2 attack of the methionine sulfur on ATP C5' with triphosphate cleavage [PMID:12888348 "the sulphur atom from methionine attacks C5' from ATP while triphosphate chain is cleaved"].

## Structure and oligomeric state
MAT I is a tetramer of two tight dimers [PMID:10873471 "The enzyme consists of four identical subunits arranged in two tight dimers"]; recombinant tetramers and dimers interconvert [PMID:9202427 "the rat liver S-adenosyl-L-methionine synthetase produced in bacterial cells is present in two oligomeric forms, tetramers and dimers, which are in equilibrium"].

## Regulation
Oxidised glutathione inhibits the enzyme [PMID:1517209 "Both are inhibited in the presence of GSSG at pH 8"]; NO inactivates it by S-nitrosylation [PMID:9755242 "S-nitrosylation of 1 thiol residue per subunit was associated with a marked inactivation of the enzyme"]. In alcoholic liver injury models liver-specific MAT activity declines with LPS [PMID:10898761]. Betaine feeding raises MAT-1 protein in ethanol-fed rat liver [PMID:19239903].

## Localization
Cytoplasmic in hepatocytes and also nuclear, binding nuclear matrix in extrahepatic tissues [PMID:19497982 "we identified methionine adenosyltransferase I/III in most rat tissues, both in the cytoplasm and the nucleus"].
