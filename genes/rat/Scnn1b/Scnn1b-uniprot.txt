CC   -!- FUNCTION: This is one of the three pore-forming subunits of the
CC       heterotrimeric epithelial sodium channel (ENaC), a critical regulator
CC       of sodium balance and fluid homeostasis (PubMed:8107805,
CC       PubMed:9118951). ENaC operates in epithelial tissues, where it mediates
CC       the electrodiffusion of sodium ions from extracellular fluid through
CC       the apical membrane of cells, with water following osmotically
CC       (PubMed:11352848, PubMed:11773057, PubMed:9118951). It plays a key role
CC       in maintaining sodium homeostasis through electrogenic sodium
CC       reabsorption in the kidneys (By similarity). This subunit is not
CC       essential for ENaC function in airway surface liquid homeostasis and
CC       proper mucus clearance (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9WU38,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11352848, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11773057,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8107805, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Na(+)(in) = Na(+)(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:34963,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:29101; Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:8107805};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Originally identified and characterized by its
CC       inhibition by the diuretic drug amiloride.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8107805}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the heterotrimeric epithelial sodium channel
CC       (ENaC) composed of an alpha/SCNN1A, a beta/SCNN1B and a gamma/SCNN1G
CC       subunit (PubMed:9118951). Interacts with WWP1 (via WW domains) (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with WWP2 (via WW domains) (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with the full-length immature form of PCSK9 (pro-PCSK9) (By
CC       similarity). Interacts (N-glycosylated) with BPIFA1; the interaction is
CC       direct and inhibits the proteolytic processing of SCNN1A and SCNN1G and
CC       the activation of ENaC (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51168,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Apical cell membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11352848, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11773057,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9118951}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P37089}. Cytoplasmic vesicle membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11352848, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11773057}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P37089}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in lung and epididymis (PubMed:30659401).
CC       In the caput region of the epididymis, expressed at the luminal and
CC       basolateral surfaces of the ducts and in the smooth muscle coat
CC       (PubMed:30659401). In the caudal region of the epididymis, expressed
CC       along the luminal border but not continuously, in the smooth muscle
CC       coat, in the interstitial muscle tissue and in sperm in the caudal
CC       lumen (PubMed:30659401). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30659401}.
CC   -!- PTM: Ubiquitinated. Can be ubiquitinated at multiple sites and undergo
CC       monoubiquitination and polyubiquitination. Ubiquitination by NEDD4 or
CC       NEDD4L inhibits the ENaC channel through endocytosis, intracellular
CC       retention and degradation of its individual subunits (PubMed:11323714,
CC       PubMed:12654927, PubMed:8665844). However, some studies could not
CC       confirm the ubiquitination of this subunit of the ENaC
CC       (PubMed:9351815). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11323714,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12654927, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8665844,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9351815}.
CC   -!- PTM: N-glycosylated (PubMed:11773057). N-glycosylation is required for
CC       interaction with BPIFA1 (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P51168,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11773057}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated on serine and threonine residues. Aldosterone and
CC       insulin increase the basal level of phosphorylation.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9501257}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the amiloride-sensitive sodium channel
CC       (TC 1.A.6) family. SCNN1B subfamily. {ECO:0000305}.
