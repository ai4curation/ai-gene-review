# Sry (rat, P36394) curation notes

- Rat carries many Sry copies with amino-acid variants [PMID:32398044 "The commonly used laboratory rat, Rattus norvegicus, is unique in having multiple Sry gene copies found on the Y chromosome, with different copies encoding amino acid variations that influence the resulting protein function."]; Sry4A, Sry1 and Sry3C are expressed at E12-E13. P36394 represents one copy.
- Adult brain: Sry in tyrosine hydroxylase neurons of the substantia nigra; knockdown lowers TH and causes motor deficits [PMID:16488877].
- Most rat GO rows are IBA/IEA; actions follow the human and mouse reviews (cell differentiation -> Sertoli cell differentiation; nuclear speck non-core).
