## Identity and molecular function

Itpr1 encodes the type 1 inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate receptor (IP3R1, InsP3R1), a 2,750-residue
multi-pass ER membrane protein that assembles into a tetrameric IP3- and Ca2+-gated Ca2+ release
channel.

- Channel activity of recombinant rat IP3R1 was recorded directly in planar lipid bilayers
  [PMID:15533917 "we compared single-channel behavior of the recombinant rat InsP3R1, InsP3R2, and
  InsP3R3 expressed in Sf9 cells, reconstituted into planar lipid bilayers"], with a high-affinity
  ATP modulatory site [PMID:15533917 "InsP3R1 has a high-affinity (0.13 mM) ATP modulatory site"].
- On-nucleus patch clamp of rat IP3R1 expressed in IP3R-null DT40 cells showed biphasic Ca2+ and
  ATP regulation [PMID:22547632 "we examined the effects of [ATP] on the Ca2+ dependency of rat
  InsP3R-1 (rInsP3R-1) and mouse InsP3R-2 (mInsP3R-2) activity expressed in DT40-3KO cells"].
- Cryo-EM of nanodisc-reconstituted rat IP3R1 resolved IP3, Ca2+ and ATP sites [PMID:36376291
  "These structures provide unprecedented details governing binding of IP3, Ca2+ and ATP,
  revealing conformational changes that couple ligand-binding to channel opening."; "The CIA-IP3R1
  structure reveals a previously unseen ATP-binding site located at the interface between the ILD
  and LNK domains"].
- Tetrameric assembly; six transmembrane segments define the pore region [PMID:10506212 "Our
  results are the first to experimentally define the presence of six membrane-spanning regions."];
  proteolytic fragments still assemble into functional tetramers [PMID:23479737 "they physically
  associate and form functional channels"].
- cAMP directly sensitises all IP3R subtypes, including rat IP3R1 expressed in DT40 cells, to IP3
  independent of PKA [PMID:20189985 "These results establish that cAMP itself increases the
  sensitivity of all IP(3)R subtypes to IP(3)."].

## Localisation

- ER membrane: rat IP3R1 is co-translationally targeted to the ER by TMD1 [PMID:19845505
  "Hydrophobic residues within TMD1 and TMD2 then ensure stable association with the ER membrane."]
  and diffuses freely in a continuous ER [PMID:21550988 "IP(3)R1 and IP(3)R3 were uniformly
  distributed within the membranes of the endoplasmic reticulum (ER), but the distribution of
  IP(3)R2 was punctate."].
- Other membranes are reported but secretory-vesicle localisation is contentious [PMID:19845505
  "and perhaps also in secretory vesicles [8], although the latter is contentious [9]"].
- Nuclear envelope / inner nuclear membrane in rat myotubes, where nuclear IP3R1 releases Ca2+
  [PMID:16014380 "type-1 receptors preferentially localized in the inner nuclear membrane"].
- Purkinje neuron ER, including smooth ER subcompartments [PMID:1311032 "Ca(2+)-ATPase and the
  InsP3 receptor have a widespread, although not identical, distribution throughout the ER."].
- ER-mitochondria contacts, coupled to VDAC1 through GRP75 [PMID:17178908 "We demonstrate that
  VDAC1 is physically linked to the endoplasmic reticulum Ca(2+)-release channel inositol
  1,4,5-trisphosphate receptor (IP(3)R) through the molecular chaperone glucose-regulated protein
  75 (grp75)."].
- Junctional ER under the plasma membrane in rat chromaffin cells [PMID:15911880 "it couples the
  plasmalemmal EGFR with IP(3)R1 at the Ca(2+) store located juxtaposed to the plasma membrane"].
- Perinuclear ER in A7r5 smooth muscle cells [PMID:12615969 "IP(3)R1 was predominantly localized in
  the perinuclear region"].

## Interacting proteins (regulatory)

HAP1A/huntingtin [PMID:12873381], 4.1N [PMID:12676536], syntaxin 1B [PMID:21424589], ANO1
[PMID:23982204], polycystin-2 [PMID:16223735], ataxin-3 [PMID:19036964], calcineurin via Bcl-2
[PMID:15212990], AKAP9/PKA [PMID:19236309], Herp-mediated degradation [PMID:22045699]. These are
regulators or scaffolds that tune IP3R1-mediated Ca2+ release; none changes the core activity.

## Physiological roles (downstream of Ca2+ release)

Hepatocyte proliferation in early liver regeneration [PMID:24814243], neurite outgrowth in PC12
cells [PMID:21211516], neuronal death after neonatal anoxia [PMID:28072885], preconditioning
neuroprotection [PMID:20434434]. All are knockdown or pharmacology phenotypes downstream of the Ca2+
signal. Expression is induced by hypoxia [PMID:17285299].
