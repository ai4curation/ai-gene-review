# Lpl (rat, UniProt Q06000): manual literature summary

No deep-research API was available for this review. This summary was written by hand from
the cached publications (`publications/PMID_*.md`) and the UniProt record
(`genes/rat/Lpl/Lpl-uniprot.txt`). Each statement carries its source.

## Identity and family

- Rat lipoprotein lipase, 474 aa, signal peptide 1-27, mature chain 28-474 [file:rat/Lpl/Lpl-uniprot.txt].
- "Belongs to the AB hydrolase superfamily. Lipase family." Catalytic triad Ser159 (nucleophile), Asp183, His268; Ca(2+) binding at 194/197/199/202; C-terminal PLAT domain 341-464 [file:rat/Lpl/Lpl-uniprot.txt].
- PANTHER PTHR11610:SF3 (LIPOPROTEIN LIPASE); the parent node PTN000906454 also contains hepatic lipase (LIPC) and endothelial lipase (LIPG).

## Molecular function

- UniProt (by similarity to human P06858 / mouse P11151): "Catalyzes the hydrolysis of triglycerides from circulating chylomicrons and very low density lipoproteins (VLDL), and thereby plays an important role in lipid clearance from the blood stream, lipid utilization and storage" and "it is primarily a triglyceride lipase with low but detectable phospholipase activity".
- Human catalytic triad: [PMID:1371284 "Lipoprotein lipase (LPL) plays a central role in normal lipid metabolism as the key enzyme involved in the hydrolysis of triglycerides present in chylomicrons and very low density lipoproteins."]
- Activation by APOC2: [PMID:10727238 "activation occurs via direct helix-helix interactions between apoC-II39-62 and the loop covering the active site of LpL"]
- Ca(2+) and folding: [PMID:16179346 "The second step was promoted by Ca2+ and converted LPL monomers from the molten globule state to dimerization-competent and more tightly folded monomers that rapidly formed active LPL dimers."]
- Heparin/HSPG binding: [PMID:11342582 "Thus, heparin association is required for LpL stability and normal physiologic functions."]

## Location and trafficking

- [PMID:20620994 "LPL is synthesized and secreted by myocytes and adipocytes, but then finds its way into the lumen of capillaries, where it hydrolyzes lipoprotein triglycerides."]
- [PMID:24726386 "Both cell-culture and in vivo studies showed that TRL margination depends on LPL bound to GPIHBP1."]
- Rat adipose tissue: [PMID:12551943 "Lipoprotein lipase (LPL) acts at the vascular endothelium."]; heparin "detaches the enzyme from the endothelial surface"; "the nutritional regulation of LPL in adipose tissue determines the activity state of extracellular LPL."
- Rat heart: [PMID:18722549 "Interestingly, LPL is not synthesised in the endothelium but is derived from the cardiomyocyte itself [4] and must be translocated across the interstitial space to be re-expressed on the luminal surface of the capillary endothelium [5]."]

## Rat experimental studies (key)

- PMID:18722549 (Cheng & Hauton 2008, full text): in isolated perfused hearts of cold-acclimated rats, "Cold acclimation significantly increased heparin-releasable LPL (P<0.05) and tissue residual LPL (P<0.01)", with 3-fold higher uptake of a triacylglycerol emulsion. LPL was assayed as triolein hydrolysis with human plasma "as a source of Apolipoprotein CII". VLDL receptor and AMPK levels did not change, so AMPK phosphorylation does not explain the increased endothelial LPL.
- PMID:11788374 (abstract): "In chow-fed rats, adipose LPL activity increased two- to fourfold postprandially, but only minimally (30%) in HSHF-fed rats." The blunted response "heightens postprandial hypertriglyceridemia through attenuated triglyceride clearance".
- PMID:21569266 (full text): "Heparin-releasable LPL, the active form of this enzyme which is placed in adipose tissue endothelium, was significantly reduced by resveratrol."
- PMID:25931001 (full text): shRNA knockdown of LPL in rat L6 myotubes increased insulin-stimulated glycogen synthesis and glucose oxidation, and purified LPL add-back reversed this: "Thus, LPL depletion appeared to mimic the action of insulin."
- PMID:11016888 (abstract): "adipose tissue lipoprotein lipase (LPL) activity was increased in masoprocol-treated rats".
- UniProt, rat-specific: "Tyrosine nitration after lipopolysaccharide (LPS) challenge down-regulates the lipase activity." (PMID:19715756); "Induced by insulin. Inhibited by isoproterenol." (PMID:2233752).
