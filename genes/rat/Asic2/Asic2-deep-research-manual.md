# Asic2 (rat, Q62962) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this review; this synthesis was written by the
reviewer from cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and the UniProt record
(Asic2-uniprot.txt). Quotes are verbatim from the cited sources.

## Identity and structure

- ASIC2 (MDEG, BNC1, BNaC1) is a member of the degenerin/epithelial Na+ channel (DEG/ENaC)
  superfamily. Two splice isoforms: ASIC2a (MDEG1, isoform 1, displayed, 512 aa) and ASIC2b
  (MDEG2, isoform 2), which differs in the N-terminal region including TM1
  [PMID:9368048 "we have cloned from mouse and rat brain a splice variant form of the MDEG1 channel which differs in the first 236 amino acids, including the first transmembrane region"].
- Topology: two TM segments, cytoplasmic N and C termini, large extracellular loop; Asn-365 and
  Asn-392 are glycosylated extracellular sites
  [PMID:15504740 "These data confirm the membrane topology prediction that the ASIC2a subtype consists of intracellular amino and carboxyl termini and two transmembrane domains connected by a large extracellular loop"].
- Proton gating depends on an aromatic pair at the extracellular/TM interface, conserved in rat
  ASIC2a [PMID:19074149 "Substitution of either one the aromatic residues by Ala or Gly in rat ASIC2a and ASIC3 also produced non-functional channels."].

## Molecular function

- ASIC2a forms homomeric proton-gated cation channels requiring more acidic pH than ASIC1
  [PMID:9368048 "We have now identified MDEG1 as a proton-gated channel with properties different from those of ASIC. MDEG1 requires more acidic pH values for activation and has slower inactivation kinetics."].
- Heteromers with ASIC3 [PMID:10842183 "The reversal potential of the ASIC2a+3 current (>/=+20 mV) reflected a cationic current mainly selective for sodium."].
- ASIC2b is not proton-gated alone but modulates heteromers
  [PMID:9368048 "MDEG2 is activated neither by mutations that bring neurodegeneration once introduced in C. elegans degenerins nor by low pH. However, it can associate both with MDEG1 and another recently cloned H+-activated channel DRASIC to form heteropolymers"].
- ASIC2b/ASIC3 heteromers are upregulated by PKC via PICK-1 binding to the ASIC2b C terminus
  [PMID:14976185 "This regulation by PKC requires PICK-1 (protein interacting with C kinase), a PDZ domain-containing protein, which interacts with the ASIC2b C terminus."].
- ASIC2a/ASIC2b heteromer in rat taste cells is a low-pH-activated channel whose amiloride block
  is lost at strongly acidic pH [PMID:12736332 "These results imply that the heteromer functions as an amiloride-insensitive proton-activated cation channel under strongly acidic conditions."].

## Cellular location

- Plasma membrane (UniProt, PMID:10842183, PMID:15504740). In mouse brain ASIC2a is in dendrites,
  dendritic spines and synaptosomes, through PSD-95 binding
  [PMID:19571134 "We identified ASIC2a in dendrites, dendritic spines, and brain synaptosomes. This localization largely relied on ASIC2a binding to PSD-95"].

## Physiological roles (mostly mouse knockout data, source of RGD ISO rows)

- Synaptic targeting of ASIC1a [PMID:19571134 "ASIC2 increased ASIC1a localization in dendritic spines"].
- Baroreceptor reflex [PMID:20064394 "The results define ASIC2 as an important determinant of autonomic circulatory control and of baroreceptor sensitivity."].
- Touch (low-threshold rapidly adapting mechanoreceptors) [PMID:11069180 "disrupting the mouse BNC1 gene markedly reduces the sensitivity of a specific component of mechanosensation: low-threshold rapidly adapting mechanoreceptors"].
- Myogenic constriction of cerebral arteries [PMID:18296560 "normal ASIC2 expression is required for normal pressure-induced constriction in the MCA"].
- Retina: negative modulator of rod phototransduction, protection from light damage
  [PMID:14762118 "We suggest that ASIC2 is a negative modulator of rod phototransduction"].
- Cochlea: no hearing loss in null mice [PMID:15537887 "ASIC2 null mice showed no significant hearing loss, indicating that the ASIC2 was not directly involved in the mechanotransduction of the mammalian cochlea"].
- DRG: heteromultimers [PMID:11854527 "combinations of two or more DEG/ENaC subunits coassemble as heteromultimers to generate transient H(+)-gated currents in mouse DRG neurons"].
