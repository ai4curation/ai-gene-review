CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms pH-gated trimeric sodium channels that act as
CC       postsynaptic excitatory sensors in the nervous system (PubMed:10842183,
CC       PubMed:22850675, PubMed:8631835, PubMed:9360943, PubMed:9368048). Upon
CC       extracellular acidification, these channels generate rapid, transient
CC       inward currents that fully desensitize (PubMed:10842183,
CC       PubMed:8631835, PubMed:9368048). Highly selective for sodium, they are
CC       permeable to other cations (PubMed:8631835). By forming heterotrimeric
CC       channels with ASIC1, could contribute to synaptic plasticity, learning,
CC       and memory. Additionally, as acid sensors at nerve terminals, plays a
CC       role in mechanosensation and phototransduction (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q925H0, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10842183,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22850675, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8631835,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9360943, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9368048}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 2]: Has no pH-gated sodium channel activity per se
CC       but can associate with other ASICs to produce functional channels with
CC       specific properties. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9368048}.

CC       functional heterotrimers producing channel with different properties
CC       (PubMed:10842183). Forms heterotrimers with ASIC1; while ASIC1
CC       determines current amplitude, ASIC2 influences the properties of the
CC       current (By similarity). Forms heterotrimers with ASIC3; resulting in
CC       channels with distinct properties (PubMed:10842183, PubMed:9360943).
CC       Interacts with STOM; STOM regulates the gating of ASIC2-containing

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in sciatic nerve and dorsal root ganglion
CC       (DRG) (at protein level). Both isoforms display the same expression
CC       pattern except in DRG where isoform 2 is more abundantly expressed.
CC       Widely distributed throughout the brain. Highly expressed in the main
CC       olfactory bulb, neo- and allo-cortical regions, hippocampal formation,
CC       habenula, basolateral amygdaloid nuclei, and cerebellum. In the
CC       olfactory system, expressed in the glomerular cell layer, the internal
CC       granular layer, and the mitral and internal plexiform cell layers.
CC       Within the glomerular layer, restricted to the periglomerular cells. In
CC       the neocortex, strongly expressed in the large pyramidal neurons in all
CC       cortical layers as well as in the oligo-, astro-, or micro-glia cells.
CC       In the hippocampal formation, expressed in dentate granule cells and
CC       hilar neurons, as well as in pyramidal cells of CA1-CA3 subfields.
CC       Expressed in stratum oriens and radiatum of all subfields. Within the
CC       thalamus, expressed moderately in the medial and lateral habenula. In
CC       the cerebellar cortex expressed in Purkinje cells and granule cells.
CC       Expressed at low levels in choroid plexus.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11842212, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8631835,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9360943, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9368048}.
