# Klf9 (rat, Q01713) - manual literature research

No deep-research API provider was available; this summary was assembled manually from
PubMed searches and cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md). Provenance is inline.

## Identity and structure

- Rat Klf9 (BTEB, BTEB1; 244 aa) was cloned from a rat liver cDNA library as a protein binding
  the basic transcription element (BTE), a GC box in the CYP1A1 promoter
  [PMID:1356762 "have been isolated from a rat liver cDNA library by using the BTE sequence as a binding probe"].
- Three C-terminal C2H2 zinc fingers (UniProt FT ZN_FING 143-167, 173-197, 203-225; PROSITE PS50157);
  Sp1 C2H2-type zinc-finger family [UniProt Q01713 "Belongs to the Sp1 C2H2-type zinc-finger protein family."].
- N-terminal alpha-helical Sin3-interacting domain (SID, aa 2-25) shared with KLF10/11/13/14
  [PMID:11438660 "we showed that an α-helical SID similar to the TIEG α-HRM domains is conserved in the N termini of BTEB1, BTEB3, and BTEB4."].

## Molecular function

- Context-dependent activator/repressor of RNA polymerase II promoters. Rat BTEB activated
  promoters with repeated GC boxes (SV40 early, HIV-1 LTR) but repressed the single-GC-box
  CYP1A1 BTE promoter in CV-1 cells [PMID:1356762 "BTEB repressed the activity of a promoter containing BTE, a single GC box of the CYP1A1 gene that is stimulated by Sp1"].
- The BTEB1 SID binds mSin3A and represses transcription when fused to GAL4
  [PMID:11438660 "The alpha-HRM from these proteins also mediates direct interaction with mSin3A and represses transcription."].
- Interacts functionally with progesterone receptor isoforms and coactivates PR-B transactivation
  [PMID:12672823 "Functional interaction with ligand-activated PR-B resulted in superactivation of PR-B transactivity"];
  ChIP shows KLF9 recruited to the SLPI promoter GC region in human endometrial cells [PMID:16384861].

## Biological roles (mostly from mouse and human orthologs)

- Uterus/implantation: Klf9-null female mice are subfertile with fewer implantation sites and
  partial progesterone resistance [PMID:15117941 "ablation of the Bteb1 gene in female mice results in uterine hypoplasia, reduced litter size"; PMID:15917344].
- Nervous system: rat BTEB is a thyroid hormone (T3)-induced gene in developing rat brain; overexpression
  increases neurite number and length [PMID:10438482 "Overexpression of BTEB in Neuro-2a cells dramatically increased the number and length of neurites"];
  antisense knockdown in rat embryonic neurons abolishes T3-induced neurite branching [PMID:12021188].
  Mouse knockout: viable, deficits in rotorod and fear conditioning [PMID:12640131]. KLF9 promotes
  oligodendrocyte differentiation in vitro (rat OPCs) and remyelination [PMID:22472204].
- Epidermis: human KLF9 is a circadian, cortisol-induced transcription factor with antiproliferative
  effects in keratinocytes [PMID:22711835 "Gain- and loss-of-function experiments showed strong antiproliferative effects of Klf9."].
- Cardiac myocytes: endothelin-1 modestly (<2-fold) and in a delayed manner increases Klf9 mRNA in
  neonatal rat cardiomyocytes [PMID:18406357 "The increases in expression of Klf9 and Klf10 were relatively small (maximal expression < 2-fold at 1.5–2 h) although levels remained elevated over 4–6 h"].

## Summary

Klf9 is a nuclear, GC-box/CACCC-binding Krüppel-like zinc-finger transcription factor that acts as a
promoter-context-dependent activator or Sin3A-dependent repressor of RNA polymerase II genes, and is
itself a hormone-responsive (T3, glucocorticoid, progesterone) gene. Physiological roles (uterine
receptivity, neurite branching, keratinocyte proliferation) are established largely by perturbation in
mouse/human systems and are downstream of its transcription-factor activity.
