# Syt3 (rat, P40748) — manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this session; this synthesis was written by the
reviewer from cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and PubMed searches.

## Identity and architecture
- Synaptotagmin-3, 588 aa, single-pass membrane protein with a short N-terminal intravesicular/extracellular
  segment carrying a cysteine motif, a TM helix (55-75), a linker and tandem C2 domains (C2A 297-418, C2B 429-563)
  [UniProtKB:P40748 "Belongs to the synaptotagmin family"].
- Homodimerizes through disulfides in the cysteine motif and heterodimerizes with SYT6, SYT9, SYT10
  [UniProtKB:P40748 "Homodimer; disulfide-linked via the cysteine motif"]. Ca2+ promotes C2B-mediated
  hetero-oligomerization with Syt1 [PMID:9830048 "Ca2+ promoted the hetero-oligomerization of synaptotagmin I with synaptotagmins III and IV"].

## Biochemistry: a high-affinity Ca2+ sensor
- Binds 3 Ca2+ per subunit on the C2 domains [UniProtKB:P40748 "Binds 3 Ca(2+) ions per subunit"].
- C2A binds phospholipids Ca2+-dependently [PMID:7791877 "The first C2 domains of Syt I, II, III, V and VII, but not of IV, VI or VIII, bind phospholipids"];
  Syt3 has one of the highest apparent Ca2+ affinities in the family
  [PMID:11823420 "the plasma membrane synaptotagmins 3 and 7 exhibited the highest Ca 2+ affinities"].
- Ca2+-promoted binding to syntaxin-1A/SNAP-25 t-SNARE heterodimers [PMID:12860971 "Isoforms III, V, VII, and X exhibited robust Ca2+-promoted binding activity"];
  binds PS-containing liposomes and PIP2 [PMID:12860971].
- Cytoplasmic domain of Syt3 stimulates SNARE-catalysed liposome fusion in response to Ca2+
  [PMID:18508778 "only the stimulatory syts I-III, V-VII, and IX-X bound t-SNAREs and PS in a Ca2+-promoted manner"].
- Binds clathrin-AP2 and syntaxins via the cytoplasmic region [PMID:7791877].

## Localization
- Enriched in synaptic plasma membranes, not synaptic vesicles [PMID:10373432 "synaptotagmin III is highly enriched in synaptic plasma membranes but not in synaptic vesicles"].
- Plasma membrane in PC12 cells [PMID:11823420].
- Postsynaptic endocytic zones [PMID:30545844]; multivesicular bodies in T cells [PMID:17179206 "mainly localized in multivesicular bodies"].

## Physiological roles
- Postsynaptic: Ca2+-sensing by Syt3 drives AMPA receptor (GluA2) internalization, LTD, LTP decay and forgetting
  [PMID:30545844 "Syt3 localizes to postsynaptic endocytic zones and removes AMPA receptors"]. Syt3-GluA2 binding also implicated in ischemic damage [PMID:36892998].
- Presynaptic: high-affinity Ca2+ sensor for synaptic vesicle replenishment and short-term plasticity
  [PMID:36261524 "Here we identify synaptotagmin-3 (SYT3)3,4 as that presynaptic high-affinity Ca2+ sensor"].
- Endocrine/PC12: proposed plasma-membrane Ca2+ sensor for exocytosis (C2A interference) [PMID:11823420].
- T cells: CXCR4 recycling [PMID:17179206].
- Melanocyte dendrite claim (human SYT3, UniProt IDA from PMID:23999003) rests on a C2-domain overexpression screen focused on SYT14L.
