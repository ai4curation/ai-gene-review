# Ace (rat, UniProt P47820) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was run for this gene. This file is a manual synthesis
written by the reviewer from the cached publications in `publications/` and the
UniProt record `Ace-uniprot.txt`. Every claim carries its provenance.

## Identity and structure

- Rat Ace encodes somatic angiotensin I-converting enzyme (ACE, kininase II,
  EC 3.4.15.1), a 1,313-residue type I membrane ectoenzyme
  [PMID:8292044 "Both cDNAs encode a single polypeptide of 1,313 amino acid residues"].
- The somatic enzyme has two homologous zinc metallopeptidase (peptidase M2)
  domains, each with its own active site
  [UniProtKB:P47820 "Composed of two similar catalytic domains, each possessing a functional active site, with different selectivity for substrates"];
  [UniProtKB:P47820 "Belongs to the peptidase M2 family."].
- Topology: signal peptide 1-35, extracellular 36-1265, single TM helix
  1266-1282, short cytoplasmic tail [UniProtKB:P47820 FT TOPO_DOM/TRANSMEM].
- A soluble (plasma) form is generated by juxtamembrane shedding
  [UniProtKB:P47820 "Soluble form that is released in blood plasma and other body fluids following proteolytic cleavage in the juxtamembrane stalk region."].
- A testis-specific isoform (germinal ACE, ACE-T), produced from an alternative
  intragenic promoter, contains only the C-domain and is needed for male fertility
  [UniProtKB:P47820 "Isoform produced by alternative promoter usage that is specifically expressed in spermatocytes and adult testis, and which is required for male fertility."].
  In rat it is on the sperm midpiece and is completely shed when sperm enter the
  epididymis [PMID:12444051 "this enzyme is immunolocalized mainly on the middle piece of rat and mouse testicular sperm"].
- Cofactors: zinc (two per somatic subunit) and chloride activation
  [UniProtKB:P47820 "Note=Binds 2 Zn(2+) ions per subunit."];
  [UniProtKB:P47820 "The dipeptidyl carboxypeptidase activity is strongly activated by chloride."].

## Molecular function

- Peptidyl-dipeptidase (dipeptidyl carboxypeptidase): releases C-terminal
  dipeptides from oligopeptides
  [UniProtKB:P47820 "Dipeptidyl carboxypeptidase that removes dipeptides from the C-terminus of a variety of circulating hormones, such as angiotensin I, bradykinin or enkephalins"].
- Rat enzyme, direct biochemistry:
  - Rat CSF peptidyl dipeptidase converts Ang I to Ang II and degrades bradykinin
    sequentially [PMID:2172462 "which sequentially degraded bradykinin (BK) by liberating the carboxy-terminal dipeptides and converted angiotensin I to angiotensin II"].
  - Rat striatal synaptic membranes: ACE converts Met-enkephalin-Arg6-Phe7 to
    Met-enkephalin [PMID:20487892 "indicating that the cleavage of the heptapeptide was mediated by the angiotensin converting enzyme"].
  - ACE is the dominant kininase in rat plasma
    [PMID:11299220 "The enzymes ACE, APP, and CPN represented 92% of this kininase activity, with relative contributions of 52, 25, and 16%, respectively."].
  - Rat intestinal brush-border ACE hydrolyses C-terminal prolyl peptides and acts
    as a digestive peptidase [PMID:2827504 "These results show that intestinal brush-border membrane ACE functions as a digestive peptidase"].
  - Hemopressin and related hemoglobin peptides interact with ACE
    [PMID:12500972 "shown to interact with ep24.15, ep24.16, and ACE"].
  - Tissue ACE in rat organs hydrolyses Hip-His-Leu and the N-domain-selective
    substrate Abz-SDK(Dnp)P [PMID:16148611 "captopril 3 micromol/l inhibited the enzymic activity"].
- Substrates by similarity to human/mouse ACE (UniProt): substance P,
  neurotensin, AcSDKP (N-domain), amyloid-beta (N-domain), GnRH, cholecystokinin
  [UniProtKB:P47820 "Degradation of hemoregulatory peptide N-acetyl-SDKP (AcSDKP) and amyloid-beta proteins is mediated by the N-terminal catalytic domain, while angiotensin I and cholecystokinin cleavage is mediated by the C-terminal catalytic region"].

## Physiology (rat)

- Blood pressure: Ace lies at the Bp1 hypertension QTL of SHRSP
  [PMID:1655275 "is closely linked to the rat gene encoding angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE)"],
  although no functional coding difference was found between SHRSP and WKY
  [PMID:8292044 "the enzymatic properties of partially purified ACE from the two strains were similar"].
  Antisense knock-down of Ace lowered blood pressure in SHR
  [PMID:10506486 "This was associated with a long-term reduction in blood pressure by approximately 15 mm Hg, exclusively in the spontaneously hypertensive rat."].
  Ace is the most likely chromosome-10 modifier of malignant hypertension and
  end-organ damage in Ren2 transgenic rats; serum Ace activity and protein track
  the QTL [PMID:19307186 "Serum Ace activity (Fig. 6a) and Western blot analysis (Fig. 6b) revealed that both Ace activity and Ace protein are lower in noninduced Ren2.L than Ren2.F."].
  The authors propose that reduced Ace, through AcSDKP accumulation, mitigates
  damage [PMID:19307186 "We hypothesize that reduced efficiency of Ac-SDKP cleavage caused by reduced levels of Ace per se may ameliorate end organ damage"].
- Ang-(1-7) is a further ACE substrate in rat vasculature
  [PMID:10642323 "The release of Ang-(1-7) is significantly influenced by inhibition of ACE, which may reflect both increased substrate (Ang I) levels and reduced metabolism of the peptide."].
- Expression is regulated developmentally and by many stimuli (thyroid hormone,
  glucocorticoids, aldosterone, shear stress, glucose, hypoxia, ACE inhibitors);
  these are regulatory inputs to Ace, not processes Ace performs
  [PMID:7977726], [PMID:1659346], [PMID:11447075], [PMID:9048650], [PMID:17626897], [PMID:14527966], [PMID:9495881].
- Many rat GO annotations derive from ACE-inhibitor pharmacology (captopril,
  enalapril, perindopril, ramipril) with downstream readouts (apoptosis, LPS
  response, neurogenesis, feeding, angiogenesis, organ regeneration, fetal growth).
  These document indirect consequences of Ang II/kinin balance rather than
  processes executed by ACE.

## Localization

- Cell surface ectoenzyme: endothelium, renal and intestinal brush border
  [PMID:2827504 "Enzyme activity was associated with the brush-border membrane fraction"],
  ciliary epithelium basal membrane and nearby vesicles
  [PMID:2841539 "ACE was found on the basal plasma membrane of the internal epithelium facing the aqueous humor and on vesicles in its vicinity"].
- Soluble ACE in serum/plasma and CSF [PMID:19307186], [PMID:2172462].
