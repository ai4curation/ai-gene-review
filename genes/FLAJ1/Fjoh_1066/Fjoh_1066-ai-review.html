Gene Description

Fjoh_1066 encodes LolB1, a structurally divergent LolB-like lipoprotein in Flavobacterium johnsoniae UW101. Native deletion and complementation implicate it in delivery of a subset of lipoproteins to the outer membrane, including clients needed for gliding and type IX secretion. Its predicted LolB fold, lipoprotein signal and functional loop support an inferred outer-membrane insertion role. These studies establish cellular trafficking function rather than a purified-protein insertion reaction. Other paralogs and retained lipoprotein localization in strains lacking known LolA/LolB proteins show that LolB1 is neither universally essential nor the only delivery route.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

outer-membrane lipoprotein insertion activity

Definition: Directly binding a mature lipoprotein and promoting incorporation of its lipid anchor into the bacterial outer membrane.

Justification: GO:0140318 provides the protein-binding and delivery genus. GO:0140598 requires movement with cargo between locations, whereas GO:0140306 specifies release from the inner membrane; neither describes terminal lipid-anchor insertion. GO:0032977 membrane insertase activity requires a transmembrane-domain-containing protein client, unlike the lipid-anchored lipoprotein substrate here. The proposed definition does not require a separate soluble donor because a bifunctional carrier can also perform the terminal step. Gene-specific support and assay limitations remain in the core function and supporting evidence.

Parent term: protein transporter activity

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:40050408 : GldK and GldJ, two lipoproteins essential for gliding motility and T9 secretion28 were much less abundant in the OM of the lolA1 and lolB1 mutants than in the WT strain
  • PMID:40050408 : Plasmid-borne complementation of lolA1 and lolB1 deletion with lolA1 and lolB1 fully restored gliding motility
