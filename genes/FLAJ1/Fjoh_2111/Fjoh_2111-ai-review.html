Gene Description

Fjoh_2111 (LolA1) is a LolA-family lipoprotein-trafficking protein of Flavobacterium johnsoniae UW101, predicted to act as a soluble periplasmic carrier. It supports delivery of a subset of lipoproteins to the outer membrane, including components needed for gliding motility and type IX secretion. Native loss of LolA1 reduces the outer-membrane abundance of GldJ and GldK and disrupts these downstream systems. Other lipoproteins retain localization without the known LolA/LolB proteins, indicating alternative trafficking routes. LolA1 has distinct functional requirements from its pigment-locus paralog LolA2 and is not freely exchangeable with the E. coli carrier under the tested conditions.
