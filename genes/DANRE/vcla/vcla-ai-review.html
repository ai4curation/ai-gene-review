Gene Description

vcla (vinculin a) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate vinculin (VCL), retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication; its paralog is vclb. It encodes a cytoplasmic actin-binding adaptor of cell-matrix (focal adhesion) and cadherin-based cell-cell (adherens) junctions. Its head domain binds talin and alpha-catenin, and its tail binds F-actin and paxillin; tension-dependent recruitment through alpha-catenin and talin reinforces the link between adhesion complexes and the actin cytoskeleton. The vcla protein keeps the binding residues and regulatory phosphosites of mammalian vinculin, including Y822, and the gene can encode the muscle metavinculin isoform. GFP-tagged Vcla localizes to focal adhesions and, via the alpha-catenin vinculin-binding site, to adherens junctions. Maternal-zygotic vcla mutants are viable and fertile without overt defects. Loss of vcla together with vclb causes mild transient cardiac edema, leakage of small molecules from embryonic blood vessels and lethality before adulthood. vcla mutant alleles whose transcripts undergo nonsense-mediated decay upregulate vclb mRNA (transcriptional adaptation), whereas a vcla locus deletion does not.
