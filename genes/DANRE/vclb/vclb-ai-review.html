Gene Description

vclb (vinculin b) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate vinculin (VCL), retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication; its paralog is vcla. It encodes a cytoplasmic actin-binding adaptor of focal adhesions and adherens junctions that binds talin and alpha-catenin through its head domain and F-actin and paxillin through its tail. The protein conserves the binding residues of mammalian vinculin but carries phenylalanine at the position of the regulatory tyrosine Y822; GFP-tagged Vclb still localizes to focal adhesions and, via the alpha-catenin vinculin-binding site, to adherens junctions. vclb is the predominant vinculin copy in the embryonic heart, including epicardium and endocardium, and is the copy most prominently expressed in endothelial cells, where it marks focal adhesions and cell-cell junctions. vclb mutants develop epicardial hyperplasia, disorganized coronary vessels with elevated FAK and ERK phosphorylation, and die as juveniles. Combined loss with vcla causes leakage of small molecules from embryonic vessels. vclb mRNA is upregulated when vcla mutant transcripts undergo nonsense-mediated decay (transcriptional adaptation).
