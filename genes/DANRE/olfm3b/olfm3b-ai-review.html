Gene Description

olfm3b (olfactomedin 3b) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate OLFM3 (optimedin, noelin-3), retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication; its paralog is olfm3a. It encodes a secreted glycoprotein with a signal peptide, an N-terminal coiled-coil region and a C-terminal olfactomedin (beta-propeller) domain, and belongs to the noelin subfamily with OLFM1 and OLFM2. In mammals OLFM3 is expressed in retina, brain and the anterior eye, forms homo- and hetero-oligomers with other noelins and binds myocilin, and the noelins together are extracellular constituents of AMPA-type glutamate receptor complexes that anchor the receptors at synapses. The zebrafish protein keeps the coiled coil, the olfactomedin domain and the cysteines of human OLFM3. olfm3b is expressed at low levels throughout embryonic and larval development; public RNA-seq detects it in adult brain, intestine and ovary. No functional study of this gene has been published.

Core Functions

Secreted noelin-family olfactomedin glycoprotein; protein features are shared with olfm3a, but olfm3b is weakly expressed during development (adult brain, intestine and ovary calls). No molecular activity or process has been measured for this copy.
