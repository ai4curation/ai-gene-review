Gene Description

tmc2b (transmembrane channel-like 2b) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate TMC2, the other being tmc2a; it lies on chromosome 5 about 5 kb from tmc1. Tmc2b is a multi-pass membrane protein of the TMC family and a pore-forming subunit of the hair-cell mechanoelectrical transduction (MET) channel, a non-selective, calcium-permeable cation channel opened by tip-link tension. GFP-tagged Tmc2b localizes to the tips of stereocilia, and its delivery to the hair bundle requires Tomt and Tmie. tmc2b is expressed only in sensory hair cells: it is the dominant Tmc transcript in lateral-line neuromasts and, in the inner ear, is expressed with tmc1 in peripheral (extrastriolar) hair cells. Tmc2b is required for mechanotransduction in most lateral-line hair cells, with Tmc2a supplying the rest, and is the main contributor to utricular (vestibular) function at low frequencies; in the saccule it contributes to hearing behind Tmc2a. A hair-cell Tmc2b transgene restores transduction, hearing and balance in larvae lacking tmc1, tmc2a and tmc2b.

Core Functions

Pore-forming subunit of the hair-cell mechanoelectrical transduction channel at stereocilia tips; the main Tmc subunit of lateral-line and extrastriolar inner-ear hair cells. Recorded as a contribution because the channel also needs other subunits (Tmie, Lhfpl5, Cib2/3).

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Can Tmc2a substitute for Tmc2b in the lateral-line hair cells that depend absolutely on Tmc2b (e.g. anterior-facing cells of posterior neuromasts) if it is expressed there? This would decide whether the pair split by expression only.

Q: What sets the Tmc2b-independent residual transduction in neuromasts IO4, SO1 and SO3, given that tmc2a is barely detectable in the lateral line by in situ hybridization?

Q: Is the reduced neuromast hair-cell number in tmc2b mutants a general consequence of lost transduction, or a Tmc2b-specific role in hair-cell maintenance?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express Tmc2a under the tmc2b regulatory region (or a lateral-line-specific driver) in tmc2b mutants and score dye uptake and microphonics in posterior neuromasts.

Hypothesis: Tmc2a and Tmc2b proteins are interchangeable and the pair differs only in expression.

Type: cross-rescue

Experiment: Compare MET current kinetics and adaptation in extrastriolar hair cells of tmc1/tmc2a double mutants (Tmc2b only) and striolar cells of tmc1/tmc2b double mutants (Tmc2a only).

Hypothesis: The utricular low-frequency role of Tmc2b reflects its extrastriolar expression rather than channel properties.

Type: electrophysiology
