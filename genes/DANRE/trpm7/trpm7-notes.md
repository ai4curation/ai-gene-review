# trpm7 review notes

Zebrafish trpm7 (UniProt Q563W7, TrEMBL/Unreviewed, 1774 aa) encodes TRPM7, a divalent-cation (Mg2+/Ca2+)-permeable channel fused to a C-terminal alpha-kinase ("chanzyme"). It is the touchtone/nutria/sweetbread/touchdown mutant locus in zebrafish. Core molecular functions are divalent-cation channel activity and alpha-kinase activity; downstream phenotypes span pigmentation, skeleton, kidney, pancreas, heart rate and touch/behavior.
