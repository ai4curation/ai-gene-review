CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription repressor. Component of the regulatory cascade
CC       that controls the development of dopaminergic (DA) and serotonergic
CC       (5HT) neurons. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12469125}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11180958}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: No expression seen in DA or 5HT neurons.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12469125}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in the most anterior part of the
CC       prospective neural plate along the rostral-caudal axis. At the tail bud
CC       stage, expressed in the prospective forebrain region. From 15-somite to
CC       24 hours post-fertilization (hpf), expression is mostly confined to the
CC       telencephalon and the anterior-ventral region of diencephalon
CC       Expression is restricted to ectoderm at 80% epiboly stage. From 15-
CC       somite to 24 hpf, expression is mostly confined to the telencephalon
CC       and the anterior-ventral region of diencephalon.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11025224, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11180958}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: In one-eyed pinhead (oep) and cyclops (cyc) mutants,
CC       expression in the ventral forebrain was eliminated. Overexpression
CC       induced ectopic dlx2 and dlx6 expression. The tof mutant has a reduced
CC       number of tyrosine hydroxylase-positive and dopamine beta-hydroxylase-
CC       negative DA neurons.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the krueppel C2H2-type zinc-finger protein
CC       family. {ECO:0000305}.
