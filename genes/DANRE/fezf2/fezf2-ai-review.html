Gene Description

Zebrafish fezf2 (Fez family zinc finger protein 2; historically fezl, and the gene disrupted in the too few (tof) mutant) encodes a forebrain-specific C2H2 zinc-finger transcription factor with six C-terminal zinc fingers and an N-terminal eh1-related repressor motif. It is one of the earliest markers of the prospective forebrain and is expressed in the telencephalon and anterior-ventral diencephalon during embryogenesis, and later in dorsal telencephalic adult neural stem cells. The protein binds DNA sequence-specifically (SELEX-derived core motif CAGCAACC), occupies genomic sites near eomesa/tbr2 and lhx2b in vivo, and activates transcription through those response elements (PMID:21471212); it also carries an eh1 repressor motif required for its in vivo function and was originally characterized as a transcriptional repressor (PMID:12469125). Genetically, fezf2 is required for regional patterning of the diencephalon (prethalamus versus zona limitans intrathalamica, via downregulation of wnt expression; PMID:17164418), for specification of hypothalamic neurons upstream of otp (PMID:18003738; PMID:23533176), and for the correct number of forebrain dopaminergic and serotonergic neurons, which it controls non-cell-autonomously downstream of canonical Wnt signalling (PMID:12469125; PMID:15219677; PMID:18799544). In adults, heterogeneous fezf2 levels among dorsal telencephalic neural stem cells pattern a Notch activity gradient that maintains stem cell quiescence (PMID:25319688).

Core Functions

Forebrain-specific C2H2 zinc-finger transcription factor. Binds a defined DNA motif through its six C-terminal zinc fingers and regulates target genes such as eomesa and lhx2b (PMID:21471212); carries an eh1 repressor motif required for in vivo function (PMID:12469125). Through this activity it establishes diencephalic subdivisions by repressing wnt expression (PMID:17164418), specifies hypothalamic neurons upstream of otp (PMID:18003738; PMID:23533176), and sets the number of forebrain dopaminergic and serotonergic neurons downstream of canonical Wnt signalling (PMID:12469125; PMID:15219677; PMID:18799544).

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is fezf2 primarily a repressor (eh1 motif, PMID:12469125; UniProt FUNCTION) or an activator (eomesa, lhx2b; PMID:21471212), and does the mode depend on cofactor context or target site?

Q: What are the direct zebrafish targets through which fezf2 represses wnt expression in the prospective diencephalon (PMID:17164418) and regulates Notch activity in adult neural stem cells (PMID:25319688)? Mouse Fezf2 represses Hes5 directly; is the same true in zebrafish?

Q: The commissural axon guidance phenotype (PMID:21471212) is transient and attributed to reduced lhx2b. Is the defect mediated entirely through lhx2b, or do other direct Fezf2 targets participate in forebrain midline guidance?

description: >-
  Zebrafish fezf2 (Fez family zinc finger protein 2; historically fezl, and the gene
  disrupted in the too few (tof) mutant) encodes a forebrain-specific C2H2 zinc-finger
  transcription factor with six C-terminal zinc fingers and an N-terminal eh1-related
  repressor motif. It is one of the earliest markers of the prospective forebrain and is
  expressed in the telencephalon and anterior-ventral diencephalon during embryogenesis,
  and later in dorsal telencephalic adult neural stem cells. The protein binds DNA
  sequence-specifically (SELEX-derived core motif CAGCAACC), occupies genomic sites near
  eomesa/tbr2 and lhx2b in vivo, and activates transcription through those response
  elements (PMID:21471212); it also carries an eh1 repressor motif required for its
  in vivo function and was originally characterized as a transcriptional repressor
  (PMID:12469125). Genetically, fezf2 is required for regional patterning of the
  diencephalon (prethalamus versus zona limitans intrathalamica, via downregulation of
  wnt expression; PMID:17164418), for specification of hypothalamic neurons upstream of
  otp (PMID:18003738; PMID:23533176), and for the correct number of forebrain
  dopaminergic and serotonergic neurons, which it controls non-cell-autonomously
  downstream of canonical Wnt signalling (PMID:12469125; PMID:15219677; PMID:18799544).
  In adults, heterogeneous fezf2 levels among dorsal telencephalic neural stem cells
  pattern a Notch activity gradient that maintains stem cell quiescence (PMID:25319688).
