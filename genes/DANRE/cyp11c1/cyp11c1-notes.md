# Zebrafish cyp11c1 evidence notes

Identity: UniProt A1XRK1 (518 amino acids) is the original cloned cyp11c1 record, linked to DQ650710/ABG35521.1, RefSeq NM_001080204.1/NP_001073673.1, GeneID 791124, and ZFIN ZDB-GENE-070828-1. PMID:17393497 describes the original cloning/expression work. Historical cyp11b, cyp11b1 and cyp11b2 aliases refer to this fish gene and do not establish mammalian paralog identity. Current additional UniProt predicted records/isoforms were not substituted for this exact accession. PANTHER PTHR24279:SF1 officially reads CYTOCHROME P450 11B2, MITOCHONDRIAL; that family label does not establish aldosterone synthesis in zebrafish.

PMID:24167225 (full text available) uses the historical name cyp11b1: knockdown and metyrapone lower cortisol; the paper explicitly distinguishes zebrafish, which do not synthesize aldosterone. Independent cyp11c1 knockout studies PMID:32222764 and PMID:32805708 (abstract-only caches) establish cortisol and 11-ketotestosterone deficiency and reproductive phenotypes. The enzyme supplies 11-hydroxyandrogen precursors; downstream oxidation to 11-ketotestosterone requires other enzymes and is not assigned as Cyp11c1 chemistry. Rescue and androgen measurements support the physiological pathway without proving every purified-substrate reaction for this exact isoform.

PMID:26650568 (full text) establishes Fdx1b-dependent glucocorticoid synthesis: pregnenolone rescues 11-deoxycortisol but not cortisol under Fdx1b depletion. This supports redox dependence of the final hydroxylation, not a direct physical binding or membrane-localization assay for Cyp11c1.

All nine seeded rows were judged. Broad oxidoreductase/monooxygenase functions refine to 11beta-monooxygenase activity; cortisol metabolism refines to cortisol biosynthesis. Heme/iron coordination has independent PIRSR active-site support in the UniProt feature record. The mitochondrial-membrane ARBA annotation remains UNDECIDED because the independent source inspection did not establish target-specific membrane localization; the core omits an unsupported location. Male gonad development and mating phenotypes are retained as non-core consequences of steroid deficiency, without claiming an obligatory role in initial sex determination. No NEW process rows were added.
