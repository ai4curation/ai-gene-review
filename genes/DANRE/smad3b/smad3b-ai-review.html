Gene Description

smad3b (SMAD family member 3b) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate SMAD3, retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication; its paralog is smad3a. It encodes a receptor-regulated SMAD with an MH1 DNA-binding domain, a linker and an MH2 domain ending in the receptor-phosphorylated SSVS motif. It mediates TGF-beta/activin/nodal signals by forming complexes with SMAD4 that regulate transcription. smad3b is the faster-evolving copy: it differs from human SMAD3 mainly in the linker and carries asparagine at the position of the CK1 phosphosite S418, while keeping the Zn-binding, DNA-binding and receptor-phosphorylation residues. It is maternally supplied and zygotically expressed at the gastrula margin, tail bud, lateral mesoderm, brain regions, retina, spinal cord, somites and heart; unlike smad3a it is not expressed with a circadian rhythm. Overexpressed smad3b induces mesoderm and organizer genes such as goosecoid, and constitutively active Smad3b increases cardiomyocyte proliferation and activates Smad3 target genes. Knockdown of smad3b, like smad3a, alters neural differentiation. Combined loss of smad3a and smad3b enlarges the ventral aorta but is compatible with adult survival.
