# mib1 (Danio rerio, Q804S5, Mind bomb) — curation notes

- Swiss-Prot Q804S5, E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase mib1 (EC 2.3.2.27), 1030 aa; MZM/ZZ N-terminal
  ligand-binding region, ankyrin repeats, three RING fingers.
- E3 activity: [PMID:17196985 "a C-terminal-most RING finger-dependent E3 ubiquitin ligase
  activity"]; Delta binding/ubiquitination/endocytosis: [PMID:15013799 "Mind bomb protein displays
  E3 ubiquitin ligase activity in vitro and that it is associated with Delta and enhances its
  ubiquitination and internalization in transfected cells"].
- Trans-activation vs cis-inhibition: [PMID:26020642 "While Mib1-mediated endocytosis of DeltaD is
  essential for effective activation of Notch in a neighboring cell (in trans) it is not required
  for DeltaD to inhibit function of Notch receptors in the same cell (in cis)."]
- Phenotype: [PMID:9806913 "diagnostic of a failure of Delta-Notch signalling"]; also Jagged2-
  dependent pronephric patterning is mib-dependent (PMID:17287248).
- Non-Notch substrates: [PMID:24714733 "Both Mib and Mib2 can ubiquitylate Trabid and Fih-1"].
- IBA nodes: PTN001197468 (ubiquitin protein ligase activity, cytoplasm, protein ubiquitination);
  PTN008608909 (endocytosis, Notch signaling pathway).
