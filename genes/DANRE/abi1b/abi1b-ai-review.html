Gene Description

abi1b (abl-interactor 1b) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate ABI1, retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication; its paralog is abi1a. It encodes an ABI-family adaptor with an N-terminal WAVE-binding and coiled-coil region, a central proline-rich disordered region and a C-terminal SH3 domain. In mammals ABI1 is a core subunit of the WAVE regulatory complex (SCAR complex), which it assembles and activates to drive Arp2/3-dependent actin polymerization in lamellipodia, and its SH3 domain binds Abl kinases and other signalling partners. The zebrafish protein conserves the WAVE-binding N-terminus, the coiled-coil domain and the SH3 domain; the predicted isoform carries a short N-terminal extension. abi1b is the predominant ABI1 transcript in the maternal and cleavage-stage embryo, falls to low levels after gastrulation, and in later stages is detected mainly in brain, retina and ovary. No functional study of this copy has been published.

Core Functions

Adaptor subunit of the WAVE regulatory (SCAR) complex that couples upstream signals to Arp2/3-dependent actin polymerization at the lamellipodial leading edge; inferred from mammalian ABI1 and the conserved WAVE-binding N-terminus and SH3 domain. Molecular function shared with abi1a.

- 13-residue N-terminal extension in the predicted isoform X1 (not verified by cDNA here).
- Not significantly faster-evolving than abi1a (gar relative-rate test).

gastrulation; Bgee calls limited to early embryo, retina, brain, ovary, bone.
