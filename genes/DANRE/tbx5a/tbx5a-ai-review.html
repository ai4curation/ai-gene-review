Gene Description

T-box transcription factor TBX5-A (heartstrings), one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of human TBX5 retained from the teleost genome duplication (paralog tbx5b). A nuclear, sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factor with a T-box domain. It binds T-box elements and activates targets including tbx2b at the atrioventricular boundary, fgf24/fgf10 in the fin field and ndrg4 in the heart tube. A cytoplasmic pool is held on actin by the LIM protein Pdlim7, and its activity is repressed by Kctd10. It is expressed in the heart field and heart (both chambers; trabecular myocardium in adults), the pectoral fin field of the lateral plate mesoderm, and the dorsal retina. tbx5a is essential for pectoral fin bud initiation: it drives fgf24 and the antero-posterior convergence of fin-field cells, and loss of function abolishes the pectoral fin. It is needed for cardiac looping and chamber and AV canal patterning, for proepicardium specification, and for adult heart regeneration. Together with tbx5b it contributes to dorsoventral patterning of the retina and to heart looping. The two paralogs are not interchangeable: each is required, and injected mRNA of one does not rescue loss of the other.
