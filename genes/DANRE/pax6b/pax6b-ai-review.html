Gene Description

pax6b is one of two zebrafish co-orthologues of vertebrate PAX6, retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication. It encodes a transcription factor with a paired domain and a paired-type homeodomain that binds cis-regulatory DNA and activates target genes with the same efficiency as its paralogue pax6a. pax6b is expressed in the developing retina and lens placode, in restricted domains of the dorsal diencephalon, midbrain-hindbrain boundary and hindbrain, and, uniquely among the two copies, in pancreatic and intestinal endocrine cells. It is required for lens and anterior eye segment (cornea, iris) development, a role that depends on its homeodomain, and for the differentiation of pancreatic endocrine cells, where it promotes beta and delta cell fates and restrains ghrelin-expressing epsilon cells, a role that does not require the homeodomain. It similarly balances enteroendocrine cell subtypes in the intestine, and it initiates proliferation of Muller glia-derived neuronal progenitors during adult retinal regeneration. The homozygous-viable sunrise mutant carries a pax6b homeodomain missense allele (L244P).

description: >-
  pax6b is one of two zebrafish co-orthologues of vertebrate PAX6, retained from the teleost-specific
  genome duplication. It encodes a transcription factor with a paired domain and a paired-type homeodomain
  that binds cis-regulatory DNA and activates target genes with the same efficiency as its paralogue pax6a.
  pax6b is expressed in the developing retina and lens placode, in restricted domains of the dorsal diencephalon,
  midbrain-hindbrain boundary and hindbrain, and, uniquely among the two copies, in pancreatic and intestinal
  endocrine cells. It is required for lens and anterior eye segment (cornea, iris) development, a role
  that depends on its homeodomain, and for the differentiation of pancreatic endocrine cells, where it
  promotes beta and delta cell fates and restrains ghrelin-expressing epsilon cells, a role that does
  not require the homeodomain. It similarly balances enteroendocrine cell subtypes in the intestine, and
  it initiates proliferation of Muller glia-derived neuronal progenitors during adult retinal regeneration.
  The homozygous-viable sunrise mutant carries a pax6b homeodomain missense allele (L244P).
