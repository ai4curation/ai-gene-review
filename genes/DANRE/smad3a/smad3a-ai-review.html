Gene Description

smad3a (SMAD family member 3a) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate SMAD3, retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication; its paralog is smad3b. It encodes a receptor-regulated SMAD (R-SMAD) with an N-terminal MH1 DNA-binding domain, a linker carrying regulatory phosphosites and a C-terminal MH2 domain ending in the SSVS motif that TGF-beta/activin/nodal type I receptors phosphorylate. On activation it forms a heteromeric complex with SMAD4, enters the nucleus and binds SMAD-binding elements to regulate transcription of target genes. smad3a is the more conserved of the two copies (97% identical to human SMAD3). Its mRNA is supplied maternally and is broadly expressed, with higher levels in paraxial mesoderm and newly formed somites. Unlike smad3b, it is expressed with a circadian rhythm and is a direct target of Clock1a. With smad2 and smad3b it mediates nodal/TGF-beta signals in mesendoderm induction. Overexpressed smad3a activates the myf5 promoter together with Smad4, and constitutively active Smad3a promotes cardiomyocyte proliferation. Combined loss of smad3a and smad3b enlarges the ventral aorta but is compatible with adult survival.

Core Functions

Receptor-regulated SMAD of the TGF-beta/activin/nodal branch. After C-terminal phosphorylation it forms a heteromeric complex with SMAD4, enters the nucleus, binds SMAD-binding elements and activates RNA polymerase II target genes (e.g. myf5). Molecular function shared with smad3b.

Interpretation

  • Both copies keep every annotated functional residue of SMAD3 except S418 in smad3b, so
    both are expected to act as TGF-beta/activin/nodal receptor-activated SMADs. Direct
    biochemical comparison is not available from these data.
  • smad3a is the conservative copy; smad3b has diverged faster, mostly in the linker, which
    carries the MAPK/CDK/GSK3 phosphosites (all of which are still present).
  • Expression overlaps broadly (both maternal and broadly zygotic). Quantitative differences
    exist (smad3a higher in paraxial mesoderm/somites, head kidney and retina), but Bgee scores
    across genes are not calibrated as absolute levels, and the gene-specific entity lists are
    confounded by study coverage. These data do not establish an expression partition.
