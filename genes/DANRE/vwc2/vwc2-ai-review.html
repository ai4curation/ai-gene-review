Gene Description

vwc2 (brorin; ZFIN ZDB-GENE-090313-315, chromosome 13) is the zebrafish ortholog of vertebrate VWC2/Brorin, supported by conserved exon-intron structure and by synteny: ikzf1 and fignl1 are its neighbours in zebrafish, mouse and gar. PANTHER pairs it with the divergent si:dkey-283b1.7 as a teleost genome duplication paralog, but synteny and other gene trees do not confirm that pairing. It encodes a 309-residue secreted protein. The signal peptide is followed by a divergent N-terminal region and a conserved core of two chordin-like von Willebrand factor C (VWFC) cysteine-rich domains. Like mouse Brorin, it acts as a secreted antagonist of BMP signalling. Injected brorin mRNA suppresses Smad1/5/8 phosphorylation and dorsalizes embryos, while knockdown raises phospho-Smad1/5/8 in the dorsal brain without activating Wnt signalling. The gene is expressed predominantly in the developing nervous system (diencephalon primordium from 16 hpf; later ventral telencephalon, prethalamus/alar hypothalamus, hindbrain, spinal cord, olfactory placode and pituitary). Morphants rescued by brorin mRNA show loss of subpallial (ventral telencephalic) identity, fewer GABAergic interneurons and oligodendrocytes, more astroglia, and failure of forebrain commissures. In mammals, Brorin is also an extracellular constituent of AMPA receptor complexes.

Core Functions

Secreted chordin-like BMP antagonist that restrains BMP/Smad1/5/8 signalling in the developing zebrafish forebrain. It is needed for subpallial patterning, for GABAergic interneuron and oligodendrocyte specification, and for commissural axon guidance.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does Brorin bind BMP ligands directly (and which), or act through another component? No binding assay has been reported for any Brorin family member.

Q: Do stable vwc2 mutants reproduce the morphant forebrain phenotypes, and do vwc2 and vwc2l act redundantly in the prethalamus, where both are expressed?

Q: Is the IMP to nervous system development from PMID:19852960 based on a vwc2 experiment, or is it vwc2l data attached to vwc2?

vwc2 (brorin) notes

ZFIN vwc2 (ZDB-GENE-090313-315, chromosome 13; synonym brorin, si:dkey-4j1.1); NCBI Gene 798521;
Ensembl ENSDARG00000076495; UniProt B0I1T8 (309 aa). PANTHER (PTHR46252) pairs it with
si:dkey-283b1.7 as a TGD_tree 1:1 pair, with human ortholog VWC2 (LDO). The separate brorin-like gene
vwc2l (ZDB-GENE-081104-169) is the VWC2L ortholog and is not part of this pair. Reviewed as part of
DANRE_DUPLICATION batch 3 (random sample).

Analyses (file:DANRE/si_dkey-283b1.7/si_dkey-283b1.7-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md)

  • The brorin MOs are vwc2-specific (0 mismatches). They have 8-10 mismatches to si:dkey-283b1.7 and vwc2l.
  • The vwc2 core is 83.6% identical to human VWC2 and 89.3% to gar vwc2. The partner si:dkey-283b1.7 is
    only 32.6% identical to vwc2 over the full length.
  • Bgee: vwc2 has brain-dominated calls; si:dkey-283b1.7 has calls in retina and brain.
