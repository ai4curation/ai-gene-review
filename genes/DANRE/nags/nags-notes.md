# nags notes

## 2026-05-09 review notes

Reviewed GOA, UniProt E7FCP8, PMID:24465614, and PANTHER family cache. The core function is mitochondrial N-acetylglutamate synthase activity, supporting N-acetylglutamate production and the zebrafish urea-cycle/ureagenesis context during development; arginine binding is a regulatory ligand annotation rather than the core function itself.

## Re-review 2026-09-29

The previous version was fully templated: every row carried the same summary sentence ("... is reviewed for zebrafish nags. The annotation is consistent with the curated UniProt/GOA record ...") and the same UniProt FUNCTION quote, including under the mitochondrial-matrix rows. All 11 rows rewritten with row-specific evidence from the one primary paper, PMID:24465614 (full text cached).

- GO:0004042 L-glutamate N-acetyltransferase activity (IDA, IBA, IEA) all ACCEPT, now anchored to the actual assay [PMID:24465614 "NAGS (EC 2.3.1.1) catalyzes the formation of N-acetylglutamate from glutamate and acetyl coenzyme A and in zebrafish is partially inhibited by L-arginine."] and the UniProt kinetics (KM 1.13 mM glutamate, 0.22 mM acetyl-CoA).
- GO:0005759 mitochondrial matrix (IBA, IEA, ISS) ACCEPT, supported by the experimentally delimited targeting signal in this species [PMID:24465614 "three regions of sequence conservation: the mitochondrial targeting signal (MTS) at the N-terminus"] rather than by the FUNCTION line.
- GO:0006526 L-arginine biosynthetic process (IBA, IEA) kept non-core, now with the actual argument: the node is dominated by microbial and fungal members for which NAG is the committed arginine-biosynthesis intermediate, while the fish support is an expression-based proposal [PMID:24465614 "The expression pattern of NAGS and other urea cycle genes in developing zebrafish suggests that they may have a role in citrulline and/or arginine biosynthesis during the first day of development and in ammonia detoxification thereafter."].
- GO:0034618 arginine binding (IDA) kept non-core with the direct binding evidence [PMID:24465614 "binding of arginine to both zfNAGS-M and zfNAGS-C in the absence of substrates"] and the species contrast [PMID:24465614 "mammalian NAGS is activated by L-arginine whereas fish NAGS is partially inhibited by L-arginine"].
- GO:0000050 urea cycle (NEW, IC) retained after running the participation and comparator tests explicitly. NAGS catalyses no cycle step but supplies the obligatory activator [PMID:24465614 "is an essential allosteric activator of CPS1; NAG deficiency results in a block of ureagenesis"], which is the cofactor-contribution case. Comparator check via QuickGO: human NAGS (UniProtKB:Q8N159) already carries GO:0000050 by IDA, while the zebrafish holders of the term are the reaction enzymes themselves (cps1, otc, ass1, arg1, arg2), so the proposal follows an existing GO convention for NAGS rather than inventing one.
- Description rewritten as standalone biology (tetramer, MTS/variable/AAK-NAT architecture, partial arginine inhibition as an intermediate state between microbial inhibition and mammalian activation); core_functions restated; suggested_questions and suggested_experiments added (both were absent).
