Gene Description

tmc2a (transmembrane channel-like 2a) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate TMC2, the paralog tmc2b being the other; both lie on chromosome 5, tmc2b next to tmc1. Tmc2a is a multi-pass membrane protein of the TMC family and a pore-forming subunit of the hair-cell mechanoelectrical transduction (MET) channel, a non-selective cation channel with high calcium permeability that opens when tip-link tension deflects the hair bundle. Its cytoplasmic N-terminus binds the cytoplasmic domain of the tip-link protein Pcdh15a. tmc2a is expressed only in sensory hair cells, from 1 dpf in the inner ear, where it is enriched in central (striolar) hair cells and in the upper layer of the cristae, and at low levels in a subset of lateral-line hair cells that respond to posterior-to-anterior deflection. Tmc2a is the main Tmc subunit for hearing through the saccule, is required for mechanotransduction in upper-layer crista hair cells and for high-frequency utricular sensitivity, and together with Tmc2b carries all lateral-line transduction; in neuromasts it enlarges the responses of the hair cells that express it.

Core Functions

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does full-length Tmc2a localize to stereocilia tips and rescue tmc2a or tmc triple mutants when expressed from a hair-cell promoter, and can Tmc2a rescue the Tmc2b-only neuromast hair cells? The reciprocal Tmc2b transgene rescues all end organs, but the Tmc2a construct never expressed.

Q: Do Tmc2a and Tmc2b form heteromeric channels in the cells that express both, and does Tmc2a confer different conductance or calcium permeability (larger responses in P-to-A neuromast cells, broader frequency range in the utricle)?

Q: Is the tmc2a/tmc2b pair a product of the teleost genome duplication although both copies are on zebrafish chromosome 5? Are the medaka co-orthologs on different chromosomes, and does a gar-anchored double-conserved-synteny analysis place both in the same ancestral block?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express Tmc2b (and Tmc2a, if a stable construct can be made) under a tmc2a enhancer in tmc2a mutants and measure dye uptake in upper-layer crista hair cells and saccular microphonics.

Hypothesis: The Tmc2a-dependent crista and saccular phenotypes reflect expression, not a Tmc2a-specific protein property.

Type: cross-rescue

Experiment: Record MET currents from neuromast hair cells of tmc2b mutants (Tmc2a-only P-to-A cells) and tmc2a mutants (Tmc2b-only cells) and compare conductance and calcium permeability.

Hypothesis: Tmc2a changes single-channel conductance or calcium selectivity relative to Tmc2b.

Type: electrophysiology

LG21/human 9q34), which is the pattern expected for duplicated segments of one ancestral region. The tmc2a segment
has been rearranged relative to gar, so its micro-synteny around the gene itself is weak. The medaka copies are on
different chromosomes, as TGD ohnologs usually are; the zebrafish copies are on the same chromosome, which could
reflect a later rearrangement in the zebrafish lineage. This analysis does not by itself distinguish the TGD from a
large segmental duplication in the teleost stem; together with the PANTHER and Compara node placement it is
consistent with the TGD.

  only in sensory hair cells, from 1 dpf in the inner ear, where it is enriched in central (striolar) hair cells
  and in the upper layer of the cristae, and at low levels in a subset of lateral-line hair cells that respond to
  posterior-to-anterior deflection. Tmc2a is the main Tmc subunit for hearing through the saccule, is required for
  mechanotransduction in upper-layer crista hair cells and for high-frequency utricular sensitivity, and together
  with Tmc2b carries all lateral-line transduction; in neuromasts it enlarges the responses of the hair cells that
  express it.
