The positive GO:0004672 protein kinase activity ISS row comes from the COQ8B paralog (Q96D53); the negated row comes from the COQ8A ortholog (Q8NI60) and traces to a direct assay [PMID:27499294 "we demonstrate that it lacks canonical protein kinase activity in trans"]. Changed the positive row KEEP_AS_NON_CORE -> MODIFY to GO:0016301 kinase activity, with a propagation_review naming COQ8B as SOURCE_BAD for this claim, and changed both negated rows (GO:0004672, GO:0006468) KEEP_AS_NON_CORE -> ACCEPT, since the negation is what the experiments established. Human COQ8A does carry an IDA for this term, so the row is not baseless, but the phosphotransfer is autophosphorylation that appears only when the regulatory loop is broken, and that same change blocks the pathway [PMID:25498144 "A single alanine-to-glycine mutation of this loop flips this coenzyme selectivity and enables autophosphorylation but inhibits coenzyme Q biosynthesis in vivo"].
- GO:0043531 ADP binding kept non-core, now grounded in the primary measurement [PMID:25498144 "ADCK3 exhibits an unusual selectivity for binding ADP over ATP"] rather than the UniProt paraphrase alone. GO:0016301 kinase activity ACCEPTed as the core MF at the level human curators chose from the same data.
- Localization rows now cite the ortholog evidence for the matrix face of the inner mitochondrial membrane [PMID:27499294 "Coq8p and COQ8A localize to the matrix face of the inner mitochondrial membrane"; PMID:29198567 "Endogenous COQ8 contains a TM domain that anchors it to the inner mitochondrial membrane (IMM) facing the matrix"].
