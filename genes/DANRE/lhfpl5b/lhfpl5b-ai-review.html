Gene Description

lhfpl5b (LHFPL tetraspan subfamily member 5b) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate LHFPL5 (TMHS), retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication; its paralog is lhfpl5a. It encodes a small four-pass membrane protein of the tetraspan junctional protein superfamily, an accessory subunit of the hair-cell mechanoelectrical transduction (MET) complex. lhfpl5b is expressed only in hair cells of the lateral-line neuromasts, from about 2 dpf, and not in the inner ear. lhfpl5b loss-of-function mutants (vo35, a frameshift that removes three of the four transmembrane helices) lose transduction in almost all lateral-line hair cells and have fewer neuromast hair cells, while hearing and balance are normal; the mutants are adult viable. Expression of Lhfpl5a in lhfpl5b mutant hair cells restores lateral-line transduction. Tmc2b still reaches the hair bundle without Lhfpl5b. Because they lack only lateral-line function, lhfpl5b mutants have been used to show lateral-line roles in swim-bladder inflation and in social regulation of pth2 expression.

Core Functions

Accessory transmembrane subunit of the MET complex in lateral-line hair cells, required for their mechanotransduction; functionally interchangeable with Lhfpl5a, which it replaces in the lateral line because of expression, not protein, differences. Recorded as a contribution to the channel complex's activity.
