Gene Description

col1a1b (collagen type I alpha 1b; also known as col1a3) encodes the pro-alpha3(I) chain, a third type I collagen alpha chain found in most bony fishes but absent from tetrapods. It is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of human COL1A1; its paralog col1a1a encodes alpha1(I), and the two arose by duplication of the ancestral alpha1(I) gene, most likely in the teleost-specific genome duplication. The 1449-aa secreted procollagen has the full fibrillar collagen architecture (signal peptide, von Willebrand factor type C domain in the N-propeptide, uninterrupted Gly-X-Y triple helix, fibrillar collagen NC1 C-propeptide) and is 78% identical to pro-alpha1(I). It differs from alpha1(I) at one of the inter-chain C-propeptide cysteines, in a more divergent chain recognition region, and in a higher Gly-Gly content of the helix. Alpha3(I) is translated in embryos and is a regular component of type I collagen in adult bone, skin and scales, where the three chains occur at about equal amounts, with a relatively higher alpha3(I) share in skin and scales than in bone. col1a1b is co-expressed with col1a1a and col1a2 in osteoblasts, tendon and ligament fibroblasts, myosepta and basal epidermis, but not in the fin fold. A dominant helix glycine substitution (dmh29) causes a severe osteogenesis imperfecta-like skeletal phenotype, showing that alpha3(I) is incorporated into bone collagen. Null mutants are viable with only mild bone changes, while combined heterozygous loss of col1a1a and col1a1b causes bone fragility.

Core Functions

Col1a1b (alpha3(I)) is an additional structural chain of zebrafish type I collagen. It is incorporated with alpha1(I) (col1a1a) and alpha2(I) (col1a2) into triple-helical type I collagen of bone, skin and scales, where the three chains are present at about equal amounts, and it contributes to the fibrillar matrix that gives these tissues tensile strength. Its function overlaps that of alpha1(I) and is partly interchangeable with it: loss of alpha3(I) alone is tolerated, but reducing both chains causes bone fragility. Its share is higher in skin and scales than in bone.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: What is the actual trimer composition in each tissue: alpha1alpha2alpha3 only, or a mixture with (alpha1)2alpha2 and (alpha3)2alpha2? How does the missing inter-chain C-propeptide cysteine of alpha3(I) constrain chain selection?

Q: Does alpha3(I) change the properties of the collagen it is part of, for example lower thermal stability from its high Gly-Gly content, or different cross-linking in skin and scales? Or is it a dosage copy of alpha1(I) with a neutral sequence drift?

Q: ZFIN has not annotated col1a1b from PMID:30082390 (the null sa12931 and the dominant dmh29 phenotypes); instead, a dmh29 genotype row is attached to col1a1a. Should that evidence be moved to col1a1b?

col1a1b C-propeptide cysteine check

Script: cpropeptide_cys.py (run from the repository root with uv run python).
Input: the cached UniProt records genes/DANRE/col1a1a/col1a1a-uniprot.txt (Q6U1J5)
and genes/DANRE/col1a1b/col1a1b-uniprot.txt (Q6PEI9). The script takes the
PROSITE "Fibrillar collagen NC1" domain of each protein (the C-propeptide) and
aligns the two domains globally (BLOSUM62, gap open -10, extend -0.5).

Output (2026-09-28):

col1a1a length 1447, NC1 starts at 1212; col1a1b length 1449, NC1 starts at 1214
Cys count in NC1: col1a1a 8 col1a1b 7
Cys not conserved: col1a1a C1265 <-> col1a1b S1267

Interpretation: all C-propeptide cysteines of alpha1(I) are conserved in
alpha3(I) except one, which is a serine in col1a1b. This agrees with the published
observation that proalpha3(I) lacks one of the four inter-chain cysteines of the
C-propeptide (Cys63 of the proalpha1(I) C-propeptide; PMID:26876635), and with
the Cys/Ser discriminator between alpha1(I) and alpha3(I) chains across fishes
(PMID:34430038). The numbering differs between sources because the C-propeptide
boundary used in the papers is not the PROSITE domain start. This check only
confirms the residue difference in the two zebrafish sequences; it says nothing
about its effect on trimer assembly.

    Col1a1b (alpha3(I)) is an additional structural chain of zebrafish type I
