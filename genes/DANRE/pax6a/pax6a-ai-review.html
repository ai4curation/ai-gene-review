Gene Description

pax6a is one of two zebrafish co-orthologues of vertebrate PAX6, retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication. It encodes a transcription factor with an N-terminal paired domain and a paired-type homeodomain that binds cis-regulatory DNA and activates target genes; alternative splicing produces short and long (exon 5a-containing) isoforms. pax6a is expressed in the developing retina and lens and, more broadly than its paralogue pax6b, in the telencephalon, diencephalon, hindbrain and spinal cord, but not in the pancreas. It is required for habenular neurogenesis in the dorsal diencephalon, acts together with pax6b in anterior eye segment development, in guidance of periocular neural crest and in hindbrain patterning, and promotes proliferation of Muller glia-derived neuronal progenitors during adult retinal regeneration. The two co-orthologues encode proteins with equivalent transactivation activity; their divergence lies mainly in expression, reflecting differential loss of cis-regulatory elements after duplication.
