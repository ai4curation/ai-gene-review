# dla (Danio rerio, Q6DI48, DeltaA) — curation notes

- Swiss-Prot Q6DI48, Delta-like protein A; MNNL + DSL + 8 EGF + TM; PANTHER PTHR24049 (CRUMBS
  FAMILY MEMBER) / PTHR24049:SF30 (unnamed).
- Co-ortholog (with dld) of mammalian DLL1.
- Localisation: [PMID:19924821 "Both experiments suggest that in the embryonic spinal cord, Dla
  protein is localized to puncta at the cell membrane and/or cortex; widespread Dla localization in
  the cytoplasm is not supported by our data"] -> cytoplasm IEA removed.
- Mib1 dependence: [PMID:15013799 "Mind bomb protein displays E3 ubiquitin ligase activity in vitro
  and that it is associated with Delta and enhances its ubiquitination and internalization in
  transfected cells"]
- Receptors: [PMID:24768892 "Two ligands, DeltaA and DeltaD, and three receptors, Notch1a, Notch1b,
  and Notch3 redundantly contribute to p2 progenitor maintenance."]
- Midline: [PMID:10074451 "The dlAdx2 mutant embryos had reduced numbers of floorplate and
  hypochord cells"]; dx2 is a dominant-negative missense allele (PMID:10572043).
- Neural crest vs RB neurons: [PMID:10851132 "have supernumerary Rohon-Beard neurons, reduced
  trunk-level expression of neural crest markers and lack trunk neural crest derivatives"];
  fin mesenchyme present in mutants -> mesenchyme development IEA removed.
- IBA nodes: PTN002371879 (Notch binding); PTN001170801 (plasma membrane, Notch signaling pathway,
  negative regulation of Notch signaling pathway [cis-inhibition]).
- Protein binding (IPI with Mib) -> MODIFY to ubiquitin protein ligase binding (GO:0031625).
