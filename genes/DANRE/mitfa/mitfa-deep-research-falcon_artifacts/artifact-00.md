| Molecular function and domains | Mitfa is a MiT/TFE-family basic helix–loop–helix leucine-zipper transcription factor. | Xanthophore role | Ordinary mitfa loss does not eliminate xanthophores, but their development can be abnormal. | RPE distinction | Unlike the neural-crest melanophore lineage, zebrafish retinal pigment epithelium develops and pigments normally after loss of mitfa, mitfb or both.
