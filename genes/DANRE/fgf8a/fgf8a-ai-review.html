Gene Description

fgf8a is the zebrafish orthologue of vertebrate FGF8 and one of two co-orthologues (with fgf8b) retained from the teleost-specific genome duplication; it is the gene mutated in acerebellar (ace). It encodes a secreted fibroblast growth factor of the FGF8/17/18 (FgfD) subfamily, with an N-terminal signal peptide and the FGF beta-trefoil core, that acts as an extracellular ligand for FGF receptor tyrosine kinases and activates Ras/MAPK signalling. Its spread through tissue is limited by endocytosis and lysosomal degradation in receiving cells, which shapes a signalling gradient. fgf8a is expressed from the blastula onward, in a dorsoventral gradient during gastrulation and later at the midbrain-hindbrain boundary, dorsal diencephalon, telencephalon, somites, tailbud, heart precursors, pharyngeal region, otic region and pectoral fin bud. It is required for the midbrain-hindbrain boundary (isthmic) organizer and cerebellum, for induction of myocardial precursors, for otic and epibranchial placode induction with fgf3, for Kupffer's vesicle ciliogenesis and left-right asymmetry, and it contributes to gastrula dorsoventral patterning, mesoderm formation and restriction of endoderm, somitic fast-muscle differentiation, hindbrain patterning, retinal patterning and pharyngeal pouch and cartilage development. Heterozygous adults show craniofacial skeletal defects. In zebrafish its paralogue fgf8b shares the midbrain-hindbrain boundary and somite expression domains but is not expressed during gastrulation or in the heart.
