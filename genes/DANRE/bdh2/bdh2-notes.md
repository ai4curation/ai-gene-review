# Notes for DANRE bdh2

- The UniProt record supports two distinct Bdh2 activities: 4-oxoproline reductase chemistry described as ketoproline detoxification [file:DANRE/bdh2/bdh2-uniprot.txt "detoxification mechanism for ketoprolines"] and separate 2,5-DHBA-associated siderophore formation [file:DANRE/bdh2/bdh2-uniprot.txt "formation of 2,5-dihydroxybenzoate (2,5-DHBA)"].
- The siderophore/heme branch is retained as a separate core role because bdh2 inactivation affects zebrafish heme biology and erythroid maturation [PMID:26929344 "results in heme deficiency and delays erythroid maturation"].
- 3-hydroxybutyrate dehydrogenase activity is kept as non-core because UniProt phrases it as a possible by-similarity activity, while zebrafish evidence focuses on siderophore/heme biology.
- Hemoglobin and erythrocyte maturation terms are real phenotypic consequences, but not the molecular core.

## Re-review 2026-09-28

Re-reviewed all 16 GOA rows against cached primary literature (PMID:20550936 full text; PMID:26929344, PMID:24777603, PMID:16380372 abstracts; PMID:35150746 full text, newly cached) and rewrote every review block, the description, references and core functions.

- Resolved the PENDING ISS row for 4-oxoproline reductase activity (GO:0016617, from human Q9BUT1) as ACCEPT. Rat and human BDH2 were shown to be the 4-oxo-L-proline reductase by purification and recombinant enzymology [PMID:35150746 "showed that it catalyzed the reversible reduction of 4-oxo-l-proline to cis-4-hydroxy-l-proline via chromatographic and tandem mass spectrometry analysis"], with 4-oxo-L-proline the best substrate [PMID:35150746 "the human enzyme acted with 12,500-fold higher catalytic efficiency on 4-oxo-l-proline than on (R)-β-hydroxybutyrate"].
- GO:0016628 (CH-CH oxidoreductase, ISS from mouse Q8JZV9): changed MODIFY (to GO:0016617) -> ACCEPT. The proposed replacement was chemically wrong: this term is the class for the EntA-type dehydrogenation that mouse Bdh2 carries by IMP from the same paper [PMID:20550936 "Significantly, 2,3-DHBA production was also catalyzed by recombinant BDH2 but not by a mutant BDH2 derivative (Tyr147 to Phe)."], whereas 4-oxoproline reductase is a CH-OH-group reaction. The endogenous 2,5-DHBA precursor is unknown, so the class term is the honest level; GO:0008667 is raised as a question.
- GO:0042168 heme metabolic process (IMP): changed ACCEPT -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE on the participation test. Bdh2 makes the siderophore that delivers iron to mitochondria [PMID:20550936 "Siderophore-depleted mammalian cells and zebrafish embryos fail to synthesize heme, an iron-dependent mitochondrial process."] but performs no reaction involving heme itself; consistent with the KEEP_AS_NON_CORE calls on hemoglobin biosynthetic process and erythrocyte maturation, which ZFIN already qualifies as acts_upstream_of_or_within.
- Dropped the two NEW rows from the previous review: GO:1990748 cellular detoxification (supported only by a UniProt "likely" statement; none of the human, mouse or rat orthologs carries a detoxification term) and GO:0016616 (a parent of the already-accepted GO:0016617, i.e. redundant). Also removed the proposed new term for a 2,5-DHBA-forming oxidoreductase, since no defined reaction (precursor) exists to define it against.
- 3-hydroxybutyrate dehydrogenase activity (IBA/IEA/ISS) kept as non-core, now grounded in the human IDA source [PMID:16380372 "Substrate screening reveals sole NAD(+)-dependent conversion of (R)-hydroxybutyrate to acetoacetate with K(m) values of about 10 mm"] and the mouse knockout [PMID:24777603 "BDH2 deficiency did not alter ketone body metabolism in vivo"].
- Cytoplasm (IBA/IEA/ISS) accepted on the human GFP localization [PMID:16380372 "GFP reporter gene analysis reveals a cytosolic localization upon transfection into mammalian cells."] and the cytosolic siderophore in zebrafish [PMID:26929344 "a cytosolic siderophore, which resembles a bacterial siderophore, facilitates mitochondrial iron import in eukaryotes, including zebrafish"].
- Core functions now: (1) 4-oxoproline reductase activity in the cytoplasm; (2) CH-CH oxidoreductase activity (EntA-like) directly involved in siderophore biosynthetic process. Validation: zero errors, zero warnings.
