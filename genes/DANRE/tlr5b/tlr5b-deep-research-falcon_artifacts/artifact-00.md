| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Protein name and gene symbol | Toll-like receptor 5b precursor; gene symbol *tlr5b* (synonym *tlr5*); target accession UniProt A0ACM8R384. |
| Organism | Zebrafish, *Danio rerio* (formerly *Brachydanio rerio*). The literature identifies *tlr5a* and *tlr5b* as the two zebrafish counterparts of vertebrate *TLR5* (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000051). |
| Protein family | Toll-like receptor family; an innate-immune pattern-recognition receptor and type-I single-pass transmembrane glycoprotein (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000020, pqac-00000052). |
| Primary molecular function | Serves as a non-catalytic receptor subunit that detects bacterial flagellin in cooperation with TLR5a and converts ligand-induced receptor rearrangement into intracellular inflammatory signaling, notably NF-κB activation (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000003). |
| Ligand specificity | The experimentally established zebrafish ligand is bacterial flagellin, including flagellins from *Salmonella enterica* and *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*; TLR5b therefore has a recognition ligand rather than an enzymatic substrate (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000006). A potential second ligand class—dsRNA/poly(I:C)—has been demonstrated for **grass-carp** TLR5b homodimers, but direct recognition by zebrafish TLR5b has not been established and should not be transferred across species without testing (pqac-00000037, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000041). |
| Receptor configuration | Native full-length zebrafish TLR5b is not an independently signaling flagellin-responsive homodimer. Productive flagellin recognition and signaling require a complementary TLR5a–TLR5b heterodimer; isolated TLR5b N-terminal ectodomain regions can support a homodimeric arrangement in engineered constructs, but the complete intracellular signaling configuration must include contributions from both paralogs (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000012). |
| Cellular localization | An integral membrane protein. In heterologous-cell imaging experiments, zebrafish TLR5b accumulated principally in LAMP-1-positive late-endosomal/lysosomal vesicles rather than predominantly at the plasma membrane. The trafficking chaperone UNC93B1 promoted receptor redistribution and functional association with TLR5a; this localization should be interpreted as direct cell-model evidence, not yet a complete in-vivo zebrafish tissue map (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000020). |
| Key structural domains | N-terminal signal peptide/precursor organization; extracellular leucine-rich repeats and LRR C-terminal cysteine-rich flanking region for ligand recognition; one transmembrane helix; and a cytosolic Toll/interleukin-1 receptor (TIR) domain for adaptor recruitment and signaling. This architecture is characteristic of TLR proteins and agrees with the supplied InterPro assignments IPR001611, IPR003591, IPR032675, IPR000483, and IPR000157 (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000052). |
| Evolutionary origin | TLR5a and TLR5b are distinct paralogs rather than splice isoforms. Recent comparative phylogenetic and synteny analysis favors a tandem duplication of an ancestral membrane-form *TLR5* gene for the zebrafish M/M pair, followed by subfunctionalization and concerted coevolution into a complementary heterodimer; broader teleost TLR5 diversity also reflects whole-genome duplication and gene loss (pqac-00000030, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000034). |
