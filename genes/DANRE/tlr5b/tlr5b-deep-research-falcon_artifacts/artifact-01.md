| Component/Process | Details with References |
|---|---|
| Ligand recognition and receptor activation | Bacterial flagellin engages a functional TLR5a–TLR5b heterodimer. Ligand-induced ectodomain rearrangement is transmitted across the membrane to juxtapose the cytosolic TIR domains; full-length TLR5b alone does not produce effective flagellin-induced NF-κB signaling in the tested system (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000013, pqac-00000024). |
| Trafficking regulator | Zebrafish UNC93B1 promotes redistribution and productive association of TLR5a and TLR5b. TLR5b was observed in LAMP-1-positive late-endosomal/lysosomal vesicles in heterologous cells (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000016). |
| Adaptor proteins | MyD88 is the best-supported adaptor for zebrafish flagellin–TLR5 signaling. General zebrafish TLR pathway inventories also include TIRAP/Mal and TRIF/TICAM1, but their direct recruitment by TLR5b has not been established with the same specificity (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000049). |
| Proximal kinases and signaling intermediates | The canonical inferred cascade is TIR-domain signaling → MyD88 → IRAK-family kinases → TRAF6 → TAK1/TAB complex. Zebrafish pathway studies identify Irak and Traf6 as conserved signaling intermediates, while *irak3* is induced as a likely negative-feedback regulator (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000026, pqac-00000028). |
| NF-κB branch | TAK1 activates the IKK complex, promoting IκB degradation and NF-κB nuclear activity. Functional TLR5a/TLR5b reconstitution directly activates an NF-κB reporter; infection-associated targets include *rel*, *rela*, *relb*, *nfkb2*, *nfkbiaa*, and *nfkbiab* (pqac-00000021, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000026). |
| MAPK/AP-1 branch | The conserved pathway predicts activation of ERK, JNK, and p38 MAPKs and AP-1-family factors. Zebrafish flagellin/infection responses include *fos*, *jun*, *atf3*, *atf4*, and *atf5*, although their dependence on TLR5b alone has not been genetically isolated (pqac-00000021, pqac-00000026). |
| Key downstream genes | Flagellin induces inflammatory and defense-associated genes including *il1b*, *il8/cxcl8*, *cxcl-C1c*, *mmp9*, and *irak3*. TLR5a/b depletion nearly abolished *mmp9*, *cxcl-C1c*, and *irak3* regulation and reduced *il1b* and *il8* responses (pqac-00000021, pqac-00000046, pqac-00000057). |
| Biological processes | The pathway mediates recognition of flagellated bacteria, pro-inflammatory cytokine and chemokine production, leukocyte-recruiting signals, extracellular-matrix remodeling through Mmp9, and feedback limitation of TLR signaling through Irak3 (pqac-00000021, pqac-00000044, pqac-00000046). |
| Response during bacterial infection | Both *tlr5a* and *tlr5b* are upregulated during *Salmonella Typhimurium* infection. Their induction also occurs with nonflagellated bacteria, indicating that infection-associated expression is broader than direct flagellin sensing; redundant pattern-recognition pathways can preserve inflammation during whole-bacterium infection (pqac-00000044, pqac-00000058). |
| In-vivo loss-of-function evidence | Embryonic morpholino knockdown—either simultaneous or separate targeting of *tlr5a* and *tlr5b*—followed by bloodstream injection of purified flagellin reduced or prevented inflammatory gene induction. Separate depletion of either paralog blocked flagellin-induced *il1b*, supporting their functional interdependence (pqac-00000054, pqac-00000055, pqac-00000059). |
| Cell-based functional and structure–function evidence | Overexpression of wild-type, chimeric, and mutant receptors in reporter cells, followed by Salmonella flagellin stimulation and NF-κB luciferase measurement, showed that productive signaling requires complementary TLR5a/TLR5b extracellular and intracellular regions (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000014, pqac-00000060). |
| Evidence limitation | Most zebrafish loss-of-function evidence is based on transient morpholinos rather than stable *tlr5b* CRISPR mutants. Consequently, developmental compensation, paralog-specific in-vivo functions, tissue distribution, and effects on pathogen burden or survival remain incompletely resolved (pqac-00000054, pqac-00000056, pqac-00000060). |
