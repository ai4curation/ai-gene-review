Gene Description

samsn1b (SAM domain, SH3 domain and nuclear localization signals 1b) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate SAMSN1 (HACS1, SLy2, NASH1); its paralog is samsn1a. It encodes an intracellular adaptor of the SLy/SASH1 family with an N-terminal disordered region, a central SH3 domain and a C-terminal SAM domain. In mammals SAMSN1 is expressed mainly in hematopoietic cells, is found in cytoplasm and nucleus, is induced by B-cell activation signals, binds tyrosine-phosphorylated proteins and, through its SH3 domain, the inhibitory receptor PIRB, and acts as an inhibitory adaptor that dampens B-cell activation (mouse knockout B cells are hyper-responsive). The zebrafish protein keeps a conserved SH3 domain (77% identical to human SAMSN1) but is much longer than human SAMSN1 and only 38% identical to samsn1a overall. samsn1b has a brief gastrula expression peak and is otherwise mainly larval and adult; in situ hybridization detects it in the pineal organ (epiphysis) and the retinal photoreceptor layer, and bulk RNA-seq places it highest in retina and granulocytes, with lower calls in spleen and head kidney. No functional study of the zebrafish gene has been published.

Core Functions

SH3-SAM intracellular signalling adaptor of the SLy/SASH1 family, expressed in retina (photoreceptor layer), pineal organ and, at lower levels, granulocytes and hematopoietic organs; by homology with mammalian SAMSN1 an inhibitory adaptor in immune-receptor signalling. No activity has been measured for the zebrafish protein, so no molecular function term is asserted.

Expression and protein (file:DANRE/samsn1a/samsn1a-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md)

  • ZFIN curated in situ (Thisse et al. 2004 direct submission, ZDB-PUB-040907-1): epiphysis (pineal)
    from 14-19 somites to high-pec; retinal photoreceptor layer at day 5. No curated blood or
    macrophage expression (those are samsn1a records).
  • E-ERAD-475: small gastrula peak (5 TPM), silent through pharyngula, 10-17 TPM in larvae.
  • Bgee RNA-seq: highest in retina (89.3) and granulocyte (88.4); also spleen, head kidney, gill,
    intestine, testis.
  • Protein 37.8% identical to samsn1a; SH3 77.4% identical to human SAMSN1; SAM 60%.
  • Compara gives samsn1b no medaka orthologue (medaka's single copy is assigned to samsn1a).

    tissues, so there is no expression argument for loss of the immune role.
