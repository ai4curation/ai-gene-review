Gene Description

tp53bp2b is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of human TP53BP2 (ASPP2), a large cytoplasmic scaffold protein with an N-terminal Ras-associating (ubiquitin-like) domain, a coiled-coil and a long proline-rich, largely disordered middle region, and a C-terminal module of four ankyrin repeats and an SH3 domain. In mammals this C-terminal module binds the p53 DNA-binding domain and enhances p53-dependent apoptosis, and ASPP2 also acts at apical cell junctions, where it binds Par-3 and scaffolds PP1 and YAP. In zebrafish embryos, Aspp2b (like its paralog Aspp2a) binds Irs-1 and restrains embryonic body growth without changing developmental rate, by dampening Akt signaling. tp53bp2b transcripts are maternally provided and present in early embryos, are low during gastrulation and organogenesis, and are found in many larval and adult tissues. A GFP-tagged form expressed in embryos is cytoplasmic, and tp53bp2b is induced by the ciliogenic transcription factor Foxj1.

Core Functions

Binds Irs-1 through its ankyrin-SH3 module and dampens insulin/IGF-Akt signaling, restraining embryonic body growth without affecting developmental timing (shown for both zebrafish copies by knockdown, overexpression and myr-Akt1 epistasis).

Own analysis

See tp53bp2a-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md.
Ankyrin repeats 90.7-97.1% identical to human; SH3 intact; relative rate equal to tp53bp2a.
tp53bp2b is the lower-expressed copy during development (maternally provided like tp53bp2a, then 2-9 TPM from
gastrula to pharyngula, 11-15 TPM in larvae), broadly expressed in adults with its highest Bgee
score in retina.
