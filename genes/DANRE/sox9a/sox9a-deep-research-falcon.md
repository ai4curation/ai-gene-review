# Functional annotation report: zebrafish **sox9a** (UniProt Q9DFH2)

## Executive summary

The requested target is correctly identified as **Danio rerio sox9a**, also called **jellyfish (jef)**, and not zebrafish *sox9b*, mammalian *SOX9*, or a similarly named gene from another fish. Zebrafish *sox9a* and *sox9b* are duplicated co-orthologs of tetrapod *SOX9* whose ancestral functions have been partitioned; consequently, evidence for one paralog cannot automatically be transferred to the other. Q9DFH2’s annotated HMG-box/SOX architecture agrees with literature describing zebrafish Sox9a as a SoxE-family transcription factor with an HMG DNA-binding region and a glutamine/serine-rich transcriptional-regulatory region. (lin2021sox9anotsox9b pages 4-5, yan2005apairof pages 1-2)

The best-supported primary function is that **Sox9a is a sequence-specific developmental transcription factor that promotes chondrocyte differentiation, cartilage-matrix gene expression, and cartilage morphogenesis**. Its most decisive role is in craniofacial and proximal pectoral-girdle cartilage. Strong loss-of-function alleles cause failure of normal chondrocyte stacking, loss of neurocranial and most pharyngeal-arch cartilage, loss of scapulocoracoid cartilage, and death before 14 days post-fertilization. (lin2021sox9anotsox9b pages 4-5, yan2005apairof pages 10-12, yan2005apairof pages 8-9)

Sox9a acts primarily in the **nucleus**, consistent with HMG-box DNA binding and transcriptional regulation. Nuclear Sox9a has been observed in gonadal cells, although regulated cytoplasmic accumulation was reported during juvenile ovary-to-testis transformation. Direct zebrafish Sox9a chromatin-occupancy data remain limited, so *col2a1* and other responsive genes should be described as strongly supported downstream genes rather than definitively proven direct targets in this species. (yan2005apairof pages 1-2, yan2005apairof pages 12-12, sun2013sox9relatedsignalingcontrols pages 1-2)

## 1. Identity verification and nomenclature

### Target match

- **Accession:** Q9DFH2.
- **Gene:** *sox9a*.
- **Organism:** *Danio rerio* (zebrafish; formerly *Brachydanio rerio*).
- **Relevant synonym:** *jef*/*jellyfish*; the foundational mutant is commonly designated *sox9a/jef*.
- **Paralog requiring exclusion:** *sox9b*.

Comparative work identifies zebrafish *sox9a* and *sox9b* as co-orthologs generated by the teleost/ray-finned-fish genome duplication. Their expression and functions subsequently diverged through subfunctionalization: Sox9a has the dominant demonstrated role in much of cranial cartilage differentiation, whereas Sox9b has distinct contributions to chondrocyte number, epithelium-associated craniofacial programs, ear development, and other domains. (yan2005apairof pages 1-2, yan2005apairof pages 8-9)

### Domain agreement

The supplied UniProt/InterPro annotations—HMG box, Sox N-terminal region, and SOX-transcription-factor signatures—fit the literature target. A 2021 CRISPR study described Sox9a as a SoxE transcription factor with an N-terminal HMG DNA-binding domain and a C-terminal glutamine/serine-rich region. Its *sox9a* Δ10 and Δ67 frameshift alleles terminate in exon 1 and remove both functional regions, providing domain-consistent strong loss-of-function alleles. (lin2021sox9anotsox9b pages 4-5)

**Verification conclusion:** the symbol, organism, aliases, evolutionary placement, and protein architecture all agree. Research on zebrafish *sox9a* can therefore proceed without invoking a different gene.

## 2. Primary molecular function and cellular localization

Sox9a is **not an enzyme or transporter**. It is a developmental transcription factor. The HMG box confers sequence-specific DNA recognition and DNA bending typical of SOX proteins, while the glutamine/serine-rich C-terminal region supports transcriptional regulation. The biological output is activation or maintenance of cell-state-specific gene programs, especially the chondrocyte extracellular-matrix and differentiation program. (lin2021sox9anotsox9b pages 4-5, yan2005apairof pages 1-2)

The expected functional compartment is the **nucleus**. Gonadal experiments detected persistent nuclear Sox9a even when MAPK inhibition removed the induced cytoplasmic pool. This supports nuclear transcriptional activity as the core function, while indicating that localization may be dynamically regulated in gonadal stromal/support cells. The mechanistic significance of cytoplasmic Sox9a is less secure and should not be considered equivalent to its established nuclear role. (sun2013sox9relatedsignalingcontrols pages 1-2)

No secretion signal, membrane-spanning role, catalytic reaction, or extracellular structural function is implicated. Sox9a instead controls cellular differentiation and tissue architecture by regulating transcription inside cartilage precursors, chondrocytes, perichondrial cells, and gonadal support-cell lineages.

## 3. Principal biological role: chondrogenesis

### Craniofacial cartilage

The strongest causal evidence comes from genetic loss of function. In *sox9a/jef* mutants, prechondrogenic condensations can form, but cells fail to complete normal chondrocyte differentiation and fail to organize into characteristic stacks. By contrast, *sox9b* mutants primarily reduce chondrocyte number while allowing some remaining cells to stack; double mutants combine fewer cells with absent stacking. This establishes a particularly specific Sox9a role in **chondrocyte differentiation and morphogenesis**, rather than initial production or migration of all cranial neural-crest cells. (yan2005apairof pages 10-12, yan2005apairof pages 8-9)

Independent CRISPR alleles provided more recent confirmation. At 5 dpf, homozygous mutants lacked the maxilla and lower jaw; Alcian-blue staining showed loss of all neurocranial cartilage and most pharyngeal-arch cartilage. Homozygotes died before 14 dpf, plausibly because the craniofacial defect prevented feeding. Cleithrum and some endoskeletal-disc cartilage were preserved, demonstrating tissue specificity rather than a universal failure of skeletogenesis. (lin2021sox9anotsox9b pages 4-5)

Sox9a is highly expressed in deep cartilage precursors of the pharyngeal arches and at lower levels in surrounding perichondrium. The perichondrium later contributes osteoblasts and bone collar, providing a cellular basis for secondary effects on cartilage-replacement bones. (yan2005apairof pages 2-3, yan2005apairof pages 10-12)

### Cartilage-to-bone consequences

At 5 dpf, *sox9a* mutants have smaller dermal bones and severe defects in cartilage-replacement bones: ceratohyal and hyomandibular bones can be absent and ceratobranchial-5 is much smaller. However, *runx2b* expression is nearly normal at 2 dpf. Thus, the principal defect is not a global loss of early osteoblast specification; much of the later bone phenotype is best interpreted as secondary to failure of the cartilage template. (yan2005apairof pages 5-6, yan2005apairof pages 10-12)

### Downstream transcriptional program

The cartilage-matrix gene *col2a1* is co-expressed with zebrafish Sox9 paralogs, and its reduction in mutant tissues tracks with whichever paralog predominates in that domain. Sox9 perturbation also affects early neural-crest-associated genes including *foxd3*, *sox10*, *snai1b*, and *crestin*, while *tfap2a* is nearly unchanged. These data place Sox9 activity within neural-crest-to-chondrocyte differentiation, but only the domain-specific mutant evidence allows confident assignment to Sox9a. (yan2005apairof pages 1-2, yan2005apairof pages 12-12)

A critical qualification is that the retrieved studies did not provide a zebrafish Sox9a-specific ChIP-seq map or an unambiguous endogenous enhancer-occupancy experiment. Therefore, reduced *col2a1* is compelling evidence of Sox9a-dependent regulation, but direct physical binding in vivo remains partly inferred from conserved SOX9 biology. (yan2005apairof pages 1-2, yan2005apairof pages 12-12)

## 4. Pectoral-fin and girdle development

At approximately 48 hpf, *sox9a* is enriched in precursors of the **scapulocoracoid**, whereas *sox9b* is more prominent in the pectoral endochondral disc. Consistent with that distribution, Sox9a loss removes or severely disrupts scapulocoracoid cartilage, while much of the endoskeletal disc remains relatively normal. Proximal disc cartilage has additive requirements for both paralogs. (yan2005apairof pages 2-3, yan2005apairof pages 10-12)

Expression of *shh* in the zone of polarizing activity and *dlx2a* in the apical ectodermal ridge is similar among wild-type, *sox9a*, *sox9b*, and double-mutant embryos at 34–52 hpf. Sox9a is therefore not required to establish these early fin-bud signaling centers; it operates later or in parallel to execute proximal cartilage differentiation. (yan2005apairof pages 5-6)

## 5. Otic development

At 14 hpf, *sox9a* is strongly expressed in the otic placode. Nevertheless, the ear of a *sox9a* single mutant is nearly normal or only mildly affected, whereas *sox9b* and especially double-mutant embryos show stronger defects. Sox9a thus participates in otic development but is substantially buffered by Sox9b; it should not be annotated as the sole or dominant zebrafish otic Sox9 factor. (yan2005apairof pages 5-6, yan2005apairof pages 2-3, yan2005apairof pages 1-2)

## 6. Gonadal support cells and sex differentiation

Sox9a is also associated with the male gonadal-support-cell program. A 2024 single-cell RNA-sequencing study of chromosomally ZZ/ZW zebrafish detected *sox9a* in support-cell precursors at 19 dpf and found strong expression in a ZZ-specific Sertoli-cell cluster at 30 dpf. This is high-resolution evidence for cell-type localization and supports use of *sox9a* as a Sertoli marker, but the study did not perturb *sox9a* and therefore does not by itself establish necessity for male fate. Published March 2024; DOI/URL: https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2024.1362228. (wilson2024directmaledevelopment pages 12-13)

Earlier juvenile-gonad work reported that approximately half of zebrafish develop ovaries between 20 and 30 dpf, whereas the remainder undergo oocyte degeneration and acquire testis morphology. During transition, Sox9a-positive stromal cells activate, extracellular matrix accumulates, follicles disassemble, and testis cords form. In cultured juvenile ovaries, gonadotropins increased cytoplasmic Sox9a and phosphorylated ERK1/2. Combined MAPK inhibition with PD98059 and U0216 removed cytoplasmic—but not nuclear—Sox9a and p-ERK1/2, reduced spreading and proliferation, and increased nuclear p53. Published November 2013; DOI/URL: https://doi.org/10.1038/cddis.2013.456. (sun2013sox9relatedsignalingcontrols pages 1-2)

These gonadal results support a Sox9a–MAPK-associated support/stromal-cell program and dynamic intracellular localization. Their causal strength is lower than the cartilage genetics because the reported experiments were pharmacological and did not include a Sox9a-specific knockout/rescue test. Sox9a should therefore be annotated as **expressed in and likely involved in Sertoli/support-cell differentiation and juvenile testis formation**, not as a proven master sex-determining gene in zebrafish.

## 7. Pathway placement

### Neural crest–cartilage axis

Sox9a acts after or during cranial neural-crest commitment to drive differentiation into organized chondrocytes. Neural-crest populations and condensations are not globally absent in *sox9a* mutants; the decisive failure occurs in cartilage differentiation, matrix-gene expression, and cell stacking. (yan2005apairof pages 10-12, yan2005apairof pages 8-9)

### BMP signaling

Sox9a expression is reduced when BMP signaling is absent during fin regeneration, and Sox9-dependent chondrogenesis operates within BMP-rich craniofacial developmental networks. However, this supports Sox9a as a BMP-responsive component or downstream effector more clearly than it proves a direct biochemical interaction with BMP pathway proteins. (lin2021sox9anotsox9b pages 4-5)

### MAPK signaling in gonad

Gonadotropin-induced Sox9a redistribution parallels ERK1/2 activation, and MAPK inhibitors remove the cytoplasmic pool while sparing nuclear Sox9a. This supports pathway coupling in gonadal stromal/support cells, although it does not establish that Sox9a directly controls ERK or vice versa. (sun2013sox9relatedsignalingcontrols pages 1-2)

### Fin-bud Hedgehog and AER programs

Normal early *shh* and *dlx2a* domains in mutants argue that Sox9a is not required to initiate the major polarizing-region or apical-ridge signaling centers. Its role lies mainly in executing the later proximal cartilage program. (yan2005apairof pages 5-6)

### Osteogenic program

Near-normal early *runx2b* expression in *sox9a* mutants distinguishes Sox9a’s chondrogenic function from a broad requirement for osteoblast specification. This is also an important paralog distinction because *sox9b* loss has a stronger effect on *runx2b* in the cited developmental context. (yan2005apairof pages 5-6, yan2005apairof pages 10-12)

## 8. Recent developments, 2023–2024

The most informative recent gene-specific advance is the 2024 gonadal single-cell atlas, which places strong *sox9a* expression in differentiated ZZ Sertoli cells at 30 dpf and detects it earlier in support-cell precursors. This refines cellular localization but remains observational rather than a functional perturbation. (wilson2024directmaledevelopment pages 12-13)

Recent work also increasingly uses *sox9a* as a molecular readout of chondrocyte state in zebrafish craniofacial signaling, skeletal growth, injury, and regeneration models. Such studies broaden real-world use of the marker but generally do not supersede the direct functional evidence from *jef* and CRISPR mutants. Current interpretation therefore continues to rely most heavily on the foundational 2005 genetic analysis and the 2021 independent CRISPR validation. (lin2021sox9anotsox9b pages 4-5, yan2005apairof pages 1-2)

A major 2024 conceptual emphasis is careful treatment of duplicated zebrafish genes. Sox9a and Sox9b retain nuanced, non-interchangeable identities: Sox9a is especially important for cartilage-cell differentiation and stacking, while Sox9b contributes more strongly to chondrocyte abundance and certain otic and epithelial contexts. (yan2005apairof pages 1-2, yan2005apairof pages 8-9)

## 9. Current applications and real-world implementation

1. **Craniofacial-disease modeling:** Sox9a-dependent cartilage phenotypes provide sensitive endpoints for testing candidate human craniofacial genes and pathways.
2. **Developmental toxicology:** *sox9a* expression and Alcian-blue cartilage morphology are commonly used together to distinguish altered chondrocyte differentiation from gross embryotoxicity.
3. **Skeletal signaling studies:** *sox9a* serves as a chondrocyte differentiation readout in BMP-, FGF-, Wnt-, and IGF-associated zebrafish experiments; interpretation must distinguish marker changes from direct regulation.
4. **Regeneration research:** Reactivation of *sox9a* is used to identify chondrogenic progenitor states during fin or joint repair, although expression alone does not demonstrate that Sox9a is the causal driver.
5. **Reproductive biology:** Recent single-cell work uses *sox9a* to identify Sertoli and gonadal support-cell populations and to resolve the timing of male differentiation. (wilson2024directmaledevelopment pages 12-13)
6. **Paralog-function analysis:** Comparing *sox9a*, *sox9b*, and double mutants is a well-developed model for studying subfunctionalization after vertebrate genome duplication. (yan2005apairof pages 1-2, yan2005apairof pages 8-9)

These are research implementations rather than approved clinical or industrial applications of Q9DFH2.

## 10. Evidence-weighted functional annotation

**Recommended primary annotation:**

> **Sox9a is a nuclear SoxE-family, HMG-box sequence-specific transcription factor that drives chondrocyte differentiation, cartilage extracellular-matrix gene expression, and morphogenetic organization in zebrafish, with essential roles in neurocranial, pharyngeal-arch, and scapulocoracoid cartilage.**

**Additional supported roles:**

- contributes redundantly with Sox9b to otic and selected skeletal programs;
- supports formation of cartilage-replacement skeletal elements through its effects on cartilage templates;
- is expressed in gonadal support-cell precursors and mature Sertoli cells and is associated with juvenile ovary-to-testis remodeling;
- functions within BMP-, MAPK-, and other developmental signaling contexts, chiefly as a transcriptional effector or cell-state marker rather than a ligand, receptor, enzyme, or transporter.

**Subcellular localization:** principally **nuclear**; regulated cytoplasmic accumulation has been reported in gonadal transition but has less firmly established functional meaning. (sun2013sox9relatedsignalingcontrols pages 1-2)

**Evidence confidence:**

- **High:** identity, SoxE/HMG transcription-factor classification, requirement for craniofacial and scapulocoracoid cartilage, chondrocyte stacking/differentiation, severe CRISPR mutant phenotype.
- **Moderate:** regulation of *col2a1* and early neural-crest/chondrogenic genes; redundant otic role; Sertoli/support-cell involvement.
- **Limited or inferential:** exact genome-wide direct targets, Sox9a-specific enhancer occupancy in zebrafish, biochemical meaning of cytoplasmic Sox9a, and direct molecular connections to BMP/FGF/Wnt/IGF pathways.
