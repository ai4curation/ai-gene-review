| Identity and domains | Q9DFH2 corresponds to *Danio rerio sox9a* (*jellyfish/jef*), one of two teleost SOX9 co-orthologs. The protein is a SoxE transcription factor containing an HMG DNA-binding domain and a glutamine/serine-rich transactivation region. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001) | Comparative genomics, domain annotation, and frameshift alleles predicted to remove both functional regions. | **High for identity and domain architecture.** Functional partitioning after duplication means findings for zebrafish *sox9b* or mammalian SOX9 cannot automatically be assigned to Sox9a. |
| Molecular function and localization | Sox9a is a sequence-specific developmental transcription factor; its HMG box supports DNA binding, and transcriptional activity is principally nuclear. Nuclear Sox9a was observed in gonadal cells, although cytoplasmic accumulation was also reported during ovary–testis transition. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000009) | Conserved domain/function, transcriptional perturbation studies, and immunolocalization during gonadal transformation. | **High for transcription-factor classification; moderate for localization.** No zebrafish Sox9a-specific genome-wide binding map or purified-protein DNA-binding study was identified. The proposed function of cytoplasmic Sox9a is less established than its nuclear role. |
| Craniofacial cartilage | Sox9a is required for differentiation and morphogenesis of cranial chondrocytes. Mutants retain prechondrogenic condensations but fail to form normal stacked chondrocytes; strong CRISPR loss-of-function mutants lack all neurocranial and most pharyngeal-arch cartilage and die before 14 dpf. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000011) | Null/hypomorphic *jef* mutants, single- and double-paralog comparisons, two independent CRISPR frameshift alleles, Alcian-blue staining, and marker analysis. | **High, direct causal evidence.** Sox9b contributes overlapping but distinct functions, particularly chondrocyte number; the severe Sox9a phenotype should not be generalized to Sox9b. |
| Pectoral fin and girdle | Sox9a is especially required for the proximal pectoral skeleton: mutants lack or severely disrupt scapulocoracoid cartilage, whereas much of the endoskeletal disc is preserved. Early *shh* and *dlx2a* signaling-center expression remains nearly normal. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000004) | Mutant skeletal staining and in-situ expression at 34–52 hpf; Sox9a is expressed in scapulocoracoid precursors at approximately 48 hpf. | **High for the structural requirement; moderate for pathway interpretation.** Results place Sox9a downstream of, or parallel to, early fin-bud patterning centers rather than proving a direct pathway connection. |
| Ear / otic development | Sox9a is expressed strongly in the otic placode near 14 hpf, but the ear phenotype of *sox9a* single mutants is mild or nearly normal. Severe ear loss emerges primarily when both *sox9a* and *sox9b* are impaired. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000010) | Embryonic expression mapping and comparison of single- and double-mutant phenotypes. | **Moderate.** Sox9a participates in a redundant Sox9 program in the ear, but Sox9b carries a larger or compensating role; Sox9a alone is not the dominant otic determinant. |
| Gonad | Sox9a is associated with male-support-cell differentiation. In 2024 single-cell data it was strongly expressed in a 30-dpf ZZ Sertoli-cell cluster and detected earlier in support-cell precursors at 19 dpf; gonadotropin/MAPK experiments also linked Sox9a localization to stromal activation and juvenile ovary–testis transformation. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009) | Single-cell RNA-seq of chromosomal ZZ/ZW zebrafish; juvenile gonad culture, immunolocalization, and MAPK inhibition with PD98059 plus U0216. | **Moderate for biological involvement; low-to-moderate for causal necessity.** The 2024 study uses *sox9a* as a Sertoli marker and did not perturb it. The earlier pharmacology is pathway-level evidence, not a Sox9a-specific knockout or rescue. |
| Targets and pathways | Reduced *col2a1* expression and defective chondrogenesis track with loss of Sox9a; early neural-crest genes including *foxd3*, *sox10*, *snai1b*, and *crestin* also respond to Sox9 perturbation. *runx2b* is comparatively preserved in *sox9a* mutants. Sox9a additionally intersects BMP, IGF, Wnt/β-catenin, FGF, and MAPK-associated developmental contexts. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005) | Mutant and gain-of-function expression assays, cartilage-marker analysis, and pathway perturbations in related zebrafish models. | **High for regulation of the chondrogenic program; limited for direct targets.** Co-expression and mutant-dependent expression support *col2a1* regulation, but zebrafish Sox9a-specific enhancer occupancy was not demonstrated in the retrieved evidence. Most pathway links position *sox9a* as an affected marker or downstream effector rather than proving direct biochemical interaction. |
| Recent 2024 interpretation | Current work reinforces subfunctionalization of *sox9a* and *sox9b* and expands the use of *sox9a* as a readout of chondrocyte state, regeneration, craniofacial signaling, and Sertoli-cell identity. The clearest new gene-specific result is strong *sox9a* expression in differentiated ZZ Sertoli cells at 30 dpf. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000010) | Recent reviews, disease-model pathway studies, regeneration studies, and gonadal single-cell transcriptomics. | **Moderate as contemporary support, but mostly observational.** Foundational mutant and 2021 CRISPR studies remain stronger evidence for primary function than most 2024 reports, which generally measure *sox9a* as a marker rather than perturbing Q9DFH2 directly. |
