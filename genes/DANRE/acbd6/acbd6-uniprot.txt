CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37951597}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9BR61}. Nucleus
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9BR61}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Highly expressed in the central nervous system,
CC       developing eyes, otic vesicle, and trunk muscles.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37951597}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Broadly expressed at 24 hours post-fertilization.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37951597}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Knockout animals, generated by CRISP-Cas
CC       technology, show an exaggerated response after lights are turned off, a
CC       reduced locomotor activity in dark period, light-induced seizure-like
CC       behavior, and excessive axonal arborizations and progressive
CC       degeneration of muscle fibers. Brain size is significantly reduced
CC       compared to controls, and musculature is disrupted. Proteomics analysis
CC       shows increased protein N-myristoylation in crispant animals.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37951597}.
