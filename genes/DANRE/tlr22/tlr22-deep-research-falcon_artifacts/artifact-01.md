| Matsuo et al. (2008), *Teleost TLR22 recognizes RNA duplex to induce IFN and protect cells from birnaviruses* | Japanese pufferfish (*Takifugu rubripes*) | Heterologous TLR22 expression, dsRNA stimulation, interferon-reporter assays, and birnavirus infection/protection assays | Demonstrated that teleost TLR22 responds to RNA duplexes, induces type-I interferon activity, and increases cellular resistance to birnavirus infection—the strongest direct functional evidence for TLR22 as an antiviral dsRNA receptor. (pqac-00000021, pqac-00000033, pqac-00000035) | Establishes the principal functional hypothesis for zebrafish TLR22 by teleost orthology, but does not directly test A0A2R8RTN4. |
| Matsuo et al. (2008), dsRNA-length experiments | Japanese pufferfish (*Takifugu rubripes*) | TLR22 expression followed by stimulation with poly(I:C) or dsRNA molecules of different lengths; interferon activity measured as the signaling output | TLR22-mediated interferon activity was dose- and length-dependent, with an approximately 1,000-bp dsRNA producing maximal activity; this supports preferential sensing of long dsRNA. (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000033) | Supports annotation of zebrafish TLR22 as a putative long-dsRNA sensor, although zebrafish-specific ligand-length thresholds and direct binding remain untested. |
| IPNV-dsRNA functional assay summarized by Aoki et al. (2015) | Japanese pufferfish receptor tested in rainbow-trout gonadal RTG-2 cells | Expression of pufferfish TLR22 in fish cells, exposure to the dsRNA genome of infectious pancreatic necrosis virus, measurement of interferon induction, and viral-resistance testing | Viral dsRNA increased type-I interferon production in TLR22-expressing cells and conferred greater resistance to IPNV, connecting ligand sensing to a biologically relevant antiviral phenotype. (pqac-00000033) | Provides mechanistic support for an antiviral role but uses another species’ receptor in a heterologous cell system; extrapolation to zebrafish should therefore be labeled comparative evidence. |
| Zebrafish developmental-expression study summarized by Gurupalli (2020) | Zebrafish (*Danio rerio*) | Embryonic and larval transcript profiling from before 1 through 7 days post-fertilization | *tlr22* transcript abundance was relatively low before 1 dpf and subsequently stabilized; this timing overlaps the emergence of macrophages and neutrophils and is consistent with an innate-immune role. (pqac-00000018) | Directly supports expression of zebrafish *tlr22* during innate-immune development, but does not establish ligand specificity, receptor localization, or pathway function. |
| Zebrafish infection-expression evidence summarized by Ghani et al. (2024) | Zebrafish (*Danio rerio*) | Infection-associated transcriptional profiling following *Mycobacterium* challenge | Zebrafish *tlr22* expression increased during mycobacterial infection, showing that the gene is responsive to infectious or inflammatory conditions. (pqac-00000020) | Direct zebrafish evidence for infection-responsive transcription, but upregulation alone does not prove that TLR22 directly recognizes mycobacterial molecules. |
| Zebrafish evolutionary/structural modeling summarized by Sousa et al. (2022) | Zebrafish (*Danio rerio*) | Comparative sequence analysis, positive-selection analysis, and ectodomain structural modeling | Selected changes in LRR motifs were associated with a flattened horseshoe-shaped ectodomain and interpreted as adaptation toward sensing long dsRNA. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000038) | Provides zebrafish-specific structural and evolutionary support for long-dsRNA recognition, but remains inferential rather than a direct ligand-binding or loss-of-function demonstration. |
| Current evidence gap for A0A2R8RTN4 | Zebrafish (*Danio rerio*) | Assessment of available receptor-specific literature | No identified study directly demonstrated binding of purified zebrafish TLR22 to dsRNA, tested A0A2R8RTN4 by knockout or rescue, or established its adaptor usage and subcellular localization in zebrafish cells. Comparative summaries associate fish TLR22 with dsRNA/poly(I:C), but do not show that every result was obtained in zebrafish. (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000037) | Functional annotation should distinguish direct zebrafish expression and modeling evidence from stronger ligand-recognition experiments performed with pufferfish TLR22. |
