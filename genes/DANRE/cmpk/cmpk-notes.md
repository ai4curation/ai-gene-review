# cmpk review notes

- Curated in DANRE batch 04. Cmpk is a straightforward pyrimidine nucleotide kinase. I accepted UMP/CMP/dCMP kinase and UDP/CDP biosynthetic process annotations, and modified broad phosphotransferase/kinase process terms to the specific pyrimidine kinase activities where appropriate.
- Follow-up: changed the de novo pyrimidine BP annotation from cross-aspect MODIFY to REMOVE after ai4c-agent review.

## Re-review 2026-09-29
- The previous review cited zero PMIDs; every row was supported only by the same four UniProt fragments plus the deep-research file. Cached and cited four primary papers on the human ortholog CMPK1 (the ISS/IBA donor): PMID:11912132, PMID:10462544, PMID:12694191, PMID:23416111. A PubMed search for zebrafish cmpk / UMP-CMP kinase returns no primary zebrafish papers, so all functional evidence is ortholog-based and is labelled as such in each reason.
- Resolved the two PENDING rows: IBA GO:0005737 cytoplasm (node PTN000599576, the broader NMP-kinase node; zebrafish cmpk is itself among the descendants seeding it) -> ACCEPT; IBA GO:0033862 UMP kinase activity (node PTN000599579) -> ACCEPT.
- Substrate-specific rows now rest on the ortholog enzymology rather than on the UniProt FUNCTION line alone: [PMID:11912132 "UMP and CMP proved to be far better substrates than dCMP"] and [PMID:10462544 "the recombinant UMP-CMP kinase phosphorylated CMP, dCMP, and UMP with highest efficiency and dUMP, AMP, and dAMP with lower efficiency"]. The UDP/CDP process rows cite [PMID:11912132 "plays a crucial role in the formation of UDP, CDP, and dCDP, which are required for cellular nucleic acid synthesis"] — the gene product itself catalyses the diphosphate-forming step, so it passes the participation test.
- GO:0004550 nucleoside diphosphate kinase activity (IEA GO_REF:0000003 and ISS from P30085) kept as KEEP_AS_NON_CORE but now argued from evidence: the human CMPK1 IDA for this term traces to [PMID:23416111 "in addition to the diphosphate products, a nucleoside diphosphate kinase (NDPK) activity was also present with subsequent triphosphates formed"], while [PMID:12694191 "in the reverse reaction, the enzyme did not catalyse the addition of the third phosphate to l-3TCDP, which was rather an inhibitor"] shows the activity is promiscuous and substrate-limited. So the transfer is sound but the activity is secondary.
- GO:0005524 ATP binding IEA KEEP_AS_NON_CORE retained, now justified from the LID-domain mechanism rather than boilerplate.
- Localization rows (nucleus and cytoplasm, IEA and IBA) ACCEPTed, recording honestly that the two ortholog studies disagree on the dominant compartment: [PMID:11912132 "UMP/CMPK localized predominantly to the cytoplasm"] versus the HAMAP note "Predominantly nuclear."; [PMID:10462544 "the fusion protein was located in the cytosol and nucleus"] supports both compartments. This discrepancy is now a suggested question.
- GO:0006207 'de novo' pyrimidine nucleobase biosynthetic process REMOVE retained, with the argument tied to the verified term definition (formation of the pyrimidine ring from simple precursors): cmpk acts only downstream of UMP and performs no nucleobase-forming step. GO:0006221 remains ACCEPT as the correct process level.
- MODIFY retained for the three overly broad MF mappings (GO:0016776, GO:0019205, GO:0050145 -> GO:0033862/GO:0036430/GO:0036431), consistent across the three rows.
- Deep-research file demoted to its single non-redundant contribution: the cmpk1/cmpk2 paralog disambiguation ["zebrafish Q7ZWE9 should **not** be automatically assumed to be mitochondrial like CMPK2"], attached to the cytoplasm IEA row; its other paraphrases of UniProt were dropped.
- Rewrote description as standalone biology; core_functions supporting quotes replaced with reaction and kinetics evidence. Validation: zero errors, zero warnings.
