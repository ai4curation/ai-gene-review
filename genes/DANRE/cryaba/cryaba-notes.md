# cryaba (Danio rerio) review notes

Zebrafish alpha-crystallin B chain a (alphaB1-crystallin / alphaBa-crystallin), UniProt Q9PUR2 (TrEMBL). The entry has no FUNCTION and no SUBCELLULAR LOCATION line, only the small heat shock protein family assignment, so all functional evidence comes from primary zebrafish literature.

## Re-review 2026-09-29
- Reconciled the yaml against the refreshed GOA. Dropped the two obsoleted GO:0051082 unfolded protein binding rows (IBA and IDA), the GO:0046872 metal ion binding IEA row and a duplicate GO:0005212 IEA row under GO_REF:0000120, none of which GOA now carries; resolved the two PENDING rows, GO:0005212 IEA (GO_REF:0000002, InterPro:IPR003090) and GO:0140309 unfolded protein holdase activity IDA (PMID:15692462), both to ACCEPT.
- As for cryaa, GO:0140309 is accepted rather than replaced by a bespoke NTR: the term's carrier/escort definition sits awkwardly on a small heat shock protein, but GO lists "holdase" among its synonyms and GOA migrated the gene onto it, so the mismatch is recorded as a suggested question and proposed_new_terms was dropped.
- Corrected a paralog mix-up in the description. The previous text said cryaba is lens-specific and then attributed to it "lower expression in heart, brain, skeletal muscle, and liver (PMID:16420472)" - but that expression pattern belongs to alphaB2 (cryabb). The paper states the opposite for this gene [PMID:16420472 "not constitutively expressed in nervous or muscular tissue and has reduced chaperone-like activity"] and concludes [PMID:16420472 "while zebrafish alphaB1 adopted a more restricted, nonchaperone role in the lens"].
- That correction drives the grading of the four PMID:25866181 morpholino rows, which come from a screen of human myofibrillar myopathy gene orthologs. GO:0007519 skeletal muscle tissue development and GO:0030239 myofibril assembly remain KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, on the grounds that chaperoning sarcomeric clients is a real participation mechanism for an sHSP (it is the basis of human CRYAB myopathy), but with the morphant and paralog-expression caveats stated. GO:0060047 heart contraction and GO:0007626 locomotory behavior changed KEEP_AS_NON_CORE -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED: contraction is performed by the sarcomere and behaviour by the whole animal, so both are readouts of the structural defect already captured, two steps removed from the molecular activity [PMID:25866181 "targeted ablation of MFM genes in zebrafish led to compromised skeletal muscle function mostly due to myofibrillar degeneration as well as severe heart failure"]. No row was removed: the curator read the full text and the morpholino is recorded in the annotation.
- GO:0036438 maintenance of lens transparency IMP ACCEPT, now grounded in the null-mutant result [PMID:38705506 "loss of the αBa-crystallin gene cryaba led to an increase in lens opacity compared to cryaa null fish at 24 months of age"] with the attribution control [PMID:38705506 "None of the three α-crystallin mutants showed a compensatory increase in the expression of the remaining two crystallins"].
- GO:0140309 holdase ACCEPTed as a core function with an explicit quantitative caveat: [PMID:15692462 "The chaperone-like activities of the two zebrafish alpha-crystallins were highly divergent, with alphaA-crystallin showing much greater activity than alphaB-crystallin"]. The in vivo cataract result is what justifies treating the weaker activity as functionally central for this paralog.
- GO:0005886 plasma membrane IDA ACCEPT -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. The row comes from a paper about lengsin (PMID:18406404) whose cached record is abstract-only; per the do-not-overrule rule the ZFIN curator's reading is accepted and the row kept, but membrane association is not treated as a core location, and plasma membrane was removed from core_functions.
- GO:0009408 response to heat IBA ACCEPT -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE and GO:0042026 protein refolding IBA MODIFY -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED, with the same arguments and propagation_reviews used for cryaa (node PTN000897708 is seeded by inducible invertebrate sHSPs; holdases do not perform the refolding step).
- Added reference_review to all five PMIDs, including a note on PMID:16420472 recording the previous misattribution, and kept one deep-research statement: the proteomics confirmation that an alphaBa-crystallin peptide present in wild-type adult lenses is absent in cryaba nulls.
