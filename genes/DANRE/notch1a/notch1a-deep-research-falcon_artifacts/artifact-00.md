| Molecular feature | Zebrafish Notch1a annotation | Functional significance | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protein identity and type | Notch1a (UniProt P46530) is a precursor, single-pass transmembrane Notch-family receptor and a zebrafish paralogue of mammalian NOTCH1. | Functions as a contact-dependent signal transducer rather than an enzyme or transporter. | (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000018) |
| Extracellular architecture | The ectodomain contains numerous EGF and calcium-binding EGF-like modules plus a negative regulatory region; the EGF modules provide the ligand-binding surface and undergo O-linked glycosylation. | Supports ligand recognition while keeping the receptor proteolytically inactive until mechanically activated. | (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000007) |
| Intracellular architecture | NICD contains a RAM region, ankyrin repeats, nuclear-localization signals, transcriptional activation sequences and a C-terminal PEST region. | RAM and ankyrin elements assemble the nuclear complex; nuclear-localization signals direct nuclear entry, while the PEST region regulates NICD turnover. | (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000018) |
| Ligands, not catalytic substrates | Membrane-bound Delta/Serrate/Lag-2-family ligands on adjacent cells activate Notch1a. Zebrafish candidates include DeltaA, DeltaC, DeltaD and Dll4, as well as Jag1a, Jag1b and Jag2a. DeltaA–Notch1a is specifically implicated in retinal progenitor proliferation. | Notch1a has ligand selectivity but no enzyme-like substrate specificity. Signaling requires contact between a ligand-bearing sender cell and receptor-bearing receiver cell. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000014, pqac-00000029) |
| Ligand-recognition module | Conserved Notch receptors bind ligands principally through an EGF-repeat module; structural work on mammalian NOTCH1 identifies EGF11–13 as the main ligand-binding region. | This is a strong family-based inference for zebrafish Notch1a, but a Notch1a-specific binding structure is not documented in the cited evidence. | (pqac-00000007) |
| Glycan-dependent ligand tuning | O-fucose, O-glucose and related modifications are added to extracellular EGF repeats; Fringe-dependent extension of O-fucose can alter Delta-versus-Jagged responsiveness. | Glycosylation tunes ligand affinity and signaling strength, although precise ligand-specific effects remain incompletely measured for zebrafish Notch1a. | (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000007) |
| Biosynthetic S1 processing | During passage through the Golgi, a Furin-family convertase performs ligand-independent S1 cleavage. The extracellular and transmembrane/intracellular portions remain noncovalently associated as a mature heterodimer. | Produces the signaling-competent receptor delivered to the cell surface; S1 cleavage does not itself generate NICD. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000018) |
| Mechanical activation | Ligand ubiquitination and endocytosis in the sender cell exert pulling force on the receptor–ligand complex. This promotes trans-endocytosis of the Notch extracellular domain and unfolds the negative regulatory region. | Converts cell contact and ligand endocytosis into exposure of the normally protected S2 cleavage site. | (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000005) |
| S2 cleavage | An ADAM-family metalloprotease—predominantly ADAM10 under physiological conditions—cleaves the exposed juxtamembrane S2 site. | Removes the extracellular portion and generates the membrane-retained Notch extracellular truncation, NEXT. | (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000005) |
| S3 intramembrane cleavage | The γ-secretase complex, containing catalytic Presenilin together with Nicastrin, APH-1 and PEN-2, cleaves NEXT at the intramembrane S3 site. | Releases the Notch1a intracellular domain, NICD, from the membrane. DAPT, RO4929097 and MK0752 inhibit this activation step. | (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000013) |
| Localization before activation | Mature full-length Notch1a is localized principally at the plasma membrane of the receiving cell, with its ectodomain exposed extracellularly. | Positions the receptor to detect ligands displayed by immediately adjacent cells; signaling is therefore juxtacrine and short-range. | (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010) |
| Localization after activation | S3 cleavage releases NICD, which translocates from the membrane to the nucleus; no stable cytoplasmic signaling pool is established by the cited zebrafish evidence. | Spatially couples ligand reception at the plasma membrane to transcriptional regulation in the nucleus. | (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000011) |
| Nuclear DNA-binding partners | NICD binds CSL/RBP-Jκ-family factors, represented in zebrafish by Rbpja and Rbpjb. | NICD converts DNA-bound RBPJ from a repressive state into an activating transcriptional complex. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018) |
| Nuclear coactivators and corepressors | Mastermind-like coactivators Maml1–3 and additional coactivators join the NICD–RBPJ complex. In the absence of NICD, factors including NCOR1/2 and SPEN contribute to repression. | Explains how proteolytic release of NICD directly changes the transcriptional state of Notch-responsive genes. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000018) |
| Principal downstream gene class | Canonical outputs include hairy/enhancer-of-split-related **her** and **hey** genes encoding basic helix–loop–helix repressors. Zebrafish examples include **her2**, **her4**, **her6**, **her4.1**, **her8a**, **her9** and **hey1**. | These repressors regulate progenitor maintenance, quiescence, proliferation and differentiation; not every listed target is proven to be controlled exclusively by Notch1a rather than another Notch receptor. | (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018) |
| Strongest Notch1a-linked target evidence | In retinal regeneration, the DeltaA–Notch1a axis supports neuronal-progenitor proliferation, and **her4** is required to generate or maintain the proliferating progenitor population. | Provides a receptor-linked functional connection from Notch1a activation to a canonical Her-family output in zebrafish tissue. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000021) |
| Context-dependent downstream readouts | Notch1a intracellular-domain expression increases **agr2**-positive and **pvalb8**-positive epidermal-cell populations; Notch inhibition can increase **ascl1a** expression in injured retina. | These are experimentally responsive markers or downstream effects, but direct NICD–RBPJ regulation of **agr2**, **pvalb8** or **ascl1a** is not established by the cited studies. | (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000014, pqac-00000020, pqac-00000024) |
| Overall molecular function | Notch1a converts a membrane-bound Delta/Jagged signal from a neighboring cell into a nuclear NICD–RBPJ–MAML response through force-dependent exposure and sequential S2/S3 proteolysis. | Its primary role is local control of cell state and fate; signaling amplitude, duration, ligand identity, glycosylation and receptor redundancy determine the biological output. | (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000015) |
