CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the NADPH-dependent reduction of several pentoses,
CC       tetroses, trioses, alpha-dicarbonyl compounds and L-xylulose
CC       (PubMed:11882650). Can use both NAD and NADP as cosubstrate but shows
CC       higher activity with NADP (PubMed:11882650). Participates in the
CC       uronate cycle of glucose metabolism (PubMed:11882650). May play a role
CC       in the water absorption and cellular osmoregulation in the proximal
CC       renal tubules by producing xylitol, an osmolyte, thereby preventing
CC       osmolytic stress from occurring in the renal tubules (PubMed:11882650).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11882650, ECO:0000303|PubMed:11882650}.
