Gene Description

grk7a (also called GRK7-1) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate GRK7, the cone opsin kinase; its paralog grk7b arose in the teleost-specific genome duplication. It is a G protein-coupled receptor kinase of the AGC serine/threonine kinase family, with an N-terminal RGS-homology domain, a central protein kinase domain and a C-terminal CAAX motif for geranylgeranyl membrane anchoring. grk7a is expressed in all cone photoreceptor subtypes and is the predominant Grk7 protein of the larval retina; it is localized to cone outer segments. Recombinant Grk7a phosphorylates light-activated rhodopsin with a Vmax far higher than that of the rod kinase Grk1a or of its paralog Grk7b. By phosphorylating light-activated cone opsins it initiates their shut-off, and loss of grk7a (morpholino knockdown or knockout) slows recovery of the cone photoresponse and lowers temporal contrast sensitivity; cone Grk1b shares this role, so knockout effects are modest. Grk7a is phosphorylated in a cAMP/PKA-dependent manner in the dark, and it mediates the slowing of cone recovery caused by raised cAMP. grk7a is also expressed in the pineal organ, where it is the main kinase that inactivates the photoproduct of the bistable opsin parapinopsin.

Core Functions

Cone opsin kinase: phosphorylates light-activated cone opsins (and rhodopsin in vitro) at high rate in the cone outer segment, initiating opsin shut-off and recovery of the cone photoresponse; its activity is down-regulated by cAMP/PKA-dependent phosphorylation. The same kinase inactivates the parapinopsin photoproduct in pineal photoreceptors.

description: >-
  grk7a (also called GRK7-1) is one of two zebrafish co-orthologs of vertebrate GRK7, the
  cone opsin kinase; its paralog grk7b arose in the teleost-specific genome duplication.
  It is a G protein-coupled receptor kinase of the AGC serine/threonine kinase family,
  with an N-terminal RGS-homology domain, a central protein kinase domain and a
  C-terminal CAAX motif for geranylgeranyl membrane anchoring. grk7a is expressed in all
  cone photoreceptor subtypes and is the predominant Grk7 protein of the larval retina; it
  is localized to cone outer segments. Recombinant Grk7a phosphorylates light-activated
  rhodopsin with a Vmax far higher than that of the rod kinase Grk1a or of its paralog
  Grk7b. By phosphorylating light-activated cone opsins it initiates their shut-off,
  and loss of grk7a (morpholino knockdown or knockout) slows recovery of the cone
  photoresponse and lowers temporal contrast sensitivity; cone Grk1b shares this role, so
  knockout effects are modest. Grk7a is phosphorylated in a cAMP/PKA-dependent manner in
  the dark, and it mediates the slowing of cone recovery caused by raised cAMP. grk7a is
  also expressed in the pineal organ, where it is the main kinase that inactivates the
  photoproduct of the bistable opsin parapinopsin.
