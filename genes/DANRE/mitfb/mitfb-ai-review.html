Gene Description

mitfb encodes microphthalmia-associated transcription factor b, a basic helix-loop-helix leucine zipper (bHLH-LZ) transcription factor of the MiT/TFE family and the second zebrafish co-ortholog of human MITF, retained from the teleost genome duplication alongside mitfa. The 500-residue protein has the MiT/TFE N-terminal region, a bHLH-LZ DNA-binding and dimerization domain and the MiT/TFE C-terminal region, with an N-terminal extension that resembles the RPE-type (A/H) isoforms of mammalian MITF rather than the melanocyte M isoform. mitfb is co-expressed with mitfa in the retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) and is also expressed in the epiphysis and olfactory bulb, but is not expressed in neural crest melanoblasts. The protein keeps the melanogenic activity of the family: expressed from the mitfa promoter it rescues neural crest melanophores in nacre/mitfa mutants. Endogenously, mitfb null fish have no obvious phenotype, mitfa;mitfb double mutants still have a normal RPE (RPE pigmentation requires mitfa, mitfb and tfec together), and loss of mitfb enhances the xanthophore deficit of mitfa mutants, most clearly in adult fins. mitfb therefore acts mainly as a partially redundant MiT factor in zebrafish; in medaka the mitfb co-ortholog has the more central role in melanophore development.

Core Functions

mitfb is a MiT/TFE bHLH-LZ transcription factor that keeps the melanogenic DNA-binding transcription factor activity of the ancestral Mitf (it can replace mitfa in melanophores). Its endogenous zebrafish roles are partially redundant: with mitfa it supports xanthophore development, and with mitfa and tfec it supports RPE pigmentation.
