### Findings with provenance

- Discovery and expression: [PMID:11543618 "mitfb is coexpressed with mitfa in the RPE at an
  appropriate time to compensate for loss of mitfa function in the nacre mutant but is not
  expressed in neural crest melanoblasts"]; [PMID:11543618 "mitfb is expressed in the
  epiphysis and olfactory bulb where mitfa is not"].
- Protein-level capacity: [PMID:11543618 "mitfb, but not a zebrafish ortholog of the closely
  related gene tfe3, can rescue neural crest melanophore development in nacre/mitfa mutant
  embryos when expressed via the mitfa promoter"].
- Isoform correspondence: [PMID:11543618 "The proteins encoded by the two zebrafish mitf genes
  appear homologous to distinct isoforms generated by alternately spliced mRNAs of the single
  mammalian Mitf gene"]; [PMID:12019239 "Fish proteins MITF-m and MITF-b correspond at both the
  structural and the expression levels to one particular bird/mammalian MITF isoform."]
- Loss of function: two ENU nonsense alleles, hu3857 (exon 2) and hu3561 (exon 7, bHLH-LZ):
  [PMID:23139843 "no obvious abnormalities in homozygous mitfb embryos or larvae were observed"];
  double mutants with mitfa: [PMID:23139843 "the loss of Mitf activity in mitfa, mitfb, or double
  mitf mutant zebrafish had no effect on RPE pigmentation or development"].
- Xanthophores: [PMID:37823232 "Compared with a single mitfa mutant, mitfa−/−; mitfb−/− double
  mutants showed a slightly more severe reduction of xanthophores"]; independently
  [PMID:40123122 "These phenotypes suggest that mitfb partially compensates for the loss of mitfa
  (though not tfec) in xanthophores and especially so in the fins."]
- Lineage-specific resolution: in medaka [PMID:37823232 "suggesting that mitfb, but not mitfa,
  plays the more central role in melanophore development in medaka"]; and
  [PMID:40123122 "Only in zebrafish has one Mitf locus, mitfb, undergone a substantial, though
  not complete (Figure 5b) reduction in pigmentary function"].
- RPE redundancy with tfec (abstract only): [PMID:42663426 "simultaneous null mutations in all
  three genes cause a complete loss of all four chromatophore types and lead to defective
  pigmentation of the retinal pigmented epithelium (RPE)"].
