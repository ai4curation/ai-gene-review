why
InterPro identifies a glycoside hydrolase superfamily domain (residues 123–582); the 99.90%-identity STRING homolog belongs to COG0366, glycosidase/amylase (phosphorylase). Mutations confer strong benefits under tungstate stress and defects under molybdate stress and several nitrogen-source conditions. Query topology predicts a cleavable signal peptide without transmembrane segments, supporting export. No characterized substrate, EC number, or definitive hydrolytic versus phosphorolytic activity is supplied.
Why this tier
Tier A is met by replicated, condition-specific molybdate/tungstate fitness phenotypes in a 99.90%-identity homolog, although these establish physiological involvement rather than a specific catalytic reaction. InterPro and STRING converge on glycosidase/amylase family membership; the 36.70%-identity experimental homolog provides only a starvation phenotype, and no cofitness or substrate-specific biochemical characterization is available. A specific glycogen-degrading enzyme or metal transporter annotation is unsupported: association with glycogen synthase does not establish substrate specificity, and the query is predicted to be soluble and exported.
