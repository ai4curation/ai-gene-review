CC   -!- FUNCTION: Aerial growth, conidiation, and dispersal of filamentous
CC       fungi in the environment depend on their ability to secrete small
CC       amphipathic proteins called hydrophobins (HPBs) with low sequence
CC       identity. Class I can self-assemble into an outermost layer of rodlet
CC       bundles on aerial cell surfaces, conferring cellular hydrophobicity
CC       that supports fungal growth, development and dispersal; whereas Class
CC       II form highly ordered films at water-air interfaces through
CC       intermolecular interactions but contribute nothing to the rodlet
CC       structure (Probable). MPG1 is a class I hydrophobin that directs
CC       formation of a rodlet layer on conidia composed of interwoven 5 nm
CC       rodlets, which contributes to their surface hydrophobicity
CC       (PubMed:12239409). Plays a role in the elaboration of infective
CC       structures by fungi and may fulfill other functions in fungal
CC       phytopathogenesis (PubMed:8312740). Required for host recognition and
CC       acts as a morphogenetic signal for efficient induction of appressoria
CC       in response to a host surface (PubMed:8755621).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12239409, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8312740,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8755621, ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Self-assembles to form functional amyloid fibrils called
CC       rodlets. Self-assembly into fibrillar rodlets occurs spontaneously at
CC       hydrophobic:hydrophilic interfaces and the rodlets further associate
CC       laterally to form amphipathic monolayers (PubMed:12239409,
CC       PubMed:15773983, PubMed:27142249). MPG1 assembly is modulated by
CC       surface active agents such as the class II hydrophobin MHP1
CC       (PubMed:27142249). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12239409,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15773983, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27142249}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12239409,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15773983}. Secreted, cell wall
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12239409, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15773983}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Highly expressed very early in plant infection concomitant
CC       with appressorium formation and also abundant at the time of symptom
CC       development (PubMed:8312740). Also expressed during conidiation and in
CC       mycelial cultures starved for nitrogen or carbon (PubMed:8312740). The
CC       MAP kinase-encoding gene PMK1 and the wide domain regulators of
CC       nitrogen source utilization, NPR1 and NUT1, are required for full
CC       expression of MPG1 in response to starvation stress (PubMed:12481998).
CC       The CPKA gene, encoding the catalytic subunit of protein kinase A, is
CC       required for repression of MPG1 during growth in rich nutrient
CC       conditions (PubMed:12481998). Expression is regulated by the
CC       transcriptional activator LDB1 (PubMed:20831406).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12481998, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20831406,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8312740}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Disulfide bonds are not becessary for self-assembly, but are
CC       required for secretion to the cell surface.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15773983}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Affects cell durface hydrophobicity and results
CC       in an 'easily wettable' phenotype (PubMed:8312740). Reduces the
CC       efficiency of appressorium formation (PubMed:8755621).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8312740, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8755621}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the fungal hydrophobin family. {ECO:0000305}.
