|---|---|---|
| **Identity and architecture** | Fungal **ACE1/MGG_12447** is a 12.4-kb gene encoding a 4,035-aa highly reducing PKS fused to one NRPS module. The reported domain order is **KS–AT–DH–CMeT–ER⁰–KR–ACP–C–A–T–R**; the embedded ER is predicted inactive, while adjacent **RAP1** supplies a trans-acting ER (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000040, pqac-00000058). | **High.** Primary sequence analysis and heterologous expression agree. **G4MVZ2 and strain 70-15** are fixed by the supplied UniProt identity; many functional experiments used Guy11 or related isolates. |
| **Precursor and substrate specificity** | Labeling with [1,2-¹³C₂]acetate established an acetate-unit-derived **heptaketide** for ACE1-only pyrone products. Labeling with [methyl-¹³C]methionine marked both methyl groups, with 9.4% incorporation at C-15 and C-16. With RAP1, NMR identified a **tyrosine-containing nonaketide amide**, supporting tyrosine selection by the NRPS adenylation module (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010). | **Moderate–high.** Polyketide and methyl origins are directly demonstrated. Tyrosine incorporation is structurally supported in the 2015 product, but native substrate kinetics were not measured. The **exogenous O-methyltyrosine used in 2021** concerned separate pathway-complementation experiments and should not be conflated with the 2015 tyrosine assignment (pqac-00000055). |
| **ACE1-only products** | ACE1 expressed without RAP1 produced the polyenyl pyrone **magnaporthepyrone**, subsequently converted in the expression hosts into **12,13-dihydroxymagnaporthepyrone (6)** or **10,11-dihydroxymagnaporthepyrone (7)** by inferred epoxidation and hydrolysis. Full-length ACE1 and an NRPS-deleted construct generated the same all-polyketide product (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009). | **High for structures; moderate for the enzymatic route.** Compounds 6 and 7 are characterized **shunt products**, not mature cytochalasans or the native avirulence signal. Host monooxygenase and epoxide-hydrolysis steps are inferred. |
| **ACE1 plus RAP1 product** | Co-expression redirected synthesis from pyrone shunts to **amide 8, C₃₀H₄₃NO₄**, a tyrosine-containing, nonaketide-derived linear product resembling a cytochalasan backbone. RAP1-mediated reduction is proposed to enable correct chain extension and methylation (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010). | **High for compound structure and RAP1 dependence; moderate for detailed programming.** Amide 8 is probably a reduced shunt metabolite, not a demonstrated native pathway endpoint. |
| **Pi33 bioassay** | Purified compounds 6 and 7 caused localized necrosis on susceptible and Pi33-containing cultivars irrespective of genotype; neither converted a virulent infection into an avirulent reaction. Amide 8 caused no symptoms and did not alter pathogenicity (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009). | **High.** Direct rice-leaf and infection assays exclude compounds 6, 7 and 8 as the Pi33-specific elicitor under the tested conditions. |
| **Cellular site and secretion** | A direct Ace1–eGFP fusion was reported to remain in fungal **appressoria** and was not detected in primary invasive hyphae. A separate promoter–eGFP reporter showed transcription peaking near penetration, with mRNA maximal around 17 hours after inoculation; reporter fluorescence in early hyphae was attributed to soluble GFP diffusion (pqac-00000047, pqac-00000057). A 2024 review classifies ACE1 as a **non-secreted** PKS–NRPS (pqac-00000020). | **Moderate.** Appressorial localization is supported, but fine subcellular localization and the metabolite-export route remain unresolved. Promoter-reporter fluorescence is not ACE1-protein localization. |
| **BIP1 regulation in 2024** | Expression profiling initially found **42** genes downregulated in Δbip1 appressoria; the authors summarized a curated network of **40**. Nine mapped to the **57-kb, 15-gene ACE1 cluster**. BIP1 bound TGACTC motifs in the shared RAP2 and ORF3 promoter; motif mutation weakened or abolished binding and ORF3 appressorium expression. ACE1/MGG_12447 and seven candidate cluster targets were downregulated during early barley infection (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000051, pqac-00000052). | **High for transcriptional regulation.** This establishes BIP1-dependent, penetration-stage cluster activation, not direct biochemical regulation of ACE1 catalysis. Deleting ACE1/SYN2 reportedly did not reduce general pathogenicity (pqac-00000038). |
| **Native elicitor remains unknown** | Cluster architecture, ACE1/RAP1 chemistry and cross-complementation of ACE1-cluster **ORFZ** and **ORF3** in a separate pyrichalasan pathway support a cytochalasan-like product. Nevertheless, the ACE1 cluster is active only briefly in appressoria, productive native knockout chemistry has been difficult, and no mature Pi33-eliciting molecule has been isolated (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000055, pqac-00000056). | **Moderate for a tyrosine-derived cytochalasan-like metabolite; low for an exact structure.** The defensible annotation is a PKS–NRPS core enzyme for a probable cytochalasan pathway, not a fully defined reaction yielding a named native end product. |
