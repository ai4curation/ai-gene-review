CC   -!- FUNCTION: Secreted effector that acts both as a host range determinant
CC       and a virulence factor (PubMed:40341381). Acts as an avirulence factor
CC       that prevents the fungus from infecting weeping lovegrass (Eragrostis
CC       curvula) (PubMed:37245238, PubMed:7549480). Barley (Hordeum vulgare)
CC       MLA3 also recognizes PWL2 and is able to confer resistance to the rice
CC       blast fungus Magnaporthe oryzae in a dosage-dependent manner
CC       (PubMed:37820736). Shows also a virulence function during a compatible
CC       interaction between the blast fungus and susceptible host plants
CC       (PubMed:38968126, PubMed:40341381). Suppresses host immunity by
CC       perturbing the plasmodesmatal deployment of the heavy metal-binding
CC       isoprenylated protein HIPP43 (PubMed:38968126, PubMed:40341381).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37245238, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37820736,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38968126, ECO:0000269|PubMed:40341381,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7549480}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with host HIPP43 (PubMed:38968126, PubMed:40341381).
CC       Interacts also with barley (Hordeum vulgare) MLA3 (PubMed:37820736).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37820736, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38968126,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40341381}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20435900,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26472068, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30182232,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31749824, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34236677,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38653755, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38722963}. Host
CC       cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20435900, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26472068,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30182232, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31749824,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34236677, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38653755}. Note=A
CC       highly localized structure, the biotrophic interfacial complex (BIC),
CC       accumulates effectors secreted by invasive hyphae (IH). In each newly
CC       entered rice cell, effectors are first secreted into BICs at the tips
CC       of the initially filamentous hyphae in the cell. These tip BICs are
CC       left behind beside the first-differentiated bulbous IH cells as the
CC       fungus continued to colonize the host cell. PWL2 continues to
CC       accumulate in BICs after IH were growing elsewhere. PWL2 then
CC       translocates into the rice cytoplasm (PubMed:20435900, PubMed:26472068,
CC       PubMed:30182232, PubMed:31749824, PubMed:34236677). Correct
CC       localization of PWL2 requires the small GTPase SEC4 (PubMed:27729922).
CC       The rice phytoalexin sakuranetin functions in resistance against PWL2
CC       by significantly decreasing translocation of PWL2 into rice cells
CC       (PubMed:38653755). Clathrin-heavy chain inhibitors endosidin-9 (ES9)
CC       and endosidin-9-17 (ES9-17) also block the internalization of the PWL2
CC       in rice cells (PubMed:38722963). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20435900,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26472068, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27729922,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30182232, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31749824,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34236677, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38653755,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38722963}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression is coupled with sequential biotrophic invasion of
CC       rice cells (PubMed:33694285, PubMed:40341381). PWL2 is induced in the
CC       appressorium upon penetration into a living rice cell but greatly
CC       declines in the highly branched hyphae when the first-invaded rice cell
CC       is dead. Expression then increases again as the hyphae penetrate into
CC       living adjacent cells (PubMed:33694285). PMK1 MAP kinase regulates PWL2
CC       expression during invasive growth (PubMed:40341381). The tandem repeats
CC       in the PWL2 promoter containing 12-base pair sequences are required for
CC       expression (PubMed:33694285). Finally, translation of AA-ending codon-
CC       rich messenger RNAs encoding unconventionally secreted cytoplasmic
CC       effectors such as PWL2 depends on the UBA4-URM1 sulfur relay system
CC       mediating tRNA anticodon wobble uridine 2-thiolation (PubMed:37563288).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33694285, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37563288,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:40341381}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Leads to a gain of virulence toward weeping
CC       lovegrass and barley MLA3 lines, but reduces blast disease severity on
CC       susceptible host plants. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:40341381}.
