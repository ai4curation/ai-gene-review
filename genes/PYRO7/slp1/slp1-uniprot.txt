CC       cells (PubMed:22267486). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22267486}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms homodimers. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22267486}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22267486}.
CC       Note=Accumulates at the plant-fungal interface during the early stages
CC       of rice blast infections. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22267486}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression is up-regulated during plant infection.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22267486}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The LysM (lysin motif) domains are small globular domains
CC       involved in binding chitin in eukaryotes. Slp1 contains 2 LysM domains.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22267486}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Leads to a significant reduction in virulence and
CC       a reduced proliferation of the fungus in plant tissue.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22267486}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: In plants, chitin acts as a microbe-associated molecular
CC       pattern (MAMP) that is recognized by lysin motif (LysM)-containing
CC       plant cell surface-localized pattern recognition receptors (PRRs) that
CC       activate a plethora of downstream immune responses. {ECO:0000305}.
