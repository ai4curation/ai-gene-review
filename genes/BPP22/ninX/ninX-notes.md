# Gene Review Notes: Salmonella phage P22 `ninX` (`Q38665`, Putative protein ninX)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `ninX`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q38665` (`NINX_BPP22`)
- **Organism**: Salmonella phage P22 (`NCBITaxon:10754`)
- **Protein length**: 112 amino acids (13.0 kDa)
- **Operon / genomic context**: Encoded in the early right ($P_R$) `nin` region between `ninE` and `ninF` ([PMID:11053393](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11053393/), [PMID:12562822](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12562822/)).

## Function and Domain Architecture
- **Uncharacterized P22-specific NinX family protein**:
  - Vander Byl & Kropinski (2000) [PMID:11053393] noted that unlike the canonical lambdoid `nin` genes (`ninB`, `ninD`, `ninE`, `ninF`, `ninG`, `ninH`), `ninX` is a non-lambdoid gene interspersed within the P22 `nin` mosaic:
    - `"The nin region shows genes which are clearly lambdoid interspersed with genes, such as ninX and ninY , which are not."`
  - Domain architecture in `ninX-uniprot.txt`: Founding member of `InterPro:IPR019701` (`Phage_P22_NinX`) and `Pfam:PF10765` (`Phage_P22_NinX`).
  - No biochemical or genetic function has yet been experimentally determined for `ninX`.
