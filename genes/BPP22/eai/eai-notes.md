# Curation Notes for Salmonella phage P22 `eai` (Q03550)

## Summary of Gene Product
`eai` encodes protein EaI (UniProt `Q03550`, 64 amino acids, 7.2 kDa) of *Salmonella* phage P22 [PMID:11053393]. Located in the early leftward (`P_L`) `ea` accessory region between `int` and `erf`, EaI was originally identified by SDS-PAGE as one of seven early proteins expressed in UV-irradiated *Salmonella* cells infected with phage P22 [PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822].

## Molecular Function, Biological Process, and Domain Context
- **Genomic context and dispensability**: The `ea` region (`int` to `erf`) is non-essential for P22 lytic propagation and lysogeny under standard laboratory conditions [PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822].
- **Conserved domain**: Sequence analysis in `file:genes/BPP22/eai/eai-uniprot.txt` classifies EaI into the conserved `Eai` / `Phage_P22_Eai` family (`IPR059721`, `PF26866`), which has no experimentally characterized biochemical or physiological function. Consequently, `core_functions` is left empty (`[]`).
