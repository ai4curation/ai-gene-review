CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required for procapsid assembly (PubMed:9792844). The
CC       interior of the prohead is filled with the scaffolding protein
CC       (PubMed:9792844). The scaffolding protein is lost from the structure
CC       during packaging (PubMed:9792844). Scaffolding protein exit is followed
CC       by the expansion of the procapsid into a mature capsid
CC       (PubMed:9792844). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9792844}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:9792844). Homotetramer (PubMed:9792844).
CC       Interacts with the portal protein; this interaction initiates procapsid
CC       assembly, thereby ensuring incorporation of only one portal ring per
CC       capsid (PubMed:30787152, PubMed:9792844). Interacts (via C-terminus)
CC       with the capsid protein; this interaction allow to form the procapsid,
CC       in which the scaffolding protein forms an internal shell in the
CC       icosahedrally arranged capsid protein subunits (PubMed:10764583,
CC       PubMed:36851569). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10764583,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30787152, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36851569,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9792844}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: The scaffolding protein negatively regulates its own
CC       synthesis when it is not assembled into proheads.
