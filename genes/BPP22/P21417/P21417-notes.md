- **Protein**: Uncharacterized 22.8 kDa protein in gp15-gp3 intergenic region (201 AA, 22.8 kDa)
- **Organism**: *Salmonella* phage P22 (`NCBITaxon:10754`)
- **Genomic context**: Located in the late operon intergenic region between the lysis gene `15` (`Rz`) and the small terminase subunit gene `3` (adjacent to `ORF80` / `P57018`) [PMID:2763468, PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822].

## Function & Architecture
- **Deletion analysis**: Casjens et al. (1989) sequenced the 2,558-bp region between genes `19` and `3` and constructed deletion mutants covering the interval between gene `15` and gene `3` (which removes `ORF80` and `ORF201`), demonstrating that phage P22 has no additional genes required for lytic growth or lysogeny in this region [PMID:2763468].
- **Domain architecture & similarity**: Contains an N-terminal/central bacteriophage Rha-like regulatory family domain (`InterPro:IPR014054` `Phage_regulatory_Rha`, `Pfam:PF09669` `Phage_pRha`, `NCBIfam:TIGR02681` `phage_pRha`) and a C-terminal domain (`InterPro:IPR018877` `Phage_P22_Orf201_C`, `Pfam:PF10549` `ORF11CD3`), and displays sequence similarity to *Haemophilus influenzae* prophage-like protein `HI_1412` [file:BPP22/P21417/P21417-uniprot.txt]. Despite homology to phage phi80/P22 Rha-family proteins, P22 `ORF201` (`P21417`) is uncharacterized (`PE 4: Predicted`) and has not been experimentally tested for the *rha* (inhibition of growth on *ihf* / *himA* host mutants) phenotype in *Salmonella* [file:BPP22/P21417/P21417-uniprot.txt].
