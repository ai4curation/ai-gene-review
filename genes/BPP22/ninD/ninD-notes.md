# Gene Review Notes: Salmonella phage P22 `ninD` (`Q38663`, Protein ninD)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `ninD`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q38663` (`NIND_BPP22`)
- **Organism**: Salmonella phage P22 (`NCBITaxon:10754`)
- **Protein length**: 57 amino acids (6.8 kDa)
- **Operon / genomic context**: Encoded in the early right ($P_R$) `nin` region between `ninB` and `ninE` ([PMID:11053393](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11053393/), [PMID:12562822](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12562822/)).

## Function and Domain Architecture
- **Conserved lambdoid NinD family protein of uncharacterized biochemical activity**:
  - Belongs to the lambdoid phage NinD family (`InterPro:IPR035346` `NinD`, `Pfam:PF17466` `NinD`, `file:genes/BPP22/ninD/ninD-uniprot.txt`, `"CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the ninD family. {ECO:0000305}."`).
  - Vander Byl & Kropinski (2000) [PMID:11053393] identified `ninD` as one of the conserved lambdoid genes in the P22 `nin` region (`"The nin region shows genes which are clearly lambdoid interspersed with genes, such as ninX and ninY , which are not."`).
  - Like its Escherichia phage lambda ortholog NinD (`P03766`), P22 NinD is non-essential for lytic growth under standard laboratory conditions and its specific molecular function and cellular targets have not been experimentally established.
