CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms the portal vertex of the capsid (PubMed:21499245). This
CC       portal plays critical roles in head assembly, genome packaging,
CC       neck/tail attachment, and genome ejection (PubMed:30787152,
CC       PubMed:30787152). Procapsid assembly may initiate with a nucleation
CC       complex composed of portal and scaffolding proteins (PubMed:30787152).
CC       The portal protein multimerizes as a single ring-shaped homododecamer
CC       arranged around a central channel (PubMed:21499245, PubMed:28134243).
CC       Switches upon genome packaging from an asymmetrical conformation in the
CC       procapsid (PC-portal) to a symmetrical ring in the mature capsid (MV-
CC       portal). This change of conformation may serve as a signal for headful
CC       packaging (PubMed:28134243). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21499245,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28134243, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30787152}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homododecamer (PubMed:21499245, PubMed:28134243,
CC       PubMed:37952769). Interacts with the terminase large subunit; this
CC       interaction allows the packaging of viral DNA (PubMed:28134243).
CC       Interacts (via C-terminus) with the head-to-tail adapter protein gp4;
CC       this interaction participates in the head completion (PubMed:28134243,
CC       PubMed:37952769). Interacts with the scaffolding protein; this
CC       interaction initiates procapsid assembly, thereby ensuring
CC       incorporation of only one portal ring per capsid (PubMed:30787152).
CC       Interacts with the capsid protein (PubMed:37952769).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21499245, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28134243,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30787152, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the p22likevirus portal protein family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
