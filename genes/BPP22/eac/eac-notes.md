# Curation Notes for Salmonella phage P22 `eac` (Q03545)

## Summary of Gene Product
`eac` encodes protein EaC (UniProt `Q03545`, 211 amino acids, 24.4 kDa) of *Salmonella* phage P22 [PMID:11053393]. Located in the early leftward (`P_L`) `ea` accessory region between `int` and `erf`, EaC was originally identified by SDS-PAGE as one of seven early proteins expressed in UV-irradiated *Salmonella* cells infected with phage P22 [PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822]. Comparative genomic analyses show that `eac` lies in a mosaic region shared across P22-like phages [PMID:18621868].

## Molecular Function and Biological Process
- The `ea` region (`int` to `erf`) is non-essential for P22 lytic propagation and lysogeny under standard laboratory conditions, and no specific biochemical activity or biological process has been experimentally assigned to EaC [PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822].
