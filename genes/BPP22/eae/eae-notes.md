# Curation Notes for Salmonella phage P22 `eae` (Q03547)

## Summary of Gene Product
`eae` encodes protein EaE (UniProt `Q03547`, 165 amino acids, 19.3 kDa) of *Salmonella* phage P22 [PMID:11053393]. Located in the early leftward (`P_L`) `ea` accessory region between `int` and `erf`, EaE was originally identified by SDS-PAGE as one of seven early proteins expressed in UV-irradiated *Salmonella* cells infected with phage P22 [PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822].
