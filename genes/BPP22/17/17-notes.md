# Curation Notes for Salmonella phage P22 `17` (P14109)

## Summary of Gene Product
Gene `17` encodes protein gp17 (UniProt `P14109`, 103 amino acids, 11.7 kDa) of *Salmonella* phage P22 [PMID:2738922]. Located in the early leftward (`P_L`) operon between `orf80` and `orf56`/`c3` (`ral-orf48-kil-arf-orf78-orf80-17-orf56-c3-orf97`), `17` is an accessory early gene dispensable on non-lysogenic laboratory hosts but required for P22 growth on *Salmonella* hosts carrying the Fels-2 prophage [PMID:2738922].

## Molecular Function, Biological Process, and Mechanism
- **Overcoming Fels-2 prophage superinfection exclusion**: In the absence of gp17, P22 lytic development is blocked by a superinfection exclusion system encoded by the *Salmonella* Fels-2 (`fel-2`) prophage (`file:genes/BPP22/17/17-uniprot.txt`). Thus, gp17 acts as a phage counter-defense (anti-exclusion) protein that neutralizes or bypasses Fels-2 exclusion.
- **Genomic and transcriptional context**: The 3' end of gene `17` overlapping its stop codon forms a potential stem-loop structure nearly identical to the bacteriophage lambda `ssb` (`ea10`) 3' sequence near the `tL2b` transcription terminator [PMID:2738922].

## Subcellular Localization
- No experimental subcellular localization has been determined, and sequence analysis reveals no signal peptide or transmembrane helices.
