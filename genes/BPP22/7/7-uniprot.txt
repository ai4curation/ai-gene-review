CC   -!- FUNCTION: Inner capsid protein that plays a role in viral DNA ejection
CC       into the host cell (PubMed:26245366, PubMed:29590587). Assembles into
CC       an extracellular trans-envelope channel completed by the internal
CC       virion proteins gp7 and probably gp16 (PubMed:30886360). This channel
CC       allows the delivery of the viral genome into the cell cytoplasm
CC       (PubMed:30886360). Displays membrane-association properties, may
CC       therefore form a simple channel spanning the outer membrane (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26245366, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29590587,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30886360, ECO:0000305|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the internal virion protein gp20; this
CC       interaction forms a tube-like structure that may allow DNA ejection
CC       through the host membranes. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000269|PubMed:328927,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the podoviruses gp7 family. {ECO:0000305}.
