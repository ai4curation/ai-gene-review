- **Protein**: Uncharacterized 6.6 kDa protein in eae-abc2 intergenic region (56 AA, 6.6 kDa)

- **Genomic context**: Located in the early left (*P*L) operon downstream of `abc2` in the `eae`–`abc2` region [PMID:3660589, PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822].

## Function & Architecture
- **Phenotypic characterization**: Murphy et al. (1987) sequenced the *P*L operon region containing three open reading frames encoding 10.9 kDa (`abc1`), 11.6 kDa (`abc2`), and 6.6 kDa (`ORF56` / `P11192`) polypeptides, constructed P22 deletion mutants lacking each ORF as well as *lacUV5* expression plasmids, and demonstrated that the 6.6 kDa polypeptide (`P11192`) is uninvolved in the anti-RecBCD (`Abc`) phenotype [PMID:3660589].
- **Domain architecture & status**: Contains the conserved domain of unknown function `InterPro:IPR020295` / `Pfam:PF10930` (`DUF2737`, residues 1..56) and a predicted coiled-coil segment (`COILED 11..38`). Annotated at UniProt protein existence level `PE 4: Predicted` with no known molecular function, biological process, or subcellular localization [file:BPP22/P11192/P11192-uniprot.txt].
