CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as a tail hub by assembling onto the head-to-tail
CC       adapter protein gp4, generating a channel that is sealed by the tail
CC       needle protein gp26 (PubMed:37952769). Together with gp4 and gp26, gp10
CC       is required for stabilization of the condensed DNA within the capsid;
CC       perhaps by plugging the hole through which the DNA enters.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer (in solution) (PubMed:37952769). Hexamer; assembles as
CC       a hexamer onto the dodecamer formed by the head-to-tail adapter gp4
CC       (PubMed:37952769). Interacts with the head-to-tail adapter protein gp4;
CC       each gp10 monomer contacts 2 head-to-tail adapter protein gp4
CC       (PubMed:37952769). Interacts with the tail needle protein gp26
CC       (PubMed:37952769). Interacts with the tail spike protein gp9; binds six
CC       tail spike trimers (PubMed:37952769). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
