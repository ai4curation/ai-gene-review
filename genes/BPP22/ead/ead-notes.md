# Curation Notes for Salmonella phage P22 `ead` (Q03546)

## Summary of Gene Product
`ead` encodes protein EaD (UniProt `Q03546`, 197 amino acids, 22.1 kDa) of *Salmonella* phage P22 [PMID:11053393]. Located in the early leftward (`P_L`) `ea` accessory region between `int` and `erf`, EaD was originally identified by SDS-PAGE among the early proteins synthesized in UV-irradiated *Salmonella* cells upon P22 infection [PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822].

## Molecular Function, Biological Process, and Domain Context
- **Similarity to bacteriophage lambda Ea22**: Sequence analysis in `file:genes/BPP22/ead/ead-uniprot.txt` shows that P22 EaD shares sequence similarity with bacteriophage lambda early protein Ea22 and contains the conserved `Ead_Ea22` domain (`IPR025153`, `PF13935`).
- **Genomic context and dispensability**: Like the other genes of the `ea` region (`int` to `erf`), `ead` is dispensable for P22 lytic growth and lysogeny under standard laboratory conditions, and no specific biochemical activity has been experimentally demonstrated for P22 EaD [PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822]. Thus, `core_functions` is left empty (`[]`).
