CC   -!- FUNCTION: Cell-perforating component and plug protein of the phage tail
CC       machine (PubMed:18059287, PubMed:20817910). Together with gp4 and gp10,
CC       gp26 is required for stabilization of the condensed DNA within the
CC       capsid by plugging the hole through which the DNA enters
CC       (PubMed:18059287). Host cell membrane perforation allows viral DNA
CC       ejection (PubMed:18059287). The needle penetrates the host outer
CC       membrane. The needle is released and the internal head protein gp7 is
CC       ejected to form an extra-cellular channel (PubMed:30886360).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18059287, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20817910,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30886360}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotrimer (PubMed:16730179, PubMed:18059287, PubMed:19482036,
CC       PubMed:26574546, PubMed:37952769). The trimer forms an elongated
CC       coiled-coil (240A x 25A) (PubMed:19482036, PubMed:26574546,
CC       PubMed:37952769). The N-terminal tip may exist in a pre-ejection
CC       extended conformation, which may fold into a trimer of hairpins only
CC       after ejection into the host (PubMed:26574546). Interacts (via N-
CC       terminus) with the tail hub gp10 (PubMed:37952769). Interacts with the
CC       head-to-tail adapter protein gp4 (PubMed:37952769).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16730179, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18059287,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19482036, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26574546,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminus forms the domain that binds to the tail hub
CC       protein gp10 and plugs the portal vertex channel, the middle coiled-
CC       coil rod, and finally a C-terminus distal tip that is a tight trimer of
CC       alpha-helical hairpins. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19482036}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the Lederbergvirus tail needle protein family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
