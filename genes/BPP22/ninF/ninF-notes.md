# Gene Review Notes: Salmonella phage P22 `ninF` (`Q38666`, Protein ninF)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `ninF`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q38666` (`NINF_BPP22`)
- **Organism**: Salmonella phage P22 (`NCBITaxon:10754`)
- **Protein length**: 58 amino acids (6.8 kDa)
- **Operon / genomic context**: Encoded in the early right ($P_R$) `nin` region between `ninX` and `ninG` ([PMID:11053393](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11053393/), [PMID:12562822](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12562822/)).

## Function and Domain Architecture
- **Conserved lambdoid NinF family protein of uncharacterized biochemical activity**:
  - Belongs to the lambdoid phage NinF family (`InterPro:IPR008712` `NinF`, `Pfam:PF05810` `NinF`, `file:genes/BPP22/ninF/ninF-uniprot.txt`, `"CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the ninF family. {ECO:0000305}."`).
  - Identified by Vander Byl & Kropinski (2000) [PMID:11053393] as one of the conserved lambdoid genes in the P22 `nin` region (`"The nin region shows genes which are clearly lambdoid interspersed with genes, such as ninX and ninY , which are not."`).
  - Like its Escherichia phage lambda ortholog NinF (`P03768`), P22 NinF is dispensable for standard plaque formation and its molecular function has not yet been experimentally determined.
