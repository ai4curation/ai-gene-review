# Curation Notes for Salmonella phage P22 `eaf` (Q03548)

## Summary of Gene Product
`eaf` encodes protein EaF (UniProt `Q03548`, 119 amino acids, 13.7 kDa) of *Salmonella* phage P22 [PMID:11053393]. Located in the early leftward (`P_L`) `ea` accessory region between `int` and `erf`, EaF was originally identified by SDS-PAGE as one of seven early proteins expressed in UV-irradiated *Salmonella* cells infected with phage P22 [PMID:11053393, PMID:12562822]. Comparative genomic analyses show that `eaf` lies within a mosaic early region shared across P22-like phages [PMID:18621868].
