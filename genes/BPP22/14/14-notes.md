# Gene 14 (Q01075 / VG14_BPP22) - Protein gp14

## Summary
- **UniProt ID**: `Q01075` (`VG14_BPP22`)
- **Gene symbol**: `14`
- **Length**: 151 AA (16.4 kDa, `PE 4: Predicted`)
- **Domains**: `IPR022568` / `PF11039` (`DUF2824`, residues 11-151)
- **Function**: Late morphogenetic operon protein (`gp14`) encoded between gene `26` and the internal virion ejection gene `7` in the *Salmonella* phage P22 late gene cluster [PMID:1853558, PMID:1522065, PMID:11053393]. Unlike `gp7`, `gp20`, and `gp16`, `gp14` is not incorporated into mature P22 virions [PMID:328927], and its molecular role in virion morphogenesis or assembly of the ejection apparatus remains biochemically uncharacterized (`DUF2824`).

## Key Literature Evidence
- **PMID:1853558** (Eppler et al. 1991): Sequenced the morphogenetic gene cluster of phage P22, noting that genes `14` and `7` lie downstream of `26` in the late operon.
- **PMID:1522065** (Conlin et al. 1992): Determined the complete nucleotide sequence of P22 gene `14` and gene `7`.
- **PMID:11053393** (Pedulla et al. 2003): Completed the full 41,724 bp genome sequence of *Salmonella enterica* serovar Typhimurium bacteriophage P22, annotating gene `14` in the late morphogenetic operon.
