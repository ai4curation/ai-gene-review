# Curation Notes for Salmonella phage P22 `sieA` (Q38674)

## Summary of Gene Product
`sieA` encodes the superinfection exclusion protein A (SieA; UniProt `Q38674`, 164 amino acids, 18.8 kDa) of *Salmonella* phage P22. It is a multi-pass integral membrane protein of the host cell inner membrane (plasma membrane) expressed from the P22 prophage during lysogeny [PMID:7768804, PMID:38236051].

## Molecular Function, Biological Process, and Mechanism
- **Superinfection exclusion (`GO:0098669`)**: SieA is both necessary and sufficient for the P22 SieA superinfection exclusion system, which prevents infection of P22 lysogens by superinfecting P22 and closely related P22-like phages (such as L, MG178, and MG40) as well as generalized transducing particles packaged in P22 virions [PMID:4610992, PMID:38236051].
- **Mechanism of DNA injection block**: Superinfecting P22 virions adsorb normally to the host O-antigen lipopolysaccharide via the tailspike protein (gp9), but their packaged DNA fails to cross the host inner membrane into the cytoplasm [PMID:4610992, PMID:7768804, PMID:38236051]. Genetic and chimera analyses of the P22 ejection proteins (`gp16`, `gp20`, `gp7`) demonstrate that the target specificity of SieA exclusion is determined primarily by the ejected pilot/conduit proteins gp16 and gp20, indicating that SieA blocks assembly or function of the periplasmic gp20 and inner-membrane gp16 DNA delivery conduit [PMID:38236051].

## Subcellular Localization and Topology
- **Host cell plasma membrane (`GO:0020002`)**: Sequence analysis and membrane fractionation in *Escherichia coli* and *Salmonella enterica* establish that SieA has three transmembrane helices (residues 8-28, 85-105, and 121-141) and localizes exclusively to the host inner membrane [PMID:7768804, PMID:38236051]. PhoA and LacZ translational fusion topology assays confirm a three-transmembrane topology with the N-terminus in the periplasm, an internal hydrophilic loop (residues ~29-84) in the cytoplasm, and the C-terminus in the cytoplasm [PMID:38236051].
