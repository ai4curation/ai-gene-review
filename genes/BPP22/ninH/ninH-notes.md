# Gene Review Notes: Salmonella phage P22 `ninH` (`Q38669`, Protein ninH)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `ninH`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q38669` (`NINH_BPP22`)
- **Organism**: Salmonella phage P22 (`NCBITaxon:10754`)
- **Protein length**: 67 amino acids (7.8 kDa)
- **Operon / genomic context**: Distal gene of the early right ($P_R$) `nin` region located between `ninY` and the late antiterminator gene `23` ([PMID:11053393](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11053393/), [PMID:12562822](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12562822/)).

## Function and Structural Architecture
- **Conserved lambdoid NinH family homeodomain-like protein**:
  - Domain architecture in `ninH-uniprot.txt`:
    - Belongs to the lambdoid NinH family (`InterPro:IPR010454` `Phage_NinH`, `Pfam:PF06322` `Phage_NinH`, `"CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the ninH family. {ECO:0000305}."`).
    - Contains a predicted homeodomain-like helix-turn-helix fold (`InterPro:IPR009057` `Homeodomain-like_sf`, `SUPFAM:SSF46689` `Homeodomain-like`).
  - Identified by Vander Byl & Kropinski (2000) [PMID:11053393] as a conserved lambdoid gene in the P22 `nin` region (`"The nin region shows genes which are clearly lambdoid interspersed with genes, such as ninX and ninY , which are not."`).
  - Like its Escherichia phage lambda ortholog NinH (`P03771`), P22 NinH is a small homeodomain-like protein whose specific nucleic acid target and biological role during phage infection remain experimentally uncharacterized.

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- **Existing GOA annotations**: None (`ninH-goa.tsv` is empty).
- **Proposed `NEW` annotations**: None proposed, as neither a specific DNA/RNA binding specificity nor a verified biological process has been experimentally demonstrated for NinH in P22 or lambda.
