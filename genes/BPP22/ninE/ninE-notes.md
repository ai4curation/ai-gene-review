# Gene Review Notes: Salmonella phage P22 `ninE` (`Q38664`, Protein ninE)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `ninE`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q38664` (`NINE_BPP22`)
- **Organism**: Salmonella phage P22 (`NCBITaxon:10754`)
- **Protein length**: 58 amino acids (6.9 kDa)
- **Operon / genomic context**: Encoded in the early right ($P_R$) `nin` region between `ninD` and `ninX` ([PMID:11053393](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11053393/), [PMID:12562822](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12562822/)).

## Function and Domain Architecture
- **Conserved lambdoid NinE family protein of uncharacterized biochemical activity**:
  - Belongs to the lambdoid phage NinE family (`InterPro:IPR007986` `NINE`, `Pfam:PF05322` `NinE`, `file:genes/BPP22/ninE/ninE-uniprot.txt`, `"CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the ninE family. {ECO:0000305}."`).
  - Identified by Vander Byl & Kropinski (2000) [PMID:11053393] as one of the conserved lambdoid genes within the P22 `nin` region (`"The nin region shows genes which are clearly lambdoid interspersed with genes, such as ninX and ninY , which are not."`).
  - Like its Escherichia phage lambda ortholog NinE (`P03767`), P22 NinE is a small basic polypeptide whose specific molecular function and biological role remain experimentally uncharacterized.
