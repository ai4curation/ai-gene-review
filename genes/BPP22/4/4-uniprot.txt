CC   -!- FUNCTION: Tail protein located at the vertex occupied by the portal
CC       ring (PubMed:16970964). 12 copies of gp4 form a structural ring at the
CC       bottom of the portal ring (PubMed:16970964). Together with gp10 and
CC       gp26, gp4 is required for stabilization of the condensed DNA within the
CC       capsid; perhaps by plugging the hole through which the DNA enters
CC       (PubMed:16970964). Probably functions as a head completion protein
CC       (PubMed:28134243). Plays a role in ejection of the bacteriophage DNA
CC       into the host cell at the initiation of infection (PubMed:16970964).
CC       Functions as an exolysin that catalyzes the cleavage of the glycosidic
CC       bonds between N-acetylmuramic acid and N-acetylglucosamine residues in
CC       peptidoglycans (PubMed:14763988). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14763988,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16970964, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21499245,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28134243}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer; homododecamer upon binding to the portal ring
CC       (PubMed:16970964). Interacts (via C-terminus) with the portal protein;
CC       this interaction participates in the head completion (PubMed:16970964,
CC       PubMed:21499245, PubMed:28134243). Interacts with the tail hub protein
CC       gp10 (PubMed:37952769). Interacts with the tail spike protein
CC       (PubMed:37952769). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16970964,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21499245, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28134243,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37952769}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminus changes its conformation when transitioning from
CC       the preassembly state to the postassembly state.
