CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transmembrane transporter that exports citrate across the
CC       cell membrane (PubMed:37216016, PubMed:38076415, PubMed:39377610).
CC       Influences fungal morphology and promotes a more branched phenotype
CC       (PubMed:38076415). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37216016,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38076415, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39377610}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=citrate(in) = citrate(out); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:33183,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:16947; Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:37216016};
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37216016};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression is repressed by Mg(2+) ions and up-regulated
CC       under manganese deficiency (PubMed:38076415, PubMed:39377610). The
CC       intracellular accumulation of citrate or acetyl-CoA also causes up-
CC       regulation of its expression (PubMed:39377610).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38076415, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39377610}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Decreases concentration of citric acid
CC       (PubMed:34071072). Increases the transcript levels of the genes
CC       encoding the mitochondrial aconitase and the cytosolic aconitase
CC       (PubMed:34071072). Strongly activates the glyoxylate shunt and
CC       increases expression of the oxaloacetate acetylhydrolase (OAH),
CC       resulting in a more than two-fold higher concentration of oxalate in
CC       the medium (PubMed:34071072). Leads also to a reduced glucose uptake
CC       and a reduced expression of high-affinity glucose transporter genes,
CC       mstG and mstH (PubMed:34071072). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34071072}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the major facilitator superfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
