CC   -!- FUNCTION: The maturation protein is required for the typical attachment
CC       of the phage to the side of the bacterial F-pili (PubMed:23810697,
CC       PubMed:27992877). Binds to sequences located toward each end of the
CC       genome, hence circularizing it (PubMed:26608810). The RNA genome-
CC       maturation protein A complex is released from the capsid upon host
CC       receptor binding (PubMed:23810697). Maturation protein A enters the
CC       cell along with the viral RNA (PubMed:4551992).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23810697, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26608810,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27992877, ECO:0000269|PubMed:4551992}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the host pilus. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23810697,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27992877, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31311931}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion. Note=A single copy of the maturation
CC       protein is present in the virion and replaces a coat protein dimer at
CC       one of the icosahedral two-fold axes. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27992877,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:23810697}.
CC   -!- PTM: During internalization, MP is proteolytically cleaved into two
CC       fragments allowing translation and replication to start.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:4551992}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the Leviviricetes maturation protein family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
