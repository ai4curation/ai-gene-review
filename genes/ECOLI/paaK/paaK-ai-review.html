Gene Description

PaaK is the cytoplasmic phenylacetate-CoA ligase (EC 6.2.1.30) of Escherichia coli, an ATP-dependent member of the ANL (acyl-CoA synthetase/adenylate-forming) superfamily encoded at the end of the paaABCDEFGHIJK operon. It activates phenylacetate to phenylacetyl-CoA with release of AMP and pyrophosphate, the first and committed step of the aerobic hybrid phenylacetate catabolic pathway in which all subsequent intermediates are processed as CoA thioesters (epoxidation by PaaABCE, isomerization by PaaG, hydrolytic ring opening by PaaZ and beta-oxidation-like steps to succinyl-CoA and acetyl-CoA). Its product, phenylacetyl-CoA, is also the inducer that relieves PaaX repression of the paa genes, so PaaK activity couples substrate availability to pathway expression.
