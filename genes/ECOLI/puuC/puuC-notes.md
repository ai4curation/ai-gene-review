# puuC (P23883) curation notes

Provider deep research was not run for this gene in this session (no provider
credentials available). Sources: PMID:15590624, PMID:18668238, PMID:22636776
(abstracts cached) and the curated UniProt record.

- Puu step: "4) dehydrogenation of gamma-glutamyl-gamma-aminobutyraldehyde"
  [PMID:15590624].
- "PuuC is a nonspecific aldehyde dehydrogenase that oxidizes all the
  aldehydes in putrescine catabolism" and is redundant with PatD and GabD
  [PMID:22636776].
- Broad in vitro substrate range, NAD+ preferred, 3-HPA best substrate
  [PMID:18668238]. The in vitro-only substrate terms (benzaldehyde,
  acetaldehyde, pentanal) are marked over-annotated; the GABA-aldehyde and
  succinate-semialdehyde activities are kept as non-core secondary activities.
- GO lacks a 4-(gamma-glutamylamino)butanal dehydrogenase MF (EC 1.2.1.99);
  proposed as a new term, GO:0004030 used meanwhile.
