Gene Description

FadB is the multifunctional alpha subunit of the cytoplasmic FadBA fatty acid oxidation complex in Escherichia coli K-12. Its hydratase and NAD-dependent (3S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase domains perform consecutive beta-oxidation reactions; cis-3/trans-2 enoyl-CoA isomerization and hydroxyacyl-CoA epimerization provide additional substrate-processing activities. Purified complex and catalytic-site mutant studies distinguish these reactions. FadA supplies the subsequent thiolase reaction; the related FadJ provides an alternative enzyme in particular conditions.
