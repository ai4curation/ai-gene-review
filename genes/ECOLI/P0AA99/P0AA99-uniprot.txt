CC   -!- FUNCTION: Hydrolase that plays a role in the regulation of
CC       peptidoglycan-outer membrane linkages (PubMed:33941679,
CC       PubMed:33947763). It has two distinctive enzymatic functions, an L,D-
CC       endopeptidase activity and a carboxypeptidase activity
CC       (PubMed:33941679, PubMed:33947763). Acts as a murein L,D-endopeptidase
CC       that catalyzes the cleavage of the cross-link between the outer
CC       membrane-anchored Braun's lipoprotein (Lpp) and peptidoglycan,
CC       detaching Lpp from the peptidoglycan layer (PubMed:33941679,
CC       PubMed:33947763, PubMed:37830798). It also exhibits glycine-specific
CC       carboxypeptidase activity on muropeptides containing a terminal glycine
CC       residue (PubMed:33941679, PubMed:33947763). DpaA hydrolyzes the amide
CC       bond between the L-center of meso-diaminopimelate in peptidoglycan stem
CC       peptides and the C-terminal L-lysine of Lpp, and between the L-center
CC       of meso-diaminopimelate and a terminal glycine residue in tetrapeptides
CC       (PubMed:33941679, PubMed:33947763). The detachment of Lpp from
CC       peptidoglycan is beneficial for the cell under certain stress
CC       conditions, suggesting that DpaA may help the cell to cope with these
CC       stress conditions (PubMed:33947763). DpaA may also cleave Lpp bound to
CC       the inner membrane (PubMed:33941679). It does not show any L,D-
CC       transpeptidase or L,D-carboxypeptidase activity (PubMed:33947763).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33941679, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33947763,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37830798}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37830798}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The substrate-binding site differs from those of the L,D-
CC       transpeptidases, which have an elongated groove containing the
CC       catalytic Cys residue that is accessible via two entrances
CC       (PubMed:37830798). It also lacks the L,D-carboxypeptidase-specific D-
CC       Ala-binding pocket (PubMed:37830798). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37830798}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Deletion of the gene does not confer any
CC       discernible phenotype, except for a slight reduction in the doubling
CC       time during the exponential phase of growth (PubMed:33941679). The
CC       peptidoglycan sacculi derived from the mutant contain a greater
CC       abundance of high-molecular-weight Lpp-bound muropeptides compared to
CC       that of wild-type strain (PubMed:33941679). The deletion mutant does
CC       not exhibit significant sensitivity to any of the cell wall
CC       antibiotics, such as cephalexin, cefsulodin, mecillinam or vancomycin
CC       (PubMed:33941679). Another study shows that the mutation does not
CC       affect susceptibility to tetracycline and ampicillin, but that the
CC       mutant is more susceptible to mecillinam, an antibiotic that induces a
CC       stark change in cell shape in growing cells (PubMed:33947763).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33941679, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33947763}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the YkuD family. DpaA/LdtF subfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
