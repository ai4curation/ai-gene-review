Gene Description

PaaJ is the beta-ketothiolase of the aerobic, CoA-thioester-based phenylacetate catabolic pathway of Escherichia coli, encoded in the paaABCDEFGHIJK operon. It acts twice in the beta-oxidation-like lower part of the pathway. First, it cleaves the ring-opening product 3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA with CoA, releasing acetyl-CoA and a C6 dehydroadipyl-CoA that is then hydrated by PaaF and oxidized by PaaH. Second, in the last step of the pathway, it cleaves 3-oxoadipyl-CoA (beta-ketoadipyl-CoA) to succinyl-CoA and acetyl-CoA, linking phenylacetate degradation to central metabolism. PaaJ belongs to the thiolase family (PcaF-type 3-oxoadipyl-CoA thiolases) and acts as a stand-alone enzyme rather than as part of a FadBA-like complex. paaJ deletion mutants cannot grow on phenylacetate.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

3-oxo-5,6-didehydrosuberyl-CoA thiolase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 3-oxo-5,6-didehydrosuberyl-CoA + CoA = 2,3-didehydroadipoyl-CoA + acetyl-CoA (EC 2.3.1.223; RHEA:34799).

Justification: EC 2.3.1.223 and RHEA:34799 describe the PaaJ C8 thiolysis step of the aerobic phenylacetate pathway, but GO has no specific term for it; only the generic GO:0003988 is available.

Parent term: acetyl-CoA C-acyltransferase activity

Function

PaaJ is a bifunctional beta-ketothiolase used twice in the lower phenylacetate pathway:

  1. C8 thiolysis: 3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA + CoA -> 2,3-dehydroadipyl-CoA + acetyl-CoA (EC 2.3.1.223).
    PMID:20660314
    The expected direct product is the 3,4-isomer; the 3,4 to 2,3 isomerization is probably by PaaG
    PMID:20660314
    Caveat: the PaaJ used by Teufel et al. was from Pseudomonas sp. strain Y2
    PMID:20660314.
    No GO term exists for EC 2.3.1.223; GO:0003988 (acyl-CoA + acetyl-CoA = CoA + 3-oxoacyl-CoA) covers it generically.
  2. Final step: 3-oxoadipyl-CoA + CoA -> succinyl-CoA + acetyl-CoA (EC 2.3.1.174; GO:0033812, an is_a child of GO:0003988).
    PMID:17259607
    (paaE is the P. putida U name of the paaJ ortholog; the abstract covers both P. putida and E. coli; abstract only.)
    PMID:20660314
  3. E. coli paaJ deletion mutant cannot grow on phenylacetate PMID:12846838.
  4. No stable complex with PaaF/PaaH PMID:22961985.

Curation decisions

  • GO:0019619 3,4-dihydroxybenzoate catabolic process (InterPro PcaF signature IPR012793): PaaJ
    catalyzes the same 3-oxoadipyl-CoA thiolysis as PcaF of the protocatechuate branch of the
    beta-ketoadipate pathway, but in E. coli the substrate comes from phenylacetate. No
    evidence was found that E. coli K-12 degrades protocatechuate, so this row is marked
    over-annotated. It is not removed because I could not cite a source showing the pathway is absent.
  • GO:0006974 DNA damage response (IEP, mitomycin C microarray): marked over-annotated, following the glgX precedent.
  • Module conformance: core_functions carry GO:0003988 (C8 thiolysis, no specific term) and GO:0033812.
