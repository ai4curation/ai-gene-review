Gene Description

PaaZ (formerly MaoC) is the bifunctional ring-opening enzyme of the Escherichia coli aerobic phenylacetate catabolic pathway, encoded in its own transcription unit divergent from the paaABCDEFGHIJK operon. It fuses an N-terminal NADP+-dependent aldehyde dehydrogenase domain to a C-terminal MaoC-like (hotdog-fold, (R)-specific enoyl-CoA hydratase-type) domain. The C-terminal domain hydrolytically cleaves the seven-membered enol-ether ring of oxepin-CoA (formed by PaaG) to the reactive open-chain 3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA semialdehyde (EC 3.3.2.12), and the N-terminal domain immediately oxidizes this aldehyde with NADP+ to 3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA (EC 1.2.1.91), which enters the beta-oxidation-like steps catalyzed by PaaJ, PaaF and PaaH. The semialdehyde is channelled between the two active sites; if it escapes it cyclizes to a dead-end tropone-like CoA ester. Cryo-EM structures show a trilobed homohexamer built from three domain-swapped dimers, with conserved basic residues proposed to pivot the CoA moiety between active sites. In vitro the hydratase domain also hydrates crotonyl-CoA. paaZ mutants cannot grow on phenylacetate and convert it to 2-hydroxyphenylacetate.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

oxepin-CoA hydrolase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 2-oxepin-2(3H)-ylideneacetyl-CoA + H2O = 3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA semialdehyde (EC 3.3.2.12; RHEA:31755).

Justification: Experimentally characterized ring-opening activity of PaaZ with an EC number and Rhea reaction but no GO term; currently only the generic ether hydrolase parent can be used.

3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA semialdehyde dehydrogenase (NADP+) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA semialdehyde + NADP+ + H2O = 3-oxo-5,6-didehydrosuberyl-CoA + NADPH + 2 H+ (EC 1.2.1.91; RHEA:31747).

Justification: Experimentally characterized activity of the PaaZ ALDH domain (and of NAD-dependent PaaN-type orthologs) with EC and Rhea entries but no GO term.

Function

  • Fusion protein: N-terminal NADP+-dependent ALDH domain + C-terminal MaoC-like (R)-specific hydratase (hotdog) domain.
    The hydratase domain hydrolytically opens the oxepin-CoA ring to a reactive semialdehyde; the ALDH domain oxidizes it
    to 3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA [PMID:21296885 abstract; PMID:20660314 "PaaZ thus functioned as an oxepin-CoA hydrolase/ 3-oxo-5,6-dehydrosuberyl-CoA semialdehyde dehydrogenase"].
  • Without ALDH activity (mutant or no NADP+), the semialdehyde cyclizes to a dead-end product, so the intermediate is
    channelled PMID:31511507.
  • CryoEM (PDB 6JQL-6JQO): three domain-swapped dimers forming a trilobed hexamer; CoA electrostatic pivoting
    proposed for intermediate transfer PMID:31511507.
  • MaoC (= PaaZ; old name) shows enoyl-CoA hydratase activity on crotonyl-CoA in vitro and supports PHA synthesis in
    engineered fadB strains PMID:12949091; side activity, not the physiological role.
  • Disruption converts phenylacetate to 2-hydroxyphenylacetate [PMID:9748275 via UniProt]; deletion mutant cannot grow on PA PMID:12846838.

GOA review summary

  • Ring cleavage is hydrolysis of an enol-ether C-O bond (EC 3.3.2.12, ether hydrolases): GO:0016803 ACCEPT (core).
  • GO:0016823 (C-C bond hydrolase in ketonic substances): no C-C bond is hydrolysed -> MODIFY to GO:0016803.
  • GO:0016726 (acting on CH/CH2) wrong sub-branch for an aldehyde oxidation (EC 1.2.1.91) -> MODIFY to GO:0033721.
  • GO:0016491 IBA/IEA -> MODIFY to GO:0033721.
  • GO:0004300 (crotonyl-CoA, in vitro) KEEP_AS_NON_CORE; GO:0042802 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE; GO:0010124 x3 ACCEPT.
