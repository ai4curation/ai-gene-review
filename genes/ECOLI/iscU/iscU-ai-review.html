Gene Description

IscU is the small (128-residue) cytosolic scaffold protein of the E. coli Isc iron-sulfur cluster biogenesis machinery, encoded in the iscRSUA-hscBA-fdx-iscX operon. It docks onto the cysteine desulfurase IscS (forming an IscS2-IscU2 heterotetramer), accepts persulfide sulfur from the IscS Cys328 loop on its own conserved cysteines (Cys63 among them), and with iron supplied by donors such as IscA, CyaY, IscX or YtfE and electrons from ferredoxin builds a transient [2Fe-2S] cluster, which can be reductively coupled to a [4Fe-4S] cluster. IscU interconverts between a structured and a partially disordered conformation; the disordered form is the preferred IscS partner and cluster assembly favours the structured form, which releases IscS and binds the HscB co-chaperone. HscB targets holo-IscU to the Hsp70 chaperone HscA, whose ATPase cycle drives transfer of the cluster to apo-acceptor proteins such as ferredoxin. Purified holo-IscU transfers [2Fe-2S] clusters to apo-ferredoxin over multiple turnovers. IscU also binds zinc, which stabilises its fold, and is a target of copper toxicity under anaerobic conditions.

Core Functions

Transient scaffold that assembles [2Fe-2S] (and, reductively, [4Fe-4S]) clusters from IscS-derived persulfide sulfur and donated iron while bound to IscS, then, as the HscB/HscA client, delivers the cluster to apo-acceptor proteins such as ferredoxin over multiple turnovers.

description: >-
  IscU is the small (128-residue) cytosolic scaffold protein of the E. coli
  Isc iron-sulfur cluster biogenesis machinery, encoded in the
  iscRSUA-hscBA-fdx-iscX operon. It docks onto the cysteine desulfurase IscS
  (forming an IscS2-IscU2 heterotetramer), accepts persulfide sulfur from the
  IscS Cys328 loop on its own conserved cysteines (Cys63 among them), and with
  iron supplied by donors such as IscA, CyaY, IscX or YtfE and electrons from
  ferredoxin builds a transient [2Fe-2S] cluster, which can be reductively
  coupled to a [4Fe-4S] cluster. IscU interconverts between a structured and a
  partially disordered conformation; the disordered form is the preferred IscS
  partner and cluster assembly favours the structured form, which releases
  IscS and binds the HscB co-chaperone. HscB targets holo-IscU to the Hsp70
  chaperone HscA, whose ATPase cycle drives transfer of the cluster to
  apo-acceptor proteins such as ferredoxin. Purified holo-IscU transfers
  [2Fe-2S] clusters to apo-ferredoxin over multiple turnovers. IscU also binds
  zinc, which stabilises its fold, and is a target of copper toxicity under
  anaerobic conditions.
