Gene Description

SecF is the smaller multi-pass inner-membrane subunit of the E. coli SecDF-YajC accessory assembly. Together with SecD, it couples proton-motive-force-dependent conformational changes to late completion of SecYEG-mediated protein translocation after the ATP-driven SecA stage.

SecF research notes

SecF is the smaller SecDF membrane partner that works with SecD in the E. coli
SecDF-YajC accessory assembly. SecDF uses proton motive force rather than ATP to
complete late translocation through the SecYEG channel PMID:21562494, and
structural synthesis describes SecDF as a protein-translocation factor distinct
from the ATP-driven SecA motor PMID:29718185.

The key correction is that SecF contributes to PMF-coupled protein transmembrane
transporter activity; it is not itself a protein-transporting ATPase. SecA
provides the ATPase motor, while SecD and SecF form the PMF-coupled accessory
subcomplex.

Purified holo-translocon work places SecF with SecD and YajC in the
SecYEG-SecDF-YajC-YidC assembly PMID:24550475. A later structural study likewise treated the E. coli
holo-translocon as a SecYEG-SecDF-YajC-YidC inner-membrane supercomplex
PMID:27435098.

The generic SecF membrane, protein transport, and intracellular protein transport rows are less informative than the direct plasma-membrane,
Sec-complex transport, and intracellular protein transmembrane transport rows.
