Gene Description

KdpB is the catalytic P-type ATPase subunit (type IA, EC 7.2.2.6) of KdpFABC, the high-affinity, ATP-driven potassium uptake system of the E. coli inner membrane that is expressed under K+ limitation under control of the KdpD/KdpE two-component system. KdpB binds Mg-ATP in its cytoplasmic N and P domains and forms a phosphoenzyme on Asp307 of the DKTGT motif, cycling through E1/E2 conformations while KdpA, KdpC and KdpF remain static. ATP hydrolysis by KdpB is coupled to K+ uptake: K+ is selected by the channel-like KdpA subunit, passes through an intramembrane tunnel to binding sites in the KdpB transmembrane domain, and is released by KdpB to the cytoplasm. KdpC acts as a catalytic chaperone at the KdpB nucleotide-binding loop. KdpB activity is inhibited by phosphorylation of Ser162 in the TGES motif of the A domain.

Core Functions

Catalytic P-type ATPase subunit of KdpFABC: binds Mg-ATP, forms the Asp307 phosphoenzyme and hydrolyses ATP, coupling this to uphill K+ uptake through KdpA and K+ release from KdpB to the cytoplasm (contributing to the complex's P-type potassium transporter activity).
