# galM (P0A9C3, E. coli K-12) review notes

## 2026-11-12 initial review

Deep research: no provider API key was available in this session, so no
`galM-deep-research-*.md` file exists. The review rests on the cached publications,
the UniProt record and the human GALM review for family context.

### Sources read

- PMID:7966338 (Bouffard, Rudd, Adhya 1994), abstract only in cache. Key points:
  - galM is the fourth cistron of the gal operon: "The new structure of the gal
    operon from the promoter end is galE-galT-galK-galM" [PMID:7966338].
  - Activity: "Genetic and biochemical analyses have revealed that the galM gene
    product has mutarotase activity, which converts alpha-aldose to the beta-anomer"
    [PMID:7966338]. The reaction is reversible; the physiological direction for the
    Leloir pathway is beta- to alpha-D-galactose.
  - In vivo requirement: "the in vivo formation of alpha-D-galactopyranose (the
    substrate for phosphorylation) from beta-D-galactopyranose (generated by
    beta-galactosidase hydrolysis of lactose) is largely dependent upon the presence
    of the mutarotase" [PMID:7966338].
  - Location caveat: "the mutarotase from E. coli does not appear to be processed and
    yet is still found in periplasm (and culture media when overexpressed) in
    significant amounts" [PMID:7966338].
- PMID:4945113 (Hucho and Wallenfels 1971, mechanism) and PMID:5337146 (Wallenfels and
  Herrmann 1965, purification, German): title and citation only in cache. Their IDA
  rows (activity, cytoplasm) are accepted with deference to the curators.
- UniProt P0A9C3: FUNCTION "Mutarotase converts alpha-aldose to the beta-anomer. It is
  active on D-glucose, L-arabinose, D-xylose, D-galactose, maltose and lactose."
  Active sites His175 (proton donor) and Glu309 (proton acceptor), checked against the
  sequence. NCBIfam TIGR02636 galM_Leloir; PANTHER PTHR10091:SF0.

### Judgment calls

- Location kept at cytoplasm (GO:0005737), not cytosol. Unlike galE, there is no
  cytosol-fraction proteomics row for GalM, and the reported periplasmic pool argues
  against claiming more than the cytoplasm. The Leloir module uses cytosol
  (GO:0005829); for E. coli GalM the supported term is its parent, cytoplasm.
- General process terms (carbohydrate, galactose and hexose metabolic process) were
  MODIFIED to GO:0033499, as in the galE review. GO:0033499 is a descendant of each
  (checked in the GO sqlite ancestors).
- isomerase activity and the ARBA racemase/epimerase row were MODIFIED to GO:0004034,
  a descendant of both.
- carbohydrate binding was marked over-annotated, the same call as the human GALM
  review: a fold signature, with no binding function apart from catalysis.
- glucose metabolic process (IBA, grounded in human GALM) kept as non-core: the
  enzyme mutarotates glucose, but most glucose enters E. coli as glucose 6-phosphate
  through the PTS.
- No NEW lactose catabolic process row was added. Bouffard show that lactose
  metabolism needs GalM, but the step GalM performs is on free galactose after LacZ
  has cleaved the lactose. That step is captured by GO:0033499.
