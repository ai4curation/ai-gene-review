Gene Description

CcmC is a six-transmembrane-helix inner-membrane protein of the cytochrome c maturation (Ccm) system I and a member of the heme-handling protein superfamily defined by a tryptophan-rich WWD motif. CcmC binds heme in its periplasmic WWD domain, coordinating the iron through two conserved histidines (His60 and His184), and presents the heme 2-vinyl group to the periplasmic chaperone CcmE so that heme becomes covalently attached to CcmE His130. CcmC is the only Ccm protein strictly required for formation of holo-CcmE. It forms a CcmCDE intermediate complex with the small accessory protein CcmD and with CcmE, and a larger CcmA2B1C1D assembly in which ATP hydrolysis by the ABC-type CcmAB subunits releases holo-CcmE for delivery to the CcmF/CcmH heme ligation machinery. Although historically named a heme exporter, a role for CcmC or CcmABC in translocating heme across the membrane has not been demonstrated.

description: >-
  CcmC is a six-transmembrane-helix inner-membrane protein of the cytochrome c
  maturation (Ccm) system I and a member of the heme-handling protein
  superfamily defined by a tryptophan-rich WWD motif. CcmC binds heme in its
  periplasmic WWD domain, coordinating the iron through two conserved
  histidines (His60 and His184), and presents the heme 2-vinyl group to the
  periplasmic chaperone CcmE so that heme becomes covalently attached to CcmE
  His130. CcmC is the only Ccm protein strictly required for formation of
  holo-CcmE. It forms a CcmCDE intermediate complex with the small accessory
  protein CcmD and with CcmE, and a larger CcmA2B1C1D assembly in which
  ATP hydrolysis by the ABC-type CcmAB subunits releases holo-CcmE for
  delivery to the CcmF/CcmH heme ligation machinery. Although historically
  named a heme exporter, a role for CcmC or CcmABC in translocating heme
  across the membrane has not been demonstrated.
