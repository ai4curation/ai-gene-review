Gene Description

NirD is the small cytoplasmic subunit of the Escherichia coli NADH-dependent nitrite reductase NirBD. It associates with NirB and supports physiological reduction of nitrite to ammonium, including nitrogen assimilation downstream of respiratory nitrate reduction. Separately, NirD directly binds the catalytic region of RelA and inhibits (p)ppGpp synthesis, restraining the stringent response. This regulatory interaction can be separated experimentally from its role in nitrite reductase.

nirD evidence notes

NirD P0A9I8 is the small cytoplasmic subunit of the E. coli NirBD enzyme. [PMID:1435259: "NirD remained associated with NirB during the initial stages of purification of the active enzyme, suggesting that NirD is a second structural subunit of the enzyme."] Native complementation/reconstitution and the PMID34323689 nitrate-only nitrogen-source growth experiment establish contribution to NADH-dependent nitrite reduction. Lack of autonomous small-chain catalysis is not a rejection criterion; the core explicitly uses contributes_to_molecular_function.

The same full primary paper establishes a separate activity: [PMID:34323689: "NirD directly binds the catalytic domains of RelA to balance (p)ppGpp accumulation."; "NirD can control RelA activity by directly inhibiting the rate of (p)ppGpp synthesis."] Its E50K variant retains nitrite-reductase function while losing RelA interaction. The RelA binding row is refined to enzyme inhibitor activity and represented as a second core; no redundant NEW row is needed. The nitrate module should use only the NirBD contribution.

The IPR036922 Rieske-superfamily transfer overstates [2Fe-2S] binding. The target has NirD-specific IPR017881/IPR012748 assignments and a Rieske-like fold, but a fold does not establish a metal center. [PMID:22965870, explicitly M. tuberculosis NirD: "NirD is related in three-dimensional structure and sequence to the Rieske proteins; however, it does not contain any Fe-S cluster or other cofactors that might be involved in electron transfer."] This comparator is not a native E. coli cofactor assay. The row is MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED, not a claim of experimental exclusion. NirB's measured [2Fe-2S] center is not transferred onto NirD.

PMID36068114 uses artificial methyl-viologen/dithionite donors for NirB-alone activity. It does not invalidate the native NADH-dependent small-subunit contribution. PANTHER PTHR40562/PTHR40562:SF1 membership comes from target UniProt; PAINT PTN002220983 is traced as an inherited complex-membership assertion with direct target support, not a circular inference.

2026-10-29: PR #5740 follow-up

One IC NEW GO:0042128 proposal applies the same participation bar used elsewhere in this panel. PMID:34323689 identifies NirBD-mediated conversion of nitrate-derived nitrite to usable ammonium and separates NirD's enzyme role from RelA regulation through E50K. Its native growth context uses nitrate as sole electron acceptor and nitrogen source. This supports a downstream assimilatory contribution even though upstream nitrate reduction is respiratory. It does not supply the missing upstream nitrate reductase or assert an independent nitrate-reducing NirD activity. The phenotype caption is not used to invent quantitative growth results. Same-role small-subunit comparator absences are retained as annotation-state cautions, not evidence of oversight; no process ancestor/descendant duplicate or P0A9I8 GO-CAM index hit occurs.

The kinase-inhibitor suggestion was checked against live QuickGO definitions and ancestry. GO:0008728 GTP diphosphokinase activity has GO:0016778 diphosphotransferase activity among its ancestors, but not GO:0016301 kinase activity. RelA transfers diphosphate and produces AMP; the kinase definition describes transfer of a phosphate group. Consequently GO:0004857 enzyme inhibitor activity is retained instead of forcing GO:0019210. Query records are in the PR follow-up audit log.

The comparative ligand script finds Cys8/20/84 and His35/62/94. Unlike Q88M68's single histidine, these totals do not rule out a canonical ligand set, but neither prove geometry nor binding. The cofactor-free M. tuberculosis homolog is comparative evidence only; MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED is therefore retained. The exact positions and source hashes are recorded in the PSEPK NirD source analysis.
