# puuA (P78061) curation notes

Provider deep research was not run for this gene in this session (no provider
credentials available); the review rests on the cached publications and the
curated UniProt record. Literature consulted: PMID:18495664, PMID:15590624,
PMID:22636776 (abstracts cached).

- Purified PuuA "catalyzed the ATP-dependent gamma-glutamylation of putrescine"
  [PMID:18495664]. Km for putrescine is high (44.6 mM) [PMID:18495664].
- "Gene deletion experiments of puuA, however, indicated that the Puu pathway
  was more critical in utilizing putrescine as a sole carbon or nitrogen
  source" [PMID:18495664].
- PuuA is a glutamine synthetase homolog and dodecamer [PMID:18495664]. The
  GS-derived IEA/IBA rows (glutamine synthetase activity, L-glutamine
  biosynthesis) are marked over-annotated, consistent with the PTHR43785 family
  review's concern that PTN000464978 over-propagates into the PuuA/PauA clade
  (PTN001839150). No cached assay tests ammonium as substrate.
- Putrescine accumulates in puuA, puuB and puuC mutants [PMID:22636776].
