Gene Description

PtsO (NPr) is the HPr-family phosphocarrier of the nitrogen-regulatory phosphotransferase system (PTS(Ntr)) of Escherichia coli, encoded in the rpoN operon. It is phosphorylated on His16 by enzyme I(Ntr) (PtsP), which does not phosphorylate HPr, and transfers the phosphoryl group to the regulatory EIIA(Ntr) protein PtsN. The chain runs in parallel to the sugar-transport PTS; NPr can exchange phosphate with sugar-PTS proteins in vitro but has no sugar-transport role. In its dephosphorylated form NPr also binds LpxD and is proposed to restrain lipid A synthesis.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

PTS histidyl phosphocarrier activity

Definition: Accepting a phosphoryl group on an active-site histidine from a phosphoenolpyruvate-dependent enzyme I-type protein and transferring it to the histidine of an EIIA-type domain, as performed by HPr-family phosphocarriers of the phosphoenolpyruvate-dependent phosphotransferase system, including the NPr carrier of the nitrogen-regulatory branch.

Justification: GO has no molecular-function term for the HPr/NPr phosphocarrier step; the generic GO:0016775 (phosphotransferase activity, nitrogenous group as acceptor) is used as a stand-in. A dedicated term would let HPr and NPr carry an informative activity without implying sugar transport.

Parent term: phosphotransferase activity, nitrogenous group as acceptor

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:7876255 : Purified IIANtr and NPr exchange phosphate appropriately with Enzyme I, HPr, and Enzyme IIA proteins of the phosphoenolpyruvate: sugar phosphotransferase system.
