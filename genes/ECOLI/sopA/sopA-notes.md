# sopA (P62556), F plasmid partition ATPase, Escherichia coli K-12

## 2026-10-30: initial review

**GOA had no annotation for SopA**, one of the two prototype plasmid partition ATPases.

- ATPase: Mg-dependent, stimulated by DNA and further by SopB [PMID:1406581 "The SopA protein
  showed a modest magnesium ion-dependent ATPase activity and this activity was stimulated in the
  presence of DNA."]. SopB supplies an arginine finger [PMID:19740757].
- Non-specific DNA binding is required for partition: K340A loses ATP-dependent DNA binding
  >100-fold and plasmid stabilization [PMID:18826408].
- On the nucleoid when expressed alone [PMID:9529520].
- Operator binding (CTTTGC repeats) and cooperative SopA-SopB repression of sopAB [PMID:2670941;
  PMID:9529520]. Non-core.
- Filaments: ATP-dependent SopA polymers in vitro and dynamic polymers in vivo [PMID:16306264].
  Whether partition works through filaments or a diffusion-ratchet gradient is unresolved
  [PMID:25107339]; the review annotates only the mutant phenotype from that paper.

Decisions: NEW ATP hydrolysis activity, DNA binding, plasmid partitioning, bacterial nucleoid
(core); DNA-binding transcription repressor activity and negative regulation of transcription
(non-core).
