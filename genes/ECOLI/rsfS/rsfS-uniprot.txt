CC   -!- FUNCTION: Functions as a ribosomal silencing factor (PubMed:22829778).
CC       Interacts with ribosomal protein uL14 (rplN), blocking formation of
CC       intersubunit bridge B8. Prevents association of the 30S and 50S
CC       ribosomal subunits and the formation of functional ribosomes, thus
CC       repressing translation (PubMed:22829778). {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_01477, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22829778}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Member of a network of 50S ribosomal subunit biogenesis
CC       factors (ObgE, RluD, RsfS and DarP(YjgA)) which assembles along the
CC       30S-50S interface, preventing incorrect 23S rRNA structures from
CC       forming (PubMed:33639093). Binds to late stage, pre-50S ribosomal
CC       subunits where it contacts ObgE and enhances the latter's GTPase
CC       activity (PubMed:33639093). Addition to isolated ribosomal subunits
CC       partially inhibits their association, preventing translation
CC       (PubMed:22829778). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22829778,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33639093}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with ribosomal protein uL14 (rplN)
CC       (PubMed:22829778). In pre-50S ribosomal subunits interacts with uL14
CC       (rplN), bL19 (rplS) and ObgE (PubMed:33639093). {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_01477, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22829778,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33639093}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       P0AAT6; P33348: yehL; NbExp=3; IntAct=EBI-560192, EBI-489750;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_01477,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16980477, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22829778,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33639093}. Note=Comigrates with the 50S ribosomal
CC       subunit in sucrose gradients (PubMed:16980477), specifically with
CC       ribosomal protein uL14 (rplN) (PubMed:22829778). In pre-50S ribosomal
CC       subunits interacts with uL14 (rplN), bL19 (rplS) and ObgE
CC       (PubMed:33639093). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22829778,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33639093}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Cell viability is dramatically reduced in
CC       stationary phase, cells have a 10 hour growth block upon transition
CC       from rich to poor medium. Protein translation in stationary phase is
CC       derepressed. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22829778}.
