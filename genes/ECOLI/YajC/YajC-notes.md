# YajC research notes

YajC is a small single-pass inner-membrane protein encoded upstream of secD and
secF. It is identified in SecDF-YajC and SecYEG-SecDF-YajC-YidC assemblies
[PMID:24550475 "SecYEG interacts with the highly conserved YidC and SecDF-YajC
subcomplex, which facilitates translocation into and across the membrane"], but
its individual mechanistic role has not been resolved; direct E. coli genetics
reported that "yajC is neither essential nor a sec gene" [PMID:7507921 "An
analysis of yajC mutations constructed in vitro and recombined onto the
chromosome indicates that yajC is neither essential nor a sec gene"].

No independent molecular function should be assigned to YajC from the available
evidence. The direct Sec-complex process annotation is acceptable for the
purified active holo-translocon, but the core statement should stay at complex
membership and inner-membrane localization.

The AcrB co-crystal structure assigned a single transmembrane density as YajC
but could not prove that the AcrB-YajC contact was physiologically responsible
for the weak drug-susceptibility phenotype [PMID:18073115 "but this effect could
not conclusively be attributed to the loss of interactions between YajC and
AcrB"]. The generic protein-binding row should therefore be removed as an
uninformative and biologically unsettled interaction record.

The generic YajC `membrane` row should be narrowed to plasma membrane based on
direct YajC topology and holo-translocon evidence.
