CC   -!- FUNCTION: The SecYEG-SecDF-YajC-YidC holo-translocon (HTL) protein
CC       secretase/insertase is a supercomplex required for protein secretion,
CC       insertion of proteins into membranes, and assembly of membrane protein
CC       complexes (PubMed:27435098). The SecYEG complex is essential for
CC       assembly of a number of proteins and complexes, assembly is facilitated
CC       in the presence of the SecDF-YajC-YidC subcomplex (PubMed:27435098).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27435098}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of the SecDF-YidC-YajC translocase complex; YajC is also
CC       present in a homooligomeric complex in excess of the other components
CC       (PubMed:16079137). The SecDF-YidC-YajC translocase forms a supercomplex
CC       with SecYEG, called the holo-translocon (HTL) (PubMed:27435098). The
CC       stoichiometry of the super complex may be SecYEG:YidC:SecDF 4:3:1, YajC
CC       is in the reconstituted complex (with SecDF) but as no antibody is
CC       available it could not be quantified (PubMed:27435098).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16079137, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27435098}.
