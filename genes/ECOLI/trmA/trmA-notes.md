# TrmA source review

## 2026-10-26

Normal seeding verified P23003, K-12 trmA, locus b3965/JW3937. Seventeen annotation payloads and both raw-file hashes were captured before review. TrmA is a specificity comparator outside the paired 23S rRNA module: its tRNA U54 and tmRNA U341 reactions must not become RlmC/RlmD activities. The gene nevertheless receives a complete standalone review, including RNA binding, cytosolic localization and chaperone function.

Actual Falcon and perplexity-lite requests failed authentication/provider access; no provider-named manual report was made. All six original PMID requests completed. Additional target sources were recovered normally, including full PMID:18451029, PMID:17459887 and PMID:32597953. The FEBA lookup for keio/b3965 failed because neither local nor remote data was available, without a fabricated fitness artifact.

The original purified-enzyme PMID:6997293 abstract establishes SAM-dependent tRNA methylation and substrate/kinetic assays. PMID:1100618 compares matched transductants with and without tRNA m5U: it distinguishes ordinary measurements lacking a defect, a linked locus affecting growth, and a competitive advantage associated with modified tRNA. These are not interchangeable with universal essentiality. Full structural PMID:18451029 uses a trapped E358Q-RNA complex; PMID:17459887 separately assays TrmA mutations. Their target experiments are not direct RlmC residue measurements.

PMID:23603891 establishes tmRNA methylation from NMR analysis of native and TLD RNA recovered from deletion backgrounds. Canonical normal fetch remained abstract-only; its public PMC full text was read through the web tool. Additional normal URL/EuropePMC recovery attempts yielded browser challenges or HTTP500, not usable full-text cache. The full paper includes Aquifex-derived TLD constructs expressed in E. coli as well as full-length E. coli tmRNA, so construct and host identities remain separate. The evidence is not a purified-enzyme rate measurement. It supports broader RNA-MT scope in addition to the existing exact tRNA U54 activity. No tmRNA-specific GO molecular-function term was recovered in the live search; narrowing all activity/process rows only to tRNA would discard real substrate breadth.

Full PMID:32597953 directly measures tRNA folding using refolding followed by aminoacylation. Catalytic variants and RNA-binding mutants separate this role from methyl transfer; the paper states: “We directly show that TrmA enhances tRNA folding in vitro independent of its catalytic activity.” The live definition of GO:0140691 RNA folding chaperone describes RNA binding that assists folding, so a supported chaperone core is appropriate alongside methyltransferase activity. The pre-existing GO:0061818 process is retained; no NEW row is needed.

PMID:8419334 explicitly identifies both undermodified tRNAs and a 3-prime 16S rRNA fragment in stable TrmA-RNA complexes. The rRNA-binding rows remain non-core: the experiment does not establish rRNA methylation, and the complex was reported not to associate with ribosomes. The tRNA-binding rows are core, corroborated by the newer RNA-domain and folding assays. An inaccessible full paper is not grounds to erase an explicit and biologically coherent binding result from its abstract.

Cytosolic location is retained with the two independent experimental curator assignments and compatible native RNA biology. Full PMID:18304323 describes cytosol fractionation in MC4100; its target identifications are in supplementary Table S2. The actual linked supplementary download returned HTML browser-check content rather than an XLS file, so no claim of inspecting a TrmA row was made. PMID:15911532 remains abstract-only and offers its detailed table on request. These access limits are recorded rather than converted into evidence that the curator localization is false. No location NEW was added.

All seventeen rows are reviewed. Live QuickGO definitions confirm that RNA processing includes maturation, tRNA folding is an assisted-folding process, and the RNA methyltransferase umbrella is appropriate for dual tRNA/tmRNA specificity. Independent annotation-reviewer consultation agreed with preserving tmRNA breadth and distinguishing rRNA binding from methylation. Logs and payload/raw proofs are under `.cache/module-audit-logs/rrna-three-*`.
