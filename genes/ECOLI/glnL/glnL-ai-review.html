Gene Description

Nitrogen regulator II (NRII, NtrB) of Escherichia coli is the soluble cytoplasmic sensor histidine kinase/phosphatase of the NtrB/NtrC two-component system that controls nitrogen-regulated (ntr) gene expression. It contains an N-terminal PAS domain and a C-terminal transmitter (HisKA dimerisation/phosphoacceptor domain with His139, and HATPase_c ATP-binding domain). Under nitrogen limitation NtrB autophosphorylates on His139 using ATP and transfers the phosphoryl group to Asp54 of the response regulator NtrC (NRI), whose phosphorylated form activates sigma-54-dependent transcription of glnA and other ntr genes. When nitrogen is sufficient, the unmodified PII signal transduction protein GlnB (and GlnK) binds the NtrB kinase domain, inhibiting autophosphorylation and stimulating dephosphorylation of NtrC-phosphate; this interaction is allosterically controlled by 2-ketoglutarate and prevented by PII uridylylation, so NtrB couples the cellular glutamine/2-ketoglutarate signal to NtrC activity.

  domain with His139, and HATPase_c ATP-binding domain). Under nitrogen limitation NtrB autophosphorylates

  kinase domain, inhibiting autophosphorylation and stimulating dephosphorylation of NtrC-phosphate; this
