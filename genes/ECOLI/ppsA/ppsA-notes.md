# ppsA (P23538) curation notes

Curated by Claude (AI-assisted), 2026-11-11, as the E. coli exemplar for the PEP synthase annoton
of `modules/bacterial_pyruvate_metabolism.yaml`. Deep research was not run in this session; the
review rests on cached publications and the Swiss-Prot record.

- Dimer with a phosphohistidine intermediate [PMID:16880 "indicate that a histidyl residue is the site of phosphorylation"].
- PMID:4383554 and PMID:4383555 (Cooper & Kornberg; Brice & Kornberg 1967) have no cached abstracts;
  their IDA/IMP rows were accepted because UniProt curates the same activity from direct assays
  (PubMed:4319237) and the gluconeogenic role follows from the reaction.
- PpsR regulation is recorded in the description only (UniProt ACTIVITY REGULATION, PubMed:20044937).
