Gene Description

psd encodes phosphatidylserine decarboxylase, a pyruvoyl-dependent enzyme that catalyzes the second and last step of phosphatidylethanolamine biosynthesis, decarboxylating phosphatidylserine made by PssA to phosphatidylethanolamine and CO2. It is made as an inactive proenzyme that cleaves itself between Gly-253 and Ser-254; the serine becomes the pyruvoyl prosthetic group at the N-terminus of the small alpha chain, which stays associated with the large beta chain. The mature enzyme is bound to the cytoplasmic (inner) membrane through an N-terminal hydrophobic helical region, acts only on the lipid-linked serine moiety, and crystallizes as a homodimer.
