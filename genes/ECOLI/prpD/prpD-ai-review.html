Gene Description

PrpD is the 2-methylcitrate dehydratase of the propionate-inducible prpBCDE operon of Escherichia coli K-12. This monomeric (about 54 kDa) member of the MmgE/PrpD family catalyzes the dehydration of 2-methylcitrate, formed by PrpC, to 2-methyl-cis-aconitate via an unusual syn elimination; the aconitase AcnB then hydrates this intermediate to 2-methylisocitrate, which PrpB cleaves to pyruvate and succinate in the 2-methylcitrate cycle of propionate oxidation. PrpD has broad hydroxy-acid dehydratase specificity and also acts on citrate and isocitrate at lower rates, accounting for the minor "AcnC" aconitase activity that remains in acnAB-null strains. The purified E. coli enzyme was reported to carry one labile [2Fe-2S] cluster per monomer needed for maximal activity, whereas the Salmonella ortholog was purified without an iron-sulfur center. Expression is induced by propionate and subject to catabolite repression.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does E. coli PrpD genuinely require a [2Fe-2S] cluster, given that the Salmonella ortholog is active without any iron-sulfur center?

Q: Can PrpD hydrate cis-aconitate (reported by Brock et al.) or not (reported by Blank et al.), and which assay conditions explain the discrepancy?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Purify E. coli PrpD anaerobically and aerobically, quantify iron and sulfide, and compare 2-methylcitrate dehydratase kinetics of apo- and reconstituted enzyme; examine the 1SZQ structure for a plausible cluster binding site.
