# nirB evidence notes

NirB P08201 is the large catalytic subunit of soluble NADH nitrite reductase. [PMID:1435259: "No NADH-dependent nitrite reductase activity was detected in extracts from bacteria lacking either NirB or NirD, but a mixture of the two was as active as an extract from wild-type bacteria."] The older paper is abstract-only in the cache, but its explicit complementation and reconstitution results support positive assertions. PMID34323689 provides independent physiological support with accessible full text.

Native biochemistry supports FAD and siroheme. [PMID:7030314: "This highly active enzyme contained one non-covalently bound FAD molecule"; "These spectral features are characteristic of sirohaem".] EPR supports a [2Fe-2S] center [PMID:6297458: "The temperature-dependence of this signal is indicative of a [2Fe-2S] iron-sulphur cluster."]. The target curated record additionally assigns a [4Fe-4S] center. The old EPR failure to observe its signal is not an exclusion. Generic cluster binding is refined to both types, with this evidence distinction preserved. NADP binding is corrected to the established NAD(H) coenzyme assignment; this is not a claim that every possible NADP interaction was excluded.

PMID30406295 explicitly supports assimilation and fermentative ammonification; it does not justify denying assimilation as mere detoxification. ["Our results demonstrate that Nir confers a significant benefit during fermentative growth that reflects fermentative ammonification rather than detoxification."] PMID31641084 full text identifies NirBD in a NarG-associated respiratory interaction network and links nirB loss to nitrosative stress. Protein association with NarG does not confer nitrate-reductase activity on NirB. Both generic-binding rows are refined to the established NADH nitrite-reductase function, preserving interaction evidence.

PMID36068114 reports NirB-alone activity with artificial donors ["Methyl viologen and sodium dithionite were used as electron couples"]. It does not overturn native NADH-dependent reconstitution. No new physiological NADH-autonomy claim is made. PANTHER PTHR43809/PTHR43809:SF1 membership is recorded in the target UniProt file; observed PAINT PTNs are documented without invented ancestral reconstruction.

The E. coli panel is a physiological comparison, not an autonomous assimilatory nitrate route: nitrate uptake requires a separate upstream nitrate reductase before NirBD acts on nitrite. Assimilation is nevertheless experimentally supported. [PMID:34323689, Figure 3 supplement 1: "nitrate as the sole electron acceptor and as the sole nitrogen source"; "Under nitrate respiration, nitrate is converted to nitrite, which is subsequently converted to ammonium (NH4) by the cytosolic NADH-dependent nitrite reductase NirBD."] The study uses E. coli K-12 MG1655 derivatives; this is not evidence for cyanobacterial NirB accessory proteins.
