Gene Description

lolC encodes one of the two integral-membrane subunits of the essential Escherichia coli LolCDE complex, a type VII ABC-superfamily machine that extracts mature outer-membrane-destined lipoproteins from the outer leaflet of the inner membrane and hands them to the periplasmic carrier LolA. LolC has four transmembrane segments and a large periplasmic domain whose Hook and Pad elements recruit LolA and prime it to receive cargo; its partner subunit LolE binds the lipoprotein, and two copies of the ATPase LolD power release. LolCDE does not transport its substrate across the membrane.

Core Functions

Integral-membrane LolC subunit of LolCDE contributing to ATP-dependent release of outer-membrane-destined lipoproteins from the inner membrane, including recruitment and priming of LolA through its periplasmic Hook and Pad.

2026-10-23

  • Review created as an experimental exemplar for the reusable module
    modules/bacterial_lol_lipoprotein_trafficking.yaml; E. coli K-12 is the
    organism in which the LolCDE-LolA-LolB pathway was defined biochemically and
    genetically (Tokuda laboratory).
  • Provider deep research was not generated: no deep-research provider API key
    is configured in this session. The review is based on the UniProt record,
    the GOA rows and the cached publications in publications/, most of which
    are abstract-only (full_text_available: false).
  • GO has specific molecular-function terms for two Lol stages:
    GO:0140306 lipoprotein releasing activity (LolCDE; already annotated to
    E. coli LolC, LolD and LolE by IDA/IMP) and GO:0140598 lipoprotein carrier
    activity (LolA). No MF term exists for the LolB acceptance/insertion step.
