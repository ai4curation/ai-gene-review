Gene Description

PtsP is enzyme I(Ntr), the GAF-domain-containing paralogue of PTS enzyme I that starts the nitrogen-regulatory phosphotransferase system (PTS(Ntr)) of Escherichia coli. It autophosphorylates from phosphoenolpyruvate and transfers the phosphoryl group specifically to the phosphocarrier NPr (PtsO), not to HPr; NPr then phosphorylates the regulatory EIIA(Ntr) protein PtsN. Glutamine and 2-oxoglutarate act on its autophosphorylation through the N-terminal GAF domain, so the phosphorylation state of the chain reports nitrogen availability. The chain is a soluble cytoplasmic signalling relay that runs in parallel to the sugar-transport PTS and transports no sugar.
