CC   -!- FUNCTION: A helicase with DNA-dependent ATPase activity
CC       (PubMed:6319401, PubMed:8419285). Unwinds DNA duplexes with 3'-5'
CC       polarity (PubMed:2170128, PubMed:8419285). Translocates on single-
CC       stranded DNA with 3'-5' polarity (PubMed:2942537). Initiates unwinding
CC       more efficiently from a nicked substrate than double-stranded DNA
CC       (PubMed:2170128, PubMed:8419285). Involved in the post-incision events
CC       of nucleotide excision repair and methyl-directed mismatch repair, and
CC       probably also in repair of alkylated DNA (Probable) (PubMed:25484163).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2170128, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2942537,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8419285, ECO:0000305|PubMed:25484163}.
