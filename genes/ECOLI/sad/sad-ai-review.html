Gene Description

Sad (YneI, b1525) is a homodimeric aldehyde dehydrogenase of Escherichia coli K-12 that oxidizes succinate semialdehyde to succinate. Purified enzyme works preferentially with NAD+ but also reduces NADP+, so its activity is best described as cofactor-general succinate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase. It is one of two E. coli succinate-semialdehyde dehydrogenases, and the division of labour between them is clear. The NADP-specific GabD serves the GABA shunt during nitrogen limitation, whereas Sad is induced by succinate semialdehyde itself and by putrescine and acts chiefly as a valve that prevents toxic accumulation of this aldehyde. Through that reaction Sad supplies succinate from gamma-aminobutyrate derived from putrescine and arginine catabolism.
