# parE: maturation/topology annotation review

## 2026-11-06

ParE P20083 is the 630-aa K-12 reference E-type Topo IV subunit. Full-length ParE is longer than an early reported sequence; PMID:8227000 distinguishes active full-length ParC2ParE2 from inactive truncated constructs. Reconstitution proves a two-subunit enzyme rather than standalone ParE decatenation. The N-terminal domain structure with an ATP analogue and ParE-site substitutions affecting ATP kinetic parameters support its role in the nucleotide gate (PMID:15105144). One NEW IC ATP hydrolysis row records the missing intrinsic catalytic subreaction; it is not asserted from whole-enzyme ATP dependence alone, and no isolated-ParE decatenation assay is invented.

Current GO:0007062 describes tethering of sisters; Topo IV removes interlinks and reduces cohesion time in PMID:18765793. The existing process is corrected to chromosome segregation, preserving its machine payload. The named Topo IV complex term is used with the experimentally established C2E2 stoichiometry rather than following its currently misleading heterodimeric definition.

PMID:8104339 distinguishes Topo IV daughter-DNA resolution from gyrase-mediated torsion relief in reconstructed oriC/pBR322 replication. Plasmid partitioning is retained as a non-core extension of this decatenase function. Cytosolic localization is consistent with the native enzyme and curated MC4100 proteomics, but no target-specific supplementary protein-abundance value is claimed. Individual ParE DNA-binding affinity remains unmeasured in the evidence inspected; the existing inference is interpreted as participation in the DNA gate.
