Gene Description

MetK is the cytosolic S-adenosylmethionine synthetase of Escherichia coli K-12. Its functional homotetramer contains intersubunit active sites that join methionine to ATP and hydrolyze the released tripolyphosphate before product release. Catalysis uses divalent metal ions and is activated by potassium. MetK supplies SAM for methyl-group transfer and other SAM-dependent chemistry and participates directly in methionine cycling. Tagged-cell studies reveal both diffuse cytoplasmic signal and focal distributions.
