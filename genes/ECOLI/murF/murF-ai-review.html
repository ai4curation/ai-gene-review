Gene Description

MurF is the ATP-dependent UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-tripeptide:D-alanyl-D-alanine ligase of Escherichia coli (EC 6.3.2.10, an EC number that covers both the meso-DAP and L-Lys tripeptide forms; GO splits them into GO:0008766 and GO:0047480). This monomeric, soluble cytosolic enzyme catalyses the last cytoplasmic step of peptidoglycan precursor synthesis, adding the D-Ala-D-Ala dipeptide made by Ddl to UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-gamma-D-Glu-meso-diaminopimelate to form UDP-MurNAc-pentapeptide, the substrate of MraY. Its third-position specificity is relaxed: it acts with similar kinetics on DAP-analogue tripeptides and, in vitro, on the L-lysine tripeptide typical of Gram-positive bacteria, although in E. coli only the meso-DAP tripeptide is made. Thermosensitive murF mutants accumulate UDP-MurNAc-tripeptide, fail to make murein and lyse.

IBA
GO_REF:0000033 		KEEP AS NON CORE
Summary: L-Lys-tripeptide form of the MurF activity propagated at PTN000566380, with S. aureus MurF (a Lys-type enzyme) among the seeds.
Reason: GO:0047480 is defined on the L-lysine-containing tripeptide (UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu-L-Lys), the Gram-positive substrate; its sibling GO:0008766 is defined on the meso-DAP tripeptide, which is the physiological substrate in E. coli (Rhea 28374 in UniProt). E. coli MurF tolerates variation at the third position: kinetics are very similar with DAP and DAP analogues, and the Barreteau et al. 2008 review (PMID:18266853, web full text; abstract-only in cache) states that the meso-A2pm and L-Lys tripeptides are equally effective substrates. The L-Lys ligation is therefore a real in vitro activity, but E. coli never makes a Lys-tripeptide because MurE excludes L-lysine, so it is non-core. The PAINT node correctly places both forms at the MurF ancestor; E. coli MurF is within the clade and retains the broad third-position tolerance.

murF (P11880, b0086) notes

Identity

  • UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-tripeptide--D-alanyl-D-alanine ligase (D-Ala-D-Ala-adding enzyme), EC 6.3.2.10; MurCDEF family, MurF subfamily (HAMAP MF_02019; TIGR01143; PANTHER PTHR43024:SF1). Monomer (PMID:2186811). Structure PDB 1GG4.
  • No deep-research file; notes from cached publications, UniProt and web searches. FEBA fitness fetch failed.

Function

  • Final cytoplasmic step: adds D-Ala-D-Ala to UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-gamma-D-Glu-meso-A2pm to make UDP-MurNAc-pentapeptide PMID:2186811; monomer, turnover 784 min-1 PMID:2186811.
  • Third-position tolerance: kinetics similar with DAP-, cystathionine- and lanthionine-containing tripeptides PMID:8021219. Barreteau et al. 2008 review (PMID:18266853, web full text; cache is abstract only) states "The two forms of UDP-MurNAc-tripeptide (meso-A2pm and l-Lys) are equally effective as substrates." So E. coli MurF can use the Lys-tripeptide in vitro, but in vivo its substrate is the DAP-tripeptide because MurE excludes L-Lys.
  • Assay substrate for inhibitor screening was the DAP-tripeptide made by Mpl PMID:16377691.

GO term structure (checked via QuickGO, 2026-10-31)

  • GO:0047480 "UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-tripeptide-D-alanyl-D-alanine ligase activity" is defined on the L-lysine tripeptide (UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu-L-Lys).
  • GO:0008766 "...2,6-diaminopimelate-D-alanyl-D-alanine ligase activity" is defined on the meso-DAP tripeptide (Rhea 28374, the reaction in UniProt P11880).
  • They are siblings under GO:0016881 acid-amino acid ligase activity. GO:0008766 is the physiological activity of E. coli MurF; GO:0047480 is a real but non-physiological (in vitro) activity. The IBA for GO:0047480 (PTN000566380, donor S. aureus Q2FWH4) is a Lys-type activity propagated into a DAP-type organism.

Genetics

  • ts mutant (murF2, A288T) with temperature-sensitive D-Ala-D-Ala adding enzyme, impaired murein synthesis and accumulation of UDP-MurNAc-tripeptide PMID:4552998.

Module discrepancy

  • modules/peptidoglycan_precursor_biosynthesis.yaml MurF annoton uses GO:0047480 (L-Lys tripeptide form) with E. coli P11880 and P. putida Q88N80 as representatives; both are DAP-type organisms. The module should use GO:0008766 (or a DAP/Lys variant set mirroring the MurE node). Not edited here (out of scope).
