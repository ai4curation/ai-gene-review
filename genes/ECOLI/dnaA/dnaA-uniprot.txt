CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays an essential in the initiation and regulation of
CC       chromosomal replication. Binds in an ATP-dependent fashion to the
CC       origin of replication (oriC) to initiate formation of the DNA
CC       replication initiation complex once per cell cycle (PubMed:3036372).
CC       Binds the DnaA box (consensus sequence 5'-TTATC[CA]A[CA]A-3') and
CC       separates the double-stranded (ds)DNA (PubMed:3036372,
CC       PubMed:18216012). Forms a right-handed helical filament on oriC DNA;
CC       dsDNA binds to the exterior of the filament while single-stranded
CC       (ss)DNA is stabiized in the filament's interior (By similarity). The
CC       ATP-DnaA-oriC complex binds and stabilizes the upper strand of the AT-
CC       rich DNA unwinding element (DUE) (PubMed:18216012). Mutagenesis of
CC       residues that line the central pore blocks dsDNA strand separation
CC       (PubMed:18216012). Subsequent binding of DNA polymerase III subunits
CC       leads to replisome formation (PubMed:3036372, PubMed:18216012). The
CC       DnaA-ATP form converts to DnaA-ADP; once converted to ADP the protein
CC       cannot initiate replication, ensuring only 1 round of replication per
CC       cell cycle (PubMed:3036372). Binds ATP, ADP and dATP equally well,
CC       hydrolyzes ATP with a half-life of about 15 minutes, ATP hydrolysis is
CC       not required for pre-priming replisome formation, nucleotide exchange
CC       is very slow (PubMed:3036372). Binds acidic phospholipids
CC       (PubMed:9478970, PubMed:26272946). DnaA inhibits its own gene
CC       expression (PubMed:2981626) as well as that of other genes including
CC       dam, rpoH (PubMed:2540187), ftsA and mioC.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O66659, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18216012,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2540187, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26272946,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2981626, ECO:0000269|PubMed:3036372,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9478970}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Also required for replication of some plasmid DNA; binds 4
CC       DnaA boxes in the minimal plasmid RK2 replication origin (oriV).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15878847, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9242693}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=ATP + H2O = ADP + phosphate + H(+); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:13065,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:15377, ChEBI:CHEBI:15378, ChEBI:CHEBI:30616,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:43474, ChEBI:CHEBI:456216;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:15878847, ECO:0000269|PubMed:3036372};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Inactivated in part by the RIDA complex
CC       (regulatory inactivation of DnaA), composed of ATP-DnaA, Hda and the
CC       DNA-loaded beta sliding clamp (dnaN), which rapidly hydrolyzes ATP-DnaA
CC       to ADP-DnaA in a DNA-dependent fashion, preventing reinitiation of DNA
CC       replication (PubMed:9674428, PubMed:18977760). DnaA inactivation by
CC       RIDA is further stimulated by DNA synthesis (PubMed:9674428). Also
CC       inactivated by IHF in combination with the datA locus, which promotes
CC       ATP hydrolysis of ATP-DnaA, called DDAH (datA-dependent DnaA-ATP
CC       hydrolysis) (PubMed:23277577). Reactivation/rejuvenation of ATP-DnaA
CC       occurs by binding to specific chromosomal loci called DARS (DnaA
CC       reactivating sequences), which promote ADP release from ADP-DnaA
CC       (PubMed:19401329). DARS1 and DARS2 act independently to elevate the
CC       ATP-DnaA level in vivo; their deletion inhibits replication initiation
CC       (PubMed:19401329). Rejuvenation of ATP-DnaA may also occur in part on
CC       the cell inner membrane (PubMed:9478970, PubMed:26272946). Acetylation
CC       decreases the binding abilities to ATP and ADP and leads to inhibition
CC       of DNA replication initiation (PubMed:27484197).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18977760, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19401329,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23277577, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27484197,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9674428, ECO:0000305|PubMed:26272946,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:9478970}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Oligomerizes as a right-handed, spiral filament on DNA at oriC
CC       (By similarity). About 20 DnaA protein molecules bind to oriC. Forms
CC       the RIDA (regulatory inactivation of DnaA) complex with ATP-DnaA, ADP-
CC       Hda and the DNA-loaded sliding beta clamp (dnaN) (PubMed:9674428,
CC       PubMed:18977760). Interacts with DiaA; this stimulates the association
CC       of DnaA with the origin of replication (PubMed:17699754).
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_00377, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17699754,

CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: At least 4 systems specifically target DnaA to prevent
