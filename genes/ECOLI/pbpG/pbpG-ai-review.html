Gene Description

PbpG is penicillin-binding protein 7 (PBP7), a low-molecular-mass penicillin-binding protein of the peptidase S11 family. It is exported to the periplasm by a cleavable signal peptide, is released from whole cells by osmotic shock and is only loosely associated with membranes; PBP8 is a proteolytic product of PBP7. PBP7 is a D,D-endopeptidase that hydrolyses the D-Ala-meso-diaminopimelate 4-3 crosslinks of high-molecular-mass murein sacculi but not of isolated dimeric muropeptides. It plays a secondary role, after PBP4, in daughter-cell separation, binds the soluble lytic transglycosylase Slt70, and is one of the peptidoglycan endopeptidases scaffolded by the outer-membrane lipoprotein NlpI.
