Gene Description

CysD is the small catalytic subunit of Escherichia coli ATP sulfurylase. Together with the GTPase CysN, it converts sulfate and ATP to adenosine 5'-phosphosulfate (APS), initiating sulfate activation for the PAPS-dependent assimilatory pathway. CysN-coupled GTP hydrolysis drives this energetically unfavorable reaction. CysD-dependent sulfur metabolism also contributes to oxidative-stress resistance, without establishing direct peroxide-reductase activity.
