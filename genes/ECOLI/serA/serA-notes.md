# serA (E. coli K-12, P0A9T0) notes

Reviewed 2026-11-11 as the experimentally characterized bacterial exemplar of step 1 of
MODULE:phosphorylated_serine_biosynthesis. No deep-research provider file was generated for
this gene; the review rests on the cached publications (all abstract-only) and the UniProt
record.

- Function: D-3-phosphoglycerate dehydrogenase, kinetics on purified enzyme
  [PMID:8550422 "Escherichia coli serA-encoded 3-phosphoglycerate (3PG) dehydrogenase catalyzes
  the first step of the major phosphorylated pathway of L-serine (Ser) biosynthesis."].
- Side activity: 2-oxoglutarate reduction to 2-hydroxyglutarate, at rates comparable to the
  main reaction [PMID:8550422 "were the major in vitro activities of the SerA enzyme."];
  coupled to SerC this conserves NAD+ [PMID:29494135 "Both NAD+ and NADH appear to remain
  tightly bound to ecPGDH during the cycle"]. Kept as non-core (GO:0120568); the weak
  (S)-isomer activity (GO:0047545) is marked over-annotated.
- Regulation: V-type allosteric inhibition by L-serine at ACT-domain interfaces
  [PMID:8771194 "there are a total of four serine-binding sites on the enzyme."].
- PANTHER: UniProt places P0A9T0 in PTHR10996:SF282, not in PTHR43761 with the P. putida
  and S. pombe enzymes; see the PTHR10996 family review.
