# ppc (P00864) curation notes

Curated by Claude (AI-assisted), 2026-11-11, as the E. coli exemplar for the PEP carboxylase
annoton of `modules/bacterial_pyruvate_metabolism.yaml`. Deep research was not run in this
session; the review rests on cached publications and the Swiss-Prot record.

- His138 is required for carboxylation [PMID:1765093 "Mutation of His138 to asparagine (H138N) produced a protein which did not show carboxylase activity."].
- Acetyl-CoA is the dominant activator [PMID:7040354 "CoASAc was the most powerful activator."].
- FBP switch for anaplerosis [PMID:22522319 "The switch-like termination of anapleurosis results from depletion of fructose-1,6-bisphosphate (FBP), an ultrasensitive allosteric activator of PEP carboxylase."].
- Gluconeogenesis rows flagged: Ppc opposes gluconeogenesis (futile cycling when not switched off).
- TCA cycle IEA removed: anaplerotic, not a cycle step. Carbon fixation flagged.
