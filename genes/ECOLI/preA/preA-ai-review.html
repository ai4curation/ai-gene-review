Gene Description

preA (yeiA, b2147) encodes the pyrimidine-reducing subunit of the NAD-dependent dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase of Escherichia coli. PreA and PreT form a cytoplasmic heterotetramer that reduces uracil and thymine to 5,6-dihydrouracil and 5,6-dihydrothymine with NADH. PreA is homologous to the C-terminal dihydroorotate-dehydrogenase-like catalytic region of single-chain eukaryotic DPYD: it carries the FMN cofactor, the pyrimidine-binding site and two [4Fe-4S] ferredoxin motifs, and receives electrons from the NADH-oxidizing PreT subunit. The enzyme is required for dihydrouracil production in vivo, although E. coli lacks the downstream steps of the reductive pyrimidine pathway.
