# preA (P25889, b2147) notes

## 2026-10-23 review

Reviewed for the `pyrimidine_degradation` module. Deep research could not be
run in this session (no provider API key); the review rests on cached abstracts
and the curated UniProt record.

- PreA is the pyrimidine-reducing subunit: dihydroorotate-dehydrogenase-like
  catalytic domain (IPR005720), FMN, substrate-binding sites and two [4Fe-4S]
  ferredoxin motifs. Orthologous role to PSEPK PydA.
- The heteromer requires both subunits [PMID:34097066 "EcDPD consists of two subunits, PreT and PreA, and requires FAD, FMN and Fe-S clusters for activity."].
- NADH dehydrogenase activity (IDA, PMID:21169495) was marked as
  over-annotated rather than removed: the NADH/FAD domain is on PreT, but the
  full text was not available to check whether PreA alone was assayed.
- Swarming-motility IMP from a Keio screen [PMID:17122336] kept as non-core.
