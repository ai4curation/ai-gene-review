Gene Description

MnmA is a cytosolic ATP-dependent tRNA-uridine 2-sulfurtransferase that incorporates sulfur at C2 of wobble uridine in tRNAs for lysine, glutamate and glutamine. It activates U34 by adenylation and couples sulfur utilization to the IscS/Tus sulfur-relay system. The physiological requirement for an iron-sulfur cofactor in this D-type MnmA and the chemistry of the terminal sulfur handoff remain unresolved.
