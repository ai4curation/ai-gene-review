Gene Description

ArgA is the cytoplasmic N-acetylglutamate synthase that initiates the linear arginine biosynthetic pathway in Escherichia coli. It transfers the acetyl group of acetyl-CoA to L-glutamate, producing N-acetylglutamate and CoA. The enzyme is feedback inhibited by L-arginine and assembles as a homohexamer; feedback-resistant variants increase arginine biosynthesis.
