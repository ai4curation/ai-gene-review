Gene Description

CysE is the serine O-acetyltransferase (SAT, EC 2.3.1.30) of Escherichia coli. This cytosolic hexapeptide-repeat acyltransferase transfers the acetyl group of acetyl-CoA to L-serine, forming O-acetyl-L-serine, the substrate that O-acetylserine sulfhydrylase (CysK or CysM) sulfhydrylates to L-cysteine. The enzyme is a hexamer (a dimer of homotrimers) and is feedback-inhibited by L-cysteine; a feedback-insensitive point mutant causes cysteine excretion. Its C terminus docks into the active site of CysK to form the bienzyme cysteine synthase complex, in which CysE remains active while the bound CysK is inhibited.

Core Functions

Catalyses acetyl transfer from acetyl-CoA to L-serine, forming O-acetyl-L-serine for sulfhydrylation by CysK/CysM, in the cytosol and as the SAT subunit of the CysE-CysK cysteine synthase complex.
