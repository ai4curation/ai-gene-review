CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the conversion of cytidine diphosphate
CC       diacylglycerol (CDP-DG) and L-serine into phosphatidylserine
CC       (PubMed:39693441, PubMed:8824831). Essential for biosynthesis of
CC       phosphatidylethanolamine, one of the major membrane phospholipids
CC       (PubMed:39693441, PubMed:8824831). Phosphatidylserine is later
CC       converted into phosphatidylethanolamine via the action of
CC       phosphatidylserine decarboxylase psd (PubMed:39693441, PubMed:8824831).
CC       Associates with the bacterial membrane for its role, which depends on
CC       the levels of anionic phospholipids in the membrane (PubMed:39693441,
CC       PubMed:8824831). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8824831}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=a CDP-1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol + L-serine = a 1,2-diacyl-sn-
CC         glycero-3-phospho-L-serine + CMP + H(+); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:16913,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:15378, ChEBI:CHEBI:33384, ChEBI:CHEBI:57262,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:58332, ChEBI:CHEBI:60377; EC=2.7.8.8;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8824831};
CC   -!- BIOPHYSICOCHEMICAL PROPERTIES:
CC       Kinetic parameters:
CC         KM=0.14 mM for CDP-1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol (at pH 7.5 and 25 degrees
CC         Celsius) {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441};
CC         KM=0.35 mM for L-serine (at pH 7.5 and 25 degrees Celsius)
CC         {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441};
CC         Note=kcat is 69.7 sec(-1) for the CDP-1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol and 59.8
CC         sec(-1) for L-serine (at pH 7.5 and 25 degrees Celsius).
CC         {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441};
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Phospholipid metabolism; phosphatidylethanolamine
CC       biosynthesis; phosphatidylethanolamine from CDP-diacylglycerol: step
CC       1/2. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Multimeric (PubMed:16294310). Interacts with ACP, YbgC and
CC       PlsB, forming altogether a complex at the inner membrane
CC       (PubMed:16294310). Monomeric and dimeric; existing in equilibrium, but
CC       the monomer probably exhibits preferential membrane association
CC       (PubMed:39693441). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16294310,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm. Cell inner membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441}; Peripheral membrane protein; Cytoplasmic
CC       side {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The phospholipase domain (PLD) repeats contain two atypical
CC       HxKxxxxD (HKD) motifs, which utilize Glu-385 and Asp-169, in place of
CC       the Asp-145 and Asp-364 found in the respective motifs, to facilitate
CC       the catalytic events that produce phosphatidylserine.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39693441}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Shows growth dependency on divalent metal Mg(2+)
CC       and significantly reduces the levels of phosphatidylethanolamine.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8824831}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the CDP-alcohol phosphatidyltransferase class-II
CC       family. {ECO:0000305}.
