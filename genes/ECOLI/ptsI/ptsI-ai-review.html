Gene Description

ptsI of Escherichia coli K-12 encodes enzyme I (EI), the general, non-sugar-specific first component of the phosphoenolpyruvate (PEP)-dependent sugar phosphotransferase system (PTS). The cytoplasmic homodimer has an N-terminal HPr-binding domain, a central domain carrying the phosphorylated His189 and a C-terminal PEP-binding domain. EI autophosphorylates from PEP in an Mg(2+)-dependent reaction and transfers the phosphoryl group to His15 of HPr, which feeds the sugar-specific Enzyme II complexes. EI also phosphorylates the HPr-like domain of the fructose multiphosphoryl transfer protein FPr, so it initiates fructose PTS uptake as well as uptake of glucose and other PTS sugars. EI concentrates near the cell pole, where the pole-localizer TmaR sequesters it and so modulates sugar uptake.

Core Functions

General enzyme I of the sugar PTS. Autophosphorylates on His189 from phosphoenolpyruvate and transfers the phosphoryl group to HPr, and to the HPr-like domain of FPr in the fructose system, initiating the phosphorelay that drives sugar uptake with concomitant phosphorylation.
