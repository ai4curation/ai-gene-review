Gene Description

MetH is the cytosolic cobalamin-dependent methionine synthase of Escherichia coli K-12. It transfers a methyl group from methyltetrahydrofolate through enzyme-bound cobalamin to zinc-activated homocysteine, producing methionine and regenerating tetrahydrofolate. Distinct domains bind homocysteine, folate, cobalamin and SAM. Occasional oxidation of the cofactor requires reductive reactivation using reduced E. coli flavodoxin and SAM; this partner interaction has biochemical specificity. Activity depends on available cobalamin and does not imply endogenous vitamin B12 synthesis.
