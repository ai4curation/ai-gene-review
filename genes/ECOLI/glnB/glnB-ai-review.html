Gene Description

GlnB (PII) is a small homotrimeric cytoplasmic signal transduction protein of the PII family that integrates the nitrogen, carbon and energy status of E. coli. Each subunit has an exposed T-loop carrying Tyr51, which the bifunctional uridylyltransferase/uridylyl-removing enzyme GlnD reversibly uridylylates in response to glutamine, while ATP/ADP and 2-oxoglutarate bind in the inter-subunit clefts. Unmodified, effector-bound GlnB binds the kinase domain of the histidine kinase NtrB (NRII), inhibiting its kinase and activating its phosphatase toward NtrC-phosphate, thereby shutting down sigma-54-dependent Ntr gene transcription, and it stimulates GlnE-catalysed adenylylation (inactivation) of glutamine synthetase; GlnB-UMP instead promotes GlnE-catalysed deadenylylation. GlnB also binds and inhibits acetyl-CoA carboxylase (BC-BCCP) and stimulates L-aspartase, and can form heterotrimers with its paralog GlnK.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

2-oxoglutarate binding

Definition: Binding to 2-oxoglutarate (alpha-ketoglutarate), the C5 dicarboxylic acid intermediate of the tricarboxylic acid cycle.

Justification: PII signal transduction proteins sense carbon/nitrogen status by binding 2-oxoglutarate at the inter-subunit ATP site, a well-defined effector-binding activity shared across the PII family. GO offers only the generic carboxylic acid binding (GO:0031406), so the PII effector site cannot be annotated at the same precision as ATP binding. A specific child of GO:0031406 would close that gap.
