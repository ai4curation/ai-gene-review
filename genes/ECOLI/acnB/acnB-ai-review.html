Gene Description

AcnB (aconitate hydratase B, aconitase B) is the major aconitase of Escherichia coli, a cytosolic monomeric enzyme of about 93 kDa that carries a single catalytic [4Fe-4S] cluster. It catalyses the reversible isomerization of citrate to isocitrate via cis-aconitate in the tricarboxylic acid cycle and is the principal aconitase during exponential growth, while the paralog AcnA is a more stable, stress- and stationary-phase-induced isozyme. AcnB also hydrates 2-methyl-cis-aconitate to (2R,3S)-2-methylisocitrate, acting with PrpD in the 2-methylcitrate cycle of propionate oxidation. AcnB has an unusual domain order (4-1-2-3) with an additional N-terminal HEAT-like domain (domain 5) that forms a substrate tunnel and mediates iron-dependent homodimerization. Its exposed cluster is highly labile to oxidants and iron depletion, and the resulting apo-protein acts as an iron-regulatory-protein-like post-transcriptional regulator: it binds the 3' untranslated regions of acnA and acnB mRNAs, stabilizing them, lowers the stability of the sodA transcript, and binds the ftsH transcript, linking central metabolism to oxidative-stress and iron-status responses.

  AcnB (aconitate hydratase B, aconitase B) is the major aconitase of
  Escherichia coli, a cytosolic monomeric enzyme of about 93 kDa that carries a
  single catalytic [4Fe-4S] cluster. It catalyses the reversible isomerization of
  citrate to isocitrate via cis-aconitate in the tricarboxylic acid cycle and is
  the principal aconitase during exponential growth, while the paralog AcnA is a
  more stable, stress- and stationary-phase-induced isozyme. AcnB also hydrates
  2-methyl-cis-aconitate to (2R,3S)-2-methylisocitrate, acting with PrpD in the
  2-methylcitrate cycle of propionate oxidation. AcnB has an unusual domain order
  (4-1-2-3) with an additional N-terminal HEAT-like domain (domain 5) that forms a
  substrate tunnel and mediates iron-dependent homodimerization. Its exposed
  cluster is highly labile to oxidants and iron depletion, and the resulting
  apo-protein acts as an iron-regulatory-protein-like post-transcriptional
  regulator: it binds the 3' untranslated regions of acnA and acnB mRNAs,
  stabilizing them, lowers the stability of the sodA transcript, and binds the
  ftsH transcript, linking central metabolism to oxidative-stress and
  iron-status responses.
