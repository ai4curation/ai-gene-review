# parC: maturation/topology annotation review

## 2026-11-06

ParC P0AFI2 is the 752-aa K-12 reference A-type Topo IV subunit. [PMID:8227000 "The native forms of ParC and ParE were a dimer and a monomer, respectively; whereas the active form of Topo IV was a heterotetramer, ParC2ParE2."] This paper corrects older truncated gene models: full-length ParC and ParE were required for activity. ParC cannot be swapped independently with GyrA in the reconstitution of PMID:1334483. The valid GO:0009340 complex label is used while its current 'heterodimeric' definition is flagged here as inconsistent with the E. coli C2E2 stoichiometry.

The DNA-binding row assigned by EcoliWiki to PMID:18023670 has a confirmed wrong reference. Its abstract explicitly reports endobronchial ultrasound sampling in patients with metastatic lung tumors. The original payload is preserved, the true DNA-binding claim is supported independently by PMID:16023670, and a WRONG_REFERENCE defect is recorded without guessing the intended source. ATP-binding IBA/IEA at PTN001607041 and the A-subunit InterPro mapping are also queried: ParE carries the nucleotide pocket (PMID:15105144), whereas ParC carries the DNA cleavage/recognition machinery.

GO:0007062 means sisters becoming tethered. PMID:18765793 instead shows Topo IV releasing cohesion; the row is corrected to chromosome segregation. The abstract supplies this direction directly, though normal force-fetch could not recover the full experiment text. Conditional membrane association is retained non-core: PMID:1334483 requires DNA, and PMID:2842295 reports Mg-dependent fraction association, not a transmembrane ParC topology. Plasmid partitioning is an in-vitro decatenation context (PMID:8104339), not evidence for a ParC partition motor.
