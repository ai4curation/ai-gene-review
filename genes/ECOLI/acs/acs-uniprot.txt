CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the conversion of acetate into acetyl-CoA (AcCoA),
CC       an essential intermediate at the junction of anabolic and catabolic
CC       pathways (PubMed:15327942, PubMed:21941, PubMed:7751300). Acs undergoes
CC       a two-step reaction. In the first half reaction, Acs combines acetate
CC       with ATP to form acetyl-adenylate (AcAMP) intermediate. In the second
CC       half reaction, it can then transfer the acetyl group from AcAMP to the
CC       sulfhydryl group of CoA, forming the product AcCoA (PubMed:15327942).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15327942, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21941,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7751300}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Enables the cell to use acetate during aerobic growth to
CC       generate energy via the TCA cycle, and biosynthetic compounds via the
CC       glyoxylate shunt. Acetylates CheY, the response regulator involved in
CC       flagellar movement and chemotaxis. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1390767,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15327942}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
