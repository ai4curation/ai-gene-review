Gene Description

PaaG is a cytoplasmic crotonase-superfamily (enoyl-CoA hydratase/isomerase fold) enzyme of the Escherichia coli aerobic phenylacetate catabolic pathway. It catalyzes the reversible isomerization of the unstable ring 1,2-epoxyphenylacetyl-CoA, produced by the PaaABCE monooxygenase, into the seven-membered O-heterocyclic enol ether 2-oxepin-2(3H)-ylideneacetyl-CoA (oxepin-CoA; EC 5.3.3.18), the substrate for hydrolytic ring opening by PaaZ. Like delta3,delta2-enoyl-CoA isomerases it uses a single catalytic aspartate (Asp-144) as a proton relay, and it is probably also responsible for the later 3,4- to 2,3-dehydroadipyl-CoA double-bond shift in the beta-oxidation-like part of the pathway. PaaG forms homotrimeric discs that pack onto a PaaF (enoyl-CoA hydratase) hexamer to form a stable four-layered PaaF-PaaG complex. paaG mutants cannot grow on phenylacetate and divert the epoxide into dead-end products such as 2-hydroxyphenylacetate.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

1,2-epoxyphenylacetyl-CoA isomerase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 2-(1,2-epoxy-1,2-dihydrophenyl)acetyl-CoA = 2-oxepin-2(3H)-ylideneacetyl-CoA (EC 5.3.3.18; RHEA:31843).

Justification: PaaG has a well-defined, experimentally demonstrated activity with an EC number and Rhea reaction but no corresponding GO term; current annotation can only use the generic EC 5.3.3 parent GO:0016863.

Function

  • Purified His-tagged PaaG reversibly isomerizes ring 1,2-epoxyphenylacetyl-CoA to oxepin-CoA (EC 5.3.3.18).
    PMID:20660314
    PMID:20660314
  • Crotonase superfamily; resembles delta3,delta2-enoyl-CoA isomerases with a single catalytic Asp.
    PMID:20660314
  • Probable second role: 3,4- to 2,3-dehydroadipyl-CoA isomerization after the first PaaJ thiolysis — stated as
    "probably" by Teufel 2010 and modelled structurally by Grishin 2012; not directly assayed. Not annotated (kept as a question).
  • paaG deletion mutant cannot grow on phenylacetate and accumulates ring-1,2-dihydroxy-1,2-dihydrophenylacetyl lactone
    PMID:12846838.
  • PaaG forms homotrimeric discs and a stable PaaF-PaaG hetero-complex (four-layer sandwich; PDB 4FZW); active site
    adapted to Paa intermediates, steric hindrance prevents octanoyl conformations PMID:22961985. This argues against
    the IBA to fatty acid beta-oxidation (propagated from mammalian ECH/ECI homologs).

  • GO:0016853 isomerase activity IDA -> MODIFY to GO:0016863 (intramolecular oxidoreductase activity, transposing C=C bonds; the EC 5.3.3 class containing EC 5.3.3.18). No GO term exists for EC 5.3.3.18 itself; proposed new term.
  • GO:0010124 x3 ACCEPT; GO:0006635 IBA REMOVE; GO:0005515 REMOVE (policy); GO:0042802 and GO:1902494 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
