Gene Description

PaaH is the NAD+-dependent 3-hydroxyadipyl-CoA dehydrogenase of the aerobic, CoA-thioester-based phenylacetate catabolic pathway of Escherichia coli, encoded in the paaABCDEFGHIJK operon. In the beta-oxidation-like lower part of the pathway it oxidizes 3-hydroxyadipyl-CoA, made by the PaaF hydratase, to 3-oxoadipyl-CoA, which the PaaJ thiolase then cleaves to succinyl-CoA and acetyl-CoA. PaaH belongs to the 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase family, with an N-terminal NAD-binding Rossmann domain and C-terminal 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase domains, and acts as a stand-alone enzyme rather than as part of a FadBA-like multienzyme complex. paaH deletion mutants cannot grow on phenylacetate and accumulate 3-hydroxyadipate.
