Gene Description

GlnK is a homotrimeric PII-family signal transduction protein of E. coli, paralogous to GlnB and encoded in the nitrogen-regulated glnK-amtB operon, so it is expressed mainly under nitrogen limitation. Like GlnB it binds ATP, ADP and 2-oxoglutarate and is reversibly uridylylated on T-loop Tyr51 by GlnD. Its principal role is control of the AmtB ammonia channel: when extracellular ammonium rises, GlnK is deuridylylated and binds the cytoplasmic face of AmtB, inserting its T-loop into the pore exit to block ammonia conduction and sequestering GlnK at the inner membrane. GlnK can also activate GlnE-catalysed adenylylation of glutamine synthetase, forms heterotrimers with GlnB, and limits Ntr gene expression during nitrogen starvation, which is important for recovery from starvation.
