Gene Description

RlmD (RumA/YgcA) is an Escherichia coli K-12 SAM-dependent methyltransferase that converts U1939 of 23S rRNA to 5-methyluridine. Purified enzyme methylates 23S rRNA and short U1939-containing transcripts, but not the tested 16S rRNA or tRNA substrates. Its RNA-binding region recognizes and remodels the target RNA around a catalytic Cys389-containing methyltransferase domain. An experimentally characterized [4Fe-4S] cluster is associated with the RNA-binding region; the methyl-transfer reaction is not a radical-SAM redox reaction, and a physiological oxidative-stress regulatory role remains a hypothesis.

Core Functions

SAM-dependent C5 methylation of uridine 1939 in 23S rRNA. RlmD can act on defined short RNA substrates; its rRNA-binding architecture and experimentally established Fe-S cluster are integral biochemical features, distinct from a hypothesized physiological redox-regulatory function.
