The first excerpt is part of the sentence reporting DLS of both mycobacterial Cpn10 and E. coli GroES. The second compares E. coli Glu53 with the experimentally coordinating Mtb Glu55. The actual E. coli coordination site remains a hypothesis.

Methods use recombinant commercial Sigma GroES; no exact E. coli donor strain or sequence verification is provided there. Table2 compares GroES near pH 7.4 without calcium with 20 mM CaCl2: the main hydrodynamic radius changes from 3.70 nm to 4.27 nm. This is biochemical response evidence corroborating an ancestral broad metal-binding inference, not a direct E. coli site-occupancy assay, a quantified affinity, or proof of physiological calcium regulation. The direct calcium-coordination structure is Mtb Cpn10; it has additional acidic roof-loop residues absent in E. coli.
