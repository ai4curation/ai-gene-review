# pyrH (E. coli K-12, P0A7E9) notes

Deep research was not generated for this gene; these notes summarise the cached
publications and the UniProt record.

- pyrH encodes UMP kinase; it is identical to smbA and belongs to the aspartokinase
  (amino-acid kinase) family, not to the adenylate-kinase-fold NMP kinases
  [PMID:7711027 "The bacterial UMP-kinase did not display significant sequence similarity to known
  nucleoside monophosphate kinases"].
- Homohexamer; GTP is an allosteric activator and UTP an inhibitor [PMID:7711027 "The protein consists
  of six identical subunits"].
- In Gram-negative UMP kinases GTP mainly reverses inhibition by excess UMP, and UTP binds a site
  overlapping the catalytic centre (PMID:17210578).
- Localization: diffuse cytoplasmic plus membrane-proximal foci (PMID:17272300); cytosol in
  proteomics (PMID:15911532, PMID:18304323).
- Family: PANTHER PTHR42833 (SF4); the PTHR42833 family review uses P0A7E9 as its reference protein.
  No PAINT IRD/IKR loss on the lineage (`just paint-losses ECOLI pyrH`).
