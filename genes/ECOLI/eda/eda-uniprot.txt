CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the degradation of glucose via the Entner-
CC       Doudoroff pathway (PubMed:15659677, PubMed:1624451). Catalyzes the
CC       reversible, stereospecific retro-aldol cleavage of 2-keto-3-deoxy-6-
CC       phosphogluconate (KDPG) to pyruvate and D-glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate
CC       (PubMed:11342129, PubMed:17962400, PubMed:17981470). In vitro, can also
CC       catalyze the cleavage of 2-keto-4-hydroxy-4-(2'-pyridyl)butyrate
CC       (KHPB), 2-keto-3-deoxy-6-phosphogalactonate (KDPGal), and of the
CC       substrate analogs 2-keto-3-deoxy-gluconate (KDG) and 2-keto-4-
CC       hydroxyoctonate (KHO) (PubMed:17962400). Can accept some nucleophiles
CC       other than pyruvate, including 2-oxobutanoate, phenylpyruvate and
CC       fluorobutanoate (PubMed:17981470). In addition to its KDPG aldolase
CC       activity, catalyzes the reversible cleavage of 2-keto-4-
CC       hydroxyglutarate (KHG) to glyoxylate and pyruvate (PubMed:1098660,
CC       PubMed:1339418, PubMed:4560498, PubMed:7016177). The enzyme is
CC       stereoselective for the S-enantiomer of KHG (PubMed:1098660,
CC       PubMed:4560498). Cleavage of KHG could serve in tricarboxylic acid
CC       (TCA) cycle regulation or, when operating in the reverse direction, in
CC       the detoxification of glyoxylate (Probable). Finally, also shows a high
CC       level of oxaloacetate beta-decarboxylase activity (PubMed:1339418,
CC       PubMed:4560498). The enzyme could have several physiological roles and
CC       function as a stress response protein in addition to its role in the
CC       catabolism of sugar acids (PubMed:15659677).

CC   -!- INDUCTION: Part of the edd-eda operon, whose expression is controlled
CC       by three functional promoters (P1, P2 and P4), each of which is
CC       responsible for induction under different growth conditions
CC       (PubMed:15659677, PubMed:1624451). Both P2 and P4 are responsible for
CC       the high basal level of expression (PubMed:15659677, PubMed:1624451).
CC       P1 controls induction on gluconate and is regulated by GntR
CC       (PubMed:15659677). P2 is induced by growth on glucuronate,
CC       galacturonate and methyl-beta-D-glucuronide, and is mediated directly
CC       by KdgR (PubMed:15659677). P4 is active under conditions of phosphate
CC       starvation and is directly controlled by PhoB (PubMed:15659677). In
CC       addition, starvation for carbon, but not the transition to stationary
CC       phase, slightly increases Eda synthesis (PubMed:15659677). CsrA exerts
CC       indirect negative control of Eda levels (PubMed:15659677).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15659677, ECO:0000269|PubMed:1624451}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Cells lacking this gene show no detectable KHG
CC       aldolase activity (PubMed:1339418). Does not affect growth on glucose
CC       and native reaction flux (PubMed:31390539).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1339418, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31390539}.
CC   -!- BIOTECHNOLOGY: Is an attractive enzyme for development as a
CC       biocatalyst, however, as with most enzymatic catalysts, substrate
CC       selectivity ultimately limits the broad use of KDPG aldolase as a
CC       synthetic reagent (PubMed:17962400). The enzyme has been the subject of
CC       directed evolution studies and structure-based approach to identify
CC       mutants with enhanced substrate specificity (PubMed:11342129,
CC       PubMed:17962400). Site-directed mutagenesis of the active site or the
CC       phosphate-binding pocket can expand substrate selectivity for unnatural
CC       substrates for use in biocatalysis applications (PubMed:11342129,
CC       PubMed:17962400). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11342129,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17962400}.
