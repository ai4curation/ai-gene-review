CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transferase that catalyzes the synthesis of a CDP-
CC       diacylglycerol (CDP-DAG), the activated form of a phosphatidate
CC       (PubMed:2995359, PubMed:30936205). It is essential for phospholipid
CC       biosynthesis (PubMed:2995359). It is also involved in the biosynthesis
CC       of MPIase, a glycolipid essential for membrane protein integration and
CC       cell growth (PubMed:30718729, PubMed:30739787). It displays a
CC       preference for phosphatidic acids bearing at least one double bond in
CC       their fatty acyl moieties (PubMed:2995359). It can use CTP and dCTP,
CC       but not ATP, UTP, dATP, dTTP and dGTP (PubMed:2995359). The reaction is
CC       reversible in vitro (PubMed:2995359). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2995359,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30718729, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30739787,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30936205}.
