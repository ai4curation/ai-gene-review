CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the type II fatty acid elongation cycle

CC       range of acyl-ACP by the addition of two carbons from malonyl-ACP to an
CC       acyl acceptor (PubMed:22017312, PubMed:9013860). Can efficiently
CC       catalyze the conversion of palmitoleoyl-ACP (cis-hexadec-9-enoyl-ACP)
CC       to cis-vaccenoyl-ACP (cis-octadec-11-enoyl-ACP), an essential step in
CC       the thermal regulation of fatty acid composition (PubMed:6988423,
CC       PubMed:9013860). Can use acyl chains from C-6 to C-16 (PubMed:22017312,
CC       PubMed:9013860). Is able to catalyze the condensation reaction when CoA
CC       is the carrier for both substrates (PubMed:22017312).
