Gene Description

NarL is the cytoplasmic response regulator of the nitrate/nitrite-responsive NarX/NarQ-NarL two-component system of Escherichia coli. It consists of an N-terminal CheY-like receiver domain that is phosphorylated on Asp59 by the membrane sensor kinases NarX or NarQ (and dephosphorylated chiefly through the phosphatase activity of NarX), joined by a linker to a C-terminal LuxR/FixJ-type helix-turn-helix DNA-binding domain. In unphosphorylated NarL the receiver domain packs against the output domain and occludes its DNA-recognition surface; phosphorylation releases the output domain, which dimerizes on DNA and binds TACYYMT heptamer sequences, preferentially arranged as 7-2-7 inverted repeats but also in other arrangements. Phospho-NarL is a dual-function transcription factor that, during anaerobic growth in the presence of nitrate or nitrite and often together with FNR, activates operons for nitrate respiration such as narGHJI (nitrate reductase), fdnGHI (formate dehydrogenase-N) and nirBDC (NADH-nitrite reductase), and represses operons for alternative anaerobic respiratory pathways such as frdABCD (fumarate reductase), dmsABC (DMSO/TMAO reductase) and ynfEFGHI, thereby directing electron flow to nitrate as the preferred anaerobic terminal acceptor.

Core Functions

Receiver-domain phosphoacceptor of the NarX/NarQ-NarL two-component system: Asp59 is phosphorylated by the nitrate/nitrite sensor kinases NarX and NarQ (and dephosphorylated via NarX phosphatase activity), switching NarL from an autoinhibited state to its DNA-binding form.

Structure / mechanism

  • Unphosphorylated NarL: output domain packs against receiver, occluding the
    DNA-recognition surface ("telephone receiver" model) [PMID:8780507;
    PMID:9521685].
  • NarL C-terminal domain-DNA co-crystal: binds as a dimer with a new
    dimerization interface, bends DNA PMID:12352954.

DNA binding and regulation

  • Binds TACYYMT heptamers; 7-2-7 inverted repeats preferred, but NarL (unlike
    NarP) also recognizes other heptamer arrangements [PMID:9302020; PMID:7854119;
    PMID:8736541].
  • Activator: narGHJI, fdnGHI, nirB (with FNR) [PMID:8736541; PMID:7854119].
  • Repressor: frdABCD, dmsABC, ynfEFGHI (binds -10 overlapping site at ynfE,
    forms NarL-FNR-RNAP-promoter complex) [PMID:19251855; PMID:7854119 (nrf
