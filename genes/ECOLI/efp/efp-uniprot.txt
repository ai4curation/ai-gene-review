CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in peptide bond synthesis. Alleviates ribosome
CC       stalling that occurs when 3 or more consecutive proline residues or the
CC       sequence Pro-Pro-Gly is present in a protein, by stabilizing the P-site
CC       tRNA thus promoting a favorable geometry for peptide bond formation
CC       (PubMed:23239623, PubMed:23239624, PubMed:29100052). Alleviates
CC       ribosome stalling at di-Pro-containing sequences; in rare cases motifs
CC       lacking di-Pro can also be targeted (PubMed:39622818). Beta-lysylation
CC       at Lys-34 is required for alleviation. The Pro codons and their context
CC       do not affect activity; only consecutive Pro residues (not another
CC       amino acid) are affected by EF-P. Has stimulatory effects on peptide
CC       bond formation between ribosome-bound initiator tRNA(fMet) and
CC       puromycin, and N-acetyl-Phe tRNA(Phe)-primed poly(U)-directed poly(Phe)
CC       synthesis. Its function partially overlaps with EfpL and UUP
CC       (PubMed:39622818). A tRNA(Pro) in the P-site and guanosine in the first
CC       position of the E-site codon promote EF-P (and EfpL) binding to the
CC       ribosome (Probable) (PubMed:39622818). Can also cause translational
CC       pausing, probably by blocking tRNA translocation to the E-site
CC       (PubMed:39622818). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23239623,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23239624, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39622818}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein biosynthesis; polypeptide chain elongation.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer (PubMed:29100052). In stalled 70S ribosomes binds in
CC       the tRNA E-site, stabilizing the peptidyl-tRNA while positioning the
CC       nascent Pro-containing peptide chain so that it extends into the lumen
CC       of the ribosome exit tunnel (PubMed:29100052). Contacts the anticodon
CC       stem loop and D-loop of peptidyl-tRNA(Pro) in the P-site, 16S rRNA, the
CC       E-site codon of mRNA (via a conserved Gly-Asp-Thr motif) and ribosomal
CC       protein uS7 (PubMed:29100052). Proportionally less EF-P binds to larger
CC       polysomes, suggesting it has a role early in translation. It is present
CC       in 1 copy per 10 ribosomes (PubMed:7011506).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20729861, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29100052,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7011506}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm.
CC   -!- PTM: Is beta-lysylated on the epsilon-amino group of Lys-34 by the
CC       combined action of EpmA and EpmB, and then hydroxylated on the C5
CC       position of the same residue by EpmC (PubMed:20729861, PubMed:21841797,
CC       PubMed:22128152, PubMed:22706199). Lysylation is critical for the
CC       stimulatory effect of EF-P on peptide-bond formation. The lysylation
CC       moiety extends toward the peptidyltransferase center and stabilizes the
CC       terminal 3-CCA end of the P-site tRNA (PubMed:29100052). The
CC       modification is also able to form hydrogen bonds with A2439 of the 23S
CC       rRNA (PubMed:29100052). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21841797,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22128152, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22706199,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29100052}.

CC       (3,6-diaminohexanoyl)-5-hydroxy-Lys-34.;
CC       Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:22706199};
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Disruption of this gene leads to lethality
CC       (PubMed:9405429) or to a very slow growth phenotype (PubMed:20729861).
CC       Triple efp-efpL-uup deletions cannot be made (PubMed:39622818).
CC       Required for the expression of poly-Pro-containing proteins.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20729861, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23239623,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39622818, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9405429}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: The EF-P:EfpL ratio is about 13:1.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39622818}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the elongation factor P family. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- CAUTION: The modification on Lys-34 was initially suggested to be a
CC       spermidine residue. {ECO:0000305|PubMed:18201202}.
