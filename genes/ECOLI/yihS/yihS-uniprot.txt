CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the isomerization of sulfoquinovose (SQ) to 6-
CC       deoxy-6-sulfo-D-fructose (SF) (PubMed:24463506, PubMed:33791429). Can
CC       also catalyze the interconversion of SQ and sulforhamnose (SR)
CC       (PubMed:33791429). Has a clear preference for beta-SQ and little-to-no
CC       activity on alpha-SQ (PubMed:33791429). In vitro, can also catalyze the
CC       interconversion of mannose, fructose and glucose, or lyxose and
CC       xylulose, but has extremely low activity with glucose
CC       (PubMed:18328504). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18328504,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24463506, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33791429}.
