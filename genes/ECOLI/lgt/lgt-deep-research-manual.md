# Manual deep research: E. coli lgt

Falcon deep research was unavailable in this Orca environment because `agentapi`
was not on `PATH` and provider API keys were not configured. This manual
research note summarizes the cached evidence used for the E. coli `lgt` review.

`lgt` encodes the inner-membrane enzyme phosphatidylglycerol--prolipoprotein
diacylglyceryl transferase. Sankaran and Wu showed that bacterial
prolipoprotein lipid modification transfers the diacylglyceryl moiety from
phosphatidylglycerol directly to the sulfhydryl group of the lipobox cysteine,
forming sn-glycerol 1-phosphate and establishing the named Lgt reaction
[PMID:8051048]. Gan et al. then assigned E. coli `umpA`/`lgt` to this essential
diacylglyceryl transferase function [PMID:7896715].

Later Lgt-specific studies refine both activity and localization. Pailler et al.
showed by fractionation and topology analysis that E. coli Lgt is an
inner-membrane protein embedded by seven transmembrane segments
[PMID:22287519]. Mao et al. solved crystal structures of E. coli Lgt with
phosphatidylglycerol and an inhibitor, confirmed recombinant EcLgt activity in
vivo by complementation of an `lgt`-conditional depletion strain, and showed
in vitro diacylglyceryl transferase activity with a GFP-based lipobox substrate
[PMID:26729647].

The 2008 cytoplasmic-side annotation was kept as a non-core topology row.
Selvan and Sankaran reported extraction behavior consistent with cytosolic-side
association [PMID:18602442], but the later seven-transmembrane topology and
structural work establish Lgt as an integral inner-membrane enzyme with
catalytic features in the periplasmic half of the membrane [PMID:22287519;
PMID:26729647].
