Gene Description

AhpF is the soluble flavoprotein reductase subunit of the E. coli AhpC/AhpF alkyl hydroperoxide reductase system. It accepts electrons from NADH through a C-terminal FAD/NADH-binding module and relays them via redox-active disulfides to its N-terminal AhpC-facing domain, regenerating oxidized AhpC for continuous hydrogen peroxide and organic hydroperoxide detoxification in the cytosol.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

NADH-dependent peroxiredoxin reductase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the NADH-dependent reduction of an oxidized peroxiredoxin, regenerating its peroxide-reducing cysteine residues.

Justification: AhpF is not the EC 1.11.1.26 peroxiredoxin that reduces hydroperoxide; it is the dedicated flavoprotein-disulfide reductase that accepts reducing equivalents from NADH and transfers them to the oxidized AhpC peroxiredoxin. Existing GO:0047134 captures the broader EC 1.8.1.8 chemistry but not the dedicated peroxiredoxin acceptor.

Core Functions

AhpF reduces oxidized AhpC using NADH-derived electrons. Its C-terminal domain binds FAD and NADH and passes reducing equivalents through redox-active disulfides to the N-terminal domain, which contacts AhpC in the alkyl hydroperoxide reductase complex and regenerates the AhpC peroxiredoxin for hydrogen peroxide and organic hydroperoxide detoxification.
