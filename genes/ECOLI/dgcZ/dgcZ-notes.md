# dgcZ: c-di-GMP module member review

## 2026-11-05

DgcZ is a CZB-GGDEF protein, not a single-domain cyclase [PMID:23769666]. Zinc ligation and dimerization are direct structural findings. Its strongest established output is PGA production [PMID:19460094, “production of c-di-GMP by the dedicated diguanylate cyclase YdeH”].

Localization is conditional: “evenly distributed in the cytoplasm in transition phase” versus polar foci in stationary phase [PMID:27402625]. FrdB association does not prove that DgcZ is a resident plasma-membrane protein. The membrane IBA is therefore over-annotated; `paint-losses` found no IRD/IKR on the available lineage. Family descent supports catalysis, not automatic conservation of accessory localization architecture.

The two generic protein-binding rows are removed as uninformative while their interaction findings are retained [PMID:27402625; PMID:29018125]. Adhesion terms are refined to regulation of biofilm formation. Motility/flagellar-abundance outputs remain non-core because the cited experiments emphasize overexpression [PMID:18713317].
