Gene Description

KdpD is the inner-membrane sensor of the E. coli KdpD/KdpE two-component system that controls the high-affinity K+ uptake pump KdpFABC. The 894-residue homodimer has a large cytoplasmic N-terminal input region (containing a Walker A/B atypical kinase domain and a Usp domain), four transmembrane helices, and a cytoplasmic C-terminal transmitter (GAF, DHp and HATPase_c domains). Under K+ limitation or salt stress KdpD autophosphorylates His673 in trans and transfers the phosphoryl group to KdpE, which then activates kdpFABC transcription; it also dephosphorylates KdpE~P, and the kinase/phosphatase balance responds to both extracellular and intracellular K+. Its activity is modulated by UspC (scaffold under salt stress) and dephosphorylated IIA(Ntr). At high K+ the N-terminal atypical serine kinase domain phosphorylates KdpB Ser162, inhibiting the KdpFABC pump post-translationally.

  that controls the high-affinity K+ uptake pump KdpFABC. The 894-residue homodimer has a
  large cytoplasmic N-terminal input region (containing a Walker A/B atypical kinase domain
  and a Usp domain), four transmembrane helices, and a cytoplasmic C-terminal transmitter
  (GAF, DHp and HATPase_c domains). Under K+ limitation or salt stress KdpD autophosphorylates
  His673 in trans and transfers the phosphoryl group to KdpE, which then activates kdpFABC
  transcription; it also dephosphorylates KdpE~P, and the kinase/phosphatase balance responds
  to both extracellular and intracellular K+. Its activity is modulated by UspC (scaffold
  under salt stress) and dephosphorylated IIA(Ntr). At high K+ the N-terminal atypical serine
  kinase domain phosphorylates KdpB Ser162, inhibiting the KdpFABC pump post-translationally.
