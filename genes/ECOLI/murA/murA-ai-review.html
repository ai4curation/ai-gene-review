Gene Description

MurA (formerly MurZ) is the cytosolic UDP-N-acetylglucosamine 1-carboxyvinyltransferase (UDP-GlcNAc enolpyruvyl transferase, EC 2.5.1.7) of Escherichia coli, a member of the EPSP synthase (enolpyruvyl transferase) family. It transfers the enolpyruvyl group of phosphoenolpyruvate to the 3'-hydroxyl of UDP-N-acetylglucosamine, releasing phosphate and forming UDP-GlcNAc-enolpyruvate, the first committed step of peptidoglycan precursor (UDP-MurNAc) synthesis; MurB then reduces this product to UDP-MurNAc. The reaction proceeds by addition-elimination through a tetrahedral intermediate, and active-site Cys115 forms a covalent phospholactoyl adduct with PEP. Cys115 is also the site of covalent inactivation by the antibiotic fosfomycin, which requires bound UDP-GlcNAc, and by the fungal product terreic acid. MurA is the sole UDP-GlcNAc enolpyruvyl transferase of E. coli and is essential for growth. It is competitively inhibited by UDP-GlcNAc 3'-phosphate made by PezT-type toxins. MurA also physically associates with the LPS assembly scaffold LapB and early lipid A and phospholipid enzymes, which may coordinate use of the shared UDP-GlcNAc pool.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does binding of MurA to LapB, LpxA, LpxC, LpxD or FabZ alter MurA catalytic activity or fosfomycin sensitivity, i.e. is the interaction a regulatory switch for partitioning UDP-GlcNAc between peptidoglycan and lipid A synthesis?

Q: Does E. coli MurA, like Enterobacter cloacae MurA, exist in cells predominantly as a dormant Cys115-PEP/UDP-MurNAc complex, and is this the physiological feedback mechanism controlling flux into peptidoglycan synthesis?

Q: Which gene carried the 90-min fosfomycin-resistant temperature-sensitive allele of Venkateswaran and Wu (1972), given that murA maps at 69.3 min?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Measure MurA steady-state kinetics and fosfomycin inactivation rates in the presence and absence of purified LapB, LpxA, LpxC, LpxD and FabZ, and test interaction-deficient MurA variants for growth and peptidoglycan/LPS balance.

Experiment: Quantify UDP-MurNAc-bound and PEP-adducted MurA in E. coli lysates by native mass spectrometry across growth phases to test the dormant-complex feedback model in vivo.

murA (P0A749) – E. coli K-12 – curation notes

No deep-research file was generated for this gene; these notes are from the cached

Identity and nomenclature

  • murA (b3189; formerly murZ) encodes UDP-N-acetylglucosamine 1-carboxyvinyltransferase
    (UDP-GlcNAc enolpyruvyl transferase, EC 2.5.1.7), 419 aa, EPSP synthase family, MurA
    subfamily (HAMAP MF_00111; TIGR01072; PANTHER PTHR43783:SF1).
  • Cloned by screening for clones conferring fosfomycin resistance; purified protein has
    the expected activity [PMID:1512209 "Identified by screening an E. coli genomic library for clones that conferred phosphomycin resistance"; "MurZ protein was purified to near homogeneity and found to have the expected UDP-N-acetylglucosamine enolpyruvyl transferase activity"].
  • Essential, and the sole UDP-GlcNAc enolpyruvyl transferase of E. coli (unlike
    Gram-positives with MurAA/MurAB) PMID:7608103.
  • Historical note: the 1972 fosfomycin-resistant, temperature-sensitive "murA" mutant of
    Venkateswaran & Wu PMID:4555418 mapped at 90 min, not at the 69.3 min murZ locus; Brown et
    al. 1995 explicitly flag this map discrepancy PMID:7608103. The 1972 biochemistry (lower enzyme affinity for fosfomycin, reduced DAP incorporation) is consistent with an enolpyruvyl-transferase lesion, but the genetic identity of that allele is uncertain. Relevant to the IMP row from PMID:4555418.

Reaction and mechanism

  • PEP + UDP-GlcNAc -> UDP-GlcNAc-enolpyruvate + Pi; first committed step of
    peptidoglycan synthesis PMID:8075064.
  • Addition–elimination through a tetrahedral ketal intermediate; stereochemistry solved
    with C115A mutant PMID:9485407.
  • Covalent phospholactoyl (PEP–Cys115) adduct is kinetically competent
    PMID:8075064.
  • In Enterobacter cloacae MurA, PEP-Cys115 adduct plus bound UDP-MurNAc (MurB product)
    forms a "dormant" complex proposed to mediate feedback regulation PMID:22378791. E. cloacae, not E. coli; treated as family-level inference.

Fosfomycin / Cys115 / UNAG

  • Crystal structure of E. coli MurA with UNAG and fosfomycin: fosfomycin covalently on
    Cys115; UNAG bound between the two domains PMID:8994972.
  • Terreic acid also alkylates Cys115; inactivation (like fosfomycin) requires UNAG; E. coli
    K-12 MurA was assayed [PMID:20392080 "terreic acid is a covalent inhibitor of the bacterial cell wall biosynthetic enzyme MurA from Enterobacter cloacae and Escherichia coli in vitro"; "Kinetic characterization established that the inactivation requires the presence of substrate UNAG"].
  • UNAG-3-phosphate made by the PezT toxin competitively inhibits E. coli MurA
    (Ki 7 uM per UniProt) PMID:21445328.

Location

  • Soluble cytosolic enzyme; detected in cytosolic fractions by proteomics
    [PMID:15911532 fractionation to "cytosol, periplasm, inner membrane, and outer membrane"]; [PMID:18304323 cytosol profiling].
    UniProt: "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm".

Interactions (not modelled as GO terms)

  • MurA binds LapB and the LPS/PL enzymes LpxA, LpxC, LpxD and FabZ (B2H + pull-downs);
    murA is a multicopy suppressor of lapB mutants; MurA is not a LapB/FtsH substrate
    PMID:42604454. Physical association only; no GO MF term is warranted (bare protein binding policy). Kept as a suggested question.

  • GO:0008760 (all rows): ACCEPT – core MF.
  • GO:0009252: ACCEPT (IBA, IEA, IMP) – core BP.
  • GO:0005829 cytosol: ACCEPT; GO:0005737 cytoplasm: MODIFY -> cytosol.
  • GO:0003824, GO:0016765: TOO_GENERAL (entailed by GO:0008760).
  • GO:0019277 UDP-GalNAc biosynthesis: REMOVE – InterPro2GO mis-mapping; MurA consumes UDP-GlcNAc and makes no UDP-GalNAc.
  • UniProt keywords "Cell division", "Cell shape" have not been propagated to GOA rows for murA; no NEW rows proposed for them (over-annotation for a precursor enzyme).
