Gene Description

Antigen 43 is a surface-displayed protein of Escherichia coli and the classical example of the type V secretion architecture: a single precursor whose N-terminal passenger is presented on the cell surface and whose C-terminal beta chain forms the outer-membrane barrel through which the passenger reaches it. The two chains are produced in equal stoichiometry and remain associated with the cell after cleavage, with the surface component releasable by mild heat treatment. Insertion of its barrel into the outer membrane requires the translocation and assembly module, whose outer-membrane subunit TamA is sufficient to initiate penetration of the membrane and whose inner-membrane subunit TamB regulates that step. Once displayed, the passenger self-associates between neighbouring cells, so expression drives autoaggregation and settling strongly enough to override flagellar motility, and the gene is subject to phase variation that gives rise to colony form variation in a clonal population.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

self-associating adhesin activity

Definition: Enables adhesion between two cells through a homotypic interaction between copies of the same surface-displayed protein presented on each of the two cells.

Justification: No existing molecular function expresses what a self-associating adhesin does. The activity is a homotypic interaction between copies of the same protein on different cells, and the terms available describe binding a named partner, which names the interactor without naming the function. Several aggregation-mediating surface proteins share this property, so the gap is not specific to Antigen 43. Pending such a term, this review asserts the aggregation process and the surface location and no molecular function at all, rather than reaching for a binding term.

Parent term: cell-cell adhesion mediator activity

  • PMID:16804184 : Ag43 overexpression produces a dominant aggregation phenotype that overrides motility in the presence of low levels of flagella.

Core Functions

Self-associating surface adhesin. The passenger chain displayed on the cell surface binds the corresponding chain on a neighbouring cell, clustering initially separate cells into aggregates that settle out of suspension. The effect is strong enough to override flagellar motility when flagellation is low, and because the gene is phase-variable, a clonal population splits into aggregating and non-aggregating subpopulations.

Substrate of the type V secretion route, carrying its own outer-membrane translocator domain. The C-terminal beta chain forms the barrel through which the N-terminal passenger crosses the outer membrane, after which the two chains are cleaved apart but remain associated. Insertion of the barrel requires the translocation and assembly module: the protein does not enter a membrane lacking it, and the outer-membrane subunit TamA is sufficient to initiate entry while the inner-membrane subunit TamB regulates the step.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should GO:0015474 autotransporter activity be revised, or deprecated in favour of describing these proteins as substrates? Its definition asserts that the passenger-bearing protein does the transporting, which the translocation and assembly module literature disputes.

Q: Does Antigen 43 use BAM as well as TAM? TamA and BamA are both Omp85-family insertases, and the type V secretion module currently models installation as BAM-dependent. Whether the two routes are redundant, sequential or substrate-specific for this protein is not established here.

Q: Is a molecular function term needed for homotypic self-association? Several aggregation-mediating surface proteins share the property that their interaction partner is another copy of themselves on a different cell, and no current term expresses it.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Test Antigen 43 barrel insertion in a bamA conditional depletion and in a tamA deletion side by side, in the same strain background, scoring surface display and autoaggregation. This would establish whether the two insertases are redundant for this substrate or whether TAM dependence is absolute, which is the question the type V secretion module currently cannot answer.

Experiment: Reconstitute passenger translocation, as distinct from barrel insertion, in the supported-bilayer system already used for Ag43 and TamA. Observing whether the passenger crosses with no component other than the barrel present would test the autonomous-transporter model directly and settle whether autotransporter activity is the right molecular function.

flu / Antigen 43 (Escherichia coli K-12) — curation notes

UniProt: P39180 (AG43_ECOLI), gene flu. The protein is made as a precursor
that is cleaved into two chains held in equal stoichiometry, an alpha
(passenger) chain displayed at the surface and a beta (translocator) chain in
the outer membrane. The original characterisation established exactly that
bipartite arrangement
PMID:3301815
and placed both chains in the outer membrane by fractionation
PMID:3301815.

UniProt's functional statement is short: "Controls colony form variation and
autoaggregation. May function as an adhesin."

What GOA has, and what it lacks

All six GOA rows are cellular_component. There is no molecular function and no
biological process annotation at all, for the protein that is the textbook
classical autotransporter of E. coli. The locations themselves are sound,
and two are experimental, but the gene's actual biology is entirely unrecorded.

The periplasmic-space row deserves a note: UniProt assigns the periplasm to the
uncleaved precursor only, and by similarity (ECO:0000250), so it describes a
transit intermediate rather than a steady-state location. Worth keeping, but
not as a core location.

Autoaggregation

Ag43 self-associates between cells, and the phenotype is strong enough to
override flagellar motility
PMID:16804184,
with the relationship dose-dependent in both directions
PMID:16804184.
GO:0098743 cell aggregation fits this precisely: "The clustering together and
adhesion of initially separate cells to form an aggregate." The work used
naturally occurring phase variants expressing and not expressing Ag43, so the
evidence is mutant-phenotype rather than overexpression artefact alone.

The important finding: Ag43 is inserted by TAM, not BAM

This is the result that bears on modules/bacterial_type_v_secretion.yaml,
which currently models barrel installation as BAM-dependent and grounds that
part on BamA alone.

Ag43's barrel insertion depends on the translocation and assembly module, TamA
in the outer membrane with TamB in the inner membrane
PMID:22466966,
a module discovered precisely because it promotes autotransporter secretion
PMID:22466966.
For Ag43 the dependence is stated directly
PMID:25341963,
and a reconstituted system shows the requirement is specific: Ag43 does not
associate with a membrane lacking the TAM
PMID:25341963,
while TamA alone suffices to start the insertion
PMID:25341963.

TamA is itself an Omp85-family protein, as BamA is, so this is a second,
parallel insertase rather than a contradiction of the BAM model. The module
should offer both routes.

Why GO:0015474 autotransporter activity is NOT proposed here

Ag43 is the obvious candidate for GO:0015474, whose definition reads
"Transports a passenger protein from the periplasm to the external milieu; the
passenger protein and the porin are the N- and C-terminal regions of the same
protein, respectively." Ag43 has exactly that architecture.

The reason for withholding it is that the definition asserts the protein does
the transporting, and the TAM literature contests precisely that
PMID:22466966.
The same papers that establish the architecture call Ag43 a substrate protein
throughout. Asserting autotransporter activity for Ag43 would commit the review
to the autonomous model on a point its own best references dispute.

So: assert the type V secretion process, which is uncontested, and record the
molecular function as an open ontology question rather than resolving it by
picking the older model. The term is left in the module, where it describes the
architecture class, with the dispute recorded as a knowledge gap.
