CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the sulfite reductase complex that catalyzes the
CC       6-electron reduction of sulfite to sulfide. This is one of several
CC       activities required for the biosynthesis of L-cysteine from sulfate.
CC       The flavoprotein component catalyzes the electron flow from NADPH ->
CC       FAD -> FMN to the hemoprotein component. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_01541}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: In addition to its role in the sulfite reductase complex, it
CC       functions as a specific partner of the YcbX molybdoenzyme in the
CC       detoxification of N-hydroxylated base analogs (PubMed:20118259). CysJ
CC       mediates the N-reductive reaction through its NADPH:flavin
CC       oxidoreductase activity (PubMed:20118259). It may provide reducing
CC       equivalents to its partner YcbX, which ultimately performs the
CC       reduction of 6-N-hydroxylaminopurine (HAP) to non-toxic adenine
CC       (PubMed:20118259). The role of CysJ in base analog detoxification is
CC       independent of CysI and sulfite reductase (PubMed:20118259).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20118259}.
