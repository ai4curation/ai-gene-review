Gene Description

PaaE is the reductase component of the multicomponent phenylacetyl-CoA 1,2-epoxidase (PaaABCE) of Escherichia coli, the ring-activating enzyme of the aerobic phenylacetate catabolic pathway encoded by the paa operon. It is a class IA reductase with an N-terminal FAD-binding/NAD(P)-binding ferredoxin-reductase module and a C-terminal plant-type [2Fe-2S] ferredoxin domain, an arrangement unlike the class IB or class III reductases of other bacterial multicomponent monooxygenases. PaaE oxidizes NAD(P)H, preferring NADPH and FAD, and is thought to pass electrons via its flavin and [2Fe-2S] cluster to the di-iron centre of the catalytic PaaA subunit; it can also reduce cytochrome c in vitro. PaaE is required for epoxidase activity in reconstitution assays and for efficient ring hydroxylation and growth on phenylacetate in vivo.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express PaaE untagged (or with a cleavable tag) together with PaaD, then measure stopped-flow electron transfer from NADPH-reduced PaaE to PaaABC and test PaaE-PaaABC association by crosslinking or native MS.
