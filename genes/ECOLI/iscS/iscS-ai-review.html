Gene Description

IscS is the housekeeping pyridoxal 5'-phosphate (PLP)-dependent cysteine desulfurase of Escherichia coli, a cytosolic homodimer encoded in the iscRSUA-hscBA-fdx operon. It converts L-cysteine to L-alanine and carries the released sulfane sulfur as a persulfide on its active-site Cys328, located on a flexible surface loop. From this persulfide IscS delivers sulfur to a range of acceptor proteins that bind one at a time around Cys328: the scaffold IscU for assembly of [2Fe-2S] and [4Fe-4S] clusters (with frataxin/CyaY, IscX and ferredoxin acting as modulators of the IscS-IscU complex); ThiI for 4-thiouridine formation in tRNA and for thiazole/thiamine biosynthesis; TusA, the entry point of the Tus relay that leads to MnmA-catalysed 2-thiolation of the wobble uridine; and FdhD for maturation of molybdenum formate dehydrogenases. IscS also degrades L-selenocysteine in vitro and has been implicated in selenium delivery for selenophosphate synthesis. Loss of IscS abolishes tRNA s4U and causes thiamine and nicotinic acid auxotrophy and slow growth.

Biochemical activity

  • IscS is a PLP-dependent cysteine desulfurase: it converts L-cysteine to L-alanine
    and a protein-bound persulfide. PMID:8663056
  • Persulfide on Cys328. PMID:11972033
  • C328A abolishes cysteine desulfurization but much less so selenocysteine
    decomposition. PMID:10739946
  • In vitro IscS also degrades L-selenocysteine to alanine. PMID:10739946
    UniProt records a physiological selenium-delivery role for selenophosphate synthesis.
  • Crystal structure, PLP on Lys206; Cys328 on a mobile loop. PMID:12860127
  • Homodimer; each subunit binds one IscU (UniProt SUBUNIT, PMID:11577100, PMID:12860127, PMID:22203963).

Sulfur-donor hub

  • Master sulfur donor. PMID:20404999
  • Acceptors bind one at a time near Cys328. PMID:20404999
  • Fe-S assembly: sulfur transfer to IscU via a Cys328–Cys63 disulfide-linked complex
    PMID:11972033;
    IscS can donate sulfur to build the [4Fe-4S] cluster of dihydroxy-acid dehydratase
    PMID:8663056; isc operon coexpression boosts holo-ferredoxin yield PMID:10393315.
  • CyaY (frataxin) binds IscS and gates cluster formation PMID:19305405.
  • tRNA s4U8: IscS -> ThiI -> tRNA PMID:10753862; deletion abolishes s4U PMID:10781607.
  • tRNA mnm5s2U34: IscS -> TusA -> TusBCD -> TusE -> MnmA PMID:16387657.
  • Thiamine (thiazole) and NAD: PMID:10781607; IscS mobilizes sulfur to ThiS thiocarboxylate.
  • Molybdenum enzymes: IscS -> FdhD -> formate dehydrogenase PMID:22194618.
