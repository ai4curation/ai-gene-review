## Biochemical activity

- IscS is a PLP-dependent cysteine desulfurase: it converts L-cysteine to L-alanine
  and a protein-bound persulfide. [PMID:8663056 "This E. coli protein contains pyridoxal phosphate as a cofactor and catalyzes the removal of sulfur from cysteine to form alanine and S0."]
- Persulfide on Cys328. [PMID:11972033 "Cys-328 of IscS attacks the sulfur atom of L-cysteine, and the sulfane sulfur derived from L-cysteine binds to the Sgamma atom of Cys-328."]
- C328A abolishes cysteine desulfurization but much less so selenocysteine
  decomposition. [PMID:10739946 "The enzyme activity toward L-cysteine was almost completely abolished by the mutations, whereas the activity toward L-selenocysteine was much less affected."]
- In vitro IscS also degrades L-selenocysteine to alanine. [PMID:10739946 "All three homologs catalyze the elimination of Se and S from L-selenocysteine and L-cysteine, respectively, to form L-alanine."]
  UniProt records a physiological selenium-delivery role for selenophosphate synthesis.
- Crystal structure, PLP on Lys206; Cys328 on a mobile loop. [PMID:12860127 "Cys328 is positioned greater than 17A from the pyridoxal phosphate cofactor"]
- Homodimer; each subunit binds one IscU (UniProt SUBUNIT, PMID:11577100, PMID:12860127, PMID:22203963).

## Sulfur-donor hub

- Master sulfur donor. [PMID:20404999 "The cysteine desulfurase IscS is a highly conserved master enzyme initiating sulfur transfer via persulfide to a range of acceptor proteins involved in Fe-S cluster assembly, tRNA modifications, and sulfur-containing cofactor biosynthesis."]
- Acceptors bind one at a time near Cys328. [PMID:20404999 "The sulfur acceptors can only bind to IscS one at a time, while frataxin and IscX can form a ternary complex with IscU and IscS."]
- Fe-S assembly: sulfur transfer to IscU via a Cys328–Cys63 disulfide-linked complex
  [PMID:11972033 "We here report that a covalent bond is formed between Cys-328 of IscS and Cys-63 of IscU."];
  IscS can donate sulfur to build the [4Fe-4S] cluster of dihydroxy-acid dehydratase
  [PMID:8663056]; isc operon coexpression boosts holo-ferredoxin yield [PMID:10393315].
- CyaY (frataxin) binds IscS and gates cluster formation [PMID:19305405 "CyaY participates in iron-sulfur (Fe-S) cluster assembly as an iron-dependent inhibitor of cluster formation, through binding to the desulfurase IscS."].
- tRNA s4U8: IscS -> ThiI -> tRNA [PMID:10753862 "the sulfane sulfur generated by IscS is transferred sequentially to ThiI and then to tRNA during the in vitro synthesis of s(4)U."]; deletion abolishes s4U [PMID:10781607 "In frame deletion of the iscS gene in Escherichia coli results in a mutant strain that lacks s(4)U in its tRNA."].
- tRNA mnm5s2U34: IscS -> TusA -> TusBCD -> TusE -> MnmA [PMID:16387657 "IscS transfers the persulfide sulfur to TusA."].
- Thiamine (thiazole) and NAD: [PMID:10781607 "the iscS(-) strain requires thiamin and nicotinic acid for growth in minimal media"]; IscS mobilizes sulfur to ThiS thiocarboxylate.
- Molybdenum enzymes: IscS -> FdhD -> formate dehydrogenase [PMID:22194618 "The interaction of IscS with FdhD results in a sulfur transfer between IscS and FdhD in the form of persulfides."].
- RhdA (A. vinelandii rhodanese, heterologous) is persulfurated by IscS [PMID:16310786].
