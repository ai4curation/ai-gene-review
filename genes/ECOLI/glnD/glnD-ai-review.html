Gene Description

GlnD is the bifunctional uridylyltransferase/uridylyl-removing enzyme (UTase/UR) of Escherichia coli K-12, a soluble single-chain protein of about 95 kDa that acts as the primary nitrogen sensor of the cell. Its N-terminal nucleotidyltransferase domain transfers UMP from UTP to a tyrosine residue of the PII signal transduction proteins GlnB and GlnK, and its central HD domain hydrolyses PII-UMP back to PII and UMP. Glutamine inhibits uridylylation and stimulates deuridylylation, probably via the C-terminal ACT domains, so that PII is uridylylated under nitrogen limitation and deuridylylated under nitrogen excess. The uridylylation state of PII in turn controls the adenylylation of glutamine synthetase by GlnE and the NtrB/NtrC two-component system, making GlnD central to the regulation of nitrogen assimilation and nitrogen-regulated gene expression.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

[protein-PII]-uridylyl-L-tyrosine hydrolase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: [protein-PII]-uridylyl-L-tyrosine + H2O = [protein-PII]-L-tyrosine + UMP + H+ (RHEA:48600).

Justification: The uridylyl-removing activity of GlnD is experimentally established and biochemically distinct from its uridylyltransferase activity (separate HD domain, separable by mutagenesis), but GO only offers the generic GO:0008081 phosphoric diester hydrolase activity. A specific child of GO:0008081 would let both halves of the bifunctional enzyme be annotated at equal precision.

Core Functions

Glutamine-inhibited transfer of UMP from UTP to Tyr51 of the PII signal transduction proteins GlnB and GlnK under nitrogen limitation, catalysed by the N-terminal nucleotidyltransferase domain, which drives NtrC-dependent nitrogen-regulated gene expression and glutamine synthetase deadenylylation.

glnD (P27249) notes

Provider deep research (just deep-research) was NOT run for this gene; these notes

Function

  • GlnD is a single bifunctional enzyme catalysing both uridylylation and deuridylylation
    of PII PMID:6130097
  • Native enzyme is a monomer of ~95 kDa PMID:6130097.
  • Kinetics: PMID:9737855
  • Glutamine is the main signal: PMID:9737855
    and PMID:9737855.
  • Domain architecture: NT domain = UTase, HD domain = UR, two ACT domains = glutamine sensing
    (UniProt CC DOMAIN, ECO:0000269 PubMed:20363937); HD->AA/QN at 514-515 abolishes UR only,
    G93/G94/D107 mutations abolish UTase only (UniProt FT MUTAGEN).
  • GlnK is also a substrate PMID:8843440.

Physiology

  • glnD::Tn10 mutants lack UTase and are glutamine auxotroph-like PMID:26660.
  • Regulation of glnALG via PII and NtrB PMID:2865248.

Curation decisions

  • GO:0005515 rows (4): REMOVE per protein-binding policy; the PII interactions are enzyme-substrate.
  • GO:0016779 -> MODIFY to GO:0008773.
  • GO:0008081 phosphoric diester hydrolase: ACCEPT as the UR activity. No specific GO term for the
    PII uridylyl-removing reaction (RHEA:48600) was found in the local GO db (oaklib go.db) or QuickGO search;
    proposed as a new term.
  • No NEW process terms added (protein uridylylation GO:0018177 was considered but not added; the
    MF term already describes the reaction).
