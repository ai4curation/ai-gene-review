# tusD (E. coli K-12) curation notes

## Sources checked

- UniProtKB record (genes/ECOLI/tusD/tusD-uniprot.txt), GOA table, and the cached
  abstracts of PMID:16387657 and PMID:16472754 (abstract-only in the cache).
- No provider deep-research file was generated for this gene.

## Findings

- TusD is the sulfur-bearing TusBCD subunit [PMID:16472754 "In vivo mutant
  analyses revealed that only Cys78, in the TusD subunit, participates in sulfur
  transfer during the s2U modification process."].
- TusD receives sulfur from TusA, stimulated by TusE [PMID:16387657 "TusE binds
  TusBCD complex and stimulates sulfur transfer from TusA to TusD."].
- GO:0016783 sulfurtransferase activity (IEA) is MODIFIED laterally to
  GO:0097163 sulfur carrier activity, which the gene already has by IDA and IBA.
