# ECOLI tusD: source-bounded annotation review

TusD is the sulfur-bearing subunit of the cytosolic TusBCD heterohexamer, which mediates sulfur transfer during C2 thiolation of tRNA wobble uridine. Sulfur from TusA enters the complex through the functionally implicated TusD Cys78 and proceeds through the TusE-dependent terminal reaction with MnmA and tRNA.
