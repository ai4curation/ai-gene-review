CC   -!- FUNCTION: Nucleotidyltransferase involved in the post-translational
CC       modification of proteins (PubMed:30270044, PubMed:32966796,
CC       PubMed:35532215, PubMed:35532216). It can catalyze the addition of
CC       adenosine monophosphate (AMP) or uridine monophosphate (UMP) to a
CC       protein, resulting in modifications known as AMPylation and UMPylation
CC       (PubMed:30270044, PubMed:32966796, PubMed:35532215, PubMed:35532216).
CC       It could be involved in stress resistance and play a crucial role in
CC       regulating essential bacterial processes, including flagella synthesis
CC       and iron uptake (PubMed:32966796, PubMed:35532215, PubMed:35532216).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30270044, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32966796,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35532215, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35532216}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Can catalyze the transfer of adenosine 5'-monophosphate (AMP)
CC       to Ser, Thr and Tyr residues of target proteins (PubMed:30270044). In
CC       vitro, prefers ATP over other nucleotides as a cosubstrate
CC       (PubMed:30270044). Substrates include SelO itself, SucA, which is
CC       AMPylated on 'Thr-405', and GrxA, AMPylated on 'Tyr-13'
CC       (PubMed:30270044, PubMed:32966796). AMPylation of the grx family of
CC       enzymes may regulate protein S-glutathionylation levels in cells
CC       (PubMed:30270044). AMPylation is probably involved in redox homeostasis
CC       (PubMed:30270044). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30270044,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32966796}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Under stress conditions, may act primarily as an
CC       uridylyltransferase that catalyzes the UMPylation of various proteins,
CC       including chaperones and transcription factors such as FlhDC and Fur
CC       (PubMed:32966796, PubMed:35532215, PubMed:35532216). Can also catalyze
CC       auto-UMPylation (PubMed:32966796). It can catalyze the Mn(2+)-dependent
CC       UMPylation on Tyr and His residues of chaperones such as GroEL, DnaK,
CC       HtpG and ClpB, leading to their inactivation (PubMed:32966796).
CC       Catalyzes the UMPylation on Ser-31 of the FlhC subunit of the major
CC       flagellar transcriptional regulator complex FlhDC (PubMed:35532215).
CC       UMPylated FlhDC complex completely loses its DNA binding activity,
CC       which inhibits the expression of secondary and tertiary flagellar
CC       genes, and interrupts flagellar synthesis (PubMed:35532215). Also
CC       catalyzes the UMPylation on His-118 residue of the major iron uptake
CC       regulator Fur, which destroys Fur dimers, (PubMed:35532216). Fur loses
CC       its DNA binding activity and therefore its repressor activity, which
CC       induces the expression of genes involved in iron uptake
CC       (PubMed:35532216). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32966796,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35532215, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35532216}.
