Gene Description

KdpE is the cytoplasmic OmpR/PhoB-family response regulator of the E. coli KdpD/KdpE two-component system. It has an N-terminal receiver domain phosphorylated on Asp52 by the sensor kinase KdpD (and by PhoR in the absence of KdpD) and a C-terminal winged helix-turn-helix DNA-binding domain. Phosphorylated KdpE dimerizes via the alpha4-beta5-alpha5 face of its receiver domain and binds two AT-rich 6-bp half-sites upstream of the kdpFABC promoter, activating transcription of the high-affinity K+ uptake pump KdpFABC under K+ limitation or salt stress. KdpD is the phosphatase that returns KdpE~P to the inactive state.
