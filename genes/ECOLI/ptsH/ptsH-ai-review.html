Gene Description

HPr is a soluble phosphocarrier in the Escherichia coli carbohydrate phosphotransferase system, relaying phosphate from enzyme I to sugar-specific EIIA proteins. Its phosphorylation state also controls interactions with metabolic enzymes, the AI-2 kinase LsrK and the antisigma factor Rsd, linking carbohydrate supply to enzyme activity and transcription.

Escherichia coli ptsH review notes

2026-11-07

P0AA04 is the canonical carbohydrate HPr comparator in PTHR33705:SF1. It is not the same-SF2 counterexample; Bacillus HPr provides that test. A complete functional review preserves its phosphostate-dependent enzyme-regulatory and Rsd-binding activities rather than reducing the protein to a negative nitrogen-relay example. No universal output or phosphorylation-direction claim is transferred to NPr paralogs.

All 23 original machine annotation payloads, qualifiers and supporting entities remain in order. Only their review sections are changed, with no NEW rows. Generic protein-binding removals follow the repository exception and do not deny experimentally observed interactions. Broad terms are refined to the specific supported descendants; precise but substrate-specific context is treated separately from the general catalytic activity.

Primary evidence was inspected in the canonical publication caches. Accessible abstracts constrain detailed assay claims: bibliographic-only PMID:5545082 is not treated as a newly inspected experiment, and complexomic abstracts do not identify every protein in their supplementary results. Existing experimental curators are not overruled on that basis. Native biochemical and structural results independently ground the major functions.

Current official GO definitions were checked for authored activities. The shared Falcon module-research request failed HTTP403 authentication; direct evidence assessment was used and no provider report was fabricated. Full corpus and module-level validation remain separate integration gates.
