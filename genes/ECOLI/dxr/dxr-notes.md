# dxr (P45568) notes

## Sources examined

No deep-research file exists for this gene; none was generated. The review rests on the cached
publications for every GOA reference, three additional cached structural papers fetched for this review
(PMID:11872159, PMID:12621040, PMID:15567415), the UniProt record, and PMC full text for PMID:9707569
(PMC21430) and PMID:11274098 (PMC95155).

## Activity and mechanism

- yaeM complements 2-C-methylerythritol auxotrophs and encodes the NADPH-dependent DXP to MEP enzyme
  [PMID:9707569 "found to catalyze the formation of 2-C-methyl-D-erythritol 4-phosphate from 1-deoxy-D-xylulose 5-phosphate in the presence of NADPH"].
- Class B (pro-S) hydride transfer [PMID:10631325 "identifies the Dxr protein of E. coli as a class B dehydrogenase"].
- Catalytic residues [PMID:10787409 "Glu(231) of E. coli DXP reductoisomerase plays an important role(s) in the conversion of DXP"].

## Metal (full text of PMID:9707569, PMC21430)

"The activity was maximal with Mn 2+ , whereas Co 2+ or Mg 2+ was less effective than Mn 2+ ." (Table 1:
MnCl2 11.8, CoCl2 6.0, MgCl2 4.0 units/mg). A Mn2+ complex structure exists [PMID:12621040]. The metal used
in vivo is not established, which is recorded as a suggested question.

## Oligomeric state (full text of PMID:9707569)

"The relative molecular mass of the enzyme under native condition was determined to be 150 kDa by Toyopearl
HW65 gel filtration. Thus the enzyme appears to form a homotetramer." Every later crystal structure shows a
homodimer [PMID:11741911]. The identical protein binding row is kept as non-core without refining it to
homodimerization, because this paper reports a different oligomer.

## dxr deletion (full text of PMID:11274098, PMC95155)

"In a control experiment, the dxr gene was deleted in E. coli by the same technique. The resulting Δ dxr
strain was dependent on mevalonate in the same way as the gcpE deletion mutant (Fig. 5 )." and "Δ dxr
bacteria could be successfully complemented with the respective episomal copy of the intact dxr gene".
This supports the EcoCyc IMP row although the abstract only discusses gcpE.

## Location

Dxr has no cytosol annotation in GOA, and no direct localization experiment on the endogenous protein was
found, so no location is asserted in core_functions and no NEW location row is proposed.
