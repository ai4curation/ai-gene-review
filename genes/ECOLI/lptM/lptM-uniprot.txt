CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the lipopolysaccharide (LPS) transport (Lpt)
CC       pathway that promotes efficient assembly of the outer membrane LPS
CC       translocon (LptDE) by the BAM complex (PubMed:37821449). Facilitates
CC       oxidative maturation of LptD by stabilizing a conformation of the LPS
CC       translocon in which LptD can efficiently acquire native disulfide
CC       bonds, thereby activating the LPS translocon (PubMed:37821449). Might
CC       stabilize an active conformation of the LPS translocon by mimicking its
CC       natural substrate (PubMed:37821449). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37821449}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the outer membrane embedded portion of the LPS
CC       translocon formed by LptD and LptE (LptDE) (PubMed:37821449). LptM
CC       binds to sites in both LptD and LptE, forming a 1:1:1 heterotrimeric
CC       complex (PubMed:37821449). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37821449}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell outer membrane
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:37821449}; Lipid-anchor {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-
CC       ProRule:PRU00303}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Inactivation of the gene impairs LptD oxidative
CC       maturation and causes the accumulation of intermediate oxidation forms
CC       (PubMed:37821449). It prolongs the residence of LptD and LptE at the
CC       BAM complex (PubMed:37821449). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37821449}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the LptM family. {ECO:0000305}.
