CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required to load the replicative helix DnaB onto single-
CC       stranded (ss)DNA, to initiate chromosomal replication
CC       (PubMed:30797687). Also loads the DnaB in the replication restart
CC       primosome (composed of PriA, PriB, PriC, DnaB and DnaT; DnaG primase
CC       associates transiently with this complex) (PubMed:6454139,
CC       PubMed:8663104, PubMed:8663105). DnaC alters the inter-domain and
CC       inter-subunit interactions of DnaB, inducing an open ring conformation
CC       that allows ssDNA to access the interior of the DnaB(6):DnaC(6) ring
CC       (PubMed:30797687). Has ATPase activity only in the presence of DnaB and
CC       ssDNA (PubMed:30797687). ssDNA binds to the central pore in the
CC       DnaB(6):DnaC(6) complex, making contacts with both subunits
CC       (PubMed:30797687). Mutations in this protein can bypass the need for
CC       PriA recognition of collapsed replication fork substrates in order to
CC       load DnaB onto recombinational intermediates in vivo (PubMed:10540288).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10540288, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30797687,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:6454139, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663104,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663105}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: The helix loader is a DnaB(6):DnaC(6) complex with a crack
CC       opening large enough to allow ssDNA into the central cavity
CC       (PubMed:30797687). Can occasionally be detected as a component of the
CC       replication restart primosome, which is composed of PriA, PriB, PriC,
CC       DnaB and DnaT (PubMed:6454139). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30797687,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:6454139}.
