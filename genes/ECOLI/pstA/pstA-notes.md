# pstA notes

## 2026-10-02

`pstA` is one of the two inner-membrane permease subunits of the E. coli
PstSACB phosphate importer. Surin et al. finished the sequence of the
`phoS pstC pstA pstB phoU` phosphate-specific transport region and identified
PstA as an integral membrane product [PMID:3881386].

The direct mutational evidence is Cox et al. 1988: the Arg220Gln substitution in
PstA abolished phosphate transport through the Pst system while leaving alkaline
phosphatase regulation repressed [PMID:2896188]. That fits PstA as a
membrane-channel component rather than a Pho-signaling component.

For core-function modeling, PstA should contribute to the complex-level
phosphate transporter activity of PstSACB. A single PstA chain does not bind
PstS, move phosphate, and hydrolyze ATP independently.
