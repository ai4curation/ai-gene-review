Gene Description

MlaA (formerly VacJ) is an outer-membrane lipoprotein that forms the outer-membrane interface of the Mla (maintenance of lipid asymmetry) intermembrane phospholipid transport system. After cleavage of its lipoprotein signal peptide and N-terminal lipidation, MlaA folds as a monomeric, mostly alpha-helical, doughnut-shaped protein embedded in the inner (periplasmic) leaflet of the outer membrane, where it associates stably with the trimeric porins OmpC and OmpF. A ridge around its central amphipathic, hydrophilic channel reaches toward the outer leaflet, allowing phospholipids that have become mislocalized in the lipopolysaccharide-rich outer leaflet to enter the channel, while inner-leaflet phospholipids are excluded. A gated beta-hairpin loop and C-terminal helices at the periplasmic opening of the channel recruit and dock the soluble carrier MlaC, juxtaposing the MlaA channel with the MlaC lipid-binding cavity so that phospholipid passes from the outer membrane into MlaC without an energy source at the outer membrane; MlaC then delivers it to the inner-membrane ABC transporter MlaFEDB. Loss of MlaA causes phospholipid accumulation in the outer leaflet, increased outer membrane permeability and sensitivity to SDS-EDTA, whereas the dominant gain-of-function allele mlaA* lets phospholipids flow into the outer leaflet, causing lipopolysaccharide increase, vesiculation and stationary-phase death.

Core Functions

Outer-membrane phospholipid extraction and hand-off: in complex with the porins OmpC/OmpF, MlaA receives phospholipids from the outer leaflet of the outer membrane into its hydrophilic channel and transfers them to the docked periplasmic carrier MlaC, the outer-membrane half of the MlaA-MlaC phospholipid transfer step of the Mla pathway.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does MlaA itself bind phospholipid with measurable affinity, or does it act only as a channel through which lipids partition into MlaC? (This decides whether GO:0005543 phospholipid binding should be asserted for MlaA.)

Q: Is there, or should there be, a GO activity term for outer-membrane phospholipid extraction by a membrane-embedded channel that hands lipid to a soluble carrier, distinct from GO:0120014 (which includes carriage through the aqueous phase)?

Q: What role, if any, do OmpC/OmpF play in lipid movement through MlaA beyond positioning and stabilizing it?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Reconstitute MlaA (wild type, MlaA*, and the hairpin-loop 3G3P variant) in asymmetric outer-membrane mimetic liposomes with labelled outer-leaflet phospholipid, and measure direct transfer to apo-MlaC with and without OmpC, to quantify MlaA-dependent extraction and its directionality.

Literature summary

  • Founding Mla paper: mla mutants accumulate PL in the OM outer leaflet PMID:19383799
  • MlaA is an OM lipoprotein that binds OmpC and OmpF; porin binding alone targets unlipidated MlaA to the OM [PMID:26314242 "We show that the OM lipoprotein MlaA interacts specifically with OmpC and OmpF." ; "This interaction is sufficient to localize MlaA lacking its lipid anchor to the OM."]
  • Structure: monomeric alpha-helical doughnut in the inner leaflet with an amphipathic pore; selectively removes outer-leaflet PLs; porins not active in transport PMID:29038444
  • OmpC-MlaA architecture: MlaA entirely within the bilayer, hydrophilic channel, gating hairpin loop PMID:29848551
  • MlaA-MlaC transient complex (cryo-EM 2.9 Å, PDB 8I8R/8I8X): MlaC docks at the MlaA periplasmic base, juxtaposing channel and MlaC cavity; apo-MlaC binding opens MlaA channel PMID:38092770.
  • mlaA* gain-of-function allele: PL flow into outer leaflet, LPS increase, vesiculation, death PMID:26929379.
  • Ekiert 2017 model: "Binding of MlaC to the lipid-bound MlaA-OmpC/OmpF complex results in lipid transfer to the hydrophobic pocket of MlaC" PMID:28388411.
  • Reconstitution with MlaA donor proteoliposomes, MlaC and MlaFEDB acceptor proteoliposomes PMID:39080293.
  • Direction: ATP drives retrograde (OM to IM) transport PMID:34873038; anterograde proposals PMID:31235958 are disputed.

Decisions

  • Protein binding (3 IPI rows, OmpC/OmpF): REMOVE per protein-binding policy; interactions are real.
  • Cell outer membrane IEA + RCA: ACCEPT. Membrane IEA: MODIFY -> cell outer membrane.
  • GO:0120010 IBA + IDA: ACCEPT.
  • Core MF: contributes_to GO:0120014 phospholipid transfer activity. GO:0120014 is defined as removing
    a PL from a membrane, carrying it through the aqueous phase in a hydrophobic pocket and delivering it.
    MlaA does the removal/hand-off step but not aqueous carriage (that is MlaC), so enables would overstate;
    contributes_to records MlaA as the OM half of the MlaA-MlaC transfer step. GO:0005543 phospholipid
    binding was not asserted: there is no direct binding measurement for MlaA (lipid interaction comes from
    MD and channel structure). No GO term exists for OM phospholipid extraction by a channel; raised as a
    suggested question. No NEW annotation rows added.
