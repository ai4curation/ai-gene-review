CC   -!- FUNCTION: The main replicative DNA helicase, it participates in
CC       initiation and elongation during chromosome replication. Travels ahead
CC       of the DNA replisome, separating dsDNA into templates for DNA
CC       synthesis. A processive ATP-dependent 5'-3' DNA helicase that acts on
CC       preformed replication forks (have single-stranded (ss)DNA tails)
CC       (PubMed:3007474). ATP is the best nucleotide for helicase activity;
CC       GTP, CTP, dCTP and dATP are poor substitutes (PubMed:3007474).
CC       Participates with DNA primase DnaG in primer RNA (pRNA) synthesis
CC       during initiation of DNA replication (PubMed:7532169). Has DNA-
CC       dependent ATPase activity (PubMed:7532169). Is loaded onto ssDNA via
CC       the action of DnaC; ssDNA binds to the central pore in the
CC       DnaB(6):DnaC(6) complex, making contacts with both subunits
CC       (PubMed:30797687). Required for restart of stalled replication forks,
CC       which reloads the DnaB replicative helicase on sites other than the
CC       origin of replication (PubMed:6454139, PubMed:8663104, PubMed:8663105).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:3007474, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30797687,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7532169}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Deletion or mutations in this gene were selected in directed
CC       evolution experiments for resistance to intense ionizing radiation
CC       (3000 Gy) (PubMed:24596148). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24596148}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: 5'-3' DNA helicase activity is stimulated 2-fold
CC       by single-stranded binding protein (SSB) and 6-fold by SSB and DnaG
CC       primase (PubMed:3007474). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:3007474}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homohexamer ring (PubMed:30582519, PubMed:7989299). The helix
CC       loader is a DnaB(6):DnaC(6) complex with a cracked opening large enough
CC       to allow ssDNA into the central cavity (PubMed:30797687). Component of
CC       the replication restart primosome, which is composed of PriA, PriB,
CC       PriC, DnaB and DnaT; DnaC is required to load the helicase on the
CC       replication fork DNA while DnaG primase associates transiently with
CC       this complex (PubMed:6454139, PubMed:8663104, PubMed:8663105).
CC       Interacts with PriC alone and in the DnaB-DnaC complex, probably 1:1
CC       binding with PriC (PubMed:27382050). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27382050,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30582519, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30797687,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:6454139, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7989299,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663104, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8663105}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: (Microbial infection) Forms a DnaB(6):lambda P(5) complex in
CC       the presence of ssDNA (PubMed:30582519). Lambda P helicase loader
CC       reconfigures the DnaB hexameric ring, generating an opening large
CC       enough to permit ssDNA into the central channel (PubMed:30582519).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30582519}.
