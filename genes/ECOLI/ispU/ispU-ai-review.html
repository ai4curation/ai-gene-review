Gene Description

IspU (UppS, formerly Rth/YaeS) is the soluble, homodimeric cis-prenyltransferase of Escherichia coli that makes undecaprenyl diphosphate (C55-PP). It condenses eight isopentenyl diphosphate units onto (2E,6E)-farnesyl diphosphate within a hydrophobic active-site tunnel whose floor residues limit elongation to C55, in a magnesium-dependent reaction. Its product is dephosphorylated to undecaprenyl phosphate, the lipid carrier on which peptidoglycan, lipopolysaccharide O-antigen, enterobacterial common antigen and other envelope glycans are assembled; the gene is essential and temperature-sensitive mutants lose undecaprenyl phosphate, swell and lyse.

Core Functions

Cytosolic homodimeric cis-prenyltransferase that condenses eight isopentenyl diphosphates onto (2E,6E)-farnesyl diphosphate to make di-trans,octa-cis-undecaprenyl diphosphate, the precursor of the undecaprenyl-phosphate carrier lipid used for envelope glycan assembly.
