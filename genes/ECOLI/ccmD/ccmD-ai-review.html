Gene Description

CcmD is a small (69-residue) single-pass inner-membrane protein of the cytochrome c maturation (Ccm) system I, with a short acidic N-terminal segment facing the periplasm, one transmembrane helix and a positively charged cytoplasmic C-terminal domain. CcmD binds CcmC and is a subunit of both the CcmCDE heme-loading intermediate and the larger CcmABCD complex, in which the ABC-type CcmAB subunits use ATP hydrolysis to discharge the heme chaperone CcmE after CcmC has attached heme to it. CcmD does not bind heme and is not required for heme transfer from CcmC to CcmE, but it is essential for release of holo-CcmE from the CcmABCD complex and increases the steady-state yield of holo-CcmE; earlier work also proposed that it stabilises CcmE in the membrane. Loss of CcmD abolishes c-type cytochrome synthesis.

Core Functions

Accessory single-pass membrane subunit of the CcmCDE heme-loading complex and the ABC-type CcmABCD assembly; binds CcmC and is essential for the ATP-dependent release of holo-CcmE, thereby supplying the heme donor for holocytochrome c synthesis. No intrinsic molecular activity is defined.
