Reviewed 2026-11-11 as the bacterial experimental exemplar of step 3 of
MODULE:phosphorylated_serine_biosynthesis. No deep-research provider file was generated; the
review rests on the UniProt record and cached publications.

- The EcoCyc/EcoliWiki IDA rows all cite Pizer 1963 (PMID:14086727), which has no PubMed
  abstract; the cached record is title-only. These rows are accepted or refined (generic
  phosphatase -> GO:0036424; amino acid biosynthesis -> GO:0006564) because the activity is
  independently established by kinetics on purified SerB recorded in UniProt from
  PMID:16990279 ("KM=0.097 mM for P-Ser (with magnesium ions as cofactor and at pH 9)").
- Mg2+ binding is kept as non-core cofactor binding, consistent with the NAD treatment in the
  E. coli SerA review.
