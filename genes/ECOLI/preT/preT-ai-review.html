Gene Description

preT (yeiT, b2146) encodes the electron-input subunit of the NAD-dependent dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase of Escherichia coli. PreT and PreA form a cytoplasmic heterotetramer (two PreT, two PreA) that reduces uracil and thymine to 5,6-dihydrouracil and 5,6-dihydrothymine with NADH, and catalyses the reverse dehydrogenation. PreT is homologous to the N-terminal NAD(P)H/FAD and iron-sulfur region of single-chain eukaryotic DPYD: it carries an FAD/NAD-binding domain, the dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase domain II and a helical ferredoxin-like iron-sulfur region that pass electrons from NADH to the FMN- and pyrimidine-binding PreA subunit. The enzyme is required for dihydrouracil production in vivo, although E. coli lacks the downstream dihydropyrimidinase and beta-ureidopropionase steps of the reductive pathway, so its physiological role is in pyrimidine pool control rather than pyrimidine-base utilization.

preT (P76440, b2146) notes

2026-10-23 review

Reviewed for the pyrimidine_degradation module, where PreT-PreA is the
biochemically characterized bacterial heteromeric dihydropyrimidine
dehydrogenase. Deep research could not be run in this session (no provider API
key); the review rests on the cached abstracts and the curated UniProt record.

  • PreT-PreA is an NADH-dependent iron-sulfur flavoenzyme that converts uracil
    to 5,6-dihydrouracil in vivo PMID:21169495.
  • E. coli lacks the rest of the reductive pathway PMID:21169495,
    so the enzyme exemplifies the first-step activity but E. coli does not
    satisfy the whole module.
  • Heterotetramer with FAD, FMN and about four [4Fe-4S] clusters per PreT-PreA
    dimer PMID:34097066.
  • PreT carries the FAD/NAD-binding domain (IPR023753), DPD domain II
    (IPR028261) and the NAD(+) binding site, so it is the NADH-oxidizing,
    electron-input subunit (orthologous role to PSEPK PydX).

Curation decisions: protein-binding IPI rows removed under policy (the partner
is PreA, captured by GO:0140690); iron-sulfur cluster binding refined to
GO:0051539; generic oxidoreductase and oxidoreductase-complex terms TOO_GENERAL.
