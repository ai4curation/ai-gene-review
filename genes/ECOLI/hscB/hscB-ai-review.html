Gene Description

HscB (Hsc20) is a 171-residue cytosolic J-type co-chaperone encoded in the iscRSUA-hscBA-fdx operon. It has an N-terminal J domain that contacts the dedicated Hsp70 HscA (Hsc66) and a three-helix C-terminal domain whose conserved hydrophobic and acidic patch binds the Fe-S scaffold protein IscU. HscB has no intrinsic chaperone activity of its own; instead it delivers IscU (apo or cluster-bound) to HscA, lowers the IscU concentration needed to activate HscA, and synergistically stimulates HscA ATP hydrolysis several hundred-fold. The resulting HscA-IscU-HscB ternary complex drives the ATP cycle that releases and transfers IscU-assembled Fe-S clusters to acceptor apoproteins, so HscB acts as the targeting and ATPase-activating component of the Isc Fe-S cluster biogenesis machinery. A weak interaction with the cysteine desulfurase IscS has also been reported.
