Gene Description

putA encodes the trifunctional proline utilization A (PutA) flavoprotein of Escherichia coli. Its N-terminal ribbon-helix-helix domain binds GTTGCA operator sites in the putA-putP intergenic control region and represses both genes; its FAD-dependent proline dehydrogenase domain oxidizes L-proline to 1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate, passing electrons to ubiquinone in the respiratory chain; and its C-terminal NAD+-dependent L-glutamate gamma-semialdehyde (P5C) dehydrogenase domain oxidizes the hydrolysed intermediate to L-glutamate, with substrate channeling between the two active sites. When proline is scarce the flavin is oxidized and the dimeric protein is cytosolic and acts as a repressor; reduction of the flavin by proline triggers a conformational switch that moves PutA to the cytoplasmic face of the inner membrane, where it catabolizes proline for use as a carbon, nitrogen and energy source and relieves repression of the put genes.
