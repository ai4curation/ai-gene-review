Gene Description

DnaQ is the epsilon proofreading subunit of Escherichia coli DNA polymerase III. Its 3′ to 5′ exonuclease removes incorrectly incorporated terminal nucleotides and promotes replication fidelity. Epsilon associates with the DnaE alpha polymerase and HolE theta subunit in the polymerase core and also improves productive DNA synthesis. Polymerization and proofreading are catalyzed by different subunits.
