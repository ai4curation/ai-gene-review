Gene Description

PaaC is the structural beta subunit of the soluble multicomponent phenylacetyl-CoA 1,2-epoxidase (PaaABCE) of Escherichia coli, the ring-activating enzyme of the aerobic phenylacetate catabolic pathway encoded by the paa operon. PaaC is a paralog of the catalytic alpha subunit PaaA (about 17% identity) and shares its ferritin-like four-helix-bundle fold, but its helix bundle is packed with side chains, lacks the substrate tunnel and does not bind CoA ligands or carry a metal site. PaaC forms a stable (PaaAC)2 heterotetramer with PaaA through a hydrophobic interface, which keeps the otherwise aggregation-prone PaaA soluble, and also forms a heterodimer with the small subunit PaaB, bridging PaaB to the PaaAC core. PaaC is required for epoxidase activity in reconstitution assays and for ring hydroxylation in vivo; in E. coli its translation is coupled to paaB.

Core Functions

Structural beta subunit of the PaaABCE phenylacetyl-CoA 1,2-epoxidase: forms the (PaaAC)2 hydroxylase core with the catalytic PaaA subunit, stabilizing PaaA, and bridges the small PaaB subunit to the core, thereby contributing to ring epoxidation in aerobic phenylacetate catabolism.
