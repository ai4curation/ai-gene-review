Gene Description

Ffh (fifty-four homolog) is the protein subunit of the Escherichia coli signal recognition particle (SRP), a ribonucleoprotein formed with the 4.5S RNA encoded by ffs. Its N domain contacts the ribosome near ribosomal protein L23, its central G domain is a GTPase, and its C-terminal M domain binds both the 4.5S RNA and the hydrophobic signal or signal-anchor sequence of a nascent membrane protein as it emerges from the ribosome. The SRP-ribosome-nascent-chain complex is delivered to the membrane-associated SRP receptor FtsY; Ffh and FtsY then form a GTP-dependent heterodimer in which each stimulates the other's GTP hydrolysis, transferring the nascent chain to the SecYEG translocon and dissociating the targeting factors for reuse. Ffh is essential for viability and for inner-membrane protein biogenesis. SRP also binds the heat-shock sigma factor sigma32 and contributes to its membrane association and homeostatic control.
