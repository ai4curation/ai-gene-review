Gene Description

DsbE (CcmG) is a membrane-anchored thioredoxin-like thiol-disulfide oxidoreductase of cytochrome c maturation system I in Escherichia coli. A single N-terminal transmembrane helix anchors it in the inner membrane, and its C-terminal thioredoxin domain, carrying a WCPTC active site, faces the periplasm. DsbE acts as a highly specific disulfide reductase in the late, reductive steps of c-type cytochrome assembly: it keeps the cysteines of the apocytochrome CXXCH heme-binding motif reduced, either by reducing apocytochrome disulfides directly or by resolving mixed disulfides between apocytochrome c and CcmH, so that heme can be covalently attached by the CcmF/CcmH synthase. DsbE is itself kept reduced by direct disulfide exchange with the N-terminal periplasmic domain of the transmembrane electron transporter DsbD, which delivers reducing equivalents from cytoplasmic thioredoxin. Deletion of dsbE abolishes formation of all c-type cytochromes.

Core Functions

Periplasmic, membrane-anchored thioredoxin-like disulfide reductase that keeps apocytochrome c CXXCH cysteines reduced for heme ligation, by reducing apocytochrome disulfides and resolving CcmH-apocytochrome mixed disulfides; it is itself re-reduced by DsbD.
