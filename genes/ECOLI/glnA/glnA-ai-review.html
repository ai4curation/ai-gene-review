Gene Description

GlnA is the type I glutamine synthetase of Escherichia coli K-12. It uses ATP to incorporate ammonium into L-glutamate, producing L-glutamine in the cytosol. This reaction supplies biosynthetic glutamine and initiates the glutamine synthetase–glutamate synthase route of ammonium assimilation. The enzyme forms a dodecamer of two hexameric rings and its activity is regulated by reversible adenylylation in response to nitrogen status.

GlnA is an ATP-dependent glutamine synthetase. Native E. coli enzyme purification is explicit in the accessible PMID:6102984 abstract: “We used the PEG procedure to purify GS from Klebsiella aerogenes, K. pneumoniae, Escherichia coli”. The PMC landing acquisition is abstract plus references, not recovered full methods. The target UniProt FUNCTION/CATALYTIC ACTIVITY and homooligomeric architecture are curated orthology transfers. The relevant broad catalytic, glutamine-metabolic and nitrogen-utilization rows refine to glutamine synthetase, L-glutamine biosynthesis and the existing ammonia-assimilation-cycle term.

2026-10-28 — PR #5696 direct structural evidence

The earlier description of the oligomeric evidence as only an orthology transfer was incomplete. The UniProt CC:SUBUNIT sentence is indeed transferred from P0A1P6, but the same immutable target record lists PDB 8V22, a 2.20-Angstrom cryo-EM structure mapped to all 469 residues of P0A9C5. The normally retrieved RCSB deposition assigns an author-defined D6 homo-12-mer biological assembly and links primary PMID:40849408. Full-text GlnA results, expression/purification and cryo-EM methods were read; the paper directly reconstructs recombinant AMPylated E. coli GlnA as a dodecamer, despite its title foregrounding mammalian mitochondrial AMPylation.

The 8V22 polymer entity has 474 residues. Its RCSB polymer-entity API maps residues 6-474 to UniProt residues 1-469 by SIFTS. Direct comparison with the immutable target UniProt sequence confirms exact identity across all 469 residues and an additional N-terminal SEFEL segment. No target mutation is present. API responses and the sequence-identity proof are retained in .cache/module-audit-logs/pr5696-glna-followup/. The structure is therefore direct evidence for the target protein, with a documented recombinant construct extension.

PMID:40849408 reports expression from GlnA ppSumo in BL21, purification of AMPylated protein, removal of the His-SUMO tag, and cryo-EM reconstruction of its assembly. This supports the description's two-ring dodecamer and the existing GO:0042802 ACCEPT decision. It is not an in-cell K-12 stoichiometry measurement, a localization assay, or a basis to annotate the engineered hinT H101N enrichment interaction as a physiological GlnA function. The two membrane decisions remain UNDECIDED. No new annotation is proposed.
