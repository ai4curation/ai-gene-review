CC   -!- FUNCTION: Genetic interactions indicate that RecG or RadD are required
CC       for DNA repair in every replication cycle; they function in different
CC       pathways, each is essential in the absence of the other
CC       (PubMed:32644157). Plays a critical role in recombination and DNA
CC       repair (PubMed:8428576, PubMed:7957087, PubMed:8127666, PubMed:9265736,
CC       PubMed:10871364, PubMed:32644157). RecG or RadD are required for DNA
CC       maintenance in every replication cycle (PubMed:32644157). Helps process
CC       Holliday junction (HJ) intermediates to mature products by catalyzing
CC       branch migration (PubMed:8428576, PubMed:7957087, PubMed:8127666,
CC       PubMed:10871364). Has replication fork regression activity, unwinds
CC       stalled or blocked replication forks to make a HJ that can be resolved
CC       by RuvC or RusA (PubMed:10778854, PubMed:11459957). Also rewinds
CC       unwound dsDNA in an ATP-dependent manner (PubMed:24013402). Has double-
CC       stranded (ds)DNA unwinding activity characteristic of a DNA helicase
CC       with 3'-5' polarity in vitro on linear dsDNA; branched duplex DNA (Y-
CC       DNA) substrates adopt different conformations that influence which of
CC       the two arms are unwound (PubMed:7957087). Binds and unwinds HJ and Y-
CC       DNA but not linear duplex DNA; binds no more than 10 nucleotides of
CC       ssDNA at a fork (PubMed:8428576, PubMed:7957087, PubMed:8127666,
CC       PubMed:10871364, PubMed:11459957, PubMed:15533834). Has a role in
CC       constitutive stable DNA replication (cSDR, DNA replication in the
CC       absence of protein synthesis) and R-loop (RNA annealed with dsDNA)
CC       formation (PubMed:7774596). Unwinds R-loops but not RNA:DNA hybrids
CC       (PubMed:8980680, PubMed:15533834). Is genetically synergistic to RadA
CC       and RuvABC (PubMed:12446634, PubMed:25484163).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10778854, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10871364,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11459957, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12446634,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15533834, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24013402,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25484163, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32644157,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7774596, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7957087,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8127666, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8428576,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9265736}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Uup probably acts upstream of RecG; they are important in
CC       repair of stalled replication forks and postreplication gaps
CC       (PubMed:32644157). Lethal double radD-recG deletions are suppressed by
CC       a reduction in recA expression, by point mutations in priA helicase, by
CC       deletions in recF, recO or uup, suggesting all these proteins create
CC       branched DNA intermediates requiring RadD or RecG for resolution
CC       (PubMed:32644157). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32644157}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: About 10% decrease in recombination, increased
CC       sensitivity to UV light, ionizing radiation and mitomycin C (MMC);
CC       recB-recG and recG-recJ double mutants are more sensitive to UV
CC       (PubMed:1846849). In the absence of DNA damaging agents 40% of cells
CC       are longer than wild-type; after UV treatment cells are hetergeneous
CC       for cell length and about 10% are anucleate (PubMed:9265736). The SOS
CC       response is induced in the absence of DNA damage (PubMed:32644157). In
CC       the absence of ruvABC, severe loss of resistance to UV
CC       (PubMed:10778854). Very slight sensitivity to ciprofloxacin (CFX), UV
CC       and azidothymidine (AZT) in single deletion mutants, while a radA-recG
CC       deletion is extremely sensitive to CFX and AZT, less so to UV
CC       (PubMed:12446634, PubMed:25484163). The SOS response is induced in the
CC       radA-recG double mutant, indicating spontaneous DNA damage
CC       (PubMed:25484163). AZT sensitivity is suppressed by further recA or
CC       recF deletions, suggesting AZT-induced DNA gaps are processed into
CC       lethal intermediates in a RecA-dependent fashion mediated by RecF
CC       (PubMed:25484163). Another study found extreme sensitivity to CPX,
CC       nitrofurazone, hydroxyurea, MMC, trimethoprim (Trim) and UV; a double
CC       recG-uup deletion restores growth to all but CPX, MMC and Trim
CC       (PubMed:32644157). Double radD-recG deletions have a severe growth
CC       phenotype in the absence of DNA damage, and acquire suppressors
CC       quickly; most spontaneous suppressors are in priA helicase
CC       (PubMed:32644157). Triple radD-recG-uup mutants grow in the absence of
CC       DNA damage, but are filamentous and highly sensitive to all DNA
CC       damaging agents tested (PubMed:32644157). Decreased genetic instability
CC       of G-quadruplex DNA; a double recG-recQ deletion shows 10-fold
CC       increased genetic instability (PubMed:37761860).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10778854, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12446634,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:1846849, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25484163,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32644157, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37761860,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9265736}.
