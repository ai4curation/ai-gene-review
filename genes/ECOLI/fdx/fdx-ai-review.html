Gene Description

Fdx is a small (111-residue) cytosolic [2Fe-2S] ferredoxin of the adrenodoxin/putidaredoxin family, encoded as the last structural gene of the iscRSUA-hscBA-fdx operon. Its single [2Fe-2S] cluster is ligated by four cysteines, and the cluster can itself be supplied by the Isc system (for example from holo-IscA). Fdx acts as a one-electron carrier for Fe-S cluster biogenesis: it binds the cysteine desulfurase IscS near the active-site Cys328 loop, on a surface that overlaps the binding sites of IscU and the frataxin CyaY, and reduced Fdx donates an electron to the IscS persulfide formed during cysteine turnover, a step needed for [2Fe-2S] cluster formation on IscU. Loss of fdx causes Fe-S enzyme defects similar to those of iscU, hscB and hscA mutants. Its physiological electron donor in E. coli has not been firmly established.
