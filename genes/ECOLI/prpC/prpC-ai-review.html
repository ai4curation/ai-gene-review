Gene Description

PrpC is the 2-methylcitrate synthase of the propionate-inducible prpBCDE operon of Escherichia coli K-12. This soluble, homodimeric member of the citrate synthase family catalyzes the Claisen condensation of propionyl-CoA with oxaloacetate to form 2-methylcitrate and CoA, the first committed step of the 2-methylcitrate cycle that oxidizes propionate to pyruvate (with succinate regenerated to oxaloacetate). PrpC also condenses acetyl-CoA with oxaloacetate to give citrate, but with lower activity and higher Km than with propionyl-CoA; it was first isolated as a dimeric "citrate synthase II" from a gltA revertant and is distinct from the hexameric, allosterically regulated housekeeping TCA-cycle citrate synthase GltA. Expression is induced by propionate (via PrpR) and subject to CRP-dependent catabolite repression.
