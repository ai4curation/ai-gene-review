Gene Description

MlaC (formerly YrbC) is the soluble periplasmic phospholipid carrier of the Mla (maintenance of lipid asymmetry) intermembrane phospholipid transport system. Exported by a cleaved N-terminal signal peptide, it adopts a mixed alpha/beta fold related to the NTF2/cystatin superfamily, with a deep hydrophobic pocket that accommodates the two acyl chains of a diacyl phospholipid while leaving the head group solvent-exposed, so binding shows little head-group selectivity; a pivoting beta-sheet opens and closes the pocket between apo and lipid-loaded states. MlaC docks transiently on the periplasmic base of the outer-membrane MlaA-OmpC/OmpF complex and on the MlaD hexamer of the inner-membrane ABC transporter MlaFEDB, and shuttles phospholipid across the aqueous periplasm between them. Purified MlaC exchanges phospholipids spontaneously with MlaD/MlaFEDB, and ATP hydrolysis by MlaFEDB drives net movement from MlaC into the inner membrane, the retrograde direction that removes phospholipids mislocalized in the outer leaflet of the outer membrane. Loss of MlaC causes phospholipid accumulation in the outer leaflet, increased outer membrane permeability and sensitivity to SDS-EDTA.

Core Functions

Periplasmic phospholipid carrier: receives a diacyl phospholipid into its hydrophobic pocket at one envelope membrane complex (MlaA-OmpC/F in the outer membrane or MlaD of MlaFEDB in the inner membrane), carries it across the aqueous periplasm and delivers it to the other, with ATP hydrolysis by MlaFEDB driving net retrograde flow into the inner membrane.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: What is the stoichiometry and head-group/acyl-chain preference of phospholipid loading by E. coli MlaC in vivo (one versus two diacyl phospholipids, cardiolipin)?

Q: How is lipid release from MlaC at MlaD coupled to MlaFEDB ATP hydrolysis, given that MlaC alone does not stimulate the transporter ATPase?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Native mass spectrometry and lipidomics of MlaC purified from the periplasm of wild-type versus mlaA and mlaFEDB mutant strains, to define which phospholipids MlaC carries in vivo and from which membrane they originate.
