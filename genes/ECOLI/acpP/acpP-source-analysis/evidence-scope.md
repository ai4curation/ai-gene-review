Recombinant AcpP was expressed in C3031I from pET28a with a thrombin-cleavable N-terminal His tag; tag cleavage and purity assessment are described. The coding donor accession is not supplied in the accessible methods. The K-12 reference protein independently has an experimentally curated MukB interaction (UniProt citing20921377).
