Gene Description

AcpP is the small soluble acyl carrier protein of Escherichia coli K-12. Its phosphopantetheine arm carries growing acyl chains and presents them to enzymes of fatty-acid, phospholipid, lipid-A and pimelate/biotin precursor metabolism. AcpP also interacts with SpoT in lipid-starvation signaling and promotes MukB ATPase function in chromosome organization. Separately, AcpP supplies a cytosolic protein cofactor for the membrane system that synthesizes the osmoregulated periplasmic glucan backbone; this role does not require its phosphopantetheine prosthetic group and does not establish intrinsic glucan-transfer catalysis.

description: AcpP is the small soluble acyl carrier protein of Escherichia coli K-12. Its phosphopantetheine
  arm carries growing acyl chains and presents them to enzymes of fatty-acid, phospholipid, lipid-A and
  pimelate/biotin precursor metabolism. AcpP also interacts with SpoT in lipid-starvation signaling and
  promotes MukB ATPase function in chromosome organization. Separately, AcpP supplies a cytosolic protein
  cofactor for the membrane system that synthesizes the osmoregulated periplasmic glucan backbone; this
  role does not require its phosphopantetheine prosthetic group and does not establish intrinsic glucan-transfer
  catalysis.
