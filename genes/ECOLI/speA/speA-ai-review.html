Gene Description

SpeA is the biosynthetic arginine decarboxylase (ADC) of Escherichia coli, a 658-residue fold-type III pyridoxal 5'-phosphate (PLP) enzyme of the Orn/Lys/Arg decarboxylase class-II family that decarboxylates L-arginine to agmatine and CO2. Agmatine is hydrolysed by agmatinase (SpeB) to putrescine, so SpeA initiates the arginine route to putrescine and, through putrescine, to spermidine. The purified enzyme is a Mg2+-dependent homotetramer with a low micromolar Km for arginine and is feedback-inhibited by putrescine and spermidine; its activity is further restrained post-translationally by antizyme and the ribosomal proteins S20 and L34. Cell fractionation placed the processed mature enzyme in the periplasm. SpeA is distinct from the acid-inducible biodegradative arginine decarboxylase AdiA.
