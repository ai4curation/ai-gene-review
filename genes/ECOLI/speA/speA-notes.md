# speA (P21170) curation notes

Deep research: no provider run (no API keys in this session); the review was built
from the Swiss-Prot record and the cached publications.

- Biosynthetic ADC; recombinant enzyme active at a level similar to native ADC.
  [PMID:19603287 "The recombinant ADC was assayed for decarboxylase activity, showing
  decarboxylase activity of 2.8 U/mg, similar to the purified native E. coli ADC."]
- Inhibited post-translationally by antizyme and ribosomal proteins S20/L34.
  [PMID:8088419 "Ornithine and arginine decarboxylases (ODC and ADC) of Escherichia coli
  are inhibited post-translationally by antizyme and ribosomal proteins S20 and L34."]
- Periplasmic location: UniProt cites PMID:2198270; the abstract shows precursor and
  mature fusion forms but the fractionation itself is in the unavailable full text.
  Kept as non-core and flagged as a question.
- PMID:4571773 / PMID:4571774 (Wu & Morris 1973) have no cached abstract; their rows
  were checked against UniProt lines that cite them (cofactor Mg2+, Km 0.03 mM,
  homotetramer) and the curators were deferred to.
- L-arginine catabolic process kept as non-core, matching the P. putida SpeA review:
  chemically accurate, but the physiological role is putrescine supply.
