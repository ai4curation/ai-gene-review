Gene Description

AsnS is the cytoplasmic, homodimeric class II asparaginyl-tRNA synthetase of Escherichia coli K-12. It activates L-asparagine with ATP and transfers the amino acid to tRNA(Asn), supplying Asn-tRNA for protein synthesis. Mutations in conserved catalytic regions impair ATP or asparagine recognition and can produce temperature-sensitive translation.
