CC   -!- FUNCTION: CRISPR (clustered regularly interspaced short palindromic
CC       repeat), is an adaptive immune system that provides protection against
CC       mobile genetic elements (viruses, transposable elements and conjugative
CC       plasmids). CRISPR clusters contain sequences complementary to
CC       antecedent mobile elements and target invading nucleic acids. CRISPR
CC       clusters are transcribed and processed into CRISPR RNA (crRNA). Cas3
CC       plus Cascade participate in CRISPR interference, the third stage of
CC       CRISPR immunity. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18703739}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as an endonuclease, a 3'-5'exonuclease, and an ATP-
CC       dependent RNA:DNA helicase (PubMed:21699496, PubMed:22521689). Anneals
CC       and unwinds R-loops (in which crRNA binds the target DNA, displacing
CC       the noncomplementary strand) (PubMed:21699496). Unwinding requires ATP,
CC       annealing does not (PubMed:21699496). Required along with the Cascade
CC       complex for resistance to bacteriophage lambda infection as well as the
CC       ability to cure CRISPR-encoding high-copy number plasmid
CC       (PubMed:22521689). A Cas3-CasA fusion protein purified with the Cascade
CC       complex nicks target plasmid in the presence but not absence of Mg(2+),
CC       and degrades plasmid fully in the presence of Mg(2+) and ATP,
CC       suggesting the helicase activity is required for complete degradation
CC       (PubMed:22521689). Probably unwinds nucleic acids in a 3'-5' direction
CC       (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:F2XG53,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21699496, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22521689}.
CC   -!- COFACTOR:
CC       Name=Mg(2+); Xref=ChEBI:CHEBI:18420;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:21699496, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22521689};
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the CasA subunit of Cascade once Cascade has
CC       recognized target DNA. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22521689}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Proteins of this family have an N-terminal nuclease domain and
CC       a C-terminal helicase/ATPase domain. In some CRISPR/Cas systems the
CC       domains are swapped, in others they are encoded separately.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Loss of plasmid silencing.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18703739}.
