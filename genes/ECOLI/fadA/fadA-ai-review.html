Gene Description

FadA is the beta-subunit 3-ketoacyl-CoA thiolase of the cytoplasmic FadBA fatty acid oxidation complex in Escherichia coli K-12. It catalyzes CoA-dependent cleavage of a 3-oxoacyl-CoA to acetyl-CoA and an acyl-CoA shortened by two carbons. FadB supplies the preceding hydration and dehydrogenation reactions. The native pathway can accumulate shorter acyl-CoA intermediates, so the presence of the four spiral reactions does not guarantee complete conversion of an input chain to acetyl-CoA. The related FadI thiolase supports an alternative pathway in particular conditions.

Core Functions

FadA, as the beta subunit of the FadBA complex, cleaves 3-oxoacyl-CoA with CoA to release acetyl-CoA and a chain-shortened acyl-CoA in the final step of each beta-oxidation turn.
