Gene Description

MmuM, formerly YagD, is a zinc-dependent homocysteine S-methyltransferase of Escherichia coli K-12. It transfers a methyl group from S-methylmethionine to homocysteine, yielding two methionine molecules, and enables utilization of imported S-methylmethionine when folate-dependent methionine synthesis is unavailable. Purified MmuM also accepts SAM as a donor and selenium-containing acceptors. SAM-dependent activity has been linked to use of damaged SAM stereoisomers in distinct experimental contexts. These reactions do not by themselves establish a complete endogenous S-methylmethionine cycle.
