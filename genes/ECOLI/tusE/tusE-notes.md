# ECOLI tusE: source-bounded annotation review

TusE is a cytoplasmic sulfur-relay protein involved in C2 thiolation of tRNA wobble uridine. It interacts with TusBCD, stimulates sulfur transfer from TusA to TusD, and associates with the MnmA–tRNA reaction. Its sulfur-carrier role is supported, while direct transfer of a TusE persulfide onto an MnmA cysteine has not been conclusively demonstrated.
