# tusE (E. coli K-12) curation notes

## Sources checked

- UniProtKB record (genes/ECOLI/tusE/tusE-uniprot.txt), GOA table, and the cached
  abstracts of PMID:16387657 and PMID:16472754 (abstract-only in the cache).
- No provider deep-research file was generated for this gene.

## Findings

- TusE binds TusBCD and the MnmA-tRNA complex [PMID:16387657 "TusE binds TusBCD
  complex and stimulates sulfur transfer from TusA to TusD. TusE also interacts
  with an MnmA-tRNA complex."]; UniProt records Cys108 as the persulfide site
  with a C108S loss-of-function mutation.
- GO:1990228 sulfurtransferase complex (IPI) is kept as non-core: TusE is not a
  stoichiometric subunit of the crystallographic TusBCD hexamer. The same
  judgement is applied to the MnmA GO:1990228 rows.
