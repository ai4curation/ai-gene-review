CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required for efficient ubiquinone (coenzyme Q) biosynthesis
CC       under aerobic conditions (PubMed:28559279, PubMed:30686758). UbiK is
CC       probably an accessory factor of Ubi enzymes and facilitates ubiquinone
CC       biosynthesis by acting as an assembly factor, a targeting factor, or
CC       both (PubMed:28559279). Dispensable for ubiquinone biosynthesis under
CC       anaerobiosis (PubMed:28559279). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28559279,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30686758}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Cofactor biosynthesis; ubiquinone biosynthesis.
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_02216, ECO:0000305|PubMed:28559279}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homotrimer (PubMed:28559279). Component of the Ubi complex
CC       metabolon, which regroups five ubiquinone biosynthesis proteins (UbiE,
CC       UbiF, UbiG, UbiH and UbiI) and two accessory factors (UbiK and the
CC       lipid-binding protein UbiJ) (PubMed:30686758). Interacts with the C-
CC       terminal part of UbiJ and forms a complex composed of 2 UbiK subunits
CC       and 1 UbiJ subunit. The UbiK-UbiJ complex interacts with palmitoleic
CC       acid (PubMed:28559279). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28559279,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30686758}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_02216,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30686758}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Deletion of the gene impacts ubiquinone
CC       biosynthesis in aerobic conditions but does not decrease menaquinone
CC       biosynthesis. Mutant accumulates octaprenylphenol, an early
CC       intermediate of the ubiquinone biosynthetic pathway.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28559279}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the UbiK family. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_02216, ECO:0000305}.
