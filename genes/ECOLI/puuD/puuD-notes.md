# puuD (P76038) curation notes

Provider deep research was not run for this gene in this session (no provider
credentials available). Sources: PMID:15590624 and PMID:16499623 (abstracts
cached) and the curated UniProt record.

- Puu step: "5) hydrolysis of the gamma-glutamyl linkage of
  gamma-glutamyl-gamma-aminobutyrate" [PMID:15590624].
- "The active center of PuuD was identified as Cys-114 by site-directed
  mutagenesis" [PMID:16499623]; puuD mutants accumulate gamma-Glu-GABA and do
  not grow on putrescine as sole nitrogen source [PMID:16499623].
- PuuD is the experimental seed of PAINT node PTN000230196 (PTHR43235), which
  carries GO:0033969 and GO:0006598.
