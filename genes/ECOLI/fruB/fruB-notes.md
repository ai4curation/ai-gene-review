# fruB (FPr, P69811) notes

Reviewed 2026-11-11 to ground the split-relay architecture of the generic
fructose PTS module (`modules/fructose_pts_uptake_and_catabolism.yaml`).

Deep research: no provider deep-research file was generated for this gene in this
session; the review rests on the cached publications and the UniProt record.

- FPr is an EIIA(Fru)-M-HPr fusion without an enzyme I region; enzyme I (PtsI)
  phosphorylates its HPr-like domain [PMID:7006754 "A protein that substitutes for
  histidine-containing protein (HPr) in the phosphoenolpyruvate, fructose
  phosphotransferase system has been found in Escherichia coli grown on fructose."].
- FPr is the phosphoryl donor (DTP) to the FruA EIIB'BC permease [PMID:8626640
  "its phosphoryl donating protein, the IIA-FPr diphosphoryl transfer protein (DTP)"].
- GO:0022877 rows are refined (MODIFY) to the proposed "PTS EIIA-to-EIIB
  phosphotransferase activity", the same proposal made for P. putida FruB, because
  the transporter activity term names FruA's EIIB-EIIC step.
- GO:0090563 IBA removed: propagated from MtlA's EIIB domain, absent from FPr.
