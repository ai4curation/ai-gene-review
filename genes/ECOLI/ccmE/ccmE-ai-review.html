Gene Description

CcmE is the heme chaperone of the Escherichia coli cytochrome c maturation (Ccm) system I. It is a monotopic inner-membrane protein with an N-terminal signal-anchor helix and a C-terminal periplasmic OB-fold heme-binding domain. In a ternary complex with CcmC and CcmD, CcmE receives heme from CcmC and binds it covalently through a single bond between His130 and a heme vinyl group, with Tyr134 providing an axial iron ligand, forming holo-CcmE. The ATP-hydrolysing CcmAB subcomplex is then needed to release holo-CcmE from CcmC, after which CcmE delivers the heme to the CcmF/CcmH holocytochrome c synthase for covalent attachment to the CXXCH motifs of periplasmic apocytochromes c. CcmE is essential for the formation of all periplasmic c-type cytochromes in E. coli.

Core Functions

Periplasmic heme chaperone: receives heme from CcmC in the CcmCDE complex, binds it covalently via His130 to form holo-CcmE, and after CcmAB-dependent release delivers the heme to the CcmF/CcmH synthase for covalent attachment to apocytochrome c.
