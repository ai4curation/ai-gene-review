Gene Description

MetE is the cobalamin-independent methionine synthase of Escherichia coli K-12. This soluble zinc enzyme transfers a methyl group directly from polyglutamyl methyltetrahydrofolate to homocysteine, producing methionine and regenerating tetrahydrofolate. Its folate substrate requirement distinguishes it from the monoglutamate reaction of MetH. Oxidative glutathionylation at Cys645 can reversibly inhibit the enzyme and limit methionine synthesis under the tested stress conditions.
