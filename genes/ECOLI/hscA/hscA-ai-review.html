Gene Description

HscA (Hsc66) is a specialized cytosolic Hsp70-family chaperone encoded with its J-type co-chaperone HscB in the iscRSUA-hscBA-fdx operon. It has a low intrinsic ATPase activity, limited by ATP hydrolysis rather than nucleotide exchange, and needs no GrpE-type exchange factor. Its substrate-binding domain recognizes a conserved LPPVK loop of the Fe-S scaffold protein IscU, and HscB delivers IscU to the ATP-bound state of HscA; together IscU and HscB stimulate HscA ATP hydrolysis about 500-fold, trapping IscU in the ADP-bound complex. ATP hydrolysis by the 1:1:1 HscA-HscB-IscU[2Fe-2S] complex distorts the IscU-bound cluster and accelerates [2Fe-2S] cluster transfer to acceptor apoproteins such as ferredoxin, so HscA acts as the ATP-driven engine of cluster release in Isc-mediated Fe-S protein maturation. HscA also binds cluster-deficient biotin synthase and improves its in vivo [2Fe-2S] cluster assembly, and its expression is induced by cold shock.
