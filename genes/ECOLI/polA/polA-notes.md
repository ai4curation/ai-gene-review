# polA: maturation/topology annotation review

## 2026-11-06

PolA P00582 is the 928-aa K-12 reference enzyme. It combines a 5′ nuclease with the Klenow polymerase/proofreading region; it is not the principal Pol III replicase. [PMID:6262323 "E. coli DNA polymerase I was needed to fill the short gap left by DNA polymerase III holoenzyme, and to remove the primer and replace it with DNA."] This is a purified E. coli enzyme reaction on a phiX174 model, not an assay of native chromosomal fragments. Repair synthesis is independently supported by the toluene-treated-cell experiments (PMID:169227) and live-cell fluorescent Pol I tracking (PMID:23630273). Normal force-fetch did not recover the latter's full text; no unverified fluorescent construct, exact strain, or detailed rate is assigned to the reference protein.

The existing double-strand-break-repair IBA is retained as non-core conditional repair synthesis. PMID:20523737 provides genetic discrimination supporting polymerase competition at primer termini in the Lac stress-mutagenesis/DSB-repair system; this is a mechanistic model from genetic evidence, not direct imaging of Pol I at breaks. By contrast, PMID:36326440 explicitly studies MG1655 derivatives and shows polA-related break accumulation/homeostasis; those phenotypes alone would not establish direct break-repair participation. The PAINT ancestral assertion is not dismissed because its listed descendants are other taxa.

Comparative limits: PMID:42133467 describes Bacillus FenA as a major primer-processing enzyme, a RNase HIII/PolA alternative, and unresolved identity of the gap-filling polymerase; its suggestion of PolC filling is a proposal. PMID:41728957 computationally finds Pol I orthologs in approximately 96% of the surveyed genomes and identifies variant domain architectures. Neither prevalence nor the E. coli full-length enzyme proves universal PolA polymerase or proofreading requirements. No additional process annotation was manufactured for pathway completeness.
