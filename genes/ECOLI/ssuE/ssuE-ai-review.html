Gene Description

SsuE is the FMN reductase of the Escherichia coli two-component alkanesulfonate monooxygenase system. It uses either NADH or NADPH with comparable measured steady-state kinetics to generate FMNH2 for the SsuD oxygenase, which releases sulfite from alkanesulfonates. SsuE also reduces FAD and riboflavin under biochemical assay conditions. Its active site spans a dimer, and a solution dimer–tetramer equilibrium shifts toward dimers upon FMN binding. SsuE–SsuD interactions promote efficient flavin transfer, while diffusion can support desulfonation under particular assay conditions.

Core Functions
