# ECOLI tusB: source-bounded annotation review

TusB is a structural subunit of the cytoplasmic TusBCD sulfur-relay complex that participates in C2 thiolation of tRNA wobble uridine. Two copies each of TusB, TusC and TusD form a heterohexamer. TusB supports the sulfur-transfer assembly, whereas the experimentally implicated sulfur-bearing cysteine is on TusD.
