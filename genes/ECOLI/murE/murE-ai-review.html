Gene Description

MurE is the ATP-dependent UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate:meso-2,6-diaminopimelate ligase of Escherichia coli (EC 6.3.2.13). It adds meso-diaminopimelate to the D-glutamate of UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu, forming UDP-MurNAc-tripeptide, the third step of the cytoplasmic Mur ligase series that builds the peptidoglycan stem peptide. The soluble, cytosolic enzyme has three domains, a carbamylated active-site lysine that positions Mg2+-ATP, and a C-terminal recognition motif that selects meso-diaminopimelate over L-lysine; it accepts some meso-DAP analogues (lanthionine, cystathionine) but not L-lysine. MurE is essential: thermosensitive mutants accumulate UDP-MurNAc-dipeptide, fail to make murein and lyse.

Core Functions

ATP-dependent addition of meso-diaminopimelate to UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu, forming UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-gamma-D-Glu-meso-DAP (EC 6.3.2.13), the third stem-peptide step of cytoplasmic peptidoglycan precursor synthesis.

murE (P22188, b0085) notes

Identity

  • UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate--2,6-diaminopimelate ligase, EC 6.3.2.13; MurCDEF family, MurE subfamily (UniProt P22188; HAMAP MF_00208; NCBIfam TIGR01085; PANTHER PTHR23135:SF4).
  • No deep-research file was generated; these notes are from the cached publications, UniProt and targeted web searches. FEBA fitness fetch failed (no local FEBA db / server unavailable).

Function

  • Adds meso-diaminopimelate (meso-A2pm) to UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu, the third cytoplasmic stem-peptide step [file:ECOLI/murE/murE-uniprot.txt "Catalyzes the addition of meso-diaminopimelic acid to the nucleotide precursor UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate (UMAG)"].
  • Enzyme over-produced and characterised; reverse and exchange reactions PMID:2269304.
  • Crystal structure 2.0 A with product bound; carbamylated Lys in active site; structural determinant for amino-acid selection identified PMID:11124264. UniProt: MOD_RES N6-carboxylysine K225; DAP recognition motif 414-417.
  • Specificity: accepts DAP analogues (lanthionine, cystathionine) with higher Km PMID:8021219; does not use L-lysine per UniProt ("but not L-lysine", ECO:0000269). Barreteau et al. 2008 review (PMID:18266853; web full text at academic.oup.com, not in cache) states "the Escherichia coli enzyme has a very weak l-Lys-adding activity". So E. coli MurE is a DAP-type MurE; the L-lysine-adding activity (GO:0047482) belongs to Gram-positive MurE orthologs and is correctly absent from GOA for this gene.
  • Substrate recognition needs many contacts across UDP-MurNAc-dipeptide PMID:3905407.

Genetics

  • ts murE mutants: low DAP-adding activity, impaired murein synthesis at 42 C and accumulation of UDP-MurNAc-dipeptide PMID:4553000.

Location

  • Soluble cytoplasmic enzyme; detected in the cytosol proteome PMID:18304323.

Annotation issues

  • InterPro2GO from IPR005761 gives regulation of cell shape and cell division: these are downstream consequences of peptidoglycan supply, not activities of MurE -> over-annotation.
  • IPI protein binding to CarA/YdaY from high-throughput screens: uninformative.
  • Module peptidoglycan_precursor_biosynthesis MurE DAP variant uses GO:0008765 with P22188 as representative: consistent with this review.
