# gyrA: maturation/topology annotation review

## 2026-11-06

GyrA P0AES4 is the 875-aa K-12 reference cleavage/wrapping subunit. The intrinsic DNA-binding domain and the complete gyrase reaction were separated experimentally: PMID:1851291 tests a 33-kDa C-terminal fragment, whereas PMID:347446 reconstitutes the A/B enzyme. [PMID:12051842 "The cleavage-religation reaction involves the formation of a 5'-phosphotyrosine intermediate with the GyrA subunit of the gyrase (A(2)B(2)) complex."] Complete type-II activity is recorded as a complex contribution in the core. Negative supercoiling is retained separately from positive-torsion relaxation; E. coli efficiency is not generalized to other gyrases.

ATP-binding IBA and IEA are rejected as inappropriate transfer of a B-subunit property onto the A chain. PMID:2174443 maps nucleotide-affinity labeling to GyrB residues 93–131, while the structural/biochemical division of labor is summarized in PMID:22206257. The real PAINT source is PTN001607041 in PTHR43493; no IRD/IKR loss was found. The unresolved upstream origin of that node assertion is not misrepresented as an experimentally demonstrated loss on the E. coli lineage. Source defects ask the assigning resources to check the transfer.

The membrane-proteomics row remains UNDECIDED because the original target-specific table is inaccessible; immunogold localization (PMID:7952188) places most GyrA in cytoplasm but cannot exclude a minor peripheral pool. CcdB-dependent polymerase blockage is retained only as a toxin-dependent non-core context (PMID:9367775). Drug-resistance phenotypes are not transformed into drug transport or detoxification. PMID:6178722 specifically compared KL161/KL166 and a gyrA mutant; its phenotype is not assigned quantitatively to wild-type K-12.
