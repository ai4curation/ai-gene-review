mxcE encodes the predicted response regulator of the MxcQE two-component system. Its receiver domain and C-terminal LuxR-type helix-turn-helix domain support phosphorelay and DNA-binding annotations, and AM1 promoter-reporter genetics place MxcQE upstream of the MxbDM two-component system in the methanol-oxidation regulatory cascade. Direct MxcQ-MxcE phosphotransfer, direct MxcE promoter binding, and the native MxcQE input signal remain unresolved.
