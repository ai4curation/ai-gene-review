mxaB encodes an orphan response regulator in the mxaFJGIRSACKLDEHB methanol oxidation gene cluster. The protein has an N-terminal receiver domain with a predicted phosphoaspartate and a C-terminal LuxR-type helix-turn-helix domain, consistent with genetic evidence that MxaB regulates methanol oxidation and is required, with MxcQE and MxbDM, for expression of the Ca2+-dependent mxa methanol dehydrogenase operon. No adjacent sensor kinase was found in the original AM1 mxaB sequence analysis, and direct MxaB phosphorylation and promoter binding remain unresolved.
