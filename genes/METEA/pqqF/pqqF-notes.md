# pqqF (C5AQL6) notes

Reviewed in November 2026 for the PQQ biosynthesis module (modules/pqq_biosynthesis.yaml), as an
experimentally characterized exemplar outside Pseudomonas.

- No provider deep-research report was generated for this gene in this session; the review rests on
  the cached publications cited in the review file and on the UniProt record.
- The M. extorquens AM1 pqq genes were renamed in 1997 (PMID:9043136): the 1994 pqqD is now pqqA,
  the 1994 pqqG is now pqqB, and the second cluster pqqEF became pqqFG. The current pqqG
  (C5AQL7, M16B partner subunit) is unrelated to the 1994 pqqG.
- The PqqF/PqqG paper (PMID:31427437) is cached as abstract only; its PMC full text could not be
  retrieved, so subunit-level residue assignments were not checked.
