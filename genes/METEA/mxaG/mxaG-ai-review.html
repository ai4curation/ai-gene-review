mxaG, also called moxG, encodes cytochrome c_L, the periplasmic c-type cytochrome electron acceptor for the Ca2+-dependent methanol dehydrogenase MxaFI. The protein is exported to the periplasm, binds one covalent heme c, and transfers electrons from reduced MxaFI into the respiratory electron transport chain during methanol oxidation. MxaG is encoded in the mxaFJGIRSACKLDEHB methanol oxidation cluster with the MxaFI structural subunits and Ca-insertion/accessory proteins.
