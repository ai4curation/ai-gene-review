mxbD encodes the membrane sensor histidine kinase of the MxbDM two-component system. Springer et al. showed that mxbDM is required for expression of an mxaF transcriptional fusion and that MxcQE controls mxbD promoter activity, placing MxbDM in the methanol-oxidation regulatory cascade. The predicted HAMP and histidine-kinase domains support histidine autokinase activity at the membrane, but the native signal, direct MxbD-MxbM phosphotransfer, and any interaction with lanthanide-free XoxF remain unresolved.

description
Membrane sensor histidine kinase of the MxbDM two-component system. MxbD has HAMP and histidine-kinase domains and functions in a methanol-oxidation regulatory cascade in which MxcQE activates mxbDM expression and MxbDM is required for expression of the mxaF methanol dehydrogenase reporter. The native input and the biochemical MxbD-MxbM phosphotransfer step remain unresolved.
