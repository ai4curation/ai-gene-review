mxcQ encodes the membrane sensor histidine kinase component of the MxcQE two-component regulatory system. MxcQE is required for mxbDM expression in AM1 and acts upstream of the MxbDM system that regulates methanol oxidation genes. The protein has predicted transmembrane helices plus HAMP, HisKA_3 and HATPase domains, supporting membrane-associated sensor-kinase activity. Direct MxcQ autophosphorylation, phosphotransfer to MxcE, the native sensed ligand and the proposed apo-XoxF interaction have not been demonstrated biochemically.
