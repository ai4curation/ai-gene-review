Gene Description

TrxB (HP_0825) is the FAD-containing, NADPH-dependent thioredoxin reductase of Helicobacter pylori 26695. It regenerates reduced thioredoxins through a redox-active disulfide, thereby supplying electrons to protein-disulfide reduction and antioxidant systems. TrxB reduces both Trx1 and Trx2, but only Trx1 supports AhpC turnover in the tested native peroxiredoxin system. The enzyme strongly prefers NADPH over NADH in the characterized coupled assay.

Core Functions

FAD- and disulfide-mediated transfer of reducing equivalents from NADPH to thioredoxins. Both Trx1 and Trx2 are substrates; the native peroxide-defense route proceeds through Trx1 to AhpC. Detailed TrxR kinetics are native-protein measurements, unlike the separate AhpC kinetic assays using E. coli TrxR.
