Gene Description

AhpC (HP_1563) is a typical two-cysteine peroxiredoxin of Helicobacter pylori 26695. It reduces hydrogen peroxide and organic hydroperoxides through a cysteine-based catalytic cycle and is regenerated by thioredoxin Trx1 (HP_0824), with NADPH providing reducing equivalents through thioredoxin reductase TrxB (HP_0825). The soluble antioxidant protects cellular redox balance; its physiological reducing system differs from the dedicated NADH/AhpF system of Salmonella. Oxidized AhpC forms intersubunit disulfides, and its activity can be damaged by oxidation of methionine residues and restored by methionine sulfoxide reductase.

Core Functions

Reduction of hydrogen peroxide and diverse organic hydroperoxides through a typical two-cysteine peroxiredoxin cycle, regenerated by native Trx1. NADPH supplies electrons upstream through TrxB; AhpC itself is not an NADPH-binding flavoprotein. The native reconstruction is distinct from detailed substrate-kinetics assays that use E. coli TrxR.
