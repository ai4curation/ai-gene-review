Gene Description

TrxA (Trx1; HP_0824) is a small thioredoxin of Helicobacter pylori 26695 with a reversible active-site dithiol/disulfide pair. It accepts reducing equivalents from the NADPH-dependent thioredoxin reductase TrxB and reduces protein disulfides, including the oxidized peroxiredoxin AhpC. This relay supports peroxide detoxification and cellular thiol-redox balance. Trx1 and the second thioredoxin Trx2 differ in client specificity: Trx2 can be reduced by TrxB but does not replace Trx1 in the tested AhpC system.

Core Functions

Protein-disulfide reduction through reversible thiol exchange, including regeneration of AhpC using electrons supplied by NADPH-dependent TrxB. Insulin and DTNB assays establish broader reductase capacity; the native AhpC client is distinguished from Trx2, which fails to substitute under the tested conditions.
