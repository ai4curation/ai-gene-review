# its8 notes

## Evidence

- Its8 is the S. pombe GPI ethanolamine phosphate transferase 1 ortholog of S. cerevisiae Mcd4p and mammalian PIGN. The its8-1 mutant had reduced inositol labeling of proteins, supporting impaired GPI-anchor synthesis [PMID:11297516, "its8(+) encodes a homolog of the budding yeast MCD4"].
- PomBase and UniProt both assign the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane row to PMID:11297516. The cached copy is abstract-only and confirms the GPI-anchor synthesis defect but does not expose the localization experiment, so the review defers to the full-text PomBase curation for the ER-membrane annotation.

## Newer-paper search

- Targeted searches for `its8`, `SPBC839.08c`, and fission-yeast Mcd4/GPI-anchor papers did not find a newer direct experimental its8 publication beyond the 2001 characterization.
- Toulmay et al. 2022 and the 2026 A. oryzae preprint provide additional background that fungal Mcd4-family proteins act in ER GPI-anchor synthesis, but they do not directly assay S. pombe Its8.
