# leu1 (SPBC1A4.02c, UniProt P18869) notes

- NAMING TRAP: S. pombe leu1 = 3-isopropylmalate dehydrogenase = S. cerevisiae LEU2 / E. coli LeuB.
- [PMID:3063400 "A DNA fragment which carries the leu1 gene encoding beta-isopropylmalate dehydrogenase in Schizosaccharomyces pombe has been isolated by complementation of an E. coli leuB mutation."]; [PMID:3063400 "This 1.5 kb DNA fragment complements not only the S. pombe leu1 mutation, but also the S. cerevisiae leu2 mutation."] (abstract only).
- Cytosolic: [PMID:35325114 "Leu1 is localized in the cytosol (Kikuchi et al. 1988, Matsuyama et al. 2006)."]
- leu1-32 marker: [PMID:35325114 "Among the Leu biosynthetic pathway, a mutation of leu1+, leu1-32, is widely used, which causes Leu auxotrophy."] (G46E helix-capping residue per review figure legend).
- Cofactors: [PMID:35325114 "This catalytic reaction requires NAD+ and divalent cations (Mn2+ or Mg2+)"].
- GOA IGI for cytosol (PMID:3063400, with SGD LEU2) is unusual evidence for a location; accepted because location is independently supported (HDA, IBA).
- GO-CAM: leu1 cytosolic GO:0003862 with a duplicated causal edge to eca39 (leucine transamination).
- Decisions consistent with S. cerevisiae LEU2 review.
