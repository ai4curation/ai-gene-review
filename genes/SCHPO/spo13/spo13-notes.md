  specifically at the meiotic spindle pole body.

  disrupted.

  formation/remodeling rows. PMID:18367542 is abstract-only in the cache, but
  the abstract directly supports Spo13-Spo2 interaction, meiotic SPB
  localization, and the spo13 SPB-modification phenotype.

  the broad `spindle pole body` SubCell row to the directly supported `meiotic
