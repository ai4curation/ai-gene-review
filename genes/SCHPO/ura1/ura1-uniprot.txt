CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multifunctional protein that encodes the first 2 enzymatic
CC       activities of the de novo pyrimidine pathway: carbamoylphosphate
CC       synthetase (CPSase; EC 6.3.5.5) and aspartate transcarbamylase (ATCase;
CC       EC 2.1.3.2) (PubMed:8590465). The CPSase-function is accomplished in 2
CC       steps, by a glutamine-dependent amidotransferase activity (GATase) that
CC       binds and cleaves glutamine to produce ammonia, followed by an
CC       ammonium-dependent carbamoyl phosphate synthetase, which reacts with
CC       the ammonia, hydrogencarbonate and ATP to form carbamoyl phosphate. The
CC       endogenously produced carbamoyl phosphate is sequestered and channeled
CC       to the ATCase active site. ATCase then catalyzes the formation of
CC       carbamoyl-L-aspartate from L-aspartate and carbamoyl phosphate (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P07259,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8590465}.

CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: GATase (glutamine amidotransferase) and CPSase
CC       (carbamoyl phosphate synthase) form together the glutamine-dependent
CC       CPSase (GD-CPSase) (EC 6.3.5.5). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P07259}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: In S.pombe, this enzyme is synthesized by 2 pathway-
CC       specific (arginine and pyrimidine) genes under separate control. One is
CC       linked to the arginine pathway and is designated CPSase A (arg5-arg4),
CC       it is localized to mitochondria and repressed by arginine. A second
CC       one, CPSase P, is part of a multifunctional protein (ura1) encoding 3
CC       enzymatic activities of the pyrimidine pathway (GATase, CPSase, and
CC       ATCase); it is localized to the cytoplasm and feedback inhibited and
CC       repressed by pyrimidines. The carbamoyl phosphate synthesized by each
CC       synthase is channeled to its respective pathway, in contrast to
CC       Saccharomyces cerevisiae, in which the 2 synthases are localized to the
CC       cytoplasm and appear to contribute to the formation of a single
CC       cellular pool of carbamoyl phosphate. {ECO:0000305|Ref.3}.
