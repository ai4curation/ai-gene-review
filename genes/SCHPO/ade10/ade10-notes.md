# ade10 (SPCPB16A4.03c, UniProt O74928) notes

Naming: ade10 = bifunctional PurH (AICAR transformylase / IMP cyclohydrolase). S. cerevisiae has two paralogs ADE16/ADE17; S. pombe has only ade10. Accession O74928 (PUR9_SCHPO) fetched correctly.
