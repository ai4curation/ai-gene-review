| Identity and ambiguity | Q9Y709 / SPBC409.05 is the *Schizosaccharomyces pombe* Skp1-family protein, historically called Psh1p. It must not be confused with *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* Psh1, a distinct CENP-A/Cse4-directed E3 ligase. | Biochemical identification of Psh1p as the fission-yeast Skp1 homolog; association with canonical SCF components supports the supplied UniProt identity. | Primary molecular role | Skp1/Psh1p is a nonenzymatic core adaptor in SCF/CRL1 ubiquitin E3 ligases: it links substrate-selecting F-box proteins to the Pcu1/Cullin-1–Pip1/Rbx1 catalytic scaffold. Substrate specificity therefore comes mainly from the associated F-box receptor, not Skp1 itself. | Co-purification and co-immunoprecipitation with Pcu1, Pip1, Pop1 and Pop2; consistent with the SKP1-family/domain annotation.
