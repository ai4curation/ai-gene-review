Gene Description

Core structural adaptor and telomerase-regulating subunit of the Schizosaccharomyces pombe telomeric shelterin complex (Pot1-Tpz1-Poz1-Ccq1-Rap1-Taz1). Tpz1 contains an N-terminal oligonucleotide/oligosaccharide-binding (OB) fold homologous to vertebrate ACD/TPP1 that forms a stable heterodimer with the single-stranded G-overhang-binding protein Pot1, while distinct C-terminal motifs bind Ccq1 (residues 421-485) and Poz1 (residues 475-508) to complete the physical bridge to duplex-telomere-bound Taz1-Rap1. Tpz1 does not bind telomeric repeat DNA directly; instead, it protects chromosome 3' overhangs from degradation and end fusions by stabilizing Pot1 on telomeric ssDNA, recruits and activates the telomerase catalytic subunit Trt1 via a co-evolved OB-fold TEL-patch surface (including Lys75, Arg76, Ile77, Thr78, and Arg81) in coordination with Rad3/Tel1-phosphorylated Ccq1-Est1, and restricts over-elongation in cis through complete Taz1-Rap1-Poz1-Tpz1-Pot1 shelterin linkage and Lys242 SUMOylation-dependent recruitment of the Stn1-Ten1 complex.

1. Gene & Protein Overview

  • Organism & Identifiers: Schizosaccharomyces pombe (strain 972 / ATCC 24843) tpz1 (synonyms mug169, SPAC6F6.16c, SPAC6F6.18c; UniProtKB:O14246, sequence version 2, 508 aa; PANTHER:PTHR14487 / PANTHER:PTHR14487:SF3 ADRENOCORTICAL DYSPLASIA PROTEIN HOMOLOG).
  • Nomenclature Warning: Do NOT confuse S. pombe tpz1 (O14246, the shelterin TPP1/ACD ortholog in PTHR14487) with S. pombe tpp1 (P78875, trehalose-6-phosphate phosphatase) or O74458 (sfc4, TFIIIC subunit).

2. Structural & Mechanistic Architecture of Tpz1 (O14246)

Tpz1 is the central architectural hub at the single-stranded/double-stranded junction of the S. pombe shelterin complex (Taz1–Rap1–Poz1–Tpz1–Pot1 plus Ccq1) (PMID:18535244, PMID:24013504, PMID:25330395, PMID:29149597):

  1. N-terminal OB-fold domain (aa 1–154) and the fission-yeast TEL patch (aa 73–81, TEKRITSLR):
  2. Like vertebrate ACD/TPP1, Tpz1 carries an N-terminal OB-fold preceded by a conserved hydrophobic P8-W9-L10 sequence (MSNCLKHPWLENGLLNLIK...).

  3. Although the surface charge diverged from the vertebrate acidic DWEEKE TEL patch to co-evolve with fission yeast telomerase reverse transcriptase Trt1, the structurally homologous OB-fold loop (T73-E74-K75-R76-I77-T78-S79-L80-R81) mediates both direct Trt1 recruitment to telomeres (Ile77 and Arg81 — tpz1-R81E causes an ever-shorter-telomere EST phenotype and abolishes Trt1 telomere recruitment, rescued by a Trt1–Tpz1 fusion; PMID:27253066) and post-recruitment telomerase activation (Lys75, Arg76, Thr78 — tpz1-K75A impairs telomere elongation despite Trt1 accumulation at telomeres; PMID:25131669).

  4. Pot1-binding domain (aa 155–240, PDB:7CUI; key residue Ile200):

  5. Binds Pot1 (which directly recognizes the 3' G-rich single-stranded telomeric overhang). Separation-of-function mutation tpz1-I200R disrupts Tpz1–Pot1 binding and breaks the Taz1–Rap1–Poz1–Tpz1–Pot1 shelterin bridge, causing runaway telomerase-dependent telomere elongation (PMID:24013504).

  6. Central SUMOylation & Stn1/Ten1 (CST) recruitment region (aa 224–420; SUMO acceptor Lys242):

  7. Pli1-dependent SUMOylation of Tpz1 at Lys242 (tpz1-K242R / tpz1-Snm) peaks in late S phase and recruits the Stn1–Ten1 (CST) complex to telomeres to restrict telomerase action and promote lagging-strand synthesis (PMID:24925530).

  8. C-terminal Ccq1-binding (aa 422–470, PDB:7CUJ; key residue Leu449) and Poz1-binding (aa 476–508, PDB:5WE0/5XXE; key residues Ile501, Arg505) hooks:

  9. Ccq1 binding (Leu449): Required both to switch telomeres out of the telomerase-nonextendible state (PMID:24013504) and to couple Rad3(ATR)/Tel1(ATM)-phosphorylated Ccq1-Thr93–Est1 recruitment to Tpz1(TEL-patch)–Trt1 engagement in late S phase (PMID:22101932, PMID:25131669, PMID:25330395, PMID:27253066), while redundantly protecting telomeres alongside Poz1 (PMID:18535244, PMID:25330395).
  10. Poz1 binding (Ile501/Arg505): Triggers an allosteric disorder-to-order transition in Poz1 that promotes Rap1 binding (PMID:29149597, PMID:29160296), completing the Taz1–Rap1–Poz1–Tpz1–Pot1 shelterin bridge that defines the telomerase-nonextendible state (GO:0032211 / GO:1904357; PMID:24013504, PMID:25330395).
