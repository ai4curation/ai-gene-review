CC   -!- FUNCTION: Adrenodoxin reductase transfers electrons from NADPH to
CC       adrenodoxin, which is involved in heme A biosynthesis and in iron-
CC       sulfur cluster assembly. Involved in the electron transfer to heme A
CC       synthase etp1(cd), a heme protein that catalyzes the conversion of heme
CC       O to heme A. Required for the de novo synthesis of Fe-S clusters on
CC       iron sulfur cluster assembly protein isu1. Involved in electron
CC       delivery for Fe-S cluster synthesis. Essential for coenzyme Q
CC       biosynthesis. May be involved in the electron transfer required for the
CC       hydroxylation reaction performed by coq6. May play a role in cellular
CC       and mitochondrial iron homeostasis. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P48360}.
