# leu2 (SPAC9E9.03, UniProt O14289) notes

- NAMING TRAP: S. pombe leu2 = isopropylmalate isomerase = S. cerevisiae LEU1 (P07264). S. pombe leu1 = IPM dehydrogenase = S. cerevisiae LEU2.
- [PMID:35325114 "In S. pombe, this enzyme is predicted to be encoded by leu2+, which is regulated by the transcription factor Fil1 acting on the general amino acid control"]; [PMID:35325114 "A loss-of-function mutation of any of leu1+, leu2+, or leu3+ leads to Leu auxotrophy (Kohli et al. 1977)."]
- Single-chain fused LeuC+LeuD (TIGR00170 + TIGR00171) aconitase-family enzyme with [4Fe-4S] (UniProt by similarity) [UniProt:O14289].
- GOA: EXP leucine biosynthesis from Kohli 1977 (abstract only, genetic mapping) - accepted, deferring to curator. ISO from SGD LEU1 (S000002977) for MF and cytosol.
- InterPro-derived "3-isopropylmalate dehydratase complex" (GO:0009316) marked over-annotated (fused monomer), as for S. cerevisiae LEU1.
- GO-CAM: leu2 activity in cytosol, but its causal edge goes directly to eca39 (leucine transamination), skipping leu1 - looks like a modelling slip.
