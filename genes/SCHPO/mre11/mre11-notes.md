# mre11 evidence notes

Mre11 (Rad32) is the DNA-binding nuclease subunit of the nuclear Mre11-Rad50-Nbs1 complex. It helps recognize and tether DNA double-strand breaks, coordinates DNA-end processing with Ctp1, and supports homologous recombination, selected nonhomologous end-joining reactions, telomere maintenance, and DNA damage signaling. Its conserved nuclease domain has endonucleolytic and 3′-to-5′ exonucleolytic activities. During meiosis it processes Rec12-linked DNA ends for repair and can initiate a separate palindrome-associated breakage pathway.

The reviewed fission yeast literature establishes nuclear MRN repair but does not resolve mitochondrial targeting or mitochondrial repair by Rad32.
