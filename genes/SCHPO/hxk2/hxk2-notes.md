# hxk2 (S. pombe, SPAC4F8.07c, UniProt P50521) notes

## Identity
- UniProt P50521 (HXK2_SCHPO), 455 aa; PANTHER PTHR19443:SF30 "GLUCOKINASE-1-RELATED" [UniProt:P50521]. Naming trap: that PANTHER subfamily name does not mean hxk2 is a glucose-specific glucokinase; it phosphorylates fructose and mannose too.

## Function
- Kinetically conventional hexokinase [PMID:8549830 "hexokinase 2, a kinetically conventional hexokinase"]; Km glucose 0.16 mM [PMID:9790975 "A mutation in the similar position of S. pombe hexokinase 2 (Asn196/Ser) increased the Km for glucose from 0.16 mM to 0.56 mM."].
- Main glucose kinase: [PMID:8549830 "Disruption of hxk1+ had no effect on growth but disruption of hxk2+ doubled the generation time in glucose."]; the double disruptant does not grow on glucose or fructose [PMID:8549830].
- Phosphorylates D-glucose, D-fructose and D-mannose [UniProt:P50521].

## Location
- Cytosol (ORFeome screen PMID:16823372; GO-CAM). GO:0032473 IBA/IC rows unsupported for fungi (see hxk1 notes).

## Comparison with S. cerevisiae HXK2
- Same core MF GO:0004396, glycolysis, cytosol. The S. cerevisiae Hxk2 glucose-repression/nuclear role (GO:0046015) has no S. pombe counterpart in GOA; hxk1(S213N), but not wild-type hxk1, partially substitutes for Sc Hxk2 in invertase repression [PMID:9790975].
