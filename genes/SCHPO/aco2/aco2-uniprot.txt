CC       synthesis of mitochondrial genome-encoded proteins, including at least
CC       some of the essential transmembrane subunits of the mitochondrial
CC       respiratory chain. The mitoribosomes are attached to the mitochondrial
CC       inner membrane and translation products are cotranslationally
CC       integrated into the membrane. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P40858}.
