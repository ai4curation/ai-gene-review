Gene Description

Sid2 is the NDR/LATS-family serine/threonine protein kinase (EC 2.7.11.1) that serves as the most downstream effector of the Septation Initiation Network (SIN), a Spg1-GTPase-regulated kinase cascade that controls the execution of cytokinesis in fission yeast. Sid2 functions as an obligate complex with its regulatory subunit Mob1, which is required for both its spindle pole body (SPB) localization and its kinase activity. Sid2-Mob1 associates with spindle pole bodies and transiently with the cell-division site and actomyosin contractile ring during late mitosis, when its cytokinetic kinase activity peaks. Localization depends on cell-cycle and reporter context: interphase SPB residence was observed with Sid2-GFP, whereas untagged Sid2 was detected in the cytoplasm during G2. During late mitosis, Sid2 promotes contractile ring constriction and septum formation by phosphorylating multiple substrates: the Cdc14-family phosphatase Clp1/Flp1 (creating 14-3-3/Rad24 binding sites that retain Clp1 in the cytoplasm), the cytokinetic formin Cdc12 (inhibiting its multimerization), the anillin-like protein Mid1 (driving it from the membrane at constriction onset), the SIN scaffold Cdc11 (a positive-feedback loop establishing SIN asymmetry), and the MOR-pathway kinase Nak1 (coordinating cytokinesis with cell separation). Independently of the rest of the SIN, Sid2-Mob1 also acts in G2 to activate the NIMA-related kinase Fin1, modulating mitotic commitment through the Pom1/Cdr1/Cdr2-Wee1 axis. Sid2 also phosphorylates the kinesin-14 Klp2, inhibiting its Mal3-dependent microtubule association to promote anaphase spindle elongation and maintain nuclear positioning when cytokinesis is delayed. The SIN, including Sid2, is additionally required for forespore membrane and spore formation during meiosis.

sid2 (Q09898, SPAC24B11.11c) review notes

Sid2 is the NDR/LATS-family Ser/Thr protein kinase that is the most downstream
effector of the Septation Initiation Network (SIN) in S. pombe. With regulatory
subunit Mob1 it forms the Sid2-Mob1 complex, localizes to the SPB throughout the
cell cycle, and translocates transiently to the cell-division site/contractile
ring at the end of anaphase to trigger ring constriction and septum formation.

Core function & localization

  • Sid2 is a SPB-resident kinase that localizes transiently to the division site during ring
    constriction and septation; localization and activity depend on all other SIN genes
    (cdc7, cdc11, cdc14, sid1, spg1, sid4) PMID:10459013.
  • In vitro kinase assay established; Sid2 kinase activity peaks during medial ring constriction and
    septation PMID:10459013.
  • A kinase-dead version (K238R) was made, confirming ATP-binding/kinase function PMID:10459013.
  • Function: required for initiation of medial ring constriction and septation (UniProt FUNCTION,
    ECO:0000269|PubMed:10459013).

Mob1 interaction / Sid2-Mob1 complex

  • Mob1p can be precipitated with Sid2p PMID:10769201.
  • Mob1p associates with Sid2p; both required for initiation of cytokinesis; Mob1 required for Sid2
    localization PMID:10837231.
  • Mob1 required for Sid2 SPB localization AND kinase activity; N-terminal region of Sid2 binds Mob1;
    Thr578 phosphorylation important PMID:15060149.

Substrates / signaling outputs

  • Sid2 directly phosphorylates Clp1/Flp1 (Cdc14-family phosphatase), creating Rad24 (14-3-3) binding sites,
    retaining Clp1 in cytoplasm PMID:18951025; direct phosphorylation
    shown PMID:18951025.
  • Rad24-Clp1 interaction depends on Sid2 (cytokinesis checkpoint) PMID:16085489.
  • Sid2 phosphorylates Cdc11 (SIN scaffold) — positive feedback, SIN asymmetry PMID:22419817.
  • Sid2 phosphorylates the cytokinetic formin Cdc12 to inhibit multimerization PMID:24115772.
  • Sid2 phosphorylates Mid1 (anillin) to drive it from the cortex at constriction onset PMID:30853434.
  • Sid2 phosphorylates MOR components Nak1 and Sog2 (SIN-MOR crosstalk) PMID:23394829.
  • SIN inhibits MOR via Sid2 phosphorylation of Nak1 PMID:20805322.
  • SIN/Sid2 inhibition of MOR enforces temporal ordering / prevents premature cell separation PMID:24972934.
  • G2 role: Sid2-Mob1 activates Fin1 NIMA kinase to control mitotic commitment via Pom1/Wee1 PMID:22684255.

Node / contractile ring biology

  • SIN activity disperses type 1 nodes (Cdr2/Mid1) — Sid2 active at mitotic SPB and contractile ring
    PMID:25501814.
  • Sid2/Mob1 localize to intermediate layer of contractile ring (80-160 nm from membrane)
    PMID:28914606.
  • Blt1p physically interacts with Sid2p/Mob1p; recruits them to division site PMID:24790095.
  • Blt1p and Gef2p localize Sid2p/Mob1p to division site, promote timely constriction PMID:31041892.
  • Septum/furrow ingression begins in early anaphase; reduced SIN activity triggers medial recruitment of
    Sid2-Mob1 PMID:29813053.

Meiosis

  • SIN required for spore formation; SIN proteins localize to SPB in meiosis PMID:16787941.

PP2A-Pab1

  • Pab1 (PP2A B subunit) physically interacts with Sid2 (and Mob1, Sid1, Cdc11) by two-hybrid PMID:20876564. The GOA
    protein-binding IPI (WITH SPAC227.07c = pab1) derives from this.
