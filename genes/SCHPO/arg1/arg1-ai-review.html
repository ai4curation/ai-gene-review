Gene Description

arg1 (SPCC777.09c) encodes the mitochondrial acetylornithine aminotransferase (EC 2.6.1.11) of S. pombe, a pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent class-III aminotransferase orthologous to S. cerevisiae ARG8. In the mitochondrial matrix it transfers the amino group from L-glutamate to N-acetyl-L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde (physiological direction; the reaction is written in reverse in EC/Rhea), forming N2-acetyl-L-ornithine, the fourth step of the acetylated ornithine pathway. arg1 deletion or mutation causes arginine auxotrophy, and arg1 is required for mitochondrial ornithine synthesis, which also feeds ornithine to siderophore (ferrichrome) production under iron limitation. Note that S. pombe arg1 is not the ortholog of S. cerevisiae ARG1 (argininosuccinate synthase, which in S. pombe is arg12).

arg1 (SPCC777.09c, UniProt O74548) notes

Naming trap

  • S. pombe arg1 = acetylornithine aminotransferase (ortholog of S. cerevisiae ARG8). S. cerevisiae ARG1 (argininosuccinate synthase) = S. pombe arg12.

Identity / activity

  • [UniProt:O74548 "RecName: Full=Probable acetylornithine aminotransferase, mitochondrial;"], class-III PLP aminotransferase, PTHR11986:SF79; activity by similarity [UniProt:O74548 "Reaction=N(2)-acetyl-L-ornithine + 2-oxoglutarate = N-acetyl-L-"].
  • Matrix location by similarity (transit peptide predicted) [UniProt:O74548 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion matrix {ECO:0000250}."].

Genetics

  • Mercier & Labbe 2010: PMID:20435771; PMID:20435771. arg1 car1 car3 lose all ornithine; matters for ferrichrome under iron starvation.
  • Ma et al. 2007 marker screen: PMID:17622533.
  • Deletion screen PMID:32896087 (EXP).

Decisions

  • urea cycle EXP (PMID:20435771) MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED: aminotransferase is upstream of ornithine, not a urea-cycle reaction.
  • Did not add GO:0006592 (ornithine biosynthesis) as NEW; ARG8 carries it as TAS but PomBase did not annotate it.
  • Core: GO:0003992, GO:0006526, mitochondrial matrix (= ARG8 core minus the ornithine BP).
