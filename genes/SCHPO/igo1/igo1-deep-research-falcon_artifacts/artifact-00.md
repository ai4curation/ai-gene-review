| Molecular class | Igo1 is a non-enzymatic, phosphorylation-regulated Endosulfine phosphoprotein rather than a catalyst or transporter. Its primary molecular role is inhibition of PP2A-B55 after activating phosphorylation. | Literature identifies Igo1 as the Endosulfine component and describes phosphorylation-dependent PP2A-B55 inhibition (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000006). | **High** for its regulatory class and lack of a catalytic role. | Additional phosphorylation | Cdk1–Cyclin B can phosphorylate Igo1 at several sites, suggesting cell-cycle modulation in addition to nutrient-responsive Ser64 phosphorylation. | In vitro kinase assays and mass spectrometry identified Cdk1-consensus phosphorylation at Ser31, Ser89 and Ser102. An S31A/S89A/S102A/S118A mutant was not phosphorylated in vitro; Ser76 and Ser115 matched PKA consensus sites in vivo (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000014). | **Moderate–high**. Cdk1 phosphorylation is experimentally supported, but its physiological contribution is less firmly resolved than Ppk18–Ser64 signaling. |
| TORC1-to-TORC2 and Gad8 pathway | Igo1 is a phosphatase-inhibitory relay connecting nutrient-responsive TOR signaling. Nitrogen starvation lowers TORC1 activity, activates Greatwall–Igo1, inhibits PP2A-B55/Pab1 and permits TORC2-dependent Gad8-Ser546 phosphorylation. | PP2A/Pab1 associates with Gad8 and opposes Gad8-S546 phosphorylation. *igo1Δ* lowers both Gad8 abundance and S546 phosphorylation (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000009). | **High** for pathway placement. Igo1 does not phosphorylate Gad8 directly; its effect is mediated through PP2A-B55 inhibition. |
| Codon-biased translation and tRNA modification | During nitrogen-starvation entry into quiescence, Igo1 supports Elongator and other tRNA-modification systems, sustaining translation of transcripts enriched for AAA rather than AAG lysine codons. | In *igo1Δ*, polysome-to-monosome ratios were 78% of wild type in nitrogen-rich medium and 27% after four hours of starvation. Translation fell to 40% for *rap1* and 30% for *ctu1*; synonymous all-AAG alleles rescued protein production. The mutant had reduced mcm5s2U34 and a 20–25% reduction in t6A37 after quiescence induction (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009). | **High**, based on codon-recoding rescue and quantitative translation and tRNA-modification measurements. These are downstream effects, not evidence that Igo1 binds tRNA or ribosomes directly. |
| G1 arrest, quiescence and differentiation | Igo1 helps convert nitrogen deprivation into G1 extension or arrest, entry into quiescence and sexual differentiation by suppressing PP2A-B55 and promoting TORC2 signaling. | *igo1Δ* cells fail to execute normal starvation-induced cell-size reduction and G1 arrest and show reduced sporulation (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000006). | **High** for pathway-level physiological function. Differentiation and cell-cycle phenotypes are partly downstream of broad translational and TOR-network changes. |
| Telomere and subtelomere maintenance | Igo1 indirectly preserves telomere attachment and subtelomeric silencing during quiescence entry by maintaining production of Rap1, Lem2, Sgo2, Clr2 and Clr3. | After nitrogen starvation, *igo1Δ* caused telomere detachment, reduced Rap1 and Sgo2, and greater than tenfold overexpression of a group of subtelomeric genes. Repressing Pab1 restored Rap1 and telomere attachment (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000012). | **High** for the starvation phenotype and PP2A-B55 dependence. The effect is indirect through codon-sensitive translation rather than evidence that Igo1 is a structural telomere component. |
| Cellular localization | Igo1 acts intracellularly within nutrient-signaling and phosphatase networks, but the retrieved evidence does not support assignment to a specific organelle or compartment. | The studies report localization changes for downstream telomeric proteins and nuclear structures but no direct microscopy or fractionation result establishing Igo1 localization (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000011). | **Unresolved**. Igo1 should not be annotated as specifically nuclear, cytosolic, telomeric or membrane-associated without independent localization evidence. |
