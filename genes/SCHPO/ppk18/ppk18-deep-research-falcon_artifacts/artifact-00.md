| Identity and family | Q8TFG6, encoded by ppk18/SPAPB18E9.02c, is the *Schizosaccharomyces pombe* serine/threonine kinase Ppk18 and a fungal Greatwall/Rim15-family kinase. It functions with the related kinases Cek1 and Ppk31 in the Greatwall–Endosulfine module. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000006) | Organism-specific literature, orthology assignment, and pathway genetics | **High.** Identity and Greatwall-family assignment are well supported. Functional redundancy means Ppk18 alone should not always be equated with total Greatwall activity. |
| Enzymatic target and reaction | Ppk18 phosphorylates Endosulfine Igo1 at Ser64. Phospho-Igo1 inhibits PP2A-B55/Pab1, allowing PP2A-sensitive phosphorylation to accumulate. (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000004) | Igo1 phosphorylation assays, pathway genetics, and nutrient-dependent Igo1-Ser64 phosphorylation | **High.** Igo1 is the principal established substrate. PP2A-B55 inhibition is mediated by phospho-Igo1, not by demonstrated direct phosphorylation of Pab1 by Ppk18. |
| Upstream nutrient regulation | Nutrient-active TORC1, acting through Sck2, negatively regulates the Ppk18–Igo1 pathway. Nitrogen limitation or TORC1 inactivation releases this inhibition and activates the Greatwall switch. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000005) | Nutrient shifts, conditional TORC1 perturbation, and genetic pathway analysis | **Moderate–high.** The retrieved evidence does not establish direct TORC1 phosphorylation of Ppk18; some regulatory steps may be indirect. |
| G2/M transition and cell size | In nitrogen-poor medium, **ppk18Δ** cells fail to accelerate mitotic entry and reduce cell size normally, whereas moderate **ppk18+** overexpression promotes mitotic entry even in rich medium. The mechanism is consistent with PP2A-B55 inhibition permitting Cdc25 activation, Wee1 inhibition, and accumulation of CDK-substrate phosphorylation. (pqac-00000002) | Single-gene deletion, moderate overexpression, nutrient-shift phenotypes, and genetic comparison with PP2A perturbation | **High.** Ppk18 contributes to nutrient-responsive G2/M timing and size control, but exact effect sizes were unavailable in the retrieved excerpts. |
| TORC2–Gad8 and differentiation | After TORC1 inhibition, Ppk18–Igo1-dependent suppression of PP2A-B55 permits accumulation of TORC2-dependent Gad8-Ser546 phosphorylation. **ppk18Δ** or **igo1Δ** compromises nitrogen-starvation G1 arrest and sporulation, linking the module to sexual differentiation. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003) | Conditional TORC1 inactivation, phospho-Gad8 immunoblotting, deletion genetics, G1-arrest assays, and sporulation assays | **Moderate–high.** Igo1 dependence of Gad8-Ser546 accumulation is particularly well supported; some differentiation conclusions remain pathway-level rather than Ppk18-specific biochemical effects. |
| Quiescence and chronological lifespan | **ppk18Δ** shows reduced survival after prolonged nitrogen starvation, whereas **cek1Δ** or **ppk31Δ** alone can resemble wild type. Combined Greatwall deletions produce stronger, Igo1-like lifespan defects, demonstrating partial redundancy. (pqac-00000011) | Nitrogen-starvation chronological-lifespan and drop assays using single and combined kinase deletions | **Moderate–high.** Ppk18 is important but not solely responsible; phenotypes of **ppk18Δ cek1Δ** cannot be attributed to Ppk18 alone. |
| 2024 translation and tRNA mechanism | The Greatwall–Igo1–PP2A-B55 pathway supports TORC2/Gad8–Elongator signaling, U34 and A37 tRNA modifications, and efficient translation of AAA-lysine-enriched transcripts during entry into quiescence. (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000008) | tRNA-modification measurements, polysome/qPCR and codon-reporter assays, protein measurements, RNA-seq, and pathway genetics | **High for the pathway; limited for Ppk18 alone.** Central experiments primarily used **igo1Δ** or the combined **ppk18Δ cek1Δ** mutant rather than isolated **ppk18Δ**. |
| Quiescence-associated genome organization | After nitrogen starvation, **igo1Δ** and **ppk18Δ cek1Δ** show related transcriptomic defects. Igo1 loss causes reduced Rap1 protein, telomere detachment, and strong subtelomeric derepression. (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000012) | RNA-seq, super-resolution telomere imaging, immunoblotting, and PP2A-B55 suppression experiments | **High for the pathway and Igo1 phenotypes; low–moderate for Ppk18 alone.** The Greatwall comparison used a double mutant. |
| Cellular localization | No experimentally resolved subcellular localization for Ppk18 itself was identified in the retrieved literature. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000012) | Evidence-gap assessment; reported imaging localized telomeres and pathway effectors rather than Ppk18 | **Unknown.** Ppk18 is intracellular, but a specific nuclear, cytosolic, membrane, or organelle localization should not be assigned without direct evidence. |
