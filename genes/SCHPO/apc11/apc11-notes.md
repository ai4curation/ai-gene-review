# apc11 (S. pombe, Q9UT86 / SPAC343.03) - review notes

## Founding identification
- Yoon et al. 2002 used TAP + DALPC on the pombe APC/C and brought the subunit count to 13, deliberately capturing small subunits [PMID:12477395 "Our data increase the total number of identified APC subunits to 13 in both yeasts and indicate that previous approaches were biased against the identification of small subunits."]. Deep research records SPAC343.03 peptides in Lid1-TAP (3) and Apc13-TAP (5) purifications [file:SCHPO/apc11/apc11-deep-research-falcon.md "These data directly connect the exact fission-yeast ORF to the APC/C and support the supplied Q9UT86 annotation."]. UniProt SUBUNIT line cites this paper for the 13-subunit composition including apc11.
- Schwickart et al. 2004 independently purified Cut9-TAP and Apc13-TAP from pombe and recovered the same subunit set [PMID:15060174 "Mass spectrometric analysis of the Cut9-TAP purification identified 10 proteins known or predicted to be APC/C subunits ( 48 )."; "Accordingly, purification of a TAP-tagged version of Apc13 yielded the same set of APC/C subunits as Cut9-TAP (Fig. 2C ), demonstrating that apc13 + encodes a subunit of the fission yeast APC/C."]. Their introduction frames Apc11 correctly [PMID:15060174 "APC/C's RING finger is located within the small subunit Apc11, which by itself has the properties of a minimal ubiquitin ligase ( 13 , 22 )."; "Apc11 is anchored to the complex through Apc2, a subunit distantly related to cullins ( 50 , 54 )."].

## Molecular role (conserved RING-E3 mechanism; direct data are from orthologues)
- Budding-yeast Apc11 alone is the minimal ligase [PMID:10888670 "Here, we provide evidence that the Saccharomyces cerevisiae RING-H2 finger protein Apc11 defines the minimal ubiquitin ligase activity of the APC."], binds Ubc4 directly [PMID:10888670 "Using purified, recombinant proteins we showed that Apc11p interacted directly with the Ubc4 ubiquitin conjugating enzyme (E2)."], polyubiquitinates Clb2 [PMID:10888670 "His-Apc11p mediated the polyubiquitination of Clb2p in the presence of Ubc4p, but not Cdc34p (Figure 5 D, compare lanes 2 and 4)."], and the zinc-ligand cysteines are essential [PMID:10888670 "However, Apc11p bearing mutations in putative metal-binding cysteines (C41, C44, or C91) showed little or no activity in this assay (Figure 5 A, lanes 3, 4, and 9), demonstrating that the RING-H2 finger is absolutely required for its E3 activity."]. No thioester intermediate [PMID:10888670 "Our work, and that of others, indicates that RING-based E3s do not act as ubiquitin carriers that form thioester intermediates, but instead act as bridges between E2s and substrates that provide a favorable environment for the transfer of ubiquitin."].
- Human APC2-APC11 is the minimal module and lacks specificity [PMID:11739784 "In combination with Ubc4 or UbcH10, a heterodimeric complex of APC2 and APC11 is sufficient to catalyze the ubiquitination of human securin and cyclin B1."; "However, the minimal APC2/11 ubiquitin ligase module does not possess substrate specificity, because it also ubiquitinates the destruction box deletion mutants of securin and cyclin B1."]. Cullin binding and zinc [PMID:11739784 "Both APC11 and UbcH10 bind to the C-terminal cullin homology domain of APC2, whereas Ubc4 interacts with APC11 directly."; "Zn(2+)-binding and mutagenesis experiments indicate that APC11 binds Zn(2+) at a 1:3 M ratio."].
- Pombe sequence check (UniProt SQ): the Cys/His inventory of the 94-aa protein is H9, C24, C27, C34, C35, C38, C45, C52, H54, H57, H59, C60, C74, i.e. the RING-H2 ligand pattern within the UniProt ZN_FING 35..78 region is intact and the extra cysteines outside the canonical C3H2C3 set match the APC11-family pattern - consistent with the C3H2C3 RING-H2 plus the extra cysteines that form the third zinc site of APC11-family proteins. No residue-level mutagenesis exists for the pombe protein (deep research: "In budding yeast, mutation of zinc-coordinating Apc11 residues disrupted complementation, showing that RING integrity is essential for function").
- Pombe-specific complex biochemistry (via deep research, Yoon 2002 / Ohi 2007): [file:SCHPO/apc11/apc11-deep-research-falcon.md "APC/C ubiquitylated a Cdc2–Cdc13 substrate in an E1-, E2-, and ATP-dependent assay, while cellular APC/C activity generated ubiquitylated Cut2."]; one copy per particle, catalytic side with Apc2 and Apc10 [file:SCHPO/apc11/apc11-deep-research-falcon.md "Apc11, Apc2, and Apc10 occupy the catalytic side of the assembly, spatially separated from much of the TPR scaffold."].
- E2s in fission yeast: Ubc11 (UbcP4) and Ubc4 [file:SCHPO/apc11/apc11-deep-research-falcon.md "Ubc11 can generate short ubiquitin chains in the absence of Ubc4, whereas Ubc4 cannot ubiquitylate APC/C substrates without Ubc11, supporting a model in which Ubc11 participates in initial substrate modification and Ubc4 supports chain extension."]; Leverson 2000 already noted [PMID:10888670 "In fission yeast, the E2 UbcP4 is essential for the metaphase-to-anaphase transition and can rescue apc temperature-sensitive defects ( Osaka et al. , 1997 )."]. Reconstituted pombe APC/C assays use Uba1 + Ubc1/Ubc4/Ubc11 [PMID:26882497 methods]. Chain linkage for pombe not established (deep research: budding yeast K48, human K11).

## Biological outputs (pombe)
- Cut2/securin: nuclear, spindle-concentrated, destroyed at anaphase by the Cut9-containing APC/cyclosome; its proteolysis is essential for sister-chromatid separation [PMID:8632802 "Cut2 is located in the nucleus, where it is concentrated along the short metaphase spindle."; "The rapid degradation of Cut2 at anaphase requires its amino-terminal region and the activity of Cut9 (ref. 14), a component of the 20S cyclosome/anaphase-promoting complex (APC), which is necessary for cyclin destruction."; "demonstrating that the regulated proteolysis of Cut2 is essential for sister-chromatid separation."]. Review statement [PMID:10651900 "Securin, like mitotic cyclins, is the target of the anaphase promoting complex (APC)/cyclosome and is polyubiquitinated before destruction in a manner dependent upon the destruction sequence."].
- Cdc13/cyclin B destruction lowers Cdc2 activity for mitotic exit (deep research; Yoon 2002 assay of Cdc2-Cdc13 substrate). Timing of APC/C activity [PMID:16950791 "The APC/C becomes active at the metaphase/anaphase transition and remains active during G(1) phase."].
- Specificity comes from coactivators, not Apc11 [PMID:18556659 "The APC/C requires activators (Cdc20 or Cdh1) to efficiently recognize its substrates, which are specified by destruction (D box) and/or KEN box signals."]. Complex Portal variants: CPX-763 core, CPX-764 (Slp1), CPX-765 (Srw1/Ste9), CPX-766 (Mfr1).
- GO-CAM: PomBase model 67369e7600002505 (mitotic spindle assembly checkpoint signalling) has the APC/C complex (apc11 as a has_part) enabling GO:0061630, part_of GO:0051306 mitotic sister chromatid separation, directly negatively regulating downstream securin and Cdc13 activities (RO:0002630). The module `metaphase_anaphase_transition_and_mitotic_exit.yaml` lists pombe apc11 as a representative of the APC11 RING unit and is grounded in GO:0007091 and GO:0010458.
