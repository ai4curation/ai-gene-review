| Exact identity | Apc11 is the product of *S. pombe* ORF **SPAC343.03**, corresponding to UniProt **Q9UT86**, with a predicted mass of approximately **11 kDa**. (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000024) | Comparative-genomics assignment and *S. pombe* proteomics | **High.** The sequence-based assignment is supported by recovery from APC/C preparations. |
| Domain architecture | Apc11 is the small zinc-binding **RING-H2/RING-box** subunit of APC/C. Conserved metal-chelating cysteine and histidine residues form its E2-binding RING fold. (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017) | Sequence comparison and mutagenesis, principally in *S. cerevisiae*, with the *S. pombe* sequence included in alignments | **High conserved inference.** Direct zinc stoichiometry and RING mutagenesis have not been reported for Q9UT86 itself. |
| APC/C membership | *S. pombe* APC/C contains **13 core subunits**. Apc11/SPAC343.03 was recovered with **3 unique tryptic peptides** in Lid1-TAP and **5 peptides** in Apc13-TAP purifications. (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000024) | TAP purification and DALPC tandem mass spectrometry in *S. pombe* | **High.** This is direct, exact-organism evidence of complex membership, although not an isolated Apc11 activity assay. |
| Stoichiometry | Apc11 occurs at **one copy per APC/C particle**. Unlike Cut9, Cut23, and Nuc2, two differently tagged Apc11 variants did not co-immunoprecipitate. (pqac-00000021) | Double-tag co-immunoprecipitation and quantitative stoichiometry in *S. pombe* | **High** for the analyzed mitotic complex. Tagged strains grew normally, reducing concern that tagging disrupted assembly. |
| Structural role | Apc11 clusters with **Apc2 and Apc10** along the right side of APC/C, forming its catalytic region and remaining spatially distinct from much of the TPR scaffold. (pqac-00000020) | Antibody labeling, mutant analysis, and cryo-EM of *S. pombe* APC/C–Slp1 | **Moderate to high.** Placement is direct, but the **27 Å** map cannot resolve atomic contacts. |
| Complex dimensions and mass | Mitotic *S. pombe* APC/C–Slp1 is an asymmetric tricorn-shaped particle measuring approximately **19 × 17 × 15 nm**, with an **11.5 × 9.5 × 6.5 nm** cavity. STEM measured **890 ± 133 kDa**; stoichiometry predicted **986 kDa** with Slp1 and **933 kDa** without it. Cryo-EM resolution was **27 Å**. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000022) | STEM, quantitative immunochemistry, and single-particle cryo-EM in *S. pombe* | **High** for overall architecture and mass range. Measurements describe the complete APC/C rather than free Apc11. |
| Catalyzed reaction | Apc11 promotes transfer of ubiquitin from an E2–ubiquitin thioester to substrate lysines, forming isopeptide-linked ubiquitin conjugates. Apc2–Apc11 constitutes the conserved minimal ubiquitin-ligase module. (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000014, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000018) | Purified recombinant human and *S. cerevisiae* proteins; conserved inference for *S. pombe* | **High mechanistic conservation.** No purified-Q9UT86 kinetic assay was found; APC11 does not independently provide physiological substrate specificity. |
| E2 partners | *S. pombe* APC/C uses **Ubc4 and Ubc11** in complementary ubiquitination functions. Depletion of either causes mitotic arrest and cyclin accumulation; Ubc11 can make short chains without Ubc4, whereas Ubc4 cannot modify APC/C substrates without Ubc11. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000007, pqac-00000022) | Genetic depletion and APC/C-pathway assays in *S. pombe* | **Moderate to high** at pathway level. Direct physical binding of Q9UT86 to each fission-yeast E2 remains unshown. |
| Substrates and specificity | Physiological APC/C targets include **Cut2/securin** and **Cdc13/cyclin B**. **Slp1/Cdc20**, **Ste9/Cdh1**, and coreceptor **Apc10** recognize substrate degrons; Apc11 performs E2-dependent ubiquitin transfer rather than selecting substrates. (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000022, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000024) | Exact-species APC/C assays plus conserved structural and biochemical evidence | **High** for pathway substrates and division of labor. Direct Apc11–substrate recognition should not be inferred. |
| Cellular localization | Q9UT86 acts **intracellularly as part of APC/C** in the mitotic cell-cycle machinery. Its demonstrated structural position is within the catalytic side of isolated APC/C; no direct live-cell localization map for Apc11 itself was found. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022) | Purified *S. pombe* mitotic APC/C and structural localization | **Moderate** for cellular context. Specific enrichment at nuclei, spindle-pole bodies, kinetochores, or chromosomes should not be asserted without direct imaging. |
| 2024 structural update | Human APC/C cryo-EM structures reached **2.9 Å** for APC/C–CDH1–EMI1 and **3.2 Å** for apo-APC/C. A 2024 budding-yeast comparison found that apo-APC/C is already positioned for E2 binding, unlike human APC/C, revealing species-specific regulation despite conserved architecture. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004) | High-resolution human and *S. cerevisiae* cryo-EM | **Authoritative but cross-species.** These conformations cannot automatically be assigned to *S. pombe* Q9UT86. |
| 2024 *S. pombe* regulatory update | During spindle-checkpoint or environmental-stress responses, Pmk1 binds and phosphorylates **Slp1/Cdc20**, including Thr480, promoting Slp1 ubiquitylation and degradation and restraining APC/C activation. (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012) | Binding assays, mutagenesis, phosphoproteomics, phospho-specific antibodies, and cellular experiments in *S. pombe* | **High** for upstream APC/C regulation. This does not show that Pmk1 directly modifies Apc11. |
