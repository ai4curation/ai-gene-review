Gene Description

car1 (SPBP26C9.02c) encodes one of the two cytosolic, manganese-dependent arginases (EC 3.5.3.1) of fission yeast, an ortholog of S. cerevisiae CAR1. It hydrolyses L-arginine to L-ornithine and urea, the first step of arginine catabolism, which feeds ornithine into the ornithine aminotransferase (car2) route to proline and glutamate and also supplies ornithine for ferrichrome siderophore synthesis. car1 complements a budding-yeast arginase mutant and its expression is induced by arginine and repressed by iron (via the GATA repressor Fep1), whereas its 88%-identical paralog aru1 is constitutive and contributes more strongly under the studied conditions. Deleting both genes abolishes detectable cellular arginase activity and nearly eliminates conversion of arginine into other amino acids.

Core Functions

Cytosolic Mn-dependent arginase that hydrolyzes L-arginine to L-ornithine and urea. Its contribution is modulated by arginine and iron conditions; deletion of the paralog Aru1 induces compensatory car1 expression, so single-mutant activities cannot be added to infer wild-type partitioning.

car1 (SPBP26C9.02c, UniProt P37818) notes

Identity and naming

  • PomBase car1 = arginase (EC 3.5.3.1), ortholog of S. cerevisiae CAR1. Paralog aru1 (SPAC3H1.07, Q10066), which is called "car3" in PMID:20435771 PMID:20435771
  • Both in PANTHER PTHR43782:SF3.

Function

  • Cloned by complementation of S. cerevisiae car1 PMID:7985419; arginase synthesis induced at transcription level PMID:7985419.
  • Arginase activity in extracts depends on car1 + aru1 PMID:20435771; car1 transcription and Car1 activity repressed by iron (Fep1), linking arginase-derived ornithine to ferrichrome synthesis.
  • Arginine conversion (to proline, glutamate, glutamine, lysine) needs arginase: single car1Δ still converts; aru1 is the major arginase; double deletion abolishes PMID:20460254, PMID:20460254, confirmed for Arg-10 in PMID:26075619.
  • Mn2+ cofactor, homotrimer by similarity [UniProt:P37818].

Location

  • Cytosol and nucleus in ORFeome screen (HDA, PMID:16823372); PomBase GO-CAM 678073a900003902 cytosol.

Curation points

  • Urea cycle rows (IBA, IEA, and PomBase IGI from PMID:20435771): S. pombe has OTC (mitochondrial arg3) and the cytosolic ASS/ASL, but arginase acts in arginine catabolism (nitrogen use, ornithine supply), not a ureotelic cycle. MODIFY to GO:0006527, consistent with the yeast CAR1 review and the module note. PomBase GO-CAM also uses urea cycle — flag for curator.
  • No S. pombe evidence for the arginase–OTC "epiarginase" complex described for budding-yeast CAR1/ARG3; S. pombe OTC is mitochondrial, so this regulatory complex probably cannot form. Not asserted.
