## Literature

- Dlp1 is the second subunit of decaprenyl diphosphate synthase [PMID:14519123 "Here we
  show that decaprenyl diphosphate synthase from Schizosaccharomyces pombe is comprised of
  a novel protein named Dlp1 acting in partnership with Dps1."] with only weak similarity
  to Dps1 [PMID:14519123 "Dps1 is highly homologous to other prenyl diphosphate synthases
  but Dlp1 shares only weak homology with Dps1."]
- The deletion lacks activity and CoQ10 [PMID:14519123 "The deletion mutant of dlp1
  lacked the enzymatic activity of decaprenyl diphosphate synthase, did not produce
  ubiquinone-10 and had the typical ubiquinone-deficient S. pombe phenotypes"]
- Mitochondrial GFP localisation [PMID:24911838 "the fluorescent signal generated by the
  Dlp1-GFP fusion protein expressed in the Δdlp1 strain overlapped with that of
  mitochondria stained using MitoTracker Red FM dye"]
- Human PDSS2 alone does not rescue the deletion, but PDSS1 plus PDSS2 rescue the double
  deletion [PMID:24911838 "the S. pombe dps1-dlp1 double deletion strain expressing HsDPS1
  and HsDLP1 was able to produce CoQ10 (Figure 4), indicating that HsDPS1 and HsDLP1 can
  form a functional complex in S. pombe."]
