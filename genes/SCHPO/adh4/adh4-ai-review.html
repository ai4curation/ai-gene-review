Gene Description

Adh4 is an NAD-dependent alcohol dehydrogenase of Schizosaccharomyces pombe belonging to the iron-containing alcohol dehydrogenase family. Mitochondrial fractionation, activity assays and gene disruption place an ethanol-oxidizing activity in the mitochondrial matrix, where it supplies reducing equivalents for respiration. Adh4 also supports compensatory ethanol production in adh1-null cells, although the compartment of that reductive activity is unresolved. The family name does not establish its physiological metal: UniProt assigns zinc by similarity, and direct native metal identification remains lacking.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Should adh4 carry an ethanol catabolic process (GO:0006068) annotation for its mitochondrial ethanol oxidation, given that S. pombe cannot use ethanol as sole carbon source?

Q: Where does adh4 act during compensatory fermentation in adh1 null cells: is a cytosolic pool produced, or is acetaldehyde reduced in the matrix with NADH exchanged by a shuttle?

Q: Is adh4 a zinc- or an iron-activated enzyme in vivo, and is its induction by zinc deficiency a metal-sparing replacement for the zinc enzyme adh1?

adh4 (SPAC5H10.06c, UniProt Q09669) notes

Identity

  • Iron-activated group III ADH family [UniProt:Q09669 "Belongs to the iron-containing alcohol dehydrogenase"], ortholog of S. cerevisiae ADH4; PANTHER PTHR11496:SF102. UniProt gives Zn2+ cofactor by similarity only.
  • Transcript induced by zinc deficiency (UniProt, PMID:18203864, not cached).

Mitochondrial ethanol oxidation

  • PMID:16999687
  • Matrix location from detergent latency PMID:16999687.
  • PMID:16999687.
  • Also mitochondrial in the ORFeome YFP screen (HDA PMID:16823372).

Back-up fermentative ADH

  • Induced in adh1 null cells PMID:15040954; adh1 adh4 double null non-fermentative PMID:15040954.
  • Open issue: how a matrix enzyme supports cytosolic fermentation (shuttle? relocalisation?). Not resolved by cached abstracts.
  • adh4 is NOT in the PomBase fermentation GO-CAM (gomodel:678073a900000393); the module lists it as the iron-ADH variant.

Review decisions

  • Historical decision, superseded by the 2026-10-25 audit below: ARBA carboxylic acid catabolic process was removed. The current review correctly refines this true pathway-level claim to fermentation.
  • All experimental rows accepted. Considered NEW ethanol catabolic process (GO:0006068) for the mitochondrial oxidation, but left as a suggested question (PomBase curators did not add it; S. pombe cannot grow on ethanol).
  • Core functions differ from S. cerevisiae ADH4 review (which uses Ehrlich leucine catabolism): S. pombe-specific evidence is for mitochondrial ethanol oxidation and back-up fermentation.
