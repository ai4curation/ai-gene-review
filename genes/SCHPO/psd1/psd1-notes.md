# psd1 (SPBC16E9.18, UniProt O14333) notes

- Mitochondrial PSD1/PISD-family enzyme (PTHR10067:SF6), pyruvoyl proenzyme [UniProt:O14333].
- psd1/psd2 are Psd1-like, psd3 Psd2-like [PMID:19286980 "Two predicted S. pombe genes were identified, which we have designated psd1 (GenBank locus tag SPBC16E9.18) and psd2 (GenBank locus tag SPAC25B8.03), encoding proteins highly homologous in structure to S. cerevisiae Psd1"].
- Triple deletion needed for phenotype: "psd1-3Delta cells produce lower levels of phosphatidylethanolamine than wild-type cells, even in medium supplemented with ethanolamine" [PMID:19286980]. No single-enzyme activity assay exists; MF from IMP/IGI.
- Localization: "SpPsd1-mNG localized to mitochondria" and "we did not detect obvious ER targeting of SpPsd1" [PMID:34818062]; "SpPsd1 is required for respiratory growth, while SpPsd2 was dispensable" [PMID:34818062].
- Difference from S. cerevisiae PSD1 review: the S. cerevisiae review has a second core function (ER-localized Psd1 pool, lipid droplets); in S. pombe no ER pool of psd1 is detected, and the LD role is carried by psd2 instead [PMID:34818062 "SpPsd2 is functionally required for the formation of an appropriate number of LDs per cell"].
- Module note: psd2 (Q9UTB5) localizes to mitochondria, ER and LDs [PMID:34818062 "SpPsd2 localizes to three distinct organelles: mitochondria, the ER, and LDs"], which resolves the module's open question about psd2 compartment (GO-CAM says ER membrane; UniProt says mitochondrial).
