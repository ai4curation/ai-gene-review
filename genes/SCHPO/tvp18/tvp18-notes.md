# tvp18 curation notes

## 2026-10-17

- Seeded rows covered Golgi membrane, Golgi apparatus, generic membrane, vesicle-mediated transport, and a PomBase ND molecular-function placeholder. No molecular-function term was added because the curated evidence supports Golgi membrane localization and conserved family membership, not a direct activity.
- PubMed searches for `tvp18`, `SPBC32F12.12c`, `SPBC32F12`, and `O74375` found no newer direct fission-yeast paper under the tvp18/SPBC32F12.12c identifiers. The broader `tvp18` search retrieved two S. cerevisiae Golgi/endosome compartment papers, including the Tlg2-compartment proteomics paper that named Tvp18 [PMID:16107716 "We found six uncharacterized but evolutionarily conserved proteins and named them Svp26"] and the Tvp18/Tvp23/Tvp15/Yip-family interaction follow-up [PMID:17178117 "Immunoprecipitation of Tvp proteins indicated that Tvp23, Tvp18 and Tvp15 are in an interactive network with Yip1-family proteins, Yip4 and Yip5."], plus a 2021 Pichia pastoris secretory-pathway screen that knocked out TVP18 [PMID:33263222 "we identified three strains with irreversible perturbations in the stacking of the Golgi cisternae"]. None provide direct S. pombe tvp18 functional data that would justify a new process annotation.
- Web searches likewise found S. pombe database/product pages, the Matsuyama ORFeome resource page reporting YFP localization as Golgi, the S. cerevisiae TVP18 Golgi/endosome paper, and the Pichia pastoris TVP18 knockout Golgi-cisternae study, but no newer direct S. pombe tvp18 publication.
- The PAINT Golgi-membrane row should be interpreted as an IBD placed at fungal node PTN001779222, seeded by SGD TVP18, and inherited by S. pombe tvp18 through the PTHR13314:SF2 fungal TVP18/calcium-channel flower homolog placement.
- The PAINT vesicle-mediated transport row should be interpreted as an IBD placed at opisthokont node PTN001779219, seeded by Drosophila fwe and SGD TVP18. It is plausible at the broad process level, but I did not add narrower process terms because the available S. pombe evidence does not establish a more specific step performed by tvp18.
- PTHR13314 also places `GO:0005262` calcium channel activity on PTN001779219, the same opisthokont node as the accepted `GO:0016192` assertion. I did not propagate that MF to S. pombe because the `GO:0005262` row is seeded only by Drosophila `fwe`, the fungal PTN001779222 node carries only the Golgi-membrane CC, and the remaining calcium-related PTHR13314 terms sit at the Eumetazoa node PTN000999743.
