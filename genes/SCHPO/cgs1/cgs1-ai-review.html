Gene Description

cgs1 encodes the regulatory subunit of fission-yeast protein kinase A. Cgs1 binds cAMP and the catalytic subunit Pka1 to form the cAMP-dependent protein kinase holoenzyme, restraining Pka1 catalytic activity until cAMP binding releases the catalytic subunit. The protein acts in the cytoplasm and nucleus, and Cgs1-dependent control of Pka1 activity and localization connects glucose-responsive cAMP signaling with sexual differentiation, stationary phase, osmotic and calcium responses.

Core Functions

Cgs1 is the cAMP-binding regulatory subunit of fission-yeast PKA: it binds cAMP and Pka1, participates in the cAMP-dependent protein kinase holoenzyme, and inhibits Pka1 catalytic activity in the cytosol and nucleus until cAMP binding releases the catalytic subunit.
