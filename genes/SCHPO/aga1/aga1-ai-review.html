Gene Description

Aga1 is the mitochondrial ArgJ-family ornithine acetyltransferase of Schizosaccharomyces pombe. It transfers an acetyl group from N-acetylornithine to glutamate, releasing ornithine and recycling N-acetylglutamate in arginine biosynthesis. Family conservation supports maturation into alpha and beta chains and a secondary acetyl-CoA-dependent glutamate acetylation activity. The latter activity has direct modest-activity and overexpression-rescue evidence in budding-yeast Arg7; its quantitative contribution in S. pombe has not been established.
