# sec2 notes

## 2026-11-20 Sec2/Rabin3 fungal-family review

- Reviewed S. pombe `sec2` as the long, vegetative Sec2 paralog in
  `PTHR14430:SF0`; it is separate from the short sporulation-specific `spo13`
  branch.
- Accepted `PANTHER:PTN000374167` GEF activity and the InterPro GEF row. The
  PomBase GEF IPI from PMID:27630265 is supported by Sec2 association with
  GTP-bound Ypt3; the direct substrate is likely Ypt2, so the row is read as
  recruitment evidence inside the Ypt3-Sec2-Ypt2 Rab cascade, not as evidence
  that Sec2 exchanges Ypt3.
- Accepted the fungal `PANTHER:PTN001024876` cell-tip IBA. Fission yeast uses
  true cell tips as vegetative growth sites, so the PAINT cell-tip transfer is
  appropriate here even though the same node over-propagates when read against
  budding-yeast mitotic bud-tip/bud-neck localization.
- Kept meiotic SPB and prospore-membrane localizations as supported but
  secondary; the 2016 Ypt3/Ypt2 paper recruits Sec2 and the exocyst to the SPB
  during forespore membrane initiation.
- Flagged PMID:27630562 as a wrong identifier. It resolves to an unrelated
  human cerebral-blood-flow paper; the intended fission-yeast Sec2/SPB support
  is PMID:27630265.
