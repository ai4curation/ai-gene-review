| **Direct, strong** | Spa2 occupies bright cortical arcs at one or both cell tips during interphase, then relocates to the division site during cytokinesis and septation. It arrived **23.9 ± 3.4 min after spindle-pole-body separation** (*n* = 8), shortly before Pos1 (**24.7 ± 2.7 min**). | Endogenous Spa2–GFP/mCherry live-cell imaging, cell-cycle markers, time-lapse microscopy, and colocalization with Cdc15 and Imp2. (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000016) | Establishes cell-cycle-regulated localization at sites of polarized growth and division; most Spa2 surrounds the Cdc15 ring and lines the forming septum rather than constricting fully with the ring. |
| **Direct, strong** | Spa2 contains a Cdc15/Imp2-SH3-binding motif at residues **188–194**. Full-length Spa2 and residues 1–219 bound Cdc15-SH3; substituting **P191A** abolished the yeast two-hybrid interaction. | Yeast two-hybrid mapping, Cdc15-SH3 pull-downs, Spa2-TAP proteomics, and reciprocal copurification of Cdc15 and Imp2. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000012) | Supports direct integration of Spa2 into the Cdc15/Imp2 cytokinetic scaffold network. |
| **Direct, strong** | Spa2-P191A retained the correct spatial pattern but accumulated significantly less at cell tips and contractile rings, with the larger effect at tips; total cellular abundance was unchanged. | Wild-type and P191A GFP signals compared in the same fields using reciprocal lectin labeling; **three biological replicates**, *n* ≥ 200 for tip/ring measurements and *n* ≥ 30 per whole-cell condition; localization differences reached **p < 0.0001**. (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000006) | The SH3 interaction is a major localization determinant, but residual ring localization shows that additional, probably indirect, recruitment mechanisms exist. |
| **Direct, strong** | The sequence-orphan protein Pos1 (SPAC16E8.08) is a Spa2-associated protein. Pos1 and Spa2 reciprocally copurified, arrived at the division site nearly simultaneously, and Pos1 localization to tips and the division site required Spa2; in `spa2Δ`, Pos1–GFP remained only on mitochondria. | Spa2-TAP proteomics, reciprocal immunoprecipitation/coproteomics, endogenous fluorescence imaging, and `spa2Δ` dependency tests. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000013) | Provides the clearest evidence that Spa2 functions as a cortical scaffold: it recruits or retains Pos1 at morphogenetic sites. Pos1 is not required for normal Spa2 localization. |
| **Direct phenotype, contextual** | `spa2Δ` abolished agar-invasive pseudohyphal growth and suppressed invasion by `fic1Δ` and `cyk3Δ`; Spa2 overexpression depolarized the actin cytoskeleton and altered growth patterns. | Twenty-day invasion assays on 2% agar and Crn1–GFP imaging after Spa2 overexpression. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000013) | Spa2 is important when cells adopt sustained unipolar/invasive growth, consistent with a role in focusing morphogenesis rather than catalysis. |
| **Direct negative evidence** | Under routine conditions (25–36°C), `spa2Δ` cells were viable, morphologically normal, established bipolar growth normally, and showed normal F-actin organization; defects appeared in some cells at 19°C. | Deletion-mutant growth, morphology, mating, polarity, and F-actin assays across temperatures. (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000008) | Spa2 is nonessential and partly redundant during ordinary vegetative growth; broad claims that it is universally required for polarity would overstate the evidence. |
| **Candidate only** | Spa2 was among proteins whose phosphopeptides decreased more than twofold after acute inhibition of analog-sensitive Kin1, making it a **candidate Kin1 substrate**. | Four-replicate quantitative phosphoproteomic screen that identified >1,400 Kin1-dependent phosphopeptides. (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000015) | Direct Kin1 phosphorylation of Spa2, responsible sites, and functional consequences were **not validated**; direct biochemical validation in that study was limited to Pal1, Tea4, and Mod5. |
