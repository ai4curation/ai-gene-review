# gsa1 (SPAC3F10.04, UniProt P35669) notes

## Identity
- Glutathione synthetase, EC 6.3.2.3; synonym gsh2 [UniProt:P35669]. UniProt still names it "Glutathione synthetase large chain" reflecting the old heterotetramer model.

## Evidence
- Purified enzyme: heterotetramer A2B2 [PMID:8192897 "existed as a heterotetramer (A2B2)"].
- Later shown both subunits come from the single gsa1/GSH2 ORF by metalloprotease cleavage; the uncleaved homodimer is fully active [PMID:12734194 "Both forms are encoded by the GSH2 gene"; "A stable homodimer was obtained by site-directed mutagenesis to remove the protease cleavage site, and this showed normal activity"].
- Cloning by complementation of cadmium hypersensitivity [PMID:1958212].
- gsh2 disruption gives glutathione auxotrophy [PMID:9017391 "Disruption of the second enzyme in glutathione biosynthesis, glutathione synthetase gsh2+, also led to glutathione auxotrophy"].
- Arabidopsis GSH2 complements the S. pombe gsh2 mutant; GSH synthetase activity measured in wild type, absent in mutant [PMID:8914526].
- A gsa1 point mutant reduces phytochelatin more than GSH; authors proposed bifunctionality [PMID:10219997 "These data strongly suggest that the GSH2 gene encodes a bifunctional enzyme"]. Phytochelatin synthase (pcs2 in PomBase) was later identified as the PC-forming enzyme, so gsa1's role in PC synthesis is most parsimoniously supply of the GSH substrate (interpretation; not verified against a cached PCS paper).

## Location
- HTP YFP cytosol [PMID:16823372].
