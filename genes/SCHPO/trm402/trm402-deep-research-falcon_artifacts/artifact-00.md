|---|---|---|---|
| Exact identity | The target is *Schizosaccharomyces pombe* Trm4b, encoded by **SPAC23C4.17** and corresponding to UniProt **O13935**. It is distinct from Trm4a (**SPAC17D4.04**) and the Dnmt2-family enzyme Pmt1, which methylates tRNA C38. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000005) | Gene assignment, PCR-verified deletion, and comparative functional genetics | **High.** The primary study directly identifies and manipulates SPAC23C4.17 as *trm4b*. |
| Primary reaction | Trm4b transfers a methyl group from **S-adenosyl-L-methionine**, also called AdoMet or SAM, to cytosine C5 in tRNA, producing **5-methylcytosine**, or m⁵C. Recombinant assays detected incorporation from radiolabeled AdoMet. (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005) | Purified recombinant His₆-Trm4b and radiometric methyl-transfer assays | **High** for SAM-dependent tRNA methyl-transfer activity. The supplied UniProt record does not specify a complete EC sub-subclass. |
| Principal in-vivo specificity | Trm4b supplies m⁵C principally at **C49**, with **C50** also reported, in the tRNA **TΨC stem**. Trm4a instead supplies C48 and selected C34 methylation. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000006) | Genome-wide tRNA bisulfite sequencing of wild type, single knockouts, and double mutant | **High** for C49; **moderate-to-high** for C50 because less quantitative evidence was retrieved for that site. |
| Quantitative genetic assignment | In tRNAPro(CGG), C49 methylation was **83% in wild type** and **9% in trm4bΔ**, while C34 remained **94%** in the mutant. Conversely, *trm4aΔ* reduced C34 from **89% to 8%** while retaining **82%** C49 methylation. (pqac-00000003) | Targeted and transcriptome-scale RNA bisulfite sequencing | **High.** Reciprocal site losses strongly distinguish the two paralogs; residual signal may include incomplete bisulfite conversion or background. |
| Direct C49 substrate test | Recombinant Trm4b methylated mature tRNAPro(CGG). Replacing **C49 with A abolished activity**, whereas C34A retained or slightly increased activity. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000004) | Defined wild-type and C-to-A mutant tRNAs; 60-minute radiometric assays with three replicates | **High.** This directly identifies C49 as the required methyl-acceptor cytosine in the mature substrate tested. |
| Precursor and intron dependence | Trm4b acts on mature tRNA, so its principal C49 reaction is intron-independent. With intron-containing pre-tRNAPro(CGG), activity persisted after either C34A or C49A alone but disappeared in the double mutant, revealing broader in-vitro reactivity. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003) | Recombinant-enzyme assays with mature and intron-containing precursor tRNAs | **High** for intron-independent C49 activity. Precursor C34 activity in vitro does not establish Trm4b-dependent C34 methylation in vivo, which is assigned to Trm4a. |
| Cellular location | Trm4b was reported to be **nuclear-localized**, consistent with a role during nuclear tRNA maturation. (pqac-00000001) | Protein-localization observation reported in the primary study | **Moderate-to-high.** Quantitative compartmentalization was not retrieved, and transient residence elsewhere cannot be excluded. |
| Biological phenotype | Deleting *trm4b*, alone or with *trm4a*, produced no strong growth or tested drug-stress phenotype. The *trm4bΔ* strain did not reproduce the mild CaCl₂ resistance associated with *trm4aΔ*. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000006) | Knockout growth and stress-sensitivity assays plus complementation | **High** only for the tested conditions. The result does not exclude subtle effects on translation, tRNA stability, or fitness under untested conditions. |
| Status of 2023–2024 research | A 2023 authoritative review reiterates the S. pombe division of labor, with Trm4a acting at C34 and C48 and Trm4b at C49 and C50, as well as the weak or absent *trm4b* growth phenotype. No retrieved 2023–2024 primary study added Trm4b-specific mechanistic evidence. (pqac-00000006) | Recent-review assessment and targeted literature searches | **Moderate.** This describes the retrieved literature rather than proving that no additional publication exists; findings about human NSUN2 or other organisms should not be assigned directly to S. pombe Trm4b. |
