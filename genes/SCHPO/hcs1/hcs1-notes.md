# hcs1 review notes

## 2026-11-21 PTHR43323 alignment

- `hcs1` encodes the single Schizosaccharomyces pombe HMG-CoA synthase in
  `PANTHER:PTHR43323:SF2`; Katayama et al. cloned the gene and showed that hcs
  disruption is lethal unless mevalonate is supplied.
- The local PTHR43323 PAINT review treats `PTN000222418` as the inherited
  HMG-CoA synthase activity / acetyl-CoA metabolism node and `PTN000933803` as
  the fungal ergosterol-biosynthesis node.
- The `PTN000222418` HMG-CoA synthase activity IBA and `PTN000933803`
  ergosterol IBA are core for hcs1. The `PTN000222418` acetyl-CoA metabolic
  process row is valid but non-core because acetyl-CoA is a substrate-level
  view of the same reaction.
- A PubMed search for 2024-2026 Schizosaccharomyces pombe hcs1/HMG-CoA
  synthase papers found no newer direct experimental papers for hcs1.
