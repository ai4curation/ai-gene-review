CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the TOM (translocase of outer membrane) receptor
CC       complex responsible for the recognition and translocation of
CC       cytosolically synthesized mitochondrial preproteins. Tom6 is involved
CC       in assembly and stability of the TOM complex.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P33448}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms part of the TOM (translocase of outer membrane) complex
CC       that consists of 7 different transmembrane proteins Tom5, Tom6, Tom7,
CC       Tom20, Tom22, Tom40 and Tom70. The translocation pore through which
CC       precursor polypeptides must pass is formed by Tom40, a beta-barrel
CC       protein structurally related to mitochondrial porin. The other TOM
CC       proteins are associated with Tom40 by their single alpha-helical
CC       transmembrane domains. The overall structure of the TOM complex is a
CC       dimer, consisting of 2 parallel Tom40 beta-barrel channels, surrounded
CC       by small TOM subunits, and tethered by 2 Tom22 subunits in the center.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P33448}.
