# Manual deep research for SCHPO tom6

Automated deep research was unavailable in this worktree, so this file records a manual review of local UniProt, GOA and cached publication evidence for S. pombe tom6.

## Findings

- The S. pombe UniProt entry describes Tom6 as a single-pass mitochondrial outer membrane protein and, by orthology to S. cerevisiae Tom6, as a TOM complex subunit involved in TOM complex assembly and stability.
- The current S. pombe GOA contains only a mitochondrial outer membrane annotation for tom6; TOM complex membership is a conservative additional annotation that is directly described in the curated UniProt record and supported by S. cerevisiae experimental evidence.
- No S. pombe-specific Tom6 mechanistic paper was found. The best primary evidence comes from budding yeast, where Tom6 promotes stable Tom22 association with Tom40 during TOM core assembly.

## Literature

- PMID:9774667 placed Tom6 with Tom5 and Tom7 in the approximately 400 kDa budding-yeast general import pore complex and showed that Tom6 promotes stable association between Tom22 and Tom40.
