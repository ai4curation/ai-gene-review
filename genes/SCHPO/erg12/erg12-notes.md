# erg12 notes

UniProt Q09780 places fission-yeast erg12 in PTHR43290:SF2, `MEVALONATE KINASE`, and assigns the EC
2.7.1.36 reaction by orthology to *S. cerevisiae* ERG12. The PANTHER family is compact and the three
root `PTN000048555` IBAs - mevalonate kinase activity, cytosol, and mevalonate-pathway IPP biosynthesis
- are sound for this member.

The fungal `PTN000825284` ergosterol-biosynthesis assertion is also sound. Erg12 catalyzes the
mevalonate-to-mevalonate 5-phosphate reaction that supplies IPP and then farnesyl diphosphate for fungal
sterol synthesis; the fungal node is seeded by the experimentally characterized budding-yeast ERG12
ortholog.

Matsuyama et al. 2006 is the local fission-yeast evidence for the cytoplasm, cytosol, and nucleus rows:
the paper is a genome-wide ORFeome/YFP localization screen, so those locations are retained but only the
cytosolic pool is treated as part of the core mevalonate kinase function. The direct catalytic evidence is
orthologous rather than a fission-yeast enzyme assay in the cached literature.
