Gene Description

mst2 encodes a fission-yeast Sas3/KAT7-family MYST histone acetyltransferase and the catalytic subunit of the Mst2 histone acetyltransferase complex. Mst2 acts in the nucleus on chromatin, acetylates histone H3K14, contributes with Gcn5 to H3K14 acetylation that supports DNA damage checkpoint activation, limits telomeric silencing, maintains euchromatic state by antagonizing H3K9-methylated heterochromatin spreading, and counterbalances Epe1 and Clr3-dependent pathways that tune heterochromatin stability and epigenetic inheritance.

Core Functions

Mst2 is the catalytic H3K14 acetyltransferase subunit of the fission-yeast Mst2 complex, acting on nuclear chromatin to counter H3K9-methylated heterochromatin spreading and maintain euchromatic state.

Molecular Function:
