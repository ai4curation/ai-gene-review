# leu3 (SPBC3E7.16c, UniProt O59736) notes

- 2-isopropylmalate synthase (EC 2.3.3.13). Functional counterpart of S. cerevisiae LEU4/LEU9 (NOT of S. cerevisiae LEU3, which is the Zn-cluster transcription factor). [PMID:35325114 "The gene leu3+ encodes 2-isopropylmalate synthase, which uses acetyl-CoA to acetylate 3-methyl-2-oxobutanoate to produce (2S)-2-isopropylmalate"]; [PMID:35325114 "While in S. pombe, Leu3 is the only known 2-isopropylmalate synthase"].
- Genetics (full text): [PMID:20657649 "Of six deletion strains tested, all were unable to grow on medium that lacked leucine, implicating this gene as functioning in the leucine biosynthesis pathway of S. pombe."]; Phycomyces leuA partially rescues [PMID:20657649 "whereas transformation with the plasmid containing the P. blakesleeanus leuA gene partially rescues this phenotype"].
- Leucine feedback: [PMID:35325114 "Leu allosterically inhibits 2-isopropylmalate synthase"].
- Localisation: [PMID:35325114 "Leu3 is localized to the mitochondria (Matsuyama et al. 2006)."]; UniProt annotates no transit peptide; GO-CAM uses GO:0005739 (mitochondrion), GOA also has IC matrix.
- Implication: with a single mitochondrial IPMS and no known cytosolic form, alpha-IPM export by oac1 may be essential in S. pombe (differs from S. cerevisiae, where cytosolic short Leu4 bypasses Oac1 [PMID:18682385]). Untested.
- Decisions: all accepted; root catalytic activity -> MODIFY GO:0003852; acyltransferase parent kept non-core.
