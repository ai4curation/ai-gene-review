Gene Description

arg4 (SPBC215.08c) encodes the large (synthetase, CarB-type) subunit of the arginine-specific carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase (CPSase A) of S. pombe, the ortholog of S. cerevisiae CPA2. It binds hydrogencarbonate, two ATP and ammonia (free or channelled from glutamine hydrolysed by the small subunit arg5) and makes carbamoyl phosphate, the carbamoyl donor for ornithine carbamoyltransferase (arg3). In S. pombe CPSase A is mitochondrial and repressed by arginine, and its carbamoyl phosphate is kept separate from the cytosolic pyrimidine-pathway pool made by ura1; in S. cerevisiae the equivalent enzyme is cytosolic. arg4 mutants require arginine. Note the nomenclature trap: S. pombe arg4 is not the ortholog of S. cerevisiae ARG4 (argininosuccinate lyase, which in S. pombe is arg41/arg7).

Core Functions

Large (synthetase) subunit of the mitochondrial arginine-specific carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase (arg5-arg4, CPSase A): ATP-dependent synthesis of carbamoyl phosphate from ammonia channelled from arg5 and hydrogencarbonate, supplying matrix OTC (arg3). Same activity as S. cerevisiae CPA2 but located in mitochondria in S. pombe.

arg4 (SPBC215.08c, UniProt O94313) notes

Naming trap

  • S. pombe arg4 = CPSase A large subunit (= S. cerevisiae CPA2). S. cerevisiae ARG4 = argininosuccinate lyase (= S. pombe arg41/arg7).

Evidence

  • [UniProt:O94313 "linked to the arginine pathway and is designated CPSase A (arg5-arg4),"]; [UniProt:O94313 "it is localized to mitochondria and repressed by arginine."]; location from Urrestarazu et al. 1977 (PMID:200419, title only cached) and ORFeome HDA.
  • Classical mutant (Kohli et al. 1977, PMID:17248775) and deletion screen (PMID:32896087).
  • Not in the PomBase arginine GO-CAM (gomodel:6690711d00000916 omits CPSase).

Decisions

  • IBA cytoplasm MODIFY -> mitochondrial matrix (ancestral cytosolic location; pombe enzyme relocated), propagation_review added.
  • Core: MF GO:0004087, contributes_to GO:0004088, in complex GO:0005951, matrix (CPA2 core but mitochondrial).
