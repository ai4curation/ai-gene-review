Gene Description

Schizosaccharomyces pombe exg3 encodes a cytoplasmic glycoside hydrolase family 5 beta-1,6-glucanase. Exg3 lacks an obvious extracellular targeting signal, is detected mainly in the cytoplasm and excluded from the nucleus in a targeted GFP study, hydrolyzes the beta-1,6-glucan substrate pustulan, and can accumulate with other wall glucanases at the Myo52-marked mating fusion focus where redundant glucanases promote local cell-wall digestion during sexual cell fusion.
