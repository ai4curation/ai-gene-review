# gcs1 (SPAC22F3.10c, UniProt Q09768) notes

## Identity
- Glutamate--cysteine ligase (gamma-glutamylcysteine synthetase), EC 6.3.2.2 [UniProt:Q09768]. Naming trap: S. cerevisiae GCS1 is an unrelated ARF GTPase-activating protein; the budding-yeast ortholog is GSH1. UniProt mnemonic GSH1_SCHPO.
- Regulatory partner: gcs2 (SPCC737.06c, O94246), a GCLM-like protein (no GCLM ortholog in S. cerevisiae).

## Evidence
- Cloned by complementing a cadmium-hypersensitive enzyme-deficient mutant; sequence matched peptides of the purified enzyme [PMID:7608113 "was cloned by complementation of the cadmium hypersensitivity of a S. pombe mutant deficient in the enzyme"].
- Mutants lacking the enzyme cannot make cadystin (phytochelatin) [PMID:2894829 "Some of them lack activity of either gamma-glutamylcysteine synthetase (EC 6.3.2.2) or glutathione synthetase"].
- gcs1/apd1 disruption gives strict glutathione auxotrophy [PMID:9017391 "is a strict glutathione auxotroph"; "encode the first enzyme in glutathione biosynthesis, gamma-glutamylcysteine synthetase, gcs1+"].
- Independent cloning (MNNG resistance) [PMID:8619315]; abstract-only, curators' IMP for GO:0004357 and cadmium response deferred to.

## Location
- HTP YFP: cytosol and nucleus [PMID:16823372].
