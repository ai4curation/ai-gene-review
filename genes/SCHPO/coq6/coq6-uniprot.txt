CC   -!- FUNCTION: FAD-dependent monooxygenase required for two non-consecutive
CC       steps during ubiquinone biosynthesis. Required for the C5-ring
CC       hydroxylation during ubiquinone biosynthesis by catalyzing the
CC       hydroxylation of 4-hydroxy-3-(all-trans-decaprenyl)benzoic acid to 3,4-
CC       dihydroxy-5-(all-trans-decaprenyl)benzoic acid. Also acts downstream of
CC       COQ4, for the C1-hydroxylation during ubiquinone biosynthesis by
CC       catalyzing the hydroxylation of 2-methoxy-6-(all-trans-
CC       decaprenyl)phenol to 2-methoxy-6-(all-trans-decaprenyl)benzene-1,4-
CC       diol. The electrons required for the hydroxylation reaction are
CC       funneled indirectly to coq6 from NADPH via a ferredoxin/ferredoxin
CC       reductase system. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03193}.
