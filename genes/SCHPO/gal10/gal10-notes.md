# gal10 (SPBPB2B2.12c, UniProt Q9HDU3) notes

## Identity
- UniProt: "Bifunctional protein gal10"; N-terminal NAD(P)-dependent epimerase/dehydratase (GalE) section and C-terminal aldose epimerase (mutarotase) section [UniProt:Q9HDU3].
- Lies in the subtelomeric gal cluster (gal7-gal10-gal1) of chromosome II that is silenced in wild type [PMID:21075050 "gal7+, gal10+, and gal1+ are localized close to a chromosomal terminal repressed by gene silencing in S. pombe"].
- Paralog for the epimerase activity: uge1 (SPBC365.14c), a monofunctional UDP-glucose 4-epimerase [PMID:19942659 "Schizosaccharomyces species are currently the only known organisms with two types of genes encoding UDP-glucose/-galactose 4-epimerase, uge1(+) and gal10(+)"].

## Evidence
- Epimerase: uge1 is the major epimerase on glucose; gal10 is expressed and contributes to galactosylation in low glucose [PMID:19942659 "gal10(+) was efficiently expressed and involved in galactosylation of cell-surface proteins in low-glucose medium"].
- Galactose assimilation: overexpression of gal7, gal10 and gal1 allows growth on galactose [PMID:21075050 "these transformants grew on galactose medium"].
- Mutarotase: UniProt catalytic activity alpha-D-glucose = beta-D-glucose by PROSITE rule only (PRU10126) [UniProt:Q9HDU3]; not tested in S. pombe; ISO from S. cerevisiae GAL10.

## GO-CAM
- 6796b94c00002484 (Leloir, usually silenced): gal10 enables GO:0003978 (ISO from SGD GAL10), cytosol (IBA), part_of GO:0033499. The model omits the mutarotase activity (aldose 1-epimerase), which the S. cerevisiae GAL10 review treats as a second core function.
