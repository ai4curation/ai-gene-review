CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transaldolase involved in the non-oxidative phase in the
CC       pentose phosphate pathway. Catalyzes the reversible conversion of
CC       sedoheptulose-7-phosphate and D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate into
CC       erythrose-4-phosphate and beta-D-fructose 6-phosphate. Transaldolase is
CC       important for the balance of metabolites in the pentose-phosphate
CC       pathway. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P15019}.
