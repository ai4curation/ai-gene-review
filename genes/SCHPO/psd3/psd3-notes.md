# psd3 (SPAC31G5.15, UniProt O14111) notes

- PSD2-type subfamily (PTHR10067:SF17), 967 aa, C2 domain + C-terminal PSD domain [UniProt:O14111].
- [PMID:19286980 "A third predicted gene, which we have designated psd3 (GenBank locus tag SPAC31G5.15), was identified; it encodes a protein highly homologous in structure to S. cerevisiae Psd2"].
- C2 domain retains Ca2+-binding residues [PMID:19286980 "contain all four amino acid residues that constitute the conserved metal binding pocket of the archetypal Ca 2+ -binding C2 domain"].
- Only high-throughput localization: "Psd3 is localized to the cytosol and, in dividing cells, to the periphery of the site of septum formation" [PMID:19286980, citing PMID:16823372].
- Endosome/Golgi/vacuole locations are all homology-based (ISS/ISO from S. cerevisiae Psd2, HAMAP MF_03209). Core location set to endosome to match the PomBase GO-CAM (6796b94c00000009) and the S. cerevisiae PSD2 review, with the caveat that there is no S. pombe evidence.
