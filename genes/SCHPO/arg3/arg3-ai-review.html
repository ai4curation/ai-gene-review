Gene Description

arg3 (SPAC4G9.10) encodes ornithine carbamoyltransferase (OTCase, EC 2.1.3.3), the ortholog of S. cerevisiae ARG3. It transfers the carbamoyl group of carbamoyl phosphate to L-ornithine to form L-citrulline. Unlike the cytosolic budding-yeast enzyme, S. pombe arg3 carries a mitochondrial targeting sequence and acts in the mitochondrial matrix together with the arginine-specific carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase (arg5/arg4), so fission yeast exports citrulline rather than ornithine from mitochondria. arg3 deletion causes arginine auxotrophy. arg3 levels and OTCase activity depend on the mitochondrial thioredoxin Trx2, which associates with arg3 and supports its function independently of its active-site cysteines; arg3 overexpression suppresses the arginine requirement of trx2 mutants.

Core Functions

Mitochondrial ornithine carbamoyltransferase: carbamoylation of L-ornithine to L-citrulline in the matrix, using carbamoyl phosphate from the matrix arginine-specific CPSase. Same activity as S. cerevisiae ARG3, but S. pombe-specific evidence places it in mitochondria rather than the cytosol.

arg3 (SPAC4G9.10, UniProt P31317) notes

Key S. pombe-specific difference

  • OTC is mitochondrial in S. pombe (cytosolic in S. cerevisiae): PMID:1313366; Arg3-GFP mitochondrial PMID:32896087. So pombe exports citrulline (GO-CAM comment; transporter SPBC365.16).

Evidence

  • Deletion auxotrophy PMID:32896087; library strain was mis-annotated.
  • Trx2 dependence (Song et al. 2008, abstract only): PMID:18849471; PMID:18849471. No protein-binding GO row present; not proposed.

Decisions

  • Core differs from S. cerevisiae ARG3 in location only (mitochondrial matrix, not cytosol); BPs GO:0006526 + GO:0019240.
  • HDA cytoplasm UNDECIDED (conflicts with fractionation/GFP; image not assessable). ARBA cytoplasm MODIFY -> matrix.
  • urea cycle IC MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED (consistent across arg3/arg12/arg41 and cerevisiae ARG1).
