CC   -!- FUNCTION: Core component of multiple cullin-RING-based SCF (SKP1-CUL1-
CC       F-box protein) E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase complexes, which mediate the
CC       ubiquitination of target proteins. The functional specificity of the
CC       SCF complex depends on the F-box protein as substrate recognition
CC       component. SCF(pop1-pop2) is required for the maintenance of ploidy and
CC       directs ubiquitination of cig2. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9990507}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of multiple Cul1-RING E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase
CC       complexes commonly known as SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box) complexes, consisting
CC       of cul1, skp1, pip1 and a variable F-box domain-containing protein as
CC       substrate-specific subunit (By similarity). Binds to the pop1
CC       homodimer, the pop2 homodimer and the pop1/pop2 heterodimer forming the
CC       SCF(pop1-pop2) complex. Interacts with pof3, pof14 and skp1.
CC       {ECO:0000250, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11809834, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15147268,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17016471, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9990507}.
