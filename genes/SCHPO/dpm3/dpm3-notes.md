# dpm3 (S. pombe, SPBC1677.02, UniProt O94633) notes

## Identity
- UniProt O94633 (DPM3_SCHPO), 90 aa, DPM3 family (PANTHER PTHR16433) [UniProt:O94633 "Stabilizer subunit of the dolichol-phosphate-mannose synthase"].

## Function
- Direct S. pombe evidence (heterologous): S. pombe dpm3 rescues DPM synthesis in DPM2-null CHO Lec15 cells [PMID:10835346 "S.pombe DPM3 restored biosynthesis of DPM in Lec15 cells, indicating its orthologous relationship to human DPM3."].
- In mammals DPM3 tethers DPM1 to the ER membrane through its C-terminal coiled coil [PMID:16280320 "A coiled-coil domain near the C terminus of DPM3 was important for tethering DPM1, the catalytic subunit of the enzyme, to the endoplasmic reticulum membrane"] and is essential for activity [PMID:16280320 "indicating that DPM3 is an essential component of this enzyme"].

## Location
