# Manual deep research for SCHPO tom5

Automated deep research was unavailable in this worktree, so this file records a manual review of local UniProt, GOA, PANTHER and cached publication evidence for S. pombe tom5.

## Findings

- The S. pombe UniProt entry describes Tom5 as a single-pass mitochondrial outer membrane protein in the Tom5 family and as a component of the TOM translocase complex responsible for recognition and translocation of cytosolically synthesized mitochondrial preproteins.
- PANTHER PTHR28188 currently contains the S. pombe and S. cerevisiae Tom5 proteins as reviewed entries in a single subfamily, supporting curator transfer from the budding-yeast TOM5 literature to S. pombe tom5.
- No S. pombe-specific mechanistic paper was found for Tom5. The best primary evidence for the small Tom5 subunit role comes from budding yeast, where Tom5 links mitochondrial import receptors to the general import pore.

## Literature

- PMID:9217162 identified S. cerevisiae Tom5 as a single-pass small TOM protein that accepts precursors from TOM receptors and mediates their insertion into the general import pore.
- PMID:9774667 placed Tom5 with Tom6 and Tom7 in the approximately 400 kDa budding-yeast general import pore complex containing Tom22 and Tom40.
- PMID:11259583 showed that stringent detergent releases the small Tom proteins from the GIP complex while Tom40 and Tom22 remain the stable core that holds accumulated preproteins.
