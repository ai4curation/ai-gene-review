# tam41 (SPBC1A4.06c, UniProt O74339) notes

- Mitochondrial CDS, Tam41 family (PANTHER PTHR13619:SF0), unrelated to bbl1 [UniProt:O74339].
- S. pombe Tam41 crystal structures and kinetics: "Here we show that Tam41 adopts sequential catalytic mechanism" [PMID:31178220]; UniProt records KM 0.26 mM CTP, 0.094 mM PA from this work [UniProt:O74339].
- Membrane association: "The C-terminal region of SpTam41 contains a positively charged amphipathic helix crucial for membrane association and participates in binding phospholipids." [PMID:31178220] (abstract-only cache).
- S. cerevisiae: "Tam41 directly catalyzes the conversion of PA to CDP-DAG in the presence of CTP" and "Tam41 is a peripheral IM protein facing the matrix" [PMID:23623749].
- Consistent with yeast TAM41 review (MF GO:0004605; BP CDP-DAG + cardiolipin biosynthesis; CC inner membrane + matrix).
