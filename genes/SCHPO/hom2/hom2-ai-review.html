Gene Description

Aspartate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase (EC 1.2.1.11) of Schizosaccharomyces pombe (SPCC1827.06c). It catalyses the NADPH-dependent reductive dephosphorylation of 4-phospho-L-aspartate to L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde, the second step of homoserine synthesis and so of threonine, isoleucine and methionine biosynthesis. It is the ortholog of S. cerevisiae Hom2. GFP-tagged hom2 is cytosolic with some nuclear signal. In fungi lysine is made by the alpha-aminoadipate pathway, so unlike its bacterial and plant counterparts hom2 does not feed lysine synthesis.
