Gene Description

mst1 encodes the fission-yeast ESA1/TIP60-family MYST histone acetyltransferase and catalytic subunit of NuA4-family chromatin complexes. Mst1 acts in the nucleus on chromatin, acetylates H2A.Z/Pht1 and histone H3K4 in addition to its conserved histone H4 activity, contributes to RNA polymerase II transcriptional regulation and pericentric heterochromatin assembly, and is recruited to double-strand breaks to promote resection and homologous-recombination repair.
