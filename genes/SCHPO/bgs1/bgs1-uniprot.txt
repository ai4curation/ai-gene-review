CC       for the assembly of the division septum and maintenance of cell
CC       polarity (PubMed:10545452). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P38631,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10545452}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the 1,3-beta-glucan synthase (GS) complex,
CC       composed of at least the alternate catalytic subunits bgs1, bgs2, bgs3,
CC       and bgs4, and a regulatory subunit chr4 (By similarity). Interacts with

CC       (PubMed:19237545). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P38631,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19237545}.
