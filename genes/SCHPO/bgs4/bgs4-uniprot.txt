CC   -!- FUNCTION: Alternate catalytic subunit of the 1,3-beta-glucan synthase
CC       (GS) complex. Synthesizes 1,3-beta-glucan, a major structural component
CC       of the yeast cell wall (By similarity). Required for maintaining cell
CC       integrity during cytokinesis and polarized growth (PubMed:15615781).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P38631, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15615781}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the 1,3-beta-glucan synthase (GS) complex,
CC       composed of at least the alternate catalytic subunits bgs1, bgs2, bgs3,
CC       and bgs4, and a regulatory subunit chr4.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P38631}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15615781};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255}. Cell septum
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15615781}. Spore wall
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15615781}. Note=Localizes to regions of active cell
CC       wall synthesis in an F-actin dependent manner. Following nuclear
CC       division and formation of the contractile ring, relocalizes from the
CC       cell ends to the assembling division septum. During sexual development,
CC       localizes to the mating projection, the contact region at the cell-cell
CC       fusion site, and to the forming spore walls.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15615781}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Cells lyse at the division site and at sites of
CC       polarized growth. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15615781}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: May synthesize a form of 1,3-beta-glucan not stained by
CC       Calcofluor White. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15615781}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the glycosyltransferase 48 family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
