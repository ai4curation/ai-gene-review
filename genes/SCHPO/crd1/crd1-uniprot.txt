CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Cardiolipin synthase (CMP-forming)]: Catalyzes the synthesis
CC       of cardiolipin (CL) (diphosphatidylglycerol) by specifically
CC       transferring a phosphatidyl group from CDP-diacylglycerol to
CC       phosphatidylglycerol (PG) (PubMed:29958934). CL is a key phospholipid
CC       in mitochondrial membranes and plays important roles in maintaining the
CC       functional integrity and dynamics of mitochondria under both optimal
CC       and stress conditions (PubMed:29958934). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29958934}.
