# met6 notes (SPBC56F2.11, O60062)

- Homoserine O-acetyltransferase; acetylates L-homoserine from acetyl-CoA [PMID:16216079 "The enzyme from the yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe activates the hydroxyl group of L-homoserine by acetylation from acetyl coenzyme A."].
- Ping-pong, acyl-enzyme intermediate on Ser163 (triad Ser163/Asp403/His432) [PMID:16216079 "Homoserine transacetylase therefore catalyzes the acetylation of L-homoserine via a covalent acyl-enzyme intermediate through an active-site Ser."].
- In vitro also turns over propionyl- and butyryl-CoA (UniProt kinetics from PMID:16216079: Km 48.4 uM propionyl-CoA, 44.1 uM butyryl-CoA; kcat 16.1 and 47.2 s-1 vs 9.34 s-1 for acetyl-CoA). Not annotated: no evidence these donors are physiological. Succinyl-CoA not reported.
- met6 cloned by complementation of met6-31 methionine auxotroph [PMID:9829945 "We isolated and sequenced the met6 gene and demonstrated that it codes for homoserine acyltransferase, the enzyme catalyzing the first reaction in the methionine-specific biosynthetic pathway."].
- Paralog cys2 (SPBC106.17c) is a second MET2 homologue whose mutant needs cysteine [PMID:17622533 "The cys2 mutation required cysteine rather than methionine."]; PAINT node PTN000107323 gives the fungal divergent subgroup L-cysteine biosynthesis (serine acyltransferase), consistent with PMID:28581482's fungal O-succinyl-L-serine transferase subgroup.
- Cytoplasm/cytosol from ORFeome YFP screen (PMID:16823372).
- PANTHER PTHR32268:SF11.
