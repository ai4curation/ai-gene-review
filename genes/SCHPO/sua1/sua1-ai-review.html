Gene Description

sua1 (SPBC27.08c; also called asp1/met3) encodes the ATP sulfurylase (sulfate adenylyltransferase, EC 2.7.7.4) of fission yeast, the ortholog of Saccharomyces cerevisiae MET3. It catalyses the first intracellular step of sulfate assimilation, joining inorganic sulfate to the adenylyl moiety of ATP to form adenosine 5'-phosphosulfate (APS) and pyrophosphate. APS is then phosphorylated by met14 to PAPS and reduced by the met16/thioredoxin and sir1/met10 enzymes to sulfide for cysteine and methionine synthesis. The protein is a cytosolic member of the eukaryotic homo-oligomeric Sat/Met3-type family, with a central catalytic domain and a C-terminal kinase-like domain inferred to support oligomerization by comparison with budding-yeast Met3. Native sua1 oligomer stoichiometry is not established by the complementation experiments. sua1 mutants, isolated as selenate-resistant strains, have low ATP sulfurylase activity and cannot use sulfate as a sulfur source; the cloned gene complements both the pombe mutants and an S. cerevisiae met3 mutant.

Core Functions

ATP sulfurylase: activation of inorganic sulfate with ATP to adenosine 5'-phosphosulfate and pyrophosphate (EC 2.7.7.4), the first step of cytosolic sulfate assimilation, upstream of met14 APS kinase. Domain resemblance does not confer the separate APS-kinase reaction performed by met14.

sua1 (SPBC27.08c, UniProt P78937) notes

Naming: PomBase sua1 (synonyms asp1, met3) is the ortholog of S. cerevisiae MET3
(ATP sulfurylase). UniProt entry name MET3_SCHPO. Do not confuse with pombe numbering of
other met genes.

Evidence journal

  • Activity: ATP sulfurylase, sulfate + ATP -> APS + PPi (EC 2.7.7.4, RHEA:18133)
    [UniProt:P78937 "FUNCTION: Catalyzes the first intracellular reaction of sulfate"]
    (HAMAP rule MF_03106; homohexamer, dimer of trimers by rule).
  • Genetics: selenate-resistant mutants of one complementation group are low in sulfate
    uptake and ATP sulfurylase activity and cannot use sulfate
    PMID:14723223.
  • Cloning by complementation restored sulfate assimilation and ATP sulfurylase levels
    PMID:18388974; sua1 also complements
    S. cerevisiae met3 PMID:18388974.
  • Localization: cytosol in the ORFeome YFP screen (PMID:16823372, HDA), cytoplasm by HAMAP.
  • PomBase GO-CAM gomodel:66a3e0bb00001342 activity 66a3e0bb00001368: sua1 enables
    GO:0004781, occurs in cytosol, part_of GO:0000103 sulfate assimilation.
  • Module: aps_dependent_assimilatory_sulfate_reduction, step 1 (homo-oligomeric Sat/Met3
    variant). S. cerevisiae ortholog MET3 core function = GO:0004781 in cytoplasm, part of
    sulfate assimilation; this review is consistent with it.

Observations

  • GO:0070814 hydrogen sulfide biosynthetic process (UniPathway UPA00140) is an upstream
    framing; kept non-core, as for MET3.
  • The GOA IMP rows for GO:0004781 rest on mutant enzyme-activity measurements
    (PMID:14723223) and complementation (PMID:18388974); no purified-enzyme kinetics for the
    pombe protein are known to me.
