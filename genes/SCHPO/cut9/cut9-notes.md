# cut9 (Cdc16/Apc6, UniProt P41889) — curation notes

Working journal for the GO annotation review of Schizosaccharomyces pombe cut9. Citations are given
inline as [PMID:NNN "verbatim text"] and refer to the cached `publications/PMID_NNN.md` files. Cached
full text is available for PMID:20924356 (Cut9-Hcn1 crystal structure), PMID:19117951 (APC/C and
heterochromatin) and PMID:15060174 (Swm1/Apc13); PMID:7798319, 8632802, 9264466, 12477395, 12615927 and
16823372 are abstract-only. The deep research file `cut9-deep-research-falcon.md` was present and was
used; its own citations (Berry 1999, Ohi 2007, Yamashita 1999, Bai 2022, Vazquez-Fernandez 2024) are not
in the publications cache and are only used as background.

## 1. What Cut9 is

- Isolated by Samejima and Yanagida as a cut (cell untimely torn) mutant: [PMID:7798319 "The cut9+ gene
  encodes a 78-kD protein containing the 10 34-amino acid repeats, tetratricopeptide repeats (TPR), and
  similar to budding yeast Cdc16."]; [PMID:7798319 "It is essential for viability, and the mutation sites
  reside in the TPR."]. UniProt records the two ts alleles as G412D (cut9-T98) and A535T (cut9-665),
  both in the TPR region (`cut9-uniprot.txt` MUTAGEN lines).
- The crystal structure shows the protein is essentially all TPR: [PMID:20924356 "Here, we report the
  crystal structure of Schizosaccharomyces pombe Cut9 (Cdc16/Apc6) in complex with Hcn1 (Cdc26), showing
  that Cdc16/Cut9 is a contiguous TPR superhelix of 14 TPR units."]; [PMID:20924356 "A C-terminal block
  of TPR motifs interacts with Hcn1, whereas an N-terminal TPR block mediates Cdc16/Cut9 self-association
  through a homotypic interface."]. UniProt annotates 14 TPRs (83-579) with the same domain split.
- Founding biochemistry: [PMID:9264466 "We show here that the fission yeast gene products Cut9 and Nuc2
  are the subunits of the 20S complex, the putative APC (anaphase promoting complex)/cyclosome which
  contains ubiquitin ligase activity required for cyclin and Cut2 destruction."]; [PMID:9264466 "The
  assembly of Cut9 into the 20S complex requires functional Nuc2, and vice versa."]; [PMID:9264466 "The
  20S assembly impaired in the cut9 mutant can be restored by elevating the level of a novel gene product
  Hcnl, similar to budding yeast Cdc26."].
- Composition: Cut9-TAP is the purification handle in both PMID:12477395 (13 subunits in both yeasts) and
  PMID:15060174: [PMID:15060174 "Mass spectrometric analysis of the Cut9-TAP purification identified 10
  proteins known or predicted to be APC/C subunits"]; [PMID:15060174 "In addition, three small proteins
  were identified as the products of the reading frames SPBC28E12.01c, SPAC27D7.05c, and SPBC83.04."]
  (Apc13, Apc14, Apc15). ComplexPortal CPX-763 and the slp1/srw1(ste9)/mfr1 variants CPX-764/765/766
  list Cut9 as a core subunit.

## 2. Molecular function: TPR-lobe scaffold, not enzyme and not substrate adaptor

- Cut9 has no catalytic module; the deep research states plainly "Cut9 is the load-bearing TPR scaffold
  of an essential cell-cycle ubiquitin ligase, not an autonomous enzyme." and "Substrate specificity is
  supplied chiefly by coactivators and substrate degrons, not by Cut9."
- Structural evidence that it bridges the other TPR subunits: [PMID:20924356 "In the context of the holo
  APC/C, Cdc16/Cut9 mediates interactions between Cdc23 and Cdc27"]; [PMID:20924356 "These results
  provide strong evidence that conserved residues on the convex face of the Cdc16/Cut9 TPR superhelix
  mediate its association with Cdc23–Apc13, and to a less extent Cdc27."] (charge-reversal of the
  conserved ridge, S. cerevisiae Cdc16 R546E/K552E/K556E/D582K/Q583K = S. pombe K388/R394/K398/D424/Q425,
  abolished Cdc23-Apc13 pull-down and severely impaired Cdc27 binding but not Apc9).
- Homodimer: [PMID:20924356 "Two Cut9 molecules homo-dimerize through a homotypic dimer interface
  generated by a two-fold non-crystallographic symmetry operation"]; [PMID:20924356 "molecular size
  analysis of Cut9–Hcn1, using multiangle light scattering (MALS), is consistent with a Cut9–Hcn1
  hetero-tetramer in solution"]; interface mutants M96R/Q97R/F105R/K109A aggregate. Two copies of each
  TPR subunit per APC/C particle (Ohi et al. 2007 via deep research).
- Hcn1 chamber: [PMID:20924356 "The N-terminal acetylated-Met residue is located within an enclosed
  chamber formed from the inner groove of the Cut9 TPR superhelix"]; the authors propose this shields
  Hcn1 from the Doa10 N-acetyl degron pathway (a model, not tested in vivo).
- Conclusion for the MF rows: GO:0160072 ubiquitin ligase complex scaffold activity ("brings together an
  ubiquitin ligase and an ubiquitin ligase-substrate adaptor", verified on QuickGO, not obsolete) is the
  informative term, matching the CDC16 (yeast) and CDC27 (human) reviews and the ANAPC2 cullin scaffold.
  The self-interaction row is better expressed as GO:0042803 protein homodimerization activity (verified
  on QuickGO, not obsolete). The IBA GO:0140767 enzyme-substrate adaptor activity (PTN002649387, single
  ANAPC7 seed; QuickGO shows ANAPC7 Q9UJX3 has an IDA for GO:0140767 from PMID:34942119) is MODIFY ->
  GO:0160072 for the same reason as in the CDC16/CDC27 reviews.

## 3. Biological process

- Anaphase onset: [PMID:7798319 "The cut9 mutants are defective in the onset of anaphase and possibly in
  the restraint of postanaphase events until the completion of anaphase."]; [PMID:7798319 "The nuc2+ and
  cut9+ genes share an essential function to initiate anaphase."]; [PMID:7798319 "Cells enter mitosis with
  chromosome condensation and short spindle formation, then block anaphase, but continue to progress into
  postanaphase events such as degradation of the spindle, reformation of the postanaphase cytoplasmic
  microtubule arrays, septation, and cytokinesis."].
- Mechanism = securin destruction: [PMID:8632802 "The rapid degradation of Cut2 at anaphase requires its
  amino-terminal region and the activity of Cut9 (ref. 14), a component of the 20S cyclosome/anaphase-promoting
  complex (APC), which is necessary for cyclin destruction."]; [PMID:8632802 "Expression of non-degradable
  Cut2 blocks sister-chromatid separation but not cell-cycle progression."].
- Participation test for involved_in on GO:0016567 / GO:0031145: Cut9 is not a substrate and not merely
  required; it is a structural subunit without which the ligase does not assemble (PMID:9264466), so it
  contributes structure that every ubiquitination step depends on. ACCEPT on all rows.
- GO:1901970 positive regulation of mitotic sister chromatid separation (two IMPs) and GO:0045842 (IBA):
  ACCEPT; the APC/C is the trigger of separation via Cut2 destruction.
- GO:0051301 cell division (IBA from worm seeds, PTN001757014): broad but true; ACCEPT with
  GRANULARITY_MISMATCH noted.

## 4. Phospho-regulation

- [PMID:9264466 "Cut9 in the 20S complex is hyperphosphorylated specifically at the time of metaphase."];
  [PMID:9264466 "Furthermore, deletion of protein kinase PKA (Pkal) suppresses the phenotype of the cut9
  mutation and reduces phosphorylation of Cut9."]; [PMID:9264466 "The Cut9 subunit is likely to be a target
  for regulating APC/ cyclosome function through protein-protein interactions and phosphorylation."].
  PP1/Dis2 mutation has the opposite effect. Sites and kinase are not defined; recorded as a second core
  function (contributes_to GO:0061630) and as a suggested question, not as a NEW annotation.

## 5. Localisation

- UniProt: "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus" with ECO:0000305 (curator inference, no imaging citation).
- Closed mitosis; the essential substrate is nuclear: [PMID:8632802 "Cut2 is located in the nucleus, where
  it is concentrated along the short metaphase spindle."].
- ChIP places Cut9 on chromatin: [PMID:19117951 "the results showed that both Cut4 and Cut9 are highly
  enriched at the dh sequences in otr1R and dh-k repeats in the cenH region spanning the mat2-mat3
  interval"]; [PMID:19117951 "Remarkably, the localization of Cut4p and Cut9p at both the regions was
  almost completely abolished in swi6 Δ strain"]; [PMID:19117951 "Cut4 and Cut9 interact directly with
  Swi6/HP1 and Clr4, whereas the mutant Cut4 does not"]. Silencing, Swi6/H3K9me2/Clr4 recruitment and
  centromeric cohesin are lost in cut9-665 at permissive temperature: [PMID:19117951 "our results showing
  enhanced chromosome loss, lagging chromosomes, and aberrant recombination are obtained at permissive
  temperature 25 °C"]. Single-group study; mechanism (substrate at heterochromatin) unresolved. The
  GO:0005721 pericentric heterochromatin IDA is kept as non-core.
- ORFeome YFP survey (PMID:16823372) scores Cut9 as cytosolic (HDA GO:0005829). This is an overexpressed
  C-terminal YFP fusion in a proteome-wide screen; kept as non-core rather than removed, and the
  discrepancy with the nuclear site of action is flagged in suggested_experiments. IBA cytoplasm
  (PTN000285227, seeded by human ANAPC6 and yeast CDC16) likewise KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
- Deep research agrees the cell-cycle localisation of Cut9 itself is not well established: "the retrieved
  primary literature does not establish a definitive Cut9-specific intracellular localization throughout
  the cell cycle".

## 7. Decisions on NEW terms

- No NEW annotations. GO:0007091 metaphase/anaphase transition of mitotic cell cycle would be a valid
  process term (used for yeast CDC16), but GO:0045842 from the IBA already covers the event and was kept
  as the core-function process term to avoid an unreflected term in core_functions.
- Heterochromatin formation (e.g. GO:0031453) was considered for the PMID:19117951 phenotypes and not
  proposed: single study, mechanism unresolved, and the participation question (which APC/C substrate,
  and does Cut9 do any of the work) cannot be answered from the paper.

## 8. Action summary
