Gene Description

erg1 (SPBC713.12) encodes squalene epoxidase (squalene monooxygenase, EC 1.14.14.17) of Schizosaccharomyces pombe, an FAD-dependent, multi-pass ER-membrane flavoenzyme that uses O2 and electrons from NADPH-cytochrome P450 reductase to convert squalene to (S)-2,3-epoxysqualene, the substrate of lanosterol synthase Erg7. It is the first oxygen-requiring step of ergosterol synthesis and the target of allylamine antifungals such as terbinafine; loss or inhibition of Erg1 causes squalene accumulation. Its expression rises under anaerobic conditions.

Module: ergosterol_biosynthesis (squalene epoxidase); S. cerevisiae ortholog ERG1 (P32476). Fetch verified: Q9C1W3.

Evidence

  • Reaction squalene + O2 + reduced CPR -> (S)-2,3-epoxysqualene; FAD cofactor [UniProt:Q9C1W3 "Reaction=squalene + reduced [NADPH--hemoprotein reductase] + O2 = (S)-"].
  • Inhibition of Erg1 (terbinafine) causes squalene accumulation PMID:33223513; UniProt disruption phenotype: squalene accumulation.
  • Takami et al. quantified fission-yeast squalene and ergosterol by LC-MS/MS after terbinafine treatment, directly tying Erg1 inhibition to squalene accumulation and ergosterol-pathway flux PMID:23145048.
  • Anaerobic induction is Sre1-independent PMID:16537923 - contradicts UniProt INDUCTION text ("up-regulated via ... sre1").
