Gene Description

SPAC17H9.13c (UniProt O13810; the fission yeast PRO1 ortholog, classical pro2 locus) encodes glutamate 5-kinase (EC 2.7.2.11), an amino-acid-kinase-family enzyme with a C-terminal PUA domain. It phosphorylates L-glutamate to L-glutamyl-5-phosphate, the first and feedback-regulated step of proline biosynthesis from glutamate. Function is assigned by orthology.

Notes

(SPAC17H9.13c-notes.md)

2026-11-07 bounded RNA-binding comparator correction

The earlier universal claim that no glutamate kinase binds RNA is contradicted by PMID:37511294: K-12 ProB, expressed with a C-terminal His tag, shows specific but weak RNA binding by SELEX and competition EMSA. The estimated KD is >>10 micromolar, the binding site is not mapped, and no native RNA-regulatory role is established. This experiment neither establishes nor excludes RNA binding by this yeast protein. The original InterPro RNA-binding row is therefore UNDECIDED, preserving every machine payload and all unrelated judgments. The kinase-regulatory role of an E. coli PUA domain is not evidence of absence of RNA binding. This was a bounded source-premise correction, not a complete new review of yeast biology.

PR6160 evidence follow-up (2026-11-07 UTC)

Re-read full PMID:37511294 and the InterPro provenance. Retained RNA-binding UNDECIDED: direct E. coli SELEX/competition EMSA is positive but weak, the PUA binding site was not mapped, and this target was not assayed. Accessibility of that paper does not resolve cross-species conservation; no physiological role is required merely to establish binding. This is an InterPro IEA, not a PAINT RNA assertion.
