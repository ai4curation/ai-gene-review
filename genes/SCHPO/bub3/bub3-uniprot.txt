CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in mitotic spindle assembly checkpoint signaling, a
CC       process that delays anaphase until chromosomes are bioriented on the
CC       spindle, and in the repair of incorrect mitotic kinetochore-spindle
CC       microtubule attachments (PubMed:15509783, PubMed:22660415). Localizes
CC       bub1 to kinetochores and suppresses activation of bub1 away from the
CC       kinetochore (PubMed:22660415). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15509783,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22660415}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of the BUB1-BUB3 complex, composed of bub1 and bub3
CC       (PubMed:15509783, PubMed:22521786, PubMed:22660415). interacts with
CC       mad3 (PubMed:11909965, PubMed:15509783). Interacts with spc7
CC       (phosphorylated on MELT motifs); to recruit the BUB1-BUB3 complex to
CC       kinetochores (PubMed:22660415). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11909965,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15509783, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22521786,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22660415}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15509783,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22521786, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22660415}. Chromosome,
CC       centromere, kinetochore {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15509783,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22521786, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22660415}. Chromosome,
CC       centromere {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15509783}. Note=Associates with
CC       kinetochores and centromeres during the early stages of mitosis.
