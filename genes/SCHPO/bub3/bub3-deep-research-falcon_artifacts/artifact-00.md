| Identity and WD40 scaffold | *S. pombe* **bub3/SPAC23H3.08c** encodes the approximately 36-kDa spindle-checkpoint protein Bub3. | Early-mitotic kinetochore localization | Bub3-GFP forms foci that colocalize with Cnp1 and Ndc80 and is enriched by ChIP at centromeric central-core **cnt/imr** chromatin, not outer repeats. It appears on short spindles in early mitosis, weakens near metaphase, and largely disappears before anaphase. | Nearly all early-mitotic cells show foci; approximately 90% of short-spindle cells have detectable Bub3/Mad3 foci. | Direct *S. pombe* imaging and ChIP. | High |
| Mutual dependence with Bub1 | Bub3 and Bub1 coimmunoprecipitate and require one another for efficient kinetochore enrichment. Bub3 loss also greatly reduces kinetochore recruitment of Mad1, Mad2, and Mad3 without disrupting the outer kinetochore generally. | Mad1/Mad2/Mad3 kinetochore ChIP signals fall by at least 90% in **bub3Δ**; Nuf2 localization is unaffected. | Direct *S. pombe* biochemical, imaging, and ChIP evidence. | High |
| Mph1–phospho-Spc7/MELT recruitment | Mph1/Mps1 phosphorylates Spc7/KNL1 MELT motifs, creating docking sites for Bub3–Bub1. Nonphosphorylatable Spc7 mutants lose Bub1/Bub3 recruitment; phosphomimetic Spc7 recruits them constitutively and can bypass kinase-dead Mph1, but not Bub3. | Spc7 has nine MELT motifs in the original analysis; converting all nine threonines to alanine reduced in-vitro phosphorylation by 63.5%. | Direct *S. pombe* genetics, biochemistry, co-IP, ChIP, and imaging. | High |
| Licensing Mad1–Mad2 signaling | Bub3 can restrain Bub1–Mad1/Mad2 association when Bub3–Bub1 is not productively engaged. Binding to phosphorylated Spc7 converts the complex into a signaling-competent platform, permitting Mph1-dependent Bub1 interaction with Mad1–Mad2. | In nonphosphorylatable **spc7-12TA**, checkpoint arrest is strongly impaired; deleting Bub3 suppresses the defect, while extra Bub1—but not extra Bub3—also improves signaling. | Direct mechanistic *S. pombe* evidence. | High |
| Checkpoint arrest is weakened, not abolished | Contrary to early interpretations, Bub3 is not absolutely required for spindle-checkpoint arrest in fission yeast. Many **bub3Δ** cells arrest after complete microtubule loss, and the residual delay depends on Mad2, even though most checkpoint proteins are no longer enriched at kinetochores. | Approximately 70% of **bub3Δ nda3** cells arrest; only about 10–15% undergo cytokinesis despite unattached kinetochores, versus roughly 90% checkpoint failure in **bub1Δ** or **mad2Δ** controls. | Direct *S. pombe* genetics and cell biology. | High |
| Checkpoint silencing and recovery | Bub3 facilitates checkpoint shutdown and APC/C reactivation after spindle recovery or Aurora/Ark1 inhibition. Loss of Bub3 delays cyclin-B destruction and anaphase and causes poor recovery from prolonged arrest. | **bub3Δ** produces an approximately 20-minute metaphase delay after nda3 release and a roughly 40–60-minute delay in silencing assays; only about 30% form colonies after prolonged arrest. | Direct *S. pombe* time-course and viability evidence. | High |
| Chromosome bi-orientation and error correction | Independently of simply generating arrest, Bub3 cooperates with Bub1 to convert monopolar attachments into bipolar attachments. This role is largely shugoshin-independent and is associated with proper Sgo2/Ark1 centromere organization. | After spindle recovery, chromosomes remained clearly mono-oriented for more than 10 minutes in 20% of **bub3Δ** cells versus 7% of wild type. | Direct *S. pombe* live-cell and genetic evidence. | High |
| Noncanonical astral/cortex response | Disrupting actin and astral-microtubule contact with the medial cortex delays anaphase, securin destruction, and cyclin-B destruction through a selective Bub1–Bub3–Mad3 pathway that does not require Mad1 or Mad2. | Prometaphase/metaphase analyses scored at least 150 cells; a Bub3-specific effect size was not reported in the extracted evidence. | Direct *S. pombe* perturbation and genetic-dependency evidence; precise molecular trigger remains less resolved. | Moderate–high |
| 2024 pathway update: Mph1–Bub1 signaling | Mph1 directly phosphorylates Bub1 at Ser532 near mitotic entry, promoting Bub1 and Mad1/Mad2 kinetochore signaling. This updates the Bub3-associated signaling network but does not directly test Bub3 itself. | Bub1-S532A caused lagging chromosomes in 54% of cells versus 8% with wild-type Bub1 in one assay. | Direct *S. pombe* pathway evidence; indirect relevance to Bub3. | Moderate for Bub3-specific annotation |
