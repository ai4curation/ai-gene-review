# Manual deep research for SCHPO tom7

Automated deep research was unavailable in this worktree, so this file records a manual review of local UniProt, GOA, PANTHER and cached publication evidence for S. pombe tom7.

## Findings

- The S. pombe UniProt entry describes Tom7 as a single-pass mitochondrial outer membrane protein in the Tom7 family and as part of the TOM complex.
- PANTHER PTHR34944 contains the S. pombe and S. cerevisiae Tom7 proteins, supporting curator transfer of conserved TOM-complex annotations.
- No S. pombe-specific Tom7 mechanistic paper was found. Conserved yeast evidence supports Tom7 as a structural or regulatory TOM small subunit rather than a stand-alone import pore, receptor or insertase.
- The GOA MF ND row is still appropriate: the literature and orthology identify Tom7 as a small TOM-complex subunit, but they do not define a specific elemental molecular_function for S. pombe Tom7.

## Literature

- PMID:9217162 summarizes budding-yeast Tom7 as a modulator of TOM machinery assembly and dissociation.
- PMID:9774667 placed Tom7 with Tom5 and Tom6 in the approximately 400 kDa budding-yeast general import pore complex.
- PMID:11259583 showed that the small Tom proteins are released from the GIP complex by stringent detergent while Tom40 and Tom22 remain the stable core that holds accumulated preproteins.
