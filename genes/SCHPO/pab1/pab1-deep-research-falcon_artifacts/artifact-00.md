| Wee1/Cdc25 and mitotic entry | PP2A–Pab1 is required for normal cell-cycle-dependent phosphorylation changes in Wee1 and Cdc25, thereby regulating the Cdc2 mitotic-entry control system. | Direct *S. pombe* | In synchronized cultures, **pab1Δ** extended the cell cycle from approximately **120 min to 180 min**; Wee1 remained partially phosphorylated and Cdc25 remained hyperphosphorylated, without equivalent loss of their abundance oscillations. | Lucena et al., 2017 (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000013) |
| PP1–PP2A mitotic relay | PP1^Dis2 reactivates PP2A-B55^Pab1 during mitotic progression; PP2A-B55 then promotes PP2A-B56 reactivation by exposing a PP1-docking site, establishing an ordered phosphatase relay. | Direct *S. pombe* | Co-immunoprecipitation detected Dis2–Pab1 association; mutation of the conserved RVxF/RxVxF-type docking site abolished association. Cdk1–cyclin B inhibition of Dis2 at T316 blocks the relay early in mitosis. | Grallert et al., 2015 (pqac-00000011) |
| Cytokinesis during metaphase arrest | PP2A-B55^Pab1 normally restrains untimely septation and contributes to mitotic-substrate dephosphorylation during prolonged metaphase arrest. | Direct *S. pombe* | Slp1 repression with **15 µM thiamine for 6 h** produced metaphase arrest; loss or inhibition of PP2A function allowed early or multiple septa despite condensed, unsegregated chromosomes and high cyclin B. About **60%** of relevant phosphatase-mutant populations were metaphase-arrested at 1 h. | Chica et al., 2022 (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000010) |
| TOR signaling and differentiation | Under nutrient-rich conditions, TORC1 supports PP2A-B55^Pab1 activity; nitrogen limitation activates Greatwall–Igo1, inhibits PP2A-B55, permits TORC2/Gad8 signaling, and promotes sexual differentiation. | Direct *S. pombe* pathway evidence | In **pab1Δ**, Spk1 phosphorylation peaked after **45 min** of nitrogen starvation versus only a faint wild-type signal at **3 h**. Transcriptomics found **240 genes upregulated ≥3-fold** in pab1Δ and **88 genes enriched in wild type**; pab1Δ also suppressed Tor2-overexpression differentiation defects. | Martín et al., 2017; Kawamukai, 2024 (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009) |

| B55 substrate recognition | Conserved B55 proteins use a WD40-surface pocket with hydrophobic and electrostatic patches to recognize diverse substrate α-helices; this supplies a mechanistic model for how Pab1 could confer substrate specificity. | Conserved inference; not directly validated for Pab1/Q12702 | AlphaFold2 modeling, mutational scanning, and binding assays identified helix contacts with B55α patches; common determinants include hydrophobic residues at helix positions **i and i+4**.
