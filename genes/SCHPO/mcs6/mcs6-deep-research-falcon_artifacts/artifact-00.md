| **Selective transcriptional program and quantitative transcriptomics** | Conditional Mcs6/Pmh1 impairment caused only about a **one-third** reduction in overall expression after prolonged severe impairment, while roughly **5%** of transcripts decreased more than twofold; another normalization identified approximately **500 genes (~10% of the genome)** changing by more than twofold. Sensitive genes included *ace2, eng1, agn1, mid2, sou1,* and *cdc15*. | **Strong transcriptomic evidence, with normalization-dependent percentages.** Effects are selective and enriched for Sep1/Ace2-regulated cytokinesis and cell-separation genes, rather than an immediate global Pol II shutdown. | Lee et al., Jun 2005, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.e04-11-0982) (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018) |
