# mac1 manual notes

## 2026-10-21

Deep research could not be generated in this environment because the configured providers were unavailable, so this review used the cached Mac1 paper, the abstract-only ORFeome localization screen, the UniProt record, and the PTHR28013 PAINT export directly.

Mac1 is in the PalI/Rim9 PTHR28013:SF3 subfamily, and `PTN001997363` carries the plasma membrane, cell division site, and growing cell tip IBA annotations. For fission yeast these transfers are supported by target-specific literature: Mac1 localizes predominantly to cell tips and the septum, and its N-terminal transmembrane region is sufficient for cell-periphery localization.

The 2002 Mac1 paper shows a high-temperature cell-separation phenotype for null and overexpression strains, but it does not resolve the direct molecular activity by which Mac1 controls cell separation. I did not add the exocytosis annotation present in UniProt because none of the cached Mac1-specific evidence establishes that Mac1 executes exocytosis itself.
