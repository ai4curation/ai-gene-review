Gene Description

IadC (Vapar_1492) is the 330-amino-acid Class IA NADH:ferredoxin oxidoreductase component of the indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) Rieske non-heme iron dioxygenase system (IadD/IadE-IadC) in Variovorax paradoxus S110. Structurally, IadC folds into a triangle-shaped three-domain monomer comprising an N-terminal FMN-binding domain (residues 1-115), a central NADH-binding domain (residues 116-238), and a C-terminal plant-type [2Fe-2S] ferredoxin domain (residues 239-329 in the crystal structure PDB 7YLR; annotated as residues 245-330 by ProSite/UniProt) coordinated by four cysteine residues. IadC oxidizes NADH and transfers electrons via its non-covalently bound FMN cofactor and intramolecular [2Fe-2S] cluster to the Rieske [2Fe-2S] center of IadD, driving the aerobic dioxygenation of IAA to 2-oxindole-3-acetic acid (oxIAA) and enabling rapid auxin clearance and reversion of microbiome-induced root growth inhibition in the plant rhizosphere.

Curation Notes for Variovorax paradoxus S110 iadC (C5CSP5, Vapar_1492)

  • Organism: Variovorax paradoxus S110 (NCBITaxon:543728)
  • Protein Function: Class IA NADH-dependent FMN- and [2Fe-2S]-containing reductase component (IadC, 330 aa) of the indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) Rieske non-heme iron dioxygenase system (IadC–IadD/IadE).

Literature & Structural Evidence

  1. In Vivo Requirement for Rapid IAA Degradation and RGI Reversion (PMID:36266335):
  2. Conway et al. (2022) demonstrated that while iadDE expressed in E. coli can slowly convert IAA using endogenous surrogate reductases, co-expression of iadC (iadCDE) substantially accelerates IAA turnover in vivo and in vitro.

  3. Crucially, in Variovorax (ΔiadC), deletion of iadC drastically reduces the rate of IAA degradation and abolishes the ability of Variovorax to rescue Arabidopsis root growth inhibition (RGI) caused by the IAA-producing commensal Arthrobacter CL28 (PMID:36266335).

  4. Crystal Structure and Electron Transfer Mechanism (PMID:37459330):

  5. Ma et al. (2023) solved the 1.82 Å crystal structure of Variovorax paradoxus S110 IadC (PDB 7YLR, mapped to C5CSP5).
  6. IadC (330 aa) is a monomeric Class IA Rieske dioxygenase reductase comprising three domains:
    1. FMN-binding domain (residues 1–115): Six-stranded antiparallel $\beta$-barrel that non-covalently binds flavin mononucleotide (FMN); the isoalloxazine ring is positioned 5.2 Å from the [2Fe-2S] cluster (PMID:37459330).
    2. NADH-binding domain (residues 116–238): Classic $\alpha/\beta$ Rossmann-like fold (PMID:37459330).
    3. Plant-type [2Fe-2S] ferredoxin domain (residues 239–329): Connected via a loop (residues 239–249) and coordinates a [2Fe-2S] cluster via four conserved cysteine residues (Cys274, Cys279, Cys282, and Cys314) (PMID:37459330).
  7. IadC oxidizes NADH, reduces FMN, transfers electrons intramolecularly to its [2Fe-2S] cluster, and shuttles electrons intermolecularly to the Rieske [2Fe-2S] cluster of IadD in the IadD/IadE oxygenase complex (PMID:37459330).

Review of Existing GOA Annotations

  • GO:0051536 (iron-sulfur cluster binding, IEA) -> MODIFY to GO:0051537 (2 iron, 2 sulfur cluster binding) (since GO:0051536 is a general parent term and the exact [2Fe-2S] stoichiometry is established by X-ray crystallography in PMID:37459330).
  • GO:0016491 (oxidoreductase activity, IEA) -> MODIFY to GO:0008860 (ferredoxin-NAD+ reductase activity).
