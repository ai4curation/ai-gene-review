# Curation Notes: `ureG` (`C5CY05`, `Vapar_4248`) in *Variovorax paradoxus* S110 (`VARPS`)

## Summary
- **Gene / Locus:** `ureG` (`Vapar_4248`, UniProtKB `C5CY05`, Swiss-Prot reviewed, 216 aa).
- **Domain Architecture & Active-Site Motifs:** SIMIBI-class G3E GTPase (`HAMAP MF_01389`, `InterPro:IPR004400`, `PANTHER:PTHR31715:SF0`, `Pfam:PF02492`) containing a P-loop GTP-binding site at residues 24–31 (`GPVGSGKT`) and the invariant `Cys72-Pro73-His74` (`CPH`) nickel-binding motif.
- **Mechanistic Role:** Couples GTP hydrolysis (`GO:0003924` `GTPase activity`, `GO:0005525` `GTP binding`) and nickel coordination (`GO:0016151` `nickel cation binding`) to release $\text{Ni}^{2+}$ into the carbamylated active site of `UreC` (`Vapar_4243`, `C5CY00`) within the `UreD`-`UreF`-`UreG` pre-activation complex ([PMID:10500143](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10500143/); [PMID:22369361](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22369361/)). Linked to `MODULE:urease_biogenesis_urea_hydrolysis` (`ureg_nickel_delivery` / `ureg_gtpase_metallochaperone`).
