Gene Description

UreG (Vapar_4248) in Variovorax paradoxus S110 is a 216-amino-acid cytoplasmic SIMIBI-class G3E-family GTPase and nickel metallochaperone (HAMAP rule MF_01389, InterPro IPR004400, PANTHER PTHR31715:SF0, Pfam PF02492) encoded in the chromosome 1 urease gene cluster (Vapar_4240-4249). UreG contains a canonical P-loop GTP-binding motif (residues 24-31, GPVGSGKT) and an invariant Cys-Pro-His (CPH, residues 72-74) metal-binding motif; within the UreD-UreF-UreG activation complex, UreG accepts Ni(2+) from UreE and couples GTP hydrolysis to nickel release into the carbamylated active site of UreC (Vapar_4243).

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Purify wild-type Vapar_4248 and C72A/H74A variants and assay Ni(2+) binding, nucleotide-dependent dimerization by size-exclusion chromatography, and in vitro activation of apo-UreABC.

Hypothesis: Substitution of Cys72 or His74 in the conserved CPH motif of Vapar_4248 abolishes Ni(2+)-dependent dimerization and GTP-dependent activation of V. paradoxus S110 apo-urease.

Curation Notes: ureG (C5CY05, Vapar_4248) in Variovorax paradoxus S110 (VARPS)

Summary

  • Gene / Locus: ureG (Vapar_4248, UniProtKB C5CY05, Swiss-Prot reviewed, 216 aa).
  • Domain Architecture & Active-Site Motifs: SIMIBI-class G3E GTPase (HAMAP MF_01389, InterPro:IPR004400, PANTHER:PTHR31715:SF0, Pfam:PF02492) containing a P-loop GTP-binding site at residues 24–31 (GPVGSGKT) and the invariant Cys72-Pro73-His74 (CPH) nickel-binding motif.
  • Mechanistic Role: Couples GTP hydrolysis (GO:0003924 GTPase activity, GO:0005525 GTP binding) and nickel coordination (GO:0016151 nickel cation binding) to release $\text{Ni}^{2+}$ into the carbamylated active site of UreC (Vapar_4243, C5CY00) within the UreD-UreF-UreG pre-activation complex (PMID:10500143(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10500143/); PMID:22369361(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22369361/)).
