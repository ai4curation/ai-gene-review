Gene Description

SsuD (Vapar_2976) in Variovorax paradoxus S110 is a 389-amino-acid cytosolic FMNH2-dependent alkanesulfonate monooxygenase (EC 1.14.14.5; HAMAP rule MF_01229) belonging to the bacterial luciferase-like TIM-barrel superfamily. Paired with an NAD(P)H-dependent FMN reductase (SsuE), it catalyzes the oxygenolytic C-S bond cleavage of aliphatic sulfonates to yield the corresponding aldehyde and inorganic sulfite, mobilizing organosulfonates in rhizosphere soils for bacterial and host plant sulfur nutrition.

Literature & Domain Evidence

  1. Genomic Context & Rhizosphere Sulfur Mobilization (PMID:21183664, UniProtKB:C5CNW9):
  2. Variovorax paradoxus S110 encodes an expansive suite of alkanesulfonate and taurine desulfonation systems (PMID:21183664), including the Swiss-Prot reviewed ssuD (Vapar_2976, C5CNW9), enabling mineralization of soil organosulfonates into bioavailable inorganic sulfur.
  3. Conserved Alkanesulfonate Monooxygenase (SsuD) Domain Architecture (UniProtKB:C5CNW9):
  4. C5CNW9 matches HAMAP:MF_01229 (Alkanesulf_monooxygen), NCBIfam:TIGR03565, InterPro:IPR019911 (Alkanesulphonate_mOase_FMN-dep), PANTHER:PTHR42847 (ALKANESULFONATE MONOOXYGENASE), and Pfam:PF00296 (Bac_luciferase TIM-barrel fold).
  5. Catalyzes the two-component monooxygenase reaction (paired with an NAD(P)H-dependent FMN reductase such as SsuE):
    an alkanesulfonate + FMNH2 + O2 = an aldehyde + FMN + H2O + 2 H+ + sulfite (RHEA:23064, EC 1.14.14.5).
