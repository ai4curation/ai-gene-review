Gene Description

NRDE-2 (nuclear RNAi defective-2) is a conserved nuclear protein of the NRDE2 family, with an N-terminal NRDE-2 domain, an SR-rich region and HAT-like (half-a-TPR) repeats. It is a core downstream factor of the C. elegans nuclear RNAi pathway. siRNA-loaded nuclear Argonautes (NRDE-3 in somatic cells, HRDE-1 in the germline) recruit NRDE-2 to nascent transcripts complementary to their guide RNAs. NRDE-2 is then needed for NRDE-1 to associate with pre-mRNA and chromatin, for siRNA-directed H3K9 and H3K27 trimethylation at target loci, and for inhibition of RNA polymerase II during transcription elongation, which silences target pre-mRNA co-transcriptionally. When antisense ribosomal siRNAs are present, NRDE-2 also moves to the nucleolus and inhibits RNA polymerase I elongation on pre-rRNA. NRDE-2 is required for multigenerational RNAi inheritance and germline immortality. The human ortholog NRDE2 binds and inhibits the exosome cofactor MTR4 in nuclear speckles. Whether worm NRDE-2 keeps this exosome-regulatory activity has not been tested.

Core Functions

siRNA-guided nuclear Argonautes (NRDE-3, HRDE-1) recruit NRDE-2 to nascent target transcripts. There it is required for NRDE-1 recruitment to pre-mRNA and chromatin, for H3K9me3 deposition, and for inhibition of RNAP II elongation, which silences targets co-transcriptionally in somatic and germ-cell nuclei. Its exact molecular activity (adaptor, or regulator of an RNA helicase such as MTR-4) is not yet known.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does C. elegans NRDE-2 keep the MTR4-interacting domain and the MTR-4/exosome-inhibitory activity of human NRDE2? If so, is that activity part of how it silences nascent transcripts?

Q: What is the direct molecular activity by which NRDE-2 links Argonaute recruitment to NRDE-1 loading, H3K9me3 and RNAP II elongation arrest?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Test in vitro whether recombinant worm NRDE-2 binds MTR-4. In vivo, test whether NRDE-2 mutants lacking the putative MTR4-interacting region keep nuclear RNAi and H3K9me3 at germline target genes.

Key facts with provenance

  • NRDE-2 is recruited by NRDE-3/siRNA to nascent transcripts PMID:20543824.
  • It inhibits RNAP II elongation PMID:20543824.
  • It is required for NRDE-1 loading and for H3K9me PMID:21901112.
  • It is required for the dsRNA-induced H3K9me3 footprint PMID:22231482.
  • In the germline, HRDE-1 recruits NRDE-2 PMID:22810588.
  • It moves to the nucleolus and inhibits RNAP I when risiRNAs are present PMID:34365510.
  • H3K27me3 is also directed through the Nrde pathway (PMID:26365259; abstract only, not
    quoted in the review).
  • Human NRDE2 is an MTR4/exosome inhibitor in speckles PMID:30842217. This has not been
    tested in worm.

Decisions

  • protein binding (NRDE-3 IPI): REMOVE (uninformative).
  • Post-transcriptional silencing IMP: MODIFY to GO:0031047 (the mechanism is
    co-transcriptional).
  • mRNA stabilization ISS: MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. Nuclear speck and negative regulation
    of RNA catabolic process: kept as non-core.
  • No core MF assigned. The direct molecular activity of NRDE-2 is unknown.
  • I considered a NEW negative regulation of transcription elongation by RNA polymerase II
    (GO:0034244) and rejected it: the comparator check failed, because no other NRDE
    factor carries this term.
