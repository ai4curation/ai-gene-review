CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor involved in maintaining normal fat
CC       levels (PubMed:19187779). Regulates the expression of genes involved in
CC       lipid metabolism in response to nutrient availability, such as the
CC       fatty-acid desaturases fat-5, fat-6 and fat-7 (PubMed:12530969,
CC       PubMed:19936816). In response to a high-glucose diet, promotes fatty
CC       acid synthesis, elongation and desaturation, acting in concert with
CC       transcription factor mxl-3 (PubMed:29113111). Plays a role in synthesis
CC       of monomethyl branched-chain fatty acids (mmBCFAs) as well as other
CC       very-long-chain fatty acids (PubMed:15340492). Downstream of the cis-
CC       Golgi membrane protein eas-1/GOLT1B and the E3 ubiquitin ligase rnf-
CC       145/RNF145, plays a role in the regulation of glial size, perhaps by
CC       modulating synthesis of long-chain polyunsaturated fatty-acids (LC-
CC       PUFA) (PubMed:33370778). Modulates expression of genes in the one-
CC       carbon cycle, which produces the methyl donor S-adenosylmethionine
CC       (SAM) (PubMed:22035958). Probably involved in a feedback loop in which
CC       decreased levels of SAM lead to increased transcriptional activity of
CC       sbp-1, thereby causing lipid accumulation (PubMed:22035958). Involved
CC       in the negative regulation of zinc homeostasis (PubMed:28710073).
CC       Involved in the response to simulated microgravity, in concert with
CC       Mediator complex subunit mdt-15, probably acting in the intestine
CC       (PubMed:32448509). Plays a role in transgenerational lipid accumulation
CC       in response to a high-fat diet, probably acting by up-regulating
CC       wdr-5.1 expression to increase the level of trimethylated 'Lys-4'
CC       histone H3 (H3K4me3), which may then induce the expression of fat-5,
CC       fat-6 and fat-7 (PubMed:35140229). May act as an oxygen sensor for
CC       lipid metabolism (PubMed:19187779). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12530969,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15340492, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19187779,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19936816, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22035958,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28710073, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29113111,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32448509, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33370778,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35140229}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Sterol regulatory element binding protein sbp-1]: Precursor
CC       of the transcription factor form, which is embedded in the endoplasmic
CC       reticulum membrane (By similarity). Processing of this form allows
CC       release of the transcription factor form that translocates into the
CC       nucleus and activates transcription of genes involved in sterol
CC       biosynthesis and lipid homeostasis (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9WTN3}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Processed sterol regulatory element binding protein sbp-1]:
CC       Key transcription factor that regulates expression of genes involved in
CC       sterol biosynthesis and lipid homeostasis.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9WTN3}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22035958,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28710073}. Note=Localization to nucleus is enhanced
CC       in a sams-1-dependent manner (PubMed:22035958). Localized to nucleus in
CC       an eas-1-dependent manner (PubMed:33370778). Nuclear localization is
CC       reduced by lowering the level of zinc (PubMed:28710073). Does not
CC       accumulate in the nucleus of AMsh glial cells during the early larval
CC       stages (L1 to L3), but has strong nuclear localization in adulthood
CC       (PubMed:33370778). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22035958,

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: RNAi-mediated knockdown causes early larval
CC       arrest and a pale, skinny appearance; paleness due to absence of dark,
CC       lipid-laden fat granules from the intestine (PubMed:12530969,
CC       PubMed:19936816). Decrease in body size, delayed growth, fewer eggs
CC       laid and 5-6 days after being laid, many eggs hatch inside the body
CC       (PubMed:19936816). Exposure to oleic or linoleic acid returns body size
CC       almost to normal and also increases number of eggs laid
CC       (PubMed:19936816). In normoxic conditions, reduces total fat levels,
CC       but has no effect in anoxia (PubMed:19187779). Reduces the increase in
CC       body width/length ratio caused by anoxia (PubMed:19187779). Alters
CC       fatty-acid composition, including of monomethyl branched-chain fatty
CC       acids (mmBCFAs) (PubMed:15340492). Exacerbates induction of reactive
CC       oxygen species (ROS) production and decrease in locomotion behavior
CC       caused by simulated microgravity (PubMed:32448509). Knockdown in the
CC       first generation offspring (F1) of adults fed a high-fat diet prevents
CC       lipid accumulation (PubMed:35140229). Tissue-specific knockdown in any
CC       of several different tissues abolishes elevated levels of H3K4me3
CC       modification (PubMed:35140229). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12530969,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15340492, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19187779,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19936816, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32448509,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35140229}.
