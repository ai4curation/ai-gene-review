# WormBase gene description for ift-20 (worm)

Y110A7A.20 encodes an ortholog of Chlamydomonas and human IFT20;Y110A7A.20 undergoes intraflagellar transport (IFT) in a mannerconsistent with its being a component of the OSM-3-driven IFT-particle Bsubcomplex; Y110A7A.20 is expressed in amphid and phasmid neurons,mechanosensory neurons, and other unidentified head neurons and tailcells; by orthology with human IFT20, Y110A7A.20 may deliver ciliarymembrane proteins from the Golgi complex to the cilium; Y110A7A.20 hasno obvious function in mass RNAi assays.
