CC   -!- FUNCTION: RNA-binding protein that coordinates cell fate specification
CC       and differentiation during early embryogenesis (PubMed:23034181,
CC       PubMed:9834181). Binds to a consensus pos-1 recognition element (PRE)
CC       consisting of the sequence 5'-UA(U 2-3)RGD(N 1-3)G-3', where R is any
CC       purine, D is A, G, or U, and N is any base (PubMed:18952820,
CC       PubMed:23034181). The PRE motif is found within the 3' untranslated
CC       region of many maternal transcripts required for early development
CC       (PubMed:18952820). Binds to the 3' untranslated region (UTR) of Notch
CC       receptor homolog glp-1, thereby repressing glp-1 translation in the
CC       posterior blastomeres in the embryo (PubMed:12702662, PubMed:23034181).
CC       Binding to glp-1 3' UTR excludes cell fate regulator gld-1 binding to
CC       an overlapping binding site in the glp-1 3' UTR (PubMed:23034181).
CC       Binds to the neg-1 3'UTR thereby opposing neg-1 expression and
CC       cytoplasmic polyadenylation of the neg-1 mRNA poly(A) tail promoted by
CC       gld-2 and gld-3 (PubMed:26096734). By inhibiting the cytoplasmic
CC       lengthening of neg-1 mRNA, restricts the accumulation of neg-1 protein
CC       and promotes endo-mesoderm development in anterior blastomeres
CC       (PubMed:26096734). Essential for germline specification
CC       (PubMed:9834181). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12702662,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18952820, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23034181,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26096734, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9834181}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18952820}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9834181}.
CC       Note=Localized to P granules in the germline blastomeres P1, P2, P3 and
CC       P4. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9834181}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Distributed asymmetrically after the first
CC       cleavage of the embryo: expression is higher in posterior blastomere
CC       (germline blastomere P1) of a 2-cell stage embryo than in the anterior
CC       blastomere (somatic blastomere AB) (at protein level) (PubMed:9834181).
CC       Initially present at similar levels in both daughters of P1, but
CC       persists at high levels only in the germline daughter, P2 (at protein
CC       level) (PubMed:9834181). The asymmetrical distribution pattern is seen
CC       at each of the subsequent divisions of germline blastomeres
CC       (PubMed:9834181). After the division of the final germline blastomere
CC       P4, not detectable in either daughter cell (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:9834181). Present predominantly in the germ precursors
CC       (PubMed:9834181). Distributed uniformly in oocytes and in newly
CC       fertilized embryos (PubMed:9834181). Expressed in immature gonads of
CC       fourth stage larvae (PubMed:9834181). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9834181}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: RNAi-mediated interference leads to maternal-
CC       effect lethality (PubMed:9834181). Expression of glp-1 in all four
CC       blastomeres including the posterior EMS and P2 blastomeres, instead of
CC       only in the ABa and ABp blastomeres at the four-cell stage
CC       (PubMed:12702662). Abolished asymmetry in neg-1 expression in AB
CC       descendants (PubMed:26096734). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12702662,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26096734, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9834181}.
