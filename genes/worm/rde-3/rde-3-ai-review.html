Gene Description

RDE-3 (also called MUT-2) is a C. elegans ribonucleotidyltransferase of the polymerase beta nucleotidyltransferase superfamily that acts as a poly(UG) polymerase. It adds long, non-templated, perfectly alternating uridine and guanosine tails (pUG tails) to the 3' ends of mRNA fragments targeted by RNA interference and to transposon RNAs. pUG tails with more than 16 perfectly alternating U and G nucleotides turn the attached RNA fragment into a template for RNA-directed RNA polymerases such as RRF-1, which synthesize secondary 22G siRNAs. Cycles of pUG RNA-templated siRNA synthesis and siRNA-directed pUGylation sustain dsRNA-triggered transgenerational silencing in the germline. RDE-3 localizes to perinuclear germline Mutator foci with RRF-1 and the RDE-8 endonuclease. It is required for siRNA accumulation, RNAi in all tissues, transposon silencing, and fertility and viability at high temperature. RDE-3 also pUGylates Orsay virus RNAs to support antiviral RNAi.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

poly(UG) polymerase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the template-independent addition of alternating uridine and guanosine monophosphate residues to the 3' end of an RNA molecule, forming a poly(UG) tail.

Justification: RDE-3/MUT-2 adds perfectly alternating UG tails, which no existing child of template-free RNA nucleotidyltransferase activity describes. RNA uridylyltransferase activity covers only the U additions.

Parent term: template-free RNA nucleotidyltransferase activity

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:30988468 : a poly(UG) polymerase, Caenorhabditis elegans MUT-2, that adds alternating uridine and guanosine nucleotides to form poly(UG) tails
  • PMID:32499657 : Here we show that, in its natural context in C. elegans, RDE-3 adds pUG tails to targets of RNA interference, as well as to transposon RNAs.

Core Functions

Poly(UG) polymerase. It adds non-templated alternating UG tails to 3' ends of RNAi-targeted mRNA fragments and transposon RNAs, which makes them templates for RdRP (RRF-1) synthesis of 22G siRNAs. No GO term exists for this activity; the nearest existing terms are RNA uridylyltransferase activity (GO:0050265, annotated by IBA) and its parent template-free RNA nucleotidyltransferase activity (GO:0098680).
