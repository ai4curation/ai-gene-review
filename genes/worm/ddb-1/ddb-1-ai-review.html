Gene Description

DDB-1 is the C. elegans ortholog of DNA damage-binding protein 1, a large triple beta-propeller adaptor of CRL4 (cullin-4-RING) E3 ubiquitin ligases. DDB-1 binds CUL-4 specifically among the worm cullins and recruits DCAF substrate receptors, linking them to the cullin scaffold. The CUL-4/DDB-1 complex associates with and degrades the replication-licensing factor CDT-1 during S phase, preventing DNA re-replication in proliferating larval blast cells; with CDT-2 it targets the translesion polymerase POLH-1 during the embryonic DNA damage response, and with the DCAF WDR-23 it represses the stress-responsive transcription factor SKN-1. DDB-1 is broadly expressed in proliferating larval cells and at high levels in the adult spermatheca, and is predicted to act in the nucleus and cytoplasm.

Core Functions

Adaptor/scaffold of CRL4 ligases: binds CUL-4 and DCAF substrate receptors (CDT-2, WDR-23) and substrates such as CDT-1, enabling their ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation.
