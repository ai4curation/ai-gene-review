## Key findings
- INS-6 adopts an insulin fold and binds and activates the human insulin receptor [PMID:12654724 "INS-6 binds to and activates the human insulin receptor"].
- Removing INS-4, INS-6 and DAF-28 together causes fully penetrant Daf-c [PMID:24671950]. daf-28 and ins-6 are non-redundant: ins-6 matters more for dauer exit [PMID:21343369 "ins-6 has a more significant role in promoting dauer exit"].
- INS-6 is a neuromodulator in salt chemotaxis, released from ASE in a BLI-4-dependent way [PMID:24013594], and in olfactory learning, acting from ASI to repress ins-7 in URX [PMID:23395381].
