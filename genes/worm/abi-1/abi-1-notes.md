# Research & Curation Notes: C. elegans abi-1 (UniProtKB:Q10929)

## 1. Identity & Evolutionary Position (`PTHR10460:SF0`)
- **Gene / Protein**: *Caenorhabditis elegans* `abi-1` (`WB:WBGene00015146`, sequence name `B0336.6`), UniProt `Q10929` (469 aa, `sequence_version: 2`, TrEMBL entry), PANTHER subfamily `PANTHER:PTHR10460:SF0` (`ABELSON INTERACTING PROTEIN, ISOFORM D`).
- **Single-copy nematode representative**: `abi-1` is the sole *C. elegans* member of the `PTHR10460` Abl interactor family, orthologous to *Drosophila* `Abi` and vertebrate `ABI1`/`ABI2`/`ABI3`. Like metazoan `ABI1`/`ABI2`, *C. elegans* `ABI-1` possesses both the N-terminal WAVE/WVE-1-binding helical region and the C-terminal SH3 domain (residues 412–469; conserved tyrosine `Tyr421`), and retains direct binding to `ABL-1` tyrosine kinase (`PMID:19402756`) as well as proline-rich effectors (`MIG-10`, `TOCA-1`/`TOCA-2`).

## 2. Direct Experimental Biology in *C. elegans*
1. **WVE-1/WAVE Regulatory Complex & Apoptotic Cell Engulfment / Cell Migration (`PMID:19402756`)**:
   - Hurwitz et al. (`PMID:19402756`, *PLoS Biol*) showed that `ABI-1` acts in the `WVE-1` (WAVE), `GEX-2` (Sra1/CYFIP), `GEX-3` (Nap1/NCKAP1), and `CED-10` (Rac) pathway to promote cytoskeletal reorganization during apoptotic cell corpse engulfment and distal tip cell (DTC) / epidermal migration (`ventral enclosure`). `ABI-1` binds `ABL-1` in vitro (`GO:0019901` `protein kinase binding`), and `ABL-1` kinase negatively regulates `ABI-1` to inhibit engulfment independently of `CED-10` GTPase activation (`GO:1901076`, `GO:0016477`).
2. **UNC-53/NAV2-Coupled Cell & Neuronal Process Outgrowth (`PMID:19168673`)**:
   - Schmidt et al. (`PMID:19168673`, *Development*) identified `ABI-1` as a direct binding partner of the N-terminal calponin homology (CH) domain of `UNC-53` (`NAV2` ortholog). `ABI-1` localizes to the cytoplasm, neuronal cell bodies, dendrites, and commissural axons (`GO:0005737`, `GO:0043025`, `GO:0030425`, `GO:0030424`), and `abi-1` loss-of-function (`tm494` / RNAi) impairs excretory cell canal outgrowth and mechanosensory/motoneuron migration alongside `wve-1` and `arx-2` (`Arp2/3`).
3. **MIG-10/Lamellipodin Coupling in Axon Guidance, Excretory Canal Outgrowth & Presynaptic Vesicle Clustering (`PMID:23022657`, `PMID:23209429`, `PMID:23028145`)**:
   - McShea et al. (`PMID:23022657`, *Dev Biol*) and Xu & Quinn (`PMID:23209429`, *PLoS Genet*) demonstrated that the C-terminal SH3 domain of `ABI-1` binds `MIG-10` (the *C. elegans* MRL/Lamellipodin adaptor) and acts with `WVE-1` downstream of `UNC-6` (Netrin) and `SLT-1` (Slit) to drive guided neuron migration, axon guidance, and excretory canal morphogenesis (`GO:0001764`, `GO:0048858`).
   - Stavoe et al. (`PMID:23028145`, *Genes Dev*) showed in AIY interneurons that isoform `MIG-10B` specifically recruits `ABI-1` to presynaptic sites downstream of Netrin/`UNC-6` and `RAC`/`CED-10` to organize presynaptic F-actin (`GO:0099140`) and instruct synaptic vesicle clustering (`GO:0097091`) via Synapsin (`SNN-1`).
4. **TOCA-1/TOCA-2 Endocytic Actin Regulation in Oocytes & Embryonic Epidermis (`PMID:19798448`)**:
   - Giuliani et al. (`PMID:19798448`, *PLoS Genet*) demonstrated direct in vitro binding between *C. elegans* `ABI-1` and the C-terminal SH3 domain of `TOCA-2` (`CeTOCA-2`, `WB:WBGene00010663` / `UniProtKB:Q9XUS7`, Figure 7C; conserving mammalian `ABI1`–`TOCA-1` SH3 binding; `GO:0017124` `SH3 domain binding`), while `toca-1` (`WB:WBGene00017298` / `UniProtKB:Q19253`) and `toca-2` act together in clathrin-dependent yolk-receptor (`RME-2`) endocytosis in oocytes (`GO:2000370`) and genetically interact with `wve-1` during embryonic epidermal morphogenesis.

## 3. Audit of WormBase MOD Gene Description (`abi-1-deep-research-wormbase.md`)
- `abi-1-deep-research-wormbase.md` (`WB:WBGene00015146`) accurately summarizes `abi-1` requirements in embryonic epidermal enclosure, excretory cell / DTC / mechanosensory / motoneuron migrations (`PMID:19168673`, `PMID:19402756`, `PMID:23022657`), apoptotic cell engulfment (`PMID:19402756`), physical interactions with `MIG-10`, `UNC-53`, and `ABL-1`, and neuronal localization (`PMID:19168673`).
- **Curatorial omissions / inaccuracies**:
  1. **Omission of core WAVE/SCAR complex identity & synaptic/endocytic roles (`MISSING_STATEMENT`)**: Despite naming `MIG-10`, `UNC-53`, and `ABL-1`, the WormBase paragraph completely omits that `ABI-1` is an obligate subunit of the `WVE-1`/`GEX-2`/`GEX-3` WAVE/SCAR regulatory complex (`GO:0031209`, `PMID:19402756`, `PMID:23209429`), as well as its established presynaptic F-actin / synaptic vesicle clustering role in AIY neurons (`PMID:23028145`) and TOCA-1/TOCA-2-coupled clathrin-mediated endocytosis in oocytes (`PMID:19798448`). Recorded as `MISSING_STATEMENT` on `WORMBASE` (`WB:WBGene00015146`).
  2. Minor phrasing note: describes human `ABI1` (`OMIM:603050`) as *"mutated in acute myeloid leukemia"*, whereas in AML `ABI1` is rearranged as a `t(10;11)(p11.2;q23)` `KMT2A`/`MLL` chromosomal translocation partner (`PMID:9694699`).
