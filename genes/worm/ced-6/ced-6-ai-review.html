Gene Description

CED-6 is the C. elegans orthologue of mammalian GULP1, a cytoplasmic adaptor protein with an N-terminal phosphotyrosine-binding (PTB) domain, an adjacent leucine zipper that mediates homodimerization, and a proline/serine-rich C-terminal half. It acts in engulfing cells, where its PTB domain binds the NPXY motif in the cytoplasmic tail of the engulfment receptor CED-1. CED-6 is recruited with CED-1 around apoptotic cell corpses and, together with CED-7, it is needed for actin reorganization and corpse engulfment. It forms a complex with clathrin heavy chain and the AP2 adaptor complex on nascent phagosomes and acts upstream of dynamin DYN-1 and the Rac GTPase CED-10. CED-6 also recruits the E3 ubiquitin ligase TRIM-21 to CED-1, which leads to proteasomal turnover of the receptor. Like other engulfment genes, ced-6 helps engulfing cells promote the death of cells that receive weak death signals, and it contributes to the loss of dopaminergic neurons damaged by oxidative stress.

Identity

  • UniProtKB:O76337 (F56D2.7), the orthologue of human GULP1. It has an N-terminal PTB domain and a proline/serine-rich C-terminal half PMID:9635426.
  • It dimerizes PMID:10734103.
  • PANTHER: UniProt and PANTHER's own classification agree, placing it in PTHR11232:SF77 (unlike ced-1). Its IBA engulfment node in the module is PTN000132688.

Core: CED-1 signaling adaptor in engulfing cells

  • It acts in the engulfing cell PMID:9635426.
  • Its PTB domain binds the NPXY motif of CED-1 PMID:11729193. This was confirmed with point mutants PMID:35929733.
  • Actin reorganization PMID:15744306.
  • Clathrin/AP2 hub PMID:23696751. The authors' model is an actin hub, not vesicle cargo loading PMID:23696751.
  • Receptor turnover PMID:35929733.

Death-promoting role (non-core)

  • Engulfment genes promote death of weakly signalled cells PMID:11449278. Also PMID:11449279.
  • The Denning 2013 and Chen 2013 (CED-8) papers use ced-6(n2095) only as a clearance-defective background PMID:23505386. This is the background-genotype variant of the clearance-defect pattern in projects/APOPTOSIS/ASSAY_READOUT_OVERANNOTATION.md.
  • 6-OHDA neurodegeneration PMID:29346382. This is not annotated in GOA and is not proposed: it is a consequence of the engulfment role.

Wnt paper (Cabello 2010)

  • The spindle, left/right and DTC IGIs with mom-5 are over-annotations. The paper reports a role for the CED-1/6/7 branch in DTC migration as PMID:20126385, and no ced-6 spindle phenotype.
