# WormBase gene description for ced-6 (worm)

ced-6 encodes an Src homology (SH) 3 and phosphotyrosine-binding (PTB) domain-containing adaptor protein, orthologous to human CED-6/GULP, that is required for cell corpse engulfment during apoptosis; CED-6 is bound by the phosphotyrosine binding motif in the cytoplasmic tail of the transmembrane protein CED-1, and and within phagocytic cells this interaction may be part of a signal transduction pathway that promotes apoptotic cell engulfment.
