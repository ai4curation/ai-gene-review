CC   -!- FUNCTION: Negative cell-cycle regulator that functions at the G1-to-S-
CC       phase transition (PubMed:10587644, PubMed:9716524). Required for
CC       suspension of the cell cycle in dauer larvae and starved L1 larvae
CC       (PubMed:10587644, PubMed:9716524). In vulval precursor cells (VPCs), a
CC       pathway of heterochronic genes acts via cki-1 to maintain VPCs in G1
CC       during the L2 larval stage (PubMed:10587644, PubMed:15247923,
CC       PubMed:9716524). Cul-2 may function in ubiquitin-mediated degradation
CC       by targeting cki-1 for degradation (PubMed:10587644). Involved in
CC       distal tip cell development by repressing and modulating cye-1/cdk-2
CC       activity levels in Z1.aa/Z4.pp and in Z1.ap/Z4.pa (PubMed:17476329).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10587644, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15247923,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17476329, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9716524}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with cyd-1 (Probable); the interaction is direct.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11684669}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10587644,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15247923}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: In embryos, expression is first seen in pharyngeal
CC       primordium and later in all differentiating cells. Post embryonic
CC       expression corresponds to developmental patterns of cell cycle
CC       progression in many tissues including sex myoblasts, distal tip cells,
CC       vulval cells, seam cells, neurons, intestine cells and hypodermal
CC       cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10587644, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9716524}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: In embryos, expression is first seen in pharyngeal
CC       primordium and later in all differentiating cells. Post embryonic
CC       expression corresponds to developmental patterns of cell cycle
CC       progression in many tissues including sex myoblasts, distal tip cells,
CC       vulval cells, seam cells, neurons, intestine cells and hypodermal cells
CC       (PubMed:10587644, PubMed:9716524). During the development of distal tip
CC       cells (DTC), expressed asymmetrically between the daughters of the Z1.a
CC       and Z4.p cells; asymmetric expression is regulated by wrm-1, a
CC       component of the Wnt/MAPK pathway (PubMed:17476329).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10587644, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17476329,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9716524}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: RNAi-mediated knockdown results in additional
CC       cell divisions in postembryonic intestinal cells characterized by a
CC       premature entry into S phase. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11684669,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15247923}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the CDI family. {ECO:0000305}.
