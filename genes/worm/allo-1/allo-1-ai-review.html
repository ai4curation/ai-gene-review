Gene Description

ALLO-1 (allophagy-1, R102.5) is a nematode-specific selective autophagy receptor (cargo adaptor) that drives allophagy. Allophagy is the autophagic removal of sperm-derived (paternal) mitochondria and membranous organelles from the early C. elegans embryo, and it underlies maternal inheritance of mitochondrial DNA. ALLO-1 is expressed in the oocyte germline. Within about a minute of fertilization it gathers on ubiquitin-modified paternal organelles. Through an N-terminal LC3-interacting region (LIR) motif it binds the Atg8/LC3 homolog LGG-1. It is also needed to recruit the ULK complex (UNC-51 and EPG-7/FIP200) around the cargo, so that autophagosomes form locally. Two splice isoforms differ in their C-termini and prefer different cargo: ALLO-1a mainly targets membranous organelles, and its C-terminal coiled-coil region binds K48- and K63-linked polyubiquitin. ALLO-1b mainly targets paternal mitochondria. The TBK1/IKKepsilon-family kinase IKKE-1 binds ALLO-1 and phosphorylates it, which is needed for ALLO-1 to accumulate on the cargo. ALLO-1 and IKKE-1 thus form a positive feedback loop that starts autophagosome formation.

Core Functions

ALLO-1 is a cargo adaptor (autophagy receptor) for allophagy. It is recruited to ubiquitinated paternal mitochondria (mainly ALLO-1b) and membranous organelles (mainly ALLO-1a, which binds K48/K63 polyubiquitin). It links the cargo to LGG-1 through its LIR motif and recruits the ULK complex, so that autophagosomes form around the paternal organelles. IKKE-1 phosphorylation of ALLO-1 drives its accumulation on the cargo.

Key findings

  • ALLO-1 is the allophagy receptor and binds LGG-1 through its LIR PMID:29255173. The 2018 paper is abstract-only in the cache.
  • IKKE-1 binds and phosphorylates ALLO-1 PMID:29255173
  • The two isoforms prefer different cargo. ALLO-1b (Q9U389-1, the canonical UniProt isoform) mainly targets paternal mitochondria; ALLO-1a (Q9U389-2) mainly targets membranous organelles PMID:38368448
  • ALLO-1 recruits the ULK complex (UNC-51, EPG-7) to the cargo PMID:38368448
  • The ALLO-1a C-terminus is a parallel coiled-coil that binds K48/K63 polyubiquitin PMID:41234204 (abstract only).

Curation decisions

  • NEW: mitophagy (GO:0000423). The comparator check passes (FUNDC1, BNIP3 in GO:0000423; OPTN in GO:0061734).
