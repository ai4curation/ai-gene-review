Gene Description

MUT-15 (also RDE-5; T01C3.8) is a germline protein of the mutator complex with no recognizable domains. MUT-15 is recruited by the B-C region of the Q/N-rich scaffold MUT-16 to perinuclear Mutator foci, and probably binds MUT-16 directly. In turn MUT-15 is needed to bring the NYN-domain proteins NYN-1, NYN-2 and the endoribonuclease RDE-8 into Mutator foci, and for RDE-8 to engage RNAi target mRNA efficiently. Like other mutator-class genes, mut-15 is required for RNAi and for the accumulation of RdRP-dependent secondary 22G siRNAs. It therefore acts as an organizing subunit that links the MUT-16 scaffold to the ribonuclease module of the siRNA amplification machinery.

Core Functions

MUT-15 is a mutator-complex subunit recruited by MUT-16 to perinuclear Mutator foci. There it recruits the NYN-domain ribonucleases RDE-8, NYN-1 and NYN-2 and helps RDE-8 engage RNAi target mRNA, so that RdRP-dependent secondary 22G-siRNA amplification can proceed. Its molecular activity (direct adaptor or something else) is not known.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does MUT-15 bind RDE-8 and NYN-1/NYN-2 directly, which would make it a protein-macromolecule adaptor between MUT-16 and the ribonuclease module? Or does it act through RNA?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Use recombinant pull-downs or yeast two-hybrid tests between MUT-15 fragments and MUT-16 (B-C region), RDE-8, NYN-1 and NYN-2. Then make separation-of-function mut-15 alleles that keep MUT-16 binding but lose RDE-8/NYN recruitment, and measure 22G-RNA levels.

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: The molecular activity of MUT-15 is unknown. It has no recognizable domains, and it has not been shown whether it binds RDE-8, NYN-1 or NYN-2 directly (as an adaptor between them and MUT-16), binds RNA, or has some other activity.

OPEN BIOLOGY MF_DARK

What is known: It is established that MUT-15 localizes to Mutator foci in a MUT-16-dependent way, co-purifies with MUT-16 and RDE-8, and is needed to recruit RDE-8 and NYN-1 to Mutator foci and for secondary siRNA accumulation.

Key facts with provenance

  • It is recruited by MUT-16 PMID:30036386.
