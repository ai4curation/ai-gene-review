- PMID:11250875 directly cloned `glc-3` and showed that its cRNA forms homomeric L-glutamate-gated chloride channels in Xenopus oocytes. The same abstract reports irreversible ivermectin activation, chloride selectivity, lack of picrotoxinin inhibition and inhibition by BIDN and fipronil.

NCBI PMC HTML was checked manually for the `glc-3` row: Fig. 6B includes `glc-3(ok321)`, the authors state "glc-3 mutants crossed the barrier more frequently" in the Cu2+/diacetyl choice assay, and AIA-specific GLC-3 expression rescued the mutant phenotype. A short exact excerpt was added to the newly cached PMID file so the Shinkai supporting text is checkable without routing through this notes file.
- PMID:16527366 is abstract-only in the local cache. The abstract shows that C. elegans GluCl alpha subunits regulate locomotory behavior and that a Haemonchus contortus GluClalpha3 receptor functionally complements the C. elegans `avr-14` locomotion phenotype, but it does not name `glc-3`; the `glc-3` locomotion row is retained by deferring to WormBase full-text curation.
- PMID:37811674 is full text in the local cache. Li et al. identify GLC-3 as the DVB glutamate-gated chloride channel that mediates autoinhibition when glutamate tone is elevated and reconstitute a GLC-3-dependent, chloride-sensitive glutamate conductance in HEK293T cells.

WormBook Table 3 also reports `glc-3`/GluClalpha4 expression in AIY and cites Wenick and Hobert 2004, but that primary expression paper was not fetched/read in this pass; the MOD description is therefore marked UNVERIFIED for the AIY clause, and later AIA/DVB functional placement is based on the Shinkai and Li full texts rather than on the brief description.
- Remaining biology gap: endogenous GLC-3 complexes have not been purified, so the native subunit stoichiometry and whether GLC-3 concentrates at a postsynaptic specialization remain open.
