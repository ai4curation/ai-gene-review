# WormBase gene description for fis-1 (worm)

fis-1 encodes a protein similar to the yeast and human Fis1 proteins involved in mitochondrial fission; fis-1 does not seem to be required for mitochondrial fission in C. elegans, but it is possible that it may function redundantly along with fis-2 and other genes; fis-1 expression is enriched in the germline.
