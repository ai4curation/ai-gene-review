# ugtp-1 (C. elegans, Q02334) manual literature summary

No deep-research API was available; this is a manual literature survey (PubMed MCP, cached
publications, UniProt, Alliance), 2026-10-28.

## Identity and family
ugtp-1 (ZK370.7/ZK370.9; WBGene00022721) encodes a 355-aa multi-pass membrane protein of the
nucleotide-sugar transporter family, SLC35A subfamily [UniProt Q02334, "Belongs to the
nucleotide-sugar transporter family"; Pfam PF04142 Nuc_sug_transp]. Its best-score vertebrate
ortholog is SLC35A1, the Golgi CMP-sialic acid transporter (Alliance orthology). Human SLC35A1 also
imports CDP-ribitol into the Golgi [PMID:34015330 "We discovered that CDP-ribitol transport relies
on the CMP-sialic acid transporter SLC35A1"].

## Experimental literature on UGTP-1
There is no published biochemical characterisation of UGTP-1 transport activity. The protein is
known from its use as a GFP marker:
- Golgi: "GBF-1 partially co-localized with the medial Golgi marker UGTP-1::GFP" in oocytes
  [PMID:23840591].
- Cortical granules: UGTP-1 localizes to cortical granules during meiotic prometaphase I and
  metaphase I [PMID:17913784, as curated by UniProt; cached abstract only].

## Related worm nucleotide-sugar transporters (context, not UGTP-1)
- SRF-3 transports UDP-Gal and UDP-GlcNAc [PMID:15123614].
- C03H5.2 transports UDP-GlcNAc and UDP-GalNAc [PMID:17060606].
- SQV-7 acts in the Golgi in glycosaminoglycan synthesis [PMID:12391314].
- HUT-1 (SLC35B1 ortholog) is an ER protein [PMID:19270184].

## Sialic acid
C. elegans has no annotated NANS, CMAS or sialyltransferase (see
ugtp-1-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md), so CMP-sialic acid transport inferred from SLC35A1 is unlikely
to be physiological in the worm. The actual substrate of UGTP-1 remains unknown.
