CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: In larvae, expressed in pharyngeal neurons, ventral
CC       and dorsal nerve cords, tail neurons, egg-laying neurons and egg-laying
CC       muscles. Detected in the neurons of the pharyngeal nerve ring, head
CC       neurons, tail neurons and egg-laying muscles in adults. Detected in
CC       male-specific tail ganglia and rays in males.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23484030}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed throughout the developmental stages with
CC       a 2-fold increase in late larval (L4) stage.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23484030}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mutant animals display a progressive defect in
CC       locomotion starting at the L2 stage, showing delayed response to
CC       prodding, irregular thrashing movements and a low pharyngeal pumping
CC       rate. Worms have shorter body length, convoluted intestine and develop
CC       gonadogenesis defects including loss of spermathecae, sterility and
CC       larval arrest at L4 stage. They also show abnormal cholinergic
CC       signaling leading to early onset of paralysis in the presence of
CC       aldicarb, an acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. RNAi-mediated knockdown
CC       results in enlargement of mitochondria in the body wall muscle. Muscle-
CC       specific knockdown shows an increased level of oxygen consumption.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22078881, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23484030}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the N-CoR nuclear receptor corepressors family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
