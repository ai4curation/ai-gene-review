# gei-8 (C. elegans) - manual literature research

No deep-research provider API keys were available; this file was compiled manually from
the cached publications and a PubMed search ("gei-8 elegans", 3 hits: PMID:23484030,
PMID:22078881, PMID:31645584) plus the original GEX-3 interactor screen (PMID:11922622).

## Identity

- UniProt P34333 (NCOR1_CAEEL), 1778 aa, WormBase gene gei-8 (GEX Interacting protein 8).
- Two N-terminal SANT/Myb-like domains (SANT 125-176, Myb-like 356-398), long disordered
  middle region, C-terminal region with predicted nuclear receptor interaction (CoRNR) motifs.
- PANTHER PTHR13992 (Nuclear receptor corepressor), subfamily SF39; UniProt: "Belongs to the
  N-CoR nuclear receptor corepressors family."

## Orthology to NCoR/SMRT

- [PMID:23484030 "Here, we identify GEI-8 as the closest homologue of NCoR and SMRT in C. elegans"]
- [PMID:23484030 "Our results confirm that GEI-8 is the orthologue of the vertebrate NCoR/SMRT corepressors and demonstrate important roles for this putative transcriptional corepressor in development and neuronal function."]
- Independently, [PMID:22078881 "A protein blast search indicated that gei-8 (GEX interacting protein family member 8) is the only putative NCoR1 homolog in the C.elegans genome."]
- In vertebrates, SANT1 forms the deacetylase activation domain (DAD) that binds and activates
  HDAC3 [PMID:23484030 "The N-terminus proximal SANT1 domain activates the HDAC3 deacetylase [8], [9] and is referred to as the deacetylase activation domain (DAD)."]. No direct
  GEI-8/HDA interaction has been shown in worm.

## Physical interactions

- GEX-3 (WAVE/SCAR complex subunit): found in a yeast two-hybrid screen
  [PMID:23484030 "GEI-8 was originally identified as a GEX-3 binding protein based on yeast-two-hybrid assays"]; the original paper is abstract-only
  in the cache [PMID:11922622 "We identified many interacting molecules by yeast two-hybrid screening"]. Physiological relevance unknown.
- NHR-60 (HNF4-related nuclear receptor): GST pull-down
  [PMID:23484030 "the C-terminal region I of GEI-8 that includes the predicted NR1 binding site showed affinity to GST-NHR-60 but not to the control protein expressing the GST anchor used for pull-down experiments"].

## Expression

- Reporter expression in neurons, muscles and intestine; [PMID:23484030 "GEI-8 is expressed as at least two isoforms throughout development in multiple tissues, including neurons, muscle and intestinal cells"].

## Loss-of-function phenotypes

- gei-8(ok1671) deletion: [PMID:23484030 "results in severe mutant phenotypes with developmental defects, slow movement and growth, arrested gonadogenesis and defects in cholinergic neurotransmission"].
- Microarray: [PMID:23484030 "Whole genome expression analysis by microarrays identified sets of de-regulated genes consistent with both the observed mutant phenotypes and a role of GEI-8 in regulating transcription."] Both up- and downregulated genes were found; Y9C9A.16 (sulfide:quinone oxidoreductase) strongly upregulated.
- Genetic interaction with 21U-RNA locus in gonadogenesis: [PMID:23484030 "suggesting that GEI-8 may function in concert with the 21-U RNAs to regulate gonadogenesis"].
- Muscle mitochondria: [PMID:22078881 "Knockdown of gei-8, the sole C. elegans NCoR homolog, also robustly increased muscle mitochondria and respiration, suggesting conservation of NCoR1 function."]
- Burrowing assay: gei-8(gk693) promoter deletion used as neuromuscular model
  [PMID:31645584 "gei-8(gk693) animals have a 1 kb deletion in the gei-8 promoter region and, therefore, the potential for a modified gei-8 expression level."]

## Gaps

- No direct demonstration of transcriptional repression activity, chromatin occupancy,
  nuclear localization of the protein itself (reporters used NLS vectors), or HDAC partnership in worm.
