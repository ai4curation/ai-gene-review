Gene Description

TMEM-231 (T26A8.2) is the Caenorhabditis elegans ortholog of human TMEM231 (JBTS20), a membrane protein of the TMEM231 family. In mammals TMEM231 is a component of the MKS/tectonic complex. In worms TMEM-231 is expressed only in ciliated sensory neurons and localises to the ciliary transition zone (TZ), just distal to the basal body. It is a core MKS-module component. Its TZ localisation requires MKS-5, MKS-2, CEP-290 and TMEM-107, but not NPHP-4. TMEM-231 is in turn needed to concentrate MKS-2 and TMEM-17 at the TZ, but not MKS-5 or NPHP-1. Loss of TMEM-231 compromises the TZ diffusion barrier, so the periciliary membrane protein TRAM-1a leaks into cilia. TMEM-231 thus helps assemble the MKS module of the TZ, which controls ciliary membrane composition and, with the NPHP module, supports sensory cilium formation. Its molecular activity is unknown.

Core Functions

Core, interdependent MKS-module membrane protein of the ciliary transition zone. It is needed to assemble MKS-module partners (MKS-2, TMEM-17) at the TZ and to maintain the TZ diffusion barrier that excludes non-ciliary membrane proteins from the cilium. No molecular activity is established, so no MF is asserted.
