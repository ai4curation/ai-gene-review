# WormBase gene description for gst-4 (worm)

gst-4 encodes a putative glutathione-requiring prostaglandin D synthase;gst-4 is required for sperm to normally migrate towards a PUFA-basedsignal exuded by oocytes; gst-4(RNAi) hermaphrodites are infertile, boththrough aberrant loss of their own sperm and through failure of males toeffectively inseminate them; in mammalian eicosanoid signalling,prostaglandin D synthase is required for the conversion of prostaglandinH2 into D2; gst-4 mRNA is expressed in adults, and accumulationincreases in response to paraquat.
