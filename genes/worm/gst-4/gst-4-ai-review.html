Gene Description

GST-4 (K08F4.7, also called Ce-GST-p24) is a sigma-class glutathione S-transferase of C. elegans, a small cytosolic enzyme that conjugates reduced glutathione to hydrophobic electrophiles, with activity towards lipid peroxidation products. gst-4 is transcriptionally induced by oxidative stress (paraquat, pathogen-generated reactive oxygen species, light) through the transcription factor SKN-1, and its promoter-GFP fusion is a widely used reporter of SKN-1 activity. The enzyme is broadly distributed in tissues, and its abundance sets organismal resistance to oxidative stress: overexpression increases, and RNAi or deletion decreases, survival under intracellularly generated oxidative stress.
