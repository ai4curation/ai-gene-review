# WormBase gene description for rhgf-2 (worm)

rhgf-2 encodes a Rho guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor (RhoGEF) with similarity to the mammalian PLEKHG5/Tech/Syx RhoGEFs; rhgf-2 is required for embryonic morphogenesis, larval development, and regulation of locomotion; when expressed in mammalian neuronal tissue culture cells, RHGF-2 induces cell rounding inhibits neurite outgrowth; RHGF-2 functions as a RhoGEF for the RHO-1 GTPase; RHGF-2 physically interacts with the multi-PDZ domain scaffold protein MPZ-1; RHGF-2 is expressed in many, but not all, C. elegans neurons from the 1.5-fold stage of embryogenesis through adulthood; in nerve ring axons, RHGF-2 expression overlaps with that of MPZ-1.
