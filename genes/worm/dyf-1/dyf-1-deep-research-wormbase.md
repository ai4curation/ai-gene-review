# WormBase gene description for dyf-1 (worm)

dyf-1 encodes a ciliary protein, belonging to a conserved but unfamiliarprotein family, specifically required for the kinesin OSM-3 to dock ontoand move intraflagellar transport (IFT) particles; DYF-1 affectsdevelopment of the amphid and phasmid neurons, chemotaxis, and dauerformation; DYF-1 function is downstream of BBS-8, which is required forIFT particles to stably bind both kinesin-II and OSM-3.
