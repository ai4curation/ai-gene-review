# WormBase gene description for cct-4 (worm)

cct-4 encodes a putative delta subunit of the eukaryotic cytosolic ('T complex') chaperonin, orthologous to human CCT4 (OMIM:605142); CCT-4 represses SKN-1-dependent transcription of gst-4; CCT-4 is expressed in pharynx, pharyngeal gland cells, intestine, renal gland cells, and neurons; CCT-4 is expressed in touch receptor neurons in a MEC-3-dependent fashion.
