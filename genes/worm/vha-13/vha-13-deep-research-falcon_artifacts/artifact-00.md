| Identity | *C. elegans* **vha-13** (Y49A3A.2; Q9XW92) encodes V-ATPase V1 catalytic subunit A, orthologous to ATP6V1A. | A3B3 rotary role | Three A and three B subunits form the alternating **A3B3** V1 head with three catalytic sites. ATP hydrolysis drives the D/F central rotor in approximately 120-degree power strokes, coupling V1 catalysis to V0 c-ring rotation and proton transport. | Conserved yeast V1 single-molecule evidence applied by orthology | High | Yanagisawa et al., March 2024, [10.3389/fmolb.2024.1269040](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmolb.2024.1269040) (pqac-00000008) |
| Oocyte lysosomal localization | Germline-expressed GFP::VHA-13 forms puncta that colocalize with LysoTracker-positive acidic lysosomes in sperm-containing hermaphrodite oocytes; it is diffuse in unmated females or sperm-depleted animals. | Direct worm reporter evidence | High, with reporter caveat | Samaddar et al., August 2021, [10.7554/eLife.62653](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.62653) (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000013) |
| Sperm-triggered recruitment | Following mating, VHA-13 redistribution begins at about **10 minutes** and produces distinct lysosomal foci within **20–30 minutes**, coincident with release from oocyte arrest. | Germline function and proteostasis | Germline-sensitive vha-13 RNAi caused sterility, whereas whole-body RNAi-defective animals did not show that response.
