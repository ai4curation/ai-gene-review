# sdpn-1 (syndapin/PACSIN) curation notes

Scaffold accession: UniProt Q6AHQ8 (TrEMBL, isoform F45E1.7b entry; the module
lists the sibling TrEMBL entry Q09989 for the same gene). PANTHER PTHR23065:SF11
"SYNDAPIN, ISOFORM C". The only C. elegans syndapin (F-BAR + SH3).

## Endosomal recycling function (Gleason et al. 2016, full text)

- [PMID:27630264 "Here we analyze the endocytic transport function of the only C. elegans syndapin, SDPN-1."]
- [PMID:27630264 "We find that SDPN-1 is a resident protein of the early and basolateral recycling endosomes in the C. elegans intestinal epithelium, and sdpn-1 deletion mutants display phenotypes indicating a block in basolateral recycling transport."]
- [PMID:27630264 "sdpn-1 mutants accumulate abnormal endosomes positive for early endosome and recycling endosome markers that are normally separate, and such endosomes accumulate high levels of basolateral recycling cargo."]
- [PMID:27630264 "Furthermore, we observed strong colocalization of endosomal SDPN-1 with the F-actin biosensor Lifeact, and found that loss of SDPN-1 greatly reduced Lifeact accumulation on early endosomes."]
- [PMID:27630264 "Taken together our results provide strong evidence for an in vivo function of syndapin in endocytic recycling, and suggest that syndapin promotes transport via endosomal fission."]
- Lipid binding / tubulation
  [PMID:27630264 "Full-length recombinant SDPN-1 tubulates acidic membranes in vitro"]
  [PMID:27630264 "Like its mammalian homologues, SDPN-1 contains one conserved N-terminal F-BAR domain and a single C-terminal SH3 domain (Figure 1, A and B)."]
- Localization
  [PMID:27630264 "In the intestine, SDPN-1::GFP localized to distinct puncta near the basolateral cortex, as well as on or near the apical intestinal plasma membrane (Supplemental Figure S1, B and C)."]
