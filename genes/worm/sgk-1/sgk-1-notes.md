## Key findings
- SGK-1 acts in parallel to AKT-1/2 in DAF-2 signaling, and its loss causes egg-laying defects, extended generation time, stress resistance and lifespan changes [PMID:15068796 "Loss of sgk-1 results in defective egg-laying, extended generation time, increased stress resistance, and an extension of life span"].
- SGK-1 phosphorylates DAF-16 [PMID:15068796] and SKN-1 [PMID:18358814].
- It acts downstream of TORC2: [PMID:23973804 "The SGK-1 kinase mediated these functions downstream of rict-1/TORC2"] and [PMID:27401555 "SGK-1 functions independently of DAF-16/FoxO; instead, SGK-1 promotes the cytoplasmic localization of the PQM-1 transcription factor"].
- It is needed for basolateral sorting of MIG-14 in the intestine [PMID:26115433 "Thus the subcellular localization of MIG-14 is dependent on SGK-1."]. SGK-1::GFP sits at the apical cortex and in the cytoplasm.
- Lifespan effects of sgk-1 loss are reported inconsistently in the literature, so no NEW lifespan term was added.
- TORC2 signaling (GO:0038203) was considered as a core process but not used: human SGK1 does not carry it in GOA (comparator check, QuickGO, 2026-10-08). It is raised as a suggested question.
