Gene Description

SGK-1 is the C. elegans ortholog of serum- and glucocorticoid-inducible kinase, an AGC-family serine/threonine protein kinase. It is activated by PDK-1 and by the TORC2 complex (RICT-1), and forms a complex with AKT-1 and AKT-2. SGK-1 directly phosphorylates the transcription factors DAF-16/FOXO and SKN-1/Nrf, promoting their cytoplasmic retention; downstream of TORC2 it also promotes cytoplasmic localization of PQM-1, independently of DAF-16, to support intestinal vitellogenin production and fat transport to the germline. sgk-1 loss of function causes slow development, egg-laying defects, small body size, altered stress resistance and lifespan, and defects in sorting the Wnt-sorting receptor MIG-14/Wntless to the basolateral intestinal membrane. SGK-1 binds PIP3 in vitro and, together with AKT-1 and AKT-2, promotes embryonic cell survival. It is expressed strongly in intestine and head and tail neurons, localizing to the cytoplasm and apical cell cortex in intestine and to both cytoplasm and nucleus in neurons.

Core Functions

SGK-1 is an AGC serine/threonine kinase that, activated by PDK-1 and acting in a complex with AKT-1 and AKT-2, phosphorylates DAF-16/FOXO and SKN-1 to transduce DAF-2 insulin/IGF-1-like signals, controlling development, stress resistance and lifespan.

Molecular Function:
protein serine/threonine kinase activity
Directly Involved In:
insulin receptor signaling pathway
Cellular Locations:
cytoplasm
In Complex:
protein kinase complex
Supporting Evidence:
  • PMID:15068796
    All three kinases of this complex are able to directly phosphorylate DAF-16/FKHRL1
  • PMID:18358814
    The IIS kinases AKT-1, -2, and SGK-1 phosphorylate SKN-1

Downstream of TORC2 (RICT-1), SGK-1 kinase activity antagonizes SKN-1 in the embryo and keeps PQM-1 cytoplasmic in the intestine, promoting vitellogenesis and fat transport to the germline independently of DAF-16.

  • PMID:23973804
    The SGK-1 kinase mediated these functions downstream of rict-1/TORC2

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does SGK-1 phosphorylate PQM-1 directly, or act through an intermediate, to control its nuclear exclusion downstream of TORC2?

Q: Given that mammalian SGK1 lacks a PH domain, which region of C. elegans SGK-1 mediates the PIP3 binding observed in vitro?

Q: Should SGK-1 carry GO:0038203 TORC2 signaling? Human SGK1, the canonical TORC2 substrate, does not carry it in GOA, so it was not proposed here; GO guidance on whether TORC2 substrates belong in that term would settle it.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: In vitro kinase assays of SGK-1 on PQM-1 and in vivo analysis of PQM-1 phospho-site mutants to test whether PQM-1 is a direct SGK-1 substrate.

Key findings

  • SGK-1 acts in parallel to AKT-1/2 in DAF-2 signaling, and its loss causes egg-laying defects, extended generation time, stress resistance and lifespan changes PMID:15068796.
  • SGK-1 phosphorylates DAF-16 PMID:15068796 and SKN-1 PMID:18358814.
  • It acts downstream of TORC2: PMID:23973804 and PMID:27401555.
  • It is needed for basolateral sorting of MIG-14 in the intestine PMID:26115433. SGK-1::GFP sits at the apical cortex and in the cytoplasm.
  • Lifespan effects of sgk-1 loss are reported inconsistently in the literature, so no NEW lifespan term was added.
  • TORC2 signaling (GO:0038203) was considered as a core process but not used: human SGK1 does not carry it in GOA (comparator check, QuickGO, 2026-10-08). It is raised as a suggested question.
