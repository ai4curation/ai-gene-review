Gene Description

IFTA-1 (C54G7.4) is the Caenorhabditis elegans ortholog of human WDR35 (IFT121), a core subunit of intraflagellar transport complex A (IFT-A). It is a large (1246 aa) scaffold made of two N-terminal WD40 beta-propellers, a tetratricopeptide-repeat (TPR)/alpha-solenoid region and a C-terminal zinc-finger-like domain, with no catalytic domain. ifta-1 is an X-box gene expressed in ciliated sensory neurons. GFP-tagged IFTA-1 concentrates at the ciliary base (transition zone) and along the axoneme, and moves bidirectionally at IFT rates. ifta-1 mutants have somewhat shortened cilia that lack part of the distal segment, accumulate IFT machinery inside the cilium (a sign of failed retrograde transport), take up dye poorly, and chemotax poorly. IFTA-1 behaves like the IFT-A subunit CHE-11/IFT140 and needs CHE-11 to reach cilia. With IFT-43 and IFT-139 it is also needed to remove periciliary membrane proteins from the cilium. Human WDR35 mutations cause cranioectodermal dysplasia and short-rib thoracic dysplasia.

Core Functions

Core subunit of intraflagellar transport complex A (IFT-A). IFTA-1 is a non-catalytic WD40 beta-propeller and TPR protein that moves on IFT trains along the axoneme and is required for retrograde (tip-to-base) transport of IFT machinery. Its specific molecular activity within IFT-A is unknown, so no MF is asserted.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does worm IFTA-1, like vertebrate WDR35, also help deliver membrane vesicles to the ciliary base, separately from its role in retrograde IFT?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express ciliopathy-equivalent IFTA-1 point mutants from the endogenous locus and measure IFT-A train loading, dynein-2 turnaround at the tip and periciliary membrane cargo exclusion.

Hypothesis: Separate IFTA-1 interfaces support retrograde train assembly and removal of membrane cargo from the cilium.

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: How IFTA-1 helps couple dynein-2 to trains at the ciliary tip is unknown. ifta-1 mutants accumulate IFT machinery inside cilia, and IFT-A as a whole is needed for train turnaround, but which IFTA-1 surface binds dynein-2 or other IFT-A subunits has not been mapped in the worm.

OPEN BIOLOGY

What is known: Established: IFT-A membership, dependence on CHE-11 for ciliary entry, and a requirement for retrograde IFT. Unknown: direct binding partners and the mechanism by which IFTA-1 supports retrograde train assembly.

Significance: WDR35 ciliopathy alleles cluster in the propellers and solenoid; knowing which interfaces carry the retrograde function would explain them.

What would resolve it: Structure-guided point mutations in IFTA-1 propeller and solenoid interfaces, read out by single-molecule IFT-A and dynein-2 imaging.

Provenance (the field's own admissions):

  • "our data establish key roles for IFT-A in mediating motor-train coupling during IFT turnaround, promoting retrograde IFT initiation, and modulating dynein-2 retrograde motility" — PMID:37883232
