# WormBase gene description for ifta-1 (worm)

ifta-1 encodes a evolutionarily conserved protein found in ciliated unicellular and multicellular organisms including humans and is absent in non-ciliated organisms like yeast; IFTA-1 contains a X-box and several N-terminal WD repeats; ifta-1 is required for retrograde intraflagellar transport and likely associates with, or is a core component of the IFT-A subcomplex; ifta-1 is also required for the proper functioning and assembly of cilia; IFTA-1 localizes to the basal body of the cilium.
