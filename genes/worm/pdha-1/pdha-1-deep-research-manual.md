# pdha-1 (T05H10.6; UniProt P52899) - manual literature summary

## Identity and family

- PDHA-1 is the single C. elegans pyruvate dehydrogenase E1 alpha subunit (397 aa, nuclear gene
  T05H10.6, WBGene00011510). UniProt names it "Probable pyruvate dehydrogenase E1 component subunit
  alpha, mitochondrial" (PE 3, inferred from homology) [file:worm/pdha-1/pdha-1-uniprot.txt
  "Probable pyruvate dehydrogenase E1 component subunit alpha, mitochondrial"].
- Family: PANTHER PTHR11516 (SF60), NCBIfam TIGR03182 PDH_E1_alph_y, Pfam PF00676 E1_dh,
  ThDP-binding fold.
- Signature scope: IPR001017 (DH_E1, Pfam PF00676) is the generic 2-oxoacid dehydrogenase E1 domain shared by PDH, BCKDH and acetoin dehydrogenase E1 alpha subunits, whereas IPR017597 / TIGR03182 is the PDH-specific E1 alpha subgroup; PDHA-1 matches both. It carries an N-terminal mitochondrial transit peptide (predicted) and the
  ThDP/Mg2+/pyruvate binding residues mapped from human PDHA1 (P08559) [uniprot FT BINDING].
- UniProt FUNCTION (curated text): "The pyruvate dehydrogenase complex catalyzes the overall
  conversion of pyruvate to acetyl-CoA and CO(2)." Subunit: "Tetramer of 2 alpha and 2 beta
  subunits." Location: "Mitochondrion matrix". Activity regulation: inactivation by
  phosphorylation of the alpha subunit (by similarity).

## Mechanistic context (from orthologs)

The E1 component (alpha2beta2) performs ThDP-dependent decarboxylation of pyruvate and reductive
acetylation of the lipoyl arm of E2 (EC 1.2.4.1, RHEA:19189). The ThDP site lies at the alpha/beta
interface (UniProt ligand note "ligand shared with beta subunit"), so the alpha subunit contributes
to, but cannot by itself perform, the E1 reaction. The C. elegans E1 beta partner is C04C3.3
(pdhb-1), E2 F23B12.5 and E3 LLC1.3 (dld-1) [PMID:21533078 "T05H10.6 (E1α), C04C3.3 (E1β),
F23B12.5 (E2), or LLC1.3 (E3)"].

## C. elegans experimental literature

All in vivo work on pdha-1 is RNAi knockdown; no biochemical assay of the worm enzyme was found.

- PMID:21533078 (Narasimhan et al. 2011, PDP-1): RNAi of each PDHc subunit including T05H10.6 is
  lethal when maintained across generations; PDP-1 (PDH phosphatase homolog) acts through PDHc.
- PMID:24445252 (Vergano et al. 2014): pdha-1(RNAi) worms show elevated alanine and increased
  glycolytic flux in 13C-glucose tracing, consistent with a block at PDH ["the PDH mutant (pdha-1)
  generated by feeding RNAi showed significantly elevated alanine"].
- PMID:42844987 (2026, DCA in PDCD models): pdha-1(RNAi) reduces survival, growth and locomotion,
  raises lactate/pyruvate and induces UPRmt; dichloroacetate (a PDK inhibitor) partially rescues.
- PMID:30586284 (2019, IC-ascarosides): partial pdha-1 RNAi reduces IC-ascaroside and indole
  metabolite production; full RNAi causes sterility ["Growth on RNAi(pdha-1) bacteria made the
  worms sterile."]. Interpreted by the authors as an indirect metabolic effect.
- PMID:15340492 (Kniazeva et al. 2004, mmBCFA): mentions in passing, citing unpublished data, that
  RNAi of "another predicted component of the BCKAD complex, pyruvate dehydrogenase (T05H10.6)"
  reduced C15ISO/C17ISO. T05H10.6 is a PDH (not BCKDH) E1 alpha; the effect is unpublished and
  indirect (possibly via acetyl-CoA supply or shared regulation), not evidence of BCKDH membership.
- PMID:37506907 (2023, aflatoxin): uses a pdha-1 mutant strain; host pyruvate metabolism mattered
  less than bacterial pyruvate metabolism (abstract only).
- PMID:20188671 (Haynes et al. 2010): source of the HDA mitochondrion annotation (mitochondrial
  proteomics); cached abstract only.

## Regulation

Worm PDH kinase PDHK-2 and phosphatase PDP-1 are the expected regulators of the alpha-subunit
phosphorylation switch (PMID:21533078; PMID:22848591, abstract).
