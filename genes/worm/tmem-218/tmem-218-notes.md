# tmem-218 (T23E7.5; UniProt Q2A955) notes

Deep research: falcon timed out (600 s), and the perplexity-lite fallback is unavailable. No deep-research file was produced, so the review relies on cached full text.

- Novel TZ protein, peripheral member of the MKS module [PMID:26982032 "Our genetic interaction, hierarchical, and in vivo functional analyses reveal that TMEM-218 is a new MKS module component."].
- Its TZ localisation needs MKS-5, CEP-290 and MKS-2, but not NPHP-4. TMEM-218 is not needed for MKS-5, MKS-2 or NPHP-4 localisation, nor for TRAM-1a exclusion [PMID:26982032 "Our data show that TMEM-218 is not required for the localisation of MKS-5, MKS-2 (“core” MKS module protein), or NPHP-4 (“core” NPHP module protein)"].
- Synthetic dye-filling defect with nphp-4 but not with mks-2. The authors note that TEM confirmation is still needed.
- No MF is known, so none is asserted.
