# rab-7 (C. elegans, Q23146) - manual literature review

No deep-research provider API keys were available, so this summary was compiled by hand
from the cached publications in `publications/` and PubMed searches ("rab-7 elegans",
56 hits on 2026-10-28). Claims carry inline provenance.

## Identity

- W03C9.3, 209 aa, unreviewed TrEMBL entry Q23146. Rab family small GTPase with Switch-I
  (36-44) and Switch-II (67-83) regions and a C-terminal disordered tail ending in a CXC
  prenylation motif ("Belongs to the small GTPase superfamily. Rab family.", UniProt
  Q23146, PROSITE-ProRule PRU00753).
- Single C. elegans Rab7 ortholog [PMID:18351800 "elegans rab-7 encodes RAB-7, a likely ortholog of human Rab7"].

## Biochemistry

- Intrinsic GTPase activity stimulated by the Rab GAP RBG-3 [PMID:17762880 "We found that RBG-3 preferentially stimulates the intrinsic GTPase activity of RAB-7 in both human and C. elegans"].
- TBC-2 accelerates GTP hydrolysis by RAB-5 and more weakly RAB-7 [PMID:20462958 "TBC-2 specifically catalyzes GTP hydrolysis by RAB-5 and to a lesser extent RAB-7"].
- GTP binding is required: GDP-locked T23N fails to rescue rab-7(ok511) [PMID:18351800 "indicating that GTP binding is essential for RAB-7 function"].

## Localization

- Late endosomes and lysosomes in the intestine [PMID:16801682 "RAB-7 labels not only late endosomes but also lysosomes"].
- Appears on maturing endosomes during early-to-late conversion in coelomocytes [PMID:26783301 "conversion of early to late endosomes is marked by a strong decrease or turnover of endosomal PtdIns3P and appearance of RAB-7"].
- Late endosome marker in intestine [PMID:23840591 "RAB-7-positive late endosomes were more abundant and formed aggregates and tubular structures" (in gbf-1 RNAi)]; [PMID:19915558 "late endosome (GFP-RAB-7)"].
- Phagosomes containing apoptotic cells [PMID:18351800 "RAB-7 was recruited to the surface of a phagosome approximately 3 min after PI(3)P appeared on the same phagosome"].

## Processes

- Endosome and endosome-to-lysosome traffic; SAND-1/Mon1 is needed for RAB-7 function at the early-to-late transition but not at lysosomes [PMID:17203072 "The small rab-GTPase RAB-7 acts in endosome and endosome to lysosome traffic"].
- Yolk trafficking in oocytes: rab-7 RNAi mislocalizes YP170::GFP to large peripheral vesicles without blocking uptake [PMID:10588660 "Although Ce-rab7 (RNAi) did not appear to significantly impair the uptake of YP170::GFP into oocytes, YP170::GFP was mislocalized in them"].
- Phagolysosome formation during apoptotic cell clearance, downstream of CED-1 [PMID:18351800 "whose function is essential for promoting the recruitment and fusion of lysosomes to phagosomes"]; acts after RAB-14/UNC-108 [PMID:20921409 "RAB-14 and UNC-108 recruit lysosomes, whereas RAB-7 mediates fusion of lysosomes to phagosomes"].
- Recruits the lysosome scission factor HPO-27 [PMID:38538795 "recruited to lysosomes by RAB-7 and enriched at scission sites"].
- Fat storage: rab-7 loss reduces stored fat, downstream of TUB-1/RBG-3 [PMID:17762880 "either mutation or RNA interference knockdown in rab-7 reduces stored fat"]; an indirect organismal phenotype.

## Phenotypes (not used for NEW process terms)

- rab-7(ok511) m-z- embryos: maternal-effect lethality and persistent cell corpses [PMID:18351800].
- RNAi progeny mildly Dpy [PMID:10588660 "Larvae and adult progeny of Ce-rab7 (RNAi) worms appeared mildly Dpy."].
