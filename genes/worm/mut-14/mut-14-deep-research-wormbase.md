# WormBase gene description for mut-14 (worm)

mut-14 encodes a DEAD box RNA helicase; MUT-14 activity is required redundantly with its paralog SMUT-1, for germline RNAi and endogenous siRNA formation; mut-14 mutant animals exhibit germline transposon activation, a deficient response to RNAi, resistance to co-suppression, and transgene desilencing.
