CC   -!- FUNCTION: Under harsh environmental conditions, larvae enter a
CC       developmentally arrested state known as dauer; TGF-beta-like daf-7 acts
CC       to inhibit dauer larva formation and promote growth (PubMed:37060828,
CC       PubMed:8910282). May be a ligand to cell surface receptor daf-4
CC       (PubMed:8413626). May act as a negative regulator of dauer larva
CC       development by transducing chemosensory information from ASI neurons
CC       (PubMed:8910282). Involved in sensitivity to CO2 levels
CC       (PubMed:18524955). Involved in mate searching behavior of males, acting
CC       in concert with the neuropeptide pdf-1 (PubMed:30024377). In AWC
CC       neurons, acts to promote expression of srsx-3, a member of the GPCR
CC       family (PubMed:20713521). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18524955,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20713521, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30024377,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37060828, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8413626,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8910282}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000250}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the chemosensory neurons, including in
CC       the ASJ neurons in males. Expressed in the ASI neurons
CC       (PubMed:37060828). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30024377,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31264582, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37060828,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8910282}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: When the food/pheromone ratio is high, its level
CC       peaks during the L1 larval stage (PubMed:8910282). Expression is
CC       detected in larvae beginning 4 to 5 hours after hatching, through the
CC       four larval stages, and in adults (PubMed:8910282). Expressed in ASJ
CC       chemosensory neurons in males from the L4 larval stage
CC       (PubMed:31264582). Expressed in ASI neurons during L2 larval
CC       development (PubMed:37060828). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30024377,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37060828, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8910282}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Dauer-inducing pheromone inhibits its expression and
CC       promotes dauer formation, whereas food reactivates its expression and
CC       promotes recovery from the dauer state. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8910282}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the TGF-beta family. {ECO:0000305}.
