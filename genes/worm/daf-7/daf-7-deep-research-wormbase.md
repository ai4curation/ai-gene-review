# WormBase gene description for daf-7 (worm)

daf-7 encodes a member of the transforming growth factor beta superfamily; in C. elegans, DAF-7 functions as part of a signaling pathway that interprets environmental conditions to regulate energy-balance pathways that affect dauer larval formation, adult lifespan, fat metabolism, egg laying, pathogen avoidance behavior, and feeding behavior; daf-7 reporter gene fusions are expressed in the ASI, ADE, and OLQ neurons, and daf-7 expression is induced in both ASI and ASJ chemosensory neurons in response to the pathogenic bacterial strain Pseudomonas aeruginosa PA14.
