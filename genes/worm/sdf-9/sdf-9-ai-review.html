Gene Description

SDF-9 (synthetic dauer formation 9; also EAK-5) is a 345-residue protein consisting almost entirely of a single protein tyrosine phosphatase (PTP)-like domain that has lost the catalytic machinery of the family: the nucleophilic cysteine of the HCxxGxxR(S/T) signature is replaced by serine (Ser223), and the P-loop histidine, P-loop serine/threonine and the phosphotyrosine-recognition tyrosine are also substituted. It is therefore a pseudophosphatase. SDF-9 is expressed specifically in the two XXXL/R head cells, endocrine cells that also express the dafachronic acid-synthesizing cytochrome P450 DAF-9, and it is a peripheral protein of the XXX plasma membrane whose membrane association does not require DAF-2 insulin-receptor signalling. Loss of sdf-9 causes partial, dauer-like larval arrest resembling daf-9 and daf-12 dauer-constitutive mutants, enhanced by cholesterol deprivation and suppressed by DAF-9 overexpression and by daf-16/FoxO loss. Genetically, SDF-9 acts with the membrane-associated EAK-4 and the PTP homolog EAK-6 in a single pathway, in parallel to AKT-1, to promote reproductive development, probably by potentiating DAF-9 dafachronic acid production in XXX cells. Its molecular activity is unknown; proposed roles as a phosphotyrosine-binding adaptor or a stabilizer of activated DAF-2 have not been substantiated biochemically.

Core Functions

Catalytically inactive PTP-like (pseudophosphatase) protein at the cytoplasmic face of the plasma membrane of the XXXL/R endocrine cells, where, together with EAK-4 and EAK-6 and in parallel to AKT-1, it promotes reproductive development and prevents dauer entry, probably by potentiating DAF-9-dependent dafachronic acid production. Its molecular activity is unknown.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: UniProt's CAUTION states the catalytic cysteine is replaced by lysine at position 223, but the current G5EGA9 sequence has serine at 223 (QSARGSSRAG aligned to PTP1B HCSAGIGRSG). Should the CAUTION text be corrected?

Q: Does SDF-9 directly bind DAF-2 (as proposed from allele-specific genetics) or tyrosine-phosphorylated proteins in vivo, given that heterologous co-IP in 293T cells was negative?

Q: Would positive regulation of insulin receptor signaling pathway (GO:0046628) or negative regulation of dauer larval development (GO:0061067) be a more precise process annotation for sdf-9 than dauer larval development, given genetic placement upstream of or in parallel to AGE-1/AKT-1 rather than demonstrated participation?

Q: Does SDF-9 act as the non-catalytic partner of EAK-6 (same PANTHER subfamily SF561), by analogy with the inactive D2 domains of receptor PTPs?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Restore the catalytic cysteine (S223C, with Q222H and A230S) in an sdf-9 rescue construct and test whether a catalytically competent version changes rescue of the dauer phenotype; in parallel, assay recombinant SDF-9 and the restored mutant for PTP activity against pNPP and phosphopeptides.

Experiment: Endogenous tagging of SDF-9 followed by XXX-cell-specific proximity labelling (TurboID) to identify membrane partners (e.g. DAF-2, EAK-4, EAK-6) and test whether SDF-9 controls DAF-9 levels or dafachronic acid output (rescue of sdf-9 Daf-c by exogenous dafachronic acid).

SDF-9 catalytic-residue check

Script: catalytic_residues.py (run with uv run python genes/worm/sdf-9/sdf-9-bioinformatics/catalytic_residues.py
from the repo root). It reads the SDF-9 (G5EGA9) sequence from sdf-9-uniprot.txt, fetches human
PTP1B/PTPN1 (P18031) from UniProt, does a BLOSUM62 local alignment (gap open -10, extend -0.5) of the
PTP1B catalytic region (residues 1-300) against SDF-9, and reports the SDF-9 residue aligned to each PTP1B
landmark. Raw output: catalytic_residues_output.tsv.

Interpretation

  • The catalytic cysteine position is Ser223 in the current SDF-9 sequence. UniProt's CAUTION text
    says "lysine at position 223"; the sequence in sdf-9-uniprot.txt has S at 223, so the CAUTION text is
    inaccurate about the identity of the substituting residue (the conclusion, loss of the nucleophile, holds).
  • Besides Cys->Ser, SDF-9 also lost the P-loop His (->Gln) and the P-loop Ser/Thr (->Ala) and the
    pTyr-recognition Tyr46 (->Val). Loss of multiple catalytic and substrate-recognition residues supports a
    catalytically dead pseudophosphatase.
  • The P-loop arginine (R229) is retained, so a residual phosphate/anion-binding pocket is possible, but the
    pTyr-recognition loop is degenerate; there is no sequence-level basis to assert phosphotyrosine binding.
  • Limitation: pairwise alignment to a single PTP; WPD- and Q-loop assignments fall in gapped regions and are
    low confidence. The P-loop assignment is unambiguous.
