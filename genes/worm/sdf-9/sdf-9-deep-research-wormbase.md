# WormBase gene description for sdf-9 (worm)

sdf-9 encodes a protein tyrosine phosphatase, paralogous to EAK-6; sdf-9 acts in parallel to akt-1 to regulate insulin-like signaling and dauer formation and may promote DAF-9 activity by means of steroid hormone signalling; sdf-9 dauer larvae resemble those of daf-9 and daf-12 dauer-constitutive mutants, and these Daf-c mutants are all enhanced by cholesterol deprivation; SDF-9 is expressed in the neuroendocrine XXXL/R cells, where it associates with the plasma membrane; ablation of the XXXL/R cells in wild-type L1 larvae also causes daf-9-like pseudodauer larvae.
