# WormBase gene description for abu-1 (worm)

abu-1 encodes a transmembrane protein with a predicted signal sequence, a glutamine/asparagine-rich domain and multiple cysteine-rich repeats (DUF139); abu-1 expression is induced by blockage of the endoplasmic reticulum unfolded protein response, and ABU-1 may help protect the organism from damage by improperly folded nascent protein; when expressed in COS1 cells, ABU-1 localizes to the endoplasmic reticulum membrane.
