CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor (PubMed:30291162). Binds to DNA sequence
CC       motif 5'-CATNNNNAAATGCAT-3' as a heterodimer with egl-46
CC       (PubMed:30291162). Represses expression of genes involved in
CC       differentiation of touch receptor neurons (TRN), probably acting as a
CC       heterodimer with egl-46, perhaps by occupying similar cis-regulatory
CC       elements as an unc-86/mec-3 heterodimer (PubMed:30291162). Plays a role
CC       in cell fate specification of neurons, including the hook neuron HOB,
CC       and touch receptor neurons (PubMed:12954713, PubMed:30291162). Involved
CC       in male mating behavior, acting in concert with egl-46, via modulation
CC       of expression of polycystins lov-1 and pkd-2, homeodomain protein ceh-
CC       26, and neuropeptide-like protein nlp-8 (PubMed:12954713). Acts
CC       upstream of egl-46 to prevent touch cell differentiation in FLP neurons
CC       (PubMed:11274062). Plays a role in neuron differentiation by repressing
CC       the expression of zag-1 in FLP neurons, probably acting as a
CC       heterodimer with egl-46; because zag-1 represses expression of egl-46
CC       and egl-44, together these proteins form a bistable, negative-feedback
CC       loop that regulates the choice between neuronal fates
CC       (PubMed:30291162). Also promotes HSN neuron development
CC       (PubMed:11274062). In association with egl-46, regulates cell cycle
CC       exit in the neuronal Q cell lineage (PubMed:23946438). Plays a role in
CC       specifying commissural dendrites of the PVD nociceptive neurons, acting
CC       in concert with egl-46 (PubMed:29031632). May be involved in thermal
CC       stress response downstream of yap-1 (PubMed:23396260).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11274062, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12954713,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23946438, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29031632,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30291162, ECO:0000303|PubMed:23396260}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via N-terminus) with egl-46 (via C-terminus); the
CC       interaction is direct; the interaction may regulate transcription
CC       (PubMed:23946438, PubMed:30291162). Interacts with yap-1 (via WW
CC       domain); the interaction may regulate transcription (PubMed:23396260).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23396260, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23946438,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30291162}.

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in HSN neurons in embryos and in the FLP
CC       neurons from the L1 stage through to adults. Not expressed in touch
CC       cells. Also expressed in larval hypodermis, intestine, pharyngeal
CC       muscle and other neurons. In adults expression is lost from some
CC       neurons, is weaker in the hypodermis but remains in the intestine
CC       (PubMed:11274062). Expressed in HOB neuron, ray neurons RnA and RnB,
CC       and the ray structural cell, Rnst; rays are male-specific genital
CC       sensilla (simple sense organs) (PubMed:12954713).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11274062, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12954713}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed from before gastrulation through to
CC       adult (PubMed:11274062). Expressed in HOB, PVX, and PVY neurons
CC       (PubMed:12954713). Expressed in most descendants of the ray precursor
CC       cells (Rn), including the ray neurons (RnA and RnB) and the ray
CC       structure cells at the L4 larval stage; rays are male-specific genital
CC       sensilla (simple sense organs) (PubMed:12954713).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11274062, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12954713}.
