# WormBase gene description for mcm-4 (worm)

mcm-4 encodes a member of the MCM2/3/5 family with similarity to human MCM4 minichromosome maintenance deficient 4 protein, and affects embryonic viability, nuclear morphology in early embryos, and larval viability.
