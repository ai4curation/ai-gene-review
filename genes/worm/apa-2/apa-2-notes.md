# apa-2 (apt-4) — AP-2 complex subunit alpha (Q22601, TrEMBL) — curation notes

## Identity
- `apa-2` (T20B5.1; older alias `apt-4`) encodes the alpha-adaptin large subunit of the AP-2 clathrin adaptor complex [PMID:23482940 "elegans, α-adaptin is encoded by the apa-2 gene (Figure 1A)."]. Two likely-null alleles exist: b1044 (925 bp deletion in the trunk) and ox422 (Lys215 premature stop); neither produces full-length protein [PMID:23482940 "We did not detect full-length APA-2 protein from either of these alleles (Figure 1—figure supplement 1), and they are likely to be null mutations."].
- APA-2::GFP is expressed in most cells and is highest in the nervous system [PMID:23482940 "The apa-2 gene appears to be expressed in most cells of the animal, and is highly expressed in the nervous system (Figure 2B,C)."].

## Molecular role within AP-2
- Alpha-adaptin forms a hemicomplex with sigma2; the alpha/sigma2 half remains localised and partially functional without mu2, and the alpha appendage ("ear") recruits accessory endocytic proteins such as dynamin, AP180, epsin and eps15 [PMID:23482940 "Conversely, in μ2 mutants, the alpha/sigma2 hemicomplex is localized and is partially functional."] [PMID:10588660 "The α-adaptin subunit is thought to mediate essential interactions, directly or indirectly, with a host of associated endocytosis factors, including dynamin"].
- Cargo specificity: recovery of di-leucine cargo depends on alpha more than mu2, whereas MIG-14/Wntless (tyrosine motif) depends on mu2 [PMID:23482940 "These data suggest that recovery of di-leucine cargo depends on α-adaptin more than μ2-adaptin."] [PMID:23482940 "In the absence of α-adaptin, MIG-14::GFP is only weakly mislocalized on the basolateral surface (27% compared to μ2 mutants; Figure 5F,G)."].
- Accessory-protein interactions documented in the worm (all via the alpha subunit): RME-6 (Vps9-domain RAB-5 GEF) co-immunoprecipitates with alpha-adaptin and carries a WxxF alpha-ear motif [PMID:15895077 "We found that α-adaptin did indeed coimmunoprecipitate with GFP::RME-6, showing a physical link of RME-6 to clathrin-coated pits."]; RME-4 (DENN protein, RAB-35 GEF) binds AP-2 (abstract) [PMID:18354496 "GFP-RME-4 also physically interacts with AP-2, is enriched on clathrin-coated pits, and requires clathrin but not RAB-5 for cortical association."]; DAB-1 binds clathrin and APT-4 in vitro (abstract) [PMID:16497226 "Ce-DAB-1 binds to clathrin and APT-4, the C. elegans homologue of the alpha-adaptin subunit of adaptor protein 2 (AP2)"]; CED-1 intracellular domain and CED-6 bind APA-2 directly and co-IP in vivo [PMID:23696751 "The GST-fused intracellular region of CED-1 (CED-1C, amino acids 933–1111), but not GST, directly interacted with 35S-labeled APA-2 synthesized by in vitro translation (Figure 4A)."].

## Localisation
- Coelomocytes: mRFP::alpha-adaptin marks plasma-membrane clathrin-coated pits together with CHC-1, unevenly distributed around the plasma membrane [PMID:15895077 "In contrast, we found strong colocalization of GFP::RME-6 with mRFP1::CHC-1 and mRFP1::α-adaptin on or near the PM of coelomocytes."] [PMID:15895077 "In particular we focused on two markers for PM clathin-coated pits: clathrin heavy chain (CHC-1) and α-adaptin (APT-4)"].
- Synapses: APT-4::GFP is predominantly at synaptic puncta and behaves as a periactive-zone / endocytic-intermediate marker whose abundance rises in synaptojanin and endophilin mutants [PMID:19043554 "We observed increased punctal fluorescence of APT-4 α2 adaptin and ITSN-1 intersectin in unc-26 synaptojanin and unc-57 endophilin A mutant synapses (Table S2), consistent with the accumulation of endocytic intermediates in these mutants"] [PMID:22588719 "periactive zone proteins (SNN-1/synapsin, APT-4/adaptin, and ITSN-1/intersectin) in the DA class of motor neurons"]. Tagged alpha-adaptin remains at synapses and the coelomocyte plasma membrane in mu2 mutants [PMID:23482940 "In apm-2 mutants, tagged α-adaptin is still localized to the synapse (Figure 6D) and the plasma membrane of coelomocytes."].
- Phagosomes: APA-2::GFP surrounds apoptotic germ-cell corpses in a CED-1/CED-6- and clathrin-dependent manner [PMID:23696751 "Furthermore, in ced-1(e1735), ced-1(RNAi) and ced-6(RNAi) animals, few germ cell corpses were labeled by APA-2::GFP or mCherry::CHC-1, suggesting that CED-1 and CED-6 are required for phagosomal association of AP2 and clathrin (Figure 2C and 2D)."].

## Functions and phenotypes
- Yolk endocytosis: alpha-adaptin RNAi abolishes YP170::GFP uptake, and embryos from such mothers die [PMID:10588660 "elegans clathrin heavy chain, α-adaptin, and β-adaptin RNAi all produced similar defects in oocytes and embryos."] [PMID:10588660 "In all of these cases, late-stage oocytes were smaller than in wild type, and embryos proved inviable."]. However, apa-2 null mutants are viable with near-normal embryonic viability (2.4% vs 1.1% lethality) and still endocytose yolk, so AP-2 assists DAB-1 rather than being absolutely required [PMID:23482940 "(A) Yolk protein, YP170::GFP is endocytosed by maturing oocytes in the wild type and both apa-2 mutants."] [PMID:23482940 "Thus, the AP2 complex appears to assist Disabled for yolk endocytosis, and α- and μ2-adaptin do not contribute differentially to this process."].
- Synaptic vesicle endocytosis: loss of alpha alone reduces synaptic vesicle number ~30% and evoked amplitude ~25–42%; complete loss of AP-2 (alpha + mu2) reduces vesicles 70% and produces large vacuoles [PMID:23482940 "Mutations in either α or μ2 subunits alone reduce the number of synaptic vesicles by about 30%; however, simultaneous loss of both α and μ2 subunits leads to a 70% reduction in synaptic vesicles and the presence of large vacuoles."] [PMID:23482940 "In mutants lacking apa-2 in the nervous system, synaptic vesicle numbers are reduced to 71% in acetylcholine neurons and 59% in GABA neurons (Figure 8B; Figure 8—figure supplement 1)."].
- Whole-animal phenotypes: variable dumpy (rescued by neuronal expression), jowls, Egl, mildly Unc; apa-2 apm-2 double mutants are almost fully embryonic lethal [PMID:23482940 "The α-adaptin mutants are egg-laying defective and mildly uncoordinated; they crawl forward well but are slightly jerky as they move backward."] [PMID:23482940 "Indeed, when the apa-2 and apm-2 mutations are combined, only 4.3% of the double mutants coming from a heterozygote are viable and the brood size of these survivors is reduced to 1.4% compared to the wild type (Figure 4A,B)."]. apa-2 mutants share the jowls phenotype with fcho-1, and fcho-1 bypass suppressors include mutations in the alpha solenoid [PMID:25303366 "(3) residues in the alpha subunit that are found in the helical solenoid that lies across the top of the complex"].
- Apoptotic cell clearance: apa-2 RNAi increases germ-cell corpses; APA-2::GFP rescues [PMID:23696751 "Likewise, APA-2::GFP driven by the apa-2 promoter (yqEx481) or the ced-1 promoter (yqIs99) rescued the accumulation of germ cell corpses in animals treated with RNAi complementary to the 3′ untranslated region (3′ UTR) of apa-2 (Figure S2C)."].
- DAB-1 localisation in oocytes depends on clathrin and AP-2 (abstract only) [PMID:17636000 "The localisation of DAB-1 in oocytes is itself dependent upon clathrin and AP2, consistent with DAB-1 acting as a clathrin-associated sorting protein during yolk protein endocytosis."].

## Curation decisions
- GO:0032794 "GTPase activating protein binding" (IPI with RME-6): RME-6 is a Vps9-domain RAB-5 guanine-nucleotide exchange factor, not a GAP, and GO has no GEF-binding term; the row is removed as mislabelled (the interaction itself stands and is recorded above).
- GO:0009792 embryo development (IMP, alpha RNAi): contradicted by viable apa-2 null strains with near wild-type embryonic viability; the RNAi lethality most likely reflects yolk deprivation of oocytes. Removed.
- Four generic protein-binding rows (RME-6, DAB-1, RME-4, CED-6) are removed as uninformative per project policy; the interactions are genuine alpha-appendage/accessory-protein contacts.

## Additional literature surfaced by deep research (not in GOA; see apa-2-deep-research-falcon.md)
- Quantified dileucine-cargo defect: an intestinal eGFP-CD4 ExxxLL reporter accumulates ~7-fold at the basolateral membrane in apa-2(ox422) [file:worm/apa-2/apa-2-deep-research-falcon.md "An intestinal eGFP–CD4 reporter carrying an ExxxLL signal accumulated at the basolateral membrane in *apa-2(ox422)* animals, with fluorescence of 14,907 ± 990 versus 1,998 ± 275 in wild type (*p*<0.0001)."].
- AP-2 subunit mutants (apa-2, apm-2, aps-2) reduce GLR-1 glutamate receptor abundance at ventral-cord synapses via an early secretory/endosomal step (Garafalo et al. 2015, DOI 10.1091/mbc.e14-06-1048).
- NCAP-1/NECAP antagonises FCHO-1 by recognising open, phosphorylated AP-2 (Beacham et al. 2018); APA-2::GFP was the reporter for complex dynamics.
- CED-1's intracellular domain binds APA-2 but not the other AP-2 subunits tested, making alpha the physical bridge to the phagocytic receptor [file:worm/apa-2/apa-2-deep-research-falcon.md "The intracellular region of CED-1 binds APA-2 in vitro, whereas the other tested AP-2 subunits did not detectably bind CED-1."].
