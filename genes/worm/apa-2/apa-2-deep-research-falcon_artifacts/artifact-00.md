APA-2::GFP associates with germ-cell-corpse phagosomes, and this labeling is reduced by `chc-1` RNAi. | **CED-1 binding and apoptotic-cell clearance** | The intracellular region of the engulfment receptor CED-1 binds APA-2 directly in vitro, whereas other tested AP-2 subunits did not. AP-2/clathrin depletion impairs corpse internalization and phagosome maturation; complex-level measurements include >120-min corpse persistence and reduced actin recruitment from 77–85% to 35–41%. | **FCHO-1 activates AP-2** | In `fcho-1` mutants, AP-2 remains membrane-associated but fails to cluster; membrane dwell time falls from approximately **35 s to <10 s**. FCHO-1 directly binds the AP-2 core through a conserved 90-residue activator region and promotes the open state that exposes membrane- and cargo-binding sites. These experiments concern AP-2 as a complex rather than a unique catalytic activity of APA-2. (pqac-00000007) | AP-2-complex regulation | **High** for AP-2; **moderate** when attributed specifically to APA-2 | Hollopeter et al., *eLife*, October 2014; https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.03648 |
