# tmem-17 (Q23481) notes

Deep research: falcon timed out and the perplexity fallback was unavailable, so no deep-research file exists. Sources are the cached PMID:26982032 and PMID:40841990 (both full text) and the PMC full text of PMID:26595381.

## Key findings
- TZ localization is CEP-290 dependent; in cep-290 mutants TMEM-17 stays in the dendrite [PMID:26982032 "TMEM-218::GFP and other MKS module components (TMEM-231::GFP, TMEM-17::GFP) no longer localise to the TZ in the cep-290 mutant"].
- Peripheral (layer 3) MKS-module protein [PMID:26982032 "This hierarchical definition implies that TMEM-17, TMEM-67 (MKS-3), TMEM-218, and TMEM-237 are more “peripheral,”"]. Its TZ localization requires TMEM-107 (PMID:26595381 full text).
- Not required for MKS-2 localization or the ciliary gate; the tmem-17;nphp-4 double mutant is Dyf [PMID:40841990].
- Patient-equivalent missense alleles (R73W, G80V, N81K) reduce TZ abundance and function [PMID:40841990].
