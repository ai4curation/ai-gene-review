CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a critical role in several secretory processes,
CC       including cuticle secretion and neurotransmitter release, and probably
CC       assists in neuronal membrane maturation or the final stages of neuronal
CC       differentiation (PubMed:1945043, PubMed:9442061). Plays a role in
CC       synaptic vesicle docking and tethering through its association with
CC       unc-18 (PubMed:21423527). Through binding to unc-18 mediates the
CC       release of the neurotransmitter acetylcholine from cholinergic motor
CC       neurons, and thereby promotes locomotory behaviors (PubMed:18596236).
CC       Essential for embryonic viability and development. Has a role in dauer
CC       formation and adult life span (PubMed:10377425). Required for
CC       locomotion (PubMed:10377425). Probably by regulating neuronal
CC       transmission downstream of lin-3 and receptor lin-23 and phospholipase
CC       plc-3 and upstream of innexin unc-7 and egl-4/PKG in ALA neurons,
CC       involved in the decrease in pharyngeal pumping during the quiescent
CC       state that precedes each larval molt (PubMed:17891142).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10377425, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17891142,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18596236, ECO:0000269|PubMed:1945043,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21423527, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9442061}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via N-terminus, in open or in closed conformation)
CC       with unc-18; the interaction is direct (PubMed:10366611,
CC       PubMed:18596236, PubMed:19032153, PubMed:8939465, PubMed:9442061).
CC       Interaction in open conformation with unc-18 promotes synaptic vesicle
CC       docking and tethering (PubMed:21423527). Interaction via N-terminus
CC       with unc-18 mediates the secretion of the neurotransmitter
CC       acetylcholine from cholinergic motor neurons (PubMed:18596236).
CC       Interaction with unc-18 is reduced in the presence of unc-13
CC       (PubMed:10366611). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10366611,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18596236, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19032153,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21423527, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8939465,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9442061}.
