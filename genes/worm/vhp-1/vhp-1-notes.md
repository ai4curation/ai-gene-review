- The direct negative VHP-1-to-KGB-1 and VHP-1-to-PMK-1 causal edges are mechanistically defensible for a MAP kinase phosphatase acting on phosphorylated MAPKs.
- The `part_of` `GO:1900425` negative regulation of defense response to bacterium context should retain an age/development caveat: reduced VHP-1 during development increases P. aeruginosa resistance through PMK-1, while adult VHP-1 loss decreases resistance through KGB-1 and DAF-16.
