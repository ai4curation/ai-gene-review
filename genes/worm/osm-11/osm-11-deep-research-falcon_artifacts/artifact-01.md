| Associative memory and cognitive aging | Acts as a secreted hypodermis-to-neuron signal linking reduced DAF-2 insulin/IGF-1 signaling to neuronal LIN-12/Notch–LAG-1–SEL-8 signaling and CREB/CRH-1-dependent memory transcription. | Loss of `osm-11` blocks the enhanced memory caused by hypodermal DAF-2 depletion; age-related reduction of `osm-11` is associated with cognitive decline. | Hypodermal OSM-11 overexpression improves associative memory, including learning and memory in aged worms. LIN-12 in RIG-associated interneurons and neuronal CREB are required for this benefit. | (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000028, pqac-00000029, pqac-00000031) |
| Hypertonic-stress physiology | Normally restrains the hypodermal hypertonic-stress response by limiting `gpdh-1` induction and glycerol accumulation. | Loss constitutively increases `gpdh-1` expression and glycerol accumulation, increasing resistance to damaging or lethal hypertonic conditions. | OSM-11 belongs to a hypodermally expressed class of extracellular `osm` proteins rather than the ASH-neuron-autonomous osmosensory machinery. Its molecular connection to `gpdh-1` remains unresolved. | (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000020, pqac-00000036) |
| Osmotic-avoidance behavior | Indirectly couples peripheral osmotic state to the ASH sensory-neuron avoidance circuit, probably through inter-tissue signaling. | `osm-11` mutants fail to avoid hypertonic stimuli normally, producing the osmotic-avoidance-abnormal phenotype. | Because `osm-11` is expressed in the hypodermis rather than ASH neurons, its behavioral effect is inferred to involve a diffusible or paracrine hypodermis-to-neuron signal; the intermediary remains unidentified. | (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000020, pqac-00000036) |
| Salinity-stress tolerance | Modulates metabolic, mitochondrial, antioxidant, calcium-signaling, and lipid-handling programs during hypersaline exposure. | Recent preprint data report substantially improved survival of independent `osm-11` loss-of-function mutants at 30‰ salinity after 24–48 hours. | Loss increases fatty-acid-metabolism and cytochrome-P450 programs, suppresses several calcium-signaling genes, and reduces mitochondrial fragmentation. `acdh-12`, `acs-17`, and `ugt-15` are required for much of the enhanced tolerance; `gpx-1`, `gpx-7`, `dct-1`, ferroptosis, and mitophagy also modify survival. These findings remain preprint evidence. | (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000022) |
| Vulval development and cell-fate specification | Functions as a soluble DOS-motif co-ligand that facilitates ligand-dependent activation of LIN-12/Notch and secondary vulval-precursor-cell fate. | Null or strong loss-of-function alleles cause defective or misshapen vulvae, inappropriate primary-fate-marker expression, reduced secondary-fate-marker expression, and egg retention; defects are enhanced by loss of other DOS proteins or DSL ligands. | Genetic epistasis places OSM-11 upstream of or at LIN-12 activation. OSM-11 interacts with LIN-12 extracellular EGF repeats 1–6 in a two-hybrid assay and can act at a distance; mammalian DLK1 partially substitutes for it. Direct in-vivo receptor binding is not conclusive. | (pqac-00000052, pqac-00000053, pqac-00000054, pqac-00000055, pqac-00000056) |
| Adult neuromuscular-junction signaling | Positively modulates LIN-12/Notch-dependent GABAergic signaling and excitation–inhibition balance, potentially through transcriptional regulation of the GABA-B receptor subunit `gbb-2`. | Loss or adult RNAi causes aldicarb hypersensitivity and faster paralysis; overexpression causes aldicarb resistance and slower paralysis. Loss also produces levamisole hypersensitivity. | Manipulation after neuronal and muscle cell fates are established reproduces or rescues the phenotype, demonstrating a physiological role distinct from developmental fate specification. | (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000014, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000023) |
| Inter-tissue signaling and extracellular localization | Serves as a small, secreted, diffusible, non-cell-autonomous signal expressed prominently by the adult hypodermis and also reported in seam cells and the spermatheca. | Loss disrupts multiple distant outputs—including neuronal memory, osmotic behavior, and vulval patterning—consistent with failed extracellular signaling rather than loss of enzymatic activity. | OSM-11 contains an N-terminal signal peptide and cysteine-rich DOS motif but lacks a canonical DSL domain. The mature protein is approximately 18.9 kDa, migrates near 20.7 kDa, is recovered from conditioned medium, and may be O-glycosylated. | (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000043, pqac-00000047, pqac-00000049, pqac-00000050) |
| Stress-responsive transcription and longevity | Has been linked to SKN-1/Nrf-dependent regulation during embryogenesis, adult longevity, and heat or oxidative-stress responses. | Perturbation alters stress-response and lifespan-related phenotypes, although the precise receptor-to-SKN-1 mechanism is not established in the cited evidence. | This branch appears context dependent: recent preprint evidence suggests that enhanced salt tolerance in `osm-11` mutants is SKN-1-independent, distinguishing it from previously reported heat- and oxidative-stress functions. | (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000037, pqac-00000021) |
| Insulin/FOXO pathway integration | Reduced hypodermal DAF-2 signaling is proposed to activate DAF-16/FOXO-dependent `osm-11` expression, positioning OSM-11 downstream of peripheral insulin signaling and upstream of neuronal Notch. | Removing `osm-11` suppresses the memory benefit of hypodermal DAF-2 depletion; a direct OSM-11–DAF-16 interaction has not been shown. | The best-supported model is DAF-2 reduction → hypodermal DAF-16 activity → increased OSM-11 → neuronal LIN-12/LAG-1/SEL-8 → CREB-dependent memory genes. Evidence linking OSM-11 to DAF-16 in salinity stress is indirect. | (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000026, pqac-00000038, pqac-00000039) |
