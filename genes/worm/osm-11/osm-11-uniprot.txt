CC   -!- FUNCTION: Probable secreted lin-12/Notch ligand or co-ligand involved
CC       in the mediation of Notch signaling (PubMed:18700817, PubMed:21549604).
CC       Involved in the lin-12/Notch pathway signaling of cell fate in vulval
CC       precursor cells (VPCs), acting redundantly with dsl-1 and lag-2
CC       (PubMed:18700817). Required for normal octanol avoidance response,
CC       acting via both lin-12/Notch and glp-1/Notch signaling pathways in
CC       neurons, in concert with lag-2 (PubMed:21549604). Involved in
CC       regulation of sleep-like quiescence during the larval to adult
CC       transition, acting via Notch receptor activation and in parallel with
CC       EGF signaling (PubMed:21549604). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700817,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21549604}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: May interact with lin-12/Notch receptor.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700817}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Apical cell membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700817}; Peripheral membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700817}. Note=Concentrated on the apical surface
CC       of vulval precursor cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700817}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in coelomocytes (at protein level).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21549604}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in VPCs and hypodermal cells during
CC       larval development (PubMed:18700817). Expressed in VPCs of L3 stage
CC       larvae prior to and during cell fate specification and in the seam
CC       cells of L1 stage larvae and adult animals (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:18700817). Expressed in developing uterus of L4 stage larvae
CC       and in the spermatheca (at protein level) (PubMed:18700817).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700817}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: RNAi-mediated knockdown causes moderately
CC       abnormal vulval development (PubMed:18700817). RNAi-mediated knockdown
CC       causes defects in octanol response (PubMed:21549604).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700817, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21549604}.
