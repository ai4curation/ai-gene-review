Gene Description

AKT-1 is one of two Caenorhabditis elegans orthologs of the AGC-family serine/threonine kinase Akt/PKB. Downstream of the DAF-2 insulin/IGF-1-like receptor and the AGE-1 PI3K, its pleckstrin homology domain binds PIP3 at the plasma membrane, where it is activated by PDK-1; it forms a kinase complex with AKT-2 and SGK-1. AKT-1 phosphorylates the FOXO transcription factor DAF-16 and the Nrf-like factor SKN-1, excluding them from the nucleus, and together with akt-2 is required to prevent constitutive dauer arrest. It also promotes cell survival, inhibiting CEP-1/p53-dependent germline apoptosis after DNA damage and embryonic apoptosis, drives germ cell proliferation via phosphorylation of the checkpoint protein MDF-1, and phosphorylates the TRPV channel OSM-9 in ASH neurons. AKT-1 activity is restrained by the PP2A regulatory subunit PPTR-1. It is expressed in neurons and other tissues remodelled during dauer formation.

  AKT-1 is one of two Caenorhabditis elegans orthologs of the AGC-family serine/threonine
  kinase Akt/PKB. Downstream of the DAF-2 insulin/IGF-1-like receptor and the AGE-1 PI3K,
  its pleckstrin homology domain binds PIP3 at the plasma membrane, where it is activated
  by PDK-1; it forms a kinase complex with AKT-2 and SGK-1. AKT-1 phosphorylates the FOXO
  transcription factor DAF-16 and the Nrf-like factor SKN-1, excluding them from the nucleus,
  and together with akt-2 is required to prevent constitutive dauer arrest. It also promotes
  cell survival, inhibiting CEP-1/p53-dependent germline apoptosis after DNA damage and embryonic
  apoptosis, drives germ cell proliferation via phosphorylation of the checkpoint protein
  MDF-1, and phosphorylates the TRPV channel OSM-9 in ASH neurons. AKT-1 activity is restrained
  by the PP2A regulatory subunit PPTR-1. It is expressed in neurons and other tissues remodelled
  during dauer formation.
