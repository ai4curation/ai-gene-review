Gene Description

HRDE-1 (heritable RNAi deficient 1, also WAGO-9) is a germline-specific nuclear Argonaute of the nematode-specific WAGO clade. It carries PAZ and PIWI domains but, like other WAGOs, lacks the slicer catalytic residues. HRDE-1 is loaded with RdRP-derived secondary 22G endo-siRNAs, including those produced downstream of piRNA (PRG-1/21U-RNA) and exogenous dsRNA targeting, and acts in germ-cell nuclei as the specificity factor of the nuclear RNAi (NRDE) pathway. siRNA-guided HRDE-1 recruits NRDE-2 to nascent target transcripts and, with NRDE-1/2/4 and the SET-25/SET-32/HPL-2 chromatin machinery, directs H3K9 trimethylation and co-transcriptional silencing of target loci. Acting in each inheriting generation, HRDE-1 propagates RNAi- and piRNA-initiated silencing across generations; it also visits perinuclear nuage, where it stimulates further small RNA amplification. Loss of HRDE-1 causes progressive loss of germline silencing and a temperature- sensitive mortal germline phenotype.

Core Functions

HRDE-1 is a germline nuclear Argonaute loaded with RdRP-derived 22G siRNAs. In germ-cell nuclei it acts as the sequence-specificity factor that recruits NRDE-2 and the downstream NRDE factors to nascent target transcripts. This directs H3K9me3 deposition and co-transcriptional silencing that is propagated across generations.
