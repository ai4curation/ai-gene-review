CC   -!- FUNCTION: Argonaute protein which is involved in the endogenous small
CC       interfering RNA (endo-siRNA) pathway and is required for RNA-mediated
CC       gene silencing (RNAi) in the germline (PubMed:22810588,
CC       PubMed:28533440). Interacts with secondary 22G-RNAs in an hrde-2-
CC       dependent manner; 22G-RNAs are RNA-dependent RNA polymerase-derived
CC       endo-siRNAs, typically 22 nucleotides in length with a 5'-guanosine
CC       residue (PubMed:22810588, PubMed:28533440). Plays a key role in
CC       transgenerational epigenetic inheritance and germline immortality
CC       (PubMed:22810588, PubMed:37083324). May be involved in
CC       transgenerational gene silencing both by inducing subnuclear-co-
CC       localization of target genes into heterochromatin and by activation of
CC       small RNA amplification in the nuage (PubMed:37083324).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22810588, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28533440,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37083324}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm, Cytoplasmic ribonucleoprotein granule
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37083324}. Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22810588}.
CC       Note=Shuttles to and from the nucleus to the peri-nuclear region of the
CC       cytoplasm called nuage (PubMed:37083324). Co-localizes with mut-16,
CC       probably to subdomains of the nuage known as Mutator foci
CC       (PubMed:37083324). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37083324}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the nuclei of male and female germ
CC       cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22810588}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mortal germline (Mrt) phenotype in which there is
CC       a progressive decline in fertility with each generation at 25 degrees
CC       Celsius, and after 2 to 3 generations the brood size is 25% that of
CC       wild-type counterparts (PubMed:22810588, PubMed:28533440). Fertility in
CC       these mutants may be restored when transferred to a 20 degrees Celsius
CC       environment (PubMed:28533440). Progressive decline in fertility is
CC       probably due to defects in gametogenesis causing a failure to produce
CC       mature oocytes or sperm (PubMed:22810588). Defective RNAi inheritance
CC       (PubMed:22810588, PubMed:28533440). Abolishes RNAi-mediated recruitment
CC       of nrde-2 to germline pre-messenger RNAs (PubMed:22810588). Exhibits
CC       depletion of repressive chromatin mark H3K9me3 in specific germline-
CC       expressed genes, especially those believed to be targets of the nuclear
CC       RNAi defective factors (PubMed:22810588). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22810588,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28533440}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the argonaute family. WAGO subfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
