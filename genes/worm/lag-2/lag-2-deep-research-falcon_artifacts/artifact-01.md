| Domain Name | Location/Position | Structural Features | Functional Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| MNNL/C2 domain | N-terminal extracellular region; MNNL region is approximately 105 amino acids | Predicted C2-like fold; canonical C2 domains form an eight-stranded antiparallel β-sandwich and can bind membrane phospholipids. LAG-2-specific calcium dependence remains unresolved. (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000011, pqac-00000014) | Required for productive LAG-2 signaling in functional-rescue experiments; likely provides a receptor-contact surface and helps orient or stabilize the ectodomain at the membrane. (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000013) |
| DSL domain | Extracellular; immediately C-terminal to the MNNL/C2 region | Defining Delta/Serrate/LAG-2 module containing six cysteines arranged into three disulfide bonds; adopts an EGF-related but distinct fold. (pqac-00000008) | Essential Notch-receptor interaction and signaling module: deleting it abolishes rescue of Notch-related mutant phenotypes. It mediates engagement of LIN-12 and GLP-1, initiating receptor proteolysis and downstream signaling. (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000008) |
| EGF-like repeats | Extracellular; downstream of the DSL domain and before the membrane-spanning segment | Repeated, disulfide-stabilized EGF-like modules forming part of the elongated ectodomain; some canonical ligand EGF repeats can bind calcium. (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000012) | Support ectodomain architecture, receptor recognition, or presentation, but appear modulatory rather than strictly essential in LAG-2 because deletion of its EGF-like region did not abolish phenotypic rescue in the reported assays. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009) |
| Transmembrane domain | Single membrane-spanning segment near the C terminus | Hydrophobic anchor characteristic of a single-pass transmembrane DSL ligand. (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000010) | Retains LAG-2 at the signal-sending cell surface for juxtacrine signaling. Membrane association is functionally important: a soluble ectodomain did not rescue *lag-2* null lethality, whereas GPI-mediated membrane tethering restored partial activity. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000051, pqac-00000056) |
| Intracellular domain | Short cytoplasmic C-terminal tail following the transmembrane segment | Variable cytosolic region; unlike the conserved extracellular receptor-binding modules, no specific catalytic domain is established. (pqac-00000012) | May influence trafficking, ubiquitination, or endocytic regulation, but is dispensable for tested *C. elegans* signaling events: LAG-2 lacking its intracellular domain, or intracellular lysines used for ubiquitination, can still activate Notch because LIN-12 and GLP-1 have relatively low force thresholds. (pqac-00000055, pqac-00000057) |
