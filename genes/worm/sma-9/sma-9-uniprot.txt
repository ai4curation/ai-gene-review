CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor, probably acting as a transcriptional
CC       activator and repressor, involved in the TGF-beta-like dbl-1 signaling
CC       pathway (PubMed:14627718, PubMed:17397820, PubMed:20687916). Plays a
CC       role in regulation of body size, and patterning of male-specific
CC       genital sensilla (simple sense organs), known as rays, and mating-
CC       associated structures, spicules (PubMed:14627718). Required for the
CC       dorsoventral patterning of the postembryonic mesodermal lineage (M
CC       lineage), acting by antagonizing the TGF-beta-like dbl-1 signaling
CC       pathway, in part by repressing expression of transcription factor unc-
CC       130 (PubMed:16790477, PubMed:18036582, PubMed:29155044). Involved in
CC       egg-laying, perhaps via modulation of cholinergic neurotransmission
CC       (PubMed:25407930). Involved in production of reactive oxygen species
CC       (ROS), acting downstream of the dbl-1 signaling pathway
CC       (PubMed:20687916). Plays a role in the mitochondrial unfolded protein
CC       response (mtUPR) (PubMed:20687916). May play a role in modulating
CC       lifespan and in responses to proteotoxic stress (PubMed:25407930).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14627718, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16790477,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17397820, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18036582,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20687916, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25407930,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29155044}.
