## Key findings
- FTT-2 binds DAF-16 and sequesters it in the cytoplasm. ftt-2 RNAi (but not par-5 RNAi) enhances daf-2 dauer formation and causes DAF-16 nuclear accumulation [PMID:17098225 "Our results indicate that FTT-2 binds to DAF-16 in C. elegans and regulates DAF-16 by sequestering it in the cytoplasm"].
- 14-3-3 proteins bind SIR-2.1 and are required for sir-2.1-driven lifespan extension. This acts in parallel to insulin signaling [PMID:16777605 "sir-2.1 and the 14-3-3 genes are not required for the regulation of life span by the insulin-like signaling pathway"].
- ftt-2 knockdown causes YAP-1 nuclear accumulation [PMID:23396260].
