# WormBase gene description for dyf-13 (worm)

dyf-13 encodes a ciliary protein, belonging to a conserved butunfamiliar protein family, that is required for cilial function andformation, undergoes IFT, and might represent a core component of theOSM-3 kinesin IFT pathway; dyf-13 is expressed in AWB neurons; dyf-13mutations affect morphology of the amphid and phasmid neurons,chemotaxis, dauer formation, growth, male mating, and hermaphroditebrood size; dyf-13 requires DAF-19 for its transcription.
