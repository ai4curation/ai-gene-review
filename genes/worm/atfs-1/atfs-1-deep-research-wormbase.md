# WormBase gene description for atfs-1 (worm)

atfs-1 encodes a bZip transcription factor; ATFS-1 is required for the mitochondrial unfolded protein response (UPRmt); in wild-type animals, ATFS-1 is imported into the mitochondria and degraded, but in response to perturbed mitchondrial protein folding and decreased mitochondrial import efficiency, some ATFS-1 accumulates in the cytosol and then, traffics to the nucleus.
