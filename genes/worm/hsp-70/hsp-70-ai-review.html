Gene Description

HSP-70 (C12C8.1) is a strictly stress-inducible cytosolic member of the HSP70 chaperone family, the C. elegans counterpart of the mammalian inducible HSPA1A/HSPA1B proteins. It is essentially undetectable under normal growth conditions and is induced more than a thousand-fold by heat shock in an HSF-1-dependent manner, which has made its promoter the standard heat-shock reporter in the worm. Like other HSP70s it has an N-terminal nucleotide-binding ATPase domain, a substrate-binding domain and a C-terminal EEVD motif for co-chaperone docking, and it functions as an ATP-dependent folding chaperone that binds exposed hydrophobic segments of denatured proteins and, with J-domain and nucleotide-exchange co-chaperones, refolds them during recovery from proteotoxic stress. Its expression is also increased by ER stress in an xbp-1-dependent manner, and HSF-1 target chaperones including the inducible HSP70s contribute to the extended lifespan of insulin-signalling mutants.

Core Functions

Heat-inducible cytosolic ATP-dependent folding chaperone: binds unfolded/misfolded proteins and, driven by ATP hydrolysis in its nucleotide-binding domain and regulated by HSP40 and NEF co-chaperones, refolds them during and after heat stress.

hsp-70 (C12C8.1) curation notes

UniProt O45246 (TrEMBL, 643 aa), WormBase C12C8.1. Inducible cytosolic HSP70
(counterpart of human HSPA1A/B). Deep research: not run (falcon times out in
this environment; perplexity-lite unavailable). Review built from cached
publications and PubMed searches.

Identity

  • C. elegans has a multigene hsp70 family PMID:2225768.
  • Four cytosolic HSP70s: HSP-1, C12C8.1, F44E5.4, F44E5.5 PMID:41387410.
  • Sequence: begins MSTCKAIGIDLG (no signal peptide), ends EEVD (cytosolic HSP70 co-chaperone motif). ARBA "ER lumen" is therefore wrong -> REMOVE.

Expression

  • Silent at ambient temperature, strongly induced by heat; C12C8.1 promoter is the standard phsp70::gfp reporter PMID:23637632.

  • 1000-fold mRNA induction after heat shock PMID:28198373.

  • HSF-1 dependent PMID:27688402.
  • Tunicamycin induction, attenuated in xbp-1 (Table I of PMID:12186849) -> ER UPR/IRE1 HEP rows kept as non-core (output gene, cytosolic).

Decisions

  • GO:0044183 (IBA, ISS) MODIFY -> GO:0140662 ATP-dependent protein folding chaperone, matching hsp-1.
  • GO:0005832 CCT (IDA, PMID:9434769, abstract-only): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. Abstract names 52-65 kDa CCT subunits and co-purifying HSP60; an HSP70 is not a CCT subunit. Not removed since full text unread.
  • Lifespan IGI (PMID:14668486) KEEP_AS_NON_CORE PMID:14668486.
  • No gene-specific biochemistry exists for C12C8.1; MF rests on the PAINT HSP70 node and ISS from HSPA1A.
