Gene Description

gsk-3 (historically sgg-1) encodes the C. elegans ortholog of glycogen synthase kinase-3, a CMGC-group serine/threonine protein kinase that preferentially phosphorylates substrates already phosphorylated by a priming kinase. In the oocyte-to-embryo transition it phosphorylates the CCCH zinc-finger protein OMA-1 at T339 after MBK-2/DYRK priming, promoting CUL-2-dependent OMA-1 degradation after the first mitosis. In the early embryo it is a component of the MOM-2/Wnt signalling network that, unusually, acts positively: it is required for Wnt-dependent endoderm induction in the EMS daughter E and, through a transcription-independent branch, for orientation of the EMS and ABar mitotic spindles; it also represses the mesendoderm GATA factors med-1/2 in the C lineage. A Wnt/Frizzled-GSK-3-APR-1 branch promotes apoptotic cell engulfment and distal tip cell migration via CED-10/Rac. Later in development GSK-3 binds the Axin-like scaffolds PRY-1 and AXL-1 and acts as a negative regulator of BAR-1/beta-catenin-dependent canonical Wnt signalling (Q neuroblast and vulval precursor lineages). In the intestine GSK-3 phosphorylates SKN-1/Nrf and keeps it out of nuclei in the absence of stress, restraining the phase II oxidative stress response.

  • 362 aa CMGC kinase, GSK-3 subfamily; kinase domain 36-320, catalytic Asp161 (UniProt FT).

    "oocyte meiosis" role sometimes attributed to GSK-3 in worm belongs to these paralogs, not to
    gsk-3 itself; gsk-3's documented germline/oocyte role is OMA-1 turnover at the
    oocyte-to-embryo transition.
