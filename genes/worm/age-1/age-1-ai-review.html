Gene Description

AGE-1 is the catalytic p110 subunit of the single class I phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K) of Caenorhabditis elegans. It partners with the p50/p55-like adaptor subunit AAP-1 and, downstream of the insulin/IGF-1-like receptor DAF-2, phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate to generate PIP3 at the inner leaflet of the plasma membrane. PIP3 recruits and activates PDK-1 and the AKT-1/AKT-2/SGK-1 kinases, which phosphorylate and exclude the FOXO transcription factor DAF-16 from the nucleus; the PTEN homolog DAF-18 opposes AGE-1 by removing the 3-phosphate. AGE-1 is found diffusely in the cytoplasm of most cells. Loss of maternal and zygotic age-1 causes constitutive dauer arrest, while partial loss greatly extends adult lifespan and increases stress resistance, effects that require daf-16. AGE-1 signalling also contributes to fertility, embryonic cell survival, and experience-dependent salt chemotaxis in sensory neurons.
