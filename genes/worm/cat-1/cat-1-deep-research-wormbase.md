# WormBase gene description for cat-1 (worm)

cat-1 encodes a synaptic vesicular monoamine transporter required for the presence of dopamine and serotonin in nerve terminals, for the normal slowing of worms on a bacterial lawn (versus clean agar), for faster pharyngeal pumping on food, and for a normally (high) rate of egg-laying.
