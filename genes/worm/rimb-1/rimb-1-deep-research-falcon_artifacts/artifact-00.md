| Identity and architecture | **Direct rimb-1 evidence:** *C. elegans* **rimb-1**, formerly **tag-168**, encodes two reported isoforms (RIMB-1a/b). | Neuronal and presynaptic localization | **Direct:** rimb-1 promoter activity is nervous-system-specific; tagged RIMB-1 forms puncta in head/tail ganglia and dorsal/ventral nerve cords. In D-type motor neurons it colocalizes with SNB-1/VAMP2, UNC-10/RIM, and UNC-2/CaV2; endogenous mScarlet-RIMB-1 and GFP-UNC-2 colocalize at all examined dorsal-cord synaptic puncta. | Promoter reporters, fluorescent transgenes, immunostaining, and CRISPR-tagged endogenous proteins in live worms | Strong cellular-localization evidence. | Primary UNC-2/UNC-10 mechanism | **Direct:** RIMB-1 and UNC-10/RIM act redundantly to retain an adequate abundance and precise positioning of UNC-2/CaV2 at presynaptic active zones. | CLA-1 recruitment, 2023 | **Direct:** Complete loss of CLA-1 isoforms drastically reduces endogenous RIMB-1 punctum intensity and number, while loss of only the long or short isoform does not. Effects were reproduced with two cla-1 null alleles and a second RIMB-1 probe. CLA-1 and UNC-10 therefore recruit/stabilize RIMB-1 through partly independent routes; residual localization likely reflects SYD-2, ELKS-1, or other scaffolds. | CRISPR-tagged GFP-RIMB-1, quantitative dorsal-cord imaging, isoform-specific/null mutants, replication with independent alleles and probes | Strong 2023 causal localization evidence. | Synaptic output and redundancy | **Direct:** rimb-1 single mutants have mild locomotor/pharmacological defects and largely robust basal NMJ release. In rimb-1;unc-10 mutants, evoked amplitude falls to **36% of wild type** and **56% of unc-10**, supporting functional redundancy. The much stronger cla-1;unc-10 phenotype shows that CLA-1 also has RIMB-1-independent functions. | Thrashing and aldicarb assays, NMJ patch-clamp electrophysiology, and electron microscopy | Strong functional evidence, with single-mutant mildness reflecting network redundancy rather than absence of molecular function. | Kushibiki et al.; 2019 (pqac-00000001); Krout et al.; 15 May 2023; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2220856120) (pqac-00000019) |

| Active-zone ultrastructure | **Direct:** rimb-1 mutants have fewer total and docked synaptic vesicles, with significantly fewer docked vesicles within the first **33 nm** of the dense projection. After stimulation, fusion shifts distally, while dense-projection size remains unchanged.
