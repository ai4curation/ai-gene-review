# WormBase gene description for bbs-2 (worm)

bbs-2 encodes a ciliary protein that is orthologous to human BBS2; bbs-2 is expressed exclusively in ciliated sensory neurons at the transition zone and along the axoneme; BBS proteins are required for ciliary structure and function; BBS-2 along with other BBS proteins undergoes intraflagellar transport (IFT); loss of function mutations in the guanylate cyclase complex GCY-35/GCY-36 results in a full or partial suppression of the non-ciliary phenotypes of BBS mutants like body size, developmental delay, and roaming defects suggesting a non-cell autonomous role for sensory cilia and BBS proteins in regulating cGMP signaling.
