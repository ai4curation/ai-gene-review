# meg-1 notes

## Upstream regulation of meg-1 expression (2026-10-30)

- WormBase states that meg-1 expression in the proximal germline is positively regulated
  by MPK-1. The likely source is the microarray and in situ study of mpk-1 mutant gonads,
  which lists K02B9.1 (meg-1) among its in situ probes [PMID:17096596 "Independent
  verification by in situ analysis demonstrates that many of these genes have expression in
  the germ line that is first detectable in the pachytene stage and is MPK-1
  signaling-dependent."]. The per-gene in situ table is not in the cached text, so the
  meg-1 result itself was not read.
- This is transcriptional regulation of meg-1 by ERK signalling, not an activity of MEG-1,
  and is not annotated.
