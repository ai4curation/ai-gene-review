Gene Description

pptr-1 encodes a B56 (B prime) regulatory subunit of the heterotrimeric serine/threonine protein phosphatase PP2A in Caenorhabditis elegans. The protein has an ARM-like helical B56 domain. With the scaffold PAA-1 and the catalytic subunit LET-92, it targets PP2A to specific phosphoproteins in the cytoplasm. In insulin/IGF-1 signaling, PPTR-1 binds AKT-1 and promotes dephosphorylation of its activation-loop Thr350. This lowers AKT-1 activity, allows DAF-16/FOXO nuclear entry, and affects dauer formation, lifespan, fat storage and stress resistance; mammalian B56beta acts the same way on Akt Thr308. In the early embryo, maternal PPTR-1, redundantly with PPTR-2, dephosphorylates the intrinsically disordered MEG proteins (MEG-1, MEG-3) that are phosphorylated by MBK-2/DYRK. This promotes condensation and stability of P granules so that they segregate to the germline. pptr-1 mutants partition P granule components equally to somatic and germline blastomeres but remain fertile except at high temperature.
