Gene Description

GCS-1 is the C. elegans glutamate-cysteine ligase (gamma-glutamylcysteine synthetase) catalytic subunit, which catalyses the ATP-dependent ligation of L-glutamate and L-cysteine to gamma-glutamylcysteine, the first and rate-limiting step of glutathione biosynthesis. It is predicted to associate with the modifier subunit GCLM-1 (E01A2.1) in a heterodimeric glutamate-cysteine ligase. gcs-1 is a canonical phase II detoxification target of the transcription factor SKN-1: it is expressed constitutively in the ASI neurons and is induced in the intestine by oxidants, arsenite and pathogen-generated reactive oxygen species. Loss of gcs-1 sensitises animals to arsenite and arsenate, a defect rescued by exogenous glutathione, and glutathione made by GCS-1 also supports disulfide reduction of cuticle components during molting.
