# WormBase gene description for nud-2 (worm)

nud-2 encodes an ortholog of human NDE1 and NDEL1, effectors of LIS1; NUD-2, along with other orthologs of LIS1-associated proteins (NUD-1, DHC-1, CDK-5, and CDKA-1), is required to prevent convulsions induced by exposure to the GABA antagonist pentylenetetrazole (PTZ); nud-2(RNAi), like RNAi of nud-1 et al., not only induces PTZ susceptibility but also causes GABA-containing synaptic vesicles in the ventral nerve cord to be distributed raggedly, and nud-2(RNAi) animals are abnormally susceptible to paralysis by the GABA agonist muscimol; nud-2(RNAi) phenotypes are dominantly enhanced by a heterozygous lis-1 allele, and nud-2(RNAi) delays the recovery from PTZ-induced paralysis of lis-1 heterozygotes.
