# WormBase gene description for pfd-3 (worm)

pfd-3 encodes a putative prefoldin, orthologous to human VBP1 (OMIM:300133), that is required for normal alpha-tubulin synthesis, microtubule growth, mitotic spindle formation and positioning, early embryonic cell division, and embryonic and larval viability; PFD-3 is expressed cytoplasmically in most, if not all, tissues.
