# ced-7 notes

## Deep research status (2026-10-05)

No provider deep-research file exists for this gene. Providers were unavailable in this environment (see the ced-1 and ced-6 notes). The synthesis below was made by hand from cached publications, with inline quotes.

## Identity

- UniProtKB:P34358, ABCA-family ABC transporter, the orthologue of ABCA1 [PMID:17205124 "MEGF10 function can be modulated by the ATP binding cassette transporter ABCA1, ortholog to CED-7"].
- PANTHER PTHR19229:SF271 (ABC TRANSPORTER CED-7). UniProt and PANTHER's own classification agree.
- Plasma membrane, broad embryonic expression [PMID:9635425 "the CED-7 protein has sequence similarity to ABC transporters, is broadly expressed during embryogenesis, and is localized to the plasma membrane"].

## Function

- Acts in both cells [PMID:9635425 "Mosaic analysis revealed that ced-7 functions in both dying cells and engulfing cells during the engulfment process"].
- Needed for CED-1 clustering [PMID:11163239 "CED-1 failed to cluster around cell corpses in mutants defective in the engulfment gene ced-7"].
- Needed for actin reorganization [PMID:15744306 "CED-1, CED-6 and CED-7 are required for actin reorganization around the apoptotic cell corpse"].
- PS efflux and vesicles [PMID:22727702 "CED-7 and TTR-52 may promote the efflux of PS from apoptotic cells through the generation of extracellular PS vesicles"].
- Phagocyte PS exposure [PMID:22727702 "Phagocytic cells also express exPS, which is dependent on the activity of CED-7, TTR-52, and TTR-52-interacting phagocyte receptor CED-1"].
- The transported substrate has not been established biochemically.
