# WormBase gene description for gst-44 (worm)

gst-44 encodes a putative omega-class glutathione transferase (GST; EC 2.5.1.18) which, like its paralog GSTO-1, might have thiol oxidoreductase and dehydroascorbate reductase activity; GST-44 is expressed in excretory cell cytoplasm; other GST-44 paralogs include C02D5.3 and K10F12.4; GST-44 has no obvious function in mass RNAi assays.
