Gene Description

sem-5 encodes the C. elegans GRB2-family SH2/SH3 adaptor that links receptor tyrosine kinases to Ras/MAPK signaling. SEM-5 is recruited to activated LET-23 and other RTK signaling complexes and, through proline-rich partner binding, helps couple these receptors to SOS-1/LET-60 signaling in vulval induction, sex-myoblast migration, and additional RTK-dependent processes.

body-wall-muscle membrane regulation were kept as real but context-specific
developmental outputs.
