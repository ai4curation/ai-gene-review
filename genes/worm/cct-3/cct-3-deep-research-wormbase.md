# WormBase gene description for cct-3 (worm)

cct-3 encodes a putative gamma subunit of the eukaryotic cytosolic ('T complex') chaperonin, orthologous to human CCT3 (OMIM:600114); in early embryos, CCT-3 is required for normally fast microtubule elongation; CCT-3 is also required to repress SKN-1-dependent transcription of gst-4; in mass RNAi assays, CCT-3 is required for normal embryonic and larval viability, and for fertility.
