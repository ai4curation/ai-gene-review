Gene Description

DAF-18 is the Caenorhabditis elegans ortholog of the PTEN tumour suppressor, a phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate 3-phosphatase that also has dual-specificity protein phosphatase activity. By converting PIP3 back to PI(4,5)P2 at the plasma membrane it opposes the AGE-1 class I PI3K and limits recruitment and activation of PDK-1 and the AKT-1/AKT-2/SGK-1 kinases, thereby permitting nuclear entry of the FOXO factor DAF-16. Loss of daf-18 suppresses the dauer-constitutive and long-lived phenotypes of daf-2 and age-1 mutants and shortens lifespan. DAF-18 is found in the cytoplasm, at the plasma membrane and in nuclei, and is expressed in neurons. Beyond insulin-like signalling it promotes developmental neurite outgrowth, embryonic apoptosis and germline quiescence during diapause, antagonises the VAB-1 Eph receptor via protein phosphatase activity, and restrains RAS/MAPK signalling during vulval induction.
