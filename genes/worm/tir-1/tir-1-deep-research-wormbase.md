# WormBase gene description for tir-1 (worm)

tir-1 encodes a Toll-Interleukin 1 Receptor (TIR) domain adaptor protein that is the C. elegans ortholog of human SARM (sterile alpha and armadillo repeats); tir-1 is required for innate immunity, as loss of tir-1 activity results in increased susceptibility to infection; tir-1 also functions in a lateral signaling pathway that specifies neuronal identity and is required for proper localization of NSY-1/MAPKKK to post-synaptic regions; TIR-1 physically interacts with a number of proteins, including UNC-43/CaMKII, the RAB-1 GTPase, and the ATP synthase f subunit encoded by R53.4; TIR-1 is expressed in neurons and localizes to post-synaptic regions of axons.
