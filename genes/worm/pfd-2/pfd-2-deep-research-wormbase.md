# WormBase gene description for pfd-2 (worm)

pfd-2 encodes a putative prefoldin subunit 2, orthologous to human PFDN2, that is required for normal microtubule growth; pfd-2(RNAi) animals have sterile progeny, and pfd-2(RNAi) embryos show a reduced microtubule growth rate; in mass RNAi assays, PFD-2 is required for normal pronuclear migration, embryonic and larval viability, body shape, and locomotion.
