# WormBase gene description for dyf-3 (worm)

dyf-3 encodes an ortholog of zebrafish cluap1 (also qilin, and like PKD2required to suppress kidney cysts) and human clusterin-associatedprotein 1 (CLUAP1); DYF-3 is predicted to have two coiled-coil domains;DYF-3 is required for normal assembly of middle and distal ciliarysegments, with dyf-3 mutants showing truncated sensory cilia; DYF-3 isrequired cell-autonomously for dye uptake; dyf-3 is expressed in amphid,phasmid, and IL2 sensory neurons; dyf-3 expression requires DAF-19.
