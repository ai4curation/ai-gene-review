# WormBase gene description for bli-4 (worm)

bli-4 encodes a variety of related subtilases (serine endoproteases of the KEX2/subtilisin class), with shared N-termini but varying C-termini, that are collectively required both for larval viability and for stable adhesion of the adult cuticle.
