CC   -!- FUNCTION: Serine endoprotease which cleaves proproteins at paired basic
CC       amino acids at the consensus RX(K/R)R motif (By similarity). Involved
CC       in N-terminal processing of cuticle collagens and plays a role in
CC       cuticle biosynthesis (PubMed:24013594, PubMed:37721936). May cleave
CC       both sqt-3 and dpy-17 collagens to promote their secretion
CC       (PubMed:37721936). Acts in ASEL sensory neurons to regulate high salt
CC       chemotaxis responses probably by cleaving insulin-like protein ins-6
CC       into its mature and active form (PubMed:24013594). Essential for
CC       embryonic and larval development (PubMed:10903434, PubMed:19716386,
CC       PubMed:7774813). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P09958,
