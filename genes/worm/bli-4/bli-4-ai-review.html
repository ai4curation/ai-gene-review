BLI-4/KPC-4 is a Caenorhabditis elegans furin/Kex2-like subtilisin proprotein convertase required for collagenous cuticle formation. Its alternatively spliced isoforms share an N-terminal signal peptide, prodomain, subtilisin peptidase domain and P domain but differ in C-terminal regulatory domains and membrane anchors. In external epithelia, BLI-4 promotes N-terminal proteolytic processing of cuticle collagens such as SQT-3 and DPY-17 before efficient secretion and cuticle matrix assembly. In ASEL sensory neurons, BLI-4 has a separate non-essential role in processing insulin-like INS-6 neuropeptides that recruit AWCON to the adult high-salt chemotaxis circuit.
