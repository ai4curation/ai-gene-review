CC   -!- FUNCTION: Probably mediates sodium-dependent uptake of unknown small
CC       molecule(s) (PubMed:21575913). By positively modulating expression, in
CC       the epidermis, of antimicrobial peptides such as nlp-29, plays a role
CC       in resistance to fungal infection and in the response to physical
CC       wounding and phorbol ester PMA treatment (PubMed:21575913). Role in
CC       response to wounding of the epidermis may be facilitated by recruitment
CC       of snf-12 to the wound site by microtubule-dependent vesicle
CC       trafficking (PubMed:31995031). Functions cell autonomously in the
CC       epidermis, in concert with STAT transcription factor sta-2, probably
CC       acting at vesicular membranes, downstream of a p38 MAPK/pmk-1 pathway
CC       (PubMed:21575913). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21575913,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31995031}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: May interact with STAT family transcription factor sta-2; the
CC       interaction is probably direct. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21575913}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Membrane {ECO:0000255}; Multi-pass membrane
CC       protein {ECO:0000255}. Cytoplasm {ECO:0000305|PubMed:21575913}. Vesicle
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:21575913}. Note=Localized to vesicles, which may be
CC       a type of endosome (PubMed:21575913). Localized to an unknown apical
CC       membrane compartment (PubMed:31995031). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21575913,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31995031}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in the epidermis throughout development
CC       (PubMed:21575913). Also expressed in the seam cells, which are
CC       specialized epithelial cells that secrete the cuticle, as well as in
CC       the excretory cell, and the amphid and phasmid socket cells
CC       (PubMed:21575913). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21575913}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the sodium:neurotransmitter symporter (SNF)
CC       (TC 2.A.22) family. {ECO:0000305}.
