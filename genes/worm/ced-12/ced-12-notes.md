# ced-12 (C. elegans) curation notes

UniProt: Q8STE5 (CED12_CAEEL), 731 aa. ELMO orthologue: ARM repeats, ELMO domain, C-terminal PH domain, proline-rich
SH3-binding motif. UniProt gives no PANTHER family; none is asserted here.

Deep-research providers were not available for this review. These notes are a manual synthesis of the cached
publications (most are abstract-only; PMID:20126385 and PMID:24225442 are full text).

## Identity and pathway

- ced-12 was found as a new engulfment gene that is also needed to clear necrotic corpses
  [PMID:11146658 "as well as ced-12, a new engulfment gene we have identified, are required for efficient elimination of corpses generated by distinct necrosis-inducing stimuli"].
- Three 2001 papers cloned ced-12 and placed it with ced-2 (CrkII), ced-5 (DOCK180) and ced-10 (Rac)
  [PMID:11595183 "In C. elegans, CED-12 is required for engulfment of dying cells and for cell migrations"]
  [PMID:11703939 "acting in a signaling pathway with ced-2 Crkll, ced-5 DOCK180, and ced-10 Rac GTPase and acting upstream of ced-10 Rac"]
  [PMID:11703940 "CED-12 physically interacts with CED-5 and forms a ternary complex with CED-2 in vitro"].
- CED-12 acts in the engulfing cell [PMID:11703939 "CED-12 acts in engulfing cells for cell corpse engulfment"].

## Molecular function

- ELMO/CED-12 binds DOCK180/CED-5 and the complex stimulates Rac exchange
  [PMID:11595183 "CED-12/ELMO-1 binds directly to CED-5/Dock180; this evolutionarily conserved complex stimulates a Rac-GEF"].
- Mechanism: the ELMO PH domain binds the DOCK180-Rac complex in trans
  [PMID:15247908 "the PH domain of ELMO, by binding the Dock180-Rac complex in trans, stabilizes Rac in the nucleotide-free transition state"].
  So CED-12 is a GEF activator (GO:0160124), not a GEF itself.
- Binding to CED-5 uses the CED-12 SH3-binding motif and the CED-5 SH3 domain
  [PMID:11703939 "ced-12 encodes a protein with a pleckstrin homology (PH) domain and an SH3 binding motif"]
  [PMID:11703939 "interacts physically with CED-5, which contains an SH3 domain"].
  The GOA row "proline-rich region binding" therefore has the orientation reversed; SH3 domain binding is the right term.
- ARM repeats bind active MIG-2/RhoG [PMID:15620647 "Armadillo (ARM) repeats within CED-12/ELMO that mediate an interaction with activated MIG-2/RhoG"].
- PH domain binds phosphoinositides in vitro [PMID:21490059 "the CED-12 PH domain can bind to PtdIns(3,5)P(2)"];
  UniProt also lists PtdIns4P and PtdIns(3,4,5)P3 from the same paper (not visible in the abstract).

## Processes

- Engulfment of apoptotic cells (core) and necrotic corpses.
- Distal tip cell migration: MOM-5/Frizzled acts through the CED-2/5/12 branch
  [PMID:20126385 "Apoptotic cell clearance and migration of the distal tip cell require the MOM-5/Fz receptor, GSK-3 kinase, and APC/APR-1, which activate the CED-2/5/12 branch of the engulfment machinery"].
- Spindle orientation is NOT routed through CED-2/5/12 in that paper
  [PMID:20126385 "neither apr-1 nor any of the other ced genes showed a strong defect, suggesting that the link to CED-10 may not occur through CED-2/5/12 as is the case for engulfment and DTC migration"].
  The spindle and left/right IGI rows for ced-12 were removed on this basis.
- Upstream regulators: MTM-1 [PMID:21490059 "Epistatic analyses place mtm-1 upstream of the ternary GEF complex, which consists of ced-2, ced-5 and ced-12, and parallel to mig-2"];
  PSR-1 (proposed PS receptor) [PMID:14645848 "possibly through direct interaction with CED-5 and CED-12"].

## Apoptosis rows (clearance-defect background)

- PMID:24225442 (CED-8 paper, full text) uses ced-12(n3261) only as an engulfment-defective background
  [PMID:24225442 "ced-8(n1891) markedly enhanced the cell corpse engulfment defect of the ced-2, ced-5 or ced-12 mutants"].
  The GO:1902742 rows were changed to GO:1904747, following ced-1/ced-6/ced-7.
- Support for engulfment genes promoting death: [PMID:11449278 "requires the genes ced-1, ced-2, ced-5, ced-6, ced-7, ced-10 and ced-12"],
  [PMID:11449278 "mutations in engulfment genes enhance the frequency of this cell survival"],
  [PMID:11449279 "genes that mediate corpse removal can also function to actively kill cells"].
  Both abstracts are cached only as abstracts and do not say which engulfment mutants were tested, so a ced-12-specific
  result is not confirmed.
