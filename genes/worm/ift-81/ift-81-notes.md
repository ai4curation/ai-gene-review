# ift-81 (A8DYR7, IFT81 ortholog) notes

## Accession
A8DYR7 is the only UniProt entry for ift-81 (F32A6.2, CE41565, unreviewed). PMID:35969738 used the same WormBase protein CE41565.

## Summary with provenance
- IFT-81 is expressed in sensory neurons and sits at the ciliary base [PMID:17535250 "IFT-81 is expressed in sensory neurons, and localized at the base of cilia"]. Loss-of-function mutants have behavioral defects and are small [PMID:17535250 "its loss-of-function mutants show an unusual behavioral property and small body size"].
- IFT-81 acts with IFT-74 in IFT [PMID:17535250 "IFT-81 and IFT-74 coordinately act in IFT in C. elegans sensory cilia"].
- Tubulin-binding module: the IFT81 CH domain binds the globular part of tubulin [PMID:23990561 "the two core IFT proteins IFT74 and IFT81 form a tubulin-binding module and mapped the interaction to a calponin homology domain of IFT81 and a highly basic domain in IFT74"]. In human cells this binding is needed to build cilia [PMID:23990561 "tubulin binding by IFT81 was required for ciliogenesis in human cells"].
- In C. elegans, phosphorylation of IFT-74 lowers the tubulin affinity of IFT-74/81 [PMID:35969738 "Tubulin dimers are loaded to IFT machinery through an interaction between tubulin and the IFT-74/81 module"].
