# WormBase gene description for ift-81 (worm)

ift-81 encodes an ortholog of the intraflagellar transport component IFT-81 in Chlamydomonas; ift-81 along with ift-74 is involved in growth, chemotaxis, intraflagellar transport and the morphogenesis of cilia; ift-81 binds ift-74 in protein binding assays and co-localizes with ift-74 to the transition zone of cilia in neurons, indicating that ift-81 may form a complex with ift-74.
