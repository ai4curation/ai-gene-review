Gene Description

IFT-81 is the Caenorhabditis elegans ortholog of IFT81, a core subunit of the intraflagellar transport complex B (IFT-B1 core). It has an N-terminal calponin-homology (CH) domain followed by long coiled-coil segments, through which it forms a heterodimer with IFT-74. The IFT-74/81 heterodimer is the tubulin-binding module of the IFT particle: the IFT81 CH domain recognizes the globular part of tubulin and the basic IFT74 N-terminus binds the beta-tubulin tail, so tubulin dimers are carried into the cilium for axoneme assembly. IFT-81 is expressed in ciliated sensory neurons, localizes at the ciliary base and in sensory cilia, and colocalizes with IFT-74. ift-81 loss-of-function mutants have mild defects in cilium formation, strongly disrupted IFT in mature cilia, chemosensory and dye-filling defects and small body size. Phosphorylation of IFT-74 by the ciliary kinase DYF-5 reduces the tubulin affinity of the IFT-74/81 module and promotes tubulin unloading at the ciliary tip.

Core Functions

With IFT-74, IFT-81 forms the tubulin-binding module of the IFT-B particle. Its N-terminal calponin-homology domain binds tubulin dimers that IFT carries into the sensory cilium for axoneme assembly, and its coiled-coil region pairs with IFT-74 in the IFT-B1 core.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Suggested Experiments

Summary with provenance

  • IFT-81 is expressed in sensory neurons and sits at the ciliary base PMID:17535250. Loss-of-function mutants have behavioral defects and are small PMID:17535250.
  • IFT-81 acts with IFT-74 in IFT PMID:17535250.
  • Tubulin-binding module: the IFT81 CH domain binds the globular part of tubulin PMID:23990561. In human cells this binding is needed to build cilia PMID:23990561.
  • In C. elegans, phosphorylation of IFT-74 lowers the tubulin affinity of IFT-74/81 PMID:35969738.
