# WormBase gene description for gon-2 (worm)

gon-2 encodes a predicted transmembrane cation channel homologous to the long transient receptor potential channel (LTRPC) subfamily of TRP channels including Drosophila DLTRP and mammalian melastatin and LTRPC7; GON-2 regulates the onset and continuation of post-embryonic mitotic cell divisions in the somatic gonad precursor cells perhaps by mediating an increase in intracellular calcium in response to activating signals.
