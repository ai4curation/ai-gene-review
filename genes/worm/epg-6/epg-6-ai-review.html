Gene Description

EPG-6 (ectopic PGL granules 6) is the C. elegans member of the WIPI3/WIPI4 (yeast Hsv2) group of PROPPINs, seven-bladed WD40 beta-propeller proteins that bind phosphoinositides. EPG-6 binds PtdIns(3)P (and, in vitro, PtdIns(5)P and more weakly PtdIns(4)P and PtdIns(3,5)P2) through a conserved LRRG motif and binds ATG-2 directly, forming an ATG-2/EPG-6 complex equivalent to the yeast Atg2-Atg18 and human ATG2A-WIPI4 complexes. The complex acts at a late step of autophagosome formation, moving PI3P-positive omegasomes on to closed autophagosomes; epg-6 and atg-2 mutants accumulate enlarged early autophagic structures and fail to degrade protein aggregates such as PGL granules and SQST-1 during embryogenesis. EPG-6 is broadly expressed and cytoplasmic. Its paralog ATG-18 (WIPI1/2 group) also binds PI3P but acts at a distinct step. Loss of epg-6 also extends adult lifespan, which may reflect a role outside autophagy.

Core Functions

EPG-6 binds PtdIns(3)P on omegasome and phagophore membranes through its WD40 beta-propeller (LRRG motif). This recruits EPG-6 to early autophagic structures, where it is needed for omegasomes to progress to autophagosomes.

EPG-6 binds ATG-2 directly and links it to PI3P-positive membranes, forming the ATG-2/EPG-6 complex (the worm counterpart of Atg2-Atg18 / ATG2A-WIPI4) that supplies lipid for phagophore expansion.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is the lifespan extension of epg-6 mutants independent of autophagy, and if so, which EPG-6 partner or tissue mediates it?

Q: Does C. elegans ATG-2 depend on EPG-6 alone, or also on ATG-18, for recruitment to PI3P-positive membranes in different tissues?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Rescue epg-6(bp242) with EPG-6 variants that cannot bind PI3P (LRRG mutant) or cannot bind ATG-2, then score PGL granule and SQST-1 degradation, ATG-2 puncta and lifespan.

Hypothesis: PI3P binding and ATG-2 binding are both required for autophagy, while the lifespan effect separates from one or both.

Type: Separation-of-function rescue

Deep research: just deep-research-falcon worm epg-6 was attempted (2026-10-08) but the
falcon provider timed out and perplexity was unavailable, so no deep-research file was
produced. The review is based on cached publications, the UniProt record and PubMed searches.

Key facts

  • WD40 PROPPIN of the WIPI3/WIPI4 (yeast Hsv2) group PMID:30871075.
  • Binds PI3P and binds ATG-2 directly; with ATG-2 drives omegasome-to-autophagosome progression PMID:21802374.
  • In vitro also binds PI5P (strongly), PI4P and PI(3,5)P2 (weakly) [file:worm/epg-6/epg-6-uniprot.txt].
  • Human counterpart: WIPI4 binds one tip of ATG2A PMID:30185561.
  • epg-6 mutants are long-lived despite an autophagy block, suggesting an autophagy-independent role PMID:30871075.

Decisions

  • PI3P binding and adaptor activity are core MFs; other phosphoinositide binding rows kept as non-core.
  • GO:0034045 (obsolete) rows modified to GO:7770114 phagophore membrane (matching human ATG2A review).
  • protein binding (IPI with ATG-2) modified to protein-macromolecule adaptor activity.
  • NEW: GO:0062079 ATG2-ATG18 complex (direct binding, IPI).
