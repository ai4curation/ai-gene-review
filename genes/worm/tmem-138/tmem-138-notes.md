# tmem-138 (Q7YX41) notes

Deep research: falcon timed out and the perplexity fallback was unavailable, so no deep-research file exists. The only worm study is Li et al. 2016 [PMID:26982032], with full text cached.

## Key findings
- TZ localization requires MKS-5 and CEP-290 but not MKS or NPHP module proteins [PMID:26982032 "Similar to CDKL-1, TMEM-138 requires MKS-5 for its localisation but does not depend on either core MKS or NPHP module proteins"].
- No synthetic dye-filling defect with mksr-1 or nphp-4, and no genetic interaction with cdkl-1; only a mild effect with cep-290 [PMID:26982032].
- Proposed to belong, with CDKL-1, to a CEP-290-associated branch distinct from the MKS and NPHP modules [PMID:26982032 "hints at the existence of a TZ module that is distinct from the MKS and NPHP modules"].
