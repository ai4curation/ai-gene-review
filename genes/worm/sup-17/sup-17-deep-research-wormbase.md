# WormBase gene description for sup-17 (worm)

sup-17 encodes an ADAM protein, an integral membrane disintegrin and zinc-activated metalloproteinase, orthologous to Drosophila and mouse KUZBANIAN and the vertebrate ADAM10 proteins; SUP-17 is required maternally for embryonic development and plays a role in LIN-12/Notch-mediated cell signaling required for several cell fate decisions during vulval development; SUP-17 is also required for normal body morphology and male tail development; SUP-17 functions cell autonomously to facilitate LIN-12 signaling and requires the LIN-12 extracellular domain to do so.
