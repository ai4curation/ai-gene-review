# WormBase gene description for rab-11.1 (worm)

rab-11.1 encodes a small GTPase homologous to the Rab GTPases that function in endocytosis, membrane fusion, and vesicular trafficking events; RAB-11.1 activity is required for completion of the final stages of cytokinesis during early embryogenesis and for efficient uptake of yolk proteins during oocyte development; RAB-11.1 is also required for normal peripheral localization of nuclei in the synctial germ cell in the ovary and for regulated secretion of CAV-1-containing cortical granules following fertilization; RAB-11.1 localizes to recycling endosomes and to cortical granules.
