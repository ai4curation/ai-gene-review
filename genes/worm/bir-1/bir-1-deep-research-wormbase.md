# WormBase gene description for bir-1 (worm)

bir-1 encodes a homolog of the baculoviral inhibitor-of-apoptosis (IAP) repeat (BIR) proteins (BIRPs) family; bir-1 is required for meiotic and mitotic chromosome segregation and for cytokinesis; BIR-1 localizes to meiotic and mitotic chromosomes and to the spindle midzone; BIR-1 forms a protein complex with CSC-1, ICP-1 and AIR-2.
