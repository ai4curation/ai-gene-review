# clic-1 — clathrin light chain (P90961, TrEMBL) — curation notes

## Identity
- `clic-1` encodes the single clathrin light chain of C. elegans (UniProt: "Belongs to the clathrin light chain family"; coiled-coil 121..167 that binds the heavy-chain hub, disordered N-terminal regions). Clathrin triskelia contain three heavy and three light chains, with the CHC hub providing the light-chain binding interface [PMID:19151157 "Clathrin forms a triskelion, composed of 3 heavy and light chains."] [PMID:19151157 "The C-terminal one-third of CHC is often referred to as the “hub domain.” This region of CHC is necessary and sufficient for clathrin self-assembly into triskelia and also provides the binding interface for CLC ( 13 )."].
- Light chains are not required for endocytosis per se in other systems but contribute to other clathrin-mediated trafficking steps [PMID:19151157 "Recent reports indicate that clathrin light chain (CLC) is not required for endocytosis but rather contributes to other clathrin-mediated trafficking steps ( 10 )."]. The chc-1 deep-research report likewise notes that CLIC-1 loss is comparatively mild while chc-1 loss is lethal [file:worm/chc-1/chc-1-deep-research-falcon.md "CHC-1 is genetically much more essential: *chc-1* RNAi or temperature-sensitive inactivation disrupts endocytosis and viability, whereas CLIC-1 loss alone is comparatively mild."].

## Localisation
- Sensory cilia: clathrin light chain, AP-2, dynamin and RAB-5 localise to a discrete periciliary membrane compartment (abstract only) [PMID:22342749 "Here we show that localization of the clathrin light chain, AP-2 clathrin adaptor, dynamin, and RAB-5 endocytic proteins overlaps with a morphologically discrete periciliary membrane compartment associated with sensory cilia."].
- Epidermis: mScarlet-tagged CLIC-1 is imaged with GFP::CHC-1 in small dispersed apical structures of hyp7 (preprint, via chc-1 deep research) [file:worm/chc-1/chc-1-deep-research-falcon.md "GFP-tagged CHC-1 and mScarlet-tagged CLIC-1 were imaged while testing effects of the phosphatidylserine-flippase mutation *tat-1(E853K)*."].

## Curation decisions
- All family-level (IBA/IEA) coat, heavy-chain-binding and endocytosis terms are accepted: CLIC-1 is the light-chain component of the clathrin triskelion that coats plasma-membrane pits and TGN/endosomal vesicles.
- Synaptic vesicle membrane (IBA, rat light chains) is kept as non-core: clathrin light chain rides on coated synaptic-vesicle intermediates rather than being a resident vesicle-membrane protein.
- The only experimental row (periciliary membrane compartment, IDA) is supported by the abstract and accepted.
