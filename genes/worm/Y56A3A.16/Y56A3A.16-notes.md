# Y56A3A.16 (RUB-1; UniProt G2HK09) notes

## Identity

- UniProt G2HK09 (unreviewed, 597 aa; WormBase Y56A3A.16b) "Rubicon Homology domain-containing protein"; PANTHER PTHR45971:SF1 "RUBICON, ISOFORM A" [file:worm/Y56A3A.16/Y56A3A.16-uniprot.txt, "PANTHER; PTHR45971:SF1; RUBICON, ISOFORM A; 1."]. The only recognised domain is the RH domain at 365-582 [file:worm/Y56A3A.16/Y56A3A.16-uniprot.txt, "DOMAIN          365..582"].
- Nakamura et al. 2019 named it RUB-1: "Y56A3A.16 has sequence similarity to human Rubicon (KIAA0226), human KIAA0226L (recently identified as Pacer)20, and Drosophila Rubicon (dRubicon, CG12772)" and "hereafter we refer to Y56A3A.16 as RUB-1" [PMID:30783089].

## Bioinformatics (file:worm/Y56A3A.16/Y56A3A.16-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md)

- Live InterPro hits: worm has RH only; no RUN domain and no PIKBD (PF21054) in either UniProt isoform (G2HK09, G2HK08). Human RUBCN = RUN + PIKBD + RH; Pacer = PIKBD + RH; fly Rubicon = RUN + PIKBD + RH.
- Worm RH domain is ~32% identical to RUBCN, Pacer and fly RH alike (equidistant), so sequence cannot assign it to the Rubicon or Pacer side. A shuffle-controlled Smith-Waterman search found no RUN-like signal in the worm N-terminus (z = -0.04 vs RUBCN RUN; 1.31 vs fly RUN). The worm RH has 13 Cys vs 17-18 in the others.
- Conclusion: a divergent, truncated family member retaining only the RH (Rab7-binding in human) module; it lacks the RUN domain that human Rubicon uses to bind/inhibit VPS34.

## Experimental function (single study)

- RNAi increases autophagy: "Knockdown of Y56A3A.16 by RNAi significantly increased the number of GFP::LGG-1 dots, an autophagosome marker5,22, in intestinal cells"; increase is bec-1/atg-18 dependent, and flux increased after BafA ("suggesting that Y56A3A.16 knockdown increases the autophagic flux") [PMID:30783089]. Abstract framing: "C. elegans Rubicon homologue negatively regulates autophagy".
- Lifespan: "knockdown of rub-1 by RNAi significantly extended wild-type worm lifespan", dependent on bec-1, unc-51, atg-18; neuronal knockdown most effective; overexpression shortened lifespan; rub-1 transcript increases with age and is lower in daf-2, eat-2, glp-1 [PMID:30783089].
- Endocytosis: "rub-1 knockdown did not largely alter the localisation of hTFR (human transferrin receptor)::GFP in intestinal cells, suggesting that rub-1 might not have obvious function in endocytosis in worms" [PMID:30783089].
- Expression: RUB-1::EGFP in neurons, intestine and pharynx (tissue-level only; no subcellular localisation reported).
- So functionally RUB-1 behaves like vertebrate Rubicon (negative regulator of autophagy), not like Pacer, despite lacking the RUN domain. Mechanism (VPS34/UVRAG inhibition? Rab7 binding?) is unknown. Only RNAi (40-50% knockdown) evidence; no mutant, no biochemistry, no independent replication.
- Yamamuro et al. 2022 (MondoA; PMID:35235784, abstract only) concerns mammalian Rubicon regulation in senescence; no C. elegans RUB-1 data in the abstract, not used.
- PubMed esearch "Y56A3A.16" (2026-10-10) returns only PMID:30783089. Literature is sparse.
