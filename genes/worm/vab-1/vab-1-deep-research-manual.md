# vab-1 (O61460) - manual literature research

No deep-research provider was run for this gene; this file collects literature work done

## Identity and domain architecture

- vab-1 (M03A1.1) encodes the single C. elegans Eph receptor tyrosine kinase, 1117 aa,
  single-pass type I membrane protein. Domains (UniProt/InterPro): Eph ligand-binding
  domain (PF01404 Ephrin_lbd), cysteine-rich region, two fibronectin type III repeats
  (PF00041), Eph transmembrane region (PF14575), tyrosine kinase domain (PF07714,
  IPR020635 Tyr_kinase_cat_dom, IPR008266 Tyr_kinase_AS), SAM domain (IPR054590 EPH_SAM).
  PANTHER PTHR46877 (Eph receptor family).
- [PMID:12403719 "The C. elegans genome encodes a single Eph receptor tyrosine kinase, VAB-1, which functions in neurons to control epidermal morphogenesis."]

## Ephrin receptor activity and ephrin signalling

- EFN-1/VAB-2 is a ligand for VAB-1 [PMID:10619431 "We show that the ephrin VAB-2 (also known as EFN-1) is a ligand for VAB-1 and can function in neurons to regulate epidermal morphogenesis."]
- Three of the four ephrins (EFN-1, -2, -3) signal through VAB-1; EFN-4 acts largely
  independently [PMID:12403719 "Three ephrins (EFN-1/VAB-2, EFN-2 and EFN-3) have been previously shown to function in VAB-1 signaling."]
- Kinase-dependent and kinase-independent functions
  [PMID:9506518 "Disruption of the kinase domain of VAB-1 causes weak mutant phenotypes, indicating that VAB-1 may have both kinase-dependent and kinase-independent activities."]

## Tyrosine kinase activity, DAF-18/PTEN

- [PMID:19853560 "the VAB-1 Eph kinase domain physically interacts with and phosphorylates PTEN (DAF-18), diminishing its protein levels and function."]
- DAF-18 antagonises VAB-1 [PMID:19853560 "We also present evidence that DAF-18 has protein phosphatase activity to antagonize VAB-1 action."]
- UniProt curates autophosphorylation and EC 2.7.10.1 catalytic activity from this paper.

## Embryonic morphogenesis (gastrulation cleft closure, ventral enclosure)

- [PMID:9506518 "vab-1 mutants are defective in the movement of neuroblasts during closure of the ventral gastrulation cleft and in the movements of epidermal cells during ventral enclosure of the embryo by the epidermis."]
- Required in neurons, non-autonomously, for epidermal enclosure
  [PMID:9506518 "VAB-1 is expressed in neurons during epidermal enclosure and is required in these cells for normal epidermal morphogenesis"]
- Pocket closure: VAB-1 in "plexin band" cells drives the cellular extensions that form
  the bridge for P-cell migration [PMID:22197242 "Bridge formation requires VAB-1 kinase-dependent extension of presumptive bridge cells over scaffold cells toward the ventral midline."]
- Partial redundancy with PTP-3/LAR [PMID:11959824, abstract via PubMed; not cached] and with
  MAB-20/semaphorin and PLX-2 [PMID:22197242].

## Axon guidance

- [PMID:10409513 "SAX-3/Robo and the VAB-1/Eph receptor both function to prevent aberrant axon crossing at the ventral midline."]
- [PMID:16386725 "The predominant axonal defect in vab-1 mutant animals was an overextension axon phenotype."]
  Gain-of-function VAB-1 kinase causes the opposite phenotype, consistent with VAB-1 transducing a repellent or stop cue.
- Midline guidance with WRK-1 [PMID:17027485 "WRK-1 functionally interacts with the Eph receptor system"].
- SDQ/SMD/PLN sublateral axon guidance with EFN-4 and LAD-2 [PMID:26903502 "Our genetic analysis reveals that VAB-1 functions in axon guidance of lad-2- expressing neurons."]

## Oocyte meiotic maturation: MSP receptor

- [PMID:12533508 "We used DNA microarray data and an in situ binding assay to identify the VAB-1 Eph receptor protein–tyrosine kinase as an MSP receptor."]
- VAB-1 is sufficient for MSP binding in COS-7 cells
  [PMID:12533508 "COS-7 cells expressing VAB-1∷DsRed bind MSP-FITC, whereas binding is not observed in cells that do not express VAB-1∷DsRed"]
- Negative regulator in the absence of sperm [PMID:12533508 "The maturation rate of unmated vab-1(dx31) females is significantly higher than that of unmated females"]
- MSP binds VAB-1 directly in vitro [PMID:16824915 "In vitro binding experiments show that MSP promotes oocyte mitogen-activated protein kinase activation and meiotic maturation in part through direct interaction with the VAB-1 Eph receptor."]
- Downstream: ITR-1, NMR-1/UNC-43 [PMID:16267094 "Ephrin/VAB-1 signaling acts upstream of ITR-1 to inhibit meiotic resumption"].
- VAP MSP domains (VPR-1) bind the VAB-1 ectodomain
  [PMID:18555774 "These data indicate that VPR-1 MSP is able to directly bind the VAB-1 ectodomain."]
