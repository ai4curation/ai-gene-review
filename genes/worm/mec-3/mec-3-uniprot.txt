CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor (PubMed:10899123, PubMed:26096732).
CC       Specifies differentiation of the set of six touch receptor neurons
CC       (TRNs) (PubMed:2898300). May positively modulate expression of both its
CC       own gene and also of homeobox ARX homolog alr-1 in TRNs, forming a
CC       positive feedback loop with alr-1, thereby restricting the variability
CC       of expression of mec-3 (PubMed:21368126). Required to determine the
CC       identity of ALM sensory neurons, acting by interacting with unc-86,
CC       thereby preventing unc-86 cooperating with pag-3 to induce BDU-neuron
CC       specific genes (PubMed:26096732). Binds cooperatively as a heterodimer
CC       with unc-86 to sites in the mec-3 gene promoter (PubMed:10899123,
CC       PubMed:1361171). Promotes outgrowth of lateral dendritic branches on
CC       the PVD nociceptive neurons, probably acting both directly, and
CC       upstream of zinc finger protein egl-46 (PubMed:29031632).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10899123, ECO:0000269|PubMed:1361171,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26096732, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2898300,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29031632}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with unc-86; the heterooligomer binds to the
CC       promoters of mec-3, mec-4 and mec-7. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10899123}.
