Gene Description

lin-3 encodes the C. elegans EGF-family ligand for the LET-23 receptor tyrosine kinase. LIN-3 is produced as a membrane-associated precursor and acts as a local inductive signal that activates LET-23/Ras/MAPK signaling in responding epithelial cells, most prominently during vulval induction and male spicule development.
