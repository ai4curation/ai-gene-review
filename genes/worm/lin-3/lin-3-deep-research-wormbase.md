# WormBase gene description for lin-3 (worm)

lin-3 encodes a member of the EGF family of peptide growth factors that affects induction of vulval development, viability, ovulation, behavioral quiescence after stress, and male spicule development; it acts genetically upstream of its presumptive receptor let-23, and is expressed in multiple locations consistent with it acting as a localized ligand; for example, the anchor cell of the developing gonad, which induces vulval development.
