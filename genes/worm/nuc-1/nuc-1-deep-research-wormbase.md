# WormBase gene description for nuc-1 (worm)

The nuc-1 gene encodes a DNase II homolog similar to mammalian and Drosophila DNaseII enzymes and is required for DNA degradation during apoptosis as well as for degradation of dietary DNA during normal feeding; during apoptosis, NUC-1 functions in apoptotic cells at an intermediate stage of DNA degradation, after the killing step, but prior to cell-corpse engulfment.
