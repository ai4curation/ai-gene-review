# WormBase gene description for lis-1 (worm)

lis-1 encodes a microtubule-association protein (MAP) that is orthologous to human LIS1, and Aspergillus nidulans nudF, which mediates nuclear migration along Aspergillus hyphae; in C. elegans lis-1 functions as a dynein regulator whose activity is required for a number of processes, including centrosome separation, spindle assembly, regulation of neurotransmission, and actin cytoskeleton integrity; LIS-1 localizes to the cytoplasm, nucleus, and microtubules.
