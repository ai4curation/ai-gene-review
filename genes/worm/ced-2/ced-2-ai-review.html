Gene Description

CED-2 is the C. elegans orthologue of the CrkII adaptor protein. It has an N-terminal SH2 domain followed by two SH3 domains and no catalytic activity. It acts in engulfing cells, where it links upstream engulfment signals to the CED-5/DOCK180 and CED-12/ELMO bipartite guanine nucleotide exchange factor, which activates the Rac GTPase CED-10. CED-2, CED-5 and CED-12 form a ternary complex, and CED-2 co-precipitates with the phosphatidylserine receptor PSR-1. Upstream inputs include PSR-1, the Frizzled receptor MOM-5 acting with GSK-3 and the APC homologue APR-1, and the Src kinase SRC-1. Rac activation drives the actin rearrangement and polarized membrane extension needed to engulf apoptotic and necrotic cell corpses. The same CED-2/CED-5/CED-12/CED-10 module is needed for the migration of the two gonadal distal tip cells. ced-2 mutants accumulate unengulfed corpses, but programmed cell death itself still occurs. Engulfment genes, including those of this pathway, also help ensure that cells receiving weak death signals die.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Which phosphotyrosine sites does the CED-2 SH2 domain bind in vivo during engulfment (for example on SRC-1 targets, APR-1 or PSR-1-associated proteins), and is any receptor tyrosine kinase involved?
