| Gene/protein identity | *C. elegans* **aps-2/F02E8.3** encodes the small **σ2 subunit of AP-2**. It is distinct from **apm-2/dpy-23**, which encodes AP-2 μ2. This agrees with UniProt Q19123’s adaptor-complex small-subunit domains. | Direct APS-2 identification | AP-2 comprises α, β2, μ2, and σ2 subunits. | Garafalo et al., 2015, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.e14-06-1048) (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000009) |
| Structural organization | APS-2/σ2 associates closely with APA-2/α to form the **α–σ2 hemicomplex**, whereas β2–μ2 constitutes the other functional half of AP-2. The α–σ2 unit can persist when μ2 is absent. | Direct tagged-protein evidence plus intact-complex structural interpretation | APS-2::GFP nerve-ring signal was **39% of wild type** in an *apm-2/μ2* mutant. | Gu et al., 2013, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.00190) (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000013) |
| APS-2 stability and localization | APS-2::GFP is detectable in the **nerve ring** and **maturing oocytes**. Its severe depletion after α loss indicates that σ2 stability and localization depend especially strongly on its α-adaptin partner. | Direct APS-2::GFP imaging and abundance evidence | Nerve-ring fluorescence: wild type **3543 ± 169 (100%)**; *apa-2/α* mutant **389 ± 28 (11%)**; *apm-2/μ2* mutant **1391 ± 51 (39%)**; comparisons **p < 0.0001**. | Gu et al., 2013, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.00190) (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000012) |
| *aps-2(tm2912)* lesion and phenotype | **tm2912** carries a deletion within the *aps-2* coding region and causes temperature-dependent growth and locomotor defects, consistent with impaired AP-2 function. | Direct APS-2 genetic evidence | **194-bp deletion**; growth nearly normal at **15°C**, with partial arrest and Gro/Unc phenotypes at **20°C**. | Kuwahara et al., 2008, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddn198) (pqac-00000007) |
| Interaction with α-synuclein toxicity | Reducing APS-2 function selectively worsens growth arrest and impaired movement in worms expressing neuronal α-synuclein, supporting a protective role for normal endocytic trafficking rather than a demonstrated biochemical interaction between APS-2 and α-synuclein. | Direct RNAi and genetic-interaction evidence; mechanism inferred | Screen tested **1,673 genes**; *aps-2* RNAi scored as a transgene-selective Unc hit, and *tm2912* strongly enhanced synWT, A53T, and A30P transgenic phenotypes. | Kuwahara et al., 2008, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddn198) (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000008) |
| GLR-1 glutamate-receptor trafficking | Mutations in *aps-2/σ2*, *apa-2/α*, or *apm-2/μ2* reduce GLR-1 abundance in the ventral nerve cord, implicating AP-2, including APS-2, in receptor trafficking. The detailed early-secretory-compartment mechanism was characterized mainly through μ2 mutants and should not be assigned specifically to σ2. | Direct qualitative APS-2 mutant finding; quantitative and mechanistic details primarily from another AP-2 subunit | Reported **26%** and **30%** decreases in GLR-1::GFP punctum fluorescence apply to *apm-2(gm17)* and *apm-2(e840)*, respectively, not specifically to *aps-2*. | Garafalo et al., 2015, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.e14-06-1048) (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000009) |
| AP-2 activation by FChO | FCHO-1 promotes conversion of AP-2 from a closed state to an open, membrane- and cargo-binding state. σ2 helps form the latch region contacted by the β2 N-terminus; mutations disrupting this interface can bypass FCHO-1 loss. This supports a conformational role for the σ2-containing interface but is not an isolated APS-2 biochemical assay. | AP-2-complex genetic and structural inference | **71** dominant bypass suppressors were recovered; **7** mapped to residues at the β2–α/σ2 latch interface. | Hollopeter et al., 2014, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.03648) (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000014) |
| Primary molecular function | APS-2 is a **nonenzymatic structural/adaptor subunit**. Within AP-2 it stabilizes the α–σ2 hemicomplex and contributes to assembling and conformationally regulating plasma-membrane machinery that couples selected cargo to clathrin-mediated internalization. No catalytic reaction or transported substrate applies. | Direct hemicomplex evidence combined with conserved AP-2-complex inference | σ2 is reported to be approximately **95% identical between *C. elegans* and mouse**, supporting strong evolutionary conservation. | Gu et al., 2013, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.00190) (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000016) |
| Evidence limits and recent literature | The strongest APS-2-specific experiments remain the 2008–2015 studies. No focused 2023–2024 primary study was identified that materially revises APS-2’s annotation. Results for APM-2/DPY-23 μ2 must not be relabeled as APS-2 evidence. | Literature-gap assessment and identity-control caveat | APS-2-specific quantitative data are sparse; many measurements concern α, μ2, or the intact AP-2 complex. | Evidence corpus summarized above (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000012) |
