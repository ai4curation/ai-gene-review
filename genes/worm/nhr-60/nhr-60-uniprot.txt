CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17579999}.
CC       Note=Localized in the nucleus from the one-cell stage of development
CC       onwards. Localizes to the nuclear periphery of all cells.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17579999}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed from embryogenesis onwards with high
CC       expression during the larval stages of development, peaking at the L3
CC       stage and decreasing at the L4 stage of larval development.
CC       Ubiquitously expressed, but expression in seam cells begins in
CC       embryonic precursors soon after fertilization and is most prominent in
CC       larval stages of development. Expression is also prominent in the germ
CC       line during the L1 stage of larval development. Also expressed in
CC       pharyngeal gland cells, VC4 and VC5 neurons, and in the hermaphrodite
CC       uterine vulval uv1 cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17579999}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: RNAi-mediated knockdown results in arrest at the
CC       two-fold stage of embryogenesis in the majority of embryos. These
CC       embryos have incomplete ventral closure and morphological defects
CC       including elongation defects, and either absent or irregularly placed
CC       seam cells in the head and tail regions. Surviving larvae also exhibit
CC       morphological defects and contain vacuoles.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17579999}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the nuclear hormone receptor family.
