Gene Description

PDK-1 is the Caenorhabditis elegans ortholog of 3-phosphoinositide-dependent protein kinase 1, an AGC-family serine/threonine kinase. In the DAF-2 insulin/IGF-1-like pathway it acts downstream of the AGE-1 PI3K and phosphorylates and activates AKT-1, AKT-2 and SGK-1, which form a complex and in turn phosphorylate the FOXO factor DAF-16 to keep it out of the nucleus. pdk-1 loss of function causes constitutive dauer arrest and extended lifespan that are suppressed by daf-16, while an activating kinase-domain mutation bypasses the requirement for age-1, showing PDK-1 is necessary and sufficient to relay PI3K signals to the AKT kinases. PDK-1 is expressed in neurons, intestine and hypodermis, and its signalling also influences salt chemotaxis learning and cadmium-responsive metallothionein expression.

Curation decisions

  • Core MF is 3-phosphoinositide-dependent protein kinase activity (GO:0004676), proposed as NEW (GOA carries only the parent GO:0004674). An earlier draft declined it on the grounds that UniProt annotates no PH domain; that was wrong. The UniProt entry has no FT DOMAIN line for the PH domain, but cross-references a PDK1-type PH domain (CDD cd01262 PH_PDK1, Gene3D 2.30.29.30, InterPro IPR033931 PDK1-typ_PH, Pfam PF14593, SMART SM00233) [file:worm/pdk-1/pdk-1-uniprot.txt "InterPro; IPR033931; PDK1-typ_PH."], and the PIAK paper defines PIAK by lacking the PH domain that PDK-1 has PMID:11274160. Comparators: Drosophila Pdk1 review uses GO:0004676 as core MF; human PDPK1 (O15530) has GO:0004676 by IDA (PMID:9368760, QuickGO). The PH-less PIAK form is phospholipid-independent and is covered by the accepted GO:0004674 rows.
  • Cellular response to ROS (PMID:28632756) marked over-annotated: the paper assays cadmium-inducible mtl-1 transcription, not ROS.
  • ARBA IEA developmental-growth / gross-anatomy / oxygen-compound terms marked over-annotated.
