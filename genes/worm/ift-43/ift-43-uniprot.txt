CC   -!- FUNCTION: As a component of IFT complex A (IFT-A), a complex required
CC       for retrograde ciliary transport and entry into cilia of G protein-
CC       coupled receptors (GPCRs), it is involved in ciliogenesis (Probable).
CC       In particular, may act redundantly with the intraflagellar transport
CC       protein ift-139 to regulate the transport of specific ciliary cargo
CC       proteins such as che-3 which are related to motility.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28479320, ECO:0000305|PubMed:28479320}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the IFT complex A (IFT-A) composed of at least

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28479320}.

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28479320}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: No visible phenotype, with no defects in cilium
