Gene Description

let-60 encodes the C. elegans Ras small GTPase that functions as a membrane-associated molecular switch downstream of receptor tyrosine kinases. SOS-1-mediated GTP loading activates LET-60, enabling interactions with effectors such as LIN-45/Raf and PLC-1 to control vulval induction, oogenesis, learning, and additional Ras-dependent developmental signals.
