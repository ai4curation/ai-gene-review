# WormBase gene description for atg-4.1 (worm)

atg-4.1 encodes a putative cysteine protease orthologous to the autophagic budding yeast protein Atg4p, and to human ATG4A and ATG4B; ATG-4.1 is paralogous to ATG-4.2 and to human ATG4C (OMIM:611339) and ATG4D (OMIM:611340); ATG-4.1 is expressed from early embryos to adults, in larval and adult intestine, renal gland cells, and neurons; ATG-4.1 exhibits protease activity towards LGG-1; ATG-4.1 localizes to the cytoplasm of most cells during embryogenesis.
