# WormBase gene description for atg-2 (worm)

atg-2 encodes a large (2,275-residue) protein orthologous to theautophagic budding yeast protein Atg2p, and to human ATG2A and ATG2B;ATG-2 is expressed in larval and adult pharynx, intestine, body wall   muscles, and neurons, as well as adult reproductive system; ATG-2 has noobvious phenotype in mass RNAi assays.
