# WormBase gene description for atg-5 (worm)

atg-5 encodes, by alternative splicing, two isoforms of an ortholog of the autophagic budding yeast protein Atg5p, and of human ATG5 (OMIM:604261); ATG-5 has no obvious function in mass RNAi assays.
