# WormBase gene description for mes-1 (worm)

The mes-1 gene encodes a receptor tyrosine kinase-like protein that is required for unequal cell divisions in the early embryonic germline; during embryonic cell divisions, mes-1 is involved in positioning of the early mitotic spindle and of associated P granules.
