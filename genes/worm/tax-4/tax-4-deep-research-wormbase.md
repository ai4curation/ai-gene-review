# WormBase gene description for tax-4 (worm)

tax-4 encodes a a cyclic nucleotide-gated channel subunit orthologous to the human gene ROD PHOTORECEPTOR CGMP-GATED CHANNEL (CNGA1; OMIM:123825); tax-4 is required for chemosensation and thermosensation; a TAX-4::GFP fusion protein is expressed in sensory neurons where it localizes to the cilia.
