# WormBase gene description for atg-13 (worm)

atg-13 encodes a divergent ortholog of the autophagic budding yeast protein Atg13p, and a more obvious ortholog of uncharacterized but well-conserved metazoan proteins such as human KIAA0652; ATG-13 is expressed broadly in early (pre-100-cell stage) embryos, in larval and adult ventral nerve cord and other neurons, and in adult pharyngeal muscle.
