# WormBase gene description for let-23 (worm)

let-23 encodes an EGF-receptor-family transmembrane tyrosine kinase that affects viability, inductive signaling during development of the vulva, male spicule formation, posterior development of the epidermis, ovulation and stress induced sleep; let-23 is genetically upstream of the let-60/RAS pathway with respect to viability and vulval development, is upstream of phosopholipase C gamma with respect to fertility and sleep, and is expressed in vulval precursor cells and the ALA neuron.
