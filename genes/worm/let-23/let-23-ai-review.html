Gene Description

let-23 encodes the C. elegans epidermal growth factor receptor, a single-pass receptor tyrosine kinase activated by LIN-3. LET-23 signals from polarized epithelial plasma membranes to initiate Ras/MAPK signaling for vulval induction, male spicule development, fertility-associated signaling, and additional cell fate and morphogenesis events.
