# ife-2 (R04A9.4, Q21693) - manual literature review

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled by hand from cached
publications and PubMed searches ("ife-2 elegans eIF4E", 8 hits, all cached).

## Identity
- IFE-2 is one of five C. elegans eIF4E family members (IFE-1 to IFE-5); 228 aa,
  Pfam IF4E (PF01652), PANTHER PTHR11960:SF11 (UniProt Q21693).
- C. elegans has two cap types: ~70% of mRNAs carry the trans-spliced
  2,2,7-trimethylguanosine (TMG) cap. [PMID:9553113 "mRNAs from 70%
  of the genes contain a different cap structure, m32,2,7GTP"]

## Cap binding (biochemistry)
- IFE-2 is retained on m7GTP-Sepharose and its binding is competed by m3(2,2,7)GTP, so it
  binds both cap types. [PMID:9553113 "binding of IFE-1 and IFE-2 to m7GTP-Sepharose
  was inhibited by m32,2,7GTP"]
- IFE-1, IFE-2 and IFE-5 bind TMG caps, are partially redundant, and at least one is
  required for viability. [PMID:10744754 "three closely related isoforms (IFE-1, IFE-2, and
  IFE-5) bind 2,2, 7-trimethylguanosine caps and are partially redundant, but at least one
  functional isoform is required for viability"]

## Translation and somatic function
- Loss of IFE-2 reduces global protein synthesis, protects from oxidative stress and
  extends lifespan, independently of DAF-16. [PMID:17277769 "loss of a specific eIF4E isoform
  (IFE-2) that functions in somatic tissues reduces global protein synthesis, protects from
  oxidative stress and extends lifespan"] (abstract only)
- ife-2 mutation reduces protein biosynthesis and extends lifespan independently of pha-4.
  [PMID:18804378 "ife-2/eIF4E also reduce protein biosynthesis and extend lifespan, but only
  rsks-1 mutations require pha-4 for adult longevity"]
- Translation-inhibition longevity review/hypothesis. [PMID:17452047]
- IFE-2 is required at 25 C for polysome loading of msh-4/him-14, msh-5, spo-11 mRNAs and
  for meiotic crossover formation. [PMID:20530576 "suggesting that IFE-2 translationally
  upregulates synthesis of MSH-4/HIM-14 and MSH-5 at elevated temperatures to stabilize
  Holliday junctions"]
- In a TORC1-sensitised background (elo-5; nprl-3), ife-2 RNAi restores L1 arrest, like
  RNAi of other TORC1 components/targets, placing IFE-2 as a downstream effector of TORC1
  in postembryonic growth. [PMID:23705068 "We found that elo-5(−) nprl-3(ku540) animals
  treated with ife-2(RNAi) or rsks-1(RNAi), with or without C17ISO supplement, yielded
  results similar to those obtained with raga-1(RNAi) and rheb-1(RNAi)"]

## Localization
- Diffuse cytoplasmic; IFE-2::GFP forms cytoplasmic granules upon heat shock or oxidative
  stress, mostly stress granules. [PMID:25061667 "This indicates that IFE-2 localizes mainly in
  SGs under stress conditions"]
- A later study from the same lab reports increasing sequestration of IFE-2 in P bodies
  during ageing and stress. [PMID:30282029 "the eIF4E isoform IFE-2 is increasingly
  sequestered in mRNA processing (P) bodies during aging and upon stress"] - this
  conflicts with PMID:25061667, which found no age-dependent IFE-2 accumulation in PBs.

## Interactions
- Y2H interaction with MXT-1 (Mextli homolog, a 4E-binding protein) in two interactome
  screens (PMID:14704431, PMID:19123269; IntAct).
