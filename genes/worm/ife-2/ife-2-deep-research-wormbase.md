# WormBase gene description for ife-2 (worm)

ife-2 encodes a translation initiation factor 4F, cap-binding subunit (eIF-4E); by homology, IFE-2 is predicted to function in cap-dependent mRNA translation initiation; loss of ife-2 activity in adult animals extends lifespan.
