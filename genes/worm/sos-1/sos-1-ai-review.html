Gene Description

sos-1 encodes the C. elegans Son of sevenless Ras guanine-nucleotide exchange factor. Acting downstream of RTK adaptors such as SEM-5, SOS-1 promotes GDP release from LET-60/Ras and thereby activates Ras/MAPK signaling downstream of LET-23 and EGL-15 during vulval, gonadal, sex myoblast, and larval developmental outputs.
