# WormBase gene description for dkf-2 (worm)

dkf-2 encodes one of two C. elegans novel protein kinase Ds; dkf-2 activity is essential for regulation of innate immunity; a dkf-2::gfp promoter fusion is expressed beginning in late embryogenesis and continuing through adulthood in the intestine and two cells in the vicinity of the posterior bulb of the pharynx.
