# unc-41 — stonin / stoned B-like synaptotagmin sorting adaptor (P90761) — curation notes

## Identity
- `unc-41` is the single C. elegans stonin-family gene, expressed as two transcripts (A and B); stonins (stoned B in flies, stonin 2 in mammals) are endocytic adaptors dedicated to sorting the synaptic vesicle protein synaptotagmin [PMID:18166656 "elegans genome contains a single member of the stonin family encoded by unc-41, expressed as two alternative transcripts A and B (unpublished data)."] [PMID:18166656 "Stonins are evolutionarily conserved adaptors dedicated to endocytic sorting of the SV protein synaptotagmin."].
- unc-41(e268) is a nonsense allele that abolishes UNC-41 protein and causes severe locomotion defects [PMID:18166656 "As expected, unc-41(e268) mutant worms carrying a nonsense mutation that abrogates UNC-41 protein expression displayed severe locomotion defects, presumably because of impaired neurotransmission."].

## Molecular function
- The mu-homology domain (μHD) of stonins binds basic clusters on the cytoplasmic C2 domains of synaptotagmin 1 through a conserved beta strand carrying the KYE motif; UNC-41B binds synaptotagmin C2 domains like stonin 2, and mutation of the KYE site abolishes binding [PMID:18166656 "The interaction involves the direct association of clusters of basic residues on the surface of the cytoplasmic domain of synaptotagmin 1 and a beta strand within the mu-homology domain of stonin 2."] [PMID:18166656 "UNC-41B expressed in fibroblasts, like its mammalian counterpart stonin 2, was able to associate with the C2 domains of synaptotagmin 1, which is consistent with its putative role in SV recycling."] [PMID:18166656 "Mutation of the KYE site within UNC-41B strongly impaired its synaptotagmin 1– binding ability (Fig."].
- Stonin 2 facilitates synaptotagmin internalisation; the binding-defective mutant fails to accumulate at synapses or to promote internalisation [PMID:18166656 "Mutation of K783, Y784, and E785 to alanine within this stonin 2 beta strand results in failure of the mutant stonin protein to associate with synaptotagmin, to accumulate at synapses, and to facilitate synaptotagmin internalization."].
- In the worm, wild-type UNC-41B but not the δKYE mutant rescues unc-41 paralysis, and GFP–UNC-41B is punctate at synapses while the δKYE mutant is not [PMID:18166656 "This phenotype was rescued by microinjection of a construct expressing WT UNC-41B cDNA but not the synaptotagmin interaction–defective δKYE mutant (Fig."] [PMID:18166656 "When analyzed by confocal microscopy, WT GFP–UNC-41B exhibited a punctate synaptic distribution within the C."].
- Synaptic localisation of AP-2 mu2 (APM-2) does not depend on UNC-41 [PMID:19047463 "Interestingly, synaptic localization of APM-2∷GFP is not dependent on AP180 (unc-11), synaptojanin (unc-26), synaptotagmin (snt-1), or stonin (unc-41) (Fig."].

## Curation decisions
- UNC-41 is a monomeric stonin adaptor that binds synaptotagmin and the AP-2/clathrin machinery; it is not a subunit of the AP-2 heterotetramer. The AP-2 adaptor complex IBA, propagated from the mu2/stonin family node whose experimental seeds are all AP-2 mu subunits, is removed as an over-propagation.
- The two generic protein-binding rows (synaptotagmin) are modified to clathrin-cargo adaptor activity, which is what the interaction demonstrates.
- Positive regulation of locomotion is a downstream organismal phenotype (non-core).

## Additional literature surfaced by deep research (not in GOA; see unc-41-deep-research-falcon.md)
- unc-41 is C27H6.1 with two transcripts (A, 12 exons; B, 10 exons); transgenic Drosophila stonedB rescues unc-41 mutants; UNC-41 carries DPF, FxDxF and WxxF AP-2-ear-binding motifs; unc-41; apm-2 double mutants show no additive synaptic-vesicle loss, placing stonin and AP-2 mu2 in one pathway (Mullen et al. 2012) [file:worm/unc-41/unc-41-deep-research-falcon.md "The *unc-41; apm-2* double mutant showed essentially the same synaptic-vesicle deficit as *unc-41* alone rather than a further reduction."].
