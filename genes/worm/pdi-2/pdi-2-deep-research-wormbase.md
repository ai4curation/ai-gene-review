# WormBase gene description for pdi-2 (worm)

pdi-2 encodes a protein disulfide isomerase beta subunit; by homology, PDI-2 is predicted to function in oxidative protein folding in the endoplasmic reticulum (ER); PDI-2 activity is required for several organismal processes including body morphology, locomotion, fertility, molting, and collagen deposition; pdi-2 transcript levels are increased in response to ER stress; pdi-2 is expressed in the hypodermis which secretes the collagen-based cuticle.
