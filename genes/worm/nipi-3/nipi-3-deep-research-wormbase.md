# WormBase gene description for nipi-3 (worm)

nipi-3 encodes a kinase similar to the human kinase Tribbles homolog 1; nipi-3 functions in the innate immune response, likely upstream of the SEK-1 MAPKK, to upregulate expression of NLP-29, an antimicrobial peptide, in response to fungal infection.
