# WormBase gene description for gcy-31 (worm)

gcy-31 encodes a soluble guanylyl cyclase; GCY-31 activity is required in the ciliated BAG head sensory neurons to sense decreases in O2 levels and effect corresponding behavioral changes; a gcy-31::GFP reporter is expressed in the BAG neurons.
