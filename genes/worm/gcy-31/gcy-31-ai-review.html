Gene Description

GCY-31 is an atypical soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) subunit of Caenorhabditis elegans of the beta3-like clade (related to Drosophila Gyc-88E), with an H-NOX heme domain, an HNOB-associated domain, a coiled coil and a C-terminal guanylate cyclase domain. It is expressed strongly in the BAG gas-sensing neurons, where together with GCY-33 (probably as a heterodimer) it is required for BAG activation by a decrease in O2. A fall in O2 is thought to relieve O2 inhibition of the heme-regulated cyclase, raising cGMP and opening TAX-2/TAX-4 cGMP-gated channels. GCY-31 is required for O2-downshift locomotory responses and starvation-enhanced hyperoxia avoidance, but not for BAG CO2 sensing, which uses the receptor guanylyl cyclase GCY-9. Three splice isoforms are annotated.

Core Functions

Atypical O2-regulated soluble guanylate cyclase subunit that, with GCY-33, produces cGMP in BAG neurons when O2 falls, opening TAX-2/TAX-4 channels.
