# WormBase gene description for daf-10 (worm)

daf-10 encodes a WD- and WAA-repeat containing protein that is the C. elegans ortholog of intraflagellar transport (IFT) complex A component IFT122; daf-10 activity is required for intraflagellar transport and thus for proper development of amphid and phasmid neurons, dauer development, chemotaxis, and normal lifespan; a DAF-10::GFP fusion protein undergoes both anterograde and retrograde intraflagellar transport in amphid or phasmid sensory neurons.
