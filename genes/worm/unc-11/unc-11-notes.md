# unc-11 — AP180 clathrin assembly protein (Q9XZI6) — curation notes

## Identity and architecture
- `unc-11` (C32E8.10) is the sole C. elegans homologue of the neuronal clathrin assembly protein AP180 (PICALM/SNAP91 family), rescued by cosmid C32E8 [PMID:10397769 "We demonstrate here that the Caenorhabditis elegans gene unc-11 encodes a homologue of AP180."] [PMID:10397769 "unc-11 maps genetically between the fog-1 and dpy-5 loci on chromosome I, and a cosmid contained within this interval, C32E8, rescued the unc-11 locomotion phenotype (Figure 1 )."]. The genome contains no second AP180 homologue [PMID:10397769 "elegans because analyses of the complete genome (consortium, 1998) and EST data bases have not revealed another AP180 homologue in C."].
- Multiple isoforms (a–h) share a conserved N-terminal ANTH domain (UniProt DOMAIN 29..161 ENTH-type) carrying the KKK phosphoinositide-binding motif, and diverge in a C-terminal low-complexity assembly domain [PMID:10397769 "Alignments of UNC-11 with AP180 family members suggest the protein consists of at least two domains: an N-terminal 290-amino acid domain that is well-conserved among metazoans (UNC-11 shares 62.8% identity with mouse AP180 in this region), and a C-terminal domain that contains only minimally conserved motifs (27.1% identity with mouse AP180 in this region)."] [PMID:10397769 "both AP180 and UNC-11 share three adjacent lysine residues in this region."]. The C-terminal region promotes clathrin assembly in vitro (cited as unpublished data) [PMID:10397769 "Likewise, the C-terminal region of UNC-11 promotes clathrin assembly in vitro despite minimal sequence similarity with AP180 (Golan, Prasad, Lafer, and Alfonso, unpublished data)."].

## Localisation
- High in the nervous system, enriched at presynaptic sites and also in cell bodies; lower in coelomocytes, uv1 cells and intestine [PMID:10397769 "In neurons, UNC-11 is enriched at presynaptic terminals but is also present in cell bodies."] [PMID:10397769 "We also detected UNC-11 expression in the coelomocytes (Figure 4 D) and diffuse staining in the intestine (Figure 4 , A, C, and D)."]. Axonal transport of UNC-11 is independent of UNC-104 [PMID:10397769 "Furthermore, UNC-11 is transported down the axon independently of the kinesin-like motor protein UNC-104."].
- UniProt (curated from the same paper) records clathrin-coated pit, clathrin-coated vesicle and Golgi localisation [file:worm/unc-11/unc-11-uniprot.txt "Note=Colocalized with clathrin in the Golgi area."].

## Function at the synapse
- unc-11 null mutants are viable but uncoordinated (coiling, jerky backing) and defective in release of multiple neurotransmitters [PMID:10397769 "Mutations in unc-11 were first identified by Brenner (1974) based on their uncoordinated locomotory phenotype: the mutant animals coil and jerk, particularly when going backward."].
- Synaptobrevin is specifically mislocalised (diffuse, plasma-membrane-like) while other synaptic vesicle proteins are targeted normally [PMID:10397769 "Furthermore, the UNC-11 protein is required to localize synaptobrevin, but not other synaptic vesicle proteins, to synaptic sites."].
- Synaptic membrane is still endocytosed, but vesicles are 33% larger in diameter (29.5 nm → 39–40 nm), i.e. AP180 sets vesicle size during clathrin coat assembly [PMID:10397769 "Finally, in unc-11 mutants, synaptic membrane still undergoes endocytosis but vesicle diameter is significantly larger."] [PMID:10397769 "In the two null mutants, mean vesicle diameter was 33% larger ( unc-11(e47) 39.1 ± 7.3 nm; unc-11(q358) 40.4 ± 8.0 nm)."] [PMID:10397769 "We conclude that AP180 provides dual functions for the biogenesis of synaptic vesicles: to recruit synaptobrevin to synaptic vesicle membranes and to regulate vesicle diameter during clathrin assembly."].
- unc-11; snb-1(hypomorph) double mutants arrest at L1, the only developmental-arrest phenotype reported [PMID:10397769 "In fact, the double mutants unc-11(e47); snb-1(md247) and unc-11(n2954) ; snb-1(md247) arrest development in the first larval stage similar to snb-1(js124) null mutants."].
- Deep research (file:worm/unc-11/unc-11-deep-research-falcon.md) adds a 2026 study showing the disordered assembly domain forms actin-recruiting condensates and bridges PI(4,5)P2 liposomes to F-actin, with actin coupling setting vesicle size [file:worm/unc-11/unc-11-deep-research-falcon.md "Full-length protein associated with 50-nm liposomes containing 2% PIP₂ and bridged them to F-actin, whereas phosphatidylcholine-only liposomes showed minimal association."], plus a role in sorting ATG-9 at synapses (Xuan et al. 2023).

## Postsynaptic GLR-1 endocytosis
- Ubiquitin overexpression lowers GLR-1 abundance at synapses and this requires unc-11 (abstract only) [PMID:12123612 "overexpression of ubiquitin decreased the abundance of GLR-1 at synapses and decreased the density of GLR-1-containing synapses, and these effects were prevented by mutations in the unc-11 gene, which encodes a clathrin adaptin protein (AP180)."].
- UNC-11 is required for GLR-1 endocytosis; unc-11 mutations suppress the GLR-1 loss seen in rab-6.2 retromer-pathway mutants [PMID:22213799 "GLR-1 AMPARs undergo activity-dependent endocytosis in a process that requires UNC-11/AP180 (Fig."] [PMID:22213799 "We analyzed GLR-1::GFP localization in rab-6.2; unc-11 double mutants and found that mutations in unc-11 prevent the decrease in GLR-1::GFP puncta number and fluorescence intensity observed in rab-6.2 mutants (Fig."].

## Interactome rows
- High-throughput Y2H/pooling interactome studies (PMID:14704431, 19123269, 19447967, 23549480) list EYA-1, ITSN-1, MAGU-1 and SORB-1 as binders; the ITSN-1 (intersectin SH3) and SORB-1 interactions are consistent with NPF/proline-motif accessory-protein contacts of AP180 family proteins, but none defines a distinct GO molecular function.

## Curation decisions
- Core: PI(4,5)P2 binding and clathrin heavy chain binding driving clathrin coat assembly at the presynaptic membrane, SNARE (synaptobrevin) cargo sorting into regenerated synaptic vesicles, and control of vesicle size; GLR-1 receptor endocytosis as a well-supported additional cargo context.
- Non-core/downstream: locomotion, Golgi localisation, GLR-1 synaptic abundance regulation.
- Over-annotated: post-embryonic development (only synthetic with snb-1); ubiquitin-dependent protein catabolic process (UNC-11 performs the internalisation step, degradation not shown).
- Generic protein binding rows removed as uninformative; necroptotic process IGI left UNDECIDED (abstract-only, unc-11 not named).
