# WormBase gene description for unc-11 (worm)

unc-11 encodes multiple isoforms of a clathrin-adaptor protein AP180 (OMIM:603025) that functions in clathrin-mediated endocytosis; UNC-11 is essential for embryogenesis, locomotion, and pharyngeal pumping, and regulates neurotransmitter release by controlling vesicle trafficking and fusion; UNC-11 is also required for localization of SNB-1/synaptobrevin to synaptic vesicles, thus acting as a sorting protein as well as a clathrin-adaptor protein; UNC-11 is expressed at high levels in nearly all neurons, where it is enriched at presynaptic terminals, and is also expressed in coelomocytes, the uv1 secretory cells of the vulva, and the intestine.
