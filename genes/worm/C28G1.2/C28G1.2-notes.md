# C28G1.2 (Q18287) notes

Full re-review, 2026-10-20. Independent of any machine-learning name/function prediction.

## Identity

- Q18287 is the only UniProt entry for WBGene00016187 (C28G1.2, chromosome X); 265 aa,
  TrEMBL, PE 4 (predicted). The Pfam Serpin domain is called only over residues 96-199.
- It is not an isoform or fragment of another C. elegans gene. It is much shorter than
  the full-length worm serpins (SRP-2 359 aa, SRP-3 362 aa) because it lacks an internal
  block and the reactive-centre loop (RCL) hinge; the C. briggsae PANTHER sister
  CBG15466 (A8XM85, 263 aa) shares exactly the same architecture, so the short product is
  a conserved Caenorhabditis feature rather than a C. elegans gene-model defect
  [file:worm/C28G1.2/C28G1.2-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md].

## Phylogeny

- PANTHER PTHR11461 (serpin), subfamily SF332 (Caenorhabditis-restricted, no characterised
  member). In the PANTHER 19 tree, C28G1.2 + CBG15466 (PTN007508270) sit in PTN002479232
  with srp-1, srp-2, srp-3, srp-6, srp-7, srp-8, within the Rhabditida clade PTN002479231.
- These worm serpins form the nematode-specific clade L, related to vertebrate clade B
  intracellular serpins [PMID:25786854 "the C. elegans serpins, like the clade B serpins,
  form their own clade (L)"].
- Nearest characterised relatives: SRP-2 (O17365) and SRP-3 (G5ECY2), paralogs in the same
  Caenorhabditis node; PANTHER does not resolve one as closer. Neither has a repo review.

## Literature

- Whisstock et al. 1999 genome survey: worm serpins range from complete to heavily
  deleted [PMID:10373004 "Some appear complete, some show extensive deletions, and others
  appear to contain only the C-terminal part of the known serpin fold"]. Abstract only;
  C28G1.2 not named in the abstract.
- Silverman et al. 2015 (full text): worm serpins are intracellular; only SRP-1, -2, -3,
  -6, -7 are full-length inhibitors [PMID:25786854 "only SRP-1 , SRP-2 , SRP-3 , SRP-6 ,
  and SRP-7 are synthesized as full-length proteins and serve as bona fide proteinase
  inhibitors, with the rest being transcribed pseudogenes or noninhibitory variants"];
  they lack signal peptides and GFP fusions are cytoplasmic [PMID:25786854 "Green
  fluorescent protein (GFP)–nematode serpin fusions show a diffuse cytoplasmic appearance
  and no evidence of secretion"]. SRP-2 is an obligate intracellular serpin.
- Pak et al. 2004 (SRP-2, cross-class inhibitor; PMID:14739286) and Pak et al. 2006 (SRP-3,
  chymotrypsin-like protease inhibitor, muscle; PMID:16584183): abstract-only; comparators,
  not C28G1.2 evidence.
- Falcon deep research found no publication on C28G1.2/Q18287.

## Annotation decisions

- GO:0005576 extracellular region (IBA, root node PTN000156123): REMOVE. The root IBD
  reflects secreted serpins (clades A, C-I, plant and insect serpins). C28G1.2 sits in the
  nematode intracellular clade whose characterised members lack signal peptides and are
  cytosolic and not secreted; the target itself has no signal peptide feature. The same
  root IBA also lands on SRP-2, where it contradicts direct imaging.
- No NEW terms: no target-specific localization or activity data, and the RCL deletion
  argues against transferring endopeptidase inhibitor activity. PAINT's
  GO:0004867 IBD at PTN002479231 has not reached C28G1.2 (nor srp-1/2/3) in GOA; even if it
  does, the RCL-less architecture would argue against it for this gene.
