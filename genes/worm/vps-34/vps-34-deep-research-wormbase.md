# WormBase gene description for vps-34 (worm)

vps-34 encodes an ortholog of the phosphoinositide 3-kinase VPS34 in S. cerevisiae, a protein that regulates multiple steps in endocytosis, and that is required for growth at normal rates during development; in C. elegans, vps-34 is required for vesicular trafficking, including endocytosis, apoptotic cell clearance, and autophagy.
