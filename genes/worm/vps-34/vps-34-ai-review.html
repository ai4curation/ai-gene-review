Gene Description

VPS-34 (LET-512, B0025.1) is the sole class III phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase of C. elegans, the ortholog of yeast Vps34 and human PIK3C3. It is a lipid kinase that phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol at the D-3 position of the inositol ring to make phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PtdIns3P); immunoprecipitated worm VPS-34 has wortmannin-sensitive PtdIns-specific kinase activity. VPS-34 acts in complexes with the Beclin ortholog BEC-1 and the regulatory kinase VPS-15, and probably with EPG-8/ATG14 (autophagy-specific complex I) or UVRAG (endocytic complex II). The PtdIns3P it makes marks early endosomes, phagosomes and autophagosome formation sites. There it recruits FYVE, PX and WD40 (PROPPIN) effectors that drive endosome fusion and maturation, endosome-to-Golgi retrograde transport, maturation of phagosomes containing apoptotic cells (with the class II PI3K PIKI-1), and autophagosome formation. The protein is expressed in all tissues and concentrates at the nuclear envelope and perinuclear region. Loss of vps-34 is lethal, with arrest at the L3/L4 molts. Mutants have cuticle-shedding defects, an expanded outer nuclear membrane, mislocalised apical LRP-1 and no detectable membrane-bound PtdIns3P. Through its role in autophagy, vps-34 is also needed for the long life span of germline-less animals.

Core Functions

VPS-34 is the catalytic subunit of the autophagy-specific class III PI3K complex I (with BEC-1, VPS-15 and EPG-8/ATG14). It phosphorylates PtdIns to make PtdIns3P at autophagosome formation sites.

  the ortholog of yeast Vps34 and human PIK3C3. It is a lipid kinase that phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol
  at the D-3 position of the inositol ring to make phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PtdIns3P); immunoprecipitated
  worm VPS-34 has wortmannin-sensitive PtdIns-specific kinase activity. VPS-34 acts in complexes with
  the Beclin ortholog BEC-1 and the regulatory kinase VPS-15, and probably with EPG-8/ATG14 (autophagy-specific
  complex I) or UVRAG (endocytic complex II). The PtdIns3P it makes marks early endosomes, phagosomes
  and autophagosome formation sites. There it recruits FYVE, PX and WD40 (PROPPIN) effectors that drive
  endosome fusion and maturation, endosome-to-Golgi retrograde transport, maturation of phagosomes containing
  apoptotic cells (with the class II PI3K PIKI-1), and autophagosome formation. The protein is expressed
  in all tissues and concentrates at the nuclear envelope and perinuclear region. Loss of vps-34 is lethal,
  with arrest at the L3/L4 molts. Mutants have cuticle-shedding defects, an expanded outer nuclear membrane,
  mislocalised apical LRP-1 and no detectable membrane-bound PtdIns3P. Through its role in autophagy,
  vps-34 is also needed for the long life span of germline-less animals.
