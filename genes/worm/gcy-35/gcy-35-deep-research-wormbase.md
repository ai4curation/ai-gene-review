# WormBase gene description for gcy-35 (worm)

gcy-35 encodes a soluble guanylyl cyclase; GCY-35 activity is required for regulation of aggregation, aerotaxis, aerokinesis, gustatory plasticity, and innate immunity; GCY-35 activity is also required in the URX sensory neurons to sense increases in O2 levels and effect corresponding behavioral changes; gcy-35::gfp reporter fusions are expressed in a number of neurons, including the AQR, PQR, and URX sensory neurons, the SDQL/R and BDU interneurons, the AVM and PVM mechanosensory neurons, the cholinergic ALN and PLN neurons, the excretory cell, and in pharyngeal and body wall muscles; gcy-35 expression in the URX neuron is positively regulated by the AHR-1 transcription complex; the heme-binding domain of GCY-35 binds molecular oxygen as well as NO and CO.
