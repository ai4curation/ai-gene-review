# WormBase gene description for hsp-60 (worm)

hsp-60 encodes a nuclear-encoded mitochondrion-specific chaperone that is a member of the GroE/Hsp10/60 superfamily of molecular chaperones; hsp-60 is involved in the mitochondrial unfolded protein response, as hsp-60 expression, normally broadly detected during development, is greatly increased in response to treatments that disrupt mitochondrial protein handling; loss of hsp-60 activity via RNAi results in severe growth defects with arrest at embryonic and early larval stages of development.
