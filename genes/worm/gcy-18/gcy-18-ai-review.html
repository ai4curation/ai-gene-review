Gene Description

gcy-18 encodes a receptor-type (transmembrane) guanylate cyclase expressed exclusively in the AFD thermosensory neurons of C. elegans. With GCY-8, GCY-18 and GCY-23 it forms an AFD-specific subfamily; each has an extracellular domain with little similarity to mammalian receptor guanylyl cyclases, a single transmembrane helix, an intracellular kinase-homology domain predicted to be catalytically inactive, and a C-terminal guanylate cyclase domain that converts GTP to cGMP. GCY-18 is concentrated at the AFD sensory ending (the microvillar receptive ending). The three AFD cyclases act partly redundantly to supply the cGMP that opens the TAX-2/TAX-4 cyclic nucleotide-gated channel in response to warming; triple mutants are athermotactic and lack temperature-evoked AFD currents. GCY-18 acts as the primary AFD cyclase at higher cultivation temperatures; gcy-18 mutants still track isotherms but in a range shifted toward colder temperatures, and their AFD neurons have a lowered temperature response threshold, indicating that GCY-18 sets the operating range of thermosensation.
