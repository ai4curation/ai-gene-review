Gene Description

RRF-1 is a C. elegans cellular RNA-directed RNA polymerase (RdRP) that synthesizes secondary small interfering RNAs (22G-RNAs). Its products are unprimed, 5'-triphosphorylated antisense RNAs made on target mRNA templates, and they are loaded into worm-specific Argonautes (WAGOs) to amplify and sustain silencing. RRF-1 is the RdRP of somatic RNAi and, in the germline, the RdRP of the Mutator pathway: it colocalizes with MUT-16 in perinuclear Mutator foci, where it is thought to use poly(UG)-tailed mRNA fragments made by RDE-3 as templates, and it binds UG-repeat RNA. RRF-1 associates with the Dicer-related helicase DRH-3 in RdRP complexes and acts partly redundantly with the germline RdRP EGO-1. It is required for exogenous RNAi, for transposon and endogenous gene silencing.

Core Functions

Synthesizes unprimed, 5'-triphosphorylated antisense secondary siRNAs (22G-RNAs) on target mRNA templates, plausibly pUG-tailed mRNA fragments, as part of an RdRP complex with DRH-3. In germ cells it acts in Mutator foci.
