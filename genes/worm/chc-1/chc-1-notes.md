# chc-1 (rme-3) — clathrin heavy chain (P34574) — curation notes

## Identity and alleles
- `chc-1` (T20G5.1) encodes the single clathrin heavy chain of C. elegans; the yolk-endocytosis mutant rme-3 is chc-1 [PMID:19151157 "Results rme-3 Encodes Caenorhabditis elegans Clathrin Heavy Chain."]. chc-1(b1024) is a promoter Tc5 insertion and chc-1(b1025ts) a 9-bp deletion around the stop codon that destabilises the hub domain [PMID:19151157 "In chc-1(b1025ts) , we identified a 9-bp deletion centered around the stop codon of the chc-1 gene."]. Loss of clathrin heavy chain is embryonic lethal in metazoans, so the ts allele was used to study acute loss [PMID:19151157 "Unfortunately, loss of clathrin heavy chain (CHC) is embryonically lethal in metazoans ( 5 , 6 ), precluding assays of presynaptic function in such mutants."].
- Clathrin forms triskelia of three heavy and three light chains; the C-terminal hub domain drives self-assembly and binds clathrin light chain [PMID:19151157 "Clathrin forms a triskelion, composed of 3 heavy and light chains."] [PMID:19151157 "The C-terminal one-third of CHC is often referred to as the “hub domain.” This region of CHC is necessary and sufficient for clathrin self-assembly into triskelia and also provides the binding interface for CLC ( 13 )."]. b1025 hub domains self-associate weakly, especially at high temperature [PMID:19151157 "b1025 mutant hub domains interacted with one another more weakly than wild-type hubs at 30°C and displayed a further weakened interaction at 37°C, suggesting that the b1025 mutation tends to impair clathrin assembly, especially at higher temperatures ( Fig."].

## Localisation
- Oocytes: endogenous CHC in small punctate structures at the plasma membrane (coated pits/vesicles); lost from the cortex in b1025ts [PMID:19151157 "In wild-type oocytes, endogenous CHC is localized largely to small punctate structures associated with the plasma membrane of oocytes, as expected for clathrin localized to coated pits and vesicles ( Fig."]. Tagged mutant protein fails to localise to the plasma membrane in gonad, intestine and synaptic regions at restrictive temperature [PMID:19151157 "at the restrictive temperature failed to localize correctly to the plasma membrane in the gonad, intestine, and synaptic regions of neurons ( Figs."].
- Coelomocytes/hypodermis: mRFP1::CHC-1 marks plasma-membrane coated pits with alpha-adaptin and RME-6 [PMID:15895077 "In contrast, we found strong colocalization of GFP::RME-6 with mRFP1::CHC-1 and mRFP1::α-adaptin on or near the PM of coelomocytes."].
- Synapses: mRFP-CHC puncta overlap GFP-synaptobrevin at presynaptic varicosities, adjacent to SYD-2 active zones [PMID:19151157 "mRFP-CHC was enriched in puncta in the neuronal processes with significant spatial overlap with GFP-synaptobrevin (GFP-SNB-1), a synaptic vesicle marker, demonstrating localization of CHC to the presynaptic varicosities in vivo ( Fig."].
- Phagosomes: GFP::CHC-1 and mCherry::CHC-1 surround apoptotic germ-cell corpses in a CED-1/CED-6-dependent manner and rescue chc-1(b1025ts) corpse accumulation [PMID:23696751 "The fusions of CHC-1 with GFP and mCherry were functional in that both GFP::CHC-1 driven by the chc-1 promoter (yqEx480) and mCherry::CHC-1 driven by the ced-1 promoter (yqIs98) rescued the cell corpse phenotype in chc-1(b1025ts) mutants (Figure S2B)."].

## Functions
- Receptor-mediated yolk endocytosis: clathrin RNAi or chc-1 mutation blocks YP170::GFP uptake into oocytes [PMID:10588660 "elegans clathrin heavy chain, α-adaptin, and β-adaptin RNAi all produced similar defects in oocytes and embryos."] [PMID:19151157 "chc-1(b1025ts) exhibited a ts defect in yolk uptake with variable uptake at the permissive temperature and uniformly blocked uptake at the restrictive temperature ( Fig."] [PMID:19798448 "We observed a similar degree of inhibition of endocytosis after cdc-42(RNAi), while ablation of chc-1/Clathrin completely blocked YP170 accumulation by oocytes (Figure 3B) [35]."]. chc-1 RNAi also delocalises the coated-pit protein RME-6 [PMID:15895077 "In chc-1 (RNAi) animals, cortical GFP::RME-6 in oocytes disappeared almost completely (27/30 animals assayed)."].
- Synapse: b1025ts animals paralyse within minutes at restrictive temperature, yet synaptic vesicle number and mini frequency are normal, so high clathrin activity is dispensable for maintaining vesicle pools [PMID:19151157 "chc-1(b1025ts) animals become paralyzed within minutes of exposure to the restrictive temperature because of a defect in the nervous system."] [PMID:19151157 "Surprisingly synaptic vesicle number is not reduced in chc-1(b1025ts) animals."] [PMID:19151157 "Taken together, these results indicate that a high level of CHC activity is required for receptor-mediated endocytosis in nonneuronal cells but is largely dispensable for maintenance of synaptic vesicle pools."].
- Apoptotic-cell clearance: chc-1 RNAi/b1025ts cause persistent corpses, 56% not fully engulfed, with failed actin recruitment and phagosome maturation; CHC-1 C-terminus binds CED-6 directly [PMID:23696751 "In chc-1(RNAi) animals, 28 out of 50 germ cell corpses examined (56%) from 5 gonad arms appeared not to be fully engulfed by sheath cells (Figure 1D)."] [PMID:23696751 "Thus, loss of chc-1 and apb-1 resulted in a failure in actin cytoskeleton rearrangement required for cell corpse engulfment, like that caused by loss of ced-1 or ced-6."].
- Epidermal E-cadherin polarity (abstract only): loss of clathrin, AP-1 or SOAP-1 mislocalises E-cadherin basolaterally [PMID:25858456 "We found that the loss of clathrin, its adaptor AP-1 and the AP-1 interactor SOAP-1 induced a basolateral localisation of E-cad without affecting the apico-basal diffusion barrier."].
- One-cell embryo (abstract only): CHC-1 limits cortical pulling forces on centrosomes and spindle poles by promoting acto-myosin cortical tension [PMID:24961801 "Here, we report that the clathrin heavy chain CHC-1 negatively regulates pulling forces acting on centrosomes during interphase and on spindle poles during mitosis in one-cell C. elegans embryos."].
- DAB-1 localisation in oocytes depends on clathrin (abstract only) [PMID:17636000 "The localisation of DAB-1 in oocytes is itself dependent upon clathrin and AP2, consistent with DAB-1 acting as a clathrin-associated sorting protein during yolk protein endocytosis."].

## Curation decisions
- Core: structural constituent of the clathrin coat (triskelion) at plasma-membrane coated pits and endocytic vesicles, driving receptor-mediated/clathrin-dependent endocytosis; clathrin light chain binding via the hub; coat of TGN/AP-1 vesicles for polarised sorting.
- Regulatory terms (positive regulation of endocytosis / of clathrin-dependent endocytosis) are modified to the process itself: clathrin executes endocytosis rather than regulating it.
- Lifespan (IGI), mitotic spindle (IDA) rest on abstract-only records that do not name chc-1 or the localisation; left UNDECIDED.
- Generic CED-6 protein binding is removed per policy; the interaction stands.

## Additional literature surfaced by deep research (not in GOA; see chc-1-deep-research-falcon.md)
- Temperature-sensitive chc-1 studies with optogenetic stimulation and EM place clathrin's synaptic role mainly in budding vesicles from synaptic endosomes, with endophilins compensating at the plasma membrane [file:worm/chc-1/chc-1-deep-research-falcon.md "CHC-1 acts principally in budding new synaptic vesicles from synaptic endosomes; endophilins can compensate for clathrin loss at the plasma membrane."].
- chc-1 disruption phenocopies AP-1 (unc-101) loss in ODR-10 ciliary sorting (Kaplan et al. 2010) and perturbs intestinal tubulogenesis with AP-1 (Zhang et al. 2012); CRISPR alleles modelling human CLTC P890L/L1047P (CHC-1 P892L/L1049P) cause synaptic-vesicle depletion and transmission defects (Pannone et al. 2023).
- CLIC-1 (light chain) loss is comparatively mild, whereas chc-1 loss is lethal [file:worm/chc-1/chc-1-deep-research-falcon.md "CHC-1 is genetically much more essential: *chc-1* RNAi or temperature-sensitive inactivation disrupts endocytosis and viability, whereas CLIC-1 loss alone is comparatively mild."].
