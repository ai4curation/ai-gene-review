# WormBase gene description for klp-11 (worm)

klp-11 encodes a kinesin motor protein that functions as a subunit of C. elegans heterotrimeric kinesin-II; in vitro microtubule gliding assays demonstrate that purified kinesin-II and OSM-3, a homomeric kinesin-II motor, cooperatively generate movement similar to that seen along cilia lacking additional regulatory factors; antibodies to KLP-11 detect high intensity staining in the cilia of chemosensory neurons, with fainter staining detected in the dorsal and ventral nerve cords as well as motor commissures.
