# WormBase gene description for gcy-36 (worm)

gcy-36 encodes a soluble guanylate cyclase; GCY-36 activity is required in the URX sensory neurons to sense increases in O2 levels and effect corresponding behavioral changes; gcy-36 genetically interacts with npr-1 with respect to feeding behavior; a gcy-36 reporter fusion is expressed in the AQR, PQR, and URX neurons.
