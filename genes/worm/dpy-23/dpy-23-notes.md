# dpy-23 (apm-2) — AP-2 complex subunit mu (P35603) — curation notes

## Identity
- `dpy-23` (also `apm-2`, ORF R160.1) encodes the only mu2 (AP50) subunit of the AP-2 clathrin adaptor complex in C. elegans [PMID:19047463 "Here, we characterize the apm-2 gene (also called dpy-23), which encodes the only micro2 subunit in the nematode Caenorhabditis elegans."].
- Two loss-of-function alleles: e840 (≈100 kb deletion removing the whole coding sequence plus 17 neighbouring ORFs) and gm17 (splice-donor point mutation in the last intron) [PMID:19047463 "Genomic Southern analysis showed that apm-2(e840) contains a deletion of ∼100 kb that includes the entire coding sequence of μ2 adaptin."].
- Mutants are dumpy, slightly uncoordinated and egg-laying defective, and all three defects are rescued by a 12 kb genomic fragment [PMID:19047463 "A 12-kb genomic PCR fragment (5 kb upstream, 5 kb coding sequence of μ2 adaptin, and 2 kb downstream) could fully rescue the dumpy, uncoordinated, and egg-laying defects of dpy-23(e840) and dpy-23(gm17)"].

## Complex membership and molecular activity
- Mu2 is the cargo-recognition subunit of AP-2: it binds tyrosine-based YxxΦ sorting motifs and contributes to membrane (PI(4,5)P2) association [PMID:19047463 "In particular, the μ2 subunit recruits cargo proteins containing the tyrosine-based Yxxφ motif (Owen and Evans, 1998) and mediates in part the association of the AP2 complex to membranes"] [PMID:25303366 "Tyrosine motifs comprised of YxxΦ, where x is any amino acid, and Φ is a large hydrophobic residue, bind the mu2 subunit of AP2 and are required for AP2-mediated internalization"].
- Loss of mu2 destabilises the complex: alpha-adaptin (APA-2) is reduced to 60% (e840) and 12% (gm17), but residual alpha remains bound to sigma2 and still localises to coelomocyte plasma membrane and synapses [PMID:19047463 "The reduction in α adaptin levels in both alleles indicates that μ2 is required to stabilize the AP2 complex; however, some residual α adaptin remains in the absence of μ2."].
- In the FCHo/SGIP allosteric-activation study, apm-2 mutants phenocopy the other AP-2 subunit mutants (Dpy, Egl, Unc, "jowls"); bypass suppressors of fcho-1 map to all four AP-2 subunits, including residues at the mu2 interface and the mu2 phosphorylation site, and an HA/TEV-tagged endogenous mu2 was used to show that FCHo promotes the open, protease-sensitive conformation of AP-2 in vivo [PMID:25303366 "A subsequent screen for bypass suppressors of fcho-1 nulls identified 71 compensatory mutations in all four AP2 subunits."] [PMID:25303366 "(4) the phosphorylation site on the mu2 subunit."].

## Endocytic roles
- Synapse: APM-2::GFP is expressed in virtually all tissues; in GABA neurons it localises to synapses, colocalising with synaptobrevin and clathrin heavy chain [PMID:19047463 "APM-2∷GFP is localized at synapses and colocalizes with the synaptic vesicle protein synaptobrevin/VAMP"] [PMID:19047463 "In addition, APM-2∷GFP colocalizes with C-terminal RFP-tagged clathrin heavy chain (CHC) at the synapse"]. In apm-2 mutants clathrin is mislocalised at synapses and synaptic vesicle number and evoked responses fall to ~60–65% of wild type, but synaptic vesicles are still recycled; mu2 therefore facilitates but is not essential for synaptic vesicle recycling [PMID:19047463 "In apm-2 mutants, clathrin is mislocalized at synapses, and synaptic vesicle numbers and evoked responses are reduced to 60 and 65%, respectively."] [PMID:19047463 "However, synaptic vesicles are still recycled in the absence of μ2."].
- Oocyte yolk uptake: mu2 RNAi did not reduce YP170::GFP uptake, unlike chc-1, apa-2 and apb-1 RNAi [PMID:10588660 "Unlike the other CCP components we tested, Ce- μ2(RNAi) and Ce- ς2(RNAi) did not result in an apparent reduction in YP170::GFP uptake into the oocyte."], and >98% of apm-2 null gonads still take up yolk, so mu2 is less important than alpha/beta2 for yolk endocytosis [PMID:19047463 "Over 98% of the gonads in both apm-2(e840) and apm-2(gm17) mutants were scored as positive for yolk protein uptake"]. RNAi of mu2 produces severely Dpy progeny [PMID:10588660 "All progeny of Ce- μ2(RNAi) and Ce- ς2(RNAi) mothers were severely dumpy (Dpy)."].
- Wnt secretion: dpy-23 mutants show Wnt-like phenotypes (HSN and Q-descendant migration, ALM/PLM polarity, asymmetric division); DPY-23 acts in EGL-20/Wnt-producing hypodermal cells, and the Wntless orthologue MIG-14 accumulates at or near the plasma membrane in dpy-23 mutants, indicating AP-2-dependent endocytosis of MIG-14 is required for its retromer-mediated retrieval and hence Wnt secretion [PMID:18160346 "dpy-23 mutants display Wnt phenotypes, including defects in neuronal migration, neuronal polarity, and asymmetric cell division."] [PMID:18160346 "DPY-23 acts in Wnt-expressing cells to promote these processes."] [PMID:18160346 "In dpy-23 mutants, MIG-14 accumulates at or near the plasma membrane."].
- Apoptotic-cell clearance: RNAi of apa-2, apb-1 or dpy-23 increases germ-cell corpses in the same genetic pathway as ced-1/ced-6; DPY-23::GFP surrounds corpses, and DPY-23 binds CED-6, DYN-1 and LST-4 directly in GST pull-downs and co-IPs with CED-6 and LST-4 in vivo [PMID:23696751 "A time-course analysis confirmed that germ cell corpses increased significantly in an age-dependent manner in animals with RNAi of apa-2, apb-1, and dpy-23, but not aps-2"] [PMID:23696751 "GST-CED-6 directly interacted with 35S-labeled APA-2, APB-1 and DPY-23, and His6-tagged CHC-1C (Figure 4A)."].
- Sensory cilia: DPY-23::GFP is enriched at the periciliary membrane compartment (PCMC) of AWB [PMID:31259686 "In wild-type animals, a DPY-23::GFP AP-2 μ2 subunit fusion protein was enriched at the PCMC of AWB in a domain similar to that occupied by TUB-1"]; AP-2 mutants show expanded ciliary/periciliary membranes and ciliary protein mislocalisation (abstract only) [PMID:22342749 "Disruption of endocytic gene function causes expansion of ciliary and/or periciliary membranes as well as defects in the ciliary targeting and/or transport dynamics of ciliary transmembrane and IFT proteins."]. In dpy-23(e840) the PI(4,5)P2 reporter is shifted proximally in the AWB dendrite and TUB-1 occupies a larger area at the ciliary base; tub-1 loss suppresses the expanded AWB ciliary membrane of dpy-23 [PMID:31259686 "As in odr-1 mutants, loss of tub-1 fully suppressed the expanded ciliary membrane phenotype of dpy-23 mutants (Figure 7C)"].
- Necrosis: the necroptosis IGI (PMID:22157748) is abstract-only in the cache; the abstract names dynamin, synaptotagmin and endophilin but not AP-2, so dpy-23's contribution cannot be verified here.

## Curation decisions (summary)
- Core: clathrin-cargo adaptor activity within the AP-2 adaptor complex at the plasma membrane; clathrin-dependent / receptor-mediated endocytosis (MIG-14, CED-1 phagocytic receptor, synaptic vesicle proteins); presynaptic and periciliary membrane localisation.
- Non-core / downstream: body morphogenesis, positive regulation of Wnt secretion, protein localisation to cilium.
- Over-propagated: GO:0006896 Golgi to vacuole transport comes from a deep AP-mu family node whose experimental seeds are AP-1/AP-3/AP-4 mu subunits; AP-2 mu acts at the plasma membrane, so this is removed.
- GO:0050767 regulation of neurogenesis (PMID:31259686) does not describe the cilia-membrane phenotype; proposed replacement GO:0044782 cilium organization.

## Additional literature surfaced by deep research (not in GOA; see dpy-23-deep-research-falcon.md)
- apm-2 mutants reduce GLR-1::GFP in ventral-cord puncta by ~26–30% with receptor accumulating in cell-body endosomes, implying AP-2 also supports a secretory/endosomal delivery step for GLR-1 (Garafalo et al. 2015, DOI 10.1091/mbc.e14-06-1048) [file:worm/dpy-23/dpy-23-deep-research-falcon.md "Two independent *apm-2* alleles reduced GLR-1::GFP fluorescence in ventral-nerve-cord puncta by 26% and 30%, respectively"].
- Reduction-of-function dpy-23 alleles (S434T, Y275D, null) suppress nekl-2; nekl-3 developmental arrest (Joseph et al. 2020, DOI 10.1371/journal.pgen.1008633) [file:worm/dpy-23/dpy-23-deep-research-falcon.md "The DPY-23 S434T allele *fd155* allowed 98% of otherwise severely affected *nekl-2; nekl-3* animals to reach adulthood"].
- SEL-5/AAK1 phosphorylates DPY-23 T160 (≈ mammalian AP2M1 T156), but the phospho-site is dispensable for the Wnt-dependent phenotypes examined (Knop et al. 2024, DOI 10.7554/eLife.91054) [file:worm/dpy-23/dpy-23-deep-research-falcon.md "SEL-5 loss reduced phosphorylation of DPY-23 at T160, corresponding to mammalian AP2M1 T156, but did not eliminate it"].
