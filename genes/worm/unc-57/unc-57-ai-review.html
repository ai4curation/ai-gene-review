Gene Description

UNC-57 is the single Caenorhabditis elegans endophilin-A, an N-BAR/SH3 protein of presynaptic terminals. Its N-terminal amphipathic helix and crescent-shaped BAR-domain dimer bind and bend the presynaptic plasma membrane, and this membrane-bending activity, acting before scission, is what drives the retrieval of synaptic vesicle membrane after exocytosis; unc-57 mutants are depleted of synaptic vesicles, retrieve synaptobrevin poorly and show reduced rates of evoked release at neuromuscular junctions. Its C-terminal SH3 domain binds the proline-rich domains of dynamin (DYN-1) and synaptojanin (UNC-26) and is required to localize and stabilize synaptojanin at synapses, although it is dispensable for endocytosis itself. UNC-57 is not essential for the ultrafast/bulk membrane retrieval step itself but is required, with synaptojanin, to convert the resulting endosomes into new synaptic vesicles. Most UNC-57 at synapses rides on the synaptic vesicle pool and is released to endocytic zones by exocytosis, coupling vesicle retrieval to release. UNC-57 is expressed in neurons and the posterior intestine; its endocytic activity also contributes, non-specifically, to the execution of necrotic neurodegeneration.

Core Functions

N-BAR membrane-bending activity: the UNC-57 dimer binds the presynaptic plasma membrane through its amphipathic helices and BAR domain and imposes positive curvature on the invaginating membrane before dynamin-mediated scission, driving synaptic vesicle endocytosis at the presynaptic endocytic zone. After ultrafast/bulk retrieval, the same activity is required to bud new synaptic vesicles from synaptic endosomes.

(schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 3-5) Direct imaging and rescue evidence; strong. Accumulation in unc-104 mutants does not prove that UNC-57 is physically carried on synaptic vesicles.

EM and optogenetic analyses indicate that endosomes can form but are inefficiently converted into new synaptic vesicles; the data support action at both plasma-membrane retrieval and endosomal budding stages. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 11-12, yu2018endophilinaand pages 1-2, yu2018endophilinaand pages 5-8) Direct genetic, physiological, and ultrastructural evidence; strong. Exact temporal partitioning between plasma membrane and endosome remains model-dependent.

1. Molecular identity and structural interpretation

UNC-57 belongs to the endophilin family. Its architecture comprises an N-terminal BAR-region/coiled-coil module and a C-terminal SH3 domain. The BAR module supports dimerization, binding to curved lipid membranes, and membrane deformation; the SH3 domain recognizes proline-rich endocytic proteins. These features closely match the supplied annotations AH/BAR domain, BAR domain, Endophilin-A SH3, and SH3-like domain. A functional UNC-57::GFP transgene rescues mutant phenotypes, providing experimental confirmation that the cloned T04D1.3 product is the relevant protein. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 3-5, schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 2-3)

Curvature sensitivity is functionally important rather than merely bioinformatic. In vivo replacement experiments in an unc-57 background tested Endophilin-A proteins with altered or substituted amphipathic curvature-sensing motifs. Rescue of locomotion, synaptic localization, and electrophysiological function depended on the properties of that motif, supporting a model in which electrostatically guided membrane insertion and curvature sensing specify endophilin activity at synaptic membranes. Much of this experiment used rat Endophilin-A1 or chimeric constructs, however, so its mechanistic relevance to UNC-57 is strong evolutionary inference plus cross-species rescue—not direct biochemistry on purified native UNC-57. (zhang2022theendophilincurvaturesensitive pages 40-41, zhang2022theendophilincurvaturesensitive pages 38-40)

Is UNC-57 an enzyme?

No defined reaction should presently be assigned. Older endophilin literature proposed lysophosphatidic-acid acyltransferase activity and phosphatidic-acid production, but the foundational worm study treated this as a mechanistic hypothesis rather than demonstrating catalysis by native UNC-57. There is consequently no experimentally established UNC-57 substrate specificity, turnover reaction, or catalytic active site. Functional annotation should emphasize membrane remodeling and protein recruitment, not LPAAT catalysis. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 11-12, schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 2-3, schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 1-2)

2. Cellular and subcellular localization

Promoter-reporter experiments detected unc-57 expression broadly in neurons and in the posterior intestine. At the protein level, rescuing UNC-57::GFP is punctate in neuropil and at neuromuscular junctions and colocalizes with the synaptic-vesicle protein synaptobrevin, establishing presynaptic enrichment. In unc-104 kinesin mutants, UNC-57::GFP accumulates in motor-neuron cell bodies, consistent with dependence on axonal delivery of synaptic cargo, although this does not prove that UNC-57 itself is physically transported on synaptic vesicles. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 3-5, schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 2-3)

At synapses, the functional membrane locations are the presynaptic plasma membrane/endocytic zone and synaptic endosomes or bulk-endocytic large vesicles. Ultrastructural and genetic evidence indicates that UNC-57 contributes to membrane retrieval at the plasma membrane and to budding or disintegration of synaptic endosomes into new vesicles. Its localization is therefore best described as cytosolic-to-membrane-associated and activity-coupled, rather than as an integral vesicle-membrane component. (yu2018endophilinaand pages 1-2, yu2018endophilinaand pages 5-8)

A 2023 endogenous split-GFP study used UNC-57 puncta to mark cholinergic and GABAergic motor-neuron presynapses. Cholinergic UNC-57 punctum fluorescence was significantly higher in adult males than hermaphrodites, whereas punctum density did not differ. This establishes sex-dependent abundance at cholinergic terminals but does not show that UNC-57 itself causes sexually dimorphic transmission; in that study the causal analysis focused on UNC-43/CaMKII. Published August 2023, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.202301117. (zeng2023camkiimediatessexually pages 2-4, zeng2023camkiimediatessexually pages 8-10)

3. Primary biological function: synaptic-vesicle recycling

Loss-of-function evidence

The clearest phenotype is depletion of releasable synaptic vesicles. At neuromuscular junctions, total vesicle numbers in unc-57(ok310) and unc-57(e406) were respectively 35% and 26% of wild type; docked vesicles were 12% and 21% of wild type. Evoked fusion frequency fell by 86%, closely matching the approximately 86% loss of docked vesicles. Synaptobrevin became diffuse along axons, and mutants exhibited aldicarb resistance, uncoordinated locomotion, rapid synaptic fatigue, accumulated coated vesicles, and abnormal vesicle strings. These observations establish a failure to replenish and correctly organize the vesicle pool rather than a primary postsynaptic defect. Published November 2003, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0896-6273(03)00667-6. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 3-5, schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 11-12, schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 2-3)

Later optogenetic and EM work refined the affected step. unc-57 synapses contained increased numbers of enlarged synaptic endosomes while retaining very few normal synaptic vesicles. Endosomes could therefore form, but their conversion into new vesicles was inefficient. During sustained cholinergic stimulation, unc-57 animals lost contraction almost completely within approximately 30 seconds, showing that the defect becomes especially severe during high vesicle turnover. Published June 2018, DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fnmol.2018.00196. (yu2018endophilinaand pages 1-2, yu2018endophilinaand pages 5-8)

The resulting pathway model is:

  1. Synaptic vesicles fuse and release neurotransmitter.
  2. Membrane and vesicle proteins are retrieved from the presynaptic plasma membrane, including through bulk/ultrafast endocytosis under strong stimulation.
  3. Endocytic intermediates form synaptic endosomes or large vesicles.
  4. UNC-57 couples curved-membrane recognition/deformation to recruitment or coordination of endocytic proteins, enabling efficient recovery of vesicle membrane and budding of new synaptic vesicles from those endosomes.
  5. Without UNC-57, endosomal intermediates accumulate, vesicle pools collapse, and transmission fatigues.

The relative contribution at plasma membrane versus endosome depends on stimulation and genetic background, so UNC-57 should not be assigned exclusively to one stage. (yu2018endophilinaand pages 1-2, yu2018endophilinaand pages 5-8)

4. Functional partners and pathway relationships

UNC-26/synaptojanin

UNC-57 and UNC-26 act in a closely shared phosphoinositide-dependent recycling pathway. unc-57 and unc-26 mutants have similar drug-response, electrophysiological, and ultrastructural phenotypes; their double-mutant physiology is not substantially worse than either single mutant. UNC-26 becomes unstable or improperly localized in the absence of UNC-57, whereas UNC-57 remains synaptically localized without UNC-26. This directional relationship supports recruitment or stabilization of synaptojanin by endophilin at endocytic membranes, where synaptojanin dephosphorylates phosphoinositides during vesicle recycling. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 11-12, schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 1-2)

The interaction is more nuanced than the classical model in which the UNC-57 SH3 domain simply binds the proline-rich domain of UNC-26. In a 2015 study, coexpression of UNC-57 lacking its SH3 domain and UNC-26 lacking its proline-rich domain restored locomotion, synaptic activity, near-normal vesicle abundance, and reporter retrieval in double mutants. Rescue was not attributable to overexpression: the truncated proteins were approximately 75% and 32% of endogenous abundance. The untreated double mutant showed a 63% increase in axonal synaptopHluorin fluorescence, which was fully rescued by the two truncated proteins. Thus, direct SH3–PRD binding is dispensable under these conditions; coordinated membrane targeting, including BAR-domain coupling to synaptojanin’s phosphatase core, can support their coincident action. Published April 2015, DOI: https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.05660. (dong2015synaptojanincooperatesin pages 5-8)

ERP-1/Endophilin B-like protein and clathrin

ERP-1 contributes to synaptic-vesicle recycling but is less important than UNC-57. The erp-1; unc-57 double-mutant phenotype was largely dominated by unc-57, implying partial redundancy and possible backup pathways. Clathrin acts prominently in generating vesicles from synaptic endosomes; conditional clathrin experiments further suggested that endophilins can compensate at the plasma membrane under high turnover, while clathrin can partially compensate when UNC-57 is absent. These are supported pathway relationships, although “backup” denotes an interpretation of conditional and non-additive genetic phenotypes rather than a demonstrated physical complex. (yu2018endophilinaand pages 1-2, yu2018endophilinaand pages 5-8)

Dynamin and amphiphysin are plausible SH3-associated partners from conserved endophilin biology, and the UNC-57 SH3 domain can recognize proline-rich endocytic proteins. Nevertheless, the strongest worm-specific evidence concerns UNC-26 localization and pathway cooperation; direct native UNC-57 interaction constants or structures with dynamin were not established in the retrieved studies. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 2-3, schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 1-2)

5. Recent developments, 2023–2024

Direct optical measurement of recycling kinetics—2023

The pOpsicle system combined optogenetic stimulation with SNG-1/synaptogyrin-pHluorin to measure vesicle fusion, retrieval, and reacidification in intact worms. In unc-57(e406), reporter amplitude was reduced, the fluorescence-rise time constant increased from 2.7 to 3.3 seconds, and post-stimulation decay slowed from 15.2 to 24.7 seconds. Moreover, 18/47 mutant animals failed prespecified signal/decay criteria, compared with 4/111 wild type. These results independently confirm impaired vesicle-cycle kinetics in vivo. Because pHluorin decay combines membrane retrieval with vesicle reacidification, it is not a pure measurement of membrane scission. Published March 2023, DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fncel.2023.1120651. (seidenthal2023popsicleanalloptical pages 9-11)

Presynaptic abundance and sexual dimorphism—2023

Endogenously tagged UNC-57 has become a practical marker for identified presynaptic terminals. The 2023 JCB study found greater UNC-57 signal intensity—but unchanged punctum density—at male cholinergic synapses. This suggests that presynaptic molecular content can differ by sex even when synapse density is similar, and illustrates a current real-world experimental application of UNC-57 tagging in circuit biology. It does not revise UNC-57’s primary annotation as a recycling protein. (zeng2023camkiimediatessexually pages 2-4)

Coupling vesicle cycling to CaV2-channel abundance—2024

A 2024 PNAS study found that unc-57(ok310) increased the intensity of presynaptic GFP::UNC-2/CaV2 puncta, as did loss of UNC-26. Independently, increasing neuronal activity for 20 minutes reduced UNC-2 punctum intensity without changing punctum number. The authors interpret these data as inverse homeostatic coupling: inefficient vesicle exocytosis/recycling promotes greater presynaptic CaV2 abundance, potentially compensating for weak transmitter release. UNC-57 should therefore be viewed as an indirect contributor to presynaptic channel homeostasis, not as a demonstrated direct CaV2 trafficking factor. Published August 2024, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2404969121. (xiong2024presynapticneuronsselftune pages 5-6)

6. Applications and experimental utility

UNC-57 currently has research rather than clinical applications:

  • Presynaptic/endocytic-zone marker: functional fluorescent or split-fluorescent UNC-57 labels motor-neuron synapses and supports cell-type-specific measurements of synapse abundance and composition. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 3-5, zeng2023camkiimediatessexually pages 2-4)
  • Reference mutant for vesicle-recycling assays: unc-57(e406) and unc-57(ok310) are positive controls for synaptic fatigue, vesicle depletion, altered endosomal ultrastructure, and defective optical reporter kinetics. (schuske2003endophilinisrequired pages 3-5, yu2018endophilinaand pages 5-8, seidenthal2023popsicleanalloptical pages 9-11)
  • In-vivo membrane-curvature model: rescue with domain deletions, chimeric curvature-sensitive helices, or cross-species Endophilin-A proteins tests how BAR-domain electrostatics and amphipathic motifs encode organelle-specific function. (dong2015synaptojanincooperatesin pages 5-8, zhang2022theendophilincurvaturesensitive pages 40-41, zhang2022theendophilincurvaturesensitive pages 38-40)
  • Pathway dissection: double mutants with unc-26, erp-1, or clathrin alleles distinguish plasma-membrane retrieval from synaptic-endosome processing and reveal compensatory pathways. (dong2015synaptojanincooperatesin pages 5-8, yu2018endophilinaand pages 1-2)
  • Homeostatic plasticity studies: unc-57 mutants can probe how vesicle-cycle efficiency feeds back onto active-zone CaV2 abundance. (xiong2024presynapticneuronsselftune pages 5-6)

7. Confidence assessment and recommended annotation

High-confidence statements

  • B1V8A0 is C. elegans UNC-57/T04D1.3, an Endophilin-A homolog.
  • UNC-57 contains BAR/coiled-coil and SH3 modules and is concentrated at presynaptic terminals.
  • It is required for maintaining total and docked synaptic-vesicle pools and sustained neurotransmission.
  • It functions in synaptic-vesicle endocytosis/recycling, including production of new vesicles from synaptic endosomes.
  • It cooperates closely with UNC-26/synaptojanin, although canonical SH3–PRD binding is not strictly required.

Moderate-confidence refinements

  • UNC-57 acts at both the plasma membrane and synaptic endosomes, with the balance depending on activity and compensatory pathways.
  • ERP-1 and clathrin provide partial or conditional backup.
  • Impaired UNC-57-dependent vesicle cycling indirectly elevates presynaptic UNC-2/CaV2 abundance.

Claims that should not be assigned as established function

  • A confirmed lysophosphatidic-acid acyltransferase reaction or defined lipid substrate.
  • Exclusive action at either the plasma membrane or endosome.
  • Direct regulation or trafficking of CaV2 channels.
  • A causal role for UNC-57 abundance in sexual dimorphism based solely on its use as a presynaptic marker.

Recommended concise functional annotation: “Presynaptic Endophilin-A-family BAR–SH3 membrane-remodeling/adaptor protein required for synaptic-vesicle endocytosis and reformation from synaptic endosomes; promotes synaptic localization and coordinated activity of synaptojanin/UNC-26 and sustains neurotransmission during repeated activity.”
