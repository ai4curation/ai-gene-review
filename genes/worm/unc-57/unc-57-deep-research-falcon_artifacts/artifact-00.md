| Identity and domain architecture | **B1V8A0 is the *Caenorhabditis elegans* `unc-57`/T04D1.3 product, an Endophilin-A homolog**, distinct from the worm Endophilin-B-like protein ERP-1. UNC-57 has an N-terminal BAR/coiled-coil membrane-binding and dimerization region and a C-terminal SH3 domain. | Corrected cDNA mapping identified T04D1.3 as `unc-57`; sequence comparison, mutant analysis, and rescuing UNC-57::GFP established the Endophilin-A identity. The BAR–SH3 architecture agrees with the supplied UniProt/InterPro annotation. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000004) | **Direct identity and genetic evidence; strong.** Specific BAR/SH3 biochemical properties are partly supported by conserved endophilin structure and family studies. |
| Presynaptic localization | UNC-57 is concentrated at presynaptic terminals, including nerve-ring neuropil and neuromuscular junctions, where it associates with synaptic-vesicle/endocytic compartments. | A functional UNC-57::GFP fusion rescued `unc-57` mutants, formed puncta in neuropil and NMJs, and colocalized with synaptobrevin. In `unc-104` kinesin mutants it accumulated in motor-neuron cell bodies, consistent with dependence on synaptic-cargo transport. (pqac-00000000) | **Direct imaging and rescue evidence; strong.** Accumulation in `unc-104` mutants does not prove that UNC-57 is physically carried on synaptic vesicles. |
| Plasma-membrane and endosomal recycling role | UNC-57 is a membrane-remodeling/adaptor protein required for efficient synaptic-vesicle membrane retrieval and for regeneration of new vesicles from synaptic endosomes. | `unc-57` mutants show synaptic fatigue, vesicle depletion, accumulated coated intermediates, and enlarged/increased synaptic endosomes. EM and optogenetic analyses indicate that endosomes can form but are inefficiently converted into new synaptic vesicles; the data support action at both plasma-membrane retrieval and endosomal budding stages. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000012) | **Direct genetic, physiological, and ultrastructural evidence; strong.** Exact temporal partitioning between plasma membrane and endosome remains model-dependent. |
| Relationship with UNC-26/synaptojanin | UNC-57 and UNC-26 act in a closely shared recycling pathway; UNC-57 promotes synaptic localization/stability of UNC-26, but the canonical Endophilin-SH3–Synaptojanin-PRD interaction is not absolutely required in vivo. | `unc-57` and `unc-26` mutants have similar ultrastructural and physiological defects; endophilin remains localized without UNC-26, whereas UNC-26 localization depends on UNC-57. Coexpression of UNC-57ΔSH3 and UNC-26ΔPRD restored locomotion, synaptic activity, near-normal vesicle numbers, and a **63%** synaptopHluorin elevation in the double mutant; protein levels were approximately **75%** and **32%** of endogenous levels, respectively. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000005) | **Direct epistasis, localization, domain-deletion, and rescue evidence; strong.** Recruitment likely involves BAR-mediated membrane coincidence or alternative interactions in addition to SH3–PRD binding. |
| Relationship with ERP-1 and clathrin | UNC-57 is the predominant endophilin in synaptic-vesicle recycling; ERP-1 has a smaller, partially redundant role, while clathrin collaborates principally in vesicle production from endosomes and can provide backup at the plasma membrane. | The `erp-1; unc-57` double-mutant phenotype was largely dominated by `unc-57`. In prolonged stimulation, `unc-57` animals lost contraction almost completely within **30 s**. EM and clathrin temperature-sensitive experiments supported combined endophilin/clathrin action at endosomes and compensatory activity at the plasma membrane under high turnover. (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000012) | **Direct genetics, optogenetics, electrophysiology, and EM; moderate-to-strong.** “Backup” relationships are mechanistic interpretations of non-additive and conditional phenotypes. |
| 2003 mutant vesicle statistics | Loss of UNC-57 severely depletes total and docked synaptic vesicles, quantitatively explaining much of the release defect. | At NMJs, total vesicles were **35% of wild type in `ok310`** and **26% in `e406`**; docked vesicles were **12%** and **21%**, respectively. Evoked fusion frequency fell **86%**, matching an approximately **86% depletion of docked vesicles**. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001) | **Direct EM and electrophysiological evidence; strong.** Measurements are from specific alleles and NMJ conditions rather than every neuronal class. |
| 2023 pOpsicle recycling kinetics | Modern all-optical measurements confirm that UNC-57 loss slows both the activity-evoked fluorescence rise and post-stimulation decay of a synaptic-vesicle reporter, consistent with impaired exocytosis/recycling dynamics. | In `unc-57(e406)`, SNG-1::pHluorin signals were reduced; rise time increased from **2.7 s to 3.3 s**, and decay time increased from **15.2 s to 24.7 s**. **18 of 47** mutant animals failed prespecified signal/decay criteria, versus **4 of 111** wild type. (pqac-00000013) | **Direct in-vivo optical evidence; strong for altered kinetics.** pHluorin decay integrates endocytosis and reacidification and is not a pure measurement of membrane scission. |
| 2023 sex-dependent localization | Endogenously tagged UNC-57 marks cholinergic and GABAergic presynaptic sites; cholinergic synaptic UNC-57 abundance is higher in adult males than hermaphrodites, without a corresponding difference in punctum density. | Cell-type-restricted split-GFP complementation at endogenous UNC-57 produced puncta at dorsal-cord NMJs. Cholinergic punctum fluorescence was significantly higher in males, whereas signal density did not differ between sexes. (pqac-00000010) | **Direct endogenous-tag imaging; moderate-to-strong.** UNC-57 served mainly as a presynaptic marker; the study did not establish that altered UNC-57 abundance causes the sex difference in transmission. |
| 2024 CaV2/UNC-2 homeostasis | UNC-57-dependent vesicle cycling participates indirectly in presynaptic homeostasis: impaired recycling is associated with increased abundance of UNC-2/CaV2 channels at active zones. | `unc-57(ok310)` increased GFP::UNC-2 short-isoform punctum intensity in the dorsal nerve cord, as did loss of UNC-26. Independently, **20 min** of optogenetically increased neuronal activity reduced UNC-2 punctum intensity without changing punctum number, supporting inverse coupling between vesicle exocytosis and CaV2 abundance. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009) | **Direct mutant and activity-manipulation evidence; moderate-to-strong.** The molecular route from UNC-57-dependent recycling to UNC-2 turnover was not resolved, so UNC-57 should not be described as a direct CaV2 trafficking factor. |
| Intrinsic enzymatic/LPAAT activity | **No native UNC-57-catalyzed reaction or substrate specificity has been established.** Its primary supported role is structural/adaptor-mediated membrane remodeling, not enzymatic catalysis. | Historical endophilin models attributed lysophosphatidic-acid acyltransferase activity to the BAR-region family, but the foundational worm study treated phosphatidic-acid production and lipid remodeling as hypotheses requiring testing. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000005) | **Unproven for native UNC-57.** It is inappropriate to annotate UNC-57 as a confirmed LPAAT enzyme or assign a defined lipid substrate solely from family-level reports. |
