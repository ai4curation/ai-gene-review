# ric-4 (UniProt A5PEW5, TrEMBL) — SNAP-25, C. elegans

## Identity
- ric-4 ("resistance to inhibitors of cholinesterase") encodes the C. elegans SNAP-25 orthologue (WormBase Y22F5A.3). The scaffold uses the TrEMBL entry A5PEW5 (234 aa, isoform b) rather than O62414; both are the same gene. Two t-SNARE coiled-coil domains (46-108 and 169-231) flanked by a central cysteine-rich linker (from `ric-4-uniprot.txt`; PANTHER PTHR19305:SF14, Pfam PF00835 SNAP-25).
- The Qbc-SNARE contributes two helices to the neuronal SNARE bundle, together with UNC-64/syntaxin (Qa) and SNB-1/synaptobrevin (R) [PMID:12973353 "Priming appears to involve the formation of trans-SNARE complexes, in which the synaptic vesicle protein synaptobrevin (also called VAMP) forms a helical bundle with the plasma membrane proteins syntaxin and SNAP-25"].

## Genetics and function
- ric-4 was identified among the 18 genes whose mutation confers recessive resistance to acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (aldicarb), a signature of defective presynaptic acetylcholine release [PMID:7498734 "Measurements of acetylcholine levels in these mutants suggest that some of the genes are involved in presynaptic functions."] (abstract-only; ric-4 is not named in the abstract but is one of the ric loci from this screen).
- The ric-4(ox45) allele disrupts the SNAP-25 gene and, like snb-1 and unc-64, reduces steady-state syntaxin by ~50% [PMID:12973353 "the ox45 mutation disrupts the gene encoding the C. elegans SNAP-25 homolog"; "mutations in other synaptic components, such as synaptobrevin and SNAP-25 also led to a 50% reduction in syntaxin levels"].
- ric-4 mutants have severe neurotransmitter-secretion defects, like unc-13, unc-10 and snb-1 [PMID:18596236 "we analyzed UNC-64 localization in mutants lacking UNC-13 Munc13, UNC-10 RIM1, RIC-4 SNAP-25, and SNB-1 Synaptobrevin-1, all of which have severe neurotransmitter secretion defects"].

## Localization and expression
- Anti-RIC-4 immunostaining is diffuse along the nerve cords (plasma membrane of axons) and absent from cell bodies; unlike UNC-64 it does not depend on UNC-18 for trafficking [PMID:18596236 "RIC-4 SNAP-25 was diffusely localized in the nerve cords, and was also absent from cell bodies"; "UNC-64 Syntaxin-1 is the only presynaptic SNARE protein that is trafficked in an UNC-18–dependent manner"].
- Neuron-type-specific expression is driven by multiple promoter and first-intron elements (P1/P2, I1h/I1m/I2h/I2m), including an UNC-86/MEC-3-dependent element for touch receptor neurons [PMID:14529613 "Different elements were required for SNAP-25 expression in different neuronal subsets; P1 was required in DA and DD motor neurons, and I1m and I2m were required in DB and DA neurons, respectively."]. The GOA IDA rows for plasma membrane and synapse cite this expression paper; the cached abstract covers reporter expression only, so the protein-localisation data must come from the full text.

## Curation notes
- All PAINT (IBA) rows derive from SNAP-25-family nodes PTN000447752/3/4 and describe the canonical Qbc-SNARE role (fusogenic SNARE activity, syntaxin binding, SNARE complex, exocytosis, priming, synaptic vesicle fusion at the active zone); all are consistent with the worm genetics.
