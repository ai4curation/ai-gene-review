CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor (PubMed:30291162). Represses expression
CC       of genes involved in differentiation of touch receptor neurons (TRN),
CC       probably acting as a heterodimer with egl-44, perhaps by occupying
CC       similar cis-regulatory elements as an unc-86/mec-3 heterodimer
CC       (PubMed:30291162). Plays a role in cell fate specification of neurons,
CC       including the hook neuron HOB, the gas-sensing neuron BAG and touch
CC       receptor neurons (PubMed:12954713, PubMed:25395666, PubMed:30291162).
CC       Plays a role in neuron differentiation by repressing the expression of
CC       zag-1 in FLP neurons, probably acting as a heterodimer with egl-44;
CC       because zag-1 represses expression of egl-46 and egl-44, together these
CC       proteins form a bistable, negative-feedback loop that regulates the
CC       choice between neuronal fates (PubMed:30291162). Acts downstream of
CC       egl-44 to prevent touch cell differentiation in FLP neurons
CC       (PubMed:11274062). Involved in male mating behavior, acting in concert
CC       with egl-44, via modulation of expression of polycystins lov-1 and pkd-
CC       2, homeodomain protein ceh-26, and neuropeptide-like protein nlp-8
CC       (PubMed:12954713). Modulates the expression of a subset of terminal
CC       differentiation genes involved in O(2)- and CO(2)-sensing, acting in
CC       parallel to ets-5 and egl-13 (PubMed:25395666). May act upstream of RFX
CC       transcription factor daf-19 to regulate gene expression specifically in
CC       the HOB neuron (PubMed:20923979). Plays a role in specifying
CC       commissural dendrites of the PVD nociceptive neurons, acting in concert
CC       with egl-44 (PubMed:29031632). In association with egl-44, regulates
CC       cell cycle exit in the neuronal Q cell lineage (PubMed:23946438).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11274062, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12954713,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20923979, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23946438,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25395666, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29031632,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30291162}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via C-terminus) with egl-44 (via N-terminus); the
CC       interaction is direct; the interaction may regulate transcription.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23946438, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30291162}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11274062,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23946438}. Note=Localized to nucleus during
CC       interphase and evenly distributed in the cytoplasm of dividing Q.a and
CC       Q.p neuroblasts. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23946438}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in touch cells, HSN cells, ventral cord
CC       motor neurons and ciliated ray neurons. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11274062}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in neuronal cells, initially in embryos
CC       after gastrulation, including ventral cord motor neurons during the L1
CC       larval stage, FLP, AVM and HSB neurons during L2, and PVM neurons
CC       during L3 (PubMed:11274062, PubMed:12954713). Expressed in the BAG
CC       neurons at the L2 and L3 stages of larval development
CC       (PubMed:25395666). Expressed in the HOB neuron beginning at the larval
CC       L4 stage and continuing throughout adulthood (PubMed:12954713).
CC       Expressed in the Q neuroblast lineage during larval development
CC       (PubMed:11274062, PubMed:23946438). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11274062,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12954713, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23946438,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25395666}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the INSM1 family. {ECO:0000305}.
