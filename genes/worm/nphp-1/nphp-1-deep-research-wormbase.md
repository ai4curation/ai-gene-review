# WormBase gene description for nphp-1 (worm)

nphp-1 encodes a novel, SH3 domain-containing protein that is orthologous to mammalian nephrocystin-1 (OMIM:607100, mutated in juvenile nephronophthisis); nphp-1 along with nphp-4 is required for the proper assembly and structure of cilia; both NPHP-1 and NPHP-4 but not NPHP-1 alone is required for the proper localization of the IFT-B polypeptide OSM-6, the OSM-3-kinesin, and the BBS proteins BBS-7 and BBS-8; NPHP-1 localizes to the transition zone of the cilium in a NPHP-4 dependant manner and colocalizes with the polycystins LOV-1 and PKD-2 in male-specific sensory neurons.
