# WormBase gene description for irg-2 (worm)

irg-2 encodes a novel protein, conserved amongst nematodes; irg-2 expression is induced in response to infection by virulent strains of Pseudomonas aeruginosa in a manner dependent upon the bZIP transcription factor ZIP-2, but independent of the p38 MAPK pathway.
