# rho-1 (Q22038) manual literature research

No deep-research API keys were available in this session, so this file is a manual
literature synthesis from cached publications (`publications/PMID_*.md`) and the
UniProt record (`rho-1-uniprot.txt`). Full text was available for PMID:16391233,
PMID:18801371, PMID:21387015, PMID:22363657 and PMID:28085666; the remaining papers
were read as abstracts only.

## Identity and family

- rho-1 (Y51H4A.3; WBGene00004357; GeneID 178458) encodes the single C. elegans RhoA
  ortholog, a 192-residue Rho-family small GTPase [PMID:7798239 "The CeRhoA cDNA isolated
  in this study encodes a sequence of 192 amino acids residues with 87.6% identity to human
  RhoA"; "Genomic Southern analysis indicates the presence of a single Rho gene in C.
  elegans"].
- UniProt: "Belongs to the small GTPase superfamily. Rho family." InterPro IPR003578
  (Small_GTPase_Rho), PANTHER PTHR24072, PROSITE PS51420 (RHO). C-terminal CAAX box with
  predicted S-geranylgeranyl Cys189 and removed propeptide 190-192 (by similarity to human
  RHOA).

## Molecular function

- GTP binding / hydrolysis: the GST fusion "displays conserved biochemical activities"
  [PMID:7798239]; high-throughput nucleotide binding, exchange and hydrolysis assays
  across Rho GTPases [PMID:15814614, abstract only].
- GAP regulation: RGA-3/RGA-4 "regulate the activity of the small GTPase RHO-1"
  [PMID:17848508]; RGA-7/SPV-1 is "a CDC-42/Cdc42 and RHO-1/RhoA-specific Rho
  GTPase-activating protein" [PMID:26578656].
- GEF regulation: ECT-2 [PMID:16921365], UNC-73/Trio [PMID:11687661], UNC-89 DH-PH
  ("The DH-PH region of UNC-89 has exchange activity for RHO-1 (C. elegans RhoA)"
  [PMID:18801371]) and RHGF-2 ("RHGF-2 RhoGEF activity is specific to the C. elegans RhoA
  homolog RHO-1 as determined by direct binding, GDP/GTP exchange" [PMID:22363657]).
  Note: RHGF-2 is a GEF, not a GAP; the GOA row GO:0032794 "GTPase activating protein
  binding" (IPI, PMID:22363657, with Q22354/rhgf-2) uses the wrong term.
- Effector/target binding: active RHO-1 binds the diacylglycerol kinase DGK-1 ("DGK-1 does
  coimmunoprecipitate with constitutively active RHO-1(G14V)"; F25N reduces binding)
  [PMID:16391233]. DGK-1 is a lipid kinase, so GOA's GO:0019901 "protein kinase binding"
  (IPI, with WBGene00000958/dgk-1) is a wrong-term choice; GO:0019900 kinase binding fits.
  RHO-1 also co-immunoprecipitates with the NADPH oxidase BLI-3, and MEMO-1 restrains this
  ("endogenous RHO-1 physically binds to endogenous BLI-3 in vivo") [PMID:28085666].
  LET-502/ROCK acts genetically downstream in P-cell migration [PMID:11687661].

## Location

- "most of CeRhoA is associated with the membrane and the cytoskeleton throughout
  development", unlike mostly cytosolic mammalian RhoA [PMID:7798239].
- Anterior cell cortex in the one-cell embryo, regulated by KIN-18 [PMID:23064028];
  asymmetric cortical distribution generated by ECT-2 exclusion [PMID:16921365];
  cleavage furrow [PMID:16580995, curator IDA].

## Biological roles

- Early embryo: cortical actomyosin contractility, PAR polarity establishment
  [PMID:16921365; PMID:17848508; PMID:23064028], cytokinesis/contractile ring
  [PMID:16580995].
- Morphogenesis: ventral enclosure, elongation [PMID:24016757; PMID:22363657], P-cell
  migration [PMID:11687661].
- Muscle: UNC-89 -> RHO-1 for myosin thick filament organization [PMID:18801371].
- Adult nervous system: presynaptic RHO-1 increases ACh release via DGK-1 inhibition/DAG
  accumulation and a DGK-1-independent route [PMID:16391233]; adult roles in locomotion,
  pumping, fecundity, defecation, ovulation [PMID:21387015].
- Redox/longevity: RHO-1-BLI-3 interaction drives ROS signalling, modulated by MEMO-1
  [PMID:28085666].
