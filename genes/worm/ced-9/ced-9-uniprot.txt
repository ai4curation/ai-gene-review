CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a major role in programmed cell death (PCD, apoptosis)
CC       (PubMed:10688797, PubMed:7907274). egl-1 binds to and directly inhibits
CC       the activity of ced-9, releasing the cell death activator ced-4 from a
CC       ced-9/ced-4 containing protein complex and allowing ced-4 to activate
CC       the cell-killing caspase ced-3 (PubMed:12894216, PubMed:15383288,
CC       PubMed:16208361, PubMed:9024666, PubMed:9027313, PubMed:9604928).
CC       During larval development, required for the elimination of transient
CC       presynaptic components downstream of egl-1 and upstream of ced-4 and
CC       ced-3 apoptotic pathway (PubMed:26074078). Has been shown in one study
CC       to be dispensable in mitochondrial dynamics and morphology during early
CC       embryonic development (PubMed:19327994). However, another study shows
CC       that a egl-1/ced-9 containing complex may promote drp-1-dependent
CC       mitochondrial fission (PubMed:21949250). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10688797,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15383288, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26074078,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7907274, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9024666,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9027313, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9604928}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with asymmetric homodimer ced-4; the interaction
CC       sequesters ced-4 (PubMed:15383288, PubMed:16208361, PubMed:9024666,
CC       PubMed:9027313). Interacts with egl-1; the interaction results in ced-4
CC       release (PubMed:12894216, PubMed:15383288, PubMed:9604928). Interacts
CC       with dre-1; the interaction inhibits ced-9 activity, either directly or
CC       indirectly (PubMed:23431138). Interacts with dct-1 (PubMed:11114722).
CC       May form a complex composed of ced-9, ced-4 and mac-1
CC       (PubMed:10101135). Interacts with dynamin-related protein drp-1 (via
CC       residues 280-502); the interaction is enhanced by GTP rather than GDP;
CC       the interaction is probably direct and may occur at the mitochondrion
CC       (PubMed:21949250). Interaction with drp-1 may be enhanced by
CC       interaction of ced-9 with egl-1, but not with ced-4 (PubMed:21949250).
CC       A ced-9/egl-1 complex may recruit drp-1 to the mitochondrial surface
CC       (PubMed:21949250). Interacts with fzo-1; interaction may be suppressed
CC       by interaction of ced-9 with egl-1 (PubMed:21949250).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10101135, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11114722,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12894216, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15383288,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16208361, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21949250,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23431138, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9024666,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9027313, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9604928}.

CC       (PubMed:26074078). Synaptic localization is dependent on axonal
CC       mitochondria (PubMed:26074078). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26074078}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Abundant expression is seen in embryos and adults
CC       (PubMed:7907274). Expressed in embryos prior to elongation, at comma
CC       stage, at 1.5-fold stage and in adult gonad (PubMed:17021039).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17021039, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7907274}.
