# WormBase gene description for cct-8 (worm)

cct-8 encodes a putative theta subunit of the eukaryotic cytosolic ('T complex') chaperonin, orthologous to human CCT8, ENSG00000020219, and CCT8L2/KCNMB3L; in mass RNAi assays, CCT-8 is required for transgene repression, distal tip cell migration, embryonic and larval viability, fertility, normal body coloration, locomotion, and vulval development, and normally rapid growth.
