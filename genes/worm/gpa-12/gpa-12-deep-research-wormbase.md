# WormBase gene description for gpa-12 (worm)

gpa-12 encodes a member of the G protein alpha subunit family of heterotrimeric GTPases that is orthologous to members of the mammalian Galpha G12/G13 family; GPA-12 activity is required for antifungal innate immunity and genetic analyses indicate that GPA-12 functions upstream of phospholipase Cs PLC-3 and EGL-8 as well as the TPA-1 protein kinase C in this process; genetic analyses also suggest that gpa-12 functions upstream of tpa-1 in pathways that regulated feeding and growth; gpa-12 is expressed in the pharynx and in the hypodermis.
