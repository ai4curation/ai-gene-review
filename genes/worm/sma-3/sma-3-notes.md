# sma-3 notes

Deep research: the wrapper reported a falcon timeout and an unavailable fallback, but a falcon job later completed and wrote `sma-3-deep-research-falcon.md`. The review uses that report, the cached publications and UniProt P45896.

- Hypodermal SMA-3 is necessary and sufficient for body size [PMID:12397107 "SMA-3 activity in the hypodermis is necessary and sufficient for normal body size"]
- Pharyngeal SMA-3 also contributes to pharynx and body growth [PMID:25480452].
- Nuclear and cytoplasmic localization; it enters the nuclei of male tail tip cells [PMID:21408209].
- Antifungal caenacin induction downstream of DBL-1 [PMID:19198592]. Also modelled in GO-CAM 5b91dbd100002241 (SMA-3: GO:0000981, positive regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II).
- Binds DAF-16; both bind the rheb-1 promoter [PMID:28549065]. The coactivator IDA is kept as non-core.
- GO:0030511 (IGI with sup-17) was changed to GO:0030509, because SMA-3 is a pathway component, not an upstream regulator.

Points from the deep-research report (primary papers not cached here):
- The Drechmeria/caenacin branch needs SMA-3 but not SMA-2 or SMA-4 (non-canonical) [Zugasti & Ewbank 2009, via the report].
- Madaan et al. 2018: GFP::SMA-3 ChIP occupies the col-141/col-142 region; SMA-4 MH1 (not SMA-3 MH1) binds the SBEs.
- Vora et al. 2025: 4,205 SMA-3 peaks; all 367 inferred direct targets are down in sma-3 mutants (an activator); SMA-3 is linked to collagen secretion via dpy-11.
- Wang et al. 2005: yeast two-hybrid interaction of SMA-3 with LIN-31 (spicule). Ciccarelli et al. 2024: pharyngeal SMA-3 supports survival on P. luminescens.
