Gene Description

CED-5 is the C. elegans orthologue of human DOCK180 (DOCK1) and Drosophila Myoblast City, a large (1781 aa) DOCK-family guanine nucleotide exchange factor with an N-terminal SH3 domain, a DHR-1 (DOCK-type C2) lipid-binding domain and a C-terminal DHR-2 (DOCKER) catalytic domain. It binds the PH-domain protein CED-12/ELMO directly and the SH2/SH3 adaptor CED-2/CrkII, and the CED-2/CED-5/CED-12 complex acts as a bipartite exchange factor that activates the Rac GTPase CED-10 at the plasma membrane of engulfing and migrating cells. Through CED-10 it drives actin reorganization and the extension of cell surfaces. It acts in engulfing cells to internalize apoptotic cell corpses, in one of two partially redundant engulfment pathways (the other led by CED-1/CED-6/CED-7), and it is required for the guided migration of the two gonadal distal tip cells. The Frizzled receptor MOM-5 and the Wnt pathway components GSK-3 and APR-1/APC act upstream of the CED-2/CED-5/CED-12 module in corpse engulfment and distal tip cell migration. ced-5 also contributes, partially redundantly with other genes, to embryonic gastrulation, and engulfment by CED-5-dependent cells helps ensure that cells receiving weak death signals actually die.
