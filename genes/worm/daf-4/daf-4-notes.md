# daf-4 (P50488) review notes

- Deep research: the first falcon run timed out at 600 s. A rerun with `--timeout 1500` produced `daf-4-deep-research-falcon.md`.
- The only type II receptor, shared by the DAF-7 (with DAF-1) and DBL-1 (with SMA-6) pathways [PMID:9847239 "In C. elegans, the TGFbeta-like type II receptor daf-4 is required for two distinct signaling pathways"].
- Binds BMP-2/4 in COS cells [PMID:8413626 "When expressed in monkey COS cells, the daf-4 receptor binds human BMP-2 and BMP-4"].
- Plasma membrane [PMID:24550286; PMID:21408209; PMID:9334330]. Recycles via ARF-6, independently of retromer [PMID:24550286]. Surface levels depend on TSP-12/TSP-14 [PMID:31988138].
- A truncated isoform from alternative polyadenylation antagonizes signaling [PMID:15254038 "a polypeptide encoded by the 2.0 kb transcript can function as an antagonist of full-length DAF-4 signaling"]. The negative-regulation row is kept as non-core and isoform-specific.
- Added NEW GO:0098821 BMP receptor activity (IC). IBA signaling receptor complex changed to BMP receptor complex.
- Two core functions: BMP receptor activity (Sma/Mab) and TGF-beta receptor activity (DAF-7, dauer).
