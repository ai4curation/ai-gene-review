Gene Description

cep-1 encodes the single C. elegans p53-family transcription factor. CEP-1 localizes to germline nuclei and uses its p53-like DNA-binding domain and dimeric C-terminal oligomerization/SAM domain to activate RNA polymerase II target genes. Its best-established apoptotic role is in the germline DNA-damage pathway, where CEP-1 induces the BH3-only genes egl-1 and ced-13 upstream of the CED-9/CED-4/CED-3 intrinsic apoptosis module. CEP-1 also regulates additional development, stress, and lifespan outputs, including fasn-1 and phg-1, and its DNA-damage apoptotic output is restrained by GLD-1-dependent translational repression and by a PRMT-5/CBP-1 complex that represses CEP-1 transcriptional activity.

Core Functions

CEP-1 is a nuclear p53-family sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription activator that binds p53 response elements and activates RNA polymerase II target genes, including fasn-1 and the damage-induced BH3-only genes egl-1 and ced-13.
