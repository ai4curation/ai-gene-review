# WormBase gene description for mks-1 (worm)

mks-1 encodes, by alternative splicing, two isoforms of an ortholog of  human MKS1, and is paralogous to  MKRS-1 and MKRS-2; MKS-1 is redundantly required with NPH-4 for roughly  normal foraging, and with both NPH-1 and NPH-4 for cilia formation;  MKS-1 is expressed in a subset of amphid and inner labial neurons, in a  DAF-19-dependent manner; MKS-1 forms a complex with MKRS-1 and MKRS-2 at  the base of cilia, requires MKRS-1 and MKRS-2 for localization to the  transistion zone, and is coexpressed with these proteins in ciliated  sensory neurons; mks-1(RNAi) suppresses the normal egg-laying defect of  egl-9 mutants.
