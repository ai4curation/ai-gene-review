Gene Description

TCTN-1 (E04A4.6) is the single Caenorhabditis elegans ortholog of the vertebrate Tectonic proteins (TCTN1-3). It has an N-terminal signal peptide, a Tectonic domain most conserved in its C-terminal region, and a predicted C-terminal transmembrane segment. Like other transition zone genes, tctn-1 carries an X-box promoter motif, depends on DAF-19, and is expressed preferentially in ciliated sensory neurons. TCTN-1::GFP concentrates at the ciliary transition zone (TZ), immediately distal to the basal body. Genetically TCTN-1 belongs to the MKS module: tctn-1 single mutants and double mutants with MKS-module genes (mks-1, mksr-1, mksr-2, mks-3) have normal cilia, whereas tctn-1;nphp-4 and tctn-1;nphp-1 double mutants lose ciliary integrity (missing or truncated axonemes, defective Y-links, mispositioned cilia). TCTN-1 is not needed for TZ localization of MKSR-1, MKS-5, MKS-6 or NPHP-4, or for ciliary targeting of ODR-10 or ARL-13. It is required, however, to keep the non-ciliary membrane protein TRAM-1a out of the cilium, so it contributes to the TZ membrane diffusion barrier (ciliary gate).

Core Functions

Tectonic-family component of the MKS module at the ciliary transition zone. It contributes to the TZ membrane diffusion barrier that excludes non-ciliary membrane proteins (TRAM-1a), and acts redundantly with the NPHP module to build intact cilia and Y-links. It is not required for TZ assembly of other MKS/NPHP proteins. Its molecular activity is unknown, so no MF is asserted.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is TCTN-1 a biochemical subunit of a worm MKS/tectonic complex, as mouse Tctn1 is? Worm evidence is genetic only, so MKS complex membership has not been asserted here.

Q: Is the TCTN-1 C-terminal hydrophobic segment a true transmembrane anchor, or a GPI-attachment signal as proposed for some vertebrate Tectonics?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Test which TZ proteins (MKS-5, MKS-1, MKSR-1, TMEM-107) are required for TCTN-1::GFP TZ localization, to place it in the MKS assembly hierarchy.
