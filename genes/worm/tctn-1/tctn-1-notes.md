# tctn-1 (O44478) notes

Deep research: falcon timed out and the perplexity fallback was unavailable, so no deep-research file exists. The primary source is Yee et al. 2015 [PMID:26540106], with full text cached.

## Key findings
- Single worm Tectonic ortholog, E04A4.6; it has a signal peptide and an X-box motif and is DAF-19 regulated [PMID:26540106].
- TZ localization [PMID:26540106 "TCTN-1-GFP was enriched specifically at the transition zone of cilia, immediately distal to the basal body and proximal to the axoneme"].
- Genetically in the MKS module [PMID:26540106 "tctn-1 interacts with nphp-4 and nphp-1, but not with the MKS complex genes mks-1, mksr-1, mksr-2 and mks-3, thereby genetically placing tctn-1 within the MKS module."].
- Gate function [PMID:26540106 "We found that C. elegans TCTN-1 controls ciliary membrane composition similarly to other transition zone proteins, by excluding the non-ciliary membrane protein TRAM-1a from the cilium"].
- Not required for TZ localization of MKSR-1, MKS-5, MKS-6 or NPHP-4.
