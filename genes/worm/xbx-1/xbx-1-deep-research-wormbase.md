# WormBase gene description for xbx-1 (worm)

xbx-1 encodes a dynein light intermediate chain; XBX-1 activity is required for retrograde intraflagellar transport and thus, for proper formation of sensory neuron cilia; an XBX-1::YFP reporter fusion protein is found at the ciliary base and migrating along the ciliary axoneme in both anterograde and retrograde directions.
