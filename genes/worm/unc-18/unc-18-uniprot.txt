CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mediates vesicle exocytosis, protein trafficking and
CC       neurotransmission (PubMed:12973353, PubMed:1347782, PubMed:18596236,
CC       PubMed:19032153, PubMed:21423527, PubMed:21445306, PubMed:28821673,
CC       PubMed:8398155). Associates with SNARE protein unc-64 and thereby
CC       promotes the anterograde transport of unc-64 out of the endoplasmic
CC       reticulum and out of neuronal cell bodies (PubMed:18596236). Plays a
CC       role in synaptic vesicle docking and tethering through its association
CC       with unc-64 (PubMed:12973353, PubMed:21423527). Competes with SNARE
CC       protein tom-1 for binding to unc-64 (PubMed:21423527). Through
CC       association with unc-64, mediates the release of the neurotransmitter
CC       acetylcholine from cholinergic motor neurons, and thereby promotes
CC       locomotory behaviors (PubMed:1347782, PubMed:18596236, PubMed:28821673,
CC       PubMed:8398155). In the AFD thermosensory neuron, plays a role in
CC       regulating temperature-dependent locomotion through its phosphorylation
CC       by pkc-2 (PubMed:22593072). Probably by regulating neuronal
CC       transmission downstream of lin-3 and receptor lin-23 and phospholipase
CC       plc-3 and upstream of innexin unc-7 and egl-4/PKG in ALA neurons,
CC       involved in the decrease in pharyngeal pumping during the quiescent
CC       state that precedes each larval molt (PubMed:17891142). Plays a role in
CC       maintaining the localization of unc-6 in muscle cells
CC       (PubMed:20382828). Modulates locomotion in response to exogenous
CC       stimulants such as ethanol (PubMed:18923141, PubMed:24244732).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12973353, ECO:0000269|PubMed:1347782,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17891142, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18596236,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18923141, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19032153,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20382828, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21423527,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21445306, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22593072,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24244732, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28821673,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8398155}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer (PubMed:8939465). Interacts with unc-64 (via N-
CC       terminus, in open or in closed conformation); the interaction is direct
CC       (PubMed:10366611, PubMed:18596236, PubMed:19032153, PubMed:21423527,
CC       PubMed:22593072, PubMed:8939465, PubMed:9442061). Competes with tom-1
CC       for binding to unc-64 (PubMed:21423527). Interaction with unc-64 via
CC       the N-terminus mediates the secretion of the neurotransmitter
CC       acetylcholine from cholinergic motor neurons (PubMed:18596236).
CC       Interaction with unc-64 in open conformation promotes synaptic vesicle
CC       docking and tethering (PubMed:21423527). When phosphorylated by pkc-2,
CC       interaction in closed conformation with unc-64 is reduced
CC       (PubMed:22593072). Interaction with unc-64 is reduced in the presence
CC       of unc-13 (PubMed:10366611). Interacts with unc-13 (via N-terminus);
CC       the interaction is direct (PubMed:10366611).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10366611, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18596236,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19032153, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21423527,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22593072, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8939465,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9442061}.
