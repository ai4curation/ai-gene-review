CC   -!- FUNCTION: Induces host cell lysis (PubMed:10760296, PubMed:11078734,
CC       PubMed:19379010, PubMed:37440661). Inhibits the host translocase MraY
CC       activity that catalyzes the synthesis of lipid I, a necessary step for
CC       the host cell wall biosynthesis (PubMed:11078734, PubMed:19379010,
CC       PubMed:37440661). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10760296,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11078734, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19379010,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37440661}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Oligomerizes (PubMed:2466836). Interacts (via N-terminal) with
CC       host SlyD; this interaction protects E from proteolysis and stabilizes
CC       the YES complex (Probable) (PubMed:37440661). Interacts (via
CC       transmembrane region) with host MraY; this interaction inhibits MraY by
CC       blocking lipid access to the active site (Probable) (PubMed:22742425,
CC       PubMed:37440661). Part of the YES complex composed of 2 host Mray
CC       molecules, 2 lysis protein E molecules and 2 host SlyD molecules
CC       (PubMed:37440661). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22742425,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2466836, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37440661,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:17005977}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Host cell membrane {ECO:0000305}; Single-pass
CC       type I membrane protein {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Most of the references in this paper show that residue
CC       89 is Arg, in most other phiX174 proteomes the residue is Gln. We have
CC       chosen to show Gln at this position. {ECO:0000305}.
