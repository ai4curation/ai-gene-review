CC   -!- FUNCTION: Diguanylate cyclase involved in the regulation of swimming
CC       motility and biofilm formation (PubMed:23475948, PubMed:31209073).
CC       Probably catalyzes the synthesis of cyclic-di-GMP (c-di-GMP) via the
CC       condensation of 2 GTP molecules (Probable). Overexpression of bdcA
CC       increases intracellular c-di-GMP concentration, which correlates with
CC       inhibition of motility and enhanced biofilm formation
CC       (PubMed:31209073). It impacts flagellar expression and host infection,
CC       likely via its diguanylate cyclase activity (PubMed:31209073). Is
CC       required to effectively establish a respiratory tract infection in a
CC       mouse model (PubMed:31209073). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23475948,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31209073, ECO:0000305|PubMed:31209073}.
