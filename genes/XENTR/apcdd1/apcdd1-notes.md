# Curation Notes for *Xenopus tropicalis* `apcdd1` (`UniProtKB:Q6DF34`, `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2`)

## 1. Orthology and Sequence Conservation
- **Gene / Accession**: *Xenopus tropicalis* `apcdd1`, `UniProtKB:Q6DF34` (510 aa, sequence version 1), 1:1 diploid frog ortholog in `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2` (`PROTEIN APCDD1`), sharing ~93% sequence identity across the mature ectodomain and transmembrane region with experimentally characterized *Xenopus laevis* `apcdd1` (`UniProtKB:Q66KI8`, `PMID:20393562`, `PMID:32320685`).
- **Conserved Structural Determinants**: Retains the N-terminal cleavable signal peptide (`aa 1–20`), all 12 conserved ectodomain disulfide cysteines including the interdomain disulfide pair `Cys46–Cys460` (homologous to `Cys51–Cys465` in *X. laevis* `Q66KI8` and `Cys52–Cys466` in human `Q8J025` / mouse `Q3U128`, `PMID:37155902`), all three conserved ectodomain N-glycosylation sequons (`Asn94`, `Asn162`, `Asn313`, plus an additional `Asn310-Asp311-Ser312` motif), the ABD2 hydrophobic pocket, and the C-terminal single-pass transmembrane helix (`aa 489–509`).

## 2. Review of Existing GOA Annotations (9 rows)
- All nine existing GOA rows — three each for `GO:0005886` (`plasma membrane`, `IBA`/`IEA`/`ISS`), `GO:0017147` (`Wnt-protein binding`, `IBA`/`IEA`/`ISS`), and `GO:0030178` (`negative regulation of Wnt signaling pathway`, `IBA`/`IEA`/`ISS`) — descend from `PANTHER:PTN002826827`, `InterPro:IPR042425`, UniProt subcellular rules, and `UniProtKB:Q8J025` ISS projection. All three claims are strongly supported by direct experimental characterization of congeneric *X. laevis* `apcdd1` (`Q66KI8`, `PMID:20393562`, `PMID:32320685`) as well as mammalian `APCDD1` (`Q8J025`, `Q3U128`, `PMID:20393562`, `PMID:29154126`, `PMID:37155902`), so all nine rows are retained as `ACCEPT`. No `NEW` rows are proposed on `Q6DF34` because all direct amphibian morpholino and reporter experiments were performed in *X. laevis* (`Q66KI8`) rather than *X. tropicalis*.
