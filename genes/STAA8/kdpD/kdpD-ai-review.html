Gene Description

KdpD is an integral membrane sensor histidine kinase of the Staphylococcus aureus KdpD/KdpE system. Its cytoplasmic signaling domains regulate expression of the Kdp potassium uptake machinery. The NCTC 8325 protein is sequence-identical to the USA300 counterpart whose universal stress protein domain binds cyclic di-AMP. In the studied LAC* cellular background, increased cyclic di-AMP suppresses salt-induced kdpFABC expression; the biochemical coupling to kinase activity remains unresolved.
