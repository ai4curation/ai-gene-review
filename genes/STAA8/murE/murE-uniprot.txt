CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the addition of L-lysine to the nucleotide
CC       precursor UDP-N-acetylmuramoyl-L-alanyl-D-glutamate (UMAG) in the
CC       biosynthesis of bacterial cell-wall peptidoglycan. Cannot use
CC       diaminopimelate as substrate (PubMed:10498701, PubMed:14114846,
CC       PubMed:20659527). Can accept L-ornithine as substrate, but the
CC       efficiency is 400-fold lower than that with L-lysine (PubMed:20659527).
CC       Seems to have a role in beta-lactam antibiotic resistance
CC       (PubMed:14996801). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10498701,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14114846, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14996801,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20659527}.
