# THET8 glyQS: source-based annotation review

GlyQS is the homodimeric glycyl-tRNA synthetase of Thermus thermophilus HB8. Its class II catalytic domain activates glycine with ATP and transfers it to tRNA(Gly). Each identical polypeptide contributes to the dimeric enzyme, an architecture distinct from the bacterial GlyQ–GlyS heterotetramer. Structural and biochemical studies establish glycine and ATP recognition and relatively broad recognition of heterologous tRNA(Gly).
