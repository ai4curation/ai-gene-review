# ERG25 notes

Candida glabrata ERG25 is the C. glabrata ortholog of S. cerevisiae ERG25 and
belongs to PTHR11863. The best cached full-text study showed that ERG25
knockdown affects demethylation of 4,4-dimethylzymosterol and changes
host-cholesterol uptake by disrupting Aus1-associated sterol-rich membrane
domains [PMID:35399500, "Candida glabrata ERG25, which encodes an enzyme that
demethylates 4,4-dimethylzymosterol, is required for cholesterol uptake from
host serum."].

The 2025 abstract-only CRISPR paper deleted ERG25 homologs in non-albicans
Candida species and reinforces C. glabrata ERG25 as an antifungal target
[PMID:40451886, "This study demonstrated deletion of the ERG25 homolog in C.
glabrata and C. auris using an RNP-based CRISPR-Cas9 system."].
