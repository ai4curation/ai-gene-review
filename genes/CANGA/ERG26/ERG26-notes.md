# ERG26 (CAGL0G00594g, UniProt Q6FTQ4) notes

Module: ergosterol biosynthesis, inferred C-3 sterol dehydrogenase/C-4 decarboxylase.

- C. glabrata ERG26 has only electronic GOA rows in the current fetch; PANTHER places Q6FTQ4 in `PTHR43245:SF51`, the same Erg26/NSDHL subfamily as S. cerevisiae Erg26.
- ER-membrane and C-4 demethylation complex membership are transferred from S. cerevisiae. The row is plausible for this close Saccharomycotina ortholog, but no cached C. glabrata ERG26 localization or co-IP paper was found.
- GO has no specific term yet for the fungal Erg25-Erg26-Erg27-Erg28 C-4 demethylation complex; the current `GO:1902494 catalytic complex` transfer is broad but acceptable.
- PAINT exact activity (`PTN000041374`), fungal ER (`PTN000820972`) and Dikarya ergosterol biosynthesis (`PTN000820973`) all cover this orthologous branch.
- Web searches for `CAGL0G00594g`, `Candida glabrata ERG26`, and related strings did not surface a newer direct paper for this gene; the review therefore stays conservative and treats organism-specific evidence as absent.
