# CANGA ERG27 notes

## 2026-11-17 PTHR43647 Erg27 review

`CANGA/ERG27` has no GOA rows. Q6FIV3 is a UniProt-reviewed *Candida glabrata* `ERG27` protein in `PTHR43647:SF1`, the same PANTHER subfamily as characterized *S. cerevisiae* Erg27, experimentally grounded *C. albicans* Erg27, and *S. pombe* erg27.

Unlike Q5A888 from *C. albicans* and Q4WQ42 from *A. fumigatus*, Q6FIV3 does not currently appear in the PAINT leaf GAF for `PTHR43647`. I therefore treated exact 3-keto sterol reductase activity and ergosterol biosynthesis as conservative orthology proposals rather than as assertions that exist in current PAINT. The direct *C. albicans* seed reports that ERG27 is "required for sterol C-4 demethylation" [PMID:15552648 "required for sterol C-4 demethylation"].
