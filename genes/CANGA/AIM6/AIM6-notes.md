# AIM6 notes

## 2026-11-21

- C. glabrata AIM6 is the CGD / Nakaseomyces representative in
  `PTHR31571:SF1`, not the active `PTHR31571:SF2` RTT109 histone
  acetyltransferase branch. It should not inherit the fungal `PTN001586545`
  H3K56 acetyltransferase, DNA damage, or nuclear chromatin assertions.
- The UniProt entry has only InterPro2GO-style fold predictions for
  phosphoric diester hydrolase activity and lipid metabolism through
  `IPR017946`, plus a predicted signal peptide. No C. glabrata AIM6 activity
  or localization paper was found during this family pass.
- The interpretation should mirror the existing budding-yeast AIM6 review:
  AIM6 belongs to a divergent PLC-like phosphodiesterase fold, but its
  substrate, activity state and biological role are unknown.
