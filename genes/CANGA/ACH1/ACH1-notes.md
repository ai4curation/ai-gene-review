# ACH1 notes

## 2026-11-17 review

QuickGO returned no rows for C. glabrata ACH1/Q6FPF3, but UniProt and the fetched PANTHER member table place Q6FPF3 in `PTHR43609:SF1`, the same single-subfamily Ach1/CoaT family that contains S. cerevisiae ACH1, C. albicans ACH1, N. crassa acu-8 and A. nidulans CoaT.

PAINT has a single root transferase node, `PTN000484586`, carrying both `GO:0003986 acetyl-CoA hydrolase activity` and `GO:0008775 acetate CoA-transferase activity`. The S. cerevisiae re-characterization paper reports that Ach1p's highest activity is CoASH transfer from succinyl-CoA to acetate and that acetyl-CoA hydrolase activity is minor, so the transferase term is the better core molecular function. I did not propose the hydrolase activity for this C. glabrata entry.

Carman et al. support Candida ACH1 conservation: C. albicans ACH1 complemented the S. cerevisiae ach1 deletion on acetate medium. That is not direct evidence for C. glabrata ACH1, but it supports the PAINT placement across Saccharomycetales/Candida alongside the direct S. cerevisiae and A. nidulans seeds.

I searched PubMed/web for `"Q6FPF3" ACH1 "Candida glabrata"` and `"CAGL0J04268g" ACH1` on 2026-11-17. I found product pages and Swiss-Prot/EnsemblFungi-derived records but no newer primary C. glabrata ACH1 biochemical or localization paper.
