Gene Description

Eaf3 is a nuclear MRG-family chromatin protein in Nakaseomyces glabratus, historically Candida glabrata. It has an N-terminal Tudor-knot/chromo-like module and a C-terminal MRG interaction domain and is inferred to act in the conserved fungal Eaf3 branch of the NuA4 histone acetyltransferase, Rpd3S histone deacetylase, and Eaf5/7/3/TINTIN chromatin complexes that couple RNA polymerase II-associated chromatin to histone H3K36 methylation-dependent acetylation control.
