# HSV2 notes

## 2026-11-21 fungal PROPPIN review

The C. glabrata Q6FXC1 protein is the local HSV2 representative in the Saccharomycotina Atg21/Hsv2 branch of PTHR11227. UniProt places Q6FXC1 in PTHR11227, and the PTHR11227-entries snapshot lists it as `Q6FXC1,SVP1-like protein 2,...,Candida glabrata...HSV2`, matching the curated HSV2 orthology rather than the Atg18 branch.

PubMed searches on 2026-11-21 did not find a direct C. glabrata HSV2-specific paper; the review therefore treats same-aspect GOA rows as electronic transfers unless direct C. glabrata evidence is present in GOA. SGD-derived transfers support the conserved Hsv2 lipid-binding and phagophore/endosome localization role, while piecemeal microautophagy remains a direct budding-yeast finding rather than a directly demonstrated C. glabrata activity.

EnsemblFungi transfers the Q6FXC1 fungal-type vacuole row from S. cerevisiae Hsv2/YGR223C; this review keeps it as orthology-supported but does not treat it as a direct C. glabrata localization experiment.
