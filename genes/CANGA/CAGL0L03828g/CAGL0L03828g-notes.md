# CAGL0L03828g notes

## Literature and PAINT

- `CAGL0L03828g` (`Q6FLF4`) is the unnamed *Nakaseomyces glabratus*
  `PTHR19359:SF150` member, orthologous to budding-yeast and *C. albicans*
  `CYB5`.
- A 2026 *Microbiology Spectrum* paper directly deleted `CYB5` in the
  azole-resistant ISTB218 clinical strain and tied CgCyb5 to ergosterol
  abundance, methylated-sterol accumulation and azole tolerance under
  fluconazole stress [PMID:41891695, "required for 14-demethylation of
  lanosterol and 22-desaturation of episterol"].
- CGD still carries root ND rows for cellular component and biological process.
  The biological-process row is stale because the 2026 paper identifies the
  ergosterol-biosynthesis role in *N. glabratus* itself. The
  cellular-component row remains a true gap: the ER-membrane location is
  electronically inferred from the Cyb5 family, but I did not find a
  C. glabrata or N. glabratus CYB5 paper with direct localization or topology
  data.

## Newer Searches

- Searches for `CAGL0L03828g`, `Q6FLF4`, and *Candida glabrata*/*Nakaseomyces*
  `CYB5` found the 2026 paper above as the only direct post-genome paper on this
  specific gene. Searches for *Aspergillus fumigatus* PTHR19359 members found
  several unreviewed SF150/SF14 proteins but did not identify a characterized
  ergosterol-pathway paralog.
