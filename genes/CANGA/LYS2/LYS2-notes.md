# CANGA LYS2 notes

Candida glabrata LYS2, synonymously Nakaseomyces glabratus LYS2, encodes
UniProt Q6FMI5, a PTHR44845:SF1 L-2-aminoadipate reductase. The local GOA rows
currently include a UniProt manual ISS transfer of `GO:0004043` from
S. cerevisiae LYS2 and automated InterPro/Rhea/EC rows for `GO:0004043`,
`GO:0009085`, and the phosphopantetheine-bearing carrier-domain property
`GO:0031177`; unlike C. albicans, Q6FMI5 does not currently receive the
PTN001186117 GO-Central IBAs.

No direct C. glabrata LYS2 alpha-aminoadipate reductase paper was found in the
cached gene directory or targeted PubMed searches. Searches covered `Candida
glabrata LYS2 alpha-aminoadipate reductase`, `Candida glabrata CAGL0K07788g`,
`Nakaseomyces glabratus LYS2`, and `Q6FMI5 Lys2`.

The current review therefore keeps the ISS/IEA molecular-function and pathway
rows because Q6FMI5 is a conserved PTHR44845:SF1 fungal Lys2 protein, but it
does not cite a species-specific Candida glabrata experiment.

The root cellular-component ND row is an inferable no-direct-data statement
rather than a true biology gap: S. pombe lys1 has direct cytosol localization
and the S. cerevisiae LYS2 review also places the ortholog in the cytosol.
