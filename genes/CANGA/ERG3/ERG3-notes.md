# C. glabrata ERG3 notes

- PMID:8593007 cloned C. glabrata ERG3 and measured the sterol composition and antifungal susceptibility of erg3 deletion mutants.
- PMID:37929964 used an expanded CRISPR-RNP toolkit and resolved a prior strain-background conflict by showing that ERG3 deletion confers azole resistance in prototrophic C. glabrata strains.
- PMID:36108742 places CgErg3 in the CgSet4/CgUpc2a antifungal-response network; this is kept as a non-core drug-response phenotype.
- The 2024-2026 search found C. glabrata ergosterol-regulation literature but no newer direct Erg3 activity or ER-localization assay beyond PMID:37929964.
