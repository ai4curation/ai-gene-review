Gene Description

ThrB (MJ1104) is the ATP-dependent homoserine kinase of Methanocaldococcus jannaschii. This GHMP-family enzyme phosphorylates L-homoserine to O-phospho-L-homoserine in threonine biosynthesis. Recombinant enzyme assays and substrate-bound structures establish its substrate and nucleotide recognition; the distinct archaeal shikimate kinase is a distant GHMP-family relative, not an interchangeable activity.
