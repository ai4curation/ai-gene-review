CC   -!- FUNCTION: Part of the sulfoquinovose monooxygenase (sulfo-SMO) pathway,
CC       a D-sulfoquinovose degradation pathway that enables the complete
CC       utilization of all carbons within sulfoquinovose (SQ) with concomitant
CC       production of inorganic sulfite (PubMed:35074914). Catalyzes the first
CC       step of the pathway, the hydrolysis of sulfoquinovosyl glycerol (SQGro)
CC       to release sulfoquinovose (SQ) (PubMed:30276262). Hydrolyzes both
CC       epimers of SQGro, with a preference for the natural 2'R isomer
CC       (PubMed:30276262). In vitro, can use the substrate analog para-
CC       nitrophenyl alpha-sulfoquinovoside (PNPSQ), but shows no detectable
CC       activity toward 4-nitrophenyl alpha-D-glucopyranoside (PNPGlc)
CC       (PubMed:30276262). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30276262,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35074914}.
