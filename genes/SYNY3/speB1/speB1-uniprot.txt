CC   -!- FUNCTION: Ureohydrolase involved in the biosynthesis of spermidine via
CC       the carboxyaminopropylagmatine (CAPA) pathway (PubMed:37878710).
CC       Catalyzes the conversion of aminopropylagmatine (APA) to spermidine and
CC       urea (PubMed:37878710). Is highly specific to APA and incapable of
CC       releasing measurable urea from CAPA, agmatine, arginine, guanidine,
CC       guanidinobutyrate and guanidinopropionate (PubMed:37878710).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37878710}.
