CC       argininosuccinate, the immediate precursor of arginine
CC       (PubMed:38478146). SyArgG is the rate-limiting step in arginine
CC       biosynthesis in Synechocystis PCC 6803 (PubMed:38478146).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38478146}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activity decreases to 53.9% and 18.4% in the
CC       presence of 1 mM and 5 mM arginine, respectively (PubMed:38478146).
CC       Activity also decreases to 80.1%, 78.1% and 92.1% in the presence of 5
CC       mM ornithine, lysine and succinate, respectively (PubMed:38478146).
CC       Activity does not decrease in the presence of glutamate, glutamine or
CC       asparagine (PubMed:38478146). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38478146}.
