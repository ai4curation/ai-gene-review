CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the reversible conversion of N-acetylornithine to
CC       N-acetylglutamate-5-semialdehyde (PubMed:36982927). In vitro, also
CC       shows very low ornithine aminotransferase (OAT) and gamma-aminobutyrate
CC       aminotransferase (GABA-AT) activity, catalyzing the conversion of
CC       ornithine (Orn) to glutamate-5-semialdehyde and of gamma-aminobutyric
CC       acid (GABA) to succinate semialdehyde (PubMed:36982927). It has been
CC       shown to function as a GABA-AT and contributes to closing the
CC       tricarboxylic acid cycle of Synechocystis sp. PCC6803 via the GABA
CC       shunt (PubMed:24989231). However, the catalytic efficiency toward N-
CC       acetylornithine is 2500-fold and 10700-fold higher than that toward
CC       ornithine and gamma-aminobutyrate, respectively, indicating that the
CC       protein mainly functions as an N-acetylornithine aminotransferase
CC       (PubMed:36982927). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24989231,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36982927}.
