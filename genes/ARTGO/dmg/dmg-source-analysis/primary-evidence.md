## PMID:19369258, full PMC article

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2719421/
Full HTML and derived text remain in ignored audit cache; hashes are recorded in identity-provenance.yaml. The normal publication cache remains abstract-only. The following short passage is from Results, Significant Inhibition of Active Site 1 by an Iminium Lactone Mimic:

> we could not easily discriminate between the linear and cyclic forms

Interpretation: a synthetic E. coli W3GM formaldehyde reporter supports channeling. Wild-type enzyme produces little reporter response when folate availability is adequate; D552A/D552N or C-terminal deletion increases formaldehyde release. The experiments distinguish impaired folate coupling from amine oxidase activity. They do not directly quantify native NRRL B-2979 flux or prove which linear/cyclic intermediate is channeled. Diffusion simulations and inhibitor preferences support mechanistic models, not direct identification of a trapped intermediate.
