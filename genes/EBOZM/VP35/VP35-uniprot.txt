CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays an essential role in viral RNA synthesis and also a
CC       role in suppressing innate immune signaling (PubMed:11027311,
CC       PubMed:35533195). Acts as a polymerase cofactor in the RNA polymerase
CC       transcription and replication complexes (PubMed:16495261,
CC       PubMed:24495995, PubMed:9971816). Serves as nucleoprotein/NP monomer
CC       chaperone prior to the formation of the large oligomeric RNA-bound
CC       complexes (By similarity). Part of the external layer of the
CC       nucleocapsid, likely tethering the nucleocapsid to the matrix and
CC       membrane (PubMed:39293445). Regulates RNA synthesis by modulating NP-
CC       RNA interactions and interacting with DYNLL1 (PubMed:25741013). VP35-NP
CC       interaction controls the switch between RNA-bound NP and free NP and
CC       thus the switch between genome replication and genome packaging into
CC       the nucleocapsid (PubMed:25865894). Prevents establishment of cellular
CC       antiviral state, thereby suppressing host DC maturation
CC       (PubMed:26962215). Acts by inhibiting host RIGI activation both by
CC       shielding dsRNA from detection and by preventing PRKRA binding to RIGI
CC       (PubMed:23870315). Blocks virus-induced phosphorylation and activation
CC       of interferon regulatory factor 3/IRF3, a transcription factor critical
CC       for the induction of interferons alpha and beta (PubMed:12829834). This
CC       blockage is produced through the interaction with and inhibition of
CC       host IKBKE and TBK1, producing a strong inhibition of the
CC       phosphorylation and activation of IRF3 (PubMed:12829834). Also inhibits
CC       the antiviral effect mediated by the host interferon-induced, double-
CC       stranded RNA-activated protein kinase EIF2AK2/PKR (PubMed:17065211).
CC       Increases PIAS1-mediated SUMOylation of IRF7, thereby repressing
CC       interferon transcription (PubMed:19557165). Also acts as a suppressor
CC       of RNA silencing by interacting with host DICER1, TARBP2/TRBP and
CC       PRKRA/PACT (By similarity). As a dimer, binds and sequesters dsRNA
CC       contributing to the inhibition of interferon production (By
