Gene Description

HPr is a soluble phosphocarrier of the Bacillus subtilis carbohydrate phosphotransferase system. It relays phosphate through His15 from enzyme I to sugar-specific EIIA proteins. A separate phosphorylation at Ser46 converts HPr into a CcpA-associated transcriptional corepressor, linking preferred-carbon availability to catabolite repression.

Bacillus subtilis ptsH review notes

2026-11-07

P08877/BSU13900 is the native carbohydrate HPr of strain 168. UniProt records its membership in PTHR33705:SF2 (official label PHOSPHOCARRIER PROTEIN NPR), but the curated native function is enzyme I → HPr → sugar-specific EIIA. This is a positive functional counterexample to assigning a nitrogen-regulatory relay solely from that subfamily name. It does not imply that every member of the subfamily is a carbohydrate carrier.

The two core activities use distinct phosphosites. His15 participates in phosphoryl transfer; Ser46 phosphorylation controls association with CcpA. PMID:1549615 provides native structural evidence and proposes transfer geometry, while PMID:8596444 directly reports purified-complex DNA footprinting. PMID:8195089 adds mutation/reversion evidence for regulation of carbohydrate utilization. The current accessible caches of these papers are abstract-only; no unseen kinetic data or detailed assay conditions are inferred.

All five seeded rows, qualifiers and supporting entities are preserved. Both bare protein-binding rows are removed under the specific repository policy, without denying the underlying associations. No NEW annotation rows are added. Core molecular functions are independently supported syntheses; the reviewed process terms were already present. The IBA is judged against native function, not donor count. No PAINT gain/loss placement is asserted.

Official current GO definitions were checked for GO:0016775 and GO:0003714. The latter allows a cofactor binding a DNA-bound transcription factor; chromatin remodeling is a common example, not a requirement that excludes this bacterial mechanism. Cytoplasm is retained because the available localization source does not resolve a finer anatomical site.

The shared Falcon module-research attempt failed provider authentication (HTTP 403); direct primary-source assessment was used. No provider-generated report was fabricated.
