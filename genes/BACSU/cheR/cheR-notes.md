## 2026-11-06 Bacillus chemotaxis review

Identity: P31105; BACSU strain 168, taxon 224308. The primary gene name, rather than historical aliases, selected this record. CheR is the S-adenosylmethionine-dependent receptor methyltransferase of Bacillus subtilis chemotaxis. It methylates glutamate carboxyl groups on methyl-accepting chemotaxis receptors and participates in the receptor-modification arm of sensory adaptation. CheR mutants are impaired in adaptation to repellents. Bacillus receptor modification has site-dependent effects on kinase output, so receptor methylation cannot be assigned a uniform activating sign from the Escherichia coli model.
