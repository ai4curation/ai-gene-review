DnaN forms the homodimeric beta sliding clamp of Bacillus subtilis. The ring encircles duplex DNA and tethers DNA polymerases to support processive synthesis. DnaN supports both PolC and DnaE and influences DnaE fidelity. It also participates in replication-initiation control through its interaction with YabA. It is neither the DNA-polymerizing nor the proofreading catalytic subunit.
