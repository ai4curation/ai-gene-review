CC   -!- FUNCTION: Confers DNA tethering and processivity to DNA polymerases and
CC       other proteins. Acts as a clamp, forming a ring around DNA (a reaction
CC       catalyzed by the clamp-loading complex) which diffuses in an ATP-
CC       independent manner freely and bidirectionally along dsDNA. Initially
CC       characterized for its ability to contact the catalytic subunit of DNA
CC       polymerase III (Pol III), a complex, multichain enzyme responsible for
CC       most of the replicative synthesis in bacteria; Pol III exhibits 3'-5'
CC       exonuclease proofreading activity. The beta chain is required for
CC       initiation of replication as well as for processivity of DNA
CC       replication (By similarity). Overexpression in vivo stimulates
CC       initiation of DNA replication from oriC (PubMed:19737352). Increased
CC       levels of DnaN remove YabA from its association with DnaA on the
CC       chromosome, allowing DnaA to bind to its targets (PubMed:21895792). Its
CC       interaction with DnaA probably serves as a sink to prevent excessive
CC       replication initiation (Probable) (PubMed:28166228).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P0A988, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19737352,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21895792, ECO:0000305|PubMed:28166228}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms a ring-shaped head-to-tail homodimer (PubMed:25170813)
CC       around DNA which binds and tethers DNA polymerases and other proteins
CC       to the DNA. The DNA replisome complex has a single clamp-loading
CC       complex (3 tau and 1 each of delta, delta', psi and chi subunits) which
CC       binds 3 Pol III cores (1 core on the leading strand and 2 on the
CC       lagging strand) each with a beta sliding clamp dimer. Additional
CC       proteins in the replisome are other copies of gamma, psi and chi, Ssb,
CC       DNA helicase and RNA primase. Interacts with YabA, and via YabA, with
CC       DnaA (PubMed:12060778, PubMed:16461910). During sporulation probably
CC       interacts with SirA (PubMed:25041308). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P0A988,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12060778, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16461910,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25041308, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25170813}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       P05649; P37542: yabA; NbExp=4; IntAct=EBI-5244587, EBI-5243764;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm, nucleoid {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19737352,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21170359}. Note=Localizes in tight foci to the
CC       chromosomal replication center at mid-cell (PubMed:19737352,
CC       PubMed:21170359). When replication is blocked most foci are also lost
CC       (PubMed:19737352). Positioning does not rely on the C-terminus of SSB
CC       (ssbA) (PubMed:21170359). During sporulation colocalizes with SirA
CC       (PubMed:25041308). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19737352,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21170359, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25041308}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Part of the dnaA-dnaN operon, which is negatively regulated
CC       by DnaA (PubMed:11395445). Transcription seems to often terminate
CC       between the 2 genes (PubMed:11395445). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11395445}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the beta sliding clamp family. {ECO:0000305}.
