## 2026-11-06 Bacillus chemotaxis review

Identity: Q05522; BACSU strain 168, taxon 224308. The primary gene name, rather than historical aliases, selected this record. CheB is a receiver-regulated receptor methylesterase in Bacillus subtilis chemotaxis. It removes methyl esters from chemoreceptor glutamates and participates in sensory adaptation, particularly at high attractant concentrations where methylation-independent mechanisms do not fully compensate. CheA can phosphorylate its receiver domain. A receptor deamidase activity is inferred from the conserved CheB enzyme family, but the native deamidation substrates and their contributions are less firmly established than receptor demethylation.
