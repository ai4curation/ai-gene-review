CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the c-di-AMP-dependent regulation of the
CC       bacterial stringent response (PubMed:33619274, PubMed:35130724).
CC       Modulates the activities of at least two enzymes under conditions of
CC       potassium limitation (PubMed:33619274, PubMed:35130724). Apo-DarB
CC       regulates the activity of the GTP pyrophosphokinase RelA by interacting
CC       directly with RelA, leading to stimulation of (p)ppGpp synthesis and
CC       induction of the stringent response (PubMed:33619274). Apo-DarB also
CC       regulates pyruvate carboxylase (PYC) at two levels: directly at the
CC       protein level by binding to the enzyme and stimulating the synthesis of
CC       oxaloacetate and indirectly, by interaction with RelA, which leads to
CC       activation of the stringent response and to the increased expression of
CC       the pycA gene (PubMed:35130724). Stimulation of these enzymes by DarB
CC       is prevented in the presence of cyclic di-AMP (c-di-AMP)
CC       (PubMed:33619274, PubMed:35130724). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33619274,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35130724}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Binds c-di-AMP (PubMed:31061098, PubMed:33619274,
CC       PubMed:35130724, PubMed:35714772). Binding of c-di-AMP to DarB inhibits
CC       the interaction with RelA and PYC (PubMed:33619274, PubMed:35130724).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:31061098, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33619274,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35130724, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35714772}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:33619274, PubMed:35714772). Forms a
CC       homodimer with a parallel, head-to-head assembly of the monomers
CC       (PubMed:35714772). Under conditions of potassium starvation and
CC       corresponding low c-di-AMP levels, apo-DarB specifically interacts with
CC       the N-terminal region of the RelA (PubMed:33619274). Under the same
CC       conditions, apo-DarB also specifically interacts with the C-terminal
CC       part of the pyruvate carboxylase (PubMed:35130724).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33619274, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35130724,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35714772}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: No conformational changes occur upon c-di-AMP binding.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:35714772}.
