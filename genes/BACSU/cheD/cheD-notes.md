CheD is a receptor-modifying and regulatory protein in Bacillus subtilis chemotaxis. It deamidates selected receptor glutamines, including McpC Gln609, and can activate receptors through binding independently of deamidation. CheD also stimulates CheC phosphatase activity. CheY-P-dependent association of CheD with CheC provides adaptation feedback by controlling its availability to receptors; McpB is activated by binding rather than being deamidated by CheD.
