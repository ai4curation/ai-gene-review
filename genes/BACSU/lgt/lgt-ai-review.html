Gene Description

lgt encodes phosphatidylglycerol--prolipoprotein diacylglyceryl transferase, a polytopic cytoplasmic-membrane enzyme of Bacillus subtilis that transfers a diacylglyceryl group from phosphatidylglycerol to the lipobox cysteine of translocated prolipoproteins, the first step of bacterial lipoprotein maturation and a prerequisite for signal peptide cleavage by LspA. Unlike in Escherichia coli, lgt is not essential in B. subtilis: non-lipidated precursors still cross the membrane but are partly released from the cell, which lowers cell-associated lipoproteins such as the PrsA foldase and impairs protein secretion. Mutations in lgt (gerF) also block spore germination, consistent with the lipoprotein nature of several germination proteins.
