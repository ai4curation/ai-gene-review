Gene Description

PBP3, encoded by pbpC, is a membrane-anchored class-B penicillin-binding protein of Bacillus subtilis. Its serine-dependent transpeptidase activity contributes to peptidoglycan construction and beta-lactam tolerance. PBP3 is dispensable in an otherwise wild-type strain 168 background but becomes necessary when the catalytic activity of divisomal PBP2B is disabled. This compensation retains the physical PBP2B protein and does not make PBP3 an obligatory component of normal division.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

serine-type peptidoglycan D,D-transpeptidase activity

Definition: Catalysis of peptide crosslink formation in peptidoglycan by a serine-dependent D,D-transpeptidase: transfer of the donor D-Ala4 acyl group from a pentapeptide stem to an amino acceptor on another stem or its bridge, with release of terminal D-Ala5. The acceptor chemistry depends on the organism; this differs from L,D-transpeptidase-mediated 3–3 linkage formation.

Justification: The reviewed enzyme performs the class-B PBP crosslink reaction; neither a carboxypeptidase hydrolysis term nor an L,D-transpeptidase term accurately represents this endpoint. Reaction and physiological-scope limits are stated in the core description.
