Gene Description

pssA (pss) encodes the phosphatidylserine synthase of Bacillus subtilis, a 177-residue integral membrane CDP-alcohol phosphatidyltransferase (type-II phosphatidylserine synthase) with several transmembrane helices. It transfers the phosphatidyl group of CDP-diacylglycerol to L-serine, forming phosphatidylserine, in a manganese-dependent reaction. The enzyme is unrelated in sequence to the peripheral-membrane, PLD-superfamily phosphatidylserine synthase of E. coli but complements an E. coli pssA mutant, and phosphatidylserine it makes is decarboxylated to phosphatidylethanolamine.

Interpretation

P39823 is the type-II phosphatidylserine synthase of B. subtilis (PTHR14269,
subfamily SF59; IPR004533; TIGR00473). The heterologous expression data
establish its activity; the pathway step is shared with E. coli PssA despite
unrelated folds.
