# dnaX research and annotation review

## 2026-11-06

Identity: P09122, BACSU, B. subtilis strain 168, taxon 224308. Current UniProt response and untouched fetch-gene seed are archived in `.cache/module-audit-logs/chromosomal-replication-20261106/bacillus-baseline.json`. Original 10 annotation payloads and raw files remain unchanged.

DnaX is the tau subunit of the Bacillus subtilis DNA clamp loader. Three tau subunits assemble with one HolA/delta and one HolB/delta-prime subunit to load the beta sliding clamp onto primed DNA using ATP. Tau also connects replisome components. It contributes to DNA synthesis through clamp loading and replisome organization rather than catalyzing nucleotide incorporation.
