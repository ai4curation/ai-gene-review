CC   -!- FUNCTION: Helps load the DnaC replicative helicase onto single-stranded
CC       (ss)DNA and simulates the helicase activity; in the presence of DnaB
CC       more helicase activity is seen (PubMed:12718886, PubMed:17003052).
CC       Regulates DnaC helicase activity, at low concentrations stimulates the
CC       DNA helicase and ATPase activities of DnaC (PubMed:12718886). Has no
CC       measurable ATPase activity after 1 hour incubation of 6 uM DnaI with or
CC       without DNA (PubMed:12718886). Another group has found the protein has
CC       weak ATPase activity that is not stimulated by ssDNA (PubMed:17003052).
CC       Whole protein binds forked DNA (but not ssDNA) weakly; ATP and ADPNP
CC       (probably 5'-adenylyl beta, gamma-imidodiphosphate) have no effect on
CC       DNA binding (PubMed:17003052). DnaB, DnaD and DnaI may be required for
CC       a PriA-independent pathway of replication fork restart
CC       (PubMed:11679082). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11679082,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12718886, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17003052}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: The DNA replisome assembles sequentially on oriC in this
CC       order; DnaA, DnaD, DnaB, DnaI-DnaC helicase (PubMed:19968790). Monomer
CC       with a very minor amount of dimer in solution (PubMed:12718886,
CC       PubMed:24048025). Interacts with replicative helicase (from
CC       G.stearothermophilus, called DnaB); this interaction is disrupted by
CC       DnaD (PubMed:15556628). Interacts with replicative helicase DnaC, forms
CC       a DnaC(6):DnaI(6) complex (PubMed:10844689, PubMed:12718886,
CC       PubMed:17003052, PubMed:24048025). Interacts with the helicase as 3
CC       dimers (PubMed:24048025). A stable complex with DnaG primase,
CC       DnaI(6):helicase(6):DnaG(3) fragment can be isolated; DnaI and DnaG do
CC       not contact each other (helicase and DnaG in this complex are derived
CC       from G.stearothermophilus) (PubMed:24048025).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10844689, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12718886,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15556628, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17003052,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19968790, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24048025}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10844689}.
CC       Note=Forms foci near both cell poles and in the middle of cells
CC       (PubMed:10844689). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10844689}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Transcribed at constant levels during exponential growth.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20071750}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminal domain (approximately residues 1-136, Nd)
CC       interacts with helicase DnaC and has a Zn(2+) cofactor
CC       (PubMed:17003052, PubMed:19255093). The C-terminal domain
CC       (approximately residues 137-311, Cd) has ATPase activity
CC       (PubMed:17003052). The ATPase activity of the Cd (not whole protein) is
CC       stimulated by ssDNA; Cd has a cryptic DNA-binding site that is masked
CC       by the Nd. The CTD binds both ssDNA and forked DNA (PubMed:17003052).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17003052, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19255093}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Essential, it cannot be deleted; in depletion
CC       experiments DNA replication slows as soon as the protein is degraded
CC       and replication stops by 1 hour (PubMed:19968790).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19968790}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the DnaI family. {ECO:0000305}.
