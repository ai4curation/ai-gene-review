# BACSU gltX: source-based annotation review

GltX is the non-discriminating glutamyl-tRNA synthetase of Bacillus subtilis. It uses ATP to charge tRNA(Glu) with glutamate and also glutamylates tRNA(Gln), supplying the intermediate that GatCAB converts to Gln-tRNA(Gln). The enzyme is a zinc-containing class I synthetase and supports both direct glutamylation and the first step of indirect glutaminylation.

## Evidence and scope

The target UniProt record describes cognate glutamylation, but direct primary experiments establish the additional tRNA(Gln) substrate. The broad aminoacyl-tRNA ligase row is refined to both activities; no redundant NEW row is introduced.
