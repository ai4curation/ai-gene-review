# BACSU asnS: source-based annotation review

AsnS is the class II asparaginyl-tRNA synthetase of Bacillus subtilis. It uses ATP to attach free L-asparagine to tRNA(Asn), supplying Asn-tRNA(Asn) for cytoplasmic translation. Its direct charging route coexists with a non-discriminating AspRS–GatCAB pathway; the presence of AsnS therefore does not imply that the indirect route is absent.

## Evidence and scope

The target record provides a HAMAP-supported AsnRS assignment; PMID:26804570 establishes coexistence with the indirect pathway but is not presented as purified target AsnS kinetics.
