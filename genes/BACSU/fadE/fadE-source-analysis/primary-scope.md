Exact short excerpt, Results page 5190: “a slight growth defect of the ysiB disruptant being observed.”

Manual interpretation: Table 1 identifies strain 168 and its pMUTIN derivatives. Figure 12 uses 4.35 mM sodium palmitate and 1 mM IPTG at 37 C. IPTG induces downstream genes to mitigate insertion polarity. yusJ/fadE, yusL/fadN, yusK/fadA and etfA/B disruptions impair growth. The weaker ysiB/fadB phenotype is compatible with redundancy. These are native growth/regulation experiments, not purified FadE-to-ETF electron-transfer measurements. Purified-protein binding assays in this paper concern YsiA/FadR, so its C14–C20 ligand response is not a FadE substrate-specificity measurement. YusL translation initiation was reassigned 78 bp downstream.

Independent frozen sequence evidence: O32178 is the current 789-aa BSU32840 protein. Its UniProt record contains IPR006176/IPR006108 dehydrogenase domains and IPR001753/PF00378 hydratase/isomerase domains. The separate 258-aa FadB therefore does not establish absence of hydratase capability in FadN. No isolated FadN hydration assay was located.

Current QuickGO GO:0003995 explicitly specifies electron-transfer flavoprotein as acceptor. Native ETF dependence supports a plausible connection, but does not by itself identify the purified FadE reaction partner. The acceptor-unspecified core preserves the acyl-CoA oxidation claim without excluding ETF.
