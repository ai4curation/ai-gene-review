enzyme

the enzyme this call names, as read out of it by a second pass
why
Query InterProScan identifies an N-terminal RpiR helix-turn-helix domain and C-terminal SIS domain. The 100%-identity STRING homolog ybbH associates strongly with murQ (963) and a PTS sugar transporter (923), supporting amino sugar utilization context. Distant Shewanella homologs show strong beneficial disruption phenotypes on NAG, but their broad phenotypes do not establish the query's effector, regulatory targets, or regulatory direction.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by convergence of query-specific regulatory domains, the identical STRING homolog's sugar-utilization associations, and substrate-linked fitness evidence. Literature homologs support transcriptional regulation but provide no direct biochemical characterization of query-specific ligand binding; no cofitness or direct target-binding data are supplied. A specific HexR assignment or N-acetylmuramic acid-specific repressor annotation is unsupported because distant homolog contexts and network associations do not establish those identities or regulatory mechanisms.
GO terms
3 terms
why these terms
The protein is explicitly described as a 'DNA-binding transcriptional regulator', which supports the molecular function 'DNA-binding transcription factor activity' (GO:0003700) and the biological process 'regulation of DNA-templated transcription' (GO:0006355). It is further associated with 'amino sugar utilization'. While 'regulation of carbohydrate utilization' (GO:0043610) exists, the trace emphasizes that it is associated with sugar utilization rather than acting as a catalyst for the metabolism itself. The trace specifically rejects more specific assignments such as 'HexR' or 'N-acetylmuramic acid-specific repressor', as these are not sufficiently supported by the data. I have not assigned 'transcription repressor activity' because the annotation says 'regulator' generally, and the trace mentions 'repressor' only as part of a rejected specific identity (N-acetylmuramic acid-specific repressor).
