# Selected primary evidence from PMID:11744713

## Strain/construct, Figure 1

Construction of a bdbD derivative of B. subtilis 168.

## Functional substrate, Results

The secretion of active
PhoA by the bdbD strain was not restored when the transcription of bdbC was induced with IPTG (2.48 ⫾ 0.39 units/A600),
showing that BdbD, like BdbC (2), assists in the secretion of
active PhoA.

## Explicit evidence limit, Discussion

Although ComGC is
likely to be a target for the BdbD-BdbC system, no direct
evidence for the role of the BdbD and BdbC proteins in folding
of ComGC protein has been obtained so far.
