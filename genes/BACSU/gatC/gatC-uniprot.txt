CC   -!- FUNCTION: Structural subunit of the Asp/Glu-tRNA(Asn/Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex GatCAB (Probable). GatCAB allows the formation
CC       of correctly charged Asn-tRNA(Asn) or Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the multi-
CC       step transamidation of misacylated Asp-tRNA(Asn) or Glu-tRNA(Gln) in
CC       organisms which lack either or both of asparaginyl-tRNA or glutaminyl-
CC       tRNA synthetases (PubMed:9789001). The reaction takes place in the
CC       presence of glutamine and ATP through an activated phospho-Asp-
CC       tRNA(Asn) or phospho-Glu-tRNA(Gln). This subunit forms a belt that
CC       wraps around the interface of the catalytic subunits A and B to
CC       stabilize the complex (By similarity). {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_00122, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9789001, ECO:0000305|PubMed:9789001}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the heterotrimeric GatCAB glutamyl-tRNA(Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex composed of GatA, GatB and GatC.
