PMID17056751, DOI10.1128/JB.01273-06, publisher full text: https://journals.asm.org/doi/full/10.1128/jb.01273-06

Exact results excerpt:
> Under the same conditions, no cystathionine β-synthase activity was detected with CysK.

Both serine and OAS were tested with homocysteine. HPLC confirmed cystathionine only with YrhA/MccA, not CysK. CysK-His6 was active with OAS and sodium sulfide (cysteine endpoint). The strain table identifies168 trpC2 and its derivatives; the cysE14 phenotype is QB944, also carrying purA26. Purified CysK was expressed from pDIA5708/pET22b in E. coli, with a C-terminal His6 tag. The activity is an independently demonstrated cysteine-forming step; methionine-use growth phenotypes do not establish direct methionine cleavage by CysE or CysK.

PMID18974048, DOI10.1074/jbc.M805951200, author-hosted full text: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/23441624_The_CymR_Regulator_in_Complex_with_the_Enzyme_CysK_Controls_Cysteine_Metabolism_in_Bacillus_subtilis

Exact results excerpt, line break normalized:
> CysK increased 7-fold the stability of binding of CymR to the ytlI promoter region.

Purified CysE and CysK were catalytically active separately. CysE addition did not alter CysK activity, nor vice versa. SPR, pulldown and two-hybrid tests failed to show CysE-CysK association under the experimental conditions. Controls included N- or C-terminal His6-CysE, MBP-CysK and cleaved CysK. CymR binding was positive, CysK alone failed to bind the promoter, and OAS promoted regulatory-complex dissociation. Sedimentation supports a CysK homodimer and concentration-dependent CymR/CysK assemblies. Native physiology measurements used168.

CysK cloning coordinates +1..+924 agree with the308-residue reference length. CysE coordinates printed +1..+626 do not reconstruct the current217-residue sequence unambiguously. This could reflect a coordinate/reporting or construct difference; no exact plasmid match, truncation, corrected start site or sequence error is asserted. Frozen UniProt maps these experiments to Q06750 and P37887 in168, independently supporting the intended gene identities.
