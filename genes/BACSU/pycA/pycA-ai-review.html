Gene Description

PycA is the single-chain, biotin-dependent pyruvate carboxylase of Bacillus subtilis. Each chain carries biotin carboxylase, carboxyltransferase and biotin carboxyl carrier domains, so the enzyme first carboxylates its covalently bound biotin with bicarbonate and ATP and then transfers the carboxyl group to pyruvate to form oxaloacetate. PycA provides the anaplerotic supply of oxaloacetate needed for growth on glucose. It is allosterically activated by acetyl-CoA and is further stimulated by direct binding of the apo form of the c-di-AMP receptor DarB under potassium limitation.
