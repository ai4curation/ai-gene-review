FadN (YusL) is a large fatty-acid beta-oxidation protein of Bacillus subtilis 168 with an NAD-dependent hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydrogenase module and an additional enoyl-CoA hydratase/isomerase-like domain. Native yusL disruption impairs palmitate utilization. Hydroxyacyl-CoA oxidation is assigned by conserved catalytic architecture; its additional hydration chemistry and exact partner organization remain incompletely characterized.
