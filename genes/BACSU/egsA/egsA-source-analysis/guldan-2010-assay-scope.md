## Recombinant kinetics (printed page 62, Table 10; PDF page 80)

The protein was produced recombinantly in E. coli; the thesis plasmid table identifies pET21a_bsaraM with a C-terminal His6 tag. The native genetic experiments use B. subtilis subsp. subtilis strain 168. These are distinct assay contexts.

Table 10 reports catalytic efficiencies of 13,800 ± 830 M−1 s−1 with NADH and 108 ± 27.77 M−1 s−1 with NADPH, approximately a 128-fold difference. NADPH turnover was measured, but its individual KM and kcat were not determined. Conditions were 50 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.2, 45°C, 1 µM active sites and saturating second substrate; three independent measurements. The PDF table was visually checked. These measurements support preferred NADH and genuine secondary NADPH usage; they do not establish intracellular cofactor usage or net flux. Different forward/reverse KM values are not an equilibrium or a native-flux measurement.

## Disruption and lipid-feeding experiments (printed pages 67–68, 86–88)

The araM disruption used pMUTIN4 with an internal araM fragment in strain 168. The Southern-blot interpretation reports multiple insertions, so this is not a clean markerless deletion. Section 4.1.5 compares lipoteichoic-acid abundance by ELISA, reporting:

> Es zeigte sich kein Unterschied in der detektierten Menge an Lipoteichonsäuren zwischen den Stämmen

Translation: no difference was detected in lipoteichoic-acid amounts between strains. The thesis says the data are not shown. This tests the lipoteichoic-acid hypothesis, not endogenous HepG synthesis.

Section 4.4.1 and Figure 27 compare WT, ΔaraM and ΔpcrB after feeding 1 µCi exogenous 14C-G1P to overnight cultures (5 ml LB, 37°C). TLC detects matching product patterns in WT and ΔaraM, with two products missing in ΔpcrB. The text states:

> Wildtyp und die B. subtilis ΔaraM-Mutante identisch sind.

External G1P bypasses its endogenous formation. Thus these experiments establish PcrB-dependent conversion of fed G1P into hydrophobic products; they do not establish that EgsA supplies native PcrB flux, nor that EgsA is dispensable without precursor feeding. No un-fed egsA knockout/complementation lipidomics establishing that coupling was identified in the sources inspected.
