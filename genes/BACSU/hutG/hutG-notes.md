# hutG (Bacillus subtilis 168, P42068) notes

## 2026-11-11

- Deep research was not run for this gene in this session; no provider file
  exists. The review rests on the curated UniProt record (P42068), the cached
  citations PMID:4990470 (citation only, no abstract) and PMID:7704263
  (abstract), and the PTHR11358 family review.
- Function: formimidoylglutamase (EC 3.5.3.8), N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate + H2O
  = formamide + L-glutamate; Mn(2+) cofactor, alkaline pH optimum
  [file:BACSU/hutG/hutG-uniprot.txt "Reaction=N-formimidoyl-L-glutamate + H2O = formamide + L-glutamate;"].
- Family: PTHR11358:SF35 FORMIMIDOYLGLUTAMASE within the arginase/agmatinase
  ureohydrolase family. The agmatinase and putrescine-biosynthesis IBAs come
  from PTN000144486, which sits above SF35; they are over-propagations for a
  formimidoylglutamase and are removed.
- The route-specific BP terms GO:0019556 (histidine catabolism to glutamate and
  formamide) and GO:0019557 (to glutamate and formate) are obsolete, so
  GO:0006548 is the process term.
- Exemplar for the one-step formamide terminal route of the histidine
  catabolism module (modules/histidine_catabolism.yaml).
