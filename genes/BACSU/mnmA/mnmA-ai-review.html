Gene Description

MnmA is an ATP-dependent tRNA-uridine 2-sulfurtransferase that introduces sulfur at C2 of wobble uridine. In Bacillus subtilis it works with the adjacent cysteine desulfurase YrvO/IscS1 through an abbreviated sulfur-delivery pathway. Biochemical reconstitution and genetic complementation support this cognate pair, distinct from the Tus-dependent relay of Escherichia coli.
