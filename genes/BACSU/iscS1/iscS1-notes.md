# BACSU iscS1: source-bounded annotation review

IscS1, also called YrvO, is a pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent cysteine desulfurase that mobilizes sulfur from L-cysteine for its cognate tRNA thiouridylase MnmA. The Bacillus subtilis YrvO–MnmA pair supports ATP-dependent C2 thiolation of tRNA wobble uridine without the Escherichia coli Tus relay. This dedicated sulfur-delivery role does not imply that IscS1 is a general iron-sulfur scaffold or the universal donor for all cellular sulfur pathways.
