PolC is the major processive replicative DNA polymerase of Bacillus subtilis, synthesizing the leading strand and most of the lagging strand with the beta sliding clamp and clamp-loader machinery. A proofreading 3′–5′ exonuclease is integrated into the same polypeptide. DnaE efficiently extends RNA primers before transfer to PolC; PolC can also proofread errors made by DnaE.
