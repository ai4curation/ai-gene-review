McpB is a plasma-membrane methyl-accepting chemotaxis receptor of Bacillus subtilis that directly senses asparagine through its extracellular sensing domain. Attractant-bound McpB stimulates the CheA kinase through coupling proteins, increasing CheY phosphorylation and smooth swimming. Receptor modification and interactions with CheD participate in sensory adaptation; CheD activates McpB through binding rather than by deamidating it.
