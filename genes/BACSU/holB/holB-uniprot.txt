CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the DNA clamp loading complex consisting of
CC       tau(3):delta(1):delta'(1) (PubMed:23525462). The DNA polymerase III
CC       holoenzyme complex contains at least 10 different subunits organized
CC       into 3 functionally essential subassemblies: the Pol III core, the beta
CC       sliding clamp processivity factor and the clamp-loading complex. The
CC       Pol III core (subunits alpha, epsilon and theta) contains the
CC       polymerase and the 3'-5' exonuclease proofreading activities. The
CC       polymerase is tethered to the template via the dimeric beta sliding
CC       clamp processivity factor. The DNA clamp-loading complex assembles the
CC       beta sliding clamp onto the primed template and plays a central role in
CC       the organization and communication at the replication fork (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P28631,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23525462}.
