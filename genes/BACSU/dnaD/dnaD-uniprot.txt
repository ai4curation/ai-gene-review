CC   -!- FUNCTION: Required to load replicative helicase DnaC onto replication
CC       forks. Binds to a DnaD recognition element (DRE) which has pairs of 5'-
CC       TnnT-3' motifs; there is a strong DRE at oriC opposite the DnaA-trios
CC       recognized by DnaA (PubMed:37093985). During DNA replication from the
CC       origin of replication (oriC) in the DNA replisome, DnaD is required
CC       after DnaA, before DnaB and subsequent helicase DnaC loading
CC       (PubMed:15186423, PubMed:19968790). A component of the replication
CC       restart primosome, which reloads the replicative helicase on sites
CC       other than oriC (PubMed:11585815). DnaB, DnaD and DnaI may also be
CC       required for a PriA-independent pathway of replication fork restart
CC       (PubMed:11679082). DnaB and DnaD work together to allow DnaB access to
CC       single-stranded (ss)DNA (PubMed:15686560). Has DNA remodeling activity
CC       that converts supercoiled plasmid into an open circular form; DnaD
CC       forms scaffolds inside the plasmid DNA (PubMed:15556628,
CC       PubMed:16002087). Plasmid relaxation incorporates both wrapping around
CC       the DnaD protein scaffold and simultaneous untwisting, no nicking of
CC       the DNA is seen (PubMed:16002087). Also converts linear DNA into an
CC       open circular form (PubMed:16002087). Disrupts a replicative helicase-
CC       DnaI complex (PubMed:15556628). Inhibits the ability of DnaA-ATP to
CC       form a helix on DNA; does not disassemble preformed helices in vitro
CC       (PubMed:23909787). Binds ssDNA (PubMed:11585815, PubMed:15556628,
CC       PubMed:15686560, PubMed:20587500, PubMed:37093985), and replication
CC       fork-like substrates (PubMed:11585815), supercoiled plasmid
CC       (PubMed:20587500), but not stably to short double-stranded (ds)DNA
CC       (PubMed:11585815, PubMed:37093985). DnaD stimulates DnaB DNA-binding
CC       activities (PubMed:11585815). DnaB and DnaD are required to load
CC       helicase on the repN plasmid origin of replication (oriN)
CC       (PubMed:36416272). Causes a severe growth defect upon overexpression
CC       even in an oriC-independent strain (PubMed:20587500).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11585815, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11679082,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15186423, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15556628,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15686560, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16002087,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19968790, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20587500,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23909787, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36416272,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37093985}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Recruitment to oriC requires DnaA but not DnaB,
CC       DnaC or DnaI (PubMed:19968790, PubMed:36416272) and is blocked by SirA
CC       (PubMed:36416272). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36416272}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: The DNA replisome assembles sequentially on oriC in this
CC       order; DnaA, DnaD, DnaB, DnaI-DnaC helicase (PubMed:19968790).
CC       Homodimer (PubMed:11585815, PubMed:15686560, PubMed:18206906).
CC       Homotetramer (PubMed:36416272, PubMed:37093985). Oligomerization in
CC       vitro is concentration dependent (PubMed:15556628, PubMed:16002087).
CC       Part of the replication restart primosome which assembles in this
CC       order; PriA, DnaD then DnaB. The preferred DNA substrate mimics an
CC       arrested DNA replication fork with unreplicated lagging strand
CC       (PubMed:11585815). Interacts with DnaA (PubMed:18506095,
CC       PubMed:36416272), DnaB (PubMed:15186423, PubMed:15686560,
CC       PubMed:36416272) and PriA (PubMed:15686560). Interaction with DnaB
CC       requires DnaD to dimerize (PubMed:36416272).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11585815, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15186423,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15556628, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15686560,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16002087, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18206906,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18506095, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19968790,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36416272, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37093985}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15186423}. Note=In
CC       strains with the dnaB75 ('S371P') allele about 15% of protein is
CC       associated with the cell membrane (PubMed:15186423).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15186423}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Transcribed at constant levels during exponential growth.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20071750}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminus (residues 1-127) forms a winged-helix with
CC       N- and C-terminal extensions (PubMed:18206906). The N-terminus mediates
CC       oligomerization; at least dimer formation is required for interaction
CC       with DnaB (PubMed:36416272). The N-terminus also mediates interaction
CC       with DnaA (PubMed:36416272). The C-terminal tail mediates interaction
CC       with DnaB (PubMed:36416272) and with ssDNA of the DnaD recognition
CC       element (DRE) (PubMed:37093985). Has 2 domains with homology to DnaB
CC       called DDBH1 and DDBH2 (DnaD DnaB Homology 1 and 2) (PubMed:20587500).
CC       Internal fragments (residues 129-196 and 129-206) do not bind
CC       supercoiled plasmid DNA while a slightly longer fragment (residues 129-
CC       215) does (PubMed:20587500). Deletion of the C-terminal tail (residues
CC       197-232) is lethal in vivo, probably due to loss of DNA-binding
CC       (PubMed:20587500). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18206906,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36416272, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37093985}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Essential, it cannot be deleted (PubMed:36416272,
CC       PubMed:37093985) even in the presence of its loss-of-function
CC       suppressor dnaB75 (PubMed:15686560). In depletion experiments DNA
CC       replication slows as soon as the protein is degraded and replication
CC       stops by 1 hour (PubMed:19968790). Very low level support growth
CC       (PubMed:37093985). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15686560,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36416272, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37093985}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: There are 3000-5000 molecules per cell at 30 or 50
CC       degrees Celsius. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15686560}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the DnaB/DnaD family.
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:20587500}.
