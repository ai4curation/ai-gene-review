CC   -!- FUNCTION: The main replicative DNA helicase, it participates in
CC       initiation and elongation during chromosome replication
CC       (PubMed:7711902). Travels ahead of the DNA replisome, separating dsDNA
CC       into templates for DNA synthesis. The monomer has helicase activity in
CC       the presence of DnaI which is further increased by DnaB; the purified
CC       oligomeric form (probably a DnaC hexamer) does not have helicase
CC       activity in vitro, nor does the DnaC(6):DnaI(6) complex
CC       (PubMed:12718886). The direction was not determined but is probably 5'-
CC       3' (Probable) (PubMed:12718886). Helicase activity requires an rNTP and
CC       is inactive with dNTPs (PubMed:12718886). Has weak ATPase activity as a
CC       monomer, as an oligomer has ATPase activity which is stimulated by
CC       single-stranded (ss)DNA and further stimulated by DnaI and more by DnaB
CC       (PubMed:12718886). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12718886,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7711902, ECO:0000305|PubMed:12718886}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: Deletion of a single T residue in the promoter region (a run
CC       of 8 Ts becomes 7 Ts) decreases the helicase levels by 50%, decreasing
CC       DNA replication initiation during fast growth in rich medium.
CC       Suppresses the synthetic lethality of a dnaA1-yabA deletion for growth
CC       on rich medium. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:36053906}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Couples ATP hydrolysis with the unwinding of duplex DNA at the
CC         replication fork by translocating in the 5'-3' direction.;
CC         EC=5.6.2.3; Evidence={ECO:0000305|PubMed:12718886};
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=ATP + H2O = ADP + phosphate + H(+); Xref=Rhea:RHEA:13065,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:15377, ChEBI:CHEBI:15378, ChEBI:CHEBI:30616,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:43474, ChEBI:CHEBI:456216; EC=5.6.2.3;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:12718886};
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: The DNA replisome assembles sequentially on oriC in this
CC       order; DnaA, DnaD, DnaB, DnaI-DnaC helicase (PubMed:19968790). Monomer
CC       in the absence of ATP, in its presence forms a probable homohexamer
CC       which is not active as a helicase in vitro (PubMed:12718886). Interacts
CC       separately and simultaneously with helicase loaders DnaB
CC       (PubMed:12718886) and DnaI (PubMed:10844689, PubMed:12718886).
CC       Interaction with DnaB does not require ATP (PubMed:12718886).
CC       Interaction with DnaI requires ATP, probably forms a DnaC(6):DnaI(6)
CC       complex, which is not active as a helicase (PubMed:12718886).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10844689, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12718886,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19968790}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       P37469; P06567: dnaI; NbExp=4; IntAct=EBI-2122822, EBI-2122847;
CC       P37469; O31435: ybdM; NbExp=3; IntAct=EBI-2122822, EBI-5255200;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm, nucleoid
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21170359}. Note=Localizes in tight foci to the
CC       chromosomal replication center at mid-cell; positioning does not rely
CC       on the C-terminus of SSB (ssbA) (PubMed:21170359).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21170359}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Essential, it cannot be deleted; in depletion
CC       experiments DNA replication stops as soon as the protein is degraded
CC       (PubMed:19968790). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19968790}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the helicase family. DnaB subfamily.
