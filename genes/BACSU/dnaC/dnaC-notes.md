## 2026-11-06

Identity: P37469, BACSU, B. subtilis strain 168, taxon 224308. Current UniProt response and untouched fetch-gene seed are archived in `.cache/module-audit-logs/chromosomal-replication-20261106/bacillus-baseline.json`. Original 17 annotation payloads and raw files remain unchanged.

DnaC is the replicative DNA helicase of Bacillus subtilis. Its ATP-dependent oligomer unwinds parental DNA ahead of the replication fork. DnaI and the accessory protein DnaB assist its functional loading, while DnaG synthesizes RNA primers on the exposed template. Bacillus DnaC is the counterpart of the helicase called DnaB in Escherichia coli; Bacillus DnaB is a different loading protein.
