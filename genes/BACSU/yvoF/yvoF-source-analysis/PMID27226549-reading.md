The primary paragraph describes sequential crude-extract and ultracentrifugation fractions assayed for acetylation activity. Activity concentrated in membrane-containing fractions. Higher salt improved recombinant YvoF recovery; the authors argued for peripheral association rather than an integral membrane protein, with the attachment mechanism unresolved. Activity fractionation supports membrane association but does not directly map the side of the membrane. The cytoplasmic-side assignment in the 2025 pathway model additionally uses intracellular acetyl-CoA as donor. The two statements are compatible: association with the cytoplasmic face does not mean a freely soluble cytosolic protein.
