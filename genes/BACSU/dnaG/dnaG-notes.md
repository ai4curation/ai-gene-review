DnaG is the DNA-directed RNA polymerase that makes short RNA primers during Bacillus subtilis chromosome replication. It cooperates with the DnaC replicative helicase at the replication fork. The primers are initially extended with DNA by DnaE before transfer to PolC for rapid synthesis. Its N-terminal zinc-binding region, central catalytic region and C-terminal helicase-interaction region support this priming activity.
