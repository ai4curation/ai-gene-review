# YqgS (UniProtKB:P54496) notes

## Identity
- UniProt P54496 = YQGS_BACSU, gene `yqgS`, locus BSU24840. UniProt protein
  existence PE 3 (inferred from homology); name "Lipoteichoic acid synthase-like
  YqgS".
- Functionally characterised in Wörmann et al. 2011 [PMID:21255105] as a
  **bona-fide LTA synthase** that makes polyglycerolphosphate polymer when highly
  expressed ["YqgS can produce polyglycerolphosphate polymers, when expressed at
  sufficiently high levels"]; in the earlier Gründling & Schneewind assay it did
  not restore S. aureus growth/LTA at lower expression [PMID:17483484].
- Acts redundantly with the major synthase (ltaS/yflE) in LTA synthesis during
  sporulation; the double loss gives an absolute sporulation block
  [PMID:19229300, full text identifies the pair as ltaS and yqgS].
