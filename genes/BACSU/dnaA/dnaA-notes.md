DnaA is the ATP-binding initiator of chromosome replication in Bacillus subtilis. It recognizes double-stranded DnaA boxes and single-stranded DnaA-trio sequences at oriC, assembling an oligomer that opens and stabilizes the origin before recruitment of DnaD, DnaB and the DnaI–DnaC helicase-loading machinery. DnaA also regulates transcription and is controlled by proteins including Soj, YabA and SirA.
