Gene Description

FtsW is the membrane-integrated SEDS peptidoglycan polymerase of the Bacillus subtilis divisome. It extends septal glycan chains in association with the class-B penicillin-binding protein PBP2B. Its catalytic role is distinct from peptide crosslinking by PBPs and from transbilayer delivery of lipid II. The strain 168 protein is the reference ortholog of FtsW studied by live-cell imaging in PY79-derived strains.

Core Functions

Polymerizes septal peptidoglycan glycan strands as the FtsW catalytic component associated with PBP2B. This strain 168 assignment combines the curated FtsW subfamily with PY79 live-cell synthase dynamics and purified ortholog biochemistry; the latter does not establish an independent purified O07639 assay. The current GO reaction uses a lysine-containing lipid-II template; native Bacillus mDAP-containing stem chemistry is a substrate-specificity caveat to that generic polymerase annotation.
