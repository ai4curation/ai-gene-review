# BACSU groEL review — 2026-11-07

GroEL is the Hsp60-family chaperonin of Bacillus subtilis. Together with the heptameric GroES lid, its ATP-driven cage promotes folding of non-native proteins in the cytoplasm. It participates in protein homeostasis and the heat-shock response, including maintenance of the HrcA regulatory system. GroEL also accumulates outside cells during stationary phase through nonclassical release.
