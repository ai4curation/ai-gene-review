Paraphrase: the purified HrcA114 DNA-binding/footprinting reactions use E. coli GroEL and GroES. Those reactions do not directly assay purified Bacillus GroE proteins. Separately, Figure 7 uses B. subtilis AS02 crude extract on immobilized HrcA114: GroEL is selectively recovered, with DnaK/HtpG controls. The native GroE-depletion experiment uses B. subtilis SR15 with a xylose-regulated groE operon. Thus the paper provides native-system dependence and native GroEL association, alongside heterologous purified biochemical activation. It does not establish that GroEL itself binds the CIRCE DNA operator or acts as a DNA-binding transcription factor.
