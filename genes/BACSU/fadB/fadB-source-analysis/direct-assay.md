Exact excerpt, Results: “FadBBs is active in the hydratation of crotonyl-CoA”.

Methods use Bacillus subtilis 168 (trpC2) DNA to amplify ysiB/fadB, confirm cloning by nucleotide sequencing and express the His-tagged protein in E. coli BL21(DE3) Gold. Hydration is monitored by loss of crotonyl-CoA absorbance at 263 nm, pH 7.5 and 25 C. This is direct enzyme evidence, not a native flux measurement or a broad chain-length survey. The text disagrees about the His-tag end; only the unambiguous His-tagged designation is used here. The assay does not independently resolve product stereochemistry. The specific 2-enoyl reaction assignment is consistent with the substrate and curated Rhea mapping.
