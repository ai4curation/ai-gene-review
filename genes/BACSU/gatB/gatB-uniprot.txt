CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic subunit of the Asp/Glu-tRNA(Asn/Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex GatCAB (Probable). GatCAB allows the formation
CC       of correctly charged Asn-tRNA(Asn) or Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the multi-
CC       step transamidation of misacylated Asp-tRNA(Asn) or Glu-tRNA(Gln) in
CC       organisms which lack either or both of asparaginyl-tRNA or glutaminyl-
CC       tRNA synthetases (PubMed:9789001). The reaction takes place in the
CC       presence of glutamine and ATP through an activated phospho-Asp-
CC       tRNA(Asn) or phospho-Glu-tRNA(Gln). This subunit specifically
CC       recognizes tRNA(Gln) and tRNA(Asn), and mediates the transamidation of
CC       misloaded Glu and Asp respectively by catalyzing formation of the
CC       activated gamma-phospho-intermediate and its subsequent aminolysis
CC       using ammonia produced by the gatA subunit. Recognizes the first base
CC       pair and the D-loop of its specific tRNA substrates (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_00121, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9789001,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:9789001}.
