# BACSU groES review — 2026-11-07

GroES is the heptameric cochaperonin of Bacillus subtilis. It binds the apical face of a GroEL ring and caps the folding chamber, enabling substrate encapsulation and productive protein folding. This heat-inducible cytoplasmic protein contributes to the ATP-dependent chaperonin cycle through its structural and regulatory role; GroEL supplies the ATPase machinery.
