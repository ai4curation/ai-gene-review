Gene Description

GlpK is the ATP-dependent glycerol kinase of Bacillus subtilis. It converts glycerol to sn-glycerol 3-phosphate for catabolism through GlpD to dihydroxyacetone phosphate. This intracellular phosphorylation traps imported glycerol and supplies the inducer of the glp regulon. GlpK activity is coupled to the phosphotransferase regulatory system, although glycerol itself is not imported by that system.
