# cysE: Bacillus subtilis168

CysE is the soluble serine acetyltransferase of Bacillus subtilis168. It produces O-acetylserine from serine and acetyl-CoA for cysteine synthesis and is feedback-inhibited by cysteine. O-acetylserine also influences the CymR-CysK regulatory system. Under the tested conditions CysE is active independently of CysK and does not form the regulatory cysteine synthase complex found in several other bacteria.
