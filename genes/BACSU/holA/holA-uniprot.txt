CC   -!- FUNCTION: Part of the beta sliding clamp loading complex, which
CC       hydrolyzes ATP to load the beta clamp onto primed DNA to form the DNA
CC       replication pre-initiation complex. DNA polymerase III is a complex,
CC       multichain enzyme responsible for most of the replicative synthesis in
CC       bacteria. This DNA polymerase also exhibits 3'-5' exonuclease activity.
CC       The delta subunit is the wrench that will open the beta subunit dimer.
CC       The DNA clamp loading complex (tau(3),delta,delta') is thought to load
CC       beta dimers onto DNA by binding ATP which alters the complex's
CC       conformation so it can bind beta sliding clamp dimers and open them at
CC       one interface. Primed DNA is recognized, ATP is hydrolyzed releasing
CC       the clamp loading complex and closing the beta sliding clamp ring
CC       around the primed DNA. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P28630}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=DNA(n) + a 2'-deoxyribonucleoside 5'-triphosphate = DNA(n+1) +
CC         diphosphate; Xref=Rhea:RHEA:22508, Rhea:RHEA-COMP:17339, Rhea:RHEA-
CC         COMP:17340, ChEBI:CHEBI:33019, ChEBI:CHEBI:61560, ChEBI:CHEBI:173112;
CC         EC=2.7.7.7;
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the DNA clamp loading complex consisting of
CC       tau(3):delta(1):delta'(1) (PubMed:23525462). The DNA polymerase III
CC       holoenzyme complex contains at least 10 different subunits organized
CC       into 3 functionally essential subassemblies: the Pol III core, the beta
CC       sliding clamp processivity factor and the clamp-loading complex. The
CC       Pol III core (subunits alpha, epsilon and theta) contains the
CC       polymerase and the 3'-5' exonuclease proofreading activities. The
CC       polymerase is tethered to the template via the dimeric beta sliding
CC       clamp processivity factor. The DNA clamp-loading complex assembles the
CC       beta sliding clamp onto the primed template and plays a central role in
CC       the organization and communication at the replication fork (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P28630,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23525462}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm, nucleoid
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21170359}. Note=Localizes in tight foci to the
CC       chromosomal replication center at mid-cell; positioning does not rely
CC       on the C-terminus of SSB (ssbA) (PubMed:21170359).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21170359}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Essential, it cannot be disrupted
CC       (PubMed:12060778). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12060778}.
