CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the transmission of sensory signals from the
CC       chemoreceptors to the flagellar motors. CheV and CheW are involved in
CC       the coupling of the methyl-accepting chemoreceptors to the central two-
CC       component kinase CheA; they are both necessary for efficient
CC       chemotaxis. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1601874, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8169224}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Chemotaxis is substantially impaired, with a 30-
CC       fold reduction in the efficiency of chemotaxis with aspartate and 11-
CC       fold reduction with 2-deoxyglucose. Inactivation of cheW appears to
CC       have a subtle effect on chemotactic behavior in the absence of
CC       chemoeffectors: the bacteria display a smooth swimming bias, with some
CC       tumbling. A double mutant lacking both CheV and CheW has a strong
CC       tumble bias, does not respond to addition of attractant, shows
CC       essentially no accumulation in capillary assays, and has greatly
CC       reduced methyl turnover on the methyl-accepting chemotaxis proteins
CC       (MCPs). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1601874, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8169224}.
