## 2026-11-06 Bacillus chemotaxis review

Identity: P23453; BACSU strain 168, taxon 224308. The primary gene name, rather than historical aliases, selected this record. FliM is a structural and regulatory component of the Bacillus subtilis flagellar switch at the basal-body C-ring. It binds phosphorylated CheY and helps convert the chemotactic signal into a change in motor rotation; loss of its N-terminal CheY-P binding region gives the clockwise phenotype of a cheY mutant. Its contribution to switch structure and flagellar assembly is distinct from the CheY-P phosphatase activity of the neighboring switch protein FliY and from torque generation by the motor.
