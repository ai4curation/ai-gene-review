# BACSU aspS: source-based annotation review

AspS is a non-discriminating class II aspartyl-tRNA synthetase in Bacillus subtilis. It attaches L-aspartate to tRNA(Asp) for translation and also to tRNA(Asn), providing the substrate for GatCAB-dependent formation of Asn-tRNA(Asn). This dual tRNA recognition persists despite the organism also possessing AsnS and free-asparagine biosynthetic enzymes.
