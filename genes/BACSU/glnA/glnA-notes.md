# Bacillus subtilis 168 GlnA review

## Biological synthesis

GlnA P12425 is a GSI-alpha glutamine synthetase (BSU17460), distinct from the GSI-beta regulatory model usually described for enteric bacteria. ATP-dependent ammonium ligation, magnesium contacts, glutamate binding and product inhibition are directly established by native biochemical/structural studies. PMID:24158439 resolves the glutamate/AMPPCP, glutamine and transition-state complexes; magnesium occupancy depends on catalytic state. Product glutamine stabilizes the inhibited conformation rather than supporting a claim that all GlnA states are inactive. PMID:8093698 reports native glnA33 substrate-kinetic changes, not merely growth necessity.

The native feedback enzyme is also a direct nitrogen regulator. PMID:11719184 shows that feedback-inhibited GS blocks TnrA DNA binding; native mutants and full PMID:25691471 reinforce that direction. PMID:21435182 additionally finds weaker binding by non-feedback-inhibited GS and ATP-sensitive interactions. PMID:23535029 demonstrates the reciprocal TnrA-to-GS inhibition of catalysis. That reciprocal result must not become an enzyme-inhibitor annotation on GlnA.

GlnR is different: full PMID:18195355 demonstrates transient GS–GlnR binding that stabilizes GlnR–DNA complexes. PMID:18331450 identifies the GlnR autoinhibitory tail. Full PMID:35778410 extends the structural mechanism: GS juxtaposes GlnR monomers into a DNA-binding-competent dimer. Activating a transcriptional repressor enhances repression, so GO:0140537 transcription regulator activator activity is the precise molecular function. The four bare protein-binding rows are MODIFY, with the two GlnR rows specifying activator and the two TnrA rows inhibitor. GO:0140297 specific transcription-factor binding remains ACCEPT; the specific binding assertion is useful. No general folding-chaperone term is invented from the word “chaperone” in the2008 title.

The enzyme is not invariably a dodecamer: full2022 cryo-EM and negative-stain data show apo BsGS dodecamers and regulator-associated tetradecamers. Other Gram-positive species differ; do not export this transition to all GS enzymes or confuse MerR-like GlnR with the actinomycete OmpR/PhoB-like namesake. The native source supports the regulatory core directly rather than through a phenotype-derived inference.

## Annotation and family decisions

All24 original rows remain. Magnesium, ATP, glutamate and glutamine binding are integral catalytic/regulatory properties. The broad catalytic row refines to existing GO:0004356, and broad negative transcription regulation refines to existing GO:0090295. GO:0043562 nitrogen response is not an ancestor of that repression term and represents direct signaling. No redundant GS–GOGAT NEW process row is added beside the glutamine biosynthetic process.

PTHR43785:SF12 is officially TYPE-1 GLUTAMINE SYNTHETASE 2, despite the broader family’s GAMMA-GLUTAMYLPUTRESCINE SYNTHETASE label. The independent TIGR00653/GS-domain evidence and native chemistry establish the function. The PTN000464978 biosynthesis IBA is biologically coherent; P12425 self-inclusion in WITH/FROM is legitimate descendant experimental grounding, not circular evidence. The cytoplasm annotation is retained from curated ECO:0000269 localization tied to17001076; its partner-focused abstract does not justify alleging misattribution. This does not assert exclusive cytoplasmic distribution or exclude peripheral membrane association.
