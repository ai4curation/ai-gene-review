# Bacteriophage P2 Gene ORF80 (Uncharacterized 8.3 kDa protein in GpA 5' region; UniProt Q06422)

## Overview
**ORF80** (`orf-80`, `ORF1`) of temperate myovirus *Escherichia phage P2* (`BPP2`) encodes a 74-amino-acid early protein (8.3 kDa; `UniProt:Q06422`, `InterPro:IPR020126` / `PF10809` `DUF2732`) located in the early operon upstream of replication endonuclease gene `A` (`PMID:8510152`, `file:BPP2/ORF80/ORF80-uniprot.txt`). In `PMID:8510152`, `orf-80` was cloned and its polypeptide product was experimentally identified; overexpression of the `orf-80` gene product was shown to be lethal to the *Escherichia coli* host cell (`PMID:8510152`). Its precise molecular target in the host cell remains uncharacterized.

## Key Literature & Experimental Evidence
- **PMID:8510152** (*Studies of bacteriophage P2 DNA replication. The DNA sequence of the cis-acting gene A and ori region and construction of a P2 mini-chromosome.*):
  - `"Five previously unknown open reading frames (orf-80, orf-81, orf-82, orf-83 and orf-91) were discovered. They have been cloned and their respective products were identified. The products of orf-80, orf-82 and gene A were found to be lethal to the host when overexpressed."`
