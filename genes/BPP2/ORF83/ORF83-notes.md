# ORF83 (`Q06425` / `YO83_BPP2` / `orf-83` / `ORF4`) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene / ORF**: `ORF83` (`orf-83`, `ORF4`, Uncharacterized 10.2 kDa protein in gpA 5' region)
- **UniProt ID**: `Q06425` (`YO83_BPP2`, 91 aa, 10.2 kDa)
- **Organism**: Helper Myovirus Escherichia phage P2 (`NCBITaxon:2905681`)
- **Genomic Context**: Encoded in the early right-arm region immediately upstream (5') of the cis-acting replication endonuclease gene `A`, downstream of `orf-82` [PMID:8510152].

## Literature & Functional Findings
1. **Identification and Conservation in P2/186 Phages (`orf-83`)**:
   - Sequencing and cloning of the right end of the P2 genome identified `orf-83` among five previously unknown open reading frames (`orf-80`, `orf-81`, `orf-82`, `orf-83`, and `orf-91`) and verified expression of its protein product [PMID:8510152].
   - Unlike `orf-80`, `orf-82`, and `gpA`, overexpression of `orf-83` was not reported to be lethal to the *Escherichia coli* host [PMID:8510152].
   - The predicted amino acid sequence of `orf-83` is homologous to the early protein CP83 of the related coliphage 186, defining the conserved phage P2/186 family of Orf83/CP83 proteins [PMID:8510152; `file:BPP2/ORF83/ORF83-uniprot.txt`].
