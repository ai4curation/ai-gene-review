# ORF91 (`Q06426` / `YO91_BPP2` / `orf-91` / `ORF5`) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene / ORF**: `ORF91` (`orf-91`, `ORF5`, Uncharacterized 12.9 kDa protein in gpA 3' region)
- **UniProt ID**: `Q06426` (`YO91_BPP2`, 109 aa, 12.9 kDa)
- **Organism**: Helper Myovirus Escherichia phage P2 (`NCBITaxon:2905681`)
- **Genomic Context**: Encoded at the extreme right end of the P2 genome in the 3' region downstream of the cis-acting replication endonuclease gene `A` [PMID:8510152].

## Literature & Functional Findings
1. **Identification and Similarity to Retron Ec67 `orfd` (`orf-91`)**:
   - Sequencing and cloning of the right end of the P2 genome identified `orf-91` among five previously unknown open reading frames (`orf-80`, `orf-81`, `orf-82`, `orf-83`, and `orf-91`) and verified expression of its protein product [PMID:8510152].
   - Unlike `orf-80`, `orf-82`, and `gpA`, overexpression of `orf-91` was not reported to be lethal to the *Escherichia coli* host [PMID:8510152].
   - The predicted amino acid sequence of the `orf-91` product resembles the hypothetical protein encoded by `orfd` of *E. coli* retron Ec67 [PMID:8510152], reflecting the historical evolutionary insertion/association of retron elements with P2-family prophages.
