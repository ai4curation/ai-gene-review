# Bacteriophage P2 Gene ORF81 (Uncharacterized 11.3 kDa protein in GpA 5' region; UniProt Q06423)

## Overview
**ORF81** (`orf-81`, `ORF2`) of temperate myovirus *Escherichia phage P2* (`BPP2`) encodes a 100-amino-acid early protein (11.3 kDa; `UniProt:Q06423`, `InterPro:IPR035404` / `PF17399` `DUF5405`) located between `orf-80` and `orf-82` in the early operon upstream of replication endonuclease gene `A` (`PMID:8510152`, `file:BPP2/ORF81/ORF81-uniprot.txt`). In `PMID:8510152`, `orf-81` was discovered, cloned, and its protein product was experimentally identified, though unlike `orf-80` and `orf-82` its overexpression was not lethal to the *Escherichia coli* host and its molecular function remains unknown (`PMID:8510152`).

## Key Literature & Experimental Evidence
- **PMID:8510152** (*Studies of bacteriophage P2 DNA replication. The DNA sequence of the cis-acting gene A and ori region and construction of a P2 mini-chromosome.*):
  - `"Five previously unknown open reading frames (orf-80, orf-81, orf-82, orf-83 and orf-91) were discovered. They have been cloned and their respective products were identified. The products of orf-80, orf-82 and gene A were found to be lethal to the host when overexpressed."`
