Gene Description

BT_3328 (BtCDH) is an 868-residue cell-surface glycosaminoglycan lyase of Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron and the founding member of polysaccharide lyase family PL29. It is a lipoprotein, with a lipobox ending at Cys19 followed by an acidic surface-exposure signal. Its only Pfam domains are DUF4988 (residues 25-199) and DUF4955 (716-867), neither of which is linked to a known function. The recombinant enzyme cleaves chondroitin sulfate (CS-A > CS-C) and hyaluronan by beta-elimination, releasing Delta4,5-unsaturated oligosaccharides, with a pH optimum of 7 and little dependence on divalent metals. Its activity on dermatan sulfate is contested, reported as degradation in one study and as little activity in another. The enzyme is encoded in the BT3328-BT3330 operon of the CS/DS/HA polysaccharide utilization locus, sits on the cell surface with the surface glycan-binding proteins BT3329 and BT3330, and starts extracellular depolymerisation of host glycosaminoglycans before uptake through the SusC/D-like transporter BT3331/BT3332. Pooled transposon fitness profiling finds it important for fitness on hyaluronan but not on chondroitin sulfate, whereas clean deletion strains grow normally on both in pure culture; how the two observations fit together is unresolved. The gene is present in every hyaluronan-degrading strain of one 17-strain Bacteroides panel and absent from the non-degraders, although a second panel scored a strain that has lost the gene among the best hyaluronan utilisers.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

chondroitin sulfate catabolic process

Definition: The chemical reactions and pathways resulting in the breakdown of chondroitin sulfate, a sulfated glycosaminoglycan composed of repeating beta-(1,4)-D-glucuronic acid-beta-(1,3)-N-acetyl-D-galactosamine disaccharide units, occurring on the free polysaccharide chain rather than on an intact chondroitin sulfate proteoglycan.

Justification: GO has GO:0030214 hyaluronan catabolic process for the unsulfated glycosaminoglycan, but for chondroitin sulfate only GO:0030207 chondroitin sulfate proteoglycan catabolic process, which requires a core protein. Gut Bacteroides degrade free dietary chondroitin sulfate chains, so neither GO:0030207 nor the parent GO:0006027 glycosaminoglycan catabolic process fits: the first asserts a proteoglycan substrate that is absent, and the second cannot distinguish chondroitin sulfate from the other glycosaminoglycans this locus also handles. The gap affects every enzyme of the BT3324-BT3350 chondroitin sulfate utilization locus, not only this one.

Core Functions

Outer-membrane-anchored, surface-exposed PL29 lyase that starts extracellular depolymerisation of hyaluronan and chondroitin sulfate by beta-elimination, working with the surface glycan-binding proteins BT3329 and BT3330 ahead of SusC/D-mediated uptake. A clean deletion grows normally on these substrates in pure culture, so this surface step is contributory rather than essential under those conditions.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does BtCDH cleave dermatan sulfate? The 2018 and 2020 studies from the same group disagree, and the answer decides between chondroitin AC and chondroitin ABC lyase terms.

Q: Why is bt3328 important for fitness on hyaluronan in pooled RB-TnSeq (PMID:33657378) when the clean deletion strain grows normally on hyaluronan in pure culture (PMID:30262663, PMID:32005816)? Surface cleavage may matter only under competition for the released oligosaccharides.

Q: Is BtCDH endo-acting, as the 2018 product profile suggests, or does it act from the reducing end, as the 2020 oligosaccharide data indicate?

Q: Should GO have a chondroitin sulfate catabolic process term for free (non-proteoglycan) CS chains, as degraded by gut Bacteroides?

Q: Which of DUF4988 and DUF4955 carries the lyase catalytic site? Pfam entries PF16315 and PF16378 could be renamed to reflect PL29 if the active site maps to one of them.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Assay purified BtCDH side by side on DS, CS-A, CS-C and HA under identical conditions, with product analysis by HPLC and NMR, to settle the substrate range and cleavage mode.

Type: in vitro enzyme kinetics

Experiment: Solve the structure of BtCDH, or use an AlphaFold model with site-directed mutagenesis, to locate the PL29 catalytic residues relative to the DUF4988 and DUF4955 domains.

BT_3328 (Q8A2H7, BtCDH, PL29) notes

2026-10-30 origin

Reviewed from the KBase Dark Protein Atlas (gene-annotate dashboard), query
CAKXSP010000006.1_105, an environmental B. thetaiotaomicron protein that
eggNOG and InterPro annotate only as DUF4955. It is 98.8% identical to BT_3328
over its full 868 aa (see BT_3328-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md). The
dashboard's LLM call was "PL29 glycosaminoglycan endolyase cleaving hyaluronic
acid, chondroitin sulfate, and dermatan sulfate by beta-elimination, with a
physiological role in hyaluronic acid utilization", with GO:0030340,
GO:0034000 and GO:0030214. UniProt Q8A2H7 is unreviewed ("DUF4955
domain-containing protein") and GOA has no annotations at all.

Literature

  • Ndeh et al. 2018 PMID:30262663: founding member of PL29. Recombinant
    BtCDH degrades CS, DS and HA; on HA it gives Delta4,5-unsaturated di- to
    octasaccharides and is classed as endo-acting. Proteinase K shaving and
    immunofluorescence show it on the cell surface; it has an N-terminal
    lipidation sequence. Only slightly affected by EDTA and divalent metals.
    The deletion mutant still degrades CS outside the cell, so other surface CS
    lyases exist.
  • Ndeh et al. 2020 PMID:32005816: in the CS/DS/HA PUL, BT3328 is the
    surface lyase; activity and affinity CS-A > CS-C > HA "and little activity
    on DS, differing from previous data". It cleaves HA oligosaccharides only
    at DP >= 8 and "cleaves glycans from the reducing end". Co-IP shows it
    interacting with the SGBPs BT3329 and BT3330 at the cell surface.
  • Liu et al. 2021 PMID:33657378: RB-TnSeq; BT3328 "was only important for
    fitness on HA, despite the activity of this enzyme against both HA and CS
    in vitro".
  • Overbeeke et al. 2022 PMID:36523841: all tested HA-degrading
    Bacteroides strains carry BT_3328 and all non-degraders lack it.

Decisions

  • MF. The DS activity is contested between the two Ndeh papers, so I did
    not use GO:0034000 chondroitin-sulfate-ABC endolyase activity, whose
    definition explicitly covers iduronate (DS) linkages. GO:0030341
    chondroitin AC lyase activity (hexosaminyl-glucuronate linkages: CS-A, CS-C
    and HA) matches the substrate set both papers agree on. GO:0030340
    hyaluronate lyase activity is added as well, because HA is the
    physiologically relevant substrate. Neither EC-style definition captures the
    endo-acting mode exactly; that is a limitation of the terms.
  • BP. GO:0030214 hyaluronan catabolic process, supported by the enzyme's own
    catalysis of HA at the cell surface (IDA), not by a growth requirement. The
    pooled RB-TnSeq HA signal PMID:33657378 conflicts with the clean deletion
    strain, which grows normally on HA in pure culture [PMID:30262663, and
    PMID:32005816 "no impact on growth was observed, consistent with Δbt3328
    mutant growth data"]; PMID:32005816 assigns the required HA lyase role to
    BT4410. Recorded as an open question; it does not affect participation.
    GO has no "chondroitin sulfate catabolic process"
    (only the proteoglycan term GO:0030207, which is wrong for dietary free CS
    chains), and GO:0006027 glycosaminoglycan catabolic process is an ancestor of
    the HA term, so I did not add it. Raised as a question.
  • CC. GO:0009986 cell surface by IDA. GO:0009279 cell outer membrane by IC:
    a lipoprotein with a surface-exposure signal that proteinase K can reach
    from outside, which in Bacteroidetes means anchored in the outer leaflet of
    the outer membrane.

2026-10-30 correction

An earlier draft of this review said the gene is "required for growth on
hyaluronan" and annotated GO:0030214 by IMP from the RB-TnSeq fitness data. That
overstated the genetics: both Ndeh papers report that the clean ΔBtCDH strain
grows like wild type on HA, CS-A, CS-C and DS in pure culture. The term is kept,
now by IDA on the enzyme's own catalysis, and the discrepancy with the pooled
fitness screen is recorded as an open question.

Check that the KBase Dark Protein Atlas query CAKXSP010000006.1_105 (an
environmental Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron assembly, annotated by eggNOG and

Method

align.py makes a global BLOSUM62 alignment (Biopython PairwiseAligner, gap
open -10, extend -0.5) of the UniProt sequence in ../BT_3328-uniprot.txt
against the query sequence CAKXSP010000006.1_105.fasta, taken from the
dashboard's review page. Output is in align_output.txt.

uv run python align.py ../BT_3328-uniprot.txt CAKXSP010000006.1_105.fasta

Results

  • Both sequences are 868 aa. The alignment has no gaps; 858 of 868 positions
    are identical (98.8% identity). The 10 differences are conservative or
    surface substitutions scattered along the chain:
    T170A L233F P321L I397V A541V S567A D675N A705T G779S V823M.
  • The N-terminus MKKVISIICITLLVALYSCDERDDL carries a lipoprotein signal peptide
    with lipobox LYSC and Cys19 as the predicted lipidated residue, as in the
    UniProt LIPID feature at position 19 (PROSITE PS51257). The residues after
    Cys19 are acidic (DERDD), the pattern of the Bacteroides lipoprotein
    export signal that sends lipoproteins to the cell surface.
  • InterProScan on the query (KBase run) finds Pfam PF16378 (DUF4988) at
    25-199 and PF16315 (DUF4955) at 716-867 (E = 2.8e-69). These are the only
    domain annotations; neither Pfam entry is linked to a function.

Conclusion

The query is an allele of BT_3328 from another B. thetaiotaomicron strain,
so the experimental characterisation of BT_3328 applies directly to it.
DeepTMHMM in the KBase run classified the protein as soluble and secreted
(signal peptide 1-18). That conflicts with the lipobox and with the measured
cell-surface location (PMID:30262663), so it is probably a mis-call of a
lipoprotein signal peptide.
