| Identity | UniProt P21683 corresponds to **crtB**, encoding 15-cis-phytoene synthase from *Pantoea ananatis* (formerly *Erwinia uredovora*). The original functional study identified ORF-E as **crtB**; its assignment agrees with the phytoene/squalene-synthase family and domain annotations (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000004) | Direct exact-source | The cited primary paper predates the UniProt accession and taxonomic renaming, so accession mapping also relies on the supplied UniProt record. |
| Reaction, substrate, and product | CrtB catalyzes the head-to-head condensation of **two C20 geranylgeranyl diphosphate (GGPP) molecules**—through prephytoene diphosphate—to produce the C40 carotenoid **15,15′-cis-phytoene**. Expression of exact-source **crtE + crtB** in *E. coli* caused phytoene accumulation (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005) | Direct exact-source | No rigorous substrate-panel study was found; GGPP specificity is strongly established, but activity on alternative prenyl diphosphates is unresolved. |
| Cofactors and inhibitors | Exact-source biochemical literature reports a requirement for **ATP** and either **Mn²⁺ or Mg²⁺**, with inhibition by **phosphate** and **squalestatin** (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000008) | Exact-source literature synthesis | These properties were recovered through a 2023 authoritative review; no dependable numerical kinetic constants were available in the retrieved evidence. |
| Pathway and operon | **crtB** performs the first committed C40-carotenoid step downstream of GGPP-producing CrtE and upstream of CrtI-mediated desaturation, CrtY-mediated cyclization, and CrtZ-mediated hydroxylation. In *P. ananatis* PA13, it lies in the plasmid-borne, polycistronic **crtEXYIB** cluster; **crtZ** is independently and oppositely transcribed (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000007) | Direct exact-source | Direct operon evidence is from strain PA13, whereas P21683 derives from the historically designated *E. uredovora* source; cluster conservation supports, but does not alone prove, identical regulation in every strain. |
| Cellular localization | CrtB is most cautiously annotated as an **intracellular enzyme**, probably acting in the cytosol or at the cytoplasmic face of membranes where GGPP is available; hydrophobic phytoene subsequently partitions into lipid environments. | Inferred | No direct localization, fractionation, imaging, signal-peptide, or membrane-association experiment for P21683 was found; a precise membrane-versus-cytosol assignment is therefore not established. |
| Native biological role | By initiating carotenoid production, CrtB contributes to the pathway whose products protect *P. ananatis* from environmental oxidative challenges, including toxoflavin and UV stress. Carotenoid production is controlled through Hfq/ArcZ, quorum sensing, RpoS, and ClpP-dependent regulation (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000013) | Pathway-level | Stress protection was demonstrated for the carotenoid pathway as a whole, not isolated as a **crtB-specific** phenotype; downstream pigments may account for much of the protection. |
