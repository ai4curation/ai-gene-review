Gene Description

DsbB is an inner-membrane thiol-disulfide oxidoreductase of Campylobacter jejuni 81116 that recycles the periplasmic DsbA1 and DsbA2 proteins during oxidative folding. Loss of DsbB shifts DsbA1 toward its reduced form and greatly lowers detectable DsbA2, while a partially purified recombinant preparation reoxidizes both proteins. This redox pathway supports activity of exported clients including AstA and PhoX. The physiological electron acceptor and detailed kinetics of the DsbB reaction remain unresolved.

Core Functions

Reoxidizes the periplasmic DsbA1 and DsbA2 catalysts in the 81116 oxidative-folding system. Native redox-state evidence and tagged, partially purified-protein assays support this role; the native quinone and isolated coupled reaction kinetics remain undetermined.

DsbB manual review: archived exact 81116 record

The canonical normal fetch returned an empty current UniProt TXT response and zero current GOA rows, but seeded A8FLS4/dsbB/CAMJ8/taxon407148 through the authentic mnemonic fallback. All three files were frozen before review. The canonical raw files remain untouched; the versioned UniSave record is stored separately and explicitly labelled historical. See identity and assay scope and its adjacent official archive/provenance. Historical GO cross-references are observations, not manufactured machine rows. No PANTHER ID is assigned.

PMID:34948248 states “The in vitro test indicated that CjDsbB oxidized both CjDsbA1 and CjDsbA2.” It also reports native DsbA1 reduction and almost undetectable DsbA2 when DsbB is absent. The recombinant protein derives from native 81116 CP000814, with a C-terminal polyhistidine tag. Native gene identity is exact; the complete tagged construct junction is not independently reconstructed. The biochemical preparation was partial, AMS readings were endpoint measurements and a no-DsbB reaction was deliberately omitted. Native genetic/redox evidence, this assay and domain classification together support IC protein-disulfide MF, not a clean isolated-enzyme IDA claim. GO0015035 is reversible. The physiological quinone, coupled quinone-reduction rate and equal partner affinities are not established, so no GO0016672 is proposed.

PMID:25181355 supports native client function: dsbB disruption left AstA activity at 8% and PhoX at 53% of wild type. These phenotypes supplement the mechanistic partner evidence; DsbB recycles the DsbAs, rather than directly catalyzing AstA sulfate transfer or PhoX phosphate hydrolysis. The NEW folding BP is supported by that catalytic/cofactor-regeneration work, not necessity alone. Same-role QuickGO queries for P0A6M2/P21482/P57701 found eight protein-folding annotation rows; those observations are only convention checks. GO0006457 ancestry and no-BP-redundancy checks were made, and the GO-CAM index has no target entry. No motility, pathogenicity, client metabolism or generic stress BP is proposed.

PMID:32203539 reports C8J_1298 redox changes in several dsb mutants, including dsbB. This does not establish a direct DsbB-C8J_1298 reaction or isomerase activity. Its correction of the older DsbA1 motility attribution also prevents propagating that phenotype as a generic Dsb-system role.
