Gene Description

Dgeo_2852 is a short KdpD-family atypical serine kinase comprising KdpD_N and universal stress protein domains. The purified Deinococcus geothermalis protein phosphorylates the regulatory serine of heterologous Escherichia coli KdpB, with stimulation by cyclic di-AMP under the tested conditions. It lacks the histidine-kinase transmitter domains of canonical KdpD. Its native pump partners, signaling inputs and cellular localization remain incompletely characterized.
