CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional repressor that is an essential upstream
CC       regulator of neural crest determination and mesoderm induction.
CC       Functions by recruiting the transcriptional corepressor tle4. Also acts
CC       in a negative auto-regulator loop by inhibiting the transcription of
CC       its own gene. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11335115,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11493569, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17138566}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with tle4. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17138566}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00089,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17138566}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Initially located within the dorsal blastopore lip
CC       (Spemann organizer) at the mid-gastrula stage. As expression fades in
CC       the neuroectoderm, expression begins in the head mesoderm in two
CC       regions at the lateral borders of the presumptive neural plate. These
CC       regions correspond to neural crest cells originating from the
CC       mesencephalon (anterior) and the rhombencephalon (posterior;
CC       rhombomeres 2, 4 and 6). Subsequently expressed within trunk
CC       premigratory neural crest cells. Expression disappears when neural
CC       crest cells begin to differentiate and migrate, except for those which
CC       populate the hyoid arch. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11335115,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11493569, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7586752,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8735935, ECO:0000269|Ref.4}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed both maternally and zygotically.
CC       Maternal expression decreases until the late blastula/early gastrula
CC       stage when zygotic expression begins. Expression is highest in the
CC       gastrula stage, after which it shows a gradual decrease, being clearly
CC       present until the tadpole stage. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11335115,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11493569, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8735935,
CC       ECO:0000269|Ref.4}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: By dorsal wnt signaling. Inhibited by bmp signaling.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11335115}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The C-terminus is required for transcriptional repression and
CC       mesoderm induction.
