CC   -!- FUNCTION: Probable transcription factor. Promotes both hatching gland
CC       and neural crest cell fates, two of the cell populations that arise
CC       from the neural plate border. Acts downstream of msx1 to induce the
CC       neural crest, cooperating with zic1 and mediating signals from both the
CC       wnt and fgf8 signaling pathways. Induction of hatching gland cell fate
CC       is independent of zic1. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15691759,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15843410, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17409353}.
