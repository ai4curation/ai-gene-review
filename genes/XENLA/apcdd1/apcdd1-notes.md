# Curation Notes for *Xenopus laevis* `apcdd1` (`UniProtKB:Q66KI8`, `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2`)

## 1. Orthology, Sequence Architecture, and Direct Experimental Evidence
- **Gene / Accession**: *Xenopus laevis* `apcdd1` (`apcdd1.L` / `Xapcdd1`, cDNA clone `BC080377`), `UniProtKB:Q66KI8` (515 aa, sequence version 1), classified in `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2` (`PROTEIN APCDD1`) and serving as a genuine experimental PAINT seed on `PANTHER:PTN002826827`.
- **Conserved Structural Determinants**: Retains the N-terminal cleavable signal peptide (`aa 1–25`), all 12 conserved ectodomain disulfide cysteines including the interdomain disulfide pair `Cys51–Cys465` (homologous to mammalian `Cys52–Cys466` in `PMID:37155902`), all three conserved N-glycosylation sequons (`Asn99`, `Asn167`, `Asn318`), the ABD2 hydrophobic pocket, and the C-terminal single-pass transmembrane helix (`aa 494–514`).
- **Endogenous Loss-of-Function & Rescue in *Xenopus laevis* (`PMID:20393562`, `PMID:32320685`)**:
  - **Wnt Pathway Inhibition (`PMID:20393562`, `PMID:32320685`)**: Shimomura et al. (`PMID:20393562`) cloned full-length *X. laevis* `Xapcdd1` (`BC080377` = `Q66KI8`) and designed translation-blocking morpholino `AS1 MO` targeting its 5'UTR/ATG. Dorsal blastomere depletion of endogenous `Xapcdd1` caused loss of anterior and dorsal structures (ventralization from ectopic zygotic Wnt activation), rescued specifically by either MO-resistant `5' mut Xapcdd1` mRNA or dominant-negative `DNWnt8` mRNA (`PMID:20393562`). Vonica et al. (`PMID:32320685`) further showed that `X. laevis apcdd1` blocks Wnt8-induced *Siamois* reporter transcription upstream of beta-catenin and acts cell-autonomously in the signal-receiving blastomere.
  - **Dual BMP Pathway Inhibition (`PMID:32320685`)**: Vonica et al. (`PMID:32320685`) discovered that endogenous *X. laevis* `apcdd1` belongs to the BMP synexpression group during neurulation and tailbud development, inhibits BMP2/Alk3 transcriptional reporter output upstream of Smad1 without affecting Activin/Smad2 signaling, and that `AS1 MO` depletion of endogenous `apcdd1` expands ventral BMP target *sizzled* expression, suppresses neural crest (*sox9*, *slug*) and epidermal keratin (*k81a1*) domains, and elevates endogenous Smad1 C-terminal phosphorylation ~4.7-fold, rescued by MO-resistant *X. laevis* `apcdd1` mRNA! We therefore propose one conservative `NEW` annotation to `GO:0030514` (`negative regulation of BMP signaling pathway`, `IMP`, `PMID:32320685`).
