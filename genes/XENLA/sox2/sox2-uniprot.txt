CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional activator (By similarity). Functions as a
CC       switch in neuronal development, participating in the differentiation of
CC       embryonic neuroectodermal cells into neural tissues by making the
CC       ectodermal cells responsive to FGF-neuralizing signals. Downstream SRRT
CC       target that mediates the promotion of neural stem cell self-renewal (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10648237,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9435279}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       O42569; Q91813: otx2-a; NbExp=5; IntAct=EBI-15695825, EBI-15695802;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00267}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: By mid-gastrulation (stage 11), strongly expressed
CC       on the dorsal side of the embryo and by stage 13, expression marks the
CC       future neural plate including the neural crest. During neurulation,
CC       neural precursors showing expression converge to form the central
CC       nervous system and eye. At stage 23 (early tailbud), expressed in the
CC       primitive brain and optic vesicle, with expression high in the anterior
CC       neural tube. In the tail bud brain, expressed in several patches along
CC       the anterior-posterior axis in the telencephalon, midbrain-hindbrain
CC       boundary and hindbrain. Also expressed in the branchial arches during
CC       tailbud stages. In the tailbud eye, expressed in both the neural retina
CC       and the lens. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17056008, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9435279,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9598349, ECO:0000269|Ref.6}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed zygotically from gastrula stage (stage
CC       11). Expression remains constant at later stages.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17056008, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9435279,
CC       ECO:0000269|Ref.6}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: By bmp-antagonism. By chordin. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9435279}.
