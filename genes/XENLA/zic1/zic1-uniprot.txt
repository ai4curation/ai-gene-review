CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional activator that induces expression of multiple
CC       genes including pax3, en2, snai2/slug, feb and a subset of wnt genes.
CC       Has multiple key roles in the regulation of neural induction and
CC       neurogenesis: acts as a neural competence factor, sensitizing the
CC       presumptive neuroectoderm to respond to subsequent neuralizing signals.
CC       Promotes both preplacodal cell fates and neural crest cell fates, two
CC       of the cell populations that arise from the neural plate border.
CC       Cooperates with pax3 in concert with wnt signaling to determine neural
CC       crest fate. Synergizes with the bmp-inhibitor noggin/nog and acts
CC       through the wnt pathway to induce expression of en2. May bind to the
CC       minimal GLI-consensus sequence 5'-TGGGTGGTC-3'.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15843410, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16871635,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16892174, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17409353,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9435279, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9655809,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9739105}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9655809}. Cytoplasm
CC       {ECO:0000250}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: During early gastrula stages, widely expressed in
CC       the dorsal ectoderm. At mid-gastrula, expressed throughout the
CC       presumptive neural plate and at late gastrula, expression gradually
CC       diminishes in the dorsal midline and increases in the anterior folds.
CC       By early neurula stage, expression becomes restricted to the lateral
CC       edges of the neural plate, corresponding to the presumptive dorsal
CC       neural plate and neural crest, and in flanking ectoderm. In early
CC       tailbud stages (stages 22-23), expressed in the dorsal forebrain,
CC       midbrain and hindbrain. Subsequently expressed in the telencephalon and
CC       at the diencephalon/mesencephalon boundary. In the spinal cord,
CC       expression is restricted to the dorsal most region including the roof
CC       plate. Also expressed in the somites but not in eye vesicles. At larval
CC       stages, expressed mainly in the dorsal neural tube throughout its
CC       anteroposterior axis. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15843410,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16307736, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16871625,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17409353, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9435279,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9655809, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9739105}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed zygotically from the blastula stage
CC       (stage 8) and peaks in the gastrula with lower expression persisting
CC       into tailbud stages. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16871625,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9435279, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9655809,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9739105}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: By chrd/chordin, zic1, zic2, zic3 and zic5. Suppressed by
CC       bmp signaling. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11091076,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16307736, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9435279}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The C2H2-type 3, 4 and 5 zinc finger domains are necessary for
CC       transcription activation (By similarity). Removal of the C-terminal
CC       regulatory domain increases activity. {ECO:0000250}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the GLI C2H2-type zinc-finger protein family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
