# STAA1/pcrB evidence notes

## Evidence review — 2026-11-07

The primary source and structure/accession checks are documented in analysis/module_audits/bacterial_heptaprenylglyceryl_ether_lipid/comparative-members/identity-and-scope.md and immutable official mapping snapshots. All original GOA payloads are preserved. The glycerophospholipid definition includes mono-O-alkyl glycerophosphate, so direct ether synthesis supports the broad biosynthetic process without assigning a complete downstream native pathway.

Actual Falcon research was attempted with `just deep-research STAA1 pcrB --provider falcon` but returned403 authentication failure. No report was invented. Primary PubMed/official PDB searches and cached full-text or curated experimental evidence support this manual review. The PAINT-loss command reported the accession absent from the family tree; it cannot establish absence of lineage losses.

A7X435 is SAHV_1891 from Mu3;3W01/3W02 independently map to that accession. PMID23322418 full text reports SaPcrB donor-length mutagenesis [“we produced A100W and Y104A mutants of SaPcrB”]. PEG/sulfate occupy the Sa structures; natural substrate complexes described in the same article are Bacillus structures. These ligands define the tunnel, not a native C35 product structure. D14A mutants of both SaPcrB and BsPcrB have no detectable activity; Mg omission/EDTA also eliminate detectable product. Strong catalytic evidence supports C35 PcrB while cellular product distribution and downstream carrier roles remain unresolved.
