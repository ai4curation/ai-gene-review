| **Definitive endoderm induction and germ-layer boundary formation** | MAPK first specifies mesoderm, while β-catenin helps establish complementary receptor–ligand territories. Membrane-bound Delta on mesodermal cells activates Notch in adjacent ectoderm; γ-secretase-dependent cleavage releases NICD, which enters nuclei at the interface and induces a definitive endoderm program. The response activates *foxA*, *brachyury*, *wnt1*, and *wnt3* without inducing the mesodermal marker *snailA*, supporting a selective lateral-induction mechanism. (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000011, pqac-00000045) | **Delta** is the best-supported functional ligand. Delta-like is also abundant in mesoderm and is a plausible contributor; Jagged1-like appears later and weakly, while Jagged1B-like is expressed at very low levels. Direct ligand–receptor binding has not been biochemically assigned for every candidate. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000040) | Mesodermal markers appear at approximately **6–8 h post-fertilization (hpf)**; endodermal markers follow at approximately **10–12 hpf**. Notch is initially ubiquitous/maternally deposited but becomes excluded from mesoderm around 10 hpf. Delta is mesodermal, peaks around 14–16 hpf, and faces Notch-positive ectoderm; nuclear NICD forms a ring at the blastopore/endoderm–mesoderm boundary. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000040) | Notch inhibition with LY-411575 suppresses endodermal markers and endoderm formation while largely sparing mesodermal markers and gastrulation. Conversely, ectopic NICD is sufficient to induce *foxA* and *brachyury* in ectodermal cells but not *snailA*. These loss- and gain-of-function results show that Notch is necessary and sufficient for endoderm induction. (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000011, pqac-00000046) |
| **Restriction of neurogenesis and control of neural progenitor number** | Notch-mediated lateral inhibition limits generation or expansion of NvSoxB(2)-positive neural progenitor cells and restrains neuronal and nematocyte differentiation. It interacts with NvSoxB(2), NvAth-like, and NvAshA regulatory programs. NvHes2/3 decline after Notch knockdown, but NICD/NvDelta gain-of-function and Hes overexpression experiments do not establish a simple canonical Hes-dependent cascade; the precise downstream route therefore remains unresolved and may be substantially non-canonical. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000036) | Delta is the principal candidate in the proposed lateral-inhibition circuit; Delta expression also shows an aboral salt-and-pepper pattern compatible with signaling among neural precursors. A specific role for Jagged in this process has not been demonstrated. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000040) | Regulation begins during embryonic development: NvSoxB(2) and NvAth-like occur in dividing progenitors from blastula stages, whereas NvAshA appears later in non-dividing differentiating cells. Nuclear NICD is detected in small patches in the aboral half of early gastrulae, consistent with neural-precursor specification. (pqac-00000019, pqac-00000021) | DAPT-mediated γ-secretase inhibition and NvNotch knockdown increase neural-marker expression, neurons, nematocytes, and NvSoxB(2)-positive cells; Notch hyperactivation suppresses neural differentiation. NvNotch knockdown reduces NvHes2/3 expression approximately **three- to fourfold**, but other perturbations leave the direct transcriptional mechanism uncertain. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000036) |
| **Regeneration, germ-layer reorganization, and axial self-organization** | Notch acts with Wnt/β-catenin in a feedback system that restores endoderm–mesoderm boundaries, endodermal identity, cell sorting, and oral–aboral polarity after tissue disruption. Dissociation transiently interrupts Notch signaling and allows endodermal cells to acquire an endomesodermal state marked by mesodermal *cadherin1*; subsequent Notch reactivation resolves this state. Notch-dependent clustering and boundary refinement enable one mesodermal cluster with attached endoderm to immigrate and form the future pharynx/inner layer. (pqac-00000030, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000032) | The ligand responsible in gastruloids has not been conclusively assigned. Delta–Notch signaling is the leading model because Delta is associated with mesoderm and Notch with adjacent endoderm/ectoderm during normal development; roles for Delta-like or Jagged paralogues remain possible but untested. (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000031) | Operates during regeneration and in reaggregated early-gastrula-cell “gastruloids.” Mesodermal and endodermal cells first sort to the aggregate surface; mesoderm forms clusters, and one cluster later internalizes at a pole to establish the organizer, inner layer, and oral–aboral axis. In intact animals, Notch is required after or separately from initial wound healing. (pqac-00000031, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000034) | Continuous Notch inhibition prevents cell reclustering and axial polarization in gastruloids. Earlier pharmacological studies showed that Notch inhibition blocks whole-body/head regeneration without blocking wound closure and disrupts pharyngeal growth, tentacles, mesenteries, endodermal morphology, and cnidocyte differentiation. (pqac-00000030, pqac-00000033, pqac-00000034) |
