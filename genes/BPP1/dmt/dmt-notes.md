# dmt (Q71TL0) Curation Notes — Escherichia phage P1

## Functional Summary & Resolution of UniProt `dmt` vs `mod` (`M.EcoP1I`) Conflation
- **Gene symbol**: `dmt` (DNA methyltransferase; 754 aa, ~84.8 kDa, `EC:2.1.1.72`).
- **True biological role of P1 `dmt` (`Q71TL0`)**:
  - As established by Sternberg & Coulby 1990 [PMID:2236019] and Łobocka et al. 2004 [PMID:15489417], P1 `dmt` encodes a C1-repressed and Lpa-activated lytic DNA methyltransferase whose C-terminal region (`aa 475–754`) is homologous to *Escherichia coli* Dam (`5'-GATC-3'` $N^6$-adenine methyltransferase, InterPro `IPR012327`) and whose N-terminal region (`aa 1–474`) resembles C5-cytosine methyltransferases (though lacking the conserved proline in the catalytic `PC` dipeptide motif).
  - During the P1 lytic cycle, `Dmt` methylates adenine residues in `5'-GATC-3'` sequences, specifically within the seven `5'-GATC-3'` hexamer repeats of the P1 packaging initiation site (`pac`). Because the P1 terminase (`PacAB`) cannot cleave unmethylated or hemimethylated `pac` DNA, expression of `dmt` is strictly required for `pac` site cleavage, lytic genome packaging (`GO:0019073` `viral DNA genome packaging`), and phage production in `dam-` *E. coli* hosts [PMID:2236019, PMID:15489417].
- **UniProt `Q71TL0` conflation with `M.EcoP1I` (`mod`, `P08763`)**:
  - In UniProt `Q71TL0` (`DMT_BPP1`), the sequence (`754 aa`, `AAQ19800.1` from `AF234172`) is genuinely P1 `dmt`, yet the UniProt curation text (`DE AltName: Full=M.EcoP1I`, `CC FUNCTION`, `CC CATALYTIC ACTIVITY`, `CC SUBUNIT`) and two experimental GOA annotations (`PMID:14715260` Sistla et al. 2004 and `PMID:21048863` Bheemanaik et al. 2010) actually describe **`M.EcoP1I`**, which is encoded by P1 **`mod` (`P08763`, 646 aa)** — the modification subunit of the EcoP1I Type III restriction-modification system that methylates the internal adenine of `5'-AGACC-3'`.
  - Both `dmt` (`Q71TL0`, `5'-GATC-3'`) and `mod` (`P08763`, `5'-AGACC-3'`) are genuine `site-specific DNA-methyltransferase (adenine-specific) activity` (`GO:0009007`, `EC:2.1.1.72`) enzymes that catalyze `DNA methylation` (`GO:0006306`). However, `dmt` (`Q71TL0`) is an **orphan lytic replicative/packaging Dam-like methyltransferase**, NOT part of a restriction-modification system (`GO:0009307`).

## Review of Existing GOA Annotations
- `GO:0003676` (`nucleic acid binding`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000119`): **MODIFY** -> `GO:0003677` (`DNA binding`). Dmt specifically binds DNA substrates containing `5'-GATC-3'` recognition sites [PMID:2236019, PMID:15489417].
- `GO:0003824` (`catalytic activity`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000119`): **TOO_GENERAL**. Entailed by `GO:0009007` (`site-specific DNA-methyltransferase (adenine-specific) activity`).
- `GO:0006304` (`DNA modification`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000002`): **TOO_GENERAL**. Entailed by `GO:0006306` (`DNA methylation`).
- `GO:0006306` (`DNA methylation`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000043` / `GO_REF:0000117`): **ACCEPT** (2 rows). Dmt methylates adenine residues in `5'-GATC-3'` sequences on concatemeric P1 DNA during lytic replication [PMID:2236019, PMID:15489417].
- `GO:0008168` (`methyltransferase activity`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL**. Entailed by `GO:0009007`.
- `GO:0008170` (`N-methyltransferase activity`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000117`): **TOO_GENERAL**. Entailed by `GO:0009007`.
- `GO:0009007` (`site-specific DNA-methyltransferase (adenine-specific) activity`, IDA `PMID:14715260`, IDA `PMID:21048863`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120`): **ACCEPT** (3 rows). P1 Dmt genuinely catalyzes site-specific $N^6$-adenine DNA methylation at `5'-GATC-3'` sites via its C-terminal Dam-homologous domain [PMID:2236019, PMID:15489417]. Note that the two IDA papers (`PMID:14715260`, `PMID:21048863`) biochemically assayed P1 `M.EcoP1I` (`mod`, `P08763`) rather than `dmt` (`Q71TL0`) due to UniProt entry conflation, but the GO term `GO:0009007` is accurate for `dmt` itself and supported by [PMID:2236019, PMID:15489417].
- `GO:0009307` (`DNA restriction-modification system`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000117`): **REMOVE**. P1 `dmt` (`Q71TL0`) is an orphan lytic Dam-like `5'-GATC-3'` methyltransferase required to license `pac` site cleavage for viral DNA packaging (`GO:0019073`), not a component of a restriction-modification system (this annotation resulted from conflation of `dmt` with the Type III RM methyltransferase `mod`/`M.EcoP1I`, `P08763`) [PMID:2236019, PMID:15489417].
- `GO:0016740` (`transferase activity`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL**. Entailed by `GO:0009007`.
- `GO:0032259` (`methylation`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL**. Entailed by `GO:0006306` (`DNA methylation`).
