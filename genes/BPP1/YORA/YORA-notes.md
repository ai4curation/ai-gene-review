# Curation Notes: YORA (Q06262) — Bacteriophage P1

## Identity & Genomic Context
- **Gene symbol**: `YORA` (UniProt AltName: `ORFA`; corresponds to the `pdcB` region immediately downstream of `doc` in the `phd-doc` operon region)
- **UniProtKB**: `Q06262` (`YORA_BPP1`, 312 aa, 36.0 kDa, PE=4 Predicted)
- **Organism**: Punavirus P1 (`NCBITaxon:2886926`, *Escherichia phage P1*)
- **Locus**: Uncharacterized open reading frame in the `doc`-`lpa` (`Gp10`) intergenic region, sequenced by Lehnherr et al. (1993) [PMID:8411153] and annotated by Lobocka et al. (2004) [PMID:15489417].

## Function & Literature Summary
- In the complete P1 genome annotation (Lobocka et al., 2004, `PMID:15489417`), two open reading frames (`pdcA` and `pdcB`) immediately downstream of `phd` and `doc` were noted to appear to be co-transcribed with `phd` and `doc`, and their function remains unknown (`"The nucleotide sequence downstream of doc contains two ORFs that could encode proteins of 66 and 347 residues, designated here pdcA and pdcB (post-doc). The 5′ end of pdcA overlaps 17 terminal nucleotides of the doc gene. The phd-doc transcript appears to include the transcripts of pdcA and pdcB"`).
- Contains `InterPro:IPR010373` (`DUF968`), a domain of unknown function, and no experimental phenotypes have been reported for `YORA` (`Q06262`).
- Consistent with its uncharacterized status (`PE=4: Predicted`), `YORA` has no existing GO annotations.
