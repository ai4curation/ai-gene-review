# Curation Notes: tec (Q06261) — Bacteriophage P1

## Identity & Genomic Context
- **Gene symbol**: `tec`
- **UniProtKB**: `Q06261` (`TEC_BPP1`, 76 aa, 8.8 kDa, PE=4 Predicted)
- **Organism**: Punavirus P1 (`NCBITaxon:2886926`, *Escherichia phage P1*)
- **Locus**: Uncharacterized 8.8 kDa open reading frame in the region between `doc` and `lpa` (`gene 10`), sequenced by Lehnherr et al. (1991) [PMID:1917870] and Lehnherr et al. (1993) [PMID:8411153].

## Function & Literature Summary
- Sequenced as part of the `phd-doc`-`lpa` interval (`EMBL:M95666`, `AF234172`) [PMID:1917870, PMID:8411153, PMID:15489417].
- In the complete P1 genome paper (Lobocka et al., 2004, `PMID:15489417`), the two ORFs (`pdcA` and `pdcB`) situated between `doc` and `lpa` were noted as open reading frames of unknown function whose transcripts appear to be included in the `phd-doc` operon transcript (`"The nucleotide sequence downstream of doc contains two ORFs that could encode proteins of 66 and 347 residues, designated here pdcA and pdcB (post-doc). The 5′ end of pdcA overlaps 17 terminal nucleotides of the doc gene. The phd-doc transcript appears to include the transcripts of pdcA and pdcB"`).
- No conserved domains, structural motifs, or experimental phenotypes have been reported for `tec` (`Q06261`).
- Consistent with its uncharacterized status (`PE=4: Predicted`), `tec` has no existing GO annotations.
