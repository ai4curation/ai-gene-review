# 16 (P22587) Curation Notes — Escherichia phage P1

## Functional Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `16` (virion morphogenesis gene 16, encoding `gp16`).
- **UniProt record**: `P22587` is a 166-aa C-terminal fragment (`NON_TER 1`) derived from the 3' end of `gene 16` sequenced immediately upstream of the `R-S-U` tail-fiber operon by Guidolin et al. 1989 [PMID:2526777]. In the complete P1 genome sequence (Łobocka et al. 2004, [PMID:15489417]), full-length `16` is the last gene of the `pmgC-tub-pmgB-sit-pmgA-bplA-16` late tail morphogenesis operon transcribed from `LP_sit`.
- **Biological role**:
  - Amber mutations in `gene 16` cause the same morphogenetic defect as mutations in baseplate wedge gene `26` (`gp53` homolog), `R`, and `21`: mutant infections produce complete heads and polysheaths, but no normal tail structures, demonstrating that `gp16` is an essential baseplate/tail morphogenesis component required for baseplate and tail assembly [PMID:15489417].
  - Quantitative LC-MS/MS proteomics of CsCl-purified P1 virions confirmed that `gp16` is a structural component associated with the mature P1 virion [PMID:35458408].
- **Existing GOA annotations**: None (`existing_annotations: []`).
- **Core functions**: Structural component of the phage P1 baseplate (`GO:0098025` `virus tail, baseplate`) required for viral tail assembly (`GO:0098003` `viral tail assembly`) [PMID:15489417, PMID:35458408].
