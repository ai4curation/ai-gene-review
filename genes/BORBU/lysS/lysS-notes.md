# BORBU lysS: source-based annotation review

LysS is the class I lysyl-tRNA synthetase of Borreliella burgdorferi B31. It uses ATP to ligate lysine to tRNA(Lys), performing the same charging chemistry as the structurally unrelated class II lysyl-tRNA synthetases found in many bacteria. The Borrelia enzyme recognizes tRNA(Lys) anticodon identity elements and is expressed during native growth.

## Evidence and scope

The current primary gene symbol is lysS. Enzyme activity was measured after expression in E. coli; native Borrelia mRNA establishes expression, not an independent purified-native-protein assay. The proteomic localization paper is abstract-only and the experimental cytosol annotation is not overruled from the absence of LysS in that abstract.
