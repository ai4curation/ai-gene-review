# RHT1 review notes

## 2026-09-20 full re-review

All five old rows were examined. Only two are source annotations: nucleus IEA and the retired signaling-pathway IEA. The other three were curator-authored NEW proposals labelled IBA and citing the Falcon report. They are not PAINT assertions. No ancestry claim is made from those rows; all original non-NEW source fields, including retired=true, are preserved.

The Falcon report and its sources were checked. PMID:27327160 images nuclear TaGID2, not RHT1 itself; its Y2H/co-IP establishes interaction with RHT proteins. The primary 2026 study PMID:41615756 provides RHT-D1–IDD5 Y2H/BiFC and nuclear localization with the explicit N-terminal truncation construct. PMID:37126674 independently reports RHT1–PLATZ1 physical interaction (cached abstract only after a PMC429 failure). PMID:33422695 directly reports hormone-induced wild-type protein degradation and translational reinitiation of the Green Revolution alleles (abstract-only here).

The key mechanistic restraint is in the 2026 paper’s Discussion: IDD5 sequestration and transcriptional coactivation are alternative models. Growth repression does not specify a transcription corepressor acting at a genomic locus, as required by the live GO:0003714 definition. Withdraw that old NEW and replace it with one directly supported GO:0140297 DNA-binding transcription factor binding proposal, using IPI from the real primary paper. This is an informative binding activity, not generic protein binding. The negative-GA-process NEW duplicated the existing MODIFY replacement and is withdrawn; cellular response NEW adds broader overlapping coverage and is also withdrawn. No NEW process term is added. Core regulatory participation rests on DELLA doing growth-control work, not on DELLA being proteolytic cargo.

Source snapshot is unusually sparse; UniProt also displays older DNA-binding and transcription IBAs absent from this frozen review. Their absence is not evidence of loss and they are not silently imported as new source assertions. The old source-pathway verdict and vocabulary-retirement commentary were disentangled. The project-independent description now describes the protein, its nuclear partners, turnover and allele phenotypes. Global OpenScientist query search for RHT1/Q9ST59/RHT-D1 found no matching report. No new adjudication was needed to replace an unsupported NEW specificity with a directly assayed interaction; unresolved partner mechanisms remain explicit research questions.
