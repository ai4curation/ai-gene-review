CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of LSm protein complexes, which are involved in RNA
CC       processing and may function in a chaperone-like manner
CC       (PubMed:10747033, PubMed:24513854). Component of the cytoplasmic LSM1-
CC       LSM7 complex which is involved in mRNA degradation by activating the
CC       decapping step (PubMed:10747033, PubMed:10761922, PubMed:17513695,
CC       PubMed:24513854). Together with PAT1, the LSM1-LSM7 complex binds to
CC       osmotic stress-activated mRNAs to attenuate the osmotic stress
CC       response, probably by limiting ribosome access to the mRNA and
CC       consequently translation (PubMed:30059503). Component of the nuclear
CC       LSM2-LSM8 complex, which is involved in spliceosome assembly
CC       (PubMed:10747033). The LSM2-LSM8 complex plays a role in the biogenesis
CC       of the spliceosomal U4/U6-U5 tri-snRNP complex by accelerating PRP24-
CC       mediated annealing of U4/U6 di-snRNA (PubMed:10747033, PubMed:24240276,
CC       PubMed:29717126). The LSM2-LSM8 complex binds U6 snRNA terminating with
CC       a non-cyclic 3' phosphate group (PubMed:29717126). LSM2-LSM8 is
CC       probably also involved in degradation of nuclear pre-mRNA by targeting
CC       them for decapping (PubMed:15485930). LSM2-LSM8 could be involved in
CC       processing of pre-tRNAs, pre-rRNAs and U3 snoRNA, although involvement
CC       may be indirect (PubMed:12077351, PubMed:12438310, PubMed:15075370). In
CC       a complex that probably contains LSM2-LSM7, but not LSM1 or LSM8,
CC       associates with the precursor of the RNA component of RNase P (pre-P
CC       RNA) and may be involved in maturing pre-P RNA; the complex also
CC       associates with snoRNA SNR5 (PubMed:10369684, PubMed:15075370).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10369684, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10747033,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10761922, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12077351,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12438310, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14627812,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15075370, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15485930,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17513695, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24240276,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24513854, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29717126,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30059503}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the heptameric LSM1-LSM7 complex that forms a
CC       seven-membered ring structure with a donut shape (PubMed:10747033,
CC       PubMed:24139796, PubMed:24513854). The LSm subunits are arranged in the
CC       order LSM1, LSM2, LSM3, LSM6, LSM5, LSM7 and LSM4 (PubMed:24139796).
CC       Except for LSM1, where a C-terminal helix crosses the ring structure to
CC       form additional interactions with LSM3 and LSM6, each subunit interacts
CC       only with its two neighboring subunits (PubMed:24139796). The LSM1-LSM7
CC       complex interacts with PAT1; within the complex PAT1 has direct
CC       interactions with LSM2 and LSM3 (PubMed:10747033, PubMed:10761922,
CC       PubMed:24139796). The LSM1-LSM7 complex interacts with XRN1
CC       (PubMed:10747033). Component of the heptameric LSM2-LSM8 complex that
CC       forms a seven-membered ring structure with a donut shape; an RNA strand
CC       can pass through the hole in the center of the ring structure
CC       (PubMed:10369684, PubMed:10747033, PubMed:24240276, PubMed:29717126).
CC       The LSm subunits are arranged in the order LSM8, LSM2, LSM3, LSM6,
CC       LSM5, LSM7 and LSM4 (PubMed:24240276). Component of the spliceosome
CC       U4/U6-U5 tri-snRNP complex composed of the U4, U6 and U5 snRNAs and at
CC       least PRP3, PRP4, PRP6, PRP8, PRP18, PRP31, PRP38, SNU13, SNU23, SNU66,
CC       SNU114, SPP381, SMB1, SMD1, SMD2, SMD3, SMX2, SMX3, LSM2, LSM3, LSM4,
CC       LSM5, LSM6, LSM7, LSM8, BRR2 and DIB1 (PubMed:10449419,
CC       PubMed:24240276). May be found in a complex comprising LSM2-LSM7
CC       without LSM1 or LSM8; the complex associates with pre-P RNA and snoRNA
CC       SNR5 (PubMed:10369684, PubMed:15075370). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10369684,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10449419, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10747033,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15075370, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24139796,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24240276, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24513854,
