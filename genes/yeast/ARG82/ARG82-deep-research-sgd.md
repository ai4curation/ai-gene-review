# SGD gene description for ARG82 (yeast)

Inositol polyphosphate multikinase (IPMK); sequentially phosphorylates Ins(1,4,5)P3 to form Ins(1,3,4,5,6)P5; also has diphosphoinositol polyphosphate synthase activity; regulates genes responsive to arginine, phosphate, and nitrogen; needed by most [PSI+] variants for prion propagation
