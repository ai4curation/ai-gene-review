Gene Description

Homoserine dehydrogenase (HSD, EC 1.1.1.3) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Hom6 reduces L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde to L-homoserine using NADPH or NADH (ordered Bi Bi mechanism, pro-S hydride transfer), the third and final step of homoserine synthesis from aspartate. Homoserine is the branch point between threonine (and isoleucine) and methionine synthesis. Hom6 is a dimer with a Rossmann-like nucleotide-binding region, a dimerisation region and a novel catalytic region, and is a candidate antifungal target. It is cytoplasmic, with some nuclear GFP signal. Hom6 also supports rDNA silencing and DNA double-strand-break repair responses. Catalytic-site mutants separate some of these functions from threonine synthesis, while functional cooperation with the MRX complex has a different catalytic requirement; a direct physical MRX interaction has not been established.
