CC   -!- FUNCTION: Alcohol dehydrogenase specific for ethanol. Acts mainyl as a
CC       mitochondrial formaldehyde dehydrogenase and has no effect on ethanol
CC       production (PubMed:2193925). Shows drastically reduced activity towards
CC       primary alcohols from 4 carbon atoms upward. Isomers of aliphatic
CC       alcohol, as well as secondary alcohols and glycerol are not used at all
CC       (PubMed:17938904, PubMed:3282541). The role of ADH4 in yeast metabolism
CC       is not yet known, but ADH4 is not responsible for the production of
CC       ethanol during growth on glucose nor responsible for the oxidation of
CC       ethanol to acetaldehyde (PubMed:22094012).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17938904, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2193925,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22094012, ECO:0000269|PubMed:3282541}.
