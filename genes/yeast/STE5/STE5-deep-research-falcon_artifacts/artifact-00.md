| Feature/domain | Principal interaction or activity | Subcellular role | Evidence/interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| N-terminal membrane-binding region | Direct, relatively weak phospholipid association; acts cooperatively with Gβγ binding | Helps recruit and orient Ste5 at the cytosolic face of the plasma membrane after pheromone stimulation | Membrane binding and Gβγ recognition are synergistic; weak intrinsic affinity limits constitutive signaling, and heterologous lipid-binding modules can replace the membrane-targeting function (pqac-00000001) |
| RING-H2 zinc-finger | Associates with Ste4, the Gβ subunit of receptor-released Ste4–Ste18 (Gβγ); historically also linked to Ste4-promoted Ste5 assembly | Couples receptor/G-protein activation to cortical recruitment of the kinase-bearing scaffold | Genetic and interaction studies place this module in Gβγ-dependent recruitment; it is a signaling interaction module, not evidence that Ste5 is an active ubiquitin ligase (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010) |
| PH domain | Binds phosphoinositide-containing membrane and interacts with the RBD-like domain of Ste11 | Reinforces plasma-membrane association while helping position the MAPKKK Ste11 in the mating complex | The isolated PH region has been studied using residues 367–525; functional and evolutionary evidence supports both lipid recognition and a fungal-specific Ste11–Ste5 interface (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000012) |
| Fus3 docking region, residues 288–316 | Binds Fus3 with approximately 1 µM affinity and promotes partial Fus3 autophosphorylation; also facilitates feedback phosphorylation of Ste5 | Tunes pathway output within the membrane-associated signaling complex rather than serving as the principal Fus3-activation mechanism | This roughly 30-residue site is modulatory: disrupting it can increase mating output. Fus3 recruitment and productive activation also depend strongly on Ste7 and the Ste5 catalytic-assistance region (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000011) |
| C-terminal vWA/Ste5-ms region | Binds Ste7; the independently folding Ste5-ms activity “unlocks” Fus3 for phosphorylation by Ste7 | Promotes selective signal propagation through Fus3 in the localized mating MAPK module | The minimal reported Ste7-binding interval is residues 759–810, within a broader C-terminal construct spanning residues 593–917. Ste5-assisted catalysis increases the Ste7→Fus3 catalytic rate by about 5,000-fold without comparably affecting Ste7→Kss1, explaining pathway specificity (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009) |
| Full Ste5 scaffold ensemble | **Nonenzymatic scaffold/cofactor** that coordinates Ste11, Ste7 and Fus3 and connects them to Ste4/Gβγ; supports interactions both within and between scaffold assemblies | Shuttles between nucleus and cytoplasm when unstimulated and becomes enriched at polarized plasma-membrane sites in pheromone-responsive G1 cells | Ste5 is not a metabolic enzyme and catalyzes no covalent reaction. Its primary function combines regulated localization, kinase co-concentration, pathway insulation and conformational assistance of Fus3 rather than formation of one obligatorily static complex (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000004) |
