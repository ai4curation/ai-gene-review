# FOL1 (YNL256W, P53848) notes

- Trifunctional enzyme with DHNA (EC 4.1.2.25), HPPK (EC 2.7.6.3) and DHPS (EC 2.5.1.15) activities [UniProt:P53848].
- "All three enzyme activities were also detected in wild-type yeast strains, whereas fol1Delta deletion strains only showed background activities" [PMID:15169867]
- Location: "Fol1p is exclusively localized to mitochondria as shown by fluorescence microscopy and immune electronmicroscopy." [PMID:15169867] Within mitochondria it is at the membranes, and the study could not resolve inner from outer membrane.
- A DHPS-specific (fol1) mutant was built and rescued by folinic acid [PMID:11731153].
- FOL1 is essential, and folinic acid auxotrophy in fol1 cells triggers pseudohyphal and invasive growth [PMID:15169867].

## Curation decisions
- All rows for the three MFs are accepted, along with the mitochondrial envelope and membrane rows.
- The cytosol RCA rows are removed, because direct evidence shows the protein is exclusively mitochondrial.
- The 1996 GFP cytoplasm IDA [PMID:8813764] is kept as non-core. GO cytoplasm includes mitochondria, and the later study refines the location.
- The folate cycle and chorismate rows are removed.
- The folic acid biosynthesis rows are changed to GO:0046654.
