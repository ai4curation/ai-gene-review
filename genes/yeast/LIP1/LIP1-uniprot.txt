CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the ceramide synthase complex required for the
CC       synthesis of ceramides (PubMed:15692566, PubMed:34492164,
CC       PubMed:37953642, PubMed:39528796). Plays a role in the localization of
CC       LAG1 to the nuclear endoplasmic reticulum (PubMed:34492164).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15692566, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34492164,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37953642, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39528796}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the ceramide synthase complex composed of at
CC       least LAC1, LAG1 and LIP1 (PubMed:37953642, PubMed:38964337,
CC       PubMed:39528796). Forms a heterotetrameric complex, where one unit of
CC       the LIP1 homodimer interacts with LAC1 and the other with either LAC1
CC       or LAG1 (PubMed:37953642, PubMed:38964337, PubMed:39528796).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37953642, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38964337,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:39528796}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:

CC       Q03579; P38703: LAG1; NbExp=3; IntAct=EBI-27640, EBI-10035;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Endoplasmic reticulum membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15692566}; Single-pass type II membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15692566}. Note=The specific interaction of LAC1
CC       and LIP1 at the lumen of the ER may be important for the enzymatic
CC       activity of the complex. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37953642}.
