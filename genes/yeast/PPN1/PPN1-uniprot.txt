CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the hydrolysis of inorganic polyphosphate (polyP)
CC       chains of many hundreds of phosphate residues into shorter lengths. Has
CC       both exopolyphosphatase and endopolyphosphatase activities at different
CC       ratios depending on divalent cations by cleaving phosphate from the
CC       chain end and by fragmenting long-chain polymers into shorter ones,
CC       respectively. The limited digestion products are 1 and 3 P(i) residues
CC       (PubMed:11102525, PubMed:11447286, PubMed:15170373, PubMed:15342119,
CC       PubMed:15792812, PubMed:31175919, PubMed:8900207, Ref.16). Also
CC       releases phosphate from dATP. dATP phosphohydrolase activity is about
CC       7-fold lower than the exopolyphosphatase activity (Ref.16).
