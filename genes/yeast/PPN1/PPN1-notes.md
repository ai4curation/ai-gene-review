# PPN1 notes

## 2026-11-06

- Fetched the S. cerevisiae `PPN1` review seed with `just fetch-gene yeast PPN1 --uniprot-id Q04119`; symbol lookup timed out twice, but the reviewed UniProt accession fetched cleanly.
- The default Falcon deep-research provider was unavailable in this checkout: `just deep-research yeast PPN1 --provider falcon` reported that only `claude_code` was configured. I therefore used the cached GOA publications, UniProt record, PANTHER PAINT slice and manual publication search for this pass.
- The GOA molecular-function rows are well supported. Sethuraman et al. cloned `PPN1`, matched purified Ppn1 peptides to `YDR452W`, showed a 25-fold activity increase on overexpression and loss of Ppn1 activity after `PPN1` disruption [PMID:11447286]. Shi and Kornberg purified mature, proteolytically processed Ppn1 and confirmed long-chain polyphosphate cleavage by the homogeneous enzyme [PMID:15792812]. Andreeva et al. later compared purified yeast Ppn1, Ppn2, Ppx1 and Ddp1 and found that Ppn1 has both high exopolyphosphatase activity and endopolyphosphatase activity [PMID:31175919].
- The older cytosolic exopolyphosphatase study [PMID:15170373] purified the high-molecular-mass activity before it had been assigned to a gene. The follow-up paper identifies the high-molecular-mass cytosolic exopolyphosphatase as a `PPN1` gene product [PMID:17009950], so the 2004 GOA row can be accepted with the 2006 assignment as additional evidence.
- Gerasimaitė et al. purified recombinant Ppn1 and showed the same enzyme can be biased toward terminal phosphate release by Co2+ or internal polyP fragmentation by Mg2+, with additional ADP/arginine/ATP modulation of the Mg2+-dependent endopolyphosphatase mode [PMID:25742176]. The in vitro switch sharpens the remaining question to whether these cation/metabolite regimes occur in native vacuolar and cytosolic Ppn1 pools in vivo.
- Reggiori and Pelham treat Phm5/Ppn1 as a membrane cargo protein sorted into multivesicular-body internal vesicles en route to the vacuole, with ubiquitination of the cytosolic tail controlling whether it reaches the vacuolar interior or accumulates on the vacuolar membrane [PMID:11566881]. I kept the fungal-type vacuole membrane rows as non-core and represented the main catalytic site at `GO:0000324 fungal-type vacuole`.
- The nuclear annotation is the only unresolved row. The cached record for PMID:16862600 is abstract-only; it states that `PPN1` inactivation eliminates the nuclear exopolyphosphatase activity, but the abstract does not prove that Ppn1 protein enters nuclei rather than controlling the nuclear activity indirectly.
- PAINT `PTN000818947` is a compact fungal PPN1 node. It carries IBD rows for `GO:0000298 endopolyphosphatase activity`, `GO:0004309 exopolyphosphatase activity`, `GO:0000324 fungal-type vacuole` and `GO:0006798 polyphosphate catabolic process`, and 2026 IRDs prune the metazoan `GO:0008081 phosphoric diester hydrolase activity` and `GO:0005576 extracellular region` assertions at the fungal node. The `WITH/FROM` self-seed `SGD:S000002860` is the experimental grounding used to place the IBD, not circular evidence. The second seed, `CGD:CAL0000182615`, is a *Candida albicans* identifier rather than the *C. glabrata* CANGA/PPN1 member.
- A newer 2025 systematic engineering study found that deleting `PPN1` increases cellular polyphosphate, chose `ppn1` as the starting deletion because it improved chain length and cellular polyP, and got the highest intracellular polyP in a `ppn1 vip1` double mutant [PMID:40291979]. This is modern in vivo support for Ppn1 as a polyP catabolic enzyme, but it does not change the term choices.
- A 2025 FRET assay paper uses known vacuolar PPN1, vacuolar PPN2 and cytosolic DDP1 biology as background, then finds an additional Mn2+-dependent endopolyphosphatase activity in a quadruple yeast mutant [PMID:40607052]. It does not refute the PPN1 assignments and suggests the PPN1 family is not the last unidentified yeast endopolyphosphatase activity.
- The SGD hand-written description fetched from Alliance summarizes the dual endo/exopolyphosphatase biology and vacuolar/cytosolic/nuclear localization. Its "homodimer" statement conflicts with the UniProt SUBUNIT line that curates Ppn1 as a homotetramer from PMID:15792812; the review records this as a structured `source_defects` query for SGD to check.
