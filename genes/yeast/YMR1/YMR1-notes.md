- Sole yeast myotubularin [UniProt:P47147 "receptor class myotubularin subfamily"].
- Activity: [PMID:10900271 "Both the recombinant yeast enzyme and a human myotubularin-related protein (KIAA0371) are able to dephosphorylate PI(3)P in vitro"]; deletion raises PI3P [PMID:10900271 "a Saccharomyces cerevisiae strain in which the myotubularin-like gene (YJR110w) is disrupted also exhibits increased PI(3)P levels"].
