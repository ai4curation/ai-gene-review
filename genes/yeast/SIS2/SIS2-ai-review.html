Gene Description

Sis2 (Hal3; YKR072C) is a moonlighting protein of Saccharomyces cerevisiae with two essential, partially redundant roles shared with its paralog Vhs3. First, its C-terminal PPCDC (HFCD flavoprotein) domain forms a heterotrimer with Cab3 that is the yeast phosphopantothenoylcysteine decarboxylase, the third step of coenzyme A biosynthesis: Hal3/Vhs3 contribute the catalytic histidine for the oxidative decarboxylation and Cab3 the catalytic cysteine, so neither homotrimer is active. Sis2 is also part of the larger Cab3-centred CoA-synthesizing protein complex. Second, Sis2 binds the C-terminal catalytic domain of the Ser/Thr phosphatase Ppz1 as a monomer and inhibits it, thereby promoting ENA1 expression, Na+/Li+ tolerance and G1/S progression. Sis2 has a long acidic C-terminal tail and is found in cytoplasm and nucleus. Sis2 dose also sets replicative lifespan: SIS2 deletion gives one of the largest lifespan extensions among yeast deletion strains, an effect that human PPCDC reverses, implicating its coenzyme A synthesis role.

Notes

(SIS2-notes.md)

SIS2 / HAL3 (YKR072C, P36024) notes

Identity

  • HFCD superfamily (PPCDC-like) protein; moonlighting: (1) non-catalytically-complete subunit of the heterotrimeric phosphopantothenoylcysteine decarboxylase (PPCDC) with Cab3 (Ykl088w); (2) inhibitory subunit of the Ser/Thr phosphatase Ppz1 [UniProt:P36024].

PPCDC role

  • Active yeast PPCDC is a Cab3 + Hal3/Vhs3 heterotrimer in which each partner contributes one catalytic residue PMID:19915539.
  • Hal3/Vhs3 carry the His (oxidative decarboxylation step) and Cab3 carries the Cys PMID:26514574. Hence the correct qualifier for PPCDC activity is contributes_to; homotrimeric Hal3 is inactive.
  • Hal3 and Vhs3 are redundant for this role (hal3 vhs3 synthetic lethality) PMID:15192104.
  • Part of the larger CoA-synthesizing protein complex: Cab3 binds Sis2 and Vhs3 PMID:23789928.

Ppz1 regulation

  • Hal3 binds the C-terminal catalytic domain of Ppz1 and inhibits it in vitro; HAL3 effects on salt tolerance require PPZ1 PMID:9636153.
  • Hal3 binds Ppz1 as a monomer, de-oligomerising from PPCDC trimers PMID:26514574.
  • Cell-cycle (G1/S) effects of HAL3 overexpression in sit4 mutants are mediated by Ppz1 PMID:10022927. The earlier chromatin association PMID:7705654 is a fractionation result whose functional relevance was not followed up.

Notes for module

  • Sis2/Vhs3 are the His-donor subunits; Cab3 is the Cys-donor subunit. PPCDC "enables" rows from YeastPathways/IBA should be read as contributes_to.
