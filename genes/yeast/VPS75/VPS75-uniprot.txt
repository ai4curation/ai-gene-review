CC       vitro) (PubMed:19172748). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17320445,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19172748, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21256037,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29300933, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31387991}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:19172748, PubMed:21256037, PubMed:21454705,
CC       PubMed:31387991). Homotetramer (PubMed:27036862). Forms a complex with
CC       RTT109; consisting of a VPS75 dimer contacted by two RTT109 subunits
CC       (PubMed:17320445, PubMed:18719104, PubMed:20560668, PubMed:21256037,
CC       PubMed:21454705, PubMed:31387991). Interacts with RTT109; the
CC       interaction is direct (PubMed:17272723, PubMed:17320445,
CC       PubMed:18719104, PubMed:20560668, PubMed:21256037, PubMed:31387991).
CC       Interacts with ASF1 (PubMed:31387991). Interacts with histone H3/H4
CC       heterodimers and heterotetramers via histone H3 (PubMed:21454705,
CC       PubMed:27036862, PubMed:31387991). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17272723,
