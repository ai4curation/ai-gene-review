CC   -!- FUNCTION: GTPase involved in activation of the TORC1 signaling pathway,
CC       which promotes growth and represses autophagy in nutrient-rich
CC       conditions (PubMed:26510498, PubMed:28993463, PubMed:32801125). Also
CC       required for TORC1 inactivation during nitrogen starvation
CC       (PubMed:28993463). Required for intracellular sorting of GAP1 out of
CC       the endosome (PubMed:16732272). Functionally associated with the
CC       inorganic phosphate transporter PHO84, and may be involved in
CC       regulating its function or localization (PubMed:1620108).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1620108, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16732272,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26510498, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28993463,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32801125}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Sensitive to rapamycin (TORC1 signaling-
CC       inhibitor) (PubMed:28993463). Decreases and delays activation of TORC1
CC       signaling in nitrogen-replete conditions (glutamine nitrogen source)
CC       (PubMed:29698392). Abnormal punctate localization of PIB2 and TOR1 in
CC       nitrogen-replete conditions (glutamine nitrogen source)
CC       (PubMed:28993463, PubMed:29698392, PubMed:32801125). Increases cellular
CC       levels of glutamine and alanine during high hydrostatic pressure
CC       (mechanical stress) (PubMed:32801125). Sensitive to high hydrostatic
CC       pressure (PubMed:32801125). Resistance to tunicamycin (endoplasmic
CC       reticulum stressor) administered together with FK506 (calcineurin
CC       inhibitor) (PubMed:26510498). Normal localization of KOG1 and TOR1 to
CC       the vacuolar membrane (PubMed:28483912). Simultaneous disruption of
CC       PIB2 results in TOR1 mislocalization and loss of TORC1 activity and
CC       viability (PubMed:29698392). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26510498,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28483912, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28993463,
