# MSS4 notes (YDR208W, P38994)

- PI4P 5-kinase [PMID:9624177 "We constructed a hemagglutinin epitope-tagged form of Mss4p and found that Mss4p has PtdIns(4)P 5K activity."]; major PI(4,5)P2 source [PMID:9624178 "These results suggest that MSS4 is the major PI(4)P 5-kinase in yeast"]; mss4 ~10% PI(4,5)P2 [PMID:9624178]
- Plasma membrane PIK patches [PMID:22562153 "We show that the conserved PtdIns(4)P 5-kinase, Mss4, forms dynamic, oligomeric structures at the PM that we term PIK patches."]
- Actin [PMID:9624178 "cells lacking MSS4 are unable to form actin cables"]; prospore membrane [PMID:19502581 "Overexpression of the phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate 5-kinase MSS4 in sso1Delta cells induced to sporulate stimulates PSM production"]
- UniProt PANTHER subfamily is PTHR23086:SF8 "PHOSPHATIDYLINOSITOL 5-PHOSPHATE 4-KINASE, ISOFORM A" despite biochemistry showing Mss4 is a PI4P 5-kinase and not a PI5P 4-kinase [PMID:9624177 "MSS4 is a functional homolog of mammalian PtdIns(4)P 5K but not of PtdIns(5)P 4K in vivo"].
