# ALD5 (YER073W, UniProt P40047) notes

## Identity
- Minor mitochondrial ALDH; "ALDH5" in Wang et al., 23-aa leader [PMID:9473035 "we deduce that the first 23 amino acids code for a putative mitochondrial leader sequence"]; import shown [PMID:9473035 "The experiments with ALDH5-LacZ fusion protein and in vitro import into isolated mitochondria confirmed that ALDH5 was located in mitochondria (data not shown)."]

## Activity
- NAD or NADP, no preference [PMID:9473035 "Mitochondrial ALDH5 could use either NAD or NADP as the cofactor and did not show a preference."]; poor enzyme [PMID:9473035 "Our in vitro enzyme kinetic data (Tables 3 and 4 ) suggest that ALDH5 is a relatively poor enzyme compared to the other two."]

## Processes
- Acetate during anaerobic glucose growth [PMID:15256563 "The deletion of ALD6 and ALD5 decreased acetate formation in both strains, demonstrating for the first time that the mitochondrial Ald5p isoform is involved in the biosynthesis of acetate during anaerobic growth on glucose."]
- Respiratory chain maintenance, mechanism unknown [PMID:10585550 "These observations show that mitochondrial ALD5 plays a role in regulation or biosynthesis of electron transport chain components."] (not annotated in GOA)
- Not the beta-alanine ALDH [PMID:12586697 "we found no evidence to indicate that the Ald5p functions directly in the conversion of 3-aminopropanal to beta-alanine"]

- RCA cytosol is_active_in removed (mitochondrial enzyme); bifid shunt removed.
