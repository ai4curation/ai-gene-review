# DFR1 (YOR236W, P07807) notes

- DHFR, EC 1.5.1.3 [UniProt:P07807]. "DHFR was overproduced about 40-fold in S. cerevisiae when the cloned gene was present in the vector YEp24." [PMID:2838385]
- Multicopy DFR1 gives methotrexate resistance [PMID:2827121].
- The dfr1 mutant needs dTMP, adenine, histidine and methionine, cannot use folinic acid in YEPD, and is respiratory-deficient [PMID:1427091].
- Location: "dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR) ... is present in both the cytoplasm and mitochondria" (citing Zelikson & Luzzati 1977) [PMID:15169867]. GFP shows cytoplasm and mitochondria [PMID:14562095].
- Dfr1p was among the candidate RNA-binding enzymes in a protein-array screen [PMID:21124907].

## Curation decisions
- The core annotations are GO:0004146 and GO:0046654.
- The mitochondrion IBA and HDA rows are accepted.
- mRNA binding, NADP binding and folic acid metabolic process are kept as non-core.
- The folate cycle row is marked over-annotated.
- The chorismate row is removed.
- The folic acid biosynthetic row is changed to GO:0046654.
