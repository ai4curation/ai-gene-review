# FAB1 notes (YFR019W, P34756)

Evidence journal (no paid deep research run; built from UniProt and cached publications).

- Sole PI3P 5-kinase; makes PI(3,5)P2 [PMID:9811604 "Fab1p shows PI(3)P 5-kinase activity both in vivo and in vitro."]
- fab1 mutants lack PI(3,5)P2 [PMID:9763421 "fab1Delta, fab1(tsf), and fab1 kinase domain point mutants lack detectable levels of PtdIns(3,5)P2"]; phenotypes = ts growth, vacuole acidification defect, enlarged vacuole [PMID:9763421 "The major phenotypes resulting from Fab1p kinase inactivation include temperature-sensitive growth, vacuolar acidification defects, and dramatic increases in vacuolar size."]
- Location: vacuole + endosome membranes [PMID:11889142 "The GFP–Fab1p localized to endosomal and vacuolar membranes in wild-type yeast"]; tethered by FYVE-PI3P [PMID:18653468 "The Fab1 complex is tethered to the vacuole via an interaction between the FYVE domain in Fab1 and PtdIns(3)P on the vacuole."]
- Complex with Vac14 (scaffold), Fig4, Vac7, Atg18 [PMID:19037259 "Vac14 exerts an effect as a scaffold for the PI(3,5)P(2) regulatory complex by direct contact with the known regulators of PI(3,5)P(2): Fig4, Fab1, Vac7 and Atg18"]
- Mitochondrion HDA rows (PMID:14576278, PMID:16823961) judged contamination; no mitochondrial function known [UniProt:P34756].
