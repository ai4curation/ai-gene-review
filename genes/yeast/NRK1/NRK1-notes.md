# NRK1 (YNL129W) notes

## Identity and activity
- Nicotinamide riboside kinase (EC 2.7.1.22) and nicotinate riboside kinase (EC 2.7.1.173); uridine-kinase-family Rossmann-fold kinase.
- Discovery of NR as an NAD+ precursor and NRK genes [PMID:15137942 "Nicotinamide riboside kinases from yeast and humans essential for this pathway were identified and found to be highly specific for phosphorylation of nicotinamide riboside and the cancer drug tiazofurin"].
- In vivo specificity: qns1 cells depend on NR and Nrk1 [PMID:17914902 "Saccharomyces cerevisiae mutants without the QNS1 gene, encoding glutamine-dependent NAD+ synthetase [21] were shown to be entirely dependent on NR and Nrk1 for viability [4]"].
- NaR: human Nrk1/Nrk2 phosphorylate NaR as well as NR in vitro; in yeast NaR supports bna1 npt1 growth via Nrk1 (and Urh1/Pnp1) [PMID:17914902 "NaR is phosphorylated by Nrk1 with highly similar kinetics to those of NR"; "Thus, phosphorylation of NaR by Nrk1 produces NaMN in a pathway"]. The kinetic data are on the human enzymes; yeast NaR kinase activity is genetic inference + ISS/IBA.
- A parallel Nrk1-independent NR route exists (Urh1, Pnp1, Meu1 split NR) [PMID:17914902 "The second NR salvage pathway is Nrk-independent and is initiated by the activity of yeast Urh1, Pnp1"].

## Location
- GFP survey: cytoplasm [PMID:14562095]. SWAT/tagged-library HDA: fungal-type vacuole [PMID:26928762], unconfirmed and without a mechanistic rationale; non-core.
