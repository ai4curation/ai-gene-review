# FAD1 (YDL045C) notes

UniProt P38913; FAD synthase / FMN adenylyltransferase, EC 2.7.7.2; PAPS reductase fold [UniProt:P38913].

## Evidence journal
- Activity [PMID:7799934 "S. cerevisiae transformed with FAD1 on a multicopy plasmid displays an increase in FAD synthetase activity. This is also true when the gene is expressed in Escherichia coli."]; essential [PMID:7799934 "FAD1 codes for an essential yeast protein, since disruption of the gene induces a lethal phenotype."].
- Wu 1995 found no mitochondrial activity in WT [PMID:7799934 "This is supported by the absence of FAD synthetase activity in yeast mitochondria"].
- Bruni 2021 detects endogenous mitochondrial Fad1, carbon-source dependent [PMID:34575116 "we showed that a natural Fad1p exists in yeast mitochondria"; "Thus, the Fad1p content in mitochondria seems to depend on the carbon source."].
- Nucleus only by similarity to S. pombe O74841 [UniProt:P38913].
