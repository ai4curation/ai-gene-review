# CYB5 (YNL111C, P40312) review notes

## Identity
- Microsomal cytochrome b5, 120 aa, heme-binding cytochrome b5 domain, C-terminal tail anchor; ER membrane, cytoplasmic side (by similarity) [UniProt:P40312].
- Tail-anchored protein class inserted into the ER [PMID:12514182 "uniformly hydrophobic tail anchors are first inserted into the endoplasmic reticulum"].

## Function
- Electron carrier for membrane oxygenases; role in fatty acid desaturation and sterol C-4 demethylation [UniProt:P40312].
- Yeast microsome studies: antibodies to cytochrome b5 inhibit C-4 demethylation but not 14alpha-demethylation [PMID:6163470 "cytochrome b5 and a cyanide-sensitive enzyme are involved in the 4-demethylation of 4,4-dimethylzymosterol, but not the 14 alpha-demethylation of lanosterol, by yeast microsomes"]. The terminal cyanide-sensitive enzyme is Erg25 (C-4 methylsterol oxidase).
- Cyb5 with NADH-cytochrome b5 reductase can fully drive CYP51 (Erg11) [PMID:10622712 "fungal CYP51 mediated sterol 14alpha-demethylation can be wholly and efficiently supported by the cytochrome b5/NADH cytochrome b5 reductase electron transport system"].
- Multicopy CYB5 suppresses ketoconazole hypersensitivity of the CPR (ncp1) mutant; cyb5 null viable [PMID:8181746 "yeast Cyb5 is not required for growth in a Yred+ strain"]; CYB5 encodes the major microsomal b5 [PMID:8181746 "This confirms that we have cloned the gene encoding the major microsomal form of Cyb5"].

## Curation conclusions
- Core MF GO:0009055 electron transfer activity (heme b5 electron carrier); BP sterol/ergosterol biosynthesis (Erg25, Erg3, alternative supply to Erg11); CC ER membrane.
- Fatty acid desaturase Ole1 and the ceramide hydroxylase Scs7 carry their own fused b5 domains, so the role of free Cyb5 in fatty acid desaturation is less clear than for sterols.
- YeastCyc lists Cyb5 only as a generic reactant of ERG25/ERG3 reactions; module adds it.
