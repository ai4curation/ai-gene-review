- Glutamine PRPP amidotransferase EC 2.4.2.14, committed step [PMID:6376509 "ADE4 encoding glutamine phosphoribosylpyrophosphate amidotransferase"]; no [4Fe-4S] cluster unlike B. subtilis [PMID:6376509 "does not contain the previously deduced sequence required for binding of a [4Fe-4S] center"].
- Feedback inhibited by ATP/ADP; controls SAICAR level and ADE gene expression [PMID:11689683 "feedback inhibition by ATP of Ade4p ... appears to regulate SAICAR synthesis in response to adenine availability"].
- Forms dynamic cytoplasmic condensates under purine depletion, TORC1-dependent [PMID:40208903 "forms dynamic and motile condensates in Saccharomyces cerevisiae cells under a purine-depleted environment"].
