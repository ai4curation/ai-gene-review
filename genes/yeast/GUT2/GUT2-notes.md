# GUT2 (YIL155C, UniProt P32191) notes

## Activity / role
- FAD-dependent mitochondrial G3P DH, EC 1.1.5.3 [UniProt:P32191]; [PMID:8256521 "A gut2 mutant of Saccharomyces cerevisiae is deficient in the mitochondrial glycerol 3-phosphate dehydrogenase and hence cannot utilize glycerol."]
- Electrons to respiratory chain [PMID:12032156 "glycerol 3-phosphate gives two electrons to the respiratory chain via mitochondrial glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase (Gut2p)-regenerating dihydroxyacetone phosphate"]
- G3P shuttle with Gpd1/Gpd2 [PMID:10781551 "a glycerol-3-phosphate shuttle consisting of soluble (Gpd1p or Gpd2p) and membrane-bound (Gut2p) glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenases"]; shuttle not essential [PMID:10781551 "Apparently, the glycerol-3-phosphate shuttle is not essential for respiratory glucose dissimilation."]
- Inhibited by active Nde1/2 [PMID:12032156 "the activation of NADH dehydrogenase inhibited the Gut2p in such a manner that, at a saturating concentration of NADH, glycerol 3-phosphate is not used as respiratory substrate."]

## Localization
- [PMID:12032156 "Both Nde1p/Nde2p and Gut2p are located in the inner mitochondrial membrane with catalytic sites facing the intermembranal space."]
- IMS import route [PMID:22984289 "only three proteins of the IMS (Gut2, Cyb2, and Mcr1) have been shown to take this import route in yeast mitochondria"]
- Supercomplex [PMID:11502169 "The complex was found to contain the five known intermembrane space-facing dehydrogenases, namely two external NADH-dehydrogenases Nde1p and Nde2p, glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase Gut2p"]
