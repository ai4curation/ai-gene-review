Gene Description

DPH1 (YIL103W) encodes one of the two paralogous catalytic subunits of the 2-(3-amino-3-carboxypropyl)histidine synthase (Dph1-Dph2 heterodimer) that performs the first step of diphthamide biosynthesis on translation elongation factor 2 (eEF2). The heterodimer is a non-canonical radical-SAM enzyme: each subunit binds a [4Fe-4S] cluster through three conserved cysteines, the reduced cluster cleaves S-adenosylmethionine at the methionine C(gamma)-S bond, and the 3-amino-3-carboxypropyl (ACP) group is transferred to C2 of the imidazole of His699, releasing 5'-methylthioadenosine. The Dph1 cluster is the catalytic one, while the Dph2 cluster is needed for its reduction by the physiological Dph3(KTI11)/Cbr1/NADH system; Dph3 also donates an iron atom that rebuilds the oxygen-damaged [3Fe-4S] cluster. Dph1 and Dph2 are not interchangeable. dph1 null cells lack diphthamide, are resistant to diphtheria toxin and sordarin and show increased -1 frameshifting. The human ortholog DPH1 (OVCA1) is mutated in a recessive developmental syndrome. Dph1 is cytoplasmic.

Core Functions

Catalytic subunit of the Dph1-Dph2 heterodimeric radical-SAM ACP-histidine synthase; its [4Fe-4S] cluster cleaves SAM and transfers the 3-amino-3-carboxypropyl group to eEF2 His699 (first step of diphthamide biosynthesis).
