# YAT1 (YAR035W; UniProt P80235) notes

- Carnitine O-acetyltransferase (EC 2.3.1.7), carnitine/choline acetyltransferase family [UniProt:P80235].
- Cloned as ethanol/acetate-inducible gene; protein in mitochondrial fraction; lacks cleavable presequence; deletion has no obvious phenotype; "likely to code for a minor mitochondrial outer carnitine acetyltransferase" [PMID:8262985].
- UniProt instead lists mitochondrion inner membrane, intermembrane side (no cited evidence) - sub-mitochondrial location is unresolved.
- Isolated by complementation of a carnitine-dependent (cit2) mutant; all three CATs (CAT2, YAT1, YAT2) essential for growth on non-fermentable carbon in cit2 background [PMID:11329169 "L-carnitine and all three CATs are essential for growth on non-fermentable carbon sources in a strain with a disrupted CIT2 gene"].
- Overexpression of single CATs does not cross-complement -> each has a specific metabolic role [PMID:18427809 "Overexpression of single CAT genes does not lead to cross-complementation"].
- ISS "carnitine O-palmitoyltransferase activity" from rat CPT2 (P18886): yeast carnitine shuttle is described only for acetyl units; the only assayed activity of Yat1 is acetyltransferase [PMID:8262985 "Carnitine acetyltransferase activities of intact mitochondria from a YAT1 deletion mutant are slightly lower than wild type"]. Specificity from CPT2 similarity is not warranted.
- YAT1-YAT2 interaction found by Y2H (PMID:18719252) and AP-MS (PMID:37968396) - possibly a hetero-complex; untested.
- YeastPathways RCA cytosol is wrong for Yat1 (mitochondrial).
