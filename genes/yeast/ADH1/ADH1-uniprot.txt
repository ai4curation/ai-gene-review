CC   -!- FUNCTION: Preferentially fermentative isozyme that reduces acetaldehyde
CC       to ethanol during the fermentation of glucose. Major enzyme required
CC       for the conversion of acetaldehyde to ethanol (Probable)
CC       (PubMed:22094012). Plays a key role in the carbohydrate metabolism
CC       through the regeneration of NAD(+) from glycolytic NADH (Probable). In
CC       the reverse reaction, preferentially catalyzes the conversion of
CC       primary unbranched alcohols to their corresponding aldehydes. Also
CC       shows activity toward secondary alcohols (Probable). Most active with
CC       ethanol, and its activity decreases as the size of the alcohol is
CC       increased (PubMed:8463307). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22094012,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8463307, ECO:0000305|PubMed:12702265}.
