# ERG5 notes (P54781, YMR015C, CYP61)

- Function: cytochrome P450 sterol C-22 desaturase, EC 1.14.19.41 (5-dehydroepisterol + NADPH + O2 -> ergosta-5,7,22,24(28)-tetraenol) [UniProt:P54781].
- Biochemistry: purified from microsomes of a CYP51-disrupted strain, reconstituted with NADPH-P450 reductase [PMID:8543054 "This allowed the function of this family of P450 to be identified as sterol delta 22-desaturation in the pathway of ergosterol biosynthesis."].
- Genetics: [PMID:8635732 "ERG5 is the putative gene encoding the C-22 sterol desaturase required in ergosterol biosynthesis."]; non-essential; nystatin-sensitivity screen.
- Cofactor heme (by similarity); single-pass ER membrane; interacts with Erg28 [UniProt:P54781].
- Location: ER by global GFP screens (PMID:14562095, PMID:26928762).
- GO notes: GO:0000249 is_a sterol desaturase activity only (not under monooxygenase); monooxygenase IEA -> MODIFY; iron ion binding over-annotation (heme iron); GO:0016705 kept non-core (P450 O2 chemistry).
