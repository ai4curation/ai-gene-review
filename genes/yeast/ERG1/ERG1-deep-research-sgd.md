# SGD gene description for ERG1 (yeast)

Squalene epoxidase; catalyzes epoxidation of squalene to 2,3-oxidosqualene; plays essential role in ergosterol-biosynthesis pathway and is specific target of antifungal drug terbinafine; directed to ER via guided entry of tail-anchored proteins (GET) pathway; degradation by ERAD triggered by membrane lipid saturation; human SQLE functionally complements the lethality of the erg1 null mutation
