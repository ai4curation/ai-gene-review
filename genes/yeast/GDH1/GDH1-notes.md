# GDH1 (YOR375C, P07262) notes

Evidence journal (no paid deep research run; built from UniProt and cached publications).

- NADP-specific glutamate dehydrogenase 1, EC 1.4.1.4, homohexamer, Glu/Leu/Phe/Val DH family; physiological direction is reductive amination of 2-oxoglutarate (right-to-left of RHEA:11612) [UniProt:P07262].
- Cloning: complements an E. coli glutamate auxotroph; "NADP-dependent glutamate dehydrogenase was overproduced 6-10-fold in Saccharomyces cerevisiae bearing GDH1 on a multicopy plasmid" [PMID:2989290]. Independently cloned by cross-hybridisation to N. crassa am; "complements the gdh- strain of S. cerevisiae and directs substantial overproduction of NADP-GDH" [PMID:2932370].
- Paralog GDH3 (whole-genome duplication): "two NADP(+)-dependent glutamate dehydrogenases (NADP-GDHs) encoded by GDH1 and GDH3 catalyze the synthesis of glutamate from ammonium and alpha-ketoglutarate"; "they showed different allosteric properties and rates of alpha-ketoglutarate utilization"; expression "differentially regulated and depends on the nature of the carbon source"; roles "nonredundant" [PMID:11562373].
- Three routes to glutamate (GDH1, GLT1/GLN1, GDH3); gdh1 glt1 gdh3 triple mutant is a strict glutamate auxotroph [PMID:9287019].
- NADP-GDH activity also measured in nuclear fractions (1976; isoform not resolved) [PMID:4126].
