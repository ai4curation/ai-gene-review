# FAA4 (YMR246W; UniProt P47912) notes

- Long-chain acyl-CoA synthetase; prefers C14:0 and cis-9 monoenes [PMID:7650027 "Among C8-C18 saturated fatty acids, Faa4p and Faa1p both prefer C14:0"].
- Redundant with Faa1p for oleoyl-CoA formation, acyl-CoA pools and fatty acid import [PMID:11477098 "The faa1 Delta faa4 Delta strain had a marked reduction in endogenous acyl-CoA pools"].
- Complex with Fat1p [PMID:12601005 "co-expressed, differentially tagged Fat1p and Faa1p or Faa4p were co-immunoprecipitated"].
- ER in growth, relocalises to LDs in stationary phase [PMID:34161133 "Dga1 and Faa4 dynamically relocalize from the ER to the surface of LDs"]; partial plasma membrane pool, weak LCB transport [PMID:27136724 "The partial localization of Faa4 in the plasma membrane is consistent with the weak transport activity of Faa4"].
- Stress granule HDA (PMID:26777405) single proteomic hit, non-core.

## Pathway-context observations
- Like FAA1, acts outside peroxisomes; contribution to beta-oxidation indirect (FAO RCA non-core, cytosol RCA over-annotated).
