# SGD gene description for MDM12 (yeast)

Mitochondrial outer membrane protein, ERMES complex subunit; required for transmission of mitochondria to daughter cells; required for mitophagy; may influence import and assembly of outer membrane beta-barrel proteins; localizes to mitochondria–ER contact sites; ERMES complex is often co-localized with peroxisomes and with concentrated areas of pyruvate dehydrogenase
