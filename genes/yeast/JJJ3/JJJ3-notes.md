# JJJ3 / DPH4 (YJR097W, P47138) notes

Module `diphthamide_biosynthesis`, step 1 accessory J-protein. Not in YeastCyc PWY-6482-1 (module added it).

- Required for diphthamide [PMID:15485916 "DPH4 encodes a CSL zinc finger-containing DnaJ-like protein"].
- Iron binding reported [PMID:22367199 "Yeast Jjj3, a functional ortholog of human Dph4 also shows a similar iron-binding property"] but disputed [PMID:24422557 "Dph4 does not readily bind iron and thus is unlikely to be an electron donor for Dph1-Dph2"; "the exact molecular function of Dph4 awaits further studies"].
- Cytoplasm and nucleus by GFP [UniProt:P47138; PMID:14562095].

Observations: no MF asserted in core function (unknown); iron binding kept as non-core given conflicting data.
