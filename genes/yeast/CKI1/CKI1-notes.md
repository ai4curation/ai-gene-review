# CKI1 (YLR133W, P20485) notes

## Activity
- Choline kinase, committed step of CDP-choline pathway [PMID:9506987 "Choline kinase catalyzes the committed step in the synthesis of phosphatidylcholine by the CDP-choline pathway."]
- Ethanolamine poor substrate [PMID:9506987 "Ethanolamine was a poor substrate for the purified choline kinase"]
- Cki1/Eki1 division of labour [PMID:10329685 "Whereas the EKI1 gene product was primarily responsible for phosphatidylethanolamine synthesis via the CDP-ethanolamine pathway, the CKI1 gene product was primarily responsible for phosphatidylcholine synthesis via the CDP-choline pathway."]
- cki1 suppresses sec14 [PMID:10329685 "Unlike cki1Delta mutants, eki1Delta mutants did not suppress the essential function of Sec14p."]

## Location
- Cytoplasm (GFP) [UniProt:P20485]; purified from cytosolic fraction [PMID:9506987 "purified 71.2-fold to homogeneity from the cytosolic fraction"]
