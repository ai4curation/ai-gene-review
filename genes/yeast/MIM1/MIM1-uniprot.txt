CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the MIM complex required for mitochondrial outer
CC       membrane protein import (PubMed:22467864). The MIM complex cooperates
CC       with the receptor TOM70 in binding of precursor proteins and promotes
CC       their insertion and assembly into the outer membrane (PubMed:21825073).
CC       Involved in import of the subset of proteins with multiple alpha-
CC       helical transmembrane segments, including UGO1 (PubMed:21825073,
CC       PubMed:21825074). Required for the assembly of the TOM (translocase of
CC       outer membrane) receptor complex, which is responsible for the
CC       recognition and translocation of cytosolically synthesized
CC       mitochondrial preproteins (PubMed:15242642, PubMed:15326197,
CC       PubMed:15608614). Required specifically for assembly of TOM40, TOM20,
CC       and TOM70, but not TOM22 (PubMed:17974559, PubMed:18187149).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15242642, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15326197,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15608614, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17974559,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18187149, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21825073,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21825074, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22467864}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the MIM complex containing at least MIM1 and MIM2
CC       (PubMed:15608614, PubMed:18187149, PubMed:21825073, PubMed:22467864).
CC       Interacts with MIM2; interaction is direct (PubMed:22467864). Interacts
CC       with TOM70 (PubMed:21825073). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15608614,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18187149, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21825073,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22467864}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       Q08176; Q3E798: MIM2; NbExp=3; IntAct=EBI-31062, EBI-3700002;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion outer membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15326197, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15608614}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Leads to reduced levels of TOM complex but
CC       retains the respiratory chain. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17974559}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the MIM1 family. {ECO:0000305}.
