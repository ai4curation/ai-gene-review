# SEC59 (P20048) notes

Module: `dolichol_phosphate_sugar_donor_supply`, role = dolichol kinase. No YeastCyc reaction.

## Evidence journal
- sec59 lacks dolichol kinase and Dol-P [PMID:1323123 "However, dolichol kinase activity was greatly decreased."; "Quantitative measurements of dolichyl phosphate in sec59 cells showed that the levels were decreased to 48% of wild type at the permissive temperature and to less than 10% at the restrictive temperature."]
- Overexpression raises activity [PMID:1323123 "When sec59 cells were transformed with a plasmid that overexpresses the wild-type gene, dolichol kinase activity increased 10-fold over wild-type levels."]
- Human DOLK complements [PMID:12213788 "hDK1 is capable of complementing the growth defect, elevating DK activity, and consequently increasing Dol-P levels in vivo and restoring normal N-glycosylation of carboxypeptidase Y at the restrictive temperature in the temperature-sensitive mutant sec59-1."]
- Hydrophobic membrane protein [PMID:2657387 "Molecular cloning and DNA sequence analysis of the SEC59 gene predicted an extremely hydrophobic protein product of 59 kilodaltons."]; original interpretation (mannose transfer) later explained by Dol-P deficiency.
