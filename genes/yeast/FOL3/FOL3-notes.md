# FOL3 (YMR113W, Q12676) notes

- Dihydrofolate synthetase, EC 6.3.2.12 [UniProt:Q12676]. "dihydrofolate synthetase catalyzing the binding of the first glutamyl side chain to dihydropteroate yielding dihydrofolate is encoded by the YMR113w gene that we propose to rename FOL3" [PMID:10799479]
- "Mutant cells bearing a fol3 mutation require folinic acid for growth and have no dihydrofolate synthetase activity." [PMID:10799479]
- "folylpolyglutamate synthetase ... is encoded by the MET7 gene" [PMID:10799479]. The two glutamate-adding activities are therefore split between the paralogs FOL3 and MET7.
- A DHFS-deficient mutant was built and rescued by folinic acid [PMID:11731153].
- Location: cytoplasm by GFP [PMID:14562095].

## Curation decisions
- The FPGS rows (GO:0004326, IBA and IEA) are marked over-annotated. This is paralog subfunctionalisation: the IBA's yeast experimental source is MET7.
- The mitochondrion IBA (from FPGS paralogs) is marked over-annotated.
- GO:0046901 (polyglutamate biosynthesis, UniPathway) is marked over-annotated.
- The folic acid metabolic and biosynthetic RCA rows are changed to GO:0006761.
- The folate cycle and chorismate rows are removed.
