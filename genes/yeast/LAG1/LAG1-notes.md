# LAG1 notes (P38703, YHL003C)

- Function: catalytic subunit (redundant with LAC1) of acyl-CoA dependent ceramide synthase; C26-CoA + PHS/DHS -> ceramide [UniProt:P38703]; [PMID:11387200 "microsomes or detergent extracts from lag1Δ lac1Δ double mutants lack an activity transferring C26 fatty acids from C26-coenzyme A onto dihydrosphingosine or phytosphingosine"]; [PMID:11694577 "lag1 Δ lac1 Δ cells have reduced sphingolipid levels due to a block of the fumonisin B1-sensitive and acyl-CoA–dependent ceramide synthase reaction"].
- Purified enzyme: [PMID:15692566 "They copurified with ceramide synthase activity, giving unequivocal evidence that they are subunits of the enzyme."]; third subunit Lip1 [PMID:15692566 "revealed the presence of a novel subunit of the enzyme, Lip1p"].
- Location: ER membrane [PMID:11694577 "Lag1p and Lac1p are two homologous transmembrane proteins of the endoplasmic reticulum"]; cortical + nuclear ER, PHS preference [UniProt:P38703].
- GO:0050291 (sphingosine N-acyltransferase) is the only GO MF for CerS; accepted despite sphingosine wording (yeast lacks sphingosine). No VLC-specific CerS GO term exists (EC 2.3.1.297) - possible ontology gap.
