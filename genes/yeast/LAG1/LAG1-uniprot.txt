CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the ceramide synthase complex that catalyzes the
CC       transfer of the acyl chain from acyl-CoA to a sphingoid base, with high
CC       selectivity toward hexacosanoyl-CoA (C26:0-CoA) (PubMed:11694577,
CC       PubMed:12869556, PubMed:37953642). N-acylates sphinganine and
CC       phytosphingosine bases to form dihydroceramides and phytoceramides,
CC       respectively (PubMed:12869556). Has a higher affinity for
CC       phytosphingosine (PHS) than dihydrosphingosine (DHS), and thus
CC       preferentially produces phytoceramides for the establishment of lateral
CC       diffusion barriers in the cortical endoplasmic reticulum and nuclear
CC       envelope (PubMed:31164445). Redundant with LAC1 but requires SUR2 to
CC       compensate for sphingolipid biosynthesis (PubMed:31164445,
CC       PubMed:39528796). Disruption or inhibition of sphingolipid synthesis
CC       leads to the activation and phosphorylation of YPK1 through the TORC2
CC       and PKH1 pathways, which phosphorylates ORM1 and LAG1 to activate
CC       sphingolipid synthesis (PubMed:34492164). Facilitates ER-to-Golgi
CC       transport of GPI-anchored proteins. Involved in the aging process.
CC       Along with LAC1, plays a role in pheromone-induced MAP kinase-
CC       activation of mating and formation of diploid cells (PubMed:26726837).
CC       May also play a role, together with LAC1, in the polarized membrane
CC       distribution of phosphatidylinositol 4,5 biphosphate required for STE5
CC       localization to the plasma membrane (PubMed:26726837).
