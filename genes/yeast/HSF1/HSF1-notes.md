## Key points
- Single essential HSE-binding transcription factor; homotrimer [file:yeast/HSF1/HSF1-uniprot.txt "Homotrimer (PubMed:8175654). Homotrimerization increases the affinity of HSF1 to DNA"].
- Specific DNA binding and transcriptional activity of the cloned protein [PMID:3044612 "Expression of specific DNA-binding activity in E. coli and of transcriptional activity in yeast confirmed the identity of the cloned gene."].
- The essential function is basal chaperone gene expression [PMID:27320198 "Strikingly, rescuing basal expression of Hsp70 and Hsp90 chaperones enabled robust cell growth in the complete absence of Hsf1."].
- Recruits Mediator through two activation domains [PMID:23447536 "Hsf1, via its dual activation domains, recruits holo-Mediator to HSP promoters"].
- The TORC1 and translation-stress annotations are indirect consequences of Hsf1 target-gene induction [PMID:18270585 "via elevated expression of Hsf1 target genes"; PMID:31124783]. Marked as over-annotated.
- The mitochondrion HDA rows come from organelle proteomics only. Marked as over-annotated.
