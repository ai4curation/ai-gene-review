CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as a component of the histone chaperone complex
CC       chromatin assembly factor 1 (CAF-1), which assembles histone octamers
CC       onto DNA during replication and repair (PubMed:9030687,
CC       PubMed:9030688). CAF-1 performs the first step of the nucleosome
CC       assembly process, bringing newly synthesized histones H3 and H4 to
CC       replicating DNA; histones H2A/H2B can bind to this chromatin precursor
CC       subsequent to DNA replication to complete the histone octamer (By
CC       similarity). Plays a role in the maintenance of heterochromatin (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q1MTN9, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9030687,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9030688}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of chromatin assembly factor 1 (CAF-1), composed of
