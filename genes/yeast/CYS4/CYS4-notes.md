# CYS4 (YGR155W, P32582) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications; no paid deep research).

## Activity
- Cystathionine beta-synthase, EC 4.2.1.22 [UniProt:P32582 "Reaction=L-homocysteine + L-serine = L,L-cystathionine + H2O;"].
- PLP enzyme without heme, unlike mammalian CBS [PMID:10766767 "Our results establish the overall catalytic mechanism of yeast cystathionine beta-synthase"]; kinetics [PMID:11948191 "Cystathionine beta-synthase found in yeast catalyzes a pyridoxal phosphate-dependent condensation of homocysteine and serine to form cystathionine."]; S289 mutants [PMID:19264153].
- H2S-generating side reactions [PMID:19531479 "CBS from both organisms shows a marked preference for H2S generation by beta-replacement of cysteine by homocysteine."].

## Pathway
- First step of reverse transsulfuration; null mutants are Cys auxotrophs [PMID:10509018; PMID:8366024].
- cys4 disruptant fails to induce sulfate transport, suggesting a regulatory input [PMID:10509018 "suggesting that CYS4 plays a role in the regulation of sulphate assimilation"].

## Moonlighting
- Non-catalytic role in START/critical size [PMID:22438835 "These results suggest that Cys4p promotes START in two ways: By promoting cell growth, which requires its catalytic activity; and by reducing critical size, which does not require Cys4p's catalytic activity."]. Kept as non-core.

## Localization
- Cytoplasm (several HDA/IDA). Mitochondrion in three HDA proteomes: non-core. SG core proteome HDA, but imaging shows Cys4 foci independent of SGs [PMID:32609300 "seven enzymes (Cdc19, Cys4, Dph2, Hem2, Imd3, Shm2, and Tpi1) had the ability to form structures independent of SGs"].
- mRNA binding (HDA, mRNP capture): marked as over-annotated.
