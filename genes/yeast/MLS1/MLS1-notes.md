# MLS1 (YNL117W, P30952) notes

- Malate synthase, glyoxylate cycle [UniProt:P30952 "Malate synthase which takes part in the glyoxylate cycle"]; reaction glyoxylate + acetyl-CoA + H2O = (S)-malate + CoA.
- Acetate non-utilising mutant [PMID:8878673 "Four genes encode enzymes of the glyoxylate cycle and gluconeogenesis: ICL1, MLS1, MDH2, and PCK1"].
- Localisation is carbon-source dependent [PMID:11846793 "Mls1p was abundant in the peroxisomes of cells grown on oleic acid, whereas in ethanol-grown cells Mls1p was primarily cytosolic"]; cytosolic SKL-less Mls1 suffices on ethanol/acetate [PMID:11846793].
- Peroxisomal import of Mls1/Mls2 by oleate-induced Pex9 [PMID:27678487 "Pex9p acts as a cytosolic and membrane-bound peroxisome import receptor for both malate synthase isoenzymes, Mls1p and Mls2p"].
- Paralog Dal7/Mls2 [PMID:23642236 "In S. cerevisiae two isoforms of malate synthase are described, encoded by MLS1 and DAL7 genes"]; Dal7 mainly in allantoin degradation.
- WW-domain microarray IPIs (Prp40, Rsp5; PMID:16606443) removed as uninformative protein binding.
