Gene Description

L-asparaginase II (cell-wall asparaginase; EC 3.5.1.1) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, encoded by ASP3-1, one of four identical tandem copies (ASP3-1 to ASP3-4) adjacent to the rDNA array on chromosome XII. The precursor carries a 25-residue signal peptide and the mature enzyme resides in the cell wall-bounded periplasmic space, where it hydrolyses external L-asparagine (and D-asparagine and N-acyl-asparagines) to aspartate and ammonium. Unlike the constitutive cytoplasmic asparaginase I (Asp1), ASP3 expression is under nitrogen catabolite repression and is induced by nitrogen starvation or poor nitrogen sources, allowing nitrogen scavenging from extracellular asparagine. The ASP3 cluster varies in copy number and is absent from most strains in the larger genome survey; four identical copies characterize the S288C reference strain.

Core Functions

Periplasmic hydrolysis of external L-asparagine to aspartate and ammonium under nitrogen limitation (asparaginase II). The four S288C loci encode identical proteins; shared classical asparaginase-II experiments do not distinguish their individual contributions.
