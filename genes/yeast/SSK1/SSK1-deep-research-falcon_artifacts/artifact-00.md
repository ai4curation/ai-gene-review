Ssk1 is therefore a phosphorylation-controlled signaling adaptor/switch, not an enzyme. | Direct binding assays, in-vitro restoration of Ssk2 activity by Ssk1, autophosphorylation assays, activation-loop mutagenesis, and genetic epistasis.
