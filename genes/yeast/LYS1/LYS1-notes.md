# LYS1 (YIR034C, P38998) notes

Pathway context: YeastPathways LYSINE-AMINOAD-PWY-2, final step (EC 1.5.1.7, saccharopine dehydrogenase, NAD+, L-lysine-forming).

- Activity: NAD+-dependent cleavage of saccharopine to lysine + 2-oxoglutarate [UniProt:P38998]; kinetic mechanism of recombinant enzyme [PMID:17002315 "Kinetic data have been measured for the histidine-tagged saccharopine dehydrogenase from Saccharomyces cerevisiae"], ordered addition of NAD then saccharopine [PMID:17002315 "suggesting the ordered addition of nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD) followed by saccharopine in the physiologic reaction direction"]. Classical papers PMID:4287986, PMID:4318475 (title-only cache).
- Location: peroxisomal matrix via PTS1/Pex5 [PMID:35563734 "the last step of lysine biosynthesis occurs in peroxisomes by the saccharopine dehydrogenase enzyme, Lys1 (Lysine requiring 1) [50], which possesses a PTS1 motif with high affinity to Pex5 [51]."]; [PMID:28928432 "Indeed GFP-Lys1p localises to peroxisomes but GFP-Lys12p does not."]. Peroxisomal NAD+ regeneration by Mdh3/Gpd1 shuttles needed; cytosolic Lys1 still works [PMID:28928432 "Firstly, by mislocalising Lys1p to the cytosol in mdh3/gpd1Δ cells, lysine biosynthesis is restored"].
- Pls1 (Ynl097c-b) import requires Lys1 [PMID:35563734 "Indeed, in LYS1-deleted cells, Ynl097c-b did not localize to peroxisomes in neither control nor lysine-deprived media"] -> IMP protein import into peroxisome matrix kept non-core.
- mRNA binding [PMID:20844764 "Lys1 interacted with >1,000 mRNAs"] -> non-core.
- Decisions: RCA cytosol rows (two pathway diagrams) MODIFY -> peroxisomal matrix GO:0005782. RCA lysine catabolic process (LYSDEGII-PWY) marked over-annotated: reverse reaction possible [PMID:35563734 "Lys1 can convert saccharopine to lysine or the reverse reaction depending on the local pH in the active site"] but no yeast evidence of Lys1-dependent lysine degradation found.
