# LPP1 (YDR503C, Q04396) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications; no paid deep research).

- Lpp1 is a Mg2+-independent lipid phosphate phosphatase, the Golgi paralogue of Dpp1. [PMID:10685032 "The product of the LPP1 gene in Saccharomyces cerevisiae is a membrane-associated enzyme that catalyzes the Mg(2+)-independent dephosphorylation of phosphatidate (PA), diacylglycerol pyrophosphate (DGPP), and lysophosphatidate (LPA)."]
- Its substrate preference is PA > LPA > DGPP. [PMID:9603941 "The order of substrate preference was PA > lyso-PA > DGPP."]
- Lpp1 and Dpp1 together account for nearly all Mg2+-independent PA/LPA phosphatase activity. [PMID:9603941 "the LPP1 and DPP1 gene products encoded nearly all of the Mg2+-independent PA phosphatase and lyso-PA phosphatase activities and all of the DGPP phosphatase activity in S. cerevisiae"]
- Lpp1 is in the Golgi. [UniProt:Q04396 "Golgi apparatus membrane"] [PMID:24196957 "are integral membrane enzymes that are localized to the vacuole and Golgi compartments, respectively"]
- Lpp1 is not the TAG-relevant PAP. [PMID:24196957 "showed the essential role of PAH1 in triacylglycerol synthesis throughout growth"]
