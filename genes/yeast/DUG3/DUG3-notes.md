# DUG3 (YNL191W, UniProt P53871) notes

- Glutamine amidotransferase class II (GATase II / Ntn) domain protein; "the DUG3 gene encoded a protein with a glutamine amidotransferase domain" [PMID:17179087].
- "Dug3p has a functional glutamine amidotransferase (GATase) II domain that is catalytically important for glutathione degradation as demonstrated through mutational analysis." [PMID:22277648]; "The C2A mutation in Dug3p led to complete loss in the activity" and the N-terminal Cys "acts as a nucleophile for the cleavage of the γ-glutamyl bond of GSH to release glutamate and Cys-Gly" [PMID:22277648].
- Requires Dug2 for activity: "Dug2p and Dug3p were required together for the cleavage of glutathione into glutamate and Cys-Gly" [PMID:22277648].
- Cytoplasm (GFP) [PMID:17179087]; UniProt "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm".
- E. coli PurF similarity only "25% identity (40% similarity) to Dug3p over a 108-aa stretch" [PMID:17179087]; the ISS amidophosphoribosyltransferase annotation transfers the PurF reaction (PRPP + Gln), for which there is no evidence in Dug3 - should be removed.

## Ontology note
- GO:0036374 glutathione gamma-glutamate hydrolase is_a GO:0070003 threonine-type peptidase activity (appropriate for GGT, an Ntn-hydrolase with Thr nucleophile) - inappropriate for the Dug2/Dug3 complex, whose nucleophile is Cys (Dug3 Cys2). Raised as a suggested question.
