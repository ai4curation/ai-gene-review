# SGD gene description for HAC1 (yeast)

Basic leucine zipper (bZIP) transcription factor (ATF/CREB1 homolog); regulates the unfolded protein response (UPR) via UPRE binding and membrane biogenesis; translation initiation repressed under non-stress conditions by un-spliced intron that interacts with 5'-UTR; under ER stress Ire1p nuclease cleaves out inhibitory intron and triggers efficient Hac1p translation; involved in UPR-mediated suppression of aneuploidy; protein abundance increases in response to DNA replication stress
