# ARH1 (YDR376W, UniProt P48360) – review notes

- Adrenodoxin reductase homolog, FAD flavoprotein (cofactor by similarity), essential [PMID:9818721 "It was named ARH1 (Adrenodoxin Reductase Homologue 1) and here we show that it is essential"].
- Recombinant Arh1p reduces bovine adrenodoxin, ferricyanide and cytochrome c using NADPH (and NADH) [PMID:9727014 "Electrons could be transferred from NADPH and NADH as measured in the cytochrome c reduction assay"]; inner-membrane localization by fractionation [PMID:9727014 "Subcellular fractionation studies have localized Arh1p to the inner membrane of yeast mitochondria"]. UniProt: EC 1.18.1.6 (adrenodoxin-NADP+ reductase, RHEA:42312) – GO represents this with GO:0004324 ferredoxin-NADP+ reductase activity (the old NADPH-adrenodoxin reductase term GO:0015039 is obsolete).
- Fe-S / iron homeostasis: [PMID:11035018 "Thus, Arh1p is required for control of cellular and mitochondrial iron levels and for the activities of Fe-S cluster proteins"]; [PMID:12970193 "the requirement of an electron transfer chain consisting of ferredoxin (yeast Yah1p) and ferredoxin reductase (yeast Arh1p) is well established"].
- CoQ: [PMID:20534343 "the mitochondrial ferredoxin Yah1p and the ferredoxin reductase Arh1p are required for Q(6) biosynthesis"].
- GOA lacks iron-sulfur cluster assembly for ARH1 although YAH1 carries it; Arh1 performs the NADPH-dependent reduction step of the electron chain feeding Isu1 -> proposed NEW (GO:0016226).
- Iron homeostasis (IMP) is a downstream consequence of Fe-S deficiency (iron regulon derepression) -> non-core.
- PANTHER PTHR48467 is named after glutamate synthase (module notes this; no PANTHER term asserted in the module).
- Not in YeastCyc pathways.
