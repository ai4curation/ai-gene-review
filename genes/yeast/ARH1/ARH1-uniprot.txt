CC   -!- FUNCTION: Adrenodoxin reductase transfers electrons from NADPH to
CC       adrenodoxin, which is involved in heme A biosynthesis and in iron-
CC       sulfur cluster assembly (PubMed:15211518, PubMed:9727014). Involved in
CC       the electron transfer to heme A synthase COX15, a heme protein that
CC       catalyzes the conversion of heme O to heme A (PubMed:11248251).
CC       Required for the de novo synthesis of Fe-S clusters on iron sulfur
CC       cluster assembly protein ISU1. Involved in electron delivery for Fe-S
CC       cluster synthesis (PubMed:15211518). Essential for coenzyme Q
CC       biosynthesis. May be involved in the electron transfer required for the
CC       hydroxylation reaction performed by COQ6 (PubMed:20534343,
CC       PubMed:21944752). May play a role in cellular and mitochondrial iron
CC       homeostasis (PubMed:11035018). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11035018,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11248251, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15211518,
