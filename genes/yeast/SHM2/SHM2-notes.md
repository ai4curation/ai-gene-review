# SHM2 (YLR058C, P37291) notes

- Cytosolic serine hydroxymethyltransferase (EC 2.1.2.1), PLP homotetramer (by similarity) [UniProt:P37291 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000305|PubMed:8132653}."].
- [PMID:8132653 "subcellular fractionation studies all suggested that the SHM1 gene encodes the mitochondrial SHMT, while the SHM2 gene encodes the cytosolic enzyme"]; GCN4-regulated.
- Main glycine and one-carbon provider when serine is the one-carbon source [PMID:9398220 "When yeast was grown on serine as the primary one-carbon source, the cytoplasmic isozyme was the main provider of glycine and one-carbon groups for purine synthesis"].
- Converges with Ade3 (C1-THF synthase) on cytosolic 5,10-CH2-THF [PMID:12700234 "Shm2p and Ade3p are cytoplasmic enzymes producing 5,10-methylene tetrahydrofolate in convergent pathways as the primary source for cellular one-carbon groups"].
- Catalysis-independent genetic interaction with SRP40 (moonlighting hint) [PMID:12700234 "point mutants of Shm2p that were catalytically inactive (i.e. failed to rescue the methionine auxotrophy of a shm2Delta ade3 strain) complemented the synthetic lethal phenotype"].
- SESAME complex member (ComplexPortal NAS rows) [PMID:26527276].
- HT localizations: plasma membrane fraction proteomics [PMID:16622836], mating projection tip imaging [PMID:19053807]; abundant (67600 molecules/cell) [UniProt:P37291], so likely contaminant/diffuse cytosol.
- IBA mitochondrion propagated from the mitochondrial SHMT2 node; contradicted by fractionation for this cytosolic paralog.
