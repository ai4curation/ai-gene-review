Gene Description

Saccharopine dehydrogenase [NADP+, L-glutamate-forming] (saccharopine reductase, EC 1.5.1.10) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Lys9 catalyses the NADPH-dependent reductive condensation of L-2-aminoadipate 6-semialdehyde with L-glutamate to form saccharopine, step 2 of the fungal-specific segment of the alpha-aminoadipate lysine pathway, between the Lys2 reductase and Lys1 saccharopine dehydrogenase steps. It follows an ordered sequential mechanism with NADPH binding first. The crystal structure (PDB 2AXQ) shows a Rossmann-fold NADP-binding domain and a saccharopine dehydrogenase C-terminal domain. Lys9 is an abundant cytosolic protein; lys9 mutants accumulate aminoadipate semialdehyde and require lysine. LYS9 expression depends on the pathway-specific activator Lys14, which senses aminoadipate semialdehyde.

LYS9 (YNR050C, P38999) notes

2026-10-24

Re-reviewed S. cerevisiae LYS9 after the PTHR11133 LYS1/lys3 branch. The
fungal PANTHER:PTN000875327 IBA is the correct LYS9/lys9 sister placement to
the PTN000875292 LYS1/lys3 branch. The broad PANTHER:PTN000123605
GO:0004753 saccharopine dehydrogenase activity placement is true at the mixed
family node but over-generic for P38999, which sits in PTHR11133:SF22 and
has direct NADP(+), L-glutamate-forming saccharopine reductase evidence.

Deep research: just deep-research-falcon yeast LYS9 could not run because the
deep-research provider API keys are unavailable in this environment. Manual
searches for Saccharomyces LYS9, P38999, and saccharopine reductase found
newer pathway and fungal-evolution papers that discuss yeast LYS9 as the
canonical saccharopine reductase step, but no newer paper changed the PTHR11133
placement, the exact EC 1.5.1.10/GO:0004755 activity, or the cytosolic
L-lysine-biosynthesis role.

Pathway context: LYSINE-AMINOAD-PWY-2, saccharopine reductase (EC 1.5.1.10).

  • Activity: PMID:18416559, ordered NADPH-first mechanism (same paper).
  • Genetics: PMID:6429126 (LYS14 is the pathway activator PMID:10103047).
  • Location: cytoplasm (Kumar 2002, Huh 2003 HDA). Cell periphery from SWAT screen scored by eye without markers -> removed. Interacts with Trm112 [UniProt:P38999] (not in GOA).
  • All exact MF/BP rows were accepted; the generic GO:0004753 parent was
    narrowed to the exact GO:0004755 activity.
