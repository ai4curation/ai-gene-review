# GFA1 (YKL104C, P14742) notes

- Sole GFAT (EC 2.6.1.16): Fru6P + Gln -> GlcN6P + Glu; Ntn-amidotransferase domain + 2 SIS domains [UniProt:P14742 "Reaction=D-fructose 6-phosphate + L-glutamine = D-glucosamine 6-"].
- Cloned by complementing the glucosamine-requiring gcn1 mutant; activity and transcription induced by alpha-factor [PMID:2656689 "increases 1.7-fold after alpha factor addition"].
- First committed enzyme of chitin precursor pathway; flux control coefficient ~0.9 over chitin synthesis; overexpression triples chitin [PMID:11895440 "the first committed enzyme of this pathway"; "flux-coefficient control of Gfa1p to be in the range of 0.90"; "Overexpression of GFA1 caused an approximately threefold increase in chitin"].
- Chitin biosynthetic process (IGI, ARBA) kept non-core: Gfa1 makes GlcN6P; UDP-GlcNAc also feeds N-glycans and GPI.
