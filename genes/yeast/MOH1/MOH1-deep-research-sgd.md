# SGD gene description for MOH1 (yeast)

Protein of unknown function, essential for stationary phase survival; influences metabolic and physiological processes important for selective stress adaptation and survival; possibly linked with vacuolar transport; Yippee-like (YPEL) family member, predicted to form a zinc-finger like metal binding pocket; human ortholog YPEL2 complements stress phenotypes associated with the null mutant
