# INP54 notes (YOL065C, Q08227)

- Tail-anchored ER 5-phosphatase lacking SAC domain [PMID:11116155 "We report that Inp54p belongs to the family of tail-anchored proteins and is localized to the endoplasmic reticulum via a C-terminal hydrophobic tail."]
- All four yeast 5-phosphatases hydrolyse PI(4,5)P2 [PMID:11116155 "all of which hydrolyze phosphatidylinositol (4,5)-bisphosphate"]
- Secretion phenotype [PMID:11116155 "Null mutation of INP54 resulted in a 2-fold increase in secretion of a reporter protein"]
- PMID:10660045 (Raucher 2000) is cited by UniProt for Inp54 catalytic activity; abstract-only, mammalian-cell PM targeting of a 5-phosphatase; accepted deferring to curator.
- Vacuole membrane HDA (SWAT N-terminal GFP, PMID:26928762) conflicts with ER; kept non-core.
