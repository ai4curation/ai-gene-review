# SGD gene description for ALG7 (yeast)

UDP-N-acetyl-glucosamine-1-P transferase; transfers Glc-Nac-P from UDP-GlcNac to Dol-P in the ER in the first step of the dolichol pathway of protein asparagine-linked glycosylation; inhibited by tunicamycin; human homolog DPAGT1 can complement yeast ALG7 mutant
