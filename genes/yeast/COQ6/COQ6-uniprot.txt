CC   -!- FUNCTION: FAD-dependent monooxygenase required for two non-consecutive
CC       steps during ubiquinone biosynthesis. Required for the C5-ring
CC       hydroxylation during ubiquinone biosynthesis by catalyzing the
CC       hydroxylation of 4-hydroxy-3-(all-trans-hexaprenyl)benzoic acid to 3,4-
CC       dihydroxy-5-(all-trans-hexaprenyl)benzoic acid (PubMed:21944752). Also
CC       acts downstream of COQ4, for the C1-hydroxylation during ubiquinone
CC       biosynthesis by catalyzing the hydroxylation of 2-methoxy-6-(all-trans-
CC       hexaprenyl)phenol to 2-methoxy-6-(all-trans-hexaprenyl)benzene-1,4-
CC       diol. The electrons required for the hydroxylation reaction are
CC       funneled indirectly from NADPH via ferredoxin (YAH1) and ferredoxin
CC       reductase (ARH1) to COQ6 (PubMed:21944752). Can also convert 3-
CC       hexaprenyl-4-aminobenzoic acid (HAB), a COQ2-prenylated pABA, to DHHB
CC       in a two step process (PubMed:26260787). HAB is first hydroxylated at
CC       C5 to yield 3-hexaprenyl-4-amino-5-hydroxybenzoic acid (HHAB) which is
CC       further deaminated at C4 by COQ6 to produce DHHB (PubMed:26260787).
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03193, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21944752,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26260787}.
