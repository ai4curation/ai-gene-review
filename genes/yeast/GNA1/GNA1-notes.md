# GNA1 (YFL017C, P43577) notes

- GlcN6P N-acetyltransferase (EC 2.3.1.4), GNAT fold, homodimer [UniProt:P43577].
- Essential; recombinant protein acetylates GlcN6P; reconstitutes UDP-GlcNAc synthesis with Agm1 (Pcm1) and Uap1 (Qri1) [PMID:9867860 "When incubated with Agm1p and Uap1p, the Yfl017c protein produced UDP-N-acetylglucosamine from glucosamine 6-phosphate"; "Disruption of YFL017C was lethal"].
- Yeast separates the GlcN6P-acetyltransferase and UAP activities (bacterial GlmU combines them) [PMID:9867860 "whereas bacterial phosphoglucosamine acetyltransferase and UDP-N-acetylglucosamine pyrophosphorylase activities are intrinsic in a single polypeptide, they are encoded by distinct essential genes in yeast"].
- 2025 report: in vitro serotonin / 5-methoxytryptamine N-acetyltransferase activity [PMID:40551389 "In vitro assays revealed that GNA1 exhibits acetyltransferase activity toward both serotonin and 5-methoxytryptamine (5-MT)"]. Kept non-core (in vivo relevance unproven); no NEW melatonin BP proposed.
- GFP: cytoplasm + nucleus [PMID:14562095]; nucleus kept non-core.
