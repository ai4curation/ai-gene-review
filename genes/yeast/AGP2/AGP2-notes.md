# AGP2 (P38090, YBR132C) notes

- Originally: plasma-membrane protein required for carnitine uptake [PMID:10545096 "a transport protein (AGP2) required for carnitine transport across the plasma membrane"]
- Re-evaluated as regulator [PMID:23755272 "We propose that Agp2 mainly acts as a regulator of polyamine transport as well as other specific biological functions"]
- Controls expression of DUR3, SAM3, HNM1 [PMID:23755272 "Agp2 controls the expression of several transporter genes including DUR3 and SAM3, the carnitine transporter HNM1"]
- Hnm1 carries much carnitine uptake [PMID:23755272 "This confirms that a substantial fraction of L-carnitine is transported by Hnm1 into the cells and that the expression of this permease is tightly controlled by Agp2"]
- agp2 completely lacks carnitine uptake [PMID:23755272 "agp2Δ cells were completely deficient in L-carnitine transport activity"]
- Low-affinity amino acid permease reported [PMID:23755272 "Additional studies identified a function for Agp2 as a low-affinity, non-specific amino acid permease in poor nutrient conditions"]
- PM localization; vacuolar sorting on N starvation [PMID:23755272 "Lack of nitrogen caused vacuolar sorting of Agp2-GFP"]

## Decisions
- SGD gives ND for MF; no carnitine transporter activity in GOA. Core = positive regulation of carnitine / polyamine transmembrane transport at plasma membrane; no MF asserted.
- Module asserts GO:0015226 carnitine transmembrane transporter activity for AGP2 -> conflicts with SGD ND and Aouida 2013.
