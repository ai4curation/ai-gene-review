# GPD2 (YOL059W, UniProt P41911) notes

## Activity / role
- NAD-GPDH isoenzyme [PMID:9171333 "The two homologous genes GPD1 and GPD2 encode the isoenzymes of NAD-dependent glycerol 3-phosphate dehydrogenase in the yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae."]; Km NADH 18 uM, DHAP 0.86 mM [UniProt:P41911]
- Anaerobic redox role, not osmotic [PMID:9171333 "Expression of GPD2 is not affected by changes in external osmolarity, but is stimulated by anoxic conditions."]; [PMID:9171333 "Thus, glycerol formation is strictly required as a redox sink for excess cytosolic NADH during anaerobic metabolism."]
- Double mutant [PMID:9171333 "Mutants deleted for both GPD1 and GPD2 do not produce detectable glycerol, are highly osmosensitive and fail to grow under anoxic conditions."]
- Overexpression as effective as GPD1 [PMID:11676566 "We showed that the overexpression of GPD2, encoding the other isoform of glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase (Gpd2p), is equally as effective as the overexpression of GPD1 in increasing glycerol production"]

## Localization
- Mito presequence [PMID:15210723 "Gpd2p is known to be a cytosolic protein but possesses a classical mitochondrial presequence, which we show is sufficient for mitochondrial targeting."]; import confirmed [PMID:17054397 "we performed mitochondrial import experiments and confirmed import of both Gut1p and Gpd2p, implying a novel dual cytosolic and mitochondrial localization"]
- Shuttle component [PMID:10781551 "a glycerol-3-phosphate shuttle consisting of soluble (Gpd1p or Gpd2p) and membrane-bound (Gut2p) glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenases"]
