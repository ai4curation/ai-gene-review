# PYC2 (YBR218C, P32327) review notes

## Evidence journal
- Second PC isozyme on chr II [PMID:2039506 "We report here the existence of two pyruvate carboxylase isozymes"]; independently cloned, 90% identical to PYC1, same Km for ATP/pyruvate [PMID:1921979 "No differences in the Km values for ATP or pyruvate were observed between the enzymes"].
- Redundant with PYC1 for anaplerosis; the double mutant needs aspartate [PMID:1921979].
- Expression: high early on glucose [PMID:8185321 "a high level of PYC2 expression only in the early growth phase"]; PYC2 alone does not support growth on ethanol without aspartate [PMID:8185321].
- Cytosolic [PMID:2665820; PMID:196563].

## Pathway observations
- Same YeastCyc superpathway inflation as PYC1 (aerobic respiration, L-homoserine biosynthesis).
- Paralog specialisation: PYC1 = major/gluconeogenic-growth isozyme; PYC2 = minor, glucose-early isozyme. Both are full PCs.
- The gluconeogenesis IMP (PMID:8185321) on PYC2 is weaker in the abstract (pyc1 null on ethanol fails); deferred to the curator.
