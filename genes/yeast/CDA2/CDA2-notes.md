# CDA2 (YLR308W, Q06703) notes

Evidence journal from UniProt and cached publications.

- Purified Cda2p: ~43 kDa N-glycosylated monomer, EC 3.5.1.41, pH opt 8, 50 C, needs >= chitobiose, Co2+-activated [PMID:11812231 "Deglycosylation of the enzyme causes total loss of enzyme activity, which can be restored by the addition of COCl(2)."]
- Signal peptide 1-44; CE4 metal-binding residues [UniProt:Q06703].
- Redundant with CDA1, sporulation specific [PMID:8940152 "We have cloned the CDA1 and CDA2 genes which together account for the total chitin deacetylase activity of the organism."]
- Ascospore wall assembly [PMID:10544249].

Curation decisions: 'chitin catabolic process' IDA modified to chitin metabolic process (deacetylation is not backbone breakdown). Cobalt ion binding kept as non-core cofactor annotation. Cytosol RCA removed.
