# SHR5 notes

## PTHR13254 fungal Erf4/GOLGA7 review, 2026-11-22

- Reviewed SHR5 as the S. cerevisiae anchor for PTHR13254:SF0, the fungal
  Erf4/GOLGA7 subfamily.
- The core direct evidence remains the 1995 SHR5 genetics paper showing Ras
  underpalmitoylation and reduced membrane attachment in `shr5` mutants
  [PMID:7532279], the 2002 Erf2-Erf4 biochemical papers showing that Erf4
  co-purifies with Erf2 and is required for Ras palmitoyltransferase activity
  [PMID:12193598; PMID:12379641], and the 2012 mechanism paper showing that
  Erf4 stabilizes the palmitoyl-Erf2 intermediate [PMID:22904317].
- A 2024 cryo-EM study of yeast Erf2-Erf4 and human DHHC9-GCP16 supports the
  accessory-subunit model: Erf4 stabilizes Erf2 architecture rather than
  directly contributing the catalytic chemistry [PMID:38182928].
- Accepted the PAINT `PTN000327115` protein-targeting and `PTN001777135`
  ER-palmitoyltransferase-complex rows. The `WITH/FROM` self-seed is expected
  because SGD SHR5 has the direct evidence used to place these IBDs.
- Kept the 2025 nucleus-vacuole junction localization as non-core pending
  evidence that it is an active Erf2-associated pool.
