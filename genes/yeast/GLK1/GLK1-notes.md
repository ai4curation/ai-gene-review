# GLK1 (YCL040W, P17709) notes

## Activity / specificity
- Glucokinase EC 2.7.1.2 [UniProt:P17709 "Reaction=D-glucose + ATP = D-glucose 6-phosphate + ADP + H(+);"]; aldohexose-specific [UniProt:P17709 "isoenzyme, GLK, is specific for aldohexoses."]; monomer.
- hxk1 hxk2 strains (Glk1 only) grow on glucose but not fructose [PMID:6300872 "one (hxk1 hxk2) lacking both hexokinase A(P-I) and B(P-II) but containing glucokinase (and hence able to grow on glucose but not fructose)"] -> IBA fructokinase activity is wrong for Glk1.
- Triple mutant hxk1 hxk2 glk1 is glucose-negative [PMID:6341351 "Genes complementing the glucose-negative fructose-negative Saccharomyces cerevisiae triple mutant strain (hxkl hxk2 glk1), which lacks hexokinase PI, hexokinase PII, and glucokinase, were obtained"].
- Glycolytic flux in Glk1-dependent strains [PMID:8501032 "Glucose phosphorylation capacity is known to be in excess of glucose flux in Saccharomyces cerevisiae wild type but not in a mutant strain lacking the two hexokinases but still having glucokinase."].

## Glucose uptake IGI rows
- Bisson & Fraenkel: kinases influence the low-Km uptake component [PMID:6300872 "the functioning of one of which, the low Km system, is influenced by the cognate kinases."]. Reviewer interpretation: transport itself is by Hxt facilitators; Glk1 is cytosolic -> over-annotation, not removal.

## Location
- Cytosol (Huh GFP, HDA). PM proteome hit likely contaminant. IBA 'cytoplasmic side of mitochondrial outer membrane' derives from mammalian HK1/HK2 VDAC binding -> removed.

## Interactions
- Glk1-Emi2 (paralog) interaction in Y2H (PMID:11283351) and AE-MS (PMID:37968396); functional meaning unknown.

## Module notes
- Reviewer interpretation (uncited): Glk1 is a minor, aldohexose-specific glucose kinase relative to the hexokinases (YeastPathways reaction EC 2.7.1.1/2.7.1.2 assignment is fine). YeastPathways mannose degradation via Glk1 is chemically possible but secondary.
