# TSC13 (YDL015C; UniProt Q99190) notes

Evidence journal (no paid deep research run; built from UniProt and cached publications).

## Function
- Very-long-chain enoyl-CoA reductase (TECR orthologue; steroid 5-alpha reductase family), EC 1.3.1.93; essential, single copy [UniProt:Q99190].
- [PMID:11113186 "These results indicate that Tsc13p catalyzes a step in elongation subsequent to the condensation step."].
- [PMID:11113186 "This is consistent with the defect in Tsc13p causing reduced activity of the trans -2,3-enoyl-CoA reductase leading to accumulation of the trans -2,3-stearoyl-CoA."].
- [PMID:11113186 "Tsc13p coimmunoprecipitates with Elo2p and Elo3p, suggesting that the elongating proteins are organized in a complex"].
- Reconstitution with two reductases [PMID:17719544].

## Location
