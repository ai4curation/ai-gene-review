- Sole delta-9 acyl-CoA desaturase; ER integral membrane; essential [PMID:2674136 "This enzyme catalyzes double bond formation between carbons 9 and 10 of palmitoyl and stearoyl coenzyme A"]; [PMID:2674136 "the OLE1 gene is essential for production of monounsaturated fatty acids"].
- Homolog of rat SCD; rat SCD rescues ole1 [PMID:1978720 "determined to encode the delta 9 fatty acid desaturase enzyme"].
- C-terminal fused cytochrome b5 domain essential [PMID:8530368 "suggesting that the cytochrome b5-like domain of Ole1p plays an essential role in the desaturase reaction"]; ER integral membrane [PMID:8530368 "an intrinsic endoplasmic reticulum membrane protein"]; [PMID:12475963 "Ole1p is an integral membrane protein of the ER"].
- mdm2 = ole1 ts allele isolated in mitochondrial inheritance screen [PMID:12475963 "Temperature-sensitive mutants of the single essential acyl chain desaturase, Ole1p, of yeast have previously been isolated in screens for mitochondrial inheritance mutants"]; relocalisation of mutant desaturase precedes mitochondrial effects -> inheritance defect is downstream of lipid changes.
