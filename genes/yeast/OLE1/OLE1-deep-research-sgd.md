# SGD gene description for OLE1 (yeast)

Stearoyl-CoA 9-desaturase; required for unsaturated fatty acid biosynthesis and for mitochondrial inheritance; catalyzes the formation of a double bond at the Δ9 position of palmitic and stearic acids to generate monounsaturated palmitoleic and oleic acids; integral membrane protein of the endoplasmic reticulum; homologous of human SCD
