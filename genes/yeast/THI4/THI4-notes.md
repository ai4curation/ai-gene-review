# THI4 notes (P32318, YGR144W)

- Suicide ADP-thiazole synthase (EC 2.4.2.60): NAD+ + Gly -> ADT, S from Cys205 -> dehydroalanine; Fe(II) dependent [PMID:22031445 "the demonstration that THI4p is a suicide enzyme undergoing only a single turnover"].
- Only thiazole-forming gene product in eukaryotes [PMID:22031445].
- thi4 auxotrophy rescued by hydroxyethylthiazole [PMID:7941734]; cytosolic by fractionation [PMID:15544818]; nucleus too [UniProt:P32318 citing PMID:16850348].
- YeastPathways also assigns Thi4 to ADT hydrolysis (ADT + H2O -> AMP + thiazole-P); no supporting evidence found in cached literature; the enzyme for that step in yeast appears unresolved.
