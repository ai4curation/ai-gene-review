# SNO1 (YMR095C, UniProt Q03144) – review notes

- PdxT/SNO class-I glutamine amidotransferase glutaminase subunit; UniProt function is by similarity [UniProt:Q03144 "Catalyzes the hydrolysis of glutamine to glutamate and"].
- Co-expressed with Snz1 forms a glutaminase-active complex; Sno1 alone barely active [PMID:14764090 "the complex of Sno1p and Snz1p is a glutamine amidotransferase with the former serving as the glutaminase, although the activity was barely detectable with Sno1p alone"] -> contributes_to glutaminase activity is correct.
- In vivo: [PMID:30498136 "This result suggested that SNO1 was required for optimal PLP synthesis"]; [PMID:12271461 "SNZ1 and SNO1 were both required for growth in certain conditions"].
- Cytoplasmic in the GFP collection (PMID:14562095).
- Antiapoptosis claim (PMID:18355271): TSC22-like motif; Δsno1 is H2O2 hypersensitive [PMID:18355271 "displayed a pronounced sensitivity to the effects of H2O2 when compared to WT cells"] – pleiotropic; marked over-annotated.
- GO issues: pyridoxine metabolic/biosynthetic process -> MODIFY (vitamin B6 biosynthesis / de novo PLP biosynthesis), consistent with SNO2 review; protein binding x3 -> REMOVE.
- Module: SNO1 is the PdxT exemplar (GO:0004359); consistent. YeastPathways PLPSAL-PWY has no SNO/SNZ genes.
