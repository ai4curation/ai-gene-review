# CDS1 (YBR029C, P38221) notes

## Activity
- Essential CDP-DAG synthase (EC 2.7.7.41) [PMID:8557688 "Therefore, the essential CDS1 gene encodes the majority, if not all, of the synthase activity."]
- cdg1 hypomorph: 25% activity, reduced CDP-DAG synthesis, elevated PA [PMID:2832385 "This defect resulted in a reduced rate of CDP-diacylglycerol synthesis, an elevated phosphatidate content, and alterations in overall phospholipid synthesis."]
- Dosage alters PI:PS synthesis ratio [PMID:8557688 "an elevation in the apparent initial rate of synthesis of phosphatidylinositol relative to phosphatidylserine"]

## Location
- ER, not mitochondria; mitochondrial CDP-DAG made by Tam41 [PMID:23623749 "Cds1FLAG was, like an ER protein marker Dpm1, mainly found in the ER, but not in highly purified mitochondria"]
- Activity also in post-Golgi secretory vesicles [PMID:2163397 "CDP-diacylglycerol synthase, phosphatidylinositol synthase, and phosphatidylinositol kinase activities were associated with post-Golgi apparatus secretory vesicles destined for the plasma membrane"]

## Curation observations
- Cytosol RCA rows -> MODIFY to ER membrane.
- PS and PI metabolic process IMPs reflect CDP-DAG substrate supply; kept non-core.
- Lipid droplet formation IBA from mammalian CDS1/2 is indirect (PA partitioning); kept non-core.
