| Catalytic reaction | Apc11 is the RING component of the APC/C E3 ligase. It recruits an E2~ubiquitin conjugate and promotes direct transfer of ubiquitin to lysine residues on recruited substrates; it is not a catalytic-cysteine E3 intermediate. Apc11 supplies the RING E2-binding surface. | GST pull-down showed direct Apc11–Ubc4 binding. | Essentiality and mutant phenotype | An intact Apc11 RING-H2 domain is essential for vegetative viability and APC/C function. | Pathways and substrates | Through APC/C, Apc11 is indispensable for the metaphase–anaphase transition and mitotic exit.
