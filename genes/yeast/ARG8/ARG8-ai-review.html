Gene Description

Acetylornithine aminotransferase (ACOAT; EC 2.6.1.11) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, a pyridoxal 5'-phosphate-dependent class-III aminotransferase imported into the mitochondrial matrix via an N-terminal transit peptide. Arg8p transfers an amino group from L-glutamate to N-acetylglutamate 5-semialdehyde to form N2-acetyl-L-ornithine, the fourth reaction of the mitochondrial acetylated segment of arginine biosynthesis (ARG2/ARG5,6/ARG8/ARG7) that produces ornithine for export to the cytosolic citrulline/arginine steps. It is homologous to ornithine aminotransferases and can also transaminate ornithine in vitro, but this side activity is of little physiological importance.
