CC   -!- FUNCTION: Iron-sulfur protein that transfers electrons in a wide
CC       variety of metabolic reactions. Involved in heme A biosynthesis and in
CC       iron-sulfur cluster assembly (PubMed:10655482). Transfers electrons
CC       from adrenodoxin reductase ARH1 to heme A synthase COX15, a heme
CC       protein that catalyzes the conversion of heme O to heme A
CC       (PubMed:11248251, PubMed:11788607). Required for the de novo synthesis
CC       of Fe-S clusters on iron sulfur cluster assembly protein ISU1. Interact
CC       in its reduced state with ISU1 to productively deliver electrons for
CC       Fe-S cluster synthesis (PubMed:12970193, PubMed:15211518,
CC       PubMed:25358379). Essential for coenzyme Q biosynthesis. May transfer
CC       the electrons required for the hydroxylation reaction performed by COQ6
CC       (PubMed:20534343, PubMed:21944752). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10655482,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11248251, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11788607,
