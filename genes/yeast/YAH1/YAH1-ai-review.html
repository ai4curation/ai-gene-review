Gene Description

YAH1 (YPL252C) encodes the essential mitochondrial adrenodoxin-type [2Fe-2S] ferredoxin of S. cerevisiae. Located in the mitochondrial matrix, Yah1 is reduced by the NADPH-dependent ferredoxin reductase Arh1 and passes single electrons to several acceptors. It is required for de novo [2Fe-2S] cluster synthesis on the Isu1 scaffold (and hence for maturation of both mitochondrial and cytosolic Fe-S proteins), for the Cox15-catalysed hydroxylation of heme O to heme A, and for coenzyme Q6 biosynthesis, where it is the probable electron donor for the Coq6 ring hydroxylation. Depletion of Yah1 causes mitochondrial iron accumulation and loss of Fe-S protein activities.

Core Functions

One-electron transfer from Arh1 (NADPH) to mitochondrial acceptors: the ISC scaffold Isu1 for Fe-S cluster synthesis, heme A synthase Cox15, and the CoQ hydroxylase Coq6.

YAH1 (YPL252C, UniProt Q12184) – review notes

  • Adrenodoxin-type [2Fe-2S] ferredoxin of the mitochondrial matrix; essential PMID:10375636. Reduced by ARH1 (NADPH) and donates electrons to several acceptors [UniProt:Q12184 "Transfers electrons from adrenodoxin reductase ARH1 to heme A synthase COX15"].
  • Fe-S cluster biogenesis: PMID:10655482; PMID:12970193.
  • Heme A synthesis (electron donor to Cox15): PMID:11788607.
  • CoQ biosynthesis: PMID:20534343; PMID:21944752. Note GO:0120538 (C1 hydroxylase) and the Coq6 C5 hydroxylation use reduced ferredoxin as donor; Yah1 would feed Coq6 and possibly Coq4/C1 chemistry.
  • GO issues:
  • GO:0016653 (NAD(P)H oxidoreductase, heme protein as acceptor; IMP/IGI PMID:11788607) describes the whole Arh1-Yah1-Cox15 chain; Yah1 itself does not oxidize NAD(P)H -> MODIFY to electron transfer activity.
  • GO:0140647 P450-containing electron transport chain (InterPro2GO) – S. cerevisiae has no mitochondrial P450 PMID:9727014 -> REMOVE.
  • Not in YeastCyc CoQ pathways. Module uses YAH1 as representative of the mitochondrial ferredoxin electron supply (GO:0009055) – consistent.
