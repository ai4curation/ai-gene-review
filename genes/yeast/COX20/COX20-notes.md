- Re-read cached PMID:10671482 and PMID:31752220. PMID:31752220 supports COX20-dependent reductions in programmed-cell-death markers under oxidative stress, but that phenotype remains secondary to Cox20's Cox2/chaperone role rather than a separate conserved core function.

- Web/PubMed searches for exact yeast COX20/Cox20/YDR231C mentions in 2024-2026 did not find a newer direct *S. cerevisiae* COX20 primary paper that changed the GO review.
- Followed up on PR #3735 by spelling out the foldase-versus-single-client-assembly-chaperone issue. SGD has already selected `GO:0044183 protein folding chaperone` for yeast Cox20 after `GO:0051082 unfolded protein binding` obsoletion; this review retains that direct IDA as the best current MF term for the PMID:10671482 evidence while making explicit that Cox20 is a Cox2-specific inner-membrane assembly chaperone, not a general GroEL/TRiC-like foldase. The human COX20 review currently omits `molecular_function` because human GOA still has only `GO:0005515 protein binding` MF rows, not because evidence argues against the conserved Cox2 assembly-chaperone mechanism.
