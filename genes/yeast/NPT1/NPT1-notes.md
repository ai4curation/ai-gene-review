# NPT1 (YOR209C) notes

## Identity and activity
- Nicotinate phosphoribosyltransferase (NAPRTase, EC 6.3.4.21; ATP-stimulated; transient His phosphorylation): nicotinate + PRPP + ATP + H2O -> NaMN + ADP + Pi + PPi. First step of the Preiss-Handler / NAD salvage route.
- Yeast enzyme cloned, purified, kinetically characterised [PMID:9521740 "We have cloned, overexpressed, and purified the yeast enzyme and report its kinetic properties, which are similar to those of the bacterial enzyme"].
- Only alternative to the kynurenine pathway for the nicotinic acid moiety; npt1 is co-lethal with bna mutants and Npt1 is essential anaerobically [PMID:12062417 "incorporated directly using nicotinate phosphoribosyl transferase (NPT1)"].
- Npt1 enzymatic activity is required for Sir2-dependent silencing (conserved His mutant) [PMID:11901108 "mutation of a highly conserved histidine residue in Npt1p results in a silencing defect, indicating that Npt1p enzymatic activity is required for silencing"].
- Increased NPT1 dosage increases Sir2-dependent silencing, rDNA stability and lifespan [PMID:11884393 "increased dosage of NPT1, encoding a nicotinate phosphoribosyltransferase critical for the NAD(+) salvage pathway, increases Sir2-dependent silencing"].
- Moonlighting chaperone (overexpression; abstract-only) [PMID:33749726 "three additional enzymes of the same pathway that achieve similar protection against proteotoxic stress: Npt1, Pnc1 and Qns1"]. Not in GOA for NPT1; not proposed as NEW.

## Location
- Nucleus-concentrated in a fraction of cells [PMID:11901108 "Myc-tagged Npt1p was highly concentrated in the nucleus in approximately 40% of the cells"]; [PMID:11884393 "are both concentrated in the nucleus"]. GFP: cytoplasm + nucleus [UniProt:P39683].
