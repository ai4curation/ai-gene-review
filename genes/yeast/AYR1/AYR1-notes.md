# AYR1 (YIL124W, P40471) notes

Evidence journal for the YeastPathways triacylglycerol_biosynthesis batch. No paid deep research was run; sources are UniProt and cached publications.

- Catalytic identity: NADPH-dependent 1-acyl-DHAP reductase (EC 1.1.1.101), an SDR family enzyme. [PMID:10617610 "Heterologous expression of Ayr1p in Escherichia coli resulted in gain of ADR activity in the prokaryote, confirming that YIL124w is the structural gene of the enzyme"]
- Deletion abolishes ADR activity in lipid particles; microsomes keep about one third of the activity, so a second microsomal ADR must exist. [PMID:10617610 "microsomes contain at least one additional ADR activity"]
- The ADR activity in yeast membranes was first detected in 1992. [PMID:1512203 "Total membrane fraction of S. cerevisiae also exhibited acyl/alkyl DHAP reductase (EC 1.1.1.101) activity, which has not been reported previously."]
- Location: Ayr1 is a constitutive lipid droplet protein, also found in the ER, and by fractionation in the mitochondrial outer membrane. [PMID:24868093 "are likely to be constitutive LD proteins"] [PMID:28916712 "Upon cellular fractionation, Ayr1 was present in both mitochondrial and microsomal fractions"]
- Secondary activity: minor TAG lipase; TAG mobilisation persists without it in a tgl3/4/5 background. [PMID:24187129 "These investigations led to the identification of Ayr1p as a novel triacylglycerol lipase of yeast lipid droplets"]
- Channel claim: reconstituted Ayr1 forms an NADPH-stimulated cation channel. This comes from a single study. [PMID:28916712 "We conclude that Ayr1 forms a cation-selective channel that is stimulated by NADPH."]
- Curation decisions: ADR activity, PA biosynthesis, and the LD/ER locations are core. Lipase, TAG catabolism, the mitochondrial locations and the channel are kept as non-core. The YeastPathways RCA "cytosol" rows are removed because the protein sits on LDs and the ER.
