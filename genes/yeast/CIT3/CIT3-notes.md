# CIT3 (YPR001W, P43635) notes

- Third citrate synthase; imported into mitochondria; cit1 cit3 poor growth on glycerol [PMID:9140965 "In vitro import experiments showed that cit3p is transported into the mitochondria."].
- Dual citrate/2-methylcitrate synthase [PMID:17570335 "Cit3p had similar catalytic efficiency with both acetyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA"]; required for propionate metabolism [PMID:17570335 "The Deltacit2Deltacit3 double mutant did not grow on propionate"].
- cit3 accumulates acetate and isobutanol (propionyl-CoA inhibition of PDH proposed) [UniProt:P43635].

Curation: YeastCyc superpathway cytosol row removed. Core: 2-methylcitrate synthase in propionate catabolism (methylcitrate cycle) + backup TCA citrate synthase.
