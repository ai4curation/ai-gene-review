# HSV2 notes

## 2026-11-21 fungal PROPPIN review

HSV2/YGR223C encodes the least-characterized budding-yeast PROPPIN sibling of Atg18 and Atg21. Early localization and lipid-binding work established that Ygr223c/Hsv2 is a PI3P/PI(3,5)P2-binding beta-propeller present at endosomes and at vacuolar or perivacuolar membranes [PMID:15103325, PMID:15155809, PMID:18769150]. Structural papers on Hsv2 and ScHsv2 defined the two phosphoinositide-binding sites used by the PROPPIN fold to recognize PI3P-containing membranes [PMID:22704557, PMID:22753491].

The direct process evidence is no longer just micronucleophagy. Yasumori and Ushimaru showed that Hsv2 is required for rapamycin-induced bulk microautophagy and micronucleophagy, with HSV2 loss attenuating GFP-Pho8 and Nop1-GFP reporter processing [PMID:41806572]. Taylor et al. then showed that Hsv2 is required for autophagy of large cargos including FAS and ribosomes, localizes to the phagophore/PAS, interacts with Vps35, and mediates vacuole fission cooperatively with Atg18 [PMID:42135946].

SGD records a high-throughput fungal-type vacuole membrane row for Hsv2 from the SWAp-Tag collection [PMID:26928762]; the cached paper does not give Hsv2-specific localization text, so the review keeps the broad HDA row as non-core localization support.

The PTHR11227 root still over-propagates pexophagy, mitophagy and glycophagy to Hsv2. Direct Hsv2 evidence supports broad autophagy, bulk microautophagy, micronucleophagy, PI3P/PI(3,5)P2-dependent localization, and retromer-associated membrane fission; it does not establish Hsv2 as an agent of every Atg18- or Atg21-seeded selective cargo process.

PubMed searches on 2026-11-21 found two 2026 `S. cerevisiae` PROPPIN papers relevant to Atg21 and Hsv2, PMIDs 42135947 and 42135946, plus the 2026 Hsv2 microautophagy paper PMID 41806572, and no newer HSV2-specific Candida paper.
