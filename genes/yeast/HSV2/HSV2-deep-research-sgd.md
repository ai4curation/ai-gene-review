# SGD gene description for HSV2 (yeast)

Phosphoinositide binding protein; required for autophagy of bulk cargoes (ribosomes and the FAS complex); roles in bulk microautophagy and micronucleophagy; amphipathic loop (6C/D) required for vacuole membrane scission and protein sorting in diploids; PROPPIN family member defined by a seven-bladed beta-propeller fold and PI binding-FRRG motif; localizes to phagophore assembly site and phagophore edges; accumulates as vacuolar membrane puncta upon starvation; functional homolog of human WDR45B
