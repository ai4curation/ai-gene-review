# GCV2 notes (YMR189W, P49095)

- P-protein, glycine dehydrogenase (decarboxylating), EC 1.4.4.2, PLP enzyme [UniProt:P49095]; cloned via gsd2 [PMID:8830251 "The Saccharomyces cerevisiae GCV2 gene, encoding the P-protein has been cloned by complementation of the gsd2 mutation which prevents cells converting glycine to serine or using glycine as the sole nitrogen source"].
- Glycine-induced; GDC activity rises in glycine [PMID:8830251 "The levels of P- and L-protein transcripts and GDC activity were also elevated in cells grown in the presence of glycine"].
- Early biochemistry: [PMID:320197 "was correlated with a lack of glycine decarboxylase activity"] (abstract does not name the gene; SGD IDA attached to GCV2; deferred to curator).
