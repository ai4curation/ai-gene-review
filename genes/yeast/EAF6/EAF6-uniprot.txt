CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complex which
CC       is involved in transcriptional activation of selected genes principally
CC       by acetylation of nucleosomal histone H4 and H2A. The NuA4 complex is
CC       also involved in DNA repair (PubMed:15485911, PubMed:15353583).
CC       Component of the NuA3 histone acetyltransferase complex. The NuA3 HAT
CC       complex has 2 functionally distinct forms. NuA3a binds H3K4me3, through
CC       the PHD finger of YNG1, and acetylates H3K14 at the promoter region of
CC       actively transcribed genes to promote transcription initiation. NuA3b
CC       binds H3K36me3 at the coding regions of actively transcribed genes,
CC       through the PWWP domain of PDP3, and coordinates transcription
CC       elongation (PubMed:17157260, PubMed:25104842).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15353583, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17157260,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25104842}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complex
CC       composed of at least ACT1, ARP4, YAF9, VID21, SWC4, EAF3, EAF5, EAF6,
CC       EAF7, EPL1, ESA1, TRA1 and YNG2 (PubMed:15485911, PubMed:15353583,
CC       PubMed:27594449, PubMed:36198799). Component of the NuA3 histone
CC       acetyltransferase (HAT) complex. The NuA3 HAT complex has 2
CC       functionally distinct forms that participate in transcription. The
CC       NuA3b HAT complex contains an additional subunit, PDP3
CC       (PubMed:17157260, PubMed:25104842). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15353583,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15485911, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17157260,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25104842, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27594449,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:36198799}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14562095}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Present with 504 molecules/cell in log phase SD medium.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14562106}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the EAF6 family. {ECO:0000305}.
