# STT4 (P37297, YLR305C) notes

Evidence journal (YeastPathways phosphoinositide_biosynthesis batch, 2026-10-06).

## Activity
- Type III PI4K (PI4KA ortholog) [UniProt:P37297 "Type III PI4K"].
- stt4 deletion lacks most PI4K activity [PMID:8288577 "Cell homogenates of the stt4 deletion mutant show normal PI3-kinase activity but lack most of the PI4-kinase activity that is detected in the wild-type."].
- Wortmannin-sensitive PI4K activity in vitro [PMID:9346907 "In vitro, the PI 4-kinase activity of STT4, but not of PIK1, was potently inhibited by wortmannin."].
- YeastPathways step PI + ATP -> PI4P (EC 2.7.1.67) is correct.

## Location / complex
- Plasma membrane [PMID:12015967 "We identify a transmembrane protein that stabilizes Stt4 at the plasma membrane where it directs synthesis of PI4P"]; PIK patches with Ypp1 and Efr3 [PMID:19075114 "Ypp1 and Efr3 are required for the formation and architecture of Stt4 PIK patches"].
- Cortical ER / ER-PM contact sites [PMID:40550329 "Here we found that Stt4 localizes to the cER regions where Scs2 and Ist2 are localized abundantly"].
- Mitochondrial proteome hits (PMID:14576278, PMID:16823961) are unsupported; marked over-annotated.

## Processes
- Feeds Mss4 PI(4,5)P2 / Rom2-Rho1-Pkc1 cell wall integrity signalling [PMID:12015967 "which is required for activation of the Rho1/Pkc1-mediated MAP kinase cascade"].
- Actin, vacuole morphology, cell wall [PMID:10930462 "Stt4p activity is required for the maintenance of vacuole morphology, cell wall integrity, and actin cytoskeleton organization"].
- Autophagy and mitophagy [PMID:22977244 "Taken together, these results demonstrated that Stt4 is required for both selective and nonselective autophagy."].
- Sac1 primarily turns over Stt4-made PtdIns4P (see SAC1 notes, PMID:11514624).
