# SGD gene description for SOD1 (yeast)

Cytosolic copper-zinc superoxide dismutase; also sulfide oxidase; detoxifies superoxide and hydrogen sulfide; stabilizes Yck1p and Yck2p kinases in glucose to repress respiration; phosphorylated by Dun1p, enters nucleus under oxidative stress to promote transcription of stress response genes; abundance increases under DNA replication stress; localization to mitochondrial intermembrane space is modulated by MICOS complex; human ortholog SOD1 implicated in ALS complements a null allele
