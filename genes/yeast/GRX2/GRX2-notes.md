# GRX2 (YDR513W, UniProt P17695) notes

- Dithiol CPYC glutaredoxin; major heat-stable glutaredoxin activity: "the grx2 mutant displayed only 20% of the wild-type activity, indicating that Grx2 accounted for the majority of this activity in vivo" [PMID:9571241]; grx2 is H2O2 sensitive [PMID:9571241].
- Recombinant Grx2 "efficiently catalyses the reduction of hydroxyethyl disulphide by GSH" and "also catalyses the reduction of GSSG by dihydrolipoamide" [PMID:11958675].
- Two isoforms from alternative in-frame AUGs: "Translation from the first AUG resulted in a long isoform carrying a mitochondrial targeting presequence" and the "cytosolic isoform of Grx2 was synthesized from the second AUG" [PMID:16606613]; unprocessed form accumulates in the mitochondrial outer membrane [PMID:16606613].
- Peroxidase/GST: "Grx2 is also a general hydroperoxidase"; "both Grx1 and Grx2 are shown be active as glutathione S-transferases" [PMID:12684511].
- Backup GSSG reduction in cytosol: "we identify Trx2 and Grx2 as efficient backup systems to glutathione reductase for cytosolic GSSG reduction" [PMID:23242256].
- Mitochondrial proteomics hits: PMID:14576278, PMID:16823961, PMID:24769239.
