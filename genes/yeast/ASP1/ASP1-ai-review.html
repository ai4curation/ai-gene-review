Gene Description

L-asparaginase I of Saccharomyces cerevisiae (EC 3.5.1.1), the constitutively expressed intracellular asparaginase that hydrolyses L-asparagine to L-aspartate and ammonium. Asp1 belongs to the asparaginase 1 family and lacks a signal peptide; the recombinant enzyme is homo-oligomeric, shows sigmoidal (allosteric) kinetics with a K0.5 in the micromolar range, and requires the catalytic residues Thr64, Tyr78, Thr141 and Lys215. Asp1 allows asparagine to be used as a nitrogen source; a natural loss-of-function allele in wine strains alters fermentation rate and acetic acid production when asparagine is the main nitrogen source. It is distinct from the nitrogen-regulated, secreted cell-wall asparaginase II encoded by the ASP3 cluster.
