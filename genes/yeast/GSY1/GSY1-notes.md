# GSY1 (YFR015C, P23337) notes

Minor glycogen synthase isoform (GT3, EC 2.4.1.11). Module: glycogen_metabolism_fungal.

- Purified yeast GS has 85 and 77 kDa polypeptides; 85-kDa N-terminus matches GSY1 [PMID:2123485]
- "Disruption of the GSY1 gene produced a strain retaining about 85% of wild type glycogen synthase activity at stationary phase, while disruption of the GSY2 gene yielded a strain with only about 10%" [PMID:1908457]
- Double mutant lacks GS activity and glycogen [PMID:1908457]
- Glycogen granule ISS from Gsy2; Gsy2 binds glycogen [PMID:20555403]
