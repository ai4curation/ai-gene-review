# ADI1 (YMR009W, Q03677) notes

## Identity and activity
- Acireductone dioxygenase (ARD/MtnD), cupin fold; Fe form gives KMTB (EC 1.13.11.54), Ni form gives MTP + CO (EC 1.13.11.53) [UniProt:Q03677 "Reaction=1,2-dihydroxy-5-(methylsulfanyl)pent-1-en-3-one + O2 = 4-"].
- Genetic evidence: adi1 deletion abolishes growth on MTA-cycle substrate as sulfur source; rescued by YMR009w or human MTCBP-1 [PMID:15938715 "Disruption of the yeast ARD homolog, YMR009w gene, abolished ability of the cells to grow in this culture condition."; PMID:15938715 "Re-expression of either the YMR009w or MTCBP-1 gene restored the cell growth."].
- Verified as the ARD of the salvage cycle [PMID:18625006 "The 5'-methylthioadenosine phosphorylase was verified as Meu1p, the 2,3-dioxomethiopentane-1-phosphate enolase/phosphatase as Utr4p and the aci-reductone dioxygenase as Adi1p."].
- Ni-ARD (off-pathway) activity: only from the HAMAP family rule; no yeast data seen. IEA Ni term -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED; SGD IMP Ni term -> UNDECIDED (full text not available; growth rescue supports Fe-type reaction).

## Localization
- Cytoplasm and nucleus (Huh 2003, PMID:14562095) [UniProt:Q03677 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm. Nucleus."].

## GO term note
- GO:0033353 "L-methionine cycle" is defined around SAM/SAH/Hcy, but ADI1 acts in the MTA-specific branch. QuickGO marks the MTA-specific GO:0019509 term obsolete, so GO:0071267 is the nearest live replacement for the GOA GO:0033353 rows.
