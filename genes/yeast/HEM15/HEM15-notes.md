# HEM15 (YOR176W, P16622) notes

Ferrochelatase, terminal enzyme of heme biosynthesis.

- Activity: inserts Fe2+ into protoporphyrin IX [PMID:2185242 "encodes ferrochelatase (EC 4.99.1.1, protoheme ferrolyase), a mitochondrial inner membrane-bound enzyme which catalyzes the insertion of ferrous ion into protoporphyrin IX, the last step in protoheme biosynthesis"]; reaction heme b + 2 H+ = protoporphyrin IX + Fe2+, EC 4.98.1.1 [UniProt:P16622].
- Purified recombinant (baculovirus) enzyme active, Km for mesoporphyrin and iron as for native [PMID:1304908 "The measured Km's for the substrates mesoporphyrin and iron are the same as those reported previously for the yeast enzyme."].
- Location: matrix side of inner mitochondrial membrane [PMID:7629135 "translocated to the matrix side of the inner mitochondrial membrane"]; mislocalised (no presequence) enzyme still makes some heme but less, and b-type cytochromes drop 80% [PMID:7629135].
- Genetics: hem15 ferrochelatase-deficient mutants [PMID:7035824]; gene cloned by complementation, 31-aa presequence [PMID:2185242].
- Curation: YeastPathways RCA "cytosol" is a GO-CAM conversion default and is wrong for this mitochondrial enzyme -> REMOVE.
