CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mitochondrial isozyme of branched-chain-amino-acid
CC       aminotransferase, involved in the biosynthesis of the branched chain
CC       amino acids (BCAAs) leucine, isoleucine, and valine (PubMed:21267457,
CC       PubMed:8702755, PubMed:8798704). Catalyzes the formation of methionine
CC       from 2-keto-4-methylthiobutyrate (KMTB) in the methionine salvage
CC       pathway primarily using BCAAs (leucine, isoleucine, and valine) as the
CC       amino donors (PubMed:18625006). Appears to be involved in the
CC       regulation of the cell cycle, although this may be indirect via
CC       metabolic changes (PubMed:29850466, PubMed:37497662). Connects BCAAs
CC       and TCA-cycle metabolism governing TCA-cycle flux to activate TORC1
CC       signaling (PubMed:26659116, PubMed:37497662). High copy suppressor of a
CC       temperature-sensitive mutation in the ABC transporter, ATM1
CC       (PubMed:8798704). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18625006,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21267457, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26659116,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29850466, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37497662,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8702755, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8798704}.
