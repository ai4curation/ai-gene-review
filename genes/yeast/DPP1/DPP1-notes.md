# DPP1 (YDR284C, Q05521) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications; no paid deep research).

- Dpp1 is the vacuolar DGPP phosphatase (PAP2/LPP family). DGPP is its preferred substrate, and it also acts on PA and LPA. [PMID:8567632 "DGPP was the preferred substrate for the enzyme with a specificity constant (Vmax/Km) 10-fold greater than that for phosphatidate."]
- The null mutant lacks DGPP phosphatase activity. [PMID:9452443 "The mutant was devoid of DGPP phosphatase activity and accumulated (4-fold) DGPP."]
- Dpp1 is in the vacuolar membrane and is regulated by zinc. [PMID:11139591 "revealed that the DGPP phosphatase enzyme was localized to the vacuolar membrane."]
- It also hydrolyses isoprenoid diphosphates. [PMID:10329682 "The action of Dpp1p on isoprenyl pyrophosphates was confirmed by characterization of the hydrolysis of geranylgeranyl-P-P by the purified protein."]
- Dpp1 is not the TAG-relevant PAP. [PMID:24196957 "showed the essential role of PAH1 in triacylglycerol synthesis throughout growth"] [PMID:24196957 "are integral membrane enzymes that are localized to the vacuole and Golgi compartments, respectively"]
- Curation decisions: DGPP and PA phosphatase MFs and the vacuole membrane are core. The YeastPathways TRIGLSYN-PWY BP is marked over-annotated, because Pah1 supplies DAG for TAG. The RCA cytosol row is removed. Self-interaction and raft rows are non-core.
