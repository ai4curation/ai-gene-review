# SGD gene description for POX1 (yeast)

Fatty-acyl coenzyme A oxidase; involved in the fatty acid beta-oxidation pathway; localized to the peroxisomal matrix; component of a light sensing pathway, converting light into a hydrogen peroxide signal, modulating nucleo-cytoplasmic shuttling of stress-responsive transcription factor, Msn2p
