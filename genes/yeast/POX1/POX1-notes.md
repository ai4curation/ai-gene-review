# POX1 (FOX1, YGL205W; UniProt P13711) notes

- Acyl-CoA oxidase, EC 1.3.3.6, FAD-dependent, first (rate-setting) step of peroxisomal beta-oxidation; sole acyl-CoA oxidase gene in S. cerevisiae [PMID:2189786 "The gene (named POX1) is unique in S. cerevisiae and has been identified through homology with the POX4 and POX5 genes of Candida tropicalis"].
- Disruption impairs growth on oleate; transcript induced >25-fold by oleate [PMID:2189786 "A disruption of the POX1 coding sequence diminishes the ability of yeast cells to grow on oleic acid as a sole carbon source"].
- Reaction a 2,3-saturated acyl-CoA + O2 = (2E)-enoyl-CoA + H2O2; cofactor FAD; homooctamer [UniProt:P13711].
- Peroxisomal matrix; imported by Pex5p via an internal non-PTS1 site [PMID:11967269 "peroxisomal import of Pox1p is nevertheless dependent on binding to Pex5p, the PTS1 import receptor"].
- PHA synthesis (readout of beta-oxidation intermediates) requires acyl-CoA oxidase [PMID:12819196 "Synthesis of polyhydroxyalkanoate was dependent on the beta-oxidation enzymes acyl-CoA oxidase"].
- RPP0 interaction (PMID:37968396 AP-MS) is a high-throughput hit with an abundant ribosomal protein; no functional meaning.
- "fatty acid binding" IBA/IEA comes from the ACOX family InterPro signature; Pox1p binds acyl-CoA substrates, not free fatty acids - over-annotation.
- YeastPathways RCA cytosol is wrong (beta-oxidation is peroxisomal in yeast).
