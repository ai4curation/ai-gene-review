# ALG2 (YGL065C, UniProt P43636) notes

## Activity / role
- Bifunctional α1,3/α1,6-MT [PMID:16878994 "this enzyme actually carries out an alpha1,3-mannosylation, followed by an alpha1,6-mannosylation, to form the first branched pentasaccharide intermediate of the pathway"]
- Activity of yeast Alg2 confirmed in parallel with human Alg2 [PMID:35136180 "Recombinant hAlg2 and ScAlg2 have similar bifunctional MTase activities"]
- Membrane topology [PMID:35136180 "ScAlg2 has four hydrophobic regions, including two helices that span the ER membrane at the N-terminus and two hydrophobic sequences at the C-terminus that bind the ER but do not traverse the membrane"]
- Interacts with Alg1 [PMID:15044395 "The two Alg1-containing complexes differ from one another in that one complex contains Alg2 and the other contains Alg11."]
- Cytoplasmic face [PMID:11308030 "early mannosylation steps occur on the cytoplasmic side of the endoplasmic reticulum with GDP-Man as donor"]
