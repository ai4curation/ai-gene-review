# GPH1 (YPR160W, P06738) notes

Glycogen phosphorylase, GT35, PLP-dependent (EC 2.4.1.1). Module: glycogen_metabolism_fungal (catabolism).

- "Haploid cells disrupted in GPH1 lacked phosphorylase activity and attained higher levels of intracellular glycogen" [PMID:2657401]
- Multicopy raises activity 10-40-fold; transcription induced late exponential [PMID:2657401]
- Purified enzyme activity 4.7 U/mg (unphosphorylated); activated by phosphorylation of a single N-terminal Thr [PMID:1613787]
- "The activated enzyme is dimeric." [PMID:6354094]
- Localisation: "GFP-Gph1p is localized to distinct spots in the cytoplasm" [PMID:24146988]; growth defects on lactate/SDS and reduced PC in gph1 [PMID:24146988] (likely indirect).
- Gph1 vs Sga1 degrade distinct pools: "Gph1p and Sga1p may therefore degrade physically distinct pools of glycogen" [PMID:11486014]
