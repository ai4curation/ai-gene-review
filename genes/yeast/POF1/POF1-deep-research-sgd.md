# SGD gene description for POF1 (yeast)

Nicotinamide mononucleotide-specific adenylyltransferase (NMNAT); catalyzes the conversion of nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) to nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+); role in the nicotinamide riboside (NR) salvage pathway of NAD+ biosynthesis; involved in NR and NAD+ homeostasis; ATPase involved in protein quality control and filamentation pathways; interacts physically with Kss1p and suppresses the filamentation defect of a kss1 deletion
