# DUG1 notes (YFR044C, P43616)

- Cys-Gly metallodipeptidase, peptidase M20A family [UniProt:P43616 "SIMILARITY: Belongs to the peptidase M20A family."].
- Biochemistry: [PMID:19346245 "We show here that Dug1p is a homodimer that can also function in a Dug2-Dug3-independent manner as a dipeptidase with high specificity for Cys-Gly and no activity toward tri- or tetrapeptides in vitro."]; [PMID:19346245 "This activity requires zinc or manganese ions."]; [PMID:19346245 "Yeast cells lacking Dug1p (dug1Delta) accumulate Cys-Gly."]
- Genetics/DUG pathway: [PMID:17179087 "Although dipeptides and tripeptides with a normal peptide bond such as cys-gly or glu-cys-gly required the presence of only a functional DUG1 gene"]
- Role restricted to Cys-Gly cleavage; GSH gamma-glutamyl bond cleaved by (Dug2-Dug3)2 GATase: [PMID:22277648 "Dug1p activity in glutathione degradation was found to be restricted to its Cys-Gly peptidase activity, which functioned downstream of the (Dug2p-Dug3p)(2) GATase."]; no Dug1-Dug2 heterodimer [PMID:22277648 "the dominant population consists of Dug1p-Dug1p and Dug2p-Dug2p homodimer, with no detectable heterodimer."]
- Location: cytoplasm by GFP [PMID:17179087 "all three proteins were observed to have uniform GFP fluorescence throughout the cytoplasm"]. Mitochondrial calls are organellar proteomics/HTP only.
