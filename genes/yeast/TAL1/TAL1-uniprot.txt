CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transaldolase involved in the non-oxidative phase in the
CC       pentose phosphate pathway. Catalyzes the reversible conversion of
CC       sedoheptulose-7-phosphate and D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate into
CC       erythrose-4-phosphate and beta-D-fructose 6-phosphate. Transaldolase is
CC       important for the balance of metabolites in the pentose-phosphate
CC       pathway. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2185015}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=D-sedoheptulose 7-phosphate + D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate =
CC         D-erythrose 4-phosphate + beta-D-fructose 6-phosphate;
CC         Xref=Rhea:RHEA:17053, ChEBI:CHEBI:16897, ChEBI:CHEBI:57483,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:57634, ChEBI:CHEBI:59776; EC=2.2.1.2;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:2185015, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8109173};
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Carbohydrate degradation; pentose phosphate pathway; D-
CC       glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate and beta-D-fructose 6-phosphate from D-
CC       ribose 5-phosphate and D-xylulose 5-phosphate (non-oxidative stage):
CC       step 2/3. {ECO:0000305|PubMed:2185015}.
