CC   -!- FUNCTION: Chromatin reader component of the transcription coactivator
CC       SAGA complex. SAGA acts as a general cofactor required for essentially
CC       all RNA polymerase II transcription (PubMed:10026213, PubMed:10864329,
CC       PubMed:15647753, PubMed:25216679, PubMed:28918903). At the promoters,
CC       SAGA is required for transcription pre-initiation complex (PIC)
CC       recruitment. It influences RNA polymerase II transcriptional activity
CC       through different activities such as TBP interaction (via core/TAF
CC       module) and promoter selectivity, interaction with transcription
CC       activators (via Tra1/SPT module), and chromatin modification through
CC       histone acetylation (via HAT module) and deubiquitination (via DUB
CC       module) (PubMed:22224423). SAGA preferentially acetylates histones H3
CC       (to form H3K9ac, H3K14ac, H3K18ac and H3K23ac) and H2B and
CC       deubiquitinates histone H2B (PubMed:10026213). SAGA interacts with DNA
CC       via upstream activating sequences (UASs) (PubMed:28918903). Also
CC       identified in a modified version of SAGA named SALSA or SLIK
CC       (PubMed:15647753). The cleavage of SPT7 and the absence of the SPT8
CC       subunit in SLIK neither drive any major conformational differences in
CC       its structure compared with SAGA, nor significantly affect HAT, DUB, or
CC       DNA-binding activities (PubMed:33864814). SGF29 specifically recognizes
CC       and binds methylated 'Lys-4' of histone H3 (H3K4me), with a preference
CC       for trimethylated form (H3K4me3) (PubMed:21685874). Component of the
CC       ADA histone acetyltransferase complex, which preferentially acetylates
CC       nucleosomal histones H3 (to form H3K14ac and H3K18ac) and H2B
CC       (PubMed:10026213). SGF29 is also required for heterochromatin boundary
CC       formation function, prevention of the spread of gene silencing at the
CC       HMR locus (PubMed:24307402). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10026213,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10864329, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15647753,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21685874, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22224423,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24307402, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25216679,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28918903, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33864814}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the 1.8 MDa SAGA (Spt-Ada-Gcn5 acetyltransferase)
CC       complex, which is composed of 19 subunits TRA1, SPT7, TAF5, NGG1/ADA3,
CC       SGF73, SPT20/ADA5, SPT8, TAF12, TAF6, HFI1/ADA1, UBP8, GCN5, ADA2,
CC       SPT3, SGF29, TAF10, TAF9, SGF11 and SUS1 (PubMed:12052880). The SAGA
CC       complex is composed of 4 modules, namely the HAT (histone
CC       acetyltransferase) module (GCN5, ADA2, NGG1/ADA3 and SGF29), the DUB
CC       (deubiquitinating) module (UBP8, SGF11, SGF73 and SUS1), the core or
CC       TAF (TBP-associated factor) module (TAF5, TAF6, TAF9, TAF10 and TAF12),
CC       and the Tra1 or SPT (Suppressor of Ty) module (TRA1, HFI1/ADA1, SPT3,
CC       SPT7, SPT8 and SPT20/ADA5). The Tra1/SPT module binds activators, the
CC       core module recruits TBP (TATA-binding protein), the HAT module
CC       contains the histone H3 acetyltransferase GCN5, and the DUB module
CC       comprises the histone H2B deubiquitinase UBP8 (PubMed:25216679). Also
CC       identified in an altered form of SAGA, named SALSA (SAGA altered, Spt8
CC       absent) or SLIK (SAGA-like) complex, which contains a C-terminal
CC       truncated form of SPT7 and is missing SPT8 (PubMed:15647753). However,
CC       it has been shown that the SAGA and SAGA-like SALSA/SLIK
CC       transcriptional coactivators are structurally and biochemically
CC       equivalent (PubMed:33864814). Interacts with dimethylated and
CC       trimethylated 'Lys-4' of histone H3 (H3K4me2 and H3K4me3), with a
CC       preference for the trimethylated form (H3K4me3) (PubMed:21685874).
CC       Component of the 0.8 MDa ADA complex, a HAT complex distinct from SAGA,
CC       which at least consists of ADA2, NGG1/ADA3, AHC1, AHC2, SGF29 and GCN5
CC       (PubMed:21734642, PubMed:22224423). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12052880,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15647753, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21685874,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21734642, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22224423,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25216679, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33864814}.
