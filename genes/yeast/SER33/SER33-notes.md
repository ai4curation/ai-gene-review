# SER33 (YIL074C, P40510) notes

Evidence journal (no paid deep research; built from UniProt and cached publications).

- Paralog of SER3; both encode D-3-phosphoglycerate dehydrogenase (EC 1.1.1.95), step 1/3 of the phosphorylated serine pathway [UniProt:P40510 "Catalyzes the reversible oxidation of 3-phospho-D-glycerate"].
- Genetics: only the double mutant lacks activity [PMID:12525494 "Unlike either single mutant, the ser3Delta ser33Delta double mutant lacks detectable phosphoglycerate dehydrogenase activity and is auxotrophic for serine or glycine for growth on glucose media"].
- SER33 is the main isoenzyme on glucose and is co-regulated with SER1/SER2 by nitrogen source [PMID:12525494 "Ser33p is likely to be the main isoenzyme of the phosphoglycerate pathway during growth on glucose"].
- NAD+ cofactor [PMID:12525494 "since phosphoglycerate dehydrogenase activity requires NAD(+) as cofactor"].
- Side activity: NADH-dependent reduction of 2-oxoglutarate to D-2-hydroxyglutarate (EC 1.1.1.399; GO:0120568 is reaction-direction neutral) [PMID:26774271 "Ser3 and Ser33, which are primarily known for oxidizing 3-phosphoglycerate in the main serine biosynthesis pathway, in addition reduce α-ketoglutarate to d -2HG using NADH and represent major intracellular sources of d -2HG in yeast"]. UniProt kinetic note: kcat 0.25 s-1 for 2-oxoglutarate reduction [UniProt:P40510] — slow side reaction.
- Cytosolic; GFP survey cytoplasm [PMID:14562095]; D-2HG formation also placed in cytosol [PMID:26774271 "we propose that d -2HG is mainly formed and degraded in the cytosol of S. cerevisiae cells"].
- Interactions: Ser33-Ser3 (NbExp=6) and Ser33-Ser33 (NbExp=3) [UniProt:P40510]; paralog heteromers are common in yeast [PMID:31454312 "we find that heteromerization is frequent among duplicated homomers and correlates with functional similarity between paralogs"].
- SESAME complex membership (ComplexPortal NAS rows for oxidative stress, epigenetic regulation, catalytic complex) [PMID:26527276 "SESAME (Serine-responsive SAM-containing Metabolic Enzyme complex), which contains serine metabolic enzymes"]; these are complex-level propagations, not Ser33 activities.
- Crystal structures available (8PIN, 8PIO, 8PIR, 8PIS, 8Q2I) [UniProt:P40510].
