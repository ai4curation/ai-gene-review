# BIO4 (YNR057C, P53630) notes

## Identity
- Dethiobiotin synthetase (BioD), EC 6.3.3.3; P-loop NTPase fold; PANTHER PTHR43210; 237 aa; Mg2+ cofactor by similarity to E. coli BioD (P13000) [UniProt:P53630].
- Reaction: DAPA + CO2 + ATP -> dethiobiotin + ADP + Pi [UniProt:P53630].
- In the BIO3-BIO4-BIO5 cluster [PMID:10333520 "This mutant allowed the characterization of a bio cluster (BIO3-4-5)."].

## Evidence
- [PMID:10333520 "We demonstrate that BIO3 (YNR058w) and BIO4 (YNR057c) encode, respectively, a 7, 8-diaminopelargonic acid aminotransferase and a dethiobiotin synthase, involved in the biotin biosynthesis pathway."] (abstract only).
- No experimental localization; YeastPathways assigns cytosol by default.

## Module note
- Yeast has separate monofunctional BioA (Bio3) and BioD (Bio4), unlike plant BIO1 (BioD-BioA fusion). The DTBS IBA on Bio3 is spurious; Bio4 is the only DTBS.
