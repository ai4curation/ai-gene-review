# SCT1 / GAT2 (YBL011W, P32784) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications; no paid deep research).

- Sct1 is a dual G3P/DHAP sn-1 acyltransferase that prefers C16 acyl chains. [PMID:11544256 "In contrast, Gat2p(Sct1p) displayed preference toward 16-carbon fatty acids."]
- Deletion reduces cellular GPAT activity. [PMID:11544256 "resulted in a substantial decrease of total cellular G-3-P acyltransferase activity."]
- Sct1 is the main supplier of DAG for TAG and Kennedy-pathway PC. [PMID:19525420 "Gat2p is the primary supplier of DAG, used mainly in triacylglycerol synthesis and phosphatidylcholine synthesis through the CDP-choline pathway"]
- It is in the ER membrane. [PMID:19525420 "are in fact microsomal GPATs, localized to both perinuclear and cortical endoplasmic reticula"]
- Curation decisions: GPAT, DHAPAT and the ER membrane are accepted. The four RCA cytosol rows are removed. Unlike Gpt2, Sct1 has no LD annotation.
