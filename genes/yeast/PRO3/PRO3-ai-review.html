Gene Description

PRO3 (YER023W; ORE2) encodes delta1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate reductase (EC 1.5.1.2), which reduces P5C to L-proline with NADPH or NADH in the final step of proline biosynthesis. It is a homo-oligomeric cytosolic enzyme; its cytosolic location separates reductive synthesis from mitochondrial P5C oxidation by Put2, while regulated proline/P5C cycling can connect these compartments. Pro3 also reduces P5C formed in the cytosol from arginine via ornithine, so arginine can supply proline in pro1 and pro2 mutants. Pro3 is the only P5C reductase in yeast: pro3 mutants are strict proline auxotrophs, and temperature-sensitive alleles (ore2) arrest in G1 unless proline is supplied.

PRO3 (YER023W, P32263) notes

  • Delta1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate reductase (EC 1.5.1.2), final step of proline biosynthesis; uses NADH or NADPH; homo-oligomer [UniProt:P32263 "Catalyzes the reduction of 1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate (PCA) to"].
  • Gene-enzyme identification; overexpression raises P5CR 20-fold PMID:2824433.
  • ORE2 = PRO3: ts cdc mutants rescued by proline PMID:1508147; G1 arrest phenotype is a consequence of proline starvation.
  • Cytoplasmic; compartmentalized away from mitochondrial P5C dehydrogenase (PUT2) to avoid futile cycling PMID:7009582; GFP cytosolic PMID:25112878.
  • Arginine catabolism: ornithine-derived P5C made in the cytosol (CAR2) is reduced by Pro3 to proline, which is then degraded in mitochondria; pro1/pro2 mutants grow without proline because of this route PMID:25112878. So arginine catabolism annotation is a genuine (non-core) participation.
  • Self-interaction in three HTP interactome screens (IPI identical protein binding), consistent with homo-oligomer.
