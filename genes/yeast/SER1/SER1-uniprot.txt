CC   -!- FUNCTION: Phosphoserine aminotransferase (PSAT) is a pyridoxal 5'-
CC       phosphate-dependent enzyme involved in the second step of the
CC       phosphorylated pathway of serine biosynthesis (PubMed:1326413).
CC       Catalyzes the reversible conversion of 3-phosphohydroxypyruvate to
CC       phosphoserine and of 3-hydroxy-2-oxo-4-phosphonooxybutanoate to
CC       phosphohydroxythreonine (PubMed:1326413). Plays an indirect role in
CC       purine biosynthesis (PubMed:10509016). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10509016,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:1326413}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:

CC         L-glutamate; Xref=Rhea:RHEA:14329, ChEBI:CHEBI:16810,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:18110, ChEBI:CHEBI:29985, ChEBI:CHEBI:57524; EC=2.6.1.52;
