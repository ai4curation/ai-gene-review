# SER1 (YOR184W, P33330) notes

- Phosphoserine aminotransferase (EC 2.6.1.52), PLP-dependent, step 2/3 of the phosphorylated serine pathway [UniProt:P33330 "phosphate-dependent enzyme involved in the second step of the"].
- Genetics: [PMID:1326413 "The ser1 mutation corresponded to phosphoserine transaminase and ser2 to that of phosphoserine phosphatase"]; glycolytic route is conditional, glyoxylate route takes over on non-fermentable carbon [PMID:1326413 "The ser1 ser2 ser10 triple mutants were totally serine auxotrophic on glucose media but serine prototrophic during growth on non-fermentable carbon sources"].
- Cloning/deletion: [PMID:8017107 "evidence that its deletion from the yeast genome leads to serine dependency"].
- ade9 is a SER1 allele; adenine requirement is indirect (serine supplies glycine/one-carbon units for purine synthesis) [PMID:10509016 "In this study we demonstrate that ade9 plays an indirect role in purine biosynthesis as a non-functional allele of SER1"; "adequate exogenous serine levels mask the adenine phenotype of ade9"].
- Homodimer; crystal structure 8I28 [UniProt:P33330 "SUBUNIT: Homodimer."].
- Cytoplasm (GFP survey) [PMID:14562095]. GCN4-regulated [UniProt:P33330].
- UniProt also lists 4-phosphohydroxythreonine transamination (PLP/pyridoxine pathway in bacteria); in yeast PLP is made by SNZ/SNO, so that reaction is unlikely to be physiological here.
- Uba1 interaction rows come from HT AP-MS [PMID:11805837; PMID:37968396]; no functional interpretation.
