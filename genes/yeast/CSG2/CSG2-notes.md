# CSG2 (YBR036C) notes

UniProt P35206, 10-TM ER membrane protein; regulatory subunit of the IPC mannosyltransferase (MIPC synthase) complexes Sur1(Csg1)-Csg2 and Csh1-Csg2; ER Ca2+ release channel [UniProt:P35206].

- MIPC synthesis: [PMID:12954640 "We also revealed that Csg2p interacts with both Csg1p and Csh1p. Deletion of the CSG2 gene reduced the Csg1p activity and abolished the Csh1p activity."]; [PMID:12954640 "two distinct inositol phosphorylceramide mannosyltransferase complexes, Csg1p-Csg2p and Csh1p-Csg2p, exist"]. Catalytic activity resides in Sur1/Csg1 and Csh1, not Csg2.
- Ca2+ sensitivity of csg2/cls2 mutants; ER membrane by IF [PMID:7854312 "Immunofluorescent staining of the yeast cells expressing epitope-tagged Cls2p suggests that Cls2p is localized to endoplasmatic reticulum (ER) membrane."]. Ca2+ sensitivity due to IPC accumulation; suppressed by sphingolipid synthesis mutants [PMID:8063782 "Other scs mutants as well as the csg2 null mutant have altered sphingolipid metabolism."].
- ER Ca2+ release channel (bilayer currents), needed for starvation-induced autophagy [PMID:37349354 "Csg2 is localized at the ER membranes and functions as a calcium release channel maintaining the calcium homeostasis in ER."].

## Pathway context
- YeastPathways attaches CSG2 to IPC + GDP-mannose -> MIPC (EC 2.4.1.-). Csg2 is a non-catalytic regulatory subunit of the MIPC synthase; the transferase is Sur1/Csh1. Appropriate as complex member, not as the catalyst.
