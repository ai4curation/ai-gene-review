# SGD gene description for VTC4 (yeast)

Vacuolar membrane polyphosphate polymerase; subunit of vacuolar transporter chaperone (VTC) complex involved in synthesis and transfer of polyP to vacuole; regulates membrane trafficking; role in non-autophagic vacuolar fusion; targeted to vacuole via AP-3 pathway; protein abundance increases in response to DNA replication stress
