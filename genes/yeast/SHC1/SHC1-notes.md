# SHC1 (YER096W, UniProt P39000) notes

- Sporulation-specific paralog of SKT5/CHS4: "SHC1 is not expressed during vegetative growth but is strongly induced during sporulation as a mid-late gene" [PMID:11918806].
- shc1/shc1 ascospores "have very low levels of chitosan in their cell walls"; "Shc1p is required for proper maturation of the ascospore through its participation in the synthesis of the chitosan layer" [PMID:11918806].
- When overexpressed in vegetative cells "it can substitute Chs4p as an activator of the CSIII activity" [PMID:11918806].
- Localization: "Shc1p-GFP localized to punctate dots within the cytoplasm and then to prospore membranes in the wild type" [PMID:15755916]; unlike Chs4p, "Shc1p is not required for the localization of Chs3p to the prospore membrane" [PMID:15755916].
- Note: no GO annotation to chitosan biosynthesis; the chitosan of the spore wall is made by Chs3 followed by Cda1/Cda2 deacetylation.
