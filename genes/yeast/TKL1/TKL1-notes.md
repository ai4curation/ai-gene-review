# TKL1 notes (P23254, YPR074C)

- Transketolase EC 2.2.1.1, ThDP + Mg2+ dependent homodimer [UniProt:P23254 "Reaction=D-sedoheptulose 7-phosphate + D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate ="; "SUBUNIT: Homodimer."].
- tkl1 extracts lack activity [PMID:8299150 "Transketolase activity was below the level of detection in mutant crude extracts."].
- Aromatic amino acid requirement (E4P) [PMID:8226984 "Finally, we found that cells disrupted for TKL1 are unable to grow in the absence of aromatic amino acids."; "This is most likely due to the fact that transketolase is required for the synthesis of erythrose-4-P, a precursor of the aromatic amino acids."]. Note PMID:8299150 reports only reduced growth without aromatics - strain difference; full block only in tkl1 tkl2 [PMID:7916691 "Double mutants for both genes, TKL1 and TKL2, are auxotrophic for aromatic amino acids, indicating a complete block in the transketolase activity."].
- Glycolytic efficiency [PMID:8226984 "We found that growth on fermentable carbon sources, but not on gluconeogenic carbon sources, is reduced in cells disrupted for TKL1."].
- Abundance 40300 molecules/cell vs Tkl2 149 [UniProt:P23254; UniProt:P33315].
- Protein binding IPIs: Tkl2 (Gavin TAP-MS PMID:16429126; Marchant PCA PMID:31454312; Michaelis PMID:37968396) and Rsp5 WW domains (PMID:16606443).
