# DPM1 (SED3, YPR183W, P14020) notes

Pathway context: dolichol-phosphate sugar donor supply (YeastPathways PWY3O-123, Dol-P + GDP-Man -> Dol-P-Man + GDP, EC 2.4.1.83).

- Structural gene; essential. [PMID:3053713 "DPM1 is the structural gene for yeast Dol-P-Man synthase since Escherichia coli transformants harboring this gene express Dol-P-Man synthase activity in vitro."] [PMID:3053713 "The DPM1 gene is essential for viability in yeast since disruption of the gene is lethal."]
- Required in vivo for N-glycosylation (LLO arrests at Man5), GPI anchoring and O-mannosylation. [PMID:2146492 "dpm1 cells were unable to make the full-length precursor Dol-PP-GlcNAc2Man9Glc3 and instead accumulated the intermediate Dol-PP-GlcNAc2Man5"] [PMID:2146492 "Dol-P-Man synthase is also required in vivo for O mannosylation of protein"]
- Mechanism/structure: cytoplasmic donor GDP-Man to membrane Dol-P; divalent cations. [PMID:16549409 "where it transfers mannose from the water soluble cytoplasmic donor, guanosine 5'-diphosphate (GDP)-Man, to the membrane-bound, extremely hydrophobic, and long-chain polyisoprenoid acceptor, dolichyl-phosphate (Dol-P)."]
- Regulation: PKA phosphorylation of Ser141 raises Vmax in vitro [PMID:15548536 "DPMS catalytic activity was indeed enhanced severalfold when the recombinant protein was phosphorylated in vitro."]; aspirin inhibits [PMID:35028934 "Importantly, aspirin decreased the expression and enzyme activity of DPM1 in S. cerevisiae."]
- Tail-anchored ER protein, cytoplasmic catalytic domain; TM domain retains Sac1p in ER. [PMID:15657391 "Dpm1p is an integral ER membrane protein that synthesizes Dol-P-Man, which serves as a mannosyl donor for glycosylation reactions in the ER lumen"] [UniProt:P14020]
