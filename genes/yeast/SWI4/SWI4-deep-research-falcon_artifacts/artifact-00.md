This agrees with the supplied InterPro APSES, ankyrin-repeat, and SWI6-like annotations. | Domain deletions, point mutants, binding assays, monomer-state analysis, and co-immunoprecipitation | Baetz & Andrews (1999), [10.1128/MCB.19.10.6729](https://doi.org/10.1128/MCB.19.10.6729); Siegmund & Nasmyth (1996), [10.1128/MCB.16.6.2647](https://doi.org/10.1128/MCB.16.6.2647) (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000004) |
