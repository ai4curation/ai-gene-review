# SAM1 (YLR180W, P10659) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications).

- AdoMet synthetase 1, EC 2.5.1.6; paralogue SAM2 [PMID:3316224 "Saccharomyces cerevisiae contains two genes, SAM1 and SAM2, encoding functional S-adenosylmethionine synthetases."].
- Double mutant blocks AdoMet synthesis [PMID:17426150 "sam1(-) sam2(-) mutants, in which the conversion of methionine to S-adenosylmethionine is blocked"].
- SESAME complex (Pyk1, Ser33, Shm2, Sam1/Sam2, Acs2) supplies AdoMet for Set1 [PMID:26527276 "SESAME interacts with the Set1 H3K4 methyltransferase complex, which requires SAM synthesized from SESAME, and recruits SESAME to target genes, resulting in phosphorylation of H3T11."]. Abstract only; the oxidative-stress NAS is UNDECIDED.
- Stress granules: Sam1 is recruited to about 30% of heat-shock SGs; AdoMet suppresses SG assembly [PMID:32609300 "The regulation of yeast SG formation by AdoMet is biphasic, with chronic changes altering SG composition and acute elevation of AdoMet suppressing SG assembly."].
- YeastPathways superpathway rows: sulfur amino acid biosynthesis and methionine salvage are over-annotations (Sam1 consumes methionine). L-methionine cycle was accepted.
