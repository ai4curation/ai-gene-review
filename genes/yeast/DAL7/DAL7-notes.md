# DAL7 / MLS2 (YIR031C, P21826) notes

- Malate synthase gene identical to DAL7 (allantoin metabolism) [PMID:8462696 "identical to the one previously described as DAL7, which is involved in allantoin metabolism"]; two differentially regulated malate synthase genes (MLS1, DAL7).
- Recycles glyoxylate from ureidoglycolate hydrolysis; NCR-sensitive, catabolite-insensitive [UniProt:P21826 "Allantoin metabolism-specific malate synthase involved in the"].
- Pex9p-dependent peroxisomal import under oleate [PMID:27678487 "Pex9p acts as a cytosolic and membrane-bound peroxisome import receptor for both malate synthase isoenzymes, Mls1p and Mls2p."].
- Malate synthase can use butyryl-CoA in vitro; dal7 deletion reduces activity [PMID:23642236 "the same impairment is caused by DAL7 deletion (data not shown)"].
- Glyoxylate-cycle isozyme on C2/fatty acids is MLS1 [PMID:23642236 "MLS1 expression is repressed by glucose and induced when cells are growing on non-fermentable carbon sources"].

Curation: glyoxylate-cycle rows (IBA/IEA/RCA) kept as non-core; core = malate synthase in allantoin catabolism. YeastCyc assigns DAL7 to the glyoxylate bypass by paralogy only.
