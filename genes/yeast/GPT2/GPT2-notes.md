# GPT2 / GAT1 (YKR067W, P36148) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications; no paid deep research). Naming trap: the gene GPT2 = GAT1, but UniProt calls the protein "Glycerol-3-phosphate O-acyltransferase 2". SCT1 = GAT2 is called "...acyltransferase 1".

- Gpt2 is a dual G3P/DHAP sn-1 acyltransferase. [PMID:11544256 "both Gat1p and Gat2p(Sct1p) are G-3-P/dihydroxyacetone phosphate dual substrate-specific sn-1 acyltransferases"]
- Gat1 and Sct1 are synthetically lethal. [PMID:11544256 "null mutations of both GAT1 and GAT2(SCT1) are synthetically lethal to yeast"]
- Gpt2 is in the ER (perinuclear and cortical). [PMID:19525420 "localized to both perinuclear and cortical endoplasmic reticula in actively proliferating cells"]
- It is associated with LDs and forms oleate-induced crescents. [PMID:22267742 "These structures are connected with the endoplasmic reticulum and are intimately associated with LPs."]
- Phosphorylation restrains Gpt2 activity. [PMID:31421179 "Phosphorylation deficiency at the conserved motif increases the enzyme activity of Gpt2 and consequently enhances TAG synthesis."]
- Curation decisions: the GPAT and DHAPAT MFs, PA/TAG/phospholipid synthesis, and the ER membrane/LD locations are accepted. The four RCA cytosol rows are removed. "Regulation of triglyceride metabolic process" is marked over-annotated, because the gene product is the regulated enzyme, not a regulator.
