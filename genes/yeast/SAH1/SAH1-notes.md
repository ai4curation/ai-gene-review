# SAH1 (YER043C, P39954) notes

Evidence journal (no paid deep research; built from UniProt and cached publications).

## Identity and activity
- S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine hydrolase, EC 3.13.2.1, NAD+-binding adenosylhomocysteinase family [UniProt:P39954 "Reaction=S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine + H2O = L-homocysteine + adenosine;"].
- Only route for AdoHcy degradation in eukaryotes [PMID:18591246 "In eukaryotes, S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine hydrolase (Sah1) offers a single way for degradation of S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine, a product and potent competitive inhibitor of S-adenosyl-L-methionine (AdoMet)-dependent methyltransferases."].
- Equilibrium favours synthesis in vitro; hydrolysis driven in vivo by removal of adenosine/homocysteine [PMID:18591246 "Whereas in vitro the equilibrium of the Sah1 reaction is in the biosynthetic direction"].

## Phenotypes
- SAH1 knockdown: AdoHcy accumulation, reduced de novo PC synthesis, TG accumulation [PMID:18591246 "Here we show that down-regulation of SAH1 expression in yeast leads to accumulation of S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine and decreased de novo PC synthesis in vivo."].
- Essential when sulfur assimilation is blocked (met25 background) [PMID:18591246 "it indicated to us that the essential function of Sah1 may be related to methionine or cysteine homeostasis"].
- PC/TG effects are indirect (AdoHcy inhibits Cho2/Opi3 methylation) -> PC kept non-core, TG marked over-annotated.
