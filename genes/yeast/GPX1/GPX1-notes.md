# GPX1 notes (YKL026C, P36014)

- Atypical 2-Cys peroxiredoxin of the GPx family [PMID:20572871 "Here, we show that Gpx1 is also an atypical 2-Cys peroxiredoxin, but uses glutathione and thioredoxin almost equally."]; EC 1.11.1.24 and 1.11.1.9 [UniProt:P36014].
- Peroxisomal matrix; oleate/Msn2/4 induction; peroxisome formation: [PMID:22659048 "We found that Gpx1 was located in the peroxisomal matrix."]; [PMID:22659048 "Peroxisome biogenesis was impaired in gpx1∆ cells, and subsequently, the growth of gpx1∆ cells was lowered in oleic acid-containing medium."]
- Earlier GPx/PHGPx view: [PMID:10480913 "whereas that of the GPX1 gene was induced by glucose starvation."]; [PMID:11445588 "Studies with cloned GPX1, GPX2, and GPX3 expressed heterologously in Escherichia coli confirmed that these genes encode proteins with PHGPx activity."]
- Mitochondrial outer membrane location asserted in UniProt citing PMID:22659048 (full text not seen).

## Curation observations
- Unlike Gpx2/Gpx3 (thioredoxin-preferring), Gpx1 uses GSH about equally, so GO:0004602 is retained as core alongside GO:0140824.
- PHGPx activity kept non-core; peroxisome organization IMP kept non-core (mechanism undefined).
