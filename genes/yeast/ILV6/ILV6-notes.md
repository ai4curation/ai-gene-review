# ILV6 notes (YCL009C, P25605)

- Regulatory (small) subunit of mitochondrial AHAS [UniProt:P25605].
- Stimulates Ilv2 and confers valine inhibition [PMID:10213630 "Reconstitution studies showed that the ilv6 protein stimulates the catalytic activity of the ilv2 protein by up to 7-fold"; "confers upon it sensitivity to inhibition by valine"].
- Mitochondrial with transit peptide removal [PMID:10213630 "YCL009c was shown to be expressed in yeast and the protein localized in mitochondria where it undergoes removal of a transit peptide targeting sequence."]
- Deletion abolishes valine feedback inhibition [PMID:8972574 "Using a yeast strain carrying a complete deletion of YCL09C, we have observed the loss of such retro-inhibition."]
- Fungal-specific insert forms MgATP site [PMID:16390333 "Five of the mutations abolished MgATP activation and are suggested to constitute the binding site for this modulator."]
- Mitochondrial nucleoid association from an mtDNA-associated protein screen [PMID:15692048 "We have identified 22 mtDNA-associated proteins in yeast"] (kept as non-core).
