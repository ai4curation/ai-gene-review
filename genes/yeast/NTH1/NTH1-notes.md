# NTH1 (YDR001C, P32356) notes

- Cytosolic neutral trehalase, GH37, EC 3.2.1.28; Ca2+-binding domain [UniProt:P32356].
- Activation: PKA phosphorylation (Ser60, Ser83) + 14-3-3 (Bmh1/Bmh2) binding [PMID:22320399 "both yeast 14-3-3 isoforms form a stable complex with phosphorylated Nth1 and significantly enhance its enzymatic activity"; PMID:17916074 "Bmh1p and Bmh2p bind to the amino-terminal region of phosphorylated trehalase, thereby modulating its enzymatic activity"]; structure of Nth1-Bmh1 [PMID:29087344].
- Cloned by complementation of trehalase-deficient mutant [PMID:8444853]; review [PMID:9308367 "The neutral trehalase is responsible for intracellular hydrolysis of trehalose"].
- nth1 deletion raises trehalose [PMID:23597844]; trehalases degrade trehalose in desiccated cells [PMID:25456447].
- Y2H interaction with DCS1 (3 screens) - function unknown.
