# GSY2 (YLR258W, P27472) notes

Major glycogen synthase (GT3, EC 2.4.1.11). Module: glycogen_metabolism_fungal.

- Predominant isoform: "The GSY2 gene product appears to be the predominant glycogen synthase with activity linked to nutrient depletion" [PMID:1908457]
- Tetramer; G6P sensed by Arg583/Arg587 [PMID:20876143]
- Glycogen-binding surface sites: "Mutation of these surface sites decreased glycogen binding and catalytic efficiency toward glycogen" [PMID:21835915]
- Pho85-Pcl10 phosphorylates Ser654/Thr667 [PMID:9584169]
- Localisation depends on glycogen; "In the absence of glycogen, Gsy2p translocates into the nucleus" [PMID:20555403]
- Binds glycogen granules, co-localises with Glc3, Gph1 [PMID:38832010]
- EXP for GS activity cites Neurospora GNN paper (abstract only) [PMID:15680913]; deferred to curator.
