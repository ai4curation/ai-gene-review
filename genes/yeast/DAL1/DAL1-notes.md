# DAL1 (YIR027C, P32375) notes

Pathway context: allantoinase (EC 3.5.2.5), first step of allantoin degradation.

- Cloned/sequenced [PMID:1803816 "The allantoinase (DAL1) gene from Saccharomyces cerevisiae has been cloned, sequenced, and found to encode a 472 amino acid protein"]; inducer-independent, NCR-sensitive expression.
- dal1 is the allantoinase structural gene; pathway proceeds via allantoate, urea, allophanate [PMID:4604238 "allantoin degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae proceeds exclusively through the intermediate formation of allantoic acid, urea, and allophanic acid"].
- Binuclear Zn by similarity, carboxylated Lys [UniProt:P32375].
- YeastPathways ids: ALLANTOINDEG-PWY = superpathway of allantoin degradation in yeast; PWY-5694 = allantoin degradation to glyoxylate I; PWY-5697 = allantoin degradation to ureidoglycolate I (urea producing); PWY-5703 = urea degradation I (deduced by matching GOA with/from to the coordinator's pathway list across DAL1/DAL2/DAL3/DUR1,2).
- Decisions: purine nucleobase catabolic process IBA marked over-annotated (S. cerevisiae lacks urate oxidase; allantoin is exogenous; GO allantoin catabolism is not under purine catabolism). Cobalt ion binding IEA marked over-annotated. Generic hydrolase terms and zinc binding non-core.
