# ATH1 (YPR026W, P48016) notes

- Acid trehalase, GH65, EC 3.2.1.28; type II single-pass membrane glycoprotein [UniProt:P48016].
- Purified ATH is the ATH1 product [PMID:8764988 "We have purified ATH and shown that it is the ATH1 gene product"]; deletion eliminates acid trehalase activity [PMID:7502577].
- Localization: MVB sorting to vacuole lumen via TMD [PMID:17475771], but >90% of activity is extracellular [PMID:15128531 "more than 90% of the activity of Ath1p is extracellular"], and cell-surface Ath1 is required for growth on trehalose; vacuolar confinement prevents growth [PMID:19703229 "the physiological significance of the cell-surface localization of Ath1 was established"].
- Hog1-dependent induction in saline stress; role in intracellular trehalose mobilization [PMID:19016884].

Pathway context: trehalose degradation (trehalose + H2O -> 2 glucose). Verified; Ath1 = extracellular/periplasmic route; Nth1 = cytosolic route.
Curation: 'trehalose transport' IMP (PMID:19016884) -> MODIFY to trehalose catabolic process (Ath1 is a hydrolase; transport is Agt1). Vacuolar locations kept as non-core.
