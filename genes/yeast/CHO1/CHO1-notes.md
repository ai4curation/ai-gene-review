# CHO1 (YER026C, P08456) notes

## Activity
- PS synthase (EC 2.7.8.8) purified from microsomes [PMID:6088519 "Membrane-associated phosphatidylserine synthase (CDP-diacylglycerol:L-serine O-phosphatidyltransferase, EC 2.7.8.8) was purified from the microsomal fraction of Saccharomyces cerevisiae"]
- cho1 mutants make no PS; require Etn/Cho [PMID:6988386 "The lipid composition suggests that cho1 mutants do not synthesize phosphatidylserine under any growth conditions."]
- Disruption abolishes PSS activity [PMID:3031455 "Disruption of the CHO1 gene resulted in the simultaneous disappearance of 1.2-kb RNA and PSS activity."]

## Location
- Microsomes/ER [UniProt:P08456]; activity also in OMM fractions [PMID:3005242 "Glycerolphosphate acyltransferase, phosphatidylinositol synthase, and phosphatidylserine synthase were exposed at the cytosolic surface of the outer mitochondrial membrane."] - likely MAM.

## Autophagy
- cho1 deletion blocks autophagosome formation [PMID:41176028 "autophagosomes were absent in the cytosol, and macroautophagic bodies were not observed in the vacuoles of the starved cho1∆ mutant"]; but increases microlipophagy in presence of Etn [PMID:41176028 "numerous microautophagic vesicles containing lipid droplets were observed in the vacuoles of cho1∆ mutants starved in the presence of Etn"]. Direction of the GOA "positive effect" on regulation of microlipophagy unclear -> UNDECIDED.
