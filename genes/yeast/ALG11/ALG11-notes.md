# ALG11 (YNL048W, P53954) notes

Pathway context: cytoplasmic-face LLO assembly (Man3 -> Man4 -> Man5GlcNAc2-PP-Dol, two alpha-1,2 mannoses; EC 2.4.1.131).

- Mutant phenotype. [PMID:11278778 "Here, we describe alg11, a new yeast glycosylation mutant that is defective in the last step of the synthesis of the Man(5)GlcNAc(2)-PP-dolichol core oligosaccharide on the cytosolic face of the ER."]
- In vitro dual activity. [PMID:16878994 "it was thus possible to define the biochemical function of Alg11, which is to catalyze the next two sequential alpha1,2-mannosylations."] [PMID:19929855 "catalysing the sequential transfer of two alpha1,2-linked mannose residues from GDP-mannose to Man3GlcNAc2-PP-Dol and subsequently to Man4GlcNAc2-PP-Dol"]
- ER localisation. [PMID:11278778 "The ALG11 gene is predicted to encode a 63.1-kDa membrane protein that by indirect immunofluorescence resides in the ER."]
- Complex with Alg1. [PMID:15044395 "one complex contains Alg2 and the other contains Alg11"]

Curation observations
- Protein binding (Alg1) removed; RCA cytosol -> GO:0098554.
