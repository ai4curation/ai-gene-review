# HFA1 (YMR207C, P32874) notes

- Mitochondrial acetyl-CoA carboxylase paralog of ACC1 [PMID:14761959 "HFA1 encodes a specific mitochondrial acetyl-CoA carboxylase providing malonyl-CoA for intraorganellar fatty acid and, in particular, lipoic acid synthesis"].
- hfa1 null phenocopies mtFAS mutants: no growth on lactate/glycerol, lipoic acid <10% [PMID:14761959 "Whereas ACC1 is involved in cytoplasmic fatty acid synthesis, the phenotype of hfa1Delta disruptants resembles that of mitochondrial fatty-acid synthase mutants"].
- MTS present and needed; Western blot places Hfa1p in mitochondria [PMID:14761959 "the HFA1-encoded protein was specifically assigned by Western blotting of appropriate cell fractions to the mitochondrial compartment"].
- MTS-less HFA1 complements acc1 (artificial cytosolic retargeting) [PMID:14761959 "the signal sequence-free HFA1 gene complemented the mutational loss of cytoplasmic acetyl-CoA carboxylase"] -> shows catalytic equivalence, not a native cytosolic role.
- No AUG near 5' end; upstream non-AUG initiation required for function/targeting [UniProt:P32874 CAUTION]. A C-terminal GFP fusion expressed from the annotated ORF may therefore partly mislocalise (possible explanation of cytoplasm HDA).
- YeastPathways places HFA1 in cytosolic FA biosynthesis pathways (PWY3O-10, PWY3O-5962, PWY3O-6336) - compartment wrong; HFA1 belongs to mtFAS (with MCT1, CEM1, OAR1, HTD2, ETR1, ACP1).
