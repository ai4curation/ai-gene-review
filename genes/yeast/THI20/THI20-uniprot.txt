CC   -!- FUNCTION: Trifunctional protein with both thiamine biosynthetic and
CC       degradative activity (PubMed:15967475). Within the thiamine
CC       biosynthesis pathway, catalyzes the phosphorylation of
CC       hydroxymethylpyrimidine (HMP) to hydroxymethylpyrimidine phosphate
CC       (HMP-P), as well as of HMP-P to HMP-PP (PubMed:10383756,
CC       PubMed:15967475). Also has thiaminase II activity and degrades thiamine
CC       using water as the nucleophile, resulting only in the formation of HMP
CC       (4-amino-2-methyl-5-hydroxymethylpyrimidine) and Thz (4-methyl-5-
CC       thiazole ethanol) (PubMed:15967475). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10383756,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15967475}.
