Gene Description

Sur1 (Csg1/Bcl21; YPL057C) is the catalytic subunit of an inositol phosphorylceramide (IPC) mannosyltransferase of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. It is a multi-pass membrane glycosyltransferase of the GT32 (OCH1-like, DXD-motif) family that transfers mannose from GDP-mannose to the inositol of IPC, forming mannosylinositol phosphorylceramide (MIPC) (EC 2.4.1.370). Sur1 acts as a heterodimer with the regulatory subunit Csg2; a paralog, Csh1, forms a second Csh1-Csg2 enzyme with different ceramide-backbone specificity, so loss of SUR1 alone lowers but does not abolish MIPC synthesis, while csg1 csh1 double mutants make no mannosylated sphingolipids. MIPC is the obligatory precursor of the major yeast sphingolipid M(IP)2C. Mannosylation is dispensable for growth on rich medium, but csg1 mutants accumulate IPC and are killed by high extracellular Ca2+, a phenotype linked to altered cell wall synthesis.

SUR1 (CSG1/BCL21; YPL057C, P33300) notes

Identity and activity

  • GT32 (OCH1-like) glycosyltransferase; catalytic subunit of IPC mannosyltransferase, IPC + GDP-Man -> MIPC (EC 2.4.1.370) [UniProt:P33300] PMID:9323360.
  • Similarity to OCH1 PMID:9323360.
  • Redundant paralog CSH1 (YBR161W) with different substrate specificity; csg1 csh1 double mutant has no mannosylated sphingolipids PMID:12954640.
  • Two complexes with regulatory subunit Csg2 PMID:12954640.

Location

  • UniProt: membrane, multi-pass (ECO:0000305). GOA: Golgi (NAS, PMID:12954640); vacuole/vacuolar membrane only from HTP datasets (PMID:26928762, PMID:19001347).

Curation decisions

  • PTHR32385 PAINT places GO:0000030 mannosyltransferase activity and GO:0051999 mannosyl-inositol phosphorylceramide biosynthetic process on PANTHER:PTN001285482, the fungal IPC mannosyltransferase node. The generic MF IBD row is seeded by CGD MIT1, SGD SUR1/CSH1, and A. fumigatus Q4WDG6; the process IBD row is seeded by the three S. pombe imt paralogs and A. fumigatus Q4WDG6.
  • The 2022 N-glycan paper is newer direct S. cerevisiae family context: Sur1 carries an N-glycan on Asn224 that helps determine the activity toward specific MIPC subtypes PMID:35713525.
  • Protein binding (IPI with CSG2) removed as uninformative; captured by mannosyltransferase complex (GO:0031501).
  • GO:0006688 glycosphingolipid biosynthetic process and GO:0030148 sphingolipid biosynthetic process are true but broad parents of the exact MIPC biosynthesis step, so the review modifies them to GO:0051999.
  • GO:0006676 M(IP)2C metabolic process is retained as non-core downstream pathway context: Sur1 makes MIPC, the precursor that Ipt1 converts to M(IP)2C, but Sur1 does not catalyze the M(IP)2C-forming step.
  • Module note: SUR1 and CSH1 are paralogous catalytic subunits, both requiring CSG2; YeastPathways reaction (IPC + GDP-Man -> MIPC) is correct.
