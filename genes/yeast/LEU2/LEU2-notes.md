# LEU2 notes (YCL018W, P04173)

- 3-isopropylmalate dehydrogenase, EC 1.1.1.85, NAD+-dependent, step 3 of Leu branch [UniProt:P04173].
- Genetic/biochemical: [PMID:5724969 "Stocks with lesions in three loci (le-2, le-3, and le-9) were primarily blocked in the third step of the pathway, catalyzed by beta-isopropylmalate dehydrogenase."]
- Cytosolic [PMID:4355481, soluble fraction with G6PDH marker].
- Promiscuous activity on beta-ethylmalate [PMID:23642236 "The second coupled reaction, catalyzed by the Leu2 activity, utilizes the produced β-ethylmalate to generate α-ketovalerate and releasing NADH as reduced equivalent."]
