Gene Description

THR4 (YCR053W) encodes threonine synthase (EC 4.2.3.1). This pyridoxal 5'-phosphate (PLP)-dependent enzyme of the fold-type II (tryptophan synthase beta-like) family catalyses the final step of threonine biosynthesis: gamma-elimination of phosphate from O-phospho-L-homoserine followed by beta-addition of water to give L-threonine. The 2.7 A crystal structure shows an active monomer of three domains, with PLP covalently bound at the domain interface. Threonine synthase activity in yeast extracts depends on THR4. thr4 mutants are threonine auxotrophs. Genetic suppression and homoserine-challenge experiments implicate toxic intermediate accumulation in their pleiotropic stress and serum sensitivities and reduced survival in mouse infection. Thr4p is a soluble cytoplasmic protein. Nuclear localization and rDNA-silencing phenotypes have also been reported; rescue of the silencing defect by threonine indicates that the phenotype can reflect metabolic supply.
