# SGD gene description for HDA1 (yeast)

Catalytic subunit of class II HDA1 histone deacetylase complex; role in azole resistance via Hsp90p, and in heat shock response; Hda1p interacts with Hda2p-Hda3p subcomplex to form an active tetramer; deletion increases histone H2B, H3 and H4 acetylation; the HDA1 histone deacetylase complex regulates aging through trehalose metabolism
