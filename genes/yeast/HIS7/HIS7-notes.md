# HIS7 (YBR248C, P33734) notes

Pathway context: YeastPathways HISTSYN-PWY, step 5 (EC 4.3.2.10, IGP synthase; glutaminase EC 3.5.1.2 half-reaction).

- Bifunctional HisH-HisF fusion: [PMID:8366040 "the yeast HIS7 gene is able to suppress His auxotrophy of corresponding Escherichia coli hisH and hisF mutants."]
- Enzymology: [PMID:10733892 "Imidazole glycerol phosphate (IGP) synthase is a glutamine amidotransferase that catalyzes the formation of IGP and 5-aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribonucleotide (AICAR)"]; [PMID:10733892 "HIS7 shows a low basal level glutaminase activity that can be enhanced 1000-fold in the presence of a nucleotide substrate or analog."]; uses ammonia as substrate unlike E. coli enzyme.
- Structure: ammonia tunnel [PMID:11591353 "An ammonia tunnel through the (beta/alpha)(8) barrel connects the glutaminase docking site at the bottom to the cyclase active site at the top."]; monomer [UniProt:P33734].
- Junction with purine synthesis via AICAR [PMID:10733892 "This enzyme represents a junction between histidine biosynthesis and de novo purine biosynthesis."]. Hence the RCA "nucleotide biosynthetic process" is marked over-annotated (not removed) for HIS7 only.
- pentosyltransferase activity (IEA) is a GO parent of GO:0000107 -> MODIFY.
