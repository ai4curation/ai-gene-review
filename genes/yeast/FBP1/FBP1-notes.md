# FBP1 (YLR377C, P09201) notes

## Activity / role
- FBPase EC 3.1.3.11 [UniProt:P09201 "Reaction=beta-D-fructose 1,6-bisphosphate + H2O = beta-D-fructose 6-"]; AMP allosteric inhibitor [UniProt:P09201 "AMP acts as allosteric inhibitor."].
- Purified enzyme phosphorylated by PKA, stimulated by Fru-2,6-P2, ~50% loss of activity [PMID:6304022 "The phosphorylation of fructose 1,6-bisphosphatase lowers its activity by about 50%."].
- Gluconeogenesis essential, no bypass [PMID:6321183 "Mutants were unable to grow on gluconeogenic carbon sources."], [PMID:6321183 "showing that there is no bypass in yeast for this enzymatic step"].
- Null mutant cannot grow on ethanol [PMID:2841162 "Constructed fbp1::HIS3 null mutants were unable to grow with ethanol, and growth was restored after transformation with the cloned fbp gene."].

## Location and regulation
- Soluble cytosol [PMID:196563 "these three enzymes of gluconeogenesis are most probably located in the soluble cytosol"].
- Catabolite degradation via Gid complex/proteasome or vacuole [PMID:12686616 "Two FBPase degradation pathways have been described, one dependent on the cytosolic ubiquitin-proteasome machinery"].
- Periplasmic FBPase in prolonged starvation (Chiang lab) [PMID:22833678 "Ultrastructural and cell extraction studies indicate that substantial amounts of FBPase were in the extracellular fraction (periplasm) during glucose starvation."].
- ROS/aging phenotype of fbp1 deletion is indirect [PMID:16199065 "through either direct signalling or an intricate adaptation in energy metabolism"].
