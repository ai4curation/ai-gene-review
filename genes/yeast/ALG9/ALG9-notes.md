# ALG9 (YNL219C, UniProt P53868) notes

## Activity / role
- Two α1,2-Man steps (7th and 9th mannose) [PMID:15987956 "we show that the dolichylphosphomannose-dependent ALG9 mannosyltransferase is the exception from this rule and is required for the addition of two different alpha-1,2-linked mannose residues to the LLO"]
- Lumen-oriented [PMID:15987956 "This report completes the list of lumen-oriented glycosyltransferases required for the assembly of the LLO."]
- Null phenotype [PMID:8692962 "deletion of the gene led to accumulation of lipid-linked Man6GlcNAc2 in vivo and to hypoglycosylation of secreted proteins"]
