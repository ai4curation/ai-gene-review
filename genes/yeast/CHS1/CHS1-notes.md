# CHS1 (YNL192W, P08004) notes

- Class I chitin synthase, EC 2.4.1.16, 7 TM, zymogen requiring proteolytic activation [UniProt:P08004].
- Repair/auxiliary enzyme: chs1 null buds lyse at acidic pH due to chitinase damage at the birth scar [PMID:2523889 "it is concluded that damage to the cell wall is caused by excessive chitinase activity at acidic pH, which can normally be repaired through chitin synthesis by Chs1."]; cannot replace Chs2 in septum formation [PMID:2523889 "Chs1 cannot substitute for Chs2 in septum formation."]
- Localization: plasma membrane and chitosomes [PMID:7952171 "Chs1 and Chs2 were both present in chitosomes and plasma membrane"]; [PMID:8970154 "We found that Chs1p and Chs3p resided partly in chitosomes"]
- QRRRW arginine mutations in Chs1 abolish activity [PMID:9760183].

Curation decisions: 'septum digestion after cytokinesis' IMP marked over-annotated (Chs1 repairs, not digests). Cytosol RCA removed. Hexosyltransferase IEA -> chitin synthase activity.
