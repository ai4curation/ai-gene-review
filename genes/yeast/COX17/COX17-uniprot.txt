CC   -!- FUNCTION: Copper metallochaperone involved in the assembly of
CC       cytochrome c oxidase (respiratory chain complex IV, CIV)
CC       (PubMed:8662933). Binds two copper ions and delivers them to
CC       metallochaperone SCO1, which relays the copper to the Cu(A) site on the
CC       cytochrome c oxidase subunit II (COX2), and to metallochaperone COX11,
CC       which relays the copper to the Cu(B) site on the cytochrome c oxidase
CC       subunit I (COX1) (PubMed:15199057, PubMed:8662933, PubMed:9585572).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15199057, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8662933,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9585572}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion intermembrane space
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22984289, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9407107}.
