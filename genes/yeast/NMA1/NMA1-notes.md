# NMA1 (YLR328W) notes

## Identity and activity
- NMN/NaMN adenylyltransferase 1 (EC 2.7.7.1 and 2.7.7.18). Catalyses ATP + NMN -> NAD+ + PPi, and ATP + NaMN -> NaAD + PPi.
- YLR328W identified as the structural gene of purified yeast NMNAT by peptide sequencing and recombinant expression [PMID:10428462 "Through data-base search a full matching was found between the sequence of tryptic fragments and the sequence of a hypothetical protein encoded by the S. cerevisiae YLR328W open reading frame"].
- Native baker's yeast enzyme: homotetramer of ~50 kDa subunits; Km NMN 0.19, NaMN 5 mM [PMID:3013296 "composed of four apparently identical Mr 50 000 subunits"; "The Km values for ATP, NMN, and nicotinate mononucleotide are 0.11 mM, 0.19 nM, and 5 mM, respectively"]. So NaMN is a poorer substrate in vitro, but Nma1/Nma2 are the only cytosolic/nuclear enzymes able to supply NaAD for Qns1 in the de novo and Preiss-Handler routes.
- Kornberg 1948 classic description of reversible NMNAT ("DPN pyrophosphorylase") from yeast [PMID:18098602, abstract-only title].
- Paralog NMA2 (YGR010W) arose from the whole-genome duplication; NMA1-NMA2 interaction repeatedly detected (IntAct; PMID:10688190, 16429126, 18719252, 31454312, 37968396) and Nma1 self-interaction (PMID:18467557 etc.) consistent with tetramer. PMID:31454312 is a study of structural pleiotropy and retention of WGD paralogs (heteromerizing paralogs).
- Overexpression of NMA1 increases telomeric and rDNA silencing [PMID:11884393 "Additional copies of the salvage pathway genes, PNC1, NMA1, and NMA2, increase telomeric and rDNA silencing"].
- Moonlighting chaperone (overexpression, abstract-only): [PMID:33749726 "Overexpression of NMNAT/Nma1, an enzyme in the NAD+ biosynthetic salvage pathway, acts as an efficient suppressor of proteotoxicities in yeast, fly and mouse models"]. Not in GOA for NMA1; not proposed as NEW here (abstract-only, overexpression).
