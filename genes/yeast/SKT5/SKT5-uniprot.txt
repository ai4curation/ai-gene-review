CC       (PubMed:9234668). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9234668}.
CC   -!- PTM: Farnesylation is required for chitin synthase CHS3 activity but is

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17142567}.

CC       (PubMed:9234668). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9234668}.
