CC   -!- FUNCTION: In complex with CCZ1, is required for multiple vacuole
CC       delivery pathways including the cytoplasm to vacuole transport (Cvt),
CC       autophagy, pexophagy and endocytosis. The MON1-CCZ1 complex acts at the
CC       fusion of vesicles with the vacuole, through its regulation of the
CC       SNARE complex during the coordinated priming and docking stages of
CC       fusion, and particularly at the stage of tethering/docking
CC       (PubMed:12134085, PubMed:12364329, PubMed:12387888, PubMed:14662743,
CC       PubMed:15721293, PubMed:32391792, PubMed:37463208). The MON1-CCZ1
CC       complex is recruited to membranes enriched in charged lipids,
CC       particularly phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PtdIns[3]P), by GTP-
CC       associated small GTPase RAB5 homologs (YPT10, YPT52, YPT53 and VPS21)
CC       (PubMed:32391792). The MON1-CCZ1 complex recruits GDP-associated small
CC       GTPase YPT7 to membranes and acts as a guanine nucleotide-exchange
CC       factor (GEF), promoting nucleotide-exchange on YPT7 and triggering
CC       endosomal maturation by recruiting downstream effectors such as
CC       components of the HOPS tethering complex (PubMed:20797862,
CC       PubMed:32391792). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12134085,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12364329, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12387888,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14662743, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15721293,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20797862, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32391792,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37463208}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: The YPT7 guanine nucleotide-exchange factor (GEF)
CC       activity of the MON1-CCZ1 complex is autoinhibited by the N-terminal
CC       disordered region of MON1 (PubMed:37463208). Inhibited by
CC       phosphorylation by YCK3 (PubMed:32391792).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32391792, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37463208}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the MON1-CCZ1 guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor
CC       complex made up of MON1 and CCZ1 (PubMed:12364329). Interacts with
CC       YPT10; this interaction has a strong preference for the GTP-associated
CC       form of YPT10 (PubMed:37463208). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12364329,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37463208}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       P53129; P38273: CCZ1; NbExp=5; IntAct=EBI-23945, EBI-21608;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Endosome, multivesicular body membrane
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:12387888}; Peripheral membrane protein. Prevacuolar
CC       compartment membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12364329}; Peripheral membrane
CC       protein. Vacuole membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12364329,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14662743}; Peripheral membrane protein. Note=The
CC       association of the MON1-CCZ1 complex with the vacuole is regulated by
CC       the C-Vps/HOPS complex. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14662743}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The disordered N-terminal is involved in autoinhibition of the
CC       MON1-CCZ1 complex guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37463208}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated by YCK3; this modification inhibits the YPT7
CC       guanine nucleotide-exchange activity of the MON1-CCZ1 complex, possibly
CC       by preventing recruitment by RAB5 homologs.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32391792}.
