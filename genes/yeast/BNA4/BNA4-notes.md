# BNA4 (YBL098W, P38169) notes

## Identity
- Kynurenine 3-monooxygenase (KMO), EC 1.14.13.9, FAD-dependent aromatic-ring hydroxylase family; mitochondrial outer membrane [UniProt:P38169].

## Evidence
- bna4 deletion abolishes 3-HK and QUIN [PMID:15806102 "As predicted, no 3HK or QUIN was detected in cells lacking Bna4 ( Figure 3a )."].
- Recombinant S. cerevisiae KMO crystallised with FAD and inhibitor UPF 648 [PMID:23575632 "We therefore turned to Saccharomyces cerevisiae KMO, which is related to human KMO (38 % identity and 51 % similarity)."]; inhibitor binding uncouples NADPH oxidation to H2O2 [PMID:23575632 "Binding UPF 648 was found to accelerate hydrogen peroxide formation by a factor of ~20-fold compared to reactions in absence of UPF 648"]. H2O2-forming NAD(P)H oxidase activity is an uncoupled side-reaction, not a physiological function.
- Mitochondrial outer membrane proteome [PMID:16407407]; peroxisome proteome, with reduced 3-HK in deletion [PMID:36164978 "such as a reduction in 3‐hydroxykynurenine in the deletion of the kynurenine 3‐monooxygenase, Bna4"].
