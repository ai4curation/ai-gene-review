Gene Description

BCY1 encodes the cAMP-binding regulatory subunit of budding-yeast protein kinase A. Bcy1 homodimerizes and binds Tpk1, Tpk2, and Tpk3 catalytic subunits to form the inactive PKA holoenzyme; cAMP binding relieves that inhibition and releases active catalytic kinase. Bcy1 is distributed between the nucleus and cytosol in a carbon-source- and phosphorylation-regulated manner, with additional chromatin and plasma-membrane-associated pools, and it gates Ras/cAMP/PKA signaling outputs that control nutrient-responsive growth, stress physiology, starvation responses, P-body assembly, and transcriptional programs.
