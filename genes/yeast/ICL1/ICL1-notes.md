# ICL1 (YER065C, P28240) notes

- icl1 deletion: no ICL activity, no growth on ethanol [PMID:1551398 "It did not have any isocitrate lyase activity and lacked the ability to grow on ethanol as the sole carbon source."].
- Purified homotetramer, Km isocitrate 1.4 mM [PMID:3059712 "composed of four identical subunits"].
- Cytosolic, not peroxisomal [PMID:8870676 "found solely in the cytoplasmic fractions, even under growth conditions that induce peroxisome proliferation"]; [PMID:9370278 "only located in the cell cytoplasm"].
- Degraded in vacuole via Vid pathway after glucose re-addition [PMID:20457600 "isocitrate lyase and phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase, were also degraded in the vacuole via the Vid pathway"].
- Also cleaves 2-methylisocitrate in vitro (EC 4.1.3.30) [UniProt:P28240].

Curation: PAINT IBA peroxisome removed (yeast-specific exception). Vacuole kept as non-core (degradation). Methylisocitrate lyase non-core.
