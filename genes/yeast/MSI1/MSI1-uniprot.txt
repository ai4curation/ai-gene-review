CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as a component of the histone chaperone complex
CC       chromatin assembly factor 1 (CAF-1), which assembles histone octamers
CC       onto DNA during replication and repair (PubMed:9030687). CAF-1 performs
CC       the first step of the nucleosome assembly process, bringing newly
CC       synthesized histones H3 and H4 to replicating DNA; histones H2A/H2B can
CC       bind to this chromatin precursor subsequent to DNA replication to
CC       complete the histone octamer (By similarity). Plays a role in the
CC       maintenance of heterochromatin (By similarity). Independently, MSI1 is
CC       involved in regulation of the RAS/cAMP pathway via sequestration of
CC       NPR1 (PubMed:11238915). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9Y825,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11238915, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9030687}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of chromatin assembly factor 1 (CAF-1), composed of
CC       MSI1/p50, CAC2/p60 and CAC1/p90 (PubMed:11238915). Interacts with
CC       protein kinase NPR1 (PubMed:11238915). Interacts with RTT106
CC       (PubMed:19683497). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11238915,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19683497}.
