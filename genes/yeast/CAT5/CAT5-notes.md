# CAT5 / COQ7 (YOR125C, P41735) notes

Demethoxy-Q6 (DMQ6) hydroxylase, di-iron carboxylate protein.

- Mitochondrial inner membrane, directly in UQ biosynthesis; CAT (catabolite) phenotype secondary to respiration loss [PMID:9452453 "We show here that Coq7p/Cat5p is a mitochondrial inner membrane protein directly involved in ubiquinone biosynthesis"].
- Peripheral, matrix side [PMID:24406904 "These results indicated that Coq2 is an integral membrane protein while Coq7 behaves as a peripheral membrane protein."].
- Di-iron hydroxylase motif [PMID:11435415]. DMQ hydroxylation is control point [PMID:19002377]. Activated by Ptc7p dephosphorylation [PMID:23940037 "Ptc7p is a mitochondrial phosphatase that activates coenzyme Q 6 biosynthesis by Coq7p dephosphorylation."].
- Structural role in complex (Coq3/Coq4 stability) [PMID:16624818 "suggests both an enzymatic activity and a structural role for the yeast Coq7"].
- Two MF representations: GO:0008682 (quinol, EC 1.14.99.60; yeast EXP) vs GO:0160224 (NADH + quinone, EC 1.14.13.253; HAMAP/IBA, human evidence). Core = GO:0008682; 160224 kept non-core.
