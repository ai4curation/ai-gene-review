# ACS2 notes (P52910, YLR153C)

Evidence journal for the ACS2 review (module: pdh_bypass_acetyl_coa_synthesis).

- Activity EC 6.2.1.1 [UniProt:P52910]; lower affinity than Acs1p, no propionate [PMID:8910545 "The Km for acetate of Acs1p was about 30-fold lower than that of Acs2p and Acs1p, but not Acs2p, could use propionate as a substrate."]
- Essential on glucose (sole ACS under repression / anaerobiosis) [PMID:8910545 "A mutant strain lacking Acs2p was unable to grow on glucose in batch cultures"; "In anaerobic glucose-limited cultures, only the ACS2 gene was expressed."]
- Nuclear acetyl-CoA for histone acetylation [PMID:16857587 "Rapid deacetylation after Acs2p inactivation suggests nuclear acetyl-CoA synthesis is rate limiting for histone acetylation."]
- Predominantly nuclear incl. nucleolus [PMID:19618123 "find that Acs2p is localized primarily to the nucleus, including the nucleolus, with a minor amount in the cytosol"]
- SESAME complex subunit [PMID:26527276 "which contains serine metabolic enzymes, SAM (S-adenosylmethionine) synthetases, and an acetyl-CoA synthetase."]
