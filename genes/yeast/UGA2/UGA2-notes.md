# UGA2 (YBR006W, P38067; alias UGA5) notes

Pathway context: GABA shunt step 3, succinate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase (EC 1.2.1.16).

- Recombinant enzyme: tetramer, SSA-specific, NAD+ or NADP+ [PMID:25092794 "The recombinant protein was highly specific for succinate semi-aldehyde (Km = 15.48 ± 0.14 µM) and could use both NAD(+) and NADP(+) as co-factors"]; NAD+ preferred (Km 579 uM vs ~1 mM NADP+; UniProt notes 2.5-fold lower activity with NADP+) [UniProt:P38067]. Inhibited by AMP/ADP/ATP.
- Gene identified as UGA5 and H2O2-inducible [PMID:11031268 "The gene encoding succinate semialdehyde dehydrogenase (UGA5) was identified and found to be induced by H(2)O(2) exposure"].
- Required for GABA as N source [PMID:3888627].
- Cytoplasm (HTP, PMID:14562095).
- Core MF chosen as GO:0009013 [NAD(P)+] since both cofactors work; specific NAD+ and NADP+ child terms also accepted (EXP).
