CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the oxidation of succinate semialdehyde to
CC       succinate. Can utilize both NAD(+) or NADP(+) as a coenzyme, but has a
CC       2.5-fold lower activity with NADP(+) than with NAD(+)
CC       (PubMed:25092794). Functions in a gamma-aminobutyrate (GABA)
CC       degradation pathway that allows growth utilizing GABA as a nitrogen
CC       source (PubMed:3888627). Functions in the GABA shunt, which allows to
CC       bypass 2 reactions in the TCA cycle by removing alpha-ketoglutarate
CC       from the cycle and feeding succinate and NADH back into the cycle
CC       (Probable). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25092794, ECO:0000269|PubMed:3888627,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:3888627}.
