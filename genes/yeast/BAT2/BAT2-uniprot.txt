CC   -!- FUNCTION: Cytoplasmic isozyme of branched-chain-amino-acid
CC       aminotransferase, which catalyzes the first reaction in the catabolism
CC       of the essential branched chain amino acids (BCAAs) leucine,
CC       isoleucine, and valine (PubMed:21267457, PubMed:8702755,
CC       PubMed:8798704). Catalyzes the formation of methionine from 2-keto-4-
CC       methylthiobutyrate (KMTB) in the methionine salvage pathway primarily
CC       using BCAAs (leucine, isoleucine, and valine) as well as lysine and
CC       proline as the amino donors (PubMed:18625006). Involved in cell cycle
CC       regulation (Probable). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18625006,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21267457, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8702755,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8798704, ECO:0000305|PubMed:8798704}.
