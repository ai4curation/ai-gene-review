CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the ceramide synthase complex that catalyzes the
CC       transfer of the acyl chain from acyl-CoA to a sphingoid base, with high
CC       selectivity toward hexacosanoyl-CoA (C26:0-CoA) (PubMed:11694577,
CC       PubMed:15692566, PubMed:37953642, PubMed:39528796). N-acylates
CC       sphinganine and phytosphingosine bases to form dihydroceramides and
CC       phytoceramides, respectively (PubMed:11694577, PubMed:15692566).
CC       Redundant with LAG1 (PubMed:39528796). Facilitates ER-to-Golgi
CC       transport of GPI-anchored proteins. Has a lower affinity for
CC       phytosphingosine (PHS) than dihydrosphingosine (DHS); PHS is required
CC       for the synthesis of phytoceramides and the formation of nuclear
CC       envelopes (PubMed:31164445). Along with LAG1, plays a role in
CC       pheromone-induced MAP kinase-activation of mating and formation of
CC       diploid cells (PubMed:26726837). May also play a role, together with
CC       LAG1, in the polarized membrane distribution of phosphatidylinositol
CC       4,5 biphosphate required for STE5 localization to the plasma membrane
CC       (PubMed:26726837). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10198056,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11387200, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11694577,
