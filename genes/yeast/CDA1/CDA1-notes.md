# CDA1 (YLR307W, Q06702) notes

Evidence journal compiled from UniProt and cached publications (no paid deep research run).

- Chitin deacetylase, EC 3.5.1.41; signal peptide 1-24; CE4/NodB polysaccharide deacetylase domain [UniProt:Q06702].
- CDA1 and CDA2 together account for all chitin deacetylase activity; redundant and equal contributors [PMID:8940152 "The two genes are functionally redundant, each contributing equally to the total chitin deacetylase activity."]
- GAL-driven expression gives activity and chitosan in vegetative cells; double deletion abolishes activity in sporulating diploids [PMID:9133736 "A diploid strain deleted to both ORFs, when sporulated, did not show deacetylase activity."]
- Required for ascospore wall assembly (chitosan layer; dityrosine layer depends on it) [PMID:10544249 "we show how their elimination directly affects the ascospore wall assembly."]; [PMID:8940152 "mutant spores are relatively sensitive to hydrolytic enzymes, ether, and heat shock"]
- GFP-Cda1 at prospore membrane in a sporulation localization screen [PMID:24390141].
- Substrate chitin is made by Chs3p during sporulation [PMID:1293886 "Chemical measurements showed that this mutant does not synthesize chitosan during sporulation."]
