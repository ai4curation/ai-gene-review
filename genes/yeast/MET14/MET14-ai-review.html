Gene Description

Met14 is the adenylyl-sulfate kinase (APS kinase, EC 2.7.1.25) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. It phosphorylates the 3'-hydroxyl of adenosine 5'-phosphosulfate (APS), made by the ATP sulfurylase Met3, using ATP, to give 3'-phosphoadenylyl sulfate (PAPS). PAPS is the activated sulfate that the PAPS reductase Met16 reduces to sulfite in the sulfate assimilation pathway that leads to sulfide, homocysteine, methionine, cysteine and glutathione. Met14 is a small, soluble, P-loop kinase in the cytoplasm. The purified yeast enzyme is a dimer whose activity rises under reducing (thiol) conditions. met14 mutants are unable to assimilate sulfate and need organic sulfur.
