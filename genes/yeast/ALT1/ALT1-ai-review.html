Gene Description

Mitochondrial matrix alanine aminotransferase (EC 2.6.1.2) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, a homodimeric PLP-dependent class-I aminotransferase that reversibly transfers the amino group between L-alanine and 2-oxoglutarate (L-alanine + 2-oxoglutarate = pyruvate + L-glutamate) by a ping-pong mechanism. Alt1 is the sole route of both alanine synthesis and alanine catabolism during respiratory growth, and is mainly catabolic during fermentative growth, when alanine is made by another route. ALT1 is induced by alanine. Its whole-genome duplication paralog Alt2 is cytosolic and has no detected alanine aminotransferase activity in the reported assays; its alternative physiological role is unresolved.
