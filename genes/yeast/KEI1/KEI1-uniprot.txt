CC   -!- FUNCTION: Regulatory component of the inositol phosphorylceramide (ICP)
CC       synthase which catalyzes the addition of a phosphorylinositol group
CC       onto ceramide to form inositol phosphorylceramide, an essential step in
CC       sphingolipid biosynthesis. Helps the medial Golgi localization of IPC
CC       synthase in a COPI vesicle-dependent manner.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19726565}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the inositol phosphorylceramide synthase complex
CC       composed of at least AUR1 and KEI1. Interacts (via C-terminal region)
CC       with COP1 and SEC21. Note=The interaction with AUR1 seems to occur with
CC       the full-length protein before cleavage by KEX2 since both full-length
CC       and short chains of KEI1 interact with AUR1.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19726565}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       Q06346; P36107: AUR1; NbExp=3; IntAct=EBI-38431, EBI-2046036;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Golgi apparatus membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14562095, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19726565}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14562095,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19726565}.
CC   -!- PTM: The precursor protein is cleaved into two polypeptide chains,
CC       KEI1N and KEI1C. The cleavage is performed in the Golgi apparatus by
CC       the KEX2 protease which recognizes residue Arg-135. Generation of KEX2
CC       cleavage site may have been an accidental event in evolution without
CC       specific advantages or disadvantages in IPC synthesis.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Present with 3420 molecules/cell in log phase SD medium.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14562106}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the KEI1 family. {ECO:0000305}.
