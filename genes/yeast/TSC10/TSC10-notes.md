# TSC10 (YBR265W, P38342) notes

## Identity and activity
- SDR-family 3-ketosphinganine reductase, NADPH-dependent, EC 1.1.1.102 [PMID:9804843 "These data indicate that Tsc10p is necessary and sufficient for catalyzing the NADPH-dependent reduction of 3-ketosphinganine to dihydrosphingosine."].
- Essential without exogenous LCB [PMID:9804843 "3-Ketosphinganine reductase (Tsc10p) is essential for growth in the absence of exogenous dihydrosphingosine or phytosphingosine."].
- Used as positive control for bacterial 3-KDSR discovery [PMID:35667415 "3-KDSR (TSC10) and confirmed the conversion of 3-KDS to SA"].

## Location / topology
- Integral ER membrane protein with cytoplasmic catalytic domain [PMID:19141869 "In contrast, protease digestion studies showed that the N terminus of Tsc10p is cytoplasmic."].
- Also found on lipid droplets [PMID:24868093 "However, we newly identified six proteins (Cab5, Rer2, Say1, Tsc10, YKL047W, and YPR147C) as localizing to LDs"].
- Mitochondrial outer membrane only from HTP proteomics (PMID:16407407) - kept non-core.

## Curation decisions
- RCA cytosol -> MODIFY to ER membrane. Human ortholog KDSR/FVT1 complements tsc10.
