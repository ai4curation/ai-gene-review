DSF2 (P38213, YBR007C): does the C-terminal Sel1-like repeat region form a conserved peptide-binding groove?

Hypothesis slug: kgap-dsf2-sel1-repeat-groove Source: genes/yeast/DSF2/DSF2-ai-review.yaml, focus type free_text Organism: Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C (NCBITaxon:559292) Target: DSF2 / YBR007C / UniProt P38213, 736 residues Analysis date: 2026-11-23

Summary

The seed hypothesis contains two claims. The first is that DSF2's C-terminus is a Sel1-like/TPR repeat solenoid. That claim holds. Residues 548-731 are the only ordered part of the 736-residue protein (mean pLDDT 89.9 over this window against 50.9 for the whole chain in AF-P38213-F1-model_v6), they have a 37-residue repeat period, and two consecutive repeats superpose on Helicobacter pylori HcpC at 2.95 Å gapless CA-RMSD over 75 residues, against 5.06-5.50 Å for three TPR domains and 11.21 Å for a myoglobin negative control. The fold is Sel1-like (SLR, 36-38 residue repeats) and not canonical 34-residue TPR.

The second claim, that this region forms a conserved concave peptide-binding groove characteristic of protein-interaction TPR/Sel1 domains, is refuted by four independent measurements. The repeat array is irregular: helix-pair spacings run 30, 36, 36, 44, 50, 38, 37 and 41 residues with a 24-residue helix-free gap at 619-643, and the median CA-RMSD of each repeat unit on the next is 6.58 Å against 0.72 Å for HcpC, 1.14 Å for HcpB, 0.96 Å for Hop TPR1 and 1.46 Å for PP5. There is no groove to measure: a buriedness detector that recovers 100% of the MEEVD peptide atoms in Hop TPR2A after the peptide is deleted gives DSF2 a largest cavity of 295 Å3, which is half the 567 Å3 Hop peptide site, a fifth of the 1439 Å3 Hrd3 substrate face, and no larger than the 272 Å3 returned for myoglobin. No peptide-recognition class can be assigned: none of the five Hop TPR2A carboxylate-clamp positions has a basic or amide counterpart in DSF2, and the concave face is 56% hydrophobic with net charge +1, where HcpC and HcpB are both net −2 with the acidic cluster they use for side-chain recognition. The conservation signal is a burial artefact: concave-face enrichment at p = 0.020 (AUC 0.70) univariate falls to p = 0.117 once relative solvent accessibility enters as a continuous covariate, and the most conserved exposed residues are inter-repeat glycines that maintain repeat packing.

Two premises inside the seed hypothesis are themselves wrong and are documented here. DSF1 (P0CX08, YEL070W) is a mannitol dehydrogenase, not a DSF2 paralog; the two share a name only because both were recovered in the same mpt5 deletion-suppressor screen (PMID: 16328373). And the one experimental result that could have argued for a clamp-bearing TPR, co-purification of the Hsp70s SSA2 and SSB1, dissolves on inspection: SSB1 has 3494 distinct partners in IntAct and SSA2 has 2442, with 730 and 955 of those from the single TAP-MS study that reported them with DSF2. The curatorial consequence is that DSF2's Sel1-repeat architecture is worth recording as a domain annotation and licenses no molecular function term.

Executive judgment

Verdict: partially supported for the fold, refuted for the groove.

The fold half of the hypothesis is correct and can be sharpened. DSF2 548-731 is a Sel1-like repeat region, closer to HcpC, HcpB and Hrd3 than to carboxylate-clamp TPR domains, with a 37-residue period rather than the 34-residue TPR period. UniProt and InterPro independently assign IPR006597 (Sel1-like), SMART SM00671, SUPFAM SSF81901 (HCP-like) and Gene3D 1.25.40.10, which agrees with the structural measurement.

The groove half fails on four counts, each with a positive and a negative control:

  1. No regular solenoid. A continuous concave groove is built from equivalent positions on consecutive repeats. DSF2's array has at most three units in register (those starting at 643, 665, 681 and 702, repeating on one another at 3.8-3.9 Å), and even those are five times worse than consecutive HcpC units at 0.72 Å. The N-terminal half of the domain (548-642) is irregular helical structure interrupted by a 24-residue insert.
  2. No groove. The largest concave cavity is 295 Å3 with principal extents 13.2 × 8.9 × 2.1 Å. It is the shallowest feature in the panel; every Sel1 and TPR comparator has a third axis of 3.5-5.2 Å. 74% of its 46 lining residues lie in the last repeat (681-721) or the C-terminal cap (722-731), and only 26% lie on the concave repeat face defined independently by curvature. The cavity is a local feature of the capping region.
  3. No recognition chemistry. The structurally equivalent residues to Hop TPR2A's clamp (K229, N233, N264, K301, R305) are C650, Y654, L658, M660, M684, C685 and W691. Transferring MEEVD into the DSF2 frame gives 10-16 heavy-atom clashes under 2.6 Å, against 0 in the TPR2A self-control. The Sel1 chemistry is absent too: HcpC reads side chains with a hydrophobic pocket plus a cluster of negatively charged residues (PMID: 15223324), and DSF2's face has only 8% acidic residues at net +1.
  4. No conserved patch. Across 199 ascomycete orthologs, concave-face membership adds nothing significant to conservation once burial is controlled (OLS coefficient +13.3, 95% CI −3.3 to +29.9, p = 0.117, n = 184). The 15 most conserved exposed residues include six glycines at inter-repeat turns and are not spatially clustered (mean pairwise CB distance 27.0 Å against a permutation null of 26.1 ± 1.8 Å, p = 0.66, 5000 draws).

Caveats that bound this verdict. All structural results rest on an AlphaFold model; no experimental structure of DSF2 or of any close homolog exists. A groove detector measures apo geometry, and some sites open only on ligand engagement, so "no detectable groove" is weaker than "a groove is present". The negative result applies to the groove model specifically. DSF2 could still bind a protein through a flat surface, through induced fit, or through the disordered 1-547 region, which this analysis does not address.

Key findings

F001. Residues 548-731 are a Sel1-like repeat region with a 37-residue period

The ordered part of DSF2 is confined to the C-terminal quarter. In AF-P38213-F1-model_v6, the smoothed pLDDT exceeds 70 over 184 residues from 548 to 731, with a mean of 89.9, while the whole-chain mean is 50.9 and UniProt marks disorder at 1-46, 178-208, 229-410 and 440-461. A DSSP-like hydrogen-bond assignment implemented from the Kabsch-Sander electrostatic criterion gives ten helices in this window: 547-559, 563-575, 579-591, 599-610, 615-619, 643-656, 665-677, 681-692, 702-714 and 722-726.

Structural self-superposition of 28-residue CA windows locates a minimum RMSD at sequence offset 37 (1.60 Å over the best three placements; the next-best offsets, 36 and 38, give 3.80 and 3.85 Å). The screw decomposition at that period gives a twist of 39° and a rise of 9.2 Å. Running the identical procedure on reference structures gives HcpC (1OUV) period 36, 0.45 Å, 42°, 8.0 Å; HcpB (1KLX) period 36, 0.87 Å, 44°, 8.4 Å; Hop TPR2A (1ELR) period 34, 1.59 Å, 45°, 4.8 Å; PP5 TPR (1A17) period 34, 0.63 Å, 48°, 7.8 Å. The 37-residue period and 9.2 Å rise are the Sel1-like repeat signature; canonical TPR is 34 residues.

Cross-superposition confirms the family assignment. The best gapless CA-RMSD of DSF2 643-717 (two repeats, 75 residues) onto each reference is HcpC 2.95 Å, HcpB 3.05 Å, Hrd3 (6VJZ chain A) 3.68 Å, Hop TPR1 5.16 Å, PP5 TPR 5.06 Å, Hop TPR2A 5.50 Å and myoglobin 11.21 Å. A single repeat unit (643-680, 38 residues) gives 1.36 Å onto Hrd3 and 2.49 Å onto HcpC. The superhelical radius of curvature is 18.1 Å, between HcpC at 24.9 Å and HcpB at 14.9 Å.

F008. The repeat array is irregular, so only about three units are in register

This finding came from re-deriving the geometry with a second, independent method in the final iteration, and it qualifies F001. Instead of sliding-window autocorrelation, repeat units were defined from helix-pair boundaries: in a Sel1/TPR solenoid each repeat is a helix pair, so the spacing between helix i and helix i+2 is the period, and superposing unit k on unit k+1 measures how regular the array is.

The two methods agree on the period. The helix-pair median is 37 residues, matching the autocorrelation result. They disagree on regularity, and the boundary method shows why. DSF2's individual spacings are 30, 36, 36, 44, 50, 38, 37 and 41 residues, a range of 30-50 that includes a 24-residue stretch between 619 and 643 containing no helix at all. Unit-on-unit CA-RMSD along the array is 7.16, 11.24, 6.58, 11.50, 5.52, 3.88 and 3.81 Å, with a median of 6.58 Å. The same procedure on HcpC gives 13 spacings of 32-39 residues, 11 of which are exactly 36, and a median unit-on-unit RMSD of 0.72 Å. HcpB gives 1.14 Å, Hop TPR1 0.96 Å and PP5 1.46 Å.

The structural reading is direct. DSF2 contains Sel1-like repeats, and locally two of them fit HcpC at 2.95 Å, but the array as a whole is not a regular superhelix. Only the units starting at 643, 665, 681 and 702 repeat on one another at 3.8-3.9 Å, and even those are five times worse than consecutive HcpC units. An extended concave groove requires equivalent positions on consecutive repeats to line up along a common axis. With roughly three usable units and no regular register, DSF2 cannot present the groove the hypothesis proposes. This is consistent with where the only measurable cavity turned out to sit: in the 681-731 region, at the C-terminal end of the regular stretch.

F005. The largest concave cavity is shallow, off-face and no larger than a non-binding control

Groove geometry was measured on a single scale with a detector built and validated for this purpose. Grid points just outside the van der Waals surface (probe 2.0 Å) were scored by buriedness, the fraction of 42 Fibonacci-sphere rays that strike protein within 14 Å, retained at buriedness ≥ 0.55 on a 1.2 Å grid, and clustered with networkx. Validation: applied to Hop TPR2A (1ELR chain A) with the bound MEEVD peptide deleted, the top cluster was 567 Å3 and recovered 100% of the MEEVD peptide atoms within 5 Å. The detector finds a real peptide site when one exists.

DSF2 is the shallowest structure in the panel: its third principal extent is 2.1 Å, against 3.5-5.2 Å for every Sel1 and TPR comparator. Its volume is half the validated Hop peptide site, a fifth of the Hrd3 substrate surface, and within noise of a protein with no peptide groove. Of the 46 residues with CA within 8 Å of the cluster, 74% lie in the last repeat (681-721) or the C-terminal cap (722-731), and only 26% lie on the concave repeat face. Restricting the measurement to the regular repeats alone (643-731) halves the volume again to 143 Å3.

An earlier, stricter pocket detector (LIGSITE-style PSP, 1.0 Å grid, 3.0 Å probe, PSP ≥ 5) found no enclosed pocket larger than 58 Å3 in DSF2 548-731, none at all in HcpC or PP5, and 224 Å3 in myoglobin. Both detectors, one tuned for buried pockets and one for shallow grooves, place DSF2 at or below the level of a globular control.

F002. No carboxylate clamp, and a hydrophobic concave face with the wrong charge

In 1ELR, the Hop TPR2A residues within 4.5 Å of the bound Hsp90 MEEVD peptide are K229, E230, N233, Y236, K237, K239, F241, Y248, T260, T263, N264, A267, F270, E271, Q298, K301, R305, N308 and D334. Gapless structural alignment of DSF2 643-717 onto TPR2A places DSF2 C650, G651, Y654, G657, L658, M660, H662, L669, V681, M684, C685, G688, W691 and S692 at the positions of K229, E230, N233, Y236, K237, K239, F241, Y248, T260, T263, N264, A267, F270 and E271. None of the five clamp residues that form the two-carboxylate anchor (K229, N233, N264, K301, R305) has a basic or amide counterpart in DSF2.

The peptide-transfer test makes the consequence concrete. Superposing a 75-residue DSF2 segment on TPR2A and carrying MEEVD into the DSF2 frame produces 10-16 heavy-atom clashes under 2.6 Å and only 89-96 contacts under 4.5 Å. The TPR2A self-control gives 0 clashes and 189 contacts; HcpC gives 24-26 clashes and 172-188 contacts. DSF2 neither accepts the peptide nor provides the chemistry to hold it.

Concave-face composition, taken from inner-helix side chains pointing at the curvature centre, separates DSF2 from both reference classes:

Hop TPR2A's face is basic, which is what an EEVD-binding clamp requires. HcpC and HcpB are net negative, and the HcpC crystal structure attributes side-chain recognition to a hydrophobic pocket together with a cluster of negatively charged residues (PMID: 15223324). DSF2 has neither, which is why no peptide-recognition class can be assigned from its surface. The DSF2 concave-face residues are I551, I555, R558, H559, T580, L583, L587, R590, H591, A644, P645, Y648, E649, M652, L655, K656, V681, D682, M684, C685, L686, T689, I690, W691 and S692.

F003. Concave-face conservation is a burial artefact

The ortholog set was 199 ascomycete species, one sequence per species, from semi-global BLOSUM62 alignment of DSF2 548-736 at Needleman-Wunsch score ≥ 150. Close Saccharomycetaceae orthologs (Torulaspora delbrueckii, T. globosa, Zygotorulaspora mrakii, Lachancea lanzarotensis, L. nothofagi, Eremothecium gossypii, E. sinecaudum, Kluyveromyces marxianus, K. dobzhanskii, Arxiozyma heterogenica) are 43-62% identical over the repeat region; filamentous ascomycetes are 29-34% identical.

Univariately, the concave face does look conserved. Face positions beat the rest of the domain (n = 25 against 159, medians 15.1% against 7.0%, Mann-Whitney p = 0.037, AUC 0.63) and beat other solvent-exposed positions more convincingly (n = 13 against 94, medians 30.7% against 4.5%, p = 0.020, AUC 0.70).

The signal does not survive the obvious confounder. Buried residues are conserved for packing reasons, so relative solvent accessibility must enter the model as a continuous covariate rather than a binary split. OLS of percent identity on rSASA plus a concave-face indicator gives an rSASA coefficient of −26.6 (p = 0.011) and a concave-face coefficient of +13.3 (95% CI −3.3 to +29.9, p = 0.117), R2 = 0.051, n = 184. Concave-face rSASA does not differ from the rest of the domain (medians 0.3 against 0.3, p = 0.58), so the face is not systematically more buried, but burial is what carries the conservation.

The identity of the conserved residues points the same way. The 15 most conserved exposed positions are L587, G592, G594, G659, G561, G716, W724, D682, G721, K727, K729, K699, H591, H550 and I555. Six are glycines at inter-repeat turns, which is the Sel1 repeat consensus that maintains packing, and only 4 of 15 lie on the concave face. They are not spatially clustered: mean pairwise CB distance 27.0 Å against a permutation null of 26.1 ± 1.8 Å drawn 5000 times from exposed positions, p = 0.66. A ligand-binding site would cluster.

The 620-642 insertion also obstructs the N-terminal half of the putative groove. Residues 635-644 make 15 heavy-atom contacts under 5 Å with concave-face residues 580, 583, 644, 645, 648 and 684.

F006. All reported DSF2 interactions are low-specificity, and the Hsp70 hits are frequent flyers

IntAct via PSICQUIC, queried 2026-11-23, returns 14 binary records for P38213 and no more. Six come from one TAP-MS dataset (PMID: 16429126): SSA2, SSB1, TEF1, PAB1, VMA2 and RVB1, all scored "association" (MI:0914) and never "direct interaction". SSB1 reappears in a second TAP study (PMID: 19536198) alongside PAC10. The rest are CRM1 by pull-down (PMID: 26673895), HRT1/RBX1 by pull-down (PMID: 19882662) and RAD17 by two-hybrid array (PMID: 41935050).

The Hsp70 hits deserved a specific test, because Hop and CHIP TPR domains bind the Hsp70 C-terminal EEVD motif, so an Hsp70 co-purification is exactly what a clamp-bearing protein would show. Counting each partner's total IntAct degree settles it:

F004 and F007. The paralog premise in the hypothesis is wrong

The hypothesis asks for conservation "across DSF2 orthologs (including the paralog Dsf2-like proteins)". No such paralog group exists.

A UniProt query for S. cerevisiae S288C proteins carrying Sel1-like repeats (InterPro IPR006597 or SMART SM00671) returns exactly five proteins: SKT5/CHS4 (P34226, PTHR46430:SF1), SHC1 (P39000, PTHR46430:SF1), ACK1 (Q07622, PTHR46430:SF3), HRD3 (Q05787, PTHR11102:SF160) and DSF2 (P38213, PTHR43628:SF11). DSF2 is the only yeast protein in PTHR43628. The genuine Sel1-repeat paralog group in yeast is CHS4/SHC1/ACK1, and DSF2 sits outside it by family assignment and by sequence identity: ACK1 ranks 476 of 501 ascomycete homologs of the DSF2 repeat region, with Needleman-Wunsch score −24 and 17.5% identity. ACK1 has its own characterisation in cell wall integrity signalling (PMID: 18806213), a different context from DSF2's annotations.

DSF1 (P0CX08, YEL070W) is a 502-residue mannitol dehydrogenase (EC 1.1.1.67) in PTHR43362, with no Sel1 or TPR repeat annotation anywhere in its record. DSF1 and DSF2 share a name because both were recovered as deletion suppressors of an mpt5 mutation (PMID: 16328373), not because they are related in sequence.

The closest functionally studied homolog of the DSF2 repeat region is S. pombe Nif1 (P87159), which ranks 371 of 501 with score 94 and 24.3% identity and shares PANTHER family PTHR43628 with DSF2. Nif1 was isolated as a Nim1 two-hybrid interactor (PMID: 9135149). This is almost certainly the source of DSF2's IBA annotation to GO:0010972, and it means that annotation traces to a single two-hybrid result in a different organism.

Mechanistic model and interpretation

The seed hypothesis follows a chain of inference that is common and usually reasonable: a Sel1/TPR repeat region implies a superhelical solenoid, which implies a concave face, which implies a peptide-binding groove, which implies a protein-interaction function, which licenses a molecular function GO term. The measurements break the chain at the second link and keep it broken at every link after.

Four measurements independently reach the same endpoint, and they are not redundant with one another. The regularity test is about the backbone, the groove test is about surface shape, the clamp test is about side-chain chemistry, and the conservation test is about evolutionary pressure. Each has its own control: HcpC for regularity, Hop TPR2A with 100% MEEVD recall and myoglobin for the groove, the TPR2A self-control at 0 clashes for the clamp, and rSASA plus a 5000-draw permutation null for conservation. A surface that was a real binding site would be expected to pass at least one.

What DSF2 is, on the evidence: a protein with roughly 547 disordered residues followed by a short, irregular Sel1-like repeat region of about three usable units. Its only phenotype is deletion suppression of mpt5/puf5 temperature sensitivity, recovered in a screen that also returned dsf1, sir2, sir3, sir4 and swe1 (PMID: 16328373). Its only process-level annotation reaches it from one S. pombe two-hybrid result. The honest molecular function is unknown.

How each paper bears on the findings

The crystal structure of Helicobacter cysteine-rich protein C at 2.0 A resolution (PMID: 15223324) is both the structural reference for the Sel1 fold and the source of the peptide-binding precedent the hypothesis invokes. It states that HcpC "revealed a super-helical topology composed of seven disulfide bridged alpha/alpha-repeats, an N-terminal capping helix and an extended C-terminal coil", which defines the architecture DSF2 was superposed against, and that "a hydrophobic pocket and a cluster of negatively charged residues recognize the side-chains of Val290 and Lys287 from the C-terminal coil". DSF2's face has the hydrophobicity but not the acidic cluster. The paper also qualifies its own precedent: the interaction is a crystal-packing contact, not a demonstrated physiological complex.

Structure of TPR domain-peptide complexes (PMID: 10786835) defines the chemistry DSF2 lacks: "Peptide binding is mediated by electrostatic interactions with the EEVD motif, with the C-terminal aspartate acting as a two-carboxylate anchor, and by hydrophobic interactions with residues upstream of EEVD." Every one of the five clamp positions maps onto a cysteine, tyrosine, leucine, methionine or tryptophan in DSF2.

Suppressor analysis of the mpt5/htr1/uth4/puf5 deletion (PMID: 16328373) is the only primary experimental phenotype for DSF2: "we screened deletion suppressors to rescue the temperature sensitivity of Deltampt5, and identified dsf1 (YEL070W), dsf2 (YBR007C), sir2, sir3, sir4 and swe1". It supplies a process-level constraint with no biochemical binding assay, and it explains the DSF1/DSF2 naming that the seed hypothesis misread as paralogy.

Nif1, a novel mitotic inhibitor in Schizosaccharomyces pombe (PMID: 9135149) identifies the evidence behind DSF2's closest functionally studied homolog: "the yeast two-hybrid system was used to isolate S.pombe cDNA clones encoding proteins that interact with Nim1. Sixteen of the 17 cDNA clones were derived from the same gene, named nif1+". This is the likely root of the IBA annotation to GO:0010972, and it is a single assay in a different organism.

The synthetic genetic network around PKC1 (PMID: 18806213) shows where ACK1 actually sits: "One of the latter group of mutants lacks Ack1(Ydl203c), an uncharacterized SEL-1 domain-containing protein that we find modulates pathway activity." ACK1 is sometimes treated as the DSF2 paralog; it is in a different PANTHER family, has 17.5% identity over the repeat region, and is characterised in a different pathway.

Mechanistic scope

The immediate molecular claim under test is a surface property of one domain: whether residues approximately 548-731 of P38213 present a concave groove that captures an extended peptide from a partner protein. That is a statement about the apo structure of a single polypeptide, and it is what the structural analysis addresses directly.

Three things must be kept separate from it, because none was tested and none follows from the fold:

Downstream phenotype. dsf2 deletion suppresses the temperature sensitivity of an mpt5/puf5 deletion (PMID: 16328373). Suppression in a sensitised background is several steps removed from a molecular activity, and the same screen recovered sir2, sir3, sir4 and swe1, which act through chromatin and cell-cycle routes rather than through a shared biochemistry.

Pathway placement by homology. Any G2/M regulatory role reaches DSF2 through S. pombe Nif1, itself defined by a two-hybrid interaction with Nim1 (PMID: 9135149). That is an inference about process, not a demonstration that DSF2 binds anything.

Co-purification. The TAP-MS partners are compartment-level and abundance-level associations. None has been shown to be direct, none is a peptide ligand, and the two most suggestive (SSA2, SSB1) have the highest interaction degrees in the entire set.

Conflicts and alternatives

The paralog premise in the seed hypothesis is wrong. DSF1 (P0CX08, YEL070W) is a 502-residue mannitol dehydrogenase in PANTHER PTHR43362 with no Sel1 or TPR annotation. The shared name comes from the mpt5 deletion-suppressor screen, not from sequence relationship. A conservation analysis that included "Dsf2-like paralogs" as the hypothesis asks would be comparing a repeat protein with an oxidoreductase.

Database carry-over risk from CHS4, SHC1 and HRD3. Yeast has exactly five Sel1-repeat proteins. CHS4/SKT5, SHC1 and ACK1 form a genuine paralog group in PTHR46430; HRD3 sits in PTHR11102; DSF2 is the sole member of PTHR43628. ACK1 ranks 476 of 501 against the DSF2 repeat region at 17.5% identity and is characterised in PKC1 signalling (PMID: 18806213). Any annotation reaching DSF2 from CHS4 (chitin synthase III activation) or from HRD3 (ERAD substrate recognition) would be a cross-family transfer the alignment does not support.

The Hsp70 competing explanation, and why it fails. The strongest competing reading of the data is that DSF2 is a clamp-bearing TPR protein that binds Hsp70, since SSA2 and SSB1 co-purify with it and Hop and CHIP TPR domains bind the Hsp70 EEVD motif. Two independent results remove the support. The interaction degrees show both Hsp70s are affinity-purification frequent flyers (3494 and 2442 partners). The structure shows DSF2 cannot form the clamp (no counterpart at any of five positions, 10-16 MEEVD clashes against 0 in the self-control).

The crystal-contact caveat on the comparator itself. The strongest statement that a Sel1 concave face binds peptide (PMID: 15223324) comes from a crystal-packing contact in HcpC, not from a physiological complex. The precedent the seed hypothesis builds on is softer than it appears, which matters when the hypothesis is framed as a property "characteristic of protein-interaction TPR/Sel1 domains".

The limit of a negative structural result. DSF2 might still bind a protein through a flat surface, through induced fit at a site that is closed in the apo model, or through the disordered 1-547 region, which this analysis does not address at all. What is refuted is the groove model and the peptide-recognition-class assignment, not protein binding in general.

Limitations and knowledge gaps

  1. No experimental structure. Checked: the PDB holds no DSF2 entry, and all geometry comes from AF-P38213-F1-model_v6. This matters because the irregular 548-642 region has lower local confidence than the 643-731 core, and the groove measurement is only as good as the model. A crystal or cryo-EM structure, or an AlphaFold-Multimer screen against candidate partners, would resolve it.

  2. The disordered N-terminal 547 residues were not analysed. Checked: the whole-chain mean pLDDT of 50.9 against 89.9 for 548-731 marks 1-547 as disordered, with UniProt disorder calls at 1-46, 178-208, 229-410 and 440-461. If DSF2 functions through a short linear motif or a conditionally folded segment in this region, the entire structural argument addresses the wrong part of the protein. Limited proteolysis mapping or a motif scan of the low-confidence region would test it.

  3. No reproduced physical partner. Checked: all 14 IntAct records. Without one partner confirmed by two independent methods, no IPI-based MF annotation is possible and no binding model can be tested against data.

  4. The basis of the IBA GO:0010972 annotation was inferred, not read. Checked: PANTHER family membership (PTHR43628 contains both DSF2 and Nif1) and homolog ranking point to Nif1, but the GO reference that generated the IBA was not retrieved programmatically. A curator should read the PAINT family tree directly to confirm the inference and see whether DSF2 falls inside or outside the annotated clade.

  5. Orthology could not be taken from OrthoDB. Checked: the UniProt cross-reference to group 2148946at2759 resolves to an unrelated F-box family, and /fasta?id=...&species=all returned 0 bytes. The ortholog set was rebuilt from PANTHER PTHR43628 plus alignment ranking, which is adequate for conservation statistics but is not a curated ortholog set. This is a genuine resource failure, reported rather than worked around.

  6. Phenix validation tools could not be run. Checked: host-side curl to the AlphaFold DB returns 403 from the proxy, and only the code executor has outbound network access, so no structure file could be placed where run_phenix_tool and compare_structures read from. All superposition, secondary-structure assignment, SASA and groove detection were therefore implemented directly in the executor and validated against positive and negative controls. This is stated here rather than presented as Phenix output. The InterPro entry/protein API also returned an unexpected response shape, so domain boundaries were taken from UniProt cross-references and from the repeat detection itself.

  7. Groove detection measures apo geometry. Both detectors used here score a static structure. A site that forms only on binding would be missed. The validation on Hop TPR2A was done on the peptide-bound conformation with the peptide deleted, which is the favourable case for detection.

Proposed follow-up experiments and actions

AlphaFold-Multimer, Boltz or Chai against a focused partner set. Run DSF2 548-731 against SSA2 and SSB1 (to settle the Hsp70 question outright), against Mpt5/Puf5 and Swe1 (from the genetics), and against a decoy set of equal size drawn from unrelated yeast proteins. The discriminating signal is a separation in interface ipTM between candidates and decoys, plus whether any predicted interface lands on the concave face rather than on the 681-731 region where the only cavity sits. This is the cheapest decisive test and requires no wet work.

Peptide array or phage display on the purified repeat region. A Sel1/TPR groove binds extended peptides. If a conserved recognition class exists, a tiling array of yeast C-terminal peptides will show a motif. A flat result closes the hypothesis experimentally rather than computationally.

Point mutants of the measured cavity. The 295 Å3 cluster is lined by Y648, M652, L655, K656, D664, E665, V681, D682, C685, L686, T689, I690, K693, W724, I725, K727 and Y730. Mutating the W724/Y730/I725 cluster and testing whether mpt5 suppression is lost separates "this cavity matters" from "this cavity is incidental packing at the capping helix".

Experimental structure of the minimal regular array. Only the units from 643 to 731 are in register. If a groove exists anywhere in DSF2, it is here. A crystal structure of this fragment, which is small enough to be tractable, would replace the model-dependent part of this analysis.

Read the PAINT tree behind GO:0010972. One step determines whether the BP annotation rests on Nif1 alone and whether DSF2 sits inside the annotated clade.

Scan the disordered 1-547 region for short linear motifs. The structural analysis covers 25% of the protein. If DSF2's function is motif-mediated, the answer is in the other 75%.
