# ECT1 (YGR007W, P33412) notes

## Activity
- CTP:phosphoethanolamine cytidylyltransferase (EC 2.7.7.14) [PMID:8982874 "The entire ORF, when combined with the glutathione S-transferase gene and expressed in Escherichia coli, exhibited ECT activity."]
- ect1 cho1 double mutant <2% PE [PMID:8982874 "In an Ect- mutant that also carried the cho1 mutation, phosphatidylethanolamine accounted for less than 2% of total phospholipids, suggesting the importance of ECT in phosphatidylethanolamine synthesis."]

## Location
- Cytoplasm and nucleus (GFP, Huh 2003) [UniProt:P33412]

## Curation observations
- Cytosol RCA correct for this soluble enzyme. Nucleus kept non-core.
