# SGD gene description for ACP1 (yeast)

Mitochondrial matrix acyl carrier protein; involved in biosynthesis of octanoate, which is a precursor to lipoic acid; coordinates mitochondrial fatty acid and iron sulfur cluster metabolism; activated by phosphopantetheinylation catalyzed by Ppt2p
