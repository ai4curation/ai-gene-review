# PDA1 (YER178W, P16387) notes

Evidence journal for the review; sources are UniProt and cached publications.

- E1 alpha of PDH (EC 1.2.4.1), ThDP/Mg2+ dependent, alpha2beta2 with Pdb1p [UniProt:P16387].
- Regulation by phosphorylation: [PMID:7589446 "PDC activity in isolated yeast mitochondria could be inactivated in an ATP-dependent fashion"]; [PMID:18180296 "Recently we identified two enzymes that are involved in the phosphorylation (Pkp1p) and dephosphorylation (Ppp1p) of Pda1p, the alpha-subunit of the pyruvate dehydrogenase complex."]; UniProt: Ser-313 phosphorylated by PKP1/PKP2, dephosphorylated by PTC5/PTC6.
- Genetics: [PMID:11589696 "The co-disruption of ALD4 and PDA1 (encoding subunit E1alpha of pyruvate dehydrogenase) prevented the growth on pyruvate for both strains"]. The PDH bypass (Pdc -> Ald -> Acs) explains why pda1 alone is not lethal on pyruvate.
- Pseudohyphal growth: [PMID:24603354 "two distinct point mutations in PDA1, the Y309A and S313A alleles, yield a dramatic fitness defect and loss of pseudohyphal differentiation in nitrogen-limiting conditions"]. Kept as non-core (likely indirect).
- mtDNA nucleoid association (PMID:15692048, IDA; abstract names only Aco1p). Kept as non-core.
