# SGD gene description for SAN1 (yeast)

Ubiquitin-protein ligase; role in proteasome-dependent degradation of aberrant nuclear proteins; targets substrates with regions of exposed hydrophobicity; contains intrinsically disordered regions involved in substrate recognition; prefers a window of exposed hydrophobicity that causes a particular level of protein insolubility, suggesting that San1p evolved to target highly aggregation-prone proteins; localizes to the cytoplasm, nucleus and peroxisomes
