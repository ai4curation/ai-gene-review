# SFA1 (YDL168W, P32771) notes

## Evidence journal
- Class III ADH / glutathione-dependent formaldehyde dehydrogenase [PMID:7649298 "Alcohol dehydrogenase class III (glutathione-dependent formaldehyde dehydrogenase) from Saccharomyces cerevisiae was purified and analyzed structurally and enzymatically."].
- Formaldehyde resistance and degradation [PMID:8483449 "In sfa::LEU2 disruption mutants, sensitivity to formaldehyde is correlated with reduced degradation of the chemical."]; oxidises long-chain alcohols [PMID:8483449 "it oxidizes long-chain alcohols and, in the presence of glutathione, is able to oxidize FA"].
- GSNO reductase [PMID:11260719 "Deleting the reductase gene in yeast and mice abolishes the GSNO-consuming activity"].
- Ehrlich final step: one of six redundant reductases [PMID:12499363 "can be accomplished by any one of the ethanol dehydrogenases (Adh1p, Adh2p, Adh3p, Adh4p, Adh5p) or by Sfa1p (formaldehyde dehydrogenase.)"].
- Cytoplasmic by GFP, also detected in mitochondrial proteomes [UniProt:P32771].
- HMF reductase IMP (PMID:16652391): abstract only describes ADH6; SFA1 data not visible -> UNDECIDED.
