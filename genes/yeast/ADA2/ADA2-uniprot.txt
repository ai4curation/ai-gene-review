CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the transcription coactivator SAGA complex. SAGA
CC       acts as a general cofactor required for essentially all RNA polymerase
CC       II transcription (PubMed:10864329, PubMed:25216679, PubMed:28918903).
CC       At the promoters, SAGA is required for transcription pre-initiation
CC       complex (PIC) recruitment. It influences RNA polymerase II
CC       transcriptional activity through different activities such as TBP
CC       interaction (via core/TAF module) and promoter selectivity, interaction
CC       with transcription activators (via Tra1/SPT module), and chromatin
CC       modification through histone acetylation (via HAT module) and
CC       deubiquitination (via DUB module) (PubMed:18950642, PubMed:7972120).
CC       SAGA preferentially acetylates histones H3 (to form H3K9ac, H3K14ac,
CC       H3K18ac and H3K23ac) and H2B and deubiquitinates histone H2B
CC       (PubMed:10026213). SAGA interacts with DNA via upstream activating
CC       sequences (UASs) (PubMed:28918903). Also identified in a modified
CC       version of SAGA named SALSA or SLIK (PubMed:12186975, PubMed:12446794).
CC       The cleavage of SPT7 and the absence of the SPT8 subunit in SLIK
CC       neither drive any major conformational differences in its structure
CC       compared with SAGA, nor significantly affect HAT, DUB, or DNA-binding
CC       activities (PubMed:33864814). Component of the ADA histone
CC       acetyltransferase complex, which preferentially acetylates nucleosomal
CC       histones H3 (to form H3K14ac and H3K18ac) and H2B (PubMed:10026213,
CC       PubMed:9224714). Positioned at chromosomal termini to participate in
CC       both transcriptional repression and activation in response to nutrient
CC       signaling, promoting transcriptional silencing at telomeres and
CC       ribosomal DNA (PubMed:19737915). May be involved in response to DNA
CC       damage by genotoxic agents (PubMed:15340070).

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12186975, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12446794,

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19737915, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25216679,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28918903, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33864814,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7972120, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9224714}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the 1.8 MDa SAGA (Spt-Ada-Gcn5 acetyltransferase)
CC       complex, which is composed of 19 subunits TRA1, SPT7, TAF5, NGG1/ADA3,
CC       SGF73, SPT20/ADA5, SPT8, TAF12, TAF6, HFI1/ADA1, UBP8, GCN5, ADA2,
CC       SPT3, SGF29, TAF10, TAF9, SGF11 and SUS1 (PubMed:9224714,
CC       PubMed:9674426, PubMed:9885573). The SAGA complex is composed of 4
CC       modules, namely the HAT (histone acetyltransferase) module (GCN5, ADA2,
CC       NGG1/ADA3 and SGF29), the DUB (deubiquitinating) module (UBP8, SGF11,
CC       SGF73 and SUS1), the core or TAF (TBP-associated factor) module (TAF5,
CC       TAF6, TAF9, TAF10 and TAF12), and the Tra1 or SPT (Suppressor of Ty)
CC       module (TRA1, HFI1/ADA1, SPT3, SPT7, SPT8 and SPT20/ADA5). The Tra1/SPT
CC       module binds activators, the core module recruits TBP (TATA-binding
CC       protein), the HAT module contains the histone H3 acetyltransferase
CC       GCN5, and the DUB module comprises the histone H2B deubiquitinase UBP8
CC       (PubMed:25216679). Also identified in an altered form of SAGA, named
CC       SALSA (SAGA altered, Spt8 absent) or SLIK (SAGA-like) complex, which
CC       contains a C-terminal truncated form of SPT7 and is missing SPT8
CC       (PubMed:12186975, PubMed:12446794, PubMed:15647753). However, it has
CC       been shown that the SAGA and SAGA-like SALSA/SLIK transcriptional
CC       coactivators are structurally and biochemically equivalent
CC       (PubMed:33864814). Component of the 0.8 MDa ADA complex, a HAT complex
CC       distinct from SAGA, which at least consists of ADA2, NGG1/ADA3, AHC1,
CC       AHC2, SGF29 and GCN5 (PubMed:10490601). Component of an ADA/GCN5
CC       complex that consists of HFI1/ADA1, ADA2, NGG1/ADA3, SPT20/ADA5 and
CC       GCN5 and probably is a subcomplex of SAGA (PubMed:7862114,
CC       PubMed:9154821). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10490601,
