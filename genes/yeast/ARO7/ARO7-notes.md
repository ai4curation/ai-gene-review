# ARO7 (YPR060C, P32178) notes

Pathway context: chorismate mutase (EC 5.4.99.5), branch point to Phe/Tyr (YeastPathways PWY3O-4153, PWY3O-4120).

- Allosteric dimer; Trp activates, Tyr inhibits [PMID:2187528 "The wild-type enzyme is activated by tryptophan and subject to feedback inhibition by tyrosine"; "Both enzymes are dimers consisting of two identical subunits of Mr 30,000"].
- Direct chorismate to prephenate conversion by NMR [PMID:2187528 "shows a direct and irreversible conversion of chorismate to prephenate"].
- T226I locks enzyme in R state [PMID:2646272 "A single base pair exchange resulting in a threonine-to-isoleucine amino acid substitution in the C-terminal part of the chorismate mutase was found in all mutant strains."].
- Same allosteric site for Tyr/Trp [PMID:9642265 "Biochemical studies reveal two binding sites per molecule for both effectors, tyrosine or tryptophan."]; six-state landscape, mixed state most active [PMID:31498992 "Surprisingly, the mixed bound state had the highest enzyme activity"].
- ARO7 = OSM2, needed for Phe and Tyr [PMID:3027508 "The chorismate mutase structural gene, ARO7, which is necessary for both phenylalanine and tyrosine biosynthesis was cloned by complementation in yeast."].
- Localisation: GFP-Aro7 exclusively cytoplasmic; nucleus only with fused Gcn4 NLS [PMID:12455686 "The resulting fusion protein was localized exclusively in the cytoplasm when analyzed by fluorescence microscopy"]. Nucleus IDA REMOVEd (full text read), nucleus HDA marked over-annotated.
- Protein binding IPI (PMID:2187528) -> MODIFY to protein homodimerization activity.
- Module note: yeast CM is AroQ-class, monofunctional, allosteric (eukaryotic CM family), unlike E. coli bifunctional PheA/TyrA CM domains.
