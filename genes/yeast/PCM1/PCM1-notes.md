# PCM1 / AGM1 (YEL058W, P38628) notes

- Phosphoacetylglucosamine mutase (EC 5.4.2.3), alpha-D-phosphohexomutase family, Mg2+ by similarity [UniProt:P38628 "Note=Binds 1 Mg(2+) ion per subunit."].
- Identified as the GlcNAc-P mutase structural gene; essential; deletion -> strings of undivided cells like glucosamine auxotrophs [PMID:8174553 "was identified as the structural gene encoding N-acetylglucosamine-phosphate mutase"; "An agm1 deletion mutant progresses through only approximately five cell cycles"].
- Side activity: phosphoglucomutase; overexpression rescues pgm1 pgm2; PGM2 cannot replace PCM1 [PMID:8174553 "over-expression of the AGM1 gene in a pgm1/pgm2 double deletion mutant which could restore phosphoglucomutase activity"]. GO:0004614 not in GOA; raised as a question rather than NEW.
- With Uap1/Qri1 converts GlcNAc6P -> UDP-GlcNAc [PMID:9603950 "together with Agm1p, it produced UDP-N-acetylglucosamine from N-acetylglucosamine-6-phosphate"].
- Localisation cytoplasm (+ nucleus by GFP) [PMID:14562095; PMID:11914276].
- Generic IEA 'carbohydrate metabolic process' -> MODIFY GO:0006048; 'intramolecular phosphotransferase activity' -> MODIFY GO:0004610.
