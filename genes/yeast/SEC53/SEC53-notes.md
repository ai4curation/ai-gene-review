# SEC53 (ALG4, YFL045C, P07283) notes

Pathway context: dolichol-phosphate sugar donor supply (PWY3O-123, Man-6-P -> Man-1-P, EC 5.4.2.8).

- SEC53 encodes phosphomannomutase. [PMID:3288631 "Several lines of evidence demonstrate that SEC53 encodes the yeast phosphomannomutase."] [PMID:3288631 "Finally, the Sec53 protein and phosphomannomutase activity cofractionate exactly in a 70-fold partial purification involving gel filtration and DEAE chromatography."]
- Glycosylation defect rescued by GDP-Man or Man-1-P + GTP, not Man-6-P + GTP. [PMID:3288631 "Normal glycosylation is restored in reactions supplemented with a cytosolic fraction from wild type cells, with GDP-mannose, or with mannose 1-phosphate and GTP, but not with mannose 6-phosphate and GTP."]
- Cytosolic. [PMID:3905826 "Cell fractionation, performed with conditions that preserve the lumenal content of the endoplasmic reticulum (ER), shows Sec53p highly enriched in the cytosol fraction."]
- Homodimer [UniProt:P07283]. Human ortholog PMM2 (PMM2-CDG).
