# NMA2 (YGR010W) notes

## Identity and activity
- NMN/NaMN adenylyltransferase 2, WGD paralog of NMA1.
- Identified and characterised as a second yeast NMNAT [PMID:12597897 "the YGR010-encoded protein was identified as a second isoform of yeast NMNAT (yNMNAT-2)"]; recombinant enzyme active [PMID:12597897 "successfully expressed in Escherichia coli BL21 cells, yielding high level of NMN adenylyltransferase activity"]. Kinetics differ from Nma1 (Km ATP 1.4 mM vs 0.11; Co2+ preferred) [UniProt:P53204].
- NaMN adenylyltransferase activity is by similarity / inference (ECO:0000305) in UniProt; no direct NaMN assay cached.
- Yeast NMNAT (isoform 2 per SGD) protects axons when expressed in mouse neurons, activity-dependent [PMID:19403820 "Nmnat enzymes with diverse sequences and structures from various species, including Drosophila melanogaster, Saccharomyces cerevisiae"]. Yeast NMNAT used as a reagent in a PPi cycling assay [PMID:21621500 "nicotinamide mononucleotide adenylyltransferase (EC 2.7.7.1) from Saccharomyces cerevisiae"].

## Location
- Nuclear concentration: [PMID:11884393 "Npt1 and a previously uncharacterized salvage pathway enzyme, Nma2, are both concentrated in the nucleus"]. UniProt lists nucleus only.
- YeastPathways RCA "cytosol" is generic pathway placement; not contradicted outright ("concentrated" implies some non-nuclear pool) but not NMA2-specific.

## Observations
- Paralog specialisation unresolved: both have NMNAT activity; Nma1 more abundant (5130 vs 1430 molecules/cell) [UniProt].
- RCA rows putting NMN/NaMN adenylyltransferase into "L-tryptophan degradation III" are superpathway inflation.
