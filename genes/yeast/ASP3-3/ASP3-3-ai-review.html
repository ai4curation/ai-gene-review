Gene Description

ASP3-3 (YLR158C) is one of four identical, tandemly arrayed copies (ASP3-1 to ASP3-4) in the ribosomal-DNA-adjacent region of chromosome XII of S288C that encode asparaginase II (L-asparagine amidohydrolase, EC 3.5.1.1), a member of the asparaginase type-I/II family. The protein carries an N-terminal signal peptide, is glycosylated and secreted to the periplasm/cell wall, where it hydrolyses external L-asparagine to L-aspartate and ammonium. Unlike the constitutive cytoplasmic asparaginase I (ASP1), asparaginase II is subject to nitrogen catabolite repression and appears only during nitrogen starvation or growth on poor nitrogen sources, allowing cells to scavenge nitrogen from extracellular asparagine. It also hydrolyses D-asparagine and N-acyl-asparagines but not L-glutamine. Four copies characterize the S288C reference strain, while ASP3 presence and copy number vary among strains.

Core Functions

Secreted asparaginase II hydrolysing external L-asparagine to L-aspartate and ammonium, providing a nitrogen source under nitrogen-limited conditions. The four S288C loci encode identical proteins; shared classical asparaginase-II experiments do not distinguish their individual contributions.

Identity

  • ASP3-3 is one of four identical copies (ASP3-1..ASP3-4) encoding asparaginase II, EC 3.5.1.1 [file:yeast/ASP3-3/ASP3-3-uniprot.txt "The 4 identical copies ASP3-1, ASP3-2, ASP3-3 and ASP3-4 are arranged in"].   • Reaction: [file:yeast/ASP3-3/ASP3-3-uniprot.txt "Reaction=L-asparagine + H2O = L-aspartate + NH4(+);"]
  • Signal peptide 1-25, secreted / periplasm [file:yeast/ASP3-3/ASP3-3-uniprot.txt "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted. Periplasm."]

Evidence

  • External, nitrogen-starvation-induced asparaginase distinct from an internal constitutive one [PMID:238936 "these strains of S. cerevisiae have an externally active asparaginase as well as an internally active one"; "The appearance of the external asparaginase is stimulated by nitrogen starvation, requires an available energy source, and is prevented by cycloheximide."]
  • ASP3 is the structural gene; NCR acts at mRNA level PMID:3042786

Curation decisions

  • Core: asparaginase activity / L-asparagine catabolic process / cell wall-bounded periplasmic space.
  • REMOVE RCA cytosol (YeastPathways pathway default; fits ASP1, not secreted ASP3).
  • amino acid metabolic process IEA: over-annotation (parent of the specific process).
  • cellular response to nitrogen starvation IDA: kept as non-core (regulated expression / N scavenging).

Module notes

  • ASP1 (cytoplasmic asparaginase I) vs ASP3 (periplasmic asparaginase II): both catalyse the same YeastPathways reaction in different compartments; ASP3 acts on external asparagine. ASP3 cluster is copy-number variable (4 copies in S288C).
