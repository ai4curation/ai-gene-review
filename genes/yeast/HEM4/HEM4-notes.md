# HEM4 (YOR278W, P06174) notes

Uroporphyrinogen-III synthase (UROS).

- Low identity to other UROS; hem4 deletion abolishes UROS activity [PMID:7597845 "it encodes a protein with 18-23% identity to uroporphyrinogen III synthases from different sources"; "Biochemical analysis confirmed the absence of uroporphyrinogen III synthase activity in both hem4 delta and cyt mutant strains."].
- hem4 deletion heme auxotroph used to clone Arabidopsis UROS [PMID:18042043 "A mutant of Saccharomyces cerevisiae was constructed in which the HEM4 gene encoding UROS was deleted."].
- Reaction hydroxymethylbilane = uroporphyrinogen III + H2O [UniProt:P06174]. Originally misattributed to mitochondrial import (UniProt caution).
- IMP from PMID:14559249 (rate-limiting steps) cannot be verified from abstract (abstract only discusses Hem2/Hem3); accepted by deference.
