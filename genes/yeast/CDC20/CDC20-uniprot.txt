CC   -!- FUNCTION: Activator protein that regulates the ubiquitin ligase
CC       activity and substrate specificity of the anaphase promoting
CC       complex/cyclosome (APC/C). At the metaphase-to-anaphase transition,
CC       recognizes and binds proteins containing a D-box including the B-type
CC       cyclins CLB2 and CLB5, HSL1 and securin PDS1, and recruits them in a C-
CC       box-dependent manner to the APC/C for ubiquitination and subsequent
CC       proteolysis. Required for sister chromatid separation and disassembly
CC       of the mitotic spindle. Target of the spindle checkpoint pathway
CC       through participation in the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC) and the
CC       MAD2-CDC20 subcomplex. MCC and presumably the MAD2-CDC20 subcomplex
CC       inhibit the ubiquitin ligase activity of the anaphase promoting
CC       complex/cyclosome (APC/C) by preventing its activation by CDC20 until
CC       proper attachment of all chromosomes to the spindle.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12152084, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15198982,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15879521}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC) which
CC       consists of MAD2, MAD3, BUB3 and CDC20, and of the MAD2-CDC20
CC       subcomplex, both of which appear to be assembled during mitoisis
CC       independently of the kinetochore. In mitose-arrested cells, MAD2-CDC20
CC       occurs in a larger amount than MCC. MCC associates with the with the
CC       APC/C complex. Interacts with MAD2, MAD3, BUB3, HSL1 and PDS1.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10704439, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11553328,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11562348, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11566880,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15879521, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9461437}.

CC       P26309; P47074: MAD3; NbExp=9; IntAct=EBI-4212, EBI-10369;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12456658,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14562095}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in late S and M phase of the cell cycle.
CC       Degraded in G1 by the APC/C in its CDH1-bound form.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9651679}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The KEN box is required for the association with the APC/C
CC       complex.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The C-box is required for the association with the APC/C
CC       complex.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminal destruction box (D-box) acts as a recognition
CC       signal for degradation via the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway.
CC   -!- PTM: Ubiquitinated by the APC/C complex.
