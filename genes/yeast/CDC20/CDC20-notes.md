# CDC20 (Saccharomyces cerevisiae, P26309 / YGL116W) - curation notes

## Session 2026-09-25 - full review of the SGD-seeded annotation set

### Identity and architecture
- WD40 Cdc20/Fizzy-family APC/C coactivator, 610 aa; seven WD repeats (257-562), N-terminal D-box (17-25), bipartite NLS (85-101), C-box (144-150), C-terminal KEN box (586-592) and IR tail (UniProt P26309 features). Checkpoint-resistant point mutants Y205N, P209Q, P210L/S lose Mad2/Mad3 binding (UniProt MUTAGEN; Hwang et al. 1998).
- The InterPro "quinoprotein ADH-like" superfamily hit is a beta-propeller fold relationship only; the deep research (falcon) correctly notes there is no dehydrogenase activity to annotate.

### Core biology (with provenance)
- Limiting, substrate-specific APC activator: [PMID:9334304 "The conserved Cdc20 and Cdh1 proteins were identified as limiting, substrate-specific activators of APC-dependent proteolysis."]; [PMID:9334304 "CDC20 was required for the degradation of the APC substrate Pds1 but not for that of other APC substrates, such as Clb2 and Ase1."]; overexpression suffices to degrade Pds1 out of schedule [PMID:9334304 "Overexpression of either CDC20 or CDH1 was sufficient to induce APC-dependent proteolysis of the appropriate target in stages of the cell cycle in which substrates are normally stable."].
- APC/C subunit: co-IP with Cdc23 and nuclear localization [PMID:9501986 "Cdc20 is localized in the nucleus and co-immunoprecipitates with an APC component, Cdc23"]; "either of two loosely associated regulatory subunits, Cdc20 and Cdh1" [PMID:16481473]; Cdc20 detected in Swm1/Apc13-containing APC/C holoenzyme [PMID:15060174 "demonstrating that Swm1/Apc13 is a component of the APC/C-Cdc20 and APC/C-Cdh1 holoenzymes"].
- Ligase activator, reconstituted: [PMID:15060174 "Cdc20 activated wild-type APC/C to ubiquitinate Pds1"], with Cdc20-IA giving background; [PMID:19362536 "purified Cdc27-A1, Cdc27-A2 and Cdc23-A APC/Cs all displayed significant defects in Cdc20-dependent activity as well as Cdh1-dependent activity in vitro, using 35S-securin as substrate"] - TPR grooves of Cdc27/Cdc23 are the Cdc20 docking sites. C-box essential in vivo, IR tail dispensable [PMID:16481473 "indicating that the C-box of Cdc20 is essential for its function"; "the IR domain of Cdc20 is dispensable for viability"].
- Substrate receptor: [PMID:16481473 "Cdc20 and Cdh1 also contain WD40 repeats that bind directly to APC targets, and thus may serve as a bridge between enzyme and substrate"]; [PMID:15060174 "Cdc20 and Cdh1 can also bind to substrates, suggesting that they activate ubiquitination by recruiting substrates to the catalytic center located in the complex"]; cooperative trimolecular activator-substrate-core model [PMID:19362536].
- Essential outputs = Pds1 and Clb5 destruction: [PMID:10647015 "show that APC(Cdc20) allows activation of Cdc14 and promotes exit from mitosis"; "both Pds1 and Clb5 can proliferate in the complete absence of Cdc20"]. Early phase of Clb2 degradation also Cdc20-dependent [PMID:12152084 "essential early phase of Clb2 degradation"]; Lim et al. found Clb2 degradation blocked without Cdc20 [PMID:9501986 "Cdc20 is an essential regulator of APC-dependent proteolysis"].
- Dominant/overexpression genetics: [PMID:9504909 "Cdc20p is a limiting factor that promotes anaphase entry by antagonizing Pds1p"; "CDC20 overexpression was unable to promote anaphase in cells deficient in APC function"].
- Spindle checkpoint target: [PMID:9461437 "Mad2 and Mad3 coprecipitated with Cdc20 at all stages of the cell cycle."; "suggesting that Cdc20 is the target of the spindle checkpoint"]; Mad3 binds Bub3 and Cdc20 [PMID:10704439 "Mad3p interacts with both Bub3p and Cdc20p"]; Bub3-Cdc20 association up-regulated on checkpoint activation and kinetochore-independent [PMID:11726501 "Cdc20, which had previously been shown to interact physically with Mad2 and Mad3, associates also with Bub3 and association is up-regulated upon checkpoint activation"]; two complexes, MCC minor and Mad2-Cdc20 major [PMID:15879521 "There is a small amount of Mad2-Mad3-Bub3-Cdc20 and a much larger amount of a complex that contains Mad2-Cdc20"; "showing that Mad2 and Cdc20 form a separate complex that does not contain Mad3"].
- Meiosis: Pds1 destroyed at onset of both meiotic divisions, Cdc20-dependent [PMID:10855492 "securin destruction depends on Cdc20p"; "accumulates within nuclei around the time of Pds1p's disappearance"]. APC/C-Cdc20 destroys the Ume6 repressor early in meiosis [PMID:17889668 "Ume6p is destroyed early in meiosis by Cdc20p, an activator of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) ubiquitin ligase"; "Incubating Ume6p with APC/C Cdc20 produced a modest, time dependent accumulation of higher molecular weight species indicative of poly-ubiquitylation"].
- Stress localization: Cdc20, Cdc27 and Apc4 among 27 proteins forming Cmr1-co-localizing perinuclear INQ foci under MG132/MMS [PMID:25817432 "several components of the anaphase-promoting complex (Cdc20, Cdc27 and Apc4) ... localize to INQ"].
- Turnover: itself an APC/C substrate (UniProt PTM; DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE "Degraded in G1 by the APC/C in its CDH1-bound form"); IR-tail deletion stabilises Cdc20 via loss of Cdc27 recognition [PMID:16481473].

### Curation decisions
- 13 x GO:0005515 IPI rows. Policy: MODIFY to the complex the paper actually demonstrates, otherwise REMOVE as uninformative (not as false). Mad2/Mad3/Bub3 rows from targeted papers (PMID:10704439, 11726501, 15879521) -> GO:0033597 MCC, except the 15879521 Mad2 row -> GO:1990333 CDC20-MAD2 subcomplex (that paper's defining result); this mirrors the terms SGD itself derived from the same papers. HTP rows (Uetz Y2H 10688190, Ho HMS-PCI 11805837, Graumann TAP-MudPIT 14660704) -> REMOVE. Bub1 (P41695) rows -> REMOVE: Bub1 is not an MCC subunit in budding yeast so no informative complex term exists; interaction not asserted false.
- GO:0007346 regulation of mitotic cell cycle (NAS) -> MODIFY to GO:0045842 (already IMP-annotated, more specific).
- GO:0016567 protein ubiquitination (NAS) -> MODIFY to GO:0031145 (same paper already supports the specific term).
- GO:0031398 positive regulation of protein ubiquitination (IMP 9504909) -> MODIFY to GO:1905786 (APC/C-specific; parallel rows from 9334304).
- GO:0005737 cytoplasm (HDA) and GO:0034399 nuclear periphery (IDA, stress-induced INQ) -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
- GO:0051321 meiotic cell cycle (IDA, Ume6 destruction) -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE: real, direct, meiosis-specific substrate targeting but general term and non-core relative to the mitotic function.
- GO:0010997 IDA from Kraft 2005 (abstract-only, Cdh1-centred) -> ACCEPT, deferring to the SGD curator; function independently established.
- All IBA rows (PTN000460086) ACCEPTed; the target's own SGD annotations appear in WITH/FROM as expected.
- No NEW terms. Considered GO:1990756 substrate-adaptor activity (human CDC20 carries it by IDA/GO-CAM; yeast evidence is Burton & Solomon 2001 and Thornton 2006) but raised it as a suggested question rather than a NEW row; used it in core_functions where ids are validated.
