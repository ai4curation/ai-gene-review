# SGD gene description for CDH1 (yeast)

Activator of anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C); antagonist of the spindle assembly checkpoint; directs ubiquitination of cyclins facilitating mitotic exit; targets the APC/C to specific substrates including: Cdc20p, Ase1p, Cin8p, Fin1p and Clb5p; partially active in metaphase, and fully active in anaphase; cell-cycle regulated; degradation in trans (auto-ubiquitination) by APC-Cdh1 following Snf1 complex-mediated phosphorylation is required for faithful meiosis
