# ERG24 (YNL280C) notes

## Evidence journal
- In vitro assay with ergosta-8,14-dienol [PMID:32908 "An in vitro assay for delta14-sterol reductase from yeast was developed, using ergosta-8,14-dien-3beta-ol as the substrate."].
- Overexpression raises activity, fenpropidin resistance [PMID:1394506 "A plasmid was isolated that transformed yeast both to resistance to fenpropidin and to an increased specific activity of sterol 14-reductase."].
- Null accumulates ignosterol [PMID:8125337 "Sterol analysis of the disrupted mutant demonstrated the accumulation of ignosterol, indicating a loss of Erg24p activity."]; [PMID:1418625 "The ERG24 null mutant produced ergosta-8,14-dienol as the major sterol"].
