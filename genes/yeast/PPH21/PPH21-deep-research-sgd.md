# SGD gene description for PPH21 (yeast)

Catalytic subunit of protein phosphatase 2A, PP2A; functionally redundant with Pph22p; C-terminally methylated; forms alternate complexes with several regulatory subunits; involved in signal transduction and regulation of mitosis; forms nuclear foci upon DNA replication stress; interaction with Tpd3p and Far11p of the PPG1-FAR complex antagonizes TORC2 signaling; PPH21 has a paralog, PPH22, that arose from the whole genome duplication
