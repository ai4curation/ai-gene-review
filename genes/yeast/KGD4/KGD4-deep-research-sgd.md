# SGD gene description for KGD4 (yeast)

Subunit of the mitochondrial alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase; recruits E3 subunit (Lpd1p) to the E1-E2 (Kgd1p, Kgd2p) core; exists in two forms derived from a single mRNA translated from two alternative start sites; evolutionarily conserved role in the organization of mitochondrial α-KGDH complexes; homolog of human KGD4
