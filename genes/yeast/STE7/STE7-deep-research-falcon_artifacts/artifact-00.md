| Upstream activator | The MAPKKK **Ste11 phosphorylates and activates Ste7**, placing Ste7 in the middle tier of the Ste11→Ste7→MAPK cascade. | Reconstituted kinase-cascade evidence; activated Ste11 increases downstream MAPK phosphorylation. | In-vitro reconstitution and pathway biochemistry; Neiman and Herskowitz, 1994; Bardwell et al., 1996 (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000008) |
| Downstream substrates | The validated Ste7 substrates are the MAPKs **Fus3 and Kss1**; Ste7-dependent dual activation-loop phosphorylation activates them. | Fus3 sites: **Thr180 and Tyr182**; analogous threonine/tyrosine activation-loop phosphorylation activates Kss1. | Substrate docking | Ste7 forms specific, high-affinity complexes with Fus3 and Kss1 through docking interactions outside its catalytic active site. | Approximate **Kᴅ ≈ 5 nM** for either MAPK; binding persists without Ste5 or Ste11. | Purified-protein binding and yeast-extract experiments; Bardwell et al., 1996 (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000008) |
| Ste5-dependent spatial control | During pheromone signaling, Ste5 binds Ste11, Ste7, and Fus3 and is recruited through Gβ/Ste4 to the **plasma membrane**, spatially assembling the mating cascade. | Membrane recruitment places Ste7 between Ste11 and Fus3 at the signaling site. | Interaction, genetic, and reconstitution studies; Good et al., 2009 (pqac-00000016) |
| Ste5-dependent catalytic specificity | Fus3 is intrinsically a poor Ste7 substrate, whereas Kss1 is efficient. The Ste5 minimal-scaffold/VWA domain acts as a substrate-specific cocatalyst that unlocks Fus3 for Ste7 phosphorylation. | Ste5-ms increases Ste7→Fus3 **kcat by about 5,000-fold** with little effect on Kₘ; Ste5-ms–Ste7 **Kᴅ ≈ 75 nM**; Ste7→Kss1 kinetics are largely unaffected. | Quantitative kinetics, binding, mutagenesis, and 1.6-Å structure; Good et al., 2009 (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017) |
| Biological pathway outputs | In mating, scaffolded Ste7 primarily activates Fus3, supporting mating transcription, G1 arrest, shmoo growth, and cell fusion. In invasive or filamentous growth, Ste7 primarily activates Kss1 in a largely Ste5-independent branch. | Constitutively active Ste7 promotes Kss1-dependent invasive growth but does not reproduce Fus3-dependent mating. | Genetics, reporters, phosphorylation assays, and reconstitution; Maleri et al., 2004; Good et al., 2009 (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000016) |
