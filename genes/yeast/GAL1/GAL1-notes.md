# GAL1 (YBR020W, P04385) notes

## Activity
- Galactokinase EC 2.7.1.6 [UniProt:P04385 "Reaction=alpha-D-galactose + ATP = alpha-D-galactose 1-phosphate + ADP"]; GHMP kinase family, GalK subfamily [UniProt:P04385 "Belongs to the GHMP kinase family. GalK subfamily."].
- Purified to homogeneity from induced cells; monomer of 58 kDa; highly galactose-specific [PMID:14144 "The enzyme is highly specific for galactose since galactokinase did not appear to phosphorylate any of the other sugars tested at a rate greater than 0.5% of the rate of galactose phosphorylation."].
- Kinetics: compulsory ordered ternary-complex mechanism [PMID:12106903 "Galactose-1-phosphate, a product of the reaction, is a mixed inhibitor both with respect to galactose and to ATP suggesting that the reaction proceeds via a compulsory, ordered, ternary complex mechanism."].
- First step of galactose catabolism [PMID:12106903 "Galactokinase (EC 2.7.1.6) catalyses the first step in the catabolism of galactose."].

## Second (regulatory) function: GAL3-like inducer
- Gal1 and its catalytically inactive paralog Gal3 act as ligand sensors in the GAL switch [PMID:12106903 "Yeast galactokinase, Gal1p, and the closely related but catalytically inactive Gal3p, also function as ligand sensors in the GAL genetic switch."].
- In the presence of galactose + ATP, Gal1 binds the repressor Gal80 (1:1) [PMID:11964151 "Gel-filtration experiments indicate that Gal3p, or the galactokinase Gal1p, interact directly with Gal80p to form a complex with 1:1 stoichiometry."].
- Multicopy GAL1 complements gal3 long-term-adaptation, independent of kinase activity [PMID:2199310 "Thus, the complementation activity provided by GAL1 is not likely due to galactokinase activity, but rather due to a distinct GAL3-like activity."; "the results indicate that GAL1 encodes a bifunctional protein"].
- Overproduction of Gal1 gives galactose-independent constitutive GAL expression; galactokinase-less alleles retain inducing ability [PMID:1317007 "Overproduction of the GAL1 protein (galactokinase) also causes constitutivity"].

## Assessment
- Core: galactokinase in the cytosolic Leloir pathway. Regulatory Gal80-binding inducer function is real but secondary to Gal3 in wild-type cells (gal3 cells show a 3-5 day lag, [PMID:2199310 "gal3 mutants exhibit a 3-5-day induction lag"]); kept as non-core.
- IPI "protein binding" with Gal80 -> transcription corepressor binding (GO:0001222).
- YeastPathways reaction (EC 2.7.1.6, alpha-D-galactose + ATP) is correct.
