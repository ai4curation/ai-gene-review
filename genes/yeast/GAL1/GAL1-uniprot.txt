CC   -!- FUNCTION: Galactokinase is a key enzyme in the galactose metabolism
CC       where it catalyzes the conversion of alpha-D-galactose to galactose 1-
CC       phosphate (PubMed:12106903, PubMed:13495476, PubMed:14144). Can also
CC       induce the transcription of the yeast GAL genes in response to the
CC       organism being challenged with galactose as the sole source of carbon
CC       (PubMed:1317007, PubMed:2656659). Its striking amino acid sequence
CC       similarity to GAL3 might explain its GAL3-like induction activity
CC       (PubMed:1317007, PubMed:2656659). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12106903,
