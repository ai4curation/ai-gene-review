# FBA1 (YKL060C, P14540) notes

## Activity
- Class II (Zn) fructose-bisphosphate aldolase, EC 4.1.2.13 [UniProt:P14540 "Reaction=beta-D-fructose 1,6-bisphosphate = D-glyceraldehyde 3-"]; two Zn2+ per subunit [UniProt:P14540 "Note=Binds 2 Zn(2+) ions per subunit."]. Only aldolase gene in S. cerevisiae.
- Disruption abolishes activity [PMID:2647491 "The haploid offsprings with the defective aldolase allele fba1::URA3 lack aldolase enzymatic activity and fail to grow in media containing as a carbon source metabolites of only one side of the aldolase reaction."].
- Mutants block both directions [PMID:6384192 "The mutants accumulate fructose 1,6-bisphosphate from glucose during glycolysis and dihydroxyacetone phosphate during gluconeogenesis."].
- Replaceable by unrelated (class I, Drosophila) aldolase [PMID:8435847 "Both heterologous enzymes, which show respectively little and no sequence homology to the corresponding yeast enzymes, fully restored wild-type properties when their genes were expressed in yeast deletion mutants."] -- argues against obligatory glycolytic multi-enzyme complexes.
- Major zinc protein [PMID:30358795 "Our analysis of two abundant zinc proteins, Fba1 aldolase and Met6 methionine synthetase, supported that hypothesis."].

## Location
- Cytosol; minor mitochondria-associated pool [PMID:16962558 "we show that all glycolytic enzymes are associated with mitochondria in yeast"].
