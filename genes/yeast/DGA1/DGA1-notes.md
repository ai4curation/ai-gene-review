# DGA1 (YOR245C, Q08650) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications; no paid deep research).

- Dga1 is the DGAT2-family acyl-CoA:DAG acyltransferase. [PMID:11751830 "we demonstrate that the gene product of YOR245c, Dga1p, catalyzes a major yeast DAGAT activity which is localized to lipid particles"]
- Dga1 and Lro1 make most cellular TAG. [PMID:11751830 "Lro1p and Dga1p are the major contributors to total cellular TAG synthesis"]
- Dga1 sits in the ER membrane with both termini facing the cytosol. [PMID:21321129 "Our data provide evidence that both the N and C termini are oriented toward the cytosol and have different catalytic roles."]
- It is enriched on LDs and acts at seipin/Nem1 ER subdomains. [PMID:32349126 "LDs induced by the yeast triacylglycerol (TAG)-synthases Lro1 and Dga1 are formed at discrete ER subdomains defined by seipin (Fld1)"]
- Secondary MGAT activity. [PMID:20554061 "Indeed, MGAT activity was decreased by 60% in Dga1p-deficient preparations, compared to the wild-type control"]
- Secondary acylceramide synthesis. [PMID:22738231 "Its biosynthesis was dependent on the diacylglycerol acyltransfereases Lro1p and Dga1p"]
- Kinetics suggest Dga1 is rate-limiting for TAG synthesis. [PMID:27620384 "This supports Dga1p being rate limiting for TAG synthesis."]
- Curation decisions: DGAT activity, TAG biosynthesis, and the ER membrane/LD locations are core. MGAT, ceramide, lipid storage and TAG homeostasis are non-core. The RCA cytosol row is removed.
