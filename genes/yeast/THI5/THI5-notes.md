# THI5 (YFL058W, P43534) review notes

## Identity
- HMP-P synthase THI5; NMT1/THI5 family; homodimer; Fe cofactor [UniProt:P43534].
- One of four subtelomeric paralogues THI5/THI11/THI12/THI13 [PMID:12777485 "The THI5 gene family of Saccharomyces cerevisiae comprises four highly conserved members named THI5 (YFL058w), THI11 (YJR156c), THI12 (YNL332w) and THI13 (YDL244w)."]. THI5, THI11 and THI12 differ at only 1-3 of 340 positions (ungapped comparison of the UniProt sequences in this batch).

## Function
- Structure/function of yeast Thi5p; PLP is a substrate, iron essential [PMID:23048037 "Mutational and yeast complementation studies identify the key residues for HMP-P biosynthesis as well as the use of pyridoxal 5'-phosphate as a substrate rather than as a cofactor."; "we could show that iron binding to HMP-P synthase is essential for the reaction"].
- Candida albicans orthologue: His66 of the enzyme is the histidine source; single turnover [PMID:22568620 "Our data suggest that His66 of the THI5 protein is the histidine source for pyrimidine formation and that the pyrimidine synthase is a single-turnover enzyme."].
- Genetic redundancy of the four paralogues [PMID:12777485 "Phenotypic analyses of these mutant strains have shown the four genes to be functionally redundant in terms of HMP formation for thiamin biosynthesis"].
- RSP5 WW-domain protein-array interaction (PMID:16606443): high-throughput; no functional meaning established.

## Curation conclusions
- Core MF GO:0106344 HMP-P synthase activity from histidine and PLP; BP GO:0009228 thiamine biosynthetic process; location not determined experimentally (presumed cytosol; ND for CC retained).
- THI5 donates its own His side chain, so it does the chemistry of the step (participant, not merely necessary).
- YeastCyc PWY3O-17 lists only THI13 for this step; THI5/THI11/THI12 are missing.
