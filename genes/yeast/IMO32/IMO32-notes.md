# Curation Notes for Saccharomyces cerevisiae IMO32 (UniProtKB:P53219)

## 1. Identity, Active-Site Conservation, and Matrix Import (`PANTHER:PTHR46118:SF4`)
- **Gene / UniProtKB**: `IMO32` (`YGR031W` / `SGD:S000003263` / `UniProtKB:P53219`, reviewed Swiss-Prot entry `IMO32_YEAST`, 342 aa, `sv=1`). Named *Intermediate cleaved by Mitochondrial Octapeptidyl aminopeptidase protein 32* (`PMID:19837041`, `PMID:21525245`); locus contains the nested antisense gene `NAG1`.
- **Saccharomycetaceae paralogy and catalytic triad (`file:human/ABHD11/ABHD11-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md`)**:
  - In budding yeast, a Saccharomycetaceae duplication within `PANTHER:PTHR46118:SF4` produced `IMO32` (`YGR031W`, `P53219`) and `EAT1` (`YGR015C`, `P53208`). Unlike `EAT1`, `IMO32` is **not** involved in ethyl acetate synthesis (`CC -!- FUNCTION: ... Not involved in the synthesis of ethyl acetate (PubMed:28356220)` in `IMO32-uniprot.txt`).
  - `Imo32` (`P53219`) conserves the canonical oxyanion-hole motif `82-HGLGG-86` and a complete alpha/beta-hydrolase catalytic triad with glutamate at the beta-6 acidic position: `Ser152` (`148-IIGHSMGGK-156`, `FT ACT_SITE 152`), `Glu176` (`172-LVCIENAPV-180`), and `His319` (`315-PRAGHWVHA-323`, `FT ACT_SITE 319`).
- **Two independent lines of mitochondrial matrix localization (`NEW` `IC` `GO:0005759` per `projects/ORGANELLES.md`)**:
  - GOA currently carries six `GO:0005739` (`mitochondrion`) rows (`HDA` `PMID:14562095`, `HDA` `PMID:16823961`, `HDA` `PMID:24769239`, `IBA` `GO_REF:0000033`, `IDA` `PMID:21525245`, `IEA` `GO_REF:0000044`) — all `ACCEPT` — and **zero** sub-compartment rows.
  - However, `Imo32` is one of the defining experimental substrates of sequential **mitochondrial matrix** peptidase processing (`PMID:19837041`, `PMID:21525245`): (1) its N-terminal presequence (`FT TRANSIT 1..38`) is cleaved by the matrix-localized mitochondrial processing peptidase (MPP), and (2) the resulting unstable intermediate octapeptide (`FT PROPEP 39..46`) is cleaved in organello and in vivo by the matrix-resident octapeptidyl aminopeptidase `Oct1` (`Mip1`) to generate stable mature `Imo32` (`residues 47..342`), corroborated by soluble mitochondrial proteome detection (`PMID:16823961`, `PMID:24769239`) and orthology to matrix-resident mammalian `ABHD11` (`PMID:32792488`). Because sequential MPP + Oct1 processing occurs exclusively in the mitochondrial matrix (`The vast majority of presequences are proteolytically removed by the mitochondrial processing peptidase (MPP) localized in the matrix`, `PMID:21525245`), we add `GO:0005759` (`mitochondrial matrix`) as `NEW` `IC` (`located_in`, `PMID:21525245`, `supporting_entities: [GO:0005739]`) beside the retained `GO:0005739` rows.

## 2. Audit of SGD Gene Description (`file:yeast/IMO32/IMO32-deep-research-sgd.md`)
- Verbatim SGD text: `Conserved mitochondrial protein of unknown function; processed by both mitochondrial processing peptidase and mitochondrial octapeptidyl aminopeptidase; gene contains the nested antisense gene NAG1`
- Assessment: Accurate on mitochondrial localization, sequential MPP + Oct1 processing (`PMID:19837041`, `PMID:21525245`), nested antisense `NAG1`, and the unresolved endogenous physiological substrate; however, calling `Imo32` simply a `"protein of unknown function"` omits its conserved alpha/beta-hydrolase catalytic-triad esterase architecture (`Ser152-Glu176-His319`, `PMID:28465236`, `PMID:28356220`). Recorded in `reference_review` (`relevance: HIGH`, `correctness: VERIFIED` with `MISSING_STATEMENT` `source_defects` suggesting mentioning its conserved alpha/beta-hydrolase serine-esterase domain).

## 3. Over-Annotated Triacylglycerol Lipase Claims (`PMID:28465236`)
- Arya et al. (`PMID:28465236`) overexpressed human `ABHD11` and yeast `YGR031W` (`IMO32`) in whole yeast cells and observed altered whole-cell `[14C]acetate`-labeled triacylglycerol levels, abolished by mutating all conserved `GXSXG` residues. However, no purified direct triacylglycerol hydrolysis kinetics were demonstrated, `Imo32` resides inside the mitochondrial matrix (`PMID:21525245`) rather than cytosolic lipid droplets, and human `ABHD11` tested in the same paper lacks mono-/diacylglycerol lipase activity (`PMID:27544863`) and physiologically deglutarylates lipoylated OGDHc (`PMID:40664791`). Thus `GO:0004806` (`triacylglycerol lipase activity`, `IDA` and `IMP`) and `GO:0006629` (`lipid metabolic process`, `IBA` and `IDA`) are `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED`, while `GO:0052689` (`carboxylic ester hydrolase activity`, `IBA`) is `ACCEPT`.
