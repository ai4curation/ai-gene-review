Gene Description

S-methyl-5'-thioadenosine phosphorylase (MTAP, EC 2.4.2.28) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, a homotrimeric PNP/MTAP-family enzyme that phosphorolyses 5'-methylthioadenosine (MTA), the by-product of spermidine/spermine synthesis, to adenine and 5-methylthioribose 1-phosphate. This is the entry step of the methionine salvage pathway and also recycles adenine. meu1 deletion abolishes MTAP activity, causes MTA accumulation, and raises ornithine decarboxylase activity and polyamine pools, because downstream salvage metabolites (notably 4-methylthio-2-oxobutanoate) feed back to repress ODC. MEU1 was first isolated as a multicopy enhancer of UAS2-dependent ADH2 expression. Meu1 is a cytoplasmic/nuclear soluble protein that has also been recovered bound to its own mRNA.

  • MTA phosphorylase, EC 2.4.2.28, PNP/MTAP family, homotrimer [UniProt:Q07938 "Reaction=S-methyl-5'-thioadenosine + phosphate = 5-(methylsulfanyl)-"].

  • Originally isolated as multicopy enhancer of UAS2-dependent ADH2 expression PMID:8807288; diffuse localization PMID:8807288.
