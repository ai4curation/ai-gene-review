# MEU1 (YLR017W, Q07938) notes

- MTA phosphorylase, EC 2.4.2.28, PNP/MTAP family, homotrimer [UniProt:Q07938 "Reaction=S-methyl-5'-thioadenosine + phosphate = 5-(methylsulfanyl)-"].
- Identified as MTAP; meu1 cells have elevated ODC and polyamines [PMID:14506228 "We show that in Saccharomyces cerevisiae the MEU1 gene encodes MTAP and that Meu1delta cells have an 8-fold increase in ODC activity, resulting in large elevations in polyamine pools."]. KMTB represses ODC (feedback from salvage to polyamine synthesis).
- Verified by Pirkov 2008 [PMID:18625006 "The 5'-methylthioadenosine phosphorylase was verified as Meu1p"].
- Originally isolated as multicopy enhancer of UAS2-dependent ADH2 expression [PMID:8807288 "Disruption of MEU1 reduced endogenous ADH2 expression about twofold but had no effect on cell viability or growth."]; diffuse localization [PMID:8807288 "A MEU1/beta-gal fusion protein was not localized to a particular region of the cell."].
- SGD IMP "L-glutamate biosynthetic process" from PMID:8807288: abstract does not mention glutamate; WebFetch of PMC1207385 returned no glutamate phenotype either (likely only partial text) -> UNDECIDED.
- mRNA binding: Meu1 associated with its own mRNA [PMID:21124907 "Four of the 13 proteins (30%) were associated with their own mRNA (Pfk2, Pre2, Map1, Meu1)."] -> non-core.
