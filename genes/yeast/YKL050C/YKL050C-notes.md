# YKL050C / LPX2 notes

- Read cached full-text PMID:24187129 and PMID:36164978. Ploier et al. tested
  Ykl050cp among GXSXG-motif candidate lipases; Yifrach et al. localized
  Ykl050c to the peroxisomal matrix, measured lipidomic changes in the deletion
  strain, and named it Lpx2.
- The exact LPX2 substrate is not known. `GO:0016298` lipase activity is the
  conservative new molecular-function proposal; process-level annotation should
  wait for a substrate or pathway-specific assay.
- PAINT PTHR28298 has 2026 IRDs at PTN002001654 that block the EIS1-family
  eisosome and eisosome-assembly calls from reaching the YKL050C/LPX2 leaf.
  The literature supports that loss: the WGD paralog has a peroxisomal lipase
  role rather than a Pil1/Lsp1 eisosome role.
