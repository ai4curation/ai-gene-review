Gene Description

DPH2 (YKL191W) encodes the second paralogous subunit of the 2-(3-amino-3-carboxypropyl)histidine synthase (Dph1-Dph2 heterodimer) that performs the first step of diphthamide biosynthesis on translation elongation factor 2 (eEF2): radical-type cleavage of S-adenosylmethionine and transfer of the 3-amino-3-carboxypropyl group to His699. Dph2 is homologous to the archaeal Dph2 homodimeric enzyme and binds its own [4Fe-4S] cluster through conserved cysteines, but in the eukaryotic heterodimer the Dph2 cluster is not catalytic; it is required for the physiological Dph3(KTI11)/Cbr1/NADH system to reduce the catalytic cluster in Dph1. Both subunits are required in vivo and neither substitutes for the other. dph2 nulls are viable but lack diphthamide and resist diphtheria toxin and sordarin; human DPH2 loss-of-function variants cause a diphthamide-deficiency developmental disorder, and such variants fail to complement in yeast. Dph2 is cytoplasmic.

Core Functions

Non-catalytic [4Fe-4S] subunit of the Dph1-Dph2 ACP-histidine synthase; its cluster is required for electron delivery from Dph3/Cbr1/NADH to the catalytic Dph1 cluster, so Dph2 contributes to the first step of diphthamide biosynthesis.

SGD gene description for DPH2 (yeast)

Protein required for synthesis of diphthamide; required along with Dph1p, Kti11p, Jjj3p, and Dph5p; diphthamide is a modified histidine residue of translation elongation factor 2 (Eft1p or Eft2p); forms a complex with Dph1p that catalyzes the first step of diphthamide biosynthesis

DPH2 (YKL191W, P32461) notes

Module diphthamide_biosynthesis, step 1, non-catalytic [4Fe-4S] subunit of Dph1-Dph2.

  • Cloned by complementation PMID:8406038.
  • Dph2 cluster enables physiological reduction of the Dph1 cluster PMID:31463593.
  • Human DPH2 patient variants impair diphthamide in yeast PMID:32576952.
  • Interacts with eEF2 (EFT2) in PCA/interactome screens [UniProt:P32461].

Observations: IEA "enables" GO:0090560 from InterPro is loose (archaeal Dph2 homodimer is catalytic; yeast Dph2 contributes_to); accepted with note. GO:1902494 -> GO:0120513.
