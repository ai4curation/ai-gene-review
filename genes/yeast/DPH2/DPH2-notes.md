# DPH2 (YKL191W, P32461) notes

Module `diphthamide_biosynthesis`, step 1, non-catalytic [4Fe-4S] subunit of Dph1-Dph2.

- Cloned by complementation [PMID:8406038 "A gene involved in diphthamide biosynthesis, DPH2, was cloned from Saccharomyces cerevisiae by complementation of a diphthamide mutant."].
- Dph2 cluster enables physiological reduction of the Dph1 cluster [PMID:31463593 "Dph3/Cbr1/NADH system cannot reduce the cluster in Dph1 without the cluster in Dph2"].
- Human DPH2 patient variants impair diphthamide in yeast [PMID:32576952 "Both DPH2 variants described here severely impair diphthamide biosynthesis as demonstrated in human and yeast cells."].
- Interacts with eEF2 (EFT2) in PCA/interactome screens [UniProt:P32461].

Observations: IEA "enables" GO:0090560 from InterPro is loose (archaeal Dph2 homodimer is catalytic; yeast Dph2 contributes_to); accepted with note. GO:1902494 -> GO:0120513.
