# PAH1 (YMR165C, P32567) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications; no paid deep research).

- Pah1 is the yeast lipin and the Mg2+-dependent PAP. [PMID:16467296 "Heterologous expression of PAH1 in Escherichia coli confirmed that Pah1p is a Mg(2+)-dependent PA phosphatase enzyme"]
- It is essential for TAG synthesis among the four PAP genes. [PMID:24196957 "showed the essential role of PAH1 in triacylglycerol synthesis throughout growth"]
- Pah1 is inactive in the cytosol and active on the membrane. [PMID:35026226 "Pah1 is inactive in the cytosol as a phosphorylated form and becomes active on the membrane as a dephosphorylated form by the Nem1-Spo7 protein phosphatase."]
- The catalytic DXDXT motif is required for all of its physiological roles. [PMID:17971454 "These results demonstrated that the PAP1 activity of the PAH1 gene product is essential for its roles in lipid metabolism and cell physiology."]
- Pleiotropic phenotypes: lipid droplet number, vacuole fusion, respiration, and plasmid stability. [PMID:21422231 "Disruption of PAH1 resulted in a 63% decrease in droplet number"] [PMID:22121197 "Deletion of PAH1 was sufficient to cause marked vacuole fragmentation and abolish vacuole fusion."] [PMID:8437575 "Disruption of the genomic SMP2 gene resulted in a respiration-deficient phenotype"]
- Curation decisions: PAP activity, DAG and TAG biosynthesis, and the nuclear/ER membrane are core. The promoter ChIP-based "cis-regulatory region binding" and the IBA "fatty acid catabolic process" (a mammalian PPARalpha-coactivator role) are marked over-annotated. The RCA "is_active_in cytosol" row is modified to ER membrane. LD organisation, vacuole fusion, respiration and plasmid maintenance are non-core.
