CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mg(2+)-dependent phosphatidate (PA) phosphatase which
CC       catalyzes the dephosphorylation of PA to yield diacylglycerol
CC       (PubMed:16467296, PubMed:16968695, PubMed:17910939, PubMed:17971454,
CC       PubMed:20876142, PubMed:21081492, PubMed:29765047, PubMed:39577771).
CC       Required for de novo lipid synthesis and formation of lipid droplets
CC       (PubMed:21422231). Controls transcription of phospholipid biosynthetic
CC       genes and nuclear structure by regulating the amount of membrane
CC       present at the nuclear envelope (PubMed:15889145). Involved in plasmid
CC       maintenance, in respiration and in cell proliferation (PubMed:8437575).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15889145, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16467296,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16968695, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17910939,
