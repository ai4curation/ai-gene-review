# URA10 (YMR271C, UniProt P30402) notes

## Function
- Minor OPRTase paralogue of URA5 [UniProt:P30402]; low abundance (815 molecules/cell) [UniProt:P30402].
- "Comparison of the predicted amino acid sequences between URA5 and URA10 genes shows more than 75% similarity" [PMID:2182197].
- "URA10 gene expression is responsible for the leaky phenotype of a ura5 mutant"; "the URA10 product contributes only 20% of the total activity" [PMID:2182197].
- Induced by DHO via Ppr1: "DHO is the effector of the positive control of the transcription of at least four genes, URA1, URA3, URA4 and URA10" [PMID:1511880].
- Proposed OMP channelling with Ura3 [PMID:2679804] (unverified hypothesis).

## Module
- de_novo_pyrimidine_synthesis lists URA10 as "Minor paralog"; YeastCyc lists URA5+URA10 on OROPRIBTRANS-RXN. Consistent.
