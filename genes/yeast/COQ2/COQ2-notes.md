# COQ2 (YNR041C, P32378) notes

4-hydroxybenzoate polyprenyltransferase (UbiA family).

- COQ2 required for PHB:polyprenyltransferase activity [PMID:1740455 "Disruption of the chromosomal COQ2 gene indicates the gene is not essential for viability, yet is required for PHB:polyprenyltransferase activity and respiratory function."]; mito leader, ~6 TM [PMID:1740455].
- Complements E. coli ubiA; broad chain-length specificity [PMID:7765507 "This strongly supports the idea that COQ2 catalyzes the same enzymatic reaction with UbiA and the substrate specificity of COQ2 is broad."].
- Activity in mitochondrial membranes [PMID:359053].
- Integral IMM, matrix-facing [PMID:24406904 "The results indicate both Coq2 and Coq7 polypeptides are inner membrane proteins facing the matrix side in yeast mitochondria."].
- Reactome TAS IMS location (R-SCE-1268015, TIM23 SORT import event) is an import transit location -> REMOVE. YeastPathways RCA cytosol -> REMOVE.
