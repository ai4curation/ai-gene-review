# SGD gene description for AAT2 (yeast)

Cytosolic aspartate aminotransferase involved in the metabolism of nitrogen and amino acids such as aspartate; localizes to and functions in peroxisomes in oleate-grown cells; moonlighting role as a ribosome-associated protein that modulates translation in response to stress
