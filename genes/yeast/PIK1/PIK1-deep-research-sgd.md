# SGD gene description for PIK1 (yeast)

Phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase; catalyzes the first step in the biosynthesis of phosphatidylinositol-4,5-biphosphate; may control cytokinesis through the actin cytoskeleton; required for autophagosome formation during autophagy and for lipophagy in both stationary phase cells and during nitrogen starvation; may control nonselective autophagy and mitophagy through trafficking of Atg9p; localizes to the nucleus and to the trans-Golgi network
