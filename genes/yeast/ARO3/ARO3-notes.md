# ARO3 (YDR035W, P14843) notes

Pathway context: DAHP synthase, step 1 of shikimate pathway (EC 2.5.1.54); Phe-inhibited isozyme.

- Two isozymes ARO3/ARO4, redundant [PMID:1348717 "Mutations in both genes are necessary to cause aromatic amino acid (aa) auxotrophy, since one isoenzyme alone is sufficient to produce enough DAHP for normal growth of the cells."].
- ARO3 = Phe-dependent isozyme; cloned with ~50x overproduction [PMID:2880280 "Gene ARO3, coding for the phenylalanine-dependent isozyme of DAHP synthase was cloned"; "DAHP synthase levels of about 50-fold the wild-type level were observed"].
- Feedback specificity determined by Ser (Aro3) vs Gly226 (Aro4) [PMID:12540830 "An exchange of glycine to serine and vice versa leads to a complete change in the regulation pattern."].
- Mitochondrion HDA (proteomics, PMID:14576278, PMID:16823961) marked over-annotated (abundant cytosolic enzyme, no MTS). Nucleus HDA (Huh GFP) kept non-core.
- Module note: class I (AroG/F/H-like, PTHR21225) DAHPS; plants and many bacteria use class II (AroGII/DAHP_synth_2) enzymes - family varies by taxon.
