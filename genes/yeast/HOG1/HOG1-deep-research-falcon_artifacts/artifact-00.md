(pqac-00000010, pqac-00000016) | Cycles between cytoplasm and nucleus basally; enriched in the nucleus after hyperosmotic shock. | Hog1 affects transcription of approximately 600 genes involved in glycerol production and restoration of osmotic balance. (pqac-00000017) | Cytoplasmic domains of the plasma-membrane aquaglyceroporin Fps1. | After 1.8 M sorbitol shock, most Rgc2 dissociates from Fps1 within 10 seconds and remains dissociated for at least 20 minutes. (pqac-00000017) |
| Cell-cycle adaptation | Hog1 directly phosphorylates nucleolar Net1, changes its interaction with Cdc14, and delays mitotic exit during osmostress; a nonphosphorylatable Net1 mutant has reduced stress viability. (pqac-00000011) | Nucleolus and nucleus during metaphase/early mitosis. | The demonstrated outcome is a Hog1-dependent mitotic delay that protects rDNA and telomere segregation; the cited study does not report a single universal delay duration. (pqac-00000011) |
| Signal termination | PP2C phosphatase Ptc1 directly removes the activation-loop phosphothreonine from Hog1, but not phosphotyrosine, thereby lowering basal activity and restoring transient signaling. (pqac-00000015) | Both nucleus and cytoplasm. | Osmotic stress increased Hog1 activity about 12-fold in wild type; PTC1 overexpression limited activation to about 3-fold. (pqac-00000015) |
