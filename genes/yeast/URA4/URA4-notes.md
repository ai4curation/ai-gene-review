# URA4 (YLR420W, UniProt P20051) notes

## Function
- Dihydroorotase (EC 3.5.2.3), third step of de novo UMP synthesis; class II (homodimeric, enterobacterial-type) DHOase, Zn2+ cofactor by similarity to E. coli PyrC [UniProt:P20051].
- "The URA4 gene of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, coding for the third enzyme of the pyrimidine pathway, has been cloned through phenotypic complementation of a ura4 mutant" [PMID:2897615].
- "S. cerevisiae dihydroorotase shows 40% homology with the S. typhimurium and E. coli enzymes" [PMID:2897615].
- Ura2 has an inactive DHOase-like domain, so Ura4 is the sole dihydroorotase: "a domain that resembles dihydroorotase (DHOase-like) but does not have DHOase activity" [PMID:2679804].
- Cytoplasmic by GFP survey [PMID:14562095] (HDA; also nucleus).
