# ELO2 (FEN1/GNS1/VBM2, YCR034W; UniProt P25358) notes

Evidence journal (no paid deep research run; built from UniProt and cached publications).

## Function
- ELO-family 3-keto-acyl-CoA synthase (condensing enzyme), first and rate-limiting step of the ER VLCFA elongation cycle; EC 2.3.1.199 [UniProt:P25358].
- Elongase II: [PMID:12684876 "Elongase II elongates palmitoyl-CoA and stearoyl-CoA up to C22 fatty acids, and elongase III synthesizes 20-26-carbon fatty acids from C18-CoA primers"]; [PMID:12684876 "Elongases I, II and III are specifically inactivated in, respectively, elo1, elo2 and elo3 mutants"].
- [PMID:9211877 "Elo2p appears to be involved in the elongation of fatty acids up to 24 carbons"]; elo2 elo3 double mutant synthetic lethal [PMID:9211877 "The simultaneous disruption of ELO2 and ELO3 has also been shown to produce synthetic lethality"].
- In vitro reconstitution: [PMID:17719544 "Proteoliposomes reconstituted here from purified membrane components-an elongase protein (Elop), a novel dehydratase, and two reductases-catalyzed repeated rounds of two-carbon addition"]; active site faces cytosol, chain-length lysine near luminal end of TM helix [PMID:17719544].
- fen1 membranes impaired in elongation beyond C18-CoA [PMID:23325927 "However, elongations beyond C18-CoA and C22-CoA were largely impaired in fen1 Δ and sur4 Δ cells, respectively."].
- Co-IP with Tsc13 [PMID:11113186 "Tsc13p coimmunoprecipitates with Elo2p and Elo3p"].

## Location
- ER membrane: [PMID:9832547 "which encode related ER-localized membrane proteins"]; Elo2-GFP ER pattern [PMID:11113186 "Even at very low expression levels, Elo2p-GFP distribution in the ER was indistinguishable from the wild-type pattern"].

## Indirect phenotypes (curated as over-annotation)
- v-SNARE bypass in secretion (VBM2) [PMID:9832547 "act at the level of lipid synthesis to affect the ability of secretory vesicles to sort and deliver protein cargo"].
- Endosomal trafficking synthetic defects with vps21 [PMID:23325927 "requirement of VLCFAs is especially high in the endosomal pathways"].
