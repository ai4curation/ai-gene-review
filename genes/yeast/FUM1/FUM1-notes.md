# FUM1 (YPL262W, P08417) notes

- Single fumarase gene; mitochondrial and cytosolic forms from one gene [PMID:3040736 "Mutations in FUM1 simultaneously abolish both activities."].
- Single translation product, MPP-processed, distributed between cytosol and mitochondria [PMID:11585823 "a single translation product of the FUM1 gene encoding fumarase is distributed between the cytosol and mitochondria"].
- fum1 mutant excretes fumarate [PMID:1587456 "accumulated extracellular fumaric acid"].
- Cytosolic pool recruited to nucleus after DSBs; activity-dependent [PMID:20231875 "recruited from the cytosol to the nucleus upon DNA damage induction"; "This function of fumarase depends on its enzymatic activity"].
