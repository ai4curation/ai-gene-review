# HEM3 (YDL205C, P28789) notes

Porphobilinogen deaminase / hydroxymethylbilane synthase.

- hem3 mutants deficient in PBG deaminase; gene cloned by complementation [PMID:1508149 "hem3 mutants in Saccharomyces cerevisiae are deficient in porphobilinogen deaminase activity."].
- Old name "uroporphyrinogen I synthase" [PMID:323256 "hem3 mutants uroporphyrin I synthase"]; hem3 also Met-requiring (siroheme) [PMID:323256].
- Reaction 4 PBG + H2O = hydroxymethylbilane + 4 NH4+; dipyrromethane cofactor [UniProt:P28789].
- HAP2/3-dependent expression [PMID:1508149]. Rate-limiting on overproduction [PMID:14559249].
- Location: cytoplasm/nucleus GFP [PMID:14562095].
