# SGA1 (YIL099W, P08019) notes

Intracellular sporulation-specific glucoamylase (GH15, EC 3.2.1.3). Module: glycogen_metabolism_fungal.

- "The partially purified enzyme was active against glycogen, starch, maltotriose and maltose" ; pH optimum 5.5; glycosylated [PMID:2493265]
- Vacuolar: "SGA was preferentially recovered in fractions which were enriched for the vacuolar hydrolases" [PMID:2493265]
- "Loss of the vacuolar glucosidase, SGA1, also protected glycogen stores, but only very late in the stationary phase" [PMID:11486014]
- "Sga1 which localizes in the vacuole or to prospore membranes during sporulation" [PMID:38832010]

## Curation decisions
- cytosol RCA (YeastPathways GLYCOCAT-YEAST-PWY compartment): REMOVE; Sga1 is vacuolar.
