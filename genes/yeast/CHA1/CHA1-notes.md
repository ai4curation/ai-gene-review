# CHA1 (YCL064C, P25379) notes

- Catabolic L-serine/threonine dehydratase, EC 4.3.1.17 and 4.3.1.19 [UniProt:P25379 "RecName: Full=Catabolic L-serine/threonine dehydratase;"].
- Discovery: [PMID:7042346 "This mutation, which also impairs the ability to utilize L-serine, has been denominated cha-, for 'catabolism of hydroxyamino acids' and was found to result in the lack of a catabolic L-serine (L-threonine) deaminase"]; prefers serine [PMID:7042346 "This enzyme which, unlike the anabolic threonine deaminase, is more active towards serine than towards threonine"]; induced [PMID:7042346 "Its synthesis is induced by L-serine and L-threonine"].
- Location: mitochondrion (GFP PMID:14562095; proteomics PMID:16823961) [UniProt:P25379 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion"]; mtDNA nucleoid association reported (PMID:15692048, abstract-only).
- Interaction with Rck1 kinase from HT screens (PMID:11805837, 17892321): protein binding -> REMOVE.

## Curation observations
- RCA cytosol rows (SERDEG-PWY, THREOCAT2-PWY) -> REMOVE; YeastPathways default compartment.
- Not a member of BCAA biosynthesis: catabolic paralog of ILV1 (PTHR48078:SF2 vs ILV1 SF11). Cha1 threonine deaminase can produce 2-oxobutanoate but Ilv1 is the biosynthetic entry enzyme; limited cross-compensation [PMID:7042346 "display a limited ability to compensate for one another's absence and appear to play clearly distinct roles under normal physiological conditions"].
