# ERG28 (YER044C, P40030) review notes

## Identity
- Ergosterol biosynthetic protein 28, alias BUD18; ERG28 family; multi-pass ER membrane protein [UniProt:P40030].
- Conserved from yeast to plants and humans [PMID:11160377 "ERG28 was also found in Schizosaccharomyces pombe and Arabidopsis as well as humans"].

## Function: non-catalytic scaffold of the C-4 demethylation complex
- Identified by expression-profile compendium as a sterol gene [PMID:10929718 "we identify and experimentally confirm that eight uncharacterized open reading frames encode proteins required for sterol metabolism"].
- Deletion: slow growth, not ergosterol auxotroph, C-4 demethylation lesion [PMID:11160377 "indicates a lesion in sterol C-4 demethylation"]; accumulation of 3-keto and carboxylic acid sterols [PMID:11160377 "Sterol profiles indicate accumulation of 3-keto and carboxylic acid sterol intermediates"].
- ER localisation and complex with Erg25/26/27 [PMID:12119386 "Green fluorescent protein-fusion protein localization studies showed that Erg26p, Erg27p, and Erg28p are all located in the endoplasmic reticulum."]; scaffold model [PMID:12119386 "we suggest that Erg28p works as a transmembrane scaffold to tether Erg27p and possibly other C-4 demethylation proteins (Erg25p, Erg26p), forming a demethylation complex in the endoplasmic reticulum."].
- Bridges to Erg6 [PMID:15522820 "Erg28p may not only anchor the C-4 demethylation enzyme complex to the ER but also acts as a protein bridge to the Erg6p enzyme required for the next ergosterol biosynthetic step."]; Erg6 ER level halves in erg28 [PMID:15522820 "the Erg6p level in the ER fraction was decreased by about 50% relative to the wild-type strain"].
- Split-ubiquitin: Erg28 with Erg11/Erg25/Erg27 forms core of "ergosome" [PMID:16300994 "Erg11p, Erg25p, Erg27p, and Erg28p appear to form a core center that can interact with other enzymes in the pathway"]; also Erg1, Erg11 [PMID:15995173 "Erg28p not only interacted with the C-4 demethylation enzymes and Erg6p but also with Erg11p and Erg1p."].
- Conserved motif needed for C-4 demethylation [PMID:29319811 "Erg28p further identified a highly conserved 10-amino acid sequence"].
- PDR10 interaction (PMID:23831759) is a high-throughput MYTH screen hit of an ABC transporter; ERG28 not discussed in the text; no functional significance.

## Curation conclusions
