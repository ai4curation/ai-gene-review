# SGD gene description for LRO1 (yeast)

Acyltransferase that converts diacylglycerol to triacylglycerol (TGA); one of several acyltransferases that contribute to TGA synthesis; relocates from ER to nuclear membrane in response cell cycle and starvation signals; Lro1p and Dga1p can O-acylate ceramides; putative homolog of human lecithin cholesterol acyltransferase
