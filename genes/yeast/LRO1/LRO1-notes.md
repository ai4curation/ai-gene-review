# LRO1 (YNR008W, P40345) notes

Evidence journal (UniProt and cached publications; no paid deep research).

- Lro1 is an LCAT-related PDAT that transfers an acyl chain from PC/PE to DAG. [PMID:10747858 "In vitro microsomal assays determined that Lro1 mediated the esterification of diacylglycerol using phosphatidylcholine as the acyl donor."] [PMID:10829075 "This reaction is catalyzed by an enzyme that we call phospholipid:diacylglycerol acyltransferase, or PDAT."]
- PDAT forms a branch distinct from LCAT. [PMID:10829075 "budding yeast PDAT and its relatives in fission yeast and Arabidopsis form a distinct branch within this protein superfamily"] For this reason the CACAO ISO row (with/from mouse LCAT P16301) is removed as mis-sourced; the activity itself is accepted on the IDA/IMP/EXP rows.
- Substrate preference: the sn-2 acyl of PE. [PMID:18037386 "the highest activity was achieved with acyl groups at sn-2 position of phosphatidylethanolamine as acyl donor and 1,2-diacylglycerols as acyl acceptor"]
- Lro1 is a type II ER membrane protein with a luminal catalytic domain. [UniProt:P40345 "type II membrane protein {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22446555}"] It relocates to the inner nuclear membrane. [PMID:31422915 "relocates from the endoplasmic reticulum to a subdomain of the inner nuclear membrane"]
- Curation decisions: PDAT activity, TAG biosynthesis, and the ER membrane/INM are core. Lipid storage, ceramide, phospholipid homeostasis, LD and cytoplasm are non-core. The RCA cytosol row is removed.
