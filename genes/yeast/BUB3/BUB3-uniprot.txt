CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in mitotic spindle assembly checkpoint signaling, a
CC       process that delays anaphase until chromosomes are bioriented on the
CC       spindle, and in the repair of incorrect mitotic kinetochore-spindle
CC       microtubule attachments (PubMed:15879521, PubMed:24066227). Component
CC       of the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC) which inhibits the ubiquitin
CC       ligase activity of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) by
CC       preventing its activation by CDC20 (PubMed:15879521).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15879521, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24066227}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC) which
CC       consists of MAD2, MAD3, BUB3 and CDC20 (PubMed:11726501,
CC       PubMed:15879521). Part of complex consisting of MAD1, BUB1 and BUB3
CC       after activation of spindle checkpoint (PubMed:10837255,
CC       PubMed:17227844). Part of the BUB1-BUB3 complex, composed of BUB1 and
CC       BUB3 (PubMed:24066227). Interacts with SPC105 (via phosphorylated MELT
CC       motifs); the interaction is direct and occurs when part of the BUB1-
CC       BUB3 complex (PubMed:24066227). Interacts with MAD3; the interaction is
CC       direct (PubMed:17227844). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10837255,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11726501, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15879521,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17227844, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24066227}.
