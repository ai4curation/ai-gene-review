Executive Judgment

Verdict: Partially supported — the KEEP_AS_NON_CORE action is justified (conservative and appropriate).

The plasma-membrane annotation is real in the databases but weakly grounded. It rests on (1) a single high-throughput proteomics detection in a stripped plasma-membrane fraction (16622836, Delom et al. 2006) recorded as HDA, and (2) an IBA phylogenetic propagation from the HSP70 family (GO_REF:0000033). Neither establishes plasma membrane as a primary site of Ssa2 function. SSA2 is one of the most abundant cytosolic Hsp70 chaperones and has no transmembrane domain, no signal peptide, and no lipid anchor (UniProt P10592), so its appearance in a membrane pellet is best explained by abundant-cytosolic-protein carryover — the same fractions routinely capture elongation factors and enolase (16544286). There is genuine cell-periphery biology (histatin-5 receptor at the cell envelope, 12761219; secreted/cell-wall and extracellular-vesicle IDA annotations), but that is cell wall/secreted, not integral plasma membrane.

Most important caveats: (a) The evidence would also support a defensible remove/do-not-propagate argument for the IBA specifically; KEEP_AS_NON_CORE is the more conservative of two reasonable choices. (b) Curators should ensure the term is never treated as core and that the richer, better- evidenced peripheral terms (cell wall GO:0009277, extracellular region/vesicle) carry the cell-surface story instead.

Mechanistic Scope

The immediate molecular function of Ssa2 is ATP-dependent Hsp70 chaperone activity (nucleotide- binding domain + substrate-binding domain) acting in the cytosol/nucleus: de novo folding, post-translational translocation across organelle membranes, tRNA nuclear import, and assembly of client complexes. Any plasma-membrane signal is downstream/associative, not a direct PM activity: (i) fraction co-purification of an abundant soluble protein, and (ii) transient, client-directed membrane engagement (e.g., inserting viral replication proteins into membranes, 19153242). None of these convert PM into a site of intrinsic Ssa2 molecular function.

Conflicts and Alternatives

  • Contamination / frequency bias: Ssa2's extreme cytosolic abundance makes membrane-pellet detection the expected null result; the control literature (16544286) confirms cytoplasmic carryover in these fractions.
  • Paralog confusion: Ssa1/Ssa2 are ~97% identical and functionally redundant; several "cell-surface Ssa" studies (e.g., histatin) cannot resolve Ssa1 vs Ssa2 vs Ssa3/4. Cell-surface claims may be family-level, not SSA2-specific. Confirmed: SSA1 (P10591) carries the identical single HDA PM annotation from the same paper (16622836), i.e. the proteomics signal was propagated to both paralogs and cannot distinguish them.
  • Evidence-code carry-over (data integrity): the seed labels the reviewed annotation IBA (GO_REF:0000033), but the live GO record has no IBA to plasma membrane; the term is HDA-only. The "IBA is consistent" rationale in the review summary does not apply and should be revised.
  • Location mis-typing: the strongest peripheral evidence points to cell wall/cell envelope and secreted, which are distinct from the plasma membrane; GO:0005886 may be a coarse stand-in for these better-supported terms.
  • IBA source strength: the phylogenetic call inherits mammalian Hsc70 PM annotations, whose own "PM" status is itself partly surface/associative.
