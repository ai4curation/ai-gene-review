# SGD gene description for WSS1 (yeast)

SUMO-ligase and SUMO-targeted metalloprotease; involved in DNA repair; removes DNA-protein crosslinks at stalled replication forks during replication of damaged DNA; clears chromatin-bound sumoylated proteins; localizes to single spot on nuclear periphery of mother cells but not daughters; exhibits vacuolar localization upon genotoxic stress; activated by DNA binding; member of minigluzincins protease family with mammalian DVC1/Spartan
