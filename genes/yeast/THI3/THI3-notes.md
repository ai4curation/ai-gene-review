# THI3 (YDL080C, Q07471) notes

## Evidence journal
- TPP-binding regulator of THI genes [PMID:19013460 "Thi3p is a TPP-binding protein and upregulates THI genes expression when TPP is not bound."].
- Interacts with Pdc2p [PMID:19013460 "We found here that Pdc2p could transactivate gene expression and interact with Thi3p"] and Thi2p [PMID:16194233 "Furthermore, Thi2p and Thi3p were demonstrated to bind each other"]; TPP-binding mutants derepress THI genes [PMID:16194233].
- No decarboxylase activity [PMID:22904058 "No decarboxylation activity was found for Thi3 with any of the substrates tested."]; UniProt CAUTION records the conflict with earlier genetics [UniProt:Q07471].
- Earlier genetics: YDL080C "solely responsible" for alpha-ketoisocaproate decarboxylation [PMID:10753893], later attributed to Aro10 [PMID:15933030]; Thi3p regulatory role [PMID:15933030 "In addition, Thi3p has been assumed to be a positive regulator of the thiamine biosynthetic pathway."].
- Nucleus (GFP) [UniProt:Q07471].

## Curation decisions
- All decarboxylase MF rows (EXP/IEA/RCA), carboxy-lyase, catalytic activity, isoleucine catabolism RCA and cytosol RCA REMOVED (contradicted by direct assay).
- Core: TF binding (Thi2p/Pdc2p coactivator) in positive regulation of thiamine biosynthesis; TPP binding as sensor.
- YeastCyc assignment of THI3 to Ehrlich decarboxylation steps (isoleucine, leucine) is not supported; module curator should drop THI3 from the decarboxylase step or mark it as regulatory only.
