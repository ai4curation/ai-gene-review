Gene Description

Large (synthetase, CarB-family) subunit of the arginine-specific carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase (CPSase A) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Cpa2p contains two ATP-grasp domains and a C-terminal MGS-like domain required for catalysis; it binds bicarbonate, two Mg-ATP and ammonia and synthesises carbamoyl phosphate. Alone it uses free ammonia (EC 6.3.4.16); in the cytosolic heterodimer with the glutaminase subunit Cpa1p it uses ammonia derived from glutamine (EC 6.3.5.5). The product is used by ornithine carbamoyltransferase (ARG3) to form citrulline in arginine biosynthesis; carbamoyl phosphate from CPSase A also enters a cellular pool shared with the pyrimidine-specific CPSase of URA2.

Core Functions

Large (CarB-type, synthetase) subunit of the cytosolic arginine-specific carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase (CPSase A). Using two ATP-grasp domains, Cpa2p phosphorylates bicarbonate, condenses carboxyphosphate with ammonia (free, or delivered from glutamine by Cpa1p) and phosphorylates carbamate to carbamoyl phosphate, which ornithine carbamoyltransferase (ARG3) uses to make citrulline in arginine biosynthesis.
