# SGD gene description for PEP1 (yeast)

Type I transmembrane sorting receptor for multiple vacuolar hydrolases; plays role in post-ER quality control in the Golgi, involved in protein targeting from the endoplasmic reticulum to the vacuole for degradation; cycles between late-Golgi and prevacuolar endosome-like compartments; targeted to vacuole via AP-3 pathway
