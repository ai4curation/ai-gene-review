# SDH1 (YKL148C, Q00711) notes

- Flavoprotein subunit of complex II [PMID:9730279 "SDH1 coding for the flavoprotein subunit of mitochondrial succinate dehydrogenase"]; covalent FAD [PMID:1939170 "the mutant contains significantly lower levels of covalently attached flavin adenine dinucleotide cofactor"].
- SDH tetramer Sdh1-4 with cytochrome b562 [PMID:9929002]. Extrinsic domain Sdh1+Sdh2 [PMID:9822678 "The membrane extrinsic domain, consisting of Sdh1p and Sdh2p, contains a covalent FAD cofactor and three iron-sulfur clusters"].
- Assembly factors: Sdh5 (flavination) [PMID:19628817 "Sdh5 is required for SDH-dependent respiration and for Sdh1 flavination"]; Sdh8 chaperone [PMID:24954416 "Sdh8 interacts specifically with the catalytic Sdh1 subunit in the mitochondrial matrix"]; Tcm62 [PMID:9822678].
- Paralog SDH1b (YJL045W) weakly expressed but functional [PMID:9730279 "the isoenzyme encoded by SDH1b is unlikely to play an important role in mitochondrial respiration"]; in sake strain, SDH activity lost only in sdh1 sdh1b or sdh1 sdh2 double disruptants [PMID:16232921].
- Location: inner membrane, peripheral, matrix side [UniProt:Q00711].
