# ALG3 (YBL082C, RHK1; UniProt P38179) notes

## Activity / role
- Alg3 is the transferase itself [PMID:11308030 "This demonstrates that Alg3p represents the mannosyltransferase itself and not an accessory protein involved in the reaction."]
- Lumenal Dol-P-Man steps [PMID:11308030 "the final reactions from Man5GlcNAc2-PP-Dol to Man9GlcNAc2-PP-Dol on the lumenal side use Dol-P-Man"]
- Null phenotype [PMID:8842708 "The inactivation of the nonessential ALG3 gene results in the accumulation of lipid-linked Man5GlcNac2 and protein-bound carbohydrates which are completely Endo H resistant."]
- KKXX ER retrieval [PMID:8842708 "with a C-terminal KKXX-retrieval sequence"]
