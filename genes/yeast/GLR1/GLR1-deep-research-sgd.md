# SGD gene description for GLR1 (yeast)

Cytosolic and mitochondrial glutathione oxidoreductase; converts oxidized glutathione to reduced glutathione; cytosolic Glr1p is the main determinant of the glutathione redox state of the mitochondrial intermembrane space; mitochondrial Glr1p has a role in resistance to hyperoxia; also detected in peroxisomes; protein abundance increases in response to DNA replication stress
