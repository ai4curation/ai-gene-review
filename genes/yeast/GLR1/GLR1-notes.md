# GLR1 notes (YPL091W, P41921)

- Glutathione reductase EC 1.8.1.7, FAD, homodimer [UniProt:P41921 "Reaction=2 glutathione + NADP(+) = glutathione disulfide + NADPH +"]; two isoforms by alternative initiation [UniProt:P41921 "Event=Alternative initiation; Named isoforms=2;"].
- Cloning: [PMID:7737505 "Yeast cells transformed with a multicopy plasmid containing the genomic clone overproduced GR activity sixfold."]
- Cytosolic redox: [PMID:23242256 "we identify Trx2 and Grx2 as efficient backup systems to glutathione reductase for cytosolic GSSG reduction."]
- Stationary phase / Yap1: [PMID:8951820 "a yeast strain deleted for GLR1, encoding glutathione reductase, shows an elevated sensitivity to H2O2 challenge during stationary phase."]
- Localisation: cytosol + nucleus by GFP [PMID:23613772 "The cytosolic and nuclear localization of Glr1 in wild type cells (A) was maintained in naa30Δ cells (B)."]; peroxisomes and mitochondria [PMID:36164978 "Measuring the activity of glutathione reductase clearly demonstrates that native Glr1 is active in both peroxisomes and mitochondria"].
- Glutathionylation: [PMID:20074363 "GSSG levels were significantly lowered by the glutaredoxin system presumably due to the activity of Glr1."]
