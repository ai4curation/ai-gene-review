# BUD16 (YEL029C, P39988) notes

## Identity
- Putative pyridoxal kinase, pyridoxine kinase family (PANTHER PTHR10534; Pfam PF08543 Phos_pyr_kin; InterPro IPR004625 PyrdxlKinase) [UniProt:P39988].
- Name derives from a bud-site-selection screen (Ni & Snyder 2001, PMID:11452010, cited in UniProt) [UniProt:P39988 "Important for bud site selection"]. No GOA annotation is based on this.
- Paralog BUD17 (YNR027W) is also PdxK-like (not reviewed here).

## Function
- bud16 deletion lowers PLP to ~2% of wild type [PMID:17696614 "We found that the PLP levels of bud16Δ cells are only 1.8% of wild-type levels (Table 3)."]
- Synthetic lethal with loss of de novo PLP synthase SNZ1/SNO1 [PMID:17696614 "Overall, the decrease in intracellular PLP levels in bud16Δ along with its synthetic lethality with sno1snz1Δ are consistent with the idea that BUD16 functions in parallel with the de novo B6 pathway as a yeast pyridoxal kinase."]
- Loss causes gross chromosomal rearrangements, Rad52 foci and checkpoint activation, attributed to PLP deficiency (downstream phenotype, not a direct function) [PMID:17696614 "We show that Pdxk is critical for the maintenance of genome integrity via its role in maintaining adequate levels of pyridoxal 5′ phosphate (PLP)"].
- Metabolomics: pyridoxine accumulates in bud16 deletion, consistent with PN kinase activity [PMID:36164978 "the accumulation of pyridoxine in the absence of the putative pyridoxal kinase, Bud16"].
- No purified-enzyme kinetics for yeast Bud16 found; activity is inferred from homology to human PDXK (ISA, PMID:9099727) plus in vivo PLP/vitamer data.

## Localization
- Cytoplasm and nucleus (GFP, Huh et al. 2003) [UniProt:P39988 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm"].
- Also detected in peroxisomes in a high-content imaging screen, often dual-localized [PMID:36164978].

## Pathway context
- YeastPathways PLPSAL-PWY assigns EC 2.7.1.35 phosphorylation of PL, PM and PN; consistent with family and vitamer data.
