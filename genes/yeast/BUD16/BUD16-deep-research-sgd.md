# SGD gene description for BUD16 (yeast)

Putative pyridoxal kinase; a key enzyme involved in pyridoxal 5'-phosphate synthesis, the active form of vitamin B6; required for genome integrity; involved in bud-site selection; localizes to cytosol and peroxisomes; similarity to yeast BUD17 and human pyridoxal kinase (PDXK)
