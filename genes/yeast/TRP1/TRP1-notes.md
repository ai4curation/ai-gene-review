# TRP1 (YDR007W, P00912) notes

Pathway context: TRPSYN-PWY-1 step 3, phosphoribosylanthranilate isomerase (EC 5.3.1.24).

- Purified enzyme kinetics: [PMID:3286643 "According to the kinetic parameters of the purified isomerase protein, the enzyme is more active than, for example, the purified TRP3 enzyme indoleglycerol-phosphate synthase."]
- Weak constitutive promoter, high activity; possible separation from IGPS domain by rearrangement (PMID:3286643).
- Gene-dose / flux study [PMID:348687 "varying the gene dose of individual enzymes of the pathway at the tetraploid level"]
- Monofunctional TrpF in S. cerevisiae (fused to TrpC in some fungi, e.g. Neurospora trp-1) - family-level observation [UniProt:P00912 "Belongs to the TrpF family."]
