# GLO2 (YDR272W) notes

UniProt Q05584; cytoplasmic glyoxalase II (hydroxyacylglutathione hydrolase), EC 3.1.2.6 [UniProt:Q05584].

## Evidence journal
- GLO2 and GLO4 are 59% identical paralogues; glo2 glo4 double mutant has no glyoxalase II activity [PMID:9261170 "a glo2 glo4 double deletion strain contains no glyoxalase II activity at all and shows no obvious phenotype during vegetative growth"] and is methylglyoxal-sensitive like glo1 [PMID:9261170 "However, this strain showed a similar high sensitivity against exogenous methylglyoxal as compared with a glyoxalase I-deficient strain"].
- Expression: GLO2 on glucose and glycerol; GLO4 only on glycerol [PMID:9261170 "Whereas the GLO2 gene is expressed on both glucose and glycerol, the GLO4 gene is only active on glycerol"].
- Localisation: Glo2p cytoplasm, Glo4p mitochondrial matrix [PMID:9261170 "The active Glo2p protein is localized in the cytoplasm and the active Glo4p in the mitochondrial matrix"].
- Purified and kinetically characterised; Glo2 has sharp pH optimum 7-8 [PMID:10600466 "The cytoplasmic and the mitochondrial enzyme differed in the pH dependence of the kinetic parameters for the main substrate, S-d-lactoylglutathione"].
- Zn cofactor (2 per subunit) by similarity to human HAGH Q16775 [UniProt:Q05584].

## Decisions
All annotations ACCEPT except zinc ion binding (RCA, zinc proteome survey) KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. YeastPathways cytosol assignment is right for GLO2 (but not GLO4).
