# ERG27 (YLR100W) notes

UniProt Q12452; 3-keto-steroid reductase, EC 1.1.1.270; SDR (HSD17B7/Erg27 subfamily); ER and lipid particles [UniProt:Q12452].

## Evidence journal
- erg27 cannot convert 3-keto to 3-hydroxy sterols; accumulates 3-keto sterols on lanosterol feeding [PMID:10535978 "We developed a strategy to isolate a mutant deficient in converting 3-keto to 3-hydroxy-sterols."].
- Non-catalytic role: stabilises Erg7 [PMID:12842197 "we present evidence that Erg27p interacts with Erg7p, facilitating the association of Erg7p with lipid particles (LPs) and preventing digestion of Erg7p both in the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) and LPs."].
- ER mainly, some LP [PMID:12842197 "While Erg27p is essentially a component of the ER, it can also be detected in LPs."].

## Decisions
