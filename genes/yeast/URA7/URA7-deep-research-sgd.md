# SGD gene description for URA7 (yeast)

Major CTP synthase isozyme (see also URA8); catalyzes ATP-dependent transfer of amide nitrogen from glutamine to UTP, forming CTP, final step in de novo biosynthesis of pyrimidines; involved in phospholipid biosynthesis; targeted to vacuole via AP-3 pathway; capable of forming cytoplasmic filaments termed cytoophidium, especially during conditions of glucose depletion; URA7 has paralog URA8 that arose from the whole genome duplication
