# SGD gene description for DEG1 (yeast)

tRNA:pseudouridine synthase; introduces pseudouridines at position 38 or 39 in tRNA both cytoplasmic and mitochondrial tRNAs; also responsible for pseudouracil modification of some mRNAs; important for maintenance of translation efficiency and normal cell growth, localizes to both the nucleus and cytoplasm; non-essential for viability
