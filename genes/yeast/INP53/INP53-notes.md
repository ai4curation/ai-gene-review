# INP53 / SJL3 / SOP2 notes (YOR109W, Q12271)

- SAC + 5-phosphatase domains both active [PMID:10224048 "The 5-phosphatase domains of synaptojanin, Inp52p, and Inp53p are also catalytic"]; SAC substrates PI3P, PI4P, PI(3,5)P2 [PMID:10224048]
- Membrane-associated [PMID:9560389]; diffuse/punctate cytoplasm, stress-induced actin patch translocation [PMID:11094088 "In resting cells recombinant GFP-tagged 5-phosphatases were expressed diffusely throughout the cell but were excluded from the nucleus."]
- Functions in clathrin-dependent TGN-to-early endosome sorting, binds CHC1 [UniProt:Q12271]; no GOA annotation captures the TGN role (curation gap, not added here).
- Same YeastPathways reaction set as INP52; supported.
