# BNA7 (YDR428C) notes

## Identity and activity
- Kynurenine formamidase (arylformamidase, EC 3.5.1.9): N-formyl-L-kynurenine + H2O -> L-kynurenine + formate; alpha/beta hydrolase with HGGXW oxyanion-hole motif [UniProt:Q04066].
- Identified by Wogulis et al. 2008: [PMID:18205391 "demonstrate that YDR428C, which we have designated BNA7, is the yeast FKF"]; and showed that Bna3, earlier proposed as FKF, is instead most likely kynurenine aminotransferase [PMID:18205391 "Bna3p is not an FKF but rather is most likely the yeast kynurenine aminotransferase"].
- Second step of the kynurenine pathway [PMID:18205391 "N-Formyl kynurenine formamidase (FKF) catalyzes the second step of the NAD+ biosynthetic pathway"].
- Disruption: slow growth without nicotinate [UniProt:Q04066 "DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Cells exhibit slow growth in the absence of"] - this is the auxotrophy-type phenotype underlying the UniProt IMP "positive regulation of cell growth" annotation, which is a misreading of a metabolic growth defect as growth regulation.

## Location
- No dedicated localisation study in GOA; RCA cytosol from YeastPathways. Plausible (soluble hydrolase, no targeting signal) but not experimentally verified.
