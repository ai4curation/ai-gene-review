# FAA1 (YOR317W; UniProt P30624) notes

- Major long-chain acyl-CoA synthetase, EC 6.2.1.3; prefers C12-C16 [PMID:8206942 "Faa1p prefers C12:0-C16:0, with myristic and pentadecanoic acid (C15:0) having the highest activities"].
- Provides most oleoyl-CoA from exogenous oleate [PMID:11477098 "oleoyl CoA levels were reduced to less than 10% wild-type levels in faa1 Delta and faa1 Delta faa4 Delta strains"]; faa1 faa4 reduces exogenous oleate beta-oxidation 3-fold [PMID:11477098].
- Vectorial acylation with Fat1p (co-IP, two-hybrid) [PMID:12601005 "fatty acid import by vectorial acylation in yeast requires a multiprotein complex, which consists of Fat1p and Faa1p or Faa4p"].
- Plasma membrane (GFP) and long-chain base uptake [PMID:27136724 "We found that Faa1-GFP was mainly localized in the plasma membrane"; "These results suggest that Faa1 and Faa4 act as transporters both for LCFAs and LCBs"].
- Lipid droplets and ER [PMID:10515935; PMID:24868093 "Faa1-GFP appeared to be visible in additional cellular compartments, likely the ER"]; mitochondrial proteomics hits are unexplained (non-core).
- Sphingolipid-to-glycerolipid pathway (hexadecenoic acid activation) with Faa4 [PMID:22633490].

## Pathway-context observations
- YeastPathways places FAA1 at the EC 6.2.1.3 activation step of fatty acid oxidation. Faa1p acts outside peroxisomes; its acyl-CoA reaches beta-oxidation via Pxa1/Pxa2. Contribution real (oleate oxidation reduced in faa1 faa4) but indirect; FAO RCA kept as non-core. Cytosol RCA over-annotated (membrane-associated).
