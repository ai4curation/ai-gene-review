Gene Description

Aspartate kinase (aspartokinase, EC 2.7.2.4) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Hom3 phosphorylates L-aspartate with ATP to L-4-aspartyl phosphate, the first committed step of the aspartate pathway leading to homoserine and thence threonine, isoleucine and methionine. The enzyme is a homohexamer allosterically feedback-inhibited by threonine, which induces a compact inactive conformation; this inhibition is the main control point of carbon flow into the pathway, and the peptidyl-prolyl isomerase FKBP12 (Fpr1) binds Hom3 and contributes to feedback inhibition. Feedback-resistant HOM3 alleles cause threonine/homoserine overproduction. Hom3 is cytoplasmic.

  L-aspartate with ATP to L-4-aspartyl phosphate, the first committed step of the aspartate pathway leading
  to homoserine and thence threonine, isoleucine and methionine. The enzyme is a homohexamer allosterically
  feedback-inhibited by threonine, which induces a compact inactive conformation; this inhibition is the
  main control point of carbon flow into the pathway, and the peptidyl-prolyl isomerase FKBP12 (Fpr1)
  binds Hom3 and contributes to feedback inhibition. Feedback-resistant HOM3 alleles cause threonine/homoserine
  overproduction. Hom3 is cytoplasmic.
