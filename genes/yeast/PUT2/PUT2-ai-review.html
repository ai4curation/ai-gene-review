Gene Description

PUT2 (YHR037W) encodes delta-1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate (P5C) dehydrogenase, an NAD+-dependent aldehyde dehydrogenase family enzyme of the mitochondrial matrix (EC 1.2.1.88). It catalyses the second and final step of proline utilization, oxidizing L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde (in equilibrium with P5C, produced by Put1 proline dehydrogenase) to L-glutamate, so that proline and arginine/ornithine-derived P5C can serve as nitrogen sources. PUT2 is induced by proline through the Put3 activator; put2 mutants cannot use proline as sole nitrogen source.
