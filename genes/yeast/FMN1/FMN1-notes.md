# FMN1 (YDR236C) notes

UniProt Q03778; riboflavin kinase, EC 2.7.1.26 [UniProt:Q03778].

## Evidence journal
- Recombinant activity, monofunctional [PMID:10887197 "The purified form of the expressed recombinant protein efficiently catalyzed the formation of FMN from riboflavin and ATP."; "the yeast enzyme did not show accompanying FAD synthetase activity"].
- Deletion rescued by FMN [PMID:10887197 "the growth of the ydr236cDelta mutants could be rescued by the addition of FMN to the medium"].
- Location: microsomes (~90%) and mitochondrial inner membrane (~10%), C-terminus in matrix [PMID:10887197 "the mitochondrial form of Fmn1p is an integral protein of the inner membrane exposing its COOH-terminal domain to the matrix space"].
- PMID:12614612 (murine pre-RC two-hybrid) cited by SGD for riboflavin kinase IDA appears to be a wrong PMID.

## Decisions
- InterPro2GO riboflavin biosynthetic process REMOVE (kinase consumes riboflavin). Cytosol RCA over-annotated (membrane protein). PMID:12614612 row UNDECIDED.
