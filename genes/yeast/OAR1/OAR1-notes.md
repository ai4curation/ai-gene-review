# OAR1 (YKL055C, P35731) notes

- Mitochondrial NADPH-dependent 3-oxoacyl-ACP reductase (SDR family, EC 1.1.1.100) of mtFAS [UniProt:P35731 "Reaction=a (3R)-hydroxyacyl-[ACP] + NADP(+) = a 3-oxoacyl-[ACP] + NADPH"].
- Disruption gives respiratory deficiency [PMID:9388293 "We disrupted these two genes which in both cases led to a respiratory deficient phenotype"].
- NADPH-dependence and functional context [PMID:19571038 "although HsKAR is NADH dependent, whereas FabG and Oar1p are NADPH dependent"]; human HSD17B8/CBR4 restore lipoic acid of oar1 null [PMID:19571038 "they rescue the respiratory deficiency and restore the lipoic acid content of oar1Delta cells"].
- mtFAS product octanoyl-ACP -> lipoic acid [PMID:19571038 "Mitochondrial fatty acid synthesis (FAS) generates the octanoyl-group that is required for the synthesis of lipoic acid"].
- YeastPathways places OAR1 in seven cytosolic FAS pathways (myristate, palmitate, stearate, elongation...) - the cytosolic KR step is the FAS2 KR domain; cytosol rows wrong.
- IBA quinone binding (from CBR4 node) - no evidence for Oar1.
- Aerobic respiration IMP: respiratory defect is a downstream consequence of loss of lipoylation/mtFAS products.
