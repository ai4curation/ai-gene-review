# EKI1 (YDR147W, Q03764) notes

## Identity
- Ethanolamine kinase, EC 2.7.1.82; choline/ethanolamine kinase family (PTHR22603); soluble cytoplasmic protein [UniProt:Q03764].

## Evidence
- Activity: Sf-9 expression gives huge ethanolamine kinase overexpression [PMID:10329685 "The heterologous expression of the S. cerevisiae EKI1 gene in Sf-9 insect cells resulted in a 165,500-fold overexpression of ethanolamine kinase activity relative to control insect cells."]
- Secondary choline kinase activity; ethanolamine preferred [PMID:10329685 "The EKI1 gene product also exhibited choline kinase activity."] [PMID:10329685 "indicated that ethanolamine was the preferred substrate"].
- Eki1 + Cki1 = all EK/CK activity; Eki1 mainly PE via CDP-ethanolamine, Cki1 mainly PC [PMID:10329685 "Whereas the EKI1 gene product was primarily responsible for phosphatidylethanolamine synthesis via the CDP-ethanolamine pathway, the CKI1 gene product was primarily responsible for phosphatidylcholine synthesis via the CDP-choline pathway."]
- Localization: cytoplasm (GFP survey) [UniProt:Q03764; PMID:14562095].

## Curation decisions
- Ethanolamine kinase + PE biosynthesis = core; choline kinase / PC biosynthesis rows kept as non-core.
- Cytosol RCA accepted (soluble enzyme), unlike the membrane enzymes of the same pathway.
- GO:0016773 (InterPro) -> MODIFY to GO:0004305.
