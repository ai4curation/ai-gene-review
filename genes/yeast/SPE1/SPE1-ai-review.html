Gene Description

Ornithine decarboxylase (ODC, Spe1p, YKL184W), a cytoplasmic pyridoxal 5'-phosphate-dependent class-II (Orn/Lys/Arg) decarboxylase that converts L-ornithine to putrescine and CO2 (EC 4.1.1.17). This is the first and rate-limiting step of polyamine biosynthesis; putrescine is then aminopropylated to spermidine and spermine by Spe3 and Spe4 using decarboxylated S-adenosylmethionine from Spe2. spe1 deletion mutants cannot make putrescine and require spermidine or spermine for growth. Activity is modulated about 200-fold by polyamine availability at a post-translational level, through antizyme (Oaz1)-mediated inhibition of homodimer assembly and ubiquitin-independent proteasomal degradation. Depletion of endogenous polyamines in ageing cells, as in polyamine-synthesis mutants, leads to histone hyperacetylation, reactive oxygen species, early necrotic death and a shortened chronological lifespan, whereas spermidine supplementation induces autophagy and extends lifespan.

Cytosolic PLP-dependent ornithine decarboxylase (EC 4.1.1.17) converting L-ornithine to putrescine, the first and rate-limiting step of polyamine (putrescine/spermidine/spermine) biosynthesis; regulated post-translationally by polyamines via antizyme.

Notes

(SPE1-notes.md)

SPE1 (YKL184W, P08432) notes

Pathway context: ornithine decarboxylase (EC 4.1.1.17), putrescine biosynthesis / polyamine biosynthesis.

  • Purified from yeast, PLP and thiol requiring PMID:6795198.
  • First step of polyamine synthesis, ~200-fold polyamine regulation, post-translational [PMID:2681188 "ODC catalyzes the first step of polyamine biosynthesis, the conversion of ornithine to putrescine"; "ODC activity was modulated approximately 200-fold by varying the availability of polyamines"].
  • spe1 delta cannot make putrescine/spermidine/spermine PMID:7929015.
  • Antizyme Oaz1 inhibits dimer assembly and targets for ubiquitin-independent degradation [UniProt:P08432].
  • Decisions: all accepted; root 'catalytic activity' IEA kept non-core. Polyamine biosynthetic process RCA from superpathway is genuine (ODC is the committed step).
