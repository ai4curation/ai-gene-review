# SPE1 (YKL184W, P08432) notes

Pathway context: ornithine decarboxylase (EC 4.1.1.17), putrescine biosynthesis / polyamine biosynthesis.

- Purified from yeast, PLP and thiol requiring [PMID:6795198 "The ornithine decarboxylase is a single polypeptide (Mr = 68,000) and requires a thiol and pyridoxal phosphate for activity"].
- First step of polyamine synthesis, ~200-fold polyamine regulation, post-translational [PMID:2681188 "ODC catalyzes the first step of polyamine biosynthesis, the conversion of ornithine to putrescine"; "ODC activity was modulated approximately 200-fold by varying the availability of polyamines"].
- spe1 delta cannot make putrescine/spermidine/spermine [PMID:7929015 "are equally unable to synthesize putrescine, spermidine, and spermine and require spermidine or spermine for growth in amine-free media"].
- Antizyme Oaz1 inhibits dimer assembly and targets for ubiquitin-independent degradation [UniProt:P08432].
- Decisions: all accepted; root 'catalytic activity' IEA kept non-core. Polyamine biosynthetic process RCA from superpathway is genuine (ODC is the committed step).
