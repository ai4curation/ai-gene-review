# PubMed figure-caption evidence for PMID:27185881

Source: [Determinants of Sir2-Mediated, Silent Chromatin Cohesion](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27185881/), accessed 2026-09-20 through the web tool. The standard publication fetch returned an abstract-only cache; PubMed separately exposes figure captions.

Figure 1 caption excerpt:

> Full-length HST1 mediates cohesion at HMR in a SUM1-1 background.

The caption describes wild-type, sir2 deletion, sir2 deletion/SUM1-1, and sir2 deletion/SUM1-1/hst1 deletion strains. It also identifies a cohesin ChIP comparison at HMR in this background. Thus the Hst1 evidence is in the paper despite its Sir2-centered title and abstract. This supports the existing conditional, non-core HMR-silencing annotation in conjunction with PMID:11313477; it does not establish native telomeric recruitment of Hst1.
