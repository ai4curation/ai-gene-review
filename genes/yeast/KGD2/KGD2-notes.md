# KGD2 (YDR148C, P19262) notes

- E2 (KE2, dihydrolipoyl transsuccinylase); pet G104 mutants lack KGDH activity [PMID:2115121 "Yeast mutants assigned to the pet complementation group G104 were found to lack alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase activity"]; disruption abolishes 2-OG-dependent NAD+ reduction [PMID:2115121].
- 42% identity to E. coli SucB [PMID:2115121]. Reaction EC 2.3.1.61 with lipoyl cofactor [UniProt:P19262].
- Forms the core of KGDH [PMID:25165143 "confirming that Kgd2 forms the core of KGDH around which the other subunits assemble"; PMID:2072900].
- Nucleoid association: crosslinks to mtDNA, kgd2-null enhances abf2-null petites [PMID:10869431 "a kgd2Delta mutation increases the petite-inducing phenotype of an abf2Delta mutation"]; YMN-1 antigen = KE2 [PMID:11926067].
- UniProt PATHWAY line lists "L-lysine degradation via saccharopine pathway; glutaryl-CoA from L-lysine" (mammalian DLST context); S. cerevisiae does not degrade lysine via saccharopine as a catabolic route, so this is not carried into the review.
- Glucose-repressed, HAP2/3-activated [PMID:2115121].
