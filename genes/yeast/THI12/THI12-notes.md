# THI12 (YNL332W, P42883) review notes

## Identity
- HMP-P synthase THI12; NMT1/THI5 family; function, cofactor and subunit annotated in UniProt by similarity to THI5 (P43534) [UniProt:P42883].
- Member of the four-gene subtelomeric THI5 family [PMID:12777485 "The THI5 gene family of Saccharomyces cerevisiae comprises four highly conserved members named THI5 (YFL058w), THI11 (YJR156c), THI12 (YNL332w) and THI13 (YDL244w)."].
- Differs from THI5 at 1 of 340 positions (ungapped comparison of the UniProt sequences in this batch).

## Function
- Genetic redundancy for HMP formation [PMID:12777485 "Phenotypic analyses of these mutant strains have shown the four genes to be functionally redundant in terms of HMP formation for thiamin biosynthesis; each promotes synthesis of HMP from the pyridoxine (vitamin B(6)) biosynthetic pathway."].
- Activity inferred from THI5 biochemistry [PMID:23048037 "Mutational and yeast complementation studies identify the key residues for HMP-P biosynthesis as well as the use of pyridoxal 5'-phosphate as a substrate rather than as a cofactor."] and the Candida orthologue [PMID:22568620 "Our data suggest that His66 of the THI5 protein is the histidine source for pyrimidine formation and that the pyrimidine synthase is a single-turnover enzyme."].
- No direct biochemical or localisation data for THI12 itself.
