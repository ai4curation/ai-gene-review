# GAD1 (YMR250W, Q04792) notes

Pathway context: GABA shunt step 1, glutamate decarboxylase (EC 4.1.1.15), YeastPathways glutamate degradation I.

- Reaction L-glutamate + H+ = GABA + CO2; PLP cofactor by similarity; group II decarboxylase [UniProt:Q04792].
- Identified as yeast GAD homologue required for oxidative stress tolerance [PMID:11031268 "we identify a Saccharomyces cerevisiae homologue of the GABA-producing enzyme glutamate decarboxylase (GAD) that is required for normal oxidative stress tolerance"].
- High-copy GAD1 raises H2O2/diamide resistance only with intact downstream pathway [PMID:11031268 "A high copy number plasmid bearing the glutamate decarboxylase gene (GAD1) increases resistance to two different oxidants, H(2)O(2) and diamide, in cells that contain an intact glutamate catabolic pathway"]; uga1/uga2 epistatic [PMID:11031268 "Loss of either gene encoding the downstream steps in the conversion of glutamate to succinate reduced oxidative stress tolerance in normal cells and was epistatic to high copy number GAD1"].
- Calmodulin binding reported [PMID:11031268 "The yeast GAD also bound to calmodulin as did the plant enzyme"]; not in GOA, not proposed as NEW (single abstract-level report; regulatory relevance untested) - recorded as suggested question.
- Cytoplasmic in HTP screens (PMID:11914276, PMID:14562095).
- Decisions: all MF/BP/CC rows accepted; generic carbon-carbon lyase and glutamate metabolic process kept as non-core; oxidative stress response non-core.
