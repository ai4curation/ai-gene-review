# CDC21 (TMP1; YOR074C, P06785) notes

- Thymidylate synthase, EC 2.1.1.45: dUMP + CH2-THF -> dTMP + DHF [UniProt:P06785].
- tmp1 and cdc21 mutants lack the activity: "Extracts of a strain bearing the temperature-sensitive cell division cycle mutation cdc21 lacked detectable thymidylate synthetase activity under all conditions tested." [PMID:334734]
- cdc21 and tmp1 are the same gene and the mutant is a dTMP auxotroph [PMID:794696].
- The cloned TMP1 restores activity and complements E. coli thyA [PMID:6287238].
- Location: "immunofluorescence analysis suggests that thymidylate synthase is situated at the nuclear periphery" [PMID:8132557].
- The gene is required for nuclear and mitochondrial DNA synthesis [UniProt:P06785].

## Curation decisions
- The mitochondrion IBA (from human TYMS and plant DHFR-TS) and the cytosol IBA (from E. coli) are marked over-annotated. Yeast data place the protein at the nuclear periphery.
- The cytosol RCA rows are marked over-annotated for the same reason.
- GO:0006207 (de novo pyrimidine nucleobase biosynthesis) is changed to GO:0006231 (dTMP biosynthesis).
- The folate cycle and folic acid metabolic rows are marked over-annotated.
- The chorismate row is removed.
