# PDB1 (YBR221C, P32473) notes

Evidence journal for the review; sources are UniProt and cached publications.

- E1 beta of PDH: [PMID:8433986 "The gene encoding the pyruvate dehydrogenase (PDH) beta subunit (E1 beta) of the PDH complex from Saccharomyces cerevisiae has been cloned, sequenced, disrupted, and expressed."]
- Mitochondrial presequence: [PMID:8433986 "encoding a putative presequence of 33 amino acid residues and a mature protein of 333 residues"].
- Rescue: [PMID:8433986 "The null mutant transformed with PDH beta 1 on a unit-copy plasmid produced mature E1 beta and a functional PDH complex."]
- Part of the E1-E2 subcomplex retained in pdx1 mutants [PMID:2007123].
- mtDNA nucleoid association (PMID:15692048, IDA). Kept as non-core.

Curation decisions
- E1 activity (GO:0004739) accepted for E1 beta. The active sites are at the alpha/beta interface, so 'enables' for both subunits follows GO practice for the heterotetramer.
- RCA 'is_active_in cytosol' removed.
