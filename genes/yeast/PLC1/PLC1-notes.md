# PLC1 (YPL268W, P32383) notes

- PI-PLC delta homologue; Ca2+-dependent PIP2 hydrolysis [PMID:8395015 "Plc1p activity was strictly Ca2+ dependent"]; [PMID:8395015 "at a low Ca2+ concentration (0.5 microM), it hydrolyzed PIP2 exclusively"].
- ts alleles lose activity [PMID:7753023 "The PLC activity of all products of mutant plc1 genes was dramatically lower than that of the wild-type product"].
- First step of IP6 pathway [PMID:10390371 "a signaling pathway from phosphatidylinositol 4, 5-bisphosphate to inositol hexakisphosphate (IP6)"].
- Glucose-induced PI turnover [PMID:9784626 "glucose triggers through activation of the PLC1 gene product a signaling pathway initiated by phosphatidylinositol turnover"].
- Vacuole fusion via DAG [PMID:15485855 "replenished by the hydrolysis of phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate to DAG by PLC"].
- Kinetochore/centromere [PMID:10779349 "Plc1p resides at centromeric loci in vivo"]; RSC recruitment via InsPs [PMID:19205744 "Plc1p and InsPs are required for recruitment of the RSC complex to kinetochores"].
- Gpr1/Gpa2 filamentation [PMID:10514491 "Diploid cells lacking Gpr1p, Plc1p, or Gpa2p fail to form pseudohyphae upon nitrogen depletion"].
- Domains per UniProt: EF-hand, PI-PLC X-box, Y-box, C2 (no PH domain annotated).
