CC   -!- FUNCTION: Central component of the spindle assembly checkpoint which is
CC       a feedback control that prevents cells with incompletely assembled
CC       spindles from leaving mitosis. Thought to be recruited to unattached
CC       kinetochores by MAD1. During checkpoint activity, MAD2 is relayed from
CC       the MAD1-MAD2 complex to the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC). MCC and
CC       presumably the MAD2-CDC20 subcomplex inhibit the ubiquitin ligase
CC       activity of the anaphase promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) by
CC       preventing its activation by CDC20. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15879521,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8232541}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC) which
CC       consists of MAD2, MAD3, BUB3 and CDC20, and of the MAD2-CDC20
CC       subcomplex, both of which appear to be assembled during mitoisis
CC       independently of the kinetochore. In mitose-arrested cells, MAD2-CDC20
CC       occurs in a larger amount than MCC. Interacts with CDC20 and BUB3.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10436016, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11726501,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15879521, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9461437}.
