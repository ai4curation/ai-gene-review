# ERG7 notes (P38604, YHR072W)

- Function: lanosterol synthase / 2,3-oxidosqualene cyclase, EC 5.4.99.7 [UniProt:P38604]; [PMID:12842197 "The oxidosqualene cyclase (Erg7p) encoded by the ERG7 gene converts oxidosqualene to lanosterol, the first cyclic component of sterol biosynthesis."].
- Enzymology: microsomal, lipophilic, purified 140x [PMID:1731628]; Tyr707 mutants give bicyclic products [PMID:18842050].
- Erg27 dependence: [PMID:12842197 "We demonstrate that Erg27p is required for oxidosqualene cyclase (Erg7p) activity in LPs, and that Erg27p co-immunoprecipitates with Erg7p in LPs but not in microsomal fractions."]; UniProt ACTIVITY REGULATION requires ERG27.
- Location: almost exclusively lipid droplets [PMID:11706015 "Occurrence of oxidosqualene cyclase in other organelles including the endoplasmic reticulum is negligible."]; minor ER pool (Huh GFP; Mo 2003).
- Genetics: erg7 ergosterol auxotrophs lacking cyclase [PMID:200835].
- No GO term for lanosterol biosynthetic process; triterpenoid biosynthetic process kept non-core.
