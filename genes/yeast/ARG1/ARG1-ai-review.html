Gene Description

Argininosuccinate synthase (citrulline--aspartate ligase, EC 6.3.4.5) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. This cytosolic homotetrameric enzyme condenses L-citrulline with L-aspartate in an ATP-dependent reaction (ATP -> AMP + PPi) to form argininosuccinate, the penultimate step of L-arginine biosynthesis, which in yeast runs in the cytosol from ornithine exported by mitochondria. Kinetic studies show positive cooperativity toward MgATP modulated by argininosuccinate and arginine. The enzyme also has a catabolic role, as the first step of citrulline utilisation as a nitrogen source (citrulline is converted to arginine and degraded via arginase). ARG1 expression is repressed by arginine (ArgR/Mcm1 complex) and induced by general amino acid control.
