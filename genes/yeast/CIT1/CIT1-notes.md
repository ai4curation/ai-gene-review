# CIT1 (YNR001C, P00890) notes

- Acetyl-CoA-specific mitochondrial citrate synthase [PMID:17570335 "that of the CIT1 gene as a specific citrate synthase"; "Recombinant Cit1p had catalytic activity only with acetyl-CoA"].
- Deletion: impaired respiration of citrate/isocitrate, no growth on acetate [PMID:2661555 "an inability to grow on acetate"].
- Ser462 phosphorylation blocks dimerisation/activity; Ptc7p reverses [PMID:28076776 "phosphorylation of S462 can eliminate Cit1p enzymatic activity likely by disrupting its proper dimerization"].
- Found in a mitochondrial dehydrogenase supercomplex [PMID:11502169 "citrate synthase Cit1p"].
- Matrix [UniProt:P00890 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion matrix"].
