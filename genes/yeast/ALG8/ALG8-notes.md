# ALG8 (YOR067C, UniProt P40351) notes

## Activity / role
- Second α1,3-glucose [PMID:3536907 "We present evidence showing that the alg8-1 mutation blocks addition of the second alpha 1,3-linked glucose."]; [PMID:3536907 "Cells carrying the alg8-1 mutation accumulate Glc1Man9GlcNAc2-lipid both in vivo and in vitro."]
- Cloning and synthetic phenotype with wbp1 [PMID:8016100 "This new synthetic phenotype made it possible to isolate the ALG8 locus, encoding a potential glucosyltransferase of the endoplasmic reticulum."]
- No purified-enzyme assay has been reported (all evidence is genetic or from microsomes, plus the Alg6 paralogue structure).

## Curation observations
- The YeastPathways cytosol row is wrong for this lumenal enzyme, so it is REMOVED. ALG8 belongs to the lumenal module.
