CC   -!- FUNCTION: Thiolesterase that catalyzes the hydrolysis of S-D-
CC       lactoylglutathione to form glutathione and D-lactic acid. Involved in
CC       the metabolism of methylglyoxal, a toxic compound for yeast
CC       proliferation, by converting methylglyoxal to lactate via S-D-
CC       lactoylglutathione by sequential enzyme reactions catalyzed by
CC       glyoxalase I and glyoxalase II. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10600466,
CC       ECO:0000269|Ref.5, ECO:0000269|Ref.6}.
