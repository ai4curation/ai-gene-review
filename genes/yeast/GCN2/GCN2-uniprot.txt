CC   -!- FUNCTION: Metabolic-stress sensing protein kinase that phosphorylates
CC       the alpha subunit of eukaryotic translation initiation factor 2 (eIF-2-
CC       alpha/SUI2) on 'Ser-52' in response to low amino acid, carbon, or
CC       purine availability (PubMed:10733573, PubMed:10983975, PubMed:17202131,
CC       PubMed:1739968, PubMed:33338396, PubMed:7623840, PubMed:8336737,
CC       PubMed:8798780, PubMed:9528799). Required for adapatation to nutrient
CC       starvation by acting as a key component of the integrated stress
CC       response (ISR), by which cells alter their translational and
CC       transcriptional output in response to starvation (PubMed:10733573,
CC       PubMed:10983975, PubMed:17202131, PubMed:1739968, PubMed:33338396,
CC       PubMed:7623840, PubMed:8336737, PubMed:8798780, PubMed:9528799).
CC       Converts phosphorylated eIF-2-alpha/SUI2 either to a competitive
CC       inhibitor of translation initiation factor eIF-2B, leading to a global
CC       protein synthesis repression, and thus to a reduced overall utilization
CC       of amino acids, or to a translational initiation activation of specific
CC       mRNAs, such as the transcriptional activator GCN4, and hence allowing
CC       GCN4-mediated reprogramming of transcription to alleviate nutrient
CC       depletion (PubMed:10733573, PubMed:10801780, PubMed:11350982,
CC       PubMed:1739968, PubMed:2188100, PubMed:24333428, PubMed:2660141,
CC       PubMed:33338396, PubMed:7621831, PubMed:7623840, PubMed:8798780). Binds
CC       uncharged tRNAs (PubMed:10983975, PubMed:7623840). Binds to
CC       aminoacylated tRNA(Phe) less tightly than to deacylated tRNA(Phe)
CC       (PubMed:10983975). Binds to double-stranded RNA (PubMed:9430731).
