# CHS3 (YBR023C, P29465; CAL1/CSD2/DIT101/KIT2) notes

- Catalytic subunit of CSIII, class IV chitin synthase, EC 2.4.1.16 [PMID:9760183 "chitin synthase III (CSIII) is responsible for the synthesis of most of the chitin found in the cell"]; [PMID:17519287 "mainly Chs3p, which is the catalytic subunit of CSIII"].
- Chitin ring at incipient bud site and bud neck [PMID:8909536 "Chitin synthase III (Chs3p) synthesizes a ring of chitin at the onset of bud emergence"]; [PMID:9008706 "a new Chs3p ring that appears at the incipient bud site"].
- Trafficking: ER exit via Chs7 and Pfa4 [PMID:16818716; PMID:29405545 "In the absence of Chs7, Chs3 forms high molecular weight aggregates and is retained in the endoplasmic reticulum (ER)."]; chitosome reservoir mobilized by Rho1/Pkc1 under stress [PMID:12928491].
- Homo-oligomerization in vivo [PMID:28346351 "We further provide for the first time evidence for Chs3 di- or oligomerization in vivo"].
- Spore wall: dit101 (=chs3) makes no chitosan in spores [PMID:1293886 "Chemical measurements showed that this mutant does not synthesize chitosan during sporulation."]; Chs3 reaches prospore membrane in an Sps1-dependent manner [PMID:15755916].
