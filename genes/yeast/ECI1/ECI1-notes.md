# ECI1 (YLR284C; UniProt Q05871) notes

- Peroxisomal Delta3-cis-Delta2-trans-enoyl-CoA isomerase, EC 5.3.3.8 [PMID:9837886 "a bacterially expressed form of this protein catalyzed the isomerization of 3-cis-octenoyl-CoA to 2-trans-octenoyl-CoA with a specific activity of 16 units/mg"].
- Purified hexamer of 32 kDa subunits; no conserved catalytic glutamate [PMID:9813046 "Eci1p is the only known member of the hydratase/isomerase protein family with isomerase and/or 2-enoyl-CoA hydratase 1 activities that does not contain a conserved glutamate at its active site"].
- Essential for growth on unsaturated but not saturated fatty acids [PMID:9813046 "A mutant devoid of Eci1p was unable to grow on media containing unsaturated fatty acids such as oleic acid"]; null has no 3,2-isomerase activity [PMID:9837886 "Cells lacking YLR284C had no detectable Delta3,Delta2-enoyl-CoA isomerase activity"].
- Peroxisomal (GFP; PEX8-dependent) [PMID:9813046 "Eci1p was shown to be located in peroxisomes of wild-type yeast cells"]; import partly Dci1p-dependent [UniProt:Q05871].
- Oleate-induced via ORE bound by Pip2p/Oaf1p [PMID:9813046].
