# LYS2 (YBR115C, P07702) notes

Pathway context: LYSINE-AMINOAD-PWY-2, aminoadipate reductase (EC 1.2.1.31 / 1.2.1.95).

- Mechanism: adenylation, PCP thioester, NADPH reductive release; requires Lys5 phosphopantetheinylation [PMID:10320345 "These results identify the Lys5/Lys2 pair as a two-component system in which Lys5 covalently primes Lys2, allowing alpha-aminoadipate reductase activity by holo-Lys2 with catalytic cycles of autoaminoacylation and reductive cleavage."].
- Genetics: cloned by complementation [PMID:3023949 "The structural gene for alpha-aminoadipate reductase (LYS2) was isolated from a Saccharomyces cerevisiae genomic DNA library by complementation of a lys2 mutant."]; three-step reaction in extracts [PMID:4401608 "The reduction of alpha-aminoadipate to alpha-aminoadipic delta-semialdehyde by a cell-free extract of Saccharomyces is shown to be a three-step process."].
- Location: cytosolic [PMID:3928261 "Five of the lysine enzymes are localized in mitochondria and three in cytosol."].
- Ontology note: GO:0004043 is written in oxidative direction (EC 1.2.1.31); no GO term for EC 1.2.1.95 (ATP-dependent reductase, Rhea:46936). Raised as suggested question. All annotations accepted; phosphopantetheine binding non-core.
