# YAT2 (YER024W; UniProt P40017) notes

- Third carnitine acetyltransferase, identified by complementation of a carnitine-dependent strain [PMID:11329169 "YER024w (YAT2), which encodes a third, previously unidentified carnitine acetyltransferase"].
- Contributes significantly to total CAT activity on ethanol [PMID:11329169 "Yat2p contributes significantly to total CAT activity when cells are grown on ethanol"].
- Cytosolic (GFP HTP PMID:14562095; IDA PMID:18427809) [UniProt:P40017 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm"]. Thus the cytosolic CAT of the yeast carnitine shuttle, converting cytosolic acetyl-CoA (e.g. from acetate/ethanol) into acetylcarnitine for mitochondrial import.
- No cross-complementation between CATs [PMID:18427809 "Overexpression of single CAT genes does not lead to cross-complementation"].
- Interacts with YAT1 in Y2H (PMID:18719252) and AP-MS (PMID:37968396).
- ARBA "carnitine O-acyltransferase activity" is a correct but less specific parent of carnitine O-acetyltransferase activity.
