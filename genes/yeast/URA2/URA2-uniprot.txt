CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multifunctional protein that encodes the first 2 enzymatic
CC       activities of the de novo pyrimidine pathway: carbamoylphosphate
CC       synthetase (CPSase; EC 6.3.5.5) and aspartate transcarbamylase (ATCase;
CC       EC 2.1.3.2). The CPSase-function is accomplished in 2 steps, by a
CC       glutamine-dependent amidotransferase activity (GATase) that binds and
CC       cleaves glutamine to produce ammonia, followed by an ammonium-dependent
CC       carbamoyl phosphate synthetase, which reacts with the ammonia,
CC       hydrogencarbonate and ATP to form carbamoyl phosphate. The endogenously
CC       produced carbamoyl phosphate is sequestered and channeled to the ATCase
CC       active site. ATCase then catalyzes the formation of carbamoyl-L-
CC       aspartate from L-aspartate and carbamoyl phosphate.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10446140, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15128434,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:3281587, ECO:0000269|PubMed:5856369,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8449292}.
