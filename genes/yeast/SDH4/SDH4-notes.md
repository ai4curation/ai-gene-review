Evidence journal for the review; sources are UniProt and cached publications.

- Small cytochrome b (CybS) membrane-anchor subunit; three predicted TM helices [PMID:8120006 "Hydrophobicity analysis predicts that Sdh4p forms three transmembrane alpha-helices."].
- Role: [PMID:8120006 "These findings indicate that Sdh4p contributes both to the membrane attachment of the catalytic flavoprotein and iron-sulfur subunits and to electron transfer to ubiquinone."]
- Heme ligand Cys-78: [PMID:18028869 "we constructed an S. cerevisiae strain expressing a mutant enzyme lacking the two heme axial ligands, Sdh3p His-106 and Sdh4p Cys-78"].
- Cloning by N-terminal sequencing of BN-PAGE-purified SDH: [PMID:8120006 "We purified succinate dehydrogenase by blue native gel electrophoresis"].
- Deletion ablates the holocomplex [PMID:24954416 "We also observed that deletion of SDH2 or SDH4, which ablates the SDH holocomplex"].
