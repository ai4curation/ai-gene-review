# GLC3 (YEL011W, P32775) notes

Glycogen branching enzyme (EC 2.4.1.18), GH13 GlgB subfamily with CBM48 [UniProt:P32775]. Module: glycogen_metabolism_fungal (biosynthesis).

- "glycogen branching activity was undetectable in extracts from a glc3-1p strain but was elevated in strains which expressed GLC3 from a high-copy-number plasmid" [PMID:1729600]
- Hypomorph gives starch-like glycogen: "the absorption spectrum resembles that of starch rather than that of glycogen" [PMID:1729600]
- Null: "Disruption of GLC3 yielded the glycogen-deficient phenotype" [PMID:1729600]
- Expression rises ~20-fold approaching stationary phase [PMID:1729600]
- Localisation: co-localises with Gsy2 on glycogen structures: "Gsy2 co-localized with other glycogen metabolism-related proteins such as Glc3 and Gph1" [PMID:38832010]
