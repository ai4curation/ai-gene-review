# ERG2 (YMR202W) notes

UniProt P32352; C-8 sterol isomerase (fecosterol -> episterol); ERG2/sigma-1 receptor family (PTHR10868) [UniProt:P32352].

## Evidence journal
- Activity basis: Baloch et al. 1984 morpholine inhibition (DOI:10.1016/S0031-9422(00)80523-3; not cached) [UniProt:P32352].
- erg2 lacks 8,7-isomerase; maize isomerase complements [PMID:18459942 "subcloned into the yeast erg2 mutant lacking 8,7SI activity. Zm8,7SI restored endogenous ergosterol synthesis."].
- ER membrane by GFP [PMID:15716577; UniProt:P32352].
- Erg2-Erg24 interaction (both morpholine-sensitive) [PMID:16300994 "Also Erg24p and Erg2p, two enzymes that are sensitive to morpholine antifungals, appear to interact with one another"].

## Observations
- Fungal Erg2 is homologous to mammalian SIGMAR1, not to EBP (the animal 8,7-isomerase): convergent isomerases.
