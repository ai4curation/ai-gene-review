# PGM1 (YKL127W, P33401) notes

## Activity
- Minor phosphoglucomutase isozyme [UniProt:P33401 "Minor phosphoglucomutase isozyme that catalyzes the"]; ~10-20% of cellular PGM activity [UniProt:P33401 "Constitutes about 10-20% of the phosphoglucomutase activity"].
- Purified Pgm1 active on Glc-1-P; only residual phosphoribomutase activity [PMID:23103740 "All tested enzymes were active on both substrates with Pgm1 exhibiting only residual activity on ribose-1-phosphate."].
- PGM1/PGM2 paralogs; pgm1 pgm2 double deletion cannot grow on galactose and lacks detectable PGM activity [PMID:8119301 "Cells deleted for both, PGM1 and PGM2, could not grow on galactose."; "No residual phosphoglucomutase activity could be measured in crude extracts or in permeabilized cells of pgm1/2 double mutants."]. Glycogen/trehalose still made at reduced level [PMID:8119301 "the mutant cells were still able to accumulate trehalose and glycogen, although at a reduced level"].
- pgm1/2 deletion in UGP1-conditional strain further lowers UDP-Glc [PMID:9252577 "A further reduction of flux towards UDP-Glc was achieved by deletion of the two phosphoglucomutase genes in the ugp1 conditional strain."].
- Cytoplasm [UniProt:P33401 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm"].

## Assessment
- Core: phosphoglucomutase (Glc-1-P <-> Glc-6-P), linking Leloir pathway output to glycolysis and, in reverse, supplying Glc-1-P for UDP-glucose (glycogen, trehalose, cell wall glucans, Dol-P-Glc). Minor isozyme relative to Pgm2; Pgm3 is the phosphoribomutase.
- Glycogen/trehalose/glycogen catabolism/UDP-glucose BP annotations derive from upstream/downstream precursor supply; kept as non-core.
- YeastPathways placements (galactose degradation, glycogen biosynthesis/catabolism, Dol-P-Glc biosynthesis) all reflect the same reversible G1P/G6P reaction; correct chemistry, superpathway-level BPs.
