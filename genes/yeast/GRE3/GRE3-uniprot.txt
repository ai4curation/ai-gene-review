CC   -!- FUNCTION: Aldose reductase with a broad substrate specificity
CC       (Probable) (PubMed:7747971). Reduces the cytotoxic compound
CC       methylglyoxal (MG) to acetol and (R)-lactaldehyde under stress
CC       conditions. MG is synthesized via a bypath of glycolysis from
CC       dihydroxyacetone phosphate and is believed to play a role in cell cycle
CC       regulation and stress adaptation (PubMed:11525399). In pentose-
CC       fermenting yeasts, aldose reductase catalyzes the reduction of xylose
CC       into xylitol, with a preference for NADPH as cosubstrate
CC       (PubMed:11481678, PubMed:11722921). The purified enzyme catalyzes this
CC       reaction, but the inability of S.cerevisiae to grow on xylose as sole
CC       carbon source indicates that the physiological function is more likely
CC       methylglyoxal reduction (Probable) (PubMed:11722921).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11481678, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11525399,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11722921, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7747971,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:9730277}.
