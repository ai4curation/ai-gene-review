# CEM1 (YER061C, P39525) notes

- Mitochondrial beta-ketoacyl-ACP synthase (KAS, EC 2.3.1.41) of the type II mitochondrial FAS (mtFAS) [UniProt:P39525 "Reaction=a fatty acyl-[ACP] + malonyl-[ACP] + H(+) = a 3-oxoacyl-[ACP]"]; mitochondrial location by HTP [UniProt:P39525].
- cem1 null is respiratory deficient with low lipoic acid; rescued by human OXSM [PMID:15668256 "is able to restore growth to the respiratory-deficient yeast mutant cem1 that lacks the endogenous mitochondrial beta-ketoacyl synthase and exhibits lowered lipoic acid levels"].
- mtFAS context [PMID:9388293 "as is the case for the genes encoding a mitochondrial acyl carrier protein and a beta-ketoacyl-ACP synthase"]; human ortholog elongates C2-C14 acyl-ACP [PMID:15668256 "The enzyme can elongate acyl-chains containing 2-14 carbon atoms"].
- YeastPathways assigns Cem1 to cytosolic FAS pathways (initial steps, stearate III fungi, elongation, superpathways) - these reactions in the cytosol are carried out by the FAS2 KS domain; cytosol RCA rows are wrong. The KAS activity itself is right.
- Elongation/stearate rows: product of mtFAS is mainly octanoyl-ACP (lipoate precursor), so "fatty acid elongation, saturated fatty acid" (from stearate superpathway) is an over-annotation.
