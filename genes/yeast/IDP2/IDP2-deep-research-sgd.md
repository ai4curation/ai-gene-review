# SGD gene description for IDP2 (yeast)

Cytosolic NADP-specific isocitrate dehydrogenase; catalyzes oxidation of isocitrate to alpha-ketoglutarate; levels are elevated during growth on non-fermentable carbon sources and reduced during growth on glucose; IDP2 has a paralog, IDP3, that arose from the whole genome duplication; mutation in human homolog IDH1 is associated with low-grade gliomas and secondary glioblastomas
