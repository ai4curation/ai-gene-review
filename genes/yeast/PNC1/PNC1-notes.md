# PNC1 Re-Review Notes

## 2026-09 IBA/GOA Re-Review

- PNC1 has no current GOA IBA rows, so there is no PAINT ancestral node to adjudicate for this gene.
- The GO_REF:0000108 GO:0000781 `chromosome, telomeric region` row is an inter-ontology inference from the accepted GO:0031509 `subtelomeric heterochromatin formation` phenotype. Pnc1 supports telomeric silencing by clearing nicotinamide and activating Sir2-family deacetylases, but direct localization places Pnc1 in the nucleus, cytoplasm, and peroxisome rather than telomeric chromatin. Marked this row `REMOVE`.
- PubMed searches for newer papers recovered several 2021-2024 PNC1/Pnc1 papers. Pinkerton et al. 2021 (PMID:33749726) reports catalytic-activity-independent moonlighting chaperone activity for NAD+ salvage enzymes including Pnc1, but the cached record is abstract-only and the work rests on an overexpression screen in heterologous yeast proteotoxicity models, so the review proposes `GO:0044183 protein folding chaperone` only as a non-core stress-associated molecular function. Hong and Huh 2021 (PMID:33837734) and Hong et al. 2024 (PMID:38588806) extend the upstream stress pathways that feed the Msn2-Pnc1-Sir2 rDNA-stability axis through Smi1/cell-wall and Arv1/UPR/Hog1 contexts. Groth et al. 2023 (PMID:37175754) places PNC1 expression under Pho2-coupled NAD/phosphate cross-regulation. None of these regulatory papers overturn the standing interpretation that the rDNA and telomeric biological-process annotations are real but downstream of Pnc1's nicotinamide-clearing activity.
