# CAB5 (YDR196C, Q03941) notes

- CoaE-family dephospho-CoA kinase [UniProt:Q03941 "Belongs to the CoaE family."]; complemented by E. coli coaE [PMID:19266201 "Null mutants could be complemented by their bacterial counterparts coaBC, coaD and coaE, respectively."]
- Mitochondrial outer membrane, extrinsic [PMID:28357284 "Taken together these results suggest that Cab5 is an extrinsic outer membrane protein"]; [PMID:28357284 "The great majority of Cab5-HA co-fractionated with the mitochondrial membrane protein porin"]
- CoA-SPC member via Cab3 N-terminus [PMID:23789928 "Mapping studies revealed that the nonconserved N-terminus of Cab3 is required for dimerization and for binding of Cab2 and Cab5."]
- HTP: ER, nuclear envelope, nucleus (PMID:14690591), lipid droplet (PMID:24868093) - kept as non-core.

Observation: YeastPathways places the step in cytosol; the enzyme sits on the cytosolic face of the mitochondrial outer membrane - compatible. Tension between a soluble Cab3-scaffolded CoA-SPC and OMM-bound Cab5 is an open question.
