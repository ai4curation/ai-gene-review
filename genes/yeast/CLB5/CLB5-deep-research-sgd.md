# SGD gene description for CLB5 (yeast)

B-type cyclin involved in DNA replication during S phase; activates Cdc28p to promote initiation of DNA synthesis; functions in formation of mitotic spindles along with Clb3p and Clb4p; required with CLB6 for premeiotic DNA replication and activation of the meiotic DNA replication checkpoint; most abundant during late G1 phase; CLB5 has a paralog, CLB6, that arose from the whole genome duplication
