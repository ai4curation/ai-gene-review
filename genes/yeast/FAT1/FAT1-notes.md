# FAT1 (YBR041W; UniProt P38225) notes

- FATP/SLC27 family; very long-chain acyl-CoA synthetase [PMID:9988704 "Fat1p accounts for approximately 90% of the C24-specific acyl-CoA synthetase activity in glucose-grown cells"]; fat1 accumulates C22-C26 [PMID:9660783 "FAT1 deletion strains grown on either dextrose or oleic acid medium accumulated very long-chain fatty acids"].
- No effect on long-chain ACS activity [PMID:11330068 "had no effect on long-chain acyl-CoA synthetase activity"], though overexpression adds oleoyl-CoA synthetase activity [PMID:12601005].
- Long-chain fatty acid import, separable from VLCS activity [PMID:15699031 "these activities intrinsic to Fat1p are separable functions"]; complex with Faa1p/Faa4p [PMID:12601005].
- Plasma membrane N(in)-C(in) topology, catalytic motifs cytoplasmic [PMID:17679730].
- Peroxisomal VLCS activity [PMID:11330068 "VLCS activity was peroxisomal and not mitochondrial"]; inner peroxisomal membrane with Pxa1/Pxa2 [PMID:22493507]; ER/internal membranes [PMID:9988704]; lipid droplets [PMID:10515935; PMID:24868093].
- fat1 causes membrane saturation, Ole1 dysfunction, ER protein QC defect and UPR [PMID:31746669 "We find that this mutant shows significant defects in ER protein quality control with compensatory induction of the UPR"] - indirect, over-annotation for "protein quality control".

## Pathway-context observations
- YeastPathways assigns FAT1 to the EC 6.2.1.3 (long-chain) activation step; in peroxisomal beta-oxidation its role is VLCFA re-esterification (VLCS, GO:0031957). RCA GO:0004467 -> MODIFY GO:0031957. Cytosol RCA removed (integral membrane protein).
