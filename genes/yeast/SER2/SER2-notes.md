# SER2 (YGR208W, P42941) notes

- Phosphoserine phosphatase (EC 3.1.3.3), HAD superfamily SerB family, Mg2+-dependent, step 3/3 of phosphorylated serine pathway [UniProt:P42941 "Belongs to the HAD-like hydrolase superfamily. SerB family."].
- Genetic identification: [PMID:1326413 "The ser1 mutation corresponded to phosphoserine transaminase and ser2 to that of phosphoserine phosphatase"].
- No purified-enzyme kinetics in the cache; MF rests on mutant enzyme activity (1326413), IBA (PTN000000002, includes E. coli SerB and human PSPH) and InterPro IPR004469.
- GFP survey: cytoplasm and nucleus [PMID:14562095]; nuclear pool has no known function.
- Expression co-regulated with SER1/SER33 by nitrogen source and glucose [PMID:12525494 "both expression and activity of all SER-encoded proteins are low, including the remaining enzymes of the phosphoglycerate pathway, Ser1p and Ser2p"].
