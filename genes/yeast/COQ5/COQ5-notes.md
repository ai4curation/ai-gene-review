# COQ5 notes (P49017, YML110C)

- MenG/UbiE-family C-methyltransferase; only C-methylation of the Q ring, DDMQ6H2 -> DMQ6H2 (EC 2.1.1.201) [PMID:9083049 "Coq5p is required for the C-methyltransferase step"; PMID:25152161 "catalyzes the methylation of demethyl-demethoxy-Q6H2 (DDMQ6H2) to form demethoxy-Q6H2 (DMQ6H2)"].
- Mitochondrial, peripheral on the matrix side of the inner membrane [PMID:14701817 "Coq5p is peripherally associated with the inner mitochondrial membrane on the matrix side"].
- Structural role: Coq5 polypeptide needed to stabilise Coq3 and Coq4; catalytic point mutants (coq5-2, coq5-5) keep them [PMID:14701817 "it is involved in stabilizing the Coq3 and Coq4 polypeptides required for Q biosynthesis"].
- Human COQ5 / E. coli UbiE rescue only when the CoQ synthome is intact [PMID:25152161].
- coq5 null respiratory deficiency is a consequence of Q loss (aerobic respiration -> non-core) [PMID:9083048].
