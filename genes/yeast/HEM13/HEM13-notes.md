# HEM13 (YDR044W) notes

UniProt P11353, oxygen-dependent coproporphyrinogen-III oxidase (EC 1.3.3.3) [UniProt:P11353].

- Cytosolic in yeast (contrast: metazoan CPOX in mitochondrial IMS) [PMID:3516695 "Coproporphyrinogen oxidase has been located in the cytosol of yeast cells."].
- Homodimer, O2-only [PMID:3516695 "Thus the enzyme appears to be a dimer with identical subunits."; "The enzyme was active only when molecular oxygen was used as electron acceptor; no anaerobic activity could be detected."].
- Gene cloned by complementation; hypoxic induction needed for anaerobic heme formation [PMID:2838478 "The loss of induction of coproporphyrinogen oxidase activity by anaerobiosis caused a considerable decrease of heme formation in anaerobic cells."].
- All annotations consistent; generic porphyrin IEA -> MODIFY to heme biosynthesis.
