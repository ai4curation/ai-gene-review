Gene Description

Eaf3 is a nuclear MRG-family chromodomain protein that binds methylated histone H3K36 and acts in the NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complex, the Rpd3S histone deacetylase complex, and the Eaf5/7/3/TINTIN module. Through these complexes it couples histone H3K36 methylation and RNA polymerase II passage to coding-region histone acetylation control, transcription elongation-associated nucleosome dynamics, and repression of cryptic intragenic and antisense transcription.
