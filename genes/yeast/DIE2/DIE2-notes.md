# DIE2 / ALG10 (YGR227W, UniProt P50076) notes

## Activity / role
- Terminal α1,2-glucose [PMID:9597543 "show in a novel in vitro assay that Alg10p is a dolichyl-phosphoglucose-dependent glucosyltransferase which adds the terminal alpha-1,2 glucose to the lipid-linked Glc2Man9GlcNAc2 oligosaccharide"]
- Needed for OST recognition [PMID:9597543 "Hypoglycosylation of secreted proteins in alg10 deletion strains demonstrates that the terminal alpha-1,2-linked glucose residue is a key element in substrate recognition by the oligosaccharyltransferase."]
