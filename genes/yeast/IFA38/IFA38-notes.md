## Function
- Very-long-chain 3-oxoacyl-CoA reductase (KAR), SDR family, EC 1.1.1.330 [UniProt:P38286].
- [PMID:12087109 "The YBR159w gene encodes the major 3-ketoreductase activity of the elongase system of enzymes required for very long-chain fatty acid (VLCFA) synthesis"].
- [PMID:12087109 "In vitro elongation assays confirm that the ybr159Delta mutant is deficient in the reduction of the 3-ketoacyl intermediates of fatty acid elongation"].
- Also needed for dehydratase function: [PMID:12087109 "The ybr159Delta mutant also displays reduced dehydration of the 3-OH acyl intermediates of fatty acid elongation"].
- Paralog backup: [PMID:12087109 "suggesting that Ayr1p is responsible for the residual 3-ketoreductase activity"].
- [PMID:12684876 "Elongases II and III share the same 3-ketoacyl reductase, which is encoded by the YBR159w gene"].
- eIF2B interaction [PMID:23263984 "the very-long-chain fatty acid (VLCFA) synthesis keto-reductase enzyme YBR159W physically interact"].
