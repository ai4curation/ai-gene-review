CC   -!- FUNCTION: Bifunctional enzyme that catalyzes the last two steps of
CC       purine biosynthesis (PubMed:10877846, PubMed:9143321). Acts as a
CC       transformylase that incorporates a formyl group to the AMP analog AICAR
CC       (5-amino-1-(5-phospho-beta-D-ribosyl)imidazole-4-carboxamide) to
CC       produce the intermediate formyl-AICAR (FAICAR) (PubMed:10877846,
CC       PubMed:9143321). Also catalyzes the cyclization of FAICAR to IMP
CC       (PubMed:10877846, PubMed:9143321). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10877846,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9143321}.
