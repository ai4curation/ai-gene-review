# ADE16 (YLR028C, P54113) notes

- Bifunctional AICAR transformylase / IMP cyclohydrolase, paralog of ADE17 (84% identity) [PMID:9143321 "The deduced amino acid sequences of the two genes are 84% identical"]; purified enzyme bifunctional, homodimer, cytosolic [PMID:10877846 "Both isozymes are localized to the cytosol"].
- Double mutant adenine + histidine auxotroph (AICAR recycling from His pathway) [PMID:10877846 "an ade16 ade17 double disruption also leads to histidine auxotrophy"].
- Paralog specialisation: ADE16 not adenine-repressed, induced on non-fermentable carbon [PMID:10877846 "Ade16p was observed to be more abundant in cells grown on nonfermentable carbon sources"].
- Ade16-Ade17 heteromer detected in several HT screens (PMID:16429126, 18467557, 31454312, 37968396) -> protein binding REMOVE (uninformative).
- PWY3O-285 salvage RCA -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED.
