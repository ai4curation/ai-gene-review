# URA3 (YEL021W, UniProt P03962) notes

## Function
- OMP decarboxylase (EC 4.1.1.23), final step of de novo UMP synthesis [UniProt:P03962].
- Purified overexpressed enzyme: "The enzyme had a Km for orotidine 5'-monophosphate of 0.7 microM"; monomer at low concentration, ligand-induced dimerisation [PMID:2061334].
- Cytosolic: "Attachment of CL1 to the cytosolic protein Ura3p destabilizes Ura3p" [PMID:18812321].
- Widely used selectable/counter-selectable (5-FOA) marker (general knowledge, not cited as evidence for annotations).
