# GDH3 (YAL062W, P39708) notes

Evidence journal (no paid deep research run; built from UniProt and cached publications).

- NADP-specific glutamate dehydrogenase 2, EC 1.4.1.4; WGD paralog of GDH1 (Glu/Leu/Phe/Val DH family, PTHR43571) [UniProt:P39708].
- Discovery: gdh1 glt1 double mutant still grows on ammonium; GDH3 "showed high identity" with GDH1; "Triple mutants impaired in GDH1, GLT1, and GDH3 were obtained. These were strict glutamate auxotrophs."; "GDH3 plays a significant physiological role, providing glutamate when GDH1 and GLT1 are impaired" [PMID:9287019].
- Biochemistry: purified GDH3 NADP-GDH differs from GDH1 in allosteric properties and 2-oxoglutarate utilisation; expression depends on the carbon source; nonredundant roles; coordinated GDH1/GDH3/GDH2 action balances 2-oxoglutarate use "under fermentative and respiratory conditions" [PMID:11562373].
- Mitochondrial proteomics hits (Sickmann 2003, Reinders 2006) [PMID:14576278, PMID:16823961]; nuclear-fraction NADP-GDH (1976; isoform unresolved) [PMID:4126].
- VID30 deletion study: GDH3 transcription down-regulated by Vid30p function on proline [PMID:11356843] (not annotated here; regulatory context).
