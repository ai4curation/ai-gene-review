Gene Description

Igo1 (Initiation of G zero 1; YNL157W) is a small (168 residue), largely disordered endosulfine-family phosphoprotein of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the paralog of Igo2 and the budding-yeast counterpart of human ENSA/ARPP19. Its defining activity is phosphorylation-gated inhibition of the PP2A-Cdc55 holoenzyme (the yeast PP2A-B55): when the Greatwall-related kinase Rim15 phosphorylates the conserved Ser64 in the YFDSGDY motif, phospho-Igo1 binds the Cdc55 regulatory subunit (not Rts1-containing PP2A) and inhibits PP2A-Cdc55 phosphatase activity, so that selected PP2A-Cdc55 substrates remain phosphorylated. Rim15 is released from TORC1/PKA inhibition on nitrogen or carbon limitation; Igo1/Igo2 then keep the transcription factor Gis1 phosphorylated and promoter-bound, sustaining Msn2/4- and Gis1-dependent expression of nutrient-regulated genes, and Rim15-phosphorylated Igo proteins also associate with the decapping activator Dhh1 and shelter these newly made transcripts from the deadenylation-dependent 5'-3' decay pathway. Through this the Rim15-Igo1/2-PP2A-Cdc55 module governs initiation of the G0/quiescence programme, glycogen accumulation and chronological life span; the same module lowers the Cln3-Cdk1 threshold for START by limiting Whi5 dephosphorylation, is required for entry into sporulation, and transmits a rapid Hog1-independent phosphorylation response to hyperosmotic stress. In cycling cells Igo1 binds PP2A-Cdc55 most strongly in late S/G2 and, paradoxically, cells lacking Igo1/Igo2 show reduced PP2A-Cdc55 activity, nuclear-retained Cdc55 and elevated Swe1-dependent Cdk1 Tyr19 phosphorylation, delaying mitotic entry under temperature stress - so in budding yeast, where PP2A-Cdc55 promotes rather than opposes mitotic entry, the endosulfines support the pro-mitotic activity of the phosphatase. Igo1 and Igo2 are largely redundant. The protein is cytoplasmic and nuclear, with a P-body-associated pool during initiation of G0.

Core Functions

Rim15 (Greatwall)-phosphorylated inhibitor of the PP2A-Cdc55 (PP2A-B55) phosphatase that gates entry into quiescence. When TORC1/PKA signalling falls on nutrient limitation, Rim15 phosphorylates Igo1 on Ser64; phospho-Igo1 binds the Cdc55 regulatory subunit and inhibits PP2A-Cdc55, so that Gis1 stays phosphorylated and promoter-bound and Msn2/4- and Gis1-dependent nutrient-regulated genes are transcribed, initiating the G0 programme, glycogen storage and normal chronological life span. The same phospho-switch lowers the Cln3-Cdk1 threshold at START (by limiting Whi5 dephosphorylation), is required for entry into sporulation, and relays a rapid Hog1-independent hyperosmotic-stress phosphorylation response.

Functional annotation report: Saccharomyces cerevisiae IGO1 (P53897)

Executive conclusion

IGO1/YNL157W encodes a small, non-enzymatic endosulfine that functions principally as a phosphorylation-dependent inhibitor of PP2A-Cdc55 (the budding-yeast PP2A-B55 holoenzyme). Under nutrient limitation, TORC1 inhibition, early-G1 metabolic conditions, or acute osmotic stress, the Greatwall-family kinase Rim15 phosphorylates Igo1 at Ser64. Phosphorylated Igo1 binds the Cdc55 regulatory subunit and inhibits PP2A-Cdc55, thereby allowing selected proteins to remain phosphorylated. Igo1 consequently acts as a molecular switch coupling nutrient or stress signals to transcription, mRNA preservation, cell-cycle commitment, quiescence, chronological survival, and gametogenesis. It generally acts redundantly with the paralog Igo2. It is not an enzyme, transporter, or structural protein, and no catalytic reaction or transported substrate should be assigned to it. (loewith2007structuralandfunctional pages 123-127, severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 2-3, severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 3-4, severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 1-2)

1. Mandatory identity verification

The supplied identity is internally consistent and matches the budding-yeast literature:

  • Gene: IGO1
  • Systematic locus: YNL157W
  • UniProt: P53897
  • Organism: Saccharomyces cerevisiae, including the S288c reference background
  • Protein class: endosulfine-family regulatory protein
  • Relevant paralog: Igo2/YHR132W-A

An unbiased Rim15 substrate screen identified YNL157W as its strongest reproducible substrate. YNL157W and YHR132W-A were recognized as yeast endosulfines and named Igo1 and Igo2. Mass spectrometry mapped Rim15-dependent phosphorylation of Igo1 to Ser64 in the conserved YFDSGDY region, strongly aligning the experimental protein with the Endosulfine/PF04667 family assignment. Recombinant Igo1 and Igo2 were both phosphorylated by Rim15 in vitro. (loewith2007structuralandfunctional pages 123-127)

No evidence reviewed here indicates confusion with a different protein named IGO1. However, the symbol is used for a homolog in Schizosaccharomyces pombe. In particular, a prominent December 2024 Nature Communications study concerns fission-yeast Igo1, not S. cerevisiae P53897; its translation and tRNA-modification findings must therefore not be transferred directly to this protein. (dedo2024thegreatwallendosulfinepp2ab55pathway pages 16-17)

2. Primary molecular function

2.1 Phosphorylation-controlled inhibition of PP2A-Cdc55

The best-supported proximal function is inhibition of PP2A-Cdc55, comprising a PP2A catalytic subunit, the Tpd3 scaffold, and the Cdc55/B55 regulatory subunit. Rim15 phosphorylation converts Igo1 into the active inhibitor:

  1. Nutrient or stress signaling activates Rim15.
  2. Rim15 phosphorylates Igo1 at Ser64.
  3. Igo1-pSer64 binds Cdc55; interaction with the catalytic subunit is indirect through Cdc55.
  4. Bound Igo1 inhibits PP2A-Cdc55 and preserves phosphorylation of selected substrates.

Two-hybrid and co-immunoprecipitation experiments showed that loss of Rim15 or the Igo1-S64A substitution abolished Igo1–Cdc55 association. Purified, Rim15-thiophosphorylated Igo1 inhibited purified PP2A-Cdc55 concentration-dependently, whereas unphosphorylated Igo1 and Igo1-S64A did not strongly inhibit a generic substrate. Against a Gis1-pSer425 peptide, unphosphorylated Igo1 was approximately 13-fold less inhibitory than Igo1-pSer64. (severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 2-3, severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 3-4)

A separate biochemical study found that phosphomimetic Igo1-S64D associated with Cdc55 independently of Rim15 and inhibited purified PP2A-Cdc55, reinforcing the phosphosite-dependent mechanism. Nevertheless, S64D does not reproduce every physiological function of genuine phosphorylation, so it should not be treated as a universally active allele. (sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 6-6, loewith2007structuralandfunctional pages 136-140)

This is best described as a regulatory inhibitor or adaptor mechanism, not catalysis. “mRNA stability protein” describes an important downstream effect, but inhibition of PP2A-Cdc55 is the more precise primary molecular annotation.

3. Biological pathways and processes

3.1 Nutrient signaling, quiescence, and chronological lifespan

Igo1 lies in the conserved TORC1/PKA → Rim15 → Igo1/Igo2 → PP2A-Cdc55 signaling branch. When nutrients are abundant, TORC1, PKA, and related nutrient-responsive inputs restrain Rim15. Nutrient limitation or pharmacological TORC1 inhibition activates Rim15 and allows Igo1/Igo2 to suppress PP2A-Cdc55, facilitating entry into a reversible, stress-resistant G0 state. (sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 1-2, severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 1-2)

Genetic epistasis strongly supports this arrangement. Deleting CDC55 fully or substantially suppressed defects of rim15Δ and igo1Δ igo2Δ cells in stress-gene expression, glycogen accumulation, quiescence entry, and chronological lifespan. Loss of the PP2A catalytic subunit Pph21 produced related suppression, whereas loss of the alternative PP2A regulatory subunit Rts1 did not, demonstrating specificity for PP2A-Cdc55 rather than PP2A generally. (severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 2-3)

3.2 Transcription and mRNA stability

Earlier work established that Igo1/Igo2 are required for stress-response-element and post-diauxic-shift transcripts controlled by Msn2/Msn4 and Gis1 to evade 5′→3′ mRNA decay during initiation of G0. The model implicates the Dhh1/Ccr4-linked decay machinery and explains the “mRNA stability protein” designation. (sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 2-4, severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 1-2)

The 2013 PP2A study added a transcriptional mechanism. Igo1-pSer64 prevents PP2A-Cdc55 from dephosphorylating Gis1, including Gis1 Ser425, thereby favoring Gis1 promoter recruitment and activation of nutrient-regulated genes. A phosphoproteomic screen identified 12 proteins whose rapamycin-induced phosphorylation was diminished in rim15Δ and igo1Δ igo2Δ but enhanced in pph21Δ cells; Gis1-pSer425 was among them. Thus, the pathway coordinates transcript production with transcript preservation rather than acting solely at RNA decay. (severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 3-4, severine2013yeastendosulfinescontrol pages 1-2)

There is an important qualification: a 2014 study did not reproduce suppression of the quiescence or gametogenesis defects by deleting DHH1 or CCR4 in its strain background. The mRNA-decay role is supported, but it is context-dependent and does not account for every Igo1 phenotype. (sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 6-6, sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 12-13)

3.3 START and cell-size homeostasis

In early G1, Rim15–Igo1/Igo2 transiently inhibits PP2A-Cdc55 and limits dephosphorylation of Whi5, the inhibitor of SBF transcription. This lowers the amount of Cln3-Cdk1 activity needed to phosphorylate Whi5 and commit cells at START. Cln3-Cdk1 in turn suppresses Rim15, producing a negative-feedback architecture that helps adapt START size to metabolic state. (talarek2017homeostaticcontrolof pages 13-15, talarek2017homeostaticcontrolof pages 1-2)

Deleting IGO1 and IGO2 delayed START primarily when G1-CDK activity was low. Deletion of CDC55 or WHI5, or ectopic CLN2 expression, suppressed this delay. One sensitized cdc28-as1 igo1Δ igo2Δ condition produced an average budding size of 75.8 fL, and the defect depended on CDC55. Igo1/Igo2 also contributed to chromosome maintenance after the shift from fermentation to respiration. These effects are conditional and relatively subtle under normal Cln3-Cdk1 activity, which is consistent with redundancy and pathway buffering. (talarek2017homeostaticcontrolof pages 12-13, talarek2017homeostaticcontrolof pages 13-15)

3.4 Gametogenesis and pre-meiotic autophagy

During nutrient starvation in diploid budding yeast, the Rim15–Igo1/Igo2–PP2A-Cdc55 module promotes entry into gametogenesis. The igo1Δ igo2Δ double mutant showed approximately 3% sporulation, versus 65% in wild type; cells failed to induce Ime1 and Rec8 normally, replicate pre-meiotic DNA, or proceed beyond a mononucleate state after 12 hours. Wild-type Igo1 restored about 50% sporulation, whereas Igo1-S64A restored only 10%, directly connecting Ser64 regulation to the phenotype. (sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 2-4)

Igo1/Igo2 are also needed for pre-meiotic autophagy, but defective autophagy does not fully explain failed sporulation. Forced IME1 expression and removal of Ume6 or Rpd3 did not rescue the defect, and the gametogenesis mechanism is separable from the Msn2/Msn4/Gis1-dependent quiescence branch. Physiologically relevant PP2A-Cdc55 substrates for this meiotic role remain incompletely defined. (sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 9-12, sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 12-13)

3.5 Hyperosmotic-stress signaling

The most recent direct mechanistic study retrieved for S. cerevisiae Igo1, published online 24 September 2021, showed that Igo1/Igo2 establish a rapid “phosphatase-centric” response parallel to the canonical Hog1 MAP-kinase pathway. Hyperosmotic stress increased phosphorylation of Igo1-Ser64 7.3-fold and Igo2-Ser63 6-fold; phosphorylation appeared within seconds and returned near baseline by approximately 15 minutes. (hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 1-2, hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 5-6)

The SILAC experiment quantified 10,790 phosphorylation sites in the cdc55Δ comparison: 22.1% increased, 4.3% decreased, and 73.6% remained static. Among 143 sites induced both by stress and by cdc55Δ, 92% (131 sites in 91 proteins) decreased in stressed igo1Δ igo2Δ cells. Fifty-three sites in 40 proteins were strongly endosulfine-dependent. The affected proteins were enriched in transcription, mRNA transport, chromatin regulation, trafficking, and actin-related processes. Deleting CDC55 rescued the growth defect of igo1Δ igo2Δ under salt stress, supporting PP2A-Cdc55 as the functionally relevant target rather than an indirect cell-cycle effect. (hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 1-2, hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 5-6)

4. Localization

Igo1 is a soluble intracellular regulatory protein; there is no evidence that it is secreted, membrane-spanning, or organelle-resident. Its demonstrated interactions and substrates place its activity in both nuclear and cytoplasmic regulatory compartments. Evidence indicates nuclear concentration of the Rim15/Igo1/Igo2 system during phosphate starvation, diauxic shift, and TORC1 inhibition, consistent with regulation of Gis1, Whi5, and transcription. Its roles in cytoplasmic mRNA decay and broad osmotic-stress phosphoregulation imply that function is not exclusively nuclear. (talarek2017homeostaticcontrolof pages 12-13)

The localization evidence is weaker than the biochemical evidence for PP2A-Cdc55 inhibition. A single constitutive compartment should therefore not be assigned. The defensible annotation is nucleocytoplasmic, condition-dependent, with demonstrated nuclear relevance during starvation and G1 signaling.

5. Redundancy, regulation, and substrate specificity

Igo1 and Igo2 are partially redundant; many decisive phenotypes require double deletion. Consequently, a mild igo1Δ phenotype does not imply dispensability. Igo1 appears to be the more experimentally characterized paralog and provides the direct biochemical evidence for Ser64-dependent Cdc55 binding. (sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 6-6, loewith2007structuralandfunctional pages 123-127)

The primary target is not every PP2A complex but Cdc55-containing PP2A. Substrate effects are selective: PP2A-Cdc55 favors particular phosphoserine/phosphothreonine motifs, and activated Igo1 preserves phosphorylation of context-specific targets such as Gis1, Whi5-associated circuitry, Sic1-related circuitry, and numerous osmotic-stress proteins. The 2021 data show enrichment for Ser/Thr-Pro motifs among affected sites and demonstrate that inhibition of one phosphatase holoenzyme can produce a signaling output comparable in scale to a kinase cascade. (hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 1-2, hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 5-6)

Igo1 has also been reported to carry a putative PKA-regulated Ser105 site. Earlier experiments suggested antagonism between Ser105 regulation and Rim15-dependent activation, whereas a later sporulation experiment found the conserved PKA site dispensable for gametogenesis. Ser105 should therefore be considered context-dependent rather than part of the core annotation. (sarkar2014therim15endosulfinepp2acdc55signalling pages 13-14, loewith2007structuralandfunctional pages 136-140)

6. Recent developments and species boundary

No retrieved 2023–2024 primary paper directly revised the functional annotation of S. cerevisiae P53897. The latest strong direct study identified here is the 2021 osmotic-stress work (DOI), which broadened Igo1 from a nutrient/quiescence regulator to a rapid, system-wide stress-signaling component. (hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 1-2, hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 5-6)

A December 2024 study (DOI) found that Greatwall–Igo1–PP2A/B55 enhances tRNA U34/A37 modification and translation of Elongator-sensitive transcripts during quiescence. However, its strains, media, and experiments were from S. pombe. This is valuable comparative evidence that endosulfines can couple PP2A regulation to translation, but it does not establish that mechanism for S. cerevisiae Igo1/P53897. (dedo2024thegreatwallendosulfinepp2ab55pathway pages 16-17)

7. Experimental use and real-world relevance

There is no established industrial, clinical, or therapeutic implementation specific to Igo1/P53897. Its current application is chiefly as a research-system component for:

  • dissecting conserved Greatwall–endosulfine–PP2A-B55 signaling;
  • separating phosphatase-driven from kinase-driven stress responses;
  • manipulating quiescence, chronological survival, sporulation, and START in yeast;
  • testing how nutrient signals regulate transcription and mRNA turnover; and
  • studying metabolic control of cell size and commitment.

Because the Greatwall–ENSA/ARPP19–PP2A-B55 architecture is conserved, budding yeast provides a tractable mechanistic model for phosphatase regulation in more complex eukaryotes. Direct human or biomedical claims should nevertheless not be inferred from Igo1 deletion experiments alone. (talarek2017homeostaticcontrolof pages 1-2, hollenstein2021aphosphatase‐centricmechanism pages 1-2)

Final recommended functional annotation

Igo1 is a conditionally activated endosulfine and non-catalytic inhibitor of PP2A-Cdc55. Rim15-dependent phosphorylation at Ser64 promotes Cdc55 binding and suppresses PP2A-Cdc55 activity, preserving phosphorylation of selected regulators during nutrient limitation, early-G1 metabolic adaptation, gametogenesis, and acute osmotic stress. Acting largely redundantly with Igo2, it coordinates quiescence-associated transcription and mRNA stability, chronological survival, START, and sporulation. Its site of action is intracellular and conditionally nucleocytoplasmic rather than membrane-associated or secreted.

Holistic picture

  1. Core molecular function: phosphorylation-gated PP2A-Cdc55 inhibitor.
    PMID:23273919; independently [PMID:23861665 "addition of phosphorylated
    Igo1 to PP2ACdc55 complexes inhibited their activity in vitro in a
    dose-dependent manner"; "efficient interaction between Igo1 and PP2A requires
    Rim15-dependent phosphorylation of Igo1 on Ser64"; Pph21 and Cdc55 but not
    Rts1 co-IP with Igo1-Pk3]. Specificity for the Cdc55 holoenzyme is also
    genetic: cdc55 and pph21 deletion suppress igo1 igo2 / rim15 quiescence
    defects, rts1 deletion does not (Falcon report, from Bontron 2013).
  2. Core process: initiation of the G0 programme / quiescence entry
    (GO:1903452). Downstream readouts: Gis1 kept phosphorylated and
    promoter-bound PMID:23273919, Msn2/4- and Gis1-dependent gene expression, glycogen accumulation,
    chronological life span.
  3. mRNA protection branch (GO:0048255, GO:1900152): Rim15-phosphorylated
    Igo proteins associate with Dhh1 and shelter newly made G0 transcripts from
    5'-3' decay PMID:20471941. UniProt records
    interactions with RIM15, DHH1, PBP1, PBP4, LSM12. Caveats: (a) the 2013 PP2A
    mechanism could account for the effect indirectly; (b) per the Falcon report,
    Sarkar 2014 did not reproduce dhh1/ccr4 suppression in its background
    ["The mRNA-decay role is supported, but it is context-dependent and does not
    account for every Igo1 phenotype."]. Kept as ACCEPT with caveats recorded;
    the curator read the full text and the finding is the paper's central claim.
  4. Mitotic role (Juanes 2013, full text). Paradoxical: phospho-Igo1 is an
    inhibitor in vitro, but in vivo igo1 igo2 cells have LOWER PP2A-Cdc55
    activity, nuclear-retained Cdc55, elevated Swe1-dependent Cdk1-Y19
    phosphorylation and delayed mitotic entry/progression at 16 C and 38 C
    (not at 25 C); swe1 deletion or Cdc28-Y19F rescues [PMID:23861665
    "Surprisingly, deletion of IGO1 and IGO2 in yeast cells leads to a decrease
    in PP2A phosphatase activity, suggesting that endosulfines act also as
    positive regulators of PP2A in yeast"; "RIM15 and IGO1/2 promote, like
    PP2A(Cdc55), timely entry into mitosis under temperature-stress"]. Igo1-Cdc55
    binding peaks in late S/G2 while Ser64 phosphorylation is constant across
    the cycle. So in budding yeast, where PP2A-Cdc55 is pro-mitotic, the
    endosulfines support rather than oppose mitotic entry. NOTE: the task brief
    said Igo1 "contributes to mitotic entry/exit timing by restraining
    PP2A-Cdc55"; the paper says the opposite for the in vivo mitotic role, and
    the review follows the paper.
  5. Other roles (Falcon only, not cached): START / cell-size homeostasis via
    Whi5 (Talarek 2017), sporulation and pre-meiotic autophagy (Sarkar 2014;

    starvation, diauxic shift, TORC1 inhibition.
