# TPS1 (YBR126C, Q00764) notes

- Trehalose-6-phosphate synthase (UDP-forming), GT20, EC 2.4.1.15; catalytic subunit of trehalose synthase complex (Tps1, Tps2, Tsl1/Tps3) [UniProt:Q00764].
- [PMID:9837904 "Deletion of TPS1 totally abolished TPS activity and measurable trehalose"]; "all subunits are required for optimal enzymatic activity"; part of Tps1 is free monomer; "TPS1 is necessary and sufficient for growth on glucose and fructose".
- 56 kDa subunit of 630 kDa enzyme, identical to CIF1 [PMID:1425702].
- Activity assays, Pi inhibition [PMID:2546763].
- Heat shock trehalose metabolism in tps mutants [PMID:9194697]; desiccation [PMID:25456447].
