# PGK1 notes (P00560, YCR012W)

- 3-phosphoglycerate kinase, EC 2.7.2.3 [UniProt:P00560 "Reaction=(2R)-3-phosphoglycerate + ATP = (2R)-3-phospho-glyceroyl"]; cloned and shown to make functional enzyme [PMID:6254992 "functional enzyme is synthesized from the cloned gene in yeast, but not in E. coli"].
- pgk mutants cannot use glucose [PMID:400791 "Cells carrying these mutations were unable to use glucose as a carbon source"]; only PGK gene, reversible step also needed for gluconeogenesis.
- Cytosolic [PMID:27385335 "exhibited a very prominent cytosolic distribution"]; minor mitochondria-associated pool [PMID:16962558 "we show that all glycolytic enzymes are associated with mitochondria in yeast"]. Plasma membrane HDA [PMID:16622836] likely contaminant of an abundant protein.
- NOT protein-disulfide reductase (mammalian PGK plasmin reductase) [PMID:11130727 "Here we show that the plasmin reductase isolated from conditioned medium of fibrosarcoma cells is the glycolytic enzyme phosphoglycerate kinase."] — negative annotation accepted.
