CC   -!- FUNCTION: Core component of the MRN complex, which plays a central role
CC       in double-strand break (DSB) repair, DNA recombination, maintenance of
CC       telomere integrity and meiosis (PubMed:14522986, PubMed:22002605,
CC       PubMed:22705791, PubMed:23080121, PubMed:7625279, PubMed:9651580). The
CC       MRN complex is involved in the repair of DNA double-strand breaks
CC       (DSBs) via homologous recombination (HR), an error-free mechanism which
CC       primarily occurs during S and G2 phases (PubMed:22002605,
CC       PubMed:22705791, PubMed:23080121). The complex (1) mediates the end
CC       resection of damaged DNA, which generates proper single-stranded DNA, a
CC       key initial steps in HR, and is (2) required for the recruitment of
CC       other repair factors and efficient activation of ATM and ATR upon DNA
CC       damage (PubMed:22002605, PubMed:23080121). Within the MRN complex,
CC       MRE11 possesses both single-strand endonuclease activity and double-
CC       strand-specific 3'-5' exonuclease activity (PubMed:14522986,
CC       PubMed:22002605). MRE11 first endonucleolytically cleaves the 5' strand
CC       at DNA DSB ends to prevent non-homologous end joining (NHEJ) and
CC       licence HR (PubMed:23080121). It then generates a single-stranded DNA
CC       gap via 3' to 5' exonucleolytic degradation, which is required for
CC       single-strand invasion and recombination (PubMed:22002605). The MRN
CC       complex is also required for the processing of R-loops
CC       (PubMed:31537797). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14522986,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22002605, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22705791,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23080121, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31537797,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7625279, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9651580}.

CC       RAD50 and MRE11 associated with a single XRS2 (PubMed:23080121,

CC       repair (PubMed:35501303). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23080121,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35501303, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9845372}.
