Gene Description

HSP78 encodes the mitochondrial Hsp100/ClpB-family AAA+ chaperone in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Hsp78 is a soluble mitochondrial matrix protein that forms ATP-dependent homohexamers, hydrolyzes ATP, binds damaged matrix proteins, and cooperates with the Ssc1/Mdj1/Mge1 mitochondrial Hsp70 system to resolubilize and refold heat-induced protein aggregates. Its activity protects respiratory competence and mitochondrial genome integrity after severe heat stress.

Core Functions

Mitochondrial Hsp100/ClpB activity that uses ATP hydrolysis to extract aggregated matrix polypeptides, resolubilize them, and return them to the Ssc1/Mdj1/Mge1 mitochondrial Hsp70 system for refolding after heat stress.

2026-10-09 fungal PAINT-family review

  • Reviewed yeast/HSP78 as the direct experimental anchor for the fungal
    mitochondrial Hsp78 branch of PTHR11638. PAINT places GO:0005759,
    GO:0042026, and GO:0043335 at PANTHER:PTN000909045, the fungal Hsp78
    node seeded by SGD:S000002666; that placement is correct and is not
    circular just because the target contributes the experimental descendant
    evidence.

  • Deep-research provider generation failed because uvx attempted to write its
    managed tool environment under read-only ~/.local/share/uv/tools. No
    HSP78-deep-research-*.md provider file was hand-written. I used cached GOA
    publications plus a 2026-10-09 live newer-paper search instead.

  • Newer-paper search found a later S. cerevisiae mitochondrial-aggregate paper
    that reinforces the same Hsp78/DUMP clearance model, but it did not alter the
    decisions on the seeded GOA rows: the 1993 to 2006 cached HSP78 papers already
    establish matrix localization, ATPase activity, misfolded-client binding,
    aggregate resolubilization, protein refolding, and mitochondrial
    thermotolerance.

  • Leonhardt et al. established that Hsp78 is a heat-regulated soluble
    mitochondrial matrix Clp/Hsp100 protein [PMID:8413229, "Submitochondrial
    fractionation showed that hsp78 is a soluble protein located in the
    mitochondrial matrix."]. This supports accepting the IDA matrix row, the
    matrix PAINT row, and the mitochondrial proteomics rows; the broad
    intracellular organelle lumen ARBA row was redirected to
    GO:0005759 mitochondrial matrix.

  • Krzewska et al. purified Hsp78 and showed ATP-dependent hexamerization,
    ATPase activity, and cooperation with Ssc1/Mdj1/Mge1 in substrate reactivation
    [PMID:11734006, "Studies on the chaperone activity of Hsp78 show that its
    cooperation with the mitochondrial Hsp70 system, consisting of Ssc1p, Mdj1p
    and Mge1p, is needed for the efficient reactivation of substrate proteins."].
    This supports accepting both direct ATP-hydrolysis rows and the protein
    refolding rows.

  • von Janowsky et al. established the in-organello ATP-dependent disaggregation
    role [PMID:16460754, "By establishing an aggregation/disaggregation assay in
    intact mitochondria we demonstrated that Hsp78 is indispensable for the
    resolubilization of protein aggregates generated by heat stress under in vivo
    conditions."]. I therefore added a NEW GO:0140545 ATP-dependent protein disaggregase activity row.

  • The broad PANTHER:PTN000181243 cytoplasm IBA was modified to mitochondrial
    matrix. Cytoplasm is a true but uninformative parent for mitochondrial Hsp78,
    and Hsp78's direct active compartment is the matrix from the descendant
    PANTHER:PTN000909045 node.

  • The two generic protein binding IPI rows to Ssa1 were removed on GO-quality
    grounds. They report generic high-throughput or Hsp70-centered interactions,
    while the informative Hsp78 molecular functions are misfolded-protein binding
    and ATP-dependent protein disaggregase activity.
