# SGD gene description for HSP78 (yeast)

Oligomeric mitochondrial matrix chaperone; cooperates with Ssc1p in mitochondrial thermotolerance after heat shock; prevents the aggregation of misfolded proteins as well as resolubilize protein aggregates; involved in response to ethanol stress
