Gene Description

Mcr1 is the Saccharomyces cerevisiae mitochondrial NADH-cytochrome b5 reductase. A single MCR1 translation product is split into p34 and p32 forms: the p34 form arrests in and anchors at the mitochondrial outer membrane, while the p32 form is processed by the inner-membrane protease and released into the mitochondrial intermembrane space. The enzyme reduces cytochrome b5 from NADH and contributes to the D-erythroascorbate antioxidant system, but its in vivo relationship to ER-localized sterol 14-demethylation is unresolved because budding yeast also carries the microsomal paralog Cbr1. Mcr1 can weakly reduce the Dph3 electron carrier in vitro.
