# IDH2 (YOR136W, P28241) notes

- Catalytic subunit of NAD-IDH [PMID:11042198 "IDH2 was previously shown to contain the catalytic site"]; IDH2 site substitutions cut Vmax 30->300-fold [PMID:11714283 "The primary effects of various residue replacements in IDH2 were reductions of 30->300-fold in V(max) values, consistent with the catalytic function of this subunit"].
- Hetero-octamer with Idh1 [PMID:16884682]; structures [PMID:18256028].
- Matrix enzyme [UniProt:P28241 "SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion matrix"]; presequence-dependent import [PMID:8626605].
- Required for isocitrate/citrate-driven respiration [PMID:2198251] and growth on acetate/glycerol [PMID:11714283].
- glutamate biosynthesis TAS (review PMID:9175438) kept non-core: NAD-IDH supplies 2-oxoglutarate but does not catalyse a glutamate-synthesis step; NADP-IDHs (Idp1/2/3) also supply 2-OG.
