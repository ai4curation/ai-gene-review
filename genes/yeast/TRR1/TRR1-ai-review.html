Gene Description

TRR1 (YDR353W) encodes the cytoplasmic thioredoxin reductase, an FAD-containing, NADPH-dependent homodimeric flavoenzyme of the low-molecular-weight (bacterial-type) thioredoxin reductase family. Trr1 reduces the cytosolic thioredoxins Trx1 and Trx2, which in turn provide electrons to thioredoxin-dependent peroxiredoxins such as Tsa1, to ribonucleotide reductase and to PAPS reductase in sulfate assimilation; it is therefore the central enzyme of the cytosolic thioredoxin system and is important for peroxide tolerance and cellular thiol-redox homeostasis. Trr1 is mainly cytosolic, with a minor pool in the mitochondrial intermembrane space. It interacts in a GTP-dependent manner with the Rho5 GTPase during H2O2-induced cell death and has been reported to bind iron.

Core Functions

Cytosolic NADPH-dependent FAD-containing thioredoxin reductase (EC 1.8.1.9; homodimer) that reduces Trx1 and Trx2, supplying electrons to thioredoxin-dependent peroxiredoxins (Tsa1, Gpx2, Hyr1), ribonucleotide reductase and PAPS reductase; a minor pool is in the mitochondrial IMS.

SGD gene description for TRR1 (yeast)

Cytoplasmic thioredoxin reductase; key regulatory enzyme that determines the redox state of the thioredoxin system, which acts as a disulfide reductase system and protects cells against both oxidative and reductive stress; protein abundance increases in response to DNA replication stress; TRR1 has a paralog, TRR2, that arose from the whole genome duplication
