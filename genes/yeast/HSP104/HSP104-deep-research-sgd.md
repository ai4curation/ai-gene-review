# SGD gene description for HSP104 (yeast)

Disaggregase; heat shock protein that cooperates with Ydj1p (Hsp40) and Ssa1p (Hsp70) to refold and reactivate denatured, protein aggregates; responds to stresses (heat, ethanol, and sodium arsenite); required for the protein aggregate solid-to-liquid phase transition and dispersal of liquid condensates; involved in [PSI+] propagation; potentiated variants eliminate TDP-43 cytoplasmic aggregates to reduce proteotoxicity; becomes more abundant and forms cytoplasmic foci during replication stress
