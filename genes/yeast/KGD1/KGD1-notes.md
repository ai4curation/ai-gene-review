# KGD1 (YIL125W, P20967) notes

- E1 (KE1) of the 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase complex [PMID:2072900 "mutations in KGD1 and KGD2, the structural genes for alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase (KE1) and dihydrolipoyl transsuccinylase (KE2) components, respectively"].
- Reaction: lipoyl-lysine + 2-oxoglutarate -> S-succinyldihydrolipoyl-lysine + CO2, TPP cofactor [UniProt:P20967].
- Assembly: KE1 binds the KE2-E3 particle [PMID:2072900 "The reconstitution of enzymatic activity correlates with binding of KE1 to the KE2-E3 particle"]; Kgd4 binds the Kgd1-Kgd2 core and recruits Lpd1 [PMID:25165143 "these data show that Kgd4 interacts with the Kgd1-Kgd2 complex and not with Kgd2"].
- Mitochondrial matrix; part of KGDC associates with nucleoids [PMID:11926067 "These results suggested that a part of KGDC in the mitochondrial matrix is associated with mt-nucleoids in vivo"].
- GO note: the overall NAD+ KGDH term (GO:0034602) is obsolete; GO:0004591 serves for the complex reaction, so RCA contributes_to rows on E2 are acceptable.
