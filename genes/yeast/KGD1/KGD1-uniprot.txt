CC   -!- FUNCTION: The 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase complex catalyzes the
CC       overall conversion of 2-oxoglutarate to succinyl-CoA and CO(2). It
CC       contains multiple copies of three enzymatic components: 2-oxoglutarate
CC       dehydrogenase (E1), dihydrolipoamide succinyltransferase (E2) and
CC       lipoamide dehydrogenase (E3). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11926067,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2503710, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8299151}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=N(6)-[(R)-lipoyl]-L-lysyl-[protein] + 2-oxoglutarate + H(+) =
CC         N(6)-[(R)-S(8)-succinyldihydrolipoyl]-L-lysyl-[protein] + CO2;
CC         Xref=Rhea:RHEA:12188, Rhea:RHEA-COMP:10474, Rhea:RHEA-COMP:20092,

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase complex (OGDC),
CC       also called alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase (KGDH) complex. The
CC       complex is composed of the catalytic subunits OGDH (2-oxoglutarate
CC       dehydrogenase KGD1; also called E1 subunit), DLST (dihydrolipoamide
CC       succinyltransferase KGD2; also called E2 subunit) and DLD
CC       (dihydrolipoamide dehydrogenase LPD1; also called E3 subunit), and the
CC       assembly factor KGD4. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2072900,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25165143}.
