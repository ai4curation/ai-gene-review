# DUR1,2 (YBR208C, P32528) notes

Pathway context: urea amidolyase; urea carboxylase (EC 6.3.4.6) + allophanate hydrolase (EC 3.5.1.54); urea degradation I and terminal steps of the yeast allantoin superpathway.

- Two activities, one gene [PMID:6105114 "In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the degradation of urea to carbon dioxide and ammonia is catalyzed by urea carboxylase and allophanate hydrolase"; "the two dur loci might in reality be domains of a single gene that codes for a multifunctional polypeptide"].
- Biotin cofactor; monomer; induced by allophanate/oxalurate [UniProt:P32528].
- Allophanate is the pathway inducer and last intermediate [PMID:1938916 "induced by the presence of allophanate, the last intermediate of the allantoin degradative pathway"]; allantoin N passes through urea and allophanate [PMID:4604238].
- Two core functions (one per domain). Allantoin catabolic process accepted: Dur1,2 performs the final steps of the yeast allantoin degradation route.
- Generic ligase, ATP binding, metal ion binding kept as non-core.
