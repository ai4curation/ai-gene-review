# INP52 / SJL2 notes (YNL106C, P50942)

- Two active sites: SAC domain converts PI3P, PI4P, PI(3,5)P2 to PI [PMID:10224048 "Purified recombinant SAC1-like domains convert yeast lipids phosphatidylinositol (PI) 3-phosphate, PI 4-phosphate, and PI 3,5-bisphosphate to PI, whereas PI 4,5-bisphosphate is not a substrate."]; 5-phosphatase domain catalytic [PMID:10224048 "The 5-phosphatase domains of synaptojanin, Inp52p, and Inp53p are also catalytic"]; [PMID:11094088 "Purified recombinant Inp52p lacking the Sac1 domain hydrolyzed phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate"]
- Actin patches/endocytic sites via Abp1 [PMID:15798181 "In this study, we show that Sjl2 is localized to cortical actin patches, sites of endocytosis."]; Bsp1 adaptor [PMID:12606027]
- Stress translocation [PMID:11094088 "Following hyperosmotic stress the GFP-tagged 5-phosphatases rapidly and transiently associated with actin patches"]
- YeastPathways assigns 3 reactions (EC 3.1.3.36, 3.1.3.64, PI(3,5)P2 -> PI3P); all supported by Guo 1999, though the SAC domain takes PI(3,5)P2 all the way to PI.
