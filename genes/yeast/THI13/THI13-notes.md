# THI13 notes (Q07748, YDL244W)

- One of four THI5-family paralogs (THI5/11/12/13), subtelomeric, Saccharomyces sensu stricto-specific expansion [PMID:12777485 "The THI5 gene family of Saccharomyces cerevisiae comprises four highly conserved members"].
- Redundant for HMP synthesis: "each promotes synthesis of HMP from the pyridoxine (vitamin B(6)) biosynthetic pathway" [PMID:12777485].
- Activity by similarity to THI5 (P43534): HMP-P from active-site His + PLP, Fe-dependent, single turnover (suicide) [UniProt:Q07748].
- Rsp5 WW-domain microarray hit [PMID:16606443]; screen noted thiamine-enzyme enrichment among Rsp5 binders -> possible turnover of spent suicide enzymes (speculative).
- Review: 10 ACCEPT, 1 REMOVE (protein binding).
