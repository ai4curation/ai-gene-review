# BNA1 (YJR025C, P47096) notes

## Identity
- 3-hydroxyanthranilate 3,4-dioxygenase (3-HAO), EC 1.13.11.6, Fe(2+) cupin enzyme [UniProt:P47096].

## Evidence
- Deletion causes nicotinic acid auxotrophy; activity in extracts proportional to gene copy number [PMID:9539135 "In cell-free extracts the 3-hydroxyanthranilic acid dioxygenase activity is proportional to the copy number of the YJR025c gene."].
- Kynurenine pathway genes are co-lethal with npt1, i.e. the only de novo NAD route [PMID:12062417 "We show that deletion of genes encoding kynurenine pathway enzymes are co-lethal with the Deltanpt1, demonstrating that no other pathway for the synthesis of nicotinic acid exists in S. cerevisiae."].
- Myc-Bna1 throughout the cell [PMID:11901108 "By immunofluorescence microscopy, Myc-tagged Bna1p was localized throughout the whole cell in an asynchronously growing population."]; UniProt lists cytoplasm and nucleus from the GFP survey.

## Ontology note
- GO:0034354 'de novo' NAD+ biosynthetic process from L-tryptophan is now obsolete, replaced_by GO:0034628 'de novo' NAD+ biosynthetic process (QuickGO). The product (2-amino-3-carboxymuconate semialdehyde) cyclises to quinolinate, so GO:0019805 quinolinate biosynthetic process is the most specific process.
