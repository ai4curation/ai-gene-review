# SGD gene description for MCA1 (yeast)

Type I metacaspase; Ca2+-dependent cysteine protease; regulates proteostasis, cleaving specific substrates during the stress response; regulates apoptosis in response to certain triggers; required for clearance of insoluble protein aggregates during normal growth; implicated in cell cycle dynamics and lifespan extension; undergoes autocatalytic processing; similar to mammalian metacaspases, but exists as a monomer due to an extra pair of anti-parallel beta-strands that block dimerization
