# MDH2 notes (P22133, YOL126C)

- Major non-mitochondrial MDH [PMID:1986231 "The major nonmitochondrial isozyme of malate dehydrogenase (MDH2) in Saccharomyces cerevisiae cells grown with acetate as a carbon source was purified"]
- Required on C2 sources [PMID:1986231 "Disruption of the MDH2 gene in a haploid yeast strain produced a mutant unable to grow on minimal medium with acetate or ethanol as a carbon source."]
- Gluconeogenic function, N-terminus-dependent interactions with PCK1/FBP1 [PMID:12730240 "A truncated form (deltanMDH2) of yeast cytosolic malate dehydrogenase (MDH2) lacking 12 residues on the amino terminus was found to be inadequate for gluconeogenic function in vivo"]
- Glucose-induced degradation via Pro/N-degron [UniProt:P22133]
