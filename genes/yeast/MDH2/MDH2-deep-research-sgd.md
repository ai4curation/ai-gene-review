# SGD gene description for MDH2 (yeast)

Cytoplasmic malate dehydrogenase; one of three isozymes that catalyze interconversion of malate and oxaloacetate; involved in the glyoxylate cycle and gluconeogenesis during growth on two-carbon compounds; interacts with Pck1p and Fbp1; mutation in human homolog MDH2 causes early-onset severe encephalopathy
