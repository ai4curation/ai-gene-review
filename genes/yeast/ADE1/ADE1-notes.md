# ADE1 (YAR015W, P27616) notes

- SAICAR synthetase, EC 6.3.2.6; step 7 of de novo IMP synthesis (CAIR + Asp + ATP -> SAICAR) [UniProt:P27616 "Catalyzes the reaction of 4-carboxy-5-aminoimidazole ribotide"].
- Cloned by complementation; overexpressed product purified as SAICAR synthetase [PMID:1756975 "produced N-succinyl-5-aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribotide (SAICAR) synthetase in yeast cells at a level of 40% of total cellular protein"].
- ade1 (like ade2) mutants accumulate red pigment [PMID:8939809 "the ade2, and/or the ade1, mutation in the adenine biosynthetic pathway leads to the accumulation of a cell-limited red pigment"].
- SAICAR is the signal metabolite for Bas1-Bas2 ADE gene activation [PMID:11689683 "SAICAR, an intermediate metabolite of the pathway, is needed for optimal activation of the ADE genes"].
- Localization: cytoplasm (+nucleus) in GFP survey [PMID:14562095].
- Curation: YeastPathways superpathway PWY3O-285 gives "purine-containing compound salvage" -> REMOVE (de novo only). Hsp82 IPI (PMID:15766533) -> REMOVE (uninformative protein binding).
