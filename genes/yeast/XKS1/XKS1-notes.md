# XKS1 (YGR194C, P42826) notes

- D-xylulokinase, FGGY kinase family, EC 2.7.1.17 [UniProt:P42826].
- Required for growth on D-xylulose [PMID:9595677 "a Saccharomyces cerevisiae gene necessary for growth in culture media with D-xylulose as the sole carbon source"].
- Deletion prevents xylulose metabolism; overexpression raises growth on xylulose to ~20% of glucose; partially purified enzyme is reversible and also accepts D-ribulose [PMID:10981687 "It is reversible and will also accept D-ribulose as a substrate"].
- Cytoplasm by GFP [UniProt:P42826; PMID:14562095].

Pathway context: final step of xylose oxidoreductase pathway (D-xylulose + ATP -> D-xylulose 5-phosphate). Verified.
