CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the synthesis of cardiolipin (CL)
CC       (diphosphatidylglycerol) by specifically transferring a phosphatidyl
CC       group from CDP-diacylglycerol to phosphatidylglycerol (PG)
CC       (PubMed:2171667, PubMed:26793177, PubMed:29167800, PubMed:9370334). CL
CC       is a key phospholipid in mitochondrial membranes and plays important
CC       roles in maintaining the functional integrity and dynamics of
CC       mitochondria under both optimal and stress conditions (PubMed:9370334).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2171667, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26793177,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29167800, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9370334}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=a CDP-1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol + a 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-
CC         phospho-(1'-sn-glycerol) = a cardiolipin + CMP + H(+);
CC         Xref=Rhea:RHEA:32931, ChEBI:CHEBI:15378, ChEBI:CHEBI:58332,
CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:60377, ChEBI:CHEBI:62237, ChEBI:CHEBI:64716; EC=2.7.8.41;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:2171667, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9370334};

CC       {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Cardiolipin absent from cells (PubMed:26793177).

CC       (PubMed:26793177, PubMed:29167800). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26793177,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29167800}.

CC       complement T.brucei CLS/cardiolipin synthetase.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:29167800}.
