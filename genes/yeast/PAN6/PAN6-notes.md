# PAN6 (YIL145C, P40459) notes

## Identity
- Pantoate--beta-alanine ligase (pantothenate synthetase), EC 6.3.2.1, PanC family [UniProt].
- "Reaction=(R)-pantoate + beta-alanine + ATP = (R)-pantothenate + AMP +" [UniProt].

## Evidence
- YIL145c required for pantothenate biosynthesis [PMID:11154694 "yeast genes ECM31 and YIL145c, which both have structural homology to genes of the bacterial pantothenic acid pathway, were also required for pantothenic acid biosynthesis"].
- Yeast ORF complements E. coli panC auxotroph [PMID:10417331 "A rice (Oryza sativum) expressed sequence tag, identified by sequence similarity to PS, was also able to complement the E. coli auxotroph, as was an open reading frame from Saccharomyces cerevisiae (baker's yeast)"]. This is the basis of the SGD IDA (functional complementation, abstract-only).

## Localization
- Cytoplasm and nucleus by GFP survey [PMID:14562095]; nuclear signal is of unknown significance.
