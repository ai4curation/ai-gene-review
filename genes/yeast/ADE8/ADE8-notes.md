- GCN4-dependent induction [UniProt:P04161 "Induced by amino acid and purine starvation in a GCN4"].
- ade8 mutants accumulate GAR (title only cached) [PMID:4581491 "The accumulation of glycinamide ribotide by ade3 and ade8 mutants of Saccharomyces cerevisiae"].
- ade8 epistatic to ade2 red pigment [PMID:8939809 "epistatic mutations in the same pathway, i.e. ade8, preclude this phenomenon, resulting in normal white colonies"].
- GFP: cytoplasm + nucleus [PMID:14562095].
