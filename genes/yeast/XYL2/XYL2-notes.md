# XYL2 (YLR070C, Q07993) notes

- Xylitol dehydrogenase / D-xylulose reductase EC 1.1.1.9, zinc MDR alcohol dehydrogenase family [UniProt:Q07993].
- [PMID:10486580 "Overexpression of this open reading frame in S. cerevisiae resulted in xylitol dehydrogenase activity"]; "The enzyme is specific for NADH"; activity is xylose-inducible in wild-type cells.
- Zinc binding by similarity; included in bioinformatic zinc proteome [PMID:30358795].
- No experimental subcellular location; cytosol assumed (IBA/RCA).

Pathway context: step 2 of xylose oxidoreductase pathway (xylitol + NAD+ -> D-xylulose + NADH). Verified.
Curation: IBA "fructose biosynthetic process" from SORD node marked over-annotated (sorbitol dehydrogenase role belongs to SOR1/SOR2; no fructose formation shown for Xyl2).
