# ENO1 (P00924) notes

## Activity and isozymes
- Enolase (EC 4.2.1.11), Mg2+-dependent [UniProt:P00924 "Reaction=(2R)-2-phosphoglycerate = phosphoenolpyruvate + H2O;"].
- Two enolase genes, ENO1 and ENO2; polypeptides assort randomly into three active dimers [PMID:6282834 "isozymes are formed in the cell by random assortment of the two polypeptides into three active enolase dimers"].
- Carbon-source regulation [PMID:6282834 "Log phase cells grown on glucose contain 20-fold more enolase 2 polypeptide than enolase 1 polypeptide, whereas cells grown on ethanol or glycerol plus lactate contain similar amounts of the two polypeptides."].
- ENO1 non-essential, ENO2 essential [PMID:16565073 "Either deletion of the non-essential ENO1 gene or diminished expression of the essential ENO2 gene causes vacuole fragmentation in vivo"].
- Reversible reaction -> glycolysis and gluconeogenesis (YeastPathways RCA for both is reasonable).

## Moonlighting / minor locations
- Vacuole fusion stimulation independent of catalysis [PMID:16565073 "Recombinant Eno1p or Eno2p stimulates in vitro vacuole fusion, as does a catalytically inactive mutant enolase"]; small vacuole-bound pool [PMID:16565073 "Enolase is a cytosolic glycolytic enzyme, but a small portion of enolase is bound to vacuoles."].
- Mitochondrial-surface complex [PMID:16962558 "part of the cellular pool of the glycolytic enzyme, enolase, is tightly associated with the mitochondrial surface"].
- Cell surface (Eno2 annotation) [PMID:16544286 "the enolase-GFP fusion co-localized with a plasma membrane marker"], noted as controversial by the authors.
- Melatonin affinity capture [PMID:31708896 "Surprisingly, most of the proteins identified participate in the glycolytic pathway"]: abundance-biased pulldown; weak evidence for real melatonin binding.
