# FAU1 (YER183C) notes

UniProt P40099; 5-formyltetrahydrofolate cyclo-ligase / MTHFS, EC 6.3.3.2 [UniProt:P40099].

## Evidence journal
- Identified by homology to human MTHFS; recombinant enzyme active [PMID:11923304 "The yeast enzyme was expressed in Escherichia coli, and the purified recombinant enzyme exhibited kinetics similar to previously purified MTHFS."].
- Physiological role: folinic acid utilisation [PMID:11923304 "when the MTHFS gene was disrupted in a strain lacking the de novo folate biosynthesis pathway, folinic acid (5-CHO-THF) could no longer support the folate requirement"]; fau1 cells accumulate 5-CHO-THF [PMID:11923304 "In fau1 disruptants, 5-CHO-THF was elevated 4-fold over wild-type yeast."].
- SHMT is the in vivo source of 5-CHO-THF [PMID:11923304 "5-CHO-THF was undetectable in strains lacking SHMT activity"].
- Location: mitochondrial proteomics only [PMID:14576278; PMID:16823961; UniProt:P40099]. Cytosol is a YeastPathways default.
