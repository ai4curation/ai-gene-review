Gene Description

Ornithine carbamoyltransferase (OTCase, EC 2.1.3.3) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. This cytosolic anabolic enzyme transfers the carbamoyl group of carbamoyl phosphate (made by the arginine-specific carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase CPA1/CPA2) to L-ornithine exported from mitochondria, forming L-citrulline; this is the first step of the conversion of ornithine to arginine. Unlike OTCases of mammals, Neurospora and fission yeast, the S. cerevisiae enzyme is not mitochondrial. ARG3 activity is regulated by the 'epiarginase' mechanism: in the presence of ornithine and arginine, OTCase forms a one-to-one complex with arginase (CAR1) in which OTCase is inhibited while arginase stays active, preventing futile synthesis of citrulline while arginine is being catabolised. ARG3 expression is repressed by arginine (ArgR/Mcm1) and induced by general amino acid control.
