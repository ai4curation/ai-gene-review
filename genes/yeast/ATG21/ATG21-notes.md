# ATG21 notes

## 2026-11-21 fungal PROPPIN review

ATG21 encodes the budding-yeast Atg21 PROPPIN, an Atg18/Hsv2 sibling that binds phosphoinositides and is required for efficient Cvt vesicle formation and Atg8 lipidation at the phagophore assembly site. The 2004 Mol Biol Cell paper showed that Atg18, Atg21 and Ygr223c bind immobilized PtdIns(3)P in vitro and "also bound to PtdIns 4-phosphate with apparently equal affinity"; it further found that "Atg21 is required for the Cvt pathway but not for nitrogen starvation-induced autophagy" [PMID:15155809]. Meiling-Wesse et al. found that growing `atg21Delta` cells fail to form prApe1-containing Cvt vesicles, retain partial starvation-induced autophagy, and show retarded Atg8-PE formation with defective Atg8 PAS recruitment [PMID:15194695].

The direct Atg21 core is now best framed as PI3P-dependent organization of Atg8 lipidation. Juris et al. showed that Atg21 binds via PI3P to the PAS, directly interacts with Atg16 and Atg8, and "defines PI3P-dependently the lipidation site by linking and organising the E3 ligase complex and Atg8 at the PAS" [PMID:25691244]. The newer Atg21-retromer paper identified Atg21 interaction with Pep8/Vps26, Vps29 and Vps35, showed that retromer binding and loop 6 C/D membrane bending are needed for Atg21-retromer vacuole fission activity, and found cross-talk between Atg21, Atg18 and Hsv2 retromer complexes [PMID:42135947].

Cargo-specific process rows need to be kept narrower than the PTHR11227 root. SGD records a non-core Atg21 mitophagy annotation from the 2009 genome-wide screen [PMID:19793921], but the accessible cached text does not name ATG21 and the evidence is likely in uncached supplement. The 2008 PMN analysis directly supports non-core PMN/nucleophagy, where GFP-Osh1 degradation was significantly reduced but not blocked in `atg21Δ` cells [PMID:18701704]. By contrast, the root PAINT pexophagy and glycophagy rows are not supported by direct Atg21 pexophagy or glycophagy experiments.

PubMed searches on 2026-11-21 found two 2026 `S. cerevisiae` PROPPIN papers relevant to Atg21 and Hsv2, PMIDs 42135947 and 42135946, and no newer ATG21-specific Candida paper.
