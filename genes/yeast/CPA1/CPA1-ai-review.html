Gene Description

Small (glutamine amidotransferase, CarA-family) subunit of the arginine-specific carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase (CPSase A) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Cpa1p forms a cytosolic heterodimer with the large synthetase subunit Cpa2p; it binds and hydrolyses L-glutamine (catalytic Cys264 and His349) and delivers the ammonia to Cpa2p, conferring glutamine-dependent carbamoyl-phosphate synthesis on the holoenzyme. CPSase A supplies carbamoyl phosphate for ornithine carbamoylation to citrulline in arginine biosynthesis and is repressed by arginine (translational control via a uORF-encoded arginine attenuator peptide). Unlike S. pombe and Neurospora, where the arginine-pathway CPSase is mitochondrial, the yeast enzyme is cytosolic and its carbamoyl phosphate joins a single pool shared with the pyrimidine-specific CPSase (URA2).

Core Functions

Glutamine amidotransferase (small, CarA-type) subunit of the cytosolic arginine-specific carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase (CPSase A; Cpa1p-Cpa2p heterodimer). Cpa1p binds and hydrolyses glutamine (catalytic Cys264/His349) and passes the released ammonia to Cpa2p, enabling glutamine-dependent carbamoyl-phosphate synthesis for ornithine carbamoylation in arginine biosynthesis.
