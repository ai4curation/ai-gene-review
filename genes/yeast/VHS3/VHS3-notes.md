# VHS3 (YOR054C, Q08438) notes

## Identity
- Hal3 paralog (HFCD superfamily, PPCDC-like). Moonlighting: His-donor subunit of the heterotrimeric PPCDC with Cab3, and inhibitory subunit of Ppz1 [UniProt:Q08438].

## Evidence
- Vhs3 binds the Ppz1 C-terminal catalytic moiety in vivo and inhibits it in vitro [PMID:15192104 "Similarly to Hal3, Vhs3 binds in vivo to the C-terminal catalytic moiety of Ppz1 and inhibits in vitro its phosphatase activity."].
- hal3 vhs3 synthetic lethality is Ppz-independent and requires His459 [PMID:15192104 "Furthermore, a Vhs3 version carrying the H459A mutation did not rescue the synthetically lethal phenotype."] -- later explained by the PPCDC role [PMID:19915539 "Our results unveil Hal3 and Vhs3 as moonlighting proteins involved in both CoA biosynthesis and protein phosphatase regulation."].
- Hal3/Vhs3 contribute the His, Cab3 the Cys [PMID:26514574 "Instead, Hal3 and Vhs3 only contain the His, while Cab3 has the requisite Cys residue, in addition to a non-functional His"].
- Vhs3 is less able than Hal3 to undergo monomer exchange [PMID:26514574 "This de-oligomerization is made possible by structural features that set Hal3 apart from Vhs3, increasing its ability to undergo monomer exchange."], suggesting Vhs3 is relatively more committed to PPCDC.
- Binds Cab3 in the CoA-SPC [PMID:23789928].

## Localization
- No direct experimental localization in GOA; cytosolic by pathway inference.
