# SPE3 (YPR069C, Q12074) notes

- Spermidine synthase, EC 2.5.1.16 [UniProt:Q12074 "Reaction=S-adenosyl 3-(methylsulfanyl)propylamine + putrescine = S-"].
- spe3 null has no spermidine synthase activity and requires spermidine [PMID:9073064 "spe3delta, which has no spermidine synthase activity and has an absolute requirement for spermidine or spermine for the growth"]. Hence Spe3 is the sole spermidine synthase (Spe4 cannot substitute).
- Interacts with Spe4 in three HT screens (PMID:16429126, PMID:18719252, PMID:37968396); bare protein binding removed per repo policy, but a Spe3-Spe4 complex is plausible.
- PWY3O-351 inherited "L-methionine salvage" BP: Spe3 produces MTA (salvage substrate) but performs no salvage step -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED.
