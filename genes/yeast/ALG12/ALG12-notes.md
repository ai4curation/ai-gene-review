# ALG12 (ECM39, YNR030W, P53730) notes

Pathway context given: n_glycan_llo_assembly_cytoplasmic. NOTE: Alg12 is a LUMINAL, Dol-P-Man-dependent enzyme (Man7 -> Man8GlcNAc2-PP-Dol, alpha-1,6; EC 2.4.1.260), not a cytoplasmic-face enzyme.

- Identification. [PMID:10336995 "We report on the identification and characterization of the ALG12 locus encoding a novel mannosyltransferase responsible for the addition of the alpha-1,6 mannose to dolichol-linked Man7GlcNAc2."]
- alg12 accumulates Man7 LLO; invertase hypoglycosylated. [PMID:12460938 "The Saccharomyces cerevisiae alg12delta mutant accumulates oligosaccharide lipid with a Man(7)GlcNAc(2) oligosaccharide."]
- Luminal steps. [PMID:15987956 "This report completes the list of lumen-oriented glycosyltransferases required for the assembly of the LLO."]; UniProt: "In the lumen of the endoplasmic reticulum, adds the eighth mannose residue in an alpha-1,6 linkage" [UniProt:P53730]

Curation observations
- RCA "cytosol" REMOVED (luminal enzyme).
- YeastPathways labels the reaction EC 2.4.1.130 (generic Dol-P-Man:LLO mannosyltransferase); UniProt/Rhea use EC 2.4.1.260.
- Module placement: belongs to the luminal LLO extension module, not the cytoplasmic module.
