# ARO2 (YGL148W, P28777) notes

Pathway context: chorismate synthase, step 7 of shikimate pathway (EC 4.2.3.5).

- Bifunctional: chorismate synthase + NADPH-dependent FMN reductase [PMID:8971708 "Here the characterization of the S. cerevisiae CS is reported in more detail and it is shown that the protein is also bifunctional."; "a flavin reductase (FR) activity able to generate reduced flavin mononucleotide in the presence of NADPH"].
- Earlier cloning paper predicted monofunctionality [PMID:1837329 "We therefore suggest that the S. cerevisiae chorismate synthase, in contrast to the Neurospora crassa enzyme, but like other chorismate synthases, is a monofunctional peptide"] - superseded by PMID:8971708.
- Structure: D2 tetramer, novel fold, requires reduced FMN [PMID:14573601 "The molecule is arranged as a tight tetramer with D2 symmetry"].
- GCN4-regulated [PMID:1837329 "ARO2 is regulated under the 'general control system' for amino acid biosynthesis by the transcriptional activator GCN4"].
- Riboflavin reductase (NADPH) rows kept non-core; FMN reductase (NADPH) accepted as second core activity.
- Module note: flavin reductase bifunctionality is fungal (Neurospora, yeast); bacterial/plant CS are monofunctional and rely on external reduced FMN.
