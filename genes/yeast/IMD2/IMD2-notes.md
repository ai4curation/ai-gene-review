# IMD2 (PUR5, YHR216W, P38697) notes

- IMPDH, IMP + NAD+ -> XMP + NADH, EC 1.1.1.205 [UniProt:P38697 "Reaction=IMP + NAD(+) + H2O = XMP + NADH + H(+)"]; rate-limiting for GTP [PMID:11441018 "IMP dehydrogenase is a rate-limiting enzyme involved in the synthesis of GTP"].
- Paralogs IMD1 (poorly expressed, subtelomeric), IMD3, IMD4; only imd2 loss causes MPA/6AU sensitivity [PMID:11441018 "loss of IMD2, but not IMD1, IMD3, or IMD4, conferred upon yeast the same drug sensitivity found in elongation mutants"].
- Uniquely regulated paralog, responds to nucleotide pools/growth rate [PMID:11441018 "responds through a uniquely regulated member of the IMD gene family"]; enzyme intrinsically drug resistant [UniProt:P38697 "IMD2 seems to be intrinsically drug resistant"].
- Mixed heterotetramers possible [UniProt:P38697 "Seems to be able to form heterotetramers composed from more than 1 of the 3 IMPDH gene products (IMD2-4)"].
- Moonlighting/high-throughput: recruited to elongating Pol II via CTD Ser2-P [PMID:20097157 "IMPDHs were recruited to elongating RNApII only when serine 2 of the CTD was phosphorylated by Ctk1"]; mRNP capture [PMID:23222640 "Various metabolic proteins were observed (Imd2, Imd3, Imd4, Cys4, Cpr1)"] -> kept non-core.
