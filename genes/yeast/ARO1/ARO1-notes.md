# ARO1 (YDR127W, P08566) notes

Pathway context: shikimate/chorismate pathway steps 2-6 (YeastPathways ARO-PWY-1); EC 4.2.3.4, 4.2.1.10, 1.1.1.25, 2.7.1.71, 2.5.1.19.

- Pentafunctional AROM, 1588 aa, mosaic of E. coli-like domains [PMID:2825635 "the arom polypeptide is a mosaic of functional domains"]; domain order DHQS - EPSPS - SK - DHQase(type I) - SDH [UniProt:P08566].
- Cloned ARO1 overproduces all five activities and complements E. coli aroA/B/D/E [PMID:6355828 "express 5 to 12 times the normal level of the five AROl enzyme activities"; "fragment also complemented aroA, aroB, aroD, and aroE mutants of Escherichia coli"].
- GCN4 general control [PMID:2848727 "This provides a very economical means of simultaneously tailoring the synthesis of five shikimate pathway enzymes to the needs of the cell."].
- aro1/aro2 homozygotes fail to sporulate [PMID:361708 "Mutations in ARO1 and ARO2 genes coding for enzymes involved in the common part of the aromatic amino acid pathway completely block the sporulation"].
- Zn: 2 Zn2+/subunit (HAMAP) [UniProt:P08566]; zinc-proteome RCA kept non-core.
- Curation: three HTP protein-binding IPIs (Gavin 2002/2006, Hsp90 map) REMOVEd as uninformative; "chorismate metabolic process" RCA (superpathway) MODIFY -> chorismate biosynthetic process.
- Module note: AROM fusion is fungal/protist-specific; bacteria/plants use separate AroB, AroD/DHQ-SDH, AroE, AroK, AroA polypeptides. Type I DHQase (not type II) in yeast.
