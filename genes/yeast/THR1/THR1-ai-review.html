Gene Description

THR1 (YHR025W) encodes homoserine kinase (EC 2.7.1.39), a ~40 kDa GHMP-family kinase that forms a homodimer. It catalyses the ATP-dependent O-phosphorylation of L-homoserine to O-phospho-L-homoserine, committing homoserine (the branch point shared with methionine synthesis) to the threonine branch of the aspartate-family pathway (step 4 of 5). THR1 is under GCN4 general amino acid control. thr1 deletion mutants are threonine auxotrophs. They also accumulate the toxic intermediate homoserine, which causes pleiotropic sensitivities (temperature, pH, salts, 5-fluorocytosine), rapid death during threonine starvation or in serum, and attenuated survival in mouse infection models. These phenotypes are suppressed by blocking homoserine production (hom3, hom6).

Proposed protein misincorporation, proteasome effects and particular DNA-damage mechanisms remain hypotheses, not new direct functions.
