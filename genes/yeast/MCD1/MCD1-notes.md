# MCD1 (Scc1/Pds3/Rhc21; YDL003W; UniProt Q12158) - curation notes

Working notes for the GO annotation review of the budding-yeast cohesin
alpha-kleisin. Session: 2026-09-26. Inputs: `MCD1-uniprot.txt`, `MCD1-goa.tsv`
(52 SGD/UniProt rows), `MCD1-deep-research-falcon.md`, cached publications.
Exemplars used for consistency: `genes/human/RAD21` and `genes/SCHPO/rad21`.

## Identity and architecture

- Mcd1 = Scc1: identified independently by Guacci/Koshland (mitotic chromosome
  determinant) and Michaelis/Nasmyth (sister chromatid cohesion) in 1997.
  [PMID:9335334 "A cohesion function for Mcd1p (Scc1p) has also been demonstrated by independent methods ( Michaelis et al., 1997 )."]
- Kleisin bridging the Smc heads: [PMID:11983169 "The two heads of the V-shaped
  dimer are connected by different ends of the cleavable Scc1 subunit."]
  In-vivo FRET: [PMID:17660750 "This suggests that the Scc1 N-terminus is
  positioned closer to the Smc3 head, but that it retains proximity also with
  Smc1."] and Scc3 sits on the kleisin C-terminal half [PMID:17660750
  "Biochemical evidence suggests that Scc3 interacts with the Scc1 C-terminal
  half, and associates with the cohesin complex in an Scc1-dependent manner
  (Haering et al, 2002)."]. UniProt DOMAIN line: C-terminal part to SMC1 head,
  N-terminal part to SMC3 head. Structures: 1W1W, 4UX3, 5FRP, 6QPQ, 6QPW, 6ZZ6.
- Four-subunit complex defined by co-IP: [PMID:9990856 "These data suggest that
  Scc1p, Scc3p, Smc1p, and Smc3p form a soluble 'Cohesin' complex in extracts
  from yeast."]; Scc2 substoichiometric, Smc2 absent.
- Tripartite ring / topological entrapment: [PMID:21139566 "Inter-connection of
  NBDs by Scc1 creates a tripartite ring"]; [PMID:21139566 "circular sister
  minichromosomes remain trapped inside rings whose three interfaces have been
  chemically cross-linked, even after denaturation (Haering et al, 2008)"].

## Cell-cycle behaviour and localization

- Levels/localization: [PMID:9335334 "Mcd1p was barely detectable in G1 phase,
  at high levels in S phase, and at lower levels in mid-M phase with a punctate
  nuclear localization in S and mid-M phases"]. Unstressed Mcd1-GFP is
  exclusively nuclear [PMID:18321989].
- Chromosome association from late G1 to anaphase, interdependent among
  subunits and dependent on Scc2: [PMID:9990856 "The amount of Scc1-HA6p bound to
  chromosomes in spreads was also strongly reduced in scc3-1 (Fig. 6 C), scc2-1,
  smc1-259 , and smc3-42 mutant"]; [PMID:10882066 "complex is to facilitate the
  loading of cohesin complexes onto chromosomes."]
- Centromeric loading and pericentric spreading: [PMID:31582854 "In
  Saccharomyces cerevisiae CEN sequences are cohesin loading sites where
  complexes initially access chromosomes before spreading towards pericentric
  regions"]; MCD1 overexpression in G1 suffices to enrich Smc1 at CEN/pericentric
  DNA (same paper, Fig. 2c).
- Establishment only in S phase: [PMID:9990856 "Although Cohesin is able to
  bind chromosomes from late G 1 until M phase, it can only establish cohesion
  during S phase"]; at the fork without new loading [PMID:16962805 "Cohesion
  establishment during DNA replication follows without further cohesin
  recruitment"].
- Anaphase: separase cleavage of Scc1 is the trigger [PMID:10403247 "A mutant
  Scc1 is described that is resistant to Esp1-dependent cleavage and which blocks
  both sister-chromatid separation and the dissociation of Scc1 from
  chromosomes."]; Cdc5 phosphorylation near the cleavage sites (S175/S263)
  promotes cleavage [PMID:11371343]; entire Scc1 pool is cleaved and rebuilt each
  cycle [PMID:21139566 "In budding yeast, the entire pool of Scc1 is cleaved at
  the onset of anaphase and cohesin complexes are only re-generated by a burst of
  Scc1 synthesis shortly before S phase."].

## Cohesion and condensation phenotypes (Guacci 1997, full text)

- Cohesion at CEN-proximal and arm loci, both establishment and maintenance:
  [PMID:9335334 "Thus, Mcd1p is required for sister chromatid cohesion at
  CEN-proximal and distal chromosomal regions in yeast cells."]; [PMID:9335334
  "These results demonstrate that Mcd1p is required for maintenance of sister
  chromatid cohesion."]
- Condensation (rDNA FISH amorphous; chr VIII/XVI probes dispersed):
  [PMID:9335334 "the results from FISH using rDNA, chromosome VIII, and
  chromosome XVI probes indicate that the mcd1-1 mutant exhibits defects in
  chromosome condensation as well as sister chromatid cohesion."]. The deep
  research report adds the Orgil 2015 Scc3 separation-of-function alleles
  (Y371A/K372A keep Mcd1 binding and cohesion but lose condensation), supporting
  a genuine cohesin condensation output.

## DNA damage

- Post-replicative DSB repair needs cohesion built in S phase: [PMID:11448778
  "We show here that passage through S phase in the presence of cohesin, and not
  cohesin per se, is essential for efficient double-strand break repair during G2
  in yeast."]
- Replication-born DSB repair by SCE: [PMID:16888651 "scc1-73 and scc2-4
  decreased SCE efficiency similarly as smc3-42"]; role is structural, not
  enzymatic [PMID:16888651 "rather than having an enzymatic role in the HR
  reaction"].
- Damage-induced cohesion is kleisin-specific (Mcd1, not Rec8) and needs Chk1
  phosphorylation of Mcd1 S83: [PMID:18614046 "serine 83 of Mcd1 and the Chk1
  kinase are critical determinants for DSB-induced cohesion"].
- Separase-dependent cohesin removal is part of repair: [PMID:23178808 "a
  separase-resistant allele ... Mcd1 (also known as Scc1), reduces double-strand
  break resection and compromises the efficiency of repair even when loaded
  during DNA damage"].
- Cdc5 docking (Kim et al. 2016, full text): PBD binds the phosphorylated middle
  region (aa 158-275) of Scc1 [PMID:27325700 "As shown in Fig. 3B, the Scc1
  subunit was co-purified with GST-PBD."; "Collectively, our data suggest that
  PBD recognizes phosphorylated forms of the Scc1 M fragment."]; cohesin is the
  major chromosomal Cdc5 platform; scc1-15A pds1D is phleomycin/bleomycin- and
  HO-break-sensitive [PMID:27325700 "This result is consistent with the notion
  that scc1-15A pds1Δ is defective in cellular response to DSB."].

## Stress-induced cleavage (Yang 2008, full text)

- H2O2 -> Esp1 (released from Pds1) cleaves Mcd1; C-terminal fragment (40 aa
  suffices) goes to mitochondria [PMID:18321989 "the GFP spots colocalized with
  MitoFluor, indicating that the Mcd1-GFP is indeed in mitochondria"]; fragment
  overexpression lowers survival, MCD1/mcd1 heterozygotes survive better
  [PMID:18321989 "underexpression of MCD1 (only one copy of functional MCD1 )
  increased cell's survival rate"]; Esp1 depletion blocks the decrease
  [PMID:18321989 "No obvious Mcd1 decrease was observed in the presence of H 2 O
  2 when Esp1 was depleted"].

## Acetylation

- Eco1 acetylates cohesin subunits in vitro; UniProt lists Mcd1 K210 as an
  acetylation site: [PMID:11864574 "In vitro Eco1 acetylates itself and
  components of the cohesin complex but not histones."]. The physiologically
  relevant Eco1 substrate for establishment is Smc3 K112/K113 (deep research;
  PMID:21139566 introduction). No function for Mcd1 acetylation is established.

## Open points

- GO:0007091 for the kleisin (see above).
- Human RAD21's proteome-scale AP-MS rows are legacy protein-binding actions that
  should be migrated to the per-row evidence policy on re-review.
- No yeast cohesin subunit carries GO:0140588 chromatin looping despite Hi-C
  evidence of cohesin-dependent cis loops; the 2024 extrinsic-motor model
  (deep research) makes the mechanism contentious. Not proposed as NEW.
- Function (if any) of Mcd1 K210 acetylation.
