CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the transcription coactivator SAGA complex. SAGA
CC       acts as a general cofactor required for essentially all RNA polymerase
CC       II transcription (PubMed:10864329, PubMed:25216679, PubMed:28918903).
CC       At the promoters, SAGA is required for transcription pre-initiation
CC       complex (PIC) recruitment. It influences RNA polymerase II
CC       transcriptional activity through different activities such as TBP
CC       interaction (via core/TAF module) and promoter selectivity, interaction
CC       with transcription activators (via Tra1/SPT module), and chromatin
CC       modification through histone acetylation (via HAT module) and
CC       deubiquitination (via DUB module) (PubMed:21734642). SAGA
CC       preferentially acetylates histones H3 (to form H3K9ac, H3K14ac, H3K18ac
CC       and H3K23ac) and H2B and deubiquitinates histone H2B (PubMed:10026213).
CC       SAGA interacts with DNA via upstream activating sequences (UASs)
CC       (PubMed:28918903). Also identified in a modified version of SAGA named
CC       SALSA or SLIK (PubMed:12186975, PubMed:12446794). The cleavage of SPT7
CC       and the absence of the SPT8 subunit in SLIK neither drive any major
CC       conformational differences in its structure compared with SAGA, nor
CC       significantly affect HAT, DUB, or DNA-binding activities
CC       (PubMed:33864814). Component of the ADA histone acetyltransferase
CC       complex, which preferentially acetylates nucleosomal histones H3 (to
CC       form H3K14ac and H3K18ac) and H2B (PubMed:10026213). May be involved in
CC       response to DNA damage by genotoxic agents (PubMed:15340070).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10026213, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10864329,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12186975, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12446794,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15340070, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21734642,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25216679, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28918903,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33864814}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the 1.8 MDa SAGA (Spt-Ada-Gcn5 acetyltransferase)
CC       complex, which is composed of 19 subunits TRA1, SPT7, TAF5, NGG1/ADA3,
CC       SGF73, SPT20/ADA5, SPT8, TAF12, TAF6, HFI1/ADA1, UBP8, GCN5, ADA2,
CC       SPT3, SGF29, TAF10, TAF9, SGF11 and SUS1 (PubMed:9674426,
CC       PubMed:9885573). The SAGA complex is composed of 4 modules, namely the
CC       HAT (histone acetyltransferase) module (GCN5, ADA2, NGG1/ADA3 and
CC       SGF29), the DUB (deubiquitinating) module (UBP8, SGF11, SGF73 and
CC       SUS1), the core or TAF (TBP-associated factor) module (TAF5, TAF6,
CC       TAF9, TAF10 and TAF12), and the Tra1 or SPT (Suppressor of Ty) module
CC       (TRA1, HFI1/ADA1, SPT3, SPT7, SPT8 and SPT20/ADA5). The Tra1/SPT module
CC       binds activators, the core module recruits TBP (TATA-binding protein),
CC       the HAT module contains the histone H3 acetyltransferase GCN5, and the
CC       DUB module comprises the histone H2B deubiquitinase UBP8
CC       (PubMed:21734642, PubMed:25216679). Also identified in an altered form
CC       of SAGA, named SALSA (SAGA altered, Spt8 absent) or SLIK (SAGA-like)
CC       complex, which contains a C-terminal truncated form of SPT7 and is
CC       missing SPT8 (PubMed:12186975, PubMed:12446794, PubMed:15647753).
CC       However, it has been shown that the SAGA and SAGA-like SALSA/SLIK
CC       transcriptional coactivators are structurally and biochemically
CC       equivalent (PubMed:33864814). Component of the 0.8 MDa ADA complex, a
CC       HAT complex distinct from SAGA, which at least consists of ADA2,
CC       NGG1/ADA3, AHC1, AHC2, SGF29 and GCN5 (PubMed:10490601,
CC       PubMed:21734642). Identified in an Ada.spt complex with SPT7 and TRA1
CC       (PubMed:9756893). Component of an ADA/GCN5 complex that consists of
CC       HFI1/ADA1, ADA2, NGG1/ADA3, SPT20/ADA5 and GCN5 and probably is a
CC       subcomplex of SAGA (PubMed:7862114, PubMed:9154821).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10490601, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12186975,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12446794, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15647753,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21734642, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25216679,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33864814, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7862114,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9154821, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9674426,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9756893, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9885573}.
