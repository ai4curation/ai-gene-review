CC       prevent ATP hydrolysis. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03168,

CC       the activity in mitochondria, whereas in the cytoplasmic fraction about
CC       10% is retained. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2850178}.
