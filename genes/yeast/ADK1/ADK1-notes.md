# ADK1 (AKY2, YDR226W, P07170) notes

- Major ATP:AMP adenylate kinase, AK2 subfamily [UniProt:P07170 "Reaction=AMP + ATP = 2 ADP"]; [UniProt:P07170 "Belongs to the adenylate kinase family. AK2 subfamily."].
- Dual localisation cytosol (~90%) + mitochondrial IMS (~10%), no cleavable presequence [PMID:2850178 "yeast adenylate kinase (long version, AKY2) simultaneously occurs and is active in mitochondria and cytoplasm of the wild type"]; [PMID:12045196 "Yeast adenylate kinase (Aky2p, Adk1p) occurs simultaneously in cytoplasm and mitochondrial intermembrane space"].
- Null: viable, slow, pet; ~10% residual cytosolic activity from a minor cytoplasmic isozyme [PMID:2850178 "The phenotype of these disruption mutants is pet"]; [PMID:2848829 "the ADK1 gene is needed for normal cell proliferation but is not essential for cell viability"]; ts at 37C [PMID:20659900 "adk1Δ cells were viable at 25 °C but not at 37°C"].
- Catalytic activity required for PHO/ADE regulon control [PMID:18433446 "adenylate kinase catalytic activity is critical for proper regulation of the PHO and ADE pathways"].
- Structures with Ap5A / ATP analogue [PMID:7670369], [PMID:8594191 "two domains, LID and NMPbind, close over the bound substrates, ATP and AMP, respectively"].
- Moonlighting: chromatin/Orc3 binding, pre-RC assembly [PMID:20659900 "Adk1-td protein depletion prevented pre-RC assembly during the M-to-G(1) transition"]; origin association by ChIP only [PMID:20659900 "Adk1p associated with replication origins by ChIP assay"] -> origin DNA binding MF marked over-annotated.
- YeastPathways "adenosine ribonucleotides de novo biosynthesis" mapped to GO "adenosine biosynthetic process" (nucleoside) -- mapping error, removed.
