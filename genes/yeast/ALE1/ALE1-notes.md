# ALE1 (YOR175C / LPT1 / SLC4) notes

UniProt Q08548; module `triacylglycerol_biosynthesis` (MBOAT LPAAT annoton, GO:0003841).

## Evidence journal
- Major LPEAT; MAM enriched [PMID:17652094 "An ale1Delta strain retains only trace amounts of acyl-CoA-dependent LPEAT activity"], [PMID:17652094 "highly enriched in the mitochondria-associated endoplasmic reticulum membrane"].
- Sole LPCAT after lyso-PC import [PMID:17951629 "Ale1p, functioned as the sole lyso-PtdCho acyltransferase in yeast"].
- Highest activity with lysoPC [PMID:26643989 "Ale1 also showed broad acyl specificity, having high activities towards lysoPtdCho, lysoPtdOH, and lysoPtdEtn with highest activity towards lysoPtdCho (Fig. 1d)"].
- Second LPAAT with Slc1 [PMID:17675291 "The simultaneous deletion of SLC1 and SLC4 is lethal"]; [PMID:17890783 "in concert with Slc1, Lpt1 plays a central role in PA biosynthesis"].
- LPAAT is secondary in vitro [PMID:18154737 "it cannot efficiently use lysophosphatidic acid produced by acylation of glycerol-3-phosphate in vitro"].
- Lyso-PAF acetyltransferase [PMID:17890783 "revealed a single mutant strain, YOR175c, with a defect in lysoPAF AT activity"].
- PC deacylation/reacylation pathway [PMID:30514764 "Gpc1 and Ale1 are the major cellular GPC and LPC acyltransferases, respectively"].

## Observations for module
- Module types Ale1 by LPAAT (GO:0003841); biologically its dominant activity is LPCAT/LPEAT (remodeling). LPAAT is a genuine but secondary activity; fine as a TAG-module role but should be flagged as secondary.
