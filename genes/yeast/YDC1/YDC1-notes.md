# YDC1 (YPL087W, Q02896) notes

## Identity and activity
- ACER-family alkaline ceramidase (human ACER3 homolog), ER membrane [UniProt:Q02896].
- Substrate preference [PMID:10900202 "YPC1p preferentially hydrolyzes phytoceramide, whereas the new ceramidase YDC1p hydrolyzes dihydroceramide preferentially and phytoceramide only slightly."].
- Reverse activity minor [PMID:10900202 "In contrast to YPC1p, YDC1p had only minor in vitro reverse activity of catalyzing dihydroceramide formation from a free fatty acid and dihydrosphingosine and no activity with phytosphingosine."].
- In lag1 lac1 background, YPC1/YDC1 account for the residual sphingolipids [PMID:11694577 "Fourth, the remaining amount of normal sphingolipids in lag1 Δ lac1 Δ cells was completely absent in cells that carry the additional deletions of YPC1 and YDC1 ."], but contribution is minor [PMID:11694577 "whereas the CoA-independent ceramide production of Ypc1p and Ydc1p is only minor"].
- ydc1 heat sensitive [PMID:10900202 "the Deltaydc1 deletion mutant but not the Deltaypc1 deletion mutant was sensitive to heat stress"].

## Curation / module notes
- YeastPathways reaction given for YDC1 is 2'-hydroxy-phytoceramide hydrolysis, which matches YPC1's preference better; YDC1 is the dihydroceramidase. Placement of YDC1 in de novo sphingolipid synthesis (RCA) kept non-core: main role is ceramide catabolism.
- RCA cytosol -> MODIFY to ER membrane. Vacuolar membrane only from HTP (PMID:22842922).
