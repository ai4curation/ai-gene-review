# SGD gene description for ALG14 (yeast)

Component of UDP-GlcNAc transferase; required for second step of dolichyl-linked oligosaccharide synthesis; anchors catalytic subunit Alg13p to ER membrane; localizes to the peroxisome; similar to bacterial and human glycosyltransferases; both human homologs ALG13 and ALG14 are required to complement yeast alg14 mutant; mutations of human ALG14 cause congenital myasthenic syndrome
