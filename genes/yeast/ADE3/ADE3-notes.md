# ADE3 (YGR204W, P07245) notes

- This is the cytoplasmic trifunctional C1-THF synthase: "possesses 10-formyl-THF synthetase (EC 6.3.4.3), 5,10-methenyl-THF cyclohydrolase (EC 3.5.4.9), and 5,10-methylene-THF dehydrogenase (EC 1.5.1.5) activities" [PMID:8464869]. MIS1 is the mitochondrial isozyme.
- Its role in purine synthesis is catalytic: "the presence of either the synthetase or dehydrogenase/cyclohydrolase domains of C1-THF synthase is enough to complement the adenine requirement in ade3 strains" [PMID:8464869]. This argues against an earlier structural-scaffold hypothesis.
- Overexpression from multicopy ADE3 raises C1-THF synthase 20-60 fold [PMID:3514599].
- Some ade3 alleles differ only in 10-formyl-THF synthetase levels [PMID:5563870].
- Location: GFP shows cytoplasm plus nucleus [PMID:14562095], and an immunolocalization survey shows cytoplasm [PMID:11914276].
