# SGD gene description for BIT2 (yeast)

Subunit of TORC2, a regulator of plasma membrane (PM) homeostasis; TORC2 also regulates actin cytoskeletal dynamics during polarized growth and cell wall integrity; interacts with Slm1p and Slm2p, homologous PH domain-containing TORC2 substrates; BIT2 has a paralog, BIT61, that arose from the whole genome duplication
