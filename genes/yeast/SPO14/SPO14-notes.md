# SPO14 (YKR031C) notes

UniProt P36126, phospholipase D1 (PLD1), PC-hydrolysing HKD-family PLD with PX and PH domains; PIP2- and Srf1-dependent [UniProt:P36126].

- PC-PLD activity, required for sporulation, not vegetative growth [PMID:8576189 "yeast bearing a disruption in this gene are unable to catalyze the hydrolysis of phosphatidylcholine"; "homozygous delta pld1 diploid cells are unable to sporulate"].
- Sec14-independent secretion and sporulation are separable functions [PMID:11514437 "Spo14 PLD catalytic activity and cellular function can be differentially regulated at the level of phosphatidylcholine hydrolysis"].
- Prospore membrane: localises there [PMID:9693364 "Spo14 localized normally to the developing prospore membrane"]; required for vesicle fusion; PA recruits Spo20 [PMID:16554438 "Spo14p, the major yeast phospholipase D, is necessary for prospore membrane formation"].
- Endosomes in vegetative cells (GFP-PLD/FM4-64) [PMID:10848624 "Comparisons of GFP-PLD and FM 4-64 staining profiles indicated that PLD exhibited predominantly endosomal staining"].
- Pheromone response: mating projection formation [PMID:12702348 "Loss of PLD1 activity was found to impair pheromone-induced changes in cellular morphology that result in formation of mating projections."]; Cdc42 stimulates, PA binds Ste20 [PMID:18036176].
- Fraction in nucleus, associated with RNA pol II (ChIP) [PMID:21954159 "demonstrate that a fraction of these proteins localizes in the nucleus"].
- PX domain binds PI3P (all yeast PX domains) [PMID:11557775].

## Pathway context
- YeastPathways: PC + H2O -> PA + choline (EC 3.1.4.4) in phospholipid degradation: correct assignment.
