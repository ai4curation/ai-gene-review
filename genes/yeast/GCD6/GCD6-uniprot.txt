CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as a catalytic component of the translation initiation
CC       factor 2B (eIF2B) complex, which catalyzes the exchange of GDP for GTP
CC       on eukaryotic initiation factor 2 (eIF2) and is regulated by
CC       phosphorylated eIF2. Its guanine nucleotide exchange factor activity is
CC       repressed when bound to eIF2 complex phosphorylated on the alpha
CC       subunit, thereby limiting the amount of methionyl-initiator methionine
CC       tRNA available to the ribosome and consequently global translation is
CC       repressed (By similarity). It activates the synthesis of GCN4 in yeast
CC       under amino acid starvation conditions by suppressing the inhibitory
CC       effects of multiple AUG codons present in the leader of GCN4 mRNA. It
CC       may promote either repression or activation of GCN4 expression
CC       depending on amino acid availability. GCD6 and GCD7 repress GCN4
CC       expression at the translational level by ensuring that ribosomes which
CC       have translated UORF1 will reinitiate at UORF2, -3, or -4 and thus fail
CC       to reach the GCN4 start site. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P56287,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8506384, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9472020}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the translation initiation factor 2B (eIF2B)
CC       complex which is a heterodecamer of two sets of five different
CC       subunits: alpha, beta, gamma, delta and epsilon. Subunits alpha, beta
CC       and delta comprise a regulatory subcomplex and subunits epsilon and
CC       gamma comprise a catalytic subcomplex (PubMed:10075937,
CC       PubMed:35031321, PubMed:8506384, PubMed:9472020). Within the complex,
CC       the hexameric regulatory complex resides at the center, with the two
CC       heterodimeric catalytic subcomplexes bound on opposite sides (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P56287,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10075937, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35031321,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8506384, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9472020}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm, cytosol
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P56287}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Present with 33800 molecules/cell in log phase SD
CC       medium. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14562106}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the eIF-2B gamma/epsilon subunits family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
