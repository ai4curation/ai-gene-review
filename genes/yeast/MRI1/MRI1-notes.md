# MRI1 (YPR118W, Q06489) notes

- Methylthioribose-1-phosphate isomerase (MtnA), EC 5.3.1.23, eIF2B alpha/beta/delta-like family, homodimer [UniProt:Q06489 "Reaction=5-(methylsulfanyl)-alpha-D-ribose 1-phosphate = 5-"].
- Crystal structure and in vivo requirement [PMID:15215245 "In vivo, Ypr118w protein is required for yeast cells to grow on methylthioadenosine in the absence of methionine, showing that Ypr118w is involved in the methionine salvage pathway."].
- Named MRI1 by Pirkov 2008 [PMID:18625006 "The genes encoding 5'-methylthioribose-1-phosphate isomerase and 5'-methylthioribulose-1-phosphate dehydratase are herein named MRI1 and MDE1, respectively."].
- Cytoplasm + nucleus (Huh 2003). Salvage step 1 of the MTR-1-P branch. All RCA rows correct.
