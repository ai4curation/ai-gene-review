Gene Description

Aspartate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase (ASADH, EC 1.2.1.11) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Hom2 catalyses the second step of the aspartate pathway, the NADPH-dependent reductive dephosphorylation of L-4-aspartyl phosphate to L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde, which is then reduced to homoserine by Hom6. Homoserine is the branch point for threonine (and hence isoleucine) and methionine synthesis, so hom2 mutants require threonine and methionine. Hom2 is a soluble cytosolic homotetramer; GFP data also show a nuclear pool, and rDNA-silencing phenotypes have been reported; rescue of the silencing defect by threonine indicates that the phenotype can reflect metabolic supply.
