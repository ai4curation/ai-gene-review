# GRX1 (YCL035C, UniProt P25373) notes

- Cytosolic/nuclear dithiol (CPYC) glutaredoxin, paralog of GRX2 (64% identity) [PMID:9571241, PMID:18992757].
- "Strains deleted for both GRX1 and GRX2 were viable but lacked heat-stable oxidoreductase activity using beta-hydroxyethylene disulfide as a substrate" [PMID:9571241]; Grx2 provides most of the in vivo activity; "a grx1 mutant was sensitive to oxidative stress induced by the superoxide anion" [PMID:9571241].
- yGrx2 has "a specific activity 15 times higher than that of yGrx1" [PMID:18992757].
- Glutathione peroxidase: glutaredoxins "directly reduce hydroperoxides in a catalytic manner, using reducing power provided by NADPH, GSH, and glutathione reductase" [PMID:11875065]. GST activity with CDNB; "Cys-27, but not Cys-30, is required for both the peroxidase and transferase activities" [PMID:12684511].
- Not a general deglutathionylase: "glutaredoxins (Grx1 or Grx2) did not display deglutathionylase activity"; overexpression of the GRX1/GLR1 system increased glutathionylated proteins ~66% [PMID:20074363].
- Localization: cytoplasm and nucleus (Huh GFP) [UniProt:P25373].

## Module/pathway
- glutathione_thioredoxin_redox_systems: GRX1/GRX2 exemplars for GO:0015038 glutathione disulfide oxidoreductase activity; consistent.
- YeastCyc GLUT-REDOX2-PWY and PWY3O-592 have the "2 glutathione + oxidized glutaredoxin -> GSSG + reduced glutaredoxin" reaction with NO gene attached; GRX1/GRX2 (and GRX3-8) are absent from YeastCyc reaction assignments (it treats glutaredoxin as a substrate, which is the conventional MetaCyc representation of a carrier protein).
