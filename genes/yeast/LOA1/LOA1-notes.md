# LOA1 (YPR139C / VPS66) notes

UniProt Q06508; module `triacylglycerol_biosynthesis` (LD LPAAT annoton, GO:0003841, location lipid droplet).

## Evidence journal
- LPA AT activity in vitro [PMID:22090344 "Loa1p immobilized on the anti-V5 beads was able to form PA from LPA and oleoyl-CoA"].
- Dual ER + LD [PMID:22090344 "Loa1p was localized to the ER and LDs when analyzed by subcellular fractionation, immunohistochemical analyses, and fluorescence microscopy"]; LD proteome [PMID:21820081 "Vps66p and Gtt1p, showed a clear and more or less exclusive localization to LP"].
- TAG reduced in loa1 [PMID:22090344 "Steady-state metabolic labeling of loa1Δ revealed a significant reduction in triacylglycerol content"].
- VPS66 name from CPY missorting screen (PMID:12134085); no mechanism -> over-annotation.

## Curation decisions
- NEW: phosphatidic acid biosynthetic process (GO:0006654), Loa1 catalyses the step.
- Module location LD only; ER is equally supported experimentally.
