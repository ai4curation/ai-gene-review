# LSB6 notes (YJL100W, P42951)

- Type II PI4K [PMID:12523934 "Lsb6p belongs to the distinct Type II class"]; recombinant activity [PMID:12361950 "The heterologous expression of the S. cerevisiae LSB6 gene in Escherichia coli resulted in the expression of a protein that possessed PI 4-kinase activity."]
- Minor in vivo role [PMID:12361950 "Although the lsb6Delta mutant did not exhibit a growth phenotype and failed to exhibit a defect in phosphoinositide synthesis in vivo"]; overexpression partially suppresses stt4 [PMID:12361950]
- PM + vacuole membrane [PMID:12523934 "Lsb6p-green fluorescent protein was found both on vacuolar membranes and on the plasma membrane, suggesting a role in endocytic or exocytic pathways."]
- Kinase-independent role in Las17-driven endosome motility: Chang FS, Han GS, Carman GM, Blumer KJ (2005) J Cell Biol 171:133-142, doi:10.1083/jcb.200501086 (not cached; found via WebSearch, https://rupress.org/jcb/article/171/1/133/51946).
- IBA TGN / Golgi organization come only from Drosophila PI4KII; not supported for Lsb6.
