# QRI1 / UAP1 (YDL103C, P43123) notes

- UDP-GlcNAc pyrophosphorylase (EC 2.7.7.23), UDPGP family; Km 13.5 uM GlcNAc1P, 20.6 uM UTP [UniProt:P43123].
- Essential; human and C. albicans UAP1 complement; recombinant enzyme active; G112/R116/K123 catalytic [PMID:9603950 "A null mutation of the S. cerevisiae UAP1 (ScUAP1) gene was lethal"; "All the recombinant ScUap1p, CaUap1p, and HsUap1p possessed UDP-N-acetylglucosamine pyrophosphorylase activities in vitro"].
- Uses GlcNAc1P; mutase step precedes uridylyl transfer [PMID:9603950 "The yeast Uap1p utilized N-acetylglucosamine-1-phosphate as the substrate"].
- GFP: cytoplasm + nucleus [PMID:14562095]; nucleus kept non-core.
- Generic 'uridylyltransferase activity' IEA kept non-core (true parent).
