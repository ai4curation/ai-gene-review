# FIG4 notes (YNL325C, P42837)

- Mg2+-activated PI(3,5)P2-selective 5-phosphatase [PMID:14528018 "We demonstrate that Fig4 is a magnesium-activated PtdIns(3,5)P2-selective phosphoinositide phosphatase in vitro."]
- Vacuole limiting membrane, via Vac14 [PMID:14528018 "Analysis of a Fig4-GFP fusion protein revealed that the Fig4 phosphatase is localized to the limiting membrane of the vacuole."]
- Genetic: fig4 deletion suppresses vac7 [PMID:11950935 "Deletion of FIG4 in vac7Delta mutant cells suppresses the temperature sensitivity and vacuolar morphology defects, and dramatically restores PtdIns(3,5)P(2) levels."]
- Dual role in synthesis and turnover [PMID:18653468 "Vac14 and Fig4 serve a dual function: they are both implicated in the synthesis and turnover of PtdIns(3,5)P(2)"]
- PAS complex member [PMID:19037259].
- Curation: RCA "PIP biosynthetic process" and cytosol from YeastPathways are superpathway/default artefacts; GO_REF:0000108 PI3P biosynthesis inferred from MF is over-annotation. Nuclear periphery (PMID:25817432) = INQ stress compartment HTP.
