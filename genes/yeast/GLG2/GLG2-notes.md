# GLG2 (YJL137C, P47011) notes

Glycogenin-2, GT8 (EC 2.4.1.186). Redundant with GLG1. Module: glycogen_metabolism_fungal.

- In vitro primers for glycogen synthase [PMID:8524228 "These genes encode self-glucosylating proteins that in vitro can act as primers for the elongation reaction catalyzed by glycogen synthase"]
- "In Glg2p, mutation of both Tyr230 and Tyr232 is necessary to suppress self-glucosylation of purified protein in vitro" [PMID:8900126]
- Still transfers to exogenous acceptor: "The mutant protein is still capable of transferring glucose to an exogeneous acceptor, n-dodecyl beta-D-maltoside" [PMID:8900126]
- Covalently in glycogen: "Glg2p was shown to be associated with carbohydrate in vivo and was released from the high molecular weight glycogen fraction by treatment with alpha-amylase" [PMID:8900126]
- MS: "glucosyl residues were attached to both Tyr232 and Tyr230 within the same peptide" [PMID:15479227]
