CC   -!- FUNCTION: Self-glucosylating initiator of glycogen synthesis. It
CC       catalyzes the formation of a short alpha (1,4)-glucosyl chain
CC       covalently attached via a glucose 1-O-tyrosyl linkage to internal
CC       tyrosine residues and these chains act as primers for the elongation
CC       reaction catalyzed by glycogen synthase (PubMed:15479227,
CC       PubMed:8524228, PubMed:8900126). Capable of transferring glucosyl
CC       residues to unbound acceptors such as free oligoglucans or oligoglucan
CC       derivatives (PubMed:8900126). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15479227,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8524228, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8900126}.
