# HEM2 (YGL040C, P05373) notes

5-aminolevulinate dehydratase (ALAD / porphobilinogen synthase).

- Purified enzyme: homo-octamer, Zn2+-stimulated, Pb2+/Cu2+-sensitive [PMID:2204246 "It is a homo-octamer with an apparent Mr of 275,000"; "maximum activity was obtained in the presence of 1.0 mM-Zn2+"].
- Zinc class of ALADs [PMID:8973546 "those requiring Zn2+ for activity (typified here by the enzymes from Escherichia coli and Saccharomyces cerevisiae, yeast)"]; 1 Zn per monomer [UniProt:P05373].
- hem2 mutants cannot convert ALA to PBG [PMID:2445751 "The mutants are unable to convert delta-aminolevulinic acid to porphobilinogen"]; lack ALAD and need methionine (siroheme) [PMID:323256 "Mutants in hem1, hem2, and hem3 had an additional requirement for methionine"].
- Overproduction -> PBG/porphyrin accumulation, rate-limiting [PMID:14559249].
- Location: cytoplasm (+ nucleus) by GFP [PMID:14562095]. Cytosolic step; RCA cytosol correct here.
