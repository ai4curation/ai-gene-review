# FAS2 (YPL231W, P19097) notes

- Alpha subunit of cytosolic type I FAS: ACP, KR, KS and PPT domains [PMID:18725634 "The 210-kDa α-chain contains a KS domain, a ketoacyl reductase (KR), and a phosphopantetheine transferase domain"]; [PMID:18725634 "the α-chain includes an N-terminal ACP, required for the transfer of intermediates between the different catalytic sites"].
- Cerulenin binds KS active-site Cys1305 [PMID:18725634 "Cerulenin attacks the FAS ketoacyl synthase (KS) domain, forming a covalent bond to the active site cysteine C1305"].
- Self-pantetheinylation by intrinsic PPT domain [PMID:10785388 "the FAS-activating PPTase of yeast represents a distinct catalytic domain of the FAS complex and resides within the C-terminal portion of subunit alpha"].
- KR is NADPH dependent [PMID:18725634 "reduced by an NADPH-dependent ketoacyl-ACP reductase"].
- Complex architecture: six alpha chains form the equatorial wheel [PMID:18725634 "an equatorial wheel-like structure constructed by six α-chains"].
- The ACP domain's carrier role (GO:0000036 acyl carrier activity) is not in GOA for FAS2; not proposed as NEW here (raised as a question instead).
