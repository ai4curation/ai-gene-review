Gene Description

Mitochondrial aspartate aminotransferase (AspAT, EC 2.6.1.1) of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, a PLP-dependent class-I aminotransferase that reversibly interconverts L-aspartate + 2-oxoglutarate and oxaloacetate + L-glutamate in the mitochondrial matrix. Under the tested nitrogen-starvation conditions, Aat1 contributes less newly synthesized aspartate than cytosolic Aat2; this comparison does not establish a universal flux ratio. Aat1 is acetylated in mitochondria and participates in metabolite exchange between mitochondria and cytosol; it is inactivated in frataxin-deficient (yfh1) cells, and its loss causes respiratory deficiency and altered iron homeostasis, consistent with a role in a malate-aspartate type NADH shuttle.
