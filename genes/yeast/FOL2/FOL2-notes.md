# FOL2 (YGR267C, P51601) notes

- GTP cyclohydrolase I, EC 3.5.4.16 [UniProt:P51601].
- The knockout "shows a recessive conditional lethality due to folinic acid auxotrophy, and lacks any detectable specific enzymatic activity" [PMID:8573145].
- Location: cytoplasm by GFP and immunolocalization [PMID:14562095, PMID:11914276]. A nuclear pool is reported by GFP and by APEX2 proximity labelling [PMID:31323700].
- Zinc: Fol2p is listed in the predicted zinc proteome [PMID:30358795].
- BH4: QuickGO (checked 2026-10) shows only FOL2 IBA and a YHL018W IEA for GO:0006729 in S. cerevisiae. No yeast PTPS or sepiapterin reductase is annotated, so the BH4 IBA is marked over-annotated (lineage mismatch).

## Curation decisions
- The core annotations are GO:0003934 and GO:0046654.
- The nucleus rows and zinc binding are kept as non-core.
- The folate cycle and chorismate rows are removed.
- The folic acid biosynthesis RCA rows are changed to GO:0046654.
