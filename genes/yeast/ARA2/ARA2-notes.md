# ARA2 (YMR041C, Q04212) review notes

## Identity
- D-arabinose 1-dehydrogenase (NAD+), EC 1.1.1.116; aldo-keto reductase family, AKR2 subfamily; homologous to plant L-galactose dehydrogenase [UniProt:Q04212].
- No experimental subcellular location in UniProt; soluble AKR without predicted TM segment or targeting signal.

## Function
- NAD+-specific D-arabinose dehydrogenase active at submillimolar D-arabinose [PMID:17097644 "We found an NAD+-specific ARA activity which is operative under submillimolar level of d-arabinose in the extracts of Saccharomyces cerevisiae."].
- Recombinant Ara2p kinetics [PMID:17097644 "Recombinant Ara2p showed NAD+-specific ARA activity with Km=0.78 mM to d-arabinose, which is 200-fold lower than that for the conventional NADP+-specific ARA, Ara1p."].
- Deletion phenotype [PMID:17097644 "Gene disruptant of ARA2 lost entire NAD+-specific ARA activity and the conspicuous increase in intracellular eAsA by exogenous d-arabinose feeding"].
- Main in vivo contributor [PMID:17097644 "It demonstrates that Ara2p, not Ara1p, mainly contributes to the production of eAsA from d-arabinose in S. cerevisiae."]; but ara1 ara2 double mutant still has eAsA, so another route exists.
