# DCI1 (YOR180C; ECI2/EHD2; UniProt Q08558) notes

- Peroxisomal Delta(3,5)-Delta(2,4)-dienoyl-CoA isomerase, EC 5.3.3.21 [PMID:10455114 "demonstrated a specific di-isomerase activity of 6 nmol x min(-1) per mg of protein"].
- Weak intrinsic 3,2-isomerase activity in vitro, but not physiological [PMID:10455114 "we reasoned that yeast di-isomerase does not additionally represent a physiological 3,2-isomerase"]; dci1 grows like wild type on oleate [PMID:10455114 "A strain devoid of Dci1p grew on oleic acid to wild-type levels"].
- Earlier study saw a mild oleate growth defect [PMID:10381339 "deletion of YOR180C from the yeast genome resulted in a mild but significant growth defect on oleic acid"].
- Peroxisomal matrix: GFP and matrix proteomics [PMID:10381339 "Yor180Cp targeted GFP to peroxisomes in a PEX8-dependent manner"]; PTS-receptor-independent targeting [PMID:10639339 "the correct targeting of Eci1p and Dci1p occurs in the absence of the receptors for the type 1 or type 2 peroxisome targeting pathway"].
- Needed for efficient Eci1p import; two-hybrid interaction [PMID:10381339 "a Yor180Cp-Eci1p interaction was detected using the yeast two-hybrid system"].
- Oleate-induced via Oaf1/Pip2 ORE [UniProt:Q08558].

## Pathway-context observations
- YeastPathways attaches DCI1 to the EC 5.3.3.8 (Delta3-Delta2-enoyl-CoA isomerase) step, which is ECI1's step. DCI1's real step is EC 5.3.3.21 (di-isomerase, auxiliary route via Sps19p 2,4-dienoyl-CoA reductase). RCA GO:0004165 reviewed as MODIFY -> GO:0051750.
- YEAST-FAO-PWY RCA "cytosol" is a conversion artefact (REMOVE).
