# ACO1 (YLR304C, P19414) notes

- Mitochondrial aconitase, EC 4.2.1.3, [4Fe-4S] [UniProt:P19414 "Catalyzes the isomerization of citrate to isocitrate via cis-"].
- Cloned by complementing glu1-1; deletion gives glutamate auxotrophy [PMID:1972545 "Disruption of the gene by deletion-insertion led to glutamate auxotrophy."].
- Small cytosolic pool serves glyoxylate shunt [PMID:15975908 "the glyoxylate shunt in the cytosol and the TCA cycle in mitochondria"].
- Minor homocitrate-dehydratase contribution to lysine synthesis; ACO2 is the main enzyme [PMID:23106124 "Aco1p mainly serves for the citric acid cycle with minor contribution to lysine biosynthesis"].
- mtDNA nucleoid component, binds ds/ssDNA, required for mtDNA maintenance independent of catalysis [PMID:15692048 "essential for mtDNA maintenance independent of its catalytic activity"]; [PMID:17698960 "Aconitase binds to both ds- and ssDNA"].
- RIP-chip: binds mitochondrially encoded transcripts [PMID:18959479 "selectively binds transcripts encoded by the mitochondrial genome"].

Curation decisions: Reactome TOM40:TOM70 import reaction gives spurious IMS (REMOVE) and cytosol TAS (over-annotated). YeastPathways glyoxylate-cycle rows for ACO1 are supported (cytosolic pool). Core: aconitate hydratase in TCA cycle (matrix) + glyoxylate cycle (cytosol).
