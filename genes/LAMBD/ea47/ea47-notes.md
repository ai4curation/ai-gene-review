# Bacteriophage lambda gene ea47 (Uncharacterized protein Ea47; UniProt P03752)

## Overview
Gene `ea47` of *Escherichia phage lambda* (`NCBITaxon:2681611`) encodes a 410-amino-acid (47.7 kDa) protein (Ea47) located in the nonessential *b2* region of the phage lambda genome (adjacent to `ea31`, `ea59`, and `int`) [file:LAMBD/ea47/ea47-uniprot.txt]. The gene was originally identified by polypeptide expression mapping of the *b2* region (~47 kDa polypeptide) and is dispensable for plaque formation on standard laboratory *Escherichia coli* K-12 hosts.

## Bioinformatics and Domain Architecture
1. **Tandem Phage_lambda_Ea47 Domains**: According to UniProt and InterPro, Ea47 contains two copies of the `Phage_lambda_Ea47` domain (`Pfam:PF26900`, `InterPro:IPR059638`) [file:LAMBD/ea47/ea47-uniprot.txt].
2. **Protein Interaction Data**: IntAct records 2 binary protein-protein interactions for `P03752` (`DR   IntAct; P03752; 2.`) from systematic bacteriophage lambda yeast two-hybrid interactome screens [file:LAMBD/ea47/ea47-uniprot.txt]. Despite its conservation across lambdoid prophages and phages in the *b2* accessory locus, the specific biochemical activity and cellular target of lambda Ea47 have not yet been defined in the curated literature.
