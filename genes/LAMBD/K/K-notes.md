# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda Gene K (gpK, P03729)

## Summary of Gene Product Function
Gene `K` encodes the tail assembly protein gpK (UniProt: `P03729`, 199 aa, ~23.0 kDa), an essential morphogenetic factor required for assembly of the 15S distal tail tip precursor complex together with `gpJ`, `gpI`, and `gpL` (`PMID:23851014`, `PMID:41237242`). Sequence and domain analyses show that gpK possesses an N-terminal MPN (JAMM/JAB1-like metallopeptidase) domain (residues 1–77) and an overlapping/C-terminal NlpC/P60 cysteine peptidase/cell-wall hydrolase superfamily domain (residues 40–186) (`file:LAMBD/K/K-uniprot.txt`). Notably, while gpK is strictly required during tail morphogenesis in the *Escherichia coli* cytoplasm to form the 15S and 25S tail tip assembly intermediates (`PMID:23851014`), high-resolution cryo-EM of the mature Ur-lambda tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`) resolved `gpM`, `gpL`, `gpI`, `gpJ`, and the C-terminal fragment of cleaved `gpH` (`gpHC`), but found that gpK's location is absent/unknown in the mature tail tip (`PMID:41237242`), indicating that gpK acts either as a transient assembly factor/protease (potentially involved in C-terminal processing of gpH or remodeling of the tail tip) or dissociates before virion maturation.

## Literature & Structural Evidence
- **Requirement in 15S tail tip precursor formation (`PMID:23851014`, `PMID:41237242`)**: Genes `J`, `I`, `L`, and `K` are required to produce the 15S slow-sedimenting tail tip precursor, which is subsequently converted by `gpH`, `gpG`/`gpGT`, and `gpM` into the 25S initiator complex (`PMID:23851014`), even though gpK is not resolved in the mature cryo-EM structure of the tail tip complex [PMID:41237242, "The tail abuts the tail tip complex that contains gpM, gpL, gpI, and gpJ. GpK is also required for tail assembly although its location is unknown."].
- **Peptidase domain architecture (`file:LAMBD/K/K-uniprot.txt`)**: UniProt annotates an N-terminal MPN domain (`FT   DOMAIN          1..77 /note="MPN"`) and an NlpC/P60 peptidase domain (`FT   DOMAIN          40..186 /note="NlpC/P60"`), which drive the electronic annotations for `cysteine-type peptidase activity` (`GO:0008234`), `metallopeptidase activity` (`GO:0008237`), `metal ion binding` (`GO:0046872`), and `proteolysis` (`GO:0006508`).

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. gpK is essential for assembling the 15S tail tip precursor and subsequent functional phage tails (`PMID:23851014`, `PMID:41237242`).
- `GO:0030430` (`host cell cytoplasm`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000044`): **ACCEPT**. gpK functions in the host cell cytoplasm to assemble the 15S tail tip complex and is not resolved in the mature virion tail tip (`PMID:41237242`).
- `GO:0008234` (`cysteine-type peptidase activity`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`), `GO:0008237` (`metallopeptidase activity`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`), `GO:0046872` (`metal ion binding`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`), and `GO:0006508` (`proteolysis`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE**. These annotations reflect the conserved MPN (metallopeptidase) and NlpC/P60 (cysteine peptidase) domains of gpK (`file:LAMBD/K/K-uniprot.txt`), which may catalyze tape measure protein (gpH) maturation cleavage or peptidoglycan hydrolysis, although direct biochemical substrate verification in lambda remains to be established.
- `GO:0008233` (`peptidase activity`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`) and `GO:0016787` (`hydrolase activity`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** to the more specific descendant terms `GO:0008234` (`cysteine-type peptidase activity`) and `GO:0008237` (`metallopeptidase activity`) already present on this gene.
