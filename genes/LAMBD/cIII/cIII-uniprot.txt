CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as a host protease inhibitor that allows the virus to
CC       initiate latency. Following infection, prevents protein cII degradation
CC       by inhibiting host protease FtsH (cII is a factor that initiates the
CC       expression of repressor cI, the major component promoting latency).
CC       Functions as a competitive inhibitor (and thus as alternative
CC       substrate) of host FtsH and thereby prevents binding of cII substrate.
CC       In turn, stabilization of cII transcriptional activator allows protein
CC       cI expression and thus initiation of latency.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17426811, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18721134,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8990286}.
