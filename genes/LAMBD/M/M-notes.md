# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda Gene M (gpM, P03737)

## Summary of Gene Product Function
Gene `M` encodes the distal tail protein gpM (UniProt: `P03737`, 109 aa, ~12.5 kDa), which forms a C6-symmetric homohexameric ring at the proximal boundary of the bacteriophage lambda tail tip complex, directly linking the C3-symmetric tail tip hub (`gpL`, `gpI`, `gpJ`) to the first C6-symmetric hexameric ring of the major tail tube protein (`gpV`). During tail morphogenesis, `gpM` acts together with `gpH` and `gpG`/`gpGT` to convert the 15S tail tip precursor (`gpJ`-`gpI`-`gpL`-`gpK`) into the 25S initiator complex on which `gpV` polymerizes (`PMID:23851014`). In Ur-lambda, the junction between the hexameric `gpV` ring and the hexameric `gpM` ring is encircled by the 12-subunit collar formed by the N-terminal domains of the side tail fiber protein `gpStf` (`PMID:41237242`). During infection initiation, conformational rearrangements transmitted from `gpJ` through the tail tip complex open the lumen through the `gpM` ring for `gpH*` and viral DNA ejection (`PMID:41237242`).

## Literature & Structural Evidence
- **Cryo-EM structure of the Ur-lambda tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`)**: Resolves six copies of gpM forming a hexameric ring at the top of the tail tip complex that abuts the bottom hexameric ring of gpV and is enveloped at the gpV-gpM junction by twelve copies of gpStfN [PMID:41237242, "The overall cryo-EM structure of the free Ur-λ tail tip complex, determined at overall resolution 3.37 Å using C3 symmetry (Fig. 2A), reveals the tail tube protein gpV, distal tail protein gpM, and central fiber protein gpJ (residues 1 to 807)."; "Twelve copies of gpStfN (gpStf-a, -b, etc.) form a collar that envelops the junction where a ring of six copies of gpV abuts a ring of six copies of gpM in the tail tip complex"].
- **Role in 25S initiator complex formation (`PMID:23851014`, `PMID:1003470`, UniProt `P03737`)**: Shows that genes `H`, `G`, and `M` convert the 15S precursor (`gpJ`, `gpI`, `gpL`, `gpK`) into the 25S initiator of tail shaft polymerization [PMID:23851014, "From these studies it was clear that a slow sedimenting (15S) precursor produced by genes J, I, L, and K could be converted by the actions of genes H, G, and M to a faster sedimenting (25S) initiator of tail shaft polymerization"].

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. gpM is required to form the 25S initiator complex and nucleate polymerization of the hexameric gpV tail tube onto the tail tip complex.
- `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. The hexameric gpM ring forms the distal portal of the tail tube lumen at the tail tip interface through which gpH* and the viral DNA pass during infection.
- `GO:0046718` (`symbiont entry into host cell`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`), which is more specific and already present on this gene.
- `GO:0098015` (`virus tail`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0098023` (`virus tail, tip`), as gpM is specifically the distal tail protein at the proximal end of the tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`).
- `GO:0098026` (`virus tail, tube`, `IDA` from `PMID:41237242`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0098023` (`virus tail, tip`). In `PMID:41237242`, gpM is explicitly identified as the "distal tail protein gpM" within the tail tip complex that abuts the tail tube protein gpV [PMID:41237242, "The tail abuts the tail tip complex that contains gpM, gpL, gpI, and gpJ."]. Therefore `GO:0098023` (`virus tail, tip`) is the accurate sub-compartment rather than `GO:0098026` (`virus tail, tube`).
- `GO:0030430` (`host cell cytoplasm`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000044`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE**. Transient biosynthetic localization in the bacterial host cytoplasm prior to virion assembly.
