# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda Gene H (gpH, P03736)

## Summary of Gene Product Function
Gene `H` encodes the tail length tape measure protein gpH (UniProt: `P03736`, 853 aa, ~92.3 kDa), a multifunctional minor tail protein that determines the exact length of the bacteriophage lambda tail tube during morphogenesis and subsequently forms part of the trans-envelope DNA ejection conduit during infection. During tail assembly, gpH is coated by the chaperones gpG and gpGT to remain soluble and bind the 15S tail tip precursor (`gpJ`-`gpI`-`gpL`-`gpK`) together with `gpM`, forming the 25S initiator complex on which major tail protein gpV polymerizes (`PMID:23851014`, `PMID:23911548`). The physical length of gpH acts as a molecular ruler that dictates the number of stacked gpV hexameric discs (`PMID:2952887`, `PMID:6096021`). After tail tube polymerization and before head attachment, gpH undergoes C-terminal proteolytic cleavage inside the tail lumen to yield `gpH*` (N-terminal main shaft segment) and `gpHC` (C-terminal fragment, residues 818–849 resolved bound to `gpI` and `gpL` in the tail tip complex) (`PMID:41237242`). Upon receptor binding and infection initiation, `gpH*` is ejected from the tail tube lumen and assembles into a hexameric trans-envelope channel across the *E. coli* cell envelope for viral DNA translocation (`PMID:41237242`).

## Literature & Structural Evidence
- **Molecular ruler discovery (`PMID:2952887`, `PMID:6096021`)**: Katsura & Kühl (1975) and Katsura (1987) demonstrated using in-frame deletions and small duplications in gene `H` that the length of the lambda tail tube varies in direct linear proportion to the length of the gpH polypeptide (~0.15 nm per amino acid residue, corresponding to an extended alpha-helical conformation) [PMID:2952887, "I have studied the tail of bacteriophage lambda, whose length is determined by the length of the 'ruler protein', the product of gene H. The length of the tail can be decreased or increased by deleting the middle part of gene H or by forming a small duplication there, and the length of the tail is proportional to the size of the protein."].
- **Chaperone interactions with gpG and gpGT (`PMID:23911548`)**: The G domains of both gpG and the frameshift fusion gpGT bind along gpH to chaperone it and recruit gpV via the T domain of gpGT [PMID:23911548, "We show that the \"G\" domains of gpG and gpGT bind to all or parts of tail length tape measure protein gpH and that the \"T\" domain of gpGT binds to major tail shaft subunit gpV, and present a model for how gpG and gpGT chaperone gpH and direct the polymerization of gpV to form a tail of the correct length."].
- **Cryo-EM of cleaved gpH (`gpH*` and `gpHC`) and trans-envelope channel (`PMID:41237242`)**: Shows that the tail tube lumen houses cleaved `gpH*`, while three copies of C-terminal residues 818–849 of `gpHC` are anchored to trimeric `gpI` and `gpL` in the tail tip complex; upon infection, `gpH*` forms a hexameric tube that fits into the in situ trans-envelope channel density for DNA ejection [PMID:41237242, "We resolved three full-length copies of the tip attachment protein, gpL (232 residues), and three of the baseplate hub protein gpI (residues 134 to 222), which are clearly associated with three copies of residues 818 to 849 (from 853) of gpH, or in the mature phage, C-terminal residues of gpHC"; "predicted hexamer models of the first two segments form tube-like structures and also fit well into the in situ trans-envelope channel density, suggesting that the gpH* hexamer is part of the conduit for DNA ejection"].

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, `IDA` from `PMID:23911548`, `IDA` and `IMP` from `PMID:6096021`, `IMP` from `PMID:2952887`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000120`): **ACCEPT**. gpH is essential both for initiating tail shaft polymerization (conversion of the 15S tail tip precursor to the 25S initiator) and for determining tail tube length.
- `GO:0098015` (`virus tail`, `IDA` from `PMID:41237242`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000120`): **ACCEPT**. Cleaved gpH spans both the tail tube lumen (`gpH*`) and the tail tip complex (`gpHC` bound to gpI/gpL), so `GO:0098015` (`virus tail`) is the appropriate cellular component term encompassing the entire tail.
- `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. `gpH*` exits the tail lumen upon LamB engagement and forms part of the trans-envelope channel for viral DNA ejection.
- `GO:0046718` (`symbiont entry into host cell`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`), which is more specific and already annotated to this gene.
