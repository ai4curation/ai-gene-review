# Bacteriophage lambda gene ninE (Uncharacterized ninR-region protein NinE / Nin60; UniProt P03768)

## Overview
Gene `ninE` of *Escherichia phage lambda* (`NCBITaxon:2681611`) encodes a 60-amino-acid (7.4 kDa), highly basic early protein (NinE, also called Nin60) located in the `ninR` region of the rightward `pR` operon between `ninD` and `ninF` (`PMID:6219042`, [file:LAMBD/ninE/ninE-uniprot.txt]). As shown by Kröger and Hobom (`PMID:6219042`), `ninE` is part of the chain of overlapping, translationally coupled reading frames in the nonessential `P-Q` (`ninR`) interval.
