# Bacteriophage lambda gene Rz (Inner membrane spanin subunit / i-spanin; UniProt P00726)

## Overview
Gene `Rz` of *Escherichia phage lambda* (`NCBITaxon:2681611`) encodes the 153-amino-acid (17.2 kDa) inner membrane spanin subunit (i-spanin), which forms a periplasm-spanning heterotetrameric complex with the outer membrane lipoprotein Rz1 (o-spanin, encoded in the +1 reading frame embedded within `Rz`) [PMID:18713319, PMID:20734329, PMID:23387988, PMID:28040784]. Together, Rz and Rz1 execute the third and final step of bacteriophage lambda host lysis: disruption of the *Escherichia coli* outer membrane via inner-outer membrane fusion after the holin (`S`) and endolysin (`R`) have permeabilized the inner membrane and degraded the peptidoglycan sacculus [PMID:22904283, PMID:25870259].

## Structure, Disulfide Homodimerization, and Membrane Fusion Mechanism
1. **Inner Membrane Anchoring and Periplasmic Coiled-Coil Architecture**: Rz is a type II integral inner membrane protein (N-in, C-out) anchored by an N-terminal transmembrane domain (residues 5–25), followed by a 128-residue periplasmic domain consisting of two predicted alpha-helical coiled-coil segments (CC1 and CC2) separated by a flexible hinge linker [PMID:18713319, PMID:20734329, PMID:28040784].
2. **Intermolecular Disulfide Bonds and Complex Assembly**: Rz forms a covalent homodimer linked by DsbA/DsbC-catalyzed intermolecular disulfide bonds at Cys-99 and Cys-152 [PMID:23387988]. Its C-terminus interacts directly with the C-terminal region of the Rz1 homodimer in the periplasm, forming a rod-like heterotetrameric Rz2:Rz1_2 complex that spans the periplasm through lacunae of the intact peptidoglycan meshwork [PMID:18713319, PMID:20734329]. Complex formation induces a dramatic disorder-to-alpha-helical conformational transition and protects Rz from endogenous periplasmic proteolysis [PMID:20734329].
3. **Essential Role in Outer Membrane Disruption via Membrane Fusion**: Whereas earlier work assumed `Rz` and `Rz1` were auxiliary lysis factors required only in the presence of millimolar divalent cations (which stabilize the outer membrane), Berry et al. (`PMID:22904283`) proved using video microscopy in shaking cultures that the spanin complex is unconditionally essential for lambda host cell lysis: in the absence of `Rz` or `Rz1`, holin-endolysin action merely converts rod-shaped *E. coli* cells into fragile spherical cells bounded by an intact outer membrane. Rajaure et al. (`PMID:25870259`) demonstrated using two-color fluorescence and spheroplast fusion assays that once endolysin `R` destroys the peptidoglycan cross-links that cage the Rz-Rz1 complexes, the spanin complexes oligomerize and undergo a coiled-coil hairpin collapse (analogous to viral class I membrane fusion proteins), fusing the inner and outer membranes to rupture the host envelope.

## GO Annotation Review Summary
- **Accepted Core Terms**:
  - `GO:0090680` (`viral release via disruption of host outer membrane`, IMP `PMID:22904283`): Core biological process of the Rz-Rz1 spanin complex.
  - `GO:0061025` (`membrane fusion`, IDA `PMID:25870259`): Direct physical mechanism by which Rz-Rz1 disrupts the host outer membrane (fusion of the inner and outer membranes).
  - `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120`): Core viral exit pathway.
  - `GO:0020002` (`host cell plasma membrane`, EXP `PMID:18713319`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120`): Subcellular localization of the i-spanin subunit Rz in the *E. coli* inner membrane.

  - `GO:0031640` (`killing of cells of another organism`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043` from `UniProtKB-KW:KW-0204`): Replaced (`MODIFY`) with `GO:0090680` (`viral release via disruption of host outer membrane`) and `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`).
