# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda Gene J (gpJ, P03749)

## Summary of Gene Product Function
Gene `J` encodes the host specificity protein / central tail fiber protein gpJ (UniProt: `P03749`, 1132 aa, ~124.4 kDa), a large trimeric structural protein at the distal tip of the bacteriophage lambda tail that serves both as the nucleus for distal tail tip assembly during morphogenesis and as the primary entry receptor-binding protein during infection (`PMID:10629200`, `PMID:16489764`, `PMID:41237242`). During tail morphogenesis in the *Escherichia coli* cytoplasm, three copies of gpJ assemble with trimeric `gpI`, trimeric `gpL`, and `gpK` to form the 15S tail tip precursor, which is converted by `gpH`, `gpG`/`gpGT`, and `gpM` into the 25S initiator of `gpV` tail tube polymerization (`PMID:23851014`, `PMID:41237242`). In the mature virion, the N-terminal domains of the gpJ trimer (residues 1–807) form the central conical hub and shaft of the tail tip complex, while the C-terminal receptor-binding domains (including the last 249 residues, 884–1132) project distally (`PMID:10629200`, `PMID:41237242`). During infection, each monomer of the trimeric gpJ binds one monomer of the trimeric outer membrane maltoporin LamB on *E. coli*, locking the tail tip onto the cell surface and triggering a conformational cascade through the tail tip complex that opens the tail channel for `gpH*` extrusion and viral DNA ejection (`PMID:10629200`, `PMID:16489764`, `PMID:41237242`).

## Literature & Structural Evidence
- **Cryo-EM structure of trimeric gpJ in the tail tip complex and bound to LamB (`PMID:41237242`)**: Resolves the trimeric central fiber protein gpJ (residues 1–807 in the free tail tip complex at 3.37-Å resolution, and the C-terminal receptor-binding region bound to trimeric maltoporin LamB during infection initiation), showing how 3:3 binding of gpJ to LamB induces conformational opening of the tail tip for `gpH*` and DNA ejection [PMID:41237242, "The tail abuts the tail tip complex that contains gpM, gpL, gpI, and gpJ. GpK is also required for tail assembly although its location is unknown. During infection, each monomer of the trimeric gpJ binds one monomer of the trimeric maltoporin"; "The overall cryo-EM structure of the free Ur-λ tail tip complex, determined at overall resolution 3.37 Å using C3 symmetry (Fig. 2A), reveals the tail tube protein gpV, distal tail protein gpM, and central fiber protein gpJ (residues 1 to 807)."].
- **C-terminal receptor-binding domain of gpJ binds LamB (`PMID:10629200`, `PMID:16489764`)**: Wang et al. (2000) and Berkane et al. (2006) demonstrated using purified fusion proteins, liposome/planar lipid bilayer binding, and electron microscopy that the C-terminal 249 residues of gpJ bind directly to trimeric LamB in vitro and on intact *E. coli* cells, blocking phage adsorption [PMID:10629200, "Solid-phase binding assays demonstrated that a purified fusion protein comprising only the last 249 residues of J could bind to LamB trimers and inhibited recognition by anti-LamB antibodies. Electron microscopy further demonstrated that the fusion protein could also bind to LamB at the surface of intact cells. This interaction prevented lambda adsorption"; PMID:16489764, "The cell surface receptor for bacteriophage Lambda is LamB (maltoporin). Responsible for phage binding to LamB is the C-terminal part, gpJ, of phage tail protein J."].

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- `GO:0046789` (`host cell surface receptor binding`, `IDA` from `PMID:41237242`): **ACCEPT**. Directly supported by cryo-EM of trimeric gpJ bound to trimeric host outer membrane maltoporin LamB (`PMID:41237242`) and biochemical binding assays (`PMID:10629200`, `PMID:16489764`).
- `GO:0098670` (`entry receptor-mediated virion attachment to host cell`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. LamB is the essential entry receptor whose engagement by gpJ triggers irreversible attachment and DNA ejection (`PMID:10629200`, `PMID:16489764`, `PMID:41237242`).
- `GO:0019062` (`virion attachment to host cell`, `IDA` from `PMID:16489764`, `IDA` from `PMID:41237242`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`) and `GO:0046813` (`receptor-mediated virion attachment to host cell`, `IMP` from `PMID:10629200`): **MODIFY** all four to `GO:0098670` (`entry receptor-mediated virion attachment to host cell`), the more specific descendant term already present on this gene.
- `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. Binding of gpJ to LamB directly triggers opening of the tail tip plug and formation of the trans-envelope DNA ejection conduit (`PMID:41237242`).
- `GO:0046718` (`symbiont entry into host cell`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0098670` (`entry receptor-mediated virion attachment to host cell`) and `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`), both of which are more specific descendants already present on this gene.
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. Three copies of gpJ nucleate assembly of the 15S tail tip precursor (`gpJ`-`gpI`-`gpL`-`gpK`) (`PMID:23851014`, `file:LAMBD/J/J-uniprot.txt`).
- `GO:0098015` (`virus tail`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0098023` (`virus tail, tip`), as gpJ forms the central fiber and distal hub of the tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`).
- `GO:0030430` (`host cell cytoplasm`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000044`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE**. Transient biosynthetic localization in the bacterial host cytoplasm prior to virion maturation.
