# Bacteriophage lambda gene ninD (Uncharacterized ninR-region protein NinD; UniProt P03767)

## Overview
Gene `ninD` of *Escherichia phage lambda* (`NCBITaxon:2681611`) encodes a 57-amino-acid (6.8 kDa) protein (NinD) located in the `ninR` region of the rightward `pR` early operon between `ren`/`ninC` (`orf290`) and `ninE` (`PMID:6219042`, [file:LAMBD/ninD/ninD-uniprot.txt]). In the tightly packed `ninR` gene cluster (`ren-ninB-ninC-ninD-ninE-ninF-ninG-ninH-ninI`), adjacent reading frames overlap at their start/stop junctions (`PMID:6219042`). The entire `nin5` deletion region (spanning `ninB` through `ninI`) removes the `tR2` Rho-dependent terminator and renders phage lambda `N`-independent (`nin`), showing that `ninD` is dispensable for plaque formation under standard laboratory conditions.
