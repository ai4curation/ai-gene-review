CC   -!- FUNCTION: The small subunit is responsible for the binding to multiple
CC       recognition elements within the packaging initiation site cos
CC       (PubMed:15755448, PubMed:16618107, PubMed:2989542). The terminase lies
CC       at a unique vertex of the procapsid and is composed of two subunits, a
CC       small terminase subunit involved in viral DNA recognition (binding to
CC       packaging sequence cos), and a large terminase subunit possessing
CC       endonucleolytic and ATPase activities (DNA maturation and packaging)
CC       (Probable). The terminase binds, cooperatively with the host factor
CC       IHFA/IHFB, to the cos site at the junction of adjacent viral genomes in
CC       the concatemeric DNA (PubMed:16618107). The endonuclease activity of
CC       the large subunit cleave the viral DNA generating 5'overhangs of 12 bp
CC       in length (Probable). The terminase remains bound to the left end of
CC       the genome to be packaged, forming a stable DNA-terminase complex
CC       (Probable). In a reaction facilitated by the viral assembly catalyst
CC       gpFI, the DNA-terminase complex binds to the portal of the procapsid
CC       and the terminase packages the viral DNA into the procapsid until the
CC       next cos site on the concatemer reaches the complex ('unit length'
CC       packaging) (Probable). The downstream cos site is then cut generating
CC       the mature right end of the genome, the heterotrimer undocks from the
CC       DNA-filled head and remains bound to the left end of concatemer's next
CC       genome (Probable). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15755448,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16618107, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2989542, ECO:0000305}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:12049735). Heterotrimer of two small and one
CC       large terminase subunits (PubMed:15755448). The catalytically competent
CC       terminase is composed of a tetramer of heterotrimers (PubMed:16618107,
CC       PubMed:30541105). The tetramer forms a ring structure large enough to
CC       encircle duplex DNA (PubMed:30541105). Host IHFA/IHFB induces bending
CC       of viral DNA to facilitate the assembly of the terminase tetramer of
CC       heterotrimers (PubMed:16618107). Interacts (via C-terminus) with the
CC       terminase large subunit (via N-terminus) (PubMed:2969839).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12049735, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15755448,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16618107, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2969839,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30541105}.

CC       {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The N-terminus contains a winged helix-turn-helix (wHTH) that
CC       binds the viral DNA (Probable) (PubMed:12049735). In the homodimer, the
CC       wHTH motifs are positioned on opposite sites of loop and probably bind
CC       to separate R elements in the cos site (PubMed:12049735). The N-
CC       terminus also contains an ATPase site that regulates DNA binding
CC       activity (PubMed:16008350, PubMed:2965248, PubMed:9705918). The central
CC       part contains a region for the self-interaction (PubMed:10571997). the
CC       C-terminus contains region of interaction with the terminase large
CC       subunit (Probable) (PubMed:10571997). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10571997,
