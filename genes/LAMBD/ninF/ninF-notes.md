# Bacteriophage lambda gene ninF (Uncharacterized ninR-region protein NinF; UniProt P03769)

## Overview
Gene `ninF` of *Escherichia phage lambda* (`NCBITaxon:2681611`) encodes a 56-amino-acid (6.4 kDa) early protein (NinF) located in the `ninR` region of the rightward `pR` operon between `ninE` and `ninG` (`rap`) (`PMID:6219042`, [file:LAMBD/ninF/ninF-uniprot.txt]). Like `ninD`, `ninE`, and `ninH`, `ninF` is part of the chain of interlinked, overlapping genes in the nonessential `P-Q` interval (`PMID:6219042`).

## Bioinformatics and Interactome Data
1. **Conserved `NinF` Family**: NinF is the founding member of the conserved bacteriophage `NinF` domain family (`Pfam:PF05810`, `InterPro:IPR008712`, covering residues 1–56) [file:LAMBD/ninF/ninF-uniprot.txt].
2. **Protein-Protein Interactions**: IntAct records 3 binary protein-protein interactions for `P03769` (`DR   IntAct; P03769; 3.`) from systematic phage-host yeast two-hybrid screens [file:LAMBD/ninF/ninF-uniprot.txt]. Its specific molecular function and cellular target during lambda infection remain experimentally uncharacterized.
