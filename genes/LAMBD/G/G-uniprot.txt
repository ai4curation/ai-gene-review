CC   -!- FUNCTION: Promotes tail assembly by creating a scaffold for the tail
CC       tube proteins. Tail assembly proteins G and GT probably wrap the linear
CC       tape measure protein to create a tail assembly scaffold. This allows
CC       the polymerization of the tail tube protein, during which G and GT are
CC       released, therefore they are absent in the mature virion. The tail
CC       assembly protein GT is produced by a rare -1 ribosomal frameshift. The
CC       ratio of translated G/GT is about 20, and this ratio is important for
CC       proper tail assembly. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_04134,
