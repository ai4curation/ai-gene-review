# Bacteriophage lambda gene ninH (Uncharacterized ninR-region protein NinH; UniProt P03771)

## Overview
Gene `ninH` of *Escherichia phage lambda* (`NCBITaxon:2681611`) encodes a 68-amino-acid (7.6 kDa) early protein (NinH) located in the `ninR` region of the rightward `pR` operon between `ninG` (`rap`) and `ninI` (encoding the serine/threonine protein phosphatase lambda-PP) (`PMID:6219042`, [file:LAMBD/ninH/ninH-uniprot.txt]). Along with the rest of the `ninR` gene cluster, `ninH` is deleted in `lambda nin5` mutants and is nonessential for lytic growth under standard laboratory conditions (`PMID:6219042`).

## Bioinformatics and Domain Architecture
1. **Conserved `Phage_NinH` Family**: NinH is the founding member of the `Phage_NinH` domain family (`Pfam:PF06322`, `InterPro:IPR010454`, spanning residues 1–65) conserved across lambdoid bacteriophages [file:LAMBD/ninH/ninH-uniprot.txt].
