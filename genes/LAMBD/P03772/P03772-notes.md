# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda `P03772` (PP_LAMBD / ninI / ORF221)

## Summary of Gene Product
Bacteriophage lambda serine/threonine-protein phosphatase (`P03772`, `PP_LAMBD`, encoded by `ninI` / `ORF221` in the `ninR` region, 221 AA, EC 3.1.3.16, EC 3.1.3.48) is the archetypal viral calcineurin-like metallophosphoesterase (PPP family phosphoprotein phosphatase, `InterPro:IPR004843`, `InterPro:IPR006186`) [file:LAMBD/P03772/P03772-uniprot.txt; PMID:11112522]. Lambda phosphatase catalyzes the `Mn2+`-dependent hydrolysis of phosphate monoesters from phosphorylated serine, threonine, and tyrosine (as well as histidine) residues in protein substrates [file:LAMBD/P03772/P03772-uniprot.txt; PMID:11112522].

## Structural and Mechanistic Architecture
- **Calcineurin-like fold with dinuclear Mn(II) active site (`PDB:1G5B`, `PMID:11112522`)**: The 2.15-A crystal structure of lambda PP (`PMID:11112522`) reveals a core beta-alpha-beta-alpha-beta scaffold conserved with eukaryotic PP1, PP2A, and calcineurin (PP2B). Each subunit binds two catalytic `Mn2+` ions (`Asp20`, `His22`, `Asp49`, `Asn75`, `His76`, `His139`, `His186`) in a narrow surface groove, where a metal-bridging hydroxide ion performs direct inline nucleophilic attack on the phosphorus atom of phosphoester substrates, assisted by `His76` as general acid [file:LAMBD/P03772/P03772-uniprot.txt; PMID:11112522].

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- **`GO:0004722` (`protein serine/threonine phosphatase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120` via `EC:3.1.3.16`, `RHEA:20629`, `RHEA:47004`)**: `ACCEPT`. Directly supported by biochemical and crystallographic characterization of lambda PP (`PMID:11112522`) and the UniProt catalytic activity record [file:LAMBD/P03772/P03772-uniprot.txt; PMID:11112522].
- **`GO:0046872` (`metal ion binding`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)**: `MODIFY` -> `GO:0030145` (`manganese ion binding`). Lambda phosphatase specifically binds a dinuclear `Mn2+` center (2 `Mn2+` ions per subunit, `ChEBI:29035`) in its active site [file:LAMBD/P03772/P03772-uniprot.txt; PMID:11112522].
- **`GO:0004721` (`phosphoprotein phosphatase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)** and **`GO:0016787` (`hydrolase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120`)**: `TOO_GENERAL`. Both are broader parent terms already entailed by `GO:0004722` (`protein serine/threonine phosphatase activity`).
- **`GO:0005515` (`protein binding`, IPI `PMID:17174311` with `UniProtKB:Q64702` [mouse Plk4/Sak])**: `REMOVE`. In `PMID:17174311`, commercial recombinant lambda protein phosphatase was used solely as an *in vitro* biochemical reagent to dephosphorylate purified murine polo-like kinase 4 (Plk4/Sak) prior to peptide array kinase assays. This artificial heterologous *in vitro* enzyme-reagent treatment does not represent a physiological biological interaction of bacteriophage lambda phosphatase.
