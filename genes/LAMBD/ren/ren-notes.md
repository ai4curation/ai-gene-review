# ren (P03761) - Curation Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `ren`
- **UniProt ID**: `P03761` (`REN_LAMBD`)
- **Protein name**: Protein ren (Rex exclusion escape protein, 96 AA)
- **Organism**: Escherichia phage lambda (`NCBITaxon:2681611`)

## Function & Biological Context
- **Escape factor against RexAB-mediated superinfection exclusion**: The `ren` gene (96 AA) is located immediately downstream of the replication genes `O` and `P` in the rightward (`PR`) early lytic operon of bacteriophage lambda (`PMID:6221115`).
- **Prevents self-exclusion by the prophage RexAB system**: Lambda `ren` mutants are unable to grow lytically in cells carrying a lambda prophage (or expressing the `rexA`-`rexB` genes) because lytic lambda replication triggers the RexAB abortive infection system unless Ren is present; overexpression of RexB suppresses the exclusion of both T4 `rII` mutants and lambda `ren` mutants [PMID:1372278, "We have determined that overexpression of RexB in lambda lysogens prevents the exclusion of both T4 rII mutants and lambda ren mutants."]. Thus, Ren functions during lytic infection (and prophage induction) to prevent RexAB activation and allow productive phage replication.
