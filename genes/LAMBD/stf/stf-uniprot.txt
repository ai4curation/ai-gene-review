CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms tail fibers that play a role in primary attachment of
CC       virion to host receptors. Assembles with Tail fiber assembly protein to
CC       form fibers attached to virion tail tip. Fibers are about 35nm long and
CC       have a thin, uniform diameter. There are presumaby six fibers per
CC       virion. They bind in a reversible manner to host molecules, attaching
CC       transiently the virion to the host until tail tip interacts with LamB
CC       and injects viral DNA into the cell.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with Tail fiber assembly protein. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the tail fiber family. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- CAUTION: The current laboratory strain of bacteriophage lambda are
CC       descendants of a recombinant, lambdaPaPa derived from cross between
CC       strains lambda Pasadena (Caltech) and Paris (Institut Pasteur). These
CC       stains carry a frameshift mutation relative to lambda originally
CC       derived from the original K12 E-Coli strain, called Ur-lambda. The Ur-
CC       lambda virions have thin, jointed tail fibers (side tail fibers) that
CC       are absent from lambda wild-type. Relative to lambda PaPa, Ur-lambda
CC       has expanded receptor specificity and adsorbs to E.coli cells more
CC       rapidly. {ECO:0000305}.
