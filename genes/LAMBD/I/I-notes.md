# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda Gene I (gpI, P03730)

## Summary of Gene Product Function
Gene `I` encodes the minor tail protein / baseplate hub protein gpI (UniProt: `P03730`, 223 aa, ~23.2 kDa), an essential structural component of the bacteriophage lambda tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`). During tail morphogenesis, gpI associates with the central tail fiber trimer (`gpJ`), the `[4Fe-4S]` tip attachment protein (`gpL`), and `gpK` to form the 15S tail tip precursor complex (`PMID:23851014`). In the 3.37-Å cryo-EM reconstruction of the mature Ur-lambda tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`), three copies of the C-terminal hub domain of gpI (residues 134–222) form a C3-symmetric trimeric ring interleaved with three copies of `gpL` and three copies of `gpJ`, while directly binding three copies of the C-terminal cleaved fragment of the tape measure protein (`gpHC`, residues 818–849) inside the tail tip lumen. Upon LamB receptor engagement by `gpJ`, conformational changes propagate through the `gpI`-`gpL` hub to release the `gpHC` plug and open the channel for `gpH*` and viral DNA ejection (`PMID:41237242`).

## Literature & Structural Evidence
- **Cryo-EM resolution of trimeric gpI in the tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`)**: Directly resolves three copies of gpI (residues 134 to 222) within the C3-symmetric tail tip complex (`gpM`, `gpL`, `gpI`, `gpJ`), showing that gpI forms the baseplate hub associated with `gpL` and three copies of C-terminal `gpHC` (residues 818–849) [PMID:41237242, "The tail abuts the tail tip complex that contains gpM, gpL, gpI, and gpJ. GpK is also required for tail assembly although its location is unknown."; "We resolved three full-length copies of the tip attachment protein, gpL (232 residues), and three of the baseplate hub protein gpI (residues 134 to 222), which are clearly associated with three copies of residues 818 to 849 (from 853) of gpH, or in the mature phage, C-terminal residues of gpHC"].
- **Requirement for 15S tail tip precursor assembly (`PMID:23851014`, UniProt `P03730`)**: Demonstrates that genes `J`, `I`, `L`, and `K` are required to assemble the 15S tail tip precursor complex that initiates tail morphogenesis (`PMID:23851014`, `file:LAMBD/I/I-uniprot.txt`).

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. gpI is an essential structural subunit of the 15S and 25S tail tip complexes that nucleate bacteriophage lambda tail assembly.
- `GO:0030430` (`host cell cytoplasm`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000044`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE**. Newly synthesized gpI assembles into the 15S tail tip precursor in the *E. coli* cytoplasm before becoming a permanent structural component of the mature virion tail tip (`GO:0098023`).
