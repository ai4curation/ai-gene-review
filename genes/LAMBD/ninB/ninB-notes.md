# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda `ninB` (P03765)

## Summary of Gene Product
Bacteriophage lambda recombination protein NinB (`ninB`, also known as `orf` or `ORF146`, UniProt `P03765`, 146 AA, `InterPro:IPR008711` `Recombinase_NinB`) is a homodimeric functional analog of the *Escherichia coli* RecF, RecO, and RecR recombination mediator complex (`RecFOR`) encoded within the `ninR` region between genes `P` and `Q` [PMID:16076958, "Phage lambda encodes an additional recombinase, Orf, which participates in the early stages of recombination by supplying a function equivalent to the Escherichia coli RecFOR complex."; file:LAMBD/ninB/ninB-uniprot.txt]. During phage recombination via the host RecBC-independent (`RecF`) or phage Red pathways, NinB (Orf) functions as a recombination mediator that binds single-stranded DNA (`GO:0003697`) and interacts with *E. coli* single-stranded DNA-binding protein (`SSB`), facilitating the loading of recombinases onto SSB-coated single-stranded DNA gaps and overhangs (`GO:0006310`, `DNA recombination`) [PMID:16076958, "Orf binds DNA, favoring single-stranded over duplex and with no obvious preference for gapped, 3'-tailed, or 5'-tailed substrates. An interaction between Orf and ssDNA-binding protein was indicated by far Western analysis."].

## Structural and Mechanistic Architecture
- **Asymmetric homodimer with central ssDNA-binding channel (`PDB:1PC6`)**: The 2.51-A X-ray crystal structure of lambda NinB (`PDB:1PC6`, residues 1–146) [file:LAMBD/ninB/ninB-uniprot.txt] reveals a crescent-shaped homodimer (`InterPro:IPR008711`) with a positively charged central groove that binds single-stranded DNA preferentially over double-stranded DNA, flanked by C-terminal alpha-helices involved in host SSB interaction [PMID:16076958].
