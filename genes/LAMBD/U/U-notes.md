# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda Gene U (gpU, P03732)

## Summary of Gene Product Function
Gene `U` encodes the tail terminator protein gpU (UniProt: `P03732`, 131 aa, ~14.6 kDa), which forms a homohexameric ring at the proximal end of the bacteriophage lambda tail tube. Once the major tail tube protein gpV has polymerized to the full 31–32 hexameric ring length dictated by the tape measure protein (gpH), the gpU hexamer binds to the top of the terminal gpV ring and the N-terminal region of gpH. This caps the tail tube, prevents aberrant further polymerization of gpV into polytail structures, and provides the docking surface for the tail completion protein gpZ and the head-tail joining protein gpFII [PMID:41237242; file:LAMBD/U/U-uniprot.txt].

## Literature & Structural Evidence
- **Cryo-EM structure of the portal-neck complex (`PMID:41237242`)**: Resolves the C6-symmetric `gpFII`-`gpU` complex at 3.18-Å resolution within the portal-neck vertex (`gpB`, `gpW`, `gpFII`, `gpU`, `gpZ`), showing that the hexameric gpU ring terminates the C6 gpV tail tube and interfaces directly with six copies of gpFII [PMID:41237242, "The portal-neck complex, including gpB-gpW with C12 symmetry, was resolved at 3.05-Å resolution and gpFII-gpU with C6 symmetry at 3.18-Å resolution, yielding a structure essentially identical to that previously described"].
- **Biochemical characterization of tail termination (`PMID:835226`, UniProt `P03732`)**: Katsura & Tsugita (1977) purified and characterized the major tail protein (gpV) and terminator protein (gpU), showing that gpU caps the polymerizing tail at its predetermined length and forms the interaction surface for gpZ [file:LAMBD/U/U-uniprot.txt, "CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays an essential role in tail assembly by capping the\nCC       rapidly polymerizing tail once it has reached its requisite length and\nCC       serving as the interaction surface for the completion protein.\nCC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2150582}."].

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. Termination of tail tube polymerization at the correct length is a core step of viral tail assembly.
- `GO:0098015` (`virus tail`, `IDA` from `PMID:835226` and `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. gpU is a structural hexameric ring at the proximal end of the virus tail.
- `GO:0046718` (`symbiont entry into host cell`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`), which is already present on this gene and more specifically captures the phage tail-mediated genome delivery mechanism.
- `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE**. As part of the continuous portal-neck-tail conduit through which viral DNA transits during infection, gpU contributes structurally to the genome ejection conduit, though its primary active role is capping the tail during assembly.
- `GO:0030430` (`host cell cytoplasm`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000044`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE**. Transient biosynthetic localization in the host cytoplasm prior to assembly into the virion.
