# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda `bet` (P03698)

## Summary of Gene Product
Bacteriophage lambda recombination protein Beta (`bet`, also known as `redB` or `Red beta`, UniProt `P03698`, 261 AA) is an ATP-independent single-stranded DNA-binding and annealing protein (synaptase / recombinase of the Redbeta/RecT family, `InterPro:IPR010183`) that forms the Red homologous recombination system together with lambda exonuclease (`exo`) [file:LAMBD/bet/bet-uniprot.txt]. Beta binds the 3'-single-stranded DNA overhang generated by lambda Exo, protects it from nuclease degradation, and catalyzes the renaturation/annealing of complementary single-stranded DNA strands during double-strand break repair, single-strand annealing, and late rolling-circle lambda DNA replication [file:LAMBD/bet/bet-uniprot.txt].

## Structural and Mechanistic Architecture
- **Oligomeric ring and helical filament assembly**: In the absence of DNA or on short ssDNA, Beta forms polymorphic oligomeric rings (~11–12 subunits), and upon annealing complementary ssDNA strands it transitions into a stable left-handed helical nucleoprotein duplex filament (`PDB:5VY9`, `PDB:7UJL`) that clamps the nascent heteroduplex DNA.
- **Direct coupling to lambda Exo (`PDB:6M9K`)**: The C-terminal domain of Beta binds directly to the homotrimeric lambda Exo toroid, positioning Beta at the exit of the Exo channel so that the nascent 3'-ssDNA tail is immediately coated by Beta as 5'-to-3' resection proceeds.
