# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda Gene L (gpL, P03738)

## Summary of Gene Product Function
Gene `L` encodes the minor tail tip attachment protein gpL (UniProt: `P03738`, 232 aa, ~25.7 kDa), an essential structural subunit of the bacteriophage lambda tail tip complex that coordinates an oxygen-sensitive `[4Fe-4S]` cluster via four conserved C-terminal cysteine residues (`Cys173`, `Cys182`, `Cys205`, `Cys212`) (`PMID:23542343`). During tail morphogenesis, three copies of full-length gpL assemble with the trimeric hub protein `gpI` and the central fiber trimer `gpJ` (assisted by `gpK`) to form the 15S tail tip precursor (`PMID:23851014`, `PMID:41237242`). In the mature virion tail tip complex resolved at 3.37-Å resolution by cryo-EM, the trimeric gpL complex bridges the hexameric distal tail ring (`gpM`) above and the trimeric hub (`gpI`) and central tail fiber (`gpJ`) below, while also contacting three copies of the C-terminal cleaved fragment of the tape measure protein (`gpHC`, residues 818–849) and providing attachment sites for the side tail fiber (`gpStf`) collar in Ur-lambda (`PMID:41237242`).

## Literature & Structural Evidence
- **Iron-sulfur cluster coordination and essentiality (`PMID:23542343`)**: Demonstrates biochemically, spectroscopically, and genetically that the C-terminal domain of lambda gpL binds a `[4Fe-4S]` cluster coordinated by four conserved cysteines (`Cys173`, `Cys182`, `Cys205`, `Cys212`), and that substituting any of these cysteines with Ser or Ala abolishes or severely impairs phage tail assembly and biological activity [PMID:23542343, "Four highly conserved cysteine residues were shown to be required for coordinating the iron-sulfur cluster, and substitution of any of these Cys residues with Ser or Ala within the context of λ gpL abolished biological activity."; "By contrast, no tails were detected for the conserved cysteine mutants, Cys173Ser, Cys182Ser and Cys205Ser, and the Cys212Ser mutant supported production of only 4.2±1.0 % of tails compared to wild type."].
- **Cryo-EM structure of the Ur-lambda tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`)**: Resolves three full-length copies of gpL (232 residues) within the C3-symmetric tail tip complex (`gpM`, `gpL`, `gpI`, `gpJ`), associated with three copies of `gpI` and three copies of C-terminal `gpHC` (residues 818–849) [PMID:41237242, "We resolved three full-length copies of the tip attachment protein, gpL (232 residues), and three of the baseplate hub protein gpI (residues 134 to 222), which are clearly associated with three copies of residues 818 to 849 (from 853) of gpH, or in the mature phage, C-terminal residues of gpHC"].

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- `GO:0051539` (`4 iron, 4 sulfur cluster binding`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. Directly supported by spectroscopic, biochemical, and mutational analysis (`PMID:23542343`) and UniProt cofactor curation (`file:LAMBD/L/L-uniprot.txt`).
- `GO:0046872` (`metal ion binding`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`) and `GO:0051536` (`iron-sulfur cluster binding`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000120`): **MODIFY** both to `GO:0051539` (`4 iron, 4 sulfur cluster binding`), which is the specific descendant term already present on this gene.
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, `IMP` from `PMID:23542343` and `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. Mutation of gpL or its `[4Fe-4S]`-coordinating cysteines abolishes tail assembly (`PMID:23542343`).
- `GO:0098015` (`virus tail`, `IDA` from `PMID:41237242` and `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0098023` (`virus tail, tip`), as cryo-EM directly resolves three copies of gpL as an integral component of the tail tip complex (`PMID:41237242`).
- `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT**. As part of the tail tip hub holding `gpHC` and connecting `gpJ`/`gpI` to `gpM`, gpL undergoes structural rearrangement during infection initiation to open the tail tip conduit for DNA ejection (`PMID:41237242`).
- `GO:0046718` (`symbiont entry into host cell`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000120`): **MODIFY** to `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`), which is more specific and already present on this gene.
- `GO:0030430` (`host cell cytoplasm`, `IEA` from `GO_REF:0000120`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE**. Transient biosynthetic localization in the bacterial host cytoplasm where the `[4Fe-4S]` cluster is loaded and the 15S tail tip precursor assembles.
