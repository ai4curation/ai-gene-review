# Bacteriophage lambda gene ea22 (Early pL-operon protein Ea22; UniProt P03756)

## Overview
Gene `ea22` of *Escherichia phage lambda* (`NCBITaxon:2681611`) encodes a 182-amino-acid (20.9 kDa) early protein (Ea22) located in the leftward `pL` early operon between `ral` (or the `att`-proximal accessory region) and `ea8.5`/`exo` [file:LAMBD/ea22/ea22-uniprot.txt]. Like neighboring accessory genes in the `int-red` interval, `ea22` is dispensable for plaque formation under standard laboratory conditions but is conserved across numerous lambdoid and Shiga toxin-converting (Stx) bacteriophages (`Ead_Ea22` family, `Pfam:PF13935`, `InterPro:IPR025153`).

## Structural and Domain Characterization
1. **Domain Architecture and Coiled-Coil Region**: Ea22 belongs to the `Ead_Ea22` domain family (`Pfam:PF13935`, `InterPro:IPR025153`, covering residues 2–179) and contains a central alpha-helical coiled-coil segment spanning residues 63–90 (`FT   COILED          63..90`) [file:LAMBD/ea22/ea22-uniprot.txt].
2. **NMR Solution Structure of the C-Terminal Domain (`PDB:8DSX`)**: The solution NMR structure of the C-terminal domain of lambda Ea22 (residues 102–182, `PDB:8DSX`) demonstrates that the C-terminal region forms a stable homodimer (`A/B=102-182`) with an alpha+beta fold (`FT   STRAND` and `FT   HELIX` elements across residues 108–178) [file:LAMBD/ea22/ea22-uniprot.txt]. Although functional studies of Ea22 homologs in lambdoid phages suggest a role in promoting lysogenic survival and attenuating lytic induction under DNA-damaging stress, the specific molecular target of lambda Ea22 in *Escherichia coli* has not yet been annotated in GOA.
