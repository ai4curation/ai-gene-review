# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda `ea10` (P68658)

## Summary of Gene Product
Bacteriophage lambda `ea10` protein (UniProt `P68658`, `EA10_LAMBD`, 122 AA, mass 13.8 kDa, historically also given the synonym `ssb` from early single-stranded DNA-cellulose elution studies, `InterPro:IPR024252` `DUF2528` / `Pfam:PF10737`) is an early leftward `pL`-operon accessory protein encoded in the `b2`-`N` region between `ral` and `git`/`kil` [file:LAMBD/ea10/ea10-uniprot.txt]. According to the UniProt record, the `ea10` gene product is implicated in the `Tro` (trans-acting inhibition of growth) phenotype observed when lambda phages carrying a `cro-` mutation and a temperature-sensitive `cI857` repressor are shifted to restrictive temperatures, where uncontrolled `pL` transcription leads to shutoff of host macromolecular synthesis [file:LAMBD/ea10/ea10-uniprot.txt].

## Structural and Mechanistic Architecture
- **DUF2528 domain (`InterPro:IPR024252`, `Pfam:PF10737`)**: Covers residues 1–121 of the 122-AA polypeptide [file:LAMBD/ea10/ea10-uniprot.txt]. Although historically noted to bind nucleic-acid affinity columns in crude extracts of lambda-infected cells and genetically linked to the `pL`-expressed `Tro` host-shutoff region alongside `ea9`/`ral`/`kil`, the direct biochemical activity and molecular target of purified `Ea10` have not been definitively established.
