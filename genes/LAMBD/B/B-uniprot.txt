CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms the portal vertex of the capsid (PubMed:6228056,
CC       PubMed:6232391). This portal plays critical roles in head assembly,
CC       genome packaging, neck/tail attachment, and genome ejection (Probable).
CC       The portal protein multimerizes as a single ring-shaped homododecamer
CC       arranged around a central channel (PubMed:6232391). Binds to the
CC       terminase subunits to form the packaging machine (PubMed:7799432).
CC       {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_04135, ECO:0000269|PubMed:6228056,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:6232391, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7799432, ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homododecamer (PubMed:6232391). Interacts with the terminase
CC       complex composed of two small and one large terminase subunits
CC       (PubMed:7799432). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:6232391,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7799432}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_04135,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:6228056}.
CC   -!- PTM: Proteolytically cleaved by the viral protease during capsid
CC       maturation. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_04135,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20620152}.
