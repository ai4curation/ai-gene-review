# Bacteriophage lambda gene ea8.5 (Early pL-operon protein Ea8.5; UniProt P03755)

## Overview
Gene `ea8.5` of *Escherichia phage lambda* (`NCBITaxon:2681611`) encodes a 93-amino-acid (10.5 kDa) early protein (Ea8.5) located in the leftward `pL` operon between `ea22` and `exo` [file:LAMBD/ea8.5/ea8.5-uniprot.txt]. Originally identified as an ~8.5–10.5 kDa polypeptide synthesized from the `pL` transcript, `ea8.5` is nonessential for lytic growth or lysogeny under standard laboratory conditions.

## Bioinformatics, Structural Classification, and Interactions
1. **Conserved `Lambda_ea8_5` Family and Winged-Helix/Homeodomain-Like Fold**: Ea8.5 is the founding member of the `Lambda_ea8_5` family (`Pfam:PF20735`, `InterPro:IPR048531` `ea8_5-like`, covering residues 1–91) and is classified in `Gene3D:1.10.10.1920` (winged-helix / homeodomain-like superfamily) [file:LAMBD/ea8.5/ea8.5-uniprot.txt].
2. **Protein-Protein Interactions**: IntAct records 6 binary interactions for `P03755` (`DR   IntAct; P03755; 6.`) from systematic bacteriophage lambda yeast two-hybrid interactome screens [file:LAMBD/ea8.5/ea8.5-uniprot.txt]. However, its physiological target and specific molecular function in *Escherichia coli* remain experimentally unestablished in GOA.
