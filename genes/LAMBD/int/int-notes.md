# Curation Notes for Escherichia phage lambda `int` (P03700)

## Summary of Gene Product
Bacteriophage lambda integrase (`int`, UniProt `P03700`, 356 AA) is the prototype heterobivalent tyrosine site-specific recombinase that catalyzes both integrative recombination (between phage `attP` and host `attB` to form the integrated prophage flanked by `attL` and `attR` during establishment of lysogeny) and excisive recombination (between `attL` and `attR` to regenerate circular phage `attP` and host `attB` upon prophage induction, in cooperation with excisionase `Xis`) [file:LAMBD/int/int-uniprot.txt; PMID:11904406; PMID:15973401].

## Structural and Mechanistic Architecture
- **N-terminal arm-type DNA-binding domain (residues 1–64)**: Recognizes distal arm-type DNA sequences (`P1`, `P2`, `P'1`, `P'2`, `P'3`) via a three-stranded antiparallel beta-sheet that inserts into the major groove, aided by an N-terminal basic tail (`Met1–Leu10`) [PMID:11904406; PMID:19324050]. Also mediates cooperative protein-protein interactions with adjacent Int protomers and with the C-terminal region of excisionase (`Xis`), with `Glu47` serving as a key determinant of both Int-Xis and Int-Int cooperativity [PMID:12832614].
- **Central core-binding domain (residues ~75–175)**: Recognizes the degenerate 9-bp inverted core-type DNA sequences (`C`, `C'`, `B`, `B'`) flanking the 7-bp overlap region via mutual-induced fit on the opposite face of the DNA duplex from the catalytic domain [PMID:18001133].
- **C-terminal catalytic domain (residues ~176–356)**: Contains the canonical tyrosine recombinase catalytic pentad (`Arg212`, `Lys235`, `His308`, `Arg311`, `His333`) that coordinates the scissile phosphodiester bond and the catalytic nucleophile `Tyr342`, which forms a transient covalent `O-(3'-phospho-DNA)-tyrosine` intermediate during sequential strand cleavage, Holliday junction formation, and resolution [file:LAMBD/int/int-uniprot.txt; PMID:15973401].
- **Quaternary assembly**: Assembles as a cyclic homotetramer bridged between arm-type and core-type DNA sites within higher-order nucleoprotein intasomes, together with host architectural factor IHF (and Xis/Fis during excision), which allosterically position the catalytic domains for concerted top-strand and bottom-strand exchange [file:LAMBD/int/int-uniprot.txt; PMID:15973401].

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- **`GO:0003677` (`DNA binding`, 5 rows: EXP `PMID:11904406`, EXP `PMID:18001133`, EXP `PMID:19324050`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120`, IMP `PMID:11904406`)**: `ACCEPT`. Directly supported by NMR and X-ray crystal structures and mutational analyses of both the N-terminal arm-type DNA-binding domain (`PMID:11904406`, `PMID:19324050`) and the central core-binding domain (`PMID:18001133`).
- **`GO:0008907` (`integrase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000002`)**: `ACCEPT`. Lambda Int is the defining phage tyrosine integrase that catalyzes site-specific DNA cleavage and strand transfer via a 3'-phosphotyrosyl covalent intermediate [file:LAMBD/int/int-uniprot.txt; PMID:15973401].
- **`GO:0015074` (`DNA integration`, IDA `PMID:6444632` and IEA `GO_REF:0000120`)**: `ACCEPT`. Core biological role of Int in inserting phage DNA into the host chromosome [file:LAMBD/int/int-uniprot.txt; PMID:6444632].
- **`GO:0044826` (`viral genome integration into host DNA`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)**: `ACCEPT`. Specific viral biological process describing integration of the lambda genome at the host `attB` locus.
- **`GO:0075713` (`establishment of integrated proviral latency`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)**: `ACCEPT`. Site-specific integration of lambda DNA into the *E. coli* chromosome establishes the integrated prophage state during lysogeny.
- **`GO:0032359` (`provirus excision`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)**: `ACCEPT`. Int provides the catalytic strand cleavage and rejoining activity in the excisive intasome alongside Xis, IHF, and Fis [file:LAMBD/int/int-uniprot.txt; PMID:11904406].
- **`GO:0006310` (`DNA recombination`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120`)**: `TOO_GENERAL`. While true, Int specifically catalyzes `GO:0044826` (`viral genome integration into host DNA`), `GO:0015074` (`DNA integration`), and `GO:0032359` (`provirus excision`) via site-specific recombination.
- **`GO:0016740` (`transferase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)** and **`GO:0016787` (`hydrolase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)**: `TOO_GENERAL`. High-level enzyme class terms from UniProt keyword mapping that are uninformative given `GO:0008907` (`integrase activity`).
- **`GO:0046718` (`symbiont entry into host cell`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)**: `REMOVE`. Artifact of broad UniProt keyword mapping (`KW-1160`). Int acts intracellularly on phage and host chromosomes after viral DNA injection and does not participate in virion attachment, penetration, or entry into the host cell.
