# ARK primary-source review — 2026-10-25

Manual literature research using the review and annotation-reviewer workflow; Falcon and Perplexity providers were unavailable in this session (previously established authentication/quota failures). No provider report was fabricated. All 11 seeded rows were reviewed.

A0A0S1VWZ1 is the directly published ARK sequence (KT581394/ALM55660.1), not a reference-proteome accession. ARK and ANR denote the same moss kinase. PANTHER PTHR44329:SF298 has the surprising official label MIXED LINEAGE KINASE DOMAIN-LIKE PROTEIN; that label is not a claim that moss ARK is mammalian MLKL.

PMID:26540727 was refreshed through the publication pipeline and full text read. Fig. 3 assays ARK kinase domain with PpSnRK2B, not SRK2A. Ser165/169 of B are direct sites; ARK-dependent phosphorylation increased activity beyond autophosphorylation. ARK-GFP is mainly cytosolic. PMID:30675528 assays ARK with PpSnRK2D; it separately shows SRK2A complementation. PMID:33655297 abstract establishes ABA-induced ARK Ser1029 phosphorylation and ppabi1 ark epistasis, not constitutive RAF activity.

PMID:27194706, PMID:33655297 remained abstract-only after forced fetch: publisher XML restriction and unsuccessful HTML/PDF fallback; direct PMC browsing also met a challenge page. These abstracts support only explicitly stated claims. Publisher correction PMID:30729190 was subsequently read at https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-019-0312-y.pdf: it corrects swapped labels in original PDF Fig. 2c. It does not alter the complementation or kinase-assay conclusions used here.

Broad catalytic terms were refined consistently. The PAS domain does not imply a DNA-binding transcription factor; positive transcriptional control is through the kinase pathway. Sugar-mediated signaling remains UNDECIDED because ABA-triggered soluble sugar accumulation is an output, not proof of sugar sensing. Core localization is backed by the ARK experiment rather than transferred from SnRK2B.

Added a NEW cytosol annotation from direct localization experiments. IBA signal-transduction refinement records a granularity issue, not a claim of erroneous PAINT node placement; no donor-specific defect was inferred.
