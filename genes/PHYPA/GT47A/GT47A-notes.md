# Physcomitrium GT47A (PpGT47A) notes, 2026-10-30

Moss comparator for the IRX10 family (PTHR11062 family review, xylan module).

- Single IRX10 homolog in the moss genome [PMID:23286876 "A single putative IRX10
  homolog is present in Physcomitrella encoded by Pp1s7_455V6 (PpGT47A)"]; the
  repository analysis (genes/ARATH/IRX10/IRX10-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md) agrees:
  A9RGD8 is the only Physcomitrium GT47 in the IRX10 range.
- Hence the "IRX10 homolog from the moss" that Jensen et al. 2014 (PMID:25139408)
  found to be a robust xylan beta-1,4-xylosyltransferase is PpGT47A. The cached
  abstract does not name it; the identification is by elimination.
- Moss walls contain GlcA-substituted glucuronoxylan (PMID:22048859).
- Genetics: gt47a knockouts have no phenotype and unchanged wall sugars; PpGT47A
  partly rescues Arabidopsis irx10 irx10-L growth but not stem xylose (PMID:23286876).
- Main curation calls: GO:0009834 secondary wall biogenesis marked over-annotated
  (IBA and ISS; bryophyte conducting tissue lacks thickened walls; expression is in
  expanding tissues). ISS GO:0080116 from AtIRX10 redirected to GO:0047517, added as
  NEW (IDA, PMID:25139408).
