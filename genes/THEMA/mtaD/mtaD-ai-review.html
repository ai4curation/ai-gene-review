Gene Description

Tm0936/MtaD is a zinc-dependent deaminase from Thermotoga maritima MSB8 that converts S-adenosylhomocysteine to S-inosylhomocysteine and 5′-methylthioadenosine to 5′-methylthioinosine. It also deaminates adenosine and 5′-deoxyadenosine in purified-protein assays. Its measured substrate profile favors the sulfur-containing adenosine derivatives in the 2013 comparison. Coupling SAH deamination to the organism's SIH-hydrolyzing AhcY can regenerate homocysteine, although the dominant native substrate and flux through that route remain unresolved. AMP and SAM were not substrates in the original assay.
