Gene Description

BC_1526 (Lit) is a four-pass membrane lipoprotein intramolecular transacylase in Bacillus cereus ATCC14579. It relocates the sn-2 ester-linked acyl chain of an N-terminal diacylglyceryl-cysteine to the cysteine alpha-amino group, producing an N-acyl-S-monoacylglyceryl lyso-lipoprotein without adding another acyl chain. The reaction occurs at the extracytoplasmic membrane interface. Target protein chemistry and native lipoprotein product analysis establish this maturation activity; a histidine-dependent acid-base mechanism is supported by structural and functional studies.
