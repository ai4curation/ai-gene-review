# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 16 (Capsid assembly scaffolding protein gp16, Q05222) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `16`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05222` (`SCAF_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 173 AA (18.8 kDa; mature chain 2-173)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Genomic Context and Function
- Encoded immediately upstream of the major capsid subunit gene `17` in the L5 head morphogenesis operon [PMID:8459766, "We describe here the complete DNA sequence of the L5 genome and initial characterization of L5 virion structure and gene expression."].
- Sequenced at the protein level (residues 2-16, with initiator methionine removed) from L5 head/virion preparations by Hatfull & Sarkis (1993) [file:BPML5/16/16-uniprot.txt, "RP   NUCLEOTIDE SEQUENCE [GENOMIC DNA], AND PROTEIN SEQUENCE OF 2-16."].
- Contains a central coiled-coil domain (residues 36-84) flanked by disordered regions and functions as the capsid assembly scaffolding protein (`CC   -!- FUNCTION: Scaffolding protein involved in the icosahedric procapsid assembly. Coassembles with the capsid proteins to form the procapsid, in which the scaffolding protein is found within the external shell of icosahedrally arranged capsid protein subunits.`) [file:BPML5/16/16-uniprot.txt, "Scaffolding protein involved in the icosahedric procapsid assembly."].
- Proposed `NEW` annotations: `GO:0005198` (`structural molecule activity`) and `GO:0019069` (`viral capsid assembly`).
