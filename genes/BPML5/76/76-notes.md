# Curation Notes: Mycobacterium phage L5 gene 76 (gp76, Q05291)

## Summary of Evidence
- **UniProt (`Q05291` / `VG76_BPML5`)**: Encodes Gene 76 protein (Gp76), a 91-amino-acid (10,073 Da) uncharacterized protein containing a `DUF7199` domain (`PF23829`, InterPro `IPR055623`) and a predicted C-terminal disordered region (residues 58–81).
- **Genomic context (`PMID:8459766`, `PMID:18667563`, `PMID:9312049`)**: Located in the right-arm early region immediately downstream (in leftward transcription order) of the cytotoxic early host-shut-off gene `77` (`gp77`). Transcription across this early operon is controlled by the phage repressor gp71.
- **GOA Status**: `76-goa.tsv` is empty. No experimental function has been reported for gp76, so `existing_annotations` and `core_functions` are left empty.
