# Curation Notes: Mycobacterium phage L5 Gene 70 (gp70, Q05284)

## Overview
- **UniProt ID:** Q05284 (`VG70_BPML5`)
- **Gene Symbol:** `70`
- **Length:** 145 amino acids (16.4 kDa)
- **Genomic Context:** Encoded on the leftwards-transcribed right arm of the Mycobacterium phage L5 genome immediately downstream of the prophage repressor gene `71` and upstream of the PD-(D/E)XK nuclease gene `69` [PMID:8459766, PMID:8594325, PMID:9636706].

## Domain & Family Architecture
- **InterPro:** `IPR035405` (`GP70`, Mycobacteriophage GP70 family)
- **Pfam:** `PF17429` (`GP70`)

## Biological Role & GO Annotation Decisions
- L5 gp70 is a 145-amino-acid protein belonging to the conserved `GP70` family (`PF17429` / `IPR035405`).
- In the closely related lytic mycobacteriophage D29, a 3.6 kb deletion near the right end of the genome removes the repressor gene `71` and adjacent sequences [PMID:9636706].
- No biochemical or genetic function has been established for L5 gp70, and no GO annotations exist in GOA (`70-goa.tsv` is empty). `existing_annotations` is kept empty.
