# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 31 (gp31, Q05240)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `31`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05240` (`VG31_BPML5`, 548 aa, 58.5 kDa)
- **Genomic context**: Large tail-region gene located near the distal end of the late structural operon of Mycobacteriophage L5 (`gp26`–`gp32`) [PMID:8459766].
- **Domain architecture**: Contains an N-terminal DUF7257 domain (`IPR055681` / `PF23918`) and a C-terminal conserved minor tail domain `Minor_tail_gp31_C` (`IPR056923` / `PF24243`, `Phage_tail_C`), named after mycobacteriophage L5 gp31.
- **Curation decisions**:
