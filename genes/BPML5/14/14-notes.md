# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 14 (Portal protein gp14, Q05220) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `14`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05220` (`PORTL_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 486 AA (53.8 kDa)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Genomic Context and Function
- Encoded immediately downstream of the large terminase subunit gene `13` in the L5 head morphogenesis operon [PMID:8459766, "We describe here the complete DNA sequence of the L5 genome and initial characterization of L5 virion structure and gene expression."].
- Belongs to the SPP1 Gp6-like bacteriophage portal protein family (`IPR021145` / `PF05133` `SPP1_portal`; `TCDB: 1.W.7.3.7`) [file:BPML5/14/14-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR021145; Portal_protein_SPP1_Gp6-like."].
- Multimerizes into a homododecameric ring at the unique five-fold portal vertex of the icosahedral capsid (`GO:0019028` `viral capsid`), where it provides `GO:0005198` (`structural molecule activity`) and is essential for `GO:0019069` (`viral capsid assembly`), `GO:0019073` (`viral DNA genome packaging`), tail attachment, and `GO:0099001` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, long flexible tail mechanism`) [file:BPML5/14/14-uniprot.txt, "Forms the portal vertex of the capsid. This portal plays critical roles in head assembly, genome packaging, neck/tail attachment, and genome ejection."].
