# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 35 (gp35, Q05245)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `35`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05245` (`VG35_BPML5`, 72 aa, 8.3 kDa)
- **Genomic context**: Small open reading frame located in the integration/early region of Mycobacteriophage L5 between the integrase gene `33` (`int`) and the excisionase gene `XIS` (`36`) [PMID:8459766].
- **Domain/sequence features**: 72-aa protein with a proline-rich N-terminal region and a hydrophobic C-terminal segment (residues 49–65); homologous to the conserved mycobacteriophage DUF2571 family (`IPR019553`).
- **Function**: Uncharacterized protein. Deletion studies of the region flanking `int` and `36` (`XIS`) indicate that only `int` is required for integration and `int` + `36` (`XIS`) are sufficient for excision [PMID:1901654, PMID:10652095].
- **GOA Status**: No existing GO annotations; `core_functions` is left empty.
