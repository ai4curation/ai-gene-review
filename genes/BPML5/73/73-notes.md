# Curation Notes: Mycobacterium phage L5 gene 73 (gp73, Q05288)

## Summary of Evidence
- **UniProt (`Q05288` / `VG73_BPML5`)**: Encodes Gene 73 protein (Gp73), an 85-amino-acid (10,250 Da) uncharacterized protein classified into Pfam family `PF25718` (`Mycobacteriophage_73`, InterPro `IPR057897`).
- **Genomic context (`PMID:8459766`, `PMID:8594325`, `PMID:9312049`, `PMID:9636706`)**: Located in the right-arm early lytic region of temperate mycobacteriophage L5, immediately upstream of the lysogenic repressor gene `71` (`gp71`) and gene `72`. Leftward transcription through the right arm is initiated at the early lytic promoter $P_{\text{left}}$ near the right cohesive end and regulated by gp71 binding at promoter and stoperator sites (`PMID:8594325`, `PMID:9312049`). In the closely related lytic phage D29, a 3.6 kb deletion removes the repressor region (`PMID:9636706`).
- **GOA Status**: `73-goa.tsv` contains no existing GO annotations. Because the molecular function, biological process, and cellular localization of gp73 remain experimentally uncharacterized, no `NEW` annotations or `core_functions` entries are asserted.
