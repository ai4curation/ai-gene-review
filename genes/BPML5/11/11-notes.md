# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 11 (Holin gp11, Q05296) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `11`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05296` (`HOLIN_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 131 AA (13.4 kDa)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Function and Domain Architecture
- L5 gp11 is the bacteriophage holin encoded between `lysA` (gene `10`) and `lysB` (gene `12`) in the Cluster A left-arm lysis cassette [PMID:19555454, "Some of the intervening genes encode putative holins and exhibit holin-like function (e.g. D29 gene 11, data not shown), whereas others (e.g. Omega gene 51) code for putative homing endonuclease HNH motifs (Fig. 1B)"].
- Belongs to the mycobacteriophage D29/L5 holin family (`HAMAP:MF_04168`, `IPR032121`, `PF16081` `Phage_holin_7_1`) with two predicted transmembrane helices (residues 9-29 and 40-60) and a C-terminal coiled-coil region (residues 74-104) [file:BPML5/11/11-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR032121; Myco_phage_holin."].
- Accumulates in the host cell plasma membrane (`GO:0020002`) and oligomerizes at a genetically programmed time (`GO:0140911` `pore-forming activity`) to permeabilize the inner membrane, enabling Endolysin A (gp10) and Endolysin B (gp12) to access the cell envelope and execute `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell`) [PMID:22470512, "Phages employ a common system to destroy these structures that involves expression of an endolysin to cleave the peptidoglycan and a holin that permeabilizes the cell membrane to enable access of the endolysin to its substrate; holins also control the timing of lysis [17]."].
