CC   -!- FUNCTION: Integrase is necessary for integration of the phage into the
CC       host genome by site-specific recombination. In the presence of host
CC       integration host factor (mIHF) forms a specific intasome complex with
CC       attP (PubMed:8986825, PubMed:9882658). The intasome subsequently binds
CC       attB and forms integration products. mIHF probably stabilizes a sharp
CC       bend in the DNA during integration (PubMed:9882658).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8986825, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9882658}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms a complex with host mIHF and attP DNA (phage attachment
CC       site) (PubMed:8986825, PubMed:9882658). The complex binds attB
CC       (bacterial attachment site) to form products (PubMed:9882658).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8986825, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9882658}.
