# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 19 (gp19, Q05225) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `19`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05225` (`VG19_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 125 AA (14.1 kDa)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Genomic Context and Domain Architecture
- Gene `19` is located between genes `18` and `20` in the putative head-to-tail connector region of the mycobacteriophage L5 virion structural gene operon [PMID:8459766, "We describe here the complete DNA sequence of the L5 genome and initial characterization of L5 virion structure and gene expression."].
- Contains the conserved `Mycophage_D29_Gp19` / `Phage_Gp19` domain (`IPR018963` / `PF09355`) conserved across Cluster A mycobacteriophages such as D29 and L5 [file:BPML5/19/19-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR018963; Mycophage_D29_Gp19."].
- Lacks direct experimental functional characterization and has no existing GOA annotations.
