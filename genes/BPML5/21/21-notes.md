# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 21 (gp21, Q05227)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `21`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05227` (`VG21_BPML5`, 111 aa, 12.1 kDa)
- **Genomic context**: Located in the late structural operon of Mycobacteriophage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`), immediately downstream of the head-to-tail connector region and upstream of the putative tail terminator gene `22` [PMID:8459766].
- **Domain architecture**: Contains a conserved DUF5403 domain (`IPR039452` / `PF17395`) conserved across cluster A mycobacteriophages.
- **Function**: Uncharacterized head-tail region protein. Its syntenic position between the head-to-tail neck/connector module and the tail terminator (`gp22`) suggests a potential role in head-to-tail joining or virion morphogenesis, though it has not been directly detected by N-terminal sequencing of mature virions or biochemically characterized [PMID:8459766].
- **GOA Status**: No existing GO annotations. Because no specific molecular function or biological process is experimentally or bioinformatically established beyond DUF5403 homology, `core_functions` is left empty.
