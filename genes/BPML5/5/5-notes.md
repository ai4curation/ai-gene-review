# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 5 (gp5, Q05267) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `5`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05267` (`VG05_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 155 AA (17.1 kDa)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Genomic Context and Function
- Encoded in the left arm of the mycobacteriophage L5 genome [PMID:8459766, "We describe here the complete DNA sequence of the L5 genome and initial characterization of L5 virion structure and gene expression."].
- Classified in the phage small terminase subunit family `Terminase_7` (`IPR057972` / `PF25673`) [file:BPML5/5/5-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR057972; Terminase_7."].
- In Cluster A mycobacteriophages, the lysis cassette (genes `10`-`12`) and tRNA/small left-arm genes are inserted to the left of the main head morphogenesis genes (`13`-`17`), separating the putative small terminase subunit (`5`) from the large terminase subunit (`13`).
- As the putative small terminase (TerS) subunit, gp5 recognizes viral DNA (`GO:0003677` `DNA binding`) and participates with the large terminase subunit gp13 in `GO:0019073` (`viral DNA genome packaging`).
