# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 15 (gp15, Q05221) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `15`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05221` (`VG15_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 294 AA (32.9 kDa)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Genomic Context and Domain Architecture
- Gene `15` is located in the mycobacteriophage L5 head morphogenesis operon strictly between the portal protein gene `14` and the capsid scaffolding protein gene `16` [PMID:8459766, "We describe here the complete DNA sequence of the L5 genome and initial characterization of L5 virion structure and gene expression."].
- Contains the conserved `VG15` domain (`IPR057369` / `PF25310`) found in Cluster A mycobacteriophages [file:BPML5/15/15-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR057369; VG15."].
- Although its syntenic position between portal (`14`) and scaffolding (`16`) genes in siphoviruses often corresponds to a prohead protease or head maturation factor, gp15 was not detected in mature L5 virions by N-terminal protein sequencing in Hatfull & Sarkis (1993) and lacks experimental biochemical characterization. Thus `core_functions` is left empty pending direct experimental evidence.
