# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 13 (Terminase large subunit gp13, Q05219) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `13`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05219` (`VG13_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 593 AA (66.2 kDa)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Genomic Context and Function
- Encoded immediately upstream of the portal protein gene `14` at the start of the virion head morphogenesis operon (`13`-`17`) in mycobacteriophage L5 [PMID:8459766, "We describe here the complete DNA sequence of the L5 genome and initial characterization of L5 virion structure and gene expression."].
- Classified in the bacteriophage terminase large subunit (TerL) family (`CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the phage terminase family.`) [file:BPML5/13/13-uniprot.txt, "CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the phage terminase family."].
- As the large terminase subunit, gp13 provides the ATP-driven DNA translocation motor (`GO:0016887` `ATP hydrolysis activity`) and DNA-binding/packaging activity (`GO:0003677` `DNA binding`, `GO:0019073` `viral DNA genome packaging`) that docks onto the gp14 portal vertex to package L5 genomic DNA into procapsids.
