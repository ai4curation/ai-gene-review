# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 17 (Major capsid protein gp17, Q05223) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `17`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05223` (`CAPSD_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 326 AA (35.2 kDa; mature chain 2-326)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Experimental Characterization and Function
- Hatfull & Sarkis (1993) identified gp17 as the major capsid (head shell) subunit of mycobacteriophage L5 by N-terminal protein sequencing (residues 2-11, following removal of Met1) and demonstrated that gp17 subunits in mature L5 heads are extensively covalently cross-linked into high-molecular-weight complexes [PMID:8459766, "We describe here the complete DNA sequence of the L5 genome and initial characterization of L5 virion structure and gene expression."]; [file:BPML5/17/17-uniprot.txt, "CC   -!- SUBUNIT: L5 head shells are composed of gp17 subunits that are extensively cross-linked."].
- Contains the canonical HK97 gp5-like phage major capsid protein fold (`IPR024455` / `PF05065` `Phage_capsid`; `TIGR01554` `major_cap_HK97`; `SSF56563`) [file:BPML5/17/17-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR024455; Phage_capsid."].
- Existing GOA annotation `GO:0019028` (`viral capsid`) is accepted (`ACCEPT`), and `GO:0005198` (`structural molecule activity`, `IDA`) and `GO:0019069` (`viral capsid assembly`, `IDA`) are added as `NEW`.
