# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 26 (Minor tail protein Gp26 / Tape measure protein, Q05233)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `26`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05233` (`VG26_BPML5`, 837 aa, 86.4 kDa; initiator methionine cleaved)
- **Experimental evidence**: Confirmed as a virion structural polypeptide by N-terminal protein sequencing (residues 2-11) of purified L5 virions [PMID:8459766].
- **Function**: Phage tail tape measure protein (TMP, 837 aa), the longest protein in the L5 tail morphogenesis module, located immediately downstream of the frameshifted tail assembly chaperones gp24/gp25 (`PMID:8459766`, `PMID:15469818`). Determines the length of the non-contractile siphoviral tail (~135 nm) by spanning the lumen of the tail tube as an extended alpha-helical scaffold during tail assembly.
- **Curation decisions**:
  - Propose `NEW` `GO:0005198` (`structural molecule activity`), `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`), and `GO:0098015` (`viral tail`).
