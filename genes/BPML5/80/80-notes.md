- **Genomic context (`PMID:8459766`, `PMID:18667563`, `PMID:9312049`)**: Located in the right-arm early region of Mycobacterium phage L5 between `ORF83` (which contains the gp71-regulated promoter driving downstream expression of `gp79`, `gp78`, and `gp77`) and `gene 79`. Unlike `gp77`, `gp78`, and `gp79`, `gp80` was not identified as individually cytotoxic to *Mycobacterium smegmatis* in `PMID:18667563`.
