# Curation Notes: Mycobacterium phage L5 gene 77 (gp77, Q05292)

## Summary of Evidence
- **UniProt (`Q05292` / `VG77_BPML5`)**: Encodes Gene 77 protein (Gp77), a 252-amino-acid (28,227 Da) early phage protein containing two conserved domains of unknown function: `DUF2786` (`PF10979` / `IPR024498`) and `DUF7168` (`PF23771` / `IPR055592`).
- **Experimental characterization as a cytotoxic early host-shut-off protein (`PMID:18667563`)**: Using an inducible mycobacterial shuttle vector, Burger et al. (2008) identified three ORFs (`gp77`, `gp78`, and `gp79`) in a right-arm early operon of mycobacteriophage L5 that are toxic to the host *Mycobacterium smegmatis* when expressed. Transcription of this operon is driven from a promoter located inside `ORF83` and repressed by the phage immunity repressor `gp71`.
- **Direct interaction with host L-serine dehydratase MSMEG_3532 (`PMID:21656815`)**: Byk et al. (2011) demonstrated that purified L5 gp77 specifically binds the *M. smegmatis* cytoplasmic enzyme `MSMEG_3532` in cell lysates and confirmed the direct interaction via reciprocal pull-down assays using purified `MSMEG_3532` as bait. Enzymatic assays established that `MSMEG_3532` is a pyridoxal-5'-phosphate-dependent L-serine dehydratase (`SdhA`) that converts L-serine to pyruvate. Although purified gp77 did not inhibit SdhA catalysis in vitro at saturating L-serine concentrations, the specific physical interaction in vivo is proposed to modulate host amino acid pools or pyruvate availability in favor of phage maturation and host shut-off.
- **Curation Decisions**:
  - `77-goa.tsv` is currently empty.
  - Propose `NEW` annotations for `GO:0044003` (`modification by symbiont of host morphology or physiology`, supported by `PMID:18667563` and `PMID:21656815`) and `GO:0030430` (`host cell cytoplasm`, supported by `PMID:21656815`) and `GO:0019899` (`enzyme binding`, supported by `PMID:21656815`).
  - Capture the core function of gp77 in `core_functions` (`molecular_function: GO:0019899` `enzyme binding`, `directly_involved_in: GO:0044003` `modification by symbiont of host morphology or physiology`, `locations: GO:0030430` `host cell cytoplasm`), along with a `knowledge_gaps` entry noting that the in vivo mechanism by which SdhA binding alters host metabolism or causes growth arrest remains unresolved at physiological substrate concentrations.
