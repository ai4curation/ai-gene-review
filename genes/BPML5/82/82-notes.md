# Curation Notes: Mycobacterium phage L5 gene 82 (gp82, Q05298)

## Summary of Evidence
- **UniProt (`Q05298` / `VG82_BPML5`)**: Encodes Gene 82 protein (Gp82), a 126-amino-acid (14,167 Da) uncharacterized protein classified into Pfam family `PF25735` (`Phage_L5_gp82`, InterPro `IPR058002`).
- **Genomic context (`PMID:8459766`, `PMID:18667563`, `PMID:9312049`)**: Located in the right-arm early region of Mycobacterium phage L5 immediately downstream of `ORF83` (which contains the gp71-controlled early promoter directing transcription across `genes 82–77`). Unlike `gp77`, `gp78`, and `gp79`, `gp82` was not individually cytotoxic when expressed in *Mycobacterium smegmatis* (`PMID:18667563`).
- **GOA Status**: `82-goa.tsv` is empty. Because no experimental data establish a molecular function, biological process, or subcellular localization for gp82, `existing_annotations` and `core_functions` are left empty.
