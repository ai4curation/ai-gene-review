# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 22 (gp22, Q05228)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `22`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05228` (`VG22_BPML5`, 139 aa, 15.7 kDa)
- **Genomic context**: Located in the late structural operon immediately upstream of the major tail subunit gene `23` [PMID:8459766].
- **Domain architecture**: Contains the conserved siphoviral tail terminator domain (`IPR057003` / `PF23841`, `Phage_tail_terminator_2`).
- **Function**: Putative tail terminator protein (head-tail connector component) that caps the polymerized major tail tube (gp23) at the length dictated by the tape measure protein (gp26), terminating tail tube polymerization and forming the interface for attachment to the phage head.
- **Curation decisions**:
  - Propose `NEW` annotations for `GO:0005198` (`structural molecule activity`), `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`), and `GO:0098015` (`viral tail`) supported by UniProt domain classification (`IPR057003`) and synteny in the L5 structural operon [PMID:8459766].
