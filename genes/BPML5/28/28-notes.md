# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 28 (Minor tail protein Gp28, Q05235)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `28`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05235` (`VG28_BPML5`, 596 aa, 66.9 kDa; initiator methionine cleaved)
- **Experimental evidence**: Confirmed as a virion structural polypeptide by direct N-terminal protein sequencing (residues 2-9) of purified L5 virions [PMID:8459766].
- **Domain architecture**: Contains the conserved siphoviral distal tail tip / baseplate hub domain (`IPR029432` `Gp28/Gp37-like_dom` / `PF14594` `Sipho_Gp37`).
- **Curation decisions**:
  - Propose `NEW` `GO:0005198` (`structural molecule activity`), `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`), and `GO:0098015` (`viral tail`).
