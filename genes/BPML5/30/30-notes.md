# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 30 (gp30, Q05239)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `30`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05239` (`VG30_BPML5`, 101 aa, 11.5 kDa)
- **Genomic context**: Encoded in the minor tail gene cluster of Mycobacteriophage L5 between minor tail genes `29` and `31` [PMID:8459766].
- **Domain architecture**: Contains a conserved DUF2746 domain (`IPR022704` / `PF10874`) found in cluster A mycobacteriophages.
- **Function**: Uncharacterized tail-region protein. Although positioned within the minor tail gene cluster (`gp27`–`gp32`), gp30 was not detected by N-terminal sequencing of mature virions [PMID:8459766] and its specific molecular role (whether a low-copy minor tail component or non-structural tail assembly factor) remains undetermined.
- **GOA Status**: No existing GO annotations; `core_functions` is left empty pending experimental characterization.
