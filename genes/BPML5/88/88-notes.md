# Curation Notes: Mycobacterium phage L5 gene 88 (gp88, Q05305)

## Summary of Evidence
- **UniProt (`Q05305` / `VG88_BPML5`)**: Encodes Gene 88 protein (Gp88), a 239-amino-acid (26,549 Da) uncharacterized protein classified into Pfam family `PF17338` (`GP88`, InterPro `IPR020290`).
- **Genomic context (`PMID:8459766`, `PMID:8594325`, `PMID:9312049`)**: Gene 88 is the rightmost protein-coding gene of Mycobacterium phage L5, located immediately downstream of the early lytic promoter $P_{\text{left}}$ at the right end of the genome. It is the first open reading frame transcribed leftward upon initiation of early lytic transcription at $P_{\text{left}}$, which is regulated by the phage immunity repressor gp71.
