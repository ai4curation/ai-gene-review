# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 32 (gp32, Q05241)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `32`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05241` (`VG32_BPML5`, 220 aa, 21.3 kDa)
- **Genomic context**: Located at the distal end of the late tail gene cluster in Mycobacteriophage L5, immediately downstream of minor tail gene `31` and upstream of the `attP`/`int` (`33`) integration region [PMID:8459766].
- **Domain architecture**: Contains a conserved glycine-rich domain (`IPR049304` `Gly_rich_dom` / `PF21722` `Gly_rich_2`) and a C-terminal glycine-rich intrinsically disordered region (residues 184–205).
- **Function**: Conserved tail-region protein of unknown molecular function. While glycine-rich repeats are frequently found in phage tail fibers and distal tail tip proteins, gp32 has not been directly identified in mature virions or functionally assayed.
- **GOA Status**: No existing GO annotations; `core_functions` is left empty.
