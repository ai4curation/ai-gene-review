# Curation Notes: Mycobacterium phage L5 gene 74 (gp74, Q05289)

## Summary of Evidence
- **UniProt (`Q05289` / `VG74_BPML5`)**: Encodes Gene 74 protein (Gp74), a 72-amino-acid (8,469 Da) uncharacterized protein classified into Pfam family `PF26893` (`Phage_L5_Gp74`, InterPro `IPR059760`).
- **Genomic context (`PMID:8459766`, `PMID:8594325`, `PMID:9312049`)**: Located in the leftward-transcribed right-arm early region of temperate mycobacteriophage L5 between genes `75` and `73`. Transcription of this region is driven from the early lytic promoter $P_{\text{left}}$ and silenced during lysogeny by the phage repressor gp71 at promoter and stoperator sites.
- **GOA Status**: `74-goa.tsv` is empty (no existing GO annotations). Because no experimental data establish a molecular function, biological process, or subcellular localization for gp74, `existing_annotations` and `core_functions` are left empty.
