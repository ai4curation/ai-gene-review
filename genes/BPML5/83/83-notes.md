# Curation Notes: Mycobacterium phage L5 gene 83 (gp83, Q05300)

## Summary of Evidence
- **UniProt (`Q05300` / `VG83_BPML5`)**: Encodes Gene 83 protein (Gp83), a 72-amino-acid (8,043 Da) uncharacterized protein with no Pfam, InterPro, or Subcellular Location annotations in `83-uniprot.txt`.
- **Genomic and transcriptional context (`PMID:8459766`, `PMID:18667563`, `PMID:9312049`)**: Located in the right-arm early region of Mycobacterium phage L5. Burger et al. (`PMID:18667563`) demonstrated that `ORF83` harbors an internal promoter that directs transcription of the downstream cytotoxic early host-shut-off genes (`gp77`, `gp78`, and `gp79`), and that transcription from this promoter in `ORF83` is repressed by the phage immunity repressor `gp71`. While `ORF83` is critical as a cis-regulatory promoter element for the downstream early operon, the biochemical function of the encoded 72-amino-acid Gp83 polypeptide itself remains unknown.
- **GOA Status**: `83-goa.tsv` is empty. Because no function or localization has been demonstrated for the Gp83 protein product itself, `existing_annotations` and `core_functions` are left empty.
