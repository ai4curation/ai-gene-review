# Curation Notes for Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 27 (Minor tail protein Gp27, Q05234)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `27`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05234` (`VG27_BPML5`, 336 aa, 38.6 kDa)
- **Experimental evidence**: Confirmed as a virion structural protein by direct N-terminal amino acid sequencing (residues 1-8) of purified L5 virions [PMID:8459766].
- **Genomic context**: Located immediately downstream of the tape measure protein gene `26` and upstream of minor tail protein gene `28` (`PMID:8459766`), the conserved genomic position of the distal tail protein (`Dit`) that forms the central hub of the siphoviral tail tip / baseplate complex.
- **Curation decisions**:
  - Propose `NEW` `GO:0005198` (`structural molecule activity`), `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`), and `GO:0098015` (`viral tail`).
