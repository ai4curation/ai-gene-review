# Mycobacteriophage L5 Gene 18 (gp18, Q05224) Notes

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `18`
- **UniProt ID**: `Q05224` (`VG18_BPML5`)
- **Length**: 57 AA (6.5 kDa)
- **Organism**: Mycobacterium phage L5 (`NCBITaxon:31757`)

## Genomic Context and Domain Architecture
- Gene `18` is located immediately downstream of the major capsid protein gene `17` in the head-to-tail connector region of mycobacteriophage L5 [PMID:8459766, "We describe here the complete DNA sequence of the L5 genome and initial characterization of L5 virion structure and gene expression."].
- Encodes a small 57-AA protein containing the conserved `DUF7302` domain (`IPR055726` / `PF23976`) and a C-terminal intrinsically disordered region (residues 33-57) enriched in basic and acidic residues [file:BPML5/18/18-uniprot.txt, "DR   InterPro; IPR055726; DUF7302."].
- Although located in the syntenic head-to-tail connector region between the major capsid (`17`) and major tail subunit (`23`), gp18 has not been experimentally characterized. No existing GO annotations are present in GOA.
