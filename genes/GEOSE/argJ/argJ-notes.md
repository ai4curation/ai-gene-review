# argJ (Geobacillus stearothermophilus, Q07908) notes

Added as a directly assayed exemplar of the bifunctional ArgJ initiating activity for the
arginine biosynthesis module (review feedback on PR #6243).

- No deep-research provider file was generated for this gene; the review rests on the UniProt
  record and the three cached primary papers (all abstract-only in the cache).
- Bifunctional: [PMID:10931207 "T. neapolitana and B. stearothermophilus ArgJ also catalyze the conversion of glutamate to N-acetylglutamate using acetylCoA as the acetyl donor"]
- One ORF, both activities: [PMID:8473852 "Both N-acetylglutamate synthase and ornithine acetyltransferase are encoded by the same 1290 bp open reading frame."]
- Self-cleavage at Thr-197: [PMID:11320085 "The T197G substitution abolishes both precursor protein cleavage and catalytic activity"]
- PANTHER: PTHR23100:SF0 (UniProt DR PANTHER line).
- No NEW terms proposed; autoproteolytic maturation is a property of the precursor and is recorded in
  the description only.
