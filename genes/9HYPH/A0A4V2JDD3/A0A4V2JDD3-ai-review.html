Gene Description

EYR15_14300 encodes Hans-LanM, a lanmodulin of the facultative methylotroph Hansschlegelia quercus. The 133-residue precursor has a predicted 23-residue Sec signal peptide and a 110-residue mature chain carrying four EF-hand loops spaced 12-13 residues apart, the short-linker arrangement that defines the lanmodulin family; only EF2 has the proline at loop position 2 that all four Mex-LanM loops carry, and the protein shares only about 33% identity with the founding lanmodulin of Methylorubrum extorquens. Recombinant mature Hans-LanM binds about three equivalents of light lanthanide ions, with picomolar apparent affinity for La(III) and Nd(III) and a cooperative disorder-to-order (helix-forming) conformational change, while Dy(III) binds more weakly and Ca(II) only at micromolar concentration. In crystal structures EF1-EF3 hold lanthanide ions and EF4 does not. Light-lanthanide binding drives head-to-tail homodimerization through an interface in which Arg100 of one subunit hydrogen-bonds to the EF3 ligands of the other, so the dimer is more than 100-fold tighter with La(III) than with Dy(III); this metal-sensitive dimerization explains its stronger light versus heavy rare-earth discrimination compared with the monomeric Mex-LanM. Its role in H. quercus itself has not been studied.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

lanthanide ion binding

Definition: Binding to a lanthanide ion (La, Ce, Pr, Nd, Pm, Sm, Eu, Gd, Tb, Dy, Ho, Er, Tm, Yb or Lu), a trivalent f-block metal cation.

Justification: Hans-LanM, like Mex-LanM, is characterized by picomolar binding of lanthanide ions at EF-hand sites with orders-of-magnitude selectivity over Ca(II); GO currently offers only the generic metal ion binding parent. The same term was proposed in the Mex-LanM review (genes/METEA/lanM).

Core Functions

High-affinity, selective binding of light lanthanide ions (La(III), Nd(III)) at three EF-hand sites (EF1-EF3) with picomolar apparent affinity and a cooperative disorder-to-order conformational change; heavy lanthanides (Dy(III)) bind with lower affinity and Ca(II) only at micromolar concentration. A specific lanthanide ion binding term does not exist in GO and is proposed below. Lanthanide binding is coupled to metal-sensitive homodimerization that enhances light versus heavy rare-earth discrimination. All evidence is from purified recombinant protein.

CMMMech record check (data/records/rare-earth-element-commodities.yaml)

The grounding to A0A4V2JDD3 is correct (same organism, ORF, PDB entries and paper). Points
where the record says more than the evidence:

  • The causal-graph node describes Hans-LanM as having "redox/dimer-sensitive LREE/HREE
    discrimination". The paper reports metal-ion-radius-dependent dimerization; nothing in it
    concerns redox, so "redox" is unsupported.
  • "Dimeric EF-hand lanmodulin": the protein is in a monomer/dimer equilibrium that is tight
    only with light lanthanides (apo and Dy forms have Kdimer of about 60-117 µM). And the R100K
    variant used on the column is monomeric, so the separation does not rely on a dimer. The
    authors suggest functional dimers are unlikely on the column at that immobilization density.
  • The separation (>98% purity) is an in vitro, engineered, synthetic-feed result with a
    C-terminal Cys variant on agarose. It is not a property of the organism and should not be
    read as industrial performance.

Hans-LanM carries Pro at loop position 2 only in EF2 (Pro59); Mex-LanM has Pro2 in all four
EF hands. Hans-LanM EF1-EF3 begin with Asn (N1) and have Glu at positions 9 and 12; EF4 lacks
an acidic residue at position 9 (Thr). Adjacent Hans-LanM loops are spaced 12-13 residues
apart (34->58->83->107 starts give 12-13 residue linkers after each 12-residue loop), the
LanM-specific spacing. EF3 in this numbering contains Asp85 (position 3) and Glu91 (position 9),
and Arg100 lies in the following helix, matching the residue numbers used for the crystal
structures in PMID:37259003 (the paper numbers residues on the full-length precursor that
UniProt displays).

Global pairwise identity

  • Full length (133 vs 133): 45 identical residues, 33.8% of Hans-LanM length (33.1% of
    alignment columns).
  • Mature chains (24-133 vs 24-133): 37 identical residues, 33.6% of Hans-LanM length.

This agrees with the 33% identity reported in PMID:37259003.

Interpretation

The sequence satisfies the LanM hallmarks used in PMID:37259003 (four EF hands, short
inter-loop spacing, at least one Pro2). Low identity to Mex-LanM and the different Pro2
pattern are consistent with the divergent metal selectivity reported experimentally; the
analysis itself does not demonstrate lanthanide binding, which rests on the published
biochemistry and crystal structures.
