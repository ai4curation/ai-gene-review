# G20 System I* primary evidence and identity

Manual source analysis, 2026-11-06. PMID20466730 full PMC HTML was read at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2906280/?report=xml; the normal publication cache remains abstract-only. PMID24044352 is cached in full. Raw downloaded HTML and genome records remain in ignored cache; identity-provenance.yaml records URLs, hashes and exact reference-sequence comparisons.

The 2010 acknowledgments explicitly name G20 as the genomic DNA donor, independently of the 2013 Desulfovibrio alaskensis species rename. Current UniProt calls the strain Oleidesulfovibrio alaskensis G20 (OLEA2, taxon207559). pDD86 was amplified from genomic DNA. This establishes the experimental operon locus and strain, but does not provide the actual sequenced plasmid insert. All seven frozen UniProt reference translations match their ENA CP000112 CDSs exactly. CcmD is the later-added 45-residue Dde_4022/F9XXF9 CDS between CcmC/Dde_1474 and CcmI/Dde_1473; its stop overlaps the CcmI start, as the article describes. CcmI is a 204-residue membrane-anchored TPR protein, consistent with the reported22-kDa candidate.

Important construct limit: paper2013 CcmC residues H68/H192 do not match the reference Q311X3 numbers; the corresponding conserved histidines are H50/H174, an18-residue offset. UniSave shows sequence version1 throughout. A start-site/numbering difference is plausible but unproved; do not silently renumber experimental mutants or claim their full sequence equals Q311X3. In contrast, CcmE C127/Y131, CcmA K41 and CcmF P147/H173/H263 match the reference. Supplementary Word files could not be retrieved: PMC returns a download interstitial and EuropePMC classifies this article as not open access. Primer-specific and plasmid-wide sequence equality remain unresolved.

The complete ccmEFABCDI operon restores maturation of Paracoccus cytochrome c550 in E. coli EC06 lacking its endogenous ccm operon. Product reaches about5% of the canonical E. coli system in the reported aerobic comparison. A second heterologous CXXCH substrate from Trypanosoma is also matured. These are heterologous reconstruction results, not G20 native knockouts or native flux measurements. Absence of CcmG/H from this tested cassette does not exclude all host reductants or native reductant partners.

CcmI deletion leaves approximately20% of the complete-cassette c550 yield, with an unchanged product spectrum. It is an efficiency-enhancing accessory under these conditions, not an obligatory subunit. Further removal of CcmD from the active ccmEFABCD cassette gives no detectable c550; this sequential comparison supports CcmD requirement in the tested reconstruction, while not measuring a purified molecular activity or every native substrate. The 2011 correction DOI10.1074/jbc.A110.133421 (PMC3190755) corrects detection sensitivity: the c550 Soret signal can be detected down to3e-8M and heme staining to approximately1e-12mol. The main paper's original sensitivity values must not be reused.

CcmE covalently carries heme through Cys127, supported by heme-staining, spectroscopy and heme-containing tryptic peptides. C127H/A/M substitutions eliminate detectable cytochrome maturation. CcmE from the two systems is not functionally interchangeable. CcmE is a chemical heme-transfer participant, not merely a protein consumed by maturation.

The 2013 K41D CcmA variant eliminates detectable mature cytochrome but leaves approximately35% holo-CcmE. This differs from accumulation of holo-CcmE with canonical CcmA inactivation. It supports an ATP-dependent step before or concomitant with CcmE covalent loading in System I*, rather than proving an exclusively post-loading release step. No purified G20 CcmA ATPase rate was reported. CcmB's mechanical coupling role is inferred from its conserved membrane-subunit architecture and the reconstructed operon, not individually assayed here. Neither ATPase homology nor the old heme-exporter name demonstrates transmembrane heme movement.

CcmC histidine variants in the experimental operon abolish detectable holo-CcmE and cytochrome production. They support heme handling upstream of CcmE, with the explicit residue-numbering limitation above. CcmF H173A/H263A variants abolish cytochrome maturation; changes in holo-CcmE accumulation distinguish upstream loading from downstream ligation effects. A membrane-embedded heme and receipt of CcmE-bound heme are supported by comparative mechanism and mutagenesis, but the exact G20 isolated synthase reaction, electron donor and proposed ligation sequence were not reconstituted. The putative quinone-binding-site P147A variant retains maturation; quinone oxidoreduction at that motif is not an established required activity.

Family and annotation limits: Q311W9 belongs to PTHR34128 despite its mitochondrial label and has the cysteine-bearing motif. Q311X0 belongs to PTHR43653 despite any canonical CcmF/H shorthand. Q311X4 maps to broad TPR family PTHR12558/SF13; the Cdc27-dominated label is not evidence for a bacterial cell-cycle function. No PAINT ancestral node is assigned without tree evidence. The current GO0018063 definition includes cytochrome/heme-protein linkage and is a descendant of both GO0017003 and GO0017004; it refines those rows. GO0015886 includes directed movement within a cell and can describe heme trafficking without asserting GO0015232 transmembrane transport.

## Same-role process comparator check

QuickGO live annotations were queried on2026-11-06 for GO1903607. E. coli CcmA/P33931 has the process (including PMID10339610 and16824107); P. putida Q88EX5 did not return that process. E. coli CcmD/P0ABM5 (including PMID10339610 and18326572) and P. putida Q88EX8 both carry it. The comparators show that ATPase and small accessory membrane components can participate in cytochrome c biosynthesis; they are not biological evidence for G20. Separate CcmI/CycH records D5ALI6 and A0ACT3PRX1 returned no annotations in this query. Thus no CcmI annotation gap is inferred from comparative prevalence. The G20 accessory assignment instead rests on the experimentally defined operon protein, membrane-anchored TPR architecture and enhanced maturation yield; its exact substrate-handling mechanism remains an inference. GO1903607 parents describe biosynthetic chemistry, while core structural machine components can participate through assembly/cofactor work; mere necessity or being a substrate would not suffice. No descendant/ancestor BP is otherwise asserted for the three authored process proposals. A search of gocams/index.tsv for the seven G20 accessions found no existing model.
