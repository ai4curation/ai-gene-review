## Native client evidence and corrected motility interpretation

PMID:25181355 measured native 81116 client activities: AstA was 44% of wildtype in ΔdsbA2, 8% in ΔdsbB, 102% in ΔdsbA1; PhoX was 6% in ΔdsbA1, 42% in ΔdsbA2 and 53% in ΔdsbB. These are substrate-activity phenotypes supporting different client contributions, not purified pairwise catalytic-rate measurements or a complete client inventory. The same study's DsbA1 motility conclusion was corrected by PMID:32203539: “the previously observed lack of motility of the ΔdsbA1 strain was a consequence of background mutation/s”. The newly constructed mutant was motile. Therefore no native motility BP is added, and E. coli complementation is kept distinct. This correction does not erase the independent recombinant RNase oxidative-folding result.
