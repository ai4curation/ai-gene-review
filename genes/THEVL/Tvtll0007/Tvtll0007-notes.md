The 2018 authors explicitly identify RKN as NIES-2134 and use BP-1 locus names for RKN orthologs (Figure 4 caption). Their native perturbations concern RKN. The one purified recombinant enzyme described is diguanylate cyclase Tlr1210 (cloned from T. elongatus genomic DNA), not XcsB, XcsC or TolC. They establish native necessity and overexpression phenotypes for the cellulose-associated genes and propose an XcsA-XcsB-TolC arrangement from architecture and homology models. Direct assembly and cellulose passage through TolC are not established; XcsC's GH10 endoglucanase chemistry is explicitly a proposal. Neither the term HlyD-like nor a knockout phenotype establishes a particular exported substrate. [PMID:29688612, "we propose that the cyanobacterial extracellular cellulose production is due to a novel variant of the bacterial tripartite secretion system."]

Exact strain distinction is consequential: the indexed 2011 discussion reports deficient induced cellulose/aggregation in BP-1 and an A654T synthase difference relative to RKN, while leaving causality unresolved versus other signaling/regulatory differences. This is not a loss-of-function conclusion about the accessory proteins. BP-1 homologs are comparative references, not demonstrated positive copies of the native RKN machinery.

## Target assessment

E5RST0 is the authentic RKN clone: AB571479 → BAJ61012.1 → E5RST0, 736 aa, taxon1048443. Q8DMV4/tll0007 is the separate BP-1 reference and is not used as the assay protein. The top-level taxon stays exactly as the deterministic seed records it.

Four original rows were reviewed. Plasma membrane and PilZ-associated cyclic-di-GMP binding are accepted as architecture-backed inferences, not direct localization/binding assays. Generic membrane is refined to plasma membrane. Hexosyltransferase is refined to GO:0016760 cellulose synthase (UDP-forming) activity, combining native cellulose output and gene-disruption specificity with GT2/PTHR43867:SF2 catalytic classification. The donor-specific biochemical reaction is inferred from the family; the paper does not report purified E5RST0 donor-specificity kinetics.

One NEW IMP row proposes GO:0030244 cellulose biosynthetic process. Participation is catalytic polymerization, not simply necessity for aggregation or use as a substrate. The parental process describes cellulose formation. QuickGO comparator check on 2026-11-05 found E. coli BcsA P37653 with GO:0030244 IBA and its GO:0090540 descendant by IMP; Pseudomonas BcsA Q88JL4 had no matching BP rows. This supports the annotation convention for synthase catalysts without treating an absence as evidence of oversight. No existing/proposed BP ancestor or descendant is duplicated.

QuickGO verified GO:0016760 and GO:0030244 definitions and active status. The similarly numbered GO:0004107 is chorismate synthase and was not used. Official PANTHER name for PTHR43867:SF2 was read from panther.obo and agrees with the frozen sequence's family cross-reference.
