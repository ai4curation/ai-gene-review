# NCGR_LOCUS10166 sequence and gene-model check

Run `uv run genes/9POAL/NCGR_LOCUS10166/NCGR_LOCUS10166-bioinformatics/check_fusion.py` from the repository root. Pinned dependencies, original GenPept responses, source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded with the script/results. Alignments use local BLOSUM62, gap opening -10 and extension -0.5. These are computational comparisons, not expression or enzyme assays.

The candidate is CAD6214881.1 / Ml02G067130.1, a 719-aa CDS model on CAJGYO010000002.1, reverse strand, spanning 132579639–132586856. The separate ARV and HDH models are CAD6206930.1 / Ml01G065960.1 and CAD6206931.1 / Ml01G065970.1 on chromosome 1 (CAJGYO010000001.1). Their spans are 133423818–133426589 and 133427640–133431244, respectively: a 1,050-base intergenic interval. Thus physical adjacency is now established from deposited coordinates, rather than inferred from sequential locus numbers. It does not prove that the chromosome-2 model is an erroneous transcript.

The candidate's residues 1–472 align without gaps to residues 1–472 of the 478-aa separate HDH model: 460/472 identities (97.46%). The final six reference residues are outside the local alignment. Both deposited CDD annotations identify catalytic E368/H369 and zinc-site Q300/H303/D402/H461, and the actual candidate sequence retains these residues. This supports an HDH-like catalytic domain; it is not a direct activity measurement.

The ARV comparison contains 171 identities across 193 alignment columns (88.60%), covering candidate 475–663 and reference 17–206. The local alignment excludes the reference's first 16 residues, including cysteines in `MGDAQAAGEGPRCVGC`, and its last 24 residues. Inspect the saved alignment: its short beginning is weakly matched; the strong conserved block starts around the reference CDKC/KAV segment. The candidate's flanking sequence is divergent. These observations justify uncertainty about preservation of the complete ARV homology domain, rather than a claim that the ARV sequence is absent or nonfunctional. Crucially, the abstract of PMID:16725371 reports that removing the cysteine-rich subdomain did not impair Arabidopsis Arvp activity, whereas a C-terminal AHD subdomain was essential. That functional subdomain must not be equated with the unaligned protein tail without mapping it. Missing cysteines alone therefore do not establish ARV loss.

The immutable UniProt record's missing-conserved-residues warning cites **RU368065, the ARV1 rule**, not an HDH rule. The old review incorrectly used that warning to question HDH catalysis. The sequence comparison finds retained annotated HDH catalytic/zinc sites.

The independent HDH and ARV genes and the candidate architecture make a merged model plausible. Genuine fusion, alternative processing or targeting remain alternatives. No transcript spanning the fusion junction, fusion protein, organelle-localization experiment or target-specific catalytic assay has been established here. Therefore the broad membrane prediction and HDH domain-based functions can be assessed separately from unresolved ARV physiology and compartment assignments. Distinct domain functions and organelle destinations are not by themselves proof of a gene-model error. The old core assertion of sterol binding had no direct binding evidence and should not be retained as an established molecular function.
