CC   -!- FUNCTION: Membrane-bound E3 ubiquitin ligase expressed during late
CC       stages of lytic replication to mediate polyubiquitination of various
CC       host membrane proteins related to the immune response
CC       (PubMed:24899205). Promotes ubiquitination and subsequent degradation
CC       of host MHC-I and CD1D molecules, DC-SIGN and DC-SIGNR, presumably to
CC       prevent lysis of infected cells by cytotoxic T-lymphocytes
CC       (PubMed:10859362, PubMed:11756476). Binds target molecules through
CC       transmembrane interaction. E3 ubiquitin-protein ligases accept
CC       ubiquitin from specific E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzymes, and then
CC       transfer it to target protein. The result of this ubiquitination is the
CC       enhancement of the endocytosis of the target chain and the delivery to
CC       the lysosome, where it is proteolytically destroyed. Induces
CC       ubiquitination not only on lysines, but also on cysteine residues.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10799607, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10859362,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11756476, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15864354,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15994556, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24899205}.
