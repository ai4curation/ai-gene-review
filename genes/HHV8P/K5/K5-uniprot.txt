CC   -!- FUNCTION: Membrane-bound E3 ubiquitin ligase expressed at the immediate
CC       early stage of viral reactivation to mediate polyubiquitination of
CC       various host membrane proteins related to the immune response
CC       (PubMed:10799607, PubMed:10859362, PubMed:11413168, PubMed:24899205).
CC       Promotes ubiquitination and subsequent degradation of host MHC-I, CD86,
CC       DC-SIGN and DC-SIGNR, ICAM1 and CD1D molecules, presumably to prevent
CC       lysis of infected cells by cytotoxic T-lymphocytes and NK cell
CC       (PubMed:11413168, PubMed:23460925). Plays a role in the down-regulation
CC       of the host stress-induced NKG2D ligands MICA, MICB and CLEC2B, which
CC       enable immune cells expressing the NKG2D receptor to recognize and
CC       annihilate infected cells prior to viral spread (PubMed:18230726).
CC       Alters monocyte metabolism and proliferation by mediating rapid
CC       internalization of cellular growth factor-binding receptor tyrosine
CC       kinases from the surface leading to increased signaling
CC       (PubMed:21490960). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10799607,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10859362, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11413168,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11751860, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11756476,
