## Evidence

- Crystal structure (PDB 1E6V): active site almost identical to Methanothermobacter MCR,
  substrates bound [PMID:11023796 "The active sites of MCR from M. barkeri and M. kandleri
  were almost identical to that of M. thermoautotrophicum and predominantly occupied by
  coenzyme M and coenzyme B."]. Thermophilic adaptation via surface glutamates.
- mcr operon upstream of mtr [PMID:8863453 "As in other methanogens, the M. kandleri mcr
  operon is located immediately upstream of the mtrE gene"].
- Methanogenesis gene set shared with the CO2-reducers M. jannaschii and
  M. thermautotrophicus [PMID:11930014 "M. kandleri shares the set of genes implicated
  in methanogenesis"]; methane evolution measured under H2/CO2 (PMID:33674723 screen).
- Residue comparison: all 13 reference positions conserved; F430 ligand Gln150.
