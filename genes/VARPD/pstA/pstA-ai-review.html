Gene Description

Phosphate transport system permease protein PstA (VPARA_10460, A0A0H2M6I3) is a 294-amino-acid six-pass inner-membrane subunit of the high-affinity PstSCAB ABC orthophosphate importer in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6, encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000005 (EMBL KLN58034.1) between pstC (VPARA_10470) and pstB (VPARA_10450) within the phoR1-phoB1-phoU-pstB-pstA-pstC-pstS-ppx cluster (VPARA_10420-VPARA_10490). PstA comprises six transmembrane alpha-helices (residues 30-51, 79-102, 123-144, 150-169, 205-223, and 267-289) forming a MetI-like type-1 ABC transmembrane domain (PROSITE PS50928, Pfam PF00528, CDD cd06261, InterPro IPR005672, NCBIfam TIGR00974, NCBIfam NF008430, PANTHER PTHR42922:SF1) and conserves the essential transmembrane Arg220 and Glu224 residues (within 219-ARIAGETA-226, corresponding to E. coli PstA Arg220) required for orthophosphate translocation. PstA heterodimerizes with PstC in the plasma membrane to form the transmembrane phosphate translocation pore gated by periplasmic PstS and powered by the cytoplasmic PstB ATPase dimer.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Generate an Arg220Gln point mutation in VPARA_10460 (pstA) in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 and measure [32P]orthophosphate uptake rates and PhoB1 regulon transcription under high- and low-phosphate growth conditions.

Hypothesis: The Arg220Gln substitution abolishes high-affinity orthophosphate transport through PstSCAB while retaining PhoU-mediated repression of PhoR1-PhoB1 in phosphate-replete medium (PMID:2896188).

Curatorial Notes for pstA (A0A0H2M6I3, VPARA_10460) in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6

  • UniProt Accession: A0A0H2M6I3 (A0A0H2M6I3_VARPD)
  • Gene Symbol: pstA
  • Locus Tag / EMBL CDS: VPARA_10460 / KLN58034.1 on contig JZWI01000005 (RefSeq:WP_080966475.1)
  • Protein Length: 294 aa (31,555 Da); 6 transmembrane helices (30..51, 79..102, 123..144, 150..169, 205..223, 267..289) and an ABC transmembrane type-1 domain (PROSITE:PS50928 at 83..286, Pfam:PF00528 BPD_transp_1, CDD:cd06261 TM_PBP2).

2. Operon Context on Contig JZWI01000005

  • Encoded between pstB (VPARA_10450, KLN58033.1) and pstC (VPARA_10470, KLN58035.1) within the phoR1-phoB1-phoU-pstB-pstA-pstC-pstS-ppx (VPARA_10420–VPARA_10490) phosphate regulon gene cluster.

3. Literature & Comparative Evidence

  • PMID:2896188 (Cox et al. 1988 J Bacteriol): Site-directed mutagenesis demonstrates that Arg220 of E. coli PstA is essential for orthophosphate translocation through the Pst system.
  • PMID:3881386 (Surin et al. 1985 J Bacteriol): Complete sequence of the phoS-pstC-pstA-pstB-phoU operon.
  • PMID:24563032 (Gardner et al. 2014 J Bacteriol): Subunit architecture of PstSCAB and coupling to PhoU and PhoR.
