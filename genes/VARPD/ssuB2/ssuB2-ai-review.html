Gene Description

Aliphatic sulfonates import ATP-binding protein SsuB2 (VPARA_09230, A0A0H2MBB8) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 276-amino-acid peripheral inner-membrane ATPase subunit (InterPro IPR003439, PANTHER PTHR42788:SF17) of the SsuABC ATP-binding cassette (ABC) importer complex. Encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000005 (EMBL KLN57912.1) at the 5' end of the aliphatic sulfonate uptake and desulfonation gene cluster immediately upstream of the transmembrane permease ssuC5 (VPARA_09240, KLN57913.1) and the alkanesulfonate monooxygenase ssuD (VPARA_09250, KLN57914.1), and clustered with the periplasmic binding protein ssuA2 (VPARA_09270, KLN57916.1) and the FMN reductase ssuE (VPARA_09290, KLN57918.1), SsuB2 contains a conserved ABC nucleotide-binding domain (PROSITE PS50893, Pfam PF00005, residues 19-240) with a canonical Walker A P-loop ATP-binding motif at residues 51-58 (51-GRSGCGKS-58) that hydrolyzes ATP to energize transmembrane import of aliphatic sulfonates across the cytoplasmic membrane.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is SsuB2 (VPARA_09230) strictly dedicated to the co-localized SsuA2-SsuC5 translocon in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6, or can it also energize any of the paralogous SsuC permeases encoded elsewhere in the TBEA6 genome?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Construct a clean in-frame ΔssuB2 (VPARA_09230) deletion in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 and measure growth on C2-C8 linear alkanesulfonates and sulfonated buffers (HEPES, MOPS) as sole sulfur sources.

Hypothesis: Deletion of ssuB2 impairs growth on alkanesulfonates imported via the VPARA_09230-VPARA_09290 ssu cluster while leaving taurine utilization via TauAB1C intact.
