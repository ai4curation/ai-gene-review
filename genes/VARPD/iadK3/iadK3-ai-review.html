Gene Description

IadK3 (VPARA_32100) is a 381-amino-acid periplasmic subcluster B-III ABC transporter solute-binding protein (SBP) encoded immediately adjacent to iadK2 within the conserved indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) catabolic operon (iad) of Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 (Proteomes:UP000035170). While biophysical and structural comparisons in PMID:37459330 were performed on the orthologous V. paradoxus strain CL14 protein, direct sequence inspection confirms that TBEA6 IadK3 (UniProtKB A0A0H2LZ84) is the syntenic ortholog and carries the exact pocket substitutions that distinguish IadK3 from the high-affinity IAA receptor IadK2—specifically Val100 in GTSGVPGAM (residues 96-104, corresponding to Ser104 in IadK2) and Leu168 in AFIGFSDALGDL (residues 160-171, corresponding to Trp172 in IadK2). Consequently, purified CL14 IadK3 shows no detectable binding to IAA or tested natural and synthetic auxin analogs (4-Cl-IAA, IBA, IPA, NAA, 2,4-D, and PAA); a weak, unquantified association with 2-oxindole-3-acetic acid (oxIAA) was noted in PMID:37459330, and its physiological ligand remains unidentified.

Core Functions

Periplasmic subcluster B-III ABC transporter solute-binding protein encoded in the Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 iad operon that lacks IAA-binding affinity due to conserved pocket substitutions (Val100 and Leu168, corresponding to S104V and W172L of IadK2); its native physiological ligand remains to be established.

Suggested Experiments

Curation Notes: Variovorax paradoxus iadK3 (A0A0H2LZ84, VPARA_32100)

Summary

  • Gene symbol & strain attribution: iadK3 (locus tag VPARA_32100, 381 aa in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6, NCBITaxon:34073, Proteomes:UP000035170; syntenic ortholog of the experimentally characterized V. paradoxus strain CL14 iadK3 / IMG 2643613661).
  • Binding-pocket sequence verification (A0A0H2LZ84 vs A0A0H2MF78): Direct sequence inspection confirms that TBEA6 A0A0H2LZ84 (iadK3, 381 aa) carries Val100 within GTSGVPGAM (residues 96–104) and Phe164/Leu168 within AFIGFSDALGDL (residues 160–171), matching the S104V and W172L binding-pocket substitutions relative to IadK2 (Ser104 in GTAGSPGAL, residues 100–108, and Phe168/Trp172 in GYIGFSDAWGDL, residues 164–175 of A0A0H2MF78) that abolish IAA binding in strain CL14 (PMID:37459330).
  • Operon context: Second subcluster B-III periplasmic solute-binding protein (SBP) encoded immediately adjacent to iadK2 (VPARA_32110) in the Variovorax iad auxin-catabolism operon (PMID:37459330).
  • Experimental findings (PMID:37459330):
  • Shares ~70% sequence identity with IadK2, but diverges at key binding-pocket residues (Trp172, Ser104, Thr125, Asn126 in IadK2) and exhibits no detectable binding to IAA or tested auxin analogs (4-Cl-IAA, IBA, IPA, NAA, 2,4-D, PAA) [PMID:37459330, "Structural comparison revealed several residues located in the binding pocket in IadK2 are not conserved in IadK3, such as Trp172, Ser104, Thr125, and Asn126 in IadK2 (S3A, S3B and S3E Fig)."].
  • Weakly associates with 2-oxindole-3-acetic acid (oxIAA), the oxidized product of IadD/IadE-IadC, though its physiological ligand and role remain undefined [PMID:37459330, "IadK3 however show no binding with these auxin analogs. Interestingly, we found IadK3 could weakly associate with oxIAA. The detailed roles of IadK3 and IadK2 in the Variovorax auxin degradation pathway, however, remain to be further characterized and defined."].
  • Annotation review:
  • MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED GO:0006865 (amino acid transport, IEA from InterPro:IPR000709 Leu_Ile_Val-bd / PR00337, matched alongside PANTHER:PTHR30483 LEUCINE-SPECIFIC-BINDING PROTEIN within the InterPro:IPR028082 Peripla_BP_I superfamily): IadK3 is a subcluster B-III aromatic-acid-type SBP in the iad operon rather than a canonical branched-chain amino-acid transporter, but amino-acid binding has not been directly tested and its physiological ligand remains unknown.
  • Omit NEW GO:0031406 (carboxylic acid binding) because the weak association with oxIAA is preliminary and unquantified; retain NEW GO:0042597 (periplasmic space, IC, PMID:37459330 and UniProtKB:A0A0H2LZ84).
