Gene Description

CzcA3 (VPARA_44890) in Variovorax paradoxus is a 1067-amino-acid predicted inner-membrane Heavy-Metal Efflux Resistance-Nodulation-Division (HME-RND) transporter subunit (NCBIfam TIGR00914, InterPro IPR004763, PANTHER PTHR32063:SF24) containing 12 transmembrane alpha-helices and two large periplasmic porter loops. Based on HME-RND family membership, CzcA3 functions as the inner-membrane proton/cation antiporter component that partners with a periplasmic membrane-fusion adaptor protein and an outer-membrane factor channel to drive transmembrane export of monoatomic metal cations.

Curation Notes for Variovorax paradoxus czcA3 (A0A0H2MBF7, VPARA_44890)

Overview

  • UniProt Accession: A0A0H2MBF7 (A0A0H2MBF7_VARPD)
  • Ordered Locus Name: VPARA_44890
  • Gene Symbol: czcA3
  • Organism: Variovorax paradoxus (NCBITaxon:34073, strain B4)
  • Protein Function: Inner-membrane proton/cation antiporter subunit (CzcA3, 1,067 aa) of the heavy-metal efflux resistance-nodulation-division (HME-RND) tripartite efflux pump (CzcCBA), which couples the proton-motive force across the cytoplasmic membrane to the active extrusion of toxic divalent transition metal cations ($\text{Co}^{2+}$, $\text{Zn}^{2+}$, $\text{Cd}^{2+}$) from the cytoplasm and periplasm to the extracellular medium.

Domain Architecture & Subfamily Classification (UniProtKB:A0A0H2MBF7)

  1. 12-Transmembrane HME-RND Pump Topology:
  2. Contains 12 transmembrane $\alpha$-helices (FT TRANSMEM spanning residues 10–30, 341–363, 367–385, 396–418, 441–463, 475–498, 539–559, 875–895, 902–921, 926–947, 976–998, 1007–1029) and large periplasmic pore/TolC-docking loops (Pfam:PF00873 ACR_tran, InterPro:IPR001036, InterPro:IPR027463).
  3. Specific Membership in the Heavy-Metal Efflux (HME-RND) CusA/CzcA Subfamily (InterPro:IPR004763, NCBIFAM:TIGR00914):
  4. Crucially, A0A0H2MBF7 matches NCBIFAM:TIGR00914 (2A0601: heavy metal efflux pump, CzcA family) and InterPro:IPR004763 (Efflux pump, RND family, CusA/CzcA subfamily), distinguishing it from hydrophobe/amphiphile (HAE-RND, AcrB/MexB) multidrug/xenobiotic efflux pumps.
  5. Annotated in the Variovorax paradoxus B4 genome as Cobalt-zinc-cadmium resistance protein CzcA; Metal cation efflux system protein (gene: czcA3, VPARA_44890).
