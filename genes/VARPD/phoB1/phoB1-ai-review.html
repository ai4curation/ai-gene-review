Gene Description

Phosphate regulon transcriptional regulatory protein PhoB1 (VPARA_10430, A0A0H2M7F4) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 230-amino-acid cytosolic OmpR/PhoB-family two-component response regulator (InterPro IPR011879, NCBIfam TIGR02154, PANTHER PTHR48111:SF40) encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000005 (EMBL KLN58031.1) between its cognate sensor histidine kinase phoR1 (VPARA_10420) and phoU (VPARA_10440) within the phoR1-phoB1-phoU-pstB-pstA-pstC-pstS-ppx cluster (VPARA_10420-VPARA_10490). PhoB1 comprises an N-terminal CheY-like receiver domain (PROSITE PS50110, Pfam PF00072, CDD cd17618, residues 5-121) containing the invariant aspartyl-phosphate acceptor Asp54 (50-LILLDWMLPGQSG-62) and a C-terminal winged-helix DNA-binding effector domain (PROSITE PS51755, Pfam PF00486, CDD cd00383, residues 130-226). Under inorganic phosphate limitation, phosphorylation of Asp54 by PhoR1 induces PhoB1 homodimerization and binding to Pho-box cis-regulatory sequences upstream of Pho regulon promoters to activate transcription of high-affinity phosphate acquisition and starvation response genes.

Core Functions

Two-component phosphorelay response regulator that is phosphorylated at Asp54 by PhoR1 under inorganic phosphate limitation, homodimerizes, and binds Pho-box cis-regulatory promoter elements via its C-terminal OmpR/PhoB-type winged-helix domain to positively regulate transcription of the phosphate starvation regulon.
