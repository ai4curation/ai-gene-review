Gene Description

Taurine transport system permease protein TauC (VPARA_03670, A0A0H2M7Y1) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 294-amino-acid multi-pass integral inner-membrane subunit (InterPro IPR000515, PANTHER PTHR30151:SF25) of the TauABC ATP-binding cassette (ABC) taurine importer complex. Encoded at the 3' end of the syntenic tauA-tauB1-tauC (VPARA_03650-VPARA_03670) operon on genomic contig JZWI01000003 (consecutive EMBL CDS accessions KLN58254.1-KLN58256.1) immediately downstream of the periplasmic binding protein tauA (VPARA_03650, KLN58254.1) and the ATPase subunit tauB1 (VPARA_03660, KLN58255.1), TauC contains six predicted transmembrane alpha-helices (residues 42-62, 100-121, 141-160, 166-185, 221-241, and 261-283) and a conserved ABC transmembrane type-1 domain (PROSITE PS50928, Pfam PF00528, residues 100-280) that forms the membrane translocation pathway for importing taurine into the cytoplasm.

Core Functions

Integral inner-membrane transmembrane permease subunit of the TauAB1C ABC importer complex that forms the membrane translocation pathway for importing taurine from the periplasmic binding protein TauA into the cytoplasm coupled to ATP hydrolysis by TauB1.
