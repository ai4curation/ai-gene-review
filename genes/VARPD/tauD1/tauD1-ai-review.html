Gene Description

TauD1 (VPARA_23370) is a 302-amino-acid cytosolic Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate (alpha-ketoglutarate)-dependent taurine dioxygenase (EC 1.14.11.17) in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6. Belonging to the TauD/TfdA non-heme iron oxygenase family (Pfam PF02668, residues 23-294), TauD1 catalyzes the oxygenolytic desulfonation of taurine (2-aminoethanesulfonate) in the presence of O2 and 2-oxoglutarate to yield sulfite, aminoacetaldehyde, succinate, and CO2. In the rhizosphere, where inorganic sulfate is frequently immobilized in organosulfur esters and sulfonates, TauD1 complements the FMNH2-dependent alkanesulfonate monooxygenase SsuD (VPARA_19220) to liberate assimilable sulfite for bacterial and plant sulfur nutrition.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: How are tauD1 (VPARA_23370) and the ssuEADCB alkanesulfonate monooxygenase system (ssuD / VPARA_19220) differentially regulated in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 during growth on taurine, aliphatic sulfonates, and plant root exudates under sulfate starvation?

Suggested experts: Kertesz MA, Steinbuechel A, Hausinger RP

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Purify recombinant His-tagged V. paradoxus TauD1 (VPARA_23370) and measure steady-state Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate-dependent sulfite release across taurine, isethionate, MOPS, and pentanesulfonate, alongside growth phenotyping of single ΔtauD1, ΔssuD, and double ΔtauD1 ΔssuD mutants on organosulfonates as sole sulfur source.

Hypothesis: TauD1 exhibits highest catalytic efficiency toward taurine whereas SsuD processes broader aliphatic sulfonates, and together they provide dual oxygenolytic desulfonation routes in the rhizosphere.

Curation Notes: tauD1 (A0A0H2MHV8 / VPARA_23370) in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6

1. Gene & Protein Identity

  • UniProt Accession: A0A0H2MHV8 (A0A0H2MHV8_VARPD, unreviewed TrEMBL, 302 aa, EC 1.14.11.17)
  • Gene Symbol / Locus Tag: tauD1 / VPARA_23370
  • Organism: Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 (NCBITaxon:34073)
  • Sequence & Active-Site Architecture:
  • Full-length TauD/TfdA-like $\text{Fe(II)}$/$\alpha$-ketoglutarate-dependent dioxygenase domain (Pfam:PF02668, residues 23–294; InterPro:IPR003819, PANTHER:PTHR30468:SF1) [UniProtKB:A0A0H2MHV8, "DE SubName: Full=Alpha-ketoglutarate-dependent taurine dioxygenase {ECO:0000313|EMBL:KLN56395.1};"].
  • Conserves the canonical mononuclear non-heme $\text{Fe(II)}$ 2-His-1-carboxylate facial triad (WHTD motif at residues 105–108: His106 and Asp108, plus distal His262 in HFTNFH at residues 257–262) and the 2-oxoglutarate/sulfonate-binding Arg residues.

2. Pathway Context & Module Linkage

  • Existing Reusable Module: modules/taurine_uptake_and_desulfonation.yaml (MODULE:taurine_uptake_and_desulfonation).
  • Complementary Rhizosphere Organosulfonate Module: Complements the $\text{FMNH}_2$-dependent alkanesulfonate monooxygenase ssuD (VPARA_19220, curated in Tranche 1 and linked to modules/bacterial_alkanesulfonate_uptake_desulfonation.yaml). Together, TauD1 ($\alpha$-ketoglutarate/$\text{Fe(II)}$-dependent dioxygenase) and SsuD/SsuE ($\text{FMNH}_2$-dependent two-component monooxygenase) equip Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 to mineralize both taurine and diverse aliphatic sulfonates in sulfate-depleted rhizosphere soils [PMID:9287300, "The Escherichia coli tauD gene is required for the utilization of taurine (2-aminoethanesulfonic acid) as a sulfur source and is expressed only under conditions of sulfate starvation."].
