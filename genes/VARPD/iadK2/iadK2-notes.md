# Curation Notes: Variovorax paradoxus `iadK2` (`A0A0H2MF78`, `VPARA_32110`)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol & strain attribution**: `iadK2` (locus tag `VPARA_32110`, 388 aa in *Variovorax paradoxus* strain **TBEA6**, `NCBITaxon:34073`, `Proteomes:UP000035170`; syntenic ortholog of the experimentally characterized *V. paradoxus* strain **CL14** `iadK2` / IMG `2643613662`).
- **Binding-pocket sequence verification (`A0A0H2MF78` vs `A0A0H2LZ84`)**: Direct sequence inspection confirms that TBEA6 `A0A0H2MF78` (`iadK2`, 388 aa) retains the exact IAA-binding pocket residues identified in CL14 `IadK2` (`PMID:37459330`)—specifically `Ser104` in `GTAGSPGAL` (residues 100–108), `Phe168`, and `Trp172` in `GYIGFSDAWGDL` (residues 164–175), as well as `Thr125`, `Asn126`, `Arg199`, `Phe308`, and `Tyr311`—whereas the adjacent TBEA6 paralog `A0A0H2LZ84` (`iadK3`, `VPARA_32100`, 381 aa) carries the IAA-non-binding substitutions `Val100` in `GTSGVPGAM` (residues 96–104) and `Leu168` in `AFIGFSDALGDL` (residues 160–171), matching the `S104V` and `W172L` substitutions characterized in CL14.
- **Operon context**: Encoded within the conserved *Variovorax* `iad` indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) catabolic operon alongside the paralogous SBP `iadK3` (`VPARA_32100`) and ABC transporter subunits `iadG`/`iadH`/`iadJ` (`PMID:32999461`, `PMID:36266335`, `PMID:37459330`).
- **Structural & biophysical evidence (`PMID:37459330`)**:
  - In *V. paradoxus* CL14, purified `IadK2` binds IAA with nanomolar affinity by isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC) and shows strong thermal stabilization in thermal shift assays (TSA) [PMID:37459330, "IadK2 could engage IAA with nanomolar affinity, whereas no obvious binding was detected for IadK3 and IAA (Figs 1C and S1B)."].
  - The 2.3 A crystal structure of `IadK2` reported in `PMID:37459330` reveals a two-lobed subcluster B-III periplasmic solute-binding protein architecture with three hinge linkers (L1–L3) containing two alpha-helices in L2 [PMID:37459330, "The hinge region comprises 3 linkers (L1–L3) with 2 α-helices in linker L2, resembling SBPs in the subcluster B-III (S3A–S3C Fig), which are usually associated with type I ABC transporters for aromatic acids uptake [19,20]."].
  - Docking, MD simulations, SAXS, and mutagenesis of `Ser104`, `Phe168`, `Trp172`, `Arg199`, `Phe308`, and `Tyr311` validate the interdomain IAA-binding pocket, and the double substitution `W172L/S104V` (introducing the corresponding `IadK3` residues) completely abolishes IAA binding [PMID:37459330, "Notably, the double mutation replacing both Trp172 and Ser104 with those of IadK3 (W172L/S104V) completely abrogated the binding of IAA."].
  - While `IadK2` facilitates IAA uptake in *Variovorax*, it was dispensable for IAA transformation when the `iad` pathway was engineered in *Escherichia coli* [PMID:37459330, "In addition to the IadD/E-IadC proteins, we also found IadK2 may also contribute to the IAA-degradation pathway of Variovorax by facilitating IAA transportation, although it was found dispensable for E. coli (Fig 6B)."].
- **Annotation review**:
  - `NEW` `GO:0010011` (`auxin binding`, `ISS` from `PMID:37459330` and `UniProtKB:A0A0H2MF78`, reflecting orthology between TBEA6 `A0A0H2MF78` and the experimentally assayed CL14 `IadK2`), `GO:0015718` (`monocarboxylic acid transport`, `IC`), and `GO:0042597` (`periplasmic space`, `IC`). Verification via QuickGO (`/ontology/go/terms/GO:0010011,GO:0060918,GO:0015718/constraints`, retrieved 2026-11-11) confirms that `GO:0010011` (`auxin binding`) and `GO:0015718` (`monocarboxylic acid transport`) carry no taxon constraints, whereas `GO:0060918` (`auxin transport`) carries `only_in_taxon NCBITaxon:33090` (`Viridiplantae`).
