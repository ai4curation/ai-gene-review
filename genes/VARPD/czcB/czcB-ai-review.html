Gene Description

Cobalt-zinc-cadmium resistance protein CzcB (VPARA_44900) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 455-amino-acid periplasmic membrane-fusion protein (MFP) adaptor subunit (NCBIfam TIGR01730, InterPro IPR006143) that bridges the inner-membrane HME-RND cation-proton antiporter CzcA3 (VPARA_44890) and the outer-membrane factor channel CzcC (VPARA_44910) across the periplasmic space to assemble the tripartite CzcCBA heavy-metal efflux pump. Anchored to the inner membrane by an N-terminal transmembrane helix (residues 18-36), CzcB extends into the periplasm via a histidine- and carboxylate-rich disordered region (residues 41-121, containing 10 histidine residues) followed by four canonical CzcB/CusB adaptor domains — a barrel-sandwich hybrid domain (Pfam PF25973, residues 155-298) with an embedded alpha-helical hairpin (Pfam PF25893, residues 194-253), a CusB-like beta-barrel domain (Pfam PF25954, residues 301-376), and a C-terminal circularly permuted SH3-like domain (Pfam PF25975, residues 383-443) — that physically couple CzcA3 and CzcC during trans-envelope export of monoatomic metal cations.

Core Functions

Periplasmic membrane-fusion structural adaptor subunit of the tripartite CzcCBA heavy-metal efflux complex that physically bridges the inner-membrane RND antiporter CzcA3 (VPARA_44890) and the outer-membrane channel CzcC (VPARA_44910) to contribute to transmembrane metal cation export.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does the histidine-rich N-terminal periplasmic region (residues 41-121, containing 10 histidine residues) of Variovorax paradoxus CzcB (VPARA_44900) deliver periplasmic Zn2+/Co2+/Cd2+ ions directly to the CzcA3 porter domain or act as an allosteric metal sensor that activates the CzcCBA pump?

Suggested experts: Nies DH, Su CC, Yu EW

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Compare heavy-metal minimum inhibitory concentrations (Zn2+, Co2+, Cd2+, Ni2+, Cu2+) of wild-type Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6, a ΔczcB (VPARA_44900) deletion mutant, and a czcB variant lacking the histidine-rich periplasmic segment (Δ45-121).

Hypothesis: Deletion of czcB abolishes CzcCBA-mediated divalent metal resistance, while truncation of the histidine-rich segment (residues 45-121) impairs allosteric activation or low-concentration periplasmic metal scavenging.

  • Genomic Context: Middle gene of the syntenic tripartite HME-RND heavy-metal efflux operon czcA3-czcB-czcC (VPARA_44890–VPARA_44910) on contig JZWI01000024:
  • VPARA_44890 (czcA3, A0A0H2MBF7, 1,067 aa): inner-membrane HME-RND proton/cation antiporter
