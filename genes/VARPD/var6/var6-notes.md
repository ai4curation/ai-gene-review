# Curation Notes: var6 (A0A0N9LX43) in Variovorax paradoxus

## Overview
- **UniProt ID:** `A0A0N9LX43` (`A0A0N9LX43_VARPD`, 2,583 aa)
- **Gene symbol / GenBank protein:** `var6` (`ALG65341.1`, `EMBL:KT362218`)
- **Organism:** *Variovorax paradoxus* strain P4B (`NCBITaxon:34073`)
- **Function:** Bimodular nonribosomal peptide synthetase (NRPS, `GO:1904091`) in the variobactin/variochelin (`var`) hybrid PKS/NRPS lipopeptide siderophore biosynthetic gene cluster.

## Domain Architecture & Biosynthetic Role
- **Domain organization:** Three condensation/epimerization domains (`PF00668`: `LCL_NRPS-like` `cd19531`, `E_NRPS` `cd19534`, `DCL_NRPS` `cd19543`), two ATP-dependent amino acid adenylation domains (`PF00501` / `PF13193` / `IPR010071`), and two 4'-phosphopantetheinylated peptidyl-carrier protein (PCP) domains (`PF00550` / `PROSITE:PS50075` at residues 981-1055 and 2483-2557, modified at Ser1016 and Ser2517).
- **Evidence:** Johnston et al. 2015 ([PMID:26412281 "A second Variovorax isolate (V. paradoxus P4B (ref. 32)) was found to possess another distinct NRPS/PKS gene cluster, corresponding to a putative lipopeptide (Fig. 2b, Supplementary Fig. 17a and Supplementary Table 7)."]) sequenced the *V. paradoxus* P4B `var` cluster (`GenBank:KT362218`) and isolated variobactin A and B. Comparative characterization of the orthologous *Variovorax* `var` cluster in *V. boronicumulans* ([PMID:27023373]) and *Variovorax* sp. H002 ([PMID:38590537 "The absolute configurations of the amino acid constituents, Pro, Ser and two modified ornithine residues, in 3–5 were assigned as ʟ, ᴅ, and ʟ, respectively (Figures S25, S33, and S41, Supporting Information File 1)."]) established that the orthologous bimodular NRPS incorporates and epimerizes D-Ser and L-Pro in variochelins.
