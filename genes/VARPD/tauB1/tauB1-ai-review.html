Gene Description

Taurine import ATP-binding protein TauB1 (VPARA_03660, A0A0H2MNF8) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 262-amino-acid peripheral inner-membrane ATPase subunit (InterPro IPR003439, PANTHER PTHR42788:SF18) of the TauABC ATP-binding cassette (ABC) taurine importer complex. Encoded as the middle gene of the syntenic tauA-tauB1-tauC (VPARA_03650-VPARA_03670) operon on genomic contig JZWI01000003 (consecutive EMBL CDS accessions KLN58254.1-KLN58256.1) immediately between the periplasmic taurine-binding protein tauA (VPARA_03650, KLN58254.1) and the transmembrane permease tauC (VPARA_03670, KLN58256.1), TauB1 contains an ABC nucleotide-binding domain (PROSITE PS50893, Pfam PF00005, residues 6-235) with a conserved Walker A P-loop ATP-binding motif at residues 40-47 (40-GASGCGKT-47) that hydrolyzes ATP to drive active import of taurine across the cytoplasmic membrane.
