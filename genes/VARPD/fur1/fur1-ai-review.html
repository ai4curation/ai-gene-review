Gene Description

Ferric uptake regulation protein Fur1 (VPARA_18200, A0A0H2M8T9; synonym fur) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 139-amino-acid cytosolic homodimeric metalloregulator of the Fur family (NCBIfam NF006999, Pfam PF01475, InterPro IPR002481, PANTHER PTHR33202:SF2) encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000008 (EMBL KLN57112.1). Fur1 comprises an N-terminal winged-helix DNA-binding domain (Gene3D 1.10.10.10, InterPro IPR036388) and a C-terminal dimerization and metal-binding domain (InterPro IPR043135) housing two distinct metal-coordination sites per monomer: a structural Zn(2+) site coordinated by four conserved cysteines at Cys92, Cys95 (92-CTSC-95), Cys132, and Cys135 (132-CGDC-135; PIRSR PIRSR602481-1), and a regulatory Fe(2+) (or Mn(2+)) sensing site coordinated by His86, Asp88 (within 85-THHDH-89), Glu107, and His124 (PIRSR PIRSR602481-2). Under iron-replete conditions, Fe(2+)-loaded holo-Fur1 binds conserved Fur-box operator sequences in target promoters and acts as a transcriptional repressor of iron-acquisition and siderophore biosynthesis/import genes, whereas iron limitation demetallates the regulatory site and derepresses siderophore-mediated iron uptake.

2. Literature & Comparative Evidence

  • PMID:8522528(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8522528/) (Ochsner, Vasil & Vasil 1995, J Bacteriol 177:7194-7201): Purified Pseudomonas aeruginosa Fur and demonstrated by gel mobility shift and DNase I footprinting that holo-Fur-$\text{Fe(II)}$ specifically binds conserved 19-bp "Fur box" operator sequences (GATAATGATAATCATTATC) overlapping iron-regulated siderophore promoters (pvdS, pchR, fepA-fes) to repress transcription under iron-replete conditions.
  • PMID:12581348(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12581348/) (Pohl et al. 2003, Mol Microbiol 47:903-915): Solved the 1.8-Å crystal structure and X-ray absorption spectroscopy of Fur, characterizing the distinct structural $\text{Zn}^{2+}$ site and regulatory $\text{Fe}^{2+}$-sensing site that couple intracellular iron occupancy to operator DNA binding.
  • PMID:20562273(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20562273/) (Chen, Bleam & Hickey 2010, Appl Environ Microbiol 76:5620-5630): Demonstrated in Pseudomonas putida KT2440 that a $\Delta fur$ deletion mutant shows altered expression of iron-homeostasis genes and increased $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ sensitivity.
