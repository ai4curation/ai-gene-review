Gene Description

Periplasmic aliphatic sulfonate-binding protein SsuA2 (VPARA_09270, A0A0H2M784) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 319-amino-acid substrate-recognition subunit (NCBIfam TIGR01728, InterPro IPR010067, PANTHER PTHR30024:SF42) of the SsuABC ATP-binding cassette (ABC) importer complex. Encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000005 (EMBL KLN57916.1) within the aliphatic sulfonate uptake and desulfonation gene cluster alongside the ATPase ssuB2 (VPARA_09230, KLN57912.1), the transmembrane permease ssuC5 (VPARA_09240, KLN57913.1), the alkanesulfonate monooxygenase ssuD (VPARA_09250, KLN57914.1), and the FMN reductase ssuE (VPARA_09290, KLN57918.1), SsuA2 carries an N-terminal twin-arginine translocation (Tat) signal peptide (PROSITE PS51318, residues 1-29, containing the Arg8-Arg9-Arg10 motif) that directs export of the mature periplasmic solute-binding domain (residues 30-319) to capture extracellular aliphatic sulfonates and present them to the inner-membrane SsuC5-SsuB2 translocon.

Core Functions

Periplasmic substrate-binding subunit of the SsuA2B2C5 ABC importer that captures extracellular aliphatic sulfonates in the periplasm and delivers them to the SsuC5 transmembrane permease for ATP-driven import into the cytoplasm.

2. Literature & Comparative Evidence

  • PMID:10781534(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10781534/) (Eichhorn, van der Ploeg & Leisinger 2000, J Bacteriol 182:2687-2695): Deletion and complementation analysis of the Escherichia coli tauABCD and ssuEADCB gene clusters established that SsuABC forms a dedicated ABC-type uptake system for aliphatic sulfonates that is functionally distinct from and non-exchangeable with TauABC ("the individual components of the two transport systems were not functionally exchangeable").
  • PMID:10781557(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10781557/) (Kahnert et al. 2000, J Bacteriol 182:2869-2878): Characterized the ssuEADCBF operon in Pseudomonas putida S-313, identifying ssuA, ssuB, and ssuC as the periplasmic binding, ATP-binding, and membrane permease subunits of the ABC-type sulfonate importer supplying the SsuED monooxygenase system ("The first group consists of ssuA , ssuB , and ssuC , which are the genes for a putative ATP-binding cassette (ABC)-type transport system.").
