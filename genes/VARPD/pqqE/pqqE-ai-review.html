Gene Description

PqqE (PqqA peptide cyclase, VPARA_00920, A0A0H2MPV4; also annotated as moaA1) in Variovorax paradoxus is a 383-amino-acid radical S-adenosyl-L-methionine (SAM) SPASM-domain metalloenzyme (EC 1.21.98.4, RHEA:56836; HAMAP MF_00660, InterPro IPR011843, NCBIfam TIGR02109, PANTHER PTHR11228:SF7) encoded at the 3' end of the pqqBCDE (VPARA_00950-VPARA_00920) gene cluster. Using a radical SAM [4Fe-4S] cluster coordinated by Cys22, Cys26, and Cys29 in its N-terminal core domain (residues 8-223) together with auxiliary iron-sulfur clusters in its C-terminal SPASM domain, PqqE reductively cleaves SAM to generate a 5'-deoxyadenosyl radical and catalyzes the first committed step of pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ) biosynthesis: formation of an intramolecular carbon-carbon cross-link between the conserved glutamate and tyrosine side chains of the PqqA precursor peptide presented by the peptide chaperone PqqD.

Core Functions

Radical SAM/SPASM-domain PqqA peptide cyclase (EC 1.21.98.4, RHEA:56836) that catalyzes the initiating committed step of pyrroloquinoline quinone biosynthesis by forming an intramolecular carbon-carbon cross-link between the conserved glutamate and tyrosine residues of PqqA in a PqqD-dependent ternary complex.
