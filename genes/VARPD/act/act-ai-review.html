Gene Description

Act (Act(TBEA6), VPARA_05450, KLN58430.1) is a 398-amino-acid cytosolic homodimeric (96 +/- 3 kDa) succinyl-CoA:3-sulfinopropionate CoA-transferase (EC 2.8.3.-; family III CoA-transferase Pfam PF02515, InterPro IPR003673) characterized in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6. Encoded immediately upstream of the 3-sulfinopropanoyl-CoA desulfinase gene acd (B9U6P5, VPARA_05440), Act catalyzes the reversible transfer of CoA from succinyl-CoA (apparent Vmax = 44.6 umol min^-1 mg^-1, Km = 0.08 mM; kcat = 36.0 s^-1), itaconyl-CoA, or glutaryl-CoA to 3-sulfinopropionate (3SP; apparent Vmax = 46.8 umol min^-1 mg^-1, Km = 5.9 mM; kcat = 37.7 s^-1) to form 3-sulfinopropanoyl-CoA (3SP-CoA) during the catabolism of 3,3'-thiodipropionate (TDP) and 3-sulfinopropionate.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

succinyl-CoA:3-sulfinopropionate CoA-transferase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: succinyl-CoA + 3-sulfinopropanoate = succinate + 3-sulfinopropanoyl-CoA (EC 2.8.3.-).

Justification: Purified and kinetically characterized in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 Act(TBEA6) (UniProtKB:B9U6P4; PMID:23772073), where it catalyzes the physiological activation of 3-sulfinopropionate to 3-sulfinopropanoyl-CoA during 3,3'-thiodipropionate degradation, but lacks a specific child term under GO:0008410 (CoA-transferase activity).

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:23772073 : Analytical size exclusion chromatography revealed a homodimeric structure with a molecular mass of 96 ± 3 kDa. Enzyme assays identified succinyl-CoA, itaconyl-CoA, and glutaryl-CoA as potential CoA donors and unequivocally verified the conversion of 3SP to 3SP-CoA. Kinetic studies revealed an apparent V(max) of 44.6 μmol min(-1) mg(-1) for succinyl-CoA, which corresponds to a turnover number of 36.0 s(-1) per subunit of Act(TBEA6). For 3SP, the apparent V(max) was determined as 46.8 μmol min(-1) mg(-1), which corresponds to a turnover number of 37.7 s(-1) per subunit of Act(TBEA6). The apparent K(m) values were 0.08 mM for succinyl-CoA and 5.9 mM for 3SP.

Curation Notes: act (B9U6P4) in Variovorax paradoxus

Overview

  • UniProt ID: B9U6P4 (B9U6P4_VARPD, 398 aa)
  • Gene symbol / locus tag: act (VPARA_05450, Act(TBEA6), KLN58430.1)
  • Organism: Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 (NCBITaxon:34073)
  • Function: Cytosolic homodimeric succinyl-CoA:3-sulfinopropionate CoA-transferase (EC 2.8.3.-, family III CoA-transferase Pfam:PF02515 / InterPro:IPR003673).

Experimental Characterization in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6

  • Direct purification and steady-state kinetics (PMID:23772073): Schürmann et al. 2013 (PMID:23772073) heterologously expressed and purified Act(TBEA6) (B9U6P4) as a 96 ± 3 kDa homodimer. It transfers CoA from succinyl-CoA ($V_{\max} = 44.6\ \mu\text{mol min}^{-1}\text{ mg}^{-1}$, $K_m = 0.08\ \text{mM}$, $k_{\text{cat}} = 36.0\ \text{s}^{-1}$), itaconyl-CoA, or glutaryl-CoA to 3-sulfinopropionate ($V_{\max} = 46.8\ \mu\text{mol min}^{-1}\text{ mg}^{-1}$, $K_m = 5.9\ \text{mM}$, $k_{\text{cat}} = 37.7\ \text{s}^{-1}$) to produce 3-sulfinopropanoyl-CoA (3SP-CoA).
  • Operon context and polar effect on acd (B9U6P5): Bruland et al. 2009 (PMID:19001372) isolated a Tn5::mob mutant in act that accumulated 3SP and failed to grow on 3SP. Schürmann et al. 2013 (PMID:23772073) showed that an unmarked $\Delta act$ deletion mutant retained growth on TDP and 3SP (indicating an alternative 3SP-activating enzyme in strain TBEA6), whereas the Tn5::mob insertion exerted a polar effect on the downstream 3SP-CoA desulfinase gene acd (B9U6P5, PMID:24317404).
  • Removal of spurious GO:0033877 (EC:2.8.3.15): B9U6P4 was automatically annotated with EC:2.8.3.15 (succinyl-CoA:(R)-benzylsuccinate CoA-transferase activity, GO:0033877) in the WGS submission KLN58430.1. PMID:23772073 directly refutes this annotation and establishes Act(TBEA6) as a homodimeric succinyl-CoA:3-sulfinopropionate CoA-transferase (GO:0008410).
