Gene Description

P1B-1-type Cu(+)-translocating efflux ATPase CopA2 (VPARA_21930, A0A0H2M2H3; EC 7.2.2.8) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 747-amino-acid eight-pass inner-membrane heavy-metal P-type ATPase (NCBIfam TIGR01511 ATPase-IB1_Cu, InterPro IPR027256, PANTHER PTHR43520:SF8, FunFam 2.70.150.10:FF:000020) encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000010 (EMBL KLN56614.1). CopA2 comprises an N-terminal cytoplasmic heavy-metal-associated (HMA) regulatory domain (PROSITE PS50846, Pfam PF00403, CDD cd00371, residues 13-80) housing the conserved Cu(+)-binding 24-MTCASCV-30 CXXC motif, eight RuleBase-annotated transmembrane alpha-helices (residues 98-116, 128-147, 159-181, 193-212, 349-371, 377-410, 700-719, and 725-742) with the intramembrane 379-CPCALGLATP-388 (Cys379-Pro380-Cys381) Cu(+) translocation site in TM6, a cytoplasmic actuator (A) domain (Pfam PF00122, with the 269-TGES-272 phosphatase loop), and a haloacid dehalogenase (HAD)-like catalytic phosphorylation (P) and nucleotide-binding (N) domain (Pfam PF00702, PROSITE PS00154, InterPro IPR023299) containing the invariant acyl-phosphate acceptor Asp423 (within 423-DKTGTLT-429) and Mg(2+)-binding hinge (615-GDGVNDAPALA-625). CopA2 couples ATP hydrolysis to active export of monovalent copper (Cu(+)) across the bacterial plasma membrane into the periplasm to maintain cellular copper homeostasis.

Core Functions

Inner-membrane P1B-1-type Cu(+)-translocating efflux ATPase (EC 7.2.2.8) that couples ATP hydrolysis at Asp423 (423-DKTGTLT-429) to active export of cytoplasmic Cu(+) into the periplasm via its N-terminal HMA domain (24-MTCASCV-30) and TM6 379-CPC-381 translocation site to maintain cellular copper ion homeostasis.

Suggested Experiments
