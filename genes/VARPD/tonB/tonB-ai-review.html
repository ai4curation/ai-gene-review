Gene Description

TonB (VPARA_25820) is a 232-amino-acid inner-membrane proton-motive-force energy transducer in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 encoded immediately adjacent to the outer-membrane TonB-dependent ferric-siderophore receptor bfrD1 (VPARA_25830). Anchored in the cytoplasmic membrane by a single N-terminal transmembrane helix (residues 18-35) within the TonB-ExbB-ExbD complex, TonB extends across the periplasmic space via a proline-rich linker (residues 58-143) so that its globular C-terminal domain (PROSITE PS52015, Pfam PF03544, residues 140-232) engages the periplasmic TonB box of ligand-loaded outer-membrane TonB-dependent receptors, mechanically coupling the inner-membrane proton gradient to active import of ferric-siderophore complexes across the outer membrane.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does VPARA_25820 (tonB) specifically energize only the adjacent BfrD1 (VPARA_25830) receptor in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6, or does it serve as a general energy transducer for multiple outer-membrane TonB-dependent siderophore and metallophore receptors encoded elsewhere in the genome?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Construct a ΔtonB (VPARA_25820) deletion mutant in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 and compare its growth under iron starvation with a ΔbfrD1 (VPARA_25830) mutant in the presence of endogenous variobactin and exogenous xenosiderophores.

Hypothesis: Deletion of VPARA_25820 abolishes BfrD1-dependent ferric-siderophore import and reveals whether parallel TonB homologs in V. paradoxus can partially compensate.
