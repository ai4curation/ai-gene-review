Gene Description

BfrD1 (VPARA_25830) is a 739-amino-acid outer-membrane TonB-dependent ferric-siderophore receptor in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 encoded immediately downstream of the inner-membrane energy transducer tonB (VPARA_25820). The precursor contains an N-terminal signal peptide (residues 1-17), a periplasm-facing N-terminal plug (cork) domain (Pfam PF07715, residues 60-159) with a conserved TonB box motif, and a 22-stranded outer-membrane beta-barrel domain (Pfam PF00593, residues 265-709). Upon binding extracellular ferric-siderophore or photoreactive lipopeptide complexes such as variobactin at the cell surface, BfrD1 interacts via its N-terminal TonB box with periplasmic TonB to couple inner-membrane proton-motive force to plug-domain unfolding and active import of the iron chelate into the periplasmic space.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Construct a ΔbfrD1 deletion mutant in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 and assay iron-limited growth rescue with purified Fe(III)-variobactin, ferrichrome, and enterobactin chelates, alongside isothermal titration calorimetry of purified detergent-solubilized BfrD1 with ferric-variobactin.
