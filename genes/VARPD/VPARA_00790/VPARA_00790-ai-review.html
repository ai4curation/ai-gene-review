Gene Description

VPARA_00790 (A0A0H2MDH4) in Variovorax paradoxus is a 608-amino-acid periplasmic pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ)-dependent methanol/alcohol dehydrogenase [cytochrome c] subunit (EC 1.1.2.7; NCBIfam TIGR03075, InterPro IPR017512, Pfam PF01011, CDD cd10278) containing a cleavable 28-residue N-terminal Sec signal peptide (residues 1-28) and a soluble mature eight-bladed beta-propeller quinoprotein catalytic chain (residues 29-608) with a conserved vicinal disulfide bond (Cys130-Cys131). The active site coordinates a PQQ prosthetic group (via Arg136, Thr180, Trp266, and Asn83) and a catalytic Lewis-acid metal ion (via Glu198, Asn286, and Asp328) to oxidize primary alcohols in the periplasm and transfer electrons to a periplasmic cytochrome c acceptor. Notably, the active-site loop of VPARA_00790 (325-PYDEWDFDGIN-335) carries the Asp328-Phe329-Asp330 (Asp-X-Asp) motif whose second aspartate (Asp330) is characteristic of lanthanide/rare-earth-element (REE)-dependent XoxF-type quinoprotein dehydrogenases, making its physiological metal cofactor selectivity (Ca(2+) versus trivalent lanthanides) an open experimental question.

Core Functions

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does Variovorax paradoxus VPARA_00790 (A0A0H2MDH4), which possesses the XoxF-like Asp328-Phe329-Asp330 active-site motif, require trivalent lanthanide ions (La(3+), Ce(3+), Nd(3+)) or Ca(2+) for catalytic activity, and what is its physiological alcohol substrate spectrum in the rhizosphere?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express and purify Variovorax paradoxus VPARA_00790 (A0A0H2MDH4) from cells grown with Ca(2+) versus light lanthanides (La(3+), Ce(3+), Pr(3+), Nd(3+)), determine bound metal stoichiometry by ICP-MS, and measure PQQ-dependent dehydrogenase kinetics across methanol, ethanol, and short-chain diols.

Hypothesis: Because VPARA_00790 contains the diagnostic Asp330 residue two positions C-terminal to the catalytic base Asp328 (328-DFD-330), it preferentially coordinates trivalent lanthanides over Ca(2+) to catalyze periplasmic alcohol oxidation.

Type: ICP-MS metal analysis and steady-state quinoprotein dehydrogenase kinetics

Structural Features & Active-Site Metal Motif Analysis

  1. Periplasmic Eight-Bladed $\beta$-Propeller Quinoprotein (FT SIGNAL 1..28, FT CHAIN 29..608):
  2. A0A0H2MDH4 contains a cleavable 28-residue Sec signal peptide (FT SIGNAL 1..28) and zero transmembrane helices. The GO:0016020 (membrane) IEA annotation from InterPro:IPR017512 is a well-known false positive on soluble periplasmic PQQ methanol/alcohol dehydrogenases (also removed in METEA/xoxF1) and is REMOVED in favor of GO:0042597 (periplasmic space, IC).
  3. Retains the conserved active-site vicinal disulfide bond (FT DISULFID 130..131, Cys130-Cys131), PQQ-binding pocket (FT BINDING 83, 136, 180, 266), and catalytic proton-acceptor base Asp328 (FT ACT_SITE 328).
  4. Active-Site Asp328-Phe329-Asp330 (DFD) Motif (PMID:24816778):
  5. Inspection of the active-site loop in A0A0H2MDH4 (321-MTPYDEWDFDGINEM-335) reveals Asp328-Phe329-Asp330 (DFD), containing the second aspartate (Asp330) two residues C-terminal to the catalytic base Asp328.
  6. As established by Keltjens et al. 2014 ([PMID:24816778 PQQ-dependent methanol dehydrogenases: rare-earth elements make a difference., "While structures of MxaFI- and XoxF-MDH are conserved, also regarding the binding of PQQ, the accommodation of a REE requires the presence of a specific aspartate residue near the catalytic site."]), $\text{Ca}^{2+}$-dependent MxaF dehydrogenases have an alanine at this position (Asp-X-Ala), whereas lanthanide/rare-earth-element ($\text{Ln}^{3+}$/REE)-dependent XoxF/ExaF dehydrogenases have a second aspartate (Asp-X-Asp) that coordinates the trivalent lanthanide ion.
  7. Because automated PIRSR (PIRSR617512-3) and InterPro (IPR017512) rules transfer GO:0005509 (calcium ion binding) indiscriminately across both $\text{Ca}^{2+}$- and $\text{Ln}^{3+}$-dependent members, and because the metal cofactor of VPARA_00790 has not yet been assayed experimentally in V. paradoxus, GO:0005509 (calcium ion binding) is MODIFIED to the parent term GO:0046872 (metal ion binding), and the $\text{Ln}^{3+}$ vs $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ selectivity hypothesis is recorded in suggested_questions and suggested_experiments.
