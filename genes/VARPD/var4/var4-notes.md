# Curation Notes: var4 (A0A0N7FMV9) in Variovorax paradoxus

## Overview
- **UniProt ID:** `A0A0N7FMV9` (`A0A0N7FMV9_VARPD`, 2,345 aa)
- **Gene symbol / GenBank protein:** `var4` (`ALG65339.1`, `EMBL:KT362218`; orthologous to `varG` in *Variovorax* sp. H002 and *V. boronicumulans*)
- **Organism:** *Variovorax paradoxus* strain P4B (`NCBITaxon:34073`)
- **Function:** Hybrid type I polyketide synthase / nonribosomal peptide synthetase (PKS/NRPS) with an embedded C-terminal TauD/TfdA-family Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate-dependent beta-aspartyl dioxygenase domain.

## Domain Architecture & Biosynthetic Role
- **Domain organization:** Ketosynthase (`PF00109`/`PF02801`/`PROSITE:PS52004`, residues 16-450), acyltransferase (`PF00698`/`IPR001227`), NADPH-dependent ketoreductase (`PF08659`/`IPR013968`), phosphopantetheine carrier protein (`PF00550`/`PROSITE:PS50075`/`IPR020806` at residues 1433-1508, modified at `Ser1468`), NRPS LCL condensation domain (`PF00668`/`IPR001242`, `cd19531`), and C-terminal TauD-like dioxygenase domain (`PF02668`/`IPR003819`). Notably, `var4` lacks a `PF00501` adenylation domain; in `GenBank:KT362218`, the upstream N-acyl-arginine starter unit is assembled by `Var3` (`ALG65336.1`, bases 1510..6735) and the downstream position-3 aspartate monomer is activated in trans by the adjacent NRPS module `Var5` (`ALG65340.1`, bases 13769..16882) immediately preceding `Var6` (`ALG65341.1`).
- **Orthology & Evidence:** Orthology between *V. paradoxus* P4B `Var4` (`KT362218`, [PMID:26412281]) and `VarG` in *Variovorax* sp. H002 / *V. boronicumulans* (`KT900023`, [PMID:27023373], [PMID:38590537]; note that the `92-99%` protein identity reported in [PMID:38590537] compares *Variovorax* sp. H002 against *V. boronicumulans* `KT900023`, not P4B `KT362218`) rests on identical `KS-AT-KR-ACP-C-TauD` domain organization and `var` cluster synteny. `Var4` extends the N-acyl-arginine starter unit with a malonate/methylmalonate ketide unit (reducing the beta-keto intermediate via its ketoreductase domain to form the 3-hydroxy group of 4-amino-7-guanidino-3-hydroxy-2-methylheptanoate), condenses it via its LCL domain (`GO:1904091`), and beta-hydroxylates the position-3 aspartate residue to form photoreactive beta-hydroxyaspartate ([PMID:26412281 "The prediction was modified as the presence of an aspartate β-hydroxylating enzyme led to the conclusion that the predicted position 3 aspartate was β-hydroxyaspartate."]). Note that while TauD-family aspartyl beta-hydroxylases reportedly generate L-erythro-beta-hydroxyaspartate ([PMID:38590537]), L-threo-beta-hydroxyaspartate was earlier reported for variochelin A ([PMID:27023373], accessed via [PMID:38590537] because `PMID:27023373` is cached abstract-only). Knockout of `varG` in *Variovorax* sp. H002 abolishes variochelin A-E biosynthesis ([PMID:38590537]). TreeGrafter projections to fatty acid synthase activity (`GO:0004312`) and fatty acid biosynthetic process (`GO:0006633`) are laterally `MODIFY`ed to polyketide synthase activity (`GO:0016218`) and siderophore/polyketide biosynthetic process (`GO:0019290`, `GO:0030639`). Cytoplasmic localization (`GO:0005737`, `NEW` `IC`) is documented in `var4-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md` (`FT SIGNAL = 0`, `FT TRANSMEM = 0`).
