Gene Description

Periplasmic high-affinity phosphate-binding protein PstS (VPARA_10480, A0A0H2M7F6) of the PstSCAB ABC-type inorganic orthophosphate importer in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 345-amino-acid precursor encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000005 (EMBL KLN58036.1) within the conserved phoR1-phoB1-phoU-pstB-pstA-pstC-pstS-ppx phosphate starvation regulon gene cluster (VPARA_10420-VPARA_10490). PstS comprises a 24-residue N-terminal Sec signal peptide (residues 1-24) that directs export across the inner membrane into the outer membrane-bounded periplasmic space, and a mature periplasmic polypeptide (residues 25-345, PRO_5002596677) folding into a two-domain Venus flytrap-type periplasmic binding protein-like II domain (Pfam PF12849, CDD cd13565, InterPro IPR005673, NCBIfam TIGR00975, PANTHER PTHR42996:SF1). PstS binds inorganic orthophosphate with submicromolar affinity in the periplasm and delivers it to the inner-membrane PstC-PstA permease channel energized by the cytoplasmic PstB ATPase dimer, while also participating with PstCAB and PhoU in sensing extracellular phosphate sufficiency to regulate the PhoR1-PhoB1 two-component system.

  • VPARA_10450 (KLN58033.1, pstB, A0A0D0L7E0): Phosphate import ATP-binding protein PstB (259 aa)
  • VPARA_10460 (KLN58034.1, pstA, A0A0H2M6I3): Phosphate transport system permease protein PstA (294 aa)
  • VPARA_10470 (KLN58035.1, pstC, A0A0H2MMT5): Phosphate transport system permease protein PstC (359 aa)
  • VPARA_10480 (KLN58036.1, pstS, A0A0H2M7F6): Periplasmic phosphate-binding protein PstS (345 aa)
  • VPARA_10490 (KLN58037.1, ppx, A0A0H2M767): Exopolyphosphatase Ppx (510 aa, curated in Tranche 3)

3. Literature & Comparative Evidence

  • PMID:2215649 (Luecke & Quiocho 1990 Nature): 1.7 Å crystal structure of E. coli periplasmic phosphate-binding protein PstS showing how the hinged two-domain cleft coordinates tetrahedral orthophosphate via 12 hydrogen bonds and an aspartate carboxylate that confers strict selectivity against sulfate.
  • PMID:6321434 (Surin et al. 1984 J Bacteriol) and PMID:3881386 (Surin et al. 1985 J Bacteriol): Sequence and organization of phoS (pstS) and the pstS-pstC-pstA-pstB-phoU operon in E. coli, establishing export of PstS to the periplasm via a cleavable N-terminal signal sequence.
  • PMID:24563032 (Gardner et al. 2014 J Bacteriol): Synthesizes the mechanism of the PstSCAB-PhoU-PhoRB complex in high-affinity $\text{P}_i$ uptake and $\text{P}_i$-sufficiency signaling.
