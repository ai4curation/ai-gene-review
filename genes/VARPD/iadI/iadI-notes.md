# Curation Notes: Variovorax paradoxus `iadI` (`A0A0H2M433`, `VPARA_32130`)

## Summary
- **Gene symbol, naming provenance & strain attribution**: `iadI` (annotated as `kynB2`, locus tag `VPARA_32130`, 270 aa in *Variovorax paradoxus* strain **TBEA6**, `NCBITaxon:34073`, `Proteomes:UP000035170`; syntenic ortholog of *V. paradoxus* strain **CL14** `iadI`, inferred as IMG `2643613664` from the stated `IadH–J` range `2643613663–2643613665` and gene order in `PMID:37459330`, from which the `iad`-operon gene symbol `iadI` is adopted per `PMID:36266335` and `PMID:37459330`).
- **Operon & pathway context (`PMID:36266335`, `PMID:37459330`)**:
  - Encoded within the conserved *Variovorax* `iad` auxin-degradation operon between `iadH` (`VPARA_32140`) and `iadJ` (`VPARA_32120`).
  - Metabolomics with $^{13}\text{C}_6$-IAA in *V. paradoxus* CL14 demonstrates that the `iad` pathway degrades IAA through dioxindole-3-acetic acid, dioxindole, isatin, and isatinic acid to anthranilic acid [PMID:36266335, "The intermediates dioxindole, isatin and isatinic acid, and the product anthranilic acid were all detected and thus produced in the bacterial cultures. Importantly, catechol, the main product of the iac IAA degradation, was not detected in any of the V. paradoxus CL14 samples."].
  - `IadI` belongs to the `PF04199` (`Cyclase`) / `IPR007325` (`KFase/CYL`) metallohydrolase superfamily, which includes both linear-amide kynurenine formamidases (`EC 3.5.1.9`, `GO:0004061`) and cyclic-amide hydrolases such as bacterial isatin hydrolases (`EC 3.5.2.14`, under `GO:0016812` `hydrolase activity, acting on carbon-nitrogen (but not peptide) bonds, in cyclic amides`) that cleave the heterocyclic lactam ring of isatin to isatinic acid (`2-(2-aminophenyl)-2-oxoacetic acid`). Because isatin and isatinic acid were directly detected in `iad` metabolomics (`PMID:36266335`), whereas `N-formylanthranilic acid` was not detected, `IadI` is curated as a putative cyclic-amide amidohydrolase (`GO:0016812`).
- **Annotation review**:
  - `MODIFY` `GO:0004061` (`arylformamidase activity`, `IEA`) to `GO:0016812` (`hydrolase activity, acting on carbon-nitrogen (but not peptide) bonds, in cyclic amides`).
  - `REMOVE` `GO:0006569` (`L-tryptophan catabolic process`, `IEA` from `InterPro:IPR007325`), which is an automated domain projection from canonical tryptophan-kynurenine pathway formamidases (`KynB`), and add `NEW` `GO:0042437` (`indoleacetic acid catabolic process`, `ISS` from `PMID:36266335` and `UniProtKB:A0A0H2M433`) and `GO:0005829` (`cytosol`, `IC`).
