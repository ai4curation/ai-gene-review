# Curation Notes: `ureF` (`A0A0H2LZ72`, `VPARA_50930`) in *Variovorax paradoxus* (`VARPD`)

## Summary
- **Gene / Locus:** `ureF` (`VPARA_50930`, UniProtKB `A0A0H2LZ72`, reference proteome `UP000035170`, 223 aa).
- **Domain Architecture:** `HAMAP MF_01385`, `InterPro:IPR002639`, `PANTHER:PTHR33620:SF1`, `Pfam:PF01730` (`UreF`).
- **Mechanistic Role:** Cytoplasmic urease accessory protein (`GO:0018237` `urease activator activity`, `GO:0030695` `GTPase regulator activity`) that binds `UreD`-bound apo-urease, recruits the SIMIBI GTPase `UreG`, and gates its nucleotide hydrolysis to ensure high-fidelity $\text{Ni}^{2+}$ incorporation ([PMID:22369361](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22369361/)). The UniRule IEA annotation `GO:0016151` (`nickel cation binding`) is `REMOVE`d because `UreE` and `UreG`, not `UreF`, are the nickel-binding carriers. Linked to `MODULE:urease_biogenesis_urea_hydrolysis` (`uredf_activation_scaffold` / `uref_activation_checkpoint`).
