# Curation Notes: ppk2 (A0A0H2MPR3) in Variovorax paradoxus

## Overview
- **UniProt ID:** `A0A0H2MPR3` (`A0A0H2MPR3_VARPD`, 268 aa)
- **Gene symbol / locus tag:** `ppk2` (`VPARA_00520`, `KLN58690.1`)
- **Organism:** *Variovorax paradoxus* strain TBEA6 (`NCBITaxon:34073`)
- **Function:** Cytosolic polyphosphate kinase 2 (`PPK2`)-family phosphotransferase (`Pfam:PF03976`, `InterPro:IPR022300` `PPK2-rel_1`, `NCBIfam:TIGR03709`, `PANTHER:PTHR34383:SF3`).

## Domain Architecture & Biochemistry
- **Single-domain P-loop NTPase fold:** Contains the `PF03976` domain at residues 31–253 with the conserved Walker A motif (`GMDSSGK`, residues 64–70) and lid module characteristic of bacterial PPK2 enzymes ([PMID:31206993, "Polyphosphate kinase 2 (PPK2) transfer phosphate from inorganic polyphosphate to nucleotides. According to their activity, PPK2 enzymes are classified into three groups."], [PMID:31907778]).
- **Distinct from `VPARA_50220` (`A0A0H2LUF2`) and `ppk` (`A0A0H2M4T4`):** Whereas `ppk` (`VPARA_28600`, `A0A0H2M4T4`) is a 710-aa `PPK1` enzyme (`IPR003414`) that synthesizes polyP from ATP via a phosphohistidine intermediate, `VPARA_00520` (`IPR022300` / `TIGR03709` / `PTHR34383:SF3`) and `VPARA_50220` (`IPR022486` / `TIGR03707` / `PTHR34383:SF1`) are paralogous `PF03976` PPK2-family phosphotransferases that use polyP to phosphorylate purine nucleotides.
