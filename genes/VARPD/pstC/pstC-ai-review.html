Gene Description

Phosphate transport system permease protein PstC (VPARA_10470, A0A0H2MMT5) is a 359-amino-acid six-pass inner-membrane subunit of the high-affinity PstSCAB ABC orthophosphate importer in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6, encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000005 (EMBL KLN58035.1) between pstS (VPARA_10480) and pstA (VPARA_10460) within the phoR1-phoB1-phoU-pstB-pstA-pstC-pstS-ppx cluster (VPARA_10420-VPARA_10490). PstC contains six transmembrane alpha-helices (residues 65-88, 108-139, 151-175, 195-221, 266-288, and 325-348) forming a MetI-like type-1 ABC transmembrane domain (PROSITE PS50928, Pfam PF00528, CDD cd06261, InterPro IPR011864, NCBIfam TIGR02138, PANTHER PTHR30425:SF1) and conserves the essential Arg276 and Glu280 residues (within 274-LGRALGETM-282) required for phosphate translocation. PstC heterodimerizes with PstA in the plasma membrane to form the transmembrane orthophosphate translocation pathway gated by periplasmic PstS and energized by the cytoplasmic PstB ATPase dimer.

Core Functions

Partners with PstA in the plasma membrane to form the transmembrane permease channel of the PstSCAB ABC importer that contributes phosphate transmembrane transporter activity during ATP-dependent orthophosphate import.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Construct site-directed Arg276Gln and Glu280Gln substitutions in VPARA_10470 (pstC) in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 and assay high-affinity [32P]orthophosphate uptake and PhoB1-regulated alkaline phosphatase repression.

Hypothesis: Substitutions at Arg276 or Glu280 abolish PstSCAB-mediated orthophosphate transport while preserving PhoU-dependent repression of PhoR1-PhoB1 under phosphate-replete conditions, mirroring E. coli pstC mutants (PMID:2646285).

Curatorial Notes for pstC (A0A0H2MMT5, VPARA_10470) in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6

1. Gene & Protein Identity

  • UniProt Accession: A0A0H2MMT5 (A0A0H2MMT5_VARPD)
  • Gene Symbol: pstC
  • Locus Tag / EMBL CDS: VPARA_10470 / KLN58035.1 on contig JZWI01000005 (RefSeq:WP_047783562.1)
  • Protein Length: 359 aa (37,449 Da); 6 transmembrane helices (65..88, 108..139, 151..175, 195..221, 266..288, 325..348) and an ABC transmembrane type-1 domain (PROSITE:PS50928 at 114..344, Pfam:PF00528 BPD_transp_1, CDD:cd06261 TM_PBP2).
  • Specific Family & Active-Site Conservation:
  • InterPro:IPR011864 (Phosphate_PstC), NCBIfam:TIGR02138 (phosphate_pstC), PANTHER:PTHR30425:SF1 (PHOSPHATE TRANSPORT SYSTEM PERMEASE PROTEIN PSTC).
  • Conserves the essential transmembrane/coupling motif 274-LGRALGETM-282 containing Arg276 and Glu280 (corresponding to E. coli PstC Arg237 and Glu240, whose substitution to Gln abolishes Pst phosphate transport in PMID:2646285).

2. Operon Context on Contig JZWI01000005

  • Encoded immediately between pstA (VPARA_10460, KLN58034.1) and pstS (VPARA_10480, KLN58036.1) within the phoR1-phoB1-phoU-pstB-pstA-pstC-pstS-ppx (VPARA_10420–VPARA_10490) phosphate regulon gene cluster.

3. Literature & Comparative Evidence

  • PMID:3881386 (Surin et al. 1985 J Bacteriol): Complete sequence and gene-polypeptide relationships of the E. coli phoS-pstC-pstA-pstB-phoU operon.
  • PMID:2646285 (Cox, Webb & Rosenberg 1989 J Bacteriol): Site-directed mutagenesis of E. coli pstC (Arg237Gln, Glu240Gln) abolishes phosphate transport through the Pst system.
  • PMID:24563032 (Gardner et al. 2014 J Bacteriol): Establishes that PstC and PstA are integral inner-membrane proteins that form the pore of the PstSCAB high-affinity $\text{P}_i$ ABC importer.
