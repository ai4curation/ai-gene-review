Gene Description

Two-component system OmpR/PhoB-family response regulator CzcR (VPARA_44930; 225 aa) in Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6, encoded in an operon with the sensor histidine kinase cusS (VPARA_44940) immediately downstream of czcA3-czcB-czcC-czcI (VPARA_44890-VPARA_44920) in the VPARA_44890-VPARA_44980 heavy-metal resistance island. Contains an N-terminal phosphorelay receiver domain (residues 2-115; Asp-51 phosphorylation site) and a C-terminal OmpR/PhoB-type winged-helix DNA-binding domain (residues 123-221) that regulates transcription of the czcCBA heavy-metal efflux operon in response to periplasmic Zn2+, Cd2+, and Co2+ sensed by CusS (CzcS).

Core Functions

Two-component system OmpR/PhoB-family response regulator and sequence-specific cis-regulatory DNA-binding transcription factor that receives phosphoryl groups at Asp-51 from the cognate heavy-metal sensor histidine kinase CusS (VPARA_44940) and binds operator DNA via its C-terminal OmpR/PhoB-type winged-helix domain (residues 123-221) to regulate transcription of the czcCBA heavy-metal efflux operon in response to Zn2+, Cd2+, and Co2+.

Q: Does phosphorylated CzcR (VPARA_44930) act strictly as a direct transcriptional activator (GO:0001216 / GO:0045893) of the czcA3-czcB-czcC operon upon metal-induced phosphorylation by CusS (VPARA_44940), as suggested by reporter and mRNA induction in Cupriavidus metallidurans CH34 (van der Lelie et al. 1997; Grosse et al. 1999, PMID:10198000)?

  • PMID:10198000(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10198000/) (Grosse et al. 1999, J Bacteriol 181:2385-2393): Purified the Alcaligenes eutrophus (Cupriavidus metallidurans CH34) CzcR response regulator, demonstrated specific DNA binding in the 5' regulatory region of the czc determinant ("The CzcR putative response regulator was purified and shown to bind in the 5' region of czcN."), and showed using $\Delta czcR$ and $\Delta czcS$ deletion mutants, lacZ reporter fusions, and cDNA analysis that the CzcRS two-component regulatory system activates czc transcription in response to $\text{Zn}^{2+}$, $\text{Co}^{2+}$, and $\text{Cd}^{2+}$ ("cDNA analysis of czcCBA mRNA synthesis under inducing and noninducing conditions showed that the CzcRS two-component regulatory system is involved in Czc regulation.").
