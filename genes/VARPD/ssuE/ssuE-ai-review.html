Gene Description

Putative NAD(P)H-dependent FMN reductase SsuE (VPARA_09290, A0A0H2M6H0; EC 1.5.1.38) in Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 is a 194-amino-acid cytosolic flavin reductase (NCBIfam TIGR03567, InterPro IPR020048, PANTHER PTHR43408:SF1, Pfam PF03358 residues 1-142) of the two-component alkanesulfonate monooxygenase system. Encoded on genomic contig JZWI01000005 (EMBL KLN57918.1) at the 3' end of the aliphatic sulfonate uptake and desulfonation gene cluster alongside the ABC importer subunits ssuB2 (VPARA_09230, KLN57912.1), ssuC5 (VPARA_09240, KLN57913.1), and ssuA2 (VPARA_09270, KLN57916.1) and the alkanesulfonate monooxygenase ssuD (VPARA_09250, KLN57914.1), SsuE catalyzes the NAD(P)H-dependent reduction of oxidized FMN to reduced FMN (FMNH2) and supplies the reduced flavin cosubstrate required by SsuD for oxygenolytic carbon-sulfur bond cleavage of aliphatic sulfonates to sulfite and the corresponding aldehyde.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 SsuE (VPARA_09290) exhibit strict NADPH preference like Pseudomonas putida S-313 SsuE or dual NADH/NADPH activity like Escherichia coli SsuE, and can alternative flavin reductases in TBEA6 support SsuD (VPARA_09250) activity in a ΔssuE background?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Purify recombinant Variovorax paradoxus SsuE (A0A0H2M6H0) and measure steady-state kinetic parameters (kcat, Km) with NADPH versus NADH and FMN versus FAD, as well as coupled desulfonation of octanesulfonate with purified V. paradoxus SsuD (A0A0H2MCI3).

Hypothesis: V. paradoxus SsuE efficiently reduces FMN and couples with SsuD (VPARA_09250) to drive stoichiometric sulfite release from linear alkanesulfonates.

2. Literature & Comparative Evidence

  • PMID:10480865(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10480865/) (Eichhorn, van der Ploeg & Leisinger 1999, J Biol Chem 274:26639-26646): Purified the two-component alkanesulfonate monooxygenase system from E. coli and demonstrated that SsuE is the NAD(P)H-dependent FMN reductase that supplies reduced flavin (FMNH2) to the alkanesulfonate monooxygenase SsuD ("SsuE is the FMN reducing enzyme providing SsuD with FMNH(2)." and "SsuD catalysis was absolutely dependent on FMNH(2) and oxygen").
  • PMID:10781557(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10781557/) (Kahnert et al. 2000, J Bacteriol 182:2869-2878): Showed in P. putida S-313 extracts that SsuE catalyzes NADPH-dependent FMN reduction ("SsuE revealed NADPH-dependent FMN reductase activity and lower levels of FAD reductase activity (32% of the value with FMN). No activity was observed with NADH.").
  • PMID:26634408(https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26634408/) (Dayal,ernen & Ellis 2015, Biochemistry 54:7531-7538): Demonstrated that optimal reduced-flavin transfer from SsuE to SsuD involves defined protein-protein interactions, while diffusional transfer can also occur under specified conditions.
