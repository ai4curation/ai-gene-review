Gene Description

Var14 (ALG65349.1) is a 559-amino-acid inner-membrane ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporter transmembrane/ATPase fusion protein (half-transporter) encoded at the 3' boundary of the variobactin (var) hybrid NRPS/PKS lipopeptide siderophore gene cluster in Variovorax paradoxus strain P4B (GenBank KT362218). Var14 belongs to the SyrD/YojI cyclic peptide and siderophore type I ABC exporter family (NCBIfam TIGR01194 / InterPro IPR005898) and combines an N-terminal six-helix ABC type-1 transmembrane domain (residues 25-299, PF00664 / PROSITE PS50929) with a C-terminal cytoplasmic ATP-binding cassette catalytic domain (residues 336-558, PF00005 / PROSITE PS50893, P-loop Walker A motif at residues 373-380). Var14 functions in ATP-driven export of newly synthesized variobactin lipopeptide siderophores across the cytoplasmic membrane.
