CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor acting as a central regulator of
CC       arbuscular mycorrhiza (AM)-related genes (e.g. AMT2;4, AMT2;5, EXO70I,
CC       STR, RAM2, LEC5, PT4, VPY, BCP1, SCP1 and RAD1) required for the
CC       morphogenesis of arbuscules upon symbiosis with AM fungi (e.g.
CC       Rhizophagus irregularis and Glomus versiforme) (PubMed:23122845,
CC       PubMed:25971550, PubMed:26511916). Promotes directly the expression of
CC       RAM2, EXO70I, STR and RAD1 (PubMed:26511916). Not necessary to enable
CC       hyphopodium formation or hyphal entry into the root but essential to
CC       support arbuscule branching (PubMed:26511916). Involved in the
CC       phosphate-mediated suppression of AM fungi colonization in mycorrhiza
CC       (PubMed:26511916). Also involved in restricting mycorrhizal
CC       colonization of the root meristem (By similarity). Required for Myc
CC       factor signaling from mycorrhizal fungi, but has no function in Nod
CC       factor signaling from rhizobial bacteria (PubMed:23122845). Regulates
CC       the expression of RAM2, a glycerol-3-phosphate acyl transferase that
CC       promotes cutin biosynthesis to enhance mycorrhizal hyphopodia formation
CC       (PubMed:23122845). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A0M4FMK2,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23122845, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25971550,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26511916}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms homodimer in nucleus and cytoplasm (By similarity).
CC       Interacts with NSP2, RAD1 and TF80 (PubMed:26511916).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A145P7T2, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26511916}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23122845}. Cytoplasm
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A0A145P7T2}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in the cortex of root colonized regions
CC       by arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (e.g. Glomus versiforme), and, to a
CC       lesser extent, in noncolonized lateral roots.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26511916}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Induced in roots during colonization by arbuscular
CC       mycorrhizal (AM) fungi (e.g. Glomus versiforme) (PubMed:23122845,
CC       PubMed:26511916). Regulated by DELLA1 during mycorrhizal symbiosis
CC       (PubMed:26511916). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23122845,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26511916}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: No visible phenotype under normal growth
CC       conditions (PubMed:23122845). Unable to be colonized by mycorrhizal
CC       fungi, with a defect in hyphopodia formation on the surface of the root
CC       (PubMed:23122845). Ineffective symbiosis with arbuscular mycorrhiza
CC       (AM) fungi (e.g. Rhizophagus irregularis) leading to rare colonizations
CC       characterized by malformed arbuscules reduced to trunk with a few
CC       initial thick hyphal branches (PubMed:25971550, PubMed:26511916).
CC       Impaired induction of AM-related genes (e.g. AMT2;4, AMT2;5, EXO70I,
CC       STR, RAM2, LEC5, PT4, VPY, BCP1, SCP1 and RAD1) in the presence of AM-
CC       fungi (PubMed:26511916). Insensitivity to phosphate-mediated regulation
CC       of the symbiosis with AM fungi (PubMed:26511916).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23122845, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25971550,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26511916}.
