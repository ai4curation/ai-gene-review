(pqac-00000003, pqac-00000036, pqac-00000037, pqac-00000051) | Establishes that PT4 is required for the functional mycorrhizal Pi pathway and normally supports arbuscule longevity. (pqac-00000020) | Direct promoter occupancy plus activation supports WRI5a as an upstream transcriptional regulator linking phosphate acquisition to the arbuscule lipid-transfer program.
