why
A 100%-identical V. cholerae VC1370 homolog binds c-di-GMP experimentally by DRaCALA and contains an RxxD motif. Query annotations identify HAMP and GGDEF domains; topology predicts two inner-membrane helices. No direct cyclase assay or substrate-specific fitness measurements are supplied.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by experimentally demonstrated binding of the explicit ligand c-di-GMP by a 100%-identical homolog. InterProScan, topology, homolog-domain, neighborhood and STRING evidence are available, but no fitness or cofitness measurements are provided. Diguanylate cyclase activity is suggested by domain and literature annotations, but binding does not establish catalysis, so a definitive cyclase reaction or EC number is omitted; SOS and quorum-sensing expression phenotypes provide regulatory context rather than a different molecular function.

This directly supports 'cyclic-di-GMP binding' (MF) and 'plasma membrane' (CC), as 'inner membrane' in a bacterial context refers to the cytoplasmic/plasma membrane. The trace mentions that c-di-GMP binding was experimentally demonstrated. The trace also explicitly rejects 'diguanylate cyclase activity', stating that 'binding does not establish catalysis', so no cyclase activity is assigned. The presence of HAMP and GGDEF domains is noted, but these are structural features and don't provide additional specific GO terms beyond the binding already identified. The 'SOS and quorum-sensing' mentions are identified as regulatory context rather than a molecular function of the protein itself.
