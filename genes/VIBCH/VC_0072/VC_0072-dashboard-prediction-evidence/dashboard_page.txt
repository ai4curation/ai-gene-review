why
A 99.60%-identical Vibrio cholerae VC0072 homolog experimentally binds c-di-GMP in a DRaCALA screen and lacks the RxxD motif. Query InterProScan identifies PAS, GGDEF, and EAL domains; topology predicts an inner-membrane anchor with cytoplasmic domains. STRING associations support c-di-GMP signaling context, but neither cyclase nor phosphodiesterase activity is experimentally established in the supplied evidence.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by experimental identification of c-di-GMP binding in a 99.60%-identical homolog, establishing a specific ligand interaction. Domain annotations and predicted membrane topology corroborate the protein architecture; no fitness, cofitness, or annotated gene-neighborhood data are supplied. A bifunctional diguanylate cyclase/phosphodiesterase annotation is rejected because domain membership and network associations do not establish catalytic activity.
