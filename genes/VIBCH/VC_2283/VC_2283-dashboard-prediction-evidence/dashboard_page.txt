enzyme

the enzyme this call names, as read out of it by a second pass
why
The 100%-identical Vibrio cholerae VC2283/AatX homolog is experimentally identified as a tryptophan transporter; deletion reduces environmental tryptophan consumption. Query InterProScan annotations identify the sodium:neurotransmitter symporter family, and STRING COG0733 independently supports sodium-dependent transport. Eleven predicted transmembrane helices support an integral inner-membrane location. Distant homolog phenotypes do not establish additional substrates for this protein.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by an experimentally characterized, 100%-identical homolog explicitly identified as the tryptophan transporter AatX. Homolog summaries, distant-homolog fitness data, query InterProScan, gene neighborhoods, STRING orthogroup information, and query membrane topology are available; no cofitness data are provided. Hypotaurine transport, reported for a 44.7%-identical homolog, is rejected in favor of the exact-match tryptophan evidence, while diverse distant-homolog fitness effects do not justify broader substrate specificity.

why these terms
The protein is identified as AatX, a sodium-dependent tryptophan symporter that mediates tryptophan uptake across the inner membrane. This supports a molecular function of tryptophan transmembrane transporter activity and a biological process of tryptophan transport. The 'inner membrane' in the context of a bacterium refers to the cytoplasmic membrane (often synonymous with the plasma membrane in bacteria). I will assign 'plasma membrane' as the most appropriate CC term given the search results.
