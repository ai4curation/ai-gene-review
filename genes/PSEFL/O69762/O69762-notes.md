# O69762 (HCHL, Pseudomonas fluorescens AN103) curation notes

## 2026-11-04 review

No provider deep-research file was generated for this entry (no provider API
keys in this session); the review uses the reviewed UniProt record and the four
cached primary papers (all abstract-only).

- Function: HCHL hydrates feruloyl-CoA and cleaves the hydrated intermediate to
  vanillin and acetyl-CoA [PMID:9461612 "A gene encoding a novel enoyl-SCoA
  hydratase/lyase enzyme for the hydration and nonoxidative cleavage of
  feruloyl-SCoA to vanillin and acetyl-SCoA was isolated and characterized from
  a strain of Pseudomonas fluorescens."].
- Substrate range: 4-coumaroyl-CoA, caffeoyl-CoA and feruloyl-CoA, but not
  cinnamoyl-CoA or sinapinoyl-CoA [PMID:10222033].
- Structure and mechanism: crotonase fold, hexamer of two stacked trimers
  [PMID:17139085]; Glu-143 essential, Tyr-239 contributes to substrate binding
  [PMID:18479250]. The 1999 purification paper describes a homodimer; the
  crystal structures and Swiss-Prot record a homohexamer. No complex CC term is
  annotated, so the discrepancy does not affect any row.
- PANTHER: PTHR42964:SF1. The TreeGrafter isoprenoid-catabolic row comes from
  the LiuC-seeded root node of that family (see the PTHR42964 family review)
  and is replaced with ferulate catabolic process.
