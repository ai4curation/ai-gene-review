CC   -!- FUNCTION: Receptor for kisspeptins (kisspeptin-10, kisspeptin-13,
CC       kisspeptin-14 and metastin/kisspeptin-54) (PubMed:12359743). The
CC       hypothalamic KISS1/KISS1R signaling system plays a central role in the
CC       regulation of the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal reproductive axis by
CC       modulating the secretion of gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) from
CC       GnRH neurons (PubMed:14573733, PubMed:14652023, PubMed:15665093). In
CC       these neurons, kisspeptin binding to its receptor activates G(q)-
CC       dependent signaling, leading to phospholipase C (PLC) activation, and
CC       hydrolysis of phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate (PIP2). The
CC       subsequent rise in intracellular calcium levels results in the
CC       inhibition of inward rectifier potassium channels and activation of
CC       TRPC-like cation channels, leading to GnRH neurons depolarization and
CC       stimulation (PubMed:18434521, PubMed:18483150). In addition to this
CC       pathway, kisspeptin also triggers G(q)-independent signaling via beta-
CC       arrestin, leading to MAPK cascade activation and ERK1/ERK2
CC       phosphorylation (PubMed:25147978). In bone tissue, activation of KISS1R
CC       by kisspeptin-10 recruits phosphatase DUSP18 and SRC to the KISS1R C-
CC       terminus through a G(q)-dependent signaling pathway. This leads to
CC       DUSP18-mediated dephosphorylation of SRC, down-regulation of osteoclast
CC       differentiation and activity, and consequently suppression of bone
CC       resorption (PubMed:38346942). KISS1R is also involved in the regulation
CC       of other processes, including cell proliferation and cell migration
CC       (PubMed:12359743). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12359743,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14573733, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14652023,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15665093, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18434521,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18483150, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25147978,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38346942}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with SRC and DUSP18; the interaction depends on
CC       receptor activation by kisspeptin-10 and is required for DUSP18-
CC       mediated dephosphorylation of SRC. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38346942}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q969F8};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Highest level in the heart and 15- and 17-day
CC       embryos. Low level in other tissues. Colocalized with gonadotropin-
CC       releasing hormone (GnRH) neurons in the hypothalamus.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11414709, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12359743,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15665093}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Conditional as well as whole body knockout causes
CC       osteoclast hyperactivation and bone loss.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38346942}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor 1 family.
CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00521}.
