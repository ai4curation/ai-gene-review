CC   -!- FUNCTION: Peroxidase that plays a central role in the host defense
CC       system of polymorphonuclear leukocytes by mediating both (1) formation
CC       of neutrophil extracellular trap (NETs) and (2) microbicidal activity
CC       (PubMed:10085024, PubMed:11593004, PubMed:23306200). Promotes NET
CC       formation by mediating chromatin disassembly: translocates to the
CC       nucleus and specifically binds to nucleosomes, both as monomer and
CC       homodimer, leading to nucleosome unstacking and initial chromatin
CC       decondensation (By similarity). Homodimers clash with one end of the
CC       nucleosomal DNA, leading to DNA unwrapping, initiating complete
CC       disassembly of nucleosomes and chromatin transformation into NETs in an
CC       ATP-independent manner (By similarity). NETs, which are mainly composed
CC       of DNA fibers and globular proteins, are then extruded into the
CC       extracellular space by neutrophils to trap pathogens and release
CC       antimicrobial proteins to destroy them (By similarity). Participates to
CC       the microbicidal activity against a wide range of organisms by acting
CC       as a peroxidase that catalyzes the formation of oxidants in presence of
CC       hydrogen peroxide (PubMed:11593004). Mediates the formation of
CC       hypohalous acids, mainly hypochlorous acid (HOCl) in physiologic
CC       situations, that greatly enhance polymorphonuclear leukocyte
CC       microbicidal activity (PubMed:11593004, PubMed:23306200). In addition
CC       to hypochlorous acid, catalyzes formation of hypobromous acid (HOBr),
CC       hypoiodous acid (HOI) and hypothiocyanous acid (HOSCN) (By similarity).
CC       Also catalyzes oxidation of nitrite into the highly reactive nitrogen
CC       dioxide radical (By similarity). Formation of oxidants are widely
CC       believed to be responsible for much of the anti-bactericidal activity
CC       of neutrophils (By similarity). Oxidants, such as hypochlorous acid or
CC       nitrogen dioxide radical, can also oxidize amino acid residues on
CC       proteins and generate chlorination and nitration post-translational
CC       modifications, respectively (By similarity). Chlorination and nitration
CC       of the lipid-free form of APOA1 impairs cholesterol transport (By
CC       similarity). Superoxides generated by MPO can also promote
CC       dioxygenation of tryptophan residues on proteins (By similarity). Also
CC       able to oxidize melatonin into N1-acetyl-N2-formyl-5-methoxykynuramine
CC       either in presence of hydrogen peroxide or superoxide (By similarity).
CC       Oxidizes urate into 5-hydroxyisourate (By similarity). Functions as a
CC       nitric oxide (NO) oxidase during inflammation, by catalytically
CC       consuming NO, impairing NO's ability to maintain vascular tone and
CC       function (By similarity). May also mediate the proteolytic cleavage of
CC       alpha-1-microglobulin to form t-alpha-1-microglobulin, which potently
CC       inhibits oxidation of low-density lipoprotein particles and limits
CC       vascular damage (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P05164,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10085024, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11593004,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23306200}.

CC   -!- PTM: Mature myeloperoxidase is a homodimer linked by disulfide bond,
CC       with each monomer composed of a heavy and light chain. Myeloperoxidase
CC       encompasses a series of critical proteolytic processing steps during
CC       maturation: after cotranslational cleavage of the signal peptide and N-
CC       linked glycosylation followed by limited deglucosylation,
CC       myeloperoxidase acquires heme in the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) to form
CC       the 84 kDa myeloperoxidase (also named promyeloperoxidase). A
CC       proconvertase then eliminates the propeptide region (49-164) in a post-
CC       ER compartment producing an intermediate species that is subsequently
CC       processed by cysteine proteases to the light chain and heavy chain
CC       followed by homodimerization. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P05164}.
CC   -!- PTM: Myeloperoxidase monomers are linked by an interchain disulfide
CC       bond at Cys-293. During maturation, the 84 kDa myeloperoxidase (also
CC       named promyeloperoxidase) precursor contains a disulfide bond between
CC       Cys-132 and Cys-293 that prevents homodimerization.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P05164}.
