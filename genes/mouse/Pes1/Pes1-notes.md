### What the trigger paper adds

PES1 knockdown in tumour cells leads to R-loop accumulation, STING-dependent interferon-stimulated gene expression and better anti-tumour immunity. PES1 interacts with DDX21, and its loss impairs DDX21 recruitment to R-loops. I added no NEW row:

- The interferon effect is several steps downstream of PES1 (R-loops, then cGAS-STING).
- R-loop accumulation is a known consequence of disrupted ribosome biogenesis, so the abstract cannot separate a direct role from nucleolar stress.
- Only the abstract is available.
