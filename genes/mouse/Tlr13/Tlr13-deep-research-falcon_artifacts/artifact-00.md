| Ligand / substrate | Sequence-selective pattern-recognition receptor for single-stranded bacterial 23S rRNA. The reported consensus is **5′-XCGGAAAGACCXX-3′**; an active 15-nt ligand is **5′-ACGGAAAGACCCCGU-3′**, and a minimal 12-nt segment can activate TLR13. Recognition is therefore not a catalytic reaction or indiscriminate response to RNA. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000027, pqac-00000033, pqac-00000047) |
| Subcellular localization | Integral membrane receptor functioning in intracellular **endosomal/endolysosomal compartments**, where bacterial RNA delivered by uptake or phagocytosis becomes accessible. This compartmentalization limits exposure to extracellular or host RNA and helps control self-reactivity. | (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000042) |
| Structural features | Canonical TLR architecture: an N-terminal signal peptide and luminal, horseshoe-shaped **leucine-rich-repeat (LRR) ectodomain** for RNA recognition; one transmembrane helix; and a cytosolic **Toll/IL-1 receptor (TIR) domain** for adaptor recruitment. Ligand engagement promotes receptor dimerization and signaling. | (pqac-00000045, pqac-00000046, pqac-00000047, pqac-00000050) |
| Trafficking requirements | **UNC93B1**, a 12-pass transmembrane chaperone, binds nucleic-acid-sensing TLRs in the endoplasmic reticulum and is required for their ER export, stability, and delivery to signaling-competent endosomes. Genetic loss of *Unc93b1* abrogates the TLR13-dependent phenotype associated with RNase T2 deficiency. | (pqac-00000042, pqac-00000044) |
| Signaling pathway | TLR13 is **MyD88-dependent**. TIR-domain engagement recruits MyD88, followed by IRAK4 and IRAK1/2, TRAF6, and downstream NF-κB and MAPK activation. Context-specific macrophage programs can additionally involve Syk–ERK–mTOR–β-catenin and the transcription factors **LXRα and MafB**. Evidence does not establish TRIF as a TLR13 adaptor. | (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000054) |
| Downstream mediators | In antibacterial responses, TLR13-containing endosomal pathways contribute to **CXCL1, CXCL2, IL-1β, TNF-α, IL-12p70, and IFN-γ**, promoting neutrophil recruitment and microbial control. In RNase T2-deficient splenic macrophages, signaling instead favors **IL-10** with little induction of TNF-α, IL-6, or IFN-β, demonstrating strong tissue and stimulus dependence. | (pqac-00000051, pqac-00000053, pqac-00000054) |
| Cell types | Demonstrated principally in myeloid cells: splenic and hepatic macrophages, Kupffer cells, Ly6C-high and Ly6C-low monocyte/macrophage populations, neutrophils, conventional dendritic cells, alveolar macrophages, and microglia. One recent mouse study detected little or no expression in T cells, B cells, or plasmacytoid dendritic cells. | (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000038, pqac-00000051) |
| Disease contexts and applications | Supports recognition and clearance of bacterial pathogens, including *Streptococcus pneumoniae* and other streptococci; TLR7/9/13 triple deficiency markedly compromises pneumococcal lung defense. Conversely, TLR2/TLR13-driven inflammation contributes to brain injury in pneumococcal meningitis, motivating combined antibiotic and receptor-inhibition strategies in mice. Microbiome-derived RNA can also drive TLR13-dependent replenishment of tissue-protective Kupffer cells and resistance to experimental acetaminophen- or LPS/D-galactosamine-induced liver injury. | (pqac-00000034, pqac-00000035, pqac-00000038, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000040) |
