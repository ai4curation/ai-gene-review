| Year | Key Finding / Development | Research Context | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | Peer-reviewed work showed that loss of endosomal RNase T2 permits microbiota-derived 23S rRNA to accumulate and activate TLR13, driving replenishment of tissue-protective Kupffer cells and resistance to experimental acetaminophen- and LPS/D-galactosamine-induced liver injury. The study also confirmed sequence-dependent activation by bacterial 23S-rRNA fragments, including a minimal 12-nt stimulatory region. | Microbiome RNA sensing, lysosomal RNA homeostasis, hepatic macrophage biology, and acute liver injury | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000040) |
| 2024 | Mouse TLR13 and TLR2 were identified as major sensors driving host responses and damaging inflammation in experimental pneumococcal meningitis. Combining ceftriaxone with TLR2 blockade and chloroquine-mediated inhibition of endosomal RNA sensing improved disease outcomes compared with antibiotic treatment alone or ceftriaxone plus dexamethasone. | Preclinical host-directed therapy for pneumococcal meningitis | (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000034) |
| 2024 | Translational comparisons treated human TLR8 as the functional counterpart of murine TLR13 for sensing pneumococcal RNA. Combined TLR2 and endosomal RNA-sensor inhibition suppressed activation of both mouse and human primary immune cells exposed to antibiotic-treated pneumococci. | Cross-species interpretation of mouse TLR13 biology and human therapeutic relevance | (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000034) |
| 2023 | A preprint reported that circulating microbiome-derived 23S rRNAs—including sequences attributed to *Escherichia coli* and *Lactobacillus helveticus/Lactococcus lactis*—reach hepatic macrophages and activate TLR13. This induced LXRα–MafB-dependent expression of CD5L, C1qb, Axl, and other tissue-clearance genes; the work was subsequently developed into the 2025 peer-reviewed study. | Microbiome–liver communication and tissue-protective macrophage programming | (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000039) |
| 2022 | A major review consolidated the sequence specificity of TLR13: it recognizes a defined 13-nt consensus, **5′-XCGGAAAGACCXX-3′**, within bacterial 23S rRNA, rather than responding indiscriminately to RNA. It also summarized UNC93B1-dependent delivery of nucleic-acid-sensing TLRs from the endoplasmic reticulum to endosomes. | Molecular specificity, receptor structure, and endosomal trafficking | (pqac-00000042, pqac-00000047) |
| 2020 | TLR13 was shown to cooperate with TLR7 and TLR9 in pulmonary defense against *Streptococcus pneumoniae*. Tlr7/9/13 triple-knockout mice were highly susceptible to pneumococcal pneumonia, with impaired macrophage production of CXCL1/CXCL2 and other mediators and defective neutrophil recruitment; single-receptor loss produced milder, tissue-dependent effects. | Antibacterial innate immunity, pneumonia, systemic spread, and CNS involvement | (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000051, pqac-00000053) |
