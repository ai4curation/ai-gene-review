CC   -!- FUNCTION: Isomerase that catalyzes the conversion of alpha-D-glucose-6-
CC       phosphate to beta-D-fructose-6-phosphate, the second step in
CC       glycolysis, and the reverse reaction in gluconeogenesis, within the
CC       cytoplasm (PubMed:2344351, PubMed:7277315, PubMed:8417789). Also shows
CC       C2-epimerase activity, interconverting D-glucose-6-phosphate (G6P) and
CC       D-mannose-6-phosphate (M6P) (By similarity). Also displays anomerase
CC       activity, interconverting alpha and beta-anomeric forms of G6P, D-
CC       fructose-6-phosphate and M6P (By similarity). In addition to its
CC       metabolic role, this enzyme functions extracellularly as a cytokine:
CC       acts as autocrine motility factor (AMF), a secreted angiogenic factor
CC       that enhances endothelial cell motility (By similarity). Functions as
CC       neuroleukin, a neurotrophic factor supporting the survival of spinal
CC       and sensory neurons (PubMed:3352745, PubMed:3764429). Released by
