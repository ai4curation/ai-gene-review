Gene Description

Casp9 encodes mouse caspase-9, the CARD-containing initiator caspase of the intrinsic mitochondrial apoptosis pathway. Pro-caspase-9 is recruited to APAF1 apoptosomes after mitochondrial cytochrome c release, where proximity-induced activation allows caspase-9 to cleave and mature executioner procaspases and thereby amplify the execution phase of apoptosis. Mouse genetics place Casp9 in both developmental neuronal apoptosis and DNA-damage-induced neural precursor apoptosis, while more specialized stress and tissue phenotypes are contextual branches of this core initiator caspase activity.

Core Functions

Full-length mouse Casp9 is recruited through its CARD domain to the APAF1 apoptosome and acts as the intrinsic-pathway initiator protease that matures executioner caspases, especially caspase-3 and caspase-7, to promote the execution phase of apoptosis.
