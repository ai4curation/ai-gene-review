# Scimp (mouse, Q3UU41) review notes

Reviewed together with human SCIMP (Q6UWF3); see `genes/human/SCIMP/SCIMP-notes.md` for the
literature summary. Deep research (falcon, perplexity-lite fallback) failed on 2026-10-16 with an
authentication error, so the literature was gathered manually from PubMed.

Mouse is the source of most experimental annotations (PMID:28098138, PMID:27288407,
PMID:28290451, PMID:34496234, PMID:35337798), which are then transferred to human by ISS/IEA.
Actions are kept identical across the two reviews for each term.

Mouse-specific points:

- The localization rows for ruffle, filopodium and phagocytic vesicle carry IMP evidence but rest
  on microscopy of wild-type and reconstituted cells; the localization itself is well supported.
- The K46 B cell line ERK defect (PMID:21930792) did not reproduce in primary Scimp-/- B cells
  (PMID:27288407); positive regulation of ERK1/2 is nonetheless retained on the strength of the
  macrophage Erk1/2 scaffold data (PMID:34496234).
- NEW: stimulatory C-type lectin receptor signaling pathway (GO:0002223), from the Dectin-1 work
  (PMID:27288407): Scimp is phosphorylated downstream of Dectin-1 and recruits its effectors, so it
  acts within the pathway rather than only responding to it.
- Tlr4 protein binding is modified to Toll-like receptor 4 binding (GO:0035662).
