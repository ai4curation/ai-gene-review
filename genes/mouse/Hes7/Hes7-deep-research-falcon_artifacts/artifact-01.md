| Identity | Protein and organism | Mouse transcription factor HES-7 (`Hes7`; UniProt Q8BKT2), also called Hairy and enhancer of split 7 or bHLH factor Hes7. The literature consistently identifies mouse Hes7 as a core presomitic-mesoderm segmentation-clock factor. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000013) |
| Protein structure | Basic helix–loop–helix domain | The bHLH region supplies sequence-specific DNA-binding and dimerization functions characteristic of HES-family transcription factors. Hes proteins interact with DNA as dimers. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000009) |
| Protein structure | Orange domain | Q8BKT2 carries the HES-family Orange domain specified in the target UniProt/InterPro annotation. In HES-family proteins, this domain contributes to partner selection and transcriptional-repressor specificity; this assignment is consistent with classification of Hes7 as a HES-family bHLH repressor. | (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000009) |
| DNA binding | N-box recognition | HES7 physically binds N-box regulatory sequences and represses transcription through these elements. Broader HES-family discussions sometimes refer collectively to E-box/N-box recognition, but the most specific evidence summarized for mouse HES7 supports N-box binding. | (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000041) |
| Primary molecular function | Transcriptional repression | HES7 is a DNA-binding transcriptional repressor rather than an enzyme, transporter, or extracellular signal. Its principal biochemical role is periodic repression of clock-network genes, including its own gene, within presomitic-mesoderm cells. | (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000012) |
| Core regulatory mechanism | Delayed negative autoregulation | HES7 binds regulatory DNA at its own locus and represses its own transcription. After short-lived Hes7 mRNA and protein decay, repression is relieved and another transcriptional pulse begins, generating a limit-cycle oscillator. | (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000034) |
| Timing mechanism | Intronic and expression delays | Transcription, intron splicing, translation, dimerization, and nuclear import delay appearance of active HES7 repressor. Removing all Hes7 introns advances expression by about 21 minutes, dampens or abolishes normal oscillation, and causes severe segmentation defects. | (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000035, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000040) |
| Protein stability | Mouse HES7 half-life | Approximately **21–22 minutes** in mouse presomitic-mesoderm cells. Rapid clearance is necessary to release autorepression between cycles; stabilization lengthens or disrupts clock dynamics. | (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000042, pqac-00000044) |
| Comparative stability | Human HES7 half-life | Approximately **40 minutes** in human presomitic-mesoderm cells, nearly twice the mouse value. This slower turnover contributes—together with slower mRNA decay and expression delays—to the longer human segmentation-clock period. | (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000042, pqac-00000044) |
| Oscillation dynamics | Mouse period | About **2 hours** in embryonic mouse PSM; reporter studies report approximately **124 minutes** in one mouse system and modeled or in-vitro values near **147–150 minutes**. Each cycle is coupled to production of one bilateral somite pair. | (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022, pqac-00000042) |
| Oscillation dynamics | Human period | Approximately **5 hours** overall; measured human PSM values are about **300–322 minutes**, compared with roughly 124–150 minutes in mouse systems. Cellular biochemical kinetics, not HES7 locus sequence alone, explain most of this species difference. | (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022, pqac-00000043) |
| Regulatory target | `Hes7` itself | Best-supported direct target: HES7 binds its own regulatory region and represses transcription, forming the central delayed negative-feedback loop. | (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000039) |
| Regulatory output | `Dll1` | HES7 oscillations propagate to oscillatory `Dll1` expression, coupling the intracellular oscillator to Delta–Notch communication. The reviewed evidence supports a regulatory-network relationship; direct mouse HES7 occupancy at `Dll1` should not be assumed without locus-specific binding evidence. | (pqac-00000056, pqac-00000062, pqac-00000065) |
| Regulatory output | `Dusp4` | HES7 periodically regulates or represses the ERK phosphatase gene `Dusp4`. Cyclic DUSP4 activity produces oscillatory ERK dephosphorylation and links the HES7 clock to the FGF–ERK wavefront. | (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000017) |
| Regulatory output | `Lfng` | Genetic and expression evidence places `Lfng` downstream of Hes7: Hes7 loss abolishes normal cyclic `Lfng` activity, and HES7-mediated repression contributes to Notch-clock timing. Evidence supports pathway regulation, although not every study establishes direct promoter binding. | (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000055, pqac-00000057) |
| Regulatory output | `Hes1` | Cyclic `Hes1` expression is lost in Hes7-null mouse PSM, placing it functionally downstream of the core HES7 oscillator. This is strong network-level evidence but does not by itself establish direct HES7 binding to the `Hes1` promoter. | (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000030, pqac-00000049) |
| Subcellular localization | Nucleus and chromatin | HES7 acts in the nucleus, where its bHLH domain mediates dimeric DNA binding and transcriptional repression at chromatin. Nuclear localization is strongly implied by its established DNA-binding repressor function; the cited recent sources do not themselves provide a dedicated localization-imaging experiment. | (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000039) |
| Tissue context | Presomitic mesoderm | HES7 activity is concentrated in embryonic PSM cells, where synchronized posterior-to-anterior expression waves provide temporal information for sequential somite formation. | (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000013, pqac-00000017) |
| Functional consequence | Somitogenesis | Proper HES7 abundance, turnover, phase, and oscillatory amplitude are essential. Loss, sustained expression, intron removal, or abnormal stabilization causes irregular or fused somites and later vertebral or rib abnormalities. | (pqac-00000037, pqac-00000049, pqac-00000050, pqac-00000053) |
