CC       kappa-B and JNK and plays a central role in the regulation of cell
CC       survival and apoptosis (PubMed:15121867, PubMed:18997792,
CC       PubMed:19150425, PubMed:19409903, PubMed:19815541, PubMed:9390694).
CC       Catalyzes 'Lys-63'-linked ubiquitination of target proteins, such as
CC       BIRC3, IKBKE, MLST8, RIPK1 and TICAM1 (PubMed:15175328,
CC       PubMed:28489822). Is an essential constituent of several E3 ubiquitin-
CC       protein ligase complexes, where it promotes the ubiquitination of
CC       target proteins by bringing them into contact with other E3 ubiquitin
CC       ligases (By similarity). Regulates BIRC2 and BIRC3 protein levels by
CC       inhibiting their autoubiquitination and subsequent degradation; this
CC       does not depend on the TRAF2 RING-type zinc finger domain
CC       (PubMed:18997794). Plays a role in mediating activation of NF-kappa-B
CC       by EIF2AK2/PKR (By similarity). In complex with BIRC2 or BIRC3,
CC       promotes ubiquitination of IKBKE (By similarity). Acts as a regulator
CC       of mTORC1 and mTORC2 assembly by mediating 'Lys-63'-linked
CC       ubiquitination of MLST8, thereby inhibiting formation of the mTORC2
CC       complex, while facilitating assembly of the mTORC1 complex
CC       (PubMed:28489822). Required for normal antibody isotype switching from
CC       IgM to IgG (PubMed:10514016). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q12933,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10514016, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15121867,
