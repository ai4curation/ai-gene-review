CC   -!- FUNCTION: Generates hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) which is required for the
CC       activity of thyroid peroxidase/TPO and lactoperoxidase/LPO (By
CC       similarity). Plays a role in thyroid hormone synthesis (By similarity).
CC       Also required for lactoperoxidase-mediated antimicrobial defense at the
CC       surface of mucosa (By similarity). Antimicrobial agent hypothiocyanite
CC       (OSCN-), which is produced by LPO from DUOX1-derived H2O2, promotes
CC       influenza virus inactivation by reducing viral binding to and entry
CC       into host cells (By similarity). Promotes antiviral immunity by
CC       increasing airway cytokine levels, promoting innate immune cell
CC       recruitment and reducing airway epithelial cell apoptosis
CC       (PubMed:34168077). In response to bacterial stimuli, activated by ATP
CC       and promotes EGFR/ERK signaling, ADAM17 activation, and EGFR ligand
CC       shedding, leading to enhanced CXCL8/IL8 expression and secretion in
CC       airway epithelia (By similarity). Synthesizes NAADP from its reduced
CC       NAADPH form which promotes Ca(2+) signaling during T cell activation
CC       (By similarity). In addition to its oxidase activity, has also been
CC       shown to have peroxidase activity through its N-terminal peroxidase-
CC       like domain (By similarity). However, another study showed that the
CC       isolated peroxidase-like domain does not bind heme and has no intrinsic
CC       peroxidase activity (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NRD9,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34168077}.

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: The NADPH oxidase activity is calcium-dependent.
CC       It is activated by release of cellular ATP which is promoted by
CC       bacterial infection. Peroxidase activity is inhibited by
CC       aminobenzohydrazide. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NRD9}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Hormone biosynthesis; thyroid hormone biosynthesis.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterotetramer with DUOXA1 consisting of 2 DUOX1-DUOXA1
CC       heterodimers (PubMed:32929281). This may represent the inactive state
CC       (PubMed:32929281). Has also been detected as a heterodimer with DUOXA1
CC       which has been proposed to be the active state (PubMed:32929281).
CC       Interacts with TXNDC11, TPO and CYBA (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NRD9, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32929281}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Following influenza airway challenge, mutants
CC       show increased mortality and morbidity, reduced levels of several
CC       cytokines including IL1B, IL2, CCL1, CCL3, CCL11, CCL19, CCL20, CCL27,
CC       CXCL5 and CXCL11, impaired lung viral clearance, and increased airway
CC       epithelial cell apoptosis (PubMed:34168077). Reduced hydrogen peroxide
CC       levels in lungs of uninfected mice (PubMed:34168077).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34168077}.

CC       expressed in E.coli. However, another study showed that the isolated
CC       domain does not bind heme and has no intrinsic peroxidase activity.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9NRD9}.
