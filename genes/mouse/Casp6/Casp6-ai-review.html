Gene Description

Casp6 is an Asp-directed cysteine endopeptidase that contributes to apoptotic substrate processing, local axon degeneration and context-dependent immune-cell death. Its cytosolic and nuclear pools cleave protein substrates, and BID cleavage can sustain mitochondrial apoptotic signaling in hepatocytes. AMPK phosphorylation restrains this feedforward mechanism. Mouse CASP6 also promotes RIPK3–ZBP1 complex assembly during influenza infection independently of protease activity. These roles are context dependent: Casp6 deficiency does not generally abolish apoptosis or development.
