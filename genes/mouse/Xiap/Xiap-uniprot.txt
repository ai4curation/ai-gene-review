CC   -!- FUNCTION: Multi-functional protein which regulates not only caspases
CC       and apoptosis, but also modulates inflammatory signaling and immunity,
CC       copper homeostasis, mitogenic kinase signaling, cell proliferation, as
CC       well as cell invasion and metastasis (By similarity). Acts as a direct
CC       caspase inhibitor (By similarity). Directly bind to the active site
CC       pocket of CASP3 and CASP7 and obstructs substrate entry (By
CC       similarity). Inhibits apoptosis in intestinal crypt cells, its activity
CC       is mitigated via its interaction with SEPTIN4 isoform ARTS
CC       (PubMed:30389919). Inactivates CASP9 by keeping it in a monomeric,
CC       inactive state (By similarity). Acts as an E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase
CC       regulating NF-kappa-B signaling and the target proteins for its E3
CC       ubiquitin-protein ligase activity include: RIPK1, RIPK2, MAP3K2/MEKK2,
CC       DIABLO/SMAC, AIFM1, CCS, PTEN and BIRC5/survivin (PubMed:18761086,
CC       PubMed:19473982). Acts as an important regulator of innate immunity by
CC       mediating 'Lys-63'-linked polyubiquitination of RIPK2 downstream of
CC       NOD1 and NOD2, thereby transforming RIPK2 into a scaffolding protein
CC       for downstream effectors, ultimately leading to activation of the NF-
CC       kappa-B and MAP kinases signaling (PubMed:29452636). 'Lys-63'-linked
CC       polyubiquitination of RIPK2 also promotes recruitment of the LUBAC
CC       complex to RIPK2 (By similarity). Regulates the BMP signaling pathway
CC       and the SMAD and MAP3K7/TAK1 dependent pathways leading to NF-kappa-B
CC       and JNK activation (By similarity). Ubiquitination of CCS leads to
CC       enhancement of its chaperone activity toward its physiologic target,
CC       SOD1, rather than proteasomal degradation (By similarity).
CC       Ubiquitination of MAP3K2/MEKK2 and AIFM1 does not lead to proteasomal
CC       degradation (By similarity). Plays a role in copper homeostasis by
CC       ubiquitinating COMMD1 and promoting its proteasomal degradation (By
CC       similarity). Can also function as E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase of the
CC       NEDD8 conjugation pathway, targeting effector caspases for neddylation
CC       and inactivation (By similarity). Ubiquitinates and therefore mediates
CC       the proteasomal degradation of BCL2 in response to apoptosis (By
CC       similarity). Protects cells from spontaneous formation of the
CC       ripoptosome, a large multi-protein complex that has the capability to
CC       kill cancer cells in a caspase-dependent and caspase-independent manner
CC       (By similarity). Suppresses ripoptosome formation by ubiquitinating
CC       RIPK1 and CASP8 (By similarity). Acts as a positive regulator of Wnt
CC       signaling and ubiquitinates TLE1, TLE2, TLE3, TLE4 and AES (By
CC       similarity). Ubiquitination of TLE3 results in inhibition of its
CC       interaction with TCF7L2/TCF4 thereby allowing efficient recruitment and
CC       binding of the transcriptional coactivator beta-catenin to TCF7L2/TCF4
CC       that is required to initiate a Wnt-specific transcriptional program (By
CC       similarity). Positive regulator of dermal wound repair, potentially via
CC       its interaction with SEPTIN4 (PubMed:23788729).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P98170, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18761086,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19473982, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23788729,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29452636, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30389919}.
