Gene Description

Mouse XIAP is a BIR-domain and RING-domain protein that inhibits apoptotic caspases and catalyzes ubiquitin transfer. Conserved BIR2/linker and BIR3 mechanisms restrain caspases-3/7 and caspase-9, respectively. Its RING-dependent ubiquitination supports NOD1/NOD2-RIPK2 innate immune signaling and other substrate-dependent outputs, including Wnt regulation. Cytoplasmic and nuclear pools serve distinct contexts. Endogenous XIAP regulates neuronal resistance to cytochrome c, Fas-dependent hepatocyte apoptosis, and intestinal crypt survival; these effects depend on cell type and the balance of XIAP antagonists.
