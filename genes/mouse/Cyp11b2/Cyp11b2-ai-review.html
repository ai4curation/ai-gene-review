Gene Description

Cyp11b2 encodes mouse aldosterone synthase, a heme-containing mitochondrial cytochrome P450 expressed in adrenal zona glomerulosa cells. It converts deoxycorticosterone to aldosterone through 11-beta hydroxylation, corticosterone 18-hydroxylation and a final C18 oxidation. The reaction sequence uses molecular oxygen and electrons delivered through the mitochondrial adrenodoxin system. Aldosterone production supports electrolyte, water and blood-pressure homeostasis; Cyp11b2-deficient mice lack aldosterone and develop hypotension and compensatory activation of the renin-angiotensin system. Corticosterone is the principal mouse adrenal glucocorticoid, and is also an intermediate in the Cyp11b2 reaction sequence.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

18-hydroxycorticosterone oxidase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: 18-hydroxycorticosterone + 2 reduced adrenodoxin + O2 + 2 H+ = aldosterone + 2 oxidized adrenodoxin + 2 H2O. The C18 hydroxymethyl group is oxidized to the aldehyde group of aldosterone.

Justification: Mouse Cyp11b2 makes aldosterone and carries the curated orthology-supported RHEA:50792 reaction. GO:0047783 names the preceding hydroxylation rather than this oxidation. The same proposed term as the human CYP11B2 review represents this chemically distinct activity without inventing a GO identifier.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on paired donors, with incorporation or reduction of molecular oxygen

PMID:1686470 directly expressed cloned mouse Cyp11b enzymes in COS7 cells: Cyp11b2 efficiently formed aldosterone, whereas Cyp11b1 formed corticosterone without aldosterone. This establishes the selected mouse protein as the terminal multistep aldosterone enzyme. PMID:15731365 independently reports loss of plasma aldosterone in Cyp11b2-deficient mice, with hypotension, hyperkalemia, compensatory renin/angiotensin activation and adrenal cortical remodeling.
