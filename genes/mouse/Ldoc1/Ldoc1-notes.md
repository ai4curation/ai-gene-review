# Ldoc1 (Sirh7 / Rtl7) — mouse curation notes

Mouse *Sirh7/Ldoc1* is a *sushi-ichi*/Ty3-gypsy-derived (Gag-like) domesticated
gene with a placental role not described for the human ortholog, plus the
conserved nuclear adapter activity inferred by orthology.

## Function
- **Placental role:** regulates trophoblast giant-cell differentiation and
  placental endocrine output
  [PMID:25468940 "Sirh7/Ldoc1 knockout (KO) mice exhibited abnormal placental cell differentiation/maturation, leading to an overproduction of placental progesterone (P4) and placental lactogen 1 (PL1) from trophoblast giant cells (TGCs)"].
- **Nuclear adapter:** predominantly nucleoplasmic, a protein-interaction adapter
  by orthology to human LDOC1 (GO:0030674)
  [PMID:10403563 "the EGFP-LDOC1 fusion protein was localized in the nucleus"].

## Curation decisions
- Core function 2 (nuclear localization) given the adaptor MF by orthology to the
  human ortholog, which carries the mechanistic (GNL3L/NF-κB) evidence.
