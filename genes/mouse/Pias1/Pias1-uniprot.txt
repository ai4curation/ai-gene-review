CC   -!- FUNCTION: Functions as an E3-type small ubiquitin-like modifier (SUMO)
CC       ligase, stabilizing the interaction between UBE2I and the substrate,
CC       and as a SUMO-tethering factor (PubMed:24061474). Catalyzes sumoylation
CC       of various proteins, such as CEBPB, MRE11, MTA1, PTK2, PML and ZNF76
CC       (PubMed:24061474). Plays a crucial role as a transcriptional
CC       coregulation in various cellular pathways, including the STAT pathway,
CC       the p53 pathway and the steroid hormone signaling pathway (By
CC       similarity). In vitro, binds A/T-rich DNA (By similarity). The effects
CC       of this transcriptional coregulation, transactivation or silencing, may
CC       vary depending upon the biological context (By similarity). Mediates
CC       sumoylation of MRE11, stabilizing MRE11 on chromatin during end
CC       resection (By similarity). Sumoylates PML (at 'Lys-65' and 'Lys-160')
CC       and PML-RAR and promotes their ubiquitin-mediated degradation
CC       (PubMed:22406621). PIAS1-mediated sumoylation of PML promotes its
CC       interaction with CSNK2A1/CK2 which in turn promotes PML phosphorylation
CC       and degradation (PubMed:22406621). Enhances the sumoylation of MTA1 and
CC       may participate in its paralog-selective sumoylation (By similarity).
CC       Plays a dynamic role in adipogenesis by promoting the SUMOylation and
CC       degradation of CEBPB (PubMed:24061474). Mediates the nuclear mobility
CC       and localization of MSX1 to the nuclear periphery, whereby MSX1 is
CC       brought into the proximity of target myoblast differentiation factor
CC       genes (PubMed:16600910). Also required for the binding of MSX1 to the
CC       core enhancer region in target gene promoter regions, independent of
CC       its sumoylation activity (PubMed:16600910). Capable of binding to the
CC       core enhancer region TAAT box in the MYOD1 gene promoter
CC       (PubMed:16600910). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O75925,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16600910, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22406621,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24061474}.

CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein sumoylation.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with NR2C1; the interaction promotes its

CC       TP53 and STAT1 (dimer), following IFNA1-stimulation. Interacts with SP3
CC       (preferentially when SUMO-modified). Interacts with KLF8; the
CC       interaction results in SUMO ligation and repression of KLF8
CC       transcriptional activity and of its cell cycle progression into G(1)
CC       phase (By similarity). Interacts with CHUK/IKKA; this interaction
CC       induces PIAS1 phosphorylation. Interacts with PTK2/FAK1; the
CC       interaction promotes its sumoylation (By similarity). Interacts with
CC       SUMO1, UBE2I, NCOA2 and AR. Interacts with NR2C1; the interaction
CC       promotes its sumoylation. Interacts with DDX5. Interacts with MTA1 (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with PML (isoform PML-12). Interacts with PRDM1
CC       (By similarity). Interacts (via N-terminus) with MSX1 (via C-terminus);
CC       the interaction is required for the localization of both proteins to
CC       the nuclear periphery and specific binding of MSX1 to the core enhancer
CC       region in target gene promoters (PubMed:16600910). {ECO:0000250,
