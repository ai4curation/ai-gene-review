CC   -!- FUNCTION: Receptor for the tachykinin neuromedin-K (neurokinin B), also
CC       able to bind and respond to tachynins substance K/neurokinin A and
CC       substance P. The rank order of affinity of this receptor to tachykinins
CC       is: neuromedin-K > substance K > substance P. Neuromedin-K binding to
CC       its receptor triggers G protein-coupled receptor signaling via
CC       activation of G(q) and phosphatidylinositol hydrolysis by phospholipase
CC       C. Neuromedin-K binding also triggers signaling via activation of
CC       adenylate cyclase activity which results in increased intracellular
CC       levels of cyclic AMP (cAMP). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P16177}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P29371};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- PTM: The anchoring of this receptor to the plasma membrane is probably
CC       mediated by the palmitoylation of a cysteine residue.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor 1 family.
