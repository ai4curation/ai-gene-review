# mouse Ar: comparative annotation review

## Provenance and scope

Normal `just fetch-gene` seeded every annotation; machine-sourced fields are preserved. Falcon deep research failed with HTTP 403 and its perplexity-lite fallback failed with HTTP 401. This is manual literature research, not a provider-generated report. Source caches were obtained through normal fetch commands and were not edited. Primary publications and curated UniProt statements were read; abstract-only evidence is not treated as proof of a wrong gene. No new biological-process annotations were proposed.

## Native mouse mechanism

PMID:27959439 directly demonstrates AR binding and activation of the Aard promoter in Sertoli cells. PMID:19574395 deletes the second zinc finger: receptor remains nuclear while transcription and spermatogenesis fail. PMID:17142810 independently shows that Fkbp4 loss impairs transcription while preserving androgen binding and nuclear import. These experiments prohibit treating ligand binding, import and transcription as interchangeable readouts. PMID:15660130 supplies additional native Sertoli-cell chaperone context.

## Tissue-specific outputs

PMID:17317769 and PMID:17652515 distinguish stromal prostate growth support from mature epithelial differentiation and proliferation restraint. PMID:11535819 demonstrates proliferative output in a different transgenic context. PMID:14676301 supports mammary developmental and ER/MAPK/IGF signaling effects, with human MCF7 rescue experiments distinguished from mouse phenotypes. PMID:21084446 epididymal perturbation has secondary obstructive effects on testis; infertility alone does not establish participation in gamete fusion. PMID:19574395 Leydig hypertrophy does not by itself settle a differentiation annotation.

## Citation and inference audit

Full cached PMID:18468998 was read: AR denotes alpha1D-adrenergic GPCR throughout its signalosome experiments, not androgen receptor. Its citation is therefore MISCITED for mouse Ar. Generic complex membership and plasma membrane localization remain biologically credible from independent curated AR information and are refined/retained accordingly; the blood-pressure process is unresolved. This is not a title-only paralog accusation. ERE binding remains unresolved rather than rejected merely because ARE binding is established. Historical PMID:5452809 has no accessible abstract; sex determination is not silently equated with differentiation. Human condensate evidence (PMID:33978290/36229685) supports an explicitly stated ortholog inference, not a claimed mouse experiment. Other fine compartments, behavioral outputs and unusual binding/process transfers are left undecided where their specific evidence could not be resolved.
