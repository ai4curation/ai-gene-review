# Mdh1 notes

## 2026-11-24 review

- Deep research: `just deep-research-falcon mouse Mdh1 --fallback perplexity-lite` failed because the Falcon provider was not available in this environment and the Perplexity fallback was unavailable. This review therefore used the seeded GOA rows, the mouse and human UniProt records, the reviewed human `MDH1` gene review, the PANTHER `PTHR23382` PAINT rows, the source GO-CAM review, Reactome gluconeogenesis and malate-aspartate shuttle summaries, and cached publications.
- Core molecular function: Mdh1 is the cytosolic NAD-dependent malate dehydrogenase, EC 1.1.1.37. UniProt records the exact reaction, `(S)-malate + NAD(+) = oxaloacetate + NADH + H(+)`, for the mouse protein.
- MGI cites PMID:5770240 for Mdh1 NAD-dependent malate dehydrogenase activity and cytosol, but the cached title names mouse NADP-malate dehydrogenase, historically consistent with the Mod-1/Me1 malic enzyme rather than Mdh1/Mor-2. Because no full text is cached, the concern is logged as an `UNVERIFIED` reference review and the rows are retained on the strength of UniProt/Rhea/EC, PAINT and orthology support.
- GO-CAM `61e0e55600001225`: activity `gomodel:61e0e55600001225/61e0e55600001726` uses Mdh1 for cytosolic reoxidation of exported malate to oxaloacetate in the malate route of gluconeogenesis. Reactome states that mitochondrial oxaloacetate is reduced to malate, exported to the cytosol, and re-oxidized to oxaloacetate. The model's `GO:0030060`/`GO:0005829`/`GO:0006094` annotations are consistent with the gene review, although the MGI enabled_by mapping paper is abstractless and the `part_of` TAS source is a review whose cached abstract does not name Mdh1.
- Malate-aspartate shuttle: Mdh1 provides the cytosolic MDH reaction. Gu et al. 2020 connected mouse Mdh1 to the shuttle in fetal liver HSCs [PMID:32396938 "STAT3 transactivated MDH1 to sustain the malate-aspartate NADH shuttle activity"].
- PAINT TCA-cycle propagation: the PTHR23382 `GO:0006099` row comes through a deep bacterial/source node (`PTN000600336`). This over-propagates the bacterial single-Mdh TCA role to the mammalian cytosolic isozyme; the mitochondrial TCA-cycle step is Mdh2.
- Root and side activities: the Rhea/UniProt transfers for aromatic alpha-keto acid reductase and 2-oxoglutarate reductase activities were kept as non-core promiscuous activities. The generic `malate dehydrogenase activity` rows were accepted rather than modified because their own InterPro/ARBA/orthology evidence stops at the parent term while separate EC/Rhea rows assert the NAD-specific child.
- Location outliers: the MitoCarta HDA mitochondrion row [PMID:18614015 "mitochondrial compendium of 1098 genes"], the purified-myelin HDA row [PMID:17634366 "identified >160 proteins in purified myelin membranes"], and the human-transferred centrosome row were treated as over-annotated, not as core locations.
