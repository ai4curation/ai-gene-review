CC   -!- FUNCTION: In the hair cortex, hair keratin intermediate filaments are
CC       embedded in an interfilamentous matrix, consisting of hair keratin-
CC       associated proteins (KRTAP), which are essential for the formation of a
CC       rigid and resistant hair shaft through their extensive disulfide bond
CC       cross-linking with abundant cysteine residues of hair keratins. The
CC       matrix proteins include the high-sulfur and high-glycine-tyrosine
CC       keratins. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with hair keratins. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed at high levels in skin and at lower
CC       levels in the developing mammary gland. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10446281}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: In skin, expression starts shortly after birth and
CC       reaches a first maximum at 9 days. A second peak of expression is
CC       observed at 3.5 weeks, then levels decline and remain low in the adult.
CC       In the developing mammary gland, expression is detected exclusively at
CC       the onset of puberty. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10446281}.
