# Slc25a10 notes

## 2026-11-24 review

- Deep research: `just deep-research-falcon mouse Slc25a10 --fallback perplexity-lite` failed because the Falcon provider was not available in this environment and the Perplexity fallback was unavailable. This review therefore used the seeded GOA rows, the mouse and human UniProt records, the completed human `SLC25A10` review, the mitochondrial dicarboxylate/tricarboxylate carrier module, the source GO-CAM review, Reactome gluconeogenesis context, and cached publications.
- Core function: Slc25a10 is the mitochondrial dicarboxylate carrier DIC, a multi-pass mitochondrial inner-membrane SLC25 antiporter that exchanges dicarboxylates such as malate or succinate for inorganic phosphate. The mouse UniProt record curates this same function and MIM location, while Mizuarai et al. directly state that mouse Slc25a10 transports malate or succinate across the mitochondrial inner membrane [PMID:16027120 "Mitochondrial solute carrier family 25 member 10 (Slc25a10) transports dicarboxylates such as malate or succinate across the mitochondrial inner membrane"].
- Additional UniProt-curated papers: An et al. directly support adipocyte DIC-dependent succinate export and the non-core adipocyte-lipolysis physiology that mouse UniProt now mentions [PMID:33746082 "mDIC is responsible for succinate transport out of mitochondria to suppress white adipocyte lipolysis"]. Cai et al. is abstract-only in the local cache, but the abstract supports a CLOCK/SLC25A10 interaction and SLC25A10-dependent metabolism in CRISPR-edited Hepa1-6 cells [PMID:30943427 "CLOCK can directly regulate cell metabolism via the mitochondrial membrane transporter SLC25A10"]. Neither paper changes the core DIC substrate assignment.
- GO-CAM `61e0e55600001225`: activity `gomodel:61e0e55600001225/61e0e55600001730` uses Slc25a10 to export mitochondrial malate from the Mdh2 step to cytosolic Mdh1. Its `GO:0015140` malate transmembrane transporter activity and `GO:0005743` inner-membrane location are consistent with this review; gluconeogenesis is a true but non-core pathway context for the carrier.
- Phosphate rows: the dicarboxylate/phosphate exchange mechanism means the generic `GO:0005315` phosphate transmembrane transporter activity and non-transmembrane `GO:0006817` phosphate ion transport rows are better represented by the coupled antiporter and phosphate ion transmembrane process, respectively.
- Oxaloacetate rows: the PANTHER oxaloacetate-transporter IBA and its electronic process projection were marked over-annotated. Mouse UniProt's curated DIC function names malonate, malate and succinate as transported dicarboxylates, so the oxaloacetate-specific term exceeds current mouse substrate evidence.
- ODC-AS row: the `GO:0046166` glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate biosynthetic process row cites Niimi et al. 2009, which characterizes ODC-AS, an alternatively spliced mitochondrial oxodicarboxylate carrier transcript with GenBank accession EU180236 [PMID:19825180 "an alternatively-spliced form of murine ODC (ODC-AS) was identified in 3T3-L1 cells"]. EU180236 is assigned to mouse `Slc25a21`, not `Slc25a10`, so this row was removed with a wrong-gene source defect.
- Outlier location: the exact-human `nucleoplasm` ISO row was removed because the source human row is a sperm-nucleoplasm HDA outlier on a polytopic mitochondrial inner-membrane carrier.
