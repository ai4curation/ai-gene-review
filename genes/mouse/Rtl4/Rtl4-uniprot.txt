CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in cognitive function in the brain, possibly via the
CC       noradrenergic system. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26402067}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: In adults, expressed in brain, eye, kidney, ovary
CC       and testis (PubMed:15716091, PubMed:26402067). Weakly expressed in
CC       thymus, heart and muscle (PubMed:15716091).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15716091, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26402067}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed at 12.5 dpc in almost all tissues
CC       tested. Expressed at 14.5 dpc in brain, eye and liver. Weakly expressed
CC       in lung, heart and kidney. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15716091,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26402067}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mutants are viable and don't show growth
CC       retardation in the pre- and postnatal periods in either female or male
CC       mice (PubMed:26402067). Animals are fertile, even in the case of mating
CC       between mutants. They exhibit abnormal behaviors related to cognition,
CC       including attention, impulsivity, and working memory (PubMed:26402067).
CC       Animals show a reduced recovery rate of noradrenaline in the prefrontal
CC       cortex region (PubMed:26402067). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26402067}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: RTL4 is one of at least 11 genes called Mar or Mart
CC       related to long terminal repeat retrotransposons. They do not
CC       correspond to functional retrotransposons, but rather to
CC       neofunctionalized retrotransposons genes. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15716091,
