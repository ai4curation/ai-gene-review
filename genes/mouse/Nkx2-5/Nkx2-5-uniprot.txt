CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor required for the development of the
CC       heart and the spleen (PubMed:16556915, PubMed:19483677,
CC       PubMed:22560297, PubMed:9584153). During heart development, acts as a
CC       transcriptional activator of NPPA/ANF in cooperation with GATA4
CC       (PubMed:9584153). May cooperate with TBX2 to negatively modulate
CC       expression of NPPA/ANF in the atrioventricular canal (PubMed:12023302).
CC       Binds to the core DNA motif of NPPA promoter (PubMed:19483677).
CC       Together with PBX1, required for spleen development through a mechanism
CC       that involves CDKN2B repression (PubMed:22560297). Positively regulates
CC       transcription of genes such as COL3A1 and MMP2, resulting in increased
CC       pulmonary endothelial fibrosis in response to hypoxia (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P52952, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12023302,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16556915, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19483677,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22560297, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9584153}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (via the homeobox); binds DNA as homodimer.
CC       Interacts (via the homeobox) with TBX5 (via the T-box); this complex
CC       binds DNA (By similarity). Interacts with HIPK1 and HIPK2, but not
CC       HIPK3 (PubMed:9748262). Interacts with the C-terminal zinc finger of
CC       GATA4 through its homeobox domain (PubMed:9584153). Also interacts with
CC       JARID2 which represses its ability to activate transcription of ANF
CC       (PubMed:15542826). Interacts with FBLIM1 (PubMed:14757752). Interacts
CC       with TBX18 (PubMed:17584735). Interacts with histone methyltransferase
CC       NSD2 (via HMG box) (PubMed:19483677). Interacts with NEDD9 (By
CC       similarity). Interacts with TBX1 (PubMed:16556915).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P52952, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14757752,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15542826, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16556915,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17584735, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19483677,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9584153, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9748262}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19483677}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Predominantly in the adult and embryonic heart, and
CC       to a lesser extent in lingual muscle, spleen and stomach.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expression preceeds the onset of myogenic
CC       differentiation, and continues in cardiomyocytes of embryonic, fetal
CC       and adult hearts. It is also expressed laterally in future pharyngeal
CC       endoderm which is believed to produce the heart inducer. After foregut
CC       closure expression in endoderm is limited to the pharyngeal floor,
CC       dorsal to the developing heart tube. The thyroid primordium a
CC       derivative of the pharyngeal floor continues to express the protein
CC       after its levels diminish in the rest of the pharynx.
