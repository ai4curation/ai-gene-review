CC   -!- FUNCTION: Thiol protease that plays a key role in programmed cell death
CC       by acting as a molecular switch for apoptosis, necroptosis and
CC       pyroptosis, and is required to prevent tissue damage during embryonic
CC       development and adulthood (PubMed:12065591, PubMed:18455983,
CC       PubMed:30361383, PubMed:30381458, PubMed:31511692, PubMed:31748744,
CC       PubMed:33397971, PubMed:9727491, PubMed:9727492). Initiator protease
CC       that induces extrinsic apoptosis by mediating cleavage and activation
CC       of effector caspases responsible for FAS/CD95-mediated and TNFRSF1A-
CC       induced cell death (PubMed:24813849, PubMed:24813850, PubMed:9654089,
CC       PubMed:9837723). Cleaves and activates effector caspases CASP3, CASP4,
CC       CASP6, CASP7, CASP9 and CASP10 (By similarity). Binding to the adapter
CC       molecule FADD recruits it to either receptor FAS/CD95 or TNFRSF1A
CC       (PubMed:29440439). The resulting aggregate called the death-inducing
CC       signaling complex (DISC) performs CASP8 proteolytic activation (By
CC       similarity). The active dimeric enzyme is then liberated from the DISC
CC       and free to activate downstream apoptotic proteases (By similarity).
CC       Proteolytic fragments of the N-terminal propeptide (termed CAP3, CAP5
CC       and CAP6) are likely retained in the DISC (By similarity). Also cleaves
CC       and activates BID, thereby promoting cytochrome C release from
CC       mitochrondria (PubMed:9727491, PubMed:9727492). In addition to
CC       extrinsic apoptosis, also acts as a negative regulator of necroptosis:
CC       acts by cleaving RIPK1 at 'Asp-325', which is crucial to inhibit RIPK1
CC       kinase activity, limiting TNF-induced apoptosis, necroptosis and
CC       inflammatory response (PubMed:31511692). Also able to initiate
CC       pyroptosis by mediating cleavage and activation of gasdermin-C and -D
CC       (GSDMC and GSDMD, respectively): gasdermin cleavage promotes release of
CC       the N-terminal moiety that binds to membranes and forms pores,
CC       triggering pyroptosis (PubMed:30361383, PubMed:30381458). Initiates
CC       pyroptosis following inactivation of MAP3K7/TAK1 (PubMed:30361383,
CC       PubMed:30381458). Also acts as a regulator of innate immunity by
CC       mediating cleavage and inactivation of N4BP1 downstream of TLR3 or
CC       TLR4, thereby promoting cytokine production (PubMed:32971525). May
CC       participate in the Granzyme B (GZMB) cell death pathways (By
CC       similarity). Cleaves PARP1 and PARP2 (PubMed:12065591).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q14790, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12065591,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18455983, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24813849,
