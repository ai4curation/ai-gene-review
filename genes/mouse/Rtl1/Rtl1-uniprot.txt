CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays an essential role in capillaries endothelial cells for
CC       the maintenance of feto-maternal interface and for development of the
CC       placenta. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18176565}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Membrane {ECO:0000305}; Multi-pass membrane
CC       protein {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in placenta and in various tissues in
CC       late-fetal stage. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18176565}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Increased expression throughout development from
CC       9.5 dpc to 18.5 dpc in placenta and, from 12.5 dpc to 15.5 dpc in
CC       embryo. Barely detectable in adult brain and midbrains of 14.5 dpc, but
CC       abundant at 8.5 dpc. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17901046,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18176565}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Down-regulated during time-course of induced adipogenesis in
CC       3T3L1 cells; Lipid accumulation is unchanged during adipocyte
CC       differentiation when Rtl1 is overexpressed.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17216273}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mice suffer from late fetal or neonatal
CC       lethality. Mice overexpressing Rtl1 show notable overgrowth and
CC       morphological abnormalities of the placenta.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18176565}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Rtl1 is one of at least 11 genes called Mar or Mart
CC       related to long terminal repeat retrotransposons. They do not
CC       correspond to functional retrotransposons, but rather to
CC       neofunctionalized retrotransposons genes.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Rtl1 is an imprinted gene located in a cluster of
CC       imprinted genes on distal chromosome 12. It is expressed from the
CC       paternal chromosome and has an antisense transcript expressed from the
CC       maternal chromosome containing 2 microRNAs, mir-136 and mir-127, with
CC       full complementarity to Rtl1; mir-136 and mir-127 are processed from an
CC       antisense transcript, Rtl1as, and may function as small interfering
CC       RNAs to silence Rtl1.
