# Abhd14a (mouse) — review notes

Family-coordinated review of mouse `Abhd14a` (`UniProtKB:Q922Q6`, `MGI:1915894`, alias *Dorz1*, `PANTHER:PTHR46197:SF1`, 247 aa).

## 1. Biology, topology, and paralog divergence from `Abhd14b`

Mouse `Abhd14a` (*Dorz1*, "Down-regulated in Zic-1-mutant protein", 247 aa, `sequence_version: 2`) is the 1:1 mammalian ortholog of human `ABHD14A` (`Q9BUJ0`, `PTHR46197:SF1`) and rat `Abhd14a` (`Q5I0C4`).
- **Cerebellar expression (*Dorz1*, `PMID:14667578`)**: Identified by microarray screening of *Zic1*-deficient mouse cerebella as one of the most strongly downregulated transcripts; *Dorz1* mRNA peaks at E17.5 in differentiating cerebellar granule neuron precursors where *Zic1* is enriched, and is upregulated upon *Zic1* overexpression in cultured cells [PMID:14667578, "The expression of Dorz1 in the developing cerebellum peaked at embryonic day 17.5, and at that stage Dorz1 transcripts were detected in cerebellar granule neuron precursors where Zic1 expression is enhanced."]. Being transcriptionally downstream of ZIC1 is expression data rather than evidence that the ABHD14A protein participates in granule neuron differentiation, so no developmental BP annotation is proposed.
- **Membrane topology vs soluble paralog `Abhd14b`**: Unlike soluble cytosolic/nuclear `Abhd14b` (`Q8VCR7`, `PTHR46197:SF2`), mouse ABHD14A carries an N-terminal signal-anchor transmembrane helix (`FT TRANSMEM 11..31`, single-pass type II membrane protein) and lumenal N-glycosylation (`Asn43`), placing its alpha/beta-hydrolase catalytic triad (`Ser147`, `Asp198`, `His225`) on the lumenal side of secretory-pathway membranes.
- **2026 biochemical characterization of human ABHD14A (`PMID:42469586`) and paralog hazard (`PMID:37974539`)**: Vaidya et al. (`PMID:37974539`) established sequence determinants separating `ABHD14A` from the KDAC `ABHD14B` because automated pipelines repeatedly conflate the two paralogs. Subsequently, Gupta, Dhaware & Kamat (`PMID:42469586`, *Biochemistry* Aug 2026) showed that an engineered N-terminally truncated soluble human ABHD14A variant turns over synthetic short-chain *p*-nitrophenyl esters with CoA-dependent enhancement (indicative of a ping-pong acetyl-transfer capability) and that overexpressed human ABHD14A-GFP localizes to the Golgi apparatus in HEK293T cells, while endogenous physiological substrates remain unknown.

## 2. Review of the 6 existing GOA rows

1. **`GO:0000139` (`Golgi membrane`, `ISS` `GO_REF:0000024` from `UniProtKB:Q5I0C4`, assigned by UniProt) -> `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`**:
   - Transferred by sequence similarity from rat `Q5I0C4` (which carries an `IDA` row citing `PMID:42469586`, where full-length ABHD14A-GFP localized specifically to the Golgi apparatus in HEK293T cells, consistent with the conserved type II signal-anchor helix `11..31` and lumenal N-glycosylation at `Asn43`). Retained as `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` rather than `ACCEPT` because localization rests on heterologous GFP-fusion overexpression (`PMID:42469586` abstract; full text unavailable) rather than endogenous mouse cerebellar protein detection.
2. **`GO:0016020` (`membrane`, `IEA` `GO_REF:0000044`, `UniProtKB-SubCell:SL-0162`) -> `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED`**:
   - Harmonized with human `ABHD14A` (`Q9BUJ0`): bare unspecified `membrane` derives from a curator-inferred `ECO:0000305` SubCell keyword and conveys neither the type II signal-anchor topology nor a verified endogenous compartment (or is superseded in specificity by `GO:0000139` `Golgi membrane`).
3. **`GO:0005737` (`cytoplasm`, `IDA` `PMID:14667578` assigned by MGI, `IBA` `PTN002475422`, `IEA` `GO_REF:0000044`) -> `ACCEPT` (harmonized with `human/ABHD14A`)**:
   - Note that `PMID:14667578` full text is unavailable (`full_text_available: false`); MGI curates `IDA` to `GO:0005737` from that paper (likely transfected epitope-tagged DORZ1 immunocytochemistry in the full text — per `AGENTS.md`, never `REMOVE` an MGI `IDA` whose full text is unavailable), and a single-pass type II membrane protein exposes its N-terminal tail to the cytoplasm while `PTN002475422` (`IBA`) is seeded in part by `MGI:MGI:1915894`. Accepted with the explicit topological caveat that the C-terminal catalytic domain faces the Golgi/secretory lumen rather than the cytosol.
4. **`GO:0016407` (`acetyltransferase activity`, `ISS` `GO_REF:0000024` from `UniProtKB:Q9BUJ0`, assigned by UniProt) -> `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED`**:
   - Projected from human `Q9BUJ0` following `PMID:42469586`, where an engineered N-terminally truncated human ABHD14A construct exhibited CoA-enhanced turnover of the artificial surrogate ester *p*-nitrophenyl acetate in vitro, while physiological substrates remain unknown [PMID:42469586]. Per `projects/ENZYME_SPECIFICITY.md`, real in vitro surrogate activity of unknown physiological substrate/relevance is `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED`.
