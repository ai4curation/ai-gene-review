CC   -!- FUNCTION: Protects actin filaments from depolymerization (By
CC       similarity). Required for correct morphology of cell membranes and
CC       maturation of intercalated disks of cardiomyocytes via facilitating
CC       localization of XIRP1 and CDH2 to the termini of aligned mature
CC       cardiomyocytes (PubMed:20360251). Thereby required for correct
CC       postnatal heart development and growth regulation that is crucial for
CC       overall heart morphology and diastolic function (PubMed:20360251).
CC       Required for normal electrical conduction in the heart including
CC       formation of the infranodal ventricular conduction system and normal
CC       action potential configuration, as a result of its interaction with the
CC       cardiac ion channel components Scn5a/Nav1.5 and Kcna5/Kv1.5
CC       (PubMed:29306897). Required for regular actin filament spacing of the
CC       paracrystalline array in both inner and outer hair cells of the
CC       cochlea, thereby required for maintenance of stereocilia morphology
CC       (PubMed:25772365). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A4UGR9,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20360251, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25772365,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29306897}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Knockout mice show growth retardation with
CC       reduced body weight and activity with a pale skin appearance
CC       (PubMed:20360251). 15% of newborns show overall heart shape
CC       abnormalities and 58% show ventricular septal defects
CC       (PubMed:20360251). Mouse viability is progressively reduced from birth
CC       and ultimately results in lethality before weaning (postnatal day 17.5
CC       (P17.5)) (PubMed:20360251). At birth the heart weight is normal however
CC       from P3.5 to P12.5 they grow much slower than controls. Disorganized
CC       myofibers are evident at P3.5 (PubMed:20360251). Myocytes show an
CC       increase in apoptosis from P3.5 to P7.5 and a decrease in proliferation
CC       at P7.5 (PubMed:20360251). Decrease in left ventricular internal
CC       dimension and volume during diastole and systole at both P3.5 and
CC       P12.5, which also shows abnormal ventricular filling and a reduction in
CC       the area of the compact myocardium, potentially as a result of
CC       diastolic dysfunction (PubMed:20360251). No difference in
CC       mechanotransduction in functional hair cells at P5 in either the
CC       cochlea or the utricle (PubMed:25772365). Increase in stereocilia gaps
CC       in the utricle initially at P6 and significantly so at P20 with a
CC       similar increase in cochlear inner hair cells seen at P20
CC       (PubMed:37294664). Normal overall stereocilia morphology however
CC       irregular actin filament spacing with numerous gaps and disordered
CC       regions within the paracrystalline array in both inner and outer hair
CC       cells from both the base and apex of the cochlea and in vestibular hair
CC       cells at P7 (PubMed:25772365). Short, disordered protrusions extending
CC       from outer hair cell apical surfaces at the fonticulusin in some mice
CC       are initially evident at postnatal day 7 (P7), by P11 almost all hair
CC       cells have one if not two and they are frequently longer than 10 um,
CC       suggestive of disorganization of the cuticular plate (PubMed:25772365).
CC       Hair cell kinocilia persist in outer hair cells at P9 and a few remain
CC       at P11, suggesting developmental delay (PubMed:25772365). Stereocilia
CC       loss in inner hair cell bundles is first evident at P9 and progresses
CC       to the middle turn by P11 and P13, the tallest row of stereocilia is
CC       also 5-10% thinner at both P11 and P13 (PubMed:25772365). The tallest
CC       row of outer hair cell stereocilia are slight thicker at both P11 and
CC       P13, additionally overall outer hair cell stereocilia are shorter
CC       (PubMed:25772365). Cardiomyocytes are irregularly shaped and have a
CC       smaller cell width at P12.5 (PubMed:20360251). No change in expression
CC       of Xirp1, alpha-actinin and Tpm1 in the heart between birth and P12.5
CC       (PubMed:20360251). Significant delay in switching off Tnnl1 expression
CC       in heart tissue, expression is still present at P13.5
CC       (PubMed:20360251). Echocardiogram shows a 45% reduction in conduction
CC       velocity in left ventricles (PubMed:29306897). Significantly prolonged
CC       RP interval and prolonged QT and corrected QT interval, suggesting a
CC       defect in the infranodal ventricular conduction system at P14 and P15
CC       (PubMed:29306897). Disorganization of the myocardium is evident from
CC       14.5 dpc (PubMed:20360251). Loss of Cdh2 and Xirp1 localization to the
CC       termini of intercalated disks in mature cardiomyocytes at P16.5
CC       (PubMed:20360251). As a result, intercalated disks at the cell termini
CC       are smaller and the cell membranes at the termini are less convoluted
CC       and wavy (PubMed:20360251). All mice show an atrioventricular
CC       conduction block with numerous P waves followed by no QRS wave at P18
CC       (PubMed:29306897). Decrease in Ache activity at the endocardial surface
CC       of the right ventricle, suggesting a developmental defect in the
CC       Purkinje fibers at P18 (PubMed:29306897). Decrease in Gja5/Cx40
CC       expression in the His-bundle of the heart, which may further suggest a
CC       defect in the infranodal conduction system (PubMed:29306897).
CC       Ventricular myocytes have increased action potential amplitude, less
CC       negative max diastolic potential, and shorter action potential
CC       depolarization (PubMed:29306897). Depressed outward potassium ion
CC       currents and current densities, and significantly increased amplitudes
CC       and current densities of rectifier potassium ion currents
CC       (PubMed:29306897). Increase in stereocilia gaps one week following
CC       noise exposure in the cochlea (PubMed:37294664). Loss of Actg1
CC       induction at noise-induced gaps in utricle and inner hair cell
CC       stereocilia (PubMed:37294664). Loss of Actb and Espn expression at
CC       noise-induced gaps in utricle stereocilia (PubMed:37294664). Mild
CC       hearing loss across all frequencies by 20 weeks of age, hearing loss
CC       becomes progressively worse over time (PubMed:37294664). Increase in
CC       hearing loss 2 weeks following damage-inducing noise levels across all
CC       frequencies as a result of impaired hearing recovery (PubMed:37294664).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20360251, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25772365,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:29306897, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37294664}.
