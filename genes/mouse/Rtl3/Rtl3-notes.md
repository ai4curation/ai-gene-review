# Rtl3 / Zcchc5 (Q6P1Y1) notes

Mouse ortholog of human RTL3. Nuclear CCHC DNA-binding protein [PMID:27446912 "Zcchc5 encodes a nuclear protein containing a CX2CX4HX4C DNA-binding motif ... allowing DNA binding to regulate transcription"]; overexpression decreases satellite-cell self-renewal [PMID:27446912 "ZFP354C and ZCCHC5 decreased self-renewal capacity when overexpressed"].
Mouse likely encodes a CA-PR fusion via -1 programmed frameshift (overlapping ORFs), unlike human [PMID:38507667 "In the mouse Rtl3 ortholog, a simple −1 frameshift would result in a fusion protein that contains both CA and PR domains"].
Calls: ND MF/CC/BP MODIFY to nucleic acid binding / zinc ion binding / nucleus / regulation of satellite cell proliferation; IEA nucleic acid + zinc binding ACCEPT.
