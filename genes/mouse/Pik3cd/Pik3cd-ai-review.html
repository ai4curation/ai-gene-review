Gene Description

Mouse Pik3cd encodes p110δ, a class IA phosphoinositide 3-kinase catalytic subunit that associates with p85-family regulatory proteins. Its production of PIP3 couples receptor activation to BTK, AKT and other membrane-recruited effectors. In B cells, p110δ supports antigen-receptor signaling, calcium responses, activation and maintenance of B1 and marginal-zone populations. Its functions extend beyond lymphocytes: spleen stromal p110δ affects chemokine expression and lymphoid organization, and endothelial p110δ contributes to pathological retinal angiogenesis.
