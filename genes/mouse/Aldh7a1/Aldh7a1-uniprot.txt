CC   -!- FUNCTION: Aldehyde dehydrogenase enzyme that mediates important
CC       protective effects. Protects cells from oxidative stress by
CC       metabolizing a number of lipid peroxidation-derived aldehydes. Involved
CC       in cellular defense against hyperosmotic stress by metabolizing betaine
CC       aldehyde to betaine, an important cellular osmolyte and methyl donor.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P49419}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 1]: Involved in lysine catabolism in the brain by
CC       mediating the conversion of L-aminoadipate-semialdehyde ((S)-2-amino-6-
CC       oxohexanoate) to L-2-aminoadipate. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32969477}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Isoform 2]: Acts as a key inhibitor of ferroptosis both by
CC       generating membrane NADH and decreasing the level of reactive
CC       aldehydes. Recruited to plasma membrane in response to ferroptotic
CC       stress and phosphorylation by AMPK, generating membrane NADH to support
CC       AIFM2/FSP1 activity, an essential ferroptosis suppressor protein. Also
CC       directly inhibits ferroptosis by decreasing lipid peroxidation via
CC       consumption of reactive aldehydes, such as 4-hydroxynonenal (4-HNE) and
CC       malonaldehyde. Also acts as a regulator of cellular energy homeostasis
CC       in response to cellular energy stress, such as starvation and hypoxia,
CC       by inhibiting COPI-mediated intracellular transport, thereby reducing
CC       cellular energy consumption. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P49419}.
