CC   -!- FUNCTION: As a major transporter of conjugated bile salts from plasma
CC       into the hepatocyte, it plays a key role in the enterohepatic
CC       circulation of bile salts necessary for the solubilization and
CC       absorption of dietary fat and fat-soluble vitamins (PubMed:10209268).
CC       It is strictly dependent on the extracellular presence of sodium
CC       (PubMed:10209268, PubMed:34060352). It exhibits broad substrate
CC       specificity and transports various bile acids, such as taurocholate,
CC       cholate, as well as non-bile acid organic compounds, such as estrone
CC       sulfate (PubMed:10209268, PubMed:34060352). Works collaboratively with
CC       the ileal transporter (NTCP2), the organic solute transporter (OST),
CC       and the bile salt export pump (BSEP), to ensure efficacious biological
CC       recycling of bile acids during enterohepatic circulation (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q14973,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10209268, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34060352}.
