CC   -!- FUNCTION: DNA-binding transcription factor that plays a central role in
CC       the differentiation and development of type B pancreatic cells, which
CC       are located within the pancreatic islets of Langerhans responsible for
CC       the production and release of insulin (PubMed:10364449,
CC       PubMed:10567552, PubMed:10891400, PubMed:15930104, PubMed:19665969,
CC       PubMed:9121556). Mainly acts as a transcription repressor that binds to
CC       a common element in the glucagon, insulin and somatostatin promoters
CC       and inhibits their expression (PubMed:10567552, PubMed:10891400).
CC       Prevents differentiation of type A pancreatic cells, probably by
CC       repressing expression of ARX (PubMed:14561778). Competes with another
CC       transcription factor, PAX6, for this same promoter binding site
CC       (PubMed:10364449). Also plays a key role in intestinal enteroendocrine
CC       cell differentiation by (1) directly promoting differentiation of
CC       enterochromaffin cells and (2) preventing differentiation of other
CC       intestinal enteroendocrine cell types (PubMed:22570716). Prevents
CC       differentiation of other intestinal enteroendocrine cell types by
CC       repressing the expression of ARX, the master transcription factor for
CC       intestinal type-L, -N, -D, -I and -G enteroendocrine cell
CC       differentiation (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O43316,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10364449, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10567552,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10891400, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14561778,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15930104, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19665969,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22570716, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9121556}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mice develop severe diabetes at birth as a result
CC       of altered islet cell development, characterized by a deficiency of
CC       type B (beta) and D (delta) pancreatic cells, together with an increase
CC       of the type A (alpha) pancreatic cell population (PubMed:9121556). Mice
CC       die within three days of birth, (PubMed:9121556). Mice lacking both Arx
CC       and Pax4 display severe hyperglycaemia prior to death and show an
CC       early-onset loss of mature pancreatic A- and B-cells, concomitantly
CC       with a virtually exclusive generation of pancreatic D-cells
CC       (somatostatin-producing) (PubMed:15930104).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15930104, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9121556}.
