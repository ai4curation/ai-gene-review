CC   -!- FUNCTION: Kisspeptins are ligands for the G protein-coupled receptor
CC       KISS1R/GPR54 (By similarity). The hypothalamic KISS1/KISS1R signaling
CC       system plays a central role in the regulation of the hypothalamic-
CC       pituitary-gonadal reproductive axis by modulating the secretion of
CC       gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) from GnRH neurons
CC       (PubMed:15217982, PubMed:15486019, PubMed:15375028, PubMed:15665093,
CC       PubMed:17563351). In these neurons, kisspeptin binding to its receptor
CC       activates G(q)-dependent signaling, leading to phospholipase C (PLC)
CC       activation, and hydrolysis of phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate
CC       (PIP2); the subsequent rise in intracellular calcium levels results in
CC       the inhibition of inward rectifier potassium channels and activation of
CC       TRPC-like cation channels, leading to GnRH neurons depolarization and
CC       stimulation (PubMed:18434521, PubMed:18483150). In addition to this
CC       pathway, kisspeptin also triggers G(q)-independent signaling via beta-
CC       arrestin, leading to MAPK cascade activation and ERK1/ERK2
CC       phosphorylation (PubMed:25147978). Kisspeptins are also involved in the
CC       regulation of other processes including cell growth, cell proliferation
CC       and cell migration (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q15726,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15217982, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15375028,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15486019, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15665093,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18434521, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18483150,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25147978}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Kisspeptin-10]: Binds the G protein-coupled receptor
CC       KISS1R/GPR54 and triggers G protein-coupled receptor signaling via
CC       activation of G(q) and phosphatidylinositol hydrolysis by phospholipase
CC       C (By similarity). Binding to the receptor also activates beta-
CC       arrestin-dependent signaling resulting in ERK1/2 phosphorylation
CC       (PubMed:25147978). Activation of the receptor inhibits cell
CC       proliferation and cell migration, and is involved in the regulation of
CC       trophoblast invasion during early stages of pregnancy. Is also involved
CC       in the modulation of airway smooth muscle cells migration (By
CC       similarity). In bone tissue, activation of KISS1R by kisspeptin-10
CC       recruits phosphatase DUSP18 and SRC to the KISS1R C-terminus through a
CC       G(q)-dependent signaling pathway. This leads to DUSP18-mediated
CC       dephosphorylation of SRC, down-regulation of osteoclast differentiation
CC       and activity, and consequently suppression of bone resorption
CC       (PubMed:38346942). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q15726,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25147978, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38346942}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Metastin]: Receptor binding triggers G protein-coupled
CC       receptor signaling via activation of G(q) and phosphatidylinositol 4,5-
CC       bisphosphate (PIP2) hydrolysis by phospholipase C.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q15726}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Animals are viable but fail to undergo sexual
CC       maturation. Mutant female do not progress through the estrous cycle,
CC       have thread-like uteri and small ovaries, and do not produce mature
CC       Graffian follicles. Mutant males have small testes, and spermatogenesis
CC       arrested mainly at the early haploid spermatid stage. Both sexes have
CC       low circulating gonadotropin (LH and FSH) and sex steroid (beta-
CC       estradiol or testosterone) hormone levels. Migration of GnRH neurons
CC       into the hypothalamus appears normal with appropriate axonal
CC       connections to the median eminence and total GnRH content. The
CC       hypothalamic-pituitary axis is functional, as shown by robust LH
CC       secretion after peripheral administration of kisspeptin. Conditional as
CC       well as whole body knockout causes osteoclast hyperactivation and bone
CC       loss (PubMed:38346942). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17563351,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38346942}.
