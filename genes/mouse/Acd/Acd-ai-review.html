Gene Description

Essential shelterin adaptor subunit (commonly termed TPP1) in Mus musculus that tethers the rodent-duplicated single-stranded telomeric G-overhang-binding paralogs POT1a and POT1b to TIN2 and the duplex telomere-binding TRF1/TRF2 scaffold, while also recruiting the CST terminator subunit STN1 (OBFC1). Mouse Acd (416 amino acids) comprises an N-terminal oligonucleotide/oligosaccharide-binding (OB) fold preceded by the hydrophobic PWI motif (Pro11-Trp12-Ile13) and bearing the acidic TEL-patch loop (Asp78-Trp79-Glu80-Glu81-Lys82-Glu83), a central POT1a/POT1b-binding region, and a C-terminal TIN2-binding motif. Acd does not bind telomeric DNA directly; by recruiting POT1a and POT1b to telomeric repeat chromatin, it represses ATR-dependent DNA damage signaling, excessive 5' C-strand resection, and non-homologous end-joining chromosome fusions, while engaging TERT via its OB-fold TEL patch to promote telomerase-mediated telomere maintenance in stem and progenitor compartments. Hypomorphic splicing mutations (acd) cause p53-dependent apoptotic developmental defects in adrenal, urogenital, and caudal axial tissues alongside acute hematopoietic stem cell failure.

Acd (Q5EE38) Curation Notes — Mouse Shelterin Subunit TPP1

1. Gene & Protein Overview

  • Organism & Identifiers: Mus musculus Acd (MGI:87873, UniProtKB:Q5EE38, sequence version 1, 416 aa; PANTHER:PTHR14487 / PANTHER:PTHR14487:SF3 ADRENOCORTICAL DYSPLASIA PROTEIN HOMOLOG), commonly referred to in mouse telomere biology as Tpp1 or Acd.
  • Conserved Domain & Residue Architecture:
  • Unlike human ACD, which produces both a 458-aa somatic TPP1-S (Q96AP0) and a spermatid-enriched 544-aa TPP1-L isoform (PMID:31216472), mouse Q5EE38 initiates directly at the conserved NOB (N-terminal of OB) start (MSDSGLLALQPWIRELIL..., 416 aa) and lacks the primate-type 86-aa basic upstream extension.
  • NOB motif: P11-W12-I13 (LLALQPWIRELIL, positions 11–13 in Q5EE38 SV 1, identical 1-based coordinates to human Q96AP0).
  • TEL patch acidic loop: D78-W79-E80-E81-K82-E83 (DWEEKE at positions 78–83 in Q5EE38 SV 1, shifted by −2 relative to D80–E85 in human Q96AP0), plus R92 and L95.
  • POT1a/POT1b-interacting domain: aa 156–245 (FT REGION 156..245). Rodents underwent duplication of Pot1 into Pot1a (repressing ATR DNA damage signaling at telomeres) and Pot1b (preventing excessive 5' C-strand resection / regulating 3' overhang length), and mouse Acd/TPP1 is required to tether both POT1a and POT1b to telomeres (PMID:17053789, PMID:17632522, PMID:22101936).
  • TIN2-interacting C-terminus & STN1 (CST) interaction (PMID:19648609).

2. Genetics of the acd Hypomorphic Mutant vs. Conditional Nulls

  1. Spontaneous acd splice-donor hypomorph (PMID:15537664, PMID:18185984, PMID:19660449, PMID:22038471):
  2. Positional cloning by Keegan et al. 2005 (PMID:15537664) identified an intronic splice-donor mutation in Acd as the cause of autosomal recessive adrenocortical dysplasia (acd), characterized by adrenocortical hypoplasia, urogenital ridge malformations, caudal regression/vertebral segmentation defects, and preaxial/postaxial limb anomalies.
  3. Else et al. 2008 (PMID:18185984) showed that acd/acd MEFs exhibit telomere deprotection, DNA damage foci (TIFs), and end-to-end chromosome/chromatid fusions (GO:0016233 telomere capping, GO:0031848 protection from non-homologous end joining at telomere).

  4. Crucially, Vlangos et al. 2009 (PMID:19660449) proved that the skeletal, caudal-axis, and limb malformations in acd embryos are caused by p53-dependent apoptosis downstream of telomere dysfunction and are rescued on a Trp53⁻/⁻ background (whereas TAp63 loss does not rescue them, PMID:22038471). Thus, the MGI IMP annotations to GO:0001501 (skeletal system development), GO:0001655 (urogenital system development), GO:0030326 (embryonic limb morphogenesis), and GO:0035282 (segmentation) with qualifier acts_upstream_of_or_within represent pleiotropic downstream consequences of telomere uncapping in rapidly proliferating progenitors rather than direct morphogenetic roles (MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED).

  5. Conditional Acd knockdown/knockout in MEFs and stem cells (PMID:17632522, PMID:22101936, PMID:24316971):

  6. Depletion or deletion of Acd in MEFs (PMID:17632522, PMID:22101936, PMID:24316971) abolishes POT1a and POT1b localization to telomeres, unleashing ATR-dependent telomere DNA damage signaling, excess 3' G-overhang generation, and non-homologous end joining fusions.
  7. In hematopoietic stem cells (PMID:24316971), acute Acd deletion causes rapid cell-cycle arrest and stem-cell depletion that is NOT rescued by Trp53 deletion.
