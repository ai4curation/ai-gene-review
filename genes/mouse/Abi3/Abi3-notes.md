# Research & Curation Notes: mouse Abi3 (UniProtKB:Q8BYZ1)

## 1. Identity & Subfamily Divergence (`PTHR10460:SF7`)
- **Gene / Protein**: Mouse `Abi3` (ABI gene family member 3; synonym `Nesh`, `MGI:1913860`), canonical sequence `Q8BYZ1-1` (367 aa, `sequence_version: 3`), PANTHER subfamily `PANTHER:PTHR10460:SF7` (`ABI GENE FAMILY MEMBER 3`), 1:1 ortholog of human `ABI3` (`Q9P2A4`).
- **Architecture & Functional Divergence from `Abi1`/`Abi2`**:
  - Retains the conserved N-terminal WAVE-binding helical region (`COILED 36..64` overlapping the WAB/HHR helical bundle) and C-terminal SH3 domain (residues 309–367), enabling incorporation into a mutually exclusive pentameric WAVE2 regulatory complex (`GO:0031209` `SCAR complex`) with WAVE2 (`Wasf2`), Nap1 (`Nckap1`), CYFIP1/2, and BRK1 (`PMID:17101133`, `PMID:26428302`).
  - **Crucial divergence (`PMID:17101133`, `PMID:26428302`)**: Unlike `Abi1` (`SF2`) and `Abi2` (`SF26`), `Abi3`/NESH lacks the inter-domain c-Abl-binding determinants, **does NOT bind c-Abl or promote c-Abl-mediated tyrosine phosphorylation of WAVE2 or Mena** (`PMID:17101133`), and forms a functionally distinct WAVE2 complex that **inhibits WAVE2 translocation to the plasma membrane (`GO:1903077`) and negatively regulates lamellipodium assembly (`GO:0010593`)** and cell motility (`PMID:11956071`, `PMID:26428302`). Ectopic ABI3 expression suppresses tumor cell motility/metastasis and induces p21-associated cellular senescence (`PMID:11956071`, `PMID:21223585`).
