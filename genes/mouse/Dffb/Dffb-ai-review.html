Gene Description

Dffb encodes CAD/DFF40, the endonuclease that cleaves chromosomal DNA during apoptotic execution. CAD folds in association with ICAD/Dffa and remains inhibited until executioner-caspase cleavage of ICAD releases nuclease activity. Cytosolic and nuclear pools can participate, with localization and activation timing dependent on cell context. Active CAD produces apoptotic DNA fragments and can contribute to chromatin remodeling; this DNA-degradation branch is not required for every route to cell death.
