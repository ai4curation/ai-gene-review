

## Full BCR-panel annotation reassessment (2026-10-26): mouse/Blnk

All 38 baseline annotations were assessed, including functions outside BCR signaling. Original terms, evidence, qualifiers, source partners and flags are unchanged. The panel acquisition performed real Falcon/fallback attempts and publication caching; provider attempts failed and no provider prose was fabricated. Prior substantive human BLNK source work was rechecked and retained where sound.

Native discovery PMID:9705962 and cross-species endogenous co-immunoprecipitation in full 21822214 corroborate the adaptor role. The latter also uses tagged primary-mouse/DT40 rescue constructs; those assays are not automatically native mouse-protein measurements. Mouse SCIMP Tyr120 recognition is distinct from human Tyr131 and from binding to someone else’s SH2 domain. Full 22949502 establishes inducible BLNK-dependent expression effects, without making BLNK a transcription factor. Lipid/condensate claims retain explicit orthology inference from human biochemistry, supported by conserved murine CIN85/CD2AP assembly rather than a claimed mouse phase-separation assay. PMID:25331958 is a real IRF8 screen whose exact BLNK pair requires the pair-level evidence; neither source is rejected just for its title.
