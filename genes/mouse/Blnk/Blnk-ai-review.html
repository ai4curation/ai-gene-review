Gene Description

BLNK (SLP-65/BASH) is a noncatalytic B-cell signaling adaptor. SYK-dependent phosphorylation creates docking sites for phospholipase Cgamma and other effectors, while BLNK’s SH2 domain couples it to phosphorylated receptors and SYK. Constitutive associations with CIN85/CD2AP help organize complexes that move from the cytoplasm to activated receptor sites at the plasma membrane. BLNK supports sustained calcium and transcriptional responses, pre-B-cell differentiation and feedback that downmodulates pre-BCR expression. It supplies spatial organization and binding interfaces rather than kinase or phospholipase catalysis.
