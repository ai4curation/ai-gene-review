CC   -!- FUNCTION: In the hair cortex, hair keratin intermediate filaments are
CC       embedded in an interfilamentous matrix, consisting of hair keratin-
CC       associated proteins (KRTAP), which are essential for the formation of a
CC       rigid and resistant hair shaft through their extensive disulfide bond
CC       cross-linking with abundant cysteine residues of hair keratins. The
CC       matrix proteins include the high-sulfur and high-glycine-tyrosine
CC       keratins. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with hair keratins. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed at high levels in skin where it is found
CC       predominantly in the keratogenous zone of hair follicle cortical cells
CC       and at lower levels in the epithelium of the developing mammary gland.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10446281, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9287118,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9804342}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: In the developing mammary gland, expression is
CC       barely detected in the immature gland at 3 weeks after birth but is
CC       evident at the onset of puberty at 3.5 weeks. Expression is high at 4
CC       weeks, declines at 4.5 weeks and is undetectable at 5 weeks. No
CC       expression is detected in the mature mammary gland during estrus,
CC       pregnancy, lactation or involution. In skin, expression starts shortly
CC       after birth and reaches a first maximum at 9 days. A second peak of
CC       expression is observed at 3.5 weeks, then levels decline and remain low
CC       in the adult. During the hair growth cycle, detected in mid- and late-
CC       anagen stages but not in catagen, telogen or early anagen phases.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10446281, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9287118,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9804342}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the PMG family. {ECO:0000255}.
