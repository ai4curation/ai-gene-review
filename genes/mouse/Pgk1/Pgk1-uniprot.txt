CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes one of the two ATP producing reactions in the
CC       glycolytic pathway via the reversible conversion of 1,3-
CC       diphosphoglycerate to 3-phosphoglycerate. Both L- and D- forms of
CC       purine and pyrimidine nucleotides can be used as substrates, but the
CC       activity is much lower on pyrimidines. In addition to its role as a
CC       glycolytic enzyme, it seems that PGK-1 acts as a polymerase alpha
CC       cofactor protein (primer recognition protein). Acts as a protein kinase
CC       when localized to the mitochondrion where it phosphorylates pyruvate
CC       dehydrogenase kinase PDK1 to inhibit pyruvate dehydrogenase complex
CC       activity and suppress the formation of acetyl-coenzyme A from pyruvate,
CC       and consequently inhibit oxidative phosphorylation and promote
CC       glycolysis. May play a role in sperm motility.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P00558}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Monomer. Interacts with kinase MAPK1/ERK2; the interaction is
CC       direct, occurs under hypoxic conditions, and promotes its interaction
CC       with PIN1. Interacts with peptidyl-prolyl cis-trans isomerase PIN1; the
CC       interaction is direct, occurs under hypoxic conditions, and targets the
CC       protein to the mitochondrion by promoting interactions with the TOM
CC       complex. Interacts with mitochondrial circRNA mcPGK1 (via its 2nd stem-
CC       loop); the interaction is direct and targets the protein to the
CC       mitochondrion by promoting interactions with the TOM complex. Interacts
CC       with pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase PDK1; the interaction is direct,
CC       occurs under hypoxic conditions and leads to PDK1-mediated inhibition
CC       of pyruvate dehydrogenase complex activity.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P00558}.
