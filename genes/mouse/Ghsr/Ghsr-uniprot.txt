CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor specific to ghrelin, an appetite-
CC       regulating peptide hormone commonly found in stomach (By similarity).
CC       Upon activation, stimulates appetite and promotes growth hormone
CC       secretion (PubMed:15070777, PubMed:16322794). Ghrelin binding causes a
CC       conformation change that triggers signaling via guanine nucleotide-
CC       binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the activity of downstream
CC       effectors, such as phospholipase C (By similarity). GHSR is coupled to
CC       G(q) G proteins and mediates production of diacylglycerol (DAG) and
CC       inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate (IP3) (By similarity). Also binds other
CC       growth hormone releasing peptides (GHRP) as well as non-peptide, low
CC       molecular weight secretagogues (By similarity). Also plays a role in
CC       synaptic plasticity independently of ghrelin by forming a heteromer
CC       with the dopamine receptors DRD1 and DRD2 in neurons (PubMed:22284186,
CC       PubMed:26590421). Following DRD1 activation by dopamine in hippocampal
CC       neurons, GHSR acts as a coreceptor, resulting in DRD1 coupling to G(q)
CC       at the expense of G(s), initiating hippocampal synaptic plasticity
CC       involved in memory and learning (PubMed:26590421). Following DRD2
CC       activation by dopamine in lumbosacral defecation centers, GHSR acts as
CC       a coreceptor, resulting in DRD1 coupling to G(q) at the expense of
CC       G(i)/G(o), regulating defecation (PubMed:40441153).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q92847, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15070777,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16322794, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22284186,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26590421, ECO:0000269|PubMed:40441153}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated by octanoylated ghrelin; octanoylation
CC       is essential for full activation of the receptor (By similarity).
CC       Stimulated by anamorelin, which binds to the same binding sites as
CC       ghrelin (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q92847}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the heterotrimeric G protein complex composed
CC       of 3 units, alpha, beta and gamma; this complex may consist of GNB1 and
CC       GNG2 (By similarity). Interacts with DRD1; forms a heteromer with DRD1
CC       in hippocampal neurons (PubMed:26590421). Interacts with DRD2; forms a
CC       heteromer with DRD2 in neurons (PubMed:22284186).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q92847, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22284186,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26590421}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q92847};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q92847}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mice display a healthy, lean phenotype,
CC       characterized by resistance to diet-induced obesity, improved insulin
CC       sensitivity, and lower blood glucose levels (PubMed:16322794). They
CC       frequently show reduced food intake, particularly in response to high-
CC       fat diets (PubMed:16322794). In contrast, no differences in cumulative
CC       food intake have been observed in response to standard chow diet
CC       (PubMed:15070777). Mice are unable to promote promotes growth hormone
CC       secretion (PubMed:15070777). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15070777,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16322794}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor 1 family.
CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00521}.
