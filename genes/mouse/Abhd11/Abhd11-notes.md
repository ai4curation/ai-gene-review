# Curation Notes for Mouse `Abhd11` (`UniProtKB:Q8K4F5`)

## 1. Gene Identity, Sequence, and Catalytic Triad

- **Gene / Accession**: *Mus musculus* `Abhd11` (synonym `Wbscr21`), `MGI:MGI:1916008`, `UniProtKB:Q8K4F5` (`ABHDB_MOUSE`, reviewed Swiss-Prot entry, 307 aa, `sv=1`), `PANTHER:PTHR46118:SF4` (`PROTEIN ABHD11`).
- **Active-site and presequence architecture**: Encodes an N-terminal mitochondrial targeting presequence (`FT TRANSIT 1..34`, mature chain `35..307`), conserved oxyanion-hole motif `65-HGLFG-69` (within `62-VFLHGLFGSK-71`), nucleophilic elbow serine **`Ser133`** (`129-LVGHSMGGK-137`, `131-GHSMG-135`, `FT ACT_SITE 133`), conserved beta-6 charge-relay aspartate **`Asp157`** (`153-LVVVDISPV-161`, structurally homologous to `Asp113` of *E. coli* YbfF `PDB:3BF7` `PMID:18215690` and `Asp156` of human `Q8NFV4`), and general-base histidine **`His288`** (`284-PNAGHWVHS-292`, `FT ACT_SITE 288`) (`genes/human/ABHD11/ABHD11-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md`). Note that UniProt `FT ACT_SITE 229` (`Asp229` in `224-WRLNLDTLA-232`) in the alpha-helical cap domain is an unverified `ECO:0000250` projection rather than the structural beta-6 triad acid.

## 2. Direct Experimental Evidence in *Mus musculus* vs Orthology

1. **Mitochondrial localization (`PMID:18614015`, `PMID:32579589`)**:
   - Pagliarini et al. 2008 (`PMID:18614015`, MitoCarta) identified endogenous mouse `Abhd11` across 14 mouse tissues in the high-confidence mitochondrial proteome (`HDA` for `GO:0005739`).
   - Escoubet et al. 2020 (`PMID:32579589`) confirmed mitochondrial localization of ABHD11 in mouse brain (`IDA` for `GO:0005739`). Matrix sub-compartment localization (`GO:0005759`) and physical association with the 2-oxoglutarate dehydrogenase complex (`GO:0045252`) are directly conserved from 90%-identical human `ABHD11` (`PMID:32792488`), and active-site inhibition of mouse ABHD11 with ML226 in cultured murine C2C12 myoblasts directly decreases functional DLST lipoylation (`PMID:32792488`).
2. **Physiological OGDHc / TCA cycle maintenance and immunometabolic phenotypes (`PMID:32792488`, `PMID:41184294`, `PMID:32733886`)**:
   - Bailey et al. 2020 (`PMID:32792488`) showed in murine C2C12 myoblasts that pharmacological inhibition of ABHD11 with ML226 impairs functional lipoylation of OGDHc-E2 (`DLST`), matching human cells.
   - Jenkins et al. 2025 (`PMID:41184294`, "Here, we show that pharmacological inhibition of ABHD11 dampens cytokine production by human and mouse T-cells. Mechanistically, the anti-inflammatory effects of ABHD11 inhibition are attributed to increased 24,25-epoxycholesterol (24,25-EC) biosynthesis and subsequent liver X receptor (LXR) activation, which arise from a compromised TCA cycle.") demonstrated in mouse T cells and female murine accelerated type 1 diabetes models in vivo that ABHD11 maintains alpha-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase (`OGDHc`) / TCA cycle function, and its inhibition suppresses effector cytokine production and delays diabetes onset.
   - Liu et al. 2020 (`PMID:32733886`) reported that `Abhd11` knockout in mouse embryonic stem cells impairs expansion and differentiation and perturbs metabolic gene expression, a downstream cellular phenotype consistent with impaired mitochondrial 2-oxoglutarate/TCA cycle homeostasis rather than direct glycerolipid hydrolysis.
3. **Whole-body `Abhd11` knockout mice refute physiological DAG lipase / 2-AG synthesis (`PMID:32579589`, `PMID:27544863`)**:
   - Escoubet et al. 2020 (`PMID:32579589`, "In vivo preclinical studies demonstrated that knock-out ABHD11 mice have a similar 2AG level as WT mice and exhibit a lean metabolic phenotype.") generated `Abhd11-/-` mice and showed that endogenous 2-arachidonoylglycerol (`2-AG`) levels across mouse tissues are indistinguishable from wild-type littermates, refuting a physiological diacylglycerol lipase role (`GO:0016298`, `GO:0120516`, `GO:0006629`). Male `Abhd11-/-` mice exhibit reduced diet-induced weight gain (linked to altered bile salts and fecal fat excretion) and progressive ocular defects.
