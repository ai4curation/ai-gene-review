

## Full BCR-panel reassessment (2026-10-26): mouse/Btk

All 121 baseline rows were reviewed across B-cell and non-B-cell functions. Original row payloads and raw UniProt/GOA files are preserved; no new annotation was proposed. The shared acquisition ran actual Falcon/fallback attempts and normal publication retrieval; failed providers were not replaced with fabricated reports.

The molecular core is non-receptor tyrosine phosphorylation; PH-domain PIP3 recognition supplies recruitment rather than lipid synthesis. PLCgamma activation by covalent phosphorylation does not establish the independent noncovalent phospholipase-activator MF. Native BCR, mast-cell and myeloid contexts remain distinct; full PMID:34554188 supports NLRP3 substrate phosphorylation and assembly under tested priming/activation conditions, not universal positive inflammatory action. PMID:23544144 establishes negative cytokine regulation during mouse Listeria infection. PMID:31431692 supports a contextual native mouse T-cell role and prevents dismissal of the TCR IBA merely because ITK/TXK are more familiar T-cell kinases.

The rat donor A0A0G2K6M9 / RGD:1359683 was traced through the current QuickGO annotation API to original experimental papers. The durable mouse Btk source-analysis JSON is a provenance trace only, never functional supporting evidence. Normally fetched PMID:25545336 explicitly shows that selective BTK inhibitors attenuate B-cell activation; negative regulation by the drug was incorrectly transferred as negative regulation by BTK. The existing source row is therefore corrected by MODIFY to positive regulation, with original machine fields unchanged. This does not contradict the separate xid pre-B-cell IL-7 negative-proliferation context in PMID:7680896.

Full PMID:28352114 resolves several rat arthritis inferences: systemic treatment lowers IL-6 and raises IL-10, while TNF/IL-17A change only slightly; peritoneal macrophage uptake and disease-state splenic proliferation differ from the isolated normal LPS-stimulated B-cell assay, where inhibitors did not significantly reduce proliferation. Synovial-cell proliferation after systemic treatment does not resolve a synoviocyte-intrinsic BTK mechanism; its existing regulatory attribution is retained as a non-core disease-context outcome, since regulation does not require cell autonomy. The specific IL-17A direction remains unresolved. The toxicity-source PMID:32484067 and remibrutinib-source PMID:32083858 remain abstract-limited, so blood-lineage counts and proteoglycan-catabolism assignments are not adjudicated from general drug efficacy/safety. PMID:10744701 directly documents fungal-spore/endogenous-ROS activation of rat BTK; it does not imply BTK responds to exogenous H2O2 alone.

Nuclear BTK is directly supported by activated murine B-cell Bright association (11120822) and BAM11 fractionation/EGFP study (11007757), not inferred absent from its cytoplasmic function. The full 23478294 RANK/EEIG1 paper supports raft complexes. PMID:14657254 and 15196959 remain limited to abstracts about HSL and Foxc proteins; source-specific uncertainties are retained rather than confident wrong-gene assertions. Bright/ARID3A binding is refined using independent direct association evidence. MyD88/TRIF/CD40, STAC2 and RANK-associated interaction mechanisms remain documented although bare binding labels are removed.


### Final synovial-context adjudication

The earlier draft wording that called the systemic arthritis synoviocyte-proliferation attribution over-annotated is superseded. Regulation of proliferation does not require BTK to act within the proliferating synoviocyte. Full PMID:28352114 supports a disease-context, cell-extrinsic regulatory effect; the existing claim is retained as KEEP_AS_NON_CORE with this limit, without asserting synoviocyte-autonomous BTK.
