Gene Description

BTK is a Tec-family non-receptor protein tyrosine kinase that couples immune-receptor activation to downstream enzymes and adaptor assemblies. Its PH domain recognizes PIP3, promoting recruitment from the cytosol to membrane signaling sites. In B cells, BTK supports PLCgamma2 activation, calcium responses and developmental progression; nuclear association with Bright/ARID3A provides an additional contextual pool. BTK also regulates innate-cell signaling, including NLRP3 assembly and stimulus-dependent cytokine or phagocytic responses. These effects depend on receptor, cell type and activation state: BTK can promote one response while restraining another.
