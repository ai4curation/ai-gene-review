Gene Description

Cd79a encodes Ig-alpha, the ITAM-bearing signaling chain of the B-cell antigen receptor. It pairs with Ig-beta and membrane immunoglobulin at the plasma membrane, supporting receptor assembly and phosphorylation-dependent recruitment of intracellular effectors. Its cytoplasmic ITAM and non-ITAM tyrosines connect receptor engagement to Syk and BLNK-dependent signaling. Mouse developmental and activation phenotypes depend on the mutated site and the availability of the Ig-beta tail. Activated receptor also enters raft-associated and multivesicular antigen-processing compartments.

Core Functions

Provides the Ig-alpha/Ig-beta receptor assembly and signal-transmission component required at B-cell developmental checkpoints. Membrane immunoglobulin recognizes antigen; the CD79 cytoplasmic motifs recruit signaling enzymes rather than catalyzing phosphorylation.
