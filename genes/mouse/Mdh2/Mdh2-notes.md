# Mdh2 notes

## 2026-11-24 review

- Deep research: `just deep-research-falcon mouse Mdh2 --fallback perplexity-lite` failed because the Falcon provider was not available in this environment and the Perplexity fallback was unavailable. This review therefore used the seeded GOA rows, the mouse and human UniProt records, the reviewed human `MDH2` gene review, the PANTHER `PTHR11540` family review, the source GO-CAM review, Reactome gluconeogenesis, TCA-cycle and malate-aspartate shuttle records, and cached publications.
- Core molecular function and location: Mdh2 is the mitochondrial matrix NAD-dependent malate dehydrogenase, EC 1.1.1.37. UniProt records the exact reaction, `(S)-malate + NAD(+) = oxaloacetate + NADH + H(+)`, and its subcellular location as `Mitochondrion matrix`.
- TCA cycle: PANTHER `PTHR11540` scopes the TCA-cycle IBA to the animal mitochondrial MDH2 node (`PTN000899458`), and the family review assesses that node as sound. Molinie et al. 2022 support MDH2-produced oxaloacetate as a regulator of TCA/respiratory-chain fueling [PMID:35063410 "MDH2 produced OAA is a metabolic switch"].
- GO-CAM `61e0e55600001225`: activity `gomodel:61e0e55600001225/61e0e55600001737` uses Mdh2 to reduce mitochondrial oxaloacetate from Pcx to malate before export to the cytosol. The model's `GO:0030060`/`GO:0005759` assertions are core for Mdh2, and `GO:0006094` gluconeogenesis is a real but non-core context for this matrix MDH reaction. PMID:12581790 measured total malate dehydrogenase activity during caloric restriction and is indirect for this specific step.
- Malate-aspartate shuttle: the Mdh2 matrix reaction is also the mitochondrial half of the shuttle; Kim et al. 2013 support an Mdh2-dependent connection to shuttle/redox metabolism during mouse 3T3-L1 adipogenesis [PMID:24134846 "MDH2 acetylation enhances its enzymatic activity"].
- RNA binding: the human 2024 paper directly shows cellular MDH2 RNA binding and reports that interactions are predominantly outside mitochondria [PMID:38609156 "MDH2-RNA interactions occur predominantly outside of mitochondria"]. The mouse transfers were kept as a plausible non-core moonlighting activity.
- Conflicts and outliers: NADP-dependent MDH rows transferred from human/rat were removed because they conflict with the NAD-dependent EC 1.1.1.37 chemistry. The purified inner-membrane proteome [PMID:12865426 "mitochondrial inner membrane"], Atg7 erythroid membrane proteome [PMID:20080761 "mitochondrial proteins were consistently and preferentially detected"], and purified-myelin proteome rows were treated as over-annotated for a soluble matrix enzyme.
