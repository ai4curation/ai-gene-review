# Peg10 (mouse, Q7TN75) — review notes
SIRH1. Calls mirror human PEG10. Key sources:
- KO early embryonic lethality, placental defects [PMID:16341224].
- Gag budding like HIV p24; RNA binding (Hbegf); USP9X [PMID:30951545 "the Gag domain of PEG10 can promote vesicle budding similar to the HIV p24 Gag protein"].
- VLP packaging/transfer of own mRNA [PMID:34413232].
- Protease-motif knock-in: perinatal lethality, fetal-capillary defects [PMID:34559199 "a mutation in the highly conserved viral aspartic protease motif in the POL-like region of PEG10"].
