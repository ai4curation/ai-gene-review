CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the first reaction in the catabolism of the
CC       essential branched chain amino acids leucine, isoleucine, and valine
CC       (PubMed:14755340, PubMed:17767905). Branched chain amino acid
CC       catabolism plays a role in adipocyte differentiation by providing
CC       lipogenic acetyl-CoA pools in differentiated adipocytes
CC       (PubMed:17767905, PubMed:35075301). Mechanistically, acetyl-CoA derived
CC       from branched chain amino acid catabolism is used by EP300/p300 to
CC       acetylate and inhibit PRDM16, thereby preventing adipose tissue
CC       browning (PubMed:35075301). May also function as a transporter of
CC       branched chain alpha-keto acids (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O35854, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14755340,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17767905, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35075301}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Mice display marked elevation of plasma branched-
CC       chain amino acids, ketoaciduria, and features resembling human maple

CC       along with increased energy expenditure (PubMed:17767905). Cells also
CC       show a moderate increase in arginine (PubMed:14755340). Conditional
CC       deletion in adipocytes protects mice against obesity (PubMed:35075301).
