Gene Description

Mouse Plcg2 encodes PLCγ2, a calcium-dependent phosphoinositide phospholipase that produces IP3 and DAG from PIP2. SH2-mediated recruitment and kinase-dependent activation connect immune receptors to calcium mobilization and downstream transcriptional responses. PLCγ2 supports B-cell maturation, survival and activation, and has distinct myeloid functions in C-type lectin signaling, antifungal responses, and CD14-dependent TLR4 trafficking. It also contributes to platelet, osteoclast and microglial signaling. These tissue functions depend on receptor and adaptor context and do not imply that PLCγ2 is the sole phospholipase in every pathway.

Core Functions

Calcium-dependent PIP2 hydrolysis produces IP3 and DAG downstream of BCR and C-type lectin receptors. IP3 subsequently activates ER calcium release; PLCγ2 is the lipase, not the calcium channel.
