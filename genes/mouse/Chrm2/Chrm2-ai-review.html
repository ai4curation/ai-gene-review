Gene Description

Chrm2 encodes the M2 muscarinic acetylcholine receptor, a seven-transmembrane GPCR that couples acetylcholine recognition to Gi/o-family signaling. It inhibits adenylyl cyclase in appropriate signaling contexts and regulates ion channels through G protein subunits. M2 receptors slow cardiac pacemaker activity and act as inhibitory presynaptic autoreceptors in selected brain regions, including hippocampus and cerebral cortex. Receptor localization and downstream effects vary by cell type, and M4 has the predominant autoreceptor role in mouse striatum.
