CC   -!- FUNCTION: Muscarinic receptor for acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter
CC       found in the brain, neuromuscular junctions and the autonomic ganglia.
CC       Ligand binding causes a conformation change that triggers signaling via
CC       guanine nucleotide-binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the
CC       activity of downstream effectors, such as adenylate cyclase. CHRM2 is
CC       coupled to G(i)/G(o) (GNAI1 or GNAO1) G proteins and mediates signaling
CC       by inhibiting adenylate cyclase activity.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08172}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (when phosphorylated) with ARRB1 and ARRB2; the
CC       interaction is associated with internalization of the receptor and
CC       short-term desensitization to the ligand. Interacts with RACK1; the
CC       interaction regulates CHRM2 internalization.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08172}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08172};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08172}.
CC       Postsynaptic cell membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08172}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08172}. Note=Phosphorylation
CC       in response to agonist binding promotes receptor internalization.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P06199}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The P-X-P-P motif is phosphorylated in response to ligand
CC       binding, promoting. association with beta-arrestin (ARRB1 and/or
CC       ARRB2). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08172}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylation of the P-X-P-P motif promotes association with
CC       beta-arrestin (ARRB1 and/or ARRB2), leading to receptor desensitization
CC       and negative regulation of G protein-coupled receptor signaling.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P08172}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the G protein-coupled receptor 1 family.
CC       Muscarinic acetylcholine receptor subfamily. CHRM2 sub-subfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00521}.
