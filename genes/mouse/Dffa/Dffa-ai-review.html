Gene Description

Dffa encodes ICAD/DFF45, the folding chaperone and inhibitor of the CAD/Dffb endonuclease. ICAD-L assists productive CAD folding and remains associated with the nuclease to prevent premature DNA fragmentation. Executioner-caspase cleavage removes the inhibitory constraint during apoptosis. ICAD isoforms differ in chaperone capacity, and loss of Dffa impairs CAD-dependent DNA fragmentation and nuclear remodeling in several mouse cell types. Cytosolic and nuclear DFF complexes provide regulated pools of the nuclease and its inhibitor.

2026-10-26: comparative execution-phase source audit

All34 seeded GOA rows were adjudicated with source payloads preserved. O54786 is mouse ICAD/Dffa; human O00273 is its DFFA ortholog, not a lipid-transfer CIDE protein. Direct mouse CAD/ICAD reconstitution in PMID:9422506 establishes inhibition and chaperone function. PMID:10336474 distinguishes ICAD-L and ICAD-S in its expression/translation conditions; no universal claim that every short isoform lacks all chaperone capacity is made.

Full PMID:14663139 was read beyond its EndoG-focused title. It includes Dffa-null and EndoG/Dffa compound-mutant animals, direct comparisons of lymphoid-cell death, and DNA fragmentation. These Dffa experiments are real. Positive cell-death phenotypes reflect the combined loss of chaperone and inhibitor roles; they do not contradict inhibition by intact ICAD. PMID:10601318 and PMID:9770511 supply native mouse thymocyte/other-cell knockout phenotypes. Those necessity phenotypes are interpreted with the direct biochemical folding work, without inventing a new DNA-cleavage process for the inhibitor.

The transferred plasma-membrane row is UNDECIDED: donor O00273 identity is verified, but the underlying compartment-specific observation was not recovered. Nuclear/cytosolic predominance alone is not proof of absence. Domain binding is refined to inhibitor plus folding-chaperone activity, while tissue-specific apoptosis remains peripheral.

PMID:9422513 has a2015 correction (PMID:26416741; publisher https://www.nature.com/articles/nature15532). The authors report duplicated lanes in Fig1a, repeat the experiment, provide corrected data, and state unchanged conclusions. This is documented as a correction, not a retraction. Independent CAD/ICAD evidence remains available. Caspase3 is not asserted to be the only mammalian ICAD-cleaving caspase.
