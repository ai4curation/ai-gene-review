# Slc25a11 notes

## 2026-11-24 review

- Deep research: `just deep-research-falcon mouse Slc25a11 --fallback perplexity-lite` failed because the Falcon provider was not available in this environment and the Perplexity fallback was unavailable. This review therefore used the seeded GOA rows, the mouse and human UniProt records, the completed human `SLC25A11` review, the malate-aspartate shuttle module, the source GO-CAM review, Reactome gluconeogenesis and shuttle context, and cached publications.
- Core function: Slc25a11 is the mitochondrial 2-oxoglutarate/malate carrier OGC, a multi-pass SLC25 antiporter in the mitochondrial inner membrane. Mouse UniProt curates the core Rhea reaction, `(S)-malate(in) + 2-oxoglutarate(out) = (S)-malate(out) + 2-oxoglutarate(in)`, with direct support from Zuna et al. 2024.
- Malate-aspartate shuttle: the OGC antiporter imports cytosolic malate in exchange for matrix 2-oxoglutarate. Bailis et al. showed that mouse Th1 cells with `Slc25a11` sgRNAs had impaired IFN-gamma production and transcriptional remodeling, supporting the malate-aspartate shuttle row [PMID:31217581 "suggesting the malate-aspartate shuttle is critical during Th1 cell activation"].
- Additional UniProt-curated paper: Gallo et al. link C. elegans MISC-1 and mammalian OGC to mitochondrial morphology and apoptosis, including Caspase-3 activation after OGC knockdown in mouse MIN6 cells [PMID:21448454 "OGC knock-down causes robust cleavage and activation of Caspase-3, an established marker of activation of apoptosis"]. This is non-core context in UniProt and does not add a current mouse GOA row.
- GO-CAMs: activity `gomodel:61e0e55600001225/62f58d8800003911` uses Slc25a11 to export malate from the mitochondrial Mdh2 reaction toward cytosolic Mdh1 in the malate route of gluconeogenesis, and `gomodel:62d0afa500000298/62f58d8800003900` uses it to export the 2-oxoglutarate co-product from mitochondrial Got2 toward cytosolic Got1 in the aspartate route. Both are consistent with the review even though gluconeogenesis is non-core for OGC overall.
- Gluconeogenesis rows: the rat-heart OGC ISO row is thin but compatible with the canonical malate-route carrier role. The 2022 mouse IMP row assays a regulatory AKG/P2RX4/Slc25a11/serpina1e axis that suppresses hepatic gluconeogenesis rather than the modeled metabolite export step, so that row is modified to negative regulation of gluconeogenesis.
- Proton transport: Zuna et al. directly measured recombinant mouse OGC-mediated proton conductance in planar lipid bilayers and found that it required fatty acids or 2,4-dinitrophenol. The proton transporter rows were kept as real but secondary side activity, not the core antiporter function.
