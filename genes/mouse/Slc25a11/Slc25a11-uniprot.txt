CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the transport of 2-oxoglutarate (alpha-
CC       oxoglutarate) across the inner mitochondrial membrane in an
CC       electroneutral exchange for malate (By similarity). Can also exchange
CC       2-oxoglutarate for other dicarboxylic acids such as malonate,
CC       succinate, maleate and oxaloacetate, although with lower affinity (By
CC       similarity). Substrate exchange across the membrane occurs
CC       consecutively with one substrate being transported first, then
CC       dissociating from the substrate binding site before the second
CC       substrate binds for transport in the opposite direction (By
CC       similarity). Does not transport glutathione (By similarity). In
CC       addition can facilitate proton transport in the presence of
CC       protonophores such as long-chain fatty acids, contributing to
CC       mitochondrial uncoupling and regulation of mitochondrial energetic
CC       efficiency (PubMed:38577966). Contributes to several metabolic
CC       processes, including the malate-aspartate shuttle, the
CC       oxoglutarate/isocitrate shuttle, gluconeogenesis from lactate, and
CC       nitrogen metabolism (By similarity). Maintains mitochondrial fusion and
CC       fission events, and the organization and morphology of cristae (By
CC       similarity). Involved in the regulation of apoptosis (PubMed:21448454).
