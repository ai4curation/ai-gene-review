# Curation Notes for Mouse `Apcdd1` (`UniProtKB:Q3U128`, `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2`)

## 1. Protein Identity, Crystal Structure, and Core Function
- **Gene / Accession**: Mouse `Apcdd1` (legacy alias `Drapc1`), `UniProtKB:Q3U128` (514 aa, sequence version 1), classified in `PANTHER:PTHR31021:SF2` (`PROTEIN APCDD1`).
- **Direct Structural & Biochemical Characterization (`PMID:37155902`, `PDB:8E0P`, `8E0R`, `8E0W`)**: Although mouse GOA currently carries `GO:0017147` (`Wnt-protein binding`) and `GO:0030178` (`negative regulation of Wnt signaling pathway`) only via homology/PAINT (`IBA`, `IEA`, `ISO`, `ISS`), **mouse APCDD1 (`UniProtKB:Q3U128`) is the exact ortholog crystallized by Hsieh et al. (2023, `PMID:37155902`)**. Its secreted glycosylated extracellular domain (`aa 27–482`, monomeric in solution at ~58 kDa) folds into two antiparallel beta-barrel domains (`ABD1` and `ABD2`) joined by six disulfide bonds including interdomain `Cys52–Cys466` and three N-glycans (`Asn100`, `Asn168`, `Asn319`). `ABD2` harbors a ~350 Å³ hydrophobic pocket that co-purifies with stearic acid (C18:0) and accommodates palmitoleate (`PAM`), mediating detergent-sensitive capture of lipidated WNT7A while showing **no detectable binding** to LRP5 or LRP6 `PE1-4` ectodomains (`PMID:37155902`). Note that stearic acid co-purification from HEK293 expression cells reflects occupancy of the WNT palmitoleoyl-recognition pocket rather than a physiological free-fatty-acid transport role, so no separate free-fatty-acid binding GO term is added.
- **Direct In Vivo Genetic Function (`PMID:29154126`, `PMID:20393562`)**:
  - In `PMID:20393562`, full-length mouse `Apcdd1` and secreted `mApcdd1ΔTM` (`aa 1–486`) suppressed canonical Wnt signaling and progenitor proliferation in electroporated chick neural tubes even more potently than human `APCDD1`.
  - In `PMID:29154126` (Mazzoni et al. 2017 *Neuron*), `Apcdd1-/-` mice exhibited precocious Norrin/canonical Wnt signaling in retinal and cerebellar endothelial cells, delayed retinal vessel pruning, and premature paracellular blood-retinal/cerebellar barrier maturation, genetically rescued across `Apcdd1-/-;Ndp-/Y` compound mutants.

## 2. Review of Existing GOA Annotations (18 rows)
- **Core annotations (`ACCEPT`)**:
  - `GO:0005886` (`plasma membrane`, `IBA`, `IEA`, `ISO`, `ISS`): Supported by conserved signal peptide (`aa 1–26`, `Leu9`), C-terminal TM helix (`aa 493–513`), and plasma-membrane colocalization (`PMID:20393562`, `PMID:22500632`).
  - `GO:0017147` (`Wnt-protein binding`, `IBA`, `IEA`, `ISO`, `ISS`): Directly corroborated on mouse `Q3U128` ECD binding WNT7A (`PMID:37155902`).
  - `GO:0030178` (`negative regulation of Wnt signaling pathway`, `IBA`, `IEA`, `ISO`, `ISS`): Directly corroborated on mouse `Apcdd1` in `PMID:20393562`, `PMID:29154126`, `PMID:25946682`, and `PMID:28242765`.
- **Non-core annotations (`KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`)**:
  - `GO:0042802` (`identical protein binding`, `IEA`, `ISO` from human `Q8J025`): Full-length membrane APCDD1 self-associates via its transmembrane region (`PMID:20393562`, `PMID:32320685`), whereas the isolated mouse ECD is monomeric (`PMID:37155902`); auxiliary oligomerization property harmonized with human `APCDD1`.
  - `GO:0001942` (`hair follicle development`, `IEA`, `ISO` from human `Q8J025`): *Apcdd1* is prominently expressed in embryonic hair follicle placodes, dermal papillae, and postnatal cycling follicles (`PMID:15465500`, `PMID:32320685`), though `Apcdd1-/-` mice lack overt alopecia (consistent with human HHS arising from dominant-negative `L9R` rather than simple haploinsufficiency, or functional redundancy in mouse skin). Retained as non-core developmental context harmonized with human `APCDD1`.
  - `GO:0042487` (`regulation of odontogenesis of dentin-containing tooth`, `IMP`, `PMID:26170146`): Antisense oligodeoxynucleotide knockdown of endogenous *Apcdd1* in cultured E13.5 mouse molar tooth germs altered cusp-forming epithelial rearrangements and reduced buccal epithelial proliferation (`PMID:26170146`), consistent with *Apcdd1* expression in dental mesenchyme/epithelium (`PMID:15465500`). Because this term is framed as `regulation of ...`, it accurately reflects a downstream developmental role mediated by pathway antagonism (`KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`).
- **Over-annotated experimental row (`MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED`)**:
  - `GO:0043615` (`astrocyte cell migration`, `IDA`, `PMID:22500632`): Kang et al. (`PMID:22500632`) showed that Sox9 and NFIA directly activate the mouse *Apcdd1* promoter in E12.5 spinal cord, and tested `Apcdd1` function by electroporating wild-type mammalian `Apcdd1` and ER-retained dominant-negative `Apcdd1-L9R` into embryonic **chick** spinal cord (where gain-of-function promoted precocious astrocyte precursor migration and `L9R` reduced migrating precursors by ~50%, alongside HeLa F-actin and HEK293 Rac1/Cdc42 co-IP). Asserting direct participation (`involved_in astrocyte cell migration`, `IDA`) on mouse `Apcdd1` overstates heterologous chick gain-of-function/dominant-negative phenotypes without endogenous mouse *Apcdd1* loss-of-function (`PMID:25946682` explicitly noted conditional astrocyte deletion remained to be done) for a cell-surface Wnt/BMP signaling antagonist.
