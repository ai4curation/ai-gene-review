CC   -!- FUNCTION: Thiol protease involved in different programmed cell death
CC       processes, such as apoptosis, pyroptosis or granzyme-mediated
CC       programmed cell death, by proteolytically cleaving target proteins
CC       (PubMed:16469926, PubMed:19168786). Has a marked preference for Asp-
CC       Glu-Val-Asp (DEVD) consensus sequences, with some plasticity for
CC       alternate non-canonical sequences (PubMed:25231987). Its involvement in
CC       the different programmed cell death processes is probably determined by
CC       upstream proteases that activate CASP7 (PubMed:18667412,
CC       PubMed:35705808). Acts as an effector caspase involved in the execution
CC       phase of apoptosis: following cleavage and activation by initiator
CC       caspases (CASP8 and/or CASP9), mediates execution of apoptosis by
CC       catalyzing cleavage of proteins, such as CLSPN, PARP1, PTGES3 and YY1
CC       (PubMed:16469926). Compared to CASP3, acts as a minor executioner
CC       caspase and cleaves a limited set of target proteins (PubMed:16469926).
CC       Acts as a key regulator of the inflammatory response in response to
CC       bacterial infection by catalyzing cleavage and activation of the
CC       sphingomyelin phosphodiesterase SMPD1 in the extracellular milieu,
CC       thereby promoting membrane repair (PubMed:35705808). Regulates
CC       pyroptosis in intestinal epithelial cells: cleaved and activated by
CC       CASP1 in response to S.typhimurium infection, promoting its secretion
CC       to the extracellular milieu, where it catalyzes activation of SMPD1,
CC       generating ceramides that repair membranes and counteract the action of
CC       gasdermin-D (GSDMD) pores (PubMed:22807671, PubMed:35705808). Regulates
CC       granzyme-mediated programmed cell death in hepatocytes: cleaved and
CC       activated by granzyme B (GZMB) in response to bacterial infection,
CC       promoting its secretion to the extracellular milieu, where it catalyzes
CC       activation of SMPD1, generating ceramides that repair membranes and
CC       counteract the action of perforin (PRF1) pores (PubMed:35705808).
CC       Following cleavage by CASP1 in response to inflammasome activation,
CC       catalyzes processing and inactivation of PARP1, alleviating the
CC       transcription repressor activity of PARP1 (PubMed:22464733). Acts as an
CC       inhibitor of type I interferon production during virus-induced
CC       apoptosis by mediating cleavage of antiviral proteins CGAS, IRF3 and
CC       MAVS, thereby preventing cytokine overproduction (PubMed:30878284).
CC       Cleaves and activates sterol regulatory element binding proteins
CC       (SREBPs) (By similarity). Cleaves phospholipid scramblase proteins
CC       XKR4, XKR8 and XKR9 (PubMed:25231987). Cleaves BIRC6 following
CC       inhibition of BIRC6-caspase binding by DIABLO/SMAC (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P55210, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16469926,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18667412, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19168786,

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25231987, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30878284,

CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: During activation, the N-terminal disordered
CC       prodomain is removed by cleavage (PubMed:18667412). Concomitantly,
CC       double cleavage gives rise to a large Caspase-7 subunit p20 and a small
CC       Caspase-7 subunit p11 (PubMed:18667412). The two large and two small
CC       subunits then assemble to form the active CASP7 complex
CC       (PubMed:18667412). Can be cleaved and activated by different caspases,
CC       depending on the context (PubMed:18667412, PubMed:35705808). Cleaved
CC       and activated by initiator caspases (CASP8 and/or CASP9), leading to
CC       execution phase of apoptosis (By similarity). Cleavage and maturation
CC       by GZMB regulates granzyme-mediated programmed cell death
CC       (PubMed:35705808). Cleavage and maturation by CASP1 regulates
CC       pyroptosis (PubMed:18667412, PubMed:35705808). Inhibited by XIAP, which
CC       directly binds to the active site pocket and obstructs substrate entry
CC       (By similarity). Phosphorylation at Ser-30 and Ser-239 by PAK2 inhibits
CC       its activity (By similarity). Inhibited by BIRC6; following inhibition
CC       of BIRC6-caspase binding by DIABLO/SMAC, BIRC6 is subjected to caspase
CC       cleavage, leading to an increase in active caspases (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P42574, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P55210,
