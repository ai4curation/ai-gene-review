## Direct functions and additional locations

- Mouse Bcl2 AAA knock-in experiments (PMID:22258505) prevent stimulus-induced BCL2–BECN1 dissociation and autophagy. The reciprocal Becn1 F121A interface mutation (PMID:29849149) increases flux. These establish direct autophagy inhibition as core biology, independent of simply keeping cells alive. PMID:36812915 measures BCL2 phosphorylation and BECN1 release in exercised mouse liver. It supports BH3 binding more directly than the molecular-adaptor annotation attached to that experiment.
- PMID:12617961 identifies endogenous calcineurin–Bcl2 complexes in mouse/rat brain mitochondrial, nuclear, microsomal and cytosol fractions. Cytosolic pools are compatible with predominant membrane pools. Mouse UniProt records a beta splice form lacking the alpha membrane anchor, but the source annotations were not assigned to that isoform without evidence. Calcineurin targeting provides an independent, contextual adaptor-like mechanism.
- The crista ISO donor is RGD:2199, rat Bcl2/P49950. Live QuickGO traces its exact GO:0030061 IDA to PMID:9731187. Cryosection immunogold and immunoblotting support rat liver inner-membrane/crista localization; this is retained as a non-core ortholog inference, not rewritten to the outer membrane. Myelin-sheath localization (PMID:7953633) remains uncertain because glial expression in the abstract does not identify the sheath-specific signal and the detailed figures remain inaccessible.
- PMID:17382917 explicitly measures Bcl2–MYC enhancement of uPA/MMP-2 activity in hypoxic mouse vascular smooth muscle. This supports non-core catalytic-activity regulation without claiming Bcl2 is itself a protease.
- PMID:9009190 separates axon regeneration from survival with a ZVAD control. PMID:9547242 observes delayed neuronal maturation despite similar neuronal survival and numbers. PMID:14699151 maps a Bcl2–paxillin BH4 interaction and tests kidney branching. Their non-core outcomes should not be dismissed as uniformly downstream of survival.
- PMID:11983915 reports cardiac myocyte mitotic/cytokinetic features with unchanged low apoptosis. Positive proliferation and cell-cycle retardation are context dependent, not logically incompatible. Hair/pigmentation and toxic-response claims were retained as non-core with their actual biological scope.

## Source-specific corrections and limits

The accompanying [primary-source checks](Bcl2-primary-source-checks.md) document the recovered full texts. PMID:12086670 demonstrates MITF binding to BCL2 promoter DNA, not DNA binding by BCL2 protein; the mouse IEA source is consequently unsuitable. Full PMID:16717086 makes BCL2 the substrate of PP2A and ubiquitination, not the reaction machinery. Full PMID:14660795 likewise assays Bcl2 phosphosite mutants and Bcl2 turnover. These source-specific modification annotations were removed without asserting that Bcl2 can never regulate modification of other proteins.

The original PDZK1, hexokinase-s and GBF-1 paper assignments remain source-verification limits. Their titles/abstracts do not prove misattribution. Independently established antiapoptotic activity and mitochondrial/cytosolic locations were retained with additional relevant evidence. The exact myelin, BCR-pathway and several interaction claims remain UNDECIDED where the available experiment could not be resolved.

Generic binding was refined only where the partner/domain or phosphatase/channel binding was identified. Otherwise it was removed as uninformative or left uncertain when the relevant assay could not be adjudicated. Prediction text and paraphrased provider snippets were removed as supporting quotations; exact primary excerpts and the actual Falcon wording replace them where used. The prior NEW negative-MOMP descendant was withdrawn as redundant with existing apoptotic control coverage, while the mechanism remains in the biological summary and core functions. No new annotation was added.
