Gene Description

Gnb5 encodes the atypical G protein beta subunit Gbeta5, a WD-repeat protein that binds the G gamma-like domains of R7-family RGS proteins. These complexes stabilize their partners and accelerate G alpha inactivation in neuronal, retinal and cardiac signaling. The long retinal product participates in the RGS9-1 phototransduction-regulatory complex, whereas other RGS partners support distinct neuronal and cardiac responses. Gbeta5 can bind selected conventional gamma subunits and form particular experimentally reconstituted heterotrimers, but its predominant native regulatory role is in RGS complexes rather than interchangeable participation in all conventional G protein heterotrimers.

Core Functions

Gbeta5 contributes to the assembly, stability and activity of R7-family GAP complexes. The RGS partner accelerates G alpha GTP hydrolysis; Gbeta5 is a required structural/regulatory component rather than the GTPase itself.
