Query domains identify tRNA-dihydrouridine synthase, DusB-like specificity, an FMN-binding domain, and a conserved catalytic signature. Homolog descriptions at 59.9–100% identity and STRING COG0042 at 99.1% identity agree. Stress and carbon-source fitness phenotypes are heterogeneous and do not establish a catalytic substrate or modification position; the isolated U20 annotation is insufficient to assign positional specificity.
Why this tier
Tier A is supported by convergent enzyme-family evidence from query domains and homolog descriptions, together with condition-specific experimental fitness data. Literature summaries concern expression, protein abundance, or stress phenotypes rather than direct biochemical characterization; no cofitness or STRING functional partners are supplied. A U20-specific assignment and a dedicated heat-stress or nutrient-utilization function are rejected because the evidence does not establish those specific molecular roles.
