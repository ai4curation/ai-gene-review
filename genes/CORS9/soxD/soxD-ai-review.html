Gene Description

SoxD is the 99-residue delta subunit of the cytoplasmic heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase (SoxABGD) of Corynebacterium sp. U-96. Crystal structures show that it binds a single zinc ion in a Cys3His zinc-finger fold and has no flavin or other redox cofactor. The assembled complex oxidises sarcosine at the FAD of the beta subunit, reoxidises its flavins with oxygen to give hydrogen peroxide and, with tetrahydrofolate present, transfers the methylene group to tetrahydrofolate, yielding glycine and 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate (EC 1.5.3.24); without folate it releases formaldehyde. SoxD has no catalytic group of its own and is a structural subunit of the complex; its precise role (assembly, stabilisation of the beta subunit) has not been tested. The soxD gene lies between soxB and soxA in a cluster of sarcosine-to-pyruvate catabolism genes.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

sarcosine oxidase (5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate-forming) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: sarcosine + (6S)-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + O2 = (6R)-5,10-methylene-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + glycine + H2O2.

Justification: This is EC 1.5.3.24 / RHEA:70455, the reaction of heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidases in the presence of tetrahydrofolate. GO:0008115 is defined by the folate-free reaction producing formaldehyde (RHEA:13313), which tetrahydrofolate suppresses in the heterotetramers, so no current GO molecular-function term represents the physiological activity.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors, oxygen as acceptor

Core Functions

Zinc-binding structural (delta) subunit of the heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase, which converts sarcosine, tetrahydrofolate and O2 to glycine, 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate and H2O2. SoxD carries no catalytic cofactor. The folate-free formaldehyde-forming reaction (GO:0008115) is a conditional secondary activity of the complex.
