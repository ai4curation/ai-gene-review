CC   -!- FUNCTION: In the presence of tetrahydrofolate, catalyzes the oxidative
CC       demethylation of sarcosine to yield glycine, 5,10-
CC       methylenetetrahydrofolate and hydrogen peroxide (PubMed:20675294). In
CC       the absence of tetrahydrofolate, catalyzes the oxidative demethylation
CC       of sarcosine to yield glycine, formaldehyde and hydrogen peroxide
CC       (PubMed:17395614, PubMed:20675294, PubMed:7240129). Can also use N-
CC       methyl-L-alanine and N-ethyl-L-glycine (PubMed:7240129). Is very
CC       specific for oxygen as an acceptor (PubMed:7240129).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17395614, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20675294,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7240129, ECO:0000305|PubMed:15914915}.
