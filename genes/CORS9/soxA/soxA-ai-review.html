Gene Description

SoxA is the 965-residue alpha subunit of the cytoplasmic heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase (SoxABGD) of Corynebacterium sp. U-96, a member of the GcvT (aminomethyltransferase) family. The assembled enzyme oxidises sarcosine at the FAD of the beta subunit (SoxB), reoxidises its flavins with molecular oxygen to hydrogen peroxide, and, when tetrahydrofolate is available, transfers the released methylene group to tetrahydrofolate to give glycine and 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate (EC 1.5.3.24); without folate the iminium intermediate is hydrolysed to glycine and free formaldehyde. Crystal structures show that SoxA has two domains, binds one non-covalent NAD+ and carries the folate-binding site through which the iminium intermediate is channelled, consistent with its GcvT-like fold. No iron-sulfur cluster is present despite an N-terminal ferredoxin-like fold. The soxA gene lies in a cluster with serine hydroxymethyltransferase, serine dehydratase and 10-formyltetrahydrofolate hydrolase genes for catabolism of sarcosine to pyruvate.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

sarcosine oxidase (5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate-forming) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: sarcosine + (6S)-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + O2 = (6R)-5,10-methylene-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + glycine + H2O2.

Justification: This proposal describes the complete folate-coupled reaction of heterotetrameric SoxABDG (EC 1.5.3.24; RHEA:70455), distinct from the glycine/formaldehyde-producing GO:0008115 reaction. It is an ontology proposal for the assembled complex, not the activity of each isolated subunit. Structural and biochemical evidence comes from the characterized U-96 heterotetramer; isolated-subunit catalysis is not inferred.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors, oxygen as acceptor

Core Functions

Contribute as the NAD/folate-binding alpha subunit to oxygen-dependent sarcosine oxidation in the assembled heterotetramer. The collective folate-coupled reaction yields glycine and methylenetetrahydrofolate; it is not assigned as an autonomous subunit activity. The folate-free formaldehyde-forming branch remains a conditional secondary complex activity.

Ligand inventory of Corynebacterium sp. U-96 heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase crystal structures

Question: does the SoxA (alpha, chain A) subunit carry an iron-sulfur cluster, as
the InterPro2GO mapping from IPR001041 (2Fe-2S ferredoxin-type domain) implies
(GO:0051536 iron-sulfur cluster binding)? Which subunit carries which cofactor?

Method: python3 pdb_ligands.py queries the RCSB Data API for the non-polymer
entities of the six PDB entries listed in the UniProt records (1VRQ, 1X31, 3AD7,
3AD8, 3AD9, 3ADA; chain A = SoxA Q50LF0, B = SoxB Q50LF2, C = SoxG Q50LE9,
D = SoxD Q50LF1). Raw output: pdb_ligands.tsv (run 2026-11-04).

Results (from pdb_ligands.tsv):

  • Chain A (SoxA): NAD in all six entries; folinic acid (FON) in 1VRQ; sulphate.
    No iron-sulfur ligand (FES, SF4, F3S) in any entry.
  • Chain B (SoxB): FAD and FMN in all six entries; dimethylglycine (1VRQ, 1X31),
    methylthioacetate (3AD7), pyrrole-2-carboxylate (3AD8), sulphite (3ADA).
  • Chain C (SoxG): sulphate only.
  • Chain D (SoxD): one zinc ion in all six entries.

Conclusion: no iron-sulfur cluster is modelled in any of the six structures, in
any chain. The only metal is the SoxD zinc. The 2Fe-2S ferredoxin-type InterPro
signature on SoxA therefore marks a fold, not a bound cluster, in this protein.
Absence of a modelled ligand is strong but not absolute evidence (a labile
cluster lost during purification would also be absent); the cofactor
reinvestigation of the purified enzyme (PMID:11902668) likewise found only NAD+,
FAD and covalent FMN.
