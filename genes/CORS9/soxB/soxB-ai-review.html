Gene Description

SoxB is the 405-residue beta subunit and catalytic flavoprotein of the cytoplasmic heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase (SoxABGD) of Corynebacterium sp. U-96, a member of the D-amino-acid-oxidase-like SoxB family. It binds one non-covalent FAD, at which sarcosine (and the substrate analogues dimethylglycine, methylthioacetate and pyrrole-2-carboxylate) binds and is oxidised to an iminium intermediate, and one FMN covalently attached to a histidine through its 8-alpha methyl group, which is reoxidised by molecular oxygen to give hydrogen peroxide. The two flavins lie about 10 A apart. The iminium intermediate is channelled to the folate site of the alpha subunit, where, with tetrahydrofolate present, glycine and 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate are formed (EC 1.5.3.24); without folate, glycine and formaldehyde are released. The soxB gene lies in a cluster with genes for serine hydroxymethyltransferase, serine dehydratase and 10-formyltetrahydrofolate hydrolase for catabolism of sarcosine to pyruvate.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

sarcosine oxidase (5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate-forming) activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: sarcosine + (6S)-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + O2 = (6R)-5,10-methylene-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofolate + glycine + H2O2.

Justification: This is EC 1.5.3.24 / RHEA:70455, the reaction of heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidases in the presence of tetrahydrofolate. GO:0008115 is defined by the folate-free reaction producing formaldehyde (RHEA:13313), which tetrahydrofolate suppresses in the heterotetramers, so no current GO molecular-function term represents the physiological activity.

Parent term: oxidoreductase activity, acting on the CH-NH group of donors, oxygen as acceptor

Core Functions

Catalytic flavoprotein subunit of the heterotetrameric sarcosine oxidase: oxidises sarcosine at its non-covalent FAD and reduces O2 to H2O2 at its covalent FMN; with the alpha-subunit folate site, the complex yields glycine and 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate. The folate-free formaldehyde-forming reaction (GO:0008115) is a conditional secondary activity.
