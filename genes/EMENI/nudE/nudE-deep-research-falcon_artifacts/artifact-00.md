| Target identity and architecture | *A. nidulans* **nudE** encodes a 586-aa NUDE-family protein homologous to *Neurospora crassa* RO11. Its conserved N-terminal region is a strongly predicted coiled coil; residues 25–183 bind NUDF, and the coiled-coil-containing N terminus can supply function when overexpressed. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000006) | Gene cloning, sequence comparison, coiled-coil prediction, truncation/complementation | High for the literature-defined protein and functional N-terminal region. Assignment to UniProt O74689/AN6125 relies additionally on the supplied database identity; domain boundaries vary slightly by construct/prediction. |
| Interaction with NUDF/LIS1 homolog | NUDE interacts with NUDF, the *A. nidulans* LIS1 homolog: interaction was detected by yeast two-hybrid analysis and confirmed by coprecipitation of NUDF with epitope-tagged NUDE from fungal extracts. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000005) | Yeast two-hybrid and co-immunoprecipitation | High for physical association; the two-hybrid signal was weak, but the independent fungal co-IP supports the interaction. These experiments do not determine affinity or stoichiometry. |
| Deletion phenotype and essentiality | **nudE** deletion is viable but impairs nuclear distribution, colony growth, and conidiation. Among ΔnudE germlings, 21% showed failed nuclear migration, 67% an intermediate phenotype, and 12% wild type; ΔnudF produced 64%, 36%, and 0%, respectively. (pqac-00000001) | Targeted deletion, DAPI-based scoring of germlings, growth and conidiation phenotyping | High for a nonessential but important nuclear-distribution role under the tested conditions. The milder ΔnudE phenotype than ΔnudF argues that NUDE supports rather than wholly accounts for NUDF function. |
| Multicopy suppression | Extra **nudE** copies improved nuclear migration in nudF7: the failed category fell from 52% to 16%, while the wild-type category rose from 5% to 28%, without increasing NUDF protein abundance. Multicopy **nudF** could completely suppress ΔnudE. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003) | Multicopy suppressor genetics, nuclear-distribution scoring, protein-level analysis | High for genetic interaction and pathway ordering. Suppression supports enhanced NUDE–NUDF function or complex formation, but does not by itself prove the molecular mechanism. |
| Intracellular localization | Full-length NUDE-GFP forms comet-like structures moving toward hyphal tips at microtubule ends, similar to NUDF. However, the functional N-terminal coiled-coil does not form comets, and ΔnudE does not eliminate NUDF or dynein-heavy-chain end comets. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000009) | Live-cell GFP localization, truncation analysis, deletion-background imaging | Moderate to high. Full-length localization is reproducible, but comet localization is not required for the demonstrated activity of the N-terminal functional region and therefore should not be equated with the essential site of action. |
| Primary molecular function and pathway | The best-supported annotation is a **nonenzymatic coiled-coil regulator/scaffold in the cytoplasmic dynein–NUDF/LIS1 nuclear-migration pathway**. NUDE binds NUDF and promotes its effective function; NUDF in turn directly associates with dynein. No catalytic reaction or substrate is known. (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000009) | Integrated genetic, biochemical, localization, and domain-dissection evidence | High for pathway membership and NUDF-supporting function; lower for the exact activation/recruitment mechanism. Direct NUDE-dependent dynein activation, stoichiometry, and cargo specificity have not been resolved in this fungus. |
| Recent 2023–2024 gene-specific evidence | No direct 2023–2024 functional study of *A. nidulans* O74689/NUDE was identified. Recent LIS1–dynein structural and single-molecule studies concern other experimental systems and provide comparative context only. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017) | Literature assessment and cross-species comparison | High confidence regarding the retrieved literature set, but not proof that no such study exists anywhere. Recent mammalian or yeast LIS1/dynein mechanisms must not be presented as direct evidence for *A. nidulans* NUDE. |
