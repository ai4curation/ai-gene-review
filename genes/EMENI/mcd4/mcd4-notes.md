# mcd4 notes

## Evidence

- A. nidulans Q5AXD1/AN7049 is the PTHR12250:SF0 ortholog of S. cerevisiae MCD4 and is supported by the PAINT, InterPro, and UniProt rows for the conserved ER GPI-anchor synthesis activity.

## Newer-paper search

- No direct AN7049, Q5AXD1, Aspergillus nidulans mcd4, or Emericella nidulans mcd4 experimental paper was found in cached publications or targeted newer-paper searches.
- The 2026 A. oryzae preprint reports that A. oryzae mcd4 disruption alters growth and wall morphology in another Aspergillus species, consistent with the conserved GPI-anchor role but not direct evidence for A. nidulans mcd4 [PMID:42327324, "Disruption of the GPI ethanolamine phosphate transferase 1 (mcd4)"].
