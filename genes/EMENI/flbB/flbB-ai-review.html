Gene Description

flbB encodes a basic-leucine-zipper (bZIP) transcription factor that is one of the upstream developmental activators (UDAs) controlling asexual sporulation in Aspergillus nidulans. FlbB shows a distinctive dual localization to the hyphal tip and the nucleus, binds DNA, and is required for timely induction of brlA and conidiation. It acts together with FlbE (with which it interacts at the hyphal tip) and with FlbD/FlbC upstream of the brlA->abaA->wetA central pathway.
