CC   -!- FUNCTION: NADH-cytochrome b5 reductase-like protein; part of the gene
CC       cluster that mediates the biosynthesis of asperlin, a polyketide
CC       showing anti-inflammatory, antitumor and antibiotic activities
CC       (PubMed:30339758). The first step of the asperlin biosynthesis is the
CC       production of the intermediate 2,4,6-octatrienoic acid by the highly
CC       reducing polyketide synthase alnA with cleavage of the PKS product by
CC       the esterase alnB (PubMed:30339758). 2,4,6-octatrienoic acid is further
CC       converted to asperlin via several steps involving the remaining enzymes
CC       from the cluster (PubMed:30339758). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30339758}.
