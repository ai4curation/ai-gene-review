Gene Description

mpkC encodes a HOG1-subfamily mitogen-activated protein kinase in Aspergillus nidulans. MpkC is one of two SakA/HogA-like stress-activated MAPKs in Aspergillus, is phosphorylated and regulated downstream of the PbsB MAPKK, and accumulates in the nucleus during asexual development and in response to osmotic or oxidative stress.

Core Functions

Acts as a cytoplasmic and nuclear HOG1-subfamily stress-activated MAP kinase that receives PbsB-dependent stress signals and modulates A. nidulans conidial development, oxidative-stress physiology, and osmotic-stress signaling in parallel with SakA/HogA.
