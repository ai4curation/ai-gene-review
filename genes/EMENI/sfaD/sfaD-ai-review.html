Gene Description

sfaD encodes the beta subunit of the heterotrimeric G protein of Aspergillus nidulans. As part of the FadA(alpha)-SfaD(beta)-GpgA(gamma) complex it is required for normal vegetative growth and for repression of asexual sporulation, and it promotes secondary metabolism (sterigmatocystin) and sexual development. SfaD functions as a Gbeta signaling adaptor.
