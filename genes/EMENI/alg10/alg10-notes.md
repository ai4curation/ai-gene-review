# alg10 notes

alg10 is the Aspergillus nidulans PTHR12989:SF10 ortholog of S. cerevisiae
Die2. The completed PTHR12989 family review supports the conserved eukaryotic
ALG10 PAINT assertions at PTN000312712: endoplasmic-reticulum localization,
protein N-linked glycosylation, and dolichyl pyrophosphate
Glc2Man9GlcNAc2 alpha-1,2-glucosyltransferase activity.
