Gene Description

abaA ("abacus") is a TEA/ATTS-domain transcription factor that controls the middle stage of asexual sporulation (conidiation) in Aspergillus nidulans. Activated by the master regulator BrlA, AbaA drives differentiation of the phialides — the sporogenous cells that bud off conidia — and is required for the terminal stages of conidiophore development. AbaA binds the sequence 5'-CATTCY-3' in the cis-regulatory regions of many developmentally regulated structural genes and of the regulatory genes brlA, wetA and abaA itself, thereby coupling the brlA -> abaA -> wetA central regulatory pathway. Loss of abaA produces aberrant, beaded ("abacus") conidiophores that make metula-like cells instead of phialides and fail to produce conidia.

abaA (Aspergillus nidulans) — curation notes

UniProt: P20945 (ABAA_EMENI). TEA/ATTS-domain transcription factor; middle
regulator of the brlA -> abaA -> wetA conidiation cascade. Member of the
conidiation_regulatory_cascade module.

Core biology (with provenance)

  • ATTS/TEA-domain sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional activator.
    AbaA binds 5'-CATTCY-3' (Y = pyrimidine) via major- and minor-groove contacts.
    PMID:8139553.
  • Regulates the developmental gene network, including target structural genes
    and the regulators brlA, wetA and abaA itself.
    PMID:8139553.
  • Controls phialide differentiation. abaA-null strains form aberrant
    conidiophores that make supernumerary metula-like cells instead of phialides
    and fail to produce conidia.
    PMID:2152124;
    PMID:2152124.
  • Middle tier of the dependent pathway, activated by BrlA and activating WetA.
    PMID:2655931; cross-species: PMID:20966095.

Annotation-review summary

All 21 GOA annotations ACCEPTED — abaA is a cleanly characterized TF. Core MF
GO:0003700 (IDA, PMID:8139553); core BP phialide/conidiophore development and
positive regulation of conidium formation. No over-annotations identified.
