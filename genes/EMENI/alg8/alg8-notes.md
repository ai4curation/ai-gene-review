# alg8 notes

alg8 is the Aspergillus nidulans FGSC A4 PTHR12413:SF2 ortholog of
S. cerevisiae Alg8. The completed PTHR12413 family review treats SF2 as
ALG8-specific and supports the conserved ER-membrane, dolichol-linked
oligosaccharide biosynthesis, and dolichyl pyrophosphate Glc1Man9GlcNAc2
alpha-1,3-glucosyltransferase PAINT assertions.

The direct activity row from `PANTHER:PTN000275692` should be accepted. The
separate PTHR12413 ALG6 node, `PANTHER:PTN000275751`, carries the related
GO:0042281 first-glucose activity and does not propagate to this SF2 ALG8
protein.
