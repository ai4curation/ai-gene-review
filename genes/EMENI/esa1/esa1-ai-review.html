Gene Description

esa1 encodes the Aspergillus nidulans fungal ESA1/MYST histone acetyltransferase, a conserved chromatin enzyme inferred to act as a NuA4 catalytic subunit. EsaA acetylates histone H4, including H4K12 at secondary metabolite gene clusters, and promotes chromatin-based regulation of nuclear transcription.
