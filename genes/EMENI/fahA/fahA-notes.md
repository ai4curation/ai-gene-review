## Identity

- UniProt Q00770 (FAAA_EMENI), gene fahA, ORF AN1896, 431 aa. FAH family
  (IPR005959, TIGR01266, PTHR43069:SF2). Ca2+/Mg2+ cofactors by similarity to
  human FAH (P35505).

## Function

- 47.1% identical to human FAH; disruption gives succinylacetone secretion and Phe
  toxicity [PMID:7568087 "fahA disruption results in secretion of succinylacetone (a
  diagnostic compound for human type I tyrosinaemia) and phenylalanine toxicity."].
- Suppressors: loss of HGD prevents Fah-deficiency effects [PMID:7568087].
- UniProt catalytic activity (ECO:0000269, PubMed:7568087): 4-fumarylacetoacetate +
  H2O = acetoacetate + fumarate + H(+). Induced by phenylacetate and hydroxy
  derivatives, Phe and Tyr; partially glucose-repressed.
- Clustered with hmgA and maiA [PMID:9417084].
