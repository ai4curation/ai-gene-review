Gene Description

AFE_1792 encodes a Type I (SqrA) sulfide:quinone oxidoreductase (SQR) of the acidophilic iron- and sulfur-oxidizing bacterium Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans. The enzyme is a homodimeric FAD flavoprotein with two tandem Rossmann-fold domains and a C-terminal pair of amphipathic helices, through which it is thought to attach monotopically to the cytoplasmic membrane. It oxidizes hydrogen sulfide to polysulfide chains and eventually cyclooctasulfur, and passes the electrons through FAD to membrane ubiquinone or menaquinone. Catalysis depends on a cysteine triad: Cys356 and Cys160 form the persulfide/polysulfide-bearing pair beside the FAD, and Cys128 is needed for the quinone reduction step. Activity, kinetics, crystal structures and mutagenesis come from the recombinant enzyme made in Escherichia coli. Its physiological contribution in A. ferrooxidans cells, which carry other SQR-family paralogs such as AFE_0267, has not been tested genetically.

Core Functions

Type I sulfide:quinone oxidoreductase. It oxidizes hydrogen sulfide to polysulfide/S8 at a cysteine pair beside FAD and passes the electrons to membrane quinone on the cytoplasmic membrane, supplying sulfide-derived electrons to the quinone pool.

AFE_1792 (B7JBP8, SQRD_ACIF2): curation notes

Identity and SQR type

  • Swiss-Prot entry for the Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans ATCC 23270 sulfide:quinone
    oxidoreductase. Recombinant enzyme crystallized and characterized by Cherney et al.
    PMID:20303979.
  • Type I (SqrA). PMID:23103448 Our pairwise analysis agrees: 41.9% identity to the Aquifex Type I
    SQR (O67931), with the cysteine triad (C128, C160, C356) conserved
    (AFE_1792-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md).
  • Mechanism: PMID:22542586
    PMID:20303979
  • Membrane: PMID:20303979.

Paralogs in A. ferrooxidans

  • The genome paper names a different locus as the sqr implicated in sulfide oxidation
    PMID:19077236. AFE_0267 (B7J411, 375 aa) is only 27.5%
    identical to AFE_1792 and has no residue aligned to Cys160 (RESULTS.md).
  • So the in vitro evidence (structure, kinetics, mutagenesis) is for AFE_1792. Physiological
    and genetic evidence for SQR involvement in A. ferrooxidans sulfur oxidation has been
    attached to AFE_0267 or to SQR activity of unspecified origin purified from membranes
    PMID:21687411. No knockout of AFE_1792 exists.

PANTHER oddity

  • B7JBP8 is in PTHR42913:SF6 (inside an AIF/NDH-2-like family, InterPro IPR051169). Its 87-94%
    identical orthologs in A. caldus (F9ZQD6) and A. ferrivorans (A0A060UVE9) are in
    PTHR43755:SF1 (InterPro IPR052541 SQRD). This inconsistency is the source of the
    TreeGrafter rows "NAD(P)H dehydrogenase (quinone) activity" and "aerobic electron transport
    chain" on this entry only.

Annotation decisions (summary)

  • GO:0070224 (EXP x2, IEA): ACCEPT, core.
  • GO:0016020 (IEA, NAS): MODIFY to GO:0005886 plasma membrane (amphipathic anchoring helices
    plus the quinone-pool acceptor).
  • GO:0016491: TOO_GENERAL.
  • GO:0003955 (TreeGrafter): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED (family NADH-donor function; enzyme is
    sulfide-dependent; NAD(P)H never shown).
  • GO:0019646 (TreeGrafter): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
  • NEW GO:0071949 FAD binding (crystal structures).

CMMMech claim check

CMMMech records (orpiment, realgar, zinc, titanium-metal, electroplating-sludge, arsenopyrite,
stannite, anglesite, nickel-flotation-tailings, tetrahedrite-concentrate, ...) use B7JBP8 as
the representative A. ferrooxidans Sqr, with functional_role "Membrane-bound sulfide-quinone
reductase (EC 1.8.5.4; AFE_1792) catalyzing oxidation of sulfide to elemental sulfur/polysulfide
coupled to quinone reduction during acidophilic sulfur/sulfide bioleaching".

  • The catalytic part is well supported, though only in vitro (recombinant enzyme).
  • "during acidophilic sulfur/sulfide bioleaching" goes beyond the evidence. No study shows
    AFE_1792 expression or function during mineral leaching, and the genome paper ties
    physiological sulfide oxidation to the paralog AFE_0267. The evidence supports the enzyme's
    capability, not its contribution to leaching. The CMMMech supports: partial wording is
    appropriate. A stronger claim would need AFE_1792-specific expression or mutant data.
  • The record description "Inner-membrane flavoprotein" is consistent with our plasma-membrane
    call. The protein is monotopic/peripheral, not an integral inner-membrane protein.

Raw output is in sqr_typing_output.md. Sequences are from UniProt REST
(sequences.fasta). Method: Biopython global pairwise alignment, BLOSUM62, gap
open -10, extend -0.5; identity is computed over the shorter sequence. No
phylogeny or profile HMM was run, so type calls rest on pairwise identity to typed
references and on conserved active-site cysteines. They are inferences, not
classifier output.

References used

  • Type I (SqrA): B7JBP8 (A. ferrooxidans AFE_1792, crystal structure and mutagenesis)
    and O67931 (Aquifex aeolicus). Both are explicitly Type I in PMID:23103448.
  • Type II: Q9Y6N5 (human SQOR).
  • Type III candidates: A8MAC9 and A8MCW1 (Caldivirga maquilingensis paralogs). It
    was not resolved which of the two is the protein characterized in PMID:23103448.
  • Type IV (SqrD): Q8KDG3 (Chlorobaculum tepidum CT1087).
  • Type V (SqrE): Q7ZAG8 (Acidianus ambivalens, characterized SQR) and Q8KE17
    (C. tepidum CT0876, an SqrE homolog that showed no SQR activity in PMID:19028893).
  • Type VI (SqrF): Q8KG51 (C. tepidum CT0117).
  • FCSD flavoprotein (sulfide:cytochrome c, not an SQR): Q8KAS4 (C. tepidum FccB-2).
  • B7J411 (A. ferrooxidans AFE_0267, the "sqr" named in the genome paper, PMID:19077236).

Main points:

  1. Three entries are close orthologs of the characterized Type I enzyme.
    F9ZQD6 (87.6%) and A0A060UVE9 (94.4%) conserve all three catalytic and
    FAD-proximal cysteines of B7JBP8 (Cys128, Cys160 and Cys356). Their SQR activity
    is a strong orthology inference.
  2. A0A059XTE6 (Leptospirillum) is a more distant Type I homolog. It shares 57%
    identity with B7JBP8 and conserves all three cysteines. Calling it an SQR is
    reasonable, but the inference is weaker than for the Acidithiobacillus
    orthologs, and no Leptospirillum SQR has been assayed.
  3. A0A543Q349 (A. thiooxidans) is not Type I. It lacks the Type I
    flavin-linked cysteine (Tyr at the Cys128 position). Its two active-site
    cysteines (C186 and C361) align with the persulfide cysteines of the Type V
    A. ambivalens SQR (C178 and C350). Its closest typed relative is C. tepidum
    CT0876 (56%), an SqrE homolog that showed no SQR activity when made
    recombinantly (PMID:19028893). The entry is therefore a putative Type V
    SQR/SqrE homolog without demonstrated activity.
  4. PANTHER placement is inconsistent within the Type I orthologs. UniProt puts
    B7JBP8 in PTHR42913:SF6 (SULFIDE-QUINONE REDUCTASE, inside the AIF/NDH-2-like
    family). Its 87-94% identical orthologs F9ZQD6 and A0A060UVE9, and the
    Leptospirillum homolog, are in PTHR43755:SF1, the family that also holds the
    Type IV, V and VI SQRs and the FCSD flavoprotein. PTHR43755 membership alone
    therefore cannot tell an SQR from an FCSD flavoprotein, or one SQR type from
    another.
  5. A. ferrooxidans ATCC 23270 encodes more than one SQR-family protein.
    AFE_0267 (B7J411) is only 27.5% identical to AFE_1792 and has no residue
    aligned to Cys160. The A. ferrooxidans genome paper calls AFE_0267 the
    experimentally validated "sqr", while the crystallized and kinetically
    characterized enzyme is AFE_1792. CMMMech's records use AFE_1792.

Limitations

  • These are pairwise global alignments only. Type assignment by Marcia et al. 2010
    (PMID:20077566) uses structure-based fingerprints, including capping loops and the
    C-terminal extension, which were not scored here.
  • The Caldivirga Type III references are uncertain (two paralogs).
  • Cysteine positions were mapped by pairwise alignment to each reference. In
    low-identity pairs (below 30%) a mapped residue can shift by a few positions.
