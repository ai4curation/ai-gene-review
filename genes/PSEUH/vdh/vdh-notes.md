# vdh (O05619, Pseudomonas sp. HR199) curation notes

## 2026-11-04 review

No provider deep-research file was generated (no provider API keys in this
session). Sources: the reviewed UniProt record and the cached abstract of
Priefert et al. 1997 (PMID:9098058; full text not cached).

- Swiss-Prot curates NAD-dependent vanillin oxidation with experimental evidence
  from PMID:9098058: "Catalyzes the NAD-dependent oxidation of vanillin to
  vanillic acid."
- Abstract: E. coli expressing the vdh subfragment "converted vanillin to
  vanillate at a rate of up to 0.3 micromol per min per ml of culture", and
  transfer of vdh with vanAB conferred growth on vanillin/vanillate to strains
  that could not use them [PMID:9098058].
- The vdh operon carries an upstream ORF2 with similarity to enoyl-CoA
  hydratases (the HR199 ech gene), the same fcs/ech/vdh organization as KT2440.
- PANTHER PTHR42986:SF1, a subfamily that also holds benzaldehyde,
  salicylaldehyde and 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde dehydrogenases; see the PTHR42986
  family review.
