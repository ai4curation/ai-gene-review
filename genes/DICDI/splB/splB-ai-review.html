Gene Description

splB (Pyk2, DPYK2) is a cytosolic tyrosine kinase-like (TKL) family protein kinase of Dictyostelium discoideum with a long, largely disordered N-terminal region and a C-terminal kinase domain. Despite TKL kinases being mostly serine/threonine-specific, Pyk2 is a tyrosine kinase: it autophosphorylates on tyrosine, and these phosphotyrosines are bound by the SH2 domain of the transcription factor STATc, forming a constitutive enzyme-substrate complex. Pyk2 then phosphorylates STATc on Tyr922, the activating tyrosine, acting as a functional analogue of metazoan JAK kinases. Pyk2 is required for STATc activation by the prestalk-inducing morphogen DIF-1, and acts redundantly with the related TKL Pyk3 in cGMP-dependent STATc activation during hyperosmotic stress. Pyk2 is semi-constitutively active, with STATc activation additionally controlled by the tyrosine phosphatase PTP3; under stress Pyk2 relocates from the cytosol into cytoplasmic granules that also contain PTP3. The gene was originally isolated as a developmentally regulated tyrosine kinase.

Core Functions

Cytosolic protein tyrosine kinase that autophosphorylates on tyrosine to create docking sites for the STATc SH2 domain and then phosphorylates STATc on its activating Tyr922, mediating STATc activation in response to DIF-1 and (redundantly with Pyk3) hyperosmotic stress.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does splB/Pyk2 possess serine/threonine kinase activity, as its UniProt name "dual specificity protein kinase" implies, or is it tyrosine-specific as described by Araki et al. 2012?

Q: How is Pyk2 activity coupled to DIF-1 and to cGMP during hyperosmotic stress, given that the DIF-1 receptor is unknown and Pyk2 appears semi-constitutively active?

Q: What are the identity and function of the stress-induced cytoplasmic granules in which Pyk2 and PTP3 co-accumulate?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: In vitro kinase assays of purified Pyk2 kinase domain against generic Ser/Thr and Tyr peptide substrates, with phosphoamino acid analysis, to test dual specificity.

Experiment: Phosphoproteomics of wild-type versus pyk2/pyk3 double-null cells under sorbitol stress to identify Pyk2 substrates beyond STATc that explain the stress-hypersensitive developmental phenotype.

Experiment: Mapping of the Pyk2 autophosphorylation sites bound by the STATc SH2 domain by mass spectrometry and mutagenesis.

Synthesis

  • Core: cytosolic TKL-family protein tyrosine kinase that autophosphorylates on Tyr, creating
    docking sites for the STATc SH2 domain, then phosphorylates STATc Tyr922 — the Dictyostelium
    analogue of a JAK. Acts in DIF-1 and hyperosmotic stress (cGMP) STATc activation; redundant
    with Pyk3 for stress.
  • Downstream phenotypes (STATc nuclear accumulation, target-gene expression, stress-tolerant
    development) are consequences of STATc activation -> non-core.
  • "Dual specificity" name: no Ser/Thr kinase activity is documented in the cached literature;
    2012 paper calls it "tyrosine-specific". Did not propose Ser/Thr activity.
  • Open: what lies upstream (DIF-1 receptor unknown; cGMP link), Pyk2 substrates beyond STATc.
