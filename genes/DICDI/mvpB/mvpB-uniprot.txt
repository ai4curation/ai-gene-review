CC   -!- SUBUNIT: The vault is a massive cage-like structure; it consists of a
CC       dimer of half-vaults, with each half-vault comprising 39 identical MVP
CC       subunits (By similarity). The two half-vaults assemble to form the
CC       complete vault, where minor vault components and vault RNAs (vRNAs) may
CC       bind to the interior (By similarity). Dictyostelium is one of the few
CC       organisms in which the major component is actually two proteins (alpha
CC       and beta). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q14764}.
