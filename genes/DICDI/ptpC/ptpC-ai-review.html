Gene Description

ptpC encodes PTP3 (tyrosine-protein phosphatase 3), a 990-residue non-receptor protein tyrosine phosphatase with a single, sequence-divergent classical PTP catalytic domain flanked by long disordered N- and C-terminal regions. PTP3 is the tyrosine phosphatase that deactivates the transcription factor STATc: it binds STATc directly and removes the activating phosphate from STATc Tyr922, opposing STATc dimerisation and nuclear accumulation. Under basal conditions PTP3 is constitutively active and largely cytosolic, counterbalancing a constitutively active STATc tyrosine kinase (the TKL kinase Pyk2). The prestalk inducer DIF-1 and hyperosmotic stress inhibit PTP3, correlating with phosphorylation of S448 and S747 through Ca2+-dependent signalling and the TKL kinase Phg2 and with downregulation by the Cbl homologue CblA, so that STATc becomes tyrosine phosphorylated and nuclear. On stress, PTP3 relocalises from the cytosol to endosome-like vesicles and granules that also contain Pyk2. PTP3 is expressed during growth and early development, enriched in prestalk and anterior-like cells, and its gene appears to be required for viability, as the second of two genomic copies could not be disrupted.

Core Functions

PTP3 is a cytosolic non-receptor protein tyrosine phosphatase that binds the transcription factor STATc and dephosphorylates its activating residue Tyr922. This keeps STATc monomeric and out of the nucleus, counterbalancing the constitutive STATc kinase Pyk2, and so negatively regulates STATc signalling. DIF-1 and hyperosmotic stress inhibit PTP3 (with S448/S747 phosphorylation), which tips the balance toward STATc activation.

Q: Which kinase phosphorylates PTP3 on S448, and does Phg2 phosphorylate S747 directly or act through another kinase?

Q: Is ptpC essential for viability, given that the second genomic copy could not be disrupted? Does it have essential substrates besides STATc?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express phosphomimetic (S448D/S747D) and non-phosphorylatable (S448A/S747A) PTP3 in Dictyostelium. Measure activity of the purified protein on a STATc pTyr922 phosphopeptide under reducing and non-reducing conditions, and measure STATc activation after DIF-1 or sorbitol in cells expressing the mutants.

Protein

  • 990 aa non-receptor PTP; single PTP catalytic domain (aa 422-715; PF00102, PS00383 active-site
    signature), flanked by long disordered N- and C-terminal regions (UniProt). PANTHER
    PTHR19134:SF549.
  • Two genomic copies in JH10/Ax3 background; one could be disrupted (slow growth), the other was
    refractory, suggesting essentiality [PMID:8628311 "There are two copies of the PTP3 gene in this
    haploid organism"; "We were unable to obtain a strain with disruptions in both PTP3 genes"].

Catalytic activity

  • Recombinant PTP3 has vanadate-sensitive phosphatase activity PMID:8628311. Intrinsic activity of the
    bacterial enzyme is low and the PTP3 sequence is divergent at otherwise conserved residues
    PMID:18305004.
  • C-to-S substrate-trapping mutant forms a stable complex with tyrosine-phosphorylated p130 (pp130)
    PMID:10207040.
  • pp130 was later identified as STATc PMID:18305004. PTP3 directly interacts with and dephosphorylates STATc
    PMID:18305004, removing
    phosphate from Tyr922 PMID:24587195.

Biological role: STATc deactivation and its regulation

  • PTP3 overexpression makes STATc refractory to activation; a dominant inhibitor (substrate trap)
    causes constitutive Tyr phosphorylation and ectopic nuclear localisation of STATc PMID:18305004.
  • DIF-1 and hyperosmotic stress inhibit PTP3 (transient 20% vs stable 40% activity decrease) and
    induce Ser/Thr phosphorylation of PTP3, an atypical STAT-activation mechanism where the phosphatase
    arm is regulated PMID:18305004.
  • Phosphosites S448 and S747; Ca2+-elevating agents stimulate their phosphorylation and activate
    STATc PMID:20159963.
  • Actin cytoskeleton disruption (latrunculin A, cytochalasin A) and DIF-1 increase S747
    phosphorylation PMID:22365144.
  • The TKL kinase Phg2 binds PTP3 in vitro and is required for full S747 phosphorylation
    PMID:24587195.
  • CblA (Cbl homologue) positively regulates STATc by downregulating PTP3 PMID:18840649.
  • Pyk2 (the STATc Tyr922 kinase) co-IPs with PTP3 and they co-localize in stress-induced granules
    PMID:25143406. STATc kinase is constitutive; the PTP3 phosphatase arm is the regulated arm for
    DIF-1 PMID:22699506.
  • ptpC expression is induced by sorbitol in a STATc- and cGMP-dependent manner (positive feedback)
    PMID:24587195.

Localization

  • Cytosolic in unstimulated cells; upon stress, translocates to structures that co-localize with
    endosomal vesicles, independently of its activity PMID:10207040. Stress granules shared with
    Pyk2 PMID:25143406.
  • Expression enriched in anterior-like and prestalk cells (lacZ reporter) PMID:8628311.
