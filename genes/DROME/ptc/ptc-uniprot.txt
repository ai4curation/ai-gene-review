CC   -!- FUNCTION: Segmentation polarity protein, which inhibits the smoothened
CC       signaling pathway by acting as a receptor for hedgehog morphogen
CC       (PubMed:10966113, PubMed:24302888, PubMed:2582494, PubMed:2797178,
CC       PubMed:8898207). In absence of hedgehog, acts as an inhibitor of
CC       smoothened protein (smo) by preventing smo access to cholesterol:
CC       mechanistically, acts as a cholesterol transporter, which depeletes
CC       cholesterol from the outer leaflet of plasma membrane, thereby
CC       preventing cholesterol-binding to the extracellular CRD region of smo
CC       (By similarity). Hedgehog-binding inhibits ptc cholesterol transporter
CC       activity, promoting smo activation by cholesterol and transduction of
CC       the smoothened signaling pathway (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q13635, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10966113,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24302888, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2582494,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2797178, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8898207}.

CC         ChEBI:CHEBI:16113; Evidence={ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q13635};
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via C-terminal cytoplasmic region) with
CC       CG5504/l(2)tid; the interaction is probably direct (PubMed:12783860).

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12783860}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10966113};

CC       membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24768000}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- PTM: Ubiquitinated at Lys-1261 by Smurf in response to hedgehog,
CC       promoting endocytosis and lysosomal degradation; degradation is
CC       essential for smoothened pathway activation.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24302888}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Embryonic lethal with epidermal pattern formation
CC       defects. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12783860}.
