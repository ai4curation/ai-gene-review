# Sord1 (O97479) notes

- Sord1 = Sodh-1 = Sdh-1 (CG1982). Two-gene SDH system; SDH-1 kinetically characterized
  [PMID:9852094 "The
SDH-1 enzyme has an enzymology with Km and kcat values an order of magnitude
higher than those for the human enzyme"]; Sdh-1 is the major transcript.
- Sodh-1/Sodh-2 double mutants accumulate hemolymph sorbitol and fail polyol-dependent Mondo activation
  [PMID:35687590 "Levels of sorbitol in the hemolymph were reduced in AR mutants and were increased in Sodh mutants"];
  fructose rescues [PMID:35687590 "Fructose, the end product of the polyol pathway, restored gene regulation in the Sodh mutants"].
- Polyol pathway produces circulating fructose sensed by Gr43a in EECs (PMID:36827377).
- Decisions: GO:0047833 (EC 1.1.99.21, acceptor-type) modified to GO:0003939 (NAD-dependent SDH, EC 1.1.1.14);
  generic oxidoreductase terms modified; zinc binding and SORD-ortholog xylitol/glucuronate terms non-core.

- Falcon deep research (Sord1-deep-research-falcon.md) is consistent: EC 1.1.1.14 NAD-dependent sorbitol -> fructose; notes
  that published "Sodh mutants" remove both Sodh-1 and Sodh-2, so single-gene contributions are unresolved.
