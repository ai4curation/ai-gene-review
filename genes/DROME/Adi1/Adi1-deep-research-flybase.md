# FlyBase gene description for Adi1 (DROME)

Acireductone dioxygenase 1 (Adi1) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the Fe-dependent oxidative cleavage of 1,2-dihydroxy-5-(methylsulfanyl)pent-1-en-3-one to produce formate and 4-methylsulfanyl- 2-oxobutanoate, the keto-acid precursor of L-methionine in the L-methionine salvage pathway (MTA cycle). Adi1 can also catalyzes the Ni-dependent oxidative cleavage of 1,2-dihydroxy-5-(methylsulfanyl)pent-1-en-3-one to produce 3-(methylsulfanyl)propanoate and formate.
