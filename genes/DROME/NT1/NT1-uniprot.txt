CC   -!- FUNCTION: Neurotrophin which may function as a ligand for the Toll-
CC       related receptors Toll-7 and Tollo (PubMed:22022271, PubMed:23892553).
CC       Binds to Toll-7 and probably acts as its ligand in promoting motor axon
CC       targeting and neuronal survival in the central nervous system (CNS)
CC       (PubMed:23892553). Involved in synaptic targeting of ISNb/d
CC       motorneurons and also some SNa motorneurons (PubMed:19018662). In
CC       larvae, involved in the negative regulation of the tracheal immune
CC       response to bacterial infection perhaps by acting as a ligand for the
CC       Toll-related receptor Tollo (PubMed:22022271). May be involved in the
CC       normal development of specific neurons at the neuromuscular junction
CC       (PubMed:24124519). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19018662,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22022271, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23892553,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24124519}.
