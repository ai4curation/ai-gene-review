Gene Description

Sos encodes the Drosophila Son of sevenless Ras guanine-nucleotide exchange factor. Drk recruits Sos to activated receptor tyrosine kinase complexes, where Sos promotes GDP release from Ras85D to activate Ras-dependent Raf/MAPK signaling downstream of EGFR, Sevenless, Torso, Pvr, and FGF receptors. Sos also links receptors to Rac activation in axon guidance and cytoskeletal contexts.
