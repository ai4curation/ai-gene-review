CC   -!- FUNCTION: Receptor-regulated SMAD (R-SMAD) involved in the activin-like
CC       TGF-beta pathway (PubMed:10320478). Acts downstream of the type I
CC       transmembrane receptor serine/threonine kinase babo (PubMed:10320478).
CC       Competitively regulates phosphorylation of Mad, the R-SMAD of the BMP
CC       signaling pathway, by babo (PubMed:22563507). Heterodimerizes with the
CC       Co-SMAD Med/medea upon activation of the activin pathway and
CC       translocates to the nucleus, where it transcriptionally activates
CC       activin-responsive genes (PubMed:10320478). Involved in axonal and
CC       dendritic pruning and neural remodeling of mushroom body gamma neurons
CC       during metamorphosis, probably through regulating expression of the
CC       ecdysteroid receptor EcR (PubMed:12581521). Involved in differentiation
CC       of dorsal cluster and ellipsoid body adult-specific neurons prior to
CC       metamorphosis (PubMed:16437159). Required for axon guidance or
CC       outgrowth of ISNb and SNa axons (PubMed:17119022). Involved in
CC       regulation of wing imaginal disc growth (PubMed:18820452). Signaling by
CC       daw or myo is involved in lifespan determination through regulation of
CC       proteasome-mediated degradation of misfolded or damaged proteins
CC       (PubMed:29615416). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10320478,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12581521, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16437159,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17119022, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18820452,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22563507, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29615416}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Associates with a receptor serine/threonine kinase complex
CC       composed of put and babo; dissociates upon being phosphorylated by babo
CC       (PubMed:10320478, PubMed:9887103). Interacts (when phosphorylated on
CC       the SSXS motif) with Med/medea; the interaction is dependent on a
CC       receptor serine/threonine kinase complex (PubMed:10320478,
CC       PubMed:16951053, PubMed:9887103). Heterodimerizes with the Co-SMAD
CC       Med/medea upon activation of the activin pathway and translocates to
CC       the nucleus, where it transcriptionally activates activin-responsive
CC       genes (PubMed:10320478). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10320478,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16951053, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9887103}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       O96660; P83949: Ubx; NbExp=3; IntAct=EBI-145058, EBI-202590;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000255|RuleBase:RU361195,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10320478}. Nucleus {ECO:0000255|RuleBase:RU361195,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10320478}. Note=Translocates from the cytoplasm to
CC       the nucleus upon activation of a receptor serine/threonine kinase
CC       complex composed of babo and punt, probably upon phosphorylation and
CC       heterodimerization with Med/medea. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10320478}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed both maternally and zygotically
CC       throughout embryogenesis; the maternal transcript is larger than the
CC       zygotic transcript (PubMed:9813169). Expressed maternally in the
CC       preblastoderm embryo and is rapidly turned over (PubMed:9887103).
CC       Zygotic expression is first detected during early gastrulation in the
CC       mesoderm and continues throughout embryogenesis (PubMed:9887103).
CC       Expressed in imaginal discs and brain of 3rd instar larvae,
CC       specifically in regions where cells are undergoing proliferation and
CC       differentiation such as the morphogenetic furrow of the eye disc and
CC       the laminar furrow of the outer proliferation center of the brain
CC       (PubMed:10320478, PubMed:9887103). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10320478,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9813169, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9887103}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated in response to Actbeta/activin and daw signaling
CC       (PubMed:12581521, PubMed:18820452, PubMed:19766717). Phosphorylated on
CC       a C-terminal SSXS motif by the receptor serine/threonine kinase complex
CC       composed of the type I receptor babo and type II receptor put/punt
CC       (PubMed:10320478, PubMed:18820452, PubMed:19766717, PubMed:22563507,
CC       PubMed:9887103). Phosphorylation by babo induces heterodimerization
CC       with med/medea and nuclear localization (PubMed:10320478).
CC       Phosphorylation by babo increases protein turnover (PubMed:22563507).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10320478, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12581521,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18820452, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19766717,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22563507, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9887103}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Central brain and optical lobe are significantly
CC       reduced in size at late 3rd instar probably due to reduced neuroblast
CC       proliferation (PubMed:38913612). Targeting of retinal axons to the
CC       lamina and medulla is impaired; R1-6 axons form a reduced lamina plexus
CC       while R7 and R8 growth cones are collapsed (PubMed:16951053,
CC       PubMed:18171686, PubMed:38913612). The medulla neuropil is reduced in
CC       size and altered in morphology due to reduced cellular proliferation
CC       (PubMed:16951053). Motoneuron axons of segmental nerve SNa and
CC       intersegmental nerve ISNb display premature stalling and defective
CC       pathfinding (PubMed:17119022). Conditional RNAi-mediated knockdown from
CC       late larval stage onwards does not affect pupal development but pharate
CC       adults fail to eclose; pharate adult brains have significantly reduced
CC       optic lobe size (PubMed:38913612). Conditional ubiquitous or muscle
CC       specific RNAi-mediated knock down in adult flies reduces lifespan by 3
CC       to 10 days in females, but not in males (PubMed:29615416). Knockdown in
CC       muscle leads to accumulation of ubiquitinated protein aggregates in
CC       tissues (PubMed:29615416). RNAi-mediated knockdown in wing results in
CC       blistered wings phenotype (PubMed:22563507).
