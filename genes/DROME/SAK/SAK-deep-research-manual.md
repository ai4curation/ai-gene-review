# SAK (Plk4) – Drosophila melanogaster – manual literature research

No deep-research API provider was available for this session; this file is a manual
literature synthesis from cached publications (`publications/PMID_*.md`) and PubMed searches.

## Identity
- UniProt O97143 (PLK4_DROME), 769 aa; FlyBase gene SAK (Sak/Plk4), NCBI Gene 36993.
- Domain architecture (UniProt/PROSITE): N-terminal Ser/Thr protein kinase domain (14-267),
  a downstream regulatory element (DRE) containing the Slimb-binding degron (DSGXXT; S293/T297),
  cryptic polo box 1 and 2 (CPB1 381-498, CPB2 499-602; = PB1-PB2), and C-terminal polo box (PB3, 660-739).
- PANTHER PTHR24345:SF91 (Serine/threonine-protein kinase PLK4).

## Function: master kinase of centriole duplication
- Loss of SAK by mutation or RNAi causes centriole loss [PMID:16326102 "downregulation of SAK in Drosophila cells, by mutation or RNAi, leads to loss of centrioles, the core structures of centrosomes"].
- Mutant spermatids lack centrioles and so cannot build sperm axonemes [PMID:16326102 "The majority of spermatids in SAK mutants lack centrioles and so are unable to make sperm axonemes"].
- Overexpression drives centriole amplification and de novo centriole formation in unfertilized eggs, requiring DSas-6 and DSas-4 [PMID:17463247 "overexpression of SAK/PLK4 could induce amplification of centrioles in Drosophila embryos and their de novo formation in unfertilized eggs"]; similar results from [PMID:17475495 "the overexpression of any one of these three proteins results in the rapid de novo formation of many hundreds of centriole-like structures in unfertilized eggs"].
- Centriole biogenesis is "locally triggered by high SAK/PLK4 activity" with SAS-6 downstream [PMID:18196975 "centriole biogenesis is a self-assembly process locally triggered by high SAK/PLK4 activity"].
- Required for formation of the proximal centriole-like (PCL) structure in spermatids [PMID:19293139 "PCL formation depends on SAK/PLK4 and SAS-6"].
- Genome-wide RNAi screen hit for centriole duplication [PMID:18798690].

## Direct substrate and mechanism
- Plk4 phosphorylates the STAN motif of Ana2, enabling Sas6 binding and recruitment [PMID:25264260 "Drosophila Plk4 phosphorylates four conserved serines in the STAN motif of the core centriole protein Ana2 to enable it to bind and recruit its Sas6 partner"].
- Active Plk4 recruits Ana2 to the site of procentriole formation [PMID:29263250 "if catalytically active, recruits Ana2 to these sites"].
- Extra DSas-6/Ana2 recruitment onto centrioles requires Sak overexpression [PMID:21145506 "extra DSas-6/Ana2 is only recruited onto centrioles when Sak/Plk4 kinase is also overexpressed"].

## Kinase activity and auto-regulation
- Recombinant Plk4 KD+DRE autophosphorylates and phosphorylates kinase-dead Plk4 in trans and MBP; D156N is kinase dead [PMID:21987638 "We found that Plk4-KD can autophosphorylate in vitro and can phosphorylate kinase-dead Plk4-KD in trans"].
- Kinase activity is needed for its own degradation [PMID:21987638 "we found that the Plk4 kinase activity is needed for its degradation because kinase-dead Plk4 expressed in S2 cells is more stable than wild-type (wt) Plk4"].
- PP2A-Twins counteracts autophosphorylation to stabilize mitotic Plk4 [PMID:21987638 "PP2A (Protein Phosphatase 2A(Twins)) counteracts Plk4 autophosphorylation, thus stabilizing Plk4 and promoting centriole duplication"].
- SCF-Slimb ubiquitin ligase targets Plk4 [PMID:19171756 "We found that Plk4 binds to Slimb and is an SCF(Slimb) target"]; [PMID:19084407 "This complex physically interacts with SAK/PLK4, and in its absence, SAK/PLK4 accumulates"].

## Structure / partners
- CPB (PB1-PB2) forms homodimer, required for Asterless binding and centriole targeting [PMID:23000383 "Surprisingly, the structure reveals two tandem homodimerized polo boxes, PB1-PB2, that form a unique winged architecture"].
- Asterless N-terminus binds the cryptic polo box; Asl required for centrosomal loading of Plk4 [PMID:20852615 "the amino terminus of which interacts with the cryptic Polo box of Plk4"].
- Alms1a interacts with Sak in male GSCs [PMID:32965218 "Alms1a interacts with Sak/Plk4, a critical regulator of centriole duplication, more strongly at the GSC mother centrosome"].

## Localization
- Centrioles, proximal end in testes [PMID:27764551 "in control germline cells these proteins are restricted to the proximal regions of the centrioles"]. No evidence for nuclear function in Drosophila.

## Physiological roles (downstream of centrioles)
- Centrioles dispensable for most somatic divisions, but syncytial embryo divisions and male meiosis fail [PMID:18196975 "mitotic abnormalities arise in syncytial SAK/PLK4-derived mutant embryos resulting in lethality. Moreover male meiosis fails in both SAK/PLK4 and DSAS-4 mutant spermatids that have no centrioles"].
- Overexpression-driven centrosome amplification can initiate tumorigenesis in larval brains [PMID:18555779].
