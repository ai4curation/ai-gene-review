CC   -!- FUNCTION: Limiting component of the male-specific lethal (MSL) histone
CC       acetyltransferase complex, a multiprotein complex essential for
CC       elevating transcription of the single X chromosome in the male (X
CC       chromosome dosage compensation) (PubMed:18724933, PubMed:23084835,
CC       PubMed:23870142, PubMed:27580037, PubMed:31320325, PubMed:33208948,
CC       PubMed:7588059, PubMed:7781064, PubMed:7796814). The MSL complex
CC       specifically associates with the single X chromosome in males and
CC       mediates formation of H4K16ac, promoting a two-fold activation of X
CC       chromosome (PubMed:16543150). Msl-2 is only produced in males,
CC       constituting the limiting component of the MSL complex
CC       (PubMed:7781064). Within the MSL complex, msl-2 mediates the selective
CC       binding to the X chromosome and recruitment of the MSL complex via two
CC       different mechanisms (PubMed:18724933, PubMed:20139418,
CC       PubMed:25452275, PubMed:27580037, PubMed:31320325, PubMed:33208948,
CC       PubMed:37602401). Recognizes DNA motifs that are enriched on X
CC       chromosome, named PionX sites, which are characterized by sequence
CC       features and distinct DNA conformation (base roll) (PubMed:18724933,
CC       PubMed:25452275, PubMed:27580037, PubMed:31320325, PubMed:37602401).
CC       Specific recognition of the X chromosome is also mediated by the
CC       formation of a gel-like state: msl-2 undergoes liquid-liquid phase
CC       separation upon binding to roX1 and roX2 non-coding RNAs, leading to
CC       nucleate the MSL complex on the X chromosome (PubMed:33208948). Msl-2
CC       is also required for translation and/or stability of msl-1 in males
CC       (PubMed:7588059, PubMed:7781064, PubMed:7796814). Also acts as an E3
CC       ubiquitin ligase: in complex with msl-1, mediates ubiquitination of
CC       histone H2B at 'Lys-34' (H2BK34Ub) (PubMed:21726816). Also catalyzes
CC       ubiquitination of msl-1, msl-3 and mof components of the MSL complex
CC       (PubMed:23084834, PubMed:28510597). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150,
