# FlyBase gene description for Idh (DROME)

Isocitrate dehydrogenase (Idh) encodes a cytosolic enzyme that catalyzes the oxidative decarboxylation of isocitrate to 2-oxoglutarate, with the concomitant reduction of NADP[+] to NADPH. It functions, along with Men, G6pd, and Pgd, in a small network of NADP reducing enzymes. Idh also participates in NADPH regeneration in the mitochondrion and the peroxisome.
