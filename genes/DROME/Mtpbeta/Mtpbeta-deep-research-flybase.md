# FlyBase gene description for Mtpbeta (DROME)

Mitochondrial trifunctional protein &bgr; subunit (Mtp&bgr;) encodes a subunit of the mitochondrial trifunctional protein. Mtp&bgr; catalyzes the last of the four reactions of fatty acid beta-oxidation which consists in the thiolytic cleavage of 3-oxoacyl-CoA into acetyl-CoA and a fatty acyl-CoA shortened by two carbon atoms.
