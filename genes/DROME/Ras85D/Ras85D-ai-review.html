Gene Description

Ras85D encodes the major Drosophila Ras switch used by receptor tyrosine kinase pathways. In its GTP-bound state, plasma-membrane Ras85D activates downstream effectors including Raf to drive the MAPK cascade downstream of Egfr, Sevenless, Torso, FGF, and Pvr signaling, while intrinsic GTP hydrolysis and RasGAPs return the protein to the GDP-bound inactive state.
