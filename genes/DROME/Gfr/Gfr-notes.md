# Gfr (Q9VHT4) notes

- CG9620/Gfr is the fly SLC35C1 ortholog; it complements LAD-II fibroblasts
  [PMID:15530860 "corrects the GDP-fucose transport defect and
reestablishes fucosylation"].
- In vitro, Gfr is specific for GDP-fucose [PMID:16344471 "specifically
transports GDP-fucose in vitro"]; Gfr nulls are viable, implying a second transporter
  [PMID:16344471 "the Gfr homozygote
was viable"].
- The second transporter is the ER-resident Efr; Gfr and Efr act redundantly for Notch O-fucosylation, while
  Gfr alone is crucial for N-glycan fucosylation [PMID:19948734 "Gfr but not Efr was crucial for the
fucosylation of N-glycans"].
- nac1 (S29L) abolishes transport and N-glycan core fucosylation (HRP epitope) [PMID:22745127 "which abolishes GDP-fucose transport in vivo and in
vitro"].
- Decisions: GDP-fucose transporter activity, Golgi membrane and GDP-fucose import into Golgi lumen accepted as core;
  Golgi apparatus, endomembrane system and GDP-fucose transmembrane transport modified to the more specific terms
  already annotated; antiporter activity (IBA) kept as non-core; all NOT specificity annotations accepted.
