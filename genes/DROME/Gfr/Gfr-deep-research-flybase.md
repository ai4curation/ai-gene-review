# FlyBase gene description for Gfr (DROME)

Golgi GDP-fucose transporter (Gfr) encodes a Golgi transmembrane transporter that specifically imports GDP-L-fucose from the cytosol into the Golgi lumen where it is used for the fucosylation of N- and O-glycans.
