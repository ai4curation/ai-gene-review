CC   -!- FUNCTION: RNA helicase component of the male-specific lethal (MSL)

CC       elevating transcription of the single X chromosome in the male (X
CC       chromosome dosage compensation) (PubMed:1653648, PubMed:25158899,
CC       PubMed:26545078). Within the MSL complex, the helicase activity of mle
CC       remodels roX non-coding RNA (roX1 and roX2)s to promote assembly of the
CC       MSL complex (PubMed:23870142, PubMed:26545078, PubMed:30649456,
CC       PubMed:30805612). Able to unwind blunt-ended RNA duplexes and has
CC       specificity for uridine-rich nucleotides (PubMed:26545078).
