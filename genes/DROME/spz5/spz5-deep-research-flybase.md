# FlyBase gene description for spz5 (DROME)

spatzle 5 (spz5) encodes one of the paralogs of spz. It is a ligand for Toll-6 and kek6. Together, they can promote neuron survival, axonal and dendritic growth and branching, and structural plasticity of synapses. Alterations in spz5 levels influence behaviour in larvae and adult flies, including in locomotion and long-term memory.
