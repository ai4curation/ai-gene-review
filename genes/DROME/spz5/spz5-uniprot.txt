CC   -!- FUNCTION: Neurotrophin which may function as a ligand for the Toll-
CC       related receptors Toll-6 and Toll-7 (PubMed:23892553). Binds to Toll-7
CC       and Toll-6, and probably acts as their ligands in the promotion of
CC       motor axon targeting and neuronal survival in the central nervous
CC       system (CNS) (PubMed:19018662, PubMed:23892553). Involved in synaptic
CC       targeting of ISNb/d motorneurons and also some SNa motorneurons
CC       (PubMed:19018662). May be involved in the normal development of
CC       specific neurons at the neuromuscular junction (PubMed:24124519).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19018662, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23892553,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24124519}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer; disulfide-linked. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P48607}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Detected in the fan-shaped body which is a
CC       component of the locomotion center in the central nervous system (CNS)
CC       (at protein level). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23892553}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: In larvae, expressed in a punctate pattern along
CC       the CNS axons (at protein level) (PubMed:23892553). Expressed in larval
CC       body wall muscles and in the synaptic boutons (PubMed:24124519). In
CC       embryos, expressed in the CNS midline and muscles (PubMed:19018662).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19018662, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23892553,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24124519}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: 'Spaetzle' means 'noodles' in German. {ECO:0000305}.
