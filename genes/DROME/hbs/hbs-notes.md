# hbs (Hibris; Drosophila melanogaster, A1Z9X4) notes

The falcon deep research report (hbs-deep-research-falcon.md) arrived after the wrapper timeout; it agrees with the picture below (Hbs as an IRM adhesion protein acting redundantly with Sns in fusion, with Rst in retinal sorting, and in sensory organ spacing; nephrocyte role unproven).

## Identity
- Nephrin-like IgSF transmembrane protein (8 Ig domains, FnIII, 160 aa cytoplasmic tail), paralog of Sns; member of the IRM with Kirre, Rst, Sns.
- [PMID:11684662 "The Hibris protein has eight Ig-like domains, a fibronectin domain and a 160 amino acid cytoplasmic tail."]

## Myoblast fusion
- [PMID:11684661 "In the somatic mesoderm, Hbs is restricted to fusion competent myoblasts and is regulated by Notch and Ras signaling pathways."]; [PMID:11684661 "Embryos that lack or overexpress hbs show a partial block of myoblast fusion, followed by abnormal muscle morphogenesis."]
- S2 aggregation: [PMID:11684662 "Our S2 cell aggregation assays have revealed a heterotypic interaction between Hibris and Dumbfounded, but not between Hibris and Irregular Chiasm-Roughest."]
- Redundancy with Sns [PMID:19270174 "demonstrating that sns and hbs function redundantly in the formation of several muscle precursors"]; [PMID:19270174 "either an Hbs or Sns cytodomain is essential for muscle precursor formation"].

## Eye
- [PMID:20599904 "Kirre binds Hbs and Sns in vivo and in vitro."]; [PMID:20599904 "mis-expression of Hbs plus Sns within a single IOC led to complete separation of the cell from neighboring ommatidia"].
- Cindr: [PMID:23027549 "Reducing Cindr during fly eye development led to incorrect distribution of the IgCAMs Roughest (Rst) and Hibris (Hbs). Both bound Cindr."]
- Notch: [PMID:22814602 "Loss of hbs results in classical N-signaling-associated phenotypes in Drosophila, including eye patterning, wing margin, and sensory organ specification defects."]; [PMID:22814602 "Our data suggest that Hbs is required for Psn maturation."]
- Ommatidial rotation (genetic interaction with Mtl) [PMID:21441778 "Phenotypic analyses and genetic interaction assays suggest that it may have a role during PCP establishment"].

## Curation thoughts
- Core: heterophilic adhesion with Kirre/Rst (cell adhesion mediator activity), myoblast fusion (redundant with Sns), IRM-mediated cell sorting in the pupal eye.
- Notch processing / positive regulation of Notch signaling: Hbs required for Psn maturation (necessity, not catalysis) -> keep non-core.
- Malpighian tubule stellate cell differentiation (PMID:9927461): abstract does not mention hbs -> UNDECIDED.
