# frc (Q95YI5) notes

- frc encodes a nucleotide-sugar transporter [PMID:11533660 "encodes a nucleotide-sugar transporter that transfers UDP-glucuronic acid,
UDP-N-acetylglucosamine and possibly UDP-xylose from the cytoplasm into the
lumen of the endoplasmic reticulum/Golgi"]; mutants affect Wg, Hh, FGF and fringe-dependent Notch signaling.
- Independently identified as UST74C, Golgi-localized [PMID:11533661 "localized to the Golgi apparatus"]; this
  study reported UDP-galactose and UDP-glucose transport too, while Selva et al. annotated these as NOT -
  the conflict is unresolved (both abstract-only in cache).
- Antiport with UMP or UDP confirmed with purified protein [PMID:17599910 "UDP, as well as UMP, may serve as an antiport substrate
of dFRC"].
- Medial Golgi cisternae (PMID:22251795).
- Decisions: UDP-GlcA and UDP-GlcNAc transport are core; conflicting UDP-Gal/UDP-Glc rows UNDECIDED; UDP-xylose and
  IBA UDP-GalNAc kept as non-core; antiporter kept as non-core (consistent with Gfr); developmental phenotypes non-core.

- Falcon deep research (frc-deep-research-falcon.md): Goto et al. used yeast microsomes and saw UDP-GalNAc, UDP-GlcA, UDP-Gal,
  UDP-Glc and UDP-GlcNAc transport; Selva et al. used Leishmania vesicles and saw UDP-GlcNAc, UDP-GlcA and UDP-Xyl but not
  UDP-Glc/UDP-Gal. The conflict is assay-system dependent, so the UNDECIDED calls stand; the UDP-GalNAc IBA is supported by
  the Goto data. About 30% of maternal/zygotic frc embryos are neurogenic, beyond Fringe-dependent effects.
