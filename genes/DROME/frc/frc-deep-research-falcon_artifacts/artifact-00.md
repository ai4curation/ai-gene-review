| Fringe–Notch mechanism | Genetics, localization and UDP-GlcNAc transport | Frc is inferred to provide UDP-GlcNAc for Fringe-mediated addition of GlcNAc to O-fucose on Notch EGF repeats; frc and fringe show phenotypic similarity and dosage-sensitive genetic interaction (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000011) | Strong mechanistic model, but the retrieved evidence does not show direct purified-Fringe glycan quantification in frc mutants. |
