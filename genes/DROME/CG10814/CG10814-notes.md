# CG10814 notes

- 2026-10-09: Initial review. CG10814 (A1Z9C1, synonyms dGBBD/gamma-BBH) is the fly homolog of
  gamma-butyrobetaine dioxygenase (BBOX1). No Drosophila publications are attached to its GOA
  rows; all annotations are ISS/IEA.
- UniProt: gamma-BBH/TMLD family, PANTHER PTHR10696:SF33 "GAMMA-BUTYROBETAINE DIOXYGENASE"
  [file:DROME/CG10814/CG10814-uniprot.txt "PANTHER; PTHR10696:SF33; GAMMA-BUTYROBETAINE DIOXYGENASE; 1."],
  Fe(II) and ascorbate cofactors and carnitine biosynthesis pathway assigned by ARBA.
- Pathway context (mammals): "Reactions 1 and 4 are catalyzed by two Fe2+-dependent dioxygenases:
  TML dioxygenase (TMLD) and γ-butyrobetaine dioxygenase (BBD), which are related by homology"
  [PMID:38615009].
- Decisions: ISS GBBD activity, carnitine biosynthesis and cytosol accepted on orthology; generic
  oxidoreductase / 2OG-dioxygenase IEA rows MODIFY to GO:0008336.
- No fly experimental data found; activity untested in flies.
- Carillo et al. 2020 review of carnitine in Drosophila [PMID:33371457]: "[39] suggested that the
  CG10814 gene was the most likely ortholog for the hγ-BBH1 gene, sharing with this the lack of a
  mitochondrial targeting sequence." Also reports age-dependent CG10814 induction and HNF4-dependent
  activation (secondary reports, not reviewed in detail). Supports cytosol ISS.
