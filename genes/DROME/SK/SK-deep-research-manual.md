# SK (dSK, Q7KVW5) - manual literature summary

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled manually from cached
publications (PubMed search "Drosophila SK channel dSK calcium-activated potassium"
returned 3 papers; PMID:22509342 added as a key paper).

## Identity
- Single Drosophila KCNN-family small-conductance Ca2+-activated K+ channel (UniProt
  KCNN_DROME, 927 aa, 7 splice isoforms; Pfam SK_channel, Ion_trans_2, CaMBD; PANTHER PTHR10153).
- RNA editing at position 782 by Adar (UniProt, PubMed:12537569, 17018572).

## Channel activity
- Whole-cell recordings of dissociated photoreceptors: slow Ca2+-activated K+ current
  abolished in dSK nulls and rescued by UAS-SKmyc [PMID:21957252 "Importantly, the current was abolished in dSK − photoreceptors and fully restored when a UAS-SKmyc rescue construct was expressed in the dSK − -null background"].
- Calmodulin-dependent [PMID:21957252 "the current was Ca 2+ /calmodulin-dependent as it was absent either when extracellular Ca 2+ was removed or in cam flies lacking calmodulin"].
- 1-EBIO enhanced, apamin-insensitive [PMID:21957252 "However, the dSK current was not altered by apamin treatment, suggesting that dSK is apamin insensitive"].

## Localization
- Lamina photoreceptor axons and non-glutamatergic monopolar cells including L4
  [PMID:21957252 "dSK is highly enriched at the lamina synaptic network where it localizes to photoreceptor axons and a group of nonglutamatergic monopolar cells"].
- Antennal lobe projection neurons [PMID:22509342 "dSK is highly expressed in PNs connecting ALs and MB calyx."].

## Physiology
- Photoreceptors: mutants depolarized, faster oscillatory adaptation; somatic light
  currents normal [PMID:21957252 "This was supported by their normal somatic light-induced currents and high dSK expression in the lamina circuits."].
- Larval NMJ: postsynaptic dSK activated by postsynaptic Ca2+ entry, depresses EPSPs
  [PMID:24671529 "Ca(2+) entering at the postsynaptic membrane was sufficient to activate dSK channels"].
- Visual network homeostasis with dSlo (BK) [PMID:31350259].
- Courtship memory [PMID:22509342 "While it negatively regulates 30 min memory acquisition, dSK is required for 24 h memory formation."].
