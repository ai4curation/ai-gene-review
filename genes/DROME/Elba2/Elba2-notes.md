# Elba2 notes

- UniProt Q9VQD6 (CG9883). BEN-solo protein; Elba heterotrimer subunit with Elba1 and adaptor Elba3 [PMID:23240086 "DNA binding depends upon the BEN domains in Elba1 and Elba2, while Elba3 mediates the assembly of an active Elba complex by interacting with the N-terminal domains of Elba1 and Elba2"].
- Boundary function: [PMID:23240086 "the blocking activity of the Elba×8 multimer is compromised by injection of dsRNA for each of elba gene"].
- Repression via Insv sites independently of Elba1/3 [PMID:25561495 "Elba2 functions as a repressor whose activity via Insv-binding sites does not depend on the other ELBA factors"]; no Notch/neural role [PMID:25561495 "Notably, Bsg25A and Elba2 can not substitute for the role of Insv in controlling Notch-mediated asymmetric cell divisions in the peripheral system"].
- H1-like chromatin architecture role [PMID:27687115 "its mutation causes a disruption of normal internucleosomal spacing of chromatin and reduced nuclear compaction in syncytial embryos"]; [PMID:27687115 "Elba2 protein is distributed ubiquitously in polytene chromosomes and strongly colocalizes with H1"].
