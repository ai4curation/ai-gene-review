Gene Description

Aubergine (Aub) is a cytoplasmic PIWI-clade Argonaute of the Drosophila germline. Loaded with antisense, 5-uridine piRNAs, Aub localizes to perinuclear nuage, where it slices complementary transposon transcripts; the cleavage products are loaded onto Ago3 (with Krimper scaffolding and Tudor-domain proteins recognising symmetric dimethylarginines in the Aub N-terminus), driving the ping-pong amplification of secondary piRNAs and post-transcriptional transposon silencing. Maternal Aub is also a component of posterior germ plasm/polar granules, where piRNA-guided binding traps germ plasm mRNAs and supports pole cell formation, while in the bulk embryo it recruits CCR4-NOT to deadenylate and clear maternal mRNAs such as nanos.
