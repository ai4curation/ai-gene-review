# Vps26 (CG14804) notes

Retromer cargo-selective complex subunit (arrestin fold).

- Wls retrieval / Wg secretion [PMID:18193032 "In the absence of the retromer components Dvps35 or Dvps26, but in presence of Wg, Wls is degraded and Wg secretion impaired."]; Hh/Dpp unaffected (UniProt: "The hh and dpp signaling pathways do not require the retromer complex").
- Notch as retromer cargo in neuroblast lineages; model returns Notch to cell surface [PMID:30176986 "Notch is a bona fide cargo protein of retromer"].
- Fat body lysosome function [PMID:26172538 "the late stages of autophagy and endocytosis are impaired in Vps26 and Vps35 deficient Drosophila larval fat body cells"].
- iPLA2-VIA binds Vps26/Vps35 [PMID:29909971 "iPLA2-VIA binds the retromer subunits Vps35 and Vps26"].

Decisions: cargo receptor activity -> cargo adaptor activity (contributes_to; retromer is peripheral, not a TM receptor); obsolete endosome-to-PM transport -> endocytic recycling (GO replaced_by); retromer complex -> cargo-selective complex; protein binding -> phospholipase binding; regulation of Wnt signaling -> positive regulation of Wnt protein secretion.
