CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the retromer complex which acts in conjunction
CC       with wingless (wg) and clathrin-mediated endocytosis to sustain a
CC       wntless (wls) traffic loop. This loop encompasses the Golgi, the cell
CC       surface, an endocytic compartment and a retrograde route leading back
CC       to the Golgi, thereby enabling wls to direct wg secretion. The hh and
CC       dpp signaling pathways do not require the retromer complex suggesting
CC       that it does not play a general role in exocytosis.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18193032}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the retromer complex, composed of Vps26 and
CC       Vps35. {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18193032}. Membrane
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18193032}; Peripheral membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18193032}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Accumulation of wg in the Golgi, preventing wg
CC       from being secreted from the cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18193032}.
