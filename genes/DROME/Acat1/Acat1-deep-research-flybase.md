# FlyBase gene description for Acat1 (DROME)

Acetyl-CoA acetyltransferase 1 (Acat1) encodes a mitochondrial enzyme that catalyzes the thiolytic cleavage of acetoacetyl-CoA in the last step of ketolysis. Acat1 is also involved in beta oxidation last step in which fatty acids are broken down to acetyl-CoA. Acat1 activity is reversible catalyzing the condensation of two acetyl-CoA into acetoacetyl-CoA in the first step of ketogenesis.
