# Nct (Nicastrin; Drosophila melanogaster, Q9VC27) notes

## Identity
- Type I transmembrane glycoprotein; non-catalytic subunit of gamma-secretase (Presenilin/Psn catalytic, Nct, aph-1, pen-2). Large ectodomain thought to engage substrate N-termini.

## Literature
- Nct mutants block Psn-dependent intramembrane cleavage of Notch and APP [PMID:11781576 "loss of Nicastrin activity blocks the accumulation of Presenilin associated with the apical plasma membrane, abolishes Presenilin-dependent cleavage of the transmembrane domains of Notch and beta-APP, and abrogates Notch signal transduction"].
- [PMID:11782315 "Genetic and inhibitor studies indicate a function for Nicastrin in the gamma-secretase step of Notch processing, similar to Presenilin."]; [PMID:11782315 "In the absence of Nicastrin, Presenilin is destabilized and mature C-terminal subunits are absent."]; model: [PMID:11782315 "Nicastrin might recruit gamma-secretase substrates into the proteolytic complex as a prerequisite for Presenilin maturation and active complex assembly."]
- S3 cleavage specificity [PMID:11782316 "Drosophila Nicastrin is required for Notch signaling, and acts specifically at the S3 cleavage step."]
- S2-cell RNAi [PMID:12110170 "RNAi-mediated inactivation of aph-1, pen-2, or nicastrin in cultured Drosophila cells reduces gamma-secretase cleavage of betaAPP and Notch substrates and reduces the levels of processed presenilin."]
- Complex assembly [PMID:12660785 "Co-expression of Drosophila Pen-2 with Aph-1 and nicastrin increases the formation of Psn fragments as well as gamma-secretase activity."]
- In vivo reconstitution [PMID:20421416 "coexpression of these four components promotes the efficient assembly of mature, proteolytically active gamma-secretase"]; substrate preference [PMID:20421416 "markedly reduced cleavage of some APP family members compared to cleavage of the Notch receptor"].
- Eye development [PMID:16145661 "Nicastrin is essential to early photoreceptor neuron development"].

## Curation thoughts
- Process terms for the intramembrane cleavage (Notch receptor processing, membrane protein intracellular domain proteolysis) are appropriate for a non-catalytic but essential subunit of the protease complex (it contributes structure to the step).
- "membrane protein ectodomain proteolysis" is the S2/ADAM (Kuzbanian) shedding step, not gamma-secretase -> MODIFY to intramembrane/intracellular domain proteolysis.
- Amyloid-beta formation: assays use human APP substrate; flies have no endogenous Abeta -> non-core.
