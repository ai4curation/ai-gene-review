# Lsp2 (Q24388) review notes

## Identity
- Larval serum protein 2; hexamerin, hemocyanin family (PANTHER PTHR11511:SF5). UniProt: homohexamer, secreted.

## Literature
- LSP-2 distinct from LSP-1 subunits [PMID:6804227 "These observations show that the LSP-1 subunits are more closely related to each other than any is to LSP-2"]
- Hexamerin storage and allocation [PMID:38851190 "preventing hexamerin stores enhances the growth of early-developing organs while compromising the emergence of late-forming ones"] (abstract only in cache)
- Wnt4/target specificity screen: ectopic expression of M13-enriched genes [PMID:17764943 "Ectopic expression of five other M13-enriched genes, including beat-IIIc and Glutactin, also inhibits synapse formation by MN12s."]

## Decisions
- Core MF nutrient reservoir activity; extracellular region.
- GO:0005616 (defined as the LSP-1 alpha/beta/gamma heterohexamer) over-annotated for the LSP-2 homohexamer; raised as a question.
- Axon guidance / synaptic target inhibition from ectopic expression marked over-annotated.
