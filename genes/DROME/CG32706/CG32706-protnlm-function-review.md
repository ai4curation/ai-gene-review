Transfer is justified by the specific family, retained RNA-binding region and conserved ribosome-assembly mechanism.

**Requirement for the specifically named sites A0, A1 and A2: UNC.** These are the experimentally characterized yeast processing sites. The available evidence does not independently map those precise sites in Drosophila or demonstrate that CG32706 is required at each. Conserved participation in early maturation does not validate that exact statement; it remains unresolved, and does not imply intrinsic nuclease activity. O74362 repeats a by-similarity paragraph and is not independent experimental validation. The target’s precise biochemical contacts remain inferred.
