| Canonical intracellular pathway | Spätzle to Toll to MyD88 to Tube and Pelle to Cactus degradation to nuclear Dorsal or Dif | Toll and MyD88 interact through Toll/interleukin-1 receptor domains. MyD88 organizes Tube and the serine/threonine kinase Pelle. Pelle-dependent signaling removes the IκB-like inhibitor Cactus, releasing Dorsal or Dif for nuclear entry and target-gene transcription.
