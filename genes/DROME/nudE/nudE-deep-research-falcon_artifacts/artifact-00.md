Streaming occurred in **22% of 50 metaphase cells**, versus **54%** for Rod. In mutants, chromosome overcondensation occurred in **89.4%** of cells; the relative mitotic index was **3.00**, with **6,055 prometaphase/metaphase cells versus 53 anaphase/telophase cells** scored. | Kinetochore recruitment and molecular partners | Tandem-affinity purification plus localization analysis in `lis1`, `cenp-meta`, RZZ-complex, dynein-heavy-chain and checkpoint-mutant backgrounds. | **Direct evidence:** Lis1 strongly associated with NudE in purification. | Centrosome migration and spindle-pole attachment | Tubulin/DSpd-2 staining of larval neuroblasts and time-lapse β-tubulin–GFP imaging of primary spermatocytes. | **Direct evidence:** `nudE39A` cells had broad or unfocused poles, curved/non-bipolar spindles and detached or supernumerary centrosomes. | Dendrite and axon morphogenesis | Live imaging of class-IV ddaC neurons in protein-null `nudE39A` homozygotes or `nudE39A/Df`; ppk-CD4–GFP visualized morphology; genomic and neuron-specific rescue constructs were tested. | **Direct evidence:** Null neurons had significantly reduced dendrite length and branch number, including fewer distal branches, plus severe splitting of normally unbranched axons. A genomic wild-type transgene rescued lethality and fully restored dendrite growth and branching; neuron-specific UAS-`nudE` produced partial rescue. Reducing dynein light-intermediate-chain activity enhanced the phenotype, whereas increasing Lis1 restored dendrite length, branch number and distribution. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000004) | **Interpretation:** NudE is a neuronal dynein cofactor that facilitates a productive Lis1–dynein interaction needed to establish dendritic architecture. | Arthur et al., *Journal of Cell Science*, June 2015. Dendritic polarity remained predominantly cell-body-directed. Reducing γ-tubulin did not eliminate mutant retrograde axonal comets. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000005) | **Interpretation:** NudE probably regulates initiation/termination or tethering of dynein-dependent Golgi-outpost transport and helps maintain stable, uniformly plus-end-distal axonal microtubules; direct tethering is unproven. | Arthur et al., *Journal of Cell Science*, June 2015.
