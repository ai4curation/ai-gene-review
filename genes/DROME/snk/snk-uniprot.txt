CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the extracellular signaling pathway that
CC       establishes the dorsal-ventral pathway of the embryo (PubMed:11486795).
CC       A protease cascade involving ndl, gd, snk and ea results in activation
CC       of the spz Toll receptor ligand; acts downstream of ndl and gd
CC       (PubMed:20605458). Activation of ea requires both activation of the
CC       ndl-gd-snk protease cascade and sulfation of a vitelline membrane
CC       component by pip (PubMed:20605458). Localized activation of the Toll
CC       receptor in the ventral region of the embryo defines cell identities
CC       along the dorsal-ventral continuum (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11486795, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20605458,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:20605458}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via N-terminal prodomain) with ea/easter (via
CC       Peptidase domain); leads to proteolytic activation of ea by snk
CC       (PubMed:20605458). This interaction does not require sulfation of a
CC       vitelline membrane component by pip but proteolytic cleavage of ea by
CC       snk does (PubMed:20605458). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20605458}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9477324}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The clip domain consists of 35-55 residues which are 'knitted'
CC       together usually by 3 conserved disulfide bonds forming a clip-like
CC       compact structure. {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU01236}.
CC   -!- PTM: Proteolytically activated by gd (PubMed:20605458). May also be
CC       cleaved by another protease (PubMed:20605458).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20605458}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the peptidase S1 family. CLIP subfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU01236}.
