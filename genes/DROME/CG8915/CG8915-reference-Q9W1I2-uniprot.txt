CC   -!- FUNCTION: Forms a complex with tut and bam involved in 3'UTR-dependent
CC       post-transcriptional repression of several 3'-RNA processing factors,
CC       which promotes germline stem cell lineage differentiation and mitosis-
CC       to-meiosis transition (PubMed:25412508, PubMed:28190776). Part of a
CC       complex with bam involved in 3'-UTR-dependent translational repression
CC       of a subset of mRNAs, including those for mei-P26, nanos and shg/E-
CC       cadherin; may act as a promiscuous RNA-binding protein tethering bam to
CC       its target mRNAs (PubMed:19470484, PubMed:19556547, PubMed:23122292,
CC       PubMed:23526974). Required for regulating the progression of
CC       gonialblast cells through transit amplification and differentiation
CC       into gametes (PubMed:9334284). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19470484,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19556547, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23122292,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23526974, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25412508,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28190776, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9334284}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of a complex composed of at least mei-P26, bam, bgcn and
CC       Sxl; this complex is involved in translational repression of nanos mRNA
CC       (PubMed:23526974). Interacts with bam (via C-terminus); the interaction
CC       is direct (PubMed:19470484, PubMed:19556547, PubMed:23122292,
CC       PubMed:23526974, PubMed:25412508). Interacts with mei-P26; the
CC       interaction is direct and does not require bam (PubMed:23526974).
CC       Weakly interacts with wh/wuho; this interaction may be required for the
CC       function or formation of the mei-P26-bgcn-bam-Sxl complex
CC       (PubMed:31941704). Part of a complex composed of at least tut, bam and
CC       bgcn; complex formation does not require RNA (PubMed:25412508).
CC       Interacts with tut; the interaction is indirect and is mediated by bam
CC       (PubMed:25412508). As part of the bam-bgcn-tut complex associates with
CC       twin; may recruit the CCR4-NOT1 deadenylation complex to mRNA 3'UTRs to
CC       mediate post-transcriptional regulation of expression
CC       (PubMed:28190776). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19470484,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19556547, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23122292,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23526974, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25412508,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28190776, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31941704}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       Q9W1I2; P22745: bam; NbExp=13; IntAct=EBI-142483, EBI-88504;
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in testis and in 5-8 germline stem cells
CC       of ovaries, immediately adjacent to terminal filament
CC       (PubMed:10924476). Expressed in ovarian germline cells throughout the
CC       germarium (at protein level) (PubMed:23526974).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10924476, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23526974}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Flies exhibit abnormal cysts undergoing transit
CC       amplification containing an excess number of cells that do not
CC       differentiate into gametes. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9334284}.
