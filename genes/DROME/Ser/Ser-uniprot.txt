CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in a temporally and spatially regulated
CC       manner throughout development in cells of ectodermal origin; first
CC       detected at 4 hours into embryogenesis, peaking between 8 and 12 hours
CC       and diminishing thereafter (PubMed:2125287). Earliest expression is in
CC       stage 10-11 embryos in the anlage of the clypeolabrum and in two
CC       longitudinal rows of foregut ectodermal cells associated with the
CC       hypopharangeal lobe, regions destined to become the roof and floor of
CC       the pharynx (at protein level) (PubMed:1840519, PubMed:2125287).
CC       Expression in the roof of the pharynx continues until end of
CC       embryogenesis while in the floor it ceases after germband extension (at
CC       protein level) (PubMed:1840519). During germ-band-extension, expressed
CC       near the proctodeum of the 8th and 9th abdominal segments and in the
CC       labial and maxillary primordium near the tracheal pit of the 1st
CC       thoracic segment (PubMed:2125287). Expressed from late stage 11 onwards
CC       in a ring of cells that eventually become the anterior part of the
CC       stomodeum (at protein level) (PubMed:1840519). Expressed in two regions
CC       of the hindgut from stage 12 onwards (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:1840519, PubMed:2125287). Expressed in a repetitive segmented
CC       pattern in the epidermis from stage 11 until after germband retraction;
CC       each segment has a dorsal and a ventral stripe of expression that are
CC       out of register with each other (at protein level) (PubMed:1840519,
CC       PubMed:2125287). From stage 15 expressed in the posterior commissures
CC       and segmental nerves of each segment (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:1840519). Expressed in the two main lateral trunks of the
CC       tracheal system from stage 13 onwards (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:1840519, PubMed:2125287). In late embryogenesis, expressed in
CC       the ectodermal invaginations that may form the primordia of imaginal
CC       discs (PubMed:2125287). In the wing imaginal disc of 3rd instar larvae
CC       expressed in cells destined to form the wing margin and the anlage of
CC       the alula, and in three lines of cells that may be destined to form
CC       wing veins (at protein level) (PubMed:1840519). Also expressed in the
CC       gnathal segments and the anlagen of the anal pads during late
CC       embryogenesis (PubMed:1840519, PubMed:2125287). Also expressed in the
CC       secretory ducts of the salivary gland and in the ventral side of the
CC       frontal sac from embryogenesis stage 14 onwards (PubMed:1840519).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1840519, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2125287}.
