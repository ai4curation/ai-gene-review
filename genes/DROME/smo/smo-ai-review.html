Gene Description

Smoothened (Smo) is the class-F seven-transmembrane receptor that transduces Hedgehog signalling in Drosophila. In the absence of ligand, Patched (Ptc) keeps Smo inactive and destabilised; Hedgehog binding to Ptc relieves that inhibition and Smo accumulates at the plasma membrane in a progressively activated state. What distinguishes the fly protein from its vertebrate orthologue is an unusually long (~480-residue) C-terminal cytoplasmic tail. Hedgehog drives graded phosphorylation of this tail by protein kinase A, casein kinase 1 and G protein-coupled receptor kinase 2, which neutralises inhibitory arginine clusters, switches the tail from a closed to an open conformation, promotes its dimerisation, and converts it into a docking platform for the kinesin-related scaffold Costal-2 and the Ser/Thr kinase Fused. Recruiting the Costal-2/Fused complex to the membrane activates Fused, blocks proteolytic processing of the transcription factor Cubitus interruptus into its repressor form, and releases the Ci activator. Because this cytoplasmic complex performs the organising role that the primary cilium plays in vertebrates, Hedgehog transduction in fly epithelia such as the imaginal discs proceeds without a cilium; in the ciliated olfactory sensory neurons, however, Smo localises to the sensory cilium and signals from there in an intraflagellar-transport-dependent manner. Smo also behaves as a canonical GPCR, coupling to Galphai to lower cAMP, and its activation state is tuned by membrane lipids: phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate binds an arginine motif in the C-tail and phosphatidic acid binds a pocket in the transmembrane core. Through these mechanisms Smo governs embryonic segment polarity, anterior/posterior patterning and growth of the imaginal discs, eye and head development, ovarian somatic stem cell maintenance and follicle cell development, and a Hedgehog-dependent arm of the gut epithelial immune response to bacterial uracil.

It resolves in favour of the node. The fly GOA record carries its own experimental ciliary
annotation, GO:0005929 cilium, evidence IDA, reference PMID:24768000 (Kuzhandaivel et al.,
Cell Rep 2014), qualifier located_in. The paper is exactly on point:

So the apparent contradiction dissolves once the cell type is specified. Drosophila is not an
unciliated animal — it has ciliated sensory neurons and sperm; what is true is that the
developmental epithelia (imaginal discs, embryonic ectoderm) where the classical pathway was worked
out are unciliated, and there the Cos2/Fu complex substitutes for the ciliary compartment. In the
ciliated OSNs, Smo goes to the cilium and signals there. The eumetazoan node placement is therefore
grounded in protostome evidence as well as vertebrate evidence, and is not a vertebrate assertion
leaking into flies.

dendritic Smo are the same observation at two resolutions.
- By contrast, GO:0071679 commissural neuron axon guidance at the same node has no fly seed —

This is a useful contrast to keep straight: three rows at one node, two of which are sound because
the fly is genuinely a seed, and one of which over-reaches because it is not.
