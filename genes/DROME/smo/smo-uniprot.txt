CC   -!- FUNCTION: G protein-coupled receptor, which transduces the smoothened
CC       signaling pathway and which is required for correct patterning of every
CC       segment (PubMed:10966113, PubMed:15616566, PubMed:15691767,
CC       PubMed:8700230, PubMed:8706127). Activated by cholesterol in response
CC       to hedgehog (hh) morphogen (By similarity). In absence of hedgehog, smo
CC       is inactivated by patched protein (ptc), which prevents smo access to
CC       cholesterol (By similarity). In response to hh-binding to ptc,
CC       inhibition is relieved, promoting smo translocation to primary cilium
CC       and activation by cholesterol: cholesterol causes a conformation change
CC       that triggers signaling via G protein G(i), mediating inhibition of
CC       adenylate cyclase activity and decreassed production of cAMP (By
CC       similarity). Essential component of a hh-signaling pathway which
CC       regulates the Duox-dependent gut immune response to bacterial uracil;
CC       required to activate Cad99C-dependent endosome formation, norpA-
CC       dependent Ca2+ mobilization and p38 MAPK, which are essential steps in
CC       the Duox-dependent production of reactive oxygen species (ROS) in
CC       response to intestinal bacterial infection (PubMed:25639794).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q99835, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10966113,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15616566, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15691767,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25639794, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8700230,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8706127}.

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24768000}; Multi-pass membrane protein

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in olfactory sensory neurons (at protein
CC       level). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24768000}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed at all developmental stages, though the
CC       levels vary. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8706127}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylation by CkIalpha and PKA regulates smo accumulation at
CC       the cell surface and its signaling activity in response to hh.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15616566}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: RNAi-mediated knockdown severely reduces adult
CC       survival following the ingestion of E.carotovora. Abolishes Cad99C-
CC       dependent formation of endosomes and DUOX-dependent up-regulation of
CC       reactive oxygen species (ROS) in the intestines of adults fed bacteria-
CC       derived uracil. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25639794}.

CC       {ECO:0000305}.
