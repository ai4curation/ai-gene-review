CC   -!- FUNCTION: Main component of the apoptosome, an adapter protein complex
CC       that mediates activation of the caspase cascade in programmed cell
CC       death initiated by the intrinsic apoptosis pathway (PubMed:10984473,
CC       PubMed:16310803, PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603, PubMed:27916517).
CC       Recruits the initiator caspase Dronc, promoting its autocatalytic
CC       cleavage and activation; triggers the caspase cascade upstream of the
CC       effector caspases Drice and dcp-1 (PubMed:10619022, PubMed:10619023,
CC       PubMed:10984473, PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603). Effector of reaper,
CC       grim and hid induced cell killing (PubMed:10559939, PubMed:10619023,
CC       PubMed:16645639). Produces at least two isoforms that may activate
CC       distinct caspases by separate pathways (PubMed:10619023). Nucleotide
CC       binding protein with strong specificity for dATP; dATP binding is not
CC       essential, but promotes homooligomerization to form the apoptosome
CC       complex (PubMed:10619022, PubMed:10619023, PubMed:16310803,
CC       PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603, PubMed:27916517). There is currently
CC       no evidence of dATPase activity and structural analysis of the
CC       nucleotide binding pocket suggests negligible to no dATPase activity
CC       (Probable). Caspase activation by the apoptosome was initially thought
CC       to be dependent on binding of cytochrome c released from mitochondria,
CC       as is the case for vertebrate orthologs (PubMed:10619022,
CC       PubMed:10619023). Apoptosis induction by the Dark apoptosome appears to
CC       be cytochrome c independent (PubMed:11901172). Required for stress-
CC       induced apoptosis, for example in response to radiation (UV or gamma)
CC       or treatment with macromolecular synthesis inhibitors like
CC       cycloheximide and actinomycin D (PubMed:11901172, PubMed:16533943).
CC       Required for most, but not all programmed cell death during embryonic
CC       development (PubMed:16645639). Involved in developmental cell number
CC       regulation by apoptosis, particularly during neuronal and sensory organ
CC       development (PubMed:10559939, PubMed:10619022, PubMed:10619023,
CC       PubMed:16533943, PubMed:16645639). Involved in reduction of midline
CC       glial cell numbers by programmed cell death during embryogenesis
CC       (PubMed:16645639). During metamorphosis involved in removal of larval
CC       salivary glands by programmed cell death, but not of the larval midgut
CC       (PubMed:16533943). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10559939,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10619022, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10619023,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10984473, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11901172,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16310803, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16533943,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16645639, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27916517,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:27916517}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Exists as an auto-inhibited monomer; this state is maintained
CC       by intramolecular interactions between the NB-ARC and WD repeat domains
CC       (PubMed:25644603, PubMed:38381783). In vitro, at high protein
CC       concentrations or in the presence of non-physiologically high dATP
CC       concentrations, assembles into homooctameric rings 300A in diameter to
CC       form the apoptosome complex; two rings can interact face to face (via
CC       CARD domains with some involvement of the WD repeat beta-propellors) to
CC       form a double ring structure (PubMed:16310803, PubMed:21220123,
CC       PubMed:25644603, PubMed:27916517). The octameric ring may represent a
CC       non-functional or off-pathway conformation (Probable). Under
CC       physiological conditions oligomerization results in formation of a
CC       single unsealed ring or partial helical spiral structure; this
CC       structure may be an assembly intermediate (PubMed:38381783). Apoptosome
CC       assembly is stimulated by dATP binding (PubMed:16310803,
CC       PubMed:25644603, PubMed:38381783). Interacts (via CARD and WD domains)
CC       with the initiator caspase Dronc (via CARD domain); the interaction
CC       stimulates Dark oligomerization to form the apoptosome and brings pairs
CC       of Dronc molecules together on the apoptosome to facilitate their
CC       dimerization and activation by autocatalytic cleavage (PubMed:10984473,
CC       PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603, PubMed:38381783). Assembly of the
CC       apoptosome does not require cytochrome c binding (PubMed:16310803,
CC       PubMed:25644603). After autocatalytic cleavage Dronc caspase domains
CC       dissociates from the apoptosome but the CARD domain remains associated
CC       (PubMed:25644603). Interacts (via N-terminus) with Dredd
CC       (PubMed:10559939). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10559939,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10984473, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16310803,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27916517, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38381783,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:27916517}.

CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed at all stages of development with
CC       highest expression levels in late stage larvae and pupae and lowest in
CC       embryos and adults (PubMed:10559939). Broadly expressed in
CC       preblastoderm embryos due to maternal contribution (PubMed:10619022,
CC       PubMed:10619023). By stage 7 of embryogenesis high levels of expression
CC       in the ventral neurogeneic region and around invagination furrows,
CC       regions where apoptosis plays an important role during subsequent
CC       developmental steps (PubMed:10619022). High levels of expression in the
CC       procephalic ectoderm and mesoderm during embryogenesis stages 10 to 11
CC       (PubMed:10619022). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10559939,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10619022, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10619023}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: [Isoform B]: The major isoform expressed
CC       throughout embryogenesis and in larvae. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10619023}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: [Isoform A]: Low levels of expression during
CC       embryogenesis but not detected in larvae.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10619023}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Induced by ionizing radiation including X-ray and UV.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10619022}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Possesses an N-terminal caspase activation and recruitment
CC       domain (CARD) (PubMed:16310803, PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603,
CC       PubMed:27916517). At high protein concentrations in vitro the CARD
CC       domain mediates the interaction of two octameric apoptosome rings to
CC       form a double ring structure (PubMed:21220123).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16310803, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27916517}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Possesses a NB-ARC domain (nucleotide-binding adapter shared by
CC       APAF-1, certain R gene products and CED-4) that forms the inner hub of
CC       the apoptosome ring and is the main mediator of oligomerization
CC       (PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603, PubMed:27916517). The interface of
CC       the NB-ARC domain and a neighboring helical region forms a binding
CC       pocket that can accommodate a nucleotide tri-phosphate with specificity
CC       for dATP (PubMed:21220123, PubMed:27916517). In its monomeric non-
CC       activated state the binding pocket may be associated with ADP
CC       (PubMed:38381783). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27916517,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38381783}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: [Isoform B]: May possess 15 tandem WD repeats that form two C-
CC       terminal beta-propellor domains; the smaller beta-propellor may be made
CC       up of 7 WD repeats and the larger C-terminal beta-propellor of 8 WD
CC       repeats (PubMed:16310803, PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603,
CC       PubMed:27916517). This region may be involved in regulating caspase
CC       activation activity (PubMed:10559939). The WD repeat region does not
CC       appear to bind cytochrome c (PubMed:16310803). The WD repeat beta-
CC       propellors interact with initiator caspase Dronc; this interaction is
CC       not essential for interaction of Dark with Dronc but is required for
CC       Dronc-induced assembly of the Dark apoptosome and Dark apoptosome-
CC       facilitation of Dronc autocatalytic activation (PubMed:25644603).
CC       Interactions between the WD repeat beta-propellor domains and the NB-
CC       ARC domain help maintain the monomeric non-active state
CC       (PubMed:38381783). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10559939,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16310803, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27916517,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38381783}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Pupal lethal (PubMed:16533943). Reduced apoptosis
CC       during neuronal development results in central nervous system
CC       hyperplasia; a larger number of differentiated neurons survive in late
CC       stage embryos and third instar larvae have larger brains
CC       (PubMed:10559939, PubMed:10619022, PubMed:10619023, PubMed:16533943).
CC       Delayed removal of the larval salivary gland due to defects in
CC       induction of apoptosis (PubMed:16533943). Imaginal discs of the eye and
CC       wing are enlarged (PubMed:16533943). RNAi-mediated knockdown
CC       significantly reduces levels of apoptosis and caspase activity during
CC       embryonic development (PubMed:10619022, PubMed:10619023).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10559939, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10619022,
