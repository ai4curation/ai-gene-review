# CycA ProtNLM2 function-description review

## Original prediction

> Essential for the control of the cell cycle at the G2/M (mitosis) transition

Original wording and all model/source metadata are retained in [CycA-protnlm-source.json](CycA-protnlm-source.json).

## Assessment

**Supported conserved function (CNN as established same-gene G2/M biology).** Cyclin A has a demonstrated role in mitotic progression, and the selected PC protein retains the characterized cyclin domain. The one-residue N-terminal difference supplies no evidence of loss of that broad role. The exact temporal claim is transferred from characterized CycA biology, not independently measured in PC.

[PMID:2564316](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2564316/) establishes cell-cycle-dependent localization and the requirement for continued division; [PMID:17431409](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17431409/) dissects cyclin/CDK substrate-recognition control. [Sequence comparison](CycA-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md) verifies complete cyclin-domain retention. The original paragraph and its plant-donor P46277 metadata are preserved; the native fly evidence supports the broad statement independently of that donor.
