# Abi (A0A0B4K774) notes

- Adaptor within WRC and beyond [PMID:21900504 "Abi represents a multi-domain adaptor protein that is not only an integral component of the WAVE complex"]; stabilises WAVE [PMID:21900504 "In abi mutants, WAVE is unstable but restored by reexpression of Abi, confirming that Abi controls the integrity of the WAVE complex in vivo."]
- WIRS binding surface (Sra + Abi) [PMID:24439376 "a sequence motif that defines these ligands binds to a highly conserved interaction surface of the WRC formed by the Sra and Abi subunits"]; Fat2 binding [PMID:26903538 "A purified GST-fusion of dFat2-CT binds to the wild-type WRC, but not to a mutant whose WIRS binding surface is disrupted by mutations"]
- Ena/VASP [PMID:25203209 "We demonstrate that Ena/VASP and the WRC control actin polymerization in a cooperative manner through the interaction of the Ena/VASP EVH1 domain with an extended proline rich motif in Abi."]
- WASp activation [PMID:16155589 "Abi binds WASP through its carboxy-terminal domain and acts as a potent stimulator of WASP-dependent F-actin formation."]
- Abl/PTP61F regulate Abi localization [PMID:17804420 "Abi can be translocalized from the cytosol to the cell membrane by either increasing Abl or reducing endogenous PTP61F."]
- Zasp paper (PMID:18166658) uses Abi RNAi only as a cell-spreading control [PMID:18166658 "Although RNAi with Abi causes spreading defects, which are characterized by a star-shaped morphology, in both S2 and S2R+ cells"]; muscle attachment annotation from it left UNDECIDED, cell adhesion marked over-annotated.
- Fat2 paper concerns follicle rotation, not centripetal migration [PMID:26903538 "Concomitantly, the AbiΔWIRS and fat2 mutant cells completely failed to migrate"] -> MODIFY GO:0060269 to GO:0007297.
