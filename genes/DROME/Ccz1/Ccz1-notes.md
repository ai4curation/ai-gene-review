# Ccz1 (Drosophila melanogaster, Q9VZL5) notes

The falcon deep research run exceeded the wrapper timeout but its report arrived later (Ccz1-deep-research-falcon.md); it adds fly autophagy data: [file:DROME/Ccz1/Ccz1-deep-research-falcon.md "a Ccz1 null allele disrupted Rab7 association with Atg8a-positive autophagic structures"].

## Identity
- Longin-domain subunit that heterodimerises with Mon1 to form the Rab7 GEF; in metazoa joined by Bulli/RMC1 as a third subunit.

## Literature
- [PMID:32499409 "Recruitment of Rab7 to endosomes requires the Mon1-Ccz1 guanine-nucleotide-exchange factor (GEF)."]; [PMID:32499409 "Both the Mon1-Ccz1 dimer and a Bulli-containing trimer display Rab7 GEF activity."]
- [PMID:37216550 "RMC1 acts as a scaffolding subunit and binds to both Mon1 and Ccz1 on the surface opposite to the RAB7A-binding site"]
- [PMID:37463208 "This requires Rab7 activation by its specific guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) Mon1-Ccz1."]
- PMID:23418349 (Dmon1 paper; Ccz1 annotations derive from it): [PMID:23418349 "loss of function of Dmon1 results in an enlargement of maturing endosomes and loss of their association with Rab7"].

## Curation thoughts
- GEF activity is a property of the heterodimer: contributes_to GEF activity accepted.
- Autophagy (ISS from yeast) kept non-core: Mon1-Ccz1/RMC1 is needed for autophagic function in metazoa via Rab7, but no fly data here.
