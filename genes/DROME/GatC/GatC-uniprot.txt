CC   -!- FUNCTION: Structural subunit of the mitochondrial Glu-tRNA(Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex GatCAB. GatCAB allows the formation of
CC       correctly charged Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the transamidation of
CC       misacylated Glu-tRNA(Gln) in the mitochondria. The reaction takes place
CC       in the presence of glutamine and ATP through an activated gamma-
CC       phospho-Glu-tRNA(Gln). This subunit probably forms a belt that wraps
CC       around the interface of the catalytic subunits A and B to stabilize the
CC       complex. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03149}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Subunit of the heterotrimeric GatCAB amidotransferase (AdT)
CC       complex, composed of A, B and C subunits. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_03149}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Mitochondrion {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03149}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: This protein may be expected to contain an N-terminal
CC       transit peptide but none has been predicted. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_03149}.
