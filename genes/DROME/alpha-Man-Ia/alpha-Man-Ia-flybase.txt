            ( 3.2.1.113 ) Gene Summary α-Mannosidase class I a ( α-Man-Ia ) encodes a transmembrane enzyme that catalyzes the sequential removal of α-1,2-linked mannose residues from protein N-linked glycan Man(9)GlcNAc(2) to produce Man(5)GlcNAc(2). It is involved in the processing of protein N-glycans in the Golgi apparatus.

(Adapted from PMID:17116466 ). Pathway (FlyBase) GOLGI N-GLYCAN TRIMMING - In the Golgi apparatus, N-glycans are further processed by class I mannosidase which can trim high mannose N-glycans (Man(8-9)GlcNAc(2)) down to Man(5)GlcNAc(2). In D. melanogaster, N-glycan processing often ends at this stage and the glycoproteins are delivered to organelles, the plasma membrane or secreted. Alternatively, Man(5)GlcNAc(2) can be further processed to GlcNAc(1)Man(3)GlcNAc(2) which constitutes a branch point intermediate for the production of complex and hybrid N-glycans. However, the removal of the terminal GlcNAc by N-acetylglucosaminidase fdl prevents any further elongation, resulting in the export of paucimannose N-glycan linked proteins. (Adapted from FBrf0235108 and FBrf0224862 .) Protein Function (UniProtKB) Involved in the maturation of Asn-linked  oligosaccharides. Progressively trim alpha-1,2-linked mannose  residues from Man(9)GlcNAc(2) to produce Man(5)GlcNAc(2).
