CC   -!- FUNCTION: S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent 2'-O-ribose
CC       methyltransferase that catalyzes the formation of 2'-O-methyluridine at
CC       position 1579 (Um1579) in the mitochondrial large subunit ribosomal RNA
CC       (mtLSU rRNA), a universally conserved modification in the peptidyl
CC       transferase domain of the mtLSU rRNA. This activity may require prior
CC       2'-O-methylguanosine modification at position 1580 (Gm1580) by MRM3.
CC       Essential for late-stage assembly of mtLSU required for efficient
CC       translation of mitochondrial DNA encoded proteins; methyltransferase
CC       activity is not required for this function. Essential for mitochondrial
CC       respiratory function. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9UI43,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:25009282}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
