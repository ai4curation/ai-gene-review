# CG10184 notes

- 2026-10-09: Initial review. CG10184 (Q9VCK6, synonym HTMLA) is a PLP-dependent threonine
  aldolase-family protein (PANTHER PTHR48097 L-threonine aldolase). No fly experimental papers.
- FlyBase ISS annotations come from mouse Tha1 (Q6XPS7), characterized in a substrate-based virtual
  screening study [PMID:38615009]: "As we found that the mouse enzyme has a strong preference (1000
  folds) for HTML towards threonine, the name hydroxytrimethyllysine aldolase (Htmla) would be a
  better descriptor of the mouse gene." and "the putative animal ortholog Tha1 could be responsible
  for HTMLA activity in those animals in which the gene is present together with the other genes
  of the pathway." [PMID:38615009]
- SHMT1/2 also show weak HTMLA activity in mammals: "The two top-ranked candidates, SHMT1 and SHMT2,
  were found to be able to catalyze the HTMLA reaction" [PMID:38615009]. Fly Shmt is the other
  candidate in the FlyBase CARNIBI group.
- Decisions: GO:0120567 HTMLA (ISS) accepted as predicted core MF; threonine / L-allo-threonine
  aldolase, glycine biosynthesis and threonine catabolism (IBA/ISS) kept as non-core; generic lyase
  and amino-acid-metabolism IEA rows MODIFY to specific terms already annotated.
- Carillo et al. 2020 review [PMID:33371457]: "the uncharacterized DmelCG10184 gene that is
  classified as the ortholog of Mmus/Tha1/GLY1 (threonine aldolase 1)". The same review also lists
  CG10184 (with CG14630, CG5321) among candidate gamma-BBH orthologs, which conflicts with its
  PLP-aldolase fold and is probably a confusion with CG10814; not used.
- Falcon deep research (CG10184-deep-research-falcon.md) agrees: no fly experimental data; HTMLA
  role is a pathway-level hypothesis; localization unknown.
