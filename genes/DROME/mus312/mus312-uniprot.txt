CC   -!- FUNCTION: Regulatory subunit that interacts with and increases the
CC       activity of different structure-specific endonucleases. Has several
CC       distinct roles in protecting genome stability by resolving diverse
CC       forms of deleterious DNA structures originating from replication and
CC       recombination intermediates and from DNA damage (By similarity).
CC       Component of the SLX1-SLX4 structure-specific endonuclease that
CC       resolves DNA secondary structures generated during DNA repair and
CC       recombination. Has endonuclease activity towards branched DNA
CC       substrates, introducing single-strand cuts in duplex DNA close to
CC       junctions with ss-DNA (By similarity). Interacts with the structure-
CC       specific MEI-9-ERCC1 endonuclease to generate meiotic crossovers.
CC       {ECO:0000250, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12504024, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19595722,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19596236}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms a heterodimer with SLX1. Interacts with mei-9; catalytic
CC       subunit of the MEI-9-ERCC1 endonuclease. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12504024,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19595722, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19596236}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000250}.
