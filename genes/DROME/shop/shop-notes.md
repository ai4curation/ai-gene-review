# shop notes

- 2026-10-09: Initial review of shop (shopper; Q9VWP4; CG7280), sulfite oxidase.
- Otto et al. 2018 [PMID:30158546] (full text): "The sulfite oxidase activity is drastically reduced upon
  lack of shopper ..."; "In S2 cells, ShopperHA is found in a punctate pattern co-localizing with the
  mitochondrial marker mitoGFP"; "shopper encodes a homolog of the human sulfite oxidase named SUOX,
  which localizes to the intermembrane space of mitochondria."; required in ensheathing glia.
  The text reports sulfite (not sulfate) measurements, so intracellular sulfate ion homeostasis IMP
  was marked over-annotated (supplements not checked).
- Martelli et al. 2024 [PMID:38416643]: ISOD model; "Adding sulfite (the substrate of sulfite oxidase)
  or thiosulfate to the Cys-free diet significantly reduced the health of shop larvae (and not
  controls)"; cysteine-free diet rescues.
- Mitochondrion rows (IBA/IEA/IDA) -> intermembrane space (TAS/IEA present); sulfur compound metabolic
  process -> sulfide oxidation; molybdenum ion binding -> molybdopterin cofactor binding.
