# FlyBase gene description for kek6 (DROME)

kekkon 6 (kek6) encodes a co-receptor for spz5, its other receptor being Toll-6. kek6 functions by activating CaMKII and inducing synaptogenesis and axonal branching at the neuromuscular junction.
