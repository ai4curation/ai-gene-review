# FlyBase gene description for Echs1 (DROME)

Enoyl-CoA hydratase, short chain 1 (Echs1) encodes a mitochondrial enzyme that catalyzes the second of the four reactions of fatty acid beta-oxidation, which consists in the hydration of (2E)-enoyl-CoA to generate (3S)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA.
