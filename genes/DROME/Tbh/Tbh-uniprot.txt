CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the hydroxylation of tyramine into octopamine, a
CC       neurotransmitter involved in ovulation and locomotion (PubMed:14623230,
CC       PubMed:14978721, PubMed:16376104, PubMed:8656284). Functions in an
CC       amine-mediated Bacc-dependent signaling pathway that negatively
CC       regulates acute ethanol sensitivity (PubMed:23142736). Involved in
CC       facilitation of nociceptive escape behavior in response to potentially
CC       damaging stimuli, such as high temperatures (PubMed:37309249).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14623230, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14978721,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16376104, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23142736,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37309249, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8656284}.

CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: At larval stage, present in cell bodies of the

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8656284}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Slightly down-regulated in adults fed a high calorie diet.
