# FlyBase gene description for Tbh (DROME)

Tyramine &bgr; hydroxylase (Tbh) encodes a monooxygenase that catalyzes the conversion of tyramine to octopamine and is the key limiting enzyme in octopamine synthesis. It regulates egg laying, positive associative olfactory learning and memory, aggression, flight initiation and maintenance, locomotor activity and attraction to ethanol containing food sources.
