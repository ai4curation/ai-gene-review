# Nf-YA notes

- UniProt Q9VSY9; NFYA/HAP2 family; NF-Y heterotrimer [PMID:17216611 "The CCAAT motif-binding factor NF-Y consists of three different subunits, NF-YA, NF-YB, and NF-YC, all of which are required for formation of the NF-Y complex and DNA-binding"]; [PMID:17216611 "NF-YA contains a DNA binding domain in its C-terminal region"].
- R7 differentiation via sev [PMID:23213364 "dNF-Y is necessary for appropriate sev expression and involved in R7 photoreceptor cell development"].
- debcl regulation [PMID:23470843 "promoter contains four dNF-Y-binding consensus sequences which play positive roles in promoter activity"].
- Deep research (falcon) available: Nf-YA-deep-research-falcon.md.

## Decisions
- DNA-templated transcription (UniRule IEA) marked over-annotated (TF regulates, does not execute, transcription).
- Protein binding (NF-YC Y2H) removed; R7 differentiation kept non-core.

## Thorax development (follow-up on FlyBase description)

- Nf-YA knockdown gives a thorax disclosed phenotype; NF-YA binds the CCAAT-containing basket (JNK) promoter
  and is needed for basket expression [PMID:18233955 "These results suggest that dNF-Y is necessary for proper bsk expression and activity of JNK pathway during thorax development."].
  Added to the review description; no process annotation added (single RNAi study, indirect role via basket transcription).
