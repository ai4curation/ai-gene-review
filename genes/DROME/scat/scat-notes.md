# scat (scattered, Vps54, CG3766) notes

- GARP-defining subunit; male sterile; acroblast/acrosome defect [PMID:27481842 "The Vps54 (Scat) protein, a subunit of the GARP tethering complex, is a central factor in retrograde transport to the trans-Golgi."; "This acrosome formation defect was due to a fault in the trans-Golgi side of the acroblast ribbon."].
- Lerp (M6PR homolog) no longer reaches trans-Golgi in scat1 [PMID:27481842 "The trans-Golgi marker Golgin245 showed co-localization with GFP-Lerp in WT, but not in the scat1 mutant postmeiotic round spermatids"].
- Scat-RFP at the trans side of the Golgi [PMID:27481842 "suggesting that Scat is localized to the trans side of the Golgi"].
- Individualization: scat1 forms hardly any investment cones [PMID:27481842 "In the case of the scat1 mutant we observed hardly any investment cones and the process of individualization did not start."]. Original isolation as an individualization mutant in PMID:9550716 (abstract only in cache).
- Binds Arl5-GTP with other GARP subunits [PMID:25795912].
- VPS54 KD has a weaker effect on Tf recycling than syndetin KD [PMID:25799061 "Vps54 KD also delayed Tf-Alexa568 recycling, albeit to a lesser extent"].
