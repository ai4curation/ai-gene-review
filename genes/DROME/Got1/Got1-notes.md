# Got1 (CG8430, A1ZAA5) notes

Module: dmel_aspartate_asparagine_metabolism (with Asns, Got2); also malate-aspartate shuttle partner of Mdh1.

## Literature journal
- Grell 1976: two AST isozymes [PMID:823072 "The aspartate aminotransferases (designated GOT1 and GOT2) are two enzymes of Drosophila melanogaster"]; low-activity allele [PMID:823072 "At the locus of Got1 a mutation with low activity was recovered and designated Got1lo."]
- Chase & Kankel 1987 (abstract only): GOT in transmitter glutamate pools [PMID:3106567 "enzymatic activities thought to be important in the regulation of transmitter-glutamate pools"].
- CPTI YFP traps localisation (HDA cytosol) [PMID:25294944]; Got1 CPTI line also used to show expression in DN1 clock neurons and glia (PMID:32210832, not cached).
- Peroxisome is an ISM prediction from Faust 2012 [PMID:22758915 "The subcellular localization of five of these predicted peroxisomal proteins was confirmed."]; Got1 C-terminus ...CENKL is not a canonical PTS1.
- UniProt lists side activities (cysteine aminotransferase, 2-aminobutanoate, cysteine sulfinate) by ARBA, from mammalian GOT1.
