# FlyBase gene description for Got1 (DROME)

Glutamate oxaloacetate transaminase 1 (Got1) encodes a cytosolic enzyme that catalyzes the interconversion of L-aspartate and alpha-ketoglutarate to oxaloacetate and L-glutamate. It is involved in glutamate and aspartate biosynthesis, gluconeogenesis and the malate-aspartate shuttle.
