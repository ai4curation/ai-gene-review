# FlyBase gene description for fng (DROME)

fringe (fng) encodes a Golgi-localized glycosyltransferase that catalyzes the attachment of N-acetyl-&agr;-D-glucosamine to O-fucosylated EGF-like repeats in selected membrane and secreted proteins. The O-fucosylation of EGF-like repeats in the N protein modulates the interaction of N with its ligands encoded by Ser and Delta.
