CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the na (narrow abdomen) sodium channel complex.
CC       In the circadian clock neurons it functions with na and unc79 to
CC       promote circadian rhythmicity. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24223770}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with unc79 and na. Can interact with unc79
CC       independently of na. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24223770}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Membrane {ECO:0000255}; Multi-pass membrane
CC       protein {ECO:0000255}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Severe disruptions in circadian rhythmicity.
CC       Decreases anticipatory behaviors and free-running rhythmicity. RNAi-
CC       mediated knockdown in pacemaker neurons decreases anticipatory
CC       behaviors under constant dark (DD) conditions but has no effect on
CC       light-dark (LD) rhythmic behavior. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24223770}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the unc-80 family. {ECO:0000305}.
