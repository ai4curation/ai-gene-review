# sgl (O02373) notes

- sgl encodes UDP-glucose dehydrogenase; recombinant protein is active [PMID:9862465 "Overexpression of sgl
in E. coli resulted in synthesis of a protein with high levels of UDPGDH
activity."].
- kiwi (= sgl) mutants phenocopy wingless and are rescued by UDP-glucuronate or heparan sulfate
  [PMID:9217004 "By rescuing the kiwi phenotype with both
UDP-glucuronate and the glycosaminoglycan heparan sulfate"].
- sgl mutants lose both chondroitin and heparan sulfate [PMID:10806213 "compromise the synthesis of both
chondroitin and heparan sulfate"].
- HS is required for Heartless/Breathless FGF signaling [PMID:10433902 "sugarless and
sulfateless mutant embryos have phenotypes similar to those lacking the
functions of two Drosophila fibroblast growth factor receptors"].
- Decisions: catalytic activity, UDP-glucuronate biosynthesis and cytosol accepted as core; GAG/HSPG/CSPG
  biosynthesis kept as non-core (Sgl supplies the precursor; chain synthesis is done by EXT/Golgi enzymes);
  signaling/migration/patterning phenotypes kept as non-core; cytoplasm and generic oxidoreductase modified.
