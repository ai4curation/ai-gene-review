# FlyBase gene description for sgl (DROME)

sugarless (sgl) encodes an UDP-glucose 6-dehydrogenase that catalyzes the conversion of UDP-D-glucose into UDP-D-glucuronate, a component of glycosaminoglycans. UDP-D-glucuronate is required for the biosynthesis of heparan sulfate and chondroitin sulfate proteoglycans.
