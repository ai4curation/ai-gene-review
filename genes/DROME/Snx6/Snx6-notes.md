# Snx6 (CG8282) notes

SNX5/SNX6 ortholog (Vps17-like); SNX-BAR partner of Snx1.

- [PMID:22041890 "DSNX6 is the homolog of mammalian SNX5/6."]; null viable [PMID:22041890 "Similarly, the Dsnx6 null mutants were also completely viable and fertile, and exhibited no obvious defects"]; not needed for Wg secretion [PMID:22041890 "we also observed no Wg secretion defects in Drosophila Dsnx1 mutants and in Dsnx6 RNAi-depleted cells"].
- Required for autolysosomal degradation in fat body [PMID:26172538 "Finally, we find that Snx3 and Snx6 are also required for proper autolysosomal degradation in Drosophila larval fat body cells."].
