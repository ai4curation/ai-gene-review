Gene Description

eyeless (ey) encodes the Drosophila ortholog of vertebrate Pax6, a nuclear transcription factor with an N-terminal paired domain (PAI and RED subdomains), a paired-type homeodomain and a C-terminal transactivation region. Expressed in the embryonic eye primordium, throughout the undifferentiated eye portion of the eye-antennal imaginal disc, and in parts of the brain, ey acts downstream of its paralog twin of eyeless (toy) and at the top of the retinal determination network: it binds eye-specific enhancers and activates sine oculis, eyes absent, dachshund, Optix and the proneural gene atonal, specifying eye-field identity and enabling compound eye formation. Loss of ey reduces or eliminates the compound eye, and targeted ectopic expression induces morphologically normal ectopic eyes on legs, wings and antennae. Its paired domain drives retinal determination, whereas the homeodomain promotes tissue growth and antagonizes paired-domain activity. Outside the eye, ey is required for mushroom body and central complex development in the brain and for differentiation and function of the insulin-producing neurons, where it directly activates dilp5.

Core Functions

Ey is a sequence-specific RNA polymerase II DNA-binding transcription activator that binds eye-specific enhancers of retinal determination genes (sine oculis, eyes absent, Optix, atonal) via its paired domain and activates their transcription in the nucleus.

Identity and domains

  • Paired-box (Pax6 class) transcription factor with an N-terminal paired domain
    (PAI + RED subdomains, aa 56-182) and a paired-type homeodomain (aa 430-489);
    C-terminal P/S/T-rich transactivation region (UniProt O18381 features).
  • Three isoforms (C/larval, A/embryonic, B lacking the N-terminal paired region).
  • Cloned as the fly homolog of mouse Small eye / human Aniridia (PAX6)
    PMID:7914031

Eye specification (core)

  • Ectopic expression is sufficient to induce eyes on wings, legs and antennae
    PMID:7892602 PMID:7892602
  • Upstream regulator: toy (second fly Pax6) directly activates the eye enhancer of
    ey PMID:10198632.
  • Downstream targets: so and eya PMID:9584118; direct binding to so intronic eye
    enhancer PMID:10207149; genome-wide direct targets incl. eya, shifted, Optix PMID:16533912; dachshund regulation PMID:16780828.
  • Direct regulation of the proneural gene atonal via an eye-specific enhancer, with
    Sine oculis PMID:17108002 and in synergy with Daughterless homodimer PMID:24886829.
  • Ey is a transcriptional activator, unlike Eyg PMID:15973436; C-terminal activation domain stronger than Toy's PMID:19406113
  • Paired domain drives eye induction; homeodomain promotes growth and antagonizes
    PD function PMID:25234589
  • Intact protein (PD, HD and C-terminus) required for rescue PMID:19666017

Brain / neuroendocrine roles (non-core)

  • Mushroom body: expressed in MB progenitors and neurons; hypomorph ey(R) loses
    medial lobe PMID:10903173
  • Adult brain structure and locomotion PMID:11153010; central complex PMID:19666017.
  • Insulin-producing cells: Ey directly activates dilp5 PMID:18852455; loss reduces body size and raises hemolymph sugar.
  • Hyperglycaemia screen hit PMID:25994086.
  • Glial migration timing in eye disc PMID:11804568.
  • Physical interaction with Antp homeodomain, inhibitory to Ey PMID:18755899.
