Gene Description

Raf encodes the Drosophila RAF-family MAP kinase kinase kinase activated downstream of Ras85D. Receptor and Ras signaling recruit and activate Raf at the cytoplasmic face of membranes, where Raf phosphorylates Dsor1/MEK and propagates signals through the Rolled/ERK MAPK cascade during EGFR, Torso, Sevenless, and FGF pathway outputs.
