# FlyBase gene description for Pde4 (DROME)

Phosphodiesterase 4 (Pde4) encodes a cAMP-specific phosphodiesterase, responsible for cAMP degradation. Pde4 product plays a pivotal role in neurological and behavioral plasticity including synaptic development and function, learning and courtship. The product of Pde4 is also known to affect regulation of several developmental processes such as oogenesis.
