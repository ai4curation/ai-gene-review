# nero (Q9V9U4) review notes

Module context: dmel_hypusine_biosynthesis (Dhps then nero on eIF5A).

Deep research: `nero-deep-research-falcon.md` (falcon). It did not retrieve the
Patel et al. 2009 full text and calls Nero localization unresolved; the cached full text
(PMID:19546244) does show ER association, so the ER annotations were accepted.

- DOHH reaction (UniProt): "Catalyzes the hydroxylation of the N(6)-(4-aminobutyl)-L-lysine intermediate to form hypusine"
- Catalytic requirement in vivo: [PMID:19546244 "expression of enzymatically impaired forms of DOHH fails to rescue nero clones, indicating that hypusination activity is important for nero function"]
- eIF5A knockdown phenocopies: [PMID:19546244 "Knockdown of eIF5A, the target of hypusination, recapitulates nero mutant phenotypes"]
- ER: [PMID:19546244 "Double labeling of imaginal discs with the Nero antibody and a monoclonal antibody that recognizes the ER retention signal KDEL shows extensive colocalization"]; [PMID:19546244 "As the Nero protein does not bear a signal peptide or a C-terminal KDEL sequence tag, Nero most likely associates with the external surface of the ER"]

Decisions: all 10 annotations accepted. Growth, autophagy and cell-cycle phenotypes are
downstream of eIF5A function and were not proposed as NEW process terms.
