# FlyBase gene description for crb (DROME)

crumbs (crb) encodes a transmembrane protein that binds to multiple proteins such as those encoded by sdt, par-6, AP-2&agr;, yrt, ex and Moe. It contributes to organization of zonula adherens, epithelial morphogenesis, apico-basal cell polarity, and is a negative regulator of Notch activity and growth control via the Hippo pathway. In photoreceptor cells it is involved in morphogenesis, the trafficking of the product of ninaE and prevention of light-dependent photoreceptor degeneration.
