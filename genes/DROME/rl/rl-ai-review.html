Gene Description

rl encodes Rolled, the Drosophila ERK-family MAP kinase activated by Dsor1/MEK in receptor-driven Ras/MAPK pathways. Activated Rolled phosphorylates cytoplasmic and nuclear substrates, including transcriptional regulators, to execute EGFR, Sevenless, Torso, FGF, Pvr, insulin, and stress-associated signaling outputs.
