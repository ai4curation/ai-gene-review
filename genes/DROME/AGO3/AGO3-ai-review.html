Gene Description

Argonaute 3 (AGO3) is a cytoplasmic PIWI-clade Argonaute of the Drosophila germline that localizes to perinuclear nuage. It binds predominantly sense-strand piRNAs (adenine at position 10) generated by Aubergine slicing of transposon transcripts, and its own slicer activity cleaves antisense piRNA-cluster transcripts to produce new antisense piRNAs for Aub, completing the ping-pong amplification cycle. Recruitment with Aub into a Krimper-scaffolded complex depends on symmetric arginine dimethylation of its N-terminus. AGO3 slicing is essential for piRNA amplification and transposon silencing in ovaries and testes, and AGO3 also restrains homotypic Aub:Aub ping-pong.
