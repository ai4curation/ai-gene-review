CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the perinuclear meiotic nuage, a germline-
CC       specific subcellular membraneless ribonucleoprotein compartment
CC       involved in production of transposable element-repressing Piwi-
CC       interacting RNA (piRNA)-induced silencing complexes (piRISCs), which
CC       are essential for maintaining germline integrity during oogenesis
CC       (PubMed:22303351, PubMed:26295961). Acts via the Piwi-interacting RNA
CC       (piRNA) metabolic process, which mediates the repression of
CC       transposable elements during meiosis by forming complexes composed of
CC       piRNAs and Piwi proteins and governs the methylation and subsequent
CC       repression of transposons (PubMed:17322028, PubMed:17346786,
CC       PubMed:19395009, PubMed:20980675). Piwi protein that directly binds
CC       piRNAs, a class of 24 to 30 nucleotide RNAs that are generated by a
CC       Dicer-independent mechanism and are primarily derived from transposons
CC       and other repeated sequence elements (PubMed:17322028, PubMed:17346786,
CC       PubMed:26212455, PubMed:26295961). Associates predominantly with sense
CC       piRNAs that contain adenine at nucleotide 10, but shows no preference
CC       for uridine at the 5' end (PubMed:17322028, PubMed:17346786,
CC       PubMed:26212455). Shows RNA cleavage or slicer activity
CC       (PubMed:17322028). Together with Piwi protein aub recruited to
CC       subregions of the perinuclear nuage by krimp, which coordinates their
CC       activity in the ping-pong amplification step of secondary piRNA
CC       biogenesis (PubMed:26295961, PubMed:34210982). Krimp recruits piRNA
CC       bound aub and unbound AGO3, bringing them into close proximity to
CC       facilitate the loading onto AGO3 of freshly cut piRNAs generated by aub
CC       cleavage of target sequences; krimp recognizes the piRNA loading state
CC       of the Piwi proteins via symmetrically dimethylated arginine
CC       modification in their N-terminus (PubMed:34210982). Important for
CC       asymmetric ping-pong amplification to bias production towards antisense
CC       piRNAs capable of silencing transposable elements (PubMed:19395009,
CC       PubMed:26212455). In testis, associates with Su(Ste) and AT-chX-1
CC       piRNAs mostly produced from antisense precursors (PubMed:20980675). In
CC       the germline, acts to amplify pools of antisense piRNAs, among others
CC       Su(Ste), AT-chX-1 and roo, and to limit sense piRNA accumulation
CC       (PubMed:20980675). Forms a complex with smg, twin, aub and specific
CC       piRNAs that targets nos mRNA (and probably other maternal mRNAS) for
CC       deadenylation promoting its decay during early embryogenesis
CC       (PubMed:20953170). Involved in transposon silencing in the adult brain
CC       (PubMed:23559253). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17322028,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17346786, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19395009,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20953170, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20980675,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22303351, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23559253,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26212455, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26295961,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34210982}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the ping-pong piRNA processing (4P) complex
CC       consisting of krimp, aub and AGO3 (PubMed:19959991, PubMed:26295961,
CC       PubMed:34210982). Interacts (via N-terminus when not methylated on
CC       arginine residues) with krimp (via non-canonical tudor domain); this
CC       interaction leads to symmetrical dimethylation on AGO3 arginine
CC       residues and its subsequent dissociation from krimp (PubMed:26212455,
CC       PubMed:26295961, PubMed:34210982). Krimp associated AGO3 is mostly free
CC       of piRNA binding and the interaction plays an important role in the
CC       loading of AGO3 with piRNAs; piRNA binding may stimulate dissociation
CC       of the two proteins (PubMed:26212455, PubMed:26295961). May form part
CC       of a piRNA processing complex consisting of tud, aub and AGO3
CC       (PubMed:19959991). Interacts (when symmetrically dimethylated on
CC       arginine residues) with tud (PubMed:19959991, PubMed:26212455). Forms a
CC       complex with smg, twin, aub, nos mRNA and piRNAs that target the nos
CC       3'-untranslated region, in early embryos (PubMed:20953170). Interacts
CC       (via the N-terminal region when symmetrically methylated on arginine
CC       residues) with papi (via C-terminus); this interaction is RNA-
CC       independent and may be required for AGO3 localization to the nuage
CC       (PubMed:21447556). Interacts with TER94 and tral (PubMed:21447556).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19959991, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20953170,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21447556, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26212455,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26295961, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34210982}.

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: In ovary, expressed in germline stem cells,

CC       found in the somatic cap cells of the germarium. In testis, expressed

CC       the testis (at protein level). Expressed in neurons throughout the
CC       adult brain and in the mushroom body subdivision in the peduncle. In
CC       the mushroom body, expressed only in gamma and core alpha-beta neurons.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17322028, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17346786,

CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed maternally. Expression is high in early
CC       embryos but gradually decreases as development proceeds (at protein
CC       level). Expressed in germline cells at early stages of spermatogenesis
CC       and throughout oogenesis (at protein level) (PubMed:22303351).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17322028, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22303351}.
CC   -!- PTM: Symmetrically dimethylated on Arg-4, Arg-68 and Arg-70, most
CC       likely by csul/PRMT5/DART5 (PubMed:19377467, PubMed:19959991,

CC       of its piRNA binding state (PubMed:34210982).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19377467, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19959991,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26212455, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34210982}.
