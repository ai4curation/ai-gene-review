CC       Essential for proper differentiation of ectoderm (PubMed:10935637).
CC       Required for the correct separation of neural and epidermal cell
CC       lineages (PubMed:10935637). Fringe (fng) acts in the Golgi to determine
CC       the type of O-linked fucose on the EGF modules in N, altering the
CC       ability of N to bind with Delta (PubMed:10935637). Ofut1 also has a
CC       role in modulating the interaction (PubMed:10935637). Interacts with
CC       glycosphingolipids, specifically N5 (GalNAc-alpha1-4GalNAc-beta1-
CC       4GlcNAc-beta1-3Man-beta1-4Glc-beta1-1Cer) the product of alpha4GT1 and
CC       alpha4GT2; the interaction regulates endocytosis and subcellular
CC       localization (PubMed:20176925). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10935637,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20176925}.
