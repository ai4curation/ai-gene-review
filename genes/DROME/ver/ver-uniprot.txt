CC   -!- FUNCTION: Part of the MTV complex that associates with the HipHop-HOAP
CC       complex to form the terminin telomere-capping complex involved in
CC       telomere maintenance and prevention of telomere fusion
CC       (PubMed:20679394, PubMed:27835648). As part of the MTV complex binds
CC       single stranded DNA in a sequence-independent manner, protecting it
CC       from degradation (PubMed:27835648). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20679394,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Probably homomultimerizes (PubMed:20679394). Component of the
CC       MTV complex, composed of moi/modigliani, tea and ver/verrocchio
CC       (PubMed:27835648). Interacts with moi/modigliani and tea (via C-
CC       terminus); the interactions are direct and require fully intact
CC       moi/modigliani and ver/verrocchio (PubMed:20679394, PubMed:27835648).
CC       The MTV complex is recruited to telomeres by the HipHop-HOAP complex,
CC       consisting of HipHop, cav/HOAP and Su(var)205/HP1 to form the terminin
CC       telomere-capping complex (PubMed:20679394, PubMed:27835648). Interacts
CC       with cav/HOAP; the interaction is direct (PubMed:20679394). Interacts
CC       with Su(var)205/HP1; the interaction is indirect and probably requires
CC       cav/HOAP or moi/modigliani (PubMed:20679394). Probably interacts with
CC       peo (via N-terminus and UBC domain) (PubMed:26110638).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20679394, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26110638,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20679394,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26110638, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}. Chromosome
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20679394, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26110638,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}. Chromosome, telomere
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20679394, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26110638,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}. Note=Localizes to euchromatic telomeres
CC       of polytene chromosomes (PubMed:26110638). Telomere localization is not
CC       dependent on peo/pendolino (PubMed:26110638). Telomere localization is
CC       dependent on cav/HOAP, tea and moi/modigliani (PubMed:27835648).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26110638, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Lethal at the larval/pupal boundary
CC       (PubMed:20679394). Frequent telomeric fusions in larval neruoblasts
CC       (PubMed:20679394). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20679394}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Multiple telomeric fusions result in multicentric linear
CC       chromosomes that resemble little trains of chromosomes, hence the name
CC       'verrocchio', the name of an Italian train named after the artist.
CC       {ECO:0000303|PubMed:20679394}.
