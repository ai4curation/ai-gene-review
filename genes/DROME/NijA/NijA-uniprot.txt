CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Ninjurin-A]: Effector of non-apoptotic necrotic cell death
CC       that mediates plasma membrane rupture (cytolysis): oligomerizes in
CC       response to death stimuli and promotes plasma membrane rupture by
CC       introducing hydrophilic faces of 2 alpha helices into the hydrophobic
CC       membrane, leading to release intracellular molecules that propagate the
CC       inflammatory response (PubMed:23028562, PubMed:33472215). Also acts as
CC       a homophilic transmembrane adhesion molecule that promotes cell
CC       adhesion by mediating homophilic interactions via its extracellular
CC       region (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O70131,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23028562, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33472215}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Secreted ninjurin-A]: Secreted form generated by cleavage,
CC       which acts as a negative regulator of cell adhesion (PubMed:16815999).
CC       Promotes the loss of cell adhesion in a cell non-autonomous manner
CC       (PubMed:16815999). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16815999}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: [Ninjurin-A]: Homooligomer. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q92982}.

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23028562, ECO:0000305|PubMed:16815999}; Multi-pass
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000255}.

CC   -!- DOMAIN: [Ninjurin-A]: Composed of 4 alpha helices: 2 hydrophobic
CC       transmembrane regions (alpha3 and alpha4) and 2 alpha helices (alpha1
CC       and alpha2). Alpha1 and alpha2 feature one hydrophobic side and a
CC       hydrophilic side. In normal conditions, NijA is inactivated and alpha1
CC       and alpha2 helices are not inserted into the membrane. Following NijA
CC       activation, alpha1 and alpha2 helices insert into the membrane and
CC       drive NijA oligomerization via interactions between alpha3 and alpha4
CC       and the hydrophobic face of alpha1 from an adjacent subunit. Such
CC       structures disrupt membrane integrity and form a lesion through the
CC       introduction of the hydrophilic faces of alpha1 and alpha2 into the
CC       hydrophobic membrane. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q92982}.

CC       ninjurin-A form. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16815999}.
