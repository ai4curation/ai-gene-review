# Vps29 (CG4764) notes

Retromer (and by homology retriever) subunit.

- Dispensable for embryogenesis; needed in aging adults; retromer mislocalized with Rab7; suppressed by TBC1D5 [PMID:32286230 "Vps29 phenotypes are suppressed by reducing Rab7 or overexpressing the GTPase activating protein, TBC1D5"].
- NMJ SV endocytosis defect [PMID:32286230 "Vps291 NMJs show reduced FM 1-43 dye uptake following KCl stimulation, consistent with impaired synaptic vesicle endocytosis."].
- Late endosome/tubular endosome retromer dynamics in salivary gland (PMID:22791894, abstract only).
- Baculovirus entry RNAi screen hit (PMID:30445201, abstract only).

Decisions: generic locations and membrane protein complex -> MODIFY to endosome / cargo-selective complex; synaptic phenotypes non-core.
