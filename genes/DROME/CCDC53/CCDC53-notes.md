# CCDC53 notes

WASH complex (SHRC) subunit; reviewed together with wash, FAM21, CCDC53, Strump and SWIP so that shared complex terms are treated identically.

- Complex composition [PMID:20498093 "WASH functions in cells within a 500 kDa core complex containing Strumpellin, FAM21, KIAA1033 (SWIP), and CCDC53"]; regulatory role [PMID:20498093 "the reconstituted core assembly is inhibited, suggesting that it functions in cells to regulate actin dynamics through WASH"] (abstract only cached; GOA IDA/IPI rows accepted on curator's reading).
- Fly WASH complex in endocytic recycling of integrins and V-ATPase [PMID:27884932 "a conserved role for WASH in the endocytic sorting and recycling of membrane proteins, such as integrins and the V-ATPase"] (abstract only; subunit IMPs accepted).
- Nuclear envelope budding with Wash, capping protein and Arp2/3 [PMID:32503943 "we identify Wash, its regulatory complex (SHRC), capping protein and Arp2/3 as new molecular components involved in the physical aspects of NE bud formation"].
