# FlyBase gene description for Tdc1 (DROME)

Tyrosine decarboxylase 1 (Tdc1) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the decarboxylation of L-tyrosine to form tyramine. Tdc1 is involved in the production of tyramine an octopamine in non-neuronal tissues.
