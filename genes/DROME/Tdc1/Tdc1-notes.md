# Tdc1 (A1Z6N2) review notes

## Identity
- Non-neural tyrosine decarboxylase; group II PLP-dependent aromatic amino acid decarboxylase (PANTHER PTHR11999).
- Paralog Tdc2 is the neural isoform.

## Literature
- Cloning of two fly Tdc genes; both active in vivo; Tdc1 non-neural, Tdc2 neural
  [PMID:15691831 "Although both gene products have TDC activity in vivo, dTdc1 is expressed nonneurally, whereas dTdc2 is expressed neurally."]
- Ectopic Tdc1 in the Tdc2 pattern fully rescues Tdc2 egg-retention sterility
  [PMID:15691831 "driving the expression of dTdc1 in the same pattern results in a complete rescue"]
- Malpighian tubule: Tdc1 in principal cells synthesizes tyramine from tyrosine
  [PMID:19915121 "It is concluded that synthesis of TA by Tdc1 in the principal cells of the MT is required for physiological responses to tyrosine."]
- Ethanol: Tdc1 was among ethanol-regulated genes surveyed for mutant phenotypes (gene-specific data not in cached text)
  [PMID:19951294 "A survey of Drosophila strains harboring mutations in ethanol-regulated genes for ethanol sensitivity and tolerance phenotypes"]
