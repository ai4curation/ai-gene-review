# EcR (Drosophila melanogaster, UniProt P34021): manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this review. This summary was compiled by the
reviewer from cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record. Every statement
carries its source.

## Identity and isoforms

- EcR encodes an NR1-subfamily nuclear receptor [UniProt P34021 "Belongs to the nuclear hormone receptor family. NR1 subfamily."].
- Three isoforms share the DNA- and ligand-binding domains and differ at the N terminus
  [PMID:8324824 "the EcR gene encodes three ecdysone receptor isoforms (EcR-A, EcR-B1, and EcR-B2) that have common DNA- and hormone-binding domains but different N-terminal regions"].
- The isoforms have unrelated AF1 regions [PMID:22912598 "The three isoforms of EcR (FlyBase ID: FBgn0000546) have unrelated AF1 regions, each capable of mediating transcriptional activation in some contexts"].

## Molecular function

- Ligand and DNA binding [PMID:1913820 "EcR protein binds active ecdysteroids"; "EcR protein binds DNA with high specificity at ecdysone response elements"].
- Heterodimer with USP is required [PMID:8247157 "The cloned ecdysone receptor (EcR) is by itself incapable of high-affinity DNA binding or transcriptional activation"; "high-affinity hormone binding requires both EcR and USP"].
- Activator and repressor [PMID:10920384 "functioning as both a transcriptional activator and repressor in vivo"]; unliganded repression gates development
  [PMID:16267093 "We propose that the unliganded receptor complex is repressive and that this repression is relieved as the hormone titers increase at the onset of metamorphosis"].
- Corepressors: Alien binds the EcR silencing region [PMID:10207062 "(B) Alien interacts with the silencing domains of both RAR (RARΔC60) and EcR."];
  dLKR/SDH binds EcR/Usp and restrains hormone-induced transcription [PMID:18695041 "that binds EcR/Usp and regulates hormone-mediated transcription"].
- Coactivators: Taiman [PMID:11163181 "TAI protein colocalized with the ecdysone receptor in vivo and augmented transcriptional activation by the ecdysone receptor in cultured cells"];
  Cmi/Trr [PMID:22569554 "CMI associates with TRR, as well as the EcR-USP receptor, and is required for hormone-dependent transcription"];
  Cryptocephal (ATF4) specifically for EcR-B2 [PMID:22912598 "Radiolabeled CRC-A bound to the EcR-B2 amino-terminus and to full-length EcR-B2, but not to EcR-B1 or to EcR-B2 carrying the E9K mutation."].
- Other partners: DHR38 [PMID:22628309 "the full-length EcR and DHR38 can also form specific complexes within the nuclei of living cells"]; Nup98, ligand-dependent
  [PMID:28366641 "We found that Nup98 gains an interaction with EcR only upon ecdysone stimulation"].

## Location

- Nuclear [PMID:1913820 "As expected, EcR is nuclear and found in all ecdysone target tissues examined"], at puffs on polytene chromosomes
  [PMID:8247157 "native EcR and USP are co-localized on ecdysone-responsive loci of polytene chromosomes"]; a cytoplasmic pool exists in some stages
  [PMID:21981918 "At the peak of 20HE following germ band extension, EcR becomes more nuclear but is still detected in the cytoplasm"].

## Biological roles (via target-gene regulation)

- Hatching, molts, metamorphosis [PMID:10851134 "Our results show that EcR is required for hatching, at each larval molt, and for the initiation of metamorphosis"].
- Embryonic morphogenesis [PMID:12958367 "Ecdysone signaling is required for germ band retraction and head involution, morphogenetic movements that shape the first instar larva"].
- Neuronal remodelling [PMID:11163268 "we found that the EcR-B1 isoform is specifically expressed in the MB gamma neurons, and is required for the pruning of larval processes"].
- Programmed autophagy and death of larval tissues [PMID:15296715 "ecdysone receptor signaling induces programmed autophagy"; PMID:24374353 "Most of these mutations disrupt the ecdysone-induced expression of death activators, thereby failing to initiate tissue destruction"].
- Oogenesis [PMID:10757764 "Our results demonstrate a requirement for EcR during female reproduction and suggest that EcR is required for normal oogenesis"]; adult sleep, memory, lifespan
  [PMID:20215472 "mutants for nuclear ecdysone receptors showed reduced sleep"; PMID:19342482 "mutants in which ecdysone signaling is reduced were defective in LTM"; PMID:12610309 "Flies that are heterozygous for mutations of the ecdysone receptor exhibit increases in life-span and resistance to various stresses"].
