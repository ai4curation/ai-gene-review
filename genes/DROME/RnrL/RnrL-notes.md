## Literature journal
- UniProt P48591: [file:DROME/RnrL/RnrL-uniprot.txt "Catalyzes the biosynthesis of deoxyribonucleotides from the corresponding ribonucleotides."]; allosteric ATP/dNTP control on the M1 subunit.

- Deep research (falcon) summarizes RNAi in intestinal stem cells [file:DROME/RnrL/RnrL-deep-research-falcon.md "In adult midgut intestinal stem cells, four days of RnrL RNAi increased the replication-associated markers γH2Av and RpA70-GFP, delayed S phase, and impaired proliferation"], IRBIT regulation (Arnaoutov and Dasso 2014; Arnaoutov et al. 2020) and maternal requirement (Perez-Mojica et al. 2025).
