# Kpc1 (CG6752; Q9VFC4) notes

## Identity
- RNF123/KPC1 homologue: N-terminal SPRY (B30.2) domain and C-terminal RING finger (UniProt InterPro IPR045129 RNF123/RKP/RSPRY1; IPR001841 Znf_RING); UniProt assigns EC 2.3.2.27 by ARBA rule.

## Literature journal
- Fly KPC complex discovered in a male-sterility screen [PMID:33378371 "Drosophila homologues of KPC1 and KPC2, which comprise the mammalian KIP1 ubiquitination-promoting complex (KPC), form a conserved unit that is required for the sperm tail tip localization of Amo"].
- Complex and mutual stabilization [PMID:33378371 "Similar to the vertebrate KPC complex, co-IP experiments showed that dKPC1 and dKPC2 physically interact and stabilize one another in a reciprocal fashion."].
- Male sterility through sperm storage failure [PMID:33378371 "Male flies lacking either KPC1 or KPC2 phenocopy amo mutants and are sterile due to a failure of sperm storage"].
- No direct binding to Amo [PMID:33378371 "Neither KPC1 nor KPC2 directly interact with Amo but they are detected in proximity to Amo at the tip of the sperm flagellum"].
- RING survey [PMID:21912646] supports RING-E3 assignment (ISM zinc binding).
- Falcon deep research (Kpc1-deep-research-falcon.md): fly E3 catalysis not directly assayed; dKPC1 required for dKPC2 monoubiquitination; Amo is a localization client, not shown to be a substrate.

## Curation thoughts
- MF: RING E3 ubiquitin ligase (phylogenetic/ISS + conserved RING) - accepted; no fly biochemical assay.
- Sperm storage is a necessity phenotype; kept non-core.
- Protein catabolic process (IBA) reflects mammalian KPC p27 degradation; fly substrate unknown, kept non-core.
