# FlyBase gene description for Gpdh1 (DROME)

Glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase 1 (Gpdh1) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the reduction of dihydroxyacetone phosphate to glycerol 3-phosphate with the concomitant oxidation of NADH to NAD(+). It is involved in triglyceride metabolism and is part of the glycerophosphate shuttle.
