CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC), which
CC       inhibits APC/C and delays chromosome segregation until all chromosomes
CC       are properly attached to the mitotic spindle. The MCC, which consists
CC       of CDC20, MAD2L1/MAD2, BUB1B/BUBR1 and BUB3, is a key player of the
CC       spindle assembly checkpoint. The MCC-bound APC/C complex is inactive
CC       and this delays the metaphase/anaphase transition. When the spindle
CC       assembly checkpoint is silenced, the MCC-APC/C complex recruits the E2
CC       enzyme UBCH10 and triggers the auto-ubiquitination of CDC20 in the MCC,
CC       thereby reactivating APC/C for transition into anaphase. In the closed
CC       conformation (C-MAD2) forms a heterotetrameric complex with MAD1L1 at
CC       unattached kinetochores during prometaphase, the complex recruits open
CC       conformation molecules of MAD2L1 (O-MAD2) and then promotes the
CC       conversion of O-MAD2 to C-MAD2. {ECO:0000256|ARBA:ARBA00095931}.
