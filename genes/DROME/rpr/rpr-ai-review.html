Gene Description

rpr encodes Reaper, a short Drosophila IAP antagonist that promotes apoptotic caspase activation by binding DIAP1 through an N-terminal IAP-binding motif, inhibiting DIAP1 survival functions, and stimulating UbcD1-dependent DIAP1 autoubiquitination and degradation. A GH3 helical region mediates phospholipid-dependent mitochondrial outer-membrane association and multimerization with Reaper/Hid/Grim family partners, increasing the efficiency of IAP turnover in developmental, steroid-triggered, and DNA-damage-induced apoptosis contexts.
