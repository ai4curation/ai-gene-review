# Septin5 (Sep5, A1Z780) notes

Accession note: `just fetch-gene DROME Septin5` resolved to Q7KLG8 (same FlyBase gene FBgn0026361, unreviewed); refetched with the expected accession A1Z780, which carries 11 GOA rows.

SEPT6-class septin; retrogene of Sep2 [PMID:24433211 "Sep5, which is the most recently evolved septin gene in Drosophila, is a retrogene copy of Sep2."]

- Redundant with Sep2 in imaginal proliferation [PMID:24433211 "Sep2 Sep5 double mutants have an early pupal lethal phenotype and lack imaginal discs, suggesting that these genes have redundant functions during imaginal cell proliferation"]
- Sep4-Sep5-Pnut complex in wound repair [PMID:38728140 "Sep4 knockdown and Sep5 mutant embryos exhibit delayed actomyosin ring disassembly"]
- GTPase ISS from Pnut is likely over-transfer: Sep2 (closest paralog) lacks activity [PMID:25355953 "Sep2 protein, on the other hand, did not possess any noticeable GTPase activity (Table 1)."] -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED.

## Deep research (falcon) follow-up

The falcon report confirms Sep5 (CG2916) is a Sep2 retrogene with no Sep5-specific biochemical activity measured, consistent with marking the GTPase ISS as over-annotated. It cites a sensory-organ RNAi screen (Bras et al. 2012: Sep5 knockdown causes bristle loss and reduced Notch signalling) that is not in GOA or the cache; no annotations were added.
