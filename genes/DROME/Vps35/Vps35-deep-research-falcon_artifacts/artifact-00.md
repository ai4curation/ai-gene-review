(pqac-00000025, pqac-00000032) | Maruzs et al., 2015; [DOI: 10.1111/tra.12309](https://doi.org/10.1111/tra.12309) |

Presynaptic MVB number and endosome size did not increase. Overall, 71.9% of Mega-positive vesicles were Vps26-positive (128 vesicles from three discs). (pqac-00000033, pqac-00000034) | Pannen et al., 2020; [DOI: 10.7554/eLife.61866](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.61866) |

(pqac-00000008) | Ye et al., 2020; [DOI: 10.7554/eLife.51977](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.51977) |
