## Literature journal

- Type I membrane protein that binds Hh and mediates response; acts at or upstream of Ptc
  [PMID:16630821 "epistasis analysis places ihog action at or upstream of the negatively acting receptor component, Patched (Ptc)"]
- Heparin-dependent Hh-Ihog complex; heparin induces dimerization
  [PMID:17077139 "Here, we show that heparin binding induces Ihog dimerization and is required to mediate high-affinity interactions between Ihog and Hh."]
- Ihog binds Ptc directly; Ihog + Ptc = Hh receptor
  [PMID:20048000 "We demonstrate that Ihog interacts directly with Ptc, is required for presentation of Ptc on the cell surface, and that Ihog and Ptc are both required for high-affinity Hh binding."]
- Redundancy with boi in eye and wing discs
  [PMID:21044292 "cells mutant for both ihog and boi failed to activate responses at either high or low thresholds of Hh signaling"]
- Ihog/Boi dispensable for sequestration without Ptc
  [PMID:25231763 "cells expressing Ptc can retain and sequester the Hh ligand without Ihog and Boi, but that Ihog and Boi cannot do so without Ptc"]
- Trans-homophilic adhesion
  [PMID:31209108 "We found that Ihog proteins are enriched at the site of cell-cell contacts and engage in trans-homophilic interactions in a calcium-independent manner."]
- Cytonemes
  [PMID:28825565 "Furthermore, both the Hh co-receptor Interference hedgehog (Ihog) and the glypicans are critical for this cell-cell cytoneme mediated interaction."]
- Vein binding in an extracellular interactome
  [PMID:23827685 "With the ECIA, we found that Vn also binds two related Hedgehog (Hh) co-receptors, Ihog (interference Hedgehog) and Boi (Brother of Ihog)."]

## Curation decisions

- Core: coreceptor activity (with Ptc) and hedgehog family protein binding; PM + cytoneme.
- protein binding rows -> hedgehog family protein binding (Hh partner) or growth factor binding (Vein).
- Generic terms MODIFY to specific ones the gene already carries (membrane -> plasma membrane,
  identical protein binding -> homodimerization, cell adhesion/cell-cell adhesion -> homotypic cell-cell
  adhesion, organ/system development -> compound eye development).
- Axon guidance IBA (CDO/BOC family node) kept non-core: no fly evidence either way.
- Same conventions applied to boi.
