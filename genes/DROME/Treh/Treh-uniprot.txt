CC   -!- FUNCTION: Enzyme that cleaves trehalose to produce 2 glucose molecules
CC       that can be used by the glycolytic pathway (Probable). Glycolysis is
CC       essential in glial cells but not in neurons; neurons rely on the citric
CC       acid cycle for their energy needs, and on lactate and alanine secreted
CC       into the hemolymph by glial cells to fuel it (PubMed:26235423).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26235423, ECO:0000305|PubMed:26235423}.

CC       Conditional RNAi-mediated knockdown in adult glial cells results in
CC       reduced lifespan due to neurodegeneration (PubMed:26235423).
CC       Conditional RNAi-mediated knockdown in adult neuronal cells has no
CC       effect on lifespan (PubMed:26235423). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26235423}.
