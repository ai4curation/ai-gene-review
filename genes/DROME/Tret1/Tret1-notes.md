# Tret1 (A1Z8N1) notes

- Fly TRET1 is a high-affinity trehalose transporter in Xenopus oocytes [PMID:20035867 "Drosophila melanogaster (10.94 +/- 7.70 mM) showed high affinity"];
  proposed to release trehalose from fat body and import it into tissues [PMID:20035867 "insect TRET1 is responsible for
the release of trehalose from the fat body and the incorporation of trehalose
into other tissues"].
- Tret1-1 in perineurial glia (blood-brain barrier): plasma membrane plus a vesicular pool; Rab7/Rab10 trafficking;
  starvation-induced via TGF-beta [PMID:34032568 "Rab-dependent intracellular
shuttling is needed for Tret1-1 integration into the plasma membrane"]. Also transports glucose
  [PMID:34032568 "Tret1-1PA can facilitate uptake of glucose, while fructose is not taken up efficiently"].
- Decisions: trehalose and glucose transporter activities, plasma membrane accepted; vesicle non-core; generic
  membrane/transport/transporter terms modified to the specific ones already annotated; NOT disaccharide rows accepted.
- Falcon deep research (Tret1-deep-research-falcon.md) is consistent: substrates trehalose and glucose; plasma membrane plus
  vesicles in perineurial glia; Tret1-1-specific CRISPR alleles are pupal lethal over a deficiency and require glial
  expression for rescue; paralog Tret1-2 is distinct and should not be pooled.
