# FlyBase gene description for Tret1 (DROME)

Trehalose transporter 1 (Tret1) encodes a transmembrane protein that mediates the bidirectional transport of trehalose. It exports trehalose synthesized in the fat body into the hemolymph and imports it into other tissues that require a carbon source for energy production. It does not transport maltose, sucrose or lactose.
