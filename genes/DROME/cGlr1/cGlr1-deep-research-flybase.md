# FlyBase gene description for cGlr1 (DROME)

cGAS-like receptor 1 (cGlr1) encodes a receptor for double stranded RNA, a hallmark of viral replication in infected cells. It produces cyclic dinucleotide second messengers that activate the STING pathway.
