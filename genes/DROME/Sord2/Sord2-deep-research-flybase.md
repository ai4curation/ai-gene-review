# FlyBase gene description for Sord2 (DROME)

Sorbitol dehydrogenase 2 (Sord2) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the reversible NAD(+)-dependent oxidation of various sugar alcohols including D-sorbitol and xylitol. It is involved in the biosynthesis of D-fructose from D-glucose and possibly in the catabolism of D-glucuronate to xylulose 5-phosphate.
