# cpb (Capping protein beta; Drosophila melanogaster, P48603) notes

The falcon deep research report (cpb-deep-research-falcon.md) arrived after the wrapper timeout and after these notes were drafted; the review relies primarily on cached publications.

## Identity
- Beta subunit of the heterodimeric barbed-end F-actin capping protein (CapZ beta ortholog); obligate partner of cpa. First CP mutants in a metazoan: [PMID:8682865 "these are the first CP mutations in a multicellular organism, and unlike CP mutations in yeast, they are lethal"]; [PMID:8682865 "Adult files that are heterozygous for a pair of weak alleles have a defect in bristle morphology that is correlated to disorganized actin bundles in developing bristles."]

## Literature
- [PMID:16143599 "The alpha/beta-heterodimer caps the barbed ends of an actin filament and restricts its growth."]; cpb = burned [PMID:16143599 "burned mutations are allelic to mutations in the previously described cpb locus"]; [PMID:16143599 "In its absence, cells progressively accumulate actin filaments and eventually die."]
- Wing disc [PMID:16887822 "cpa or cpb mutant cells in this region, but not in the remainder of the wing disc, are extruded from the epithelium and undergo apoptosis"].
- Subunit co-regulation [PMID:24788460 "Both subunits regulate each other's protein levels."]
- JNK [PMID:23644660 "limits Src-induced apoptosis or tissue overgrowth by restricting JNK activation"]; [PMID:23644660 "CP requires the actin-binding domain of the α-subunit to limit Src64B-induced apoptosis"].
- Lamellipodia [PMID:17331727 "Depletion of capping protein by RNAi results in the displacement of the Arp2/3 complex and disappearance of the lamellipod."]
- Filopodia in neurons [PMID:21464901 "confirm a negative role of capping proteins in filopodia formation"]; cytonemes shortened by Cpa RNAi (PMID:24121526).
- Oogenesis [PMID:19576200 "This revealed important roles in oocyte determination, nurse cell cortical integrity and nurse cell dumping"].
- Z-disc [PMID:32980309 "CapZ, the barbed end actin capping protein, localizes mainly at the Z-disc in wild-type muscles"].
- Dynactin barbed-end cap [PMID:22726940 "the conventional actin capping proteins Capα and Capβ, which are located at the barbed-end of the mini-filament"]; WASH [PMID:20714399 "the invention of the WASH complex has involved the incorporation of an independent complex, the CapZ alpha/beta heterodimer"]; NE budding [PMID:32503943 "we identify Wash, its regulatory complex (SHRC), capping protein and Arp2/3 as new molecular components involved in the physical aspects of NE bud formation"].

## Curation thoughts
- Core: barbed-end capping (BP), actin filament binding (MF), F-actin capping protein complex, actin cytoskeleton.
- "dynein-driven meiotic oscillatory nuclear movement" is the fission-yeast horsetail movement, stated in a dynactin-evolution paper; not applicable to Drosophila -> REMOVE.
- Developmental/signaling rows (oocyte fate, wing disc, JNK, filopodia) non-core.
- Border cells: [PMID:23733343 "Warts phosphorylates and inhibits the actin regulator Ena to activate F-actin Capping protein activity on inner membranes"].
- Lamella in S2 cells [PMID:12975351 "barbed end capping (capping protein)"].
- NMJ (miR-8/Ena) row from PMID:24718988 concerns Ena antagonism of capping proteins; cpb role at NMJ is indirect.
