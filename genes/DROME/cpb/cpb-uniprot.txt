CC   -!- FUNCTION: F-actin-capping proteins bind in a Ca(2+)-independent manner
CC       to the fast growing ends of actin filaments (barbed end) thereby
CC       blocking the exchange of subunits at these ends. Unlike other capping
CC       proteins (such as gelsolin and severin), these proteins do not sever
CC       actin filaments. Forms, with CAPZB, the barbed end of the fast growing
CC       ends of actin filaments in the dynactin complex and stabilizes dynactin
CC       structure. The dynactin multiprotein complex activates the molecular
CC       motor dynein for ultra-processive transport along microtubules (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A9XFX6,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8682865}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the F-actin capping complex, composed of a
CC       heterodimer of an alpha and a beta subunit (By similarity). Subunit of
CC       dynactin, a multiprotein complex part of a tripartite complex with
CC       dynein and a adapter, such as BICDL1, BICD2 or HOOK3 (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:A9XFX6}.
