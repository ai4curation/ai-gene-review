CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the LINC (LInker of Nucleoskeleton and
CC       Cytoskeleton) complex involved in the connection between the nuclear
CC       lamina and the cytoskeleton (By similarity). Is required to nuclear
CC       migration in eye and to anchor klar in the nuclear membrane
CC       (PubMed:18820457). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O94901,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18820457}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Core component of the LINC complex which is composed of inner
CC       nuclear membrane SUN domain-containing proteins coupled to outer
CC       nuclear membrane KASH domain-containing nesprins.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O94901}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Membrane {ECO:0000255}; Multi-pass membrane
CC       protein {ECO:0000255}. Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton, microtubule organizing
CC       center {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32066907}. Cytoplasm, perinuclear region
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18820457, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32066907}. Note=Lam is
CC       required for perinuclear localization of Koi (PubMed:18820457). In the
CC       fat body, localizes to a perinuclear non-centrosomal microtubule-
CC       organizing centers (ncMTOCs) (PubMed:32066907).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18820457, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32066907}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in all cells in the eye disk.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18820457}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Homozygous mutant flies live to adulthood and are
CC       fertile and exhibit rough and irregular eyes (PubMed:18820457). Flies
CC       have an aberrant R-cell rhabdomeres morphology and R-cell and cone cell
CC       nuclei are mispositioned (PubMed:18820457). RNAi-mediated knockdown
CC       produces mild defects in nuclear positioning within fat body cells
CC       (PubMed:32066907). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18820457,
