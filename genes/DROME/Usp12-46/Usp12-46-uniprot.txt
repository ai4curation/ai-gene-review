CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic component of the Usp12-46 deubiquitylase complex
CC       (PubMed:37798281). Deubiquitylates the wg/wingless-signaling receptor
CC       arr/arrow, which stabilizes the receptor and increases its
CC       concentration at the cell surface; this enhances the sensitivity of
CC       cells to wg/wingless-signal stimulation (PubMed:37798281). This
CC       increases the amplitude and spatial range of the signaling response to
CC       the wg/wingless morphogen gradient, facilitating the precise,
CC       concentration-dependent regulation of its target genes
CC       (PubMed:37798281). Required for wg/wingless-mediated signaling in the
CC       wing imaginal disc and for wg/wingless-dependent regulation of adult
CC       intestinal stem cell proliferation (PubMed:37798281). Negative
CC       regulator of Notch signaling, possibly by regulating lysosomal
CC       degradation of N/Notch and affecting cell surface receptor levels; this
CC       may be context and cell-type specific function involved in external
CC       sensory organ development but not in wing imaginal-disc dorsoventral
CC       boundary signaling (Probable) (PubMed:22778262). Protects against
CC       HTT/huntingtin-induced polyglutamine expansion-dependent
CC       neurodegeneration (PubMed:30266909). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22778262,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30266909, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37798281,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:37798281}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Thiol-dependent hydrolysis of ester, thioester, amide, peptide
CC         and isopeptide bonds formed by the C-terminal Gly of ubiquitin (a 76-
CC         residue protein attached to proteins as an intracellular targeting
CC         signal).; EC=3.4.19.12; Evidence={ECO:0000305|PubMed:37798281};
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Catalytic component of the Usp12-46 deubiquitylase complex
CC       consisting of Usp12-46, Wdr20 and Uaf1 (PubMed:37798281). The Usp12-46
CC       deubiquitylase complex associates with arr/arrow; the interaction leads
CC       to deubiquitination and stabilization of arr/arrow (PubMed:37798281).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37798281}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in the larval wing imaginal disc.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37798281}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Viable but sterile with reduced lifespan
CC       (PubMed:37798281). Severe defects in adult intestinal epithelium;
CC       increased number of intestinal stem and progenitor cells in the adult
CC       midgut (PubMed:37798281). Conditional RNAi-mediated knockdown in the
CC       central region of the notum results in notum malformation and
CC       disorganized thoracic bristle socket pattern with socket duplications
CC       or loss of bristles (PubMed:22778262). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22778262,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37798281}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the peptidase C19 family. {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-
