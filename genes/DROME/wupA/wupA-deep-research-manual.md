## Identity and gene structure
- wupA (wings up A; heldup/hdp; CG7178) is the sole TnI gene in Drosophila [PMID:11359941 "The wupA gene is the sole TnI-encoding gene in Drosophila"].
- Maps to the haplolethal region of the Shaker gene complex; encodes many TnI isoforms by alternative/mutually exclusive exons [PMID:1899228 "Members of this family are brought about by two alternative and two mutually exclusive exons in conjunction with two differential polyadenylation sites."].
- 13 transcripts/11 isoforms; haplolethality reflects combined haploinsufficiency of many isoforms [PMID:33739971 "We conclude that the wupA associated HL results from the combined haploinsufficiency of a large set of TnI isoforms."].

## Core muscle function: Ca2+-dependent inhibition of the thin filament
- TnI is part of the troponin complex regulating contraction [PMID:15075240 "TnI is part of the thin filament troponin complex which regulates muscle contraction."].
- Mechanism: competition of TnI and myosin for actin [PMID:21680742 "In the absence of calcium, TnI binds actin more tightly than myosin; in the presence of calcium, the TnI interaction is weakened, and now myosin binds more tightly."].
- hdp2 (A116V) thin filaments: tropomyosin stuck in Ca2+-on position [PMID:14990488 "Thus, cross-bridge cycling would be expected to proceed uninhibited in these fibers, even in relaxing conditions"]; in vitro motility activated at lower Ca2+ [PMID:20658179 "the mutant troponin I caused activation of motility at pCa values higher than wild type"].
- Genetic suppression: myosin-null/headless myosin, Mhc missense (D45), Tm2 and intragenic (near actin-binding domain) suppressors [PMID:1908472; PMID:12750333; PMID:21680742; PMID:11359941; PMID:8589447 "within a heptameric leucine string motif adjacent to the actin binding domain of TnI"].

## Muscle development/maintenance phenotypes (largely secondary)
- hdp3 lacks TnI in IFM/TDT; sarcomeres fail, partially suppressed by headless myosin [PMID:15075240 "indicating the need for inhibition of muscle contraction during muscle development"].
- fliH cis-regulatory allele: reduced TnI and coordinated reduction of other troponin proteins [PMID:25747460].
- heldup needed for maintenance of muscle structure [PMID:410901 "another (heldup) is involved in the maintenance of muscle structure once formed"].

## Heart
- TnI mutant adult flies show impaired systolic function [PMID:16432241 "cardiomyopathic flies that contain a mutation in troponin I or tropomyosin show severe impairment of systolic function"]; hdp2 hearts enlarged, rescued by SERCA overexpression [PMID:24098595].

## Non-muscle roles
- Maternal TnI; nuclear division and polarity defects in embryos; nuclear TnI in S2 cells, sumoylation-dependent [PMID:19567471].
- Proliferation and oncogenic overgrowth; nuclear TnI upregulates InR, Rap1, Dilp8 [PMID:27437768].
- Apical TnI binds Baz and Dlg, controls their localization [PMID:30872455].
- Nuclear isoform K up-regulates CDK2, CDK4, Rap, Rab5 [PMID:33739971].
