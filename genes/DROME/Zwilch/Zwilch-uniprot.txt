CC   -!- FUNCTION: Essential component of the mitotic checkpoint, which prevents
CC       cells from prematurely exiting mitosis (PubMed:15886105,
CC       PubMed:17576797). Required for the assembly of the dynein-dynactin,
CC       Mad2 complexes and spindly/CG15415 onto kinetochores (PubMed:15886105,
CC       PubMed:17576797). Its function related to the spindle assembly
CC       machinery is proposed to depend on its association in the RZZ complex
CC       (PubMed:22685323). Failure to assemble the complex due to the absence
CC       of any one of its components, results in the incorrect redistribution
CC       of the remaining components to diverse membrane compartments
CC       (PubMed:22685323). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12686595,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15886105, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17576797}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the RZZ complex composed of rod, Zw10 and Zwilch.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12686595}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22685323}.
CC       Chromosome, centromere, kinetochore {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12686595,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22685323}. Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton, spindle
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22685323}. Note=Dynamic pattern of localization
CC       during the cell cycle (PubMed:22685323). In most cells at interphase,
CC       present diffusely in the cytoplasm (PubMed:22685323). At metaphase,
CC       detected at the kinetochores and kinetochore microtubules, and in late
CC       anaphase and telophase accumulates at the spindle envelope midzone
CC       (PubMed:12686595, PubMed:22685323). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12686595,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22685323}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Lagging chromosomes at anaphase and precocious
CC       sister chromatid separation upon activation of the spindle checkpoint
CC       (PubMed:12686595). In spermatocytes the number of Golgi structures are
CC       not affected but they show a mild defect in shape (PubMed:22685323).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12686595, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22685323}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the ZWILCH family. {ECO:0000305}.
