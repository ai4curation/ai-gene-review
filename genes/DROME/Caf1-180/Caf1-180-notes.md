# Caf1-180 notes

## Identity
- UniProt Q9W3D1 (unreviewed), CG12109. CAF-1 large subunit (CHAF1A/Cac1 ortholog).

## Function
- CAF-1 deposits H3-H4 on newly synthesized DNA [PMID:19066929 "This complex promotes histone H3-H4 deposition onto newly synthesised DNA in vitro"].
- Recombinant p180+p105+p55 active [PMID:11533245 "The purified recombinant p180+p105+p55 dCAF-1 complex is active for DNA replication-coupled chromatin assembly"].
- Essential gene; silencing and repair phenotypes [PMID:17916346 "dCAF-1-p180 mutant flies are hemizygous lethal"]; [PMID:17916346 "reduction of dCAF-1-p180 activity suppresses gene silencing at heterochromatin and antagonizes Polycomb-mediated cell fate determination"]; [PMID:17916346 "heterozygous dCAF-1-p180 mutant flies display an increased sensitivity to gamma-irradiation and a reduced efficiency in recombinational double strand break (DSB) repair"].
- Human p150 rescues [PMID:17916346 "human hCAF-1-p150 can rescue the dCAF-1-p180 mutant flies"].
- Endocycles [PMID:19066929 "perturbation of nucleosomal organisation, reduction of the replication efficiency of euchromatic DNA and accumulation of DNA damage"].
- Deep research (falcon) summarizes later work: P180 binds HP1a via a C-terminal HP1-interacting motif whose deletion separates viability (rescued) from PEV/heterochromatin functions; P180 is enriched on replicating polytene chromosomes overlapping PCNA.
