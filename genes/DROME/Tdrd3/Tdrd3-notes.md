# Tdrd3 (CG13472, dTDRD3) review notes

## Literature journal
- Complex with Top3beta: [PMID:30470739 "Both antibodies (TDRD3-A and C) isolated two major polypeptides with about equal molar ratio, which were identified as TDRD3 and Top3β by mass spectrometry (MS)"]
- Scaffold: [PMID:30470739 "suggest that Drosophila TDRD3 acts as a scaffold linking Top3β to FMRP and p68"]
- Nucleic acid binding and stimulation of Top3beta: [PMID:27582462 "Whereas TDRD3 has a structure-specific binding preference, it does not discriminate between DNA and RNA."]; [PMID:27582462 "TDRD3 stimulates the relaxation activity of Top3β on hypernegatively supercoiled DNA and changes the reaction from a distributive to a processive mode."]
- Localization (deep research, Lee et al. 2025 Cell Rep): [file:DROME/Tdrd3/Tdrd3-deep-research-falcon.md "Its demonstrated sites of action include the cytoplasm of cultured fly cells and, in ovarian germ cells, the cytoplasm and perinuclear"] nuage.
- No splicing evidence: [file:DROME/Tdrd3/Tdrd3-deep-research-falcon.md "No direct fly experiment located demonstrates a spliceosomal function or stress-granule localization for CG13472/Q9VUH8."]

## Curation decisions
- Core: adaptor/scaffold (GO:0030674) of the Top3beta-TDRD3 complex; ss nucleic acid binding accepted.
- mRNA processing, Cajal body and nuclear speck (IEA, family/human-derived) marked over-annotated.
- Nucleus, histone H4 reader (IBA) and siRNA heterochromatin formation kept as non-core.
- Not curated: Lee et al. 2025 nuage/piRNA study (not in GOA or cache).
