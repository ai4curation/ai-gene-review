CC       development (PubMed:9199347). In spermatocytes detected at the spindle
CC       envelope, spindle poles and astral membrane throughout meiosis I,
CC       whereas mostly depleted in meiosis II (PubMed:27402967). Colocalizes
CC       with nuclear pore complex component Nup107 throughout meiosis I
CC       (PubMed:27402967). Cytoplasmic lamin is more soluble than nuclear lamin
CC       (PubMed:2492999). Oocytes also possess an intranuclear pool not
CC       associated with the lamina (PubMed:2492999). A cytoplasmic pool is
CC       detectable surrounding the oocyte nucleus and nuclei of embryos during
CC       the first 3 hours of embryogenesis (PubMed:21989239, PubMed:2492999).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16439308, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21989239,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2492999, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27402967,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7593280, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9199347}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Constitutively expressed in all tissues and cells
CC       (at protein level) (PubMed:7593280, PubMed:8537453). Expressed in
CC       spermatocytes but not in mature sperm (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:27402967). Expressed in the nuclei of nurse cells, follicle
CC       cells and oocyte during oogenesis (PubMed:2492999).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2492999, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27402967,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7593280, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8537453}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed both maternally and zygotically during
CC       embryogenesis (at protein level) (PubMed:15996653, PubMed:21989239).
CC       Constitutively expressed in all stages of embryogenesis, especially
CC       during the first 6-9 hours (at protein level) (PubMed:21989239,
CC       PubMed:7593280). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15996653,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21989239, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7593280}.
CC   -!- PTM: Three forms of lamin have been identified in D.melanogaster, lamin
CC       Dm0 is rapidly processed to lamin Dm1 in the cytoplasm, Dm1 is then
CC       assembled in the nuclear envelope and is phosphorylated, forming lamin
CC       Dm2 (PubMed:18327897, PubMed:3126192, PubMed:8537453). Cells undergoing
CC       mitosis or meiosis, or trapped in mitosis by drugs, possess an
CC       alternatively phosphorylated form called Dm(mit); this form is more
CC       soluble, correlating with nuclear disassembly (PubMed:2492999).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18327897, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2492999,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:3126192, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8537453}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylation by CDK1 on Ser-42 and Ser-45 or by PKA on Ser-50
CC       interferes with head-to-tail oligomerization and stimulates lamin
CC       disassembly (PubMed:9013881). During interphase can be phosphorylated
CC       on Ser-25, Thr-435 and Ser-595 (PubMed:10194384, PubMed:12378269).
CC       During mitosis the soluble form known as Dm(mit) is phosphorylated on
CC       Ser-45; a subset of molecules may also be phosphorylated on Ser-25
CC       (PubMed:10194384). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10194384,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12378269, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9013881}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Late pupal lethal (PubMed:15996653). After
CC       puparium formation development is delayed and ceases at the pharate
CC       adult stage (PubMed:15996653). Abnormal organogenesis at later stages
CC       of development, including retarded growth of gonad and central nervous
CC       system (PubMed:15996653). Ventriculus hypertrophy with increased cell
CC       proliferation but normal tissue organization (PubMed:15996653).
CC       Defective eye development with photoreceptor nuclei that fail to
CC       migrate apically as they are specified, resulting in aberrantly shaped
CC       photoreceptors in adult eyes (PubMed:14617811, PubMed:15996653). Some
CC       early knockout alleles result in embryonic lethality with terminal
CC       branches of the tracheal system following abnormal projection patterns
CC       during branch outgrowth (PubMed:11533666). RNAi-mediated knockdown in
CC       embryos arrests development at stage 10 of embryogenesis; cell nuclei
CC       are easily damaged and nuclear pore complexes form aggregates
CC       (PubMed:15054108). RNAi-mediated knockdown in spermatocytes results in
CC       spindle envelope disintegration, failure to form contractile rings and
CC       central spindle microtubules during meiosis I resulting in abnormal
CC       cytokinesis and multinuclear cell formation (PubMed:27402967).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11533666, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14617811,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15054108, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15996653,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27402967}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Named 'misguided' due to the abnormal projection
CC       patterns of terminal branches during tracheal system development
CC       observed in mutant flies. {ECO:0000305|PubMed:11533666}.
