# FlyBase gene description for G6pd (DROME)

Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase (G6pd) encodes a cytosolic enzyme that catalyzes the oxidation of glucose 6-phosphate to 6-phosphogluconate with the concomitant reduction of NADP+ to NADPH in the oxidative branch of the pentose phosphate pathway.
