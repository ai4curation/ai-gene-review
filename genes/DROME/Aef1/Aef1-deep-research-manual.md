# Aef1 (P39413, CG5683, FBgn0005694) - manual literature review

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled manually from cached
publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and a PubMed search
(`(AEF-1 OR Aef1 OR "adult enhancer factor") AND Drosophila`, 10 hits).

## Identity and structure
- 308 aa; N-terminal Q/A-rich low-complexity region, four C-terminal C2H2 zinc fingers
  (UniProt FT ZN_FING 184-290). PANTHER PTHR23235:SF142.
- "The AEF-1 protein contains four zinc fingers and an alanine-rich sequence." [PMID:1508206]

## DNA binding and repression (classical studies)
- Binds a negative element in the Adh adult enhancer (AAE): "We have identified a protein in
  Drosophila nuclear extracts that binds specifically to a site within the AAE" [PMID:1508206].
- In vivo: "Base substitutions in the AEF-1 binding site that disrupt AEF-1 binding in vitro
  result in a significant increase in the level of Adh expression in vivo." [PMID:1508206]
- Repressor in cotransfection [PMID:1508206].
- Yolk protein fat body enhancer: "The aef1 site represses ectopic transcription in ovaries and
  dsxA antirepresses this activity in fat bodies." [PMID:7851798]
- Adh promoter switch: "this Adh promoter switch is regulated by a zinc finger repressor protein
  (AEF-1)" targeting the proximal promoter initiator [PMID:9463385].
- Fbp1 EcRU: GATAb antagonizes "a ubiquitous repressor ... We identified this repressor as
  AEF-1" [PMID:11493575].
- Heterologous rat ADH promoter: "AEF-1 inhibits induction of the rat pADH by C/EBP alpha"
  [PMID:7959011].

## Genome-wide studies (S2 cells)
- Interacts with SAGA DUB module; binds promoters of active genes co-occupied by SAGA,
  SWI/SNF, ORC [PMID:39709566, abstract only, Russian].
- Non-promoter sites are nucleosome-depleted, CBP/H3K27Ac-marked putative enhancers; motif
  "(CAA)n"; RNAi alters "342 genes" [PMID:40771843, full text]. Direction not reported.

## Phenotype
- Gene trap BG01171 (BDGP-mapped to an Aef1 exon) shows olfactory neuron expression and reduced
  olfactory trap response, but "BG00973 and BG01171 have two" insertions and no excision
  rescue was done [PMID:22530061, full text]. Weak evidence for Aef1-specific olfactory role.

## Not found
- No structural, biochemical affinity, or null mutant studies. No FlyBase experimental GO rows
  in GOA (only IBA/IEA).
