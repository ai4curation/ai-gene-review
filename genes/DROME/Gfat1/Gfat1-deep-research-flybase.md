# FlyBase gene description for Gfat1 (DROME)

Glutamine:fructose-6-phosphate aminotransferase 1 (Gfat1) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the transamination of fructose 6-phosphate to D-glucosamine 6-phosphate. It is involved in the biosynthesis of UDP-N-acetylglucosamine, a precursor for chitin biosynthesis and protein glycosylation.
