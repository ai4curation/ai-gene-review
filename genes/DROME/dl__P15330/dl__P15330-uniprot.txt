CC   -!- FUNCTION: Embryonic developmental transcription factor
CC       (PubMed:10072776, PubMed:2598266, PubMed:30982648). The lateral or
CC       ventral identity of a cell depends upon the concentration of this
CC       protein in its nucleus during the blastoderm stage (PubMed:2598266).
CC       Acts as a morphogenetic transcription factor that specifically binds to
CC       the kappa-B-related consensus sequence 5'-GRGAAAANCC-3', located in the
CC       enhancer region of zygotic genes such as Zen, Twist, Snail and
CC       Decapentaplegic, promoting their expression (PubMed:10072776,
CC       PubMed:2598266, PubMed:30982648). Part of a signaling pathway involving
CC       NF-kappa-B and Toll-related receptors, that functions in the apoptosis
CC       of unfit cells during cell competition (PubMed:25477468). Mediates an
CC       immune response in larvae (PubMed:10072776). May be part of a NF-kappa-
CC       B and Tollo signaling cascade that regulates development of the
CC       peripheral nervous system (PubMed:18000549).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10072776, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18000549,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25477468, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2598266,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30982648}.
