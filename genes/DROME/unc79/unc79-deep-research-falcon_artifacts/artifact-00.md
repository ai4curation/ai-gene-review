| **Channel scaffold/partner and NA stability** | NA, UNC79 and UNC80 co-immunoprecipitate from adult-head membrane extracts. Loss of any component lowers the other proteins with comparatively little transcript change, while excess NA or UNC80 cannot replace UNC79 function (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000020). Developmentally produced NA-complex proteins, including UNC79 measured in head extracts, persist with little decay for at least **5–7 adult days** (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000021). | Human cryo-EM resolves an approximately **1-MDa** channelosome in which UNC79 contains **32 HEAT repeats** and intertwines with UNC80 as an intracellular superhelical scaffold contacting NALCN linkers (pqac-00000029, pqac-00000032). | Fly co-immunoprecipitation shows association, not which contacts are direct. | **Functional compartment** | *unc79* knockdown and rescue establish function in neurons, including **PDF-positive circadian pacemaker neurons**; recovery with membrane fractions places UNC79 in a membrane-associated complex (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000027). | Human structures place UNC79 on the **cytoplasmic face of the plasma-membrane NALCN complex**, beneath the channel’s voltage-sensor domains (pqac-00000029, pqac-00000030). | Cytoplasmic-face placement in fly is a strong orthology-based inference, not direct microscopy. Endogenous distribution among soma, axon, dendrite, synapse and intracellular membranes remains unresolved. |
| **Ionic selectivity and catalysis** | No enzymatic reaction or transport substrate is attributable to UNC79 itself.
