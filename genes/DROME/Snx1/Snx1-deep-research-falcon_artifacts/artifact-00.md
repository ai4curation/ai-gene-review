| Claim | Status for fly Snx1 | Evidence and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and family | **Verified** | *Drosophila melanogaster* **Snx1/Dsnx1 = CG2774**, the fly homolog of yeast Vps5 and mammalian SNX1/2. This agrees with Q9VQQ6's sorting-nexin-family assignment and PX, BAR/AH-BAR, and Vps5_C annotations. Zhang et al., 2011, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/cr.2011.167). (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004) |
| Endosomal PX–BAR membrane scaffold | **Strong family/structure-based inference; not directly demonstrated for fly Snx1** | Conserved SNX1/2-type proteins use PX/BAR architecture for phosphoinositide-dependent endosomal association, curvature sensing, and membrane tubulation; SNX1/2 partner with SNX5/6 in ESCPE-1-like coats. The mechanistic evidence is principally from yeast or mammalian proteins, not Q9VQQ6 itself. Simonetti et al., 2019, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-019-0393-3); Rowlands & Moore, 2024, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2022.0384). (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000016) |
| Null viability, fertility, and Wingless secretion | **Directly observed negative phenotype** | A likely null allele deleting 1,566 bp—from 130 bp upstream of the start codon into exon 3—was viable and fertile, with no detectable Wingless-signaling or secretion defect in the tested wing-disc assays. DSNX1 overexpression also failed to rescue loss of Dsnx3. Zhang et al., 2011, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/cr.2011.167). (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005, pqac-00000027) |
| Axon-midline guidance with Snx6 | **Direct genetic evidence; mechanism preliminary** | Zygotic loss of either gene alone caused no dramatic coarse axon phenotype, potentially because of maternal product. In sensitized *slit/robo* and FraΔC assays, reducing *snx1* and *snx6* dosage modified abnormal midline crossing, with combined reduction producing stronger effects. This supports shared or interacting functions but does not prove a physical Snx1–Snx6 complex in flies. Simonetti et al., 2019, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-019-0393-3). (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000007, pqac-00000008) |
| Direct fly localization, lipid specificity, physical Snx6 pairing, and exact cargo | **Unresolved** | The retrieved primary literature did not directly establish Q9VQQ6 colocalization with Rab5/Rab7 endosomes or Golgi, its phosphoinositide specificity, biochemical binding to fly Snx6, or a transmembrane cargo transported by fly Snx1. Endosomal localization and ESCPE-1 pairing therefore remain conserved-mechanism inferences rather than fly-specific demonstrations. (pqac-00000010) |
| Wntless/Wingless recycling | **Do not assign to Snx1 as its established primary cargo pathway** | In flies, Wntless recycling and Wingless secretion are directly attributed to **Snx3–retromer**; *snx1* mutants lacked the corresponding defect. Recent review synthesis allows possible context-dependent SNX-BAR compensation but does not overturn the direct fly result. Zhang et al., 2011, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/cr.2011.167); Wolf & Boutros, 2023, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.201352). (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000020) |
| Lysosome/autophagy phenotype | **Not directly attributable to Snx1** | Fly retromer-core loss impairs late endocytic and autolysosomal degradation, while the tested sorting-nexin requirements implicated **Snx3 and Snx6**, not Snx1. Those findings support the biological importance of related endosomal sorting machinery but are not evidence of a Q9VQQ6-specific lysosomal function. Maruzs et al., 2015, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/tra.12309). (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003) |


*Table: Evidence is separated into direct fly observations, conserved mechanistic inference, and unresolved claims. This prevents Snx3-, Snx6-, or mammalian SNX1 findings from being misassigned to Q9VQQ6.*
