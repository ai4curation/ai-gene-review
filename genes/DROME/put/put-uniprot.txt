CC   -!- FUNCTION: Type II transmembrane receptor serine/threonine kinase
CC       involved in activin-like TGF-beta signaling and dpp/BMP-like signaling
CC       pathways (PubMed:19766717, PubMed:22563507, PubMed:7697719,
CC       PubMed:7697720, PubMed:8289834, PubMed:8415726). Forms a complex with
CC       type I transmembrane receptor serine/threonine kinases, and upon
CC       activation by signaling ligands induces the type I receptor to
CC       phosphorylate downstream receptor-regulated SMAD (R-SMAD) transcription
CC       factors, inducing their nuclear localization and resulting in
CC       regulation of ligand-responsive genes (PubMed:17119022,
CC       PubMed:18820452, PubMed:7697720, PubMed:8289834). Receptor for the
CC       activin-like TGF-beta ligands daw and Actbeta/activin when in complex
CC       with the type I receptor babo (PubMed:10320478, PubMed:17119022,
CC       PubMed:18820452, PubMed:8289834). Activation by daw requires babo
CC       isoform C (PubMed:19766717). Receptor for the BMP-like ligand dpp when
CC       associated with the type I receptors tkv or sax (PubMed:7697719,
CC       PubMed:7697720). Activation by activin-like TGF-beta ligands, including
CC       daw and Actbeta/activin, results in phosphorylation of the R-SMAD
CC       Smox/SMAD2 (PubMed:10320478, PubMed:17119022, PubMed:18820452,
CC       PubMed:19766717, PubMed:8289834). Activation by BMP-like ligands,
CC       including gbb and dpp, results in phosphorylation of the R-SMAD Mad
CC       (PubMed:18820452, PubMed:19766717). Involved in dpp-signaling-dependent
CC       dorsoventral patterning of the epidermis during embryogenesis
CC       (PubMed:7697719, PubMed:7697720, PubMed:9504926). Involved in axonal
CC       and dendritic pruning and neural remodeling of mushroom body gamma
CC       neurons during metamorphosis, probably through regulating expression of
CC       the ecdysteroid receptor EcR; functions redundantly with wit
CC       (PubMed:12581521). Involved in dpp/BMP-like signaling during
CC       development of the embryonic gut tracheal system (PubMed:7697719,
CC       PubMed:7697720). Required for dpp/BMP-like signaling involved in
CC       imaginal disc development (PubMed:9504926). Involved in differentiation
CC       of dorsal cluster and ellipsoid body adult-specific neurons prior to
CC       metamorphosis (PubMed:16437159). Required in motoneurons for axon
CC       guidance or outgrowth of ISNb and SNa axons (PubMed:17119022). Required
CC       in somatic cyst cells for regulation of germ cell proliferation in
CC       testis (PubMed:9334286). Involved in adult wing development and wing-
CC       vein formation (PubMed:7697720). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10320478,

CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed maternally during early stages of
CC       development and zygotically throughout development at varying levels
CC       (PubMed:8415726). Highly expressed in nurse cells and the developing
CC       oocyte during oogenesis (PubMed:8415726). High levels of expression
CC       during early stages of embryogenesis which drops sharply during
CC       cellularization and adopts an asymmetric distribution, accumulating at
CC       the posterior end underneath the developing pole cells
CC       (PubMed:8415726). Expressed throughout the embryo during gastrulation
CC       but particularly in the invaginating mesoderm encompassing the entire
CC       length of the germband during extension (PubMed:8415726). Expressed
CC       throughout the developing gut peaking at stage 13/14 of embryogenesis
CC       (PubMed:8415726). After dorsal closure at stage 16 expression is
CC       limited to the hindgut and anterior end of the midgut (PubMed:8415726).
CC       Expressed in all imaginal disc cells of 3rd instar larvae
CC       (PubMed:8415726). In adults highly expressed in females
CC       (PubMed:8415726). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8415726}.
