[PMID:25291190](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25291190/) is cached with full text and establishes gene-specific nuclear localization, neuronal phenotypes and cholinergic-cell requirements. Its full-length rescue construct is identified as CG2052-PA; the selected B form retains the zinc-finger machinery, supporting broad functional transfer but not identical regulation at every target.

The actual family relationship to Rotund/Squeeze and ZNF384 is stated in the primary Dati paper. [PMID:11867614](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11867614/) directly maps Nmp4 DNA-binding fingers and demonstrates context-dependent transcriptional activation. This supports broad cis-regulatory DNA binding as an inference, not target promoter identity or activation direction in Dati. QuickGO definitions for GO:0003700, GO:0000978 and GO:0001228 were inspected. The binding prediction is CNN, the activating activity UNC. Six original GOA rows are reviewed.

## 2026-10-20 — Full re-review, independent of the ProtNLM2 predictions

**Identity.** Q9V4C9 is the unreviewed UniProt entry for dati isoform B (1140 aa). It is one of four unreviewed products of FBgn0262636, and no Swiss-Prot entry exists. The comparison is in `dati-bioinformatics/compare_isoforms_rereview.py` and `RESULTS-rereview.md`. Q9V4C9 is identical to Q7KQN5 (isoforms A/C, 1150 aa) apart from a 10-residue segment (KELQLHLGVL) that B lacks after residue 552, inside finger 4. In B, finger 4 has canonical C-x2-C-x12-H-x4-H spacing. All six C2H2 fingers (motifs starting at 441, 469, 497, 533, 566 and 657) are present. Isoform B is therefore a full-length product of the gene, not a fragment, and the gene-level genetics in PMID:25291190 apply to it. Isoform D (H9XVN7, 791 aa) lacks most of the N-terminal region but keeps the finger array. GH06573p (Q7K3X3) starts at residue 54.

**Evolutionary grounding.** UniProt places B and A/C in PANTHER PTHR16515:SF49. That subfamily is a heterogeneous mix (CTCF, MAZ, glass, hamlet, E4F1, ZSCAN5), and it carries no PAINT/IBA rows for dati. GH06573p is placed in SF66 with Squeeze, and isoform D in a different family (PTHR24393), although the finger arrays are identical. The subfamily boundary is unstable for this gene. The primary paper's phylogeny says true dati orthologs exist only in arthropods, and that Rotund, not dati, is the ortholog of C. elegans LIN-29. ZNF384 is in PTHR23234, a different PANTHER family. **Change:** the first-pass core function cited Nmp4/ZNF384 finger-mapping data (PMID:11867614) as support for DATI DNA binding. That transfer is not justified phylogenetically, so it was removed. The reference is kept, with reference_review MISCITED and "no longer used". The finger array is closest to Squeeze (52% identity), then Rotund (49%) and ZNF384 (45%).

**Annotation actions.** Nucleus ACCEPT (immunostaining with a nuclear reporter). Generation of neurons and neuron projection development ACCEPT as core developmental outputs. For neuron projection development, a more direct quote now supports it: the antennal-lobe-to-lateral-horn cholinergic projections are affected. Adult locomotory behavior, mushroom body development and positive regulation of female receptivity are KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
