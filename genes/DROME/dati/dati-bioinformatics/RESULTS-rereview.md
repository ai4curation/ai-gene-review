## Interpretation (2026-10-20 re-review)

Generated by `compare_isoforms_rereview.py` (UniProt REST, fetched 2026-10-20). The tables above are the script output; the text below is the reviewer's reading of them.

- **Identity of the benchmark accession.** Q9V4C9 (isoform B, 1140 aa) is one of several unreviewed UniProt products of FlyBase gene FBgn0262636; no Swiss-Prot entry exists for dati. It is identical to Q7KQN5 (isoforms A/C, 1150 aa) except that it lacks a 10-residue segment (KELQLHLGVL) after residue 552, inside the fourth C2H2 finger. In isoform B that finger has canonical C-x2-C-x12-H-x4-H spacing; in A/C the insertion lengthens the H-H spacing. All six C2H2 motifs and the flanking sequence are otherwise identical, so Q9V4C9 is a full-length product of the gene and not a fragment. Isoform D (H9XVN7, 791 aa) lacks residues 6-138 and 143-368 of isoform B but keeps the full finger array. GH06573p (Q7K3X3) is a cDNA product that starts at residue 54 and carries the A/C insertion.
- **PANTHER placement is inconsistent across dati products.** B and A/C map to PTHR16515:SF49, GH06573p maps to PTHR16515:SF66 (the subfamily of Squeeze), and isoform D maps to a different family (PTHR24393). Because the finger arrays are identical, this split reflects HMM scoring of the flanking sequence, not real divergence. SF49 itself holds unrelated characterised proteins (CTCF, MAZ, glass, hamlet, E4F1, ZSCAN5), so it says nothing specific about dati function.
- **Relatives.** The dati finger array is most identical to Squeeze (52%, same PANTHER family PTHR16515), then Rotund (49%, PTHR14003) and ZNF384 (45%, PTHR23234). The 2014 primary study's phylogeny says that dati orthologs are restricted to arthropods, and that Rotund, not dati, is the ortholog of LIN-29. None of these relatives is an ortholog. The first-pass phmmer donor ZNF208 (PTHR16515:SF51) is a KRAB-ZNF and the most distant of the four.
