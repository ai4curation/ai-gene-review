# Gfat1 (Q7PLC7, Gfat1-PA) review notes

## 2026-10-21 — Full re-review, independent of the ProtNLM2 predictions

**Scope and identity.** Q7PLC7 is the 694-aa Gfat1-PA polypeptide of *Drosophila melanogaster* Gfat1 (FBgn0287209; CG12449; synonym zeppelin/zep), an unreviewed TrEMBL entry with PE 1. The folder is `Gfat1__Q7PLC7` because `genes/DROME/Gfat1` holds the review of the 434-aa C-terminal PF isoform (A8Y5A1). That review's sequence comparison (file:DROME/Gfat1/Gfat1-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md) shows A8Y5A1 is an exact suffix of Q7PLC7; Q7PLC7 is the full-length two-domain enzyme. Its sequence starts `MCGIFAY...`, so the N-terminal Cys2 nucleophile of the glutamine amidotransferase type-2 domain (UniProt ACT_SITE 2, PROSITE-ProRule PRU00609) is retained. Two SIS (isomerase) domains are at 372..512 and 543..684. ProtNLM2 files were not opened.

**Deep research.** `just deep-research DROME Gfat1__Q7PLC7 --provider falcon --fallback perplexity-lite` failed: falcon "Failed to authenticate", perplexity HTTP 401 (insufficient quota). No deep-research file was produced for this folder. The review uses the three cached primary papers that GOA cites, plus the gene's UniProt record and the PANTHER/PAINT files.

**Evolutionary placement.** UniProt puts Q7PLC7 in PTHR10937:SF0 (with E. coli GlmS P17169, yeast GFA1 P14742 and fly Gfat2 Q9VAW3, per interpro/panther/PTHR10937/PTHR10937-entries.csv). Human GFPT1 (SF12) and GFPT2 (SF10) are in other subfamilies of the same family. PAINT places the IBD for GO:0004360 and GO:0006048 at PTN000101302, the root of the eukaryote+bacteria GFAT clade. Fly Gfat1 (FB:FBgn0287209) is a seed for GO:0004360 but not for GO:0006048, which was seeded by Gfat2, human and E. coli. The IBA rows on Q7PLC7 are therefore consistent with the gene's own experimental data. PAINT placed GO:0006002 (fructose 6-phosphate metabolic process) only at a deuterostome node (PTN000859281, taxon 33511), so it was not propagated to fly. The fly TAS for that term is a separate FlyBase assertion.

**Experimental evidence in this species.**
- Graack et al. 2001 [PMID:11716769 "Expressing Dmel/GFAT1 in yeast we showed that Dmel/GFAT1 activity is controlled by UDP-N-acetylglucosamine and PKA in the yeast total protein extract system."]: the cDNA was cloned and expressed in yeast, and the activity was measured with feedback inhibition by UDP-GlcNAc and PKA-dependent regulation. This is the FlyBase IDA for GO:0004360. Only the abstract is cached. mRNA is found in "embryonic chitin-synthesizing tissues and in the corpus cells of salivary glands".
- Chen et al. 2020 [PMID:32615907]: CRISPR null alleles of gfat1 and gfat2 are each lethal ("Deletions for either enzyme were fully lethal"). Dietary GlcN6P rescues gfat2 but "glucosamine-6-phosphate did not rescue gfat1-/- animals". FlyBase draws IMP (GO:0004360, UDP-GlcNAc metabolic process) and IDA (cytoplasm, cytosol) from this paper; only the abstract is cached, so the localisation data cannot be checked here. These annotations are accepted on the basis of the curators' reading of the full text.
- Cotsworth et al. 2022 [PMID:35159258, full text]: zep is Gfat1. Lethal zep alleles include nonsense and isomerase-domain missense lesions (A414T, L588M, C656Y), and homozygotes show the "blimp" expanded-cuticle phenotype. Ubiquitous UAS-Gfat1+ (RA cDNA) rescues zep, and Gfat1 and Gfat2 cross-rescue each other ("these data suggest that the two Gfat enzymes are functionally equivalent"). RNAi shows Gfat1 is required in the trachea but not in the nervous system or muscle. The cuticle and tracheal phenotypes are downstream of HBP/chitin supply. They are not process participation and are not proposed as NEW terms (chitin synthesis is carried out by chitin synthase; Gfat1 supplies the precursor several steps upstream).

**Decisions.**
- GO:0004360 (IBA, IDA, IEA, IMP): ACCEPT, core. This is the experimentally established activity for the fly protein.
- GO:0006048 UDP-GlcNAc biosynthetic process (IBA, IEA, ISS from Gfat2): ACCEPT, core. GFAT catalyses the first committed step, so the protein does part of the work of the pathway. The fly GO-CAM 663d668500001806 models Gfat1 this way (GO:0004360 part_of GO:0006048, occurs_in cytosol).
- GO:0006047 (IMP; QuickGO lists it as obsolete, OLS4 does not): MODIFY to GO:0006048. Per OLS ancestors, GO:0006048 is_a GO:0006047.
- Cytosol IDA: ACCEPT. Cytoplasm IDA: MODIFY to cytosol, which comes from the same paper.
- Carbohydrate metabolic process (UniRule IEA): MODIFY to GO:0006048 (lateral). OLS shows that neither GO:0006048 nor GO:0006002 is under GO:0005975; sugar phosphates fall in the carbohydrate-derivative branch.
- Carbohydrate derivative metabolic/biosynthetic process (InterPro IEA): MODIFY to GO:0006048, which is a descendant of both.
- Carbohydrate binding (UniRule IEA): MODIFY to GO:0097367 (lateral correction, as in the Gfat1-PF review).
- Carbohydrate derivative binding (InterPro IEA): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. The SIS domains bind sugar phosphates (substrate F6P, product GlcN6P), and Graack 2001 shows UDP-GlcNAc feedback regulation. Ligand-specific binding has not been measured for the fly protein, and substrate binding is already implied by the catalytic term. GO:0070095 fructose-6-phosphate binding (is_a GO:0097367) was considered and not adopted because there are no fly binding data.
- L-glutamine metabolic process (UniRule IEA): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. Glutamine is the amide donor and is hydrolysed to glutamate by the GATase domain (Cys2).
- Fructose 6-phosphate metabolic process (TAS, PMID:35159258): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. True, because the enzyme consumes F6P, but peripheral to the core biosynthetic role.

**What is established in fly versus inferred.** Established for fly Gfat1: GFAT enzymatic activity (heterologous expression), UDP-GlcNAc feedback sensitivity, PKA-dependent regulation in extracts, cytosolic localisation (FlyBase IDA), essentiality, a zygotic requirement for embryonic cuticle and tracheal function, and functional interchangeability with Gfat2 in rescue assays. Inferred (phylogeny and domain architecture): the Cys2-dependent glutaminase mechanism, the SIS-domain isomerisation, and the homodimeric/tetrameric assembly. These are not tested in fly. The failure of GlcN6P to rescue gfat1 nulls, while it does rescue gfat2, is unexplained (possibly uptake or tissue access, or a role beyond flux). This is recorded as a question and is not used to argue against the core activity.
