# AMPKalpha (SNF1A; CG3051; UniProt O18645) notes

- Single catalytic alpha subunit of fly AMPK; activated by AMP and ATP depletion, Thr184 phosphorylation
  [PMID:12093363 "The Drosophila kinase (DmAMPK) was activated by AMP in cell-free assays (albeit to a smaller extent than mammalian AMPK), and by stresses that deplete ATP (oligomycin and hypoxia), as well as by carbohydrate deprivation, in intact cells."]
- Heterotrimer: RNAi of alpha, beta or gamma ablates activity [PMID:12093363 "Expression of double stranded (ds) RNAs targeted at any of the putative alpha, beta or gamma subunits ablated this activity"].
- Active phospho-AMPKalpha nuclear in stressed cells [PMID:12093363 "the phosphorylated DmAMPK alpha subunit was mainly detected in the nucleus"].
- Substrates: Gigas/TSC2 Ser1338 [PMID:25826530 "phosphorylated by AMPK in vitro"]; MRLC in visceral muscle [PMID:20478298 "AMPK regulates visceral smooth muscle function through phosphorylation of MRLC"]; Atg1 (Bombyx, conserved sites) [PMID:36493962].
