# FlyBase gene description for AMPKalpha (DROME)

AMP-activated protein kinase &agr; subunit (AMPK&agr;) encodes the &agr; subunit of the AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) complex. In general AMPK helps cells conserve energy through inhibition of energy consuming processes (fatty acid synthesis, protein synthesis). AMPK is usually active when the TOR pathway is inhibited.
