CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the MRN complex, which plays a central role in
CC       double-strand break (DSB) repair, DNA recombination, maintenance of
CC       telomere integrity and meiosis (PubMed:15135728, PubMed:15296753). The
CC       MRN complex is involved in the repair of DNA double-strand breaks
CC       (DSBs) via homologous recombination (HR), an error-free mechanism which
CC       primarily occurs during S and G2 phases (By similarity). The complex
CC       (1) mediates the end resection of damaged DNA, which generates proper
CC       single-stranded DNA, a key initial steps in HR, and is (2) required for
CC       the recruitment of other repair factors and efficient activation of ATM
CC       and ATR upon DNA damage (By similarity). The MRN complex possesses
CC       single-strand endonuclease activity and double-strand-specific 3'-5'
CC       exonuclease activity, which are provided by MRE11, to initiate end
CC       resection, which is required for single-strand invasion and
CC       recombination (By similarity). Within the complex, RAD50 is both
CC       required to bind DNA ends and hold them in close proximity and regulate
CC       the activity of MRE11 (By similarity). RAD50 provides an ATP-dependent
CC       control of MRE11 by positioning DNA ends into the MRE11 active site:
CC       ATP-binding induces a large structural change from an open form with
CC       accessible MRE11 nuclease sites into a closed form (By similarity).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:G0SHW7, ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q92878,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15135728, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15296753}.
