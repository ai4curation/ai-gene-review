CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic subunit of protein phosphatase 2A (PP2A), a
CC       serine/threonine phosphatase involved in the regulation of a wide
CC       variety of enzymes, signal transduction pathways, and cellular events
CC       (By similarity). PP2A is the major phosphatase for microtubule-
CC       associated proteins (MAPs) (By similarity). Key mediator of a quality
CC       checkpoint during transcription elongation as part of the Integrator-
CC       PP2A (INTAC) complex (PubMed:32966759). The INTAC complex drives
CC       premature transcription termination of transcripts that are unfavorably
CC       configured for transcriptional elongation: within the INTAC complex,
CC       mts/PP2A catalyzes dephosphorylation of the C-terminal domain (CTD) of
CC       Pol II subunit POLR2A/RPB1 and Spt5, thereby preventing transcriptional
CC       elongation (PubMed:32966759, PubMed:37995689).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P67775, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32966759,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37995689}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: PP2A exists in several trimeric forms, all of which consist of
CC       a core composed of a catalytic subunit associated with a 65 kDa
CC       regulatory subunit (PR65) (subunit A) (By similarity). The core complex
CC       associates with a third, variable subunit (subunit B), which confers
CC       distinct properties to the holoenzyme (By similarity). Component of the
CC       Integrator-PP2A (INTAC) complex, composed of the Integrator core
CC       complex and protein phosphatase 2A subunits mts/PP2A and Pp2A-29B
CC       (PubMed:32966759, PubMed:37995689). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P67775,
