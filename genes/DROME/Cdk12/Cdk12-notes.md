# Cdk12 notes

- UniProt Q9VP22, CG7597; single fly CDK12/CDK13 ortholog, Ctk1 ortholog [PMID:20952539 "Using Drosophila, we demonstrate that dCDK12 (CG7597) is a transcription-associated CTD kinase, the ortholog of yCtk1"].
- Localization with elongating Pol II [PMID:20952539 "the distribution of dCDK12 on formaldehyde-fixed polytene chromosomes is virtually identical to that of hyperphosphorylated RNA polymerase II (RNAPII), but is distinct from that of P-TEFb (dCDK9 + dCyclin T)"].
- Activity [PMID:20952539 "Drosophila dCDK12 purified from nuclear extracts manifests CTD kinase activity in vitro"]; Ser2 [PMID:20952539 "RNAi knockdown of dCDK12 in S2 cells alters the phosphorylation state of the CTD, reducing its Ser2 phosphorylation levels"]; cyclin K [PMID:20952539 "we find that cyclin K is associated with purified dCDK12, implicating it as the cyclin subunit of this CTD kinase"].
- RAS/MAPK screen [PMID:24643257 "At least three other factors (Cdk12, Fip1, and CG1603) also seemed to modulate the transcript levels of mapk"].
- Antiviral RNAi [PMID:39601580 "Notably, CDK12 abrogated the RNAi suppression exerted by B2"].
- Deep research (falcon) available: Cdk12-deep-research-falcon.md.

## Decisions
- Core: CTD kinase activity, positive regulation of transcription elongation, CycK-CDK12 complex.
- protein binding (CycK) -> cyclin binding; regulation of cell cycle (IEA) marked over-annotated; cytosol, antiviral RNAi and protein sequestering kept non-core.
