CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the activation cascade of caspases responsible
CC       for apoptosis execution (PubMed:10200258, PubMed:10675329,
CC       PubMed:10984473). Effector of steroid-mediated apoptosis during insect
CC       metamorphosis (PubMed:10984473). Overexpression promotes programmed
CC       cell death (PubMed:10200258, PubMed:10675329, PubMed:10984473).
CC       Interaction with Diap1 is required to suppress Dronc-mediated cell
CC       death; via Diap1-mediated ubiquitination of Dronc (PubMed:10675329,
CC       PubMed:14517550). Rate-limiting caspase in rpr, grim and hid death
CC       pathway (PubMed:10984473, PubMed:14517550). Recruited to the Dark
CC       apoptosome, an adapter protein complex that mediates activation of the
CC       caspase cascade in programmed cell death initiated by the intrinsic
CC       apoptosis pathway (PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603). Association with
CC       the Dark apoptosome stimulates autocatalytic cleavage and activation of
CC       Dronc, promoting Dronc-mediated cleavage of downstream effector
CC       caspases such as Drice (PubMed:21220123, PubMed:25644603).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10200258, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10675329,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10984473, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14517550,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Strict requirement for an Asp residue at position P1 and with
CC         a marked preference for His at position P2. It has a preferred
CC         cleavage sequence of Leu-Gly-His-Asp-|-Xaa.; EC=3.4.22.62;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603};
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Zymogen activated by autocatalytic cleavage;
CC       association with the Dark apoptosome brings multiple molecules together
CC       to facilitate their dimerization and activation by autocatalytic
CC       cleavage. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38381783}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via residues 114-125) with Diap1 (via BIR 2
CC       domain); binding blocks Dronc-mediated cell death (PubMed:10675329,
CC       PubMed:10984473, PubMed:14517550). Can form a stable complex with Drice
CC       (PubMed:10675329, PubMed:14517550). Rpr, hid and grim can out-compete
CC       Dronc for binding Diap1, therefore removing Diap1-mediated
CC       ubiquitination (PubMed:10675329, PubMed:14517550). Interacts (via CARD
CC       domain) with Dark (via Dark CARD and WD domains); the interaction
CC       stimulates Dark oligomerization to form the apoptosome and brings pairs
CC       of Dronc molecules together on the apoptosome to facilitate their
CC       dimerization and activation by autocatalytic cleavage (PubMed:21220123,
CC       PubMed:25644603, PubMed:38381783). Binding to Dark stimulates
CC       apoptosome assembly (PubMed:25644603, PubMed:38381783). After
CC       autocatalytic cleavage the Dronc caspase domain dissociates from the
CC       apoptosome but the CARD domain remains associated (PubMed:25644603).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10675329, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10984473,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14517550, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21220123,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25644603, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38381783}.

CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed both maternally and zygotically.
