CC   -!- FUNCTION: Type I transmembrane receptor serine/threonine kinase
CC       involved in the activin-like TGF-beta signaling pathway
CC       (PubMed:10320478, PubMed:17119022, PubMed:18820452, PubMed:8289834).
CC       Forms a receptor complex with a type II transmembrane receptor
CC       serine/threonine kinase, and upon activation by an activin/TGF-beta-
CC       like ligand phosphorylates downstream receptor-regulated SMAD (R-SMAD)
CC       transcription factors, inducing their nuclear localization resulting in
CC       regulation of activin/TGF-beta-responsive genes (PubMed:10320478,
CC       PubMed:17119022, PubMed:18820452, PubMed:8289834). When part of a
CC       heteromeric complex with the type I receptor put/punt can be activated
CC       by the activin/TGF-beta-like ligands Actbeta/activin, daw/dawdle and
CC       possibly myo/myoglianin; ligand specificity is probably isoform-
CC       dependent (PubMed:17119022, PubMed:18820452, PubMed:8289834). Upon
CC       activation phosphorylates the R-SMAD Smox/SMAD2 to activate the
CC       canonical TGF-beta pathway (PubMed:10320478, PubMed:17119022,
CC       PubMed:18820452, PubMed:19766717, PubMed:9887103). Can also
CC       phosphorylate Mad, the R-SMAD of the dpp BMP-like signaling pathway, in
CC       response to daw signaling but to a lesser extent (PubMed:18820452,
CC       PubMed:22563507). Phosphorylation of Mad is competitively regulated by
CC       Smox/SMAD2 (PubMed:22563507). Positive regulator of cell proliferation,
CC       probably through activation of the SMAD2-dependent activin signaling
CC       pathway (PubMed:9887103). Required for normal proliferation of medulla
CC       neuroblasts and laminar precursor cells in the optic lobe
CC       (PubMed:18171686). Involved in regulation of the ecdysteroid hormone
CC       receptor EcR in neuronal cells throughout the central nervous system
CC       (PubMed:38913612). Involved in axonal and dendritic pruning and neural
CC       remodeling of mushroom body gamma neurons during metamorphosis through
CC       regulation of EcR (PubMed:12581521). Involved in differentiation of
CC       dorsal cluster and ellipsoid body adult-specific neurons prior to
CC       metamorphosis (PubMed:16437159). Required in motoneurons for axon
CC       guidance or outgrowth of ISNb and SNa axons (PubMed:17119022). Involved
CC       in regulation of wing growth (PubMed:18820452). Signaling by daw or myo
CC       is involved in lifespan determination through regulation of proteasome-
CC       mediated degradation of misfolded or damaged proteins
CC       (PubMed:29615416).
