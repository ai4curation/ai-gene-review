## Literature journal

- TORC2 components rictor and sin1 needed for Akt HM phosphorylation; minor growth phenotype
  [PMID:17369395 "Mutants  removing critical TORC2 components, rictor and sin1, strongly reduced AKT  hydrophobic motif (HM) phosphorylation and AKT activity, but showed only minor  growth impairment."]
- rictor-null: reduced Akt-induced hyperplasia and Ser505 phosphorylation
  [PMID:17462592 "In rictor-null mutants, Akt-induced tissue hyperplasia was reduced  and Akt-Ser-505 phosphorylation was decreased."]
- TORC2 regulates growth via Myc nuclear localization
  [PMID:25999153 "Furthermore, loss of TORC2 disrupted the nuclear localization of Myc, and inhibited Myc-dependent transcription."]
- Heat-stress stress granules
  [PMID:26054799 "TORC2 is specifically required for stress granule formation under heat stress in Drosophila cells and tissues"]
- Dendritic tiling with Trc
  [PMID:19875983 "Similar to trc mutants, dendrites of sin1 and rictor mutants show  inappropriate overlap of the dendritic fields."]
- Deep research: Rictor itself is not catalytic
  [file:DROME/rictor/rictor-deep-research-falcon.md "there is therefore no reaction catalyzed by isolated Rictor and no transporter substrate to assign."]
