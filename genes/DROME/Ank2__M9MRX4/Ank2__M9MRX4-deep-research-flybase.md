# FlyBase gene description for Ank2__M9MRX4 (DROME)

Ankyrin 2 (Ank2) encodes a cytoskeletal binding protein required for proper function of the product of &bgr;-Spec via direct interaction. It contributes to the regulation of short-term memory, sound perception, microtubule cytoskeleton and neuromuscular junction development and synapsis.
