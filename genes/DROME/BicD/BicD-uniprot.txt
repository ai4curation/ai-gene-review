CC   -!- FUNCTION: This protein is essential for differentiation. It may play a
CC       role in localizing of Nanos (a maternal determinant) activity in
CC       oocytes. Functions redundantly with BicDR (PubMed:38264934). During
CC       oogenesis, plays a specific role, together with Rab6 but independently
CC       of Sec5, in the polarization of the oocyte microtubule cytoskeleton, in
CC       oskar mRNA localization and in the anterodorsal secretion of grk. Plays
CC       a role in the biogenesis of annulate lamellae containing nuclear pore
CC       complex components (PubMed:31626769). During macrochaetae development,
CC       together with BicDR, involved in Rab 6 and Spn-F stability and
CC       distribution and actin cytoskeleton organization (PubMed:38264934).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17329360, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17827179,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2590944, ECO:0000269|PubMed:31626769,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38264934}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: May homodimerize but does not interact with BicDR
CC       (PubMed:38264934). Interacts (via C-terminal domain) with Rab6.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17329360, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17827179,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38264934}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       P16568; P29742: Chc; NbExp=5; IntAct=EBI-112159, EBI-160368;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: In ovaries, expressed in oocyte and nurse cells.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2576013}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed maternally and zygotically
CC       (PubMed:2576013). Expressed during oogenesis and throughout development
CC       (PubMed:11546740, PubMed:2576013). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11546740,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:2576013}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Viable and fertile (PubMed:38264934). Post
CC       eclosion discolored, short and brittle posterior scutellar bristle
CC       macrochaetae; this phenotype is more common in females than in males
CC       (PubMed:38264934). Pupal to adult lethal when combined with mutations
CC       in BicDR (PubMed:38264934). Mutant embryos show Nanos mislocalization
CC       and thus bicaudal development (PubMed:2590944). RNAi-mediated knockdown
CC       results in sterility and egg chamber defects including loss of annulate
CC       lamellae (PubMed:31626769). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2590944,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31626769, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38264934}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the BicD family. {ECO:0000305}.
