CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the survival motor neuron (SMN) complex that
CC       catalyzes the assembly of small nuclear ribonucleoproteins (snRNPs),
CC       the building blocks of the spliceosome, and thereby plays an important
CC       role in the splicing of cellular pre-mRNAs (PubMed:30563832). Nuclear
CC       receptor cofactor for the ecdysone-regulated processes of molting and
CC       puparium formation (PubMed:14645129). Acts downstream from ecdysone
CC       biosynthesis and release to control the expression of specific
CC       ecdysone-regulated genes such as Eip74EF (E74) (PubMed:14645129).
CC       Essential in muscle and neuronal tissues for motor function, including
CC       climbing ability and flight (PubMed:24391840).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14645129, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24391840,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30563832}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the core survival motor neuron (SMN) complex
CC       composed of Smn, Gem2, Gem3, rig/Gem5 and one of 3 almost identical
CC       Gem4 paralogs encoded by Glos/Gem4a, Gem4b or Gem4c (PubMed:30563832).
CC       Interacts with nuclear receptors EcR, svp (seven up), usp
CC       (ultraspiracle), Hr39 and Hr3 (PubMed:14645129).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14645129, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30563832}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       Q86BY9; Q05192: Hr39; NbExp=2; IntAct=EBI-422757, EBI-75048;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14645129,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24391840, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26098872}. Cytoplasm
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14645129, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20452345,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24391840, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26098872}. Cytoplasm,
CC       U-body {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20452345}. Nucleus, gem
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24391840}. Note=Shuttles between the nucleus and
CC       cytoplasm at different stages of development.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14645129}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the brain and salivary glands of early
CC       and late second instar larvae. Expressed in nurse cells and oocytes.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14645129, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20452345}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: During second and third instar larval development,
CC       it is localized primarily to the cytoplasm of cells in the brain and
CC       imaginal discs (tissues that are fated to form specific parts of the
CC       adult fly during metamorphosis). Also localized to the cytoplasm of
CC       larval salivary gland cells during the second and early third instar
CC       stages. It however, begins to shift into the nucleus of these cells in
CC       the mid-third instar maintaining this localization through the end of
CC       larval development. At puparium formation, it shuttles out of the
CC       nucleus of salivary gland cells to become more abundant in the
CC       cytoplasm. A similar dynamic movement occurs in cells of the larval
CC       midgut during third instar development. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14645129}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Contains one Leu-Xaa-Xaa-Leu-Leu (LXXLL) motif, which is
CC       usually essential for the association with nuclear receptors.
CC       {ECO:0000250}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Late larval lethal associated with molting
CC       defects at the 3rd instar stage (PubMed:26098872). RNAi-mediated
CC       knockdown is late larval to adult lethal; the phenotype is the same if
CC       knockdown is restricted to muscle tissue, neuronal tissue or motor
CC       neuron cells (PubMed:24391840). Conditional RNAi-mediated knockdown in
CC       the central nervous system or muscle tissue of adults results in age-
CC       dependent decline of motor functions, including climbing ability and
CC       flight (PubMed:24391840). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24391840,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:26098872}.
