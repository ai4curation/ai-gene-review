# Mgtor (Megator, Mtor, Bx34; CG8274; FBgn0013756) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research provider was available for this review; this synthesis was written by hand from
the cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record A1Z8P9 (TPR_DROME).

## Identity and domain architecture

- 2346 aa nuclear protein; N-terminal ~70% is predicted extended coiled coil, C-terminal ~30% acidic
  and unstructured [PMID:9152019 "The Bx34 N-terminal 70% (180 kDa) is predicted to"].
- Ortholog of vertebrate TPR and yeast Mlp1/Mlp2 (PANTHER PTHR18898:SF2 NUCLEOPROTEIN TPR; Pfam
  TPR_MLP1_2) [PMID:19273613 "From this complex, Mtor is the only protein that shows clear sequence conservation with proteins in other organisms, such as the nuclear pore complex (NPC) protein translocated promoter region (Tpr) in mammals"].
- Essential gene: P-element allele l(2)k03905 is embryonic/larval lethal and reverted by precise
  excision [PMID:15356261 "That Megator is an essential protein necessary for viability is supported by the embryonic lethality observed as a consequence of P insertions in the Megator gene."].

## Localization

- Interphase: nuclear rim on/near NPCs, plus intranuclear extrachromosomal space; fractionates with
  nuclear matrix [PMID:9152019 "Biochemically, Bx34 isolates exclusively with the nuclear matrix"].
- Basket nucleoporin recruited to reforming NE in G1 [PMID:18562695 "concomitantly with the recruitment of the basket nucleoporin Mtor"].
- N-terminal coiled coil required for NPC and spindle-matrix localization
  [PMID:22855526 "These data suggest that the amino-terminal coiled-coil domain of Megator is required for localization to both nuclear pore complexes and to the spindle matrix"].
- Mitosis: reorganizes into a fusiform, MT-independent spindle matrix with Skeletor, Chromator, EAST
  [PMID:19273613 "Collectively, these data indicate that Mtor is part of a dynamic, nuclear derived spindle matrix surrounded by a fenestrated membranous system containing lamin B"].
  Not enriched at kinetochores [PMID:19273613 "Given that Mtor does not specifically accumulate at KTs"].
  Midbody at telophase [PMID:15356261 "the majority of Megator is localized to the spindle midbody"].
- Heat shock: intranuclear pool moves to 93D puff [PMID:12027452 "great majority of the Tpr in the deep nuclear interior accumulates at a single"].

## Spindle assembly checkpoint

- Mtor RNAi: faster mitosis, lower mitotic index, weakened SAC response; Mad2 and Mps1 (not Mad1)
  reduced at unattached kinetochores; Mad2 co-IPs with Mtor; conserved with human TPR
  [PMID:19273613 "Overall, our results support a model in which Mtor/Tpr acts as a spatial regulator of SAC, ensuring a timely and effective recruitment of Mad2 and Mps1 to unattached KTs as cells enter mitosis"].
- Slower anaphase spindle elongation and poor congression are corrected by delaying anaphase with
  MG132, i.e. indirect [PMID:19273613 "Such problems in completing chromosome congression are corrected if anaphase onset is delayed by treating cells with the proteasome inhibitor MG132"].
- Megator coiled-coil fragments bind Mad1 N-terminus directly; Mps1 phosphorylation (T1259, T1302,
  T1338, T1390) releases Mad1 from NPCs in prophase, required for kinetochore loading of Mad1-C-Mad2
  [PMID:31913420 "Phosphorylation of Megator by Mps1 abrogates Megator interaction with Mad1, which is essential for KT localization of Mad1-C-Mad2 at levels required to sustain robust SAC signaling and accurate chromosome segregation."].
- Testis GSCs: loss causes Apc2 mislocalization, misoriented centrosomes, spindle defects and GSC loss,
  rescued by Mad2/Mps1 [PMID:26714316 "Expression of Mtor, mad2, and mps1 significantly rescued the GSC loss phenotypes of the Mtor-mutant GSC clones, while expression of Apc2 had no significant function."].

## Chromatin and transcription

- MSL complex purification co-purifies Mtor and Nup153; knockdown disrupts MSL X-chromosome staining
  [PMID:16543150 "with several components of the nuclear pore, in particular Mtor/TPR and Nup153."].
- ChIP-chip: Mtor and Nup153 occupy NARs (25% of genome, enriched on male X, active marks)
  [PMID:20174442 "we show that Nup153 and Mtor interact with 25% of the Drosophila genome in large domains spanning 10–500 kb in size."].
  Mtor-depletion expression data were not shown [PMID:20174442 "and also upon Mtor-depletion (data not shown)."].
- Mtor attenuates male X transcription: depletion over-activates MSL targets (nascent level), rescues
  MSL mutant male lethality, and acts on RNAPII Ser5 phosphorylation
  [PMID:34133927 "These results suggest that normally Mtor may restrict gene expression by regulating the transition from hypo-phosphorylated to Ser5P RNAPII and thus the initial entry into transcription initiation."].
- Interacts with Nup98, ecdysone-independent; Mtor RNAi did not affect ecdysone-gene behaviour
  [PMID:28366641 "RNAi depletion of Mtor did not affect transcriptional behavior of ecdysone-inducible genes"].

## Peripheral

- ER-stress natural variation screen candidate only [PMID:23667151 "Megator has no known function in ER stress"].
