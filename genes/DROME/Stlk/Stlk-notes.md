# Stlk (STRAD; CG40293; UniProt P83098) notes

- STE20-family pseudokinase; UniProt: "The protein kinase domain is predicted to be catalytically inactive."
- Falcon deep research: no direct fly catalytic assay; classified as a pseudokinase
  [file:DROME/Stlk/Stlk-deep-research-falcon.md "a 2015 *Drosophila* Ste20-family screen explicitly called it a pseudokinase and *excluded* it from the screen of candidate active kinases"].
- LKB1 cofactor in fly cells [PMID:36899949 "We finally tested whether impaired LKB1 T353 phosphorylation results in changes in binding of the LKB1 cofactors Stlk and Mo25"].
- Mammalian mechanism: STRAD/MO25 enhance LKB1 activity and nuclear export
  [PMID:36899949 "its export from the nucleus and its activity are enhanced upon binding to the pseudokinase STRADα and to the adaptor protein Mo25"].
- No Stlk-specific loss-of-function phenotype or endogenous localization in flies found (deep research).
- NOT protein kinase activity (IKR, PMID:19809514) accepted.
- TORC1 regulation sign inverted in GOA -> MODIFY to negative regulation of TORC1 signaling (consistent with Lkb1 and Mo25).
