# FlyBase gene description for Gmer (DROME)

GDP-4-keto-6-deoxy-D-mannose 3,5-epimerase/4-reductaseGDP-L-fucose synthase (Gmer) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the two-step NADP-dependent conversion of GDP-4-dehydro-6-deoxy-D-mannose to GDP-L-fucose, involving an epimerase and a reductase reaction. It is involved in the last step of the 'de novo' synthesis of GDP-L-fucose from GDP-mannose.
