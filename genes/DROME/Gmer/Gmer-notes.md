# Gmer (Q9W1X8) notes

- Gmer is the single fly GDP-4-keto-6-deoxymannose 3,5-epimerase/4-reductase (GER/FX); recombinant
  protein has the predicted activity [PMID:16650000 "the encoded proteins found to have the predicted activity"].
- De novo route is the only GDP-fucose source in flies [PMID:11698403 "The Drosophila genome does not have orthologs
for the salvage pathway enzymes"].
- Decision: ISS "GDP-mannose 3,5-epimerase activity" (GO:0047918 = GDP-mannose -> GDP-L-galactose, EC 5.1.3.18)
  is the plant GME reaction, not the GMER reaction; modified to GO:0050577 GDP-L-fucose synthase activity.
- Generic oxidoreductase and nucleotide-sugar biosynthesis IEA terms modified to the specific terms.

- Falcon deep research (Gmer-deep-research-falcon.md; it finished after the wrapper's 600 s timeout) reports an in vivo
  Gmer study (Ayukawa and colleagues, Gmer^SH allele; not in GOA or the local cache, PMID not verified here): homozygotes
  die as third-instar larvae with loss of AAL lectin staining and of Notch-dependent Wingless at the D/V boundary, and
  Gmer-deficient clones are rescued non-autonomously by GDP-fucose from neighbouring cells. The earlier note that no in
  vivo Gmer phenotype is described is superseded; description and suggested questions/experiments updated accordingly.
