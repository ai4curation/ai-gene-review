CC   -!- FUNCTION: E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase involved in regulation of Notch
CC       signaling (PubMed:15760269, PubMed:15829515, PubMed:20176925).
CC       Ubiquitinates the intracellular domains of Notch ligands Dl/delta and
CC       Ser/serrate, leading to their endocytosis and degradation
CC       (PubMed:15760269, PubMed:15829515, PubMed:20176925). Regulates a subset
CC       of Notch signaling events, including wing margin specification, leg
CC       segmentation and vein determination, that are distinct from those
CC       events requiring neuralize (neur) activity (PubMed:15760269,
CC       PubMed:15829515, PubMed:20176925). Also modulates lateral inhibition, a
CC       neur- and Dl-dependent signaling event, suggesting a distinct but
CC       partially complementary function with neur (PubMed:15760269,
CC       PubMed:15829515). Involved in the development of sensory organ
CC       precursors and wings (PubMed:15829515). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15760269,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15829515, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20176925}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=S-ubiquitinyl-[E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme]-L-cysteine +
CC         [acceptor protein]-L-lysine = [E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme]-L-
CC         cysteine + N(6)-ubiquitinyl-[acceptor protein]-L-lysine.;
CC         EC=2.3.2.27;
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via N-terminus) with intracellular domains of Dl
CC       and Ser. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15829515}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15760269}.
CC       Cytoplasm, cell cortex {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15760269,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15829515}. Note=Localizes at the apical cortex of
CC       cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15760269}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Ubiquitous in the wing imaginal disk (at protein
CC       level). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15760269, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15829515}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The C-terminal RING zinc finger domains are required for
CC       enhancement of Notch signaling. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15829515}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Pupal lethal (PubMed:15760269, PubMed:15829515).
CC       Pharate adults are eyeless and have vestigial wings and squat jointless
CC       legs (PubMed:15760269, PubMed:15829515). Wing loss is due to defective
CC       wing margin formation and wing pouch growth (PubMed:15829515,
CC       PubMed:20176925). Pharate adults also display ectopic macrochetes and
CC       increased microchete density on the dorsal thorax (PubMed:15760269).
CC       RNAi-mediated knockdown in wing imaginal discs result in loss of
CC       expression of Cut and Wingless in the wing margins due to inhibition of
CC       Notch signaling, and wings have a nicked phenotype (PubMed:20176925).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15760269, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15829515,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20176925}.
