# FlyBase gene description for IKKepsilon (DROME)

I-kappaB kinase &egr; (IKK&egr;) encodes a serine-threonine kinase that targets the inhibitor of apoptosis encoded by Diap1 for degradation. It also negatively regulates protein kinase C and the dynein adaptor encoded nuf. The product of IKK&egr; contributes to the specification of bristle cells, bristle cell morphogenesis, oocyte polarity, and dendrite development.
