# FlyBase gene description for Arc42 (DROME)

Activator-recruited cofactor subunit 42 (Arc42) encodes a mitochondrial short-chain acyl-CoA dehydrogenase that catalyzes the first of the four reactions of fatty acid beta-oxidation, which consists in the dehydrogenation of fatty acyl-CoA thioesters to generate (2E)-enoyl-CoA. The electrons produced are transferred to the mitochondrial respiratory chain via the FAD regeneration system.
