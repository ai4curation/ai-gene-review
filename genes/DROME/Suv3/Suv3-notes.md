# Suv3 (Q9VN03, CG9791) review notes

Module context: dmel_mitochondrial_degradosome (Suv3 with PNPase).

Deep research: `Suv3-deep-research-falcon.md` (falcon).

- Loss of function: [PMID:26152302 "Loss of dmsuv3 led to the accumulation of mitochondrial mRNAs, without increasing rRNA levels, de novo transcription or decay intermediates"]
- Processing: [PMID:26152302 "we observed a severe decrease in mitochondrial tRNAs accompanied by an accumulation of unprocessed precursor transcripts"]; [PMID:26152302 "our results propose that SUV3 is predominantly required for the processing of mitochondrial polycistronic transcripts in metazoan and that this function is independent of PNPase"]
- Localization: [PMID:26152302 "DmSUV3 shows clear mitochondrial localization"]
- Antisense decay with PNPase: [PMID:31365523 "are therefore rapidly degraded in a SUV3/PNPase-dependent manner"]
- Deep research caveat: "No purified-DmSUV3 ATPase or duplex-unwinding assay was reported in the fly studies examined"

Decisions: all accepted except DNA helicase activity (ISS from human SUPV3L1), marked as
over-annotation since fly Suv3 acts on mitochondrial RNA and is not nuclear. Two core
functions: degradosome RNA helicase in decay, and PNPase-independent precursor processing.
