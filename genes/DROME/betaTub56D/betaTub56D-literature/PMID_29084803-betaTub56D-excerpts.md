## Expression

> The maternally contributed, abundant β-Tubulin 56D is ubiquitously and uniformly distributed during embryogenesis.

## Loss of function

> The lethality associated with βTub56DNP0949/Df(2R)BSC782 occurred after embryogenesis during the first larval instar.

> Indeed, the abundant maternal βTub56D contribution was found to be stable throughout embryogenesis.

## Transgenic rescue

> UASt-βTub56D expression resulted in complete rescue of βTub56DNP0949/Df(2R)BSC782 first instar larval lethality.

> In contrast, UASt-βTub97EF(4B) and UASt-βTub97EF(4C) provided only limited rescue.

## Hemocytes

> By exploiting larval hemocytes from βTub56D mutants in combination with expression of UASt tubulin transgenes, we were able to alter the relative abundance of β-Tubulin 97EF and β-Tubulin 56D.

Reading note: the zygotic betaTub56D null dies as a first-instar larva, after maternal protein has
carried it through embryogenesis. A betaTub56D transgene fully rescues; betaTub97EF transgenes rescue
only partially. So betaTub56D supplies a function the 97EF paralog cannot fully replace when expressed
from the same driver. The paper does not show which molecular property is responsible.
