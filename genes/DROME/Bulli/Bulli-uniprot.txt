CC   -!- FUNCTION: Positive regulator of the Rab7 guanyl-nucleotide exchange
CC       activity of the Mon1-Ccz1 complex, possibly by enhancing its endosomal
CC       membrane association (PubMed:32391792, PubMed:32499409). As part of the
CC       Mon1-Ccz1 complex involved in endolysosomal biogenesis possibly by
CC       mediating Rab conversion, the replacement of Rab5 with Rab7 during late
CC       endosome maturation (PubMed:32499409). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32391792,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32499409}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the Mon1-Ccz1 guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor
CC       complex made up of Mon1, Ccz1 and Bulli; the interaction of Bulli with
CC       the Mon1-Ccz1 heterodimer is mediated via the C-terminal Mic1 domain of
CC       Bulli (PubMed:32499409, PubMed:37155863). Mon1 and Ccz1 form a stable
CC       complex which displays Rab7 GEF activity with or without Bulli; GEF
CC       activity is enhanced by Bulli possibly by improving membrane
CC       association of the complex (PubMed:32499409, PubMed:37155863).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32499409, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37155863}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Late endosome {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32499409}.
CC       Note=Colocalizes with Rab7, but not Rab5 positive endosomal structures.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32499409}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The C-terminal Mic1 domain consists of an alpha-solenoid
CC       structure that acts as a protein interaction scaffold for the CCZ1-MON1
CC       complex. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37155863}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Viable and fertile but with reduced lifespan,
CC       possibly due to impaired nephrocyte scavenger function
CC       (PubMed:32499409). Severely impaired nephrocyte function under
CC       physiological stress conditions such as aging (PubMed:32499409).
CC       Accumulation in nephrocytes of third instar larvae of enlarged alpha-
CC       vesicles (late endosomes) due to defects in endolysosomal maturation
CC       and lysosome acidification (PubMed:32499409).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32499409}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: 'Bulli' is the nickname of a classical German bus from
CC       the 1970s (the Volkswagen Type 2 van), which facilitated cargo
CC       trafficking. {ECO:0000303|PubMed:32499409}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the RMC1 family. {ECO:0000305}.
