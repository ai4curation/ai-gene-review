# Bulli (CG8270; Drosophila melanogaster, Q9VRX1) notes

The falcon deep research report (Bulli-deep-research-falcon.md) arrived after the wrapper timeout. It adds: membrane targeting via a beta-propeller basic patch (Wilmes et al. 2025) and phylogenomic evidence that RMC1 is widespread in eukaryotes rather than strictly metazoan [file:DROME/Bulli/Bulli-deep-research-falcon.md "Bulli is **not itself an enzyme, transporter, or the indispensable Rab7-exchange catalytic core**"].

## Identity
- Fly RMC1 (regulator of MON1-CCZ1) ortholog; third subunit of the Mon1-Ccz1 Rab7 GEF (absent from yeast; the 2023 cryo-EM paper calls it metazoan, later phylogenomics finds RMC1 broadly in eukaryotes).

## Literature
- [PMID:32499409 "Here, we show that full function of the Drosophila Mon1-Ccz1 complex requires a third stoichiometric subunit, termed Bulli (encoded by CG8270)."]; [PMID:32499409 "Bulli localises to Rab7-positive endosomes, in agreement with its function in the GEF complex."]; nephrocyte phenotypes [PMID:32499409 "absence of Bulli results in (i) reduced endocytosis, (ii) Rab5 accumulation within non-acidified enlarged endosomes, (iii) defective Rab7 localisation and (iv) impaired endosomal maturation"]; [PMID:32499409 "Both the Mon1-Ccz1 dimer and a Bulli-containing trimer display Rab7 GEF activity."]
- Structure [PMID:37216550 "RMC1 acts as a scaffolding subunit and binds to both Mon1 and Ccz1 on the surface opposite to the RAB7A-binding site"]; [PMID:37216550 "the assembly of RMC1 with Mon1-Ccz1 is required for cellular RAB7A activation, autophagic functions and organismal development in zebrafish"].

## Curation thoughts
- Bulli is not required for catalysis per se (dimer is active) but is a scaffold that stabilises the complex and is needed for full function in vivo: protein complex scaffold activity is the core MF; contributes_to GEF accepted.
- Regulation of autophagy (IBA/IEA) kept non-core; autophagy evidence is from zebrafish RMC1.
