## Deep research (falcon) follow-up

The falcon report describes Hem/Kette as a structural scaffold of the WRC with no intrinsic enzymatic activity (Kunda 2003; Stephan 2011), matching the review's choice to give Hem no core MF and to place it in SCAR complex and regulation of Arp2/3 nucleation. It notes that the historical six-transmembrane prediction for Kette was never validated, consistent with treating Hem as a peripheral membrane-recruited protein. Additional papers it cites (Hamp et al. 2016 on myoblast junction dissolution; Chaudhari et al. 2024 on Frazzled WIRS binding) are not in GOA or the cache; no annotations were added.
