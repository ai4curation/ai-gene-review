CC   -!- FUNCTION: Part of the MTV complex that associates with the HipHop-HOAP
CC       complex to form the terminin telomere-capping complex involved in
CC       telomere maintenance and prevention of telomere fusion
CC       (PubMed:19240120, PubMed:27835648). Potentially functions downstream of
CC       mei-41/ATR (PubMed:19240120). As part of the MTV complex binds single
CC       stranded DNA in a sequence-independent manner, protecting it from
CC       degradation (PubMed:27835648). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19240120,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Probably homodimerizes (PubMed:15684427). Component of the MTV
CC       complex, composed of moi/modigliani, tea and ver/verrocchio
CC       (PubMed:27835648). Interacts with ver/verrochio and tea (via C-
CC       terminus); the interactions are direct and require fully intact
CC       moi/modigliani and ver/verrocchio (PubMed:20679394, PubMed:27835648).
CC       The MTV complex is recruited to telomeres by the HipHop-HOAP complex,
CC       consisting of HipHop, cav/HOAP and Su(var)205/HP1 to form the terminin
CC       telomere-capping complex (PubMed:19181850, PubMed:20679394,
CC       PubMed:27835648). Interacts with cav/HOAP and Su(var)205/HP1; the
CC       interactions are direct (PubMed:19181850). Probably interacts with peo
CC       (via N-terminus and UBC domain) (PubMed:26110638).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15684427, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19181850,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20679394, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26110638,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}.

CC   -!- CAUTION: The naming of this protein has been complicated due to being
CC       expressed from a bicistronic mRNA. The upstream ORF (uORF) encodes
CC       moi/modigliani while the downstream ORF (dORF) encodes Tgs1

CC       (DTL) for the RNA-binding activity of Tgs1, and the respective ORF
CC       products have been referred to as DTLu and DTLd. The ORF identifier
CC       CG31241 was originally assigned to the gene encompassing both products,
CC       which have since been given separate ORF identifiers (CG42350 for
