CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic component of Mettl1-wuho methyltransferase complex
CC       that mediates the formation of N(7)-methylguanine in a subset of RNA
CC       species, such as tRNAs, mRNAs and microRNAs (miRNAs) (PubMed:39317727).
CC       Catalyzes the formation of N(7)-methylguanine at position 46 (m7G46) in
CC       a subset of tRNAs that contain the 5'-RAGGU-3' motif within the
CC       variable loop (By similarity) (PubMed:39317727). M7G46 interacts with
CC       C13-G22 in the D-loop to stabilize tRNA tertiary structure and protect
CC       tRNAs from decay (By similarity). Required for the elongation step of
CC       spermatid formation (PubMed:39317727). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9UBP6,
CC       ECO:0000255|HAMAP-Rule:MF_03055, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39317727}.

CC       reduced copulation behavior (PubMed:39317727).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39317727}.
