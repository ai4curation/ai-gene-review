## Literature journal

- Ihog family incl. Boi binds Hh via FNIII domain
  [PMID:16630821 "Other members of the Ihog family, including Drosophila Boi and mammalian CDO and BOC, also interact with Hh ligands via a specific FNIII domain"]
- Ihog or Boi required for Hh response
  [PMID:20048000 "Here we show that activity of Ihog (Interference hedgehog), or of its close relative Boi (Brother of Ihog), is absolutely required for Hh biological response"]
- ihog boi double mutants fail to activate Hh pathway in eye and wing discs
  [PMID:21044292 "cells in the developing eye disc that were mutant for both ihog and boi failed to activate the Hh pathway, causing severe disruption of photoreceptor differentiation in the retina"]
- Boi sequesters Hh in ovary apical cells, limiting FSC proliferation
  [PMID:21098113 "We show that Boi is expressed in apical cells and exerts its suppressive effect on FSC proliferation by binding to and sequestering Hh on the apical cell surface"]
- Boi and Dally prevent apical Hh dispersion; cytonemes
  [PMID:23276604 "We show that Dally and Boi are required to prevent apical dispersion of Hh"]

## Curation decisions

- Same conventions as ihog. Core: coreceptor activity; Hh binding + retention of Hh on the cell surface
  (maintenance of protein location in extracellular region accepted for boi because of direct ovary data).
