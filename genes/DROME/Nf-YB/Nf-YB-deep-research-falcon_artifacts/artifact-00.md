| **CG10447 is the *Drosophila melanogaster* NF-YB ortholog.** | CG10447 encodes a **153-aa** protein whose histone-fold motif is **65% identical** to human NF-YB; it is distinct from CG3891/dNF-YA and CG3075/dNF-YC. (pqac-00000004) | **Strong, gene-specific identification:** primary sequence comparison plus biochemical compatibility with the other fly NF-Y subunits. The Q8ST61 accession mapping derives from the supplied UniProt record. |
| **Nf-YB is an obligate structural subunit of the fly CCAAT-binding NF-Y complex.** | Recombinant dNF-YA, dNF-YB, and dNF-YC generated a shifted CCAAT-containing DNA complex only when **all three subunits** were present. (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000005) | **Strong direct biochemical evidence**, although obtained with recombinant proteins and EMSA rather than endogenous chromatin. It establishes complex-dependent DNA binding, not stand-alone DNA-binding activity by Nf-YB. |
| **The conserved role of NF-YB is to heterodimerize with NF-YC and provide a histone-like scaffold for NF-YA and DNA.** | NF-YB/NF-YC histone-fold domains form an H2B/H2A-like heterodimer required for NF-YA association. Structural work shows the dimer contacting the DNA backbone while NF-YA reads the CCAAT bases and bends DNA by about **80°**. (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000015, pqac-00000023) | **Strong family-level mechanism, inferred for Q8ST61:** the atomic structures used non-*Drosophila* NF-Y components rather than CG10447 protein. |
| **Nf-YB likely functions in the nucleus as part of assembled NF-Y.** | NF-Y is a nuclear transcription complex. In studied non-fly systems, NF-YB and NF-YC undergo coordinated nuclear entry as an HFD dimer, whereas NF-YA has intrinsic nuclear-entry capacity. (pqac-00000019, pqac-00000024) | **Moderate homology-based inference.** Nuclear action is mechanistically expected and consistent with fly CCAAT-binding activity, but no CG10447-specific localization experiment was found. |
| **Nf-YB transcripts are maternally supplied in early embryos.** | The three canonical fly NF-Y genes, including CG10447, were reported as maternally expressed based on embryonic expression resources. (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000025) | **Moderate transcript-level evidence.** This does not establish CG10447 protein abundance, subcellular localization, or tissue-specific activity. |
| **A role for Nf-YB in Mes4-dependent mesodermal transcription is plausible but unproven.** | Mes4 is **CG11301**, a tissue-specific NF-YC-related protein, not CG10447. Ectopic Mes4 caused weak ectopic *snail* expression, leading to a proposed Mes4-containing NF-Y complex; CG10447 requirement was not tested. (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000022, pqac-00000025) | **Indirect evidence only.** The experiment supports Mes4/NF-YC involvement but cannot establish that Q8ST61/Nf-YB participates in *snail* regulation. |
| **The indexed 2023 eye-disc report is not verified gene-specific evidence for Nf-YB.** | The indexed article concerns **NF-YC**, while the retrieved page text corresponds to an unrelated 2007 G1-arrest article rather than the 2023 publication. (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000012) | **Exclude from direct Q8ST61 annotation.** It examines another NF-Y subunit, and the full-text mismatch prevents verification from the retrieved material. |
