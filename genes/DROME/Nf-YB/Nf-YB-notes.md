## Follow-up on description sources (2026-11-01)

- MAPK: the Alliance automated text includes "positive regulation of MAPK cascade", from a FlyBase GO row not in our GOA snapshot. The evidence is indirect: NF-Y supports *sev* transcription upstream of the MAPK cascade [PMID:23470843 "Expression of the sev gene, or the D-raf gene, a downstream component of the MAPK cascade, could rescue the rough eye phenotype and the loss of R7 signals in dNF-YB knockdown flies"]. Added to the description as biology; no new annotation (NF-YB regulates transcription of a pathway component, not the cascade itself).
- Thorax: FlyBase says Nf-YB "is involved in eye and thorax development". The thorax study knocked down NF-YA, not NF-YB [PMID:18233955 "Knockdown of Drosophila NF-YA (dNF-YA) in the notum compartment of wing discs by a pannir-GAL4 and UAS-dNF-YAIR mainly resulted in a thorax disclosed phenotype"]. The paper is abstract-only here, so an NF-YB-specific thorax phenotype could not be verified. No change to annotations.
