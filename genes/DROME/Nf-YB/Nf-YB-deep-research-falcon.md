# Functional annotation report: *Drosophila melanogaster* Nf-YB (CG10447; UniProt Q8ST61)

## Identity and principal function

**The target is the fly NF-YB subunit, not NF-YA or NF-YC.** The supplied UniProt accession Q8ST61 identifies *D. melanogaster* Nf-YB/CG10447. Independent primary-literature sequence analysis identifies CG10447 as a 153-amino-acid protein whose histone-fold region has **65% identity** to that of human NF-YB. The corresponding fly NF-YA and canonical NF-YC genes are **CG3891** and **CG3075**, respectively. This assignment agrees with the supplied NFYB/HAP3-family and histone-fold domain annotations; the accession-to-gene mapping itself comes from the supplied UniProt record. (yoshioka2007complexinterferencein pages 2-3, stathopoulos2002wholegenomeanalysisof pages 5-6)

**Primary molecular role:** Nf-YB is a histone-fold *structural and DNA-association subunit* of nuclear factor Y (NF-Y), a transcription-regulatory complex. It is **not an enzyme or transporter**; its relevant substrate is CCAAT-containing double-stranded regulatory DNA *when assembled with NF-YA and NF-YC*, rather than a small molecule acted upon by Nf-YB alone. In a direct fly-protein electrophoretic mobility-shift assay, a CCAAT-containing oligonucleotide produced the characteristic complex only when recombinant dNF-YA, dNF-YB and dNF-YC were all supplied. The assay establishes a requirement for dNF-YB in **in-vitro trimer-dependent DNA binding**, not direct binding by isolated dNF-YB or occupancy of a particular endogenous fly promoter. (yoshioka2007complexinterferencein pages 2-3, yoshioka2007complexinterferencein pages 1-2)

The mechanistic interpretation is supported by structural studies of evolutionarily conserved NF-Y complexes **outside this specific fly protein**. NF-YB and NF-YC pair through their histone-fold domains to form an H2B/H2A-like scaffold; this dimer recruits NF-YA and contacts the DNA backbone. NF-YA provides much of the **base-specific recognition** of the CCAAT sequence. In a conserved-domain NF-Y–DNA crystal structure, NF-Y binding bent the DNA by approximately **80°**. Thus Nf-YB is best annotated as a component of a CCAAT-binding transcription-factor complex and a promoter-architecture scaffold—not as an independently sequence-specific transcription factor or a conventional nucleosomal histone. Those atomic details are **family-level inference for Q8ST61**, not a solved CG10447 structure. (romier2003thenfybnfycstructure pages 1-2, nardini2013sequencespecifictranscriptionfactor pages 1-2, nardini2013sequencespecifictranscriptionfactor pages 2-4)

## Cellular location and biological context

**Expected site of action: the nucleus, at transcription-regulatory DNA.** This follows from the fly trimer’s CCAAT-binding activity and NF-Y’s established transcriptional function. Studies of other NF-Y systems describe coordinated nuclear entry of the NF-YB/NF-YC dimer, followed by its participation in nuclear CCAAT binding. **A CG10447-specific protein-localization experiment was not identified**, so constitutive nuclear localization, its timing, and any additional compartments should not be asserted as demonstrated in flies. (yoshioka2007complexinterferencein pages 2-3, dolfini2012nfyandthe pages 5-6)

In embryogenesis, the three canonical fly NF-Y genes, including **CG10447**, were reported to be **maternally expressed** on the basis of embryonic expression resources. This is transcript-level context, not a measurement of Nf-YB protein localization or activity. A proposed connection to mesodermal transcription must distinguish Nf-YB from **Mes4/CG11301**, which is a *different gene* encoding an NF-YC-related subunit. Mes4 expression becomes enriched in presumptive mesoderm; experimentally imposed ectopic Mes4 expression produced weak ectopic expression of *snail*. The original investigators consequently proposed a tissue-specific Mes4-containing NF-Y complex. **They did not establish that CG10447 is required for Mes4-dependent *snail* expression**, or show dNF-YB bound to the *snail* regulatory region. This is a plausible partner-dependent pathway, not a validated Nf-YB target assignment. (stathopoulos2002wholegenomeanalysisof pages 5-6, stathopoulos2002wholegenomeanalysisof pages 7-9)

Likewise, fly experiments altering **NF-YA** expression and observing eye-development phenotypes demonstrate functions of *another* NF-Y subunit; they do not by themselves establish a CG10447 loss-of-function eye phenotype. The directly verified implication for Nf-YB remains its requirement for formation of the recombinant fly CCAAT-binding complex. (yoshioka2007complexinterferencein pages 2-3, yoshioka2007complexinterferencein pages 1-2)

## Recent research, applications and evidence gaps

The **2023** report *Cell Cycle Regulation by NF-YC in Drosophila Eye Imaginal Disc* concerns **NF-YC**, not CG10447/Nf-YB. Although its indexed abstract reports effects of NF-YC depletion on cyclins and Eyes absent in the eye disc, the retrievable document text was an unrelated **2007** eye-cell-cycle article; its experimental details could not be checked here. Those results are therefore **not used as gene-specific evidence** for Nf-YB. A **2013** primary article explicitly titled *dNF-YB plays dual roles in cell death and cell differentiation during Drosophila eye development* was identified, but its full text was unobtainable in this search. Its title indicates a potentially important direct phenotype study, **not sufficient evidence here to specify mechanism, magnitude, targets or localization**. No verified 2023–2024 experiment directly characterizing Q8ST61 was recovered. (avellino2023cellcycleregulation pages 1-2, avellino2023cellcycleregulation pages 10-11)

In practical terms, CG10447 is an **experimental handle on the fly NF-Y transcriptional system**: recombinant complex assembly and CCAAT binding are established, while assignment of particular endogenous promoters, chromatin effects, pathway interactions and developmental phenotypes requires gene-specific perturbation and promoter-occupancy evidence. The most defensible current annotation is therefore: **a likely nuclear, histone-fold NF-YB partner of NF-YC and NF-YA, required in vitro for the fly NF-Y complex to bind CCAAT DNA; embryonic Mes4-associated and eye-development roles require separate CG10447-specific verification.** (yoshioka2007complexinterferencein pages 2-3, nardini2013sequencespecifictranscriptionfactor pages 2-4, stathopoulos2002wholegenomeanalysisof pages 5-6)
