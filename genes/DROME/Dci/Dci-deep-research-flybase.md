# FlyBase gene description for Dci (DROME)

Dodecenoyl-CoA delta-isomerase (Dci) encodes a putative peroxisomal enzyme that catalyzes the isomerization of 3-cis (3Z) and 3-trans (3E) double bonds into 2-trans (2E) in mono- and polyunsaturated enoyl-CoA thioesters in peroxisomal fatty acid beta-oxidation. When overexpressed, extends lifespan, enhances stress tolerance to oxidative and starvation stress and activates the product of foxo.
