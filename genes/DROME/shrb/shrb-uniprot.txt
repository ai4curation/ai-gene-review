CC   -!- FUNCTION: Probable core polymerization component of the endosomal
CC       sorting required for transport (ESCRT) III complex involved in multiple
CC       cellular processes requiring the outward bending of membranes,
CC       including vesicle budding, membrane repair and cytokinesis (Probable).
CC       The ESCRT pathway involves 4 complexes (ESCRT-0, -I, -II and -III) that
CC       sequentially assemble on the cytoplasmic side of membranes and induce
CC       membrane remodeling, budding and scission. As part of the ESCRT-III
CC       complex, involved in the budding of intraluminal vesicles (ILVs) into
CC       endosomes to form multivesicular bodies (MVBs), which target their
CC       contents for degradation via the endolysosomal pathway
CC       (PubMed:16713958). Involved in regulation of signal transduction
CC       pathways, including the Notch and BMP/decapentaplegic (dpp) pathways,
CC       by sequestering the intracellular domains of activated receptors into
CC       ILVs, isolating them from the cytoplasm and targeting them for
CC       lysosomal degradation (PubMed:22162134, PubMed:25804739). Involved in
CC       targeting ubiquitilated proteins, such as mono-ubiquitilanated N/Notch,
CC       to MVBs for degradation (PubMed:22162134, PubMed:33660341). Plays a
CC       role in wing development by regulating Notch signaling
CC       (PubMed:22162134). Involved in abscission of germline cells during
CC       oogenesis (PubMed:25647097). Involved in spermiogenesis
CC       (PubMed:33660341). Required for efficient cytoplasmic isolation and
CC       abscission during cytokinesis of epithelial sensory organ precursor
CC       cells (PubMed:37226981). May be involved in septate junction remodeling
CC       and maintenance (PubMed:37226981). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16713958,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22162134, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25647097,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25804739, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33660341,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37226981, ECO:0000305|PubMed:22162134}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: May be regulated by aurB/Aurora kinase B-dependent
CC       phosphorylation. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25647097}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homopolymer; forms elongated striated filaments of uniform
CC       ~10nm width (PubMed:27452459). Monomers interact in a staggered
CC       arrangement mediated by complementary charged electrostatic surfaces
CC       (PubMed:27452459). Interacts with l(2)gd1 (via DM14 domains 1 and 3);
CC       the interaction is direct and blocks access to the surface involved in
CC       homopolymerization (PubMed:22389409, PubMed:27452459, PubMed:28564595).
CC       This interaction may be required for the ESCRT-III complex role in
CC       multivesicular body formation (PubMed:22389409).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22389409, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27452459,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28564595}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Endosome {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33349255}. Endosome,
CC       multivesicular body {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22162134}. Midbody
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25647097, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37226981}.
CC       Note=Localizes to ring canals and midbodies connecting germline stem
CC       cell and daughter cystoblast prior to abscission and to the spectrosome
CC       during G2 phase and mitosis (PubMed:25647097). Punctate localization
CC       along the fusome and at ring canals of germ cell cysts in region 2 of
CC       the germarium but not in region 1 (PubMed:25647097). Localizes to
CC       either side of the midbody during cytokinesis as daughter cells are
CC       poised for abscission (PubMed:37226981). Is recruited to finger like
CC       extrusions located near the midbody of neighboring dividing cells
CC       (PubMed:37226981). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25647097,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:37226981}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed at considerably higher levels in testis
CC       than in ovary (PubMed:33660341). Expressed in midgut, eye, mouthparts
CC       and male accessory gland (PubMed:33660341).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33660341}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: In the pupal notum expressed in both epidermal
CC       cells and sensory organ precursor cells (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:37226981). Low expression levels during embryogenesis
CC       (PubMed:33660341). Expression increases during larval development,
CC       peaking at the third instar larval stage and decreasing during pupation
CC       (PubMed:33660341). Increased expression levels in 1 day old adult males
CC       but not 1 day old adult females (PubMed:33660341).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33660341, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37226981}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Lethal late in embryogenesis (PubMed:16713958).
CC       Dendritic arborization neurons have dramatically reduced dendritic
CC       field sizes but increased dendritic branching (PubMed:16713958).
CC       Increased ectopic axonal branching (PubMed:16713958). Conditional RNAi-
CC       mediated knockdown in germline stem-cells (GSCs) of the germarium
CC       delays or prevents abscission during GSC division resulting in multi-
CC       cell cysts that share a cytoplasm and undergo synchronous cell division
CC       (PubMed:25647097). Conditional RNAi-mediated knockdown in germline stem
CC       cells (GSCs) of the germarium produces egg chambers with 32 germline
CC       cells instead of 16 (PubMed:25647097). Conditional RNAi-mediated
CC       knockdown in testis results in defects in spermiogenesis, spermatid
CC       elongation and spermatid individualization, resulting in reduced male
CC       fertility (PubMed:33660341). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16713958,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25647097, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33660341}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Some multidendritic neurons possess extensive and
CC       intricate dendrite outgrowth known as dendritic arborization that
CC       resemble trees when imaged by microscopy. Mutant larvae possess less
CC       extensive and smaller arborization patterns giving the name Shrub.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the SNF7 family. {ECO:0000305}.
