Gene Group (FlyBase)
GLUCOSYLCERAMIDASES -
Glucosylceramidases (Glycoside hydrolase family 30) are lysosomal enzymes that catalyze the hydrolysis of glycolipid glucosylceramide (also called glucocerebroside) into simple fat ceramide and glucose. (Adapted from FBrf0230029).
UNCLASSIFIED GLUCOSYLTRANSFERASES -
This group comprises glucosyltransferases that do not classify under other groups in FlyBase.
BETA-GLUCOSIDASES -
β-glucosidases catalyze the hydrolysis of terminal, non-reducing beta-D-glucose residues with release of beta-D-glucose.
Protein Function (UniProtKB)
Non-lysosomal glucosylceramidase that catalyzes the conversion of glucosylceramide to free glucose and ceramide.
