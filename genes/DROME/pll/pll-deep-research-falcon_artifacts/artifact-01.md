| Toll–NF-κB innate immunity | Receptor-proximal serine/threonine kinase recruited through the MyD88–Tube–Pelle death-domain complex; directly phosphorylates signal-responsive sites in Cactus. | Slimb-dependent ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of Cactus release Dif/Dorsal for nuclear entry and induction of Toll-responsive genes, including antimicrobial peptides. | Direct Cactus-kinase evidence, 2013; recent immunity synthesis, 2022 (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000008, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000049) |

| Ras-driven epithelial tumorigenesis | Acts as a rate-limiting intracellular Toll–NF-κB signaling hub within *RasV12; scrib* tumor tissue. | Pelle knockdown drastically reduces tumor size; downstream Dorsal signaling supports growth and malignancy by opposing apoptosis, suppressing differentiation, and promoting invasion. | Drosophila carcinoma preprint, 2024; peer-reviewed confirmation, 2025 (pqac-00000057, pqac-00000058, pqac-00000059, pqac-00000060) |
| Wing development and tissue homeostasis | Preserves cell number by suppressing inappropriate dFoxO-dependent apoptosis rather than by increasing cell size or proliferation. | Loss of *pll* causes reduced wing tissue and anterior cross-vein defects through enhanced apoptotic signaling. | Genetic evidence, 2015 (pqac-00000028) |
| Mesoderm and muscle development | Supplies the kinase output of strong embryonic Toll signaling required for ventral fate specification. | Nuclear Dorsal induces mesoderm; loss of Tube/Pelle signaling dorsalizes embryos and eliminates ventral mesodermal derivatives and normal muscle development. | Rescue and loss-of-function evidence summarized in 2009 (pqac-00000029, pqac-00000030) |
| Neuronal development and axon targeting | Genetic studies implicate Pelle in photoreceptor axon targeting, although the immediate substrate and relationship to canonical Toll signaling remain incompletely defined. | Contributes to establishment of appropriate visual-system connectivity. | Developmental role summarized in 2015; mechanistic evidence remains limited (pqac-00000029, pqac-00000048) |
