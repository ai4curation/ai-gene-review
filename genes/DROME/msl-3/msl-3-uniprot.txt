CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the male-specific lethal (MSL) histone
CC       acetyltransferase complex, a multiprotein complex essential for
CC       elevating transcription of the single X chromosome in the male (X
CC       chromosome dosage compensation) (PubMed:17936709, PubMed:19029895,
CC       PubMed:7768187). The MSL complex specifically associates with the
CC       single X chromosome in males and mediates formation of H4K16ac,
CC       promoting a two-fold activation of X chromosome (PubMed:16543150,
CC       PubMed:18510926). Acts as a histone reader that specifically recognizes
CC       and binds histone H3 trimethylated at 'Lys-36' (H3K36me3) and histone
CC       H4 monomethylated at 'Lys-20' (H4K20me1) (PubMed:17936709,
CC       PubMed:19029895). Within the MSL complex, mediates the spreading of the
CC       MSL complex from initiation sites on the male X chromosome to flanking
CC       chromatin (PubMed:19029895). Following initial recruitment of the MSL
CC       complex to male X chromosome by msl-2, msl-3 binds H3K36me3 and
CC       promotes spreading of the MSL complex in cis (PubMed:19029895). In
CC       addition to its role in dosage compensation in males, promotes germline
CC       stem cell differentiation in females: recognizes and binds H3K36me3,
CC       promoting recruitment of the ATAC complex and transcription of genes,
CC       such as RpS19b (PubMed:34878097). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17936709, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18510926,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19029895, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34878097,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7768187}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the male-specific lethal (MSL) histone
CC       acetyltransferase complex, composed of mof, mle, msl-1, msl-2 and msl-3
CC       proteins, as well as roX1 and roX2 non-coding RNAs (PubMed:10679323,
CC       PubMed:11014199, PubMed:16543150, PubMed:18510926). Component of a
CC       maternal MSL subcomplex composed of mof, msl-1 and msl-3
CC       (PubMed:32502394). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10679323,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11014199, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18510926, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32502394}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00972,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7768187}. Chromosome {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18510926, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19029895,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7768187}. Note=Msl-3 is associated with hundreds of
CC       discrete sites along the length of the X chromosome in males and not in
CC       females, and is also associated with 10-20 autosomal sites in males
CC       (PubMed:16543150, PubMed:18510926, PubMed:19029895). Recruited to the
CC       male X chromosome following association with roX1 and roX2 non-coding
CC       RNAs (PubMed:11014199). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11014199,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18510926,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19029895}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The chromo domain specifically recognizes and binds histone H3
CC       trimethylated at 'Lys-36' (H3K36me3) (PubMed:19029895). The chromo
CC       domain also mediates association with roX1 and roX2 non-coding RNAs,
CC       promoting recruitment to the male X chromosome (PubMed:11014199).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11014199, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19029895}.
CC   -!- PTM: Ubiquitinated by msl-2. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23084834}.
