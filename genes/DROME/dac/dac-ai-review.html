Gene Description

dachshund (dac) encodes a nuclear protein of the Ski/Sno/Dac family, distinguished by two conserved domains: the N-terminal DD1/Dachbox-N (Ski/Sno/Dac homology domain, a winged-helix-like fold in the vertebrate ortholog) and the C-terminal DD2/Dachbox-C coiled-coil. Dac is a core member of the retinal determination network with Eyeless, Sine oculis and Eyes absent: it is required for eye formation, it and eya positively regulate each other's expression, Dac and Eya physically interact and act synergistically, and targeted dac expression is sufficient to induce ectopic retina. No sequence-specific DNA binding has been demonstrated for the fly protein; it behaves as a transcriptional cofactor that partners with Eya and binds the corepressor CtBP directly. Outside the eye, dac specifies medial (intermediate) leg segments in proximodistal patterning, is required for antennal joint formation, mushroom body neuron differentiation and axon guidance, lamina neuron differentiation in the optic lobe, and sex-specific development of the genital disc.

Core Functions

Dac is a nuclear transcriptional cofactor of the Ski/Sno/Dac family that complexes with Eyes absent and binds the corepressor CtBP; acting with Eya, Ey and So it regulates RNA polymerase II transcription of retinal determination genes (including cross-activation of ey and eya) to specify eye identity.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does fly Dac bind DNA directly through its DD1 (Dachbox-N) domain, as reported for vertebrate DACH1, or does it reach chromatin only via partners such as So/Eya, so that the IBA-propagated DNA-binding TF activity should be revisited?

Q: Is the PRDLS motif in Dac required for CtBP binding and for Dac function in eye specification, i.e. is Dac predominantly a corepressor in vivo?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: CUT&RUN for endogenously tagged Dac in eye-antennal discs, with and without so/eya, combined with in vitro EMSA of the fly DD1 domain, to determine whether Dac binds DNA autonomously and identify its direct targets.

Experiment: CRISPR knock-in of a PRDLS-mutant dac allele to test whether CtBP binding is required for retinal determination and leg patterning.
