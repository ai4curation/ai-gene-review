# pins (Partner of Inscuteable / Rapsynoid; Q9VB22) notes

## Identity
- GPSM-family protein (LGN/AGS3/GPSM2 orthologue): N-terminal TPR repeats and three C-terminal GoLoco motifs (UniProt Q9VB22 InterPro IPR003109 GoLoco_motif; IPR019734 TPR_rpt).

## Literature journal
- Discovered as Rapsynoid, a Galphai two-hybrid partner [PMID:10875939 "Rapsynoid (Raps), which interacts in a two-hybrid assay with the signal transduction protein Galpha(i)"]. raps mutant neuroblasts lose Insc asymmetry and divide into equal-sized daughters [PMID:10875939 "the neuroblast division produces two daughter cells of similar sizes"].
- Pins stabilizes the apical complex and binds Insc via its TPRs [PMID:12571286 "Pins acts to stabilize the apical complex during neuroblast divisions"; "its N-terminal tetratricopeptide repeats can directly interact with the asymmetric localization domain of Insc"]. Mouse LGN rescues pins [PMID:12571286 "Pins can fulfill all aspects of pins function in Drosophila neuroblast asymmetric cell divisions"].
- Pins/Galphai and Baz/aPKC act in parallel to control spindle geometry and daughter size [PMID:12526793 "two parallel pathways (Bazooka/DaPKC and Pins/G alpha i) within the apical complex"].
- GoLoco proteins Pins and Loco act as Galphai GDIs to release Gbetagamma [PMID:15937221 "Loco and Pins act synergistically to release free Gbetagamma in neuroblasts"].
- Pins directly binds Mud (NuMA) and recruits it apically [PMID:16648843 "Pins directly binds, and coimmunoprecipitates with, the NuMA-related Mushroom body defect (Mud) protein"; "Pins recruits Mud to the neuroblast apical cortex"].
- Autoinhibited closed state; Galphai binding to GoLocos 2/3 opens the TPRs to Mud [PMID:17726110 "Pins forms a \"closed\" state via intramolecular GoLoco-tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) interactions, which regulate Mud binding"].
- Canoe binds the Pins TPR domain to activate the Pins/Mud/dynein pathway [PMID:22024168 "a previously uncharacterized portion of Canoe directly binds the Partner of Inscuteable (Pins) tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) domain"].
- Crystal structure of Pins TPR with Insc; Insc and NuMA compete [PMID:22171003 "The crystal structure of the Drosophila ortholog of LGN (known as Pins) in complex with Insc reveals a modular interface"].
- Microtubule/Khc-73/Dlg pathway also polarizes Pins/Galphai [PMID:16377571 "astral microtubules, kinesin Khc-73, and Discs large (Dlg) induce cortical polarization of Pins/Galphai in Drosophila neuroblasts"].
- Go (Galphao) binds Pins in SOP cells [PMID:16617104 "Go is shown to interact with Pins"].
- pins mutant brain tissue grows as tumours after transplantation [PMID:16142234 "raps (also called pins), mira, numb or pros grew to more than 100 times their initial size"] - indirect consequence of altered asymmetric division.
- Pins enriches Rok and Pkn apically to bias myosin [PMID:28712722 "Partner of Inscuteable (Pins) sequentially enriches Rok and Protein Kinase N (Pkn) on the apical neuroblast cortex"].

## Curation thoughts
- Core MF: GoLoco-mediated Galphai-GDP binding / GDI activity, and TPR-mediated scaffolding of Mud/Insc/Canoe (adaptor).
- Generic protein binding IPIs replaced/kept as non-core; IEA GTPase regulator activity refined to GDI activity.
- Determinant localization and tumour/proliferation terms are downstream/indirect.

## Deep research (falcon) additions
- Falcon report (pins-deep-research-falcon.md) agrees: Pins is a cortical adaptor for spindle positioning ["Pins binds Mud directly through its TPR region."]. It also notes tissue specificity: Pins-null wing-disc cells keep normal division orientation (Bergstralh et al. 2016, doi:10.1242/dev.135475; not in local cache), and an Aurora-A/Dlg/Khc73 branch from the Pins linker.
