# FlyBase gene description for Tdc2 (DROME)

Tyrosine decarboxylase 2 (Tdc2) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the decarboxylation of L-tyrosine to form tyramine. Tdc2 is involved in the production of tyramine an octopamine in neuronal tissues.
