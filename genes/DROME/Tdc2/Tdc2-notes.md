# Tdc2 (A1Z6N4) review notes

## Identity
- Neural tyrosine decarboxylase; group II PLP-dependent aromatic amino acid decarboxylase (PANTHER PTHR11999).

## Literature
- Two fly Tdc genes; Tdc2 neural; Tdc2 mutants lack neural tyramine and octopamine and retain eggs
  [PMID:15691831 "Flies with a mutation in dTdc2 lack neural tyramine and octopamine and are female sterile due to egg retention."]
  [PMID:15691831 "dTdc2 mutants release eggs into the oviducts but are unable to deposit them"]
- Locomotion and cocaine: [PMID:17638385 "Tdc2(RO54) flies have dramatically reduced basal locomotor activity levels and are hypersensitive to an initial dose of cocaine."]
  rescued by Tdc2-driven Tdc1 [PMID:17638385 "Tdc2-driven expression of Tdc1 rescues the phenotypes"]
- Circadian: rhythmically translated in clock cells; required in clock neurons for rhythmicity
  [PMID:24348200 "Thus, tdc2 is required within clock neurons for normal locomotor activity rhythms."]
  RO54 allele [PMID:24348200 "Tdc2RO54 mutant, which carries a point mutation that abolishes the enzymatic activity of TDC2"]

## Decisions
- Core MF tyrosine decarboxylase activity; BP tyramine and octopamine biosynthesis (Tdc2 performs the first step).
