# FlyBase gene description for Mdh1 (DROME)

Malate dehydrogenase 1 (Mdh1) encodes a cytosolic enzyme that reversibly catalyzes the oxidation of S-malate to oxaloacetate using the reduction of NAD+ to NADH. It is involved in gluconeogenesis and the malate-aspartate shuttle.
