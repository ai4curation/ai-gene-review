# Sec71 (Drosophila melanogaster, Q9VJW1, CG7578, FBgn0028538): manual literature research

No deep-research API was available for this review. This summary was compiled by hand
from PubMed searches ("Sec71 AND Drosophila", 7 hits) and the cached publications in
`publications/`. Quotes are verbatim from the cached files.

## Identity and family

- Sec71 is the single fly member of the BIG1/BIG2 (ARFGEF1/2) subfamily of large
  Sec7-domain Arf GEFs [PMID:22291037 "Sec71 is the Drosophila ortholog of human BIG1/BIG2, although it has not been extensively characterized."].
- UniProt Q9VJW1 (TrEMBL, 1653 aa) carries Pfam DCB, Sec7-like_HUS, Sec7, Sec7-like_HDS and
  BIG2_C, with the SEC7 domain at 575-765 (PROSITE PS50190). No transmembrane segment is
  predicted (Sec71-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md).
- The name is shared with yeast Sec71, which is Sec66, a small single-pass subunit of the
  Sec63 translocon complex; the two proteins are unrelated (Sec71-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md).

## Molecular function: Arf1 GEF

- [PMID:28420712 "We further identified Sec71 as a guanine nucleotide exchange factor for Arf1 that preferentially interacts with its GDP-bound form."] (abstract only in cache)
- [PMID:28420712 "Arf1 and Sec71 are interdependent for their localizations on Golgi."]

## Recruitment by Arl1 to the trans-Golgi

- [PMID:22291037 "Applying this to Drosophila melanogaster cell extracts revealed a direct interaction between Arl1 and Sec71, the Drosophila ortholog of the trans-Golgi GEFs BIG1/2."]
- [PMID:22291037 "Thus, Sec71 binds to Arl1-coated liposomes in vitro and colocalizes with Arl1 on the trans-Golgi in vivo."]
- [PMID:22291037 "This indicates that Arl1 recruits Sec71 to Golgi membranes, but it appears that Arl1’s effects are augmented by additional interactions elsewhere in the protein."]
- Purified Sec71(N-453) binds Arl1-GTP liposomes directly; the C-terminal region is cytosolic.

## Golgi structure and brefeldin A

- [PMID:33262309 "These results indicate that Sec71 is the sole BFA target for BFA body formation and controls Golgi stack separation."] (abstract only)

## Secretory trafficking

- [PMID:31649044 "Taken together, our observations indicate that Sec71 contributes to the secretion of both apical and basolateral proteins in PRCs, suggesting a defect in Golgi processing (Figure 3E)."]

## Downstream developmental roles (phenotypes)

- Dendrite pruning: [PMID:28420712 "Like Arf1, Sec71 is also important for dendrite pruning, but not for apoptosis, of sensory neurons."]
- Endocytosis of Neuroglian: [PMID:28420712 "we show that the Sec71/Arf1-mediated trafficking process is a prerequisite for Rab5-dependent endocytosis to facilitate endocytosis and degradation of the cell-adhesion molecule Neuroglian (Nrg)."]
- Neural stem cell reactivation (PMID:37567172, abstract) and neuroblast polarity:
  [PMID:40208939 "Taken together, these observations indicate that Arf1 and Sec71 may facilitate the localization of nonmuscle myosin II to the cell cortex in mitotic neuroblasts."]

These phenotypes follow from the general Golgi/Arf1 function and are not treated as core
processes.
