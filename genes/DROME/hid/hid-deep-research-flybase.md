# FlyBase gene description for hid (DROME)

head involution defective (hid) encodes a member of the RHG (Reaper-Hid-Grim) family that promotes apoptosis by binding to and inhibiting the inhibitor of apoptosis protein Diap1, thereby enabling caspase activation and cell death. Hid functions within the intrinsic apoptotic pathway and is a key effector of developmental and stress-induced apoptosis throughout Drosophila development. Its activity is negatively regulated by the Ras/MAPK signaling pathway through direct phosphorylation, which suppresses Hid-mediated apoptosis and promotes cell survival.
