# Kpc2 (CG11025; A0A0B4KFW5, isoform C) notes

## Identity
- UBAC1/KPC2 homologue with N-terminal UBL and two UBA domains (UniProt InterPro IPR052476 UBAC1; IPR015940 UBA).

## Literature journal
- KPC complex in fly sperm [PMID:33378371 "KPC is a heterodimer composed of KPC1, an E3 ligase, and KPC2 (or UBAC1), an adaptor protein"]; [PMID:33378371 "Like their mammalian counterparts Drosophila KPC1 and KPC2 physically interact and they stabilize one another at the protein level"].
- Modified Kpc2 in mature sperm [PMID:33378371 "In flies, KPC2 is monoubiquitinated and phosphorylated and this modified form of the protein is located in mature sperm"].
- Required for Amo localization and sperm storage [PMID:33378371 "Male flies lacking either KPC1 or KPC2 phenocopy amo mutants and are sterile due to a failure of sperm storage"].
- ER localization (FlyBase IDA) not located in cached text; Amo, not Kpc2, is described as ER-localized in spermatocytes.
- Falcon deep research (Kpc2-deep-research-falcon.md): Kpc2 is an adaptor; proteasome/p27 roles are mammalian-only inferences; isoform B rescues the mutant phenotypes.

## Curation thoughts
- No experimentally established fly MF; MF ND retained. Core function described via complex membership and sperm flagellum location.
