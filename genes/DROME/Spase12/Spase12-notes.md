# Spase12 review notes

UniProt Q9VAL0 (Signal peptidase complex subunit 1, SPCS1 ortholog); PANTHER PTHR13202.

## Literature journal

- Essential in flies [PMID:23573290 "Our findings show that spase12 mutants are embryonic lethal, while spase12 LOF clones result in developmental defects in all tissues tested."]
- But possibly dispensable for cleavage of tested substrates [PMID:23573290 "Conversely, Spase12 may not be necessary for SPC cleavage and subsequent localization of the putative SPC substrates tested."]
- Melanotic masses in mosaics [PMID:23573290 "spase12 mosaic tissue is susceptible to melanotic mass formation suggesting that spase12 LOF activates immune response pathways"].

## Curation decisions

SPC module conventions as in twr-notes.md. No developmental GO rows exist in GOA; none proposed (the phenotypes are pleiotropic consequences of secretory-pathway failure).

## Deep research

`Spase12-deep-research-falcon.md` (falcon) arrived after the review was first committed. It agrees with the review: Spase12 (CG11500) is a noncatalytic, membrane-integral SPCS1/Spc1-type accessory subunit of the ER SPC and is required for fly development. Its clients and its fly-specific subcellular distribution are unknown, and its mechanism is inferred from yeast and human orthologs. No annotation decision changed.
