# FlyBase gene description for Mtap (DROME)

Methylthioadenosine phosphorylase (Mtap) encodes a cytosolic enzyme that catalyzes the phosphorolytic cleavage of S-methyl-5'-thioadenosine (MTA), a by-product of the polyamine biosynthesis, to 5-(methylsulfanyl)-alpha-D-ribose 1-phosphate and adenine. Mtap plays a key role in the L-methionine salvage pathway and in the production of endogenous cellular adenine.
