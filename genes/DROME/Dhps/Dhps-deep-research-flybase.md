# FlyBase gene description for Dhps (DROME)

Deoxyhypusine synthase (Dhps) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the NAD(+)-dependent oxidative cleavage of spermidine and the subsequent transfer of its butylamine group to the epsilon-amino group of a lysine residue of the eukaryotic translation elongation factor 5 eEF5 to form the intermediate deoxyhypusine.
