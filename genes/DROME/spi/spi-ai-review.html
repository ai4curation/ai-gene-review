Gene Description

spi encodes Spitz, a Drosophila EGF-family ligand synthesized as a membrane-tethered precursor in the secretory pathway. After Star-dependent trafficking and Rhomboid-dependent intramembrane proteolysis, processed Spitz is released to bind and activate the EGF receptor in neighboring cells. Spitz drives EGFR/Ras/MAPK signaling during embryonic ectoderm patterning, photoreceptor recruitment, glial migration, muscle development, and other local inductive events.
