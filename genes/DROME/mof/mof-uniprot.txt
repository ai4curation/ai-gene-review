CC   -!- FUNCTION: Histone acetyltransferase that catalyzes the formation of the
CC       majority of histone H4 acetylation at 'Lys-16' (H4K16ac), an epigenetic
CC       mark that prevents chromatin compaction and constitutes the only
CC       acetylation mark intergenerationally transmitted (PubMed:10882077,
CC       PubMed:18510926, PubMed:20620953, PubMed:22421046, PubMed:32502394).
CC       Catalytic component of the male-specific lethal (MSL) complex, a
CC       multiprotein complex essential for elevating transcription of the
CC       single X chromosome in the male (X chromosome dosage compensation)
CC       (PubMed:10882077, PubMed:11014199, PubMed:16543150, PubMed:18510926,
CC       PubMed:34133927, PubMed:9155031). The MSL complex specifically
CC       associates with the single X chromosome in males and mediates formation
CC       of H4K16ac, promoting a two-fold activation of X chromosome
CC       (PubMed:11258702, PubMed:18510926, PubMed:20620953, PubMed:22421046).
CC       Dosage compensation ensures that males with a single X chromosome have
CC       the same amount of most X-linked gene products as females with two X
CC       chromosomes (PubMed:18510926, PubMed:9155031). In oocytes, mof is also
CC       part of a maternal MSL subcomplex that mediates H4K16ac deposition for
CC       intergenerational transmission: H4K16ac prepares the chromatin
CC       landscape for establishment of nucleosome accessibility and poises
CC       genes for future activation (PubMed:32502394). H4K16ac constitutes the
CC       only acetylation mark maintained from oocytes to fertilized embryos
CC       (PubMed:32502394). Mof also constitutes the catalytic component of the
CC       non-specific lethal (NLS) complex, which promotes expression of
CC       housekeeping genes on X chromosome and autosomes (PubMed:20620953,
CC       PubMed:20620954, PubMed:22723752). The NSL complex promotes strong
CC       expression of housekeeping genes compared to the two-fold expression
CC       mediated by the MSL complex on X chromosome, suggesting that the
CC       activation potential of mof is constrained in the context of dosage
CC       compensation (PubMed:20620953). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10882077,
