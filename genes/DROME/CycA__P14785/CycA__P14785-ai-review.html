Gene Description

Cyclin A (CycA, CG5940) is the single A-type cyclin of Drosophila melanogaster and a member of the cyclin AB subfamily. It is a non-catalytic regulatory subunit of cyclin-dependent kinases: through its N-terminal and C-terminal cyclin-box folds it binds and activates Cdk1 (Cdc2) and also associates with Cdc2c (Cdk2), and a hydrophobic patch on the first cyclin box contributes to substrate recognition and is the binding site of the cyclin inhibitor Fruhstart. CycA is expressed in all proliferating cells; in interphase it is mostly cytoplasmic, kept there by nuclear export, and in some germline cells it is enriched on the spectrosome/fusome. It enters the nucleus around prophase and is destroyed by APC/C-dependent proteolysis during metaphase via a complex N-terminal degradation signal. CycA-Cdk1 drives entry into mitosis, acting synergistically with cyclins B and B3, and it is the only mitotic cyclin essential for mitosis in the embryo. Its essential mitotic role is to keep the APC/C activator Fizzy-related (Fzr/Cdh1) inactive in G2 so that mitotic cyclins and String/Cdc25 are not degraded prematurely, which is critical before terminal divisions. CycA must be degraded for sister chromatid disjunction at the metaphase-anaphase transition. Residual or overexpressed CycA can drive the G1/S transition, an activity restrained in G1 by APC/C-mediated destruction, inhibitory phosphorylation of Cdk1 and the inhibitor Roughex.

What is experimentally established in Drosophila (for transfer)

  • Cdk regulatory subunit: binds Cdc2 and Cdc2c and is needed for their H1 kinase activity
    in complex with it (PMID:11027291, PMID:10607563). The hydrophobic patch
    (M235/L239/W242) binds Frs (PMID:17431409).
  • Mitotic entry (G2/M), acting synergistically with CycB (PMID:8428595, PMID:2139805,
    PMID:11350926). The C-terminal cyclin box region is enough for this.
  • Essential mitotic function: CycA-Cdk activity inhibits Fzr/Cdh1 in G2, which prevents
    premature APC/C-Fzr degradation of CycB, CycB3 and Stg (PMID:15203007,
    PMID:16854973).
  • CycA destruction is required for sister chromatid disjunction (PMID:11369230). This is
    regulation, not participation.
  • CycA has S-phase-promoting activity, restrained in G1 (PMID:9210381). This is non-core;
    cyclin E is the physiological G1/S cyclin.
  • Localization: cytoplasmic in interphase, nuclear from prophase. In germ cells it is on
    the spectrosome/fusome (PMID:22024320, PMID:27170181).
