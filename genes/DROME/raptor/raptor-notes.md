## Literature journal

- raptor knockdown decreases S6K Thr398 phosphorylation and S6K-driven overgrowth
  [PMID:17462592 "However, the knockdown of  another TOR binding partner, raptor, decreased S6K-Thr-398 phosphorylation and  inhibited S6K-induced cell overgrowth."]
- raptor depletion causes premature autophagy in dying midgut
  [PMID:25882046 "raptor depletion resulted in premature autophagy similar to Tor ablation, suggesting that raptor and Tor function in TORC1 and negatively regulates autophagy in this cell death context"]
- TORC1 negative feedback on Akt
  [PMID:20585550 "Under wild-type conditions, negative feedback to the PI3K/Akt signaling branch is mediated by TORC1 (containing TOR, Lst8 and Raptor), independently of S6K."]
- Muscle raptor knockdown blocks eclosion
  [PMID:25866192 "muscle-specific knockdown of Raptor causes flies to be too weak to emerge from their pupal cases during eclosion"]
- NMJ: raptor knockdown does not phenocopy Tsc2/rictor overgrowth
  [PMID:23393158 "Increased  synaptic growth was also observed in rictor mutants, while raptor knockdown did  not phenocopy the TSC mutant phenotype"]
