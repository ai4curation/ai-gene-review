# FlyBase gene description for Men (DROME)

Malic enzyme (Men) encodes a dehydrogenase that catalyzes the oxidation of malate to pyruvate, with the concomitant reduction of NADP[+] to NADPH. It functions, along with the products of Idh, G6pd, and Pgd, in a small network of NADP reducing enzymes.
