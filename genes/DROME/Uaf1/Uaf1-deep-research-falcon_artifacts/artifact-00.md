| **Uaf1 loss lowers Wingless output without lowering ligand production** | Posterior-compartment RNAi and independent CRISPR sgRNAs targeting different coding regions of **Uaf1/CG9062** reduced the Wingless target **Senseless (Sens)** in *Drosophila* wing discs. **wingless (wg)** expression at the dorsal–ventral boundary was retained. | Uaf1 is required in Wingless-responsive cells for signal reception or downstream transduction, rather than for Wingless-ligand production. | **High—direct fly genetics with independent perturbations and spatial controls.** (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000008, pqac-00000029) |
| **Uaf1 maintains the amplitude and range of intestinal Wingless signaling** | **Uaf1-null mosaic clones** in the adult *Drosophila* posterior midgut showed cell-autonomous loss or reduction of **fz3-GFP** and **nkd-lacZ**, especially farther from the midgut–hindgut Wingless source. | Uaf1 enables cells to respond across the Wingless morphogen gradient; loss preferentially compromises lower-signal, source-distant responses. | **High—direct fly null-clone and reporter evidence.** (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000011, pqac-00000042) |
| **Uaf1 maintains Arrow receptor abundance in vivo** | Adult posterior-midgut **Uaf1-null clones** showed cell-autonomous reduction of membrane-associated and cytoplasmic **Arrow**, the fly LRP5/6 ortholog. | **Primary molecular role: noncatalytic WD40 regulator of Arrow deubiquitylation** | Fly genetics and biochemistry place Uaf1 with **Usp46–Wdr20**; the complex reduces Arrow ubiquitylation and turnover and increases cell-surface receptor abundance.
