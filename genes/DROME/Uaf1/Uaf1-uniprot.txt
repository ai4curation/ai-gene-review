CC   -!- FUNCTION: Regulatory component of the Usp12-46 deubiquitylase complex
CC       (PubMed:37798281). activates deubiquitination by increasing the
CC       catalytic turnover without increasing the affinity of deubiquitinating
CC       enzymes for the substrate (By similarity). The complex deubiquitylates
CC       the wg/wingless-signaling receptor arr/arrow, which stabilizes the
CC       receptor and increases its concentration at the cell surface; this
CC       enhances the sensitivity of cells to wg/wingless-signal stimulation
CC       (PubMed:37798281). This increases the amplitude and spatial range of
CC       the signaling response to the wg/wingless morphogen gradient,
CC       facilitating the precise concentration-dependent regulation of its
CC       target genes (PubMed:37798281). Together with Wdr20 and Usp12-46
CC       required for wg/wingless-mediated signaling in the wing imaginal disc
CC       and for wg/wingless-dependent regulation of intestinal stem cell
CC       proliferation (PubMed:37798281). {ECO:0000250,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q8TAF3, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37798281}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Catalytic component of the Usp12-46 deubiquitylase complex
CC       consisting of Usp12-46, Wdr20 and Uaf1; regulatory subunit that,
CC       together wtih Wdr20, stabilizes Usp12-46 (PubMed:37798281). The Usp12-
CC       46 deubiquitylase complex associates with arr/arrow; the interaction
CC       leads to deubiquitination and stabilization of arr/arrow
CC       (PubMed:37798281). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37798281}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in the larval wing imaginal disc.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37798281}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Viable but sterile with reduced lifespan.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37798281}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the WD repeat WDR48 family. {ECO:0000305}.
