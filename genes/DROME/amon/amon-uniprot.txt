CC   -!- FUNCTION: Serine endopeptidase which is involved in the processing of
CC       hormone and other protein precursors at sites comprised of pairs of
CC       basic amino acid residues (PubMed:10749852, PubMed:20523747,
CC       PubMed:21138435). Required during embryonic and larval development,
CC       probably by proteolytically processing peptide hormones involved in
CC       hatching, larval growth and larval molting (PubMed:12586710). Required
CC       for the processing and activation of Akh which maintains normal
CC       hemolymph sugar levels (PubMed:20523747). Has been shown in one study
CC       to be required for processing of sli into slit N-product and slit C-
CC       product in the embryo which is necessary for lateral transverse muscle
CC       elongation but has been shown in another study not to be required for
CC       sli cleavage (PubMed:27628033, PubMed:32994163). Required for larval
CC       hatching (PubMed:10436051). Also required for normal larval wandering
CC       behavior which occurs prior to pupariation (PubMed:21138435). Required
CC       during pupal development for head eversion, leg and wing disk
CC       extension, and abdominal differentiation (PubMed:19559693). Required
CC       during eye development for R8 photoreceptor cell specification by
CC       regulating processing of ligands required for the BMP and activin
CC       signaling pathways (PubMed:28853393). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10436051,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10749852, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12586710,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19559693, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20523747,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21138435, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27628033,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:28853393, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32994163}.
CC   -!- CATALYTIC ACTIVITY:
CC       Reaction=Release of protein hormones and neuropeptides from their
CC         precursors, generally by hydrolysis of -Lys-Arg-|- bonds.;
CC         EC=3.4.21.94; Evidence={ECO:0000269|PubMed:10749852};
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10749852}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the central nervous system (CNS) and
CC       midgut endocrine cells of third instar larva (at protein level)
CC       (PubMed:19559693). In the CNS, expressed in the CA-LP1 and CA-LP2
CC       neurons which innervate the corpus allatum, and in the CC-MS2 neurons
CC       which innervate the corpora cardiaca of the ring gland
CC       (PubMed:19559693, PubMed:21138435). Also expressed in the CC-MS1, SP3,
CC       Tv and Va neurons (PubMed:21138435). Expressed in Akh-producing cells
CC       of the corpora cardiaca (PubMed:20523747). In the embryo, restricted to
CC       the final stages of embryogenesis where expression is found in anterior
CC       sensory structures and in only 168 cells in the brain and ventral nerve
CC       cord (PubMed:10436051). After larvae hatch, the sensory structures and
CC       most cells in the CNS turn off or substantially reduce expression
CC       (PubMed:10436051). In third instar larva, expressed at higher levels in
CC       the anterior section than in the posterior section (PubMed:10749852).
CC       Little expression is detected in the adult head (PubMed:10749852). In
CC       the developing eye, expressed at higher levels in pale-type R7
CC       photoreceptor cells than in yellow-type R7 cells although expression is
CC       not seen in all pale-type R7 cells (PubMed:28853393). Also expressed in
CC       outer photoreceptor cells (PubMed:28853393).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10436051, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10749852,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19559693, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20523747,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21138435, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28853393}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Detected during embryogenesis, larval development,
CC       and pupal development (at protein level) (PubMed:10749852,
CC       PubMed:19559693). Also expressed in the adult (PubMed:10749852). In the
CC       embryo, first observed at 12-16 hours and peaks at the end of
CC       embryogenesis (PubMed:10436051). Levels drop in the first larval stage
CC       and reach a very low level by the second larval stage which continues
CC       throughout the remainder of larval and most of pupal life
CC       (PubMed:10436051). Expression increases in late pupae and peaks once
CC       again in adults (PubMed:10436051). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10436051,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10749852, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19559693}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: RNAi-mediated knockdown in Akh-producing cells of
CC       the corpora cardiaca results in a significant decrease in combined
CC       glucose and trehalose levels (PubMed:20523747). RNAi-mediated knockdown
CC       results in defective muscle patterning (PubMed:27628033). RNAi-mediated
CC       knockdown results in a reduction in the pale ommatidia subtype
CC       (PubMed:28853393). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20523747,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27628033, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28853393}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the peptidase S8 family. Furin subfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- CAUTION: Has been shown in one study to be required for processing of
CC       sli into slit N-product and slit C-product (PubMed:27628033). However,
CC       has been shown in another study not to be required for sli cleavage
CC       with the protease required for sli cleavage being identified as tok
CC       (PubMed:27628033). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27628033}.
