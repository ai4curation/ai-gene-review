CC   -!- FUNCTION: E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase which accepts ubiquitin from an
CC       E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme in the form of a thioester and then
CC       directly transfers the ubiquitin to targeted substrates
CC       (PubMed:11703946, PubMed:12754252, PubMed:24302888). Down-regulates Dpp
CC       signaling after gastrulation by promoting MAD ubiquitination and
CC       subsequent degradation (PubMed:12754252). Acts as a positive regulator
CC       of smoothened signaling pathway by mediating ubiquitination of patched
CC       (ptc), leading to ptc endocytosis and lysosomal degradation
CC       (PubMed:24302888). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11703946,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12754252, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24302888}.
