# Efr (Q9W429) notes

- CG3774/Efr is the fly SLC35B4 ortholog; microsomes expressing it transport three nucleotide sugars
  [PMID:19948734 "CG3774 transport of GDP-fucose, UDP-GlcNAc, and UDP-xylose was more than 1.5 times that of control samples"]
  but not others [PMID:19948734 "CG3774 did not transport CMP-sialic acid, UDP-glucuronic acid, GDP-mannose, or UDP-glucose"].
- ER-resident via dilysine motif [PMID:19948734 "CG3774 was predominantly localized to the ER; therefore, we called it Efr"].
- Redundant with Gfr for Notch O-fucosylation; needed for heparan sulfate synthesis
  [PMID:19948734 "Efr is a multifunctional nucleotide sugar
transporter involved in the biosynthesis of heparan sulfate-glycosaminoglycan
