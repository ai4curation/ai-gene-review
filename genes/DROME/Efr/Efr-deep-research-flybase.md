# FlyBase gene description for Efr (DROME)

ER GDP-fucose transporter (Efr) encodes a multifunctional endoplasmic reticulum nucleotide sugar transporter that transports GDP-fucose, UDP-N-acetylglucosamine and UDP-xylose from the cytosol into the ER lumen.
