| **Direct fly identity and localization** | The predicted *Drosophila melanogaster* gene **CG30173** was cloned as **HSPC300**; antibody validation identified an approximately 8-kDa product. Transcripts were detected throughout development, and protein was enriched in the embryonic CNS, particularly longitudinal axon tracts, commissures, and motor axons. (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001) | Confirms that the research target is fly CG30173/HSPC300 rather than a similarly named gene. Localization is based on fixed-tissue immunolabeling, not live trafficking or ultrastructural analysis. | Qurashi et al.; **25 September 2007**; https://doi.org/10.1186/1749-8104-2-18 |
| **Direct fly biochemical evidence** | Anti-HSPC300 immunoprecipitation from S2-cell cytoplasmic extracts recovered **CYFIP and SCAR**, whereas control IgG did not. (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000016) | Establishes physical association with the fly WAVE/SCAR regulatory complex, but co-IP alone does not prove pairwise direct binding or identify contact residues. | Qurashi et al.; **25 September 2007**; https://doi.org/10.1186/1749-8104-2-18 |
| **Direct fly complex-stability evidence** | After maternal protein had faded, HSPC300-null third-instar larvae showed markedly reduced **CYFIP, Kette, SCAR, and Abi** abundance; zygotic-null embryos retained near-normal SCAR/CYFIP while maternal HSPC300 remained. (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000016) | Supports a dosage-sensitive role in maintaining WRC subunit stability in vivo. It does not establish the degradation mechanism or show that HSPC300 itself catalyzes actin nucleation. | Qurashi et al.; **25 September 2007**; https://doi.org/10.1186/1749-8104-2-18 |
| **Direct fly neuronal phenotype and rescue** | Mutant larval NMJs had normalized synapse length **1.36 × 10⁻³ ± 0.11 µm⁻¹**, versus **2.06 × 10⁻³ ± 0.137 µm⁻¹** in wild type—about **66%** of wild type—and **6.42 ± 0.60** buds versus **3.51 ± 0.39** (**88% more**); both comparisons had *p* < 0.001. Pan-neuronal expression rescued NMJ morphology, whereas muscle expression did not. (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012) | Strong gene-specific evidence for a presynaptic/motoneuronal role in NMJ morphogenesis. The phenotype supports WRC-dependent cytoskeletal remodeling but is not a direct actin-polymerization assay. | Qurashi et al.; **25 September 2007**; https://doi.org/10.1186/1749-8104-2-18 |
| **Fly dosage/genetic interaction** | Mean NMJ lengths were **87.46 ± 2.28 µm** in wild type, **68.18 ± 3.18 µm** in the CYFIP transheterozygote, and **62.00 ± 2.69 µm** after adding one mutant HSPC300 copy; the latter comparison had approximately **p = 0.142**. (pqac-00000010) | The direction is consistent with dosage interaction, but the reported 62.00-versus-68.18 µm difference was **not statistically significant** and should not be cited as definitive synergy. | Qurashi et al.; **25 September 2007**; https://doi.org/10.1186/1749-8104-2-18 |
| **Recent direct receptor–fly-WRC biochemistry** | In S2R+ cells and embryonic lysates, Frazzled co-immunoprecipitated with HSPC300; deleting or mutating Fra’s WIRS motif reduced association. Purified Fra cytoplasmic domain bound reconstituted fly WRC directly, whereas FraΔWIRS, mutation of the WRC WIRS-binding pocket, or competition by a wild-type WIRS peptide disrupted binding. (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000021) | Establishes a direct **Fra–intact-WRC** interaction and places the complex downstream of the netrin receptor. HSPC300 served as a WRC handle/subunit; the data do not show that Fra binds HSPC300 directly. | Chaudhari et al.; **October 2024**; https://doi.org/10.1126/scisignal.adk2345 |
| **Recent fly functional evidence with negative HSPC300 result** | FraΔWIRS failed to rescue midline crossing efficiently: **42%** of EW axons still failed to cross, versus **13%** after FraWT rescue and **56%** in *fra* mutants. In a FraΔcyto sensitized background, reducing CYFIP or SCAR increased non-crossing from **33%** to **49%** or **46%**, respectively, but removing one HSPC300 copy had **no effect**. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000020) | Strong evidence that Fra–WRC recruitment supports axon guidance, but no positive HSPC300-specific genetic effect was detected in this dosage assay. Maternal contribution, residual protein, or insufficient haploinsufficiency may limit interpretation. | Chaudhari et al.; **October 2024**; https://doi.org/10.1126/scisignal.adk2345 |
| **Cross-species structural/assembly inference** | In human cells, free BRK1 formed homotrimers, entered newly synthesized WAVE complexes, and restored complex assembly and morphology after BRK1 depletion; depletion reduced WAVE2, NAP1, and SRA1 abundance. (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000035) | Supports an evolutionarily plausible assembly-precursor/scaffold model for fly HSPC300, consistent with the fly stability phenotype. Because the mechanistic experiments were principally performed in human cells, this architecture remains an orthology-based inference for Q8MLQ0 rather than direct fly proof. | Derivery et al.; **18 June 2008**; https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0002462 |
