# HSPC300 (Brk1, Q8MLQ0) notes

- Indispensable WRC subunit in flies [PMID:17894861 "we demonstrate that HSPC300 is an indispensable component of the complex controlling axonal and neuromuscular junction (NMJ) growth"]; stability [PMID:17894861 "Moreover, HSPC300 loss of function conditions are marked by a decrease in all members of the WAVE/SCAR complex"]
- Brk1 trimer precursor (human; Drosophila Brk1 also tested) [PMID:18560548 "Drosophila and plant Brk1 proteins are likely to form trimers as demonstrated in the case of the human Brk1 protein."]
- Interestingly, in vitro HSPC300 is dispensable for assembly/Arp2/3 activation [PMID:17894861 "In addition, in in vitro experiments, lack of HSPC300 affects neither complex assembly [29] nor the complex's ability to activate Arp2/3 in vitro [22], two features that characterize other complex subunits."]

Decisions: general actin IEA/IBA terms MODIFIED to regulation of Arp2/3 complex-mediated actin nucleation; four protein binding rows (SCAR, AP-MS, SYG-1 WIRS assay) REMOVED.

## Deep research (falcon) follow-up

The falcon report identifies HSPC300 as a small structural/regulatory WRC subunit whose main demonstrated role in flies is maintaining abundance of the other subunits (Qurashi 2007), consistent with the review (no core MF; SCAR complex; regulation of Arp2/3 nucleation). It cites a 2024 study (Chaudhari et al., Sci Signal) in which Frazzled co-immunoprecipitates with tagged HSPC300 via its WIRS motif; that paper is not in GOA or the cache, so no annotations were added.
