Gene Description

Egfr encodes the Drosophila epidermal growth factor receptor, a plasma-membrane receptor tyrosine kinase activated by EGF-family ligands such as Spitz, Gurken, Vein, and Keren. Ligand-dependent Egfr activation signals primarily through the Drk/Sos/Ras85D/Raf/Dsor1/Rolled MAPK cascade to control embryonic patterning, photoreceptor recruitment, dorsal closure, tracheal branching, oogenesis, and other developmental responses.
