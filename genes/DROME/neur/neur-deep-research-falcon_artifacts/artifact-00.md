| Property | Molecular and biochemical annotation | Evidence and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Protein classification | Peripheral-membrane-associated **RING-finger E3 ubiquitin ligase** and positive regulator of ligand-dependent Notch signaling. As a RING E3, Neur positions a ubiquitin-charged E2 for direct transfer of ubiquitin to a recruited substrate. | The classification is supported by its C-terminal RING domain and experimentally established regulation of DSL ligands (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000050, pqac-00000053). |
| Domain structure | **N-terminal basic region:** contains the reported phospholipid-binding sequence KKIKKR. **NHR1:** recognizes ligand intracellular domains and contributes to Neur oligomerization. **NHR2:** supports oligomerization and full E3 activity but is less well characterized. **C-terminal RING finger:** recruits the E2–ubiquitin conjugate and supplies catalytic ligase activity. These features align with the supplied UniProt and InterPro annotations, including NHR, B30.2/SPRY-superfamily, Neuralized, and RING signatures. | Domain functions are supported by biochemical and functional studies of ligand recognition, membrane association, oligomerization, and catalysis (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000046, pqac-00000047). |
| Enzymatic function | Catalyzes ubiquitin transfer from an E2 conjugating enzyme to lysine residues in substrate intracellular domains. For DSL ligands, this modification principally promotes signaling-competent endocytosis and trafficking rather than being established primarily as a proteasomal-degradation signal. Neur can also undergo auto-ubiquitylation *in vitro*. | RING-dependent ubiquitin transfer and auto-ubiquitylation are supported experimentally, although the physiologically relevant ubiquitin-chain topology remains unresolved (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000047). |
| Substrate specificity | Best-supported substrates are the DSL Notch ligands **Delta (Dl)** and **Serrate (Ser)**, particularly their cytoplasmic intracellular domains. Delta K742 has been reported as a preferred Neur-modified lysine. Neur may also modify itself or another member of the Delta–Neur complex, so neither K742 nor Delta should be considered its exclusive site or substrate. | Delta and Serrate are established ligand targets, but the complete physiological substrate repertoire and ubiquitin-chain types remain incompletely defined (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000015). |
| Substrate-recognition motifs | NHR1 recognizes an **NxxN Neur-box** in ligand intracellular domains; the reported Delta sequence is **NEQN**. Functional mapping describes strong Delta binding through **icd1** and weaker binding through **icd2**. Bearded-family inhibitors contain a related Neur-binding motif and can interfere with ligand regulation. | Motif recognition explains selective recruitment of Delta and competitive inhibition by Bearded-family proteins (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000067, pqac-00000069). |
| Ubiquitin-independent function | Neur is a relatively stable component of a **Delta endocytic and signaling complex** and can act as an endocytic co-adaptor. Direct Neur–Delta association promotes dynamin-dependent internalization and weak-to-moderate Notch activation even when Neur lacks its RING domain or Delta lacks intracellular ubiquitin-acceptor lysines. Ubiquitination normally amplifies this basal adaptor activity. | Proposed amplification mechanisms include stronger cargo–adaptor engagement, coated-pit formation, mechanical pulling, ligand recycling, and relief of Delta–Notch cis-inhibition (pqac-00000031, pqac-00000039, pqac-00000041, pqac-00000044). |
| Subcellular localization | Neur is an intracellular **peripheral plasma-membrane and cortical protein** operating in the ligand-producing cell, not a secreted factor. It associates with Delta-containing membrane and endocytic puncta and co-traffics through endosomal compartments. During sensory-organ precursor mitosis, it concentrates at the antero-basal cortex and is inherited preferentially by the pIIb daughter. | Localization places Neur where it can couple Delta recognition to ligand internalization at specialized cell–cell signaling interfaces (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000020). |
| Key interacting proteins | **Direct or motif-supported partners:** Delta, Serrate, Neur itself through oligomerization, and inhibitory Bearded-family proteins. **Functional machinery:** ubiquitin-loaded E2 enzymes, dynamin-dependent endocytic machinery, and context-dependent adaptors such as Epsin or Liquid facets. **Related but not established here as a direct Neur-binding partner:** Mindbomb1, a distinct ligand E3 that can provide compensatory ubiquitination. Neur also regulates Crumbs–Stardust complex turnover in epithelial morphogenesis. | Evidence is strongest for direct Delta recognition; several other associations are functional or context-dependent and should not automatically be interpreted as direct physical binding (pqac-00000032, pqac-00000038, pqac-00000047, pqac-00000064, pqac-00000072, pqac-00000073). |


*Table: Molecular annotation of Drosophila Neuralized, integrating its RING-E3 activity, ligand-recognition domains, substrates, membrane localization, and separable endocytic-adaptor function. Qualifications distinguish established interactions from mechanistic proposals and unresolved substrate specificity.*
