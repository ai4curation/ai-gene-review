| Biological process or context | Role of Neuralized (Neur) | Key mechanisms |
|---|---|---|
| Notch signaling regulation | Acts in the signal-sending cell as a positive regulator of Delta- and Serrate-dependent Notch activation. Neur combines RING-dependent E3 ubiquitin-ligase activity with a separable endocytic-adaptor function. | Neur binds DSL-ligand intracellular domains, ubiquitylates Delta and Serrate, and promotes productive ligand endocytosis. Internalization of receptor-bound ligand generates force that exposes the Notch S2 cleavage site. A Delta–Neur complex can support signaling even when Delta ubiquitylation is impaired. (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000008, pqac-00000032) |
| Neural development | Controls Delta–Notch-dependent neural-versus-nonneural fate decisions and coordinates differentiation in embryonic CNS and optic-lobe lineages. | Proneural transcriptional programs induce neur in selected cells; Neur then regulates Delta abundance and activity non-cell-autonomously. Reduced Neur activity produces neurogenic defects consistent with insufficient Notch-mediated lateral inhibition. (pqac-00000021, pqac-00000026, pqac-00000028) |
| Sensory organ development | Helps select sensory organ precursors and subsequently establishes distinct pIIa and pIIb daughter-cell identities. | During sensory-organ-precursor division, Neur becomes enriched in pIIb, where it promotes Delta endocytosis and trans-activation of Notch in pIIa. Notch-active pIIa generates external sensory cells, whereas pIIb produces internal lineage cells. (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000023) |
| Asymmetric cell division | Functions as an asymmetrically inherited cell-fate determinant that biases the direction of Notch signaling between daughter cells. | Neur localizes to the antero-basal cortex during mitosis and segregates preferentially into the anterior pIIb daughter at cytokinesis. Its polarized inheritance makes pIIb the Delta-sending cell and pIIa the Notch-receiving cell. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000025) |
| Lateral inhibition | Strengthens signal-sending capacity in selected neural or sensory precursors, enabling them to suppress equivalent fates in neighboring cells through Notch. | Neur-dependent Delta trafficking and endocytosis support directional trans-signaling. Notch activity in adjacent cells represses proneural fate, refining spatial patterns of neural and sensory precursors. (pqac-00000021, pqac-00000022, pqac-00000029) |
| Neural stem-cell progression | Regulates the transition of optic-lobe neuroepithelial cells through PI and PII progenitor states toward neuroblast identity. | Lethal of scute induces neur at the transition zone. Neur alters Delta levels and produces dynamic Notch activity: high in PI progenitors, reduced in PII progenitors, and elevated again after neuroblast formation. (pqac-00000026) |
| Epithelial morphogenesis | Couples cell-fate specification to epithelial polarity, apical constriction, contractility, and tissue remodeling, including functions beyond canonical Delta regulation. | Neur regulates the apical Crumbs complex through Stardust isoforms and contributes to junctional myosin organization and apical constriction. Bearded-family inhibitors spatially restrain Neur activity during epithelial remodeling and furrow invagination. (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000027, pqac-00000053) |
