# Comprehensive Research Report: Drosophila melanogaster Neuralized (neur) Gene

## Gene Identity Verification

The gene **neur** (UniProt: P29503) in *Drosophila melanogaster* encodes the protein Neuralized, a highly conserved E3 ubiquitin ligase that plays central roles in Notch signaling and neural development. This protein contains characteristic Neuralized Homology Repeat (NHR) domains and a C-terminal RING finger domain, consistent with the UniProt annotation for domains B30.2/SPRY_sf, NHR_dom, and Znf_RING (liu2017thenhrdomains pages 4-4, liu2017thenhrdomains pages 4-6, seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

## Primary Molecular Function

### E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Activity

Neuralized functions as a RING-type E3 ubiquitin ligase with a C3HC4 RING finger domain that catalyzes the transfer of ubiquitin to lysine residues in substrate proteins (liu2017thenhrdomains pages 4-4, liu2017thenhrdomains pages 4-6, seib2021theroleof pages 8-9). The RING domain recruits ubiquitin-charged E2 enzymes and facilitates ubiquitin conjugation to target substrates, particularly the Notch ligands Delta and Serrate (liu2017thenhrdomains pages 4-6, taal2019neuralizedfamilymember pages 1-2). Recent studies from 2023 have revealed that Neuralized has a **dual molecular function**: it acts both as a catalytic ubiquitin ligase and as a non-catalytic endocytic adaptor (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15).

### Substrate Specificity

The primary substrates of Neuralized are the Delta-Serrate-LAG2 (DSL) family Notch ligands, **Delta (Dl)** and **Serrate (Ser)** (liu2017thenhrdomains pages 4-4, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 1-2, troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2). Neuralized preferentially ubiquitinates Delta at **lysine K742**, though it can modify other lysines in the Delta intracellular domain (ICD) (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19). Importantly, 2023 research has shown that Neuralized's interaction with Delta—rather than ubiquitination per se—is the critical requirement for basal signaling activity (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 1-2). Neuralized may also ubiquitinate other components of the Delta–Neuralized complex, potentially including itself, though the precise additional substrates remain to be fully identified (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19).

The **NHR1 domain** directly binds the intracellular domains of Delta and Serrate through recognition of an NxxN consensus motif (the "Neur-box," which is NEQN in Drosophila Delta) (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9, liu2017thenhrdomains pages 4-6). ## Subcellular Localization

Neuralized is classified as a **peripheral membrane protein** rather than an integral membrane protein (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9, contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 11-11, liu2017thenhrdomains pages 6-6). It localizes to the cytoplasmic face of the plasma membrane through its N-terminal basic region (residues 82-87: KKIKKR), which binds specific plasma membrane phospholipids (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9). Neuralized also localizes to **intracellular endocytic vesicles and puncta**, where it co-traffics with Delta through the endosomal pathway (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15). This dual localization—at the plasma membrane and within endosomes—reflects Neuralized's role in both initiating and sustaining Delta endocytosis (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19).

## Mechanism of Delta Endocytosis and Notch Activation

Recent research has elucidated how Neuralized promotes Delta endocytosis to enable Notch receptor activation. Neuralized ubiquitinates Delta and triggers its internalization from the plasma membrane (papagiannouli2022endocytosisatthe pages 6-9, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10). This endocytosis is not merely degradative; it serves a **mechanotransductive function** in Notch activation (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 1-2, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19). When Delta, bound to Notch on a neighboring cell, undergoes endocytosis, it generates a pulling force on the Notch extracellular domain. This force induces a conformational change in Notch's negative regulatory region (NRR), exposing the normally buried S2 cleavage site to ADAM metalloproteases (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 1-2, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 19-20). Subsequent proteolytic processing releases the Notch intracellular domain (NICD), which translocates to the nucleus to activate target gene expression (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 1-2).

Importantly, 2023 studies have shown that Neuralized can promote Delta endocytosis through both RING-dependent and RING-independent mechanisms (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15). The protein functions as an endocytic adaptor that recruits or organizes the endocytic machinery through dynamin-dependent pathways (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19). Ubiquitination enhances this process but is not absolutely required for moderate signaling activity in certain developmental contexts, particularly in the central nervous system (CNS) lineages (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 1-2, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15).

### Neural Development

Neuralized plays essential roles in neural development by controlling Delta-Notch-dependent fate decisions (contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 11-11, contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 1-3, liu2017thenhrdomains pages 6-6). In the developing optic lobe, the proneural transcription factor Lethal of scute (L'sc) induces *neur* expression at the transition zone where neuroepithelial cells progress through progenitor states to become neuroblasts (contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 1-3). Neuralized regulates Delta levels, producing dynamic Notch activity that is high in PI progenitors, reduced in PII progenitors, and elevated again after neuroblast formation (contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 1-3). This dynamic regulation coordinates neural stem cell state progression and differentiation (contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 11-11, contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 1-3).

### Asymmetric Cell Division

Neuralized functions as an asymmetrically inherited cell-fate determinant in multiple developmental contexts (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 1-2, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 4-6). During sensory organ precursor (SOP) division, Neuralized localizes to the antero-basal cortex during mitosis and segregates preferentially into the anterior pIIb daughter cell at cytokinesis (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10). In pIIb, Neuralized ubiquitinates Delta and promotes its endocytosis, leading to trans-activation of Notch in the neighboring pIIa cell (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10). This asymmetric distribution establishes distinct daughter cell fates: Notch-active pIIa generates external sensory cells, whereas pIIb produces internal lineage cells (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 1-2, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10).

### Lateral Inhibition

Through its regulation of Delta trafficking and endocytosis, Neuralized strengthens the signal-sending capacity of selected neural or sensory precursors, enabling them to suppress equivalent fates in neighboring cells via Notch-mediated lateral inhibition (contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 11-11, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 1-2, papagiannouli2022endocytosisatthe pages 6-9). This process refines spatial patterns of neural and sensory precursors during development (contreras2018dynamicnotchsignalling pages 11-11, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 1-2).

### Epithelial Morphogenesis

Beyond its canonical role in Delta regulation, Neuralized contributes to epithelial polarity and morphogenesis (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 17-18, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 16-17). It regulates the apical Crumbs complex through specific Stardust isoforms and influences junctional myosin organization and apical constriction (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 17-18, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 16-17). Bearded-family proteins spatially restrain Neuralized activity during epithelial remodeling and furrow invagination (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 17-18, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 16-17).

## Relationship with Mindbomb1 (Mib1)

Neuralized and Mindbomb1 (Mib1) are distinct E3 ubiquitin ligases that both regulate Notch ligands but through different mechanisms (guo2016structureandfunction pages 1-2, troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2, seib2021theroleof pages 8-9). While both can ubiquitinate Delta and promote Notch signaling, they are structurally unrelated and recognize non-overlapping epitopes on the ligand intracellular domain (guo2016structureandfunction pages 1-2, seib2021theroleof pages 8-9). In *Drosophila*, Mib1 and Neuralized act in largely complementary developmental contexts, though Neuralized can compensate for Mib1 loss in some processes such as wing development (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

Recent 2023-2025 research has revealed a key mechanistic distinction: **Mib1-dependent Delta signaling requires ubiquitination**, whereas **Neuralized can activate Delta through ubiquitination-independent mechanisms** (troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15, vullings2025anothertailof pages 1-2). In Neuralized-controlled processes such as neural development, Delta can signal effectively even when intracellular lysines are mutated to prevent ubiquitination, particularly because Neuralized also functions as an endocytic co-adaptor (troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15). By contrast, Mib1-mediated activation depends more strictly on its E3 ligase activity and Delta lysine ubiquitination (troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2, vullings2025anothertailof pages 1-2). The two ligases can act cooperatively: when Neuralized's catalytic activity is compromised, Mib1-mediated ubiquitination can enhance Delta-Neuralized signaling (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19).

## Regulation by Bearded Family Proteins

Bearded-family proteins function as negative regulators or inhibitors of Neuralized activity during *Drosophila* development (liu2017thenhrdomains pages 6-6, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 16-17). They suppress Neuralized-mediated endocytosis of Delta and thereby modulate Delta signaling activity (liu2017thenhrdomains pages 6-6). The regulation involves a conserved Neuralized-binding motif present in both Bearded proteins and Delta, suggesting competitive or regulatory interactions through this shared binding interface (liu2017thenhrdomains pages 6-6). This regulatory mechanism provides spatial and temporal control over Neuralized function during processes such as epithelial morphogenesis and contractility (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 16-17).

## Current Understanding and Recent Developments (2023-2024)

The most significant recent advances in understanding Neuralized function have come from studies published in 2023-2024 that have separated its catalytic E3 ligase activity from its non-catalytic adaptor function. Kalodimou et al. (2023) demonstrated that Neuralized's physical interaction with Delta is more critical than ubiquitination per se for basal signaling, revealing that Neuralized forms an active Delta–Neuralized signaling complex that promotes force-generating endocytosis (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 1-2). Troost et al. (2023) showed that Delta can signal without ubiquitination of its intracellular domain in Neuralized-dependent but not Mindbomb1-dependent processes, establishing Neuralized as a bifunctional protein with both enzymatic and scaffolding activities (troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2). Vüllings et al. (2025) further clarified that Delta signaling comprises multiple modes—ubiquitination-independent, Neuralized-dependent/ubiquitination-independent, and ubiquitination-dependent—that together produce full Notch activation (vullings2025anothertailof pages 1-2).

## Summary

Neuralized is a multifunctional RING-type E3 ubiquitin ligase that serves as a central regulator of Notch signaling in *Drosophila melanogaster*. Its primary substrates are the Notch ligands Delta and Serrate, which it ubiquitinates primarily at Delta K742. Structurally, Neuralized integrates membrane targeting (via its N-terminal basic region), substrate recognition (via NHR1), oligomerization and catalytic enhancement (via NHR2), and ubiquitin ligase activity (via its C-terminal RING domain). The protein functions as both a catalytic enzyme and a non-catalytic endocytic adaptor, promoting Delta internalization through dynamin-dependent pathways. This endocytosis generates mechanical pulling forces that conformationally activate the Notch receptor in neighboring cells. Neuralized localizes to the plasma membrane and intracellular endocytic vesicles, co-trafficking with Delta through the endosomal pathway. It plays essential roles in neural development, sensory organ formation, asymmetric cell division, lateral inhibition, neural stem cell progression, and epithelial morphogenesis. Recent research has established that Neuralized can promote Notch signaling through both ubiquitination-dependent and ubiquitination-independent mechanisms, distinguishing it from the related E3 ligase Mindbomb1 and revealing the mechanistic complexity underlying developmental Notch pathway regulation.
