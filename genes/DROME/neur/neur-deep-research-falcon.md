# Comprehensive Research Report: Drosophila melanogaster neuralized (neur) Gene

## Gene Identity and Verification

The research target has been verified as the *Drosophila melanogaster* **neuralized** gene (gene symbol: *neur*; synonyms: *neu*; UniProt: P29503; CG11988), encoding a protein with the full name "Protein neuralized" (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 2-4). The protein contains the expected domain architecture annotated in UniProt and InterPro: Neuralized Homology Repeat domains (NHR_dom, IPR006573), a RING zinc-finger domain (Znf_RING, IPR001841), B30.2/SPRY superfamily assignment (IPR043136), and the Neuralized signature (IPR037962) (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9). This gene identity matches the query specifications.

## 1. Molecular Function and Enzymatic Activity

### 1.1 E3 Ubiquitin Ligase Activity

Neuralized is a **RING-finger E3 ubiquitin ligase** that functions as a positive regulator of ligand-dependent Notch signaling in *Drosophila melanogaster* (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2, lv2024evolutionandfunction pages 2-4). As a RING-domain E3 enzyme, Neuralized does not transiently accept ubiquitin on a catalytic cysteine but rather positions a ubiquitin-loaded E2 conjugating enzyme to facilitate direct ubiquitin transfer to recruited substrate proteins (hale2025emergingrolesfor pages 1-2, hale2025emergingrolesfor pages 2-4).

The C-terminal RING finger domain catalyzes the transfer of ubiquitin from an E2 enzyme to lysine residues in the intracellular domains (ICDs) of substrate proteins (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2). In vitro studies demonstrate that Neuralized can undergo auto-ubiquitylation, though its principal physiological activity is substrate modification rather than self-regulation (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

### 1.2 Substrate Specificity

The best-established substrates of Neuralized are the **DSL (Delta/Serrate/Lag-2) family Notch ligands**, specifically **Delta (Dl)** and **Serrate (Ser)** (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9, troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2). Recent evidence from Kalodimou et al. (2023) indicates that Neuralized preferentially ubiquitinates Delta at **lysine 742 (K742)**, though this is not its exclusive modification site (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19).

Importantly, the complete substrate repertoire remains incompletely defined. Studies suggest that Neuralized may also ubiquitinate other components of the Delta–Neuralized signaling complex, potentially including Neuralized itself or associated endocytic adaptors (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 10-14). The substrate specificity is mediated by recognition of conserved intracellular motifs rather than indiscriminate bulk ubiquitination (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

### 1.3 Substrate Recognition Mechanism

Substrate recognition by Neuralized is mediated primarily by its **N-terminal Neuralized Homology Repeat 1 (NHR1) domain**, which recognizes a conserved **NxxN consensus sequence** termed the "Neur-box" in ligand intracellular domains (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9). In Drosophila Delta, this motif is **NEQN** (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9). Functional mapping studies have identified Delta's intracellular domain contains at least two Neuralized-binding regions: **icd1** (which provides strong interaction through the NxxN motif) and **icd2** (which provides weaker interaction) (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 10-14, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 15-17).

The NHR1 domain also mediates Neuralized homo-oligomerization, while the less well-characterized **NHR2 domain** contributes to oligomerization and supports full E3 ubiquitin ligase activity (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9). An N-terminal basic region containing the sequence **KKIKKR** targets Neuralized to specific plasma membrane phospholipids, positioning it near its ligand substrates at the membrane (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

### 1.4 Dual Molecular Function: A Paradigm Shift

A major advance from recent studies (2017-2024) has been the recognition that Neuralized possesses **separable ubiquitin-dependent and ubiquitin-independent functions** (berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 19-21, troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15). Beyond its E3 ligase activity, Neuralized functions as a relatively stable **endocytic co-adaptor** that directly associates with Delta to form a functional Delta–Neuralized signaling complex (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2).

Importantly, this adaptor function can promote weak-to-moderate Delta-dependent Notch activation even when Neuralized lacks its RING catalytic domain or when Delta lacks ubiquitin-acceptor lysines (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 19-21, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19). Ubiquitination normally amplifies this basal adaptor activity, likely by strengthening cargo–adaptor interactions, promoting coated-pit formation, increasing endocytic pulling force, facilitating ligand recycling, or relieving inhibitory cis-interactions between Delta and Notch (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19).

The 2023 study by Troost et al. demonstrated that unlike Mindbomb1 (Mib1)-dependent processes—which strictly require Delta ubiquitylation—Neuralized-controlled neural development can proceed efficiently even when Delta's intracellular lysines cannot be ubiquitinated (troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2). This finding revised the earlier model that Delta ubiquitination is absolutely required for all Notch signaling.

## 2. Subcellular Localization

Neuralized is an **intracellular, peripheral plasma membrane-associated protein** that operates in the ligand-producing (signal-sending) cell, not as a secreted factor or extracellular component (berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 18-19). It localizes to Delta-containing membrane sites and endocytic puncta, co-trafficking with Delta through endosomal compartments (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15).

During asymmetric cell divisions in sensory organ precursor (SOP) cells, Neuralized exhibits striking polarized localization: it concentrates at the **antero-basal cortex** during mitosis and becomes unequally inherited by the **pIIb daughter cell** at cytokinesis (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 7-8). This asymmetric distribution contributes to the differential activation of Notch signaling that establishes distinct pIIa and pIIb cell fates (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 1-2).

Neuralized also localizes to specialized receptor–ligand signaling clusters at daughter-cell interfaces, particularly the basolateral membrane regions where Delta endocytosis and Notch activation occur (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 7-8). The N-terminal basic region (KKIKKR) mediates membrane association through specific phospholipid binding, ensuring proper positioning for substrate recognition and modification (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

## 3. Role in Notch Signaling Pathway

### 3.1 Position in the Pathway

Neuralized is a positive regulator of canonical Delta/Serrate–Notch signaling that acts on the **ligand side** of the pathway rather than by directly modifying the Notch receptor (lv2024evolutionandfunction pages 2-4, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 2-4, sachan2023notchsignallingmultifaceted pages 10-11). It functions intracellularly in the **signal-sending cell** to control the signaling competence of membrane-bound ligand, enabling trans-activation of Notch in adjacent signal-receiving cells (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 27-28).

### 3.2 Mechanism of Notch Activation

The mechanistic model, supported by recent structural and functional studies, proceeds as follows:

1. **Ligand recognition and complex formation**: Neuralized binds to the intracellular domain of Delta through its NHR1 domain, recognizing the NxxN/NEQN Neur-box motif (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19).

2. **Ubiquitination (context-dependent)**: The RING domain catalyzes ubiquitin transfer to Delta lysines (preferentially K742) and possibly other complex components (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

3. **Endocytic activation**: Neuralized promotes Delta endocytosis through a dynamin-dependent pathway, functioning both as an E3 ligase and as an endocytic adaptor that helps organize the internalization machinery (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15).

4. **Mechanical force generation**: Ligand internalization generates pulling forces on the ligand-bound Notch receptor in the adjacent cell. This mechanical stress induces a conformational change that exposes the otherwise masked S2 protease cleavage site in Notch's negative regulatory region (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, lv2024evolutionandfunction pages 2-4, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19).

5. **Receptor processing**: ADAM metalloproteases cleave at S2, producing the NEXT intermediate. Subsequent γ-secretase cleavage releases the **Notch intracellular domain (NICD)**, which translocates to the nucleus and activates Notch-responsive gene transcription through RBPJ/CSL and Mastermind-like transcriptional coactivators (lv2024evolutionandfunction pages 2-4).

This mechanism directly connects Neuralized-mediated ligand trafficking to the mechanotransduction step that initiates canonical Notch signaling (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, lv2024evolutionandfunction pages 2-4).

### 3.3 Developmental Processes Requiring Neuralized

**Embryonic neurogenesis and lateral inhibition**: Neuralized is a predominant ligand-activating factor during the selection of embryonic neuroblasts through lateral inhibition (sachan2023notchsignallingmultifaceted pages 4-4, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 2-4). In this process, cells expressing high levels of Delta activate Notch in neighboring cells, suppressing their neural fate and causing them to adopt epidermal identity. Loss of *neur* causes excess neural tissue at the expense of ventral epidermis—the classic embryonic neurogenic phenotype—and can result in embryonic lethality (sachan2023notchsignallingmultifaceted pages 4-4, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15).

**CNS neuronal lineages**: In larval brain and ventral nerve cord lineages, Neuralized is the major activator of Delta during ganglion mother cell (GMC) sibling specification (berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 18-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 5-7, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15). After a GMC divides, Delta–Notch signaling induces different fates in the two newborn neurons. Berndt et al. (2017) demonstrated that *neur* loss abolished the Notch target gene *Hey* expression in approximately 80% of mutant GMC lineages; combined loss of *neur* and *mib1* increased the defect to 100%, whereas *mib1* alone had only an accessory role (berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 18-19).

**Sensory organ precursor lineage**: During sensory organ precursor mitosis, Neuralized's asymmetric inheritance establishes signaling directionality. Accumulated in the pIIb daughter cell, Neuralized promotes Delta endocytosis, which activates Notch in the adjacent pIIa daughter, thereby establishing distinct pIIb and pIIa fates that give rise to different components of the sensory organ (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 1-2).

**Epithelial morphogenesis**: Beyond its canonical Notch signaling role, Neuralized regulates epithelial polarity and morphogenesis through control of Crumbs–Stardust complex endocytosis and turnover (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 16-17, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 19-20). This function links Neuralized to broader tissue organization and contractility regulation during developmental processes such as furrow invagination (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 16-17).

### 3.4 Relationship with Mindbomb1 (Mib1)

Neuralized and Mindbomb1 are distinct RING-finger E3 ubiquitin ligases with partly overlapping but non-identical functions in DSL ligand activation (berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 2-3, seib2021theroleof pages 8-9, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2). Key differences include:

**Expression patterns**: Neuralized is enriched in mesoderm, neuroectoderm, and neural precursor cells, whereas Mib1 is more ubiquitously expressed and therefore supports most DSL signaling throughout development (berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 2-3).

**Structural organization**: Mib1 is a large cortical protein with multiple ligand-binding MZM and REP domains, ankyrin repeats, and three RING fingers. Neuralized is a smaller peripheral membrane protein with two NHR domains and a single C-terminal RING finger (seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

**Ligand recognition**: Neuralized binds Delta primarily through the icd1/NxxN motif and forms a relatively stable Delta–Neuralized complex. Mib1 recognizes different intracellular motifs (icd2 and icd3/N-box and C-box regions) and appears to interact more transiently (berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 2-3, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19).

**Ubiquitination dependence**: Mib1-dependent signaling processes strictly require Delta ubiquitylation for full activity. In contrast, Neuralized-controlled neural development can proceed efficiently even when Delta intracellular lysines cannot be ubiquitinated, because Neuralized can function as an endocytic co-adaptor independently of its catalytic activity (troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2).

**Tissue-specific requirements**: Neuralized predominates in embryonic neurogenesis and CNS lineages, whereas Delta signaling in the wing pouch—where Neuralized is not expressed—depends entirely on Mib1 (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15, berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2). Nevertheless, they can partially compensate for each other in some developmental contexts, including during wing development (berndt2017ubiquitylationindependentactivationof pages 1-2, seib2021theroleof pages 8-9).

**Functional complementation**: When Neuralized's RING domain is inactive, Mib1-mediated ubiquitination of Delta can restore enhancement of Delta–Neuralized signaling activity, indicating that the two ligases can cooperate and that ubiquitin supply from either source can amplify the basal Delta–Neuralized adaptor complex (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15).

## 4. Recent Developments (2023-2024)

Several landmark studies have advanced our understanding of Neuralized function:

**Troost et al. (2023, BMC Biology)**: Demonstrated that a Delta knock-in allele lacking all intracellular lysines (DlK2R) can support completion of *Drosophila* development, revealing that ubiquitylation is not absolutely required for Neuralized-dependent processes but is specifically required for Mib1-dependent signaling (troost2023themeaningof pages 1-2). This study established that Neuralized can activate Delta through both ubiquitin-dependent and ubiquitin-independent mechanisms.

**Kalodimou et al. (2023, Cells)**: Provided detailed evidence for the separable roles of Neuralized's adaptor and catalytic functions in Delta signaling in CNS lineages (kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 17-19, kalodimou2023separablerolesfor pages 14-15). Showed that the Delta–Neuralized interaction itself is more important for basal signaling than ubiquitylation, while ubiquitin enhances the efficiency of the preformed complex.

**Pinot & Le Borgne (2024, Cells)**: Reviewed the spatiotemporal regulation of Notch activation during asymmetric cell division in sensory organ precursors, emphasizing Neuralized's role in establishing signaling directionality through its polarized inheritance and localized activity (pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 8-10, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 7-8, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 16-17, pinot2024spatiotemporalregulationof pages 1-2).

**Lv et al. (2024, International Journal of Molecular Sciences)**: Provided an evolutionary perspective on Notch signaling components, including Neuralized, across invertebrate phyla, highlighting the conserved role of E3 ubiquitin ligases in ligand activation and mechanotransduction (lv2024evolutionandfunction pages 2-4).

**Sachan et al. (2024, The FEBS Journal)**: Published a comprehensive review on Notch signaling's multifaceted roles in development and disease, positioning Neuralized within the broader regulatory network of E3 ubiquitin ligases that control pathway activity (sachan2023notchsignallingmultifaceted pages 4-4, sachan2023notchsignallingmultifaceted pages 10-11).

## 6. Conclusions

The *Drosophila melanogaster* **neuralized** gene (UniProt P29503) encodes a multifunctional RING-finger E3 ubiquitin ligase that serves as a key regulator of Notch signaling by controlling DSL ligand activation. Recent research has fundamentally revised our understanding of Neuralized function, demonstrating that it possesses both ubiquitin-dependent catalytic activity and a separable ubiquitin-independent endocytic adaptor function.

**Primary molecular function**: Neuralized catalyzes ubiquitin transfer to lysine residues in the intracellular domains of Delta and Serrate ligands, with preferential modification of Delta K742. Its NHR1 domain recognizes the NxxN/NEQN Neur-box motif in ligands, while its C-terminal RING domain provides E3 ligase activity.

**Subcellular localization**: Neuralized is a peripheral plasma membrane-associated protein that localizes to the antero-basal cortex during asymmetric divisions and associates with Delta in endocytic compartments within signal-sending cells.

**Signaling pathway role**: Neuralized functions in the signal-sending cell to promote Delta endocytosis and activation, generating mechanical forces that expose the Notch receptor's S2 cleavage site in neighboring cells. This initiates the canonical Notch signaling cascade leading to NICD nuclear translocation and target gene transcription.

**Developmental processes**: Neuralized is essential for embryonic neurogenesis through lateral inhibition, GMC sibling specification in CNS lineages, sensory organ precursor cell fate decisions, and epithelial morphogenesis. It works cooperatively with Mindbomb1, with tissue-specific division of labor.

**Current understanding**: The 2023-2024 literature establishes that Neuralized's adaptor function can support moderate Delta signaling independently of ubiquitination, while ubiquitin amplifies the efficiency of the Delta–Neuralized complex. This dual functionality explains how Neuralized-dependent processes can tolerate loss of Delta ubiquitylation better than Mib1-dependent processes, representing a major mechanistic insight into context-dependent Notch signaling regulation.
