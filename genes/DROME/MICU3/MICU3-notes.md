# MICU3 (CG4662) review notes

UniProt A0A0B4KHF1 (TrEMBL, isoform D); PANTHER PTHR12294:SF13.

## Literature journal

- Not a major gatekeeper [PMID:31042479 "These results are consistent with MICU3 not being a major uniporter gatekeeper in vivo."]; cannot replace MICU1 [PMID:31042479 "indicating that MICU3 is not functionally equivalent to MICU1 in vivo"].
- Neuronal role [PMID:31042479 "These results indicate a function for MICU3 in proper maintenance of neuronal function, consistent with its neuronally restricted expression."]
- Enhancer [PMID:31042479 "The enhanced phenotype of MCU:MICU3 co-expression is consistent with a report that MICU3 enhances MCU-mediated Ca2+ uptake"].

## Curation decisions

- Calcium channel regulator activity ACCEPTED as core MF. Uniporter module conventions as in MCU-notes.md.

## Deep research

`MICU3-deep-research-falcon.md` (falcon) agrees: MICU3 is an EF-hand regulator of MCU-EMRE calcium uptake, not the principal gatekeeper; mammalian MICU3 forms a disulfide-linked heterodimer with MICU1 and enhances uptake, but no fly study has measured MICU3 sub-mitochondrial localization or MICU3-dependent calcium flux. It also cites a 2023 RNAi climbing defect and a 2020 thesis (not cached here; not used for annotations).
