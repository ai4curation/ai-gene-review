# FlyBase gene description for Actbeta (DROME)

Activin-&bgr; (Act&bgr;) encodes a ligand of the TGF beta superfamily. It signals through the type I receptor encoded by babo and the Smad family member encoded by Smox. Act&bgr; is expressed primarily in the central and peripheral nervous systems and its product regulates aspects of metabolism, insulin signaling, hemocyte development and body size.
