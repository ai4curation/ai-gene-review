CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the 9-1-1 checkpoint clamp complex
CC       (PubMed:17327271, PubMed:22666434). Involved in both meiotic and
CC       somatic DNA damage responses (PubMed:17327271). Essential for
CC       activation of the meiotic checkpoint in response to double-strand DNA
CC       breaks; required for the S-phase checkpoint but not the G2-M phase
CC       checkpoint (PubMed:17327271). Involved in double strand break repair by
CC       homologous recombination during meiosis; influences the organization of
CC       chromosomal DNA in the meiotic nucleus (PubMed:17327271,
CC       PubMed:19501158). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17327271,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19501158, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22666434}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the 9-1-1 checkpoint clamp complex consisting of
CC       Rad9 isoform A, Rad1 and Hus1-like; the interactions with Rad1 and Rad9
CC       are direct (PubMed:17327271, PubMed:22666434). This complex probably
CC       also forms with Rad9 isoform B, however 9-1-1 complex containing Rad9
CC       isoform A localizes to the nuclear periphery (PubMed:22666434).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17327271, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22666434}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cytoplasm {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22666434}. Nucleus
CC       envelope {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22666434}. Note=Localizes to the nuclear
CC       periphery when part of the 9-1-1 complex with Rad9 isoform A and Rad1.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22666434}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in ovary. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17327271}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Viable but females are sterile (PubMed:17327271).
CC       Aberrant formation of the karyosome, the compact spherical cluster of
CC       condensed chromosomes that forms in the oocyte nucleus during meiotic
CC       prophase (PubMed:17327271). Aberrant synoptenemal complex disassembly
CC       in the oocyte; possibly contributing to the karyosome phenotype
CC       (PubMed:19501158). The karyosome and synaptonemal complex phenotypes
CC       are caused by persistent DNA double stranded breaks that activate a
CC       CHK2 meiotic checkpoint pathway (PubMed:19501158). Activation of the
CC       meiotic checkpoint leads to defective oocyte localization
CC       (PubMed:19501158). Mutant flies are sensitive to genotoxic stress
CC       caused by hydroxyurea and methyl methanesulfonate, but not by X-ray
CC       irradiation (PubMed:17327271). When combined with disruption of Brca2
CC       females do not lay eggs and have reduced size ovaries with apoptotic
CC       egg chambers (PubMed:19501158). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17327271,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19501158}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the HUS1 family.
