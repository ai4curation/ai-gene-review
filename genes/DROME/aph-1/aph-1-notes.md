# aph-1 (Drosophila melanogaster, Q9VQG2) notes

## Identity
- Seven-TM subunit of gamma-secretase; scaffold that binds nicastrin and stabilises the presenilin holoprotein during complex assembly.

## Literature
- Discovery and conservation [PMID:12110170 "A C. elegans genetic screen revealed two genes, aph-1 and pen-2, encoding multipass transmembrane proteins, that interact strongly with sel-12/presenilin and aph-2/nicastrin."]; fly S2 RNAi [PMID:12110170 "RNAi-mediated inactivation of aph-1, pen-2, or nicastrin in cultured Drosophila cells reduces gamma-secretase cleavage of betaAPP and Notch substrates and reduces the levels of processed presenilin."]; [PMID:12110170 "aph-1 and pen-2, like nicastrin, are required for the activity and accumulation of gamma-secretase."]
- Stabilises Psn [PMID:12660785 "Here we show that Drosophila APH-1 (Aph-1) increases the stability of Drosophila presenilin (Psn) holoprotein in the complex."]; [PMID:12660785 "APH-1 stabilizes the presenilin holoprotein in the complex"].
- Reconstitution [PMID:20421416 "coexpression of these four components promotes the efficient assembly of mature, proteolytically active gamma-secretase"].

## Curation thoughts
- protein-macromolecule adaptor activity (IBA) fits the scaffolding role; core.
- Decisions on shared gamma-secretase terms made consistently with Nct and pen-2 (ectodomain proteolysis -> MODIFY; amyloid-beta formation -> non-core).
