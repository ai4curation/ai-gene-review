# rod (Rough deal; Q9V9W7) notes

## Identity
- KNTC1/ROD family (PANTHER PTHR15688), large ~2089 aa beta-propeller/alpha-solenoid protein; largest RZZ subunit.

## Literature journal
- rod mutants missegregate sister chromatids; Rod needed to recruit Zw10 and dynein [PMID:10523511 "The activity provided by rod also appears to be necessary for the recruitment of two known kinetochore components, Zw10 and cytoplasmic dynein"]. Dynamic localization [PMID:10523511 "localizing first to kinetochores in prometaphase, but moving to kinetochore microtubules at metaphase"].
- Required for SAC [PMID:11146659 "Rough Deal (Rod) and Zeste-White 10 (Zw10) are required for the proper functioning of the metaphase checkpoint in flies"].
- Rod/Zw10 complex [PMID:11590237 "ROD and ZW10 proteins are in fact physically associated"].
- GFP-Rod streams from kinetochores along microtubules; proposed corona component [PMID:14711415 "We propose that the Rod complex is a major component of the fibrous corona"].
- Mad2 recruitment [PMID:15886105 "Rod/Zw10 thus contributes to checkpoint activation by promoting Mad2 recruitment and to checkpoint inactivation by recruiting dynein/dynactin"].
- rod(Z3) maternal-effect allele: RZZ not recruited in syncytial embryos, no SAC [PMID:25616898 "Consequently, the embryos have no spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC), and syncytial mitoses are profoundly perturbed"].
- Golgi role [PMID:22685323 "Rod is concentrated at the Golgi but not at the ER"; "Rod plays a Golgi-related function that is not required for spermatocyte cytokinesis"].
- Rab18 binding [PMID:25453831 "Antisera against two NRZ subunits confirmed the interaction with Rab18 and showed it to be GTP specific"].
- rod RNAi used as tool to induce CIN [PMID:26859353] - downstream consequences only.
