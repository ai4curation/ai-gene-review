# FlyBase gene description for IKKbeta (DROME)

I-kappaB kinase &bgr; (IKK&bgr;) encodes a a component of the I&kgr;B Kinase (IKK) complex together with the product of key. IKK complex participates in the activation of the transcription factor encoded by Rel in the immune deficiency pathway. This pathway regulates the antibacterial response and other less characterized cellular processes.
