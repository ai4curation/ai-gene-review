# aust (Australin, Drosophila melanogaster) curation notes

Accession: Q9VLD7 (CG17009).

Deep research: `aust-deep-research-falcon.md` (falcon; completed after the review was first written). It confirms Aust as a testis-specific Borealin paralog lacking ~140 central residues of Borr, required for male meiotic cohesion, kinetochore CPC recruitment, alignment, segregation and central spindle/cytokinesis, and able to substitute for Borr in S2 cells; it notes the Borr-Shrub (ESCRT-III) interaction maps to the Borr region absent from Aust, a proposed reason for the paralog switch. No change to annotation decisions.

## Literature journal

- Testis-specific Borealin paralog [PMID:18268101 "Aust expression is limited to the two male meiotic divisions"];
  can replace Borr in S2 cells [PMID:18268101 "Aust can replace Borr during mitosis in S2 cells"].
- Localization [PMID:18268101 "DmINCENP and Aust colocalize at kinetochores"] [PMID:18268101 "By telophase, Aust staining is apparent at the central spindle midzone"].
- Function [PMID:18268101 "We also show that Aust directs the CPC to kinetochores, regulating sister chromatid cohesion and chromosome alignment and segregation"]
  [PMID:18268101 "cells lacking Aust do not initiate central spindle formation, accumulate anillin or actin at the cell equator, or undergo equatorial constriction"].
- DNA binding [PMID:18268101 "MBP-Borr and MBP-Aust but not MBP alone were able to interact with DNA"].

## Decisions

- Mitotic metaphase chromosome alignment IBA modified to meiotic metaphase chromosome alignment (Aust is meiosis-only).
- Meiotic cytokinesis and spindle midzone modified to male meiosis cytokinesis and meiotic spindle midzone (already carried).
