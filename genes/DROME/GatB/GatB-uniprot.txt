CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalytic subunit of the mitochondrial Glu-tRNA(Gln)
CC       amidotransferase complex GatCAB. GatCAB allows the formation of
CC       correctly charged Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the transamidation of
CC       misacylated Glu-tRNA(Gln) in the mitochondria. The reaction takes place
CC       in the presence of glutamine and ATP through an activated gamma-
CC       phospho-Glu-tRNA(Gln). This subunit probably recognizes tRNA(Gln) and
CC       mediates the transamidation of misloaded Glu by catalyzing formation of
CC       the activated gamma-phospho-intermediate and its subsequent aminolysis
CC       using ammonia produced by the gatA subunit. {ECO:0000255|HAMAP-
CC       Rule:MF_03147}.
