# GatB (Q9VCD0; CG5463) review notes

## Identity
- GatB/GatE-family B subunit of mitochondrial GatCAB (human GATB/PET112 ortholog); ATP-dependent
  kinase and amidation of Glu-tRNA(Gln). [file:DROME/GatB/GatB-uniprot.txt "This subunit probably recognizes tRNA(Gln)"].
- Named in [PMID:26761199 "CG5463 and CG33649 genes – we propose to name the latter two GatB and GatC, respectively"].
- No fly experimental data; annotations are IEA/IBA/ISS.

## Decisions

  obsolete GO:0070681 -> GO:0043685; mitochondrial translation non-core; generic catalytic
  activity -> GO:0050567; ligase and C-N ligase parents non-core.
