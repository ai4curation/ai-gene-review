# FlyBase gene description for nbs (DROME)

nbs (nbs) encodes a component of the conserved MRN complex, which plays an important role in the repair of DNA double strand breaks. The product of nbs is required to prevent chromosome breakage, interacts with the telomere capping complex and prevents telomeric fusions.
