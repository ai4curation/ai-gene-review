Gene Description

twin of eyeless (toy) encodes one of the two Drosophila Pax6 transcription factors, arising from an insect-lineage duplication of the ancestral Pax6 gene; Toy is more similar to vertebrate Pax6 than its paralog Eyeless (Ey). It carries an N-terminal paired domain and a paired-type homeodomain, both DNA-binding, and a C-terminal transactivation region, and acts in the nucleus as a sequence-specific RNA polymerase II transcription factor. Toy is expressed early in the embryonic head, brain and visual primordia and sits at the top of the retinal determination gene network: it directly binds and activates the eye-specific enhancer of eyeless, so it is required to initiate ey expression, and ectopic Toy induces ectopic eyes largely through Ey. Toy also binds enhancers of sine oculis and the Clock locus. Together with ey it is required for growth and development of the entire eye-antennal imaginal disc (strong mutants are headless), and it is the principal Pax6 gene for development of the dorsal ocelli, where it initiates eyes absent and sine oculis expression with Hedgehog signalling. Toy also contributes to embryonic brain patterning and mushroom body development, and regulates Clock expression in circadian pacemaker neurons.

Core Functions

Toy is a paired-domain/homeodomain sequence-specific RNA polymerase II transcription factor that binds cis-regulatory enhancers of retinal determination genes (the ey eye-specific enhancer, the so10 enhancer of sine oculis) and activates their transcription in the nucleus.

Identity and evolution

  • toy is the second Drosophila Pax6 gene, arising by an insect-lineage duplication
    PMID:10198632.
  • Toy is closer to vertebrate Pax6 than Ey PMID:10198632.
  • The ey/toy redundancy is ancient in arthropods PMID:28993201.

Molecular function: sequence-specific DNA-binding transcription factor

  • Directly regulates the eye-specific enhancer of ey PMID:10198632.
  • Binds the so10 enhancer of sine oculis at sites distinct from Ey PMID:11830564.
  • Binds multiple paired-domain sites at the Clock locus by EMSA PMID:24916389.
  • Blanco et al. 2005 (abstract-only in cache; full text checked online): His-TOY binds the chicken
    delta1-crystallin DC5 enhancer in EMSA and, together with SoxN, activates DC5 in S2 cells and in vivo;
    however D-Pax2 is the physiological regulator of DC5 in cone cells
    PMID:15790965.
    This supports a generic Pax6-type DNA-binding TF activity for Toy (IDA for GO:0000981).
  • Paired domain of Toy has DNA-binding properties distinct from Ey, and the C-terminal region differs in
    transactivation PMID:15253940.
  • Phosphorylated by Hipk in paired domain and C-terminal transactivation domain PMID:29205612.

Biological roles

  • Compound eye: acts upstream of ey; required for ey initiation; ectopic Toy induces eyes via Ey
    PMID:10198632.
    Toy can also induce eyes in ey mutants PMID:15253940.
  • Eye-antennal disc / head: strong toy mutants are headless PMID:11861484;
    ey+toy together promote proliferation/survival of the whole disc via tsh and eyg PMID:28584125.
  • Ocelli (dorsal head simple eyes): toy is the main Pax6 for ocelli; it initiates eya/so in ocellar
    primordium with hh PMID:21104743;
    effect on so is largely eya-mediated PMID:20580700;
    TOY binding sites in so10 are required for ocellus development PMID:11830564.
  • Brain: toy mutants show embryonic neuromere and mushroom body defects PMID:19901536.
    Earlier, null/RNAi showed no gross embryonic CNS defects PMID:11335113.
  • Circadian pacemaker neurons: regulates Clk in s-LNvs PMID:24916389. Not in GOA; not proposed as NEW (single study; process would be "regulation of gene expression" in a cell type rather than circadian rhythm execution).
  • Partial interchangeability with ey PMID:19484263.
  • Upstream regulation of toy: oc and salm positive, ems repressive PMID:26976323.

Curation summary

Core: Pol II DNA-binding TF (paired + homeodomain), positive regulator of transcription of eye
determination genes (ey, so, eya); compound eye specification upstream of ey; ocellus development;
eye-antennal disc development. Non-core: brain/mushroom body development, sensory organ morphogenesis.
Generic ARBA terms (post-embryonic development, cell differentiation, system development) and
"DNA-templated transcription" judged over-annotations.
