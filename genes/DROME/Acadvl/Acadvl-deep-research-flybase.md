# FlyBase gene description for Acadvl (DROME)

Acyl-CoA dehydrogenase very long chain (Acadvl) encodes a mitochondrial enzyme that catalyzes the first of the four reactions of fatty acid beta-oxidation, which consists in the dehydrogenation of fatty acyl-CoA thioesters to generate (2E)-enoyl-CoA. The electrons produced are transferred to the mitochondrial respiratory chain via the FAD regeneration system.
