# FlyBase gene description for Shmt (DROME)

Serine hydroxymethyl transferase (Shmt) encodes a conserved pyridoxal phosphate-containing enzyme that converts serine into glycine and N5,N10-methylentetrahydrofolate. This reaction represents a major source for activated C1 units and tetrahydrofolate-mediated C1 metabolism. Shmt is also predicted to catalyze the cleavage of 3-hydroxy-trimethyllysine into 4-(trimethylamino)butanal and glycine, the second step of carnitine biosynthesis.
