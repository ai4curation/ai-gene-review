# spn-A (Drosophila Rad51, Q27297): manual literature synthesis

No deep-research API was available; this synthesis was compiled by hand from cached publications and PubMed.

## Identity
- spn-A (spindle-A, CG7948, FBgn0003479) is the Drosophila RAD51 ortholog [PMID:14592983 "Here we report the identification of spnA as the Drosophila Rad51 gene, whose sequence among the five known Drosophila Rad51-like genes is most closely related to the Rad51 homologs of human and yeast."]
- Cloned as a recA-like gene with ovary-enriched expression [PMID:8625736 "Transcript levels are induced at least tenfold in ovaries"].
- UniProt: "Belongs to the RecA family. RAD51 subfamily."

## Biochemistry
- Purified DmRad51 has strand exchange activity [PMID:14592983 "Biochemical analysis of in vitro purified DmRad51 has shown that it has strand exchange capabilities"], citing Alexiadis & Kadonaga 2002 [PMID:12414729 "observed that Rad54 and Rad51 function cooperatively in the ATP-dependent" remodeling of chromatin].

## DSB repair in soma and germline
- Gene conversion requires Rad51 [PMID:17660539 "We show that Drosophila Rad51 and Rad54 are both required for homologous repair by gene conversion, but are dispensable for single-strand annealing repair."]
- SDSA repair synthesis after P-element excision requires spn-A [PMID:15238522 "As expected, there is little or no repair synthesis" in homozygous spn-A mutants].
- DR-white HR events are Rad51-dependent [PMID:24368780 "These genetic and molecular analyses demonstrate that a majority of DSB repair events detected in our system are repaired through the Rad51-dependent HR pathway in wild-type flies."]
- Null mutants viable but IR-sensitive [PMID:14592983 "spnA null mutants are viable but show sensitivity to irradiation"].
- DmBRCA2 binds spn-A [PMID:17822964 "Dmbrca2 physically interacts with dmrad51 (spnA)"]; Rrp6 required for RAD51 recruitment to DSBs [PMID:25632158 "factor RAD51 to the damaged sites, without affecting RAD51 levels."]

## Meiosis and oogenesis
- Persistent meiotic DSBs activate a meiotic recombination checkpoint [PMID:14592983 "In spnA oocytes synapse of homologous chromosomes is correctly initiated during meiosis but its resolution is delayed and unrepaired double-stranded breaks persist longer than in wild type causing the activation of a meiotic recombination checkpoint"].
- Eggshell/axis phenotypes are suppressed by chk2 [PMID:14592983 "As detailed in Table II B, females doubly mutant for chk2 and spnA produced progeny with wild-type egg shape (100 versus 6%)"] and by mei-W68 [PMID:14592983 "mei-W68 also suppressed the embryonic lethality associated with loss of maternal SpnA."]. Axis, gurken and oocyte-selection phenotypes [PMID:9362456] are therefore indirect.
