CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays an important role in homologous strand exchange, a key
CC       step in DNA repair through homologous recombination (HR) (By
CC       similarity). Binds to single and double-stranded DNA and exhibits DNA-
CC       dependent ATPase activity. Underwinds duplex DNA (By similarity).
CC       Spindle genes are required for each of the symmetry-breaking steps that
CC       generate polarity during egg axis formation; oocyte positioning at the
CC       posterior of the cyst to generate the first AP polarity and inhibition
CC       of gurken (grk) signaling to the follicle cell layer to polarize first
CC       the AP axis and then DV axis. May have a role in female meiosis
CC       (PubMed:8625736, PubMed:9362456). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q06609,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8625736, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9362456}.
