Gene Description

Dsor1 encodes the Drosophila MEK/MAP2K in the canonical Ras/Raf/ERK cascade. Raf-dependent activation of Dsor1 allows this dual-specificity kinase to phosphorylate and activate Rolled/ERK, transmitting EGFR, Torso, Sevenless, Pvr, FGF, and other receptor inputs to ERK-dependent developmental and stress-response outputs.
