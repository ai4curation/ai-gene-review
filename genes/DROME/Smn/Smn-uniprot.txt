CC   -!- FUNCTION: Core component of the survival motor neuron (SMN) complex
CC       that plays an essential role in spliceosomal small nuclear
CC       ribonucleoprotein (snRNP) assembly in the cytoplasm, is required for
CC       pre-mRNA splicing in the nucleus and acts as a chaperone that
CC       discriminates target and non-target RNAs of Sm proteins
CC       (PubMed:18923150, PubMed:23063130, PubMed:23063131, PubMed:30563832). A
CC       major component of nuclear bodies known as gems (gemini of Cajal
CC       bodies) thought to be storage depots of excess SMN complexes
CC       (PubMed:21627586). Required for normal expression of spliceosomal
CC       snRNAs and for U12 intron splicing. Required in cholinergic neurons,
CC       but not in motor neurons, to ensure correct splicing and proper levels
CC       of stas mRNA and normal neurotransmitter release by motor neurons
CC       (PubMed:23063130, PubMed:23063131). However, Smn is required in motor
CC       neurons, but not in cholinergic neurons, for normal motor behavior but
CC       plays no role in synaptic transmission according to a report
CC       (PubMed:23103409). In both muscle and neurons, required for the
CC       formation of a normal neuromuscular junction (NMJ) structure. Plays a
CC       neuron-specific role in long-term homeostatic compensation at the
CC       larval NMJ. In the thorax of adult flies, required for Act88F, an
CC       indirect flight muscle (IFM)-specific actin, expression and for proper
CC       IFM myofibril formation. In nurse cells, oocytes and follicle cells,
CC       required to maintain normal organization of nuclear compartments
CC       including chromosomes, nucleoli, Cajal bodies, histone locus bodies and
CC       heterochromatin. Required for the functional integrity of the
CC       cytoplasmic U snRNP body (U body) and P body. Required in dividing
CC       postembryonic neuroblasts (pNBs) for the correct basal localization of
CC       mira. The tight regulation of its expression is critical for stem cell
CC       division, proliferation and differentiation in male germline and
CC       developing central nervous system (CNS). Required for tracheal terminal
CC       cell lumen formation. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12783845,

CC       PubMed:23333303). The SMN complex associates with the entire set of
CC       spliceosomal snRNP Sm proteins, SmB, SmD1, SmD2, SmD3, SmE, SmF and

CC       function (PubMed:28949413). Interacts weakly with Gem3
