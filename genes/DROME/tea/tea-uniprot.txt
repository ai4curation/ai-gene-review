CC       complex to form the terminin telomere-capping complex involved in
CC       telomere maintenance and prevention of telomere fusion
CC       (PubMed:27835648). As part of the MTV complex binds single stranded DNA
CC       in a sequence-independent manner, protecting it from degradation
CC       (PubMed:27835648). May mediate recruitment of the MTV complex at
CC       telomeres through interaction with components of the HipHop-HOAP
CC       complex (PubMed:27835648). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the MTV complex, composed of moi/modigliani, tea
CC       and ver/verrocchio (PubMed:27835648). Interacts (via C-terminus) with
CC       moi/modigliani and ver/verrocchio; the interactions are direct and
CC       require fully intact moi/modigliani and ver/verrocchio
CC       (PubMed:27835648). The MTV complex is recruited to telomeres by the
CC       HipHop-HOAP complex, consisting of HipHop, cav/HOAP and Su(var)205/HP1
CC       to form the terminin telomere-capping complex (PubMed:27835648).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}.

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27835648}. Chromosome, telomere
