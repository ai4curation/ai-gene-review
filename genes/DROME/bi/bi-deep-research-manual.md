## Identity and domain architecture
- UniProt Q24432 (OMB_DROME), 972 aa, FlyBase FBgn0000179 (bi / omb / l(1)omb / Qd).
- Central T-box DNA-binding domain (residues 332-513, PROSITE PS50252) flanked by long
  disordered regions; Tbx2 subfamily (CDD cd20188 T-box_TBX2_3-like), sole fly ortholog of
  TBX2/TBX3 [PMID:20033428 "In Drosophila, there is one corresponding ortholog, Optomotor-blind (Omb)."].

## Molecular function
- DNA binding by the T-domain [PMID:1497674 "The conserved domain of Omb is here shown to possess general DNA binding affinity"].
- Null missense mutations in conserved T-domain residues abolish DNA binding
  [PMID:20033428 "provide evidence for loss of Omb DNA-binding activity in all three newly identified missense mutations"].
- DNA-binding specificity characterized [PMID:24912380 "we characterized the Omb DNA-binding specificity"].
- Weak/absent activation in transfection compared with ORG-1
  [PMID:15582779 "In cell culture transfection assays, ORG-1 was a strong transcriptional activator, whereas OMB appeared neutral."].
- Repression of targets in vivo: kuz/kul derepressed in omb null wing discs
  [PMID:30599067 "Analysis of a TBX2 loss-of-function Drosophila model revealed that kuzbanian and kuzbanian - like , orthologs of ADAM10, were derepressed compared to wild type."];
  ap [PMID:36907311 "Collectively, Omb is a repressive signal for ap expression in the ventral compartment"];
  Dpp/Hh targets in haltere [PMID:25793870 "limits growth by repressing several Decapentaplegic and Hedgehog targets"];
  mtv [PMID:15063183 "to repress the expression of the gene master of thick veins"].

## Regulation of omb
- Dpp and Wg control omb in the wing [PMID:8599120 "optomotor-blind (omb) expression is required for distal wing development and is controlled by both dpp and wg"].
- Brinker directly represses the omb wing enhancer [PMID:11080162].
- Hh activates omb in the abdomen [PMID:9367427 "This gradient is due to activation of omb by Hh secreted by posterior compartment cells."].
- bantam miRNA down-regulates omb in optic-lobe glia [PMID:22412948 "These results indicate that regulation of omb by bantam is important in maintaining glial cell number and distribution in the optic lobe."].

## Developmental roles
- Wing: central wing reduction and cell death in mutants [PMID:15063183]; A/P boundary maintenance
  [PMID:15708556 "We show that a transcriptional response to Dpp involving the T-box protein Optomotor-blind is required to maintain the A/P boundary."];
  suppression of A/P folds [PMID:18171611]; graded Omb controls cell affinity [PMID:20178599 "Graded Omb expression is necessary for normal cell morphogenesis and cell affinity"]; L5 vein initiation with brk [PMID:15073155].
- Abdomen: posterior tergite identity/polarity; Quadroon pigmentation [PMID:9367427; PMID:1741374].
- Optic lobe: ICg-glia migration and morphology, IOC axon projection [PMID:18234176 "expression of the transcription factor OMB in ICg-glial cells is autonomously required for glial cell migration and morphology"]; HS/VS lobula plate cells [PMID:24912380].
- Null mutants are mainly pupal lethal with optic lobe, wing and pigmentation defects [PMID:1741374].
