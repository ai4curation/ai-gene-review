# Ugp (Q9VSW1) notes

- Ugp is the fly UGP2 ortholog (UGPase, EC 2.7.7.9). UGPase is conserved and forms UDP-glucose reversibly
  [PMID:22132858 "UGPase reversibly catalyses the formation of UDP-glucose"]; the cached record for this
  FlyBase IDA source is abstract-only and centred on human UGPase.
- Cytosolic in a CPTI YFP-trap survey (PMID:25294944; HDA, gene-specific data in supplement).
- UGP knock-down by P-element insertion increased survival in hyperoxia [PMID:20668515 "down-regulation of several candidate
genes including Tropomyosin 1, Glycerol 3 phosphate dehydrogenase, CG33129, and
UGP"].
- Decisions: UGPase activity, UDP-glucose biosynthesis and cytosol are core; UDP-glucose metabolic process,
  uridylyltransferase, cytoplasm and glycogen metabolic process modified to more specific terms already
  annotated; glycogen biosynthesis kept as non-core (one of several consumers of UDP-glucose); hyperoxia
  response marked as over-annotation.

- Falcon deep research (Ugp-deep-research-falcon.md) is consistent (CG4347 = UGPase, EC 2.7.7.9). It mentions a fly
  galactosemia study suggesting Ugp may also form UDP-galactose from galactose-1-phosphate when Galt is lost (genetic, not
  kinetic), and a 2025 kinetic study of a "DmUGP" whose cited accession (A5XCL5) differs from Q9VSW1.
