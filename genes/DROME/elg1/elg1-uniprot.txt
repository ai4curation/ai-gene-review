CC   -!- FUNCTION: Has an important role in DNA replication and in maintaining
CC       genome integrity during replication stress (By similarity). Promotes
CC       PCNA deubiquitination (By similarity). As component of the Elg1 RFC-
CC       like complex, regulates the functions of the DNA polymerase
CC       processivity factor PCNA by unloading it from DNA after replication
CC       during the S phase of the cell cycle (PubMed:27198229). The PCNA-
CC       unloading might be regulated via interaction with the Enok
CC       acetyltransferase complex (PubMed:27198229). Might have a role in
CC       restarting of stalled/regressed replication forks during replication
CC       stress (By similarity). In the ovaries, has a role in nurse cell
CC       endoreplication (PubMed:27198229). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q96QE3,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27198229}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of a heteropentameric Elg1 RFC-like complex composed
CC       of one large subunit (elg1) and four small subunits (RfC4, RfC38,
CC       CG8142 and RfC3) (PubMed:27198229). As part of the complex, might
CC       interact with the Enok complex, composed of enok, Br140, Eaf6 and Ing5
CC       (PubMed:27198229). Within the Enok complex, interacts directly with
CC       Br140 (PubMed:27198229). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27198229}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27198229}.

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: RNAi-mediated knockdown in the ovaries, results
CC       in lower DNA content in the nurse cells and defective development of
CC       egg chambers (PubMed:27198229). Simultaneous knockdown of elg1 and
CC       enok, results in relatively bigger and higher DNA content than single
CC       elg1 knockdown (PubMed:27198229). Further, egg chambers in ~25% of the
CC       ovarioles are able to develop beyond stage 9 with morphologically
CC       normal nurse cells (PubMed:27198229). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27198229}.
