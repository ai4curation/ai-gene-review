Gene Description

Piwi is the nuclear PIWI-clade Argonaute of Drosophila, expressed in ovarian somatic follicle and germline cells and in testes. Loaded in the cytoplasm (Yb bodies and on mitochondria, with Armitage and Zucchini-dependent processing) with antisense, 5-uridine primary/phased piRNAs, mature Piwi-piRISC is imported into the nucleus, where it scans nascent transcripts and, via Asterix/Gtsf1, Panoramix/SFiNX, SUMOylation and Eggless/SetDB1, establishes H3K9me3 heterochromatin and co-transcriptional silencing of transposons. Its slicer activity exists in vitro but is dispensable for silencing. Piwi is also required in somatic niche cells and germline for germline stem cell self-renewal, and maternal Piwi in polar granules contributes to pole cell formation.
