| Nuclear-envelope budding | Cpa showed a general nuclear distribution and enrichment at lamin buds. The experiment measures bud abundance and localization, not barbed-end kinetics at individual buds. | [Verboon et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1242/jcs.243576) (pqac-00000033, pqac-00000037, pqac-00000061) |
