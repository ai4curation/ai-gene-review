CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the extracellular signaling pathway that
CC       establishes the dorsal-ventral pathway of the embryo (PubMed:12493753,
CC       PubMed:2107028, PubMed:9477324). A protease cascade involving ndl, gd,
CC       snk and ea results in activation of the spz Toll receptor ligand; acts
CC       downstream of ndl, gd and snk and is required for proteolytic
CC       processing of spz (PubMed:20605458, PubMed:9477324). Activation of ea
CC       requires both activation of the ndl-gd-snk protease cascade and
CC       sulfation of a vitelline membrane component by pip (PubMed:20605458).
CC       Localized activation of the Toll receptor in the ventral region of the
CC       embryo defines cell identities along the dorsal-ventral continuum
CC       (PubMed:9477324). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12493753,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20605458, ECO:0000269|PubMed:2107028,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9477324}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Activated proteolytically by snk; activation
CC       requires both activation of the ndl-gd-snk protease cascade and
CC       sulfation of a vitelline membrane component by pip (PubMed:16566925,
CC       PubMed:20605458). Inhibited by binding of the serpin Spn27A
CC       (PubMed:14654000, PubMed:14667416). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14654000,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14667416, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16566925,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20605458}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with Spn27A; the two proteins are covalently linked
CC       leading to inhibition of ea catalytic activity (PubMed:14667416).
CC       Interacts (via Peptidase domain) with snk (via N-terminal prodomain);
CC       leads to proteolytic activation of ea by snk (PubMed:20605458).
CC       Sulfation of a vitelline membrane component by pip is required for
CC       proteolytic cleavage of ea by snk but not for the interaction of ea
CC       with snk (PubMed:20605458). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14667416,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:20605458}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Secreted {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9477324}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed both maternally and zygotically.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9477324}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The clip domain consists of 35-55 residues which are 'knitted'
CC       together usually by 3 conserved disulfide bonds forming a clip-like
CC       compact structure. {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU01236}.
CC   -!- PTM: Proteolytically cleaved by snk (PubMed:20605458). Activation
CC       peptide and active catalytic domain remain associated by a disulfide
CC       bond (Probable). Processed ea/easter is present in extremely low
CC       amounts in the early embryo as it is rapidly converted into a high
CC       molecular mass complex made up of ea covalently bound to the serpin
CC       Spn27A (Probable) (PubMed:14667416). Zymogen activation is also
CC       controlled by a negative feedback loop from Dorsal.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14667416, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20605458,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:20605458}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the peptidase S1 family. CLIP subfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU01236}.
