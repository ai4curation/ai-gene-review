| **Direct — fly oogenesis (2018)** | Antibody staining detected *D. melanogaster* FAM21 in stage 7–9 germ cells, enriched at the oocyte cortex. (pqac-00000006, pqac-00000020, pqac-00000025) | FAM21 is required, as part of nuclear SHRC, for efficient nuclear-envelope budding—an Arp2/3-dependent export mechanism for large macromolecular cargo.
