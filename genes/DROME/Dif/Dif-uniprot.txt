CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mediates an immune response in larvae (PubMed:8242747). DIF
CC       binds to the kappa-B consensus sequence 5'-GGRNNYYCC-3', located in the
CC       enhancer region of the immunity gene CECA1 (PubMed:8242747). Involved
CC       in the melanization immune response to bacterial challenge, possibly by
CC       acting in a Toll signaling pathway that down-regulates Spn27A in
CC       response to infection (PubMed:12456640). Part of a signaling pathway
CC       involving NF-kappa-B (rel) and Toll-related receptors, that functions
CC       in the apoptosis of unfit cells during cell competition
CC       (PubMed:25477468). May be part of a NF-kappa-B and Tollo signaling
CC       cascade that regulates development of the peripheral nervous system
CC       (PubMed:18000549). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12456640,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18000549, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25477468,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8242747}.
