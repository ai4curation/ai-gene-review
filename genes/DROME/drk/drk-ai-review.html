Gene Description

drk encodes the Drosophila GRB2-family adaptor Downstream of receptor kinase. Its SH2 domain binds phosphotyrosine motifs on activated receptor tyrosine kinases and its SH3 domains bind proline-rich partners such as Sos and Dos, recruiting Ras activators and scaffolds to receptor complexes. Drk couples EGFR, Sevenless, Torso, and additional RTKs to Ras/MAPK signaling.
