Gene Description

grim encodes a short Drosophila Reaper/Hid/Grim-family IAP antagonist that promotes apoptotic caspase activation. Grim binds BIR domains of DIAP1/DIAP2-family IAPs through its N-terminal IAP-binding motif, countering IAP restraint of DrICE-family caspases, and also uses an internal GH3 amphipathic helix to associate with mitochondria and remodel cytochrome-c distribution during apoptosis induction in selected developmental cell-death programs.

Core Functions

Grim uses its N-terminal IAP-binding motif to bind IAP BIR domains, antagonizing DIAP1 and DIAP2 interactions with caspases and promoting apoptotic caspase activation.

Molecular Function:
BIR domain binding
Directly Involved In:
apoptotic signaling pathway positive regulation of apoptotic process
Supporting Evidence:
  • PMID:15107838
    RHG proteins use their N-terminal IAP-binding motifs to bind to the same surface groove
  • PMID:18166655
    Rpr and Grim preferentially bound to the BIR2 domain of DIAP2
  • PMID:10481910
    RPR, HID, and GRIM promote apoptosis by disrupting productive IAP-caspase interactions

Grim uses its GH3 amphipathic helix to associate with mitochondria and remodel cytochrome c distribution, a mitochondrial pro-apoptotic route separable from its N-terminal IAP antagonism.

Directly Involved In:
apoptotic mitochondrial changes apoptotic signaling pathway
Cellular Locations:

Supporting Evidence:

    GH3, a 15 amino acid internal Grim domain absolutely required for its proapoptotic activity
  • PMID:12093734

  • PMID:12093734
    Two independent pathways may thus be triggered by Grim

grim apoptosis curation notes

2026-09-30

  • Reviewed all 23 seeded FlyBase GOA rows for grim using the local UniProt record and cached publications.
  • Treated Grim as an RHG/IAP antagonist with two separable pro-apoptotic regions:
    an N-terminal IAP-binding motif that binds IAP BIR domains and a GH3 amphipathic
    helix that supports mitochondrial apoptotic changes.
  • Downcasted generic GO:0006915 apoptotic process and GO:0012501 programmed cell death rows to GO:0097190 apoptotic signaling pathway where the source
    assayed Grim as the upstream pro-death trigger.
  • Replaced the generic DIAP2 GO:0005515 protein binding row with
    GO:1990525 BIR domain binding; Ribeiro et al. directly state that Rpr and
    Grim preferentially bind the BIR2 domain of DIAP2 PMID:18166655.
  • Broadened the GO:0005759 mitochondrial matrix row to
    GO:0005739 mitochondrion; Claveria et al. used a matrix marker to show that
    Grim-positive rings are mitochondria associated, not that Grim enters the
    matrix PMID:12093734.
  • Broadened positive regulation of release of cytochrome c from mitochondria
    to GO:0008637 apoptotic mitochondrial changes because the same paper reports
    no substantial free cytochrome-c release into the cytosol in Grim-expressing
    Drosophila cells, only cytochrome-c redistribution into Grim-positive puncta
    PMID:12093734.
  • Kept Malpighian tubule nuclear localization and neuronal/glial developmental
    death rows as non-core or context-specific: they are real deployments or
    restraint contexts of the Grim program rather than separate molecular
    activities.
  • Left rows from abstract-only papers UNDECIDED when the visible cache did not
    expose Grim-specific evidence, notably lifespan, melanization, larval CNS
    remodeling, plasma membrane, and one Sickle-paper top-level apoptosis row.
