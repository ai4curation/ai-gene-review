The supplied record calls Q9VFA8 a “VWFC domain-containing protein” and assigns VWF_dom/VWFC (IPR001007) plus IDD (IPR042378). A domain match identifies a sequence region with similarity to a known structural module; it does not demonstrate the full protein’s biochemical activity.
