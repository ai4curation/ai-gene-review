# 18w (18 wheeler, Toll-2, A1ZBR2) — review notes

Batch 1 (Toll/TLR axis) of the INNATE_IMMUNITY project. Falcon deep research had not
started for this gene when the review was drafted; notes are built from the cached
GOA publications and the UniProt record.

## Identity

- Toll family LRR-TM-TIR receptor, also called Toll-2 [PMID:7600965 "18w encodes a transmembrane protein with an extracellular moiety containing many leucine rich repeats and cysteine motifs, and an intracellular domain bearing homology to the cytoplasmic portion of the interleukin-1-receptor"].
- No ligand has been identified. Spätzle-1 did not activate Toll-2 in S2 cells [PMID:31088910 "cells co-expressing Toll-2 and the cystine knot for Spz-1 also resulted in either little (3.5–14-fold) or no activation of the drosomycin promoter"].

## Developmental roles (core)

- Morphogenesis / adhesion [PMID:7600965 "Expression of 18W protein in non-adhesive Schneider 2 cells promotes rapid and robust aggregation of cells."].
- Convergent extension: with Toll-6 and Toll-8 forms a positional code [PMID:25363762 "three Toll family receptors, Toll-2, Toll-6 and Toll-8, are expressed in overlapping transverse stripes along the anterior-posterior axis and act in combination to direct planar polarity and polarized cell rearrangements during convergent extension"].
- Follicle cell migration and eggshell shape [PMID:16607637 "Females carrying loss-of-function 18w mutant clones in their ovaries show delayed follicle cell migrations."].
- CNS: an 18w allele was recovered in an Ap4/FMRFa specification screen [PMID:26092715 "18w encodes a Toll-like receptor and has been found to be important for imaginal disc developmental and antibacterial defense"].

## Immunity: not a PRR, adult role dispensable

- Original proposal [PMID:9321392 "In the absence of the 18W receptor, larvae are more susceptible to bacterial infection."].
- Re-evaluation [PMID:12101100 "Here we re-examine the role of 18-Wheeler and show that in adults it is dispensable for immune responses. In larvae, 18wheeler is required for normal fat body development, and in mutant larvae induction of all antimicrobial peptide genes, and not only of those directed against Gram-negative bacteria, is compromised. 18-Wheeler does not qualify as a pattern recognition receptor of Gram-negative bacteria."].
- A Toll-ectodomain/18W-TIR chimera did not induce drosomycin [PMID:10973475 "neither the 18W nor the Toll-6 to Toll-8 chimerae were able to induce drosomycin"], whereas an isolated overexpressed 18W TIR modestly did [PMID:31088910 "each TIR significantly activated the drosomycin reporter (7–54-fold) above an empty plasmid/pGL3B control"].
- Project question 1: no PRR (GO:0038187) or GO:0002224 annotation on 18w. Its immune annotations (antibacterial humoral response IMP; immune response IEP) are best read as secondary to the larval fat-body developmental defect; marked over-annotated. The GOA NOT annotation (antimicrobial humoral response) is supported and accepted.
