# pnut (Peanut, P40797) notes

Septin, SEPT7 class; shared subunit of both Drosophila septin complexes (Sep1-Sep2-Pnut and Sep4-Sep5-Pnut).

## Literature journal

- Original identification: pnut required for cytokinesis; mutant imaginal tissues become multinucleate [PMID:8181057 "in pnut mutants, imaginal tissues fail to proliferate and instead develop clusters of large, multinucleate cells"]; localized to cleavage furrow and intercellular bridge [PMID:8181057 "Pnut protein is localized to the cleavage furrow of dividing cells during cytokinesis and to the intercellular bridge connecting postmitotic daughter cells."]
- Genetic interaction with sina is the only basis for the photoreceptor NAS annotations [PMID:8181057 "pnut displays genetic interactions with seven in absentia"] -> marked over-annotated on all septins.
- Embryo-purified complex Pnut/Sep2/Sep1 binds and hydrolyses GTP [PMID:8636235 "It binds and hydrolyzes exogenously added GTP."]; one bound nucleotide per subunit [PMID:8636235 "The complex copurifies with one molecule of bound guanine nucleotide per septin polypeptide."]
- Orc6 binds the Pnut coiled-coil and stimulates complex GTPase [PMID:18987337 "the binding of Orc6 to Pnut increases the intrinsic GTPase activity of the Drosophila septin complex"]
- Recombinant complex: function depends on GTP domains of Pnut, Sep1 and Sep2 [PMID:25355953 "We show that Drosophila septin complex functions depend on the intact GTP-binding and/or hydrolysis domains of Pnut, Sep1, and Sep2."]; Sep2 alone has no GTPase activity [PMID:25355953 "Sep2 protein, on the other hand, did not possess any noticeable GTPase activity (Table 1)."]
- Pnut phosphorylation regulates membrane association [PMID:29079679 "All Pnut variants were detected at cell membranes during interphase, and at the cleavage furrow between dividing cells."]
- Septins bundle actin into rings [PMID:24633326 "In vitro reconstitution assays show that septins alone bundle actin filaments into rings"]
- Wound repair: two complexes [PMID:38728140 "Taken together, our findings further suggest that Sep1/Sep2/Pnut and Sep4/Sep5/Pnut are functioning as distinct complexes within the context of cell wound repair."]; all five septins bind/bundle F-actin individually [PMID:38728140 "Strikingly, we find that all five Drosophila Septins can bind to and bundle actin filaments"]; complexes bend bundles [PMID:38728140 "Interestingly, we observe curved F-actin bundles induced by two separate Septin complexes, whereas individual Septins can only bind to and bundle F-actin."]
- Classes [PMID:38728140 "Drosophila has 5 Septins that are grouped into three classes: Sep2 and Sep5 (SEPT6 class), Sep1 and Sep4 (SEPT2 class), and Pnut (SEPT7 class)"]
- Border cell migration [PMID:37329886 "Expressing RNAi against Sep1, Sep2, or Pnut impaired migration (Figures 2B–2D)."]
- Peanut downregulation (miR-8) causes cytokinesis failure in tumour model [PMID:26853367 "Cytokinesis failure due to downregulation of Peanut is required for tumorigenesis."]
- Apoptosis modifier: pnut mutations suppress RHG killing [PMID:15029247 "mutations in peanut, which encodes a Drosophila homologue of ARTS, can dominantly suppress cell killing by Reaper, Hid and Grim"]
- Parkin ubiquitinates Pnut and Sep1 [PMID:17456438 "peanut and septin1, D. melanogaster septin proteins, were also ubiquitinated by D. melanogaster Parkin"]
