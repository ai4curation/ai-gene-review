# FlyBase gene description for Pgd (DROME)

Phosphogluconate dehydrogenase (Pgd) encodes a cytosolic enzyme that catalyzes the oxidation of 6-phospho-D-gluconate to D-ribulose 5-phosphate, with the concomitant reduction of NADP+ to NADPH in the oxidative branch of the pentose phosphate pathway.
