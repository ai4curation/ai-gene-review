# FlyBase gene description for ttv (DROME)

tout-velu (ttv) encodes a glycosyltransferase that forms a complex with sotv to catalyze the alternating transfer of glucuronic acid (GlcA) and N-acetylglucosamine (GlcNAc) to their respective acceptors, GlcNAc and GlcA, during the elongation of the heparan sulfate glycosaminoglycan chain.
