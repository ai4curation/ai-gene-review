# Stim (Drosophila melanogaster, P83094) notes

## Identity
- Single fly STIM (stromal interaction molecule) orthologue; single-pass ER membrane protein with luminal EF-hand/SAM domain and cytosolic coiled-coil/SOAR region that gates Orai.

## Evidence
- Roos et al. 2005 identified Stim in an S2-cell RNAi screen: [PMID:15866891 "RNAi-mediated knockdown of Stim in Drosophila S2 cells significantly reduced TG-dependent Ca2+ entry."]; [PMID:15866891 "Patch-clamp recording revealed nearly complete suppression of the Drosophila Ca2+ release-activated Ca2+ (CRAC) current"]; EF hand proposed as sensor [PMID:15866891 "Its NH2-terminal putative EF-hand Ca2+-binding motif is located outside the cell and, presumably, in the lumen of the ER where it could function as a Ca2+ sensor."]. Note: Ca2+ binding was proposed from sequence, not measured directly in this paper.
- Stim-Orai co-IP enhanced by store depletion [PMID:16921385 "interaction between wild-type Stim and Orai, assessed by co-immunoprecipitation, is greatly enhanced after treatment with thapsigargin"].
- Stim C terminus gates Orai and drives Orai oligomerisation [PMID:18820677 "Stim senses depletion of the ER Ca(2+) store and physically relays this information by translocating from the ER to junctions adjacent to the plasma membrane"].
- Development: [PMID:18950512 "We demonstrate an essential role for dSTIM in larval development and survival, and a tissue-specific role in cell fate specification of mechanosensory bristles in the notum and in specification of wing vein thickness."]
- Neurons: Stim needed for flight [PMID:19515818 "the endoplasmic reticulum Ca(2+) store sensor encoded by dSTIM are necessary for normal flight"].
- ER-PM contacts at presynaptic terminals [PMID:36952540 "We found that reduced STIM-mediated ER-plasma membrane contacts underlie presynaptic Ca2+ defects in Rtnl1 mutants."].

## Curation thoughts
- Plasma membrane annotations: STIM1 has a small PM pool in mammals, but fly Stim acts from ER at ER-PM junctions; PM IEA/NAS/IBA kept as non-core/over-annotation considered. Zhang 2006 NAS says "translocates to the plasma membrane" — mechanistically it is ER-PM junction, not PM insertion. Mark as over-annotated for NAS/IEA; IBA PM also over-annotated.
- calcium ion sensor activity (IMP) fits the EF-hand sensing role; accept.
