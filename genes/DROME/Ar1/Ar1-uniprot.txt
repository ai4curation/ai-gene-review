CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the NADPH-dependent reduction of a wide variety of
CC       carbonyl-containing compounds to their corresponding alcohols. Displays
CC       enzymatic activity towards endogenous metabolites such as aromatic and
CC       aliphatic aldehydes, ketones, monosaccharides, bile acids and
CC       xenobiotics substrates. Key enzyme in the polyol pathway, catalyzes
CC       reduction of glucose to sorbitol during hyperglycemia. Reduces steroids
CC       and their derivatives and prostaglandins (By similarity). Through
CC       production of prostaglandin F2alpha may regulate the activity of non-
CC       muscle myosin II in an autocrine or paracrine fashion; influences
CC       border cell and nurse cell stiffness to facilitate border cell cluster
CC       migration (PubMed:38283991). Also regulates the cell surface
CC       localization of integrins in an autocrine or paracrine fashion;
CC       influences border cell adhesion to maintain border cell cluster
CC       morphology (PubMed:38283991). In hemocytes, probably contributes to
CC       production of sugar alcohols in the hemolymph, which act as alarmins
CC       involved in gut-fat body innate immunological communication (GFIC);
CC       leads to activation of the imd/Relish signaling pathway in the fat body
CC       (PubMed:31350199). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P15121,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:31350199, ECO:0000269|PubMed:38283991}.
