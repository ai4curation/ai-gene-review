# FlyBase gene description for Ar1 (DROME)

Aldose reductase 1 (Ar1) encodes an enzyme that catalyzes the NADPH-dependent reduction of aldose sugars and other carbonyl-containing compounds to their corresponding alcohols. It is involved in the biosynthesis of D-fructose from D-glucose.
