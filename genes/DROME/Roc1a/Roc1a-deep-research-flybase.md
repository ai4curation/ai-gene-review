# FlyBase gene description for Roc1a (DROME)

Regulator of cullins 1a (Roc1a) encodes a C2H2 type zinc finger protein and component of several Cullin-Ring E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes. It binds directly to the Cullin subunit within the CRL complex and functions to recruit ubiquitin-charged E2 proteins.
