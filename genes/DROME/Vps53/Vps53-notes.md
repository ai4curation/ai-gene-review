# Vps53 (CG3338) notes

Large helical-rod subunit shared by GARP and EARP.

- Co-purifies with GARP subunits on Arl5-GTP and Rab4-GTP [PMID:25795912 "Amongst the more abundant proteins that bound specifically to Arl5-GTP were the Drosophila orthologues of the four subunits of the GARP complex"].
- Co-IPs with CG4996/Vps50 in S2 cells; human VPS53 relocated to endosomes by Rab4 [PMID:25453831 "We also examined a GARP subunit (Vps53) in mammalian cells and found that it could be relocated to endosomes by overexpression of Rab4"].
- EARP shares Vps53 [PMID:25799061 "The two complexes share the Ang2, Vps52 and Vps53 subunits"].

Decisions: same convention as Vps51/Vps52 (GARP ACCEPT, NEW EARP complex, ND -> vesicle membrane tethering activity).
