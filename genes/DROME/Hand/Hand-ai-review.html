Gene Description

Hand is the single Drosophila member of the Hand family of class B basic helix-loop-helix (bHLH) transcription factors (ortholog of vertebrate HAND1/HAND2). It is expressed in all cells of the dorsal vessel (cardioblasts and pericardial nephrocytes), in progenitors of the circular visceral muscles, in lymph gland hematopoietic progenitors, garland cells and a few CNS cells. Hand is a direct target of the cardiogenic factors Tinman (NK-2) and Pannier (GATA) in the heart, of Serpent (GATA) in the lymph gland and of Biniou (FoxF) in visceral mesoderm, and is expressed after these determinants, consistent with a role in differentiation rather than specification. Hand is a potent transcriptional activator that, probably as a heterodimer with E-protein (Daughterless) or other bHLH partners, regulates muscle structural genes such as Zasp52 and Msp-300. Loss of Hand causes semilethality, lymph gland deficiency, and abnormal adult hearts (disorganized myofibrils, impaired contractility) and midguts, reflecting a main requirement during pupal remodeling of the larval aorta into the adult heart; whether embryonic heart formation also requires Hand differs between mutant alleles. Hand is also required for wing heart formation.

Hand (Drosophila melanogaster, Q9VL05) - curation notes

Automated deep research failed for this run (falcon 402, OpenAI 401); notes below are from cached
publications fetched with just fetch-pmid.

Identity and expression

  • Single fly member of the Hand bHLH family (HAND1/HAND2 ortholog); PANTHER PTHR23349 (Twist family).
  • Expressed in all cardioblasts and pericardial cells, circular visceral muscle progenitors, lymph gland,
    garland cells and a few CNS cells PMID:12424518.

Upstream regulation

  • Direct target of Tinman/Pannier in heart and Serpent in lymph gland PMID:15975941.
  • Direct target of Biniou in visceral mesoderm; dispensable for initial visceral mesoderm differentiation PMID:17511863.

Molecular function

  • Transcriptional activator PMID:16467358.
  • Targets include muscle genes PMID:26252215.
  • Candidate dimer partners PMID:23747982.
  • No direct DNA-binding assay for fly Hand found in cached literature.

Phenotypes - conflicting embryonic heart data

  • Han et al. 2006: PMID:16467358.
  • Lo et al. 2007: PMID:17904115; main requirement is pupal PMID:17904115.
  • Lymph gland/hematopoiesis requirement is reproducible across both studies.
  • Wing hearts require Hand (PMID:23747982).

Review decisions

  • MODIFY GO:0032968 (elongation) and GO:0006355 -> GO:0045944.
  • Embryonic heart tube development kept as non-core because of the allele conflict.
  • NEW GO:0007512 adult heart development (Lo et al. 2007).
  • Module annoton (GO:0000981, heart development) is consistent with this review.
