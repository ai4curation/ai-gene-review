# Gmd (Q9VMW9) notes

- Gmd is the single Drosophila GDP-mannose 4,6-dehydratase; recombinant enzyme has the predicted activity
  [PMID:16650000 "the insect has, like mammalian species, only one homologue of each (gmd
and gmer)"].
- Flies lack the fucose salvage pathway, so GDP-fucose is made only de novo by GMD and GMER
  [PMID:11698403 "is formed exclusively via the de novo pathway from
GDP-mannose through enzymatic reactions catalyzed by GDP-D-mannose
4,6-dehydratase (GMD)"].
- Gmd loss impairs Notch-dependent commitment of intestinal stem cell daughters
  [PMID:21965616 "required for commitment but dispensable for terminal differentiation"].
- Two Gmd alleles were recovered in an early-oogenesis clonal screen (follicular proliferation class)
  [PMID:23450845 "2 alleles of GDP-mannose 4,6-dehydradtase (Gmd)"].
- PMID:17329366 (O-fut1 endocytosis) is abstract-only in cache; Gmd IMP annotations deferred to curator.
  The Gmd H78 allele originates from this paper [PMID:23450845 "GmdH78/CyO (Sasamura et al. 2007)"].
- Decisions: catalytic MF and de novo GDP-fucose BP accepted; GDP-mannose metabolic process modified to
  GO:0042351; Notch, cell-fate and oogenesis phenotypes kept as non-core (indirect, via fucosylation of Notch).

- Falcon deep research (Gmd-deep-research-falcon.md; it finished after the wrapper's 600 s timeout) is consistent:
  it reports that Gmd-null animals lack detectable GDP-fucose.
