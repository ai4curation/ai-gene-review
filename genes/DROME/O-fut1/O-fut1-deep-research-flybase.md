# FlyBase gene description for O-fut1 (DROME)

O-fucosyltransferase 1 (Ofut1) encodes an endoplasmic reticulum enzyme that catalyzes the attachment of &bgr;-L-fucose to the hydroxyl group of serine or threonine residues in protein EGF-like repeats via an &agr;-glycosidic bond. It is involved in the synthesis of the O-fucosylated glycans on the N protein, which regulates N binding to its ligands encoded by Delta and Ser.
