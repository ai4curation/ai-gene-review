| **Enzymatic O-fucosyltransferase activity** | In the ER lumen, Ofut1 transfers fucose from GDP-fucose to Ser/Thr in appropriately folded Notch EGF-like repeats, principally at `C2-X4-(S/T)-C3` motifs. This generates the O-fucose monosaccharide on which subsequent glycan regulation depends. (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000017) | Structural and biochemical work on POFUT1-family enzymes shows recognition of the folded EGF domain—not merely a short linear motif—and transfer of fucose from GDP-fucose to the acceptor hydroxyl without a manganese requirement. (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010, pqac-00000011) | Ofut1-mediated O-fucosylation establishes a glycan-sensitive state of Notch that can alter ligand interaction and receptor stability, but the monosaccharide is not an absolute requirement for every Drosophila Notch response. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022) |
| **Enzymatic O-fucosyltransferase activity** | Ofut1 acts upstream of Fringe. (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000044) | Fringe gain-of-function requires O-fucose: when GDP-fucose delivery is eliminated in `Efr; Gfr` double mutants, Fringe overexpression cannot produce its normal signaling phenotype. (pqac-00000044, pqac-00000046, pqac-00000047) | At approximately endogenous expression, catalytically inactive Ofut1-R245A supports residual Notch activity but produces wing phenotypes resembling `fringe` loss, consistent with loss of the O-fucose substrate and therefore loss of Fringe-dependent regulation. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022) | The catalytic arm primarily fine-tunes ligand selectivity and spatial patterning—for example, at the wing dorsoventral boundary—rather than serving as an invariant on/off switch for Notch. | **Enzymatic O-fucosyltransferase activity** | Catalysis depends on the cellular GDP-fucose pool. Drosophila synthesizes GDP-fucose de novo through Gmd and Gmer; Efr and Gfr provide partially redundant delivery into the ER/Golgi secretory system. (pqac-00000036, pqac-00000037, pqac-00000038) | Combined loss of Efr and Gfr eliminates adequate GDP-fucose delivery, disrupts Notch O-fucosylation, and causes loss of the Notch target Wingless at the wing margin. Neighboring cells can rescue GDP-fucose-deficient cells through Innexin-2-containing gap junctions. (pqac-00000036, pqac-00000037) | The catalytic pathway is metabolically coupled to GDP-fucose synthesis, intracellular transport, and even local intercellular metabolite transfer; however, these defects need not phenocopy complete Ofut1 loss because the protein also has a catalytic-independent function. (pqac-00000037, pqac-00000043) |
| **Enzymatic O-fucosyltransferase activity** | O-fucosylation is dispensable in some Fringe-independent contexts. The receptor can therefore remain signaling competent when Ofut1 protein is present but catalysis is abolished. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022) | Ofut1-R245A, which lacks detectable transferase activity, rescues the embryonic neurogenic phenotype of `Ofut1` mutants; embryos lacking Gmd—and thus broadly deficient in fucosylation—also retain the examined Notch-dependent embryonic neurogenesis. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022) | During embryonic lateral inhibition, Ofut1’s nonenzymatic role is sufficient. During imaginal development, catalytic loss chiefly resembles loss of Fringe-dependent modulation rather than complete loss of Notch signaling. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022) |
| **Nonenzymatic chaperone activity** | Soluble ER-resident Ofut1 binds or assists newly synthesized Notch, promoting proper folding, maturation, and quality-control passage independently of fucose transfer. (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000028) | Ofut1 depletion or mutation causes Notch to accumulate intracellularly with ER markers; surface staining is lost even though receptor protein remains detectable intracellularly. (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000032) | This activity is required to generate secretion-competent, cell-surface Notch and explains why complete `Ofut1` loss is more severe than simply eliminating O-fucose or Fringe. (pqac-00000021, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000032) |
| **Nonenzymatic chaperone activity** | Ofut1 supports ER exit and trafficking through the secretory pathway; without it, Notch is retained before productive plasma-membrane delivery. (pqac-00000023, pqac-00000026, pqac-00000032) | In mutant wing-disc cells, Notch is intracellular and absent from the surface. Reintroduction of catalytically inactive but chaperone-competent Ofut1 restores substantial receptor activity and trafficking. (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000032) | Proper receptor biogenesis—not O-fucosylation alone—is the broadly essential contribution of Ofut1 to Notch signaling in Drosophila. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000023) |
| **Nonenzymatic chaperone activity** | The chaperone and catalytic arms are separable. Arg-to-Ala substitution in the GDP-fucose-binding/catalytic region abolishes detectable enzyme activity while retaining sufficient chaperone function. (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000028) | Ofut1-R245A rescues embryonic neurogenesis and permits substantial signaling in imaginal tissues, although Fringe-like defects remain where O-fucose-dependent modulation is needed. (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022, pqac-00000028) | The mutant provides the strongest genetic evidence that Ofut1 protein itself, rather than its glycan product, is essential in many fly Notch contexts. Interpretation should nevertheless distinguish fly results from mammalian POFUT1, where catalytic-mutant stability and rescue behavior differ. (pqac-00000028, pqac-00000033, pqac-00000035) |
| **Nonenzymatic chaperone activity** | Ofut1 may couple GDP-fucose availability to Notch quality control: successful O-fucosylation can weaken prolonged Ofut1–Notch association, whereas donor deficiency may retain the complex and favor receptor endocytosis or degradation after surface delivery. (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000029) | Studies supporting an extracellular/endocytic model reported that, under GDP-fucose deficiency, Ofut1 remains associated with Notch and promotes internalization and degradation. Other experiments instead interpret the dominant defect as ER retention and failed secretion. (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000029, pqac-00000031) | The well-supported consensus is an ER chaperone/quality-control role; a later extracellular role in constitutive Notch endocytosis has been proposed but remains mechanistically more controversial. (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000031, pqac-00000034) |
| **Nonenzymatic chaperone activity** | Chaperone function is broadly required in embryonic neurogenesis and wing Notch signaling, while its dosage must be controlled. (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000026) | Ofut1 is not merely a constitutive biosynthetic enzyme: an appropriate amount of chaperone activity is needed for productive receptor maturation, whereas excess protein can inhibit signaling independently of glycosylation.
