| Property | Ofut1 annotation and evidence |
|---|---|
| Target identity | *Drosophila melanogaster* **Ofut1** encodes protein O-fucosyltransferase 1; synonyms are **neurotic**, **nti**, **O-fut1**, and **CG12366**. CG12366 was independently identified as the fly O-FUT1 candidate, distinguishing it from O-FUT2/CG14789 (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000005). |
| Protein classification | GDP-fucose–dependent protein O-fucosyltransferase; EC 2.4.1.221; glycosyltransferase family 65. It is the fly ortholog of mammalian POFUT1 and is distinct from POFUT2, which modifies thrombospondin type-1 repeats (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000008). |
| Overall reaction | **GDP-β-L-fucose + folded EGF-domain Ser/Thr-OH → GDP + EGF-domain Ser/Thr-O-α-L-fucose.** Thus, GDP-fucose is the nucleotide-sugar donor, the hydroxyl group of a selected serine or threonine is the acceptor, and the product is an O-fucosylated protein EGF repeat (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000012). |
| Acceptor-site consensus | The canonical motif lies between the second and third conserved cysteines of an EGF-like domain: **C²-X₄-(S/T)-C³**, commonly written **C²XXXX[S/T]C³**; some literature allows a four- or five-residue spacing before the acceptor (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010). |
| Domain-level specificity | The short consensus sequence is not sufficient by itself. Ofut1/POFUT1 recognizes a **properly folded, disulfide-bonded EGF-like domain**, using contacts with the C²–C³ loop and additional surfaces such as the C¹–C² loop and C⁵–C⁶ subdomain. This makes Ofut1 a conformation-sensitive, domain-specific enzyme rather than a general Ser/Thr fucosyltransferase (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000010). |
| Fine acceptor preferences | Both serine and threonine can be modified, but structural data indicate a preference for **threonine**, whose methyl group forms a favorable apolar contact. Loop length and local residues also affect recognition; steric or charged substitutions can weaken binding (pqac-00000011). |
| Major physiological substrate | **Notch** is the best-established substrate. Ofut1 modifies multiple EGF repeats in the receptor’s extracellular region while Notch is being synthesized in the secretory pathway (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000021). |
| Additional pathway substrates | EGF repeats in the Notch ligands **Delta** and **Serrate** are also reported substrates, although the strongest functional evidence concerns receptor-side Notch modification (pqac-00000004). |
| Structural architecture | POFUT1-family enzymes adopt a **GT-B glycosyltransferase fold**. GDP-fucose occupies a conserved interdomain cavity connected to a larger solvent-exposed groove that accommodates the folded EGF domain (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000010). |
| Catalytic stereochemistry and cofactor requirement | POFUT1-family enzymes are **inverting glycosyltransferases** and do not require Mn²⁺ for catalysis. Structural and computational work supports an unusual dissociative, **SN1-like** mechanism in which donor-bond cleavage substantially precedes nucleophilic attack by the acceptor hydroxyl (pqac-00000009). |
| Catalytic residues | Structural work on a POFUT1 homolog identified **Arg240** as central to donor cleavage and **Asn51** as contacting the acceptor hydroxyl; an ordered water may relay proton transfer to GDP phosphate. In fly OFUT1, the corresponding invariant GDP-binding/catalytic arginine is experimentally represented by **R245**: the **R245A** substitution abolishes detectable fucosyltransferase activity while preserving substantial non-catalytic chaperone function (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000012). |
| Subcellular localization | Ofut1 is a **soluble endoplasmic-reticulum-resident protein** and acts primarily in the ER lumen, where newly synthesized Notch EGF repeats fold and become O-fucosylated before receptor export through the secretory pathway (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000016, pqac-00000017). |
| Donor-substrate supply | Cytosolic GDP-fucose is generated through the de novo Gmd/Gmer pathway and delivered to the secretory pathway by the ER transporter Efr and Golgi transporter Gfr, which can act redundantly to sustain Notch O-fucosylation (pqac-00000036, pqac-00000038). |
| Immediate biochemical consequence | Ofut1-generated O-fucose is the obligatory acceptor for **Fringe**, a β1,3-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase that adds GlcNAc to selected O-fucose residues and thereby changes Notch ligand responsiveness—generally enhancing Delta-dependent and suppressing Serrate-dependent signaling in flies (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000044). |
| Non-catalytic property relevant to annotation | In addition to catalysis, Ofut1 functions as a Notch-selective ER chaperone supporting receptor folding, ER exit, and cell-surface delivery. This activity is experimentally separable from O-fucosylation and explains why complete *Ofut1* loss is generally more severe than loss of GDP-fucose or Fringe-dependent glycan elongation (pqac-00000020, pqac-00000022, pqac-00000032). |


*Table: Biochemical and structural annotation of Drosophila Ofut1 (Q9V6X7), including identity, reaction chemistry, folded-domain specificity, catalytic features, substrates, and ER localization. Homolog-derived structural conclusions are distinguished from fly-specific mutational evidence.*
