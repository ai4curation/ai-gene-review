CC   -!- FUNCTION: Catalyzes the reaction that attaches fucose through an O-
CC       glycosidic linkage to a conserved serine or threonine residue found in
CC       the consensus sequence C2-X(4,5)-[S/T]-C3 of EGF domains, where C2 and
CC       C3 are the second and third conserved cysteines (PubMed:12909620,
CC       PubMed:15692013). Specifically uses GDP-fucose as donor substrate and
CC       proper disulfide pairing of the substrate EGF domains is required for
CC       fucose transfer (By similarity). Plays a crucial role in Notch
CC       signaling (PubMed:12909620, PubMed:12917292, PubMed:17329366). Initial
CC       fucosylation of N/Notch by Ofut1 generates a substrate for Fng/Fringe,
CC       an acetylglucosaminyltransferase that can then extend the fucosylation
CC       on the Notch EGF repeats (PubMed:12909620). This extended fucosylation
CC       is required for optimal ligand binding and canonical NOTCH signaling
CC       induced by Delta/Dl or Ser/Serrate (PubMed:12909620, PubMed:12917292).
CC       Required for transport of N/Notch to the early endosome and to the
CC       apical membrane cell surface (PubMed:15692013, PubMed:17329366,
CC       PubMed:18194540). Facilitates secretion of N/Notch and may function as
CC       a chaperone independent of its fucosyltransferase activity
CC       (PubMed:15692013, PubMed:18194540). Fucosyltransferase activity is
CC       dispensible for embryonic developmental processes including
CC       neurogenesis (PubMed:18194540). Involved in Notch signaling during wing
CC       imaginal disc development (PubMed:18194540).
