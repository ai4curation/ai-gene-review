- Key points (see mus81-deep-research-manual.md for quotes):
  - Fly MUS81 forms MUS81-MMS4 heterodimer (Y2H, PMID:17603121).
  - No meiotic crossover role detected [PMID:17603121 "Unlike the case in fungi, we did not
    find any role for MUS81-MMS4 in meiotic crossing over."] -> IBA "resolution of meiotic
    recombination intermediates" marked over-annotated for the fly.
  - Mitotic role: essential in absence of Blm [PMID:22022278], generates spontaneous mitotic
    crossovers together with SLX1 in Fancm mutants [PMID:25205745], backup to GEN for
    DNA-damage repair [PMID:28369583].
  - "mitotic intra-S DNA damage checkpoint signaling" IBA is seeded by S. pombe only
    (Cds1-dependent replication slowing); no fly evidence, MUS81 is a checkpoint target /
    effector not a signalling component -> over-annotated.
  - "DNA catabolic process" IEA: structure-specific cleavage of repair intermediates is not
    DNA catabolism (consistent with SCHPO/mus81 review).
- No NEW terms proposed: no biochemical or localisation work exists for the fly protein.
