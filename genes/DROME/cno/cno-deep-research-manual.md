# cno (Canoe) - manual literature research

Deep-research APIs were not available for this session; this summary was compiled manually
from cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and PubMed metadata searches. Provenance is
given inline.

## Identity

- Drosophila melanogaster canoe (cno, CG42312, FBgn0259212, GeneID 40620); UniProt A0A0B4KF82
  (TrEMBL, "Canoe, isoform I", 1818 aa). Ortholog of mammalian afadin (AFDN/AF-6).
- Domains (UniProt/InterPro): two Ras-association (RA) domains (39-133, 256-348), FHA,
  dilute (689-917), PDZ (1012-1097), and a long C-terminal intrinsically disordered region
  carrying actin- and alpha-catenin-binding motifs. PANTHER PTHR10398 (AFADIN), SF2.

## Molecular interactions (direct, biochemical)

- Rap1: "Cno binds to the activated form of Rap1 in a yeast two-hybrid assay, the two molecules
  colocalize to the adherens junction" [PMID:14504224].
- RanGTP: "We show that the Canoe Ras-association (RA) domains directly bind RanGTP"
  [PMID:22024168]; GST pull-down with GMPPNP- vs GDP-loaded Ran, co-IP in S2 cells.
- Pins TPR domain: "a previously uncharacterized portion of Canoe directly binds the Partner of
  Inscuteable (Pins) tetratricopeptide repeat (TPR) domain" [PMID:22024168]; mapped to Canoe
  aa 1,755-1,950 (the "Pins-binding domain").
- F-actin: "GST-Cno (aa 1,560–2,051) also pelleted with F-actin" (cosedimentation)
  [PMID:19596848].
- DE-cadherin: "we documented a direct interaction of its PDZ domain with DEcad"
  [PMID:19596848]; but "Cno ... is not a core part of the cadherin-catenin complex".
- Pins co-IP in vivo in neuroblasts [PMID:18499457]; Robo complex in neurons [PMID:22815517];
  Ser/Notch complex in optic lobe neuroepithelium [PMID:23970418].

## Localization

- Adherens junctions in pupal retina [PMID:10639730], embryos [PMID:19596848] with enrichment at
  tricellular junctions; apical junctional belt in CPTI YFP trap [PMID:25294944].
- Cortical in S2 cells via C-terminal actin-binding domain; apical crescent in metaphase
  neuroblasts [PMID:22024168; PMID:18499457].
- "Cno localizes to AJs by a Rap1- and actin-dependent mechanism" [PMID:19596848]; RA domains
  mediate AJ/tricellular recruitment [PMID:29361565]; Abl phosphorylation of a tyrosine in the
  actin-binding domain mediates tension-dependent recruitment to tricellular junctions
  [PMID:33243859].

## Biological roles

- Junction-cytoskeleton linkage during morphogenesis: in maternal/zygotic null embryos
  "Apical constriction of mesodermal cells initiates but is not completed. The actomyosin
  cytoskeleton disconnects from AJs" [PMID:19596848]; germband extension
  [PMID:21613546]; parallel role with Pyd/ZO-1 [PMID:31188739].
- Apical-basal polarity establishment with Rap1 (Baz/AJ positioning) [PMID:23363604].
- Dorsal closure, as Rap1 effector; Cno also stimulates JNK pathway activity in a Rap1-independent
  manner [PMID:14504224].
- Neuroblast asymmetric division: spindle orientation, determinant localization; acts with
  Insc-Pins-Galphai and Mud [PMID:18499457]; mechanism: Canoe/RanGTP required for Mud
  recruitment to Pins(TPR) crescents [PMID:22024168].
- Eye: rhabdomere morphogenesis [PMID:10639730]; genetic interaction with Pak kinase mbt
  [PMID:17097274].
- Optic lobe: Ser-Notch-Canoe complex at neuroepithelial AJs [PMID:23970418].
- Axon guidance: Slit-Robo signalling [PMID:22815517].
