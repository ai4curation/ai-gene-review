# Elba1 notes

- UniProt Q9VR17 (Bsg25A, CG12205). BEN-solo protein; blastoderm-specific expression [PMID:9332389 "the transcript is maximally expressed during the late syncytial blastoderm stage, disappears rapidly during the cellular blastoderm stage"].
- Elba heterotrimer (Elba1, Elba2, Elba3) required for DNA binding and insulator activity [PMID:23240086 "Elba is an unusual hetero-tripartite protein complex that requires all three proteins for DNA binding and insulator activity"]; site [PMID:23240086 "Elba recognizes an asymmetric 8-bp sequence, CCAATAAG"]; DNA contacts via BEN domains [PMID:23240086 "DNA binding depends upon the BEN domains in Elba1 and Elba2"].
- In vivo insulator occupancy by DSG/DSP ChIP [PMID:24135698 "it is possible to capture Elba association with insulator elements in 2-5 h embryos using this new cross-linking procedure"].
- Individually binds Insv sites and represses reporters [PMID:25561495 "both Bsg25A and Elba2 can individually recognize Insv consensus sites efficiently"], but does not regulate Notch-dependent neural development [PMID:25561495 "Notably, Bsg25A and Elba2 can not substitute for the role of Insv in controlling Notch-mediated asymmetric cell divisions in the peripheral system"].
- Deep research (falcon) available: Elba1-deep-research-falcon.md; consistent with the above.

## Decisions
- InterPro2GO family transfers (transcription corepressor activity, negative regulation of Notch signaling, positive regulation of neuron differentiation) REMOVED based on direct negative evidence (PMID:25561495).
- IEP syncytial blastoderm development marked over-annotated (expression only).
- "chromatin silencing complex" kept as non-core (term misdescribes the insulator complex; Elba complex term proposed).
