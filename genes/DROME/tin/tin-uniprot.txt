CC   -!- FUNCTION: DNA binding protein (PubMed:9694800). Acts synergistically
CC       with the Mad-Med transcription factor complex to regulate certain dpp-
CC       responsive genes (PubMed:9694800). Required for the development of
CC       heart and visceral muscle; for the formation of somatic muscles. Has a
CC       crucial function in the early mesodermal subdivisions.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1982429, ECO:0000269|PubMed:32950464,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:7915669, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9694800}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Is initially expressed throughout the presumptive
CC       mesoderm and becomes restricted to cardiac and visceral muscle
CC       (PubMed:1982429). Expressed in dorsal mesoderm of stage 10-11 embryos
CC       (PubMed:9694800). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1982429,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9694800}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: By a combination of autoregulation and dpp/BMP signaling.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9694800}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Absence of cardiac and midgut visceral muscle,
CC       and defects in a subset of dorsal body wall muscles.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:7915669}.
