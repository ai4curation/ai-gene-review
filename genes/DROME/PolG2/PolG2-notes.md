# PolG2 (Q9VJV8) curation notes

- Accessory (beta) subunit of Pol gamma; physical association [PMID:9153213 "Demonstration of the physical association of the two subunits in the Drosophila enzyme"].
- Efficiency: [PMID:10506144 "This provides evidence of a critical role for the accessory subunit in the catalytic efficiency of Drosophila mitochondrial DNA polymerase"].
- Does not contact DNA: [PMID:7499423 "the alpha but not the beta subunit of mitochondrial DNA polymerase makes close contact with DNA"]. Therefore ssRNA and DNA/RNA hybrid binding IDA rows (PMID:11294646, abstract only, mentions only a proposed RNA-primer role) set UNDECIDED.
- Null mutants: [PMID:11917141 "Mutations in the accessory subunit cause lethality during early pupation, concomitant with loss of mtDNA and mitochondrial mass"].
- Cytoplasm IBA comes from the glycyl-tRNA synthetase side of PTHR10745; marked over-annotated.
- Falcon deep research present: summarizes PolG2 as noncatalytic processivity factor, notes fly enzyme is alpha-beta heterodimer (not alpha-beta2 as in human).
