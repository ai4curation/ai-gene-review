# SREBP (HLH106, dSREBP; CG8522; UniProt Q9VW37) notes

- Membrane fraction precursor, nuclear processed form [PMID:8577739 "we find the full-length gene product in the membrane fraction and a shorter cross-reacting form in the nuclear fraction"].
- DNA binding and transactivation [PMID:9632664 "HLH106 binds to both palindromic E-boxes and direct repeat sterol regulatory elements (SREs) efficiently"];
  [PMID:9632664 "The amino-terminal \"mature\" protein activates transcription from mammalian SREs in both mammalian and Drosophila tissue culture cells."]
- PE (not sterol) regulates processing in flies [PMID:11988566 "phosphatidylethanolamine, the major phospholipid in Drosophila, controls the release of sterol regulatory element-binding protein (SREBP) from Drosophila cell membranes"].
- Mutants are fatty acid auxotrophs [PMID:16753579 "These data indicate that dSREBP deficiency renders Drosophila larvae auxotrophic for fatty acids."]
- Falcon deep research [file:DROME/SREBP/SREBP-deep-research-falcon.md "Loss of dSREBP reduces expression of acetyl-CoA synthase (**ACS**), acetyl-CoA carboxylase (**ACC**), and fatty-acid synthase (**FAS**)"].
- All annotations accepted except heterologous perinuclear membrane and miR-210 lipid droplet formation (kept non-core).
