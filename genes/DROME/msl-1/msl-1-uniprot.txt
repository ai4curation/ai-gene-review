CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the male-specific lethal (MSL) histone
CC       acetyltransferase complex, a multiprotein complex essential for
CC       elevating transcription of the single X chromosome in the male (X
CC       chromosome dosage compensation) (PubMed:21726816, PubMed:7781064). The
CC       MSL complex specifically associates with the single X chromosome in
CC       males and mediates formation of H4K16ac, promoting a two-fold
CC       activation of X chromosome (PubMed:16543150, PubMed:18510926). In
CC       complex with msl-2, promotes ubiquitination of histone H2B
CC       (PubMed:21726816). In addition to its role in dosage compensation in
CC       males, regulates the activity of gene promoters: acts together with
CC       Cdk7 to promote phosphorylation of 'Ser-5' of the C-terminal
CC       heptapeptide repeat domain (CTD) of the largest RNA polymerase II
CC       subunit Polr2A (PubMed:27183194). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18510926, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21726816,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27183194, ECO:0000269|PubMed:7781064}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Component of the male-specific lethal (MSL) histone
CC       acetyltransferase complex, composed of mof, mle, msl-1, msl-2 and msl-3
CC       proteins, as well as roX1 and roX2 non-coding RNAs (PubMed:10679323,
CC       PubMed:11014199, PubMed:16543150, PubMed:18510926). Interacts (via PEHE
CC       domain) with mof (via HAT domain) and msl-3 (via MRG domain); both
CC       interactions are direct. Interacts with tamo via the nuclear
CC       localization signal (PubMed:12653959). Component of a maternal MSL
CC       subcomplex composed of mof, msl-1 and msl-3 (PubMed:32502394).
CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU01397, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10679323,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11014199, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12653959,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18510926,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32502394}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150}. Chromosome
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11014199, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18510926}. Note=Msl-1 is associated with hundreds of
CC       discrete sites along the length of the X chromosome in males and not in
CC       females, and is also associated with 10-20 autosomal sites in males.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11014199, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16543150,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18510926}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylation at Ser-18, Thr743, Thr-747 and Thr-751 is required
CC       to promote phosphorylation of 'Ser-5' of the C-terminal heptapeptide
CC       repeat domain (CTD) of the largest RNA polymerase II subunit Polr2A
CC       (PubMed:27183194). Phosphorylated by Cdk7 in vitro (PubMed:27183194).
CC       In contrast, phosphorylation at Ser-18, Thr743, Thr-747 and Thr-751
CC       does not affect its role in dosage compensation in males
CC       (PubMed:34426916). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27183194,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34426916}.
CC   -!- PTM: Ubiquitinated by msl-2. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23084834}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the msl-1 family. {ECO:0000305}.
