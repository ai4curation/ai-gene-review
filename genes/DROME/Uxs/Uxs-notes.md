# Uxs (Q9VSE8) notes

- Uxs is the fly UXS1 ortholog (UniProt: UDP-glucuronic acid decarboxylase 1, EC 4.1.1.35, rule-based).
  No direct enzyme assay of fly Uxs is in the cached literature.

  domain is luminal, as for mammalian UXS1.
