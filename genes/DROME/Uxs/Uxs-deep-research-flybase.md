# FlyBase gene description for Uxs (DROME)

UDP-xylose synthase (Uxs) encodes a Golgi enzyme that catalyzes the NAD-dependent decarboxylation of UDP-&agr;-D-glucuronate to UDP-&agr;-D-xylose. UDP-&agr;-D-xylose is required for the biosynthesis of the tetrasaccharide linker of heparan sulfate and chondroitin sulfate proteoglycans and for the addition of xylose to O-glucose on EGF repeats.
