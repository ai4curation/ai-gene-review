# cal1 (CG5148, Q9VEN2) - manual literature research

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled manually from cached
publications in `publications/` and PubMed records.

## Identity
- Drosophila melanogaster cal1 (chromosome alignment defect 1), CG5148, FlyBase FBgn0038478,
  GeneID 42065, UniProt Q9VEN2 (TrEMBL, 979 aa). Crystal structures 6XWS/6XWT/6XWV.
- Fly-specific protein; no detectable sequence homology to HJURP/Scm3, yet functionally the
  CENP-A (CID) chaperone of Drosophila.

## Key findings
- Identified in a genome-wide S2-cell RNAi screen for spindle assembly genes; kinetochore
  localization reported [PMID:17412918, abstract only].
- Cal1 links CENP-A/Cid and CENP-C: "Cenp-A/Cid and Cenp-C interact with the N- and C-terminal
  domains of Cal1, respectively." Low centromeric copy number; "Cal1 limits centromeric
  deposition of Cenp-A/Cid and Cenp-C during exit from mitosis" [PMID:20940262, abstract only].
- Nucleolar partner Modulo: "Notably, CAL1 localizes to both centromeres and the nucleolus."
  Modulo depletion reduces nucleolar CAL1 and recruitment of new CAL1 to centromeres
  [PMID:23028777, full text].
- CAL1 is the CENP-A assembly factor: "CAL1 selectively interacts with CENP-A and is sufficient
  to assemble CENP-A nucleosomes"; tethering CAL1 to lacO "is sufficient to heritably recruit
  CENP-A, kinetochore proteins, and microtubule attachments"; "The CENP-A assembly activity of
  CAL1 resides within an N-terminal domain, whereas the C terminus mediates centromere
  recognition through an interaction with CENP-C" [PMID:24469636, full text].
- Meiosis: cal1 C-terminal truncation genetically interacts with cenp-C, disrupting centromere
  clustering and causing meiotic nondisjunction [PMID:24248385, abstract only].
- Structure: "an N-terminal CAL1 fragment wraps around CENP-A/H4 through multiple physical
  contacts, a C-terminal CAL1 fragment directly binds a CENP-C cupin domain dimer"
  [PMID:32134144, full text].
