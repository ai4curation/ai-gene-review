# Elba3 notes

- UniProt Q9VR19 (CG15634). No recognizable domains besides Elba3-specific Pfam families. Adaptor of the Elba complex [PMID:23240086 "Elba3 mediates the assembly of an active Elba complex by interacting with the N-terminal domains of Elba1 and Elba2"].
- Bypass with GST dimerization [PMID:23240086 "it is possible to circumvent the requirement for Elba3 by fusing a heterologous dimerization domain, in this case GST, to the N-terminus of full length Elba1 and Elba2"].
- Required for boundary activity in vivo [PMID:23240086 "all three are required for chromatin domain boundary function in vivo"].
- Deep research (falcon) available: Elba3-deep-research-falcon.md.

## Decisions
- protein-macromolecule adaptor activity accepted as core MF; boundary formation accepted; chromatin silencing complex kept as non-core (proposed Elba complex term).
