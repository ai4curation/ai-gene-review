# FlyBase gene description for ple (DROME)

pale (ple) encodes a cytosolic tyrosine hydroxylase that catalyzes the hydroxylation of L-tyrosine to form L-dopa, the first and rate-limiting step in the synthesis of dopamine (and eventually, melanin). Dopamine acts as a neurotransmitter regulating various processes. It is also a precursor for the synthesis of melanin and the cuticle sclerotization precursors, N-acetyldopamine (NADA) and N-&bgr;-alanyldopamine (NBAD).
