# Caf1-105 notes

## Identity
- UniProt A1Z898 (unreviewed), FBgn0033526, CG12892. CAF-1 p105 subunit; CHAF1B/Cac2 ortholog; WD40 repeats.
- A C-terminally truncated p75 form exists in separate CAF-1 complexes [PMID:11533245 "the putative 75-kDa polypeptide of dCAF-1 is a C-terminally truncated form of p105 that does not coexist in dCAF-1 complexes containing the p105 subunit"].

## Function
- CAF-1 = p180 + p105 + p55; recombinant complex active [PMID:11533245 "The purified recombinant p180+p105+p55 dCAF-1 complex is active for DNA replication-coupled chromatin assembly"].
- p105 is the contact for Asf1 (RCAF) [PMID:11533245 "The binding of dASF1 to dCAF-1 is mediated through the p105 subunit of dCAF-1"].
- Chromatin localization [PMID:11533245 "dASF1 and dCAF-1 p105 colocalized in vivo in Drosophila polytene chromosomes"].
- Deep research (falcon) additionally summarizes a role for p105 at Notch-responsive enhancers (Su(H) occupancy, H4 acetylation) and a proposed p75 role in protamine loading during spermiogenesis; not used for annotation (no cached primary sources).
