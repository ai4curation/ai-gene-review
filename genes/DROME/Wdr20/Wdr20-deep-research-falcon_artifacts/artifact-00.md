(pqac-00000017) | Supports annotation of WDR20-family proteins as WD40 scaffolding or allosteric cofactors rather than catalytic deubiquitinases. | **Ortholog-based inference only.** The mammalian structure does not establish the exact fly interfaces, activation magnitude, substrate recognition, or localization. |

(pqac-00000024, pqac-00000025) | Demonstrates a recent application of the complex to the degradation-versus-recycling decision for a membrane receptor. | **Ortholog evidence, not a fly substrate claim.** The study does not show that fly Wdr20 regulates integrins; it localizes complex activity, not WDR20 itself, to early endosomes. |

Evidence included cell lines, mouse models, three patient-derived organoids, and an **88-sample** HCC tissue microarray. (pqac-00000020) | Extends the conceptual role of WDR20 from catalytic activation to substrate-adaptor or scaffolding activity. | **Human disease evidence only.** c-Myc regulation, senescence, and oncogenic activity have not been demonstrated for fly Wdr20/CG6420. |

(pqac-00000018, pqac-00000022) | Supports a conserved role in receptor deubiquitination and trafficking and demonstrates physiological regulation in an intact nervous system. | **Nematode ortholog evidence only.** It does not establish glutamate-receptor regulation, neuronal function, or endosomal localization for fly Wdr20/CG6420. |
