# dlg1 (Discs large 1, P31007) -- manual literature review

No deep-research provider was available for this session. This summary was compiled by hand
from cached publications in `publications/` and PubMed searches. Quotes are verbatim from the
cached files.

## Identity and domain architecture

- Drosophila *dlg1* (CG1725) encodes Discs large, the founding MAGUK (membrane-associated
  guanylate kinase homolog). UniProt P31007: L27 (isoform S97 only), three PDZ domains, SH3, and
  a C-terminal guanylate kinase-like (GUK) domain; "Belongs to the MAGUK family" (UniProt).
- Two major isoform classes: DlgA (PSD-95-like) and DlgS97 (SAP97-like, adds an N-terminal L27)
  [PMID:18171947 "These differ only by the inclusion in DLGS97 of an L27 domain, important for the formation of supramolecular assemblies."].
- The GUK domain is a protein-interaction domain, not an enzyme. Ancestral reconstruction of the
  MAGUK GK protein-interaction domain (GKPID) shows the transition from enzyme to scaffold
  [PMID:26740169 "Specifically, we found that Anc-GK1PID has no detectable guanylate kinase activity, but it binds Pins with moderate affinity and is highly effective in orienting the mitotic spindle in cell culture"].
  The Dlg GUK binds GukH and Pins, and is regulated by the adjacent SH3 domain
  [PMID:19769408 "Membrane-associated guanylate kinases (MAGUKs) organize protein complexes at specific cellular sites by regulating interactions with their COOH-terminal guanylate kinase-like domains (GKs)."].

## Epithelial polarity and septate junctions

- Dlg localizes to the septate junction (SJ) at the apicolateral membrane
  [PMID:1651169 "the dlg gene product is localized in an apical belt of the lateral cell membrane, at the position of the septate junction"].
- Dlg is required for SJ maintenance and apicobasal polarity, and is a neoplastic tumour
  suppressor [PMID:8830775 "we show that the Dlg protein is localized on the cytoplasmic face of the septate junction and is required for the maintenance of this structure"].
- Dlg acts with Scrib and Lgl (the Scrib module) [PMID:10884224 "Our data indicate that the three tumor suppressors act together in a common pathway to regulate cell polarity and growth control."].
- Dlg stabilizes Scrib at the cortex [PMID:32414916 "Cortical recruitment of Scrib does not require palmitoylation or polar phospholipid binding but instead an independent cortically stabilizing activity of Dlg."].
- Dlg is required at tricellular junctions and associates with Gliotactin there
  [PMID:17032735 "Discs large is essential for the formation of the TCJ and the localization of Gliotactin."].

## Neuroblast asymmetric division and spindle orientation

- Dlg is required for cortical Lgl and basal determinant targeting
  [PMID:11117748 "Dlg protein is apically enriched and is required for maintaining cortical localization of Lgl protein."].
- Dlg links astral microtubules (via Khc-73) to cortical Pins/Galphai
  [PMID:16377571 "Khc-73 localizes to astral microtubule plus ends, and Dlg/Khc-73 and Dlg/Pins coimmunoprecipitate, suggesting that microtubules induce Pins/Galphai cortical polarity through Dlg/Khc-73 interactions."].
- In induced-polarity S2 cells, phosphorylated Pins LINKER recruits Dlg, and the Dlg GK domain
  acts through Khc-73 [PMID:19766567 "We conclude that the PinsLINKER recruits Dlg to the cortex, where the DlgGK domain promotes spindle orientation."].
- Dlg acts in a pathway parallel to Canoe/Ran/Mud [PMID:22024168 "double RNAi knockdowns of ran and the PinsLINKER pathway component dlg led to a more severe spindle orientation phenotype than ran RNAi alone"].

## Neuromuscular junction

- Dlg clusters Fas2 and Shaker through their C-termini [PMID:9390515 "the PDZ-containing protein Discs-Large (Dlg) controls the localization of these proteins, most likely through a direct interaction with their C-terminal amino acids"].
- Dlg stabilizes GluRIIB-containing receptors [PMID:15638945 "Our results suggest that DLG controls glutamate receptor subunit composition by selectively stabilizing GluRIIB-containing receptors at the synapse."].
- Dlg assembles the subsynaptic reticulum (SSR) [PMID:27838296 "Discs large (Dlg), a key protein that assembles the subsynaptic reticulum (SSR) in Type 1b boutons"].
- PAR-1 phosphorylation controls Dlg postsynaptic targeting [PMID:17224403 "PAR-1 directly phosphorylates Dlg at a conserved site and negatively regulates its mobility and targeting to the postsynapse."].
- Dlg, GukH and Scrib form a synaptic complex [PMID:11937021 "We show that DLG, GUKH, and SCRIB form a tripartite complex at synapses, in which DLG and GUKH are required for the proper synaptic localization of SCRIB."].
- DlgS97 is needed for adult behaviours (courtship, phototaxis, circadian activity) [PMID:18171947]
  and ethanol tolerance [PMID:23145041].

## Other reported phenotypes (indirect)

- Follicle cell patterning defects downstream of polarity loss [PMID:19948068].
- miR-34 target; RNAi raises Diptericin in S2 cells [PMID:27893816].
