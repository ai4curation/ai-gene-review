# Enterobacteria phage M13 — Gene III (G3P / pIII)

## Overview
- **UniProt Accession:** P69168 (`G3P_BPM13`)
- **Gene Symbol:** `III`
- **Protein Product:** Attachment protein G3P (pIII, minor coat protein), synthesized as a 424 aa precursor with an 18 aa N-terminal signal peptide cleaved to yield the 406 aa mature protein (residues 19–424) (`file:BPM13/III/III-uniprot.txt`).

## Domain Architecture & Structure
- Mature pIII forms a homopentamer (~5 copies per virion) together with a pentamer of pVI at the proximal ("pointy" / adsorption) tip of the filamentous virion (cryo-EM structure at 3.30 Å, PDB: 8IXK, 8JWX; X-ray structures of N1–N2, PDB: 1G3P, and N1–TolA complex, PDB: 1TOL) (`file:BPM13/III/III-uniprot.txt`).
- Comprises three functional domains separated by flexible glycine-rich linkers (G1, residues 86–104; G2, residues 236–274):
  1. **N1 (D1, residues 19–85):** Binds the C-terminal domain III of the host inner-membrane entry co-receptor TolA after pilus binding unlocks the N1–N2 interface.
  2. **N2 (D2, residues 142–228):** Binds the tip of the host conjugative F-pilus (primary adhesion receptor).
  3. **CT (C-terminal domain, residues 275–424, including transmembrane helix 398–418):** Anchors pIII in the host inner membrane prior to assembly, integrates with pVI and pVIII into the proximal virion tip, and mediates terminal scission/release of the nascent virion during viral extrusion (`file:BPM13/III/III-uniprot.txt`).
