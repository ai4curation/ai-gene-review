CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts in the assembly and extrusion of the bacteriophage by
CC       forming a channel across the host outer membrane. This channel is just
CC       large enough to allow a newly synthesized phage particle to pass
CC       through (13.5 nm in diameter and 22-24 nm in length with an inner pore
CC       of about 9 nm). The central pore is closed by a gate. Extrusion is a
CC       process of concomitant assembly and secretion and takes place at
CC       specific assembly sites where host inner and outer membranes are in
CC       close contacts. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P03666}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homomultimer (By similarity). The channel is composed of 15
CC       G4P subunits that confer a barrel-like structure (By similarity). Part
CC       of a G1P-G11P-G4P complex that spans the inner (G1P-G11P) and outer
CC       host membranes (G4P) (PubMed:35746635). Interacts (via N-terminus) with
CC       G1P/G11P; this interaction allows the formation of the transmembrane
CC       assembly machine that spans the host inner and outer membranes (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P03666,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35746635}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Host cell outer membrane
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P03666}; Single-pass type I membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the bacterial secretin family. {ECO:0000305}.
