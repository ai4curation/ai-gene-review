CC   -!- FUNCTION: Self assembles to form a helical capsid wrapping up the viral
CC       genomic DNA. The capsid is made of gently-curved alpha-helix rods and
CC       displays a filamentous structure with a length of 760-1950 nm and a
CC       width of 6 nm (By similarity). Belongs to the capsids of class I
CC       filamentous phages, which have a helical rise of about 16 Angstrom and
CC       a helical twist of about 36 degrees (PubMed:25587134). The virion
CC       assembly and budding take place at the host inner membrane (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P69539, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25587134,
CC       ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homomultimerizes. There are several thousands of this protein
CC       in the phage capsid (By similarity). Interacts with G3P; this
CC       interaction allows integration of G3P and G6P into the virion head
CC       pointy tip. Interacts with G6P; this interaction allows integration of
CC       G3P and G6P into the virion head pointy tip. Interacts with G7P; this
CC       interaction allows proper integration of G7P and G9P into the virion
CC       tail round tip. Interacts with G9P; this interaction allows proper
CC       integration of G7P and G9P into the virion tail round tip (By
CC       similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P69539,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P69540}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37669979}. Host
CC       membrane {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P69539}; Single-pass type I membrane
CC       protein {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P69539}. Note=Prior to assembly, the
CC       major capsid protein is found associated with the bacterial host inner
CC       membrane. The capsid is composed of approximately 2700 copies of the
CC       capsid protein G8P (Probable). {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: Each capsid protein forms a long alpha-helix. The mature is a
CC       straight helix with a kink before the last 11 amino acids. The N-
CC       terminus is acidic and the C-terminus facing the cytoplasm is rich in
CC       basic residues, which probably interact with the DNA core.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P69539}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the inovirus capsid protein family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
