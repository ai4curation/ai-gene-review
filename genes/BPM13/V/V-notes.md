# Enterobacteria phage M13 — Gene V (G5P / pV)

## Overview
- **UniProt Accession:** P69544 (`G5P_BPM13`)
- **Gene Symbol:** `V`
- **Protein Product:** Single-stranded DNA-binding protein G5P (pV), 87 aa (~9.7 kDa) OB-fold homodimer (`file:BPM13/V/V-uniprot.txt`).

## Biological Role in the M13 Lifecycle
- **Cooperative ssDNA Binding & Replication Switch:** pV forms a tight homodimer with an oligonucleotide/oligosaccharide-binding (OB) fold (solution NMR structures PDB: 2GVA, 2GVB; solid-state NMR structure of the viral ssDNA–pV nucleoprotein filament PDB: 8ACZ) (`file:BPM13/V/V-uniprot.txt`). As pV accumulates during infection, ~1,500 pV dimers bind highly cooperatively and without sequence specificity (except leaving the hairpin packaging signal exposed) to newly synthesized viral (+)ssDNA strands produced by rolling-circle replication (`file:BPM13/V/V-uniprot.txt`, [PMID:35746635]).
- **Pre-Extrusion Nucleoprotein Filament Formation:** By coating progeny (+)ssDNA, pV sequesters the single-stranded genome from host RNA polymerase/primase and DNA polymerase III, preventing conversion into double-stranded replicative form (RF) DNA and collapsing the circular (+)ssDNA into a flexible left-handed helical nucleoprotein rod (~880 nm long, ~8 nm diameter) with the packaging signal hairpin at one end (`file:BPM13/V/V-uniprot.txt`, [PMID:4939035, PMID:35746635]).
- **Docking to the Assembly Machine:** The pV–ssDNA complex docks onto the cytoplasmic face of the inner-membrane pI/pXI (G1p/G11p) assembly complex (whereas free pV without ssDNA does not bind), where pV dimers are stripped from the DNA and replaced by membrane-embedded coat protein pVIII during ATP-driven viral extrusion [PMID:35746635].
