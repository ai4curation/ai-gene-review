CC   -!- FUNCTION: Binds to DNA in a highly cooperative manner without
CC       pronounced sequence specificity (By similarity). During synthesis of
CC       the single-stranded (progeny) viral DNA, thousands of G5P homodimers
CC       bind to newly synthesized strands of DNA, preventing the conversion
CC       into the double-stranded replicative form and preparing the circular
CC       genome in an elongated conformation for assembly of a new virion
CC       structure. G5P is displaced by the capsid protein G8P during phage
CC       assembly on the inner bacterial membrane (By similarity).
