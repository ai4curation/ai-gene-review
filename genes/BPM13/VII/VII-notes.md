# Enterobacteria phage M13 — Gene VII (G7P / pVII)

## Overview
- **UniProt Accession:** P69535 (`G7P_BPM13`)
- **Gene Symbol:** `VII`
- **Protein Product:** Tail (distal tip) minor coat protein G7P (pVII, Coat protein C polypeptide I), 33 aa (~3.6 kDa) single-pass hydrophobic peptide (`file:BPM13/VII/VII-uniprot.txt`).

## Biological Role in the M13 Lifecycle
- **Distal ("Round") Virion Tip Plug:** In the mature M13 filamentous virion (cryo-EM structure at 3.50 Å, PDB: 8IXL, 8JWW), five copies of pVII form a C5-symmetric α-helical plug at the apex of the distal ("tail" / round) tip, surrounded by a pentameric ring of pIX and the first layer of major coat protein pVIII (`file:BPM13/VII/VII-uniprot.txt`).
- **Initiation of Virion Assembly and Extrusion:** Prior to assembly, pVII inserts spontaneously (Sec/YidC-independently) into the *Escherichia coli* inner membrane (`GO:0020002` host cell plasma membrane) [PMID:35746635]. Together with pIX, pVII enters the inner-membrane pI/pXI assembly complex laterally and recognizes the exposed hairpin packaging signal of the progeny (+)ssDNA via its positively charged C-terminus, forming the distal cap that emerges first from the host cell to initiate concomitant virion assembly and extrusion (`GO:0099045` viral extrusion, `GO:0019068` virion assembly) (`file:BPM13/VII/VII-uniprot.txt`, [PMID:35746635]).
