CC   -!- FUNCTION: May initiate with G7P the virion concomitant assembly-budding
CC       process, by interacting with the packaging signal of the viral genome.
CC       The assembly-budding takes place at the host inner membrane. In turn,
CC       G7P and G9P are present at the end of the filamentous virion that
CC       emerges first from the bacterial host.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with G7P; this interaction allows integration of G7P
CC       and G9P into the virion tail round tip. Interacts with capsid protein
CC       G8P; this interaction forms the G7P/G9P round tip of the virion.
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P69537}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000269|PubMed:6945579}. Host
CC       membrane {ECO:0000305}; Single-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000305}.
CC       Note=Prior to assembly, is found associated with the bacterial host
CC       inner membrane. There are about five copies of this protein per mature
CC       phage that are located on the tail side of the filamentous virion with
CC       G7P (By similarity). {ECO:0000250}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The C-terminus comprising approximately one-third of the helix,
CC       is straight and nearly parallel to the main axis of the virion. A
CC       bending at the middle of the protein introduces a curvature similar to
CC       those of the G8P helices. The bend creates an angle of about 13 degrees
CC       between the N-terminal portion of the Gp9 helix and the main axis of
CC       the virion. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:37669979}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the inovirus G9P protein family. {ECO:0000305}.
