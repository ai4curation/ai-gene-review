# Enterobacteria phage M13 — Gene IX (G9P / pIX)

## Overview
- **UniProt Accession:** P69538 (`G9P_BPM13`)
- **Gene Symbol:** `IX`
- **Protein Product:** Tail (distal tip) minor coat protein G9P (pIX, Coat protein C polypeptide II), 32 aa (~3.7 kDa) single-pass transmembrane α-helical peptide (`file:BPM13/IX/IX-uniprot.txt`).

## Biological Role in the M13 Lifecycle
- **Distal ("Round") Virion Tip Outer Ring:** In the mature M13 filamentous virion (cryo-EM structure at 3.50 Å, PDB: 8IXL, 8JWW), five copies of pIX form a C5-symmetric pentameric ring that wraps around the central pentameric plug of pVII and interfaces with the first pentameric layer of major coat protein pVIII to form the rounded distal tip of the filament (`file:BPM13/IX/IX-uniprot.txt`). Each pIX subunit forms a single bent α-helix (residues 2–31) with an ~13° kink near the middle that matches the curvature of the adjacent pVIII coat helices (`file:BPM13/IX/IX-uniprot.txt`).
- **Initiation of Virion Assembly and Extrusion:** Prior to virion incorporation, pIX inserts into the *Escherichia coli* inner membrane (`GO:0020002` host cell plasma membrane) in a YidC-dependent manner [PMID:35746635]. Together with pVII, pIX laterally enters the inner-membrane pI/pXI assembly complex to recognize the viral (+)ssDNA packaging signal and nucleate the distal cap that emerges first from the cell during viral extrusion (`GO:0099045` viral extrusion, `GO:0019068` virion assembly) (`file:BPM13/IX/IX-uniprot.txt`, [PMID:35746635]).
