# A. fumigatus erg3C notes

- erg3C is the third A. fumigatus C-5 sterol desaturase homolog. PMID:16436696 explicitly left Erg3C for later work after demonstrating the major role of Erg3B, so the current review treats Erg3C as a predicted minor/conditional Erg3-family desaturase.
- PMID:18191972 measured the sterol pathway and discusses the A. fumigatus C-5 desaturase set.
- The 2024-2026 search did not find a direct erg3C deletion or purified-enzyme study.
