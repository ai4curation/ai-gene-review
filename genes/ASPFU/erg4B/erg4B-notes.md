# erg4B notes

PMID:27986720 identifies AFUB_007490 as erg4B, localizes Erg4B-GFP to the ER marker Erg11A-RFP, and shows that the erg4A erg4B double mutant blocks the terminal ergosterol step. PANTHER places Erg4B in the diverged SF48 branch but GO_Central still propagates the PTN000479182 fungal ERG4 C-24(28) reductase IBA to it, which fits the direct A. fumigatus evidence.
