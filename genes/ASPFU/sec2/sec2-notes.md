# sec2 notes

## 2026-11-20 Sec2/Rabin3 fungal-family review

- Reviewed `ASPFU/sec2` as the Af293 member of the long Sec2 branch in
  `PTHR14430`, distinct from the short Spo13-like meiotic GEFs.
- Accepted both GEF rows: PAINT's `PANTHER:PTN000374167` node is the broad
  Opisthokonta Sec2/Rabin3 GEF node, and InterPro `IPR040351` is the
  Rab3IL/Rab3IP/Sec2 domain.
- Accepted the fungal `PANTHER:PTN001024876` cell-tip IBA. The budding-yeast
  exception in the completed `yeast/SEC2` review does not apply to A.
  fumigatus, whose growth is genuinely hyphal/tip-directed.
- Added PMID:39852455, the 2025 A. fumigatus sec2 deletion study, as a
  medium-relevance primary reference. Its growth, autophagy-marker, CWI, and
  mouse-burden phenotypes are useful new evidence, but they are downstream
  phenotypes from Sec2p disruption rather than direct evidence for NEW
  autophagy, cell-wall integrity, or virulence GO rows.
