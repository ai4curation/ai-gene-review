CC   -!- FUNCTION: Guanine nucleotide exchange factor that plays an important
CC       role in regulating the growth and virulence, probably by regulating the
CC       autophagy pathway (PubMed:39852455). Affects the sensitivity to cell
CC       wall disruptors and the cell wall thickness by regulating the
CC       expression levels of the cell wall integrity (CWI) pathway genes, thus
CC       coordinating the growth and virulence (PubMed:39852455). Positively
CC       regulates the autophagy pathway to enhance the expression of CWI
CC       pathway genes in the presence of autophagy inducers (PubMed:39852455).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39852455}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Leads to slower growth and reduces the fungal
CC       burden and mortality of infected mice (PubMed:39852455). Significantly
CC       up-regulates the expression of atg1 and atg12 and accumulates more
CC       autophagosomes (PubMed:39852455). Leads to down-regulation of CWI
CC       pathway genes including the chitin synthase genes chsB and chsC, the
CC       alpha-1,3-glucan synthase genes ags1 and ags3, the beta-1,3-glucan
CC       synthase gene fksA, and 1-3-beta-glucan glucosyltransferase gene gel1;
CC       but not chsA, chsG and gel2 (PubMed:39852455).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:39852455}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the SEC2 family. {ECO:0000305}.
