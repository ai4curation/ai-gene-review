# ASPFU mvd1 notes

## 2026-11-22 fungal PTHR10977 review

- `ASPFU/mvd1` is UniProt `Q4WNV9` and PANTHER `PTHR10977:SF3`, matching the
  conserved fungal diphosphomevalonate decarboxylase clade.
- Current GOA has only PAINT and InterPro/UniPathway inferences for
  diphosphomevalonate decarboxylase activity, cytosol and mevalonate-pathway
  IPP biosynthesis.
- The cached A. fumigatus ergosterol-pathway papers provide pathway context:
  PMID:16110826 is an abstract-only ergosterol-pathway review, and the full
  text for PMID:22106303 links mevalonate metabolism to ergosterol and
  siderophore biosynthesis without directly assaying `mvd1`.
- Literature searches for newer A. fumigatus `mvd1` papers found pathway and
  transcriptomic context but no direct deletion, localization or purified Mvd1
  assay that would add a stronger species-specific GO row.
