# AFUA_8G05580 notes

## 2026-11-17 review

The first project QuickGO fetch returned no rows for Q4WC63/AFUA_8G05580, but the UniProt `DR GO` block already showed four GO-Central IBA rows. A direct QuickGO query for UniProtKB:Q4WC63 returned 10 current rows: four PAINT rows, a Rhea/EC acetyl-CoA hydrolase row, InterPro2GO rows for the transferase activity and acetate process, a UniProtKB-SubCell cytoplasm row, and a UniProt ARBA mitochondrion row.

The UniProt flat file places AFUA_8G05580 in PANTHER `PTHR43609:SF1`, the fungal Ach1/CoaT subfamily that also contains characterized S. cerevisiae and Neurospora enzymes. Fleck and Brock purified the responsible A. nidulans acyl-CoA:carboxylate CoA-transferase and found that it used succinyl-, propionyl- and acetyl-CoA as CoASH donors, while acetyl-CoA hydrolase activity was hardly detectable [PMID:18331473 "The enzyme used succinyl-, propionyl- and acetyl-CoA as CoASH donors and the corresponding acids as acceptor molecules. Although the protein displayed high sequence similarity to acetyl-CoA hydrolases this activity was hardly detectable."]. Fleck and Brock then showed that yeast Ach1p has highest activity for succinyl-CoA:acetate transfer with only a minor acetyl-CoA hydrolase side activity, and that A. nidulans coaT complements a yeast ach1 mutant [PMID:19298859 "Ach1p showed highest specific activity for the CoASH transfer from succinyl-CoA to acetate and only a minor acetyl-CoA-hydrolase activity. Complementation of an ach1 mutant with the coaT gene reversed the growth defect on acetate confirming the in vivo function"]. Those papers support reviewing Q4WC63 as an inherited mitochondrial acetate CoA-transferase, with the hydrolase annotations kept as non-core minor side-activity rows.
