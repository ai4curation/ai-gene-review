# A. fumigatus erg3B notes

- PMID:16436696 showed that deleting erg3B, alone or with erg3A, reduced ergosterol and accumulated non-C-5-desaturated sterols, establishing Erg3B as the major A. fumigatus C-5 desaturase in the tested strain and conditions.
- PMID:18191972 confirmed the three A. fumigatus C-5 sterol desaturase homologs in the reconstructed sterol pathway.
- The 2024-2026 search found A. fumigatus sterol-mutant papers but no newer Erg3B-specific activity or localization paper that changed the review.
