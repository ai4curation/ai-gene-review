# mcd4 notes

## Evidence

- A. fumigatus Q4W9R7/AFUA_4G03970, also called AfpigN, is the PTHR12250:SF0 ortholog of S. cerevisiae MCD4 and is supported by the PAINT, InterPro, and UniProt rows for the conserved ER GPI-anchor synthesis activity.
- UniProt reports AfpigN as a submitted A. fumigatus mRNA sequence whose submitter name already identified it as a phosphoethanolamine transferase involved in GPI biosynthesis, but no peer-reviewed A. fumigatus AfpigN functional paper was found.

## Newer-paper search

- No direct AFUA_4G03970, Q4W9R7, AfpigN, or A. fumigatus mcd4 experimental paper was found in cached publications or targeted newer-paper searches.
- The 2026 A. oryzae preprint reports that A. oryzae mcd4 disruption alters growth and wall morphology in another Aspergillus species, consistent with the conserved GPI-anchor role but not direct evidence for A. fumigatus mcd4 [PMID:42327324, "Disruption of the GPI ethanolamine phosphate transferase 1 (mcd4)"].
