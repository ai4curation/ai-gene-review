# erg5 notes

- UniProt Q4WK25 is the A. fumigatus Af293 erg5 / AFUA_1G03950 product, in PTHR24286:SF228 with S. cerevisiae Erg5, S. pombe erg5, and C. albicans Erg5.
- The A. fumigatus pathway has duplicated early/late sterol branches relative to Saccharomyces: erg3 has several paralogs, erg4 has erg4A and erg4B, and the pathway goes through eburicol before demethylation. This does not change the expected CYP61 C-22 side-chain desaturation performed by erg5.
- PMID:35437626 directly tested A. fumigatus erg5 and reports that deletion of erg5 blocks ergosterol biosynthesis and conidiation, with the phenotype verified using a Tet-on conditional-expression strain. This provides direct species support for the GO-Central PTN001209378 ergosterol-biosynthesis IBA.
- PMID:16110826 names the A. fumigatus ergosterol-pathway gene set and PMID:18191972 used GC-MS to analyze A. fumigatus ergosterol-pathway mutants, but both are abstract-only in the cache and are background here rather than exact Erg5 evidence.
- The ARBA `secondary metabolite biosynthetic process` row is not appropriate for the primary ergosterol-pathway CYP61 member; keep the specific ergosterol biosynthetic process instead.
