Gene Description

alg3 encodes a multi-pass endoplasmic-reticulum membrane enzyme of the ALG3 glycosyltransferase family. By homology to characterized ALG3 enzymes, it is predicted to catalyze the first Dol-P-Man-dependent lumenal mannose transfer in lipid-linked oligosaccharide biosynthesis, adding mannose in alpha-1,3 linkage to Man(5)GlcNAc(2)-PP-dolichol to form Man(6)GlcNAc(2)-PP-dolichol during assembly of N-glycan precursors for protein N-linked glycosylation.

ASPFU alg3 notes

2026-11-06

  • Falcon deep research was not available for this seed because agentapi was
    unavailable. I did not create an alg3-deep-research-falcon.md file.
  • A newer-literature search for Aspergillus fumigatus alg3, AFUA_5G11990,
    and Afu5g11990 did not recover a same-species experimental characterization
    of alg3. The useful ASPFU-specific evidence remains curated homology support:
    UniProt assigns Q4WVG2 as an ALG3-family ER membrane enzyme by similarity to
    S. cerevisiae ALG3, the PANTHER member inventory places Q4WVG2 in
    PTHR12646:SF0, and the completed PTHR12646 family review accepts
    PANTHER:PTN000291297 for the conserved ALG3 activity, ER location, and broad
    glycoprotein biosynthesis pathway context.
  • Reviewed anchors on origin/main for yeast ALG3 and pombe alg3 both
    retain the PTHR12646/PTN000291297 molecular-function IBA. The yeast review
    anchors the enzyme assay to S. cerevisiae ALG3, while the pombe review treats
    deletion of alg3 as phenotypic support for loss of the same lipid-linked
    oligosaccharide step rather than an in vitro enzyme assay.
