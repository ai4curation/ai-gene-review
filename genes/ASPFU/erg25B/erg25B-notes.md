# erg25B notes

erg25B is the second Aspergillus fumigatus C-4 sterol methyl oxidase paralog.
Blosser et al. 2014 found that deletion of either erg25A or erg25B caused
C4-methyl sterol accumulation, with a stronger accumulation in the erg25A
mutant and evidence for complementary rather than identical paralog functions
[PMID:25107308, "These paralogues each serve as a functional oxidase, as their
respective genetic null mutants accumulated elevated levels of C4-methyl
sterols."].
