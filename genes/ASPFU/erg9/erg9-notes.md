# erg9 notes

- Deep research was unavailable because the local provider keys were not
  configured, so this review used the cached UniProt record, the PTHR11626
  family review, the two abstract-only A. fumigatus ergosterol-pathway papers,
  and the full-text 2024 conditional-mutant paper.
- Ferreira et al. place erg9 in the A. fumigatus ergosterol pathway at review
  level: "About 20 genes are involved in the A. fumigatus ergosterol
  biosynthesis pathway." [PMID:16110826]
- Alcazar-Fuoli et al. used sterol profiling of A. fumigatus strains and
  mutants to refine the species pathway; the abstract states that mutant sterol
  composition "led to a better understanding of the ergosterol biosynthesis
  pathway in this important fungus." [PMID:18191972]
- Elsaman et al. constructed conditional A. fumigatus erg9tetOn and erg1tetOn
  strains and noted that "Erg9 and Erg1 are both essentially required to form
  squalene or, respectively, squalene epoxide which are precursors of
  lanosterol" in ergosterol biosynthesis. [PMID:39060235]
- The PTHR11626 family review puts Q4WAG4 in PTHR11626:SF2, the canonical
  squalene synthase subfamily, and supports accepting the IBA rows to
  GO:0051996, GO:0005789 and the fungal GO:0006696 node for A. fumigatus. The
  PAINT pruning concern for GO:0051996/GO:0045338 concerns inactive Arabidopsis
  SQS2 in SF3, not erg9.
