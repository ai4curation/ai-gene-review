# ASPFU erg20 notes

## 2026-11-22 review

- Reviewed the new GOA-derived stub for A. fumigatus erg20 against the
  Swiss-Prot record, the cached Aspergillus ergosterol/mevalonate papers, and
  the PTHR11525 PAINT sidecar.
- PMID:16110826 is a review of the A. fumigatus ergosterol pathway; its abstract
  reports that "About 20 genes are involved in the A. fumigatus ergosterol
  biosynthesis pathway", but it is not a direct erg20 enzymology paper.
- PMID:22106303 is full-text cached and establishes a mevalonate/ergosterol
  connection in A. fumigatus siderophore biology, but its mechanistic work is
  upstream of FPP synthase and does not require non-core GO process additions to
  erg20.
- Literature searching in the 2024-2026 window did not turn up a newer direct
  A. fumigatus erg20 biochemical or localization paper. The best current
  species-specific support remains the curated Swiss-Prot synthesis plus
  family-level PAINT inference from the eukaryotic FPPS node.
