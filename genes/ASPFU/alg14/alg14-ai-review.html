Gene Description

alg14 is the ER membrane-anchor subunit of the Alg13/Alg14 UDP-GlcNAc transferase complex. It recruits the soluble catalytic Alg13 subunit to the cytoplasmic face of the ER membrane, enabling the second step of dolichol-linked oligosaccharide assembly during protein N-glycosylation.

  • PubMed searches for "Aspergillus fumigatus" alg14, "Aspergillus fumigatus" "UDP-N-acetylglucosamine transferase subunit", and fungal ALG14/LLO combinations did not find an Aspergillus fumigatus alg14 functional characterization.

  • The molecular-function and complex assertions are consistent with the S. cerevisiae Alg13/Alg14 evidence: Gao et al. 2005 showed that Alg14 recruits Alg13 to the cytosolic ER face PMID:16100110; Bickel et al. 2005 showed that Alg13 and Alg14 form the active UDP-GlcNAc transferase for GlcNAc2-PP-dolichol synthesis PMID:16100113; and Noffz et al. 2009 extended this to a larger early LLO assembly complex with Alg7 PMID:19129246.
