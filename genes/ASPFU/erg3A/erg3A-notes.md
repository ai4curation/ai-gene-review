# A. fumigatus erg3A notes

- erg3A is one of three A. fumigatus C-5 sterol desaturase homologs. PMID:16436696 disrupted erg3A and erg3B and found Erg3B was the major C-5 desaturase under the tested conditions, with no obvious erg3A-only sterol defect.
- PMID:18191972 profiled the A. fumigatus sterol pathway and incorporated erg3A/B/C into the late pathway.
- The 2024-2026 search did not find a direct newer erg3A activity paper.
