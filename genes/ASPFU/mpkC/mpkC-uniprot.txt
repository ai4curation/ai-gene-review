CC   -!- FUNCTION: Mitogen-activated protein kinase; part of an osmotic and
CC       general signal pathways involved in regulation of the response to the
CC       cell wall damage, oxidative stress, drug resistance, and establishment
CC       of infection (PubMed:25459537, PubMed:26878695, PubMed:27538790,
CC       PubMed:31134001, PubMed:32743127). Required for growth on media where
CC       sorbitol or mannitol is the sole carbon source (PubMed:16998074). With
CC       sakA, plays a redundant or cooperative role in the conidial stress
CC       resistance (PubMed:25459537). Also plays a supportive role in osmotic
CC       stress adaptation when sakA is deficient (PubMed:25459537,
CC       PubMed:27538790). Involved in paradoxical growth, the cell wall
CC       integrity (CWI) pathway and biofilm formation (PubMed:26878695,
CC       PubMed:32743127). Acts by modulating sakA activity upon exposure to
CC       several types o stresses and during cell wall biosynthesis
CC       (PubMed:26878695). Also collaborates with sakA to allow ful virulence
CC       in a neutropenic murine model of invasive pulmonary aspergillosis
CC       (PubMed:26878695). MpkC and sakA have both independent and
CC       collaborative functions during the transcriptional response to
CC       transient osmotic stress, and mpkC plays a major role in the modulation
CC       of the response to DNA metabolism while activating mitochondrial
CC       functions and cation transport (PubMed:27706915).
