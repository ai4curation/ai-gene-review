# A. fumigatus erg10B review notes

## 2026-11-23 review

- Reviewed the new A. fumigatus erg10B stub against Swiss-Prot Q4WCL5, the
  PTHR18919 PAINT snapshot, and the cached A. fumigatus ergosterol/mevalonate
  literature.
- Erg10B is not merely predicted. The Q4WCL5 Swiss-Prot record cites Marshall,
  Bond, and Bruning 2018, DOI:10.1021/acscatal.7b02873, as Ref.6 for direct
  crystallography, catalytic activity, K(+) activation, kinetic parameters, and
  the homotetrameric subunit call.
- PMID:16110826 reviews the roughly 20-gene A. fumigatus ergosterol pathway and
  notes that "There are several duplicated genes in this pathway." PMID:22106303
  establishes mevalonate as a precursor shared by ergosterol and
  triacetylfusarinine C biosynthesis in A. fumigatus; neither paper is the
  direct Erg10B enzyme assay.
- PMID:17352532 is the conditional-promoter replacement screen that initially
  identified A. fumigatus erg10B/AFUA_8G04000 as essential, whereas
  PMID:32005728 localizes AfErg10A to mitochondria and shows that Erg10A is the
  distinct mitochondrial paralog.
- Searches in the 2024-2026 window found no newer paper directly revisiting
  A. fumigatus Erg10B kinetics, localization, or essentiality.
