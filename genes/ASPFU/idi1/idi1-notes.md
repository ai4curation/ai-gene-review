# ASPFU/idi1 notes

- Q4WM52 is the reviewed A. fumigatus Af293 type-I isopentenyl-diphosphate delta-isomerase in PTHR10885:SF0.
- Searches on 2026-11-22 for direct A. fumigatus idi1 biochemistry or mutants found no species-specific idi1 functional paper. Search strings included `"Aspergillus fumigatus" idi1 isopentenyl`, `AFUA_6G11160`, and `Q4WM52`.
- PMID:16110826 lists idi1/AFUA_6G11160 in the annotated A. fumigatus ergosterol pathway; it is a review and gene inventory rather than a primary idi1 assay.
- PMID:22106303 establishes mevalonate as a shared precursor for A. fumigatus ergosterol and triacetylfusarinine C siderophore biosynthesis under iron limitation, but does not assay idi1 itself.
