# erg25A notes

erg25A is the predominant Aspergillus fumigatus C-4 sterol methyl oxidase
paralog. The cached Blosser et al. paper deleted erg25A and erg25B separately
and found both are functional, partially compensating SMOs, with erg25A playing
the stronger role [PMID:25107308, "Characterization of the two genes encoding
SMOs in Aspergillus fumigatus revealed that both serve as functional
C4-demethylases, with Erg25A serving in a primary role"].

Re-review notes:

- Accepted the PTN000221246 C-4 methylsterol oxidase and ER membrane IBAs.
- Accepted the PTN000933387 ergosterol biosynthesis IBA.
- Kept sterol biosynthetic process as a correct non-core parent and iron ion
  binding as cofactor-level evidence.
- Marked lipid biosynthetic process too general.
- The hypoxia/DTT phenotypes in the paper were treated as downstream stress
  adaptation effects, not as new core GO process annotations.
