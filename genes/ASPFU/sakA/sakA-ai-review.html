Gene Description

SakA is a Hog1/Sty1-family mitogen-activated protein kinase in Aspergillus fumigatus that acts with the related MAPK MpkC in the high-osmolarity glycerol stress-response pathway. SakA is activated by environmental stresses, shuttles between the cytoplasm and nucleus under osmotic and cell-wall stress, and regulates transcriptional, cell-wall, carbohydrate-mobilization, oxidative, and biofilm responses that support adaptation to hostile growth conditions.
