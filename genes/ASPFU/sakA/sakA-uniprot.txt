CC   -!- FUNCTION: Proline-directed serine/threonine-protein kinase involved in
CC       a signal transduction pathway that is activated by changes in the
CC       osmolarity of the extracellular environment (PubMed:16702099,
CC       PubMed:24312504, PubMed:25459537, PubMed:27538790, PubMed:32743127).
CC       Controls osmotic regulation of transcription of target genes
CC       (PubMed:16702099). Involved in environmental stress response
CC       (PubMed:22678624). With mpkC, plays a redundant or cooperative role in
CC       the conidial stress resistance (PubMed:25459537). Also plays a
CC       supportive role in osmotic stress adaptation when sakA is deficient
CC       (PubMed:25459537). Involved in paradoxical growth, the cell wall
CC       integrity (CWI) pathway and biofilm formation (PubMed:26878695,
CC       PubMed:32743127). Also collaborates with mpkC to allow ful virulence in
CC       a neutropenic murine model ofinvasive pulmonary aspergillosis
CC       (PubMed:26878695). MpkC and sakA have both independent and
CC       collaborative functions during the transcriptional response to
CC       transient osmotic stress and sakA not only seems to modulate pathways
CC       involved in nucleotide, fatty acid, nitrogen and organic acid
CC       biosynthesis but is also important for the activation of genes involved
CC       in mitochondrial and endoplasmic reticulum functions (PubMed:27706915).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16702099, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22678624,
