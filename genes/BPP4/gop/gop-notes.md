# Enterobacteria Phage P4 Gene gop (Host-Killing Protein Gop) — Curation Notes

## Overview
Enterobacteria satellite phage P4 gene *gop* (`P13058`, 146 aa, ~16.6 kDa) is the promoter-proximal gene of the rightward `gop-beta` operon in the left-end nonessential region of the P4 genome [PMID:2403440, PMID:2183201].

## Genetic and Functional Characterization
- **Host-Killing Activity Suppressed by Beta**: Ghisotti et al. (1990) sequenced the nonessential region of phage P4 and demonstrated that the `gop` gene product kills *Escherichia coli* host cells when the cotranscribed Beta protein (`P13057`) is absent (`GO:0031640`, `killing of cells of another organism`) [PMID:2403440, "The newly sequenced region encodes three nonessential genes, which are called gop, beta, and cII (in order, from left to right). The gop gene product kills Escherichia coli when the beta protein is absent; the gop and beta genes are transcribed rightward from the same promoter."].
- **Domain Architecture**: Contains the conserved domain `PF09849` / `IPR019176` (`Phage_gop`). Mutations in `gop` were originally isolated as suppressors allowing growth of P4 `beta` temperature-sensitive or amber mutants (`gop` = **g**rowth **o**f **P**4 *beta* mutants) [PMID:2403440].
