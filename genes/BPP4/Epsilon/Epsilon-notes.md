# Enterobacteria Phage P4 Gene Epsilon (Helper Phage Derepression Protein gpEpsilon) — Curation Notes

## Overview
Enterobacteria satellite phage P4 gene *epsilon* (`P05463`, 95 aa, ~10.9 kDa) is located in the essential early region of the P4 genome (`PMID:6095206`, `PMID:2183201`).

## Biological Role
- **Derepression of Helper Prophage P2**: Lin (1984) sequenced the essential region of coliphage P4 and identified the `epsilon` gene (`epsilon am104`) encoding a ~10 kDa protein [PMID:6095206, "The epsilon gene likely encodes a 10 kd protein with epsilon am104 being located at the beginning of the gene."]. In *Escherichia coli* cells lysogenic for helper phage P2, gpEpsilon derepresses the resident P2 prophage by binding and inactivating the P2 immunity repressor C (`GO:0050434`, `positive regulation of viral transcription`), thereby inducing expression of helper P2 DNA replication and late morphogenetic/lysis genes required by satellite phage P4 [PMID:6095206, PMID:2183201].
