# Enterobacteria Phage P4 Gene cII (Protein cII) — Curation Notes

## Overview
Enterobacteria satellite phage P4 gene *cII* (`P13059`, 264 aa, ~30.1 kDa) is located in the left-end nonessential region of the P4 genome immediately rightward of `gop` and `beta`, but transcribed in the opposite (leftward) orientation [PMID:2403440, PMID:2183201].

## Genetic and Functional Context
- Ghisotti et al. (1990) sequenced the nonessential region of phage P4 and identified `cII` as a leftward-transcribed gene encoding a ~30 kDa protein (`P13059`) [PMID:2403440, "The newly sequenced region encodes three nonessential genes, which are called gop, beta, and cII (in order, from left to right)."].
- Although originally named `cII` based on the mapping of a clear-plaque mutation (`cII` mutation, Calendar et al. 1981), Ghisotti et al. (1990) showed that loss-of-function mutations in `cII` do not impair lysogenization, and that the original mutation caused a temperature-sensitive lethal phenotype rather than a true defect in lysogenic maintenance [PMID:2403440]. Its molecular function remains unknown (`PF11191` / `IPR020964`, `DUF2977`).
