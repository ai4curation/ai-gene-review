# Enterobacteria Phage P4 Gene Beta (Gop-Inhibiting Protein Beta) — Curation Notes

## Overview
Enterobacteria satellite phage P4 gene *beta* (`P13057`, 354 aa, ~39.9 kDa) is located in the left-end nonessential region of the P4 genome (`gop-beta-cII` gene cluster) [PMID:2403440, PMID:2183201].

## Genetic and Functional Characterization
- **Antitoxin/Inhibitor of Gop-Mediated Host Killing**: Ghisotti et al. (1990) sequenced the nonessential region of phage P4 and demonstrated that `gop` and `beta` are cotranscribed rightward from the same promoter, and that the `gop` gene product is lethal to *Escherichia coli* when functional Beta protein is absent (`beta` mutants such as *beta*am1 or temperature-sensitive *beta*ts) [PMID:2403440, "The newly sequenced region encodes three nonessential genes, which are called gop, beta, and cII (in order, from left to right). The gop gene product kills Escherichia coli when the beta protein is absent; the gop and beta genes are transcribed rightward from the same promoter."].
- **Predicted Fold**: Contains the conserved domain `Pfam:PF14350` (`Beta_protein`) / `InterPro:IPR025683` (`Protein_beta`), and together with Gop forms an anti-phage defense / toxin-antitoxin-like module in the P4 prophage lifestyle. However, its direct enzymatic substrate or biochemical mode of Gop neutralization has not been experimentally defined in the cached literature [PMID:2403440].
