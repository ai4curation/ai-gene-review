## Function

- pr1 mutants accumulate pelargonidin (red) instead of cyanidin (purple) in the aleurone
  [PMID:21385724, abstract].
- The protein has the cytochrome P450 hallmarks: "These domains include a heme-binding site (HBS),
  oxygen-binding site (OBS), and the characteristic hydrophobic membrane anchor present at the
  amino terminus." [PMID:21385724]. UniProt places Cys450 as the axial heme ligand (PIRSR).
- 35S::ZmF3'H1 complements Arabidopsis tt7 (F3'H-deficient), restoring cyanidin glycosides and
  brown seed coat: "these results demonstrate that the putative Zmf3′h1 sequence encodes a
  functional F3′H enzyme capable of rescuing the tt7 mutant phenotype in Arabidopsis."
  [PMID:21385724]. Transient expression restored purple pigment in pr1 BMS cells (data partly not
  shown), and a Ds insertion allele independently confirmed the role [PMID:21385724].
- No in vitro enzyme assay with recombinant ZmF3'H1 is reported in these papers; the activity is
  inferred from genetic complementation plus product chemistry (3'-hydroxylated vs
  3'-unhydroxylated flavonoids in Pr1 vs pr1).
- Sharma et al. 2012 extend the role to 3-deoxyflavonoids: Pr1;P1 cob glumes accumulate luteoforol
  (3',4'-dihydroxy) whereas pr1;P1 accumulate apiforol (4'-hydroxy); maysin (3',4') vs apimaysin
  silk flavones also depend on Pr1; P1 binds the Zmf3'h1 promoter (EMSA, ChIP) [PMID:23113982].
  Pr1 silks with more maysin slowed corn earworm larval growth [PMID:23113982].
- Regulation: c1/r1 required for pr1 expression in aleurone [PMID:21385724]; P1 directly
  regulates it in pericarp, cob glume and silk [PMID:23113982].

## Localization

Not experimentally determined. The N-terminal hydrophobic anchor and membership of plant
class II P450s suggest ER membrane anchoring with a cytosolic catalytic domain, but no assay
of ZmF3'H1 location is reported.
