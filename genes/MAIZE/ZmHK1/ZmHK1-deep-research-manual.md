# ZmHK1 (Zea mays histidine kinase 1; UniProtKB Q9FRY7) - manual literature research

No deep-research provider was available for this review; this summary was compiled by hand
from cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and PubMed searches
("ZmHK1 OR Hairy Sheath Frayed1 OR Hsf1 maize cytokinin receptor", 9 hits).

## Identity

- UniProtKB Q9FRY7 (TrEMBL, unreviewed), 974 aa, GeneID 541634, RefSeq NP_001104859.
- Domain architecture (UniProt FT, PROSITE): CHASE input domain 86-298, histidine kinase
  domain 366-655 (phospho-His369), receiver domain 830-966 (phospho-Asp880).
- PANTHER PTHR43719:SF51 "HISTIDINE KINASE 4" - i.e. the CRE1/AHK4 subfamily.
- Ortholog of Arabidopsis CRE1/AHK4/WOL [PMID:15064375 "Homology analyses revealed that ZmHK1,
  ZmHK2, and ZmHK3a/b were closely related to AHK4, AHK3, and AHK2, respectively"].

## Receptor activity

- Cloned from maize leaf cDNA; expressed in E. coli delta-rcsC cps::lacZ it conferred
  cytokinin-inducible reporter expression, i.e. it replaced the RcsC sensor kinase in a His-Asp
  phosphorelay in a ligand-dependent way [PMID:15064375 "at least three ZmHKs functioned as
  cytokinin-responsive HKs in E. coli"].
- Ligand preference in that assay: iP most effective; free bases much stronger than ribosides;
  unlike AHK4 it responds to cis-zeatin as well as trans-zeatin
  [PMID:15064375 "These results demonstrate that ZmHK1 recognizes cZ as a ligand."].
- Direct radioligand binding ([3H]tZ, live bacterial spheroplasts): nM apparent KD; ZmHK1
  binds iP best, tZ and cZ nearly equally, DZ poorly, adenine not at all
  [PMID:21778179 "ZmHK1 bound tZ and cZ almost equally well with moderate affinity"].
- Plant membrane assay (transient expression in N. benthamiana): free bases are the active
  ligands, ribosides bind weakly; ZmHK1 binding is strongly pH-dependent, maximal at pH 9
  [PMID:25609827 "Thus ZmHK1 has a unique property: unlike the other receptors, it binds with
  equal affinity the trans- and cis-isomer of zeatin."].
- Additional ligand-profiling studies with ZmHK1 in the bacterial assay: topolins
  [PMID:15728338], N9-substituted iP derivatives [PMID:22019467], C2-substituted aromatic
  cytokinin ribosides [PMID:26706318] (abstracts only consulted for the latter two).

## Subcellular location

- Lomin et al. 2011: ZmHK1-GFP and ZmHK1TM-GFP co-localise with the ER network and nuclear
  envelope in maize protoplasts; endogenous ZmHK1 (monoclonal antibody) co-fractionates with BiP
  and shows the Mg2+-dependent density shift diagnostic of rough ER; a small plasma-membrane
  pool is not excluded [PMID:21778179 "ZmHK1 co-fractionated with the ER marker BiP and
  demonstrated a diagnostic Mg2+-dependent density shift characteristic for the ER in density
  gradients."].

## Genetics / physiology

- Hairy Sheath Frayed1 (Hsf1): three semidominant gain-of-function missense alleles in the
  ZmHK1 ligand-binding (CHASE) domain cause cytokinin hypersignalling and ectopic proximal
  (sheath/auricle/ligule) outgrowths from the blade margin [PMID:32205456, abstract only].
- Hsf1 hypersignalling reduces leaf growth via reduced cell division and elevates jasmonate
  [PMID:37631225, abstract only].
- Ectopic overexpression in Arabidopsis (35S::ZmHK1) delayed leaf senescence, reduced seed size
  and root growth, and induced ARR5 [PMID:24585212, abstract only].
- Expression: transcripts in all tissues tested, relatively abundant in tassels, low in leaf
  blades [PMID:15064375].

These developmental phenotypes reflect altered cytokinin signalling output; they do not by
themselves establish participation of ZmHK1 in specific developmental processes.
