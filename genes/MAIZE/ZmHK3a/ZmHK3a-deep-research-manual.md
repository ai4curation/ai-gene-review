# ZmHK3a (Zea mays histidine kinase 3a; UniProtKB Q76GZ9) - manual literature research

No deep-research provider was available for this review; this summary was compiled by hand
from cached publications (publications/PMID_*.md) and a PubMed search
("ZmHK3a OR (ZmHK3 AND cytokinin) OR (maize cytokinin receptor ZmHK)", 4 hits:
PMID:15064375, PMID:21778179, PMID:22019467, PMID:24585212).

## Identity

- UniProtKB Q76GZ9 (TrEMBL, unreviewed; PE 2 transcript level), 1201 aa, GeneID 541642,
  RefSeq NP_001104867.1, EMBL AB102957 (ZmHK3a cDNA).
- Domain architecture (UniProt FT, PROSITE): four predicted TM helices (21-40, 191-208,
  243-266, 544-567; Phobius) flanking the CHASE cytokinin-binding domain (311-535); histidine
  kinase domain 603-878 (phospho-His606 by ProRule); receiver-like domain 902-1034 and receiver
  domain 1058-1195 (predicted phospho-Asp959 and Asp1108).
- PANTHER PTHR43719:SF35 "HISTIDINE KINASE 2" - the AHK2 subfamily of plant CHASE-domain
  cytokinin receptors.
- Ortholog of Arabidopsis AHK2 [PMID:15064375 "Homology analyses revealed that ZmHK1, ZmHK2,
  and ZmHK3a/b were closely related to AHK4, AHK3, and AHK2, respectively"].
- The ZmHK3 locus produces two transcripts; ZmHK3a (1201 aa) retains exon 3, ZmHK3b
  (1049 aa, AB121445) skips it and lacks about a quarter of the input domain
  [PMID:15064375 "The longer cDNA with a reading frame of 1,201 amino acids was denoted
  ZmHK3a , while the shorter one (1,049 amino acids) was termed ZmHK3b"].

## Receptor activity

- Conserved catalytic residues: [PMID:15064375 "All of the amino acid residues required for
  the HK and receiver function ( Mizuno, 1998 ) were conserved (data not shown)"].
- Expressed in E. coli delta-rcsC cps::lacZ, ZmHK3a conferred cytokinin-inducible reporter
  expression, replacing the RcsC sensor kinase in the RcsC-YojN-RcsB phosphorelay
  [PMID:15064375 "cytokinin-dependent expression of the reporter gene was detected in the
  E. coli strains expressing ZmHK1 , ZmHK2 , and ZmHK3a"]. ZmHK3b did not respond
  [PMID:15064375 "ZmHK3b did not respond to any cytokinin tested"].
- Ligand profile in that assay: free bases > nucleosides, like ZmHK1; unlike ZmHK1, the
  phenylurea TDZ was active [PMID:15064375 "TDZ, a synthetic phenylurea cytokinin, was
  effective with ZmHK3a but not with ZmHK1"]; cis-zeatin also active
  [PMID:15064375 "In the E. coli mutants expressing ZmHK1, ZmHK2 , and ZmHK3a , cZ
  significantly activated the reporter gene expression"].
- Direct radioligand binding ([3H]tZ, live bacterial spheroplasts): nM apparent KD; ZmHK3a
  prefers iP, binds tZ better than cZ, DZ weakest, adenine inactive [PMID:21778179 "The
  properties of ZmHK3a resembled that of ZmHK1 except that ZmHK3a had a higher affinity to tZ
  than cZ, and BA was a rather moderate competitor"; "ZmHK3a preferred iP."]. Non-specific
  binding was high for ZmHK3a (about 50 %).
- Binding and reporter data agree [PMID:21778179 "In the case of ZmHK3a, data obtained by both
  methods were also in close agreement."].
- Synthetic N9-substituted iP derivatives that fail to activate CRE1/AHK4 and ZmHK1 gave weak
  activation of ZmHK3a in the bacterial assay [PMID:22019467 "several derivatives induced low
  but detectable cytokinin-like activation in maize ZmHK3a receptor"] (abstract only).

## Localization

- No direct localization data for ZmHK3a. The paralog ZmHK1 is in the ER membrane (immunoblot
  of fractionated root membranes and GFP fusions), and the authors generalize to the family
  [PMID:21778179 "Taken together, our results suggest that ZmHKs are localized predominantly in
  the ER membrane."]. Most high-affinity tZ binding of maize membranes is in endomembrane
  fractions. The UniProt endomembrane-system location is an ARBA prediction.

## Expression

- Transcripts in all tissues examined, highest in silk; low in leaf blades and cultured cells
  [PMID:15064375 "ZmHK3a transcripts were predominantly detected in the silk."].

## Physiology / genetics

- No maize loss-of-function mutant of ZmHK3a has been described. Ectopic overexpression of
  several ZmHKs in Arabidopsis altered root and shoot growth [PMID:24585212, abstract only];
  the abstract names ZmHK3b-OX, not ZmHK3a, among lines with phenotypes. These data are not
  used for process annotation.
