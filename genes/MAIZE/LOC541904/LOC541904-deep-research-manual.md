# LOC541904 (ZmEXPA1, maize alpha-expansin 1) - manual literature research

No deep-research provider was available; this summary was assembled manually from
cached publications, NCBI Gene and UniProt records.

## Identity

- UniProtKB A0A804NBL1 (TrEMBL, Zm00001eb149460_P001) is the B73 v5 gene model.
  It cross-references GeneID 541904, RefSeq NP_001105040.1 and STRING 4577.Q94KT7.
- NCBI Gene 541904 is "alpha expansin 1" (aliases expa1, GRMZM2G339122,
  ZEAMMB73_Zm00001d043047). The older TrEMBL entry Q94KT7 (gene name expA1,
  EMBL AF332169) carries the same RefSeq protein and cites Wu et al. 2001
  (PMID:11351085) for the cDNA.
- Li et al. 2014 give the v3 model: "EXPA1 [GRMZM2G339122_T01]" [PMID:24758373].

## Family and mechanism

- Belongs to the expansin family, expansin A subfamily (UniRule RU365023, PANTHER
  PTHR31867, InterPro IPR002963/IPR007118). Domain architecture: signal peptide
  (1-25), DPBB/EG45 domain D1 (49-161), and expansin C-terminal cellulose-binding-like
  domain D2 (171-250) (UniProt A0A804NBL1).
- Expansins induce wall extension without detectable hydrolysis: "Despite the presence
  of these conserved GH45 motifs, no hydrolytic activity has been detected for either
  α-expansin or β-expansin proteins." [PMID:16356276]
- Proposed mechanism: "A nonenzymatic mechanism has been proposed for expansin action,
  in which expansin disrupts noncovalent bonds that tether matrix polysaccharides to the
  surface of cellulose microfibrils or to each other" [PMID:16356276].
- EXPAs act on cellulose-cellulose junctions and mediate acid growth: "α-expansins
  (EXPAs), which act on cellulose-cellulose junctions" and "EXPAs mediate acid growth"
  [PMID:38724021, abstract only].
- Location: "With immunolocalization by light and electron microscopy, expansin proteins
  were localized to the cell wall" and "In cell-fractionation studies, expansins are
  found bound to the cell wall" [PMID:16356276]. No evidence in these sources that
  expansins are membrane proteins.

## Maize EXPA1-specific data (all expression-level)

- Wu, Meeley & Cosgrove 2001 cloned five maize alpha-expansins (Exp1-Exp5): "Upon
  sequence analysis we have divided the 13 cDNAs into two groups, α-expansins (five
  cDNAs, named Exp1–Exp5)..." and "The mRNA for α-expansin Exp1 was detected in several
  organs from juvenile and mature plants" [PMID:11351085]. Exp1 was among the most
  strongly expressed expansins on northern blots.
- Salt stress: "the transcript levels of ZmEXPA1, ZmEXPA3, ZmEXPA5, ZmEXPB1, ZmEXPB2
  and ZmXET1 were increased" in roots under 200 mM NaCl [PMID:24758373].
- Transcriptional regulation: SDG128 "directly targets ZmEXPA1" (H3K4me3) in leaf
  auricles [PMID:34612535, abstract only]; DBB2/PIF4 enhance transcription of
  "cell elongation-related genes, such as ZmEXPA1" under shade [PMID:39936861,
  abstract only].
- No biochemical (wall extension) assay, localization or mutant study specific to
  ZmEXPA1 protein was found.

## PMID:17225858 (Valdivia et al. 2007)

This paper concerns EXPB1 (Zea m 1d, a beta-expansin, GenBank AY197353), not EXPA1:
"a maize line containing a Mu transposon insertion in EXPB1 (GenBank Accession
AY197353), a gene that codes for Zea m 1d". It contains no EXPA1 data and is not
evidence for LOC541904 function; it is relevant only as family background
(expansin role in pollen-tube invasion of maternal tissues).
