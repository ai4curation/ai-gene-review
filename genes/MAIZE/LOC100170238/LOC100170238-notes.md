- fetch-gene resolved A0A804Q7I2 as expected (GeneID 100170238, Zm00001eb301540); 4

- Gene identity: LOC100170238 = ZmTSA of Kriechbaumer et al. 2008 [PMID:18430213
  "A full-length ZmTSA cDNA clone was isolated [GenBank:EU334442]."]; the native-complex
  MS peptides (GTTFEDVISMVK, TLEEAASPEEGLK) are in this sequence. NCBI Gene names it
  "tryptophan synthase alpha" (alias TSA). See -deep-research-manual.md.
- Key facts: plastid GFP localization; weak stand-alone IGP lyase activity [PMID:18430213
  "A low, but clearly detectable IGP turnover (Kcat = 0.006 s-1) was observed (Table 1)."];
  alpha2beta2 complex with ZmTSB1 boosts alpha efficiency 32-fold and performs the full
  Trp synthase reaction; ZmTSA is the alpha subunit of the native leaf TS complex.
  [PMID:31090986 "Here, a zmTrpA enzyme is activated by zmTrpB1 in the canonical TS
  whereas a zmBX1 enzyme displays a high IGP lyase activity without an interaction
  partner."]
