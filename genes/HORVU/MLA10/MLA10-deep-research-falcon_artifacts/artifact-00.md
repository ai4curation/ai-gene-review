| Question | Strongest direct evidence | Important limit |
|---|---|---|
| Does MLA10 associate specifically with AVRA10? | **2019:** AVRA10–MLA10 produced **3.5-fold higher split-luciferase activity** than AVRA10-V/AVRA22-V or AVRA22 controls. Cognate association was also detected in yeast, where plant bridging proteins are absent. (pqac-00000033, pqac-00000040) | Split-LUC measures proximity or association rather than an equilibrium binding constant. Full-length proteins were not purified for an in-vitro binding assay. |
| Does the MLA10 LRR determine effector specificity? | **2021:** MLA10 and MLA22 LRRs differ at **58 amino acids**. In barley protoplasts, the **chimeric receptors** MLA10LRR22 with AVRA22 and MLA22LRR10 with AVRA10 reduced relative LUC activity by about **80%** and **40%**, respectively, consistent with transferred recognition specificity. (pqac-00000031) | These values concern **LRR-swap chimeras, not wild-type MLA10 or MLA22**. Reduced LUC is a proxy for cell death, not a direct fungal-growth measurement. |
| Where does MLA10 execute resistance and cell-death functions? | **2012:** In barley single-cell Bgh assays, haustorium indices were approximately **29%** for MLA10-YFP, **35%** for MLA10-YFP-NLS, **30%** for MLA10-NLS and **65%** for the inactive P-loop mutant K207R. A prior experiment reported approximately **52%** for nuclear-depleted MLA10-YFP-NES, similar to empty vector. Nuclear targeting retained resistance, whereas cytoplasmic enrichment enhanced cell death and nuclear targeting suppressed it. (pqac-00000029, pqac-00000030, pqac-00000034) | NLS/NES tags and heterologous *Nicotiana benthamiana* cell-death assays may alter trafficking or activity. The evidence supports compartment-dependent output but does not establish where native MLA10 is first activated. |
| Does activated MLA10 regulate MYB6/WRKY1-dependent transcription? | **2013:** Autoactive MLA10(D502V) increased the MYB6 reporter to approximately **28 units** with MYB6 and approximately **24 units** with MYB6 plus WRKY1, supporting relief of WRKY1 repression. The MLA10 CC domain also disrupted the WRKY1–MYB6 complex in interaction assays. (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000017) | D502V is an artificial autoactive MHD mutant, and reporter assays used heterologous protoplast systems. The values do not directly quantify endogenous barley transcription after AVRA10 recognition. |
| Is MLA10 attenuated by miR9863? | **2014:** In Mla10 barley, MIR9863a/b/c left the Bgh haustorial index near the control at **about 6–7%**. By comparison, in Mla1 barley, MIR9863a or MIR9863b increased the index from **less than 3%** to approximately **22%** or **15%**, respectively. MLA10-triggered cell death was likewise unaffected. (pqac-00000052, pqac-00000054) | This demonstrates allele-specific non-targeting under the tested expression conditions, not that MLA10 lacks all post-transcriptional regulation or can never be affected by small RNAs. |


*Table: Direct experimental evidence for barley MLA10 recognition, domain specificity, compartment-dependent signaling, transcriptional output, and miRNA regulation. Chimeric-receptor results are explicitly distinguished from wild-type MLA10.*
