Gene Description

Mercuric reductase (MerA, locus Tbd_1341) of the chemolithoautotrophic, facultatively anaerobic sulfur oxidizer Thiobacillus denitrificans. It is a 541-residue class-I pyridine nucleotide-disulfide oxidoreductase. One N-terminal heavy-metal-associated NmerA domain (GMTCDHC) is joined to an FAD/NAD(P)H-binding catalytic core that carries the redox-active CVNVGC disulfide and the MerA-specific C-terminal Cys-Cys pair. It is encoded as the last gene of a merRTPA operon inside a chromosomal metal-resistance island. By homology to the characterized Tn501 enzyme, it is predicted to be a homodimeric flavoenzyme that uses NADPH to reduce Hg(II), delivered by MerP/MerT, to volatile, relatively inert Hg(0). This confers resistance to inorganic mercury. No activity, expression, localisation or phenotype has been measured for this protein.

Core Functions

NADPH-dependent, FAD-containing mercuric reductase that reduces Hg(II) to volatile Hg(0) as the detoxifying enzyme of the chromosomal merRTPA mercury resistance operon. Hg(II) is acquired by the N-terminal NmerA domain and reduced at the active site formed by the redox-active disulfide and the C-terminal Cys-Cys pair. Predicted by homology; not assayed for this protein.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is the Tbd_1341 merRTPA operon of Thiobacillus denitrificans ATCC 25259 induced by Hg(II), and does it confer measurable mercury resistance and Hg(0) volatilization in this organism?

Q: In sulfur-oxidizing Thiobacillus species growing on mercury sulfide minerals, is MerA responsible for the Hg(0) produced, or do abiotic reductants generated by sulfur oxidation account for part of it?

Q: Does the single NmerA domain of Thiobacillus MerA accept Hg(II) directly from MerT/MerP, and how does the handoff compare with the two-domain MerA proteins?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express Tbd_1341 in a mercury-sensitive Escherichia coli host and measure Hg(II)-dependent NADPH oxidation and Hg(0) volatilization, with the C538A/C539A double mutant as a catalytic control.

Experiment: Expose T. denitrificans ATCC 25259 to sub-lethal Hg(II) and measure merRTPA transcript levels by RT-qPCR to establish MerR-dependent induction in this organism.

Experiment: Construct a merA deletion in a sulfur-oxidizing Thiobacillus and compare Hg(0) emission from metacinnabar incubations with the wild type, to test whether MerA is required for the volatilization attributed to these cultures.

clade rather than to a paralogous family member, and the identity gap quantifies it: 61.2% to the
characterised MerA against 33.3% to the SF1 quinone reductase that seeds the GO:0003955 IBD and 32.2%
to the SF4 RclA representative. It is sequence evidence only: no activity, expression or phenotype has
been measured for this protein.
