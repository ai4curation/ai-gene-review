# Gene 4 (Transcriptional regulator gp4 / p4, P03682) — Curation Notes

## Summary of Function
Gene `4` of *Bacillus phage phi29* (`NCBITaxon:2884424`) encodes the 125-amino-acid sequence-specific DNA-binding transcriptional regulator (gp4, p4; UniProt `P03682`). Protein p4 forms a homodimer in solution and binds specific inverted-repeat recognition sites (sites 1, 2, and 3) in the central intergenic promoter region of the phi29 genome (`GO:0003700`, `GO:0043565`), inducing a pronounced (~80–85°) DNA bend (`PMID:2107318`, `file:BPPH2/4/4-uniprot.txt`).
Through direct protein-protein contacts with the C-terminal domain of the $\alpha$ subunit ($\alpha$-CTD) of host *Bacillus subtilis* $\sigma^A$-RNA polymerase and with the viral architectural nucleoprotein p6 (`P03685`), p4 mediates the early-to-late transcriptional switch (`GO:0046782`) (`PMID:2107318`, `PMID:12426390`, `file:BPPH2/4/4-uniprot.txt`):
1. **Activation of late promoter A3**: Binds upstream of the late A3 promoter (which lacks a consensus -35 element), bends the DNA, and stabilizes closed-complex formation by contacting the $\alpha$-CTD of host $\sigma^A$-RNAP (`PMID:2107318`).
2. **Repression of early promoters A2b and A2c**: Binds overlapping/adjacent sites and, both directly via RNAP $\alpha$-CTD over-stabilization/promoter occlusion and cooperatively with histone-like protein p6 (`PMID:12426390`), represses the main early promoters A2b and A2c (`file:BPPH2/4/4-uniprot.txt`).

## Domain Architecture & Structural Features
- **InterPro / Pfam**: `InterPro:IPR008771` (Bacteriophage phi-29 transcription regulator Gp4), `InterPro:IPR038246` (Phi-29-like superfamily), `Pfam:PF05464` (`Phi-29_GP4`).
- **Structure**: Elongated homodimer with an N-terminal helix-turn-helix-like beta-turn/hook DNA-binding motif that inserts into the major groove of inverted repeats separated by 15 bp (`file:BPPH2/4/4-uniprot.txt`). Arg120 in the C-terminal helix directly contacts the C-terminal domain of the host RNAP $\alpha$ subunit (`file:BPPH2/4/4-uniprot.txt`).

## Critical Evaluation of Existing GO Annotations
- **`GO:0003677` (`DNA binding`, IDA `PMID:2107318`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`)**: Refined via `MODIFY` to `GO:0043565` (`sequence-specific DNA binding`) and `GO:0003700` (`DNA-binding transcription factor activity`).
- **`GO:0003899` (`DNA-directed RNA polymerase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000002` from `InterPro:IPR008771`)**: Marked `REMOVE`. Protein p4 is a 125-AA non-catalytic transcription factor that recruits and modulates the host *Bacillus subtilis* RNA polymerase; it has no RNA polymerase catalytic activity of its own.
- **`GO:0016987` (`sigma factor activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120` from `InterPro:IPR008771`, `UniProtKB-KW:KW-0731`)**: Marked `MODIFY` to `GO:0003700` (`DNA-binding transcription factor activity`). UniProt historically applied keyword `KW-0731` (`Sigma factor`) to phage transcriptional activators, but p4 is not a sigma factor: it does not replace $\sigma^A$ on core RNAP, and instead binds upstream DNA inverted repeats and contacts the $\alpha$-CTD of the host $\sigma^A$-RNAP holoenzyme (`PMID:2107318`, `file:BPPH2/4/4-uniprot.txt`).
- **`GO:0006355` (`regulation of DNA-templated transcription`, IDA `PMID:8617213`, IEA `GO_REF:0000120`)** and **`GO:2000142` (`regulation of DNA-templated transcription initiation`, IEA `GO_REF:0000108`)**: Refined via `MODIFY` to `GO:0046782` (`regulation of viral transcription`), as p4 specifically regulates viral early (A2b, A2c) and late (A3) promoters.
- **`GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`, IMP `PMID:820555`)**: Marked `REMOVE`. In `PMID:820555`, gene 4 mutants fail to lyse the host cell only as a downstream pleiotropic consequence of failing to activate transcription from the single late promoter A3, which drives the entire late morphogenesis and lysis operon (encoding holin gp14 and endolysin gp15). Protein p4 has no direct role in cell lysis.
