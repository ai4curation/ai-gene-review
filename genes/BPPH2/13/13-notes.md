# Curation Notes: Bacillus phage phi29 Gene 13 (Tail-tip cell-wall-degrading enzyme & morphogenetic factor gp13, P15132)

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Product**: Tail-associated peptidoglycan-degrading enzyme and tail knob morphogenetic factor gp13 (UniProt `P15132`, 365 aa, ~40.8 kDa).
- **Bifunctional Domain Architecture**: X-ray crystallography (1.8 Å) and cryo-EM reconstructions ([PMID:18606992](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_18606992.md)) revealed that gp13 comprises two spatially separated catalytic domains connected by a flexible linker:
  1. **N-terminal GH24 lysozyme-like domain** (residues 1–160): Cleaves beta-1,4-glycosidic bonds of the *Bacillus subtilis* peptidoglycan polysaccharide backbone (`GO:0003796` `lysozyme activity`).
  2. **C-terminal M23 family zinc-dependent metalloendopeptidase domain** (residues 178–365, structurally homologous to *S. aureus* LytM): Coordinates a catalytic Zn2+ ion to cleave peptide cross-links of the peptidoglycan cell wall (`GO:0004222` `metalloendopeptidase activity`).
- **Localization & Role in Tail Assembly and Infection**: Present in ~2 copies packaged inside the distal end of the `gp9` tail knob (`GO:0098023` `virus tail, tip`). Both `gp9` and `gp13` are mutually required to assemble an active tail knob (`GO:0098003` `viral tail assembly`; [PMID:18394643](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_18394643.md)). During infection, gp13 is the first virion protein released from the tail tip to locally digest the *B. subtilis* peptidoglycan layer (`GO:0098932`), allowing the `gp9` tail tube to reach the plasma membrane for genome ejection (`GO:0099002`).

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
1. `GO:0003824` (`catalytic activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL** — Entailed by the specific catalytic activities on this record.
2. `GO:0006508` (`proteolysis`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE** — Reflects cleavage of peptidoglycan peptide cross-links by the M23 metalloendopeptidase domain, whereas the biological context is specifically captured by `GO:0098932` (`symbiont entry into host cell via disruption of host cell wall peptidoglycan`).
3. `GO:0008233` (`peptidase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL** — Entailed by `GO:0008237` (`metallopeptidase activity`).
4. `GO:0008237` (`metallopeptidase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** -> `GO:0004222` (`metalloendopeptidase activity`) — The C-terminal domain is an M23-family zinc-dependent metalloendopeptidase (LytM homolog) that cleaves internal peptide cross-links of peptidoglycan ([PMID:18606992](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_18606992.md)).
5. `GO:0016787` (`hydrolase activity`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL** — Entailed by `GO:0016798` and `GO:0008237`.
6. `GO:0016798` (`hydrolase activity, acting on glycosyl bonds`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **MODIFY** -> `GO:0003796` (`lysozyme activity`) — The N-terminal domain is a GH24 lysozyme-like glycosidase that cleaves the peptidoglycan polysaccharide backbone ([PMID:18606992](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_18606992.md)).
7. `GO:0031640` (`killing of cells of another organism`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **REMOVE** — False keyword mapping from `KW-0081` (`Bacteriolytic enzyme`). Unlike the late endolysin `gp15`, `gp13` is a tail-tip virion exolysin (2 copies/virion) that locally punctures the peptidoglycan cell wall to allow viral DNA entry without killing/lysing the host cell upon entry.
8. `GO:0042742` (`defense response to bacterium`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **REMOVE** — False keyword mapping from `KW-0081`; bacteriophage phi29 is a viral pathogen infecting *B. subtilis*, not a host organism mounting an immune defense response to bacteria.
9. `GO:0046718` (`symbiont entry into host cell`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL** — Entailed by `GO:0098932` and `GO:0099002`.
10. `GO:0046872` (`metal ion binding`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE** — Coordinates a catalytic Zn2+ ion in the C-terminal M23 metalloendopeptidase active site.
11. `GO:0071555` (`cell wall organization`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **REMOVE** — False keyword mapping from `KW-0961` (`Murein hydrolase`); gp13 degrades the host cell wall locally during phage entry rather than organizing or remodeling a cell wall for cellular physiology.
12. `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT** — Directly supported by [PMID:18394643](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_18394643.md), which showed that gp13 is required for assembly of an active gp9/gp13 tail knob.
13. `GO:0098004` (`virus tail fiber assembly`, IDA `PMID:18394643`): **MODIFY** -> `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`) — [PMID:18394643](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_18394643.md) showed that gp13 co-assembles with gp9 to form the distal tail **knob** (not the gp12* tail fibers/appendages); generalizing to `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`) accurately captures its morphogenetic role.
14. `GO:0098023` (`virus tail, tip`, IDA `PMID:18394643`): **ACCEPT** — Directly demonstrated by immunoelectron microscopy and cryo-EM reconstruction ([PMID:18394643](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_18394643.md), [PMID:18606992](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_18606992.md)).
15. `GO:0098932` (`symbiont entry into host cell via disruption of host cell wall peptidoglycan`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT** — Core biological process of gp13 during infection.
16. `GO:0098994` (`symbiont entry into host cell via disruption of host cell envelope`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL** — Entailed by its more specific child term `GO:0098932` (`symbiont entry into host cell via disruption of host cell wall peptidoglycan`) on this record.
17. `GO:0099002` (`symbiont genome ejection through host cell envelope, short tail mechanism`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **ACCEPT** — gp13 plugs the distal tail tip prior to ejection and drills through the peptidoglycan layer to allow short-tail genome ejection.
