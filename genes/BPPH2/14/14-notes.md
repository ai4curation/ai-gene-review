# Curation Notes: Bacillus phage phi29 Gene 14 (Holin / Antiholin gp14, P11188)

## Summary of Gene Function
- **Product**: Dual-start holin/antiholin protein gp14 (UniProt `P11188`, 131 aa full-length antiholin isoform `P11188-1`; 129 aa holin isoform `P11188-2` initiated at Met-3).
- **Function & Mechanism**: Gene 14 lies immediately upstream of the endolysin gene 15 in the late lysis cassette of phage phi29. As demonstrated by Steiner et al. ([PMID:8432697](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_8432697.md)), gp14 is a three-transmembrane-domain class I holin (functional homolog of phage lambda S protein) that accumulates in the host cytoplasmic membrane (`GO:0020002`) during late infection and forms nonspecific membrane lesions/pores (`GO:0140911` `pore-forming activity`) at a genetically programmed time. This collapses the membrane potential and allows the cytoplasmic endolysin (`gp15`) to access and degrade the cell wall peptidoglycan, resulting in host cell cytolysis (`GO:0044659` `viral release from host cell by cytolysis`, `GO:0044660` `viral release via pore formation in host cell membrane`).
- **Dual-Start Regulation**: Like phage lambda S107/S105, translation initiation at Met-1 produces a 131-aa polypeptide (`P11188-1`, Antiholin) with an extra positively charged N-terminal residue (Lys-2) that opposes pore formation, whereas initiation at Met-3 produces the active 129-aa pore-forming Holin (`P11188-2`) ([14-uniprot.txt](file:///Users/cjm/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/4b846598-3920-4c66-ba84-db24d924c2d1/scratch/prd1_phi29/genes/BPPH2/14/14-uniprot.txt)).

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
1. `GO:0020002` (`host cell plasma membrane`, IEA `GO_REF:0000044` and ISS `GO_REF:0000024`): **ACCEPT** — Correct localization of the three-pass transmembrane holin/antiholin in the *B. subtilis* cytoplasmic membrane.
2. `GO:0031640` (`killing of cells of another organism`, IEA `GO_REF:0000043`): **TOO_GENERAL** — Entailed by the more specific child term `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`) present on this record.
3. `GO:0044659` (`viral release from host cell by cytolysis`, IDA `PMID:8432697`): **ACCEPT** — Directly demonstrated by co-expression of gp14 with phi29 lysozyme (gp15) or heterologous endolysins in [PMID:8432697](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_8432697.md).
4. `GO:0140911` (`pore-forming activity`, IDA `PMID:8432697`): **ACCEPT** — Directly supported by [PMID:8432697](file:///Users/cjm/orca/workspaces/ai-gene-review/aigr-agy-phages/publications/PMID_8432697.md), which showed that membrane-bound gp14 forms nonspecific lesions in the cytoplasmic membrane that permit endolysin release.
