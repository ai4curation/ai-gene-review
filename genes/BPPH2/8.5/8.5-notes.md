# Gene Review Notes: Bacillus phage phi29 `8.5` (`B3VMP4`, `CAPSF_BPPH2`)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol**: `8.5`
- **UniProt ID**: `B3VMP4` (`CAPSF_BPPH2`)
- **Protein name**: Capsid fiber protein gp8.5 (head fiber protein)
- **Length**: 280 amino acids (29,500 Da)
- **Taxon**: *Bacillus phage phi29* (`NCBITaxon:2884424`)
- **InterPro / Pfam domains** (from `file:BPPH2/8.5/8.5-uniprot.txt`):
  - `InterPro:IPR022741` (`Phage_B103_Gp8`)
  - `Pfam:PF11133` (`Phage_head_fibr`)

## Literature & Bioinformatic Synthesis
- **Capsid Decoration Architecture**: gp8.5 forms the 55 protruding head fibers (~110 Å long) that decorate the outer surface of the prolate phi29 procapsid (`GO:0046729`) and mature capsid (`GO:0019028`, `GO:0098021` `viral capsid, decoration`, `GO:0098022` `viral capsid, fiber`) at quasi-3-fold symmetry axes where it contacts the C-terminal BIG2 domains of the major capsid protein `gp8` [PMID:21383126; PMID:22225806; PMID:409854].
- **Structure**: Each head fiber is a homotrimer of gp8.5 consisting of a pseudohexagonal base that anchors to the gp8 shell and an elongated fibrous shaft formed by a supercoiled triple repeating helix-turn-helix motif topped by a three-stranded beta-sheet tip (`PMID:21383126`, PDB `3QC7`/`3QDH`).
- **Non-essentiality and Accessory Cell-Wall Interaction**: Genetic and morphogenetic studies (`PMID:409854`) established that gp8.5 is completely dispensable for procapsid assembly, DNA packaging, and plaque formation; fiberless mutant virions assemble and infect *B. subtilis* efficiently. Thin-section electron microscopy (`PMID:21383126`; `file:BPPH2/8.5/8.5-uniprot.txt`) suggests that the head fibers may weakly interact with the outer *B. subtilis* cell wall to enhance initial virion positioning/attachment (`GO:0019062`), whereas productive receptor binding and cell-wall penetration are mediated by the tail neck appendages (`gp12*`).

## Annotation Review Summary
- `GO:0019062` (`virion attachment to host cell`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000043`): `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`. Head fibers are dispensable capsid decorations (`PMID:409854`) that may weakly assist virion positioning on the host cell wall (`PMID:21383126`), whereas primary receptor attachment is performed by the tail appendages (`gp12*`).
- `GO:0042802` (`identical protein binding`, IPI, `PMID:21383126`): `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`. Reflects homotrimerization of gp8.5 (`PMID:21383126`), which is a structural self-association rather than a standalone core molecular function.
- `GO:0046718` (`symbiont entry into host cell`, IEA, `GO_REF:0000043`): `REMOVE`. Derived from the UniProt keyword `Viral penetration into host cytoplasm` (`KW-1160`), which is a false positive for an external, dispensable capsid head fiber that does not participate in host cell envelope penetration or genome entry (`PMID:409854`; `PMID:21383126`).
- `GO:0046729` (`viral procapsid`, IDA, `PMID:22225806`): `ACCEPT`. gp8.5 is incorporated into the procapsid during head morphogenesis (`PMID:22225806`).
- `GO:0098022` (`viral capsid, fiber`, IDA, `PMID:409854`): `ACCEPT`. Directly supported by `PMID:409854` and `PMID:21383126`.
