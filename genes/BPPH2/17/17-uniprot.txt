CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the replication of viral DNA (PubMed:9858710). It
CC       is required at the very beginning of the virus amplification,
CC       conditions in which a low number of viral DNA molecules enter the host
CC       cell, possibly to recruit the limiting amount of initiation factors at
CC       the replication origins (PubMed:9858710). Once the infection process is
CC       established and the other replication proteins reach optimal
CC       concentration, it becomes dispensable (PubMed:9858710). Optimizes the
CC       binding of protein p6 at the viral DNA ends, thus favoring the
CC       initiation of replication (PubMed:12480935).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12480935, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9858710}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. Interacts with the histone-like protein p6; this
CC       interaction optimizes the binding of protein p6 at the viral DNA ends,
CC       thus favoring the initiation of replication.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12480935}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expressed in the early phase of the viral replicative cycle
CC       (PubMed:6274853). The C2 promoter controls protein p17 transcription
CC       and is strongly inhibited by the viral histone-like protein p6,
CC       probably because of the formation of a p6-DNA nucleoprotein complex at
CC       the right end of the viral genome. Thus, protein p17 is synthesized
CC       very early after infection, and its synthesis declines later on due to
CC       the inhibition of the C2 promoter by protein p6.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:6274853}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the phi29likevirus protein p56 family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
