Gene Description

FruK is a soluble ATP-dependent fructose-1-phosphate kinase of the PfkB carbohydrate kinase family. It converts the fructose 1-phosphate produced by the fructose PTS to fructose 1,6-bisphosphate for further metabolism. This activity is distinct from the free-fructose kinase used by the non-PTS bypass. The ATCC33913 protein is assigned its function from conserved FruK architecture and biochemical/genetic characterization in ATCC13951; activity toward ribose has not been established for this target.
