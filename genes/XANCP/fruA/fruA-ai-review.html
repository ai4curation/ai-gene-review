Gene Description

FruA is the membrane EIIB'BC component of the fructose phosphotransferase system. Its EIIC region translocates fructose while its EIIB phosphocysteine relay transfers phosphate to form intracellular fructose 1-phosphate. The duplicated N-terminal EIIB' region assists the cognate phosphotransfer interaction. FruB supplies phosphate from phosphoenolpyruvate, and FruK converts the imported fructose 1-phosphate to fructose 1,6-bisphosphate. The ATCC33913 protein is assigned this role by conserved architecture and work on the related ATCC13951 strain; Xanthomonas also has a distinct non-PTS fructose-utilization route.

Core Functions

Catalyzes transport-coupled phosphorylation of D-fructose to intracellular fructose 1-phosphate through its EIIC and EIIB domains. The residue-level function is inferred for ATCC33913 from conserved Cys126/type-2 architecture and ATCC13951 biochemistry; upstream FruB supplies the phosphorelay.
