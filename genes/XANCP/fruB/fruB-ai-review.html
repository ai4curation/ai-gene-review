Gene Description

FruB is the multiphosphoryl transfer protein (MTP) of the fructose-specific phosphoenolpyruvate-dependent phosphotransferase system (PTS) of Xanthomonas campestris pv. campestris. A single 838-residue cytoplasmic polypeptide carries an N-terminal fructose-specific EIIA domain, a central HPr domain and a C-terminal enzyme I domain, so the fructose PTS needs no separate enzyme I or HPr. The enzyme I domain autophosphorylates from phosphoenolpyruvate and the phosphoryl group passes through the HPr and EIIA histidines to the EIIB domains of the membrane permease FruA (EIIB'BC), which imports fructose as fructose 1-phosphate. fruB is cotranscribed with fruK (1-phosphofructokinase) upstream of fruA, and a fruB mutant cannot grow on fructose.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

PTS EIIA-to-EIIB phosphotransferase activity

Definition: Catalysis of phosphate transfer from the phosphorylated histidine of a phosphoenolpyruvate-dependent sugar phosphotransferase system EIIA domain to the cognate EIIB phosphoacceptor.

Justification: As for P. putida FruB (Q88PQ5), GO:0008965 covers only the enzyme I entry step; no GO molecular-function term describes the EIIA(Fru) domain's transfer of phosphate to the FruA EIIB domain, which the X. campestris MTP must perform for fructose uptake. Existing GO:0008982, GO:0022877 and GO:0090563 describe terminal transport-coupled transfer to the sugar. GO:0016772 is used only as a placeholder parent.

Parent term: transferase activity, transferring phosphorus-containing groups

Supporting Evidence:

  • file:XANCP/fruB/fruB-uniprot.txt : DOMAIN: The PTS EIIA type-2 domain is phosphorylated by phospho-HPr on a histidyl residue. Then, it transfers the phosphoryl group to the PTS EIIB type-2 domain.

Core Functions

FruB autophosphorylates from phosphoenolpyruvate on its enzyme I domain and relays the phosphoryl group through its fused HPr and EIIA(Fru) domains to the FruA EIIB'BC permease, driving fructose uptake as fructose 1-phosphate.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Why does loss of FruB block growth on mannose, mannitol and sucrose as well as fructose in X. campestris; are these sugars routed through fructose or fructose 1-phosphate, or does FruB also phosphorylate other EII permeases?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Purify FruB and FruA-containing membranes, measure PEP-dependent phosphorylation of FruB and of fructose to fructose 1-phosphate, and test FruB variants with substitutions at the predicted EI (His460), HPr (His175) and EIIA (His67) phosphohistidine sites.

Hypothesis: The enzyme I domain of X. campestris FruB is sufficient to phosphorylate its own HPr domain, and phosphate reaches FruA only through the EIIA(Fru) histidine.

Type: in vitro phosphotransfer and site-directed mutagenesis
