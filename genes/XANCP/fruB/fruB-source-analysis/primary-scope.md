# Xanthomonas fructose PTS: source and identity boundaries

Reviewed 2026-11-06. The downloaded publication caches remain unchanged. The web reader accessed the full PDFs below; direct Python downloads returned HTTP403 and are not represented as successful acquisitions. No complete article is redistributed here.

## PMID7496537 (Microbiology 1995), methods and results

Publisher: https://www.microbiologyresearch.org/content/journal/micro/10.1099/13500872-141-9-2253?crawler=true&mimetype=application%2Fpdf

Methods name NRRL B1459, equivalent to ATCC13951 in the frozen UniProt citation. The fruB insertion is polar on fruK. A separate FruK-negative control retained PEP-dependent phosphorylation, arguing against polarity alone explaining the FruB phenotype. Uptake and toluenized-cell phosphorylation therefore support relay participation; they do not measure purified P45597 kinetics. The paper distinguishes fruBK cotranscription from a proposed separate fruA promoter. Non-fructose growth phenotypes are not direct measurements of transport of those sugars.

## PMID1655739 (JBC 1991), methods, FruK results and discussion

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0021-9258(18)55249-2
Full PDF accessed through: https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=42ee7b2a10b6ed66fd5712e423d8b14c6712ca4d&repid=rep1&type=pdf#page=1

Methods use NRRL B1459 and derivatives. Extract activity and heterologous expression assign FruK to fructose-1-phosphate phosphorylation. The reported negative substrates are mannose and glucose; absence of ribokinase activity is not established. The separate free-fructose kinase belongs to the bypass, not FruK. The downstream partition between central pathways was unresolved. Conserved FruA Cys126 is mechanistic inference, not a target-specific substitution assay.

## Current sequence boundary (frozen UniProt sources)

All three records represent ATCC33913 (NCBITaxon190485), not the historical assay strain. Their EMBL AE008922 translations are AAM41650.1 (FruA), AAM41648.1 (FruB), and AAM41649.1 (FruK). The old translations are AAA27602.1, CAA85482.1, and AAA27601.1, respectively. Every record has sequence CONFLICT features against its historical sequence. The differences are not assumed to be gene-model errors. They preclude claiming exact current-protein assays, while conserved architecture and curated function support strain-qualified transfer.

P45597 has EIIA type-2 residues7–147, HPr161–253 and EI274–838; its conserved predicted relay histidines are67,175,460. The historical FruB is837 residues. PTHR46244:SF6 alone does not establish this fusion; the actual domains do. This review does not claim direct experimental measurement of each intermediate in currentP45597.
