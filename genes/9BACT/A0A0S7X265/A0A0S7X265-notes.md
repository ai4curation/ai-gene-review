# A0A0S7X265: bacterial SepCysS

This Parcubacteria bacterium DG_74_2 SepCysS converts phosphoseryl-tRNA(Cys) to cysteinyl-tRNA(Cys), completing the chemical conversion in an indirect cysteine-encoding pathway. The recombinant enzyme is active in anaerobic assays supplied with pyridoxal phosphate and sulfide. It lacks two conserved cysteines associated with the iron–sulfur form of some SepCysS homologs, so an obligatory iron–sulfur cluster is not inferred. Its sequence derives from a mixed metagenomic bin rather than a cultured native isolate.
