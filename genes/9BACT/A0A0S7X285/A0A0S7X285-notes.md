# A0A0S7X285: bacterial SepRS

This Parcubacteria bacterium DG_74_2 SepRS activates O-phosphoserine with ATP and attaches it to tRNA(Cys), generating the intermediate of indirect cysteinyl-tRNA synthesis. Activity of the sequence-defined recombinant protein is established in vitro. Its paired SepCysS locus supports an indirect cysteine-encoding system in this metagenomic context; the DG_74_2 bin contains multiple genomes and does not represent a characterized native isolate.
