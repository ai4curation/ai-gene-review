# Y981_02650 (A0A059XTE6): curation notes

- TrEMBL entry, Leptospirillum ferriphilum YSK (Nitrospirota). The name, EC 1.8.5.4 and
  membrane location come from ARBA rules. PE 3. gene_symbol is the ordered-locus tag
  (no gene name in UniProt), following the `genes/9BACT/I9U7L5` precedent.
- **Type I (SqrA)**: 57.5% identical to A. ferrooxidans AFE_1792 and 41.7% to the Aquifex
  aeolicus Type I SQR, with the cysteine triad conserved (C128/C160/C354)
  (`file:ACIF2/AFE_1792/AFE_1792-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md`). This supports the SQR call, but
  it is a more distant homolog than the Acidithiobacillus orthologs.
- Physiology: L. ferriphilum is [PMID:35486621 "a Gram-negative, and obligately aerobic
  iron-oxidizing chemoautotroph"]. In strain DSM 14647 an sqr was up-regulated under chloride
  stress, and the authors suggest a role in detoxifying endogenous sulfide [PMID:35486621 "The
  Sqr enzyme could play a role in the detoxification of endogenous H2S"]. That strain's locus
  was not mapped to Y981_02650.
- The UniProt submission paper on arsenite resistance [PMID:26064886] does not mention sqr.

## Annotation decisions

## CMMMech claim check
functional_role (traction-battery-hydrometallurgical-residue): "Membrane-bound
sulfide-quinone reductase (EC 1.8.5.4; Y981_02650) in Leptospirillum ferriphilum YSK
annotated in the reference proteome of this acidophilic bioleaching consortium member."
- This is worded appropriately as an annotation-level claim. It should not be read as
  evidence that L. ferriphilum contributes sulfur oxidation to leaching. The organism is an
  obligate iron oxidizer, and any SQR role is more plausibly sulfide detoxification.
