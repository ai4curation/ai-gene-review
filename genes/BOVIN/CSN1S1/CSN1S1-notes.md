# CSN1S1 (Bos taurus, P02662) - curation notes

## Identity
- Alpha-S1-casein, 214 aa precursor, signal peptide 1-15, mature chain 16-214; ~8-9 phosphoserines (B variant) [file:BOVIN/CSN1S1/CSN1S1-uniprot.txt "Important role in the capacity of milk to transport calcium phosphate."]
- Calcium-sensitive casein of the SCPP-derived casein family; alpha-s, beta = Ca-sensitive, kappa = Ca-insensitive micelle stabiliser [PMID:21245413 "Ca-sensitive caseins (α(s)- and β-caseins) bind Ca but precipitate at high Ca concentrations, whereas Ca-insensitive casein (κ-casein) does not usually interact with Ca but instead stabilizes the micelle."]

## Core biology
- Calcium phosphate sequestration: casein phosphate centres sequester amorphous calcium phosphate nanoclusters [PMID:23958008 "A typical casein micelle contains thousands of casein molecules, most of which form thermodynamically stable complexes with nanoclusters of amorphous calcium phosphate."]; sequestration happens in the Golgi region [PMID:14735250 "Sequestration of calcium phosphate by caseins occurs in the Golgi region of mammary secretory cells during lactation, where it helps to prevent calcification of the gland and to deliver high concentrations of calcium and phosphate to the neonate in the form of milk."]
- Milk Ca/Pi far exceeds solubility because of nanocluster formation [PMID:25632319 "As a result, calcium and phosphate concentrations in milk can be far in excess of their solubility."]
- Secretory pathway role: alpha-s1 is needed for ER export of beta- and kappa-casein (goat alpha-s1 null) [PMID:10504344 "We conclude that optimal export of the caseins out of the endoplasmic reticulum is dependent upon alpha(S1)-casein."]; membrane-associated disulfide-dimerised form in ER (rat/goat) [PMID:20704729 "Our data suggest that alphaS1-casein, which is required for efficient export of the other caseins from the endoplasmic reticulum, plays a key role in early steps of casein micelle biogenesis and casein transport in the secretory pathway."]
- Mouse alpha-casein (Csn1s1) KO: less milk protein and calcium phosphate secretion, pup growth delay [PMID:21789179 "Absence of α-casein protein significantly curtails secretion of other milk proteins and calcium-phosphate, suggesting a role for α-casein in the establishment of casein micelles."]
- Anti-amyloid / chaperone-like: alpha-s1 potently inhibits alpha-s2 fibrillation [PMID:18302322 "our findings indicate that alpha s2-casein fibril formation is potently inhibited by its natural counterpart, alpha s1-casein"]; alpha-s-casein chaperone-like activity against aggregation of whey proteins [PMID:10336443 "It also prevents aggregation of its natural substrates, the whey proteins."]; Holt review: micelle formation is the alternative to amyloid [PMID:23958008 "Fibrils do not form in milk because an alternative aggregation pathway is followed that results in formation of the casein micelle."]
- In vitro inhibition of Abeta(1-40) fibrillogenesis by bovine alpha-s1 [PMID:22155633 "α(s1)-Casein sequesters the amyloid peptide on its surface thus exerting a chaperone-like activity by means a colloidal inhibition mechanism."] -- non-physiological substrate.

## GOA-cited references assessed
- PMID:3585228 (Shamay 1987): steroids at ~1000x physiological inhibit casein synthesis/mRNA in explants -> the gene is a readout, not executor of hormone response.
- PMID:16174355 (Sakamoto 2005): GH induces alpha-s1 mRNA and alpha-casein release in BMEC -> readout.
- PMID:3549815 (Cohick 1986): abdominal casein infusion into cows; does not assay alpha-s1 localisation directly; claim (extracellular/milk) is trivially true.
- PMID:5168866 (Beery 1971): casein micelles in Golgi vesicles; abstract-only (no abstract text in cache).
- PMID:8186264 (Heegaard 1994): tPA bound to casein micelles in milk; alpha-s1 not named as tPA binder (kappa multimers and alpha-s2 dimer).
