# gshAB / gshF (Pasteurella multocida Pm70, Q9CM00) notes

- 2026-11-10: Review written for the glutathione_biosynthesis module. Automated deep research
  could not be generated in this session (no provider API keys); the review uses the UniProt
  record (HAMAP MF_00782 annotations) and the cached abstract of PMID:16339152.
- Primary characterization: GshF has an N-terminal gamma-proteobacterial GshA-like domain and
  a C-terminal ATP-grasp domain resembling cyanophycin synthetase, forms dimers, and its
  gamma-ECL activity is feedback-inhibited by GSH [PMID:16339152].
- PANTHER places the whole fusion in PTHR38761:SF1 through the N-terminal domain.
- InterPro2GO D-Ala-D-Ala ligase activity (IPR011095) removed as a wrong mapping and recorded as
  a source defect. Cytosol row is TreeGrafter IEA (GO_REF:0000118), not PAINT IBA.
