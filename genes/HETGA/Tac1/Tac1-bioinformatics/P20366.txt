CC   -!- FUNCTION: Tachykinins are active peptides which excite neurons, evoke
CC       behavioral responses, are potent vasodilators and secretagogues, and
CC       contract (directly or indirectly) many smooth muscles.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Substance P]: Is a ligand for TACR1, and triggers G protein-
CC       coupled receptor signaling via activation of phosphatidylinositol
CC       hydrolysis by phospholipase C. Substance P binding to TACR1 also
CC       triggers signaling via activation of adenylate cyclase activity which
CC       results in increased intracellular levels of cyclic AMP (cAMP) (By
CC       similarity). Is also a TACR3 agonist with low receptor affinity
CC       (PubMed:37391393). Basic secretagogue neuropeptide released from the
CC       terminals of specific sensory nerves (PubMed:30686732). Acts as a
CC       ligand for MRGPRX2 receptor in mast cells, initiating a signaling that
CC       mediates neurogenic inflammation and pain (PubMed:30686732,
CC       PubMed:34789875). Neurogenic inflammation includes mast cell
CC       activation, recruitment of immune cells and release of inflammatory
CC       mediators, such as cytokines and chemokines (By similarity). The
CC       inflammatory response can then activate or sensitize nociceptors,
CC       promoting pain (By similarity). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P06767,
CC       ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P41539, ECO:0000269|PubMed:30686732,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34789875, ECO:0000269|PubMed:37391393}.
CC   -!- FUNCTION: [Neurokinin A]: Is a ligand for TACR2, and triggers G
CC       protein-coupled receptor signaling via activation of G(q) and
CC       phosphatidylinositol hydrolysis by phospholipase C (PubMed:35882833).
CC       Binding to TACR2 also triggers signaling via activation of adenylate
CC       cyclase activity which results in increased intracellular levels of
CC       cyclic AMP (cAMP). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P06767,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35882833}.
