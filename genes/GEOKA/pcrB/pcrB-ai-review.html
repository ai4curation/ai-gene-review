Gene Description

PcrB of Geobacillus kaustophilus HTA426 is a homodimeric heptaprenylglyceryl phosphate synthase. It forms an ether bond between the C35 heptaprenyl donor and the C3 hydroxyl of sn-glycerol-1-phosphate, producing heptaprenylglyceryl phosphate. Its TIM-barrel active site belongs to the group I GGGP/HepGP synthase family; geranylgeranyl diphosphate is a much weaker alternative donor. The downstream fate and physiological role of the lipid in this strain remain less defined than its enzymatic synthesis.

Actual Falcon research was attempted with just deep-research GEOKA pcrB --provider falcon but returned403 authentication failure. No report was invented. Primary PubMed/official PDB searches and cached full-text or curated experimental evidence support this manual review. The PAINT-loss command reported the accession absent from the family tree; it cannot establish absence of lineage losses.

Q5L3C1 is GK0274 from HTA426. Official mappings identify it in4NAE/4NAF;4MM1 is the different archaeal O26652 comparator. PMID21761520/24684232 caches are abstract-only; the target's reviewed ECO0000269 FUNCTION/CATALYTIC ACTIVITY statement supplies target-specific C35 chemistry [UniProt Q5L3C1, “producing heptaprenylglyceryl phosphate (HepGP)”]. Curated weaker C20 use is not a reason to replace the physiological C35 core with GGGPS. No experimental row is rejected merely because the full paper is inaccessible. Magnesium binding remains an explicitly identified HAMAP mechanistic inference.

Primary-access follow-up

PMID24684232 publisher full text was subsequently obtained and independently read at https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/mmi.12596 . Its Figure2 uses a Bacillus delta-pcrB assay host. Figure5 distinguishes fjGGGPS I90A from wild type; groupII magnesium is modeled, not observed density. The local abstract cache remains unchanged. The separate full-text-scope note records these boundaries.
