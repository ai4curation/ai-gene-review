## Folder / symbol convention
UniProt gives no gene name, only `ORFNames=Tb927.5.4460`. Following the locus-tag folder
convention used for other symbol-less genes (e.g. `genes/SCHPO/SPAC1093.02`,
`genes/MYCTU/Rv0311`), the folder and `gene_symbol` are the TriTrypDB locus tag. The 2025
paper's working name "MVP1" is not an official symbol.

## Key findings
- Three paralogs: [PMID:40945728 "We detected three MVP paralogs in the T. brucei genome that we designated MVP1 (Tb927.5.4460), MVP2 (Tb927.10.1990), and MVP3 (Tb927.10.6310)"]. MVP1 is closest to animal MVP: [PMID:40945728 "Notably, MVP1 shares significantly higher sequence similarity with the mammalian MVP (67% for the rat MVP (Q62667)) than with its paralogs"].
- Note: Figure 1 legend writes "MVP1 (Tb927.5.4660)", a typo; the text and Fig. 6 use Tb927.5.4460.
- Localisation: [PMID:40945728 "revealed the formation of equally sized, small particles, sometimes elongated, localizing to the cytoplasm but not the nucleus"]. TrypTag: [PMID:40945728 "N-terminal tagging led to a patchy cytoplasmic signal for MVP1, and cytoplasmic points were observed for MVP2 and MVP3, resembling our imaging results"].
- Shell composition: [PMID:40945728 "The high enrichment observed for MVP2 and MVP3 throughout all MVP1 interactome analyses suggested that all three MVP paralogs are incorporated into a single vault shell"]; [PMID:40945728 "the observed colocalization data strongly suggest that each vault shell is a multimeric assembly of all three vault paralogs"]; AlphaFold3 predicts hetero- and homotrimers.
- vtRNA: [PMID:40945728 "Whilst this provided robust evidence that vtRNA is part of the vault particle, consistent with mammalian systems"]; nuclear vtRNA is probably vault-free.
- Interactors: TEP1 is the only clear overlap with mammalian vault interactomes; no vPARP ortholog co-purifies. High-confidence cohort dominated by splicing factors (TSR1, TSR1IP, U5-200k, U2AF35) and RBSR1.
- Dispensable: [PMID:40945728 "Monitoring growth over the same time-period revealed no difference to uninduced control"] (auxin degron, PCF); MVP1 RNAi did not alter cisplatin sensitivity.
- vtRNA biology (Kolev et al. 2019, PMID:31439669, abstract only): TBsRNA-10 is a vtRNA (TEP1-associated), Pol III-transcribed; knockdown impairs trans-spliced mRNA production in permeabilised cells. MVPs not studied.
- Signalling context: [PMID:16164760 "A key difference between host and parasite kinomes is the complete lack of ePKs that map to the tyrosine kinase (TK) and tyrosine kinase-like (TKL) groups in the trypanosomatids"].

## Curation decisions
- cytoplasm (IBA, IDA TrypTag, IEA, ISS): ACCEPT. Kept at cytoplasm (not cytosol): the evidence is light microscopy of particles; consistent with the human review accepting cytoplasm rows.
- nucleus (IBA, IEA, ISS): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED — direct imaging shows MVP1 excluded from the nucleus; human evidence for a minor envelope pool does not transfer.
- signal transduction (IBA, seeded by human MVP only): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED — human roles rest on pTyr/SH2 partners absent from trypanosomatids; differs from the human review's KEEP_AS_NON_CORE because the trypanosome evidence differs.
- NEW: structural molecule activity (IDA, PMID:40945728) as core MF, matching human MVP; NEW: ribonucleoprotein complex (IDA, vtRNA co-IP).
- No BP added: vault involvement in trans-splicing is association only (participation test not met), and MVP1 depletion has no phenotype.
- PAINT losses: none on this lineage (`just paint-losses TRYB2 Tb927.5.4460`).
