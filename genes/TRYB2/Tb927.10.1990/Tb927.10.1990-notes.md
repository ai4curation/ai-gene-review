## Identity and folder convention
- UniProt Q38C44 "Major vault protein, putative" (submitter name, ECO:0000313), `ORFNames=Tb10.70.5840`
  (old systematic name); the current TriTrypDB locus, from the UniProt VEuPathDB cross-reference,
  is Tb927.10.1990. No gene symbol, so the folder and `gene_symbol` use the current locus tag
  (locus-tag convention as in `genes/SCHPO/SPAC1093.02`).
- Named MVP2 in PMID:40945728: [PMID:40945728 "We detected three MVP paralogs in the T. brucei genome that we designated MVP1 (Tb927.5.4460), MVP2 (Tb927.10.1990), and MVP3 (Tb927.10.6310)"].
- **Full-length, divergent paralog, not a fragment.** 863 aa with the complete MVP architecture:
  Pfam Vault repeats (PF01505 x4, PF17794, PF17795, PF17796), MVP shoulder (PF11978), PROSITE MVP
  repeats from residue 58, and C-terminal region; its N-terminus resembles MVP repeat 1 of MVP1.
  Divergence: [PMID:40945728 "These three paralogs exhibit surprising divergence: MVP1 shares 58% and 56% sequence similarity with MVP2 and MVP3, respectively"]. PANTHER places it in PTHR14165:SF13 ("vault protein, putative-related"), a kinetoplastid subfamily distinct from MVP1's SF3. MVP2-clade orthologs are conserved across trypanosomatids but absent from some Euglenozoa.
