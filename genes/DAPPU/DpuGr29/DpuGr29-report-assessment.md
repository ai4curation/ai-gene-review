# DpuGr29 focused report: critical incorporation, 2026-09-20

The complete OpenScientist report, both provenance CSV tables, and HTML/PDF report variants were inspected. The CSVs contain judgments/evidence summaries, not the executed motif scan or a reconstructed tree. This assessment preserves their useful evidence while rejecting unsupported negative inferences.

## Actual ancestry and behavioral scope

The live PTHR21143 POST tree places exact E9FXF3/DAPPUDRAFT_346859 at PTN002326688 below PTN001080220, the positive arthropod courtship and chemosensory-behavior IBD node. Root PTN000475107 supplies the three neuronal locations. The lineage, full-response hash, IBD rows and current donor records are saved in DpuGr29-paint-source-check.json; no on-lineage NOT/IRD was found.

The report explicitly did not inspect the live tree (HTTP403) and nevertheless rejects courtship because only insect donors have experiments. A lineage expansion or lack of one-to-one orthology to a dipteran receptor does not refute a deeper ancestral assertion. Nor does a biological-process annotation become invalid because it is not a molecular function. PMID:19383158 establishes the distinct Daphnia expansion and discusses chemical mating cues, without demonstrating loss of an inherited behavioral capacity. Courtship is therefore retained as an inferred, non-core role. Ligand, circuit, expression pattern and the direction of behavioral modulation in Daphnia remain unestablished.

The report misidentifies FBgn0032416 as Gr32a: the actual tree/QuickGO mapping is Gr33a/Q9VKA5. Its claim that the IBD rests on one source study is also incorrect. Current exact-term annotations include Gr33a PMID:19765987, Gr32a/Q9VKJ7 PMID:18641642, and Gr39a/P58957 PMID:21416142 and PMID:25586066. These records are source provenance, not an evidential donor-count score. Gr68a/Q9VTN0 currently returns the IBA only; that snapshot does not negate the recorded historical IBD evidence.

PMID:19765987 full text directly assays Gr33a mutant/rescue courtship and gustatory responses; its Gr32a statement is background to prior work. It supports active chemosensory contribution to behavior, without establishing the same ligand or circuit in Daphnia. The report's proposed interpretation that absence of a knockout mating phenotype would prove annotation error is too strong: redundancy, assay context and incomplete perturbation could mask a capacity.

## Channel mechanism and the withdrawn NEW

The report usefully points to BmGr9 structure and to the target TYLVILYQF motif. A reproducible sequence-only scan independently confirms TYLVILYQF at E9FXF3 residues 364-372; its exact hash and an unrelated-input control are saved under DpuGr29-bioinformatics. This confirms a motif match, not functional gating, cation selectivity or oligomerization. No target-clade electrophysiology or structural channel analysis was supplied. The prior authored GO:0099094 NEW remains withdrawn, with channel function retained as a mechanistic question.

PMID:38187590 is the bioRxiv preprint updated as PMID:38573859, not an independent structural experiment. The report's four independent papers comprise that duplicate pair plus review-level sources. The peer-reviewed primary structure is the appropriate reference. Its introduction explicitly distinguishes ORCO-dependent ORs from GRs, which lack an analogous common coreceptor and can operate combinatorially or as homomers. Thus lack of Daphnia ORCO is not itself evidence against a GR channel; DpuGr29 subunit requirements remain unresolved for other reasons.

The report CSV recommends retaining taste as generic, but does not resolve the contact-versus-non-contact sensory modality. The original Daphnia primary and the Gr33a primary both distinguish these modalities within the wider GR repertoire. No taste IBD exists on the inspected target ancestry. The existing InterPro taste mapping remains UNDECIDED, with the unresolved classification stated explicitly rather than claiming aquatic animals cannot taste. No duplicate adjudication is requested: the completed report and its limits are now incorporated.
