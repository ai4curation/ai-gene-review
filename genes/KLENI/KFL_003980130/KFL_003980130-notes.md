# KFL_003980130 (Klebsormidium KfXYS1) notes, 2026-10-30

Algal comparator for the IRX10 family. Jensen et al. 2018 (PMID:29460505)
characterised KfXYS1 but gave no accession. Mapping to A0A1Y1IIS5 rests on
genes/ARATH/IRX10/IRX10-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md:

- It is the only one of 23 K. nitens GT47 (PF03016) proteins in the IRX10 range
  (0.72 identity to AtIRX10L; next best 0.47), PANTHER SF200 with AtIRX10L.
- The peptides encoded by the paper's cloning primers match: RSSLF at residue 26
  and the C-terminus RGDDEN.
- Gene-model discrepancy: UniProt has 470 residues; the paper's construct ends at
  residue 445 with the same ends. Not resolved; recorded as a suggested question
  rather than a gene_model_issue, because it is not known which model is right.

The paper found IRX10/XYS1 orthologs among algae only in Klebsormidiophyceae and
Zygnematophyceae. The repository analysis agrees for Chlamydomonas: its 42 GT47
proteins are all at background identity (<=0.36) to IRX10L.
