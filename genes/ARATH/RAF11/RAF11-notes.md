At1g67890 RAF11 is a B2 Raf-like kinase, not Rubisco assembly factor RAF1.1. F4HVH9 is the selected UniProt record; Q9C9V5 is an alternative product for this locus. Published constructs and mutants establish locus-level function; exact F4HVH9-versus-Q9C9V5 construct identity was not independently resolved and is explicitly qualified in the review/core.

PMID:37276398 full text read: RAF11 co-IPs and shows BiFC interaction with subclass III SRK2D (Arabidopsis SnRK2.2), not the tested Arabidopsis subclass I SRK2A. Recombinant RAF11 phosphorylates kinase-inactive SRK2D and increases its activity toward AREB1. RAF11 is constitutively active under ABA and dehydration comparisons; ABA acts through PP2C relief rather than direct RAF11 activation. Keep distinct from moss ARK activation by ABA.

GFP-B2 RAFs localize to cytoplasm; discussion specifies cytosol and not nuclei. The AtSubP nucleus prediction is therefore removed on direct contradictory localization evidence. BiFC with FM4-64 suggests a partial plasma-membrane interaction pool; membrane is refined with that limitation. Positive transcriptional regulation reflects kinase signaling, not a PAS DNA-binding activity.

PMID:25324504 abstract supports RAF11 kinase activity and parallel knockout/overexpression plus raf10 raf11 double-mutant dormancy phenotypes. Glucose-response-specific results were not retrieved; experimental annotation remains UNDECIDED, not removed.
