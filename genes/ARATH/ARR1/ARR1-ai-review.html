Gene Description

ARR1 (ARABIDOPSIS RESPONSE REGULATOR 1) is a type-B response regulator and the founding member of the subfamily-1 type-B ARR transcription factors that execute cytokinin signaling in Arabidopsis thaliana. It has an N-terminal receiver domain with a conserved phospho-accepting aspartate, a central Myb-related GARP (B-motif) DNA-binding domain that recognizes AGAT(T)-containing cis-elements, and a C-terminal transactivation domain. ARR1 is nuclear-localized independently of its receiver domain; phosphorylation of the receiver domain by AHP histidine phosphotransfer proteins relieves its autoinhibition, and ARR1 directly activates cytokinin primary-response genes, including the type-A ARRs (e.g. ARR6) that provide negative feedback, and developmental targets such as SHY2/IAA3 and WUSCHEL. Acting redundantly with ARR10 and ARR12, ARR1 controls root meristem size, shoot growth, shoot regeneration and stem-cell niche specification, chlorophyll and anthocyanin levels, callus formation, and negatively regulates drought tolerance.
