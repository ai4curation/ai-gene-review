Gene Description

FLOWERING LOCUS D (FLD) is a plant homolog of the human lysine-specific demethylase LSD1 (KDM1A), with an N-terminal SWIRM domain and a C-terminal FAD-dependent amine oxidase domain. FLD is a chromatin-associated histone H3K4 demethylase that promotes flowering in the autonomous pathway by repressing the floral repressor FLOWERING LOCUS C (FLC). FLD tightly associates in vivo with LUMINIDEPENDENS (LD) and the SET-domain protein SDG26, forming a co-transcriptional chromatin module that removes H3K4me1 over the FLC gene body; SDG26 contacts the CPSF subunit FY, coupling FCA/FY-mediated proximal polyadenylation of FLC antisense (COOLAIR) transcripts to FLD-dependent demethylation, reduced H3K36me3 and increased PRC2-mediated H3K27me3. Genome-wide, FLD localizes to actively transcribed genes with elongating RNA polymerase II and removes H3K4me1 at loci with convergent overlapping (antisense) transcription. FLD also interacts with the histone deacetylases HDA6 and HDA5 and is required for histone deacetylation at FLC, and acts partially redundantly with its paralogs LDL1 and LDL2. FLD is additionally required for systemic acquired resistance and for dehydroabietinal-promoted flowering.

Core Functions

LSD1-type histone H3K4 demethylase that, in a complex with LD and SDG26, removes H3K4me1 from the FLC gene body (and other convergently transcribed genes) downstream of FCA/FY-mediated proximal polyadenylation of antisense transcripts, leading to reduced FLC transcription, PRC2 silencing and promotion of flowering.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does purified FLD (alone or with LD/SDG26) demethylate H3K4me1/me2 nucleosomes in vitro, and does it require a CoREST-like partner as human LSD1 does?

Q: Is FLD recruited to FLC by FY-SDG26 contacts during proximal termination, by the APRF1/LD phosphatase module, or by elongating Pol II directly?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Reconstitute recombinant FLD (with or without LD/SDG26) and test FAD-dependent demethylation of H3K4me1/me2 nucleosomes; complement fld with catalytic-dead FLD (FAD-binding or active-site lysine mutants) and measure FLC H3K4me1 and flowering time.

Hypothesis: FLD catalytic activity is required for FLC repression and directly demethylates H3K4me1.

Type: In vitro enzymology + genetic complementation
