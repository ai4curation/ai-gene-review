# LPXB (At2g04560, F4IF99) notes

Deep research could not be run in this environment (provider auth failure); the notes below
come from the cached full text of Li et al. 2011 (PMID:21709257, PMC3136250), the UniProt
record, and PubMed searches.

## Identity
- Single-copy Arabidopsis ortholog of E. coli LpxB (lipid-A-disaccharide synthase, EC 2.4.1.182,
  GT19 family, Pfam PF02684 LpxB, PANTHER PTHR30372:SF4) [PMID:21709257 "Single copy genes encoding
  AtLpxB (AT2G04560), AtLpxK (AT3G20480) and AtKdtA (AT5G03770) ( Table 1 ) were also found, with 31,
  26, and 34% sequence identity to the corresponding E. coli proteins, respectively"].
- UniProt: predicted mitochondrial transit peptide 1..58 (ECO:0000255); "Belongs to the LpxB family".

## Genetics and lipid chemistry (Li et al. 2011, full text)
- Alleles: atlpxb-1 (SALK_087537), atlpxb-2 (SALK_087529), Col-0 background; homozygous nulls viable,
  no visible phenotype [PMID:21709257 "Null mutants of AtLpxA , AtLpxD2 , AtLpxB , AtLpxK , and AtKdtA ,
  as well as AtLpxC-RNAi knock-down plants, show no obvious phenotypic differences compared to wild type
  under laboratory conditions"].
- Lipid X (2,3-diacylglucosamine 1-phosphate; LpxB acceptor substrate) accumulates strongly
  [PMID:21709257 "Lipid X accumulates 42-fold in the atlpxb-1 mutant compared to the Col-0 wild type by
  this criterion"].
- UDP-2,3-diacyl-GlcN (LpxB donor substrate) is detectable only in atlpxb-1 [PMID:21709257 "The
  accumulation of lipid X together with UDP-2,3-diacyl-GlcN in three independently grown atlpxb-1 null
  mutant plants ( Figs. 3 and 4 ) demonstrates that both compounds are likely substrates for AtLpxB in
  vivo"].
- The LpxB product (disaccharide 1-phosphate) is seen only downstream, in lpxk and kdta mutants
  [PMID:21709257 "The disaccharide-1-phosphate intermediate generated by LpxB ( Fig. 1 ) accumulated in
  the atlpxk-1 and atkdta-1 mutants"].
- Second allele confirms [PMID:21709257 "The independently isolated atlpxb-2 , atlpxk-2 , and atlpxk-3
  mutants showed similar lipid accumulation patterns as the atlpxb-1 and atlpxk-1 mutants."].
- Epistasis: LpxC-RNAi and lpxd2 in the lpxb-1 background lower lipid X accumulation, placing LpxB
  after LpxC/LpxD2, as in E. coli.
- No in vitro activity assay and no E. coli complementation was reported for AtLpxB.

## Localization
- Full-length AtLpxB-GFP was too dim; a 5'-terminal (N-terminal region) fusion co-localised with
  MitoTracker in roots [PMID:21709257 "However, 5′-terminal fusions of AtLpxD1, AtLpxD2, AtLpxB, and
  AtLpxK to GFP showed the same mitochondrial pattern as full-length AtLpxA and AtLpxC"].
- In leaf mesophyll the fusion dots did not co-localise with chloroplasts [PMID:21709257 "In mesophyll
  cells, GFP signals were shown as 1 μm dots, but were not colocalized with chloroplasts (data not
  shown)."]. So the chloroplast ISM (AtSubP) prediction is not supported for the enzyme.
- Lipid X in atlpxb-1 fractions: mitochondria-enriched [PMID:21709257 "Lipid X levels in mitochondria
  were 3- and 48-fold higher than in chloroplasts or whole cell homogenates, respectively."]. This is
  the basis of the IMP mitochondrion annotation (substrate pool location, indirect for the enzyme).

## RAR context
- Wei et al. 2020 (PMID:32470107) flagged plant lipid A biosynthetic process annotations, including
  LPXB, as misannotations. Refuted by the mutant chemistry above (see projects/RAR_MISANNOTATION.md).

## Other literature checked
- PubMed searches ("LpxB/LpxK/lipid A AND Arabidopsis") return no further LpxB-specific work.
  PMID:21896887 (Duncan 2011, mitochondrial outer membrane proteome) discusses lipid A only via KDSB/CKS;
  PMID:28202596 (mtFAS mtHD) reports reduced 3-hydroxytetradecanoic acid (lipid A hallmark) in mtFAS
  mutants, supporting a mitochondrial acyl-ACP source but not about LpxB itself.
