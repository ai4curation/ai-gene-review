Gene Description

LUX ARRHYTHMO (LUX; also PHYTOCLOCK 1, PCL1) is a nuclear GARP-family single-MYB-domain transcription factor and the DNA-binding subunit of the evening complex (EC) of the Arabidopsis circadian clock, made up of LUX, ELF3 and ELF4. LUX binds the LUX binding site (LBS; consensus GAT(A/T)CG) through its MYB DNA-binding domain and recruits ELF3, and through ELF3 also ELF4, to target promoters. At dusk and in the early night the EC represses the morning-loop gene PRR9, LUX itself (a negative autoregulatory loop), and growth regulators such as PIF4 and PIF5, which couples the oscillator to diurnal gating of hypocotyl growth. LUX expression is circadian, peaking in the evening, and is repressed in the morning by CCA1 and LHY through evening elements in its promoter. lux loss-of-function mutants are arrhythmic in continuous light and darkness, have elongated hypocotyls and flower early. The EC's DNA binding is temperature-dependent, which contributes to thermoresponsive growth.

  • Positive regulation of circadian rhythm IMP (UniProt): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE; better captured as an oscillator component.
  • Regulation of gene expression IMP (RVE8 paper): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (indirect).
