CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor that is essential for the generation of
CC       the circadian clock oscillation. Is necessary for activation of CCA1
CC       and LHY expression. Is coregulated with TOC1 and seems to be repressed
CC       by CCA1 and LHY by direct binding of these proteins to the evening
CC       element in the LUX promoter. Directly regulates the expression of PRR9,
CC       a major component of the morning transcriptional feedback circuit, by
CC       binding specific sites on PRR9 promoter. Binds to its own promoter,
CC       inducing a negative auto-regulatory feedback loop within the core
CC       clock. Binds to ELF3 and associates with ELF4 in a diurnal complex
CC       which is required for the expression of the growth-promoting
CC       transcription factors PIF4 and PIF5 and subsequent hypocotyl growth in
CC       the early evening. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16006522,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16164597, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21236673,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21753751, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22311777}.
