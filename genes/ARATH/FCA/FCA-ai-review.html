Gene Description

FCA is a plant-specific nuclear RNA-binding protein with two RNA recognition motifs (RRMs) and a C-terminal WW protein-interaction domain that promotes the floral transition in Arabidopsis as a founding member of the autonomous flowering pathway. FCA binds nascent RNA co-transcriptionally and, through its WW domain, transiently engages the conserved cleavage and polyadenylation specificity factor (CPSF) subunit FY/WDR33, thereby directing 3'-end processing to promoter-proximal poly(A) sites. This activity negatively autoregulates FCA itself (promoting cleavage and polyadenylation within FCA intron 3 to give the truncated FCA-beta transcript) and promotes proximal polyadenylation of the FLOWERING LOCUS C (FLC) antisense transcripts (COOLAIR), which in turn triggers FLD-dependent H3K4 demethylation, PRC2/H3K27me3 deposition and transcriptional repression of the floral repressor FLC. FCA localizes to liquid-like nuclear bodies whose formation is promoted by the coiled-coil protein FLL2, and acts redundantly with the spen-family RNA-binding protein FPA. FCA also promotes proximal polyadenylation and silencing at other loci genome-wide and, together with FPA, is required during female gametophyte and early embryo development. FCA does not bind abscisic acid, contrary to an earlier (retracted) proposal.

Core Functions

RRM-containing RNA-binding protein that binds nascent transcripts (its own pre-mRNA and the FLC antisense COOLAIR lncRNA) and, via its WW domain interaction with the CPSF subunit FY, directs cleavage and polyadenylation to promoter-proximal poly(A) sites in nuclear bodies.
