Gene Description

ERECTA (ER) is a plasma-membrane leucine-rich-repeat receptor-like serine/threonine kinase (LRR-RLK) with an extracellular domain of 20 LRRs, a single transmembrane segment and a cytoplasmic kinase domain. With its paralogs ERL1 and ERL2 it forms the ERECTA family (ERf), which perceives secreted cysteine-rich EPF/EPFL peptides. In the leaf epidermis ERECTA is the principal receptor for EPF2: MEPF2 binds the ERECTA ectodomain within a constitutive complex with the receptor-like co-receptor TOO MANY MOUTHS (TMM), ligand binding recruits SERK co-receptor kinases (BAK1/SERK3 and others) with which ERECTA transphosphorylates, and the signal is relayed through the MAPKKK YODA and MKK4/MKK5 to MPK3/MPK6, which phosphorylate and destabilize the stomatal-entry bHLH SPEECHLESS. ERECTA signalling thereby limits stomatal lineage entry, while the antagonistic peptide stomagen/EPFL9 competes for the same receptor. In shoot organs, ERECTA perceives EPFL4/EPFL6 from the endodermis/phloem and, through the same YODA-MKK4/5-MPK3/6 module, promotes localized cell proliferation that determines inflorescence architecture, pedicel and internode elongation and organ shape. ERECTA additionally contributes to transpiration efficiency, thermotolerance, vascular and ovule development, and quantitative resistance to some bacterial and fungal pathogens.

Core Functions

Cell-surface LRR receptor kinase that binds the EPF2 peptide (with co-receptor TMM), recruits SERK co-receptor kinases and activates the YODA-MKK4/5-MPK3/6 cascade to inhibit stomatal lineage entry (SPEECHLESS destabilization), with its paralogs ERL1/ERL2 enforcing stomatal spacing.

description: 'ERECTA (ER) is a plasma-membrane leucine-rich-repeat receptor-like serine/threonine kinase
  (LRR-RLK) with an extracellular domain of 20 LRRs, a single transmembrane segment and a cytoplasmic
  kinase domain. With its paralogs ERL1 and ERL2 it forms the ERECTA family (ERf), which perceives secreted
  cysteine-rich EPF/EPFL peptides. In the leaf epidermis ERECTA is the principal receptor for EPF2: MEPF2
  binds the ERECTA ectodomain within a constitutive complex with the receptor-like co-receptor TOO MANY
  MOUTHS (TMM), ligand binding recruits SERK co-receptor kinases (BAK1/SERK3 and others) with which ERECTA
  transphosphorylates, and the signal is relayed through the MAPKKK YODA and MKK4/MKK5 to MPK3/MPK6, which
  phosphorylate and destabilize the stomatal-entry bHLH SPEECHLESS. ERECTA signalling thereby limits stomatal
  lineage entry, while the antagonistic peptide stomagen/EPFL9 competes for the same receptor. In shoot
  organs, ERECTA perceives EPFL4/EPFL6 from the endodermis/phloem and, through the same YODA-MKK4/5-MPK3/6
  module, promotes localized cell proliferation that determines inflorescence architecture, pedicel and
  internode elongation and organ shape. ERECTA additionally contributes to transpiration efficiency, thermotolerance,
  vascular and ovule development, and quantitative resistance to some bacterial and fungal pathogens.'
