## Identity

- Swiss-Prot Q8VZA5, "Probable 3-deoxy-D-manno-octulosonic acid transferase,
  mitochondrial" (AtKdtA), 447 aa, single-copy nuclear gene. Belongs to the
  glycosyltransferase group 1 family, GT30 subfamily (UniProt SIMILARITY, ECO:0000305);
  Pfam PF04413 Glycos_transf_N; InterPro IPR039901 Kdotransferase; PANTHER PTHR42755:SF1.
- Ortholog of E. coli WaaA/KdtA (P0AC75), the bifunctional enzyme that transfers two
  Kdo residues from CMP-Kdo onto lipid IVA (EC 2.4.99.12 and 2.4.99.13).
- Li et al. 2011 report 34% identity to E. coli KdtA
  [PMID:21709257 "Single copy genes encoding AtLpxB (AT2G04560), AtLpxK (AT3G20480) and AtKdtA (AT5G03770) ( Table 1 ) were also found, with 31, 26, and 34% sequence identity to the corresponding E. coli proteins, respectively"].

## Function: lipid A precursor pathway (not RG-II)

- Séveno et al. 2010 (abstract only cached) tested the long-standing hypothesis that
  AtKDTA adds Kdo to the pectin rhamnogalacturonan II (RG-II). Null mutants had no
  developmental phenotype and unaltered RG-II
  [PMID:20124190 "the structure of RG-II from the null mutants was not altered and contained wild-type amount of Rha-alpha(1-5)Kdo side chain"].
  GFP fusion targeted to mitochondria
  [PMID:20124190 "the fusion protein was demonstrated to be targeted to the mitochondria"].
  Conclusion: [PMID:20124190 "AtKDTA encodes a putative KDTA involved in the synthesis of a mitochondrial not yet identified lipid A-like molecule rather than in the synthesis of the cell wall RG-II"].
- Li et al. 2011 (full text cached) identified Arabidopsis orthologs of LpxA, LpxC,
  LpxD, LpxB, LpxK and KdtA and characterised insertion mutants by LC-ESI/MS.
  The atkdta-1 (SALK_035981) null accumulates the LpxK substrate and is the only line
  in which lipid IVA (the KdtA acceptor) was detected:
  [PMID:21709257 "The disaccharide-1-phosphate intermediate generated by LpxB ( Fig. 1 ) accumulated in the atlpxk-1 and atkdta-1 mutants"];
  [PMID:21709257 "Lipid IV A ( Fig. 1 ) was detected only in the atkdta-1 mutant"].
  Lipid X was also 2.2-fold elevated in atkdta-1
  [PMID:21709257 "In the atlpxk-1 and atkdta-1 mutants, lipid X levels were 2.6- and 2.2-fold higher than in the Col-0 wild type, respectively"].
- Caveat: Kdo-containing products were not detected, and the plant end product is unknown
  [PMID:21709257 "We were unable to detect Kdo-lipid IV A , Kdo 2 -lipid IV A , and putative penta- or hexa-acylated derivatives"];
  [PMID:21709257 "The end products of the plant lipid A pathway remain unknown."].
  No in vitro enzyme assay of AtKdtA has been reported in the cached literature, nor
  complementation of an E. coli kdtA mutant (contrast AtLpxA, which complements
  [PMID:22545860 "lpxA complements an E. coli mutant lacking the chromosomal lpxA and promotes the
synthesis of lipid A in vivo"]). Catalytic activity is therefore inferred from
  orthology plus the substrate-accumulation phenotype.
- Mutants are viable without visible phenotype
  [PMID:21709257 "Null mutants of AtLpxA , AtLpxD2 , AtLpxB , AtLpxK , and AtKdtA , as well as AtLpxC-RNAi knock-down plants, show no obvious phenotypic differences compared to wild type under laboratory conditions"].
- Localisation: Li et al. did not themselves image KdtA; they cite Séveno
  [PMID:21709257 "The AtKdtA protein was recently localized to mitochondria of Arabidopsis ( 22 ), although no characterization of its activity was reported."].
  Lipid X in atlpxb-1 is enriched in mitochondria
  [PMID:21709257 "Lipid X levels in mitochondria were 3- and 48-fold higher than in chloroplasts or whole cell homogenates, respectively."].
  Sub-mitochondrial location (outer vs inner membrane, matrix) for KdtA is not established.

## Kdo in RG-II vs lipid A (disambiguation)

- Kdo is also a constituent of pectic RG-II; CMP-Kdo synthetase (CKS, At1g53000) is
  essential for pollen tube growth and is also mitochondrial
  [PMID:21893514 "Our analyses localized the Arabidopsis CKS protein to mitochondria."];
  [PMID:21893514 "These results suggest that KDO is an indispensable component of RG-II"].
  The RG-II Kdo transferase is a different, Golgi enzyme (still unidentified as far as
  the cached literature shows); AtKDTA is not it (Séveno 2010 above). Annotations of
  KDTA to RG-II/cell-wall processes would be wrong.
- Duncan et al. 2011 (mitochondrial outer membrane proteomics) concerns KDSB/CKS
  (At1g53000), not KdtA [PMID:21896887 "At1g53000.1 encodes 3-deoxy- d -manno-2-octulosonic acid (Kdo) transferase, which catalyzes the transfer of Kdo onto cytidine triphosphate to form CMP-Kdo"]
  (note this paper mislabels the cytidylyltransferase as a "Kdo transferase"). Not used
  as evidence for KdtA.
- Guan et al. 2017: mitochondrial FAS (mtKAS/mtHD) mutants have reduced
  3-hydroxytetradecanoic acid, described as a hallmark of lipid A-like molecules
  [PMID:28202596 "reduced accumulation of 3-hydroxytetradecanoic acid (i.e. a hallmark of lipid A-like molecules)"].
  Abstract only; context for a mitochondrial acyl-chain supply to the pathway.

## Wei et al. 2020 RAR flag (PMID:32470107)

The RAR paper flagged plant GO:0009245 lipid A biosynthetic process annotations as
likely misannotations. For KDTA this flag is refuted: the IBA (PTN000602593, E. coli
WaaA) is supported by gene-specific mutant chemistry (lipid IVA accumulates only in
atkdta-1) and by Séveno 2010 ruling out the alternative RG-II role. Low RAR reflects
sparse plant curation of a minor, non-essential pathway, not absence.
