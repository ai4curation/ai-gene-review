Gene Description

GID1C (GA INSENSITIVE DWARF1C; locus At5g27320) is one of three soluble gibberellin (GA) receptors in Arabidopsis thaliana, together with GID1A and GID1B. It has the alpha/beta-hydrolase fold of the hormone-sensitive lipase (GDXG) family but no hydrolase activity; the fold forms a pocket that binds bioactive GAs (highest affinity for GA4), after which an N-terminal lid closes over the hormone and creates a surface that binds the N-terminal DELLA domain of the DELLA growth repressors (GAI, RGA, RGL1, RGL2, RGL3). The GA-GID1-DELLA complex is recognized by the SCF(SLY1) E3 ubiquitin ligase, leading to DELLA polyubiquitination and proteasomal degradation; GA-GID1 binding can also inhibit DELLA activity without proteolysis. GID1C groups with GID1A in the eudicot GID1ac clade and acts mainly with GID1A: the gid1a gid1c double mutant is dwarfed, germinates poorly and has reduced pod elongation, and GID1C is expressed in stems, fruit valves and style. GID1C binds the floral DELLA RGL2 with much lower affinity than GID1A or GID1B, which helps explain why it cannot replace them in stamen development. Loss of all three GID1 receptors abolishes GA responses.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

gibberellin receptor activity

Definition: Binding to a gibberellin and, as a result, undergoing a conformational change that promotes gibberellin-dependent binding of DELLA repressor proteins, thereby transducing the gibberellin signal.

Justification: GID1 proteins, including GID1C, are bona fide soluble GA receptors but GO has no gibberellin receptor activity term (checked in GO via OAK/OLS); gibberellin binding (GO:0010331) captures ligand binding but not signal transduction. Same proposal as in the GID1A review.

Core Functions

Soluble gibberellin receptor: binds bioactive GAs (preferentially GA4) in its hydrolase-fold pocket; the GA-bound receptor then engages the DELLA domain of DELLA repressors, initiating GA signaling. Positive regulator of GA signaling: by forming the GA-GID1-DELLA complex it promotes SCF(SLY1)-mediated DELLA ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation and also inhibits DELLA repression without proteolysis, partially redundantly with the other GID1 receptors.

description: 'GID1C (GA INSENSITIVE DWARF1C; locus At5g27320) is one of three soluble gibberellin
  (GA) receptors in Arabidopsis thaliana, together with GID1A and GID1B. It has the alpha/beta-hydrolase
  fold of the hormone-sensitive lipase (GDXG) family but no hydrolase activity; the fold forms
  a pocket that binds bioactive GAs (highest affinity for GA4), after which an N-terminal
  lid closes over the hormone and creates a surface that binds the N-terminal DELLA domain
  of the DELLA growth repressors (GAI, RGA, RGL1, RGL2, RGL3). The GA-GID1-DELLA complex is
  recognized by the SCF(SLY1) E3 ubiquitin ligase, leading to DELLA polyubiquitination and
  proteasomal degradation; GA-GID1 binding can also inhibit DELLA activity without proteolysis.
  GID1C groups with GID1A in the eudicot GID1ac clade and acts mainly with GID1A: the gid1a
  gid1c double mutant is dwarfed, germinates poorly and has reduced pod elongation, and GID1C
  is expressed in stems, fruit valves and style. GID1C binds the floral DELLA RGL2 with much
  lower affinity than GID1A or GID1B, which helps explain why it cannot replace them in stamen
  development. Loss of all three GID1 receptors abolishes GA responses.'
