Gene Description

SMAX1-LIKE8 (SMXL8; D53-like 3) is a nuclear Clp-ATPase-related (double Clp-N / P-loop NTPase domain) protein that acts redundantly with SMXL6 and SMXL7 as a strigolactone (SL)-regulated transcriptional co-repressor. Via its EAR motif it recruits TOPLESS-related co-repressors (TPR2) and represses target genes such as BRANCHED1 (BRC1), allowing axillary bud outgrowth. SL perception by D14 triggers SCF(MAX2)-dependent polyubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of SMXL8. The SMXL6/7/8 group also regulates leaf shape, lateral root density and drought tolerance, and SMXL8 alone has little phenotypic effect.

Core Functions

Strigolactone-regulated transcriptional co-repressor: SMXL8 recruits TOPLESS-related co-repressors through its EAR motif to repress SL-responsive genes such as BRC1, and is removed by D14/SCF(MAX2)-dependent ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation when SL is perceived, de-repressing bud-outgrowth inhibitors.
