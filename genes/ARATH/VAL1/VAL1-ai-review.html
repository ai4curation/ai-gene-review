Gene Description

VAL1 (VIVIPAROUS1/ABI3-LIKE 1; also HSI2) is a nuclear B3-domain transcriptional repressor. It combines a B3 DNA-binding domain that recognizes the Sph/RY element (CATGCA), a PHD-like domain implicated in reading H3K27 methylation, a CW-type zinc finger and a C-terminal EAR repression motif. VAL1 binds RY elements in target loci and recruits repressive chromatin machinery, including the Polycomb proteins LHP1/PRC1 and the PRC2 methyltransferases CLF and SWN, as well as the ASAP complex and histone deacetylase activity. Redundantly with VAL2, it represses the seed-maturation program (LEC1, ABI3, FUS3, DOG1) after germination, and during vernalization it binds two RY elements in the FLC intragenic nucleation region to trigger Polycomb (PHD-PRC2) nucleation and epigenetic silencing of FLC.
