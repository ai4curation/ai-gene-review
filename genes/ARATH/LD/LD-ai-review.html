Gene Description

LUMINIDEPENDENS (LD) is a large, largely disordered nuclear protein of the Arabidopsis autonomous flowering pathway, with an N-terminal homeodomain-like/TFIIS-type four-helix-bundle region, a glutamine-rich region and a prion-like domain. LD promotes flowering by repressing the floral repressor FLOWERING LOCUS C (FLC). LD associates tightly in vivo with the LSD1-type histone H3K4 demethylase FLD and the SET-domain protein SDG26 (an autonomous pathway complex that also contains APRF1 and EFL proteins); this complex removes H3K4me1 over the FLC gene body and at genes with convergent antisense transcription, antagonizing active transcription marks and favouring Polycomb (H3K27me3) silencing. LD is structurally related to yeast Ref2 and mammalian PNUTS, regulatory subunits of the CPF phosphatase module, and interacts with the WDR82/Swd2 homolog APRF1 and with PP1-type phosphatase TOPP4, linking RNA 3'-processing/termination at FLC to FLD-dependent chromatin changes. LD also binds the zinc-finger protein SUF4, suppressing its FRIGIDA-independent activation of FLC, and is expressed mainly in proliferating regions such as shoot and root apices. No sequence-specific DNA-binding activity has been demonstrated for LD.

Core Functions

Scaffold subunit of the FLD/LD/SDG26 autonomous pathway chromatin complex and of an APRF1/TOPP4 CPF-like phosphatase module (Ref2/PNUTS-like), coupling RNA 3'-processing and transcription termination at FLC to FLD-mediated H3K4me1 removal and Polycomb silencing, thereby promoting flowering.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does LD act as a bona fide PP1 regulatory subunit (PNUTS-like) for TOPP4 at FLC, and is phosphatase activity on RNA Pol II CTD or termination factors required for LD function?

Q: What is the in vivo relevance of the LD prion-like domain for flowering memory?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Mutate a putative PP1-docking (RVxF-type) motif in LD, test TOPP4 binding and complementation of ld, and measure FLC proximal termination, H3K4me1 and flowering time.

Hypothesis: LD recruits PP1/TOPP4 via a PNUTS-like docking motif, and this is required for FLC repression.

Type: Mutagenesis + co-IP + genetic complementation
