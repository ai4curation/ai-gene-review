| Attribute | PAS2 annotation and evidence |
|---|---|
| Gene identity | **PAS2 / PASTICCINO2**, Arabidopsis locus **At5g10480**; synonyms **PEP / PEPINO**; UniProt **Q8VZB2**. The literature consistently identifies this protein as the Arabidopsis very-long-chain fatty-acid elongation dehydratase, confirming the requested identity rather than an unrelated PAS2 gene (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000019). |
| Protein family and domains | Member of the eukaryotic **3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydratase (HACD/HCD)** family; contains a protein-tyrosine-phosphatase-like **PTPLA/Tyr_Pase-like** domain. Its experimentally supported primary activity is dehydration, not protein-tyrosine dephosphorylation; the dehydratase-associated residues include **Tyr156 and Glu163** (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000007). |
| Primary protein function | Catalytic HCD component of the membrane-bound fatty-acid elongase complex, performing the **third of four reactions** in each very-long-chain fatty-acid (VLCFA) elongation cycle. PAS2 is essential and can be limiting for VLCFA production (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000017). |
| EC number | **EC 4.2.1.134**, very-long-chain (3R)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydratase (functional assignment supported by biochemical genetics and complementation) (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000005). |
| Catalyzed reaction | **(3R)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA → trans-2-enoyl-CoA + H₂O.** The trans-2-enoyl-CoA is subsequently reduced by CER10/ECR to generate an acyl-CoA elongated by two carbons relative to the substrate entering that cycle (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000020). |
| Substrate class and specificity | Acts on **3-hydroxyacyl-CoA elongation intermediates**, not free fatty acids. Accumulation of saturated **3-hydroxy-C18:0-, C20:0-, and C22:0-CoA** species in pas2 mutants provides direct pathway evidence. Available studies support broad long-/very-long-chain intermediate use but do **not** establish a complete kinetic chain-length or unsaturation profile; chain-length selectivity of an elongase complex is primarily imposed by its KCS condensing enzyme (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000002, pqac-00000022). |
| Biochemical context | ER fatty-acid elongation extends C16/C18-derived acyl-CoAs by repeated two-carbon cycles comprising KCS condensation, KCR reduction, PAS2 dehydration, and CER10/ECR reduction. PAS2-dependent VLC acyl-CoAs supply sphingolipids, phospholipids, cuticular waxes, suberin-associated lipids, and seed triacylglycerols (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000049, pqac-00000053). |
| Core subcellular localization | Principally associated with the **endoplasmic-reticulum membrane**, the established location of microsomal VLCFA elongation (pqac-00000009, pqac-00000013, pqac-00000014). |
| Additional reported localization | A functional native-promoter GFP-PAS2 fusion was also detected at the **Golgi**, with more than 90% signal overlap with tested Golgi markers in dark-grown hypocotyl cells and colocalization with Golgi cellulose-synthase complexes. Because other fusion designs produced ER-only localization, Golgi residence should be regarded as supported but construct- and context-sensitive, pending confirmation for native untagged PAS2 (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012). |
| Experimentally reported interactors | **CER10/ECR**, the next enzyme in the elongation cycle, interacts with PAS2 in ER-localized BiFC assays. PAS2 also associates with **PAS1**, **KCR**, and multiple KCS condensing enzymes; reported KCS partners include **KCS1, KCS5, KCS6, KCS8, KCS9, KCS10, and KCS18**. These associations support participation in multiple elongase assemblies rather than action as a soluble enzyme (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000040, pqac-00000041, pqac-00000042). |
| Tissue and developmental expression | PAS2 is developmentally regulated and detected in seedlings, roots, leaves, stems, and flowers. Strong epidermal/L1 expression is supported by an **L1-box** in its promoter and regulation by epidermal factors ATML1/PDF2; root analyses additionally reported PAS2 enrichment mainly in the **endodermis**, indicating organ- and cell-context specificity rather than universal epidermal restriction (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000045, pqac-00000047). |
| Key functional validation | pas2 mutants accumulate the predicted hydroxyacyl-CoA substrates and lose VLCFAs from several lipid pools; the yeast ortholog **PHS1** complements mutant biochemical and developmental defects. Strong loss of function is embryo-lethal, whereas partial loss produces severe developmental abnormalities, demonstrating that the annotated enzymatic function is biologically essential (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000027, pqac-00000028). |
| Key references | **Bach et al., 2008, PNAS**—primary biochemical identification of PAS2 as the essential, limiting VLCFA dehydratase (pqac-00000000); **Roudier et al., 2010, The Plant Cell**—VLCFA elongase organization and developmental patterning (pqac-00000020); **Bach et al., 2011, Journal of Cell Science**—PAS2-dependent VLCFAs in cytokinesis and cell-plate maturation (pqac-00000065); **Morineau et al., 2016, PLOS ONE**—dual dehydratase complexes and KCS interactions (pqac-00000041); **Zhu et al., 2020, Plants**—ER/Golgi localization and cellulose-synthase dynamics (pqac-00000011); **Batsale et al., 2021, Cells**—authoritative synthesis of plant VLCFA pathways and products (pqac-00000016). |
