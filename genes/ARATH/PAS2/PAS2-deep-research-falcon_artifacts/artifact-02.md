| Embryogenesis and viability | Strong loss-of-function alleles cause embryonic arrest and lethality; partial-loss alleles survive with severe developmental abnormalities. | PAS2 is an essential, limiting component of very-long-chain fatty-acid (VLCFA) synthesis; severe loss prevents production of VLCFA-containing lipids required during embryogenesis. | Bach et al., 2008 (pqac-00000015); Roudier et al., 2010 (pqac-00000028) |
| Seedling and organ morphology | Dwarf seedlings; short, thick hypocotyls; fused or misshapen leaves; postgenital organ fusion; abnormal differentiation. | Reduced VLCFAs impair cuticle formation, membrane-lipid composition, cell differentiation, and coordinated tissue patterning. | Bach et al., 2008 (pqac-00000024, pqac-00000027); Roudier et al., 2010 (pqac-00000026) |
| Root growth | Five-day-old **pas2-1** roots reach about half the wild-type length; root division and elongation zones are shortened. | Defective membrane dynamics, cytokinesis, cellulose synthesis, and cell elongation collectively restrict root growth. | Bach et al., 2011 (pqac-00000023); Zhu et al., 2020 (pqac-00000025) |
| Cell proliferation and meristem activity | Ectopic or excessive cell division; increased mitotic-marker expression; enlarged shoot apical meristem; enhanced callus-like growth and cytokinin sensitivity. | PAS2-dependent VLCFAs normally help coordinate cell-cycle progression and restrain inappropriate proliferation; the precise signaling mechanism remains incompletely defined. | Roudier et al., 2010 (pqac-00000026); Bach et al., 2008 (pqac-00000024) |

| Endomembrane trafficking | Delayed endocytosis—approximately 20 min in **pas2-1** versus 5 min in wild type—and altered brefeldin-A responses; abnormal membrane and endosomal aggregates. | Altered VLCFA-dependent membrane composition disrupts selected endosomal and recycling processes rather than causing a complete block of secretion. | Bach et al., 2011 (pqac-00000060, pqac-00000065, pqac-00000071) |
| Cell elongation and cellulose synthesis | Extremely short etiolated hypocotyls and shorter light-grown roots; crystalline cellulose reduced by approximately 22% in both examined systems. | PAS2 deficiency alters VLCFA metabolism and cellulose-synthase-complex behavior, compromising cell-wall deposition and elongation. | Zhu et al., 2020 (pqac-00000025, pqac-00000029) |

| Cuticle and epicuticular wax | Reduced stem and leaf wax loads; fewer wax crystals with abnormal, predominantly globular morphology; organ fusion consistent with defective surface barriers. | PAS2-dependent VLC acyl-CoAs supply fatty acids and derivatives used to form cuticular waxes. | Bach et al., 2008 (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000051) |
| VLCFA metabolism | Global VLCFA depletion and accumulation of the blocked 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA intermediates, including C18:0, C20:0, and C22:0 species. | Loss of PAS2 blocks the third elongation-cycle reaction: dehydration of 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA to trans-2-enoyl-CoA. | Bach et al., 2008 (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000021, pqac-00000022) |
| Sphingolipid homeostasis | VLCFAs are almost absent from mutant glucosylceramides; GIPC VLCFAs decline more modestly; 16:0 ceramide, hydroxyceramide, and free long-chain bases accumulate. | Inadequate VLCFA elongation shifts ceramide acyl-chain composition and selectively disrupts complex sphingolipid pools. | Bach et al., 2008 (pqac-00000061, pqac-00000062) |
