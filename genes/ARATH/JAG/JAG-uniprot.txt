CC   -!- FUNCTION: Controls the morphogenesis of lateral organs. Functions in
CC       lateral organ shape and is sufficient to induce proliferation and
CC       growth of lateral organ tissue. Is necessary and sufficient for bract
CC       formation, but its expression is excluded from the cryptic bract, which
CC       could be a cause of bractless flowers in Arabidopsis. Participates with
CC       FIL and YAB3 in regulating valve margin development. Functions with JGL
CC       to define stamen and carpel shape. Functions with AS1 and AS2 in the
CC       sepal and petal primordia to repress boundary-specifying genes for
CC       normal development of the organs. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14973281,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14973282, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16192305,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16554365, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18156293}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with GATA18/HAN. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26390296}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14973281,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14973282}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the emerging leaf, sepal, petal,
CC       stamen and carpel primordia (PubMed:26390296). Not expressed in the
CC       apical shoot meristem (SAM). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14973281,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14973282, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26390296}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Stimulated by GATA18/HAN. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:26390296}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Lateral organs with serrated margins. Irregular
CC       shape of floral organs. The double mutant han-2 jag-3 exhibits reduced
CC       petal numbers, more serrated sepals and narrower petals
CC       (PubMed:26390296). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14973281,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14973282, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26390296}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Plants overexpressing JAG form bract-like organs on the
CC       inflorescence.
