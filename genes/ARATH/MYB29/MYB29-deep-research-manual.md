# MYB29 (At5g07690; Q9FLR1) manual literature research

No deep-research API was available, so this summary was compiled by hand from cached
publications in `publications/` and PubMed searches ("MYB29 Arabidopsis glucosinolate").

## Identity and domain structure

- R2R3-MYB transcription factor, subgroup 12 (with MYB28/HAG1, MYB76/HAG2, MYB34/ATR1,
  MYB51/HIG1, MYB122). Synonyms HAG3, PMG2; locus At5g07690. 336 aa, two HTH Myb-type
  repeats (residues 9-65 and 66-116; Pfam PF00249 x2) [file:ARATH/MYB29/MYB29-uniprot.txt].
- Predicted nuclear; subgroup 12 MYBs carry an SV40-type NLS
  [PMID:23580754 "These six MYB transcriptional factors, MYB28, MYB29, MYB76, MYB34, MYB51, and MYB122, might function in the nucleus due to an amino acid sequence acting as an SV40-type nuclear localization signal motif"].

## Regulation of aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis (core)

- Found by co-expression with aliphatic glucosinolate genes; MYB29 is MeJA-inducible and
  has an accessory role in jasmonate-induced aliphatic GSL synthesis
  [PMID:17420480 "In contrast, PMG2/Myb29 plays an accessory role in MeJA-mediated induction of aliphatic GSL biosynthesis."].
  A knockdown line alone showed little effect
  [PMID:17420480 "However, there was no apparent change in the expression of GSL biosynthetic genes or GSL content in myb29 gene-knockdown plants"].
- Positive regulator; transactivates promoters and represses indolic regulators
  [PMID:18042203 "Both HAG2/MYB76 and HAG3/MYB29 were shown to be positive regulators of aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis."].
- myb29 null reduces short-chain aliphatic GSLs about 50%; myb28 myb29 has none
  [PMID:18446225 "We now show that MYB28 and MYB29 are partially redundant, but in the absence of both, the synthesis of all aliphatic glucosinolates is blocked."].
- Hierarchy MYB28 > MYB29 > MYB76
  [PMID:20348214 "Based on the chemotypes of the single knockouts, MYB28 was regarded as the major important player in planta, MYB29 as a lieutenant, and MYB76 bearing an accessory role"];
  MYB29 alone activates promoters in cultured cells
  [PMID:20348214 "However, previous transactivation assays showed that MYB29 alone activates transcription of the promoters of aliphatic biosynthetic genes in cultured Arabidopsis cells"].
- Native-promoter complementation of myb28 myb29
  [PMID:23792303 "Transgenic MYB29 expression activated the same biosynthetic pathway, but induction of the genes involved in side chain elongation was weaker than that by MYB28, resulting in a weaker recovery of AGSLs."].
- Interacts with MYC2/3/4
  [PMID:23943862 "yeast two-hybrid and pull-down experiments indicated that MYC2/MYC3/MYC4 interact directly with GS-related MYBs"].
- Y1H binding to aliphatic GSL pathway promoters (e.g. AOP2) [PMID:25352272].

## Defence consequences (indirect)

- Insects: [PMID:18446225 "Larval weight gain was 2.6 fold higher on the double myb28myb29 mutant completely lacking aliphatic glucosinolates"].
- Bacteria/ISR: [PMID:23073694 "showed that camalexin and glucosinolates are indeed required for the induction of Pst resistance by Pf"].
- Fungi: [PMID:21418358 "Mutant plant lines deficient in camalexin, indole, or aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis were hypersusceptible to S. sclerotiorum"].

## Mitochondrial retrograde signalling (RAO7)

- [PMID:28167700 "MYB DOMAIN PROTEIN29 (MYB29) was identified as a negative regulator (regulator of alternative oxidase1a 7 [rao7] mutant) in a genetic screen"];
  regulates ERF and WRKY genes; myb29 is more sensitive to combined moderate light and drought
  stress. Abstract only (PMC full text restricted).

## Expression

- Induced by drought, repressed by aphid feeding [PMID:23144921]; wound- and JA-inducible,
  down under sulfur deficiency [PMID:17420480; PMID:23792303 "the expression levels of MYB29 and MYB76 were positively correlated with sulfur concentration"].
