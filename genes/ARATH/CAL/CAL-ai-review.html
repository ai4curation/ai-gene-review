Gene Description

CAULIFLOWER (CAL; AGL10) is a MIKC-type MADS-domain transcription factor of the SQUAMOSA/AP1 clade and the closest Arabidopsis paralog of APETALA1. It is expressed in young flower primordia, where it acts partially redundantly with AP1 (and, more weakly, with FRUITFULL) to assign floral meristem identity to the lateral meristems produced by the inflorescence meristem. cal single mutants have essentially wild-type flowers, but ap1 cal double mutants convert every flower into a reiterating inflorescence-like meristem (the "cauliflower" curd), and ap1 cal ful triple mutants fail to make flowers at all. CAL promotes floral fate in part by up-regulating LEAFY and repressing TERMINAL FLOWER1 in floral primordia, and it forms heterodimers with other MADS proteins such as SOC1. The cauliflower phenotype of Brassica oleracea var. botrytis is associated with a non-functional CAL ortholog.

Core Functions

Sequence-specific MADS-domain transcription factor that, partially redundantly with AP1 (and FUL), promotes floral meristem identity in lateral primordia of the inflorescence by up-regulating LEAFY and restricting TERMINAL FLOWER1 expression, so that floral meristems become determinate flowers rather than reiterating shoots.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does CAL bind the same CArG-box targets as AP1 genome-wide (e.g., at LFY, TFL1, SOC1, AGL24, SVP), or does its weaker single-mutant phenotype reflect lower expression rather than distinct target specificity?

Q: Is a GO process term for floral meristem identity specification (distinct from floral meristem determinacy) warranted for the AP1/CAL/FUL clade?

Suggested Experiments
