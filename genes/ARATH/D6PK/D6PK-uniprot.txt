CC   -!- FUNCTION: Protein kinase that regulates the auxin transport activity of
CC       PIN auxin efflux facilitators by direct phosphorylation. D6PK-mediated
CC       PIN phosphorylation promotes auxin transport in the hypocotyl and this
CC       is a prerequisite for PHOT1-dependent hypocotyl bending. Phosphorylates
CC       PIN1, PIN2, PIN3, PIN4 and PIN7 in vitro and PIN1 in vivo.
CC       Phosphorylates PIN proteins (e.g. PIN1 and PIN2), especially when NPY
CC       proteins (e.g. NPY1/MAB4 and NPY5/MEL1) are recruited at the plasma
CC       membrane; this enhances the polarized localizations (apical or basal)
CC       of PINs in the cell by limiting their lateral diffusion-based escape
CC       (PubMed:33705718). Acts as a lipid domain-dependent mediator of root
CC       epidermal planar polarity via the binding to phosphatidylinositol
CC       phosphates (e.g. PtdIns(3)P, PtdIns(4)P, PtdIns(5)P, PtdIns(3,5)P(2),
CC       PtdIns(4,5)P(2), PtdIns(3,4,5)P(3) and phosphatidic acid)
CC       (PubMed:27251533). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19168677,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23709629, ECO:0000269|PubMed:27251533,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33705718}.

CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts (via C-terminal PIF domain) with PDPK1
CC       (PubMed:14749726). Component of a complex made of PINs (e.g. PIN1 and
CC       PIN2), MAB4/MELs (e.g. NPY1/MAB4 and NPY5/MEL1) and AGC kinases (e.g.
CC       D6PK and PID) at the plasma membrane (PubMed:33705718). Binds directly
CC       to NPY5/MEL1 (PubMed:33705718). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14749726,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33705718}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19168677,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27251533, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33705718}; Peripheral
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19168677}. Note=Colocalizes with
CC       PIN1 to the basal (lower) membrane of root stele, cortex, epidermis and
CC       lateral root cap cells (PubMed:19168677). Undergoes a
CC       phosphatidylinositol-4,5-bisphosphate- and sterol-dependent basal-to-
CC       planar polarity switching into the polar, lipid-enriched domain of root
CC       hair-forming cells just before hair formation; this process is mediated

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27251533}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: The activation loop within the kinase domain is the target of

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: No visible phenotype under normal growth
CC       conditions, but the quadruple d6pk, d6pkl1, d6pkl2 and d6pkl3 mutants
CC       are deficient in lateral root formation and mildly agravitropic, have
CC       fused or single cotyledons and narrow and twisted leaves, form few
CC       axillary shoots, exhibits basal shift in root hairs positioning, are
CC       almost infertile and impaired in phototropic hypocotyl bending when
CC       exposed to lateral white light. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19168677,
