Gene Description

ARF7 (AUXIN RESPONSE FACTOR 7; NPH4/MSG1/TIR5; At5g20730) is a class A (activator) AUXIN RESPONSE FACTOR transcription factor. It has an N-terminal B3 DNA-binding domain that binds TGTCTC-type auxin-response elements (AuxREs) as a dimer, a glutamine-rich middle region that activates transcription, and a C-terminal PB1 domain through which it homo-oligomerizes and heterodimerizes with Aux/IAA repressors (e.g. IAA14/SLR, IAA18/CRANE, IAA19/MSG2, IAA17). At low auxin, PB1-bound Aux/IAAs recruit TOPLESS co-repressors and keep ARF7 targets silent; auxin-triggered SCF(TIR1/AFB)-mediated Aux/IAA degradation releases ARF7 to activate early auxin-responsive genes. ARF7 acts largely redundantly with its closest paralog ARF19; together they directly activate LBD16/LBD29 (and LBD18) to initiate lateral roots, and they are required for hypocotyl and root tropic growth, leaf cell expansion, auxin-induced callus formation (recruiting the chromatin modifiers ATXR2 and JMJ30 to LBD promoters) and ethylene responses in roots. ARF7 activity is further tuned by BIN2 phosphorylation and by partitioning into cytoplasmic PB1-dependent condensates in tissues with low auxin responsiveness.

  ARF7 (AUXIN RESPONSE FACTOR 7; NPH4/MSG1/TIR5; At5g20730) is a class A (activator) AUXIN
  RESPONSE FACTOR transcription factor. It has an N-terminal B3 DNA-binding domain that binds
  TGTCTC-type auxin-response elements (AuxREs) as a dimer, a glutamine-rich middle region
  that activates transcription, and a C-terminal PB1 domain through which it homo-oligomerizes
  and heterodimerizes with Aux/IAA repressors (e.g. IAA14/SLR, IAA18/CRANE, IAA19/MSG2, IAA17).
  At low auxin, PB1-bound Aux/IAAs recruit TOPLESS co-repressors and keep ARF7 targets silent;
  auxin-triggered SCF(TIR1/AFB)-mediated Aux/IAA degradation releases ARF7 to activate early
  auxin-responsive genes. ARF7 acts largely redundantly with its closest paralog ARF19; together
  they directly activate LBD16/LBD29 (and LBD18) to initiate lateral roots, and they are required
  for hypocotyl and root tropic growth, leaf cell expansion, auxin-induced callus formation
  (recruiting the chromatin modifiers ATXR2 and JMJ30 to LBD promoters) and ethylene responses
  in roots. ARF7 activity is further tuned by BIN2 phosphorylation and by partitioning into
  cytoplasmic PB1-dependent condensates in tissues with low auxin responsiveness.
