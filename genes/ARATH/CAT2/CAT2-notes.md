## Identity

- CATALASE2, At4g35090, 492 aa, monofunctional heme catalase (catalase family; PANTHER
  PTHR11465:SF23; Pfam PF00199 and PF06628). One of three Arabidopsis catalases (CAT1
  At1g20630, CAT2 At4g35090, CAT3 At1g20620).

## Molecular function

- Catalase (EC 1.11.1.6), 2 H2O2 = O2 + 2 H2O; UniProt function statement is by similarity to
  human catalase P04040 [file:ARATH/CAT2/CAT2-uniprot.txt "Catalyzes the degradation of
  hydrogen peroxide (H(2)O(2)) generated by peroxisomal oxidases to water and oxygen, thereby
  protecting cells from the toxic effects of hydrogen peroxide."]. Heme cofactor (by similarity).
- CAT2 provides most leaf catalase activity: [PMID:25700484 "deletion of CAT2 reduces catalase
  activity by 80%"] and [PMID:25700484 "Consistent with published results, deleting CAT2 but not
  CAT3 significantly reduced catalase activity in Arabidopsis"].
- Recombinant CAT2 is active, mainly as a tetramer [PMID:25700484 "the size of a CAT2 tetramer)
  had the highest activity in our assay"].
- Forward genetics: hydroxyurea-resistant EMS mutants are CAT2 alleles with reduced catalase
  activity [PMID:20452979 "identified a point mutation in the CATALASE2 gene (AT4G35090)"],
  [PMID:20452979 "When compared with the wild type, all mutants displayed a significant decrease
  (95% confidence level) in catalase activity"]. HU inhibits catalase [PMID:20452979 "Our
  results show that HU directly inhibits catalase-mediated hydrogen peroxide decomposition."].
- Catalase-deficient (CAT2-deficient) transgenic lines: [PMID:15200641 "Reduced peroxisomal
  catalase activity increased sensitivity toward both ozone and photorespiratory
  H(2)O(2)-induced cell death in transgenic catalase-deficient Arabidopsis thaliana."]
  (abstract only; the abstract does not itself name CAT2).

## Assembly, chaperone and partners

- NO CATALASE ACTIVITY1 (NCA1) is a chaperone required for catalase activity; it binds CAT2 1:1
  in the cytosol and increases its activity [PMID:25700484 "When CAT2 was pulled down by NCA1,
  the catalase activity was 10-fold higher than in CAT2 alone"], [PMID:25700484 "NCA1
  colocalizes and interacts with CAT2 in cytosol"].
- Other reported partners (not adjudicated as specific MFs): NDK1 (yeast two-hybrid,
  PMID:14581623), 14-3-3 omega TAP-MS (PMID:19452453), LSD1, which interacts with all three catalases
  [PMID:23958864 "Yeast two-hybrid and coimmunoprecipitation analyses demonstrated that lsd1 interacted with all three catalases both in vitro and in vivo"].

## Localization

- Peroxisome (matrix): [PMID:25700484 "The GFP signal was predominately observed in the
  peroxisomes in both Col-0 and nca1-3"]; CAT2 detected in leaf peroxisomes (PMID:17951448).
  UniProt: cytosol and peroxisome matrix, both from PMID:25700484.
- A cytosolic pool exists before/while being folded by NCA1 (BiFC in cytosol, PMID:25700484). This
  contradicts the GOA NOT cytosol (RCA, PMID:21166475) row.
- Mitochondrion, plasma membrane, stromule, cytosolic ribosome detections come from bulk
  proteomics and are most likely carry-over of this abundant peroxisomal protein.

## Biological process

- Photorespiration: [PMID:22303256 "H 2 O 2 produced by the glycolate oxidase reaction is
  disproportionated to H 2 O and O 2 by catalase (CAT, EC 1.11.1.6) in order to prevent
  accumulation of reactive oxygen species."] and [PMID:22303256 "only one gene ( Cat 2,
  At4g35090) encodes the major enzyme involved in photorespiration"].
- cat2 knockout is a conditional photorespiratory mutant [PMID:17877712 "Growth of Arabidopsis
  knock-out mutants for CATALASE2 (cat2) in ambient air caused severely decreased rosette
  biomass, intracellular redox perturbation and activation of oxidative signalling pathways.
  These effects were absent when cat2 was grown at high CO(2) levels to inhibit
  photorespiration"]. Day length controls lesion formation in cat2 (photoperiod-dependent cell
  death), which is a conditional phenotype of the mutant rather than a role of CAT2 in
  photoperiodism.
- cat2 is a widely used model of intracellular H2O2 stress (review PMID:20876333, abstract only).
- Second-site gox1 mutation suppresses cat2 [PMID:27225899], placing CAT2 downstream of GOX1 H2O2
  production.
- Expression: light-stimulated and clock regulated (PMID:22303256 citing Zhong et al. 1997);
  cat2 mRNA increased under sulfur depletion (PMID:15032878); CAT2 transcripts up after cold
  and dark treatments (PMID:18230142: "clearly up regulated after all three treatments i.e.
  under the Cold/Light, Cold/Dark and Dark conditions"). These are expression responses, not
  evidence of participation.

## GO term checks (OLS4)

- GO:0004096 catalase activity; GO:0042744 hydrogen peroxide catabolic process; GO:0098869
  cellular oxidant detoxification; GO:0005782 peroxisomal matrix; GO:0009853 photorespiration
  (parent of GO:0009854 oxidative photosynthetic carbon pathway). All current.
- QuickGO (2026-10-20): no catalase of any species carries GO:0009853 or descendants; the only
  experimental photorespiration annotations outside Arabidopsis are rice GLO1. In Arabidopsis,
  photorespiration is carried by GLU1, GLYK, HPR, SHM1, PLGG1, PEX10, PUMP1 and others (IMP/TAS).
  The absence for CAT2 is therefore not a systematic cross-species convention with a body of
  experimental catalase annotations behind it; it is proposed here as NEW with TAS evidence,
  because CAT2 catalyses the disproportionation of the H2O2 produced inside the pathway.
