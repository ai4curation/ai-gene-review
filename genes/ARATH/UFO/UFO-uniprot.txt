CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of SCF(ASK-cullin-F-box) E3 ubiquitin ligase
CC       complexes, which may mediate the ubiquitination and subsequent
CC       proteasomal degradation of target proteins (By similarity). Considered
CC       as a meristem identity factor required for normal growth of the young
CC       floral meristem. Acts together with LEAFY to positively regulate the B
CC       class floral homeotic genes APETALA3 and PISTILLATA. In this way,
CC       operates as a region-specific regulator for petal and stamen
CC       development. Alternatively, may play a role as a negative regulator of
CC       the C class floral homeotic genes. Interacts together with the SKP1-
CC       like protein ASK1 to form a ubiquitin E3 ligase complex and could
CC       indirectly promote the ubiquitination and degradation of specific
CC       proteins controlling the floral primordia development like repressors
CC       of B class floral homeotic genes. {ECO:0000250,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:10607296, ECO:0000269|PubMed:8565821,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9016705}.
CC   -!- PATHWAY: Protein modification; protein ubiquitination.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of a putative SCF (ASK/Cullin/F-box) ubiquitin ligase
CC       complex. Interacts with SKP1A/ASK1, SKP1B/ASK2 and ASK11.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11526079, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12169662,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12826617}.
