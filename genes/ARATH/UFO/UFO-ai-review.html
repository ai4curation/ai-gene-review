Gene Description

UNUSUAL FLORAL ORGANS (UFO; FBX1, At1g30950) is a nuclear F-box protein expressed in shoot apical meristems and in a specific domain of young flowers. Genetically it acts together with the floral meristem identity transcription factor LEAFY: ufo mutants show partial loss of floral meristem identity and determinacy, abnormal whorl patterning, and reduced activation of the B-class organ identity gene APETALA3, whereas ectopic UFO activates AP3 only where LEAFY is present. UFO binds SKP1-like proteins (ASK1, ASK2) through its F-box and assembles an SCF(UFO) ubiquitin ligase that associates with the COP9 signalosome, but its principal mechanism is as a transcriptional cofactor of LEAFY: UFO binds LEAFY and DNA directly, forming a LEAFY-UFO-DNA complex that redirects LEAFY to new cis-elements (for example in the AP3 promoter) and activates them. In this way UFO restricts LEAFY's broad meristem-identity activity to region-specific floral programs.
