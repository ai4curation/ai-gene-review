Gene Description

MYC3 (bHLH005, ATR2) is a nuclear basic helix-loop-helix transcription factor of the subgroup IIIe MYC clade that acts with its paralogs MYC2 and MYC4 as a master activator of jasmonate-responsive gene expression in Arabidopsis. MYC3 binds G-box (CACGTG) and related cis-elements with a specificity nearly identical to MYC2, forms homo- and heterodimers with MYC2 and MYC4, and recruits the Mediator subunit MED25 through its N-terminal JAZ-interaction domain (JID). In the resting state, the Jas motif of JAZ repressors binds the JID, displacing the MYC N-terminal helix and competitively blocking MED25 recruitment; JA-Ile-triggered degradation of JAZ proteins by SCF(COI1) releases MYC3. MYC3 acts additively with MYC2 and MYC4 to control defense against bacteria and insect herbivores, glucosinolate and anthocyanin production, jasmonate-mediated growth responses and stomatal development, and also represses the florigen gene FT in photoperiodic flowering.

Core Functions

Nuclear bHLH DNA-binding transcription factor that binds G-box-type cis-elements and activates jasmonate-responsive genes, acting additively with MYC2 and MYC4. Its activity is repressed by JAZ proteins binding the N-terminal JID and released upon JA-Ile-triggered JAZ degradation.

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Genome-wide binding (ChIP-seq/DAP-seq) of MYC2, MYC3 and MYC4 in matched tissues with and without JA-Ile, combined with single and higher-order mutant transcriptomes.
