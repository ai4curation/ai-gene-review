CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor required for normal cell
CC       differentiation. Positively regulates LATERAL ORGAN BOUNDARIES (LOB)
CC       within the shoot apex, and the class III HD-ZIP genes REV, PHB, and
CC       PHV. Interacts directly with ASYMMETRIC LEAVES 2 (LBD6/AS2) to repress
CC       the knox homeobox genes BP/KNAT1, KNAT2, and KNAT6 and the abaxial
CC       determinants ARF3/ETT, KAN2 and YAB5. May act in parallel with the
CC       RDR6-SGS3-AGO7 pathway, an endogenous RNA silencing pathway, to
CC       regulate the leaf morphogenesis (PubMed:11076771, PubMed:11140682,
CC       PubMed:11882937, PubMed:12750468, PubMed:16006579, PubMed:16699177,
CC       PubMed:17395603, PubMed:17559509, PubMed:23271976). Binds directly to
CC       KNAT1, KNAT2, and KNATM chromatin, regulating leaf development
CC       (PubMed:23271976). LBD6 is required for this binding (PubMed:23271976).
CC       Positive regulator of flowering that binds to the promoter of FT
CC       (PubMed:21950734). Regulates FT expression by forming a functional
CC       complex with CO (PubMed:21950734). Involved in leaf polarity
CC       establishment by functioning cooperatively with NUCL1 to repress
CC       abaxial genes ARF3, ARF4, KAN1, KAN2, YAB1 and YAB5, and the knox
CC       homeobox genes KNAT1, KNAT2, KNAT6, and STM to promote adaxial
CC       development in leaf primordia at shoot apical meristems at high
CC       temperatures (PubMed:27334696). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11076771,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:11140682, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11882937,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12750468, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16006579,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16699177, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17395603,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17559509, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23271976,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27334696}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:12750468, PubMed:23271976). Heterodimer with
CC       LBD6 (PubMed:23271976). Interacts with LBD6/AS2, HIRA, a probable
CC       histone chaperone, and RIK, a predicted RNA binding protein
CC       (PubMed:12874130, PubMed:16243907). Part of the AS1 repressor complex
CC       composed of AS1, LBD6/AS2 and HDA6 (PubMed:23271976). Interacts with CO
CC       (PubMed:21950734). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12750468,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12874130, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16243907,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21950734, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23271976}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23271976}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in roots, stems, leaves, flowers,
CC       siliques and in lateral organ promordia (PubMed:17559509). Found in the
CC       inner domain between the adaxial and abaxial domains of leaves
CC       (PubMed:17559509). Expressed in the phloem tissues of leaves,
CC       cotyledons, hypocotyls, and roots (PubMed:21950734).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17559509, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21950734}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Preferential expression in young and immature
CC       plant tissues. In embryos, expressed from the late globular stage
CC       onwards. After germination, detected in leaf founder cells and on
CC       flowering, in primordia of all floral organs.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Circadian-regulation with an afternoon peak in long days and
CC       with a broad night peak in short days (PubMed:21950734). Expression of
CC       AS1 in stem cells of the shoot apical meristem is prevented by SHOOT
CC       MERISTEMLESS (STM). Expression is activated by GTE6 during leaf
CC       morphogenesis (PubMed:16166385). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16166385,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21950734}.
CC   -!- DOMAIN: A C-terminal domain (252-356) is required for dimerization.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Plants with double mutations in this protein and
CC       in RH10 or AS2 protein have abaxialized filamentous and trumpet-like
CC       leaves at high temperatures. In double mutants, shapes of epidermal
CC       cells of the filamentous leaves are simple and rectangular, similar to
CC       those of a petiole, but different from those of flat leaves of wild-
CC       type plants. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27334696}.
