Gene Description

NSP1 (nitrile-specifier protein 1; At3g16400; also called JAL28, and AtNSP3 in the nomenclature of Kissen and Bones) is a cytosolic specifier protein of the glucosinolate-myrosinase defence system. It has two parts: an N-terminal jacalin-related lectin (JAL) domain with a beta-prism fold, and a C-terminal Kelch-repeat six-bladed beta-propeller that carries the active site, including a predicted EXXXDXXXH-type iron-binding triad. The protein is a monomer. NSP1 has no activity on intact glucosinolates. After tissue damage it acts on the unstable aglucone released by myrosinase and converts it to a simple nitrile (with release of sulfur and sulfate; EC 4.8.1.5) instead of the default isothiocyanate. It does this with aliphatic, aromatic (benzyl) and indolic glucosinolates, but it does not form epithionitriles or thiocyanates. In Columbia-0, which lacks functional ESP in leaves, NSP1 accounts for most constitutive simple-nitrile formation in rosette leaves and for the rise in nitrile formation after feeding by Pieris rapae larvae; its transcript is induced by that herbivory. It is also the main nitrile-forming activity in seedlings and, together with NSP3, in roots. nsp1 mutants show altered glucosinolate content in seeds and during germination, which suggests an additional role in glucosinolate turnover. No function of the lectin domain, such as carbohydrate or myrosinase binding, has been demonstrated experimentally.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

nitrile-forming thiohydroximate-O-sulfate sulfolyase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: a (Z)-N-(sulfonatooxy)alkanimidothioate (a glucosinolate aglucone) = a nitrile + sulfur + sulfate (EC 4.8.1.5; RHEA:59956). Converts myrosinase-released glucosinolate aglucones to simple nitriles instead of isothiocyanates.

Justification: Nitrile-specifier proteins are characterized catalysts with a curated EC number and Rhea reaction. GO has no term below carbon-sulfur lyase activity (GO:0016846) for this reaction. It is the sister activity of the epithionitrile-forming (ESP) and thiocyanate-forming (TFP) sulfolyases proposed in those reviews.

Parent term: carbon-sulfur lyase activity

Core Functions

NSP1 is a nitrile-forming specifier protein, a carbon-sulfur lyase. It acts in the cytosol on the thiohydroximate-O-sulfate aglucone released by myrosinase and converts it to a simple nitrile with release of sulfur and sulfate (EC 4.8.1.5). It does this with aliphatic, aromatic and indolic glucosinolates, but it does not form epithionitriles or thiocyanates. It is the main source of constitutive and herbivore-induced simple-nitrile formation in Col-0 rosette leaves and of nitrile formation in seedlings and roots.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does the N-terminal jacalin-related lectin domain of NSP1 bind carbohydrates (or glycosylated myrosinase), and does it contribute to capturing the aglucone by bringing NSP1 close to myrosinase?

Q: Is NSP1 truly Fe(II)-dependent in vivo? Recombinant NSP1 was insensitive to EDTA in one study, while paralogs bind iron through a conserved EXXXDXXXH triad.

Q: Is the seed and germination glucosinolate phenotype of nsp1 a direct turnover role of NSP1 in intact tissue, or an indirect result of feedback on hydrolytic enzymes?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Glycan-array or affinity binding assays with the isolated NSP1 JAL domain, and pull-down/proximity labelling with TGG1/TGG2 and PYK10-type myrosinases, to test lectin function and myrosinase association.

Experiment: ICP-MS/Ferene S iron quantification and E386Q/D390N/H394A mutagenesis of purified NSP1, to confirm the iron cofactor and its requirement for nitrile formation.

Experiment: Germination-rate and dormancy assays (after-ripening, stratification response) on nsp1 alleles and complemented lines, to test whether any developmental phenotype exists beyond altered glucosinolate content.

NSP1 (Q9SDM9, At3g16400) curation notes

Nomenclature warning

  • In Burow et al. 2009 and the Wittstock-lab papers, NSP1 is At3g16400 (this gene).
  • Kissen & Bones 2009 call At3g16400 "AtNSP3" and use "AtNSP1" for At5g48180 PMID:19224919.
  • In PMID:30395611, "AtNSP3" is At3g16390, a tandem paralog PMID:30395611. Its iron data therefore do not apply directly to NSP1.
  • Kong et al. 2012 (PMID:22954730, cached, abstract-only) uses the Kissen numbering, so its "AtNSP1" is probably At5g48180. It is not used as evidence here.
  • Also named JAL28 (jacalin-related lectin 28).

Function

  • The nsp1 T-DNA mutant (SALK_072600) lacks constitutive and herbivore-induced leaf nitrile formation PMID:18987211.
  • Purified NSP1 forms simple nitriles only PMID:18987211.
  • In vitro, its activity did not depend on added iron PMID:18987211. UniProt still lists an Fe(2+) cofactor (by similarity or from other studies), so no iron binding NEW was proposed.
  • Organ roles PMID:27990154.
  • Structure: monomer with an N-terminal JAL domain [PMID:30900313 "NSP1 from A. thaliana (AtNSP1) (which is a monomer)"; PMID:30395611 "In addition, AtNSP1 possesses an N-terminal JAL domain"; PMID:28479247 crystal structure].
  • Seeds and germination PMID:31850033. The cache is abstract-only, and the phenotype is metabolic, so the seed germination and dormancy rows are UNDECIDED.

Decisions

  • Specifier activity: MODIFY enzyme regulator activity (IBA) to GO:0016846 carbon-sulfur lyase activity, consistent with ESP and TFP. Proposed new term: nitrile-forming thiohydroximate-O-sulfate sulfolyase activity (EC 4.8.1.5).
  • Lectin domain: carbohydrate binding (IEA) is KEEP_AS_NON_CORE, because glycan binding is untested PMID:19224919.
  • Nucleus (IBA) is MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. It is seeded only by ESP's WRKY53-dependent nuclear import PMID:17369373.
  • mRNA binding (HDA) is UNDECIDED, because the paper is abstract-only.
  • Response to herbivore (IMP) is KEEP_AS_NON_CORE. NSP1 is required for the P. rapae-induced nitrile shift, but no protective effect has been shown.
  • No falcon deep-research file was available when this review was written.
