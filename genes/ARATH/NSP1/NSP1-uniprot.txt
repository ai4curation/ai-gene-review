CC   -!- FUNCTION: Specifier protein responsible for constitutive and herbivore-
CC       induced simple nitrile formation, especially in seedlings and roots
CC       (PubMed:18987211, PubMed:19224919, PubMed:27990154). Promotes simple
CC       nitriles, but not epithionitrile or thiocyanate formation
CC       (PubMed:18987211, PubMed:19224919). Converts allylglucosinolate (allyl-
CC       GSL), 4-methylsulfinylbutylglucosinolate, 4-
CC       methylthiobutylglucosinolate, 2-propenylglucosinolate (sinigrin),
CC       indol-3-ylmethylglucosinolate (glucobrassicin), benzylisothiocyanate
CC       and benzylglucosinolate (glucotropaeolin) to their corresponding simple
CC       nitriles in the presence of myrosinase (PubMed:18987211,
CC       PubMed:19224919, PubMed:22954730). Catalyzes mainly the conversion of
CC       benzylisothiocyanate when benzylglucosinolate is used as the initial
CC       substrate of myrosinase (PubMed:19224919, PubMed:22954730). Involved in
CC       the regulation of glucosinolate content in seeds, during stratification
CC       and germination (PubMed:31850033). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18987211,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19224919, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22954730,
