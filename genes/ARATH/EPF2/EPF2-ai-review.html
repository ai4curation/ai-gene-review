Gene Description

EPIDERMAL PATTERNING FACTOR 2 (EPF2) is a small secreted cysteine-rich peptide hormone of the EPF/EPFL family (120-aa precursor with a signal peptide; the mature C-terminal peptide MEPF2 is held in a scaffold by three/four disulfide bonds and a functional loop). It is produced by meristemoid mother cells (MMCs) and their early stomatal-lineage descendants and acts non-cell-autonomously on neighbouring protodermal cells to limit how many cells enter, and remain in, the stomatal lineage. MEPF2 binds directly to the leucine-rich-repeat ectodomain of the receptor kinase ERECTA (its principal receptor; ERL1 is also bound) in a complex with the receptor-like co-receptor TOO MANY MOUTHS (TMM), and triggers rapid activation of the YODA-MKK4/5-MPK3/6 MAP kinase cascade that phosphorylates and destabilizes the stomatal-entry bHLH SPEECHLESS. EPF2 is antagonized by the positive peptide stomagen/EPFL9, which competes for the same ERECTA-TMM receptor, and is inactivated by cleavage by the CO2-induced secreted protease CRSP. Loss of EPF2 produces excess small stomatal-lineage cells and higher stomatal density, whereas overexpression nearly abolishes stomata; EPF2 is required for the reduction of stomatal development at elevated CO2.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: What is the processed, bioactive form of EPF2 in planta, and which proteases (besides CRSP, which inactivates it) generate or degrade MEPF2 in the apoplast?

Q: How does EPF2 binding to the ERECTA-TMM complex recruit SERK co-receptors and activate the receptor kinase, given that stomagen binds the same receptor without activating MAPKs?

Identity

  • UniProt Q8LC53 EPF2_ARATH, 120 aa, signal peptide 1-25, mature MEPF2 chain 69-120, four disulfides; plant cysteine-rich secretory peptide family, EPF subfamily.

Key findings

  • Produced by MMCs and early lineage cells; limits MMC fate non-cell-autonomously PMID:19435754.
  • Loss -> excess small lineage cells; overexpression -> virtually no stomata PMID:19398336.
  • Direct ligand of ERECTA (primary) with TMM as modulator PMID:22241782; MEPF2 also binds TMM PMID:22241782.
  • Stomagen competes with EPF2 for ER/TMM; EPF2 elicits MPK3/6 phosphorylation within 10 min PMID:26083750.
  • Required for CO2 repression of stomatal development; CRSP protease cleaves EPF2 PMID:25043023.

Curation decisions

  • Core MF: receptor ligand activity (GO:0048018, IBA accepted). IPI "protein kinase binding" rows MODIFIED to receptor ligand activity (TMM has no kinase domain; ERECTA binding is ectodomain-ligand binding).
  • Core BP: negative regulation of stomatal complex development (GO:2000122). Guard cell differentiation (IBA and IMP) MODIFIED to GO:2000122 - EPF2 acts at lineage entry and inhibits; IBA propagation_review records TERM_SCOPING_PROBLEM / regulatory sign.
