Gene Description

FRUITFULL (FUL; AGL8, At5g60910) is a MIKC-type MADS-domain transcription factor of the SQUAMOSA/AP1 clade. FUL is induced early at the shoot apex during the floral transition (by FT/FD, miR156-regulated SPL factors and other flowering signals) and accumulates in the inflorescence meristem, inflorescence stem and cauline leaves, but is excluded from young floral primordia by APETALA1. It promotes the reproductive transition partially redundantly with SOC1, and acts redundantly with AP1 and CAULIFLOWER in floral meristem identity: ap1 cal ful triple mutants never form flowers because LEAFY is not up-regulated and TERMINAL FLOWER1 is ectopically expressed. Later, FUL is expressed in the carpel valves, where it is required for valve cell elongation and differentiation and for silique growth; it also regulates stem growth and branch angle (for example by repressing SAUR10), and in the inflorescence meristem it directly represses APETALA2 and AP2-like genes to bring about the end-of-flowering proliferative arrest. FUL binds CArG boxes and forms heterodimers with other MADS proteins such as SOC1, SVP, AGL24 and SEP3.

Floral transition and meristem identity

  • AGL8 is expressed in the inflorescence meristem, stem and cauline leaves, excluded from floral primordia, partly by AP1
    PMID:8535133

  • FT-dependent accumulation PMID:16155177

  • FUL redundantly with SOC1 promotes flowering; heterodimer model
    PMID:24465009.
  • soc1 ful double mutants: perennial-like phenotypes, meristem determinacy PMID:18997783

Fruit and other roles

  • Fruit: PMID:9502732
  • Stem/branch growth via SAUR10 PMID:28586421.
  • End of flowering (global proliferative arrest) via direct AP2 repression PMID:29422669.
