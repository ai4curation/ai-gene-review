# JMJ25 / IBM1 (At3g07610, UniProt Q9SSE9) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research API was available; this synthesis was written manually from the cached
publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record. Provenance is inline.

## Identity
- Arabidopsis thaliana JMJ25, also INCREASE IN BONSAI METHYLATION 1 (IBM1), At3g07610, 1027 aa,
  JmjC domain (635-860) of the JHDM2/KDM3-like clade (PANTHER PTHR12549:SF11), with degenerate
  RING-type and B-box zinc fingers. Fe(II)-binding residues His679, Asp681, His828
  [file:ARATH/JMJ25/JMJ25-uniprot.txt].

## Molecular function
- H3K9me2/me1 demethylase; no activity on H3K9me3 or on H3K4/H3K27/H3K36 marks
  [UniProt CC FUNCTION, citing PMID:18218897, PMID:20834229]. H679A and D681A abolish activity on
  H3K9me2/1 [UniProt FT MUTAGEN, PMID:20834229].
- "Here, we show in Arabidopsis that the exclusion depends on a histone demethylase gene, IBM1"
  [PMID:20834229].

## Location
- YFP-IBM1 "was exclusively nuclear-localized in protoplasts" and associates with the gene bodies
  of DCL3 and RDR2 by ChIP [PMID:22772985].

## Biological role
- Prevents ectopic H3K9me2 and CHG methylation on transcribed genes; the hypermethylation of ibm1
  depends on KYP/SUVH4 and CMT3 [PMID:20834229; PMID:19262562 "IBM1 prevents the CHG methylation in
  genes, but not in transposons."].
- Directly demethylates H3K9 at RDR2 and DCL3, keeping them expressed; indirectly affects RdDM
  targets [PMID:22772985].
- Developmental defects of ibm1 (leaf, flower, pollen, fertility) depend on H3K9 methylation
  [PMID:18218897]; stomatal-lineage overproduction caused by gene-body hypermethylation of
  ERECTA-family genes, suppressed by kyp and cmt3 [PMID:27697902].
- IBM1 expression requires intronic DNA methylation and RNA processing factors (EDM2, IBM2/ASI1/SG1)
  that promote the long functional transcript [PMID:22580822; PMID:24404182; PMID:41036628].
- JMJ24 antagonizes IBM1 function [PMID:28400174, abstract only].
- IBM1 (but not JMJ14) counteracts DNA methylation of endogenous genes [PMID:33986281].
