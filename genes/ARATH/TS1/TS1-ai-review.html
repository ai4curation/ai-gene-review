Gene Description

TS1, also called MTO2, is a chloroplast pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent threonine synthase that converts O-phosphohomoserine and water to threonine and phosphate. The homodimeric enzyme is allosterically activated by S-adenosylmethionine and competes with the methionine branch for O-phosphohomoserine. TS1-dependent threonine metabolism supports root meristem growth; impaired TS1 can alter threonine and methionine pools without establishing that the enzyme is itself a transcriptional regulator.

TS1 annotation review notes

PMID10429206 directly establishes PLP-dependent TS activity, homodimerization and SAM activation. PMID11344332 is the apo structure: its proposed PLP/SAM sites are not ligand-bound observations. PMID16319072 later directly resolves PLP and AdoMet complexes. PMID10806229 describes the native mto2-1 metabolic phenotype, supporting competition with the Met branch, not direct Met synthesis by TS1. Full PMID36278862 distinguishes measured root cell/marker phenotypes from modeled local Thr distributions: whole-root freeThr can increase despite impaired meristem growth; the predicted stem-cell depletion is not directly measured. Meristem maintenance is retained as non-core. Current UniProt PTHR10314 has official name CYSTATHIONINE BETA-SYNTHASE; verified membership does not assign CBS activity to TS1. PMID28887381 supplemental Mapp row1113 detects At4g29840.1 but labels it plastid; plasma-membrane residence remains unresolved, not excluded by absence of a membrane anchor.
