CC       epithionitriles after hydrolysis of alkenylglucosinolates containing a
CC       terminal double bond. Mediates indol-3-ylacetonitrile (IACN) production
CC       from indol-3-ylmethylglucosinolate (glucobrassicin) (PubMed:22954730).

CC       catalysis by myrosinase (PubMed:19224919, PubMed:22954730). Seems
CC       inactive toward benzylglucosinolate (glucotropaeolin)
