CC   -!- FUNCTION: May be a transcription factor part of a circadian clock input
CC       pathway. Acts within a 'zeitnehmer' feedback loop and is involved in
CC       its own circadian regulation. Has no role in regulating circadian clock
CC       function in the dark. Part of a corepressor complex consisting of ELF4,
CC       ELF3, and LUX involved in the transcriptional regulation of APRR9. The
CC       activity of the protein may be decreased in long day conditions due to
CC       its interaction with phytochrome B (phyB). Can regulate the initiation
CC       of flowering independently of phyB. Also involved in responses to
CC       nematode parasitism, like the formation of the nematode feeding
CC       structure. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11402161, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22307044,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22327739}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts specifically with both Pr and Pfr forms of
CC       phytochrome B. Interacts with ELF4. May form a homodimer.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22327739}.
