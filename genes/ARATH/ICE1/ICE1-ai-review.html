Gene Description

ICE1 (INDUCER OF CBF EXPRESSION 1; also SCREAM/SCRM, bHLH116) is a constitutively expressed nuclear MYC-type (group IIIb) basic helix-loop-helix transcription factor. In the stomatal lineage it is the obligate heterodimerization partner, redundantly with its paralog SCRM2, of the stage-specific bHLHs SPEECHLESS, MUTE and FAMA, specifying the three successive transitions of lineage entry/asymmetric division, meristemoid-to-guard-mother-cell differentiation and guard cell differentiation; scrm scrm2 loss sequentially phenocopies fama, mute and spch. ICE1 also acts as a scaffold that couples the MAP kinases MPK3/MPK6 to SPEECHLESS: a bipartite MAPK-docking/KRAAM (KiDoK) motif binds MPK3/6, promoting phosphorylation and degradation of SCRM and SPCH, and the dominant scrm-D (R236H) mutation in this motif abolishes MAPK binding and converts the whole epidermis into stomata. In the endosperm ICE1 heterodimerizes with ZHOUPI to control endosperm breakdown and seed dormancy (repressing ABI3), and it modulates ABA responses during germination through interactions with ABI5 and DELLA proteins. ICE1 was originally identified as an activator of CBF3/DREB1A and freezing tolerance; it binds MYC elements in CBF promoters and is regulated by HOS1-mediated ubiquitination and SIZ1-mediated sumoylation, but its requirement for cold-induced CBF expression has been disputed because the original ice1-1 CBF3 phenotype was shown to arise from transgene-induced silencing.

Key findings

  • SCRM = ICE1; SCRM/SCRM2 heterodimerize with SPCH, MUTE, FAMA; scrm scrm2 successively phenocopies fama, mute, spch; scrm-D (R236H) gives stomata-only epidermis PMID:18641265.

  • Cold: ICE1 binds MYC sites in CBF3 promoter and is a transcriptional activator, constitutively expressed PMID:12672693; HOS1 ubiquitinates PMID:16702557; SIZ1 sumoylates (K393) PMID:17416732.
  • DISPUTE: ice1-1 DREB1A/CBF3 repression is transgene-induced silencing; ICE1 OE or ice1 scrm2 loss did not alter DREB1A PMID:32034036; rebuttal with ICE1 ChIP-seq at CBF/COR promoters PMID:32068336.
  • Seeds: ICE1 with ZOU controls endosperm and dormancy; represses ABI3 PMID:30570804; antagonizes ABI5/DELLA in germination PMID:31123050.
