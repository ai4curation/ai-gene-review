Gene Description

C3H15 (AtC3H15; also CALLOSE DEFECTIVE MICROSPORE 1, CDM1; At1g68200) is a plant tandem CCCH zinc-finger (TZF) protein of the TTP/TIS11-related family, with two C-X8-C-X5-C-X3-H fingers near its C-terminus. Its closest relative is the duplicated paralog C3H14, and most mutant phenotypes appear only in c3h14 c3h15 double mutants. C3H15 has two biochemical activities. (1) Like animal TTP proteins, it binds target mRNAs and promotes their decay. Recombinant C3H15 binds a poly(U) region in the IAMH2 3' UTR and represses a reporter carrying that UTR. C3H15 also localizes to P-bodies, interacts with the decapping enzyme DCP1, and associates with the CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex. In male meiocytes, C3H14 and C3H15 clear highly expressed and identity-inappropriate transcripts around the zygotene-pachytene transition; without both proteins, meiocytes arrest and degenerate. An earlier cdm1 T-DNA allele (SALK_065040), predicted to make a truncated protein lacking the second finger, was male sterile with defective callose metabolism around the tetrad. (2) C3H15 also binds G-rich DNA elements (CHRE, typically GGGAGA) in promoters and acts as a transcriptional repressor. In the absence of brassinosteroid (BR), the kinase BIN2 phosphorylates C3H15 at Ser111 and C3H15 stays in the cytoplasm. BR leads to its dephosphorylation and nuclear accumulation, where it antagonizes BZR1/BES1 at shared targets such as SAUR15, and so negatively regulates BR-induced cell elongation. C3H14 and C3H15 are also required for normal secondary wall thickening. C3H15 is expressed strongly in meiocytes and tapetum and in tissues forming secondary walls. Under heat stress, it moves to stress granules and is degraded via the E3 ligase MBR2.

Core Functions

Sequence-specific mRNA 3'-UTR-binding adaptor of the TTP/TIS11 family. It binds target transcripts (for example, a poly(U) motif in the IAMH2 3' UTR) and directs them to decay through P-body decapping (DCP1) and CCR4-NOT deadenylation. Acting redundantly with C3H14, this activity clears unwanted and highly expressed transcripts from male meiocytes and is needed for meiotic progression and microsporogenesis.

Synthesis

Two well-supported activities:
1. Sequence-specific mRNA 3'-UTR binding that promotes mRNA decay (P-body, DCP1,
CCR4-NOT), conserved TZF mechanism. Strongest direct evidence for C3H15 itself:
RNA-EMSA + reporter (PMID:42844995). Biological contexts: male meiocyte
transcript clearance (microsporogenesis), IAMH2 in hypocotyls.
2. Nuclear, BR-gated DNA binding to G-rich CHRE elements and transcriptional
repression (PMID:35139225), opposing BZR1/BES1.
Which activity underlies the secondary-wall, heat, defence and metal phenotypes is
not established.
