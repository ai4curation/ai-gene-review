Gene Description

VRN1 (VERNALIZATION 1) is a nuclear Arabidopsis protein with two plant-specific B3 DNA-binding domains. Unlike most B3 proteins, it binds DNA without sequence specificity, through a defined positively charged surface on the second B3 domain. VRN1 associates broadly with euchromatic chromosomes (not chromocenters) in interphase and mitosis, but not in meiosis, independently of vernalization or VRN2. It is required to maintain the stable repression of the floral repressor FLC after winter cold: in vrn1 mutants FLC is down-regulated in the cold but reactivated afterwards. It needs vernalization-specific factors to act at FLC. VRN1 also has vernalization-independent roles in flowering (through FT, seen on overexpression) and, judging from dominant repressor fusions that overcome redundancy, in essential developmental processes.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Which vernalization-specific factors target the sequence-nonspecific VRN1 to FLC, and does VRN1 physically associate with PRC2, LHP1 or VAL1 at the nucleation region?

Q: What essential developmental processes are revealed by VRN1-SRDX, and which B3 paralogs act redundantly with VRN1?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Profile VRN1 genome-wide by ChIP-seq before, during and after vernalization in wild type, vrn2 and lhp1 backgrounds. Use IP-MS of VRN1 to identify partners and test whether the DNA-binding-dead R249E/R289E/R296E mutant complements vrn1.

Hypothesis: VRN1 is recruited to FLC after cold by interaction with Polycomb maintenance factors.
