Gene Description

C3H14 (AT1G66810) is a 310-residue tandem CCCH zinc-finger (TZF) protein of the ZFP36/tristetraprolin-like family. It carries two C-terminal C3H1-type zinc fingers (residues 232-298) spaced as in animal TTP, and it acts largely redundantly with its paralog C3H15. Its best-supported activity is sequence-specific binding of target mRNAs through the TZF domain, including 3' UTR elements of the polygalacturonase ADPG1 mRNA and a poly(U)-rich motif in the IAMH2 3' UTR. Binding represses target expression post-transcriptionally. C3H14/C3H15 associate with processing-body proteins and the CCR4-NOT deadenylase, and they clear abundant mRNAs from male meiocytes at the zygotene-pachytene transition, which is needed for meiotic progression and meiocyte identity. C3H14 localizes to cytoplasmic foci and is also found in the nucleus. In the nucleus it binds G-rich promoter elements and activates target genes such as NIMIN1 and NIMIN2. C3H14 is a direct MYB46 target expressed in secondary-wall-forming tissues. It promotes secondary wall thickening, restrains cell elongation, promotes basal defense against Botrytis cinerea through WRKY33, and acts as an MPK4-inactivated negative regulator of PAMP-triggered immunity and systemic acquired resistance against Pseudomonas syringae.

Core Functions

Sequence-specific binding of target mRNA 3' UTRs through the tandem CCCH zinc-finger domain, which leads to post-transcriptional repression and decay of the bound transcripts. Characterised C3H14 targets include ADPG1 and IAMH2. Together with C3H15, it is required for clearing abundant transcripts from male meiocytes. The protein acts in the cytoplasm with P-body and CCR4-NOT decay machinery, redundantly with C3H15.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Native-promoter C3H14-VENUS forms cytoplasmic foci in meiocytes (PMID:41758611), but DCP1 colocalization and binding were tested only for C3H15. Does C3H14 itself colocalize with or bind DCP1? If so, P-body (GO:0000932) would be a justified location.

Q: In meiocytes, C3H15 binds its targets mainly in coding regions (PMID:41758611), whereas C3H14's characterised targets (ADPG1, DUF1218, IAMH2) are bound in 3' UTRs. Do the paralogs differ in binding-site preference, or does it depend on cell type?

Q: Is the DNA-binding and transcriptional-activation role of C3H14 (CHRE elements, NIMIN1/2, WRKY33) reproducible outside the group that reported it? How does a TTP-type tandem zinc finger discriminate DNA from RNA targets?

Q: Should TAIR's GO:1901347 negative regulation of secondary cell wall biogenesis row from PMID:25228083 be re-pointed to GO:1901348, given that the paper reports enhanced phloem fiber formation on overexpression?

Q: Given the 2026 meiocyte data, should male meiosis or meiocyte-identity process terms be annotated to C3H14? Is the RNA-clearance role better captured as regulation of a meiotic process than as participation in it?
