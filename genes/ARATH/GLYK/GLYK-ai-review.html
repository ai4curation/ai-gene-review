Gene Description

GLYK (D-glycerate 3-kinase; At1g80380) is the single-copy ATP-dependent glycerate kinase of Arabidopsis, which phosphorylates D-glycerate to 3-phospho-D-glycerate (EC 2.7.1.31). This is the concluding reaction of the photorespiratory C2 cycle: glycerate formed in peroxisomes from serine is imported into the chloroplast, and the 3-phosphoglycerate made by GLYK re-enters the Calvin-Benson cycle. GLYK has a P-loop NTPase fold related to phosphoribulokinase and uridine kinase but has neither of those activities, and it founded a glycerate kinase family, present in plants, fungi and some cyanobacteria, that is unrelated to the bacterial and animal glycerate kinases. The main isoform carries a chloroplast transit peptide and acts in the stroma. Knockout mutants lack leaf glycerate kinase activity, accumulate glycerate and cannot survive in normal air but grow in CO2-enriched air. Phytochrome-controlled use of an alternative promoter produces a shorter, cytoplasmic GLYK isoform in shade, which provides a cytosolic photorespiratory bypass that reduces photoinhibition under fluctuating light.

    (uridine kinase family). Three isoforms in UniProt; isoform 3 lacks residues 1-92 (no
    transit peptide).
  • Structurally unrelated to bacterial and animal glycerate kinases PMID:15980259.

Molecular function: D-glycerate 3-kinase (EC 2.7.1.31)

  • Purified from leaves and identified by MS PMID:15980259.
  • Recombinant protein active; substrate specificity tested PMID:15980259 and neither PRK nor uridine kinase activity PMID:15980259.
  • The only GLYK activity source in leaves PMID:15980259.
  • UniProt catalytic activity: (R)-glycerate + ATP = (2R)-3-phosphoglycerate + ADP + H+
    (Rhea:23516; ECO:0000269 PubMed:15980259). ATP-binding P-loop at 215-222 (ECO:0000255).
  • A 2024 study on thermostability engineering of GLYK (PMID:39550762, not cached) reports
    Km values for ATP and glycerate for AtGLYK; not used here.

Biological process: photorespiration (final step of the C2 cycle)

  • PMID:15980259
  • Glycerate accumulates ~200-fold in the mutants PMID:15980259.
  • Review PMID:22303256 and PMID:22303256. Unlike most photorespiratory genes, GLYK is not
    light induced PMID:22303256.
  • GO:0009854 "oxidative photosynthetic carbon pathway" (C2 pathway: 2-phosphoglycolate to
