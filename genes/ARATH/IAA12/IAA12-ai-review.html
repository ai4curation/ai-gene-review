Gene Description

IAA12 (BODENLOS, BDL; At1g04550) is an Aux/IAA transcriptional repressor of auxin-responsive gene expression. It is a short-lived nuclear protein with an N-terminal domain I carrying an EAR (LxLxL) motif that recruits the TOPLESS co-repressor, a domain II degron that binds the TIR1/AFB auxin receptors in an auxin-dependent manner, and a C-terminal PB1 domain (domains III/IV) through which it heterodimerizes with AUXIN RESPONSE FACTOR proteins, principally the activator ARF5/MONOPTEROS. At low auxin, IAA12 binds MP and recruits TPL to repress MP target genes; auxin promotes IAA12 binding to SCF(TIR1/AFB), ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation, releasing MP. The bdl gain-of-function mutation in domain II stabilizes IAA12 and blocks MP-dependent specification of the hypophysis and embryonic root meristem, and causes apical-basal patterning and vascular defects; these are suppressed by tpl-1.

Summary: Phylogenetic propagation of transcription cis-regulatory region binding across the Aux/IAA clade (PTN001272225), seeded largely from yeast one-hybrid promoter-binding data for Aux/IAA family members.
Reason: Aux/IAA proteins lack a DNA-binding domain; the βαα fold once proposed for domain III is now known to be a PB1 protein-protein interaction domain. Aux/IAAs reach auxin-responsive promoters indirectly by heterodimerizing with DNA-bound ARFs. Yeast one-hybrid positives for Aux/IAAs most plausibly reflect such indirect recruitment, so direct cis-regulatory region binding over-states the evidence. The family-level function is transcription corepressor activity.

2026-10 review (auxin_nuclear_signaling module)

  • Deep research (falcon) failed (HTTP 402); review based on cached literature.
  • Identity: UniProt Q38830 IAA12_ARATH, synonym BDL. Correct.

Function

  • bdl is a domain II (degron) gain-of-function: "the bodenlos phenotype results from an amino-acid exchange in the conserved degradation domain of IAA12"; "BODENLOS and MONOPTEROS interact in the yeast two-hybrid assay" PMID:12101120.
  • Embryo phenotype: "BDL is involved in auxin-mediated processes of apical-basal patterning in the Arabidopsis embryo" PMID:10068632.
  • TPL co-repressor: "TOPLESS (TPL) can physically interact with IAA12/BODENLOS (IAA12/BDL)" and TPL "is required for IAA12/BDL repressive activity"; "tpl-1 can suppress the patterning defects of the bdl-1 mutant" PMID:18258861.
  • Aux/IAA domain I = active, transferable repression domain with LxLxL motif PMID:14742873.
  • Receptors: "all TIR1/AFB proteins interact with BDL, and BDL is stabilized in triple mutant plants" PMID:15992545; IAA12 needs high auxin to bind TIR1/AFB2 in yeast PMID:22466420.
  • Specificity: "stabilization of BDL/IAA12 or its sister protein IAA13 prevents MP/ARF5-dependent embryonic root formation" PMID:15889151; MP and BDL act in the proembryo to specify the hypophysis PMID:16459305.

Decisions

  • GO:0003700 (ISS, TF catalogue) -> MODIFY to GO:0003714 transcription corepressor activity (no DNA-binding domain).
  • GO:0000976 IBA and Y1H IPI -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED (indirect promoter association via ARFs).
  • Protein binding: ARF partners -> GO:0140297; Aux/IAA partners -> GO:0046982; unrelated HT partners -> REMOVE.
  • NEW: GO:0009734 auxin-activated signaling pathway (IAA12 is the co-receptor/substrate that does the signalling step).

description: >-
  IAA12 (BODENLOS, BDL; At1g04550) is an Aux/IAA transcriptional repressor of auxin-responsive
  gene expression. It is a short-lived nuclear protein with an N-terminal domain I carrying
  an EAR (LxLxL) motif that recruits the TOPLESS co-repressor, a domain II degron that binds
  the TIR1/AFB auxin receptors in an auxin-dependent manner, and a C-terminal PB1 domain (domains
  III/IV) through which it heterodimerizes with AUXIN RESPONSE FACTOR proteins, principally
  the activator ARF5/MONOPTEROS. At low auxin, IAA12 binds MP and recruits TPL to repress
  MP target genes; auxin promotes IAA12 binding to SCF(TIR1/AFB), ubiquitination and proteasomal
  degradation, releasing MP. The bdl gain-of-function mutation in domain II stabilizes IAA12
  and blocks MP-dependent specification of the hypophysis and embryonic root meristem, and
  causes apical-basal patterning and vascular defects; these are suppressed by tpl-1.
