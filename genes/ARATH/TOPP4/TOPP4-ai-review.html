Gene Description

TYPE ONE PROTEIN PHOSPHATASE 4 (TOPP4) is a PP1-family serine/threonine protein phosphatase whose regulatory partners and substrates connect it to several developmental and stress responses. TOPP4 dephosphorylates DELLA proteins RGA and GAI in gibberellin signaling, PIF5 during red-light responses, PIN1 in pavement-cell polarity and trafficking, and ATG13a during carbon-starvation-induced autophagy. It has nuclear, cytoplasmic and plasma-membrane-associated pools. TOPP4 also associates with APRF1 and LD in a transcription-termination-linked module involved in FLC chromatin repression, although that module's direct phosphatase substrate has not been established. Substrate dephosphorylation has different consequences in different pathways: it promotes DELLA turnover but stabilizes PIF5.

Core Functions

PP1-type serine/threonine protein dephosphorylation with substrate-specific developmental consequences: DELLA turnover in GA signaling, PIF5 stabilization in red-light signaling, PIN1 polarity/trafficking and ATG13a regulation in autophagy. Regulatory subunits specify context; TOPP4 is also associated with a flowering-related termination module whose substrate remains unresolved.
