Gene Description

ALAAT1 is a mitochondrial pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent alanine aminotransferase. It reversibly interconverts alanine and 2-oxoglutarate with pyruvate and glutamate and is especially important for alanine utilization during recovery from low oxygen. It also contributes to mitochondrial glyoxylate transamination in a conditional photorespiratory side pathway.

ALAAT1 evidence audit

PMID:17319845 supports native alanine breakdown during post-hypoxia recovery; enzyme induction alone is not evidence for obligatory alanine synthesis during hypoxia. Full text of PMID:22268146 distinguishes ALAAT1 RFP mitochondrial localization/native RNAi/activity-loss and tobacco overexpression from the purified multisubstrate assays, which used ALAAT2. No ALAAT2 kinetic values are assigned to ALAAT1. Cytosolic classification and ATP-affinity capture are not negated by mitochondrial localization or PLP dependence. Exact ALAAT1 ATP-capture evidence could not be resolved after publisher/supplement 403 attempts. UniProt C4-pathway statement is not used as proof that Arabidopsis performs canonical C4 photosynthesis.

All original annotation payloads retained; only review objects were curated. Falcon and perplexity-lite fallback were attempted concurrently with publication caching and failed (403/401 provider errors); no provider report was fabricated. FEBA is not applicable to this plant panel.

All annotations were assessed as biological claims. Specific true functions were retained across evidence codes; broad terms were refined; unresolved compartment/affinity-screen claims were not converted into negative evidence. No NEW annotations were added solely to fill module coverage.

Supplementary-table provenance and exact target rows are in ../ASP2/ASP2-source-analysis/ (or the local ASP2-source-analysis directory for ASP2).
