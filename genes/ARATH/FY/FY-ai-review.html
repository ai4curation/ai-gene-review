Gene Description

FY is the Arabidopsis ortholog of yeast Pfs2p and human WDR33, a WD40-repeat core subunit of the cleavage and polyadenylation specificity factor (CPSF) complex that is required for pre-mRNA 3'-end cleavage and polyadenylation. FY associates stably in vivo with CPSF100 and CPSF160 and carries a plant-specific C-terminal domain with two PPLPP motifs that bind the WW domain of the RNA-binding protein FCA. This transient FCA-FY interaction changes FY/CPSF complex composition and directs 3'-end processing to promoter-proximal poly(A) sites, both in the FCA pre-mRNA (negative autoregulation of FCA) and in the FLC antisense COOLAIR transcripts, leading to repression of the floral repressor FLC and promotion of flowering through the autonomous pathway. FY also contacts the SET-domain protein SDG26, physically linking proximal polyadenylation to the FLD/LD/SDG26 H3K4 demethylation module. Null fy alleles are embryo lethal, reflecting an essential general role in mRNA 3'-end formation, whereas hypomorphic alleles that lose the FCA-binding C terminus are late flowering. FY is a nuclear protein that colocalizes with FCA in nuclear condensates.

Core Functions

WDR33/Pfs2p-type core subunit of the CPSF 3'-end processing complex, required for pre-mRNA cleavage and polyadenylation (essential function).

Directly Involved In:
mRNA 3'-end processing
Cellular Locations:
nucleus
In Complex:
mRNA cleavage and polyadenylation specificity factor complex
Supporting Evidence:
  • PMID:19439664
    AtCPSF100 and AtCPSF160, but not FCA, are stably associated with FY in vivo and form a range of different-sized complexes.
  • PMID:16033802
    Hence, FY may mediate both regulated and constitutive RNA 3'-end processing.

Docking platform through which FCA (via its WW domain binding FY PPLPP motifs) and the FLD-complex subunit SDG26 engage CPSF, directing proximal polyadenylation of FCA pre-mRNA and COOLAIR and coupling it to FLC chromatin silencing, thereby promoting flowering.

Directly Involved In:
mRNA alternative polyadenylation regulation of flower development
Supporting Evidence:
  • PMID:16033802
    The FY C-terminal domain binds FCA and in vitro assays demonstrate a requirement for both C-terminal FY-PPLPP repeats during this interaction.
  • PMID:32541063
    SDG26 interacts with the RNA 3' processing factor FY (WDR33), thus linking activities for proximal polyadenylation of the antisense transcripts to FLD/LD/SDG26-associated H3K4 demethylation.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does plant FY, like human WDR33, directly contact the poly(A) signal RNA, and does FCA binding to the FY C terminus alter this recognition at proximal sites?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Cryo-EM or crosslinking mass spectrometry of Arabidopsis CPSF with and without the FCA WW domain bound to FY PPLPP motifs, combined with in vitro cleavage/polyadenylation assays on COOLAIR proximal and distal poly(A) sites.

Hypothesis: FCA binding remodels FY/CPSF to favour cleavage at proximal poly(A) sites.

Type: Structural biology / in vitro 3'-processing reconstitution
