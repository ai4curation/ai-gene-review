Gene Description

ALDH12A1/P5CDH is a mitochondrial aldehyde dehydrogenase that oxidizes glutamate semialdehyde, in equilibrium with pyrroline-5-carboxylate, to glutamate. This completes proline degradation and provides a catabolic outlet for ornithine-derived intermediates. By controlling P5C metabolism it influences mitochondrial redox balance and sensitivity to excess proline, and it supports carbon and nitrogen remobilization during seed filling.
