Gene Description

ASN2 is a glutamine-dependent asparagine synthetase that couples ATP-dependent amidation of aspartate to glutamine hydrolysis. It supplies asparagine for nitrogen assimilation and redistribution during vegetative growth, with prominent expression in leaf phloem companion cells and contributions to nitrogen metabolism under salt stress.

ASN2 evidence audit

PMID:9881155 complements a yeast asparagine auxotroph with ASN2/ASN3 and identifies the glutamine-binding architecture. This supports the conserved glutamine-hydrolyzing ASNS class, but is not a purified donor-selectivity measurement. Full text of PMID:22789031 publisher text was inspected at https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/j.1365-3040.2012.02576.x : native knockout/knockdown, phloem-companion-cell immunolocalization and 15N remobilization support asparagine synthesis. Its recombinant Cys2Gly protein was an antibody antigen, not a native glutaminase kinetic assay. The proposed GABA-shunt compensation is a metabolite-based interpretation, not measured flux. Salt response in PMID:21478030 remains contextual. Full text of PMID:30664249 publisher text was inspected; target-specific SG supplement download returned 403, so ASN2 sequestration remains unresolved here, without asserting absence.

All original annotation payloads retained; only review objects were curated. Falcon and perplexity-lite fallback were attempted concurrently with publication caching and failed (403/401 provider errors); no provider report was fabricated. FEBA is not applicable to this plant panel.

All annotations were assessed as biological claims. Specific true functions were retained across evidence codes; broad terms were refined; unresolved compartment/affinity-screen claims were not converted into negative evidence. No NEW annotations were added solely to fill module coverage.

Supplementary-table provenance and exact target rows are in ../ASP2/ASP2-source-analysis/ (or the local ASP2-source-analysis directory for ASP2).
