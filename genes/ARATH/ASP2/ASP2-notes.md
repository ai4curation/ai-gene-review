# ASP2 evidence audit

Primary9535706 characterizes recombinant ASP2;12068109 full text resolves the nomenclature: ASP2 encodes activity isozyme AAT2, whereas ASP5 encodes chloroplastic activity AAT3. Native mutations reduce cytosolic AAT activity and alter Asp/Asn pools; the alleles are not all nulls. Broad amino-acid metabolism is refined to measured Asp/Glu/2-oxoglutarate metabolism. Native cytosolic evidence does not exclude every possible minor organellar or membrane-associated pool. Proteomic side localizations are reviewed separately rather than rejected solely for lack of a targeting sequence.

All original annotation payloads retained; only review objects were curated. Falcon and perplexity-lite fallback were attempted concurrently with publication caching and failed (403/401 provider errors); no provider report was fabricated. FEBA is not applicable to this plant panel.

All annotations were assessed as biological claims. Specific true functions were retained across evidence codes; broad terms were refined; unresolved compartment/affinity-screen claims were not converted into negative evidence. No NEW annotations were added solely to fill module coverage.

Supplementary-table provenance and exact target rows are in `../ASP2/ASP2-source-analysis/` (or the local ASP2-source-analysis directory for ASP2). PMID28887381 is a mixed-fraction protein-correlation study, not automatic evidence of every annotated compartment. PMID21533090 S3 peptide detection and S4 targeting classification were inspected; S5 merely lists prior GO assertions and was not used as independent evidence.


### Final negative-localization audit

The seeded annotation explicitly excludes Golgi localization. Native cytosolic ASP2 activity is established, but the exact target-specific fractionation and exclusion evidence in PMID22430844 could not be resolved from the inspected material; supplementary download attempts failed. Cytosolic residence alone does not prove absence from Golgi. The unrelated ASP5 negative-prephenate rationale was removed from this localization row and its source assessment. Original annotation fields, including negation, are preserved.
