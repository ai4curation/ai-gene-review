Gene Description

IAMH1 (INDOLE-3-ACETAMIDE HYDROLASE 1, AT4G37550) is a soluble amidohydrolase of the acetamidase/formamidase (FmdA_AmdA) family, encoded in tandem with its 90%-identical paralog IAMH2 (AT4G37560) on chromosome 4. IAMH1-like genes are present across the green lineage, from Chlamydomonas and Physcomitrella to flowering plants. IAMH1 was identified in a forward-genetic screen for mutants insensitive to indole-3-acetamide (IAM) but with normal auxin responses. Loss-of-function alleles resist exogenous IAM, and complementation, including with a functional IAMH1-GFP fusion, restores IAM sensitivity. Together with IAMH2, IAMH1 is required to convert exogenous or bacterial iaaM-derived IAM into the auxin indole-3-acetic acid (IAA), and iamh1 iamh2 double mutants suppress iaaM-driven auxin overproduction. The double mutants develop normally and keep wild-type IAA levels, so the IAM route seems to contribute little to bulk auxin under standard conditions. IAMH1 is broadly expressed in seedlings and flowers. An IAMH1-GFP fusion is excluded from the nucleus, and proteomic studies place the protein in the cytosol.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does purified IAMH1 hydrolyse IAM in vitro, with what kinetics, and does it retain the formamidase or acetamidase activity of its bacterial and fungal family members?

Q: The iamh1 single mutant was isolated as IAM-insensitive even though IAMH2 is 90% identical, so what determines the non-redundant contribution of IAMH1 (expression level, tissue pattern or catalytic efficiency)?

Q: Does the residual IAM-to-IAA conversion in iamh1 iamh2 double mutants depend on AMI1 or other amidase-signature enzymes?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Purify recombinant IAMH1 and IAMH2 and compare kinetic parameters with IAM, formamide, acetamide and phenylacetamide.

Experiment: Generate iamh1 iamh2 ami1 triple mutants and test residual IAM sensitivity and IAM-derived IAA formation.

Experiment: Determine the subcellular localization of IAMH1-GFP at higher resolution, with organelle markers, to test the chloroplast prediction and the vacuolar and membrane proteomic detections.

IAMH1 (AT4G37550) curation notes

Identity

  • Locus AT4G37550. There are seven unreviewed UniProt entries for this locus (B9DFI3, Q9SZE9, F4JS37, F4JS39,
    A0A178UXH8, A0A1P8B5Z2, A0A1P8B5Z4), corresponding to alternative gene models. I used A0A178UXH8 (456 aa,
    reference proteome UP000006548) because it is the entry that carries TAIR's GO annotations; the other
    entries carry only the InterPro2GO IEA row (QuickGO, 2026-10-21).
  • Symbol IAMH1 (INDOLE-3-ACETAMIDE HYDROLASE 1) was assigned by Gao et al. 2020
    PMID:32327358.
  • Tandem paralog of IAMH2 (AT4G37560), 90% identical PMID:32327358.
    See genes/ARATH/IAMH2/IAMH2-notes.md for the shared family and domain analysis.

Function evidence (Gao et al. 2020, PMID:32327358; full text read)

  • Isolated from an EMS forward screen for IAM-insensitive mutants that keep normal auxin sensitivity. The
    allele iamh1-1 carries a premature stop codon; a T-DNA allele (iamh1-2) is also IAM-resistant.
  • Complementation with the genomic fragment and with IAMH1pro:IAMH1-GFP:
    PMID:32327358
    PMID:32327358
  • Expression: PMID:32327358
    At the reproductive stage, GUS was seen in young flowers, gynoecia and inflorescences.
  • Localization: PMID:32327358
    The cached text describes the signal only as non-nuclear and does not name another compartment.
  • Double mutants iamh1-1 iamh2-2 and iamh1-1 iamh2-3 suppress iaaM-driven auxin overproduction and the
    IAM-induced rise in IAA and IAA conjugates, but have no developmental defect under normal growth conditions.
    Steady-state IAA is similar to wild type.
  • I found no in vitro enzyme assay with purified IAMH1.
  • Orthologs: PMID:32327358

GO annotation decisions (summary)

  • GO:0016811 (IEA): MODIFY to GO:0043864 indoleacetamide hydrolase activity (a descendant; OLS checked).
  • GO:0005829 cytosol (HDA, Aryal et al. 2014): ACCEPT. It fits the non-nuclear GFP signal of a soluble
    enzyme without targeting signals.
  • GO:0000325 plant-type vacuole (HDA, Carter et al. 2004 vegetative vacuole proteome): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
  • It is an experimental high-throughput detection and I have not re-examined the per-protein data.
  • The IAM substrate and the cytosolic location argue against a vacuolar enzymatic role.
  • The paper itself notes contaminants in the preparation (it excluded 15% of hits as potential
    chloroplast or mitochondrial contaminants).
  • I left it as non-core rather than removing it, because I cannot see the curator's per-protein evidence.
  • GO:0005886 plasma membrane (HDA, membrane protein-correlation profiling, PMID:28887381): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
    That study found many soluble proteins partitioned between cytosolic and membrane-associated pools;
    peripheral membrane association is possible but has no known functional significance.
  • GO:0009507 chloroplast (ISM, AtSubP prediction): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED.
  • It is a single sequence-based prediction with no experimental support.
  • The first 80 residues of IAMH1 and IAMH2 are nearly identical (compared from the two UniProt files),
    yet IAMH2 has no chloroplast annotation.
  • None of the three proteomic datasets places IAMH1 in plastids.
  • NEW GO:0009684 indoleacetic acid biosynthetic process (IMP, PMID:32327358).
  • Participation: IAMH1 is proposed to catalyse IAM to IAA, so it performs a step of the process.
  • Evidence: genetic and metabolite data. Comparator AMI1 carries GO:0009684 by IDA (QuickGO, taxon 3702).
  • Caveat: the evidence concerns IAM supplied exogenously or produced from the iaaM transgene. Endogenous
    IAA is unchanged in the double mutant.
