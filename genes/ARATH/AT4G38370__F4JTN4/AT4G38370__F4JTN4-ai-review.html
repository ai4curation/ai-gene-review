Gene Description

At4g38370 encodes a 225-residue protein consisting of a single histidine phosphatase superfamily branch-1 domain (the phosphoglycerate-mutase-like fold, Pfam PF00300, CDD HP_PGM_like). It belongs to PANTHER family PTHR47821, a lineage of land plants, green and other algae, diatoms, choanoflagellates, fungi and some bacteria in which no member has been characterised experimentally. The protein retains the catalytic apparatus of the fold: the RHG-motif histidine (His17) that forms the phosphohistidine intermediate, the glutamate proton donor/acceptor (Glu109) and a C-terminal histidine (His173) aligned with the transition-state stabiliser of characterised branch-1 enzymes, so it is probably an active histidine-dependent phosphotransfer enzyme (a phosphomonoesterase or a mutase). Its substrate, reaction type, subcellular location and biological role have not been determined experimentally. It has no signal peptide or classical nuclear localisation signal, and the protein has been detected in large-scale proteomics.
