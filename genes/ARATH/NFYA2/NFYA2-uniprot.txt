CC   -!- FUNCTION: Stimulates the transcription of various genes by recognizing
CC       and binding to a CCAAT motif in promoters (By similarity). Promotes the
CC       expression of heat stress-inducible genes by contributing to the
CC       formation of a heat stress-specific transcriptional complex with NF-Y
CC       subunits (e.g. DPB3-1, NF-YA2 and NF-YB3) and DREB2A at the promoter of
CC       target genes, thus promoting heat tolerance (PubMed:25490919).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9SFD8, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25490919}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterotrimeric transcription factor composed of three
CC       components, NF-YA, NF-YB and NF-YC (By similarity). NF-YB and NF-YC
CC       must interact and dimerize for NF-YA association and DNA binding (By
CC       similarity). Component of a heat stress-inducible transcriptional
CC       complex with NF-YA and NF-YB subunits made, at least, of NFYA2, NFYB3
CC       and DPB3-1 in cooperation with DREB2A (PubMed:25490919).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P23511, ECO:0000269|PubMed:25490919}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25490919}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Ubiquitous (PubMed:11250072, PubMed:9662544).
CC       Expressed in seedlings, roots, petioles, hypocotyls, reproductive organ
CC       tissues and leaves (PubMed:25490919). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11250072,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25490919, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9662544}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: In seedlings, first expressed in petioles and leaf
CC       blades of the cotyledons as well as tops and bottoms of the hypocotyls.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:25490919}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the NFYA/HAP2 subunit family.
CC       {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00966}.
