Gene Description

FPA is a nuclear RNA-binding protein with RNA recognition motifs and a SPOC domain that regulates alternative cleavage and polyadenylation in Arabidopsis. It associates with chromatin near transcribed gene ends and controls termination and the production of read-through and chimeric RNAs. FPA promotes flowering by limiting FLC expression and also affects the processing of other transcripts, including immune-receptor genes. Its genetic overlap with FCA contributes to female gametophyte development, while their biochemical interactions are distinct.

Core Functions

Spen-family RRM RNA-binding protein that acts as a trans-acting regulator of poly(A) site choice, promoting proximal/premature cleavage and polyadenylation (including COOLAIR at FLC, redundantly with FCA), thereby limiting read-through transcription. Through COOLAIR 3'-end formation it acts upstream of FLC repression in the autonomous pathway; loss of FPA strongly delays flowering and overexpression accelerates flowering.
