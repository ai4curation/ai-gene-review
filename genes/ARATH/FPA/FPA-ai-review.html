Gene Description

FPA is a nuclear spen-family RNA-binding protein with three N-terminal RNA recognition motifs (RRMs) and a C-terminal SPOC domain that promotes flowering in Arabidopsis through the autonomous pathway. FPA is a trans-acting regulator of RNA 3'-end formation: it promotes cleavage and polyadenylation at promoter-proximal or intronic poly(A) sites, thereby controlling alternative polyadenylation, preventing transcriptional read-through and chimeric RNA formation, and mediating premature termination of transcripts such as those of NLR immune receptor genes. FPA negatively autoregulates its own pre-mRNA processing and acts redundantly with FCA to promote proximal polyadenylation of the FLC antisense COOLAIR transcripts, which contributes to repression of the floral repressor FLC. FPA does not act as a core component of the cleavage and polyadenylation machinery. Together with FCA, FPA is required for silencing at several loci and for female gametophyte and early embryo development.

Core Functions

Spen-family RRM RNA-binding protein that acts as a trans-acting regulator of poly(A) site choice, promoting proximal/premature cleavage and polyadenylation (including COOLAIR at FLC, redundantly with FCA), thereby limiting read-through transcription. Through COOLAIR 3'-end formation it acts upstream of FLC repression in the autonomous pathway; loss of FPA strongly delays flowering and overexpression accelerates flowering.
