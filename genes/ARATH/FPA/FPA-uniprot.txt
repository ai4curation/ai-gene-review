CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a role in the regulation of flowering time in the
CC       autonomous flowering pathway by decreasing FLOWERING LOCUS C mRNA
CC       levels. Required for RNA-mediated chromatin silencing of a range of
CC       loci in the genome. Cotranscriptionally recognizes aberrant RNA and
CC       marks it for silencing. Controls alternative cleavage and
CC       polyadenylation on pre-mRNAs and antisense RNAs. Acts redundantly with
CC       FCA to prevent the expression of distally polyadenylated antisense RNAs
CC       at the FLC locus. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11402170,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17916737, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20079695}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17916737}.
