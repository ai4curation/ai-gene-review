Gene Description

TGG2 (THIOGLUCOSIDE GLUCOHYDROLASE 2, BGLU37; At5g25980) is one of the two classical shoot myrosinases of Arabidopsis, a glycoside hydrolase family 1 beta-thioglucosidase (EC 3.2.1.147) that hydrolyzes the thioglucosidic bond of glucosinolates, releasing glucose and an unstable aglucone that rearranges to isothiocyanates or, with specifier proteins, to nitriles, epithionitriles or thiocyanates. TGG2 is encoded next to TGG1 in an inverted tandem arrangement and is expressed in myrosin idioblasts associated with the phloem, where the signal-peptide-bearing glycoprotein is stored in vacuole-derived compartments separated from glucosinolates until tissue damage. Its correct trafficking depends on the myrosinase-associated protein MVP1, which binds TGG2 specifically. TGG2 acts redundantly with TGG1: either enzyme alone supports normal damage-induced glucosinolate breakdown, whereas the tgg1 tgg2 double mutant lacks detectable leaf myrosinase activity, fails to hydrolyze aliphatic glucosinolates on damage and is more susceptible to generalist lepidopteran herbivores. The double mutant is also impaired in ABA- and methyl jasmonate-induced stomatal closure.

Core Functions

Beta-thioglucosidase (myrosinase) of myrosin idioblasts that, redundantly with TGG1, hydrolyzes glucosinolates on tissue damage to initiate formation of isothiocyanates and related defensive products against generalist insect herbivores.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: How does TGG2 contribute to ABA- and MeJA-induced stomatal closure if it is not detectably expressed in guard cells - low guard-cell expression, a non-cell-autonomous effect, or indirect effects of altered glucosinolate levels in the double mutant?

Q: Do TGG1 and TGG2 differ in substrate preference, ascorbate activation or complex formation with myrosinase-associated/binding proteins (e.g. MVP1) in a way that shapes hydrolysis product profiles?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Express and purify recombinant TGG2 and compare its kinetics on aliphatic, indole and benzyl glucosinolates, ascorbate activation and O-beta-glucosidase activity with TGG1.

Experiment: Use cell-type-specific complementation of tgg1 tgg2 (TGG2 under a guard-cell versus a myrosin-idioblast promoter) to test where TGG2 must act to restore stomatal responses and herbivore resistance.
