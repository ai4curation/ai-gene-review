Gene Description

ANTHESIS PROMOTING FACTOR 1 (APRF1/S2LA) is a WD40 scaffold associated with chromatin and RNA-processing machinery. It participates with LD, FLD, SDG26 and EFL proteins in repression of FLOWERING LOCUS C (FLC), and associates with LD and the PP1-family phosphatase TOPP4 in a termination-linked module. Its nuclear function couples RNA 3′ processing and transcription termination to a repressive chromatin state; the direct phosphatase substrate is unresolved. APRF1 also has a cytoplasmic pool and compartment-specific HSP90 associations. Although APRF1 can functionally complement yeast Swd2, native Arabidopsis evidence distinguishes its FLC-repressing role from the COMPASS-associated activating role of the related S2LB protein. APRF1 itself is not established as a histone methylation catalyst.

Core Functions

Chromatin-associated WD40 scaffold supporting FLC repression through the autonomous-pathway and APRF1/LD/TOPP4 termination modules. This is a noncatalytic contribution; the phosphatase substrate and any native COMPASS function are not assumed.
