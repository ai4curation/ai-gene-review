CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of a chromatin regulatory complex involved in
CC       regulating chromatin structure in the nucleus (By similarity). Promotes
CC       flowering under long days (LD) via the regulation of bolting
CC       (PubMed:27968983). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9VLN1,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27968983}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27968983}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in the shoot apical meristem (SAM),
CC       embryos, seedlings, cotyledons, leaves primordia, young leaves and
CC       roots. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27968983}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: During seed development, accumulates in the
CC       endosperm and in the developing embryo. Strongly expressed following
CC       germination throughout seedling growth, especially in aerial part.
CC       Present in cotyledons, including the vascular system, hydathodes and
CC       trichomes, as well as in young leaves and leaves primordia. In roots,
CC       mostly expressed 7 days after sowing. Localized in the central cylinder
CC       of roots and the lower hypocotyl area. Observed in the shoot apical
CC       meristem (SAM) and in the vascular system, particularly in the xylem
CC       parenchyma and phloem. During reproductive development, observed in
CC       flowers of stages 13-15, predominantly in the male reproductive
CC       tissues, mostly in the developing pollen grains, the anther tissues and
CC       the filaments, and, to a lower extent, in the vascular system of sepals
CC       and the gynoecium, and in petals. After fertilization, confined to the
CC       placenta and the abscission zone of the siliques.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27968983}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Altered shoot apical meristem (SAM) and increased
CC       growth rates, as well as prolonged vegetative phase and delayed bolting
CC       resulting in an impairment of flowering under long days (LD). Altered
CC       expression of FLC and SOC1. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:27968983}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the WD repeat SWD2 family. {ECO:0000305}.
