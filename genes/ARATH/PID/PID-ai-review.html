Gene Description

PINOID (PID) is a plant AGCVIII serine/threonine protein kinase that phosphorylates PIN auxin efflux carriers. Its peripheral plasma-membrane association involves its insertion domain and lipid interactions. PID promotes phosphorylation-dependent PIN sorting, including basal-to-apical shifts in particular developmental contexts, and also directly activates PIN-mediated auxin efflux. These functions have different experimental readouts: kinase distribution need not itself be apically enriched, and transport activation is not equivalent to repolarization. PID cooperates with related WAG kinases and MAB4/MEL proteins and is opposed by phosphatases in controlling PIN-dependent auxin distribution, cotyledon and floral development, and root tropic responses.

Core Functions

Plasma-membrane-associated protein serine/threonine kinase that phosphorylates PIN hydrophilic loops and regulates their sorting and polar localization. PID can promote basal-to-apical PIN shifts without itself having a universal apical polarity.
