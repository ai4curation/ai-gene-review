CC   -!- INDUCTION: Induced by drought. Accumulates in response to light, but

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Very low NADH-dependent hydroxypyruvate reductase

CC       photosynthetic performance. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18952776,
