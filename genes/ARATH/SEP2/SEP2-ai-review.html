Gene Description

SEPALLATA2 (SEP2; AGL4; At3g02310; UniProt P29384) is a MIKC-type MADS-box transcription factor of the E-class (SEPALLATA) subfamily, closely related to SEP1. Its MADS domain binds CArG-box regulatory DNA, and its K-box mediates heterodimerization and higher-order complex formation with other floral MADS proteins, including AG (originally isolated as an AG K-domain interactor) and ABS/TT16. SEP2 is expressed in floral meristems and organ primordia. Its function is masked by redundancy: sep2 single mutants are near wild type, but sep1 sep2 sep3 triple mutants convert petals, stamens and carpels into sepals. SEP2 therefore acts as an E-class partner that lets B- and C-class proteins specify organ identity within floral quartet MADS complexes, and SEP dosage also contributes to ovule identity.

Core Functions

SEP2 is a nuclear CArG-box-binding MADS-domain transcription factor. Redundantly with the other SEPALLATA proteins, it supplies the E-class activity that floral quartet complexes need to specify floral organ identity.
