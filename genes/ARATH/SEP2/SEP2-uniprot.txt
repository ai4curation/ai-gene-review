CC   -!- FUNCTION: Probable transcription factor. Functions with SEPALLATA1/AGL2
CC       and SEPALLATA3/AGL9 to ensure proper development of petals, stamens and
CC       carpels and to prevent the indeterminate growth of the flower meristem.
CC       Forms a heterodimer via the K-box domain with AG, that could be
CC       involved in genes regulation during floral meristem development.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterodimer with AGAMOUS capable of binding to CArG-box
CC       sequences (PubMed:9418042). Interacts with TT16/AGL32
CC       (PubMed:16080001). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16080001,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9418042}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed early during flower development.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Triple mutations in the SEP1, SEP2 and SEP3 genes
CC       result in the replacement of the stamens and petals by sepals and of
CC       the carpels by a new mutant flower with sepaloid organs.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9418042}.
