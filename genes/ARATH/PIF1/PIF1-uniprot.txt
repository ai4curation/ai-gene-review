CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription activator. Negatively regulates chlorophyll
CC       biosynthesis and seed germination in the dark, and lightinduced
CC       degradation of PIF1 relieves this negative regulation to promote
CC       photomorphogenesis. Binds to the G-box motif (5'-CACGTG-3') found in
CC       many light-regulated promoters. Promotes the expression of SOM, and
CC       thus modulates responses to abscisic acid (ABA) and gibberellic acid
CC       (GA). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15448264, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16359394,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18487351, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18539749,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18591656}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: DNA-binding ability is inhibited by PCH1 and PCHL
CC       to negatively regulate the expressions of its target genes.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32061894}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (PubMed:25944101). Interacts with the photoactivated
CC       conformer (Pfr) of phytochromes A and B, PHYA and PHYB. Also interacts
CC       with APRR1/TOC1. Binds to RGL2, RGA and FHY3 (via N-terminus).
CC       Associates to PTAC12/HMR/PAP5 which acts as a transcriptional
CC       coactivator (PubMed:25944101). Binds directly to PCH1 and PCHL; this
CC       interaction facilitates its association with phyB and its subsequent
CC       light-induced degradation (PubMed:32061894).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12826627, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15448264,

CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Accumulates progressively in maturating seeds to
CC       reach a peak in dry seeds (PubMed:23708772). Slightly induced upon seed
CC       imbibition (PubMed:23708772). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23708772}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Repressed by red (R) and far red (FR) light treatments in a
CC       phyB- and phyA-dependent manner, and via a PTAC12/HMR/PAP5 regulated
CC       process (PubMed:15448264, PubMed:25944101). Down-regulated by abscisic
CC       acid (ABA) and auxin (IAA), but up-regulated by cold and heat
CC       (PubMed:23708772). Follows a free-running tenous circadian rhythm, with
CC       slightly higher levels during in the light phase (PubMed:23708772).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15448264, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23708772,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25944101}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated at Thr-10, Thr-197, Ser-202, Ser-464, Ser-465, Ser-
CC       466 and Ser-469 by CK2 (PubMed:21330376). Phosphorylated and
CC       ubiquitinated after an exposure to light (especially red and far-red),
CC       in a phytochrome-dependent manner. Modified proteins undergo a
CC       proteasome-dependent degradation. Its stability and degradation plays a
CC       central role in photomorphogenesis of seedlings.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16359394, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18539749,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21330376}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Plants overaccumulate free protochlorophyllide in
