Gene Description

UGT74C1 (At2g31790) is a soluble family 1 UDP-glycosyltransferase of the UGT74 clade that acts as a thiohydroximate S-glucosyltransferase in glucosinolate core structure biosynthesis. It transfers glucose from UDP-glucose to the sulfur of thiohydroximates (produced by the C-S lyase SUR1), forming desulfoglucosinolates that are then sulfated by desulfoglucosinolate sulfotransferases. UGT74C1 is specialized for, and co-expressed with, the methionine-derived (aliphatic) branch, whereas its paralog UGT74B1 is the principal enzyme of the core pathway and particularly of the tryptophan-derived (indole) branch; UGT74C1 functions as an accessory enzyme that can complement the chemotypes and phenotypes of ugt74b1 mutants when expressed and shows thiohydroximate glucosyltransferase activity in planta. Like other family 1 UGTs, it lacks a membrane anchor or targeting peptide and is expected to act in the cytosol.
