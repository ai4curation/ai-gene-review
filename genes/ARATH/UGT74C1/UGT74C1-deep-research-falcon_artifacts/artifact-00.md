|---|---|---|
| Identity | **UDP-glycosyltransferase 74C1**; gene **UGT74C1**; ordered locus **At2g31790**; UniProt **Q9SKC1** | Identifiers match the UniProt target specified for this report; the literature also associates UGT74C1 with *Arabidopsis* glucosinolate biosynthesis (pqac-00000007, pqac-00000010) |
| Organism | *Arabidopsis thaliana* (mouse-ear cress) | The 2024 targeted-proteomics study measured UGT74C1 as one of the enzymes in *A. thaliana* glucosinolate pathways (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000012) |
| Protein family and domains | Plant family-1 UDP-dependent glycosyltransferase, UGT74 subfamily; assigned to phylogenetic group L; contains the UDP-glycosyltransferase/PSPG-type machinery represented by InterPro **IPR002213**, **IPR035595** and Pfam **PF00201** | UGT74C1 and UGT74B1 group with family-1 UGTs associated with specialized-metabolite glycosylation, including thiohydroximate metabolism; phylogeny alone is insufficient to establish exact specificity (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000014) |
| Primary reaction | Transfers glucose from **UDP-glucose** to the sulfur of a glucosinolate-pathway **thiohydroximate**, producing a **desulfoglucosinolate** and UDP; functionally, this is an **S-glucosylation** step | UGT74 enzymes act after thiohydroximate formation and before terminal sulfation (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000004); Vik places the UGT74B1/UGT74C1-catalyzed step immediately after SUR1 (pqac-00000010) |
| Acceptor substrates | Physiological substrate class: glucosinolate-pathway thiohydroximates. Evidence supports a preferential role in intermediates of **aliphatic, chain-elongated-methionine-derived glucosinolates**; a complete quantitative substrate panel or definitive single preferred acceptor is not established in the cited material | Protein abundance responds to loss of aliphatic-glucosinolate regulators MYB28/MYB29 but not to loss of indole-pathway MYB34/MYB51/MYB122, supporting aliphatic-pathway specialization (pqac-00000010). Exact specificity should not be inferred solely from UGT74 phylogeny (pqac-00000013) |
| Products | Corresponding **desulfoglucosinolate(s)**, which are subsequently O-sulfated to mature glucosinolates | Desulfoglucosinolate formation is the penultimate core-assembly stage; sulfotransferases then add sulfate (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000004) |
| Pathway position | Glucosinolate core-structure biosynthesis: amino-acid-derived oxime → activated intermediate/glutathione conjugate → SUR1-generated thiohydroximate → **UGT74C1-dependent S-glucosylation** → desulfoglucosinolate → sulfotransferase-dependent glucosinolate | Vik identifies UGT74B1 or UGT74C1 as catalyzing the step immediately following SUR1 and preceding SOT16/SOT17/SOT18 activity (pqac-00000010) |
| Biological role | Contributes to biosynthesis of sulfur- and nitrogen-containing glucosinolate defense metabolites, particularly the aliphatic class | Glucosinolates are characteristic Brassicales defense compounds whose hydrolysis products deter herbivores and pathogens; UGT74C1 supplies their conserved thioglucose-containing core rather than catalyzing downstream hydrolysis (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000015) |
| Relationship to UGT74B1 | **Partially overlapping paralogs:** either UGT74B1 or UGT74C1 can catalyze the post-SUR1 glucosylation. UGT74B1 behaves more like an enzyme shared between aliphatic and indole pathways, whereas UGT74C1 shows stronger association with the aliphatic pathway | UGT74B1 transcript/protein patterns resemble shared-pathway enzymes; its abundance is unaffected in the aliphatic-regulator mutant but reduced in indole and MYC mutants. UGT74C1 protein instead decreases in the aliphatic **myb28/29** and global **myc2/3/4** mutants, but not in **myb34/51/122** (pqac-00000010) |
| UGT74B1 biochemical benchmark | Recombinant UGT74B1 glucosylates phenylacetothiohydroximic acid to desulfobenzylglucosinolate; reported acceptor **Kₘ ≈ 5.8 µM** and no activity toward IAA, phenylacetic acid or salicylic acid under the tested conditions | These measurements directly characterize UGT74B1, not UGT74C1, and therefore support the shared reaction class but must not be transferred quantitatively to UGT74C1 (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000006) |
| Transcription-factor regulation | Associated with the aliphatic R2R3-MYB module (**MYB28/MYB29**) and the broader jasmonate-responsive bHLH module (**MYC2/MYC3/MYC4**) | UGT74C1 protein was significantly reduced in **myb28/29** and **myc2/3/4**, but not in the indole-pathway **myb34/51/122** mutant (pqac-00000010) |
| Transcript–protein coupling | Regulation cannot be inferred reliably from transcript abundance alone: UGT74C1 transcripts decreased similarly in all three transcription-factor mutant backgrounds, while protein abundance distinguished aliphatic from indole regulator mutants | This divergence indicates post-transcriptional and/or post-translational control of UGT74C1 abundance (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000012) |
| Methyl-jasmonate response | After MeJA treatment, measured glucosinolate-pathway transcripts increased within **1 hour**, but corresponding proteins generally did not increase; UGT74C1 protein was significantly **lower at 25 hours** | The experiment revealed temporal uncoupling among transcripts, proteins and metabolites, arguing against interpreting rapid UGT74C1 mRNA induction as immediate enzyme accumulation (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000012) |
| 2024 technical advance | Standard-flow LC–selected-reaction-monitoring targeted proteomics quantified UGT74C1 among **12 glucosinolate-biosynthetic enzymes** and compared protein with transcript abundance in the same biological samples | The method provided direct pathway-enzyme measurements using triple-quadrupole MS and exposed regulatory behavior missed by transcript profiling alone; the report is a **bioRxiv preprint**, so its conclusions await peer-reviewed confirmation (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000012) |
| Subcellular localization | Best-supported assignment is a **soluble cytosolic enzyme**, but this remains principally a prediction rather than direct UGT74C1 imaging evidence | Arabidopsis UGT sequence analyses found no secretion signal or membrane-targeting motif, supporting cytoplasmic localization for UGT74C1 and related plant UGTs (pqac-00000000) |
| Evidence limitations | The pathway assignment is strong, but the available cited evidence does not provide UGT74C1-specific kinetic constants, a comprehensive purified-enzyme substrate screen, a solved experimental structure or direct fluorescence-based localization | Family-level and paralog evidence should therefore be separated from direct UGT74C1 measurements; the strongest recent direct evidence concerns protein abundance and pathway regulation rather than catalytic kinetics (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000013) |
