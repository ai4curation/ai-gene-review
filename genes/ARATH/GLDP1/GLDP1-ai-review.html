Gene Description

GLDP1 (At4g33010) encodes one of the two Arabidopsis P proteins (glycine dehydrogenase (decarboxylating), EC 1.4.4.2) of the mitochondrial glycine decarboxylase complex (GDC, glycine cleavage system). The PLP-dependent homodimeric enzyme binds glycine through its pyridoxal phosphate cofactor, releases CO2 and transfers the remaining aminomethyl group to the lipoyl arm of the H protein; the T and L proteins then complete the conversion of glycine to 5,10-methylene-tetrahydrofolate, NH3 and NADH. GDC, with serine hydroxymethyltransferase, forms the mitochondrial part of the photorespiratory C2 cycle and also supplies one-carbon units. GLDP1 is the predominant P protein in leaves (about four-fold more than GLDP2 in the leaf complex) and carries the larger share of photorespiratory glycine decarboxylation, but it is functionally redundant with GLDP2: single mutants grow normally, whereas gldp1 gldp2 double mutants arrest as seedlings even at high CO2. GDC activity is inhibited by S-nitrosylation and glutathionylation of GLDP1 cysteines.
