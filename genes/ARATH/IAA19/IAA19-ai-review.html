Gene Description

IAA19 (MASSUGU2/MSG2; At3g15540) is an Arabidopsis Aux/IAA transcriptional repressor that interacts with ARF transcription factors, especially ARF7/NPH4. Its conserved repression region, domain-II degron and C-terminal PB1 interaction region couple auxin-regulated stability to transcriptional control. Dominant domain-II msg2 alleles impair hypocotyl gravitropism and phototropism, apical-hook maintenance, lateral-root formation and stamen-filament growth. ARF7 contributes to auxin induction of IAA19 expression, creating a contextual negative-feedback circuit. IAA19–ARF7 fusion-pair fluorescence is nuclear; biochemical interaction with MYB77 has not been confirmed by the reported BiFC tests.
