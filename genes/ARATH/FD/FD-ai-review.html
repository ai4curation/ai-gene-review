Gene Description

FD (bZIP14; At4g35900) is a group A basic leucine-zipper (bZIP) transcription factor expressed in the shoot apex before and during the floral transition. FD binds G-box (CACGTG) and related ACGT-containing elements in the nucleus and is the DNA-binding partner of the mobile florigen FLOWERING LOCUS T (FT): FT protein produced in leaf phloem companion cells moves to the shoot apical meristem, where it associates with FD through 14-3-3 proteins that recognize FD phosphorylated at its C-terminal SAP motif (Thr282, a target of the calcium-dependent kinases CPK6 and CPK33). The resulting florigen activation complex activates floral integrator and floral meristem identity genes such as SOC1, LEAFY, APETALA1 and SEPALLATA genes, promoting the transition to flowering; in floral meristems FD with its paralog FDP also supports floral homeotic gene expression. FD is also recruited by the antagonistic PEBP protein TERMINAL FLOWER 1, which competes with FT for chromatin-bound FD to repress the same targets, and FD/FDP contribute to abscisic acid responses in seedlings.

Core Functions

Shoot-apex bZIP transcription factor that binds G-box/ACGT elements at floral integrator and floral meristem identity genes (e.g. SOC1, AP1, SEP genes) and, together with florigen FT, activates their transcription to promote the floral transition.
