CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional repressor of CCA1 and LHY, and positive
CC       regulator of LWD1 and LWD2 expression. Controls photoperiodic flowering
CC       response and temperature compensation. Involved in the positive and
CC       negative feedback loops of the circadian clock. Expression of several
CC       members of the ARR-like family is controlled by circadian rhythm.
CC       Regulated at the transcriptional level by a corepressor complex
CC       consisting of ELF4, ELF3, and LUX. APRR9, APRR7, and APRR5 coordinately
CC       act on the upstream region of the target genes to repress their
CC       expression from noon until midnight. The particular coordinated
CC       sequential expression of APRR9, APRR7, APRR5, APRR3 and APPR1 result to
CC       circadian waves that may be at the basis of the endogenous circadian
CC       clock. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11100772, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20233950,
