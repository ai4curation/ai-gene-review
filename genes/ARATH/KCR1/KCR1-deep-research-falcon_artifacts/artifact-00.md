| Property | Evidence-based annotation |
|---|---|
| Target identity | *Arabidopsis thaliana* **KCR1**, locus **At1g67730**; UniProt **Q8L9C4**; encodes very-long-chain 3-oxoacyl-CoA reductase 1 (β-ketoacyl-CoA reductase 1), the functional KCR of the microsomal fatty-acid elongase (FAE) system (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003) |
| Nomenclature caveat | UniProt lists **GL8/GLOSSY8** as an alternative name, but primary literature generally uses **GL8A/GL8B for maize homologs**, not as the principal Arabidopsis locus name. AtKCR1 shares approximately **55–56% amino-acid identity** with maize GL8A/GL8B; therefore, cross-species “GL8” literature should not be treated as direct evidence for AtKCR1 without verification (pqac-00000037, pqac-00000038) |
| Protein size and family features | Predicted **318-amino-acid**, **35.7-kDa** oxidoreductase. Sequence features include a putative dinucleotide-binding site and the conserved **Ser–Tyr–Lys** catalytic residues characteristic of short-chain dehydrogenase/reductase chemistry (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000035) |
| Primary enzymatic function | Catalyzes the first reductive step of each VLCFA-elongation cycle: **3-oxoacyl-CoA + NAD(P)H + H⁺ → 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA + NAD(P)⁺**. This follows KCS-catalyzed condensation and precedes PAS2/HCD-catalyzed dehydration and CER10/ECR-catalyzed reduction (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000001, pqac-00000028) |
| Substrate specificity | Acts on **3-oxoacyl-CoA intermediates** generated during elongation of long-chain acyl-CoAs into VLCFAs. Available evidence supports **broad chain-length acceptance**; the associated KCS isoenzyme—not KCR1—principally determines acyl-chain-length specificity. No rigorous KCR1-specific kinetic preference series has been reported (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000004, pqac-00000015) |
| Cofactor requirement | Reduction requires a nicotinamide cofactor. Reviews commonly describe plant KCR activity as **NAD(P)H-dependent** or NADPH-dependent, whereas KCR1 sequence analysis identified a putative **NADH-binding site**. Direct comparative kinetics establishing strict NADH versus NADPH preference remain unavailable, so **NAD(P)H** is the most defensible designation (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000035) |
| Subcellular localization | **Endoplasmic reticulum/microsomal membrane system**, where plant VLCFA elongation occurs. YFP–AtKCR1 fluorescence colocalized with ER markers in Arabidopsis and transiently transformed tobacco leaves (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000006) |
| ER-retention feature | AtKCR1 contains an apparent **dilysine ER-retention motif**, consistent with its experimentally observed ER localization; no evidence supports mitochondrial localization (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000035) |
| FAE-complex organization | Functions with a chain-length-selective **KCS**, **PAS2/HCD**, and **CER10/ECR** in a four-enzyme ER FAE system. Different KCS isoenzymes are thought to recruit or share the broadly acting KCR1–HCD–ECR core (pqac-00000029, pqac-00000030, pqac-00000034) |
| Protein interactions | Interaction studies summarized for Arabidopsis support a KCR-containing core with **HCD and ECR** and associations with the scaffold/chaperone **PAS1** and accessory **CER2**. Because some assays label the protein generically as “KCR,” these interactions should be regarded as supportive of KCR1-complex organization rather than uniformly proven as KCR1-specific direct binding (pqac-00000027, pqac-00000031) |
| Functional validation | AtKCR1, but not AtKCR2, restores elongase activity in the yeast **ybr159Δ** KCR mutant. AtKCR2 also fails to rescue Arabidopsis kcr1 embryo lethality, supporting KCR1 as the essential functional microsomal isoform (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000036) |
| Expression pattern | Broadly expressed in **roots, leaves, inflorescence stems, flowers, siliques, seedlings, and developing embryos**, with particularly strong expression in flowers and siliques. Unlike KCR2, KCR1 transcript is readily detected in roots and predominates during embryogenesis (pqac-00000022, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000024) |
| Metabolic outputs | Supplies VLCFAs for **cuticular waxes, sphingolipids, suberin-associated lipids, seed triacylglycerols, and root glycerolipids**. It is therefore a shared upstream VLCFA-supply enzyme rather than a wax-specific catalyst (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000012, pqac-00000043) |
| Complete loss-of-function phenotype | Homozygous insertional loss of KCR1 is **embryo lethal**, demonstrating an essential requirement for KCR1-dependent VLCFA synthesis during embryogenesis (pqac-00000019, pqac-00000020) |
| Partial-loss phenotypes | RNAi suppression produces severity-dependent **dwarfism, twisted or fused organs, abnormal flowers and siliques, reduced fertility, seed abortion, abnormal roots and root hairs, defective trichomes, swollen epidermal cells, and dehydration sensitivity** (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) |
| Glossy/cuticle phenotype | Strongly suppressed plants develop glossy inflorescence stems lacking normal epicuticular wax crystals and show increased cuticle permeability. In severe RNAi lines, total stem wax was only **11.4% or 3.9% of wild type**; weaker nonglossy lines retained approximately **75–80%** (pqac-00000018) |
| Lipid-composition phenotype | KCR1 suppression reduces root **C20, C22, and C24** fatty acids, lowers seed-oil VLCFAs by approximately **30%** in severe lines, and decreases many sphingolipid species, linking the developmental phenotype to disruption of several VLCFA-dependent lipid pools (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000018, pqac-00000042) |


*Table: Evidence-based summary of the identity, catalytic role, localization, complex membership, expression, and loss-of-function consequences of Arabidopsis KCR1. It also flags unresolved cofactor specificity and the potentially misleading cross-species GL8 nomenclature.*
