| Pathway or process | KCR1 role and evidence | Biological output or consequence | Recent perspective |
|---|---|---|---|
| ER fatty-acid elongation cycle | KCR1 is the shared 3-oxoacyl-CoA reductase of the ER-localized fatty-acid elongase (FAE) complex. After KCS condenses an acyl-CoA with malonyl-CoA, KCR1 reduces the resulting 3-oxoacyl-CoA to 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA using NAD(P)H; PAS2/HCD then dehydrates the product, and CER10/ECR completes the two-carbon elongation cycle (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000028) | Produces very-long-chain acyl-CoAs used by several membrane, storage, and extracellular-barrier lipid pathways | A 2023 CRISPR-engineered yeast platform reconstituted Arabidopsis FAE complexes containing KCR1, PAS2, CER10, and individual KCS proteins, enabling systematic analysis of KCS-dependent product profiles (pqac-00000040, pqac-00000041) |
| Chain-length specification | KCR1 accepts a broad range of 3-oxoacyl-CoA elongation intermediates. The associated KCS enzyme principally determines substrate and product chain length, whereas KCR1, HCD, and ECR constitute broadly acting shared machinery (pqac-00000004, pqac-00000034) | Permits multiple KCS-defined FAE complexes to generate the diverse VLCFA chain lengths required throughout the plant | Current models emphasize combinatorial FAE organization, possible KCS homo-/heterodimerization, and partner-dependent activity rather than KCR1-driven chain-length selection (pqac-00000040) |
| Cuticular-wax biosynthesis | KCR1 supplies C20–C34 VLCFA precursors that are converted through wax pathways into aldehydes, alkanes, primary and secondary alcohols, ketones, and wax esters (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000015) | Supports epicuticular wax crystals and the hydrophobic aerial barrier. Severe KCR1 knockdown reduced stem wax to 11.4% or 3.9% of wild type and produced glossy stems, cuticle permeability, and dehydration sensitivity (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018) | Recent interactome analysis supports organization of KCR with HCD, ECR, PAS1, and accessory proteins such as CER2, potentially coordinating wax-directed elongase activity (pqac-00000027) |
| Sphingolipid formation | KCR1-dependent elongation supplies VLCFAs—commonly C22–C26—for ceramide and complex sphingolipid synthesis. In Arabidopsis, VLCFAs constitute nearly 80% of sphingolipid fatty-acid chains; KCR1 suppression decreases many measured sphingolipid species (pqac-00000042, pqac-00000045) | Maintains plasma-membrane organization, membrane-domain properties, signaling capacity, and normal development | Current expert interpretation treats KCR1 as a common upstream VLCFA-supply enzyme rather than a sphingolipid-specific catalyst; lipid-class allocation occurs downstream of elongation (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000044) |
| Suberin and cell-wall-associated barriers | KCR1 supplies VLCFA precursors for suberin-associated ω-hydroxy fatty acids, dicarboxylic acids, fatty alcohols, and related compounds. Reduced KCR1 activity lowers longer-chain root fatty acids and causes atypical oxygenated and dicarboxylic products (pqac-00000013, pqac-00000043) | Contributes to hydrophobic root and periderm barriers governing water loss, solute movement, nutrient homeostasis, and environmental protection | VLCFA-derived suberin is induced by drought, waterlogging, and nematode infection, although these stress responses are established primarily for the pathway and are not all directly demonstrated as KCR1-specific regulation (pqac-00000045) |
| Seed triacylglycerol and root glycerolipids | KCR1 supplies elongated acyl chains incorporated into seed storage lipids and root glycerolipids. Strong knockdown reduced seed-oil VLCFAs by approximately 30% and altered root C20, C22, and C24 fatty acids (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000018) | Supports storage-lipid composition, germination-related reserves, and root lipid homeostasis | Reconstituted FAE systems now provide a route to distinguish which KCS–KCR1 combinations generate acyl chains allocated to storage versus structural lipid pools (pqac-00000040, pqac-00000041) |
| Embryogenesis and morphogenesis | Complete KCR1 loss is embryo lethal and cannot be rescued by KCR2, establishing KCR1 as the essential Arabidopsis microsomal KCR. Partial depletion disrupts several VLCFA-dependent lipid classes simultaneously (pqac-00000003, pqac-00000020) | Strong knockdown causes dwarfism, fused or twisted organs, abnormal roots and trichomes, reduced fertility, seed abortion, and severe epidermal defects (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000019) | The prevailing interpretation is that these phenotypes reflect combined defects in membrane sphingolipids, cuticular waxes, suberin, and other VLCFA-containing lipids rather than one isolated downstream pathway (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000014) |
| Biotic-stress signaling | Fatty-acid elongation genes, including KCR, can be induced downstream of MYB30 during the hypersensitive response to avirulent bacteria. VLCFA-derived sphingolipids are proposed to participate in defense-associated membrane signaling and programmed cell death (pqac-00000016) | Potentially links VLCFA production to pathogen-triggered signaling, membrane remodeling, and defense responses | The mechanistic link remains incompletely resolved: evidence is stronger for VLCFA and sphingolipid pathways generally than for a unique signaling function of the KCR1 protein itself (pqac-00000016) |
| Abiotic-stress adaptation | Through wax, suberin, sphingolipid, and phospholipid production, KCR1-dependent VLCFA synthesis contributes indirectly to water-barrier function and membrane homeostasis under drought and other environmental stresses (pqac-00000011, pqac-00000043) | Influences cuticular water loss, root barrier properties, membrane stability, and stress acclimation; strong KCR1 suppression produces dehydration sensitivity (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000045) | Recent research increasingly frames FAE complexes as modular metabolic assemblies whose partner composition may tune VLCFA outputs under developmental or environmental conditions, but direct 2023–2024 evidence for KCR1-specific stress regulation remains limited (pqac-00000027, pqac-00000041) |
| 2024 comparative FAE research | Work on maize showed that KCR homologs can rescue yeast KCR deficiency and that GL2/GL2-LIKE accessory BAHD proteins alter KCS-dependent VLCFA profiles, including production up to C30 in some engineered contexts (pqac-00000039) | Demonstrates the biotechnology potential of manipulating KCR-containing FAE systems to modify VLCFA and wax-precursor production | This is comparative evidence from maize, not direct evidence about Arabidopsis KCR1; it informs general FAE-complex regulation but must not be conflated with AtKCR1-specific function (pqac-00000037, pqac-00000039) |


*Table: Summary of KCR1’s reaction within the ER fatty-acid elongase cycle, its downstream contributions to major VLCFA-containing lipid classes, and associated developmental and stress-related processes. The table distinguishes direct Arabidopsis evidence from recent comparative findings in maize.*
