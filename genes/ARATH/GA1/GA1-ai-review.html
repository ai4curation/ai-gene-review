Gene Description

GA1 (ent-copalyl diphosphate synthase, CPS; At4g02780) is a plastid-targeted class II diterpene cyclase of the terpene synthase family that catalyses the protonation-initiated cyclization of (E,E,E)-geranylgeranyl diphosphate to ent-copalyl diphosphate, the first committed step of gibberellin biosynthesis. Catalysis uses a DXDD motif as general acid in an active site at the interface of the beta and gamma domains; activity is inhibited synergistically by Mg2+ and GGPP, a property suggested to limit flux into the GA pathway when plastid Mg2+ rises during de-etiolation. The enzyme is imported into chloroplasts/plastids, and its promoter is active mainly in provascular tissue of germinating seeds and in growing organs. ga1 null mutants (e.g. ga1-3) are severely GA-deficient, non-germinating, male-sterile dwarfs rescued by exogenous GA.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Is CPS the main flux-controlling step of GA biosynthesis in vivo, and how important is Mg2+/GGPP substrate inhibition relative to transcriptional control of GA1?

Q: How is ent-CPP or a downstream early intermediate moved from provascular cells (where GA1 is expressed) to the cortex/endodermis cells that express late GA biosynthetic genes in germinating seeds?

GA1 (ent-copalyl diphosphate synthase, CPS; At4g02780; UniProt Q38802) - curation notes

  • Accession check: Q38802 = KSA_ARATH, gene names GA1/CPS/CPS1/ABC33, At4g02780. Fetched by accession (just fetch-gene ARATH Q38802 --alias GA1).
  • Falcon deep research was attempted (just deep-research-falcon ARATH GA1) and failed (provider exit code 1); review based on cached primary literature.

Function

  • First committed step of GA biosynthesis, GGPP -> ent-CPP (RHEA:14841, EC 5.5.1.13) PMID:7994182
  • ga1-3 complemented by the cDNA PMID:7994182.
  • Class II cyclase chemistry (DXDD general acid) PMID:20430888; GA-specific CPS have a His switch residue PMID:20430888.
  • Mg2+/GGPP synergistic substrate inhibition PMID:17384166.
  • Structure: active site at beta/gamma domain interface PMID:21602811.

Location

  • Imported and processed in pea chloroplasts PMID:7994182; GFP targeting to chloroplasts PMID:11722763.
  • Promoter active in provasculature of germinating seeds, spatially separate from late steps PMID:11737781.

Curation decisions

  • terpene synthase activity (GO:0010333) and lyase activity (GO:0016829): GO places GO:0010333 under lyase activity; ent-CPP synthase (GO:0009905) is under isomerase/intramolecular lyase. Lyase REMOVED (wrong EC class); terpene synthase MODIFY -> GO:0009905.
  • GA signaling TAS: over-annotation (biosynthesis is upstream of signaling).
  • Mg2+ binding: non-core.
