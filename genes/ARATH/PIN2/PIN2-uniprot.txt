CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as a component of the auxin efflux carrier
CC       (PubMed:20439545). Seems to be involved in the root-specific auxin
CC       transport, and mediates the root gravitropism (PubMed:20439545). Its
CC       particular localization suggests a role in the translocation of auxin
CC       towards the elongation zone (PubMed:20439545). Recruits NPY proteins
CC       (e.g. NPY1/MAB4 and NPY5/MEL1) to the plasma membrane in a polar basal
CC       localization in root epidermis; this activity is optimized by AGC
CC       kinases-mediated (e.g. D6PK and PID) phosphorylation that limits their
CC       lateral diffusion-based escape (PubMed:33705718).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20439545, ECO:0000269|PubMed:33705718}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer (By similarity). Interacts with FYPP1 and FYPP3
CC       (PubMed:22715043). Component of a complex made of PINs (e.g. PIN1 and
CC       PIN2), MAB4/MELs (e.g. NPY1/MAB4 and NPY5/MEL1) and AGC kinases (e.g.
CC       D6PK and PID) at the plasma membrane (PubMed:33705718). Binds directly
CC       to NPY1/MAB4, NPY5/MEL1 and PID (PubMed:33705718).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:Q9C6B8, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22715043,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33705718}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20439545,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:33705718}; Multi-pass membrane protein
CC       {ECO:0000305}. Note=Localized the plasma membrane in a polar apical
CC       localization. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33705718}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Root-specific. Localized to the cortex, epidermis
CC       and lateral root cap, predominantly at the upper side of cells.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Down-regulated by endoplasmic reticulum stress treatment.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24180465}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Loss-of-function mutations impair the root
CC       gravitropic response, lead to an increased sensitivity to ethylene and
CC       auxin transport inhibitors, and give rise to an auxin accumulation in
CC       root tips (PubMed:9679062, PubMed:9844024). Exclusive NPY5/MEL1
CC       cytosolic subcellular localization and in apolar aggregates close to
CC       the plasma membrane in root epidermis and lateral root cap cells
CC       (PubMed:33705718). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:33705718,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:9679062, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9844024}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the auxin efflux carrier (TC 2.A.69.1) family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
