Gene Description

ABCB19 is a full-size ABCB transporter with two nucleotide-binding and two transmembrane domains. At the plasma membrane it supports cellular auxin export and long-distance auxin distribution, including basipetal transport in shoots and acropetal transport through roots. Where ABCB19 and PIN1 overlap, their interaction can stabilize PIN1 and alter transport efficiency; ABCB19 also acts outside those overlapping domains. ABCB19 directly exports bioactive brassinosteroids, supplying extracellular hormone for BR signaling. Purified-protein studies readily demonstrate BR transport but did not detect IAA transport under the same reconstitution conditions, whereas cells expressing ABCB19 with TWD1 show IAA export. Its physiological effects include light-dependent growth, lateral-root elongation, organ-boundary formation, late stamen elongation and shoot branching.
