# MYB76 (HAG2; At5g07700) - manual literature research

No deep-research API was available; this summary was compiled by hand from cached
publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record (Q9SPG5).

## Identity
- R2R3-MYB transcription factor, 338 aa, two HTH Myb-type repeats (residues 9-65, 66-116)
  followed by a disordered C-terminal region (UniProt Q9SPG5).
- Member of R2R3-MYB subgroup 12 with MYB28/HAG1 and MYB29/HAG3 (aliphatic glucosinolate
  regulators) and MYB34/MYB51/MYB122 (indolic glucosinolate regulators).
- PANTHER PTHR10641:SF1405 (TRANSCRIPTION FACTOR MYB29-RELATED).

## Function
- Identified in a transactivation screen with HAG1/MYB28 and HAG3/MYB29 as a regulator of
  aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis; overexpression and repression lines, real-time PCR,
  cotransformation (trans-activation) assays and glucosinolate measurements showed it is a
  positive regulator [PMID:18042203 "Both HAG2/MYB76 and HAG3/MYB29 were shown to be positive
  regulators of aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis."].
- The three HAG factors reciprocally transactivate each other and downregulate indolic
  glucosinolate regulatory genes [PMID:18042203 "HAG1/MYB28, HAG2/MYB76 and HAG3/MYB29
  reciprocally transactivated each other in the control of aliphatic glucosinolate
  biosynthesis and downregulated the expression of genes involved in the control of indolic
  glucosinolate biosynthesis"].
- Sonderby et al. 2010: MYB76 can induce aliphatic GLS independently of MYB28/MYB29 and shapes
  their spatial distribution in the leaf [PMID:20348214 "We unequivocally show that MYB76 is
  not dependent on MYB28 and MYB29 for induction of aliphatic GLSs and that MYB76 plays a role
  in determining the spatial distribution of aliphatic GLSs within the leaf"]. myb76
  knockdowns reduce FMO GSOX1, AOP2, SOT17 and MYB29 transcripts [PMID:20348214 "However, the
  role of MYB76 is not limited to FMO GSOX1 , as it also decreases transcripts of AOP2 ,
  another secondary modification enzyme, the core biosynthetic sulfotransferase SOT17 , as well
  as MYB29 ( Table II )."].
- Li et al. 2013: expressed from its own promoter in myb28 myb29, MYB76 could not activate
  AGSL biosynthesis in leaves but restored short-chain AGSL in seeds; MYB76 expression is
  positively correlated with sulfur [PMID:23792303 "MYB76 was considered to require both MYB28
  and MYB29 for its normal level of expression in leaves, and could not activate AGSL
  biosynthesis on its own."]. This conflicts in part with the 35S overexpression data of
  PMID:20348214 (promoter strength / expression domain likely explains the difference).
- Partners: MYC2/MYC3/MYC4 bHLH factors interact directly with glucosinolate MYBs
  [PMID:23943862 "yeast two-hybrid and pull-down experiments indicated that MYC2/MYC3/MYC4
  interact directly with GS-related MYBs"].
- Promoter binding (yeast one-hybrid): MYB76 was among TFs binding aliphatic GLS pathway
  promoters (TAIR IPI rows with AT3G03190 and AT4G03060) [PMID:25352272 "A systematic yeast
  one-hybrid study was used to identify hundreds of unique potential regulatory interactions
  with a nearly complete complement of 21 promoters for the aliphatic glucosinolate pathway."].
  A further eY1H nitrogen network (PMID:30356219) lists MYB76 binding to AT1G73870 and
  AT2G46830 promoters (abstract only in cache).

## Expression / stimulus response
- Expressed in vegetative and generative organs; around the midvein; induced by mechanical
  stimuli/wounding in inflorescences (UniProt, from PMID:18042203).
- Up-regulated two-fold by drought; down-regulated by aphid feeding [PMID:23144921 "Under
  drought stress, MAM1 and two transcription factors involved in aliphatic glucosinolate
  biosynthesis - MYB29 and MYB76 - were up-regulated two-fold"].
- Transcript detected in trichomes [PMID:23115560].

## Open questions
- Whether MYB76 binds a defined cis-element directly in planta (no ChIP data).
- Mechanism behind its effect on leaf spatial distribution of aliphatic GLS (transport vs
  local synthesis).
