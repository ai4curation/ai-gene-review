CC   -!- FUNCTION: Hydrolyzes L-arginine to urea and L-ornithine; the former is
CC       involved in recycling of nitrogen into ammonium and the latter feeds
CC       back into the urea cycle or serves as a precursor for polyamine and
CC       proline synthesis (PubMed:35752016). Also acts as an agmatinase,
CC       converting agmatine to putrescine (PubMed:28716421). Binds phosphatidic
CC       acid (PubMed:35752016). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:28716421,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35752016}.
