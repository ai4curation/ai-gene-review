Gene Description

ARGAH2 is a manganese-dependent arginase/agmatinase with demonstrated arginine-to-ornithine/urea and agmatine-to-putrescine activities. It supplies most measured arginase activity in the examined germinating seedlings and can participate with arginine decarboxylase in putrescine production. Mitochondrial matrix targeting and a chloroplast pool have been demonstrated in distinct expression systems. Phosphatidic acid binds the enzyme and stimulates arginase activity in vitro; effects on defense and amino-acid pools depend on tissue and treatment.

ARGAH2 annotation review notes

Identity: Q9ZPF5 / At4g08870. Verified fetched PTHR11358 ARGINASE/AGMATINASE and SF32 ARGINASE 2, CHLOROPLASTIC_MITOCHONDRIAL.

PMID:35752016 abstract directly establishes recombinant ARGAH2 arginase activity and PA-dependent enhancement, lower Km with PA and binding of recombinant enzyme to immobilized PA. The curated UniProt reaction explicitly hydrolyzes arginine to ornithine+urea, with PMID-linked kinetics: Km12.98mM in dioleoyl PA versus32.75mM in phosphatidylcholine,37C/pH9.5. This supports module arginine hydrolysis independently of agmatinase evidence. No universal requirement for PA in vivo, or new stress-signaling assertion, follows from the assay.

PMID:28716421 abstract explicitly distinguishes both-paralog yeast complementation from purified AtARGAH2+AtADC1 coupled conversion to agmatine/putrescine. Both substrate-specific activities are core capacities; native fractional flux and proposed dominance during stresses remain open. Full publisher access returned403 and no open primary copy was found, so precise chloroplast stroma IDA remains UNDECIDED while chloroplast localization is supported by its explicit transient Nicotiana assay. Chloroplast and mitochondrial pools come from separate experimental systems; they are not universal simultaneous targeting in every Arabidopsis cell.

PMID:18567826 full normal URL cache: direct C-terminal myc targeting, matrix E1beta marker, selective permeabilization; argah2 native knockout accounts for the major measured germination/seedling activity. Refine mitochondrial location to matrix; no paralog-universal contribution claim. PMID:18385124 abstract and25293756 full proteomics are complementary evidence, not reasons to discard independently supported mitochondria.

PMID:22188168 abstract: ARGAH2 overexpression lowers Botrytis susceptibility; simultaneous silencing of both genes leaves susceptibility similar toWT. Retain fungal defense non-core and specific to that context. Proline-metabolic assignment remains UNDECIDED because accessible text gives amino-acid pool changes, not target-specific proline flux; OAT null results caution against assuming an obligatory OAT-driven proline biosynthetic route. Ornithine metabolism is refined to its actual production; putrescine metabolism to demonstrated synthesis.

Urea cycle retained contextually as ornithine recycling without a ureotelic requirement. Cobalt IMAC and cytosol HDA remain UNDECIDED; broad metal binding refines to conserved manganese cofactor inferred from the directly characterized paralog and curated binding sites.
