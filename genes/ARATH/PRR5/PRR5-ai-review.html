Gene Description

PSEUDO-RESPONSE REGULATOR 5 (PRR5, APRR5) is a nuclear transcriptional repressor of the Arabidopsis circadian oscillator whose expression and protein peak in the afternoon to early evening, after PRR9 and PRR7. It has an N-terminal pseudo-receiver (PR) domain, which lacks the phospho-accepting aspartate, and a C-terminal CCT motif through which it associates with target promoters, which are enriched in G-box-like elements. PRR5 represses CCA1 and LHY, acting with PRR9 and PRR7 and recruiting TOPLESS/TPR corepressors. It also directly represses many clock-output transcription factors that control flowering time (CDFs), hypocotyl elongation (PIF4/PIF5) and cold-stress responses. PRR5 heterodimerizes with TOC1 through their PR domains, promoting TOC1 phosphorylation, nuclear accumulation and recruitment to subnuclear foci. PRR5 protein is rhythmically turned over: its phosphorylated form is recognised through the PR domain by the F-box photoreceptor ZEITLUPE (SCF-ZTL) and degraded by the 26S proteasome, mainly in the dark, and O-fucosylation by SPINDLY also promotes its proteolysis. prr5 mutants have a shortened period, and in combination with prr7/prr9 they lose rhythmicity.

Core Functions

Afternoon/evening-phased transcriptional repressor of CCA1 and LHY. PRR5 binds target promoters through its CCT motif and recruits TOPLESS/TPR corepressors, acting with PRR9 and PRR7 in the negative arm of the CCA1/LHY-PRR feedback loop.

  • TOC1 protein binding: MODIFY to protein heterodimerization activity (GO:0046982), which has a demonstrated functional consequence. SPY binding: REMOVE (PRR5 is the substrate).
  • Response to water deprivation IEP (PMID:41566362, a drought GWAS): UNDECIDED, because the abstract does not mention PRR5.
  • Import into nucleus (acts upstream, on TOC1): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
