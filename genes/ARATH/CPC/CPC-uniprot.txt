CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor. Determines the fate of epidermal cell
CC       differentiation. Represses trichome development by lateral inhibition.
CC       Together with GL3 or BHLH2, promotes the formation of hair developing
CC       cells (H position) in root epidermis, probably by inhibiting non-hair
CC       cell formation. Represses the expression of GL2 and WER in H cells.
CC       Positively regulates stomatal formation in the hypocotyl
CC       (PubMed:19513241). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11910008,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12356720, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16291794,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:19513241, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9262483}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with GL3 and BHLH2. Interacts with SIEL
CC       (PubMed:21924907). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12917293,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14627722, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21924907}.
CC   -!- INTERACTION:
CC       O22059; Q9CAD0: BHLH2; NbExp=3; IntAct=EBI-533386, EBI-533398;
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16291794}. Note=Moves
CC       from developing non-hair cells (atrichoblasts) to developing hair cells
CC       (trichoblasts).
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in trichomes and in young developing
CC       leaves, as well as in root hair and stele cells (pericycle and vascular
CC       tissues). Expressed in epidermal root hairless cells (atrichoblasts)
CC       and moves to root hair cells (trichoblasts) by a cell-to-cell movement
CC       through plasmodesmata (at protein level). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:15795220,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16291794}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in leaves primordia and later confined
CC       to trichomes. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12356720}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Transcriptional repression correlates with reduced histone
CC       acetylation on H3 and H4 mediated by HDA18 in root epidermis N cells
CC       (non-hair developing cells). Induced by WER. Negative autoregulation by
CC       interfering with the binding of WER to its WER-binding sites (WBS)
CC       promoter region, especially in H cells. Down-regulated by GEM. Down-
CC       regulated by TMM (PubMed:19513241). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11910008,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15795220, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16176989,
