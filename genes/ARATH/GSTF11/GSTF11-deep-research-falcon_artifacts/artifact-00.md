| Gene ID | **GSTF11 / AtGSTF11**, ordered locus **At3g03190**, from *Arabidopsis thaliana*; **UniProt Q96324**. Historical literature and database records may also use **GSTF6**, so locus/accession matching is essential to avoid confusion with other GST genes (pqac-00000000, pqac-00000020). |
| Protein classification | Soluble **phi-class glutathione transferase (GSTF)** in the GST superfamily. Arabidopsis has 13 authentic phi-class genes, GSTF2–GSTF14 (pqac-00000038, pqac-00000039). |
| Molecular function / enzymatic activity | Catalyzes or facilitates **glutathione (GSH) conjugation to electrophilic compounds**. In glucosinolate metabolism, GSTF11 is a candidate catalyst for formation of sulfur-containing GS-conjugate intermediates, but its exact endogenous reaction has not been definitively assigned because relevant conjugates can also form spontaneously and GST redundancy is substantial (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000028). |
| Substrate specificity | Recombinant AtGSTF11 was reported to be most active with **benzyl isothiocyanate (BITC)** among substrates tested, supporting an ability to detoxify reactive glucosinolate-breakdown products. A unique physiological substrate and quantitative preference among native aliphatic glucosinolate precursors remain unresolved (pqac-00000011). |
| Subcellular localization | Most Arabidopsis GSTFs examined as GFP fusions were predominantly **cytosolic**, consistent with cytosolic glucosinolate-conjugate processing; weak nuclear fluorescence can result from passive diffusion. However, GSTF11 itself was insoluble or undetectable in one recombinant survey, so its localization should be regarded as a strong class/pathway-based inference rather than an unambiguous GSTF11-specific localization result (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000006, pqac-00000007). |
| Role in metabolic pathways | Implicated chiefly in **aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis**. The proposed step is GSH addition to a CYP83-generated reactive aldoxime derivative, yielding a GS-conjugate/GS-thiohydroximate-related intermediate and supplying reduced sulfur. Cytosolic GGP1/GGP3 subsequently remove the γ-glutamyl group, after which further processing leads toward the glucosinolate core (pqac-00000018, pqac-00000019, pqac-00000024, pqac-00000025). |
| Biological processes | Most defensible role: specialized sulfur metabolism and chemical defense through glucosinolate production and metabolism of reactive isothiocyanates. A direct role in Arabidopsis anthocyanin transport is **not established**; that function belongs more securely to GSTF12/TT19 and related GSTFs (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000013). |
| Stress responses | Heterologous expression in *Brassica napus* was associated with less severe powdery mildew infection, altered glutathione redox status, and responses to cold, but not improved drought tolerance. These findings support biotechnological potential yet do not constitute direct loss-of-function evidence for endogenous Arabidopsis GSTF11 (pqac-00000012, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000033). |
| Expression pattern | Transcriptomic synthesis indicates comparatively stronger expression in **roots** than aerial developmental samples and relatively weak fold-change across broad abiotic-stress datasets. Thus, GSTF11 does not behave as a universally strong stress-inducible GST in Arabidopsis (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009, pqac-00000035). |
| Structural features | Expected to share the canonical soluble GST architecture: an N-terminal thioredoxin-like **G-site** that binds GSH, a predominantly α-helical C-terminal **H-site** that binds hydrophobic/electrophilic substrates, and usually a homodimeric organization. Unlike most GSTFs, AtGSTF11 reportedly lacks the normally conserved N-terminal catalytic serine; no GSTF11-specific experimental three-dimensional structure was identified, so detailed residue assignments from other GSTFs should not be transferred directly (pqac-00000036, pqac-00000047, pqac-00000048, pqac-00000049). |
| Evolutionary classification | GSTFs occur broadly in terrestrial plants and underwent extensive lineage-specific duplication and functional diversification. GSTF copy number varies markedly among plant genomes; Arabidopsis GSTF11 is one of seven phi-class singletons rather than a member of the prominent GSTF4–GSTF7 or GSTF9–GSTF10 tandem clusters (pqac-00000036, pqac-00000038, pqac-00000040). |
| Overall annotation confidence | **High** for identity and phi-GST family membership; **moderate** for cytosolic placement and BITC-conjugating activity; **suggestive rather than definitive** for a unique catalytic role in aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis. A 2024 expert review emphasized that GSTF11 overexpression did not significantly raise glucosinolate production and that spontaneous conjugation, paralog redundancy, and rapid intermediate turnover complicate assignment (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000028). |


*Table: Evidence-weighted summary of AtGSTF11 identity, catalytic activity, localization, pathway involvement, expression, stress biology, structure, and evolution. Qualifications distinguish direct GSTF11 evidence from phi-family inference and heterologous-expression results.*
