Gene Description

KAO2 (ent-kaurenoic acid oxidase, CYP88A4; At2g32440) is an endoplasmic reticulum membrane-anchored cytochrome P450 that catalyses three successive oxidations of ent-kaurenoic acid (ent-7alpha-hydroxylation, then formation of GA12-aldehyde with B-ring contraction, then oxidation to the C-7 carboxylic acid) to produce GA12, the first gibberellin and common precursor of all bioactive GAs. KAO2 and its paralog KAO1 are expressed mainly in germinating seeds and young developing organs and act redundantly: single mutants are wild-type, whereas the kao1 kao2 double mutant is a non-germinating, severely GA-deficient dwarf resembling ga1-3.

Core Functions

ER-membrane cytochrome P450 (CYP88A) that oxidizes ent-kaur-16-en-19-oate in three steps (via ent-7alpha-hydroxykaurenoic acid and GA12-aldehyde) to GA12 (RHEA:33219), the common precursor of all gibberellins; acts redundantly with KAO1.
