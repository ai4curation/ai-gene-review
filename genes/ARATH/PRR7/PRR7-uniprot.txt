CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcriptional repressor of CCA1 and LHY, and positive
CC       regulator of LWD1 and LWD2 expression. Represses the expression of
CC       other clock proteins and master regulators of plant growth, development
CC       and response to abiotic stress. Involved in the positive and negative
CC       feedback loops of the circadian clock. Controls photoperiodic flowering
CC       response and temperature compensation. Expression of several members of
CC       the ARR-like family is controlled by circadian rhythm. APRR9, APRR7,
CC       and APRR5 coordinately act on the upstream region of the target genes
CC       to repress their expression from noon until midnight. The particular
CC       coordinated sequential expression of APRR9, APRR7, APRR5, APRR3 and
CC       APPR1 result to circadian waves that may be at the basis of the
CC       endogenous circadian clock. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11100772,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14563930, ECO:0000269|PubMed:20233950,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:21098730, ECO:0000269|PubMed:21357491,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23027938, ECO:0000269|PubMed:23808423,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24153186}.

CC   -!- INDUCTION: Up-regulated by heat-shock and light at dawn, and down-
CC       regulated by the sugars produced by photosynthesis.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23933882, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24153186}.
CC   -!- PTM: Phosphorylated. Phosphorylation varies throughout the diurnal
CC       cycle. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18562312}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Insensitive to the effect of sucrose on the
CC       circadian period. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24153186}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Regulated at the level of mRNA maturation and
CC       alternative splicing by SKIP (PubMed:22942380). The expression of
CC       APRR9, APRR7, and APRR5 requires the presence of LWD1 and/or LWD2,
CC       indicating the existence of a positive feedback loop within the
CC       circadian clock (PubMed:21357491). {ECO:0000305|PubMed:21357491,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:22942380}.
