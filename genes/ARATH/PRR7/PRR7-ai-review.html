Gene Description

PSEUDO-RESPONSE REGULATOR 7 (PRR7, APRR7) is a nuclear transcriptional repressor of the Arabidopsis circadian oscillator. Its transcript and protein peak from midday into the afternoon, after PRR9 and before PRR5/TOC1. Like other PRRs, it has an N-terminal pseudo-receiver domain, which lacks the phospho-accepting aspartate, and a C-terminal CCT domain that mediates promoter association. PRR7 binds the CCA1 and LHY promoters and represses them together with PRR9 and PRR5, recruiting TOPLESS/TPR corepressors. This forms part of the daytime negative arm of the CCA1/LHY-PRR feedback loop. PRR7 also directly represses many morning-expressed output genes, including master regulators of growth, light signalling and abiotic-stress responses. prr7 mutants have a lengthened period and an altered clock phase, prr5 prr7 double mutants are nearly arrhythmic, and PRR7 is needed for clock entrainment by sugars (its promoter is a target of the sugar-responsive bZIP63) and for red/far-red light responses during seedling de-etiolation.

Core Functions

Midday-phased transcriptional repressor of the core clock genes CCA1 and LHY. PRR7 associates with their promoters through its CCT domain and recruits TOPLESS/TPR corepressors, acting with PRR9 and PRR5 in the daytime negative arm of the CCA1/LHY-PRR feedback loop that sets period and phase.

  • Fetched with just fetch-gene ARATH Q93WK5 --alias PRR7. Falcon deep research failed; review based on cached publications.

Key findings

  • Repressor of CCA1/LHY with PRR9/PRR5 PMID:20233950; acts with TPL/TPR PMID:23267111.
  • Directly represses morning-expressed growth, light and stress regulators PMID:23808423.
  • Nuclear PRR7-GUS; prr7 de-etiolation defect and phase advance PMID:14563930.
  • prr5 prr7 double mutant nearly arrhythmic PMID:15695441.
  • Sugar entrainment: bZIP63 regulates PRR7 transcription PMID:30078562. The TAIR protein-binding row citing this paper has bZIP63 in WITH, but the paper's central result concerns PRR7 promoter regulation.

Decisions

  • Mitochondrion HDA (PMID:14671022, proteome of 416 proteins, about half predicted mitochondrial): MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED, likely co-purification.
  • Phosphorelay and cytokinin IEA: REMOVE. DNA-binding TF activity IBA: MODIFY to GO:0001227.
  • Protein binding: REMOVE.
