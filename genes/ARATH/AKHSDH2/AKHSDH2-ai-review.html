Gene Description

AKHSDH2 is a plastid bifunctional aspartate kinase/homoserine dehydrogenase. Its kinase domain supplies aspartyl phosphate to the shared aspartate-family amino-acid pathway, and its dehydrogenase domain reduces aspartate semialdehyde to homoserine for threonine and methionine synthesis. Threonine and several other amino acids regulate the enzyme. Compared with AKHSDH1, its homoserine dehydrogenase activity is much less inhibited by threonine at concentrations measured in leaf chloroplasts.

AKHSDH2 annotation review notes

PMID12602885 explicitly studies akthr2 on chromosome4 and complements yeast hom6 and hom3. PMID16216875 full text explicitly maps At4g19710 to isoform II and compares it with At1g31230 isoform I; its HSDHII is relatively insensitive at physiological Thr while high concentrations can inhibit. PMID11812230 reports a470-kDa apparent oligomer converting to a320-kDa form with Thr; this is not an obligatory in vivo dimer claim. PMID12435751 specifies the Q443/Q524 regulatory sites on isoform II. PMID8204822 is the older akthr1 cloning paper, not direct new characterization of AKHSDH2; the common aspartate-metabolism claim is independently supported by isoform-II studies. NADPH/NADP assays are established, whereas NAD+ binding is retained as unresolved, not rejected from lack of NADH assay.
