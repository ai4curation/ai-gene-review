> **SOT17 (At1g18590; AtST5c)** is a cytoplasmic, PAPS-dependent desulfoglucosinolate sulfotransferase that catalyzes the terminal step of glucosinolate core biosynthesis. It transfers a sulfate group from 3′-phosphoadenosine 5′-phosphosulfate (PAPS) to a desulfoglucosinolate, generating PAP and the corresponding mature glucosinolate; this sulfation completes the glucosinolate core structure. SOT17 acts alongside the related SOT16 and SOT18 enzymes but is functionally differentiated by its preference for long-chain, methionine-derived desulfoglucosinolates—particularly C6–C8 substrates—while lacking detectable activity toward tryptophan-derived indolic substrates. Available evidence places this soluble enzyme and its reaction in the cytoplasm, although protein-specific microscopy evidence is limited.
