Gene Description

EBF2 (EIN3-BINDING F-BOX PROTEIN 2, At5g25350) is a leucine-rich-repeat F-box protein, paralogous to EBF1, that acts as the substrate-recognition subunit of an SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box) E3 ubiquitin ligase. SCF(EBF2) binds the ethylene-responsive transcription factors EIN3 and EIL1 and directs their ubiquitination and 26S proteasome-dependent degradation, so negatively regulating ethylene signalling. ebf2 mutants are ethylene-hypersensitive and ebf1 ebf2 double mutants show constitutive ethylene responses and severe growth arrest. Unlike EBF1, EBF2 is transcriptionally induced by ethylene through direct binding of EIN3 to its promoter, forming a negative feedback loop; EBF2 acts mainly in the later phase of the ethylene response and in growth recovery after ethylene removal. Ethylene lowers EBF2 levels by promoting EBF2 protein turnover and by EIN2-directed translational repression mediated by the EBF2 mRNA 3' UTR. EBF2 is nuclear.

Core Functions

Substrate-recognition F-box subunit of the SCF(EBF2) E3 ubiquitin ligase that recruits EIN3/EIL1 for ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation; ethylene-induced via EIN3, providing negative feedback and promoting recovery after ethylene removal.
