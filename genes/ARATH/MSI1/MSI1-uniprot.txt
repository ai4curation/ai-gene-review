CC   -!- FUNCTION: Core histone-binding subunit that may target chromatin
CC       assembly factors, chromatin remodeling factors and histone deacetylases
CC       to their histone substrates in a manner that is regulated by
CC       nucleosomal DNA. Component of several complexes which regulate
CC       chromatin metabolism. These include the chromatin assembly factor 1
CC       (CAF-1) complex, which is required for chromatin assembly following DNA
CC       replication and DNA repair, and the fertilization independent seed
CC       (FIS) complex, a polycomb group protein complex which is required to
CC       maintain the transcriptionally repressive state of homeotic genes
CC       throughout development. Required for several aspects of plant
CC       development including normal leaf expansion, correct development of
CC       flowers, normal endosperm development, repression of parthenogenetic
CC       seed development and repression of floral homeotic genes in leaf
CC       tissue. Also required for the recruitment of chromosomal DNA into
CC       heterochromatic chromocenters. Also involved in regulation of imprinted
CC       genes. Acts together with RBR1 to repress the expression of MET1 during
CC       female gametogenesis. This in turn activates expression of the
CC       imprinted genes FIS2 and FWA. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11163246,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12736201, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12970192,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:15151989, ECO:0000269|PubMed:15854908,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16452472, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17021044,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18045841, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700816}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Binds directly to helix 1 of the histone fold of histone H4, a
CC       region that is not accessible when H4 is in chromatin (By similarity).
CC       Component of the chromatin assembly factor 1 (CAF-1) complex, composed
CC       of FAS1, FAS2 and MSI1. Component of the FIS complex which includes at
CC       least MEA, FIE, MIS1 and possibly FIS2. Interacts with EMF1. Component
CC       of the plant homeodomain / polycomb repressive complex 2 (PHD-PRC2)
CC       large complex during prolonged cold, composed of core PRC2 components
CC       (VRN2, EZA1, FIE and MSI1), and three related PHD finger proteins
CC       (VIL1, VIL2 and VIN3) that mediates histone H3 trimethylation on 'Lys-
CC       27' H3K27me3. Interacts with RBR1. Binds to ALP1 (PubMed:26642436).
CC       {ECO:0000250, ECO:0000269|PubMed:11163246, ECO:0000269|PubMed:12970192,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18281509, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18700816,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18854416, ECO:0000269|PubMed:26642436}.
