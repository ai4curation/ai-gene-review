Gene Description

MSI1 (MULTICOPY SUPPRESSOR OF IRA1) is an Arabidopsis WD40-repeat histone-binding protein of the p55/RbAp48 (RBBP4/7) family. It is a shared subunit of several chromatin complexes. MSI1 is present in all plant Polycomb Repressive Complex 2 variants: FIS-PRC2 in the female gametophyte and endosperm, EMF-PRC2 and VRN-PRC2 in the sporophyte, and the cold-formed PHD-PRC2 at FLC. It is required for PRC2-mediated H3K27 trimethylation, repression of seed development before fertilization, and competence to flower after vernalization. MSI1 binds LHP1, linking PRC2 to H3K27me3-marked chromatin in a positive feedback loop. MSI1 is also the p48 subunit of chromatin assembly factor CAF-1 (with FAS1 and FAS2), a component of the RBR1-associated DREAM complex, and a DDB1-binding CUL4 substrate receptor. With RBR1 it represses MET1 during female gametogenesis.

Core Functions

Histone-binding WD40 subunit of all plant PRC2 complexes. It is required for PRC2 H3K27me3 deposition at targets including FLC, and it bridges PRC2 to the H3K27me3-binding protein LHP1.
