Gene Description

GA2 (ent-kaurene synthase, KS; At1g79460) is a plastid-targeted class I diterpene synthase of the terpene synthase family that converts ent-copalyl diphosphate, the product of GA1/CPS, into the tetracyclic hydrocarbon ent-kaur-16-ene with release of diphosphate, the second committed reaction of gibberellin biosynthesis. Catalysis uses Mg2+ bound by DDXXD-type motifs to ionize the diphosphate. The protein is imported into chloroplasts/plastids, where it is a soluble stromal enzyme, and is expressed throughout the plant. ga2 mutants (e.g. ga2-1, carrying a premature stop that removes the C-terminal catalytic region) are gibberellin-deficient dwarfs. A single active-site residue change converts land-plant KS enzymes into pimaradiene synthases, illustrating how specialized diterpene metabolism derives from this enzyme.
