# AOP2 (Q945B5, At4g03060; Cvi functional allele) review notes

## Session 2026-09-30

No falcon deep-research file was present during review.

### Entry identity
- Q945B5 is the functional Cvi allele (432 aa). Columbia AOP2 (Q9ZTA2) has a 5-bp deletion
  and frameshift [PMID:20699011 "which is in agreement with the Col AOP2 gene product
  (AOP2-3) having been reported as non-functional"].

### Function
- 2-ODD converting methylsulfinylalkyl to alkenyl glucosinolates [PMID:11251105 "we show
  that AOP2 catalyzes the conversion of methylsulfinylalkyl glucosinolates to alkenyl
  glucosinolates"].
- E. coli-expressed AOP2: 3-MSP -> allyl, 4-MSB -> 3-butenyl, confirmed by LC-MS
  [PMID:11251105 "This finding shows that AOP2 is capable of catalyzing the GS-ALK reaction
  with both the 3-methylsulfinylalkyl and 4-methylsulfinylalkyl glucosinolates."]
- AOP2 and AOP3 are mutually exclusively expressed across accessions [PMID:11251105 "No
  ecotype coexpressed both genes."]
- In planta: 35S:AOP2 (Pi allele) in Col produces 2-propenyl and 3-butenyl GSLs
  [PMID:20699011 "The over-expression of a functional AOP2 allele was found to successfully
  convert the precursor methylsulfinyl alkyl glucosinolate into the alkenyl form."]
- Light-regulated, highest in leaf/stem [PMID:20699011 "Expression of the AOP2 gene was
  found to be most abundant in leaf and stem tissue and was also found to be light dependent"]
