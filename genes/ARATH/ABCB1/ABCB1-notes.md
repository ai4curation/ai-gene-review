## PR5579 follow-up: prediction scope and BR regulation (2026-10-26)

The live QuickGO definition of GO:1900459 is positive regulation of brassinosteroid-mediated signaling, not homeostasis. The normally cached PMID:38513023 author manuscript (Ghent PDF, Results/Fig. 4 and Fig. S13) reports reduced dephosphorylated BES1 in abcb19 and double mutants and increased readout in ABCB1/ABCB19 overexpression plants. Full PMID:39497419 adds ABCB1 ATP-dependent BL transport and protoplast CSA export. Both paralogs retain three supported GO:1900459 rows as KEEP_AS_NON_CORE; direct BR transport remains a core MF, without a core signaling BP. This is a core-function scope decision, not a claim that upstream ligand supply cannot positively regulate signaling. No native ABCB1 gain-of-function experiment is invented: the source includes overexpression lines and gain-of-function BES1 downstream rescue.

The AtSubP GO_REF:0000122 ISM location prediction is now MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED. It was assessed as a computational compartment assignment against positive target localization and carrier architecture, rather than deferred under the rule for inaccessible experimental papers. Native imaging and the multipass carrier architecture support plasma-membrane transport; the classifier provides no independently established nuclear targeting, nuclear-envelope transport role or cleaved nuclear fragment. Nuclear membranes can contain integral membrane proteins, so topology is not claimed to make every nuclear pool impossible. This revises the earlier unresolved judgment without inventing an exclusion assay or asserting loss of a phylogenetically inherited location. All original row payloads, flags, evidence codes and raw source files are preserved; no new annotation is proposed.
