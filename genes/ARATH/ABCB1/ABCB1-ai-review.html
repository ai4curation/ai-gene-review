Gene Description

ABCB1/PGP1 is an ATP-binding cassette transporter that exports auxin and bioactive brassinosteroids across the Arabidopsis plasma membrane. It contributes to tissue auxin movement and shares developmental functions with ABCB19, including cell elongation, light-dependent growth and stamen and pollen development. TWD1 regulates its membrane localization and auxin transport activity. Purified ABCB1 directly transports brassinolide in proteoliposomes, and structural studies resolve hormone and nucleotide binding. Auxin export is supported by native and heterologous cell assays, including TWD1 coexpression, but was not detected in the same purified proteoliposome system; the molecular basis of this assay dependence remains incompletely resolved.
