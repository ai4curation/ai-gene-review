CC   -!- FUNCTION: Brassinosteroid exporter that, in conjunction with ABCB19,
CC       supports the accumulation of exogenous brassinosteroids (BR) in the
CC       apoplast, thus promoting BR signaling initiation involving the specific
CC       receptor BRI1 and required for plant growth and stress responses
CC       (PubMed:38513023, PubMed:39497419). Auxin efflux transporter that acts
CC       as a negative regulator of light signaling to promote hypocotyl
CC       elongation (PubMed:32855213, PubMed:39497419). May contribute to the
CC       regulation of leaf position and morphology during PHOT1-mediated blue
CC       light responses involving auxin distribution, especially in low light
CC       fluence (PubMed:32855213, PubMed:35528937). Together with ABCB19 and in
CC       a FKBP42/TWD1-dependent manner, supports seed development by promoting
CC       stamen elongation and, to a lesser extent, anther dehiscence and pollen
CC       maturation, probably as auxin transporters (PubMed:35512423,
CC       PubMed:35528937). Mediates the accumulation of chlorophyll and
CC       anthocyanin, as well as the expression of genes in response to light.
CC       Participates directly in auxin efflux and thus regulates the polar
CC       (presumably basipetal) auxin transport (from root tips to root
CC       elongating zone). Also transports some auxin metabolites such as
CC       oxindoleacetic acid and indoleacetaldehyde. Involved in diverse auxin-
CC       mediated responses including gravitropism, phototropism and lateral
CC       root formation. Confers resistance to herbicides such as dicamba,
CC       pendimethalin, oryzalin, and monosodium acid methanearsonate (MSMA),
CC       but not to herbicides such as glyphosate, atrazine, bentazon and
CC       fluazifop-p-butyl. Also mediates resistance to xenobiotics such as
CC       cycloheximide and the cytokinin N6-(2-isopentenyl)adenine (2IP).

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Reduced brassinosteroid export (PubMed:39497419).
CC       Lower petiole angles under red plus far-red light (PubMed:32855213).
CC       The abcb1 abcb19 double mutant is severely dwarfed, with excessive leaf
CC       and petiole curling, reduced internode lengths, increased stem and
CC       silique waving and skewing, and exhibits poor flower fertility due to
CC       short stamen filaments not sufficiently elongated to position the
CC       anthers above the stigma during anthesis; this phenotype is associated
CC       with lower free auxin levels in blue light and reduced length of
CC       epidermal cells in the middle of stamen (PubMed:32855213,
CC       PubMed:35512423, PubMed:35528937). The double mutant abcb1 abcb19 is
CC       also deficient in brassinosteroid export and is insensitive to
CC       brassinolide and its biosynthetic precursor 22-hydroxycampesterol,
CC       resulting in decreased brassinosteroid signaling; this phenotype is
CC       restored by bikinin (BIK) treatment, which can activate brassinosteroid
CC       signaling downstream of the BRI1 receptor (PubMed:38513023,
CC       PubMed:39497419). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32855213,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35512423, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35528937,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:38513023, ECO:0000269|PubMed:39497419}.
