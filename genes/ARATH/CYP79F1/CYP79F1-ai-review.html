Gene Description

CYP79F1 (BUS1/SPS/SUPERSHOOT; dihomomethionine N-hydroxylase) is an endoplasmic reticulum membrane-anchored cytochrome P450 monooxygenase that catalyzes the entry step of core structure formation in methionine-derived (aliphatic) glucosinolate biosynthesis. Using O2 and electrons from NADPH-cytochrome P450 reductase, it performs two successive N-hydroxylations of chain-elongated methionine homologues, followed by dehydration and decarboxylation, to yield the corresponding omega-(methylthio)alkanaldoximes (EC 1.14.14.42). CYP79F1 has a broad chain-length range, converting mono- to hexahomomethionine, but is the main or only enzyme for the short-chain homologues (di- and trihomomethionine) that give rise to the predominant leaf glucosinolates 4-methylthiobutyl and 4-methylsulfinylbutyl glucosinolate; its tandem paralog CYP79F2 is restricted to long-chain substrates. CYP79F1 is expressed in vascular tissue of cotyledons, leaves, stems and siliques and is induced by methyl jasmonate. Null mutants lack short-chain aliphatic glucosinolates and, together with cyp79f2, abolish aliphatic glucosinolate synthesis. The striking bushy/supershoot phenotype of cyp79f1 mutants (loss of apical dominance, shoot overproliferation, altered auxin/cytokinin levels) is an indirect consequence of accumulation of the unconverted methionine-derived substrates, not a direct developmental function.

Core function

  • ER-anchored P450 converting chain-elongated methionines to aldoximes (two
    N-hydroxylations, then dehydration/decarboxylation).
    PMID:11133994
  • Substrate range mono- to hexahomomethionine; short-chain (di-/tri-) preferred (Km ~34-37 uM
    vs ~200 uM for tetra/penta per UniProt from PMID:12609033).
    PMID:12609033
  • Genetics: null mutant lacks short-chain aliphatic GSLs; long-chain GSLs increase (substrates
    undergo more elongation cycles). Double cyp79f1 cyp79f2 abolishes aliphatic GSLs.
    PMID:11226190
    PMID:15194821

Localization

  • ER: N-terminal 62 aa-GFP fusion co-localizes with ER-GFP.
    PMID:11226190
  • Chloroplast (AtSubP ISM) contradicted -> REMOVE. Nucleus (HDA, PMID:28887381 fractionation)
    -> MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED (likely ER/nuclear envelope co-fractionation).

Pleiotropic phenotypes (not core)

  • bushy/supershoot: loss of apical dominance, crinkled leaves, retarded vasculature; linked to
    altered auxin/IAN/indole-GSL or cytokinin levels -> indirect consequence of the metabolic block.
    PMID:11226190
    PMID:15194821
    No development/branching GO terms are proposed.

Regulation

  • Induced by MeJA PMID:12529537; repressed after aphid feeding PMID:23144921 and by
    lepidopteran oral secretions PMID:40546745 -> IEP response to insect kept as non-core.

  • GO:0120526 homomethionine N-monooxygenase activity (IEA, Rhea/EC) -> ACCEPT (core MF)
  • GO:0016709 IDA (PMID:12609033) -> MODIFY to GO:0120526

  • GO:0019761 IBA/IMP -> ACCEPT; ER (IBA/IDA), ER membrane, membrane -> ACCEPT
