In fact, `gstu4` knockout plants accumulated more camalexin, whereas GSTU4 overexpression reduced it, indicating a competing or regulatory role rather than an indispensable catalytic role (pqac-00000021). |

CYP71A27 is a separate, root-biased gene and must not be conflated with the target CYP71A13/At2g30770. |
