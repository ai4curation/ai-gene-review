CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in subepidermal cells of anlagen
CC       regions, then in abaxial part of primordia and finally in
CC       differentiating organs. Levels decrease in differentiated organs. In
CC       embryo the expression starts during the heart stage in the cotyledon
CC       anlagen. Later, expression expands to abaxial domain of the cotyledon
CC       primordia and decrease as the embryo matures. In stamen, expression
CC       restricted to the abaxial region differentiating into the connective.
CC       In gynoecium, expressed in the abaxial cell layers differentiating into
CC       the valves. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10457020}.
