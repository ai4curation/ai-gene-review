CC   -!- FUNCTION: PPIases accelerate the folding of proteins (By similarity).
CC       It catalyzes the cis-trans isomerization of proline imidic peptide
CC       bonds in oligopeptides (By similarity). Modulates the uptake of MRP
CC       substrates into the vacuole; reduces metolachlor-GS (MOC-GS) and
CC       enhances 17-beta-estradiol 17-(beta-D-glucuronide) (E(2)17betaG)
CC       uptake. Regulates cell elongation and orientation. Functions as a
CC       positive regulator of PGP1-mediated auxin transport. Confers drug
CC       modulation of PGP1 efflux activity as interaction with NPA or flavonol
CC       quercetin prevents its physical and functional interaction with PGP1.
CC       Required for the proper localization of auxin-related ABCB transporters
CC       (PubMed:35512423, PubMed:35528937). Plays a role in brassinosteroid
CC       (BR) signaling pathway. Required for seed development by promoting
CC       stamen elongation and, to a lesser extent, anther dehiscence and pollen
CC       maturation, probably as a chaperone helping ABCB1 and ABCB19 auxin
CC       transporters localization and activation (PubMed:35512423,
CC       PubMed:35528937). Involved in auxin signaling in nectaries to promote
CC       starch accumulation to attract visiting pollinators (PubMed:35512423).
CC       {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P20081, ECO:0000269|PubMed:14517332,

CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Plants display helical rotation of several organs
CC       (PubMed:12410806). Reduced number of small and curly flowers with low
CC       petal formation, as well as poor flower fertility due to short stamen
CC       filaments not sufficiently elongated to position the anthers above the
CC       stigma during anthesis; this phenotype is associated with reduced
CC       length of epidermal cells in the middle of stamen (PubMed:35512423,
CC       PubMed:35528937). Loss of auxin signaling in nectaries leading to
CC       reduced starch accumulation (PubMed:35512423). Impaired ABCB1 and
CC       ABCB19 localization at the plasma membrane of endothecium and middle
CC       layer cells of stamen (PubMed:35512423). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12410806,
