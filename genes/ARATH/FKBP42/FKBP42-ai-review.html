Gene Description

FKBP42/TWISTED DWARF1 is a membrane-associated immunophilin-like regulator of selected ABC transporters. It binds ABCB1 and ABCB19, promotes their plasma-membrane localization and auxin transport, and works with HSP90 in transporter maturation and stability. A separate TPR-mediated interaction with vacuolar ABCC transporters modulates conjugate uptake. TWD1 has experimentally demonstrated antiaggregation activity and calmodulin binding and occurs at the plasma membrane, endoplasmic reticulum and tonoplast. Its transport-regulatory functions influence organ and cell elongation, leaf shape, stamen growth and pollen development. Calmodulin-dependent prolyl isomerase activity has recently been reported for its isolated FKBP domain in a preprint; activity of intact native TWD1 and direct isomerization of an ABCB peptide bond remain unresolved.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

protein holdase activity

Definition: Binding to non-native proteins to suppress their aggregation, without necessarily catalyzing productive refolding.

Justification: TWD1 directly suppresses thermal aggregation of citrate synthase. The assay establishes holdase activity and should not be treated as autonomous productive folding.

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:12410806 : Recombinant AtFKBP42 prevents aggregation of citrate synthase in almost equimolar concentrations

Core Functions

Binding and activation of selected ABCB auxin exporters, especially ABCB1, together with maintenance of their functional membrane localization. Direct activation is supported without requiring a proven ABCB peptide-bond isomerization mechanism.
