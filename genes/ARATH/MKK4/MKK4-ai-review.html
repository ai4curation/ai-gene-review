Gene Description

MAP KINASE KINASE 4 (MKK4; At1g51660) is a group C plant MAPK kinase (MAP2K) of 366 aa that, redundantly with its close paralog MKK5, phosphorylates the activation-loop Thr and Tyr of MPK3 and MPK6. MKK4/MKK5 are the shared middle tier of several MAPK modules: downstream of the MAPKKK YODA they repress stomatal lineage entry and spacing (and, downstream of ERECTA, promote localized cell proliferation in inflorescences); downstream of IDA-HAE/HSL2 they control floral organ abscission; and downstream of pattern-recognition receptors (via MAPKKK5/MEKK-type kinases) they activate MPK3/MPK6 for immune gene expression, camalexin and ethylene production. Upstream kinases such as YODA and MAPKKK5 phosphorylate its activation loop, and its own activity is inhibited indirectly by BIN2 acting on YODA. MKK4 is mainly cytoplasmic/nuclear, with a minor chloroplast-stromal pool.
