Gene Description

AHP1 (ARABIDOPSIS HISTIDINE-CONTAINING PHOSPHOTRANSFER PROTEIN 1; ATHP3) is a small (154 aa) histidine-containing phosphotransfer (HPt) protein of Arabidopsis thaliana that acts as the mobile intermediate of the multistep His-Asp phosphorelay. It receives a phosphoryl group on a conserved histidine from the receiver domains of hybrid sensor histidine kinases - the cytokinin receptors AHK2, AHK3 and CRE1/AHK4, and also CKI1 and ETR1 in interaction assays - and transfers it to the receiver aspartate of response regulators, including the nuclear type-B ARR transcription factors and the type-A ARR feedback regulators. AHP1 complements a yeast ypd1 HPt mutant and is distributed between the cytosol and nucleus. Acting redundantly with AHP2, AHP3 and AHP5, it is a positive regulator of cytokinin signaling; higher-order ahp mutants are cytokinin-insensitive and show defects in growth and, through the CKI1 pathway, in female gametophyte development.
