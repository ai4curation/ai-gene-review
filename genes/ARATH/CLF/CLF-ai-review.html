Gene Description

CLF (CURLY LEAF) is one of three Arabidopsis homologs of Drosophila Enhancer of zeste. It is a SET-domain histone H3 lysine 27 methyltransferase and a catalytic subunit of sporophytic Polycomb Repressive Complex 2, in EMF2-PRC2 and VRN2-PRC2 together with FIE and MSI1. CLF acts partly redundantly with SWINGER to deposit H3K27me3 and silence developmental regulators, including the floral homeotic gene AGAMOUS, class I KNOX genes, FT, FLC and senescence-associated genes. Loss of CLF gives curled leaves and early flowering. During vernalization, CLF is required to spread H3K27me3 from the FLC nucleation region over the whole locus after return to warmth, turning metastable silencing into long-term epigenetic memory.

Core Functions

Catalytic E(z)-type subunit of sporophytic PRC2 (with EMF2 or VRN2, FIE and MSI1) that trimethylates H3K27 to silence developmental genes. It spreads H3K27me3 over FLC after vernalization.
