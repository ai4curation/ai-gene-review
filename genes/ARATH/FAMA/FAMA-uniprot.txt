CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription activator (PubMed:17088607, PubMed:17183265,
CC       PubMed:17183267). Together with MYB88 and MYB124, ensures that stomata
CC       contain just two guard cells (GCs) by enforcing a single symmetric
CC       precursor cell division before stomatal maturity (PubMed:24571519).
CC       Together with SPCH and MUTE, regulates the stomata formation. Required
CC       to promote differentiation and morphogenesis of stomatal guard cells
CC       and to halt proliferative divisions in their immediate precursors.
CC       Mediates the formation of stomata (PubMed:17088607, PubMed:17183265,
CC       PubMed:17183267). Prevents histone H3K27me3 marks and derepresses stem
CC       cell gene expression (PubMed:24654956). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17088607,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17183265, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17183267,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:24571519, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24654956}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with FAMA through its LxCxE motif (PubMed:25303364).
CC       Self-interacts. Also interacts with bHLH071 and bHLH093
CC       (PubMed:17088607). Interacts with RBR1 (PubMed:24571519).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17088607, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24571519,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:25303364}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000255|PROSITE-ProRule:PRU00981,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:17088607}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Resctricted to stomatal cell lineages (at protein
CC       level). Expressed in roots, leaves, stems, and flowers.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12679534, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17088607}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Not expressed in meristemoids, but strongly
CC       expressed in guard mother cells (GMCs) and in young guard cells (at
CC       protein level) (PubMed:17088607). Expressed at the transition to
CC       terminal stomatal differentiation, just before and after the symmetric
CC       division of stomatal differentiation, being confined to late-stage GMC
CC       and to young, still differentiating guard cells (PubMed:24571519).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:17088607, ECO:0000269|PubMed:24571519}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Inhibited by low relative humidity (LRH) via epigenetic CG
CC       methylation, thus leading to a reduced stomatal index.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:22442411}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Excess symmetric divisions during stomata
CC       development leading to abnormal guard cells clusters formation. Reduced
CC       seeds set and low biomass. These phenotypes are complemented by MYB124.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24571519}.
CC   -!- MISCELLANEOUS: Plants overexpressing FAMA exhibit ectopic stomata.
