Gene Description

PGLP1 (2-phosphoglycolate phosphatase 1; UniProt name PGLP1A; At5g36700) is the chloroplast-stromal haloacid dehalogenase (HAD)-superfamily phosphatase that hydrolyses 2-phosphoglycolate to glycolate and phosphate (EC 3.1.3.18). 2-Phosphoglycolate is made in large amounts by the oxygenase reaction of Rubisco and inhibits Calvin-Benson cycle enzymes such as triose-phosphate isomerase and sedoheptulose-1,7-bisphosphatase, so PGLP1 catalyses the entry reaction of the photorespiratory C2 cycle that salvages this carbon back to 3-phosphoglycerate. The nuclear-encoded precursor carries an N-terminal chloroplast transit peptide that is removed on import. Knockout of PGLP1, like the classical EMS mutant CS119 (a splice-site allele of the same gene), abolishes almost all leaf phosphoglycolate phosphatase activity and is lethal in normal air but viable in CO2-enriched air. The close paralog PGLP2 (At5g47760) is an active, putatively cytosolic enzyme without a photorespiratory role. PGLP1 activity is inhibited by H2O2-dependent sulfenylation of Cys-86 under heat stress, and altered PGLP1 levels change plant thermotolerance through their effect on 2-phosphoglycolate levels.

Core Functions

Chloroplast-stromal 2-phosphoglycolate phosphatase that hydrolyses the 2-phosphoglycolate produced by Rubisco oxygenation to glycolate and phosphate, catalysing the entry reaction of the photorespiratory C2 cycle and preventing 2-phosphoglycolate from inhibiting Calvin-Benson cycle enzymes.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Are At5g36700 (PGLP1A, P0DKC3) and At5g36790 (PGLP1B, P0DKC4) two real loci or a genome double annotation of one gene? The PGLP1 paper reports a double annotation in this region, and the answer decides whether annotations should be split between two accessions.

Q: Does PGLP1 activity in planta depend on chloride and Mg2+ as reported for the purified spinach and tobacco enzymes, and is the stromal enzyme regulated by ribose-5-phosphate as reported for algal and spinach PGLPs?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Determine the crystal structure of mature Arabidopsis PGLP1 with 2-phosphoglycolate and Cl-/Mg2+ to define the substrate specificity determinants that distinguish it from the broader HAD-IIA phosphatase clade.

Experiment: Express a Cys86Ser PGLP1 variant in the pglp1 background and test growth, 2-phosphoglycolate levels and photosynthesis under heat and fluctuating light, to separate redox regulation of PGLP1 from its catalytic role.

Identity

  • UniProt P0DKC3, "Phosphoglycolate phosphatase 1A, chloroplastic", gene name PGLP1A,
    locus At5g36700; 362 aa precursor, transit peptide 1-54 (proteomics-defined), HAD-like
    hydrolase superfamily, CbbY/CbbZ/Gph/YieH family (UniProt SIMILARITY, InterPro
    IPR006349 PGP_euk, NCBIfam TIGR01452).
  • The literature name is PGLP1. A second UniProt entry, P0DKC4 (PGLP1B, At5g36790),
    carries an identical gene model; the original paper notes a genome double annotation in
    this region PMID:17478634. All functional work cited here is on At5g36700.

Molecular function: 2-phosphoglycolate phosphatase (EC 3.1.3.18)

  • Recombinant PGLP1 (mature form, without the transit peptide) has PGLP activity
    PMID:17478634.
  • Knockout (pglp1-1, SALK_130837) reduces leaf PGLP activity by 97%
    PMID:17478634.
  • UniProt curated function and catalytic activity (ECO:0000269 PubMed:17478634):
    "Photorespiratory enzyme that dephosphorylates the 2-phosphoglycolate produced by the
    RuBisCO oxygenation reaction."; Rhea:14369, 2-phosphoglycolate + H2O = glycolate +
    phosphate.
  • Enzyme properties (from plant/algal enzymes; review) PMID:17478634.
  • Redox regulation: H2O2 sulfenylates PGLP1 at Cys-86 and inhibits it under heat stress
    PMID:38409783.

Biological process: photorespiration (entry step of the C2 cycle)

  • PMID:17478634
  • Conditional lethality in air, rescued by high CO2 PMID:17478634; the classical EMS
    mutant CS119 carries a missplicing mutation in At5g36700 PMID:17478634.
  • Review PMID:22303256 and PMID:22303256.
  • The paralog PGLP2 (At5g47760) is active but non-photorespiratory, putatively cytosolic
    PMID:17478634.
  • Physiological consequence: 2PG that PGLP1 removes inhibits Calvin-Benson enzymes
    PMID:28947491.
    This is a regulatory consequence of the metabolite, not an additional PGLP1 activity; no
    regulatory process term is proposed.
  • Thermotolerance: antisense lines are heat-sensitive and overexpressors more tolerant
    PMID:38409783. This is a downstream
    consequence of 2PG detoxification; I did not propose a "response to heat" annotation.

Location: chloroplast stroma

  • Predicted 62-aa transit peptide PMID:17478634; the transit peptide cleavage after Met-54 and
    N-acetylation of Thr-55 were mapped by proteomics (UniProt FT TRANSIT, ECO:0007744
    PubMed:22223895).
  • Found in the chloroplast proteome PMID:17478634. Peltier et al. 2006 is the stromal oligomeric proteome (PMID:16207701).
  • Mature protein ~34 kDa in vivo after import PMID:38409783.
  • TAIR RCA NOT cytosol (PMID:21166475) is consistent: the cytosolic proteome paper set
    aside plastid-targeted proteins.
