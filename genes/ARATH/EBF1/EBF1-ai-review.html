Gene Description

EBF1 (EIN3-BINDING F-BOX PROTEIN 1, At2g25490) is a leucine-rich-repeat F-box protein that serves as the substrate-recognition subunit of an SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box) E3 ubiquitin ligase. Through its N-terminal F-box it binds the SKP1-like proteins ASK1/ASK2 and thereby CUL1, and through its LRR domain it binds the EIN3/EIL family transcription factors EIN3 and EIL1, directing their ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation. In the absence of ethylene, SCF(EBF1) and its paralog SCF(EBF2) keep EIN3/EIL1 levels low; ebf1 mutants are ethylene-hypersensitive, ebf1 ebf2 double mutants show a constitutive ethylene response and growth arrest, and EBF1 overexpression confers ethylene insensitivity. Ethylene signalling via EIN2 down-regulates EBF1/EBF2, both by promoting proteasomal turnover of the EBF proteins and by EIN2-directed translational repression of EBF1/EBF2 mRNA through their 3' UTRs, so that EIN3/EIL1 accumulate in the nucleus. EBF1 is the predominant regulator during ethylene-free growth and early responses, whereas EIN3-induced EBF2 acts more in feedback and recovery. EBF1 localizes to the nucleus and cytoplasm.

description: EBF1 (EIN3-BINDING F-BOX PROTEIN 1, At2g25490) is a leucine-rich-repeat F-box protein that
  serves as the substrate-recognition subunit of an SCF (SKP1-CUL1-F-box) E3 ubiquitin ligase. Through
  its N-terminal F-box it binds the SKP1-like proteins ASK1/ASK2 and thereby CUL1, and through its LRR
  domain it binds the EIN3/EIL family transcription factors EIN3 and EIL1, directing their ubiquitination
  and proteasomal degradation. In the absence of ethylene, SCF(EBF1) and its paralog SCF(EBF2) keep EIN3/EIL1
  levels low; ebf1 mutants are ethylene-hypersensitive, ebf1 ebf2 double mutants show a constitutive ethylene
  response and growth arrest, and EBF1 overexpression confers ethylene insensitivity. Ethylene signalling
  via EIN2 down-regulates EBF1/EBF2, both by promoting proteasomal turnover of the EBF proteins and by
  EIN2-directed translational repression of EBF1/EBF2 mRNA through their 3' UTRs, so that EIN3/EIL1 accumulate
  in the nucleus. EBF1 is the predominant regulator during ethylene-free growth and early responses, whereas
  EIN3-induced EBF2 acts more in feedback and recovery. EBF1 localizes to the nucleus and cytoplasm.
