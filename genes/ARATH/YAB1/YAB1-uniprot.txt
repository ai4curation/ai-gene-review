CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in the abaxial cell fate determination during
CC       embryogenesis and organogenesis. Regulates the initiation of embryonic
CC       shoot apical meristem (SAM) development (PubMed:10323860,
CC       PubMed:10331982, PubMed:10457020, PubMed:11812777, PubMed:12417699,
CC       PubMed:19837869, PubMed:9878633, Ref.3, Ref.7). Required during flower
CC       formation and development, particularly for the patterning of floral
CC       organs. Positive regulator of class B (AP3 and PI) activity in whorls 2
CC       and 3. Negative regulator of class B activity in whorl 1 and of SUP
CC       activity in whorl 3. Interacts with class A proteins (AP1, AP2 and LUG)
CC       to repress class C (AG) activity in whorls 1 and 2. Contributes to the
CC       repression of KNOX genes (STM, KNAT1/BP and KNAT2) to avoid ectopic
CC       meristems. Binds DNA without sequence specificity. In vitro, can
CC       compete and displace the AP1 protein binding to DNA containing CArG box
CC       (PubMed:10323860, PubMed:10331982, PubMed:10457020, PubMed:11812777,
CC       PubMed:12417699, PubMed:9878633, Ref.3, Ref.7).

CC       Interacts with DNA as monomer. The release of 1 zinc ion from a monomer

CC       adaxial cell identity in leaves as well as embryonic shoot apical
CC       meristem (SAM) initiation and postembryonic SAM maintenance

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in abaxial regions of young lateral
CC       aerial organs, and in primordia leading to cotyledons, leaves, flower
CC       meristems, sepals, petals, stamen and carpels, but not in roots.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10323860, ECO:0000269|PubMed:10457020,

CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in subepidermal cells of anlagen
CC       regions, then in abaxial part of primordia and finally in
CC       differentiating organs. Levels decrease in differentiated organs. In
CC       embryo the expression starts during the transition between globular and
CC       heart stages in the cotyledon anlagen. From the heart stage, expression
CC       expands to abaxial domain of the cotyledon primordia and decrease as
CC       the embryo matures. In stamen, expression restricted to the abaxial
CC       region differentiating into the connective. In gynoecium, expressed in
CC       the abaxial cell layers differentiating into the valves.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10457020}.
