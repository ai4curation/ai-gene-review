Gene Description

At1g14810 encodes the Arabidopsis aspartate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase, a homodimeric enzyme detected in the chloroplast stroma. It uses NADPH to reduce aspartyl phosphate to aspartate semialdehyde, providing the common intermediate for the diaminopimelate lysine branch and for homoserine-derived threonine and methionine synthesis. Purified recombinant enzyme exhibits cooperative dependence on aspartyl phosphate. The experimentally demonstrated NADPH reaction does not establish an equivalent native NADH-dependent route.

At1g14810 annotation review notes

UniProtQ8VYI4 has no official gene-name field, so the ordered locus At1g14810 is used. Its ARBA-only catalytic/pathway/subunit prose is not evidence. Direct primary PMID11812229 reports purified Arabidopsis ASDH, NADPH oxidation (Km92µM), homodimeric36-kDa subunits and cooperative aspartyl-phosphate dependence (K0.5 37µM, not Km). The exact locus attribution is supported by the independently curated NCBI Gene838046 linkage and full Arabidopsis Book PMID22303247, which explicitly links At1g14810 to Paris et al2002. Publisher full text returned403; the accessible primary abstract establishes these measurements but does not expose a NADH comparison. NAD binding therefore remains unresolved, while NADP binding is supported. Protein dimerization is refined to measured homodimerization. Primary18431481 supplementary S1 row73, S2 row299, S3 row41 and S5 row694 directly detect and assign At1g14810.1 to plastid stroma. The review proposes one NEW HDA chloroplast-stroma annotation based on these independently inspected primary protein detections; no new BP is proposed.
