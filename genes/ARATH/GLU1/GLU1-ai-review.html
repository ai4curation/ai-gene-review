Gene Description

GLU1 (At5g04140; Fd-GOGAT1, GLS1) encodes the major ferredoxin-dependent glutamate synthase of Arabidopsis leaves, a large (about 1600 residue) FMN- and iron-sulfur-containing enzyme with an N-terminal chloroplast transit peptide. In the chloroplast stroma it transfers the amide nitrogen of glutamine to 2-oxoglutarate using electrons from reduced ferredoxin, producing two glutamate; together with plastidic glutamine synthetase GS2 it forms the GS/GOGAT cycle. GLU1 is the Fd-GOGAT isoform required for photorespiration: gls/glu1 mutants are chlorotic and accumulate ammonium and glutamine in air but are rescued by elevated CO2, while the paralog GLU2 acts mainly in roots. GLU1 also contributes to primary nitrogen assimilation in leaves, and its expression is highest in leaves and induced by light and sucrose. A small fraction of GLU1 is reported to be targeted to mitochondria, where it interacts with serine hydroxymethyltransferase SHM1 and is required, independently of its catalytic activity, for wild-type photorespiratory SHMT activity. In chloroplasts its abundance is controlled post-transcriptionally through interaction with the ACT-domain protein ACR11.

Core Functions

Ferredoxin-dependent glutamate synthase in the chloroplast stroma: converts glutamine and 2-oxoglutarate to two glutamate using reduced ferredoxin, the GOGAT step of the GS/GOGAT cycle that refixes photorespiratory ammonia and assimilates primary ammonium in leaves.

Identity

  • Ferredoxin-dependent glutamate synthase 1 (Fd-GOGAT1; GLS1), 1622 aa with 105-aa transit
    peptide; glutamate synthase family; FMN and [3Fe-4S] cofactors by similarity (UniProt).
  • Two Fd-GOGAT genes in Arabidopsis; only GLU1 is photorespiratory
    PMID:22303256.

Activity and process

  • Reaction: L-glutamine + 2-oxoglutarate + 2 reduced ferredoxin -> 2 L-glutamate
    (EC 1.4.7.1; UniProt, ECO:0000269 PubMed:19223513).
  • Photorespiratory role from gls mutants PMID:9596633;
    T-DNA knockout confirmation PMID:19223513.
  • Also primary nitrogen assimilation in leaves PMID:9596633;
    glu1 mutant accumulates photorespiratory ammonium and amides PMID:19555410;
    PMID:20307264.
  • Expression: leaf-predominant, induced by light and sucrose PMID:9596633;
    promoter active in mesophyll and phloem PMID:16034598.

Localization

  • Chloroplast stroma (transit peptide; GFP; fractionation; stromal proteomes)
    PMID:19223513.
  • Minor mitochondrial pool PMID:19223513,
    undetectable in FFE-purified mitochondria PMID:19223513
    PMID:22303256.
