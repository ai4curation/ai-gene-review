Gene Description

GA3OX1 (GA4, AtGA3ox1; At1g15550) encodes a gibberellin 3beta-hydroxylase (GA 3-oxidase), a soluble 2-oxoglutarate- and Fe(II)-dependent dioxygenase that catalyses the final step of bioactive gibberellin biosynthesis, converting GA9 to GA4 (preferred substrate) and GA20 to GA1 by regiospecific 3beta-hydroxylation. ga4 mutants are semi-dwarfs that accumulate GA9 and lack 3beta-hydroxylated GAs. With its paralog GA3ox2, GA3OX1 supplies bioactive GA during vegetative growth and seed germination, where both are expressed in the cortex and endodermis of the embryo axis. GA3OX1 transcription is strongly feedback-repressed by bioactive GA (a direct DELLA target; requires the AT-hook protein AGF1) and induced by red light in imbibed seeds via phytochrome-dependent removal of the repressor PIL5.

Core Functions

Cytosolic 2-oxoglutarate/Fe(II)-dependent dioxygenase that 3beta-hydroxylates GA9 to bioactive GA4 (RHEA:68996; preferred substrate) and GA20 to GA1 (RHEA:10104), the final step of bioactive gibberellin formation; with GA3ox2 supplies bioactive GA during vegetative growth and is under DELLA-mediated negative feedback.
