AT5G03810 (Q9FFN0): catalytic competence and subfamily placement

Gene: Arabidopsis thaliana At5g03810, UniProt Q9FFN0, 353 aa, GDSL esterase/lipase family (GELP). Hypothesis slug: kgap-at5g03810-gdsl-site-and-subfamily. Focus type: free text. Source file: genes/ARATH/At5g03810/At5g03810-ai-review.yaml.

Summary

Verdict: partially supported. The seed hypothesis bundles two claims, and they resolve in opposite directions. The catalytic-competence claim is supported. The substrate-clade claim is unresolved, and the evidence shows it cannot be resolved by homology transfer at all.

Mapping the GDSL conserved blocks onto the AlphaFold model AF-Q9FFN0-F1 (v6, global pLDDT 94.0) finds exactly one assembled SGNH active site. Ser37 sits in the block I GDSxxD motif (G35-D36-S37-V38-V39-D40), and Asp328 with His331 sit in the block V DxxH motif. Ser37 OG lies 2.73 Å from His331 NE2, and His331 ND1 lies 2.74 Å from Asp328 OD1, with per-residue pLDDT of 98.4, 98.5 and 98.7. Those distances are at least as short as in four experimentally determined SGNH structures measured by the same detector (E. coli thioesterase I 1IVN at 3.02/2.62 Å, P. aeruginosa ChoE 6UQV at 2.63/3.08 Å, V. vulnificus hemolysin 6JL1 at 2.78/3.20 Å, L. pneumophila lysophospholipase A 8A24 at 2.66/3.14 Å). The oxyanion hole has three nitrogen donors within 6.5 Å of Ser37 OG. The protein also carries a cleavable N-terminal signal peptide and three disulfides that form only in the mature chain, which is what a secreted hydrolase looks like. On those grounds AT5G03810 is predicted catalytically competent as a carboxylic ester hydrolase.

The subfamily half fails for a concrete reason. Global Needleman-Wunsch alignment of Q9FFN0 against all 111 reviewed Arabidopsis GDSL-family entries places it in a five-member clade with At5g03820 (90.4 percent), At5g22810 (61.2 percent), At3g53100 (60.4 percent) and APG/At3g16370 (55.5 percent), with a gap to the next member at 43.1 percent. All five share CATH FunFam 3.40.50.1110:FF:000003. No member of that clade has a demonstrated substrate. The nearest Arabidopsis GELP with a published enzyme assay, SFAR4/At3g48460 (PMID: 28510842), is 26.1 percent identical, outside the clade by a wide margin. Assigning AT5G03810 to the acetylesterase, cutin/suberin or lipid-hydrolysing class would mean transferring substrate preference across a 26 percent identity gap in a family where that preference is not conserved at such distance.

One structural feature complicates the simple reading and is the most interesting result of this work. At the block III oxyanion position, where the GDSL family carries a conserved Asn, this clade carries Gln173. The substitution is present in all five Arabidopsis clade members, in none of the other 22 Arabidopsis GELPs modelled, and in 20 of 20 Brassicaceae homologs in UniRef50_Q9FFN0 spanning 77.9-91.0 percent identity. A crystallographic precedent exists in the Arabidopsis protein At4g34215, where a Gln functionally replaces the block III Asn (PMID: 16301800). The reading supported by the data is a working but structurally distinct oxyanion hole, with substrate preference as the open question.

Key findings

F1. The SGNH catalytic triad is fully assembled

Automated triad detection on AF-Q9FFN0-F1-model_v6 (criteria: Ser OG within 3.6 Å of a His NE2, and that His ND1 within 3.6 Å of an Asp or Glu carboxylate oxygen) returns exactly one triad in the whole chain: Ser37-Asp328-His331. The distances are 2.73 Å (Ser37 OG to His331 NE2) and 2.74 Å (His331 ND1 to Asp328 OD1). Local model confidence at those three positions is high: pLDDT 98.4, 98.5 and 98.7 against a global model mean of 94.0, so the geometry is not being read out of a low-confidence region.

The same detector was run on four experimental SGNH crystal structures as a positive control and reproduced the published triads, including Ser10-Asp154-His157 in E. coli thioesterase I, the triad reported in PMID: 12842470. That validation matters: the method is not tuned to the Arabidopsis model, and the Arabidopsis model scores at the tight end of the experimental range.

Applied across AlphaFold models of 26 other Arabidopsis GELPs, the detector finds an assembled triad in 25 of 26. The single exception is MVP1/GOLD36 (Q7XA74), which is a useful internal negative: the method does distinguish a non-assembled site when one exists. UniProt annotates ACT_SITE 37, 328 and 331 on Q9FFN0 by ECO:0000250 (similarity) only, so this analysis is independent structural support for positions that were previously asserted by transfer.

F2. Gln173 replaces the canonical block III Asn, in the whole clade

Nitrogen hydrogen-bond donors within 6.5 Å of Ser37 OG in AF-Q9FFN0-F1: the Ser37 backbone amide at 2.75 Å (the block I donor), the Ala114 backbone amide at 4.22 Å and the Gly113 backbone amide at 5.27 Å (block II, motif N109-F110-A111-S112-G113), and Gln173 NE2 at 4.84 Å. No Asn side-chain amide lies within 6.5 Å. In the four reference crystal structures the third donor is an Asn side chain (Asn73 ND2 at 5.44 Å in 1IVN, Asn147 ND2 at 4.87 Å in 6UQV, Asn247 ND2 at 3.10 Å in 6JL1).

Across AlphaFold models of 27 Arabidopsis GELPs the third donor is Asn in 19, Gln in 5, and absent in 2 (ESM1 Q9LJG3 and EXL3 Q94CH6), with 1 lacking a triad. All five Gln cases are AT5G03810 and its four closest paralogs: At5g03820 (Gln173 at 4.58 Å), At5g22810 (Gln183 at 4.44 Å), At3g53100 (Gln172 at 5.16 Å) and APG/At3g16370 (Gln173 at 5.02 Å). The next closest family member, At5g42170 (Q9FHW9) at 43.1 percent identity, carries Asn189. The Gln NE2 to Ser OG distances in the clade (4.44-5.16 Å) fall inside the range measured for Asn ND2 in the Asn-containing Arabidopsis GELPs (4.25-4.96 Å), so the donor sits in the same place, one methylene further out along a longer side chain.

The precedent for this arrangement is direct and comes from the same organism. PMID: 16301800 reports for the Arabidopsis At4g34215 structure at 1.6 Å that "the conserved asparagine from block III, which provides a hydrogen bond for an oxyanion hole in the SGNH-hydrolase superfamily enzymes, is missing in At4g34215 and is functionally replaced by Gln30 from block I". A Gln can do the job. The substitution in AT5G03810 is a clade marker, not a lesion specific to this locus, which rules out reading it as evidence of pseudoenzyme status.

F3. The Gln is fixed across Brassicaceae, alongside an invariant triad

UniRef50_Q9FFN0 holds 23 members. Sequences were retrieved for 21 by UniProtKB accession (two UniParc entries have no UniProtKB record), each aligned to Q9FFN0 by global Needleman-Wunsch (BLOSUM62, gap open 11, extend 1), and the residue mapped to query positions 37, 173, 328 and 331 read off.

Identity to Q9FFN0 ranges from 91.0 percent (Arabidopsis lyrata, D7LWU1) to 77.9 percent (Eruca vesicaria, A0ABC8K2G5). Every member is a Brassicaceae species: A. thaliana, A. lyrata, A. halleri, A. suecica, A. thaliana x arenosa, Brassica campestris, B. oleracea, B. carinata, B. cretica, B. napus, Raphanus sativus, Eruca vesicaria. No sequence outside Brassicaceae reaches the UniRef50 threshold against this seed.

A position that tolerated drift would not stay invariant while the surrounding sequence diverges to 78 percent identity. The simplest reading is purifying selection on a working oxyanion hole in which Gln substitutes for Asn. This moves the catalytic-competence call from "geometry looks right in one predicted model" to "geometry looks right and the unusual residue that makes it work is fixed across the ortholog group".

F4. The clade contains no characterised enzyme, so substrate class cannot be transferred

Pairwise identity of Q9FFN0 against all 111 reviewed Arabidopsis GDSL-family UniProt entries, followed by UPGMA clustering of an all-against-all matrix over 42 taxa (the 30 nearest neighbours plus every reviewed Arabidopsis GELP carrying a UniProt FUNCTION annotation), places Q9FFN0 in a tight five-member clade separate from GLIP1/GLIP2, LTL1, the EXL group, ESM1, MVP1/GOLD36, At1g28580 and At4g30140.

Of the 111 reviewed Arabidopsis GDSL entries only 11 carry any UniProt FUNCTION annotation (MVP1/GOLD36, GLIP1, GLIP2, ESM1, FXG1, LTL1, At1g29660, EXL4, At3g26430, GLL23, EXL6). None of them is in the AT5G03810 clade. The clade's best-known member, APG, has UniProt protein existence level 2 (transcript) and no FUNCTION statement; its published description is gametophytic and sporophytic anther expression (PMID: 8401599), with no enzymology.

F5. A cleavable signal peptide and three mature-chain disulfides

Kyte-Doolittle hydropathy (window 9) over the 353 aa sequence gives a single N-terminal peak at 1.95 (residue 12). No internal peak reaches the 1.6 membrane-anchor threshold; the global internal maximum is 1.56 at residue 222. There is no transmembrane segment. The N-terminus has the tripartite architecture of a signal peptide: n-region net charge +1, h-region (residues 6-18) mean hydropathy 1.72, and a c-region with Gly at both the -3 and -1 positions of a cleavage site after Gly24, which satisfies the (-3,-1) rule for signal peptidase I.

The AlphaFold model corroborates the boundary by a route that does not use hydropathy at all. Mean pLDDT is 51.4 over residues 1-24 and 97.1 over residues 25-353, with a sharp transition (44.0 at residue 23, 80.2 at residue 25). The modelled mature chain contains three disulfides at SG-SG distances of 2.02-2.04 Å (Cys223-Cys239, Cys298-Cys318, Cys299-Cys310). The only cysteines left free, Cys10 and Cys16, lie inside the signal peptide itself. Five N-X-S/T sequons sit at Asn100, 255, 256, 260 and 320. There is no C-terminal KDEL/HDEL ER-retention motif.

Three disulfides formed only after signal-peptide removal, zero free cysteines in the mature chain, and five glycosylation sequons is the pattern of a protein that matures in an oxidizing compartment and leaves it. This raises GO:0005576 from prediction-transfer to a structure-corroborated call. It does not separate apoplast from cell wall or vacuole.

Mechanistic model

The chemistry under test is hydrolysis of a carboxylic ester by a Ser-Asp-His charge-relay system, with the tetrahedral intermediate stabilised by a three-donor oxyanion hole. Everything the data supports sits in the left and middle of the chain below. Everything to the right of the dashed links is untested.

The direct gene-product activity is ester hydrolysis. The model supplies the nucleophile, the general base, the acid and three donors converging on the position an oxyanion would occupy. It cannot supply the acyl chain length, the leaving group, or whether water is the only acceptor. PMID: 15697222 makes that limit concrete: in E. coli TAP, chain-length preference is set by a switch loop that moves on substrate binding, and a static apo model has nothing to say about it.

Everything else in the current annotation set is downstream or imported. Lipid biosynthetic process is a pathway-level claim carried over from a bacterial hydrolase. Lipase activity is a substrate-class claim with no measurement behind it for this protein or any clade member. The seed-biased protein abundance is a context: it narrows where the enzyme acts without saying what it acts on. No loss-of-function phenotype exists for At5g03810, so no developmental or physiological outcome can be attributed to it.

Conflicts and alternatives

The APG tissue label does not transfer. APG is described as anther-specific in PMID: 8401599, where "APG transcript is confined to the anther during the period of microspore development". In the Mergner proteome, APG protein is detected in all 30 tissues with a petal maximum, while At5g03810 peaks in mature imbibed seed. Within one clade the maximum spans pollen (EXL4, just outside), petal (APG), hypocotyl (At3g53100) and seed (the target and its two nearest paralogs). The discrepancy for APG itself may be transcript-against-protein or specificity-against-dynamic-range, and either way the clade gives no shared tissue context to build a BP term on.

Gln173 read as degeneration. A curator could take the missing block III Asn as a broken active site. Three observations argue against that: the triad is intact and geometrically tight (F1); the Gln is invariant in 20 of 20 Brassicaceae homologs across 77.9-91.0 percent identity (F3); and a Gln performs the block III role in the Arabidopsis At4g34215 crystal structure (PMID: 16301800). PMID: 33505839 shows the broader point for the CE16 acetylesterases, where the triad Asp is replaced by Asn family-wide and an alternative dyad mechanism is predicted: substitutions in SGNH catalytic machinery are compatible with function. The live alternative is that Gln173 shifts substrate preference relative to Asn-containing GELPs, which is testable and is the most interesting consequence of this work.

Paralog confusion is a real risk for any future claim. At5g03810 and At5g03820 are 90.4 percent identical and adjacent on chromosome 5, and At5g03820 protein is roughly 15x more abundant in imbibed seed. Peptide-level attribution in proteomics and T-DNA insertion specificity in genetics both need checking before a phenotype is assigned to either gene.

The family is functionally promiscuous at this distance. Characterised GELPs in other species act on targets as different from one another as very-long-chain fatty acyl esters in stomatal cuticle (GELP80, PMID: 42191640) and xyloglucan or xylan acetyl groups (AtGELP53, PMID: 40694629). Both are Arabidopsis GELPs. Substrate class is not predictable from family membership, which is the structural reason the 26.1 percent gap to SFAR4 blocks transfer.

Limitations and knowledge gaps

  1. No enzyme assay exists for At5g03810 or any of its four clade members. Checked: PubMed searches for the locus identifiers and the clade, and the full GO annotation set, which contains no EXP, IDA or IMP evidence code. This is the gap that blocks any substrate-specific MF term.
  2. The functional consequence of Gln173 is unmeasured. Checked: structural modelling of 27 Arabidopsis GELPs and conservation across 20 Brassicaceae homologs. It matters because it is the one feature that distinguishes this clade biochemically, and a Gln-for-Asn swap can change acyl chain-length preference.
  3. Every structural statement rests on a predicted model. There is no experimental structure of Q9FFN0, no ligand complex and no kinetics. PMID: 15697222 shows that substrate preference in this fold depends on loop motion that a static apo model does not capture.
  4. Sub-compartment within the secretory pathway is unknown. Checked: signal peptide, absence of a transmembrane segment, absence of KDEL/HDEL. Apoplast, cell wall and vacuole are all compatible. This matters for choosing a CC term more specific than GO:0005576.
  5. No loss-of-function phenotype. The literature search returned nothing for At5g03810. Redundancy with At5g03820 at 90.4 percent identity is the likely reason, which means a higher-order mutant is needed.
  6. The phylogeny is distance-based. The EBI Clustal Omega REST service returned HTTP 400 ("temporarily blocked from submitting new sequence analysis jobs") throughout this run, so clade membership rests on all-against-all pairwise global alignment plus UPGMA, with no branch support values and no maximum-likelihood tree.
  7. Transcript-level tissue profile was not retrieved. The E-MTAB-7978 TPM matrix (24 MB) exceeded the 60 s executor timeout, so only the protein-level atlas was analysed. This affects corroboration of the seed bias, which the proteomics already shows.
  8. OrthoDB could not be queried for this accession. The search API returned HTTP 200 with a null payload, so the cross-species analysis used UniRef50 instead. No result from OrthoDB is reported.

Discriminating tests

Chain-length ladder assay. Express the mature chain (residues 25-353) in Pichia or insect cells, which support disulfide formation and glycosylation, and assay against p-nitrophenyl esters of C2, C4, C8, C12 and C16. This separates acetylesterase from short-chain esterase from true lipase in one experiment and is the single most informative missing measurement. It also decides directly between retaining GO:0016298 and generalizing to GO:0052689.

Q173N substitution. Compare kcat/KM of Q173N against wild type across the same ladder. A shift in the chain-length optimum shows that the clade-defining residue tunes the substrate; no shift shows it is a neutral geometric equivalent of Asn. Include S37A as the catalytic null control for both assays.

Substrate classes the ester ladder misses. Test suberin and cutin monomer esters, and acetylated seed-coat polysaccharides, which the seed and silique bias suggests and which AtGELP53 work (PMID: 40694629) shows are accessible to this family.

Double mutant. Build an At5g03810 At5g03820 double mutant (the two are 90.4 percent identical and adjacent, so single mutants are likely redundant) and score seed coat permeability by tetrazolium uptake plus germination under osmotic stress.

Apoplast fractionation. Fractionate apoplast against total protein from imbibed seed and look for At5g03810-unique peptides, which converts GO:0005576 from prediction to assay and resolves gap 4.

Maximum-likelihood phylogeny. Rerun the clade placement with a proper MSA and ML tree when Clustal Omega or an equivalent is reachable, to attach branch support to the five-member clade.
