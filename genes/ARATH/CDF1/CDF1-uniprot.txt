CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor that binds specifically to a 5'-AA[AG]G-
CC       3' consensus core sequence. A flanking TGT sequence contributes to the
CC       specificity of binding. Regulates a photoperiodic flowering response.
CC       Transcriptional repressor of 'CONSTANS' expression. The DNA-binding
CC       ability is not modulated by 'GIGANTEA' but the stability of CDF1 is
CC       controlled by the proteasome-dependent pathway. Ubiquitinated by the
CC       SCF(ADO3) E3 ubiquitin ligase complex. Binds to the FT promoter in the
CC       morning. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16002617, ECO:0000269|PubMed:19619493,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:22628657}.
