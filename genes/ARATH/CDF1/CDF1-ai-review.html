Gene Description

CYCLING DOF FACTOR 1 (CDF1; DOF5.5; At5g62430) is a nuclear Dof-type zinc-finger transcriptional repressor whose mRNA and protein peak around dawn. CDF1 binds AAAG-containing Dof sites in the CONSTANS (CO) and FLOWERING LOCUS T (FT) promoters in leaf vascular (phloem) tissue and represses their transcription in the morning, in part by recruiting the TOPLESS (TPL) co-repressor through a conserved non-EAR N-terminal motif. CDF1 is a substrate of the blue-light-activated FKF1-GIGANTEA complex: in long afternoons FKF1 and its LKP2/ZTL paralogs bind CDF1 and target it for proteasomal degradation, relieving repression of CO and FT and thereby coupling day length to flowering time. CDF1 acts redundantly with CDF2, CDF3 and CDF5, and the CDF module downstream of GI also influences freezing tolerance and hypocotyl growth.

Core Functions

Morning-phased nuclear Dof transcriptional repressor that binds Dof sites in the CO and FT promoters and represses their transcription, thereby preventing photoperiodic induction of flowering until it is removed by FKF1-GI-mediated degradation in long-day afternoons.
