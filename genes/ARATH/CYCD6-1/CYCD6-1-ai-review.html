Gene Description

CYCLIN D6;1 (CYCD6;1, At4g03270) is a D-type cyclin of Arabidopsis that acts as the regulatory subunit of cyclin-dependent kinases in a small set of formative (asymmetric, periclinal) cell divisions. CYCD6;1 is a direct transcriptional target of the SHORT-ROOT (SHR)-SCARECROW (SCR) complex: its promoter is bound by SHR and SCR and is switched on in the cortex/endodermis initial (CEI) and its daughter at the onset of the division that separates the endodermis and cortex layers, and later in a subset of endodermal cells that form middle cortex and in lateral root primordia. Expression is enhanced by auxin in a SHR/SCR-dependent manner, restricted by RETINOBLASTOMA-RELATED (RBR) and repressed by NAC1-TOPLESS. CYCD6;1 binds and stimulates B-type CDKs (CDKB1;1/CDKB1;2), and the CYCD6;1-CDKB1 kinase phosphorylates RBR, relieving RBR inhibition of SCR and so forming a feed-forward loop that positions the asymmetric division. The protein is found in the nucleus and cytoplasm and is turned over by the proteasome. cycd6;1 mutants show fewer CEI-daughter periclinal divisions and reduced middle-cortex formation without general proliferation defects, and ectopic CYCD6;1 partially restores formative divisions in shr.

Core Functions

Regulatory D-type cyclin that binds and activates B-type CDKs (CDKB1;1/1;2); the CYCD6;1-CDKB1 kinase phosphorylates RBR to drive the SHR-SCR-induced formative periclinal division that separates endodermis and cortex.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Which RBR phosphosites does CYCD6;1-CDKB1 target, and is relief of RBR-SCR binding sufficient to explain the feed-forward positioning of the formative division?

Q: Which other D-type cyclins provide the redundancy implied by the milder cycd6;1 phenotype compared with shr?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Compare formative divisions in cycd6;1 combined with other CYCD mutants, and express a CDKB1-binding-deficient CYCD6;1 in the CEI to test whether kinase activation is required.
