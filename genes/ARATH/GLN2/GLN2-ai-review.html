Gene Description

GLN2 (At5g35630; GS2) encodes the only plastid-type glutamine synthetase of Arabidopsis, a nuclear-encoded, light-induced, leaf-expressed enzyme with an N-terminal chloroplast transit peptide. It catalyses the ATP-dependent amidation of L-glutamate with ammonium to form L-glutamine in the chloroplast stroma and, together with ferredoxin-dependent glutamate synthase (GLU1), forms the GS/GOGAT cycle that assimilates ammonium into glutamate. GS2 refixes the ammonia released by glycine decarboxylation during photorespiration and also assimilates primary ammonium from nitrate reduction. In contrast to barley and Lotus, Arabidopsis gln2 knockouts are viable in air but are smaller, slightly chlorotic and altered in nitrogen metabolism, consistent with partial compensation by cytosolic GS1 isoforms. GLN2 activity in shoots under high ammonium supply causes acidification and contributes to ammonium toxicity. A minor mitochondrial pool of GS2 has been reported from GFP fusion studies but is disputed. Its activity is stimulated in vitro by the ACT-domain protein ACR11.

Identity

    PMID:28339983;
    PMID:22303256.
  • Glutamine synthetase family (PROSITE GLNA_1/GLNA_ATP, Pfam Gln-synt_C); 45-aa transit
    peptide (UniProt TRANSIT 1..45).
  • Leaf-specific, light-induced transcript (lambda Atgsl1) PMID:1684022.

Activity

  • Glutamine synthetase (EC 6.3.1.2): ATP + L-glutamate + NH4+ -> L-glutamine (UniProt,
    ECO:0000269 PubMed:15273293). GS activity measured in leaf mitochondria and attributed to GLN2
    PMID:15273293.
  • Recombinant GS2 activity is increased by the ACT-domain protein ACR11 PMID:28339983.

Localization

  • Chloroplast (stroma): many chloroplast/stromal proteomics datasets (PMID:16207701,
    PMID:18431481, PMID:20061580, PMID:18633119) plus transit peptide and targeting work.
  • Mitochondria (disputed): GLN2::GFP in mitochondria and chloroplasts by microscopy and
    immunoblot PMID:15273293;
    but GS was depleted from FFE-purified mitochondria PMID:19223513
    PMID:22303256.

Physiological roles

  • Photorespiratory ammonia refixation (with Fd-GOGAT GLU1) is the textbook role
    PMID:22303256.
  • But GS2-deficient photorespiratory mutants come from barley (and Lotus), not Arabidopsis
    PMID:22303256.
  • Arabidopsis gln2 T-DNA mutants are viable in air, smaller and slightly chlorotic, with
    impaired nitrogen metabolism PMID:31622939.
    Redundancy with GS1 and GDH is proposed PMID:34400629.
  • Primary nitrogen assimilation: gln2 mutants are impaired even at low photorespiration
    PMID:34400629.
  • GLN2 activity in shoots causes ammonium toxicity via acidification PMID:34400629.
  • Review consensus PMID:35279841
    with the Arabidopsis caveat PMID:35279841.

Salicylic acid binding

  • GS2 was a candidate in two SA-binding screens but failed validation in the same paper
    PMID:25628632;
    PMID:25628632.
    The HDA salicylic acid binding annotation is therefore contradicted by its own source.
