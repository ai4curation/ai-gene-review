Gene Description

BRANCHED1 (BRC1; TCP18, TEOSINTE BRANCHED1-LIKE1) is a class II (CYC/TB1-type) TCP-domain transcription factor expressed mainly in axillary buds, where it promotes bud dormancy and suppresses bud outgrowth; it is the Arabidopsis counterpart of maize TEOSINTE BRANCHED1. BRC1 is nuclear, binds GC-rich TCP-type sites (consensus GGgcCCmc) and directly activates the class I HD-ZIP genes HB21, HB40 and HB53, which in turn promote NCED3 expression and abscisic acid accumulation inside the bud; it also down-regulates cell-cycle and ribosome gene networks. BRC1 transcription integrates branching signals: it is raised by strigolactone (via SL-triggered degradation of the SMXL6/7/8 co-repressors), shade (low R:FR) and apical dominance, and lowered by decapitation and sugar/cytokinin signals. brc1 mutants are highly branched and show reduced responses to strigolactone, shade and auxin-mediated apical dominance, although BRC1 expression sets bud activation potential rather than being strictly necessary or sufficient for bud arrest.

Core Functions

Bud-expressed TCP transcription factor that integrates branching signals (strigolactone, shade, apical dominance) and keeps axillary buds dormant, in part by directly activating HD-ZIP genes HB21/HB40/HB53 that boost ABA synthesis in the bud.

Function

  • Bud-expressed TCP factor that arrests bud development, downstream of MAX pathway PMID:17307924.
  • Nuclear GFP:BRC1 PMID:17307924.
  • Required for shade-induced bud suppression PMID:23524661.
  • Binds GGgcCCmc motif, directly activates HB21/HB40/HB53 -> NCED3/ABA in buds PMID:28028241.
  • SL induces BRC1 via SMXL6/7/8 degradation PMID:26546446; PMID:32528176.
  • BRC1 not strictly necessary/sufficient; sets bud activation potential PMID:28289131.
  • brc1brc2 reduced but significant SL response PMID:30865619.
