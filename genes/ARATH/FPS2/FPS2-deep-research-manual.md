# FPS2 (At4g17190, Q43315) - manual literature research

Deep-research API providers were not available in this environment; this summary was
compiled manually from cached publications and the UniProt record.

## Identity
- FPS2, farnesyl diphosphate synthase 2, At4g17190, 342 aa, FPP/GGPP synthase family
  (UniProt Q43315: "Belongs to the FPP/GGPP synthase family.").
- Paralog FPS1 (At5g47770, Q09152) shares ~90% identity; FPS1 has a mitochondrial long
  isoform, FPS2 does not.

## Activity
- Cloned by RT-PCR; rescues the lethal erg20 yeast mutant and catalyses both condensations
  [PMID:8631820 "Arabidopsis FPS2 was able to rescue the lethal phenotype of an ERG20-disrupted yeast strain"]
  [PMID:8631820 "We demonstrate that FPS2 catalyzes the two successive condensations of IPP with both DMAPP and geranyl diphosphate leading to FPP."]
- Recombinant FPS2 kinetics [PMID:23145086 "Both enzymes displayed typical Michaelis-Menten behaviour, as observed for other FPSs, and exhibited similar K m values in the µmolar range (from 8.3 to 31.5 µM) for both IPP and the allylic substrates DMAPP and GPP."]
  and FPS2 has higher kcat than FPS1S [PMID:23145086 "FPS2 showed catalytic rate constant (kcat) values for IPP, DMAPP, and GPP that were 3.2-, 2.7- and 2.7-fold higher, respectively, than FPS1S"].
- FPS2 is more NaCl-sensitive and more thermostable than FPS1S [PMID:23145086 "At all NaCl concentrations tested, FPS2 was markedly more sensitive to the inhibitory effect of NaCl than FPS1S."].

## Location
- GFP fusions in N. benthamiana: cytosolic, not peroxisomal
  [PMID:23145086 "Under our experimental conditions all the four fusion proteins localized exclusively in the cytosol (Figure 5)."].

## Physiology
- fps1 fps2 double knockout embryo lethal; single mutants largely normal; FPS2 is the major
  isozyme in seeds; fps2 seeds have reduced sitosterol and mevastatin hypersensitivity
  [PMID:20497375 "FPS2 appears to have a major role in seeds and during the early stages of seedling development."]
  [PMID:20497375 "Lack of FPS2 activity in seeds, but not of FPS1 activity, is associated with a marked reduction in sitosterol content"].
- FPS1S expressed from the FPS2 promoter rescues fps2 seed phenotypes
  [PMID:23145086 "This finding demonstrated that under normal conditions FPS1S and FPS2 are completely functionally interchangeable"].
- [PMID:23145086 "In fact, FPS2 is by far the major contributor to total FPS activity in mature seeds, though shortly after germination FPS1 replaces FPS2 as the major provider of FPS activity."]
