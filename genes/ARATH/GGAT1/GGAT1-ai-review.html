Gene Description

GGAT1 (glutamate:glyoxylate aminotransferase 1; also AOAT1/GGT1; At1g23310) is a peroxisomal, pyridoxal 5'-phosphate-dependent class-I aminotransferase of the alanine aminotransferase subfamily. It is the major leaf glutamate:glyoxylate aminotransferase of the photorespiratory C2 cycle, transaminating glyoxylate (produced by glycolate oxidase) to glycine with glutamate as amino donor and releasing 2-oxoglutarate. The recombinant enzyme also catalyses alanine:glyoxylate, alanine:2-oxoglutarate and glutamate:pyruvate transaminations, but not serine:glyoxylate or aspartate transamination. GGAT1 carries a C-terminal type 1 peroxisomal targeting signal, and its activity is confined to peroxisomes. Knockout plants lose most leaf GGAT activity and show a mild, high-CO2-rescuable photorespiratory growth defect with altered glutamate and serine levels; the closely related GGAT2 (At1g70580) supplies residual activity. GGAT1 is also expressed in roots, and overexpression raises leaf serine, glycine and citrulline, indicating a wider role in amino acid metabolism.

Identity and family

  • GGAT1 (synonyms AOAT1, GGT1, AlaAT3-like) is one of four alanine aminotransferase (AlaAT)-like
    class I PLP-dependent aminotransferases in Arabidopsis; GGAT1 and GGAT2 (At1g70580) carry a C-terminal
    PTS1 PMID:12529529.
  • UniProt: "Belongs to the class-I pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent aminotransferase family. Alanine
    aminotransferase subfamily." PANTHER PTHR11751:SF472.

Activities (substrate breadth)

  • Recombinant GGAT1 and GGAT2 catalyse four transaminations PMID:12529529.
  • No serine:glyoxylate or aspartate aminotransferase activity PMID:12529529.
  • Kinetics (UniProt, from PMID:12529529): Km 0.27 mM glyoxylate, 3 mM glutamate, 7.16 mM alanine,
    0.27 mM 2-oxoglutarate, 0.33 mM pyruvate.
  • The AGT (Ala:glyoxylate) reaction was detected but not characterized kinetically, and its
    physiological significance is disputed PMID:12529529.

Judgement: the glutamate:glyoxylate reaction (GO:0047958, glycine:2-oxoglutarate transaminase,
written in the reverse direction) is the core photorespiratory activity. Ala:2-oxoglutarate (GO:0004021)
and Ala:glyoxylate (GO:0008453) are genuine but non-core.

Physiological role

  • The aoat1-1 (ggat1) knockout has drastically reduced leaf GGAT activity and a conditional,
    high-CO2-rescued growth phenotype [PMID:12631323 "The activity of GGAT and alanine:glyoxylate
    aminotransferase (AGAT) in the above-ground tissues of aoat1-1 was reduced drastically"; "These
    phenotypes resembled those of photorespiration-deficient mutants."].
  • Phenotype is weak because GGAT2 provides residual activity PMID:22303256.
  • Overexpression raises Ser, Gly and citrulline in light, repressed by high CO2 PMID:16950862.
  • Expression in roots suggests non-photorespiratory roles PMID:16950862.
  • ggt1 mutants were recovered in an ABA/RD29A:LUC screen; altered H2O2, ABA and proline
    PMID:17318317. Treated as an indirect consequence
    of photorespiratory impairment.
