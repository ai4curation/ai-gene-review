CC   -!- FUNCTION: LRR-rich receptor-like kinase (LRR-RLK) that, similarly to
CC       CLV1 and BAM3, participates in the detection of the CLAVATA-family
CC       peptides (e.g. CLE25, CLE26 and CLE45), and to the regulation of the
CC       triggered signaling required for protophloem differentiation and
CC       meristems maintenance through the phosphorylation of CIK proteins
CC       (CIKs) and of PBL34, PBL35 and PBL36 (PubMed:29581511, PubMed:34651321,
CC       PubMed:34935965). Necessary for male gametophyte development, as well
CC       as ovule specification and function. Involved in cell-cell
CC       communication process required during early anther development, and
CC       regulating cell division and differentiation to organize cell layers.
CC       Required for the development of high-ordered vascular strands within
CC       the leaf and a correlated control of leaf shape, size and symmetry.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16367950, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16751349,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18780746, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29581511,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34651321, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34935965}.

CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in seedlings, roots, leaves,
CC       inflorescences, flowers and siliques (PubMed:16367950). Expressed
CC       broadly in the proximal root meristem, including the protophloem
CC       (PubMed:34651321). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16367950,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34651321}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed in a ring surrounding the center of
CC       meristems extended in the cortex of developing stems and older
CC       pedicels. Present in all developing floral organs, especially in
CC       anthers and gynoecium. Observed in anthers at stage 2 in the
CC       archesporial cells. At stage 3, localized in the primary sporogenous
CC       and primary parietal cells. Subsequently preferentially expressed in
CC       the sporogenous cells at anther stage 4. Later restricted to the
CC       tapetum and pollen mother cells (PMCs) before disappearing
CC       progressively. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16367950,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16751349}.
CC   -!- PTM: Autophosphorylated. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:34651321,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34935965}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Rescues partially CLV3 disruption. When
CC       associated with BAM2 disruption, abnormal anthers at a very early stage
CC       and later lack of endothecium, middle and tapetum layers. Loss of stem
CC       cells at the shoot and flower meristems. Impaired CIK1 phosphorylation
CC       upon CLV3 treatment in the triple mutant clv1 bam1 bam2
CC       (PubMed:29581511). The triple mutant clv1 bam1 bam3 is insensitive to
CC       CLV3-like (e.g. CLE25, CLE26 and CLE45) peptides-triggered protophloem
CC       differentiation and proximal root meristem consumption, thus leading to
CC       the formation of premature protophloems (PubMed:34651321).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16367950, ECO:0000269|PubMed:16751349,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:18780746, ECO:0000269|PubMed:29581511,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34651321}.
