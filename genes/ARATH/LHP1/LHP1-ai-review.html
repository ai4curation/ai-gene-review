Gene Description

LHP1 (LIKE HETEROCHROMATIN PROTEIN 1; also TERMINAL FLOWER 2, TFL2) is the single Arabidopsis homolog of the HP1 chromodomain family. It carries an N-terminal chromodomain and a C-terminal chromo shadow domain, homodimerizes, and is a nuclear protein that associates almost exclusively with histone H3 lysine 27 trimethylation (H3K27me3)-marked genes in euchromatin. Its chromodomain binds H3K27me3, and LHP1 acts as a subunit of plant PRC1-like Polycomb complexes (with RING1A/B) while also interacting with PRC2-associated proteins such as MSI1, thereby helping to maintain and propagate H3K27me3-dependent epigenetic repression. Targets include floral regulators (FT, AG, AP3, PI, SEP3), KNOX genes and FLC; lhp1/tfl2 mutants flower early, form terminal flowers, are dwarfed, and fail to maintain vernalization-induced silencing of FLC after return to warm temperatures.
