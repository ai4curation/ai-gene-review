Gene Description

VRN5 (VERNALIZATION 5; also VIN3-LIKE 1, VIL1) is a constitutively expressed nuclear VEL-family protein of Arabidopsis with an atypical PHD superdomain, a fibronectin III domain and a C-terminal VEL polymerization domain. It is an accessory subunit of plant Polycomb Repressive Complex 2. VRN5 binds the PRC2 core (via VRN2) through its compact PHD-superdomain/FNIII module and heterodimerizes with the cold-induced paralog VIN3 through the VEL domain, so it bridges VIN3 to PRC2. During prolonged cold, VRN5 associates, in a VIN3-dependent way, with a small nucleation region in FLC intron 1, where the resulting PHD-PRC2 complex deposits H3K27me3. After return to warmth, VRN5 spreads across the FLC locus as H3K27me3 increases, stably silencing FLC. VRN5 also contributes to Polycomb silencing of the related floral repressor FLM, which promotes flowering in short days.

Core Functions

PRC2 accessory adaptor that binds the PRC2 core (VRN2) and heterodimerizes with VIN3 via the VEL domain, recruiting the cold-induced VIN3 into a PHD-PRC2 complex that nucleates and then spreads H3K27me3 at FLC during and after vernalization. VRN5 is an accessory subunit of the PHD-PRC2, not a core ESC/E(Z) subunit, so its complex is recorded as chromatin silencing complex (as in its IDA and IBA annotations); the catalytic core subunits (VRN2, SWN, CLF, FIE, MSI1) carry ESC/E(Z) complex.
