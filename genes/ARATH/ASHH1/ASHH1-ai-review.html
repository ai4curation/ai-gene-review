Gene Description

ASHH1 (SDG26) is a nuclear SET2-family chromatin protein with separable histone-methylation and DNA-binding functions. Recombinant SDG26 methylates oligonucleosomal histones, and conserved SET2-family evidence supports H3K36 methyltransferase activity. At SOC1, SDG26 occupancy and SDG26-dependent H3K4/H3K36 trimethylation have been reported, although chromatin dependence does not alone identify the directly methylated lysine. In the autonomous-pathway complex with FLD, LD, APRF1 and EFL proteins, its C-terminal coiled-coil region binds double-stranded DNA and supports association with FLC chromatin and transcriptional repression. A conserved SET-domain mutant rescues the tested FLC/flowering phenotype, supporting a noncatalytic role for SDG26 in that particular complex. Additional phenotypes implicate SDG26 in UV-B DNA-damage responses.
