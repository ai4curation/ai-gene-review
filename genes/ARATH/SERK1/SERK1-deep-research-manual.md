# SERK1 (At1g71830, UniProt Q94AG2) - manual literature synthesis

No deep-research API was available for this review. This file was written by hand from
the cached publications in `publications/` and the UniProt record. Quotes are verbatim.

## Identity and architecture

- Single-pass type I LRR receptor-like kinase of the five-member SERK family (LRR-RLK II):
  signal peptide 1-26, extracellular LRR domain (27-238), TM helix 239-259, cytoplasmic
  Ser/Thr kinase domain 302-589 (UniProt Q94AG2 features).
- "The SERK1 and SERK2 genes show a complex expression pattern throughout development"
  [PMID:16284305].

## Kinase activity

- "The AtSERK1-glutathione S-transferase fusion protein mainly autophosphorylates on threonine
  residues" with Km for ATP of 4 uM, Mg2+ dependent; "A K330E substitution in the kinase domain of
  AtSERK1 abolishes all kinase activity"; "AtSERK1 phosphorylation on myelin basic protein and
  casein showed tyrosine, serine, and threonine as targets, demonstrating that AtSERK1 is a dual
  specificity kinase" [PMID:11509554].
- "In vitro, the AtSERK1 kinase domain is able to transphosphorylate and bind both AtCDC48 and
  GF14lambda" [PMID:15592873]; "SERK1 trans-phosphorylates CDC48A on Ser-41" [PMID:17693538].
- SERK1 and EMS1 kinase domains transphosphorylate each other [PMID:27920157]; "the HAESA kinase
  domain can transphosphorylate SERK1 and vice versa" [PMID:27058169].

## Localisation

- "the AtSERK1-YFP fusion protein is targeted to plasma membranes in both plant and animal cells"
  [PMID:11397085]; in planta YFP fusion "predominantly at the cell periphery, most likely the plasma
  membrane" with BFA-sensitive internalisation [PMID:16231101]; co-localisation with CDC48A "in the
  endoplasmatic reticulum (ER) and at the plasma membrane (PM)" [PMID:16621602].
- Detected in a purified plasma membrane proteome [PMID:17644812].

## Co-receptor roles

- Brassinosteroid signalling: SERK1 co-immunoprecipitates BRI1 and BAK1; "SERK1 and SERK3 are part of
  BRI1-containing multiple protein complexes" and serk1-1 enhances bri1-119 [PMID:16473966].
  Crystal structure: "a direct and steroid-dependent interaction between the BRI1 and SERK1 LRR
  domains" [PMID:23929946]. "The triple knock-out mutant serk1-8 bak1-4 bkk1-1 shows a de-etiolated
  phenotype almost identical to that of the BRI1 null mutant, bri1-701"; "SERK1, BAK1, and BKK1 are
  the major players for BR signaling at the Arabidopsis seedling stage" [PMID:22253607].
- Anther development: "Double mutants of SERK1 and SERK2 are completely male sterile due to a failure
  in tapetum specification" [PMID:16284305]; serk1 serk2 anthers lack a tapetum [PMID:16284306];
  "SERK1 and SERK2 may function redundantly as coreceptors of EMS1" with BiFC/FRET/co-IP evidence
  [PMID:27920157].
- Floral abscission: SERK1 is the HAESA co-receptor, "a positive regulator of the floral abscission
  pathway", binding IDA-bound HAESA with nanomolar affinity [PMID:27058169]. Earlier genetics showed
  serk1 mutations rescue abscission in nev (ARF-GAP) mutants and restore their Golgi structure, while
  serk1 single mutants have normal Golgi [PMID:20230490].
- Peptide receptors: PSK enhances PSKR-SERK heterodimerisation [PMID:26308901]; SERKs act as RGF
  co-receptors [PMID:27229311]; EPF2/EPF1 induce association of ER/ERL1 with SERK1 and SERK1 also
  associates with TMM; SERK1 is a minor contributor to stomatal patterning [PMID:26320950].
- Immunity: SERK1 forms ligand-induced complexes with FLS2 and EFR, but serk1 does not reduce
  flg22/elf18 responses in bak1-4 [PMID:21693696].

## Somatic embryogenesis

- "an increased AtSERK1 level is sufficient to confer embryogenic competence in culture"; ectopic
  expression gives no altered plant phenotype [PMID:11706164]. Single serk1 mutants have no visible
  phenotype (UniProt); the serk1 serk2 bak1 bkk1 quadruple is embryo lethal [PMID:22253607].
