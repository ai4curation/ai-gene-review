Gene Description

YODA (YDA; MAPKKK4) is a large Raf-like/MEKK-type mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase kinase with an N-terminal autoinhibitory regulatory domain, a central Ser/Thr kinase domain and a C-terminal extension. It is the MAPKKK that relays signals from cell-surface receptors to the MKK4/MKK5-MPK3/MPK6 module during development. In the leaf epidermis YODA acts downstream of the EPF2/EPF1 peptides perceived by ERECTA-family receptor kinases with TMM and SERK co-receptors, phosphorylating and activating MKK4/MKK5, whose targets MPK3/MPK6 phosphorylate and destabilize the bHLH transcription factor SPEECHLESS and its partner SCREAM, thereby repressing stomatal lineage entry and enforcing stomatal spacing; null yda mutants form epidermes composed almost entirely of clustered stomata, while constitutively active YODA eliminates stomata. YODA is recruited to the cell cortex of asymmetrically dividing stomatal precursors by the polarity scaffold BASL, forming a polarized BASL-YODA-MPK3/6 feedback module, and is inhibited by the brassinosteroid-regulated GSK3 kinase BIN2. The same cascade controls elongation of the zygote and extra-embryonic suspensor fate downstream of SHORT SUSPENSOR, and inflorescence architecture downstream of ERECTA.

Core Functions

MAP kinase kinase kinase that phosphorylates and activates MKK4/MKK5 (and thereby MPK3/MPK6) downstream of ERECTA-family receptors, forming the YDA-MKK4/5-MPK3/6 cascade.

Molecular Function:
MAP kinase kinase kinase activity
Directly Involved In:
MAPK cascade
Cellular Locations:
cell cortex
Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:22307275
    BIN2 phosphorylates YDA to inhibit YDA phosphorylation of its substrate MKK4

Relays EPF-ERECTA signalling in the leaf epidermis to repress stomatal lineage entry and enforce spacing (via MPK3/6-mediated SPEECHLESS phosphorylation), acting in a BASL-polarized cortical module during asymmetric division.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: How is YODA activated by ERECTA-family receptors - is there a receptor-associated kinase (e.g. BSK/SSP-like) that relieves YODA N-terminal autoinhibition in the epidermis, as SSP does in the zygote?

Q: How is signalling specificity maintained when the same YDA-MKK4/5-MPK3/6 module controls stomatal fate, embryo patterning, inflorescence growth and immunity-related outputs?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Identify YODA-activating partners in stomatal lineage cells by proximity labelling (TurboID-YDA) with and without EPF2 treatment, followed by genetic tests of candidate BSK-family kinases.
