Gene Description

BES1 (BRI1-EMS-SUPPRESSOR 1; also BZR2; At1g19350) is a plant-specific BZR/BES1-family transcription factor and, together with its closest homolog BZR1, the principal nuclear effector of brassinosteroid (BR) signalling. In the absence of BR, the GSK3-like kinase BIN2 phosphorylates BES1 on multiple sites, which inhibits DNA binding, promotes 14-3-3 binding, cytoplasmic retention and nuclear export, and destabilizes the protein. BR perception inactivates BIN2 and PP2A/BSU1 phosphatases dephosphorylate BES1, which then accumulates in the nucleus. Nuclear BES1 binds BRRE and E-box elements in thousands of target promoters, cooperating with bHLH partners such as BIM1 to activate BR-induced genes and with repressors such as MYBL2 to repress BR-repressed genes; it also recruits chromatin regulators (ELF6, REF6, SDG8) and the elongation factor IWS1. BES1 controls cell elongation, male fertility, root quiescent-centre division (via BRAVO), flavonol accumulation and UV-B responses, and contributes to immunity after phosphorylation by MPK6. The semidominant bes1-D (P233L) mutation stabilizes BES1 and causes constitutive BR responses. Arabidopsis thaliana also expresses a long isoform (BES1-L) with an extra nuclear localization signal.

Core Functions

Plant-specific BZR-family DNA-binding transcription factor that, when dephosphorylated in response to brassinosteroid, accumulates in the nucleus and binds BRRE (CGTGT/CG) and E-box (CANNTG) elements, alone or with partners such as BIM1, to activate BR-induced genes and repress BR-repressed genes (e.g. BRAVO, MYB11/12/111).

description: >-
  BES1 (BRI1-EMS-SUPPRESSOR 1; also BZR2; At1g19350) is a plant-specific BZR/BES1-family transcription
  factor and, together with its closest homolog BZR1, the principal nuclear effector of brassinosteroid
  (BR) signalling. In the absence of BR, the GSK3-like kinase BIN2 phosphorylates BES1 on multiple sites,
  which inhibits DNA binding, promotes 14-3-3 binding, cytoplasmic retention and nuclear export, and destabilizes
  the protein. BR perception inactivates BIN2 and PP2A/BSU1 phosphatases dephosphorylate BES1, which then
  accumulates in the nucleus. Nuclear BES1 binds BRRE and E-box elements in thousands of target promoters,
  cooperating with bHLH partners such as BIM1 to activate BR-induced genes and with repressors such as
  MYBL2 to repress BR-repressed genes; it also recruits chromatin regulators (ELF6, REF6, SDG8) and the
  elongation factor IWS1. BES1 controls cell elongation, male fertility, root quiescent-centre division
  (via BRAVO), flavonol accumulation and UV-B responses, and contributes to immunity after phosphorylation
  by MPK6. The semidominant bes1-D (P233L) mutation stabilizes BES1 and causes constitutive BR responses.
  Arabidopsis thaliana also expresses a long isoform (BES1-L) with an extra nuclear localization signal.
