Gene Description

MAP KINASE KINASE 5 (MKK5/MEK5; At3g21220) is a group C plant MAPK kinase (MAP2K) of 348 aa that, redundantly with its paralog MKK4, phosphorylates and activates MPK3 and MPK6. Downstream of the MAPKKK YODA, MKK4/MKK5 repress stomatal lineage entry and spacing and, downstream of ERECTA, promote localized cell proliferation in inflorescences; downstream of IDA-HAE/HSL2 they control floral organ abscission; and downstream of pattern-recognition receptors (via MAPKKK5 and related MAPKKKs) they drive immune responses, ethylene and camalexin production and, when constitutively active, hypersensitive-response-like cell death. MKK5 also relays ABA signals from the MAPKKK AIK1 to MPK6. It is a soluble cytoplasmic/nuclear kinase and is recruited to stress granules under stress.
