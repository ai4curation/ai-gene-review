Gene Description

ABCB4/PGP4/MDR4 is an ATP-binding cassette auxin transporter expressed prominently in the Arabidopsis root cap and epidermis. It contributes to shootward auxin movement near the root apex and regulates cellular auxin levels, root growth and gravitropic responses. Transport direction depends on auxin concentration and experimental context: low-concentration uptake and efflux at higher cellular auxin have both been observed. ABCB4 is strongly retained at the plasma membrane and cycles differently from PIN proteins. It can also mediate uptake of the synthetic auxin 2,4-D under defined conditions; this does not imply broad xenobiotic export or brassinosteroid transport.

ABCB4 review notes

2026-10-26 complete source-specific annotation review

ABCB4 shows condition-dependent auxin import/export (PMID21992190; full PMID16243904, PMID18156217 and PMID17557805 recovered normally). The comparative author manuscript of PMID23147222 independently distinguishes ABCB4/21 uptake at low internal IAA from export after loading, unlike ABCB1. These are distinct assay states, not conflicting positive and NOT annotations. Root gravitropic phenotypes and tissue-level basipetal flux do not mean that transporter polarity or direction is invariant.

ATP binding/hydrolysis are integral supported biochemical properties. The ABC-type xenobiotic MF is overannotated: its verified definition specifies ATP-coupled inside-to-outside xenobiotic movement, whereas direct 2,4-D uptake supports the nondirectional xenobiotic transport process without establishing xenobiotic export. Cytokinin-induced expression alone does not establish a functional cytokinin response. Native auxin transport and root-hair growth roles are retained with construct/context limits.

PMID28887381 classifies the exact target protein group as membrane-associated; cholate-soluble microsomes are not cytosol. The cytosolic claim exceeds that source, not because a multipass protein could never have an unusual soluble pool. Nuclear prediction remains unresolved. Original PMID21533090 TableS1 directly identifies ABCB4 (four unique peptides) in a PD-enriched fraction; non-core plasmodesmal HDA localization is retained without assuming exclusive localization or transport through PD.

  Arabidopsis root cap and epidermis. It contributes to shootward auxin movement near the root apex and
  regulates cellular auxin levels, root growth and gravitropic responses. Transport direction depends
  on auxin concentration and experimental context: low-concentration uptake and efflux at higher cellular

  from PIN proteins. It can also mediate uptake of the synthetic auxin 2,4-D under defined conditions;
