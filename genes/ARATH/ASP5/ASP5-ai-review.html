Gene Description

ASP5 is a plastid pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent aspartate aminotransferase that reversibly interconverts aspartate and 2-oxoglutarate with oxaloacetate and glutamate. It contributes to plastid amino-acid and organic-acid metabolism. Unlike the distinct plastid class-Ib enzyme PAT, recombinant ASP5 lacks detectable prephenate aminotransferase activity under the tested conditions.

ASP5 evidence audit

P46248 At4g31990 is plastid ASP5, activity AAT3; PANTHER SF46 official name includes CYTOPLASMIC but does not override direct plastid evidence. Primary9535706 initially suggested amyloplast rather than chloroplast from electrophoretic comparisons;7766905 import and12068109 native mutant assignment support plastids/chloroplasts. Full20883697 publisher text was independently inspected at https://febs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1016/j.febslet.2010.09.037 : Methods2.6 explicitly expresses both At2g22250 and At4g31990; Results3.2 directly finds no prephenate transfer by purified ASP5 at1mM prephenate. Both seeded NOT annotations are therefore accepted, not misread as positive claims. Source cache remains abstract-only; no fabricated full-text quote was inserted. Stromule is not synonymous with stroma: exact16923014 stromule claim remains unresolved.

All original annotation payloads retained; only review objects were curated. Falcon and perplexity-lite fallback were attempted concurrently with publication caching and failed (403/401 provider errors); no provider report was fabricated. FEBA is not applicable to this plant panel.

All annotations were assessed as biological claims. Specific true functions were retained across evidence codes; broad terms were refined; unresolved compartment/affinity-screen claims were not converted into negative evidence. No NEW annotations were added solely to fill module coverage.

Supplementary-table provenance and exact target rows are in ../ASP2/ASP2-source-analysis/ (or the local ASP2-source-analysis directory for ASP2). PMID28887381 is a mixed-fraction protein-correlation study, not automatic evidence of every annotated compartment. PMID21533090 S3 peptide detection and S4 targeting classification were inspected; S5 merely lists prior GO assertions and was not used as independent evidence.

Final negative-localization audit

The seeded annotation explicitly excludes cytosolic localization. Plastid/chloroplast targeting of ASP5 supports its principal organellar location, but does not alone establish absence of a minor or conditional cytosolic pool. The precise target-specific exclusion behind the PMID21166475 proteomic classification could not be resolved from accessible material. The unrelated ASP5 negative-prephenate rationale was removed from this localization row and its source assessment. Original annotation fields, including negation, are preserved.
