CC   -!- FUNCTION: Acts as a component of the auxin efflux carrier; this
CC       activity is enhanced when activated by PID-mediated phosphorylation
CC       (PubMed:20439545, PubMed:35917926). Seems to be involved in the lateral
CC       auxin transport system (PubMed:20439545). Together with PIN4 and PIN7,
CC       involved in the connective auxin transport (CAT) that ensures
CC       communication across the shoot system, and modulates strigolactone-
CC       mediated shoot branching control (PubMed:30865619). Binds auxins
CC       including indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) (PubMed:35917926). Coordinated
CC       polar localization of PIN3 is directly regulated by the vesicle
CC       trafficking process (PubMed:20439545). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20439545,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30865619, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35917926}.
CC   -!- ACTIVITY REGULATION: Auxin efflux carrier activity is competitively
CC       inhibited by naptalamate (N-1-naphthylphthalamic acid, NPA).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:35917926}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:35917926}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:20439545};
CC       Multi-pass membrane protein {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Predominantly expressed at the lateral side of
CC       shoot endodermis cells as well as root pericycle and columella cells.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Expressed during embryogenesis. Detected in the
CC       precursors of the columella root cells. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14614497}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Down-regulated by endoplasmic reticulum stress treatment.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:24180465}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Plants display defects in hypocotyl differential
CC       growth (PubMed:11845211). The triple mutant pin3 pin4 pin7 is able to
CC       suppress partially the highly branched phenotype of strigolactone
CC       deficient mutants lacking MAX2 and MAX4 (PubMed:30865619). The abcb19
CC       pin3 pin4 pin7 quadruple mutant exhibits an additive phenotype on
CC       strigolactone-mediated bud outgrowth responses and shoot branching
CC       control (PubMed:30865619). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:11845211,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:30865619}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the auxin efflux carrier (TC 2.A.69.1) family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
