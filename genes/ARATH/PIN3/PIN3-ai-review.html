Gene Description

PIN3 is a homodimeric auxin efflux transporter that redistributes indole-3-acetic acid across the Arabidopsis plasma membrane. Its cell-type-specific polarity and regulated vesicular trafficking direct lateral auxin redistribution during gravitropism and phototropism. Phosphorylation of the central hydrophilic loop regulates trafficking and transport-associated developmental responses. PIN3 also contributes to auxin distribution in developing seeds and to root development; its polarity and physiological effects depend on tissue and stimulus rather than a single fixed membrane face.

Core Functions

Auxin efflux through a homodimeric PIN carrier supports directional and lateral IAA redistribution. Cell-type-specific PM polarity and actin-dependent cycling change during gravity/light responses. In the lateral-root-emergence cortex, sequential induction with LAX3 supports a distinct tissue-level transport circuit.

PIN3 review notes

2026-10-26 complete source-specific annotation review

PIN3 directly mediates IAA efflux and forms a homodimer (PMID35917926). Its PM polarity is tissue/stimulus dependent; polarity and cellular transport direction are separate claims. PMID11845211 supports PM and recycling-vesicle localization, and native gravitropic/phototropic experiments support lateral redistribution. PMID24150423 adds a cortical lateral-root-emergence circuit: native reporter/time-course evidence and a multicellular model support sequential PIN3 then LAX3 induction. This is not physical binding and is distinct from endodermal initiation-stage reflux.

The full PMID36582637 experiment specifically contrasts PIN3 with PIN7 under alkaline stress: PIN3 PM abundance and pin3 response are unchanged in the relevant assays. The alkaline-response annotation is removed using actual same-target negative evidence and the verified stimulus-specific term, not the title’s emphasis on PIN7. Root-hair initiation is overinterpreted from visible hair counts and strong elongation phenotypes; supported elongation is retained as non-core.

PMID28887381 original supplements identify PIN3 AT1G70940 in Mapp row1879 as a membrane protein; the imported SUBAcon PM assignment is not a new location assay. See the reproducible ABCB19 bioinformatics artifact. Mitochondrial residence remains unresolved, not excluded by PM localization. The PAINT ER node PTN001589272 is also unresolved: long-/short-loop PIN biology alone does not prove loss of an inherited ER pool. No supported root-organelle claim was narrowed away.

  label: Arabidopsis thaliana
description: PIN3 is a homodimeric auxin efflux transporter that redistributes indole-3-acetic acid across
  the Arabidopsis plasma membrane. Its cell-type-specific polarity and regulated vesicular trafficking
  direct lateral auxin redistribution during gravitropism and phototropism. Phosphorylation of the central
  hydrophilic loop regulates trafficking and transport-associated developmental responses. PIN3 also contributes
  to auxin distribution in developing seeds and to root development; its polarity and physiological effects
  depend on tissue and stimulus rather than a single fixed membrane face.
