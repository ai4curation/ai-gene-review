Gene Description

GA3 (ent-kaurene oxidase, KO, CYP701A3; At5g25900) is the sole Arabidopsis ent-kaurene oxidase, a multifunctional cytochrome P450 that catalyses three sequential oxidations of the C-19 (C4-alpha) methyl group of ent-kaur-16-ene via ent-kaurenol and ent-kaurenal to ent-kaurenoic acid, with intermediates retained in the active site and the first hydroxylation rate-limiting. KO is targeted to the outer face of the plastid envelope (organelle proteomics also co-fractionate it with the ER), where it is thought to connect the plastid-localized cyclase steps (GA1/CPS, GA2/KS) to the ER-localized KAO step. It is broadly expressed, most strongly in inflorescences and in the cortex/endodermis of germinating embryos. ga3 nonsense mutants lack KO activity and are gibberellin-responsive dwarfs.

Core Functions

Multifunctional cytochrome P450 (CYP701A3) that oxidizes the C-19 methyl of ent-kaur-16-ene in three successive steps (ent-kaurenol, ent-kaurenal) to ent-kaur-16-en-19-oate (RHEA:32323), the first oxidative step of gibberellin biosynthesis; associated with the outer chloroplast envelope, linking plastid and ER steps.
