CC   -!- FUNCTION: Transcription factor. Involved in epidermal cell fate
CC       specification. Negative regulator of trichome development, including
CC       endoreplication, by lateral inhibition involving intercellular
CC       interactions. Promotes the formation of hair developing cells
CC       (trichoblasts) in H position in root epidermis, probably by inhibiting
CC       non-hair cell (atrichoblasts) formation. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:10368181,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:12356720}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with GL3 and thus prevents GL1 GL3 interaction. Also
CC       interacts with BHLH2. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12917293,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:14561633}.

CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Nucleus {ECO:0000269|PubMed:14561633}.
CC       Note=Detected in trichome nucleus.
CC   -!- TISSUE SPECIFICITY: Expressed in roots, leaves, siliques and
CC       inflorescences. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12356720}.
CC   -!- DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE: Ubiquitous in young leaves. Later, restricted to

CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:12356720}.
