Gene Description

LAX3 is an eleven-transmembrane auxin influx carrier of the Arabidopsis AUX/LAX family. It imports indole-3-acetic acid at the plasma membrane and also transports the synthetic auxin 2,4-D in heterologous uptake assays. Auxin-induced LAX3 expression in cortical and epidermal cells overlying lateral-root primordia reinforces local auxin responses and cell-wall remodeling, facilitating lateral-root emergence. LAX3 also contributes redundantly with other AUX/LAX carriers to embryonic root-cap organization. Transport is pH dependent; the proposed histidine-mediated proton-coupling mechanism and its stoichiometry remain incompletely resolved.

LAX3 review notes

2026-10-26 complete source-specific annotation review

Full-length LAX3 directly mediates auxin uptake in oocytes (PMID40759769 and the recovered full PMID22773749). The 2025 structural study uses an N-terminally truncated 44–470 construct that is inactive in the uptake assay; the transport conclusion comes from full-length protein. Histidine-mediated proton coupling is proposed, and integer proton/IAA stoichiometry is not established. LAX3 also imports 2,4-D in the tested system.

The native cortical/epidermal emergence role is supported by PMID18622388; PMID24150423 independently resolves functional native-promoter LAX3-YFP expression and sequential induction after PIN3 in the cortex. Model predictions are distinguished from measured expression/localization. The 2012 ectopic AUX1-promoter targeting defect is not evidence against native LAX3 localization; curated membrane inference, native expression and transport support PM. Root-cap development is retained as a redundant AUX/LAX developmental role from the multiple-mutant study, not LAX3-exclusive necessity.

PAINT PTN001118955 amino-acid transport MF/BP remain UNDECIDED. Direct auxin recognition and a distinct AUX/LAX branch establish specialization but do not supply a negative amino-acid assay. The amino-acid family name is neither proof of retained substrate activity nor sufficient grounds for rejecting it.
