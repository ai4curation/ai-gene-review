Gene Description

NINJA (NOVEL INTERACTOR OF JAZ; UniProt name AFPH2, AFP homolog 2) is a nuclear transcriptional repressor adaptor of the ABI five-binding protein (AFP) family that links JAZ repressors to the Groucho/Tup1-type corepressor TOPLESS (TPL) and the TPL-related proteins TPR2 and TPR3. Its C-terminal region binds the TIFY motif in the ZIM domain of most JAZ proteins and of other group-II TIFY proteins such as PEAPOD1/2 and TIFY8, while an N-terminal EAR (ethylene-responsive element binding factor-associated amphiphilic repression) motif in domain A binds TPL. In the resting state this JAZ-NINJA-TPL assembly represses MYC-dependent transcription; NINJA overexpression reduces and NINJA knockdown enhances jasmonate responses. Through its interaction with PEAPOD2, NINJA also restricts cell proliferation in the leaf blade, controlling leaf flatness, and it has been implicated in root stem cell niche maintenance through interaction with PAT1H1.

Reason: The interaction rests on an in vitro MBP pull-down (NINJA was pulled down by all JAZ-MBP fusions except JAZ7, JAZ11 and JAZ12), while the same paper's systematic Y2H found NINJA binding most JAZ proteins "except JAZ7 and JAZ8", and UniProt's curated NINJA interactor list omits TIFY5A/JAZ8. JAZ8 carries its own N-terminal EAR motif that recruits TOPLESS directly, and JAZ8-mediated repression does not require the ZIM domain that recruits NINJA in other JAZ proteins (PMID:22327740). That paper's own pull-down recovered much less NINJA with JAZ8 than with JAZ10.1, though more than with the MBP control. The row is kept as the curator's observation but marked as an over-annotation rather than converted to transcription corepressor binding.
