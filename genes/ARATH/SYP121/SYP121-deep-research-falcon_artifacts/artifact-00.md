| Functional evidence | Mechanism and localization | Experimental basis / evidence strength | Major caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plasma-membrane SNARE exocytosis** | SYP121/PEN1 is a plasma-membrane **Qa-SNARE** that assembles with Qbc-SNARE SNAP33 and vesicle R-SNARE VAMP721 or VAMP722. The resulting four-helix SNARE bundle promotes secretory-vesicle fusion with the plasma membrane. | **Strong:** plasma-membrane localization by functional fluorescent fusions and fractionation; ternary-complex formation by in-vitro and in-planta co-precipitation; a SNARE-motif mutation disrupted complex formation and defense function. [Pajonk et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M805236200) (pqac-00000010, pqac-00000037) | VAMP721 and VAMP722 are redundant and essential in combination; biochemical complex formation does not identify every physiological cargo or prove the complete fusion sequence in vivo. |
| **Polarized fungal-papilla immunity** | Following powdery-mildew attack, SYP121 is recruited from the plasma membrane/endosomal pool to membrane domains beneath fungal appressoria, supporting rapid polarized secretion and papilla assembly. | **Strong:** functional GFP-PEN1 accumulated about **6.3-fold** over background plasma-membrane signal; *pen1-1* delayed papilla formation by approximately **2 h**. Nonhost fungal penetration was **26.6 ± 2.5%** in *pen1-1*, and the broader analysis reported a **50–70% increase** relative to wild type. [Assaad et al., 2004](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.e04-02-0140) (pqac-00000033, pqac-00000043, pqac-00000044) | Mature papillae still form, so SYP121 controls timing rather than being the sole papilla pathway. The precise secreted antimicrobial or wall-building cargo remains incompletely defined. |
| **KC1/AKT1 K⁺-channel gating** | SYP121 directly binds the regulatory channel subunit KC1 through its N-terminal **F<sub>9</sub>xRF** motif and forms an SYP121–KC1–AKT1 complex at the plasma membrane, shifting channel gating to support inward K⁺ current and low-K⁺ acquisition. **SYP121 is a gating/trafficking protein, not a K⁺ transporter.** | **Strong:** split-ubiquitin, BiFC, heterologous electrophysiology and Arabidopsis root-epidermal voltage clamp. F9A and F12A substitutions failed to restore normal inward current, whereas motif-retaining S10A restored wild-type-like gating. [Grefen et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1105/tpc.110.077768) (pqac-00000008, pqac-00000025, pqac-00000045) | Channel binding is separable from canonical cargo delivery. The later model in which channel binding displaces SEC11 and triggers SNARE assembly is mechanistically supported but remains partly inferential in intact plants. |
| **PIP2;7 aquaporin delivery** | At the plasma membrane and post-Golgi/TGN route, SYP121 physically interacts with PIP2;7 and cooperates with SYP61 to deliver aquaporins, thereby setting membrane water permeability. | **Strong:** colocalization, co-immunoprecipitation and BiFC plus functional perturbation. Dominant-negative SYP121-Sp2 reduced peripheral PIP2;7 fluorescence by **57%** and protoplast osmotic water permeability by **64%**. [Hachez et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1105/tpc.114.127159) (pqac-00000027, pqac-00000032, pqac-00000042) | SYP121-Sp2 also perturbed other PIPs and BRI1, although not PIN2, so the phenotype is not uniquely PIP2;7-specific. Association supports—but does not fully establish—the exact SYP61–SYP121 fusion complex or trafficking direction. |
| **Extracellular-vesicle/senescence association (2024)** | PEN1 marks a plasma-membrane-associated extracellular-vesicle population in the apoplast. Loss of PEN1 correlates with early senescence and increased abundance of the distinct TET8-associated apoplast population. | **Moderate/emerging:** in 8-week-old *pen1* rosettes, apoplast TET8 was approximately **2-fold** higher than wild type without increased TET8 transcript; no difference occurred at 4 weeks. TET3/TET8 mutations reversed early *pen1* senescence. [Zimmerman et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1002/pld3.70006) (pqac-00000036, pqac-00000041) | TET8 abundance was used as an approximation of TET8-associated vesicles. The study establishes genetic association and correlation, not that SYP121 directly generates extracellular vesicles or that altered vesicle flux causes senescence. |


*Table: Evidence-strength summary for Arabidopsis thaliana Q9ZSD4/SYP121 (PEN1/SYR1), separating established SNARE, immunity, channel-gating and aquaporin-trafficking functions from the emerging extracellular-vesicle/senescence association.*
