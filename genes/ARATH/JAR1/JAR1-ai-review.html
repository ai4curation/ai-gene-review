Gene Description

JAR1 (JASMONATE RESISTANT 1; also FIN219 and GH3.11) is a cytoplasmic GH3-family acyl acid-amido synthetase of the ANL adenylate-forming enzyme superfamily. It activates jasmonic acid by ATP-dependent adenylation and then conjugates it to an amino acid, with a strong preference for L-isoleucine, producing jasmonoyl-L-isoleucine (JA-Ile), the bioactive jasmonate perceived by the COI1-JAZ co-receptor. Loss of JAR1 strongly reduces JA-Ile levels and causes jasmonate insensitivity in root growth, gene induction, guard-cell signaling and induced systemic resistance, while residual JA-Ile formation in jar1 mutants indicates partial redundancy with other conjugating activities. JAR1 is auxin- and wound-inducible, and as FIN219 it also acts in phytochrome A-mediated far-red light responses, where its activity is enhanced by interaction with the tau-class glutathione S-transferase FIP1 (GSTU20).

description: JAR1 (JASMONATE RESISTANT 1; also FIN219 and GH3.11) is a cytoplasmic GH3-family acyl acid-amido
  synthetase of the ANL adenylate-forming enzyme superfamily. It activates jasmonic acid by ATP-dependent
  adenylation and then conjugates it to an amino acid, with a strong preference for L-isoleucine, producing
  jasmonoyl-L-isoleucine (JA-Ile), the bioactive jasmonate perceived by the COI1-JAZ co-receptor. Loss
  of JAR1 strongly reduces JA-Ile levels and causes jasmonate insensitivity in root growth, gene induction,
  guard-cell signaling and induced systemic resistance, while residual JA-Ile formation in jar1 mutants
  indicates partial redundancy with other conjugating activities. JAR1 is auxin- and wound-inducible,
  and as FIN219 it also acts in phytochrome A-mediated far-red light responses, where its activity is
  enhanced by interaction with the tau-class glutathione S-transferase FIP1 (GSTU20).
