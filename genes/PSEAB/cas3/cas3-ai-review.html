Gene Description

Cas3 of the Pseudomonas aeruginosa UCBPP-PA14 type I-F (Yersinia-subtype) CRISPR-Cas system is a 1076-residue Cas2-Cas3 fusion protein: type I-F systems encode no separate cas2 gene, so a Cas2 module occupies the N-terminal approximately 120 residues ahead of an HD-family metal-dependent nuclease domain and a superfamily 2 ATP-dependent helicase domain. The two halves serve the two halves of the pathway. Through its Cas2 module the protein is required for capture and integration of new spacers alongside Cas1, and through its nuclease and helicase domains it is the destruction machine of the interference stage: once the Csy surveillance complex has formed a PAM-licensed R-loop on an invading DNA, Cas3 is recruited to the C-terminal helical bundle of the Csy large subunit Cas8f and processively unwinds and degrades the target. Point substitutions in either the nuclease or the helicase catalytic centre abolish function in vivo. The recruitment step is the sole target of the phage anti-CRISPR protein AcrIF3, which clamps Cas3 in a nucleotide-bound state, occludes the helicase DNA-binding tunnel and masks the surface the Csy complex uses.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Does the Cas2 module of PA14 Cas2-3 contribute anything to interference, or is its role confined to adaptation? The D124 position sits at the module boundary and is also the HD nuclease metal ligand in the numbering used by the mutagenesis studies, so the two functions are hard to separate genetically.

Q: AcrIF3 blocks Cas3 recruitment while leaving the Csy complex able to bind its target, which converts the system into a sequence-specific DNA-binding module. Should that gain-of-function state (reported as conversion into a transcriptional repressor) be captured in GO at all, given that it occurs only during phage infection?

Q: Is PA14 Cas3 recruited as a monomer or as an oligomer, and does the nucleotide state that AcrIF3 traps correspond to the recruitment-competent conformation or to a post-translocation one?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Reconstitute the Csy complex with a target plasmid carrying strand-specific labels and run time-course cleavage with purified PA14 Cas2-3, mapping products by denaturing gel and sequencing. Repeat with HD-dead (D124A) and helicase-dead (D576N) proteins to assign products to each catalytic centre.

Hypothesis: PA14 Cas3 degrades the displaced non-target strand first and processively, as the Escherichia coli orthologue does, rather than cleaving both strands at the R-loop.

Type: in vitro nuclease mapping

Experiment: Measure ATPase and unwinding of defined forked substrates by purified Cas2-3 alone and in the presence of the Csy complex, and test whether AcrIF3 inhibits the intrinsic activity or only the Cas8f-dependent stimulation.

Hypothesis: The helicase domain of PA14 Cas3 is an ATP-dependent 3' to 5' DNA translocase whose activity is licensed by Cas8f contact.

Type: single-molecule or ensemble helicase assay

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: No nuclease or helicase activity has been measured on purified Pseudomonas aeruginosa Cas3 in isolation, so the mode of DNA cleavage (endonucleolytic versus exonucleolytic, strand preference, processivity) and the duplex substrate of the helicase are undetermined for this orthologue.

OPEN BIOLOGYCURATION

What is known: What is established is that Cas3 is necessary and sufficient to convert Csy-bound DNA into degraded product in vitro, that both catalytic centres are required in vivo, and that the helicase domain binds nucleotide and has a DNA-binding tunnel. The Escherichia coli orthologue's detailed activities (single-stranded DNA endonuclease, 3'-5' exonuclease, DNA/RNA helicase) have not been reproduced on this protein.

Significance: The absence of any molecular-function annotation on Q02ML8, in contrast to nine on the Escherichia coli orthologue, makes the destruction step of the best-studied anti-CRISPR system invisible to GO-based reasoning, and leaves the nature of the Cas2-3 fusion's division of labour unresolved.

What would resolve it: Run the standard Cas3 biochemical panel (single-stranded and double-stranded DNA nuclease assays, directional unwinding, ATPase) on purified PA14 Cas2-3 and on isolated HD and helicase domains, then annotate the specific children of GO:0004536 and GO:0004386 that the results support.

What this protein is

PA14_33340, 1076 aa, UniProt name "CRISPR-associated nuclease/helicase Cas3 subtype
I-F/YPEST". It is a Cas2-Cas3 fusion, not a bare Cas3:
PMID:26586803. The InterPro
complement reflects this (IPR048823 Cas3_I-F_Cas2, IPR006483 Cas3-HD,
IPR027417 P-loop NTPase), and UniProt places an HD Cas3-type domain at 102–318 and a
DEAD-box motif at 576–579.

GOA for Q02ML8 is strikingly thin: only the CACAO IMP rows on
GO:0043571. No IEA at all — no InterPro2GO, no UniRule — even though the orthologue
P38036 (E. coli Cas3) carries nine molecular-function annotations. The most likely
reason is that the fused Cas2-3 architecture does not match the signatures the
pipelines key on. That is a curation gap, not a biological absence, and it is the main
thing this review has to fix.

Role in the pathway

Cas3 is the destruction step: the Csy complex finds and verifies the target, then
recruits Cas3.
PMID:28985564. The docking site is the Csy large subunit:
PMID:32170016.

This recruitment step is the target of the anti-CRISPR AcrIF3:
PMID:26416740.
The mechanism is structurally resolved on this protein:
PMID:27455460 and
PMID:27455460.

Direct evidence for activities, in this protein

DNA nuclease activity (IDA). The cryo-EM/biochemistry study of the type I-F complex
reconstituted Csy + Cas3 + dsDNA in vitro with ATP and Mg(2+), and read out DNA
cleavage that is strictly Cas3-dependent — the no-Cas3 lane is the negative control:
PMID:32170016 and, on relief of inhibition,
PMID:32170016. The Csy complex came from the PA14
expression plasmid and the paper identifies the Cas3 as P. aeruginosa (quote above),
so this is direct evidence on the gene product being reviewed.

ATP binding (IDA). PaCas3 was crystallised with nucleotide bound — "locks PaCas3 in
an ADP-bound form" (quote above) — placing a bound adenine nucleotide in the helicase
domain of this protein.

Both catalytic centres are required in vivo (IMP). Point mutants in each domain
abolish function:
PMID:26586803, and independently
PMID:21398535. The Cas2 moiety is separately required:
PMID:26586803.

Curation decisions

  • GO:0043571 maintenance of CRISPR repeat elements, IMP,
    acts_upstream_of_or_within_positive_effect → ACCEPT. Well grounded: the Cas2-moiety
    truncation and both catalytic-domain point mutants abolish spacer acquisition. The
    hedged qualifier is the right one, because in type I-F the adaptation requirement is
    partly carried by the fused Cas2 domain and partly indirect (via the Csy complex and
    crRNA).

Gaps identified (missing annotations)

Comparator check against E. coli Cas3 (P38036, genes/ECOLI/ygcB/ygcB-goa.tsv): it
holds GO:0000014, GO:0008296, GO:0033677, GO:0003690, GO:0000287,
GO:0003724 and GO:0005524, all by IDA/EXP/IBA. PA14 Cas3 holds none. Every type I
system's Cas3 is the degradation nuclease, so a systematic absence here is one-protein
pipeline failure, not an ontology convention. Proposing, conservatively:

  • GO:0004536 DNA nuclease activity (NEW, IDA, PMID:32170016). Deliberately the
    moderately specific term, not E. coli's GO:0000014 single-stranded DNA endonuclease activity or GO:0008296 3'-5'-DNA exonuclease activity: the in vitro assay used
    dsDNA presented by the Csy complex and did not resolve exo- versus endonucleolytic
    mode or strand preference for this orthologue. Asserting the E. coli-specific
    children would be transferring a result I cannot cite for PaCas3.
  • GO:0004386 helicase activity (NEW, IMP, PMID:26586803 + PMID:21398535, with
    PMID:27455460 for the structure). The DEAD-box point mutant D576N abolishes function
    in vivo and the AcrF3 co-crystal shows a nucleotide-bound helicase domain with a
    DNA-binding tunnel. Again the generic term rather than E. coli's GO:0033677 DNA/RNA helicase activity: no unwinding assay on PaCas3 has established the substrate pairing.
  • GO:0005524 ATP binding (NEW, IDA, PMID:27455460).
  • GO:0099048 CRISPR-cas system (NEW, IMP, PMID:26586803). The term's definition names
    "target interference" as one of its three stages, which is the step Cas3 performs.
    Checked via QuickGO: not an ancestor or descendant of GO:0043571.

Not proposed, and why

  • GO:0051607 defense response to virus. E. coli Cas3 carries it by IMP and IDA, but
    the PA14-specific phenotype runs the other way:
    PMID:21398535. Later work shows the
    locus does restrict other phages
    PMID:23242138, but that is a
    whole-locus (ΔCR/cas) deletion, not a cas3 allele. With a published negative result
    for this gene and no gene-specific positive one, I leave the term off and raise it as
    a question. GO:0099048 carries the immunity role without the contested claim.
  • GO:0046872 metal ion binding / GO:0000287 magnesium ion binding. UniProt's Mg(2+)
    sites at 124 and 220 are ECO:0000255 (sequence-model) only, and I have no direct
    metal-binding measurement for PaCas3.
  • The biofilm phenotype (DMS3-dependent loss of biofilm formation) is a strain- and
    prophage-specific downstream consequence of target recognition, not a function of
    Cas3. Recorded here, not annotated.
