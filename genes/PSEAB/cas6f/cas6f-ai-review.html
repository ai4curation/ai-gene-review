Gene Description

Cas6f (also called Csy4) is the pre-crRNA endoribonuclease of the Pseudomonas aeruginosa UCBPP-PA14 type I-F (Yersinia-subtype) CRISPR-Cas system. It recognises the hairpin formed by each 28-nucleotide CRISPR repeat in the primary array transcript, clamping the RNA stem between the protein body and an arginine-rich helix, and cleaves immediately downstream of the hairpin using a serine-histidine catalytic dyad. Catalysis is metal-ion independent and leaves 5'-hydroxyl and 3'-phosphate ends, releasing single-spacer guide RNAs. Cas6f does not release its product: it remains clamped on the crRNA 3' hairpin, and that retention nucleates assembly of the Csy surveillance complex (one Cas8f/Csy1, one Cas5f/Csy2, six Cas7f/Csy3, one Cas6f and a single 60-nucleotide crRNA), in which Cas6f occupies the PAM-distal head. The protein is therefore both the crRNA-biogenesis enzyme and a structural subunit of the effector it loads, and loss of it abolishes mature crRNA and with it both target interference and new spacer acquisition.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

CRISPR RNA-guided surveillance complex

Definition: A ribonucleoprotein complex composed of CRISPR-associated (Cas) proteins assembled around a single mature CRISPR RNA (crRNA), which uses the crRNA as a guide to locate nucleic acid complementary to its spacer sequence. In type I systems the complex additionally recognises a protospacer-adjacent motif, forms an R-loop at the target, and recruits a trans-acting nuclease for target destruction. The Pseudomonas aeruginosa type I-F complex, Csy, has the composition Cas8f(1)/Cas5f(1)/Cas7f(6)/Cas6f(1) with one 60-nucleotide crRNA; the type I-E equivalent is Cascade.

Justification: GO's entire CRISPR vocabulary is five biological-process terms (GO:0099048, GO:0043571, GO:0098672 and the obsolete GO:0110132 and GO:0110133), with no cellular-component term, so every subunit of every CRISPR surveillance complex can currently only be placed on the bare GO:0032991 root or on its generic child GO:1990904. The complex is a well-defined, stoichiometrically determined, structurally solved entity that ComplexPortal already models for the type I-E case (CPX-1005, Cascade complex), and the Cas7f backbone in particular is the surface targeted by several families of phage-encoded anti-CRISPR proteins, so a specific term would make a large body of structural and counter-defence curation expressible. This is the same request made from the Escherichia coli type I-E Cascade subunit reviews, so that the two systems ask for one term rather than two.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Cas6f has two separable activities, repeat cleavage and product retention, and the second is the one that builds the effector. Should GO express the retained-subunit role as a distinct function, or is RNA binding plus complex membership sufficient?

Q: GO has no molecular-function term for crRNA-guided target recognition, the activity the assembled Csy complex has. Cas6f contributes the guide and the 3' anchor but not the target contacts. If such a term were created, should a biogenesis subunit retained in the complex carry it, contribute to it, or neither?

Q: In the PA14 strain background, is the mature crRNA pool limited by Cas6f cleavage rate or by the availability of Csy1, Csy2 and Csy3 to protect the product from degradation?

Suggested Experiments

Experiment: Complement a PA14 csy4 H29A strain with pre-cleaved mature crRNA supplied in trans (for example from a tRNA-flanked expression construct that needs no Cas6f processing) and test whether spacer acquisition and interference are restored. Restoration would confirm that the adaptation requirement is purely upstream.

Hypothesis: The H29A catalytic mutant abolishes spacer acquisition solely by failing to make mature crRNA, not by any separate role of Cas6f in adaptation.

Type: genetic complementation

Experiment: Use the separation-of-function mutant set from the structural work (binding competent, cleavage dead versus cleavage competent, release accelerated) in pulse-chase northern blots and quantitative pull-downs of the Csy complex to measure the partition of crRNA between free, Cas6f-bound and complex-bound pools.

Hypothesis: Cas6f product retention, rather than cleavage per se, is what commits a crRNA to complex assembly.

Type: biochemical kinetics

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: GO has no cellular-component term for a CRISPR RNA-guided surveillance complex, so Csy complex membership can only be asserted with the generic GO:1990904 ribonucleoprotein complex, which does not distinguish it from a ribosome, a spliceosome or any other RNA-protein assembly.

OPEN ONTOLOGY

What is known: The composition and stoichiometry are settled to near-atomic resolution (Cas8f(1), Cas5f(1), Cas7f(6), Cas6f(1), crRNA(1)), and the Escherichia coli Cascade subunits are annotated the same uninformative way, so this is a shared ontology limitation rather than a gap in knowledge about this protein. ComplexPortal does model the type I-E equivalent as CPX-1005; GO does not.

Significance: Every anti-CRISPR protein acts on this complex; without a term for it the host target of a whole class of phage counter-defence proteins is not machine-readable.

What would resolve it: Request a cellular-component term for a CRISPR RNA-guided surveillance complex under GO:1990904, with subtype children for Cascade and the Csy complex; the request is made in this review's proposed_new_terms.

System context

PA14 carries a type I-F (Yersinia-subtype, "Csy") CRISPR-Cas system at
PA14_33300–PA14_33350: cas6f/csy4, csy3, csy2, csy1, cas2-3 (annotated
cas3), cas1, flanked by two CRISPR arrays. Unusually for type I, Cas2 is not a
separate protein: PMID:26586803.

The interference effector is the Csy surveillance complex:
PMID:32170016, arranged as
PMID:32170016. In the PA14 nomenclature Cas8f = Csy1, Cas5f = Csy2, Cas7f = Csy3,
Cas6f = Csy4. This matches UniProt's stoichiometry
Csy1(1),Csy2(1),Csy3(6),Cas6/Csy4(1)-crRNA(1) and the original biochemistry
PMID:21536913.

This is the host side of the anti-CRISPR system reviewed in
genes/BPZF4/AcrF8/AcrF8-ai-review.yaml: AcrIF8 binds the Cas7f (Csy3) backbone and
the crRNA; AcrIF3 sequesters Cas3.

Cas6f/Csy4 is the pre-crRNA endoribonuclease

Csy4 is the enzyme that makes mature crRNA:
PMID:20829488. The substrate is RNA, not DNA, and catalysis is metal-independent:
PMID:20829488.
Independently confirmed: PMID:22522703.

Recognition is sequence- and structure-specific for the repeat hairpin:
PMID:20829488.
Catalysis uses a Ser/His dyad; the catalytic residues are separable from binding:
PMID:20829488. That last
result is the cleanest evidence that Csy4 has two separable activities — RNA
endonuclease and high-affinity crRNA binding.

Dual role: enzyme and retained complex subunit

Like E. coli Cas6e/CasE, Csy4 does not release its product:
PMID:22522703, and this retention is what builds the effector:
PMID:22522703. Structurally it sits
at the PAM-distal head on the crRNA 3' hairpin
PMID:28985564. The IntAct
interaction behind GOA's two GO:0005515 rows is Csy4–Csy3 (Q02MM2–Q02MM1, NbExp=12),
i.e. the head-to-backbone contact of this same complex.

In vivo requirement

Loss of Csy4 abolishes crRNA:
PMID:21398535. The catalytic residue is required for spacer
acquisition as well as interference:
PMID:26586803 — the adaptation defect is downstream of the crRNA
defect, since PMID:26586803.

proposed_new_terms:
- proposed_name: CRISPR RNA-guided surveillance complex
  proposed_definition: >-
    A ribonucleoprotein complex composed of CRISPR-associated (Cas) proteins
    assembled around a single mature CRISPR RNA (crRNA), which uses the crRNA as
    a guide to locate nucleic acid complementary to its spacer sequence. In type
    I systems the complex additionally recognises a protospacer-adjacent motif,
    forms an R-loop at the target, and recruits a trans-acting nuclease for
    target destruction. The Pseudomonas aeruginosa type I-F complex, Csy, has the
    composition Cas8f(1)/Cas5f(1)/Cas7f(6)/Cas6f(1) with one 60-nucleotide crRNA;
    the type I-E equivalent is Cascade.
  proposed_parent:
    id: GO:1990904
    label: ribonucleoprotein complex
  justification: >-
    GO's entire CRISPR vocabulary is five biological-process terms (GO:0099048,
    GO:0043571, GO:0098672 and the obsolete GO:0110132 and GO:0110133), with no
    cellular-component term, so every subunit of every CRISPR surveillance
    complex can currently only be placed on the bare GO:0032991 root or on its
    generic child GO:1990904. The complex is a well-defined,
    stoichiometrically determined, structurally solved entity that ComplexPortal
    already models for the type I-E case (CPX-1005, Cascade complex), and the
    Cas7f backbone in particular is the surface targeted by several families of
    phage-encoded anti-CRISPR proteins, so a specific term would make a large
    body of structural and counter-defence curation expressible. This is the same
    request made from the Escherichia coli type I-E Cascade subunit reviews, so
    that the two systems ask for one term rather than two.
