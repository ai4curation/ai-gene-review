# PA14 identity and assay scope (PMID:24327342)

The primary article names PA14_72450 (DsbA1), PA14_59960 (DsbA2), PA14_07000 (DsbB1), and PA14_69400 (DsbB2). Frozen current UniProt records map these loci to Q02DM0, A0A0H2ZG77, Q02TM7 and Q02EA7, respectively, in UCBPP-PA14 (taxon 208963). This is not a PAO1 strain transfer.

Text S2, Plasmid constructions, states (line breaks normalized):

> amplified from the chromosome of P. aeruginosa PA14 strain

> Sequences were checked by DNA sequencing

The supplement records signal-sequence-deleted pET23a constructs for purified DsbA1 and DsbA2, expressed in BL21 and Rosetta-gami, respectively. The pET23a table specifies a C-terminal His6 tag. The A1 forward primer starts the native DDY region (residue 23 of Q02DM0); the A2 forward primer starts VELL (residue 35 of A0A0H2ZG77), after the engineered initiating methionine. These mappings support mature-chain assays rather than untouched full-length precursor assays. The additional-Cys mutant is a separate construct; wild-type biochemical evidence is not inferred from that mutant.

The main text calls the DsbB preparations partially purified after expression in an E. coli dsbB mutant. Text S2 additionally describes pET23a DsbB1/B2 preparations in BL21C43 and pBAD expression in MC1000 dsbB::kan (JFC234) for extract experiments. These are different recombinant preparations and cannot all be relabeled native PA14 membrane assays. The native PA14 redox/genetic comparisons are a separate evidence line. No exact preparation-to-figure assignment beyond the article's explicit descriptions is imposed here.

A2 catalyzes insulin reduction at 150 micromolar insulin, 10 micromolar enzyme and 0.8 mM DTT; this demonstrates protein-disulfide exchange without identifying insulin as a native substrate. A2 reoxidation by either B protein is in vitro. Absence of detectable native A2 expression and unsuccessful plasmid rescue of an A1 mutant leave its native pathway unresolved.
