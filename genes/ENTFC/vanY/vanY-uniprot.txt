CC   -!- FUNCTION: Cleaves the C-terminal D-alanine residue of UDP-muramyl-
CC       pentapeptide (UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu-mDAP-D-Ala-D-Ala) or diacetyl-L-
CC       Lys-D-Ala-D-Ala (PubMed:1510448, PubMed:9257766). However the
CC       physiological substrate likely contains L-Lys instead of mDAP at the
CC       third position of the pentapeptide (Probable). Also releases the C-
CC       terminal D-lactate from UDP-MurNAc-L-Ala-D-Glu-mDAP-D-Ala-D-lactate, a
CC       depsipeptide produced by the vancomycin resistance protein VanA.
CC       Therefore, VanY should contribute in vivo to the hydrolysis of both the
CC       D-alanyl-D-alanine- and the depsipeptide-containing peptidoglycan
CC       precursors (PubMed:1510448). Is not necessary for vancomycin resistance
CC       of E.faecium BM4147 and perhaps not W14-9 (PubMed:1398115,
CC       PubMed:9257766). Does not display transpeptidase or beta-lactamase
CC       activities (PubMed:1510448). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1398115,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:1510448, ECO:0000305}.
