CC   -!- FUNCTION: Member of the two-component regulatory system VanS/VanR
CC       (PubMed:1556077, PubMed:8494882). Functions as a sensor protein kinase
CC       which is autophosphorylated at a histidine residue in response to
CC       environmental stimuli, such as glycopeptide antibiotics
CC       (PubMed:8494882, PubMed:8664263). VanS transfers its phosphate group to
CC       transcriptional regulatory protein VanR, thereby modulating expression
CC       of target genes (PubMed:1556077, PubMed:30677074, PubMed:8161518,
CC       PubMed:8494882, PubMed:8664263). Binds directly to, and
CC       autophosphorylation activity is enhanced by, the glycopeptides
CC       vancomycin and teicoplanin, in vitro (PubMed:28511809). However it has
CC       also been reported that autophosphorylation, phosphate transfer to VanR
CC       and dephosphorylation of phospho-VanR are all unaffected by the
CC       presence of vancomycin, in vitro (PubMed:30677074). In the absence of
CC       vancomycin, negatively regulates VanR-mediated activation of vanS,
CC       vanH, vanA and vanX, probably as a result of dephosphorylating phospho-
CC       VanR (PubMed:1556077, PubMed:30677074, PubMed:8981985, PubMed:9294451).
CC       May inhibit promoter-specific DNA binding by VanR (PubMed:8161518).
CC       Involved in conferring vancomycin resistance (PubMed:1556077).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:1556077, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28511809,

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:8981985, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9294451}.
