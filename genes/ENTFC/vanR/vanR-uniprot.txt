CC   -!- FUNCTION: Member of the two-component regulatory system VanS/VanR
CC       (PubMed:1556077). Binds to the promoter regions of target genes,
CC       including vanH and vanR; phosphorylation of VanR increases binding
CC       affinity to the vanH and vanR promoters significantly (PubMed:8161518,
CC       PubMed:8494882, PubMed:8664263). DNA binding may be inhibited by the
CC       cognate sensor protein, VanS (PubMed:8161518). Activates the
CC       transcription of vanH, vanA and vanX in response to vancomycin which
CC       results in vancomycin resistance (PubMed:1556077). Involved in
CC       conferring vancomycin resistance (PubMed:1320585).
