Gene Description

DR_B0073 encodes FruA, a membrane fructose phosphotransferase-system component with two N-terminal EIIB-like domains and a C-terminal EIIC translocation domain. The membrane component supports fructose uptake coupled to phosphorylation, while the upstream cytosolic machinery determines the phosphate-donor route. Deinococcal membranes can support PEP-dependent phosphorylation with Rhodobacter capsulatus cytosol; ATP-dependent phosphorylation was reported with the older deinococcal cytosolic preparation. The 598-residue Q9RZP7 sequence is also present in ATCC13939K, which retains an intact fruB locus, but native PEP-driven uptake in that stock has not been demonstrated.

Core Functions

Provides the fructose-specific membrane phosphotransfer/translocation component. The EIIB phosphocysteine-to-fructose reaction is inferred from conserved type-2 domains and fraction-reconstitution evidence; a compatible cytosolic relay is required. The upstream phosphate donor and productive native PEP flux remain stock-dependent questions.
