Gene Description

DR_B0074 encodes a predicted soluble 1-phosphofructokinase that uses ATP to convert fructose 1-phosphate to fructose 1,6-bisphosphate. It belongs to the PfkB carbohydrate kinase family and lies beside the fructose PTS genes. The 315-residue Q9RZP6 sequence is identical to KDR_B0074 in ATCC13939K. Substrate assignment is supported by FruK-specific classifications and a curated reaction rule; direct kinetic or substrate-panel characterization of this protein has not been established.
