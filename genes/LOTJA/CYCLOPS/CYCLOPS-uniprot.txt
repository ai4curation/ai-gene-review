CC   -!- FUNCTION: Involved in symbiotic signaling. Required for root infection
CC       by symbiotic rhizobia, infection thread (IT) formation, and nodule
CC       development (PubMed:19074278). Probably not involved in nodule
CC       organogenesis (PubMed:19074278). Involved in arbuscular mycorrhizal
CC       (AM) symbiosis (PubMed:19074278). Required for fungal infection of the
CC       outer cortical cell layers, and for arbuscule development during the AM
CC       symbiosis, by binding, as a complex comprising CCaMK, CYCLOPS, and
CC       DELLA, to RAM1 promoter cis element thus promoting its expression
CC       (PubMed:19074278, PubMed:27020747). Acts downstream of CCAMK
CC       (PubMed:19074278). Binds to the promoter of ERN1 and strongly
CC       transactivates ERN1, a transcriptional regulator required for
CC       nodulation (PubMed:28503742). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:19074278,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27020747, ECO:0000269|PubMed:28503742}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Forms homodimers. Interacts with CCAMK.

CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:27020747}.
