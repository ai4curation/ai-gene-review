CC   -!- FUNCTION: ADP-riboxanase effector that mediates arginine ADP-
CC       riboxanation of host caspases (PubMed:34671164, PubMed:35338844). ADP-
CC       riboxanation of host apoptotic caspases (CASP3, CASP8 and CASP9)
CC       prevents their activation, thereby inhibiting host cell extrinsic and
CC       intrinsic apoptosis (PubMed:32657447, PubMed:35338844). Does not
CC       catalyze ADP-riboxanation of host CASP4/CASP11 (PubMed:34671164).
CC       Independently of its ADP-riboxanase activity, acts as an inhibitor of
CC       calcium signaling by inhibiting host calmodulin, preventing activation
CC       of the JAK-STAT signaling pathway in response to interferon-beta
CC       (PubMed:35568036). Mechanistically, acts by binding to the apo form of
CC       calmodulin, preventing calcium-binding and ability to activate host
CC       CaMK2 (CAMKII), which is required to stimulate the JAK-STAT signaling
CC       pathway in response to interferon-beta (PubMed:35568036).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:32657447, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34671164,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35338844, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35568036}.
