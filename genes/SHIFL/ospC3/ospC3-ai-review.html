Gene Description

OspC3 is a 484-residue effector protein encoded on the large virulence plasmid of Shigella flexneri and injected into the cytosol of infected epithelial cells by the type III secretion system. It is the best characterized of the three paralogous OspC-family effectors and has two modules: an N-terminal catalytic domain with an ADP-ribosyltransferase-like fold that binds NAD+, and a C-terminal ankyrin-repeat domain that selects the substrate. OspC3 is an arginine ADP-riboxanase (EC 4.3.99.-). It modifies a single arginine in the catalytic centre of the LPS-sensing caspases, Arg314 of human caspase-4 and Arg310 of mouse caspase-11, in two steps: NAD+-dependent ADP-ribosylation of the guanidinium nitrogen, then deamination and cyclization to an oxazolidine ring. The modified caspase can no longer autoprocess or cleave gasdermin D, so cytosolic LPS no longer triggers pyroptosis and the infected cell survives as a replicative niche; a ΔospC3 mutant provokes caspase-4-dependent epithelial cell death and is cleared better in animal infection. Catalysis depends on an unusual eukaryotic cofactor: Ca2+-free calmodulin binds the N-terminal domain, reorganizes the catalytic pocket so NAD+ can bind, and stabilizes the ternary complex with caspase-4. The same binding event has a second consequence that needs no catalysis: it holds host calmodulin in the Ca2+-free state so that it cannot activate Ca2+/calmodulin-dependent protein kinase II; interferon-induced STAT1 phosphorylation and interferon-stimulated gene expression are then lost, for interferons of all three families. OspC3 therefore makes the infected cell both pyroptosis-resistant and interferon-blind, through one host-protein interaction read two ways and one catalytic activity aimed at a single caspase arginine.

Core Functions

Calmodulin-activated arginine ADP-riboxanase acting on the LPS-sensing caspases. Using NAD+, OspC3 transfers ADP-ribose onto the guanidinium nitrogen of a single arginine in the caspase catalytic centre (Arg314 of human caspase-4, Arg310 of mouse caspase-11) and then deaminates and cyclizes it to an oxazolidine ring. The modified caspase cannot autoprocess and cannot recognize or cleave gasdermin D, so cytosolic LPS no longer triggers pyroptosis and the infected epithelial cell survives as a replicative niche. Substrate choice rests on the C-terminal ankyrin-repeat domain, which in OspC3 but not in its paralogs recognizes caspase-4/11; catalysis rests on Asp231 and Asp177 for the two half-reactions.

Knowledge Gaps

What is not known — curated, literature-grounded statements of the open unknowns (the inverse of core functions).

Gap: How OspC3 reconciles its two opposite uses of calmodulin in the same infected cell is unknown. Catalysis requires calmodulin to be bound, yet the interferon block works by denying calmodulin to the host, and the two activities place different demands on the complex: the enzyme needs turnover with caspase-4, the sequestration needs the calmodulin to stay occupied. Whether one pool of OspC3-calmodulin serves both, whether local calmodulin concentration or Ca2+ flux partitions them, and what the stoichiometry is at the effector doses delivered during infection are all open.

BIOLOGY MF_DARK

What is known: Firmly established: apo-calmodulin binds the N-terminal domain, is required for ADP-riboxanation of caspase-4/11, and is held in the Ca2+-free state so that CaMKII is not activated; the catalytic residues and the ankyrin domain are dispensable for the interferon block, and active CaMKII bypasses it.

Significance: The answer decides whether the interferon block is a side effect of recruiting a cofactor or an independently selected activity, and it bears on whether a calmodulin-interface inhibitor could disable both arms at once.

Gap: Why OspC3 inhibits human caspase-4 but, in the mouse study of cytosolic LPS sensing, not caspase-11 is unresolved, even though the biochemical work maps ADP-riboxanation to Arg310 of caspase-11 and shows that it paralyses pyroptotic defence in infected mice. Whether the discrepancy is a matter of recognition by the ankyrin domain, of effector dose or timing in the mouse cell, or of which cell types were compared has not been settled.

BIOLOGY BP_DARK

What is known: Firmly established: Arg314 of human caspase-4 and Arg310 of mouse caspase-11 are both ADP-riboxanated in vitro, the modification blocks autoprocessing and gasdermin D cleavage, and ospC3 mutation stimulates caspase-11- and gasdermin-D-dependent immunity in mice.

Significance: Shigella is a human-restricted pathogen and mouse models underpin much of the work on this axis; whether OspC3 engages caspase-11 in vivo determines how far those models report on human infection.
