CC   -!- FUNCTION: ADP-riboxanase effector that inhibits host cell pyroptosis
CC       (PubMed:23684308, PubMed:34409271, PubMed:34671164, PubMed:36624349).
CC       Acts by mediating arginine ADP-riboxanation of host CASP4/CASP11,
CC       blocking CASP4/CASP11 autoprocessing (PubMed:34671164, PubMed:35338844,
CC       PubMed:35568036, PubMed:36624349, PubMed:37014865). This prevents CASP4
CC       activation and ability to recognize and cleave GSDMD, thereby
CC       inhibiting LPS-induced pyroptosis (PubMed:34671164, PubMed:36624349).
CC       ADP-riboxanation takes place in two steps: OspC3 first catalyzes ADP-
CC       ribosylation of target Arg, and then initiates a deamination to remove
CC       one N-omega group (PubMed:34671164). Independently of its ADP-
CC       riboxanase activity, acts as an inhibitor of calcium signaling by
CC       inhibiting host calmodulin, preventing activation of the JAK-STAT
CC       signaling pathway in response to interferon-beta (PubMed:35568036).
CC       Mechanistically, acts by binding to the apo form of calmodulin,
CC       preventing calcium-binding and ability to activate host CaMK2 (CAMKII),
CC       which is required to stimulate the JAK-STAT signaling pathway in
CC       response to interferon-beta (PubMed:35568036).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:23684308, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34409271,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:34671164, ECO:0000269|PubMed:35338844,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:35568036, ECO:0000269|PubMed:36624349,
