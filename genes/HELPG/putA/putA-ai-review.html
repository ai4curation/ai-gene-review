Gene Description

PutA is a fused proline-catabolic enzyme with an FAD-dependent proline dehydrogenase region and an NAD-dependent glutamate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase region. These activities oxidize L-proline to L-glutamate through pyrroline-5-carboxylate and its ring-opened semialdehyde. The Helicobacter protein has the bifunctional catalytic architecture rather than the N-terminal ribbon-helix-helix regulatory architecture of enterobacterial trifunctional PutA proteins.
