# Curation Notes: Bacteriophage T4 `46` (Exonuclease subunit 2 / Rad50 homolog gp46, P04522)

## Overview
- **Gene symbol:** `'46'` (gp46)
- **UniProt ID:** `P04522` (`EXO2_BPT4`)
- **Length:** 560 amino acids
- **Function:** Rad50-family ABC-type ATPase subunit of the heterotetrameric T4 Mre11-Rad50 (gp47-gp46) DNA end-processing complex required for homologous recombination, double-strand break (DSB) repair, recombination-dependent DNA replication (RDR), and degradation of cytosine-containing host chromosomal DNA.

## Biological Role and Mechanism
- gp46 is an SMC/Rad50-family P-loop ATPase containing N- and C-terminal ABC ATPase segments separated by a coiled-coil region with a central CXXC zinc-hook motif.
- Together with gp47 (the Mre11-family metallophosphoesterase nuclease subunit), gp46 forms a heterotetrameric `gp46(2)-gp47(2)` Mre11-Rad50 (MR) complex.
- Alone, gp46 exhibits a low basal ATPase rate (`kcat` ~ 0.15 s^-1), which is stimulated ~20-fold (`kcat` ~ 3.2 s^-1) with positive cooperativity (Hill coefficient ~ 2.4) upon binding gp47 and dsDNA.
- ATP binding and hydrolysis by gp46 drive conformational transitions and translocation of the MR complex along DNA ends, licensing processive dsDNA exonuclease activity by gp47 (~4 nucleotides excised per ATP hydrolyzed) to generate recombinogenic 3' single-stranded DNA tails for UvsY/UvsX-mediated strand invasion and recombination-dependent replication.
- In addition to DNA repair and RDR, genes 46 and 47 are required in vivo for the conversion of cytosine-containing host DNA fragments (generated by T4 endonucleases II and IV) into acid-soluble mononucleotides used for phage hydroxymethylcytosine DNA synthesis (PubMed 4305016, PubMed 4886654, PubMed 7026800).

## Review of Existing GO Annotations
- `GO:0005524` (ATP binding, IEA, GO_REF:0000043) & `GO:0016887` (ATP hydrolysis activity, IEA, GO_REF:0000002): **ACCEPT**. Core intrinsic catalytic function of the Rad50-like gp46 subunit.
- `GO:0000166` (nucleotide binding, IEA, GO_REF:0000043): **MODIFY** to `GO:0005524` (ATP binding).
- `GO:0016787` (hydrolase activity, IEA, GO_REF:0000043): **MODIFY** to `GO:0016887` (ATP hydrolysis activity).
- `GO:0003677` (DNA binding, IEA, GO_REF:0000043): **ACCEPT**. gp46 binds DNA ends in the MR complex, which strongly stimulates its ATPase activity.
- `GO:0004527` (exonuclease activity, IEA, GO_REF:0000043): **KEEP_AS_NON_CORE**. While the assembled gp46/gp47 complex acts as an ATP-dependent dsDNA exonuclease, the nuclease active site resides in the Mre11 homolog gp47, whereas gp46 is the ATPase motor subunit that allosterically licences processive exonucleolysis.
- `GO:0004518` (nuclease activity, IEA, GO_REF:0000043): **MODIFY** to `GO:0004527` (exonuclease activity).
- `GO:0006302` (double-strand break repair, IEA, GO_REF:0000002): **ACCEPT**. Core biological role of the T4 MR complex in resecting DSBs for homologous recombination and RDR.
- `GO:0006281` (DNA repair, IEA, GO_REF:0000043) & `GO:0006974` (DNA damage response, IEA, GO_REF:0000043): **MODIFY** to `GO:0006302` (double-strand break repair).
- `GO:0099015` (degradation of host chromosome by virus, IEA, GO_REF:0000043): **ACCEPT**. Genes 46 and 47 are required for exonucleolytic degradation of cytosine-containing *E. coli* chromosomal DNA following endonucleolytic cleavage (PubMed 4305016).
- `GO:0039657` (symbiont-mediated suppression of host gene expression, IEA, GO_REF:0000043): **REMOVE**. Automated UniProt keyword mapping artifact (`Host gene expression shutoff by virus`). Host transcriptional shutoff occurs early via Alc, AsiA/MotA, and ADP-ribosyltransferases, whereas gp46/gp47 degrades fragmented host DNA later for nucleotide salvage.
