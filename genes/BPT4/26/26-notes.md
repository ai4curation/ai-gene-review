# Bacteriophage T4 Gene 26 (gp26) — Curation Notes

## Summary of Gene Function
Bacteriophage T4 gene `26` encodes the baseplate hub assembly protein gp26 (UniProt `P13335`, 208 amino acids, ~23.9 kDa). Classical T4 morphogenesis genetics placed gene 26 in the "group II" baseplate hub genes (alongside genes 5, 27, 28, 29, and 51) required for assembly of the central hub/plug of the T4 baseplate.

## Expression, Processing, and Role in Baseplate Hub Morphogenesis
- **Dual Translation Products and Proteolytic Maturation**: Gene 26 contains an internal in-frame start site producing a shorter ~10–11 kDa polypeptide (gp26\*) in addition to full-length gp26 (~24 kDa) (PubMed 3363870, PubMed 8122363). Furthermore, N-terminal Edman sequencing of gp26 isolated from purified tail-tube–baseplates identified a mature N-terminus starting at Phe6 (`F-D-V-R-V-G-S-K-I-I-N-`), demonstrating removal of the N-terminal pentapeptide (`MYEYK`) (PubMed 15342608).
- **Assembly Role vs. Structural Presence**: Mutants in gene 26 fail to assemble functional central hubs and instead accumulate 15S peripheral wedge precursors and unstable hub-less 70S baseplate-like shells. While SDS-PAGE and N-terminal sequencing of purified tube-baseplate complexes detected mature gp26 at low stoichiometry (~24 kDa band, PubMed 15342608), high-resolution cryo-EM reconstructions of the T4 baseplate did not resolve ordered density for gp26 in the final icosahedrally/hexagonally averaged hub structure (PubMed 27193680), leading to its annotation in UniProt both as a baseplate hub assembly chaperone and as a virion baseplate component.

## Annotation Review Notes
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, IEA from `GO_REF:0000043`): Directly supported by classical genetic complementation and extract morphogenesis studies showing gp26 is essential for assembling the central hub of the T4 tail baseplate (`ACCEPT`).
