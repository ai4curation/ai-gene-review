# Bacteriophage T4 Gene 29 (gp29) — Curation Notes

## Summary of Gene Function
Bacteriophage T4 gene `29` encodes the tail tape measure protein (TMP, also known as the tail length ruler protein) gp29 (UniProt `P13337`, 590 amino acids, ~64.4 kDa). It serves both as an essential component of the central baseplate hub required to initiate gp19 tail-tube polymerization and as the physical molecular ruler that determines the exact length (24 hexameric rings of gp19, ~925 Å) of the bacteriophage T4 tail tube.

## Structural Organization and Role in Tail Morphogenesis
- **Hub Incorporation and Tube Initiation**: During baseplate morphogenesis, gp29 is incorporated into the central hub (alongside group II hub components gp5, gp26, gp27, gp28, and gp51). Following completion of the hexagonal baseplate and addition of the hexameric junction rings gp48 and gp54, gp54 in association with gp29 initiates polymerization of gp19 hexamers to form the tail tube.
- **Tape Measure Mechanism**: As gp19 polymerizes, the coiled-coil rich polypeptide of gp29 extends inside the ~40-Å lumen of the growing tail tube. Tube polymerization terminates when the N-terminal end of fully extended gp29 is reached, allowing the tail completion protein gp15 to cap the distal gp19 ring. Earlier folylpolyglutamate synthetase / dihydrofolate synthase enzyme annotations for gp29 were retracted from Swiss-Prot due to lack of experimental support and structural re-assignment of gp29 as the coiled-coil tape measure protein (PubMed 15342608, PubMed 27193680).

## Annotation Review Notes
- `GO:0098003` (`viral tail assembly`, IEA from `GO_REF:0000043`): Directly supported by genetic, biochemical, and structural morphogenesis studies showing that gp29 is both an essential baseplate hub component for initiating tail tube assembly and the molecular ruler that defines tail tube length (`ACCEPT`).
