# Mup09 (Q38482) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup09` (aliases: `gp9`, `E8`, `Gene product 9`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38482` (`GP9_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 99 amino acids (11,593 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup09` (`E8`) is located in the early semi-essential (`SE`) region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `Mup08` (`E7`) and `gam` (`Mup10`/`E9`).
- It is co-transcribed in the early `Pe` operon during the early phase of the lytic cycle, negatively regulated by the lysogenic repressor Repc (`c`) and favored by Ner (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes a 99-aa soluble polypeptide (11.6 kDa) lacking signal peptides or transmembrane domains, with a notably acidic C-terminal region (`DFVWDEDV...`) immediately upstream of the DNA end-protection gene `gam`.
