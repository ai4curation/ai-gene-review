# Mup06 (Q38477) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup06` (aliases: `gp6`, `E5`, `Gene product 6`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38477` (`GP6_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 76 amino acids (8,541 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup06` (`E5`) is located in the early semi-essential (`SE`) region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669), downstream of `B` (`Mup04`) and `kil` (`Mup05`/`E4`) within the `Pe` early transcript.
- Transcription of the early operon is repressed during lysogeny by the phage repressor Repc (`c`) and modulated during lytic development by the Ner regulator (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Sequence analysis reveals a conserved 76-aa polypeptide (InterPro `IPR059686` / Pfam `PF26833`, `Phage_Mu_gp6`) lacking signal peptides or transmembrane helices. Notably, gp6 contains six cysteines arranged in three conserved cysteine/histidine motifs (`HCQCH` at residues 14–18, `CPGC` at residues 34–37, and `CCPWCS` at residues 55–60), consistent with a putative metal-binding (e.g., zinc-coordinating) or redox-active cytoplasmic factor.

## Existing GOA Annotations Review
- `GO:0030430` (`host cell cytoplasm`, `IEA`, `GO_REF:0000044`, `UniProtKB-SubCell:SL-0381`): **ACCEPT**. As a soluble 76-aa early phage protein lacking transmembrane segments or export signals, gp6 localizes to the bacterial host cytoplasm upon expression from the `Pe` operon.
