# Mup07 (Q38479) Curation Notes

## Summary

- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 87 amino acids (9,218 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup07` (`E6`) resides in the semi-essential (`SE`) early region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `Mup06` (`E5`) and `Mup08` (`E7`).
- It is co-transcribed as part of the early `Pe` operon during the early phase of the viral replicative cycle, regulated negatively by the lysogenic repressor Repc (`c`) and modulated by Ner (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes an 87-aa soluble polypeptide (9.2 kDa) with no predicted signal peptide, transmembrane helices, or known InterPro/Pfam domains.
