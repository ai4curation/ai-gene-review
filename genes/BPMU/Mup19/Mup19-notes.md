# Mup19 (Q38646) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup19` (aliases: `gp19`, `E19 protein`, `Gene product 19`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38646` (`GP19_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 120 amino acids (13,444 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup19` (`E19`) is located in the middle region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `Mup18` (`E18`) and `Mup20` (`gp20`), upstream of the late transcription activator gene `C` (`Mup21`).
- Expressed during the intermediate phase of the viral replicative cycle (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes a 120-aa multi-pass integral membrane protein (13.4 kDa) containing four predicted helical transmembrane segments (`FT   TRANSMEM` at residues 9–29, 32–52, 68–88, and 94–114). Because bacteriophage Mu is a non-enveloped tailed dsDNA phage (`Myoviridae` morphotype), gp19 integrates into the bacterial host cell membrane during infection.
