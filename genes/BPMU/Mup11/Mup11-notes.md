# Mup11 (Q9T1X7) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup11` (aliases: `gp11`, `Gene product 11`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q9T1X7` (`GP11_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 176 amino acids (19,577 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup11` (`gp11`) is encoded in the early semi-essential (`SE`) region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `gam` (`Mup10`) and `Mup12` (`E13`).
- In the earlier partial sequence of the `SE` region (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470; GenBank `M24367`), sequencing frameshifts divided this locus into three smaller open reading frames (`E10`/`E11`/`E12`, `AAA32396.1`–`AAA32398.1`), which were unified into the single 176-aa `Mup11` reading frame (`AAF01090.1`) in the complete phage Mu genome sequence.
- Contains the conserved C-terminal `Gp11_C` domain (`InterPro:IPR057850` / `Pfam:PF25754`) and lacks signal peptides or transmembrane helices.
- Expressed during the early phase of the viral replicative cycle from the `Pe` promoter, regulated by Repc and Ner.
