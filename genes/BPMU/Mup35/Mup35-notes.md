# Mup35 (Q9T1W0) — Uncharacterized protein gp35

## Summary
- **UniProt**: `Q9T1W0` (`VG35_BPMU`), 136 aa, locus tag `Mup35`.
- **Function**: Uncharacterized late-phase phage protein encoded between the major capsid gene *T* (`Mup34`) and the neck/head-tail joining gene *J* (`Mup36`). Contains an N-terminal FluMu_N domain (`PF17891` / `IPR041227`, residues 6–65) conserved across Mu-like prophages such as *Haemophilus influenzae* FluMu ([file:BPMU/Mup35/Mup35-uniprot.txt](Mup35-uniprot.txt)).
- **Regulation & Localization**: Transcribed during the late phase of the viral lytic cycle under activation by the viral late transcription activator C, and localized to the host cell cytoplasm ([file:BPMU/Mup35/Mup35-uniprot.txt](Mup35-uniprot.txt)).

## Key References
