# Mup18 (Q38625) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup18` (aliases: `gp18`, `E18`, `E18 protein`, `Gene product 18`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38625` (`GP18_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 72 amino acids (8,444 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup18` (`E18`) is encoded in the middle region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) immediately downstream of `mor` (`Mup17`, the middle operon transcriptional activator) and upstream of `Mup19` (`E19`) and `Mup20` (`gp20`).
- Expressed during the intermediate (middle) phase of the viral replicative cycle (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes a 72-aa soluble polypeptide (8.4 kDa) belonging to the conserved `Mup18` / `Phage_Mu_gp18` domain family (`InterPro:IPR059647` / `Pfam:PF26867`), lacking predicted signal peptides or transmembrane helices.
