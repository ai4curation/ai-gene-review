# Mup15 (Q9T1X4) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup15` (aliases: `gp15`, `Gene product 15`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q9T1X4` (`GP15_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 88 amino acids (9,686 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup15` (`gp15`) is located at the distal end of the early semi-essential (`SE`) region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `Mup14` (`E15`) and `gemA` (`Mup16`/`E16`).
- Transcribed during the early phase of the viral replicative cycle from the early `Pe` operon, which is repressed during latency by Repc (`c`) and favored by Ner (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes an 88-aa soluble polypeptide (9.7 kDa) lacking predicted signal peptides, transmembrane helices, or classified InterPro/Pfam domains.
