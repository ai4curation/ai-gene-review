# Mup13 (Q38490) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup13` (aliases: `gp13`, `E14`, `Gene product 13`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38490` (`GP13_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 60 amino acids (6,669 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup13` (`E14`) is located in the early semi-essential (`SE`) region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `Mup12` (`E13`) and `Mup14` (`E15`).
- Transcribed from the early promoter `Pe` during the early phase of the viral replicative cycle, repressed by Repc (`c`) during lysogeny and favored by Ner (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes a 60-aa cysteine-rich polypeptide (6.7 kDa) containing four `CxxC` motifs (`CPTC` at residues 8–11, `CPHC` at residues 21–24, `CNHC` at residues 32–35, and `CMNC` at residues 49–52), characteristic of a dual zinc-ribbon or metal-binding fold.
