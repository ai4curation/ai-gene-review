# Mup20 (Q38623) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup20` (aliases: `gp20`, `Gene product 20`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38623` (`VG20_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 39 amino acids (4,278 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup20` (`gp20`) is encoded in the middle region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) immediately downstream of `Mup19` (`E19`) and immediately upstream of `C` (`Mup21`, the late operon transcriptional activator).
- Expressed during the intermediate (middle) phase of the viral replicative cycle (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes a small 39-aa single-pass integral membrane polypeptide (4.3 kDa) with a predicted C-terminal transmembrane alpha-helix spanning residues 18–38 (`FT   TRANSMEM        18..38`).
