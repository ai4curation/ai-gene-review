CC   -!- FUNCTION: Component of the spanin complex that disrupts the host outer
CC       membrane and causes cell lysis during virus exit. The spanin complex
CC       conducts the final step in host lysis by disrupting the outer membrane
CC       after endolysin action has degraded the host peptidoglycans. Host outer
CC       membrane disruption is possibly due to local fusion between the inner
CC       and outer membrane performed by the spanin complex.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:35471081}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the spanin outer membrane subunit. Part of the
CC       spanin complex which spans the entire periplasmic space. The spanin
CC       complex is composed of spanin inner membrane subunit and spanin outer
CC       membrane subunit. {ECO:0000305|PubMed:35471081}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Host cell inner membrane {ECO:0000305}; Single-
CC       pass type II membrane protein {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expressed in the intermediate phase of the viral replicative
CC       cycle. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:2524470}.
