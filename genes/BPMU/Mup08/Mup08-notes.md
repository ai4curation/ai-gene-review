# Mup08 (Q38480) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup08` (aliases: `gp8`, `E7`, `Gene product 8`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38480` (`GP8_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 139 amino acids (16,311 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup08` (`E7`) is encoded in the early semi-essential (`SE`) region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `Mup07` (`E6`) and `Mup09` (`E8`).
- It is transcribed from the early promoter `Pe` during the early phase of the viral replicative cycle, repressed by the immunity repressor Repc (`c`) and favored by Ner (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes a 139-aa soluble polypeptide (16.3 kDa) lacking predicted transmembrane helices, signal peptides, or currently classified InterPro/Pfam domains.
