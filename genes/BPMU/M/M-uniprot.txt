CC   -!- FUNCTION: Polymerizes to make up the central tail tube that is
CC       surrounded by the tail sheath protein (TSP). Tail tube protein
CC       polymerization takes place around the tape measure protein (TMP) and is
CC       probably directed by chaperone proteins. Upon binding to host cell, the
CC       tail sheath contracts and the tail tube penetrates the host envelope.
CC       The tail tube is involved in viral genome delivery during ejection.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:9714755}.
