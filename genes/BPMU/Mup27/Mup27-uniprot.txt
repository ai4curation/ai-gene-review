CC   -!- FUNCTION: The terminase small subunit binds to the packaging initiation
CC       site (pac) and regulates the ATPase activity of the terminase large
CC       subunit. The terminase lies at a unique vertex of the procapsid and is
CC       composed of two subunits, a small terminase subunit involved in viral
CC       DNA recognition (packaging sequence), and a large terminase subunit
CC       possessing endonucleolytic and ATPase activities. Both terminase
CC       subunits heterooligomerize and are docked on the portal protein to form
CC       the packaging machine. The terminase large subunit exhibits
CC       endonuclease activity and cleaves the viral genome concatemer once the
CC       capsid is full (headful packaging). Once the capsid is packaged with
CC       the DNA, the terminase complex is substituted by neck proteins.
CC       {ECO:0000305|PubMed:14745593}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Heterodimer with the terminase large subunit gp28; the active
CC       complex is probably heterooligomeric. {ECO:0000305|PubMed:14745593}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Host cytoplasm {ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expressed in the late phase of the viral replicative cycle.
CC       Expression of late genes is activated by the viral late transcription
CC       activator C. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:8293968}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the mulikevirus small terminase family.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
