# Mup12 (Q38488) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup12` (aliases: `gp12`, `E13`, `Gene product 12`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38488` (`GP12_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 176 amino acids (19,895 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup12` (`E13`) is located in the early semi-essential (`SE`) region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `Mup11` and `Mup13` (`E14`).
- Transcribed as part of the early `Pe` operon during the early phase of the lytic cycle, repressed during latency by Repc (`c`) and favored by Ner (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes a 176-aa soluble polypeptide (19.9 kDa) classified in the conserved `Mup12` / `Phage_Mu_Gp12` domain family (`InterPro:IPR059625` / `Pfam:PF26885`).
