# Mup14 (Q38492) Curation Notes

## Summary
- **Gene symbol**: `Mup14` (aliases: `gp14`, `E15`, `Gene product 14`)
- **UniProt accession**: `Q38492` (`GP14_BPMU`)
- **Organism**: *Escherichia phage Mu* (`NCBITaxon:2681603`)
- **Length**: 100 amino acids (11,695 Da)

## Literature & Genomic Context
- `Mup14` (`E15`) is located in the early semi-essential (`SE`) region of the bacteriophage Mu genome (`AF083977`; Morgan et al. 2002, PMID:11922669) between `Mup13` (`E14`) and `Mup15` / `gemA` (`Mup16`).
- Transcribed from the early `Pe` promoter during the early phase of the viral replicative cycle, repressed during latency by Repc (`c`) and favored by Ner (Stoddard & Howe 1989, PMID:2524470).
- Encodes a 100-aa soluble polypeptide (11.7 kDa) conserved across Mu-like transposable phages (`mulikevirus gp14 protein family`).
