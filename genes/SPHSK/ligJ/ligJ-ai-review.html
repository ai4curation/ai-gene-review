Gene Description

LigJ is a zinc-dependent hydratase of the amidohydrolase superfamily (distorted (beta/alpha)8-barrel, cog2159) that catalyses the reversible syn addition of water across the C3=C4 double bond of (3Z)-2-keto-4-carboxy-3-hexenedioate (KCH) to form (4S)-4-carboxy-4-hydroxy-2-oxoadipate (CHA). The single active-site Zn(2+) polarizes the substrate C2 carbonyl, and Glu-284 acts as general base and then general acid. Originally described as a 4-oxalomesaconate (OMA) hydratase, LigJ acts preferentially on KCH, the product of the LigU 1,3-allylic isomerase, so that in sphingomonads the OMA-to-CHA conversion is carried out by LigU plus LigJ. The homodimeric enzyme is required for growth on vanillate and syringate, functioning in the lower protocatechuate 4,5-cleavage pathway that also receives gallate-derived 4-oxalomesaconate, and thereby in the bacterial catabolism of lignin-derived aromatic compounds.

Proposed New Ontology Terms

2-keto-4-carboxy-3-hexenedioate hydratase activity

Definition: Catalysis of the reaction: (3Z)-2-oxo-4-carboxy-3-hexenedioate + H2O = (2S)-2-hydroxy-4-oxobutane-1,2,4-tricarboxylate ((4S)-4-carboxy-4-hydroxy-2-oxoadipate).

Justification: LigJ catalyses RHEA:58508 (EC 4.2.1.-) in the sphingomonad protocatechuate 4,5-cleavage pathway. The related substrate-specific GO term, GO:0047584 4-oxalmesaconate hydratase activity, is defined on (1E)-4-oxobut-1-ene-1,2,4-tricarboxylate (OMA; RHEA:17401, the GalB-type reaction), whereas LigJ's preferred substrate is the LigU product KCH.

Parent term: hydro-lyase activity

Core Functions

Zinc-dependent hydration of KCH to (4S)-CHA in the lower protocatechuate 4,5-cleavage pathway that also processes gallate-derived carbon. This substrate-specific activity is distinct from GalB hydration of OMAenol. Gallate-branch participation combines the shared catalytic reaction with DesB entry evidence and syringate-pathway genetics; the original LigJ disruption study did not directly test growth on free gallate.

Identity

  • LigJ, 341 aa, amidohydrolase superfamily (cog2159; Pfam PF04909 Amidohydro_2; PANTHER
    PTHR21240 ACMSD-like), zinc-dependent hydratase. Originally named 4-oxalomesaconate (OMA)
    hydratase; now known to act on (3Z)-2-keto-4-carboxy-3-hexenedioate (KCH), the product of LigU.
  • Not homologous to the P. putida GalB OMA hydratase (genes/PSEPK/galB), which is the
    founding member of a distinct zinc hydratase family; the two are analogous enzymes for
    the equivalent pathway step.

    BRENDA files LigJ under EC 4.2.1.83).

GO term availability

  • GO:0047584 "4-oxalmesaconate hydratase activity" is defined on (1E)-4-oxobut-1-ene-1,2,4-tricarboxylate
    (OMA, the LigU substrate; RHEA:17401), i.e. the GalB reaction, not the KCH reaction.
    OLS/QuickGO searches for "hexenedioate", "oxalomesaconate" and the KCH reaction found no term.
    Hence a new term is proposed: "2-keto-4-carboxy-3-hexenedioate hydratase activity"
    (RHEA:58508; parent GO:0016836 hydro-lyase activity), and GO:0016836 is added as NEW.

Pathway / in vivo

  • PMID:11092855

  • Gallate branch: PMID:16030198
    So LigJ catalyses a step of both PCA meta-cleavage and gallate catabolism (NEW GO:0019617 and GO:0019396).

Curation decisions

  • hydrolase activity (InterPro2GO, amidohydrolase superfamily): REMOVE - LigJ adds water across a C=C bond
    (a lyase/hydratase, EC 4.2.1), it does not hydrolyse a bond.
  • carboxy-lyase activity (InterPro2GO, ACMSD family IPR032465): REMOVE - no decarboxylation; the
    ACMSD-family mapping is an over-propagation of the decarboxylase subfamilies' activity.
  • secondary metabolic process (TreeGrafter): MODIFY -> lignin catabolic process (its descendant, already on the gene).
  • lignin catabolic process (UniPathway): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE.
  • cytoplasm (TreeGrafter): KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (soluble enzyme; no direct localization study).
