Gene Description

LigK is a Mg(2+)-dependent class II pyruvate aldolase of the LigK/PcmE (RraA-like fold) family that catalyses the last step of the protocatechuate 4,5-cleavage (meta-cleavage) pathway: the retro-aldol cleavage of 4-carboxy-4-hydroxy-2-oxoadipate (CHA) to pyruvate and oxaloacetate. The homohexameric enzyme also decarboxylates oxaloacetate to pyruvate and CO2 at a lower rate, and can condense two pyruvate molecules to 4-hydroxy-4-methyl-2-oxoglutarate (HMG), the reverse of the HMG aldolase reaction. Because protocatechuate (from vanillate/guaiacyl units) and gallate or 3-O-methylgallate (from syringate/syringyl units) all converge on 4-oxalomesaconate and then CHA, LigK is essential for growth on vanillate and syringate and channels the carbon of lignin-derived aromatic compounds into central metabolism. It is the sphingomonad counterpart of the GalC CHA aldolase of gallate-degrading pseudomonads.

ligK (G2IQQ8, Sphingobium sp. SYK-6) - curator notes

Identity

  • LigK = 4-carboxy-4-hydroxy-2-oxoadipate (CHA) aldolase / oxaloacetate decarboxylase,
    228 aa, LigK/PcmE family (TIGR02798; RraA-like fold, PF03737). PANTHER PTHR33254:SF16,
    the same subfamily as P. putida GalC (genes/PSEPK/galC).
  • Strain name history: SYK-6 was originally Sphingomonas paucimobilis SYK-6, now
    Sphingobium sp. SYK-6.

Function (experimental)

  • CHA aldolase: PMID:12486039
  • Oxaloacetate decarboxylase (secondary): PMID:12486039
    The CHA reaction is much faster: PMID:12486039
  • Metal dependence / class II aldolase: PMID:12486039
  • Oligomer: PMID:12486039
  • Product inhibition: PMID:12486039
  • HMG formation from 2 pyruvate is attributed by UniProt to PMID:29658701 (abstract-only in cache; abstract does not mention LigK)
    [file:SPHSK/ligK/ligK-uniprot.txt "Is also able to catalyze the formation of 4-hydroxy-4-methyl-2-oxoglutarate (HMG) from two molecules of pyruvate"].

Pathway role

  • Terminal step of the PCA 4,5-cleavage pathway: PMID:12486039
  • Essential in vivo: PMID:12486039
  • Syringate-derived carbon (3-O-methylgallate, gallate) converges on OMA:
    PMID:12486039
    and DesB converts gallate to OMA PMID:16030198
    Hence LigK also performs the last step of gallate catabolism in this organism (NEW GO:0019396),
    matching GalC's GO:0019396 in P. putida.
  • Upstream sphingomonad route OMA -> KCH (LigU) -> CHA (LigJ):
    PMID:29658701

Curation decisions

  • GO:0047443 (HMG aldolase; definition includes the CHA reaction) = core MF, ACCEPT all three rows.
  • GO:0008948 oxaloacetate decarboxylase = real but secondary -> KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (both rows).
  • GO:0019617 PCA meta-cleavage IDA = ACCEPT (core BP).
  • GO:0046274 lignin catabolic process (UniPathway IEA) = KEEP_AS_NON_CORE (acts on lignin-monomer-derived
    intermediates, not the polymer).
  • Proposed CHA-specific aldolase term (same proposed_name as galC review).
