CC   -!- FUNCTION: Assembly protein part of the packaging complex that packages
CC       the viral RNA segments, replicate them into a double-stranded form and
CC       transcribe them. Required for efficient procapsid assembly. Necessary
CC       for stable packaging. May stabilize the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase
CC       (RdRP) in its position at the three-fold axis on the inner side of
CC       empty-unexpanded procapsids. Could play a role in viral RNA
CC       recognition. Seems to be involved in the regulation of plus strand
CC       synthesis (transcription) as a fidelity factor.
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:18793644, ECO:0000269|PubMed:22896624,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:23077625, ECO:0000269|PubMed:9086268}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Homodimer. Part of the packaging complex composed of RDRP, P4
CC       and P7. Interacts with RDRP (Probable). {ECO:0000305|PubMed:18793644,
CC       ECO:0000305|PubMed:23077625}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Virion {ECO:0000305}. Note=Found in the capsid
