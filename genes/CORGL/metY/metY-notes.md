# metY (Q79VI4, Corynebacterium glutamicum ATCC 13032) notes

## 2026-11-13 review

- Core activity: O-acetylhomoserine sulfhydrylase (GO:0051009). Expression of cloned metY raised
  activity 30-fold [PMID:11844756 "a 30-fold increase in O-acetylhomoserine sulfhydrylase activity"];
  purified enzyme characterized kinetically [PMID:18050920 "MetB (cystathionine gamma-synthase) and
  MetY (O-acetylhomoserine sulfhydrylase) from C. glutamicum were purified to homogeneity"].
- Parallel routes: metY and metB single mutants are prototrophs, the double mutant is a methionine
  auxotroph [PMID:11844756 "an additional mutation in the metY or the metB gene resulted in methionine
  auxotrophy"].
- Secondary activity: methanethiol/dimethyldisulfide are added to O-acetylhomoserine to give
  methionine directly; this is the GO:0003961 reaction, kept as non-core [PMID:20798582 "Both sulfur
  compounds are incorporated as entire molecule, adding the terminal S-CH3 group to
  O-acetylhomoserine"].
