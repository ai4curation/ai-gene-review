Gene Description

Corynebacterium glutamicum ProA is a soluble cytoplasmic gamma-glutamyl phosphate reductase that uses NADPH to reduce L-glutamyl 5-phosphate to L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde. The latter cyclizes spontaneously to pyrroline-5-carboxylate, a precursor of proline. ProA supplies the reductive step of the glutamate route; a bypass observed in another C. glutamicum strain does not invalidate the canonical enzyme.
