Gene Description

Corynebacterium glutamicum ProB is a soluble cytoplasmic glutamate 5-kinase that transfers phosphate from ATP to L-glutamate, producing L-glutamyl 5-phosphate for the first step of proline biosynthesis. ProB supplies the initiating phosphorylation step of the glutamate route to proline.
