## Direct observations and limits

The PDF methods specify B.flavum No2247; it is not the reviewed ATCC13032 strain. The preparation is partial purification by ammonium sulfate and gel filtration; FractionIII contains residual Asd, dihydrodipicolinate synthetase and NADPH oxidase. The standard reaction mixture contains ASA and NADPH. The paper reports NADPH as the first binding substrate and NADP+ as the last releasing product. It establishes NADPH reaction chemistry and threonine feedback in the characterized strain, not target-specific NADH catalytic efficiency.

PMID:1743520 is a distinct1991 study of C.glutamicum Hom regulation. Its available abstract states wild-type threonine inhibition and a C-terminal mutant phenotype but does not identify the strain or compare NAD/NADP. The full publisher endpoint returned403. PMID:2835591 is the hom-thrB operon paper; the reviewed record assigns that sequence source to ATCC13059. The current target record is ATCC13032 and also includes complete-genome sources. These identities are not collapsed into one native purified-enzyme experiment.

The conservative supported target function is homoserine dehydrogenase with NADPH chemistry, supported by family plus corynebacterial biochemical evidence. NAD-linked activity/binding remains unresolved. No source record was edited, and no native NADH assay or lack of NADH activity was invented.
