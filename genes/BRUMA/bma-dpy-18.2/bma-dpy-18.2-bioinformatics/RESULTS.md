# Bma-PHY-1 Sequence Identity

## 2026-11-21

Command:

```bash
./verify_phy1_identity.sh
```

Result:

```text
A0A1P6BZ71 length: 541 aa
AJ297845.1 CDS translation length: 541 aa; identity to A0A1P6BZ71: 541/541 (100.0%)
AJ421993.1 CDS translation length: 541 aa; identity to A0A1P6BZ71: 541/541 (100.0%)
```

AJ297845.1 and AJ421993.1 both encode 541-aa CDS translations that are
identical to the 541-aa A0A1P6BZ71 UniProt sequence.

The `AJ421993.1` NCBI record is the *Brugia malayi* `phy-1` genomic record, and
the identical `AJ297845.1` mRNA record is the Bma-PHY-1 transcript cloned and
biochemically characterized by PMID:12417582.
