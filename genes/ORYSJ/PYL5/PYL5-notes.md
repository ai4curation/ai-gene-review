# Rice PYL5 / RCAR5 curation notes

## 2026-10-25

Q6I5C3 is PYL5/RCAR5 at Os05g0213500 (LOC_Os05g12260). He et al. 2014 (PMID:24743650) explicitly maps RCAR5 to OsPYL11 in that paper's numbering. Tian et al. 2015 (PMID:26362328) uses a different numbering scheme: its OsPYL5 is the target, whereas its OsPYL11–13 are other sequence models. Names alone cannot identify the protein across these publications.

The receptor has two experimentally reconstituted signaling contexts: RCAR5–PP2C30–SAPK2–OREB1 in PMID:22071266 and RCAR5–PP2C6–SAPK9–BZIP46 in PMID:26300907. These are alternative tested combinations, not evidence that all rice kinases or PP2Cs are interchangeable.

The biochemical and cell-based assays differ in basal receptor activity. [PMID:24743650, "OsPYL10, 11 largely exist as monomers and can inhibit OsPP2C06 phosphatase activity without ABA."] Therefore receptor inhibition cannot be represented as universally requiring exogenous ABA. The protoplast reporter nevertheless shows ABA/concentration-dependent rescue.

All 29 annotations were reviewed. Protein-phosphatase inhibitor activity is refined to the serine/threonine-specific term; cytoplasm is refined to cytosol. Both broad IBA assertions remain biologically supported by PTN001581255 in the cached PAINT table; the refinement reflects stronger target-specific evidence, not a phylogenetic failure. The target appearing among localization donors is not circularity. Stress and developmental phenotypes are retained as non-core consequences of receptor function. Generic protein binding is removed as uninformative.


Research method: reviewed the cached publications and the accession-specific curated UniProt record, checked original GOA reference identities, and used primary rice experiments rather than transferring Arabidopsis phenotypes. Provider research was not relaunched because this same curation session had already established Falcon authentication failure and Perplexity fallback insufficient quota; manual notes are used instead of fabricated provider reports. Protein-binding actions follow the project policy and do not deny observed interactions. Source-generated annotation identity fields were retained.
