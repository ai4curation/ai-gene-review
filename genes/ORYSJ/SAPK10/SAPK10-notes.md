# Rice SAPK10 curation notes

## 2026-10-25

Q75H77 is SAPK10 at Os03g0610900 (LOC_Os03g41460), PANTHER PTHR24343:SF509. It is an ABA-responsive subgroup III SnRK2. This review keeps it separate from the SAPK9 transcriptional relay.

PMID:15084714 establishes subgroup-specific ABA activation. The full-text guard-cell study [PMID:31134357, "SAPK10 phosphorylated OsSLAC1 in vitro"] supplies direct kinase-substrate evidence; guard-cell expression and transgenic water-loss results support a water-conservation role. PP2C50/PP2C53 regulate this branch; their direct interactions with SLAC1 mean the wiring is not necessarily a single unbranched chain. PMID:26491145 provides ABIL2–SAPK10 interaction context and curated nuclear/cytosolic locations; PMID:28827170 characterizes the PP2C50 receptor-interface context.

All 14 annotations were reviewed. Generic kinase activity is refined to serine/threonine kinase activity; uninformative protein-binding rows are removed without denying PP2C contacts. Catalytic activity, ATP binding, nuclear/cytosolic locations and ABA signaling are accepted. No NEW process term is manufactured from a water-loss phenotype alone.


Research method: reviewed the cached publications and the accession-specific curated UniProt record, checked original GOA reference identities, and used primary rice experiments rather than transferring Arabidopsis phenotypes.
