# Rice SAPK9 curation notes

## 2026-10-25

Q75V57 is SAPK9 at Os12g0586100 (LOC_Os12g39630), PANTHER PTHR24343:SF326. This is a subgroup III SnRK2, not SAPK2. Direct ten-member kinase comparison states [PMID:15084714, "only SAPK8, SAPK9, and SAPK10 were activated by ABA"]. The same experiment also activates the family with hyperosmotic stress, so ABA responsiveness is not exclusivity to ABA.

PMID:26300907 reconstitutes PP2C6–SAPK9–BZIP46 reporter control and localizes the PP2C6–SAPK9 complex to nucleus and cytosol. PMID:22301130 and its curated UniProt statement establish BZIP46 phosphorylation; the primary full-text retrieval returned an abstract-only cache, which is not grounds to deny the curated biochemical result. PMID:34343171 independently demonstrates SAPK9 phosphorylation of OsMADS23 and activation of ABA-biosynthesis feedback.

All ten annotations were reviewed. Generic kinase activity and the BZIP46 binding row are refined to the supported kinase activity; cytoplasm is refined to cytosol. ABA signaling, nuclear localization, ATP binding and serine/serine-threonine kinase activities are retained. No stomatal-channel substrate is transferred from SAPK10 to SAPK9.


Research method: reviewed the cached publications and the accession-specific curated UniProt record, checked original GOA reference identities, and used primary rice experiments rather than transferring Arabidopsis phenotypes. Provider research was not relaunched because this same curation session had already established Falcon authentication failure and Perplexity fallback insufficient quota; manual notes are used instead of fabricated provider reports. Protein-binding actions follow the project policy and do not deny observed interactions. Source-generated annotation identity fields were retained.
