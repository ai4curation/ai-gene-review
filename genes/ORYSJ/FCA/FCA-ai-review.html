Gene Description

Rice FCA (OsFCA) is a nuclear protein containing two RNA recognition motifs and a WW interaction domain. It interacts with OsFY and other RNA-processing proteins, supporting an RNA-regulatory role related to that of Arabidopsis FCA. OsFCA transcripts undergo alternative processing. Rice FCA partially complements late flowering in Arabidopsis fca mutants without restoring FLC repression or Arabidopsis FCA autoregulation. Overexpression of isolated rice FCA RNA-binding domains delays flowering and increases cell and seed size. These results distinguish conserved RNA-processing interactions from the downstream flowering circuitry in the two plants.

Suggested Questions for Experts

Q: Which native rice RNAs bind full-length OsFCA, and does OsFCA participate directly in spliceosomal assemblies or instead interact transiently with their regulators?

Q: Do endogenous OsFCA loss-of-function alleles alter heading date and seed/cell size in the same direction as isolated RRM overexpression?

Suggested Experiments
