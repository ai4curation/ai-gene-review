CC   -!- FUNCTION: Plays a major role in the promotion of the transition of the
CC       vegetative meristem to reproductive development (PubMed:16240176).
CC       Required for RNA-mediated chromatin silencing of a range of loci in the
CC       genome. Cotranscriptionally recognizes aberrant RNA and marks it for
CC       silencing. Controls alternative cleavage and polyadenylation on pre-
CC       mRNAs and antisense RNAs (By similarity). Regulates flowering time,
CC       seed size and cell volume, probably via the modulation of cell size
CC       (PubMed:17597396). {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:O04425,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:16240176, ECO:0000269|PubMed:17597396}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with FY (PubMed:16820865, PubMed:18854333,
CC       PubMed:19561057). Binds to SF1, FIK, RPRD1B,
CC       Os09g0509000/LOC_Os09g33480 and MADS8 (PubMed:19561057).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:16820865, ECO:0000269|PubMed:18854333,
