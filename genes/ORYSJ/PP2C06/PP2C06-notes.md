## 2026-10-25

Q0JLP9 is PP2C06/ABIL1 at Os01g0583100 (LOC_Os01g40094). It is distinct from ABIL2/PP2C53. PMID:26491145 foregrounds ABIL2, but UniProt's target-specific comparative location annotations must not be rejected from that title or abstract. Independent reconstitution in PMID:26300907 directly observes the PP2C6–SAPK9 complex in nucleus and cytosol and supports the same locations.

The source words support the tested functional connection; they do not establish that every inhibitory effect measured in protoplasts was direct dephosphorylation of the kinase.
