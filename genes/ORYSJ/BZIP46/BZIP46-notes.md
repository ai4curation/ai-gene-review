# Rice BZIP46 / ABF2 / ABL1 curation notes

## 2026-10-25

Q69TW5 at Os06g0211200 (LOC_Os06g10880) is BZIP46, also ABF2, ABF3, ABL1 and AREB8. This is not the PP2C protein ABIL1. Primary papers using OsABF2 (PMID:20576316), ABL1 (PMID:21546455) and BZIP46 (PMID:22301130) concern this locus.

[PMID:21546455, "The ABL1 protein is localized to the nucleus and can directly bind ABA-responsive elements"] supports the nuclear ABRE-binding activity. Reporter activation and endogenous gene responses establish a positive transcriptional role. Native overexpression and the domain-D-deleted constitutively active form give different drought results in PMID:22301130; phosphorylation and de-repression matter. PMID:27468891 identifies MODD-mediated repression/degradation, not a repressive transcriptional function intrinsic to BZIP46.

All 19 annotations were reviewed. Generic TF activity is refined to RNA polymerase II-specific DNA-binding transcription activator activity; broad transcription regulation is refined to the positive term already present. Nuclear and ABRE-binding assertions are accepted. Stress phenotypes are retained as non-core consequences. Kinase/MODD contacts remain documented while the uninformative protein-binding rows are removed.
