## Sources and sequence identity

[PMID:31134357, Two Clade A Phosphatase 2Cs Expressed in Guard Cells Physically Interact With Abscisic Acid Signaling Components to Induce Stomatal Closure in Rice](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6536566/): full text inspected. OsSLAC1-GFP colocalizes with PM-mCherry in rice protoplasts; promoter-GUS gives guard-cell expression; rice overexpression reduces water loss. SAPK10 interaction is supported by BiFC and co-IP; in vitro phosphorylation uses the N-terminal OsSLAC1 fragment. PP2C50/PP2C53 co-IP uses N-terminal constructs. Neither interaction alone establishes a phosphatase catalytic reaction on intact SLAC1.

The article body prints Os04g18530, apparently inconsistent with the channel locus. Parent inspected supplementary primer workbook: promoter primer is named pOs04g48530-F, and full-length forward primer CACCATGGCAGCAAAGCCGTCCTC contains the coding N-terminus MAAKPS matching Q7XTX7 residues 1–6. The retained [bioinformatics results](SLAC1-bioinformatics/RESULTS.md) also match the reverse primer to residues 570–578 (DKEEEEKTE), and six independent internal boundaries to the expected positions. SAPK10 matches both termini as a positive control; AK106615/BAG97777.1 has no peptide match of at least five residues. This establishes construct consistency, not full-length insert identity or genome-wide uniqueness. The experimental sequence evidence takes precedence over a body locus string.

[PMID:26481009, Protein kinase OsSAPK8 functions as an essential activator of S-type anion channel OsSLAC1, which is nitrate-selective in rice](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00425-015-2418-x): abstract and publisher preview inspected; full text remains unavailable. The abstract directly establishes nitrate selectivity with no obvious permeability to chloride, malate or sulfate, SAPK8 interaction and activating phosphorylation at S129, and rescue of Arabidopsis slac1-3 drought hypersensitivity. It does not establish every reported voltage-dependent parameter; no new voltage-gating claim is inferred merely from the presence of electrophysiology or current–voltage plots.

[PMID:22915747, Increased leaf photosynthesis caused by elevated stomatal conductance in a rice mutant deficient in SLAC1, a guard cell anion channel protein](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3444276/): valid citation, but printed Os04g0674700/LOC_Os04g57850 and AK106615 identifiers conflict with the current channel locus. The latter maps to an AMP-ligase annotation in the identity investigation. This is not yet evidence that the experiment assayed the wrong protein; accession strings may be reporting errors. The paper is not used to support Q7XTX7 functional decisions pending construct/sequence reconciliation. Constant-light growth is not a direct light-response experiment.

## Ontology and row decisions

All 14 original rows independently reviewed. Direct plasma-membrane evidence supports ACCEPT. Broad membrane and endomembrane rows are refined to plasma membrane. QuickGO definition of GO:0012505 includes cell membrane, even though GO:0005886 has no formal descendant path beneath it; the correction does not allege that the source proved a distinct internal compartment.

Nitrate is a polyatomic anion. QuickGO on 2026-10-25 confirms GO:0008308 and GO:0008509 carry monoatomic-anion labels (their prose definitions still retain broad anion wording), while GO:0098656 explicitly defines a negatively charged ion consisting of exactly one atom. These claims are refined to GO:0015112 nitrate transmembrane transporter activity and GO:0015706 nitrate transmembrane transport. GO:0015267 channel activity captures the demonstrated channel mechanism without a monoatomic-substrate restriction. Inorganic-anion transport and general transmembrane transport are refined to nitrate transport. This is a substrate-specific correction based on rice experiments, not a rejection of channel-family membership.

GO:0010542 nitrate efflux transmembrane transporter activity was considered but not used: its ontology ancestry includes secondary active transport. The direction and mechanism must not be changed to fit a label. GO:0022832 voltage-gated channel activity is substrate-neutral but not newly asserted because the available abstract does not independently establish voltage-dependent gating.

Monoatomic-ion homeostasis is MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED: a nitrate pore and downstream changes in guard-cell potassium flux do not establish direct maintenance of a steady-state monoatomic-ion level. Light response remains UNDECIDED. Protein kinase/phosphatase binding are retained as non-core regulatory interactions, not converted to kinase or phosphatase regulator activities. Closure and regulation of closure are retained based on the inherited effector role, native rice guard-cell/overexpression data, and complementation. One Arabidopsis donor does not weaken the PAINT ancestral assertion; no lineage-specific loss of stomatal function is alleged.

No NEW process term is proposed. The nitrate-specific replacements describe the transport step executed by the channel itself; the closure terms already exist in GOA. The core nitrate-transporter function retains the demonstrated channel mechanism in its description, with plasma-membrane location.
