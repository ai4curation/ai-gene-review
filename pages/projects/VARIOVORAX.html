Variovorax: Rhizosphere Plant–Microbe Interactions & Critical Metal Recovery

Overview

Members of the betaproteobacterial genus Variovorax (NCBITaxon:34072, family Comamonadaceae) are metabolically versatile "hub" bacteria in terrestrial and rhizosphere ecosystems. Across synthetic community (SynCom) plant microbiome studies and bio-mining/bioremediation surveys, Variovorax stands out for two tightly linked capabilities:

  1. Keystone Rhizosphere Chemical Signaling & Plant Growth Promotion:
  2. Auxin (IAA) Homeostasis (iad Operon): In complex root microbiomes, diverse bacteria secrete indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) or stimulate host auxin/ethylene signaling, triggering severe root growth inhibition (RGI). A single genus — Variovorax — is both necessary and sufficient to reverse microbiome-induced RGI across phylogenetically diverse bacterial communities via a conserved indole-3-acetic acid degradation (iad) operon (Finkel et al. 2020, PMID:32999461; Conway et al. 2022, PMID:36266335; Ma et al. 2023, PMID:37459330).
  3. Ethylene Stress Buffering (acdS) & Nitrile/Amide Crosstalk (amiE): Variovorax strains co-express pyridoxal 5'-phosphate (PLP)-dependent 1-aminocyclopropane-1-carboxylate (ACC) deaminase (acdS), which cleaves the immediate precursor of plant ethylene into 2-oxobutanoate and ammonia, dampening stress-induced ethylene spikes in colonized roots (Han et al. 2011, PMID:21183664; Finkel et al. 2020, PMID:32999461). Concurrently, aliphatic amidase (amiE) hydrolyzes rhizosphere short-chain aliphatic amides into organic acids and bioavailable ammonium.

  4. Organosulfur & Urea Nutrient Mobilization (ssuA2, ssuB2, ssuC5, ssuD, ssuE, tauA, tauB1, tauC, tauD1, acd, ureA, ureB, ureC, ureD, ureE, ureF, ureG): Under sulfate limitation in the rhizosphere, Variovorax paradoxus TBEA6 (VARPD) imports aliphatic sulfonates via the SsuABC ABC transporter (ssuA2 VPARA_09270, ssuB2 VPARA_09230, ssuC5 VPARA_09240) and mineralizes them via the $\text{FMNH}_2$-dependent alkanesulfonate monooxygenase (ssuD VPARA_09250 in TBEA6; curated Swiss-Prot ortholog ssuD Vapar_2976 / C5CNW9 in V. paradoxus S110 VARPS) paired with the NAD(P)H-dependent FMN reductase (ssuE VPARA_09290), while importing taurine via the TauABC ABC transporter (tauA VPARA_03650, tauB1 VPARA_03660, tauC VPARA_03670) for oxidative cleavage by the $\text{Fe(II)}$/2-oxoglutarate-dependent taurine dioxygenase (tauD1 VPARA_23370, EC 1.14.11.17) and cleaving 3-sulfinopropanoyl-CoA via 3-sulfinopropanoyl-CoA desulfinase (acd VPARA_05440, EC 3.13.1.4) to supply inorganic sulfite and propionyl-CoA (van der Ploeg et al. 1996, PMID:8808933; Eichhorn et al. 1997, PMID:9287300; Eichhorn et al. 1999, PMID:10498703; Bruland et al. 2009, PMID:19001372; Schürmann et al. 2013, PMID:24317404). Simultaneously, cytoplasmic nickel-dependent urease — assembled as a $(\text{UreABC})_3$ trimer of heterotrimers (ureA Vapar_4240, ureB Vapar_4242, ureC Vapar_4243, EC 3.5.1.5) and matured by the UreD-UreF-UreG activation complex (ureD Vapar_3971, ureF VPARA_50930, ureG Vapar_4248) and nickel metallochaperone UreE (ureE VPARA_50940) — hydrolyzes urea into ammonium and carbonate/bicarbonate, supplying assimilable nitrogen and elevating local pH to promote microbially induced carbonate precipitation (Park et al. 1994, PMID:7909161; Soriano & Hausinger 1999, PMID:10500143; Carter et al. 2009, PMID:20046957; Han et al. 2011, PMID:21183664).

  5. PQQ Biosynthesis, Quinoprotein Oxidation, TonB-ExbB-ExbD Siderophore Import & Heavy-Metal Homeostasis:

  6. Coenzyme PQQ Biosynthesis (pqqA, pqqB, pqqC, pqqD, pqqE) & Periplasmic Quinoprotein Dehydrogenases (gcd, VPARA_00790): Variovorax paradoxus synthesizes pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ) de novo from a ribosomally synthesized precursor peptide (pqqA, Vapar_3057) via the radical SAM peptide cross-linker pqqE (VPARA_00920) and its peptide chaperone pqqD (VPARA_00930), the $\text{Fe(II)}$-dependent oxygenase/hydroxylase pqqB (VPARA_00950), and the cofactorless ring-closing oxidase pqqC (VPARA_00940) (Klinman & Bonnot 2014, PMID:24050525; Latham et al. 2015, PMID:25667491). PQQ powers membrane-bound glucose dehydrogenase (gcd, VPARA_65850) for periplasmic D-gluconate production (mineral acidolysis) as well as periplasmic PQQ-dependent alcohol/methanol dehydrogenase (VPARA_00790, EC 1.1.2.7), a candidate lanthanide-associated XoxF/ExaF-like dehydrogenase (Keltjens et al. 2014, PMID:24912846; Good et al. 2016, PMID:27285220).
  7. TonB-ExbB-ExbD-Energized Ferric-Siderophore Import (exbD5, exbB4, tonB, bfrD1), Ferric Uptake Regulation (fur1) & Cytosolic Iron Trafficking (fetP): Encoded in the syntenic four-gene exbD5-exbB4-tonB-bfrD1 (VPARA_25800-VPARA_25830) operon, the inner-membrane pentameric proton-channel stator exbB4 (VPARA_25810, A0A0H2MHH3) and dimeric proton-coupling Asp23 rotor exbD5 (VPARA_25800, A0A0H2M1F3) harvest the cytoplasmic-membrane proton-motive force and energize the periplasm-spanning transducer tonB (VPARA_25820, A0A0H2M2E3) to open the outer-membrane TonB-dependent ferric-siderophore receptor bfrD1 (VPARA_25830, A0A0H2M677) for uptake of ferric-siderophore chelates (Noinaj et al. 2010, PMID:20420522; Johnston et al. 2015, PMID:26412281; Braun et al. 2023, PMID:37219427), while the cytosolic $\text{Fe}^{2+}$-sensing metalloregulator fur1 (VPARA_18200, A0A0H2M8T9) represses iron-acquisition operons under iron sufficiency and the YggX-family protein fetP (Vapar_2982, C5CNX5) mediates intracellular $\text{Fe}^{2+}$ trafficking.
  8. Intracellular Polyphosphate Turnover (ppk, ppx) & Heavy-Metal Efflux and Two-Component Regulation (czcA3, czcB, czcC, czcD, cadA2, czcI, czcR, cusS): Polyphosphate kinase 1 (ppk, VPARA_28600, A0A0H2M4T4) polymerizes the terminal $\gamma$-phosphate of ATP into long-chain inorganic polyphosphate (polyP) while exopolyphosphatase (ppx, VPARA_10490, A0A0H2MBK5, EC 3.6.1.11) processively hydrolyzes terminal orthophosphate residues to balance intracellular polyP turnover (Nikel et al. 2013, PMID:23687963). Across the cell envelope, the inner-membrane CDF antiporter czcD (VPARA_15480, A0A0H2M558) and the P1B-2-type $\text{Zn}^{2+}$/$\text{Cd}^{2+}$-exporting ATPase cadA2 (VPARA_44980, A0A0H2LVW9, EC 7.2.2.12) export cytoplasmic heavy-metal cations into the periplasm, while the tripartite HME-RND efflux complex — inner-membrane RND antiporter czcA3 (VPARA_44890, A0A0H2MBF7), periplasmic 10-His membrane-fusion adaptor czcB (VPARA_44900, A0A0H2LX82), and outer-membrane factor channel czcC (VPARA_44910, A0A0H2M0U4) — extrudes divalent heavy-metal cations across the outer membrane under the control of the periplasmic modulator czcI (VPARA_44920, A0A0H2LVR9) and the czcR-cusS (VPARA_44930-VPARA_44940, A0A0H2LVW5/A0A0H2MBG2) two-component regulatory system (Grosse et al. 1999, PMID:10198000; Anton et al. 1999, PMID:10559151; Legatzki et al. 2003, PMID:12867443; Nies 2003, PMID:12829273).
