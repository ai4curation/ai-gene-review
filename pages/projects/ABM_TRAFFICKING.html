Agent-Based Modeling of Intracellular Trafficking

Bottom line: Boolean networks represent pathway activities as binary ON/OFF states and cannot conserve discrete cargo particles or quantify how competing sorting branches split flux. This project extends the Executable Models architecture ("the module is the model") to Agent-Based Modeling (model_type: AGENT_BASED, derivation: DERIVED_FROM_MODULE): module_to_abm() compiles a curated ModuleReview YAML directly into a particle-conserving cohort simulator ($N = 100, T = 24$) with no external rule files, and just validate-modules checks every perturbation scenario (expected_active, expected_inactive, expected_outcome_order) in CI. Why trafficking needs agents rather than Boolean states

A Boolean network represents each module activity as a binary truth value (ON or OFF). That abstraction fits catalytic signaling cascades (erk_cascade, jak_stat_signaling), where an active kinase broadcasts a signal downstream and the biological question is whether the network settles into a fixed point or a limit cycle.

Intracellular trafficking poses a fundamentally different question: given a finite cohort of discrete cargo particles entering at the plasma membrane, how does the cohort partition across competing intracellular destinations over time?

Biological phenomenon Boolean network (BOOLEAN) Agent-Based Trafficking (AGENT_BASED)
Cargo conservation No conservation: every downstream branch can turn ON simultaneously Strict particle conservation: $\sum_i n_i(t) = N_{\text{agents}}$ at every step $t$
Competing branch flux (early_endosome $\to$ recycling vs. late endosome vs. cytosolic escape) All reachable branches evaluate to 1 unless mutually exclusive logic is hard-coded Competing outflow transitions split the compartment's particle pool proportionally to their active transition rates
Knockout of a dominant sink (e.g., Rab7 loss in PMID:41814093) Sets the escrt_rab7_late_endosome bit to 0; cannot quantify how much trapped cargo is diverted to escape vs. recycling Relieves competing-pathway attenuation and diverts trapped early-endosomal cargo into cytosolic_cargo_escape ($10\% \to 47\%$) and ehd1_recycling_branch ($47\% \to 53\%$)
Downstream enzymatic block (e.g., LIPA loss in Wolman disease or CTSD loss in CLN10) Sets the terminal hydrolysis bit to 0; upstream nodes remain 1 indistinguishably from wild type Cargo physically accumulates in the immediately upstream endolysosomal compartment (escrt_rab7_late_endosome or ldlr_endocytosis)

How a module compiles into an Agent-Based Model

In src/ai_gene_review/module_abm.py, module_to_abm() compiles a ModuleReview YAML document directly into an ABMModel with no external rule files:

  1. Compartments (ABMStage): Every leaf activity (AtomicActivity) or complex (ProteinComplex) that participates in at least one resolved module connection (with containers expanded to their constituent leaves via PART_OF) becomes a discrete stage.
  2. Entry stages: Stages with outgoing cargo flow (PROVIDES_INPUT_FOR / CAUSES / HAS_INPUT) and no incoming cargo flow (e.g., tfrc_cargo_binding or apob_ligand; falling back to the first flow stage if all flow stages have incoming flow) split the initial cohort of $N = 100$ cargo particles at $t = 0$.
  3. Pure regulators (is_flow_stage = False): Stages that only emit or receive regulatory edges (POSITIVELY_REGULATES / NEGATIVELY_REGULATES) and have no incoming or outgoing flow edges (e.g., pcsk9_degradation in ldl_receptor_mediated_endocytosis) are flagged with is_flow_stage = False and default_active = False. They act as regulatory switches rather than cargo-holding compartments.
  4. Flow transitions (ABMTransition): Every PROVIDES_INPUT_FOR, CAUSES, or HAS_INPUT connection between stages becomes a directed particle transition with a uniform uncalibrated default base rate:
  5. Uniform base transition rate: $r_{\text{base}} = 0.45\text{ step}^{-1}$ (DEFAULT_BASE_RATE = 0.45). Branch partitioning arises from the module topology and regulatory edges rather than stage-name heuristics.
  6. Regulatory coupling (NEGATIVELY_REGULATES and POSITIVELY_REGULATES):
  7. Competing-pathway attenuation: When a flow stage $A$ NEGATIVELY_REGULATES a sibling flow stage $B$ (e.g., escrt_rab7_late_endosome $\dashv$ cytosolic_cargo_escape), active sequestration down pathway $A$ multiplies transitions into $B$ by COMPETING_INHIBITOR_FACTOR = 0.25. Knocking out $A$ (active: false) relieves that attenuation, allowing trapped upstream particles to divert into $B$.
  8. Pure regulator gating: When a pure regulator $R$ NEGATIVELY_REGULATES a transport stage $T$ (e.g., pcsk9_degradation $\dashv$ ldlr_endocytosis), locking $R$ ON (active: true) gates off $T$ and sets transitions into $T$ to zero, trapping cargo upstream.
  9. Positive modulation: When a flow stage $M$ POSITIVELY_REGULATES a target stage $T$, transitions into $T$ are boosted by POSITIVE_REGULATOR_BOOST = 1.35 when $M$ is active and gated to $0$ when $M$ is inactive (unless overridden by an explicit activation_logic expression).
  10. Conservation-preserving discrete stepping (simulate_abm):
  11. At each step $t \in [1, 24]$, if a compartment has total active outflow rate $R = \sum_k r_k > 0.85$ (DEFAULT_MAX_LEAVE_FRACTION), outgoing rates are scaled proportionally so at most $\lceil 0.85 \cdot n_u(t) \rceil$ particles leave in a single step.
  12. Integer particle movements are assigned via the largest-remainder method, guaranteeing exact particle conservation ($\sum_i n_i(t) = N_{\text{agents}}$) at every time step in both Python (module_abm.py) and browser JavaScript (executable_abm.html.j2).

2. Hepatocyte ASGR1/ASGR2 GalNAc-Conjugate Uptake & Endosomal Escape (asgr_galnac_hepatocyte_delivery)

  • Module: modules/asgr_galnac_hepatocyte_delivery.yaml
  • Interactive ABM: pages/models/asgr_galnac_delivery_abm.html
  • Biology & Qualitative Parameterization: Models hepatocyte-targeted trivalent GalNAc-siRNA/ASO capture by the sinusoidal C-type lectin receptor complex ASGR1/ASGR2 (PANTHER:PTHR22803, PMID:12171918, PMID:29069408), clathrin/AP-2/dynamin-2 internalization (AP2M1, CLTC, DNM2, PMID:24970086), V-ATPase early endosomal acidification and $\text{Ca}^{2+}$-dependent GalNAc uncoupling (ATP6V1A), rapid EHD1 receptor recycling (ehd1_receptor_recycling, 47%), TSG101/RAB7A late-endosomal maturation (escrt_rab7_late_endosome) and CTSD lysosomal sequestration/degradation (ctsd_lysosomal_degradation, 43%), and rate-limiting cytosolic oligonucleotide escape (cytosolic_galnac_escape, 10% in wild-type, rising to 47% upon RAB7A loss). Note that the 10% baseline escape (47/43/10) and 10% -> 47% shift upon RAB7A loss result from uniform uncalibrated ABM rates (DEFAULT_BASE_RATE = 0.45, COMPETING_INHIBITOR_FACTOR = 0.25) and represent a qualitative cross-modality extrapolation from hepatic LNP delivery (PMID:41814093), whereas only a minuscule portion of endocytosed clinical GalNAc-siRNA/ASO conjugates escapes into the cytosol in vivo (PMID:29069408).

3. Neonatal Fc Receptor (FCGRT/B2M) pH-Dependent IgG & Albumin Salvage (fcrn_igg_albumin_salvage_transcytosis)

  • Module: modules/fcrn_igg_albumin_salvage_transcytosis.yaml
  • Interactive ABM: pages/models/fcrn_salvage_transcytosis_abm.html
  • Biology & Qualitative Parameterization: Models how endothelial and barrier epithelial cells maintain the ~21-day circulatory half-life of human IgG and serum albumin (ALB, PANTHER:PTHR11385:SF15, PMID:36979511). Following pinocytic and clathrin/AP-2/DNM2 uptake (clathrin_dnm2_endocytic_uptake) and V-ATPase acidification to pH ~6.0 (early_endosome_acidification), protonated histidine residues on IgG and albumin bind the endosomal FCGRT/B2M (FcRn) heterodimer (fcrn_ph_dependent_salvage, PMID:28330995, PMID:24469444). Active FcRn capture negatively regulates rab7_late_endosome_routing, rescuing 80% of internalized cohort via EHD1/SNAP23 exocytic recycling (ehd1_snap23_salvage_exocytosis, PMID:30811271) vs. 20% lysosomal catabolism (ctsd_lysosomal_catabolism). Inactivating fcrn_ph_dependent_salvage (modeling FCGRT/B2M deficiency or therapeutic anti-FcRn blockers such as efgartigimod) diverts 100% of internalized IgG/albumin to lysosomal catabolism.

4. Retromer (VPS35/VPS26A/VPS29) & SORL1 Endosome-to-TGN Retrograde Retrieval (retromer_endosome_to_tgn_retrieval)

  • Module: modules/retromer_endosome_to_tgn_retrieval.yaml
  • Interactive ABM: pages/models/retromer_retrograde_sorting_abm.html
  • Biology & Qualitative Parameterization: Models retrograde endosome-to-Golgi retrieval of the Alzheimer-associated sorting receptor SORL1 (SorLA/LR11, PANTHER:PTHR12106:SF27, PMID:16174740) by the heterotrimeric retromer cargo-selective complex (VPS35 PANTHER:PTHR11099:SF0, VPS26A PANTHER:PTHR12233:SF4/SF1, VPS29 PANTHER:PTHR11124:SF12, PMID:15078902, PMID:18193037, PMID:18193032, PMID:32286230) across early and maturing endosomes and TGN docking via RAB9A and AP-1 (AP1B1 PANTHER:PTHR11134:SF31/SF3, AP1G1 PANTHER:PTHR22780:SF26, PMID:8164745, PMID:34793709, PMID:23415225). In wild-type sorting (wt_retromer_tgn_retrieval), active retromer recruitment (retromer_csc_cargo_selection) opposes RAB7A degradative progression and delivers 80% of internalized receptors to the TGN (rab9a_ap1_tgn_delivery) vs. 20% lysosomal proteolysis (ctsd_lysosomal_proteolysis). Retromer loss (retromer_vps35_loss_degradation) diverts 100% of cargo to lysosomal degradation, whereas RAB7A block (rab7_reduction_suppresses_degradation) restores 100% TGN retrieval (PMID:32286230).

5. LDL Receptor-Mediated Endocytosis (ldl_receptor_mediated_endocytosis)

  • Module: modules/ldl_receptor_mediated_endocytosis.yaml
  • Interactive ABM: pages/models/ldlr_endocytosis_abm.html (alongside the existing Boolean model)
  • Dual-model demonstration: The exact same 4-stage module (apob_ligand, ldlr_endocytosis, lipa_ce_hydrolysis, pcsk9_degradation) compiles into both a Boolean network and an Agent-Based cohort simulator:
  • normal_ldl_clearance: Maximal PCSK9-independent clearance (pcsk9_degradation defaults to false), delivering 100% of the apob_ligand cohort to lipa_ce_hydrolysis.
  • pcsk9_gain_of_function (pcsk9_degradation: true): Pure-regulator inhibition of ldlr_endocytosis traps 100% of LDL particles in the extracellular apob_ligand pool.
  • ldlr_loss_fh (ldlr_endocytosis: false): Familial hypercholesterolemia receptor loss traps 100% of LDL particles in apob_ligand.
  • lipa_loss_wolman (lipa_ce_hydrolysis: false): Wolman disease / CESD acid lipase loss internalizes LDL normally (apob_ligand = 0%) but traps 100% of unhydrolyzed cholesteryl esters in the endolysosomal ldlr_endocytosis compartment.

Scenario Validation in CI (validate_executable_models)

Every scenario declared on a module-derived AGENT_BASED model is executed and verified automatically by just validate-modules (src/ai_gene_review/validation/module_validator.py):

  • expected_active: flow stages that must be enabled (element_active = True) and visited by cargo (peak_counts > 0) during the simulation, or pure regulators that evaluate to element_active = True.
  • expected_inactive: flow stages that must either be disabled (element_active = False) or never reached by cargo (peak_counts == 0), or pure regulators that evaluate to element_active = False.
  • expected_outcome_order: stages listed in strictly descending order of final particle count at $T = 24$ ($n_{\text{final}}(s_1) > n_{\text{final}}(s_2) > \dots$), allowing quantitative branching predictions (such as ehd1_recycling_branch > ctsd_lysosomal_degradation > cytosolic_cargo_escape in wild-type vs. ehd1_recycling_branch > cytosolic_cargo_escape > ctsd_lysosomal_degradation in Rab7 loss) to be regression-tested against module topology edits.
