Variovorax Enzyme Curation & Module Plan

This document defines the batch curation plan and module architecture for Variovorax (NCBITaxon:34072) enzymes in ai-gene-review, with primary focus on rhizosphere plant–microbe chemical signaling and mineral solubilization and heavy-metal homeostasis.

1. Taxonomic & Proteome Strategy

Following UniProt's 2026_02 reference proteome policy (PROTEOME_REMOVAL), curation is anchored on active UniProtKB reference proteomes and Swiss-Prot reviewed records:

  1. Variovorax paradoxus S110 (VARPS, NCBITaxon:543728, Proteome UP000001552):
  2. Multipartite genome (Chromosome 1 CP001635 and Chromosome 2 CP001636, locus tags Vapar_0001–Vapar_6257; Han et al. 2011, PMID:21183664) with 234 Swiss-Prot reviewed proteins and 6 retained TrEMBL entries in UniProtKB, notably including the four core proteins of the iad auxin-catabolism locus (Vapar_1489 C5CSP2 iadR, Vapar_1492 C5CSP5 iadC, Vapar_1493 C5CSP6 iadD, and Vapar_1494 C5CSP7 iadE).
  3. Also provides Swiss-Prot reviewed anchors for rhizosphere amide turnover (amiE C5CWZ4), organosulfonate desulfonation (ssuD C5CNW9), cytosolic YggX-family iron trafficking (fetP C5CNX5), coenzyme PQQ precursor peptide (pqqA C5CPT7), and nickel-dependent urea hydrolysis (ureC C5CY00).
  4. Variovorax paradoxus (VARPD, NCBITaxon:34073, Reference Proteome UP000035170 / UP000019090):
  5. Hosts experimentally characterized Swiss-Prot entries including 1-aminocyclopropane-1-carboxylate deaminase (acdS, Q6J256) and 3-sulfinopropanoyl-CoA desulfinase (acd, B9U6P5, VPARA_05440), alongside active reference-proteome entries for PQQ biosynthesis (pqqB A0A0H2M8Q2, pqqC A0A0H2MDI9, pqqD A0A0H2M992, pqqE A0A0H2MPV4), periplasmic PQQ-dependent glucose (gcd A0A0H2LUW4) and alcohol (VPARA_00790 A0A0H2MDH4) dehydrogenases, TonB-dependent ferric-siderophore uptake (tonB A0A0H2M2E3, bfrD1 A0A0H2M677), polyphosphate kinase (ppk A0A0H2M4T4), the czcCBAIR heavy-metal RND efflux island (czcA3 A0A0H2MBF7), and taurine dioxygenase (tauD1 A0A0H2MHV8).

2. Tranche 1 Curation Batches (Completed in PR #6000)

Batch 1A: Auxin (IAA) Catabolism (iad Operon) & Root Growth Inhibition Reversal

Variovorax is the keystone genus in plant root microbiomes responsible for degrading excess bacterial and plant indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) to prevent ethylene- and auxin-driven root growth inhibition (Finkel et al. 2020, PMID:32999461; Conway et al. 2022, PMID:36266335; Ma et al. 2023, PMID:37459330).
