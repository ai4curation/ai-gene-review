Mechanistically supported but parked too high 12 3 tp53/PMID:21209837, casp3a/PMID:18515717, ptmab/PMID:24032683

This is a row count, not a unique-biology count: the tp53 package alone
contributes eight UniProt rows for the same ZFIN genotype/morpholino evidence,
and casp3a, hpdl, dmrt2a, and timm50 are also repeated over accessions.

The likely over-annotation set has the assay-to-function shape this project is
tracking: a perturbation changes TUNEL, acridine-orange, caspase, or generic
cell-death signal, while the assayed product is a somite transcription factor
(dmrt2a), a ribosomal protein (rpl11), a mitochondrial protein-import
factor (timm50), or an HPDL-linked mitochondrial metabolism factor (hpdl).
Those rows should be reviewed before any DANRE review accepts exact
apoptotic process.

The likely pushdown set is qualitatively different. casp3a should move toward
execution-phase apoptosis, tp53 toward p53-mediated apoptotic signaling or
its regulation, and ptmab toward negative regulation of apoptosome or
caspase-3 activation rather than exact top-level apoptosis.
