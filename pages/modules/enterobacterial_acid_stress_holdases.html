E. coli HdeA/HdeB periplasmic acid-stress holdases

A periplasmic acid-stress module in which the ATP-independent chaperones HdeA and HdeB bind acid-unfolding client proteins, suppress irreversible aggregation, and preserve them for refolding after neutralization. HdeA is activated under extreme acid through dimer dissociation and disordering, whereas HdeB acts at milder acidic pH through a distinct dynamic folded-dimer mechanism. HdeB also supports client refolding after neutralization, but the direct release step has not yet been separated from potential downstream folding factors. Acid-sensing transcriptional regulation, cytoplasmic pH homeostasis, HdeB-specific post-neutralization handoff, and the post-neutralization cooperation of DegP and SurA are outside this focused in-situ holdase module.
