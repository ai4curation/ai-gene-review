Rad9-Hus1-Rad1 (9-1-1) checkpoint clamp in Drosophila melanogaster

The 9-1-1 complex is a heterotrimeric ring of Rad9, Hus1 and Rad1 that is structurally related to the PCNA sliding clamp. An RFC-like clamp loader places it at 5' recessed junctions of single-stranded DNA gaps and resected ends, where it helps recruit and activate the ATR kinase in the DNA damage and replication checkpoint. In Drosophila, the Hus1 ortholog is named Hus1-like.
