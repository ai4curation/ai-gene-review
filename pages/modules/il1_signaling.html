IL-1 signaling pathway module

IL-1 alpha or mature IL-1 beta binds IL1R1, which recruits the co-receptor IL1RAP. The paired TIR domains recruit MYD88, which nucleates the Myddosome with IRAK4. IRAK4 phosphorylates IRAK1 or IRAK2, which leave the complex and engage the E3 ligase TRAF6. TRAF6 K63-linked chains activate the TAK1-TAB complex, which drives NF-kappaB and MAPK inflammatory outputs. TOLLIP restrains IRAK1 in resting cells.
