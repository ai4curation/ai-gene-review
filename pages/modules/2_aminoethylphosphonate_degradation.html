PhnWX 2-aminoethylphosphonate degradation

A reusable two-reaction bacterial pathway that degrades 2-aminoethylphosphonate through phosphonoacetaldehyde. PhnW first transfers the amino group to pyruvate, producing L-alanine and phosphonoacetaldehyde. PhnX then hydrolytically cleaves the carbon-phosphorus bond, yielding acetaldehyde and inorganic phosphate. Transport of 2-aminoethylphosphonate and downstream acetaldehyde metabolism are outside the module boundary.

Phosphonoacetaldehyde hydrolase (phosphonatase) catalyzes the hydrolysis of phosphonoacetaldehyde to acetaldehyde and inorganic phosphate. In this study, the genes encoding phosphonatase in Bacillus cereus and in Salmonella typhimurium were cloned for high- level expression in Escherichia coli. The kinetic properties of the purified, recombinant phosphonatases were determined. The Schiff base mechanism known to operate in the B. cereus enzyme was verified for the S. typhimurium enzyme by phosphonoacetaldehyde-sodium borohydride- induced inactivation and by site-directed mutagenesis of the catalytic lysine 53.

The bacterial taxonomic envelope covers the sampled Bacillota (Bacillus cereus) and Pseudomonadota (Salmonella typhimurium and Pseudomonas putida), without asserting universal pathway retention. Each step has a narrow verified PANTHER subfamily and representatives from both phyla. Salmonella PhnW and Salmonella/Bacillus PhnX provide direct biochemical anchors; the KT2440 proteins and Bacillus PhnW remain distinguished as homology-supported exemplars. Q8RLU1 is a probable fragment with a sequence caution, not an experimentally established intact Bacillus PhnW. Neither phosphate-starvation regulation nor a particular transporter is a defining component: regulatory wiring differs among studied strains. PhnWAY, oxidative AEP deamination and broad-substrate C-P lyase routes remain outside this two-reaction PhnWX boundary. PbfD-mediated PAA formation explains why phnX presence alone is insufficient to infer PhnWX. The KT2440-specific experimental and regulatory gaps below remain open.
