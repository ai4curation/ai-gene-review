PhnWX 2-aminoethylphosphonate degradation

A reusable two-reaction bacterial pathway that degrades 2-aminoethylphosphonate through phosphonoacetaldehyde. PhnW first transfers the amino group to pyruvate, producing L-alanine and phosphonoacetaldehyde. PhnX then hydrolytically cleaves the carbon-phosphorus bond, yielding acetaldehyde and inorganic phosphate. Transport of 2-aminoethylphosphonate and downstream acetaldehyde metabolism are outside the module boundary.
