Bacterial dimethylglycine and sarcosine catabolism

A reusable bacterial two-stage module for oxidative demethylation of dimethylglycine to sarcosine and then glycine. First-stage alternatives include a DgcA/DdhC-associated pathway with genetically implicated electron-transfer partners and an oxygen-dependent DMGO that couples oxidation to tetrahydrofolate capture. The second stage may use a folate-coupled SoxABDG heterotetramer, a monomeric sarcosine oxidase, or a historically characterized dehydrogenase whose gene identity remains unresolved. Upstream betaine demethylation and downstream glycine metabolism are separate mechanisms.

Historical C-83 sarcosine-dehydrogenase identity and assay audit
A native C-83 sarcosine-oxidizing dehydrogenase provides a biochemical alternative, but its gene, evolutionary family and physiological electron acceptor remain unidentified.
The biochemical alternative remains explicitly ungrounded at the gene/family level.

Comparative scope spans Pseudomonadota and Actinomycetota, without asserting universal bacterial distribution. Twenty-two identified proteins have individual reviews: original seventeen plus ARTGO DMGO and four CHRI1 Sox subunits. DgcA/DgcB family intent is described without a falsely specific PANTHER id; Sox subunits retain specific NCBIfam models. DMGO has verified PTHR43757:SF11 membership despite that subfamily name not matching its substrate. The historical C-83 alternative has no identified gene/family and remains a visible coverage gap. The 2017 CHRI1 deletion figures do not establish four individual Sox knockouts. PMID17951379 uses PA14 main mutants and PAO1 locus numbering. DgcA-to-DgcB-to-ETF ordering remains a mechanistic proposal. THF-dependent Sox chemistry is collective, not an autonomous core activity of each subunit. Whole-organism formaldehyde generation does not determine an individual enzyme sequence or imply complete flux from a module-capacity result. Existing PAINT data ambiguity is preserved rather than repaired by guessing. Module integration and validation are in progress; DRAFT records that state.

Substrates: sarcosine tetrahydrofolate oxygen
Products: glycine 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate hydrogen peroxide

This is EC 1.5.3.24 / RHEA:70455. No current GO molecular function term accurately represents the folate-coupled reaction; GO:0008115 is reserved for the water/formaldehyde chemistry. In low folate, the same complex can release formaldehyde conditionally. The complete activity belongs to the assembled complex. All four subunits contribute; beta-site catalysis and cofactor binding do not establish an autonomous beta enzyme. The conditional formaldehyde branch remains real. Exact U-96 structure mappings and the tested recombinant assembly support the interpretation; native CHRI1 and KT2440 product partitioning is not directly measured.
