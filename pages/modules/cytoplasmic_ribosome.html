Human cytosolic 80S ribosome — the 40S decoding subunit and the 60S peptidyl-transferase subunit, with their ribosomal proteins as structural units and tissue-specific paralogue alternatives (RPS4Y1/RPS4Y2, RPS27L, RPL3L, RPL10L, RPL36AL, RPL39L)

The cytosolic 80S ribosome translates nuclear-encoded mRNAs in the cytosol and, when bound to the endoplasmic reticulum, on its surface. It consists of a small 40S subunit, built on the 18S rRNA with 33 ribosomal proteins and RACK1, and a large 60S subunit, built on the 28S, 5.8S and 5S rRNAs with 46 ribosomal proteins. The 40S subunit binds mRNA, scans it during initiation and carries the decoding centre in which codon-anticodon pairing is checked at the A site. The 60S subunit carries the peptidyl transferase centre, an all-RNA active site in the 28S rRNA that forms each peptide bond, the polypeptide exit tunnel, and the GTPase-associated centre and P-stalk that bind the translational GTPases. The two subunits join at initiation and are split again after termination. The ribosomal proteins are named by the universal (u) and eukaryote-specific (e) nomenclature; they fold and stabilise the rRNA and line the functional sites, and three of them (eS30, eS31, eL40) are made as fusions with ubiquitin or the ubiquitin-like FUBI and cleaved before assembly. Several positions have paralogues that are alternatives rather than extra subunits: a Y-encoded eS4 in males, a striated-muscle uL3 (RPL3L), testis-specific uL16 and eL39 (RPL10L, RPL39L), and the ubiquitous eL42 paralogue RPL36AL.

Derived from the RGD Pathway Ontology diagrams PW:0000101 (translation pathway) and PW:0001066 (ribosome biogenesis pathway) through the RGD_PATHWAY_ONTOLOGY project, as module 1 of the translation split plan (projects/RGD_PATHWAY_ONTOLOGY/broad_pathway_split_plans.md; seeds in projects/RGD_PATHWAY_ONTOLOGY/data/module_seeds.yaml, both flagged broad, 180 and 138 RGD-curated human genes). Both seeds contain the same 82 ribosome genes, 81 ribosomal proteins (46 RPL, 35 RPS) and RACK1, and this module models them once; the sibling modules translation_initiation, translation_elongation_and_termination and nonsense_mediated_mrna_decay, and the later ribosome biogenesis module, refer to this one rather than listing ribosomal proteins. The node ids small_subunit and large_subunit, and the annoton ids small_subunit_complex, large_subunit_complex and peptidyl_transferase_centre, are intended as stable reference points (for example cytoplasmic_ribosome#small_subunit). PW children (PW:0000580 initiation, PW:0000581 elongation, PW:0000582 termination) belong to the sibling modules and are not evidence sources here. UniProt accessions and recommended names are from UniProt REST (November 2026), cross-checked against the local UniProt files for the five members with gene reviews (RACK1, RPL18A, RPL36A, RPL36AL, RPS3). Molecular functions are from each protein's experimental human GOA rows in QuickGO (November 2026). The structure follows modules/dmel_cytoplasmic_ribosome.yaml (structure only; no Drosophila evidence is used). Boundary and modelling decisions. (1) rRNAs are not participants. The 18S, 28S, 5.8S and 5S rRNAs are the ribosome's catalytic and decoding core, but they are not gene products in this model, so the complex descriptors stand for the ribonucleoprotein subunits as wholes and only proteins are listed as units. (2) Structural constituent of ribosome on the units. GO defines it as the action of a molecule that contributes to the structural integrity of the ribosome: a structural role, not a catalytic one, and a property of each protein in the assembled particle. Human GOA asserts it on 77 of the ribosomal proteins by IDA from the human 80S structure (PMID:23636399), with further IDA, HDA and IMP rows, and all four human gene reviews of ribosomal proteins in this repository (RPL18A, RPL36A, RPL36AL, RPS3) keep it as the core function. It is therefore placed on each unit that has such a row (or, for RPL36AL, the gene review core function), and on no unit without one. This is the only function the ribosomal protein units carry; no unit carries a catalytic, decoding or channel activity. (3) The ribosome's own molecular functions sit on the complexes. Peptidyl transferase activity is catalysed by the 28S rRNA (the GO definition says so) and is placed on the 60S complex annoton peptidyl_transferase_centre, which lists no protein units; human GOA asserts it only on the Complex Portal 60S complexes. Decoding by the 40S subunit is rRNA-centred too and has no GO molecular function term; it is described in prose, and no ontology request is made because GO represents ribosome activity through the structural-constituent term and the translation process. The two subunit annotons carry structural constituent of ribosome at subunit level, as the human GO-CAMs pinned here do for the 60S complex. (4) RACK1 is a 40S constituent. It sits on the head of the 40S subunit in the human 80S structures, UniProt and Complex Portal (CPX-5223) list it as a 40S component, and RGD draws it binding the 40S subunit. Its only experimental human cellular-component evidence for the 40S subunit is indirect (Complex Portal NAS; cytoplasm EXP from PMID:23636399), so it carries ribosome binding, the core function of its gene review, rather than structural constituent. Its signalling-scaffold functions are outside this module. (5) Paralogues are alternatives at one position, listed as units with their roles stated rather than as variant sets. Each ribosome carries one protein per position: eS4 from RPS4X or, in males, RPS4Y1 (shown in human ribosomes, PMID:8139551) or RPS4Y2 (sequence and curated complex membership only); eS27 from RPS27 or RPS27L; uL3 from RPL3 or the striated-muscle RPL3L; uL16 from RPL10 or the testis-specific RPL10L; eL39 from RPL39 or the male germ-cell RPL39L; eL42 from RPL36A or the ubiquitous RPL36AL. Complex Portal defines a canonical 60S (CPX-5183), a testis 60S with RPL10L and RPL39L (CPX-7664) and a striated muscle 60S with RPL3L (CPX-7665); variant sets were not used because they would repeat 46 shared units for each tissue and because, apart from these Complex Portal entries, which paralogue combinations coexist in single ribosomes is not established for every position. RPS27L is included because RGD and Complex Portal list it; its incorporation into human ribosomes rests on its near-identity to RPS27, not on experimental human evidence. (6) Members added beyond the RGD list: RPL41 (eS32, formerly eL41), a core constituent in Complex Portal and in GOA (HDA and IDA cellular-component rows), and the paralogues RPL3L, RPL10L, RPL36AL and RPL39L, which RGD omits. RPL41 is placed in the small subunit, following UniProt (eS32, a small-subunit protein forming an intersubunit bridge); GOA and Complex Portal place it in the 60S subunit, and the human pre-60S structures lack it (PMID:32669547). It has no experimental molecular-function row and is a unit without a function. (7) Excluded: RPL22L1 and RPL26L1 (Complex Portal lists them as alternatives to RPL22 and RPL26, but they have no experimental human evidence of incorporation and RGD omits them; candidates for a later revision), RPL7L1 (a ribosome biogenesis factor despite its name), the newly named RPSA2 and the ribosomal protein pseudogene entries, and the mitochondrial ribosomal proteins (the mitoribosome is in mitochondrial_translation). The ubiquitin and FUBI moieties released from RPS27A, UBA52 and FAU are not ribosome constituents. (8) Processes. All annotons carry cytoplasmic translation, the cytosolic child of the PW:0000101 mapped process. Ribosome biogenesis (the PW:0001066 mapped process) is not assigned to the ribosomal proteins: they are required for biogenesis because they are built into its product, and the steps are performed by the biogenesis factors, snoRNPs and nucleases of the later ribosome biogenesis module, so necessity evidence (for example IMP rows for rRNA processing after knockdown, PMID:18697920) is not read as participation. Extra-ribosomal roles of individual proteins (RPS3 in DNA repair, RPL5 and RPL11 in the p53-MDM2 response, RPSA as a laminin receptor, RACK1 as a signalling scaffold, RPS27L as a p53 target) are outside this module. (9) Out of scope and modelled elsewhere: initiation factors (translation_initiation), elongation and termination factors and ABCE1 recycling (translation_elongation_and_termination), nonsense-mediated decay (nonsense_mediated_mrna_decay), the ER-bound ribosome and SRP targeting (er_cotranslational_translocation), mRNA export (mrna_nuclear_export), the mitoribosome (mitochondrial_translation), and ribosome biogenesis, a later module. snoRNA and microRNA host genes in the RGD lists are not participants. PANTHER family ids and PTNs are deliberately not asserted. GO-CAM links. In gocams/index.tsv the human models that contain ribosomal proteins or the ribosomal subunit complexes all give them structural constituent of ribosome with cytoplasmic translation or a ribosome-recycling process. As for the 40S subunit, each ribosomal protein unit carries structural constituent of ribosome from its experimental human GOA rows (IDA from PMID:23636399, PMID:25957688 and PMID:32669547; HDA from 60S proteomics, PMID:12962325), and RPL36AL from the core function of its gene review; no unit carries a catalytic activity. RPL3L and RPL39L have experimental 60S cellular-component rows (HDA, IPI) but no experimental molecular-function row, and are units without a function. Four positions have paralogue alternatives, and each ribosome carries one protein at each: uL3 (RPL3, or RPL3L in heart and skeletal muscle), uL16 (RPL10, or RPL10L in testis), eL39 (RPL39, or RPL39L in male germ cells) and eL42 (RPL36A or the ubiquitous RPL36AL). Complex Portal records the tissue-specific combinations as separate 60S variants (canonical CPX-5183, testis CPX-7664, striated muscle CPX-7665); here they are listed in one descriptor with their roles stated, rather than as variant sets that would repeat 46 shared units three times. The subunit-level function follows the GO-CAMs pinned here, which give the 60S complex structural constituent of ribosome.

Peptidyl transfer is catalysed by rRNA, so this activity is placed on the 60S complex as a whole and on no protein unit; the descriptor deliberately lists no active units. The ribozyme mechanism was established on the archaeal large subunit, where only rRNA contacts the substrates (PMID:10937990), and the GO term definition states that the reaction is catalysed by a ribozyme. In human GOA the activity is asserted on the Complex Portal 60S complexes (CPX-5183 and CPX-7665, NAS) and on no ribosomal protein. rRNAs are not gene products in this model, so the catalytic entity itself is not a participant.
