TRAIL-activated apoptotic signaling — TNFSF10 ligation of TRAIL-R1/R2, FADD/caspase-8 DISC assembly restrained by c-FLIP and decoy receptors, and type I (direct executioner caspase) versus type II (BID/mitochondrial) caspase amplification

TRAIL (TNFSF10, Apo2L) is a TNF-family ligand that exists as a type II transmembrane protein and as a shed soluble form; both act as homotrimers. A TRAIL trimer binds the two death-domain receptors TRAIL-R1 (DR4, TNFRSF10A) and TRAIL-R2 (DR5, TNFRSF10B), which form homomeric and heteromeric ligand-receptor clusters at the plasma membrane. The receptor death domains recruit the adaptor FADD through death-domain contacts, and FADD in turn recruits procaspase-8 (and, where expressed, procaspase-10) through death-effector-domain contacts, forming the TRAIL death-inducing signaling complex (DISC). Proximity-induced dimerisation activates caspase-8 at the DISC. The catalytically inactive caspase-8 paralog c-FLIP (CFLAR) is recruited to the same FADD platform and restrains caspase-8 activation, and the decoy receptors TRAIL-R3 (TNFRSF10C, which lacks an intracellular domain) and TRAIL-R4 (TNFRSF10D, which carries a truncated death domain) can bind TRAIL without assembling a DISC and protect cells when overexpressed. Active caspase-8 then propagates the signal by one of two routes. In type I cells enough caspase-8 is generated at the DISC to cleave and activate the executioner caspases-3 and -7 directly. In type II cells caspase-8 output is limiting and must be amplified through the mitochondrial pathway: caspase-8 cleaves the BH3-only protein BID to truncated tBID, which moves to mitochondria and activates BAX/BAK-dependent outer membrane permeabilisation, cytochrome c release, apoptosome assembly and caspase-9 activation, which then feeds the same executioner caspases.

Active caspase-8 reaches the executioner caspases by one of two routes, which is the type I / type II distinction. The two variants are alternative implementations of the same step, not successive steps.

Variant set: Type I (direct) versus type II (mitochondrial) amplification by cell-type-dependent signal amplification route (One Or More)

The axis is the cell's dependence on mitochondria downstream of the DISC, defined for CD95 in PMID:9501089 and shown for TRAIL in PMID:12407450, where BID cleavage is the limiting step. The two routes are not mutually exclusive in a given cell, so ONE_OR_MORE; which dominates is a knowledge gap.
