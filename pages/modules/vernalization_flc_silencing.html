FRIGIDA-dependent FLC activation and vernalization-induced Polycomb silencing of FLC

In winter-annual Arabidopsis thaliana accessions, the plant-specific scaffold protein FRIGIDA (FRI) assembles the FRI-C transcription activator complex (with SUF4, FLX, FES1 and FRL1) at the FLOWERING LOCUS C (FLC) promoter. FRI-C recruits H3K4 and H3K36 methyltransferase and H2A.Z exchange activities and the nuclear cap-binding complex, raising expression of the MADS-box floral repressor FLC. FLC, with its partner SVP, represses the floral integrators FT (in leaves) and SOC1 (in the meristem), so flowering is blocked before winter. Prolonged cold (vernalization) overcomes this block in two mechanistically separate phases. (1) Nucleation: the slow, quantitative cold induction of the VEL protein VIN3 is the molecular readout of the length of winter. VIN3 heterodimerizes through its VEL domain with the constitutively expressed VRN5 (VIL1), which bridges it to a VRN2-containing PRC2 core (SWN/CLF, FIE, MSI1). The resulting PHD-PRC2 is targeted to a three-nucleosome nucleation region near the first exon-intron boundary of FLC, where the sequence-specific repressor VAL1 also binds. VIN3 VEL-domain head-to-tail polymerization builds multivalent assemblies that nucleate H3K27me3, switching individual FLC alleles digitally into a metastable silenced state. (2) Spreading and maintenance: after return to warmth, H3K27me3 spreads over the whole FLC locus in a step that needs CLF-PRC2 and LHP1, and the chromatin-associated, sequence-nonspecific DNA-binding B3 protein VRN1 is needed to keep FLC stably silenced through later development. Silencing of FLC de-represses FT and SOC1, so flowering follows in spring. The autonomous pathway (FCA, FPA, FLD, FVE, FY, LD), which represses FLC independently of cold, COOLAIR antisense transcription, and resetting of FLC in each generation are outside this module.

Part 1: FRI-dependent activation of FLC (winter-annual habit)
FRI-C activation of FLC transcriptionRegulatory Stepfri_activation

FRI has no DNA-binding domain. It scaffolds FRI-C, in which the zinc-finger protein SUF4 binds an FLC promoter cis-element and FLX/FES1 supply activation potential. FRI-C recruits COMPASS-like (WDR5a) H3K4 methylation, SDG8 H3K36 methylation and SWR1 H2A.Z exchange, and FRI binds the cap-binding protein CBP20. In autumn cold, FRI moves into nuclear condensates away from the active FLC locus, which lowers FRI occupancy at FLC.

Part 3: cold-dependent nucleation of Polycomb silencing (PHD-PRC2)
VIN3/VRN5 PHD-PRC2 nucleation of H3K27me3 at FLCProtein Complexphd_prc2_nucleation

During prolonged cold, VIN3 accumulates and joins VRN5 and a VRN2-PRC2 core (SWN, FIE, MSI1; VEL1 is also present) at the FLC nucleation region. VRN2 is bound across FLC regardless of cold. VRN5 binding at the nucleation site needs VIN3, and VIN3 joins PRC2 through VRN5. VEL-domain polymerization of VIN3 provides multivalent chromatin association. The output is local H3K27me3 that holds a metastable silenced state.

Part 4: post-cold spreading and long-term maintenance of silencing
H3K27me3 spreading and maintenance (CLF-PRC2, LHP1, VRN1)Biological Processspreading_maintenance

After return to warmth and faster growth, H3K27me3 spreads from the nucleation region over the whole FLC gene body. Spreading needs CLF-PRC2 and LHP1 and converts metastable silencing into long-term epigenetic memory. VRN2 is needed for FLC to stay low after cold, and the chromatin-associated B3 protein VRN1 is likewise needed to maintain repression.
