Hydrogenotrophic methanogenesis from CO2 (Methanothermobacter)

Methane formation from CO2 and H2 in Methanobacteriales. CO2 is bound to methanofuran and reduced to the formyl level, the formyl group is transferred to tetrahydromethanopterin (H4MPT), and the C1 unit is reduced through the methenyl, methylene and methyl levels. The methyl group is transferred to coenzyme M by a membrane-bound, Na+-pumping methyltransferase, and methyl-coenzyme M is reduced to methane with coenzyme B, releasing the heterodisulfide CoM-S-S-CoB. Electrons come from H2 by three routes: F420 reduced by the F420-reducing hydrogenase serves the methenyl and methylene reductions, the H2-forming methylene-H4MPT dehydrogenase can replace the F420 route for one step, and the cytoplasmic hydrogenase-heterodisulfide reductase complex couples heterodisulfide reduction to ferredoxin reduction by flavin-based electron bifurcation, supplying the reduced ferredoxin for the first step. Several steps are catalysed by multisubunit complexes whose subunits each carry part of the reaction.

The module realizes this GO process.

Concrete module for Methanothermobacter thermautotrophicus strain Delta H, with the reviewed genes in genes/METTH/. Most of the biochemistry was done on the closely related strain Marburg (Methanothermobacter marburgensis); the Delta H reviews record sequence identities to the Marburg orthologs. The C1 steps on H4MPT are the archaeal counterpart of the methyl branch of the Wood-Ljungdahl pathway (modules/wood_ljungdahl_pathway.yaml), but here the methyl group goes to coenzyme M and methane, not to acetyl-CoA. Energy conservation is through the Na+ gradient built by Mtr; the anaplerotic energy-converting hydrogenases (Eha, Ehb) that supply reduced ferredoxin when bifurcation falls short are left out. Three activities have no GO term and are given by name only, as proposed in the gene reviews: the two half-reactions of formylmethanofuran dehydrogenase (FwdB, FwdA) and the bifurcating H2:CoB-CoM heterodisulfide,ferredoxin reductase reaction. GO:0051912 (CoB--CoM heterodisulfide reductase activity) is deliberately not used, because it is defined with methanophenazine as the electron acceptor, which this organism lacks. M. jannaschii orthologs (genes/METJA/mcrA, mtrA, hdrA) are reviewed separately.
