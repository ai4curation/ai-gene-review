De novo inosine monophosphate biosynthesis

A reusable pathway that assembles the purine ring on 5-phosphoribosyl diphosphate (PRPP) to form inosine monophosphate (IMP). The module represents ten ordered reaction positions and separates reaction roles from lineage-specific protein fusions. It includes alternative folate-dependent PurN and ATP/formate-dependent PurT routes for GAR formylation, and alternative two-enzyme and direct routes for AIR carboxylation. Enzyme architecture varies widely: bacteria commonly use separate PurF, PurD, PurN/PurT, PurL, PurM, PurK, PurE, PurC, PurB and PurH proteins; animals fuse PurD, PurM and PurN in GART and direct AIR carboxylase with SAICAR synthetase in PAICS; fungi fuse PurD with PurM in a bifunctional GAR synthetase-AIR synthetase and PurK with PurE in a bifunctional AIR carboxylase that works through the N5-carboxyaminoimidazole ribonucleotide intermediate. In eukaryotes the pathway is cytosolic. The first enzyme, amidophosphoribosyltransferase, is feedback-inhibited by adenine nucleotides. IMP-to-AMP and IMP-to-GMP branches are outside the module, although PurB also has a second physiological role in AMP synthesis.

Biochemical role of the Cryptococcus neoformans ADE2 protein in fungal de novo purine biosynthesis.
Fungal ADE2 is a two-domain bifunctional protein: an N-terminal PurK-like domain forms N5-CAIR from AIR, ATP and bicarbonate and a C-terminal PurE-like domain converts N5-CAIR to CAIR.

PMID:11689683

PMID:3502942
Sequence of the bifunctional ade1 gene in the purine biosynthetic pathway of the fission yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe.
S. pombe ade1 encodes a bifunctional protein carrying the second and fifth steps (GAR synthetase and AIR synthetase), homologous to S. cerevisiae ADE5,7.
PMID:9143321
Saccharomyces cerevisiae expresses two genes encoding isozymes of 5-aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribonucleotide transformylase.

PMID:10877846
Characterization of two 5-aminoimidazole-4-carboxamide ribonucleotide transformylase/inosine monophosphate cyclohydrolase isozymes from Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
Ade16p and Ade17p are cytosolic bifunctional AICAR transformylase/IMP cyclohydrolase homodimers; Ade17p is adenine-repressed and Ade16p is not.
PMID:15744050
Revisiting purine-histidine cross-pathway regulation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae: a central role for a small molecule.

Exact UniProt exemplars delimit each reaction role without restricting the module taxonomically. A multidomain protein may satisfy more than one leaf: GART combines PurD-, PurN-, and PurM-like roles, PAICS combines direct AIR carboxylase and SAICAR synthetase roles, and PurH/ATIC commonly combines the final two reactions. GART remains an exact fusion exemplar on the PurD and PurN leaves, where its reviewed domain descriptions establish those activities without using its whole-protein family as a reaction selector. The PurM leaf uses PTHR10520:SF12 because that whole-protein subfamily contains both standalone bacterial PurM proteins and the GART fusion; the leaf molecular function constrains it to the shared cyclo-ligase role. Separate bacterial and eukaryotic adenylosuccinate-lyase families are retained for the PurB leaf. Parent PANTHER families containing both standalone enzymes and fusion proteins are selector scaffolds constrained by the leaf molecular function and exemplars. No ancestral PTN node is asserted without verified PAINT IBD evidence. ALIGN with the YeastPathways crosswalk (PWY-6122-1, PWY-6123-1, DENOVOPURINE2-PWY, DENOVOPURINE3-PWY, PRPP-PWY-1) and the PomBase model 663d668500001911. Fungal architecture: ADE4 (PurF), ADE5,7 / S. pombe ade1 (PurD-PurM fusion, both leaves; PMID:3502942), ADE8 / ade5 (standalone PurN; fungi have no PurT), ADE6 / ade3 (large PurL), ADE2 / ade6 (fused PurK-PurE, modelled as the fused_ade2_n5_cair_route variant; PMID:9500840), ADE1 / ade7 (standalone PurC), ADE13 / ade8 (PurB) and the ADE16/ADE17 isozyme pair / ade10 (PurH-type bifunctional transformylase and cyclohydrolase; PMID:9143321, PMID:10877846). All YeastCyc catalysts are correct and none was missing. YeastCyc corrections: AIR carboxylation is drawn as the direct EC 4.1.1.21 reaction, and both YeastPathways and PomBase GO-CAMs annotate ADE2/ade6 with GO:0004638 (the direct, class II-type carboxylase term) rather than GO:0034028 + GO:0034023, although fungal ADE2 proceeds through free N5-CAIR. The YeastPathways models annotate the ADE13 SAICAR reaction with GO:0004018 (adenylosuccinate lyase) rather than GO:0070626. The superpathway frames DENOVOPURINE2-PWY and DENOVOPURINE3-PWY also contain IMP-to-AMP/GMP, NMP/NDP kinase and ribonucleotide reductase reactions (purine_nucleotide_interconversion, dntp_de_novo_synthesis) and two gene-less formate-dependent class III ribonucleotide reductase reactions that do not occur in yeast. PRPP-PWY-1 places every activity under the root-level GO:0009165 and the PomBase model additionally includes ade9 (one-carbon supply, folate_one_carbon_interconversion). Regulation (outside the module core): adenine nucleotides inhibit Ade4p, and the intermediates SAICAR and AICAR promote Bas1-Bas2 transcriptional activation of the ADE and HIS genes (PMID:11689683, PMID:15744050). PRPP supply (pentose_phosphate_pathway, PRS1-PRS5) and the 10-formyl-THF donor (folate_one_carbon_interconversion) are upstream inputs.
