Pan-metazoan Hedgehog/Smoothened signaling pathway module

A taxon-neutral decomposition of Hedgehog (Hh) signaling, the metazoan cell-cell communication system in which a dually lipidated Hedgehog ligand released by a signal-sending cell controls the activator/repressor balance of Ci/GLI zinc-finger transcription factors in a receiving cell. The module is written around the segments that are shared from cnidarians and insects to vertebrates: autocatalytic cholesterol-coupled processing and N-terminal palmitoylation of the ligand; reception by the twelve-pass Patched receptor and its Ig/fibronectin-domain co-receptors; relief of Patched-mediated restriction of sterol access to the class-F receptor Smoothened; kinase-driven proteolytic processing of Ci/GLI into a transcriptional repressor when the pathway is off; and Smoothened-dependent conversion of Ci/GLI into an activator through Suppressor of fused, Fused-family kinases, and a kinesin-4 scaffold. Where lineages differ they are modeled as variants on an explicit axis rather than as separate pathways: the vertebrate implementation runs the activation step in the primary cilium with GPR161, intraflagellar transport, and KIF7, while the Drosophila implementation runs the equivalent step on a cytoplasmic Hedgehog signaling complex nucleated by the Costal-2 kinesin at the plasma membrane. Sonic hedgehog (SHH) is the vertebrate exemplar ligand, but it is one of three vertebrate paralogs of the single ancestral Hedgehog ligand and is used here only as a representative member.

Boundary decisions. This document replaces an earlier, explicitly vertebrate sketch of the same module id; the pan-metazoan scope is the requested change, so the ciliary machinery that was previously stated as module-level context now sits inside the vertebrate variant, where it belongs. Ligand biogenesis is kept inside the module because Hedgehog performs its own autoproteolysis and cholesterol transfer, so those are activities of a core participant rather than upstream biology. Transcriptional targets, tissue-level patterning outcomes, and pathway-level crosstalk (Wnt, BMP, primary-cilium assembly) are deliberately outside the boundary. HHIP, SCUBE2, and GAS1 are vertebrate accessories and are modeled as an optional reception-tier variant rather than as core steps; glypicans are modeled alongside them because the same heparan-sulfate proteoglycan role is documented in both flies (dally) and vertebrates. Taxon scope. "Pan-metazoan" here means the module is written for the animal clade in which ligand, receptor, transducer, and Ci/GLI effector co-occur, which includes cnidarians, most protostomes, and vertebrates. It is not a claim of universality: nematodes have lost recognizable Hedgehog and Smoothened while expanding Patched-related and Hedgehog-related families (PMID:16204193), and any realization in that lineage would be a different module, not a variant of this one. Grounding provenance. Every UniProt exemplar was taken from a local artifact rather than recalled: the PANTHER family entry tables under interpro/panther/ and the cached GO-CAM activity index gocams/index.tsv. PANTHER family and subfamily labels are copied verbatim from interpro/panther/panther.obo, and the four PTN ancestral nodes are resolved from the committed PAINT IBD slices. Where the pathway has a genuine component whose accession could not be verified from a local artifact in this environment, no identifier was invented (see knowledge_gaps): the Dispatched release transporter carries no term and no representative member, and the Drosophila orthologs of Patched, Fused, and Slimb are named only in prose. `just refresh-panther-members` could not be run (data.pantherdb.org is blocked by the same egress policy), so several cited accessions are absent from interpro/panther/panther-members.tsv and the validator reports them as unchecked; each was instead confirmed by hand against the committed interpro/panther/<PTHR>/<PTHR>-entries.csv table, which lists the same accession, gene symbol, organism, and subfamily. Part 1: Hedgehog ligand biogenesis and release (signal-sending cell)
Hedgehog autoprocessing, dual lipidation, and releaseBiological Processhh_ligand_biogenesis

The Hedgehog precursor is its own processing enzyme. Its C-terminal Hint/Hog domain cleaves the protein and couples cholesterol to the new C-terminus of the N-terminal signaling domain; a membrane-bound O-acyltransferase (HHAT in vertebrates, Rasp/Skinny hedgehog in Drosophila) then palmitoylates the new N-terminus. The doubly lipidated product is membrane-tethered and requires a dedicated release transporter to leave the producing cell.

Part 3: Smoothened activation

Part 4: Pathway-off state - kinase-driven Ci/GLI repressor production
PKA-primed phosphorylation and limited proteolysis of Ci/GLIRegulatory Stepci_gli_repressor_processing

Without ligand, protein kinase A phosphorylates Ci/GLI at a cluster of sites, priming further phosphorylation by casein kinase 1 and glycogen synthase kinase 3. The phosphodegron recruits the F-box protein Slimb/beta-TrCP within an SCF complex, and partial proteasomal degradation removes the C-terminal activation domain, leaving an N-terminal DNA-binding repressor that shuts off target genes. This kinase cassette is shared by flies and vertebrates.

Part 6: Transcriptional output
Ci/GLI activator-driven target-gene expressionRegulatory Stepci_gli_transcriptional_output

The activator form of Ci/GLI enters the nucleus and induces Hedgehog target genes, among them the pathway's own negative regulators (patched, and HHIP in vertebrates) and, in vertebrates, the amplifying paralog GLI1. The balance between activator and repressor forms, not the presence of ligand alone, is what the target genes read.
