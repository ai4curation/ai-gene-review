Activin signaling pathway in Drosophila melanogaster

The Drosophila activin branch of TGF-beta signaling is triggered by the activin-like ligands Activin-beta, Dawdle and Myoglianin. These ligands bind a receptor complex of the type I serine/threonine kinase Baboon and a type II receptor kinase (Punt, or Wishful thinking). The activated type I receptor phosphorylates the receptor-regulated SMAD Smox (dSmad2), which partners with the co-SMAD Medea and enters the nucleus to regulate target genes. The pathway acts in neuronal remodeling, cell and tissue growth, and metabolic homeostasis.
