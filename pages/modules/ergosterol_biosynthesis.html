Ergosterol biosynthesis (squalene -> lanosterol -> ergosterol)

Fungal sterol synthesis converts farnesyl diphosphate from the isoprenoid trunk into ergosterol, the principal sterol of fungal membranes, through about twenty reactions on the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane. The first half is shared with animal cholesterol synthesis. Squalene synthase reductively joins two farnesyl diphosphates into squalene, squalene epoxidase oxidises it to 2,3-oxidosqualene, and lanosterol synthase cyclises that to lanosterol. The cytochrome P450 sterol 14alpha-demethylase (CYP51) then removes the C-14 methyl, a Delta14-reductase saturates the resulting double bond, and a C-4 demethylation complex removes both C-4 methyls in two rounds: a C-4 methylsterol oxidase oxidises the methyl to a carboxylate, a 3beta-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase/C-4 decarboxylase releases it as CO2, and a 3-ketosteroid reductase restores the 3beta-hydroxyl. A membrane scaffold (Erg28) tethers the three C-4 demethylation enzymes, yielding zymosterol. The ergosterol-specific tail follows. A sterol C-24 methyltransferase adds the C-28 methyl group that animals lack. A C-8 sterol isomerase that is unrelated to the animal EBP isomerase moves the Delta8 bond to Delta7. A C-5 desaturase homologous to animal SC5D adds the Delta5 bond, a cytochrome P450 C-22 desaturase (CYP61) adds the side-chain Delta22 bond, and a C-24(28) reductase completes ergosterol. The P450 steps take electrons from NADPH-cytochrome P450 reductase, and the C-4 oxidase and C-5 desaturase use cytochrome b5, which can also support CYP51. The pathway is the target of the main antifungal drug classes: azoles (CYP51), allylamines such as terbinafine (squalene epoxidase) and morpholines (Delta14-reductase and C-8 isomerase).

The mevalonate and FPP-synthase segments of the superpathway are the separate mevalonate_pathway and isoprenoid_diphosphate_biosynthesis modules. The ERG10 review places ERG10 in the mevalonate segment that feeds this pathway. Structure. Part 1 (ERG9, ERG1, ERG7) conforms to sterol_commitment_squalene_to_lanosterol with identical tier functions; the only deviation is the connection type. Part 2 is one ordered 10-tier post-lanosterol bundle that conforms with deviations to cholesterol_synthesis_post_lanosterol. The shared core is CYP51 (ERG11), the C-4 methyl oxidase (ERG25 = MSMO1), the C-4 decarboxylase (ERG26 = NSDHL), a functionally equivalent but nonhomologous Delta8-Delta7 isomerase (ERG2 vs EBP) and the C-5 desaturase (ERG3 = SC5D). ERG24 and ERG27 are explicit tiers here but abbreviated in the template. YeastCyc corrections. ERG28 (C-4 demethylation scaffold), NCP1 (P450 reductase) and CYB5 (cytochrome b5) were added; YeastCyc does not list them. The three ERG28-family and C-4 complex members are grounded on PMID:12119386, PMID:15522820 and PMID:11160377. DAP1 (heme-binding stabiliser of Erg11, PMID:15713626) is recorded here as a regulator but not modelled as a part. Compartments: YeastCyc-derived GO-CAMs place every activity in cytosol. Here the enzymes are on the ER membrane, and Erg7 is also on lipid droplets (PMID:12842197). Ordering: ERG6 acts on zymosterol after C-4 demethylation, and ERG3 -> ERG5 -> ERG4 is the canonical YeastCyc order.
