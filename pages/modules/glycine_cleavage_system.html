Glycine cleavage system

A four-component lipoyl-relay system that oxidatively decarboxylates glycine, transfers its one-carbon unit to tetrahydrofolate, and regenerates the oxidized H-protein carrier. The P protein loads the aminomethyl intermediate onto the H protein, the H protein carries it to the T protein, the T protein forms 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate and ammonium, and the shared L protein reoxidizes the reduced lipoyl arm with NAD+. H-protein lipoylation, glycine-generating pathways, and downstream one-carbon metabolism are separate modules.

The boundary is the four-role catalytic cycle rather than a universal stable complex: P loads the H carrier, T transfers the one-carbon unit to tetrahydrofolate, and a potentially shared L enzyme reoxidizes H. H-protein lipoylation, glycine supply, downstream one-carbon metabolism, regulation, and clinical consequences remain outside the module. Representative members orient family roles and do not prescribe a species-specific composition. ALIGN with YeastPathways (2026-10): GLYCLEAV-PWY has the P (GCV2), T (GCV1) and L (LPD1) reactions, with correct catalysts, but omits the H-protein carrier GCV3 (YAL044C, PMID:9020168), which is added as an exemplar; the YeastCyc reactions are written as H-protein half-reactions and map one-to-one onto the P, T and L parts. S. cerevisiae and S. pombe exemplars were added to all four roles. The eukaryotic system is in the mitochondrial matrix (the yeast and fission-yeast proteins carry mitochondrial targeting signals); no location is asserted because the module also covers bacteria. In yeast it lets cells use glycine as a nitrogen source and supplies one-carbon units, so that mitochondrial SHMT can make serine from glycine (glycine_serine_interconversion). GO-CAM disagreements: YeastPathways_GLYCLEAV-PWY places all activities in cytosol, omits GCV3, and has no causal edges. PomBase 67b1629100001554 types the H protein with GO:0140104 molecular carrier activity, whereas this module keeps GO:0031405 lipoic acid binding to stay consistent with the human GCSH review; the carrier term arguably describes the swinging-arm role better and is worth revisiting. Its aim22 lipoate-protein ligase activity belongs to H-protein lipoylation, outside this module.
