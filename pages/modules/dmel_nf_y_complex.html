NF-Y CCAAT-binding factor in Drosophila melanogaster

NF-Y is a heterotrimeric transcription factor that binds the CCAAT box found in many eukaryotic promoters. Nf-YB and Nf-YC form a histone-fold dimer that binds DNA nonspecifically and creates the surface for Nf-YA, which confers sequence-specific recognition of CCAAT. In Drosophila, NF-Y regulates genes in eye and neural development and in cell proliferation.
