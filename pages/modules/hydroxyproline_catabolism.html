Bacterial trans-4-hydroxy-L-proline catabolism to 2-oxoglutarate

A reusable bacterial four-reaction route that converts trans-4-hydroxy-L-proline to 2-oxoglutarate. A PLP-independent epimerase first forms cis-4-hydroxy-D-proline. An FAD-dependent oxidoreductase generates 4-hydroxy-1-pyrroline-2-carboxylate, a deaminase converts that cyclic imine to 2,5-dioxopentanoate and ammonium, and an aldehyde dehydrogenase-family enzyme completes oxidation to 2-oxoglutarate.
