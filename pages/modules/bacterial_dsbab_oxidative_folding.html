Bacterial DsbA/DsbB oxidative protein folding

A bacterial envelope mechanism in which a membrane DsbB-family relay regenerates oxidized DsbA-family proteins, which introduce disulfides into exported substrate proteins. Quinone-coupled recycling is directly established in selected systems; native acceptor identity and isolated kinetics remain unresolved for the Bacillus and Campylobacter comparisons. Catalytic domains act in the Gram-negative periplasm or outside the Bacillus cytoplasmic membrane. Initial disulfide insertion does not guarantee the native disulfide pattern. DsbC/DsbD repair and DsbA/VKOR oxidation are distinct mechanisms.
