Cytoplasmic dynein and dynactin in Drosophila melanogaster

Cytoplasmic dyneins are multi-subunit minus-end-directed microtubule motors. Cytoplasmic dynein 1, built around the heavy chain Dhc64C, moves organelles, vesicles, mRNAs and chromosomes toward microtubule minus ends and acts in spindle assembly, nuclear positioning and oocyte polarity. It contains intermediate chains (Short wing, sw), light intermediate chains (Dlic) and LC8, Tctex and roadblock families of light chains. Dynein 1 is processive only when bound to dynactin and a cargo adaptor. Dynactin is a large complex with a short filament of Arp1 capped at the barbed end by capping protein and at the pointed end by a complex including Arp10 (Arp11), plus a projecting shoulder and arm formed by p150Glued (DCTN1-p150), p50 dynamitin (DCTN2-p50) and p24 (DCTN3-p24). Cytoplasmic dynein 2, with the heavy chain Beethoven (btv), drives retrograde intraflagellar transport in the cilia of sensory neurons.

Boundary. The FlyBase dynein light chain group (DYN-LC) also contains two axonemal light chains, ODA-Dnal1 (outer-arm LC1 homolog) and Dnali1 (inner-arm p28 homolog); they are excluded here and modelled in dmel_axonemal_dynein_arms. Cargo adaptors (for example BicD), Lis1 and NudE are regulators and not parts. The dynein 2 intermediate chains and dedicated light chains are not in the FlyBase groups and are not modelled. cpa and cpb also form the stand-alone actin capping protein (dmel_f_actin_capping_protein).
