RNA polymerase II elongation complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

After initiation, RNA polymerase II pauses shortly downstream of most Drosophila promoters, held by DSIF (Spt4-Spt5) and NELF. Release into productive elongation requires P-TEFb (Cdk9-CycT), which phosphorylates NELF, Spt5 and the polymerase C-terminal domain; P-TEFb is held inactive by Hexim within the 7SK snRNP and is also recruited within the super elongation complex (SEC), which carries the ELL elongation factor Su(Tpl). The related little elongation complex (LEC) promotes transcription of snRNA genes. During elongation, the PAF1 complex couples polymerase to co-transcriptional histone modification, FACT reorganizes nucleosomes in front of and behind the polymerase, and elongin stimulates the elongation rate.

Boundary. The module groups the FlyBase elongation-factor complexes; RNA polymerase II is in dmel_nuclear_rna_polymerases. Eaf and Su(Tpl) are members of both SEC and LEC, and ear of both SEC and TFIIF, as in FlyBase. P-TEFb is a component of SEC in mammals but FlyBase lists it as a separate group, which is followed here. The testis-specific PAF1C is modelled as a variant of PAF1C because its subunits are testis paralogs of the somatic subunits; Ski8 (homolog of the PAF1C subunit WDR61/SKIC8) is listed by FlyBase in the testis variant. The 7SK snRNA genes have no protein product and are grounded to FlyBase gene ids.
