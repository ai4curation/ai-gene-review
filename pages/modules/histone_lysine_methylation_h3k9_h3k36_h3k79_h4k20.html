Human histone lysine methylation outside the Polycomb and COMPASS systems — H3K9 methylation by SUV39H1/SUV39H2 (trimethylation in constitutive heterochromatin), the G9a/GLP (EHMT2/EHMT1) heteromer (mono- and dimethylation) and SETDB1 with ATF7IP (trimethylation, transposable element silencing); H3K36 dimethylation by NSD1, NSD2, NSD3 and ASH1L and trimethylation by SETD2 on elongating RNA polymerase II; H3K79 methylation by the non-SET enzyme DOT1L with AF10; and H4K20 monomethylation by KMT5A (SET8) followed by dimethylation by KMT5B/KMT5C

Histone lysines other than H3K4 and H3K27 are methylated by distinct families of S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent lysine methyltransferases, each specific for one site and for a defined number of methyl groups. Histone H3 lysine 9 (H3K9) is methylated by three systems. SUV39H1 and SUV39H2 trimethylate H3K9 at pericentric heterochromatin, building the H3K9me3 mark that HP1 proteins bind. G9a (EHMT2) and GLP (EHMT1) form a heteromeric complex that mono- and dimethylates H3K9 in euchromatin and silences genes. SETDB1, stabilised and stimulated by its partner ATF7IP, trimethylates H3K9 at promoters and at transposable elements, which it silences together with the HUSH complex and KRAB zinc-finger repressors. Histone H3 lysine 36 (H3K36) is mono- and dimethylated by the NSD family (NSD1, NSD2, NSD3) and ASH1L, which act on nucleosomes and are associated with gene activation, and is trimethylated by SETD2, the only human enzyme that makes H3K36me3; SETD2 binds the hyperphosphorylated C-terminal domain of elongating RNA polymerase II and its mark suppresses cryptic intragenic transcription initiation. Histone H3 lysine 79 (H3K79), in the nucleosome core, is mono-, di- and trimethylated by DOT1L, the only known lysine methyltransferase without a SET domain; its partner AF10 reads unmodified H3K27 and is required for H3K79 dimethylation, and mistargeting of DOT1L by MLL fusions drives leukaemia. Histone H4 lysine 20 (H4K20) is methylated in sequence: KMT5A (SET8, PR-Set7), a cell-cycle regulated nucleosome-specific enzyme, makes H4K20me1, which KMT5B and KMT5C (SUV420H1/2) convert to H4K20me2, the mark read by 53BP1 in non-homologous end joining; H4K20me3 at heterochromatin also depends on KMT5B/KMT5C in cells.

This module fills that gap, which is recorded in the notes of modules/histone_arginine_methylation.yaml ("Lysine methyltransferase gap"), by covering H3K9, H3K36, H3K79 and H4K20 methylation in one module with one part per site. PW:0001338 is the only PW evidence source; it has no PW children for lysine methylation, and its diagram names "Other complexes" that modify K9 and K36 without linking a PW id. Process concept. GO has obsoleted every histone lysine methylation process term as a molecular function, so the module concept is peptidyl-lysine methylation, matching histone_arginine_methylation's use of peptidyl-arginine methylation. Each part carries the process its mark serves, taken from experimental human GOA rows or gene review core functions: heterochromatin formation for the H3K9 part, with pericentric heterochromatin formation for SUV39H1/2 (IDA), constitutive heterochromatin formation as the SUV39H node concept, negative regulation of gene expression, epigenetic for G9a/GLP (EHMT1 review core) and transposable element silencing by heterochromatin formation for SETDB1 (IDA); transcription elongation by RNA polymerase II for SETD2 (IMP) and positive or general regulation of DNA-templated transcription for the H3K36 dimethylases (NSD1 IDA, NSD3 IMP, ASH1L review core, NSD2 authored); positive regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II for DOT1L (IDA/IMP); positive regulation of double-strand break repair via nonhomologous end joining for KMT5B/KMT5C (IMP). No new process term is requested, because GO's policy is to represent histone methylation as molecular functions, and the site-specific activity terms exist for every reaction modelled. Membership. EHMT1 and ASH1L have gene reviews in this repository, and their core functions are used unchanged (EHMT1 histone H3K9 methyltransferase activity with negative regulation of gene expression, epigenetic; ASH1L histone H3K36 dimethyltransferase activity with regulation of DNA-templated transcription). Modelling decisions. (1) One part per histone site, each a coexisting system, so the four parts are all required and the alternatives are within parts. (2) H3K9. SUV39H1 and SUV39H2 are a REALIZER variant set of single-subunit trimethylases at pericentric heterochromatin. G9a and GLP are one complex annoton because they act as a stoichiometric heteromer (detected in human HeLa and HEK293T cells, PMID:15774718); both subunits carry their own catalytic function and the complex carries the general H3K9 methyltransferase term with products restricted to me1 and me2. SETDB1 with ATF7IP is a complex annoton; ATF7IP's methyltransferase regulator activity is curator-authored (no experimental human GOA row on that term). The HUSH complex (TASOR, MPHOSPH8, PPHLN1, none on the RGD list) recruits SETDB1 to H3K9me3-rich loci but is not a stable unit of the SETDB1-ATF7IP complex, and MPP8 is a reader, so HUSH is not modelled; nor are KAP-1/TRIM28 and the KRAB zinc-finger proteins, which recruit SETDB1. No PRECEDES link is drawn between the H3K9 systems: SUV39H enzymes prefer H3K9me1 (PMID:23200123) but the human source of that substrate (G9a/GLP, SETDB1 or the cytoplasmic PRDM route) is not settled, and the reported multimeric complex of SUV39H1, G9a, GLP and SETDB1 is not used. (3) H3K36. NSD1, NSD2, NSD3 and ASH1L are a REALIZER variant set of mono/dimethylases and SETD2 is a separate part as the sole trimethylase (in human myeloma cells SETD2 depletion lowers H3K36me3 but not me2, PMID:22099308). No PRECEDES connection is drawn from the dimethylases to SETD2: SETD2 performs all three steps on unmodified nucleosomes in vitro (PMID:26002201), and whether it uses NSD-made H3K36me2 as its substrate in human cells is not established in the sources used. NSD1 and NSD3 carry the general H3K36 term, as no experimental row asserts the dimethyl child for them. SETD2's coupling to elongating Pol II rests on its association with hyperphosphorylated Pol II (PMID:16118227); the process term transcription elongation by RNA polymerase II is GOA's IMP row and is read as the elongation-coupled chromatin step. (4) H3K79. DOT1L with AF10 is one complex annoton. AF10 binds DOT1L directly and its PZP reader couples unmodified H3K27 recognition to H3K79 dimethylation, so it is a stable, role-bearing unit. The other DotCom proteins found by proteomics in human cells (ENL/MLLT1, AF9/MLLT3, AF17/MLLT6, TRRAP, SKP1, beta-catenin; PMID:20203130) are not modelled: ENL and AF9 are also subunits of the super elongation complex, their contribution to H3K79 methylation in human cells is shown mainly with leukaemic fusion proteins, and the Wnt factors are not methylation units. (5) H4K20. KMT5A monomethylation PRECEDES KMT5B/KMT5C dimethylation (a REALIZER variant set), the product-substrate chain of RHEA:60344 and RHEA:60348; the connection carries chaining_status NOT_APPLICABLE because the intermediate is a methyl-lysine residue, not a CoA carrier. The H4K20 trimethyltransferase term is not used; the identity of the H4K20me3 writer is a knowledge gap. (6) Chemistry. SAM, SAH, H+ and the unmodified, mono-, di- and trimethyl L-lysyl residue states are grounded from the Rhea reactions cited on each function (RHEA:60276, 60280, 60284, 60288, 60308, 60312, 60316, 60320, 60324, 60328, 60344, 60348), with ChEBI labels checked in OLS (November 2026). The histones are substrates, not participants, and are not listed as units. (7) Authored functions not backed by an experimental GOA row on that exact term or a gene review core function: ATF7IP methyltransferase regulator activity; NSD2 positive regulation of DNA-templated transcription (process). MLLT10 histone binding is its IPI row. (8) Readers. Chromodomain, ankyrin and Tudor reading by the enzymes themselves (SUV39H1/2, EHMT1, SETDB1) is recorded in role text, not as separate activities. HP1/CBX5, MPP8 and other readers of the marks are outside the module. Excluded enzymes. SETD7 (SET7/9) was identified as an H3K4 monomethyltransferase on free histones (EXP, PMID:12540855), not on nucleosomes, and its many experimental GOA rows are for non-histone substrates (p53, TAF10, ER alpha and others); under the CLAUDE.md rule that measured activity is not automatically function it is treated as a non-histone protein-lysine methyltransferase and excluded, and its H3K4 activity in any case belongs to compass_family_h3k4_methylation's site rather than to this module. SMYD1, SMYD2 and SMYD3 have no experimental histone methyltransferase row in human GOA except one SMYD3 IMP row for histone H4 methyltransferase activity (PMID:25738358); SMYD2's experimental rows are p53 methylation, and SMYD1 is a muscle cofactor; they are excluded. The PRDMs are excluded: PRDM2, PRDM6 and PRDM8 have weak or in vitro histone activity, PRDM16 and MECOM's cytoplasmic H3K9 monomethylation rests mainly on mouse studies (the human GO-CAM model 682fbcd000002172 draws it), and PRDM9's robust H3K4/H3K36 trimethylation designates meiotic recombination hotspots, a different mechanism that would belong in a meiotic recombination module. KMT2E is left out as in compass_family_h3k4_methylation. The H3K9, H3K36 and H4K20 demethylases are in histone_lysine_demethylation; H3K27 methylation and its antagonism by H3K36 methylation are in polycomb_repressive_complexes; H3K4 methylation is in compass_family_h3k4_methylation.
