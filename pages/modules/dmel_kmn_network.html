KMN network of the outer kinetochore in Drosophila melanogaster

The KMN network (Knl1, Mis12 complex and Ndc80 complex) is the core microtubule-binding module of the outer kinetochore. The Mis12 complex links the network to the centromere-proximal inner kinetochore; in Drosophila it binds the CENP-C homolog Cenp-C, because flies lack the CENP-T pathway. The Ndc80 complex binds spindle microtubules directly and forms load-bearing attachments. Knl1 (Spc105R in Drosophila) provides a scaffold for spindle assembly checkpoint proteins and for protein phosphatase 1. Drosophila has two Nnf1 paralogs, Nnf1a and Nnf1b, which form alternative Mis12 complexes.
