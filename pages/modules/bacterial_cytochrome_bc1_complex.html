Bacterial cytochrome bc1 complex (ubiquinol:cytochrome c oxidoreductase)

Reusable bacterial module for the three-subunit cytochrome bc1 complex (respiratory complex III). PetA supplies the mobile Rieske 2Fe-2S branch, PetB is the transmembrane cytochrome b core containing the two quinone sites and b hemes, and PetC is the cytochrome c1 output branch. Together they oxidize quinol, reduce periplasmic cytochrome c, and operate the Q cycle to support vectorial proton movement. The module excludes unrelated KEGG ppu00190 complexes and the non-homologous alternative complex III systems.
