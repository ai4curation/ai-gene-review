RNase P and RNase MRP complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

Ribonuclease P removes the 5' leader from precursor tRNAs. Nuclear RNase P is a ribonucleoprotein whose catalytic RNA (RNaseP:RNA) is bound by several proteins; the related RNase MRP shares most of these proteins but has its own RNA (RNaseMRP:RNA) and cleaves other substrates, including pre-rRNA. In mitochondria, tRNA 5' processing is carried out by a protein-only RNase P made of a tRNA methyltransferase subunit (rswl, MRPP1 homolog), a short-chain dehydrogenase subunit (scu, MRPP2/HSD17B10 homolog) and the PRORP-type nuclease subunit (mldr).

Substrate specificity of Drosophila RNase MRP has not been characterized directly and is inferred from other eukaryotes.
