Bacterial RNase E RNA degradosome

Reusable gammaproteobacterial RNase E-centered RNA degradosome module. RNase E provides the scaffold and initiating endoribonuclease, a recruited DEAD-box helicase can unwind structured RNA, and a 3'-to-5' exoribonuclease degrades exposed RNA. RhlB/PNPase and RhlE/RNase R are represented as lineage-variable implementations rather than a fixed universal composition.

The boundary is an RNase E scaffold plus conditional helicase-assisted and exonuclease-mediated RNA decay. Exact partner composition varies across Gammaproteobacteria. RhlB and PNPase are candidates in KT2440, but RhlE-family helicases and RNase R are plausible alternatives, so the PSEPK realization is not asserted as resolved. Enolase is not included because its degradosome association has not been demonstrated in PSEPK. PAP I, RppH, Hfq, polyphosphate enzymes, chaperones, RecQ, and Rho are adjacent RNA-metabolism or stress proteins outside this boundary. Connections describe a conditional structured-RNA decay route, not an obligatory sequence for every substrate. Molecular functions are asserted only on activity leaves.
