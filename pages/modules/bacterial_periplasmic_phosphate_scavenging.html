E. coli periplasmic organophosphate scavenging

A phosphate-starvation effector module in which E. coli periplasmic phosphatases liberate inorganic phosphate from extracellular-facing organophosphate substrates. PhoA hydrolyzes phosphate monoesters through its alkaline phosphatase activity, while AppA contributes a phytase/acid phosphatase branch that is induced by phosphate starvation and stationary phase. Sec export, PhoB/PhoR transcriptional induction, and Pst phosphate import are adjacent processes outside this mature periplasmic scavenging boundary.
