Mitochondrial ribosome in Drosophila melanogaster

The mitochondrial ribosome (mitoribosome) translates the small set of mitochondrial-DNA-encoded mRNAs, which in animals encode core subunits of the respiratory chain and ATP synthase. It consists of a small subunit built on the mitochondrially encoded 12S-type rRNA (mt:srRNA) and a large subunit built on the 16S-type rRNA (mt:lrRNA), together with nuclear-encoded mitochondrial ribosomal proteins. Animal mitoribosomes are protein-rich: they contain bacterial-type ribosomal proteins plus many mitochondria-specific proteins. In Drosophila, loss of the small-subunit protein tko (mRpS12) causes the technical knockout phenotype of bang sensitivity.
