mTOR complex signaling — mTORC1 (MTOR-RPTOR-MLST8, PRAS40, DEPTOR) and mTORC2 (MTOR-RICTOR-MAPKAP1-MLST8, PRR5/PRR5L) kinases, RHEB activation on the lysosome under TSC control, and amino acid sensing through Rag-Ragulator, GATOR1, GATOR2, FLCN-FNIP, Sestrin2, CASTOR1 and SAMTOR

The serine/threonine kinase mTOR acts in two multiprotein complexes. mTOR complex 1 (mTORC1) contains mTOR, the substrate-recruiting scaffold raptor (RPTOR) and mLST8, and is held in check by two inhibitory subunits, PRAS40 (AKT1S1) and DEPTOR. mTOR complex 2 (mTORC2) contains mTOR, mLST8, the scaffold rictor (RICTOR), mSIN1 (MAPKAP1) and one of the Protor proteins (PRR5 or PRR5L), and DEPTOR also binds and inhibits it. mTORC1 is the growth controller: it phosphorylates S6K1 and S6K2, 4E-BP1, ULK1, TFEB and lipin-1, promoting translation, ribosome biogenesis and lipid synthesis and suppressing autophagy and lysosome biogenesis. mTORC2 phosphorylates the hydrophobic motifs of AGC kinases, most notably AKT on Ser473, SGK1 and protein kinase C, and so feeds into PI3K-AKT signalling and cytoskeletal control. mTORC1 is activated on the cytosolic face of lysosomes only when two inputs coincide. Growth factor and energy signals act through the small GTPase RHEB, which binds mTOR and allosterically activates the kinase; the TSC1-TSC2-TBC1D7 complex is the GTPase-activating protein that turns RHEB off, and AKT and AMPK act on it in opposite directions. Amino acid sufficiency acts through the heterodimeric Rag GTPases (RagA or RagB with RagC or RagD), which are anchored to the lysosome by the pentameric Ragulator complex (LAMTOR1-5) and, in the RagA/B-GTP and RagC/D-GDP state, bind raptor and recruit mTORC1 to the lysosomal surface where RHEB resides. The nucleotide state of the Rags is set by GATOR1 (DEPDC5, NPRL2, NPRL3), a GTPase-activating protein for RagA/B that inhibits the pathway; by GATOR2 (MIOS, WDR24, WDR59, SEH1L, SEC13), which inhibits GATOR1; and by the folliculin-FNIP complex, a GTPase-activating protein for RagC/D that is needed for mTORC1 activation and especially for TFEB phosphorylation. Cytosolic sensors connect specific amino acids to GATOR: Sestrin2 binds GATOR2 until leucine binds Sestrin2, CASTOR1 binds GATOR2 until arginine binds CASTOR1, and SAMTOR binds GATOR1 until S-adenosylmethionine binds SAMTOR. Folding and assembly of mTOR into both complexes depends on the TEL2-TTI1-TTI2 co-chaperone complex.
