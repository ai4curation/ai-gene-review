Human ADAT2:ADAT3 tRNA wobble I34 editing complex

The human ADAT2:ADAT3 heterodimer edits adenosine 34 to inosine in several precursor tRNA families in the nucleoplasm. ADAT2 supplies the zinc-dependent catalytic deaminase active site, while the catalytically inactive ADAT3 subunit recognizes tRNA substrates and positions their anticodon loops for A34 deamination.
