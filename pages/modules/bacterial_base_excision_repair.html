Bacterial base-excision repair system

A reusable bacterial DNA-damage response module in which lesion-specific DNA glycosylases remove small damaged bases, AP-site processing enzymes create a repairable 3-prime hydroxyl, DNA polymerase I restores the missing nucleotide, and an NAD-dependent DNA ligase seals the nick. The bacterial MutT-MutY-MutM 8-oxoguanine defense is represented across nucleotide-pool sanitation and lesion excision. Nucleotide excision repair, mismatch repair, double-strand break repair, and damage signaling are outside this module.

Species-aware retrieval identifies a complete KT2440 BER relay, an Xth-only AP-endonuclease architecture, a missing MutT candidate, and a spurious Crc hit.

The module is species-neutral. Pseudomonas putida KT2440 supplies concrete representatives for every role. Organisms vary in their glycosylase repertoire and may use Nfo/Endonuclease IV instead of or in addition to Xth-family AP endonucleases. MutT is an upstream preventive branch rather than a DNA-bound intermediate-processing step and therefore has no PRECEDES connection.

  • ung_excision → xth_processing [NOT_CHECKED]
    Ung creates an AP site that is incised by a class II AP endonuclease.
  • mutm_excision → xth_processing [NOT_CHECKED]
    MutM class I AP-lyase cleavage leaves blocked termini. Xth-family homologs can clean such 3-prime ends, but that secondary chemistry is not asserted as a separate GO function for the untested PSEPK target.
  • muty_excision → xth_processing [NOT_CHECKED]
    MutY creates an AP site after adenine excision.
  • nth_excision → xth_processing [NOT_CHECKED]
    Nth beta elimination leaves a blocked 3-prime terminus. Xth-family homologs have phosphodiesterase/phosphatase activities, but the exact blocked-end specificity remains to be tested for PSEPK XthA.
  • tag_excision → xth_processing [NOT_CHECKED]
    Tag creates an AP site after 3-methyladenine excision.
  • xth_processing → pola_synthesis [NOT_CHECKED]
    AP-site incision creates a 3-prime hydroxyl; class I AP-lyase products additionally require blocked-end cleanup before synthesis.
  • pola_synthesis → liga_sealing [NOT_CHECKED]
    DNA polymerase I gap filling leaves the nick sealed by LigA.
