Beta-cell glucose-stimulated insulin secretion (K-ATP triggering pathway)

The triggering pathway by which the pancreatic beta cell converts a rise in blood glucose into insulin release. Glucokinase phosphorylates glucose at a rate that tracks the physiological glucose range and so sets the threshold of the pathway; glycolytic and mitochondrial oxidation of the resulting glucose 6-phosphate raises the cytosolic ATP/ADP ratio; the rise in ATP/ADP closes the ATP-sensitive potassium (K-ATP) channel formed by the Kir6.2 pore and the SUR1 regulatory subunit; the loss of K+ efflux depolarises the plasma membrane, opening L-type voltage-gated calcium channels; and the Ca2+ influx triggers exocytosis of insulin-containing dense-core granules. Glutamate dehydrogenase couples amino-acid (leucine/glutamine) oxidation into the same ATP/ADP signal, and the mitochondrial enzyme HADH restrains glutamate dehydrogenase; gain of the first and loss of the second both produce hyperinsulinism.

Scope: the K-ATP-dependent triggering pathway only. The K-ATP-independent amplifying pathway, incretin (GLP-1/GIP) and neural potentiation, insulin biosynthesis and prohormone processing (PCSK1/PCSK2/CPE), and the downstream actions of secreted insulin (modules/insulin_receptor_signaling.yaml) are outside the boundary. Steps described but not modelled as annotons, because no member has a reviewed gene record in this repository yet: beta-cell glucose uptake (GLUT2/SLC2A2 in rodents; GLUT1/SLC2A1 is the dominant human beta-cell glucose transporter), the glycolytic and mitochondrial machinery that converts glucose 6-phosphate into ATP (pyruvate carboxylase, the TCA cycle and oxidative phosphorylation), and the granule exocytosis machinery (SNAP25, syntaxin-1A, synaptotagmin-7 and VAMP2). These should be added as annotons once those genes are reviewed; the mitochondrial ATP step is represented here as a connection description rather than a part. Glucokinase glucose phosphorylation (glucose sensor)Reactionglucose_sensing

Part 2: K-ATP channel closure
ATP-sensitive potassium channel closureProtein Complexkatp_channel_closure

The octameric K-ATP channel (four Kir6.2 pore subunits, four SUR1 regulatory subunits) holds the resting beta cell hyperpolarised. ATP binding to Kir6.2 closes the pore, while Mg-ADP acting on the SUR1 nucleotide-binding domains opposes closure, so the channel reads the cytosolic ATP/ADP ratio.

Part 5: amino-acid coupling and its brake (modifiers)
Glutamate dehydrogenase coupling of amino-acid oxidation, restrained by HADHRegulatory Stepgdh_amino_acid_coupling

Glutamate dehydrogenase converts glutamate to alpha-ketoglutarate in the mitochondrial matrix, feeding the TCA cycle and raising ATP; it is allosterically activated by leucine and ADP and inhibited by GTP. HADH (SCHAD) binds and inhibits glutamate dehydrogenase in beta cells. These are modifiers of the triggering pathway rather than steps of it.
