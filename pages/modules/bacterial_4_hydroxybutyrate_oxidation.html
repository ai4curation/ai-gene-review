Bacterial free-acid 4-hydroxybutyrate oxidation to succinate

Two consecutive non-CoA reactions oxidize 4-hydroxybutyrate to succinate: NAD-dependent Gbd forms succinate semialdehyde, and a nonacylating succinate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase forms succinate. The experimentally supported implementation is grounded in selected or experimentally expressing Cupriavidus necator backgrounds. Pseudomonas putida candidates and a distinct 3-hydroxybutyrate enzyme provide comparative context without establishing a complete native free-acid route. Uptake, upstream diol oxidation, GABA supply, CoA-dependent beta-oxidation, PHA synthesis and reverse succinate degradation are outside the boundary.
