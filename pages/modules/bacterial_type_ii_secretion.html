Bacterial type II secretion

A species-neutral module for export of folded proteins from the periplasm across the outer membrane by the type II secretion system. A multiprotein inner-membrane platform recruits the secretion ATPase and supports assembly of a dynamic pseudopilus, which displaces substrates through an oligomeric outer-membrane secretin. Delivery of substrates into the periplasm by Sec or Tat, substrate folding, and unrelated fimbrial or pilus systems are outside this module.

Reusable boundary: the module begins with the assembled type II secretion machine acting on folded periplasmic substrates and ends with passage through the outer-membrane secretin. Sec and Tat are upstream substrate-delivery routes, not parts of this module. Type IV pili share homologous ATPase, platform, prepilin, and secretin folds but are distinct surface structures; unrelated fimbrial proteins must not be admitted solely from shared domains. GspN-like proteins are lineage-variable accessory factors and are not forced into the conserved four-part core.
