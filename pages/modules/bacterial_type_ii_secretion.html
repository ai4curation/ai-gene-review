Bacterial type II secretion

A species-neutral module for export of folded proteins from the periplasm across the outer membrane by the type II secretion system of diderm (Gram-negative) bacteria. A multiprotein inner-membrane platform recruits the secretion ATPase and supports assembly of a dynamic pseudopilus, which displaces substrates through an oligomeric outer-membrane secretin. The pseudopilin subunits are matured by a prepilin peptidase shared with type IV pilus and competence systems. Delivery of substrates into the periplasm by Sec or Tat, substrate folding, and unrelated fimbrial or pilus systems are outside this module.

Reusable boundary: the module begins with the assembled type II secretion machine acting on folded periplasmic substrates and ends with passage through the outer-membrane secretin. Sec and Tat are upstream substrate-delivery routes, not parts of this module. Type IV pili share homologous ATPase, platform, prepilin, and secretin folds but are distinct surface structures; unrelated fimbrial proteins must not be admitted solely from shared domains. GspN-like proteins are lineage-variable accessory factors and are not forced into the conserved four-part core. Taxonomic scope: the machinery is confined to diderm bacteria, since it ends at an outer membrane, and every characterized system belongs to the Pseudomonadota (Klebsiella Pul, Vibrio Eps, Dickeya and Pectobacterium Out, Pseudomonas Xcp and Hxc, Aeromonas Exe, Xanthomonas Xps). The module's taxon context is therefore recorded as Bacteria with that restriction stated here rather than as a narrower taxon, because secretin-based systems also occur in other diderm lineages whose machinery is not characterized. Exemplar policy: each family descriptor carries the Pseudomonas putida KT2440 member of the batch this module came from plus the corresponding Pseudomonas aeruginosa Xcp protein, the best-characterized complete system, and the Klebsiella PulC connector. The criterion for admitting an exemplar is that it has a gene review in which every annotation is actioned, with no PENDING or UNDECIDED row left; that is the sense in which the module's derived QC calls a review complete, and it reports 24 of 24 for the groundings here. The review files themselves differ in their declared status: the KT2440 set, PSEAE pilD, PSEAE xcpQ (promoted in the change that added its core molecular function) and the five reviews written for this module are COMPLETE, while PSEAE xcpR, xcpS, xcpV, xcpW and xcpX are still DRAFT although fully actioned, having come from the earlier PseudoCAP batch. Promoting those five is a judgement on their own evidence rather than on this module, so it is left to a review of them. Klebsiella Pul, Vibrio Eps and Dickeya Out members of the remaining parts (PulD, PulE, PulF, PulG, PulI, PulJ, PulK, PulL, PulM and their Eps and Out counterparts) are the natural next exemplars and are named in the family reviews under interpro/panther/; they are not listed here until those gene reviews exist, so that the module's grounding stays checkable. Family grounding: the PANTHER families of the machinery are reviewed in interpro/panther/PTHR30012, PTHR30093, PTHR30258, PTHR30332, PTHR30487, PTHR38779, PTHR38831 and PTHR39583, and each descriptor below states its own grounding and the reason for it; this paragraph only summarises them. Three roles use a PANTHER grounding. PTHR38831 (GspK) and PTHR30487 (the prepilin peptidase) are used at family level: every reviewed member of the first is a T2SS GspK protein, and every reviewed member of the second is an A24 prepilin peptidase, an enzyme that type II secretion shares with type IV pilus and competence systems by serving all of them. PTHR38779:SF2 (GspI) and PTHR39583:SF2 (GspJ) are used at subfamily level: SF2 of PTHR38779 holds only GspI proteins, while SF2 of PTHR39583 holds the GspJ clade together with Bacillus ComGD and a Thermus pilin-like protein, which is why its family review scopes the T2SS terms to the proteobacterial clade and the node PAINT restricts to Pseudomonadota. The other roles use a T2SS-specific InterPro or NCBIfam signature that both exemplars carry, because the PANTHER subfamily either crosses systems or leaves an in-scope one out: GspF (SF0 also holds the competence protein ComGB), GspG (SF44 also holds three reviewed type IV major pilins and, through UniProt, the GspH minor pseudopilins), GspD (SF24 also holds filamentous-phage pIV proteins and excludes the Xanthomonas secretin XpsD of SF25) and GspE (SF27 excludes both the Xanthomonas XpsE and the Pseudomonas HxcR ATPases). GspH, GspL and GspM use a signature for a different reason: PANTHER places their exemplars in no family of their own, the P. aeruginosa GspH being assigned to the GspG subfamily SF44 and the GspL and GspM exemplars to no family at all. GspM is the one role InterPro splits between two sibling family entries, one per exemplar, so it declares both. GspC is left without a family term because no one entry covers its exemplars: the Klebsiella connector carries the GspC family entry IPR001639 with NCBIfam TIGR01713, while the Pseudomonas XcpP proteins match no InterPro entry and no PANTHER family.
