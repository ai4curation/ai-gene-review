Glutathione-dependent formaldehyde detoxification

A detoxification module found in bacteria and eukaryotes in which formaldehyde is captured by glutathione as S-(hydroxymethyl)glutathione, the hemithioacetal is oxidized to S-formylglutathione by a class-III alcohol dehydrogenase, and the thioester is hydrolyzed to formate while regenerating glutathione. Chemical capture, oxidation, and hydrolysis are represented as separate steps; formate can then be oxidized to CO2 by an NAD-dependent formate dehydrogenase.

The boundary begins with glutathione capture of formaldehyde and ends at formate plus regenerated glutathione. Formate oxidation to carbon dioxide, formaldehyde efflux, DNA repair, protein-quality control, and nitrosothiol reduction are distinct downstream or parallel functions. Organisms can carry additional formaldehyde oxidation routes, so this module represents the glutathione-dependent branch rather than the entire cellular response. In KT2440, PP_3970 has genetic evidence for a contribution to formaldehyde mineralization, but that does not place it in the defined FrmA-FrmC reaction sequence. The literature uses conflicting names and glutathione-dependence assignments for PP_3970, while UniProtKB:Q88FV8 currently assigns only a generic zinc-dependent, NAD(P)-binding oxidoreductase function. Its precise chemistry therefore remains unresolved. The exact contribution of PP_1616/FrmA and PP_1617/FrmC to this branch remains based on homology and should be tested directly. ALIGN (YeastPathways project, 2026-10): the bacterial module was widened to bacteria and eukaryotes. S. cerevisiae SFA1 (P32771) shares PANTHER PTHR43880:SF12 with E. coli FrmA, and YJL068C (P40363) and human ESD share PTHR10061 with FrmB/YeiG. Human ADH5 is in PTHR43880:SF4 and is therefore not listed under the SF12 selector. SFA1 is also the yeast S-nitrosoglutathione reductase; that activity is outside this module. The YeastCyc formate dehydrogenase step (FDH1) was added as an optional part 4, because formate oxidation is not required for formaldehyde detoxification. FDH2 is a pseudogene split into YPL275W/YPL276W in S288C. GO-CAM disagreement: the SGD model attaches SFA1 only to the root molecular_function term in cytoplasm, while GO:0051903 is directly supported. No PomBase model is linked for this group. The KT2440 PP_3970 gap was reclassified as BIOLOGY because its substrate and glutathione dependence need direct enzymology.

Part 1: Glutathione capture of formaldehyde
S-(hydroxymethyl)glutathione formationReactionformaldehyde_glutathione_capture

Formaldehyde reacts with glutathione to form the hemithioacetal S-(hydroxymethyl)glutathione, which feeds the subsequent dehydrogenase step. This capture is spontaneous and has no gene participant.

Part 2: Hemithioacetal oxidation
S-(hydroxymethyl)glutathione oxidationReactionfrma_hemithioacetal_oxidation

A class-3 alcohol dehydrogenase oxidizes S-(hydroxymethyl)glutathione to S-formylglutathione. The GO term permits NAD(P)+, whereas characterized family members are NAD+-biased.
