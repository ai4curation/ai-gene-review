CCR4-NOT deadenylase complex in Drosophila melanogaster

CCR4-NOT is the major cytoplasmic mRNA deadenylase in eukaryotes and also acts in transcription and translational repression. Its large scaffold subunit Not1 binds two deadenylases, Pop2 (CAF1) and twin (CCR4), which shorten poly(A) tails, the first step of most mRNA decay. Not1 also binds Rcd-1 (CNOT9), Not10 and Not11, and a NOT module of Rga (Not2) and Not3. RNA-binding proteins, microRNA silencing complexes and translational repressors recruit CCR4-NOT to specific mRNAs; in Drosophila it controls maternal mRNA decay and germline stem cell maintenance.
