Mitochondrial cardiolipin metabolism — TAMM41 CDP-diacylglycerol synthesis, PGS1/PTPMT1 phosphatidylglycerol synthesis, CRLS1 cardiolipin synthase and deacylation-reacylation remodelling by phospholipases and tafazzin

Cardiolipin is a dimeric phospholipid, two phosphatidyl groups joined through a central glycerol and carrying four acyl chains, that is made in and largely confined to the mitochondrial inner membrane, where it supports the respiratory chain complexes, ATP synthase, the ADP/ATP carrier and cristae structure. Its synthesis is a four-step pathway on the matrix side of the inner membrane. Phosphatidic acid, which must first be moved from the outer to the inner membrane by the intermembrane-space PRELID1-TRIAP1 lipid transfer complex, is activated with CTP to CDP-diacylglycerol by the mitochondrial CDP-diacylglycerol synthase TAMM41. PGS1 transfers the phosphatidyl group of CDP-diacylglycerol to sn-glycerol 3-phosphate, forming phosphatidylglycerophosphate, the committed step. The protein tyrosine phosphatase-like enzyme PTPMT1 dephosphorylates it to phosphatidylglycerol, and cardiolipin synthase CRLS1 condenses phosphatidylglycerol with a second CDP-diacylglycerol to give nascent cardiolipin and CMP. Nascent cardiolipin carries mixed, often saturated acyl chains and is remodelled to its mature, unsaturated (in heart mostly tetralinoleoyl) form by deacylation and reacylation. A phospholipase removes one acyl chain to give monolysocardiolipin; candidates are the alpha/beta hydrolase ABHD18 and the calcium-independent phospholipases A2 PLA2G6 (iPLA2-beta) and PNPLA8 (iPLA2-gamma). The CoA-independent transacylase tafazzin (TAFAZZIN) then transfers an acyl chain from another phospholipid, mainly phosphatidylcholine, to monolysocardiolipin; acyl-CoA-dependent monolysocardiolipin acyltransferase activity of the trifunctional protein alpha subunit (HADHA) has also been reported. Loss of tafazzin causes Barth syndrome, with monolysocardiolipin accumulation and cardiomyopathy, and biallelic variants in TAMM41, PTPMT1 and CRLS1 cause mitochondrial disease.

Derived from the RGD Pathway Ontology record and diagram PW:0001344 (cardiolipin metabolic pathway) through the RGD_PATHWAY_ONTOLOGY project's diagram->module route; GO mapping GO:0032048 cardiolipin metabolic process (skos:closeMatch). Of the 10 RGD human genes, TAMM41, PGS1, PTPMT1, CRLS1 and TAFAZZIN are participants. The module is modelled as eight parts, two of them optional: (1, optional) PRELID1-TRIAP1 phosphatidic acid transfer; (2) TAMM41 CDP-diacylglycerol synthesis; (3) PGS1; (4) PTPMT1; (5) CRLS1; (6) a variant set of deacylating phospholipases (ABHD18, PLA2G6, PNPLA8); (7) tafazzin transacylation; and (8, optional) HADHA acyl-CoA-dependent monolysocardiolipin reacylation. ChEBI ids are the Rhea participants of RHEA:16229, RHEA:12593, RHEA:33751, RHEA:32931, RHEA:32935, RHEA:33731 and RHEA:35839, with labels checked against OLS. RGD record error: the record text says that in the first step "Tamm41 uses cytidine triphosphate and phosphatidylglycerol"; the substrate is phosphatidic acid, as the RGD diagram itself draws (phosphatidic acid and CTP to CDP-diacylglycerol). The module follows the diagram. Boundary decisions. (a) The outer branch of the RGD diagram is not modelled: translocation of cardiolipin to the outer membrane (with CKMT1A, CKMT1B and NME4 bridging the membranes and the scramblase PLSCR3), its peroxidation by cytochrome c (CYCS), and its signalling roles in mitophagy and apoptosis. These genes do not make or remodel cardiolipin; cardiolipin is their binding partner or substrate, and their roles belong to modules/mitophagy.yaml and modules/intrinsic_apoptotic_signaling.yaml (RGD PW:0001842 and PW:0000104, mentioned here only as diagram links). Creatine kinase and nucleoside diphosphate kinase are also kinases of energy transfer, not lipid enzymes. The diagram's downstream outputs (electron transport chain, ATP synthesis, metabolite carriers) are uses of cardiolipin and are likewise out of scope. (b) PRELID1-TRIAP1 is included as an optional transport step rather than context because it performs the delivery of the TAMM41 substrate to the inner membrane (direct lipid transfer activity, PMID:23931759), not merely because it is required; it is optional because it is substrate supply and is not drawn by RGD. Other PRELI-domain pairs (PRELID3B with TRIAP1, transferring phosphatidylserine for PE synthesis) are not part of this pathway. (c) The CDP-diacylglycerol step is modelled directly with human TAMM41 rather than by module_ref to cdp_dag_phospholipid_synthesis#cdp_dag_synthesis (or its mito_tam41 variant): that module is scoped to Fungi with S. cerevisiae and S. pombe exemplars, and a module_ref node carries no annotons, so referencing it would drop TAMM41, an RGD-curated human member, and assert fungal proteins in a concrete human module. The two modules describe the same reaction, and the fungal module's PGS1, GEP4 and CRD1 nodes are the fungal counterparts of parts 3 to 5. The ER CDS1/CDS2 synthases are not included: they supply phosphatidylinositol synthesis, not mitochondrial cardiolipin. (d) Remodelling. Deacylation is a variant set because no single human enzyme is established: ABHD18 (mouse and fly genetics, PMID:40378955), PLA2G6 (in vitro cardiolipin hydrolysis, PMID:23533611; the human GO-CAM uses it) and PNPLA8 (oxidised cardiolipin, PMID:28442572; no remodelling process asserted on it). Tafazzin is a required part because its loss alone causes Barth syndrome. HADHA is an optional acyl-CoA-dependent reacylation route. LCLAT1 (ALCAT1, AGPAT8), an ER multi-pass acyltransferase that UniProt describes as an acyl-CoA:lysocardiolipin acyltransferase only by similarity, is not included: two studies of the human protein found no lysocardiolipin acyltransferase activity attributable to it (PMID:19075029, PMID:16620771), its experimental GOA function is lysophosphatidic acid acyltransferase activity, and its ER location places it away from the inner-membrane cardiolipin pool. It could be added as an optional variant of part 8 if later human evidence supports it. Other phospholipases reported to act on cardiolipin (secreted and cytosolic PLA2s) are not included. (e) Fly and bacterial counterparts: modules/dmel_cardiolipin_biosynthesis_remodeling.yaml models the same Pgs1-Ptpmt1-Cls-Taz chain with Abhd18 and iPLA2-VIA deacylation (it leaves tafazzin without a function term); modules/bacterial_phosphatidylglycerol_cardiolipin_biosynthesis.yaml covers the bacterial route, whose phospholipase D-type cardiolipin synthase condenses two phosphatidylglycerols. Neither is referenced as a part. (f) Not modelled: cardiolipin degradation beyond remodelling, the regulatory condensate scaffold SPATA18 (Mieap) present in the GO-CAM as a positive regulator of the enzymes, and transcriptional control. Part 1: phosphatidic acid supply to the inner membrane (optional)
PRELID1-TRIAP1 transfer of phosphatidic acid across the intermembrane spaceTransport Steppa_intermembrane_transfer

Phosphatidic acid made in the endoplasmic reticulum reaches the outer mitochondrial membrane and is carried across the intermembrane space to the inner membrane by a heterodimer of the PRELI-domain lipid transfer protein PRELID1 and its chaperone TRIAP1, a mammalian counterpart of the yeast Ups1-Mdm35 pair. Loss of either subunit lowers cardiolipin and is bypassed by supplying phosphatidylglycerol. Marked optional because it delivers the substrate rather than performing a step of cardiolipin chemistry, and because the RGD diagram does not draw it.

Part 2: CDP-diacylglycerol synthesis in the inner membrane
TAMM41 mitochondrial CDP-diacylglycerol synthaseReactionmito_cdp_dag_synthesis

TAMM41 activates phosphatidic acid with CTP to CDP-diacylglycerol on the matrix face of the inner membrane, supplying the CDP-diacylglycerol used twice in the pathway, by PGS1 and by CRLS1. The ER CDS1/CDS2 enzymes make the CDP-diacylglycerol pool for phosphatidylinositol synthesis and cannot replace TAMM41 for cardiolipin. Part 6: cardiolipin deacylation to monolysocardiolipin (first half of remodelling)
Phospholipase deacylation of cardiolipin (ABHD18, PLA2G6, PNPLA8)Reactioncardiolipin_deacylation

A phospholipase removes one acyl chain from nascent cardiolipin to give monolysocardiolipin, the acceptor for reacylation (RHEA:32935). Which enzyme carries most of this flux in human tissues is not settled, so three candidates with direct evidence are modelled as alternatives. ABHD18 is the functional homologue of yeast Cld1 and deacylates cardiolipin in mouse cells and flies, with preference for the saturated, less unsaturated species of nascent cardiolipin. The calcium-independent phospholipases A2 PLA2G6 and PNPLA8 hydrolyse cardiolipin in vitro; PLA2G6 yields mainly monolysocardiolipin, and PNPLA8 is the main enzyme releasing oxidised acyl chains from cardiolipin.

Part 7: transacylation of monolysocardiolipin to mature cardiolipin (second half of remodelling)
Tafazzin phospholipid-monolysocardiolipin transacylaseReactiontafazzin_transacylation

Tafazzin transfers an acyl chain, preferentially linoleoyl, from phosphatidylcholine (and other phospholipids) to monolysocardiolipin without acyl-CoA, regenerating cardiolipin and releasing lysophosphatidylcholine (RHEA:33731). The reaction is reversible, and repeated cycles of deacylation and transacylation enrich cardiolipin in the unsaturated species favoured by membrane packing rather than by tafazzin's own specificity. Its loss in Barth syndrome causes monolysocardiolipin accumulation and loss of tetralinoleoyl cardiolipin.
