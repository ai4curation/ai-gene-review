Mitochondrial unfolded protein response in Caenorhabditis elegans

The mitochondrial unfolded protein response (UPRmt) is a mitochondria-to-nucleus signaling pathway that raises the expression of nuclear genes encoding mitochondrial chaperones and proteases when protein folding in the mitochondrial matrix is disturbed. In Caenorhabditis elegans the pathway was defined by genetic screens with the hsp-6 (mtHSP70) and hsp-60 (HSP60) promoter reporters. Misfolded matrix proteins are degraded by the ClpP protease CLPP-1, and the resulting peptides leave the matrix through the inner-membrane ABC transporter HAF-1. The central transcription factor is the bZIP protein ATFS-1, which carries both an N-terminal mitochondrial targeting sequence and a nuclear localization signal. In healthy cells ATFS-1 is imported into mitochondria and degraded; when mitochondrial import efficiency falls, part of the ATFS-1 pool stays in the cytosol, enters the nucleus and activates chaperone, protease, glycolysis and innate-immunity genes. ATFS-1 that does reach the matrix binds mitochondrial DNA and limits OXPHOS transcript accumulation. A chromatin arm acts alongside ATFS-1: the homeodomain protein DVE-1 and the ubiquitin-like protein UBL-5 form a complex that relocates to the nucleus and binds mitochondrial chaperone promoters, while the H3K9 methyltransferase MET-2 and its nuclear cofactor LIN-65 compact bulk chromatin and the H3K27 demethylase JMJD-3.1 is needed for full UPRmt induction. The induced matrix chaperones HSP-6 and HSP-60 are both the standard reporters and the protective output of the response.

Boundary. This concrete C. elegans module covers the ATFS-1-centred UPRmt: generation and export of the matrix stress signal (CLPP-1, HAF-1), the ATFS-1 import checkpoint, nuclear transcriptional activation by ATFS-1, the chromatin arm (DVE-1/UBL-5, LIN-65, MET-2, JMJD-3.1) and the induced matrix chaperones (HSP-6, HSP-60). It is modeled as five ordered parts. Left out. The GCN-2/eIF2alpha translational arm is activated by mitochondrial dysfunction but is complementary to and independent of ATFS-1 activation (PMID:22719267), so it belongs with the integrated stress response; the gcn-2 review lists it as a core UPRmt process, which is recorded here only as context. The neuronal and germline signals that switch on UPRmt in the intestine cell-non-autonomously (serotonin, the retromer-dependent Wnt EGL-20 signal, PMID:30057120) are upstream inter-tissue regulation and are not modeled. The H3K9me2 demethylase JMJD-1.2 acts with JMJD-3.1 (PMID:27133168) but has no review yet and is only named. The SUMO peptidase ULP-4 and the NuRD/HDA-1 regulators (PMID:30642431, PMID:32789178, PMID:32934238) are modulators, not core parts. The matrix LON protease that degrades imported ATFS-1 is not named in the cached abstracts and is not modeled. Downstream innate immunity and lifespan extension are outputs of the transcriptional program and are not included as module processes. ATFS-1 dual targeting. ATFS-1 appears in two parts, each with its own annoton, because it performs two activities in two compartments: in the matrix it binds mitochondrial DNA promoters (mitochondrial transcription factor activity) before degradation, and in the nucleus it activates RNA polymerase II transcription. The import-checkpoint part negatively regulates the nuclear part. UniProt. The atfs-1 review uses Q23272 (the reviewed entry for ZC376.7); this is used throughout. HAF-1 (O45278) has no gene review; its MF term rests on the mitochondrial peptide efflux assay of PMID:20188671. Related modules. ATFS-1-dependent transcription of mitophagy genes is treated in MODULE:c_elegans_mitophagy, the cytosolic heat shock response in MODULE:c_elegans_heat_shock_response and the ER UPR in MODULE:c_elegans_er_unfolded_protein_response. Comparison with the human UPRmt. In mammals ATF5 is the ATFS-1 functional counterpart and the OMA1-DELE1-HRI branch feeds eIF2alpha phosphorylation; neither the DELE1 branch nor a HAF-1 orthologue role is established in worms, so this module stays species-scoped.

Part 2: ATFS-1 mitochondrial import checkpoint
ATFS-1 mitochondrial import and matrix activityRegulatory Stepatfs1_import_checkpoint

ATFS-1 has an N-terminal mitochondrial targeting sequence that is essential for UPRmt repression. In unstressed cells ATFS-1 is efficiently imported into mitochondria and degraded, so it does not reach the nucleus. The import efficiency of ATFS-1 thus reports the capacity of mitochondrial protein import. Imported ATFS-1 also binds OXPHOS gene promoters in mitochondrial DNA and limits OXPHOS transcript accumulation during stress.

Part 4: chromatin arm that makes UPRmt target genes accessible
DVE-1/UBL-5 and chromatin reorganizationBiological Processchromatin_arm

Mitochondrial stress causes DVE-1 and UBL-5 to form a complex and accumulate in nuclei, where DVE-1 binds mitochondrial chaperone promoters. In parallel, MET-2 with its nuclear cofactor LIN-65 deposits H3K9me2 and compacts most of the genome, while the regions that stay open are bound by DVE-1. The H3K27 demethylase JMJD-3.1 is needed for full UPRmt induction and for mitochondrial stress-induced longevity.
