FGFR signaling pathway in Drosophila melanogaster

Drosophila has two FGF receptor tyrosine kinases with distinct ligands. Breathless (Btl) is activated by Branchless (Bnl) and guides tracheal branch migration, and Heartless (Htl) is activated by Pyramus and Thisbe and directs the spreading and migration of mesoderm and glial cells. Both receptors use the FGFR-specific cytoplasmic adaptor Stumps (Downstream of FGFR, Dof). Signaling proceeds through Drk, Sos, Ras85D and the Raf-Dsor1-Rolled MAP kinase cascade, ending in ETS factor output (Pointed activation, Yan/Aop relief). Pebble, a Rho exchange factor, acts in Heartless-dependent mesoderm migration.
