Protein phosphatase 2A holoenzymes in Drosophila melanogaster

Protein phosphatase 2A (PP2A) is a major serine/threonine phosphatase formed by a core dimer of a scaffold (A) subunit and a catalytic (C) subunit, joined by one of several regulatory B subunits that select substrates and subcellular location. In Drosophila the catalytic subunit is Microtubule star (Mts) and the scaffold is Pp2A-29B. The B55-family subunit Twins (Tws) directs PP2A to mitotic substrates and regulates mitotic exit, while the B56 subunits Widerborst (Wdb) and Well rounded (Wrd) act in planar cell polarity, Hedgehog and Wingless signaling, insulin signaling and synapse development.
