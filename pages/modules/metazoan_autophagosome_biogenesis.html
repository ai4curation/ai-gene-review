Metazoan autophagosome biogenesis and maturation

In animals, macroautophagy builds a double-membrane autophagosome around cytoplasmic cargo and delivers it to lysosomes. The core steps are shared with yeast, but several are wired differently. An initiation complex of the Atg1/ULK kinase, ATG13 and ATG101 marks the site of formation, which in animals lies on endoplasmic reticulum (ER) subdomains called omegasomes. The class III phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase complex I (VPS34, the pseudokinase VPS15, Beclin 1 and ATG14) makes phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PI3P) on this membrane. Two kinds of WD40 PROPPIN read PI3P. WIPI1/2-type proteins recruit the conjugation machinery, and WIPI3/4-type proteins partner the ATG2 rod in an ATG2-ATG18 complex. ATG2 carries lipids from the ER to the growing phagophore, and the ATG9 scramblase spreads them between the two leaflets. Atg8-family proteins are primed by ATG4 and conjugated to phosphatidylethanolamine by the E1 ATG7, the E2 ATG3 and the E3-like ATG12-ATG5-ATG16 complex, with ATG16 setting where conjugation happens. Animals have two Atg8 subfamilies, GABARAP-type and LC3-type, and the stage at which each acts differs between lineages: in nematodes the GABARAP-type protein acts early and the LC3-type protein late, whereas in mammalian cells the GABARAP subfamily dominates autophagosome-lysosome fusion. Cargo receptors such as p62/SQSTM1 link ubiquitinated cargo to Atg8-family proteins. Fusion with late endosomes and lysosomes needs EPG5, a metazoan-specific RAB7 effector that binds Atg8-family proteins and SNAREs and makes sure autophagosomes fuse with the correct partner.

Boundary. Bulk and selective macroautophagy in animals, from the initiation complex to fusion of the autophagosome with late endosomes and lysosomes. Eight parts in pathway order: ULK1 initiation complex, PI3KC3-C1 nucleation, PI3P effectors, lipid supply (ATG2, ATG9), Atg8 conjugation, Atg8-family membrane proteins (with a lineage variant set for the stage at which each subfamily acts), optional cargo recruitment (SQSTM1/SQST-1), and EPG5-dependent fusion. Upstream regulation by mTOR and AMPK, transcriptional induction by TFEB/HLH-30, lysosomal degradation itself, and the PI3KC3-C2 endocytic complex are outside the boundary. HLH-30 and the LIPL-4 lipid branch are modelled in MODULE:c_elegans_lysosomal_lipid_signaling. Mitochondrion-specific receptors are modelled in MODULE:mitophagy. Context taxon. Metazoa. The boundary is drawn around steps that are shared by nematodes and mammals and that differ from budding yeast: ATG101 in the initiation complex (absent from Saccharomyces cerevisiae), the omegasome/ER origin, the split of Atg8 into GABARAP and LC3 subfamilies, and EPG5 at fusion (no yeast ortholog). Representative members are the C. elegans protein (where the genetics of the metazoan-specific steps was worked out) and the human ortholog, each with a gene review. Human ATG101, ATG3, ATG4B and WDR45 (WIPI4) have no gene review here, so those roles are represented by the worm protein only. Relation to MODULE:phagophore_assembly_site. That module is yeast-centred and models the Atg17-Atg29-Atg31 scaffold, which animals lack (FIP200 takes its place in mammals), and the condensate biophysics of the PAS. This module models the same conserved activities at the level of families, and adds the metazoan-specific steps. MODULE:phagophore_organelle_contact_site models the ATG2 bridge and scramblases structurally; here they appear only as the lipid-supply part. Members noted but not modelled as annotons. ATG-4.2 acts mainly at the delipidation/maturation stage (PMID:22767594) and would be a separate annoton if modelled. BEC-1 also acts in the PI3KC3-C2 complex in endosome-to-Golgi retrograde transport and phagosome maturation (PMID:21183797). ATG-18, EPG-5 and LGG-1 also act in phagocytes to degrade apoptotic corpses (PMID:22451698). These roles are noted in the gene reviews and are outside this module. Lipidation nuance. In C. elegans, LGG-1 cleavage, not PE conjugation, is essential for autophagosome initiation (PMID:37395461), and in human cells autophagosomes still form without any Atg8 protein (PMID:27864321).
