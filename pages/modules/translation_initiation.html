Human cap-dependent translation initiation — eIF2 ternary complex and its eIF2B exchange factor, 43S preinitiation complex assembly with eIF1, eIF1A and eIF3, eIF4F-mediated mRNA activation and 43S recruitment, scanning and start codon recognition with eIF5 as GAP, and eIF5B-catalysed subunit joining

Translation initiation assembles an elongation-competent 80S ribosome whose P site holds the initiator methionyl-tRNA base-paired to the start codon. On most cytoplasmic mRNAs it is cap-dependent and works by scanning. eIF2, a heterotrimer whose gamma subunit binds GTP and Met-tRNAi, forms the ternary complex (TC), and the decameric eIF2B regenerates eIF2-GTP from the eIF2-GDP released after each round. eIF1, eIF1A and the 13-subunit eIF3 bind the 40S subunit, hold it in an open conformation and recruit the TC; eIF5 joins through eIF2-beta, giving the 43S preinitiation complex (PIC). The mRNA is activated by eIF4F: the cap-binding protein eIF4E, the DEAD-box helicase eIF4A and the scaffold eIF4G, which also binds poly(A)-binding protein (closing the mRNA loop) and eIF3. eIF4B or eIF4H stimulates eIF4A, and the cap-eIF4E-eIF4G-eIF3-40S chain of contacts loads the 43S complex near the cap. The PIC then scans the 5' UTR, helped by eIF4A, eIF4B, eIF4G and, on highly structured leaders, the 40S-bound DExH helicase DHX29. eIF1 and eIF1A keep the complex scanning-competent and reject non-AUG and poor-context triplets; when the anticodon pairs with an AUG in good context, eIF1 is displaced, the eIF5-stimulated hydrolysis of eIF2-bound GTP becomes irreversible by Pi release, and the 48S complex closes. eIF2-GDP and eIF5 leave, and the GTPase eIF5B, bound to the eIF1A C-terminal tail, joins the 60S subunit; GTP hydrolysis then releases eIF5B and eIF1A from the 80S initiation complex, which begins elongation.

Part 4: scanning, start codon recognition and commitment
The 48S complex scans the 5' UTR to the start codon; AUG recognition displaces eIF1 and makes eIF5-stimulated GTP hydrolysis by eIF2 irreversibleBiological Processscanning_and_start_codon_recognition

The attached complex moves 5' to 3' along the leader. Scanning needs the open conformation induced by eIF1 and eIF1A (annotons of part pic_43s_formation) and is assisted by eIF4A, eIF4B and eIF4G (part mrna_activation_and_recruitment) and, on highly structured leaders, by DHX29 bound to the 40S subunit. eIF1 rejects non-AUG and poor-context triplets. Base pairing of Met-tRNAi with an AUG in good context tightens eIF1A binding and displaces eIF1, which switches the complex to the closed state. eIF5, bound to eIF2-beta, is the GTPase-activating protein for eIF2-gamma; GTP is hydrolysed during scanning, but Pi release, which commits the ribosome to the codon, waits for eIF1 displacement. GO has no term for scanning or for start codon recognition; two are requested in proposed_new_terms, and the step uses the module process meanwhile.
