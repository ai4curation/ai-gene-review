Protocatechuate ortho-cleavage branch

Reusable bacterial beta-ketoadipate-pathway branch that converts protocatechuate to 3-oxoadipate. PcaG/PcaH perform intradiol ring cleavage, followed by PcaB cycloisomerization, PcaC decarboxylation, and PcaD enol-lactone hydrolysis. Activation and cleavage of 3-oxoadipate by the shared lower beta-ketoadipate pathway are outside this branch.
