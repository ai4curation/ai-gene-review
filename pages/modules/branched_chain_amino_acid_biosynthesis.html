L-valine, L-leucine, and L-isoleucine biosynthesis

De novo biosynthesis of the three branched-chain amino acids in bacteria, archaea, fungi and plants; the pathway is absent from animals. L-isoleucine synthesis begins with threonine deamination to 2-oxobutanoate. An acetohydroxy-acid synthase (a catalytic large subunit with a regulatory small subunit), ketol-acid reductoisomerase and dihydroxy-acid dehydratase then form a shared catalytic trunk that processes either the valine precursor derived from two pyruvate molecules or the isoleucine precursor derived from pyruvate plus 2-oxobutanoate. The valine intermediate 2-oxoisovalerate also enters a three-reaction leucine extension (2-isopropylmalate synthase, isopropylmalate isomerase and 3-isopropylmalate dehydrogenase). A branched-chain amino-acid aminotransferase performs the terminal transamination for all three products. The reaction chain is conserved, but two features vary by lineage. First, isopropylmalate isomerase is a heteromeric LeuC-LeuD enzyme in bacteria and plants but a single fused polypeptide in fungi. Second, in fungi the pathway is split between compartments: threonine deaminase, the shared trunk, most 2-isopropylmalate synthase and a biosynthetic aminotransferase are in the mitochondrial matrix, 2-isopropylmalate is exported to the cytosol by a mitochondrial carrier, and the isomerase and dehydrogenase are cytosolic. In budding yeast, mitochondrial BAT1 and cytosolic BAT2 are paralogous aminotransferases, with BAT1 favoured for biosynthesis and BAT2 for catabolism.

YeastPathways alignment (BRANCHED-CHAIN-AA-SYN-PWY-1, ILEUSYN-PWY-1, VALSYN-PWY, LEUSYN-PWY-1). All four SGD frames are subsets of this module and no YeastCyc reaction was rejected. Corrections and additions to the YeastCyc gene lists: LEU2 is the 3-isopropylmalate dehydrogenase (RXN-13158 has no gene in the pathway summary); OAC1 exports 2-isopropylmalate from mitochondria (PMID:18682385) and is modelled as an optional fungal transport part; BAT2 is retained as a catalyst but is mainly the catabolic paralog (PMID:21267457). Compartments: the SGD GO-CAM conversions place every activity in the cytosol, but ILV1, ILV2/ILV6, ILV5, ILV3, BAT1, LEU9 and the long LEU4 form are mitochondrial; only LEU1, LEU2, BAT2 and the short LEU4 form are cytosolic (PMID:12626680, PMID:3275644, PMID:8798704). The SGD conversion also types all BAT1/BAT2 reactions as GO:0052656 L-isoleucine transaminase, including the valine and leucine reactions; the module uses the parent GO:0004084. The PomBase model has no valine transamination and no causal edge from tda1 to AHAS, and places one ilv5 activity in GO:0006551 L-leucine metabolic process rather than GO:0009098. S. pombe gene symbols are confusing relative to budding yeast: S. pombe ilv1 is the AHAS catalytic subunit (ILV2 ortholog), tda1 is the threonine deaminase, leu3 is 2-isopropylmalate synthase, leu2 the isomerase and leu1 the dehydrogenase. Upstream supplies (thiamine diphosphate import for AHAS, CoA and acetyl-CoA in mitochondria, Fe-S cluster assembly for ILV3/LEU1), regulators (Leu3/alpha-IPM, Gcn4), tRNA charging and BCAA transport are outside the module. The PSEPK ppu00290 bucket also contains shorter serine/threonine dehydratases, two additional AHAS-like large subunits, AlaA, and leucine dehydrogenase. They are not counted as module members because pathway-map membership alone does not establish that they supply the physiological biosynthetic route. IlvA is required only for the isoleucine precursor input. Its PROVIDES_INPUT_FOR edge records 2-oxobutanoate supply without making IlvA a prerequisite for the pyruvate-only valine branch.
