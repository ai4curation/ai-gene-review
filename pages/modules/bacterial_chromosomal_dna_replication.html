Bacterial chromosomal DNA replication

Bacterial chromosome duplication coordinates origin recognition and helicase loading with template unwinding, protection and priming, replicative synthesis and proofreading, clamp-dependent processivity, RNA-primer replacement, nick sealing and topological separation. Alternative loader and polymerase architectures are grounded in Proteobacteria, Bacillota and Actinomycetota examples.
