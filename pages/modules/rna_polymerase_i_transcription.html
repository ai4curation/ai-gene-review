Human RNA polymerase I transcription of the rDNA — UBF and SL1 promoter recognition, RRN3-dependent recruitment of the 13-subunit Pol I, pre-rRNA synthesis, and TTF1/CAVIN1 (PTRF) termination and release

RNA polymerase I (Pol I) transcribes a single class of gene, the tandemly repeated ribosomal DNA (rDNA), producing the 47S precursor rRNA that is processed into the 18S, 5.8S and 28S rRNAs. Transcription takes place in the nucleolus. The rDNA promoter is bipartite, with a core element around the transcription start site and an upstream control element. The HMG-box protein UBF binds and bends promoter and upstream DNA and activates transcription; the promoter-selectivity factor SL1, made of the TATA-box binding protein TBP and the Pol I-specific TBP-associated factors TAF1A, TAF1B, TAF1C and TAF1D (with TAF12), binds the core promoter and assembles with UBF into the preinitiation complex. RRN3 (TIF-IA) marks the initiation-competent subpopulation of Pol I and recruits it to the promoter by binding the SL1 subunits TAF1B and TAF1C. Pol I itself has 13 subunits in human: the two large catalytic subunits RPA1 (POLR1A) and RPA2 (POLR1B), the RPAC1 and RPAC2 subunits shared with Pol III (POLR1C, POLR1D), the five RPABC subunits shared with Pol II and Pol III (POLR2E, POLR2F, POLR2H, POLR2K, POLR2L), and the Pol I-specific RPA43 stalk (POLR1F), the TFIIF-like RPA49/RPA34 heterodimer (POLR1E, POLR1G) and the TFIIS-like RNA cleavage subunit RPA12 (POLR1H). Downstream of the transcribed region, the termination factor TTF1 binds the terminator repeats and pauses the elongating polymerase, and CAVIN1 (PTRF) binds the U-rich 3' end of the nascent pre-rRNA and releases the transcript and the polymerase. Pol I output is tuned by nutrient and stress signalling through RRN3, SL1, UBF and the RPA49 subunit.

A family-of-subunits descriptor was rejected because the subunits are not paralogues but distinct obligate parts of one enzyme. (2) Shared subunits. The five RPABC subunits (POLR2E, POLR2F, POLR2H, POLR2K, POLR2L) and the two RPAC subunits (POLR1C, POLR1D) are listed in full in both this module and modules/rna_polymerase_iii_transcription.yaml, with the same accessions and roles, so that each enzyme is complete where it is modelled. No human RNA polymerase II module exists, and none is created here. (3) SL1 is one complex annoton. TAF12 is included as a unit without a function because RGD lists it and draws it in SL1; its experimental functions in GOA (transcription coactivator activity, contributes to RNA polymerase II general transcription initiation factor activity) concern its TFIID and SAGA roles. TBP is shared with TFIIIB in the Pol III module and with TFIID. (4) RRN3 is its own step rather than a unit of the Pol I complex: it binds only the initiation-competent subpopulation of the enzyme (Pol I beta) and is released after initiation. (5) Elongation is intrinsic to the enzyme in this module. The RGD diagram also draws FACT (SSRP1, SUPT16H), the PAF1 complex (PAF1, CTR9, LEO1, CDC73, RTF1, SKIC8), DSIF (SUPT4H1, SUPT5H) and nucleolin as positively affecting pre-rRNA synthesis. These are excluded: they are general chromatin and elongation factors shared with Pol II transcription (and FACT with replication and repair), and their Pol I roles rest mainly on yeast genetics and on effects on elongation rate rather than on a step the basal machinery cannot perform. They are candidates for an optional elongation-accessory part when a human Pol II elongation module exists to reference. (6) Termination is modelled with TTF1 and CAVIN1 (PTRF), both RGD genes drawn together on the diagram. The PTRF release activity was shown with the murine protein (PMID:9582279); the CAVIN1 gene review keeps the termination process and the rRNA primary transcript binding function while marking the human-specific IDA rows UNDECIDED, and the module follows the review. (7) Regulation is out of scope. CREBBP (acetylates RPA49), SIRT7 (deacetylates RPA49), SIRT1 (deacetylates TAF1B) and the mTOR, ERK, AMPK and p53 pathways drawn on the diagram (PW:0000180, PW:0000102, PW:0000542, PW:0000718) regulate Pol I output but do not perform a step of transcription; they are named here and in the description, not modelled. The same applies to the chromatin modification/remodeling pathway (PW:0001337), and to rRNA processing (PW:0001574) and ribosome biogenesis (PW:0001066), which are downstream. (8) Substrate versus participant: the rDNA template and the pre-rRNA product are not participants; CAVIN1 is a participant because its own transcript binding performs the release step. GO-CAM links. The nuclear pool of CAVIN1 acts as the Pol I and transcript release factor. The function is the CAVIN1 gene review's second core function (rRNA primary transcript binding); the review records that the biochemistry was done with the murine protein. CAVIN1's main cellular role, in caveola coat assembly, is outside this module.

PTRF dissociates paused Pol I ternary complexes, interacts with TTF-I and Pol I, and binds the 3' end of pre-rRNA.
Consistent with its function in transcription termination, PTRF interacts with both TTF-I and Pol I. Moreover, we demonstrate specific binding of PTRF to transcripts containing the 3' end of pre-rRNA.
