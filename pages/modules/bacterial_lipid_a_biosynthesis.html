Bacterial lipid A biosynthesis

Reusable Gram-negative bacterial module for synthesis of Kdo-decorated lipid A from UDP-N-acetylglucosamine. The pathway is divided into early glucosamine acylation, lipid-disaccharide formation and phosphorylation, Kdo precursor supply and transfer, and late secondary acylation. Optional remodeling after lipid A synthesis is outside this core module.

Pseudomonas putida KT2440 lipopolysaccharide biosynthesis
KEGG ppu00540 identifies the KT2440 enzymes for lipid A and Kdo assembly.

KT2440 encodes two KdsA paralogs, KdsA1 and KdsA2; either family member can satisfy the Kdo-8-phosphate synthase role, but this module does not assert equal physiological contribution. PP_0063 is a second late lipid A acyltransferase-family protein. PagL deacylases and LpxO-family hydroxylases remodel lipid A after core synthesis and are excluded.
