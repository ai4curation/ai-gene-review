Bacterial Pst phosphate uptake

A reusable bacterial high-affinity phosphate-uptake module in which PstS captures inorganic phosphate, the PstA/PstC membrane subunits form the translocation pathway, and a PstB dimer supplies ATP-dependent energy coupling. The module represents uptake by the PstSACB transporter and ends with cytoplasmic phosphate delivery. PhoU-mediated homeostasis and PhoR/PhoB starvation signaling regulate this system but are not transport steps.
