Canonical Notch signaling pathway module

A cross-species decomposition of canonical (CSL-dependent) Notch signaling, a contact-dependent juxtacrine pathway present across eumetazoans. A membrane-bound DSL-type ligand on a signal-sending cell is ubiquitylated by a Mind bomb or Neuralized E3 ligase and endocytosed; the resulting pulling force on the bound Notch receptor of the neighboring cell unfolds its negative regulatory region, exposing the S2 site to an ADAM10-family metalloprotease. The membrane-tethered remnant is then cut within its transmembrane segment by gamma-secretase, releasing the Notch intracellular domain (NICD). NICD enters the nucleus, binds the DNA-bound CSL transcription factor (RBPJ, Su(H), LAG-1), and together with a Mastermind-family coactivator converts CSL from a corepressor-bound repressor into an activator of target genes, prominently the HES/E(spl) and HEY bHLH repressors. Receptor competence is set beforehand in the ER and Golgi by POFUT1-type O-fucosylation and, in arthropods and vertebrates, by Fringe elongation of those O-fucose glycans, which tunes receptor responsiveness to Delta- versus Serrate/Jagged-type ligands. The module is written so that each role is a union over the lineage-specific implementations found in vertebrates, insects, nematodes, and cnidarians.

Union modeling. Each role that is filled by different, often non-homologous, proteins in different lineages is a variant_set. selection ONE_OR_MORE is used where a single organism can deploy several implementations at once (Delta- and Serrate/Jagged-type ligands in flies and vertebrates; Mind bomb and Neuralized ligand E3s; HES/E(spl) and HEY target families), so a concrete realization is the union of the variants present. selection EXACTLY_ONE is used only for the Mastermind coactivator lineage, where each organism uses one of three sequence-unrelated or PANTHER-disjoint solutions. Optional parts (Fringe, CSL default repression, cis-inhibition) are absent from at least one well-studied lineage (C. elegans has no Fringe and no Hairless ortholog). PANTHER grounding. Part 1: receptor maturation (signal-receiving cell, secretory pathway)
Notch receptor O-glycan maturationBiological Processnotch_receptor_maturation

Before reaching the surface, Notch EGF repeats are O-fucosylated in the ER by POFUT1/O-fut1, which also acts as a Notch-selective folding chaperone. In arthropods and vertebrates, Fringe enzymes in the Golgi extend O-fucose with GlcNAc, raising responsiveness to Delta-type and lowering responsiveness to Serrate/Jagged-type ligands. Vertebrate receptors are also cleaved at S1 by furin; this constitutive step produces the heterodimer but does not activate signaling and is not modeled as a separate part.
