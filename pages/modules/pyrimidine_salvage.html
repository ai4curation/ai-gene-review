Pyrimidine base and nucleoside salvage

Recycling of preformed pyrimidine bases and ribonucleosides, taken up from the environment or released by RNA and nucleotide turnover, into UMP and CMP. Uracil is converted directly to UMP by uracil phosphoribosyltransferase with PRPP. Uridine and cytidine are phosphorylated by uridine-cytidine kinase to UMP and CMP; alternatively, cytidine is deaminated to uridine by cytidine deaminase, and the ribonucleosides are cleaved to their free bases either by a nucleoside hydrolase (fungi and protists) or by uridine phosphorylase (animals and bacteria). Cytosine is deaminated to uracil by cytosine deaminase, an enzyme present in fungi and bacteria but absent from animals; this is why the antifungal prodrug 5-fluorocytosine is selectively toxic to fungi. CMP made by salvage is phosphorylated to CDP by UMP/CMP kinase, and UMP joins the de novo product for conversion to UTP and CTP. The same enzymes act on some deoxyribonucleosides (deoxycytidine is phosphorylated or cleaved), but thymidine salvage requires thymidine kinase, which animals and many bacteria have and budding and fission yeasts lack. Uptake of pyrimidine bases and nucleosides by plasma-membrane permeases precedes the module.

NEW module from the YeastPathways crosswalk (YEAST-RNT-SALV, YEAST-SALV-PYRMID-DNTP) and the PomBase model 69a0c46f00003691, scoped to fungal pyrimidine base/nucleoside salvage with animal and bacterial alternatives as variants. All YeastCyc catalysts (FUR1, URK1, CDD1, URH1, FCY1, URA6, YNK1) are correct; the CDP-to-CTP step (YNK1) is the NDP kinase step of de_novo_pyrimidine_synthesis and is not repeated. The defining realisation caveats are absences: S. cerevisiae and S. pombe have no thymidine kinase, uridine/thymidine phosphorylase or deoxyuridine kinase (the gene-less THYKI-RXN, THYM-PHOSPH-RXN, URA-PHOSPH-RXN and DURIDKI-RXN of YEAST-SALV-PYRMID-DNTP; no S288C or S. pombe UniProt protein carries EC 2.7.1.21, 2.4.2.3 or 2.4.2.4, checked 2026-10-06), so the thymidine kinase part is optional and has only animal/bacterial exemplars; deoxyuridine cannot replace uracil for ura- yeast. Deoxycytidine is handled by URK1 and URH1 (PMID:12111094). GO-CAM disagreements: FCY1 carries the obsolete GO:0102480 and URA6 the obsolete GO:0004127; URK1 cytidine phosphorylation uses GO:0004849 rather than GO:0043771. In the PomBase model, uracil phosphoribosyltransferase activity is asserted for three proteins: Q9HE15 (SPAC1399.04c, a UPRT domain protein classified by PANTHER as a uridine kinase-family member), O13867 (SPAC1B3.01c) and urg2 (Q9US43, a plastid-type UPRT, PTHR32315:SF4); only O13867, annotated part_of uracil salvage, is used as an exemplar, and the PomBase cytidine kinase SPAC227.14 (Q9UTC5) is noted but not used. The PomBase S. pombe fcy1 (O59834) is classified by PANTHER in the TAD2 subfamily; it is retained as an exemplar because PomBase asserts cytosine deaminase activity. Uptake permeases (FUR4 uracil, FUI1 uridine, FCY2 purine-cytosine) are transport steps outside the enzymatic core, as are the 5'-nucleotidases and the pyrimidine catabolism activities of the PomBase model (pyrimidine_nucleotide_catabolism). Downstream, UMP and CDP join de_novo_pyrimidine_synthesis (UTP/CTP) and dntp_de_novo_synthesis.
