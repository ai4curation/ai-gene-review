Ethylene signaling (ETR/ERS receptor-CTR1-EIN2-EBF-EIN3/EIL1 module)

Ethylene signaling in land plants is a double-negative relay from the endoplasmic reticulum to the nucleus. Ethylene is perceived by a family of ER-membrane receptors related to bacterial two-component histidine kinases (in Arabidopsis ETR1 and ERS1 of subfamily 1 and ETR2, ERS2 and EIN4 of subfamily 2), which bind the gas through a copper cofactor in their N-terminal transmembrane domain. In the absence of ethylene the receptors keep the associated Raf-like protein kinase CTR1 active; CTR1 phosphorylates the cytosolic C-terminal domain of the NRAMP-like ER membrane protein EIN2, preventing its signalling. Ethylene binding inactivates the receptors and CTR1, so EIN2 is dephosphorylated and proteolytically cleaved; the released C-terminal fragment (EIN2-CEND) moves to the nucleus and also binds the 3' UTRs of EBF1 and EBF2 mRNAs, repressing their translation in cytoplasmic P-bodies. EBF1 and EBF2 are F-box substrate adaptors of SCF E3 ubiquitin ligases that continuously target the transcription factors EIN3 and EIL1 for proteasomal degradation; their inhibition, together with ethylene-induced turnover of the EBF proteins, stabilizes EIN3/EIL1 in the nucleus. EIN3/EIL1 bind EIN3-binding sites in target promoters and activate the ethylene transcriptional cascade (e.g. ERF1), including EBF2 and receptor genes as negative feedback. Ethylene biosynthesis (ACC synthase and ACC oxidase), receptor-associated regulators such as RTE1, and downstream ERF-family transcription factors are outside this module.

Part 1: ethylene perception by ER-membrane receptors
Ethylene perception by ETR/ERS receptorsRegulatory Stepethylene_perception

Ethylene binds the copper-containing transmembrane sensor domain of dimeric ER-membrane receptors. Without ethylene the receptors keep CTR1 active (receptor output is "on"); ethylene binding switches receptor output off. Receptor genes are redundant, with subfamily 1 (ETR1, ERS1) predominant.

Part 4: SCF(EBF1/EBF2)-mediated EIN3/EIL1 degradation
SCF(EBF1/EBF2) turnover of EIN3/EIL1Regulatory Stepebf_degradation

In the absence of ethylene, the LRR F-box proteins EBF1 and EBF2 recruit EIN3 and EIL1 to SCF E3 ligases (with ASK1/ASK2, CUL1 and RBX1) for polyubiquitination and 26S proteasome degradation.
