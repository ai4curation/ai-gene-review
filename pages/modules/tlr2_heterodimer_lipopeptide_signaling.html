TLR2-heterodimer lipopeptide sensing module

Recognition of bacterial lipopeptides by TLR2 heterodimers and the first steps of the MyD88-dependent signal. LBP and CD14 deliver the lipopeptide; TLR2 pairs with TLR1 for triacylated or with TLR6 for diacylated lipopeptides; the PIP2-anchored sorting adaptor TIRAP recruits MYD88, which recruits the kinase IRAK4. Downstream TRAF6, TAK1 and NF-kappaB steps are shared with other MyD88-dependent TLRs and are modelled in the toll-like receptor signaling module.

TLR2 recognises di- and triacylated lipopeptides, and the partner (TLR1 or TLR6) sets the specificity.
Toll-like receptor 2 (TLR2) initiates potent immune responses by recognizing diacylated and triacylated lipopeptides. Its ligand specificity is controlled by whether it heterodimerizes with TLR1 or TLR6.

Written as the reference representation for GO-CAMs 5fb9cc0600000727 and 5fce9b7300000030, whose receptor activities use GO:0001875 (lipopolysaccharide immune receptor activity) for lipopeptide recognition. Here the heterodimer carries GO:0038187 and the ligand specificity is expressed by the partner subunit's binding term. Downstream steps: modules/toll_like_receptor_signaling.yaml.
