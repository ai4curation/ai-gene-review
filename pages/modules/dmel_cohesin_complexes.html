Mitotic and meiotic cohesin complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

Cohesin is a ring-shaped SMC complex that holds sister chromatids together from DNA replication until anaphase and also organizes interphase chromatin into loops. The ring is built from the SMC1-SMC3 heterodimer, whose ATPase heads are bridged by a kleisin subunit, which in turn binds a stromalin (SA) subunit. In mitotic cells the kleisin is Rad21, encoded in Drosophila by verthandi (vtd), and the SA subunit is SA1; separase cleavage of Rad21 opens the ring at anaphase. Drosophila meiosis uses meiosis-specific cohesion proteins: C(2)M, a kleisin-family protein required for synaptonemal complex formation, and Solo and Sunn, which are required for meiotic centromeric cohesion.
