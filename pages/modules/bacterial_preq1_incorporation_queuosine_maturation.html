Bacterial preQ1 incorporation and queuosine maturation

A reusable bacterial pathway that converts preQ0 to the mature queuosine modification at wobble position 34 of tRNA(Asp), tRNA(Asn), tRNA(His), and tRNA(Tyr). QueF reduces preQ0 to preQ1, Tgt exchanges guanine-34 for preQ1, QueA transfers and rearranges the ribose moiety of S-adenosylmethionine to form epoxyqueuosine, and either QueG or the nonhomologous QueH family reduces epoxyqueuosine to queuosine.

The pathway has the ordered core QueF, Tgt, QueA, and a final QueG or QueH epoxyqueuosine reductase, while preQ0 synthesis and salvage are outside this module boundary.
PMID:34652139
Epoxyqueuosine Reductase QueH in the Biosynthetic Pathway to tRNA Queuosine Is a Unique Metalloenzyme.
QueH is a structurally distinct, cobalamin-independent replacement or supplement for QueG that catalyzes the terminal epoxyqueuosine-reduction step.
PMID:28128549
Identification of a Novel Epoxyqueuosine Reductase Family by Comparative Genomics.
Double-deletion and heterologous-complementation experiments establish DUF208/QueH as a nonorthologous QueG replacement, while Acinetobacter baylyi demonstrates that both families can coexist.
UniProtKB:Q9WZJ0
Reviewed Thermotoga maritima QueH exemplar
Q9WZJ0 is a reviewed QueH epoxyqueuosine reductase in InterPro IPR003828 and PANTHER PTHR36701.

This module begins with preQ0 supplied by upstream 7-deazaguanine biosynthesis and ends with mature Q34-tRNA. QueF therefore belongs here as the committed preQ1-supply reaction, but FolE/QueD/QueE/QueC and GTP supply are outside the boundary. The eukaryotic queuine-salvage route is also outside this bacterial module. Generic electron-donor regeneration and synthesis of the cobalamin and iron-sulfur cofactors used by QueG or the iron-sulfur cofactor used by QueH are supporting context, not pathway parts. Pseudomonas putida KT2440 implements the terminal step with QueG; QueH is retained as a reusable nonhomologous alternative.
