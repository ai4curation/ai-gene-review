Strigolactone perception and D14-MAX2-SMXL signalling in shoot-branching control

Strigolactones (SLs) are carotenoid-derived hormones that inhibit axillary bud outgrowth. In Arabidopsis thaliana, SL is perceived by the alpha/beta-hydrolase receptor DWARF14 (D14), which binds and slowly hydrolyses the butenolide D-ring. SL binding changes the conformation of D14 so that it associates with the F-box protein MORE AXILLARY GROWTH2 (MAX2) in an SCF(MAX2) E3 ubiquitin ligase and with the SMAX1-LIKE co-repressors SMXL6, SMXL7 and SMXL8. The SMXLs are then polyubiquitinated and degraded by the proteasome, and D14 itself is degraded as negative feedback. In the absence of SL, the nuclear SMXL6/7/8 proteins repress target genes by recruiting TOPLESS-related co-repressors through an EAR motif, and SMXL6 can also bind target promoters itself. Their removal de-represses the bud-expressed TCP transcription factor BRANCHED1 (BRC1), which keeps buds dormant, in part by activating HD-ZIP genes that raise abscisic acid in the bud. SL-triggered SMXL degradation also reduces PIN1 accumulation and auxin transport in the stem, which inhibits bud activation in parallel with BRC1. The sister karrikin pathway uses the same F-box protein, MAX2, with the D14 paralog KAI2 and the SMAX1/SMXL2 repressors. That pathway, SL biosynthesis (D27, MAX3, MAX4, MAX1) and the auxin-transport branch are outside this module.

Part 1: strigolactone perception
Strigolactone perception by the D14 hydrolase-receptorRegulatory Stepsl_perception

D14 binds SL in its alpha/beta-hydrolase pocket and switches to a conformation that associates with MAX2 and the SMXL co-repressors. D14 also hydrolyses SL, which deactivates the hormone. Whether hydrolysis is needed to activate the receptor is still debated.

Part 2: SCF(MAX2)-mediated ubiquitination of SMXL co-repressors
SCF(MAX2) recruitment and SMXL6/7/8 ubiquitinationRegulatory Stepsmxl_ubiquitination

MAX2, the F-box subunit of SCF(MAX2), binds SL-activated D14 and the SMXL6/7/8 proteins. The SMXLs are polyubiquitinated and degraded by the proteasome within minutes of SL treatment. D14 is degraded in an SL- and MAX2-dependent manner as negative feedback.

Part 3: SMXL6/7/8 co-repression of branching-inhibitor genes
SMXL6/7/8 transcriptional co-repressionRegulatory Stepsmxl_corepression

Without SL, the redundant nuclear SMXL6, SMXL7 and SMXL8 proteins recruit TOPLESS-related co-repressors (TPR2) through an EAR motif and repress BRC1 and other SL-responsive genes. SMXL6 can also bind target promoters directly. Degradation of the SMXLs after SL perception lifts this repression.

Part 4: BRC1 transcriptional output in axillary buds
BRC1-mediated bud dormancyRegulatory Stepbrc1_output

BRC1 is a TCP transcription factor expressed in axillary buds. It is induced when the SMXLs are degraded, and it promotes bud dormancy, partly by directly activating HB21/HB40/HB53 and so raising abscisic acid in the bud. BRC1 sets how likely a bud is to activate, working in parallel with SL effects on auxin transport.
