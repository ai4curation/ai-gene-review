Three-subunit bacterial urease biogenesis and urea hydrolysis

A reusable bacterial module in which UreA, UreB, and UreC assemble the urease apoprotein, UreD, UreE, UreF, and UreG install the carbamylated dinickel metallocenter in a GTP-dependent activation process, and the mature urease complex hydrolyzes urea. The module covers the common three-chain bacterial UreABC implementation and its UreDEFG maturation system. It does not include general nickel uptake or homeostasis, urea transport, regulation of urease expression, or downstream assimilation of released ammonia.

The formation of active urease in Klebsiella aerogenes requires the presence of three structural genes for the apoprotein (ureA, ureB, and ureC), as well as four accessory genes (ureD, ureE, ureF, and ureG) that are involved in functional assembly of the metallocenter in this nickel-containing enzyme.

Based on studies involving a nonhydrolyzable analog of GTP, we conclude that nucleotide hydrolysis, not just binding, is required for this process.
