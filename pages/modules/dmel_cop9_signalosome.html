COP9 signalosome in Drosophila melanogaster

The COP9 signalosome (CSN) is an eight-subunit complex that removes the ubiquitin-like protein Nedd8 from cullins, thereby controlling the activity and assembly of cullin-RING ubiquitin ligases. Six subunits (CSN1, CSN2, CSN3, CSN4, CSN7 and CSN8) contain PCI domains and form a horseshoe-shaped scaffold, and two subunits (CSN5 and CSN6) contain MPN domains; CSN5 is the JAMM metalloprotease that carries the deneddylase activity. Drosophila has two CSN1 paralogs: CSN1b in the ubiquitous complex and CSN1a in a testis-specific variant. The Drosophila CSN2 subunit is encoded by alien.
