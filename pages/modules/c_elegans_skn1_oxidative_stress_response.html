SKN-1 oxidative and xenobiotic stress response in Caenorhabditis elegans

SKN-1 is the Caenorhabditis elegans CNC-family transcription factor related to mammalian Nrf1/Nrf2. In the intestine it drives the phase II detoxification response to oxidative and xenobiotic stress, inducing genes such as the glutamate-cysteine ligase gcs-1 and the glutathione S-transferase gst-4. SKN-1 binds DNA as a monomer through a Skn domain that joins a bZIP-like basic region to an N-terminal arm. Its activity is set by the balance of several post-translational inputs. Without stress, the WD40 substrate receptor WDR-23 associates with the CUL-4/DDB-1 ubiquitin ligase and keeps SKN-1 protein levels and nuclear accumulation low, presumably by directing it to proteasomal degradation. Glycogen synthase kinase-3 (GSK-3) phosphorylates SKN-1 and keeps it out of intestinal nuclei. Insulin/ IGF-1-like signaling kinases (AKT-1, AKT-2, SGK-1) phosphorylate and inhibit SKN-1 in parallel. Oxidative stress activates the NSY-1/SEK-1/PMK-1 p38 MAPK cascade, and PMK-1 phosphorylates SKN-1, which then accumulates in intestinal nuclei and activates its target genes. Loss of WDR-23 gives constitutive SKN-1 nuclear accumulation and strong target-gene induction, and skn-1 mutants are sensitive to oxidative stress and short lived.

Boundary. This is a concrete C. elegans module for the intestinal SKN-1 (mainly the SKN-1C isoform) phase II detoxification response. It has three post-translational inputs on SKN-1 (WDR-23/CUL-4/DDB-1 degradation, GSK-3 inhibitory phosphorylation and PMK-1 activating phosphorylation), the SKN-1 transcription step, and a phase II enzyme output part. The developmental role of maternal SKN-1 in EMS blastomere specification, the ER-associated SKN-1A proteasome-recovery response, and SKN-1B in ASI neurons for dietary restriction longevity are outside this boundary. The insulin/IGF-1-like AKT-1/AKT-2/SGK-1 inhibitory input (PMID:18358814) is real but the kinases are not reviewed here, so it is recorded as context. The GATA factor ELT-3 is a SKN-1 co-activator for gst-4 (PMID:28600327) and BRAP-2 restrains the p38 input (PMID:28600327); both are context, not members. Relation to the p38 immunity module. The NSY-1/SEK-1/PMK-1 cascade is shared with intestinal antibacterial immunity, where PMK-1 acts through ATF-7 and other targets. The cascade itself (TIR-1, NSY-1, SEK-1, PMK-1) is modeled in MODULE:c_elegans_pmk1_intestinal_immunity, which is the place for kinase-relay detail. Here the cascade is a single input part whose leaf annotons carry only each kinase's catalytic activity, and the module adds what is specific to the stress response: PMK-1 phosphorylation of SKN-1. The taxon-neutral MODULE:p38_cascade gives the generic relay. Relation to mammalian Nrf2. Worms have no KEAP1. WDR-23 is the DCAF11 ortholog, and the CUL-4/DDB-1/WDR-23 E3 ligase occupies the position that KEAP1-CUL3 holds for Nrf2 in mammals. This is an analogy of role, not of sequence, and no KEAP1-like cysteine-sensor step is modeled. Grounding caveats. The wdr-23 review is filed on S6FN32, a 498-residue product that lacks the N termini of the experimentally studied cytoplasmic WDR-23A and nuclear WDR-23B isoforms (both from P90794). The nuclear WDR-23B isoform is the one that restrains SKN-1 target genes (PMID:31409866). CUL-4 (Q17392), DDB-1 (Q21554), GCS-1 (Q20117) and GST-4 (Q21355) have no worm gene review; their accessions come from UniProt and their molecular functions are assigned from UniProt function and catalytic activity annotation and from family biology (human DDB1 review), so they are less firmly grounded than the reviewed members. Ubiquitination of SKN-1 by CUL-4/DDB-1/WDR-23 has not been shown biochemically in the cached literature; the 2009 study states it as presumed.

CUL-4/DDB-1/WDR-23 restraint of SKN-1Protein Complexwdr23_degradation

The WD40 protein WDR-23 associates with the CUL-4/DDB-1 ubiquitin ligase and represses SKN-1 protein levels, nuclear accumulation and activity, presumably by targeting SKN-1 for proteasomal degradation. The nuclear WDR-23B isoform restrains SKN-1 target genes; loss of WDR-23 raises gst-4 mRNA about 40-fold.
