NR3C4 androgen receptor genomic signaling in characterized jawed vertebrates

Full-length NR3C4 receptors couple androgen sensing to nuclear regulatory-DNA recognition and transcriptional responses in characterized jawed vertebrates. Cytosolic HSP90 and context-dependent co-chaperones support receptor competence. Nuclear availability, cooperative receptor-DNA assembly and coregulator recruitment are distinct aspects of the genomic response, not a universally ordered import switch. Mammalian FKBP4/FKBP5, p23, NCOA1/NCOA2 and NCOR1 provide experimentally grounded optional implementations. Fish paralogs differ in localization, ligand response and native physiological requirement. Steroid synthesis/transport, non-NR3C4 androgen sensors, non-genomic monomer signaling and ligand-binding-domain-deficient cancer variants lie outside this module.
