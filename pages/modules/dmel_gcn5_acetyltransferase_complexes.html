Gcn5-containing histone acetyltransferase complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

The histone acetyltransferase Gcn5 acetylates histone H3 as the catalytic subunit of several coactivator complexes. Gcn5 always sits in a HAT module with Ada3, Sgf29 and an Ada2 paralog. With Ada2b it forms part of SAGA, a large coactivator that also contains a TBP-interacting core, the Tra1 homolog Nipped-A, and a deubiquitinase module that removes ubiquitin from histone H2B through the protease Nonstop. With Ada2a it forms ATAC, which also contains the Atac1-3 subunits. Smaller ADA and CHAT (Chiffon-containing) complexes contain only the HAT module, with Chiffon in CHAT.
