Part 3: R-SMAD/SMAD4 complex formation and nuclear import
R-SMAD/SMAD4 complex formation and nuclear importTransport Stepsmad_complex_nuclear_import

Receptor-phosphorylated SMAD1/5/9 leave the receptor, bind the common mediator SMAD4, and accumulate in the nucleus. GO-CAM models compress this into a single edge from a plasma-membrane type I receptor kinase to a nuclear SMAD transcription factor activity (see projects/GOCAM_COMPARTMENTS.md). Two other gene products are needed: SMAD4, a pathway-specific partner, and the import machinery (importin-7/8 and importin-beta1 with the Ran system), included here by reference.
