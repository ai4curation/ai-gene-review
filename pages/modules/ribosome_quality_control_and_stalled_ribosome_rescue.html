Human ribosome-associated quality control and stalled-ribosome rescue — ZNF598 collision sensing, splitting by the ASC-1 (RQT) complex or PELO-HBS1L with ABCE1, and NEMF-LTN1 nascent-chain quality control with ANKZF1 release

Cytosolic ribosomes that stall during elongation, whether on an mRNA feature that blocks decoding (no-go), on a poly(A) tail or truncated 3' end that lacks a stop codon (nonstop), or through slow decoding, are detected and dismantled by a dedicated quality-control pathway. When a trailing ribosome runs into a stalled one, the two 40S subunits form a composite interface that the RING E3 ubiquitin ligase ZNF598 recognises; ZNF598 monoubiquitinates the 40S protein eS10 and builds K63-linked polyubiquitin chains on uS10. The ubiquitinated leading ribosome is split into subunits by the ASC-1 complex (the human RQC-trigger complex), in which the ATP-dependent helicase ASCC3 does the work, the CUE-domain protein ASCC2 reads the K63 chains and TRIP4 is the third subunit. Ribosomes stalled at the 3' end of an mRNA, with an empty mRNA channel and A site, are instead recognised by PELO, the eRF1-like factor, delivered by its GTPase partner HBS1L, and split by the ABC-family ATPase ABCE1. Splitting leaves a 60S subunit carrying peptidyl-tRNA. NEMF binds this 60S complex, senses the tRNA, and recruits and stabilises the E3 ligase listerin (LTN1), which polyubiquitinates the nascent chain at the exit tunnel; TCF25 biases the chains towards K48 linkage. NEMF also adds untemplated C-terminal alanine tails to the nascent chain, which expose buried lysines to LTN1 and, when LTN1 fails, act as C-terminal degrons. The tRNA-specific endonuclease ANKZF1 cuts the 3'CCA end off the P-site tRNA to release the ubiquitinated chain for proteasomal degradation, and the VCP-UFD1-NPLOC4 segregase is thought to extract it. In parallel, the stalling mRNA is silenced: GIGYF2 and the cap-binding protein 4EHP (EIF4E2) block further initiation on that message, and the mRNA is degraded.

Boundary and modelling decisions. (1) Substrates are not participants. The ribosomal proteins that ZNF598 ubiquitinates (eS10/RPS10, uS10/RPS20, and uS3/RPS3 in some studies), the ribosome itself, the stalling mRNA, the nascent chain and the P-site tRNA are acted on; they are not units. Ribosomal proteins are modelled once in cytoplasmic_ribosome, including RACK1, whose gene review records its role in resolving poly(A)-stalled ribosomes as a 40S constituent; RACK1 is referenced there, not repeated. The E2 that ZNF598 uses (UBE2D family) and ubiquitin supply are generic and not modelled. (2) ZNF598 has two annotons, the collision-sensor activity and the E3 activity, as in its review and in gomodel:63e5937500000591; its third GO-CAM activity, protein-RNA adaptor activity in gomodel:63e5937500000997, is pinned to the initiation-repression node rather than given a third annoton because that model is the only source for it. (3) Splitting is a variant set with two routes chosen by ribosome state, as in the human reconstitutions (PMID:32579943 for ASC-1 on collided ribosomes; PMID:21448132 and PMID:27543824 for PELO-HBS1L-ABCE1 on ribosomes with little mRNA past the P site). The human RQC-trigger complex is ASCC3, ASCC2 and TRIP4 only (PMID:36302773); ASCC1, the fourth subunit of the nuclear ASC-1 coactivator complex, has no RQC evidence and is excluded. TRIP4 and TCF25 are listed without a molecular function because each was assayed only within its complex and their reviews assign none; ASCC2 gets its ubiquitin-reading function and no ATPase or splitting activity. Whether ABCE1 also acts after the ASC-1 route is not established in the cited human work, so ABCE1 sits in the PELO-HBS1L route; the RQC GO-CAMs draw both ASCC3 and ABCE1 upstream of NEMF, and those ABCE1 activities are pinned to the referencing node. (4) ABCE1 is modelled in translation_elongation_and_termination (abce1_recycling) and is included here by module_ref; its rescue and RQC GO-CAM activities, which that module left for this one, are pinned here. (5) The nascent-chain branch follows the mammalian reconstitution (PMID:25578875, PMID:30244831): NEMF has two annotons (60S binding that recruits listerin; alanyl-tRNA binding for CAT tailing, as in its review and in the GO-CAMs); the peptidyl transfer that adds the tail is catalysed by the 60S rRNA.
