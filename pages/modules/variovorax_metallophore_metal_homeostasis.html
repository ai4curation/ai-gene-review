Variovorax periplasmic D-gluconate production, intracellular iron trafficking, polyphosphate turnover, and heavy-metal cation efflux

A functional grouping of four independent metabolic and metal-homeostasis subsystems across the cell envelope and cytoplasm of Variovorax paradoxus: (1) periplasmic PQQ-dependent glucose dehydrogenase (Gcd) oxidation of D-glucose to D-glucono-1,5-lactone coupled to membrane ubiquinone reduction in the D-gluconate pathway; (2) cytosolic YggX-family Fe(2+)-trafficking protein (FetP) binding of ferrous iron for intracellular iron transactions and Fe-S cluster assembly/repair; (3) cytoplasmic inorganic polyphosphate (polyP) polymerization by polyphosphate kinase 1 (Ppk) and hydrolytic depolymerization to orthophosphate by exopolyphosphatase (Ppx); and (4) transmembrane export of divalent heavy-metal cations (Co2+, Zn2+, Cd2+) by the tripartite CzcCBA HME-RND trans-envelope efflux complex (CzcA3 inner-membrane RND antiporter, CzcB periplasmic membrane-fusion adaptor, and CzcC outer-membrane factor channel) and the inner-membrane CzcD cation diffusion facilitator (CDF) antiporter.
