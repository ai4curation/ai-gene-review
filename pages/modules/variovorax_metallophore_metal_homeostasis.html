Variovorax periplasmic D-gluconate production, intracellular iron trafficking, polyphosphate synthesis, and RND metal cation efflux

A functional grouping of four independent metabolic and metal-homeostasis activities across the cell envelope and cytoplasm of Variovorax paradoxus: (1) periplasmic PQQ-dependent glucose dehydrogenase (Gcd) oxidation of D-glucose to D-glucono-1,5-lactone coupled to membrane ubiquinone reduction in the D-gluconate pathway; (2) cytosolic YggX-family Fe(2+)-trafficking protein (FetP) binding of ferrous iron for intracellular iron transactions and Fe-S cluster assembly/repair; (3) cytoplasmic polyphosphate kinase 1 (Ppk) ATP-dependent synthesis of long-chain inorganic polyphosphate (polyP); and (4) inner-membrane HME-RND proton/cation antiporter (CzcA3) transmembrane export of monoatomic metal cations.

Grounded on verified InterPro family entries (IPR017511, IPR007457, IPR003414, IPR004763) from the corresponding UniProt records. Because these four proteins act in distinct physiological pathways and do not form a sequential substrate-handoff cascade (and no siderophore/metallophore step is included in this module), no causal connections are asserted between the four parts.
