De novo deoxyribonucleoside triphosphate synthesis by class I ribonucleotide reductase

De novo formation of the four DNA precursors dATP, dGTP, dCTP and dTTP from ribonucleoside diphosphates in eukaryotes and other organisms with a class I (oxygen-dependent) ribonucleotide reductase. The reductase replaces the 2'-hydroxyl of ADP, GDP, CDP and UDP with hydrogen using electrons from thioredoxin or glutaredoxin; its large (alpha) subunit binds substrates and the allosteric effectors that balance the four products, and its small (beta) subunit carries the di-iron tyrosyl radical cofactor. In budding yeast the active enzyme is an alpha2 homodimer of Rnr1 (or the damage-induced Rnr3) with a beta heterodimer of the radical-bearing Rnr2 and the catalytically inactive but essential Rnr4. Nucleoside-diphosphate kinase then phosphorylates dADP, dGDP and dCDP to the triphosphates. dTTP cannot be made by direct reduction: dUMP is produced either by hydrolysis of dUTP by dUTP diphosphatase, which also keeps dUTP out of DNA, or by deamination of dCMP by dCMP deaminase; thymidylate synthase then methylates dUMP to dTMP with 5,10-methylenetetrahydrofolate, dTMP kinase makes dTDP and nucleoside-diphosphate kinase makes dTTP. Activity and subunit levels are tightly controlled by the cell cycle and the DNA damage checkpoint. Class II (adenosylcobalamin) and class III (anaerobic, glycyl-radical) reductases of prokaryotes, deoxynucleoside salvage and the mitochondrial dNTP supply pathway of animals are outside the module.

Scope: class Ia ribonucleotide reduction and dNTP formation in eukaryotes, with S. cerevisiae, human and E. coli exemplars; no PomBase GO-CAM is listed for this group. Reaction assessment: the four NDP reductions are one RNR annoton; the five NDP kinase reactions (dADP, dGDP, dCDP, dUDP, dTDP) are YNK1 annotons in parts 2 and 3. Enzyme corrections: YeastCyc lists a dCTP deaminase reaction (DCTP-DEAM-RXN) without a gene - S. cerevisiae has no dCTP deaminase (no EC 3.5.4.13 protein in UniProt for S288C) and instead deaminates dCMP with DCD1 (PMID:3023902), which the frame omits; DCD1 was added. The dCMP kinase reaction (RXN-7913) has no gene in YeastCyc but URA6 in the GO-CAM (URA6 phosphorylates CMP/dCMP, PMID:1333436); it supplies dCMP-to-dCDP salvage flux and is modelled in pyrimidine_salvage / de_novo_pyrimidine_synthesis rather than here. CDC8 dUMP kinase (RXN-14122) is a minor side activity (PMID:6094555). The formate-dependent class III reductase reactions RXN0-745/RXN0-746 listed by YeastCyc with no gene do not exist in yeast. GO-CAM disagreements: activities are filed under unrelated processes (GO:0046085 adenosine metabolic process, GO:0106387 'de novo' GMP biosynthesis, GO:0006207 pyrimidine nucleobase biosynthesis) rather than deoxyribonucleotide biosynthesis; CDC21 is placed in cytosol whereas the reviewed location is the nuclear periphery; URA6 carries the obsolete GO:0004127. The RNR is enabled by SGD complex entities CPX-1102 and CPX-1103 (Rnr1- and Rnr3-containing holoenzymes). Out of scope: thioredoxin/glutaredoxin reduction of RNR (TRX1/TRX2, TRR1), regulation by the Mec1-Rad53-Dun1 checkpoint, the Sml1 and Dif1 regulators and Crt1 repression (regulatory, not catalytic steps), class II and III reductases, the human p53-inducible RRM2B small subunit and mitochondrial salvage (mitochondrial_dntp_supply), and deoxynucleoside salvage (pyrimidine_salvage). The dTTP branch overlaps thymidylate_synthesis (human DUT/TYMS/DTYMK; folded into this module in the crosswalk). Thymidylate synthase is the only native dTMP source in budding and fission yeast, which lack thymidine kinase (PMID:14500848, PMID:15157888).
