Nuclear DNA-directed RNA polymerases I, II and III in Drosophila melanogaster

Eukaryotic nuclei contain three multisubunit DNA-directed RNA polymerases. RNA polymerase I transcribes the large ribosomal RNA precursor in the nucleolus, RNA polymerase II transcribes mRNAs and most snRNAs and microRNA precursors, and RNA polymerase III transcribes tRNAs, 5S rRNA, U6 snRNA and other short structured RNAs. Each enzyme has two large catalytic subunits that form the active site, a set of subunits shared by all three enzymes (RPABC1, RPABC2, RPABC3, RPABC4 and RPABC5), subunits shared by Pol I and Pol III (RPAC1 and RPAC2), and enzyme-specific subunits.
