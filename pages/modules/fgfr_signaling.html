FGFR signaling pathway module

Secreted FGF ligands, presented by heparan sulfate (paracrine FGFs) or by alpha/beta-Klotho co-receptors (endocrine FGFs), activate one or more FGFR receptor tyrosine kinase paralogs. The activated receptor phosphorylates the FRS2-family docking protein, which recruits GRB2-SOS to drive Ras-MAPK signalling and GRB2-GAB1-p85 to engage PI3K, while PLC-gamma binds the receptor directly and hydrolyses PIP2. The kinase-less paralog-like FGFRL1 attenuates signalling by sequestering ligand.

Receptor specificity of the fibroblast growth factor family. The complete mammalian FGF family.
Establishes the ligand-by-receptor-paralog (and splice-isoform) specificity matrix for the whole mammalian FGF family, the basis for modelling receptor paralogs as a union of overlapping alternatives rather than as interchangeable copies.

Paralog union modelling (2026-09-30). The vertebrate FGF system is a whole-genome-duplication expansion: four receptor kinases (FGFR1-4), about eighteen secreted FGF ligands, two FRS docking proteins (FRS2, FRS3) and two SOS Ras GEFs (SOS1, SOS2), whereas protostomes carry one or two receptors (Drosophila breathless and heartless, C. elegans egl-15). Each step that is carried by a paralog group is therefore modelled as a variant set with axis "paralog" and selection ONE_OR_MORE: a concrete realization (a cell type, tissue, or species) uses any non-empty subset of the paralogs, and the module step as a whole is the union of what the paralogs do. Shared ancestral function is recorded once per paralog through the common PAINT nodes (PTN004704281, PTN000699279, PTN001230349), while lineage-specific nodes (PTN002814653 for FGFR1, PTN004704289 for FGFR4) and paralog-specific features (IIIb/IIIc splicing in FGFR1-3 only, FGFR4 recycling and FGF19/beta-Klotho bile-acid signalling, FGFR1c/Klotho FGF23 signalling) are recorded on the individual variant. Paralogs are not assumed to be interchangeable: the union says that each one can realize the step, not that each can substitute for every other in every context. FGFRL1 (sometimes called FGFR5) shares the FGFR ectodomain architecture but sits in a different PANTHER family (PTHR19890) and lacks a kinase domain, so it cannot satisfy the receptor kinase step and is modelled as a separate optional negative-regulatory part rather than as a fifth receptor variant. Heparan sulfate is a glycan cofactor, not a gene product, and is recorded in step descriptions rather than as an annoton. Negative feedback is split by scope: the FGFR-proximal attenuators IL17RD/SEF and CBL (recruited through FRS2-GRB2) are modelled here as an optional attenuation part, while Sprouty/SPRED and DUSP6, which act on Ras-RAF and ERK downstream of many receptors, are modelled in MODULE:erk_cascade. The context-dependent JAK-STAT output is outside the current boundary. Falcon integration (2026-06-28): Falcon findings are linked as evidence and family-level support; machine-generated Falcon citations remain curation support and are not manually adjudicated here.

Part 1: ligand and co-receptor engagement
FGF ligand and co-receptor engage FGFRRegulatory Stepfgf_fgfr_binding

A secreted FGF binds the FGFR ectodomain together with a co-receptor. Paracrine FGFs bind heparan sulfate with high affinity, and heparan sulfate is required for the active asymmetric receptor dimer. Endocrine FGFs (FGF19, FGF21, FGF23) have low heparan sulfate affinity, circulate systemically, and need an alpha- or beta-Klotho co-receptor to form a high-affinity complex.

Variant set: FGF ligand paralog classes and their co-receptors by paralog (One Or More)
PMID:17623664
The fibroblast growth factor (FGF) 19 subfamily of ligands, FGF19, FGF21, and FGF23, function as hormones that regulate bile acid, fatty acid, glucose, and phosphate metabolism in target organs through activating FGF receptors (FGFR1-4).

The FGF ligand paralogs split into heparan-sulfate-dependent paracrine FGFs and Klotho-dependent endocrine FGFs. A given tissue can respond to either or both classes, so the step is the union of the two. Intracellular FGFs (FGF11-14, also in PTHR11486) do not signal through FGFRs and are excluded.

Paracrine FGF with heparan sulfateRegulatory Stepparacrine_fgf_variant

Canonical paracrine FGFs (FGF1, FGF2, FGF7, FGF8, FGF10 and others) bind the receptor with heparan sulfate proteoglycan as cofactor. Ligand choice is constrained by receptor paralog and IIIb/IIIc isoform; FGF1 is the only ligand that activates every receptor isoform, while FGF7 and FGF10 are selective for the epithelial FGFR2 IIIb isoform. Paracrine FGFs bind heparin/heparan sulfate directly (heparin binding, GO:0008201).

Endocrine FGF with Klotho co-receptorRegulatory Stependocrine_fgf_variant

Endocrine FGF19, FGF21 and FGF23 require a Klotho-family co-receptor bound to the FGFR. Which receptor paralog and which Klotho is expressed decides the target tissue: FGF23 acts mainly through Klotho-FGFR1c in kidney and parathyroid, FGF21 mainly through beta-Klotho-FGFR1c in adipose tissue (it can also signal through beta-Klotho bound to FGFR2c or FGFR3c, but not efficiently through FGFR4), and FGF19 through beta-Klotho-FGFR4 in hepatocytes. These ligands act as circulating hormones (hormone activity, GO:0005179) and, unlike paracrine FGFs, are largely non-mitogenic.
