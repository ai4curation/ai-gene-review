Mammalian SWI/SNF (BAF) chromatin remodeling complexes — canonical BAF (ARID1A/B, DPF1-3), PBAF (ARID2, PBRM1, BRD7, PHF10) and non-canonical GBAF/ncBAF (BICRA/BICRAL, BRD9) built on a shared SMARCC-SMARCD-SMARCE1-SMARCB1 core and a SMARCA4/SMARCA2 ATPase module

The mammalian SWI/SNF or BAF (BRG1/BRM-associated factor) complexes are ATP-dependent chromatin remodelers of the SWI2/SNF2 family. Each complex uses one of two mutually exclusive ATPases, SMARCA4 (BRG1) or SMARCA2 (BRM), to translocate on nucleosomal DNA, sliding or ejecting histone octamers and making promoters and enhancers accessible to transcription factors. The ATPase sits in an ATPase module with the actin-related protein ACTL6A or ACTL6B, beta-actin and a BCL7 protein. Most complexes also share a core module built from a SMARCC1/SMARCC2 dimer and one SMARCD paralogue, onto which SMARCE1 and SMARCB1 dock. Three classes are distinguished by their specific subunits. Canonical BAF (cBAF) contains ARID1A or ARID1B, a DPF protein (DPF1, DPF2 or DPF3) and SS18. Polybromo-associated BAF (PBAF), the RSC-type complex, contains ARID2, PBRM1, BRD7 and PHF10, and lacks SS18. Non-canonical BAF (ncBAF or GBAF) contains BICRA (GLTSCR1) or BICRAL (GLTSCR1L) with BRD9 and SS18, a reduced core of SMARCC1 and SMARCD1, and no SMARCE1, SMARCB1 or DPF subunit. Paralogue choice and cell-type-specific subunits (for example ACTL6B and DPF1/DPF3 in post-mitotic neurons) create many complex variants. The complexes are recruited to chromatin by transcription factors and modified histones and both activate and repress genes. Subunits are mutated in about a fifth of human cancers and in Coffin-Siris syndrome.

RGD curates 74 human genes to PW:0001339 and has no gene-level diagram; its text describes four remodeler families (SWI/SNF, INO80/SWR1, ISWI and CHD) that share no subunits beyond actin-related proteins and perform different reactions. The project therefore models the pathway as one human module per SNF2-family complex group. This module is the SWI/SNF group; the ISWI group is modules/iswi_chromatin_remodeling (drafted together with this one). The INO80, SRCAP, TIP60/p400 and CHD/NuRD genes on the RGD list are left for other modules and are not modelled here. RGD genes in scope (22): SMARCA2 SMARCA4 SMARCB1 SMARCC1 SMARCC2 SMARCD1 SMARCD2 SMARCD3 SMARCE1 ARID1A ARID1B ARID2 PBRM1 BRD7 BRD9 PHF10 DPF1 DPF2 DPF3 ACTL6A ACTL6B ACTB. All 22 are modelled. Seven gene products not on the RGD list are added because the current subcomplex definitions cannot be stated without them: BICRA (GLTSCR1) and BICRAL (GLTSCR1L), the defining subunits of ncBAF/GBAF (RGD lists BRD9 but not its partner); SS18 and SS18L1, which distinguish cBAF and ncBAF from PBAF; and BCL7A, BCL7B and BCL7C, pan-mSWI/SNF ATPase-module subunits identified by proteomics (PMID:23644491, PMID:30343899). BCL11A and BCL11B, also reported as BAF subunits in PMID:23644491, are not added: they are cell-type-restricted transcription factors whose stable incorporation is less well established, and treating them as subunits would bring transcription-factor targeting inside the module boundary. Structure. Following the Drosophila analogue modules/dmel_brahma_complexes (BAP, PBAP, ncBAP), the three classes are variants of one REALIZER variant set (selection ONE_OR_MORE, since they coexist), each a protein-complex annoton whose active units are listed in full. The shared subunits therefore appear once per class; this repetition is deliberate so that each complex annoton states its own composition. Within each class the paralogue alternatives are FAMILY units with representative members, not separate variants: SMARCA4/SMARCA2, ACTL6A/ACTL6B, SMARCC1/SMARCC2, SMARCD1-3, ARID1A/ARID1B, DPF1-3, BCL7A-C, SS18/SS18L1 and BICRA/BICRAL. The first representative listed is the predominant member. In ncBAF the core positions are single gene products (SMARCC1, SMARCD1, ACTL6A) because the purifications detected only those paralogues (PMID:30343899) and the GBAF definition names BAF53a (PMID:29374058). SMARCC is a dimer in cBAF and PBAF (homo- or heterodimer), recorded in the unit role rather than as stoichiometry. Cell-type-restricted complexes named by RGD (npBAF, nBAF, esBAF) and the GO terms for them are paralogue selections of the cBAF class (for example ACTL6B with DPF1/DPF3 in neurons, SMARCD3 in neural progenitors) and are not modelled as further variants. The Drosophila module is the evolutionary counterpart and is not referenced as a module_ref because its members are fly proteins. Complex typing. GO has no class that groups exactly the three mammalian SWI/SNF classes: GO:0070603 SWI/SNF superfamily-type complex also covers the ISWI-, CHD- and INO80-type remodelers, so it is used only as an evidence entry, and the module concepts are the three class terms plus chromatin remodeling. cBAF is typed SWI/SNF complex (whose definition names SMARCA4/BRG1), PBAF RSC-type complex (whose definition names PB1/BAF180) and ncBAF GBAF complex (defined from PMID:29374058).
