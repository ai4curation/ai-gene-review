SREBP-SCAP complex in Drosophila melanogaster

SREBP is a membrane-bound basic helix-loop-helix leucine zipper transcription factor held in the endoplasmic reticulum by its escort protein SCAP. When lipid levels fall, SCAP carries SREBP to the Golgi, where two proteolytic cleavages release the N-terminal transcription factor domain, which enters the nucleus and activates genes of lipid synthesis. In Drosophila, which cannot make sterols, SREBP processing is controlled by phosphatidylethanolamine rather than by sterols, and SREBP mainly regulates fatty acid synthesis.
