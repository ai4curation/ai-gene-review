EGFR signaling pathway module

Bilaterian EGFR signaling couples an EGF-family ligand to an EGFR/ERBB-family receptor tyrosine kinase, creates phosphotyrosine docking sites for GRB2/drk/Sem-5 adaptors and SOS Ras GEFs, and routes the signal through Ras-RAF-MEK-ERK. The conserved invertebrate and vertebrate core is represented with fly and worm variants, while vertebrate ERBB heterodimer and PI3K branches are kept as optional extensions.

The module is intentionally centered on the conserved Bilaterian EGFR -> GRB2/SOS -> Ras/MAPK path. Human EGF/EGFR, Drosophila Spi/Egfr, and C. elegans LIN-3/LET-23 are alternative receptor-layer realizations, whereas the SH2/SH3 adaptor, Ras GEF, Ras, RAF, MEK, and ERK tiers are represented as broad families with human, fly, and worm exemplars. Vertebrate ERBB2/PI3K signaling remains optional because ERBB heterodimerization and strong PI3K recruitment are vertebrate-specific elaborations rather than requirements for fly or worm EGFR signaling. The Ras/MAPK tier is the same biological sidecar detailed in MODULE:erk_cascade.
