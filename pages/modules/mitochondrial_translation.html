Human mitochondrial translation — mt-tRNA modification, aminoacylation and Glu-tRNAGln transamidation, mitoribosome, and initiation/elongation factors

Thirteen polypeptides of the oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS) system — seven Complex I subunits, cytochrome b of Complex III, three Complex IV subunits and two Complex V subunits — are encoded by mitochondrial DNA and are made inside the mitochondrial matrix by a dedicated translation system whose protein components are all nuclear-encoded and imported. The system has four kinds of part. (1) The 22 mtDNA-encoded tRNAs must be post-transcriptionally modified to read the mitochondrial genetic code: the GTPBP3-MTO1 complex installs 5-taurinomethyluridine (taum5U) at the wobble position U34 of mt-tRNAs for Leu(UUR), Trp, Gln, Glu and Lys; NSUN3 methylates C34 of the single mt-tRNAMet to m5C34, which the dioxygenase ALKBH1 oxidises to 5-formylcytidine (f5C34) so that one tRNAMet can decode both AUG and AUA; and TRIT1 isopentenylates A37 (i6A37) of a subset of mt-tRNAs. (2) Nuclear-encoded mitochondrial aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases (the *ARS2 family, e.g. DARS2) charge the tRNAs; because mitochondria lack a glutaminyl-tRNA synthetase, EARS2 first mischarges tRNAGln with glutamate and (3) the heterotrimeric glutamyl-tRNA(Gln) amidotransferase GatCAB (QRSL1/GatA, GATB, GATC) transamidates Glu-tRNAGln to Gln-tRNAGln. (4) The 55S mitoribosome — whose 28S small subunit contains, among others, uS7m (MRPS7), mS22 (MRPS22) and the pentatricopeptide-repeat protein mS39 (PTCD3) at the mRNA entry channel — translates the leaderless mt-mRNAs with the initiation factors MTIF2 and MTIF3 and the elongation factors TUFM (EF-Tu), TSFM (EF-Ts, its nucleotide exchange factor) and GFM1 (EF-G1). Because every one of the 13 products depends on the same apparatus, a lesion anywhere in it produces a combined (multi-complex) OXPHOS deficiency rather than a single-complex defect. The dismech knowledge base curates these as the combined oxidative phosphorylation deficiency series: GTPBP3 -> COXPD23 (MONDO:0014525); MTO1 -> MTO1 deficiency / COXPD10 (MONDO:0013865); NSUN3 -> COXPD48 (MONDO:0033566); TRIT1 -> COXPD35 (MONDO:0054742); GATC -> COXPD42 (MONDO:0030008); MRPS7 -> COXPD34 (MONDO:0054741), also a Perrault syndrome gene (MONDO:0017312); PTCD3 -> COXPD51 (MONDO:0033631); TUFM -> COXPD4 (MONDO:0012534); and MRPS22 -> 46,XX gonadal dysgenesis (MONDO:0009299). ALKBH1 is curated in the dismech COXPD48 entry as the pathway partner of NSUN3, not as a disease gene.
