Human SRCAP complex — ATP-dependent exchange of nucleosomal H2A-H2B for H2A.Z-H2B by the SRCAP ATPase, the RUVBL1-RUVBL2 ring, the YL1 (VPS72) H2A.Z chaperone with ARP6 and ZNHIT1, and the ACTL6A-actin-DMAP1-GAS41 module; SRCAP/RUVBL1/RUVBL2/VPS72/ACTR6/ZNHIT1/ACTL6A/ACTB/DMAP1/YEATS4

The SRCAP complex is the human SWR1-type chromatin remodeler. It replaces a canonical H2A-H2B dimer in a nucleosome with a dimer of the histone variant H2A.Z and H2B, which places H2A.Z in the nucleosomes that flank promoters and other regulatory elements. The complex has about ten subunits. The SNF2-family ATPase SRCAP is the scaffold and motor: its split ATPase domain engages nucleosomal DNA, and the long insertion between its two ATPase lobes threads into a hexameric ring of the AAA+ ATPases RUVBL1 and RUVBL2. A module of YL1 (VPS72), the actin-related protein ARP6 (ACTR6) and ZNHIT1 binds the incoming H2A.Z-H2B dimer: YL1 is an H2A.Z-specific deposition chaperone, and ARP6 and ZNHIT1 are needed for complex integrity and nucleosome binding. A second module of ACTL6A, actin and the SANT-domain protein DMAP1 binds the helicase-SANT- associated helix of SRCAP, together with the YEATS-domain acetyl-histone reader GAS41 (YEATS4). The complex has no acetyltransferase subunit. The same exchange chemistry is performed in a different setting by the p400 (EP400) ATPase within the TIP60 complex, which shares the RUVBL1-RUVBL2 ring, YL1, DMAP1, GAS41, ACTL6A and actin but lacks ARP6 and ZNHIT1.

Derived from the RGD Pathway Ontology term PW:0001339 (chromatin remodeling pathway) through the RGD_PATHWAY_ONTOLOGY project. PW:0001339 has no diagram genes and curates 74 human genes that span all four SNF2 remodeler families (BAF/PBAF, INO80 and SWR1, ISWI, CHD/NuRD), so the project models one human module per complex group rather than one module for the pathway. This module is the SRCAP group. The sister module tip60_p400_complex models the p400 (EP400) ATPase in the TIP60 complex; the INO80 complex, BAF/PBAF, ISWI and CHD/NuRD complexes are drafted separately. RGD genes modelled here: SRCAP, ZNHIT1, VPS72, YEATS4, DMAP1, ACTR6, RUVBL1, RUVBL2, ACTL6A and ACTB. ACTL6A and actin are included because the cryo-EM structure lists both as subunits (PMID:30337683). The shared subunits RUVBL1-RUVBL2, VPS72, the ACTL6A-actin-DMAP1 module and YEATS4 are written identically in this module and in tip60_p400_complex. Molecular functions were taken from each subunit's experimental GOA rows in QuickGO (November 2026). The SRCAP annoton carries ATP-dependent H2AZ histone chaperone activity, a descendant of the ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler activity that SRCAP has by IDA (PMID:17617668); the more specific term is authored here from the reconstituted exchange reaction (PMID:16634648) and matches the IMP row of yeast Swr1 for the same term. RUVBL1 and RUVBL2 carry their experimental ATP hydrolysis activity, VPS72 its histone chaperone activity (IMP, PMID:26974126) and YEATS4 its H3K27ac reader activity (IDA, PMID:30071723). ACTR6, ZNHIT1, ACTL6A, actin and DMAP1 have no experimental molecular function in human GOA that describes their role in this complex (DMAP1 has a transcription-factor binding row and ZNHIT1 a histone binding row, neither informative here), so they are units without a function and their roles are given in prose. Boundary decisions. (1) H2A.Z is the target, not a part. H2AZ1 and H2AZ2 are the cargo of the exchange reaction: the complex binds the free H2A.Z-H2B dimer and leaves it in the nucleosome, and H2A.Z performs no step of the exchange. Applying the substrate-versus-participant rule of the project guidelines: the entity that performs the step is the SRCAP ATPase with the YL1 chaperone; the comparator check shows that neither human H2AZ1 and H2AZ2 nor yeast Htz1 is annotated part_of Swr1 complex in GOA (all three are part_of nucleosome), while the remodeler subunits are; and the co-purification of an endogenous H2A.Z-H2B dimer with the complex (PMID:30337683) is a loaded intermediate, not a subunit. H2A.Z therefore appears as the cargo of the SRCAP and VPS72 functions. Neither histone is on the RGD list. (2) Transcriptional coactivation is context. SRCAP was first described as a CBP-interacting coactivator and carries IDA transcription coactivator rows; GO-CAM gomodel:682fbcd000002112 models that coactivator role in cAMP/PKA signalling. It is the downstream consequence of H2A.Z deposition at promoters, not a part of the exchange machinery, and is not linked here. (3) Other SWR1-family subunits. CFDP1 (BCNT, the Swc5 homologue) has only IBA rows for Swr1 complex and is not on the RGD list or in the structure's subunit list, so it is named but not modelled. ANP32E, a chaperone that removes H2A.Z from chromatin (PMID:24463511), acts in the opposite direction and associates with the p400/TIP60 complex; it is excluded. (4) INO80 shares RUVBL1, RUVBL2 and ACTL6A with this complex and can also exchange histone dimers; it is modelled by another module and not here. (5) DNA repair and muscle-gene activation (ZNHIT1 phosphorylation by p38 MAPK, PMID:20473270) are biological contexts of H2A.Z deposition and are not modelled as parts. (6) Submodule grouping into the ATPase, RUVBL ring, YL1-ARP6-ZNHIT1 module and actin module follows the head, C and N modules of PMID:30337683. PANTHER family ids and PTNs are deliberately not asserted. GO-CAM links. No human GO-CAM in gocams/index.tsv models the SRCAP exchange activity. SRCAP appears only in gomodel:682fbcd000002112 as a transcription coactivator (decision 2). The seed's best_gocam lead, gomodel:617b481400000460 (KAT5 in homologous recombination), concerns the TIP60 complex and DNA repair and is not linked here.

Three RUVBL1-RUVBL2 heterodimers form the hexameric head of the SWR1-type exchange machinery. The insertion between the two lobes of the SNF2-family ATPase (SRCAP or EP400) threads into the ring, which anchors the H2A.Z-H2B chaperone VPS72 and the ATPase C-lobe. The ring is a structural hub in this context; its own ATP hydrolysis is measured in vitro, but no step of histone exchange is known to be driven by it. The same hexamer is also a subunit of INO80 and of the R2TP chaperone, which are outside this module.
