Neurotrophin signaling through Toll-family receptors module

In Drosophila the secreted cystine-knot neurotrophins DNT1 (NT1, Spz2) and DNT2 (Spz5) act on neurons through the Toll-family receptors Toll-6 and Toll-7 rather than through Trk-family receptor tyrosine kinases. DNT binding to Toll-6 or Toll-7 regulates neuronal survival, motor axon targeting and structural synaptic plasticity; the kinase-less Trk-like receptor Kekkon-6 binds DNT2 and acts together with Toll-6 at synapses.

Boundary. The module has two required steps, a secreted Drosophila neurotrophin (DNT) ligand and a Toll-family neurotrophin receptor on the responding neuron, plus one optional step, the kinase-less Trk-like co-receptor Kekkon-6 (Kek-6), which binds DNT2 and acts with Toll-6 at the neuromuscular junction (PMID:28846707) and in a dopaminergic circuit (PMID:39704728). Kek-6 is optional because Toll-6 and Toll-7 signal without it in the neuronal-survival and axon-targeting assays (PMID:23892553). The intracellular adaptors are not modelled as parts and are recorded here: Toll-6 promotes survival through MyD88 and NF-kappaB (Dorsal, Dif) and death through Weckle (Wek), Sarm (Ect4/dSarm) and JNK, and the outcome also depends on DNT cleavage and on which Toll is engaged (PMID:28373203). These adaptors are shared with Toll-1 and Toll-2 signaling (the MyD88-Tube-Pelle complex is modelled in dmel_toll_signaling), the choice among them is context dependent, and Toll-6/Toll-7 instruct olfactory wiring without their cytoplasmic domains (PMID:25741726), so no single adaptor route is a required step of neurotrophin signaling through Toll receptors. Kek-6 signals through CaMKII and VAP33A rather than through a Toll adaptor (PMID:28846707); those partners are also left out. The immune and dorsal-ventral Spaetzle-Toll-1 pathway (protease cascades, Spz, Toll-1, MyD88-Tube-Pelle, Cactus, Dorsal/Dif) stays in dmel_toll_signaling. Spz5 also activates Toll-1 (PMID:30361090) and acts as a glial Toll-6 ligand released from dying neurons (PMID:30745142); these roles are not neurotrophic and are outside the module. Ligands included and excluded. DNT1 (NT1, UniProtKB:B7TB45) and DNT2 (spz5, UniProtKB:Q9VZX1) are the ligands: both bind Toll-6 and Toll-7 (PMID:23892553). Spaetzle (spz; not to be confused with DNT-3, which is spz3) has neurotrophic functions (PMID:19018662, PMID:24124519) and its cystine-knot domain binds the Toll-7 ectodomain in vitro (PMID:31088910), but its neurotrophic receptor has not been shown to be Toll-6 or Toll-7, and its canonical receptor is Toll-1; it is therefore not a representative member of the ligand step. DNT-3 (spz3) promotes visual-system neuronal survival (PMID:42552905) with no Toll receptor yet assigned. Toll-2 works with DNT-2 in the visual system by genetic interaction (PMID:42552905) and regulates adult brain plasticity (PMID:32066523); because DNT-2 binding to Toll-2 has not been shown biochemically, Toll-2 is recorded here and not added as a receptor representative. The same evidence bar (genetic interaction alone does not make a module member) keeps Multiplexin out of the ligand step of collagen_ddr_signaling. Ligand function term. The ligand step uses GO:0008083 growth factor activity, an is_a child of GO:0048018 receptor ligand activity (verified in QuickGO), following the project decision that a ligand step takes receptor ligand activity or a fitting child. Both DNTs carry GO:0008083 in GOA (IMP, PMID:19018662; IBA from PAINT node PTN002354018) and GO:0048018 (IDA, PMID:31088910; spz5 also IDA PMID:30361090 and IPI PMID:28846707). The receptor-specific GO:0005121 Toll binding, which both DNTs also carry (IDA PMID:31088910 and IBA), is not used. Ligand family and PAINT node. NT1 and spz5 are both in PANTHER:PTHR23199 (NEUROTROPHIN 1-RELATED; UniProt DR PANTHER: NT1 PTHR23199:SF12, spz5 PTHR23199:SF16). The family is broader than the ligand role: it also contains Spaetzle (SF12), Spz3 (SF13), Spz4 (SF5), Spz6 and the horseshoe-crab clotting protein coagulogen (SF12), and UniProt lists members in tardigrades, molluscs and echinoderms. The role is therefore restricted to the DNT1 and DNT2 members. PAINT node PTN002354018 in PTHR23199-paint.tsv carries F:GO:0008083 growth factor activity (seeds FB:FBgn0003495 spz, FB:FBgn0035379 spz5, FB:FBgn0261526 NT1) and F:GO:0005121 Toll binding (same seeds plus FB:FBgn0035056 spz6), and propagates to NT1 and spz5 in GOA (IBA WITH PTN002354018). The node's taxon column is empty in the local table. Receptor family. No PANTHER id is asserted for the receptor step. The two sources disagree on Toll-6: UniProt's cross-reference places Toll-6 (UniProtKB:Q9VUN0) in PTHR24366 (IG(IMMUNOGLOBULIN) AND LRR(LEUCINE RICH REPEAT) DOMAINS), subfamily SF96, while PANTHER's own tree places it in PTHR45836 (SLIT HOMOLOG), subfamily SF8 (TOLL-LIKE RECEPTOR 6), which is why Slit PAINT nodes propagate into it (see below and interpro/panther/PTHR45836/PTHR45836-review.yaml, which lists SF8 among the branches wrongly inheriting Roundabout binding). Which placement is right is not decided here, and no override is recorded. Toll-7 (UniProtKB:Q7KIN0) is in PTHR45712 (AGAP008170-PA), subfamily SF22; neither receptor is in PTHR24365 (TOLL-LIKE RECEPTOR). No PAINT node is shared by the two receptors: the only Toll-7 IBA node, PTN002809265 (F:GO:0038023 signaling receptor activity), is a broad leucine-rich-repeat receptor node placed at Eukaryota whose other seeds are mammalian TLR3, CD180 and related proteins, and Toll-6's IBA rows come from the Slit nodes PTN002911633 (heparin binding, Roundabout binding) and PTN002911625 (axon guidance), which look like over-propagation into a Toll receptor. Neither node is attached to the step. The receptor function is GO:0005030 neurotrophin receptor activity, which GOA gives Toll-6 and Toll-7 by IMP (PMID:23892553) and which the Toll-7 gene review uses as its core function; GO:0005030 is_a GO:0038023 signaling receptor activity and part_of GO:0038179. A Toll-like receptor activity term is not used because Toll-6 and Toll-7 here sense an endogenous growth factor, not a pathogen pattern. Taxon scope. The module is scoped to Drosophila melanogaster (NCBITaxon:7227), the only species in which the ligand-receptor pairing has been tested. Scope criterion, shared with collagen_ddr_signaling: a module takes the taxon of the PAINT nodes that ground its steps; where no PAINT node grounds a step, it takes the clade in which the ligand-receptor pairing has been tested. Here the ligand node has no taxon and no node grounds the receptor step. DNT1 and Spz5 are conserved across insects (PMID:19018662: the DNT1 cystine knot is conserved in mosquitoes, honey bee, jewel wasp and body louse; Spz5 is "very highly conserved in insects"), so the module may extend to Insecta, but no cached paper shows Toll-6/Toll-7 orthologues acting as neurotrophin receptors outside Drosophila, and PAINT gives no taxon for the ligand node. The broader scope is left as a knowledge gap. Relation to vertebrate neurotrophin signaling. The vertebrate pathway (NGF, BDNF, NT-3, NT-4 acting through TRK tyrosine kinases and p75NTR) is modelled in neurotrophin_trk_signaling. The two modules share the GO parent GO:0038179 but not their receptors: Drosophila has no Trk, p75NTR or Sortilin orthologue (PMID:23892553), and its Trk-like Kek proteins lack the kinase domain (PMID:28846707). Zhu et al. propose that DNT1 and the vertebrate neurotrophins share a cystine-knot fold and a common bilaterian ancestor, but they also state that the similarity could be convergent (PMID:19018662). The module therefore does not assert homology between the DNTs and the vertebrate neurotrophins. Annotation state (QuickGO, queried 2026-11-06). GO:0038179 neurotrophin signaling pathway is annotated in D. melanogaster only to Toll-6 (UniProtKB:Q9VUN0), Toll-7 (UniProtKB:Q7KIN0) and kek6 (UniProtKB:A0A0B4KHL5), each by IEA from GO_REF:0000108 (inferred from their GO:0005030 neurotrophin receptor activity, IMP PMID:23892553 for Toll-6 and Toll-7; IDA PMID:28846707 and IBA PTN008615184 for kek6). Neither ligand is on the pathway: NT1 (UniProtKB:B7TB45) and spz5 (UniProtKB:Q9VZX1) carry GO:0008083 and GO:0048018 but their only signaling-pathway process is GO:0008063 Toll signaling pathway (IDA PMID:31088910; spz5 also IDA and IGI PMID:30361090), with neuronal processes GO:0043524 negative regulation of neuron apoptotic process, GO:0008045 motor neuron axon guidance and GO:0031637 regulation of neuronal synaptic plasticity in response to neurotrophin from PMID:19018662. This is the Category B gap of the LIGANDS_IN_SIGNALING check (ligand only on a related pathway term). The fly row for the sibling term GO:0038180 nerve growth factor signaling pathway is Arms (UniProtKB:Q9W2I0, IBA PTN001190877), a propagation from a PAINT node whose fly descendant is the KIDINS220/ARMS orthologue; it is not part of this module. Of the module's members only Toll-7 has a gene review in this repository (genes/DROME/Toll-7); NT1, spz5, Toll-6 and kek6 have none.
