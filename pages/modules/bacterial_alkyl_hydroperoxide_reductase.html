Bacterial AhpC peroxide reduction and regeneration systems

Bacterial AhpC peroxiredoxins reduce hydroperoxides and are recycled by distinct electron-donor systems. Sampled implementations couple AhpC to AhpF in E. coli, Salmonella and Bacillus, to a thioredoxin/reductase pair in H. pylori and M. tuberculosis, or to the mycobacterial Lpd–lipoyl-DlaT–AhpD relay. Pairing, donor preference, substrate range and oligomeric state are bounded by their native or reconstituted experimental contexts.
