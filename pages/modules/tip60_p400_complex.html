Human NuA4/TIP60-p400 complex (TIP60-C) — KAT5 acetylation of histones H4 and H2A by the piccolo core (KAT5, EPC1/EPC2, ING3, MEAF6) combined with EP400-driven exchange of H2A-H2B for H2A.Z-H2B on the RUVBL1-RUVBL2 ring with YL1 (VPS72), plus the actin module, TRRAP and the MRG15-MRGBP-BRD8 reader module; EP400/KAT5/TRRAP/EPC1/EPC2/ING3/MEAF6/MORF4L1/MORF4L2/MRGBP/BRD8/DMAP1/YEATS4/VPS72/ACTL6A/ACTB/RUVBL1/RUVBL2

The human TIP60 complex (TIP60-C, also called NuA4/TIP60 or TIP60-p400) is a supercomplex of about twenty subunits that carries two chromatin-modifying activities which budding yeast splits between the NuA4 acetyltransferase and the SWR1 remodeler. Its MYST-family acetyltransferase KAT5 (TIP60), held in a piccolo core with EPC1 or EPC2, ING3 and MEAF6, acetylates nucleosomal histone H4 (K5, K8, K12, K16) and H2A, as well as H2A.Z and H2AX. Its SNF2-family ATPase EP400 (p400) exchanges canonical H2A-H2B dimers for H2A.Z-H2B, using the same RUVBL1-RUVBL2 hexamer, the H2A.Z chaperone YL1 (VPS72) and the actin module (ACTL6A, actin, DMAP1) as the SRCAP complex; the ARP6 and ZNHIT1 subunits of SRCAP are absent. EP400 is the scaffold that joins the SWR1-like and NuA4-like parts. The large PIKK-family protein TRRAP is a flexibly tethered platform that binds transcriptional activators, and acetyl- and methyl-histone readers (GAS41/YEATS4, ING3, BRD8 and the MRG15/MRGX-MRGBP module) bind modified chromatin. Acetylation of the nucleosome by the complex stimulates its own H2A.Z exchange activity. TIP60-C acts in transcriptional activation and in the repair of DNA double-strand breaks.

Boundary decisions. (1) H2A.Z is the target, not a part. As in srcap_h2az_deposition, H2AZ1 and H2AZ2 are the cargo of the exchange (and a substrate of KAT5); the near-stoichiometric H2A.Z-H2B dimer in purified TIP60-C (PMID:39260417) is a loaded intermediate held by VPS72. By the substrate-versus-participant rule and the comparator check (no human or yeast H2A.Z is annotated part_of NuA4 or Swr1 complex in GOA; all are part_of nucleosome), H2A.Z appears only as cargo and target. (2) DNA repair and transcription-factor coactivation are context, not parts.
