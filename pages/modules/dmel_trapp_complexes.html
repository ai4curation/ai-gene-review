TRAPP complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

Transport protein particle (TRAPP) complexes are multisubunit guanine nucleotide exchange factors for Rab GTPases in the secretory and autophagic pathways. A shared core, in which Bet3, Bet5 and Trs23 form the catalytic surface for Rab1 nucleotide exchange, is extended with Trs20, Trs31, Trs33 and the Trs20-like CG9067. Addition of the large subunits Trs120 (SIDL) and Trs130 (brunelleschi, brun) forms TRAPPII, which acts at the Golgi and in Drosophila is required for male meiotic cytokinesis; addition of Trs85 (gryzun, gry) and the TRAPPC11, TRAPPC12 and TRAPPC13 homologs forms TRAPPIII, which acts in ER-to-Golgi transport and autophagy.

Decomposition. The shared core is split into the FlyBase catalytic core group (Bet3, Bet5, Trs23) and the other shared subunits; the complex-specific subunits are modelled as alternatives. TRAPPII-specific subunits (SIDL, brun) and TRAPPIII-specific subunits (gry, l(3)76BDm, CG11396, CG4953) follow the FlyBase TRAPPII and TRAPPIII groups. TRAPPII is reported to activate Rab11 as well as Rab1 in some systems; that specificity is not resolved for Drosophila and is not modelled. FlyBase groups realized by this module: TRAPP (FBgg0000149), TRAPP-CAT (FBgg0002202). Subgroups folded into it: TRAPPII, TRAPPIII.
