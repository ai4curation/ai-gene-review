Mutator foci 22G-RNA amplification in Caenorhabditis elegans

In the Caenorhabditis elegans germline, silencing guided by primary small RNAs is weak, and is made effective by a second round of small RNA synthesis. A primary Argonaute such as RDE-1 recognizes a target transcript and recruits the NYN-domain endoribonuclease RDE-8, which cuts it. The ribonucleotidyltransferase RDE-3 (MUT-2) gives the 5' fragment a poly(UG) tail, and RNA-dependent RNA polymerases (RdRPs) then use the tailed RNA as a template to synthesize abundant 22-nucleotide, 5'-guanosine secondary siRNAs (22G-RNAs). Most of this amplification takes place in Mutator foci. These are perinuclear, phase-separated condensates that lie next to P granules at nuclear pores. The intrinsically disordered Q/N-rich protein MUT-16 is the scaffold of the foci. Distinct regions of MUT-16 recruit the RdRP RRF-1; MUT-15 (RDE-5), which in turn recruits RDE-8; the DEAD-box RNA helicase MUT-14, which acts redundantly with SMUT-1 to start amplification; RDE-3; and, through the adaptor RDE-2 (MUT-8), the 3'-5' exoribonuclease MUT-7. The 22G-RNAs made in this way are loaded into worm-specific Argonautes (WAGOs) that lack slicer residues, such as WAGO-1, and silence transposons, pseudogenes and other germline-repressed genes. A second germline RdRP, EGO-1, makes most of the 22G-RNAs bound by the catalytic Argonaute CSR-1, which target most germline-expressed genes and protect them from WAGO-mediated silencing instead of silencing them. The two RdRP systems are not fully separate: EGO-1 also contributes, partly redundantly with RRF-1, to WAGO-class 22G-RNAs.

RDE-2 (MUT-8) has no catalytic activity. Its N-terminal region binds MUT-16, and its C-terminal region binds a Caenorhabditis-specific insertion in the MUT7-C domain of MUT-7. Part 3: target mRNA engagement and cleavage
MUT-14 helicase and MUT-15-recruited RDE-8 cleavage of target mRNAMolecular Functiontarget_cleavage

Amplification starts on the target mRNA. The primary Argonaute RDE-1 recruits the NYN-domain endoribonuclease RDE-8 to target RNA, and RDE-8 cuts it. The cut both contributes to silencing and makes the 5' fragment that RDE-3 tails and RRF-1 copies. MUT-15 (RDE-5), recruited to Mutator foci by MUT-16, in turn recruits RDE-8. The DEAD-box RNA helicases MUT-14 and SMUT-1 act redundantly to start amplification in Mutator foci, possibly by recruiting or retaining target mRNA; their position relative to cleavage is not known.
