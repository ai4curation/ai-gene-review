NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complex in Saccharomyces cerevisiae

NuA4 is the essential histone H4/H2A acetyltransferase of budding yeast, a 13-subunit complex of about 1 MDa. It acetylates the N-terminal tails of nucleosomal histones H4 and H2A and of the variant H2A.Z (Htz1), acting as a transcriptional coactivator, in DNA double-strand break repair and in cell cycle progression. The complex has three parts. The catalytic HAT module, piccolo NuA4, contains the MYST acetyltransferase Esa1, the Enhancer of Polycomb scaffold Epl1, the ING protein Yng2 and Eaf6; Epl1 activates Esa1 and binds the nucleosome, and Yng2 orients the module so that the H4 and H2A tails reach the active site. Piccolo NuA4 is also found as a free complex that acetylates chromatin globally without targeting. A rigid hub of Tra1 and an Eaf1-scaffolded core (Swc4, Arp4, Act1 and the Epl1 C-terminus), with Yaf9, carries the activator-binding Tra1 subunit that recruits NuA4 to promoters, and the Epl1 C-terminus docks the HAT module onto the Eaf1 HSA domain. The Eaf3-Eaf5-Eaf7 trimer (TINTIN), anchored through Eaf5, adds an H3K36me3 reader and also exists apart from NuA4. Reader domains in the complex (the Yng2 PHD finger for H3K4me3, the Eaf3 chromodomain for H3K36me3, the Yaf9 YEATS domain for H3K27ac, Arp4 for phosphorylated H2A) help select nucleosomes near the recruitment site.

Boundary. The module contains the 13 NuA4 subunits in three parts: the piccolo NuA4 HAT module (Esa1, Epl1, Yng2, Eaf6), the Tra1-Eaf1 hub and core (Tra1, Eaf1, Swc4, Arp4, Act1, Yaf9) and the TINTIN trimer (Eaf3, Eaf5, Eaf7). Histones H4, H2A and H2A.Z are substrates, not parts. Act1, Arp4, Swc4 and Yaf9 are shared with the SWR1 H2A.Z exchange complex, Tra1 with SAGA, and Eaf6 with the NuA3 H3 acetyltransferase; SWR1, SAGA and NuA3 are separate modules. Eaf3 is also a subunit of Rpd3S. The part boundaries follow the structural studies (PMID:36263929, PMID:36198799), which place Yaf9 outside the rigid hub; it is put with the hub part here as a recruitment and reader subunit. Two forms of the HAT module. Piccolo NuA4 is found inside NuA4 and as a free complex; Boudreault et al. proposed that the free form performs untargeted global acetylation and the holo-complex targeted acetylation. Disrupting the Epl1-Eaf1 contact releases piccolo NuA4 (PMID:36066422). The relative contributions of the two forms in vivo are recorded as a knowledge gap. Ontology. The module concept is GO:0035267 NuA4 histone acetyltransferase complex, which GO classifies under GO:0043189 H4/H2A histone acetyltransferase complex and GO:0000123 histone acetyltransferase complex, and so as part_of GO:0000785 chromatin. NuA4 acts on nucleosomes: native piccolo NuA4 strongly prefers chromatin to free histones, Esa1 alone cannot acetylate nucleosomes, and the HAT module binds the nucleosome disk face. The inherited chromatin location is therefore reasonable for this complex, which is not a counterexample to the GO:0000123 edge questioned in modules/scer_hat_b_complex.yaml. The only activity on non-chromatin histones is in vitro: recombinant Esa1 alone, or with Epl1, acetylates free histones (PMID:21984211), a property of partial complexes. Esa1 also acetylates non-histone proteins, such as Atg3 at pre-autophagosomal structures (PMID:22539722); that is a separate, non-histone activity recorded in the ESA1 review. Piccolo term. GO:0032777 piccolo histone acetyltransferase complex carries no capable_of axiom, so unlike NuA4 it is not classified under GO:0043189, GO:0000123 or chromatin, although it is the part of NuA4 that does the acetylation; the missing edge is recorded under proposed_ontology_changes. Its text definition calls the complex heterotrimeric (Esa1, Yng2, Epl1), as first purified, whereas reconstitution and structural studies define the module with Eaf6 as a fourth subunit. The module follows the structural studies and includes Eaf6 in part 1; whether native free piccolo NuA4 contains Eaf6 is a knowledge gap, so no definition change is requested yet. GOA does not annotate Eaf6 to GO:0032777, and the EAF6 review does not add it. Gene reviews. ESA1, EPL1, YNG2, EAF6 and EAF3 have reviews in this repository. Their core functions support the annotons: histone H4 acetyltransferase activity (Esa1); acetyltransferase activator activity, nucleosome binding and protein-macromolecule adaptor activity, and a contribution to H4 acetylation (Epl1); histone H3K4me3 reader activity and a contribution to H4 acetylation (Yng2); a contribution to H4 acetylation (Eaf6); histone H3K36me3 reader activity (Eaf3). Tra1, Eaf1, Swc4, Arp4, Act1, Yaf9, Eaf5 and Eaf7 have no reviews; their annotons rest on the cited papers only, and the module validator reports them as lacking review support.
