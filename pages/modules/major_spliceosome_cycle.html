Human major (U2-type) spliceosome cycle — snRNPs (Sm and LSm2-8 rings, U1, U2 with SF3a/SF3b, U4/U6, U5, tri-snRNP) assembling through the E/A, B, Bact/B*, C, C*/P and ILS states, the NineTeen complex, and the DEAD/DEAH-box ATPases that drive each transition, with RNA-catalysed branching and exon ligation

The major spliceosome removes U2-type (GT-AG) introns from pre-mRNA by two transesterification reactions. It is not a preformed enzyme but assembles anew on each intron from five small nuclear ribonucleoproteins (snRNPs) and many non-snRNP proteins. U1 snRNP recognises the 5' splice site, SF1 and U2AF the branch-point sequence and polypyrimidine tract (E complex), and the 17S U2 snRNP, through its SF3a and SF3b subcomplexes and the DEAD-box protein DDX46 (PRP5), pairs U2 snRNA with the branch site to form the prespliceosome (A complex). The pre-assembled U4/U6.U5 tri-snRNP then joins (pre-B); DDX23 (PRP28) hands the 5' splice site from U1 to U6 (B complex), and the U5 helicase BRR2 (SNRNP200) unwinds U4/U6, releasing U1 and U4 and letting U6 pair with U2. The NineTeen complex (PRP19/CDC5L) and NTC-related proteins join to form the activated Bact complex, which DHX16 (PRP2) converts to the catalytically primed B*. The first reaction (branching) forms the lariat intermediate (C complex); DHX38 (PRP16) remodels C into C*, the second-step factors position the 3' splice site, and exon ligation produces the post-catalytic P complex. DHX8 (PRP22) releases the spliced mRNA, carrying the exon junction complex deposited during catalysis, and DHX15 (PRP43) disassembles the intron lariat spliceosome so that the snRNPs can be recycled. Both chemical steps are catalysed by RNA: U6 snRNA positions two catalytic metal ions within a U2/U6 active site held on the protein scaffold PRPF8.

After mRNA release the intron lariat spliceosome (ILS) is disassembled by DHX15 (PRP43), releasing the lariat for debranching and the U2, U5 and U6 snRNPs; SART3 re-anneals U4 and U6 to regenerate the di-snRNP for another round.
