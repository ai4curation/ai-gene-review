Bacterial lipoyl-dependent acetoin and stereoisomer-specific butanediol catabolism

Lipoyl-dependent acetoin cleavage produces acetaldehyde and acetyl-CoA through an AcoA/AcoB E1 reaction, AcoC acetyl transfer and E3-mediated carrier regeneration. NAD-dependent R,R/meso oxidation and metal-dependent PQQ-mediated S,S-butanediol oxidation can supply acetoin upstream. The compared bacterial implementations differ in E2 architecture, availability of a dedicated E3 and strength of direct biochemical evidence. Native systems are kept separate; the bacterial envelope does not imply universal conservation.
