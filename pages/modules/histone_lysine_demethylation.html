Human histone lysine demethylation — FAD-dependent amine-oxidase demethylases KDM1A (LSD1) and KDM1B (LSD2) erasing H3K4me1/me2, and the Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate-dependent JmjC demethylase families KDM2 (H3K36me1/me2), KDM3 (H3K9me1/me2), KDM4 (H3K9/H3K36me2/me3), KDM5 (H3K4me2/me3), KDM6 (H3K27me2/me3) and KDM7/PHF (H3K9me1/me2)

Methyl marks on histone lysines are removed by two unrelated classes of enzyme, each specific for the site and degree of methylation. The KDM1 (LSD) family, KDM1A (LSD1) and KDM1B (LSD2), are flavin amine oxidases: bound FAD oxidises the methylated epsilon-amine of a mono- or dimethyl lysine to an imine, which hydrolyses to the demethylated lysine and formaldehyde, and the reduced flavin is reoxidised by an electron acceptor, giving hydrogen peroxide when the acceptor is oxygen. The mechanism needs a protonated amine and so cannot act on trimethyl lysine. Both enzymes erase H3K4me1 and H3K4me2; KDM1A works mainly within corepressor complexes such as CoREST and, with the androgen receptor, also removes H3K9me1/me2. The second class are JmjC-domain dioxygenases. Using Fe(II), oxygen and 2-oxoglutarate, which is decarboxylated to succinate and carbon dioxide, they hydroxylate the methyl group, and the resulting hemiaminal releases formaldehyde; this chemistry also removes trimethyl marks. The human histone demethylases of this class fall into six families: KDM2A/B remove H3K36me1/me2; KDM3A/B remove H3K9me1/me2; KDM4A-D remove H3K9me3/me2, and KDM4A-C also H3K36me3/me2; KDM5A-D remove H3K4me3/me2; KDM6A (UTX) and KDM6B (JMJD3) remove the Polycomb mark H3K27me3/me2; and the PHD-JmjC enzymes KDM7A, PHF8 and PHF2 remove H3K9me1/me2, with H3K27me2 (KDM7A) or H4K20me1 (PHF8) as further substrates. Demethylation of activating marks (H3K4) accompanies gene repression, and demethylation of repressive marks (H3K9, H3K27) accompanies activation, so each family is paired with the methyltransferases that write its mark.

Reactions were taken from Rhea (the EC master reactions cross-referenced from each GO term) and every ChEBI label was checked in OLS. For the FAD-dependent reaction Rhea writes a generic acceptor; FAD, FADH2, O2 and H2O2 are written from the LSD1 mechanism paper PMID:15811342. GO process. GO has obsoleted histone demethylation, histone lysine demethylation and every site-specific histone demethylation process (H3K4, H3K9, H3K27, H3K36, H4K20), each because it represents a molecular function, so the content now lives in the histone demethylase activity terms on the annotons and no new process term is requested. The module process is GO:0006338 chromatin remodeling, which human GOA uses for the JmjC families (IBA, and IDA for KDM2B). It is the neutral choice: H3K4 demethylation accompanies repression and H3K9/H3K27 demethylation accompanies activation, so neither a positive nor a negative regulation of transcription term fits the module as a whole. Boundary and modelling decisions. (1) Two catalytic parts by chemistry. Part 1 is the FAD-dependent amine oxidases, part 2 the Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate-dependent JmjC dioxygenases, as RGD's own description divides them. Each enzyme is one gene-product annoton with its own activity, because each was shown to be active as an isolated protein. Paralogues are grouped as variants (selection ONE_OR_MORE, since paralogues are co-expressed and act at different loci rather than excluding one another); within part 2 the six families are sub-parts, each defined by the mark it erases. (2) One function per annoton. Several enzymes have a second genuine site: KDM1A on H3K9me1/me2 with the androgen receptor, KDM4A-C on H3K36me3/me2, KDM7A on H3K27me2 and PHF8 on H4K20me1. The annoton carries the activity with the strongest evidence and the second site is in its role text, rather than duplicating annotons. (3) Complexes are left to their modules. KDM1A is the catalytic subunit of CoREST, which the sibling histone_deacetylation models with KDM1A as a unit without this activity; KDM6A is a substoichiometric MLL3/MLL4 subunit in compass_family_h3k4_methylation; KDM2B is the PRC1.1 targeting subunit in polycomb_repressive_complexes. Each sibling defers the demethylase activity to this module, and it is written here once. Cofactor and partner proteins (RCOR1 for KDM1A on nucleosomes, NPAC/GLYR1 for KDM1B, ARID5B for PHF2) are named in role text and not modelled as units here. (4) Measured activity is not automatically function. Each JmjC candidate was judged on whether it evolved to demethylate histone lysines. JMJD1C (on the RGD list) is excluded: a systematic comparison found no histone demethylase activity for it where KDM3A and KDM3B were active (PMID:23593242), and its one human GOA row is an IMP for the parent H3K9 term (PMID:32034158) that cannot separate catalysis from an indirect effect. KDM8 (JMJD5, on the RGD list) is excluded: it was reported as an H3K36me2 demethylase (PMID:20457893), but substrate screening and structures assign it as an arginyl C-3 hydroxylase rather than a KDM (PMID:29563586), and it also has peptidase rows. Not on the RGD list and not added: JMJD6, whose histone arginine demethylase claim is disputed against its lysyl hydroxylase activity (RGD's own text says so) and which in any case acts on arginine; the ribosomal oxygenases RIOX1 (NO66) and RIOX2 (MINA), whose experimental human rows are for ribosomal protein histidyl hydroxylation, not histone demethylation; HR (hairless), which has no experimental human row; KDM4E, which has in vitro H3K9me3/me2 activity (PMID:21914792) but is not on the RGD list and has no cellular evidence cached here; and UTY (KDM6C), whose H3K27 demethylase activity is real but reduced in vitro (PMID:24798337) and whose in vivo role is not established in human. These are named here only. (5) Readers. The PHD, Tudor and ARID domains of the demethylases are part of the enzymes and appear in role text; free-standing methyllysine readers stay out, as the split plan says. (6) Substrates. Histones are substrates, not participants, and are represented only as ChEBI lysyl-residue groundings on the functions. (7) Writers. The plan's "paired by the mark they erase" is carried by the family node descriptions and by reference to the writer modules: H3K4 methylation in compass_family_h3k4_methylation (erased by KDM1 and KDM5), H3K27 methylation in polycomb_repressive_complexes (erased by KDM6), and H3K9 and H3K36 methylation in histone_lysine_methylation_h3k9_h3k36_h3k79_h4k20 (erased by KDM3, KDM4 and KDM2). Part 1: FAD-dependent amine-oxidase demethylation of mono- and dimethyl H3K4
KDM1 (LSD) family FAD-dependent amine-oxidase histone demethylasesMolecular Functionamine_oxidase_demethylases

KDM1A (LSD1) and KDM1B (LSD2) oxidise the methylated epsilon-amine of a mono- or dimethyl lysine with bound FAD, giving an imine that hydrolyses to the demethylated lysine and formaldehyde; the reduced flavin is reoxidised by an electron acceptor, O2 in vitro, which yields hydrogen peroxide. The chemistry cannot act on trimethyl lysine.

KDM1A carries EXP and IDA rows for GO:0140682 (PMID:15620353, PMID:15811342, PMID:20164337, PMID:26214369, PMID:20228790, PMID:27292636). The amine-oxidase chemistry needs a protonated alpha-amine hydrogen, so it removes only mono- and dimethyl marks. As a subunit of corepressor complexes (CoREST, modelled with KDM1A as a unit in histone_deacetylation) it erases H3K4me1/me2; with the androgen receptor it removes H3K9me1/me2 (PMID:16079795), a switch controlled by H3T6 phosphorylation (PMID:20228790). That second activity is GO:0140685 FAD-dependent histone H3K9me/H3K9me2 demethylase activity; it is recorded here in prose because an annoton carries one function and H3K4 is the activity of the corepressor complexes. KDM1A acts on free histones and peptides alone; its activity on nucleosomes needs CoREST, which is why CoREST is modelled as the complex in the sibling module.

Part 2: Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate-dependent hydroxylation-based demethylation of mono-, di- and trimethyl lysines
JmjC domain Fe(II)/2-oxoglutarate-dependent histone lysine demethylases (KDM2-KDM7)Molecular Functionjmjc_demethylases

JmjC-domain dioxygenases hydroxylate the methyl group of a methylated lysine using Fe(II), O2 and 2-oxoglutarate, which is decarboxylated to succinate and CO2; the hemiaminal collapses to the demethylated lysine and formaldehyde. Because no protonated amine is needed they can act on trimethyl lysine. Each family is specific for a site and methylation state, grouped here by family, with paralogues as alternatives.
