NuRD nucleosome remodeling and deacetylase complex in Drosophila melanogaster

NuRD couples ATP-dependent nucleosome remodeling by the CHD-family ATPase Mi-2 with histone deacetylation by HDAC1 (Rpd3). The deacetylase core also contains the MTA homolog MTA1-like and the histone-binding protein Caf1-55 (p55), and the complex further contains MBD-like, the GATAD2 homolog Simjang and CDK2AP1. NuRD represses transcription, contributes to Polycomb-dependent silencing of Hox genes, and in Drosophila Mi-2 also forms the dMec complex with the zinc-finger protein MEP-1.
