RNA polymerase II general transcription factors in Drosophila melanogaster

The general transcription factors TFIID, TFIIA, TFIIB, TFIIE, TFIIF and TFIIH assemble with RNA polymerase II on core promoters to form the preinitiation complex. TFIID, built from the TATA-box binding protein (TBP) and TBP-associated factors (TAFs), recognizes core promoter elements; TFIIA stabilizes TBP binding to DNA. TFIIF escorts polymerase into the complex and TFIIE recruits TFIIH. TFIIH contains the XPB (hay) translocase that opens promoter DNA, the XPD helicase, and the CDK-activating kinase (Cdk7, CycH, Mat1), which phosphorylates the polymerase C-terminal domain during initiation; TFIIH also acts in nucleotide excision repair. In Drosophila testis, paralogous testis-specific TAFs (tTAFs) and a germline TFIIA small subunit variant drive the spermatocyte transcription program.
