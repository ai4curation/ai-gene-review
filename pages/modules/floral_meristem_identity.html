Specification of floral meristem identity at the Arabidopsis shoot apex

After floral induction the Arabidopsis shoot apical meristem becomes an indeterminate inflorescence meristem whose lateral primordia must be assigned floral, rather than shoot, fate. Floral integrators supply the input: the florigen FT, a PEBP-family protein made in leaves, reaches the apex and acts as a transcriptional coactivator of the bZIP factor FD to activate APETALA1 (AP1) and the MADS-box integrators SOC1 and FRUITFULL (FUL); SOC1 (as a heterodimer with AGL24) binds the LEAFY (LFY) promoter and activates it, and FUL promotes the reproductive transition partially redundantly with SOC1. LFY, a plant-specific DNA-binding transcription factor, directly induces AP1 in the floral anlagen, and AP1 in turn up-regulates LFY, so the two lock in floral fate through positive feedback. The SQUAMOSA-clade MADS factors AP1, CAULIFLOWER (CAL) and FUL act partially redundantly in this step (ap1 cal ful triple mutants make no flowers); AP1 also acts as a master repressor in the young flower, directly silencing the flowering-time/inflorescence genes SOC1, AGL24 and SVP so that the shoot program does not continue in floral meristems. The F-box protein UNUSUAL FLORAL ORGANS (UFO) binds LFY and DNA to form a LFY-UFO transcriptional complex that redirects LFY to region-specific targets in the young flower. TERMINAL FLOWER1 (TFL1), a PEBP paralog of FT, is expressed in the centre of the inflorescence meristem and acts as an FD-dependent corepressor that keeps LFY and AP1 out of the shoot apex, maintaining indeterminate inflorescence identity; AP1 and LFY reciprocally repress TFL1 in floral primordia. The A-class factor APETALA2 contributes to floral meristem establishment alongside AP1. Floral organ identity (the ABCE program activated later by LFY, UFO and AP1 together with SEP proteins), the leaf-side photoperiod pathway that produces FT, and the age, vernalization and gibberellin pathways converging on the integrators are separate modules.
