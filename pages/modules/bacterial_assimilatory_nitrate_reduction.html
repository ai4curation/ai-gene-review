Bacterial assimilatory nitrate uptake and reduction

Bacterial nitrate utilization supplies ammonium for biosynthesis through successive nitrate-to-nitrite and nitrite-to-ammonium reductions. Four species-native implementations distinguish ferredoxin-dependent cyanobacterial enzymes, a multisubunit Bacillus nitrate reductase, and fused pyridine-nucleotide-linked nitrate reductases in Pseudomonas and Mycolicibacterium. Uptake assemblies are required when extracellular nitrate is the input; an intracellular-nitrate boundary permits omission of uptake. A separate E. coli comparison illustrates nitrate-fed assimilation during respiration and depends on an upstream respiratory nitrate reductase outside the represented protein panel. The module ends at ammonium, before its incorporation into glutamine or glutamate.
