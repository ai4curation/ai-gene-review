Bacterial lipopolysaccharide transport

Reusable Gram-negative bacterial module for ATP-driven extraction of lipopolysaccharide (LPS) from the outer leaflet of the inner membrane, transfer across the periplasmic Lpt bridge, and insertion into the outer leaflet of the outer membrane. The module separates the LptB2FGC inner- membrane machine, soluble LptA bridge, and LptDE outer-membrane translocon.
