Beta-ketoadipate lower pathway from 3-oxoadipate to central metabolites

Species-neutral bacterial module for the shared lower beta-ketoadipate pathway that converts 3-oxoadipate to acetyl-CoA and succinyl-CoA. A two-subunit PcaIJ-like CoA-transferase first transfers CoA from succinyl-CoA to 3-oxoadipate, producing 3-oxoadipyl-CoA and succinate. A PcaF-like thiolase then cleaves 3-oxoadipyl-CoA with CoA to acetyl-CoA and succinyl-CoA. The module begins after catechol- and protocatechuate-derived upper branches converge at 3-oxoadipate and excludes those branch-specific ring-cleavage reactions.
