Glycerolipid / triacylglycerol biosynthesis (glycerol-3-phosphate and DHAP routes)

Triacylglycerol (TAG) is the principal energy-storage lipid of eukaryotic cells. It is assembled from a three-carbon glycerol backbone and fatty acyl-CoAs, and the pathway's intermediates phosphatidic acid (PA) and diacylglycerol (DAG) are also the branch points to the membrane glycerophospholipids. Lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) is formed either by sn-1 acylation of glycerol-3-phosphate (glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase, GPAT) or by acylation of dihydroxyacetone phosphate followed by NADPH-dependent reduction of 1-acyl-DHAP; in fungi the same GPAT enzymes accept both acceptors, whereas the DHAP acyltransferase of animals belongs to peroxisomal ether-lipid synthesis. LPA is acylated at sn-2 to PA by 1-acylglycerol-3-phosphate O-acyltransferases (LPAATs) of the AGPAT family and, in fungi, also by MBOAT-family and lipid-droplet acyltransferases. A Mg2+-dependent lipin-family phosphatidate phosphatase, a soluble enzyme that acts on membranes, dephosphorylates PA to DAG. DAG is finally esterified at sn-3 either with an acyl-CoA by the diacylglycerol O-acyltransferases (the MBOAT-family DGAT1 and the DGAT2-family enzymes) or, independently of acyl-CoA, by a phospholipid:diacylglycerol acyltransferase (PDAT) that transfers an acyl chain from a glycerophospholipid. Synthesis takes place on the endoplasmic reticulum and on lipid droplets, into which TAG is packaged; in animals the first acylation is also performed at the mitochondrial outer membrane. Inherited defects in the mammalian enzymes cause metabolic disease (AGPAT2 congenital generalized lipodystrophy, LPIN1 recurrent rhabdomyolysis, DGAT1 congenital diarrhoea).

GENERALIZE (YeastPathways project, 2026-10). The former human-only module (GPAM -> AGPAT2 -> LPIN1 -> DGAT1/DGAT2) was broadened to eukaryotic glycerolipid/TAG synthesis. All human leaves, families and gene-review evidence are kept unchanged. Structural changes: (1) LPA formation is now a ONE_OR_MORE variant set of the glycerol-3-phosphate route and the DHAP route. The G3P-acylation node holds two GPAT annotons because animal GPAM (PANTHER PTHR12563, mitochondrial outer membrane) and fungal GPT2/SCT1 (PTHR31605, ER and lipid droplets) are not in the same PANTHER family; the fungal enzymes are dual G3P/DHAP acyltransferases (PMID:11544256), so they also ground the DHAP-acylation step. The animal DHAP acyltransferase GNPAT acts in peroxisomal ether-lipid synthesis (ether_lipid_plasmalogen_biosynthesis) and is not used here. (2) LPA acylation gained fungal exemplars: SLC1 and S. pombe SPAC1851.02 join AGPAT2 in PTHR10434, and two further annotons were added for the MBOAT-family ALE1/LPT1 (PTHR13906:SF4) and the lipid-droplet LOA1/VPS66 (PTHR23063:SF60). ICT1 (Q12385), reported as an LPAAT, was not added because its contribution to de novo PA synthesis is not established in the cached literature. (3) PAH1 and S. pombe ned1 were added to the lipin family node. (4) DAG acylation is a ONE_OR_MORE variant set of acyl-CoA-dependent DGAT (DGAT1 MBOAT; DGAT2 family with yeast DGA1 and S. pombe dga1) and acyl-CoA-independent PDAT (LRO1, S. pombe plh1). The sterol acyltransferase ARE2 (PTHR10408, the DGAT1 family) contributes a minor DGAT activity in yeast (PMID:11751875) but is not used as an exemplar because its principal function is steryl-ester synthesis. YeastCyc corrections: DPP1 and LPP1, listed as PA phosphatases in TRIGLSYN-PWY, are broad-specificity lipid phosphate phosphatases that are not involved in de novo TAG synthesis (PMID:22345606) and were not added; ALE1 and LOA1 are missing from YeastCyc and were added; RXN-12383 (DAG:DAG transacylase) has no yeast gene and is not modelled; DGK1 (RXN-12959) runs the reverse reaction (DAG -> PA with CTP, PMID:18458076) and is noted as a regulator of the PA/DAG balance outside the module; GPD1/GPD2 glycerol-3-phosphate supply is in glycerol_metabolism. Compartments: YeastCyc-derived GO-CAMs put every activity in cytosol, but the yeast GPATs and SLC1 are ER and lipid-droplet enzymes, AYR1 is on lipid droplets and ER, DGA1 is enriched on lipid droplets, and PAH1 is a soluble enzyme that acts on the nuclear/ER membrane (PMID:22345606, PMID:10617610, PMID:11751830, PMID:16467296). GO-CAM disagreements: (a) all four YeastPathways models place activities in cytosol; (b) YeastPathways_TRIGLSYN-PWY includes DPP1 and LPP1 as PA phosphatases and DGK1 as part of TAG biosynthesis; (c) the PomBase model 6796b94c00005663 also contains SPAC6G10.03c as an LPA acyltransferase (UniProt names it a probable mitochondrial cardiolipin-specific deacylase), SPAC977.08/ayr2 as a second 1-acyl-DHAP reductase (UniProt: uncharacterized oxidoreductase), ptp4/dgk1, pld1, gpd1/gpd2 and ept1, which are either unverified for this activity or outside this module. Plant seed-oil assembly is modelled separately in seed_triacylglycerol_biosynthesis; the CDP-diacylglycerol and Kennedy branches that consume PA and DAG are cdp_dag_phospholipid_synthesis and kennedy_pathway_phospholipid_synthesis.
