Wnt-TCF (canonical Wingless) signaling in Drosophila melanogaster

In canonical Wnt signaling, secreted Wnt proteins, chiefly Wingless, bind Frizzled receptors and the LRP5/6 co-receptor Arrow. Wnt secretion requires palmitoleoylation by Porcupine and transport by Wntless. In the absence of ligand, a destruction complex of Axin, Adenomatous polyposis coli (Apc or Apc2), Casein kinase I alpha and Shaggy (GSK3) phosphorylates Armadillo (beta-catenin) and targets it for degradation. Ligand engagement recruits Dishevelled and Axin to the receptor, inhibiting the destruction complex, so that Armadillo accumulates, enters the nucleus and joins Pangolin (TCF), Legless (BCL9), Pygopus, Chip and Ssdp in a Wnt enhanceosome that activates Wingless target genes. The pathway patterns the embryonic segments, the wing, and many other tissues.
