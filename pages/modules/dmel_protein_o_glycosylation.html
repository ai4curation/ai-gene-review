Protein O-glycosylation in Drosophila melanogaster

O-glycosylation attaches sugars to the hydroxyl groups of serine, threonine or hydroxylysine. Flies use several independent O-glycosylation pathways, each started by a dedicated transferase. Mucin-type O-GalNAc glycans are initiated by a family of polypeptide GalNAc transferases (Pgant) and extended by core 1 galactosyltransferase (C1GalTA) and glucuronyltransferase. EGF repeats of Notch and other receptors carry O-fucose added by Ofut1 and elongated by the GlcNAc transferase Fringe, and O-glucose added by Rumi and extended with xylose by Shams and Xxylt. Thrombospondin repeats are O-fucosylated by Ofut2. O-mannose glycans are added in the ER by the Rotated abdomen-Twisted (POMT1-POMT2) complex and, on cadherins, by the TMTC transferases. Nuclear and cytosolic proteins are modified by single O-GlcNAc residues by Super sex combs (OGT) and the modification is removed by O-GlcNAcase, while EGF repeats receive extracellular O-GlcNAc from Eogt. Collagen hydroxylysines are galactosylated after hydroxylation by Plod.

Boundary. FlyBase's protein O-glycosylation group also contains the chondroitin sulfate and heparan sulfate proteoglycan biosynthesis subgroups (GACSB, GAHSB), which are xylose-initiated O-glycans; they are modelled in the separate module dmel_glycosaminoglycan_biosynthesis and are excluded here. The POMT1-POMT2 complex group is realized as the POMT variant of the O-mannosylation part. The O-GalNAc, O-fucose, O-glucose and hydroxylysine branches are shown with their elongation steps in order; the different O-glycan classes are independent of one another. No molecular function is given to GlcAT-P in this branch (its glucuronyltransferase specificity on core 1 has been shown in vitro but the GO term for that acceptor is not established for this reaction) or to the TMTC transferases, whose O-mannosyltransferase activity in flies is inferred from their human orthologs. Plod is listed by FlyBase under O-galactosylation because lysine hydroxylation precedes galactose transfer; Plod itself is a hydroxylase. The intracellular O-GlcNAc cycle is a regulatory modification of nucleocytoplasmic proteins, distinct from the secretory-pathway glycans.
