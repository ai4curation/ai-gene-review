Eiger (TNF) signaling pathway in Drosophila melanogaster

Eiger is the single Drosophila member of the tumor necrosis factor superfamily. It is a type II transmembrane protein that can be released as a soluble ligand by the metalloprotease Tace. Eiger binds the TNF receptors Grindelwald and Wengen, which signal through Traf proteins and the Tak1-Tab2 kinase complex to the JNK kinase kinases Hemipterous and Mkk4 and the JNK Basket. The Ste20-family kinase Misshapen acts upstream in the JNK cascade. JNK activation by Eiger induces apoptosis and cell elimination in development and tumour suppression, and also contributes to tissue growth, regeneration, pain sensitization and immune responses.
