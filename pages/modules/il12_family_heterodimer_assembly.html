IL-12 family heterodimer assembly module

Assembly of the IL-12 family cytokines from shared subunits, which is the step that decides which cytokine a cell releases. Four cytokines are built from five chains: IL12B (p40) pairs with IL12A (p35) to make IL-12 or with IL23A (p19) to make IL-23, while EBI3 pairs with IL27 (p28) to make IL-27 or with IL12A to make IL-35. No subunit is active alone, and an unpaired subunit does not leave the cell: calnexin holds EBI3 in the endoplasmic reticulum until a partner arrives, and co-expression of the partner is what permits secretion. So two cells transcribing the same chain can release different cytokines, or none, depending on what else they transcribe. Receptor engagement and the signalling that follows are separate modules.

Built for projects/PRODUCTION_TERMS.md, where the curated classification marks the IL-12, IL-23, IL-27, IL-35 and IL-30 production terms (5 production, 12 regulation) as resting on dedicated machinery: an obligate assembly step. This is the clearest case in that project of a "production" term naming something real that is nevertheless not transcription. GO already models the products, as GO:0043514, GO:0070743, GO:0070744 and GO:0070745, so unlike the TGF-beta and interferon modules this one needs no new term. Boundary: starts with the separately transcribed subunits and ends with a secreted heterodimer, or with an unpaired subunit that is retained or released as an antagonist. Receptor binding and the JAK-STAT signalling that follows belong to modules/jak_stat_signaling.yaml and the cytokine-specific receptor modules. Transcription of each subunit gene is upstream and is not modelled here; that is what the production terms name. Calnexin is given no molecular function on purpose. Its role here is to hold an unassembled subunit, and GO's generic terms for that were obsoleted (GO:0051082 unfolded protein binding, GO:0003754 chaperone activity) without a replacement that fits a holdase; see projects/UNFOLDED_PROTEIN_BINDING.md. An omitted function says "not established", which is accurate, where GO:0051787 misfolded protein binding would say something false, because an unpaired subunit is unassembled rather than misfolded. No cached GO-CAM has an activity on subunit assembly: the cached IL-12 and IL-23 models all start at the receptor. So there are no pins.

Competitive pairing of shared and partner subunitsRegulatory Stepsubunit_pairing

Each shared chain can take either of two partners, and each pairing yields a different cytokine with different biology. The alternatives are genuinely exclusive for a given molecule of the shared chain, which is what makes this a choice rather than an elaboration.
