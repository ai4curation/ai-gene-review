Human Polycomb repressive complexes — PRC2 (EZH1 or EZH2 with EED, SUZ12 and RBBP4/7, in PRC2.1 and PRC2.2 forms) trimethylating histone H3 lysine 27, and canonical (CBX-PHC-PCGF2/4) and variant (PCGF1/3/5/6 with RYBP or YAF2) PRC1 monoubiquitylating histone H2A lysine 119 through the RING1A/RING1B E3 ligase

Polycomb group proteins maintain the repressed state of developmental regulators through two classes of chromatin-modifying complexes that build facultative heterochromatin. Polycomb repressive complex 2 (PRC2) mono-, di- and trimethylates histone H3 lysine 27 (H3K27) using S-adenosyl-L-methionine. Its catalytic subunit is EZH2 or its paralogue EZH1, which is active only in the core complex with EED and SUZ12; the histone-binding WD40 proteins RBBP4 and RBBP7 complete the core. EED binds pre-existing H3K27me3 and allosterically activates the complex, so that the mark propagates. Accessory subunits define two mutually exclusive forms: PRC2.1 carries one Polycomb-like protein (PHF1, MTF2 or PHF19), whose Tudor domains bind H3K36me3, and PRC2.2 carries JARID2 and AEBP2, which contact H2AK119-ubiquitylated nucleosomes and stimulate PRC2 on them. Polycomb repressive complex 1 (PRC1) monoubiquitylates histone H2A lysine 119 (H2AK119ub1). Its E3 ligase is a heterodimer of RING1B (RNF2) or RING1A (RING1) with one of six PCGF proteins. Canonical PRC1 pairs RING1A/B with PCGF2 or PCGF4 (BMI1) and carries a CBX chromodomain protein, which binds H3K27me3 and so recruits canonical PRC1 to PRC2-modified chromatin, and a PHC protein; it has low intrinsic ligase activity and compacts chromatin. Variant PRC1 complexes pair RING1A/B with PCGF1, PCGF3, PCGF5 or PCGF6 and with RYBP or YAF2, which excludes the CBX and PHC subunits and stimulates ligase activity; they deposit most H2AK119ub1 and are recruited independently of H3K27me3, for example to unmethylated CpG islands by KDM2B in PRC1.1. H2AK119ub1 in turn promotes PRC2.2 binding, so the two systems reinforce each other.

Modelling decisions. (1) Catalytic annotons are the complexes, with the catalytic subunit as a unit carrying its own experimental function: EZH2 and EZH1 (histone H3K27 trimethyltransferase activity, IDA) in PRC2; RNF2 (ubiquitin protein ligase activity, IDA/IMP) and RING1 (same term, IBA only) in PRC1. The EZH proteins are inactive outside PRC2 and RING1A/B needs a PCGF partner, so the complex carries the histone-specific function. The complex-level PRC1 function, histone H2AK119 ubiquitin ligase activity, is curator-authored from PMID:15386022 (human GOA has it on RNF2 only by ISS/IEA); it is the only function in the module not taken from an experimental human GOA row or a gene review core function, apart from the RING1 IBA row. (2) Paralogous alternatives (EZH1 or EZH2, RBBP4 or RBBP7, RING1A or RING1B, the PCGF, CBX and PHC families, RYBP or YAF2) are listed as units with roles stating the alternative, as in mediator_complex, rather than as variant sets, because mixed occupancy is possible and the combinations present in cells are not all established. (3) The PRC2.1/PRC2.2 split is a variant set with EXACTLY_ONE selection because the two accessory modules compete for the same SUZ12 surface and define mutually exclusive subtypes (PMID:29628311, PMID:29348366). EPOP and PALI1/PALI2 (PRC2.1 accessory proteins; PALI1 is encoded by the LCOR locus) are not modelled: neither is on the RGD list and neither has experimental human molecular function rows; they would be added as further PRC2.1 units. (4) Canonical and variant PRC1 are separate parts rather than a variant set, because both coexist in the same cell, have different recruitment logic and different connections (canonical PRC1 reads PRC2's product; variant PRC1 provides PRC2.2's input). The six PCGF-defined groups (PRC1.1 to PRC1.6, PMID:22325352) are recorded in unit roles; PCGF2 and PCGF4 can also form RYBP-containing variant complexes, which is not modelled separately. (5) Reader subunits are kept only inside the complex that recruits them: CBX8 (H3K27me3 reader, IDA) in canonical PRC1, PHF1 and PHF19 (H3K36me3 reader, IDA) in PRC2.1, KDM2B (unmethylated CpG binding, IDA) in PRC1.1. EED's H3K27me3-binding allosteric activation (PMID:19767730) is recorded in prose because human GOA records EED only as an enzyme activator. CBX2, CBX4, CBX6 and CBX7 have no experimental H3K27me3 reader row and are given none, although they share the chromodomain (PMID:21047797). (6) Connections: PRC2 PROVIDES_INPUT_FOR canonical PRC1 (H3K27me3 read by the CBX chromodomain; PMID:12351676, PMID:22325148) and variant PRC1 PROVIDES_INPUT_FOR PRC2 (H2AK119ub1 bound by JARID2 and AEBP2 in PRC2.2; PMID:33479123). The first edge applies to canonical PRC1 only, since most PRC1 does not colocalise with H3K27me3 (PMID:22325352). (9) Accessory subunits of PRC1.3/1.5 (AUTS2, casein kinase 2 subunits, FBRS) and PRC1.6 (E2F6, MGA, MAX, L3MBTL2, WDR5) are not modelled: none is on the RGD list, AUTS2- PRC1 activates rather than represses transcription (AUTS2 gene review), and the PRC1.6 DNA-binding subunits are targeting context; SCMH1/SCML2 (SCM homologues) are likewise omitted.
