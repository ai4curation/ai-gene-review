Clathrin and heterotetrameric adaptor protein coats in Drosophila melanogaster

Clathrin-coated vesicles carry cargo from the plasma membrane and the trans-Golgi network into the endosomal system. Clathrin heavy and light chains assemble into three-legged triskelia that polymerize into a lattice over the budding membrane. Heterotetrameric adaptor protein (AP) complexes, each made of two large subunits, a medium (mu) and a small (sigma) subunit, select cargo and link the membrane to the coat. AP-2 acts in endocytosis at the plasma membrane, including synaptic vesicle recycling; AP-1 acts at the trans-Golgi network and endosomes. AP-3 sorts cargo from endosomes towards lysosome-related organelles; in Drosophila the AP-3 subunits are encoded by the classic eye-colour genes garnet (g), ruby (rb), carmine (cm) and orange (or), whose mutants are defective in pigment granule biogenesis.
