Bacterial type V secretion

A species-neutral module for delivery of a passenger protein to the surface of a diderm bacterium by the type V route. The precursor crosses the inner membrane through Sec and is held in a translocation-competent, unfolded state in the periplasm by holdase chaperones. A beta-barrel translocator, which is either a domain of the passenger's own polypeptide or a separate partner protein, is installed in the outer membrane by the BAM complex. The passenger then crosses the outer membrane through that barrel and folds on the far side. No ATP or ion gradient is available at the outer membrane, so vectorial folding of the emerging passenger supplies the directionality. Sec translocation itself, the biological activity of the displayed passenger, and the general biogenesis of outer-membrane proteins unrelated to secretion lie outside this module.

Reusable boundary: the module begins with a Sec-delivered precursor in the periplasm and ends with the passenger folded on the outer face of the outer membrane. Three things are deliberately excluded. Sec translocation is a shared upstream route, as in the sibling type II module. The passenger's own biological activity is excluded because the same architecture carries proteases, adhesins, lipases, and toxin-delivery proteins, so admitting passenger chemistry would make the module a list of unrelated functions rather than a description of a secretion mechanism. General outer-membrane protein biogenesis is excluded even though BAM is shared with it: BAM is admitted only for the specific step of installing the type V translocator. The periplasmic holdases are included rather than treated as upstream because the precursor's unfolded state is a requirement of the transport step itself, not merely of its delivery. They are nonetheless not type V specific, and the module asserts no exclusivity for them.

Part 1: Keep the Sec-delivered precursor soluble and unfolded during periplasmic transit
Periplasmic maintenance of the type V precursorBiological Processperiplasmic_precursor_maintenance

The periplasm contains no ATP, so the precursor cannot be actively unfolded once it has aggregated. Holdase chaperones bind it as it leaves Sec and deliver it to BAM in a state competent for barrel assembly. These chaperones serve outer-membrane protein biogenesis generally; their role here is the type V specific consequence of that activity.

Part 3: Translocate the passenger across the outer membrane and fold it outside
Type V passenger translocation and surface displayTransport Steppassenger_translocation

The passenger crosses the outer membrane through the translocator barrel and folds on the outside. Architectures differ in whether the barrel and the passenger are one polypeptide or two, and in the order of the two elements within a single polypeptide. That ordering determines which molecular function term applies.

Variant set: Type V architecture by arrangement of the translocator barrel relative to its passenger (Exactly One)

The subtype letters are a description of architecture, not of phylogeny: all the barrels belong to broadly related outer-membrane protein folds, and it is the passenger-barrel arrangement that changes.

Classical monomeric autotransporter (type Va)Transport Stepclassical_autotransporter

One polypeptide with an N-terminal passenger and a C-terminal twelve-stranded barrel. This is the arrangement GO:0015474 describes. Some passengers stay attached to the surface while others are released by cleavage inside the barrel.

The variant is retained because the architecture is distinct, but its grounding is weaker than the other four; see the knowledge gaps. The PANTHER family is asserted at family rather than subfamily level on purpose, against the precision advisory. PANTHER places this protein in a family named for its eukaryotic members, which it joins through the shared patatin-like domain, and the corresponding subfamily is named for a specific animal esterase. Descending to that subfamily would name a protein this exemplar is not, so the family level is the honest grounding here.
