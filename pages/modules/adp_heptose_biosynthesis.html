ADP-heptose precursor biosynthesis

A bacterial nucleotide-sugar pathway that converts D-sedoheptulose 7-phosphate to ADP-D-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose through GmhA, the kinase activity of HldE, GmhB, and the adenylyltransferase activity of HldE. In lineages that use ADP-L-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose, HldD performs a subsequent C-6 epimerization. Transfer of the activated heptose into the LPS core by heptosyltransferases is downstream of this module.
