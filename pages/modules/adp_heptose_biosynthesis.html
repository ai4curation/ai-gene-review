ADP-heptose precursor biosynthesis

ADP-manno-heptose precursor synthesis converts sedoheptulose 7-phosphate into ADP-D-glycero-beta-D-manno-heptose through isomerization, C-1 phosphorylation, C-7 dephosphorylation and adenylylation. A further C-6 epimerization supplies the L,D stereoisomer where required. The same reaction sequence is implemented by separate, bifunctional or trifunctional enzymes and supplies Gram-negative lipopolysaccharide and selected Gram-positive secondary metabolites. An archaeal enzyme set also produces ADP-heptose in an engineered archaeal host; native biological uses outside bacteria remain unresolved.

NDP-β-d-manno-heptoses are small molecule agonists sensed by the vertebrates to discriminate organisms of different kingdoms.
This author commentary reviews cross-kingdom heptose enzymes and unresolved physiological roles. Its shorthand reference to M. maripaludis S2 is read together with the primary engineered-host experiment rather than as evidence of an endogenous pathway.

Bacteria and Archaea are non-universal envelopes for complete demonstrated enzyme sets. Functional individual enzymes also occur in viruses and eukaryotes, but their partial complements do not establish a complete native route. The archaeal experiment expressed DSM2067 enzymes in HBE-negative Methanococcus maripaludis S2; it is not evidence of endogenous S2 production. The core endpoint is ADP-D,D-manno-heptose; HldD/SepC epimerization is optional only at this generic module boundary and is required in realizations whose downstream product uses the L,D donor. The GDP-alpha-heptose route, HygP-dependent ADP-altro-heptose synthesis, glycan transfer reactions and host ALPK1 signaling are outside this module. The kinase adds phosphate at C-1 despite its substrate-based name; the adenylyltransferase transfers AMP, not ADP. Protein fusions do not collapse nonadjacent reactions. Family labels dominated by HldE or DiaA do not establish all activities on every member: the explicit required function and reviewed representative provide the biological restriction. No unverified PAINT ancestral placement is asserted. The ADP route does not imply ATP-exclusive nucleotidyltransferase specificity: some enzymes also use CTP or UTP. The listed fusion architectures are examples; HldF isomerase/nucleotidyltransferase and HldG phosphatase/nucleotidyltransferase fusions are also reported. Archaeal HldD activity and L,D-heptose production were not demonstrated by the four-gene reconstruction; this does not establish epimerase absence. Archaeal representatives are the DSM2067 locus proteins preserved in dated UniSave records. The kinase has an exact match to the published synthetic construct; the other three are family/domain-and-context assignments, not asserted exact experimental constructs.
