Camalexin biosynthesis (tryptophan-derived indole phytoalexin)

Camalexin (3-thiazol-2'-yl-indole) is the main phytoalexin of Arabidopsis thaliana and is made from tryptophan after pathogen attack. The ER-anchored cytochrome P450s CYP79B2 and CYP79B3 convert tryptophan to indole-3-acetaldoxime (IAOx), a branch point shared with indole glucosinolate and auxin biosynthesis. CYP71A13, with a minor contribution from its paralog CYP71A12, commits IAOx to the camalexin branch by dehydrating it to indole-3-acetonitrile (IAN) and forming the reactive indole-3-cyanohydrin intermediate that is conjugated to glutathione. Glutathione transferases, including GSTF6, are implicated in this conjugation. The cytosolic gamma-glutamyl peptidases GGP1 and GGP3 remove the gamma-glutamyl residue of the glutathione conjugate (GS-IAN), and an unidentified peptidase removes glycine to give the cysteine conjugate Cys(IAN). The bifunctional P450 CYP71B15 (PAD3) then closes the thiazoline ring of Cys(IAN) with cyanide release to form dihydrocamalexic acid, and oxidatively decarboxylates it to camalexin. The pathway P450s associate with each other and with P450 reductase in an ER-anchored metabolon that channels intermediates. Pathogen-induced transcription of the biosynthetic genes depends on the MAP kinases MPK3 and MPK6, which phosphorylate the WRKY33 transcription factor; WRKY33 binds and activates the CYP71A13 and PAD3 promoters. These two components regulate the pathway and are not pathway enzymes.

Parts 1-4 are the biosynthetic pathway (GO:0010120); part 5 is kept as an explicit regulatory part with its own concept (GO:1901183) so that the enzyme-versus-regulator split made in the MPK3 and WRKY33 gene reviews is visible: MPK3/MPK6 and WRKY33 regulate the pathway and are not parts of it. This is recorded in prose rather than as a complex part because the complex has no GO term beyond generic catalytic complex and the soluble steps (GST, GGP) are not shown to be in it. CYP79B3, CYP71A13, CYP71A12, GSTF6, GGP3 and GSTU4 have no gene review yet and are grounded by UniProt only. PANTHER. Only GGP1 is in the local PANTHER member index, so the other family descriptors carry no PANTHER id.

Part 3: glutathione conjugation and processing to the cysteine conjugate (GSTF6, GGP1/GGP3)
Glutathione conjugation and processingMetabolic Pathwaygsh_conjugation

Glutathione, not free cysteine, supplies the thiazole sulfur. The activated IAN intermediate is conjugated to glutathione (GS-IAN); the gamma-glutamyl residue is removed in the cytosol by GGP1 (and GGP3), and glycine is then removed by an unidentified peptidase to give Cys(IAN). The extracellular gamma-glutamyl transpeptidases GGT1/GGT2 were proposed for the gamma-glutamyl step but the intermediate-accumulation, localization and enzyme-activity evidence favours GGP1.
