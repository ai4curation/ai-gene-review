Bacterial RuvABC Holliday-junction processing

A reusable bacterial homologous-recombination module in which RuvA recognizes and opens a four-way Holliday junction, RuvB uses ATP to drive branch migration, and RuvC cleaves the migrated junction to produce duplex recombination products. RecG supplies a mechanistically parallel branch-migration route in bacteria that encode it. Presynaptic RecFOR and RecBCD processing, RecA strand exchange, replication restart, and DNA ligation are neighboring modules.

OpenScientist PSEPK RuvABC Holliday-junction-processing synthesis
Species-aware retrieval resolves the contiguous KT2440 ruvCAB locus, the parallel RecG route, the intact RuvC active site, and the spurious PP_0151 resolvase name.

The module is species-neutral: RuvABC and RecG are conserved across bacteria. Escherichia coli K-12 supplies the experimentally characterized exemplar for every role (RuvA, RuvB, RuvC and RecG biochemistry, structures and mutants), and Pseudomonas putida KT2440 (contiguous ruvCAB locus PP_1215-PP_1217 and recG/PP_5310) is kept as a homology-grade second exemplar. Other lineages are not added as representatives because no further gene reviews are needed to orient the roles. RecG is an alternative branch-migration motor, not a RuvABC subunit and not a resolvase. Family groundings follow the family reviews: RuvB is grounded to PANTHER PTHR42848:SF1 (PTHR42848 itself is unnamed, and SF1 is the whole family) and RuvC to PTHR30194:SF3 (the whole family). RuvA is grounded to InterPro IPR000085 rather than to PANTHER PTHR33796, because PTHR33796 classifies only a minority of RuvA proteins and KT2440 RuvA (Q88NJ1) has no PANTHER assignment. RecG is grounded to InterPro IPR004609 rather than to PANTHER PTHR47964, because PTHR47964:SF1 also holds Mfd (transcription-repair coupling factor) by the UniProt/InterPro assignments and no PANTHER subfamily separates RecG from Mfd; the RecG-specific PAINT node is PTN002773745 (DNA helicase activity). The RuvA four-way junction helicase assertion at PAINT node PTN008659601 (PTHR33796) is read as a contribution: RuvA confers junction specificity, RuvB is the ATPase. A RusA-type backup resolvase is lineage-restricted (present in E. coli as a cryptic prophage gene, not identified in the KT2440 candidate set) and is not part of the conserved module. The two InterPro-grounded steps (RuvA, RecG) have no PANTHER or Pfam family review, which module QC reports as an informational notice; their representatives are covered by complete gene reviews.
