Bacterial iad-type aerobic indole-3-acetic acid catabolism

A conserved rhizosphere bacterial pathway module mediating aerobic uptake and catabolism of the plant hormone indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) via the iad gene cluster. The module comprises the MarR-family IAA-sensing transcriptional repressor IadR, the high-affinity periplasmic subcluster B-III ABC transporter solute-binding protein IadK2 (paired with its non-IAA-binding paralog IadK3), the Class IA NADH:ferredoxin oxidoreductase component IadC, the heterohexameric Rieske non-heme iron aromatic-ring-hydroxylating dioxygenase complex IadD/IadE that catalyzes the initial oxidative conversion of IAA into biologically inactive 2-oxindole-3-acetic acid (oxIAA), and the downstream cytoplasmic enzymes—including the heterodimeric TPP/[4Fe-4S]-dependent 2-oxoacid:ferredoxin oxidoreductase complex IadA/IadB and the putative cyclic-amide amidohydrolase / isatin hydrolase IadI (KynB2)—that participate in processing oxidized indole intermediates through dioxindole-3-acetic acid, dioxindole, isatin, and isatinic acid to anthranilic acid, thereby buffering rhizosphere auxin levels and preventing root growth inhibition.

Diverse MarR bacterial regulators of auxin catabolism in the plant microbiome.
Demonstrates that co-expression of iadC with iadD and iadE is required to revert Arthrobacter-dependent root growth inhibition in the rhizosphere, characterizes the IAA-responsive MarR-family repressor IadR binding upstream of iadA, identifies iotA/iadA and iorB/iadB as exclusive markers of iad-like operons, and shows by 13C6-IAA metabolomics that the iad pathway degrades IAA via dioxindole-3-acetic acid, isatin, and isatinic acid to anthranilic acid.

indoleacetic acid catabolic processGO:0042437

Grounded on the Variovorax paradoxus strain S110 (NCBITaxon:543728) exemplars C5CSP2 (IadR, Vapar_1489), C5CSP5 (IadC, Vapar_1492), C5CSP6 (IadD, Vapar_1493), and C5CSP7 (IadE, Vapar_1494) together with the syntenic Variovorax paradoxus strain TBEA6 (NCBITaxon:34073, Proteomes:UP000035170) iad operon proteins A0A0H2MF78 (IadK2, VPARA_32110), A0A0H2LZ84 (IadK3, VPARA_32100), A0A0H2MF87 (IadA, VPARA_32210), A0A0H2LZ92 (IadB, VPARA_32200), and A0A0H2M433 (IadI/KynB2, VPARA_32130), with experimental biochemical, structural, genetic, and metabolomic characterization performed in the orthologous V. paradoxus strain CL14 (PMID:32999461, PMID:36266335, PMID:37459330). The co-encoded paralogous B-III solute-binding protein IadK3 (A0A0H2LZ84) lacks IAA-binding activity due to pocket substitutions (Val100 and Leu168, corresponding to S104V and W172L of IadK2) and weakly associates with 2-oxindole-3-acetic acid (oxIAA). The inner-membrane ABC transporter permease/ATPase subunits (IadG, IadH, IadJ) and CoA-transferase/acyl-CoA subunit (IadF) are not yet curated as separate module nodes.
