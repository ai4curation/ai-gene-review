Phytochrome control of photomorphogenesis (phyA/phyB - PIF - COP1/SPA - HY5)

Red and far-red light control seedling de-etiolation and growth in Arabidopsis thaliana through the phytochrome photoreceptors. In darkness two repressor systems keep seedlings etiolated: the PIF bHLH transcription factors (PIF1, PIF3, PIF4 and relatives) promote skotomorphogenic growth and repress chlorophyll biosynthesis and germination, and the nuclear CUL4-DDB1-COP1-SPA E3 ubiquitin ligase targets photomorphogenesis-promoting transcription factors such as HY5 for proteasomal degradation. Light converts phytochromes to the active Pfr form, phyB mainly under red light and phyA under far-red light. Pfr phytochromes enter the nucleus and form photobodies, where they act on both repressor systems. (1) They bind PIFs through APB and APA motifs. This triggers rapid PIF phosphorylation, with SPA1 kinase activity contributing in a phyB-PIF1-SPA1 complex, followed by ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation; PIF1 is turned over through the CUL4-COP1-SPA ligase. (2) They bind SPA proteins and disrupt the COP1-SPA interaction, which inactivates the ligase. HY5 then accumulates and, with the loss of PIF activity, drives photomorphogenic gene expression: hypocotyl growth inhibition, cotyledon expansion and greening. In darkness PIF1 also enhances COP1 E3 activity, so the two repressor systems act synergistically. Blue-light cryptochromes (CRY1) inhibit the same COP1-SPA node and are included only as a converging input. UVR8, PIF-independent phyB outputs, thermomorphogenesis (PIF4-temperature), photoperiodic flowering (COP1-SPA-CO) and hormone crosstalk are outside the module boundary.

Annoton functions follow each gene review's core_functions. SPA1 has two annotons because it has two separable roles: COP1 activator in the ligase node and PIF1 kinase in the PIF node. HY5 phosphorylation by SPA is not modelled as a separate connection. CRY1 is included as a converging blue-light input on COP1-SPA and is context only. Part 1: phytochrome photoperception
Phytochrome photoperception and nuclear photobody formationRegulatory Stepphytochrome_photoperception

Part 2: PIF growth-promoting transcription factors and their light-induced removal
PIF repressors of photomorphogenesis (degraded in response to phytochrome)Regulatory Steppif_repressors

PIF bHLH factors bind G-box elements and repress photomorphogenesis (and, for PIF1, germination and chlorophyll biosynthesis) in darkness. Binding of Pfr phytochrome triggers rapid PIF phosphorylation, ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation. For PIF1, SPA1 kinase activity in a phyB-PIF1-SPA1 complex and the CUL4-COP1-SPA ligase are required.

Part 3: COP1-SPA E3 ubiquitin ligase
CUL4-DDB1-COP1-SPA E3 ubiquitin ligaseRegulatory Stepcop1_spa_ligase

COP1 is a RING E3 ubiquitin ligase. Bound through its coiled-coil to SPA proteins and associated with CUL4-DDB1, it ubiquitinates HY5 and other photomorphogenesis-promoting factors for proteasomal degradation in darkness. SPA1 stimulates COP1 E3 activity. Light-activated phytochromes and cryptochromes bind SPA proteins and disrupt the COP1-SPA interaction; light also triggers COP1 nuclear exclusion.
