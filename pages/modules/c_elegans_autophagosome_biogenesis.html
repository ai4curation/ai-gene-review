Autophagosome biogenesis and maturation in Caenorhabditis elegans

Macroautophagy in Caenorhabditis elegans follows the conserved eukaryotic sequence of nucleation, expansion, cargo capture and lysosomal delivery, but with metazoan-specific features. A class III phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K) complex built around the Beclin 1 ortholog BEC-1 and the lipid kinase VPS-34 (LET-512) produces phosphatidylinositol 3-phosphate (PI3P) on early autophagic membranes. EPG-8, a divergent Atg14 homolog that binds BEC-1, directs this complex to autophagy. The WD40 PROPPIN ATG-18 (WIPI1/2 ortholog) reads PI3P through its FRRG motif and acts in autophagosome formation, distinct from its paralog EPG-6 (WIPI4), which works with ATG-2. The worm has two Atg8 family proteins with different roles. The GABARAP-type LGG-1 is cleaved by ATG-4 and conjugated to phosphatidylethanolamine. Cleavage is needed to start autophagosome formation, and the lipidated form helps recognise cargo. LGG-1 acts upstream of the LC3-type LGG-2, which is recruited to LGG-1-positive autophagosomes and drives their maturation and tethering to lysosomes through the HOPS subunit VPS-39. Fusion with late endosomes and lysosomes needs EPG-5, a metazoan-specific RAB-7 effector that also binds LGG-1 and SNARE complexes, so that autophagosomes fuse with the correct partner and become degradative autolysosomes. Selective cargo, such as aggregates of the p62 homolog SQST-1, is linked to LGG-1-labelled membranes by cargo receptors and the scaffold EPG-7.

Boundary. This is a concrete C. elegans module for bulk and selective macroautophagy from nucleation of the autophagic membrane to the autolysosome. It has five parts in pathway order: PI3K complex nucleation (BEC-1, VPS-34), the PI3P effector step (ATG-18), Atg8-family membrane anchoring in biogenesis (LGG-1), cargo recruitment (SQST-1, optional), and maturation and lysosome fusion (LGG-2, EPG-5). Upstream regulation by TOR, transcriptional induction by HLH-30/TFEB and PHA-4, and lysosome-derived lipid signals are outside the boundary. HLH-30 and the LIPL-4 lipid branch are modelled in MODULE:c_elegans_lysosomal_lipid_signaling. Mitochondrion- specific receptors (DCT-1, ALLO-1 and others) are modelled in MODULE:c_elegans_mitophagy. Relation to MODULE:phagophore_assembly_site. The yeast module covers the conserved core: the Atg1 complex condensate, Atg9 vesicles, PI3K complex I, the Atg2-Atg18 lipid-transfer bridge and the Atg8 conjugation system. This module does not repeat that machinery. It keeps only the worm members that have gene reviews and the metazoan-specific steps that the yeast module cannot show: the omegasome/ER origin of autophagosomes, the split of Atg8 into GABARAP-type LGG-1 and LC3-type LGG-2 with sequential roles, and EPG-5 as a RAB-7 effector at fusion. Worm components that are present but not modelled as annotons here because they lack gene reviews in this repository: UNC-51/Atg1, ATG-13, EPG-1, EPG-9, ATG-9, EPG-8/Atg14, VPS-15, EPG-6/WIPI4, ATG-2, ATG-4.1/ATG-4.2, ATG-7, ATG-3, ATG-5, ATG-16.1/16.2, EPG-3/VMP1, EPG-4/EI24, VPS-39 and EPG-7. VPS-34 (Q9TXI7, Swiss-Prot) is included without a gene review because it is the catalytic subunit of the nucleation step. BEC-1 also acts in the PI3KC3-C2 complex in endosome-to-Golgi retrograde transport and phagosome maturation (PMID:21183797). ATG-18, EPG-5 and LGG-1 also act in phagocytes to degrade apoptotic corpses (PMID:22451698). These roles are noted in the gene reviews and are outside this module. Lipidation nuance. A 2023 study (PMID:37395461) shows that LGG-1 cleavage, not PE conjugation, is essential for autophagosome initiation and for development, while the lipidated form helps recognise cargo and degrade autophagosomes. The LGG-1 annoton keeps phosphatidylethanolamine binding as its MF to match the lgg-1 gene review core function, and this caveat is recorded in the annoton description.

Part 1: autophagic membrane nucleation by the class III PI3K complex
BEC-1/VPS-34 class III PI3K nucleationProtein Complexpi3k_nucleation

The class III PI3K complex I (VPS-34, VPS-15, BEC-1 and the Atg14 homolog EPG-8) makes PI3P on ER-associated omegasomes and phagophores. BEC-1 is the scaffold subunit and is needed for VPS-34 function. EPG-8 binds BEC-1 directly and directs the complex to autophagy. A second complex containing BEC-1 (PI3KC3-C2) acts in endocytic trafficking and is outside this module.

Part 5: autophagosome maturation and fusion with late endosomes/lysosomes
LGG-2 and EPG-5 in autophagosome maturation and lysosome fusionBiological Processmaturation_fusion

LGG-2 (LC3) is recruited to LGG-1-positive autophagosomes, controls their maturation and helps tether them to lysosomes by binding the HOPS subunit VPS-39. EPG-5 is a metazoan-specific RAB-7 effector. It binds RAB-7 and VAMP7/8 on late endosomes and lysosomes, and LGG-1 and STX17-SNAP29 on autophagosomes. It stabilises the correct trans-SNARE complex so that autophagosomes fuse with the right partner. Without EPG-5, non-degradative hybrid vesicles build up.
