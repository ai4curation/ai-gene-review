Serotonin and melatonin biosynthesis (Trp -> 5-HTP -> serotonin -> N-acetylserotonin -> melatonin)

Serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine) and the pineal hormone melatonin are the indoleamine products of a four-step pathway from the essential amino acid L-tryptophan. Tryptophan hydroxylase - the neuronal isoform TPH2 in brainstem raphe serotonergic neurons, the peripheral isoform TPH1 in gut enterochromaffin cells, pineal gland and skin - catalyses the rate-limiting hydroxylation of L-tryptophan to 5-hydroxy-L-tryptophan (5-HTP) using tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4), O2 and a non-heme Fe(II) centre. The shared aromatic-L-amino-acid decarboxylase (DDC/AADC, curated with the catecholamine pathway) decarboxylates 5-HTP to serotonin. In the pineal gland (and retina) serotonin is then converted to melatonin in two further steps that are gated by the circadian clock: serotonin N-acetyltransferase (AANAT, the "timezyme") N-acetylates serotonin to N-acetylserotonin using acetyl-CoA - the sharply night-elevated, cAMP/14-3-3-regulated rate-controlling step - and acetylserotonin O-methyltransferase (ASMT/HIOMT) O-methylates N-acetylserotonin to melatonin using S-adenosyl-L-methionine. Serotonin acts as a central and enteric neurotransmitter and platelet mediator, while melatonin signals darkness/photoperiod. Variants across the pathway are studied in mood, autism- spectrum and sleep phenotypes.

Serotonin and melatonin biosynthesis (GO:0042427 -> GO:0030187), grounded to five completed human gene reviews (four new + the shared DDC, already merged). Route: TPH2 (Q8IWU9, neuronal) / TPH1 (P17752, peripheral/pineal) both PTHR11473 (same family as TH/PAH), GO:0004510, BH4/Fe(II), Trp -> 5-HTP (rate- limiting); DDC (P20711 PTHR11999, GO:0036467, PLP) 5-HTP -> serotonin (shared with the catecholamine module's catecholamine_biosynthesis); then the pineal melatonin steps: AANAT (Q16613 PTHR10908, GO:0004059, acetyl-CoA) serotonin -> N-acetylserotonin (the circadian "timezyme", cAMP/14-3-3-gated); ASMT/HIOMT (P46597, PTHR11746 per PANTHER 19.0; GO:0017096, SAM) N-acetylserotonin -> melatonin. All cytosolic. Precursor tryptophan and BH4 come from separate pathways; DDC and TPH share the aromatic-amino-acid-hydroxylase/decarboxylase logic with the catecholamine pathway (TPH/TH same PANTHER family; DDC the common decarboxylase). Curation: TPH1 peripheral so neuron-projection IBA over-annotated (belongs to TPH2); AANAT's GO:0006474 N-terminal- protein-acetylation IDA is a small-molecule-vs-protein misclassification (over-annotated); ASMT's S-methyltransferase TAS is wrong branch (O-, not S-methylation). GO term ids/labels verified against the local go.db; module passes structural + term-label validation.
