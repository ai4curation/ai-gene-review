Mammalian ISWI chromatin remodeling complexes — SMARCA5 (SNF2H) or SMARCA1 (SNF2L) ATPase paired with a BAZ/BPTF-family regulatory subunit: ACF (BAZ1A), CHRAC (BAZ1A, CHRAC1, POLE3), WICH (BAZ1B), NURF (BPTF, RBBP4/7), RSF (RSF1), NoRC (BAZ2A) and CERF (CECR2)

ISWI (imitation switch) remodelers are ATP-dependent chromatin remodelers of the SNF2 family that slide histone octamers along DNA. Most of their complexes assemble and space nucleosomes into regular arrays. Humans have two ISWI ATPases, SMARCA5 (SNF2H), which is ubiquitous, and SMARCA1 (SNF2L), which is enriched in the nervous system. Each ATPase pairs with one large regulatory subunit, and the regulatory subunit defines the complex and where it acts. BAZ1A (ACF1) forms ACF, and with the histone-fold heterodimer CHRAC1-POLE3 it forms CHRAC; both help replicate condensed heterochromatin. BAZ1B (WSTF) forms WICH, which acts at replication forks and at RNA polymerase I and III genes. BPTF forms NURF with RBBP4 or RBBP7; BPTF reads H3K4me3 and acetylated H4 and recruits NURF to active promoters. RSF1 forms RSF, which deposits histones and spaces nucleosomes. BAZ2A (TIP5) forms the nucleolar NoRC, which silences rRNA genes. CECR2 forms CERF, which acts in neural development. GO and the first purifications assign one ATPase to each complex, but both ATPases co-purify with every regulatory subunit, so each complex can exist in a SNF2H and a SNF2L form.

INO80, SRCAP, TIP60/p400 and CHD/NuRD genes on the RGD list are left for other modules. RGD genes in scope (7): SMARCA5 SMARCA1 BAZ1A BAZ1B BPTF CHRAC1 POLE3, all modelled. RGD draws ACF, CHRAC, WICH and NURF. Added beyond the RGD list: RBBP4 and RBBP7 as the histone-binding NURF subunits (both are on the RGD list, presumably for NuRD, and are used here only in their NURF position), and three further complexes with their regulatory subunits RSF1 (RSF), BAZ2A (NoRC) and CECR2 (CERF). These three are included because each has a GO complex term whose definition names its human subunits, a human purification with remodeling activity cached here (PMID:9836642 and PMID:12972596 for RSF, PMID:11532953 for NoRC, PMID:15640247 for CERF), experimental GOA rows on its subunits, and co-purification of the regulatory subunit with endogenous ISWI ATPases (PMID:28801535). Excluded: BAZ2B, the seventh regulatory subunit identified in PMID:28801535 (a BAZ2B-SMARCA5 complex is so far supported only by that proteomic study and has no GO term, so it is noted rather than modelled); DNA polymerase epsilon (POLE3 also belongs to it with POLE4, and POLE4 is not a CHRAC subunit); the larger B-WICH assembly (WICH with nuclear myosin 1, DEK, MYBBP1A, DDX21 and others; PMID:16603771), recorded as WICH context; WINAC (BAZ1B with SWI/SNF subunits), which is neither an ISWI complex nor modelled in the SWI/SNF module; and TTF-I, pRNA, HDAC1, SIN3 and DNMTs that act with NoRC. Structure. Following the Drosophila analogue modules/dmel_iswi_complexes (NURF, NORC, ACF, CHRAC, TORC, RSF), each complex is a variant of one REALIZER variant set (selection ONE_OR_MORE) with a protein-complex annoton listing its subunits. The ATPase position is a FAMILY unit in every complex, with the ATPase named by the GO definition listed first (SMARCA5 for ACF, CHRAC, WICH, RSF and NoRC; SMARCA1 for NURF and CERF) and the other paralogue second, because endogenous SMARCA1 and SMARCA5 each co-purify with every regulatory subunit (PMID:28801535) and GOA has NURF-complex IDA rows for both ATPases (PMID:20850016). RBBP4/RBBP7 is a FAMILY unit; the cached quote names RBBP4, and RBBP7 rests on its NURF IPI row from PMID:14609955 (abstract-only cache). Drosophila TORC has no mammalian counterpart and is not modelled. The fly module is the evolutionary counterpart and is not referenced as a module_ref. Molecular functions. Functions come from each protein's experimental GO annotations in QuickGO (November 2026) and, where a gene review exists (BPTF, CHRAC1, BAZ2A, POLE4), from its core functions. The SMARCA5-led ATPase family carries histone octamer slider activity (SMARCA5 IDA, PMID:9836642), the SMARCA1-led family ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler activity (SMARCA1 IDA, PMID:15640247). Complex annotons carry ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler activity, except RSF, which carries nucleosome array spacer activity (its defining reaction, PMID:9836642), and NoRC, which carries histone octamer slider activity (PMID:11532953); all carry chromatin remodeling and chromatin, and NoRC also nucleolus. BAZ1A, BAZ1B, BAZ2A and CECR2 are units without a function.
