Human co-transcriptional pre-mRNA capping — RNGTT triphosphatase and guanylyltransferase, RNMT-RAMAC guanine-N7 methylation, and CMTR1 cap1 2'-O-methylation of RNA polymerase II transcripts

The 5' end of every RNA polymerase II transcript is capped soon after it emerges from the polymerase, while the transcript is only a few tens of nucleotides long. Capping is a chain of four enzymatic steps on the RNA 5' end. The bifunctional capping enzyme RNGTT first removes the gamma phosphate of the 5'-triphosphate end with its N-terminal cysteine-phosphatase domain, then, with its C-terminal nucleotidyltransferase domain, forms a covalent enzyme-GMP intermediate from GTP and transfers GMP to the 5'-diphosphate end, giving an inverted guanosine linked 5'-5' through a triphosphate bridge. The cap guanine is then methylated at N7 by the methyltransferase RNMT, which in vertebrates works with its activating subunit RAMAC (RAM), using S-adenosyl-L-methionine as the methyl donor; this gives the 7-methylguanosine cap (cap0). In the nucleus, CMTR1 then methylates the 2'-O-ribose of the first transcribed nucleotide (cap1). RNGTT, RNMT and CMTR1 are each recruited to the elongating polymerase through the Ser5-phosphorylated C-terminal domain of its largest subunit, which couples capping to promoter escape and early elongation. The finished cap is read by the nuclear cap-binding complex and later by the cytoplasmic translation factor eIF4E.

Derived from the RGD Pathway Ontology diagram PW:0000127 (RNA polymerase II transcription pathway) as item 4 of the Pol II split plan (projects/RGD_PATHWAY_ONTOLOGY/broad_pathway_split_plans.md), with RGD's capping sub-pathway PW:0001578 (5'-end pre-mRNA capping pathway), whose id and label were checked against .cache/rgd_pw/pw.obo and which is cited as an evidence source because the module covers it. The PW:0000127 diagram draws Rngtt as the capping enzyme and describes capping as a triphosphatase-guanylyltransferase polypeptide followed by Rnmt methylation, with the capping enzyme interacting with DSIF and the methyltransferase complex and the nuclear cap-binding complex binding the capped RNA. RGD annotates five human genes to PW:0001578: RNGTT, RNMT, RAMAC, NCBP1 and NCBP2. Three are modelled (RNGTT, RNMT, RAMAC). One gene outside the RGD list is added, CMTR1, for the reasons in (3). No gene review exists in this repository for any member, so molecular functions were taken from each protein's experimental GO annotations in QuickGO (November 2026). One function is curator-authored without an experimental human GOA row on that exact term: RNGTT mRNA 5'-triphosphate monophosphatase activity (see the annoton). UniProt accessions and labels are from UniProt REST (November 2026). Reaction participants were grounded from Rhea (RHEA:67004, RHEA:67012, RHEA:67008, RHEA:67020, the EC master reactions) and each ChEBI label was checked in OLS; the substrates chain, so the product of each step is the substrate of the next. Boundary decisions. (1) RNGTT is one polypeptide with two catalytic domains in metazoa, so it appears as two parts with one annoton each, rather than as a complex. GO:0031533 mRNA capping enzyme complex is not used: its definition is the two-subunit fungal enzyme. (2) RNMT and RAMAC are one complex annoton (GO:0160130) with RNMT as the catalytic unit and RAMAC as an activator unit. RAMAC gets no catalytic function, because RNMT is active alone and RAMAC raises its activity and RNA affinity. The paralog RAMACL has only a phylogenetic capping row and is not modelled. (3) CMTR1 (cap1) is included. The definition of GO:0006370 includes ribose methylation of the first transcribed nucleotide; CMTR1 is nuclear, binds the Pol II CTD, and every human mRNA carries cap1, so cap1 is added to the nascent transcript as part of the same capping process. RGD does not list CMTR1; it is the only gene added beyond RGD. (4) CMTR2 (cap2) is excluded from the parts. It is in both nucleus and cytosol, only part of the mRNA population carries cap2 at steady state, and cap2 is added slowly in the cytosol as mRNAs age (PMID:36725932), so it is not part of co-transcriptional capping. Its GOA row for 7-methylguanosine mRNA capping (IDA, PMID:21310715) is consistent with the GO definition and is not an error; leaving it out is a scope decision. It would fit a later cap-maturation module with the cap-adjacent N6-methyladenosine writer PCIF1, which is likewise excluded here because it modifies only transcripts that start with adenosine and is not needed to form the cap. (5) The nuclear cap-binding complex (NCBP1, NCBP2) is excluded although RGD lists it. Applying the substrate-versus-participant rule: the complex binds the finished m7G cap (its experimental functions are RNA 7-methylguanosine cap binding) and performs none of the triphosphatase, guanylyltransferase or methyltransferase chemistry; it is a reader of the product. Comparator check: the other human cap readers, EIF4E and NCBP3, carry no GO:0006370 annotation in QuickGO, and among cap-binding proteins only NCBP1 carries one (IDA, PMID:12434151), which rests on a crystal structure of the complex with a cap analogue (see the possible upstream error below). CBC's downstream roles in splicing, export and translation are context in modules/mrna_nuclear_export.yaml and elsewhere. (6) The polymerase and its CTD are not modelled here. Recruitment of RNGTT, RNMT and CMTR1 by the Ser5-phosphorylated CTD is recorded in the description and evidence; the Pol II enzyme and the TFIIH CDK7 kinase that phosphorylates Ser5 belong to the sibling module rna_polymerase_ii_core_and_initiation, and DSIF (SUPT4H1, SUPT5H), which binds and stimulates the capping enzyme during promoter-proximal pausing, to rna_polymerase_ii_elongation_and_termination. (7) DXO, drawn on the PW:0000127 diagram with XRN2 in termination, removes incompletely capped and unmethylated caps; it is cap quality control and decay, not cap formation, and is left to the elongation and termination sibling. (8) Regulation of capping by signalling to RNMT-RAMAC and the interferon induction of CMTR1 are out of scope. (9) The snRNA caps made by the same enzymes on Pol II snRNA transcripts, and their later hypermethylation by TGS1, are not modelled; the module concerns pre-mRNA. (10) SAGA and ATAC belong to a later histone-modification tranche and are not involved here. GO-CAM links. No human production GO-CAM in gocams/index.tsv contains RNGTT, RNMT, RAMAC, CMTR1, CMTR2, NCBP1 or NCBP2. The S. pombe model gomodel:66187e4700001573 (RNA capping) models the same three core steps with Pct1, Ceg1 and Pcm1, and its activities are linked to the three matching annotons as orthologous activities. Its other two activities, the Ser5-phosphorylated Rpb1 CTD as an adaptor and the Mcs6 CTD Ser5 kinase, are sibling-module biology and are not linked. The SARS-CoV-2 model gomodel:5e72450500004237 has viral cap N7 and cap1 methyltransferases (nsp14, nsp16) in host evasion; it is viral, not this pathway, and is not linked. PANTHER family ids and PTNs are deliberately not asserted. Possible upstream errors. (a) The RNGTT triphosphatase row GO:0050355 inorganic triphosphate phosphatase activity (IDA, PMID:9473487) names hydrolysis of free triphosphate; the paper measured RNA 5'-triphosphatase activity, which is GO:0140818, a term whose definition cites the same paper. Query to GOA (UniProt). (b) The NCBP1 row GO:0006370 (IDA, PMID:12434151) annotates a cap reader to cap formation from a binding structure; candidate over-annotation, query only. (c) The definition of GO:0006370 (and of its parent GO:0009452) mislabels the ribose methylations, and GO:0004482 lacks the Rhea cross-reference for its reaction; both are recorded in proposed_ontology_changes.
