Bacterial L-cysteine biosynthesis via O-acetyl-L-serine

A reusable two-step bacterial pathway in which serine O-acetyltransferase converts L-serine and acetyl-CoA to O-acetyl-L-serine, followed by a pyridoxal-phosphate-dependent O-acetylserine sulfhydrylase that incorporates sulfide to produce L-cysteine. The terminal reaction may be implemented by a CysK-type or CysM-type enzyme. Sulfide generation by assimilatory sulfate reduction or other sulfur-acquisition pathways supplies an input but is not part of this module.
