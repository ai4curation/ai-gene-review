RISC-loading complexes and miRISC in Drosophila melanogaster

Small RNA duplexes produced by Dicer are loaded into Argonaute proteins by RISC-loading complexes. In Drosophila the miRNA pathway uses Dicer-1 with the dsRNA-binding partner Loquacious (Loqs-PB) and loads miRNAs mainly into AGO1, whereas the siRNA pathway uses Dicer-2 with R2D2, which senses duplex asymmetry and loads siRNAs into AGO2. FlyBase also defines an alternative siRNA loading complex in which Loqs (Loqs-PD isoform) partners Dicer-2 with AGO2. Mature miRNA-loaded AGO1 associates with the GW182 protein Gawky to form the miRNA-induced silencing complex (miRISC), which represses translation and promotes deadenylation and decay of target mRNAs.
