Bacterial aminoacyl-tRNA charging

Production of the twenty canonical aminoacyl-tRNAs used in bacterial protein synthesis, through direct aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases and species-matched indirect amino-acid conversion on tRNA. The reusable program distinguishes class I and class II LysRS, homodimeric and heterotetrameric GlyRS, heteromeric PheRS, direct versus GatCAB-dependent Asn/Gln formation, and the rare bacterial SepRS–SepCysS route to Cys-tRNA. The boundary ends at a correctly aminoacylated tRNA. It excludes ribosomal elongation, initiation-specific formylation, tRNA maturation, selenocysteine/pyrrolysine recoding and off-tRNA amino-acid synthesis. Editing improves fidelity but is not modeled as a universally obligatory independent enzyme for each output.
