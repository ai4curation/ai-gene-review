Core binding factor (Runt/CBFbeta) in Drosophila melanogaster

Core binding factors are heterodimeric transcription factors made of a Runt domain DNA-binding alpha subunit and a non-DNA-binding beta subunit that increases the alpha subunit's DNA-binding affinity. Drosophila has four Runt domain proteins, runt, lozenge (lz), RunxA and RunxB, and two beta subunits, Brother (Bro) and Big brother (Bgb). Runt acts as a pair-rule and sex determination gene and in neurogenesis, while lozenge specifies cell fates in the eye and crystal cells among hemocytes.
