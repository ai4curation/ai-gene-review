Nuclear piRNA pathway complexes (RDC and PICTS) in Drosophila melanogaster

Two nuclear complexes act in the Drosophila germline piRNA pathway. The Rhino-Deadlock-Cutoff (RDC) complex binds dual-strand piRNA clusters, where the HP1 paralog Rhino recognises H3K9me3, and licenses non-canonical transcription of cluster precursors that are processed into piRNAs. The Panoramix-induced co-transcriptional silencing complex (PICTS, also called SFiNX), made of Panoramix, Nxf2, Nxt1 and dynein light chain Ctp, is recruited by piRNA-guided Piwi to nascent transposon transcripts and triggers H3K9 methylation and heterochromatin formation over the target.

The two FlyBase groups act at different steps (piRNA production versus piRNA-guided silencing) and are linked only through piRNAs and Piwi, which are not modelled here; no direct connection is asserted. Ctp (dynein light chain LC8) also has many cytoplasmic roles; here it is the PICTS dimerising subunit. The module concept is the overall nuclear piRNA heterochromatin pathway that both complexes serve. FlyBase groups realized by this module: RDC (FBgg0001774), PICTS (FBgg0001963).
