Human histone deubiquitination — the ATXN7L3-ENY2 deubiquitination modules (SAGA DUBm with USP22 and ATXN7, and the SAGA-independent USP27X and USP51 modules) removing H2BK120ub1, the BAP1-ASXL Polycomb repressive deubiquitinase (PR-DUB) and USP16 removing the PRC1 mark H2AK119ub1, and the further H2A deubiquitinases MYSM1 (2A-DUB) and USP3

Monoubiquitin on histone H2B lysine 120 (H2BK120ub1) and on histone H2A lysine 119 (H2AK119ub1) are the two abundant histone ubiquitin marks of mammalian chromatin, and each is removed by dedicated deubiquitinases. H2BK120ub1, which accompanies transcription, is erased mainly by small deubiquitination modules built around two adaptor proteins, ATXN7L3 and ENY2. In the SAGA coactivator the ubiquitin-specific protease USP22 is the catalytic subunit of the deubiquitination module (DUBm), which ATXN7 anchors to the rest of SAGA; the related proteases USP27X and USP51 form equivalent modules with ATXN7L3 and ENY2 outside SAGA and compete with USP22 for the adaptors. None of the three proteases is active alone. The Polycomb mark H2AK119ub1, deposited by the RING1A/B ligases of PRC1, is removed by the Polycomb repressive deubiquitinase (PR-DUB), in which the ubiquitin C-terminal hydrolase BAP1 is activated by the DEUBAD domain of ASXL1, ASXL2 or ASXL3. PR-DUB is specific for K119 and keeps active genes from PRC1-mediated silencing. USP16 (Ubp-M) is a nucleosome-preferring H2A deubiquitinase that removes H2AK119ub1 before mitosis, a prerequisite for histone H3 serine 10 phosphorylation and chromosome segregation, and that also reverses silencing near DNA breaks. The JAMM metalloprotease MYSM1 (2A-DUB), with PCAF, removes H2A ubiquitin during activation of androgen receptor target genes, and USP3 removes ubiquitin from both H2A and H2B during S phase and the DNA damage response.

No new process term is requested. Boundary and modelling decisions. (1) Parts by mark and machine. Part 1 removes H2BK120ub1, parts 2 to 4 remove H2A ubiquitin. (2) The ATXN7L3-ENY2 modules are one part with three variants (ONE_OR_MORE, since they coexist and compete for the adaptors), each a complex annoton carrying the H2B deubiquitinase activity with the USP as the catalytic unit, because none of the three USPs is active alone (PMID:27132940). ATXN7L3 and ENY2 therefore appear as units in all three variants. Only the USP22 module is SAGA's DUBm; ATXN7 is its SAGA anchor and is absent from the USP27X and USP51 modules. The SAGA HAT module is modelled in histone_acetyltransferase_complexes, and the SAGA structural core, TRRAP and the splicing subunits are named there and not here; this module models the DUBm, not SAGA as a whole. ENY2 is also in TREX-2 (mRNA export), and the cytoplasmic paralogue ATXN7L3B competes for ENY2 (PMID:27601583); these are outside the module. (3) PR-DUB is one complex annoton with BAP1 as catalytic unit and ASXL1, ASXL2 and ASXL3 as alternative activator units, listed as units with their alternatives in the role text, as in mediator_complex and polycomb_repressive_complexes, rather than as a variant set: they bind the same BAP1 surface and are mutually exclusive in one complex, but redundant within cells (PMID:30664650). (4) BAP1-associated factors are not units. FOXK1, FOXK2, HCFC1, OGT, KDM1B, MBD5, MBD6 and YY1 were the optional candidates. In human cells the BAP1-ASXL core (BAP1.com) engages FOXK1/2, HCFC1 and KDM1B transiently; size exclusion shows that their co-elution with BAP1 does not reflect a stable complex, and KDM1B associates only with BAP1-ASXL2 (PMID:30664650). FOXK2 recruits BAP1 to its targets and BAP1 bridges FOXK2 to HCF-1 (PMID:25451922); YY1 and HCF-1 form a ternary complex with BAP1 at specific promoters (PMID:20805357). These are recruitment and substrate relationships (HCFC1 and OGT are also BAP1 substrates), not stable subunits, so they are named here only. ATXN7L3-ENY2 histone H2B deubiquitination modules (SAGA DUBm, USP27X and USP51 modules)Protein Complexh2b_dub_modules

Three ubiquitin-specific proteases with an N-terminal ZnF-UBP domain, USP22, USP27X and USP51, each assemble with the adaptors ATXN7L3 and ENY2 into a deubiquitination module that removes ubiquitin from H2BK120ub1. None is active in isolation, and the three compete for the limiting adaptors, so ATXN7L3 and ENY2 set the total H2B deubiquitinase capacity. Only the USP22 module is incorporated into SAGA, through ATXN7.

Variant set: USP22, USP27X or USP51 with ATXN7L3 and ENY2 by catalytic USP paralogue and incorporation into SAGA (One Or More)
PMID:27132940
USP27X and USP51 are H2Bub1 deubiquitinases that act independently of SAGA and compete with USP22 for ATXN7L3 and ENY2.
These observations led us to discover two H2Bub1 DUBs, USP27X and USP51, which function independently of SAGA and compete with USP22 for ATXN7L3 and ENY2 for activity.

The three modules coexist in the same cells and act on the same mark; depletion of USP22 alone lowers rather than raises global H2Bub1, whereas depletion of the shared adaptors raises it, which is how the SAGA-independent modules were found (PMID:27132940).
