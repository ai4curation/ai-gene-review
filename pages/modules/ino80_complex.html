Human INO80 chromatin remodeling complex — INO80 Snf2 ATPase with the RUVBL1-RUVBL2, ARP5-IES6 and IES2 motor module, the ARP4-ARP8-actin-YY1 HSA module and the metazoan NFRKB-UCHL5 module; INO80/INO80B-E/ACTR5/ACTR8/ACTL6A/ACTB/RUVBL1/RUVBL2/YY1/NFRKB/MCRS1/UCHL5/TFPT

The human INO80 complex is an ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler of the INO80 subfamily of SNF2 ATPases that slides nucleosomes. Its catalytic subunit INO80 is a scaffold whose separate domains assemble three modules. The Snf2-type ATPase domain, with its large insertion enclosed by a RUVBL1-RUVBL2 AAA+ heterohexamer, the ARP5-IES6 (ACTR5-INO80C) pair and IES2 (INO80B), forms the motor module: the ATPase translocates DNA, ARP5-IES6 grips the nucleosome on the opposite face and couples ATP hydrolysis to sliding, and IES2 stimulates the ATPase. The helicase-SANT-associated (HSA) and post-HSA region binds the actin-related proteins ARP4 (ACTL6A) and ARP8 (ACTR8), nuclear actin and the zinc-finger protein YY1; ARP8 binds nucleosomes and YY1 binds Holliday-junction DNA. The INO80 N terminus binds a metazoan-specific module of NFRKB, MCRS1, TFPT, INO80D, INO80E and the deubiquitinase UCHL5, which is held inactive in the complex; this module is not required for nucleosome remodeling. INO80 positions nucleosomes at promoters and acts in transcription regulation, DNA double-strand break repair and replication-fork progression; those roles, and the other complexes that share RUVBL1/2, ACTL6A and actin (SRCAP, TIP60/p400, BAF), are context for this module.

Derived from the RGD Pathway Ontology term PW:0001339 (chromatin remodeling pathway) through the RGD_PATHWAY_ONTOLOGY project (seed in projects/RGD_PATHWAY_ONTOLOGY/data/module_seeds.yaml, tier related_module_only; mapped GO process chromatin remodeling, skos:closeMatch). RGD curates 74 human genes to PW:0001339 without a gene-linked diagram; the definition groups SNF2-family remodelers into four families (SWI/SNF, INO80/SWR1, ISWI, CHD) and the diagram draws separate complex nodes, including INO80, SRCAP and TRRAP/Tip60 for the INO80/SWR1 family. The project models one human module per complex group; this module is the INO80 complex. Its 16 RGD genes (INO80 INO80B INO80C INO80D INO80E ACTR5 ACTR8 ACTL6A ACTB NFRKB MCRS1 UCHL5 TFPT YY1 RUVBL1 RUVBL2) are all modelled. The SRCAP and TIP60/p400 complexes, which share RUVBL1, RUVBL2, ACTL6A and actin, are drafted as separate modules; ACTR6, SRCAP, ZNHIT1, VPS72, YEATS4, EP400, TRRAP, KAT5 and the other TIP60 genes belong there, and the remaining RGD genes to the NuRD (nurd_complex), SWI/SNF and ISWI groups. Structure. The three parts are the three modules that assemble on three domains of INO80 in the human complex (PMID:21303910): (1) the Snf2 ATPase domain with IES2, IES6, RUVBL1/2 and ARP5; (2) the HSA/post-HSA region with ARP4, ARP8 and YY1, to which actin is added from the structural literature (PMID:29643506) and RGD; (3) the N terminus with the metazoan-specific subunits. Placement of the six metazoan-specific subunits in part 3 follows the module definition in PMID:21303910 (all metazoan-specific subunits) and their IDA Ino80 complex rows from that paper; the cached abstract does not name them individually, which is why their annotons carry no subunit-level quote. There are no human paralogue alternatives for any subunit, so no variant sets are used. Boundary decisions. (1) RUVBL1, RUVBL2, ACTL6A and ACTB are shared with SRCAP, TIP60/p400 and (ACTL6A, ACTB) BAF complexes, and RUVBL1/2 with R2TP; only their INO80 role is modelled. (2) YY1 is a subunit, not context, because it is part of the HSA module and required with the conserved subunits for remodeling by the core complex (PMID:21303910); its role as a sequence-specific transcription factor that targets INO80 is context, so the four-way junction DNA binding row from the YY1-INO80 paper (PMID:18026119) is used as its function rather than its DNA-binding transcription factor activity. (3) ACTB is included because RGD lists it and actin is an HSA-module subunit, but human GOA has no Ino80 complex row for it and the actin isoform in human INO80 is unassigned (ACTG1 is equally possible). (4) UCHL5 is modelled with its deubiquitinase activity (IDA), with the role description stating that it is latent within INO80; its proteasomal K48-linked chain trimming is outside the module. The module validator warns that the UCHL5 gene review core function (K48-linked deubiquitinase activity, on the proteasome) is more specific; the parent term is kept because linkage specificity has not been shown for INO80-bound UCHL5. (5) Yeast-specific subunits (the Nhp10 module and Ies1, Ies3, Ies4, Ies5) have no human orthologues in the complex. (6) INO80 roles in transcription regulation (promoter nucleosome positioning, Polycomb and pluripotency gene regulation), homologous recombination, DNA end resection, UV damage repair, replication-fork progression and checkpoint signalling are context, not parts; RGD's edge to the cell cycle checkpoint pathway is recorded here only as context. (7) Molecular functions come from experimental GOA rows (QuickGO, November 2026) except: INO80 remodeler activity is an authored refinement of its IDA ATP-dependent activity, acting on DNA row, supported by complex-level sliding assays; ARP5-IES6 nucleosomal DNA binding is the ACTR5 gene review core function; ACTR8 nucleosome binding is the ACTR8 gene review core function. INO80B, INO80C, INO80D, INO80E, TFPT, MCRS1, NFRKB, ACTL6A and ACTB are given no function because no experimental row describes their INO80 role. Gene reviews exist for ACTR5, ACTR8, ACTB and UCHL5. GO-CAM. No GO-CAM model in gocams/index.tsv contains an INO80 complex activity or any of INO80, INO80B-E, ACTR5, ACTR8, ACTL6A, NFRKB, MCRS1, TFPT, RUVBL1 or RUVBL2. YY1 appears only in a model of its phosphorylation by CK2 and caspase-7 cleavage in apoptosis, which is unrelated to INO80, and is not linked. The seed's best GO-CAM lead (617b481400000460, KAT5 in homologous recombination) overlaps the RGD list through TIP60-complex genes and is unrelated to INO80. No gocam_associations are recorded. Observations for upstream review. (a) INO80 lacks an experimental ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler activity row although PMID:16230350 and PMID:21303910 show nucleosome sliding by the purified complex; INO80 has it only by IEA. (b) ACTB has no Ino80 complex annotation while ACTL6A, ACTR5 and ACTR8 do. (c) RUVBL1 and RUVBL2 carry DNA helicase activity by IDA from early studies of the isolated proteins; it is not modelled here because no INO80 function depends on it, and ATP hydrolysis is the activity used. PANTHER family ids and PTNs are deliberately not asserted.
