Generic gluconeogenesis module

A generic, taxon-neutral decomposition of gluconeogenesis: the synthesis of a hexose phosphate (and, in some lineages, free glucose) from three- and four-carbon precursors such as pyruvate, lactate, glycerol, amino-acid skeletons, or the malate and oxaloacetate made by the glyoxylate cycle from two-carbon substrates. Seven near-equilibrium reactions are shared with Embden-Meyerhof-Parnas glycolysis and run in reverse. The three effectively irreversible glycolytic steps are bypassed by dedicated enzymes. Pyruvate kinase is bypassed either in two steps through oxaloacetate (a pyruvate carboxylase or a malate dehydrogenase makes oxaloacetate, and a phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase decarboxylates and phosphorylates it) or in one step by a pyruvate dikinase. Phosphofructokinase is bypassed by fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase. Hexokinase is bypassed only where free glucose is released, by glucose-6-phosphatase. Organisms that keep the product as glucose 6-phosphate for storage polysaccharides and cell-wall glucans stop one step earlier. The module is written as function selectors grounded by representative enzyme families, so that it can represent bacterial, fungal, plant and animal implementations.

Context
cytosolGO:0005829

Scope. The module starts at pyruvate or at cytosolic C4 acids and ends at glucose 6-phosphate or free glucose. The pathways that supply its precursors (glyoxylate cycle, lactate oxidation, amino-acid catabolism, glycerol kinase and glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase) and the pathways that consume glucose 6-phosphate (trehalose, glycogen, glucan synthesis) are separate modules. Regulators such as fructose-2,6-bisphosphate metabolism and catabolite inactivation of FBPase and PEPCK are not included. Yeast realisation (YeastPathways GLUCONEO-PWY-1). Cytosolic pyruvate carboxylase PYC1/PYC2 or cytosolic MDH2 makes the oxaloacetate, the ATP-type PCK1 makes phosphoenolpyruvate, the glycolytic trunk enzymes (ENO1/ENO2, GPM1, PGK1, TDH1-3, TPI1, FBA1) run in reverse, and FBP1 and PGI1 complete the route to glucose 6-phosphate. MDH2 rather than PYC is the oxaloacetate source on ethanol or acetate. Corrections relative to YeastCyc and its GO-CAM: TPI1 is added, because the frame and GO-CAM omit the triose-phosphate isomerase step. MAE1, MDH1 and MDH3 are not counted as members. The GO-CAM types the mitochondrial NAD-malic enzyme MAE1 twice, as GO:0008948 oxaloacetate decarboxylase activity (1.1.1.39-RXN) and as GO:0030060 L-malate dehydrogenase (NAD+) activity (activity 6690711d00001655). Neither is the malic-enzyme activity, and the reaction runs in the anaplerotic/pyruvate-supplying direction, not as a gluconeogenic step. The GO-CAM correctly places PYC1/PYC2 in the cytosol. Structure. Compared with the earlier sketch, the pyruvate kinase bypass is now a variant set (two steps through oxaloacetate, or one step by a dikinase). Oxaloacetate supply has a carbon-source variant set (pyruvate carboxylation or malate oxidation), and pyruvate carboxylase has a compartment variant set. The reversible trunk is expanded into its six reactions, and the glucose-6-phosphate isomerase step, which was missing, has been added. Terminal glucose-6-phosphatase and the GTP- and diphosphate-dependent PEPCKs and PPDK stay as ungrounded function selectors; concrete human grounding is in gluconeogenesis_human.
