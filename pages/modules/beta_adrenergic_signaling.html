Beta-adrenergic receptor signaling — epinephrine acting through ADRB1/ADRB2, Gs, adenylyl cyclase/cAMP, PKA-dependent Gi switching, and GRK/beta-arrestin desensitization

The catecholamine hormone and neurotransmitter epinephrine (adrenaline) signals through the beta-1 and beta-2 adrenergic receptors (ADRB1, ADRB2), class A G protein-coupled receptors that couple to the stimulatory G protein. Agonist binding lets the receptor act as a guanine nucleotide exchange factor for G alpha s (GNAS); GTP-bound G alpha s stimulates adenylyl cyclase, and the cAMP produced activates protein kinase A (PKA), whose targets in cardiac myocytes include proteins of excitation-contraction coupling. The signal is limited at the G protein by the GTPase-accelerating activity of the RGS-domain sorting nexin SNX13 (RGS-PX1), a Gs-alpha-selective GTPase activator. The receptors are switched off by G protein-coupled receptor kinases (GRK2, GRK5), whose phosphorylation of the occupied receptor promotes binding of beta-arrestins (ARRB1, ARRB2); arrestin binding sterically blocks G protein coupling (desensitization) and targets the receptor to clathrin-mediated internalization. PKA also phosphorylates the receptors; for ADRB2 this both uncouples it from Gs (heterologous desensitization) and switches its coupling to inhibitory Gi proteins.

ADRB3 is not on the RGD diagram and is not modelled. SNX13. RGD draws Snx13 as acting negatively on Gnas. SNX13 is RGS-PX1, a sorting nexin with an RGS domain; its RGS domain binds G alpha s, accelerates its GTP hydrolysis and attenuates Gs signalling (PMID:11729322), so it is the RGS-family GAP that the record text describes terminating the G protein signal, and it is modelled as a negative regulator of the Gs step with GO:0005096 GTPase activator activity. GOA has no SNX13 GTPase activator MF row; the IDA row from the same paper is the process positive regulation of GTPase activity (GO:0043547), so the MF is an author-supplied refinement documented by the quoted abstract. Its PX-domain sorting-nexin role in endosomal trafficking is outside the module. ADRB2 Gi switching. PKA phosphorylation of ADRB2 switches its coupling from Gs to Gi (PMID:9363896; the ADRB2 review accepts GO:0071881, and gomodel:680ad14200005738 models ADRB2-Gi-ADCY6). It is modelled as an optional regulatory part fed by PKA, with the ADRB2 annoton carrying the inhibitory process; the Gi alpha subunits are not on the RGD gene list and are left to the Gi arm of modules/gpcr_camp_pka_signaling.yaml in prose. The same PKA phosphorylation that drives switching also uncouples ADRB2 from Gs (heterologous desensitization), recorded on the PKA-to-switch connection. Whether ADRB1 switches similarly is not asserted. Termination. GRK2 (RGD Adrbk1) and GRK5 phosphorylate the occupied receptors and beta-arrestins bind them (PMID:2163110).
