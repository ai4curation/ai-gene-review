Bacterial shikimate and chorismate biosynthesis

Reusable bacterial module for conversion of phosphoenolpyruvate and erythrose 4-phosphate to chorismate through the seven-step shikimate pathway. Chorismate is the branch-point precursor for aromatic amino acids, folate, ubiquinone, and other metabolites; those downstream branches are outside this module.

Pseudomonas putida KT2440 phenylalanine, tyrosine and tryptophan biosynthesis
KEGG ppu00400 identifies the KT2440 shikimate-pathway enzymes and their aromatic-amino-acid branches.

KT2440 contains three DAHP-synthase-family proteins, three type-II dehydroquinases, and four shikimate-dehydrogenase-family proteins. These are represented as alternative family members rather than duplicated pathway steps. AroA/PP_1770 is a fusion containing EPSP synthase and prephenate dehydrogenase regions; only its EPSP-synthase activity belongs to this module.
