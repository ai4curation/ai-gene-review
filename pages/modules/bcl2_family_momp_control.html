BCL2-family mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization control module

Mammalian BCL2-family proteins control mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization during intrinsic apoptosis. BH3-only proteins activate or derepress the pore-forming effectors BAX and BAK, while pro-survival BCL2-like proteins bind BH3 helices to sequester activators and effectors. When BH3-only activity overwhelms the pro-survival buffer, BAX and BAK oligomerize at the mitochondrial outer membrane to release cytochrome c and other intermembrane-space proteins into the apoptotic execution pathway.
