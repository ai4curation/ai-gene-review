Benzoate upper degradation pathway

A bacterial aromatic-catabolism module for the aerobic upper benzoate degradation route that converts benzoate to catechol in two reactions. A multicomponent (class IB) Rieske benzoate 1,2-dioxygenase first dihydroxylates benzoate to the cis-dihydrodiol 1,6-dihydroxycyclohexa-2,4-diene-1-carboxylate. This enzyme is built from a BenA-family catalytic alpha subunit carrying the Rieske [2Fe-2S] cluster and the mononuclear iron, a BenB-family non-catalytic beta subunit, and a BenC-family NADH-dependent ferredoxin-reductase that delivers electrons directly to the oxygenase. A BenD-family NAD+-dependent short-chain cis-diol dehydrogenase then oxidatively decarboxylates the diol to catechol, which feeds catechol ring-cleavage and beta-ketoadipate modules. The route is conserved, with the same four-protein architecture and benABCD gene organization, in Gammaproteobacteria (Pseudomonas, Acinetobacter) and in Actinobacteria (Rhodococcus).

BOUNDARY. The module is the benzoate-to-catechol segment (KEGG M00551): benzoate 1,2-dioxygenation (GO:0018623) followed by cis-diol dehydrogenation (GO:0047116). Catechol ring cleavage (ortho or meta), the 2-hydroxymuconate/4-oxalocrotonate steps, the CoA-dependent aerobic box route and anaerobic benzoyl-CoA routes are separate modules. The broader KEGG ppu00622 "xylene degradation" bucket in which the KT2440 genes appear intersects several aromatic maps; its PP_1791/PP_2504 ppu00621 spillover genes belong to downstream catechol/meta-cleavage modules. TAXONOMIC SCOPE. The route was first defined genetically and biochemically in Acinetobacter baylyi ADP1 (formerly A. calcoaceticus) and Pseudomonas putida, and the same benABCD architecture operates in the actinobacterium Rhodococcus jostii RHA1, so the context taxon is Bacteria rather than one genus. Representative members were chosen for experimental support in three genera spanning two phyla: P. putida KT2440 (PSEPK; gene reviews and genome context), A. baylyi ADP1 (ACIAD; reviewed Swiss-Prot entries, purified reconstituted enzyme, BenC crystal structure) and R. jostii RHA1 (RHOJR; heterologous reconstitution and a benA knockout). The pathway is not universal among bacteria. The UniProtKB REST query `(xref:ncbifam-TIGR03229 OR xref:ncbifam-NF040811) AND organism_id:<id>`, run on 2026-10-23 for organism_id 83333 (Escherichia coli K-12), 224308 (Bacillus subtilis 168), 559292 (Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C) and 9606 (human), returned zero entries for each, so E. coli appears here only as a heterologous host for reconstitution and no eukaryotic members are modelled. The closely related TOL-plasmid toluate dioxygenase XylXYZ and dehydrogenase XylL, and the Rhodococcus sp. 19070 BopXYZ/BopL system, are broader-specificity homologues of the same architecture that were not added as exemplars. MODELLING. The terminal oxygenase is modelled as a PROTEIN_COMPLEX of two role-bearing units, BenA and BenB, which is the stable (alpha-beta)3 ISP and carries the complex-level GO:0018623. The BenA unit carries the dioxygenase activity (Rieske cluster, mononuclear iron and the main substrate-specificity determinants); the BenB unit is non-catalytic and is left without a separate GO function, since its gene reviews record contributes_to benzoate 1,2-dioxygenase activity. BenC is deliberately NOT a unit of that complex: it is a separable monomeric reductase that docks transiently to re-reduce the ISP, and the two components were purified independently and reconstituted in vitro (PMID:12949084), so it is a sibling annoton in the same REACTION node carrying its own ferredoxin-NAD+ reductase activity. This follows the VanA/VanB two-component precedent in hydroxycinnamate_vanillate_catabolism.yaml. The reductase is also the most architecturally variable component: RHA1 BenC (512 residues) and Rhodococcus sp. 19070 BopZ have C-terminal extensions absent from the proteobacterial proteins. FAMILY GROUNDING. Participants are grounded to the NCBIfam equivalogs that define each role exactly, rather than to PANTHER: TIGR03229 (benzoate 1,2-dioxygenase large subunit), TIGR03232 (small subunit), NF040810 (BenC) and NF040811 (BenD). All twelve representative proteins match their equivalog in UniProt. The PANTHER families containing these proteins are much broader and functionally heterogeneous: PTHR43756 "CHOLINE MONOOXYGENASE, CHLOROPLASTIC" (BenA, subfamily SF1 "3-PHENYLPROPIONATE_CINNAMIC ACID DIOXYGENASE SUBUNIT ALPHA"), PTHR41534 "BLR3401 PROTEIN" (BenB), PTHR47354 "NADH OXIDOREDUCTASE HCR" (BenC) and PTHR42760 "SHORT-CHAIN DEHYDROGENASES/REDUCTASES FAMILY MEMBER" (BenD). TreeGrafter propagation from these nodes produced the 3-phenylpropionate catabolism (BenB) and fatty-acid elongation (BenD) annotations that the member gene reviews remove. No PANTHER family or PAINT ancestral node is therefore asserted for any role. Each of the four families nevertheless has a completed FamilyReview recording where the pathway-specific subfamily sits and why the family term is too broad: interpro/panther/PTHR43756 (BenA, SF1), PTHR41534 (BenB, SF1), PTHR47354 (BenC, SF5) and PTHR42760 (BenD, SF123). Two of those reviews adjudicate the mis-propagations directly, each judged TOO_DEEP against the PANTHER 19 tree: PTN002222817 (GO:0019380, seeded only by E. coli HcaF but placed at the PTHR41534 root, so it reaches the BenB clade) and PTN001211919 (GO:0030497, seeded by FabG but placed at a Eubacteria node spanning 14 subfamilies including BenD's SF123). CANDIDATE ASSESSED, NOT MODELLED: PcMNX1 (PMID:42567519). A 2026 report identifies PcMNX1, a group A flavoprotein monooxygenase from the white-rot fungus Phanerodontia (Phanerochaete) chrysosporium, as the enzyme for NAD(P)H-dependent oxidative decarboxylation of syringate to dimethoxyhydroquinone; it also converts vanillate and 4-hydroxybenzoate, with products not stated in the cached abstract. It is not a member of this module. (i) Chemistry: PcMNX1 is a single-component FAD monooxygenase giving a hydroquinone, so it neither occupies either modelled step nor supplies the cis-dihydrodiol that BenD consumes. CO2 release is not a discriminator, since the BenD step is itself decarboxylating. (ii) Substrate, route and taxon: syringate rather than benzoate, the fungal lignin-aromatic hydroquinone route rather than benzoate-to-catechol, and a fungus rather than the Bacteria context. Its vanillate activity is also a different fate from the VanAB O-demethylation in hydroxycinnamate_vanillate_catabolism.yaml. (iii) Grounding: no UniProtKB entry cross-references PMID:42567519, and none was found for P. chrysosporium under the gene name MNX1. The related GsMNX1 of Gelatoporia (Ceriporiopsis) subvermispora was not run down further, so the not-found claim is deliberately narrow. Re-evaluate if a fungal lignin-derived aromatic catabolism module is created.

BenABC benzoate dioxygenationReactionbenabc_dioxygenation

A two-component (class IB) Rieske benzoate 1,2-dioxygenase introduces both atoms of dioxygen into benzoate, producing the cis-dihydrodiol used by the downstream dehydrogenase step. The BenAB oxygenase does the oxygen chemistry; the BenC reductase supplies electrons from NADH directly to it.
