Carnitine acyltransferase shuttle (mitochondrial import of acyl and acetyl units)

The carnitine shuttle moves activated acyl groups across membranes that are impermeable to acyl-CoA. A carnitine acyltransferase on the donor side transfers the acyl group from CoA to L-carnitine; the resulting acylcarnitine is carried across the mitochondrial inner membrane by a mitochondrial-carrier-family carnitine/acylcarnitine translocase in exchange for free carnitine; and a matrix carnitine acyltransferase reverses the transfer, regenerating acyl-CoA and releasing carnitine for recycling. The same chemistry serves different cargo in different lineages. In animals the shuttle imports long-chain fatty acids for mitochondrial beta-oxidation: carnitine palmitoyltransferase 1 on the outer membrane catalyses the committed, malonyl-CoA-inhibited step and carnitine palmitoyltransferase 2 on the matrix side of the inner membrane regenerates long-chain acyl-CoA. In fungi such as budding yeast, where beta-oxidation is peroxisomal, the shuttle carries acetyl units: carnitine acetyltransferases in the peroxisome, cytosol and at the mitochondrial outer membrane form acetylcarnitine from acetyl-CoA produced by beta-oxidation or by cytosolic acetate and ethanol metabolism, and the mitochondrial isoform regenerates acetyl-CoA in the matrix for the tricarboxylic acid cycle. Cells take up carnitine through a plasma-membrane transporter; budding yeast cannot make carnitine and depends on uptake. Inherited defects of the animal components (SLC22A5, CPT1A, SLC25A20, CPT2) cause fatty-acid-oxidation disorders.

Context
EukaryotaNCBITaxon:2759

GENERALIZE (YeastPathways project, 2026-10): the former human long-chain shuttle was broadened to carnitine acyltransferase shuttles with the same chemistry but different cargo. The two transferase steps became ONE_OR_MORE variant sets on the axis "acyl cargo": CPT1/CPT2 (GO:0004095, long-chain, animals) vs carnitine acetyltransferases (GO:0004092, acetyl, fungi). All transferases are in PTHR22589. The donor-side acetyl variant groups the three S. cerevisiae paralogs (peroxisomal CAT2 isoform, YAT1 at the mitochondrial outer membrane, cytosolic YAT2) without a single location; the matrix variant is the mitochondrial CAT2 isoform (one gene, two targeted forms; PMID:7628448). The translocase step gained CRC1 (same PANTHER family PTHR45624 as SLC25A20; short-chain preference, PMID:10622748). Carnitine uptake became an EXACTLY_ONE variant set with SLC22A5 (animals) and AGP2 (budding yeast, PMID:10545096). Module-level compartment context was replaced by annoton locations because the donor compartment differs by cargo (mitochondrial outer membrane, peroxisome, cytosol). In yeast the shuttle is in vivo effectively unidirectional (import of acetyl units into mitochondria; PMID:27143389), and it is redundant with the peroxisomal citrate synthase/glyoxylate route (PMID:7628449, PMID:11329169). YeastCyc corrections: PWY3O-4 names Crc1p in its comment but attaches it to no reaction, and AGP2 is absent. GO-CAM disagreements: YeastPathways_PWY3O-4 places CAT2, YAT1 and YAT2 in cytosol (CAT2 is peroxisomal/mitochondrial, YAT1 mitochondrial outer membrane), gives no location for the matrix reaction and lacks the translocase. The medium-chain/peroxisomal mammalian acyltransferases CRAT and CROT are not modelled. Downstream, long-chain acyl-CoA enters mitochondrial beta-oxidation (fatty_acid_beta_oxidation) and acetyl-CoA enters the TCA cycle (tca_cycle).
