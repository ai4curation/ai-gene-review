Bacterial cellulose biosynthesis

Bacterial UDP-glucose-dependent cellulose polymerization coupled to architecture-dependent membrane translocation, periplasmic support and extracellular passage. BcsA-family synthases provide the conserved catalytic role. Characterized BcsB/BcsC systems coexist with typeIII BcsC-independent and cyanobacterial BcsB-independent architectures whose envelope mechanisms remain incompletely resolved. A distinct monoderm CslA–GlxA complex establishes cellulose-synthesizing capacity in vitro, with native polymer composition unresolved. PilZ-dependent activation and accessory assembly factors are modeled in their applicable implementations. The taxon context describes diverse bacterial lineages, not universal occurrence or a universally sufficient protein inventory.
