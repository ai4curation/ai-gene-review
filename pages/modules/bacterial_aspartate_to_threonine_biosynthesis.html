Bacterial L-threonine biosynthesis from L-aspartate

Three species-native examples of the five-reaction aspartate-to-threonine pathway distinguish Pseudomonas standalone enzymes, E. coli bifunctional AK/HDH isozymes, and the Corynebacterium alpha2beta2 aspartokinase. Every complete route requires the intervening aspartate-semialdehyde dehydrogenase. Shared upstream steps also supply other amino-acid branches. The boundary begins at intracellular aspartate and ends at threonine; cofactors and reductant are supplied. A separate P. aeruginosa ThrH comparison uses phosphoserine instead of ATP for homoserine phosphorylation.
