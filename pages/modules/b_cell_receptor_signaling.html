Proximal B cell receptor signaling

Antigen-engaged B-cell receptors use CD79 signaling tails, SYK and BLNK to organize tyrosine phosphorylation. Class IA PI3K supplies PIP3 for BTK recruitment, and BTK promotes PLCG2-dependent IP3 and DAG production. CD19, BCAP and LYN modulate this network according to receptor and cellular context.
