Apoptotic execution phase module

Mammalian apoptotic execution begins when initiator caspases activate executioner caspases-3, -6, and -7. The active cysteine endopeptidases cleave nuclear, DNA-repair, cytoskeletal, membrane-trafficking, and signaling substrates; remove IAP restraint; activate DFF/CAD DNA fragmentation through DFFA/ICAD cleavage; and enable downstream nuclear condensation and apoptotic surface changes. The module starts after receptor or apoptosome signaling and keeps execution-caspase substrates separate from the upstream MOMP and DISC decisions.
