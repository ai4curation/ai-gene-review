Bacterial alkanesulfonate uptake and FMN-dependent desulfonation

SsuABC-mediated alkanesulfonate uptake can supply an aerobic two-component desulfonation system in which a flavin reductase provides reduced FMN and SsuD cleaves the carbon–sulfur bond to release sulfite and an aldehyde. The sampled native implementations are E. coli K-12 and Pseudomonas putida KT2440 and S-313. Their transporter alternatives, substrate ranges and reductase requirements differ. Sulfite assimilation, TauD chemistry, regulatory induction and aromatic-sulfonate accessory pathways lie outside this boundary.
