Replication-dependent histone pre-mRNA 3' end processing in Drosophila melanogaster

Replication-dependent histone mRNAs are not polyadenylated; instead their 3' end is formed by a single endonucleolytic cleavage between a conserved stem-loop and a purine-rich histone downstream element. The stem-loop binding protein (Slbp) binds the stem-loop, and the U7 snRNP, whose U7 snRNA base pairs with the downstream element and whose Sm ring contains the U7-specific Lsm10 and Lsm11, recruits FLASH and the histone pre-mRNA cleavage complex. This complex reuses the Cpsf73 endonuclease, Cpsf100 and Symplekin together with other CPSF and CstF subunits. In Drosophila these factors concentrate in the histone locus body.
