Integrator complex in Drosophila melanogaster

Integrator is a multisubunit complex that binds promoter-proximal RNA polymerase II and cleaves nascent transcripts. Its endonuclease module, in which IntS11 is the catalytic subunit related to the cleavage and polyadenylation endonuclease Cpsf73, processes the 3' ends of snRNAs and terminates transcription of paused or non-productive polymerases at protein-coding genes, enhancers and long noncoding RNA loci. Integrator also recruits protein phosphatase 2A through IntS6 and IntS8, forming the Integrator-PP2A (INTAC) complex that dephosphorylates the polymerase C-terminal domain and Spt5 and opposes P-TEFb. In Drosophila, Integrator attenuates transcription at many genes and is required for snRNA processing.
