Respiratory chain and ATP synthase assembly factors in Drosophila melanogaster

The complexes of the mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation system are built from nuclear- and mitochondrially encoded subunits with the help of dedicated assembly factors that are not part of the mature enzymes. Complex II (succinate dehydrogenase) assembly factors include SDHAF2-type proteins required for covalent flavinylation of the SdhA subunit and SDHAF3 and SDHAF4 homologs that protect the iron-sulfur subunit and intermediates. Complex III assembly requires the AAA ATPase Bcs1, which inserts the Rieske iron-sulfur protein, the LYR protein LYRM7 that chaperones it, and the UQCC proteins that support cytochrome b. HIG1 domain proteins associate with complexes III and IV and support respiratory supercomplex formation. ATP synthase (complex V) assembly factors include ATPAF1-type chaperones of the F1 domain, TMEM70 and the metalloprotease ATP23.
