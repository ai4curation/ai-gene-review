TLR4-MD-2 lipopolysaccharide sensing module

Recognition of bacterial lipopolysaccharide (LPS) by the TLR4-MD-2 receptor and its two adaptor branches. LBP and CD14 deliver monomeric LPS; MD-2 binds lipid A in its hydrophobic pocket and TLR4 transmits the signal; the receptor first signals through TIRAP-MYD88 at the plasma membrane and, after endocytosis, through TRAM-TRIF from endosomes. This is the module where GO:0001875 lipopolysaccharide immune receptor activity belongs.
