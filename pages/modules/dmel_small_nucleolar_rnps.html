Small nucleolar ribonucleoprotein complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

Small nucleolar ribonucleoproteins (snoRNPs) guide chemical modification and processing of ribosomal RNA in the nucleolus. Box H/ACA snoRNPs contain a guide RNA and four core proteins, the pseudouridine synthase dyskerin (Nop60B in Drosophila), Gar1, Nhp2 and Nop10, and convert target uridines into pseudouridine. Box C/D snoRNPs contain the methyltransferase fibrillarin with Nop56, Nop58 and a 15.5K protein and catalyse 2'-O-methylation of target nucleotides. The U3 box C/D snoRNP instead acts in early pre-rRNA cleavage within the small-subunit processome.
