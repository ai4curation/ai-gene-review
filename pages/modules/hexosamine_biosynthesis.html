Eukaryotic hexosamine biosynthesis and GlcNAc salvage

A reusable eukaryotic pathway that converts fructose 6-phosphate to UDP-N-acetylglucosamine through glutamine-dependent amination, acetyl-CoA- dependent N-acetylation, phosphomutase conversion, and UTP-dependent uridylyl transfer, all catalysed by soluble cytosolic enzymes. The eukaryotic order (N-acetylation of glucosamine 6-phosphate before the 6-phosphate to 1-phosphate mutase step) differs from the bacterial route, in which glucosamine 6-phosphate is first isomerised to the 1-phosphate and then acetylated and uridylylated by a bifunctional enzyme. In animals and some other eukaryotes a GlcNAc-kinase salvage branch enters at the N-acetylglucosamine 6-phosphate intermediate. Feedback inhibition of the first, glutamine-dependent amidotransferase by UDP-GlcNAc couples pathway flux to nutrient availability and glycosylation demand. The product supplies protein N-glycosylation, GPI-anchor synthesis, chitin synthesis in fungi, proteoglycan synthesis and O-GlcNAcylation in animals, and the sialic-acid route.

ALIGN (YeastPathways project, 2026-10): the human-grounded module was kept at eukaryotic scope and gained S. cerevisiae (GFA1 P14742, GNA1 P43577, PCM1 P38628, QRI1 P43123) and S. pombe (gfa1 Q09740, gna1 O13738, the two phosphoacetylglucosamine mutase paralogs SPAC13C5.05c Q09687 and SPAC1296.01c Q09770, uap1 O94617) exemplars; every yeast protein falls in the same PANTHER family as the human representative (PTHR10937, PTHR13355, PTHR45955, PTHR11952). YeastCyc gene assignments for UDPNAGSYN-YEAST-PWY are correct and complete for the four de novo steps. The NAGK salvage branch is now marked optional because neither the SGD nor the PomBase model contains a GlcNAc kinase; S. cerevisiae cannot use exogenous GlcNAc. GO-CAM agreement: both yeast models place all four activities in cytosol with the same MF terms used here; the PomBase model additionally includes pgi1 (glucose-6-phosphate isomerase, glycolysis) upstream, which is outside this module. Connections were converted to PRECEDES for the de novo chain. UDP-GlcNAc is the amino-sugar donor for the n_glycan_llo_assembly_cytoplasmic module (ALG7/DPAGT1 and ALG13/ALG14), chitin synthesis, GPI anchors and, in animals, O-GlcNAcylation and sialic-acid synthesis. Bacteria use a different order (GlmS, GlmM mutase on glucosamine 6-phosphate, then bifunctional GlmU acetyltransferase/uridylyltransferase), which is a separate pathway realization, not a variant of this eukaryotic chain. Notable moonlighting captured in the human gene reviews (kept non-core here): UAP1 protein-serine pyrophosphorylase (IRF3, type-I-IFN) and NAGK muramyl-dipeptide kinase (NOD2). Disorders: GFPT1 CMS12, GNPNAT1 rhizomelic skeletal dysplasia, PGM3-CDG.
