Bacterial DnaK/DnaJ/GrpE protein refolding cycle

A bacterial client-refolding mechanism that couples J-protein-assisted client engagement to ATP-driven DnaK remodeling and GrpE-mediated nucleotide exchange. Canonical activating J proteins and the Thermus DnaJ2 client-handoff implementation have distinct experimental support. Reviewed Ecoli, Pseudomonas, mycobacterial and Thermus proteins ground the roles, with exact assay and sequence limits retained. DafA can stabilize an inhibited Thermus assembly but is not required for productive refolding. ClpB aggregate dissolution, RpoH feedback and ATP-independent native-protein protection are outside the required cycle.
