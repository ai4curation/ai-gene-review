Bacterial DnaK/DnaJ/GrpE protein refolding cycle

A bacterial cytosolic Hsp70 refolding module in which the Hsp40 co-chaperone DnaJ captures unfolded or misfolded clients, stimulates DnaK ATP hydrolysis through its J domain, and promotes substrate locking onto DnaK; GrpE then drives nucleotide exchange to reset the DnaK cycle and release clients for folding. The sigma32/RpoH feedback branch, ClpB-dependent disaggregation, and DnaK's ATP-independent holdase side activity are outside this focused DnaK/DnaJ/GrpE folding cycle.
