Alginate O-acetylation

Bacterial alginate O-acetylation modifies mannuronate residues as the polymer traverses the cell envelope. In characterized pseudomonads, the membrane MBOAT component AlgI and periplasm-facing SGNH protein AlgJ act with the interaction mediator AlgF and polymer-modifying enzyme AlgX. Azotobacter uses homologous proteins, with AlgV corresponding to Pseudomonas AlgJ. AlgX directly transfers acetyl groups to polymannuronate in vitro, while the physiological donor and complete AlgI-to-AlgJ-to-AlgX relay remain unresolved. Polymer precursor synthesis, polymerization, export and mannuronate epimerization are separate but physically coupled processes.

AlgI has a broad MBOAT PANTHER assignment whose official label names teichoic-acid modification; that label is not evidence of AlgI substrate specificity. AlgF is not assigned intrinsic acetyltransferase activity solely because it is required for acetylation. GO:0051979 is not a descendant of GO:0042121; modification and polymer biosynthesis/export are distinct roles. The lack of a substrate-specific AlgX GO term is an ontology note, not an unknown biological mechanism.
