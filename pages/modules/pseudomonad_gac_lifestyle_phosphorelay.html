Pseudomonad GacS/GacA lifestyle-control phosphorelay

The pseudomonad Gac two-component system, a global switch between motile or acute and sessile or community behaviour. The inner-membrane hybrid sensor kinase GacS autophosphorylates and relays phosphate through its own receiver and HPt domains to the conserved receiver aspartate of the NarL/FixJ-type response regulator GacA. Phosphorylated GacA then activates transcription of genes for small RNAs that titrate RsmA/CsrA-family translational repressors. A second inner-membrane hybrid protein, RetS, has lost its own kinase activity and opposes the relay by binding GacS, removing phosphate from it and dephosphorylating its receiver domain. The module covers sensing, the His->Asp->His->Asp phosphotransfer, its antagonism by RetS, and GacA's transcriptional output. The Rsm protein arm and the individual downstream regulons (adhesins, type VI secretion, exopolysaccharides) are outside its boundary.

Scope. The module is CONCRETE and scoped to Pseudomonas. The underlying relay chemistry is the taxon-neutral MODULE:two_component_relay motif, which the inner phosphorelay bundle declares. The GacS/GacA pair itself is gammaproteobacterial: Escherichia coli BarA/UvrY (with CsrB/CsrC and CsrA) and Vibrio VarS/VarA are orthologous systems with experimental evidence (BarA autophosphorylates and phosphorylates UvrY specifically, PMID:11022030). They were not added as representative members because the module's antagonist input, RetS, is a pseudomonad feature with no counterpart in those systems, because their outputs differ (carbon storage and virulence rather than the pseudomonad lifestyle programs), and because none of those genes has a review in this repository. A taxon-neutral BarA/GacS-UvrY/GacA-Csr/Rsm module would be the right home for them. Modelling. GacS is a hybrid kinase: its receiver and HPt modules are domains of the sensor, so the template's histidine-phosphotransfer tier is filled by the GacS HPt domain (His863) rather than a separate protein, recorded as the conformance deviation. RetS has no HPt domain and, in the P. aeruginosa ortholog, no kinase activity (degenerate HATPase without G-boxes); KT2440 RetS lacks the same motifs. It is therefore modelled by its transmitter phosphatase activity and as a negative regulator of the relay, not as a second sensor kinase. LadS-type positive inputs are not modelled because no KT2440 ortholog has been characterised. Families. GacS and RetS both fall in PANTHER:PTHR45339:SF1, a heterogeneous subfamily of active hybrid kinases, the kinase-dead RetS, the RcsD phosphotransferase and fungal response regulators. The family review (interpro/panther/PTHR45339/PTHR45339-review.yaml) scopes sensor kinase activity as RESIDUE_DETERMINED by the HATPase G1 and G2 boxes, with GacS as positive and RetS as negative controls. Because no PANTHER subfamily separates the kinases from the rest, no PTHR45339 id is asserted on any of the three GacS/RetS annotons; those roles are grounded by representative member and gene review. GacA belongs to PANTHER:PTHR43214:SF3 (RESPONSE REGULATOR UVRY), the BarA/UvrY subfamily of NarL/FixJ regulators, whose family-wide phosphorelay response regulator and DNA-binding transcription factor activities support both GacA annotons. Representative members are listed per role only (GacS is not listed as a RetS-role exemplar, nor RetS as a GacS-role exemplar). Boundary. GacA activates the RsmY/RsmZ sRNA genes; the sRNAs and the RsmA, RsmE and RsmI proteins of KT2440 that carry the signal post-transcriptionally are outside this boundary. Their gene reviews and a connected Rsm module are outstanding curation work. Adhesin (LapA/LapF), biofilm and K1-T6SS outputs documented in KT2440 are downstream of that arm and are recorded in the gene reviews rather than modelled here.
