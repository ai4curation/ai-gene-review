Bacterial MnmA tRNA wobble-uridine thiolation

Bacterial MnmA pathways mobilize sulfur from cysteine and use ATP to install sulfur at the 2-position of tRNA wobble uridine. Sulfur delivery can use the multi-protein Tus relay or compatible direct desulfurase–MnmA partnerships. The active enzyme state, donor pairing and substrate tRNA determine which implementation can operate.

Bacteria denotes the occurrence envelope, not universal conservation. E. coli and KT2440 orient the Tus route; the latter is a conserved-function inference. Bacillus PS832/168 and Thermus HB27 supply contrasting direct-delivery examples. These exemplars do not establish exclusivity across their lineages. Sulfur-delivery capacity is separate from native flux; simplified E. coli two-enzyme assays do not establish physiological Tus dispensability. U8/U54 thiolation, cytosolic Ncs6/NcsA systems and the other IscS branches are outside the boundary. C5 elaboration is separate and not an obligatory downstream step.
