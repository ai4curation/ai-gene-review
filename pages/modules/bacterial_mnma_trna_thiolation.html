Tus-dependent bacterial MnmA tRNA wobble-uridine thiolation

A bacterial sulfur-relay pathway that installs the 2-thio group of 5-methylaminomethyl-2-thiouridine (mnm5s2U) at wobble position 34 of tRNA(Lys), tRNA(Glu) and tRNA(Gln). The PLP-dependent cysteine desulfurase IscS mobilizes sulfur from L-cysteine as an enzyme-bound persulfide and transfers it to the sulfur carrier TusA, whose binding stimulates IscS and commits the sulfur to tRNA thiolation. TusA passes the sulfur to Cys78 of TusD within the heterohexameric TusBCD complex, a dimer of TusB-TusC-TusD heterotrimers in which TusB and TusC are structural subunits. TusE binds TusBCD, stimulates the TusA-to-TusD step, accepts the sulfur on its C-terminal cysteine and delivers it to the ATP-dependent thiouridylase MnmA bound to substrate tRNA. MnmA adenylates uridine 34 and incorporates the relay sulfur to form 2-thiouridine. Sulfur mobilization, the two carrier hand-offs, complex-mediated transfer and the final tRNA thiolation are modeled as separate roles.

Scope. The module is the Tus-dependent route to s2U34 and is kept within Gammaproteobacteria rather than generalized to all bacteria. MnmA itself is near-universal in bacteria (PTHR11933:SF5 coincides with the PAINT 2-thiouridylase root), but the TusB, TusC, TusD and TusE subunit families are lineage-restricted: their PAINT node leaves in the tree check are gammaproteobacterial Tus orthologs plus Dsr-system homologs of sulfur-oxidizing and sulfate-reducing organisms, Thermotoga and Dictyoglomus, and the Bacillus subtilis entries of PTHR33279 sit in SF6, not the TusA subfamily. Bacteria without the Tus genes and mitochondria (TRMU/MTU1, Slm3) thiolate U34 with MnmA-family enzymes fed by other sulfur donors; those routes are outside this module. Exemplars. Escherichia coli K-12, where the relay was discovered, reconstituted and solved structurally, is the experimental exemplar for every role; Pseudomonas putida KT2440 is a second exemplar whose assignments are ortholog-, signature- and locus-based (no KT2440 relay experiment exists). Families. Each role is grounded to the PANTHER subfamily that holds both exemplars, and each family has a curated review. Several of these subfamilies are broader than the relay: PTHR33279:SF2 also contains TusA-like paralogs (for example KT2440 PP_1233) that lack the TusA-specific IPR022931/HAMAP MF_00413 signature, and the TusB, TusC and TusE families also contain Dsr-type or uncharacterized homologs. For that reason the family reviews leave GO:0002143 UNRESOLVED at family scope for PTHR33279, PTHR38780, PTHR37526 and PTHR37010, while the carrier and catalytic activities used by the annotons are covered. Boundaries. IscS also feeds iron-sulfur cluster assembly, molybdopterin, thiamine and 4-thiouridine synthesis (modules/bacterial_isc_iron_sulfur_cluster_assembly.yaml models the Isc branch with the same IscS grounding); the MnmEG/MnmC elaboration of the modified uridine at C5 is downstream and also excluded. TusA's secondary contributions to molybdopterin synthesis are recorded in its gene review, not here.
