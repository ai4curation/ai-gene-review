Tus-dependent bacterial MnmA tRNA wobble-uridine thiolation

A Tus-dependent bacterial sulfur-relay module that mobilizes sulfur from cysteine and passes it through TusA, the TusBCD complex, and TusE to MnmA, which installs 2-thiouridine at the wobble uridine of substrate tRNAs. Sulfur mobilization, carrier handoffs, complex-mediated transfer, and tRNA thiolation are modeled as separate substantive roles.

This module represents the Tus-dependent implementation of the MnmA pathway; it is not asserted to be universal across bacteria. IscS also supplies sulfur to iron-sulfur cluster and cofactor pathways; those branches are outside this boundary. MnmC-dependent elaboration of the modified uridine is downstream and is likewise excluded. The exact KT2440 gene names for Q88FT8, Q88FT7, and Q88FT6 remain locus-tag based in the review files, while their family roles are represented as TusC, TusB, and TusE here.
