JAK-STAT signaling pathway in Drosophila melanogaster

The Drosophila JAK-STAT pathway has a minimal set of core components. The secreted Unpaired cytokines (Upd1, Upd2, Upd3) bind the receptor Domeless, which is related to vertebrate gp130-type cytokine receptors. Receptor activation stimulates the associated Janus kinase Hopscotch, which phosphorylates Domeless and the single STAT, Stat92E. Phosphorylated Stat92E dimerizes, enters the nucleus and activates target genes. The pathway acts in embryonic segmentation, stem cell maintenance, hematopoiesis, intestinal regeneration, cellular immunity and stress responses.
