Human NuRD nucleosome remodeling and deacetylase complex — MTA-HDAC-RBBP deacetylase core with an MBD-GATAD2 bridge to a CHD remodeling ATPase and CDK2AP1; HDAC1/2, MTA1-3, RBBP4/7, MBD2/3, GATAD2A/B, CHD3/4/5, CDK2AP1

The nucleosome remodeling and deacetylase (NuRD) complex couples ATP-dependent nucleosome remodeling by a CHD-family (Mi-2) ATPase with class I histone deacetylation in one assembly of about 1 to 2 MDa. Each of its seven subunit types is encoded by two or three paralogues in vertebrates, and the paralogues define mutually exclusive, functionally distinct complexes. The core is a deacetylase subcomplex in which MTA1, MTA2 or MTA3 scaffolds the histone deacetylases HDAC1 and HDAC2 and the histone-binding WD40 proteins RBBP4 and RBBP7; the HDAC-RBBP pair alone is a core shared with Sin3 complexes and is catalytically weak until MTA engages it. A methyl-CpG-binding domain protein (MBD2 or MBD3) binds this core, and a GATAD2 protein (GATAD2A or GATAD2B, p66alpha or p66beta) bridges the MBD to the C-terminal region of the CHD remodeler (CHD3, CHD4 or, in brain, CHD5), which carries the peripheral subunit CDK2AP1. MBD2-NuRD is targeted to methylated CpG DNA, while MBD3-NuRD does not bind methylated DNA directly. NuRD is best known as a transcriptional corepressor recruited by many DNA-binding transcription factors, but CHD3- and CHD4-NuRD can also support activation of target genes, and NuRD accumulates at sites of DNA damage. Transcription-factor targeting, DNA repair and the other HDAC1/2- and RBBP4/7-containing complexes (Sin3, CoREST, PRC2, NURF) are context for this module, not parts of it.

RGD curates 74 human genes to PW:0001339 and its page has no gene-linked diagram; the definition divides the SNF2-family remodelers into four families (SWI/SNF, INO80/SWR1, ISWI, CHD) and the diagram draws separate complex nodes (BAF, PBAF, ACF, CHRAC, NURF, WICH, INO80, SRCAP, TRRAP/Tip60, NuRD). Because these are unrelated complexes, the project models one human module per complex group; this module is the NuRD (CHD family) group. Its 14 RGD genes are CHD3 CHD4 CHD5 HDAC1 HDAC2 MTA1 MTA2 MTA3 MBD2 MBD3 GATAD2A GATAD2B RBBP4 RBBP7, all modelled. The remaining RGD genes belong to the SWI/SNF (BAF/PBAF), ISWI, INO80 (ino80_complex), SRCAP and TIP60/p400 groups, modelled or to be modelled in sibling modules. Structure. The parts follow the topology from cross-linking IP-mass spectrometry of human NuRD (PMID:33283408) and the earlier subunit analysis (PMID:10444591): (1) an MTA-HDAC-RBBP deacetylase core, and (2) a remodeling module in which an MBD binds the core, a GATAD2 binds the MBD (CR1) and the CHD C terminus (CR2), and the CHD ATPase carries CDK2AP1. Each vertebrate subunit type is a paralogue family whose members define mutually exclusive complexes, so each family is a variant set. HDAC1/2, MTA1-3 and RBBP4/7 use selection ONE_OR_MORE because the core contains two copies of each (an MTA dimer with two HDACs and several RBBPs) and mixed paralogue occupancy cannot be excluded; MBD2/3, GATAD2A/B and CHD3/4/5 use EXACTLY_ONE because a single copy is present per complex and CHD3- versus CHD4-NuRD (PMID:28977666) and MBD2- versus MBD3-NuRD are distinct complexes. Some structural studies place the MBD with the deacetylase core (a "NuDe" subcomplex); here it is placed in the remodeling module as the bridge, following PMID:33283408, and this choice does not change any annoton. Boundary decisions. (1) HDAC1, HDAC2, RBBP4 and RBBP7 are shared with the Sin3, CoREST, PRC2 and NURF complexes; only their NuRD role is modelled. The RBBP histone binding evidence (PMID:9651585) comes from the Sin3 complex, whose HDAC-RBBP core is the one NuRD shares (PMID:10444591). (2) CDK2AP1 is added although RGD does not list it: it has two IDA NuRD complex rows in human GOA (PMID:20523938, PMID:33283408), copurifies with every NuRD subunit and cross-links to the CHD C terminus. (3) CDK2AP2 is not added: PMID:33283408 reports it as a NuRD subunit, but that rests on one study whose peptides are largely shared with CDK2AP1; it would be a paralogue variant of part 2.4 if confirmed. (4) Substoichiometric NuRD interactors (ZMYND8, ZNF687 and ZNF592, the Z3 module) and the PWWP2A/B and other chromatin readers are not parts. (5) DNA-binding transcription factors that recruit NuRD (for example ZNF410, BCL11A, SALL4, FOG1/ZFPM1, IKZF1) are targeting context, not parts. (6) NuRD recruitment to DNA damage sites and its replication-coupled roles are context. (7) CHD5 membership rests on a mouse-brain purification (PMID:21931736) because human CHD5 has no experimental NuRD complex row. (8) Molecular functions are the subunits' experimental GO rows (QuickGO, November 2026) except: CHD5 remodeler activity is IBA-supported; MTA1-3, MBD3, GATAD2B and CDK2AP1 are given no function because no experimental row describes their scaffold role (generic protein binding is not used).
