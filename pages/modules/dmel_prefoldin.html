Prefoldin complex in Drosophila melanogaster

Prefoldin is a heterohexameric co-chaperone shaped like a jellyfish, with six coiled-coil tentacles that bind nascent or unfolded polypeptides, notably actin and tubulin, and pass them to the CCT chaperonin. It is built from two alpha-class subunits (Pfdn3, encoded by mgr, and Pfdn5) and four beta-class subunits (Pfdn1, Pfdn2, Pfdn4 and Pfdn6). In Drosophila, mgr is needed for normal tubulin levels and spindle formation.
