Entner-Doudoroff glycolysis and gluconeogenesis module

A taxon-neutral module for bacterial hexose-phosphate central carbon metabolism in organisms that route glucose through the phosphorylative Entner-Doudoroff (ED) pathway rather than, or in addition to, a complete forward Embden-Meyerhof-Parnas (EMP) glycolytic route. 6-phosphogluconate is supplied either by phosphorylation and oxidation of glucose or by peripheral oxidative routes, and is split by 6-phosphogluconate dehydratase and KDPG aldolase into pyruvate and glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate. The module separates that committed ED branch from the shared lower glycolytic trunk and from the gluconeogenic bypass reactions that regenerate hexose phosphate from pyruvate, phosphoenolpyruvate or triose phosphate.

Generalization. The phosphorylative ED branch (Edd and Eda) is conserved across bacteria that use it (E. coli, Zymomonas, Pseudomonas), so the module is ABSTRACT and the ED steps carry both E. coli K-12 and P. putida KT2440 exemplars; the lower trunk adds the characterized B. subtilis GpmI. The KT2440 EDEMP architecture (upper EMP enzymes running gluconeogenically during glucose growth, no phosphofructokinase) is lineage-characteristic and is modeled only as a described connection, not as a required part. Boundary. Periplasmic glucose oxidation and the 2-ketogluconate loop are included by reference as one 6-phosphogluconate supply variant; the gluconate-kinase route, the non-phosphorylative and semi-phosphorylative archaeal ED variants, and pentose-phosphate recycling are not modeled. PEP carboxykinase is included by reference to the gluconeogenesis module. KT2440 exemplars and locus tags: glk/Q88P42, zwfA/Q88P31, pgl/Q88P30, edd/Q88P43, eda/Q88P29, tpiA/Q88DV4, gapA/Q88P44, pgk/Q88D64, gpmI/Q88CX4, eno/Q88MF9, pykA/Q88N54, pyk/Q88EZ9, ppsA/Q88L53, fba/Q88D67, fbp/Q88CY9, pgi1/Q88LW9 and pgi2/Q88DW7. KEGG ppu00010 alone is an incomplete satisfiability boundary for KT2440, because edd, zwfA, pgl and eda are assigned through neighboring pentose-phosphate or glutathione-metabolism maps. The second KT2440 GAPDH gapB/Q88KZ0 (PTHR43454) and the minor G6PDH paralogs are deliberately not representatives, because their contributions are unmeasured.

Glucose 6-phosphate can also come from phosphotransferase uptake or from gluconeogenic recycling, so the glucokinase part is optional.
