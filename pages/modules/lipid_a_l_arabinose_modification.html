ArnE/ArnF lipid A L-Ara4N donor flipping

A focused inner-membrane submodule of polymyxin-resistance lipid A modification in which ArnF acts with ArnE to move undecaprenyl-phosphate-alpha-L-Ara4N from the cytoplasmic leaflet to the periplasmic leaflet, positioning the lipid-linked aminoarabinose donor for ArnT-dependent transfer to lipid A. BasS/BasR-PmrA/PmrB induction, upstream ArnBCAD synthesis of the donor, and the ArnT transfer reaction are outside this reviewed ArnF-anchored module.
