CCT/TRiC chaperonin-mediated folding in Caenorhabditis elegans

The chaperonin containing TCP-1 (CCT, also called TRiC) is the group II chaperonin of the eukaryotic cytosol. It is a double ring of two hetero-octameric rings, each made of one copy of eight paralogous subunits (alpha to theta), which C. elegans encodes as cct-1 to cct-8. Each subunit has an equatorial ATP-binding domain, an intermediate hinge and an apical client-binding domain with a built-in lid, so CCT closes its folding chamber without a separate co-chaperonin. Cycles of ATP binding and hydrolysis drive lid closure and folding of clients held in the chamber. The main obligate clients are actin and tubulin. Nascent actin and tubulin chains are captured by the hexameric jellyfish-shaped co-chaperone prefoldin (PFD-1 to PFD-6) and handed to CCT. In C. elegans the cct genes are expressed at all stages and are essential for embryogenesis. Depleting CCT disrupts actin and microtubule organization: intestinal cells lose apical actin and microvilli, and tubulin levels fall. Prefoldin loss lowers alpha-tubulin levels and slows microtubule growth, causing defects in embryonic cell division and in distal tip cell migration. Raising the level of the CCT-8 subunit in somatic tissues extends lifespan in a CCT-dependent way.

Prefoldin client capture and deliveryMolecular Functionprefoldin_delivery

Prefoldin is a heterohexamer of two alpha-class and four beta-class subunits. Its coiled-coil tentacles bind non-native actin and tubulin chains without ATP and pass them to CCT. In C. elegans, prefoldin RNAi lowers alpha-tubulin and slows microtubule growth, and pfd-1 mutants show distal tip cell migration defects that CCT or tubulin RNAi phenocopy.

Part 2: ATP-dependent client folding in the CCT chamber
CCT/TRiC ATP-dependent folding chamberMolecular Functioncct_folding_chamber

The eight CCT subunits occupy fixed positions in each ring. Their apical domains provide client-binding surfaces and the built-in lid. ATP binding and hydrolysis in the equatorial domains close the lid and fold the enclosed client. Subunits differ in ATPase activity: alpha (CCT-1) keeps a canonical high-activity site, whereas theta (CCT-8) is a low-ATPase subunit.
