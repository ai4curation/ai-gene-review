Survival motor neuron (SMN) complex in Drosophila melanogaster

The survival motor neuron (SMN) complex assembles the heptameric Sm protein ring onto the Sm site of spliceosomal snRNAs in the cytoplasm, the key step of Sm-class snRNP biogenesis. SMN oligomerizes and binds Gemin2, which holds a pentamer of Sm proteins; Gemin5 recognizes the snRNA, and Gemin3 is a DEAD-box helicase subunit. Drosophila has a reduced set of Gemin proteins compared with vertebrates and carries three Gemin4 paralogs (Glos/Gemin4a, Gem4b, Gem4c); FlyBase defines SMN complex variants that differ in which Gemin4 paralog they contain. Loss of Smn in flies causes defects in snRNP assembly, motor function and viability, modelling spinal muscular atrophy.
