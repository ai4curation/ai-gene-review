Slit-Robo signaling pathway module

Slits are secreted leucine-rich-repeat glycoproteins that bind Roundabout (Robo) receptors on migrating cells and axons. Slit-Robo signaling is best known as the repulsive cue that keeps axons from crossing, or recrossing, the CNS midline, and it also patterns the lateral position of longitudinal axon pathways.

These results support the hypothesis that Slit proteins have an evolutionarily conserved role in axon guidance as repulsive ligands for Robo receptors.

Boundary. The module has two steps: a secreted Slit ligand and a Robo receptor on the responding cell. Intracellular effectors (for example Abelson kinase, Enabled, Son of sevenless and the srGAP Rho GTPase-activating proteins), the Robo regulator Commissureless and its vertebrate counterparts, and heparan sulfate proteoglycan co-receptors are not modelled as parts. Representative members are human (SLIT1-3, ROBO1, ROBO2) and Drosophila (sli, robo1, robo2, robo3). Human ROBO3 and ROBO4 are omitted because they diverge from the canonical Slit-receptor role. Ligand function term. The ligand step uses GO:0048495 Roundabout binding, the term the Slit PAINT node propagates. GO:0048495 is_a signaling receptor binding, not receptor ligand activity (GO:0048018). Human SLIT2 additionally carries chemorepellent activity (GO:0045499), which is under receptor ligand activity, so a check that requires a receptor ligand activity gene finds one in the human pathway; Drosophila sli has only Roundabout binding, so the same check finds none in the fly pathway. This is the same ontology question as for netrin receptor binding in the netrin module. Receptor family. Robo receptors are split across PANTHER families: human ROBO1-3 and Drosophila robo2 are in PTHR12231, ROBO4 and Drosophila robo1 in PTHR44170, and Drosophila robo3 in PTHR10075 (UniProt PANTHER cross-references). No family id is asserted for the receptor step. Annotation state (GOA GAFs dated 2026-05-21). Human: SLIT2, SLIT3 and ROBO1 are annotated to GO:0035385; SLIT1 and ROBO2 are not. Drosophila: sli and robo1 are annotated to GO:0035385; robo2 and robo3 are not, and neither has a receptor MF.
