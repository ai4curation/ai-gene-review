Bacterial chemotaxis signal transduction to flagellar motor output

Bacterial chemoreceptor-to-flagellar-motor signaling through receptor-coupled CheA phosphotransfer and a motor-output CheY receiver. Membrane and cytoplasmic receptor architectures, CheW/CheV coupling, lineage-dependent motor responses and optional receptor-adaptation/reset mechanisms are represented separately. The model includes Bacillus CheC/CheD and FliY mechanisms, divergent Helicobacter CheZ/ChePep organization, and Sinorhizobium phosphate drainage. Taxon scope describes the reusable bacterial mechanism, not universal occurrence or equivalent native kinetics. Sodium-conducting PomAB and coexisting CheX/CheZ reset exemplars extend the mechanistic scope.
