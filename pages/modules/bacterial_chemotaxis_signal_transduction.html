Bacterial chemotaxis signal transduction to flagellar motor output

Reusable bacterial chemotaxis signaling module linking chemoreceptor input to CheA autophosphorylation, CheY response-regulator phosphorylation, flagellar switch control, and MotAB-powered motor output. Receptor repertoires and attractant specificity vary by species; exact Pseudomonas putida exemplars ground one realization without making the module species-specific.

Identifies the PSEPK receptor, CheA/CheY, switch, and stator realization.

The module captures signal flow rather than the entire receptor repertoire. McpS and PcaY are exact receptor exemplars, not a claim that all PSEPK MCPs sense the same ligands. CheZ-mediated dephosphorylation is included as signal reset. Flagellar construction is outside this module and is modeled separately.
