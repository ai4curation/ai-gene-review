Bacterial unsaturated fatty acid biosynthesis

Formation of the cis-unsaturated acyl chains of bacterial membrane lipids. Bacteria introduce the double bond by one or more of several routes. In the oxygen-independent routes the double bond is made during type II fatty acid synthesis at the C10 stage: proteobacterial FabA dehydrates 3-hydroxydecanoyl-ACP and isomerizes the resulting trans-2-decenoyl-ACP to cis-3-decenoyl-ACP, which FabB then commits to elongation; Enterococcus-type FabN, a FabZ-family dehydratase, performs the same two reactions; and Streptococcus-type FabM, an unrelated crotonase-fold enzyme, isomerizes trans-2-decenoyl-ACP made by the general dehydratase. In the oxygen-dependent route a membrane di-iron desaturase, such as Bacillus subtilis Des, inserts a cis double bond into saturated acyl chains already esterified in membrane phospholipids. A FabF-type condensing enzyme can extend the C16:1 product of the anaerobic routes to C18:1. The reduction and dehydration reactions of the shared elongation cycle are outside this module.

Context
BacteriaNCBITaxon:2

The module is abstract and species-neutral. Exemplars come from organisms with experimental evidence: E. coli K-12 for FabA, FabB and FabF (with P. putida KT2440 orthologs), S. pneumoniae for FabM, E. faecalis for FabN and B. subtilis for Des. The cis double-bond step is modelled as a ROUTE variant set because the routes use different chemistry and unrelated enzymes: the FabA/FabB route keeps three separate reactions because FabB commitment is specific to it; the FabN route is FabA-like but its isomerase is a member-level property within the FabZ subfamily (PTHR30272:SF1), so the isomerase annoton carries no PANTHER identifier while the dehydratase annoton uses SF1, which the family review supports family-wide; the FabM route is grounded on the NCBIfam model NF050134 recorded in the FabM UniProt record, because PANTHER PTHR42964:SF1 also holds hydratase-lyases and polyketide dehydratases. GO has no term for Delta5 acyl-lipid desaturase activity, so the Des annoton uses the oxygen-dependent desaturase class GO:0016717. The FabN accession Q820V3 is inferred from the "FabZ1" genome annotation named by Wang and Cronan (2004). P. aeruginosa DesA and DesB (oxygen-dependent desaturases acting on phospholipids and acyl-CoA) are further aerobic examples not used as exemplars because their entries are unreviewed. TesA, TesB, and PP_5331 of the KEGG ppu01040 map are excluded because they hydrolyse thioesters rather than introduce or elongate cis unsaturation. FabZ and the reductive FAS-II enzymes are shared-cycle context; the type_ii_fatty_acid_synthesis module is authoritative for the shared elongation cycle and this module for the unsaturated branch. PANTHER's official PTHR11712:SF336 label includes "MITOCHONDRIAL" even though the subfamily also contains bacterial FabF proteins; the label is retained verbatim for identifier validation and is not a localization assertion.
