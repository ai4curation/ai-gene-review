Aliphatic glucosinolate biosynthesis and myrosinase activation (glucosinolate-myrosinase defense system)

This module describes the chain-elongated methionine glucosinolate pathway and classical myrosinase activation in characterized Brassicaceae. It is not a universal pathway for all glucosinolate-producing plants. Methionine-derived (aliphatic) glucosinolates are made in three phases: (1) side-chain elongation, a cycle recruited from leucine biosynthesis that runs partly in the cytosol and partly in the chloroplast: methionine is deaminated to its 2-oxo acid in the cytosol (BCAT4), and after import into the chloroplast the 2-oxo acid is condensed with acetyl-CoA (MAM), isomerized (IPMI), oxidatively decarboxylated (IPMDH1) and transaminated back to a chain-elongated methionine homolog (BCAT3), each turn adding one methylene group; (2) formation of the core glucosinolate structure, in which CYP79F converts the chain-elongated amino acid to an aldoxime, CYP83A1 activates the aldoxime for glutathione conjugation, the conjugate is trimmed by gamma-glutamyl peptidase (GGP1) and cleaved by the C-S lyase SUR1 to a thiohydroximate, which is S-glucosylated (UGT74C1/UGT74B1) and finally sulfated from PAPS by desulfoglucosinolate sulfotransferases (SOT16-18); and (3) side-chain modification, in which flavin monooxygenases (FMO GS-OX) S-oxygenate methylthioalkyl to methylsulfinylalkyl glucosinolates and the 2-oxoglutarate-dependent dioxygenase AOP2 converts these to alkenyl glucosinolates such as allylglucosinolate (sinigrin). The classical damage-activated defense chemistry depends on hydrolysis of the stored glucosinolate. On tissue damage, thioglucosidases (myrosinases, TGG1/TGG2) hydrolyze it to an unstable aglucone that rearranges by default to an isothiocyanate; Fe(II)-dependent specifier proteins (ESP, NSPs, TFP) instead redirect the aglucone to epithionitriles, simple nitriles or organic thiocyanates. Natural variation at MAM, AOP and ESP loci accounts for much of the glucosinolate and breakdown-product diversity between accessions and species. Transcriptional regulation (MYB28/MYB29 with MYC2-4), glucosinolate transport (GTR1/GTR2), indole and aromatic glucosinolates, and sulfate activation to PAPS are outside this module's boundary. BCAT3 is a characterized short-chain exit route rather than the sole exit enzyme for all chain lengths. Native transport between compartments is required; transport proteins are outside this module. Decoration and specifier branches vary among species, accessions and tissues.

Part 1: methionine side-chain elongation
Methionine chain-elongation cycleMetabolic Pathwaychain_elongation

A cycle recruited from leucine biosynthesis that adds one methylene group per turn to methionine. The deamination is cytosolic; the condensation, isomerization, oxidative decarboxylation and terminal transamination run in the chloroplast. The number of turns (set mainly by MAM paralogs) determines side-chain length; one turn yields homomethionine, the precursor of C3 (propyl/allyl) glucosinolates.

Myrosinase (thioglucosidase) hydrolysisReactionmyrosinase_hydrolysis

Myrosinases are kept apart from glucosinolates in intact tissue; on damage they hydrolyze the thioglucoside bond to release glucose and an unstable aglucone that, without a specifier protein, rearranges spontaneously to an isothiocyanate.
