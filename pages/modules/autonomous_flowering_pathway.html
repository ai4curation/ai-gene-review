Autonomous flowering pathway repressing FLOWERING LOCUS C (Arabidopsis)

The autonomous pathway is the set of photoperiod-independent Arabidopsis flowering promoters whose common output is quantitative repression of the MADS-box floral repressor FLOWERING LOCUS C (FLC). It couples co-transcriptional RNA 3'-end processing to chromatin silencing. At FLC, the nuclear RRM/WW RNA-binding protein FCA binds the nascent antisense COOLAIR transcripts in liquid-like nuclear bodies and engages the CPSF subunit FY (WDR33/Pfs2p) through FY's C-terminal PPLPP motifs. This directs cleavage and polyadenylation to the promoter-proximal antisense poly(A) site. The spen-family RNA-binding protein FPA acts redundantly with FCA on poly(A) site choice. Proximal termination is handed over to a chromatin module made up of the LSD1-type H3K4 demethylase FLD, LUMINIDEPENDENS (LD, a Ref2/PNUTS-like subunit linked to an APRF1/TOPP4 CPF-like phosphatase module) and SDG26, which contacts FY. FLD/LD/SDG26 remove H3K4me1 over the FLC gene body, which reduces H3K36me3 and transcription and favours Polycomb (PRC2) H3K27me3. The RbAp48-like histone binding protein FVE (MSI4) promotes FLC repression as a shared subunit of HDA5/HDA6-FLD histone deacetylase complexes and of a CLF-PRC2. Repressed FLC releases its repression of the floral integrators FT and SOC1, so the pathway promotes flowering. FRIGIDA-mediated FLC activation, cold-induced vernalization silencing (VIN3/VRN-PRC2), photoperiod signalling and the FT/SOC1-triggered meristem identity program are outside this module. Here FLC, FT and SOC1 appear only as the regulated output.

Each annoton is grounded on an owned, reviewed gene in genes/ARATH (FCA O04425, FY Q6NLV4, FPA Q8LPQ9, FLD Q9CAE3, FVE/MSI4 O22607, LD Q38796), and its function term matches that review's core_functions. FLC, FT and SOC1 are read-only output members reviewed elsewhere. FY and LD annotons carry no molecular-function term. FY is modelled as a CPSF subunit, because direct poly(A)-signal binding by plant FY has not been shown. LD is a Ref2/PNUTS-like scaffold for which no GO MF is established. Other components named in the literature are not modelled as annotons here because they are not reviewed in this batch: SDG26, APRF1, TOPP4, EFL2/EFL4, the FCA nuclear-body partner FLL2, the CstF64/CstF77 3'-processing factors, the FLD paralogs LDL1/LDL2, HDA5/HDA6, CLF and the FPA/FCA-independent autonomous genes FLK and REF6. No cached GO-CAM in gocams/index.tsv contains these genes. PANTHER family ids are not asserted. Older literature lists FCA, FY, FPA, FLD, FVE and LD (plus FLK) as autonomous pathway genes. Vernalization/FRIGIDA biology belongs to a separate module. The COOLAIR node uses GO:0110104 mRNA alternative polyadenylation even though COOLAIR is a lncRNA: the node's defining event is poly(A)-site choice (proximal vs distal), which the general GO:0031123 RNA 3'-end processing does not express, GO has no lncRNA-specific alternative polyadenylation term, and the same FCA/FPA/FY machinery acts on mRNAs in this node (FCA autoregulation of its own pre-mRNA, FPA genome-wide poly(A)-site choice), matching the reviewed core functions of FCA and FPA. The term is a slight over-specification for the antisense transcript; a lncRNA alternative polyadenylation term would be the better fit if GO adds one.
