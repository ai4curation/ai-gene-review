Arabidopsis autonomous flowering contributions to FLC repression

Arabidopsis autonomous flowering regulators promote floral transition through overlapping mechanisms that reduce FLOWERING LOCUS C (FLC) expression. FCA and FY couple processing of COOLAIR antisense RNA to chromatin-associated and termination machinery involving FLD, LD, SDG26/ASHH1, APRF1 and TOPP4. FPA contributes through a distinct RNA-processing route; FLK recognizes modified FLC sense mRNA; FVE/MSI4 supports chromatin-regulatory associations. These relationships vary with genetic background, dosage and locus. Reduced FLC can release repression of FT and SOC1, while FVE also has a distinct direct regulatory effect at FT. Conserved proteins in other plants share biochemical features without necessarily sharing this native RNA-target and chromatin circuit.
