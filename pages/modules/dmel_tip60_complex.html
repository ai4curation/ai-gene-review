Tip60 histone acetyltransferase and exchange complex in Drosophila melanogaster

The Drosophila Tip60 complex combines the activities that budding yeast splits between NuA4 and SWR1. Its MYST-family acetyltransferase Tip60 acetylates nucleosomal histone H4 and the H2A variant H2Av, and the SWR1-family ATPase Domino exchanges phosphorylated H2Av for unmodified H2Av, which links the complex to DNA damage responses. The complex also contains the AAA+ ATPases Pontin and Reptin, the Tra1 homolog Nipped-A, actin, and chromatin-reader subunits including MRG15, Brd8 and Gas41. It contributes to transcriptional regulation, DNA repair and cell-cycle control.
