Fungal chitin synthesis and deacetylation to chitosan

Chitin, a linear beta-1,4-linked polymer of N-acetylglucosamine, is a minor but essential structural polysaccharide of the fungal cell wall, septa and spore walls. It is made at the plasma membrane by chitin synthases, processive multi-pass membrane GT-2 glycosyltransferases that transfer N-acetylglucosamine from cytosolic UDP-N-acetylglucosamine to the growing chain and extrude the chain through a transmembrane channel to the cell surface. Fungi have several chitin synthase classes with different cellular jobs. In budding yeast, chitin synthase II (Chs2) makes the primary septum at the bud neck, chitin synthase III (catalytic Chs3 activated by the Chs4 or, in sporulation, Shc1 subunit) makes most cell-wall chitin, the chitin ring at bud emergence and the chitin of the spore wall, and chitin synthase I (Chs1) repairs the birth scar after cell separation. Loss of all three is lethal. Some nascent chitin is then deacetylated by secreted or membrane-anchored CE4-family chitin deacetylases to give chitosan, a beta-1,4-glucosamine polymer. In budding yeast this happens only in the ascospore wall, where the sporulation-specific deacetylases Cda1 and Cda2 convert Chs3-made chitin into the chitosan layer; in other fungi, such as Cryptococcus neoformans, deacetylases also make chitosan in the vegetative wall. The supply of UDP-N-acetylglucosamine (hexosamine pathway), chitin synthase trafficking and localisation factors, and chitin cross-linking or degradation are outside the module.

fungiNCBITaxon:4751

NEW module (YeastPathways crosswalk, 2026-10) combining PWY3O-15 (chitin biosynthesis) and PWY3O-70 (chitosan biosynthesis). The polymerisation step is modelled with chitin-synthase class variants (CSIII, CSII, CSI); the deacetylation step is an optional second part, linked specifically to CSIII. Corrections and additions to YeastCyc: (1) the three synthases share one reaction in YeastCyc; they are separated here because they build different structures (PMID:2050738, PMID:2523889, PMID:8257107). (2) The CSIII activator Chs4/Skt5, named in the YeastCyc comment, and its sporulation paralog Shc1 were added as non-catalytic activator subunits (GO:0008047). (3) The chitin synthases are placed in the plasma membrane (CSII at the bud neck), not the cytosol. GO-CAM disagreements: the YeastPathways PWY3O-15 model puts all three chitin synthase activities in the cytosol and omits Chs4; there is no GO-CAM for PWY3O-70. Consistency with gene reviews: the CHS7 review gives Chs7 a protein folding chaperone function in ER-to-Golgi transport of Chs3, so Chs7, like the exomer (Chs5/Chs6) and the chitin-ring scaffold Bni4, is treated as trafficking and localisation machinery outside the module. UDP-N-acetylglucosamine supply is the hexosamine_biosynthesis module (GFA1, GNA1, PCM1, QRI1 in yeast). GO has no chitosan biosynthetic process term, so the deacetylation part uses chitin metabolic process (GO:0006030) and its S. cerevisiae annoton uses ascospore wall assembly; a chitosan biosynthesis term could be requested. The Cryptococcus deacetylases (PMID:17400891) generalise the step to vegetative walls but are not used as exemplars because their UniProt accessions were not verified. No PomBase GO-CAM is linked; S. pombe is not used as an exemplar.
