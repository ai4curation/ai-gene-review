Retromer complex in Drosophila melanogaster

Retromer retrieves transmembrane cargo from endosomes and returns it to the trans-Golgi network or to the plasma membrane. Its cargo-selective core is a heterotrimer of Vps35, Vps26 and Vps29 that recognizes sorting motifs in cargo tails; it associates with sorting nexins, including the SNX-BAR proteins Snx1 and Snx6, which bind endosomal phosphoinositides and shape membrane tubules. In Drosophila, retromer recycles the Wnt cargo receptor Wntless (Wls) to the Golgi, which is needed for Wingless secretion, and it also maintains rhodopsin and other cargo in photoreceptors.
