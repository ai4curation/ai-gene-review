Glucarate and galactarate catabolism to 2-oxoglutarate

A reusable three-stage aldarate catabolic route in which substrate-specific dehydratases convert D-glucarate or D-galactarate to the common intermediate 5-dehydro-4-deoxy-D-glucarate. A second dehydratase then forms 2,5-dioxopentanoate, which an aldehyde dehydrogenase-family enzyme oxidizes to 2-oxoglutarate. Organisms can encode either or both substrate-entry reactions.

The boundary starts at the substrate-specific aldarate dehydratases and ends at 2-oxoglutarate. Aldarate uptake, upstream uronate production, and downstream central metabolism are outside the module. The two entry reactions are ONE_OR_MORE alternatives because a genome may support either substrate or both. The PSEPK OpenScientist synthesis supports this boundary but is treated as retrieval context; exact functions remain grounded in GO, Rhea, UniProt, PANTHER, and PAINT records. Molecular functions are confined to the reaction annotons; no generic cytoplasmic localization is asserted.
