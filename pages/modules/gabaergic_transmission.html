Human GABAergic transmission through ionotropic GABA-A receptors

Fast GABAergic inhibition in the vertebrate nervous system. GAD1/GAD2 decarboxylate glutamate to GABA in the cytosol of the GABAergic terminal, VGAT (SLC32A1) loads GABA into synaptic vesicles, and calcium-triggered exocytosis releases it. Released GABA opens pentameric GABA-A receptors, which are GABA-gated chloride channels. Two receptor classes are modelled as alternatives. Synaptic gamma2-containing receptors, with (alpha1)2(beta2)2gamma2 as the most abundant example, mediate phasic inhibitory postsynaptic currents. Extrasynaptic delta-containing alpha4/alpha6 receptors respond to ambient GABA and mediate tonic inhibition. The retinal rho1 homopentamer (formerly "GABA-C") is an optional comparator. The plasma-membrane GABA transporter GAT-1 (SLC6A1) terminates the signal by sodium- and chloride-coupled reuptake. Metabotropic GABA-B signalling, GABA catabolism and gephyrin-dependent receptor clustering are outside this module.

PTHR18945 family review
The family review shows that GABA-gated chloride channel terms in PAINT belong to the GABA-A clades (PTN008360627, PTN008566841), not to the whole neurotransmitter-gated ion channel family. This module therefore names exact subunits and does not use a family-wide selector.

The GABA-A receptor is modelled as specific assembled pentamers, each a complete channel; the subunits are not interchangeable. Native stoichiometry is established only for the abundant (alpha1)2(beta2)2gamma2 receptor; the 2alpha-2beta-1gamma2 arrangement of other gamma2 receptors is assumed by analogy, and the delta receptors are given composition without stoichiometry. VGAT loading and exocytosis are included by module_ref and shared with glycinergic transmission. GAT-3 (SLC6A11) astrocytic uptake is a recognised second clearance route but is not yet grounded here. None of the GABA-A subunits, VGAT or GAT-1 has a gene review in this repository, so the functional assertions rest on the cited literature and PAINT, and status is DRAFT.
