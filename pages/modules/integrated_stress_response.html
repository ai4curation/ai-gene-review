Integrated stress response module

The integrated stress response (ISR) converts many unrelated stresses into one translational signal. A stress-activated eIF2alpha kinase phosphorylates the alpha subunit of eIF2 on Ser51 (Ser52 counting the initiator methionine). Phosphorylated eIF2-GDP binds its guanine nucleotide exchange factor eIF2B tightly and inhibits it, so the eIF2-GTP-Met-tRNAi ternary complex falls. Global cap-dependent translation initiation drops, while mRNAs with short upstream ORFs, ATF4 in animals and GCN4 in fungi, are translated more because scanning ribosomes take longer to reacquire ternary complex and bypass the inhibitory uORF (delayed reinitiation). ATF4 or GCN4 then drives a transcriptional program for amino-acid supply, redox defence and recovery. In animals, PP1 holophosphatases targeted to eIF2alpha by the constitutive regulatory subunit PPP1R15B (CReP) and the stress-induced PPP1R15A (GADD34) dephosphorylate eIF2alpha and end the response.

Created 2026-11-02 as a species-independent ISR core. Taxon scope: Opisthokonta is a curator judgement. The full chain (eIF2alpha kinase, eIF2alpha Ser51, eIF2B inhibition, uORF-controlled translation of a bZIP effector) is established in budding yeast (GCN2-GCN4) and in animals (four kinases, ATF4), and the cited papers state it is conserved from yeast to mammals (PMID:15277680, PMID:14638860, PMID:11106749). eIF2alpha kinases are older: PAINT places GO:0004694 on PTHR11042 nodes with plant, Dictyostelium and Plasmodium seeds (PTN000867953, PTN008315216), so the kinase step alone would fit Eukaryota, but a uORF-regulated effector transcription factor and eIF2B-mediated global repression are not grounded here outside fungi and animals. The kinase set differs by lineage (one GCN2 in yeast; HRI, PKR, PERK, GCN2 in vertebrates), which is why kinases are REALIZER variants. Boundary with er_unfolded_protein_response: that module's perk_arm node models PERK, eIF2alpha and ATF4 as the ER-specific branch with BiP control. This module models the shared core once; the PERK realizer repeats only the PERK kinase activity, and the eIF2alpha and ATF4 annotons here are the general ISR roles. PERK is not module_ref'd because the referenced node would bring eIF2alpha and ATF4 in a second time. The five human GO-CAMs are instances; their activities map onto the single eIF2 node, the kinase realizers and the ATF4 realizer, with the defects listed in gocam_associations. COX7A2L/SCAF1 and DDIT3/CHOP are ATF4 target-gene outputs and are left out of the module. eIF2 is represented by its alpha subunit because that is the phosphorylated, regulated subunit; the factor is the alpha-beta-gamma eIF2 complex. GO has no molecular-function term for the eIF2B-inhibitory activity of phosphorylated eIF2, so the inhibition is expressed by connections rather than by a second, differently typed eIF2 node. ISRIB-type eIF2B activators, eIF2B decamer assembly and alternative ISR outputs (ATF5, CHOP, QRICH1) are outside the boundary.
