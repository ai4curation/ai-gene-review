Integrated stress response

The integrated stress response (ISR) converts four unrelated stress signals into one translational and transcriptional output by phosphorylating a single residue. Four eIF2alpha kinases each sense a different input: HRI (EIF2AK1) heme deficiency, mitochondrial stress relayed by the OMA1-DELE1 pathway and cytosolic protein misfolding; PKR (EIF2AK2) double-stranded RNA; PERK (EIF2AK3) endoplasmic-reticulum folding stress; and GCN2 (EIF2AK4) amino-acid limitation and collided ribosomes. All four phosphorylate Ser51 of the alpha subunit of eIF2 (EIF2S1). Unphosphorylated eIF2 delivers initiator methionyl-tRNA to the ribosome and is recycled from the GDP to the GTP form by the decameric guanine-nucleotide exchange factor eIF2B; phosphorylated eIF2 instead binds eIF2B in an exchange-inactive mode and inhibits it, so a substoichiometric phospho-eIF2 pool blocks recycling of the whole eIF2 pool. Reduced eIF2-GTP lowers global initiation and, because delayed reinitiation lets scanning ribosomes bypass an inhibitory upstream ORF, raises translation of ATF4, which activates cytoprotective, amino-acid and redox genes. ATF4 drives a secondary bZIP arm (CHOP/DDIT3, ATF3) that shapes the response and, under severe stress, directs it toward death. ATF4 and CHOP also induce GADD34 (PPP1R15A), which, like the constitutive paralogue CReP (PPP1R15B), targets protein phosphatase 1 to phospho-eIF2alpha and closes a negative feedback loop that restores translation.

Boundary. This module is the ISR signalling relay itself: the four eIF2alpha kinases, the eIF2 hub whose Ser51 phosphorylation is the core event, the eIF2B exchange factor that the phospho-mark inhibits, the ATF4 translational output with its secondary bZIP arm, and the PP1-based feedback that erases the mark. It has five parts and is grounded on GO:0140467 integrated stress response signaling. The effector genes that ATF4 and CHOP induce are downstream biology, not parts of the relay. Taxon scope. The module context is Mammalia (NCBITaxon:40674), the clade for which all five parts including the DELE1 relay and both PP1 targeting subunits are established. The eIF2alpha-phosphorylation core is far older - budding yeast runs it with a single kinase (Gcn2) and the bZIP factor Gcn4 in the ATF4 role - and the four-kinase quartet is vertebrate-wide, so the kinase variant set is a route set rather than a claim that every lineage has four inputs. Representatives are human throughout (EIF2AK1 Q9BQI3, EIF2AK2 P19525, EIF2AK3 Q9NZJ5, EIF2AK4 Q9P2K8, GCN1 Q92616, EIF2S1 P05198, EIF2S2 P20042, EIF2S3 P41091, EIF2B1 Q14232, EIF2B2 P49770, EIF2B3 Q9NR50, EIF2B4 Q9UI10, EIF2B5 Q13144, ATF4 P18848, DDIT3 P35638, ATF3 P18847, PPP1R15A O75807, PPP1R15B Q5SWA1, PPP1CA P62136); accessions follow the gene reviews where one exists. The kinase variant set. Selection is ONE_OR_MORE, not EXACTLY_ONE: a single stress generally engages one kinase, but co-occurring stresses engage several, and GCN2 and PERK are both activated during amino-acid limitation with ER load. The set is declared ROUTE because the four variants differ in the sensed input and in the sensing module, not merely in which protein carries one activity; all four carry the same molecular function (GO:0004694), so the REALIZER inference would otherwise fire. Composition, not duplication. The HRI branch takes the OMA1-DELE1 mitochondrial relay by reference from MODULE:mitochondrial_upr (nodes oma1_dele1_cleavage and dele1_hri_activation) rather than re-authoring the OMA1 and DELE1 annotons. Both referencing parts are optional: HRI is also activated by heme deficiency and by cytosolic protein misfolding, neither of which needs DELE1. The PERK branch deliberately does not use a module_ref: MODULE:er_unfolded_protein_response node perk_arm covers the same chemistry but also carries its own eIF2alpha and ATF4 annotons, so including it would duplicate this module's parts 2 and 4. The relationship is recorded here and on the PERK variant instead. eIF2 and the missing molecular function. GO has no molecular-function term for the activity of phosphorylated eIF2 toward eIF2B - the eIF2B inhibition that is the mechanistic core of the ISR. The hub annoton therefore records eIF2's ordinary translation initiation factor activity (GO:0003743) and states the inhibitory role in role_description and in the connection to the eIF2B annoton. The same tension is recorded on the worm eif-2alpha annoton of MODULE:er_unfolded_protein_response; it is an ontology question, not a knowledge gap. Human EIF2S1 has no gene review yet, so the hub annoton is grounded on UniProtKB:P05198 and on the GO-CAM activities listed below. Complexes. eIF2 (GO:0005850) and eIF2B (GO:0005851) are modelled as complex participants with active units, so that the subunit that carries the regulatory site (EIF2S1) and the subunit that carries the catalytic exchange activity (EIF2B5/epsilon) stay visible. The EIF2B1-EIF2B4 reviews assert contributes_to GO:0005085 within GO:0005851 rather than the activity itself, which matches the complex-level assertion used here. Feedback. GADD34 and CReP are variants of one role - the PP1 targeting subunit that dephosphorylates eIF2alpha - and the set is a REALIZER set with ONE_OR_MORE selection because the two are not interchangeable in time: CReP sets the basal phospho-eIF2alpha level in unstressed cells, while GADD34 is itself an ISR-induced gene and acts late. Both GO-CAM activities type them as molecular adaptor activity (GO:0060090) targeting PPP1CA, and that is the typing used here; the PP1 catalytic subunit is a separate annoton with GO:0004722. Left out. ASNS is an ATF4 target gene (PMID:11960987) and a project candidate, but asparagine synthase activity is an effector of the transcriptional output, not a step of the relay; the same holds for the chaperone, amino-acid transporter and redox genes. ISRIB and related eIF2B activators act on the same node as phospho-eIF2 but are pharmacology, not parts. The eIF2 ternary-complex cycle with eIF5, initiator tRNA and the 40S subunit belongs to translation initiation. Negative regulators of individual branches are left out as regulation rather than steps: the SIFI (UBR4-KCMF1) complex terminates HRI-mediated signalling by ubiquitinating DELE1 and EIF2AK1 (gomodel:6494e2e900001856); AKT1 and ATAD3A inhibit PERK (gomodel:66b5638000001719, gomodel:66b5638000001329) and BiP restrains it in unstressed cells (gomodel:66b5638000000739, modelled in MODULE:er_unfolded_protein_response); p58IPK/DNAJC3 inhibits GCN2 and PKR.
