Isoprenoid (prenyl) diphosphate biosynthesis (IPP/DMAPP -> GPP -> FPP -> GGPP)

The isoprenoid-diphosphate trunk converts the universal five-carbon isoprene units produced by the mevalonate pathway (or, in bacteria and plastids, the MEP pathway; both curated separately) into the linear trans-prenyl diphosphates that seed the major downstream isoprenoid classes. Isopentenyl- diphosphate delta-isomerase (type 1 IDI) reversibly interconverts isopentenyl diphosphate (IPP) and its allylic isomer dimethylallyl diphosphate (DMAPP), supplying the allylic primer. Farnesyl diphosphate synthase then carries out two sequential head-to-tail (trans, 1'-4) condensations: DMAPP + IPP -> geranyl diphosphate (GPP, C10), and GPP + IPP -> farnesyl diphosphate (FPP, C15). FPP is the central branch point: it feeds sterol synthesis (cholesterol in animals, ergosterol in fungi, via squalene), heme A, ubiquinone side chains, dolichol (cis-prenyltransferases) and protein farnesylation. A geranylgeranyl diphosphate synthase adds one more IPP to FPP giving geranylgeranyl diphosphate (GGPP, C20), the donor for protein geranylgeranylation. The GGPP step has two architectures: a dedicated homomeric GGPP synthase (human GGPS1, budding-yeast BTS1), or, in fission yeast, which lacks a GGPS1/BTS1 homolog, a heteromer of the FPP synthase Fps1 with an inactive FPP-synthase-like subunit Spo9. All enzymes are soluble Mg2+-dependent isomerases/prenyltransferases (animal IDI1/IDI2 also peroxisomal). The pathway is the pharmacological target of nitrogen bisphosphonates (FDPS); human GGPS1 loss-of-function causes a muscular dystrophy / sensorineural hearing loss / ovarian insufficiency syndrome.

Isoprenoid (prenyl) diphosphate biosynthesis trunk (GO:0008299), the bridge between the mevalonate pathway (mevalonate_pathway) and the sterol (sterol_commitment_squalene_to_lanosterol, ergosterol_biosynthesis), dolichol, ubiquinone and protein-prenylation branches. Aligned to YeastCyc PWY-5123 and the PomBase isoprenoid GO-CAM: S. cerevisiae IDI1 (P15496) and ERG20 (P08524), S. pombe idi1 (Q10132) and fps1 (O14230) were added as representatives of the existing families, and BTS1 (Q12051; PTHR12001), which YeastCyc does not list in this frame, was added to the GGPP step. The GGPP step became a variant set, because fission yeast makes GGPP with a heteromer of the FPP synthase Fps1 and the FPS-like Spo9 instead of a GGPS1/BTS1 homolog (PMID:17596513). The PomBase GO-CAM types spo9 as an FPP synthase (GO:0004337) and does not model the heteromer's GGPS activity. Its UniProt name (geranylgeranyl pyrophosphate synthase) reflects the heteromer and should not be read as activity of Spo9 alone. COQ1 (hexaprenyl diphosphate synthase), mentioned in the crosswalk, belongs to coenzyme Q biosynthesis (YeastCyc HEXPPSYN-PWY-2) and is excluded; cis-prenyltransferases for dolichol (RER2/SRT1, S. pombe rer2) are also downstream branches. Human content kept: IDI1/IDI2 (cytosol + peroxisome via PTS1), FDPS (nitrogen-bisphosphonate target; partial reactions GO:0004161/GO:0004337 share one active site) and GGPS1 (homohexamer; loss-of-function -> muscular dystrophy/sensorineural hearing loss/ovarian insufficiency). Connections were retyped from PROVIDES_INPUT_FOR to PRECEDES.

Heteromeric FPS / FPS-like GGPP synthase (S. pombe Fps1-Spo9)Protein Complexfps_heteromer_ggps_variant

S. pombe has no GGPS1/BTS1-family protein. GGPP synthase activity appears only when Fps1 and the FPS-like Spo9 are co-expressed, the two proteins interact, and spo9 mutants have reduced GGPS activity and impaired geranylgeranylation (PMID:17596513). Spo9 alone does not complement an FPS-deficient budding-yeast mutant. Both proteins belong to the FPP synthase family PTHR11525.
