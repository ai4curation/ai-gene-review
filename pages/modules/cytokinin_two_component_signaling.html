Cytokinin two-component (His-Asp phosphorelay) signaling in Arabidopsis

The cytokinin signal transduction pathway of Arabidopsis thaliana is a eukaryotic multistep His-Asp phosphorelay. Cytokinins bind the extracytosolic CHASE domains of three partially redundant hybrid sensor histidine kinase receptors (AHK2, AHK3 and CRE1/AHK4), located mainly in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane. Ligand binding activates autophosphorylation of a conserved histidine in the transmitter domain; the phosphoryl group passes to an aspartate in the receptor's receiver domain and then to a conserved histidine of the small, nucleocytosolic histidine phosphotransfer proteins (AHP1, AHP2, AHP3, AHP5). AHPs deliver the phosphoryl group to the receiver aspartate of nuclear type-B response regulators (principally ARR1, ARR10 and ARR12), which are Myb-related (GARP) DNA-binding transcription factors; receiver phosphorylation activates them to bind AGAT(T)-containing cis-elements and induce cytokinin primary-response genes. Among these targets are the type-A response regulators (e.g. ARR5), receiver-only proteins that are themselves phosphorylated and stabilized through the relay and attenuate the pathway, closing a negative feedback loop. Without ligand, CRE1/AHK4 can reverse the relay and dephosphorylate AHPs. The phosphorelay core (receptor -> AHP -> type-B ARR) conforms to the generic two-component relay motif; transcriptional output and type-A feedback are this pathway's extensions. Cytokinin biosynthesis, transport and degradation, the cytokinin-independent histidine kinase CKI1, CRFs, and downstream developmental targets are outside the module boundary.

Members are grounded with verified UniProtKB accessions (AHK2 Q9C5U2, AHK3 Q9C5U1, AHK4 Q9C5U0, AHP1 Q9ZNV9, ARR1 Q940D0, ARR12 P62598, ARR5 Q9SB04). Only the seven reviewed members are listed as participants; redundant paralogs (AHP2, AHP3, AHP5; ARR10 and other subfamily-1 type-B ARRs; ARR3/4/6/7/8/9/15/16/17 type-A ARRs) perform the same roles and are named in prose only. AHP6 (a pseudo-HPt lacking the phospho-accepting histidine) is an inhibitor and outside this boundary. GOA IBA rows place the receptor kinase function at PAINT node PTN000703785 and the AHP phosphotransfer function at PTN002000688; these are recorded here as provenance rather than asserted ancestral nodes. Part 1: cytokinin receptor histidine kinases
Cytokinin perception and receptor autophosphorylationReactionck_receptors

Cytokinin binding to the CHASE domain of AHK2, AHK3 or CRE1/AHK4 in the ER membrane activates autophosphorylation and intramolecular transfer to the receiver domain, from which the phosphoryl group is passed to AHPs. The three receptors are partially redundant; AHK3 dominates in leaves and AHK4 in roots.
