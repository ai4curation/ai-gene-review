Bacterial RecFOR single-strand-gap repair

A reusable bacterial homologous-recombination module for repairing postreplication single-strand gaps. RecQ helicase and RecJ exonuclease enlarge the gap, SSB protects the exposed single-stranded DNA, the RecF-RecO-RecR mediator system replaces SSB with RecA, and the resulting RecA filament catalyzes homology search and DNA strand exchange. RecBCD processing of double-strand-break ends, RuvABC Holliday-junction migration and resolution, replication restart, translesion synthesis, and SOS transcriptional control are separate modules.

Species-aware retrieval resolves the complete KT2440 RecFOR presynaptic pathway, recovers RecQ omitted from the KEGG candidate list, and separates RecFOR from RecBCD, RuvABC, and replication-map artifacts.

The module is species-neutral. Pseudomonas putida KT2440 supplies a concrete representative for every role, but the strain's documented poor homologous- recombination efficiency means gene-set satisfiability should not be interpreted as high pathway flux. RecQ and RecJ cooperate in gap enlargement and are shown as parallel inputs rather than a strict linear enzyme sequence. SSB, RecA, RecQ, and RecJ are shared with other DNA-metabolism contexts.
