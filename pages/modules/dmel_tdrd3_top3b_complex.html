Tdrd3-Top3beta topoisomerase complex in Drosophila melanogaster

Topoisomerase 3-beta (TOP3B) is a type IA topoisomerase that can act on both DNA and RNA. It forms a conserved complex with the Tudor-domain protein TDRD3, which stimulates its activity, recruits it to messenger ribonucleoprotein complexes and links it to the fragile X protein FMRP. In Drosophila, Top3beta and Tdrd3 associate with each other and with Fmr1, and the complex has been implicated in synapse formation and in mRNA translation.
