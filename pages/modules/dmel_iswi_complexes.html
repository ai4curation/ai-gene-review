ISWI chromatin remodeling complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

ISWI is an ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler that slides nucleosomes. In Drosophila the single ISWI ATPase is the catalytic subunit of several complexes defined by their accessory subunits. ACF (with Acf) and CHRAC (Acf plus the histone-fold pair Chrac-14 and Chrac-16) assemble and space regularly arrayed nucleosomes. NURF (with the large subunit E(bx)/NURF301, Caf1-55 and the pyrophosphatase Nurf-38) helps activate transcription of target genes, for example in ecdysone and JAK/STAT signaling. RSF, NORC and TORC (with Toutatis and CtBP) are further ISWI complexes; TORC is linked to transcriptional repression.
