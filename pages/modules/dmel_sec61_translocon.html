Sec61 translocon and associated complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

The Sec61 complex is the protein-conducting channel of the endoplasmic reticulum membrane. Its alpha subunit forms an hourglass-shaped pore with a lateral gate through which signal sequences and transmembrane segments of nascent polypeptides enter the lipid bilayer; the small beta and gamma subunits stabilize the channel. Sec61 receives ribosome-nascent chain complexes from the SRP receptor for cotranslational translocation, and with the Sec62-Sec63 complex it also translocates proteins post-translationally. Translocon-associated proteins such as TRAM and the TRAP complex assist the insertion and translocation of particular substrates. Drosophila has three Sec61 gamma genes, Sec61gamma, CG8860 and the testis-expressed CG13426, giving alternative forms of the channel.
