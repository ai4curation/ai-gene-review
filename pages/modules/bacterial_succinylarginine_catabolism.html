Bacterial succinylarginine catabolism to glutamate and succinate

A five-reaction bacterial pathway that transfers a succinyl group from succinyl-CoA to L-arginine, hydrolyzes the guanidino group, transaminates the resulting N-succinyl-L-ornithine, oxidizes the semialdehyde, and removes the succinyl group to produce L-glutamate and succinate. The initiating transferase may be a single-chain enzyme or a two-chain complex. Arginine transport, regulation, alternative arginine-catabolic routes, and downstream central metabolism are outside the module boundary.
