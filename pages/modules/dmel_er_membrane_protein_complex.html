ER membrane protein complex (EMC) in Drosophila melanogaster

The ER membrane protein complex (EMC) is a multisubunit membrane protein insertase in the endoplasmic reticulum. It inserts tail-anchored proteins with moderately hydrophobic transmembrane segments and the first transmembrane segment of many multipass membrane proteins in the N-terminus out orientation, and it supports biogenesis of other multipass proteins. The conserved hydrophilic vestibule formed by EMC3 and EMC6 in the membrane carries out insertion, while the cytosolic EMC2 and EMC8/9 and the large lumenal EMC1, EMC7 and EMC10 subunits shape the complex. In Drosophila the EMC is required in photoreceptors for the biogenesis of rhodopsin and other multipass membrane proteins. Drosophila has two EMC2 paralogs, EMC2A and EMC2B, which FlyBase treats as alternative subunits.
