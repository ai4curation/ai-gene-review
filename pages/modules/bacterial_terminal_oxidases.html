Bacterial aerobic terminal oxidase branches

Reusable module for bacterial aerobic terminal oxidases that reduce oxygen at the end of branched respiratory chains. Cytochrome c oxidases accept electrons from periplasmic cytochrome c, whereas quinol oxidases accept electrons directly from the membrane quinol pool. Individual bacteria may encode one or more variants with different donor preferences, oxygen affinities, inhibitor sensitivities, and proton-coupling properties. The module therefore separates the two donor classes and models their enzyme families as coexisting variants rather than forcing one universal complex.
