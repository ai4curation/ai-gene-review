AMPK energy sensing — LKB1-STRAD-MO25 or Ca2+/calmodulin-CAMKK2 Thr172 activation of the AMPK alpha/beta/gamma heterotrimer, PP2A/PPM1E/PPM1F/PP1-R6 inactivation, and TSC2-mTORC1, ACC and CPT1 outputs; PRKAA1/2, PRKAB1/2, PRKAG1/2/3, STK11, STRADA, CAB39, CALM1-3, CAMKK1/2, PPP2CA, PPM1E, PPM1F, PPP1CA/B, PPP1R3D, TSC1, TSC2

AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) is the principal cellular energy sensor of mammals. It is a heterotrimer of a catalytic alpha subunit (PRKAA1 or PRKAA2), a scaffolding beta subunit (PRKAB1 or PRKAB2) that carries a glycogen-binding domain and bridges alpha and gamma, and a regulatory gamma subunit (PRKAG1, PRKAG2 or PRKAG3) whose four tandem cystathionine-beta-synthase (CBS) repeats form the adenine nucleotide-binding sites. The kinase is active only when Thr172 in the alpha-subunit activation loop is phosphorylated. Two upstream kinases do this. The LKB1 complex, in which the kinase STK11 (LKB1) is held in an active conformation by the pseudokinase STRADA and the scaffold CAB39 (MO25alpha), is constitutively active, so its output is set by the AMPK side: when ATP production falls or consumption rises, AMP and ADP displace ATP from the gamma-subunit sites, which promotes Thr172 phosphorylation, protects the phosphate from phosphatases and (for AMP) allosterically activates the kinase. Alternatively, a rise in cytosolic calcium activates calmodulin, which activates the calmodulin-dependent protein kinase kinases, chiefly CAMKK2, and these phosphorylate Thr172 without any change in adenine nucleotides. The signal is terminated by dephosphorylation of Thr172 by protein phosphatase 2A, by the PPM-family phosphatases PPM1E and PPM1F, and, in pancreatic beta cells, by a protein phosphatase 1 holoenzyme targeted to AMPK by its regulatory subunit PPP1R3D (R6). Active AMPK switches off ATP-consuming processes and switches on ATP-producing ones. It phosphorylates TSC2, increasing the GTPase-activating activity of the TSC1-TSC2 complex towards RHEB, and it phosphorylates the mTORC1 subunit raptor; both inhibit mTORC1 and so protein synthesis and cell growth. It phosphorylates and inhibits acetyl-CoA carboxylase, lowering malonyl-CoA, which both reduces fatty acid synthesis and relieves malonyl-CoA inhibition of carnitine palmitoyltransferase 1, so that long-chain fatty acids enter mitochondria for beta-oxidation.

Part 1: Thr172 phosphorylation of the AMPK alpha subunit by an upstream kinase
Upstream AMPK kinases (LKB1 complex or Ca2+/calmodulin-CAMKK)Regulatory Stepampk_upstream_kinases

AMPK is active only when Thr172 in the alpha-subunit activation loop is phosphorylated. The constitutively active LKB1 complex supplies this phosphorylation under energy stress, when nucleotide binding to AMPK gamma makes the site available; calcium-activated CAMKKs supply it independently of adenine nucleotide levels.
