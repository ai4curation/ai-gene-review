GDP-mannose biosynthesis in Drosophila melanogaster

GDP-mannose is the mannose donor for N-glycan precursor assembly, GPI anchors and protein O-mannosylation (through dolichol-phosphate-mannose), and the precursor of GDP-fucose. It is made in the cytosol from hexose 6-phosphates. Hexokinases phosphorylate glucose or mannose; glucose 6-phosphate is isomerized to fructose 6-phosphate by glucose-6-phosphate isomerase (Pgi), and mannose-6-phosphate isomerase (Mpi) interconverts fructose 6-phosphate and mannose 6-phosphate. Phosphomannomutase (Pmm2) converts mannose 6-phosphate to mannose 1-phosphate, and GDP-mannose pyrophosphorylase, formed by the catalytic subunit Gmppb and the regulatory subunit Gmppa, condenses mannose 1-phosphate with GTP to make GDP-mannose.
