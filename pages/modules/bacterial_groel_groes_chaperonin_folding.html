Bacterial GroEL/GroES chaperonin folding

A bacterial cytosolic chaperonin module in which GroEL double rings, capped by GroES, use cycles of ATP binding and hydrolysis to encapsulate non-native polypeptides and release folded clients. The HOCl-activated chaperedoxin CnoX can plug into this system by protecting oxidizing GroEL clients through holdase binding and reversible mixed-disulfide formation, then dissociating when GroES binds.
