Bacterial GroEL/GroES chaperonin folding

Bacterial group I chaperonins cooperate with GroES co-chaperonins to fold non-native proteins using ATP-driven cycles. The canonical cage is distinct from optional CnoX-mediated client protection. E. coli CnoX can bind GroEL, and structural states allow either coexistence with GroES or loss from the capped ring; GroES association does not impose unconditional CnoX displacement.
