Bacterial Kdp potassium-homeostasis system

A reusable bacterial module coupling KdpD/KdpE phosphorelay control to the high-affinity KdpFABC potassium pump. KdpD senses conditions associated with potassium limitation and phosphorylates KdpE, activated KdpE promotes kdpFABC transcription, and the membrane KdpFABC complex couples ATP hydrolysis to potassium uptake. Constitutive potassium channels and lower-affinity uptake systems are outside this module.

The module is species-neutral; Pseudomonas putida KT2440 proteins provide a concrete implementation. The environmental input to KdpD can integrate potassium limitation, ionic strength, and membrane-state signals differently among bacteria, so the first role is described conservatively. KdpC and KdpF are retained as pump components but are not assigned independent catalytic molecular functions. KdpF stabilization is grounded by E. coli biochemical evidence; conservation of that quantitative contribution in KT2440 remains inferential. InterPro:IPR011726 is used pragmatically for KdpF because this 29-residue membrane peptide has no PANTHER family assignment.
