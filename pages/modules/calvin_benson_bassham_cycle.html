Calvin-Benson-Bassham reductive pentose-phosphate cycle

Carbon fixation by the Calvin-Benson-Bassham reductive pentose-phosphate cycle, as a taxon-neutral decomposition: Rubisco carboxylation of ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate, reduction of 3-phosphoglycerate to triose phosphate, and regeneration of the acceptor. Two regulatory parts travel with the cycle because they act on its own enzymes: Rubisco activase, which removes inhibitory sugar phosphates from Rubisco active sites, and the CP12 redox scaffold, which assembles an autoinhibitory PRK-GAPDH complex in the dark and releases it on thioredoxin/NTRC reduction. The cycle consumes the ATP and NADPH produced by MODULE:thylakoid_light_reactions, and is the Rubisco compartment that carbon-concentrating mechanisms feed, including the algal MODULE:pyrenoid_ccm. It is not restricted to oxygenic phototrophs: the same cycle fixes carbon in anoxygenic phototrophs and in chemoautotrophs, which is why it is modelled separately from photosynthesis. The non-CBB fixation routes are siblings rather than parts: MODULE:reductive_tca_cycle, MODULE:wood_ljungdahl_pathway and MODULE:hydroxypropionate_bicycle. The carbon-concentrating mechanisms that raise CO2 at this cycle's Rubisco are also siblings: MODULE:c4_carbon_shuttle and MODULE:cam_carbon_shuttle each include this module by reference, while MODULE:pyrenoid_ccm does not - it models Rubisco carboxylation inline and carries no module_ref - so it feeds this cycle in biology without composing it in the model. Converting it is a separate change against that module.
