Interleukin-34 / CSF1R signaling pathway module

Secreted homodimeric Interleukin-34 (IL34) binds the extracellular D2-D3 immunoglobulin-like junction of the class III receptor tyrosine kinase CSF1R (macrophage colony-stimulating factor 1 receptor), driving receptor homodimerization and trans-autophosphorylation of intracellular tyrosine residues. Phosphorylated CSF1R recruits SH2-domain adaptors and enzymes (GRB2, PI3K regulatory subunit PIK3R1, and PLC-gamma-2) and activates downstream Ras-MAPK/ERK, PI3K-AKT, and STAT3 signaling cascades that promote monocyte survival, macrophage differentiation, and microglial and Langerhans cell homeostasis.
