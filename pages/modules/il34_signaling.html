Interleukin-34 / CSF1R signaling pathway module

Secreted homodimeric Interleukin-34 (IL34) binds the N-terminal immunoglobulin-like domains of the class III receptor tyrosine kinase CSF1R (macrophage colony-stimulating factor 1 receptor), which it shares with the structurally homologous but sequence-unrelated cytokine CSF1. Ligand binding dimerizes the receptor and activates its intracellular tyrosine kinase, initiating signaling that supports monocyte viability, macrophage differentiation, and the tissue-specific maintenance of Langerhans cells and microglia.

Two-step ligand-receptor module for Interleukin-34 (IL34, family PTHR28606) signaling through the class III RTK CSF1R (PTHR24416:SF47). Per ligand-in-signaling conventions (projects/LIGANDS_IN_SIGNALING.md), the IL34 ligand annoton uses GO:0005125 (cytokine activity, an is_a child of GO:0048018 receptor ligand activity) rather than the receptor-binding term GO:0005157 (macrophage colony-stimulating factor receptor binding) that PAINT asserts at PTN002823014; that mismatch is recorded in the PTHR28606 family review. Boundary decisions. (1) Downstream effectors are excluded: the phosphotyrosine-recruited adaptors and enzymes (GRB2, PIK3R1, PLCG2, the PI3K catalytic subunit PIK3CA), the ERK1/2 kinases MAPK1/MAPK3, and STAT3 are shared by CSF1 and IL34 and are generic CSF1R effectors, so they belong in a CSF1R receptor-signaling module, which does not exist yet; Reactome:R-HSA-6787820 summarizes them. An earlier draft of this module split the PI3K holoenzyme across two steps and typed ERK1/2 with the generic GO:0004674 rather than GO:0004707 (MAP kinase activity); both problems should be avoided when that CSF1R module is built. (2) PTPRZ1 is not modelled as an alternative receptor. IL34 binds PTPRZ1 (PMID:30066957, Reactome:R-HSA-8981657), but the only cached signaling evidence is a single study in a CSF1R-deficient glioblastoma line summarized by Reactome, which is too thin to define a PTPRZ1 receptor step with a molecular function. (3) SDC1 binds IL34 and modulates CSF1R signaling (Reactome:R-HSA-9009558); it is a co-receptor modulator, not a pathway step. (4) GO:0038145 (macrophage colony-stimulating factor signaling pathway) is not a module concept because it is defined by CSF1 binding, not IL34 binding; CSF1R carries both GO:0038145 and GO:0061514.
