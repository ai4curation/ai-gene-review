Human serotonergic signalling through ionotropic 5-HT3 receptors

The ionotropic arm of serotonergic transmission. Serotonin is synthesised from tryptophan by TPH2 and DDC, loaded into vesicles by VMAT2 (SLC18A2) and released by calcium-triggered exocytosis. Released serotonin opens 5-HT3 receptors, the only pentameric ligand-gated channels among the serotonin receptors; the other 5-HT receptor classes are G-protein-coupled and are outside this module. Two receptor forms are modelled as alternatives: the 5-HT3A homopentamer, which is calcium-permeable but has a conductance too small to resolve directly, and the 5-HT3A/5-HT3B heteromer, which has a large single-channel conductance and low calcium permeability. The signal is terminated by SERT (SLC6A4), which recaptures serotonin into the presynaptic terminal. Melatonin synthesis, monoamine oxidase degradation and the metabotropic 5-HT receptors are outside this module.

PTHR18945 family review
The family review finds the serotonin-gated activity gain placed too deep in PAINT, at PTN004502671, where three IRDs then cancel it for the alpha9, alpha10 and ZAC branches. The 5-HT3 nodes carry the serotonin-activated channel complex gains. This module therefore names exact subunits and takes its receptor claims from the structural and electrophysiological literature rather than from the family-level propagation.

The subunit stoichiometry of the 5-HT3A/5-HT3B heteromer is not asserted; the paper that identified 5-HT3B did not fix it, and later reports disagree. The homomer is given five copies of 5-HT3A, which the structural work supports. HTR3C, HTR3D and HTR3E exist and inherit the serotonin-activated channel complex term at PAINT node PTN002707828, but their native assemblies are not established, so they are not modelled. TPH1 is the peripheral isoform; the biosynthesis reference covers both. Synthesis, vesicular loading and exocytosis are included by module_ref. No 5-HT3 subunit, VMAT2 or SERT has a gene review in this repository, although TPH2 and DDC do, so status is DRAFT. Module deep research has not been run.
