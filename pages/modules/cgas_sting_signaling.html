cGAS-STING DNA-sensing signaling pathway module

cGAS detects cytosolic double-stranded DNA and synthesises the second messenger 2'3'-cyclic GMP-AMP, which activates the ER adaptor STING1; oligomerised STING1 traffics to the ERGIC/Golgi, recruits and activates TBK1, and TBK1 phosphorylates IRF3 to induce type I interferons.
