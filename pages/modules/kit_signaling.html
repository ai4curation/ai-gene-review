Kit signaling pathway module

Stem cell factor (KIT ligand) binds and dimerizes the class III receptor tyrosine kinase KIT, whose autophosphorylated intracellular tyrosines recruit SH2-domain effectors (GRB2, PI3K p85, SHP2, Src-family kinases) that drive Ras-MAPK, PI3K-AKT, and STAT5 outputs controlling survival, proliferation, and migration of hematopoietic progenitors, mast cells, melanocytes, germ cells, and interstitial cells of Cajal.

GO:0038109
Kit signaling pathway
The module is grounded in the GO biological process term for Kit signaling pathway, defined as the series of molecular signals initiated by stem cell factor binding to the tyrosine kinase receptor KIT, followed by receptor dimerization, autophosphorylation, and phosphorylation of substrates that activate distinct downstream cascades.
PMID:23073628
Stem cell factor receptor/c-Kit: from basic science to clinical implications.
Physiological Reviews summary (Lennartsson and Ronnstrand 2012): SCF is a dimeric ligand that activates c-Kit; activation leads to autophosphorylation, and signaling proteins are recruited through SH2 and PTB interaction domains that bind phosphorylated tyrosines in the intracellular region of c-Kit. c-Kit signaling mediates cell survival, migration, and proliferation and is crucial for hematopoiesis, pigmentation, fertility, and gut movement. Abstract verified against PubMed; used to set the module boundary and the three-step decomposition.
PMID:15526160
Signal transduction via the stem cell factor receptor/c-Kit.
Review (Ronnstrand 2004): ligand stimulation leads to receptor dimerization, activation of intrinsic tyrosine kinase activity, and phosphorylation of key receptor tyrosines that serve as docking sites for SH2-containing signal transduction molecules, which are recruited to the receptor and often activated by receptor-mediated phosphorylation. Abstract verified against PubMed; supports the phosphotyrosine effector-recruitment step.
PMID:17662946
Structural basis for activation of the receptor tyrosine kinase KIT by stem cell factor.
Crystal structures of the KIT ectodomain before and after SCF binding show that KIT dimerization is driven by SCF binding, followed by lateral D4-D4 and D5-D5 contacts; this is the GOA IDA reference for GO:0038109 on human KIT (UniProtKB:P10721). Abstract verified against PubMed.

Curated compact signaling-pathway module built to the same three-step template as the sibling receptor tyrosine kinase modules (pdgfr_signaling, egfr_signaling, fgfr_signaling): ligand engagement, phosphotyrosine effector recruitment, and downstream output. KIT is a class III receptor tyrosine kinase in the same PANTHER family (PTHR24416) as PDGFRA/PDGFRB, CSF1R, and FLT3; the module is kept separate from pdgfr_signaling because GO models Kit signaling as its own pathway term (GO:0038109) with its own PAINT node (PTN002814378) and its own ligand (KITLG, PTHR11574, which has no local PANTHER cache directory, so KITLG is grounded as a UniProt exemplar only). Effector choice follows the reviews cited in evidence: GRB2 and PI3K p85 are the canonical SH2 adaptors recruited to phosphorylated KIT, SHP2 (PTPN11) is the KIT-associated phosphatase that couples to the Ras-ERK branch, and LYN is the Src-family kinase representative for the juxtamembrane phosphotyrosine docking site. Downstream outputs are limited to the three branches with direct GOA support on human KIT (positive regulation of MAPK cascade, PI3K-AKT, and STAT activation); PLCG1 and other Ras-branch components are deliberately left to broader modules. PTN anchors are included only when the exemplar accession appears in an IBD seed row in interpro/panther; roles without local PAINT seeds remain grounded by verified UniProt exemplar accessions. UniProt protein names were taken verbatim from the UniProt REST API; PANTHER family labels from interpro/panther/panther.obo; PMIDs and titles were checked against PubMed. None of KIT, KITLG, GRB2, PIK3R1, LYN, SOS1, PIK3CA, or STAT5A yet has a gene review in this repository, and gocams/index.tsv contains no KIT activity, so this module is not yet cross-checked against a production GO-CAM. Falcon integration (2026-09-25): Falcon findings are linked as evidence; the report's characterization of STAT5 as a conditional, lineage-dependent output is reflected in the output-step description and in the recorded knowledge gap. Automatically extracted open-question headings were not retained as knowledge_gap entries because they were not manually curated, and machine-generated Falcon citations remain curation support rather than adjudicated references.

Part 3: ras-mapk, pi3k-akt, and stat output
Ras-MAPK, PI3K-AKT, and STAT5 outputRegulatory Stepkit_ras_pi3k_stat_output

Recruited effectors activate three canonical branches, Ras-MAPK through GRB2-bound SOS, PI3K-AKT through the p85-p110 lipid kinase, and STAT5 transcriptional output, which together drive survival, proliferation, and migration. The Ras-MAPK and PI3K-AKT branches are robust and partly redundant; STAT5 activation is a conditional, lineage-dependent output.
