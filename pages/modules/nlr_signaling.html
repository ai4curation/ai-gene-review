Family review supports GO:0004709 for PANTHER:PTHR46716:SF1. MAP3K activity defines the bilaterian TAK1 clade. TAK1 phosphorylates MKK3, MKK6 and MKK4, fly Tak1 is the MAPKKK of the JNK pathway, and MOM-4/Tak1 activates the MAPK-like LIT-1/NLK. SF1 is the family's only subfamily, so the scope is stated as SF1 with the Dictyostelium kinase Phg2 excepted. The human MAP3K7 gene review confirms this scoping: it accepts MAP3K activity (IDA on MKK3/MKK6 and MKK4/MKK7) as its core MF and accepts the Bilateria-node IBA. The C. elegans mom-4 gene review also accepts MAP3K activity on direct evidence. Wild-type but not kinase-dead (D176N) MOM-4 phosphorylates the LIT-1 activation-loop Thr220 (TXE motif) in vitro, with no MAP2K in between. The term therefore holds for MOM-4 even though MOM-4 is not a leaf of the PANTHER 19 tree.

Part 2: nlr adaptor assembly
NLR adaptor assemblyRegulatory Stepnlr_adaptor_assembly

RIPK2 and ASC/PYCARD link activated NLRs to downstream signaling complexes. On the NOD1/NOD2 branch, RIPK2 is ubiquitinated by XIAP (K63-linked) and LUBAC (linear chains), forming the scaffold that recruits TAK1-TAB and IKK. On the NLRP3 branch, ASC nucleates caspase-1 activation. XIAP and LUBAC are not modeled as parts here.

Part 3: tak1 kinase activation
TAK1-TAB kinase complex activation (NOD-RIPK2 branch)Regulatory Steptak1_tab_kinase_activation

On the NOD1/NOD2 branch, RIPK2 carries K63-linked chains added by XIAP and linear chains added by LUBAC. These chains recruit the TAK1-TAB complex (TAK1 with TAB1 and the ubiquitin-binding subunits TAB2/TAB3), and TAK1 is activated. TAK1 phosphorylates IKKbeta (canonical NF-kappaB) and MAP2Ks (p38/JNK). This is the NF-kappaB/MAPK output of the module. RIPK2 acts here as a ubiquitinated scaffold; its own kinase activity is not required for this output. The step is not on the NLRP3-ASC-caspase-1 inflammasome branch.
