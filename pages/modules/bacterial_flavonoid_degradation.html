Bacterial naringenin A-ring degradation

A species-neutral model of the bacterial fde pathway that converts naringenin through hydroxylation and oxygenolytic A-ring cleavage to smaller aromatic products. The module exposes six reaction parts corresponding to KEGG R13074-R13079. FdeE (genetics, metabolite profiling and purified-enzyme assays) and FdeC (genetics and metabolite profiling) have experimental support in Herbaspirillum seropedicae, and the fde operon as a whole is naringenin-induced and required for naringenin degradation, whereas the individual roles assigned to FdeD, FdeH, FdeJ, FdeB, and FdeI remain pathway and orthology predictions. KEGG assigns Fde orthologs to roughly 80-140 bacterial genomes per protein, so the module is written for bacteria generally, with H. seropedicae SmR1 and Pseudomonas putida KT2440 as exemplars. The module therefore distinguishes required pathway roles from directly established molecular functions and does not treat every co-assigned protein as an independently characterized catalyst.

Scope: the fde route is bacterial but not restricted to Herbaspirillum or Pseudomonas; KEGG lists each Fde orthology group (K26179-K26185) in about 80-140 genomes, so the module stays at NCBITaxon:2 with H. seropedicae SmR1 (the only organism with experimental data) and P. putida KT2440 as exemplars. No third organism was added because no other fde locus has experimental support. Family groundings follow the PANTHER assignments of the representative proteins (as in PR #5076), except that FdeE is grounded to subfamily PTHR13789:SF309, where the family review scopes the NAD(P)H-monooxygenase term. Every PANTHER family used is far broader than its Fde orthology group and carries no flavonoid-specific function; the family reviews record this. One FAMILY_FUNCTION_UNREVIEWED warning is expected: the PTHR22946 review leaves carboxylic ester hydrolase activity UNRESOLVED because its SF12 subfamily mixes ester and carbon-carbon hydrolases. The FdeH, FdeJ and both FdeI annotons carry free-text functions without GO ids (NO_FUNCTION_ID warnings) because no reaction class is established for them; the PTHR43211 review leaves carboxy-lyase activity UNRESOLVED. FdeC has no PANTHER family in its UniProt record, so no family id is asserted for it. FdeG (Hsero_1009, a putative cyclase whose mutant was studied) and FdeA have no KEGG reaction assignment and are not modeled as parts.
