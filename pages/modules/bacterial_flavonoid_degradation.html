Bacterial naringenin A-ring degradation

The Fde-associated oxidative route in Pseudomonadota begins with naringenin hydroxylation and oxygenolytic A-ring cleavage. Herbaspirillum seropedicae SmR1 supplies FdeE/FdeC mutant and metabolite evidence, and purified FdeE establishes NADPH-dependent hydroxylation. Related loci and whole-cell phenotypes extend the comparison across alpha-, beta- and gammaproteobacteria. Six chemical steps retain the proposed KEGG R13074-R13079 sequence, with unverified late enzyme assignments explicitly separated from demonstrated entry chemistry. FdeD and FdeH are associated proteins whose reaction position and physiological necessity remain unresolved. Pseudomonas putida KT2440 and Bradyrhizobium diazoefficiens USDA110 provide sequence-grounded comparisons, not demonstrated complete pathway reconstructions.

Scope is Pseudomonadota (NCBITaxon:1224), based on the experimentally studied Herbaspirillum route, independent ORS285 locus evidence, and a dated census spanning alpha-, beta- and gammaproteobacteria. All seven KO groups co-occur in 50 sampled genomes of this phylum; two Actinomycetota carry individual FdeE assignments. This bounds the present model without asserting universal absence elsewhere. KEGG coassignment and broad PANTHER hits alone do not establish pathway capacity. The 21 reviewed representative proteins span three classes; only HERSS has the step-specific assays used here. H. rubrisubalbicans M1 provides an additional phenotype but lacks a resolved UniProt mapping for its FdeE, so no other strain is substituted. PP_3199 retains sequence-based FdeE support although current KEGG gene and link queries no longer assign K26180; historical analysis is preserved. Family identifiers describe broader evolutionary groups, not substrate-specific Fde clades. The FdeC group has no common verified family identifier across its exemplars: BRADU Q89GB8 maps to PTHR21366:SF14, while HERSS lacks a PANTHER assignment. Keep that group as a sequence-grounded free-text family rather than applying the BRADU identifier to every member. FdeA insertion in the 2013 study was probably polar and did not establish autonomous FdeA chemistry. FdeG was mutated in the 2016 study, but its outcome is not accessible in the abstract; exclusion from assigned reaction steps is not evidence of dispensability. Optional FdeD/FdeH context parts encode unresolved dependence, not experimentally demonstrated deletion tolerance. The six-step chemical chain remains a hypothesis beyond the demonstrated entry; neither model satisfaction nor family membership establishes native flux. See the module notes and audit evidence for source access and assay boundaries.
