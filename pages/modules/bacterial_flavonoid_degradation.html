Bacterial naringenin A-ring degradation

A species-neutral model of the bacterial fde pathway that converts naringenin through hydroxylation and oxygenolytic A-ring cleavage to smaller aromatic products. The module exposes six reaction parts corresponding to KEGG R13074-R13079. FdeE and FdeC have experimental genetic and metabolite support in Herbaspirillum seropedicae, whereas the roles assigned to FdeD, FdeH, FdeJ, FdeB, and FdeI remain pathway and orthology predictions. The module therefore distinguishes required pathway roles from directly established molecular functions and does not treat every co-assigned protein as an independently characterized catalyst.
