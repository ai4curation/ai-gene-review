Microsomal fatty-acid elongation cycle (ELOVL or KCS -> KAR -> HACD -> ECR)

Most fatty acids longer than the C16 palmitate made by cytosolic fatty-acid synthase are produced by the microsomal elongation system, a four-reaction cycle on the cytosolic face of the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane that adds two carbons per turn. Each round begins with the rate-limiting, substrate-selective condensation of an acyl-CoA with malonyl-CoA to a 3-oxoacyl-CoA (releasing CO2), catalysed by one of seven fatty-acid elongases (ELOVL1-7) whose chain-length and saturation preferences define which fatty acids a cell makes: ELOVL1/3/6 handle saturated and monounsaturated substrates (ELOVL6 the C16->C18 lipogenic step, ELOVL1 the C22->C26 very-long-chain step for sphingolipids/myelin, ELOVL3 skin/brown-fat lipids), ELOVL2/5 the C20/C22 polyunsaturated fatty acids en route to DHA, ELOVL7 C18-C20 substrates, and ELOVL4 the unique ultra-long-chain (C26-C38) fatty acids of retina, skin and brain. The 3-oxoacyl-CoA is then reduced by the NADPH-dependent 3-ketoacyl-CoA reductase HSD17B12 (KAR) to (3R)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA, dehydrated by the 3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydratases HACD1/HACD2 to (2E)-enoyl-CoA, and reduced by the NADPH-dependent trans-2-enoyl-CoA reductase TECR to the two-carbon-extended saturated acyl-CoA, which re-enters the cycle. The reductase/dehydratase/reductase enzymes are shared across all ELOVLs; substrate specificity resides in the elongase. Inherited defects span the products: ELOVL1 (IKSHD ichthyosis/spastic quadriplegia), ELOVL4 (Stargardt-like macular dystrophy, SCA34, ichthyosis), HACD1 (congenital myopathy), TECR (intellectual disability), reflecting the roles of very-long-chain and ultra-long-chain fatty acids in skin barrier, myelin, retina and muscle. Plants run the same four-step cycle but use an unrelated condensing enzyme, the thiolase-fold 3-ketoacyl-CoA synthases (KCS; e.g. FAE1/KCS18 for seed erucic acid, CER6/KCS6 for cuticular wax), while the downstream steps are carried out by orthologs of the animal enzymes (KCR1, PAS2, ECR/CER10 in Arabidopsis). The condensation step is therefore modelled as alternative enzyme families, and the three shared steps list animal and plant exemplars.

Microsomal fatty-acid elongation cycle (GO:0030497 + GO:0042761), grounded to ten completed human gene reviews plus Arabidopsis (FAE1, CUT1, KCR1, PAS2, ECR) and field pennycress (FAE1) reviews, with pennycress orthologs as additional examples; all ER-membrane enzymes of the fatty acid elongase complex (GO:0009923). CONDENSATION (rate-limiting, substrate-selective): six of the seven ELOVLs reviewed here (ELOVL1 Q9BW60, ELOVL2 Q9NXB9, ELOVL3 Q9HB03, ELOVL4 Q9GZR5, ELOVL6 Q9H5J4, ELOVL7 A1L3X0) share PTHR11157 and all carry GO:0009922 fatty acid elongase activity (the single correct MF — no finer chain-length GO term exists; specificity noted per-gene: ELOVL6 C16->C18, ELOVL1 sat/monounsat VLCFA, ELOVL2 C20/C22 PUFA->DHA, ELOVL3 skin/brown-fat, ELOVL4 ultra-long-chain C26-C38, ELOVL7 C18-C20; ELOVL5 already reviewed earlier). The other three steps are SHARED across all ELOVLs: HSD17B12/KAR (Q53GQ0 PTHR43899, GO:0141040 3-oxoacyl-CoA reductase — its legacy 17-beta-HSD/estradiol activity is non-core), HACD1 (B0YJ81, muscle) + HACD2 (Q6Y1H2, ubiquitous) sharing PTHR11035 (GO:0102158 (3R)-3-hydroxyacyl-CoA dehydratase), and TECR (Q9NZ01 PTHR10556, GO:0102758 trans-2-enoyl-CoA reductase). Recurrent curation note: several reviews flag PMID:10970790 (HELO1 = the ELOVL5/ELOVL2 clone) as a WRONG_IDENTIFIER Reactome-EXP source though the elongase MF itself is correct. GO term ids/labels verified against the local go.db. Products feed sphingolipid/ceramide synthesis (curated modules), membrane phospholipids and skin/myelin/retina lipids. Disorders: ELOVL1 IKSHD; ELOVL4 Stargardt-like STGD3 / SCA34 / ISQMR; HACD1 congenital myopathy; TECR intellectual disability (MRT14).

Part 1: condensation (rate-limiting; substrate selectivity)
Condensing enzyme (ELOVL or 3-ketoacyl-CoA synthase)Reactioncondensation

The condensation of acyl-CoA with malonyl-CoA is carried out by two unrelated enzyme families that converged on the same reaction (GO:0009922): the membrane-embedded ELO/ELOVL elongases of animals and fungi, and the thiolase-fold 3-ketoacyl-CoA synthases (KCS/FAE1) of plants. The downstream reductase, dehydratase and reductase steps are homologous across both lineages.
