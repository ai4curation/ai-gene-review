Dolichol phosphate and lipid-linked sugar-donor supply for glycosylation

Assembly of the dolichol-linked oligosaccharide (and of GPI anchors and protein O- and C-mannosylation) depends on a dolichyl-phosphate (Dol-P) lipid carrier, on the cytosolic nucleotide sugars GDP-mannose and UDP-glucose, and on two lipid-linked monosaccharide donors made from them, dolichyl-phosphate-mannose (Dol-P-Man) and dolichyl-phosphate-glucose (Dol-P-Glc). GDP-mannose is made in the cytosol from fructose 6-phosphate by mannose-6-phosphate isomerase, phosphomannomutase and GDP-mannose pyrophosphorylase; UDP-glucose is made from glucose 6-phosphate by phosphoglucomutase and UDP-glucose pyrophosphorylase. Dol-P is made at the endoplasmic-reticulum membrane by a three-step detour from polyprenol: an SDR-family polyprenol dehydrogenase oxidises polyprenol to polyprenal, the steroid-5-alpha-reductase-like polyprenal reductase saturates the alpha-isoprene unit to give dolichal, and the same SDR enzyme reduces dolichal to dolichol, which dolichol kinase phosphorylates with CTP. Dol-P is then charged with mannose from GDP-mannose by Dol-P-Man synthase and with glucose from UDP-glucose by Dol-P-Glc synthase (ALG5). Dol-P-Man synthase exists in two architectures: a single-subunit enzyme with its own C-terminal membrane anchor (budding yeast, Ustilago, trypanosomes) and a soluble catalytic subunit held at the membrane by two small accessory subunits (animals, fission yeast). In mammals a utilisation factor (MPDU1/Lec35) is additionally required for lumenal use of both lipid-linked donors.

ALIGN (YeastPathways project, 2026-10). Scope widened to the crosswalk boundary "GDP-mannose, Dol-P-Man and Dol-P-Glc supply": the cytosolic GDP-mannose (PMI40/SEC53/PSA1) and UDP-glucose (PGM1/PGM2/UGP1) branches from YeastCyc PWY3O-123 and PWY3O-1565 were added as sub-pathways, the module was made eukaryote-wide, and S. cerevisiae and S. pombe exemplars were added to every role. This module intentionally overlaps dolichyl_phosphate_biosynthesis at the terminal dolichol-synthesis detour and DOLK/SEC59 phosphorylation step: that older module follows de novo Dol-P carrier production from cis-PT through DOLPP1 and DOLK, while this donor-supply module repeats the detour and kinase because they directly replenish the Dol-P input for Dol-P-Man and Dol-P-Glc synthesis. The revised detour is modeled as three steps (DHRSX/TDA5 polyprenol dehydrogenase, SRD5A3/DFG10 polyprenal reductase, DHRSX/TDA5 dolichal reductase; PMID:38821050, PMID:42201967), matching the GO:0160198 polyprenal reductase term already used. DHRSX and TDA5 fall in different PANTHER families, so that role carries no family id. The cis-prenyltransferase (RER2/SRT1 + NUS1; rer2/nus1) and the polyprenyl-diphosphate dephosphorylation that produce polyprenol are upstream and outside this module, although the PomBase model includes rer2 and nus1. Dol-P-Man synthase is now an EXACTLY_ONE variant set: single-subunit, C-terminally anchored DPM1 (S. cerevisiae) versus the three-subunit DPM1/DPM2/DPM3 complex (human, S. pombe), following PMID:9223280 and PMID:10835346. Both catalytic forms share PANTHER PTHR43398:SF1, so the anchored class is represented by a concrete gene product. MPDU1 is marked optional because neither yeast has a PTHR12226 member. YeastCyc corrections: the PSA1 reaction should be the GTP-dependent EC 2.7.7.13 (GO:0004475), not the GDP/phosphate EC 2.7.7.22 reaction the SGD GO-CAM encodes (GO:0008928); YHL012W is listed beside UGP1 but is uncharacterised. GO-CAM disagreements: both YeastPathways models place the ER-membrane enzymes DPM1 and ALG5 in cytosol and use broad processes (GO:0006013 mannose metabolic process, GO:0016051 carbohydrate biosynthetic process). The cytosolic phosphoglucomutase and UDP-glucose pyrophosphorylase steps are shared with glycogen, trehalose and galactose metabolism; they are included because they are the dedicated route to the Dol-P-Glc donor in the source pathway. This module supplies the Dol-P carrier used by ALG7/DPAGT1 in n_glycan_llo_assembly_cytoplasmic, GDP-mannose for ALG1/ALG2/ALG11 there, and the Dol-P-Man/Dol-P-Glc donors used in n_glycan_llo_assembly_lumenal, GPI-anchor synthesis and O-mannosylation. Disorders: PMM2-CDG, MPI-CDG, GMPPB dystroglycanopathy, SRD5A3-, DHRSX-, DOLK-, DPM1-, DPM2-, DPM3-, MPDU1- and ALG5-CDG.
