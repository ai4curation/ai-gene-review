Lysosomal glycoprotein (N-glycan) degradation; NEU1/GLB1/HEX/MAN2B1/MANBA/FUCA1/AGA/NAGA

Glycoproteins delivered to the lysosome are proteolysed to glycopeptides whose glycans are then dismantled by lumenal exoglycosidases working from both ends. From the non-reducing end, N-glycan antennae are trimmed residue by residue: capping sialic acid (NEU1), terminal galactose (GLB1), antennal N-acetylglucosamine (beta-hexosaminidase, HEXA/HEXB), alpha-linked mannoses (MAN2B1) and the core beta-mannose (MANBA); terminal alpha-N-acetylgalactosamine of blood group A and mucin-type determinants is removed by NAGA. From the reducing end, the core alpha1,6-fucose is removed by FUCA1, which is required because core fucosylation blocks aspartylglucosaminidase (AGA); AGA then cleaves the N-glycosidic GlcNAc-Asn bond, separating the glycan from the asparagine residue. Loss of any step causes a glycoproteinosis (oligosaccharidosis) with lysosomal storage and urinary excretion of the corresponding oligosaccharides or glycoasparagines: sialidosis, GM1 gangliosidosis, Sandhoff disease, alpha- and beta-mannosidosis, fucosidosis, aspartylglucosaminuria and NAGA deficiency.

Steps 6-7 (FUCA1, AGA) and the GLB1 and NAGA steps are grounded in completed human gene reviews; the NEU1, HEXA/HEXB, MAN2B1 and MANBA steps are grounded only in UniProt/EC and await gene reviews (NEU1, HEXA and HEXB reviews are INITIALIZED; MAN2B1 and MANBA have none). The reducing-end di-N-acetylchitobiase CTBS (UniProtKB:Q01459), which cleaves the chitobiose core after AGA in primates and rodents, is not modelled as a part because no GO molecular function term for its activity was identified. The cytosolic ENGASE/NGLY1 route for misfolded ER glycoproteins is a separate pathway. GLB1, NEU1 and the hexosaminidases are shared with the glycosphingolipid and keratan sulfate degradation modules; sialic acid released by NEU1 is exported by SLC17A5 (sialic_acid_metabolism module). The order of the non-reducing-end steps is the canonical order for complex N-glycans; for oligomannose glycans steps 1-3 do not apply.
