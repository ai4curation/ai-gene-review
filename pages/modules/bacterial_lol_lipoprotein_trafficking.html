Bacterial Lol-dependent outer-membrane lipoprotein trafficking

A species-neutral diderm-bacterial module for ATP-dependent release of outer-membrane-destined mature lipoproteins from the inner membrane by LolCDE, periplasmic carriage by LolA, and delivery into the outer membrane by LolB. Lipoprotein maturation and inner-membrane retention decisions are upstream of this module.

OpenScientist PSEPK Lol-pathway satisfiability review
The report independently identifies lolC/PP_2154, lolD/PP_2155, lolE/PP_2156, lolA/PP_4003, and lolB/PP_0724 as the complete canonical KT2440 system and finds no biological gap.

Reusable boundary: this module begins with extraction of an already mature outer-membrane-destined lipoprotein and ends with outer-membrane delivery. Lgt/LspA/Lnt maturation is upstream and substrate-specific lipoprotein function is downstream. Pseudomonas putida KT2440 provides exemplars for all five canonical Lol components. The modeled three-part relay is the canonical LolCDE-LolA-LolB implementation, not a claim that every diderm lineage has LolB or a heterodimeric LolC/LolE extractor.
