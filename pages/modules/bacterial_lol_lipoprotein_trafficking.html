Bacterial Lol-dependent outer-membrane lipoprotein trafficking

A diderm-bacterial mechanism that extracts mature lipoproteins from the periplasmic leaflet of the inner membrane and delivers them for incorporation into the outer membrane. ATP-coupled release is performed by a LolC1-LolE1-LolD2 or homodimeric LolF2-LolD2 assembly, without translocating cargo across the inner-membrane bilayer. A periplasmic LolA carrier then transfers cargo to a compatible classical or distant LolB-like inserter, while bifunctional carrier/inserter proteins provide an alternative delivery architecture. The machinery and substrate selectivity vary among lineages, and conditional alternative delivery can preserve some lipoprotein localization when known LolA/LolB proteins are absent.
