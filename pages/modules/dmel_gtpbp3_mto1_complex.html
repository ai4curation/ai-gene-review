GTPBP3-MTO1 mitochondrial tRNA modification complex in Drosophila melanogaster

GTPBP3 and MTO1, the mitochondrial orthologs of bacterial MnmE and MnmG, act together to install a taurinomethyl group at the wobble uridine (U34) of several mitochondrial tRNAs, forming taurinomethyluridine (taum5U). This modification supports accurate decoding during mitochondrial translation. GTPBP3 is a GTPase that binds the methylene donor, and MTO1 is an FAD-dependent enzyme. In Drosophila the orthologs are CG18528 (GTPBP3) and CG4610 (MTO1).
