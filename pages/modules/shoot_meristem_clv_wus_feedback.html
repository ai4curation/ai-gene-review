CLAVATA-WUSCHEL negative feedback loop in shoot apical meristem stem-cell homeostasis

The size of the stem-cell pool in the shoot apical meristem is held steady by a two-cell-layer negative feedback loop. The homeodomain transcription factor WUSCHEL (WUS) is made in the organizing center (OC), moves through plasmodesmata into the overlying central zone, and there both specifies stem-cell fate and directly activates CLAVATA3 (CLV3). CLV3 is processed and secreted as an arabinosylated 12/13-amino-acid CLE peptide. It is perceived at the plasma membrane of the underlying cells by parallel receptor systems: the LRR receptor kinase CLV1 and its paralogs BAM1/BAM2, which bind the peptide directly, and a heteromeric complex of the kinase-less LRR receptor-like protein CLV2 with the pseudokinase CORYNE (CRN). Whether CLV2/CRN binds the ligand directly is disputed; it acts at least as a co-receptor. Receptor signalling, relayed through CIK co-receptor kinases and PBL-type cytoplasmic kinases, represses WUS transcription and confines the OC. More stem cells therefore make more CLV3, which reduces WUS and so limits stem-cell number. When CLV1 is lost, BAM genes are de-repressed in the meristem centre and partly compensate. The KNOX homeodomain protein SHOOT MERISTEMLESS (STM), which keeps meristem cells undifferentiated and interacts genetically with the CLV genes, is shown only as optional context. RPK2, the CIK and PBL relay kinases, CLE processing, floral-meristem termination by AGAMOUS, and the root CLE40-ACR4 and CLE45-BAM3 pathways are outside this module boundary.

Part 3: CLV3 perception at the plasma membrane
Parallel CLV3 receptor systemsSignaling Pathwayclv3_perception

Two receptor systems that act at the same time sense CLV3 at the plasma membrane of OC and rib-zone cells: CLV1-family receptor kinases (CLV1, BAM1, BAM2) and the CLV2/CRN complex. Either system alone gives partial repression of WUS. Loss of both (for example clv1 bam1 or clv1 clv2) strongly enlarges the meristem. CIK co-receptor kinases and PBL34/35/36 cytoplasmic kinases relay the signal and are outside this module boundary.

CLV1-family LRR receptor kinasesProtein Complexclv1_bam_receptor_kinases

CLV1 homomers and CLV1/BAM heteromers. Their LRR ectodomains bind CLV3 directly, and their kinase domains phosphorylate CIK and PBL substrates. In the wild type, BAM1 is mainly peripheral. It is up-regulated centrally in clv1 mutants, and clv1 bam1 double mutants have strongly enlarged meristems with expanded WUS.

CLV2/CRN co-receptor complexProtein Complexclv2_crn_coreceptor_complex

A heteromer of the kinase-less LRR receptor-like protein CLV2 and the transmembrane pseudokinase CRN. The two subunits assemble through their transmembrane domains, and each is needed for the other to leave the ER. Radioligand assays reported CLE binding by CLV2 (Kd about 32 nM), but photoaffinity labelling did not detect it. The complex is therefore modelled as a co-receptor whose signalling depends on the CRN pseudokinase domain and on CIK kinases.
