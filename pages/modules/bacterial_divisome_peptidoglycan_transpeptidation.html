Bacterial divisome peptidoglycan polymerization and crosslinking

A septal peptidoglycan-synthesis module for the bacterial divisome core in which the SEDS glycosyltransferase FtsW polymerizes lipid II-derived glycan strands and the class B penicillin-binding protein FtsI/PBP3 forms 4-3 peptide crosslinks at the division septum. FtsW and FtsI form the cognate division synthase pair; upstream FtsZ-ring positioning, precursor lipid II synthesis, class A PBP backup polymerases, and downstream cell separation are outside this focused module.
