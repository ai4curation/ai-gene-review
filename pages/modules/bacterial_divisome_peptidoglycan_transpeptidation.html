Bacterial divisome peptidoglycan polymerization and crosslinking

A focused FtsW/class-B-PBP septal peptidoglycan-synthesis mechanism with two required reactions: lipid-II-derived glycan polymerization and serine-dependent D,D peptide crosslinking. FtsW requires a compatible bPBP activation interface, whereas crosslink catalysis can be supplied conditionally by Bacillus PBP3 while physical PBP2B remains present. The acceptor amino group may lie on a stem peptide or an attached bridge; mDAP is not universal. Sampled E. coli, Bacillus, Staphylococcus and Streptococcus implementations illustrate this mechanism, which is not a universal description of bacterial division. Precursor supply, divisome positioning and upstream activation, class-A-PBP alternatives and cell separation lie outside this focused boundary.
