Thylakoid light reactions

The light-driven electron transport and photophosphorylation half of oxygenic photosynthesis, as a taxon-neutral decomposition: light harvesting by the antenna, water oxidation at photosystem II, plastoquinol oxidation and proton translocation by the cytochrome b6f complex, the inter-photosystem mobile carrier, P700 photo-oxidation and ferredoxin reduction at photosystem I, NADPH production by ferredoxin-NADP+ reductase, and ATP synthesis from the proton-motive force. Phrased as functions, complexes and pathway segments rather than a fixed gene list, so it can represent cyanobacterial, algal and plant implementations. Carbon fixation is a separate module (MODULE:calvin_benson_bassham_cycle), which consumes the ATP and NADPH this module supplies. Photoprotection and cyclic/pseudocyclic electron balancing stay with the parent MODULE:oxygenic_photosynthesis, since they regulate the ratio between the two halves rather than belonging to either. Anoxygenic photosynthesis (single reaction center, non-water electron donors) is out of scope.
