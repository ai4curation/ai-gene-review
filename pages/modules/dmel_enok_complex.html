Enok histone acetyltransferase complex in Drosophila melanogaster

Enok (Enoki mushroom) is the Drosophila MOZ/MORF (KAT6) family MYST histone acetyltransferase. It forms a complex with the bromodomain-PHD scaffold Br140 (BRPF homolog), the ING-family protein Ing5 and Eaf6, the same subunit composition as the mammalian MOZ/MORF complexes. Enok acetylates histone H3 and was first identified through its requirement for mushroom body neuroblast proliferation.
