Gamma-tubulin complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

Gamma-tubulin complexes nucleate microtubules at centrosomes, spindles and other microtubule-organizing centres. The basic unit is the gamma-tubulin small complex (gamma-TuSC), two gamma-tubulin molecules bound to Grip84 (GCP2) and Grip91 (GCP3). In the larger gamma-tubulin ring complex (gamma-TuRC), several gamma-TuSCs assemble with additional GCP-family proteins (Grip75, Grip128, Grip163) and Grip71 into a ring that templates the 13-protofilament microtubule. Drosophila has two gamma-tubulins: gammaTub23C, used in most tissues, and gammaTub37C, which is maternally supplied and acts in the female germline and early embryo. In the testis a separate gamma-TuRC built from testis-specific t-Grip84, t-Grip91 and t-Grip128 paralogs acts in spermatogenesis.
