SRP-dependent cotranslational targeting in Drosophila melanogaster

The signal recognition particle (SRP) recognizes signal sequences and transmembrane segments as they emerge from the ribosome, slows elongation and delivers the ribosome-nascent chain complex to the SRP receptor on the endoplasmic reticulum membrane. SRP is a ribonucleoprotein built on the 7SL RNA. Its Alu domain (SRP9 and SRP14) arrests elongation, and its S domain (SRP19, SRP54, SRP68 and SRP72) binds the signal sequence through the methionine-rich domain of the GTPase SRP54. The receptor is a heterodimer of the GTPases SRalpha and SRbeta. GTP-dependent interaction of SRP54 with SRalpha transfers the nascent chain to the Sec61 translocon, and reciprocal GTP hydrolysis releases SRP from its receptor.
