Plant NLR resistosome activation and signalling

Effector-triggered immunity by plant NB-LRR receptors. Direct or indirect recognition of a pathogen effector switches the NB-ARC domain from ADP- to ATP-bound and drives oligomerisation into a resistosome. The N-terminal signalling domain then determines the output chemistry: coiled-coil (CC) domains assemble a funnel that acts as a calcium-permeable cation channel, whereas TIR domains assemble paired active sites with NAD+-cleaving activity whose nucleotide products are received by EDS1-family heterodimers, which in turn recruit helper RNLs that are themselves calcium channels. Both branches converge on Ca2+ influx and the hypersensitive response.

Boundary. The module starts at effector recognition and ends at the Ca2+-conducting resistosome or RNL channel. Upstream PRR/PTI signalling and the downstream transcriptional and salicylate programmes are separate biology and are left out. Why one module with variant sets rather than separate CNL and TNL modules. The two branches share the same backbone - LRR-mediated recognition, NB-ARC nucleotide switch, oligomerisation - and converge on the same Ca2+ output. Splitting them would duplicate parts 1 and 2 and hide the convergence, so the N-terminal domain class is modelled as the variation axis instead. Part 3 is optional because it is reached only from the TIR variant. Deliberately kept as prose, not modelled as annotons: (1) NRC networks. The helper NRCs of asterids form hexameric resistosomes and act downstream of sensor CNLs without being incorporated into the active complex. The key structures (NbNRC2 PDB 9FP6, SlNRC3 9RI9, NbNRC4 9CC8), the AVRcap1b inhibition mechanism and the inositol-phosphate cofactor come from very recent work and in several cases from preprints cited by PMID:41614442 (Guo et al. 2025, Seager et al. 2025). The activation-and-release model would also need a connection type this module cannot yet assert cleanly, since no hetero-oligomer between sensor and helper has been demonstrated. Revisit when the primary papers are published and the NRC accessions are in reviewed UniProt entries; N. benthamiana NRC2 has no reviewed accession at present. (2) Other CNL architectures. Sr35 (wheat) is a directly-recognising pentameric CNL and would fit the cnl_direct variant, but its only UniProt record is the unreviewed S5ABD6, whose protein name is "CNL9"; rather than attach a confusing label, the direct-recognition CNL case is represented here by the TNL direct variant and by this note. MLA13 forms a heterodimer with its effector with no resistosome detected, and CCG10-NLRs such as RPS2 may be octameric - both are single recent reports and neither is modelled. (3) 2'cADPR. It is a biomarker of TNL activation and a plausible precursor of pRib-AMP, but it does not itself activate the EDS1 complexes, so it is not modelled as a messenger of this pathway. (4) NRG1C. The truncated NRG1C variant inhibits ESN signalling by sequestering EDS1-SAG101. This is negative regulation of part 3 rather than a step of it. Channel activities on ZAR1, ADR1 and NRG1.1 are asserted at module level from electrophysiology, and GOA currently carries no calcium-channel annotation for any of them. They are recorded here as module claims with their primary evidence; whether they should become gene annotations is a question for the respective gene reviews (ARATH EDS1, PAD4, RPM1 and RPS2 reviews exist; ZAR1, ADR1, NRG1.1, RPP1 and SAG101 have no gene folder yet).

Part 1: effector recognition and resistosome assembly
Effector recognition and resistosome assemblyRegulatory Stepeffector_recognition_resistosome

The LRR domain, often extended by a C-terminal jelly-roll/Ig-like domain in TNLs, either binds the effector directly or monitors effector-induced modification of a host decoy. Recognition releases ADP from the NB-ARC domain; subsequent ATP binding and oligomerisation produce the resistosome. Stoichiometry tracks the N-terminal domain class rather than the recognition mode.

Variant set: Receptor architecture and recognition mode by N-terminal signalling domain class and effector recognition mode (Exactly One)
CNL with indirect recognition via a decoy kinase (ZAR1)Regulatory Stepcnl_indirect_recognition

ZAR1 is pre-complexed with the pseudokinase RKS1 through its LRR. The Xanthomonas effector AvrAC uridylylates the host kinase PBL2, and uridylylated PBL2 is recognised exclusively through RKS1. Ligand binding stabilises the RKS1 activation segment, which sterically blocks ADP binding by ZAR1; ATP binding then drives assembly of a wheel-like pentameric resistosome.

Part 2: resistosome output chemistry
Resistosome output chemistryRegulatory Stepresistosome_output

The assembled resistosome converts recognition into one of two biochemical outputs, set by the N-terminal domain class. CC domains refold so that the N-terminal amphipathic alpha-1 helix protrudes from the ring and inserts into the plasma membrane, forming a funnel that conducts Ca2+. TIR domains instead become NAD+-cleaving enzymes, generating nucleotide second messengers rather than conducting ions themselves.

Variant set: CC channel versus TIR enzyme output by N-terminal signalling domain chemistry (Exactly One)
CC-domain calcium-permeable channelReactioncc_calcium_channel_output

In the pentameric CNL resistosome the alpha-1 helices form a funnel-shaped pore. The channel is cation-selective and Ca2+-permeable, and a conserved acidic residue in the pore is required both for conductance and for cell death.

Part 3: second-messenger relay to helper RNLs (optional)
EDS1-family receptor modules and helper RNL channelsRegulatory Stepeds1_rnl_relay

This part runs only downstream of the TIR branch. EDS1 heterodimerises with PAD4 or SAG101; the heterodimer is the receptor for the TIR-derived nucleotides, binding them in a pocket formed by the two EP domains. Ligand binding rotates the partner EP domain, which licenses association with a helper RNL through the RNL LRR domain. The recruited RNL is itself a Ca2+-permeable channel, so the TIR branch reaches the same Ca2+ output as the CC branch by proxy. Pairing is exclusive: pRib-AMP/pRib-ADP select EDS1-PAD4-ADR1, ADPr-ATP/di-ADPR select EDS1-SAG101-NRG1.
