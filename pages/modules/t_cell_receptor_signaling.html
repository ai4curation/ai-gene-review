T cell receptor signaling pathway module

The proximal T cell receptor (TCR) signaling pathway, from antigen engagement at the plasma membrane to calcium/NFAT-driven transcription. Peptide-MHC binding by the clonotypic TCR alpha/beta chains is transmitted by the invariant CD3 and CD3 zeta subunits, whose ITAMs are phosphorylated by the Src-family kinase LCK, carried to the complex by the MHC-matched coreceptor (CD4 or CD8) and kept primed by the phosphatase CD45. Doubly phosphorylated ITAMs dock the tandem SH2 domains of ZAP70, which LCK then activates. ZAP70 phosphorylates the transmembrane scaffold LAT and the cytosolic adaptor SLP-76 (LCP2), which the adaptor GADS bridges into the TCR signalosome; the signalosome recruits PLC gamma 1 and the Tec kinase ITK that activates it. PLC gamma 1 hydrolyses PIP2 to IP3 and DAG; IP3-driven calcium entry activates calcineurin, which dephosphorylates NFAT so that it enters the nucleus and activates target genes. The Ras-MAPK/AP-1 and PKC-theta/NF-kappaB branches that also leave the LAT signalosome are outside this module and are modelled in their own modules. Leaf molecular functions follow the ADAPTIVE_IMMUNITY gene reviews of each member.

Part 1: Antigen recognition and ITAM presentation
TCR-CD3 complex antigen recognitionRegulatory Steptcr_cd3_triggering

The octameric TCR-CD3 complex (TCR alpha/beta with CD3 gamma-epsilon, CD3 delta-epsilon and CD3 zeta-zeta) binds peptide-MHC through the clonotypic alpha/beta chains and transmits that event across the membrane through the ten ITAMs of the invariant CD3 and CD3 zeta chains. The receptor activity belongs to the complex: the CD3 chains contribute adaptor activity and do not bind antigen themselves.

Part 2: Proximal tyrosine kinase relay
LCK and ZAP70 tyrosine kinase activationReactionlck_zap70_relay

Coreceptor-associated and free LCK phosphorylates the CD3 and CD3 zeta ITAMs. ZAP70 docks on doubly phosphorylated ITAMs through its tandem SH2 domains, is activated by LCK phosphorylation of its interdomain and activation-loop tyrosines, and then phosphorylates LAT and SLP-76. ZAP70 cannot phosphorylate ITAMs itself, so the two kinases act in sequence.

Part 3: LAT-SLP-76 signalosome assembly
LAT and SLP-76 signalosome assemblyRegulatory Steplat_slp76_signalosome

ZAP70-phosphorylated tyrosines on the palmitoylated transmembrane scaffold LAT dock PLC gamma 1, GRB2 and GADS; GADS bridges LAT to SLP-76, whose own ZAP70-phosphorylated tyrosines recruit VAV1, NCK and ITK. The resulting multivalent assembly forms membrane-associated condensates at the immunological synapse and positions PLC gamma 1 for activation by ITK.

Part 4: Second-messenger generation
PLC gamma 1 PIP2 hydrolysisReactionplcg1_second_messengers

LAT-docked PLC gamma 1, phosphorylated by ITK, hydrolyses phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate to inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate (IP3) and diacylglycerol (DAG). IP3 releases ER calcium and triggers store-operated calcium entry; DAG feeds the RasGRP/Ras-MAPK and PKC-theta branches modelled elsewhere.

Part 5: Calcium-NFAT transcriptional output
Calcineurin-NFAT transcriptional outputRegulatory Stepnfat_output

Sustained cytosolic calcium activates the phosphatase calcineurin, which dephosphorylates NFAT proteins and exposes their nuclear localization signals. Nuclear NFATC1 binds DNA with AP-1 and other partners to activate target genes such as IL2.
