De novo tetrahydrofolate and folylpolyglutamate biosynthesis

De novo synthesis of tetrahydrofolate (THF) and its polyglutamylated forms, as found in bacteria, fungi, plants and many protists but absent from animals, which must take up preformed folate. The pathway joins two branches. In the pterin branch, GTP cyclohydrolase I opens the GTP imidazole ring to give 7,8-dihydroneopterin triphosphate; the triphosphate is removed (a Nudix-type pyrophosphatase followed by a phosphatase) to give 7,8-dihydroneopterin; dihydroneopterin aldolase releases glycolaldehyde to give 6-hydroxymethyl-7,8-dihydropterin; and 6-hydroxymethyl-7,8-dihydropterin pyrophosphokinase activates it as the diphosphate. In the p-aminobenzoate (pABA) branch, chorismate is aminated by glutamine-dependent aminodeoxychorismate synthase and 4-amino-4-deoxychorismate is cleaved by aminodeoxychorismate lyase to pABA and pyruvate. Dihydropteroate synthase condenses the pterin diphosphate with pABA to 7,8-dihydropteroate, dihydrofolate synthase adds the first L-glutamate to give 7,8-dihydrofolate, and dihydrofolate reductase reduces it with NADPH to THF. Folylpolyglutamate synthase then adds further gamma-linked glutamates, which retain folates in cells and organelles and make them preferred cofactors of folate-dependent enzymes. The reaction chain is conserved, but enzyme architecture varies: dihydroneopterin aldolase, pyrophosphokinase and dihydropteroate synthase are separate proteins in many bacteria and a single trifunctional polypeptide in fungi; aminodeoxychorismate synthase is a two-subunit PabA-PabB enzyme in many bacteria and a fused polypeptide in fungi and plants; and dihydrofolate synthase and folylpolyglutamate synthase are one bifunctional FolC protein in many bacteria but separate proteins in fungi and plants. In eukaryotes the steps are split between compartments (for example, the fungal trifunctional pterin enzyme and plant dihydrofolate synthase are mitochondrial). Chorismate supply, folate salvage and transport, and the interconversion and use of one-carbon-substituted THF are outside the module.
