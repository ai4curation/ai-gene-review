Nematode cuticle collagen maturation

Nematode cuticle collagens are synthesized as secretory procollagens and are matured by a conserved series of processing reactions. In the endoplasmic reticulum, PHY-1 and PHY-2 prolyl 4-hydroxylase alpha subunits assemble with the PDI-2 beta subunit as dimeric or mixed tetrameric enzymes that hydroxylate prolines in collagen Gly-Xaa-Pro repeats. The furin/kexin-like subtilisin BLI-4 promotes N-terminal processing of cuticle collagens in the secretory pathway to allow efficient early secretion, and the extracellular astacin DPY-31 cleaves carboxyl-terminal propeptides to produce mature collagens for cuticle assembly. The module is centered on the nematode exoskeleton: the C. elegans proteins define the genetically supported pathway, while Brugia malayi and Haemonchus contortus DPY-31 orthologs anchor parasite representatives of the same astacin C-proteinase subfamily.

PMID:10805750
Prolyl 4-hydroxylase is an essential procollagen-modifying enzyme required for exoskeleton formation and the maintenance of body shape in the nematode Caenorhabditis elegans.
C. elegans encodes two procollagen prolyl 4-hydroxylase alpha subunits and one PDI beta subunit, and phy-1/dpy-18 loss reduces cuticle-collagen hydroxyproline and gives a dumpy body morphology.
We identified genes encoding two PHYs and a single associated PDI and showed that all three are expressed in collagen-synthesizing ectodermal cells at times of maximal collagen synthesis. Disruption of the pdi gene via RNA interference resulted in embryonic lethality. Similarly, the combined phy genes are required for embryonic development. Interference with phy-1 resulted in a morphologically dumpy phenotype, which we determined to be identical to the uncharacterized dpy-18 locus. Two dpy-18 mutant strains were shown to have null alleles for phy-1 and to have a reduced hydroxyproline content in their exoskeleton collagens.
PMID:12036960
The exoskeleton collagens in Caenorhabditis elegans are modified by prolyl 4-hydroxylases with unique combinations of subunits.
PHY-1, PHY-2 and PDI-2 assemble in vivo as a PHY-1/PHY-2/(PDI-2)2 mixed tetramer and as PHY-1/PDI-2 and PHY-2/PDI-2 dimers, all expressed in cuticle-collagen-synthesizing hypodermis.
We report here that three unique P4H forms are assembled from these polypeptides and the single beta subunit PDI-2, both in a recombinant expression system and in vivo, namely a PHY-1/PHY-2/(PDI-2)(2) mixed tetramer and PHY-1/PDI-2 and PHY-2/PDI-2 dimers. The mixed tetramer is the main P4H form in wild-type C. elegans but phy-2-/- and phy-1-/- (dpy-18) mutant nematodes can compensate for its absence by increasing the assembly of the PHY-1/PDI-2 and PHY-2/PDI-2 dimers, respectively.
PMID:37721936
The proprotein convertase BLI-4 promotes collagen secretion prior to assembly of the Caenorhabditis elegans cuticle.
BLI-4/PCSK is required for efficient early secretion of the cuticle collagens SQT-3 and DPY-17, and BLI-4 loss phenocopies mutation of their predicted N-terminal consensus furin cleavage sites.
Furthermore, this early secretion depends on BLI-4/PCSK; in bli-4 and cleavage-site mutants, SQT-3 and DPY-17 are not efficiently secreted and instead form large intracellular puncta.
PMID:19883650
Collagen processing and cuticle formation is catalysed by the astacin metalloprotease DPY-31 in free-living and parasitic nematodes.
DPY-31/NAS-35 is an astacin metalloprotease that cleaves procollagen C-termini, with experimentally tested members from C. elegans, B. malayi and H. contortus.
In addition, the recombinant DPY-31 enzymes from both H. contortus and B. malayi were shown to efficiently process the C. elegans cuticle collagen SQT-3 at the correct C-terminal procollagen processing site.

BRUMA dpy-31 gene review
The Brugia malayi dpy-31 review accepts metalloendopeptidase activity in extracellular procollagen C-terminal processing as the DPY-31 core function.

Boundary. The module starts with hydroxylation of nascent cuticle collagen chains by prolyl 4-hydroxylase complexes, continues through a BLI-4-dependent N-terminal collagen-processing step before efficient early secretion, and ends with DPY-31-family C-terminal cleavage in the extracellular/cuticular space. It is not a full cuticle-assembly module: collagen transcription, trimer folding, cross-linking, the BLI-5 Kunitz-domain cofactor, and extracellular collagen polymerization are outside this first boundary until their direct molecular roles and ortholog sets are reviewed. Grounding. PTHR10869 grounds nematode procollagen proline 4-hydroxylase alpha proteins, represented here by the two C. elegans alpha subunits DPY-18/PHY-1 and PHY-2. PTHR18929 grounds the protein disulfide isomerase family, represented by C. elegans PDI-2, the beta subunit of the cuticle collagen prolyl 4-hydroxylase complexes. PTHR42884 grounds BLI-4 in the furin/kexin proprotein convertase family. PTHR10127:SF813 specifically groups DPY-31/NAS-35 astacins from B. malayi, H. contortus and C. elegans; those three representatives now have matching gene reviews.
