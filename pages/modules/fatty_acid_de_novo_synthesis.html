Eukaryotic cytosolic de novo fatty acid synthesis (type I FAS)

Cytosolic de novo fatty acid synthesis builds long-chain saturated fatty acids (mainly C16:0 and C18:0) from acetyl-CoA in eukaryotes, supplying membrane phospholipids, sphingolipids, storage triacylglycerol and acylated proteins. Cytosolic acetyl-CoA is supplied either by ATP-citrate lyase, which cleaves citrate exported from mitochondria (animals, plants and many fungi such as fission yeast), or, in yeasts without ATP-citrate lyase, by acetyl-CoA synthetase acting on acetate. A biotin-dependent multidomain acetyl-CoA carboxylase then carboxylates acetyl-CoA to malonyl-CoA in the committed, highly regulated step. The chain is assembled by a type I fatty acid synthase (FAS), a multifunctional megasynthase that carries all the acyl-transfer, condensation, reduction, dehydration and acyl-carrier activities on large polypeptides and iteratively adds two-carbon units from malonyl-CoA using NADPH. Two unrelated architectures carry out this step: the animal FAS is a homodimer of a single polypeptide that releases free palmitate through a thioesterase, whereas the fungal FAS is a 2.6 MDa alpha6-beta6 barrel built from two polypeptides that releases the product as acyl-CoA (mostly palmitoyl- and stearoyl-CoA) through a malonyl/palmitoyl-transferase domain. In animals an auxiliary thioesterase can release medium-chain fatty acids early. Saturated acyl-CoAs are commonly desaturated by an endoplasmic-reticulum delta-9 stearoyl-CoA desaturase to palmitoleoyl- and oleoyl-CoA. Chain extension beyond C16-C18 is carried out by the separate microsomal elongation cycle, and the mitochondrial dissociated (type II) FAS that makes the octanoyl precursor of lipoic acid is a distinct pathway.

GENERALIZE (YeastPathways project, 2026-10): the former human-only lipogenesis module (ACLY -> ACACA/ACACB -> FASN -> OLAH) was broadened to eukaryotic cytosolic type I FAS. Nine YeastCyc frames (PWY3O-5962, PWY3O-6336, PWY3O-10, PWY-5971-1, PWY3O-8514, PWY3O-355, PWY3O-31723, PWY3O-5268, PWY3O-1801) are partitions of one pathway and map onto parts 2, 3 and 5. Changes: (1) acetyl-CoA supply became a ONE_OR_MORE variant set with ATP-citrate lyase (human ACLY, S. pombe acl1) and the acetate / acetyl-CoA synthetase route used by S. cerevisiae, which lacks ATP-citrate lyase (ACS2 representative; pdh_bypass_acetyl_coa_synthesis is authoritative for that route). S. pombe ATP-citrate lyase is heteromeric (acl1 + acl2); acl2 is in a different PANTHER family and is mentioned only in the acl1 description. (2) ACC gained S. cerevisiae ACC1 and S. pombe cut6 (both PTHR45728). (3) chain assembly became an EXACTLY_ONE variant set: animal homodimeric FASN (GO:0004312, free palmitate) vs the fungal alpha6-beta6 FAS complex (GO:0005835; FAS1 beta + FAS2 alpha; GO:0004321 fatty-acyl-CoA synthase activity because the product leaves as acyl-CoA). The fungal subunits carry no PANTHER term: UniProt and PANTHER place both FAS1 and FAS2 in PTHR10982:SF21 "FATTY ACID SYNTHASE SUBUNIT BETA" under the malonyl-CoA ACP transacylase family, which does not describe the alpha subunit; family membership was not used as a role descriptor. (4) OLAH became optional (animal-specific). (5) a new optional delta-9 desaturation part (SCD/OLE1/ole1, PTHR11351, GO:0004768, ER membrane) was added. The module context is now Eukaryota + cytosol; the desaturase location is given on its annoton. ACACB's mitochondrial outer membrane location and its regulatory role in beta-oxidation remain in its gene review rather than in module context. YeastCyc corrections: HFA1 is the mitochondrial acetyl-CoA carboxylase (PMID:14761959), and CEM1, OAR1 and MCT1 are mitochondrial FAS II enzymes; YeastCyc lists them as catalysts of the cytosolic FAS reactions, and they are modelled in type_ii_fatty_acid_synthesis instead. YeastCyc gives no gene for many 3-oxoacyl-ACP reductions (FAS2 KR domain) and none for the final acyl-CoA hydrolysis steps. GO-CAM disagreements: the YeastPathways models place every activity, including ER-membrane OLE1, in cytosol, attach mitochondrial HFA1/CEM1/OAR1/MCT1 to cytosolic FAS reaction nodes, type two FAS1 partial reactions only as GO:0003674, type one FAS1 enoyl-reductase node as GO:0004318 (NADH) although the fungal enzyme uses NADPH, and attach TES1 (a peroxisomal thioesterase) to the cytosolic acyl-CoA hydrolysis reactions. The PomBase model 678073a900002931 agrees with the cytosolic core and additionally contains mitochondrial FAS II, the ER elongation cycle, citrate transport and lipoylation, which belong to other modules. Very-long-chain extension is in fatty_acid_elongation_cycle.
