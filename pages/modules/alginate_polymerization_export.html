Alginate polymerization and export

A synthase-dependent bacterial exopolysaccharide module in which the inner-membrane Alg8-Alg44 complex polymerizes GDP-mannuronate, periplasmic proteins guide and modify the nascent alginate chain, AlgL removes escaped polymer, and the AlgE outer-membrane channel exports the product. Precursor synthesis, O-acetylation chemistry, and transcriptional regulation are separate modules.

The polymerase is modeled as a two-part synthase complex: Alg8 supplies catalysis and Alg44 binds cyclic-di-GMP and contributes regulatory copolymerase function. AlgG maturation and AlgL quality control are optional parts because polymerization/export can be distinguished from product composition and escaped-polymer removal. AlgI, AlgJ, AlgF, and the O-acetyltransferase activity of AlgX belong to the separate alginate O-acetylation module.
