Interleukin-22 signaling pathway module

Secreted Interleukin-22 (IL22), produced by Th17/Th22 cells, gamma-delta T cells, and group 3 innate lymphoid cells (ILC3), binds with high affinity to the private epithelial receptor subunit IL22RA1 (IL-22R1) and recruits the shared class II cytokine receptor subunit IL10RB (IL-10R2) into a ternary signaling complex on barrier epithelial cells, keratinocytes, and hepatocytes. Receptor assembly activates IL22RA1-associated JAK1 and IL10RB-associated TYK2, which phosphorylate IL22RA1 cytoplasmic tyrosines (Y251, Y301) to recruit and phosphorylate STAT3, driving antimicrobial peptide expression, acute-phase protein synthesis, and epithelial barrier proliferation and repair. Extracellular IL22 bioactivity is antagonized by the secreted soluble decoy receptor IL22RA2 (IL-22BP).
