E. coli ManXYZ mannose-family PTS

A bacterial phosphoenolpyruvate-dependent group-translocation module in which the soluble/peripheral ManX EIIAB subunit relays phosphate from HPr through its EIIA and EIIB histidines to sugar entering through the inner-membrane ManY/ManZ channel. ManY and ManZ form the EIIC/EIID membrane translocation subcomplex, while all three subunits are required for efficient transport and concomitant phosphorylation of mannose-family substrates. Upstream enzyme I and HPr, dedicated fructose PTS systems, nitrogen-regulatory PTS signaling, sugar chemotaxis, and bacteriophage lambda DNA penetration are neighboring processes outside this transport module.

ManXYZ is broad-substrate relative to many sugar PTS transporters: mannose is the named core substrate, but the same EII complex also imports and phosphorylates 2-deoxyglucose and several other hexoses or amino sugars. This module therefore represents the ManXYZ EII apparatus rather than trying to partition every secondary sugar into a separate route. Phage lambda DNA penetration and sugar chemotaxis use the ManYZ membrane subcomplex but are separate biological outputs.
