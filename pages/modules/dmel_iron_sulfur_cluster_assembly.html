Mitochondrial and cytosolic iron-sulfur cluster assembly in Drosophila melanogaster

Iron-sulfur (Fe-S) clusters are cofactors of respiratory complexes, the TCA cycle, DNA replication and repair enzymes and many other proteins. In eukaryotes all clusters depend on the mitochondrial iron-sulfur cluster (ISC) machinery. In the matrix, the cysteine desulfurase Nfs1, stabilised by Isd11 (bcn92) and acyl-ND-ACP, supplies sulfur and frataxin (fh) stimulates assembly of a [2Fe-2S] cluster on the scaffold IscU, with electrons from ferredoxin (Fdx1) and ferredoxin reductase (dare). The Hsc20-Hsc70-5 chaperone pair with glutaredoxin 5 transfers the cluster to client proteins, and the ISA proteins (MagR, CG13623) with IBA57 (CG8043) convert [2Fe-2S] into [4Fe-4S] clusters for mitochondrial targets. A sulfur-containing compound made by the ISC machinery is exported by the ABC transporter Abcb7 and used by the cytosolic iron-sulfur protein assembly (CIA) system: the diflavin reductase CG13667 and anamorsin (Ciapin1) supply electrons, the Nubp1-Nubp2 scaffold builds [4Fe-4S] clusters, and the CIA targeting complex (Ciao1, CG17683, galla2 and Mms19) inserts them into cytosolic and nuclear apoproteins.

Boundary. The module joins five FlyBase groups: the general Fe-S cluster assembly group (FESCA) and the four complex groups NIAUF (core ISC complex), ISA, CIA-SCAF and CTC. Every FESCA member is placed in one step. Fly subunit roles follow orthology to the human and yeast ISC and CIA proteins (for example bcn92 = Isd11/LYRM4, CG8043 = IBA57, CG13667 = NDOR1, CG17683 = CIAO3/NARFL, galla2 = CIAO2B); most have not been studied directly in flies. Molecular functions are given only to Nfs1, Fdx1, dare and Abcb7, whose activities are well conserved; the scaffold and carrier proteins have no clean catalytic activity. ND-ACP is also part of mitochondrial fatty acid synthesis (dmel_mitochondrial_fatty_acid_synthesis) and of respiratory complex I. The exact sulfur-containing compound exported by Abcb7 is not known. MagR has also been proposed as a magnetoreceptor; that proposal is not modelled.
