Rtt109-Vps75 histone acetyltransferase complex in Saccharomyces cerevisiae

Rtt109 is the fungal histone H3 acetyltransferase that acetylates newly synthesized histone H3 before it is deposited into chromatin. It is active only in complex with a histone chaperone. With the NAP-family chaperone Vps75 it forms a stable complex, in which a Vps75 dimer binds one or two Rtt109 molecules. Vps75 stabilizes Rtt109, brings it into the nucleus, raises its catalytic rate about 100-fold and positions the H3 N-terminal tail in a central cavity facing the Rtt109 active site, so the complex preferentially acetylates H3 K9 and K27 in the tail. Acetylation of H3 K56 in the histone-fold core requires the other chaperone, Asf1, which presents the H3-H4 dimer to Rtt109. Neither Rtt109-chaperone complex acts on nucleosomal H3. H3 K56 acetylation marks newly replicated chromatin and is needed for genome stability; the Rtt109-Vps75 complex is dispensable for that mark and for resistance to genotoxic agents, while Vps75 also has Rtt109-independent roles as a histone chaperone.

Boundary. The module contains the two subunits of the stable complex, Rtt109 (catalytic) and Vps75 (chaperone and activator). Asf1 is not a subunit: it binds Rtt109 through H3-H4 and through the Rtt109 C-terminus, and presents the H3-H4 dimer for K56 acetylation. It is recorded in the K56 annoton's role description rather than as a part, and belongs with its own replication-coupled assembly module. Histones H3 and H4 are substrates. The two crystal structures disagree on stoichiometry: a symmetric Vps75 dimer with two Rtt109 molecules (PMID:21256037) and an asymmetric Vps75 dimer with one Rtt109 (PMID:21454705); Vps75 also forms tetramers on H3-H4 (PMID:27036862). Division of labour. Vps75 is needed for Rtt109 to acetylate the H3 tail (K9, K27) but not H3 K56, which requires only Asf1 in vivo. Rtt109 is unstable without Vps75, so early vps75 deletion phenotypes mixed loss of the complex with loss of Rtt109; an Rtt109 mutant stable without Vps75 separated the two (PMID:21463458). Ontology. The module concept is GO:0070775 H3 histone acetyltransferase complex, defined as a complex capable of histone H3 acetyltransferase activity. A reasoner places it under GO:0000123 histone acetyltransferase complex, which is part_of GO:0000785 chromatin, so the module and the GO:0070775 annotations on Rtt109, Vps75 and Asf1 entail a chromatin location. The Rtt109-Vps75 complex has no detectable activity on nucleosomal H3, so it is a second counterexample to that edge, after HAT-B (modules/scer_hat_b_complex.yaml). The edge deletion is recorded under proposed_ontology_changes. RTT109 has a gene review in this repository; VPS75 has a scaffold from just fetch-gene with its annotations still PENDING.
