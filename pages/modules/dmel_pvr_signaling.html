PVR receptor tyrosine kinase signaling in Drosophila melanogaster

PVR is the single Drosophila receptor tyrosine kinase related to vertebrate PDGF and VEGF receptors. It is activated by three secreted ligands, Pvf1, Pvf2 and Pvf3. PVR signaling guides the migration of border cells in the ovary and of other cells during development, and supports the survival, proliferation and migration of embryonic and larval hemocytes, and acts in dorsal closure and thorax closure. Downstream of the receptor, signaling runs through several branches: the Ras-Raf-MEK-ERK cascade, a Crk-Myoblast city-Ced-12 route to the Rac GTPase that drives actin-based migration, a PI3K-Akt-TOR branch that supports growth and survival, and JNK and p38 kinase cascades.
