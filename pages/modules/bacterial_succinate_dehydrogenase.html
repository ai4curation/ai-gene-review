Bacterial succinate dehydrogenase complex and SdhE-dependent maturation

Reusable bacterial module for respiratory complex II, the membrane-bound succinate:quinone oxidoreductase that connects the tricarboxylic acid cycle to the respiratory quinone pool without pumping protons. The SdhA flavoprotein oxidizes succinate to fumarate, SdhB relays electrons through iron-sulfur centers, and SdhC/SdhD form the membrane anchor and quinone-reduction domain. A separate SdhE maturation step promotes covalent FAD attachment to SdhA. This module excludes the paralogous fumarate reductase system and mitochondrial-lineage assembly factors that are not part of the conserved bacterial realization.

UniProtKB entry for PSEPK SdhE
Q88MZ4 is the conserved FAD assembly factor that promotes covalent FAD attachment to SdhA.

The P. putida KT2440 structural complex is encoded by the compact sdhCDAB locus PP_4193-PP_4190. SdhE/PP_1424 is encoded elsewhere and is a maturation factor rather than a stoichiometric holoenzyme subunit. The complete succinate:quinone oxidoreductase activity is an emergent property of SdhABCD; individual subunits contribute distinct catalytic, relay, or anchoring roles.
