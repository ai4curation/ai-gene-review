Conserved oligomeric Golgi (COG) complex in Drosophila melanogaster

The conserved oligomeric Golgi (COG) complex is an eight-subunit multisubunit tethering complex at the Golgi apparatus. It tethers COPI-derived vesicles that recycle Golgi resident proteins, such as glycosylation enzymes, between cisternae, and it interacts with Golgi SNAREs, Rab GTPases and COPI coat proteins. The complex is organized into two lobes, lobe A (Cog1-Cog4) and lobe B (Cog5-Cog8). In Drosophila, Cog5 is encoded by four way stop (fws), whose mutants are defective in male meiotic cytokinesis and Golgi organization during spermatogenesis.
