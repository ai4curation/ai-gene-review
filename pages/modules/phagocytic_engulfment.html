Phagocytic engulfment

Phagocytic engulfment is the receptor-driven internalization of a large particle (a microbe, an opsonized target, or an apoptotic cell) into a plasma-membrane-derived vacuole, the nascent phagosome. A cell-surface receptor recognizes the particle, either directly (beta-glucan, phosphatidylserine) or through a bound opsonin or bridging ligand (IgG, iC3b, GAS6). Receptor engagement then activates Rho-family GTPases, Rac and Cdc42 for Fc-gamma and apoptotic-cell receptors and RhoA for complement receptor 3. The GTPases drive Arp2/3-dependent actin polymerization that extends pseudopods around the particle. Class I PI3K activity and dynamin are then needed to complete pseudopod extension and close the phagocytic cup. The receptor-to-Rac core is conserved between nematodes and mammals: the CED-1/CED-6 and CED-2/CED-5/CED-12 routes of C. elegans correspond to MEGF10/GULP1 and CRK/DOCK1/ELMO1 upstream of Rac1.

Module: Phagosome maturation
Sibling module. The sealed nascent phagosome produced here is the input to phagosome maturation.

Boundary: this module runs from particle recognition to closure of the phagocytic cup. It does not include phagosome maturation (the Rab5-to-Rab7 transition, lysosome fusion, acidification and the NADPH oxidase), which is in MODULE:phagosome_maturation. Also excluded: generation of "eat-me" signals on the target (for example XKR8-mediated phosphatidylserine exposure, GO:0070782), find-me chemotaxis, "don't-eat-me" checkpoints (CD47-SIRPA), and the inflammatory and transcriptional outputs of phagocytic receptors. LC3-associated phagocytosis (RUBCN, NOX2, ATG16L1 WD40 domain) is a related route that has not been modelled here. Receptor variants are alternatives (ONE_OR_MORE). A professional phagocyte usually uses several at once, and opsonic and non-opsonic routes can cooperate. The apoptotic-cell variant combines mammalian receptors (TIMD4, the GAS6-MERTK bridge) with the CED-1/MEGF10 receptor family and its CED-6/GULP adaptor. The CED-1/MEGF10 receptor is grounded on PTHR24035, the family PANTHER's own classification and PAINT use (ced-1 and Draper in SF109, MEGF10 in SF136). The UniProt/InterPro cross-references name PTHR24043 and PTHR24052 instead; see projects/PANTHER_FAMILY_ASSIGNMENT_DISCREPANCIES.md. CED-12/ELMO representatives are human ELMO1 and Drosophila Ced-12, which UniProt places in PTHR12771. C. elegans CED-12 (Q8STE5) has no PANTHER cross-reference and is named only in prose. The worm DYN-1 gene review places DYN-1's engulfment-cell role at phagosome maturation (GO:0090386). Mammalian dynamin 2 also acts earlier, at pseudopod extension. The dynamin family therefore appears in this module for cup closure (DNM2 evidence) and again in MODULE:phagosome_maturation (DYN-1 evidence).

Pseudopod extension completion and phagocytic cup closureBiological Processphagocytic_cup_closure

Class I PI3K turns over PI(4,5)P2 at the cup base, which ends actin polymerization. Dynamin is recruited to the forming phagosome. Both are needed for pseudopods to finish extending and for the cup to seal into a nascent phagosome. Blocking either arrests uptake at the same stage, after actin-cup formation.
