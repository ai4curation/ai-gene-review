Efferocytosis-induced anti-inflammatory programme

A macrophage that has engulfed apoptotic cells suppresses inflammation and makes pro-resolving signals such as IL-10, TGF-beta1 and prostaglandin E2. Several parallel routes read components of the digested cell: sterols activate the liver X receptors, lipids activate PPAR-delta, fatty acids fuel mitochondrial oxidation that raises NAD+ and activates SIRT1 and PBX1, tryptophan feeds IDO1 and the aryl hydrocarbon receptor, DNA signals through TLR9 to the same receptor, and methionine supplies the methyl groups DNMT3A uses to keep ERK signalling on. Chloride sensing through SLC12A2 decides whether the corpse is read as anti-inflammatory. Each route has cargo-specificity controls: engulfing beads, necrotic cells or cargo-free phosphatidylserine beads does not trigger it.

Boundary: this module starts with the digested cargo of an engulfed apoptotic cell and ends with suppressed inflammatory output and production of IL-10, TGF-beta1 and PGE2. Engulfment and digestion belong to the phagocytosis modules. IL-10, TGF-beta1 and the opsonin C1q are outputs or readouts and are not modelled as participants. The routes come mostly from separate laboratories and model systems. Two disagree: PMID:39242914 places IDO1 upstream of AhR, while PMID:29760532 found AhR activation intact without IDO1. They are modelled as independent routes rather than one chain. Some routes respond to engulfed cells in general, not only apoptotic ones (IgG-coated red cells also induce DNMT3A-dependent COX2 in PMID:35361955). Taxon scope: all shown in mouse, with some steps also in human macrophages. Whether any route operates outside mammals is not known.

Cargo-derived signal routesSignaling Pathwaycargo_signal_routes

Each route reads a different component of the digested cell. The routes are additive alternatives: losing one blunts the response but does not abolish all of it, and several converge on IL-10 or TGF-beta1.

Sterol route: LXRSignaling Pathwaysterol_lxr_route

Sterols from the engulfed cell activate the liver X receptors. LXR induces the engulfment receptor MERTK, which speeds further clearance, and is needed for induction of IL-10 and TGF-beta and for repression of inflammatory genes. Depleting sterols from the corpses blunts LXR target induction.

Part 2: interpretation gate (optional)
Chloride sensing sets the anti-inflammatory readingRegulatory Stepchloride_sensing_gate

Corpse uptake lowers cytosolic chloride. The WNK1-OSR1/SPAK kinases activate the chloride importer SLC12A2, and chloride influx limits further uptake and is needed for the corpse to be read as anti-inflammatory. Without SLC12A2, phagocytes switch to a pro-inflammatory programme even though they eat more; phagocytes made to eat more by TIM4 overexpression do not switch.
