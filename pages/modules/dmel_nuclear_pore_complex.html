Nuclear pore complex in Drosophila melanogaster

The nuclear pore complex (NPC) is a large assembly of about 30 different nucleoporins, each present in multiple copies, that spans the nuclear envelope and controls traffic between nucleus and cytoplasm. A symmetric core of stacked rings (two outer rings built from the Nup107-160 Y-complex, an inner ring, and a transmembrane ring) anchors a central channel filled with phenylalanine-glycine (FG) repeat nucleoporins that form the permeability barrier. Asymmetric cytoplasmic filaments and a nuclear basket attach to the core and act in mRNA export and in docking of transport receptors. Several Drosophila nucleoporins also act off the pore, for example in gene regulation and in mitosis.
