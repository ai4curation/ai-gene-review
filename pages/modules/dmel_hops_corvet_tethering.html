HOPS and CORVET tethering complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

HOPS and CORVET are related hexameric tethering complexes of the endolysosomal system that share a class C Vps core of Vps11, Vps16, Vps18 and the Sec1/Munc18-family protein Vps33. HOPS adds Vps39 and Vps41, binds active Rab7 and promotes SNARE-dependent fusion of late endosomes, autophagosomes and lysosome-related organelles with lysosomes. CORVET uses Vps8 (with Vps3 in yeast and mammals) and acts at the Rab5-positive early endosome stage. In Drosophila the core subunits are encoded by Vps11, Vps16A, deep orange (dor, Vps18) and carnation (car, Vps33A), and the HOPS-specific subunits by Vps39 and light (lt, Vps41); several of these were identified as eye-colour mutants with pigment granule defects. Drosophila also has paralogs Vps16B and Vps33B, which form a separate complex related to the mammalian VPS33B-VIPAS39 (CHEVI) complex.
