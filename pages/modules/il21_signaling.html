Interleukin-21 signaling pathway module

Secreted Interleukin-21 (IL21) binds the private alpha receptor subunit IL21R and recruits the shared common gamma chain (IL2RG) to assemble a heterodimeric class I cytokine receptor complex on B cells, T cells, and natural killer (NK) cells. Receptor assembly juxtaposes and activates receptor-associated Janus kinases JAK1 (on IL21R) and JAK3 (on IL2RG), which phosphorylate IL21R cytoplasmic tyrosines (notably Y519) to recruit and phosphorylate STAT3 (and contextually STAT1 and STAT5), driving germinal center B cell differentiation, immunoglobulin class-switch recombination, T follicular helper (Tfh) cell differentiation, and NK/CD8+ T cell effector maturation.
