Sevenless receptor tyrosine kinase signaling in Drosophila melanogaster

Sevenless is a receptor tyrosine kinase expressed in a subset of cells of the developing eye that is required for the R7 photoreceptor to adopt its fate. It is activated by Bride of sevenless, a seven-pass transmembrane ligand on the neighbouring R8 cell. The activated receptor recruits the adaptor Drk (Grb2), the docking protein Daughter of sevenless and the tyrosine phosphatase Corkscrew, which together activate Son of sevenless and Ras. Ras drives the Raf-MEK (Dsor1)-ERK (Rolled) cascade, aided by the scaffolds Ksr and Cnk. ERK phosphorylates the ETS factors Pointed (activating it) and Yan/Aop (relieving its repression), and induces Phyllopod, which with the E3 ligase Seven in absentia targets the repressor Tramtrack for degradation, allowing neuronal R7 differentiation.
