GARP and EARP tethering complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

The Golgi-associated retrograde protein (GARP) complex and the endosome-associated recycling protein (EARP) complex are related multisubunit tethering complexes that share Vps51, Vps52 and Vps53. GARP contains Vps54 (scattered, scat, in Drosophila) and tethers endosome-derived vesicles at the trans-Golgi network, supporting retrograde transport of cargo such as lysosomal hydrolase receptors. EARP contains Vps50 in place of Vps54 and acts in recycling from endosomes to the plasma membrane. Drosophila scat mutants are male sterile, with defects in spermatid differentiation.
