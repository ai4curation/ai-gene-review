Type-II (dissociated) fatty-acid synthesis, bacterial and mitochondrial

A reusable dissociated fatty-acid synthase (FAS II) pathway in which each reaction is catalysed by a separate enzyme acting on acyl chains carried by a free acyl carrier protein (ACP). Malonyl-CoA formed by acetyl-CoA carboxylase is loaded onto ACP, an acyl-ACP chain is initiated, and the chain is iteratively elongated through condensation, beta-keto reduction, dehydration, and enoyl reduction. The same architecture operates in bacteria (and plastids) and, as a conserved, endosymbiont-derived system, in the mitochondrial matrix of eukaryotes, where it is independent of the cytosolic type I FAS and its best-defined product is octanoyl-ACP, the precursor of lipoic acid. Mitochondrial FAS II uses a homomeric multidomain acetyl-CoA carboxylase, a mitochondrial ACP and partly unrelated reductase and dehydratase families. An optional FabA/FabB branch introduces and extends cis unsaturation in bacteria. Enzyme-family, cofactor and compartment variants are represented explicitly rather than collapsed into a type-I fatty-acid synthase.

Type-II fatty-acid synthesisMetabolic Pathwaytype_ii_fatty_acid_synthesis
fatty acid biosynthetic processGO:0006633

ALIGN (YeastPathways project, 2026-10): added eukaryotic mitochondrial FAS II. YeastCyc FASYN-ELONG2-PWY ("fatty acid elongation") is a generic ACP-based elongation round that mixes cytosolic FAS1/FAS2 with the mitochondrial FAS II enzymes CEM1 and OAR1; the FAS1/FAS2 assignments belong to fatty_acid_de_novo_synthesis and are not used here. Mitochondrial representatives (S. cerevisiae, S. pombe from PomBase model 678073a900002931) were added to shared roles where the family matches: ACP (ACP1, SPAC4H3.09 in PTHR20863), malonyl-CoA:ACP transacylase (MCT1, mct1; descriptor has no PANTHER id because human MCAT is in PTHR47170 while yeast MCT1 is in PTHR42681), and the FabF/KAS-II variant (CEM1, cem1 in PTHR11712:SF336, whose "MITOCHONDRIAL" label now also matches these members). New variants were added where the mitochondrial enzyme belongs to a different family: a mitochondrial homomeric acetyl-CoA carboxylase (HFA1, PTHR45728; PMID:14761959), an OAR1-type ketoreductase (PTHR42760:SF133), an HTD2 hotdog dehydratase (PTHR28152) and a MECR/ETR1 NADPH-dependent enoyl reductase (PTHR43981, GO:0141148). Mitochondrial leaves are located in mitochondrion; mitochondrial ACP activation by PPT2 (S. pombe ppt2) is activation context. YeastCyc corrections: HTD2, ETR1, MCT1, ACP1 and HFA1 are missing from FASYN-ELONG2-PWY; HFA1, MCT1, CEM1 and OAR1 are instead listed as catalysts of the cytosolic FAS frames. GO-CAM disagreements: YeastPathways_FASYN-ELONG2-PWY places CEM1 and OAR1 in cytosol, attaches them to the same nodes as cytosolic FAS2, types the FAS1 dehydratase node (3-HYDROXYDECANOYL-ACP-DEHYDR-RXN) as enoyl-ACP reductase GO:0141148 and TES1 as palmitoyl-(protein) hydrolase GO:0008474, and uses GO:0030497 fatty acid elongation as the process. The PomBase model agrees with the mitochondrial FAS II chain but places cut6 (cytosolic ACC) and fas1 acetyltransferase activities in the mitochondrion; S. pombe has no HFA1 paralog in this model. Human mtFAS (OXSM, MCAT, CBR4/HSD17B8, HTD2, MECR, NDUFAB1) is not fully represented: only MECR shares a PANTHER family with its fungal counterpart among the added roles. Exact KT2440 accessions are representative members and do not restrict the module to Pseudomonas. Same-family paralogs without a distinct, supported pathway role remain gene-level curation questions rather than separate module variants. ACP phosphopantetheinylation is required before use but is activation context outside this pathway boundary. FabI is grounded to the functionally appropriate PTHR43159 parent and PAINT node PTN001215190 because the current SF2 name is chloroplast-biased even though the subfamily contains bacterial FabI. PANTHER's assignments for the FabK exemplar (PTHR32332:SF20, a broad 2-nitropropane-dioxygenase-like group) and the FabL exemplar (PTHR43639:SF1, a broad SDR group) are deliberately not used to delimit those variants; experimentally characterized UniProt accessions are retained as exemplars. The compact cis_unsaturated_branch is integration context only; bacterial_unsaturated_fatty_acid_biosynthesis is authoritative for the branch-specific FabA/FabB chemistry and optional FabF extension. PANTHER's broad PTHR11712:SF336 subfamily contains bacterial FabF, KasA/KasB, polyketide-synthase components, and genuine mitochondrial KAS proteins. Its official "MITOCHONDRIAL" label is retained verbatim, while the reviewed bacterial exemplars and exact leaf function constrain the intended FabF role; the label is not a localization assertion for the module. The Mad-route condensing-enzyme descriptor remains at parent PTHR11712 because it intentionally spans FabB and FabF representatives in different subfamilies. The existing fatty_acid_de_novo_synthesis module describes type-I mammalian lipogenesis and is intentionally separate.
