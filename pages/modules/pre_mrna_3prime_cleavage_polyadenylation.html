Human pre-mRNA 3'-end cleavage and polyadenylation — AAUAAA recognition by mPSF (CPSF1, CPSF4, WDR33, FIP1L1), UGUA enhancement by CFIm (NUDT21 with CPSF6 or CPSF7), downstream GU-rich element binding by CstF (CSTF1, CSTF3, CSTF2 or CSTF2T), CF IIm (CLP1, PCF11), RBBP6-activated cleavage by the CPSF3 endonuclease in mCF (CPSF2, SYMPK), and poly(A) addition by PAPOLA or PAPOLG with PABPN1

The 3' ends of almost all RNA polymerase II pre-mRNAs are made in two linked steps: an endonucleolytic cut at the poly(A) site, followed by addition of a poly(A) tail of about 250 adenylates to the upstream fragment. The cis-elements are the A(A/U)UAAA polyadenylation signal 10-30 nucleotides upstream of the cut, a U/GU-rich downstream element about 30 nucleotides past it, and auxiliary UGUA motifs further upstream. Four multisubunit factors assemble on these elements. CPSF has two subcomplexes: mPSF (CPSF160/CPSF1, WDR33, CPSF30/CPSF4 and hFip1/FIP1L1), in which CPSF30 and WDR33 contact the AAUAAA bases on a CPSF160 scaffold and hFip1 tethers poly(A) polymerase; and mCF, the cleavage module of the metallo-beta-lactamase endonuclease CPSF73 (CPSF3), its inactive paralogue CPSF100 (CPSF2) and the scaffold symplekin (SYMPK). CstF, a dimer of CstF77-CstF64-CstF50 trimers (CSTF3, CSTF2 or its paralogue CSTF2T, CSTF1), binds the downstream element. Cleavage factor Im, a CFIm25 (NUDT21) dimer with two CFIm68 (CPSF6) or CFIm59 (CPSF7) subunits, binds UGUA and activates nearby poly(A) sites, which makes it a central regulator of alternative polyadenylation. Cleavage factor IIm is a PCF11-CLP1 heterodimer; PCF11 binds the Pol II C-terminal domain and couples processing to termination, and ATP bound to CLP1 supports cleavage. In a fully reconstituted system, cleavage needs mPSF, mCF, CstF, CF IIm, poly(A) polymerase and RBBP6, whose DWNN domain contacts and activates CPSF73; CFIm stimulates but is not essential. After the cut, CPSF stays on AAUAAA and, with the nuclear poly(A)-binding protein PABPN1 coating the growing tail, holds poly(A) polymerase (PAPOLA, or the paralogue PAPOLG) in one processive event that stops at about 250 nucleotides when PABPN1 disrupts the CPSF-polymerase contact. The downstream product is degraded by XRN2, which terminates transcription.
