Human cytoplasmic ribosome biogenesis — rDNA transcription by reference, box C/D and H/ACA snoRNP modification, the SSU processome and 5' ETS processing, exonucleolytic spacer removal by XRN2 and the nuclear exosome, ITS1 cleavage, nuclear pre-40S and pre-60S maturation, CRM1-dependent export, and cytoplasmic 40S and 60S maturation

Ribosome biogenesis builds the cytoplasmic 40S and 60S ribosomal subunits from four rRNAs and about 80 ribosomal proteins. In human cells RNA polymerase I transcribes the 47S precursor rRNA (pre-rRNA), in which the 18S, 5.8S and 28S rRNAs are separated by internal transcribed spacers (ITS1, ITS2) and flanked by external transcribed spacers (5' ETS, 3' ETS); the 5S rRNA is made separately by RNA polymerase III. While it is transcribed, the pre-rRNA is modified at specific sites by small nucleolar ribonucleoproteins (snoRNPs): box C/D snoRNPs, whose catalytic subunit is the methyltransferase fibrillarin, add 2'-O-methyl groups, and box H/ACA snoRNPs, whose catalytic subunit is the pseudouridine synthase dyskerin, convert uridines to pseudouridines; the snoRNA in each particle selects the site by base pairing. The 5' part of the transcript assembles the small subunit (SSU) processome, a nucleolar particle built from the UTP-A and UTP-B subcomplexes, the U3 snoRNP, the MPP10 subcomplex, the BMS1-RCL1 GTPase pair and the PIN domain proteins UTP23 and UTP24, which folds the 18S rRNA domains and directs cleavages in the 5' ETS; the DEAH-box helicase DHX37 then releases the U3 snoRNA so that the central pseudoknot can form. Cleavage in ITS1 separates the pre-40S and pre-60S particles, and each endonucleolytic cleavage is followed by exonucleolytic trimming and spacer degradation by the 5'-3' exonuclease XRN2 and the nuclear RNA exosome with its helicase MTR4 and cofactors C1D and MPP6. The pre-60S particle matures in the nucleolus and nucleoplasm with the PeBoW complex (PES1, BOP1, WDR12) and the AAA ATPase midasin (MDN1); the pre-40S particle acquires late factors such as bystin (ENP1), PNO1, RRP12 and LTV1, with the endonuclease NOB1 bound in an inactive state. Both particles leave the nucleus through the nuclear pore by the exportin CRM1 (XPO1) and Ran-GTP, with NMD3 as the CRM1 adaptor of pre-60S and exportin 5 as a second pre-60S exportin. In the cytoplasm the RIO kinases and casein kinase 1 delta and epsilon drive the release of the late pre-40S factors, and NOB1 cleaves the 18S-E pre-rRNA at site 3 to form the 18S rRNA 3' end; on the 60S side, the GTPase EFL1 with SBDS removes the anti-association factor eIF6 and ERI1 trims the 3' end of the 5.8S rRNA, giving subunits that can join in translation initiation.
