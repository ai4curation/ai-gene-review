Wzy-dependent O-antigen chain-length control

A structural regulation module for Wzy-dependent O-antigen biosynthesis in which the inner-membrane Wzz-family polysaccharide co-polymerase FepE controls very-long modal O-antigen chain length. FepE is a PCP-1 family scaffold/regulator of Wzy polymerase processivity and is not a protein kinase or ferric enterobactin transporter.
