TopAI-YjhQ toxin-antitoxin neutralization

A protein-sequestration module for the Escherichia coli topAI-yjhQ toxin-antitoxin pair in which YjhQ acts as the antitoxin for TopAI/YjhX. YjhQ binding neutralizes TopAI toxicity and relieves TopAI-mediated inhibition of topoisomerase I. YjhQ's predicted GNAT-family acetyltransferase chemistry remains substrate-unknown and is outside this toxin-sequestering module.
