Bacterial undecaprenyl-phosphate carrier supply

A reusable bacterial module that supplies the C55 polyprenyl-phosphate lipid carrier used for cell-envelope glycan assembly. UppS first elongates farnesyl diphosphate with eight isopentenyl diphosphates to form undecaprenyl diphosphate. An integral-membrane undecaprenyl-diphosphatase then removes one phosphate to generate undecaprenyl phosphate. The carrier supports multiple pathways, including peptidoglycan and lipopolysaccharide synthesis; transfer of pathway-specific sugars onto the carrier and downstream glycan polymerization are outside this module.

OpenScientist PSEPK carrier-supply satisfiability review
The two canonical steps are satisfiable with uppS/Q88MH6 and uppP/Q88IY7; PP_0251, PP_0900, and PP_4813 are uncertain PAP2-family redundancy candidates that must not receive blanket GO:0050380 transfer.

The module represents carrier supply, not a peptidoglycan-specific pathway: C55-P is shared by several bacterial envelope-glycan systems. The same phosphatase reaction also returns carrier released as C55-PP after glycan transfer to the usable C55-P pool. A PANTHER term is deliberately omitted for the UppS descriptor because PTHR10291:SF0 contains the correct bacterial exemplars but has the divergent official label "DEHYDRODOLICHYL DIPHOSPHATE SYNTHASE 2"; the exact UniProt exemplars state the intended family without a misleading machine-readable assertion. Species-aware research also identifies three PAP2-family candidate phosphatases, but their broad family membership is insufficient to assign undecaprenyl-diphosphatase activity.
