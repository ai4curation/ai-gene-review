Electron-transfer flavoprotein system

Reusable module for the two-stage electron-transfer flavoprotein (ETF) relay. A soluble alpha/beta ETF heterodimer accepts reducing equivalents from partner flavin-dependent dehydrogenases. ETF:quinone oxidoreductase then reoxidizes ETF and reduces a membrane quinone, linking diverse substrate oxidations to an electron-transport chain. Bacterial ETF systems act in the cytoplasm and plasma membrane; the homologous eukaryotic system acts in the mitochondrial matrix and inner membrane.
