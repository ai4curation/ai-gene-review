COMPASS-family histone H3K4 methyltransferase complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

Drosophila has three COMPASS-family complexes, each built around a different SET-domain histone H3 lysine 4 methyltransferase. Set1/COMPASS performs most bulk H3K4 di- and trimethylation, the Trithorax (Trx) complex maintains expression of Hox and other developmental genes against Polycomb silencing, and the Trithorax-related (Trr) complex monomethylates H3K4 at enhancers and carries the H3K27 demethylase Utx. All three share the WRAD core (Wds, Rbbp5, Ash2 and Dpy-30L1) that stimulates the methyltransferase. Trx is also found in TAC1, a complex with the CBP acetyltransferase Nejire and Sbf.
