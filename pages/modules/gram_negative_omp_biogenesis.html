Gram-negative outer-membrane protein chaperone delivery

A periplasmic carrier-holdase module for Gram-negative beta-barrel outer-membrane protein biogenesis. SurA is the primary route for bulk OMP transit to the BAM/YaeT assembly complex, while the trimeric Skp chaperone provides a parallel ATP-independent holdase route that maintains OMP clients in soluble unfolded states and cooperates with lipopolysaccharide; DegP contributes an optional quality-control branch that can clear or, under some conditions, protect unfolded OMPs in protease-chaperone cages. Sec export across the inner membrane, BAM-mediated membrane insertion itself, and LPS synthesis are outside this focused module.
