Cpx envelope-stress periplasmic quality control

A periplasmic envelope-stress quality-control module coupling the auxiliary Cpx protein CpxP with the stress-induced chaperone Spy. CpxP inhibits the CpxA sensor kinase in the unstressed state and can act as a DegP adaptor for a subset of misfolded periplasmic substrates, while Spy binds non-native periplasmic clients in situ to prevent aggregation and assist refolding. Transcriptional control by CpxR/BaeR and DegP proteolysis itself are outside this CpxP/Spy-focused module.
