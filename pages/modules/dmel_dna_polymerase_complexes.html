B-family DNA polymerase complexes and DNA polymerase gamma in Drosophila melanogaster

Eukaryotic genome replication uses three multisubunit B-family DNA polymerases. DNA polymerase alpha-primase synthesizes the RNA-DNA primers that start leading and lagging strands. DNA polymerase epsilon extends the leading strand and DNA polymerase delta extends the lagging strand and matures Okazaki fragments; both have 3'-5' proofreading exonuclease activity. DNA polymerase zeta, made of the catalytic subunit Rev3 (PolZ1), Rev7 (PolZ2) and the two Pol delta accessory subunits, performs translesion synthesis past DNA damage. DNA polymerase gamma, a family A enzyme made of a catalytic subunit and an accessory subunit, replicates mitochondrial DNA.
