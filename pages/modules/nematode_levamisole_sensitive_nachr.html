Nematode levamisole-sensitive nicotinic acetylcholine receptor

Levamisole-sensitive acetylcholine receptors are nematode Cys-loop cation channels that mediate excitatory body-wall muscle neurotransmission and are selectively activated by the anthelmintic agonist levamisole. In C. elegans, the receptor pore contains five distinct subunits: the non-alpha UNC-29 and LEV-1 subunits plus the alpha subunits UNC-38, UNC-63 and LEV-8. Functional heterologous expression requires three conserved ancillary proteins, RIC-3, UNC-50 and UNC-74, that support receptor biogenesis and trafficking before the assembled receptor reaches postsynaptic membranes. This first draft is grounded in the C. elegans receptor and tracks Brugia malayi, Haemonchus contortus, Onchocerca volvulus and Ascaris suum candidates for later duplicate-resolution and orthology review.

Boundary. This module models the levamisole-sensitive AChR expression system reconstituted by Boulin et al.: the five-subunit C. elegans pore plus the RIC-3, UNC-50 and UNC-74 ancillary factors needed for robust receptor expression. It deliberately excludes the ACR-16 nicotine-sensitive AChR, the ACC/DEG-3/DES-2/MPTL nicotinic-like channels, the avermectin-sensitive GluCl chloride channels, monepantel targets, and GABA/glycine/EXP-1/MOD-1-like Cys-loop channels that also sit in PANTHER PTHR18945. It also leaves synaptic clustering by LEV-10, LEV-9, OIG-4, CRLD-1 and later-acting postsynaptic scaffolds for later extension or a separate trafficking module. Ortholog resolution. PTHR18945 is much broader than the L-AChR pore. The Brugia malayi A0A1P6C5A7, A0A4E9EUJ4 and A0A4E9FKF9 records are plausible UNC-29, UNC-63 and UNC-38 representatives, and live UniProt also exposes ACR-8-lane candidates in Brugia, Haemonchus and Ascaris. The H. contortus reconstitution and resistance literature makes ACR-8 a named parasite L-AChR alpha-subunit lane rather than a simple LEV-8 projection, so parasite pore compositions must be resolved against sequence, gene model and literature before choosing review folders. O. volvulus P54247 is a reviewed non-alpha acetylcholine receptor candidate, but the current O. volvulus PTHR18945 slice is also crowded with unnamed records that may include an ACR-8 ortholog, plus ACR-16-like, glutamate-gated and GABA-like channel records.
