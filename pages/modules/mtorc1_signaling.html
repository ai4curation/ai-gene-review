mTORC1 nutrient, growth-factor and stress signaling at the lysosome/vacuole

A taxon-neutral decomposition of the TORC1 (mTOR complex 1) growth-control module, the conserved kinase hub that couples nutrient, growth-factor and energy status to cell growth. The module fixes the invariant core shared from fungi to metazoa and pushes the lineage-specific variation into explicit variant sets. The invariant core is: (1) the TORC1 holoenzyme, a rapamycin-sensitive serine/threonine kinase (mTOR/Tor) scaffolded by a raptor/Kog1 substrate-adaptor subunit and an MLST8/Lst8 subunit, held off by the DEPTOR and PRAS40 inhibitors; (2) two GTPase switches that gate the kinase at the lysosomal (metazoan) or vacuolar (fungal) membrane - the Rheb switch, whose GTP-bound GTPase is the direct allosteric activator of the kinase and whose GAP is the TSC complex, and the Rag switch, a GTPase heterodimer that recruits the holoenzyme to the membrane and whose RagA/Gtr1 GEF is the Ragulator/EGO complex and whose GAPs are GATOR1/SEACIT (for RagA/Gtr1) and FLCN-FNIP/Lst4-Lst7 (for RagC/Gtr2); and (3) a fixed output set - the translation program (S6K activation, 4E-BP repression relieved), autophagy initiation (ULK1/Atg13 inhibition) and, in metazoa, TFEB/TFE3 regulation. The variable layers are modeled as variant sets: the holoenzyme subunit roster by taxon, the presence of the Rheb/TSC switch by taxon (budding yeast has lost it), and the upstream sensor repertoire (amino-acid sensors SESN2, CASTOR1, SAMTOR, SLC38A9 feeding GATOR2->GATOR1; growth-factor/insulin input via AKT->TSC2; energy input via AMPK->TSC2 and ->raptor; sterol input via LYCHOS). Both GTPase switches embed the generic GTPase nucleotide-switch motif through `conforms_to`. Growth-factor input, energy input and autophagy output are modeled as interfaces to neighbouring modules (MODULE:pi3k_akt_mtor, MODULE:phagophore_assembly_site) rather than re-modeled here.

Boundary decisions. (1) The two GTPase switches are modeled as separate parts rather than one switch because they have different GEF/GAP logic, different outputs (Rheb-GTP allosterically activates the kinase; the Rag heterodimer positions the holoenzyme at the membrane) and different taxon distributions - budding yeast retains the Rag/EGO switch but has lost Rheb and the TSC complex. (2) Single-regulator post-translational events on core nodes that the cached GO-CAMs enumerate one model at a time (OGT/OTUB1 on raptor, RNF152/USP4/ATXN3 on Rheb, RNF167 on SESN2/CASTOR1, KLHL22/UBE4B on DEPDC5/RagC, PELI1 on TSC1, NLK/PKA/TBK1 on raptor) are recorded as gocam_associations or notes on the node they modify, not promoted to parts, to keep the module from regrowing the per-model sprawl. (3) mTORC2 is a distinct complex and module (MODULE:mtorc2_signaling); its only edge to this module is the S6K1 negative feedback onto RICTOR/mSIN1, left as an interface. Representative members and the function terms on each node were verified against the gene reviews in this repository and the two anchor GO-CAMs; family/complex descriptors are used where a verified local exemplar is not yet available (e.g. LYCHOS, TFEB).

The Rheb nucleotide switch. GTP-bound Rheb (Rhb1 in fungi) is the direct allosteric activator of the TORC1 kinase at the membrane; its GAP is the TSC complex (TSC1-TSC2-TBC1D7; TSC2/tuberin carries the GAP domain).
