Bidirectional transsulfuration between homocysteine and cysteine (fungal)

Interconversion of L-homocysteine and L-cysteine through the thioether intermediate L-cystathionine, catalysed by four pyridoxal 5'-phosphate (PLP) dependent enzymes acting in two opposite directions. In the homocysteine-to-cysteine direction, cystathionine beta-synthase condenses L-homocysteine with L-serine to L-cystathionine, and cystathionine gamma-lyase cleaves cystathionine to L-cysteine, 2-oxobutanoate and ammonia; this transfers the sulfur atom of methionine-derived homocysteine onto the serine carbon skeleton and is the only transsulfuration route of mammals. In the cysteine-to-homocysteine direction, cystathionine gamma-synthase condenses L-cysteine with an O-acylated L-homoserine to cystathionine, and cystathionine beta-lyase cleaves cystathionine to L-homocysteine, pyruvate and ammonia; this is the main route to homocysteine in enteric bacteria and plants. Budding yeast and other fungi that assimilate reduced sulfur primarily into the four-carbon chain of homocysteine (by direct sulfhydrylation of O-acetylhomoserine) possess both directions, so they can make cysteine from homocysteine and can also use cysteine as a sole sulfur source for methionine synthesis. Other lineages carry only one direction, and the module is therefore satisfied by either direction or by both.

NEW module (2026-10) generalising YeastPathways HOMOCYS-CYS-CONVERT, CYSTEINE-SYN2-PWY, HOMOCYSDEGR-PWY-1 and the transsulfuration part of PWY-821-1. Boundary: from L-homocysteine or L-cysteine to the other via cystathionine. Homoserine O-acetylation (MET2) and direct sulfhydrylation of O-acetylhomoserine (MET17) are kept in methionine_biosynthesis, whose trans-sulfuration route variant uses the same STR2/STR3 (and MetB/MetC) exemplars and GO terms (GO:0003962, GO:0047804) as the cysteine-to-homocysteine variant here; homocysteine remethylation and the human CBS/CTH disease context are in homocysteine_metabolism, whose transsulfuration arm conforms to the homocysteine-to-cysteine variant here. The CBS/CTH-derived hydrogen sulfide production (GO:0070814, for which CYS4 has an IDA) is a side activity outside the module. Generalisation: the module is fungal in scope because only fungi are known to require both directions, but each direction is a variant that also matches the animal (CBS/CTH) or bacterial/plant (CGS/CBL) arm, and the variant set is ONE_OR_MORE. YeastCyc assessment - all four catalysts are correct; the STR2 assignment rests on disruption genetics only (PMID:10821189), STR3 has been assayed (PMID:22572787). CYS3 also shows cystathionine gamma-synthase activity with O-succinylhomoserine when expressed in E. coli (PMID:1577698), but O-succinylhomoserine is absent from yeast and this is not treated as a physiological route. YeastCyc's "forward" (cysteine to homocysteine) and "reverse" (homocysteine to cysteine) labels are the opposite of the convention in the crosswalk rationale (the S. cerevisiae gene reviews follow YeastCyc); directions are named explicitly here. Compartment is asserted per leaf, following the gene reviews: CYS4 and CYS3 cytoplasm, STR2 cytosol, and STR3 peroxisome (Str3p carries a C-terminal PTS1 and was found in the peroxisome proteome, PMID:11565790), so cystathionine produced by STR2 in the cytosol must reach the peroxisome; no module-level compartment is asserted. CYS4 mitochondrial HDA rows in GOA are not used. GO-CAM comparison - the four YeastPathways models agree with the module on genes and functions but have no causal edges, place STR3 in the cytosol rather than the peroxisome, and CYSTEINE-SYN2-PWY uses cytoplasm where the others use cytosol. In the PomBase model 66a3e0bb00001342 the S. pombe met3 cystathionine gamma-synthase activity is placed part_of GO:0019344 L-cysteine biosynthetic process, although this reaction consumes cysteine and leads to homocysteine (GO:0071269 would fit), and a cystathionine beta-synthase activity is enabled only by a placeholder protein (see knowledge gap). GO:0019346 transsulfuration is obsolete in current GO, so it is not used as a concept.
