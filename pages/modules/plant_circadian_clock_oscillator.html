Arabidopsis core circadian oscillator (CCA1/LHY - PRR - evening complex - ZTL)

The core circadian oscillator of Arabidopsis thaliana is a network of interlocked transcriptional repressor loops that generates a roughly 24 h rhythm in the nucleus. It is reinforced by timed, light-gated proteolysis. At dawn, the single-MYB transcription factors CCA1 and LHY peak and repress evening-phased clock genes (TOC1, ELF4, LUX, ELF3) through evening elements in their promoters. Through the day, the pseudo-response regulators PRR9, PRR7, PRR5 and finally TOC1 (PRR1) accumulate in sequential waves. Each binds target promoters through its C-terminal CCT domain and represses CCA1 and LHY, recruiting TOPLESS/TPR corepressors with histone deacetylase, so that CCA1/LHY expression stays low until the end of the night. At dusk the evening complex (EC) assembles: the scaffold ELF3 bridges the DNA-binding MYB protein LUX and the small protein ELF4. The EC represses PRR9, PRR7, GI and LUX itself through the LUX binding site, closing the evening-to-morning connection and gating growth genes (PIF4/PIF5) to the end of the night. Post-translational control sets the pace. The LOV-domain F-box photoreceptor ZEITLUPE (ZTL), as the substrate adaptor of an SCF E3 ligase, targets phosphorylated TOC1 and PRR5 for proteasomal degradation, mainly in the dark. GIGANTEA binds ZTL in a blue-light-enhanced manner and stabilizes it during the day, so ZTL activity rises at dusk. Light input through phytochromes and cryptochromes, temperature input (sensed partly by the ELF3 prion-like domain), and output pathways such as photoperiodic flowering (GI-CO-FT, CDFs) are outside this module's boundary except where a component acts directly within the oscillator loops.

Part 2: daytime PRR repressor wave
PRR9 -> PRR7 -> PRR5 sequential repressorsRegulatory Stepprr_repressor_wave

Pseudo-response regulators expressed in successive waves from morning (PRR9) through midday (PRR7) to afternoon/evening (PRR5). Each represses CCA1 and LHY through its CCT domain with TPL/TPR corepressors, keeping the morning genes off until the end of the night. The three are partially redundant: the prr9 prr7 prr5 triple mutant is arrhythmic.

Part 5: ZTL-mediated proteolysis of TOC1 and PRR5 (gated by GI and blue light)
SCF-ZTL degradation of TOC1/PRR5Regulatory Stepztl_proteolysis

The LOV/F-box/Kelch photoreceptor ZTL is the substrate adaptor of an SCF E3 ligase that recognizes phosphorylated TOC1 and PRR5 via their pseudo-receiver domains and targets them for 26S proteasomal degradation, mainly in the dark. GIGANTEA binds ZTL in a blue-light-enhanced manner and stabilizes it during the day, so ZTL protein levels oscillate and its activity rises at dusk.
