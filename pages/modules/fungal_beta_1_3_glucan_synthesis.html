Fungal beta-1,3-glucan synthesis at the plasma membrane

Beta-1,3-glucan is the main structural polysaccharide of most fungal cell walls and septa. It is made at the plasma membrane by glucan synthase, a multi-pass GT48 (FKS family) glycosyltransferase. The synthase transfers glucose from cytosolic UDP-glucose to the growing chain at an active site on the cytosolic face and threads the chain through a transmembrane channel into the wall. The small GTPase Rho1 is the regulatory subunit: GTP-bound Rho1 binds the synthase and is needed for its activity. Many fungi have several synthase paralogs with distinct jobs. Saccharomyces cerevisiae has Fks1 and Gsc2/Fks2. Schizosaccharomyces pombe has four: Bgs1 makes the linear beta-1,3-glucan of the primary septum, Bgs4 makes the major branched beta-1,3-glucan of the wall and secondary septum, Bgs2 acts in the spore wall, and Bgs3 is essential but its product is less well defined. Bgs3 works with Ghs2, a Kre6-related membrane protein with an extracellular GH16 domain. In the current model Ghs2 adds beta-1,6-linked branches to the chain Bgs3 extrudes, but that reaction has not been assayed. UDP-glucose supply, synthase trafficking and localization factors, Rho1 GEFs and GAPs, and later cross-linking and remodeling of wall glucan by GH72/GH16/GH17 enzymes are outside the module.

fungiNCBITaxon:4751

NEW module (2026-11), built after reviewing S. pombe bgs1, bgs3, bgs4 and Ghs2 (SPAC17G6.11c). Boundary decisions: (1) Rho1 is inside because it is a structural regulatory subunit of the holoenzyme (PMID:8602515, PMID:40021629). Its GEFs (Rgf1, Rgf3) and other effectors are outside. (2) Synthase partners that only control trafficking or localization (Sbg1 for Bgs1, Smi1, exomer, TRAPP-II, Myo52) are outside. Ghs2 is inside, as an optional third part, because its GH16 catalytic residues are needed and its loss reduces branched glucan, so it is proposed to modify the product, not just deliver the synthase. (3) S. pombe Bgs2 (spore wall) shares PANTHER subfamily SF48 with Fks1/Gsc2 and is mentioned, not modelled. (4) alpha-1,3-glucan (fungal_alpha_1_3_glucan_synthesis: Ags1 with Aah1/Aah3) and chitin (fungal_chitin_chitosan_synthesis) are separate modules. The linear/branched split of GO:0003843 (GO:0140753, GO:0140752) rests on the wall composition of mutants. The Bgs3-Ghs2 finding that a separate GH16 protein makes branches calls into question whether any synthase is itself "branched". This is recorded as a knowledge gap, not changed here.
