Bacterial F-type ATP synthase

Reusable bacterial module for the F1Fo ATP synthase that couples downhill proton movement across the plasma membrane to rotary synthesis of ATP. The soluble F1 sector contains the alpha/beta catalytic head and gamma/epsilon central rotor. The membrane-associated stator holds the catalytic head fixed, while the Fo a/c sector conducts protons and rotates the c ring. The module models these mechanical parts separately so that whole-complex ATP synthase activity is not assigned independently to every subunit.
