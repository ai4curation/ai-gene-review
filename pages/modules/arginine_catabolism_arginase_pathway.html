Arginine catabolism via arginase and ornithine aminotransferase

Arginase and ornithine aminotransferase route arginine through ornithine to glutamate 5-semialdehyde, which equilibrates spontaneously with pyrroline-5-carboxylate (P5C). This comparative module covers demonstrated budding- and fission-yeast, Arabidopsis and human enzymes, without asserting identical flux or compartmentation throughout Eukaryota. In budding yeast, cytosolic P5C reduction makes proline, which can enter mitochondrial proline oxidation and semialdehyde oxidation; anaerobic cultures instead retain proline as an endpoint. Mitochondrial ornithine aminotransferase and P5C dehydrogenase permit direct semialdehyde oxidation in the plant and animal implementations. The aminotransferase is reversible, and its intestinal biosynthetic direction is outside this catabolic boundary. Plant arginases belong to a distinct arginase/agmatinase family. Urea disposal, transmembrane substrate supply, arginine re-synthesis, polyamine synthesis and regulation remain adjoining pathways.

The taxon envelope denotes characterized eukaryotic implementations, not universal presence or physiological equivalence. The full proline detour and anaerobic endpoint have direct budding-yeast evidence; fission-yeast homologs and arginine-to-proline conversion support comparative coverage but do not independently demonstrate every shuttle step. ARG2 is the mitochondrial human exemplar; hepatic ARG1 and the complete ureagenic cycle are outside this boundary. Plant ARGAH1/ARGAH2 belong to PTHR11358, separate from PTHR43782 fungal/animal arginases, and their agmatinase activity is not the activity selected here. Mitochondrial localization supports the catabolic implementation; transient Nicotiana chloroplast localization and context-dependent regulation are preserved in the individual reviews. Human OAT and ALDH4A1 biochemical evidence replaces citations to review YAML files. P5C/GSA equilibration is spontaneous, with the open-chain aldehyde used by P5CDH; it is not assigned to an invented enzyme. SGD imports put mitochondrial PUT1/PUT2 activities in cytosol; primary localization evidence governs this module. The PomBase GO:0000050 annotations are not rejected merely because S. pombe is not a mammalian ureotelic organism: PMID26075619 explicitly models Arg3-dependent arginine re-synthesis. Its isotope data and proposed detailed route are distinguished. Epiarginase inhibition of ARG3 is a regulatory edge outside the catalytic chain. The 2018 arginase review contains an overgeneralized localization/copy-number statement and is not used as authority for it. Native flux is not inferred from family labels alone; exact PANTHER names may reflect a different dominant activity, so selectors retain explicit required functions.
