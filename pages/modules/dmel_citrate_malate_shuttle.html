Citrate-malate shuttle in Drosophila melanogaster

The citrate-malate shuttle moves acetyl units from mitochondria to the cytosol for fatty acid and sterol synthesis and for histone acetylation. Citrate made in the mitochondrial matrix is exported by the mitochondrial citrate carrier, encoded in Drosophila by scheggia (sea), in exchange for malate. In the cytosol ATP-citrate lyase (Acly) cleaves citrate into acetyl-CoA and oxaloacetate, and cytosolic malate dehydrogenase (Mdh1) reduces the oxaloacetate to malate, which can return to the mitochondria through the citrate carrier or be decarboxylated by malic enzyme.
