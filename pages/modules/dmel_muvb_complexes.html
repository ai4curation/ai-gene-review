MuvB-containing transcription complexes in Drosophila melanogaster

MuvB is a conserved core of five proteins (Mip130, Mip120, Mip40, Lin-52 and Caf1-55) that assembles with different partners to repress or activate cell-cycle and differentiation genes. With E2F2, Dp and the retinoblastoma-family proteins Rbf or Rbf2 it forms dREAM, which represses E2F target genes and developmentally regulated genes. With the Myb transcription factor it forms the Myb-MuvB complex (MMB), which activates G2/M genes and also controls site-specific chorion gene amplification. In spermatocytes a testis-specific relative, tMAC, which contains Aly, Comr, Tomb and Topi with Mip40 and Caf1-55, activates the meiotic and spermatid differentiation gene program.
