Bacterial fatty acid beta-oxidation spiral

A four-reaction bacterial mechanism that converts an accepted fatty acyl-CoA into acetyl-CoA and a two-carbon-shorter acyl-CoA. Dehydrogenation, hydration, NAD-dependent oxidation and thiolysis are implemented by distinct enzyme families and compatible multifunctional complexes. The sampled enterobacterial, pseudomonad, mycobacterial and Bacillus systems differ in assembly, substrate range, electron-transfer evidence and native endpoint. Fatty-acid transport/activation, cofactor recycling, unsaturated-substrate auxiliary reactions and odd-chain product disposal are external requirements rather than hidden steps of this core.
