Variobactin and variochelin photoreactive lipopeptide siderophore biosynthesis and transport

A conserved hybrid polyketide synthase / nonribosomal peptide synthetase (PKS/NRPS) biosynthetic and iron-transport module (the var gene cluster) in soil and rhizosphere Variovorax species (exemplified by Variovorax paradoxus P4B GenBank KT362218 producing variobactin A/B, and Variovorax boronicumulans and Variovorax sp. H002 producing variochelins A-E). Downstream of the unreviewed starter NRPS Var3 (ALG65336.1, loading the N-acyl-arginine starter unit), the cytoplasmic hybrid PKS/NRPS/TauD megasynthase Var4 (VarG) extends the N-acyl-arginine starter with a malonate/methylmalonate ketide unit, reduces the beta-keto group, condenses the ketide-extended intermediate with the position-3 aspartate monomer loaded in trans on Var5 (ALG65340.1), and beta-hydroxylates that aspartate residue via its C-terminal TauD domain to install the photoreactive beta-hydroxyaspartate (Hya; reported as L-erythro-Hya for TauD-type hydroxylases in PMID:38590537, though L-threo-Hya was earlier reported for variochelin A in PMID:27023373) ligand; the bimodular NRPS Var6 elongates and epimerizes two internal peptide monomers (established as D-Ser and L-Pro in Variovorax sp. H002 variochelins in PMID:38590537, with Ser/Thr combinatorialization and amino-acid substitutions noted in V. paradoxus P4B variobactin A/B in PMID:26412281); and the terminal bimodular NRPS-thioesterase Var7 incorporates two C-terminal modified N-hydroxyornithine hydroxamate chelators (established as N-delta-acetyl-N-delta-hydroxy-L-ornithine in H002 variochelins and combinatorialized across N-formyl/N-acetyl/unmodified N-hydroxyornithine in P4B variobactin) and cleaves the mature lipopeptide siderophore. The inner-membrane SyrD-family type I ABC half-transporter Var14 exports the apo-siderophore across the cytoplasmic membrane. After extracellular Fe(III) chelation (and optional light-driven Fe(III)-to-Fe(II) photolysis), the outer-membrane TonB-dependent receptor Var8 imports ferric-variobactin/variochelin across the outer membrane, and following uptake into the cytoplasm, the FhuF-family ferric-siderophore reductase Var13 reduces complexed Fe(III) to release bioavailable Fe(II).

Variochelins, Lipopeptide Siderophores from Variovorax boronicumulans Discovered by Genome Mining.
Characterizes the orthologous Variovorax var PKS/NRPS gene cluster and demonstrates that the ferric complexes of its lipopeptide siderophores are photoreactive.
In this paper, we describe the genomics-driven discovery and characterization of variochelins, lipopeptide siderophores from the bacterium Variovorax boronicumulans, which thrives in soil and freshwater habitats. Variochelins are different from most other lipopeptide siderophores in that their biosynthesis involves a polyketide synthase. We demonstrate that the ferric iron complex of variochelin A possesses photoreactive properties and present the MS-derived structures of two degradation products that emerge upon light exposure.

Grounded on the six GOA-annotated proteins of the Variovorax paradoxus strain P4B (NCBITaxon:34073) variobactin gene cluster (GenBank KT362218, 41,899 bp, var1-var14; PMID:26412281) — A0A0N7FMV9 (Var4, ALG65339.1), A0A0N9LX43 (Var6, ALG65341.1), A0A0N9LYB5 (Var7, ALG65342.1), A0A0N9M610 (Var8, ALG65343.1), A0A0N9M354 (Var13, ALG65348.1), and A0A0N9LS79 (Var14, ALG65349.1) — together with comparative characterization and varG::cmR knockout validation of the orthologous variochelin var gene clusters in Variovorax boronicumulans (PMID:27023373) and Variovorax sp. H002 (PMID:38590537). Remaining ORFs in GenBank KT362218 lacking UniProt GOA records account for the upstream starter and intervening assembly-line steps: var1 (ALG65337.1, type II editing thioesterase), var2 (ALG65338.1, 4'-phosphopantetheinyl transferase), var3 (ALG65336.1, starter NRPS loading the N-terminal fatty-acyl and L-arginine units upstream of Var4), var5 (ALG65340.1, intervening NRPS module between var4 and var6 that activates and tethers the position-3 aspartate monomer in trans for Var4's LCL condensation and TauD beta-hydroxylase domains), var9/var10 (ALG65344.1/ALG65345.1, FecR-like anti-sigma factor and ECF sigma-24 subunit paired with Var8's STN domain), and var11/var12 (ALG65346.1/ALG65347.1, ornithine N-monooxygenase and N-acyltransferase supplying the modified N-hydroxyornithine substrates of Var7). Similarly, the inner-membrane transporter mediating periplasm-to-cytoplasm import of ferric-variobactin between outer-membrane Var8 and cytoplasmic Var13 is uncharacterized and recorded as KNOWLEDGE_GAP.
