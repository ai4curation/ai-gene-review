Bacterial branched-chain amino acid catabolism

A reusable bacterial pathway framework for degradation of L-leucine, L-isoleucine, and L-valine. Reversible branched-chain amino-acid transamination supplies the corresponding 2-oxo acids, and a branched-chain 2-oxoacid dehydrogenase complex converts those intermediates to branched-chain acyl-CoA thioesters. Substrate-specific downstream routes then produce acetyl-CoA, acetoacetate, and/or propionyl-CoA. The module does not impose a cellular compartment and separates conserved entry chemistry from downstream branches whose enzyme families and gene assignments vary among bacteria.
