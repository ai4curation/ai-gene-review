3-Hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate CO2 fixation cycle (Sulfolobales)

The autotrophic CO2 fixation cycle of the Sulfolobales. Acetyl-CoA is carboxylated to malonyl-CoA and reduced through malonate semialdehyde and 3-hydroxypropionate to propionyl-CoA, which is carboxylated a second time and rearranged to succinyl-CoA. Succinyl-CoA is reduced through succinate semialdehyde to 4-hydroxybutyrate, which is activated, dehydrated to crotonyl-CoA and converted through acetoacetyl-CoA into two molecules of acetyl-CoA. One acetyl-CoA restarts the cycle, so each turn fixes two bicarbonate molecules into one acetyl-CoA. Several enzymes act twice: one carboxylase acts on acetyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA, one reductase on malonyl-CoA and succinyl-CoA, and one crotonase-family enzyme dehydrates 3-hydroxypropionyl-CoA and hydrates crotonyl-CoA.

Concrete module for the crenarchaeal (Sulfolobales) form of the cycle, with M. sedula DSM 5348 proteins as representative members. GO:0043427 carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate cycle is deliberately not used: it is defined as the Chloroflexus pathway that fixes CO2 into glyoxylate, which is a different cycle. The gene reviews propose 'carbon fixation by 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate cycle' and four missing activity terms; four steps therefore carry a function descriptor without a GO id. Thaumarchaeota run a related cycle with partly convergent enzymes (PMID:33472982) and are outside this module.
