NuA3 histone acetyltransferase complex in Saccharomyces cerevisiae

NuA3 (nucleosomal acetyltransferase of histone H3) is a Gcn5-independent MYST-family histone acetyltransferase of budding yeast that acetylates lysine 14 of histone H3 on nucleosomes at actively transcribed genes. Its catalytic subunit Sas3 is the yeast counterpart of MOZ/MORF, and Nto1, Yng1 and Eaf6 correspond to the BRPF scaffold, ING reader and MEAF6 subunits of the metazoan MOZ/MORF and HBO1 complexes. In the cryo-EM structure, Sas3 and Nto1 form the core of the complex and together build the cleft that binds the H3 tail and selects lysine 14; the N-terminal helices of Yng1, the C-terminal helix of Nto1 and Eaf6 form a helical bundle beside the core; and the extra-terminal domain of Taf14, a subunit shared with TFIID, TFIIF and three chromatin remodelers, docks onto Sas3. Yng1 is required for NuA3 to bind and acetylate nucleosomes but not for complex integrity. NuA3 exists in two forms that differ in how they are targeted: NuA3a binds H3K4me3 at the 5' ends of genes through the Yng1 PHD finger, and NuA3b, which carries the additional PWWP subunit Pdp3, binds H3K36me3 in transcribed regions. The two readers recruit NuA3 independently, but NuA3 occupancy alone does not ensure acetylation of the bound nucleosomes. NuA3 promotes transcription of a subset of genes, NuA3b acts as a positive elongation factor, and NuA3 opposes the Rpd3S and Rpd3L deacetylases to set the induction kinetics of genes they repress. NuA3a, but not NuA3b, is essential in cells lacking the SAGA acetyltransferase Gcn5.

Histone H3 is the substrate. The FACT subunit Spt16, which binds Sas3 (PMID:10817755), the Set1 and Set2 methyltransferases that write the marks NuA3 reads, and the Rpd3S and Rpd3L deacetylases that oppose NuA3 are outside the boundary. Taf14's other host complexes belong to their own modules. Two forms. NuA3a and NuA3b are modelled as a variant set along the axis of the methylated-histone reader that targets the complex. The five-subunit core is shared; Pdp3 is the NuA3b-specific subunit. UniProt describes NuA3b as NuA3a plus Pdp3, and peptide pulldowns show that part of NuA3 carries both Yng1 and Pdp3, with the Yng1 N-terminal region holding Pdp3 on the core (PMID:33010166), so Yng1 is modelled as a shared part. Selection is ONE_OR_MORE because both readers recruit NuA3 in the same cells, independently (PMID:28108585). Whether the forms are separate complexes is recorded as a knowledge gap. Taf14 contacts. Nguyen et al. found that the Taf14 extra-terminal domain binds short motifs in both Sas3 and Yng1 and that both contacts are needed for NuA3 function (PMID:38914563). In the cryo-EM structure the Taf14 extra-terminal domain binds the Sas3 motif, while the Yng1 motif lies far from it (PMID:41318527). The Sas3 contact is common to both; the Yng1 contact is recorded in the Taf14 role description as reported but not seen in the structure. Ontology and chromatin. The module concept is GO:0033100 NuA3 histone acetyltransferase complex, which a reasoner classifies under GO:0000123 histone acetyltransferase complex and so under its part_of GO:0000785 chromatin. NuA3 acetylates histone H3 on nucleosomes (PMID:10817755, PMID:25473596, PMID:41318527), so the inherited chromatin location is reasonable for this complex, unlike the HAT-B and Rtt109-Vps75 complexes, which act on histones before deposition (modules/scer_hat_b_complex.yaml, modules/scer_rtt109_vps75_complex.yaml). The definition of GO:1990468 NuA3b histone acetyltransferase complex names the wrong mark for Pdp3; a correction is recorded under proposed_ontology_changes. Gene reviews. SAS3, NTO1, YNG1, EAF6 and TAF14 have gene reviews in this repository; Pdp3 has none. The Sas3, Nto1, Yng1 and Taf14 annotons use the core molecular functions of those reviews: histone H3K14 acetyltransferase activity (Sas3), histone binding contributing to that activity (Nto1), histone H3K4me3 reader activity (Yng1) and histone H3K9ac reader activity (Taf14). The Eaf6 annoton carries no molecular function because none is known for Eaf6 in NuA3.
