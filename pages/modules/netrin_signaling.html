Netrin signaling pathway module

Netrins are secreted laminin-related guidance cues. A netrin binds receptors of the DCC family (vertebrate DCC, Drosophila Frazzled) on a responding cell to mediate attraction, and receptors of the UNC5 family to mediate repulsion, steering migrating cells and axons toward or away from the netrin source.

They act through receptors of the DCC and UNC5 families. DCC receptors have been implicated in both attraction and repulsion by Netrins. UNC5 receptors are required only for repulsion.

Boundary. The module has two steps: a secreted netrin ligand, and a netrin receptor on the responding cell, with attractive (DCC family) and repulsive (UNC5 family) receptor variants. Intracellular transducers (FAK/PTK2, SRC-family kinases, TRIM9, Rho-family GTPase regulators), the co-receptor candidates DSCAM and neogenin, and laminins in the same PANTHER family are not modelled as parts. The module is species-neutral; the representative members (human NTN1, NTN3, DCC and UNC5A-D; Drosophila NetA, NetB, fra and unc-5) are examples, not a species scope. Ligand function term. The ligand step uses GO:0048018 receptor ligand activity, following the GO-CAM signaling receptor guideline ("a ligand 'enables' receptor ligand activity (GO:0048018) or a child"). Neither chemoattractant nor chemorepellent activity fits on its own because netrins do both. The receptor-specific GO:1990890 netrin receptor binding is not used: it records binding only, is not under receptor ligand activity, and in GOA is carried by receptor-side proteins (DSCAM, tubulins) rather than by netrins. The NTN1 and NTN3 gene reviews use receptor ligand activity as their core function. Annotation state (GOA GAFs dated 2026-05-21). Human: UNC5A-D are annotated to GO:0038007, but DCC, NTN1, NTN3 and NTN4 are not, and no netrin has a receptor ligand activity MF. Drosophila: fra and unc-5 are annotated to GO:0038007 (IEA), but NetA and NetB are annotated only to axon guidance terms and have no MF annotation.
