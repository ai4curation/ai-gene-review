JAK-STAT signaling as a Boolean network

Boolean network derived from module 12 variables · 14 signed edges modules/jak_stat_signaling.yaml

The module's connections read as a signed regulatory graph and translated by ai-gene-review module-to-bnet. Each negative regulator is wired where the module says it acts: SOCS and the phosphatases on JAK, the phosphatases and PIAS on nuclear STATs. STAT transcription carries activation_logic because ERK serine phosphorylation of STATs only tunes maximal transcription, so it is declared an omitted regulator. In every scenario the phosphatase, PIAS and JAK-independent STAT inputs are held off unless stated.

Boxes are steps of the moduleEach is either ON (filled) or OFF. Grey arrows activate; orange flat-headed lines inhibit.
