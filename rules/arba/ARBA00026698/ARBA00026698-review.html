Description

Assigns GO:0007034 vacuolar transport through 40 alternative condition sets, each a conjunction of InterPro or CATH FunFam signatures with a taxon. The sets cover ESCRT-III/Snf7-family proteins, ESCRT-I and ESCRT-II subunits, Vps4, sortilin/Vps10 and plant vacuolar sorting receptors, vacuolar syntaxins, GGA/VHS adaptors, NEDD4/Rsp5-type ubiquitin ligases and a few other families.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all 40 live condition sets were examined. Twenty-six match Swiss-Prot entries, and in every one of them at least one matched entry carries GO:0007034 (vacuolar transport) or a descendant with experimental evidence; no matched Swiss-Prot entry lacks the term. The other 14 sets are Archaea or Bacteria copies of eukaryotic sets and match no UniProtKB entry. Meaning and parsimony: the working sets all concern traffic to the vacuole or lysosome, but they fall into four groups that curated annotation distinguishes: protein targeting to the vacuole by sorting receptors (4 sets), the ESCRT and multivesicular body route from late endosomes (10), lysosomal transport in animals (9) and Golgi-to-vacuole SNAREs and adaptors (3). Each group has a child term of the consequent, and rules for three of those terms already match 8 of the working sets exactly. A third of the rule (14 sets) can never fire, and set 1 repeats an InterPro2GO mapping.

Action Rationale

The term is true and the families are related, so this is a mild case, but the curated annotations sit one level lower in every set and the lower-level rules exist (ARBA00087315, ARBA00033548, ARBA00026727, ARBA00086886). Splitting the 26 working sets among those terms gives each family the route it actually serves; the 14 empty sets should simply be deleted.

Assessments

REDUNDANT

Fourteen of 40 sets match no UniProtKB entry. Of the 26 that do, 8 are matched exactly by a rule for a descendant term (sets 1, 13, 21, 24 and 29 by ARBA00033548, GO:0007041 (lysosomal transport); sets 3 and 7 by ARBA00087315, GO:0006623 (protein targeting to vacuole); set 18 by ARBA00026727 and ARBA00086453) and 9 more working sets (9, 14, 19, 20, 22, 23, 30, 36, 39) share signatures with such a rule under a different taxon (rest.uniprot.org/arba search over all 35 descendants of the consequent, 2026-11-12). Sets 3 and 7 both select the plant vacuolar sorting receptors (8 Swiss-Prot entries each), and sets 5 and 8 both select fungal sortilins (63 Swiss-Prot entries each), by different signatures. rules/arba/_interpro2go.txt maps IPR005024 to the consequent (line 'InterPro:IPR005024 Snf7 family > GO:vacuolar transport ; GO:0007034'), so set 1, the same entry restricted to Primates, adds nothing to InterPro2GO.

STRONG

Curated experimental GO annotations on matched Swiss-Prot entries are listed per set in rule.condition_sets[].notes (QuickGO, November 2026), for example VPS10_YEAST GO:0006623 IMP PMID:8187177 (set 5), VPS4_YEAST GO:0045324 IMP PMID:9211789 (set 9) and SYP42_ARATH GO:0007034 IGI PMID:22307646 (set 14).

TOO_BROAD

Experimental annotation on the matched proteins is to children of the consequent: GO:0045324 (late endosome to vacuole transport) tops 9 sets, GO:0007041 (lysosomal transport) 8, GO:0006623 (protein targeting to vacuole) 6 and GO:0006896 (Golgi to vacuole transport) 3. The consequent is their common parent and is one level too high for every set.

APPROPRIATE

The taxa on the working sets mostly follow the biology: plant receptors and syntaxins in Streptophyta, Embryophyta or eudicotyledons, fungal ESCRT and sortilin sets in Fungi or Dikarya, lysosomal factors in animal clades. Some animal sets are narrower than the families (Primates for CHMPs in sets 1 and 13, Muridae for LAMP2 in set 21, Haplorrhini for HOOK in set 23), which is narrow coverage. The Archaea and Bacteria sets are unnecessary: their signatures do not occur there.
