Description

Assigns GO:0047545 ((S)-2-hydroxyglutarate dehydrogenase activity) to proteins that have the FAD dependent oxidoreductase domain (IPR006076), the FAD/NAD(P)-binding domain superfamily (IPR036188) and membership of PANTHER family PTHR43104 (L-2-hydroxyglutarate dehydrogenase, mitochondrial).

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: the live rule has one condition set, a three-way conjunction that selects the L-2-hydroxyglutarate dehydrogenase family. All 17 reviewed entries that match it carry GO:0047545 ((S)-2-hydroxyglutarate dehydrogenase activity), 14 by manual evidence, including experimentally characterised enzymes from human, Arabidopsis, Drosophila and Escherichia coli. Meaning and parsimony: the set selects one enzyme family and the consequent is the specific activity of that family, with no more specific descendant in use. The InterPro conditions are needed: PANTHER family PTHR43104 alone matches 200 reviewed entries, most of them malate:quinone oxidoreductases, and the conjunction cuts these to the 17 L-2-hydroxyglutarate dehydrogenases.

Action Rationale

One family, one specific term, and conditions that are necessary to exclude the malate:quinone oxidoreductase branch of the PANTHER family. The rule is accurate, coherent and economical.
