Description

Assigns GO:0007031 (peroxisome organization) through 12 alternative condition sets that identify peroxins (PEX5, PEX7, PEX11, PEX12, PEX14/PEX33, the PEX28-PEX32 family), the dynamin-related fission GTPases DNM1/DRP3/DNM1L and VPS1, plant FIS1, and the fungal kinases KIN4 and FRK1.

Review Summary

All 12 live condition sets were examined by retrieving the Swiss-Prot entries that match the whole conjunction and looking up their curated annotations. Every set has at least one Swiss-Prot match with an experimental annotation to GO:0007031 or an is_a/part_of descendant (protein import into peroxisome matrix and its steps, peroxisome fission, peroxisome inheritance). The dynamin, VPS1 and FIS1 sets match proteins that also act in mitochondrial fission; their peroxisome annotations are experimental. Set 8 matches S. cerevisiae KIN4 and FRK1, annotated to peroxisome inheritance by IGI from one paper. Sets 7 and 12 have no taxon condition; set 7 also matches Toxoplasma gondii DrpA (S8EYU3), which has no curated annotation to the term. The check uses Swiss-Prot, the source ARBA rules are mined from, and does not measure precision on unreviewed entries.

Action Rationale

Each condition set identifies a characterised peroxisome biogenesis, fission or inheritance factor, and each has reviewed members with experimental support for the term or a descendant. Confidence is reduced by set 8, which rests on one genetic-interaction paper, and by the two dynamin sets without a taxon condition.
