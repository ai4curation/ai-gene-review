Review Summary

The first 10 of the 375 live condition sets were examined. They select aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases, RNase III helicases, pseudouridine synthases, terminal nucleotidyltransferases, plant RNA-editing factors and queuine tRNA-ribosyltransferases. In the sets for which both counts were obtained, 2794 of 2794 reviewed matches carry GO:0016070 or a descendant. The other 365 sets were not examined.

Action Rationale

The rule was sampled, not examined set by set, so no verdict is given. The sampled sets are sound, which removes the grounds of the earlier verdict but does not establish that every set is right. The counts are from Swiss-Prot, which is also what ARBA learns its rules from, so they show the condition sets are coherent rather than giving an independent error rate for unreviewed entries.
