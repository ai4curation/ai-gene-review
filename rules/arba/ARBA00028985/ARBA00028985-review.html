Description

Assigns GO:0016070 (RNA metabolic process) through 375 alternative condition sets built from InterPro entries, PANTHER families and CATH FunFams, about a quarter of them with a taxon.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: the first 10 of the 375 live condition sets were examined. They select aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases, Dicer-type RNase III helicases, pseudouridine synthases, terminal nucleotidyltransferases, plant RNA-editing factors, eL30-fold proteins and queuine tRNA-ribosyltransferases, and 2794 of 2794 reviewed matches carry GO:0016070 (RNA metabolic process) or a descendant. The other 365 sets were not checked against proteins. Meaning and parsimony: GO:0016070 has 507 descendants and is the root of nearly all RNA biology. The sampled sets resolve into seven unrelated units, and for each one curated annotation gives a far more specific descendant: tRNA aminoacylation, pre-miRNA processing, pseudouridine synthesis, RNA 3'-end processing, organellar RNA modification, rRNA processing and tRNA wobble guanine modification. No sampled unit is curated to the consequent itself. 289 of the rule's sets are already matched by rules asserting a more specific term, so most of the rule emits nothing, and what it does emit is the least informative thing that can be said about an RNA enzyme.

Action Rationale

The rule is an umbrella over families that each have a better term, and most of it is already superseded. It should be split toward the family-level terms. The judgement is from the 10 recorded sets (the first ten of the rule) plus the rule-wide redundancy count; the proportion of covered sets (289 of 371 in the redundancy table) indicates that the sample is representative of the rest.

Assessments

REDUNDANT

rule_redundancy.tsv: 293 of 375 sets are matched by a rule asserting a more specific term, and so emit nothing. UniProt ARBA API 2026-11-12: 375 sets, none whose conditions are a superset of another's. In the sample, sets 1, 6, 7 and 10 are four separate constructions for aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases, each a broad class-level signature with a taxon.

TOO_BROAD

Every unit in the sample carries a descendant several levels below GO:0016070 (QuickGO): GO:0006418, GO:0031054, GO:0001522, GO:0031123, GO:1900865, GO:0006364, GO:0002099.

UNNECESSARY

83 of 375 sets carry a taxon. In the sample the taxa do not follow biology: aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase class signatures are limited to Saccharomycotina, Pseudomonadota, Archaea and Euteleostomi in turn, and the pseudouridine synthase superfamily to Primates, although the enzymes and the process are universal. These are artefacts of where the training annotations were; they narrow coverage and make no annotation wrong. On set 8 the genus restriction is what keeps a fold-level entry from spreading further.
