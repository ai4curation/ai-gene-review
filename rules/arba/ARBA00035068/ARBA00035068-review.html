Description

Assigns GO:0004438 phosphatidylinositol-3-phosphate phosphatase activity through two condition sets: CATH FunFam 2.30.29.30:FF:000038 in Primates (MTM1, MTMR1, MTMR2) and CATH FunFam 3.30.40.10:FF:000073 in Eukaryota (the FYVE-domain region of MTMR3 and MTMR4).

Review Summary

Both live condition sets were checked against Swiss-Prot. Set 1 is supported: its 5 reviewed matches all carry GO:0004438 with non-electronic evidence. Set 2 is not specific to myotubularins. Of its 19 reviewed matches, 6 are MTMR3 or MTMR4 experimentally annotated to GO:0004438, for example Q9NYA4 MTMR4 by IDA from PMID:11302699, and 13 are insect Lst2 homologues that have a FYVE domain but no phosphatase domain and carry GO:0004438 only from this rule (e.g. Q9VB70, IEA GO_REF:0000117 with ARBA:ARBA00035068). In UniProtKB as a whole, 126 of 3192 matches of set 2 (3.9%) lack the myotubularin phosphatase domain IPR010569.

Action Rationale

Set 2 rests on a FunFam of the FYVE zinc-finger region, which MTMR3 and MTMR4 share with unrelated FYVE proteins. The rule therefore assigns a phosphatase activity to Lst2 homologues that have no phosphatase domain. Set 1 is sound.
