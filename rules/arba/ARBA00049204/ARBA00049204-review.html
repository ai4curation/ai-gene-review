Description

Assigns the superoxide dismutase reaction 2 superoxide + 2 H(+) = H2O2 + O2 (RHEA:20696, EC 1.15.1.1) through 23 alternative condition sets. Sets 1 and 3 to 8 and the FunFams of superfamilies 1.10.287.990 and 3.55.40.20 identify iron/manganese superoxide dismutases; set 2 and the FunFams of superfamily 2.60.40.200 identify copper/zinc superoxide dismutases. Most sets carry a taxon condition.

APPROPRIATE

The live sets that use copper/zinc signatures match only EC 1.15.1.1 proteins in Swiss-Prot (set 2: 42 of 42).
