Description

Assigns the catalytic activity EC 2.3.2.27 (RING-type E3 ubiquitin transferase) through 304 alternative condition sets built from InterPro entries, PANTHER families and CATH FunFams, 55 of them with a taxon.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: 7 of the 304 live condition sets were examined, the first six and the one bacterial set. They select RING-finger, U-box, Bre1, SIAH and plant ATL families and the bacterial novel E3 ligase effectors, and every reviewed match carries EC 2.3.2.27 (254 of 254, summed over sets). The rule contains no HECT-domain condition. The other 297 sets were not checked against proteins. Meaning and parsimony: EC 2.3.2.27 names one enzyme class, RING-type E3 ubiquitin transferases, and six of the seven recorded sets select families of that class. Two units do not belong to it by mechanism. Set 12 selects IpaH/SspH-type bacterial effectors, which UniProt records as forming a glycyl thioester intermediate on an active-site cysteine, unlike RING and U-box ligases. And a scan of the names of all conditions in the live rule found one set built from three PIAS FunFams (not among the sets recorded here): it gives the ubiquitin transferase reaction to E3 SUMO-protein ligases, on the strength of the EC number that Swiss-Prot puts on PIAS4 alone among the PIAS proteins.

Action Rationale

The sampled RING and U-box sets are sound and the rule is a coherent statement about one enzyme class, apart from the two units named. The PIAS set propagates a ubiquitin-transfer reaction to SUMO ligases and should be removed; set 12 reproduces a curated EC assignment that fits the overall reaction but not the RING-type mechanism, and is flagged, not removed. The verdict rests on a seven-set sample plus a name scan of all 304 sets, hence the moderate confidence.

Biological Meaning

COHERENT

Judged on the 7 recorded sets (the first six of the rule plus the bacterial set, chosen by the first pass) and on a scan of condition names across all 304 sets for SUMO, HECT, RBR and E2 terms, which found only the PIAS set. RING-finger and U-box families share the direct E2-to-substrate transfer that EC 2.3.2.27 describes. rule_redundancy.tsv has no row for catalytic activity rules.

The consequent term has descendant terms. condition set(s) are also matched by a rule asserting a more specific term. 7 condition set(s) were examined for this judgement.
