Review Summary

Accuracy: every one of the 2,158 live condition sets was put through one check in this pass, namely whether its Swiss-Prot matches carry the keyword (UniProtKB queries, November 2026); 35 sets were also read in detail and are recorded. In 2,157 sets every Swiss-Prot match carries KW-0560 Oxidoreductase: summed over sets, 76,705 of 76,705 matches. The remaining set, 1969, matches no UniProtKB entry at all. No protein wrongly given the keyword was found; the earlier report on the copper chaperone for superoxide dismutase could not be reproduced. Meaning and parsimony: the keyword names EC class 1, and the sets select oxidoreductase families: in the sample the reviewed matches are dehydrogenases acting on CH-OH groups (10 sets), oxygenases and oxidases (7) and enzymes of nine other EC 1 subclasses (16). One sampled set selects the RuBisCO large subunit, an EC 4 enzyme that Swiss-Prot also labels Oxidoreductase for its oxygenase reaction. That is the level at which the keyword exists, so the rule is coherent. One set is idle; none duplicates or contains another.

Confidence is modest because the check on 2,157 sets is the reviewed entries' own keyword, not an inspection of the 7.2 million TrEMBL entries the rule annotates.
