Review Summary

The live rule has one condition set, FunFam 1.20.1730.10:FF:000006 in Fungi, and it was examined. Its Swiss-Prot matches are S. cerevisiae DUR3 (P33413), C. albicans DUR3 (A0A1D8PDB5, named Spermidine transporter DUR3) and S. pombe dur3-1 (O94469). P33413 has spermidine transmembrane transporter activity by IDA and IMP from PMID:17218313, and A0A1D8PDB5 by IMP from PMID:22033918; GO:0015606 is part_of GO:1903711. The MFS multidrug transporter set that an earlier version of the rule contained (FunFam 1.20.1250.20:FF:000011 in Taphrinomycotina) is no longer in the rule.

Action Rationale

The only condition set identifies fungal DUR3 orthologs, and two of the three reviewed matches have experimental evidence of spermidine transport. Confidence is limited because 1,445 unreviewed fungal entries match and S. pombe has several dur3 paralogs whose substrates have not been tested.
