Description

Assigns GO:0140115 (export across plasma membrane) through 14 alternative condition sets that identify efflux transporters: bacterial SMR and Emr multidrug exporters, fungal MFS and ABC drug exporters, plant ABCB auxin transporters, metazoan ABCG2, sodium-exporting P-type ATPases, Na+/K+-ATPase alpha subunits, fungal Na+/H+ antiporters and Na+/Ca2+ exchangers.

Review Summary

All 14 live condition sets were examined by retrieving the Swiss-Prot entries that match the whole conjunction and looking up their curated annotations. Every set has at least one Swiss-Prot match with an experimental annotation to GO:0140115 or an is_a/part_of descendant: xenobiotic detoxification by transmembrane export across the plasma membrane (GO:1990961), sodium, potassium or calcium ion export across plasma membrane (GO:0036376, GO:0097623, GO:1990034), auxin export across the plasma membrane (GO:0010315) and L-cysteine export across plasma membrane (GO:0033228). In several sets only some of the reviewed matches are annotated; for example 4 of 47 in set 3 and 2 of 8 in set 12. The check uses Swiss-Prot, the source ARBA rules are mined from, and does not measure precision on unreviewed entries. The curator concern in geneontology/go-annotation issue 6140 could not be read during this review, so the set or protein it refers to is not identified here.

Action Rationale

Each condition set is a narrow conjunction, usually with a taxon condition, and each has reviewed members with experimental evidence of export across the plasma membrane. The parent term is true of all of them and is less specific than the substrate-specific terms the reviewed members carry. Confidence is moderate because the unannotated reviewed matches were not checked individually and the cited curator issue was not available.
