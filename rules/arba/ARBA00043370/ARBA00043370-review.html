Description

Assigns GO:0140115 (export across plasma membrane) through 14 alternative condition sets that identify efflux transporters: bacterial SMR and Emr multidrug exporters, fungal MFS and ABC drug exporters, plant ABCB auxin transporters, metazoan ABCG2, sodium-exporting P-type ATPases, Na+/K+-ATPase alpha subunits, fungal Na+/H+ antiporters and Na+/Ca2+ exchangers.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all 14 live condition sets were examined by retrieving the Swiss-Prot entries that match the whole conjunction and looking up their curated annotations. Every set has at least one Swiss-Prot match with an experimental annotation to GO:0140115 (export across plasma membrane) or an is_a/part_of descendant. In several sets only some reviewed matches are annotated, for example 4 of 47 in set 3 and 2 of 8 in set 12. The curator concern in geneontology/go-annotation issue 6140 could not be read, so the set it refers to is not identified. Meaning and parsimony: the 14 sets select unrelated exporter families that share only the direction of transport. They resolve into multidrug efflux transporters, plant ABCB auxin exporters, sodium-exporting pumps and exchangers, and fungal citrate exporters. Curated annotation gives the first three groups substrate-specific descendants: GO:1990961 (xenobiotic detoxification by transmembrane export across the plasma membrane), GO:0010315 (auxin export across the plasma membrane) and GO:0036376 (sodium ion export across plasma membrane). Rules asserting those three terms already cover 7 of the 14 sets (1, 3, 5, 9, 14; 2; 8), which therefore emit nothing here. What the substrate is, is the informative part of an export annotation, and the consequent leaves it out.

Action Rationale

Each family has a better term than the parent, and for half the sets a rule asserting that better term already exists. The remaining sets should follow the same pattern: assert the substrate-specific term of their unit. Only the citrate exporter set, whose members are curated to GO:0140115 itself, has no narrower curated term. Confidence is moderate because the reviewed matches without annotations were not checked one by one and because two units have mixed substrates (sodium or potassium for the ENA ATPases, sodium or calcium for the exchangers).

Biological Meaning

TRUE BUT UNINFORMATIVE

Four units from different transporter superfamilies (MFS, SMR, ABC, P-type ATPase, cation antiporters), related only by exporting something across the plasma membrane. Three carry substrate-specific descendants of the consequent by experiment. The term is true of all; it is the least that can be said of each.

rule_redundancy.tsv: go_descendants 19, condition_sets 14, covered_sets 7, covered_by_terms GO:1990961, GO:0010315, GO:0036376. TOO_BROAD

The reviewed members of 13 of 14 sets carry substrate-specific is_a/part_of descendants of GO:0140115: GO:1990961, GO:0010315, GO:0036376, GO:0097623, GO:1990034 and GO:0033228 (labels in biological_units). Only set 12 is curated at the parent.

TOO_NARROW

Several restrictions follow the species of the supporting annotations and not the family's range: Rattus for the Na+/Ca2+ exchangers (set 4), Primates for Na+/K+-ATPase alpha (set 8), Escherichia for SMR exporters (set 3), Debaryomycetaceae for ENA ATPases (set 7). That is narrow coverage, not wrong annotation. Set 1 (three InterPro and PANTHER signatures) and set 10 have no taxon condition.
