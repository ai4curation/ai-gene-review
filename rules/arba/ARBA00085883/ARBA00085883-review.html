Review Summary

All four live condition sets were examined. The Swiss-Prot matches of every set carry a descendant of GO:0030167 with experimental evidence: beta-galactosidase GLB1 (keratan sulfate proteoglycan catabolic process, IMP), alpha-L-iduronidase (dermatan sulfate and heparan sulfate proteoglycan catabolic process, IDA and IMP), mouse HEXA and HEXB (dermatan sulfate proteoglycan catabolic process, IGI) and N-acetylglucosamine-6-sulfatase (heparan sulfate proteoglycan catabolic process, IDA and IMP). Beta-galactosidase and beta-hexosaminidase also degrade gangliosides; that is a further function, not a reason to withhold this one.

Action Rationale

Each set selects one lysosomal enzyme family whose reviewed members are experimentally annotated to a child of the rule's term. The taxon conditions on sets 1 and 3 are narrow, which limits coverage but does not make the annotation wrong.
