Review Summary

Accuracy: the first 10 of the 392 live condition sets were examined. Six match reviewed entries, and 78 of 79 of those entries carry GO:0048468 (cell development) or a descendant, 48 by manual evidence. Four of the ten repeat the conditions of a neighbouring set for Archaea or Bacteria and match no UniProtKB entry. The other 382 sets were not checked against proteins. Meaning and parsimony: GO:0048468 has 672 descendants. The sampled sets are domain architectures (BTB with C2H2 zinc fingers, SH2 with SH3, the tyrosine phosphatase domain, hedgehog, peptidase M17) restricted to an animal clade, and their reviewed members reach the term through unrelated developmental phenotypes: axon guidance for Abl, Ptp69D, lola and Shh, spermatid development for the Drosophila sperm leucyl aminopeptidases and Dhh, photoreceptor development for ttk. The consequent is the common ancestor of those phenotype terms, not a property the architectures share. Across the whole rule, 103 sets of conditions occur three times, once each for Archaea, Bacteria and Eukaryota, so 206 of the 392 sets are copies of animal developmental families under prokaryotic taxa; all four such copies in the sample match nothing.

Action Rationale

The term is true of the reviewed matches and uninformative, and it fits the units for unrelated reasons, so the rule is heterogeneous. More specific rules already cover 76 sets. The remedy is to drop the prokaryotic copies, re-point each remaining family to the developmental term its members are curated to, and remove sets that generalise one paralog group's phenotype to a whole enzyme family. Based on 10 of 392 sets, with the triplication measured on the whole rule.
