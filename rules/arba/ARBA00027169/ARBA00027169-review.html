Description

Assigns GO:0120114 Sm-like protein family complex through 27 alternative condition sets of InterPro and CATH FunFam signatures, some with a taxon. The sets cover Sm and Lsm proteins and other protein components of the U1, U2, U5, U6, U7 and U11/U12 snRNPs.

Review Summary

All 27 live condition sets were examined. Every set matches Swiss-Prot entries, and in each at least one matched entry carries GO:0120114 or a descendant with experimental evidence; no matched Swiss-Prot entry lacks the term. Spliceosomal snRNPs are subclasses of GO:0120114 (GO:0030532 small nuclear ribonucleoprotein complex is_a GO:0120114), so snRNP proteins without an Sm fold, such as SF3B1, PRPF8, BRR2 and Snu114, are correctly covered.

Action Rationale

Each set selects snRNP or Lsm-complex components with experimental cellular-component annotation under the rule's term. Narrow taxon conditions on some sets (for example Rattus in set 15) limit coverage but do not make the annotation wrong.
