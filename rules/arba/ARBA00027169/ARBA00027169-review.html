Description

Assigns GO:0120114 Sm-like protein family complex through 27 alternative condition sets of InterPro and CATH FunFam signatures, some with a taxon. The sets cover Sm and Lsm proteins and other protein components of the U1, U2, U5, U6, U7 and U11/U12 snRNPs.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all 27 live condition sets were examined. Every set matches Swiss-Prot entries, and in each at least one matched entry carries GO:0120114 (Sm-like protein family complex) or a descendant with experimental evidence; no matched Swiss-Prot entry lacks the term. Spliceosomal snRNPs are subclasses of the term (GO:0030532 (small nuclear ribonucleoprotein complex) is_a GO:0120114 (Sm-like protein family complex)), so snRNP proteins without an Sm fold, such as SF3B1, PRPF8, BRR2 and Snu114, are covered correctly. Meaning and parsimony: 24 of the 27 sets (4 to 27) select proteins of a particular snRNP or of the Lsm2-8 ring, and for all 24 a rule asserting a more specific term already uses the same signatures: ARBA00033820 asserts GO:0030532 (small nuclear ribonucleoprotein complex) for 22 of them and ARBA00089766 asserts GO:0120115 (Lsm2-8 complex) for sets 5 and 6, with further rules for the individual U1, U2, U5, U6 and U7 snRNPs. Those sets emit nothing from this rule. Only sets 1 to 3, which are InterPro signatures spanning both Sm and Lsm paralogs, do work, and for them the umbrella term is the right level.

Action Rationale

The annotations are true, but the rule is nine-tenths duplicate. Sets 4 to 27 are matched on their signatures by rules for descendants of the consequent, and none of the 15 that lack a taxon here matches any non-eukaryotic entry, so the Eukaryota condition on the covering rule excludes nothing. Removing those sets changes no annotation. What remains, sets 1 to 3, selects the Sm and Lsm fold families themselves, whose members sit in different Sm-like rings, and GO:0120114 (Sm-like protein family complex) is the most specific term true of all of them.

Assessments

REDUNDANT

Twenty-four of 27 sets do no work. Each of sets 4 and 7 to 27 shares its signatures with a set of ARBA00033820 (GO:0030532 (small nuclear ribonucleoprotein complex), 23 sets), and ARBA00089766 (GO:0120115 (Lsm2-8 complex)) contains those of sets 5 and 6 (rest.uniprot.org/arba search over all 29 descendants of the consequent, 2026-11-12). Where the covering rule adds Eukaryota to a set that has no taxon here (15 sets), the UniProtKB matches outside Eukaryota number 0 in each. Sets 3 and 5 both select Lsm3 (8 and 6 Swiss-Prot entries). rules/arba/_interpro2go.txt has no InterPro entry mapped to the consequent.

STRONG

Curated experimental GO annotations on matched Swiss-Prot entries are listed per set in rule.condition_sets[].notes (QuickGO, November 2026), for example P36048 (SN114_YEAST) GO:0005682 U5 snRNP IDA PMID:11720284 (set 13) and SMD3_YEAST GO:0005682 IDA PMID:11720284 (set 14).

TOO_BROAD

Too broad for sets 4 to 27, whose members are annotated to GO:0030532 (small nuclear ribonucleoprotein complex) or its descendants (GO:0005685 (U1 snRNP), GO:0005686 (U2 snRNP), GO:0005682 (U5 snRNP), GO:0005688 (U6 snRNP), GO:0005683 (U7 snRNP)) or to GO:0120115 (Lsm2-8 complex). Appropriate for sets 1 and 2, where Sm and Lsm paralogs are selected together.

APPROPRIATE

Most sets are unrestricted or Eukaryota, which fits complexes found in all eukaryotes. A few are narrower than the biology (Rattus in set 15 for SmB and SmN, Mammalia in set 17, Craniata in set 18), following the species of the annotated proteins; those sets are among the ones to remove. Set 1 has no taxon and 223 of its 7,545 UniProtKB matches are outside Eukaryota; whether the term is apt for those was not checked.
