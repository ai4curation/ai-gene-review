Review Summary

Accuracy: the first pass examined no condition set. Fourteen of the 1340 served sets were drawn at random (Python random.sample, seed 4, over set numbers) and checked against curated annotations of their Swiss-Prot members; in every one a reviewed member carries a curated descendant of GO:0016740 (transferase activity), so the annotation is true for the sample and no false positive was found. The other 1326 sets were not checked. Meaning and parsimony: the term is the root of all transferase activities and says nothing about a protein whose reaction is known. The fourteen sets resolve into fourteen unrelated families (phage polymerases, plant UDP-glycosyltransferases, arginine kinase, malate synthase, sulfotransferases, histone acetyltransferases, protein kinases, aminotransferases, prenyl diphosphate synthases and others), each with its own curated activity term. rule_redundancy.tsv finds 1013 of 1340 sets already matched by a rule asserting a more specific term, so three quarters of the rule emits nothing.

Action Rationale

A random sample gives the same answer in fourteen of fourteen sets: the unit has a specific curated activity and the umbrella term adds nothing to it. Most of the rule is already duplicated by rules for more specific terms. The remainder should be split toward the activity of each family, not kept under the root term.

OVERLY_COMPLEX

rule_redundancy.tsv: 1013 of 1340 sets are matched by a rule asserting a more specific term (most often by rules for phosphotransferase, kinase, acyltransferase and glycosyltransferase terms). Of the 14 sampled sets, 10 are covered. QuickGO lists 118745 annotations attributed to the rule, which come from the uncovered sets. Each signature is used in one set; no single InterPro2GO mapping could replace a rule over unrelated families.

TOO_BROAD

GO:0016740 (transferase activity) has 2172 descendants. Every sampled unit has a curated descendant several levels more specific, e.g. GO:0004474 (malate synthase activity), GO:0046316 (gluconokinase activity), GO:0050694 (galactose 3-O-sulfotransferase activity).

APPROPRIATE

227 of 1340 sets carry a taxon condition. In the sample the restrictions fit the family (Uroviricota for phage polymerases, Ecdysozoa for arginine kinase, Rhabditida for a nematode GST FunFam, Gentianales for a plant UGT family); Gentianales and Ascomycota (MYST acetyltransferases) are narrower than the families, which is narrow coverage, not wrong annotation.
