Description

Assigns GO:0005681 (spliceosomal complex) through 19 alternative condition sets, each one or two InterPro entries or CATH FunFams with a taxon. The sets cover spliceosomal cyclophilins, DEAH-box helicases, Sm proteins, snRNP proteins and other splicing factors.

Each set selects proteins curated as spliceosome components, many with experimental evidence. Every set carries a taxon condition. The counts are from Swiss-Prot, which is also what ARBA learns its rules from, so they show the condition sets are coherent rather than giving an independent error rate for unreviewed entries.
