Description

Assigns GO:0005681 (spliceosomal complex) through 19 alternative condition sets, each one or two InterPro entries or CATH FunFams with a taxon. The sets cover spliceosomal cyclophilins, DEAH-box helicases, Sm proteins, snRNP proteins and other splicing factors.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all 19 live condition sets were examined. They match 86 reviewed entries; 85 carry GO:0005681 (spliceosomal complex) or a descendant, 70 by manual evidence. One set is wider than its evidence: set 2 requires only two generic DEAH-box helicase signatures in Taphrinomycotina, and three of its eight reviewed Schizosaccharomyces pombe matches carry the term only from this rule while PomBase places them in the nucleolus or cytosol. Meaning and parsimony: eighteen sets select subunits and stably associated factors of one machine (Sm core proteins, U1A and U2B'', spliceosomal DEAH-box helicases, PRP17, SNRNP40, cyclophilins, the exon junction factor MAGOH, branchpoint-binding protein and others), and the general complex term is the one curated on most of them, usually beside several stage-specific descendants, so it is the right level for a rule that spans stages. Set 2 is the exception. Three sets are covered by rules asserting more specific spliceosome terms, and InterPro2GO already maps an Sm F entry to the term.

Action Rationale

The rule is a coherent statement about one complex and should be kept, with set 2 removed or tightened: sets 3 and 4 already select the fungal spliceosomal DEAH-box helicases by FunFam, and set 2 adds the ribosome-biogenesis helicases prh1 (Q03319) and Q9HDY4 and the cytosolic ucp12 (O94536). The primate-level taxon limits on several sets are narrow coverage and not an error.

Assessments

ACCEPTABLE

3 sets are covered by rules with more specific spliceosome terms. In rules/arba/_interpro2go.txt IPR034100 (Small nuclear ribonucleoprotein F) maps to GO:0005681, which overlaps set 17. Set 2 is made unnecessary by sets 3 and 4 for the proteins it matches correctly. Set 13 matches 5 unreviewed entries. No set matches nothing.

APPROPRIATE

GO:0005681 is curated directly on most reviewed matches. Members that carry descendants carry several, for different spliceosome types and stages, so the parent is the informative common term.

TOO_NARROW

Sets 1, 6, 9 and 10 are limited to Primates, Haplorrhini or Catarrhini and set 7 to Euarchontoglires, although cyclophilin PPIL1, hnRNP R/Q, SmB, MAGOH and U1A/U2B'' are spliceosome components throughout animals or eukaryotes. The limits track human-centred annotation; they reduce coverage and cause no wrong annotation.
