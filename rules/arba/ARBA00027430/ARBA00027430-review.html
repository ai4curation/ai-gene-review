Description

ARBA rule assigning GO:0006066 alcohol metabolic process through 83 alternative condition sets built from InterPro, CATH FunFam, PANTHER and taxon conditions.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: ten of the 83 served condition sets were checked against the curated annotations of their Swiss-Prot members (sets 2, 4, 5, 16, 18, 19, 21, 24, 43 and 56). In each a reviewed member carries a curated annotation under GO:0006066 (alcohol metabolic process), so no false positive was found; the remaining sets were read by signature label only. Meaning and parsimony: the term is a chemical grouping. Under it the rule gathers inositol phosphate kinases, the retinoid isomerase RPE65, ceramidases, thiamine and sterol biosynthesis enzymes, yeast pyruvate decarboxylases, plant aconitases, leptin and dysferlin. Eight of the ten units carry a specific curated descendant that says what they do; for leptin and dysferlin the term is reached only through mutant-phenotype annotations and their informative terms lie outside it. rule_redundancy.tsv finds 40 of 83 sets already matched by a rule asserting a more specific term, and QuickGO lists no ARBA annotation to the term at all.

Action Rationale

An umbrella term over families that each have a better one should be split toward those terms. The units are real, their specific terms are curated with experimental evidence, and about half of the sets are already duplicated by rules for more specific terms. The verdict rests on a sample of ten sets chosen for recognisable family names across the rule (three already recorded, seven added); confidence reflects that.
