Description

ARBA rule assigning GO:1990840 response to lectin through two condition sets: two PAK-family kinase FunFams in Primates, and two p300/CBP FunFams in Catarrhini.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: both condition sets were examined. Set 1 selects PAK kinases in Primates and set 2 selects p300/CBP in Catarrhini; the five human proteins they match carry GO:0002223 (stimulatory C-type lectin receptor signaling pathway), a descendant of GO:1990840 (response to lectin), as a Reactome traceable author statement (R-HSA-5621481), so the rule repeats a curated annotation one level up. Meaning and parsimony: the two sets select unrelated families, a cytoskeletal signalling kinase and a transcriptional coactivator, joined only by membership of one Reactome pathway; neither has an experimental annotation to any lectin-response term, and the primate restriction reflects where the Reactome annotations sit, not where the proteins act. Both sets are also the exact condition sets of ARBA00087826, which asserts the descendant GO:0002223, and of ARBA00089180; QuickGO shows no annotation from ARBA00089176.

Action Rationale

Every condition set is covered by a served rule asserting a more specific term from the same conditions, so this rule emits nothing. Independently of that, response to lectin says little about PAK kinases or p300/CBP: the term reaches them through a pathway-membership mapping, not through anything specific to lectins.

Biological Meaning

HETEROGENEOUS

Two unrelated units. PAK kinases are experimentally annotated to cytoskeletal processes such as GO:0030036 (actin cytoskeleton organization); p300/CBP to transcription, e.g. GO:0045944 (positive regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II). The consequent fits both only through the Reactome mapping of the CLEC7A/DC-SIGN pathway onto generic downstream signalling components.

Assessments

REDUNDANT

Both sets are identical to the two sets of ARBA00087826 and of ARBA00089180, which assert the more specific terms named in the summary and in the redundancy table. QuickGO withFrom=ARBA:ARBA00089176 returned 0 annotations and withFrom=ARBA:ARBA00087826 returned 323 on 2026-11-12. Within set 2 the two FunFams co-occur in the same five Swiss-Prot proteins.

MODERATE

Curated database support only: Reactome pathway R-HSA-5621481 is the source of the TAS annotations of human PAK1, PAK2, PAK3, EP300 and CREBBP to GO:0002223. No experimental GO annotation to GO:1990840 or GO:0002223 exists for these proteins in QuickGO.

NONE

The two condition sets share no protein (recorded Swiss-Prot intersection counts of 0 between the kinase and p300 FunFams). Within set 2 the two FunFams co-occur in the same five proteins.

TOO_BROAD

The curated annotations are to GO:0002223 (stimulatory C-type lectin receptor signaling pathway), a descendant of the rule's term, and a rule for that descendant already exists.

TOO_NARROW

Primates (set 1) and Catarrhini (set 2) are where the supporting human Reactome annotations and their close orthologues are, not a boundary of PAK or p300/CBP biology; both families are conserved across animals. This is narrow coverage, not a wrong annotation, and widening it would spread a pathway-mapping term further.
