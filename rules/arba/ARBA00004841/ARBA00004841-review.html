Description

Assigns the pathway comment "Glycan metabolism; heparin biosynthesis" to chordate proteins that carry both the sulfotransferase domain (IPR000863) and the heparan sulphate N-deacetylase domain (IPR021930), or CATH FunFam 3.40.50.300:FF:000176. Both sets identify the bifunctional heparan sulfate N-deacetylase/N-sulfotransferases (NDST1-4).

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: both live condition sets were checked against Swiss-Prot on 2026-11-09. Each matches 12 reviewed entries, all NDST family members (human P52848 NDST1, P52849 NDST2, O95803 NDST3, Q9H3R1 NDST4), and all 12 carry the pathway comment. TrEMBL matches were counted but not inspected. Meaning and parsimony: the two sets select one unit, the bifunctional heparan sulfate N-deacetylase/N-sulfotransferases, and the pathway comment is the one Swiss-Prot gives that family, so the rule is coherent. The reviewed matches of the two sets are the same 12 entries; the sets differ only in the signature used and in their UniProtKB reach (4029 and 3386 entries).

Action Rationale

The comment is correct for every reviewed match and both sets describe the same enzyme family. Two alternative signatures for one family is modest redundancy, not a defect.
