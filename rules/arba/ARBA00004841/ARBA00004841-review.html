Description

Assigns the pathway comment "Glycan metabolism; heparin biosynthesis" to chordate proteins that carry both the sulfotransferase domain (IPR000863) and the heparan sulphate N-deacetylase domain (IPR021930), or CATH FunFam 3.40.50.300:FF:000176. Both sets identify the bifunctional heparan sulfate N-deacetylase/N-sulfotransferases (NDST1-4).
