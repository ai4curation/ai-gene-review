Description

Assigns the catalytic activity alpha-D-glucose 6-phosphate = beta-D-glucose 6-phosphate (RHEA:16249, EC 5.1.3.15) to proteins matching three InterPro entries together: IPR025532 Glucose-6-phosphate 1-epimerase, IPR008183 Aldose 1-/Glucose-6-phosphate 1-epimerase and IPR011013 Galactose mutarotase-like domain superfamily.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: the single condition set was examined. The rule annotates 9,242 TrEMBL entries. All five Swiss-Prot members carry EC 5.1.3.15, one from a published assay (Q03161, PubMed:16857670) and four by similarity to it. Meaning and parsimony: the set selects one family, IPR025532 (Glucose-6-phosphate 1-epimerase), and the asserted reaction (RHEA:16249) is the activity the family is named for, so the rule is a coherent statement at the right level. It is not parsimonious: IPR025532 is nested in the two other conditions, IPR008183 (Aldose 1-/Glucose-6-phosphate 1-epimerase) and IPR011013 (Galactose mutarotase-like domain superfamily), which together exclude one of 9,248 entries. The rule is equivalent to the single condition IPR025532.

Action Rationale

Nothing is wrong with what the rule asserts. The one defect is that two of its three conditions do no work, so the recommended change is to reduce the set to IPR025532 (Glucose-6-phosphate 1-epimerase). The change alters the output by one protein, which is why confidence in asking for it is modest.

REDUNDANT

MODERATE

APPROPRIATE
