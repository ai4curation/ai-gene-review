Description

Assigns GO:0045454 (cell redox homeostasis) through 14 alternative condition sets, each one to three CATH FunFams with a taxon. The sets cover nitric oxide synthases in Primates, fungal and plant thioredoxins, peroxiredoxins, glutathione reductase, KRIT1, selenide water dikinase 1, the yeast mitochondrial porin and fungal SCO1-like proteins.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all 14 live condition sets were examined. Eleven match reviewed entries, 32 in total, and every one carries GO:0045454 (cell redox homeostasis), 30 by manual evidence. The nitric oxide synthase set rests on a Reactome TAS annotation only. Three sets match no UniProtKB entry. Meaning and parsimony: the rule mixes two kinds of unit. Thioredoxins, peroxiredoxins and glutathione reductase (sets 2, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14) are the thiol redox system that the term describes, and the consequent is their most informative process term. KRIT1, selenide water dikinase 1, the yeast mitochondrial porin and SCO proteins (sets 4, 6, 7, 12) carry the term from a mutant phenotype while their defining curated processes lie elsewhere. The nitric oxide synthases (set 1) have it only from a Reactome pathway mapping, and their curated process is GO:0006809 (nitric oxide biosynthetic process). Sets 3, 5 and 8 repeat the conditions of sets 4 and 9 for Archaea or Bacteria and match nothing.

Action Rationale

The consequent has no descendants and no set is covered by a more specific rule, so the rule cannot be split toward better terms; the remedy is to remove the sets that do no work and the one that rests on a pathway mapping. The phenotype-supported sets are true annotations of pleiotropic proteins and are kept, with the reservation that they make the rule a list of unrelated proteins and not a statement about a redox system.

Assessments

REDUNDANT

3 of 14 sets (3, 5, 8) match no UniProtKB entry and do no work; set 10 matches 45 unreviewed and 0 reviewed entries. In rules/arba/_interpro2go.txt IPR046952 (Glutathione reductase/thioredoxin reductase-like) and IPR004502 (Thioredoxins/glutaredoxin) already map to GO:0045454, so part of the core unit is also reachable without the rule.

APPROPRIATE

GO:0045454 has no is_a or part_of descendants (rule_redundancy.tsv, QuickGO), so no more specific term is available for the thiol redox unit. For set 1 the term is a pathway-mapping artefact.

UNNECESSARY

The Archaea and Bacteria copies of the KRIT1 and glutathione reductase FunFam sets (3, 5, 8, 10) restrict to taxa where the FunFams do not occur or have no curated members. Primates on set 1 follows where the Reactome annotations are (human), not where nitric oxide synthases occur. The fungal and plant restrictions on sets 2, 7, 11, 12, 13 and 14 follow the organisms in which the phenotypes were shown, which is narrow coverage and not an error.
