Review Summary

Accuracy: ten of the 88 served condition sets were checked against curated annotations of their Swiss-Prot members (sets 4, 5, 6, 11, 12, 24, 25, 32, 53 and 85). In each a reviewed member carries a curated descendant of GO:0065003 (protein-containing complex assembly), so no false positive was found; the other 78 sets were not checked. Meaning and parsimony: the term covers three different kinds of thing. Dedicated assembly factors (Rubisco accumulation factor 1, SdhE, SCO1/SCO2, NDUFAF5) and structural subunits (gamma-tubulin complex components, septins, dynein regulatory complex protein 1) each carry a named assembly term for their complex. Metabolic enzymes such as serine hydroxymethyltransferase, threonine dehydrogenase and fumarase are in the rule only because curators recorded that they form tetramers. Every unit examined has a more specific curated term, and rule_redundancy.tsv finds 38 of 88 sets already matched by a rule asserting one.

Action Rationale

The consequent says only that a protein takes part in forming some complex. Each unit examined has a term that names the complex or the oligomeric state, most with experimental evidence, so the rule should be split toward those terms. The verdict rests on a sample of ten sets (three recorded by the first pass, seven added for families the signature label names); confidence reflects that.
