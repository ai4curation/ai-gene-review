Review Summary

GO:0046467 is obsolete in GO, and UniProt returned 404 for this rule on 2026-11-09. The 51 condition sets recorded are those of the last version reviewed. Nine of them (sets 2, 5, 12, 15, 19, 26, 31, 37 and 45) were checked against Swiss-Prot members and their GO annotations. In each case a matched protein carries a curated annotation to a lipid biosynthetic process: the ceramidases, sphingomyelinase and glucosylceramidase are annotated to biosynthesis of their products ceramide and sphingosine; protein kinase D and the chloroplast phosphoglycerate kinases are annotated to sphingolipid and galactolipid biosynthesis; and the Bacteroidota set selects serine palmitoyltransferases. So these sets reflect existing annotations rather than fold-level errors. The other 42 sets were not checked.

Action Rationale

The rule has been withdrawn by UniProt and its only consequent is an obsolete GO term.
