Review Summary

The rule has one condition set and it was examined. Its Swiss-Prot matches are S. cerevisiae RPL12A (P0CX53) and RPL12B (P0CX54) and C. albicans RPL12 (Q5AJF7). SGD annotates both S. cerevisiae proteins to ribophagy by IGI from PMID:39934334, "Rpl12 is a conserved ribophagy receptor", with the qualifier acts_upstream_of_or_within. The C. albicans protein has no curated ribophagy annotation, and the 159 unreviewed matches across Saccharomycotina rest on transfer from the two S. cerevisiae paralogs.

Action Rationale

The only condition set identifies uL11 in budding yeasts, and the two reviewed S. cerevisiae members carry an experimental annotation to the exact term. Confidence is moderate because the support is a single paper and one species, and the curated qualifier is acts_upstream_of_or_within rather than involved_in.
