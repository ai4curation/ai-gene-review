Review Summary

Accuracy: the rule has one condition set, the two 60S ribosomal protein L12 (uL11) CATH FunFams in Saccharomycotina. Its Swiss-Prot matches are S. cerevisiae RPL12A (P0CX53) and RPL12B (P0CX54) and C. albicans RPL12 (Q5AJF7). SGD annotates both S. cerevisiae proteins to GO:0034517 (ribophagy) by IGI from PMID:39934334, with the qualifier acts_upstream_of_or_within; the 159 unreviewed matches rest on transfer from those two paralogs. Meaning and parsimony: the set selects one unit, ribosomal protein uL11 of budding yeasts, and the consequent is the specific, experimentally shown role of that protein as a ribophagy receptor. GO:0034517 has no descendants and no other ARBA rule covers the set; QuickGO holds 162 annotations from this rule. The two FunFams are the two domains of one protein and the set carries no idle condition.
