Review Summary

Accuracy: 40 of the 773 live condition sets were examined in this pass; the first pass had examined none. In 36 of them the Swiss-Prot matches carry an experimental annotation to a descendant of GO:0006139 (nucleobase-containing compound metabolic process), and in 3 more (sets 221, 280 and 378) the only manual support is a NAS, TAS or IC annotation. One set has no support: set 278 selects yeast glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenases (P00359, P00358, P00360), whose curated process is GO:0006096 (glycolysis), which is not under the consequent in the current ontology. Meaning and parsimony: the consequent has 1,374 descendants and says only that a protein handles nucleic acids or nucleotides. The sampled sets resolve into RNA processing enzymes, aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases, DNA repair and replication factors, transcription factors, RNA decay enzymes and nucleotide metabolism enzymes, each with its own term, plus enzymes that reach the term only because acetyl-CoA counts as a nucleotide derivative. rule_redundancy.tsv counts 711 of 799 sets as already matched by a rule for a more specific term, and QuickGO holds no annotation attributed to this rule at all.

Action Rationale

The rule emits nothing: QuickGO returns 0 annotations for ARBA00026572 and no ARBA annotation to the term from any rule, so removing it loses no annotation. What it would say is uninformative for nearly every set, since the families it selects have specific terms that other ARBA rules already assert for most of them, and it is unsupported for at least one sampled set. The sets that no specific rule covers yet are better added to the rules for their own terms than kept under this one.
