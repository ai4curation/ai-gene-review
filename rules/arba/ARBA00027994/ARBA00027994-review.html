Review Summary

The live rule has 167 condition sets. Four (sets 1, 7, 13 and 19) were read for the identity of their signatures; each is a conjunction or a family-specific entry naming histone methyltransferases or bromodomain proteins. In GO, histone modifying activity (GO:0140993), histone deacetylase activity (GO:0004407) and ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler activity (GO:0140658) all sit under GO:0006338 through part_of, so a rule that covers histone modifiers and ATP-dependent remodelers together follows the ontology. A reported false positive on human UBR7 (Q8N806) could not be reproduced: no live condition set matches its signatures and it carries no chromatin annotation in QuickGO. 163 sets were not examined, so no verdict is given.

Four of 167 condition sets were read and none was checked against annotated proteins. No current false positive is known.
