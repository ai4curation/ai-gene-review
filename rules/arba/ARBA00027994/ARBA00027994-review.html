Description

ARBA rule assigning GO:0006338 chromatin remodeling through 167 alternative condition sets built from InterPro, CATH FunFam, PANTHER and taxon conditions.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: nine of the 167 served condition sets were checked against curated annotations of their Swiss-Prot members (sets 1, 7, 13, 19, 46, 63, 108, 130 and 140). In each a reviewed member carries a curated term that is GO:0006338 (chromatin remodeling) or an is_a/part_of descendant, so no false positive was found; the earlier report of a false positive on human UBR7 was not reproducible. The other 158 sets were not checked. Meaning and parsimony: GO places histone modifying activities, ATP-dependent remodeler activity and heterochromatin formation under the term, so the rule is true across its sets, but it flattens units that each carry a more informative term: histone methyltransferases, class I histone deacetylases, Polycomb EZH1/EZH2 and DNA methyltransferase 1 (heterochromatin formation), CHD1 (nucleosome organization), transcription-coupled remodeling factors, a histone variant, and DNA topoisomerase I, whose defining process is outside the term. rule_redundancy.tsv finds 47 of 167 sets already matched by a rule asserting a more specific term.

Action Rationale

The consequent is an umbrella over the whole of chromatin biology and each unit examined has a better curated term, several of which already have their own ARBA rules. Splitting toward those terms is the useful change. The verdict rests on a sample of nine sets (four recorded by the first pass, five added for families the signature label names); confidence reflects that.

OVERLY_COMPLEX

Those sets emit nothing from this rule. QuickGO lists 35483 annotations attributed to the rule, so the uncovered sets are active. Each signature is used in one set and no set is contained in another. No single InterPro2GO mapping could replace the construction, because the sets are different families.

TOO_BROAD

GO:0006338 (chromatin remodeling) has 235 is_a/part_of descendants, and the units examined carry curated descendants such as GO:0031507 (heterochromatin formation), GO:0034728 (nucleosome organization) and GO:0042054 (histone methyltransferase activity).

APPROPRIATE

Chromatin remodeling is pan-eukaryotic and most sets are unrestricted or limited to large clades. Some restrictions follow where the supporting annotation sits (Taphrinomycotina for the bromodomain set 7, whose S. pombe members are PomBase-annotated; Mus, Muridae, Suina on sets not examined here); that is narrow coverage, not wrong annotation.
