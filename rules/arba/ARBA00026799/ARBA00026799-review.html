Review Summary

All five condition sets of the rule as served on 2026-11-10 were turned into UniProtKB queries. In every set the reviewed matches are NAD kinases by curated EC number (698 of 704 across the sets), and sets 1 and 5 include members with experimental GO:0003951 annotations. The one soft spot is in set 1, which also matches the mitochondrial NADH kinase POS5 and its S. pombe ortholog; these are curated as NADH kinases (EC 2.7.1.86), and NAD+ kinase activity is not annotated for POS5.

Action Rationale

Each condition set selects the NAD kinase family, and the consequent is the family's defining activity. The NADH kinase members of set 1 are few (3 of 541 reviewed matches), belong to the same family, and are not shown to lack NAD+ kinase activity, so they do not justify a change. The counts are from reviewed entries, which the rule was derived from; unreviewed matches were counted, not inspected.
