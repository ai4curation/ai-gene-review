Review Summary

Accuracy: all 82 live condition sets have now been examined (UniProtKB and QuickGO queries, November 2026). Every set matches Swiss-Prot entries, and in every set at least one matched entry carries GO:0004721 (phosphoprotein phosphatase activity) or a descendant on manual evidence, in all 82 from an experiment. No clear false positive was found, but three sets select proteins whose main activity lies outside the term: set 65 (inorganic pyrophosphatases PPA1 and PPA2), set 66 (glycogen-targeting regulatory subunits of protein phosphatase 1, where rat PPP1R3E is annotated to the term with the qualifier contributes_to) and set 67 (the lipid phosphatases MTMR3 and MTMR4). Meaning and parsimony: the rule is an umbrella. Its sets resolve into protein tyrosine phosphatases (29 sets), protein serine/threonine phosphatases (26), dual-specificity phosphatases (12), RNA polymerase II CTD phosphatases (8), calcineurin (2) and the PP1 beta catalytic subunit (2), and each of these units carries a curated child term that says which kind of phosphatase it is. Rules for the two largest child terms already exist and match 32 of the 82 sets exactly, so those sets emit nothing here.

Action Rationale

The parent term is true of 79 sets but tells a user nothing that the child terms do not, and the child-term rules that exist (ARBA00027656 for GO:0004725 (protein tyrosine phosphatase activity), ARBA00027653 for GO:0004722 (protein serine/threonine phosphatase activity)) already duplicate 32 of the sets. The remedy is to send every set to the rule for its child term and retire the parent rule, after deciding what to do with the three sets whose proteins are not protein phosphatases in the first place.

TOO_BROAD

Curated annotation on the matched proteins is to children of the consequent: GO:0004725 (protein tyrosine phosphatase activity) tops 34 sets and GO:0004722 (protein serine/threonine phosphatase activity) 42, with GO:0008138 (protein tyrosine/serine/threonine phosphatase activity), GO:0033192 (calmodulin-dependent protein phosphatase activity), GO:0008420 (RNA polymerase II CTD heptapeptide repeat phosphatase activity) and GO:0017018 (myosin phosphatase activity) more specific still for their units.

TOO_NARROW

Forty-one of 82 sets carry a taxon. Several are single genera or narrower than the family warrants (Rattus in sets 8 and 45, Mus in set 48, Homo in set 9, Hominidae in set 36, Glires in set 61, Myomorpha in set 65), which follows the species of the training annotations, not where the phosphatase families occur. Others are sensible lineage limits for lineage-specific families (Viridiplantae, Bacteria, Saccharomycotina). Narrow coverage, not wrong annotation.
