Description

Assigns the pathway comment "Nitrogen metabolism; urea cycle; L-ornithine and urea from L-arginine: step 1/1" (the arginase step) through nine alternative condition sets: InterPro arginase signatures in Chordata, a PANTHER subfamily in Bacteria, and seven CATH FunFams of superfamily 3.40.800.10, each with its own taxon.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all nine live condition sets were checked against Swiss-Prot on 2026-11-09. Every reviewed match of every set carries the pathway comment (16, 13, 9, 8, 6, 6, 6, 4 and 3 entries for sets 1 to 9). TrEMBL matches were counted but not inspected. Meaning and parsimony: sets 1 to 8 select one unit, the arginases, for which the arginase step of the urea cycle is the right comment. Set 9 selects a different unit. Its three mammalian Swiss-Prot matches are AGMAT orthologues (human Q9BSE5), which Swiss-Prot names guanidino acid hydrolase; the record says the enzyme prefers taurocyamine and guanidino propanoic and butanoic acids and "Metabolizes L-arginine with low efficiency", and its pathway comment is a curator inference (ECO:0000305). The comment is therefore true of set 9 only through a weak side activity, and a rule that propagates it to 174 UniProtKB entries presents a guanidino acid hydrolase as a urea-cycle arginase. Among the arginase sets, sets 3 and 8 have no reviewed match outside set 2 and sets 4 and 7 none outside set 1, which is mild redundancy.

Action Rationale

The comment is accurate for sets 1 to 8 and those sets describe one enzyme family. Set 9 should be dropped: it extends an arginase pathway step to the AGMAT family on the strength of a low-efficiency activity that the curated record itself separates from the enzyme's function. Confidence is moderate because Swiss-Prot does carry the comment on the three AGMAT entries, so the question is one of meaning, not of a wrong annotation.

Biological Meaning

HETEROGENEOUS

Two units. Arginases (sets 1 to 8) fit the consequent. The AGMAT guanidino acid hydrolases (set 9) fit it only by a pathway comment inferred from a low-efficiency activity. The consequent is a pathway comment, so rule_redundancy.tsv has no row for this rule.

Assessments

ACCEPTABLE

Nine sets, 44 distinct reviewed matches. Sets 3 and 8 have no reviewed match outside set 2, and sets 4 and 7 none outside set 1; these were compared on reviewed entries only, so they may still reach unreviewed entries the larger sets miss. A single condition on IPR014033 (Arginase) would not replace the rule: six reviewed plant arginases matched by set 5 lack it, and nine of its 44 reviewed members are outside sets 1 to 8.

APPROPRIATE

Each set carries one taxon condition that goes with its signature (Chordata, Bacteria, Eukaryota, Viridiplantae, Fungi, Metazoa, Pseudomonadati, Mammalia). The comment names a reaction step and Swiss-Prot gives it to arginases of organisms without a complete urea cycle, so the taxa are not a statement about where the cycle exists.
