Review Summary

All six live condition sets were examined. They match 40 reviewed entries in total and every one carries GO:0051998 or a descendant, 39 by manual evidence. GO:0051998 is the common parent of the specific activities of the three families, so the annotation is true for every family the rule covers. More specific terms exist for each family and are already supplied by InterPro2GO for two of them.

Action Rationale

The annotation is correct for all three families. It is less specific than the per-family child terms, but a general true term is not an error, and one rule spanning three families can only use their shared parent. The counts are from Swiss-Prot, which is also what ARBA learns its rules from, so they show the condition sets are coherent rather than giving an independent error rate for unreviewed entries.
