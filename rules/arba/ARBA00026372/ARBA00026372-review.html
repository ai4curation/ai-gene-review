Description

Assigns GO:0051998 (protein carboxyl O-methyltransferase activity) through six alternative condition sets covering three enzyme families: isoprenylcysteine carboxyl methyltransferases (sets 1, 3, 4), protein-L-isoaspartate O-methyltransferases (sets 2, 5) and leucine carboxyl methyltransferase 1 (set 6).

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all six live condition sets were examined. They match 40 reviewed entries and every one carries GO:0051998 (protein carboxyl O-methyltransferase activity) or a descendant, 39 by manual evidence, so the annotation is true for every family the rule covers. Meaning and parsimony: the six sets resolve into three unrelated enzyme families, each of which has its own curated child term: isoprenylcysteine carboxyl methyltransferase (sets 1, 3, 4), GO:0004671 (protein C-terminal S-isoprenylcysteine carboxyl O-methyltransferase activity); protein-L-isoaspartate O-methyltransferase (sets 2, 5), GO:0004719 (protein-L-isoaspartate (D-aspartate) O-methyltransferase activity); and leucine carboxyl methyltransferase 1 (set 6), GO:0018423 (protein C-terminal leucine carboxyl O-methyltransferase activity). The consequent is the shared parent and says less than any of them. Four of the sets are already matched by rules asserting a more specific term, so they emit nothing, and InterPro2GO maps both InterPro entries of set 1 to GO:0004671.

Action Rationale

The rule is true but uninformative: an umbrella term over three families that each carry a more specific curated term with experimental evidence (O60725, P22061, Q9UIC8). The families share no substrate site (a C-terminal prenylcysteine, an internal isoaspartyl residue, a C-terminal leucine of PP2A), so nothing is gained by annotating them at the parent. Splitting by family gives the term that curators use, and most of the rule is already redundant with more specific rules and with InterPro2GO.

Assessments

REDUNDANT

5 sets are matched by a rule with a more specific term (rule_redundancy.tsv), so they emit nothing. rules/arba/_interpro2go.txt maps IPR007269 (Isoprenylcysteine carboxyl methyltransferase) and IPR025770 (Protein-S-isoprenylcysteine O-methyltransferase) to GO:0004671, which makes set 1 unnecessary. All 6 reviewed matches of set 3 and both reviewed matches of set 4 also match set 1 (UniProtKB query 2026-11-12).

TOO_BROAD

GO:0004671, GO:0004719 and GO:0018423 are is_a descendants of GO:0051998 (QuickGO) and are the terms curated on the characterised members of the three families. The consequent is the most specific term common to the three, which is a property of how the rule was assembled, not of the proteins.

APPROPRIATE

Sets 2, 4 and 5 carry Eukaryota, Fungi and Metazoa. These bound FunFams and follow where the eukaryotic enzymes occur; bacterial protein-L-isoaspartate O-methyltransferases are outside the rule, which is narrow coverage, not a wrong annotation.

The literature contradicts the rule.
general is not wrong · 2026-11-10		 The papers cited describe the two enzymes as carboxyl methyltransferases acting on proteins, which agrees with the term. They show that the families differ, not that the annotation is false.
