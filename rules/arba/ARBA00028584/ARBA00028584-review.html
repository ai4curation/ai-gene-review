Description

Assigns GO:0005778 (peroxisomal membrane) through 16 alternative condition sets covering peroxins (PEX2/10, PEX4, PEX11, PEX12, PEX13, PEX14 and the fungal DysF-domain peroxins), peroxisomal ABC transporters, PMP34 (SLC25A17), ATAD1, fatty acyl-CoA reductase, NBR1 and a Penicillium transporter FunFam, each with a taxon restriction.

Review Summary

All 16 live condition sets were examined. They match 83 reviewed entries in total; 82 carry GO:0005778 or a descendant, 77 by manual evidence. Fourteen sets are supported by experimental localisation of at least one reviewed match. Two are weaker: the NBR1 set rests on Reactome TAS annotations only, and the Penicillium transporter set includes sorT (B6HNK5), which has no peroxisomal annotation.

Action Rationale

The rule selects proteins that are curated to the peroxisomal membrane, almost all with experimental evidence. The two weaker sets are recorded in annotation_assessments; nothing checked shows either to be wrong. The counts are from Swiss-Prot, which is also what ARBA learns its rules from, so they show the condition sets are coherent rather than giving an independent error rate for unreviewed entries.

Assessments

STRONG

Peroxisomal membrane location is documented for the transporter and import-machinery families the rule covers.

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:22185573: Recombinant and purified SLC25A17 was reconstituted into liposomes
  • PMID:35908918: ABCD1-3 are localized to peroxisomal membranes and involved in the transport of various acyl-CoAs from the cytosol to the peroxisomal lumen.
  • PMID:36541703: PEX13, a component of the peroxisomal matrix import system, is required to prevent the degradation of otherwise healthy peroxisomes.
APPROPRIATE

GO:0005778 is the cellular component term carried, with experimental evidence, by reviewed members of the sets (accessions in annotation_assessments).

References (4)

  • PMID:22185573
    The human gene SLC25A17 encodes a peroxisomal transporter of coenzyme A, FAD and NAD+

    SLC25A17 is the only member of the mitochondrial carrier family localized to the peroxisomal membrane. It transports CoA, FAD, FMN, AMP, and to a lesser extent NAD+, PAP, and ADP into peroxisomes.

  • PMID:35908918
    Substrate Specificity and the Direction of Transport in the ABC Transporters ABCD1-3 and ABCD4

    ABCD1-3 are localized to peroxisomal membranes and transport various acyl-CoAs from the cytosol to the peroxisomal lumen. ABCD4 is lysosomal. Mutations in ABCD1, ABCD3, and ABCD4 cause genetic diseases.

  • PMID:36541703
    PEX13 prevents pexophagy by regulating ubiquitinated PEX5 and peroxisomal ROS

    PEX13 is a component of the peroxisomal matrix import system and is required to prevent degradation of healthy peroxisomes. Loss of PEX13 causes accumulation of ubiquitinated PEX5 on peroxisomes and induces pexophagy.

  • PMID:14617799
    Pex30p, Pex31p, and Pex32p form a family of peroxisomal integral membrane proteins regulating peroxisome size and number in Saccharomyces cerevisiae

    Reference for the IDA annotations of GO:0005778 on PEX30, PEX31 and PEX32.
