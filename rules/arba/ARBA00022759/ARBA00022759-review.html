Review Summary

11 of the 320 live condition sets were examined: the first six and five that contain a domain shared with non-nuclease proteins. In each of the eleven, every reviewed match carries the Endonuclease keyword (4008 of 4008, summed over sets). The five sets with a broadly shared domain each pair it with a signature for a known endonuclease: FEN1, the nidovirus NendoU domain, UV-damage endonuclease UvdE, plant MORC proteins and retroviral polymerase polyproteins. The remaining 309 sets were not examined.

Action Rationale

The rule was sampled, not examined set by set, so no verdict is given. The sampled sets are sound, which removes the grounds of the earlier verdict but does not establish that every set is right. The counts are from Swiss-Prot, which is also what ARBA learns its rules from, so they show the condition sets are coherent rather than giving an independent error rate for unreviewed entries.
