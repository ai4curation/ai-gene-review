Review Summary

ARBA00027389 is a severely problematic rule that incorrectly predicts cell wall biogenesis for a heterogeneous collection of 29 condition sets. While some genuine cell wall biosynthetic enzymes are included (cellulose synthases, glucan synthases, chitin deacetylases), the majority of condition sets target proteins with entirely different primary functions: polyketide synthases for secondary metabolite production, leucine-rich repeat receptor kinases for signal transduction, Rho GTPases for cytoskeletal regulation, and fatty acid ligases for lipid metabolism. This creates a high risk of false positive annotations that would mislead functional predictions and pathway analyses. The rule conflates indirect effects on cell wall composition with direct biosynthetic roles and should be deprecated in favor of more specific, mechanistically coherent rules.

Action Rationale

This rule should be removed due to severe biological incoherence. It incorrectly predicts cell wall biogenesis for numerous protein families that have no direct role in cell wall synthesis, including secondary metabolite polyketide synthases, plant receptor kinases, Rho GTPases, and various other functionally unrelated proteins. The rule conflates genuine cell wall biosynthetic enzymes with proteins that may affect cell wall composition indirectly or have entirely different primary functions.
