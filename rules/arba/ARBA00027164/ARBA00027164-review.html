Review Summary

Accuracy: all 52 live condition sets were examined by retrieving the Swiss-Prot entries that match the whole conjunction and looking up their curated annotations to GO:0006401 (RNA catabolic process) and its is_a/part_of descendants. In 51 sets at least one Swiss-Prot match has an experimental annotation; the exception is set 28 (pelota FunFams), whose two matches carry GO:0070481 (nuclear-transcribed mRNA catabolic process, non-stop decay) by IEP only. Helicases, nucleotidyltransferases and RNA-binding proteins in the rule take part in decay pathways, which is what a process term records. Meaning and parsimony: the 52 sets resolve into eleven units, and for nine of them curated annotation uses a more specific descendant of the consequent: decapping, poly(A) tail shortening, deadenylation-dependent decay, nonsense-mediated decay, polyadenylation-dependent ncRNA decay, polyuridylation-dependent mRNA decay, miRNA catabolism, nuclear RNA surveillance and mitochondrial RNA catabolism. Only the exosome and its helicase cofactors are curated at the level of the consequent itself. Rules asserting more specific terms already cover a large part of the rule: rule_redundancy.tsv counts 20 covered sets, and on the rule set downloaded 2026-11-12 it is 21 of 52, 18 of them by the mRNA catabolic process rule ARBA00027180. Those sets emit nothing here. A minority of sets select proteins that reach the term through one paper on an indirect role (HSP70, the 3',5'-bisphosphate nucleotidase MET22, the cap-binding protein CBC2), which a parent term absorbs without comment.

Action Rationale

RNA catabolic process is true of every unit and specific to none: an mRNA decapping enzyme, a miRNA-binding protein and a mitochondrial PNPase are annotated alike. Curators distinguish them, the GO branch has 64 descendants to do it with, and other ARBA rules already assert several of those descendants from the same signatures. The rule should be split so that each uncovered set asserts the term of its unit, leaving the exosome and the unclassified single-family sets under the parent. Confidence is moderate because units were assigned from the per-set receipts recorded in the first pass (the most specific experimental term per set), not from a fresh lookup of every member, and some families act in more than one decay pathway.

Several units are selected more than once (DCP2 by sets 1 and 6; MTR4 by sets 9, 42 and 44; SKI2 by sets 8 and 44). rules/arba/_interpro2go.txt maps nine InterPro entries to GO:0006401, including IPR011807 (Exosome complex component Rrp41) and IPR016438 (ATP-dependent RNA helicase SKI2-like), which overlap the units of sets 46 and 8 but not the rest.

TOO_BROAD

The descendants named in biological_units (GO:0000290, GO:0000289, GO:0000288, GO:0000184, GO:0043634, GO:1990074, GO:0010587, GO:0071027, GO:0000957) are the terms curated by experiment on members of 31 of the 52 sets, taken from the first pass's QuickGO lookups of GO:0006401 and its is_a/part_of descendants.

TOO_NARROW

Many restrictions follow the species of the supporting annotations: Mus for the ZFP36 family (set 7), Glires for TUT4/TUT7 (set 20), Primates for exosome PH-domain subunits and IRE1 (sets 2, 22), Haplorrhini for HSP70 (set 23), Hominidae for MAGOH (set 35). These families are conserved across eukaryotes or animals, so this is narrow coverage, not wrong annotation. Restrictions such as Bacteria for RelE (set 37), Fungi for the NOT module (set 3) and Viridiplantae for plant XRNs (set 11) follow the unit.
