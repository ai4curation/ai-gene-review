Description

Assigns GO:0005608 (laminin-121 trimer) through two condition sets, each a conjunction of three CATH FunFams with fungi excluded. Set 1 matches laminin subunit beta-2 and set 2 matches laminin subunit gamma-1.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: both live condition sets were examined. Set 1 matches three Swiss-Prot entries, all laminin subunit beta-2 (human P55268, rat P15800, mouse Q61292); human and mouse LAMB2 are experimentally annotated to GO:0005608 (laminin-121 trimer). Set 2 matches laminin subunit gamma-1 of human (P11047) and mouse (P02468), both annotated to the term by TAS. For these reviewed vertebrate chains the annotation is true. Meaning and parsimony: the sets select two chains of one complex, so the rule is a coherent statement, with two limits. The term names one trimer among many that each chain builds: curated annotation puts human gamma-1 in eleven laminin trimers and beta-2 in nine, and no set selects alpha-1, the chain that distinguishes laminin-121. And the only taxon condition, NOT Fungi, does no work for a metazoan protein family and lets set 1 reach 26 non-vertebrate entries, 21 of them nematode laminin beta chains that QuickGO shows annotated to laminin-121 by this rule. The alpha1, beta2, gamma1 chain nomenclature is defined for vertebrate laminins.

Action Rationale

The rule is kept, because each set selects a real subunit of the named complex and emits 1,060 annotations no other rule provides, but its taxon condition should follow the biology: restrict both sets to Vertebrata. That removes the nematode and arthropod matches of set 1 (set 2 already matches vertebrates only) and replaces a condition that excludes nothing relevant. Confidence is moderate: whether a nematode beta chain should be called a member of laminin-121 was judged from nomenclature, not from an experiment.

Biological Meaning

COHERENT

Two units that are subunits of one complex: laminin beta-2 (set 1) and laminin gamma-1 (set 2). GO:0005608 (laminin-121 trimer) is true of both and has no descendants. It is one of several equally specific sibling terms true of each chain, and the alpha-1 chain (P25391, curated to GO:0005608 by IPI PMID:10964500) is not selected by any set, so the rule is a partial statement about the complex, not a misleading one.

The consequent term has 0 descendant terms. 0 condition set(s) are also matched by a rule asserting a more specific term. 2 condition set(s) were examined for this judgement.

Assessments

ACCEPTABLE

Two sets, one per chain, each a conjunction of three FunFams. rule_redundancy.tsv: go_descendants 0, covered_sets 0; no other ARBA rule asserts a laminin trimer term from these sets, and QuickGO returns 1,060 annotations with withFrom ARBA:ARBA00087037 (2026-11-12). The condition NOT Fungi is idle: laminins are metazoan and the FunFam conjunctions have no fungal matches to exclude. Whether all three FunFams of each conjunction are needed was not tested. No InterPro2GO mapping to GO:0005608 exists in rules/arba/_interpro2go.txt.

APPROPRIATE

The reviewed matches of both sets are curated to GO:0005608 itself (P55268 IPI PMID:10964500, Q61292 IDA PMID:20566382, P11047 and P02468 TAS PMID:21421915). The term is as specific as GO offers; the caveat is selectivity among sibling trimers, not level.

TOO_BROAD

NOT Fungi is the only taxon condition. UniProtKB counts (2026-11-12): set 1 matches 878 entries, 852 in Vertebrata, 26 outside (21 Nematoda, 2 Arthropoda); set 2 matches 182, all in Vertebrata. QuickGO shows 21 nematode entries annotated to GO:0005608 with ARBA:ARBA00087037, for example A0A0B2UNP1 and A0A0D6M206. Laminin beta-2 and the laminin-121 (alpha1, beta2, gamma1) designation are vertebrate, so the scope should be Vertebrata.
