Description

ARBA rule assigning GO:0004791 thioredoxin-disulfide reductase (NADPH) activity through three condition sets: three thioredoxin reductase InterPro signatures together, a thioredoxin reductase FunFam in Eukaryota, and a second thioredoxin reductase FunFam in Glires.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all three condition sets were examined and the annotation is correct for what they match. Set 1 rests on IPR005982 (Thioredoxin reductase), which InterPro2GO maps to GO:0004791 (thioredoxin-disulfide reductase (NADPH) activity); the FunFams of sets 2 and 3 each hold 18 Swiss-Prot proteins, all annotated to the term. Meaning and parsimony: the sets select one enzyme, thioredoxin reductase, in its low molecular weight form (sets 1 and 2) and its mammalian selenoprotein form (set 3), and the consequent is the defining activity, so the statement is coherent and at the right level. Most of the rule repeats InterPro2GO: every one of the 19539 UniProtKB entries matched by set 1 and of the 77 matched by set 3 carries an InterPro entry that already maps to the term, and only 136 of the 2641 entries matched by set 2 do not.

Action Rationale

The rule says the right thing about the right family, but two of its three sets do no work that the InterPro2GO mappings of IPR005982, IPR006338 and IPR045870 do not already do. The specific change is to keep the part that adds annotations (set 2, 136 proteins) and drop the rest. Confidence is moderate because a duplicate true annotation does no harm, and leaving the rule alone is defensible.

APPROPRIATE

APPROPRIATE
