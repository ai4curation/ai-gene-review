Review Summary

All three live condition sets were examined. Set 1 rests on IPR005982 (Thioredoxin reductase), which InterPro2GO already maps to GO:0004791. The FunFams of sets 2 and 3 each hold 18 Swiss-Prot proteins, all annotated to GO:0004791. The annotation is correct for what the rule matches. Most of the rule duplicates InterPro2GO: sets 1 and 3 add nothing to the IPR005982 and IPR006338 mappings, and set 2 adds 136 UniProtKB proteins that carry neither signature.

Action Rationale

Every condition set is supported: by a curated InterPro2GO mapping for set 1, and by complete agreement with Swiss-Prot annotation for the FunFams of sets 2 and 3. Overlap with InterPro2GO makes the rule largely redundant, which is not a fault in its annotations.
