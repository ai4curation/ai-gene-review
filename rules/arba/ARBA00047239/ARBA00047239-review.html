Description

Assigns GO:0032206 positive regulation of telomere maintenance through eight alternative condition sets of CATH FunFams, most with a taxon condition: TRF2 (Mammalia), ATM (Eukaryota), tankyrase (Primates), MAPK1/MAPK3 (Haplorrhini), T-complex protein 1 subunit eta (Catarrhini), RTEL1, RuvB-like helicase (Mus) and SLX4 (Metazoa).

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all eight live condition sets were examined. In each set the Swiss-Prot matches carry GO:0032206 (positive regulation of telomere maintenance) or a descendant with experimental evidence: TERF2 (IDA), ATM (IMP), tankyrases (IDA), MAPK1 and MAPK3 (IMP), CCT subunits (IMP), RTEL1 (IMP), mouse RUVBL1 and RUVBL2 (IMP) and SLX4 (IDA). No wrongly annotated protein was found. Meaning and parsimony: the rule is a list of eight unrelated proteins or protein pairs that have each been shown to promote telomere maintenance, by different routes (a shelterin subunit, checkpoint and MAP kinases, a poly-ADP-ribosyltransferase, a chaperonin, helicases, a nuclease scaffold). For four of them (ATM, tankyrase, CCT, RuvB-like helicases) the curated term is a descendant of the consequent, and a separate rule already asserts that descendant with the same signatures, so those four sets emit nothing. For the other four (TERF2, MAPK1/MAPK3, RTEL1, SLX4) the consequent is the curated term itself.

Action Rationale

The annotations are right, but half of the rule does no work. Sets 2, 3 and 7 are matched exactly by rules for descendant terms, and set 5 by the same FunFam pair under a wider taxon. Removing them leaves four sets for which the consequent is the most informative curated term. The rule stays a mixed list and not a family, which is acceptable for a regulation term as long as each set rests on its own experiment.

Biological Meaning

TRUE BUT UNINFORMATIVE

Eight unrelated units share the consequent only because each has an experimental annotation under it; the rule is a lookup table of curated regulators, not a family or a complex. Four units carry a more specific descendant that another rule already asserts (rule_redundancy.tsv: 3 of 8 sets covered, by GO:0032212, GO:1904358 and GO:1904507). For the remaining four the consequent is the level curators chose, and RTEL1 and SLX4 have no single descendant shared by their human and mouse annotations.

Assessments

REDUNDANT

Four of eight sets do no work. Sets 2, 3 and 7 are identical to sets of ARBA00047255, ARBA00047236 (and ARBA00089794) and ARBA00090219, which assert descendants of the consequent; set 5 uses the same two FunFams as a set of ARBA00047236 with Catarrhini in place of the wider Haplorrhini (rest.uniprot.org/arba search, 2026-11-12). The rule still emits 1,232 annotations in QuickGO (withFrom=ARBA:ARBA00047239), from the uncovered sets. Each FunFam conjunction is narrow, two to six Swiss-Prot proteins, so the conditions inside a set are not excessive.

STRONG

Curated GO annotations (QuickGO, November 2026): Q15554 (TERF2) GO:0032206 IDA PMID:24012755; Q13315 (ATM) GO:1904358 IMP PMID:17694070 and PMID:26586427; O95271 (TNKS) GO:0032212 IDA PMID:12782650; P28482 (MAPK1) and P27361 (MAPK3) GO:0032206 IMP PMID:21531765; P17987 (TCP1) GO:0032212 IMP PMID:25467444; Q8IY92 (SLX4) GO:0032206 IDA PMID:24012755. GO:0032212, GO:1904358 and GO:1904507 are is_a children of GO:0032206.

APPROPRIATE

Appropriate for sets 1, 4, 6 and 8, where experimental annotation is to GO:0032206 (positive regulation of telomere maintenance) itself. Too broad for sets 2, 3, 5 and 7, whose members are annotated to GO:1904358 (positive regulation of telomere maintenance via telomere lengthening), GO:0032212 (positive regulation of telomere maintenance via telomerase) or GO:1904507 (positive regulation of telomere maintenance in response to DNA damage); those are the sets to remove.

TOO_NARROW

The taxa track the species of the experiments, not the biology: Mus for the RuvB-like helicases, Primates for tankyrase, Haplorrhini for MAPK1 and MAPK3, Catarrhini for CCT, although all of these proteins are conserved across mammals or wider. Only ATM (Eukaryota) and SLX4 (Metazoa) are broad, and RTEL1 has no taxon. Narrow coverage, not wrong annotation.
