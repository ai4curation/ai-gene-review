All 69 live condition sets were checked against Swiss-Prot. In all but one, every reviewed match carries GO:0015980 or a descendant. Set 29 (pyruvate:ferredoxin oxidoreductase FunFams) has 9 reviewed matches of which 4 carry the term; the other 5 are annotated to electron transport chain only. The counts are from Swiss-Prot, which is also what ARBA learns its rules from, so they show the condition sets are coherent rather than giving an independent error rate for unreviewed entries.
