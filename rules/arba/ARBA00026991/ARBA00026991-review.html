Description

Assigns GO:0015980 (energy derivation by oxidation of organic compounds) through 69 alternative condition sets built from InterPro entries, PANTHER families and CATH FunFams, most with a taxon. The sets cover enzymes of glycolysis, the tricarboxylic acid cycle, respiratory electron transport and glycogen metabolism.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all 69 live condition sets were checked against Swiss-Prot. In all but one, every reviewed match carries GO:0015980 (energy derivation by oxidation of organic compounds) or a descendant. Set 29 has 9 reviewed matches of which 4 carry the term. Set 1 is too wide as written: it requires only the thiamin diphosphate-binding fold in Thermoproteota, a condition met by 51 unreviewed acetolactate synthases, which are biosynthetic enzymes. Meaning and parsimony: the 69 sets resolve into about ten pathway-level units, and curated annotation gives each a specific descendant: glycolysis, the tricarboxylic acid cycle, pyruvate decarboxylation to acetyl-CoA, the respiratory electron transport chain, anaerobic respiration, glycogen biosynthesis, glycogen catabolism and pyruvate fermentation. No reviewed match examined is curated to the consequent itself. Calling a glycogen synthase, a nitrate reductase and an enolase "energy derivation by oxidation of organic compounds" is true by ontology and tells a user nothing the specific terms do not. 51 sets are already matched by rules asserting a more specific term and emit nothing.

Action Rationale

The term is the common ancestor of several pathways, not a property the sets share in any useful sense, and the pathway terms are available and curated on the members. The rule should be split by pathway. Most of it is already superseded by more specific rules (51 covered sets); what is left should be re-pointed, and set 1 narrowed as the first pass found.
