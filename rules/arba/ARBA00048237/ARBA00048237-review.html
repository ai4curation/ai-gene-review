Review Summary

All 8 condition sets served on 2026-11-17 were examined (counts re-run on 2026-11-19 against the same release) as conjunctions in UniProtKB 2026_04. Together they match 3,535 entries: 3,460 fungal, 75 non-fungal eukaryotes and no bacteria or archaea. Every one of the 28 reviewed matches is a fungal FAS alpha or beta subunit whose EC 2.3.1.86 is curated in Swiss-Prot, by experiment or by similarity to S. cerevisiae FAS1/FAS2 or A. parasiticus aflA/aflB, and none carries it from ARBA. An earlier version of the rule did reach bacteria: bacterial polyketide synthases carried EC 2.3.1.86 from this rule in the June and September 2026 releases (19 of 20 entries sampled from the 529 in each release; the twentieth carried it in both from a source other than this rule) and lost it in 2026_04, but QuickGO still serves 529 GO:0004321 EC2GO rows on them.

Action Rationale

Each of the 8 sets matches fungal FAS subunits, and the in-sample support is uniform: the reviewed matches in every set carry EC 2.3.1.86 as curated Swiss-Prot annotation, the same consequent the rule assigns. The rule's consequent repeats Swiss-Prot's own practice of giving the system reaction to both FAS subunits; whether that reaction should be an activity of a single chain is a question for the EC/GO mapping, not for this rule. The 75 non-fungal eukaryote matches (stramenopile FAS-alpha-like models, several fragments, and single proteins from protists, plants and one amphipod) are uncertain rather than shown wrong, about 2% of the rule's output. The bacterial false positives that motivated this review are no longer produced by the rule as served.

Biological Meaning

COHERENT

All 8 sets resolve into the two subunits of the fungal type I fatty acid synthase, plus a small fringe of uncertain non-fungal FAS-like entries. For both subunits the consequent is exactly what Swiss-Prot curates on its reviewed members, and there is no more specific catalytic activity to prefer: the partial reactions are separate EC numbers that Swiss-Prot lists alongside it. This is a catalytic-activity rule, so projects/ARBA_REVIEW/rule_redundancy.tsv has no row for it.

Assessments

ACCEPTABLE

Alpha-subunit sets (1, 5, 6, 8), beta-subunit sets (3, 4, 7) and set 2, which matches both subunits (8 alpha and 6 beta of its 14 reviewed matches), overlap heavily, as alternative routes to the same two subunit families; the overlap adds recall across FunFam and InterPro coverage gaps and does not change what is annotated.

MODERATE

The fungal FAS is a 2.6-MDa alpha6beta6 complex whose two chains together carry all the catalytic domains of the cycle (PMID:17431175), so the overall EC 2.3.1.86 reaction belongs to the complex both subunits form. Support for each set comes from the curated Swiss-Prot FAS subunits it matches (RESULTS-condition-sets.md).

Supporting Evidence:

  • PMID:17431175: We report crystal structures of the 2.6-megadalton alpha6beta6 heterododecameric fatty acid synthase from Thermomyces lanuginosus at 3.1 angstrom resolution.
  • PMID:17431175: The alpha and beta polypeptide chains form the six catalytic domains required for fatty acid synthesis
MINOR

Sets split into two groups by subunit; within each group sets reach the same reviewed entries (for example P07149 and P34229 are matched by sets 3, 4 and 7). Alternative sets are expected to overlap this way when they back each other up across signature databases.

APPROPRIATE

Sets 1, 3 and 7 carry no taxon condition; set 3 is constrained by the fungal family IPR016452 (Fatty acid synthase beta subunit AflB /Fas1-like, fungi), while sets 1 and 7 rest on signature conjunctions alone. Their signatures confine them to fungal-type FAS in practice: no bacterial or archaeal entry matches any set in 2026_04. Adding a Fungi condition to set 1 would remove 71 stramenopile and protist FAS-alpha-like entries; that is worth doing only if those are shown not to be fungal-type FAS, which this review did not establish.
