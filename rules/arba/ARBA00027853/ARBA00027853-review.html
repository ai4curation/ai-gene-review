Review Summary

All 89 live condition sets were queried. Each set was turned into a UniProtKB query and its Swiss-Prot matches were inspected on 2026-11-09. A match counts as supported when it carries the term or an is_a/part_of descendant with evidence other than ARBA; annotations placed by ARBA rules on Swiss-Prot entries were not counted. Every set has reviewed matches. In 75 sets every reviewed match is supported and in 14 sets (CS1, CS5, CS7, CS9, CS18, CS22, CS25, CS30, CS35, CS37, CS42, CS47, CS58, CS72) some are not. One of these is a demonstrable defect: CS18 (PANTHER PTHR43734 in Pseudomonadati) matches GlpB, a glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase subunit with no isoprenoid role, in 8 of its 22 reviewed matches. Two further matches are doubtful: Q6PQJ9, a 3-oxo-Delta(4,5)-steroid 5-beta-reductase that carries the term only from this rule (CS37), and Q54E48, an NADH dehydrogenase assembly factor homologue (CS1). The retinoid-related sets on ADH7, hormone-sensitive lipase, PNPLA2, LRP1 and CYP2C family members are supported by curated annotation in the isoprenoid branch. QuickGO lists 9043 annotations attributed to this rule, while the rule statistics served by UniProt report zero proteins.

Action Rationale

The term is supported for 75 sets outright and for most matches of the rest, so the rule should be kept. CS18 as published matches GlpB proteins, which are not isoprenoid enzymes, and needs a further condition.
