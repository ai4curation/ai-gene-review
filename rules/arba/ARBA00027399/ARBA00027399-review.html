Review Summary

All 45 live condition sets were checked against Swiss-Prot. They match 292 reviewed entries in total and every one carries GO:0008236 or a descendant, 225 by manual evidence. One set is wrong: set 13 selects BMP1 and the tolloid-like proteins, which are zinc metalloproteases. They carry a serine-type endopeptidase term only through a Reactome TAS annotation, and the rule passes that term on to 134 unreviewed entries. The other sets were checked by counts only.

Action Rationale

Set 13 propagates a serine-type activity to zinc metalloproteases of MEROPS family M12 and should be removed. No defect was found in the other 44 sets. The counts are from Swiss-Prot, which is also what ARBA learns its rules from, so they show the condition sets are coherent rather than giving an independent error rate for unreviewed entries.
