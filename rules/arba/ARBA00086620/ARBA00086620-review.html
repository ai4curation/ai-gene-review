Description

Assigns GO:0043935 (sexual sporulation resulting in formation of a cellular spore) through four alternative condition sets, each a CATH FunFam with a taxon restriction: 14-3-3 proteins in Saccharomycetes, a syntaxin FunFam in Saccharomycetales, a chitin deacetylase FunFam in Eukaryota excluding Dictyostelia, and the sporulation protein kinase mde3 FunFam in Fungi.

The domain overlap table is not shown for this review. It is drawn from a cached analysis of the rule's condition sets, and the cache does not match the sets recorded here.

Review Summary

Accuracy: all four live condition sets were examined. Each matches two reviewed proteins, and all eight carry an experimentally supported annotation to a descendant of GO:0043935 (sexual sporulation resulting in formation of a cellular spore). The rule is sound for the proteins that can be checked. Meaning and parsimony: the four sets select four unrelated families (14-3-3 proteins, Sso t-SNAREs, chitin deacetylases, a sporulation kinase FunFam), and none of their members is curated to the consequent itself: every one carries a more specific descendant, GO:0030437 (ascospore formation), GO:0031321 (ascospore-type prospore assembly) or GO:0030476 (ascospore wall assembly). Two of the sets are already matched by a rule asserting GO:0030437 and emit nothing, and set 4 matches no unreviewed entry, so it annotates only two proteins that are already curated.

Action Rationale

The consequent is an umbrella over fungal ascospore terms that the curators use directly. Because every set is confined to fungi (set 3 in effect: all 72 of its matches outside Dictyostelia are fungal), the ascospore terms apply to everything the rule reaches. The sets should be re-pointed to those terms, and set 4 can be dropped. For the 14-3-3 and Sso sets the sporulation role is one of many curated processes of pleiotropic proteins, so confidence in keeping them at all is lower than for the chitin deacetylase and kinase sets.

Assessments

REDUNDANT

2 sets are covered by a rule asserting GO:0030437 (rule_redundancy.tsv) and emit nothing. Set 4 matches 0 unreviewed entries. That leaves at most two sets doing work, and both would be better served by a more specific term. rules/arba/_interpro2go.txt has no mapping to GO:0043935.

TOO_BROAD

GO:0030437 (ascospore formation), GO:0031321 (ascospore-type prospore assembly) and GO:0030476 (ascospore wall assembly) have GO:0043935 among their ancestors (QuickGO) and are the terms the reviewed matches carry on experimental evidence. No reviewed match is curated to GO:0043935 itself.

APPROPRIATE

Sets 1, 2 and 4 are fungal clades, which is where ascospores exist. Set 3 is written as Eukaryota excluding Dictyostelia, but all 72 UniProtKB entries it matches are fungal (query 2026-11-12), so the wording is odd and not harmful; Fungi would say what is meant. The narrow clades for the pleiotropic 14-3-3 and Sso families follow where the yeast evidence is and are a sensible limit.
