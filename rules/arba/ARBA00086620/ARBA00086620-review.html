Description

Assigns GO:0043935 (sexual sporulation resulting in formation of a cellular spore) through four alternative condition sets, each a CATH FunFam with a taxon restriction: 14-3-3 proteins in Saccharomycetes, a syntaxin FunFam in Saccharomycetales, a chitin deacetylase FunFam in Eukaryota excluding Dictyostelia, and the sporulation protein kinase mde3 FunFam in Fungi.

Review Summary

All four live condition sets were examined. Each matches two reviewed proteins, and all eight carry an experimentally supported annotation to a descendant of GO:0043935 (ascospore formation, ascospore wall assembly or prospore assembly). The rule is sound for the proteins that can be checked.

Action Rationale

Every condition set selects reviewed fungal proteins with direct experimental evidence for a role in ascospore formation. The four sets are alternatives and are expected to select different protein families. The counts are from Swiss-Prot, which is also what ARBA learns its rules from, so they show the condition sets are coherent rather than giving an independent error rate for unreviewed entries.
