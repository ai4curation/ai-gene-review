description: LigD is a multifunctional ATP-dependent DNA repair ligase that combines strand sealing with
  repair polymerization. Bacillus subtilis LigD combines an N-terminal ligase module and C-terminal polymerase
  module and lacks the phosphoesterase module of some other LigD proteins. Its polymerase module can incise
  abasic sites near recessed 5′ ends, and Ku supports coordinated incision, nucleotide insertion and joining.
  LigD also has 5′-deoxyribose-phosphate lyase activity and contributes to spore DNA repair.
