`0f35f259940fe77212ea281b15d33ae23a7c5e8ccc2d64566f50d0cc53aa20d4` matches the historical receipt.

the script files are pre-format versions. `prior-assertions.json` is deterministically

not generated substitutes. The full PMID16046407 cached abstract is recovered from

recreated validation logs and do not claim fresh validation. The old final-report

fabricated. See recovery-report.json for gaps and recovery-manifest.json for hashes
of the recovered artifacts, which are NEW recovery hashes rather than old seal hashes.
