# DsbA/DsbB oxidative folding audit

Thirteen gene reviews are complete across E. coli, P. putida KT2440, P. aeruginosa PA14, B. subtilis 168 and exact-sequence Campylobacter jejuni records. They preserve 78 original annotation payloads and 26 raw sources, and add nine supported annotations. Root verification checks each handoff against its frozen seeds and source hashes.

The comparative catalog curates 13 references, including four reviews, into 23 source-checked findings. The mechanism is DsbB-dependent extracellular protein oxidation. DsbA/VKOR is an alternative, and initial oxidation does not guarantee native disulfide pairing. PA14 supports DsbA1 recycling by DsbB1 **or** DsbB2; PA14 DsbA2 native expression/function remains unresolved. Bacillus BdbD acts outside the cytoplasmic membrane rather than in a Gram-negative periplasm, while its BdbC recycling chemistry remains inferred.

Campylobacter DsbA1/A2 are full-sequence matches between current species-level records and the characterized 81116 proteins. Exact DsbB UniProt entries are deleted; the ENA/UniParc identity is retained as an explicit gap rather than replaced by a different strain. The later primary study also corrects the older DsbA1 motility phenotype to background mutations.

Family assessments and module integration remain in progress. Module validation, full-corpus validation, dedicated publication, independent review, CI and actual merge are still required.
