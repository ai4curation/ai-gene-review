# Flavonoid follow-up: source and inference boundaries

## 2026-11-06 source checks

The accompanying `round1-evidence/*.json` files preserve official API responses and URLs. QuickGO confirms GO:0003978 as UDP-glucose 4-epimerase activity, defined as "Catalysis of the reaction: UDP-glucose = UDP-galactose." Its EC mapping is EC:5.1.3.2. The problem is the unsupported substrate-specific EC on Hsero_1012, not an incorrect EC2GO dictionary relation.

The frozen D8J0X4 UniProt record attributes both name and EC to the EMBL submitter (`ECO:0000313`, `EMBL:ADJ62529.1`). InterPro IPR001509 spans NAD-dependent nucleotide-sugar reactions; its official description says "This domain is found in proteins that utilise NAD as a cofactor and use nucleotide-sugar substrates for a variety of chemical reactions". A domain match cannot identify UDP-glucose specificity. The separate K01784 genes and FdeJ placement identify a competing classification, not a negative activity assay. Accordingly GO:0003978 is MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED, with the probable unsupported EC source defect restored. Deletion of the unsubstantiated database EC assertion is requested without asserting biological incapacity.

## FdeB comparison

All three FdeB candidates belong to IPR050261. Reviewed UniProt Q93NG6 also carries IPR050261 and experimentally supported FUNCTION text: "Plays a role in nicotine catabolism by cleaving a C-C bond in 2,6-dihydroxypseudooxynicotine" (ECO:0000269 with PMID:16321959 and PMID:17275835). Its curated catalytic reaction is EC:3.7.1.19. Q4WZB3 is another reviewed IPR050261 protein, a heptaketide hydrolyase. These are family-breadth controls, not proposed functional donors for FdeB. They establish that this family name and hydrolase fold do not choose ester rather than C-C hydrolysis. The InterPro family overview is marked reviewed-LLM in the API; its broad text is not treated as an assay of these targets.

PP_3195 and bll6428 share 81.6% and 81.0% aligned identity, respectively, with Hsero_1004 in the existing reproducible comparisons. No FdeB protein in this panel has a substrate-resolved assay. Sequence similarity to an unassayed homolog does not resolve the shared uncertainty. The three peptide/proteolysis claims are consistently MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED, while carboxylic ester hydrolase activity is UNDECIDED. Broad family-level hydrolysis remains supported where already annotated, but it is not promoted into an uninformative core-function synthesis. Neither a peptide-negative assay nor evolutionary loss is claimed.

The inherited PP_3198/FdeD process proposal is withdrawn because the protein doing a flavonoid-catabolic step has not been established: donor, acceptor, role and necessity are unresolved. Hsero_1006 and bll6426 likewise carry no asserted process row. For consistency, the two inherited FdeB process proposals are also withdrawn: the locus identifies candidates, but the particular physiological step remains a hypothesis with no independently assayed FdeB donor. All inherited rows remain present, with MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED judgments and explicit retired:true flags recording curator withdrawal from the active set rather than silently deleting the proposals. This is not an upstream GOA disappearance. The schema defines retired as an annotation being retired or replaced; docs/goa_source_refresh.md preserves withdrawn curator-authored assertions through explicit retirement. No claim of absence is made. The corresponding process assertions are removed from core_functions and kept as questions/knowledge gaps.

The module retains the hypothesized R13077 chemical role and its ester-hydrolysis GO class. This says what the modeled reaction would be, not that all three candidate carriers demonstrably execute it. The capacity checker tests proposed carrier coverage only, never validated member activity or native flux.


## Complete inherited-process audit

The same participation criterion was applied to every inherited NEW process row in the 21-member panel. Nine proposals are now explicitly retired: Hsero_1004/FdeB, PP_3195/FdeB, PP_3198/FdeD, Hsero_1010/FdeH, PP_3204/FdeH, Hsero_1011/FdeI, PP_3205/FdeI, Hsero_1012/FdeJ and PP_3206/FdeJ. The same-role BRADU B/D/H/I/J comparators already assert no process annotations. The proposed late enzyme assignments and the probably polar whole-operon phenotype do not establish an individual catalytic or accessory contribution. Their row payloads remain preserved; the current review records withdrawal and the retired flag excludes the proposal from the active set.

Four process proposals are retained: HERSS Hsero_1005/FdeC and Hsero_1007/FdeE, and their PSEPK counterparts PP_3197 and PP_3199. These have an experimentally grounded step to transfer, unlike the unassayed late components. PMID:27059806 reports accumulation of the hydroxylated intermediate in the fdeC mutant and loss of naringenin conversion in the fdeE mutant; PMID:39107441 directly assays purified FdeE. The PSEPK proposals combine that donor evidence with the existing whole-protein and conserved-locus comparisons (PP_3197 69.5% aligned identity; PP_3199 reciprocal-best-hit relationship and 40.4% aligned identity). Their status remains inference, not a claim of direct KT2440 activity. The three existing FdeC reaction-class MF proposals are also retained. Thus seven NEW rows remain active, with no new rows added in this follow-up.
