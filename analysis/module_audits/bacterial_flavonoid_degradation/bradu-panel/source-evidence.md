## Primary source boundaries
2013 primary: Marin et al., PMID23125118, DOI https://doi.org/10.1099/mic.0.061135-0. Author-uploaded full-paper transcription read at https://www.academia.edu/50645228/Naringenin_degradation_by_the_endophytic_diazotroph_Herbaspirillum_seropedicae_SmR1 (2026-11-06), page170. It explicitly includes Bradyrhizobium japonicum USDA110 among genomes with similar operons. Exact operative excerpt: "although their functions in these organisms remain unknown". The historical species name refers to the USDA110 strain now named Bradyrhizobium diazoefficiens, matching frozen taxon224911 records. This is locus-homology evidence, not a USDA110 substrate assay.
PMID39107441 studies recombinant HERSS FdeE; PMID38707841 studies Variovorax V35 Ifc enzymes. The latter demonstrates substrate divergence among related monooxygenases and downstream activity of cupins; neither paper assays these BRADU proteins. IfcD1/D2 equivalence to bll6423 has not been established here.
The comparison supports homologous architecture. It does not assign naringenin versus isoflavone preference, a native full pathway, partner dependence, or exact late hydrolytic/decarboxylating chemistry.
