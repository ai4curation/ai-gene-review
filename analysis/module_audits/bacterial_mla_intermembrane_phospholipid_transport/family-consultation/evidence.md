# Evidence adjudication

Authoritative URLs, retrieval date, response SHA256 and compressed-byte SHA256 are recorded in `acquisition.json` and `additional-acquisition.json`. Official trees and MSAs were independently parsed, not reconstructed from gene annotations. `phylogeny-evidence.json` reports exact leaf sets, subfamilies and sequence motif observations. A motif match establishes neither cargo specificity nor a causal loss residue.

Current QuickGO GO0120010 definition: “The transport of phospholipids between membranes in which a phospholipid molecule is transported through an aqueous phase from the outer leaflet of a donor membrane to the outer leaflet of an acceptor membrane.” It does not specify bacteria, diderms or inner-to-outer direction. GO0120014 specifically includes removal from a membrane/particle, aqueous shielding in a pocket and delivery to a membrane/particle. GO1990531 explicitly specifies a P-Type ATPase complex; the independent ABC-versus-P-type correction remains supported. Exact current responses are in `ontology.json`.

## Primary evidence read for the corrections

* **PMID27529189**, full cached primary: “Within this complex, MlaD forms an SDS-resistant hexamer via its soluble domain, and we demonstrate that this domain binds PLs.” This is positive binding/assembly evidence. MlaFEB ATPase and MlaD modulation distinguish subunit roles; no universal inability of an isolated subunit to contribute is inferred.
* **PMID34873038**, full cached primary and authentic full preflight source: “such anterograde movement is instead abolished by ATP hydrolysis”. Exchange is reversible without ATP and biased by ATP at the characterized MlaC–MlaFEDB interface. This is not proof of powered anterograde transport or all-taxon native whole-cell flux.
* **PMID28388411**, full cached primary: direct MlaC interactions are measured “using biolayer interferometry”. Proposed ferry handoff in the discussion is not mislabelled as a measured transfer experiment. Later transfer work is considered separately.
* **PMID33837253**, full cached primary: PAO1 material expressed homologously and heterologously can carry two diacyl phospholipids or one cardiolipin. Cargo binding and native periplasmic extraction differ from heterologous crystal-construct structure and full-route flux. This supports the positive Pseudomonas carrier without licensing non-bacterial-labelled reference leaves.
* **PMID32602838**, full cached primary: “can be rescued by overexpression of either WT MlaB or MlaF”. This is conditional rescue of the E. coli MlaB deletion phenotype; the source still supports MlaB-mediated MlaF stabilization/assembly at ordinary expression. It does not make all MlaB proteins unnecessary.
* **PMID36518166**, full cached primary and preflight supplement: “we were not able to identify an MlaB homologue in B. pertussis”. Native B213 MlaF/OMPLA perturbations and surface-PE measurements support asymmetry maintenance with lineage/context caveats. Other operon-component identities are not independent native transfer assays.
* **PMID36642182**, full cached primary: “Fatty acids and cholesterol are imported across the Mtb cell wall via the dedicated Mce1 and Mce4 transporters, respectively.” MceG contributes ATP-dependent stability/transport. This positive alternate-cargo work does not assay absence of secondary phospholipid activity. The MceG/MceD/YrbE controls are rejected as a demonstrated compatible bacterial Mla panel because their positively established partners, cargo and context differ, not because the family name proves every other activity impossible.
* **PMID20128927**, full cached primary, Results: “We focused on representatives from three groups (membrane and related proteins (emrK, yrbB), cytosolic enzymes (ycjU), and regulators (ycjW, ybcM)). In each case, complementation was observed (Fig. 3).” The library was constructed in E. coli K-12 AB1157, phenotypes transferred to DM4100, and ASKA expression used for complementation. Genotoxic survival and unchanged bacteriostatic susceptibility are genuine observations. They do not distinguish a response-specific activity from constitutive envelope protection, and do not justify a categorical family-wide DNA-response negative.

The retained TGD1 plasma-membrane loss is supported by the original curated chloroplast localization evidence and current official Q8L4R0 record; exact loss object and member exception are independently preserved. The MlaE plant IRD covers 50 current reference leaves, rather than only the Arabidopsis representative.

Some older references remain abstract-only (including PMID17938172, 12743031 and 8367727). Existing positive statements are not treated as freshly read full assays, and this consultation introduces no new experimental negative on that basis. The unrelated older host-entry assessment is preserved as such. No provider research file was fabricated or raw publication cache refreshed.
