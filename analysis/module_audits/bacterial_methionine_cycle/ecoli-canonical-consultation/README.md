This consultation completes the newly seeded metK (P0A817), metE (P25665) and metH (P13009) reviews. All 73 ordered seed annotation payloads, source qualifiers and evidence codes are retained. No annotation rows were added, deleted or retired. The prior PSEPK four-gene and E. coli pfs/luxS releases remain byte-identical.

MetK has direct E. coli SAM-synthetase, cofactor and functional oligomer evidence. The complete synthetase reaction includes tripolyphosphate hydrolysis; this does not justify adding arbitrary ATPase or downstream methylation-process claims. Self-binding refines to the experimentally established homodimer interface within the tetramer. Chromosomal fluorescent fusions in DY380 show diffuse cytoplasmic signal plus foci, not a uniform untagged-protein distribution.

MetE retains the canonical polyglutamyl-folate reaction GO:0003871. Both monoglutamate GO:0008705 rows are refined to this activity on substrate evidence. The current GO:0008705 definition does not require cobalamin, so B12 independence is not the argument. PMID16618097 explicitly summarizes the monoglutamate restriction and directly assays triglutamate with wild-type MetE expressed from pJG816 in K-12 GW2531; its negative statement cites earlier literature. The independently acquired full article is retained only in ignored cache, with a scoped excerpt and provenance here. The publication cache is abstract-only and unchanged after the initial normal fetch. Zinc binding and reversible oxidative inactivation are kept distinct from autonomous stress-response functions. The recorded binding partner P03018 is UvrD.

MetH retains cobalamin-mediated homocysteine remethylation, catalytic zinc and folate regeneration. The E. coli flavodoxin/SAM reactivation route has direct partner-specific evidence; arbitrary ferredoxins or flavodoxins are not interchangeable. Truncated-domain half-reactions are not asserted to reproduce the entire intact-enzyme cycle. Both methionine synthases directly regenerate methionine from homocysteine, consistent with the broad current salvage definition; this is not a claim of upstream methylthioadenosine salvage or measured complete native cycle flux.
