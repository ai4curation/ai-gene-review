## Lnt: restore the original argument, retain unresolved ancestral scope

The original baseline and commit c9d4df9a8a contain a substantive chemistry objection, not merely an inference from absent GOA. PTN000557706/GO0050126 occurs in both PTHR38686 and PTHR43674 slices and names NLP1 (AT2G27450) and AguB (Q9I6J8). Fresh official records identify AT2G27450 as Q8VYF5, alias NLP1, and classify both seeds as PTHR43674:SF2. Q8VYF5 has publication-linked experimental hydrolysis evidence (PMID12435743). The AguB record is unreviewed; its name alone is not independent chemistry evidence. The primary abstract at https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12634339/ independently describes characterized P. aeruginosa AguB N-carbamoylputrescine amidohydrolase and its putrescine-biosynthesis role. This abstract was read; no claim of reading its full text is made.

Full cached PMID21676878 instead establishes E. coli Lnt phospholipid-to-apolipoprotein N-acyl transfer. The fresh GO0050126 definition specifically requires N-carbamoylputrescine hydrolysis. Shared CN-hydrolase architecture does not license that substrate-specific assignment. These are valid grounds to withhold a positive family mapping, and have been restored explicitly in the family review. The prior characterization of the original reasoning as GOA-only, and the requirement to exclude every imaginable secondary activity, have been removed.

The requested categorical reversal is nevertheless not adopted. `TermScopeEnum.NOT_APPLICABLE` means “Not safe for any member; the family is misnamed or the mapping is wrong.” The incomplete cached Lnt tree lacks PTN000557706. Two overlapping family slices and distinct seed chemistry do not reconstruct its descendants or establish that a specific ancestral placement is wrong. The term and node therefore remain UNRESOLVED; this does not license an amidase assignment to any Lnt-only member. An exhaustive negative activity assay is not being required: the missing evidence is the actual descendant/domain context needed to adjudicate the mapping. No PTN, loss, exception or new PAINT row is invented.

GO0009247 and GO0004582 remain UNRESOLVED for a different reason. Their O53493 seed is a real Lnt/Ppm1 fusion with curated glycosyltransferase evidence, including a dolichyl-phosphate reaction mapping linked to PMID11931640. That domain-specific activity must not be generalized from the Lnt domain to standalone Lnt. Conversely, it cannot be categorically excluded from the whole fusion protein. Original fusion-domain arguments remain explicit. The official PANTHER-versus-UniProt classification disagreement for O53493 remains disclosed.

The original, published and revised judgments are preserved in `judgment-ledger.json`. PTHR38686 is the sole changed family review; statuses and all ordered assertion payloads are unchanged. PTHR40042 is read-only and unchanged.
