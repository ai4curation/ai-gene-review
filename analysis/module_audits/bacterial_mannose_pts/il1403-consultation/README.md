# Native IL1403 mannose PTS member consultation

Three complete reviews cover PtnAB/Q9CEX8, PtnC/Q9CEX7 and PtnD/Q9CEX6 in Lactococcus lactis IL1403, taxon 272623. All 15 seeded annotations remain in their original order with their source payloads intact. There are no new or retired annotations. Eight rows are ACCEPT and seven are MODIFY. General sugar-transporter and phosphotransfer functions refine to both native supported mannose and glucose coupled reactions; broad membrane/cytoplasm locations refine to supported locations.

Both primary full texts (PMID17284603 and PMID38047704) were consulted without altering their caches. The 2007 paper explicitly identifies the IL1403 ptn operon in genome AE005176, matching the official target records. Its native co-isolation/mass spectrometry identifies PtnAB, PtnC and PtnD. Whole-operon disruption reduces glucose/mannose growth. The reported complementation primarily establishes bacteriocin receptor rescue, not measured uptake flux. PtnC/PtnD alone suffice for receptor sensitivity; that fact does not establish sugar phosphorylation by either membrane protein.

The 2024 paper (online December 2023) examines native IL1403 mutants using primers based on genome CP033607.1. Sugar assays measure OD600 over 24 hours at 30°C in M17 with 0.5% glucose, mannose or cellobiose, plus no-sugar controls. Mutants 1 and 5 retain near-wild-type utilization. Other mutations outside the sequenced ptn genes were not excluded. These data support native sugar utilization, not purified flux or exclusive dependence on this route. The receptor phenotype and transport phenotype are not interchangeable.

Component functions combine this native evidence with specific PROSITE/InterPro domain assignments. ARBA-only prose is not used as independent evidence. PtnAB supplies the inferred EIIA/EIIB catalytic relay; PtnC/PtnD supply sugar-binding/translocation machinery. All three cores use contributes_to_molecular_function GO:0022870 because the term describes the complete coupled membrane-transport reaction. This does not deny catalytic phosphate transfer by PtnAB. Exact E. coli phosphohistidine numbering, intra/inter-subunit kinetics and complete-complex stoichiometry are not transferred to IL1403. The PtnC conserved-residue caution remains an explicit question, not evidence that transport is absent.
