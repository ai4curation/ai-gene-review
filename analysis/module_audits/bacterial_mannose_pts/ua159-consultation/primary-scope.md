# UA159 amino-sugar evidence

PMID24928869 full text was read at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4135778/?report=xml ; the captured source and hash are frozen in the literature consultation. The normal publication cache remains abstract-only.

Results and Fig2 compare native UA159 with deletion of the complete manLMN operon using PEP-dependent sugar-transport measurements. The authors report:

> almost no detectable PTS-dependent uptake of GlcNAc

This directly supports the intact apparatus, beyond the separate ManL mutant comparison. The paper also measures reduced glucosamine transport with residual alternative activity. ManL-only complementation gives partial GlcNAc growth recovery; a stable full-operon complement was not obtained. Thus complete restoration is not claimed. Domain and locus evidence identifies ManM/ManN as the membrane actors, but their individual purified activities were not independently measured. The discussion distinguishes divergent ManM/ManN lineages within S. mutans, so other strains cannot supply an unqualified interface or exact-residue inference.
