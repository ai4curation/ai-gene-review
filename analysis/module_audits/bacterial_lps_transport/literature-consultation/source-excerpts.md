## PMID:41407669: Structural snapshots of Pseudomonas aeruginosa LptB(2)FG and LptB(2)FGC reveal insights into lipopolysaccharide recognition and transport.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12738788/

> tagged LptB variants are at least partially functional in vivo in P. aeruginosa

Full primary Results/Methods read. Genes were amplified from PAO1-LAC DNA and expressed in E. coli BL21/ClearColi. A C-terminally His-tagged LptB is used; PAO1 conditional complementation supports partial in-vivo function. Cross-species LptC non-substitution is cited prior work, not newly measured here.

## PMID:41285762: Structural basis of lipopolysaccharide assembly by the outer membrane translocon holo-complex.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12644819/

> together, LptM and LptY stabilize the β-taco domain thereby facilitating the connection of the OM translocon

Full Results/Discussion and Methods examined. E. coli LptDE(M/Y) complexes, structural analysis, crosslinking and functional tests support a terminal-transport contribution. Presence in some enterobacterial lineages does not establish universal requirement.
