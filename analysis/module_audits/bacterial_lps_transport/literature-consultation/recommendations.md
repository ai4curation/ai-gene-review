# LPS module literature and taxon consultation

The reusable boundary is LPS/LOS in the outer leaflet of the inner membrane to
its exposure in the outer leaflet of the outer membrane. MsbA flipping,
biosynthesis and core/O-antigen elaboration are upstream. The literature supports
coupled extraction, periplasmic transfer and terminal insertion, with cytoplasmic
ATP turnover powering the system. Individual subunits supply different positive
parts of this work; an isolated ABC ATPase or generic transporter-family hit is
not a complete LPS extractor. The donor and acceptor interfaces must be compatible.

Three review articles were searched and read manually. PMID26038291 is especially
informative for taxonomic distribution and homologs in LPS-free diderms;
PMID38816673 reviews the structural mechanics; PMID35850151 separates Lpt from
IM flippers and other lipid-trafficking systems and states open stoichiometric
questions. Their broad architecture is supported by claim-specific primary studies
in the ledger. The latter includes the newest 2026 E. coli regulation study and
2025 accessory-translocon and Pseudomonas structures. The role of LptM/Y should
not be dismissed as solely assembly: their terminal-gate/bridge contributions
support lineage-qualified optional enhancement. They are not universal mandatory
components. A static seven-essential-protein assertion should be restricted to
the canonical E. coli context, including its genetic-background caveats.

## Additional representative panel

The most informative non-gammaproteobacterial additions are Neisseria LptD and
sequence-matched carrier LptH and LptE. Official accession/strain/CDS evidence is
provided in identity/. F0MIT7 is a direct H44/76 reference; Q9K0A7 and Q7DDQ9 are
MC58 records with exact H44 CDS matches. These are positives for the transport
role of LptD/H and a role-difference control for LptE. The source phenotype
remains H44/76/HB-1; the three proteins do not constitute a complete reviewed
native transport panel. Never use the unrelated neisserial lipid A modifying
LptA as the carrier. No LptC exact sequence bridge is claimed.

PAO1-LAC LptBFG(C) is another strong structural/biochemical anchor, but it adds
another gammaproteobacterial context. Its heterologous expression host is not
the protein species. The tagged LptB was partly functional in a PAO1 conditional
assay. Binding of commercial LPS, cryo-EM states and ATPase measurements are not
complete native trans-envelope flux reconstitution. In the 2026 bridge paper,
LPS-null Acinetobacter is an expression host for E. coli proteins; it must not
be added as a native Acinetobacter machinery panel from that experiment.

## Capacity and uncertainty limits

- Substrate matters: the 2015 survey finds Lpt-like sequences in LPS-free diderms.
  Family membership alone cannot identify LPS transport, and broad diderm/bacterial
  membership is not a positive capacity observation.
- Meningococcal viability despite loss of LPS does not imply intact LPS transport.
  LptC/H/F/G deletions fail surface-localization assays. The LptE result differs:
  surface accessibility persists although LptD levels fall.
- Selected E. coli suppressor backgrounds can bypass LptC (PMID27529623). This
  bounds canonical-profile negatives; it does not warrant treating LptC as
  universally optional or inventing an unverified ancestral alternative node.
- Cross-species interfaces are neither freely interchangeable nor universally
  impossible. The 2016 primary cites prior Pa LptC complementation under altered
  LptB expression. Keep matched-context gates and acknowledge exceptional tests.
- Native LptA count remains context-dependent/unresolved. Engineered fusion and
  crosslinked bridge structures establish architecture, not universal native
  one-copy or tetramer stoichiometry.
- Detailed LptD contracted/extended dynamics and accessory occupancy contain
  mechanistic proposals alongside measured structure/function. Distinguish these
  from directly measured complete transport and avoid assigning an autonomous
  reaction to an accessory protein merely from a binding result.
