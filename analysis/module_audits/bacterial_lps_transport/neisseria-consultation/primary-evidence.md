## surface

> Only the LPS of the wild type and of the Δ lptE mutant was surface-accessible

## levels

> Cellular LptD levels were similar in all mutants except in the Δ lptE strain where they were severely decreased

## restore

> This defect was completely restored in a strain complemented with a copy of lptE under IPTG control on plasmid

## interaction

> Affinity purification using Ni 2+ -NTA beads on membrane extracts resulted in co-purification of LptD with LptE

## control

> This interaction was specific because no Omp85/BamA was co-purified and no LptD was purified when membranes containing overexpression levels of LptE without a His tag were used

## other

> The LPS of other lpt mutants was not affected by neuraminidase treatment, showing that it is not exposed at the cell surface

## name

> we will use the designation LptH for the neisserial homolog of E. coli LptA.

## strain

> HB-1 H44/76 with the capsule locus, including the galE gene, inactivated resulting in expression of truncated LPS

## fold

> The absence of LptE did not grossly affect any of the electrophoretic properties of LptD, although some unfolded LptD was detected in unboiled preparations of the Δ lptE mutant cell envelopes

Interpretation: H44/76 and its HB-1 capsule/galE derivative are experimental strains; MC58 locus identifiers are homology coordinates.
