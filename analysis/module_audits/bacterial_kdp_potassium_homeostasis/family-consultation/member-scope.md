# KdpD family architecture and member scope — 2026-11-07

Official UniProt REST snapshots and exact URLs are in Q1J2K3.json, P73870.json, Q725T9.json and member-access.json. They are separate dated evidence captures; frozen family entries, PAINT, metadata and gene records are untouched.

All three records map to PANTHER PTHR45569:SF1. P73870 is370aa and its signature assignments cover KdpD N-terminal/P-loop architecture without the canonical histidine-kinase transmitter. Q725T9 is379aa; Q1J2K3 is365aa; their annotated domains are the N-terminal KdpD region and UspA. These records contain no transmembrane feature and no HisKA/HATPase transmitter annotation. This is architecture evidence against autonomous canonical histidine phosphorelay kinase activity in those short proteins, not an experimental negative for every kinase chemistry. No-TM annotation does not exclude peripheral membrane association or protein-mediated recruitment, so universal plasma-membrane placement is unresolved rather than universally false.

Full cached PMID38622146 explicitly names the assayed Deinococcus geothermalis comparator as Q1J2K3 in the Results paragraph comparing full-length and short forms. It demonstrates atypical serine-kinase regulation by c-di-AMP for the short KdpD/Usp form. The main-family interpretation therefore distinguishes absence of the C-terminal histidine transmitter from absence of catalytic activity. No serine-kinase activity is granted automatically to P73870, Q725T9, KT2440 KdpD or every family member. The separate new-gene identity audit checks supplemental sequence information; this review does not infer strain identity from the protein's length alone.

The old P73870 and Q725T9 exception objects are preserved verbatim in historical-scope.json. Their negative judgments concern GO:0000155, not a universal kinase negative. Family-wide scope with only those exceptions is withdrawn because architectural classes are not exhaustively partitioned by the single subfamily. The old assertion that Q725T9's missing transmitter is necessarily supplied by its adjacent gene is preserved as history but is not reused as proof without a checked locus/primary assay. No pruning-node identity or causal node placement is invented.

KdpE SF50 remains a supported potassium-homeostasis regulator. The old exclusive SF50 list is archived because no survey established absence of potassium-homeostasis roles in all other OmpR/PhoB-family subfamilies. This is a bounded Kdp reconciliation, not a new audit of the entire family or its unrelated PAINT/IRD judgments.
