# Bacillus OpuC consultation

All four reference proteins are Bacillus subtilis 168 (NCBITaxon:224308): O34992/opuCA, O34878/opuCB, O32243/opuCC, O34742/opuCD. They are distinct from OpuB paralogs; closely related OpuB does not import glycine betaine, so shared PANTHER names alone do not establish substrate breadth.

PMID9925583 establishes complex-level choline-O-sulfate uptake, competitive substrate interaction and intact cytoplasmic accumulation; native JH642 and RMKB derivatives are from strain168. COS did not support sole carbon, nitrogen or sulfur nutrition under tested conditions. These proteins provide an import/osmoprotection route, not a sulfate-ester cleavage or sulfur assimilation route. Uptake is supported by direct radiolabeled measurements, not merely necessity.

PMID10216873 assigns the four subunit types: OpuCA conserved ABC ATPase, OpuCB/OpuCD integral-membrane translocation proteins and OpuCC an extracellular lipid-anchored binding protein. Lipid modification was experimentally tested. Two OpuCA copies plus CB, CC and CD give the five-polypeptide substrate-binding-subunit-containing importer model. Native uptake remains a whole-complex activity. The paper explicitly reports that JH642 and genome-project sequences agree except for two adjacent OpuCC differences. Current O32243 documents the historical sequence conflict and erroneous initiation of a submitted sequence; neither is asserted to be a proven error in the frozen canonical reference protein.

PMID31061098 full text obtained via normal force fetch: target genes amplified from strain168 DNA; OpuCA binding measured by DRaCALA in E. coli expression lysates, with specificity against ATP competition. The native OpuC transport response to cyclic-di-AMP remains unresolved: functional inhibition was shown for KimA. Specific cyclic-di-AMP binding is an intrinsic OpuCA MF.

PMID21366542 provides isolated OpuCC ligand binding and structures for carnitine, glycine betaine, choline and ectoine. RCSB 3PPQ entity1 equals MGHHHHHH + the complete frozen O32243 sequence (303 aa), establishing identity independently of a species label. COS was not a ligand in this structure series. Choline binding is an intrinsic OpuCC MF. The full paper repeats older whole-cell transport affinities as dissociation constants; the review does not treat these as newly measured purified-protein affinities.
