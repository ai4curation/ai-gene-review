# Independent nitrogen-PTS scientific review

The reviewed draft supports the canonical native PtsP–NPr/HPr–PtsN relay in three panels. No blocking biological correction was identified. This is an independent review of the hashed draft and its evidence, not a claim of universal pathway presence, purified reconstitution of every edge, or final publication/source closure.

## Scientific decisions

1. **Boundary and chemistry.** Three substantive roles are appropriate: PEP-dependent entry, histidyl-carrier transfer, and phosphorylation-state-dependent terminal regulation. PtsN receives phosphate but its downstream regulatory action is noncovalent. No evidence supports adding phosphate-transfer edges to KdpD, AceE, SpoT or YcgO. GO:0016775 describes the carrier's nitrogenous acceptor without inventing ATP dependence or a His-to-Asp response-regulator mechanism. GO:0098772 is a defensible shared genus for enzyme and transporter regulation. The terminal `REACTION` node is an activity-model representation; its description explicitly specifies noncovalent regulation rather than chemical transformation of the output target.
2. **Native panel specificity.** The nine native representatives match the reviewed genes and the evidence. E. coli PtsP–PtsO–PtsN, KT2440 PtsP–PP_0948–PtsN, and NA1000 PtsP–HPr–PtsN are separate native panels. KT2440 `ptsH` is the established Q88PA2/PP_0948 record for literature NPr/ptsO. The NA1000 HPr name is legitimate. Neither a shared GO activity nor a mixed-species set of representatives establishes native partner compatibility.
3. **Entry and relay evidence.** PMID10473571 gives purified E. coli PtsP specificity for NPr under its assay conditions; the abstract does not establish absolute insulation. PMID7876255 reports reversible purified phosphotransfer including carbohydrate-system exchange; it does not establish every exchange as native flux. PMID18296519 uses KT2440-derived MAD2 mutants and tagged PtsN, with native-state evidence for the principal route and fructose-dependent alternative input. Its cached `full_text_available: true` is insufficient by itself: Methods/Results are present in the separately acquired full PMC text. PMID27109061 uses NA1000 derivatives and EI-containing fractions for entry phosphorylation, not homogeneous purified EI.
4. **Terminal output evidence.** E. coli KdpD kinase activation is directly reported by PMID19400808, whose local cache is abstract-only; it must not dictate the sign of KT2440 KdpD regulation. PMID26224366 supports KT2440 PtsN–KdpD interaction and potassium-dependent transcriptional repression, but does not resolve the kinase/phosphatase catalytic mechanism from the accessible abstract. PMID21236318 supports KT2440 AceE association and PDH inhibition using immunoprecipitation, colocalization and extract activity, not purified binary inhibition. The draft says this accurately. PMID27109061 supports NA1000 phospho-PtsN–SpoT interaction and hydrolase regulation using heterologous bacterial two-hybrid and engineered native hydrolase readouts; HPr also affects alarmone synthesis indirectly. It does not establish a stable four-protein assembly. PMID39742999 supplies a distinct E. coli transporter-inhibition output; its detailed CorC/RCK activation mechanism remains a model.
5. **Alternative paths and independent outputs.** PA14 PMID37074168 supports FruB substitution when PtsO is absent, not a PAO1 claim or a universal purified bypass edge. PA14 PMID40748077 shows PtsO-dependent outputs without PtsN; a three-component model must not absorb all upstream phenotypes. NA1000 PMID26489861 co-complex association is contextual, not purified binary binding. Sinorhizobium PMID24633875 supports purified entry/carrier chemistry and glutamine regulation, but unresolved donor-strain provenance and incomplete reviewed triplet prevent a fourth native panel. The late-2026 Rhizobium leguminosarum study in prior triage likewise does not license a complete additional panel or an experimentally established ManX–MDH interaction.
6. **Family and controls.** PTHR46244:SF6 includes both NA1000 PtsP and E. coli carbohydrate PtsI. PTHR33705:SF2 includes native NPr/HPr and Bacillus carbohydrate HPr. Omitting an exact role-family ID for the first two nodes is honest. PtsN SF1 is a classification, not a grant of one downstream target, regulatory sign or complete upstream route. The controls' genuine phosphotransfer activities are retained; their exclusion is from the demonstrated canonical nitrogen panel, not a claim that cross-talk is chemically impossible.
7. **Remaining limitations.** Boolean module compilation cannot establish cognate pairing, substrate availability, phosphostate, target identity or regulated flux. The separate capacity audit must enforce those source-qualified predicates and distinguish unavailable demonstrated capacity from biological absence. Short verbatim excerpts in the module are valid, but scientific interpretation depends on the complete source-specific statement and access limits recorded here; quote matching alone does not establish causality.

## Additional literature search

Primary and publisher searches identified the 2010 review by Pflüger-Grau and Görke, PMID20202847, DOI10.1016/j.tim.2010.02.003. Root normally acquired it; the abstract supports the comparative regulatory framing and is not a new native assay. Search also identified primary PMID41960855, DOI10.1111/mmi.70068, published April/June2026. Its PubMed abstract links PtsN/YcgO-dependent potassium regulation to methylglyoxal resistance, while explicitly leaving acidification versus potassium effects incompletely separable. A direct PMC browser attempt returned CAPTCHA, so this review does not claim independently inspected full Methods. Root was asked to acquire the normal publication. No new detoxification annotation or module step is warranted from necessity/phenotype alone.
