description: PtsN is the terminal EIIA(Ntr) phosphoprotein of the nitrogen-regulatory phosphotransferase
  system in Pseudomonas putida KT2440. It receives phosphate through the principal PtsP–NPr relay, with
  conditional input from the fructose PTS, and couples phosphorylation state to regulatory interactions.
  PtsN interacts with KdpD and controls expression of the high-affinity potassium transporter KdpFABC.
  Its non-phosphorylated form associates with the pyruvate dehydrogenase E1 subunit AceE and reduces PDH
  activity in cellular extracts. PtsN is cytoplasmic and is not a carbohydrate-phosphorylating transport
  enzyme.
