  Characterized PtsN members convert phosphostate information into noncovalent regulation of native protein
  targets. Targets and effects differ: E. coli PtsN activates KdpD and inhibits potassium-transport systems,
  KT2440 PtsN regulates KdpD-dependent expression and PDH activity, and NA1000 PtsN regulates SpoT hydrolase.
  This common regulatory role does not imply a universal kinase target or regulatory sign.'
