summary: An alpha/beta-hydrolase-fold family named for vertebrate ABHD6, a membrane-anchored monoacylglycerol
  lipase that also associates with AMPA-type glutamate receptors (SF5). Most other members are uncharacterized
  bacterial, fungal, algal and plant AB hydrolases (SF31, SF33, SF26 and others). The Pseudomonas putida
  PpG2 acetoin-system E2 AcoC (Q59695) and the P. putida KT2440 NicD protein are classified in SF33 through
  their C-terminal AB hydrolase-1 domain. AcoC adds an N-terminal lipoyl domain and is assigned as a dihydrolipoyllysine-residue
  acetyltransferase. The same SF5 also contains the bacterial intracellular-PHA-mobilization protein PhaZ
  P26495 from Ectopseudomonas oleovorans GPo1; the ABHD6 subfamily label does not establish monoacylglycerol
  specificity for that member.
