# GPo1 identity and native PHA evidence

P26494, P26495 and P26496 are the exact translations of M58445 CDS AAA25932.1, AAA25933.1 and AAA25934.1, respectively (559,283,560 residues). The official archived names phaA/phaB/phaC correspond to modern phaC1/phaZ/phaC2. UniProt ties PMID1989978 to strain GPo1. This is documented by the frozen literature-consultation/identity/results.json and official records, not by an inferred alias from a similarly named gene.

PMID11133449 full publisher PDF was acquired from the University of Buenos Aires repository: https://bibliotecadigital.exactas.uba.ar/download/paper/paper_00992240_v67_n1_p225_Ruiz.pdf . Its PDF/text hashes are bound in the frozen literature source closure. Methods compare GPo1 and the GPo500 phaZ mutant. Rescue uses a phaC1/phaZ-containing fragment and promoter; it is not purified single-protein reconstitution. Results/Figs5–6 directly follow intracellular polymer decline. The text explicitly notes “leakiness of the phaZ mutant.” Polymer is primarily C8/C6 under these conditions. This supports intracellular mcl-PHA turnover without proving all products have the oligomer size1–5 specified by GO:0050527. Nucleotide accumulation and survival are downstream outcomes, not additional PhaZ molecular activities. The introduction's acyl-CoA discussion is not product-resolved evidence for this enzyme.

Neither the full turnover study nor the consulted curated UniProt evidence demonstrates GPo1 PhaZ monoacylglycerol hydrolysis or physiological glycerolipid degradation. Nor does positive polyester turnover prove those secondary activities absent. The TreeGrafter node PTN002465558 exists in PTHR43798, but P26495 is absent from the cached reference tree; its exact graft placement cannot be independently reconstructed here. The family label ABHD6 and alpha/beta-hydrolase fold are not target-specific substrate evidence. No loss node or catalytic-residue loss is inferred.

GO:0016020 defines a lipid bilayer with associated proteins. GO:0070088 defines a PHA inclusion with a phospholipid monolayer. These definitions do not turn a functional intracellular-polymer association into an assayed bilayer-membrane location. GPo1 PhaZ localization beyond its intracellular role remains unresolved; no sequence-topology negative or absence claim is made.

PhaC1/PhaC2 have publication-linked UniProt functional statements for PHA synthesis and original locus complementation. The observed medium-chain-length polymer supports general PHA biosynthesis; the C4-only GO:0042619 term is more specific than these data. Generalizing to GO:0042621 does not assert an absolute inability to accept C4. A direct target-specific purified substrate panel was not recovered. The original paper's abstract says no clear specificity difference was found between the two synthases, so no obligatory sequential division is assigned.
