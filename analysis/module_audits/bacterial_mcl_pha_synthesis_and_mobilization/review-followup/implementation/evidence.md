# PR6194 implementation evidence

## PhaZ reaction and interpretation

PMID17170116, DOI10.1074/jbc.M608119200, full author-uploaded primary was read at the ResearchGate URL recorded in ../source-notes.md. Results/Fig.6 identifies HO monomer, HO–HO dimer and mixed Hx–HO dimer by LC-MS during KT2442 PhaZ-catalyzed degradation of P(HO-co-HX). Exact short phrase: “amount of dimers released by the depolymerase was higher than that of monomers”. The reaction reaches predominantly monomer at six hours. This directly supports cleavage yielding size-one/two products; it does not prove a pure-HO substrate experiment or a complete KT2442-to-KT2440 sequence identity. The earlier protected abstract remains unchanged.

The GO0050527 inference for KT2440 combines closely related KT2442 product-resolved enzyme evidence with target locus/domain/native-granule evidence. GPo1 native turnover is independently supported, but its precise purified-enzyme product distribution has not been established by that genetic work.

GO0004806 includes triacylglycerol hydrolysis without a chain-length threshold. The measured overnight tributyrin reaction is retained as a biochemical observation; absence of physiological relevance and the very weak assay signal place the annotation in the repository's uncertain-relevance activity tier, not its genuine secondary-function tier.

## PHA synthase phylogenetic scope

The frozen official PTHR36837 reference tree places five SF5 leaves below PTN002198309: Q89M33, Q89NV2, Q9KLQ4, G3XCV5 and G3XCZ9. New official UniProt JSON snapshots independently identify PhaC_N (IPR010941), PHA/PHB_synthase (IPR051321), and class-I (IPR010963) or class-II (IPR011287) signatures in every one of these five proteins. The first three are class I and the last two class II. This supports a common PHA-polymerization/acyl-transfer role, not identical chain-length specificity, partner independence or physiological flux.

The named experimentally grounded SF5 representatives P23608, P26494/P26496 and Q88D25/Q88D23 independently anchor the common reaction. The resulting SF5 scope is a phylogenetic/domain-supported functional inference, not five new direct assays. SF4's submitter-named phaZ is not a proven hydrolase exception. Other family branches remain unresolved. No PANTHER MSA was available in the frozen acquisition; the checked MSA endpoint returned404 (msa-access.json). No catalytic residue loss, alignment-derived specificity boundary or reconstructed ancestral loss is claimed.

## BioH versus PhaG

Normally fetched PMID36689471 now supplies full primary evidence. Its PAO1 PA0502/BioH deletion and complementation, with biotin rescue in defined medium, establish a dedicated native BioH requirement under the tested conditions. This argues against assigning the BioH physiological pathway role to PhaG by the broad ancestral node. It does not directly assay PhaG methyl-ester hydrolysis and does not establish an absolute secondary-activity negative.
