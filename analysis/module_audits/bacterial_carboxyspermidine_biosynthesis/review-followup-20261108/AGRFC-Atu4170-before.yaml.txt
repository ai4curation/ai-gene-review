description: Atu4170 is a carboxyspermidine dehydrogenase that reductively condenses putrescine with L-aspartate
  4-semialdehyde to form carboxyspermidine, the first intermediate in an alternative spermidine biosynthetic
  pathway. The adjacent NspC-family decarboxylase converts this intermediate to spermidine. Native C58
  deletion and metabolite analysis establish the spermidine pathway. The enzyme pair also produces spermine
  and thermospermine through carboxylated intermediates in heterologous E. coli reconstruction. In C58,
  replacing putrescine supply with 1,3-diaminopropane or cadaverine permits synthesis of norspermidine
  or aminopropylcadaverine. Additional tetraamine products are detected when homospermidine or norspermidine
  is supplied to the reconstructed pathway; these activities do not establish the predominant substrates
  in the native bacterium.
