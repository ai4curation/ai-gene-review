description: >-
  NADPH-dependent carboxypolyamine dehydrogenase characterized as BfCASDH. The 397-residue protein sequence
  is identical to the assayed RefSeq WP_005801052.1. Purified enzyme condenses aspartate semialdehyde
  with putrescine or 1,3-diaminopropane, favoring putrescine by approximately 5.6-fold in measured catalytic
  efficiency. It forms a dimer, binds NADP(H), and exhibits cooperative substrate behavior. Native substrate
  use relative to agmatine and the contribution to cellular spermidine production remain untested.
