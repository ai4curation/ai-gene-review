## Important 1: core synthesis and electron-transfer meaning

The six ACCEPT rows describe central catalysis, respiratory electron entry or functional assembly. They are retained, and the core synthesis now explicitly includes the five distinct claims: GlpD electron transfer, aerobic respiration and homodimerization; GlpK phosphorylation and self-association (two original rows). Functional dimers/tetramers are not peripheral merely because their role is assembly rather than catalysis. The primary GlpK source states: “The enzyme exists in solution as functional dimers that associate reversibly to form tetramers.” PMID:18296637 supports a physiologically relevant GlpD homodimer and protection of extensive hydrophobic surfaces. The core distinguishes electron-coupling from a separate diffusible-carrier function, and aerobic participation from universal obligacy or exclusion of nitrate respiration.

The reviewer's carrier-only reading of GO:0009055 is incompatible with the current official definition. The [QuickGO record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/ontology/go/terms/GO:0009055) explicitly includes “numerous enzymatic reactions involved in metabolic pathways.” Purified GlpD acceptor transfer in PMID:340460 and FAD/ubiquinone chemistry in PMID:18296637 supply the biological evidence. The [GO:0004368 ancestor query](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/ontology/go/terms/GO:0004368/ancestors?relations=is_a,part_of) does not contain GO:0009055; a hierarchical MODIFY between these terms would be inaccurate. Snapshot responses, definitions and exact URLs are in ontology-evidence.json and the GlpD source-analysis note. No supported narrower phosphorylation-process or self-binding MF was invented to force a refinement; restricting GlpK self-association to homodimerization would omit regulatory tetramers.

## Important 2: mercury and DNA responses require different evidence judgments

**Mercury: UNDECIDED retained.** Current [GO:0071288](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/ontology/go/terms/GO:0071288) describes a mercury-induced change in cell state or activity. Channel inhibition alone does not demonstrate work by GlpF in such a response. Nevertheless, the specific GlpF mercury experiment underlying PMID:8584435 IDA remains unavailable: PubMed and the [publisher landing page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02207279) expose the abstract, which documents GlpF oocyte glycerol transport and phloretin sensitivity but not that mercury experiment. Independently curated mercury inhibition derives from PMID:7512955. We do not replace the inaccessible target experiment with another paper or infer a categorical source defect from the title/abstract. Heterologous expression alone does not invalidate molecular or transport evidence; no adaptive native mercury-response function is asserted. The possibility remains unresolved, rather than demonstrated true or false.

**DNA response: UNDECIDED→MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED.** Full publisher Table 1 of PMID:11967071 explicitly contains `b3926 | glpK | Glycerol kinase | U3 | 2`. That is real expression evidence, not a wrong-gene citation. However, an induced transcript does not show that the encoded glycerol kinase executes a chemical, structural or regulatory step in DNA-damage response. Current [GO:0006974](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/services/ontology/go/terms/GO:0006974) includes expression changes, but that does not equate the regulated entity with the machinery executing the response. The accessible experiment supports a bounded over-annotation judgment, not proof that every secondary role is absent. A PROBABLE source-defect CHECK requests reassessment without denying the IEP observation, changing its evidence code or demanding deletion as a confirmed misattribution. The Table 1 entry now replaces the former editorial self-quotation.

UNDECIDED is not reserved exclusively for inaccessible papers: the enum also covers unclear biology. This action change follows the accessible expression-only experiment and the participation judgment, not the reviewer's narrower interpretation of the enum. Mercury and DNA response are deliberately not treated as mechanically equivalent cases.
