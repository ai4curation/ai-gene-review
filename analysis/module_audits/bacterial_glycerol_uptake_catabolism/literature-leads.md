# Glycerol comparative audit literature leads

2026-11-07 UTC. Prior module PR5936 and gene PRs3871/2321 merged; this is a dedicated comparative audit, not adoption of those completed PRs.

- PMID30883020 (Poblete-Castro et al.): Pseudomonas glycerol utilization review, PMC6922529. Distinguish phosphorylating GlpK/GlpD from GldA/DhaKLM and anaerobic GlpABC; do not treat GlpD as an oxygen-reacting oxidase.
- PMID31069487: review of glycerol metabolism/regulation in lactic acid bacteria. GlpO/O2/H2O2 is a different electron-acceptor implementation, not interchangeable with quinone GlpD.
- PMID28961963: Mycoplasma glycerol/virulence review, DOI10.1093/femsre/fux033; use as a comparator and follow primary oxidase work.
- PMID825019: Lin1976 broad review; no abstract, publisher paywall. Do not claim it was read unless full text becomes available.
- PMID2127799: Bacillus glpK/glpD sequencing/genetic complementation; primary full text needed for strains/assays.
- DOI10.1099/00221287-139-2-349: Bacillus glpF/glpP1993, publisher abstract available; glpF upstream glpK and G3P/GlpP antitermination context. Resolve PMID normally.
- PMID25827416: existing KT2440 primary source; re-read direct evidence versus pathway attribution.

Official UniProt search identifies BACSU168 GlpF P18156, GlpK P18157, GlpD P18158. Fetch through standard seeding. These will extend the two gammaproteobacterial systems to Bacillota; assess activity and family placement independently.
