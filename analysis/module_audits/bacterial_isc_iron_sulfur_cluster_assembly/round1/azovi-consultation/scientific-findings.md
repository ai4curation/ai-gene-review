## IscA phenotype and localization

PMID16936042 full Results/Figure5B was inspected at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1636278/ on2026-11-07. It reports “a small but reproducible effect on aconitase activity” upon IscA depletion. This supports a modest client-maturation phenotype, not an aconitase defect comparable to IscS/U depletion. The oxygen-dependent growth and conserved-cysteine findings support a housekeeping IscA contribution without resolving its chemical ligand/delivery mechanism. IscA is distinct from NifIscA.

The broad intracellular/cytoplasmic inference is compatible with the verified housekeeping locus and native ISC function. The round1 evidence does not independently establish the finer cytosol assignment or recover the TreeGrafter target leaf. Cytoplasm is retained as ACCEPT and cytosol is UNDECIDED for this specificity uncertainty. No other compartment is asserted. This does not impose fractionation as the only valid evidence type: a sufficient sequence/topology or comparative localization analysis could support refinement, but none is claimed here. The prior absence-of-targeting statement and the mere presence of a second annotation are not independent evidence.

## HscA/B: the measured endpoint matters

PMID16964969's target-organism experiment measures cluster transfer with CD/EPR and concomitant phosphate production. Its native-system evidence supports HscA ATP hydrolysis, HscB activation/interaction and participation in cluster maturation. It does not directly assay recovery of a tertiary fold. The original full-text consultation and accessible primary abstract specify apoferredoxin as cluster recipient, not electron donor. Fresh PMC access exposed the abstract/intro but later full-text retrieval was challenged; EuropePMC XML attempts returned500. No new uninspected method is asserted.

PMID22782893 full cached E. coli results favor disordered IscU with HscA and structured IscU with HscB. The holdase interpretation is a positive state-stabilization result, not proof that every AZOVI folding endpoint is impossible. PMID9852006's E. coli antiaggregation and model-substrate evidence is comparative; it is not an AZOVI luciferase assay. Accordingly the two AZOVI protein-folding rows retain UNDECIDED with these boundaries. Neither description asserts that folding is absent.

GO0051087 explicitly allows binding to a chaperone ensuring correct folding **or transport**. Target HscB's HAMAP interaction statement and the native HscA/HscB transfer system support this partnership without requiring an independent AZOVI foldase claim. The HscB oligomerization row remains MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED: the InterPro HscB_C fold name and functional transient interaction do not establish that HscB promotes oligomer formation. This is mapping specificity overreach, not a claim of measured negative oligomerization.
