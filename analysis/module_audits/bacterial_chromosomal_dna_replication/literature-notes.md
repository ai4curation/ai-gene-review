# Chromosomal replication comparative audit — research in progress

The starting model has 24 named exemplars but only nine local gene reviews. The
frozen initial inventory contains 77 rows and records every existing raw/provider
hash before edits. Fifteen original exemplars require genuine fetch-gene seeds.

The bacterial boundary cannot require the E. coli alpha–epsilon implementation
universally. The 2026 comparison (PMID:42133467;
https://doi.org/10.1093/femsre/fuag021) distinguishes the Bacillus PolC/DnaE
arrangement, accessory clamp-loader components and initiation machinery. The
review of helicase loading (PMID:36877032) distinguishes DnaC, DnaI and DciA.
These are structural alternatives to evaluate, not interchangeable gene names.

The expanded polymerase survey (PMID:41728957;
https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag133) is a contemporary primary comparative analysis.
Intrinsic PHP proofreading is directly tested in mycobacteria (PMID:25894501),
while the later DnaQ study (PMID:39141351) requires avoiding an absolute claim
that their separate DnaQ contributes nothing. Exact assay species and proteins
must remain distinct. The root is reviewing MYCTU DnaE1; its presence alone is
not a whole-pathway exemplar.

Before editing the module, resolve the boundary between replisome synthesis and
completed chromosome maturation: the original includes ligation but excludes
primer removal and gap filling. Do not imply continuous DNA from RNA primers
without explaining or modeling maturation. Topological management, termination
and restart also require explicit scope decisions. Existing specialized modules
should be reused where appropriate, not copied wholesale.

Literature retrieval currently encounters NCBI HTTP429 for several sources.
Read accessible primary/publisher text and retry normal caching after the seed
wave. Never manufacture publication caches or provider output.
