# Independent capacity and evidence consultation

The COMPLETE module supports bounded, assembled Lol trafficking capacity. It does not prove measured native flux, universal diderm essentiality, or unrestricted family-wide activity. The source-qualified production-engine cases pass; no additional scientific edit is requested.

## What the production engine actually does

`check_capacity.py` invokes the repository's `compile_node`, `iter_atoms`, `core_atoms`, and `is_satisfied`. It evaluates the actual module document, including mutations of that document. Seven compiled atoms produce six formal extraction/delivery combinations. The intersection of atom identities is empty because both stages have alternative realizers; this does not mean that extraction or delivery is optional.

The compiler does not expand `PROTEIN_COMPLEX.active_units`. The external predicate reads those units directly, matches exact reviewed exemplars and official family classifications, requires the corresponding positive subunit role and curated contribution to GO:0140306, and checks a cognate assembly. It also verifies ATP hydrolysis in the ATPase core. Stoichiometry denotes copies of a polypeptide, so LolF2 uses one gene product twice, not two distinct F genes. A0ACM8PZZ0 and Q9EYM3 are independently verified identical-sequence labels for this same native ADP1 product.

Insertion remains a proposed molecular function with no GO ID. The predicate checks the actual proposed core function; the Boolean atom's empty `function_ids` does not mean that any family member supplies insertion activity. No activity is read from GOA as evidence. Core assertions, their actual quotations, raw membership sources and released identity proofs form the independent input closure.

Compatibility is another external gate. A native panel label is not a genotype: member accessions must actually be present, and their reviewed taxa are checked explicitly. Two individually supported proteins from different panels are not assumed compatible. This conservative absence of licensing is not evidence that every such pair is biologically incapable. FLAJ1/E. coli A/B noninterchangeability has direct experimental support. A carrier/insertase pair from FLAJ1 plus an E. coli extractor does not become a supported complete native panel.

## Evidence tiers and boundary decisions

- **ECOLI:** canonical C1/E1/D2 extraction and split LolA/LolB delivery are supported by target-source studies. The module is a capacity model, not an expression or substrate-flux measurement. C and E contribute distinct cargo/recruitment work to the complete release complex, rather than being two independently active complete release enzymes.
- **PSEPK KT2440:** the five matched orthologs support an inferred complete panel. The E. coli mechanistic experiments are not reassigned to KT2440. Shared PTHR30489:SF0 does not distinguish PP_2154 LolC from PP_2156 LolE; locus identity and role evidence do. No native LolC dispensability is inferred from the E. coli purified DE control.
- **ACIAD ADP1:** native LolDF purification/structural work and controlled ATPase experiments in PMID42736309 support F2D2. Defined detergent single-cycle BamE transfer supports LolA involvement; the cognate LolB terminal step is an orthology-based inference, not an assay in that paper. Only one engaged LolA was observed. No claim follows about every acylation state, all charged +3 clients, or complete native route flux. Raw LolD's stale C/E-subunit sentence is explicitly adjudicated in the released review.
- **FLAJ1:** A5FI22/Fjoh_2111 LolA1 and A5FL25/Fjoh_1066 DUF4292 LolB1 support native, client-selective delivery. Neither a complete reviewed extractor nor universal client coverage is supplied. A5FKZ5/Fjoh_1085 LolA2 is a real SF1 member in the pigment-associated context, whose lipoprotein-carrier function remains unestablished. It is excluded by positive activity licensing, not assigned a demonstrated loss.
- **CB15:** both carrier and insertion functions must occur in the same Q9A261 polypeptide. Native bifunctionality is inferred from engineered heterologous experiments. PMID36716372 replaced its final 12 residues with E. coli LolA sequence and tested permissive E. coli backgrounds; the native protein complemented poorly. A source-scoped engineered profile is positive, while native CB15 delivery alone does not prove a complete native CB15 pathway.

## Positive biological controls outside the canonical profile

PMID17509078 reports purified LolD/LolE release reconstitution without LolC under its phospholipid/reconstitution conditions. The case reports this positive release result separately from failure to instantiate the canonical native CDE plus terminal delivery profile. The abstract explicitly states the result; this is not a negative-annotation judgment drawn from missing full text.

PMID28416660 full text reports a `cpxA24 Δlpp ΔrcsB ΔlolA ΔlolB` background and an alternative trafficking route, while LolCDE remain required in that context. The primary full text was read from the independently captured PMC response documented by the ECOLI consultant; the immutable normal publication cache remains abstract-only. Lpp deletion alone is not sufficient. The positive bypass control is deliberately outside the modeled carrier/inserter route; a negative canonical predicate does not deny this biology or invent the unidentified bypass machinery.

## Checks and sensitivity

The 32 cases cover the three complete panels, each missing ATPase/carrier/insertase, distinct C/E roles, the F homodimer/alias issue, isolated components, unlicensed pigment-family membership, mismatched partners, incomplete FLAJ1 and CB15 panels, the engineered CB15 route, and two external biological controls. Five actual module mutations are detected: deleting D, making delivery optional, forcing CDE, replacing E's selector with C, and splitting bifunctional activities onto different proteins.

Five sensitivity tests remove assembly, delivery compatibility, extractor compatibility, construct scope, or activity licensing. Each produces an observable false positive relative to the qualified predicate. The activity test deliberately masks a real carrier's curated activity while preserving its identity and family; it is a methodological perturbation, not a claim that the carrier is inactive.

Source closure verifies every released consultation manifest, all 17 COMPLETE member reviews and their taxonomic identities, official identity records, and all 178 distinct in-scope review/module quotations plus eight boundary excerpts using repository publication/local-source matchers without baseline exemptions. The source correction for PMID25583975 (an E. coli inhibitor paper, not Francisella LolF evidence) was reported to root and fixed before the final module freeze. Actual Francisella PMID25755189 is separate supporting context; direct ADP1 evidence grounds the represented F2D2 complex.
