# Module integration decisions

The rewritten topology contains two required capacities, extraction and delivery,
while preserving the original explicit components and source IDs. There are
seven compiled atoms and six formal combinations. `draft-logic.json` records the
compiler view; it is not the biological oracle.

- Existing LolCDE active units and established PAINT node references are retained.
  New LolDF units use the exact ADP1 identity bridge and distinct ATPase family.
  No imaginary PTN is attached to newly named external members.
- C/E/F function descriptors name their coupled subfunctions without falsely
  assigning independent recognition/release; whole complex annotons retain
  GO0140306. D retains ATP hydrolysis.
- The separate delivery route has a supported carrier and an insertion-family
  realizer. Names and exact members qualify family hits. The bifunctional route
  requires both capabilities on Q9A261; this is not evidence of two proteins.
- ECOLI, PSEPK and ACIAD are complete matched member panels with unequal assay
  coverage. FLAJ1 and CB15 remain partial; gene review completion does not change
  that boundary.
- LolDE reconstitution, engineered LolAB bypass, and FLAJ1 residual delivery are
  positive conditional observations. They neither make canonical roles optional
  by default nor justify universal biological-negative predictions.
- Original evidence source IDs are retained. Overbroad original essentiality,
  family coherence and provider no-gap statements are corrected scientifically.
  Original source bytes and external frozen gene/family releases are not edited.
- Three selected reviews are manually assessed and scoped. Unsupported discovery
  leads and the Opinion paper are excluded from the final reference evidence.

All 17 representative gene reviews have now been frozen and released, including
Fjoh_2111. The module curation is COMPLETE; this is not a claim of complete
experimental assay coverage. Biological and comparative-coverage gaps remain
explicitly machine-readable. The family member-index refresh is parent-owned.

The production compiler represents each complex as one atom and does not expand
active_units. Its six structural combinations are not six independently
established biological routes. The capacity oracle must verify all cognate
active units, exact roles, same-cell interfaces and identity of the protein
providing both bifunctional roles. Gene coverage is distinct from direct native
assay coverage.
