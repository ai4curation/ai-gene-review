# CbcWV round-1 primary evidence

Read 2026-11-07. Existing publication caches remain unchanged. The full cached
PMID19919675 establishes the PAO1 cloning origin of pNcbcXWV and its cbcX-deleted
pNcbcWV derivative, and radiolabeled choline/betaine/carnitine accumulation in
transport-deficient hosts. Table 5 separates binding-protein specificity from
core-mediated translocation; core-only MKH13 and empty-vector controls do not
establish uptake. Native P. aeruginosa mutant backgrounds are PA14, not PAO1.

## COS: PMID23763788, Results and Figure 5

Full publisher text accessed through web retrieval:
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/tpj.12262

Exact result excerpt:

> following the introduction of pNCbcWV (vector 2), BTbetX::tet could import l-carnitine, its analog acetylcarnitine, and COS

Figure 5 compares B728a BT derivatives lacking betX, caiX, or cosX, with empty
pN, core-only pNCbcWV, or pNCbcXWV. COS-dependent QAC-uidA induction requires
the core and compatible CosX. This reporter readout supports cellular entry,
not a purified COS flux measurement. The core's PAO1 origin is established by
PMID19919675; the chromosomal binding proteins and reporter host are B728a.
This supports conditional organic sulfur-cargo transport, not inorganic sulfate
transport or a native PAO1 ecological role.

## Osmolarity: PMID21478341, Results and Figure 6

Full publisher text accessed through web retrieval:
https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jb.00160-11

Exact hyperosmolar-growth excerpt:

> the wild type and ΔbetT1 and ΔcbcV mutants all had similar growth kinetics

PA14 mutant growth was compared in low-, physiological- and high-osmolarity
media. CbcV loss strongly impaired low-osmolarity choline nutrition, whereas
BetT3 dominated high-osmolarity nutrition. Supplementary Fig. S3B described
retained choline osmoprotection in the triple transporter mutant. These
comparisons do not isolate a PAO1 CbcV stress-response function or rule one out.
The PAO1 cbcXWV plasmid is a complementation construct, not the native PA14
chromosomal allele. The cached abstract and externally consulted full text
remain separate source-access records.
