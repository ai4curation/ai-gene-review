# PMID:21219457: transport and strain evidence

Source: [publisher article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/j.1365-2958.2010.07448.x), Results, first transport subsection; retrieved 2026-11-07 through indexed publisher text. These are bounded excerpts with separate curator interpretation, not a replacement publication cache.

At line 229, the reported substrate concentration and mutant/parent rates are:

> uptake of 10 µM GA

> 4.2 nmol min^{−1}

> 7.4 nmol min^{−1}

The first rate belongs to KTGAL lacking galP; the second belongs to its KTGAL parent. Both rates are normalized per mg protein. Growth on gallate was unaffected. This supports a contribution to uptake under the tested conditions, without proving selective transport by isolated GalP.

At line 190, the sequence comparison and adaptation mechanism include:

> identical to that of the wild-type strain

> suppression of a frameshift mutation

The authors identify deletion of cytosine 2867088 as the sole cluster difference between KTGAL and KT2440, restoring GalT. This supplies the strain bridge for the other gal genes; it does not show native gallate uptake by unadapted KT2440.
