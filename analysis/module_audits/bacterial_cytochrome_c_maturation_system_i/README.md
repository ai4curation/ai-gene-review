# Bacterial cytochrome c maturation System I audit

This audit models two intact bacterial architectures: canonical histidine-linked CcmE with CcmG-supported CcmFH maturation, and the experimentally reconstructed G20 cysteine-CcmE System I* cassette. Linked chemistry and terminal machinery remain together, so a mixture of incompatible pieces does not satisfy a route. Bacteria is the scope boundary, not a claim that all bacteria use System I. Archaeal and mitochondrial systems, heme biosynthesis, Sec export and upstream DsbD/CcdA electron supply are outside the modeled machinery.

The module contains 24 curated references, 55 findings and 16 annotons with direct step evidence. Comparative reviews PMID21958041,24631867 and40460019 were evaluated alongside primary loading, release, structural, redox, ligation and System I* studies. The 2025 review is abstract-only and does not substantiate detailed claims beyond its accessible text. PMID18786143 establishes thiol-independent CcmG handling and redox bypass in altered Rhodobacter backgrounds; PMID24397552 supplies engineered CcmFGH-only functional evidence, without making those synthetic conditions an ordinary native route.

All 29 representative gene reviews are COMPLETE: 8 ECOLI, 9 PSEPK, 7 OLEA2, 4 RHOCB and 1 SHEON. The first three are complete curated route panels; the last two provide selected roles only. The frozen baseline retains 227 historical rows (224 machine rows and 3 inherited NEW), and 6 new proposals yield 233 current rows. All 58 raw UniProt/GOA files and 6 pre-existing provider artifacts are preserved. There were 29 genuine Falcon attempts, all blocked by provider authentication; manually sourced evidence is kept in notes and source analyses, never mislabeled as generated provider research.

Seven existing family reviews were refined while preserving 12 original PAINT node identities, seed sets, metadata and verdicts and all original term-scope verdicts. CcmE-family membership does not imply a histidine ligand; CcmF does not universally require CcmH. PTHR47870 SF1 contains both redox CcmH and TPR accessory CcmI. G20 uses an alternate CcmA PANTHER family and an alternate small CcmD family. Its CcmI descriptor retains the verified protein and TPR intent without claiming a specific functional PANTHER identity from its Cdc27-dominated family name. No new ancestral node is invented.

The 151 capacity controls use production module logic with an independently curated role, family, chemistry, strain and reviewed-core predicate. They exercise every required role, optional I, loss of chemistry/support, mixed strains, incompatible CcmE variants and false CcmI-to-CcmH substitutions. A negative partial-panel result means insufficient reviewed coverage, not absence from a genome. The checker is not a native-flux predictor or an automatic genome-discovery pipeline.

## Evidence limits

- PMID40026579 proposes a CcmB conduit from conditional overproduction/complementation and associated assays; PMID40167305 disputes that model using CcmCD heme-contact mapping. The module preserves supported handling/release roles and the disagreement without choosing a conduit by date. Shewanella excess-CcmB effects are conditional, not a universal native transporter requirement.
- G20 denotes current Oleidesulfovibrio alaskensis G20. The original genomic-DNA donor and reference CDSs are verified; actual full plasmid sequence is unavailable. CcmC paper H68/H192 versus reference H50/H174 differs by 18 residues and is not silently reconciled. CcmB's mechanical role is inferred from architecture and the functional cassette, without an individual deletion assay. CcmI enhances yield; further CcmD deletion eliminates detectable product in that reconstruction. The 2011 erratum's corrected detection limits are retained in source notes.
- G20 CcmA timing is inferred from perturbation, and CcmF contributes to collective ligation rather than being asserted sufficient as an isolated synthase. Other host/native reductants are not excluded. CcmE linkage chemistries are not functionally interchangeable merely because their proteins share a family.
- Rhodobacter tagged/truncated constructs and MT1131-derived backgrounds are distinguished from the frozen SB1003 reference proteins. KT2440 roles are comparative inference; its CcmE H124 linkage assignment combines sequence and HAMAP, not a direct assay. DsbA-null bypasses and engineered CcmFGH-only maturation constrain genetic-essentiality language.
