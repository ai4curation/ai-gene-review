# MnmA comparative boundary decisions — 2026-11-07

The reusable boundary begins with cysteine sulfur mobilization and ends with ATP-dependent tRNA wobble U34 thiolation by MnmA. These are at least two substantive roles, even when a carrier relay is absent. Preserve the existing Tus implementation and its seven target representatives; add experimentally grounded direct-donor realizations. Bacteria is an occurrence envelope, not universal conservation or pathway presence. U8, U54, cytosolic Ncs6/NcsA chemistry, general Fe-S assembly, biotin/Moco sulfur branches and C5 elaboration are outside this module. C5 modification is not necessarily an obligatory downstream step.

The Tus realization has sulfur mobilization, TusA, a TusBCD complex, TusE-assisted delivery and MnmA-catalyzed U34 thiolation. E. coli is the characterized panel; KT2440 is a matched family/locus/active-site inference. TusB/C contribute structural organization rather than an invented autonomous carrier activity. Direct covalent TusE-to-MnmA persulfide loading remains mechanistically unproven, so a delivery connection must not claim that intermediate was directly measured (critical review PMID39660893; source-ledger and full preflight text).

The Bacillus realization uses cognate YrvO (current official iscS1) and MnmA, with the PS832-to-168 derivative bridge stated. PMID25825430 supports purified capacity, ATP-dependent sulfur transfer and native conditional MnmA involvement. Heterologous rescue is separate evidence. Failed cross-species replacements are bounded by tested conditions; they do not prove universal biochemical incompatibility.

The Thermus HB27 realization uses the exact TT_C1727 MnmA and either TT_C0087 or TT_C1373 in purified donor assays. PMID31801798 removed reductants before cysteine-fed assays and supports direct-pair capacity, while PMID16547008 supplies the donor construct/locus bridge. Native donor choice and possible unknown carriers remain unresolved. Reconstituted C-type MnmA requires its cluster under the tested conditions; do not impose this cofactor rule on D-type E. coli/Bacillus enzymes, for which published mechanistic findings disagree. Chemical sulfide rescue alone is not a native sulfur-supply pathway.

Families orient the roles but do not by themselves establish compatibility. E. coli IscS and Bacillus YrvO share PTHR11601:SF34, while C- and D-type MnmAs share PTHR11933:SF5. Broad PTHR43586/SF8 includes alternate PLP-dependent sulfur chemistry. Exact protein identity, supported catalytic role, cognate pairing, substrate, active enzyme state and assay scope must remain explicit independent requirements. Family reviews are being assessed separately; this note does not pre-empt their final scope judgments.

These decisions guide the pending ModuleReview edit and capacity audit. They do not constitute a completed module, a native flux claim or a new annotation.
