# Furin cleaves ANTXR2-bound pagA to yield pagA(197-794)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5210912](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5210912)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Furin or a related protease at the cell surface cleaves ANTXR2-bound pagA (Anthrax Protective Antigen, full-length). The larger cleavage product, pagA(197-794), remains bound to the receptor while a smaller product, pagA(30-196), is released into the extracellular space (Klimpel et al, 1992; Molloy et al. 1992).
