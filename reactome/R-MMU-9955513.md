# Sirt5 desuccinylates Cps1
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9955513](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9955513)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Cps1 is succinylated at multiple sites in vivo (Du et al, 2011; Weinert et al, 2013). Sirt5 desuccinylates Cps1 at residue K1291, activating the enzyme (Du et al, 2011). Consistent with this, Sirt5 knockout mice show elevated levels of succinylated Cps1 and decreased Cps1 enzyme activity (Du et al, 2011).
