# Conversion of Glycerol to Glycerol-3-phosphate
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-75887](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-75887)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Glycerol can be a source for glycerol-3-phosphate, in which case, a phosphate form ATP is transferred to glycerol by glycerol kinase forming glycerol-3-phosphate and ADP.<BR>
