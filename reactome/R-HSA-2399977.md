# AKT1 E17K mutant phosphorylates AKT1S1 (PRAS40)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2399977](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2399977)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

AKT1 E17K gain-of-function mutant is expected to phosphorylate AKT1S1 (PRAS40), like the wild-type AKT (Kovacina et al. 2003), but this has not been experimentally tested.
