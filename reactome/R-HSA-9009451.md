# RUNX2 binds SMURF1 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9009451](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9009451)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

RUNX2, presumably in complex with CBFB, binds RUNX2 binding elements in the promoter of the SMURF1 gene, encoding an E3 ubiquitin ligase SMURF1 (Yang et al. 2014).
