# PRC2 (EZH2) core complex methylates nucleosomes at CDH1 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9764932](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9764932)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In human lung and colon cancer cell lines, increased occupancy of the CDH1 (E-cadherin) gene promoter by components of the PRC2 histone methyltransferase complex upon TGF-beta stimulation correlates with increased levels of the repressive H3K27 mark at the CDH1 gene promoter. The PRC2 complex is known to create H3K27 repressive marks on chromatin. H3K27 methylation at CDH1 promoter-bound nucleosomes is reduced upon siRNA-mediated knockdown of EED2 (Oktyabri et al. 2014). While EZH2 associates with nucleosomes whose histone H3 is methylated on lysine residue 28 (H3K27 mark), and while H3K27 mark is present on the CDH1 promoter in human gastric cancer cells, upregulation of CDH1 upon siRNA-mediated knockdown of EZH2 does not involve change in the DNA-methylation status at the CDH1 gene promoter (Fujii and Ochiai 2008). SNAI2 (Slug) may be involved in the recruitment of PRC2 and PRC2-mediated H3K27 methylation at CDH1 gene promoter (Tien et al. 2015).
