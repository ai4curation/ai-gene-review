# MyD88 forms a complex with the activated TLR receptor
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-975866](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-975866)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MyD88 is the downstream adaptor which is utilized by all TLRs, except TLR3. MyD88 comprises an N-terminal Death Domain (DD) and a C-terminal TIR. Upon ligand binding to the IL-1R or a TLR, MyD88 is rapidly recruited to the activated receptor via homotypic interactions of TIR domain, whereas the DD module recruits serine/threonine kinases such as interleukin-1-receptor-associated kinases (IRAKs).
