# TGFBR3(1-783) binds to TGFB1,TGFB2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9839368](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9839368)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Soluble fragments of TGFBR3(1-783) genearted by MMPs maintain their TGFB1/TGFB2 binding ability. It binds with TGFB1/TGBF2 and sequester TGFB ligands (TGFB1/TGBF2) away from TGFBR2 receptor leading to suppression of TGFbeta signaling (Lopez-Casillas et al., 1994).
