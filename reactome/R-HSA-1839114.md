# BCR-FGFR1:GRB2:p-GAB2 binds PIK3R1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1839114](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1839114)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Based on analogy with studies of the BCR-ABL fusion, phosphorylated GAB2 recruits the regulatory subunit of PI3K to the BCR-FGFR1 fusion (Sattler, 2002; Demiroglu, 2001; Roumiantsev, 2004).
