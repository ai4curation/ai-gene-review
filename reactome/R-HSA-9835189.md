# NCK1 binds PKR
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9835189](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9835189)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NCK1 binds to inactive protein kinase R (PKR, EIF2AK2) and prevents its activation, limiting the signaling induced by dsRNA. The mechanism of this process is unclear, however (Cardin & Larose, 2008).
