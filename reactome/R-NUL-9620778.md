# Cdkn1b gene expression is stimulated by FOXO1,FOXO3
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9620778](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9620778)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

Transcription from the rat Cdkn1b (p27Kip1) gene promoter is stimulated by recombinant human FOXO1 or recombinant human  FOXO3 (Lees et all. 2008).
