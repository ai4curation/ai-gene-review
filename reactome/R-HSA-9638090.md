# LARGE1,2 elongates matriglycan chain with Xyl
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9638090](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9638090)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Xylosyl- and glucuronyltransferase 1 (LARGE1) and 2 (LARGE2) are possible subunits of the B4GAT1:LARGE complex. They transfer a xylosyl moiety from UDP-Xyl to the terminal GlcA moiety of growing matriglycan chains on alpha-dystroglycan (Inamori et al., 2012, Inamori et al., 2013). Mutations in LARGE1 can cause muscular dystrophy-dystroglycanopathies (MDDGB6, MIM:608840, Longman et al., 2003).
