# AcK-NFE2L2 binds the SOD3 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796049](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796049)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SOD3 is under transcriptional control of NFE2L2 and contains a functional antioxidant response element confirmed by ChIP assay in MCF-10A Cells  (Singh et al, 2012, Folz et al, 1994)
