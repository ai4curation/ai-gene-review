# Phosphorylated STAT5 dimer binds the IL4R gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9978250](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9978250)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylated STAT5 dimers resulting from IL2 signaling bind the first intron of the IL4R gene, which encodes the alpha subunit of the IL4 receptor (Liao et al. 2008).
