# NFkB binds the NFE2L2 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796056](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796056)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

High Levels of nuclear  NFE2L2 expressions are regulated by high levels of nuclear NFkB in AML cells. The presence of the κB2 site, located at +270 upstream of the transcription start site, is responsible for the increased expression of  NFE2L2 seen in AML cells. (Rushworth et al, 2012)
