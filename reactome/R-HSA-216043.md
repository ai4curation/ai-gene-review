# Collagen type II binds integrin alpha10beta1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-216043](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-216043)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The integrin alpha10beta1 is a collagen type II-binding integrin on chondrocytes. This integrin is of great importance during chondrogenesis. alpha10 binds the collagen-II with its I domain displaying similar binding properties as alpha1 I domain.
