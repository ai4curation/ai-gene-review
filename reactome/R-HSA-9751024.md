# SLC29A1,2 transport 6MP from extracellular region to cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9751024](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9751024)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Candidates for the transport of thiopurines like 6-mercaptopurine (6MP) into the cell include the transporters SLC29A1 (equilibrative nucleoside transporter 1, ENT1) and SLC29A2 (equilibrative nucleoside transporter 2, ENT2). Down-regulation of these influx transporters may play a major role in 6MP resistance of cells (Fotoohi et al. 2006, Peng et al. 2008, Karim et al. 2011).
