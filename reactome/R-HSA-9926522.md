# MITF-M-dependent MCM2 gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926522](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926522)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MCM2, a component of the MCM2-7 replicative helicase complex, has been identified as a direct target of MITF-dependent expression (Strub et al, 2011). MITF-M binds to an E-box element in the 5' untranslated region (UTR) and expression of MCM2 is repressed upon treatment of cells with siRNA against MITF (Stub et al, 2011).
