# KIT gene expression is stimulated by TFAP2A,(TFAP2B) homodimers
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8864698](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8864698)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of AP-2 alpha (TFAP2A) dimer to the KIT gene promoter stimulates KIT transcription. AP-2 beta (TFAP2B) may also stimulate KIT transcription. TFAP2A and KIT levels are frequently reduced in metastatic melanoma (Huang et al. 1998).
