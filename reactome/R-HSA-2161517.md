# Abacavir transmembrane transport
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2161517](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2161517)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cytosolic levels of abacavir are determined by the balance of its facilitated diffusion into the cell mediated by organic cation transporters SLC22A1, 2, and 3, and its ATP-dependent efflux from cells mediated by ABCG2 and ABCB1 (Klaasen and Aleksunes 2010; Pan et al. 2007; Shaik et al. 2007).
