# MITF-M binds CREBBP,EP300
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926478](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926478)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M interacts with CREBBP and EP300 to activate expression at target genes (Sato et al, 1997; Brown et al, 2023 a,b). Interaction is mediated by two transactivation domains (TAD) in MITF-M, one at the N-terminal and one in the disordered C-terminal region ((Sato et al, 1997; Brown et al, 2023 a,b). Both regions of MITF-M interact with the TAZ1 and TAZ2 domains of the histone acetyltransferases (Brown et al, 2023 a,b). Some studies have shown that the interaction between MITF and CREBBP and EP300 is enhanced by MAPK1-dependent phosphorylation of MITF-M at serine 73, however other studies have not reproduced this effect (Price et al, 1998; Xu et al, 2000; Sato et al, 1997)
