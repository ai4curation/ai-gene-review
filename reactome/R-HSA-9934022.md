# DPF2, PHF10 bind SMARCC1:SMARCC2 dimer:SMARD1:ARID1A
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9934022](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9934022)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Based on the purification of the mouse complex, esBAF appears to contain either DPF2 or PHF10 (Ho, Jothi et al, 2009, Ho, Ronan et al, 2009; reviewed in Kadoch and Crabtree, 2015; Innis and Cabot, 2020; Cenik and Shilatifard, 2021).
