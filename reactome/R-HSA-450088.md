# GLUT1 tetramer binds 4xATP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-450088](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-450088)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cytosolic ATP reversibly associates with GLUT1. This association inhibits GLUT1 glucose transport (Blodgett et al. 2007).
