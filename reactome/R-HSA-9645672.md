# p14ARF translocates to the nucleus
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9645672](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9645672)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

p14ARF is mainly localized inside the nucleus, specifically the nucleolus (Zhang and Xiong 1999, Lindstrom et al. 2000), similar to its mouse orthologue p19ARF (Tao and Levine 1999).
