# IGFBP2 binds IGF forming IGF:IGFBP2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-381412](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-381412)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

IGFBP 2 binds IGF I or IGF II via the conserved N terminus and C terminus of IGFBP 2.
IGFBP 2 is enriched in cerebrospinal fluid and inhibits IGF function. IGFBP 2 is not significantly degraded in circulation.
