# COS binds to SMO
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-216222](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-216222)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

SMO:COS:FU:CI complexes are present prior to Hedgehog signalling owing to direct binding of Smoothened (SMO) to both Coastal2 (COS) and the Ser/Thr kinase Fused (FU).
