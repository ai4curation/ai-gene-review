# AdoSeMet is converted to AdeSeHCys by MetTrans(2)
**Reactome ID:** [R-RNO-2408545](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-RNO-2408545)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

In the cytosol of rat, a set of methyltransferases (MetTrans(2)) consisting of the proteins histamine-N-methyltransferase (Hnmt), glycine-N-methyltransferase (Gnmt), and nicotinamide-N-methyltransferase (Nnmt) remove a methyl group from adenosylselenomethionine (AdoSeMet) to leave adenosylselenohomocysteine (AdeSeHCys) (Kajander et al. 1991).
