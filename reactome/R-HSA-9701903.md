# ALK-, STAT3- and MECP2-dependent STAT5A gene repression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9701903](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9701903)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

STAT5A expression is repressed in NPM-ALK T-cell lymphomas in a manner that depends on STAT3 and MeCP2. STAT3 activation downstream of NPM-ALK stimulates STAT5A promoter hypermethylation, providing binding sites for MeCP2 and resulting in the repression of STAT5 expression (Zhang et al, 2007; Zhang et al, 2002). In turn, STAT5 has been shown to have binding sites at two places in the NPM-ALK fusion gene: an enhancer element located upstream of the endogenous NPM gene, and in intron 14 of the ALK gene.  STAT5A has been shown to bind to these elements by electrophoretic mobility shift assay (EMSA) and by co-immunoprecipitation (Zhang et al, 2007).
