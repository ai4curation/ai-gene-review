# TFAP2B homodimer binds YEATS4
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8864595](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8864595)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TFAP2B (AP-2 beta) binds YEATS4 (GAS41). Formation of the TFAP2B:YEATS4 complex increases DNA binding and transcriptional activity of TFAP2B homodimers (Ding et al. 2006).
