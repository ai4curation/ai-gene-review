# Tbx21 positively regulates expression of the C-X-C chemokine type 3 receptor (Cxcr3).
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9943794](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9943794)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Tbx21 directly upregulates expression of the chemokine receptor Cxcr3 in T helper 1 cells (Th1 cells) (Szabo et al. 2000, Lord et al. 2005, Zhu et al. 2012, Gökmen et al.2013).
