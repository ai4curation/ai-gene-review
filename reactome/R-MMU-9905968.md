# CaV3.2 (Cacna1h:Cacna2d1:Cacnb1,2,3:Cacng7) transports calcium from the extracellular region to the cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9905968](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9905968)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Cacna2d1, the regulatory subunit of voltage-gated calcium channels (VGCCs), normally associates with L-type VGCCs, however, in osteocytes Cacna2d1 associates with the T-type VGCC Cacna1h (CaV3.2) and is required for the opening of Cacna1h in response to membrane stretch (Thompson et al. 2011). Cacna2d1 also directly interacts with perlecan (heparan sulfated Hspg2) of the extracellular matrix (Reyes Fernandez et al. 2022). Mechanical force on perlecan thus regulates the calcium permeability of the CaV3.2 channels (Pei et al. 2020).<br>The opening of Cacna1h channels is required for the release of ATP by osteocytes in response to membrane stretch (Thompson et al. 2011). Loss of Cacna2d1 causes loss of skeletal strength in males and females and loss of response to skeletal loading in males (Kelly et al. 2024).
