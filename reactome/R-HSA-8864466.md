# TFAP2A,(TFAP2B,TFAP2C) homo- and heterodimers and YY1 stimulate ERBB2 gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8864466](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8864466)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of AP-2 transcription factors TFAP2A (AP-2 alpha), TFAP2B (AP-2 beta) and TFAP2C (AP-2 gamma) to AP-2 response elements in the ERBB2 gene promoter stimulates ERBB2 transcription, with TFAP2B having the weakest effect (Bosher et al. 1996). Association of YY1 transcription factor with TFAP2A (and, possibly, TFAP2B and TFAP2C) significantly increases the ERBB2 gene transcription rate (Begon et al. 2005).
