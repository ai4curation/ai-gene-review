# Expression of TFAP2C in primordial germ cells
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9827867](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9827867)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

BMP4 signaling initiates expression of transcription factor AP-2 gamma (TFAP2C) (Irie et al. 2015, Sasaki et al. 2015, Fang et al. 2020, Tang et al. 2022) independently of SOX17 (Kojima et al. 2017) in human primordial germ cells (PGCs) differentiated in vitro. BMP signaling activates phosphorylation of SMAD1, SMAD5, and SMAD8, but whether any of these SMADs bind the TFAP2C promoter is not known. SOX17 and TFAP2C maintain expression of TFAP2C in differentiating PGCs (Kojima et al. 2017, Tang et al. 2022).
