# Activated Ntrk3 binds PI3K
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9603412](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9603412)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Recombinant rat Ntrk3 (TrkC), activated by recombinant human NTF3 (NT-3), co-immunoprecipitates with the endogenous mouse PI3K complex composed of Pik3ca and Pik3r1 subunits (Yuen and Mobley 1999). It is not known whether the interaction between Ntrk3 and PI3K is direct.
