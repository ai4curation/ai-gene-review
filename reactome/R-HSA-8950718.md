# Expression of TCP1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8950718](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8950718)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Experiments using human cord blood CD4(+) T cells show 22 protein spots and 20 protein spots, upregulated and downregulated proteins respectively, following Interleukin-12 stimulation (Rosengren et.al, 2005). Among the proteins that is identified in more than one spot, which probably is due to different isoforms, is T-complex protein 1 subunit alpha (TCP1), one isoform of this protein is upregulated, the other down-regulated.
