# p38MAPK phosphorylates MSK1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-198669](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-198669)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MSK1 (Ribosomal protein S6 kinase alpha-5) is a serine/threonine kinase that is localised in the nucleus. It contains two protein kinase domains in a single polypeptide. It can be activated 5-fold by p38MAPK through phosphorylation at four key residues.<br>
