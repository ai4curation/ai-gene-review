# Arachidonate is hydroxylated to 19-HETE by CYP(2)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2161814](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2161814)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Several cytochrome P450s (CYPs) convert arachidonate to 19-hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acid (19-HETE).  The CYPs and their references are as follows: CYP2C8 (Bylund et al. 1998); CYP2C9 (Bylund et al. 1998); CYP2C19 (Bylund et al. 1998); CYP4A11 (Gainer et al. 2005); CYP2U1 (Chuang et al. 2004); CYP1A1, CYP1A2, CYP1B1 (Choudhary et al. 2004).
