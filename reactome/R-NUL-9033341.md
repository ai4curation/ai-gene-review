# Ntrk2 and Cdk5 promote activation of Rac1 by Tiam1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9033341](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9033341)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In cultured mouse neurons, stimulated by recombinant human BDNF, phosphorylation of mouse Ntrk2 by Cdk5 is needed for BDNF-induced activation of Rac1 by Tiam1 (Lai et al. 2012).
