# CHST10 sulfates matriglycan chain
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9940718](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9940718)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Carbohydrate sulfotransferase 10 (CHST10, HNK-1) transfers sulfate from 3'-phosphoadenylyl sulfate (PAPS) to position 3 of the terminal glucuronic acid (GlcA) moiety of growing matriglycan chains on alpha-dystroglycan. This modfication prevents further polymerization by LARGE glycosyltransferases (Sheikh et al., 2020; reviewed in Katz & Diskin).
