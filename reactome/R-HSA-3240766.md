# E2F1/2/3:DP1/2 binds SUZ12 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3240766](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3240766)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

E2F1, E2F2, E2F3 and E2F4 are all able to bind the promoter of SUZ12 (ChET 9) (Weinmann et al 2001), a subunit of the Polycomb repressor complex 2 (PRC2).
