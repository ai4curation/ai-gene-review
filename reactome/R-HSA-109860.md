# MAP2K1 phosphorylates MAPK3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-109860](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-109860)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MAP2K1 (also known as MEK1) phosphorylates the critical Thr202 and Tyr204 on MAPK3 (ERK1), converting two ATP to ADP. Phosphorylation of MAPK3 activates its kinase activity.<p>MAP2K1 activation requires the phosphorylation of two serine residues (S218 and S222) in the activation loop.
