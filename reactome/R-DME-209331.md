# Formation of the 'scaffold complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209331](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209331)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The kinesin-related protein, Costal2 (COS), forms a microtubule-associated 'scaffold complex' with Cubitus Interruptus (CI) and the Ser/Thr protein kinase Fused (FU).  CI also associates directly with the Supressor of Fused (SU(FU)) protein in a separate complex.  Complexes containing CI, COS, FU and SU(FU) are detectable but rare.  The Ser/Thr protein kinases: Protein kinase A (PKA-C1), Shaggy (SGG), Casein kinase I alpha (CKIalpha), and Casein kinase I epsilon (DCO) all bind to the scaffold protein COS.
