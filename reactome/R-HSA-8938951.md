# RUNX1 binds PAX5 (BSAP)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938951](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938951)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds to PAX5, also known as BSAP (B-cell specific activating protein). The interaction involves the runt domain of RUNX1 and the paired DNA-binding domain of PAX5 (Libermann et al. 1999).
