# PTK6 binds AKT1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8848751](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8848751)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PTK6 binds AKT1 and this interaction involves the SH3 domain of PTK6 and the proline rich sequence in the C-terminus of AKT1 (Zheng et al. 2010).
