# MET bound PI3K generates PIP3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8852019](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8852019)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphatidylinositol-4,5-bisphosphate 3-kinase, PI3K, recruited to activated receptor tyrosine kinases such as EGFR (Rodrigues et al. 2000) and MET (Schaeper et al. 2000) via GAB1, catalyzes phosphatidylinositol-3,4,5-triphosphate (PIP3) synthesis, triggering downstream signaling by AKT family kinases.
