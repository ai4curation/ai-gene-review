# MITF-M binds ITGA2 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926481](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926481)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ITGA2, encoding integrin alpha-2, has been identified as a direct target of MITF-M regulation in melanoma cells (Dilshat et al, 2021; Webster et al, 2014; Laurette et al, 2015). MITF-M binds to a cognate motif at the ITGA2 locus as assessed by ChIP-seq and CUT-and-RUN analysis (Dilshat et al, 2021; Webster et al, 2014; Laurette et al, 2015). CRISPR- or siRNA-mediated knockdown of MITF-M in melanoma cell lines results in upregulation of ITGA2 expression, implicating MITF-M as a negative regulator of ITGA2 (Dilshat et al, 2021).
