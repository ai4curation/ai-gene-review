# MITF-M binds PXN gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926480](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926480)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M binds to a cognate site in the PXN gene to negatively regulate its expression as assessed by ChIP-seq, CUT-and-RUN and transcriptomic analyses (Webster et al, 2014; Laurette et al, 2015; Dilshat et al, 2021). Through its regulation of expression of genes involved in extracellular matrix organization, focal adhesions and epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition, MITF-M modulates the morphology and invasive behaviour of cells (Carreira et al, 2006; Dilshat et al, 2021; reviewed in Rambow et al, 2019; Goding and Arnheiter, 2019).
