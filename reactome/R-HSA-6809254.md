# MTMR8 binds MTMR9
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6809254](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6809254)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MTMR8 binds to MTMR9, an enzymatically inactive myotubularin family member, which results in increased stability and increased catalytic activity of MTMR8 (Zou et al. 2012).
