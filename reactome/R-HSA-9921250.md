# C binds HNRNPK
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9921250](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9921250)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In human 293T and hamster BHK cells, Dengue virus 2 (DENV-2) capsid (core, C) protein binds to heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein K (HNRNPK) (Chang et al., 2001). Subsequent research on DENV-2 in human A549 and monkey Vero cells showed that HNRNPK translocates to the cytoplasm during infection and that its depletion inhibits viral multiplication, indicating it is a host factor required for DENV replication (Brunetti et al., 2015; reviewed in Wang et al., 2022).
