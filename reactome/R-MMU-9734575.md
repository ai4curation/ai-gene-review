# Gata4, Isl1, Tbx20, and Nkx2-5 bind the Mef2c gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9734575](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9734575)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Gata4 binds two sites and Isl1 binds two sites in the noncoding region of the Mef2c gene (Dodou et al., He et al. 2011). Nkx2-5 also binds the promoter of the Mef2c gene (He et al. 2011). An Isl1:Ldb1 complex (Caputo et al. 2015) and Tbx20 (Takeuchi et al. 2005) bind the anterior heart field enhancer of the Mef2c gene.
