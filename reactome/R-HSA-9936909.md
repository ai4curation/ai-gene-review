# factor XII + β-amyloid fibril:Zn2+ -> factor XII:β-amyloid fibril:Zn2+
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9936909](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9936909)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Aggregated amyloid beta (Aβ) peptides, such as APP(672-711) and APP(672-713), a hallmark of Alzheimer's disease (AD), provide a negatively charged surface that facilitates the binding and activation of factor XII (FXII) (Joseph K et al., 1999; Bergamaschini L et al., 2001; Zamolodchikov D et al., 2015, 2016). Surface-bound FXII is converted to its active form, FXIIa, which subsequently initiates the kallikrein-kinin system, leading to the release of proinflammatory mediators such as bradykinin. Active FXIIa may also contribute to blood clot formation (Schmaier AH, 2016a, b). The interaction between Aβ aggregates and FXII may contribute to vascular dysfunction, prothrombotic state, and neuroinflammation associated with Alzheimer’s disease pathology. Therapeutic antibody lecanemab targets Aβ plaques blocking this interaction (Chen ZL et al., 2023; Johannesson M et al., 2024).
