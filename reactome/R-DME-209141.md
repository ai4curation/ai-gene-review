# ProSPZ:SPZ dimer binds to TL receptor at the plasma membrane
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209141](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209141)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

On binding to the Toll (TL) receptor ectodomain, Spatzle (SPZ) dimer sheds its Pro-domains.  The SPZ dimer binds to the TL ectodomain at its N-terminus.
