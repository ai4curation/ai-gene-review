# Dll1 binds Notch2
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-2060798](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-2060798)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Mouse Dll1, exogenously expressed on the surface of Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) cells, binds Notch2, a predominant Notch protein on the surface of mouse BaF3 cells, inducing Notch2 cleavage and translocation of Notch2 intracellular domain (NICD2) to the nucleus (Shimizu et al. 2000).
