# MITF-M dimer binds MYO5A gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9856464](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9856464)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M binds to the MYO5A promoter to stimulate transcription as assessed by ChIP, reporter gene assay and RT-PCR (Strub et al, 2011; Alves et al, 2017; reviewed in Cheli et al, 2010; Goding and Arnheiter, 2019).<br>MYO5A contributes to pigmentation by virtue of its interaction with the small GTPase RAB27A (also a direct MITF-M target), MLPH (also known as melanophilin) and actin. In response to alpha-MSH and MC1R signaling, the complex of MYO5A, RAB27A and MLPH directs melanosomes along the actin filaments to dendrites, where the transfer of melanosomes to adjacent keratinocytes is facilitated (Bahadoran et al, 2001; Fukuda et al, 2002; Nagashima et al, 2002; Kuroda and Fukuda, 2044; reviewed in Seabra and Coudrier, 2004; Hume and Seabra, 2011). In the absence of MITF-M, melanosomes instead adopt a perinuclear localization that can be rescued by overexpression of RAB27A (Chiaverini et al, 2008; Alves et al, 2017).
