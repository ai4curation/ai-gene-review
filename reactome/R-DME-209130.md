# Full-length SPZ dimer is cleaved by EA/SPE serine protease
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209130](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209130)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

In dorsoventral patterning, the trypsin-like serine protease Easter (EA) is known to cleave full-length Spatzle (SPZ) dimer between residues Arg220 and Val221.  This frees the Pro-domain and the C-terminal SPZ dimer aka C-106.  In the innate immunity response, the serine protease cleavage enzyme is the Spatzle-processing enzyme aka SPE (CG16705).  The Pro-domains and the SPZ dimer remain associated after cleavage.
