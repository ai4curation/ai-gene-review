# Expression of pax3 in the neural border
**Reactome ID:** [R-XLA-9835813](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-XLA-9835813)
**Species:** Xenopus laevis

## Summary

Tfap2a activates expression of pax3 in the neural plate border (de Croze et al. 2011). Gbx2 is expressed prior to pax3 and activates expression of pax3 (Li et al. 2009).
