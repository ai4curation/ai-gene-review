# PIAS4 SUMOylates VHL with SUMO1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-4551721](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-4551721)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PIAS4 SUMOylates VHL at lysine-159, lysine-171, and possibly lysine-196 with SUMO1 (Cai et al. 2010, Chien et al. 2013). SUMOylation facilitates the oligomerization of VHL, abolishes the inhibitory function of VHL on HIF1A, and abolishes the tumor suppressor function of VHL by inactivating the ubiquitinylation activity of VHL.
