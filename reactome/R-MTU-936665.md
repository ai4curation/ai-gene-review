# OPS is added to the carrier sulfide
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-936665](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-936665)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

Similar to thiamine or pterine biosynthesis, the sulfide carrier protein CysO serves as substrate to the pyridoxal phosphate-dependent, dimeric CysM which transfers O-phosphoserine onto the sulfur (OLeary et al, 2008; Agren et al, 2008).
