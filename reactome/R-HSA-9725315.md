# ALK-stimulated MYCN gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9725315](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9725315)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MYCN gene expression is stimulated downstream of wild-type and activated ALK signaling in neuronal and neuroblastoma cell lines as well as in murine neuroblastoma models (Schonherr et al, 2012; Montavon et al, 2014). MYCN and ALK are both located on chromosome 2, which is subject to amplifications in neuroblastoma, and ALK and MYCN are both implicated as contributing to neuroblastoma progression (Mosse et al, 2008; Caren et al, 2008; Hasan et al, 2012; De Brouwer et al, 2010; Montavon et al, 2012; Zhu et al, 2012). Abrogation of ALK activity with protein tyrosine kinase inhibitors or with anti-siRNA against ALK decreases MYCN transcript and protein levels, but the direct mechanisms for ALK-dependent MYCN gene expression have not been identified (Schonherr et al, 2012; Hallberg and Palmer, 2013; Roskoski, 2013).
