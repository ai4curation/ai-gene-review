# AKT1 E17K mutant phosphorylates NR4A1 (NUR77)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2399988](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2399988)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

AKT1 E17K gain-of-function mutant is expected to phosphorylate NR4A1, like the wild-type AKT (Pekarsky et al. 2001), but this has not been experimentally tested.
