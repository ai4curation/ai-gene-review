# E2F1 binds the CDC25A gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8961961](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8961961)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

E2F1 binds to E2F binding sites in the promoter of the CDC25A gene, encoding M-phase inducer phosphatase 1 (DeGregori et al. 1995, Vigo et al. 1999).
