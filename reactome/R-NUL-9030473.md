# BDNF- or NTF4-activated Ntrk2 binds Shc1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9030473](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9030473)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Mouse Ntrk2, activated by recombinant human BDNF or NTF4 (NT-4), binds  Shc1 isoforms p52 (Shc-1) and p47 (Shc1-3) (Minichiello et al. 1998).
