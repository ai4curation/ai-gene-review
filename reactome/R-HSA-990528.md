# Interaction of PCBP2 with MAVS
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-990528](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-990528)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Poly(rC) binding protein 2 (PCB2), is one of the negative regulators of RIG-I (DDX58) and IFIH1 (MDA5) signaling pathways. It interacts with MAVS (IPS-1) and mediates its ubiquitin/proteasomal degradation by recruiting E3 ligase AIP4 (ITCH).
