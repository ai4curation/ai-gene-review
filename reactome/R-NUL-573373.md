# Ttf-I:rRNA Promoter:Ercc6:Ehmt2 complex dimethylates histone H3 at lysine-9
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-573373](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-573373)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Ehmt2 (histone methyltransferase G9a) dimethylates histone H3 at lysine-9 in the transcribed region of the rRNA gene (Yuan et al. 2007). Dimethylation of histone H3 in the transcribed region causes increased rRNA expression. ATPase activity of the Chd4 subunit of the NuRD complex is required to activate transcription by repositioning a nucleosome in the promoter region of an rRNA gene (Xie et al. 2012).
