# ALB binds ST
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9750666](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9750666)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Salicylate binds to multiple sites on serum albumin, but at therapeutic doses only one site seems relevant, for kinetic reason. The exact binding site is unknown. The binding is reversible (Reynolds & Cluff, 1960; Borgå et al, 1976; Shen et al, 1991).
