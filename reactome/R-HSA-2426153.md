# SREBP1A,1C binds the ACACB promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2426153](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2426153)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SREBP1A (SREBF1A) or SREBP1C, together with SP1, bind and transactivate promoter II of the ACACB gene in human HepG2 cells (Oh et al. 2003).
