# RUNX1 binds the OCLN gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8935980](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8935980)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds to the promoter of the OCLN gene, encoding Occludin, a component of the tight junction (Miao et al. 2015).
