# Prlr binds Shp2
**Reactome ID:** [R-RNO-1369092](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-RNO-1369092)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

Prlr can bind the protein-tyrosine Phosphatase Shp2, (Ptpn11) via its C-terminal SH2 Domain. This binding does not occur when tyrosine 599 is mutated to alanine (Ali & Ali 2000).
