# Activated Ntrk2 recruits PI3K
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9031077](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9031077)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Rat Ntrk2 (TrkB) receptor, activated by recombinant human BDNF or NTF4, co-immunoprecipitates with the mouse PI3K complex (Yuen and Mobley 1999).
