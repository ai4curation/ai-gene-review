# Binding of MyoD to TGFBR3 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9860169](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9860169)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TGFBR3 (Betaglycan) promoters contain several DNA‑binding sites for MyoD and MEF2 proteins. Upon induction of muscle differentiation, these proteins bind to the regulatory regions and activate the transcription of skeletal muscle genes, such as creatine kinase (Lopez‑Casillas et al, 2003 and Ramírez‑Vidal et al, 2023).
