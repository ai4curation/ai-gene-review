# MITF-M binds the MCM5 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926493](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926493)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M binds an E-box element at-23kb of the MCM5 promoter to stimulate expression, as assessed by ChIP and RNA seq (Strub et al, 2011).
