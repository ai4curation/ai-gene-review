# RHOJ GEFs activate RHOJ
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9017491](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9017491)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DOCK8 guanine nucleotide exchange factors (GEFs) was shown to bind RHOJ and catalyze GDP to GTP exchange on RHOJ, resulting in formation of the active RHOJ:GTP complex (Ruusala and Aspenstrom 2004).<br>PREX1 (Jaiswal et al. 2013) and TRIO (Jaiswal et al. 2013) exhibited very low RHOJ-directed GEF activity, being hundred times and thousand times, respectively, less active on RHOJ than on CDC42 and RHOG. They are annotated as candidate RHOJ GEFs.<br><br>The following GEFs do not act on RHOJ:<br>ARHGEF1 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>ARHGEF11 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>ARHGEF12 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>ARHGEF15 (Kusuhara et al. 2012)<br>ARHGEF28 (also known as p190RhoGEF, KIAA1998 or RGNEF) (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>ARHGEF4 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>ARHGEF9 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>DNMBP (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>DOCK9 (Nishikimi et al. 2005)<br>DOCK11 (Nishikimi et al. 2005)<br>ITSN1 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>MCF2 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>MCF2L (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>TIAM1 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>VAV2 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)
