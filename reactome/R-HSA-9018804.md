# RAC3 GEFs activate RAC3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9018804](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9018804)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The following guanine nucleotide exchange factors (GEFs) were shown to bind RAC3 and catalyze GDP to GTP exchange on RAC3, resulting in formation of the active RAC3:GTP complex:<br>MCF2 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>PREX1 (Waters et al. 2008; Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>TIAM1 (Haeusler et al. 2003)<br>TRIO (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>VAV2 (Rosenberg et al. 2017; Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br><br>DOCK10 is annotated as a candidate RAC3 GEF, because it binds to RAC3 but its ability to activate RAC3 is uncertain (Ruiz-Lafuente et al. 2015).<br><br>The following GEFs do not act on RAC3:<br>DOCK11 (Ruiz-Lafuente et al. 2015)<br>ITSN1 (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>MCF2L (Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>PLEKHG5 (De Toledo et al. 2001)
