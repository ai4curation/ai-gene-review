# Smad7:SMURF1 binds phosphorylated TGFBR1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2169036](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2169036)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Smad7 recruits SMURF1 to TGF-beta (TGFB1)-activated TGF-beta receptor complex. In this study, recombinant mouse Smad7 and recombinant human SMURF1, TGFBR2 and TGFBR1 were exogenously expressed in COS7 cells (Ebisawa et al. 2001).
