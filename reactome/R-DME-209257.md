# Phosphorylated REL-68 forms a complex with PCAF at its target gene and activates transcription
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209257](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209257)

## Summary

In the nucleus,  phosphorylated Relish (REL-68) forms a complex with the histone acetyltransferase, PCAF, at its target gene and transcription is activated.
