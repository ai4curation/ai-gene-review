# D-beta hydroxybutyrate+NAD+ <=> acetoacetate+NADH+H+
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-73920](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-73920)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

D-beta-hydroxybutyrate dehydrogenase tetramer (BDH1) in the mitochondrial matrix catalyzes the reversible reaction of D-beta hydroxybutyrate and NAD+ to form acetoacetate and NADH + H+ (Marks et al. 1992).
