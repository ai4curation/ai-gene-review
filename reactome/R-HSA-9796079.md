# NFE2L2 dependent ABCC3 expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796079](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796079)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ABCC3 is under transcriptional control of NFE2L2 and contains a functional antioxidant response element within the eighth intron of the human ABCC3 gene (Canet et al, 2015; Pellegatta et al, 2019). It plays a significant role in multidrug resistance.
