# CHD8 binds WDR5
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9944456](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9944456)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CHD8 interacts with WDR5 as assessed by co-immunoprecipitation as well as by in vitro GST-pulldown (Thompson et al, 2008). There is conflicting evidence as to whether CHD8 is part of a larger MLL complex or only interacts with a subset of MLL complex members (Dou et al, 2005; Dou et al, 2006: Thompson et al, 2008).
