# Ntrk2 promotes Tiam1 phosphorylation
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9033333](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9033333)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Mouse Ntrk2 (Trkb), activated by recombinant human BDNF, and phosphorylated by Cdk5 at serine residue S478, promotes phosphorylation of mouse Tiam1 at tyrosine residue Y829 (Lai et al. 2012).
