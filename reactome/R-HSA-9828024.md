# SOX17 and TFAP2C bind the NANOG gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9828024](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9828024)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SOX17 and TFAP2C binds enhancers upstream of the transcription start site of the NANOG gene (Tang et al. 2022). Both SOX17 and TFAP2C activate expression of NANOG (Tang et al. 2022).
