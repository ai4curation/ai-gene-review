# NS3 binds RIPK1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9921857](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9921857)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DENV NS3 protein interacts with Receptor-interacting serine/threonine-protein kinase 1 (RIPK1), inhibiting RIPK1-mediated necroptosis via decreased MLKL phosphorylation. The interaction does not involve NS3 protease activity, nor does it lead to increased RIPK1 degradation (Udawatte et al., 2022).
