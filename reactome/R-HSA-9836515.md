# p-PKR dimer phosphorylates PTPN2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9836515](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9836515)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Protein kinase R (PKR, EIF2AK2) phosphorylates the tyrosine-protein phosphatase PTPN2 (TCPTP) on an unknown residue. It is unclear if this directly affects the catalytic activity of PTPN2 (Wang et al, 2006).
