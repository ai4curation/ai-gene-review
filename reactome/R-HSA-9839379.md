# TGFBR3 binding to FGF2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9839379](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9839379)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TGFBR3 (Betaglycan) bind bFGF via its heparan sulphate chains(GAGs) and form ternary complexes with FGFs and FGF receptors in neuronal development (Andrea et al. 1992 and Knelson et al, 2013)
