# Glycosylated alpha-DAG1 localizes to Golgi
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9940714](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9940714)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Alpha-dystroglycan (DAG1) modified on one or more threonine residues with the core M3 trisaccharide linker GalNAc-GlcNAc-ManP is transported by COPII vesicles from ER to Golgi (reviewed in Watson & Stevens, 2005; Gomez-Navarro & Miller, 2016; Tang & Ginsburg, 2023).
