# NR1D1 (REV-ERBA) binds heme, the NPAS2 gene, and recruits corepressors
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5663245](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5663245)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NR1D1 (REV-ERBA) binds the promoter of the NPAS2 gene and recruits corepressors to repress transcription (Crumbley et al. 2010). Recruitment of repressors appears to depend on the binding of heme by NR1D1.
