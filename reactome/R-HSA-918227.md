# Recruitment of TRAF3 to MAVS
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-918227](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-918227)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TRAF3 a E3 ligase for K63-linked polyubiquitination, is one of the critical molecules required for mediating MAVS-dependent type I IFN production. TRAF3 interacts directly with MAVS (IPS-1) through the TRAF domain of TRAF3 and a TRAF-interaction motif (TIM) within MAVS (IPS-1).
