# Arachidonate is epoxidated to 8,9-EET by CYP(5)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2161899](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2161899)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Several cytochrome P450s (CYPs) convert arachidonate to 8,9-epoxyeicosatrienoate (8,9-EET). The CYPs and their references are: CYP1A1, CYP1A2, CYP1B1 (Choudhary et al. 2004); CYP2C8, CYP2C9 (Rifkind et al. 1995); CYP2C19 (Bylund et al. 1998, Rifkind et al. 1995); CYP2J2 (Wu et al. 1996).
