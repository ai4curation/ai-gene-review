# Collagen type III degradation by MMP15
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2473584](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2473584)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The membrane-type MMP MMP15 (MT2-MMP) is a fibrillar collagenase able to degrade collagen type I (Morrison & Overall 2006) and believed able to degrade collagen types II and III (Somerville et al. 2002).
