# Notch3 coactivator complex binds the Ptcra gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9016915](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9016915)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Mouse Rbpj transcription factor directly binds to Rbpj/Csl elements in the promoter of the Ptcra gene, encoding Pre-TCR-alpha. As Notch3 intracellular domain and Maml proteins are also needed for induction of the Ptcra gene transcription, it is plausible that Rbpj binds to the Ptcra promoter in the context of the Notch3 coactivator complex (Bellavia et al. 2007).
