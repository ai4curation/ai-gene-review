# SREBP1A/1C/2 is retained in the endoplasmic reticulum by SCAP:cholesterol:INSIG
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2317526](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2317526)
**Species:** Cricetulus griseus

## Summary

SREBPs (SREBP1A/1C/2, SREBFs) bind SCAP in the endoplasmic reticulum membrane. Luminal loop 1 of SCAP binds cholesterol (Adams et al. 2003) which prevents SCAP from interacting with Sec24 in the CopII coat complex and allows SCAP to interact with INSIG instead (Sakai et al. 1997, Yang et al. 2002, Dobrosotskaya et al. 2003, Adams et al. 2004, Feramisco et al. 2005, Sun et al. 2005). These interactions retain SCAP:SREBP1A/1C/2 in the endoplasmic reticulum.
