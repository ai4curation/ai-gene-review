# Secreted NS1 binds to other cell
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9920147](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9920147)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Soluble NS1 binds to the surface of uninfected cells, primarily via interactions with heparan sulfate and chondroitin sulfate E (Avirutnan et al., 2007).
