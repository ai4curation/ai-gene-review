# CHD8:CTNNB1 represses AXIN2 expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9944652](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9944652)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

AXIN2 expression is upregulated in CHD8 knockdown HCT116 cells, consistent for a role of CHD8:CTNNB1 in the negative regulation of this WNT target gene (Thompson et al, 2008; Sakamoto et al, 2000; Kobayashi et al, 2002).
