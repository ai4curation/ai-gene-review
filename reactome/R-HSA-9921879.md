# NS5 binds PAF1C
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9921879](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9921879)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Nuclear DENV NS5 interacts with Polymerase associated factor 1 complex (PAF1C), inhibiting the expression of STAT2-independent immune response genes (Shah et al., 2018; Petit et al., 2021).
