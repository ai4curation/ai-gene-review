# Furin cleaves pro-protein S to protein S
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-159773](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-159773)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Furin, associated with the Golgi membrane, cleaves pro-protein S to form mature, gamma-carboxylated protein S (Wasley et al. 1993).
