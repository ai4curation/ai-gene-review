# Collagen type IX binds integrin alpha11beta1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-4086223](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-4086223)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Collagen IX, expressed in cartilage and eye, is a high-avidity ligand for all four collagen receptor integrins including alpha11beta1.
