# DL dimer binds to TUB and phosphorylated PLL in TL:WEK:MYD88:TUB:PLL 'signalling complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-214880](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-214880)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

In dorsoventral patterning in the embryo, Dorsal (DL) homodimers in complex with the NF-kappaB inhibitor orthologue, Cactus (CACT), bind to the adaptor proteins Tube (TUB) and phosphorylated Pelle (PLL) in the receptor 'signalling complex' at the plasma membrane.
