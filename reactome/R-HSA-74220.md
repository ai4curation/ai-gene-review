# (d)AMP + ATP <=> (d)ADP + ADP (AK1)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-74220](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-74220)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cytosolic adenylate kinase 1 (AK1) catalyzes the reversible reactions of AMP and dAMP with ATP to form ADP and dADP respectively, plus ADP (Tsuboi 1978; Matsuura et al. 1989).
