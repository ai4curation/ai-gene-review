# Collagen VII binds collagen IV
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-4084497](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-4084497)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The NC1 domain of collagen VII is able to bind collagen type IV and laminin-322 (laminin-5) (Brittingham et al. 2006). This facilitates stabilization of the basement membrane structure.
