# MET phosphorylates CBL
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8875451](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8875451)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MET phosphorylates CBL on unknown tyrosine residues. This is thought to precede CBL-mediated MET ubiquitination and may promote stronger interaction of CBL with the activated MET receptor complex (Peschard et al. 2001, Petrelli et al. 2002).
