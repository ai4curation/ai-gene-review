# RNA Polymerase III Abortive Initiation
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-111943](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-111943)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

Abortive initiation, the repetitive formation of short oligonucleotides, is a ubiquitous feature of transcriptional initiation.  For pol III, it has been analyzed only in S. cerevisiae (Bhargava and Kassavetis, 1999).  The released products of abortive initiation, principally di- and tri-nucleotides, indicate the participation of retractive endonucleolytic cleavage of these initially forming abortive products.  The transition from abortive to productive transcription may occur at bp +5.
