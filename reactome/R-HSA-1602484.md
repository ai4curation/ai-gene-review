**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1602484](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1602484)

## Summary

proMMP11 has a furin recognition sequence and is activated by furin in the Golgi (Pei & Weiss 1996, Santavicca et al. 1996).
