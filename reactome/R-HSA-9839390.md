# TGFBR3 complex binds to TGFBR2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9839390](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9839390)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TGFB1,TGFB2 binds to TGFBR3 and facilitates the TGFB1/2 binding to TGFBR2. This further amplifies the signaling induced by TGFbeta/TGFBR2 by facilitating the binding of ligands and amplifies TGFb signaling pathway (F López-Casillas et al., 1993,  Blobe et al., 2001).
