# TFAP2A,TFAP2C homodimers stimulate CGA gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8864433](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8864433)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Homodimers of AP-2 family transcription factors TFAP2A (AP-2 alpha) and TFAP2C (AP-2 gamma) directly stimulate CGA (chorionic gonadotropin alpha) transcription by binding to the CGA promoter (Johnson et al. 1997, LiCalsi et al. 2000).
