# RUNX1:CBFB binds CREBBP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938231](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938231)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds CREBBP (CBP) histone acetyltransferase. The interaction involves the C-terminal domain of RUNX1 (Oakford et al. 2010).
