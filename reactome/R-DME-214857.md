# DL/DIF dimer binds to TUB and phosphorylated PLL in TL:MYD88:TUB:PLL 'signalling complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-214857](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-214857)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The Dorsal (DL) and Dorsal-related immunity factor (DIF) homodimers in complex with the NF-kappaB inhibitor orthologue, Cactus (CACT), bind to the adaptor proteins Tube (TUB) and phosphorylated Pelle (PLL) in the receptor 'signalling complex' at the plasma membrane.
