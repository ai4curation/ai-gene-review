# NKX2-5 binds the GATA4 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9735143](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9735143)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NKX2-5 binds sites (called NKE sites) in the promoter upstream of the first exon of GATA4 in embryonic hearts (Riazi et al. 2009). NKX2-5 enhances transcription of GATA4 (Riazi et al. 2009), which then directly enhances transcription of NKX2-5.
