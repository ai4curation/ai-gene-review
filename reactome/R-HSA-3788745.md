# EHMT1:EHMT2 methylates IL8 promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3788745](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3788745)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

EHMT1 (GLP) and EHMT2 (G9a) histone methyltransferases dimethylate histone H3 (HIST1H3A) on lysine residue 10, creating an H3K9Me2 mark on nucleosomes associated with the IL8 promoter (Takahashi et al. 2012).
