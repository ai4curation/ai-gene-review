# NEK1 phosphorylates ME1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9861642](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9861642)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Serine/threonine-protein kinase NEK1 (aka NimA-related protein kinase 1) phosphorylates cytosolic malic enzyme 1 (ME1) at Ser-336, inhibiting its catalytic activity (Zhu et al., 2020). Mutations in NEK1 can lead to a form of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS24, MIM:617892; Mann et al., 2023; reviewed by Yao et al., 2021), as well as a form of short-rib thoracic dysplasia (SRT6, MIM:263520; El Hokayem et al., 2012).
