# MET phosphorylates SHC1-2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8851890](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8851890)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated MET receptor phosphorylates the splicing isoform 2 of the adapter protein SHC1 (SHC1-2) on tyrosine residue Y317 (Pelicci et al. 1995).
