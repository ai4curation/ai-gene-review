# CDC48 hexamer, UFD1, and NPL4 bind K48polyubiquitin of RKR1:RQC2:60S ribosome subunit:peptidyl-tRNA with K48polyUb-nascent peptide
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-9950045](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-9950045)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

A hexamer of CDC48 subunits and the NPL4:UFD1 heterodimer bind polyubiquitin containing lysine-48 linkages (K48polyUb) conjugated to the nascent peptide emerging from the exit tunnel of the 60S ribosomal subunit (Brandman et al. 2012, Defenouillère et al. 2013, Verma et al. 2013). NPL4 (homolog of human NPLOC4) and UFD1 (homolog of human UFD1L) establish the selectivity of CDC48 binding to K48 polyubiquitin (Tsuchiya et al. 2017, Sato et al. 2019, Williams et al. 2023).
