# RUNX1 binds ELF1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938913](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938913)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds to ELF1 (MEF), a member of the ETS family of transcription factors (Mao et al. 1999, Cho et al. 2004). The interaction involves the ETS-interacting subdomain (EID) in the C-terminal portion of the RUNX1 Runt domain and a region of ELF1 that is N-terminal to its ETS domain. ELF1 does not directly interact with CBFB (Mao et al. 1999).
