# WASP/N-WASP activate the Arp2/3 complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-442561](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-442561)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

The binding of the ARP2/3 complex to CDC42:GTP-activated N-WASP was demonstrated using rat Cdc42 and N-Wasp and bovine ARP2/3 complex (Rohatgi et al. 1999, Kiselar et al. 2007, Padrick et al. 2011).
