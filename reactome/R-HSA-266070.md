# LTC4 is exported from the cytosol by ABCC1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-266070](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-266070)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

On formation, leukotriene C4 (LTC4) is exported to the extracellular region by the ABCC1 transporter (Sjolinder et al. 1999, Leier et al. 1994, Lam et al. 1989) and processed further by cleavage reactions.
