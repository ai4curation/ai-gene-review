# Rab6 positively regulates Notch trafficking through Golgi
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-2032746](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-2032746)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Drosophila homolog of RAB6, Rab6, was isolated as the product of warthog (wrt) gene, identified in a genetic screen as a modifier of Notch eye phenotype. RAB6 is a GTP-ase that regulates trafficking through Golgi and trans-Golgi network (TGN).
