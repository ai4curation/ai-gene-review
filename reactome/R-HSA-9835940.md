# HCMV TRS1 binds PKR dimer
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9835940](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9835940)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Human cytomegalovirus TRS1 protein binds protein kinase R (PKR, EIF2AK2), after its activation, and inhibits PKR kinase activity, promoting hCMV protein synthesis and replication (Braggin et al, 2016; Vincent et al, 2017).
