# Tyrosine phosphorylated IL6ST binds STAT3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9958878](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9958878)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

STAT3 binds to IL6ST (gp130) via phosphotyrosine residues 905 and 915 within the two YXPD recognition motifs, as well as two additional sites around Y767 and Y814 that have less restricted sequence recognition requirements (YXXQ) (Gerhartz et al. 1996). After receptor binding, STATs are phosphorylated on a single tyrosine residue by JAKs (Hemmann et al. 1996).
