# Coat Assembly
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-203979](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-203979)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Sar1p-GTP recruits the cytoplasmic Sec23p-Sec24p complex. (Though not represented in the subsequent steps, Sec23p-Sec24p would bind to members of the p24 protein family of possible cargo receptors, and together with Sar1p bind the appropiate v-SNARE, and Rab-GTP.)<br><br><br>
