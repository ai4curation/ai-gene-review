# BDNF binds Ntrk2
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9025041](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9025041)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

A recombinant human BDNF binds to and activates a recombinant mouse Ntrk2 (TrkB) (Klein et al. 1991). Activation of rat Ntrk2 by human BDNF binding was also demonstrated (Soppet et al. 1991).
