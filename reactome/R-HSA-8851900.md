# SHC1-2 bound to MET recruits GRB2:SOS1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8851900](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8851900)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SHC1 splicing isoform 2 (SHC1-2) phosphorylated by MET on tyrosine residue Y317 recruits the GRB2:SOS1 complex to the activated MET receptor (Pelicci et al. 1995).
