# ERBB4s80:ESR1 binds ERBB4 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9612672](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9612672)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The complex of intracellular ERBB4 fragment, ERBB4s80 (E4ICD), and activated estrogen receptor (ESR1:estrogen), binds to estrogen-response elements in the promoter of the ERBB4 gene (Zhu et al. 2006).
