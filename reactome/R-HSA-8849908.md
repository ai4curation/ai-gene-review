# SALM4 binds flotillin-1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8849908](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8849908)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SALM4 induces neurite branching by binding to flotillin-1, a lipid raft-associated protein that interacts with NMDA receptors (Swanwick et al. 2009, 2010).
