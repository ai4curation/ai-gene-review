# ARHGAP35 stimulates RHOA GTPase activity
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8849082](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8849082)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ARHGAP35 (p190 Rho GAP), phosphorylated on tyrosine residue Y1105 by PTK6 (BRK), activates RHOA GTPase activity, resulting in RHOA inactivation (Shen et al. 2008).
