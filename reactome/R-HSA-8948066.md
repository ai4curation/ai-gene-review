# K63polyUb-IRAK1 dissociates
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8948066](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8948066)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Pellino1, 2 and possibly 3 are believed to directly catalyse polyubiquitylation of IRAK1 (Xiao et al. 2008; Butler et al. 2007; Ordureau et al. 2008). Following ubiquitination IRAK1 dissociates.
