# GCN2 binds tRNA
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-9633467](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-9633467)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

The histidyl-tRNA synthetase-like domain of GCN2 binds uncharged tRNA, resulting in activation of the protein kinase domain of GCN2 (Wek et al. 1995, Dong et al. 2000, Qiu et al. 2001, Zaborske et al. 2009, Lageix et al. 2014, Lageix et al. 2015). In the absence of tRNA, GCN2 appears to exist in equilibrium between antiparallel and parallel dimers. Upon binding of tRNA, the parallel dimer is stabilized and the C-terminal domain shifts away from the protein kinase domain, resulting in activation of the kinase activity of GCN2 (Qiu et al. 1998, Qiu et al. 2001, Narasimhan et al. 2004, Dey et al. 2007, He et al. 2014, Lageix et al. 2014, Lageix et al. 2015). GCN1 interacts with GCN2 and ribosomal protein RPS10 and is required for efficient phosphorylation of SUI2 (eIF2-alpha) by GCN2 (Garcia-Barrio et al. 2000, Kubota et al. 2000, Sattlegger and Hinnebusch 2000, Sattlegger and Hinnebusch 2005, Lee et al. 2015).
