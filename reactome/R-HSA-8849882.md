# SALMs 1-4 bind reticulon 3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8849882](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8849882)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In the brain, reticulon 3 (RTN3) tightly associates with SALM2 and SALM3 to form a complex, and interacts relatively weakly with SALM1 and SALM4. This interaction is mediated by the LRR domain in SALMs (Chang et al. 2010).
