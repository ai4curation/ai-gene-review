# FOXP3 in complex with RUNX1 binds to the TNFRSF18 (GITR) gene intron 1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8877490](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8877490)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

FOXP3 bound to the RUNX1:CBFB complex binds to the first intron of the TNFRSF18 (GITR) gene (Ono et al. 2007). It is probable that FOXP3 acts as part of a complex with NFATC2 (Wu et al. 2006).
