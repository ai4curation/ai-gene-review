# IL6:IL6R-2 binds IL6ST-2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1067676](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1067676)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Soluble IL6ST (sgp130) binds to circulating Interleukin-6 (IL6) bound to the soluble form of IL6 receptor alpha (IL6:IL6R-2), preventing binding to IL6ST (gp130) on the plasma membrane, thereby specifically inhibiting IL6 trans-signaling (Jostock et al. 2001).
