# Factor H binds to C3bBb
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-977363](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-977363)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Factor H (CFH) binds to C3bBb, leading to displacement of Bb. Complement factor H-related protein 3 (FHR3) has also been reported to bind C3Bb leading to inhibition of C3Bb C3 convertase activity (Fritsche et al. 2010). CFH also acts as a cofactor for the factor I-mediated proteolytic inactivation of C3b to iC3b.
