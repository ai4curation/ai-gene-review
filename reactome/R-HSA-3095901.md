# ABCC1 transports cytosolic RCbl to extracellular region
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3095901](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3095901)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Multidrug resistance associated protein 1 (ABCC1, MRP1) mediates the ATP dependent export of free cobalamins (RCbl) from small intestine cells to the portal vein (Shah et al. 2011, Nielsen et al. 2012).
