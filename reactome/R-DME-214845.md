# CACT and DL dimer are phosphorylated in TL:WEK:MYD88:TUB:PLL 'signalling complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-214845](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-214845)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Pelle (PLL) may be the kinase that phosphorylates both Cactus (CACT) and the Dorsal (DL) homodimer but the biochemical evidence for this is currently inconclusive.  CACT is phosphorylated in its N-terminal half within the first 125 residues.  In this region, there are two IKK target-like motifs, involving Ser74 and Ser78 in one, and Ser116 in the other.  However, it is not clear which of these residues need to be phosphorylated.  DL homodimer is phosphorylated at both Ser317 residues while associated with CACT.
