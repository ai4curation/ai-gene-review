# RUNX1 and ELF1 bind the IL3 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938938](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938938)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

RUNX1 (AML1) and ELF1 (MEF) bind to adjacent RUNX1- and ETS- response elements in the promoter of the IL3 gene, encoding interleukin-3. While RUNX1 and ELF1 can independently activate the IL3 gene transcription, formation of the complex between RUNX1 and ELF1 has a synergistic effect on IL3 expression (Mao et al. 1999).
