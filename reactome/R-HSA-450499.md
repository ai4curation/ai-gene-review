# Protein Kinase B (AKT) phosphorylates KSRP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-450499](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-450499)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

KSRP is phosphorylated by activated protein kinase B/AKT at serine 193 (Diaz-Moreno et al. 2009). Phosphorylation does not prevent KSRP from binding RNA.
