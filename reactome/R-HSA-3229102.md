# p-MAPKAPK3 phosphorylates BMI1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3229102](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3229102)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In response to stress-activated p38 signaling, MAPKAPK3 phosphorylates BMI1, leading to the dissociation of the PRC1.4 complex from chromatin and transcription of p14-ARF (Voncken et al. 2005).
