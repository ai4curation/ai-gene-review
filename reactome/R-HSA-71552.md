# limit dextrin-glycogenin => ((1,6)-alpha-glucosyl)poly((1,4)-alpha-glucosyl) glycogenin
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-71552](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-71552)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cytosolic debranching enzyme associated with glycogen granules transfers 3-glucose blocks from branches to the main linear polyglucose chain of limit dextrin formed on glycogenin 1 or glycogenin 2 (Chen et al. 1988; Shen et al. 1996).
