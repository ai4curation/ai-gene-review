# LMCD1 binds GATA6, sequestering it
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5683888](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5683888)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The GATA transcription factors regulate gene expression in a variety of cell types including cardiovascular, pulmonary and hematopoietic tissues. LIM and cysteine-rich domains protein 1 (LMCD1) is a transcriptional cofactor that binds GATA6 and thus restricts its function by inhibiting its DNA-binding, resulting in repression of GATA6 transcriptional activation of downstream target genes (Rath et al. 2005).
