# MYD88:TUB binds to TL receptor
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209100](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209100)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Upon Spatzle (SPZ) dimer binding, leading to Toll (TL) receptor homodimerisation and activation, MYD88:TUB complex binds to the endoplasmic Toll/Interleukin-1 receptor (TIR) domain of TL receptor at the plasma membrane.
