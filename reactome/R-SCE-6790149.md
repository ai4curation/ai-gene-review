# NCL1 (TRM4) methylates cytidine-49 of tRNA(Asp) yielding 5-methylcytidine-46
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-6790149](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-6790149)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

NCL1 (TRM4) transfers a methyl group from S-adenosylmethionine to the 5 position of cytidine-49 of tRNA(Asp) (Motorin and Grosjean 1999).
