# Furin cleaves pro-BGLAP to BGLAP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6807224](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6807224)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Furin, associated with the Golgi membrane, cleaves pro-BGLAP to form mature, gamma-carboxylated BGLAP (osteocalcin) (Wasley et al. 1993).
