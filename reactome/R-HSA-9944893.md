# NuRD binds Ikaros family member proteins
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9944893](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9944893)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The Ikaros proteins are a family of Cys2His2 zinc finger transcription factors with roles in haematopoietic and lymphocyte development (reviewed in Heizmann et al, 2018; Georgopoulos et al, 2012). Family members Ikaros (IKZF1), Helios (IKZF2) and Aiolos (IKZF3) are expressed predominantly in haematopoietic cells, while two other family members, Eos (IKZF4) and Pegasus (IKZF5) are more broadly expressed. IKZF proteins are characterized by four N-terminal zinc fingers that contribute to the recognition of their GGGAA binding site in vitro and in vivo (Molnar and Georgopoulous, 1994; Hahm et al, 1998; Cobb et al, 2000; Perdomo et al, 2000; reviewed in Heizmann et al, 2018; Geogopoulos et al, 2012). Two C-terminal zinc fingers appear to contribute to dimerization, and Ikaros family proteins have been demonstrated to interact with each other (Molnar and Georgopoulous, 1994; Sun et al, 1996; McCarty et al, 2003). In addition, Ikaros family proteins have been shown to interact with the NuRD complex to repress transcription (Kim et al, 1999; Koipally et al, 1999; Sridharan and Smale, 2007; Caballero et al, 2007; Bottardi et al, 2014; Zhang et al, 2012; Wang et al, 2016; Liang et al, 2017; reviewed in Heizmann et al, 2018; Georgopoulos et al, 2012).
