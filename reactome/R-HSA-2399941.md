# AKT1 E17K mutant phosphorylates BAD
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2399941](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2399941)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

AKT1 E17K gain-of-function mutant phosphorylates BAD (Guo et al. 2010). AKT-mediated BAD phosphorylation inactivates BAD, thereby preventing BAD-mediated apoptosis (Del Peso et al. 1997).
