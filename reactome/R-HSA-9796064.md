# AcK-NFE2L2 binds the NOTCH1 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796064](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796064)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NFE2L2 appears to directly affect Notch1 expression in MEFs and this is likely mediated by the binding of NFE2L2 to a regulatory domain(s) in the −1640 proximal promoter of Notch1 (Wakabayashi et al, 2010; Kim et al, 2014).
