# 2x p-5Y-RET:GDNF:GFRA complexes binds DOK1,DOK2,DOK4,DOK5,DOK6
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8855617](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8855617)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Docking protein 1 (DOK 1), 2, 4, 5, and 6 adaptor proteins all interact with RET at phosphorylated tyrosine-1062 (Y1062) (Grimm et al. 2001, Crowder et al. 2004, Kurotsuchi et al. 2010). <br><br>DOKs are adaptor proteins that can inhibit mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) signaling, cell proliferation, and cellular transformation. DOK1 and 2 may exert their inhibitory effects by recruiting Ras GTPase-activating protein 1 (RASA1, RasGAP), which is a negative regulator of Ras signaling, but DOK2 can attenuate EGF receptor-induced MAP kinase activation without RASA1. DOK3 negatively regulates signaling by recruiting INPP5D and CSK (Grimm et al. 2001).<br><br>RET promotes neurite outgrowth of the rat pheochromocytoma cell line PC12 via Y1062. RET-DOK4/5 fusion proteins induced ligand-dependent axonal outgrowth of PC12 cells, while RET-DOK2 fusions did not. DOK4/5 do not associate with RASA1 or NCK, and enhance RET-dependent activation of MAPK (Grimm et al. 2001).
