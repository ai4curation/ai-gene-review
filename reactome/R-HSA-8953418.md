# EHMT1:EHMT2 complex binds E2F6.com-1 core
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8953418](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8953418)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The complex of histone methyltransferases EHMT1 (GLP) and EHMT2 (G9a) is recruited to the core E2F6.com-1 complex, consisting of E2F6, TFDP1 (DP-1), MGA and MAX (Ogawa et al. 2002).
