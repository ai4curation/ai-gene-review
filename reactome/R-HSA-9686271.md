# CASP4 dimerizes
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9686271](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9686271)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Sensing of cytoplasmic lipopolysaccharide (LPS) by caspase-4 (CASP4) is thought to bring CASP4 molecules into close proximity, facilitating their dimerization and/or oligomerization, which leads to activation via self-cleavage (Shi J et al. 2014; Wacker MA et al., 2017; Fernández-Duran I et al., 2022; reviewed by Clark AC, 2016). SERPINB1 binding limits the activity of CASP4 by preventing CASP4 oligomerization and activation (Choi YJ et al., 2019).
