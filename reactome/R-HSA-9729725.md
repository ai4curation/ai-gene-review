# SARS-CoV-2 nsp3 deISGylates ISGylated IRF3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9729725](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9729725)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The endopeptidase activity of SARS-CoV-2 nsp3 (PLpro) includes cleavage of ISG15 from ISGylated proteins, so called deISGylation (Leite et al, 2021). Since interferon responsive factor 3 (IRF3) is ISGylated this activity contributes to the cleavage of IRF3 and attenuates type I interferon responses (Freitas et al, 2020; Shin et al, 2020).
