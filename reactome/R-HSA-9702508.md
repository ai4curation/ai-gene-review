# FLT mutants bind type I TKIs
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9702508](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9702508)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Type I tyrosine kinase inhibitors bind to the ATP-binding site in the active conformation of the receptor. As a consequence, type I inhibitors are generally active both against FLT3 with ITD and FLT3 with activating mutations in the tyrosine kinase domain (reviewed in Klug et al, 2018; Larrosa-Garcia and Baer, 2017;
