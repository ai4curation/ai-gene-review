# ARID1A binds CDH1 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9766293](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9766293)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ARID1A, a component of the SWI/SNF complex, binds to the CDH1 gene promoter (Yan et al. 2014, Baldi et al. 2022), likely recruiting the SWI/SNF chromatin remodeling complex to the CDH1 gene promoter.
