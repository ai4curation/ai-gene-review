# WG, FZ and ARR bind to each other
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209074](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209074)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The extracellular ligand Wingless (WG) binds to the extracellular domains of the plasma membrane Frizzled (FZ/FZ2) and Arrow (ARR) receptors.  Both of these transmembrane receptors additionally interact with each other.
