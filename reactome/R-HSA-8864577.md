# TFAP2C translocates to the nucleus
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8864577](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8864577)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Like other AP-2 (TFAP2) transcription factor family members, TFAP2C (AP-2 gamma) mainly localizes to the nucleus. WWOX inhibits nuclear translocation of TFAP2C (Aqeilan et al. 2004).
