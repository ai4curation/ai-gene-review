# GDIs block activation of RHOA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9012835](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9012835)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The following GDP dissociation inhibitors (GDIs) bind to RHOA and inhibit its activation:<br>ARHGDIA (Boulter et al. 2010; Man et al. 2010; Gupta et al. 2013)<br>ARHGDIB (Watanabe et al. 2012)<br><br>ARHGDIG was shown to bind to RHOA by Adra et al. 1997, but no binding and no RHOA inhibition was detected by Zalcman et al. 1996. ARHGDIG is annotated as a candidate RHOA GDI.
