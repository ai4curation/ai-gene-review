# Interaction of integrin alpha9beta1 with Tenascin-C
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-216068](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-216068)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The integrin alpha 9 subunit forms a single heterodimer, alpha9beta1 that mediates cell adhesion to a site within the third fibronectin type III repeat of tenascin-C.
