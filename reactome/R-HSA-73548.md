# (d)CMP or UMP + ATP <=> (d)CDP or UDP + ADP (CMPK1)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-73548](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-73548)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cytosolic UMP-CMP kinase (CMPK1) catalyzes the reversible reaction of CMP, dCMP, or UMP and ATP to form CDP, dCDP, or UDP and ADP (Liou et al. 2002; Scott and Wright 1979).
