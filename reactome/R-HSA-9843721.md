# ABCC1,ABCG2 transport C18-S1P to extracellular region
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9843721](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9843721)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The proton pumps ABCC1, ABCG2 transport sphing-4-enine 1-phosphate (sphingosine-1-phosphate, C18-S1P) out of cells. ABCC1 is mainly expressed in mast cells, fibroblasts, and endothelial cells (Mitra et al., 2006; Nieuwenhuis et al., 2009). Export of C18-S1P by ABCG2 has been shown in MCF-7 epithelial cells (Takabe et al., 2010). These processes support a role of C18-S1P secretion in mast cell migration, cytoprotection, maintenance of epithelial barriers, and cancer/multidrug resistance (reviewed by Reitsema et al., 2014).
