# Expression of PTCH1 is repressed by GLI3R in the absence of Hh signaling
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5612510](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5612510)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Expression of the PTCH1 gene is repressed in the absence of Hh signaling by GLI3R (Agren et al, 2004; Lee et al, 2010; Vokes et al, 2007; Vokes et al, 2008).  GLI3R may exert its repression activity through the SKI-dependent recruitment of HDACs (Dai et al, 2002).
