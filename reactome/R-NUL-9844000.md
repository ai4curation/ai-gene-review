# TRIM28 (KAP1) binds CHD3 (Mi-2alpha)
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9844000](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9844000)
**Species:** Bos taurus

## Summary

Bovine TRIM28 binds human CHD3 (Mi-2alpha), a subunit of the NuRD repressor complex, therefore TRIM28 is inferred to associate with the NuRD complex (Schultz et al. 2001).
