# DDX41 ubiquitination by TRIM21
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3134946](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3134946)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TRIM21 (Ro52/SSA1) is a member of the TRIM (Tripartite Motif) family of E3 ligases. E3 activity of TRIM21 was found to be a RING domain-dependent and required E2-conjugating enzymes UBE2D1/2/3/4 and UBE2E1/2 (Espinosa A et al. 2011).<p>TRIM21 can form a complex with DDX41 leading to the K48-linked ubiquitination and degradation of DDX41 (Zhang Z et al. 2013).
