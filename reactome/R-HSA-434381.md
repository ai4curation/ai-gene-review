# CD36 (FAT) translocates a long chain fatty acid from the extracellular region to the cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-434381](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-434381)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CD36 (FAT) located in the plasma membrane of pancreatic beta cells transports long chain fatty acids such as palmitate into the cell (Noushmehr et al. 2005).
