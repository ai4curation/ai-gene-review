# NatC acetylates ARL3
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-6814089](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-6814089)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

The NatC complex acetylates the amino-terminal methionine of ARL3, a ARF-like GTPase with roles in retrograde traffic at the Golgi membrane  Acetylation and interaction with the membrane protein Sys1 are needed for the Golgi localization of ARL3 (Behnia et al, 2004; Setty et al, 2004).
