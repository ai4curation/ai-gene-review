# MGAT5B transfers GlcNAc from UDP-GlcNAc to GlcNAc-Man-DAG1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8932427](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8932427)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

A core M1 structure (Gal-beta1,4-GlcNAc-beta1,2-mannosyl glycan) on alpha-dystroglycan (DAG1) is the starting point for a core M2 structure, initiated by beta1,6-linked GlcNAc extension of M1. The Golgi membrane-associated protein alpha-1,6-mannosylglycoprotein 6-beta-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase B (MGAT5B, aka GNT-Vb) catalyses the transfer of GlcNAc to core M1 (GlcNAc1,2-mannose) as a beta-1,6 linkage (Inamori et al. 2003). Core M2 glycans play a role in neural cell adhesion and migration.
