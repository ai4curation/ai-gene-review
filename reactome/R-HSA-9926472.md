# MITF-M binds the MCM2 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926472](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926472)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M binds to a cognate E-box element at +177 in the 5' UTR of the MCM2 gene to stimulate expression as assessed by ChIP and RNA seq (Stub et al, 2011).
