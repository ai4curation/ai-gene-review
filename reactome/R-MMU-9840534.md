# Oma1 self-cleaves
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9840534](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9840534)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

In response to depolarization of the inner membrane or apoptotic stimulus, OMA1 cleaves itself to yield an activated form, S-OMA1, which is then degraded (Baker et al. 2014, Zhang et al. 2014). The response of OMA1 to depolarization requires an N-terminal hydrophobic region of OMA1 and an adjacent positively charged region (Baker et al. 2014). Cleavage occurs between residues 443 and 452 of the mouse OMA1 (Zhang et al. 2014). The activated OMA1 cleaves OPA1 to regulate mitochondrial fusion (Baker et al. 2014, Zhang et al. 2014).
