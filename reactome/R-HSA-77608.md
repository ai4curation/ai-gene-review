# Binding of A1CF to stem-looped RNA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-77608](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-77608)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

A1CF protein is cytosolic in origin and is translocated to nucleus where it binds to the target RNA. The order of events in the formation of editosomes, namely, binding of A1CF and APOBEC-1 are not well elucidated in human cells (Maris et al.2005).
