# Activated NFκB complex binds IL6 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9958869](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9958869)

## Summary

The NFκB transcription complex, activated by RAC1, binds the promoter of the IL6 gene (Shimizu et al. 1990, Libermann and Baltimore 1990).
