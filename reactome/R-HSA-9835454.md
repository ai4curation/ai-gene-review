# DNAJC3 binds PKR
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9835454](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9835454)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DnaJ homolog subfamily C member 3 protein (DNAJC3, P58IPK), which is upregulated in influenza A infection, binds to PKR inhibiting its activation and ensuring ongoing protein translation during viral infection (Polyak et al, 1996; Tang et al, 1996; Melville et al, 1999; Yan et al, 2002).
