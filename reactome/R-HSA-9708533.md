# BACH1 translocates from the cytosol to the nucleoplasm
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9708533](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9708533)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Transcription regulator protein BACH1 is functional in the nucleus so it is implied that it localizes there after translation (Blouin et al, 1995).
