# Furin cleaves pro-prothrombin to prothrombin
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-159728](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-159728)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Furin, associated with the Golgi membrane, cleaves pro-prothrombin to form mature, gamma-carboxylated prothrombin (Wasley et al. 1993).
