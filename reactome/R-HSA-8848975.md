# PTK6 phosphorylates KHDRBS1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8848975](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8848975)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PTK6 phosphorylates KHDRBS1 (SAM68) on tyrosine residues Y435, Y440 and Y443, located within the nuclear localization signal (NLS) of KHDRBS1 (Lukong et al. 2005). PTK6-mediated phosphorylation inhibits the interaction of KHDRBS1 with RNA (Derry et al. 2000, Coyle et al. 2003).
