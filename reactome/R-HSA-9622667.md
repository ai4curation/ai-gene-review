# BBC3 gene expression is stimulated by FOXO3,(FOXO1)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9622667](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9622667)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Transcription of the pro-apoptotic BBC3 (PUMA) gene is directly stimulated by FOXO3 (Dudgeon et al. 2010, Fitzwalter et al. 2018). FOXO1 upregulates BBC3 transcription (Hughes et al. 2011, Liu et al. 2015, Wu et al. 2016, Liu et al. 2017), but direct binding to the BBC3 gene locus has not been demonstrated.<br>FOXO-mediated upregulation of BBC3 gene transcription is positively regulated by DDIT3 (CHOP) through an unknown mechanism. DDIT3 is able to bind FOXO3, but the physiological role and context of this protein complex is not known (Ghosh et al. 2012).
