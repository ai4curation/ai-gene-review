# AMP binds to gamma subunit of AMP kinase heterotrimer
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-163664](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-163664)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

At the beginning of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'AMPK heterotrimer (inactive)', and 1 molecule of 'AMP' are present. At the end of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'AMPK heterotrimer:AMP' is present.<br><br> This reaction takes place in the 'nucleus'.<br>
