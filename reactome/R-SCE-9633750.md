# GCN2 dimer autophosphorylates
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-9633750](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-9633750)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

After binding uncharged tRNA, the GCN2 dimer trans-autophosphorylates threonine-882 and threonine-887, resulting in activation of the kinase domain of GCN2 (Romano et al. 1998).
