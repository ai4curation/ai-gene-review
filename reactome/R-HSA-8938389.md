# RUNX1:CBFB binds the LGALS3 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938389](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938389)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds to the RUNX1 response element in the promoter of the LGALS3 gene, encoding Galectin-3 (Zhang et al. 2009).
