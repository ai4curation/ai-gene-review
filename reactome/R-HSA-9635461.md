# PtpA dephosphorylates GSK3A
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9635461](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9635461)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Mtb infection inhibits the cleavage of inactive caspase-3 into its active forms by dephosphorylation of human glycogen synthase kinase-3 alpha (GSK3α). Tyr-279 is essential for the full activity of GSK3α. Mtb protein tyrosine phosphatase (PtpA) dephosphorylates Tyr-279 and thereby inactivates GSK3α (Poirier et al. 2014).
