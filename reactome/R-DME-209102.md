# SLMB binds to phosphorylated ARM to form the 'destruction complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209102](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209102)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The phosphorylated Armadillo (ARM) is now recognised and bound by the F-box protein Slimb (SLMB), in the E3 ubiquitin ligase assembled complex additionally containing the S-phase kinase-associated protein SKPA, the Cullin orthologue, LIN19, the Ring-box protein containg ROC1A, and the ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme orthologue EFF.
