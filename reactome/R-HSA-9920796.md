# NS1 binds VTN
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9920796](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9920796)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Secreted NS1 binds to vitronectin (VTN), inhibiting membrane attack complex (MAC) formation and preventing the completion of the C5b9 lytic pore (Conde et al., 2016).
