# Phosphorylated GAB1 recruits PI3K to MET
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8851954](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8851954)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylated tyrosine residues Y447, Y472 and Y589 of GAB1 are docking sites for the regulatory subunit PIK3R1 of the phosphatidyl inositol-3 kinase (PI3K) complex (Rodrigues et al. 2000). GAB1, phosphorylated by MET, thus recruits PI3K to activated MET (Schaeper et al. 2000).
