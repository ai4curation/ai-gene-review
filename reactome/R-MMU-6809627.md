# I(1,3)P2 is dephosphorylated into I1P by Mtmr7:Mtmr9
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-6809627](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-6809627)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Formation of the complex between mouse Mtmr9 and mouse Mtmr7 results in 2- to 5-fold increase in Mtmr7 inositol-1,3-bisphosphate 3-phosphatase catalytic activity (Mochizuki and Majerus 2003).
