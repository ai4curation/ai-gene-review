# Addition of a third glucose to the N-glycan skeleton by Alg10
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-449661](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-449661)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

The last glucose is added to the N-glycan precursor. This reaction occurs inside the ER lumen and uses Dol-P-Glc as the glucose donor. In yeast, this reaction is catalyzed by ALG10 (Burda P and Aebi M,1998).
