# ABHD14B hydrolyses PNPB
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5695964](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5695964)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Alpha/beta hydrolase domain-containing protein 14B (ABHD14B) can hydrolyse the model substrate p-nitrophenyl butyrate (PNPB) to p-nitrophenol (PNP) and butyric acid (BUT)<br>(Padmanabhan et al. 2004). Its physiological substrate is unknown.
