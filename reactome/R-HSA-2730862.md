# Autophosphorylation of LYN kinase
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2730862](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2730862)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

LYN localized in lipid rafts undergoes an intermolecular autophosphorylation at tyrosine 397. This residue is present in the activation loop, and its phosphorylation promotes LYN kinase activity.
