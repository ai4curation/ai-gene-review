# AhpC reduces peroxidated lipids
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1222526](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1222526)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Reduction of peroxidated lipids depends on reduced AhpC, the only alkyl hydoperoxidase in <i>Mtb</i> (Chauhan & Mande 2001).
