# RHOB binds effectors on endosomes
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9690493](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9690493)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Endosome-bound activated RHOB (RHOB:GTP) recruits the following effectors:<br>DIAPH1 (Fernandez-Borja et al. 2005; Higashi et al. 2008)<br>DIAPH3 (Wallar et al. 2007)<br>RHPN2 (Mircescu et al. 2002).
