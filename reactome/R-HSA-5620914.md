# ARF4:GTP binds VxPx-motifs of membrane proteins
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5620914](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5620914)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ARF4 is a small GTPase that facilitates the targeting of some membrane proteins destined for the cilium. ARF4-mediated targeting of this cargo may depend in part on a VxPx-like motif in the C-terminal tails (Deretic et al, 2005; Geng et al, 2006; Jenkins et al, 2006; Mazelova et al, 2009; Ward et al, 2011; Wang et al, 2012; reviewed in Li et al, 2012). ARF4 is the only ARF protein with a role in ciliogenesis, and its localization at the TGN positions it to act as primary sorting mechanism for membrane proteins destined for the cilium (reviewed in Deretic, 2013; Li et al, 2012). ARF4 is myristoylated at the N-terminus and is membrane-associated in its GTP-bound form (reviewed in Li et al, 2012).
