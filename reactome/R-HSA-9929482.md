# CDK4:CCND1 phosphorylates SPOP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9929482](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9929482)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cyclin D1:CDK4, a cell cycle-dependent kinase complex phosphorylates SPOP on Ser-9 (S9) and induces its sequestration by 14-3-3-gamma protein. This increases stability of PD-L1 (CD274) by inhibiting its degradation by the SPOP:CUL3:RBX1 E3 ubiquitin ligases complex (Zhang et al, 2018)..
