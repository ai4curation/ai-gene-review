# Recruitment of GRB2:Sos1 to P-Shc1:P-Erbb2mut complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-1250500](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-1250500)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Exogenously expressed human GRB2 in complex with mouse Sos1 is recruited to phosphorylated mouse Shc1 bound to rat Neu oncoprotein (mutant Erbb2 with valine 661 replaced with glutamic acid, isolated from rat neuroblastoma) stably expressed in transformed mouse fibroblasts (Xie et al. 1995).
