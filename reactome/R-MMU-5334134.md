# Dnmt3a:Dnmt3l binds chromatin
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-5334134](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-5334134)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Dnmt3l is a non-catalytic homologue of Dnmt3a and Dnmt3b which binds unmethylated lysine-4 of histone H3 (H3K4) and recruits Dnmt3a to DNA (Suetake et al. 2004, Gowher et al. 2005, Nimura et al. 2006, Hu et al. 2009, Zhang et al. 2010). Both Dnmt3l and Dnmt3a interact with unmethylated lysine-4 of histone H3 (Zhang et al. 2010, Li et al. 2011). Dnmt3l and Dnmt3a form tetramers having the subunit structure Dnmt3l:Dnmt3a:Dnmt3a:Dnmt3l (Jia et al. 2007). Dnmt3a without Dnmt3l can form oligomers (Jurkowska et al. 2011, Rajavelu et al. 2012, Jeltsch and Jurkowska 2013). Dnmt3l dissociates from the Dnmt3a:Dnmt3l complex following Dnmt3a binding to DNA. (Gowher et al. 2005).
