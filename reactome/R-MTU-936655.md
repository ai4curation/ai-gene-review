# sulfite results from reduction of APS
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-936655](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-936655)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

Reduction and cleavage of the sulfate moiety of APS happens in two distinct steps. First, the C-terminal cysteine of CysH binds sulfite to form an enzyme-thiosulfonate intermediate, which is then reduced by a thioredoxin  (donating two electrons, thereby freeing two protons) (Williams et al, 2002; Carroll et al, 2005).
