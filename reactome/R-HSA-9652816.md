# Constitutively active MAPK1 mutants are not dephosphorylated by DUSPs
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9652816](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9652816)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Constitutively active forms of MAPK1 have been identified at low frequency in a number of cancers (Ojesina et al, 2014; TCGA, 2015). These mutants are not susceptible to DUSP-mediated dephosphorylation and so promote sustained signaling (Brenan et al, 2016).
