# Expression of CCND1 is stimulated by CTNNB1:TCF4,LEF1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8951443](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8951443)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of the complex of beta-catenin (CTNNB1) and TCF7L2 (TCF4) or LEF1 transcription factors to TCF/LEF binding sites in the promoter of the cyclin D1 (CCND1) gene stimulates CCND1 transcription (Tetsu and McCormick 1999, Shtutman et al. 1999).
