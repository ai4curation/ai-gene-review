# SREBP1A,1C,2 binds Importin beta-1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2065550](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2065550)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SREBP2 dimer (also called SREBF2 dimer) and, by inference SREBP1A,1C dimer, binds Importin beta-1 via the helix-loop-helix leucine zipper domain of SREBP1A,1C,2 (Nagoshi et al. 1999, Di Pardo et al. 2020).
