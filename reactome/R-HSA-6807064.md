# DHCR24 reduces ZYMOL to ZYMSTNL
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6807064](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6807064)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DHCR24 (delta(24)-sterol reductase) associated with the endoplasmic reticulum membranecatalyzes the reduction of ZYMOL (zymosterol) to ZYMSTNL (zymostenol) (Kandutsch & Russell 1960; Mitsche et al. 2015; Waterham et al. 2001).
