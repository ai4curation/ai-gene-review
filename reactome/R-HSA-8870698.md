# FASLG (FasL) gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8870698](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8870698)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Transcription of the FASLG (FasL) gene, encoding the death receptor ligand, is stimulated by FOXO3 phosphorylated by aberrantly activated CDK5 (Shi et al. 2016).
