# Recruitment of Pak to Dock
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-428527](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-428527)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

In Drosophila, Dock bound to Robo1 recruits Pak to specific sites at the growth cone membrane, where Pak, activated by Rac1, regulates the recycling and retrograde flow of actin filaments (Galisteo et al. 1996, Fan et al. 2003).
