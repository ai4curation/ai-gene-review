# Trimethylation of CD274 gene chromatin by MLL1/3 complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909628](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909628)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MLL-1 and MLL-3 induced the CD274 gene promotor/enhancer tri-methylation(H3K4me3) thus regulating its expresion positively. Hypermethylation of CD274 by MLL1 and MLL3 is obeserved in pancreatic cancer cells and PC3 cells. (Lu et al.,2017, Xiong et al., 2019)
