# TRPC1 translocates calcium from the extracellular region to the cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2089943](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2089943)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TRPC1 forms a channel that transports Ca2+ across the plasma membrane. TRPC1 is gated by STIM1 (Ong et al. 2007).
