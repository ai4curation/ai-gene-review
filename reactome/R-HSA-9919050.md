# Furin cleaves prM:E
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9919050](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9919050)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Shortly before the virion exits the cell, at the low pH conditions of the late secretory pathway, the trimers of Dengue virus prM:E heterodimers change their conformation such that the host's furin (FURIN) protease is able to cleave them, releasing the pr peptide. This reaction is essential for the activation/maturation of the virion (Stadler et al., 1997, using tick-borne encephalitis virus, TBEV; Zheng et al., 2014, using DENV-1). Virion maturation exposes a lipid pocket on E proteins, enabling it to bind (more) phosphatidylethanolamine (PE), and possibly improving the stabilization of the virion (Renner et al., 2021).<br><br>Luteolin is a flavonoid, occuring in a wide spectrum of plants. It inhibits furin protease activity in vitro and reduces DENV-2 (EDEN2 strain) viremia in mice (Peng et al., 2017). From this result we infer that furin cleavage occurs in DENV-2.<br><br>Niclosamide is an approved anthelminthic and causes endosomal deacidification, suppressing virion membrane fusion on entry and virion maturation on release (Kao et al., 2018).
