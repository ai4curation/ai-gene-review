# GLUT1:ATP tetramer dissociates to GLUT1 tetramer and 4xATP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-450092](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-450092)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

GLUT1:ATP complexes reversibly dissociate, restoring the glucose transport activity of GLUT1 glucose transport, with the result that depletion of cellular ATP leads to increased glucose uptake (Blodgett et al. 2007).
