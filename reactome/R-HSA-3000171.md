# Non-integrin membrane-ECM interactions
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3000171](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3000171)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Several non-integrin membrane proteins interact with extracellular matrix proteins. Transmembrane proteoglycans may associate with integrins and growth factor receptors to influence their function, or they can signal independently, often influencing the actin cytoskeleton.
