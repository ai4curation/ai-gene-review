# Dissociation of CI from COS
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-216133](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-216133)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Coastal2 (COS) phosphorylated on Ser572 has reduced affinity for Cubitus Interruptus (CI).  This may contribute to partial dissociation of CI from COS complexes.
