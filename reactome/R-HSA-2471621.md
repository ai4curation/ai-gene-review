# Endostatin degradation by cathepsins
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2471621](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2471621)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

As well as generating endostatin from collagen XVIII, cathepsins L and B quickly degrade it, as do cathepsins D and K. In contrast MMPs that produce endostatin do not cleave it further (Ferreras et al. 2000).
