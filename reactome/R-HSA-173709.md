# C7 binds C5b:C6
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-173709](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-173709)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The C5b:C6 complex binds C7. Normally the C5b:C6 complex remains bound to the C3/C5 convertase until it binds a single C7 molecule.
