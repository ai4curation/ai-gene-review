# ARTN binds RET:GFRA1,GFRA3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8853800](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8853800)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Artemin (ARTN) is a ligand for GDNF family receptor-alpha (GFRA) 1 and 3, preferentially binding GFRA3 (Baloh et al. 1998).
