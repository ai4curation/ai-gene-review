# NS5 binds mTORC1,2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9947739](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9947739)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DENV NS5 binds both complexes mTORC1 and mTORC2 (TORC1, TORC2), modulating mTOR signaling during infection (Carpp et al., 2014; Carter et al., 2022; reviewed in Zhang et al., 2025). This interaction apparently stabilizes the mTORC2 complex, amplifies signaling through Akt, boosts cell survival, and facilitates optimal viral propagation (Carter et al., 2022).
