# NADPH transfers electrons to FDXR
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2395517](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2395517)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Two electrons and one proton are transferred from NADPH to the FAD moiety of ferredoxin reductase. A proton from the medium is also taken up by ferredoxin reductase.
