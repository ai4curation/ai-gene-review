# Engulfment of particulate antigen into phagocytic vesicle
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1236956](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1236956)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

After internalization, F-actin is depolymerized from the phagosome, and the newly denuded vacuole membrane becomes accessible to early endosomes (Aderem & Underhill. 1999).
