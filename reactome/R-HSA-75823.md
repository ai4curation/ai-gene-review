# Relocalization of nuclearly localized Cyclin D1 to the cytoplasm
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-75823](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-75823)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In this reaction, 1 molecule of  'phospho(T286)-Cyclin D1' is translocated from nucleoplasm to cytosol.<br><br>This reaction takes place in the 'nuclear envelope' (Diehl et al. 1997).<br>
