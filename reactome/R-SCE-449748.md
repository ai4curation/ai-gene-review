# Addition of a third mannose to the N-glycan precursor by Alg2
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-449748](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-449748)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

A third mannose is added to the N-glycan precursor skeleton by alg2 using its alpha1,6-mannosyltransferase activity. This has been demonstrated in yeast (O'Reilly MK et al, 2006; Kämpf M et al, 2009).
