# PIP2 to PIP3 conversion by PI3K bound to phosphorylated heterodimer of ERBB2 and ERBB4 CYT1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1306979](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1306979)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Active PI3K in complex with phosphorylated ERBB2:ERBB4cyt1 heterodimer phosphorylates PIP2 into PIP3, leading to activation of AKT signaling.
