# RUNX1 binds ELF2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938930](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938930)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds to ELF2 (NERF), a member of the ETS family of transcription factors. The interaction involves a basic amino acid region upstream of the ETS domain of ELF2 and the runt domain of RUNX1 (Cho et al. 2004).
