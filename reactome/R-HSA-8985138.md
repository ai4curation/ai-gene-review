# OAS2 binds viral dsRNA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8985138](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8985138)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The human oligoadenylate synthetase 2 (OAS2) is activated by double-stranded RNA (dsRNA) (Hovanessian AG et al. 1988; Sarkar SN et al., 1999, 2002; Behera AK et al. 2002; Kristiansen H et al. 2011).
