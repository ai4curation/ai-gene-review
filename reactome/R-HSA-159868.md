# Furin cleaves pro-factor VII to factor VII
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-159868](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-159868)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Furin, associated with the Golgi membrane, cleaves pro-factor VII to form mature, gamma-carboxylated factor VII (Wasley et al. 1993).
