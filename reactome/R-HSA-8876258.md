# CBL monoubiquitinates InlB-bound MET
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8876258](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8876258)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CBL promotes monoubiquitination of MET receptor that is activated by binding of the Listeria monocytogenes cell wall protein InlB. CBL-mediated monoubiquitination of MET promotes endocytosis and entry of Listeria monocytogenes into host cells (Veiga and Cossart 2005).
