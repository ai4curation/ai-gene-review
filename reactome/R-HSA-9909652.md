# NFE2L2 binding to CD274 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909652](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909652)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

 NFE2L2 (NRF2/(Nuclear Factor Erythroid 2–Related Factor 2) regulates CD274 (PD-L1) transcription by binding to antioxidant response elements (AREs) in the PD-L1 promoter region. Upon activation by oxidative stress or electrophilic compounds, NFE2L2 translocates to the nucleus and binds to AREs, recruiting transcriptional co-activators (CEBP/p300) and machinery to initiate PD-L1 gene transcription. (Duan et al., 2023, Payandeh et al., 2021, Zhu et al., 2018)
