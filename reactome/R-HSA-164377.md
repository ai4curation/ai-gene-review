# Activated Adenylate cyclase catalyses cAMP synthesis
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-164377](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-164377)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated adenylate cyclase associated with the plasma membrane catalyzes the reaction of cytosolic ATP to form 3',5'-cyclicAMP and pyrophosphate (Martin et al. 1987).
