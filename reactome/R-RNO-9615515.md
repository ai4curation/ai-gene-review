# p-S133-Creb1 binds Mir132 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-RNO-9615515](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-RNO-9615515)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

Endogenous rat Creb1 binds to evolutionarily conserved cAMP response elements (CREs) in the promoter region of the endogenous rat Mir132 gene, encoding microRNA miR-132 (Vo et al. 2005).
