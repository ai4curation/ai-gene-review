# MTMR6 binds MTMR9
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6809309](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6809309)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MTMR6 binds to  MTMR9, an enzymatically inactive myotubularin family member, which leads to increased catalytic activity of MTMR6 (Zou et al. 2009).
