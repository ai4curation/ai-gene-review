# ANGPTL4 binds to CDH11v
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9762333](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9762333)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The variant isoform of CDH11 (CDH11v), which does not possess the cytoplasmic domain and does not bind CTNNB1, can compete with the canonical CDH11 for cANGPTL4 binding (Teo et al. 2017).
