# SERPINC1 binds heparan sulfate
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9823290](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9823290)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Antithrombin III, encoded by the SERPINC1 gene, is a serine protease inhibitor in plasma that inactivates thrombin and other proteases involved in blood clotting (e.g. factor Xa) resulting in an overall decrease in clotting ability (Holmer et al., 1986; Eriksson et al., 1995; van Boven & Lane 1997; Mushunje et al., 2003). Antithrombin III (SERPINC1) binds to membrane-associated glycosaminoglycans such as heparan sulfate on the surface of normal endothelial cells. This binding significantly increases the affinity of antithrombin III (SERPINC1) for thrombin (Holmer et al., 1986; Eriksson et al., 1995; Mushunje et al., 2003; Izaguirre et al., 2007, 2014; Van Walderveen et al., 2010; Atkinson et al., 2024; Olson et al., 2010).
