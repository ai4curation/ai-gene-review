# Rap1 sequesters Raf1 to inhibit ERK cascade
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-392835](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-392835)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Active Rap1 binds but does not activate Raf-1, preventing Raf-1 from participating in the Ras/ERK pathway.
