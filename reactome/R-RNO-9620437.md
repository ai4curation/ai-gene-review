# Camk2 phosphorylates GluA1 (Gria1)
**Reactome ID:** [R-RNO-9620437](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-RNO-9620437)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

Recombinant rat Camk2a phosphorylates recombinant rat GluA1 (Gria1) (McGlade-McCulloh et al. 1993, Barria et al. 1997, Mammen et al. 1997) on serine residue S849 (Mammen et al. 1997).
