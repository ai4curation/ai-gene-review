# prM binds DYNLT1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9921297](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9921297)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The ectodomain of the DENV membrane protein (prM) binds Dynein light chain Tctex-type 1 (DYNLT1), which is localized at perinuclear regions in infected human cells. Silencing DYNLT1 inhibited the production of recombinant subviral particles (RSPs) in a human cell line expressing DENV-1 proteins. The binding interaction was demonstrated for several DENV serotypes, including the DENV-2 N.1409 strain (Brault et al., 2011).
