# YAP1/TAZ binding to CD274 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909654](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909654)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

YAP (Yes-associated protein) and TAZ (Transcriptional co-activator with PDZ-binding motif) regulate CD274 (PD-L1) transcription via the Hippo signaling pathway. When Hippo signaling is inactive, YAP/TAZ translocate to the nucleus and interact with TEAD transcription factors. The YAP/TAZ-TEAD complex binds to specific sites in the PD-L1 promoter, recruiting the transcriptional machinery, thereby enhancing PD-L1 transcription. This increased PD-L1 expression on tumor cells inhibits T cell activity, facilitating immune evasion (Janse Van Rensburg et al., 2018, Han et al.,2021, Kim et al., 2018).
