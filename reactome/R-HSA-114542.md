# RAC1,RAC2,RHOG activate PI3K
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-114542](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-114542)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PIP3 produced by PI3K activity is essential for receptor-driven stimulation of Rac activation, but PI3K also lies downstream of Rac, as Rac1 can form a complex with PI3K alpha leading to its activation.
