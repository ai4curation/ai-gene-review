# p-PKR dimer phosphorylates SNCA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9836435](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9836435)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated protein kinase R (PKR, EIF2AK2) can phosphorylate alpha-synuclein (SNCA) on S129 and other residues. In synucleopathies (Parkinson disease, Lewy body dementias), about 90% of aggregated SNCA found in inclusion bodies are phosphorylated. Inhibition or overexpression of PKR in cells has decreasing or increasing effect, respectively, on cellular degeneration (Reimer et al, 2018; Reimer et al, 2022).
