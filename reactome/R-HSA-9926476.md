# UBE2I sumoylates MITF-M
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926476](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926476)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M is sumoylated at residues 182 and 316 (Miller et al, 2005; Murakami and Arnheiter, 2005). Sumoylation depends on the E1 heterodimer SAE1:UBA2 and on the E2 UBE2I (also known as UBC9). SUMO transfer to MITF-M may be mediated by UBE2I directly, or may require the activity of an E3 ligase such as PIAS, but the evidence is conflicting (Miller et al, 2005; Murakami and Arnheiter, 2005).<br>Sumoylation decreases the transcription factor activity of MITF-M at promoters containing multiple MITF-binding sites, but not at those with only a single site (Miller et al, 2005; Murakami and Arnheiter, 2005).
