# Activation of highly calcium permeable postsynaptic nicotinic acetylcholine receptors
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-629595](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-629595)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Acetylcholine binding activates postsynaptic acetylchloine receptors that show Ca2+ currents which facilitate the process of long term potentiation (LTP).
