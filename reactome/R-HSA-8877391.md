# FOXP3 in complex with RUNX1 binds intron 1 of the IL2RA (CD25) gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8877391](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8877391)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

FOXP3 bound to the RUNX1:CBFB complex binds the first intron of the IL2RA (CD25) gene (Ono et al. 2007). It is probable that FOXP3 acts as part of a complex with NFATC2 (Wu et al. 2006).
