# PAK1-3 dimer disassociates
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5357445](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5357445)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of Rac1 increases p21 activated kinases 1-3 (PAK1-3) kinase activity and breaks the PAK dimer into monomers.
