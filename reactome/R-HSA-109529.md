# Equilibrative transport (export) of nucleosides and free bases by solute carrier family 29 (nucleoside transporters), member 2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-109529](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-109529)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The plasma membrane-associated protein SLC29A2 mediates the reversible transport of one molecule of adenine, adenosine, cytidine, cytosine, guanine, guanosine, hypoxanthine, inosine, thymidine, thymine, uracil, or uridine from the cytosol to the extracellular space.
