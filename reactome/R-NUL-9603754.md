# PTPRS dephosphorylates Ntrk3
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9603754](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9603754)
**Species:** Gallus gallus

## Summary

Recombinant chicken protein tyrosine phosphatase PTPRS (PTPsigma) binds to and dephosphorylates recombinant rat Ntrk3 (TrkC) activated by recombinant human NTF3 (NT-3) (Faux et al. 2007).
