# RHOA:GTP:RTKN binds TAX1BP3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5666216](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5666216)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The PDZ domain of TAX1BP3 (TIP-1) binds the C-terminus of RTKN (rhotekin) associated with activated RHOA (RHOA:GTP). Binding of TAX1BP3 to the RHOA:GTP:RTKN complex stimulates SRF-mediated transcription through an unknown mechanism (Reynaud et al. 2000).
