# Expression of TWINKLE (TWNK, PEO1)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1592239](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1592239)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The TWINKLE (TWNK, PEO1) gene is transcribed to yield mRNA and the mRNA is translated in the cytosol to yield precursor protein. TWNK protein is imported into the mitochondrial matrix where it may play a role in DNA replication.
