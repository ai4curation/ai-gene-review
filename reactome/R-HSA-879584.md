# SLCO3A1 isoform 1 has a broad substrate specificity
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-879584](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-879584)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The human gene SLCO3A1 encodes the organic anion transporting polypeptide D. Several variants are expressed but isoform 1 is ubiquitous and can transport a range of substrates including the prostaglandins E1 and E2, thyroxine and vasopressin (AVP) (Huber RD et al, 2007).
