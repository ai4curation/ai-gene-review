# Formation of MSH1:PMS2 Complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5444523](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5444523)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The heterodimer of MLH1 and PMS2 and is required for mismatch repair. The complex is known as MutL with mutations causing deficiencies in DNA mismatch repair.
