# Dnmt3b:Dnmt3l binds chromatin
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-5336403](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-5336403)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Dnmtl associates with Dnmt3b (Van Emburgh and Robertson 2011). Dnmt3b also binds the unmodified N-terminal region (amino acid residues 1-19) of histone H3 (Zhang et al. 2010).
