# NS2B3 cleaves FAM134B
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9921595](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9921595)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DENV protease NS2B:NS3 (NS2B3) cleaves Reticulophagy regulator 1 (RETREG1, FAM134B), inhibiting its oligomerization and blocking the formation of ER and DENV protein-enriched autophagosomes. This suggests that the cleavage of FAM134B serves to specifically suppress the reticulophagy pathway (Lennemann & Coyne, 2017). Expression of BPI fold-containing family B member 3 (BPIFB3) is required for DENV and ZIKV infection by regulating the availability of ER membranes for viral remodeling of the ER. Silencing BPIFB3 enhances FAM134B-mediated reticulophagy, which decreases the availability of ER membranes for flavivirus replication (Evans et al., 2020).
