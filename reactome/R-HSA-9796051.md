# AcK-NFE2L2 binds the AREG gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796051](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796051)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The knockdown of NFE2L2 and KEAP1 via short hairpin RNA in BEAS-2B cells and an EMSA with lung tissue revealed that AREG is regulated by NFE2L2 (Reiss et al, 2014).
