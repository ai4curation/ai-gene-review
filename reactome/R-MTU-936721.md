# Cysteine synthesis from O-acetylserine
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-936721](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-936721)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

The cysteine biosynthetic pathway using the intermediate O-acetylserine is well preserved among bacteria, and it is also the main pathway in <i>M. tuberculosis</i> in normal medium. It depends on a six electron reduction of sulfite to sulfide (Bhave et al, 2007; Pinto et al, 2007).
