# Activation of STAT5a/b by JAK2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-879909](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-879909)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

JAK2 phosphorylates STAT5; phosphorylated STAT5 dimerizes and translocates to the nucleus (Darnell et al., 1994), binds DNA and activates target genes including c-fos, pim-1, oncostatin M, and Id-1 (Mui et al. 1996). STAT5 activation is believed to be the primary signaling mechanism for Bc (Ihle, 2001).
