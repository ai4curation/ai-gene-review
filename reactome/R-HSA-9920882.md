# NS2B binds CGAS
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9920882](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9920882)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DENV NS2B binds to cyclic GMP-AMP synthase (CGAS, MB21D1), leading to drastic degradation of CGAS, which decreases the activation of STING in the infected cell (Aguirre et al., 2017).
