# PTK6 Down-Regulation
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8849472](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8849472)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The kinase activity of PTK6 is negatively regulated by both PTPN1 phosphatase (Fan et al. 2013), which dephosphorylates tyrosine Y342 of PTK6, and SRMS kinase (Fan et al. 2015), which phosphorylates PTK6 on tyrosine residue Y447.
