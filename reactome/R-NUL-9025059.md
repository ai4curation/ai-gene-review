# NTF3 binds Ntrk2
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9025059](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9025059)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

A recombinant human NTF3 binds to and activates a recombinant mouse Ntrk2 (TrkB) (Klein et al. 1991). Activation of rat Ntrk2 by human NTF3 binding was also demonstrated (Soppet et al. 1991).
