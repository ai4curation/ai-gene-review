# SIRT5 deglutarylates CPS1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9955504](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9955504)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CPS1 is glutarylated at multiple lysine residues in mouse and Hela cells (Tan et al, 2014). Deglutarylation of at least 8 of these sites (K55, K219, K412, K889, K892, K915, K1360 and K1486) is mediated by SIRT5. Knockdown of SIRT5 leads to hyperglutarylation of CPS1 and reduced CPS1 enzymatic activity, consistent with a role for SIRT5 in activating the enzyme. Prolonged culturing of SIRT5 knockdown cells results in an accumulation of ammonia in the media and eventual toxicity (Tan et al, 2014).
