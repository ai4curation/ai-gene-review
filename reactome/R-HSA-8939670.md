# RUNX2:CBFB binds the ITGA5 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8939670](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8939670)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

RUNX2, presumably associated with CBFB, binds to OSE2 (osteoblast-specific cis-acting element 2) sites in the promoter of the ITGA5 gene, encoding Integrin alpha-5 (Li et al. 2016).
