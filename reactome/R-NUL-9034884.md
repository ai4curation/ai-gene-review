# Activated Ntrk3 binds Shc1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9034884](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9034884)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Rat Ntrk3, activated by human NTF3 (NT-3), binds mouse Shc1 isoforms p52 (Shc1-2) and p46 (Shc1-3), which function as activators of Ras signaling (Yuen and Mobley 1999).
