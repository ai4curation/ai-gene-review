# ULK1 phosphorylates ATG13 and RB1CC1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5675868](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5675868)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ULK1 (and ULK2) can phosphorylate both ATG13 and RB1CC1 (Hosakawa et al. 2009, Jung et al. 2009). Similarly Drosophila Atg1, the orthologue of ULK1, phosphorylates Drosophila Atg13 (Chang & Neufeld 2009). This phosphorylation is believed to be activating,  opposing the inactivating phosphorylation of ATG13 by mTORC1 (Kamada et al. 2000, Hosakawa et al. 2009,Chang & Neufeld 2009, Jung et al. 2009) under conditions of nutrient sufficiency.
