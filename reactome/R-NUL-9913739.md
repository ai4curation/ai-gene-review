# DMD binds ACTA1,ACTG1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9913739](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9913739)
**Species:** Bos taurus

## Summary

Based on studies with human DMD and bovine actin from skeletal muscle (ACTA1) or brain (ACTG1), DMD interacts with actin through its N-terminal domain (Renley et al, 1998; Rybakova et al, 1996; Rybakova and Ervasti, 1997).
