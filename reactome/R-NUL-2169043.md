# Smad7:SMURF2 binds phosphorylated TGFBR1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2169043](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2169043)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Smad7 recruits SMURF2 to TGF-beta (TGFB1)-activated TGF-beta receptor complex. In this study, recombinant mouse Smad7 and recombinant human SMURF2, TGFBR2 and TGFBR1 were exogenously expressed in COS1 cells (Kavsak et al. 2000).
