# NRF1, PPRC1:HCFC1:NRF2 bind the TFB1M promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1592250](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1592250)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Both PRC (PPRC1) and NRF2 bind HCF1 (Vercauteren et al. 2008). PRC, like PGC-1alpha, can coactivate NRF2 (Gleyzer et al. 2005).
