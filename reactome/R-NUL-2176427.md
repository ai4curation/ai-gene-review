# NEDD4L ubiquitinates Smad7 and TGFBR1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2176427](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2176427)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NEDD4L, recruited to the activated TGF-beta receptor complex by Smad7, ubiquitinates TGFBR1. Ubiquitination of Smad7 by NEDD4L has not been examined in this context (Kuratomi et al. 2005).
