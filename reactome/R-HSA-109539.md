# Concentrative transport (import) of nucleosides plus sodium ions by solute carrier family 28 (sodium-coupled nucleoside transporter), member 2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-109539](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-109539)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The plasma membrane-associated protein SLC28A2 mediates the transport of one molecule of adenosine, guanosine, inosine, or uridine, and one sodium ion, from the extracellular space to the cytosol.
