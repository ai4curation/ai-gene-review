# NCL1 (TRM4) methylates cytidine-34 of unspliced tRNA(Leu) yielding 5-methylcytidine-34
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-6790189](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-6790189)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

NCL1 (TRM4) transfers a methyl group from S-adenosylmethionine to the 5 position of cytidine-34  in unspliced tRNA(Leu) (Motorin and Grosjean 1999).
