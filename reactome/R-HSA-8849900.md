# SALMs1-3 bind each other in cis interactions
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8849900](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8849900)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SALM1, SALM2, and SALM3 form homo- and heteromeric complexes in a cis manner. These SALMs 1-3 coimmunoprecipitate with each other in extracts from brain. Whether the homo  and heteromeric complexes formed between SALMs 1-3 contribute to synapse formation or neurite outgrowth remains to be determined (Seabold et al. 2008).
