# ABCG2 tetramer transports BMG,BDG from cytosol to extracellular region
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9661417](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9661417)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Bilirubin glucuronides (BMG and BDG) are transported out of hepatocytes through their apical surfaces into the bile ducts, mainly by ABCC2 (MRP2) but also by the tetrameric efflux pump ATP-binding cassette sub-family G member 2 (ABCG2) (Xu et al. 2004).
