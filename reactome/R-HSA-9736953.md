# FURIN translocates to the plasma membrane
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9736953](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9736953)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

While FURIN mainly localizes to the Golgi apparatus/trans-Golgi network sites (TGN), it also localizes to the plasma membrane and endosomes and cycles between these subcellular compartments (Mayer et al. 2004).
