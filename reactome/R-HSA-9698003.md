# FLT3 mutants phosphorylate GAB2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9698003](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9698003)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

GAB2 is phosphorylated by the activated FLT3 receptor. Phosphorylated GAB2 interacts with PTPN11 and the regulatory subunit of PI3K and promotes signaling through AKT and STAT5  (Zhang et al, 2000; Masson et al, 2009; Chonabayashi et al, 2013; reviewed in Kazi and Ronnstrand, 2019).
