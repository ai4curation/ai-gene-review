# TP53 stimulates MDM2 transcription
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3700992](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3700992)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of TP53 (p53) to the p53 response element in the first intron of the MDM2 gene stimulates MDM2 transcription (Wu et al. 1993).
