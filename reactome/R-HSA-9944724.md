# TCF19:NuRD represses G6PC gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9944724](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9944724)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

G6PC encodes the catalytic subunit of glucose-6-phosphatase, a key enzyme in gluconeogenesis (reviewed in Shah and Wondisford, 2023; Bian et al, 2022). G6PC expression is repressed under high glucose conditions by the binding of TCF19 to H3K4me3 marks at an insulin-responsive IRE element in its promoter (Sen et al, 2017; Mondal et al, 2020). TCF19 recruits the NuRD complex to the gene as assessed by ChIP, and contributes to repression through the removal of H4K5Ac, H4K8Ac and H3K9Ac marks, associated with active chromatin (Sen et al, 2017).
