# DraE binds Collagen IV
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9638628](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9638628)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The Dr family of adhesins of E. coli are associated with urinary tract infections and diarrhea. The Dr haemagglutinin DraE specifically binds to type IV collagens (Collagen IV) on colonic, bronchial and urinary tract tissue (Nowicki et al.1988, Carnoy & Moseley 1997).
