# EPM2A dimer binds PPP1R3C:phosphoglycogen-GYG1 complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3781001](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3781001)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

EPM2A (laforin) dimer binds to the PPP1R3C:phosphoglycogen-GYG1 (glycogenin 1) complex, probably through interactions with the PPP1R3C (PTG) and glycogen moieties of the complex (Worby et al. 2008).
