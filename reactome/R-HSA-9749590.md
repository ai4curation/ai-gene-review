# ASA- acetylates ALB
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9749590](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9749590)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Acetylsalicylate (ASA-) acetylates several lysine residues on albumin, which subsequently release their acetate over a long time span. This pseudo-esterase activity contributes to overall aspirin esterase activity in blood plasma (Hawkins et al, 1969; Liyasova et al, 2010).
