# Collagen type II degradation by MMP15
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2473594](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2473594)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The membrane-type MMP MMP15 (MT2-MMP) is a fibrillar collagenase. MMP15 is able to degrade collagen type I (Morrison & Overall 2006) and believed able to degrade types II and III (Somerville et al. 2003).
