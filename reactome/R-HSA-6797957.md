# SLC22A4 transports BET from mitochondrial matrix to cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6797957](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6797957)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SLC22A4 (Solute carrier family 22 member 4) associated with the mitochondrial membrane transports betaine (BET) plus a sodium ion from the mitochondrial matrix to the cytosol (Lamhonwah & Tein 2006, Wagner et al. 2000, Yee et al. 2020).
