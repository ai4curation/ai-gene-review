# Phosphorylated HPO dimer phosphorylates TH
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-390120](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-390120)

## Summary

Activated Hippo (HPO) serine/threonine kinase phosphorylates Thread (TH) aka drosophila inhibitor of apoptosis protein 1 (Diap1) at unknown residues.  This affects TH's stability.
