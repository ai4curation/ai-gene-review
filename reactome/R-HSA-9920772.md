# prM binds IFIH1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9920772](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9920772)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Dengue virus serotype 2 (DENV2) prM protein binds to melanoma differentiation–associated protein 5 (MDA5, IFIH1) and suppresses MDA5-induced type I interferon production (Sui et al., 2023).
