# Integrin alpha4beta1 binds JAM2:JAM3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-202706](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-202706)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Several key IgSF cell adhesion molecules engage integrin and in so doing impact on the multi-step paradigm of leukocyte emigration. The interaction between JAM2 (JAM-B) and Integrin alpha4beta1 (VLA-4) requires prior binding of JAM2 to JAM3 (JAM-C).
