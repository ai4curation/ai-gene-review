# CHD1 and CHD2 bind H3K4me3 nucleosomes
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9943412](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9943412)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CHD1 and CHD2 bind to H3K4me3 nucleosomes associated with transcriptionally active regions, although CHD2 binds with significantly lower affinity than CHD1 (Sims et al, 2005; Sims et al, 2007; Flanagan et al, 2005; Flanagan et al, 2007). H3K4me3 binding appears to be mediated at least in part by the N-terminal chromodomains of the proteins (Sims et al, 2005; Sims et al, 2007; Flanagan et al, 2005; Flanagan et al, 2007; Morettini et al, 2011; Kelley et al, 1999). Whether H3K4me3 binding is sufficient for recruitment of CHD to promoters in vivo is not clear; recruitment may instead be mediated by interaction with the RNA polymerase II transcriptional machinery (Siggens et al, 2015). CHD1 and CHD2 have also been associated with transcriptional elongational and splicing factors (Simic et al, 2003; Smolle et al, 2012; Sims et al, 2007; Lee et al, 2017). Studies in Drosophila, yeast, and humans have suggested that CHD1 interacts with Mediator and other chromatin modifying complexes such as FACT (a heterodimer of SSRP1 and SUPT16H in humans), the SAGA and PAF complexes (Kelley et al, 1999; Pray-Grant et al, 2005, Dey et al, 2011).
