# CDC42 translocates to the endoplasmic reticulum membrane
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9692800](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9692800)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CDC42 mainly localizes to the plasma membrane but it can also be found on endomembranes, such as endoplasmic reticulum membrane (Michaelson et al. 2001). The trafficking mechanism is not known.
