# Disassembly of the destabilised 'destruction complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209081](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209081)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The destabilised 'destruction complex' disassembles into the cytoplasm.  The cytoplasmic concentration of Axin (AXN) has now decreased.
