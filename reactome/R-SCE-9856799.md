# HFD1 oxidizes 4-HBz
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-9856799](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-9856799)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

Yeast dehydrogenase HFD1 catalyzes the oxidation of 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde (4-HBz) to 4-hydroxybenzoate (PHB) (Payet et al., 2016; Stefely et al., 2016).
