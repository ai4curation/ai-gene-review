# Arachidonate is epoxidated to 5,6-EET by CYP(4)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2161890](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2161890)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Several cytochrome P450s (CYPs) convert arachidonate to 5,6-epoxyeicosatrienoic acid (5,6-EET).  The CYPs and their references are as follows: CYP1A1, CYP1A2, CYP1B1 (Choudhary et al. 2004); CYP2J2 (Wu et al. 1996).
