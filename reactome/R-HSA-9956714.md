# CASP1 cleaves GBP1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9956714](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9956714)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Caspase-1 (CASP1) cleaves guanylate-binding protein 1 (GBP1) at aspartate 192 (D192), generating fragments that lose the ability to target intracellular Salmonella Typhimurium, recruit caspase-4 (CASP4), or promote pyroptosis. This cleavage is thought to serve as a feedback mechanism to balance CASP1- and CASP4-mediated cell death during Salmonella infection, but does not occur during Toxoplasma gondii infection, indicating pathogen-specific regulation (Naschberger E et al., 2017; Fisch D et al., 2020).
