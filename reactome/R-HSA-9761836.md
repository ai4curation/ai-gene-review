# p14ARF binds NFE2L2:CREBBP, EP300
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9761836](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9761836)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

p14ARF binds to NFE2L2 and inhibits its CREBBP/EP300-dependent acetylation (Chen et al, 2017). Acetylation has been shown to contribute to DNA-binding and therefore transcription factor activity at some NFE2L2 target genes (Sun et al, 2009). Consistent with this, binding of p14ARF to NFE2L2 inhibits the expression of SLC7A11, an NFE2L2 target with roles in suppressing ferroptosis (Chen et al, 2017; Ishii et al, 2000; reviewed in Baird and Yamamoto, 2020; Kuang et al, 2020; Kajarabille and Latunda-Dada, 2019).
