# Cbx4 (Pc2) Sumoylates Dnmt3a with Sumo1
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-4655387](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-4655387)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Cbx4 (Pc2) sumoylates Dnmt3a with Sumo1 at more than one site in the N-terminal (regulatory) domain (Ling et al. 2004, Li et al. 2007). Sumoylation reduces the interaction of Dnmt3a with Hdac1 and Hdac2.
