# Thrombin-activated PARs binds G-protein Gq
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-396996](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-396996)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Thrombin signalling through PARs is mediated in part through the Gq family of G-proteins. Gq knockout mice have defective platelet responses to thrombin (as well as to ADP and thromboxane).
