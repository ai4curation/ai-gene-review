# IL13RA1 binds TYK2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6785762](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6785762)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Interleukin-13 receptor subunit alpha-1 (IL13RA1) constitutively associates with Non-receptor tyrosine-protein kinase 2 (TYK2) (Umeshita-Suyama et al. 2000, Roy et al. 2002, LaPorte et al. 2008, Bhattacharjee et al. 2013).
