# PP1CC dephosphorylates TGFBR1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2167872](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2167872)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylated TGFBR1 is significantly dephosphorylated when incubated with Smad7, PPP1R15A (GADD34) and PP1CC, immunoprecipitated from previously transfected COS1 cells. Dephosphorylation is inhibited by addition of PP1 inhibitor I-1 (Shi et al. 2004).
