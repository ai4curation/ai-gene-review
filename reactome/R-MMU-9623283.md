# Pck1 gene expression is stimulated by Foxo1:Ppargc1a
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9623283](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9623283)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

In the mouse liver, Foxo1 and Ppargc1a (Pgc-1alpha) directly stimulate transcription of the Pck1 gene, encoding a gluconeogenesis enzyme Phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase (Puigserver et al. 2003).
