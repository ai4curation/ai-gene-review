# fibrinogen + β-amyloid fibril:Zn2+ -> fibrinogen:β-amyloid fibril:Zn2+
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9936699](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9936699)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The interaction between amyloid beta (Aβ) peptide aggregates and fibrin or fibrinogen results in the formation of abnormal blood clots that are more stable and resistant to fibrinolysis. This process may contribute to the vascular pathology observed in Alzheimer's disease. The Aβ peptides described here are APP(672-711) and APP(672-713).
