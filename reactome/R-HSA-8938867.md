# CCND3,(CCND1,CCND2) binds RUNX1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938867](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938867)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cyclin D3 (CCND3) binds to the runt domain and the activation domain (AD) of RUNX1, thus inhibiting RUNX1 association with CBFB and RUNX1 binding to DNA. Based on in vitro studies, cyclins D1 (CCND1) and D2 (CCND2) can also bind to RUNX1 (Peterson et al. 2005).
