# Activated Adenylyl cyclase synthesizes cyclic AMP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-381607](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-381607)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated adenylyl cyclase catalyzes the conversion of one molecule of ATP to one molecule of 3',5'-cyclic AMP (cAMP) and one molecule of pyrophosphate.
