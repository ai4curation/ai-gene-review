# Relocalization of nuclearly localized phospho-(T286):cyclin D1:Cdk4 to cytoplasm
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-75822](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-75822)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In this reaction, 1 molecule of  'phospho(T286)-Cyclin D1:Cdk4' is translocated from nucleoplasm to cytosol.<br><br>This reaction takes place in the 'nuclear envelope' (Dhiel et al.1994).<br>
