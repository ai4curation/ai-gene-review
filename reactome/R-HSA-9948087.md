# CAMK2 complex phosphorylates VIM:NS4A
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9948087](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9948087)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CAMK2 phosphorylates Vimentin at the Ser-39 residue, which is of major importance in maintaining and regulating vimentin structure (Eriksson et al., 2004; Teo & Chu, 2014). We assume the interaction happens in the ER.
