# C3 binds C3 inhibitors
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9957021](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9957021)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Uncontrolled activation of the complement system significantly contributes to the pathogenesis of various acute and chronic diseases. C3 inhibitors, such as compstatin-based cyclic peptides, bind directly to complement component C3 and prevent its cleavage, thereby blocking the generation of downstream pro-inflammatory effectors. Pegcetacoplan, a member of the compstatin family, is approved for the treatment of conditions such as paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH) and geographic atrophy (GA), an advanced form of age-related macular degeneration (AMD). Other compstatin analogs, including AMY-101, have demonstrated therapeutic potential in both preclinical and clinical studies for complement-related diseases (Mastellos DC et al., 2015, 2022; Berger N et al., 2018; Lamers C et al., 2022; Skendros P et al., 2022; Ricklin D, 2024).
