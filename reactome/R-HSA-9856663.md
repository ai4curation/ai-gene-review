# FOXO3 activates expression of CAT
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9856663](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9856663)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

During the response to unfolded proteins in mitochondria (mtUPR), FOXO3 that is activated by deacetylation translocates to the nucleus and activates expression of the Catalase (CAT) gene (Papa and Germain 2014). FOXO3 may bind the promoter of the CAT gene, but this has not been assayed during mtUPR.
