# IFI27 binds IFIH1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9924831](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9924831)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Interferon alpha-inducible protein 27 (IFI27) may regulate the intensity of innate immune responses induced after viral infections in cell cultures and in vivo (Villamayor L et al., 2023; Rivero V et al., 2024). The molecular mechanism likely involves the interaction of IFI27 with dsRNAs and pattern recognition receptors such as DDX58 (RIG-I) and IFIH1 (MDA5), impairing the receptor activation by viral RNA (Villamayor L et al., 2023; Rivero V et al., 2024).<p>This Reactome event shows interaction between IFI27 and IFIH1(MDA5) in the presence of viral RNA.
