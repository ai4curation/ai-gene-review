# The FA anion diffuses away laterally from UCP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-166387](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-166387)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

At the beginning of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'FA anion:UCP dimer  "head-out" complex' is present. At the end of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'UCP dimer', and 1 molecule of 'Fatty Acid anion "head-out"' are present.<br><br> This reaction takes place in the 'mitochondrial inner membrane' (Garlid et al. 1996).<br>
