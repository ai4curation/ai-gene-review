# MITF-M binds EDIL3 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926486](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926486)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M binds at the EDIL3 gene to repress its expression as assessed by CRISPR- and si/shRNA-mediated MITF-M knockdown, ChIP-seq and CUT-and-RUN DNA binding analysis (Dilshat et al, 2021; Laurette et al, 2015, Webster et al, 2014). EDIL3 (EGF-like repeat and discoidin 1-like domain containing protein 3, also known as DEL1) encodes a secreted protein that binds integrins by virtue of its EGF repeats and with phosphatidylserine and glycosaminoglycans through its discoidin I-like domain (Hidai et al, 1998; Schurpf et al, 2012; Hidai et al, 2007; reviewed in Li et al, 2021). EDIL3 plays roles in inflammation, cell adhesion and invasion (reviewed in Li et al, 2021; Jiang et al, 2024).
