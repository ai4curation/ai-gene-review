# YAP1 binds TEAD2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8871260](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8871260)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The YAP1 transcriptional coactivator can bind any one of the four TEAD transcription factors to form a complex. The stoichiometry of this complex is unknown (Chan et al. 2009).
