# RUNX2:CBFB binds the ITGBL1 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8939833](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8939833)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

RUNX2, presumably associated with CBFB, binds to RUNX2 binding sites in the promoter of the ITGBL1 gene, encoding Integrin beta-like protein 1 (Li et al. 2015).
