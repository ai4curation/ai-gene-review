# CHD1:H3K4me3 interacts with post-initiation transcription complexes
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9943567](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9943567)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CHD1 interacts with complexes associated with promoter escape, transcription elongation and splicing, including the FACT and PAF1C complexes and the U2 snRNP complexes (Kelley et al, 1999; Sims et al, 2007; Dey et al, 2011; Simic et al, 2003; Lee et al, 2017; reviewed in Mills et al, 2017; Alendar and Berns, 2021).
