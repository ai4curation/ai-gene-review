# ESR1:ESTG binds CXXC5 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9018325](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9018325)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ESR1 binds to an ERE at -242 in the promoter of the CXXC5 gene and promotes transcription in an estrogen-dependent fashion. CXXC5 is a member of the zinc finger CXXC family of transcription factors and plays roles in cellular proliferation and differentiation (Nott et al, 2009; Yaşar et al, 2016).
