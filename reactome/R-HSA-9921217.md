# NS1 binds CLU
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9921217](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9921217)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Extracellular NS1 from the Dengue serotype-2 (DENV-2) NG-C strain binds to human clusterin (CLU), a protein that naturally inhibits the formation of the terminal complement complex (TCC). Based on this interaction, the authors hypothesize that NS1 may contribute to plasma leakage in Dengue hemorrhagic fever (DHF) by interfering with the inhibitory function of CLU (Kurosu et al., 2007).
