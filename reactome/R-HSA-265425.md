# Interaction of integrin alphaVbeta3 with von Willbrand Factor
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-265425](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-265425)

## Summary

Integrin alphaVbeta3 interacts with von Willebrand factor (vWF) on the RGD motif (1744-1746 residues) present on the mature vWF subunit.
