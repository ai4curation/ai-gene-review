# Anthrax lef cleaves target cell MAP2K1 (MEK1)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5211340](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5211340)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

lef (Anthrax LF, lethal factor), a zinc metalloprotease (Klimpel et al, 1994) in the target cell cytosol, cleaves MAP2K1 (MEK1, mitogen activated protein kinase kinase 1) at the N-terminus. While the kinase domain of MAP2K1 is unaffected, an aminoterminal docking domain is disrupted by the cleavage and the protein fails to interact normally with substrates (Duesbery et al. 1998; Vitale et al. 1998).
