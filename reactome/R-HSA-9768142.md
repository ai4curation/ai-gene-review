# Dimerized phospho-IRF3, IRF7 is transported to the nucleus
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9768142](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9768142)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylation-induced dimerization and activation of interferon regulatory factor 3 (IRF3)  or IRF7 results in the translocation of IRF3/IRF7 dimer from the cytosol into the nucleus.<p> SARS-CoV-2 6 (ORF6) interacts with importin KPNA2 and components of the nuclear pore complex, NUP98 and RAE1, to block nuclear translocation of transcription factors such as IRF3 (Xia H et al. 2020; Miorin L et al. 2020).
