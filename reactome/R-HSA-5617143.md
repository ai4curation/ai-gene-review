# B4GAT1:LARGE transfers initial GlcA to matriglycan chain
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5617143](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5617143)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Beta-1,4-glucuronyltransferase 1 (B4GAT1) subunit of the B4GAT1:LARGE complex transfers a glucuronyl (GlcA) moiety from UDP-GlcA to the first Xyl moiety of growing matriglycan chains on alpha-dystroglycan (Bao et al., 2009; Praissman et al., 2014; Willer et al., 2014). Mutations in B4GAT1 can cause muscular dystrophy-dystroglycanopathies (MDDGA13, MIM:615287, Buysse et al., 2013).
