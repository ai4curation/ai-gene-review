# ER calcium channels open in response to activated PKA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-381644](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-381644)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated Protein Kinase A promotes the release of calcium from the endoplasmic reticulum into the cytosol. This may be due to phosphorylation of ER calcium channels by PKA, however this has not been demonstrated.
