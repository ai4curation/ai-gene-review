# RUNX1:CBFB:CREBBP binds the CSF2 (GM-CSF) gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938228](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938228)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The complex of the RUNX1:CBFB heterodimer and histone acetyltransferase CREBBP (CBP) binds the promoter of the CSF2 (GM-CSF) gene, encoding Granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (Oakford et al. 2010). RUNX1 can also recruit the SWI/SNF complex to the CSF2 gene promoter (Bakshi et al. 2010).
