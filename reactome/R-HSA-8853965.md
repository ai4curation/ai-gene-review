# VENTX disrupts binding of CTNNB1 (beta-catenin) to TCF4 or LEF1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8853965](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8853965)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

VENTX binds to TCF4/LEF1 transcription factors at the cyclin D1 (CCND1) promoter and disrupts the interaction of beta-catenin (CTNNB1) with TCF4 and/or LEF1 (Gao et al. 2010).
