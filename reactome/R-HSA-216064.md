# Interaction of integrin alpha8beta1 with Tenascin-C
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-216064](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-216064)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Tenascin-C (TN-C) and its isoforms are multidomain extracellular matrix (ECM) proteins that are believed to be involved in the regulation of stromal-epithelial interactions. Some of the interactions between TN-C and cells are mediated by integrins. Alpha8beta1 integrin interacts with the third FN type III repeats which contains an RGD sequence in tenascin-c.
