# AcK-NFE2L2 binds the ABCC1 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796077](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796077)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The promoter of ABCC1 (MRP1) contains two ARE sites occupied by NFE2L2 which is confirmed through ChIP and induced its expression under oxidative stress conditions (Ji et al, 2013)<br>
