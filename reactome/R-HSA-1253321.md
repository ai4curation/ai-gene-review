# GFAP gene expression is inhibited by ERBB4:TAB2:NCOR1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1253321](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1253321)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Transcription of the GFAP gene, involved in astrocyte differentiation, is inhibited by binding of the ERBB4s80:TAB2:NCOR1 complex to the GFAP promoter (Sardi et al. 2006).
