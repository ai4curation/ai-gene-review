# Fibrillin-1 degradation by MMP14
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2514831](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2514831)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Fibrillin-1 is the major structural component of microfibrils (Kielty et al. 2005). It can be degraded by the membrane-associated MMP14 (Ashworth et al. 1999). <br>
