# FLT4 gene expression is stimulated by Notch4
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9604519](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9604519)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Recombinant mouse Notch4 intracellular domain fragment (NICD4) stimulates transcription from the promoter of the human FLT4 (VEGFR) gene (Shawber et al. 2007).
