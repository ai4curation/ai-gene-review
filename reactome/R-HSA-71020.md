# D-methylmalonyl-CoA <=> L-methylmalonyl-CoA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-71020](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-71020)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

At the beginning of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'D-methylmalonyl-CoA' is present. At the end of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'L-methylmalonyl-CoA' is present.<br><br> This reaction takes place in the 'mitochondrial matrix' and  is mediated by the 'methylmalonyl-CoA epimerase activity' of 'methylmalonyl-CoA epimerase'.<br>
