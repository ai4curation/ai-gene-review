# Arachidonate is hydroxylated to 20-HETE by CYP(3)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2161940](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2161940)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Several cytochrome P450s (CYPs) convert arachidonate to 20-hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acid (20-HETE).  The CYPs and their references are as follows: CYP4A11 (Gainer et al. 2005, Powell 1998); CYP4F2 (Powell et al. 1998, Kikuta et al. 2002); CYP2U1 (Chuang et al. 2004); CYP1A1, CYP1A2, CYP1B1 (Choudhary et al. 2004).
