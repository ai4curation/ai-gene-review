# Arachidonate is oxidised to PGG2 by PTGS1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-140355](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-140355)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Prostaglandin G/H synthase PTGS1 exhibits a dual catalytic activity, a cyclooxygenase and a peroxidase.  The cyclooxygenase function catalyzes the initial conversion of arachidonate to an intermediate, prostaglandin G2 (PGG2) (Hamberg et al. 1974, Nugteren 1973).
