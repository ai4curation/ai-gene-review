# FLVCR1-2 transports heme from mitochondrial matrix to cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9661408](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9661408)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Feline leukemia virus subgroup C receptor-related protein 1 isoform 2 (FLVCR1-2), located on the mitochondrial membrane of all hematopoietic tissues, is a heme transporter that mediates heme efflux from the mitochondrion to the cytosol (Chiabrando et al. 2012). Silencing of FLVCR1-2 causes mitochondrial heme accumulation and termination of erythroid differentiation.
