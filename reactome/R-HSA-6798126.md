# TP53 stimulates CASP6 expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6798126](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6798126)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of TP53 (p53) to the third intron of the CASP6 (caspase-6) gene stimulates CASP6 expression (MacLachlan and El-Deiry 2002).
