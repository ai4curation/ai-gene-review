# RUNX2:CBFB binds the LGALS3 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938371](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938371)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX2:CBFB complex binds the RUNX2 response element in the promoter of the LGLAS3 gene, encoding Galectin-3 (Vladimirova et al. 2008, Zhang et al. 2009).
