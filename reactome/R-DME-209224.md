# SLMB binds to and ubiquitinates phosphorylated CI
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209224](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209224)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Phosphorylated Cubitus Interruptus (CI) is now recognised by Slimb (SLMB), which is part of the SCF (Skp1, Cdc53, and F-box) ubiquitin ligase complex.  CI associates with SLMB via its phosphorylated residues and is then ubiquitinated by the SCF ubiquitin ligase complex.
