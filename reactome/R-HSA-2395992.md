# p-T,4S-IRF3 is dimerized
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2395992](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2395992)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

IRF3 phosphorylation promotes IRF3 dimerization and nuclear translocation, which results in the production of type I interferons (IFNs).
