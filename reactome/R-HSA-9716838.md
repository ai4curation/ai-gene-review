# PTGS1,2 dimers bind PTGS Inhibitors
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9716838](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9716838)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Inhibition of PTGS2 in cells involved in inflammation, fever and pain explains the therapeutic activity of non-specific PTGS inhibitors (paracetamol (APAP), flurbiprofen, diclofenac, meclofenamic acid, nimesulide, ketoprofen, ibuprofen, naproxen, ketorolac, mefenamic acid, oxaprozin, phenylbutazone, piroxicam, suprofen) which show good anti-inflammatory and analgesic efficiency. Due to their simultaneous inhibition of PTGS1, they have side effects like ulcers and upper gastrointestinal complications, as well as increased heart attack and stroke risk (Grosser et al, 2017).
