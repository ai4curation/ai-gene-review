# AcK-NFE2L2 binds the ATF4 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796068](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796068)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Stress induction of ATF4 is significantly regulated transcriptionally through an NFE2L2-dependent mechanism. NFE2L2 binding to ATF4 promoter is confirmed by ChIP assay. (Miyamoto et al, 2011, DeNicola et al, 2015)<br>
