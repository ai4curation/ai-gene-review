# ASL variants don't synthesize fumarate and arginine
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9956524](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9956524)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Loss-of-function mutations in the ASL gene encoding argininosuccinate lyase cause the autosomal recessive urea cycle disorder argininosuccinic aciduria (reviewed in Nagmani et al, 2019; Schmitt Ribas et al, 2022; Matsumoto et al, 2019). <br>The following mutations have been identified in patients with argininosuccinic aciduria, are generally classified as pathogenic or likely pathogenic by ClinVar (although there are conflicting reports on pathogenicity for alleles R279Q and M360T), and are annotated here as either candidates or members depending on the extent of their biochemical characterization:<br>Candidates:<br>ASL E73K (Trevisson et al, 2009)<br>ASL D87G (Yu et al, 2001; Walker et al, 1997)<br>ASL R113Q (Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009; Erez et al, 2011)<br>ASL R182Q (Trevisson et al, 2009; Balmer et al, 2014)<br>ASL R186QL (Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009)<br>ASL R236W (Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009; Erez et al, 2011)<br>ASL D273N (Balmer et al, 2014)<br>ASL R297Q (Trevisson et al, 2009)<br>ASL Q354* (AlTassan et al, 2018)<br>ASL M360T (Yu et al, 2001)<br>ASL M382R (Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009)<br>ASL A398D (Yu et al, 2001; Walker et al, 1997)<br>ASL R456W (Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009)<br><br>Members:<br>ASL R12Q (Sampaleanu et al, 2001; Hu et al, 2015)<br>ASL D31N (Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009)<br>ASL R95C (Walker et al, 1990; Hu et al, 2015)<br>ASL V178M (Kleijer et al, 2002; Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009; Hu et al, 2015)<br>ASL Q286R (Walker et al, 1990; Sampaleanu et al, 2001; Yu et al, 2001; Barbosa et al, 1991; Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009; Walker et al, 1997; Hu et al, 2015)<br>ASL V335l (Trevisson et al, 2007; Trevisson et al, 2009; Hu et al, 2015)<br>ASL R379C (Kleijer et al, 2002; Hu et al, 2015)<br>ASL R385C (Kleijer et al, 2002; Hu et al, 2015)
