# MITF-M dimer binds GPR143 promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9856056](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9856056)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M binds to an E-box at position -34 of the promoter of the GRP143 gene to stimulate expression (Vetrini et al, 2004; Strub et al, 2011; reviewed in Cheli et al, 2010).
