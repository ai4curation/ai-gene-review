# IL6 binds IL6R
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1067667](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1067667)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Interleukin-6 (IL6) site I interacts with the Interleukin-6 receptor subunit α (IL6Rα), which is non-signaling but ligand-specific (Taga et al. 1989).
