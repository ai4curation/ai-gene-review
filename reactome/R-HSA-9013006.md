# RHOA:GTP translocates to the ER membrane
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9013006](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9013006)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

RHOA localizes to both plasma membrane and endoplasmic reticulum membrane (Cussac et al. 1996). The mechanism of RHOA intracellular trafficking has not been elucidated.
