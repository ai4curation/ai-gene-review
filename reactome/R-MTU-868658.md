# 1,4-alpha-glucan is converted to glucanotrehalose
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-868658](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-868658)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

Linear alpha-1,4-glucans are modified at one end to a glucanotrehalose by the enzyme TreY (De Smet et al, 2000).
