# Collagen types VI, IX bind integrin alpha10beta1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-4084903](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-4084903)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Integrin alpha10beta1 binds collagen types I, IV, and VI with similar affinities (Tulla et al. 2001) and also collagen IX (Kapyla et al. 2004).
