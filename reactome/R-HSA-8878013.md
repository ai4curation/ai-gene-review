**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8878013](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8878013)

## Summary

The complex containing RUNX2 and SMAD1 activated downstream of BMP2 signaling, binds the promoter of the SMAD6 gene (Wang et al. 2007).
