# BICRA, BICRAL and BRD9 bind SMARCC dimer:SMARCDs
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9932443](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9932443)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In place of an ARID subunit (characteristic of cBAF and pBAf complexes), non-canonical BAF complexes (ncBAF) recruit one of two paralogous proteins, BICRA (also known as GLTSCR1) or BICRAL (also known as GLTSCR1L), and a bromodomain containing protein BRD9 that interacts with acetylated lysine residues (Alpsoy and Dykhuizen, 2018; Michel et al, 2018; Mashtalir et al, 2018; Mashtalir et al, 2020; Flynn et al, 2015, Filippakopolous et al, 2012). <br>BICRA (BRD4-interacting chromatin remodeling complex associated) binds to BRD4 (Rahman et al, 2011). BRD4 is an epigenetic reader protein that interacts with acetylated histones and has roles in epigenetic memory, transcriptional regulation and super-enhancer regulation (reviewed in Donati et al, 2018; Qian et al, 2023). BICRAL is 21% identical to BICRA and appears to occur in ncBAF complexes in a mutually exclusive way with BICRA (Alpsoy and Dykhuizen, 2018).
