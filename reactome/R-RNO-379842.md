# Dimerization of p-S133-Creb1
**Reactome ID:** [R-RNO-379842](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-RNO-379842)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

Homodimerization of rat Creb1 is induced by activating phosphorylation of Creb1 on serine residue S133 (Yamamoto et al. 1988) and involves the C-terminal DNA binding and dimerization domain (Yun et al. 1990).
