# PI3K catalyses the phosphorylation of PIP2 to PIP3 downstream of mutant PDGFR
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9672177](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9672177)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PI3K/AKT signaling is initiated downstream of PDGFRA gain-of-function missense and in-frame deletion mutants as assessed by the presence of phosphorylated AKT in Western blot analysis (Heinrich et al, 2003; Osashi et al, 2005; Bahlawane et al, 2015; Paugh et al, 2013; reviewed in Corless et al, 2011).
