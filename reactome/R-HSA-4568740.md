# AP-1 stimulates IL1A transcription
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-4568740](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-4568740)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The AP-1 complex (FOS:JUN) binds IL1A promoter and stimulates IL1A transcription (Bailly et al. 1996 Alheim et al. 1996, Burleson et al. 1996, Huang et al. 1999, Niu et al. 2004, Moerman-Herzog and Barger 2012, Murakami et al. 2013).
