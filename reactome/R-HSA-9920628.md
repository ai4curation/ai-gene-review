# NS1 binds C4A/B and C1S
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9920628](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9920628)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Secreted DENV NS1 binds complement subcomponents C1S and C4 (either C4A or C4B) in a complex, thus reducing the amount of native C4 available for complement activation through C4beta (the beta chain of C4A or C4B) deposition and C3 convertase (C4b2a) activity. The complex also cleaves C4 to C4beta in the fluid phase, which reduces the amount of native C4 available to participate in complement activation. This results in reduced C4beta deposition on cell surfaces and decreased C3 convertase (C4b2a) activity. These mechanisms were demonstrated in experiments using human complement proteins and DENV NS1 from non-human cells, including hamster cell lines (BHK, CHO) and sheep erythrocytes (Avirutnan et al., 2010).
