# Activation of BMF and translocation to mitochondria
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-139910](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-139910)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In healthy cells, BMF is bound to the myosin V motor complex through its interaction with DLC2. UV irradiation or anoikis induces MAPK8 (JNK) to phosphorylate Dynein Light Chain 2 (DLC2) to release BMF.
