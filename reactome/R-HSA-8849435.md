# PTPN1 dephosphorylates PTK6
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8849435](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8849435)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Protein tyrosine phosphatase PTPN1 (PTP1B) dephosphorylates tyrosine residue Y342 of PTK6 (BRK), resulting in PTK6 inactivation (Fan et al. 2013).
