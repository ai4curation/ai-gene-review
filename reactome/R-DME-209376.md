# SPN27A binds to and inhibits EA protease activity
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209376](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209376)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

In dorsoventral patterning, the serpin SPN27A binds to Easter (EA) serine protease.  This complex inhibits Spatzle (SPZ) dimer processing.
