# ATF5 and HSF1 trimer bind the bidirectional promoter of the HSPD1 gene and HSPE1 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9857071](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9857071)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Both HSF1 and ATF5 bind the bidirectional promoter of the HSPD1 gene (HSP60 gene) and HSPE1 gene (HSP10 gene) after unfolded proteins accumulate in the mitochondrial matrix (Sutandy et al. 2023, the presence of HSF1 at the HSPD1/HSPE1 promoter is also inferred from mouse homologs in Katiyar et al. 2020).
