# SMAD6 gene expression is stimulated by RUNX2 and SMAD1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8878023](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8878023)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of the complex of RUNX2 and SMAD1 to the promoter of the SMAD6 gene positively regulates SMAD6 transcription (Wang et al. 2007).
