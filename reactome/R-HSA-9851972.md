# PLK1 phosphorylates FIRRM at S43
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9851972](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9851972)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PLK1 phosphorylates FIRRM (also known as Apolo1 or FLIP) at serine residue S43, which increases the binding affinity of FIRRM for PLK1 (Xu et al. 2021).
