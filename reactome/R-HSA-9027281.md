# EPO:phospho-EPOR:phospho-JAK2:LYN:IRS2 binds GAB1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9027281](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9027281)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

GAB1 binds directly to phosphotyrosines 344 and 402 of the EPOR (Bouscary et al. 2003, Montoye et al. 2005). GAB1 can also indirectly bind the EPOR via SHC1 or GRB2. GAB2 is absent from human erythroid progenitors but present in mouse erythroid progenitors (Wickrema et al. 1999).
