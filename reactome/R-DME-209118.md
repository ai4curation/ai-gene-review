# Membrane localised SGG phosphorylates ARR
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209118](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209118)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The resulting transmembrane co-receptor complex is now primed for phosphorylation on the C-terminal tail of Arrow (ARR) by a membrane localised Shaggy (SGG).  Ser1490 in Human LRP6 is phosphorylated by GSK3beta.
