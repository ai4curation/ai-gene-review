# Expression of Nkx2-5 in cardiogenesis
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9733796](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9733796)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

The Nkx2-5 (Nkx2.5) gene is transcribed to yield mRNA and the mRNA is translated to yield Nkx2-5 protein (Bondue et al. 2008, Bondue et al. 2011, Kokkinopoulos et al. 2015). In cardiac progenitors, Nkx2-5 is initially activated by Mesp1 (Bondue et al. 2008, Bondue et al. 2011, Chiapparo et al. 2016) and then, as expression of Mesp1 is terminated (Kokkinopoulos et al. 2015), Nkx2-5 becomes regulated by Gata4 (Lien et al. 1999, Reecy et al. 1999, Brown et al. 2004). Nkx2-5 is also directly activated by Smad1:Smad4 which may result from Bmp signaling (Brown et al. 2004). In the second heart field, Mef2c mediates binding of Nkx2-5 to its own promoter (Clark et al. 2013). Tbx20 binds the cardiac enhancer of the Nkx2-5 gene and enhances transcription (Nakeuchi et al. 2005). Gata4 and Nkx2-5 regulate each other's expression during cardiogenesis. Insufficient expression of Nkx2-5 causes abnormalities of the cardiac conduction system (Jay et al. 2004).
