# Degradation of fibrilin-1 by Elane
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2514835](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2514835)
**Species:** Bos taurus

## Summary

Fibrillin-1, the major structural component of microfibrils (Kielty et al. 2005), can be degraded by neutrophil elastase (ELANE) (Kielty et al. 1994).
