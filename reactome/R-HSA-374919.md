# Noradrenaline clearance from the synaptic cleft
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-374919](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-374919)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Noradrenaline is cleared from the synaptic cleft by Noaradrenaline uptake transporter. This reaction is carried out by neurons as well as astrocytes.
