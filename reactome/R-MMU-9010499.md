# Hoxa2 binds Robo2 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9010499](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9010499)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Endogenous mouse homeobox transcription factor Hoxa2 binds an evolutionarily conserved Hox-Pbx binding site in the second intron of the endogenous mouse Robo2 gene (Geisen et al. 2008).
