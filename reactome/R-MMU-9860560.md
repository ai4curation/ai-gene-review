# Phosphorylation of Pi3k in Pecam1:Cdh5:PiK3ca,b,d:Pik3r2:p-799,Y1052,Y1172-Kdr:p-Y1063,Y1230-Flt4:Ctnnb1
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9860560](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9860560)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

After binding phosphorylated Kdr (Vegfr2), phosphoinositol 3-kinase (Pi3k, Pik3ca,b,d:Pik3r2) is phosphorylated on tyrosine-458 of the regulatory subunit Pik3r2 (p85) (Dayanir et al. 2001, Tzima et al. 2005). A Src family kinase acts upstream of the phosphorylation of Pi3k and may phosphorylate Pi3k directly (Tzima et al. 2005).
