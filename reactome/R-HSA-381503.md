# IGFBP6 binds IGF forming IGF:IGFBP6
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-381503](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-381503)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

IGFBP-6 binds IGF I or IGF II via the conserved N terminus and C terminus of IGFBP-6. IGFBP-6 binds IGF II with greater affinity than IGF I.
