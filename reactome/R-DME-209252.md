# CI75 inhibits transcription
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209252](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209252)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Upon arrival in the nucleus, truncated Cubitus Interruptus (CI75) represses transcription.  Sensitive target genes include hh and dpp, but not ptc and repression depends on direct DNA-binding sites for CI75.
