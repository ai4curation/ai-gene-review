# factor XIIa binds SERPING1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-158357](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-158357)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated factor XII (factor XIIa) binds to the plasma protease C1 inhibitor (C1INH, encoded by the SERPING1 gene) (Bock SC., et al. 1986) to form a stable, inactive complex (Schneider AD et al., 1973). While several protease inhibitors can form stable complexes with XIIa in vitro, only C1INH does so to a significant extent under normal conditions in vivo (Pixley RA et al. 1985). C1INH accounts for 92% inhibition of FXIIa in plasma (Pixley RA et al., 1985).
