# E2F1 binds RAD51 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9007561](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9007561)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

E2F1, presumably in complex with TFDP1 (DP-1) or TFDP2 (DP-2) binds the promoter of the RAD51 gene (Bertoli et al. 2013, Wu et al. 2014).
