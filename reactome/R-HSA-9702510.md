# FLT mutants bind type II TKIs
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9702510](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9702510)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Type II tyrosine kinase inhibitors bind to a hydrophobic region adjacent to the ATP-binding site that is only present in the inactive conformation of the receptor. As a consequence, type II inhibitors tend to be more effective against FLT3 with ITD than against FLT3 with activating mutations in the tyrosine kinase domain (reviewed in Klug et al, 2018; Larrosa-Garcia and Baer, 2017; Patnaik, 2018; Daver et al, 2019).<br><br>The following FLT3 mutants are sensitive to these type II tyrosine kinase inhibitors:<br><br>ponatinib:<br>FLT3 D835G (Nguyen et al, 2017)<br>FLT3 D835N (Nguyen et al, 2017)<br>FLT3 Y591_V592insVDFREYEYDH (Nguyen et al, 2017)<br><br>quizartinib:<br>FLT3 E598_Y599insFDFREYE (Moore et al, 2012)<br>FLT3 L610_E611insLKWEFPRENL (Peschel et al, 2017)<br>FLT3 Y591_V592insVDFREYEYDH (Peschel et al, 2017)<br>FLT3 F621L (Williams et al, 2013)<br>FLT3 D835G (Nguyen et al, 2017)<br>FLT3 D835H (Galanis et al, 2014)<br>FLT3 D835N (Galanis et al, 2014; Nguyen et al, 2017)<br><br>sorafenib:<br>FLT3 N676D (Albers et al, 2013)<br>FLT3 E598_Y599insFDFREYE (Moore et al, 2012)<br>FLT3 D835G (Nguyen et al, 2017)<br>FLT3 D835N (Nguyen et al, 2017)<br><br>pexidartinib:<br>FLT3 F691L (Smith et al, 2015)<br><br>tandutinib:<br>FLT3 D835A (Clark et al, 2004)<br>FLT3 D835E (Clark et al, 2004)<br>FLT3 D835H (Clark et al, 2004)<br>FLT3 D835N (Clark et al, 2004)<br>FLT3 I836del (Clark et al, 2004)<br>FLT3 E598_Y599insFDFREYE (Kelly et al, 2002; Moore et al, 2012)<br><br>carbozantinib:<br>FLT3 L610_E611insLKWEFPRENL (Ko et al, 2019)<br><br>sunitinib:<br>FLT3 F663Q (Schittenhlem et al, 2006)<br>FLT3 F691L (Albers et al, 2013)<br>FLT3 Y842H (Albers et al, 2013)<br>FLT3 N676D (Albers et al, 2013)<br>FLT3 D835G (Nguyen et al, 2017)<br>FLT3 D835N (Nguyen et al, 2017)<br>FLT3 Y591_V592insVDFREYEYDH (Schittenhelm et al, 2006)<br>
