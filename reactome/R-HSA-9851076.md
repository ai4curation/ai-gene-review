# p-T,Y MAPK8,9 bind TP53
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9851076](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9851076)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NPM1-ALK and JNK signaling regulate TP53 activity and stability in ALCL cells and tissues (Cui et al, 2009), consistent with a role for JNK in TP53 activity seen in other studies (Cui et al, 2009; Fuchs et al, 1998). Inhibition of JNK (MAPK8 and 9) phosphorylation inhibits formation of a TP53:JNK complex, stabilizes TP53 protein levels and decreases TP53 ubiquitination, supporting a role for phosphorylated JNK signaling in promoting the degradation of TP53 in these cells (Cui et al, 2009).
