# BIL binds ALB
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9661432](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9661432)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The serum protein albumin (ALB) binds unconjugated bilirubin (BIL), preventing BIL toxicity (Griffiths et al. 1975, Weisiger et al. 2001). ALB-bound BIL is a water-soluble complex and is transported to the liver where it is selectively absorbed by hepatocytes.
