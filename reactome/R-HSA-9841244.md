# Phosphorylation of AKT1 on serine-473 in response to reactive oxygen species
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9841244](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9841244)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The presence of unfolded protein in the mitochondrial intermembrane space causes the cytosolic kinase AKT to be phosphorylated on serine-473 by an uncharacterized mechanism that requires the generation of reactive oxygen species (Papa and Germain 2011).
