# RUNX1 recruits KMT2A to the Spi1 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-8865513](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-8865513)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Recombinant human RUNX1 and KMT2A bind to mouse Spi1 (PU.1) gene. Endogenous mouse Cbfb is required for this interaction (Huang et al. 2011).
