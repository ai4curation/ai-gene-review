# Hbp(53-1100) cleaves hemoglobin
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9927009](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9927009)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

E.coli serine protease Hbp cleaves hemoglobin (HbA) subunits at unknown positions, releasing heme and binding two molecules of it (Otto et al., 1998; Otto et al., 2002; Otto et al., 2005; reviewed in Clarke et al., 2022).
