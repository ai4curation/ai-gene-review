# Phosphorylated PER is phosphorylated by CK2
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-432590](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-432590)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Phosphorylated Period (PER) is phosphorylated on serine residues 149, 151, 153, by casein kinase II (CK2).  CK2 phosphorylation appears to be important for subsequent nuclear entry of PER.
