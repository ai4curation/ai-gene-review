# RUNX1 binds the CR1 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8939082](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8939082)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds the promoter of the CR1 (CD35) gene, encoding Complement receptor type 1 (Kim et al. 1999, Rho et al. 2002).
