# P:PP1 dephosphorylates M2-1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9831948](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9831948)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Dephosphorylation of protein M2-1 is catalyzed by a complex formed between P and the cellular phosphatase PP1 and is required, in a cyclic manner, for efficient viral transcription (Richard et al, 2018).
