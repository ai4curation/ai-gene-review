# Dasatinib-resistant KIT mutants do not bind dasatinib
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9669855](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9669855)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

KIT receptors bearing mutations in the ATP-binding pocket are resistant to treatment with dasatinib (Schittenhelm et al, 2006; Serrano et al, 2019).
