# Regulation of KIF23 (MKLP1) by phosphorylation
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-156673](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-156673)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The polo-like kinase PLK1 phosphorylates KIF23 (also known as MKLP1 i.e. mitotic kinesin-like protein 1) on serine residues S911 and S912.
