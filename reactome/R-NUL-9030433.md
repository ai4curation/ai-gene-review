# BDNF- or NTF4-activated Ntrk2 recruits Plcg1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9030433](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9030433)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Mouse Plcg1 is recruited to autophosphorylated mouse Ntrk2 in response to stimulation with recombinant human BDNF or NTF4 (Minichiello et al. 1998, Minichiello et al. 2002).
