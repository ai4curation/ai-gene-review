# GEF binds to the inactive DRheb
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-165127](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-165127)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The interaction of DRheb with its guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) has not been studied experimentally, but is inferred here by analogy to the very well-studied interaction of Ran GTPase with RCC1 (Azuma et al. 1999; Renault et al. 2001). GEF binds reversibly to the DRheb-GDP complex, forming a GEF:GTPase:GDP  complex. Complex formation induces a change in the shape of the GTPase, substantially reducing its affinity for bound guanine nucleotide.
