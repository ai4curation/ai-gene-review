# JAK2 binds to p-KIT mutants
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9670413](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9670413)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activation of STAT signaling has been observed downstream of a number of oncogenic KIT mutants, both juxtamembrane and kinase domain mutants, although the biological relevance or each STAT isoform varies between tumor types (Brizzi et al, 1999; Ning et al, 2001; Frost et al, 2002; Growney et al, 2005; Hana et al, 2017; Obata et al, 2017; Duensing et al, 2004; Bauer et al, 2007; Deberry et al, 1997; Ronnstrand, 2004). Although the pathway details haven't been examined in all cases, this likely occurs through the recruitment of JAK2 and SRC family kinases, as is the case for the wild type receptor (reviewed in Lennartsson and Roonstrand, 2012).
