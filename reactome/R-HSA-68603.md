# ORC5 binds ORC2:ORC3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-68603](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-68603)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ORC5 forms a heterotrimer with ORC2 and ORC3. ATP is not required for the formation of this complex nor for its maintenance (Ranjan and Gossen 2006; Siddiqui and Stillman 2007).
