# SLC1A5-mediated exchange of glutamine and alanine across the plasma membrane
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-352385](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-352385)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SLC1A5, associated with the plasma membrane, mediates the exchange of extracellular glutamine for cytosolic alanine (Broer et al. 2000).
