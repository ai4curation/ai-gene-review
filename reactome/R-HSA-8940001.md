# RUNX2:CBFB binds the MMP13 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8940001](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8940001)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

AKT-mediated phosphorylation of RUNX2 increases affinity of the RUNX2:CBFB complex for the promoter of the MMP13 gene, encoding Colagenase-3 (Matrix metalloproteinase-13) (Pande et al. 2013).
