# MET binds SHC1-2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8851888](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8851888)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylated tyrosine residues Y1349 and Y1356 in the cytoplasmic tail of MET create docking sites for the SH2 domain of SHC1 splicing isoform 2 (SHC1-2) (Pelicci et al. 1995).
