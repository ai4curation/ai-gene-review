# STUB1 polyubiquitinates RUNX2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9009308](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9009308)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

STUB1 (CHIP), an E3 ubiquitin ligase, promotes polyubiquitination of RUNX2, targeting it for proteasome-mediated degradation (Li et al. 2008).
