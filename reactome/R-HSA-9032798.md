# DOCK3 activates RAC1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9032798](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9032798)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DOCK3 activates RAC1 and contributes to recruitment of the RAC1 effector WASF1 (WAVE1). DOCK3-mediated activation of RAC1 is involved in BDNF-induced axonal sprouting (Namekata et al. 2010).<p>WASF1 has recently been implicated in endocytosis of the BDNF:NTRK2 (BDNF:TRKB) complex (Xu et al. 2016).
