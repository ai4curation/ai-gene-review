# NR1D1 (REV-ERBA) binds heme, the CLOCK gene, and recruits corepressors
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5663271](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5663271)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NR1D1 (REV-ERBA) binds the promoter of the CLOCK gene and recruits corepressors to repress transcription (Crumbley and Burris 2011). Recruitment of repressors appears to depend on the binding of heme by NR1D1.
