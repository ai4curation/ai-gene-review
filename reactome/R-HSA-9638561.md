# MrkD binds Collagen V
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9638561](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9638561)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Klebsiella type-3 pili have hemagglutinating activity. The adhesin MrkD in these pili binds to type-V collagen (Collagen V) on tubular basement membranes, the Bowman's capsule, arterial walls and interstitial connective tissue (Gerlach et al. 1989, Tarkkanen et al. 1990).
