# Transfer of NEDD8 to AcM-UBE2F
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8951764](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8951764)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

UBA3:NAE1 transfers NEDD8 to the catalytic cysteine residue of UBE2F. Because two of the three E1-E2 interaction interfaces are created by conformational states present in the doubly-NEDDylated NAE1, transfer of NEDD8 to UBE2M is thought to weaken the interaction between UBA3:NAE1 and UBE2F, contributing to UBE2F release (Walden et al, 2003; Huang et al, 2004; Hunag et al, 2005; Huang et al, 2007).
