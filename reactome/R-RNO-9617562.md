# Activated calmodulin binds Adcy8
**Reactome ID:** [R-RNO-9617562](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-RNO-9617562)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

Recombinant rat calmodulin (Calm1, also known as CaM), activated by binding to calcium ions (Ca2+) binds to recombinant rat adenylate cyclase 8 (Adcy8, also known as AC8) (Masada et al. 2012).
