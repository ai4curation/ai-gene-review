# Activated Ntrk2 binds Fyn
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9032516](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9032516)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

Endogenous rat Fyn associates with endogenous rat Ntrk2 (TrkB) activated by stimulation with recombinant BDNF (Iwasaki et al. 1998). Recombinant BDNF is assumed to be of human origin.
