# SLC46A1 transports hemes from extracellular region to cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-917870](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-917870)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Uptake of iron from meat happens mostly in the form of ferriheme (FeHM), and via the same transporter that is used for folate. The process is more effective than taking up iron ions (Shayeghi M et al, 2005). In general, heme transporters do not differentiate between ferroheme and ferriheme.
