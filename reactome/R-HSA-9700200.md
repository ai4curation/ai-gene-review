# PTPRZ dephosphorylates ligand-bound ALK dimers
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9700200](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9700200)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ALK is dephosphorylated by PTPRZ leading to loss of activity (Perez-Pinera et al, 2007). <br>Some studies suggest that, rather than being canonically activated by direct binding of PTN and MDK, ALK is activated in a ligand-independent manner by the binding of these ligands to PTPRZ (and possibly PTPRB) instead. PTN- and MDK-binding to PTPRZ/B promotes their oligomerization and catalytic inactivation, thus inhibiting the phosphatase activity and allowing the autoactivation of ALK to predominate (Perez-Pinera et al, 2007; Fukada et al, 2006; Maeda et al, 1999; Kuboyama et al, 2016; reviewed in Deuel, 2013). Discrepancies between these two models remain to be worked out.
