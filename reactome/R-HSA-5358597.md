# EXO1 interacts with MSH2:MSH6 (MutSalpha) and MLH1:PMS2 (MutLalpha)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5358597](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5358597)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

EXO1 interacts with MSH2 and MLH1 (and therefore is presumed to interact with their respective heterodimers MSH2:MSH6 and MLH1:PMS2), forming a ternary complex with MLH1:PMS2 (Nielsen et al. 2004). EXO1 also interacts with PCNA in S phase (Nielsen et al. 2004, Zhang et al. 2005, Constantin et al. 2005, Knudsen et al. 2007, Liberti et al. 2011)
