# Transport of Extracellular Glucose to the Cytosol by GLUT1 and GLUT2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-499981](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-499981)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Human pancreatic beta cells express glucose transporters 1 and (GLUT1, GLUT2), which are responsible for uptake of glucose from the extracellular medium into the cytosol. (Rodent pancreatic beta cells express only Glut2.)
