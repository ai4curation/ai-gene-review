# ARG1 variants don't synthesize urea and ornithine
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9956512](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9956512)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Mutations in ARG1 lead to Arginase 1 deficiency, also known as argininemia (OMIM 207800) (reviewed in Diez-Fernandez et al, 2018; Sun et al, 2020). The following variants have been identified in patients and are generally classified as pathogenic or likely pathogenic in ClinVar. They are annotated as candidates or members depending on the extent to which they have been biochemically characterized.<br><br>Variants that destabilize protein structure:<br>Candidates:<br>ARG1 R21* (Cardoso et al, 1999)<br>ARG1 A125V (Wu et al, 2013)<br>ARG1 R180T (Wu et al, 2013)<br>ARG1 T290S (Grody et al, 1992)<br>ARG1 R291* (Grody et al, 1992)<br><br>Members:<br>ARG1 I11T (Carvalho et al, 2012; Uchino et al, 1995)<br>ARG1 G27D (Carvalho et al, 2012)<br>ARG1 G74D (Carvalho et al, 2012)<br>ARG1 G138V (Uchino et al, 1995)<br>ARG1 R308Q (Carvalho et al, 2012)<br><br>Variants that affect residues involved in catalysis and substrate binding:<br>Candidates:<br>ARG1 W122* (Uchino et al, 1992; Wu et al, 2013)<br>ARG1 T134I (Carvalho et al, 2012)<br>ARG1 G235R (Uchino et al, 1992; Wu et al, 2013)<br>
