# Collagen type I degradation by MMP15
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2473596](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2473596)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The membrane-type MMP MMP15 (MT2-MMP) is a fibrillar collagenase able to degrade collagen type I (Morrison and Overall 2006) and believed able to degrade types II and III (Somerville et al. 2003).
