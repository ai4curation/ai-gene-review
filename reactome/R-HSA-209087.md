# IKBA is phosphorylated by Phospho IKKB kinase
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-209087](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-209087)

## Summary

Human IKBA, orthologue of Drosophila Cactus (CACT), is phosphorylated by activated IKKB kinase at residues Ser32 and Ser36.
