# RAC1 GDIs block activation of RAC1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9013147](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9013147)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The following GDP dissociation inhibitors (GDIs) bind to RAC1 and inhibit its activation:<br>ARHGDIA (Boulter et al. 2010; Gupta et al. 2013; Ando et al. 1992)<br>ARHGDIB (Adra et al. 1993, Zhang et al. 2009)<br><br>ARHGDIG does not act on RAC1 (Zalcman et al. 1996, Adra et al. 1997)
