# MITF-M binds the CDC25B gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926488](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926488)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MITF-M binds to an E-box element at +83 of the CDC25B 5' untranslated region (UTR) to upregulate expression as assessed by ChIP and RNA seq (Strub et al, 2011).
