# MRN complex binds shortened telomeres
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5682020](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5682020)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The MRN complex (MRE11:RAD50:NBS1 also known as MRE11A:RAD50:NBN) binds telomeric DNA, and MRN association with telomeric DNA is mutually exclusive with shelterin binding (Wu et al. 2007).
