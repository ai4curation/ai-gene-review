# RNA Polymerase III Initiation At a Simple Start Sequence
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-111942](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-111942)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

Transcription by pol III initiates at characteristic, simple start sequences.  The universal core of these start sites is a pyrimidine-purine step, transcription initiating most frequently with ATP or GTP (Fruscoloni et al., 1995)
