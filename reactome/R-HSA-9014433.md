# RHOG GEFs activate RHOG
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9014433](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9014433)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Movilla and Bustelo 1999)<br>PREX1 (Damoulakis et al. 2014; Jaiswal et al. 2013)<br>TRIO (Blangy et al. 2000; Estrach et al. 2002; Peurois et al. 2017; Jaiswal et al. 2013; supported by interaction of TRIO with inactive RHOG mutant reported by Bagci et al. 2020)<br>VAV1 (Schuebel et al. 1998)<br>VAV3 (Movilla and Bustelo 1999)<br><br>The following GEFs, annotated as RHOG candidate GEFs, were shown to activate RHOG in some, but not all studies:<br>ARHGEF16 (Hiramoto Yamaki et al. 2010; Harada et al. 2011: RHOG directed GEF activity; Bagci et al. 2020: no binding to inactive RHOG)<br>ARHGEF26 (Ellerbroek et al. 2004: RHOG directed GEF activity; Bagci et al. 2020: no binding to inactive RHOG)<br>ARHGEF5 (Wang et al. 2009: weak RHOG directed GEF activity; Bagci et al. 2020: no binding to inactive RHOG)<br>MCF2L (Wennerberg et al. 2002; Jaiswal et al. 2013: RHOG directed GEF activity; Bagci et al. 2020: no binding to inactive RHOG)<br>VAV2 (Schuebel et al. 1998; Wennerberg et al. 2002; Jaiswal et al. 2013: RHOG directed GEF activity; Bagci et al. 2020: no binding to inactive RHOG)<br><br>The following GEFs were shown to either not act on RHOG or to not bind to inactive RHOG mutant in the high throughput screen by Bagci et al. 2020:<br>AKAP13 (Bagci et al. 2020)<br>ARHGEF1 (Bagci et al. 2020)<br>ARHGEF11 (Jaiswal et al. 2013;
