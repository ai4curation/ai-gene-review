# Trimethylation of CD274 gene promoter by EZH2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909631](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909631)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

EZH2 binds to CD274 promoter and induces methylation of CD274 at H3K27 site and suppress its expression (Xiao et al.,2019, Morel et al.,2021).
