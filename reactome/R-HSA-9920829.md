# NS5 binds HSP90
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9920829](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9920829)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DENV NS5 (possibly in conjunction with other NS proteins) interacts with host HSP90, leading to inappropriate chaperoning of JAK proteins, loss of their activity, protein kinase instability, and subsequent degradation via the proteasome. This result was obtained from experiments with the Dengue serotype-2 (DENV-2) NG-C strain (Carpp et al., 2014; Roby et al., 2020).
