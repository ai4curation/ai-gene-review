# FASL binds FAS Receptor
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-75244](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-75244)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

FASL (FAS antigen ligand) binds FAS receptor (CD95) (Brunner et al. 1995). Herpesvirus 8 (HHV8, KSHV) protein K1 physically associates with FAS at the plasma membrane through its extracellular immunoglobulin-like domain, binding the CRD3 region of the FAS ectodomain and preventing engagement of FAS ligand or agonistic anti-FAS antibody, thereby blocking initiation of death-inducing signaling complex assembly (Berkova et al. 2009).
