# (d)CDP or UDP + ADP <=> (d)CMP or UMP + ATP (CMPK1)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-75125](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-75125)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cytosolic UMP-CMP kinase (CMPK1) catalyzes the reversible reaction of CDP, dCDP, or UDP and ADP to form CMP, dCMP, or UMP and ATP (Liou et al. 2002; Scott and Wright 1979).
