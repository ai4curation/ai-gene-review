# Liberation of CI from SU(FU) repression
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209220](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209220)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Cubitus Interruptus (CI) that is spared from proteolysis is liberated from the repressive effects of its direct binding partner, Supressor of Fused (SU(FU)), by Fused (FU) Ser/Thr kinase activity.  FU is believed to phosphorylate SU(FU) but it is not known if this relieves repression.
