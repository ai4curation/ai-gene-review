# Concentrative transport (import) of a nucleoside and two sodium ions by solute carrier family 28 (sodium-coupled nucleoside transporter), member 3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-109538](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-109538)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The plasma membrane-associated protein SLC28A3 mediates the transport of one molecule of adenosine, cytidine, guanosine, inosine, thymidine, or uridine, and two sodium ions, from the extracellular space to the cytosol.
