# ERKs phosphorylate MCRIP1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9765960](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9765960)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated MAPKs (ERKs) phosphorylate MCRIP1 on conserved residues serine 21 (S21) and threonine 30 (T30), which negatively impacts binding of MCRIP1 to CTBP1 (Ichikawa et al. 2015).
