# ARL2:GTP binds ARL2BP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5250217](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5250217)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ADP-ribosylation factor-like protein 2 (ARL2) is a small  (21kDa) GTPase protein that is able to bind GDP or GTP. It is implicated in a range of processes from trafficking processes to regulation of microtuble dynamics. ADP-ribosylation factor-like protein 2-binding protein (ARL2BP aka BART) is an effector protein that binds ARL2 (Zhang et al. 2009, Bailey et al. 2009). This binding is dependent on ARL2 being in its GTP-bound form.
