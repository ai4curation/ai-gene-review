# SMAD7 binds SERPINE1 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9736966](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9736966)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SMAD7 can bind the SERPINE1 (PAI-1) gene promoter (and probably other TGF-beta-responsive promoters) and inhibit PAI-1 expression by competing with SMAD2,3:SMAD4 binding (Zhang et al. 2007).
