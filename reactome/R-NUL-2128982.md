# Strap binds phosphorylated TGF-beta receptor complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2128982](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2128982)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In COS-1 cells, recombinant mouse Strap binds the complex of recombinant human TFGBR2 and recombinant human TGFBR1 phosphorylated as a result of TGF-beta treatment (Datta et al. 1998).
