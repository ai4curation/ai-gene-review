# TP53 stimulates CASP10 expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6798139](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6798139)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Upon binding to p53 response elements at the CASP10 (caspase-10) gene locus, TP53 (p53) stimulates CASP10 expression (Rikhof et al. 2003). CASP10 is involved in apoptosis triggered by death receptor signaling (Wang et al. 2001, Sprick et al. 2002).
