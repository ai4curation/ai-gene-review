# PLL kinase binds to TL:MYD88:TUB 'signalling complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209072](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209072)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The Ser/Thr kinase Pelle (PLL) binds to the death domain of Tube (TUB) in the 'signalling complex' at the plasma membrane.  On binding to TUB, PLL is activated.
