# ERK1/2-activated AP1 complex binds KDM6B promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3222533](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3222533)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of the AP-1 (JUN:FOS) subunit JUN to the KDM6B (JMJD3) promoter, as well as the transcription of KDM6B gene increases after ERK activation (Lin et al. 2012).
