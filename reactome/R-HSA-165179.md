# Formation of TSC1:TSC2 complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-165179](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-165179)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

A membrane-associated TSC1 (hamartin) binds TSC2 (tuberin) and recruits it to the plasma membrane where it can exert its function as a GAP (GTPase activating protein) for the small GTPase RHEB (Cai et al. 2006).
