# TFAP2A,TFAP2C homodimers:CITED2 binds PITX2 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8864718](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8864718)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Homodimers of AP-2 transcription factor family members TFAP2A (AP-2 alpha) and TFAP2C (AP-2 gamma), in complex with CITED2, bind to the evolutionarily conserved AP-2 response elements in the promoter of the PITX2 gene (Bamforth et al. 2004, Li et al. 2012).
