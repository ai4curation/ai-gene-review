# NR1D1 (REV-ERBA) binds heme, the BMAL1 (ARNTL) gene, and recruits corepressors
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1368069](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1368069)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NR1D1 (REV-ERBA) binds heme (Raghuram et al. 2007). The NR1D1:heme complex is then able to recruit the corepressors NCoR and HDAC3 (Phelan et al 2010). Corepressors do not bind NR1D1 in the absence of heme (Yin et al. 2007). NR1D1:heme binds a RRE element in the promoter of the ARNTL (BMAL1) gene, recruits corepressors, and represses transcription (Yin and Lazar 2007).
