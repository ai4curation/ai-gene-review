# Gria2 gene expression is inhibited by Mecp2:Sin3a complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-RNO-9022366](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-RNO-9022366)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

Transcription of rat Gria2 (Glur2) gene, encoding glutamate receptor 2, is inhibited by binding of the rat Mecp2:Sin3a:Hdac1 complex to the promoter of the Gria2 gene (Qiu et al. 2012).
