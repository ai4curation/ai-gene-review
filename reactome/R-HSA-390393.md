# Peroxisomal uptake of very long-chain fatty acyl CoA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-390393](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-390393)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ABCD1 (ATP-binding cassette sub-family D member 1) mediates the ATP-dependent, coupled peroxisomal import and hydrolysis of cytosolic very long chain fatty-acyl-CoA to form peroxisomal very long-chain fatty acid and CoASH (Kawaguchi et al. 2021; van Roermund et al. 2011; van Roermund et al. 2012).
