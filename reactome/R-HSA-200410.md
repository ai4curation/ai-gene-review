# CPT2 converts acylcarnitine to acyl-CoA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-200410](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-200410)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CPT2, associated with the inner mitochondrial membrane, catalyzes the reaction of C8-C18 acylcarnitines and CoASH to form acyl-CoA and carnitine (Verderio et al. 1995; Violante et al. 2010).
