# ESR1-dependent CCND1 expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9623355](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9623355)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Expression of CCND1, the gene encoding cyclin D1, is stimulated by treatment of cells with E2. Expression is dependent on ESR1-mediated signaling through the PKA and AKT pathways, and consistent with this, levels of phosphorylated CREB1 increase upon treatment with E2. The CCND1 promoter has a CRE element that is bound by phosphorylated CREB1 upon E2 treatment as assessed by electrophoretic mobility shift assay (Park et al, 2001; Felty et al, 2005).
