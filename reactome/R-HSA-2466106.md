# BGN binds Collagen types I, VI, (IX)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2466106](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2466106)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Biglycan is a member of the small leucine-rich repeat proteoglycan family (SLRPs) which also includes decorin, fibromodulin (Hedlund et al. 1994 - binding to collagen II), lumican and asporin (Hedbom & Heinegard 1993, Ezura et al. 2000). All appear to be involved in collagen fibril formation and matrix assembly (Ameye & Young 2002).  <br><br>Biglycan binds collagen types I (Schönherr et al. 1995), II (Bovine, using pig byglycan - Vynios et al. 2001, Bovine, using bovine biglycan - Douglas et al. 2008), III (Bovine, using bovine biglycan - Douglas et al. 2008), VI (Wiberg et al. 2001, 2002, human) and IX (Chen et al. 2006 -  species source of collagen/biglycan unknown).
