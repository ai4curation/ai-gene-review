# TCF19:NuRD represses FBP1 gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9944719](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9944719)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

FBP1 encodes fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase 1, a rate-limiting enzyme in gluconeogenesis (reviewed in Shah and Wondisford, 2023; Bian et al, 2022). FBP1 expression is repressed under high glucose conditions by the binding of TCF19 to H3K4me3 marks (Sen et al, 2017; Mondal et al, 2020). TCF19 recruits the NuRD complex to the gene as assessed by ChIP, and contributes to repression through the removal of active chromatin marks H4K5Ac, H4K8Ac and H3K9Ac marks (Sen et al, 2017).
