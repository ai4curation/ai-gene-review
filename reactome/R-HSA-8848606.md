# PTK6 phosphorylates PXN
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8848606](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8848606)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated PTK6 (BRK) phosphorylates PXN (paxillin) on tyrosine residues Y31 and Y118. PTK6-mediated phosphorylation of PXN can be triggered by EGF stimulation (Chen et al. 2004).
