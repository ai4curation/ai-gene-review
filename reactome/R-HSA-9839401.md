# TGFBR3 degradation
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9839401](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9839401)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Type III TGF β receptor (TGFBR3) binds to GIPC1 at a Class I PDZ binding motif in the cytoplasmic domain of the TGFBR3 and prevents its degradation thus stabilizing the receptor complex at plasma membrane (Blobe et al.,2001)
