# Dengue virion gets internalized
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9922480](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9922480)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DENV-2 virions enter host cells, such as human HeLa cells, mainly through clathrin-dependent endocytosis (Krishnan et al., 2007; reviewed in Cruz-Oliveira et al., 2015). In HeLa cells infected with DENV-2 (NGC strain), fusion occurs in early endosomes (Krishnan et al., 2007), while in monkey BS-C-1 cells infected with DENV-2 (S1 strain), some virion-containing endosomes appear to progress to perinuclear regions, suggestive of late endosomes, before fusion and genome release (van der Schaar et al., 2007; reviewed in Cruz-Oliveira et al., 2015). However, in monkey Vero cells, DENV-2 (NGC strain) internalization is clathrin-independent but dynamin-dependent (Acosta et al., 2009).<br><br>Several host factors are required for flavivirus internalization. In human H1-HeLa cells, Ribonuclease kappa (RNASEK) is required for DENV internalization (Hackett et al., 2015; Perreira et al., 2015). Lymphocyte antigen 6E (LY6E) and Microtubule-associated protein RP/EB family member 3 (MAPRE3, EB3) are also required for DENV2-NGC uptake in human U2OS cells (Hackett & Cherry, 2018). The AP-2 complex, whose function in clathrin-mediated endocytosis is regulated by phosphorylation, is also implicated in DENV infection in human Huh7 cells (Tongmuang et al., 2020). LY6E, RNASEK, and EB3 have been described as part of a specialized pathway in human U2OS cells required for the uptake of large clathrin-dependent endocytosis cargoes, including flaviviruses (Hackett & Cherry, 2018).<br><br>In human Huh-7.5 cells infected with DENV-2 (strain 16681), the receptor HAVCR1 (TIM-1) is involved in activating autophagy. HAVCR1-bound virions are internalized, and TIM-1 can recruit p85 (a PI3K subunit) to Rab5-positive endosomes, leading to the formation of autophagosomes that subsequently contain DENV particles and TIM-1; this process facilitates viral production (Chu et al., 2019).Sunitinib, a small molecule cancer therapeutic, inhibits AAK1, a kinase that phosphorylates the AP-2 complex crucial for clathrin-mediated endocytosis. Sunitinib treatment reduced DENV production in human Huh7 cells infected with DENV-1 (Hawaii strain), DENV-3 (H87 strain), and DENV-4 (H241 strain) (Tongmuang et al., 2020).
