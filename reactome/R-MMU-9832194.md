# Expression of Dll1 during nephron development
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9832194](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9832194)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Dll1 is expressed in the distal regions of renal vesicles and comma-shaped bodies and in proximal regions of S-shaped bodies (Kobayashi et al. 2005, Heliot et al. 2013, Massa et al. 2013). Lhx1 activates Dll1 expression in renal vesicles (Kobayashi et al. 2005) then Hnf1b enhances Dll1 expression in comma-shaped bodies and S-shaped bodies (Heliot et al. 2013, Massa et al. 2013).
