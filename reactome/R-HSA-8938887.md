# PML binds RUNX1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938887](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938887)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

RUNX1 interacts with PML, and the interaction involves the C-terminus of PML and the C-terminus of RUNX1. PML targets RUNX1 to nuclear bodies, which may be important for activation of some RUNX1 target genes, such as CSF2 (GM-CSF) (Nguyen et al. 2005).
