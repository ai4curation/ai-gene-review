# CTNNB1 binding to CD274 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909632](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909632)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Beta-catenin (CTNNB1) regulates CD274 (PD-L1) transcription by binding to its promoter and induced its expression. WNT pathway enables the nuclear accumulation of CTNNB1 which further recruits T-cell factor/lymphoid enhancer factor (TCF/LEF) transcription factors at the PD-L1 promoter to induce its expression (Du et al.,2020, Gan et al.,2020). In cancers, the Wnt/beta-catenin pathway's activation leads to increased PD-L1 expression, helping tumor cells evade immune detection by inhibiting T cell responses (Wang et al, 2023).
