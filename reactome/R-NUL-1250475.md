# Direct recruitment of GRB2:Sos1 to P-Erbb2mut dimer
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-1250475](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-1250475)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Exogenously expressed human GRB2 in complex with mouse Sos1 is directly recruited to phosphorylated rat Neu onocoprotein (Erbb2 mutant isolated from rat neuroblastoma, with valine at position 661 replaced with glutamic acid) stably expressed in transformed mouse fibroblasts.
