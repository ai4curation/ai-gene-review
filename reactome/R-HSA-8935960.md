# RUNX1 binds the TJP1 (ZO-1) gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8935960](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8935960)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds the promoter of the TJP1 gene, which encodes the tight junction component ZO-1 (Miao et al. 2015).
