# p-VAV family:PIP3 binds RAC1:GDP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8951586](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8951586)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Tyrosine-phosphorylated VAVs bind RAC1:GDP as RAC1 guanine nucleotide exchange factors (GEFs), catalysing the exchange of bound GDP for GTP. RAC1 is a key regulator for actin cytoskeleton and cell migration and is also a critical component of endothelial NADPH oxidase (Wittmann et al. 2003, Tan et al. 2008, Ushio–Fukai 2007, Price et al. 2002).
