# N interacts with IFIH1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9833393](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9833393)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

hRSV nucleoprotein N, localized in viral inclusion bodies near the host cell plasma membrane, binds MDA5 (IFIH1), downregulating the interferon response activated by the MAVS:IFIH1 complex assembly (Lifland et al, 2012).
