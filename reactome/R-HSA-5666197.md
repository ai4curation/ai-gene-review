# RHOA:GTP:RTKN binds LIN7B
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5666197](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5666197)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

RTKN (rhotekin) associated with activated RHOA (RHOA:GTP) binds LIN7B, a cell polarity-related protein. Both LIN7B and RTKN are highly expressed in neurons (Sudo et al. 2006).
