# MITF-M binds STT3B gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926489](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926489)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

STT3B was identified as a target of MITF-M-dependent repression in melanoma cells by CRISPR- or si/shRNA-mediated knockdown, ChIP-seq and CUT-and-RUN DNA binding analysis (Laurette et al, 2015; Webster et al, 2014: Dilshat et al, 2021). Global DNA binding motif analysis identified a MITF-M binding site at the STT3B locus, but neither reporter gene nor mutational analysis with the identified motif has been conducted (Dilshat et al, 2021).
