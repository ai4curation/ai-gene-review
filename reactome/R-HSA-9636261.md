# NOS2 gene produces NOS2 protein
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9636261](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9636261)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The inducible nitric oxide synthase (NOS2) gene produces NOS2 protein. It is regulated by a NOS2 promotor, which itself is regulated by mycobacterial PPE family protein PPE2 (Bhat et al. 2017).
