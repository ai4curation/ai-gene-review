# PAPSe is reduced to SeO3(2-) by MET16
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-2408518](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-2408518)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

In yeast, phosphoadenosine phosphosulfate (PAPS) reductase (MET16) is involved in the reduction of 3'-phosphoadenylyl selenate (PAPSe) into selenite (SeO3(2-)) and adenosine3',5'-bismonophosphate (PAP) (Banszky et al. 2003).
