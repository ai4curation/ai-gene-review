# Furin cleaves pro-factor IX to factor IX
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-159796](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-159796)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Furin, associated with the Golgi membrane, cleaves pro-factor IX to form mature, gamma-carboxylated factor IX (Wasley et al. 1993).
