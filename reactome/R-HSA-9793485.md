# PRKN polyubiquitinates RIPK3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9793485](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9793485)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase parkin (PRKN, also known as PARK2), was shown to negatively regulate necroptosis in human and mouse cells through promoting K33-linked polyubiquitination of receptor-interacting protein 3 (RIPK3) (Lee SB et al. 2019). PRKN-catalyzed polyubiquitination of RIPK3 at K197, K302 or K364 decreased RIPK1:RIPK3-mediated necroptosis in human colorectal adenocarcinoma HT-29 cells in response to T/C/Z treatment (TNF-ɑ with cycloheximide and the caspase inhibitor zVAD (Z)) (Lee SB et al. 2019). <br>
