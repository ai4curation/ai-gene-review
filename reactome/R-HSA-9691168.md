# RAC1 translocates to the endoplasmic reticulum membrane
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9691168](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9691168)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

RAC1 mainly localizes to the plasma membrane but it also localizes to endomembranes, such as endoplasmic reticulum (Michaelson et al. 2001).
