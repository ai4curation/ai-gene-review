# Maltose is converted to trehalose
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-868709](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-868709)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

Maltose is converted to trehalose. The enzymatic activity of TreS for this reaction is 2-5 fold stronger than for the reverse reaction (De Smet et al, 2000).
