# G protein alpha (i)-SRC complex catalyzes SRC to p-Y419-SRC
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8964242](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8964242)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

G-Protein Coupled Receptors sense extracellular signals and activate different Guanine nucleotide binding proteins. Upon activation, the alpha subunit of the G protein (GNAI) can directly bind to SRC. In the presence of active GNAI, SRC can autophosphorylate the 419-tyrosine residue, which leads to the subsequent activation of SRC. Physiologically, the SRC family have implications in cell growth and cancer.
