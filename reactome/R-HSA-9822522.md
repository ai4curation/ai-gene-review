# factor VIII -> factor VIIIa + factor VIII B chain+ factor VIII A3 acidic polypeptide +VWF multimer (TF:FVIIa:FXa catalyst)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9822522](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9822522)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Small amounts of factor VIII (FVIII, F8) can be activated by proteolytic cleavage in the early stages of coagulation (Nogami K et al., 2003; Soeda T et al., 2010; Yada K et al., 2011; Kamikubo Y et al., 2017; Furukawa S et al., 2019; Nakajima Y & Nogami K 2024). The cleavage is catalyzed by activated factor X (FXa) or activated factor VII (FVIIa), which associate with tissue factor (TF) to form the ternary TF:FVIIa:FX complex on the surface of damaged endothelial cells (Norledge BV et al., 2003; Venkateswarlu D et al., 2003; reviewed in Vadivel K & Bajaj SP 2012). Cleavage at Arg391, Arg759 and Arg1708 of FVIII by FXa (and possibly by FVIIa) produces pro-coagulant active cofactor FVIIIa, which is essential for the activation of FX (Eaton D et al., 1986; Soeda T et al., 2010; Griffiths AE et al., 2013; Kamikubo Y et al., 2017; Furukawa S et al., 2019; Nakajima Y & Nogami K 2024). In contrast, cleavage at Arg355 or Arg581 by FXa (and FVIIa) inactivates FVIII resulting in a loss of coagulant activity (Eaton D et al., 1986; Nogami K et al., 2003; Plantier JL et al., 2010; Yada K et al., 2011; DeAngelis JP et al., 2012; Nakajima Y & Nogami K 2024).<p>This Reactome event describes the FXa-catalyzed cleavage of FVIII (F8), which circulates in its inactive form bound to von Willebrand factor (VWF). The cleavage occurs at Arg391, Arg759 and Arg1708 producing several smaller polypeptides, FVIII (20-391), FVIII (392-759) and FVIII (1709-2351), that form heterotrimeric activated FVIIIa. The polypeptide FVIII (760-1332) of the C-terminal B domain of the heavy chain is released. The a3 acidic polypeptide on the N-terminal side of the A3 domain of the light chain is also released, however, and as this polypeptide mediates the association of FVIII with VWF, the activated FVIIIa is released from VWF (reviewed in Shen BW et al., 2008; Camire RM & Bos MHA 2009; Childers KC et al., 2022). Activated FVIIIa then binds FIXa contributing to FIXa:FVIIIa-mediated generation of FXa during the initiation phase. FXa together with cofactor FVa proteolytically cleaves prothrombin (FII) to thrombin (FIIa). Once activated, thrombin cleaves more FVIII, leading to the amplified production of FVIIIa, formation of FVIIIa:FIXa complex and activation of FX on the platelet surface during the amplification and propagation phases (reviewed by Fay PJ 2004; Olgasi C et al., 2024).
