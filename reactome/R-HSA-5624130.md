# Myristoylated NPHP3 translocates into the ciliary membrane
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5624130](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5624130)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of ARL3 to UNC119B induces a conformational change that obstructs UNC119B cargo-binding and promotes the release of the myristoylated cargo into the ciliary membrane (Wright et al, 2011; Ismail et al, 2012).
