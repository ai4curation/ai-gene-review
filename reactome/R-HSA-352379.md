# SLC1A5-mediated exchange of alanine and glutamine across the plasma membrane
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-352379](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-352379)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SLC1A5, associated with the plasma membrane, mediates the exchange of extracellular alanine for cytosolic glutamine (Broer et al. 2000).
