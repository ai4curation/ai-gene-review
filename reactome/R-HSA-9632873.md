# p-S10 CDKN1B translocates to the cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9632873](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9632873)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Estrogen stimulation causes a partial redistribution of  CDKN1B into the cytosol in a manner that depends on Ras and PI3K signaling and the atypical PKC zeta (Rodier et al, 2002; Castoria et al, 2004). Nuclear localization of CDKN1B inhibits cell cycle progression (Reynisdottir et al, 1997).
