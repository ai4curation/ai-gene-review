# AcK-NFE2L2 binds the ABCC3 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796065](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796065)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ABCC3 is under transcriptional control of NFE2L2 and contains a functional antioxidant response element within the eighth intron of the human ABCC3 gene. The same has been confirmed through ChIP and Pull-down assay (Canet et al, 2015; Pellegatta et al, 2019)<br><br>
