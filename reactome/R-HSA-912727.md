# CBL binds VAV
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-912727](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-912727)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cbl and Vav interact in thymocytes and peripheral T cells (Marengere et al. 1997). Cbl phosphorylated at Y700 binds Vav1 in 293T cells, leading to Vav ubiquitinylation and proteolytic degradation.
