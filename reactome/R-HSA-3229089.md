# PRC1.4 complex binds H3K27Me3 nucleosomes on CDKN2A promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3229089](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3229089)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PRC1.4 complex, which includes PCGF4 (BMI1), RING1 (RING1A) and RNF2 (RING1B) ubiquitin ligases and CBX proteins (Gao et al. 2012), binds nucleosomes trimethylated on the lysine residue 28 of histone H3 (Me3K-28-HIST1H3A, also known as H3K27Me3), located on CDKN2A promoter (Dietrich et al. 2007, Agherbi et al. 2009, Voncken et al. 2005), through interaction of BMI1 and CBX proteins with Me3K-28-HIST1H3A mark (Gao et al. 2012, Dietrich et al. 2007).
