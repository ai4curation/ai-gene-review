# MAPK3,(MAPK1) phosphorylates GRB2-1:SOS1:p-Y-IRS1,p-Y-IRS2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-109823](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-109823)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

At the beginning of this reaction, 4 molecules of 'ATP', and 1 molecule of 'GRB2:SOS:IRS-P' are present. At the end of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'GRB2:IRS-P', 1 molecule of 'phospho-SOS', and 4 molecules of 'ADP' are present.<br><br> This reaction takes place on the 'internal side of plasma membrane' and  is mediated by the 'kinase activity' of 'ERK1'.<br>
