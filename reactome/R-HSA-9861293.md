# ADM:CALCRL:RAMP2:G-alpha(s):GTP:G-beta:G-gamma dissociates
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9861293](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9861293)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

After binding GTP, G-alpha(s) dissociates from the G-beta:G-gamma subcomplex (Inoue et al. 2019, Ahn et al. 2023) and binds adenylate cyclase, thereby activating production of cAMP.
