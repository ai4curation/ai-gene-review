# ASA- acetylates PTGS2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2314686](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2314686)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The ionized form of aspirin, acetylsalicylate (ASA-) reacts spontaneously with one subunit of PTGS2 dimer (Dong et al. 2011) to acetylate serine residue 516 (Lecomte et al. 1994). The modified enzyme is no longer capable of catalyzing the conversion of arachidonate to PGH2, but acquires the ability to convert it to 15R-HETE.
