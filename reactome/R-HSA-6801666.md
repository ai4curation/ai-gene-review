# PLK2 phosphorylates CENPJ
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6801666](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6801666)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PLK2 phosphorylates centrosome protein CENPJ (CPAP) on serine residues S589 and S585, which is a prerequisite for procentriole formation and centrosome duplication (Chang et al. 2010).
