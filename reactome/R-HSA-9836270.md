# TFAP2A binds TFAP2C
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9836270](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9836270)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TFAP2A may bind TFAP2C through an interaction between a dimerization motif in the C-terminal region of each monomer (Bosher et al. 1996, and inferred from TFAP2A homodimers in Williams and Tjian 1991a, 1991b).
