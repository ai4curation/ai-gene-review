# Dimerization of p-S446-IRF5
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9860273](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9860273)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylated IRF5 (p-S446-IRF5, refers to the UniProtKB canonical sequence) forms homodimers and translocates to the nucleus to stimulate production of type I interferons.
