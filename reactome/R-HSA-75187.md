# TRAIL:TRAIL receptor trimers bind FADD
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-75187](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-75187)

## Summary

The trimeric complex of TRAIL and TRAIL receptor-1 (TNFSF10:TNFRSF10A) or TRAIL receptor-2 (TNFSF10:TNFRSF10B) binds FADD (Sprick et al. 2000).
