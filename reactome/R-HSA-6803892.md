# SFN dimer binds BAX
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6803892](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6803892)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SFN (14-3-3-sigma) dimer forms a complex with BAX. Binding of SFN to BAX prevents BAX translocation from cytosol to mitochondria, thus inhibiting cytochrome C release and apoptosis. SFN therefore promotes G2 cell cycle arrest while simultaneously preventing apoptosis (Samuel et al. 2001).
