# GTSE1 binds PLK1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8852324](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8852324)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

GTSE1 binds PLK1. The two proteins co-localize on centrosomes from G2 phase to prophase, but not after metaphase (Liu et al. 2010).
