# CDC42 GDIs block activation of CDC42
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9013158](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9013158)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The following GDP dissociation inhibitors (GDIs) bind to CDC42 and inhibit its activation:<br>ARHGDIA (Gupta et al. 2013; Murphy et al. 2001)<br>ARHGDIG (Adra et al. 1997)<br>ARHGDIB (Adra et al. 1993)<br>CAV1 (Nevins and Thurmond 2006)
