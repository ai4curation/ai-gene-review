# Formation of tri-heterotetramers of GluN1 (GRIN1), GluN2B (GRIN2B) and GluN3A (GRIN3A)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9609743](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9609743)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

GluN1 (GRIN1) binds to GluN2B (GRIN2B) and GluN3A (GRIN3A) to form a tri-heteromeric NMDA receptor GluN1:GluN2B:GluN3A (GRIN1:GRIN2B:GRIN3A) (Das et al. 1998, Al-Hallaq et al. 2002, Nilsson et al. 2007).
