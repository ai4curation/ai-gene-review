# SREBP1A,2 binds the SC5DL promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2426164](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2426164)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SREBP1A (SREBF1A) or SREBP2, together with NF-Y, bind and transactivate the promoter of the SC5DL gene (Reed et al. 2008, Rome et al. 2008).
