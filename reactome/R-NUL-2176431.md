# Smad7 recruits NEDD4L to activated TGF-beta receptor complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2176431](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2176431)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In an affinity cross-linking assay performed in COS7 cells transfected with recombinant mouse Smad7 and recombinant human NEDD4L, TGFBR2 and TGFBR1, NEDD4L is coimmunoprecipitated with the activated TGF-beta receptor complex in the presence of Smad7 (Kuratomi et al. 2005).
