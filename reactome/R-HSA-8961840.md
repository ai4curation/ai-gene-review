# E2F1 binds the CCNE1 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8961840](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8961840)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

E2F1 binds to E2F binding sites in the promoter of the CCNE1 gene, encoding cyclin E1 (DeGregori et al. 1995, Ohtani et al. 1995).
