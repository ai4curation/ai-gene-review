# NuRD deacetylates telomeric nucleosomes
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9941704](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9941704)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ZNF827-dependent recruitment of NuRD to telomeric nucleosomes results in H4K5 and H4K8 deacetylation and contributes to displacement of shelterin and chromatin compaction (Coromos et al, 2104; Yang et al, 2018; reviewed in Pickett and Reddel, 2015; Goo and Pickett, 2022).
