# Further phosphorylation of CI by CKIalphaepsilon
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209187](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209187)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Previous phosphorylation of Cubitus Interruptus (CI) at Ser852 and Ser888 primes further phosphorylation by CKIalphaepsilon (CKIalpha/DCO) at Ser855, Ser858, and Ser891.
