# PIAS1 SUMOylates SAFB with SUMO2,3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-4085372](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-4085372)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PIAS1 SUMOylates SAFB at lysine 231 and lysine 294 with SUMO2,3 (Garee et al. 2011). SUMOylation of SAFB is required for co-repressor activity.
