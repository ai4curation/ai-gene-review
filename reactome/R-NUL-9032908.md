# Activated Ntrk2 binds Cdk5
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9032908](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9032908)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

In rat neurons, BDNF-activated Ntrk2 (TrkB) binds Cdk5 indirectly, by interacting with p35 (Cdk5r1), a non-cyclin activator of Cdk5 which forms a complex with Cdk5 (Cheung et al. 2007).
