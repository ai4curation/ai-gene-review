# GDIs block activation of RHOG
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9014456](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9014456)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The following GDP dissociation inhibitors (GDIs) bind to RHOG and inhibit its activation:<br>ARHGDIA (Elfenbein et al. 2009)<br>ARHGDIB (Vignal et al. 2001)<br>ARHGDIG (Zalcman et al. 1996; Brunet et al. 2002)
