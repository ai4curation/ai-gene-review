# AMPK is dephosphorylated
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-380949](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-380949)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Normally under low AMP:ATP conditions, the active AMPK is dephosphorylated (possibly by PP2C), and thus inactivated.
