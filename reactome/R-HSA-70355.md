# GALK1 phosphorylates Gal to Gal1P
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-70355](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-70355)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cytosolic galactokinase (GALK1) catalyses the reaction of ATP and D-galactose to form ADP and D-galactose 1-phosphate (Ai at al. 1995).
