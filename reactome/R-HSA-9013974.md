# Auto-ubiquitination of TRAF3 within activated TLR3 complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9013974](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9013974)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

TRIF(TICAM1) signaling activates TRAF3 self-mediated polyubiquitination trough Lys-63 of ubiquitin. The ubiquitinated TRAF3 in turn activates the interferon response (Tseng PH et al. 2010).
