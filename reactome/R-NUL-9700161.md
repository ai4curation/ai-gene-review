# p-7Y ALK dimer phosphorylates Frs2
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9700161](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9700161)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Frs2 is phosphorylated in an ALK kinase-dependent manner when Frs2 and ALK are co-expressed in HEK293 cells (Degoutin et al, 2007).
