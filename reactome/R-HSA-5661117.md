# CCND1:CDK4:PRMT5:pT5-WDR77 methylates methyl-arginine-9 of histone H3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5661117](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5661117)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Histone H3 arginine-9 (H3R8) dimethylation is a PRMT5-specific mark (Pal et al. 2004) associated with gene silencing (Chen et al. 1999, Pal et al. 2004, 2007).
