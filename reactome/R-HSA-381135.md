# MBTPS1 (S1P) cleaves ATF6 (ATF6-alpha)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-381135](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-381135)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Once in the Golgi, ATF6-alpha undergoes two sequential proteolytic cleavages. S1P catalyzes the first of these, probably cleaving the ATF6-alpha polypeptide between residues 418 and 419 based on homology with known S1P cleavage sites in other proteins.
