# AcK-NFE2L2 binds the BCL2L1 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796084](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796084)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NFE2L2 in control of BCL2L1 expression and apoptotic cell death with implications in antioxidant protection, survival of cancer cells, and drug resistance. Promoter mutagenesis, transfection, and chromatin immunoprecipitation assays identified an ARE between nucleotides -608 and -600 in the forward strand of the proximal BCL2L1 promoter that bound to NFE2L2 and led to increased BCL2L1 gene expression (Niture et al, 2013)
