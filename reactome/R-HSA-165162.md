# Phosphorylation of TSC2 by AKT (PKB)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-165162](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-165162)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

AKT (PKB) phosphorylates TSC2 decreasing its activity and disrupting TSC1:TSC2 heterodimer formation (reviewed in Hay & Sonenberg 2004). AKT-mediated phosphorylation induces ubiquitination of the free TSC2 (Inoki et al. 2002). All three isoforms of AKT, AKT1 (Inoki et al. 2002), AKT2 (Kearny et al. 2019), and AKT3 (Wainstein et al. 2022) phosphorylate TSC2. AKT1 may be the dominant TSC2 kinase, with the involvement of different AKT isoforms depending on subcellular localization at which phosphorylated TSC2 is detected and the cell type (Wainstein et al. 2022).
