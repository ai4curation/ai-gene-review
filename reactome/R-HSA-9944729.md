# TCF19:NuRD represses PCK1 gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9944729](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9944729)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PCK1 encodes phosphoenolpyruvate caboxykinase, a key rate-liminting enzyme in gluconeogenesis (reviewed in Shah and Wondisford, 2023; Bian et al, 2022). PCK1 expression is repressed under high glucose conditions by the binding of TCF19 to H3K4me3 marks (Sen et al, 2017; Mondal et al, 2020). TCF19 recruits the NuRD complex to the gene as assessed by ChIP, and contributes to repression through the removal of H4K5Ac, H4K8Ac and H3K9Ac marks, associated with active chromatin (Sen et al, 2017).
