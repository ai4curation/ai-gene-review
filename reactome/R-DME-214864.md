# Phosphorylated CACT and DL/DIF dimer dissociate from the TL:MYD88:TUB:PLL 'signalling complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-214864](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-214864)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Once phosphorylated, Cactus (CACT), in complex with the Dorsal/Dorsal-related immunity factor (DL/DIF) homodimer, dissociates from the Toll (TL) receptor 'signalling complex'.
