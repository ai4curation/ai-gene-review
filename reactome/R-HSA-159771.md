# Furin cleaves pro-protein C to protein C
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-159771](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-159771)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Furin, associated with the Golgi membrane, cleaves pro-protein C to form mature, gamma-carboxylated protein C (Wasley et al. 1993).
