# AcK-NFE2L2 binds the ABCF2 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9796039](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9796039)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ABCF2 is an NFE2L2 target gene that contains a functional ARE sequence in the promoter which is confirmed through ChIP assay in Human Ovarian cancer cell-lines (Bao et al, 2017).
