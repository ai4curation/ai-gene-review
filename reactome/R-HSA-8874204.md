# MBTPS1 (S1P) cleaves CREB3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8874204](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8874204)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In the Golgi membrane, CREB3 (LUMAN) is cleaved by regulated intramembrane proteolysis  (Raggo et al. 2002, Liang et al. 2006, Stirling and O'Hare 2006, Eleveld-Trancikova et al. 2010). As inferred from other cleaved proteins, the reaction is probably catalyzedby MBTPS1 (S1P) at an RQLR motif (Stirling and O'Hare 2006).
