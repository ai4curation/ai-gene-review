# GAB1 recruits CRK,CRKL to MET
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8875540](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8875540)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Splicing isoforms of adapter protein CRK, CRK-1 (CRKI) and CRK-2 (CRKII), as well as the related adapter protein CRKL can be recruited to the activated MET receptor via association with tyrosine phosphorylated GAB1 (Garcia-Guzman et al. 1999, Schaeper et al. 2000, Sakkab et al. 2000, Lamorte et al. 2000, Lamorte et al. 2002).
