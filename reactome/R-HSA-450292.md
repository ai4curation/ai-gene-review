# Formation of Activated Protein 1 (AP-1) complex. cFOS/c-JUN heterodimer.
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-450292](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-450292)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The bZIP domains of Jun and Fos form an X-shaped -helical structure, which binds to the palindromic AP-1 site (TGAGTCA) (Glover and Harrison, 1995).
