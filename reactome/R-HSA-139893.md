# Granzyme-B activates BID by cleavage
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-139893](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-139893)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

GZMB (granzyme B) cleaves BID to produce a p15 truncated form of BID (tBID) (Alimonti et al. 2001).
