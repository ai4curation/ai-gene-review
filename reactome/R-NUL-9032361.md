# Ntrk2-phosphorylated Frs2 binds Ptpn11
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9032361](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9032361)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Upon phosphorylation of rat Frs2 by rat Ntrk2 (TrkB) activated by human BDNF, phosphorylated Frs2 recruits rat Ptpn11 (Shp2) (Easton et al. 2006).
