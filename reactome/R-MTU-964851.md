# Dehydratation of DHQ yields DHS
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-964851](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-964851)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

AroQ, which catalyzes the dehydratation of 3-dehydroquinate (DHQ) to 3-dehydroshikimate (DHS), is a type II DHQ dehydratase. Unlike fungi DHQ dehydratases, AroQ doesn't take part in catabolism reactions (Moore et al, 1992).
