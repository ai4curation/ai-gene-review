# ERBB4 forms heterodimers with ERBB3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1977958](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1977958)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Ligand-stimulated ERBB4 was shown to form heterodimers with ligand-stimulated ERBB3 when human ERBB4 and ERBB3 were exogenously expressed in mouse pro-B-lymphocyte cell line. Heterodimers of ERBB4 and ERBB3 undergo trans-autophosphorylation, but the exact phosphorylation pattern, downstream signaling and physiological significance of these heterodimers have not been studied (Riese et al. 1995).
