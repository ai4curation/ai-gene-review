# Foxo4, Mef2c, and Nkx2-5 bind the Myocd gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9735428](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9735428)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Nkx2-5 (Ueyama et al. 2003), Foxo4, and Mef2c (Creemers et al. 2006) bind the promoter of the Myocd (Myocardin) gene. The major site (called NKE) for binding Nkx2-5 is about 1 kb upstream of the transcription strt site (Ueyama et al. 2003). Foxo4 binds 4 sites and Mef2c binds one site upstream of the transcription start site of Myocd (Creemers et al. 2006).
