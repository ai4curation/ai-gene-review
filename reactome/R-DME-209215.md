# CI activates transcription
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209215](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209215)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Cubitus Interruptus (CI) activates transcription of ptc and several tissue-specific target genes that include direct CI binding sites in their regulatory sequences.  CREB binding protein, CBP (NEJ) co-activator contributes to transcriptional activation.
