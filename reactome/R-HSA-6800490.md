# ATM phosphorylates PIDD1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6800490](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6800490)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In response to DNA damage, activated ATM phosphorylates PIDD1 at threonine residue T788, a prerequisite for the subsequent interaction with CRADD (RAIDD) (Ando et al. 2012).
