# Diphthamide biosynthesis protein 7 hydrolyzes a methyl group on Me-diphthine elongation factor 2
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-5367021](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-5367021)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

Yeast cytosolic diphthamide biosynthesis protein 7 (RRT2) catalyzes the removal of a methyl group of Me-diphthine elongation factor 2 (EFT1), yielding diphthine EFT1 (Lin et al. 2014).
