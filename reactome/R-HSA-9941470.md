# SLC26A11 imports SO4(2-) and H+ into cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9941470](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9941470)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Sodium-independent sulfate anion transporter SLC26A11 transports a sulfate anion (SO4(2-)) together with a proton from extracellular space to the cytosol (Vincourt et al., 2003).
