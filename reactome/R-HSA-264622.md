# Cho is acetylated to AcCho by CHAT
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-264622](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-264622)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In the cytosol, choline O-acetyltransferase (CHAT) acetylates choline (Cho) to produce acetylcholine (AcCho) (Toussaint 1992).<br><br>AcCho is synthesised in the cytoplasm of cholinergic neurons from acetyl-CoA and Cho by CHAT enzyme.
