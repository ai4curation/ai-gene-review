# SETDB1 in me3-K16-ATF7IP:SETDB1:MORC2:HUSH complex:L1 RNA trimethylates lysine-9 of histone H3 (H3K9me3)

SETDB1 tethered to retroelement nascent RNA via the HUSH complex trimethylates lysine-9 of histone H3 in nucleosomes of retroelement chromatin (Seczynska et al. 2022).
