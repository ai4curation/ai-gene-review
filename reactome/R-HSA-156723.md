# Regulation of KIF20A (MKL2) by phosphorylation
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-156723](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-156723)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The polo-like kinase PLK1 phosphorylates KIF20A (also known as MKLP2 i.e. mitotic kinesin-like protein 2) on serine residue S528.
