# 2x p-5Y-RET:GDNF:GFRA complexes bind PLCgamma1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8853755](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8853755)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylated RET binds Phospholipase C gamma 1 (PLCG1) via tyrosine 905 (Borello et al. 1996) and/or tyrosine-1015 (Lundgren et al. 2012).
