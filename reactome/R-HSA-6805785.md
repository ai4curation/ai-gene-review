# AKT phosphorylates PHF20
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-6805785](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-6805785)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

AKT phosphorylates PHF20 on serine residue S291 (Li et al. 2013).  AKT-mediated PHF20 phosphorylation inhibits DNA damage-induced stabilitzation of TP53 protein (Li et al. 2013).
