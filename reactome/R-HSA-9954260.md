# SFN binds S-farn-C589,p-S156,T590-GBP1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9954260](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9954260)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylation of GBP1 at Ser156 promotes GBP1 binding to 14-3-3σ (SFN), which sequesters GBP1 in the cytosol and prevents its association with membranes. This inhibitory interaction is thought to suppress GBP1 activity in uninfected cells (Fisch D et al., 2023).
