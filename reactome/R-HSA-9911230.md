# ELANE binds NOTCH2NLA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9911230](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9911230)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

<p>ELANE (also known as ELA2 or NE), a human neutrophil elastase, is a serine protease that interacts with NOTCH2NLA (also known as N2N or NOTCH2NL), as initially detected in a yeast two hybrid screen and confirmed <i>in vitro</i>, using GST-tagged NOTCH2NLA and radioactively labeled ELANE (Duan et al. 2004). The experimental evidence suggests that the C-terminus of ELANE is involved in NOTCH2NLA binding (Duan et al. 2004). The C-terminus of NOTCH2NLA is required but not sufficient for the interaction with ELANE (Duan et al. 2004).</p><p>Disease variants involving the C-terminus of ELANE, which are the underlying cause of human hereditary neutropenia, diminish the interaction between ELANE and NOTCH2NLA (Duan et al. 2004).</p>
