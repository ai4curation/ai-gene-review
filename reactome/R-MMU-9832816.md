# Expression of Pax6 in anterior neural plate
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9832816](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9832816)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

During gastrulation at embryonic day 7.5, Pax6 is strongly expressed in the anterior neural plate and more weakly expressed in the posterior neural plate (Iwafuchi-Doi et al. 2012). During differentiation of embryonic stem cells to neural tissue, Pax6 is directly activated by Pou3f1 (Zhu et al. 2014) and Znf521 (Zfp521) (Kamiya et al. 2011).
