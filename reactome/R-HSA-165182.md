# Phosphorylation of complexed TSC2 by PKB
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-165182](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-165182)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Phosphorylation of TSC2 by AKT (PKB) disrupts TSC1:TSC2 heterodimer formation (Hay & Sonenberg 2004) inducing ubiquitination and degradation of both TSC1 and TSC2 through the proteasome pathway (Inoki et al. 2002 and Proud 2002). Degradation of TSC2 is not always seen; dissociation of TSC2 from the lysosomal membrane has been suggested as an alternative mechanism (Menon et al. 2014).
