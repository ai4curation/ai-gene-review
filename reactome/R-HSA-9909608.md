# IRF1 binding to CD274 promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909608](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909608)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

IRF1 (Interferon Regulatory Factor 1) regulates CD274 (PD-L1) transcription primarily in response to interferon signals. Upon activation by interferons, IRF1 translocates to the nucleus and binds to specific interferon-stimulated response elements (ISREs) in the PD-L1 promoter region. This binding recruits the transcriptional machinery necessary for initiating PD-L1 gene transcription. In the tumor microenvironment, IRF1-mediated upregulation of PD-L1 enhances the tumor cells' ability to evade immune detection by inhibiting T cell activity (Padmanabhan et al., 2022, Garcia-Diaz et al., 2019, Moon et al., 2017). IRF1 binding to PDL-1 promoter is further enhanced by BRD4 by helping IRF1 recruitment ot the promoter of PDL-1 to increase PDL1 transcription (Wang et al., 2022).
