# PI3K bound to phosphorylated heterodimers of ERBB2 KD mutants and ERBB3 converts PIP2 to PIP3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9664664](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9664664)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

For the ERBB3-binding  ERBB2 KD mutants that were shown to activate PI3K/AKT signaling (evidenced by activating phosphorylation of AKT), it is assumed that, like the wild type ERBB2:ERBB3 heterodimer, the heterodimers of these ERBB2 KD mutants with ERBB3 bind to the PI3K complex, leading to conversion of PIP2 to PIP3.<br><br>Phosphorylated heterodimers of the following ERBB2 KD mutants with ERBB3 are assumed to, like the wild type ERBB2:ERBB3 heterodimer, recruit and activate the PI3K complex, resulting in conversion of PIP2 to PIP3:<br><br>ERBB2 L755S (Kancha et al. 2011, Cocco et al. 2018, Croessmann et al. 2019);<br>ERBB2 L755P (Kancha et al. 2011);<br>ERBB2 I767M (Ng et al. 2015);<br>ERBB2 V777L (Kancha et al. 2011);<br>ERBB2 T798I (Hanker et al. 2017);<br>ERBB2 T798M (Kancha et al. 2011, Rexer et al. 2013);<br>ERBB2 T862A (Kancha et al. 2011);<br>ERBB2 L869R (Hanker et al. 2017);<br>ERBB2 H878Y (Kancha et al. 2011, Hu, Hu et al. 2015, Hu, Wan et al. 2015);<br>ERBB2  Y772_A775dup (Wang et al. 2006)<br><br>Activation of PI3K/AKT signaling downstream of the following ERBB2 KD cancer mutants has not been studied and they are annotated as candidates:<br><br>ERBB2 L755M<br>ERBB2 L755W<br>ERBB2 V777M<br>ERBB2 V777E<br>ERBB2 T733I<br>ERBB2 L869Q<br>ERBB2 H878R
