# Expression of Leptin
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1183003](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1183003)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The Ob gene encoding leptin is transcribed to yield mRNA and translated to yield protein. Expression of leptin is positively regulated by C/EBPalpha (CEBPA, Miller et al. 1996, Melzner et al. 2002) and negatively regulated by PPARG in adipocytes (De Vos et al. 1996).
