# Activated Ntrk3 binds Plcg1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9034794](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9034794)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Upon activation by recombinant human NTF3 (NT-3), autophosphorylated recombinant rat Ntrk3 (TrkC) binds mouse Phospholipase C gamma1 (Plcg1) (Yuen and Mobley 1999, Huang and Reichardt 2001).
