# Translation of p14ARF mRNA is inhibited by miR-24
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3209111](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3209111)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

MicroRNA miR-24 inhibits translation of p14ARF mRNA without causing mRNA degradation. This results in high p14ARF transcript level accompanied by low p14ARF protein level (To et al. 2012).
