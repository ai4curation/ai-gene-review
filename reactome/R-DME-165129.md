# GDP is exchanged for GTP on DRheb
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-165129](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-165129)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The GEF:DRheb-GDP complex freely exchanges its bound nucleotide for free nucleotide from the cytosol. Normally, the concentration of cytosolic GTP is about ten times greater than the concentration of GDP, so under physiological conditions, this exchange event favors formation of DRheb-GTP.
