# The complex of RUNX1 and YAP1 binds the ITCH gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8956649](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8956649)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The complex of RUNX1, presumably associated with CBFB, and YAP1 binds the promoter of the ITCH gene, encoding the E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase ITCH (Levy, Reuven and Shaul 2008).
