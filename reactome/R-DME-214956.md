# Full-length EA is cleaved by SNK serine protease
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-214956](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-214956)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

In dorsoventral patterning, the full-length serine protease zymogen, Easter (EA) is cleaved by active serine protease, Snake (SNK), between residues Arg127 and Ile128.  The removal of the inhibitory prodomain results in an active EA serine protease.
