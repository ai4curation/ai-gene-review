# SERPINB1 binds CASP4
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9948160](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9948160)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SERPINB1 binding can inhibit the activation of inflammatory caspase-4 (CASP4), thereby modulating CASP4-mediated inflammation and pyroptosis (Choi YJ et al., 2019).
