# p-S516-Arhgef7 activates Rac1
**Reactome ID:** [R-RNO-9619811](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-RNO-9619811)
**Species:** Rattus norvegicus

## Summary

In rat neurons, Camk1-mediated phosphorylation of Arhgef7 (Beta-Pix) at serine S516 enhances guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) activity of Arhgef7, leading to Rac1 activation. Rac1 and its downstream effector Pak1 are involved in NMDA receptor activity-dependent synaptogenesis (Saneyoshi et al. 2013).
