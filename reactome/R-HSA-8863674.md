# CDC25C binds YWHAE (14-3-3-epsilon)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8863674](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8863674)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of CDC25C to 14-3-3-epsilon (YWHAE), which promotes retention of CDC25C in the cytosol, is inhibited by CDK5-mediated phosphorylation of CDC25C (Chang et al. 2012).
