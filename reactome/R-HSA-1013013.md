# Association of GABA B receptor with G protein beta-gamma subunits
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1013013](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1013013)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activation of GABA B receptors by GABA stimulates Gi/o proteins releasing the G beta gamma subunit that binds to voltage dependent Ca2+ channels.
