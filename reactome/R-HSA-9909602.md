# MYC,MYCN binding to CD274 gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909602](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909602)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

c-Myc (MYC) regulates CD274 (PD-L1) transcription by directly binding to E-box sequences within the PD-L1 promoter region. Upon activation, c-Myc forms a complex with Max, another transcription factor, and this complex attaches to the E-box motifs, recruiting transcriptional co-activators and the transcriptional machinery to initiate PD-L1 gene transcription. This upregulation of PD-L1 by c-Myc contributes to immune evasion in MYC-overexpressing cancer, as increased PD-L1 expression inhibits T cell activity, allowing tumor cells to escape immune detection (Liang et al., 2020, Casey et al., 2016, Atsaves et al., 2017)
