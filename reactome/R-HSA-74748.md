# De-phosphorylation of p-Y427-SHC1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-74748](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-74748)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

At the beginning of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'phospho-SHC' is present. At the end of this reaction, 1 molecule of 'Orthophosphate', and 1 molecule of 'SHC transforming protein' are present.<br><br> This reaction takes place in the 'cytosol' and  is mediated by the 'protein tyrosine phosphatase activity' of 'protein tyrosine phosphatase' (Sasaoka et al. 1996).<br> This is a  black box event since the event of dephosphorylation is inferred from other reaction where this process occurs after other stimulation (Gu et al. 1999).
