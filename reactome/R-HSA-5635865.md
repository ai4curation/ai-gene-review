# SMURF1/2 dissociates from ub-PTCH1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5635865](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5635865)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

After ubiquitinating PTCH1, the SMURF E3 ligase presumably dissociates, although this has not been studied in detail (Huang et al, 2013; Yue et al, 2014).
