# NPM1-ALK and FOXM1-dependent CCNB1 gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9851092](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9851092)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Expression of CCNB1, a known FOXM1 transcriptional target, is upregulated in NPM1-ALK+ ALCL cells in a NPM1-ALK and FOXM1-dependent manner (Haque et al, 2019; Leung et al, 2001).  FOXM1 binds to a consensus sequence in the CCNB1 promoter as assessed by chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP), and knockdown of FOXM1 abrogates CCNB1 expression in NPM1-ALK+ cells.  Expression of CCNB1 and other FOXM1 targets contributes to cell growth and colony formation in NPM1-ALK cell lines (Haque et al, 2019).
