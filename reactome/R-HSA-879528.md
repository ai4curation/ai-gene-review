# SLCO2A1 transports PGT substrates from extracellular region to cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-879528](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-879528)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The human gene SLCO2A1 encodes prostaglandin transporter PGT. It is ubiquitously expressed and can transport the protaglandins PGD2, PGE1, PGE2 and PGF2A (Lu et al. 1996).
