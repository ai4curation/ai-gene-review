# RUNX1:CBFB binds the PF4 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8938176](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8938176)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The RUNX1:CBFB complex binds to two RUNX1 response elements in the promoter of the PF4 gene, encoding Platelet factor 4 (Aneja et al. 2011).
