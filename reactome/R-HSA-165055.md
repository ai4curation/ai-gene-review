# Hydrolysis of Ran:GTP to Ran:GDP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-165055](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-165055)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Ran-GAP, a Ran-specific GTPase-activating protein converts Ran-GTP to Ran-GDP, producing a Ran-GTP gradient across the nuclear membrane.
