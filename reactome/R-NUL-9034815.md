# Ntrk3 phosphorylates Plcg1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9034815](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9034815)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Kinase activity of rat Ntrk3 (TrkC) is needed for tyrosine phosphorylation and activation of rat Plcg1 in response to recombinant human NTF3 (NT-3) (Marsh and Palfrey 1996). Ntrk3-mediated phosphorylation sites on Plcg1 have not been determined but are predicted to involve four tyrosine residues whose phosphorylation is known to be required for the phospholipase activity of Plcg1: Y472, Y771, Y783 and Y1253.
