# NS1 binds MMP9 and CTNNB1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9921198](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9921198)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

DENV NS1 protein binds to matrix metalloproteinase-9 (MMP9) and beta-catenin (CTNNB1). This interaction apparently contributes to disruption of the junctions between endothelial cells and could be a factor in Dengue hemorrhagic fever. Additionally NS1 appears to enhance MMP9 expression. These results were obtained from experiments using human cell lines and mouse models with the Dengue serotype-2 (DENV-2) NG-C strain (Pan et al., 2021).
