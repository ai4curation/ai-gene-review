# AVP:AVPR2:heterotrimeric G(s) complex exchanges GDP for GTP
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-432195](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-432195)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The AVP:AVPR2 complex activates G-protein alpha s by causing a conformational change in G-protein alpha s that causes it to release GDP and bind GTP.
