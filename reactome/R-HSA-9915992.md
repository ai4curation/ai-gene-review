# Arachidonate is hydroxylated to 17-HETE by CYP(1)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9915992](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9915992)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Cytochrome P450s 1A1 (CYP1A1), 1A2 (CYP1A2), and 1B1 (CYP1B1) convert arachidonate to 17-hydroxyeicosatetraenoate (17-HETE) (Choudhary et al. 2004).
