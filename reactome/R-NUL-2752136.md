# Osteopontin binds Integrin alpha4beta1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-2752136](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-2752136)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Osteopontin (SPP1) is a highly phosphorylated sialoprotein that is a prominent component of the mineralized extracellular matrices of bones and teeth. It binds multiple integrins including alpha4beta1 (Bayliss et al. 1998).
