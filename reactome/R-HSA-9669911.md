# Phosphorylation of juxtamembrane domain KIT mutants
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9669911](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9669911)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Juxtamembrane mutations in KIT lead to constitutive, ligand-independent autophosphorylation of receptor, activating downstream signaling (Hirota et al, 1998; Kitayama et al, 1995; Tsujimura et al, 1994; Furitsu et al, 1993; reviewed in Roskoski, 2018).
