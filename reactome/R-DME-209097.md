# ARM binds to APC and AXN in the 'destruction complex'
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209097](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209097)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Phosphorylated APC (APC/APC2) and Axin (AXN) are able to bind cytoplasmic Armadillo (ARM) more strongly.  This now brings ARM into the 'destruction complex' and within reach of the complexed kinases.
