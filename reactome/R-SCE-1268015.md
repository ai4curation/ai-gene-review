# Precursor proteins enter TIM23 SORT complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-SCE-1268015](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-SCE-1268015)
**Species:** Saccharomyces cerevisiae

## Summary

The TIM23 Complex resides in the inner membrane of the mitochondrion and transfers precursor proteins to the inner membrane.  The TIM23 complex appears to adopt different configurations (and perhaps different subunit compositions) depending on whether the substrate is destined for the inner membrane or the matrix. Here we refer to the TIM23 SORT complex as the configuration that delivers inner membrane proteins. The TIM21 subunit is required for this activity.The N-terminal presequence of precursors first interacts with TIM50 and TIM23 (Mokranjac et al. 2003, Martinez-Caballero et al. 2007, Tamura et al. 2009). The TIM17 and TIM23 subunits form a channel and are required to initiate translocation of precursors (Milislav et al. 2001).<br>Substrates of the TIM23 SORT complex targeted to the inner membrane include CYB2 (Krayl et al. 2007, Hutu et al. 2008, Popov-Celeketic et al. 2008, Tamura et al. 2009, Mokranjac et al. 2009), DLD (Mokranjac et al. 2009), ATP9 (Leuenberger et al. 1999, Milisav et al. 2001, Tamura et al. 2009), COQ2 (Leuenberger et al. 1999), TIM54 (Leuenberger et al. 1999, Kurz et al. 1999), COX4 (Martinez-Caballero et al. 2007), COX5A (Mokranjac et al. 2003), and ATP2 (Dekker et al. 1997, Mokranjac et al. 2003).
