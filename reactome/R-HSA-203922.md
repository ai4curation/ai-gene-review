# Exportin-5 recognizes 3' overhang of pre-miRNA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-203922](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-203922)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Exportin-5 binds pre-microRNAs having 2-nucleotide overhangs at the 3' end. Binding is independent of sequence and depends on GTP.
