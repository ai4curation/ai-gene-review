# Activated PPARG binds PTEN gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8944099](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8944099)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The nuclear receptor PPARG (PPARgamma), activated by ligand binding, binds to peroxisome proliferator response elements (PPREs) in the promoter of the PTEN gene to activate PTEN transcription. It has not been tested whether nuclear receptors that heterodimerize with PPARG are involved in transcriptional regulation of PTEN (Patel et al. 2001).
