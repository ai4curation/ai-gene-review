# SLC46A1 transports FOLA from extracellular region to cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-200729](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-200729)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

SLC46A1 protein in the plasma membrane mediates the reversible transport of folate between the extracellular space and the cytosol. Retention of folate within the cell is dependent on polyglutamate addition. The SLC46A1 gene is expressed in several tissues in the body, and is needed for absorption of dietary folate from the intestinal lumen (Chen et al. 1996; Qiu et al. 2006; Umpathy et al. 2007).
