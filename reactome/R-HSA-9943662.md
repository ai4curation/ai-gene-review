# CHEK1 phosphorylates NEK11
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9943662](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9943662)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

NEK11 kinase is activated by DNA damage in an ATR- and CHEK1-dependent manner, but independently of ATM and CHEK2 (Melixetian et al. 2009). The consensus CHEK1/CHEK2 site in NEK11, serine 273 (S273), is phosphorylated by CHEK1 <i>in vitro</i> and required for ionizing radiation (IR)-induced activation of NEK11 (Melixetian et al. 2009). Endogenous NEK11 is phosphorylated on S273 in response to IR in a CHEK1-dependent manner (Melixetian et al. 2009).
