# ACE2 is glycosylated to glycosylated-ACE2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9683488](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9683488)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

In the course of its transport to the cell surface Angiotensin Converting Enzyme 2 (ACE2) is glycosylated, a process inhibited by chloroquine in cell culture conditions (Vincent et al. 2005).
