# Autocleavage of HH
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209313](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209313)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The autoprocessing event results in cleavage of Hedgehog (HH) between Gly207 and Cys208, coupled to the covalent addition of cholesterol to Gly207 via an ester linkage to form N-HH.
