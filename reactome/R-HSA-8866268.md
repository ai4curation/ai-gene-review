# ADBRK1,2 phosphorylate AVPR2
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8866268](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8866268)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

AVPR2 is phosphorylated by ADBRK1 and 2 in an agonist-dependent manner as a prerequisite for beta-arrestin binding and endocytosis (Ren et al, 2005; reviewed in Gurevich and Gurevich, 2006).
