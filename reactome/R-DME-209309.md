# Phosphorylated REL-68 transports from the cytosol to the nucleus
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209309](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209309)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The phosphorylated N-terminal cleaved domain of Relish (REL-68) translocates from the cytosol to the nucleus.  The corresponding phosphorylated C-terminal cleaved domain (REL-49) remains in the cytoplasm.
