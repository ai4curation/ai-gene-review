# acetylation of serine
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-936615](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-936615)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

Although there is no direct evidence for CysE catalyzing the acetylation of serine, it has 58% identity with the serine acetyltransferase of <i>Corynebacterium glutamicum</i> and is upregulated in stress conditions (Haitani et al, 2006; Schnell et al, 2007; Mehra and Kaushal, 2009).
