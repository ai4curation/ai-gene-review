# Stat6, Nfatc2 (Nfat1), and Nicd1,2 bind the Gata3 gene and cause displacement of repressor complexes (Prc1, Rbpj:NcoR) by activator complexes (Mll1, Rbpj:Nicd1,2)
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9977960](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9977960)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Il4 signaling via Il4ra produces phosphorylated Stat6 dimers (reviewed in Keegan et al. 2021), which enter the nucleus and bind within the transcribed region of the Gata3 gene (Wei et al. 2010, Onodera et al. 2010) and in the proximal and distal promoters of Gata3 (Scheinman and Avni 2009). Binding of phosphorylated Stat6 causes displacement of repressive Prc1 complexes and recruitment of Mll1 complexes (Onodera et al. 2010). The displacement of Prc1 by Mll1 complexes is most pronounced between the proximal and distal promoters of the Gata3 gene and is not evident about 5 kb upstream of the distal promoter (Onodera et al. 2010).<br>Signaling by the T cell receptor results in increased cytosolic Ca2+, which activates calcineurin to dephosphorylate multiply phosphorylated Nfatc2 (Nfat1), yielding monophosphorylated Nfatc2 (p-S245-Nfatc2) (Scheinman and Avni 2009) that enters the nucleus and binds 3 conserved noncoding sequences, the distal promoter, and the proximal promoter of the Gata3 gene (Scheinman and Avni 2009).<br>Jagged1 or Jagged2 on antigen presenting cells activates signaling by Notch1 and Notch2 in T cells to enhance expression of Gata3 (Amsen et al. 2004, 2007, Fang et al. 2007). Nicd1 or Nicd2 resulting from Notch signaling binds Rbpj bound to the upstream (distal) promoter of the Gata3 gene, about 10 kb upstream of the proximal promoter (Amsen et al. 2007, Fang et al. 2007). Nicd1,2 causes displacement of Rbpj-bound NCoR corepressors by Maml and coactivators.
