# sulfite is reduced to sulfide
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-936703](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-936703)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

Three molecules of reduced ferredoxin are needed to reduce sulfite to sulfide. Electrons are transferred to sulfite with the help of Sir, an enzyme with siroheme and an iron-sulfur cluster as cofactors (Schnell et al, 2005; Pinto et al, 2007).
