# MITF-M-dependent CDH2 gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926529](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926529)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The CDH2 gene encodes N-cadherin, a calcium-dependent cell adhesion protein. CDH2 gene has been identified as a target of MITF-M-dependent repression in melanoma cells (DIlshat et al, 2021: Laurette et al, 2015). Consistent with this, siRNA- or CRISPR-mediated knockdown of MITF-M increases CDH2 expression, confirmed by RT-qPCR and Western blot analysis (Dilshat et al, 2021).<br>CDH2 is generally considered to be a mesenchymal marker and its upregulation is associated with epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT) (reviewed in Brabletz et al, 2021).
