# 2xRANK binds RANKL,sRANKL trimer:RANK
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-5676592](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-5676592)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The binding of RANKL trimer to RANK causes the trimerization of the receptor, which activates the signalling pathway and results in osteogenesis from progenitor cells and the activation of mature osteoclasts (Liu et al. 2010)
