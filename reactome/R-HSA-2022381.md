# Cathepsin Z (Cathepsin X) hydrolyzes AGT(25-34) (Angiotensin-(1-10)) to AGT(25-32) (Angiotensin-(1-8))

Cathepsin Z (cathepsin X) hydrolyzes angiotensin-(1-10) (angiotensin I) to yield angiotensin-(1-8) (angiotensin II) (Nagler et al. 2010).
