# ELANE cleaves NOTCH2NLA
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9911236](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9911236)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

<p>ELANE (also known as ELA2 or NE), a human neutrophil elastase, is a serine protease that interacts with NOTCH2NLA (also known as N2N or NOTCH2NL) and cleaves it within EGF repeats (Duan et al. 2004). ELANE-mediated proteolysis of NOTCH2NLA is slowed down by the C-terminus of NOTCH2NLA (Duan et al. 2004). Some of the ELANE mutations that cause cyclic and severe congenital neutropenia disrupt the interaction between NOTCH2NLA and ELANE and impair ELANE-mediated NOTCH2NLA proteolysis (Duan et al. 2004). NOTCH2 may also be cleaved by ELANE (Duan et al. 2004). Wild type ELANE inhibits NOTCH2 transcriptional activity in a dose-dependent manner (Duan et al. 2004).</p>
