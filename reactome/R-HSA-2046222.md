# CHST2,3,5,6 transfer sulfate to GlcNAc on keratan chain
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2046222](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2046222)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Carbohydrate sulfotransferases 2, 3, 5 and 6 (CHST2, 3, 5 and 6) catalyze the transfer of sulfate to position 6 of non-reducing ends of N-acetylglucosamine (GlcNAc) residues within keratan-like molecules (Sakaguchi et al. 2000, Lee et al. 1999, Akama et al. 2002). Keratan(4)-PG represents keratan before sulfation has occurred.
