# Expression of Dll1 in paraxial mesoderm
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9793915](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9793915)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Delta-like 1 (Dll1) is expressed in paraxial (presomitic) mesoderm and then becomes confined to caudal halves of somites during somitogenesis (Bettenhausen et al. 1995, Teppner et al. 2007). Expression of Dll1 is activated by Wnt signaling in paraxial mesoderm (Dunty et al. 2008) via Lef1, which binds multiple sites in the DLL1 promoter and recruits beta-catenin (Ctnnb1) (Galceran et al. 2004, Hofmann et al. 2004). Tbx6 also directly activates Dll1 expression in paraxial mesoderm (Hofmann et al. 2004, White and Chapman 2005, Beckers et al. 2000). Expression of Dll1 on the cell surface of paraxial mesoderm cells is oscillatory (Bone et al. 2014, Takagi et al. 2020). Presenilin 1 (Psen1) is required for expression of Dll1 and Notch1 in the paraxial mesoderm (Wong et al. 1997).
