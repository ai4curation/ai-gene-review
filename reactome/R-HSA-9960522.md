# CASP5 cleaves IL1B at D27
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9960522](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9960522)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Caspase-5 (CASP5) cleaves pro–IL‑1β at D27, generating a p27 fragment that is considered inactive and unable to engage the interleukin-1 receptor (IL-1R), thereby blocking IL-1β signaling (Exconde PM et al., 2023; reviewed by Exconde PM, 2024).
