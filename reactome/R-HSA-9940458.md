# GATAD2B is SUMOylated
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9940458](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9940458)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

GATAD2B is SUMOylated at lysine residues K33 (Gong et al, 2006). SUMOylation of GATAD2B contributes to the repressive activity of the NuRD complex, and mutation of K33 reduces the interaction of GATAD2B with RBBP4 in HEK293 and NIH 3T3 cells (Gong et al, 2006). Post-translational modifications of NuRD components may alter the compositional bias of the complex and in this way affect function (reviewed in Reid et al, 2023; Torchy et al, 2015).
