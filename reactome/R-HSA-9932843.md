# PHF10 binds SMARCC dimer:SMARCDs:SMARCE1:SMARCB1:ARID2:BRD7
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9932843](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9932843)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PHF10 is a bromodomain-containing protein that is specifically part of the pBAF complex (Yuan et al, 2022; Wang et al, 2022; Mashtalir et al, 2018; Mashtalir et al, 2020; Michel et al, 2018). PHF10 interacts with pBAF-specific component ARID2 as well as with SMARCC and SMARCB1 (Wang et al, 2022; Yuan et al, 2022; Mashtalir et al, 2018; Mashtalir et al, 2020). Along with BRD7, PHF10 contributes to the histone tail recognition function of pBAF (Yuan et al, 2022; Wang et al, 2022). In melanoma cells, PHF10 has been shown to interact with MYC, helping to recruit pBAF to target gene promoters (Soshnikova et al, 2021).
