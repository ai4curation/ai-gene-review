# CHD7 and CHD8 bind FAM124B
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9944450](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9944450)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

CHD7 and CHD8 have been shown to interact by yeast two hybrid, co-immunoprecipitation and bimolecular fluorescence complementation assay (Batsukh et al, 2010; Batsukh et al, 2012). Both proteins additionally interact with a poorly characterized protein FAM124B (Batsukh et al, 2012). The functional significance of this complex is unknown, but mutations in CHD7 that are associated with CHARGE syndrome destabilize CHD7:CHD8 two hybrid interactions, suggesting a potential role for a multisubunit CHD complex in the disorder (Batsukh et al, 2012).
