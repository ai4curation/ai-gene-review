# Glucanotrehalose is hydrolyzed to 1,4-alpha-glucan and trehalose
**Reactome ID:** [R-MTU-868657](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MTU-868657)
**Species:** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

## Summary

From the glucanotrehalose, the enzyme TreZ cleaves one molecule of trehalose, leaving a linear glucan that is two sugars shorter than before. This is the rate-limiting step in the TreYZ pathway of trehalose biosynthesis (De Smet et al, 2000).
