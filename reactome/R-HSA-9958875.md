# NFκB stimulates IL6 gene expression
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9958875](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9958875)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The NFκB complex directly activates IL6 gene transcription, likely in collaboration with other transcription factors such as CEBPB (Shimizu et al. 1990, Libermann and Baltimore 1990, Matsusaka et al. 1993, Acosta et al. 2008).
