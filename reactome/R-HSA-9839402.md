# TGFBR3 binding to GIPC1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9839402](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9839402)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Type III TGF β receptor (TGFBR3) binds to GIPC1 at a Class I PDZ binding motif in the cytoplasmic domain of TGFBR3 and prevent TGFBR3 degradation resulting in enhanced expression at plasma membrane (Blobe et al., 2001).
