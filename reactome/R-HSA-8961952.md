**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8961952](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8961952)

## Summary

E2F1 directly stimulates transcription of the CDT1 gene, encoding DNA replication factor Cdt1 (Yoshida and Inoue 2004).
