# TIM is phosphorylated by CK2
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-432535](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-432535)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

Timeless (TIM) is phosphorylated on unknown serine/threonine residues by casein kinase II (CK2).  CK2 phosphorylation appears to be important for subsequent nuclear entry of TIM.
