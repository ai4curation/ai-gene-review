# Masitinib-resistant KIT mutants do not bind masitinib
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9669863](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9669863)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Activated KIT receptors with oncogenic mutations in the ATP-binding cleft and the activation loop of the kinase are resistant to inhibition with masitinib (Serrano et al, 2019).
