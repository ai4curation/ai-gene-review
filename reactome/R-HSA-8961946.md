# E2F1 binds the CDT1 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8961946](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8961946)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

E2F1 binds to E2F binding sites in the promoter of the CDT1 gene, encoding DNA replication factor Cdt1 (Yoshida and Inoue 2004).
