# Phosphorylation of SMO by PKA-C1
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209341](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209341)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The Ser/Thr kinase, Protein kinase A (PKA-C1) carries out priming phosphorylation of the transmembrane protein Smoothened (SMO) at Ser667, Ser687, and Ser740.
