# Trimerization of TRAIL:TRAIL receptor complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-141139](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-141139)

## Summary

The complex of TRAIL (TNFSF10) and TRAIL receptor-1 (TNFRSF10A) or TRAIL receptor-2 (TNFRSF10B) trimerizes (Sprick et al. 2000, Reis et al. 2011).
