# FOXP3 in complex with RUNX1 binds the CTLA4 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8877414](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8877414)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The complex of FOXP3 and the RUNX1:CBFB heterodimer binds the promoter of the CTLA4 gene (Ono et al. 2007). It is probable that FOXP3 acts as part of a complex with NFATC2 (Wu et al. 2006).
