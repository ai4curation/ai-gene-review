# Neurotransmitter uptake and metabolism In glial cells
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-112313](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-112313)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Neuotransmitter uptake by astrocytes is mediated by a specific transporter located on the astrocytic membrane. The imported neurotransmitter is metabolized and transported back to the neuron.
