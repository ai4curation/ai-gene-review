# Dentin matrix protein 1 binds integrin alphaVbeta3
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-4086200](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-4086200)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Dentin matrix phosphoprotein 1 (DMP1) is a non-collagenous, acidic extracellular matrix protein expressed chiefly in bone and dentin. DMP1 acts via interaction with alphaVbeta3 integrin (Wu et al. 2011).
