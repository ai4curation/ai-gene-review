# PREDL:ALB dissociates
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9762343](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9762343)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Binding of prednisolone (PREDL) to albumin (ALB) is high-capacity but weak affinity. This means the PREDL:ALB complex is on average short-lived and soon dissociates (Pugeat et al, 1981; Boudinot and Jusko, 1984).
