# Equilibrative transport (export) of nucleosides and free bases by solute carrier family 29 (nucleoside transporters), member 1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-109534](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-109534)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The plasma membrane-associated protein SLC29A1 mediates the reversible transport of one molecule of adenosine, cytosine, guanosine, inosine, thymidine, or uridine from the extracellular space to the cytosol.
