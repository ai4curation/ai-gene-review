# GATAD2A NuRD binds ZMYND8:ZNF532:ZNF592:ZNF687
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9940948](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9940948)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

ZMYND8 is a transcriptional regulator that interacts specifically with the GATAD2A paralog of the NuRD complex (Spruijt et al, 2016). The direct interaction is mediated by the MYND protein-protein interaction domain of ZMYND8 and a PPPL(phi) motif that is present in GATAD2A but not in GATAD2B (Spruijt et al, 2016). ZMYND8 additionally interacts with at least three other zinc-finger transcription factors, ZNF532, ZNF592 and ZNF687, which also co-purify at substoichiometric levels with NuRD complexes (Malovannaya et al, 2011; Eberl et al, 2013; Smits et al, 2013; Spruijt et al, 2016; Ghosh et al, 2018). <br>ZMYND8 appears to play a role in recruiting the GATAD2A-containing NuRD complex to sites of DNA double strand breaks (Gong et al, 2015; Spruijt et al, 2016).
