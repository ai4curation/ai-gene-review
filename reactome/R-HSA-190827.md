# Transport of connexins along the secretory pathway
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-190827](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-190827)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Connexins follow the classical secretory transport route from the ER to the plasma membrane: ER -> ERGIC -> Golgi -> TGN (Trans Golgi Network)  -> PM (Plasma Membrane).  All connexins assemble or oligomerize into hexameric connexons. The site of  assembly  varies and depends on Cx isoform, or cell type (see Koval et al., 2006).<br>Oligomerization of connexins has been observed during ER membrane insertion (Cx32), just after exit from the ER,  in the ER-Golgi-intermediate compartment (Cx26) and inside the  Trans-Golgi Network  (Cx43) (Falk et al. 1997; Ahmad et al. 1999; Musil and Goodenough 1993; Diez et al. 1999).
