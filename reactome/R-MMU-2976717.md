# Notch2 binds Hes1 promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-2976717](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-2976717)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

In Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) cells, recombinant mouse Notch2 intracellular domain (NICD2) in complex with recombinant mouse Rbpj (and probably other components of the Notch2 coactivator complex derived from CHO cells, not shown here), binds Rbpj elements in Hes1 promoter (Shimizu et al. 2002).
