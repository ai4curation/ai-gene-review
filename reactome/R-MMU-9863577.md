# Phospho-Pi3k in p-Pecam1:Cdh5:Pik3ca,b,d:p-Y458-Pik3r2:p-Y799,Y1052,Y1172-Kdr:p-Y1063,Y1230-Flt4:Ctnnb1 phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol-4,5-bisphosphate to yield phosphatidylinositol-3,4,5-trisphosphate
**Reactome ID:** [R-MMU-9863577](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-MMU-9863577)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Phosphorylated phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (Pi3k, Pik3ca,b,d:p-Pik3r2) associated with Kdr during laminar shear stress phosphorylates phosphatidylinositol-4,5-bisphosphate to yield phosphatidylinositol-3,4,5-trisphosphate (Dayanir et al. 2001, Tzima et al. 2005).
