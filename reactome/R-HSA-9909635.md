# BRD4 binding to CD274 gene chromatin
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909635](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909635)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

BRD4 (Bromodomain-containing protein 4) regulates CD274 (PD-L1) transcription by binding to acetylated histones at the PD-L1 promoter region, facilitating the recruitment of transcriptional machinery. BRD4 interacts with transcription factors (IRF4 binding to CD274 promoter) and co-activators, enhancing the transcription of PD-L1. It plays a key role in maintaining active chromatin states, thereby promoting PD-L1 gene expression. This regulation is critical in cancer, as BRD4-driven upregulation of PD-L1 allows tumor cells to evade immune detection by inhibiting T cell activity (Jing et al., 2020, Wang et al.,2022, Zhu et al., 2016).
