# Formation of tri-heterotetramers of GRIN1, Grin2a and Grin2c
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9610202](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9610202)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Recombinant rat GluN2C (Grin2c) can bind recombinant human GluN1 (GRIN1) and recombinant rat GluN2A (Grin2a) to form a tri-heteromeric NMDA receptor complex (Wafford et al. 1993).
