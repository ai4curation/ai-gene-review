# Ntrk2 binds Tiam1
**Reactome ID:** [R-NUL-9033304](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-NUL-9033304)
**Species:** Mus musculus

## Summary

Mouse Ntrk2 (TrkB), stimulated by recombinant human BDNF, and phosphorylated by Cdk5 at serine residue S478, binds to mouse Tiam1, a Rac1 guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) (Lai et al. 2012).
