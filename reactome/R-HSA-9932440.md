# ARID2 binds SMARCC dimer:SMARCDs:SMARCE1:SMARCB1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9932440](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9932440)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Recruitment of ARID2 to the forming pBAF complex represent a branching point that distinguishes it from cBAF, which contains ARID1A or ARID1B (Yan et al, 2005; Mashtalir et al, 2018; Mashtalir et al, 2020; Yuan et al, 2022; Wang et al, 2022). ARID2 is a core component of the base module of the pBAF complex and makes contacts with other auxiliary pBAF proteins, including the pBAF-specific PHF10, as well as making non-specific contact with DNA (Yuan et al, 2022; Wang et al, 2022; Pastialou et al, 2005)
