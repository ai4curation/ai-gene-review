# Expression of Carbonic Anhydrase IX (CA9)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-1235035](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-1235035)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The gene encoding carbonic anhydrase IX (CA9) is transcribed to yield mRNA and the mRNA is translated to yield protein. Hypoxia-inducible factor binds the promoter of CA9 and enhances expression of CA9.
