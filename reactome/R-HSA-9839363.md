# TGFBR3 phosphorylation by TGFBR2 complex
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9839363](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9839363)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The binding of b‑arrestin 2 ARBB2 to type III TGF‑β receptor (TGFBR3) is triggered by phosphorylation of TGFBR3 on its cytoplasmic domain (likely at threonine 841). However, such phosphorylation is mediated by the kinase activity of type II TGF‑β receptor (TGFBR2) itself, rather than by a G protein–coupled receptor kinases (GRKs). (Chen et al.,2003)
