# Transcriptional activation of AGPAT1 by ChREBP:MLX
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-163748](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-163748)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The AGPAT1 (1-acyl-sn-glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase alpha) gene, activated by binding of MLXIPL:MLX (ChREBP:MLX) to its promoter region, is transcribed and the resulting mRNA is translated to yield ACACB protein (Ma et al. 2005, 2006, 2007).
