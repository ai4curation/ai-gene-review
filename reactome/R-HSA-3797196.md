# AP-1 transcription factor binds IGFBP7 promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3797196](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3797196)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

FOS:JUN (AP-1) transcription factor, formed in response to oncogenic RAF-MAPK signaling which also triggers oxidative stress, binds the promoter of IGFBP7 gene (Wajapeyee et al. 2008).
