# G6PC2 hydrolyzes G6P to form Glc and Pi (islet)
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3266566](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3266566)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

G6PC2 (Glucose-6-phosphatase 2), associated with the endoplasmic reticulum membrane catalyzes the hydrolysis of glucose-6-phosphate (G6P) to glucose (Glc) and orthophosphate (Pi). This reaction is essentially irreversible. This enzyme is expressed in islet cells of the pancreas (Petrolonis et al. 2004).
