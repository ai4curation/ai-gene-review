# Phosphorylation of CI by CKIalphaepsilon (CKIalpha/DCO)
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209293](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209293)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The priming phosphorylations of Cubitus Interruptus (CI) results in additional phosphorylation by kinases CKIalphaepsilon (CKIalpha/DCO) at Ser841, Ser844, Thr845, Ser849, Ser852, Ser859, Ser862, and Ser895.
