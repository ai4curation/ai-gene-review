# tRNA(Sec) is serylated to Ser-tRNA(Sec) by SARS dimer
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-2408526](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-2408526)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The synthetic cycle of selenocysteine (Sec) synthesis starts with misacylation (serylation) of the cognate transfer RNA (tRNA) for Sec, tRNASec, with serine (Ser) by Serine--tRNA ligase (SARS) homodimer.  The structural homology of tRNASec and tRNASer enables SARS to form Ser-tRNASec (Vincent et al. 1997, Amberg et al. 1996, Heckl et al. 1998).
