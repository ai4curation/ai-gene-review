# MLL2 complex trimethylates H3K4 at the MYC gene
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-3364026](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-3364026)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The recruitment of SET1-type complex MLL2, in association with MEN1, at the MYC enhancer correlates with increased H3K4 trimethylation in vivo, a mark associated with active chromatin (Sierra et al, 2006).  Studies in yeast have shown that H3K4 trimethylation depends on previous H2B ubiquitination by Rad6 and Bre1, which in turn are recruited to DNA by the Paf1 complex (reviewed in Shilatifard, 2006).  In this context, the identification of Paf1 components as interactors with the C-terminal activation domain of beta-catenin is intriguing (Sierra et al, 2006)
