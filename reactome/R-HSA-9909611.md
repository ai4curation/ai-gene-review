# PRC2(EZH2) core binding to CD274 gene promoter
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9909611](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9909611)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

EZH2 (Enhancer of Zeste Homolog 2) regulates CD274 (PD-L1) transcription through its role as a histone methyltransferase in the Polycomb Repressive Complex 2 (PRC2). EZH2 catalyzes the trimethylation of histone H3 at lysine 27 (H3K27me3), leading to chromatin compaction and gene silencing. In cancer, EZH2 can repress transcription of genes that negatively regulate PD-L1 expression, thereby indirectly increasing PD-L1 levels. Additionally, EZH2 can sometimes directly bind to the PD-L1 promoter and influence its transcriptional activity. (Xiao et al, 2019, Morel et al., 2021)
