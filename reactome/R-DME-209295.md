# Phosphorylated DL/DIF dimer transports from the cytosol to the nucleus
**Reactome ID:** [R-DME-209295](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-DME-209295)
**Species:** Drosophila melanogaster

## Summary

The phosphorylated Dorsal/Dif (DL/DIF) homodimer, now free from its cytoplasmic anchor, Cactus (CACT), is imported to the nucleus.  Phosphorylation of the DL/DIF homodimer is important for its nuclear localisation.
