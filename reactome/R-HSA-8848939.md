# PTK6 binds KHDRBS1
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-8848939](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-8848939)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

PTK6 (BRK) associates with the RNA binding protein KHDRBS1 (SAM68) in the nucleus, where PTK6 and KHDRBS1 colocalize in the SAM68-SLM nuclear bodies (SNBs) (Derry et al. 2000).
