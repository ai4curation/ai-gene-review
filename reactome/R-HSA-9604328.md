# AKT1 phosphorylates NOTCH4
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9604328](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9604328)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

Recombinant human AKT1 phosphorylates recombinant human NICD4 on four AKT-target sites conserved in primates: S1495, S1847, S1865 and S1917 (Ramakrishnan et al. 2015).
