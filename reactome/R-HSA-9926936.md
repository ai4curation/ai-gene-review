# EspC(54-1018) cleaves hemoglobin
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-9926936](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-9926936)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

E.coli serine protease EspC(54-1018) cleaves hemoglobin (HbA) subunits at unknown positions, releasing heme to extracellular space. The reaction may contribute to the utilization of heme iron by the bacterium (Stein et al., 1996; Drago-Serrano et al., 2006; reviewed in Clarke et al., 2022).
