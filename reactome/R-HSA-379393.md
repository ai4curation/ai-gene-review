# SLC6A3 cotransports DA, Na+, Cl- from extracellular region to cytosol
**Reactome ID:** [R-HSA-379393](https://reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-379393)
**Species:** Homo sapiens

## Summary

The human gene SLC6A3 encodes the sodium-dependent dopamine transporter, DAT which mediates the re-uptake of dopamine from the synaptic cleft (Gu et al., 1994; Vandenbergh DJ et al, 2000). Dopamine can then be degraded by either COMT or monoamine oxidase.
