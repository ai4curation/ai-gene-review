    GO-CAM structural lint

    ai-gene-review gocam-lint (or just gocam-lint) reads the cached GO-CAM models under gocams/<id>/<id>-src.yaml and reports structural anomalies: graph shapes that are impossible, redundant or ambiguous. It does not judge whether the biology is true. Use it to start a GoCamReview from a short list of suspicious places, then check each finding against the literature.

    just gocam-lint 59cc772c00000000                 # one model
just gocam-lint --min-severity WARNING            # all cached models, to stdout
just gocam-lint -o reports/gocam-lint.tsv         # all models, as TSV
just gocam-lint --list-rules
just gocam-lint --no-ontology 59cc772c00000000    # graph rules only, no GO download

    Ontology rules use the oaklib GO SQLite build (sqlite:obo:go, about 300 MB compressed, downloaded on first use). The code is in src/ai_gene_review/gocam_lint.py; the tests are in tests/test_gocam_lint.py.

    Each finding has a rule, a severity (ERROR, WARNING, INFO) and, where one fits, the GoCamQcFlagEnum value a reviewer would record.

    Rules

    Rule Needs GO What it finds QC flag
    compartment_crossing yes A direct causal edge between activities on opposite sides of a membrane, with no transport or secretion step (see below) DIRECT_VS_INDIRECT_CAUSAL
    transcription_direct_edge yes A DNA-binding transcription factor directly regulates a non-transcriptional activity. WARNING when the model names the target as the TF's input gene, INFO otherwise DIRECT_VS_INDIRECT_CAUSAL
    receptor_ligand_input yes A receptor activity has input the gene product of an activity upstream of it (the ligand belongs on the causal edge) HAS_INPUT_MISUSE
    generic_mf yes MF is a grouping term (kinase activity, catalytic activity, binding, ...) GENERIC_MF
    binding_mf yes MF is a binding term (protein binding WARNING, other binding INFO) BINDING_AS_FUNCTION
    same_gene_chain no Activities of one gene product chained by causal edges. WARNING when the MF repeats in one location, INFO when the MFs differ (activation states, distinct steps)
    duplicate_activity no Same gene product and MF in more than one node. WARNING when the context is identical
    opposite_sign_fanout no One activity activates one node of a gene and inhibits another node of the same gene
    contradictory_edges no The same pair of activities joined by both a positive and a negative edge
    self_loop no An activity regulates itself
    duplicate_edge no The same edge asserted several times (usually merged evidence; INFO)
    edge_evidence_copied no A causal edge cites exactly the references of the upstream enabled_by
    orphan_activity no An activity with no causal or metabolite-flow link, in a multi-activity model ORPHAN_ACTIVITY
    disconnected_components no Several components. WARNING when two share at least half their genes (a switch or choice, or cloned variant paths)
    missing_evidence no A causal edge with no evidence MISSING_EVIDENCE
    missing_location no An activity with no occurs_in MISSING_LOCATION
    small_model no Two or fewer activities
    no_narrative no No curator comment beyond automated changes
    near_duplicate_models no Corpus rule: two models whose (gene, MF) activity sets overlap with Jaccard ≥ 0.6

    Unknown --rule or --min-severity values are rejected with the list of valid choices. near_duplicate_models is a corpus rule: it runs only when several models are linted together.

    Connectivity rules count metabolite flow as well as causal edges: in pathway models, A has output X and B has input X links A to B. Gene-centric models, where every activity has the same enabler (MGI/SGD imports), are exempt from the orphan and component rules.

    Compartment topology

    A direct edge (directly positively/negatively regulates, directly provides input for, removes input for, constitutively upstream of) asserts a single step. Each occurs_in term is mapped to the topological spaces it touches:

    Code Space
    C cytosolic space (cytosol and nucleus, joined through nuclear pores)
    E extracellular
    P periplasm
    L lumen of the secretory and endocytic system (topologically outside)
    M mitochondrial matrix
    I mitochondrial intermembrane space
    X peroxisomal matrix
    S, T plastid stroma, thylakoid lumen

    A membrane touches both of its sides (plasma membrane: C, E, P). An organelle root term touches its outside and its insides (ER: C, L). cytoplasm touches every intracellular space. A term inherits the faces of its most specific anchor through is_a/part_of. Leaflet terms resolve to one side, so cytoplasmic side of plasma membrane is C. Generic intracellular anchors are ignored when a more specific anchor matches. Host-cell terms and terms with no anchor (membrane, cell, complexes) are skipped. The anchor table and its partial order live in the code, and an integration test checks them against the current GO build.

    Two activities whose face sets intersect can be linked directly. Otherwise the least severe pairing of faces decides the severity:

    • ERROR: interior (C, M, I, X, S, T) against exterior (E, P). Example: CASP1 in the cytosol "directly positively regulates" IL1B cytokine activity in the extracellular region, which skips GSDMD-pore secretion.
    • WARNING: two separated interior spaces, e.g. cytosol and mitochondrial matrix with no import step. Metabolite flow between cytosol and the intermembrane space is INFO, because porins make the outer membrane permeable to small molecules.
    • INFO: lumen and exterior (Golgi processing followed by secretion), which is topologically continuous.

    Corpus results (November 2026)

    The run over 2,037 cached models gave 10,021 findings in 1,924 models; 757 models have at least one ERROR or WARNING. The committed report, reports/gocam-lint.tsv, keeps only the 3,138 ERROR and WARNING findings (just gocam-lint --min-severity WARNING -o reports/gocam-lint.tsv) so that it does not churn on every cache refresh; regenerate the INFO rows locally.

    All six ERRORs are plausible missing steps: CASP1 to extracellular IL1B (59cc772c00000000), TGFB1 directly regulating nuclear USP44 (6348a65d00002174), two transcriptional repressors directly regulating secreted proteins (568b0f9600000284, 632cf60a00000169), and MPO in the nucleus directly activating proteases in neutrophil extracellular traps (68fac5ed00000028).

    The rules were checked against manual reviews of problematic models (see the GoCamReview files for 671ae02600002832, 66b5638000001589, 635b1e3e00001811, 65a1f4f800002884 and the 62b4ffe3000044xx/54xx calmodulin set). opposite_sign_fanout catches the duplicated eIF2alpha node of the integrated stress response model. same_gene_chain catches the NLRP3 chain in the inflammasome model. disconnected_components and duplicate_activity catch the parallel NBN repair-choice chains, and near_duplicate_models catches the four calmodulin models split from one paper.

    Known limits

    • Molecules carry no location in gocam-py, so a crossing reached through a has input molecule is not checked.
    • cytoplasm is ambiguous, so it never produces a crossing with lumenal spaces.
    • Some proposed rules need text or ontology knowledge not yet wired in: checking an edge's sign against its cited paper, checking reaction participants against Rhea, checking an MF against the gene's known compartments, and checking that the model's title is reached by its graph.
