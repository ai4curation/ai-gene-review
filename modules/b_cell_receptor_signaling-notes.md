# B-cell receptor signaling audit notes

## 2026-10-26 — boundary and comparative evidence

The intended boundary is antigen-triggered proximal BCR signaling through lipid second-messenger production. Engaged membrane immunoglobulin is an upstream input. CD79A/B transmit the signal and contribute receptor assembly; they do not supply antigen specificity. Human IgM and IgG structures establish the distinction and receptor stoichiometry [PMID:35981043; PMID:35981028]. No arbitrary unrearranged immunoglobulin accession will stand in for every antigen receptor. The 2025 triggering review explicitly finds no unifying mechanism across receptor/ligand diversity [PMID:39256626, abstract only]. Therefore the module will not assert a universal antigen-induced conformational step.

The original seven human products remain in the all-row gene review panel, including PIK3CG and PLCG2. PIK3CG is not an adequate default for the class IA BCR PI3K activity: the p85-associated delta mechanism, GPCR-associated gamma mechanism, and tonic alpha/delta compensation differ [PMID:23551098; PMID:20699475]. Native mouse genetics plus acute delta inhibition support PI3K-dependent BTK/PLCG2 activation and calcium responses [PMID:12235209; PMID:16179367]. These findings correct the original linear ordering that placed PI3K after a completed SYK–BTK cascade. Gamma/delta double-mutant developmental and cell-extrinsic effects remain real biology requiring individual PIK3CG review [PMID:20200404]; they do not establish gamma as the same receptor-coupled catalytic component.

BLNK phosphotyrosines organize multiple signaling proteins in cis [PMID:12456653]. The source-defined steps will distinguish kinase catalysis, scaffold recruitment, PI3K lipid production and PLCG2 lipid hydrolysis. CD19 and BCAP have complementary recruitment roles in the mouse study [PMID:18025150]; neither a flat AND of both nor unqualified interchangeability in every stimulus is established. LYN is also bidirectional and stimulus dependent. DT40 LYN loss and SYK loss have different IP3/calcium effects [PMID:8137818], so LYN will not be the uniquely indispensable initiating kinase in every route. BTK/SYK concerted support of PLCG2 is established by DT40 perturbations, which must not be relabeled as purified direct phosphorylation [PMID:8691147].

Comparative evidence includes human target experiments, native mouse genetics and chicken DT40 mechanism studies [PMID:17623905; PMID:24167721]. Assay host and rescue-construct species will be recorded separately. Shark CD79-alpha expression and sequence support a wider evolutionary context, but the primary explicitly leaves its physical association with immunoglobulin for future work [PMID:23454429]. A Gnathostomata label alone therefore cannot certify each enzyme choice or edge throughout jawed vertebrates. The panel has 12 human, seven mouse and three chicken proteins; no full avian or fish implementation is inferred from a partial panel.

The 2023 CD79 review and 2025 signaling/cytoskeleton reviews provide additional source leads and boundary checks [PMID:38203179; PMID:39917832; PMID:40243025]. Differentiation, antigen endocytosis, integrin adhesion, calcium release, PKC/NF-kappaB, MAPK and Akt effector chemistry will be interfaces unless their additional participants and steps are explicitly reviewed. The underlying proximal mechanism remains a network of interacting roles, not a complete cell-fate model.

`b_cell_receptor_signaling-source-analysis/evidence-findings.yaml.txt` records 18 manually assessed source findings with checked titles, verbatim supporting passages, species/access limits and candidate roles. Four recent reviews were normally fetched after the initial acquisition queue; three have full text and PMID:39256626 is abstract-only. The initial queue cached all 749 distinct PMIDs (220 full text, 529 abstract/metadata); this availability does not imply that every paper has been read. Eighteen new gene provider attempts failed with actual authentication/quota errors; four existing substantive reports were reused. No provider report was fabricated.

All 22 gene seeds preserve their released baseline of 2,645 rows, including 1,715 preexisting rows. Fifteen added normal-fetch rows and machine-field augmentations are separately proven. Full individual review is now assigned across three balanced panels. The existing module YAML remains DRAFT while source findings and member core functions are integrated; this note is not a claim that the final graph, sources, family placements or full validation are complete.


## 2026-10-26 — proximal mechanism and comparative integration

The first integrated draft has six required human steps, 20 family-grounded activity records, 12 evidence-backed connections and 25 manually assessed references with findings. Antigen-engaged membrane immunoglobulin is an upstream context; the CD79 assembly provides signaling tails, not autonomous antigen recognition. CD79B's contribution is represented within the complex without assigning the isolated subunit a receptor molecular function. The human p85-alpha/p110-delta assembly produces PIP3 upstream of BTK recruitment; p85 regulates and p110 performs lipid catalysis. [PMID:15135396, "the three human Class Ia PI3K (alpha, beta, and
delta) p110 catalytic domains were cloned and co-expressed with the p85alpha
regulatory domain in Sf9 cells."]

SYK-to-BLNK scaffolding and BTK-to-PLCG2 phosphorylation are separate edges. Human PLCG2 rescue in DT40 does not make the construct chicken-derived. [PMID:11606584, "Btk had much greater ability to phosphorylate recombinant
PLCgamma2 in vitro, whereas Syk efficiently phosphorylated adapter protein BLNK."] Human BCAP supports p85 recruitment; the BTK-to-BCAP connection records biochemical capacity for reinforcement, without claiming a quantified native feedback cycle. [PMID:31527084, "the p85 SH2 domain binds to the three C-terminal YXXM in BCAP"]

CD19, BCAP and LYN are contextual modifiers, not three universal independent requirements. LYN's inhibitory CD22/SHP1 role is retained in the evidence and notes rather than encoded as a direct inhibitory edge from LYN to PLCG2. The gamma PI3K isoform remains fully reviewed as a boundary comparator; it cannot substitute for the concrete class IA assembly. Mouse and chicken panels provide native genetics and construct-specific comparisons but are explicitly incomplete alternative circuits. The root taxon list enumerates the sampled species; no complete jawed-vertebrate implementation is inferred from shark CD79 sequence/expression.

The GO-CAM index has 29 activities involving panel members, mainly in other receptor contexts. The cached CD21/FCRL5 model (gomodel:66187e4700002637) has matching human BTK and PLCG2 activities, now linked narrowly. The CD180/CD19 model (gomodel:67f85f2b00001619) concerns LPS-associated signaling and is not imported as antigen-triggered BCR topology. Its CD19 coreceptor term remains a separate gene-review adjudication, not an automatic module override.

Initial checks: schema and production module validation pass with zero errors. Five initial advisories reflect two unlabelled CD79B contribution descriptors (reported at gene and family levels) and the still-in-progress chicken SYK function review. Twenty independent member-choice cases reject empty contexts, missing CD79/p85 units, gamma-for-delta substitution and mixed-species replacement. Member panels and final cross-file reconciliation remain in progress; this draft is not audit completion.
