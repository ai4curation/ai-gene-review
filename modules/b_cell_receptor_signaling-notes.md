# B-cell receptor signaling audit notes

## 2026-10-26 — boundary and comparative evidence

The intended boundary is antigen-triggered proximal BCR signaling through lipid second-messenger production. Engaged membrane immunoglobulin is an upstream input. CD79A/B transmit the signal and contribute receptor assembly; they do not supply antigen specificity. Human IgM and IgG structures establish the distinction and receptor stoichiometry [PMID:35981043; PMID:35981028]. No arbitrary unrearranged immunoglobulin accession will stand in for every antigen receptor. The 2025 triggering review explicitly finds no unifying mechanism across receptor/ligand diversity [PMID:39256626, abstract only]. Therefore the module will not assert a universal antigen-induced conformational step.

The original seven human products remain in the all-row gene review panel, including PIK3CG and PLCG2. PIK3CG is not an adequate default for the class IA BCR PI3K activity: the p85-associated delta mechanism, GPCR-associated gamma mechanism, and tonic alpha/delta compensation differ [PMID:23551098; PMID:20699475]. Native mouse genetics plus acute delta inhibition support PI3K-dependent BTK/PLCG2 activation and calcium responses [PMID:12235209; PMID:16179367]. These findings correct the original linear ordering that placed PI3K after a completed SYK–BTK cascade. Gamma/delta double-mutant developmental and cell-extrinsic effects remain real biology requiring individual PIK3CG review [PMID:20200404]; they do not establish gamma as the same receptor-coupled catalytic component.

BLNK phosphotyrosines organize multiple signaling proteins in cis [PMID:12456653]. The source-defined steps will distinguish kinase catalysis, scaffold recruitment, PI3K lipid production and PLCG2 lipid hydrolysis. CD19 and BCAP have complementary recruitment roles in the mouse study [PMID:18025150]; neither a flat AND of both nor unqualified interchangeability in every stimulus is established. LYN is also bidirectional and stimulus dependent. DT40 LYN loss and SYK loss have different IP3/calcium effects [PMID:8137818], so LYN will not be the uniquely indispensable initiating kinase in every route. BTK/SYK concerted support of PLCG2 is established by DT40 perturbations, which must not be relabeled as purified direct phosphorylation [PMID:8691147].

Comparative evidence includes human target experiments, native mouse genetics and chicken DT40 mechanism studies [PMID:17623905; PMID:24167721]. Assay host and rescue-construct species will be recorded separately. Shark CD79-alpha expression and sequence support a wider evolutionary context, but the primary explicitly leaves its physical association with immunoglobulin for future work [PMID:23454429]. A Gnathostomata label alone therefore cannot certify each enzyme choice or edge throughout jawed vertebrates. The panel has 12 human, seven mouse and three chicken proteins; no full avian or fish implementation is inferred from a partial panel.

The 2023 CD79 review and 2025 signaling/cytoskeleton reviews provide additional source leads and boundary checks [PMID:38203179; PMID:39917832; PMID:40243025]. Differentiation, antigen endocytosis, integrin adhesion, calcium release, PKC/NF-kappaB, MAPK and Akt effector chemistry will be interfaces unless their additional participants and steps are explicitly reviewed. The underlying proximal mechanism remains a network of interacting roles, not a complete cell-fate model.
