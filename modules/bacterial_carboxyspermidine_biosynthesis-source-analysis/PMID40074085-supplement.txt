Figure S1. N1-aminopropylhomospermidine and norspermine production by CASDH/CASDC in E.
coli BL21speD. Spermidine-void E.coli BL21speD strains coexpressing CASDH/CASDC were grown
with either 500 µM homospermidine or 500 µM norspermidine. Polyamines from cell extracts were
benzoylated and analyzed by LC-MS. Shown are the Extracted Ion Chromatograms (EICs) for
tetrabenzoylated N1-aminopropylhomospermidine (mass tolerance window 633.04:634.04),
tetrabenzoylated norspermine (605:606) and tetrabenzoylated spermine/thermospermine (619.02:620.02).
CASDH and CASDC from the indicated species were coexpressed from pETDuet-1 and pACYCDuet-1,
respectively, in spermidine-void E.coli BL21speD. The y-axis represents arbitrary units of ion intensity,
and all samples were grown, extracted and analyzed together.
