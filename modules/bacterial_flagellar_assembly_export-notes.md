# Flagellar assembly and export audit notes

## 2026-11-06 — comparative evidence and boundary checks

The starting module has 27 activity/structural roles and 28 exemplar appearances: 27 PSEPK proteins and ECOLI FliI. Nineteen review files exist, with 129 original annotation rows; nine PSEPK exemplars still require normal seeding and complete reviews. The initial baseline preserves all seeded annotation objects and 57 raw/provider source hashes.

The 2024 review (PMID:39766194, full text cached) separates structural assembly from motor energization. It describes the MS ring as an assembly template, the FliP/FliQ/FliR conduit, and the ATPase complex as an activator of the ion-driven export gate. Its cross-species comparisons also distinguish the sodium-dependent Vibrio stator from proton-driven systems. These observations require per-step taxon and ion context; family membership alone will not establish the assembled machine or its energy source.

The native PSEPK stator study (PMID:36409076, full text cached) uses KT2440 with FliC S267C, which the authors call wild type after citing preserved motility. MotAB and MotCD phenotypes depend on viscosity and assay environment. The existing MotAB-only step must be checked against both loci and the measured conditions. Do not equate agar spreading with flagellum-dependent swarming or infer generic stator interchangeability.

PMID:18216858 and PMID:18216859 describe Salmonella export without the usual FliI ATPase requirement in defined experimental contexts. The module must distinguish physiological efficiency from absolute biochemical necessity and avoid labeling FliI an ATP synthase from its legacy protein name.

PMID:37260402, PMID:25613993, PMID:24064315 and PMID:41263576 are currently abstract-only in the cache. Their existence and broad relevance are verified, but detailed mechanism assertions require accessible primary text. PMID:41263576 has restricted PMC full-text access at the time of this audit. No cached publication is rewritten to fill that gap.

Individual reviews and family grounding are in progress. These notes are a research plan, not a completed module assessment.

The comparative panel adds PSEPK MotC/MotD, six Salmonella proteins (FliI, FliJ, FlhA, FliK, FlgE and FlgM), and Vibrio alginolyticus PomA/PomB. Exact protein and construct identity is being documented separately. PMID:29946050 provides an important energy-coupling qualification: ATP-driven export in an inverted-membrane-vesicle assay without bulk PMF must be represented alongside intact-cell evidence. Neither universal ATP dependence nor universal PMF dependence is inferred from a single assay.
