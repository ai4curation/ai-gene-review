# Doi2019 correction

PMID31147755; DOI10.1007/s00253-019-09930-9. Publisher landing page verifies identity, but did not expose the correction text. The published correction text was read in the indexed publisher preview at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333518126_Correction_to_Glycerol_metabolism_and_its_regulation_in_lactic_acid_bacteria on2026-11-07UTC.

The correction swaps the erroneous GlpD/GlpA assignments: Lactococcus lactis subsp. cremoris carries GlpA, and Lactobacillus alimentarius carries GlpD in the reviewed analysis. It corrects the former operon to glpKAF and the strain to SK11, and repairs a mistaken Lactococcus/Lactobacillus genus name. These are published corrections, not new experimental demonstrations of acceptor specificity. The audit does not infer a whole lactic-acid-bacterial route from the uncorrected review.
