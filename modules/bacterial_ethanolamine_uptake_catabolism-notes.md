# Comparative ethanolamine catabolism

The root describes EutBC-dependent deamination coupled to an oxidative acetaldehyde fate. Separate evaluated endpoints distinguish nitrogen liberation, acetyl-CoA formation, acetate formation, reductive ethanol disposal, acetyl-phosphate-dependent ATP production, a defined private-cofactor recycling scheme, repair after cofactor inactivation, and sustained native carbon utilization. A positive chemical capacity result is not a native-growth prediction.

EutBC is one assembled enzyme. Five exact pairs represent E. coli K-12, Salmonella LT2, P. putida KT2440, P. aeruginosa PAO1 and Listeria monocytogenes10403S. Source qualifiers stay intact and source-specific concerns are qualified.

Protein uptake is conditional. LT2 EutH supports access at low uncharged ethanolamine; the assay-specific growth threshold is not a Km. The EutE and non-acylating ALDH branches retain separate chemistry and can coexist with EutG reduction. A defined two-deamination/one-oxidation/one-reduction scheme cancels tracked NAD and CoA carriers and supplies one acetate, one ethanol and one ATP through EutD and acetate kinase. This is a reaction scheme, not measured cellular flux or a universal fermentation ratio. Listeria EGDe product measurements and10403S EutB/Ndh2 genetics imply additional redox context; the proposed shell electron-transfer conduit remains unproven. EutQ kinase and LT2 N-terminal cargo scaffolding are distinct activities. The scaffold annoton does not establish a complete shell, and shorter Gram-positive EutQ architecture does not inherit the LT2 mechanism automatically.

EutA repair requires inactivated cognate enzyme, ATP/Mg2+ and replacement adenosylcobalamin; ethanolamine access and an already active bound cofactor are not prerequisites for repair. EutA is not required after each catalytic turnover or in every tested nitrogen-growth context. Acinetobacter provides a mechanistic comparator for these distinctions and for a broader adenosylcobamide range; no unverified accession selector is assigned to that comparison.
