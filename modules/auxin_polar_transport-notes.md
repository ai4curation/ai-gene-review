# Polar auxin transport module audit

## Boundary and meaning of satisfaction (2026-10-26)

This module represents **multiactivity mechanisms contributing to polar auxin transport**, not a sufficient molecular recipe for tissue polarity or a complete transport network. Each satisfying alternative has two substantive activities and native-context evidence. Protein presence does not establish tissue coexpression, membrane-face polarity, a driving gradient or a measured native flux. Those biological contexts remain explicit preconditions of each route. The GO process concept is therefore a process to which these mechanisms contribute, not a claim that any isolated pair recreates it.

There are six supported Arabidopsis combinations: AUX1/PIN2 in the root outer-tissue stream; sequential PIN3/LAX3 during lateral-root emergence; PIN1/ABCB19 cooperation in overlapping membrane domains; D6PK/PIN3 activation in the hypocotyl transport context; PID/PIN1 phosphorylation and polarity control; and TWD1/ABCB1 regulation in a native developmental setting. The Boolean alternatives prevent a universal requirement for AUX1, ABCB19, PID and D6PK together. Passive IAAH entry and other carriers are acknowledged without inventing a protein activity for diffusion.

Rice OsAUX1 and PIN2 establish complementary native capacities, but the separate studies do not demonstrate a coupled pair in the same native cells. They are an optional sampled segment and cannot satisfy the parent module. Moss PINA/PINB and liverwort MpPIN1 are optional exporter comparators, not evidence for conservation of the complete Arabidopsis circuit. Their combination also cannot satisfy it. Optional ABCB4 and NPF6.3 contextual functions likewise cannot satisfy the root alone.

The Embryophyta taxon is the envelope of the curated native comparisons, not an ancestral-origin assertion or a universal conservation claim. Cellular export predates this sampled tissue-network boundary. Exact gene selectors keep claims restricted to the 17 reviewed products. Official PANTHER classification descriptors document membership only; the labels were read from `interpro/panther/panther.obo`, and membership from the normal target UniProt records. In particular, PTHR31752:SF18 includes proteins with differing loop/localization biology and does not define a universally polar PM exporter; PTHR48017 and PTHR11654 are broader than auxin influx, and PTHR45637 is broader than these PIN-regulatory kinases. The mitochondrial wording in the ABCB broad-family name does not localize Arabidopsis ABCBs.

Nuclear TIR1/AFB–Aux/IAA–ARF signaling, biosynthesis, short-PIN/PILS intracellular compartmentation, general trafficking machinery and bulk phloem transport remain outside this boundary. Transcriptional feedback can induce transporters but is not modeled as an obligatory transport-cycle step. No corpus `module_ref` targets a prior internal node of this module; the original root identifier remains unchanged.

## Primary evidence for each connection

| Mechanism | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|
| AUX1 → PIN2 input continuity | PMID:16244669 uses tissue-specific AUX1 expression and modeling to identify the root-cap/epidermal gravitropic signal path. Direct AUX1 uptake (16677815) and PIN2 efflux (9844024) independently identify the molecular work. This is shared IAA continuity, not a physical complex. PMID:20562236 confirms opposite cortical versus epidermal PIN2 polarity; the module uses PM rather than an unqualified apical location. |
| PIN3 → LAX3 emergence route | Full PMID:24150423 combines a spatial model and experiments to establish consecutive induction and restricted LAX3 expression over lateral-root primordia. No direct binding edge is asserted. This cortical emergence context is distinct from PIN3-dependent endodermal reflux during initiation. Full-length LAX3 uptake is established by 40759769; its structurally resolved 44–470 construct is inactive, and fixed proton stoichiometry is not measured. |
| ABCB19 → PIN1 support | PMID:17237354 combines native localization and heterologous transport;18774968 specifically supports membrane-domain stabilization where expression overlaps. This does not make all PINs dependent on all ABCBs or prove a fixed obligatory complex. PIN1 direct transport comes from35917925. |
| D6PK → PIN3 activation | Full24948515 directly measures kinase-dependent PIN1/PIN3 activation in oocytes. Full23709629 connects D6PK-family mutants to reduced native PIN3 phosphorylation and hypocotyl basipetal transport. D6PK paralog redundancy is retained; an isolated D6PK knockout is not claimed to abolish all transport. Polarity changes can still occur without D6PK activity. |
| PID → PIN1 activity/polarity | Direct24948515 activity and native15514156 polarity evidence establish a mechanistic combination. The signed edge means positive carrier-activity regulation, not a uniformly positive effect on every directional tissue stream. PID/WAG can be apolar (33705718); PID/D6PK are not interchangeable, and PIN2 lacks the same S4/S5 motifs assayed in PIN1/PIN3. |
| TWD1 → ABCB1 activation | Full32699109 directly supports ABCB1 activation and a tested D/E-P motif. Full publisher text of35512423 supplies native stamen localization, abundance, rescue and DR5 context; its directional-flow model is inferred, not an independent native isotope-flux assay or new heterologous activation experiment. TWD1's PM activity is distinguished from ER chaperone/trafficking effects. |

A separate TWD1→ABCB19 satisfying route was deliberately not retained. The source owner inspected the Bouchard2006 primary: its positive stimulation experiment is PGP1/ABCB1, while PGP19 could not be tested in the yeast or HeLa settings used. A later introduction's broader wording is not direct pair-specific activation evidence. ABCB19 native trafficking/stability dependence can be biologically real without being the same demonstrated activation mechanism. The2026 CaM-dependent FKBD 1–180 PPIase preprint does not establish intact native TWD1 catalysis or direct ABCB1 bond isomerization. The module asserts transporter activation, not that unsettled catalytic mechanism.

## Comparative primary findings and current literature

- PMID:29294052 identifies native rice PIN2/lra1 and polar PM staining with mutant specificity; native and cross-species complementation support its exporter assignment. PMID:29650967 identifies OsAUX1 specifically as Os01g63770, not Os05g37470. Correction29717128 only adds a funding acknowledgement. These are individually strong genes but not a joint native pairing experiment.
- Full37528584 separates native-promoter PINA tip polarity from a more even PINB PM distribution with intracellular signal. Its H3-IAA export assay tests PINA and MpPINZ in moss, not PINB. PINB's function is supported by native PINA/PINB redundancy and family-level transport evidence from25448003/25448004; collective PpPINA–C figure labels are not treated as a verified PINB-specific construct assay.
- Full41984944 provides an essential stage distinction: early growing moss phyllids have apolar PINA/PINB and PIN-dependent intracellular auxin depletion. Older differentiated tips can show (bi)polar localization. This does not erase prior filament-tip observations, but rules out a universal polar phyllid stream inferred from PIN presence. PMID:41061699 supports a physics-based symplasmic diffusion model of moss branch patterning; its abstract is available, whereas a repository PDF extraction failed, so detailed model parameters are not asserted.
- Full42130089 distinguishes local PIN-associated gradients and symplastic moss branching models from long-range vascular-plant transport. Its comparative interpretation is kept distinct from the more specific direct41984944 phyllid result.
- Native Marchantia CRISPR/complementation and localization in36880411 establish MpPIN1 contributions to dorsiventral development and gametangiophore tropisms, with other tropisms retained. The MpPINZ-GFP export assay in37528584 used a moss host. The reviewed genomic protein and clone are exactly sequence-identical; the gene folder contains the reproducible comparison. Tissue-specific native polarity does not imply all Marchantia cells have a canalized stream.
- Full J-STAGE review DOI10.5685/plmorphol.36.5 explicitly separates cell-to-environment, non-canalized intercellular and canalized transport. Its homology table distinguishes functional tests from sequence presence. The module follows that distinction rather than treating every homolog or phosphosite as a conserved circuit.
- Full36786017 calls for functional validation of predicted orthologs; this supports the restricted comparison. Abstract-only31712756 shows an early streptophyte exporter, while42271607 reports limited/absent directional-export behavior in tested non-plant homologs. Neither licenses a universal absence statement about algae.
- Full42618840 is useful for subcellular homeostasis and PIN/ABCB cooperation, but its generalized ABCB4-efflux wording is qualified by21992190: low-concentration uptake and concentration/cell-dependent export. Native shootward movement is not identical to cell-level efflux. NPF6.3 auxin uptake is nitrate-inhibited (20627075), with phosphorylation-context evidence31431511; it is an optional nutrient-sensitive branch.
- ABCB19 and ABCB1 are not auxin-exclusive transporters. Direct brassinosteroid evidence38513023/39497419 and the reviewed cellular IAA assays are reconciled by assay context. The module does not turn an IAA-negative isolated-proteoliposome assay into a universal biological negative.
- Reviews38211951,36608348,39931795 and40100293 are abstract-only after normal attempts; they provide biochemical diversity, model/experiment integration, posttranslational regulation and broad ABCB context, respectively. Detailed structural or mechanistic claims are anchored to the available primaries instead.

## Validation semantics

`auxin_polar_transport-source-analysis/check_module.py` uses the production validator with a bounded membership mapping read from all 17 normal UniProt records. It never builds the large all-family index or substitutes mocked term resolution. Its route cases check empty inventories, each native mechanism, optional-only inventories, mixed-species pseudo-pairs, individual exporters, combined bryophyte comparators and the full sampled panel. Six minimal two-activity mechanisms remain; optional branches create additional supersets, which are not additional minimal mechanisms. The tests establish representation semantics, not experimental sufficiency of protein presence for native tissue flux.

No new gene annotations were made to clear module coverage warnings. Unresolved native ER residence in comparative gene reviews is not contradicted by asserting their supported PM exporter activity. No module-level knowledge gap is used for mere ontology or review-coverage bookkeeping.

## Q05085 canonical-review consolidation — PR #5579 follow-up

The NPF6.3 and CHL1 gene reviews described the same Q05085/AT1G12110 protein. They are consolidated as **NRT1.1**, the NCBI Official Symbol sourced from TAIR, while NPF6.3 and CHL1 remain synonyms. The module preserves the stable `npf63_auxin_influx` activity ID, accession, family and route logic; only its display label and local evidence path now resolve to the canonical review. All 19 current-GOA rows retain their machine payloads, and a durable projection maps the 14 older CHL1 claims, including six formerly absent donor-metadata fields. Direct conditional auxin influx and basipetal carrier participation survive alongside nitrate transport and genetically separable sensing. Legacy source files and alias HTML redirects are retained; no second curated Q05085 YAML remains. The unrelated Q9LUJ8 BioReason artifacts under CHL1 are not moved into the transporter review.
