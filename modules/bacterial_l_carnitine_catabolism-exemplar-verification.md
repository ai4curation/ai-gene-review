# Bacterial L-carnitine catabolism exemplar verification

Checked against the UniProt REST records on 2026-08-13.

## CaiX accession

- [UniProtKB Q9HTH6](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9HTH6.txt) records
  primary accession `Q9HTH6`, ordered locus name `PA5388`, and a 312-aa
  periplasmic ABC-type substrate-binding protein. The entry is unreviewed and
  does not itself assign the gene name CaiX.
- PMID:19919675 identifies PA5388 as CaiX and experimentally establishes its
  requirement for Cbc-mediated L-carnitine uptake. Thus Q9HTH6 grounds the
  PA5388 identity, while the paper grounds the CaiX function.

## Enzyme accessions

- [UniProtKB Q9HTH7](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9HTH7.txt) is the
  reviewed `cdhC` / `PA5387` 3-dehydrocarnitine:acetyl-CoA trimethylamine
  transferase entry (EC 2.3.1.317; RHEA:47044).
- [UniProtKB Q9HTH9](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9HTH9.txt) is the
  reviewed `cdhB` / `PA5385` betainyl-CoA thioesterase entry (EC 3.1.2.33;
  RHEA:45716).

The exact CdhC and CdhB reactions in those reviewed records cite
PMID:24240508; their carnitine-catabolism roles cite PMID:19406895.
