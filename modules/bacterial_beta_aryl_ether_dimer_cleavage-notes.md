# Bacterial beta-aryl ether dimer cleavage audit

## 2026-11-02 — scope and source review

The boundary is intracellular GGE-like beta-O-4 dimer conversion to guaiacol and HPV, with NAD+ and two reduced-glutathione equivalents supplied. It excludes polymer depolymerization, uptake, downstream aromatic assimilation, and NAD+/GSH regeneration. Capacity to process all four GGE stereoisomers requires both alpha configurations and both beta configurations; choosing one arbitrary enzyme at each stage is insufficient. The represented chemistry must preserve beta configuration during oxidation and invert it during glutathione substitution.

The initial module conflates the alphaR enzyme pair with individual beta isomers. Full PMID:19542348 states that LigL and LigN have the same stereospecificity and that LigO has the same stereospecificity as LigD. Native ligO contribution was negligible under the reported conditions despite demonstrated recombinant activity; biochemical capacity and native knockout phenotypes must be separated.

PMID:30541921 establishes the F199 BaeAB heterodimer and direct NaLigE/F1 assays; PMID:25232892 and PMID:25186983 already characterize the F199 etherases. The initial blanket statement that all F199 upstream homologs were only expression-supported is too broad: the dehydrogenase identities remain predictions, while several downstream enzymes have direct assays. PMID:33244017 establishes the complementary native LigG/LigQ roles. The 2018 Nu-class study (PMID:29449375) supplies both native F199 evidence and an isolated E. coli comparator; it does not establish an E. coli dimer catabolic pathway.

Live UniProt taxonomy places SYK-6 (627192) in Sphingobiaceae and F199 (279238) in Sphingomonadaceae, with Sphingomonadales (204457) as their shared order. Older genus/family names in papers are retained as historical names, not silently interpreted as current taxonomy. LigG Q9WXJ9 retains its legacy SPHPI/taxon13689 record; LigN C0SUK1, LigP E1CJ68 and LigQ G2IKM4 require versioned archival sources. The source record and experimentally identified strain are recorded separately.

Live GO definitions were checked before assigning functions: GO:0004364 specifies a halide leaving group, whereas beta-etherases release an aryloxy group; GO:0016846 describes elimination of substituted hydrogen sulfide, whereas the terminal reaction is reductive deglutathionylation. Specific missing activities will be described without invented GO identifiers. GO:0046274 explicitly describes lignin polymers, so dimer cleavage alone must not manufacture a new polymer-catabolism assertion.

The 2026 pathway review PMID:41705825 and the 2025/2024 beta-etherase reviews PMID:40514178 and PMID:39395522 were searched. The latter two currently have accessible abstracts only and can frame scope, but cannot substitute for primary reaction evidence. Sixteen member reviews and independent source-specific assessment are in progress. No full validation or PR approval is claimed at this stage.
