## 2026-11-06 — scope and research foundation

Started from published main ce73640bac2b5ee8101295e31aaaf3da62646b66 in an independent Orca worktree. The original module has22 representatives, all with existing COMPLETE gene reviews,318 total annotation rows including21 inherited proposals, and44 raw source files. This is not a claim that broad PR diff inventories are exhaustive.

Comparative reviews PMID33684668 (full), PMID38985972 (abstract/publisher metadata) and PMID31473052 (abstract) distinguish signal-core conservation from receptor-array, adaptation and reset diversity. PMID38038435 is the peer-reviewed CheA architecture survey; use it instead of its earlier preprint. PMID41495260 provides primary evidence for CheX/CheZ coexistence and distinct roles, with native ATCC27562 experiments separate from the CMCP6 recombinant CheX construct. Curated reference assessments and verbatim findings are now attached to the module.

Pending scientific integration: separate receptor sensing from CheA's current ontology definition; add cytoplasmic-receptor alternatives; model CheZ, CheC/FliY and phosphate-drainage routes without declaring them taxonomically exclusive; distinguish Bacillus McpC deamidation from McpB sensing; preserve sign differences and avoid universal motor-direction claims. H. pylori lacks CheR/CheB and carries a divergent CheZ/ChePep arrangement, so absence of the E. coli adaptation pair cannot mean absence of bacterial chemotaxis. Individual primary sources and source-strain identities are being checked before adding these roles.

Annotation-reviewer consultations cover the seven original E. coli members,15 original KT2440 members and nine Bacillus exemplars. Already identified issues include an obsolete4:2 stator-stoichiometry claim, overly categorical sensor-kinase and self-catalysis arguments, and a Bacillus CheX generalization. These are pending findings, not completed reviews or validated model changes. New reviews use normal seeding; unavailable provider research is documented honestly.
