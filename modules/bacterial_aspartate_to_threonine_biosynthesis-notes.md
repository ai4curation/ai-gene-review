# Bacterial aspartate-to-threonine biosynthesis audit

## 2026-11-01 — scope and source assessment

Audit the complete five-reaction route in Pseudomonas putida KT2440, Escherichia coli K-12 and Corynebacterium glutamicum ATCC 13032. The 20-member panel comprises eight PSEPK proteins (including three disputed paralogs), six ECOLI proteins, five CORGL proteins and PSEAE ThrH as a separate alternative-step comparator. This sampling establishes concrete native examples, not universal pathway presence in Bacteria. The root boundary starts at intracellular L-aspartate and ends at L-threonine; precursor supply, cofactor regeneration, uptake and downstream metabolism are outside it.

Comparative literature includes PMID:11352712 (central enzymes; abstract-only review), PMID:19946135 (full-text evolutionary/architectural review), and PMID:4391829 (primary comparative regulation and cofactor study; abstract-only cache). The latter cautions that homoserine-dehydrogenase pyridine-nucleotide usage differs between organisms; donor preference cannot be inferred from a generic enzyme name. Evolutionary scenarios from the review are not converted to guessed PAINT nodes.

The original module calls PMID:5441401 a direct KT2440 PP_4473 assay. The cached record contains neither an abstract nor full text and does not establish that strain-to-protein link. A title naming P. putida cannot supply an accession-specific assay. PMID:5441405 has the same limitation for historical homoserine dehydrogenase. These are retrieval/identity limits, not evidence that the underlying assays are wrong.

PMID:40236303 provides a newly retrieved full-text 2025 KT2440 engineering study. Its native ThrB/ThrC coexpression results inform pathway capacity, but construct identity and joint-engineering effects must be separated from purified native kinetics. Its statement that E. coli ThrA replaces LysC, Asd and Hom is chemically incorrect: the fusion contains aspartokinase and homoserine-dehydrogenase activities, while the intervening Asd reaction remains necessary. The source claim will be explicitly assessed rather than copied into the route.

C. glutamicum LysC's alpha and beta products arise from alternative initiation in one gene; they are not distinct loci. E. coli ThrA/MetL fusions span two nonadjacent chemical steps, with Asd still required between them. P. aeruginosa ThrH uses phosphoserine as phosphate donor and forms serine; it is not an ATP-driven kinase [PMID:14699121]. The native thrB/thrH double-mutant evidence [PMID:10220164] supports an alternative step, not substitution into the three sampled species.

Annotation reviewers are examining the species panels against source evidence. The 15 pre-existing reviews contain 154 annotation rows, and all 30 fetched GOA/UniProt files were checksum-pinned before editing. The five CORGL records will be seeded normally and preserved independently. This entry records work in progress; validation and PR review are not yet complete.
