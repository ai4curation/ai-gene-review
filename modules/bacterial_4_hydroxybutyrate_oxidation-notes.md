# Bacterial free-acid 4-hydroxybutyrate oxidation

## 2026-10-26 evidence and boundary audit

The reusable boundary remains two successive non-CoA reactions: Gbd oxidizes 4HB to succinate semialdehyde with NAD, then a nonacylating SSADH oxidizes that aldehyde to succinate. Upstream diol oxidation, substrate uptake, GABA supply, downstream TCA metabolism, PHA formation and CoA-dependent alternatives are outside the core. This is a catalytic sequence, not a stable two-protein complex.

The required implementation now uses the two exact published Cupriavidus proteins, Q59104 and Q9RBF6. PMID:7851418 explicitly distinguishes selected HF39-derived SK4040 from parental H16/HF39, which did not grow on 4HB; cloned DNA conferred growth on H16. PMID:10564790 identifies the downstream GabD clone, recorded as SHF3 in AF175740/AAF19796.1. These studies support the species-level selected/expressing implementation, not an assertion that both purified proteins were jointly reconstituted or that every strain constitutively completes the route. Boolean satisfaction represents the availability of both catalytic roles in that stated context; it does not compute expression or growth from genome presence.

Q59104 is identical to the current H16_A1553/Q0KBD6 sequence, but Q9RBF6 is not identical to H16 GabD1/Q0K2K1 (11 differences across 483 residues). The original and directly published accessions are retained. Reproducible source records and comparison code are under `genes/CUPNE/gbd/gbd-source-analysis/`. Exact strain genealogy beyond the accessible primary text remains a source-access qualification rather than an invented evolutionary conclusion.

The 1999 primary full text could not be recovered by normal PMID retrieval, the repository provider chain, or ordinary publisher requests. BRENDA's normally cached EC1.2.1.79 page independently resolves the cloned GabD's cofactor capacity: Q9RBF6/reference 288054 has activity with NADP and NAD, reporting 132.1 and 14.9 micromol/min/mg respectively at pH 11.0 and 30°C. The module uses the cofactor-general GO:0009013 to summarize those supported alternatives, not to imply equal efficiency or a fixed physiological ratio. The separate native NAD-dependent isoenzyme in the abstract is not identified with this clone. Numerical database transcription anomalies elsewhere on the page are not used. No publisher text or quotation was fabricated.

The original Pseudomonas candidates remain reviewed and visible as optional comparisons. Q88JJ9 is PP_2650, distinct from PP_2049/Q88L86. Full PMID:32256468 shows the latter's genetic dependence during 4HB growth but unsuccessful direct extract assays and unresolved alternative routes. New full PMID:42328986 directly assays PP_2049 oxidation of R-3HB to acetoacetate with NAD; that is a different substrate and product from 4HB-to-SSA chemistry. Full PMID:37067433 supports a CoA/beta-oxidation/GCL interpretation in evolved NB10, preventing a universal free-acid-route claim. PP_3151 is named SadI in the 2020 paper, while current locus-resolved nomenclature is sad-II. Neither Sad gene has a demonstrated physiological pairing with PP_2650 or PP_2049. Family-based NAD capacity is retained as inference without asserting NADP exclusion.

Official PANTHER descriptors accompany exact gene-product selectors. SF102 is not substrate-specific enough to define a Gbd family role: it contains the characterized 4HB enzyme, an unverified 4HB candidate and the experimentally characterized 3HB enzyme. Cupriavidus GabD belongs to PTHR43353:SF5, distinct from the PSEPK PTHR43217 Sad families. The word mitochondrial in the official family label is not a bacterial location assignment. Family coverage advisories are retained rather than creating unsupported family-wide functions.

Full review PMID:40970732 explicitly describes three possible downstream 4HB routes. Full review PMID:34885814 supplies reverse/biosynthetic context; reductive SSA-to 4HB and CoA-dependent Clostridium chemistry do not establish an additional oxidative implementation. No reverse-pathway comparator was added solely to inflate taxon coverage. Six reviewed members provide one supported paired route plus qualified comparisons, not multiple fabricated native circuits.

The old module's hybrid completion and family-wide substrate implication are removed. The two legacy reaction-node IDs remain available, although no external module currently references them. Every one of 64 member subsets is tested against the expected condition that both Cupriavidus activities are required; all candidate-only and mixed incomplete sets fail. Optional context does not turn an isolated enzyme into a complete pathway. Ontology-only prose was removed from biological knowledge gaps; the remaining gap concerns real KT2440 flux and enzyme assignment.
