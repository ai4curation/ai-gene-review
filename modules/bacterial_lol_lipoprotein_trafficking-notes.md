# Lol lipoprotein trafficking module notes

## 2026-11-07: architecture and evidence integration

The module boundary is mature cargo in the inner-membrane periplasmic leaflet
through outer-membrane incorporation. Sec/Tat export and lipid maturation are
upstream. Gliding, T9 secretion and surface exposure are downstream client
functions. None is added as a required step or inferred from generic necessity.

Required capacities are extraction and delivery. The whole extractor is either
C1/E1/D2 or F2/D2. C/E/F supply demonstrated coupled cargo/interface functions;
D supplies ATP hydrolysis. The intact assembly enables GO0140306. Neither a
single membrane subunit nor a generic ABC ATPase establishes complete release.
The 2007 positive LolDE reconstitution is retained as condition-specific evidence,
not generalized to native E. coli or called impossible.

Delivery comprises a compatible carrier and classical TIGR00548 or distant
DUF4292 inserter, or two functions in the same bifunctional protein. Classical
and distant inserters are not assigned one invented family. PTHR35869:SF1
membership is not sufficient for cargo specificity or insertion. The native
F. johnsoniae pair is not freely interchangeable with E. coli. The Caulobacter
representative is an inference to the native CB15 protein from a construct with
an E. coli-compatible terminal segment in a permissive host; it does not prove
native whole-module flux. Unidentified bypasses remain gaps outside this
encoded mechanism.

The completed comparative member panels are E. coli K-12, P. putida KT2440 and
A. baylyi ADP1. Evidence tiers differ: target ADP1 extractor/carrier biochemistry
does not directly assay its terminal LolB insertion, and KT2440 coverage is
predominantly conserved-function inference. F. johnsoniae and CB15 are partial
delivery panels; no unverified extractor identities are supplied.

Three searched reviews provide context (PMIDs30900542,42023066,40536045); the
claim-specific primary studies and frozen gene consultations determine member,
construct and mechanism scope. PMID39421643 is Opinion and is not counted as a
review; the discovery-only PMID25583975 is not used as Francisella architecture
evidence. The existing provider reports remain immutable historical sources;
their broad no-gap/essentiality generalizations are bounded by primary evidence.

Module consultation artifacts record the pre-edit document, source preservation,
focused checks and final release. No full-corpus validation, index rebuild,
commit or publication is performed by this consultation.
