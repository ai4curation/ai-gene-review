# Comparative literature assessment

The 2009 review (PMID19416363) and 2015 review (PMID25986150) were read in full from the publisher and PMC, respectively. Retrieval URLs, timestamps and hashes are preserved alongside this note. Their normal publication cache entries remain abstract-only and were not rewritten. PMID38714141 was read as an abstract only; it supplies review-level framing, not detailed structural claims.

The reviews support treating bacterial chaperonin duplication, co-chaperonin compatibility, oligomer stability and client specialization as biological variables. They do not establish all paralog functions, phylogenetic node placements, universal bacterial occurrence, or interchangeable partners. Proposed C-terminal-tail correlations in the 2015 discussion are not used as classification or mechanistic rules.

Primary PMID22834700 explicitly supports mycobacterial Cpn60.2 complementation with cognate and E. coli co-chaperonins. Its full publisher text returned 403, so strain and construct details absent from the accessible abstract remain unasserted. PMID15327959 reports ATP-independent lower-oligomer refolding preparations: those results are retained beside the canonical ATP-dependent capacity, rather than treated as a contradiction to remove.

PMID12082092 combines native Bacillus GroEL association/GroE-depletion experiments with purified E. coli GroE reagents in the HrcA activation assay. The gene-level Methods reading preserves this distinction. Target curated HAMAP/UniProt functions support the Bacillus and KT2440 pairs without inventing purified target kinetic assays.

PMID30482828 and official supplements identify the CB15N Caulobacter proteins and its cognate cage. PMID33996917 supplies the CCNA_00109 locus crosswalk; functional evidence remains the primary study. The Caulobacter CnoX reductase and constitutive holdase differ from the E. coli HOCl-induced holdase and basal GroEL-associated redox protection. The source-specific CnoX branches do not assert stable Caulobacter cage binding or direct substrate-transfer kinetics.

PMID42567683 captures E. coli CnoX and GroES on the same ring using substrate-free AMP-PNP preparations. This qualifies the 2023 displacement model without proving kinetic order or productive folding in every observed state.
