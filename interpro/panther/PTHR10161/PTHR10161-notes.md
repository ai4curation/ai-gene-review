- PANTHER's PTHR10161 family label is `TARTRATE-RESISTANT ACID PHOSPHATASE TYPE 5`; InterPro integrates the same PTHR as `Metallophosphoesterase Purple Acid Phosphatase`, which better captures the broad family.
- PTN004128707 carries the acid phosphatase activity, ferrous iron binding, and ferric iron binding IBD rows. ACP5 has no PAINT IRD/IKR loss on its lineage, so all three rows descend to human ACP5.
- PTN000017834 carries the bone resorption IBD row. The PANTHER 19.0 tree cache resolves `PTN000017834` as a 29-leaf node that includes vertebrate ACP5 leaves plus four `Ciona intestinalis` leaves, `Branchiostoma floridae LOC118405250`, and two `Strongylocentrotus purpuratus` leaves. Its descendant `PTN002563058` is the 22-leaf vertebrate ACP5 node: human, other eutherian and metatherian/monotreme mammals, sauropsids, amphibians, gar, zebrafish, and medaka.
